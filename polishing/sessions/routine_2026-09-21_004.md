## Work done

Ran `build/review_accuracy.py --range 8001 9000 --budget 2.5` (default gloss/translation/tags/notes
dimensions). Reviewed 1,000 entries, flagged 230, cost $0.4791.

**offvocab (235 issues, ~172 entries)**: deterministic off-vocabulary semantic tags. Built a
tag-to-closed-vocabulary migration map covering all 153 distinct off-vocab tags seen in this range
(e.g. `loanword`→`language`, `gardening`→`daily-life`, `documents`→`communication`,
`restaurant`→`food`) and applied it script-wise, deduplicating against tags the entry already
carried. 173 entries had their `metadata.tags.semantic` rewritten; every entry retained at least
one closed-vocabulary tag afterward.

**wrong-category (25 issues)**: opened each entry and checked the flagged tag against the entry's
own senses/examples. 8 were genuine mistags and were removed or corrected (e.g. removed stray
`cognition` from 脚力/腕力/握力 grip-strength entries, removed `education` from 雑巾がけ, fixed an
`organization`→`business` offvocab mismigration on 整え直す back to `daily-life`). 17 were
model misreads — the flagged tag was supported by a sense or example the model missed (e.g.
`transportation` on 間引く is valid because sense 2 covers reduced train/bus service; `person` on
店屋 is valid because sense 2 is "shopkeeper") — rejected with the supporting evidence noted.

**gloss-meaning / translation-meaning (19 issues, 11 entries)**: 9 applied (trimmed an
over-broad or misleading gloss on 腐食, 持ち込み, 掻き消す, 鉄筋, ほんと, ノーヒット, 一眼, iOS; and
対角, whose top-level gloss wrongly said "diagonal" — it means "opposite angle/corner" — and whose
first example sentence used the wrong headword, 対角線, so it was rewritten). 3 rejected as sound
as written (酒蔵, 運転見合わせ, 更年期 — the last already covers 男性更年期 in its notes).

**register (5 issues)**: 3 applied — two formality tags (不明瞭, 頻繁, 誇張) contradicted their own
notes' "somewhat formal" claim and were changed to `formal`; お詫び's politeness tag was `plain`
though its notes describe the お- prefix as making it polite, changed to `polite`. 2 rejected
(好ましい and 飲酒's tags already matched their notes).

**notes-fact (43 issues, 31 entries)**: the highest false-positive rate of any family this run —
about 20 of 43 flags quoted text attributed to a section where it doesn't appear (e.g. claiming a
"FORMS" section contains text that is actually in a different section, or a claim already
corrected one line later in the same note) or misjudged a fact that was correct as written (e.g.
calling the correct series/parallel circuit current/voltage explanation "reversed"). These were
rejected with the specific mismatch noted. 10 were genuine and fixed: a wedding gift-cord note that
recommended the wrong knot type (蝶結び) for weddings, a confusing "bird hunting (not hunting)"
gloss for 探鳥, a tax-evasion/tax-avoidance terminology mixup on 脱税, a moon-phase note that
presented 半月 as a phase distinct from 上弦/下弦 rather than the general term for either, and
similar clarity/accuracy fixes on 熱中症, グリーン車/グランクラス, その前, 霜降り, HP/ホームページ,
and シーン's silence onomatopoeia. One entry, 車席 (08989), looked like it may be a fabricated
headword duplicating 座席 (02832_zaseki already exists) — flagged to `reviews/needs_curator.txt`
rather than touched. One wedding gift-amount etiquette note (08356, the odd/even yen-amount rule)
was also flagged rather than guessed at.

Logged 208 apply / 36 reject / 2 flag decisions to `reviews/decisions.jsonl`. Added a `[tooling]`
observation to `polishing/observations.md` about the notes-fact false-positive rate this run.
