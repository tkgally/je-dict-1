## Rejections — a new false-positive family

Both rejections were in `01440_shinbun`'s list of major newspapers:

- ⟦{朝日|あさひ}→朝日：noentry⟧ in 朝日新聞 → `23495_asahi` ("morning sun; rising sun")
- ⟦{毎日|まいにち}→毎日：noentry⟧ in 毎日新聞 → `00729_mainichi` ("every day")

Each paper is named after the word, so the link is etymologically true and pragmatically
wrong — the reader clicking it lands on a sense the sentence is not using. **Proper names
whose target entry carries only the common-noun sense** is a family distinct from the
polysemous-loanword (フライ) and abbreviation (海パン) families, and class R cannot see it
because the readings agree.

It does not generalise to proper nouns as such: 上野 in 上野動物園 → `28394_ueno`, and
横浜/札幌 in a list of cities, were applied without hesitation, because there the target entry
*is* the proper noun. The discriminator is whether the target entry covers the proper-noun
sense, not whether the surface is a name.

Families verified as groups in this band: faculty compounds (商学部/薬学部/農学部), style and
origin suffixes (日本式/西洋式, 日本製/中国製/金属製), city and ward names (横浜/札幌/特別区),
猿 compounds (三猿/日本猿/犬猿/猿真似), 竹 compounds (竹刀/青竹/竹取/竹ざお), 貝 compounds
(貝塚/貝柱), suffix entries (〜化/〜代/〜物/〜金), and 載る/載せる/計る/量る verb-family splits.

Running false-positive rate across the 358 hand-verified short-base pairs (2026-08-09 plus
this run): **~0.6%**.
