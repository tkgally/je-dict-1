## Work

1. **Built `build/check_okurigana_ruby.py`**: a read-only review-queue generator implementing the
   backlog item's detection rule (single-kanji wrapper `{K|R}`, flag `R` when a proper prefix of
   `R` is a common reading of `K` elsewhere in the dictionary — `R` ≤3 occurrences, prefix ≥10).
   Mirrors `check_furigana_format.py`'s CLI shape (`--json`/`--summary`/`--range`).
2. **Scanned the whole dictionary**: 121 instances / 89 distinct (kanji, reading) pairs across 99
   entries at scan time (close to the 2026-08-16 measurement of 123/90/dictionary-then-smaller).
3. **Verified every instance by hand** (per-entry check, as the backlog item requires — not
   mechanical). For each I checked whether the "extra" kana in the ruby is genuinely separable in
   real orthography (a real, recoverable okurigana split) or whether the multi-mora reading is a
   complete, non-decomposable reading with no separate kana slot to move it to.
   - **77 real bugs, 76 entries fixed.** Most were the straightforward pattern (swallowed
     okurigana that should sit outside the braces, e.g. `{受|うけ}` → `{受|う}け`), including
     several radical-name entries (`{雨|あめかんむり}` → `{雨|あめ}かんむり`, matching 00023,
     03906, 07898, 26560). A handful had actually produced a *visible* duplicated character
     because the swallowed kana were **also** already written outside the ruby (`{切|きり}り` →
     `{切|き}り`, `{振|ふる}る` → `{振|ふ}る`, `{失|うせ}せ` → `{失|う}せ`, `{遂|とげ}げる` →
     `{遂|と}げる`, `{三|みつ}つ` → `{三|み}つ`, `{透|すか}かし` → `{透|す}かし`) — those read
     visibly wrong on the site before the fix (きりり, ふるる, うせせ …), not just structurally.
   - **30 instances / 20 pairs / 24 entries are genuine exceptions**, left unchanged: complete
     atomic readings with no separate kana slot in real orthography (`{冷|ひや}{奴|やっこ}`,
     `{源|みなもとの}{頼朝|よりとも}`, お`{坊|ぼっ}`ちゃん, `{御|おん}〜`), name/nanori readings
     (黒澤`{明|あきら}`, 太宰`{治|おさむ}`, `{助|すけ}`っ人), on-yomi readings the detector
     mistook for kun-yomi + okurigana (`{換|かん}`, 問屋の`{問|とん}`, 悪化の`{悪|あっ}` —
     sokuon assimilation, not okurigana), and one spurious 1-mora prefix match
     (寂`{寂|さみ}`しい, where さ isn't a real base reading of 寂). Also left `{頂|いただき}`
     (mountain summit) alone in all 3 occurrences — 頂 as a bare-kanji noun with no trailing き
     is a legitimate spelling in its own right, not obviously a bug, so I erred conservative
     rather than changing the underlying word spelling.
4. **Mechanical pass** on the 75 directly-edited entries: `normalize_notes.py` (0 rewrites — no
   header issues introduced), `auto_link.py` (+89 new inline links, mostly on the okurigana text
