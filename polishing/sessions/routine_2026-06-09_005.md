## Work performed

### reading-truncated sub-pattern (74 instances → 0)

The error: wrappers like `{やり方|かた}` include leading hiragana in the surface but the reading only covers the kanji. Browsers render `かた` over the entire `やり方`. Fix: move the hiragana prefix outside the wrapper → `やり{方|かた}`.

Fixed 61 entry files (some had multiple instances):
- **Common patterns**: {やり方|かた}→やり{方|かた}, {やり直|なお}→やり{直|なお}, {ど忘|わす}→ど{忘|わす}, {たんぱく質|しつ}→たんぱく{質|しつ}, {ぶどう狩|が}→ぶどう{狩|が}, etc.
- **Special cases**:
  - `{書き込み|かきこ}` (09225_kakikomi): was an abbreviation note, not furigana; replaced wrapper with plain text `かきこ`
  - `{ただ一人|たったひとり}` (23369_yuiitsuno): changed reading to `ひとり` → `ただ{一人|ひとり}`
  - `{ぶどう狩|がり}` (27924_ringogari): reading `がり` included okurigana; split to `ぶどう{狩|が}り`
- All entries with `{てんてこ舞|ま}い`, `{ひな祭|まつ}り`, `{たこ焼|や}き`, `{どんど焼|や}き`, etc. correctly preserved the okurigana outside the wrapper
