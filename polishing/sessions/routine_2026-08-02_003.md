## The finding: off-vocab semantic tags are 75% of this range

A deterministic scan against `VALID_SEMANTIC` (the single source of truth per the 2026-06-11
semantic-tag policy) found **226 of 300 entries carrying at least one tag outside the 81-tag
vocabulary**, spread over **179 distinct off-vocab tags**. The reviewer independently flagged 290 of
these instances — its `tags` dimension and the scan agree almost exactly. Dictionary-wide the
population is **4,899 instances across 818 distinct tags**.

Per §A step 4, "not in the list" is correct by definition, so all 226 were migrated. The map was
built in two provably different classes:

- **Forced renames** — destination determined by the tag name alone, independent of the entry:
  `medicine`/`medical`/`healthcare`→`health`, `biology`/`chemistry`/`physics`/`math`→`science`,
  `linguistics`/`writing`→`language`, `grammar`/`interrogative`→`grammatical`, `time`→`time-general`,
  `people`→`person`, `animals`→`animal-general`, `mammal`/`rodent`→`animal-mammal`,
  `transport`/`train`/`aviation`→`transportation`, `tools`/`stationery`→`tool`,
  `places`/`location`/`city`→`geography`, `cuisine`/`ingredient`→`food`, and ~90 more.
- **Dropped** — register/etymology markers (`loanword`, `literary`, `slang`), contentless catch-alls
  (`object`, `material`, `event`), and **context-dependent tags with no entry-independent
  destination**. This second group is the important one: a first draft that forced them produced
  `education` for 立証 "proof", `nature` for 焼却炉 "incinerator", `time-season` for ゲレンデ "ski
  slope", `business` for 溶鉱炉 "blast furnace", and `politics` for 所定事項 "required items on a
  form". Dropping an off-vocab tag can never add a false claim, so drop became the default for
  `academic`, `safety`, `environment`, `winter`, `conflict`, `industry`, `bureaucracy`, `department`,
  `statistics`, `innovation`, `design`, `structure`, `service`, `organization`, `infrastructure`,
  `energy`, `disaster`, `death`, `sexuality`, and the rest of that class.

Result: **0 off-vocab semantic tags remain in 23608–23907**; all 300 entries validate.
