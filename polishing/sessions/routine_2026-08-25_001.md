### Detector-class test (the main methodological result)

Three previous updates on this item proposed a new mechanical detector class: scan the
link target's `notes` for a "X as Y is a different word with the same reading"
self-declaration and demote such pairs out of the mechanical bucket. Tested against this
band, the naive version **fired on 7 of 175 pairs at 1/7 precision** — the six
non-rejections were good entries disambiguating themselves from *other* homophones
(臨む/望む, 端午/単語, 利く/聞く・効く, 生む/産む, 五円玉/ご縁).

The refinement that makes it work: **the declaration is a demotion signal only when it
names the marker's own base form.** That keeps all six good pairs, catches ロック, and
would have caught the フライ/パン and コマ rejections. It would *not* have caught the バー
rejection, whose target contrasts バー only with 居酒屋 and スナック — so the class raises
precision but does not replace reading the context. Written up in the backlog item, the
P35 prose page, and `polishing/observations.md`.
