### Gloss (16 issues) — 8 applied, 3 rejected (5 were duplicate flags of the same fix)

- **19212 {海獣|かいじゅう}** — "marine mammal; sea beast" → "marine mammal", and
  the sense gloss "marine mammal; sea creature" → "marine mammal". The entry's own
  note says it refers *specifically* to marine mammals and warns against confusion
  with the homophone {怪獣|かいじゅう} (monster) — "sea beast" invited exactly that.
- **19246 {写真映|しゃしんば}え** — noun headword glossed with an adjective:
  "photogenic; …" → "photogenic quality; looking good in photos".
- **19252 {軽装|けいそう}** — "traveling light" (an action) → "light equipment",
  matching the entry's own noun/な-adjective description.
- **19344 {吉|きち}** — "good fortune; lucky" → "good fortune; good luck".
- **19383 {飛行士|ひこうし}** — top gloss "pilot; aviator; astronaut" → "pilot;
  aviator". 飛行士 alone is not "astronaut"; that is {宇宙|うちゅう}{飛行士|ひこうし}.
- **19387 {日|ひ}の{丸|まる}** — dropped "rising sun flag": that is the
  {旭日旗|きょくじつき}, a different flag, and a politically loaded confusion.
- **19390 {雀蜂|すずめばち}** — "hornet; yellowjacket" → "hornet". Yellowjackets are
  *Vespula*; スズメバチ are *Vespa*.
- **19496 {口火|くちび}を{切|き}る** — top gloss "to open fire; to start things off"
  → "to be the first to speak; to start things off". "To open fire" is the
  etymology (which the notes already explain), not the meaning.

Rejected: **19373 {交|か}わす** ×2 — "(greetings, words, etc.)" is open-ended and
covers 握手を交わす; **19383** sense 2 — removing "(in compounds)" would assert
that 飛行士 alone means astronaut; **19460 {是非|ぜひ}** — the reviewer wanted the
adverbial "by all means" added, but that is deliberately a separate entry
(`00911_zehi`), and this entry's notes say so explicitly.

- **APPLIED — 19320 {瓜|うり}{二|ふた}つ**: 「{若|わか}い{頃|ころ}の{母|はは}の
