## Systemic-fix work (§B)

**The defect**: inline links use `⟦surface→baseform：entry_id⟧`; `docs/styles.css:245` renders the
baseform segment as the hover tooltip. When the baseform is written in kana equal to the target
entry's `reading` while the target's `headword` is kanji, the tooltip shows the learner the
pronunciation instead of the dictionary form — e.g. hovering 期間 in an example sentence showed
`きかん` instead of `期間`.

**Detector/fixer**: wrote `build/fix_link_baseform_kana.py` (dry-run by default, `--apply` to
write). For every link, if `baseform == target.reading` and
`strip_furigana(target.headword) != baseform`, rewrite the baseform to
`strip_furigana(target.headword)` — unless the target's headword is itself kana (nothing to fix)
or lists multiple variants (`／`-separated — which variant is right depends on the sentence, so
left for per-entry judgment).

**Re-measured before fixing**: 5,463 links / 3,512 entries — well up from the 3,567/1,712 measured
at the 2026-07-29 filing. The five-week gap saw ongoing `auto_link.py` passes over previously
unlinked text, and much of the growth is concentration rather than spread: a handful of extremely
common function words dominate (で、ください→下さい at `02899_kudasai` alone accounted for a
large share, since ください appears in examples across the whole corpus). Not evidence of an
active leak — `auto_link.py` doesn't choose baseform text, it only links surface forms; the
kana-baseform choice traces to whatever placed the original link, and this was a census of a fixed
historical population.

**Applied to all 3,512 entries** (whole-corpus sweep, not banded — the fix unit is the target
entry's own headword, so batch size added no risk, same reasoning as the 2026-07-26 dead-target-id
sweep). Spot-checked a random sample of the diffs (ところ→所, ください→下さい, あやまる→謝る,
ためる→溜める, もしくは→若しくは, ごはん→ご飯, …) — all correct, surface and target_id untouched
in every case.

**Residue**: 4 links left untouched (base やさしい／やさしく → `00475_yasashii`, whose headword
易しい／優しい has two variants and picking between them needs the sentence's sense) across
03643, 04419, 04595, 04598. Filed as new backlog item `link-baseform-kana-multivariant`.
445 links correctly left alone (target headword is itself kana — が, の, に, and similar function
words).

Genuine errors fixed:
- **00072 ごめん**: notes said the kanji spelling was "formal" while the word "is casual" —
  self-contradictory; clarified.
- **00324 ノー**: notes listed ノーマル as a compound of ノー; it's a direct loanword from
  "normal," not a compound — removed.
- **00334 各々**: notes claimed an alternate reading かくかく "in very formal contexts," linked to
  an unrelated word (カクカク, "jerky, choppy motion"). Removed.
- **00528 消す**: main gloss missing "to put out" (a fire) — added.
- **00091 挟む**: "疑いを挟む" translated as "to harbor doubts"; it means to interject/introduce
  doubt into a conversation, not to hold it internally — fixed.
- **00565 取る**: "虫を取ってくれない？" translated as "get rid of the bug," too strong (implies
  killing it) — softened to "take the bug away."
- **00606 休む**: sense 2 (be absent) was labeled 他動詞 (transitive) taking を, but this is the
  well-known departure-point construction (家を出る, 山を登る) — genuinely intransitive despite
  taking を. Fixed the label and explained why.
- **00876 怒る, 01464 注意**: both mix an intransitive sense with a real transitive one (怒る
  someone = scold; を注意する = warn/scold someone) but carried a single `transitivity:
  intransitive` tag. Changed to `both`.
- **00926 ですから**: gloss and notes called it "polite," which reads as a direct clash against
  its own `politeness: plain` tag (the entry's `formality: formal` is what the text actually
  means) — reworded to "formal" throughout.
- **01063 手袋**: notes described ミトン (mittens) as "fingerless" — mittens enclose all four
  fingers together, they aren't fingerless. Fixed.
- **01072 ズボン**: etymology said the French jupon (petticoat) sense "shifted" to pants in
  Japanese; more accurately, the word was reinterpreted — reworded.
- **01091 並べる**: "大きい順に" ("in order of size") translated as "from largest to smallest,"
  asserting a direction the Japanese doesn't specify — fixed.
- **01254 決して**: intro sentence said it "MUST be used with negative verb forms," but it also
  attaches to negative adjectives (per the entry's own MODIFIES line) — broadened to "negative
  predicate."
- **01311 ご覧になる**: notes said it "elevates the listener" (twice); honorific verbs elevate the
  subject of the action, not the listener — fixed both occurrences.
- **01593 ご主人**: 「いらっしゃらない」(negative) translated as "your husband is home" — exactly
  backward. Fixed to "is not home."
- **01681 明かり**: SIMILAR WORDS called it light "from artificial sources" only, directly
  contradicted by the entry's own 月の明かり (moonlight) example — fixed.
- **01972 乗せる**: 「彼の口に乗せられて」("talked into it by him") translated without "by him" —
  added.
- **02021 押さえる**: "要点を押さえる" translated as "grasp the key points," a sense not covered by
  any of the entry's three listed senses (press down / restrain / secure) — retranslated to
  "secure," which sense 3 does cover.
- **02116 支払う**: notes twice call it "more formal, used in business/official contexts" while
  the tag said `neutral` — changed to `formal`.

Flagged to the curator (genuine uncertainty, not mechanical): 00447 解答 (transitivity of 解答する
splits between に and を usage in real corpora, couldn't confirm which dominates), and 00562 つける
