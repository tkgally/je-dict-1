- **00804 重い** (62) — no USAGE section. Rewritten around the point the entry never made: the
  severity sense attaches only to nouns naming an illness, injury, penalty, or burden, and does
  *not* extend to problems in general (重大な問題 / 深刻な問題, never 重い問題). Added the
  重さ / 重み split (measurable weight vs. gravitas), the 気が重い idiom, and the note that a
  body part as subject means a leaden feeling rather than actual weight. Cross-references added
  to 気が重い (06881) and 重大 (01641), both with back-links. Semantic tag `quantity` added.
- **01092 置く** (77) — **aspect error corrected**: the notes claimed 置いている expresses the
  resulting state ("have put and it's still there"). It does not; 置く names the moment of
  placing, so plain ている is progressive or habitual and the resulting state is 置いてある or
  置かれている. All three now contrasted explicitly. The 〜ておく auxiliary, which is the entry's
  whole second sense and had two lines, is now a section covering its three jobs plus the
  とく/どく contractions. Added SIMILAR VERBS (入れる, 乗せる, 並べる, 仕舞う) and three
  cross-references with back-links.
- **06484 あくび** (47) — no COMMON PATTERNS section. **Content error corrected**: 生あくび was
  glossed "unrestrained/raw yawning"; it is the opposite — a half-yawn that never quite comes
  out, and a noted sign of fatigue or illness. Also explained why あくびが出る is the everyday
  phrasing and あくびをする the marked one (yawning is involuntary, so you report it happening to
  you). Cross-reference to 眠気 (06534) with a back-link.
- **06730 書き留める** (47) — no TRANSITIVITY or COMMON PATTERNS section. Both added, plus a
  WATCH OUT block for the homograph trap: 書留 (かきとめ), the same two characters without
  okurigana, is registered mail and an unrelated word. Cross-references to 書留 (00150) and
  メモする (25501), both with back-links.
- **06529 連敗** (49) and **06528 連勝** (51) — see the `[pattern]` observation below. Both were
  already content-rich and scored in the forties purely because their section was headed
  `GRAMMAR:` instead of the `COMMON PATTERNS:` the verb-suru template requires. Headers
  normalised, collocation lists merged in, `・` bullets converted, and the repetitive
  "COUNTING STREAKS" block (2連敗 / 5連敗 / 10連敗) collapsed into one line. The genuinely new
  content is the pair's asymmetry: a winning streak 止まる (intransitive, it ends by itself) while
  a losing streak is something you 止める.
- **00696 元気** (64) — no USAGE section. Added the 元気 / 健康 distinction (how someone seems vs.
  what the body's condition is — a person can be 健康 but not 元気) and, more importantly, the
  warning that お元気ですか is **not** a daily greeting: it asks after someone you have not seen in
  a while and sounds strange said to a colleague each morning. Cross-reference to 元気づける
  (21545) with a back-link.
- **02900 ぐらい** (55) — no FUNCTIONS section, and only two of its three functions documented.
  Added the third (a yardstick for degree: 涙が出るぐらい嬉しい, where ぐらい does not mean "about"
  at all) and a CONTRASTS section carrying two high-value learner points: 約 goes *before* the
  number where ぐらい goes after, and ごろ marks an approximate point in time while ぐらい marks an
  approximate span (3時ごろ vs. 3時間ぐらい). Back-link added on 程 (01724).

## Frontier lane (07059–07064)

All six are 2026-01-vintage entries with **no inline word links whatsoever** and legacy ALL-CAPS
notes using `・` bullets, exactly as the 2026-08-30 log predicted. Each received a full tier-1
link pass over every example plus a notes rewrite. Beyond that:

- **07059 拝観料** — the flat list of fee words replaced with the rule that picks between them
  (拝観料 temples and shrines, 入館料 for 〜館 buildings, 入場料 for grounds and events, 観覧料 for
  places you go to look at something). Explained why the word is confined to religious sites: 拝
  is the character of 拝む, and it frames entry as reverent viewing rather than buying a ticket.
- **07060 バー** — `formality` was tagged `informal`, which is wrong for a plain noun; changed to
  `neutral`. The RELATED TERMS list became a real four-way distinction between 居酒屋, バー,
  スナック, and 酒場 on the dimensions that actually separate them (food vs. drink, table vs.
  counter, pricing model, and how current the word is).
- **07061 トラブル** — added what the entry most needed for a loanword: トラブル is *narrower*
  than English "trouble". It names a countable incident that has gone wrong, so "I'm in trouble"
  in the sense of being in a bad position is not トラブル, and a maths exercise is a 問題 and
  never a トラブル.
- **07062 手芸** — made the 手芸 / 工芸 line explicit (thread and fabric vs. crafts at large,
  including pottery and lacquer), since English translates both as "crafts". Cross-reference to
  工芸 (04182) with a back-link.
- **07063 木彫り** — semantic tag was sole-`general`; retagged `art` + `culture`. Added the
  彫刻 contrast (sculpture as an art form in any material vs. a named material and technique
  covering craft objects) and kept the Hokkaido bear note with its actual provenance.
- **07064 控え室** — the SIMILAR WORDS list previously included 控え室 itself as one of the three
  entries, and listed 病院の控え室 as a context. A hospital waiting room is a 待合室; 控え室 is for
  people waiting for a scheduled turn. Corrected both, and added the 楽屋 distinction (a
  performer's room for changing and preparing, not for waiting). Cross-reference to 待合室
  (07373) with a back-link, and a back-link added on 楽屋 (08376).
