## Headline finding — semantic-tag vocabulary contamination is a hot spot

A deterministic scan of the 500 entries against `VALID_SEMANTIC` (the closed
88-tag list in `build/validate_tags.py`) found **341 of 500 entries (68%)
carrying off-vocabulary semantic tags, across 264 distinct invented tags** —
`office`, `stationery`, `kitchen`, `medical`, `legal`, `housing`, `gardening`,
`competition`, `loanword`, and so on.

Dictionary-wide the same scan reports **1,603 of 30,365 entries (5.3%)**. So this
ID block is roughly 13× the background rate — a batch-creation artifact where
free-form tags were invented instead of drawn from the closed list, not even
drift. This block alone holds about a fifth of the dictionary's entire
off-vocabulary-tag population.

This also means the paid `tags` review dimension was largely redundant here:
426 of the 429 issues it raised over 346 entries were "tag not in the valid
list" flags, which the free deterministic check already knows.
