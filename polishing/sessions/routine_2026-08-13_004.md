### Glosses, example translations, semantic tags

All 495 entries were reviewed; 124 came back flagged, carrying 143 individual issues. **Three
content corrections were applied**, all cases where the entry contradicted itself:

- **09425 パッとしない** — the entry explains the word as "neither bad nor good, just mediocre",
  but its first example translated 最近パッとしないな as "Things haven't been going well lately",
  which says something is wrong. Now "Things have been pretty unremarkable lately."
- **09447 途方** — the gloss read "way, means, direction" while the entry's own explanation and
  notes say the word almost never appears outside the set expressions 途方に暮れる and 途方もない.
  A learner reading only the gloss would learn the wrong thing. Now "way, means (chiefly in set
  expressions)".
- **09570 他人事** — the third example translated 彼は他人事だと思っている as "He thinks it's not his
  problem", a negated construction that collides with the entry's other example for 他人事ではない
  ("This is *not* someone else's problem"). Now "He thinks it's someone else's problem."

**Seven other gloss and translation flags were rejected**, all of them scope-expansion or
style preferences rather than errors: 09312 ナンパ (the entry already splits the act and the
person into two senses), 09324 盲点 (the entry's own notes give 法律の盲点 as "legal loophole",
