## Session: Aspect/ている Notes
Date: 2026-04-11
Entries checked: 00500-01999
Verbs checked: ~300 (many already-documented verbs were confirmed via a directory-wide ASPECT scan rather than individually re-read; suru-verb entries with obvious progressive ている were skipped without individual reads)
Verbs modified: 13

### Context

The 00500-00999 range was already comprehensively polished — nearly every verb in that range (95 verbs) already had an ASPECT section in its notes. The two verbs without one ({会議|かいぎ}する and {旅行|りょこう}する) have obvious progressive ている meanings and were skipped per the rule of thumb. Focus shifted to 01000-01999, where about 189 verbs lacked ASPECT sections. Most are suru-verbs whose ている form is a straightforward progressive (e.g., {面接|めんせつ}している = "is interviewing") and were skipped. The 13 modifications below target cases where the ている form is genuinely non-obvious for an intermediate learner.

### Changes Made

- 01094_shucchou {出張|しゅっちょう}: Added ASPECT — resultative state ("is away on business trip"), analogous to 行く
- 01155_imi {意味|いみ}: Added ASPECT — stative ("means/signifies"), not progressive
- 01443_seikatsu {生活|せいかつ}: Added ASPECT — habitual/stative ("lives"), parallel to 住む
- 01497_hantai {反対|はんたい}: Added ASPECT — stative ("opposes/is opposed to"), describes a current stance
- 01517_yakusoku {約束|やくそく}: Added ASPECT — resultative state ("has a standing commitment")
- 01811_aisuru {愛|あい}する: Added ASPECT — stative emotional state ("loves"); warned learners that plain {愛|あい}する is mainly literary
- 01862_atsukau {扱|あつか}う: Added ASPECT — habitual ("deals in/carries") for businesses and shops
- 01889_utagau {疑|うたが}う: Added ASPECT — stative mental attitude ("suspects/doubts"), like 信じる
- 01936_kireru {切|き}れる: Added ASPECT — resultative change-of-state ("is out of stock", "battery is dead")
- 01957_chigau {違|ちが}う: Added ASPECT — inherent stative verb; both plain and ている are used; noted 違います as the standard polite denial
- 01959_tsukamaru {捕|つか}まる: Added ASPECT — covers both senses: resultative "is in custody" (sense 1) and ongoing "is holding on" (sense 2)
- 01964_tsumoru {積|つ}もる: Added ASPECT — resultative ("is piled up"), classic snow-accumulation example
- 01982_fukumu {含|ふく}む: Added ASPECT — stative ("contains/includes"); noted that ている (and passive 含まれている) is the standard for describing contents

### Skipped (already documented or obvious progressive)

- 00500-00999: Essentially all ~95 verbs in the range already have ASPECT sections (basic tier was previously polished). The two without ASPECT — 00710 {会議|かいぎ}する and 00748 {旅行|りょこう}する — have obvious progressive meaning.
- 01100 面接, 01172 計画, 01179 経験, 01388 競争, 01396 研究, 01398 見物, 01405 故障, 01421 支配, 01422 試験, 01431 準備, 01432 紹介, 01435 招待, 01437 勝負, 01444 生産, 01449 説明, 01464 注意, 01466 駐車, 01471 電話, 01495 発明, 01522 予約, 01523 料理, 01524 連絡, and most other suru-verbs in 01000-01999: skipped as obvious progressive.
- 01295-01326 keigo/humble verbs (いらっしゃる, おっしゃる, ございます, くださる, なさる, 召し上がる, ご覧になる, いたす, 申す, 参る, おる, いただく): their ている behavior inherits from the base verb; left for a dedicated keigo pass if needed.
