Adjudication (full detail in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`):

- **28009_wakime (wrong-category) — applied.** `脇芽` (side shoot / axillary bud) is a plant part,
  not a leisure activity; the dictionary's own convention for plant parts (`葉`, `茎`) is
  `plant-general` alone. Retagged from `["nature", "leisure"]` to `["plant-general"]`.
- **29451_ruida (gloss-meaning) — applied.** `塁打` alone means the base(s) gained by one hit, not
  the aggregated "total bases" stat (that's `塁打数`). Reworded the gloss, the sense-1 definition,
  and the first example's translation to stop conflating the two.
- **29774_goreijou (gloss-meaning) — applied.** The gloss listed "your daughter" and "another's
  daughter" as if they were two senses; they're one honorific concept used for either referent.
  Collapsed to "your/another's daughter (honorific)".
- **27883_wazurau (notes-fact) — rejected.** Reviewer called the "-わずらう attaches to the
  masu-stem to mean 'have trouble doing'" note wrong; masu-stem and the attaching form are the
  same conjunctive form here, and the note's own examples (思い煩う, 言い煩う) support the gloss.
- **28169_gou (notes-fact) — rejected.** Reviewer read the REGISTER note as claiming 業を煮やす
  means "karma"; it doesn't — the note only observes how often the ごう reading survives in
  everyday speech, and the idiom already has its own correct gloss in COMMON EXPRESSIONS.
