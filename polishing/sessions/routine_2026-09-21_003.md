## Work done

Worked the transitivity disagreement queue top to bottom, 100 entries (22383_magari through
25359_shukushousuru). For each, judged the noun+する verb's own valency from the entry's example
sentences, applying the same standing rules as prior runs: a genuine external patient marked with
を counts as transitive evidence; a light-verb "Xを{headword}する" self-reference where X is the
compound's own baked-in noun (信号無視, 鍵交換, 吸気, 抜刀, 燃料補給, 配水, 口を閉ざす, 頬杖をつく
— the compound already names its own patient, so no further external argument exists) does not;
a departure-point/path を (漫歩する street-traversal, 行き来する route-between) does not; a
に/へ-marked target (依拠する, 口出しする, 横入りする, 騎乗する) is tagged intransitive, matching
this dictionary's existing tagging of 対処する/参加する/出る-family verbs. Self-directed grooming
verbs (男装する, 剃髪する, 化粧する) were tagged intransitive, matching how the dictionary already
treats similarly reflexive verbs. Three verbs — 間借りする, 射出する, デッサンする/スケッチする/
ロールプレイする-type activity verbs, タイプする, 手術する, 入庫する, 完読/飛ばし読み/斜め読み-type
reading verbs, 伝導する, and 縮小する — were tagged `both` where the entry's own examples (or, for
伝導, standard physics phrasing) showed genuine alternation between a transitive use with an
external patient and an intransitive use without one.
