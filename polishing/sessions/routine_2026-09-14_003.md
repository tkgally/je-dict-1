ID range, then manually assigned this run's IDs starting at 30913 (one past its highest), and
excluded its 20 words from selection. One further candidate, ぶり "for the first time in ~"
(C23423), turned out to be a duplicate of existing entry 28358 〜{ぶり} independent of PR #3293 —
removed and pushed as a small standalone commit before the entry-writing work started.
