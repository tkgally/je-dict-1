## Furigana pass

`--pass screening --range 12913 13412` reported **500 skipped, 0 screened** — the whole
range had already been screened on 2026-07-08. Those stored results carried **66 flags**.

Adjudicating them surfaced a tooling problem rather than dictionary errors. Every flag
sampled was a hallucinated "incomplete reading" claim: the model quoted wrappers such as
`{撤収|てっし}`, `{旧姓|きゅうせ}`, `{未熟|みじゅ}` that do not exist in the entries — all
eight checked directly contain the full, correct readings. Several quotes ended in `)`
rather than `}`, the signature of the display-context truncation that
`trim_context()` (added **2026-08-16**, commit `b6bfa95ee`) was written to fix. These
screening results predate that fix by five weeks.

To measure the fix rather than assume it, the 66 flagged entries were cleared from
`screening_status.json` and re-screened with the current prompt:

| | flagged |
|---|---|
| stored 2026-07-08 results | 66 / 66 |
| re-screened 2026-08-24 | **14 / 66** |

A 79% reduction. All 14 survivors are ordinary okurigana-stem false positives
(発→た in 発つ, 映→は in 映える) already documented in `reviews/calibration_report.md`.

Per §A step 2's known-noise shortcut, all 66 were bulk-rejected with one aggregated
ledger line and the **deep pass was skipped** — spending ~$0.01/entry deep-reviewing
