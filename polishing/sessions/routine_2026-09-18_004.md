## Work done

Ran `review_accuracy.py --range 2001 3000 --budget 2.5` (gloss/translation/tags/notes
dimensions). 989 entries reviewed, 137 flagged with 167 total issues, cost $0.4845.

Split the 137 flagged entries into four batches and adjudicated every issue by opening
the entry and checking the claim, never applying blindly. Final tally: **92 applied, 70
rejected, 5 flagged to the curator**.

Applied fixes covered a wide range: wrong or missing transitivity tags (教わる, 思い付く,
支度する, 続ける, 限る, 曲げる, and others), off-vocabulary or ill-fitting semantic tags
migrated to valid ones (furniture/geography removed from 〜製; body-part → action on 負う;
people → person on 金持ち-family entries; etc.), formality/politeness tags corrected to
match what the entry's own notes already said (昼ご飯 informal, 際中 neutral, ええ neutral,
それでは formal, and several more), missing or wrong senses added or fixed (取り上げる's
"deliver a baby" sense, レコード's "record/achievement" sense, 散らす's "distract" sense,
狩り's "viewing" sense), unnatural or misleading example/translation wording smoothed over
(三連敗北 → 三連続の敗北 style fixes, "new judgment" → "new trial", "seeds of talent" →
"budding talent"), and several outright wrong claims removed (a fabricated 角を曲げる
collocation, a bogus "added emphasis" morpheme explanation for 引っ張る's small tsu, a
misplaced note section on 差 that was actually describing an unrelated suffix).

Rejected flags were mostly cases where the reviewing model misread furigana/ruby markup
(seeing two identical-looking kanji strings without noticing the disambiguating readings),
asked for formality values outside the closed vocabulary (e.g. "very formal," "slightly
formal" — the closest valid value was already in use), or flagged something that was
already correct or a defensible lexicographic judgment call (transitivity of causative
constructions, established proverb translations, standard dictionary conventions already
applied consistently to sibling entries).

Five items were set aside for a curator's judgment rather than fixed unilaterally (see
below).
