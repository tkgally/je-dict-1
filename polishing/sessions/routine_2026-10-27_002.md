## The fix (build/review_accuracy.py)

- `quote_in_notes()` no longer accepts a quote whose first 25 characters match. The whole quote must occur inside one line of the notes; a quote may skip text with an ellipsis, but its pieces must appear in order on that line. Spliced quotes (two bullets joined) and quotes with an invented tail are dropped. The same gate covers register flags.
- The notes go to the reviewer with readings as 漢字(かな) (`annotated_jp()`), so {一日|ついたち} and {一日|いちにち} no longer look identical. A quote with or without the readings matches.
- Prompt: readings are shown in parentheses; each list line is about the word that begins it; copy the quote from one line, never join lines. PROMPT_VERSION 4 → 6, CLAUDE_PROMPT_VERSION 5 → 7.
- Unit test `test_notes_quote_must_sit_on_one_line`; the payload test updated for the annotated notes.
