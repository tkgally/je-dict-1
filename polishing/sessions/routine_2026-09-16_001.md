## Tooling finding

About a dozen of the 27 rejected `notes-fact` flags were the reviewer's own artifact, not real
defects: when a note has two adjacent bullets and the second opens with a furigana/inline-link
span, `review_accuracy.py`'s quote extraction drops the boundary between them and concatenates
unrelated sentences into a garbled composite (e.g. 27349_funkakou, 27600_nakerebanaranai), or
strips furigana that was the whole point of the sentence, making two different readings of the
same kanji look identical (27965_ikaru, 28118_shitsuu). Logged as a `[tooling]` observation in
`polishing/observations.md` with the full entry list — worth a look at the `quote` field's
extraction logic.
