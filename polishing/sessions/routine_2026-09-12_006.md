## Systemic-fix (§B)

Backlog item: inline-link base forms written with furigana braces instead of plain kanji
(`⟦{来|き}て→{来|く}る：00254_kuru⟧` instead of `⟦{来|き}て→来る：00254_kuru⟧`). This defect is
user-visible: the base-form slot renders as the hover tooltip on the live site
(`docs/styles.css` `content: attr(data-baseform)`), so a braced base form puts literal `{`,
`|`, `}` characters on screen. The item had been independently rediscovered by four separate
polish runs since 2026-07-25 and promoted to priority 5, but a batch-ready, provably-safe item
had still never actually been worked because the systemic-fix selector kept reaching other
items first.

**Detect**: `grep -rlE '→\{[^}]*\|[^}]*\}：' entries/` — 30 entries (2 of the previously-measured
35 had already been cleared incidentally by other sessions since the last 08-12 measurement).

**Fix**: mechanical strip of `{`, the reading, and `}` from the base-form segment only (anchored
between `→` and `：`, so the surface segment, where braces are correct, is never touched). 147
link instances fixed across the 30 entries, including two multi-brace bases the simple
single-group pattern doesn't show on its own (`{紙|かみ}{袋|ぶくろ}` → `紙袋` in 00712_kami,
`{話|はなし}{上手|じょうず}` → `話上手` in 00707_jouzu — both still fully anchored between the
arrow and colon).

`validate.py --changed-only`: 30/30 valid, including the whole-dictionary link-target check.
The detect regex now matches zero files dictionary-wide — this item is closed for good, and
`backlog-queue.json` / `cleanup-backlog.md` are both updated to `resolved`.

A word-lookup cross-check (which the backlog item specifically asked for, since a braced base
form can mask a link that never resolved to the right headword) turned up four small,
**pre-existing** mismatches, unrelated to this fix and not introduced by it — none are broken
links (every target ID exists), just base-form text that doesn't match the target's exact
headword spelling: a missing counter tilde on four 〜人 links (00975/00976/00984/00985), an
okurigana-spelling mismatch on 話上手 vs. the target's headword 話し上手 (00707), and two
harmless orthographic variants (早い vs. a dual-spelling headword; a plain vs. full-width tilde
on 〜やすい). Logged in `polishing/observations.md` rather than fixed here — outside this item's
bounded, mechanical scope. Also logged there: a related but differently-shaped defect this
regex correctly leaves alone — base forms where furigana covers only part of a word with plain
kana outside the braces (e.g. `{書|か}く`, `{折|お}り{紙|がみ}`) — worth its own future backlog
item and detector.
