## Work done

Worked the transitivity disagreement queue top to bottom, 100 entries (19850_shakou through
22364_torikosu). For each, judged the noun+する verb's own valency — an entry's example
sentences were treated as evidence only when they showed a genuine external patient marked
with を (something acted upon or transferred), not a light-verb "Xを{headword}する"
self-reference (盗撮をしていた男 doesn't tell you whether 盗撮する itself takes an object) or a
departure-point を on a motion-out verb (会議を中座した is analogous to 家を出る, which this
dictionary already tags intransitive) — falling back to standard usage knowledge where the
examples didn't show the pattern either way. に-marked targets (対処する, 奉仕する, 集中する,
貢献する, etc.) were tagged intransitive, matching how this dictionary already tags similar
verbs (参加する family, 出る). A handful of communication/aid verbs (連絡する, 助成する family)
were tagged transitive on the strength of how this dictionary already tags their close
siblings (報告する, 知らせる, 通知する, 問い合わせる — all transitive, taking content as a を
object). Three verbs (再選, 正常化, 停止する) were tagged `both`: they genuinely alternate
between an agentive transitive use and a subject-only intransitive use in ordinary Japanese.

No real 自他 (transitive/intransitive) pairs turned up needing `prominent_see_also`: every
`pairs` hint in this batch was empty `{}` (again mostly two-character kango + する nouns).
