## Session: Aspect/ている Notes
Date: 2026-04-11
Entries checked: 02000-02135
Verbs checked: ~120 (many already had ASPECT from prior polishing)
Verbs modified: 28

### Context

The 02000-02135 range contains a dense cluster of general-tier verbs. About 70 verbs in entries/02000/ already had ASPECT sections from previous polishing (confirmed via a directory-wide grep for "ASPECT"). This session focused on the remaining verbs that lacked ASPECT, adding sections to those with genuinely non-obvious ている behavior. Verbs with obvious progressive meaning (like {裂|さ}く "tear", {築|きず}く "build", {拝見|はいけん}する "view", {尽|つ}くす "exhaust") were skipped.
