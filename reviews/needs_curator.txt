2026-08-15T18:27:27Z convention — a suggestion for the note-writing skill, for you to accept or decline. Notes sometimes use an English section header with a Japanese suffix embedded in it, like "THE 〜物 SERIES:". The Japanese character inside the header is displaying the suffix rather than being read as running text, so it is easy to forget that it still needs its reading annotation, and two of twenty entries in one recent run had exactly that gap. Eight such entries remain across the whole dictionary. Either convention works: write the header in plain English ("SEASONAL SERIES:") and show the annotated suffix in the sentence below it, or annotate the character inside the header. Worth one line in .claude/skills/vocabulary-notes/SKILL.md whichever you choose.
