- **13525 {甥|おい}** — example 3 read 「{姉|あね}の{甥|おい}っ{子|こ}が…」, i.e. *the sister's*
  nephew, who would be the speaker's own son or a brother's son — not the headword's referent.
  The English ("My sister's nephew…") faithfully translated a Japanese sentence that was
  itself wrong. Japanese rewritten to 「{甥|おい}っ{子|こ}が{大|おお}きくなって、もう{高校生|こうこうせい}になった。」
  with the matching English. The reviewer had proposed fixing the English instead, which would
  have made the translation unfaithful.
