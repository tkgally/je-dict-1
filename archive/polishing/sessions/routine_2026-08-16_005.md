### Applied — glosses and example translations (4 entries)

- **12016 {国債|こくさい}** — gloss was "government bond; national debt". 国債
  is the bond itself; the aggregate national debt is 国債残高 or 国の借金, so
  the second gloss invited a real production error. Gloss narrowed to
  "government bond" and the USAGE note now states the distinction explicitly.
- **12127 {回忌|かいき}** — the entry's own explanation says the count is
  offset by one (三回忌 = second anniversary), and its 三回忌 and 十三回忌
  examples follow that rule, but the 七回忌 example was translated "seventh
  memorial service". Corrected to the sixth anniversary, so the entry no
  longer contradicts itself.
- **12137 {国賓|こくひん}** — 「国賓としての訪問」 was translated "a state
  visit", which is a different concept from a state guest and hid the headword
  from the example. Now "a visit as a state guest".
- **12305 {官邸|かんてい}** — two examples had their English translations
  swapped: the press-conference sentence carried the emergency-task-force
  translation and vice versa. Each translation read plausibly on its own, which
  is why neither previous polishing nor the reviewer's per-sentence check
  caught the swap; the reviewer only noticed that one of them rendered 官邸 as
  "prime minister's office". Both restored to their own sentences.
