## Headline finding: the furigana screener was manufacturing its own false positives

The first screening pass over this range flagged **21 of 130 entries (16%)**, far above the
0–5% documented for already-polished ranges. Spot-checking the first three flags against the
source files showed all three were wrong — 21902 `協議会`, 21912 `注力`, 21935 `防衛線` all
hold the full, correct reading.

Root cause, reproduced exactly: `extract_furigana_pairs()` captured only **10 characters** of
following context, and `build_screening_prompt()` rendered it as
`(followed by: {協議会|きょうぎか)`. A 10-character window almost always cuts *inside* the next
`{kanji|reading}` wrapper, and the annotation's closing paren then reads as the wrapper's
closing brace — so the model was shown a complete wrapper holding a truncated reading and
correctly reported what it saw. Every "the reading is incomplete / truncated" flag in the
batch came from this.

**Fixed in `build/review_runner.py`** (both the screening and the deep prompt, which shared the
defect):

- context capture widened 10 → 24 chars;
- new `trim_context()` cuts the excerpt back to a wrapper boundary so a half-wrapper is never
  displayed;
- delimiter changed from bare parens to 「」 so it cannot be confused with the markup;
- explicit instruction added: the excerpt is display-truncated, never infer a truncated
  reading from it.

The contaminated screening results for the range (130 files) were deleted and the range
re-screened from scratch with the fixed prompt.

**Effect**: flag rate fell from **21/130 (16%) → 6/171 (3.5%)**, and all 6 residual flags are
documented false-positive families (verified individually against source):
