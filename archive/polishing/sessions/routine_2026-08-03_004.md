## Headline: 246 off-vocab tags migrated, then three independent checks caught 16 bad destinations

**Deterministic pre-scan (free, no API).** Following the now-established Tooling-46 pattern,
`metadata.tags.semantic` was diffed against `VALID_SEMANTIC` before spending anything:
**246 of 600 entries (41%)** in 24501–25100 carried off-vocabulary tags, across **138 distinct
labels**. This is the third consecutive block in the 40–53% band. What has changed is the shape
of the tail: **106 of the 138 labels occur only once or twice**, so a frequency-ranked map runs
out of road quickly and the residue is all one-off, context-dependent labels.

**The migration was split by class, per the 2026-08-02 harvest.** Forced renames — where the
destination follows from the label itself (`arts`→`art`, `people`→`person`, `transport`→
`transportation`, `occupations`→`occupation`, `emotions`→`emotion`, and ~60 more) — were applied
mechanically. Context-dependent labels were decided per entry against the headword and gloss,
with 25 explicit per-entry overrides (e.g. 梗塞/失禁 → `body-internal` rather than the generic
`body-part`; 赤口 → `time-day-of-week`; 貴石 → `nature`+`money`; 合わせ鏡 → `daily-life`+`tool`).

**Then the diff was audited against the entries, and that caught 8 more.** Reading the entry
*list* before migrating is not the same as reading the *diff* afterwards. The clearest miss was
`place`→`building` turning **パリ** into a building; also `environment`→`nature` on 焼却場 (an
incinerator plant is not about nature), `material`→`nature` on 銅板, `literature`→`art` on
好色/典故/発行所, and `academic`→`cognition` on 連関. **Every one was in the context-dependent
class; zero forced renames were wrong.**
