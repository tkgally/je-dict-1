### Notes
- Self-referential verbal noun patterns (e.g., 予約した in the 予約 entry) are left unlinked
- Compound words containing the headword (e.g., バスタオル, ハンドタオル, ガムテープ, ソフトクリーム) are left unlinked as they contain the self-referential headword
- で after na-adjective (e.g., 心配で) is left unlinked since it's the te-form of the copula, not the particle で
