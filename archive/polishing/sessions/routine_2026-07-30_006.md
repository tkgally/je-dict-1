## §4 self-check outcome — 16 flagged, 9 applied, 7 rejected (**it caught four of this run's own slips**)

The 43 entries changed outside the accuracy pass's range were sent back for an independent
`--dimensions tags` check. This was not a formality — the model caught four migrations of mine
that were mechanically valid and semantically wrong:

| Entry | My migration | Why it was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 22826 再生紙 | `environment`→`nature` | recycled paper is not a nature word → `daily-life` |
| 22869 マグ | `food-drink`→`food` | a mug is drinkware, not food → `tool`, `daily-life` |
| 22892 髭剃り | `body`→`body-part` | a razor/shaving is not a body part → `daily-life`, `tool` |
| 22620 招き入れる | `social`→`society` | a verb needs `action`; the domain tag alone mislabels it |

I had flagged the first as "a stretch" when applying it and applied it anyway. The general
lesson: a 1:1 tag map is a claim about the *tag*, not about the *entry*, and the two come apart
on exactly the entries where the original tag was already loose. Also applied: 3 off-vocab tags
the check resolved (`emergency`, `interpersonal`, `philosophy`), 1 wrong-category tag
(`communication` on 手を貸す), and 22678's politeness.

Rejected 7 as additive or narrowness nits between in-list tags (`add action` to 分娩/永続/頬杖をつく,
`grammatical`→`descriptive` on まだしも, removing `body-part` from 末梢 whose gloss carries the
anatomical sense, removing `time-general` from こまめに, `transportation`→`building` on ガード
whose sense 2 is a railway overpass).

## The `general` family, rejected as a group

About a quarter of the reviewer's tag suggestions were "replace `location` / `place` /
`position` / `object` / `space` / `status` / `document` with `general`". Applying them would
trade a descriptive tag for the catch-all and inflate the `tag-sole-general` detector's queue —
a conformance gain paid for with information loss. All rejected as one family and left in
`needs_curator.txt`, because the real question is a taxonomy one (does `VALID_SEMANTIC` want a
spatial-position tag?) that deserves a single decision rather than 7 entry-by-entry
substitutions.
