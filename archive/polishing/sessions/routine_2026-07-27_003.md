### Beyond the wrapper family

1. **22070's whole-word headword wrapper had silently corrupted its conjugation table.**
   `add_conjugations.py` strips the ichidan る from the rendered tail; a whole-word wrapper hid it,
   so all **33** generated forms doubled the stem — `{走り続ける|はしりつづける}る` rendering as
   走り続けるる, `…ます` as 走り続けるます, and so on. Present since creation (2026-04-04); the entry
   validated cleanly throughout. Rewrapped the headword, then regenerated with
   `add_conjugations.py --start 22070 --end 22070 --force`. Swept the whole dictionary for the same
   signature (`strip_furigana(forms[0].affirmative) != strip_furigana(headword)` for ichidan/godan):
   **0 remaining mismatches**, so 22070 was the only live instance.

2. **A malformed no-pipe brace span `{コンビニ}` in 22061**, surfaced by the §4 screener.
   `FURIGANA_PATTERN` requires a pipe, so such spans are never matched or stripped and render with
   **literal braces** on the live site — and no `check_furigana_format.py` subpattern can see them.
   Fixed in 22061; measured dictionary-wide at **887 instances across 616 entries**.

Both are logged as `[tooling]` observations and written up in the P9 section of
`cleanup-backlog.md`; the no-pipe family is recommended as a P9 sibling item with its own detector
subpattern (with `{1, 2, 3, ...}` set notation, `{X}` placeholders, and `{emotion}` labels as a
documented reject family — this one is not mechanically sweepable either).
