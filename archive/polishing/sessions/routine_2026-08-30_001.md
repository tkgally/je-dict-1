- `[tooling]` The deep furigana pass is unusable inside a Routine run (~15 min per
  entry via gemini-2.5-pro, vs ~6 entries/min for screening and accuracy in the
  same session). Needs a faster second model, a per-entry timeout, or demotion to
  a curator-only tool.

  tag for stative verbs; it draws repeated flags whose replacements are all
  wrong. Needs a taxonomy decision.
