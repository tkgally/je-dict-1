## Main finding — off-vocabulary semantic tags

**315 of the 592 entries (53%)** carried semantic tags outside `VALID_SEMANTIC`:
374 occurrences across 142 distinct tags. Per the §A semantic-tag policy
("not in the list is correct by definition"), all were migrated:

- Applied `check_tag_drift.py`'s 1:1 map where it covered the tag (72 occurrences).
- Built the rest of the mapping by hand from the 81-tag taxonomy — almost all were
  plural or near-synonym variants (`people`→`person`, `plants`→`plant-general`,
  `tools`→`tool`, `emotions`→`emotion`, `arts`→`art`, `medical`→`health`,
  `architecture`/`construction`/`housing`→`building`, and so on).
- Dropped tags with no taxonomy equivalent (`object`, `materials`, `safety`,
  `event`, `disaster`, `position`, …) rather than force a wrong in-list tag.
- Hand-assigned a specific tag to the 23 entries that dropping would have left with
  no semantic tag at all (e.g. 24308 石材 → `building`, 24451 発根 → `plant-general`,
  24323 最後尾 → `direction`), so nothing was pushed to a bare `general`.

After migration: `validate.py` 592/592 valid, `validate_tags.py` reports no unknown
