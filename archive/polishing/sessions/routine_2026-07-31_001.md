## §4 self-check — 25 flagged, **12 applied**, 13 rejected (it caught a real content loss)

The 122 changed entries went back for an independent `--dimensions tags` pass. The most important
catch was not a tag-vocabulary question:

- **22842 箱入り** — I migrated `[daily-life, interpersonal]` → `[daily-life]`. The entry has a
  **second sense, "sheltered; overprotected"** (箱入り娘), and `interpersonal`, off-vocabulary as it
  was, had been the only tag carrying it. Dropping it silently deleted a sense from the tag record.
  Restored as `[daily-life, personality]`. The general lesson extends the previous run's: an
  off-vocab tag is not only sometimes *wrong about the entry*, it is sometimes the **only tag

  checked, not just the gloss.
