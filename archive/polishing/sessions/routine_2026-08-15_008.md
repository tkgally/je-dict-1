### Applied — example and translation fixes (3)

- **11695 {今日|こんにち}** — the reviewer flagged the first example's English; checking it showed a
  larger problem. Both examples for sense 1 (「今日はここまでにしましょう」, 「今日の会議はこれで
  終了いたします」) are read きょう, not こんにち, so the entry was teaching a wrong reading.
  Replaced with two sentences where こんにち is unambiguous (「今日あるのは皆さんのおかげです」,
  「会社が今日まで続いたのは社員のおかげだ」) and added a notes line warning that everyday
  calendar-day sentences take きょう. The sense split itself is flagged for the curator (below).
- **11848 {勝|まさ}る** — example 3 mashed two unrelated set phrases together (「百聞は一見に勝る
  とも劣らない経験だった」 crosses the proverb 百聞は一見に如かず with 勝るとも劣らない and does not
  parse). Replaced with a clean 勝るとも劣らない sentence.
- **11919 {十字|じゅうじ}** — example 3's English said "in an X-shape"; 十字 is a cross/plus shape,
  which is the point of the headword. Reworded.
