りんご

りんご
noun
apple

Definitions

1. apple
A round fruit with red or green skin.

Examples

りんごを食(た)べる。
To eat an apple.
りんごは赤(あか)い。
Apples are red.

Notes

りんご means apple.

KANJI:
Can be written as 林檎(りんご) but hiragana is more common.

PRODUCTION:
Aomori Prefecture is famous for apple production in Japan.

COMPOUNDS:

  • りんごジュース: apple juice
  • りんご飴(あめ): candied apple
