～製(せい)

せい
suffix
made in, manufactured by

Definitions

1. made in, manufactured by
Indicates the country of manufacture or the material something is made from.

Examples

これは日本(にほん)製の車(くるま)です。
This is a car made in Japan.

Notes

EXAMPLES:

  • 日本(にほん)製: made in Japan
  • 外国(がいこく)製: foreign-made
  • 手(て)製: handmade
  • 木(き)製: made of wood
