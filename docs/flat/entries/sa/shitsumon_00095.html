質問(しつもん)

しつもん
noun
question

Definitions

1. question
Something asked to get information or clarification. Used with the verb する to mean 'to ask a question'.

Examples

質問(しつもん)がありますか。
Do you have any questions?
Common classroom phrase
先生(せんせい)に質問(しつもん)しました。
I asked the teacher a question.
いい質問(しつもん)ですね。
That's a good question.

Notes

Forms a verb with する: 質問(しつもん)する (to ask a question). The answer to a question is 答(こた)え or 返事(へんじ).

Related: 質問者(しつもんしゃ) (questioner).
