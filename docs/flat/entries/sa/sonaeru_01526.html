具(そな)える

そなえる
ichidan verb
to be equipped with, to possess

Definitions

1. to be equipped with, to possess
To have something as a built-in feature or quality.

Examples

才能(さいのう)を具えている。
To possess talent.

Notes

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 具(そな)える: to be equipped with (innate quality)
  • 備(そな)える: to prepare, provide, equip
  • 供(そな)える: to offer (to gods/ancestors)

The 具える form emphasizes inherent qualities.
