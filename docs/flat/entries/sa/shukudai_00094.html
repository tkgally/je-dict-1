宿題(しゅくだい)

しゅくだい
noun
homework

Definitions

1. homework, assignment
Work assigned by a teacher to be done outside of class. A fundamental part of Japanese education.
2. unfinished task, pending issue
Can also refer to something left undone that needs to be addressed, like 'homework' from a meeting or project.

Examples

宿題(しゅくだい)はもう終(お)わりましたか。
Have you finished your homework already?
宿題(しゅくだい)を忘(わす)れてしまいました。
I forgot my homework.
毎日(まいにち)宿題(しゅくだい)がたくさんあります。
There's a lot of homework every day.

Notes

宿題(しゅくだい)をする means 'to do homework'. Japanese schools typically assign homework even during summer vacation (夏休(なつやす)みの宿題(しゅくだい)). The kanji 宿(しゅく) means 'lodging/home' and 題(だい) means 'topic/problem'.
