様々(さまざま)

さまざま
na-adjective, noun
various, diverse

Definitions

1. various, varied, diverse
Indicates many different kinds or types of something. Emphasizes variety and diversity.

Examples

様々(さまざま)な意見(いけん)がありました。
There were various opinions.
この店(みせ)には様々(さまざま)な商品(しょうひん)があります。
This store has various products.
人(ひと)は様々(さまざま)です。
People are diverse.
Predicate use

Notes

様々(さまざま) is a na-adjective meaning various or diverse.

FORMS:

  • Noun modifier: 様々(さまざま)な + noun
  • Predicate: noun + は様々(さまざま)だ

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 様々(さまざま)な種類(しゅるい) (various kinds)
  • 様々(さまざま)な理由(りゆう) (various reasons)
  • 様々(さまざま)な問題(もんだい) (various problems)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いろいろ: various (casual, more common in speech)
  • 様々(さまざま): various (slightly more formal)
  • 多様(たよう): diverse (formal, emphasizes diversity)
