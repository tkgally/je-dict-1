七月(しちがつ)

しちがつ
noun
July

Definitions

1. July
The seventh month of the year. The rainy season ends and summer begins in earnest. Features Tanabata (July 7th).

Examples

七月(しちがつ)は暑(あつ)いです。
July is hot.
七月(しちがつ)七日(なのか)は七夕(たなばた)です。
July 7th is Tanabata.
七月(しちがつ)に夏休(なつやす)みが始(はじ)まります。
Summer vacation starts in July.

Notes

July uses しち (7), not なな. However, some people say なながつ to avoid confusion with いちがつ (January) when speaking. Tanabata (七夕, Star Festival) on July 7th celebrates the annual meeting of the stars Orihime and Hikoboshi.
