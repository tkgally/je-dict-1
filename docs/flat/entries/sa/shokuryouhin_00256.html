食料品(しょくりょうひん)

しょくりょうひん
noun
groceries, food products

Definitions

1. groceries, food products, foodstuffs
Items of food for purchase.

Examples

食料品(しょくりょうひん)を買(か)いに行(い)きます。
I'm going to buy groceries.
食料品(しょくりょうひん)売(う)り場(ば)はどこですか。
Where is the food section?

Notes

Commonly seen in department stores and supermarkets. Related: 食料品店(しょくりょうひんてん) - grocery store.
