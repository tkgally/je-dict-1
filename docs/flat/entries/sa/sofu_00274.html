祖父(そふ)

そふ
noun
grandfather

Definitions

1. grandfather
The father of one's parent.

Examples

祖父(そふ)は医者(いしゃ)だった。
My grandfather was a doctor.
祖父(そふ)から昔(むかし)の話(はなし)を聞(き)いた。
I heard stories about the old days from my grandfather.

Notes

Formal term for one's own grandfather. Casual: おじいちゃん, おじいさん. For someone else's grandfather, use おじいさま (polite) or おじいさん.
