三千(さんぜん)

さんぜん
number
three thousand

Definitions

1. three thousand
The number three thousand. Note the sound change: さん + せん becomes さんぜん (the せ becomes voiced to ぜ).

Examples

この時計(とけい)は三千(さんぜん)円(えん)でした。
This watch was three thousand yen.
富士山(ふじさん)は三千(さんぜん)メートル以上(いじょう)あります。
Mt. Fuji is over three thousand meters tall.
三千(さんぜん)年(ねん)の歴史(れきし)があります。
It has three thousand years of history.

Notes

Three thousand has a sound change where せん becomes ぜん. This is called rendaku (sequential voicing). Similarly, 八千(はっせん) (8000) has a different change where the reading becomes はっせん. Other thousands (2000, 4000, 5000, 6000, 7000, 9000) keep the regular せん sound.
