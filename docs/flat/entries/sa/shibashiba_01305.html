しばしば

しばしば
adverb
often, frequently

Definitions

1. often, frequently
Happening many times.

Examples

しばしば訪(おとず)れる。
To visit frequently.
この問題(もんだい)はしばしば起(お)こる。
This problem occurs frequently.

Notes

しばしば is literary/formal.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • よく: often (common)
  • 頻繁(ひんぱん)に: frequently (formal)
  • たびたび: often, repeatedly

REGISTER:
しばしば is more formal than よく.
