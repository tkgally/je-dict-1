スプーン

すぷーん
noun
spoon

Definitions

1. spoon
A utensil with a shallow bowl-shaped end used for eating, stirring, and serving food. Borrowed from English.

Examples

スプーンをください。
Please give me a spoon.
スープはスプーンで食(た)べます。
You eat soup with a spoon.
スプーンとフォークがあります。
There are spoons and forks.

Notes

A loanword from English. Written in katakana. Used alongside traditional Japanese utensils (箸(はし) - chopsticks). Western-style restaurants provide スプーン and フォーク (fork).
