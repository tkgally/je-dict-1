すれ違(ちが)う

すれちがう
verb (godan)
to pass by one another, to miss each other

Definitions

1. to pass by one another
To go past each other from opposite directions.
2. to miss each other
To fail to meet or understand each other.

Examples

道(みち)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other on the road.
気持(きも)ちがすれ違(ちが)う。
Our feelings don't connect.

Notes

すれ違(ちが)う means to pass by.

USAGE:

  • Physical: passing by each other
  • Figurative: misunderstanding, not connecting

RELATED:

  • すれ違(ちが)い: passing each other, miscommunication
