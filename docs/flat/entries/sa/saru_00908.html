去(さ)る

さる
verb (godan)
to leave, to go away, to depart

Definitions

1. to leave, to go away
To depart from a place.
2. to pass, to elapse
For time to go by.

Examples

彼(かれ)は町(まち)を去(さ)った。
He left the town.
去(さ)る者(もの)は追(お)わず。
Don't chase after those who leave.
Proverb

Notes

去(さ)る means to leave.

PROVERBS:

  • 去(さ)る者(もの)は追(お)わず: Don't chase those who leave
  • 去(さ)る者(もの)日日(ひび)に疎(うと)し: Out of sight, out of mind

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 去(さ)る: to leave (verb)
  • 猿(さる): monkey (noun)
