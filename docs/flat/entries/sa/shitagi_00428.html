下着(したぎ)

したぎ
noun
underwear

Definitions

1. underwear, undergarments
Clothing worn under outer garments, next to the skin.

Examples

下着(したぎ)を買(か)いに行(い)きます。
I'm going to buy underwear.
新(あたら)しい下着(したぎ)に着替(きが)えた。
I changed into fresh underwear.
下着(したぎ)は毎日(まいにち)洗(あら)います。
I wash my underwear every day.

Notes

下着(したぎ) is a general term for underwear, covering all types of undergarments.

TYPES OF UNDERWEAR:

  • パンツ: underpants, briefs
  • ブラジャー: bra
  • シャツ: undershirt
  • 肌着|はだぎ}: underwear worn next to skin

RELATED TERMS:

  • 上着(うわぎ): jacket, outer garment
  • 服(ふく): clothes (general)
  • 着替(きが)え: change of clothes

Note: 下着(したぎ) is a compound of 下(した) (under) + 着(ぎ) (wearing/clothes).
