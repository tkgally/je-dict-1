失敗(しっぱい)

しっぱい
noun, verb (suru)
failure, mistake

Definitions

1. failure, blunder, mistake
Not achieving a desired outcome; making an error or mistake in an attempt.

Examples

試験(しけん)に失敗(しっぱい)しました。
I failed the exam.
失敗(しっぱい)は成功(せいこう)のもとです。
Failure is the mother of success.
Common proverb
また失敗(しっぱい)してしまった。
I made a mistake again.

Notes

失敗(しっぱい) is the opposite of 成功(せいこう) (success).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail
  • 失敗(しっぱい)に終(お)わる: to end in failure
  • 〜に失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail at ~

RELATED TERMS:

  • 成功(せいこう): success
  • 間違(まちが)い: mistake, error
  • ミス: mistake (loanword)
  • 落(お)ちる: to fail (an exam)

PROVERB: 失敗(しっぱい)は成功(せいこう)のもと - Failure leads to success (equivalent to 'learn from your mistakes')
