育(そだ)つ

そだつ
verb (godan)
to grow up, to be raised

Definitions

1. to grow up, to be raised
To develop from childhood to adulthood.
2. to grow, to develop
To become bigger or more mature.

Examples

田舎(いなか)で育(そだ)った。
I grew up in the countryside.
野菜(やさい)がよく育(そだ)つ。
The vegetables grow well.
愛情(あいじょう)をもって育(そだ)てられた。
I was raised with love.

Notes

育(そだ)つ means to grow up.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 育(そだ)つ (intransitive): to grow up
  • 育(そだ)てる (transitive): to raise

PATTERN:

  • Xで育(そだ)つ: to grow up in X
