裂(さ)ける

さける
ichidan verb, intransitive
to tear, to split, to rip

Definitions

1. to tear, to split
To come apart or be divided.

Examples

布(ぬの)が裂(さ)けた。
The cloth tore.
傷口(きずぐち)が裂(さ)けた。
The wound split open.

Notes

裂(さ)ける is intransitive (happens by itself).

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 裂(さ)く: to tear (something)

DISTINCTION FROM 避(さ)ける:

  • 裂(さ)ける: to tear/split
  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid
