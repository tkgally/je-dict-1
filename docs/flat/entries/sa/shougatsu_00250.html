正月(しょうがつ)

しょうがつ
noun
New Year

Definitions

1. New Year, New Year's
The first month of the year, especially the first few days.

Examples

正月(しょうがつ)は家族(かぞく)と過(す)ごします。
I spend New Year's with my family.
お正月(しょうがつ)に神社(じんじゃ)に行(い)った。
I went to a shrine for New Year's.

Notes

Often prefixed with お (お正月(しょうがつ)) for politeness. New Year's is the most important holiday in Japan. Related: 正月(しょうがつ)休(やす)み - New Year's holiday.
