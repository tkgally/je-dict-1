ぜひ

ぜひ
adverb
by all means, definitely

Definitions

1. by all means, definitely
Expresses strong desire or recommendation. Used to strongly encourage someone or express earnest hope.

Examples

ぜひ来(き)てください。
Please do come.
Strong invitation
ぜひ食(た)べてみてください。
You really should try eating this.
ぜひ日本(にほん)に行(い)きたいです。
I definitely want to go to Japan.
Strong personal desire

Notes

Can be written as 是非(ぜひ) in kanji but hiragana is more common. Adds emphasis and sincerity to requests, invitations, or expressions of desire. Often used when inviting someone or recommending something.
