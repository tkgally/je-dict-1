備(そな)える

そなえる
verb (ichidan)
to prepare, to furnish, to equip

Definitions

1. to prepare for, to provide against
To get ready for a future event or emergency.
2. to equip, to furnish
To provide with necessary items or features.

Examples

地震(じしん)に備(そな)える。
To prepare for an earthquake.
才能(さいのう)を備(そな)えている。
To be equipped with talent.

Notes

備(そな)える means to prepare.

PATTERN:

  • 〜に備(そな)える: to prepare for ~
  • 〜を備(そな)える: to be equipped with ~

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 備(そな)える: to prepare, to equip
  • 供(そな)える: to offer (to gods/ancestors)
