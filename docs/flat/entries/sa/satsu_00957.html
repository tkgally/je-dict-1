札(さつ)

さつ
noun
bill, banknote, paper money

Definitions

1. bill, banknote
Paper currency issued by a government.

Examples

千円札(せんえんさつ)で払(はら)う。
To pay with a 1000-yen bill.
札(さつ)を崩(くず)す。
To break a bill (get change).

Notes

札(さつ) refers to paper money.

JAPANESE BILLS:

  • 千円札(せんえんさつ): 1,000 yen bill
  • 五千円札(ごせんえんさつ): 5,000 yen bill
  • 一万円札(いちまんえんさつ): 10,000 yen bill

NOTE:
Also read as 札(ふだ) meaning tag or label.
