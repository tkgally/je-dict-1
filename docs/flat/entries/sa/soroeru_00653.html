揃(そろ)える

そろえる
verb (ichidan)
to arrange, to collect

Definitions

1. to arrange, to put in order
To line things up neatly.
2. to collect, to gather
To bring together a complete set.

Examples

靴(くつ)を揃(そろ)える。
To arrange shoes neatly.
道具(どうぐ)を揃(そろ)える。
To gather the necessary tools.
息(いき)を揃(そろ)える。
To synchronize breathing.

Notes

揃(そろ)える means to arrange or collect.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 揃(そろ)える (transitive): to arrange
  • 揃(そろ)う (intransitive): to be complete

COMMON USES:

  • 靴(くつ)を揃(そろ)える: to arrange shoes
  • 材料(ざいりょう)を揃(そろ)える: to gather ingredients
