十一(じゅういち)

じゅういち
number
eleven

Definitions

1. eleven
The number eleven. Formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 一(いち) (one).

Examples

今(いま)十一(じゅういち)時(じ)です。
It's eleven o'clock now.
十一(じゅういち)月(がつ)は秋(あき)です。
November is autumn.
November
学生(がくせい)が十一(じゅういち)人(にん)います。
There are eleven students.

Notes

Japanese numbers 11-19 follow a simple pattern: 十(じゅう) + single digit. So 11 is simply 'ten-one.' This makes the Japanese number system very regular and easy to learn.
