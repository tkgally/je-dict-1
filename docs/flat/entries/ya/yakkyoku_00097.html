薬局(やっきょく)

やっきょく
noun
pharmacy

Definitions

1. pharmacy, drugstore
A store where medicines are sold and prescriptions are filled.

Examples

薬局(やっきょく)で薬(くすり)を買(か)いました。
I bought medicine at the pharmacy.
この処方箋(しょほうせん)を薬局(やっきょく)に持(も)っていってください。
Please take this prescription to the pharmacy.
近(ちか)くに薬局(やっきょく)はありますか。
Is there a pharmacy nearby?

Notes

薬局 are pharmacies that can fill prescriptions. ドラッグストア (drugstore) refers to larger stores selling over-the-counter medicines, cosmetics, and daily goods. Many pharmacies in Japan are open late and some are attached to clinics.
