なぜ

なぜ
noun
why

Definitions

1. why, how come
Used to ask for the reason or cause of something.

Examples

なぜ遅(おく)れたのですか。
Why were you late?
なぜそう思(おも)うのですか。
Why do you think so?

Notes

More formal than どうして. Can be written as 何故. Also commonly used as an adverb.
