流(なが)す

ながす
verb (godan)
to let flow, to wash away

Definitions

1. to let flow, to pour
To cause liquid to move.
2. to wash away, to flush
To remove by flowing water.

Examples

涙(なみだ)を流(なが)す。
To shed tears.
水(みず)を流(なが)す。
To flush water.
汗(あせ)を流(なが)す。
To sweat.

Notes

流(なが)す means to let flow or wash away.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 流(なが)す (transitive): to let flow
  • 流(なが)れる (intransitive): to flow

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 涙(なみだ)を流(なが)す: to shed tears
  • 汗(あせ)を流(なが)す: to sweat
