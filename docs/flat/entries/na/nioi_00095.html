匂(にお)い

におい
noun
smell, scent

Definitions

1. smell, scent, odor
Something perceived by the nose. Can be pleasant or unpleasant depending on context.

Examples

いい匂(にお)いがします。
It smells good.
がする = to perceive a smell
この花(はな)の匂(にお)いが好(す)きです。
I like the smell of this flower.
変(へん)な匂(にお)いがしますね。
There's a strange smell, isn't there?

Notes

Often used with がする to express smelling something: いい匂(にお)いがする (smells good), 変(へん)な匂(にお)いがする (smells strange). Can also be written as 臭い but 匂(にお)い is more neutral while 臭い often implies bad smell.
