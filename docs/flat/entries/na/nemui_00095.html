眠(ねむ)い

ねむい
adjective (i-adjective)
sleepy

Definitions

1. sleepy, drowsy
Feeling the need or desire to sleep. The state of being tired and wanting to sleep.

Examples

とても眠(ねむ)いです。
I'm very sleepy.
昨日(きのう)よく眠(ねむ)れなかったから眠(ねむ)いです。
I'm sleepy because I couldn't sleep well yesterday.
眠(ねむ)くなってきました。
I'm getting sleepy.
～くなる for becoming

Notes

Related to the verb 眠(ねむ)る (to sleep). 眠(ねむ)くなる means 'to become sleepy'. Different from 疲(つか)れた (tired) which is more about physical exhaustion than wanting to sleep.
