羨(うらや)ましい

うらやましい
i-adjective
envious, enviable

Definitions

1. envious, jealous
Feeling desire for what others have.
2. enviable
Deserving envy.

Examples

彼(かれ)がうらやましい。
I'm envious of him.
うらやましい生活(せいかつ)だ。
It's an enviable lifestyle.

Notes

うらやましい describes feeling envy (not jealousy in romance).

USAGE:

  • 〜がうらやましい: to envy ~

RELATED:

  • 羨(うらや)む: to envy (verb)
  • 嫉妬(しっと): jealousy (stronger, negative)
