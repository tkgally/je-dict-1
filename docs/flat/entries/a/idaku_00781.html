抱(いだ)く

いだく
verb (godan)
to hold, to embrace, to harbor

Definitions

1. to hold, to embrace
To hold someone or something in one's arms (literary).
2. to harbor, to have (feelings)
To have feelings, thoughts, or ideas.

Examples

夢(ゆめ)を抱(いだ)く。
To have a dream.
疑問(ぎもん)を抱(いだ)いた。
I harbored doubts.
希望(きぼう)を抱(いだ)いて生(い)きる。
To live with hope.

Notes

抱(いだ)く is a literary/formal verb.

COMMON OBJECTS:

  • 夢(ゆめ)を抱(いだ)く: to have a dream
  • 疑問(ぎもん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor doubts
  • 希望(きぼう)を抱(いだ)く: to have hope
  • 不安(ふあん)を抱(いだ)く: to feel anxiety

RELATED:

  • 抱(だ)く: to hold (more common for physical holding)
  • 抱(かか)える: to carry in arms
