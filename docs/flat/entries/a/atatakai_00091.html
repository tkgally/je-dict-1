暖(あたた)かい

あたたかい
adjective (i-adjective)
warm

Definitions

1. warm (weather, temperature)
Describes pleasantly warm weather or temperature. Written 暖かい for weather/climate.
2. warm (feeling, personality)
Describes a warm, kind personality or warm feelings. Can also be written 温かい for this meaning.

Examples

今日(きょう)は暖(あたた)かいですね。
It's warm today, isn't it?
春(はる)は暖(あたた)かくなります。
It gets warm in spring.
暖(あたた)かい家族(かぞく)です。
They are a warm family.
Warm personality

Notes

暖かい (with 暖, meaning 'warmth') is used for weather and climate. 温かい (with 温, meaning 'temperature') is used for warm objects, food, or figuratively for warm personalities. Both are read あたたかい. Also commonly shortened to あったかい in casual speech.
