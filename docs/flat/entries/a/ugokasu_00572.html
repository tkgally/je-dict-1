動(うご)かす

うごかす
verb (godan)
to move, to operate

Definitions

1. to move, to shift
To change the position of something.
2. to operate, to run
To make a machine or system work.

Examples

机(つくえ)を動(うご)かす。
To move the desk.
機械(きかい)を動(うご)かす。
To operate a machine.
心(こころ)を動(うご)かされた。
I was moved (emotionally).

Notes

動(うご)かす means to move or operate.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 動(うご)かす (transitive): to move something
  • 動(うご)く (intransitive): to move

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 心(こころ)を動(うご)かす: to move someone's heart
  • 体(からだ)を動(うご)かす: to exercise
