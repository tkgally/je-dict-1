一方(いっぽう)

いっぽう
noun/conjunction
one side, on the other hand, while

Definitions

1. one side, one party
One of two sides or parties.
2. on the other hand, while, meanwhile
Used to contrast two things or indicate simultaneous actions.
3. only, keep ~ing
Used after verb dictionary form to indicate continuous action in one direction.

Examples

一方(いっぽう)では賛成(さんせい)、一方(いっぽう)では反対(はんたい)だ。
On one hand there's agreement, on the other hand opposition.
彼(かれ)は勉強(べんきょう)する一方(いっぽう)で、スポーツもする。
While he studies, he also does sports.
Conjunctive usage
物価(ぶっか)は上(あ)がる一方(いっぽう)だ。
Prices keep going up.
Verb + 一方(いっぽう)だ pattern

Notes

一方(いっぽう) has multiple uses as noun, conjunction, and in grammar patterns.

AS NOUN:

  • 一方(いっぽう)の (one side's)
  • 一方(いっぽう)だけ (only one side)

AS CONJUNCTION:

  • 一方(いっぽう)で (on the other hand, while)
  • Connects contrasting or simultaneous facts

GRAMMAR PATTERN:

  • Verb dictionary form + 一方(いっぽう)だ
  • Indicates continuous change in one direction
  • Example: 増(ふ)える一方(いっぽう)だ (keeps increasing)
