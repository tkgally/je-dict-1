集(あつ)まり

あつまり
noun
gathering, meeting, collection

Definitions

1. gathering, meeting
An occasion when people come together. An assembly or get-together.
2. collection, group
A set of things or people gathered in one place.

Examples

今日(きょう)は友達(ともだち)の集(あつ)まりがあります。
There's a gathering with friends today.
良(よ)い人(ひと)の集(あつ)まりです。
It's a group of good people.
集(あつ)まりが悪(わる)いですね。
The turnout is poor.
Common expression

Notes

集(あつ)まり is the noun form of 集(あつ)まる (to gather).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)の集(あつ)まり (a gathering of people)
  • 集(あつ)まりが良(よ)い/悪(わる)い (good/poor turnout)
  • 集(あつ)まりに参加(さんか)する (participate in a gathering)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 集(あつ)まる: to gather (intransitive verb)
  • 集(あつ)める: to collect (transitive verb)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会合(かいごう): meeting (formal)
  • 集(あつ)まり: gathering (casual)
  • 集会(しゅうかい): assembly, rally
