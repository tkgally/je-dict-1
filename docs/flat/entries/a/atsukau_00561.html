扱(あつか)う

あつかう
verb (godan)
to handle, to deal with

Definitions

1. to handle, to treat, to deal with
To manage or work with something or someone.

Examples

この機械(きかい)は丁寧(ていねい)に扱(あつか)ってください。
Please handle this machine carefully.
彼(かれ)を子供(こども)扱(あつか)いしないで。
Don't treat him like a child.
この店(みせ)は輸入品(ゆにゅうひん)を扱(あつか)っています。
This store deals in imported goods.

Notes

扱(あつか)う means to handle or treat.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を扱(あつか)う: to handle ~
  • 〜扱(あつか)いする: to treat as ~
  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)う: to deal with, handle

RELATED TERMS:

  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)い: handling, treatment
  • 扱(あつか)い方(かた): way of handling
