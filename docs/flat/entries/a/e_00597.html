柄(え)

え
noun
handle, grip, shaft

Definitions

1. handle, grip
The part of a tool or utensil that is held.
2. shaft, stem
The long part connecting the working end to the handle.

Examples

傘(かさ)の柄(え)を握(にぎ)る。
To grip the umbrella handle.
刀(かたな)の柄(え)が美(うつく)しい。
The sword's hilt is beautiful.
柄(え)の長(なが)いスプーン。
A long-handled spoon.

Notes

柄(え) refers to handles or grips.

READINGS:

  • え: handle, grip (most common)
  • がら: pattern, design; build, character (different meaning)

COMMON USES:

  • 傘(かさ)の柄(え): umbrella handle
  • 刀(かたな)の柄(え): sword hilt
  • 鍋(なべ)の柄(え): pot handle
  • 斧(おの)の柄(え): axe handle

NOTE:

  • When read as がら, means pattern or character (人柄(ひとがら), 柄(がら))
