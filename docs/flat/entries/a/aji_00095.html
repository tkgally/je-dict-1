味(あじ)

あじ
noun
taste, flavor

Definitions

1. taste, flavor
The sensation perceived by the tongue when eating or drinking something. Can refer to sweet, salty, sour, bitter, or umami flavors.

Examples

この料理(りょうり)は味(あじ)がいいです。
This dish has good flavor.
味(あじ)はどうですか。
How does it taste?
Common way to ask about food
もう少(すこ)し塩(しお)の味(あじ)がほしいです。
I want a bit more salty taste.

Notes

Often used with がする to describe experiencing a taste: 甘(あま)い味(あじ)がする (it tastes sweet). Can also be used metaphorically to describe the 'flavor' or character of something.
