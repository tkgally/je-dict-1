家(いえ)

いえ
noun
house, home

Definitions

1. house, home
A building for dwelling; one's residence.

Examples

家(いえ)に帰(かえ)ります。
I'm going home.
大(おお)きい家(いえ)に住(す)んでいます。
I live in a big house.
家(いえ)で勉強(べんきょう)します。
I study at home.

Notes

Also read as うち (more colloquial/casual).

Related:

  • 部屋(へや) (room)
  • 家族(かぞく) (family).
