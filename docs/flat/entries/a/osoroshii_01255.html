恐(おそ)ろしい

おそろしい
i-adjective
frightening, terrible

Definitions

1. frightening, terrifying
Causing fear.
2. terrible, awful
Extremely bad or serious.
3. amazing, incredible
Extraordinary (colloquial intensifier).

Examples

恐ろしい夢(ゆめ)を見(み)た。
I had a frightening dream.
恐ろしいスピードで走(はし)る。
To run at a terrific speed.

Notes

恐ろしい is a strong adjective.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怖(こわ)い: scary, afraid (more common)
  • 恐(きょう)怖(ふ): fear, terror (noun)

INTENSIFIER USE:

  • 恐ろしく + adjective: terribly ~
