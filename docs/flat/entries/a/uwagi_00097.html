上着(うわぎ)

うわぎ
noun
jacket, coat

Definitions

1. jacket, coat, outerwear
An outer garment worn over other clothes. Literally means 'upper garment.'

Examples

寒(さむ)いから上着(うわぎ)を着(き)てください。
Please put on a jacket because it's cold.
上着(うわぎ)を脱(ぬ)いでもいいですか。
May I take off my jacket?
上着(うわぎ)はどこにかけましょうか。
Where should I hang my jacket?

Notes

上着 is a general term for outer garments like jackets, blazers, and coats. The word combines 上 (upper) and 着 (clothing). It contrasts with 下着 (underwear).
