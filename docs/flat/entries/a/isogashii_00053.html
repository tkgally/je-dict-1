忙(いそが)しい

いそがしい
i-adjective
busy

Definitions

1. busy
Having a lot to do; occupied with tasks.

Examples

今日(きょう)は忙(いそが)しいです。
I'm busy today.
忙(いそが)しくて時間(じかん)がありません。
I'm too busy and don't have time.
お忙(いそが)しいところすみません。
Sorry to bother you when you're busy.
Polite expression

Notes

Antonym: 暇(ひま) (free, not busy). Common complaint in Japanese work culture.

Related Words

See also: 暇(ひま)
