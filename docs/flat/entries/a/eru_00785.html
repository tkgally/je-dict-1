得(え)る

える
verb (ichidan)
to get, to obtain, to gain

Definitions

1. to get, to obtain
To acquire something.
2. to gain, to earn
To receive as a result of effort.
3. can, to be able to (auxiliary)
Used after verb stems to indicate possibility.

Examples

知識(ちしき)を得(え)る。
To gain knowledge.
許可(きょか)を得(え)た。
I obtained permission.
あり得(え)ない。
It's impossible.
Lit: cannot exist

Notes

得(え)る has multiple uses.

AS MAIN VERB:

  • 利益(りえき)を得(え)る: to gain profit
  • 信頼(しんらい)を得(え)る: to gain trust

AS AUXILIARY (〜得(え)る):

  • あり得(え)る: possible
  • あり得(え)ない: impossible
  • 起(お)こり得(え)る: can happen

ALTERNATIVE READING:

  • 得(う)る: classical/literary form
