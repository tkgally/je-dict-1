以前(いぜん)

いぜん
noun/adverb
before, formerly, previously

Definitions

1. before, prior to
Earlier than a specified time or event.
2. formerly, in the past
At some time in the past.

Examples

以前(いぜん)ここに住(す)んでいました。
I used to live here before.
3年(ねん)以前(いぜん)の話(はなし)です。
It's a story from more than 3 years ago.
以前(いぜん)の状態(じょうたい)に戻(もど)す。
To restore to the previous state.

Notes

以前(いぜん) indicates "before" in time.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 以前(いぜん)は (before, formerly)
  • X以前(いぜん) (before X, prior to X)
  • 以前(いぜん)の + noun (former ~, previous ~)
  • 以前(いぜん)から (from before, for a while)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 以前(いぜん): before (formal, specific point)
  • 前(まえ): before, in front (common, general)
  • かつて: once, formerly (literary)

NOTE:

  • Different from 依然(いぜん) (still, as before) - same reading, different kanji
