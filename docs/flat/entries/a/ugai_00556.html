うがい

うがい
noun/suru verb
gargling

Definitions

1. gargling
The act of rinsing the mouth and throat with liquid.

Examples

うがいをする。
To gargle.
帰(かえ)ったら手洗(てあら)いとうがいをしなさい。
Wash your hands and gargle when you get home.
うがい薬(ぐすり)を使(つか)う。
To use gargling medicine.

Notes

うがい refers to gargling as a hygiene practice.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • うがいをする (to gargle)
  • うがい薬(ぐすり) (gargling medicine, mouthwash)
  • 手洗(てあら)いとうがい (handwashing and gargling)

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • Gargling is emphasized as a health practice in Japan
  • Common advice: うがい、手洗(てあら)い (gargle, wash hands)
  • Especially promoted during cold/flu season
