オーバー

おーばー
noun
overcoat, over

Definitions

1. overcoat
A long warm coat.
2. over, exceeding
Going beyond a limit.

Examples

オーバーを着(き)て出(で)かけた。
I went out wearing an overcoat.
予算(よさん)オーバーだ。
It's over budget.

Notes

From English "over(coat)."
