あげる

あげる
verb (ichidan)
to give, to raise

Definitions

1. to give (to someone of equal/lower status)
Used when giving something to someone else. The giver is typically the speaker or someone close to the speaker. Not used when giving to superiors.
2. to raise, to lift up
To move something to a higher position. Written as 上(あ)げる in this sense.

Examples

友達(ともだち)にプレゼントをあげました。
I gave a present to my friend.
Sense 1: giving
猫(ねこ)にえさをあげます。
I give food to the cat.
Often used for pets and children
手(て)を上(あ)げてください。
Please raise your hand.
Sense 2: raising

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: XがYにZをあげる (X gives Z to Y).

For the 'raise' meaning (上(あ)げる): Pair verb is 上(あ)がる (intransitive, to rise). Pattern: Xを上(あ)げる.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. あげている = 'is giving' or 'is raising.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • プレゼントをあげる (give a present)
  • 手(て)を上(あ)げる (raise one's hand)
  • 声(こえ)を上(あ)げる (raise one's voice)
  • 〜てあげる (do something for someone's benefit)

REGISTER: For giving to superiors, use 差(さ)し上(あ)げる (humble). For receiving, the counterpart is もらう.
