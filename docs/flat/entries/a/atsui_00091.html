暑(あつ)い

あつい
adjective (i-adjective)
hot (weather)

Definitions

1. hot (weather, temperature)
Used to describe hot weather or ambient temperature. For hot objects or drinks, use 熱い (atsui) with a different kanji.

Examples

今日(きょう)は暑(あつ)いですね。
It's hot today, isn't it?
夏(なつ)は暑(あつ)いです。
Summer is hot.
暑(あつ)くて眠(ねむ)れません。
It's so hot I can't sleep.

Notes

暑い (with the 'sun' radical 日) describes hot weather. Don't confuse with 熱い (with the 'fire' radical 灬) which describes hot objects or food. Both are pronounced あつい. Conjugation: 暑くない (negative), 暑かった (past).
