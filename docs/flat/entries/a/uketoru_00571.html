受(う)け取(と)る

うけとる
verb (godan)
to receive, to accept

Definitions

1. to receive, to accept
To take something that is given or sent.
2. to interpret, to take (as)
To understand or perceive something in a certain way.

Examples

荷物(にもつ)を受(う)け取(と)る。
To receive a package.
給料(きゅうりょう)を受(う)け取(と)った。
I received my salary.
冗談(じょうだん)として受(う)け取(と)ってください。
Please take it as a joke.

Notes

受(う)け取(と)る means to receive or interpret.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive a package
  • 給料(きゅうりょう)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive a salary
  • 手紙(てがみ)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive a letter

RELATED:

  • 受(う)け取(と)り: receipt
  • 受(う)ける: to receive
