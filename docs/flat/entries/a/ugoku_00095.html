動(うご)く

うごく
verb (godan)
to move

Definitions

1. to move
To change position or location. Can describe physical movement of people, animals, vehicles, or objects. Also used for machines that are operating or functioning.

Examples

動(うご)かないでください。
Please don't move.
Negative request form
この時計(とけい)は動(うご)いていません。
This clock isn't working.
Used for machines/devices functioning
電車(でんしゃ)が動(うご)き始(はじ)めました。
The train started to move.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 動(うご)かす (transitive, to move something). Pattern: Xが動(うご)く.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action or state. 動(うご)いている = 'is moving' or 'is running/working' (for machines).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)が動(うご)く (the train moves)
  • 時計(とけい)が動(うご)いている (the clock is working)
  • 動(うご)かないで (don't move)
  • 動(うご)き出(だ)す (start to move)

For machines, 動(うご)いている means 'is functioning/working.'

Related Words
