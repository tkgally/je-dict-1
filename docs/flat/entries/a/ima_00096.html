今(いま)

いま
noun, adverb
now, the present

Definitions

1. now, at this moment
Refers to the current time or moment.
2. soon, just now
Can indicate something that just happened or is about to happen.

Examples

今(いま)何時(なんじ)ですか。
What time is it now?
今(いま)行(い)きます。
I'm coming now. / I'll be right there.
Common response when called
今(いま)、何(なに)をしていますか。
What are you doing now?

Notes

今(いま) is one of the most common time expressions in Japanese. It appears in many compound words: 今日(きょう) (today), 今週(こんしゅう) (this week), 今月(こんげつ) (this month), 今年(ことし) (this year). When someone calls you, responding 今(いま)行(い)きます means you'll be there shortly.
