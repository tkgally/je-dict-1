異(い)

い
noun
objection, difference

Definitions

1. objection, dissent
A statement of disagreement or opposition.
2. difference, distinction
Something that is different or unusual.

Examples

異(い)を唱(とな)える。
To raise an objection.
Formal expression
異(い)を挟(はさ)む余地(よち)はない。
There is no room for objection.
異(い)なし。
No objection.
Formal/legal

Notes

異(い) means objection or difference.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 異(い)を唱(とな)える (to raise an objection)
  • 異(い)を挟(はさ)む (to object)
  • 異(い)なし (no objection)
  • 異(い)存(ぞん)ない (no objection - formal)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 異常(いじょう): abnormal
  • 異動(いどう): personnel transfer
  • 異議(いぎ): objection, protest
  • 異国(いこく): foreign country
