お帰(かえ)りなさい

おかえりなさい
expression
welcome back

Definitions

1. welcome back, welcome home
A set phrase said to someone who has just returned home or to a place. It is the standard response to ただいま.

Examples

「ただいま」「お帰(かえ)りなさい」
"I'm home." "Welcome back."
Standard exchange
お帰(かえ)りなさい。疲(つか)れたでしょう？
Welcome back. You must be tired.
Showing concern
お帰(かえ)り！
Welcome back!
Casual form

Notes

This is the response to ただいま. The casual form is お帰り. The even more polite form お帰りなさいませ is used by service staff in hotels or shops.
