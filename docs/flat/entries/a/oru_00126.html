折(お)る

おる
verb (godan)
to break, to fold

Definitions

1. to break, to snap
To break something by bending or applying pressure. The transitive form—someone breaks something.
2. to fold, to bend
To fold paper or other flexible materials. Used for origami and folding.

Examples

枝(えだ)を折(お)った。
I broke a branch.
紙(かみ)を折(お)って鶴(つる)を作(つく)る。
Fold paper to make a crane.
Origami context
足(あし)を折(お)ってしまった。
I broke my leg.
Used for bone fractures

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 折(お)れる (intransitive, to break/be folded). Pattern: Xを折(お)る (break/fold X).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed action with result. 折(お)っている = 'has broken/folded.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 枝(えだ)を折(お)る (break a branch)
  • 骨(ほね)を折(お)る (break a bone; also: to take pains)
  • 紙(かみ)を折(お)る (fold paper)
  • 折(お)り紙(がみ) (origami—noun)
  • 心(こころ)が折(お)れる (to lose heart—idiomatic, uses intransitive)

IDIOMS:

  • 骨(ほね)を折(お)る (to make great efforts, take pains)
  • 足(あし)を折(お)る (also: to interrupt, cut short)

HOMOPHONE: Don't confuse with おる (humble form of いる) which is different.

Related Words
