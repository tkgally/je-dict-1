糸(いと)

いと
noun
thread; string; yarn

Definitions

1. thread, string
A thin strand of fiber used for sewing or weaving.
2. string (of instrument)
The strings on a musical instrument.

Examples

針(はり)に糸(いと)を通(とお)す。
To thread a needle.
Common collocation
糸(いと)が切(き)れた。
The thread broke.
赤(あか)い糸(いと)
Red thread (of fate)
Cultural reference to fate connecting lovers

Notes

糸(いと) means 'thread' or 'string.'

Counter: 本(ほん)

Common compounds:

  • 毛糸(けいと): yarn, wool
  • 絹糸(きぬいと): silk thread
  • 縫(ぬ)い糸(いと): sewing thread

Cultural note: 赤(あか)い糸(いと) (red thread) refers to the Japanese/Chinese legend that an invisible red thread connects those destined to meet, especially romantic partners.

Related words:

  • 紐(ひも): string, cord (thicker)
  • 縄(なわ): rope (much thicker)
