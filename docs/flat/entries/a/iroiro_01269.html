色々(いろいろ)

いろいろ
na-adjective, adverb
various, many kinds of

Definitions

1. various, diverse
Of many different kinds.
2. in many ways
Adverbially, in various manners.

Examples

色々(いろいろ)な人(ひと)がいる。
There are various people.
色々(いろいろ)ありがとうございました。
Thank you for everything.

Notes

色々 can be na-adjective or adverb.

USAGE:

  • 色々な + noun: various ~
  • 色々と + verb: variously, in many ways

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 様々(さまざま): various (more formal)
  • 種々(しゅじゅ): various (formal)
