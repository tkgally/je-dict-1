思(おも)い出(だ)す

おもいだす
verb (godan)
to recall, to remember

Definitions

1. to recall, to remember, to recollect
To bring a memory back to mind. Focuses on the active process of retrieving a memory, rather than simply having memorized something.

Examples

昔(むかし)のことを思(おも)い出(だ)した。
I recalled things from the past.
を marks what is remembered
彼女(かのじょ)の名前(なまえ)を思(おも)い出(だ)せない。
I can't remember her name.
Potential negative: 思(おも)い出(だ)せない
この曲(きょく)を聞(き)くと、学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)を思(おも)い出(だ)す。
When I hear this song, I remember my student days.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを思(おも)い出(だ)す (recall X). Compound verb: 思(おも)う + 出(だ)す.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action or state. 思(おも)い出(だ)している = 'is recalling' or 'has recalled.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 昔(むかし)を思(おも)い出(だ)す (recall the past)
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)せない (can't remember)
  • ふと思(おも)い出(だ)す (suddenly remember)
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)すたびに (every time I remember)

CONTRAST WITH 覚(おぼ)える:

  • 覚(おぼ)える: to memorize, to learn (putting into memory)
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to recall (retrieving from memory)

RELATED NOUN: 思(おも)い出(で) (memory, recollection)
