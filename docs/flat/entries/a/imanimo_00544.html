今(いま)にも

いまにも
adverb
at any moment, on the verge of

Definitions

1. at any moment, about to
On the verge of happening immediately.
2. on the point of
Ready to happen at any second.

Examples

今(いま)にも泣(な)きそうだ。
He looks like he's about to cry any moment.
今(いま)にも雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうな空(そら)だ。
The sky looks like it could rain any moment.
今(いま)にも倒(たお)れそうな建物(たてもの)だ。
It's a building that looks like it could collapse any moment.

Notes

今(いま)にも expresses imminence - something about to happen.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 今(いま)にも〜そうだ (looks like it will ~ any moment)
  • 今(いま)にも〜しそう (about to ~)

COMPARISON:

  • 今(いま)に: soon, eventually (less immediate)
  • 今(いま)にも: at any moment (more immediate, imminent)

USAGE:

  • Usually followed by そう (looks like)
  • Emphasizes urgency and imminence
