噂(うわさ)

うわさ
noun/suru verb
rumor, gossip, hearsay

Definitions

1. rumor, gossip
Unverified information spread from person to person.
2. reputation, talk
What people say about someone or something.

Examples

噂(うわさ)を聞(き)いた。
I heard a rumor.
噂(うわさ)をすれば影(かげ)。
Speak of the devil.
Proverb
彼(かれ)の噂(うわさ)が広(ひろ)まっている。
Rumors about him are spreading.

Notes

噂(うわさ) means rumor or gossip.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 噂(うわさ)を聞(き)く (hear a rumor)
  • 噂(うわさ)が広(ひろ)まる (rumor spreads)
  • 噂(うわさ)になる (become the subject of rumors)
  • 噂(うわさ)をする (to gossip)

PROVERBS:

  • 噂(うわさ)をすれば影(かげ): Speak of the devil (and he shall appear)
  • 人(ひと)の噂(うわさ)も七十五日(しちじゅうごにち): Rumors die down in 75 days
