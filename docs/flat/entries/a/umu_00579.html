産(う)む

うむ
verb (godan)
to give birth, to produce

Definitions

1. to give birth, to bear
To bring forth offspring.
2. to produce, to create
To bring something into existence.

Examples

子供(こども)を産(う)む。
To give birth to a child.
卵(たまご)を産(う)む。
To lay eggs.
新(あたら)しいアイデアを産(う)む。
To produce new ideas.

Notes

産(う)む means to give birth or produce.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 産(う)む: give birth, produce
  • 生(う)む: give birth (less common)

RELATED:

  • 産(う)まれる: to be born
  • 出産(しゅっさん): childbirth
