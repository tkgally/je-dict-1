居間(いま)

いま
noun
living room

Definitions

1. living room
The main room in a house where the family gathers for everyday activities.

Examples

居間(いま)でテレビを見(み)る。
To watch TV in the living room.
居間(いま)に集(あつ)まりました。
We gathered in the living room.
居間(いま)は日当(ひあ)たりが良(よ)い。
The living room gets good sunlight.

Notes

居間(いま) is the traditional Japanese term for living room.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 居間(いま): living room (traditional term)
  • リビング: living room (loanword, common)
  • リビングルーム: living room (full loanword)
  • 茶(ちゃ)の間(ま): tea room, family room (traditional)

NOTE:

  • 居間(いま) sounds slightly formal or literary
  • リビング is more commonly used in modern Japanese
