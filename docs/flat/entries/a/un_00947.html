運(うん)

うん
noun
luck, fortune, fate

Definitions

1. luck, fortune
Good or bad chance that happens by accident.
2. fate, destiny
The course of events beyond one's control.

Examples

今日(きょう)は運(うん)がいい。
I'm lucky today.
運(うん)が悪(わる)かった。
I was unlucky.

Notes

運(うん) refers to luck or fortune.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 運(うん)がいい: lucky
  • 運(うん)が悪(わる)い: unlucky
  • 運(うん)を試(ため)す: to try one's luck

COMPOUNDS:

  • 運命(うんめい): fate, destiny
  • 幸運(こううん): good fortune
  • 不運(ふうん): misfortune
