一時(いちじ)

いちじ
noun/adverb
temporarily, for a time, one o'clock

Definitions

1. temporarily, for a time, for a while
For a limited period, not permanent.
2. at one time, once
At a certain point in the past.
3. one o'clock
The time of day.

Examples

一時(いちじ)的(てき)な措置(そち)です。
It's a temporary measure.
一時(いちじ)は諦(あきら)めようかと思(おも)った。
At one point I thought about giving up.
午後(ごご)一時(いちじ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet at 1 PM.
Time reading

Notes

一時(いちじ) has multiple uses.

AS "TEMPORARILY":

  • 一時(いちじ)的(てき)な (temporary)
  • 一時(いちじ)停止(ていし) (temporary stop, pause)
  • 一時(いちじ)帰国(きこく) (temporary return home)

AS "AT ONE TIME":

  • 一時(いちじ)は (at one time, once)
  • 一時(いちじ)〜したことがある (once did ~)

AS TIME:

  • 一時(いちじ) = 1 o'clock
  • Alternate reading: ひととき (a moment, short time)
