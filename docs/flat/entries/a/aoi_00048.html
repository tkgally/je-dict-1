青(あお)い

あおい
i-adjective
blue, green

Definitions

1. blue
Having the color of a clear sky.
2. green (for vegetation)
Traditionally used for green things like traffic lights and unripe fruit.

Examples

空(そら)が青(あお)いです。
The sky is blue.
青(あお)い信号(しんごう)で渡(わた)ります。
Cross when the light is green.
Sense 2: traffic light
海(うみ)が青(あお)くてきれいです。
The sea is blue and beautiful.

Notes

Noun form: 青(あお). Japanese traditionally used 青(あお)い for both blue and green. Modern Japanese has 緑(みどり) for green, but 青(あお)い is still used for traffic lights.
