合(あ)わせる

あわせる
verb (ichidan)
to combine, to match

Definitions

1. to combine, to put together
To join things together.
2. to match, to adjust
To make things correspond or fit together.

Examples

時計(とけい)を合(あ)わせる。
To set the clock.
力(ちから)を合(あ)わせる。
To combine efforts.
予定(よてい)を合(あ)わせましょう。
Let's coordinate our schedules.

Notes

合(あ)わせる means to combine or match.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時計(とけい)を合(あ)わせる: to set a clock
  • 力(ちから)を合(あ)わせる: to join forces
  • 目(め)を合(あ)わせる: to make eye contact

INTRANSITIVE: 合(あ)う (to match, fit)
