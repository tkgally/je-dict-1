いつまでも

いつまでも
adverb
forever, indefinitely, always

Definitions

1. forever, always
For all time, without end.
2. indefinitely, endlessly
For an extended or unlimited period.

Examples

いつまでも忘(わす)れません。
I'll never forget.
いつまでもお元気(げんき)で。
Stay healthy forever.
Common well-wish
いつまでも待(ま)っていられない。
I can't wait forever.

Notes

いつまでも expresses indefinite continuation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いつまでも + verb (forever ~)
  • いつまでも + negative (can't ~ forever)
  • いつまでもお幸(しあわ)せに (may you be happy forever)

NUANCES:

  • Positive: eternal, lasting (love, memories)
  • Negative: won't end, dragging on

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ずっと: all along, continuously
  • いつまでも: forever (emotional emphasis)
  • 永遠(えいえん)に: eternally (formal)
