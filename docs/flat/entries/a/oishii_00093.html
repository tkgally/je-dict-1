美味(おい)しい

おいしい
adjective (i-adjective)
delicious, tasty

Definitions

1. delicious, tasty
Describes food or drink that tastes good. One of the most common and useful adjectives for expressing positive opinions about food. Used in everyday conversation.

Examples

この料理(りょうり)は美味(おい)しいですね。
This dish is delicious, isn't it?
美味(おい)しいレストランを知(し)っていますか。
Do you know a good restaurant?
美味しい here means 'good' for food
お母(かあ)さんの料理(りょうり)が一(いち)番(ばん)美味(おい)しい。
Mom's cooking is the most delicious.

Notes

The kanji 美味(おい)しい is sometimes used but the word is more commonly written in hiragana (おいしい). The opposite is 不味(まず)い (mazui, bad-tasting). In casual speech, うまい can also mean delicious, though it's more masculine/casual. Saying おいしい when eating is a polite way to compliment the cook.
