写(うつ)す

うつす
verb (godan)
to copy, to photograph

Definitions

1. to copy, to transcribe
To reproduce text or content by writing or typing it. Often used for copying from a source.
2. to photograph, to film
To capture an image using a camera. To record visually.

Examples

黒板(こくばん)の字(じ)をノートに写(うつ)す。
Copy the writing on the blackboard into your notebook.
に marks the destination of the copy
友達(ともだち)の宿題(しゅくだい)を写(うつ)してはいけません。
You must not copy your friend's homework.
景色(けしき)を写(うつ)しました。
I photographed the scenery.
Photography meaning

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 写(うつ)る (intransitive, to be photographed/reflected). Pattern: Xを写(うつ)す (copy/photograph X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 写(うつ)している = 'is copying/photographing.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ノートに写(うつ)す (copy into a notebook)
  • 写真(しゃしん)を写(うつ)す (take a photograph—though 撮(と)る is more common)
  • 文章(ぶんしょう)を写(うつ)す (copy text)
  • そのまま写(うつ)す (copy exactly as is)

HOMOPHONES: Several verbs are pronounced 'うつす':

  • 写(うつ)す: to copy, photograph
  • 移(うつ)す: to move/transfer something
  • 映(うつ)す: to project (image), reflect

The kanji helps distinguish meaning. 写(うつ)す relates to reproduction/capture of images or text.
