驚(おどろ)かす

おどろかす
verb (godan)
to surprise, to startle

Definitions

1. to surprise, to startle, to astonish
To cause someone to feel sudden shock or amazement.

Examples

彼(かれ)を驚(おどろ)かそう。
Let's surprise him.
驚(おどろ)かさないでください。
Please don't startle me.
世界(せかい)を驚(おどろ)かせた発見(はっけん)。
A discovery that astonished the world.

Notes

驚(おどろ)かす means to surprise or startle.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 驚(おどろ)かす (transitive): to surprise someone
  • 驚(おどろ)く (intransitive): to be surprised

USAGE:

  • Often used for pranks or unexpected reveals
  • Can be positive or negative surprises
