おめでとう

おめでとう
expression
congratulations

Definitions

1. congratulations
An expression of congratulation for achievements, special occasions, or celebrations. The polite form is おめでとうございます.

Examples

誕生日(たんじょうび)おめでとう！
Happy birthday!
合格(ごうかく)おめでとうございます。
Congratulations on passing!
Polite form
あけましておめでとうございます。
Happy New Year!
New Year greeting

Notes

おめでとう is used for celebrations and congratulations. The polite form おめでとうございます is used in formal situations. Common uses include birthdays, New Year, weddings, graduation, and passing exams. The word comes from the adjective めでたい (auspicious, happy).
