怒(おこ)る

おこる
verb (godan)
to get angry

Definitions

1. to get angry, to be angry
To experience or display anger. Can be directed at someone (に) or about something (を/に).
2. to scold
To express anger at someone, often a child or subordinate, in order to correct their behavior.

Examples

母(はは)は怒(おこ)っています。
My mother is angry.
ている form for current state
先生(せんせい)に怒(おこ)られました。
I was scolded by the teacher.
Passive form meaning 'got scolded'
怒(おこ)らないでください。
Please don't get angry.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) for 'get angry'; can be 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) for 'scold.' No pair verb. Pattern: Xが怒(おこ)る (X gets angry) or XがYを怒(おこ)る (X scolds Y).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 怒(おこ)っている = 'is angry' (state, not 'is getting angry').

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 怒(おこ)っている (is angry - state)
  • 怒(おこ)られる (be scolded - passive)
  • 怒(おこ)らないで (don't get angry)
  • すぐ怒(おこ)る (get angry easily)

HOMOPHONE: Different from 起(お)こる (to happen/occur) which uses different kanji.
