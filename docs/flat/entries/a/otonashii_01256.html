大人(おとな)しい

おとなしい
i-adjective
quiet, docile

Definitions

1. quiet, gentle
Being calm and not causing trouble.
2. obedient, docile
Compliant and well-behaved.

Examples

おとなしい子(こ)だ。
He/She is a quiet child.
おとなしくしていなさい。
Behave yourself / Stay quiet.

Notes

大人しい is often positive but can imply being too passive.

NOTE:
Despite the kanji 大人 (adult), this word means 'gentle/quiet', not 'adult-like'.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 静(しず)か: quiet (environment)
  • 穏(おだ)やか: calm, mild
