ホテル

ほてる
noun
hotel

Definitions

1. hotel
A Western-style accommodation establishment. From English 'hotel.'

Examples

駅前(えきまえ)のホテルに泊(と)まりました。
I stayed at the hotel in front of the station.
ホテルを予約(よやく)しましたか。
Did you book a hotel?
このホテルは高(たか)いですが、サービスがいいです。
This hotel is expensive, but the service is good.

Notes

ホテル refers to Western-style hotels with beds, as opposed to 旅館 (りょかん) which are traditional Japanese inns with futons and tatami rooms. ビジネスホテル are budget-friendly hotels for business travelers.
