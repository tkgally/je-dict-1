返事(へんじ)

へんじ
noun
reply, answer

Definitions

1. reply, answer, response
A verbal or written response to a question, call, or message. Often used with the verb する.

Examples

返事(へんじ)をしてください。
Please reply.
メールの返事(へんじ)が来(き)ました。
The reply to my email came.
名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)ばれたら返事(へんじ)をしてください。
Please answer when your name is called.

Notes

Forms a verb with する: 返事(へんじ)する (to reply). Also commonly used with が来(く)る (a reply comes). Different from 答(こた)え which is more about answers to questions/problems, while 返事(へんじ) is about responding to calls or messages.
