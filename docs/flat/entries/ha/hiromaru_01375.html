広(ひろ)まる

ひろまる
godan verb
to spread, to be propagated

Definitions

1. to spread, to become widespread
To extend over a wider area or to more people.

Examples

噂(うわさ)が広まった。
The rumor spread.
この習慣(しゅうかん)は世界中(せかいじゅう)に広まった。
This custom spread throughout the world.

Notes

広まる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 広(ひろ)める (transitive): to spread, propagate
  • 広まる (intransitive): to spread

SIMILAR:

  • 広(ひろ)がる: to spread, expand (space)
