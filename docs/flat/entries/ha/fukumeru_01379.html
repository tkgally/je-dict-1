含(ふく)める

ふくめる
ichidan verb
to include

Definitions

1. to include, to count in
To make part of a total or group.
2. to imply, to hint
To suggest without saying directly.

Examples

税金(ぜいきん)を含めて1000円(えん)。
1000 yen including tax.
私(わたし)を含めて5人(にん)。
Five people including me.

Notes

含める is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 含める (transitive): to include
  • 含(ふく)む (intransitive/transitive): to contain

COMMON PATTERN:

  • 〜を含めて: including ~
