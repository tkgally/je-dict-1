外(はず)れる

はずれる
ichidan verb
to come off, to miss

Definitions

1. to come off, to be disconnected
To become detached.
2. to be off (target), to miss
To fail to hit.
3. to deviate from, to be outside
To be away from the expected.

Examples

ボタンが外れた。
The button came off.
予想(よそう)が外れた。
My prediction was wrong.
町(まち)外れに住(す)む。
To live on the outskirts of town.

Notes

外れる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 外(はず)す (transitive): to remove
  • 外れる (intransitive): to come off

NOUN FORM:

  • 外(はず)れ: outskirts, edge
  • 町外(まちはず)れ: outskirts of town
