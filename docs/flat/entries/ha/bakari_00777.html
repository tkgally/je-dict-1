〜ばかり

ばかり
particle
just, only, nothing but

Definitions

1. just (did), have just
Indicates an action was just completed.
2. only, nothing but
Indicates limitation or exclusivity.
3. about, approximately
Indicates approximate quantity.

Examples

今(いま)来(き)たばかりだ。
I just arrived.
彼(かれ)は文句(もんく)ばかり言(い)う。
He does nothing but complain.
百(ひゃく)円(えん)ばかり貸(か)してください。
Please lend me about 100 yen.

Notes

ばかり has multiple uses.

PATTERNS:

1. Verb (た form) + ばかり: just did

  • 食(た)べたばかり: just ate

2. Noun + ばかり: only, nothing but

  • 肉(にく)ばかり: only meat

3. Number + ばかり: about, approximately

  • 三日(みっか)ばかり: about three days

VARIATIONS:

  • ばっかり/ばっか: casual forms
