花見(はなみ)

はなみ
noun, verb (suru)
flower viewing

Definitions

1. flower viewing, cherry blossom viewing
The traditional Japanese custom of enjoying the beauty of flowers, especially cherry blossoms, typically as a social gathering in parks.

Examples

今週(こんしゅう)花見(はなみ)に行(い)きませんか。
Would you like to go flower viewing this week?
花見(はなみ)の季節(きせつ)になりました。
It's become flower viewing season.
公園(こうえん)で花見(はなみ)をしました。
We had a flower viewing party in the park.

Notes

花見(はなみ) is composed of 花(はな) (flower) + 見(み) (viewing).

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Hanami primarily refers to cherry blossom (桜(さくら)) viewing in spring, a beloved tradition in Japan. People gather under cherry trees to eat, drink, and enjoy the blossoms.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花見(はなみ)に行(い)く: to go flower viewing
  • 花見(はなみ)をする: to have a flower viewing party
  • 花見(はなみ)客(きゃく): flower viewers
  • お花見(はなみ): flower viewing (polite)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 桜(さくら): cherry blossom
  • 開花(かいか): blooming
  • 満開(まんかい): full bloom
  • 桜前線(さくらぜんせん): cherry blossom front (progression northward)
