箸(はし)

はし
noun
chopsticks

Definitions

1. chopsticks
A pair of sticks used for eating in Japan and other Asian countries.

Examples

箸(はし)を使(つか)って食(た)べてください。
Please eat using chopsticks.
箸(はし)の使(つか)い方(かた)がわかりますか。
Do you know how to use chopsticks?
割(わ)り箸(ばし)をください。
Please give me disposable chopsticks.

Notes

箸 are essential eating utensils in Japan. お箸 is the polite form. 割り箸 (わりばし) are disposable wooden chopsticks. There are many chopstick etiquette rules in Japan, such as never sticking chopsticks upright in rice (associated with funerals).
