歯(は)

は
noun
tooth, teeth

Definitions

1. tooth, teeth
The hard structures in the mouth used for biting and chewing. Used for dental care, physical descriptions, and tooth-related expressions.

Examples

歯(は)を磨(みが)きましたか。
Did you brush your teeth?
歯(は)が痛(いた)いので、歯(は)医者(いしゃ)に行(い)きます。
My tooth hurts, so I'm going to the dentist.
子供(こども)の歯(は)が抜(ぬ)けました。
The child's tooth fell out.

Notes

歯(は)医者(いしゃ) means 'dentist'. 歯(は)ブラシ is 'toothbrush' and 歯(は)磨(みが)き粉(こ) is 'toothpaste'. Common expressions include 歯(は)が立(た)たない (to be no match for someone/something).
