人(ひと)

ひと
noun
person, human

Definitions

1. person, human being
A human individual. The basic word for referring to people in general.
2. someone, other people
Used to refer to others in general. 人(ひと)に言(い)う (to tell someone), 人(ひと)の話(はなし) (what others say).
3. character, personality
The nature or personality of a person. いい人(ひと) (a good/kind person).

Examples

あの人(ひと)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is that person?
人(ひと)が多(おお)いですね。
There are a lot of people, aren't there?
人(ひと)のものを取(と)らないでください。
Please don't take other people's things.

Notes

人(ひと) has multiple readings: ひと (standalone or in some compounds), じん (nationality/occupation suffix like 日本人(にほんじん), アメリカ人(じん)), にん (counter for people like 三人(さんにん)). As a suffix, 〜人(じん) often indicates nationality or a type of person.
