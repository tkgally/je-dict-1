速(はや)い／早(はや)い

はやい
adjective (i-adjective)
fast, early

Definitions

1. fast, quick
Moving or happening at high speed. Written as 速(はや)い. Opposite of 遅(おそ)い (slow).
2. early
Happening before the expected or usual time. Written as 早(はや)い. Opposite of 遅(おそ)い (late).

Examples

この電車(でんしゃ)は速(はや)いです。
This train is fast.
Sense 1: speed
朝(あさ)早(はや)く起(お)きました。
I woke up early in the morning.
Sense 2: early time
もう帰(かえ)るの？早(はや)いね！
You're leaving already? That's early!
Sense 2: sooner than expected

Notes

Two different kanji share this reading: 速(はや)い (speed) and 早(はや)い (time). Both are i-adjectives. Adverb form: 速(はや)く/早(はや)く. The opposite for both meanings is 遅(おそ)い.
