箱(はこ)

はこ
noun
box, case, carton

Definitions

1. box
A container with flat sides, typically square or rectangular.
2. case, carton
A container for holding or transporting items.

Examples

箱(はこ)を開(あ)ける。
To open a box.
お菓子(かし)の箱(はこ)をもらった。
I received a box of sweets.

Notes

箱(はこ) refers to boxes and containers.

TYPES:

  • 段ボール箱(だんボールばこ): cardboard box
  • 弁当箱(べんとうばこ): lunch box
  • 貯金箱(ちょきんばこ): piggy bank
  • ゴミ箱(ごみばこ): trash can

COUNTER:
Boxes are counted with 箱(はこ): 一箱(ひとはこ), 二箱(ふたはこ)
