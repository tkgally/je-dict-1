広(ひろ)げる

ひろげる
verb (ichidan)
to spread, to extend

Definitions

1. to spread, to open out
To make something cover a wider area.
2. to expand, to widen
To increase the scope of something.

Examples

地図(ちず)を広(ひろ)げる。
To spread out a map.
視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げる。
To broaden one's horizons.
翼(つばさ)を広(ひろ)げる。
To spread wings.

Notes

広(ひろ)げる means to spread or extend.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 広(ひろ)げる (transitive): to spread
  • 広(ひろ)がる (intransitive): to spread

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げる: to broaden one's horizons
  • 手(て)を広(ひろ)げる: to spread one's arms
