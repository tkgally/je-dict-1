春(はる)

はる
noun
spring (season)

Definitions

1. spring (season)
The season between winter and summer, typically from March to May in Japan, known for cherry blossoms.

Examples

春(はる)は桜(さくら)がきれいです。
Cherry blossoms are beautiful in spring.
もうすぐ春(はる)ですね。
Spring is almost here.
春(はる)は暖(あたた)かくなります。
It gets warm in spring.

Notes

春 is a significant season in Japan, associated with cherry blossoms, new beginnings (new school year, new jobs start in April), and hanami (flower viewing).

Related:

  • 春休み (haruyasumi, spring break)
  • 春分の日 (shunbun no hi, spring equinox).
