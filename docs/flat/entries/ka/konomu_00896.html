好(この)む

このむ
verb (godan)
to like, to prefer

Definitions

1. to like, to prefer
To have a liking for something.

Examples

彼(かれ)は甘(あま)いものを好(この)む。
He likes sweet things.
お好(この)みの色(いろ)はありますか。
Do you have a preferred color?

Notes

好(この)む means to like.

SIMILAR:

  • 好(この)む: to like, to prefer (slightly formal)
  • 好(す)き: to like (adjective, casual)

RELATED:

  • 好(この)み: preference, taste
  • お好(この)み: one's preference
