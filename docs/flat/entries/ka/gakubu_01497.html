～学部(がくぶ)

がくぶ
suffix
faculty, department (university)

Definitions

1. faculty, department
A division of a university focused on a particular field of study.

Examples

経済(けいざい)学部で学(まな)んでいる。
I'm studying in the economics department.

Notes

EXAMPLES:

  • 文(ぶん)学部: faculty of literature
  • 経済(けいざい)学部: faculty of economics
  • 工(こう)学部: faculty of engineering
  • 法(ほう)学部: faculty of law
  • 医(い)学部: faculty of medicine
