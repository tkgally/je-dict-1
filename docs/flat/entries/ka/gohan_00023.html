ごはん

ごはん
noun
rice, meal

Definitions

1. cooked rice
Steamed white rice, the staple food of Japan.
2. meal
Food in general, or a meal.

Examples

ごはんを食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat (a meal/rice).
Can mean either
朝(あさ)ごはんは何(なに)を食(た)べましたか？
What did you eat for breakfast?
Sense 2: meal
ごはんとみそ汁(しる)をください。
Rice and miso soup, please.
Sense 1: rice

Notes

One of the most important words in Japanese. 朝(あさ)ごはん (breakfast), 昼(ひる)ごはん (lunch), 晩(ばん)ごはん/夜(よる)ごはん (dinner). Written as ご飯(はん) with kanji.
