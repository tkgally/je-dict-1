きっと

きっと
adverb
surely, certainly

Definitions

1. surely, certainly, definitely
Expresses strong confidence that something will happen or is true. Used when the speaker feels certain about a prediction or expectation.

Examples

明日(あした)はきっと晴(は)れますよ。
It will surely be sunny tomorrow.
きっと大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですよ。
I'm sure it will be fine.
Reassuring someone
彼(かれ)はきっと来(き)ます。
He will definitely come.

Notes

Expresses a higher degree of certainty than たぶん (probably). Often used to encourage or reassure someone. Usually written in hiragana. Can be used with both positive and negative predictions.
