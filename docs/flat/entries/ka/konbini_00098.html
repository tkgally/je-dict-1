コンビニ

こんびに
noun
convenience store

Definitions

1. convenience store
A small retail store open long hours, selling food, drinks, and daily necessities. Short for コンビニエンスストア.

Examples

コンビニで弁当(べんとう)を買(か)いました。
I bought a bento at the convenience store.
コンビニは24時間(じかん)営業(えいぎょう)しています。
Convenience stores are open 24 hours.
コンビニでATMを使(つか)えます。
You can use an ATM at the convenience store.

Notes

コンビニ are ubiquitous in Japan and offer many services beyond retail: ATMs, bill payment, ticket purchases, photocopying, and package pickup. Major chains include 7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart. They are an essential part of daily life in Japan.
