咥(くわ)える

くわえる
verb (ichidan)
to hold in one's mouth

Definitions

1. to hold in one's mouth
To grip something between the lips or teeth.

Examples

タバコを咥(くわ)える。
To hold a cigarette in one's mouth.
犬(いぬ)がボールを咥(くわ)えている。
The dog is holding a ball in its mouth.
ペンを咥(くわ)えて考(かんが)える。
To think while holding a pen in one's mouth.

Notes

咥(くわ)える means to hold in the mouth.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 咥(くわ)える: to hold in mouth
  • 加(くわ)える: to add

RELATED:

  • 噛(か)む: to bite, chew
