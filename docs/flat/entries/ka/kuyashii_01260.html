悔(くや)しい

くやしい
i-adjective
frustrating, regrettable

Definitions

1. frustrating, vexing
Feeling upset about failure or unfairness.
2. regrettable, mortifying
Causing a sense of remorse or chagrin.

Examples

負(ま)けて悔しい。
I'm frustrated about losing.
悔しくて泣(な)いた。
I cried out of frustration.

Notes

悔しい expresses frustration over failure or unfairness.

USAGE:

  • Often describes competitive disappointment
  • Can motivate one to try harder

RELATED:

  • 悔(く)やむ: to regret
  • 悔(く)い: regret (noun)
