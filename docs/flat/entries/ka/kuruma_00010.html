車(くるま)

くるま
noun
car, vehicle

Definitions

1. car, automobile
A motor vehicle for transportation. The most common word for car in everyday Japanese.
2. wheel
A circular object that rotates. This is the original meaning of the kanji.

Examples

新(あたら)しい車(くるま)を買(か)いました。
I bought a new car.
車(くるま)で会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)きます。
I go to work by car.
車で = by car
車(くるま)に乗(の)ってください。
Please get in the car.
車に乗る = to get in a car

Notes

Also written as くるま in hiragana in casual contexts. More formal term is 自動車(じどうしゃ).

Related words:

  • 電車(でんしゃ) (train)
  • 自転車(じてんしゃ) (bicycle)
  • 車庫(しゃこ) (garage).
