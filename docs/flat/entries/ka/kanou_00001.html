可能(かのう)

かのう
noun, na-adjective
possible, feasible

Definitions

1. possible, feasible, practicable
Capable of being done or achieved.

Examples

それは可能(かのう)ですか。
Is that possible?
実現(じつげん)可能(かのう)な計画(けいかく)。
A feasible plan.
可能(かのう)な限(かぎ)り努力(どりょく)します。
I'll try as much as possible.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 可能(かのう)な限(かぎ)り (as much as possible)
  • 〜可能(かのう) (~ capable/able)
  • 可能(かのう)性(せい) (possibility)

GRAMMAR: 可能(かのう)形(けい) (potential form) - verb conjugation expressing ability/possibility.

Opposite: 不可能(ふかのう) (impossible)
