課題(かだい)

かだい
noun
task, assignment, issue

Definitions

1. assignment, homework
A task given by a teacher or supervisor.
2. issue, problem, challenge
A matter that needs to be addressed or solved.

Examples

課題(かだい)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit your assignment.
これは大(おお)きな課題(かだい)です。
This is a major issue.
課題(かだい)に取(と)り組(く)む。
To work on an assignment / tackle an issue.

Notes

USAGE:

  • School/work assignments: 課題(かだい)を出(だ)す (to assign), 課題(かだい)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit)
  • Issues/challenges: 課題(かだい)に取(と)り組(く)む (to tackle an issue), 課題(かだい)を解決(かいけつ)する (to solve an issue)

Related: 宿題(しゅくだい) (homework - more casual)
