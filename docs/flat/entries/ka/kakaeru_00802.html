抱(かか)える

かかえる
verb (ichidan)
to hold in one's arms, to have (problems)

Definitions

1. to hold in one's arms, to carry
To hold something against one's body.
2. to have, to be burdened with
To have problems, debts, or staff.

Examples

荷物(にもつ)を抱(かか)える。
To carry luggage in one's arms.
問題(もんだい)を抱(かか)えている。
I'm dealing with problems.
会社(かいしゃ)は100人(にん)の社員(しゃいん)を抱(かか)えている。
The company has 100 employees.

Notes

抱(かか)える has physical and figurative uses.

USES:
1. Physical: to hold/carry in arms
2. Figurative: to have (problems, staff, debt)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 問題(もんだい)を抱(かか)える: to have problems
  • 借金(しゃっきん)を抱(かか)える: to have debt
  • 不安(ふあん)を抱(かか)える: to have anxiety
