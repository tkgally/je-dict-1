～区(く)

く
suffix
ward, district, borough

Definitions

1. ward, district
An administrative division of a city, especially in Tokyo and other major cities.

Examples

渋谷(しぶや)区に住(す)んでいる。
I live in Shibuya Ward.

Notes

Tokyo has 23 special wards (特別区).

EXAMPLES:

  • 新宿(しんじゅく)区: Shinjuku Ward
  • 渋谷(しぶや)区: Shibuya Ward
  • 港(みなと)区: Minato Ward
