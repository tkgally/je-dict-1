超(こ)す

こす
godan verb
to exceed, to surpass

Definitions

1. to exceed, to surpass
To go beyond a limit or amount.
2. to pass through, to tide over
To get through a difficult time.

Examples

予算(よさん)を超す。
To exceed the budget.

Notes

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 超(こ)す: to exceed, surpass
  • 越(こ)す: to cross over, move

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 限度(げんど)を超す: to exceed the limit
  • 予想(よそう)を超す: to exceed expectations
