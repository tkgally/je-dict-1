試(こころ)みる

こころみる
verb (ichidan)
to try, to attempt

Definitions

1. to try, to attempt
To make an effort to do something.

Examples

新(あたら)しい方法(ほうほう)を試(こころ)みる。
To try a new method.
説得(せっとく)を試(こころ)みた。
I attempted to persuade them.
挑戦(ちょうせん)を試(こころ)みる。
To attempt a challenge.

Notes

試(こころ)みる means to try or attempt.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 試(こころ)みる: to try (formal, deliberate attempt)
  • 試(ため)す: to test, try out
  • やってみる: to try doing (casual)

RELATED:

  • 試(こころ)み: attempt, trial
