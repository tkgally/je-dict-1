腐(くさ)る

くさる
verb (godan)
to rot, to go bad, to decay

Definitions

1. to rot, to go bad, to spoil
To decay or decompose.
2. to become corrupt, to be depressed
Figuratively, to deteriorate morally or emotionally.

Examples

果物(くだもの)が腐(くさ)った。
The fruit went bad.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に入(い)れないと腐(くさ)る。
It will spoil if you don't put it in the refrigerator.
気持(きも)ちが腐(くさ)る。
To feel dejected.

Notes

腐(くさ)る means to rot or spoil.

USES:
1. Physical: food spoiling, wood rotting
2. Figurative: mood souring, character corrupting

RELATED:

  • 腐(くさ)った: rotten, spoiled
  • 腐(くさ)らせる: to let spoil (causative)
