ごめんなさい

ごめんなさい
interjection
I'm sorry

Definitions

1. I'm sorry
A direct apology, often used when you have done something wrong or caused someone trouble.

Examples

約束(やくそく)を忘(わす)れて、ごめんなさい。
I'm sorry for forgetting our promise.
ごめんなさい、私(わたし)が悪(わる)かったです。
I'm sorry, it was my fault.
ごめんね。
Sorry. (casual)
Casual form

Notes

ごめんなさい is a more heartfelt apology than すみません, often used when admitting fault. The casual form is ごめん or ごめんね. It can also be used when refusing an invitation or request (ごめんなさい、今日(きょう)は無理(むり)です - Sorry, I can't today).
