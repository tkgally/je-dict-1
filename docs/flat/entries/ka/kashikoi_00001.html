賢(かしこ)い

かしこい
adjective (i-adjective)
clever, wise, smart

Definitions

1. clever, wise, intelligent
Having or showing good judgment, quick understanding, or practical wisdom.

Examples

賢(かしこ)い子(こ)ですね。
What a clever child.
賢(かしこ)い選択(せんたく)だと思(おも)う。
I think it's a wise choice.
この犬(いぬ)はとても賢(かしこ)い。
This dog is very smart.

Notes

賢(かしこ)い emphasizes practical wisdom and good judgment.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 頭(あたま)がいい (smart, intelligent)
  • 利口(りこう) (clever, smart)
  • 知的(ちてき) (intellectual)

賢(かしこ)い can be used for both people and animals.
