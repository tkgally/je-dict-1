短(みじか)い

みじかい
adjective (i-adjective)
short

Definitions

1. short (length)
Having little length. The opposite of 長(なが)い (long).
2. short (time/duration)
Lasting only a brief amount of time.

Examples

このスカートは短(みじか)いです。
This skirt is short.
Sense 1: physical length
髪(かみ)を短(みじか)くしました。
I cut my hair short.
Adverb form: 短く
夏休(なつやす)みは短(みじか)かったです。
Summer vacation was short.
Sense 2: duration

Notes

An i-adjective. Opposite of 長(なが)い. Adverb form: 短(みじか)く. Often used with する to mean 'to make shorter': 短(みじか)くする.
