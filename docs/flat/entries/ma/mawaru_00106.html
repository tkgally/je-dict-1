回(まわ)る

まわる
verb (godan)
to go around, to revolve

Definitions

1. to turn, to revolve, to rotate
To move in a circular motion around an axis or center.
2. to go around, to make rounds
To visit multiple places in succession.
3. to go via, to take a detour
To reach a destination by an indirect route.

Examples

地球(ちきゅう)は太陽(たいよう)の周(まわ)りを回(まわ)っている。
The Earth revolves around the sun.
いくつかの店(みせ)を回(まわ)った。
I went around to several stores.
'Visit' meaning
右(みぎ)に回(まわ)ってください。
Please turn right.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 回(まわ)す (transitive, to turn something). Pattern: Xが回(まわ)る (X turns/goes around).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 回(まわ)っている = 'is turning/going around.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぐるぐる回(まわ)る (spin around)
  • 店(みせ)を回(まわ)る (visit stores)
  • 周(まわ)りを回(まわ)る (go around the perimeter)
  • 頭(あたま)が回(まわ)る (head spins; can think clearly)
  • 血(ち)が回(まわ)る (blood circulates)

CONTRAST:

  • 回(まわ)る (intransitive): X turns
  • 回(まわ)す (transitive): turn X

RELATED:

  • 回転(かいてん) (rotation—noun)
  • 一回(いっかい)り (one round, one lap)
