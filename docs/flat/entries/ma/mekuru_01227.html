捲(めく)る

めくる
godan verb
to turn (pages), to flip

Definitions

1. to turn (pages)
To flip pages of a book or document.
2. to roll up, to turn over
To fold or flip something upward.

Examples

ページをめくる。
To turn the page.
袖(そで)をめくる。
To roll up one's sleeves.

Notes

めくる is often written in hiragana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ページをめくる: to turn pages
  • 布団(ふとん)をめくる: to turn back the bedding
  • カレンダーをめくる: to flip the calendar
