結(むす)び

むすび
noun
end, conclusion, knot

Definitions

1. end, conclusion
The closing part.
2. rice ball, onigiri
Hand-formed rice ball.

Examples

結(むす)びの言葉(ことば)
Closing words

Notes

結び comes from 結(むす)ぶ (to tie).

MEANINGS:

  • 結(むす)びの言葉(ことば): closing remarks
  • おむすび: rice ball (polite)
