基(もと)づく

もとづく
godan verb
to be based on

Definitions

1. to be based on, to be grounded on
To have something as a foundation or basis.

Examples

事実(じじつ)に基づく話(はなし)
A story based on facts
法律(ほうりつ)に基づいて判断(はんだん)する。
To judge based on the law.

Notes

基づく takes the particle に.

COMMON PATTERN:

  • 〜に基づく: based on ~
  • 〜に基づいて: based on ~ (te-form)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜を元(もと)にする: to base on ~
  • 〜を踏(ふ)まえる: to take ~ into consideration
