負(ま)ける

まける
verb (ichidan)
to lose, to be defeated

Definitions

1. to lose, to be defeated
To fail to win in a game, competition, or conflict.
2. to give in, to yield
To concede or surrender to pressure or temptation.
3. to give a discount
Colloquially, to reduce the price as a favor.

Examples

試合(しあい)に負(ま)けた。
I lost the game.
誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)けてしまった。
I gave in to temptation.
少(すこ)し負(ま)けてくれませんか。
Could you give me a small discount?
Discount meaning—casual

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xに負(ま)ける (lose to X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 負(ま)けている = 'is losing' or 'has lost.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試合(しあい)に負(ま)ける (lose a game)
  • 相手(あいて)に負(ま)ける (lose to an opponent)
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)ける (yield to temptation)
  • 負(ま)けず嫌(ぎら)い (competitive, hates to lose)
  • 負(ま)け犬(いぬ) (loser—lit. 'losing dog')

OPPOSITE: 勝(か)つ (to win)

DISCOUNT USAGE (colloquial):

  • 負(ま)けてよ (Give me a discount!)
  • 負(ま)けてあげる (I'll give you a discount)

Related Words

See also: 勝(か)つ
Antonym: 勝(か)つ (to win)
