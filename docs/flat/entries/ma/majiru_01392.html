混(ま)じる

まじる
godan verb
to be mixed in

Definitions

1. to be mixed in, to be included
To be combined or present among other things.

Examples

白髪(しらが)が混じっている。
There are gray hairs mixed in.
外国人(がいこくじん)が混じっている。
There are foreigners among them.

Notes

混じる is similar to 混ざる but emphasizes presence.

NUANCE:

  • 混ざる: focuses on blending
  • 混じる: focuses on being present among

SIMILAR:

  • 交(まじ)わる: to intersect, associate
