目的(もくてき)

もくてき
noun
purpose, objective

Definitions

1. purpose, goal, objective
What one aims to achieve.

Examples

旅行(りょこう)の目的は何(なん)ですか。
What's the purpose of your trip?

Notes

目的 is formal.

SIMILAR:

  • 目標(もくひょう): goal, target
  • 狙(ねら)い: aim

COMMON PHRASE:

  • 目的地(ち): destination
