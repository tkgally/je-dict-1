息子(むすこ)

むすこ
noun
son

Definitions

1. son
A male child in relation to their parents. This is the neutral/humble form used when referring to one's own son.

Examples

息子(むすこ)は大学生(だいがくせい)です。
My son is a university student.
息子(むすこ)が二人(ふたり)います。
I have two sons.
息子(むすこ)は来年(らいねん)結婚(けっこん)します。
My son will get married next year.

Notes

息子(むすこ) is the humble term used when referring to your own son. When referring to someone else's son politely, use 息子(むすこ)さん. The kanji 息(いき) means 'breath' and 子(こ) means 'child', together suggesting 'the child of one's life/breath'.
