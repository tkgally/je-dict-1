無理(むり)

むり
noun
impossible, unreasonable

Definitions

1. impossible, unreasonable
Beyond what is possible or reasonable.
2. forcing, overdoing
Doing something beyond one's capacity.

Examples

それは無理(むり)だ。
That's impossible.
無理(むり)しないでください。
Please don't overdo it.

Notes

Functions as noun and な-adjective. 無理(むり)する means to force oneself or overdo things.
