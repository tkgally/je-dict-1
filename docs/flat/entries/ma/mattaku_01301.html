全(まった)く

まったく
adverb
entirely, really

Definitions

1. entirely, completely
To the full extent (with negatives).
2. really, truly
Used for emphasis or exasperation.

Examples

まったく分(わ)からない。
I don't understand at all.
まったく困(こま)ったものだ。
What a nuisance!
Expression of exasperation

Notes

まったく has formal and informal uses.

WITH NEGATIVE:

  • まったく〜ない: not at all

EXCLAMATORY:

  • まったく！: Really! / Honestly!
  • Shows frustration or exasperation
