回(まわ)す

まわす
verb (godan)
to turn, to rotate

Definitions

1. to turn, to rotate
To cause something to move in a circular motion.
2. to pass around, to circulate
To send something around to multiple people or places.
3. to forward, to transfer
To redirect a call or send something to another destination.

Examples

ハンドルを回(まわ)す。
Turn the steering wheel.
書類(しょるい)を回(まわ)してください。
Please circulate the documents.
電話(でんわ)を担当者(たんとうしゃ)に回(まわ)す。
Transfer the call to the person in charge.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 回(まわ)る (intransitive, to turn). Pattern: Xを回(まわ)す (turn X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 回(まわ)している = 'is turning.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ハンドルを回(まわ)す (turn a handle/steering wheel)
  • ねじを回(まわ)す (turn a screw)
  • 書類(しょるい)を回(まわ)す (circulate documents)
  • 電話(でんわ)を回(まわ)す (transfer a call)
  • 頭(あたま)を回(まわ)す (use one's head, think hard)

CONTRAST:

  • 回(まわ)す (transitive): turn X
  • 回(まわ)る (intransitive): X turns
