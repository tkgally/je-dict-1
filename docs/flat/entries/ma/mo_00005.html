Japanese-English Dictionary

も

も
particle
also, too, even

Definitions

1. also, too
Indicates that something applies to an additional item or person. Replaces は, が, or を. 私(わたし)も行(い)きます (I'll go too).
2. even
Emphasizes an extreme or unexpected case. 子供(こども)でも分(わ)かる (even a child can understand).
3. both...and, neither...nor
Used in pairs to mean 'both' (AもBも) or with negatives for 'neither.' 犬(いぬ)も猫(ねこ)も好(す)き (I like both dogs and cats).
4. as many as, as much as
Emphasizes a large quantity. 三時間(さんじかん)も待(ま)った (waited for as long as 3 hours).

Examples

私(わたし)も日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I'm also studying Japanese.
Replaces は
これも買(か)いたいです。
I want to buy this too.
Replaces を
誰(だれ)も来(き)ませんでした。
Nobody came.
Question word + も + negative = nobody/nothing
お茶(おちゃ)もコーヒーもあります。
We have both tea and coffee.
AもBも = both A and B
三時間(さんじかん)も待(ま)ちました。
I waited for as long as three hours.
Quantity + も = emphasis on amount
東京(とうきょう)にも行(い)きました。
I also went to Tokyo.
も attaches after に

Notes

も replaces は, が, and を but attaches after other particles: に→にも, で→でも, と→とも. With question words and negatives: 誰(だれ)も...ない (nobody), 何(なに)も...ない (nothing), どこも...ない (nowhere). These patterns are essential for expressing 'nobody/nothing/nowhere.'
