違(ちが)う

ちがう
verb (godan)
to differ, to be wrong

Definitions

1. to differ, to be different
To not be the same.
2. to be wrong, to be incorrect
To not be right.

Examples

答(こた)えが違(ちが)う。
The answer is wrong.
考(かんが)えが違(ちが)う。
We think differently.
違(ちが)います。
That's not right. / No.

Notes

違(ちが)う means to differ or be wrong.

PATTERNS:

  • XとYが違(ちが)う: X and Y are different
  • Xと違(ちが)って: unlike X

USAGE:

  • 違(ちが)います: polite way to say "no"

RELATED:

  • 違(ちが)い: difference
  • 間違(まちが)い: mistake
