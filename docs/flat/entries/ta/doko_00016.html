どこ

どこ
pronoun
where

Definitions

1. where
A question word asking about location or place.

Examples

トイレはどこですか？
Where is the restroom?
どこに住(す)んでいますか？
Where do you live?
どこへ行(い)きますか？
Where are you going?
どこへ for direction

Notes

One of the essential question words. Related expressions: どこか (somewhere), どこも (nowhere/everywhere depending on context), どこでも (anywhere). Part of the こそあど series: ここ (here), そこ (there), あそこ (over there), どこ (where).
