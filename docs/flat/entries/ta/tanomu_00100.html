頼(たの)む

たのむ
verb (godan)
to request, to ask (a favor)

Definitions

1. to request, to ask (for help/a favor)
To make a request to someone, often asking them to do something for you.
2. to order (food, etc.)
To request a specific item at a restaurant or store.
3. to rely on, to depend on
To count on someone for help or support.

Examples

友達(ともだち)に手伝(てつだ)いを頼(たの)みました。
I asked my friend for help.
ビールを二(ふた)つ頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered two beers.
Ordering at a restaurant
お願(ねが)いがあるんですが、頼(たの)んでもいいですか。
I have a favor to ask. May I ask you?

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを頼(たの)む (request X) / Xに頼(たの)む (ask X [person]).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing/repeated state. 頼(たの)んでいる = 'have requested' or 'am relying on.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)に頼(たの)む (ask someone)
  • 仕事(しごと)を頼(たの)む (request work/task)
  • ビールを頼(たの)む (order beer)
  • 頼(たの)むよ (please!
  • casual request)

Related:

  • お願(ねが)いします (polite request)
  • 頼(たの)み (noun - favor/request).
