散(ち)らす

ちらす
godan verb, transitive
to scatter, to disperse

Definitions

1. to scatter, to disperse
To spread things in different directions.

Examples

花(はな)びらを散(ち)らす。
To scatter flower petals.
注意(ちゅうい)を散(ち)らす。
To distract attention.

Notes

散(ち)らす is the transitive form.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 散(ち)る: to scatter (intransitive)

COMMON USES:

  • 気(き)を散(ち)らす: to distract
