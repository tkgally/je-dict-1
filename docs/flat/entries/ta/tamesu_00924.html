試(ため)す

ためす
verb (godan)
to try, to test, to attempt

Definitions

1. to try, to attempt
To make an effort to do something.
2. to test, to experiment
To check if something works.

Examples

新(あたら)しい方法(ほうほう)を試(ため)す。
To try a new method.
味(あじ)を試(ため)してみる。
To try the taste.

Notes

試(ため)す means to try or test.

PATTERN:

  • 〜を試(ため)す: to try ~
  • 試(ため)してみる: to try (and see)

RELATED:

  • 試(ため)し: trial, test
  • 試験(しけん): examination
