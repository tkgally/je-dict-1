店員(てんいん)

てんいん
noun
store clerk, shop staff

Definitions

1. store clerk, shop staff
A person who works in a store, helping customers and handling sales. Can refer to any retail employee.

Examples

店員(てんいん)に聞(き)いてください。
Please ask the store clerk.
店員(てんいん)さんがとても親切(しんせつ)でした。
The store clerk was very kind.
さん added for politeness
店員(てんいん)として働(はたら)いています。
I work as a store clerk.

Notes

Combines 店(みせ) (store) and 員(いん) (member/staff). Often addressed with さん: 店員(てんいん)さん.

Related words:

  • 店長(てんちょう) (store manager)
  • 従業員(じゅうぎょういん) (employee).
