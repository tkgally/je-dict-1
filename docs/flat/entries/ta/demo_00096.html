でも

でも
conjunction
but, however

Definitions

1. but, however
A casual conjunction used to contrast or introduce an exception. Used at the beginning of sentences.
2. even (with particle)
When attached to nouns, means 'even' (e.g., 子供(こども)でも - even a child).

Examples

食(た)べたいです。でも、お金(かね)がありません。
I want to eat. But, I don't have money.
でも、それは本当(ほんとう)ですか。
But, is that true?
子供(こども)でも分(わ)かります。
Even a child can understand.
Particle usage

Notes

でも is a casual conjunction used at the start of sentences to introduce contrast. It's softer than しかし (however) and more conversational. When attached to nouns (〜でも), it means 'even' or 'or something like.' 何(なに)でもいい means 'anything is fine.'
