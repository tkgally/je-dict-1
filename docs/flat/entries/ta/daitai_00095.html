だいたい

だいたい
adverb
roughly, approximately

Definitions

1. roughly, approximately, about
Used to indicate an approximate amount, time, or degree. Not exact but close enough.
2. generally, mostly
For the most part, in general terms.

Examples

だいたい三十分(さんじゅっぷん)かかります。
It takes about 30 minutes.
Sense 1: approximate time
仕事(しごと)はだいたい終(お)わりました。
The work is mostly finished.
Sense 2: generally
だいたいわかりました。
I roughly understand.

Notes

Can be written as 大体(だいたい) in kanji. Very useful for giving approximate information without committing to exact numbers. Similar to 約(やく) (approximately) but だいたい is more casual and versatile.
