大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)

だいじょうぶ
adjective (na-adjective)
okay, all right, safe

Definitions

1. okay, all right, fine
Indicates that something is satisfactory or that there is no problem.
2. safe, secure
Indicates that someone or something is safe and not in danger.

Examples

大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですか。
Are you okay?
Very common expression of concern
大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)です。心配(しんぱい)しないでください。
I'm fine. Please don't worry.
これで大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですか。
Is this okay?

Notes

大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですか is one of the most useful phrases in Japanese, used to ask if someone is okay or if something is all right. It can be used as a response meaning 'I'm fine' or 'No, thank you' (declining an offer politely).
