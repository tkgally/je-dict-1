滝(たき)

たき
noun
waterfall

Definitions

1. waterfall
A cascade of water falling from a height.

Examples

滝(たき)を見(み)に行(い)った。
I went to see a waterfall.
滝(たき)の音(おと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the sound of the waterfall.

Notes

滝(たき) means waterfall.

FAMOUS WATERFALLS:

  • 華厳(けごん)の滝(たき): Kegon Falls (Nikko)
  • 那智(なち)の滝(たき): Nachi Falls (Wakayama)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 滝(たき)のような汗(あせ): sweating profusely (like a waterfall)
  • 滝行(たきぎょう): waterfall meditation (standing under a waterfall)
