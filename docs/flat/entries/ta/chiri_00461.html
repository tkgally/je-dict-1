地理(ちり)

ちり
noun
geography

Definitions

1. geography
The study of places and the relationships between people and their environments. Also refers to the physical features of an area.

Examples

地理(ちり)が得意(とくい)です。
I'm good at geography.
この辺(あた)りの地理(ちり)に詳(くわ)しいですか。
Are you familiar with the geography of this area?
地理(ちり)の授業(じゅぎょう)で日本(にほん)の山(やま)について習(なら)った。
I learned about Japan's mountains in geography class.

Notes

地理(ちり) can refer to the academic subject or knowledge of an area's layout.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 地理(ちり)に詳(くわ)しい: familiar with the geography/layout
  • 地理(ちり)が分(わ)からない: don't know the area

RELATED TERMS:

  • 歴史(れきし): history
  • 社会(しゃかい): social studies
  • 地図(ちず): map
  • 地形(ちけい): terrain, topography

SCHOOL SUBJECTS:

  • 国語(こくご): Japanese language
  • 数学(すうがく): mathematics
  • 理科(りか): science
  • 社会(しゃかい): social studies (includes 地理(ちり) and 歴史(れきし))
