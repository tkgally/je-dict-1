できるだけ

できるだけ
adverb
as much as possible

Definitions

1. as much as possible; to the extent one can
Emphasizes making maximum effort within one's capabilities.

Examples

できるだけ早(はや)く終(お)わらせます。
I'll finish as quickly as possible.
できるだけ毎日(まいにち)運動(うんどう)しています。
I exercise every day as much as possible.
できるだけ安(やす)いものを探(さが)しています。
I'm looking for something as cheap as possible.

Notes

できるだけ literally means 'to the extent that one can do' and emphasizes effort.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • できるだけ: as much as possible (emphasizes effort/ability)
  • なるべく: as much as possible (softer, preference-based)
  • 精一杯(せいいっぱい): with all one's might
