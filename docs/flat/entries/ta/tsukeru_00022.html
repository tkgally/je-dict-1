つける

つける
verb (ichidan)
to turn on, to attach

Definitions

1. to turn on, to switch on
To activate electrical devices like lights, TV, or air conditioning.
2. to attach, to put on
To fasten or attach something to something else.

Examples

電気(でんき)をつけてください。
Please turn on the light.
Sense 1
テレビをつけましょうか？
Shall I turn on the TV?
Sense 1
名前(なまえ)をつける。
To give a name (attach a name).
Sense 2

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: つく (intransitive). Pattern: Xをつける (turn on/attach X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. つけている = 'have turned on' or 'is attached.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電気(でんき)をつける (turn on the light)
  • テレビをつける (turn on the TV)
  • 名前(なまえ)をつける (give a name)
  • 気(き)をつける (be careful)

PAIR: つく (intransitive) - 電気(でんき)がついた (light turned on). Opposite: 消(け)す (turn off).
