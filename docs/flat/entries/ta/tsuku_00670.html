付(つ)く

つく
verb (godan)
to be attached, to stick

Definitions

1. to be attached, to stick
To become connected or adhered.
2. to be included, to come with
To be provided along with something.

Examples

ゴミが付(つ)いている。
There's dirt stuck on it.
朝食(ちょうしょく)付(つ)きのホテル。
A hotel with breakfast included.
力(ちから)が付(つ)く。
To gain strength.

Notes

付(つ)く means to be attached or included.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 付(つ)く (intransitive): to attach
  • 付(つ)ける (transitive): to attach

COMMON USES:

  • 気(き)が付(つ)く: to notice
  • 身(み)に付(つ)く: to acquire (skill)
  • 運(うん)が付(つ)く: to be lucky
