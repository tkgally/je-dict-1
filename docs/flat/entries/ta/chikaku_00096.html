近(ちか)く

ちかく
noun, adverb
nearby, vicinity

Definitions

1. nearby, vicinity, neighborhood
An area close to a reference point. As a noun, refers to the surrounding area.
2. nearly, close to (amount)
Approximately or almost a certain amount.

Examples

駅(えき)の近(ちか)くにコンビニがあります。
There's a convenience store near the station.
この近(ちか)くに住(す)んでいます。
I live near here.
百(ひゃく)人(にん)近(ちか)くが参加(さんか)しました。
Nearly one hundred people participated.
Using 'nearly' meaning

Notes

近(ちか)く is the noun form of 近(ちか)い (close, near). When describing location, use [place]の近(ちか)く (near [place]). この近(ちか)く means 'near here' or 'in this area.' It can also mean 'nearly' or 'close to' when used with numbers or amounts.
