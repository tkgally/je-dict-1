慧眼(けいがん)

けいがん
noun
discerning eye, keen insight
1. discerning eye, keen insight, sharp perception
The ability to perceive the true nature of things clearly. A formal/literary word emphasizing exceptional judgment and perception.
慧眼(けいがん)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)
a person of keen insight
さすが社長(しゃちょう)、お慧眼(けいがん)ですね。
As expected of the president, you have excellent judgment.
彼(かれ)の慧眼(けいがん)は、誰(だれ)も気(き)づかなかった問題(もんだい)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
His keen insight detected a problem that no one else had noticed.

FORMALITY: Formal/literary. Often used to praise someone's judgment, especially in business or formal contexts.

USAGE: Commonly used with お慧眼(けいがん) as a polite expression praising someone's discernment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 慧眼(けいがん)の持(も)ち主(ぬし) (person of keen insight)
  • お慧眼(けいがん) (your excellent judgment - honorific)
  • 慧眼(けいがん)を持(も)つ (to have keen insight)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく) (insight, perception)
  • 先見(せんけん)の明(めい) (foresight)
  • 眼力(がんりき) (discerning eye - less formal)
