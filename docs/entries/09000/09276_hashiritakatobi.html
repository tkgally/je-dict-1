走(はし)り高跳(たかと)び

はしりたかとび
noun
high jump
1. high jump
A track and field event in which athletes jump over a horizontal bar without any aids.
走(はし)り高跳(たかと)びで金(きん)メダルを取(と)った。
She won a gold medal in high jump.
走(はし)り高跳(たかと)びの世界記録(せかいきろく)は2メートル以上(いじょう)だ。
The world record in high jump is over 2 meters.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)から走(はし)り高跳(たかと)びを練習(れんしゅう)してきた。
I have been practicing high jump since I was a child.

走(はし)り高跳(たかと)び is often shortened to 高跳(たかと)び. Modern high jumpers typically use the "Fosbury Flop" technique, going over the bar backward.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 走(はし)り高跳(たかと)びの選手(せんしゅ) (high jumper)
  • バーを越(こ)える (to clear the bar)
  • 踏(ふ)み切(き)る (to take off)

TECHNIQUES:

  • 背面跳(はいめんと)び (Fosbury Flop, lit. "back jump")
  • 挟(はさ)み跳(と)び (scissors jump, older technique)
