はなくそ

はなくそ
noun
booger, dried nasal mucus
1. booger, dried nasal mucus, snot
Dried mucus from inside the nose. A common childhood word that remains in casual adult usage.
はなくそをほじる。
To pick one's nose.
はなくそを取(と)ってあげる。
I'll get the booger out for you.
人前(ひとまえ)ではなくそをほじらないで。
Don't pick your nose in front of people.

KANJI: Can be written as 鼻糞(はなくそ) or 鼻屎(はなくそ).

WORD FORMATION: 鼻(はな) (nose) + くそ (excrement/waste).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • はなくそをほじる (to pick one's nose, lit. 'to dig out boogers')
  • はなくそを取(と)る (to remove a booger)

REGISTER: Casual/crude. Used commonly with children and in informal settings. Not appropriate for formal contexts.
