おでこ

おでこ
noun
forehead
1. forehead
Casual, slightly childish word for forehead. More common in everyday speech than the formal term.
おでこを触(さわ)って。
Touch my forehead.
おでこが熱(あつ)いね。
Your forehead is hot.
おでこに汗(あせ)をかいている。
There's sweat on my forehead.

KANJI: Can be written as お凸(でこ).

WORD FORMATION: お (prefix) + でこ (from 凸(でこ), meaning convex/protruding).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • おでこを出(だ)す (to show one's forehead - hair pulled back)
  • おでこが広(ひろ)い (to have a wide forehead)
  • おでこにキス (a kiss on the forehead)

HEALTH CONTEXT: Touching a child's forehead is a common way to check for fever: おでこを触(さわ)って熱(ねつ)があるか確(たし)かめる.

FORMAL TERM: 額(ひたい) is the more formal word for forehead.
