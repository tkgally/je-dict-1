連絡船(れんらくせん)

れんらくせん
noun
ferry, connecting boat
1. ferry, connecting boat
A ship that transports passengers and cargo between two points, typically connecting to other transportation routes.
連絡船(れんらくせん)に乗(の)る。
To board the ferry.
連絡船(れんらくせん)は毎日(まいにち)運航(うんこう)している。
The ferry operates every day.
島(しま)への唯一(ゆいいつ)の交通手段(こうつうしゅだん)は連絡船(れんらくせん)だ。
The only means of transportation to the island is the ferry.

連絡船(れんらくせん) refers to a ferry that connects different locations, often linking islands to the mainland or providing connections to other modes of transportation like trains.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連絡船(れんらくせん)に乗(の)る (board a ferry)
  • 連絡船(れんらくせん)を降(お)りる (get off a ferry)
  • 連絡船(れんらくせん)が出(で)る (ferry departs)
  • 連絡船(れんらくせん)が着(つ)く (ferry arrives)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • フェリー: general term for ferry (loanword)
  • 渡(わた)し船(ぶね): small ferry boat, especially for crossing rivers
