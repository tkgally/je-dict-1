意気込(いきご)み

いきごみ
noun
enthusiasm; eagerness; determination; resolve
1. enthusiasm; eagerness; determination; resolve
A strong feeling of enthusiasm and readiness to take action; the eager spirit with which one approaches a task.
意気込(いきご)みを語(かた)る。
To express one's enthusiasm.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)たちの意気込(いきご)みが伝(つた)わってきた。
The eagerness of the new employees came through.
意気込(いきご)みは十分(じゅうぶん)だが、経験(けいけん)が足(た)りない。
The enthusiasm is there, but the experience is lacking.

意気込(いきご)み describes enthusiastic readiness to act.

WORD FORMATION:
Nominalization of 意気込(いきご)む (to be eager, to be enthusiastic).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意気込(いきご)みを語(かた)る: to express one's enthusiasm
  • 意気込(いきご)みを見(み)せる: to show one's eagerness
  • 意気込(いきご)みが伝(つた)わる: eagerness comes through
  • 意気込(いきご)みは十分(じゅうぶん): plenty of enthusiasm

USAGE NOTE:
Often used in contexts like interviews, speeches, or when someone is starting something new and wants to express their determination.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 気概(きがい): mettle, guts (more about inner strength)
  • やる{気(き)}: motivation
  • 熱意(ねつい): passion, zeal
