準(じゅん)ずる

じゅんずる
verb
to conform to; to apply correspondingly
1. to conform to; to apply correspondingly; to treat as equivalent
To follow a standard or model, or to apply rules/conditions in an analogous way. Often used in legal and formal contexts meaning 'the same applies' or 'mutatis mutandis'.
前項(ぜんこう)の規定(きてい)に準(じゅん)ずる。
The provisions of the preceding paragraph apply correspondingly.
正社員(せいしゃいん)に準(じゅん)ずる待遇(たいぐう)を受(う)けられる。
You can receive treatment equivalent to regular employees.
この規則(きそく)は契約社員(けいやくしゃいん)にも準(じゅん)じて適用(てきよう)される。
This rule also applies correspondingly to contract employees.

CONJUGATION:

  • 準(じゅん)ずる (dictionary form)
  • 準(じゅん)じる (alternative form)
  • 準(じゅん)じない (negative)
  • 準(じゅん)じた (past)
  • 準(じゅん)じて (te-form)

Verb type: サ変動詞(さへんどうし) (suru-verb variant)
Transitivity: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) - takes に

USAGE:

  • Common in legal documents, contracts, and regulations
  • Often translated as "mutatis mutandis" in legal contexts
  • Also written as 準(じゅん)じる

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～に準(じゅん)ずる: to follow, conform to
  • ～に準(じゅん)じた扱(あつか)い: treatment equivalent to
  • 準(じゅん)じて適用(てきよう)する: to apply correspondingly
