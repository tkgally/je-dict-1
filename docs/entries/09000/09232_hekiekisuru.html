辟易(へきえき)する

へきえきする
suru verb
to be put off, to be disgusted, to be fed up
1. to be put off, to be disgusted
To feel repelled or disgusted by something or someone.
しつこさに辟易(へきえき)した。
I was put off by the persistence.
あの態度(たいど)には辟易(へきえき)する。
I'm disgusted by that attitude.
毎日(まいにち)の残業(ざんぎょう)に辟易(へきえき)している。
I'm fed up with the daily overtime work.
2. to be overwhelmed, to cower
To shrink back or feel overwhelmed in the face of something powerful.
彼(かれ)の勢(いきお)いに辟易(へきえき)した。
I was overwhelmed by his intensity.
厳(きび)しい質問(しつもん)に辟易(へきえき)する。
I cower at the harsh questions.
相手(あいて)の強引(ごういん)な態度(たいど)に辟易(へきえき)して引(ひ)き下(さ)がった。
I was overwhelmed by the other person's forceful attitude and backed off.

辟易(へきえき)する originally meant to retreat or cower from something, but the modern primary meaning is to be disgusted or fed up.

USAGE:

  • Often used with に to indicate what one is fed up with
  • Common pattern: Xに辟易(へきえき)する (to be fed up with X)

REGISTER: Somewhat formal/literary; neutral to negative connotation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しつこさに辟易(へきえき)する: to be fed up with persistence
  • 暑(あつ)さに辟易(へきえき)する: to be overwhelmed by the heat
  • 態度(たいど)に辟易(へきえき)する: to be disgusted by one's attitude
