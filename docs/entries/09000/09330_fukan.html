俯瞰(ふかん)

ふかん
noun, suru verb
bird's-eye view, overview
1. bird's-eye view, looking down from above
The act of looking down at something from a high position, such as from a mountain or tall building.
山(やま)から町(まち)を俯瞰(ふかん)する。
Look down at the town from the mountain.
展望台(てんぼうだい)から俯瞰(ふかん)した景色(けしき)は美(うつく)しかった。
The view from above at the observation deck was beautiful.
ドローンで俯瞰(ふかん)撮影(さつえい)をする。
Take aerial footage with a drone.
2. overview, broad perspective
Looking at a situation or issue from a detached, comprehensive viewpoint. Taking in the whole picture rather than focusing on details.
問題(もんだい)を俯瞰(ふかん)してみる。
Try looking at the problem from a broader perspective.
俯瞰(ふかん)して見(み)ると、全体像(ぜんたいぞう)が分(わ)かる。
When you look at it from a broader perspective, you can see the whole picture.
自分(じぶん)の人生(じんせい)を俯瞰(ふかん)する機会(きかい)を持(も)つことは大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to have opportunities to take an overview of your own life.

俯瞰(ふかん) means to look down from above, either literally or figuratively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 俯瞰(ふかん)する (to look down upon, to overview)
  • 俯瞰(ふかん)的(てき) (from an overhead/broad perspective)
  • 俯瞰(ふかん)図(ず) (bird's-eye view map)
  • 俯瞰(ふかん)撮影(さつえい) (aerial photography)

RELATED TERM: 鳥瞰(ちょうかん) is a synonym meaning "bird's-eye view" (literally "bird's view").
