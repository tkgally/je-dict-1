食(く)い違(ちが)い

くいちがい
noun
discrepancy, disagreement
1. discrepancy, inconsistency, mismatch
A difference or contradiction between two things that should match. Used for statements, records, or accounts that don't align.
証言(しょうげん)に食(く)い違(ちが)いがある。
There are discrepancies in the testimony.
数字(すうじ)の食(く)い違(ちが)いを確認(かくにん)する。
Confirm the discrepancy in the numbers.
記録(きろく)と実際(じっさい)の数(かず)に食(く)い違(ちが)いがあったので、調査(ちょうさ)が始(はじ)まった。
There was a discrepancy between the records and the actual count, so an investigation began.
2. disagreement, conflict of opinion
A situation where people have different views or opinions on something.
意見(いけん)の食(く)い違(ちが)いがある。
There's a disagreement in opinion.
方針(ほうしん)をめぐって食(く)い違(ちが)いが生(しょう)じた。
A disagreement arose over the policy.
両者(りょうしゃ)の間(あいだ)には根本的(こんぽんてき)な食(く)い違(ちが)いがあり、解決(かいけつ)は難(むずか)しい。
There's a fundamental disagreement between the two parties, making resolution difficult.

食(く)い違(ちが)い refers to situations where things don't match or people disagree.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食(く)い違(ちが)いがある (there is a discrepancy)
  • 食(く)い違(ちが)いが生(しょう)じる (a discrepancy arises)
  • 意見(いけん)の食(く)い違(ちが)い (difference of opinion)
  • 食(く)い違(ちが)いを解消(かいしょう)する (resolve a discrepancy)

RELATED VERB: 食(く)い違(ちが)う (to differ, to be at odds)
