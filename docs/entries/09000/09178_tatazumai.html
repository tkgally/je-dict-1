佇(たたず)まい

たたずまい
noun
appearance, atmosphere, bearing
1. appearance, atmosphere, bearing, ambiance
The overall impression or atmosphere created by the way someone stands or how a place looks. Often used to describe the dignified or distinctive character of buildings, streets, or people.
静(しず)かな佇(たたず)まい
a quiet, serene atmosphere
この寺(てら)は荘厳(そうごん)な佇(たたず)まいを見(み)せている。
This temple presents a solemn appearance.
古(ふる)い町並(まちな)みの佇(たたず)まいに惹(ひ)かれて、この地(ち)に移(う)り住(す)んだ。
Drawn to the atmosphere of the old streets, I moved to this area.

ETYMOLOGY: From the verb 佇(たたず)む (to stand still), referring to the way something stands or presents itself.

NUANCE: 佇(たたず)まい emphasizes the overall impression or ambiance rather than specific details. It's commonly used for architecture, landscapes, and places that have character, often with a sense of dignity or tranquility.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 静(しず)かな佇(たたず)まい (quiet atmosphere)
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な佇(たたず)まい (solemn appearance)
  • 古(ふる)い佇(たたず)まい (old-world charm)
  • 佇(たたず)まいを見(み)せる (to present an appearance)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 雰囲気(ふんいき) (atmosphere - more general)
  • 趣(おもむき) (charm, flavor)
  • 風情(ふぜい) (elegance, atmosphere)
