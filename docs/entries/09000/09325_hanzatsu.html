煩雑(はんざつ)

はんざつ
adjective (na-adjective), noun
complicated, cumbersome
1. complicated, cumbersome, complex
Describes procedures, processes, or systems that are troublesome because they involve many tedious or intricate steps. Often used for bureaucratic or administrative matters.
手続(てつづ)きが煩雑(はんざつ)だ。
The procedures are complicated.
煩雑(はんざつ)な書類(しょるい)作業(さぎょう)に時間(じかん)がかかる。
The tedious paperwork takes time.
このシステムは煩雑(はんざつ)で、改善(かいぜん)の余地(よち)がある。
This system is cumbersome and has room for improvement.

煩雑(はんざつ) describes things that are annoyingly complicated or involve many tedious steps.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 煩雑(はんざつ)な手続(てつづ)き (complicated procedures)
  • 煩雑(はんざつ)な作業(さぎょう) (tedious work)
  • 煩雑(はんざつ)さを避(さ)ける (avoid complexity)

CONJUGATION:

  • 煩雑(はんざつ)な + noun (attributive)
  • 煩雑(はんざつ)だ (predicative)
  • 煩雑(はんざつ)に (adverbial)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 複雑(ふくざつ) - complex (neutral; can be positive)
  • 煩雑(はんざつ) - complicated (negative; implies annoyance)
