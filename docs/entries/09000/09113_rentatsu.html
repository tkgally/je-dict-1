練達(れんたつ)

れんたつ
noun, na-adjective
mastery, proficiency, expertise
1. mastery, proficiency, expertise
A high level of skill achieved through extensive practice and training. Implies refined expertise developed over time.
練達(れんたつ)の技(わざ)
masterful skill
彼女(かのじょ)は練達(れんたつ)した通訳者(つうやくしゃ)として知(し)られている。
She is known as an accomplished interpreter.
練達(れんたつ)の医師(いし)でなければ、この手術(しゅじゅつ)は難(むずか)しい。
Unless you're an expert physician, this surgery is difficult.

USAGE: 練達(れんたつ) emphasizes skill acquired through rigorous training and practice. Often used with の to modify nouns (練達(れんたつ)の職人(しょくにん)).

FORMALITY: Formal/literary. More common in written language than casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 練達(れんたつ)の士(し)/者(しゃ) (master, expert)
  • 練達(れんたつ)した技術(ぎじゅつ)/腕前(うでまえ) (mastered skill)
  • 練達(れんたつ)を極(きわ)める (to attain mastery)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 熟練(じゅくれん) (skilled - more commonly used)
  • 熟達(じゅくたつ) (proficiency - similar meaning)
  • 達人(たつじん) (master, expert - noun form)
