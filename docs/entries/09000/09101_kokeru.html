1. to fall down, to tumble, to trip
To fall down, especially by tripping or losing balance. Very common in casual and children's speech.
こけた！
I fell!
石(いし)につまずいてこけた。
I tripped on a stone and fell.
走(はし)っていたらこけて膝(ひざ)をすりむいた。
I fell while running and scraped my knee.

KANJI: Can be written as 転(こ)ける.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive.

REGIONAL NOTE: More common in Western Japan (Kansai region). In Eastern Japan (Kanto), 転(ころ)ぶ is more common.

CONJUGATION: Despite ending in -eru, this is an ichidan (vowel-stem) verb: こけない, こけます, こけて, etc.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 転(ころ)ぶ (to fall - more standard/Eastern)
  • 倒(たお)れる (to fall over, collapse - larger scale)
