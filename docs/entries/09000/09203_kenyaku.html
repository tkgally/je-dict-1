倹約(けんやく)

けんやく
noun, suru verb
thrift, frugality, economy
1. thrift, frugality, economy
The practice of saving money by avoiding unnecessary spending; being economical with resources.
倹約(けんやく)を心(こころ)がける。
To be mindful of saving money.
倹約(けんやく)して貯金(ちょきん)を増(ふ)やした。
I saved money by being frugal and increased my savings.
母(はは)は倹約(けんやく)家(か)で、無駄遣(むだづか)いをしない。
My mother is frugal and never wastes money.

倹約(けんやく) refers to the practice of being economical and avoiding wasteful spending.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 倹約(けんやく)する: to economize, to be frugal
  • 倹約(けんやく)家(か): a thrifty person, a penny pincher
  • 倹約(けんやく)を心(こころ)がける: to try to be economical
  • 倹約(けんやく)生活(せいかつ): frugal living

RELATED WORDS:

  • 節約(せつやく): similar meaning, more commonly used in daily contexts
  • 倹約(けんやく) has a slightly more old-fashioned or literary nuance

Related Words

Antonym: 浪費(ろうひ) (waste)
Synonym: 節約(せつやく) (saving)
