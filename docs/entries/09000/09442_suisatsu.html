推察(すいさつ)

すいさつ
noun
surmise, conjecture, inference
1. surmise, conjecture, inference
The act of guessing or inferring something based on available evidence or circumstances, typically regarding someone's thoughts, feelings, or situation. Often used in formal contexts to politely acknowledge understanding of another's position.
ご事情(じじょう)は推察(すいさつ)いたします。
I can surmise your circumstances.
彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちを推察(すいさつ)するのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to surmise his feelings.
状況(じょうきょう)から推察(すいさつ)すると、計画(けいかく)は変更(へんこう)されたようだ。
Judging from the situation, it appears the plan was changed.
ご心労(しんろう)のほどは推察(すいさつ)に難(かた)くありません。
It is not hard to imagine your distress.

推察(すいさつ)する is used to politely or formally indicate that one understands or can imagine another person's situation, feelings, or circumstances.

Common patterns:

  • 推察(すいさつ)する (to surmise, conjecture)
  • 推察(すいさつ)に難(かた)くない (not hard to imagine)
  • ご〜は推察(すいさつ)いたします (formal acknowledgment of understanding)

Related words:

  • 推測(すいそく): conjecture (more about predicting outcomes)
  • 憶測(おくそく): speculation (often with negative nuance)
  • 察(さっ)する: to sense, perceive (more intuitive)
