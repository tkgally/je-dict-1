がめつい

がめつい
i-adjective
greedy, grasping, money-grubbing
1. greedy, grasping, money-grubbing
Describes someone who is excessively focused on getting money or material gain, often at the expense of others. Has a strongly negative connotation.
あいつはがめついな。
That guy is so greedy.
がめつい商売(しょうばい)をするな。
Don't do business in such a greedy way.
彼(かれ)はがめつくて評判(ひょうばん)が悪(わる)い。
He has a bad reputation for being greedy.
そんながめついことを言(い)うなよ。
Don't say such greedy things.

がめつい is a colloquial i-adjective with strong negative connotations. It suggests not just wanting money, but being willing to do unscrupulous things to get it.

Similar words:

  • 欲(よく)が深(ふか)い (greedy - more neutral)
  • ケチ (stingy - different nuance, about not spending)
  • 貪欲(どんよく) (avaricious - literary/formal)

This word is quite direct and insulting if used about someone present.
