淹(い)れ立(た)て

いれたて
noun, no-adjective
freshly brewed, just made
1. freshly brewed, just made
Describing coffee, tea, or other beverages that have just been prepared.
淹(い)れ立(た)てのコーヒーを飲(の)む。
To drink freshly brewed coffee.
淹(い)れ立(た)ての紅茶(こうちゃ)は香(かお)りがいい。
Freshly brewed tea has a nice aroma.
毎朝(まいあさ)、淹(い)れ立(た)てのコーヒーで一日(いちにち)を始(はじ)める。
Every morning, I start the day with freshly brewed coffee.

淹(い)れ立(た)て is part of a common pattern using ～立(た)て to indicate something just done.

WORD FORMATION:

  • 淹(い)れる (to brew) + 立(た)て (just done)

SIMILAR ～立(た)て PATTERNS:

  • 焼(や)き立(た)て: freshly baked
  • 揚(あ)げ立(た)て: freshly fried
  • 出来立(できた)て: freshly made
  • 炊(た)き立(た)て: freshly cooked (rice)

SPELLING NOTE:

  • Also written as 入(い)れ立(た)て using a simpler kanji
  • Often written in hiragana as いれたて

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 淹(い)れ立(た)てのコーヒー: freshly brewed coffee
  • 淹(い)れ立(た)ての緑茶(りょくちゃ): freshly made green tea
