読解力(どっかいりょく)

どっかいりょく
noun
reading comprehension, reading ability
1. reading comprehension, reading ability
The ability to read and understand written text.
読解力(どっかいりょく)を鍛(きた)える。
To train one's reading comprehension.
読解力(どっかいりょく)のテストで高(たか)い点(てん)を取(と)った。
I scored high on the reading comprehension test.
子(こ)どもの読解力(どっかいりょく)を伸(の)ばすには読書(どくしょ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Reading is important to develop children's reading comprehension.

読解力(どっかいりょく) refers to the ability to understand written texts. It is a key skill tested in language exams and important for academic success.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 読解力(どっかいりょく)を高(たか)める (to improve reading comprehension)
  • 読解力(どっかいりょく)テスト (reading comprehension test)
  • 読解力(どっかいりょく)の向上(こうじょう) (improvement in reading ability)

RELATED SKILLS:

  • 語彙力(ごいりょく) (vocabulary ability)
  • 聴解力(ちょうかいりょく) (listening comprehension)
  • 表現力(ひょうげんりょく) (expressive ability)
