へぼい

へぼい
i-adjective
lousy, crappy, incompetent
1. lousy, crappy, incompetent
A casual, somewhat rude way to describe something or someone as being of poor quality or skill.
へぼい選手(せんしゅ)だな。
What a lousy player.
この作品(さくひん)、へぼくない？
Isn't this work kind of crappy?
へぼいミスをしてしまった。
I made a stupid mistake.
腕(うで)がへぼいくせに偉(えら)そうにするな。
Don't act all high and mighty when you're so incompetent.

へぼい comes from へぼ (an unskilled person) and is used as a casual, somewhat insulting adjective. It implies lack of skill or poor quality.

Related words:

  • へぼ (noun: a loser, an amateur)
  • しょぼい (shabby, pathetic - similar nuance)
  • ダメ (no good - more general)

Typically used in casual conversation, often when criticizing or complaining about something.
