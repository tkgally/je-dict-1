ノリがいい

のりがいい
expression
fun, easy-going, enthusiastic
1. fun, easy-going, willing to join in
Describes someone who readily participates in activities, goes along with the mood, and is enjoyable to be around.
あの人(ひと)、ノリがいいね。
That person is really fun.
ノリがいいから誘(さそ)いやすい。
They're easy to invite because they're so easy-going.
彼女(かのじょ)はノリがいいからパーティーで人気(にんき)がある。
She's popular at parties because she's so enthusiastic.
ノリがいい友達(ともだち)と一緒(いっしょ)だと何(なん)でも楽(たの)しくなる。
Everything becomes fun when you're with friends who are easy-going.

ノリがいい describes someone who matches the mood of the group, participates enthusiastically, and makes social situations more enjoyable. The ノリ comes from 乗(の)り (riding, getting on), suggesting someone who "gets on board" with the energy or vibe.

Related expressions:

  • ノリが悪(わる)い: being a killjoy, not fun
  • ノリのいい人(ひと): a fun, enthusiastic person
  • ノリで: on impulse, caught up in the moment

This is casual vocabulary commonly used among young people.

Related Words

Antonym: ノリが悪(わる)い (being a killjoy)
