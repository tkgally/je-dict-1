繁華街(はんかがい)

はんかがい
noun
downtown, busy shopping/entertainment district
1. downtown, busy shopping/entertainment district
A bustling urban area with many shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues.
繁華街(はんかがい)で遊(あそ)ぶ。
To hang out downtown.
夜(よる)の繁華街(はんかがい)はネオンが美(うつく)しい。
The neon lights are beautiful in the downtown area at night.
渋谷(しぶや)は若者(わかもの)に人気(にんき)の繁華街(はんかがい)だ。
Shibuya is a downtown district popular with young people.
週末(しゅうまつ)の繁華街(はんかがい)は人(ひと)で溢(あふ)れている。
The downtown area is overflowing with people on weekends.

繁華街(はんかがい) refers to busy commercial and entertainment districts. Famous examples include Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Ikebukuro in Tokyo, and Minami in Osaka.

The term emphasizes bustling activity and commerce, not specifically entertainment like 歓楽街(かんらくがい).

Related terms:

  • 歓楽街(かんらくがい): entertainment/nightlife district
  • 商店街(しょうてんがい): shopping street
  • 住宅街(じゅうたくがい): residential area
