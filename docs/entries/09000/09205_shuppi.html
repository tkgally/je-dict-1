出費(しゅっぴ)

しゅっぴ
noun, suru verb
expense, expenditure, spending
1. expense, expenditure, spending
Money spent on something; an outlay of funds.
出費(しゅっぴ)が増(ふ)えた。
Expenses increased.
予想外(よそうがい)の出費(しゅっぴ)があった。
There were unexpected expenses.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しで大(おお)きな出費(しゅっぴ)になった。
Moving became a major expense.

出費(しゅっぴ) refers to money spent or expenses incurred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出費(しゅっぴ)がかさむ: expenses pile up
  • 予想外(よそうがい)の出費(しゅっぴ): unexpected expense
  • 大(おお)きな出費(しゅっぴ): major expense
  • 出費(しゅっぴ)を抑(おさ)える: to keep expenses down
  • 出費(しゅっぴ)を控(ひか)える: to cut back on spending

USAGE NOTES:

  • Neutral term for spending money
  • Often used when discussing budgets or financial planning
