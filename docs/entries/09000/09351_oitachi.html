生(お)い立(た)ち

おいたち
noun
upbringing, background
1. upbringing, background, personal history
The circumstances of one's growth from childhood, including family environment, education, and experiences that shaped who one is today.
彼(かれ)の生(お)い立(た)ちを聞(き)いた。
I heard about his upbringing.
複雑(ふくざつ)な生(お)い立(た)ちを持(も)っている。
They have a complicated background.
作家(さっか)の生(お)い立(た)ちを知(し)ると、作品(さくひん)の理解(りかい)が深(ふか)まることがある。
Knowing about an author's background can deepen your understanding of their works.

生(お)い立(た)ち refers to the narrative of how someone grew up — their family environment, upbringing, and experiences from childhood through adolescence.

ETYMOLOGY:
Noun form of the verb 生(お)い立(た)つ (to grow up).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生(お)い立(た)ちを語(かた)る: to tell about one's upbringing
  • 生(お)い立(た)ちを聞(き)く: to hear about someone's background
  • 複雑(ふくざつ)な生(お)い立(た)ち: complicated background
  • 恵(めぐ)まれた生(お)い立(た)ち: privileged upbringing
  • 生(お)い立(た)ちの記(き): record of one's upbringing (often read at weddings)

USAGE:
Common in biographical contexts, interviews, and wedding ceremonies. At Japanese weddings, the 生(お)い立(た)ちの紹介(しょうかい) (introduction of the couple's backgrounds) is a standard part of the program.
