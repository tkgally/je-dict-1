よ

よ
particle
sentence-ending particle (emphasis, assertion)
1. assertion, emphasis
Used to assert information that the speaker believes the listener doesn't know or to emphasize a point.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っているよ。
It's raining, you know.
もう時間(じかん)だよ。
It's time already!
これ、おいしいよ。
This is delicious, you know.
2. giving advice or commands
Adds emphasis to suggestions, advice, or gentle commands.
早(はや)く起(お)きなさいよ。
Wake up early!
気(き)をつけてね。
Be careful, okay?

よ is used to assert or emphasize information.

MAIN USES:
1. Assertion: 知(し)ってるよ (I know [assertive])
2. New information: 電車(でんしゃ)が来(き)たよ (The train has come!)
3. Emphasis: 本当(ほんとう)だよ (It's true!)
4. Advice/urging: 食(た)べなよ (Go ahead and eat)

CONTRAST: よ vs ね

  • よ: asserts information the listener may not know
  • ね: seeks agreement, confirms shared knowledge

COMBINATION: よね

  • Combines assertion with seeking agreement: そうだよね (That's right, you know?)
