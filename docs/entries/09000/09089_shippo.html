しっぽ

しっぽ
noun
tail
1. tail (of an animal)
The tail of an animal. The most common everyday word for tail, used especially when talking about pets.
犬(いぬ)がしっぽを振(ふ)る。
The dog wags its tail.
猫(ねこ)のしっぽは長(なが)い。
The cat's tail is long.
しっぽを踏(ふ)まないように気(き)をつけて。
Be careful not to step on its tail.
2. end, tail end
The end or trailing part of something, used figuratively.
しっぽを出(だ)す。
To show one's true colors (lit. 'to let one's tail show').
しっぽを掴(つか)む。
To catch someone red-handed (lit. 'to grab the tail').
犯人(はんにん)のしっぽを掴(つか)んだ。
We caught the culprit's trail.

KANJI: Can be written as 尻尾(しっぽ).

WORD FORMATION: From 尻(しり) (buttocks) + 尾(お) (tail).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • しっぽを振(ふ)る (to wag one's tail)
  • しっぽを巻(ま)いて逃(に)げる (to run away with one's tail between one's legs)
  • しっぽを出(だ)す (to reveal one's true nature)
  • しっぽを掴(つか)む (to catch someone's trail/evidence)

ALTERNATIVE: 尾(お) is a more literary/formal word for tail.
