身悶(みもだ)える

みもだえる
ichidan verb
to writhe, to squirm, to wriggle in agony
1. to writhe (in pain), to squirm, to wriggle in agony
To twist and move one's whole body intensely due to pain, discomfort, or strong emotion. More emphatic than 悶(もだ)える.
激痛(げきつう)に身悶(みもだ)える
to writhe in severe pain
彼(かれ)は苦(くる)しさに身悶(みもだ)えしていた。
He was writhing in agony.
恥(は)ずかしさで身悶(みもだ)えするほどの失敗(しっぱい)をした。
I made such an embarrassing mistake that I wanted to squirm.

FORMATION: Compound of 身(み) (body) + 悶(もだ)える (to writhe). The prefix 身(み) emphasizes the involvement of the whole body.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)).

CONJUGATION: Ichidan verb: 身悶(みもだ)えない, 身悶(みもだ)えます, 身悶(みもだ)えて.

USAGE: Often used figuratively for emotional distress (embarrassment, frustration) where one metaphorically wants to writhe.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 痛(いた)み/苦(くる)しみに身悶(みもだ)える (to writhe in pain/agony)
  • 恥(は)ずかしさに身悶(みもだ)える (to squirm with embarrassment)
  • 身悶(みもだ)えするほど (to the point of writhing)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 悶(もだ)える (to writhe - less emphatic)
  • のたうち回(まわ)る (to roll around in agony)
  • 身(み)をよじる (to twist one's body)
