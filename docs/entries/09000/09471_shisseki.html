叱責(しっせき)

しっせき
noun
reprimand, scolding, rebuke
1. reprimand, scolding, rebuke
The act of severely scolding someone for their mistakes or wrongdoing. Used in both personal and professional contexts, typically by someone in a position of authority.
上司(じょうし)に叱責(しっせき)された。
I was reprimanded by my boss.
厳(きび)しい叱責(しっせき)を受(う)けた。
I received a harsh reprimand.
部下(ぶか)のミスを叱責(しっせき)する。
To reprimand a subordinate for their mistake.
人前(ひとまえ)での叱責(しっせき)は避(さ)けるべきだ。
You should avoid reprimanding someone in public.

叱責(しっせき) is a formal word combining 叱(しか)る (to scold) and 責(せ)める (to blame/criticize), emphasizing both the scolding and criticism aspects.

Usage:

  • 叱責(しっせき)する (to reprimand, scold)
  • 叱責(しっせき)を受(う)ける (to be reprimanded)
  • 叱責(しっせき)を浴(あ)びる (to be subjected to reprimand)

Compared to similar words:

  • 叱(しか)る: to scold (general, informal)
  • 叱責(しっせき): to reprimand (formal, often workplace)
  • 譴責(けんせき): censure (very formal, official)
  • 戒(いまし)める: to admonish, warn

Related words:

  • 説教(せっきょう)する: to lecture, preach
  • 小言(こごと)を言(い)う: to nag, grumble
  • 怒鳴(どな)る: to yell at
