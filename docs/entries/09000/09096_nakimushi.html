なきむし

なきむし
noun
crybaby
1. crybaby, person who cries easily
A person, typically a child, who cries frequently or easily. Can be used teasingly or descriptively.
なきむし！
Crybaby!
あの子(こ)はなきむしだ。
That child is a crybaby.
小(ちい)さい頃(ころ)はなきむしだったけど、今(いま)は強(つよ)くなった。
I was a crybaby when I was little, but now I've become strong.

KANJI: Can be written as 泣(な)き虫(むし).

WORD FORMATION: 泣(な)き (crying) + 虫(むし) (bug). The むし suffix is used in personality descriptions (similar to 弱虫(よわむし) 'coward').

USAGE: Often used by children to tease each other. Can also be used self-deprecatingly by adults.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 弱虫(よわむし) (coward, weakling)
  • 意気地(いくじ)なし (coward)
  • 甘(あま)えん坊(ぼう) (spoiled child)
