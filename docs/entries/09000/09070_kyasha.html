華奢(きゃしゃ)

きゃしゃ
na-adjective
delicate; slender; slight; fragile
1. delicate; slender; slight; fragile
Having a delicate, slim build or appearance; looking fragile or fine-boned. Can describe both people and objects.
華奢(きゃしゃ)な体(からだ)つきだ。
She has a delicate build.
華奢(きゃしゃ)な手首(てくび)にブレスレットが似合(にあ)う。
The bracelet looks good on her slender wrist.
華奢(きゃしゃ)に見(み)えるが、意外(いがい)と力(ちから)がある。
She looks delicate but is surprisingly strong.

華奢(きゃしゃ) describes a delicate, slender appearance.

READINGS:

  • きゃしゃ: standard reading
  • かしゃ: alternative reading (same meaning)

WORD FORMATION:

  • 華(か) (flower, splendid) + 奢(しゃ) (luxury)
  • The reading きゃしゃ is irregular (ateji-like usage)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 華奢(きゃしゃ)な体(からだ)つき: delicate build
  • 華奢(きゃしゃ)な手足(てあし): slender limbs
  • 華奢(きゃしゃ)な作(つく)り: delicate construction

NUANCE:
Often carries a positive aesthetic connotation, suggesting elegance and refinement rather than weakness. However, can also imply fragility.

FOR OBJECTS:
Can describe furniture, jewelry, or other items with fine, delicate construction.
