自重(じちょう)

じちょう
noun
self-restraint, prudence
1. self-restraint, self-control, prudence
The act of restraining oneself from rash or inappropriate actions; exercising caution and discretion in one's behavior.
自重(じちょう)を求(もと)める
to call for self-restraint
発言(はつげん)には自重(じちょう)してください。
Please exercise restraint in your remarks.
政府(せいふ)は国民(こくみん)に外出(がいしゅつ)の自重(じちょう)を呼(よ)びかけた。
The government called on citizens to refrain from going out.

USAGE: Used as a noun or with する to form the verb 自重(じちょう)する (to exercise self-restraint).

HOMOPHONE: Different from 自重(じじゅう) (one's own weight), which is read differently.

NUANCE: 自重(じちょう) implies voluntary self-control, often used in formal or official contexts when asking people to moderate their behavior.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自重(じちょう)する (to exercise self-restraint)
  • 自重(じちょう)を求(もと)める (to call for self-restraint)
  • 自重(じちょう)を促(うなが)す (to urge self-restraint)
  • 発言(はつげん)の自重(じちょう) (restraint in remarks)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 慎(つつし)む (to refrain from, be prudent)
  • 控(ひか)える (to refrain from, hold back)
  • 自粛(じしゅく) (voluntary restraint)
  • 自制(じせい) (self-control)
