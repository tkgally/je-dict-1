見栄(みば)え

みばえ
noun
appearance; visual appeal
1. appearance; visual appeal; how something looks
The visual impression something makes; how attractive or impressive something appears when viewed.
見栄(みば)えのする料理(りょうり)を作(つく)る。
To make a visually appealing dish.
見栄(みば)えが良(よ)いほうが売(う)れやすい。
Things that look better sell more easily.
見栄(みば)えより味(あじ)を重視(じゅうし)したい。
I want to prioritize taste over appearance.

見栄(みば)え focuses on visual impression.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 見栄(みば)えがする: to look impressive/attractive
  • 見栄(みば)えが良(よ)い: to have good visual appeal
  • 見栄(みば)えを良(よ)くする: to improve the appearance

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often used for food, presentations, displays
  • Emphasizes the visual aspect specifically

RELATED WORDS:

  • 見(み)た目(め): appearance, looks (more general)
  • 体裁(ていさい): form, appearance, decorum
  • 外見(がいけん): outward appearance
