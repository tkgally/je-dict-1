けど

けど
conjunction, particle
but, although, however
1. but, however (contrast)
Connects two clauses that contrast with each other.
高(たか)いけど、買(か)いたい。
It's expensive, but I want to buy it.
行(い)きたいけど、時間(じかん)がない。
I want to go, but I don't have time.
知(し)ってるけど、言(い)えない。
I know, but I can't say.
2. although (concession)
Introduces a clause that provides background or softens what follows.
明日(あした)のことなんですけど...
About tomorrow... (softened introduction)
3. sentence softener
Used at the end of a sentence to soften the statement or leave it open-ended.
ちょっとお聞(き)きしたいんですけど。
I'd like to ask you something... (polite)

けど is a casual conjunction meaning 'but' or 'although'.

VARIATIONS (formal to casual):

  • けれども (most formal)
  • けれど
  • けど (casual)
  • だけど (more casual, with だ)

MAIN USES:
1. Contrast: A けど B (A but B)
2. Softening: 〜んですけど (introducing a topic politely)
3. Trailing off: 〜けど... (leaving unsaid)

NOTE: けど is very common in spoken Japanese. It often softens requests or introduces topics without sounding too direct.
