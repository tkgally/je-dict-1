1. divergence, deviation, gap
A significant separation or difference between things that should be close or aligned.
理想(りそう)と現実(げんじつ)の乖離(かいり)。
The gap between ideal and reality.
世論(せろん)と政策(せいさく)が乖離(かいり)している。
Public opinion and policy are diverging.
株価(かぶか)と実体経済(じったいけいざい)の乖離(かいり)が指摘(してき)されている。
The divergence between stock prices and the real economy is being pointed out.
両者(りょうしゃ)の認識(にんしき)には大(おお)きな乖離(かいり)がある。
There is a significant gap in the understanding of both parties.

乖離(かいり) is a formal term often used in academic, business, and political contexts. It suggests things that should be connected or aligned have drifted apart.

Common collocations:

  • 乖離(かいり)が生(しょう)じる: a gap emerges
  • 乖離(かいり)が大(おお)きい: a large gap
  • 〜と〜の乖離(かいり): gap between ~ and ~

Related terms:

  • 隔(へだ)たり: gap, distance
  • {ずれ}: discrepancy, gap
  • 齟齬(そご): discrepancy, mismatch

Related Words

Synonym: 齟齬(そご) (discrepancy, mismatch)
Related: 溝(みぞ) (gap, gulf)
