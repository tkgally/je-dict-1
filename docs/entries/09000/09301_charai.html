チャラい

ちゃらい
i-adjective
flashy; frivolous; superficial
1. flashy; gaudy; showy
Describing an appearance or style that is overly flashy or attention-seeking.
チャラい服(ふく)だね。
Those are flashy clothes.
チャラい髪(かみ)型(がた)にした。
I got a flashy hairstyle.
あのアクセサリーはちょっとチャラい。
Those accessories are a bit gaudy.
2. frivolous; superficial; flaky
Describing a person who seems shallow, unreliable, or overly focused on having fun and flirting.
チャラい男(おとこ)は嫌(きら)いだ。
I don't like frivolous guys.
彼(かれ)はチャラく見(み)えるけど真面目(まじめ)だ。
He looks superficial but he's actually serious.
チャラい態度(たいど)を取(と)るのはやめて。
Stop acting so frivolous.

チャラい comes from チャラチャラ, an onomatopoeia for jingling sounds (like cheap accessories) or frivolous behavior.

REGISTER:
This is casual slang, often used critically. It's not appropriate for formal contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • チャラい男(おとこ)/チャラ男(お): a frivolous/flashy guy
  • チャラく見(み)える: to look frivolous/flashy

CONJUGATION:
As an i-adjective: チャラくない (negative), チャラかった (past), チャラく (adverb form).
