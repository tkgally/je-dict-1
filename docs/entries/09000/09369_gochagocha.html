ごちゃごちゃ

ごちゃごちゃ
adverb / noun / na-adjective
cluttered, messy, jumbled
1. cluttered, messy, in disarray
Describes a physical state where things are mixed up, disorganized, or scattered in a disorderly way.
部屋(へや)がごちゃごちゃだ。
The room is a mess.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)がごちゃごちゃしている。
The desk is all cluttered.
引(ひ)き出(だ)しの中(なか)がごちゃごちゃで何(なに)も見(み)つからない。
The drawer is so messy I can't find anything.
2. nagging, complaining, making a fuss
Describes repetitive, annoying speech, especially complaints or nitpicking.
ごちゃごちゃ言(い)うな。
Stop nagging.
ごちゃごちゃ文句(もんく)を言(い)わないで。
Stop complaining so much.
細(こま)かいことをごちゃごちゃ言(い)うのはやめて。
Stop nitpicking about every little thing.

ごちゃごちゃ is a mimetic word (onomatopoeia) that vividly describes disorder and confusion.

Usage patterns:

  • ごちゃごちゃ(と)した + noun: a messy/cluttered ~
  • ごちゃごちゃ言(い)う: to nag, complain repeatedly
  • ごちゃごちゃになる: to become jumbled/mixed up

Similar expressions:

  • ぐちゃぐちゃ: squishy mess (often wet)
  • めちゃくちゃ: completely messed up, chaotic
