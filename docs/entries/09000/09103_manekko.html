1. copycat, imitator, imitation
A childish word for someone who copies others, or the act of copying. Often used teasingly among children.
まねっこしないで！
Stop copying me!
あの子(こ)はまねっこばかりする。
That child always copies others.
赤(あか)ちゃんは大人(おとな)のまねっこをして成長(せいちょう)する。
Babies grow by imitating adults.

まねっこ is a childish, playful word for copying or imitating someone.

WORD FORMATION:
真似(まね) (imitation) + っこ (childish diminutive suffix). Can also be written as 真似(まね)っこ.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • まねっこする: to copy, to imitate
  • まねっこしないで！: stop copying me!
  • まねっこ遊(あそ)び: imitation play

NUANCE:
Almost exclusively used by or about children. When a child says まねっこしないで!, it's a mild, teasing complaint. In parenting contexts, まねっこ is viewed positively — toddlers learn through imitation.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 真似(まね): imitation (neutral, all-ages)
  • 真似(まね)する: to imitate (general use)
  • まねっこ: copycat (childish, playful)
  • 模倣(もほう): imitation (formal, academic)
