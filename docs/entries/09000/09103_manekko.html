まねっこ

まねっこ
noun
copycat, imitation
1. copycat, imitator, imitation
A childish word for someone who copies others, or the act of copying. Often used teasingly among children.
まねっこしないで！
Stop copying me!
あの子(こ)はまねっこばかりする。
That child always copies others.
赤(あか)ちゃんは大人(おとな)のまねっこをして成長(せいちょう)する。
Babies grow by imitating adults.

KANJI: Can be written as 真似(まね)っこ.

WORD FORMATION: 真似(まね) (imitation) + っこ (childish diminutive suffix).

USAGE: Functions as a noun and as a する verb (まねっこする).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • まねっこする (to copy, to imitate)
  • まねっこ遊(あそ)び (imitation play)

NUANCE: Childish and playful. When used accusingly, it's a mild complaint. Among toddlers, まねっこ is a normal part of development.
