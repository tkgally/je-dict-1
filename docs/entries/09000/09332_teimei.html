低迷(ていめい)

ていめい
noun, suru verb
stagnation, slump
1. stagnation, slump, sluggishness
A state of being stuck at a low level without improvement. Often used for economic conditions, business performance, or other measurable indicators that remain depressed.
景気(けいき)が低迷(ていめい)している。
The economy is stagnant.
売(う)り上(あ)げの低迷(ていめい)が続(つづ)いている。
The slump in sales continues.
株価(かぶか)は低迷(ていめい)を続(つづ)けており、回復(かいふく)の見通(みとお)しが立(た)たない。
Stock prices continue to stagnate, with no prospect of recovery in sight.

低迷(ていめい) describes a prolonged state of poor performance or stagnation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 景気(けいき)の低迷(ていめい) (economic slump)
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)の低迷(ていめい) (sluggish business performance)
  • 低迷(ていめい)から脱(だっ)する (break out of a slump)
  • 長期(ちょうき)低迷(ていめい) (prolonged stagnation)

OPPOSITE: 好調(こうちょう) (favorable, thriving)
