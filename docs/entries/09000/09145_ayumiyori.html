歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)り

あゆみより
noun
compromise, meeting halfway
1. compromise, meeting halfway, coming together
The act of both parties moving toward each other's position to reach an agreement. Emphasizes mutual effort and willingness to find common ground.
歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りの姿勢(しせい)を見(み)せる
to show a willingness to compromise
双方(そうほう)の歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りが必要(ひつよう)だ。
Compromise from both sides is necessary.
長年(ながねん)の対立(たいりつ)を終(お)わらせるため、両国(りょうこく)が歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りを見(み)せた。
Both countries showed a willingness to compromise in order to end years of conflict.

USAGE: Often used in expressions like 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りを見(み)せる (to show willingness to compromise) or 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りがある (there is compromise). The related verb 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)る means "to approach" or "to compromise."

NUANCE: 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)り emphasizes the mutual nature of compromise—both parties meeting in the middle rather than one side simply giving in. It has a positive connotation of cooperation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りの姿勢(しせい) (attitude of compromise)
  • 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りを見(み)せる (to show willingness to compromise)
  • 相互(そうご)の歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)り (mutual compromise)
  • 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)りがない (no willingness to compromise)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 譲歩(じょうほ) (concession - more one-sided)
  • 妥協(だきょう) (compromise - more general)
  • 折(お)り合(あ)い (compromise, settlement)
