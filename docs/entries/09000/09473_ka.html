か

か
particle
question marker, or, whether
1. question marker
Placed at the end of a sentence to form a question. With polite forms, rising intonation is optional; with plain forms, rising intonation is typical.
これは何(なん)ですか。
What is this?
明日(あした)来(き)ますか。
Are you coming tomorrow?
日本語(にほんご)が分(わ)かりますか。
Do you understand Japanese?
お名前(なまえ)は何(なん)ですか。
What is your name?
一緒(いっしょ)に食(た)べませんか。
Won't you eat with me?
2. or (between options)
Used between nouns or clauses to present alternatives.
コーヒーか紅茶(こうちゃ)
coffee or tea
行(い)くか行(い)かないか、決(き)めてください。
Please decide whether you're going or not.
電車(でんしゃ)かバスで帰(かえ)ります。
I'll go home by train or bus.
お茶(ちゃ)か水(みず)をください。
Tea or water, please.
3. whether, if (embedded questions)
Marks embedded questions, equivalent to 'whether' or 'if' in English.
彼(かれ)が来(く)るかどうか分(わ)からない。
I don't know whether he's coming.
何(なに)を買(か)ったか教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me what you bought.
誰(だれ)が来(く)るかまだ聞(き)いていません。
I haven't heard yet who is coming.
彼(かれ)が元気(げんき)かどうか知(し)りたい。
I want to know whether he is doing well.
いつ行(い)くか決(き)めましたか。
Have you decided when you're going?

か is the primary question-forming particle in Japanese.

MAIN USES:
1. Yes/No questions: 行(い)きますか (Are you going?)
2. WH-questions: 何(なん)ですか (What is it?)
3. Alternative questions: Aか B (A or B)
4. Embedded questions: …かどうか (whether…)

PATTERNS:

  • Sentence + か: direct question
  • A か B: A or B
  • Verb/Adj + か + どうか: whether or not
  • Question word + か: embedded question

WITH POLITE VS. PLAIN FORMS: か is standard after です and ます forms, where it is the normal, neutral way to ask a question. After plain forms it is much less common in everyday conversation and can sound blunt, literary, or challenging (行(い)くの? is the usual casual question instead).

AかB VS. AかBか: with two nouns, コーヒーか紅茶(こうちゃ) is the ordinary pattern; repeating か after the second option (行(い)くか行(い)かないか) is normal when the alternatives are clauses, and sets up a choice the listener is asked to resolve.

NOTE: In casual speech, か may be omitted and replaced by rising intonation alone. In very casual male speech, か can sound blunt.

Related Words

Related: の (casual question particle used instead of か)
Related: ね (sentence-final particle seeking agreement)
Related: でしょう (…ですか softened to a supposition)
Related: かな (self-directed question: I wonder)
