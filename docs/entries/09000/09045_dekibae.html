出来栄(できば)え

できばえ
noun
result; quality of work; execution
1. quality of work; result; execution; workmanship
How well something is made or done; the quality of the finished product or performance.
出来栄(できば)えに満足(まんぞく)している。
I'm satisfied with how it turned out.
作品(さくひん)の出来栄(できば)えを審査(しんさ)する。
To judge the quality of the works.
予想(よそう)を上回(うわまわ)る出来栄(できば)えで完成(かんせい)した。
It was completed with a result exceeding expectations.

出来栄(できば)え emphasizes quality of completion.

WORD FORMATION:

  • 出来(でき) (completion, result) + 栄(ば)え (flourishing, impressive appearance)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 出来栄(できば)えがいい: turned out well
  • 出来栄(できば)えに満足(まんぞく)する: to be satisfied with the result
  • 出来栄(できば)えを確認(かくにん)する: to check the quality

USAGE:

  • Used for crafts, projects, performances, productions
  • Focuses on the quality of execution

RELATED WORDS:

  • 見栄(みば)え: visual appearance
  • 仕上(しあ)がり: finish, completion
  • 出来(でき): result, outcome
