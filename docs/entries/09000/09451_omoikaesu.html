思(おも)い返(かえ)す

おもいかえす
verb
to reconsider; to recall, to look back on
1. to reconsider, to think again
To think about something again and possibly change one's mind or perspective on it.
よく思(おも)い返(かえ)してみた。
I thought it over carefully.
冷静(れいせい)に思(おも)い返(かえ)せば、彼(かれ)の言(い)うことも分(わ)かる。
Thinking it over calmly, I can understand what he's saying.
2. to recall, to look back on, to reflect on
To bring past events or memories to mind and think about them again.
今(いま)思(おも)い返(かえ)しても恥(は)ずかしい。
Even now when I recall it, I feel embarrassed.
学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)を思(おも)い返(かえ)す。
To look back on one's student days.
あの日(ひ)のことを思(おも)い返(かえ)すと涙(なみだ)が出(で)る。
When I recall that day, tears come to my eyes.
過去(かこ)を思(おも)い返(かえ)して反省(はんせい)する。
To reflect on the past and learn from one's mistakes.

思(おも)い返(かえ)す combines 思(おも)う (to think) with 返(かえ)す (to return), conveying the sense of returning to past thoughts or reconsidering.

Usage patterns:

  • 〜を思(おも)い返(かえ)す (to recall/reconsider something)
  • 今(いま)思(おも)い返(かえ)しても (even now when I think back)
  • よく思(おも)い返(かえ)してみる (to think it over carefully)

Transitivity: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
Conjugation: 五段(ごだん) (godan/u-verb)

Related words:

  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember, recall (more spontaneous)
  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to look back on, reflect
  • 考(かんが)え直(なお)す: to reconsider, think again
