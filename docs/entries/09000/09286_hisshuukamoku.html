必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)

ひっしゅうかもく
noun
required course, compulsory subject
1. required course, compulsory subject
An academic course that students must take to fulfill graduation requirements.
英語(えいご)は必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)だ。
English is a required course.
必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)を落(お)とすと卒業(そつぎょう)できない。
If you fail a required course, you can't graduate.
一年生(いちねんせい)の必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)は全部(ぜんぶ)で十(じゅう)科目(かもく)ある。
There are ten required courses in the first year.

必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく) refers to courses that are mandatory for all students in a program. Failing to complete required courses prevents graduation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)を取(と)る (to take a required course)
  • 必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)を履修(りしゅう)する (to enroll in a required course)
  • 必修科目(ひっしゅうかもく)の単位(たんい) (credits for required courses)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 選択科目(せんたくかもく) (elective course)
  • 必修(ひっしゅう) (compulsory, required)
  • 履修(りしゅう) (course enrollment)
