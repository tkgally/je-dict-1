尽力(じんりょく)

じんりょく
noun, suru verb
great effort, exertion
1. great effort, exertion, endeavor
The act of devoting all one's strength and effort to achieving something. A formal term often used in business and official contexts.
ご尽力(じんりょく)いただきありがとうございます。
Thank you for your efforts.
成功(せいこう)のために尽力(じんりょく)する。
To make every effort for success.
問題(もんだい)解決(かいけつ)に尽力(じんりょく)してまいりますので、今(いま)しばらくお待(ま)ちください。
We are working hard to resolve the issue, so please wait a little longer.

尽力(じんりょく) is a formal term for making great effort or exerting oneself.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ご尽力(じんりょく)いただき (for your efforts - honorific)
  • 尽力(じんりょく)してまいります (I/we will do our best - humble)
  • 尽力(じんりょく)を惜(お)しまない (spare no effort)

USAGE NOTE: More formal than 頑張(がんば)る or 努力(どりょく). Often used in business correspondence and formal speeches to acknowledge or promise dedicated effort.
