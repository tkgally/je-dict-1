辞令(じれい)

じれい
noun
letter of appointment; official notice
1. letter of appointment; official notice of personnel change
A formal document issued by an organization notifying an employee of appointment, promotion, transfer, or other personnel changes.
転勤(てんきん)の辞令(じれい)が出(で)た。
I received a transfer notice.
昇進(しょうしん)の辞令(じれい)を受(う)け取(と)った。
I received a promotion notice.
来月(らいげつ)の人事異動(じんじいどう)の辞令(じれい)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
The personnel change notices for next month were announced.

辞令(じれい) is a formal document used in Japanese companies and organizations.

COMMON TYPES:

  • 採用(さいよう)辞令(じれい): appointment letter
  • 昇進(しょうしん)辞令(じれい): promotion notice
  • 転勤(てんきん)辞令(じれい): transfer notice
  • 異動(いどう)辞令(じれい): personnel change notice
  • 退職(たいしょく)辞令(じれい): retirement notice

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 辞令(じれい)が出(で)る: to receive an official notice
  • 辞令(じれい)を受(う)ける: to receive an appointment
  • 内示(ないじ): informal notice (before official 辞令(じれい))

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese companies, 辞令(じれい) is typically issued at the beginning of the fiscal year (April) when major personnel changes occur.
