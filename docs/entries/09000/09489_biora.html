ビオラ

びおら
noun
viola (musical instrument)
1. viola (musical instrument)
A stringed instrument larger than a violin, played with a bow, with a deeper, mellower tone.
ビオラを弾(ひ)く。
To play the viola.
彼女(かのじょ)はビオラを習(なら)っている。
She is learning the viola.
ビオラはバイオリンより大(おお)きい。
The viola is larger than the violin.

ビオラ is the viola, a stringed instrument in the violin family.

STRING FAMILY (high to low):

  • バイオリン (violin) - highest
  • ビオラ (viola)
  • チェロ (cello)
  • コントラバス (double bass) - lowest

Note: Do not confuse with ビオラ (the flower viola/pansy), though the same word is used.
