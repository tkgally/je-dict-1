売(う)り込(こ)み

うりこみ
noun
sales pitch, self-promotion
1. sales pitch, promotion, marketing
The act of actively promoting or trying to sell a product, service, or idea to potential customers.
新製品(しんせいひん)の売(う)り込(こ)みに行(い)く。
Go to pitch a new product.
営業(えいぎょう)マンが売(う)り込(こ)みに来(き)た。
A salesperson came with a sales pitch.
電話(でんわ)での売(う)り込(こ)みはあまり効果(こうか)がない。
Phone sales pitches aren't very effective.
2. self-promotion, pitching oneself
The act of promoting one's own abilities, talents, or qualifications to gain opportunities or recognition.
自分(じぶん)を売(う)り込(こ)む。
To pitch oneself, to self-promote.
面接(めんせつ)では自分(じぶん)の売(う)り込(こ)みが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Self-promotion is important in job interviews.
芸能界(げいのうかい)で成功(せいこう)するには、積極的(せっきょくてき)な売(う)り込(こ)みが欠(か)かせない。
To succeed in show business, aggressive self-promotion is essential.

売(う)り込(こ)み refers to promoting products or oneself actively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売(う)り込(こ)みに行(い)く (go on a sales call)
  • 自分(じぶん)を売(う)り込(こ)む (promote oneself)
  • 売(う)り込(こ)み電話(でんわ) (sales call)
  • 売(う)り込(こ)み攻勢(こうせい) (aggressive sales push)

RELATED VERB: 売(う)り込(こ)む (to promote, to pitch)
