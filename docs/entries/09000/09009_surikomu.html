刷(す)り込(こ)む

すりこむ
verb
to imprint; to instill; to rub in
1. to imprint; to instill (ideas, values)
To deeply embed ideas, beliefs, or behaviors through repeated exposure or conditioning, especially during formative periods.
子供(こども)に良(よ)い習慣(しゅうかん)を刷(す)り込(こ)む。
Instill good habits in children.
幼(おさな)い頃(ころ)に刷(す)り込(こ)まれた価値観(かちかん)。
Values imprinted in early childhood.
ひな鳥(どり)は最初(さいしょ)に見(み)たものを親(おや)だと刷(す)り込(こ)まれる。
Baby birds are imprinted to see the first thing they see as their parent.
2. to rub in; to work into
To physically rub something into a surface so it penetrates or absorbs.
クリームを肌(はだ)に刷(す)り込(こ)む。
Rub the cream into your skin.
ワックスを床(ゆか)に刷(す)り込(こ)んだ。
I rubbed wax into the floor.
下味(したあじ)を肉(にく)に刷(す)り込(こ)む。
Rub the seasoning into the meat.

刷(す)り込(こ)む has psychological and physical meanings.

SENSE 1 - PSYCHOLOGICAL:

  • 刷(す)り込(こ)み: imprinting (psychology term)
  • 価値観(かちかん)を刷(す)り込(こ)む: to instill values
  • 刷(す)り込(こ)まれる: to be conditioned/imprinted

IMPRINTING (psychology):
The term 刷(す)り込(こ)み is used in Japanese psychology for "imprinting," the phenomenon where young animals form attachments during critical periods.

SENSE 2 - PHYSICAL:

  • クリームを刷(す)り込(こ)む: to rub in cream
  • 調味料(ちょうみりょう)を刷(す)り込(こ)む: to rub in seasonings
