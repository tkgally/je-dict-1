値動(ねうご)き

ねうごき
noun
price movement, price fluctuation
1. price movement, price fluctuation
Changes or trends in the price of stocks, commodities, or other assets.
値動(ねうご)きを見(み)る。
To watch the price movement.
今日(きょう)の株(かぶ)の値動(ねうご)きは小(ちい)さかった。
Today's stock price movement was small.
この銘柄(めいがら)は値動(ねうご)きが激(はげ)しい。
This stock has volatile price movements.
為替(かわせ)の値動(ねうご)きを分析(ぶんせき)して投資(とうし)する。
I analyze currency price movements for investing.

値動(ねうご)き is a neutral term describing any price change, whether up or down. It's essential vocabulary for stock trading and investment discussions.

Common collocations:

  • 値動(ねうご)きが激(はげ)しい: volatile price movement
  • 値動(ねうご)きを追(お)う: to track price movements
  • 値動(ねうご)きを分析(ぶんせき)する: to analyze price movements

Related terms:

  • 乱高下(らんこうげ): wild fluctuation
  • 変動(へんどう): fluctuation
  • 推移(すいい): transition, trend
