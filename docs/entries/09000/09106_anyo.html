1. foot, leg (baby talk)
Baby talk word for foot or leg.
あんよを出(だ)して。
Put out your feet.
あんよが冷(つめ)たいね。
Your feet are cold, aren't they?
2. walking, toddling (baby talk)
Baby talk word for walking, especially a baby's first steps.
あんよが上手(じょうず)だね。
You're walking so well!
赤(あか)ちゃんがあんよを始(はじ)めた。
The baby started walking.
あんよできるかな？
Can you walk?
よちよちあんよがかわいい。
Their toddling is so cute.

あんよ is baby talk (幼児語(ようじご)) for "foot" or "walking." It comes from 足(あし) (foot/leg) softened into a child-friendly form.

USAGE:

Functions as a noun (foot/leg) and a する verb (to walk).

  • あんよが上手(じょうず): good at walking (praise for toddlers)
  • あんよする/あんよできる: to walk (baby talk)
  • よちよちあんよ: toddling walk

MILESTONE:
Often used when discussing a baby's first steps — a key developmental milestone. Parents eagerly await when their child can あんよする.

RELATED BABY TALK WORDS:

  • おてて: hands (from 手(て))
  • おめめ: eyes (from 目(め))
  • ねんね: sleep (from 寝(ね)る)
  • まんま: food (from ごはん/まま)
  • おくち: mouth (from 口(くち))

REGISTER:
Used only with babies and very small children. Using it with adults would be humorous or condescending.
