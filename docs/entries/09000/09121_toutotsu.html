唐突(とうとつ)

とうとつ
na-adjective
sudden, abrupt, unexpected
1. sudden, abrupt, unexpected, out of the blue
Happening suddenly without warning or introduction. Often implies a breach of social expectations or conversational flow.
唐突(とうとつ)な質問(しつもん)
an abrupt question
唐突(とうとつ)ですが、ご結婚(けっこん)されていますか。
This is sudden, but are you married?
会議(かいぎ)中(ちゅう)に彼(かれ)は唐突(とうとつ)に辞職(じしょく)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
He abruptly announced his resignation during the meeting.

USAGE: 唐突(とうとつ) often implies social awkwardness or a break in expected conversational flow. The phrase 唐突(とうとつ)ですが is commonly used to acknowledge that a question or statement comes without proper lead-in.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 唐突(とうとつ)な質問(しつもん)/発言(はつげん)/訪問(ほうもん) (abrupt question/remark/visit)
  • 唐突(とうとつ)ですが... (this is sudden, but...)
  • 唐突(とうとつ)に始(はじ)まる/終(お)わる (to start/end abruptly)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 突然(とつぜん) (sudden - more neutral, less socially loaded)
  • 急(きゅう) (sudden, hasty - emphasizes speed)
  • 藪(やぶ)から棒(ぼう) (out of nowhere - idiomatic)
