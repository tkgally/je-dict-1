直進(ちょくしん)

ちょくしん
noun, suru verb
going straight, straight ahead
1. going straight, straight ahead
Proceeding forward in a straight line without turning; used especially in traffic and navigation contexts.
ここを直進(ちょくしん)する。
Go straight from here.
交差点(こうさてん)を直進(ちょくしん)してください。
Please go straight through the intersection.
直進(ちょくしん)する車(くるま)が優先(ゆうせん)だ。
Vehicles going straight have priority.

直進(ちょくしん) means to proceed straight ahead, commonly used in traffic and navigation contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 直進(ちょくしん)する: to go straight
  • 直進(ちょくしん)車線(しゃせん): through lane
  • 直進(ちょくしん)禁止(きんし): no through traffic
  • 直進(ちょくしん)優先(ゆうせん): straight-through priority

TRAFFIC DIRECTIONS:

  • 左折(させつ): turn left
  • 直進(ちょくしん): go straight
  • 右折(うせつ): turn right
