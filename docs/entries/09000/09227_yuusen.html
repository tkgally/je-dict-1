有線(ゆうせん)

ゆうせん
noun, no-adjective
wired, cable
1. wired, cable
Communication or technology that uses physical cables or wires for connection, as opposed to wireless.
有線(ゆうせん)LANの方(ほう)が安定(あんてい)している。
Wired LAN is more stable.
有線(ゆうせん)イヤホンを使(つか)っている。
I use wired earphones.
ゲームには有線(ゆうせん)接続(せつぞく)がおすすめだ。
A wired connection is recommended for gaming.

有線(ゆうせん) means 'wired' or 'cabled,' referring to connections using physical wires.

WORD COMPOSITION:

  • 有(ゆう): having, existing
  • 線(せん): wire, line

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有線(ゆうせん)LAN: wired LAN, Ethernet
  • 有線(ゆうせん)接続(せつぞく): wired connection
  • 有線(ゆうせん)マウス: wired mouse
  • 有線(ゆうせん)放送(ほうそう): cable broadcasting

USAGE NOTES:

  • Preferred for stable, high-speed connections
  • Common in gaming and professional audio/video setups
  • Sometimes called ケーブル}{接続(せつぞく)

Related Words

Antonym: 無線(むせん) (wireless)
