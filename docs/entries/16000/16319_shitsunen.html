失念(しつねん)

しつねん
noun, verb (suru)
forgetting, lapse of memory
1. forgetting, lapse of memory
A formal or polite way to say one has forgotten something. Commonly used in business or formal settings as a humble expression when admitting to having forgotten.
失念(しつねん)しておりました。
I'm afraid I forgot. (humble/formal)
名前(なまえ)を失念(しつねん)してしまいました。
I'm sorry, I've forgotten the name.
大事(だいじ)な会議(かいぎ)の日程(にってい)を失念(しつねん)しており、大変(たいへん)申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I deeply apologize for having forgotten the date of the important meeting.

USAGE NOTES:
失念(しつねん) is a formal and somewhat humble way to say "I forgot." It is most commonly used in business correspondence, emails, and formal spoken language. Using 失念(しつねん) instead of 忘(わす)れる shows self-awareness and politeness when admitting a mistake.

REGISTER:
This is a formal word. In casual conversation, 忘(わす)れる is standard. 失念(しつねん) would sound stiff or overly formal among friends.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 忘(わす)れる: to forget (everyday word)
  • 度忘(どわす)れ: a momentary lapse of memory, tip-of-the-tongue forgetting
