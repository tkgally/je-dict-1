避暑(ひしょ)

ひしょ
noun / suru verb
escaping the summer heat; summer retreat
1. escaping the summer heat; summer retreat
The practice of going to a cooler location, typically mountains or highland areas, to escape the intense summer heat. Also refers to the stay itself.
軽井沢(かるいざわ)に避暑(ひしょ)に行(い)く。
To go to Karuizawa to escape the summer heat.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)は山(やま)で避暑(ひしょ)する。
Every summer, I retreat to the mountains to escape the heat.
避暑地(ひしょち)として有名(ゆうめい)な高原(こうげん)のホテルに一週間(いっしゅうかん)泊(と)まった。
I stayed for a week at a hotel in the highlands that is famous as a summer retreat.

USAGE:
A seasonal concept closely tied to Japan's hot and humid summers. Popular 避暑地(ひしょち) (summer retreat destinations) include 軽井沢(かるいざわ), 那須(なす), and 箱根(はこね). Historically associated with the wealthy, but now accessible to all.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 避暑地(ひしょち) (summer retreat destination)
  • 避暑(ひしょ)に行(い)く (to go on a summer retreat)
  • 避暑(ひしょ)客(きゃく) (summer visitors escaping the heat)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 避寒(ひかん): escaping the winter cold — the winter counterpart, going to warmer places
  • 納涼(のうりょう): enjoying the cool — seeking coolness through activities like fireworks or river outings, without necessarily traveling
