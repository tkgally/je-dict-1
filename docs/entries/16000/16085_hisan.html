飛散(ひさん)

ひさん
noun
scattering; dispersal; flying about
1. scattering; dispersal; flying about
The act of small particles or fragments scattering or being dispersed through the air. Commonly used for pollen, debris, sparks, and other airborne materials.
花粉(かふん)が飛散(ひさん)している。
Pollen is being dispersed in the air.
今年(ことし)は花粉(かふん)の飛散(ひさん)量(りょう)が多(おお)い。
The amount of pollen dispersal is high this year.
ガラスの破片(はへん)が飛散(ひさん)して危険(きけん)なので、近(ちか)づかないでください。
Glass fragments have scattered and it's dangerous, so please stay away.

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered in news and weather reports about pollen season (花粉(かふん)飛散(ひさん)). Also used for debris from explosions, accidents, or construction. Used as a suru-verb (飛散(ひさん)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 花粉(かふん)飛散(ひさん) (pollen dispersal)
  • 飛散(ひさん)防止(ぼうし) (prevention of scattering)
  • 飛散(ひさん)量(りょう) (amount of dispersal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 散乱(さんらん): scattering, being strewn about — for objects on the ground rather than in the air
  • 拡散(かくさん): diffusion, spreading — broader dispersal over a wider area
