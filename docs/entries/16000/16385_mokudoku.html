黙読(もくどく)

もくどく
noun / suru-verb
silent reading; reading to oneself
1. silent reading; reading to oneself
Reading without vocalizing the words, as opposed to reading aloud. Commonly used in educational settings.
この文章(ぶんしょう)を黙読(もくどく)してください。
Please read this passage silently.
図書館(としょかん)では黙読(もくどく)がマナーだ。
Reading silently is the proper etiquette in the library.
音読(おんどく)より黙読(もくどく)のほうが速(はや)く読(よ)める。
You can read faster with silent reading than reading aloud.

CONTRAST:
黙読(もくどく) (silent reading) is the opposite of 音読(おんどく) (reading aloud). In Japanese schools, both are practiced. 音読(おんどく) is emphasized in language learning because it helps with pronunciation and retention, while 黙読(もくどく) is used for comprehension exercises.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黙読(もくどく)する (to read silently)
  • 黙読(もくどく)で読(よ)む (to read by silent reading)
  • 黙読(もくどく)の時間(じかん) (silent reading time)

KANJI NOTE:
黙(もく) means silence, as in 沈黙(ちんもく) (silence). 読(どく) means reading.
