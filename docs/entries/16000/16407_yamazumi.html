山積(やまづ)み

やまづみ
noun
huge pile; heaping stack
1. huge pile; heaping stack; mountain of things
A large quantity of items piled high, either literally (physical objects stacked up) or figuratively (an overwhelming number of tasks, problems, or issues).
書類(しょるい)が山積(やまづ)みになっている。
Documents are piled up in a mountain.
倉庫(そうこ)には段(だん)ボールが山積(やまづ)みにされていた。
Cardboard boxes were stacked high in the warehouse.
解決(かいけつ)すべき問題(もんだい)が山積(やまづ)みで、何(なに)から手(て)をつけていいかわからない。
There's a mountain of problems to solve, and I don't know where to start.

USAGE:
Most commonly appears in the pattern 山積(やまづ)みになっている (to be piled up) or 山積(やまづ)みにする (to pile up). Also used figuratively for abstract things like 課題(かだい) (issues) and 問題(もんだい) (problems).

RELATED:
The verb form 山積(さんせき)する is a more formal synonym used primarily in written or business contexts to describe accumulated problems: 問題(もんだい)が山積(さんせき)している.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 山積(やまづ)みになる (to become piled up)
  • 山積(やまづ)みにする (to pile up)
  • 書類(しょるい)が山積(やまづ)み (documents piled high)
  • 問題(もんだい)が山積(やまづ)み (a mountain of problems)
