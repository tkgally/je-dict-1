講義室(こうぎしつ)

こうぎしつ
noun
lecture room; lecture hall
1. lecture room; lecture hall
A room in a university or educational institution designed for lectures. Typically has tiered seating arranged for listening to a speaker.
講義室(こうぎしつ)に向(む)かう。
To head to the lecture room.
この講義室(こうぎしつ)は三百人(さんびゃくにん)収容(しゅうよう)できる。
This lecture hall can accommodate three hundred people.
講義室(こうぎしつ)の前(まえ)の席(せき)に座(すわ)った方(ほう)が、先生(せんせい)の話(はなし)がよく聞(き)こえる。
If you sit in the front of the lecture room, you can hear the professor better.

USAGE:
講義室(こうぎしつ) is primarily used in university settings. Smaller university rooms may be called 教室(きょうしつ) (classroom), while 講義室(こうぎしつ) implies a room specifically set up for lectures.

WORD FORMATION:
講義(こうぎ) (lecture) + 室(しつ) (room).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(だい)講義室(こうぎしつ): large lecture hall
  • 講義室(こうぎしつ)の変更(へんこう): room change for a lecture
