1. mandatory retirement; retirement at the prescribed age
Leaving one's employment upon reaching the company's or organization's mandatory retirement age. In Japan, this is typically 60 or 65 years old.
来年(らいねん)定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)する。
I will retire at mandatory age next year.
父(ちち)は六十五歳(ろくじゅうごさい)で定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)した。
My father retired at age 65.
定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)後(ご)は趣味(しゅみ)の旅行(りょこう)を楽(たの)しみたいと思(おも)っている。
After mandatory retirement, I want to enjoy my hobby of traveling.

Compound noun: 定年(ていねん) (mandatory retirement age) + 退職(たいしょく) (resignation, leaving a job). Commonly used with する as a suru-verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)する (to retire at mandatory age)
  • 定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)後(ご) (after mandatory retirement)
  • 定年退職(ていねんたいしょく)を迎(むか)える (to reach mandatory retirement)

CULTURE:
Japan's mandatory retirement age has been gradually raised. Many companies now offer 再雇用(さいこよう) (re-employment) programs allowing employees to continue working past the official retirement age, typically at reduced pay.
