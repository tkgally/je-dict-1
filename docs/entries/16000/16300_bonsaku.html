凡作(ぼんさく)

ぼんさく
noun
mediocre work; unremarkable piece
1. mediocre work; unremarkable piece
A creative work (book, film, painting, etc.) that is ordinary and lacks distinction. Not necessarily bad, but not noteworthy either.
あの映画(えいが)は凡作(ぼんさく)だった。
That movie was mediocre.
期待(きたい)していたが、凡作(ぼんさく)に終(お)わった。
I had high hopes, but it turned out to be an unremarkable work.
天才(てんさい)と呼(よ)ばれた画家(がか)にも凡作(ぼんさく)はある。
Even a painter called a genius has some mediocre works.

USAGE:
凡作(ぼんさく) is used primarily in criticism and reviews of creative works. It implies something merely average — not terrible, but lacking in originality or impact. The 凡(ぼん) character means "ordinary" or "mediocre."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 凡作(ぼんさく)に終(お)わる (to end up as a mediocre work)
  • 凡作(ぼんさく)と言(い)わざるを得(え)ない (one must call it mediocre)

SIMILAR WORDS:
傑作(けっさく) (masterpiece) and 駄作(ださく) (terrible work) are at opposite ends of the quality spectrum. 凡作(ぼんさく) sits in the middle — better than 駄作(ださく) but far from 傑作(けっさく). 佳作(かさく) (commendable work) is slightly above 凡作(ぼんさく), indicating decent quality.
