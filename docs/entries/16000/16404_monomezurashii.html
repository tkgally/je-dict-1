物珍(ものめずら)しい

ものめずらしい
adjective
unusual; novel; curious
1. unusual; novel; curious; unfamiliar
Describes something that catches one's interest because it is rare or unfamiliar. Often implies looking at something with wide-eyed curiosity, as if encountering it for the first time.
子供(こども)たちは物珍(ものめずら)しそうに見(み)ていた。
The children were watching with curiosity.
外国(がいこく)の食(た)べ物(もの)が物珍(ものめずら)しくて色々(いろいろ)試(ため)した。
I was curious about foreign foods and tried all sorts of things.
最初(さいしょ)は物珍(ものめずら)しかった田舎(いなか)の生活(せいかつ)にもすっかり慣(な)れた。
I've gotten completely used to rural life, which at first seemed so novel.

USAGE:
物珍(ものめずら)しい often appears in the adverbial form 物珍(ものめずら)しそうに ("as if finding something novel") to describe the way someone looks at or reacts to something unfamiliar. The nuance is lighter than 珍(めずら)しい alone, emphasizing the curiosity of the observer.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物珍(ものめずら)しそうに見(み)る (to look at with curiosity)
  • 物珍(ものめずら)しさ (novelty, unfamiliarity)
  • 物珍(ものめずら)しい目(め)で見(み)る (to look at with curious eyes)

SIMILAR WORDS:
珍(めずら)しい is the more common and general word for "rare" or "unusual." 物珍(ものめずら)しい specifically emphasizes the viewer's sense of novelty and curiosity.
