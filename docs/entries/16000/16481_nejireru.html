1. to be twisted; to be warped; to be contorted
For something to become twisted, bent, or spiraled out of its original shape.
ロープが捻(ね)じれている。
The rope is twisted.
強風(きょうふう)で木(き)の枝(えだ)が捻(ね)じれた。
The tree branches got twisted by the strong wind.
古(ふる)い柱(はしら)が年月(ねんげつ)とともに捻(ね)じれて、独特(どくとく)な形(かたち)になっていた。
The old pillar had twisted over the years into a unique shape.
2. to be complicated; to be strained; to become dysfunctional
Used figuratively for relationships, personalities, or political situations that have become complicated or dysfunctional.
国会(こっかい)が捻(ね)じれている。
The parliament is in a gridlock.
彼(かれ)の性格(せいかく)は少(すこ)し捻(ね)じれている。
His personality is a bit warped.
与党(よとう)と野党(やとう)の関係(かんけい)が捻(ね)じれて、法案(ほうあん)がなかなか通(とお)らない。
The relationship between the ruling and opposition parties is strained, so bills have trouble passing.

USAGE:
The intransitive form of 捻(ね)じる (to twist). In Japanese politics, 捻(ね)じれ国会(こっかい) (twisted Diet) is a well-known expression for a divided parliament where different parties control each chamber.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 捻(ね)じれた関係(かんけい) (strained relationship)
  • 捻(ね)じれ国会(こっかい) (divided parliament)
  • 捻(ね)じれた性格(せいかく) (warped personality)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 歪(ゆが)む: to be distorted, to warp — emphasizes deformation from the original shape
  • 曲(ま)がる: to bend, to curve — a simpler bending without spiral twisting
