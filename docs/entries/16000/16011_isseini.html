一斉(いっせい)に

いっせいに
adverb
all at once; simultaneously; in unison
1. all at once; simultaneously; in unison
Describes multiple people or things acting at the same time in a coordinated or synchronized manner.
生徒(せいと)たちが一斉(いっせい)に立(た)ち上(あ)がった。
The students all stood up at once.
合図(あいず)とともに一斉(いっせい)にスタートした。
They all started simultaneously at the signal.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)が一斉(いっせい)に咲(さ)き始(はじ)めると、公園(こうえん)は大勢(おおぜい)の花見客(はなみきゃく)でにぎわう。
When the cherry blossoms all start blooming at once, the park bustles with crowds of flower-viewing visitors.

USAGE:
一斉(いっせい)に emphasizes that an action happens simultaneously across a group. The noun form 一斉(いっせい) can also be used as a prefix: 一斉(いっせい)検査 (simultaneous inspection), 一斉(いっせい)送信 (mass sending/broadcast).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一斉(いっせい)に立(た)ち上(あ)がる (to stand up all at once)
  • 一斉(いっせい)に始(はじ)まる (to begin simultaneously)
  • 一斉(いっせい)に咲(さ)く (to bloom all at once)
  • 一斉(いっせい)検挙 (mass arrest)

NUANCE:
Compared to 同時(どうじ)に, which simply means "at the same time," 一斉(いっせい)に implies a larger group acting together and often carries a more dramatic or striking impression.
