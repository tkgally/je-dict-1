給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)

きゅうとうしつ
noun
office kitchenette; hot-water room
1. office kitchenette; hot-water room; pantry
A small room in an office or building equipped with a sink, a kettle or hot water supply, and sometimes a microwave. Used by staff to make tea, wash cups, and take short breaks.
給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)でお茶(ちゃ)を入(い)れた。
I made tea in the kitchenette.
給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)は各階(かくかい)に一(ひと)つずつある。
There is one kitchenette on each floor.
昼休(ひるやす)みに給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)で同僚(どうりょう)と雑談(ざつだん)するのが日課(にっか)になっている。
Chatting with colleagues in the kitchenette during lunch break has become part of my daily routine.

USAGE:
給湯室(きゅうとうしつ) literally means "hot water supply room." It is the standard Japanese term for an office kitchenette or break area with a sink and hot water. In many Japanese offices, it functions as a communal space for making tea and coffee.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)でお茶(ちゃ)を入(い)れる (to make tea in the kitchenette)
  • 給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)を掃除(そうじ)する (to clean the kitchenette)
  • 給湯室(きゅうとうしつ)の利用(りよう) (use of the kitchenette)
