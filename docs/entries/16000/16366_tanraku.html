短絡(たんらく)

たんらく
noun
short circuit; jumping to conclusions
1. short circuit
An electrical fault where current flows along an unintended path, bypassing the normal circuit.
配線(はいせん)の短絡(たんらく)で火災(かさい)が発生(はっせい)した。
A fire broke out due to a short circuit in the wiring.
電気(でんき)系統(けいとう)の短絡(たんらく)を防(ふせ)ぐためにヒューズが使(つか)われている。
Fuses are used to prevent short circuits in electrical systems.
回路(かいろ)が短絡(たんらく)して機械(きかい)が動(うご)かなくなった。
The circuit shorted out and the machine stopped working.
2. jumping to conclusions; hasty reasoning; oversimplification
Thinking that skips logical steps and reaches a conclusion too quickly, often resulting in an incorrect or shallow judgment.
そんな短絡的(たんらくてき)な考(かんが)えはよくない。
Such simplistic thinking is no good.
一(ひと)つの失敗(しっぱい)で全(すべ)てを否定(ひてい)するのは短絡的(たんらくてき)だ。
It's simplistic to dismiss everything because of one failure.
問題(もんだい)の原因(げんいん)を短絡的(たんらくてき)に決(き)めつけず、もっと深(ふか)く調(しら)べるべきだ。
Instead of jumping to conclusions about the cause of the problem, we should investigate more deeply.

短絡(たんらく) literally means "short connection" — 短(たん) (short) + 絡(らく) (connection, entanglement).

The figurative sense (sense 2) is more commonly encountered in everyday use, particularly through the na-adjective form 短絡的(たんらくてき) (simplistic, hasty in reasoning). The metaphor mirrors the English concept of a "short circuit" in thinking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 短絡的(たんらくてき)な考(かんが)え (simplistic thinking)
  • 短絡的(たんらくてき)に判断(はんだん)する (to judge hastily)
  • 短絡(たんらく)する (to short-circuit; to jump to conclusions)
