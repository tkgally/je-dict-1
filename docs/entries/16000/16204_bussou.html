物騒(ぶっそう)

ぶっそう
na-adjective
dangerous; unsafe; disturbing
1. dangerous; unsafe; insecure
Describes a situation, area, or atmosphere that feels unsafe or prone to crime or violence.
この辺(あた)りは物騒(ぶっそう)だ。
This area is unsafe.
最近(さいきん)、物騒(ぶっそう)な事件(じけん)が多(おお)い。
There have been a lot of disturbing incidents recently.
夜(よる)の一人歩(ひとりある)きは物騒(ぶっそう)だから気(き)をつけて。
Walking alone at night is dangerous, so be careful.
2. alarming; disturbing; unsettling
Describes talk or events that are unsettling or ominous.
物騒(ぶっそう)な話(はなし)だね。
That's a disturbing story.
物騒(ぶっそう)なことを言(い)うなよ。
Don't say such alarming things.
包丁(ほうちょう)を持(も)ち歩(ある)くなんて物騒(ぶっそう)な人(ひと)だ。
Carrying a kitchen knife around — what a dangerous person.

FORMS:

  • 物騒(ぶっそう)な + noun (attributive)
  • 物騒(ぶっそう)だ (predicative)

USAGE:
物騒(ぶっそう) describes an atmosphere of danger or unease rather than specific, immediate physical danger. It is commonly used in everyday conversation about crime, safety, and unsettling events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物騒(ぶっそう)な世(よ)の中(なか): a dangerous world
  • 物騒(ぶっそう)な事件(じけん): a disturbing incident
  • 物騒(ぶっそう)な話(はなし): a disturbing story

SIMILAR WORDS:
危険(きけん) describes concrete, specific danger. 物騒(ぶっそう) describes a general sense of unease about safety or public order.
