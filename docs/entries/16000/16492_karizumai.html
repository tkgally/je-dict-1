仮住(かりず)まい

かりずまい
noun
temporary residence; makeshift home
1. temporary residence; makeshift home; temporary lodging
A place where one lives temporarily, such as during home renovation, relocation between houses, or after a disaster. Implies the situation is not permanent.
リフォーム中(ちゅう)は仮住(かりず)まいをしている。
I'm staying in a temporary place during renovations.
家(いえ)が見(み)つかるまで実家(じっか)で仮住(かりず)まいしている。
I'm temporarily living at my parents' house until I find a place.
災害(さいがい)で自宅(じたく)が被害(ひがい)を受(う)け、半年(はんとし)ほどアパートで仮住(かりず)まいをしていた。
Our home was damaged in the disaster, so we lived temporarily in an apartment for about half a year.

USAGE:
Can be used as a noun or with する as a suru verb (仮住(かりず)まいする = to live temporarily). Often comes up in contexts of home renovation, natural disasters, or the transitional period between moving out of one home and into another.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮住(かりず)まいをする (to live temporarily)
  • 仮住(かりず)まい先(さき) (temporary residence location)
  • 仮住(かりず)まい用(よう)のアパート (apartment for temporary residence)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仮設住宅(かせつじゅうたく): temporary housing — prefabricated housing provided after disasters
  • 間借(まが)り: renting a room — living in someone else's house by renting a room
