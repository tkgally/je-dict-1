積(つ)み木(き)

つみき
noun
building blocks; toy blocks
1. building blocks; toy blocks
Small wooden or plastic blocks of various shapes that children stack and arrange to build structures. A classic children's toy.
子(こ)どもが積(つ)み木(き)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The child is playing with building blocks.
積(つ)み木(き)を高(たか)く積(つ)み上(あ)げて塔(とう)を作(つく)った。
I stacked the blocks high and built a tower.
カラフルな積(つ)み木(き)は、子(こ)どもの想像力(そうぞうりょく)や手先(てさき)の器用(きよう)さを育(そだ)てるのに役立(やくだ)つ。
Colorful building blocks are useful for developing children's imagination and manual dexterity.

USAGE:
積(つ)み木(き) typically refers to traditional wooden blocks, though modern versions come in various materials. It is one of the most common and recognizable children's toys in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 積(つ)み木(き)で遊(あそ)ぶ (to play with blocks)
  • 積(つ)み木(き)を積(つ)む (to stack blocks)
  • 積(つ)み木(き)を崩(くず)す (to knock down blocks)
  • 木製(もくせい)の積(つ)み木(き) (wooden blocks)

NUANCE:
Sometimes used figuratively to describe something built up carefully that can be easily toppled, similar to "house of cards" in English.
