一般道(いっぱんどう)

いっぱんどう
noun
public road; local road; ordinary road
1. public road; local road; ordinary road
A regular road as opposed to an expressway or highway. In Japan, this refers to toll-free roads with intersections and traffic lights, as distinct from toll expressways.
一般道(いっぱんどう)を走(はし)る。
To drive on a local road.
高速(こうそく)を使(つか)わず一般道(いっぱんどう)で行(い)った。
I went by local roads without using the expressway.
渋滞(じゅうたい)を避(さ)けるため、高速道路(こうそくどうろ)を降(お)りて一般道(いっぱんどう)に切(き)り替(か)えた。
To avoid the traffic jam, I exited the expressway and switched to local roads.

Short form of 一般道路(いっぱんどうろ): 一般(いっぱん) (general, ordinary) + 道(どう) (road). Contrasts with 高速道路(こうそくどうろ) (expressway). Navigation systems and traffic reports use this term frequently.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一般道(いっぱんどう)を走(はし)る (to drive on local roads)
  • 一般道(いっぱんどう)に降(お)りる (to get off onto local roads)
  • 一般道(いっぱんどう)の制限速度(せいげんそくど) (speed limit on local roads)
