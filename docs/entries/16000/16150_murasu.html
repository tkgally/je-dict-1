蒸(む)らす

むらす
verb (godan)
to steam; to let sit and steam
1. to steam; to let sit and steam; to let rest (in its own heat)
To cook or finish cooking by allowing food to sit in steam or its own retained heat. An essential step in Japanese rice cooking — after the rice cooker finishes, the rice is left to rest and absorb remaining moisture. Also used for steaming bread, tea, and other foods.
ご飯(はん)を十分(じゅっぷん)蒸(む)らす。
Let the rice steam for ten minutes.
炊(た)き上(あ)がったら蓋(ふた)を開(あ)けずに蒸(む)らしてください。
Once the rice is done cooking, let it steam without opening the lid.
パンを焼(や)いた後(あと)、少(すこ)し蒸(む)らすとふっくら仕上(しあ)がる。
If you let the bread rest after baking, it turns out fluffy.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (す ending, conjugates as s-row: 蒸(む)らさない, 蒸(む)らします). Transitive — takes a direct object marked with を.

USAGE:
蒸(む)らす is a key cooking term in Japanese cuisine. The 蒸(む)らし (steaming/resting) step after cooking rice is considered essential for achieving the ideal texture. Most Japanese rice cooker instructions include this step.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご飯(はん)を蒸(む)らす (to let rice rest/steam)
  • 蓋(ふた)をして蒸(む)らす (to cover and let steam)
  • 蒸(む)らし時間(じかん) (steaming/resting time)
  • 五分(ごふん)蒸(む)らす (let rest for five minutes)
