さながら

さながら
adverb
just like; exactly as if; as though
1. just like; exactly as if; as though
Used to draw a vivid comparison, indicating that something closely resembles something else. More literary and expressive than simply using ように.
さながら夢(ゆめ)のようだった。
It was just like a dream.
会場(かいじょう)はさながらお祭(まつ)りのような雰囲気(ふんいき)だった。
The venue had an atmosphere just like a festival.
雪(ゆき)に覆(おお)われた街(まち)はさながら別世界(べっせかい)のようで、思(おも)わず息(いき)を飲(の)んだ。
The snow-covered town looked exactly like another world, and I caught my breath in wonder.

USAGE:
さながら is a somewhat literary adverb that adds vividness to a comparison. Common patterns include さながら〜のようだ and さながら〜だ. It elevates the tone of a sentence compared to using まるで or ように alone.

GRAMMAR:

Typically placed before the thing being compared to:

  • さながら + noun + のようだ
  • さながら + noun + だ

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • さながら夢(ゆめ)のよう (just like a dream)
  • さながら戦場(せんじょう)のよう (like a battlefield)
  • さながら別世界(べっせかい) (like another world)

SIMILAR WORDS:
まるで is more conversational and common. あたかも is more formal and literary. さながら falls between the two, adding an evocative, descriptive quality.
