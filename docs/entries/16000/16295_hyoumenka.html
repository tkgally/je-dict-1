表面化(ひょうめんか)

ひょうめんか
noun, verb-suru
coming to the surface; becoming apparent
1. coming to the surface; becoming apparent
A hidden problem, conflict, or issue becoming visible or publicly known. Combines 表面 (surface) with the suffix 化 (becoming), expressing the process of something emerging into the open.
問題(もんだい)が表面化(ひょうめんか)した。
The problem came to the surface.
社内(しゃない)の対立(たいりつ)がついに表面化(ひょうめんか)した。
The internal conflicts within the company finally came out into the open.
長年(ながねん)隠(かく)されてきた不正(ふせい)が内部告発(ないぶこくはつ)によって表面化(ひょうめんか)した。
Fraud that had been concealed for years was brought to light by a whistleblower.

USAGE:
表面化(ひょうめんか) is used when something previously hidden or latent becomes visible or publicly known. It nearly always refers to negative things — problems, conflicts, scandals, or deficiencies. Rarely used for positive developments.

PATTERNS:

  • 問題(もんだい)が表面化(ひょうめんか)する (a problem comes to the surface)
  • 対立(たいりつ)が表面化(ひょうめんか)する (a conflict becomes apparent)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問題(もんだい)の表面化(ひょうめんか) (emergence of a problem)
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)が表面化(ひょうめんか)する (contradictions come to light)
  • 表面化(ひょうめんか)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent something from coming to light)

SIMILAR WORDS:
露呈(ろてい)する means "to be exposed" with a stronger nuance of revealing something embarrassing. 発覚(はっかく)する means "to be discovered" and is used for wrongdoing or secrets. 表面化(ひょうめんか) focuses on the gradual process of becoming apparent.
