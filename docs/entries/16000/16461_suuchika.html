数値化(すうちか)

すうちか
noun
quantification; converting to numerical values
1. quantification; converting to numerical values; digitization
The process of expressing something in numerical form, making abstract or qualitative concepts measurable through numbers and data.
成果(せいか)を数値化(すうちか)する。
To quantify results.
顧客(こきゃく)満足度(まんぞくど)を数値化(すうちか)して比較(ひかく)する。
To quantify and compare customer satisfaction.
感覚的(かんかくてき)な判断(はんだん)ではなく、データを数値化(すうちか)して客観的(きゃっかんてき)に評価(ひょうか)すべきだ。
Rather than relying on intuitive judgment, we should quantify the data and evaluate objectively.

Compound noun: 数値(すうち) (numerical value) + 化(か) (transformation, -ification). Commonly used with する as a suru-verb. Frequently appears in business and academic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 数値化(すうちか)する (to quantify)
  • 数値化(すうちか)できる (can be quantified)
  • 数値化(すうちか)しにくい (difficult to quantify)
