道(みち)なり

みちなり
noun
following the road; along the road
1. following the road; along the road; as the road goes
Following the natural course of a road without turning off. Very common in giving and receiving directions.
道(みち)なりに進(すす)んでください。
Please go straight along the road.
この道(みち)を道(みち)なりに行(い)くと駅(えき)に着(つ)きます。
If you follow this road, you'll reach the station.
信号(しんごう)を過(す)ぎたら道(みち)なりに右(みぎ)にカーブして、二(ふた)つ目(め)の角(かど)を左(ひだり)に曲(ま)がってください。
After passing the traffic light, follow the road as it curves right, then turn left at the second corner.

USAGE:
道(みち)なり combines 道(みち) (road) and なり (as it is, following). It is almost always used with the particle に (道(みち)なりに) to mean "following the road." This is an essential expression for giving and understanding directions in Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)なりに進(すす)む (to proceed along the road)
  • 道(みち)なりに行(い)く (to go along the road)
  • 道(みち)なりに歩(ある)く (to walk along the road)

SIMILAR WORDS:
まっすぐ means straight ahead, while 道(みち)なり means following the road even if it curves. When a road curves, 道(みち)なりに means to follow the curve rather than going straight off the road.
