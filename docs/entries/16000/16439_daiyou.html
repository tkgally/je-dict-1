1. substitution; using something in place of another
The act of using one thing as a replacement or stand-in for another. Often used with する to form a suru-verb.
バターの代用(だいよう)にする。
To use as a substitute for butter.
砂糖(さとう)の代用(だいよう)として蜂蜜(はちみつ)を使(つか)った。
I used honey as a substitute for sugar.
専用(せんよう)の道具(どうぐ)がなくても、身近(みぢか)なもので代用(だいよう)できることが多(おお)い。
Even without specialized tools, you can often substitute with things you have at hand.

代用(だいよう) means using one thing in place of another. Compound of 代(だい) (substitute) + 用(よう) (use). Commonly used as a する verb.

KEY PATTERNS:

  • AをBで代用(だいよう)する: to substitute B for A
  • Aの代用(だいよう)としてBを使(つか)う: to use B as a substitute for A
  • ～で代用(だいよう)できる: can be substituted with ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代用(だいよう)品(ひん): substitute product/item
  • 代用(だいよう)食(しょく): substitute food
  • 代用(だいよう)教員(きょういん): substitute teacher

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 代替(だいたい): replacement, alternative (broader scope; often used for systems or policies)
  • 代(か)わり: replacement, substitute (more casual and everyday)
