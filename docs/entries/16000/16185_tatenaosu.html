建(た)て直(なお)す

たてなおす
verb (godan)
to rebuild; to reconstruct
1. to rebuild; to reconstruct (a building)
To demolish an old or damaged building and construct a new one in its place.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)を建(た)て直(なお)す。
To rebuild an old house.
地震(じしん)で壊(こわ)れた学校(がっこう)を建(た)て直(なお)す計画(けいかく)がある。
There are plans to rebuild the school that was destroyed in the earthquake.
祖父(そふ)の代(だい)に建(た)て直(なお)したこの家(いえ)は、もう築(ちく)五十(ごじゅう)年(ねん)になる。
This house, rebuilt in my grandfather's generation, is now fifty years old.
2. to reorganize; to restore; to get back on track
To rebuild something abstract such as a business, relationship, health, or life after a setback. Often written as 立て直す for this figurative sense.
経営(けいえい)を建(た)て直(なお)す。
To restructure the business.
体調(たいちょう)を建(た)て直(なお)してから復帰(ふっき)する。
I'll return after getting my health back on track.
赤字(あかじ)が続(つづ)いていたが、新(あたら)しい社長(しゃちょう)のもとで会社(かいしゃ)を建(た)て直(なお)すことができた。
The company had been running deficits, but under the new president they managed to turn it around.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (す ending, conjugates as s-row: 建(た)て直(なお)さない, 建(た)て直(なお)します). Transitive — takes a direct object marked with を.

KANJI NOTE:
Sense 1 (physical rebuilding) uses 建(た)て直(なお)す. Sense 2 (figurative rebuilding) is often written 立(た)て直(なお)す, though the two are sometimes used interchangeably.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家(いえ)を建(た)て直(なお)す (to rebuild a house)
  • 経営(けいえい)を建(た)て直(なお)す (to restructure a business)
  • 体調(たいちょう)を建(た)て直(なお)す (to restore one's health)
  • 生活(せいかつ)を建(た)て直(なお)す (to get one's life back together)
