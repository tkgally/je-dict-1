言(い)い返(かえ)す

いいかえす
verb
to talk back; to retort; to answer back
1. to talk back; to retort; to answer back
To respond defiantly or argumentatively to something someone has said. Implies pushing back against criticism, a scolding, or an accusation.
親(おや)に言(い)い返(かえ)してはいけない。
You must not talk back to your parents.
悔(くや)しかったが、何(なに)も言(い)い返(かえ)せなかった。
I was frustrated, but I couldn't say anything back.
上司(じょうし)に理不尽(りふじん)なことを言(い)われたので、つい言(い)い返(かえ)してしまった。
My boss said something unreasonable, so I couldn't help talking back.

USAGE:
言(い)い返(かえ)す implies defiance or resistance. It is different from simply replying (返事(へんじ)する). Often appears in negative contexts: either someone is being told not to talk back, or they wish they could have but didn't.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (consonant stem: 返(かえ)す). Transitive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言(い)い返(かえ)せない (unable to retort)
  • 言(い)い返(かえ)す言葉(ことば)もない (to be speechless / have no comeback)
  • つい言(い)い返(かえ)してしまう (to end up talking back despite oneself)

WORD FORMATION:
言(い)う (to say) + 返(かえ)す (to return). The compound means "to say something back."
