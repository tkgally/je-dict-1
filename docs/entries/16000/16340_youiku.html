養育(よういく)

よういく
noun, verb (suru)
upbringing, rearing, raising
1. upbringing, rearing, raising a child
The act of nurturing and raising a child, providing food, shelter, education, and care until they can be independent.
子(こ)どもを養育(よういく)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
There is an obligation to raise the child.
養育(よういく)費(ひ)を毎月(まいつき)払(はら)っている。
I pay child support every month.
離婚後(りこんご)の養育(よういく)権(けん)について話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed custody rights after the divorce.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 養育(よういく)費(ひ): child support payments
  • 養育(よういく)権(けん): custody (rights to raise a child)
  • 養育(よういく)環境(かんきょう): child-rearing environment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 育児(いくじ): childcare (day-to-day care, especially of young children)
  • 子育(こそだ)て: raising children (everyday, colloquial)
  • 養護(ようご): protection and care (used in institutional contexts)
