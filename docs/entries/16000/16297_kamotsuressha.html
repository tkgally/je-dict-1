貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)

かもつれっしゃ
noun
freight train; goods train
1. freight train; goods train
A train designed for transporting cargo rather than passengers. Composed of 貨物 (freight, cargo) and 列車 (train).
貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)が通過(つうか)した。
A freight train passed through.
長(なが)い貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)が踏切(ふみきり)を塞(ふさ)いでいた。
A long freight train was blocking the railroad crossing.
深夜(しんや)になると貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)が工場(こうじょう)から港(みなと)へ荷物(にもつ)を運(はこ)ぶ。
Late at night, freight trains carry goods from factories to the port.

USAGE:
貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ) is a straightforward compound noun. In everyday speech, it is sometimes shortened to 貨物(かもつ) when the context makes it clear that a train is being discussed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)が走(はし)る (a freight train runs)
  • 貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)の通過(つうか) (passage of a freight train)
  • 貨物列車(かもつれっしゃ)のダイヤ (freight train schedule)

SIMILAR WORDS:
旅客列車(りょかくれっしゃ) is the counterpart, meaning passenger train. 貨車(かしゃ) refers to an individual freight car/wagon rather than the whole train.
