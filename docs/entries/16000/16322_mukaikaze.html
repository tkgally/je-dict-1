向(む)かい風(かぜ)

むかいかぜ
noun
headwind
1. headwind, wind blowing against one
A wind blowing directly against one's direction of travel. Also used figuratively for adverse circumstances or opposition.
強(つよ)い向(む)かい風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
A strong headwind is blowing.
向(む)かい風(かぜ)で自転車(じてんしゃ)が進(すす)まない。
The bicycle won't move forward because of the headwind.
経済(けいざい)の向(む)かい風(かぜ)にもかかわらず、会社(かいしゃ)は成長(せいちょう)を続(つづ)けた。
Despite economic headwinds, the company continued to grow.
Figurative usage

USAGE NOTES:
向(む)かい風(かぜ) is used both literally (a wind blowing against you) and figuratively (adverse conditions, opposition). The figurative usage is common in news and business contexts.

OPPOSITE:

  • 追(お)い風(かぜ): tailwind; favorable circumstances

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆風(ぎゃくふう): adverse wind, headwind (more literary/formal; common in figurative usage)
