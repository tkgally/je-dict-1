張(は)り合(あ)う

はりあう
verb (godan)
to compete; to rival; to vie with
1. to compete; to rival; to vie with
To compete or contend with someone, often with a sense of stubbornness or determination not to lose.
彼(かれ)と張(は)り合(あ)っても無駄(むだ)だ。
It's pointless to compete with him.
大企業(だいきぎょう)に張(は)り合(あ)える技術力(ぎじゅつりょく)がある。
They have the technical capability to rival a large corporation.
あの二人(ふたり)はいつも張(は)り合(あ)っていて、どちらも負(ま)けたくないようだ。
Those two are always competing, and neither one wants to lose.

USAGE:
張(は)り合(あ)う implies a determined, sometimes stubborn competition. The competitor is marked with と or に. It often has a slightly negative nuance of pointless or stubborn rivalry.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 張(は)り合(あ)う: to compete with
  • 張(は)り合(あ)い: competition, rivalry (noun form)
  • 張(は)り合(あ)いがある: to feel a sense of worthwhile competition
  • 張(は)り合(あ)いがない: to feel there's no challenge

SIMILAR WORDS:
競(きそ)う focuses on straightforward competition. 張(は)り合(あ)う adds a nuance of stubbornness or determination not to be outdone.
