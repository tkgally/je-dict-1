大好物(だいこうぶつ)

だいこうぶつ
noun
favorite food; great favorite
1. favorite food; great favorite
Something one loves to eat or, by extension, something one is extremely fond of. Combines the intensifier 大 with 好物 (favorite food). Often used to talk about what someone loves most.
カレーは私(わたし)の大好物(だいこうぶつ)だ。
Curry is my absolute favorite food.
祖母(そぼ)の大好物(だいこうぶつ)はお寿司(すし)だった。
My grandmother's favorite food was sushi.
子供(こども)の大好物(だいこうぶつ)ばかり作(つく)ると、栄養(えいよう)が偏(かたよ)ってしまう。
If you only make the children's favorite foods, their nutrition will become unbalanced.

USAGE:
大好物(だいこうぶつ) is an emphatic form of 好物(こうぶつ) (favorite food). The 大(だい) prefix intensifies the meaning, expressing strong fondness. Primarily used for food, but occasionally extended to other strong preferences.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大好物(だいこうぶつ)の一(ひと)つ (one of one's favorites)
  • 大好物(だいこうぶつ)を食(た)べる (to eat one's favorite food)
  • 子供(こども)の大好物(だいこうぶつ) (children's favorite food)

SIMILAR WORDS:
好物(こうぶつ) is the base form meaning "favorite food" without the emphatic nuance. 大好き(だいすき) (love, really like) is a na-adjective that applies broadly to anything, while 大好物(だいこうぶつ) is a noun specifically centered on food.
