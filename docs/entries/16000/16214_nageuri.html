投(な)げ売(う)り

なげうり
noun
fire sale; dumping; selling at a loss
1. fire sale; dumping; selling at a loss
Selling goods at far below their normal price, usually to quickly dispose of inventory. Can also refer to selling assets at a steep discount out of desperation.
在庫(ざいこ)を投(な)げ売(う)りする。
To sell off inventory at fire-sale prices.
閉店(へいてん)セールで商品(しょうひん)が投(な)げ売(う)りされていた。
Products were being sold at rock-bottom prices during the closing sale.
株価(かぶか)が急落(きゅうらく)して、投資家(とうしか)たちが投(な)げ売(う)りに走(はし)った。
Stock prices plummeted, and investors rushed to dump their holdings.

USAGE:
投(な)げ売(う)り can describe both physical retail (selling goods cheaply to clear stock) and financial markets (panic selling of stocks or assets). It always implies selling at a significant loss.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投(な)げ売(う)りする: to sell at a steep discount
  • 投(な)げ売(う)り価格(かかく): fire-sale price
  • 投(な)げ売(う)りに走(はし)る: to rush to dump (assets)
  • 投(な)げ売(う)り状態(じょうたい): a state of dumping

WORD FORMATION:
投(な)げ (throw) + 売(う)り (selling). The image is of throwing goods away by selling them cheaply.
