必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)

ひつようふかけつ
na-adjective, noun
absolutely essential; indispensable
1. absolutely essential; indispensable
Expresses that something is so necessary it cannot be done without. A strong emphatic compound combining 必要 (necessary) and 不可欠 (indispensable).
水(みず)は生活(せいかつ)に必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)だ。
Water is absolutely essential for daily life.
チームワークは成功(せいこう)に必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)な要素(ようそ)だ。
Teamwork is an indispensable element for success.
この地域(ちいき)の経済(けいざい)発展(はってん)には道路(どうろ)の整備(せいび)が必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)である。
Road development is absolutely essential for the economic growth of this region.

USAGE:
必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ) is an emphatic expression formed by combining 必要(ひつよう) (necessary) with 不可欠(ふかけつ) (indispensable). It is somewhat redundant by design, used for strong emphasis. Common in formal writing, speeches, and business contexts.

PATTERNS:

  • Aに必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)だ (is absolutely essential for A)
  • 必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)な＋noun (an indispensable ...)
  • 必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ)である (formal copula form)

SIMILAR WORDS:
必要(ひつよう) means "necessary" and 不可欠(ふかけつ) means "indispensable" — either can be used alone with slightly less emphasis. 必要不可欠(ひつようふかけつ) is the strongest way to express absolute necessity.
