公益(こうえき)

こうえき
noun
public interest; public benefit; public welfare
1. public interest; public benefit; public welfare; common good
The general welfare or benefit of the public as a whole, as opposed to private or individual interests. Frequently used in legal, governmental, and organizational contexts.
公益(こうえき)のために活動(かつどう)する。
To work for the public interest.
この団体(だんたい)は公益(こうえき)法人(ほうじん)として認定(にんてい)されている。
This organization is certified as a public interest corporation.
私利私欲(しりしよく)ではなく公益(こうえき)を優先(ゆうせん)すべきだという議論(ぎろん)が高(たか)まっている。
There is growing debate that public interest should be prioritized over private gain.

USAGE:
公益(こうえき) combines 公(こう) (public) and 益(えき) (benefit, profit). It is a formal term used in legal and administrative language.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公益(こうえき)法人(ほうじん) (public interest corporation)
  • 公益(こうえき)事業(じぎょう) (public interest enterprise)
  • 公益(こうえき)に資(し)する (to contribute to the public good)
  • 公益(こうえき)を守(まも)る (to protect the public interest)

SIMILAR WORDS:
公共(こうきょう)の利益(りえき) means public benefit in a broader sense. 公益(こうえき) is a more concise, formal term often used as a modifier in compound nouns.
