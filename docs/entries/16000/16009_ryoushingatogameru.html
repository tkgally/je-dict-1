良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)める

りょうしんがとがめる
expression
to feel guilty; to have a guilty conscience
1. to feel guilty; to have a bad conscience; to be troubled by one's conscience
An expression describing the internal discomfort felt when one knows one has done or is doing something morally wrong. Literally, 'one's conscience reproaches.' The subject is 良心 (conscience), not the person.
嘘(うそ)をついて良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)めた。
I felt guilty about lying.
良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)めて、本当(ほんとう)のことを話(はな)すことにした。
My guilty conscience led me to decide to tell the truth.
友人(ゆうじん)を裏切(うらぎ)ったことが良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)めて、何年(なんねん)も苦(くる)しんだ。
I was tormented for years by guilt over having betrayed my friend.

USAGE:
In this expression, 良心(りょうしん) (conscience) is the grammatical subject that does the 咎(とが)める (reproaching). The person who feels guilty is not the subject but the one whose conscience is acting. The verb 咎(とが)める on its own means to blame or find fault with. This is a relatively literary expression.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)めて〜する (to do something because of a guilty conscience)
  • 良心(りょうしん)の呵責(かしゃく) (pangs of conscience — related noun phrase)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 後(うし)ろめたい: feeling guilty, uneasy — an adjective for the same emotion
  • 罪悪感(ざいあくかん): guilt, sense of sin — a more direct noun for the feeling of guilt
