一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く

いっこくもはやく
expression
as soon as possible; without a moment's delay
1. as soon as possible; without a moment's delay; at the earliest possible moment
An emphatic expression conveying extreme urgency, meaning not even a single moment should be wasted. Stronger and more literary than できるだけ早く.
一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く逃(に)げなさい。
Run away immediately!
一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)しなければならない。
We must solve this problem as soon as possible.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)を一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く救助(きゅうじょ)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
The disaster victims need to be rescued without a moment's delay.

USAGE:
一刻(いっこく) literally means "one moment" or "one instant." The expression 一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く conveys that even the smallest unit of time matters — there must be no delay. It is more emphatic and literary than できるだけ早(はや)く or なるべく早(はや)く. Often used in urgent situations involving safety, emergencies, or critical deadlines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く〜する必要(ひつよう)がある (need to do ~ immediately)
  • 一刻(いっこく)も早(はや)く〜してほしい (want ~ done ASAP)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • できるだけ早(はや)く: as soon as possible — less emphatic, everyday usage
  • なるべく早(はや)く: as soon as possible — polite, everyday usage
  • 至急(しきゅう): urgently — formal, often used in business
