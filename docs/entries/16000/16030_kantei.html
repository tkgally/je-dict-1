鑑定(かんてい)

かんてい
noun
appraisal; expert assessment; authentication
1. appraisal; expert assessment; authentication
The act of evaluating the authenticity, value, or nature of something using specialized knowledge. Used for art, antiques, gemstones, forensic evidence, and mental health assessments.
この絵(え)の鑑定(かんてい)を依頼(いらい)した。
I requested an appraisal of this painting.
DNA鑑定(かんてい)の結果(けっか)、犯人(はんにん)が特定(とくてい)された。
The culprit was identified through DNA analysis.
専門家(せんもんか)に鑑定(かんてい)してもらったところ、この壺(つぼ)は江戸(えど)時代(じだい)のものだと判明(はんめい)した。
When I had an expert appraise it, the vase turned out to be from the Edo period.

USAGE:
鑑定(かんてい) can be used as a noun or as a suru-verb (鑑定(かんてい)する). It implies expert-level knowledge and authority.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鑑定(かんてい)する (to appraise, to assess)
  • 鑑定(かんてい)結果(けっか) (appraisal results)
  • 鑑定(かんてい)書(しょ) (appraisal certificate)
  • DNA鑑定(かんてい) (DNA analysis)
  • 精神(せいしん)鑑定(かんてい) (psychiatric evaluation)
  • 鑑定(かんてい)士(し) / 鑑定(かんてい)人(にん) (appraiser, expert witness)

CAUTION:
Do not confuse with 官邸(かんてい) (official residence, e.g., the Prime Minister's residence), which is a homophone.
