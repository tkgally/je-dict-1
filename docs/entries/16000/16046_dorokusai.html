泥臭(どろくさ)い

どろくさい

1. smelling of mud; muddy-smelling
Literally having the smell of mud or earth, as with freshwater fish or unwashed vegetables.
この魚(さかな)は泥臭(どろくさ)い。
This fish smells muddy.
川(かわ)で釣(つ)った魚(さかな)は泥臭(どろくさ)いことがある。
Fish caught in rivers can sometimes have a muddy smell.
泥臭(どろくさ)さを取(と)るために牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)に漬(つ)けておく。
Soak it in milk to remove the muddy smell.
2. unrefined; rustic; unsophisticated; rough around the edges
Figuratively, lacking polish or elegance. Can be used critically to mean crude, or positively to mean earnest and down-to-earth.
泥臭(どろくさ)いやり方(かた)だ。
It's an unrefined way of doing things.
泥臭(どろくさ)い営業(えいぎょう)スタイルが彼(かれ)の持(も)ち味(あじ)だ。
His gritty sales style is his strong point.
スマートではないが、泥臭(どろくさ)い努力(どりょく)が最後(さいご)には実(みの)を結(むす)んだ。
It wasn't elegant, but the unglamorous hard work bore fruit in the end.

USAGE:
泥臭(どろくさ)い is an i-adjective combining 泥(どろ) (mud) and 臭(くさ)い (smelling of). The figurative sense (sense 2) can be either negative (crude, unsophisticated) or positive (earnest, gritty, down-to-earth), depending on context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 泥臭(どろくさ)い努力(どりょく) (unglamorous effort)
  • 泥臭(どろくさ)いやり方(かた) (rough-and-ready approach)
  • 泥臭(どろくさ)い営業(えいぎょう) (gritty salesmanship)

SIMILAR WORDS:
野暮(やぼ)ったい means unfashionable or unsophisticated in appearance or taste. 泥臭(どろくさ)い emphasizes a raw, unpolished quality that can imply hard work and persistence.
