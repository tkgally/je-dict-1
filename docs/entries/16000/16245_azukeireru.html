預(あず)け入(い)れる

あずけいれる
verb (ichidan)
to deposit (money); to check in (luggage)
1. to deposit money
To put money into a bank account or financial institution for safekeeping.
銀行(ぎんこう)にお金(かね)を預(あず)け入(い)れる。
To deposit money in a bank.
毎月(まいつき)定額(ていがく)を預(あず)け入(い)れている。
I deposit a fixed amount every month.
ATMで現金(げんきん)を預(あず)け入(い)れることができる。
You can deposit cash at the ATM.
2. to check in; to deposit (belongings)
To hand over luggage or belongings for safekeeping, such as checking luggage at an airport or leaving items at a storage facility.
荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)け入(い)れる。
To check in luggage.
空港(くうこう)で大(おお)きいスーツケースを預(あず)け入(い)れた。
I checked in a large suitcase at the airport.
コインロッカーに荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)け入(い)れてから観光(かんこう)に出(で)かけた。
I put my luggage in a coin locker and then went sightseeing.

USAGE:
預(あず)け入(い)れる is the compound verb form of 預(あず)ける + 入(い)れる. The noun form 預(あず)け入(い)れ is commonly seen on ATM screens and banking documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お金(かね)を預(あず)け入(い)れる: to deposit money
  • 荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)け入(い)れる: to check in luggage
  • 預(あず)け入(い)れ金額(きんがく): deposit amount
  • 預(あず)け入(い)れ限度額(げんどがく): deposit limit
