うろつく

うろつく
verb, godan
to loiter; to prowl; to wander around
1. to loiter; to prowl; to wander aimlessly
To walk around without a clear purpose or destination, often with a nuance of suspicion or restlessness.
怪(あや)しい人(ひと)がうろついている。
A suspicious person is prowling around.
夜中(よなか)に近所(きんじょ)をうろつくのはやめなさい。
Stop wandering around the neighborhood in the middle of the night.
道(みち)に迷(まよ)って、同(おな)じ場所(ばしょ)をうろつくことになってしまった。
I got lost and ended up wandering around the same area.

うろつく is an intransitive godan verb.

ASPECT (ている):
うろついている describes someone currently wandering or loitering.

USAGE:
うろつく often carries a negative nuance — suggesting suspicious behavior, restlessness, or aimlessness. It can describe both people and animals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 辺(あた)りをうろつく: to loiter around the area
  • 夜中(よなか)にうろつく: to prowl at night
  • うろうろする: a more colloquial synonym using the mimetic うろうろ

SIMILAR WORDS:
うろうろする is very close in meaning but slightly more colloquial. 徘徊(はいかい)する is more formal and often used for wandering associated with dementia.
