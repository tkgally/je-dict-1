下取(したど)り

したどり
noun / suru-verb
trade-in; part exchange
1. trade-in; part exchange
The practice of giving an old item (typically a car, appliance, or electronic device) to a seller to reduce the price of a new purchase.
古(ふる)い車(くるま)を下取(したど)りに出(だ)す。
To trade in an old car.
下取(したど)り価格(かかく)は思(おも)ったより高(たか)かった。
The trade-in price was higher than I expected.
スマホを買(か)い替(か)えるとき、今(いま)の機種(きしゅ)を下取(したど)りしてもらえるか聞(き)いてみた。
When replacing my smartphone, I asked if they could take my current model as a trade-in.

USAGE:
下取(したど)り is most commonly used when buying cars, electronics, and appliances. The seller evaluates the old item and deducts its value from the new purchase price.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下取(したど)りに出(だ)す (to trade in, to offer as a trade-in)
  • 下取(したど)り価格(かかく) (trade-in price/value)
  • 下取(したど)りしてもらう (to have something accepted as a trade-in)
  • 下取(したど)り査定(さてい) (trade-in appraisal)

SIMILAR WORDS:
買(か)い取(と)り means buyback or purchase, where the store buys your item outright. 下取(したど)り specifically implies exchanging an old item when purchasing a new one.
