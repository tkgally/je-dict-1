不平不満(ふへいふまん)

ふへいふまん
noun
complaints and grievances; discontent
1. complaints and grievances; discontent
A four-character compound combining 不平 (dissatisfaction) and 不満 (discontent) to express a general state of complaint or grumbling. Emphasizes persistent, accumulated dissatisfaction rather than a single complaint.
不平不満(ふへいふまん)を言(い)っても仕方(しかた)がない。
There's no point in complaining.
社員(しゃいん)の不平不満(ふへいふまん)が増(ふ)えている。
Complaints among employees are increasing.
不平不満(ふへいふまん)ばかり言(い)う人(ひと)とは一緒(いっしょ)にいたくない。
I don't want to be around people who do nothing but complain.

USAGE:
不平不満(ふへいふまん) is a yojijukugo (four-character compound) that pairs two near-synonyms for emphasis. It describes chronic or accumulated dissatisfaction rather than a single, specific complaint.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不平不満(ふへいふまん)を言(い)う (to voice complaints)
  • 不平不満(ふへいふまん)を漏(も)らす (to let slip one's grievances)
  • 不平不満(ふへいふまん)が溜(た)まる (for grievances to pile up)
  • 不平不満(ふへいふまん)だらけ (full of complaints)

SIMILAR WORDS:
文句(もんく) is a more casual, everyday word for a complaint or gripe. 不平不満(ふへいふまん) is more formal and suggests deeper or more widespread discontent.
