怒号(どごう)

どごう
noun
angry roar; furious shout
1. angry roar; furious shout; bellow of rage
A loud, angry shout or roar, often in a chaotic or confrontational situation.
怒号(どごう)が飛(と)び交(か)った。
Angry shouts flew back and forth.
観客(かんきゃく)から怒号(どごう)が上(あ)がった。
Angry roars erupted from the audience.
議会(ぎかい)は賛成派(さんせいは)と反対派(はんたいは)の怒号(どごう)で騒然(そうぜん)となった。
The parliament was thrown into uproar by angry shouts from both supporters and opponents.

USAGE:
怒号(どごう) is primarily a written or news-style word, often appearing in reports about protests, arguments, or parliamentary sessions. It is rarely used in casual conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 怒号(どごう)が飛(と)び交(か)う: angry shouts fly back and forth
  • 怒号(どごう)が上(あ)がる: angry roars erupt
  • 怒号(どごう)と罵声(ばせい): angry roars and jeers

WORD FORMATION:
怒(ど) (anger) + 号(ごう) (cry, call).
