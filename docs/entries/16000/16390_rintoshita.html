凛(りん)とした

りんとした
expression
dignified; poised; crisp
1. dignified; poised; stately
Having a composed, dignified bearing or appearance. Describes someone whose manner commands respect through quiet confidence and elegance.
凛(りん)とした態度(たいど)だ。
A dignified attitude.
あの女性(じょせい)は凛(りん)とした美(うつく)しさがある。
That woman has a dignified beauty about her.
彼女(かのじょ)は困難(こんなん)に直面(ちょくめん)しても凛(りん)とした姿勢(しせい)を崩(くず)さなかった。
Even when faced with difficulties, she never lost her dignified composure.
2. crisp; sharp; bracing
Describes air or atmosphere that is refreshingly cold and clear, conveying a sense of briskness.
凛(りん)とした冬(ふゆ)の空気(くうき)。
Crisp winter air.
朝(あさ)の凛(りん)とした空気(くうき)が気持(きも)ちいい。
The bracing morning air feels refreshing.
雪(ゆき)の降(ふ)る神社(じんじゃ)の境内(けいだい)は凛(りん)とした雰囲気(ふんいき)に包(つつ)まれていた。
The shrine grounds in the falling snow were enveloped in a crisp, solemn atmosphere.

USAGE:
凛(りん)とした is used as a pre-noun modifier. The base form 凛(りん)と can also function as an adverb meaning 'with dignity' or 'crisply.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 凛(りん)とした態度(たいど) (dignified attitude)
  • 凛(りん)とした表情(ひょうじょう) (dignified expression)
  • 凛(りん)とした美(うつく)しさ (dignified beauty)
  • 凛(りん)とした空気(くうき) (crisp air)
  • 凛(りん)と立(た)つ (to stand with dignity)

NOTE:
This word has very positive connotations and is often used to praise women's strength and composure, though it can apply to men and atmospheres as well.
