大歓迎(だいかんげい)

だいかんげい
noun
warm welcome; very welcome; big welcome
1. warm welcome; enthusiastic reception
A grand or enthusiastic welcome given to a person or group.
大歓迎(だいかんげい)を受(う)けた。
We received a warm welcome.
空港(くうこう)では大勢(おおぜい)のファンが大歓迎(だいかんげい)した。
A large crowd of fans gave an enthusiastic welcome at the airport.
選手団(せんしゅだん)は現地(げんち)の人々(ひとびと)から大歓迎(だいかんげい)を受(う)けて感動(かんどう)していた。
The athletes were moved by the warm welcome they received from the local people.
2. very welcome; more than welcome
Used to express that something or someone is eagerly accepted or strongly encouraged. Often seen in advertisements and invitations.
初心者(しょしんしゃ)大歓迎(だいかんげい)！
Beginners are very welcome!
差(さ)し入(い)れは大歓迎(だいかんげい)です。
Treats and snacks are more than welcome.
未経験者(みけいけんしゃ)大歓迎(だいかんげい)の求人(きゅうじん)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for job listings that welcome people with no experience.

USAGE:
Sense 1 describes an actual warm reception. Sense 2 is extremely common in advertisements, job postings, and invitations, typically in the pattern 〜大歓迎(だいかんげい) meaning "〜 very welcome." Functions as both a noun and a suru-verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大歓迎(だいかんげい)を受(う)ける (to receive a warm welcome)
  • 初心者(しょしんしゃ)大歓迎(だいかんげい) (beginners welcome)
  • 未経験者(みけいけんしゃ)大歓迎(だいかんげい) (no experience required / inexperienced welcome)
  • 大歓迎(だいかんげい)する (to give a big welcome)

WORD FORMATION:
大(だい) (big, great) + 歓迎(かんげい) (welcome). The prefix 大(だい) intensifies the meaning.
