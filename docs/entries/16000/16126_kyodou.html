挙動(きょどう)

きょどう
noun
behavior; movements; demeanor
1. behavior; movements; demeanor
Observable actions and mannerisms, especially when they seem suspicious or unusual.
挙動(きょどう)不審(ふしん)な人物(じんぶつ)を見(み)かけた。
I spotted a suspicious-looking person.
彼(かれ)の挙動(きょどう)がおかしかったので、声(こえ)をかけた。
His behavior seemed strange, so I called out to him.
警備員(けいびいん)は入場者(にゅうじょうしゃ)の挙動(きょどう)を注意深(ちゅういぶか)く観察(かんさつ)していた。
The security guard was carefully observing the behavior of those entering.

USAGE:
挙動(きょどう) is a relatively formal word referring to observable behavior and physical movements. It is most commonly used in the compound 挙動(きょどう)不審(ふしん) (suspicious behavior), which is standard police and security terminology.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 挙動(きょどう)不審(ふしん) (suspicious behavior)
  • 挙動(きょどう)がおかしい (to be behaving strangely)
  • 挙動(きょどう)を監視(かんし)する (to monitor someone's movements)
  • 挙動(きょどう)を注視(ちゅうし)する (to watch someone's behavior closely)
