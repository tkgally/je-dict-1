参観(さんかん)

さんかん
noun
observation visit; attending to observe
1. observation visit; attending to observe
Visiting a place to observe activities, most commonly used for parents visiting their child's school to observe classes. Also used for visiting factories, facilities, or events as an observer.
授業(じゅぎょう)参観(さんかん)に行(い)く。
I'm going to a class observation day.
来週(らいしゅう)の参観(さんかん)日(び)に母(はは)が来(く)る予定(よてい)だ。
My mother plans to come on next week's observation day.
工場(こうじょう)参観(さんかん)では、製品(せいひん)が作(つく)られる工程(こうてい)を見学(けんがく)できる。
During a factory observation visit, you can watch the production process.

USAGE:
The most common usage is 授業(じゅぎょう)参観(さんかん) (classroom observation), where parents visit school to watch their children's classes. This is a regular event at Japanese schools. 参観(さんかん)日(び) (observation day) is the specific day set aside for this.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 授業(じゅぎょう)参観(さんかん) (classroom observation day)
  • 参観(さんかん)日(び) (observation day)
  • 参観(さんかん)する (to visit and observe)
  • 工場(こうじょう)参観(さんかん) (factory observation visit)
