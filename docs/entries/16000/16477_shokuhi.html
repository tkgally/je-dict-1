食費(しょくひ)

しょくひ
noun
food expenses; food budget
1. food expenses; food costs; food budget
The amount of money spent on food, whether for a household, individual, or organization. A common category in personal budgeting and household economics.
食費(しょくひ)を節約(せつやく)する。
To cut food expenses.
今月(こんげつ)は食費(しょくひ)が高(たか)くなった。
Food expenses were higher this month.
一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしを始(はじ)めてから、食費(しょくひ)を月(つき)三万円(さんまんえん)に抑(おさ)えるようにしている。
Since I started living alone, I've been trying to keep my food budget under 30,000 yen a month.

USAGE:
One of the most common household expense categories. Often discussed alongside other expense types like 家賃(やちん) (rent), 光熱費(こうねつひ) (utility bills), and 交通費(こうつうひ) (transportation costs).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食費(しょくひ)を節約(せつやく)する (to save on food expenses)
  • 食費(しょくひ)を抑(おさ)える (to keep food costs down)
  • 月(つき)の食費(しょくひ) (monthly food expenses)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生活費(せいかつひ): living expenses — the broader category that includes food, housing, and other costs
  • 外食費(がいしょくひ): dining-out expenses — money spent specifically on eating out
