薄化粧(うすげしょう)

うすげしょう
noun
light makeup; subtle cosmetics
1. light makeup; subtle cosmetics
A minimal, natural-looking application of cosmetics, as opposed to heavy or dramatic makeup.
薄化粧(うすげしょう)で出(で)かける。
To go out with light makeup.
彼女(かのじょ)は薄化粧(うすげしょう)のほうが似合(にあ)う。
Light makeup suits her better.
面接(めんせつ)のときは薄化粧(うすげしょう)で清潔感(せいけつかん)のある印象(いんしょう)を心(こころ)がけた。
For the interview, I aimed for a clean impression with light makeup.

Compound noun: 薄(うす) (thin, light) + 化粧(けしょう) (makeup, cosmetics). The opposite is 厚化粧(あつげしょう) (heavy makeup).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薄化粧(うすげしょう)で出(で)かける (to go out with light makeup)
  • 薄化粧(うすげしょう)が似合(にあ)う (light makeup suits someone)

CULTURE:
In Japanese business and social settings, 薄化粧(うすげしょう) is generally considered appropriate and professional, while heavy makeup may be seen as unsuitable for formal contexts.

Related Words

Antonym: 厚化粧(あつげしょう) (heavy makeup)
