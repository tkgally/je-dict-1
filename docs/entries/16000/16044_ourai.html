往来(おうらい)

おうらい
noun
coming and going; traffic; street
1. coming and going; traffic; passage of people
The movement of people or vehicles back and forth along a route. Refers to the flow of traffic or pedestrians.
人(ひと)の往来(おうらい)が激(はげ)しい。
There is heavy foot traffic.
この通(とお)りは車(くるま)の往来(おうらい)が多(おお)い。
This street has a lot of vehicle traffic.
工事中(こうじちゅう)のため、歩行者(ほこうしゃ)の往来(おうらい)が制限(せいげん)されている。
Pedestrian traffic is restricted due to construction.
2. street; road; thoroughfare
A road or street where people pass through, especially a public road in a town. A somewhat traditional or literary word for street.
往来(おうらい)で遊(あそ)んではいけない。
You must not play in the street.
往来(おうらい)に面(めん)した店(みせ)は目立(めだ)つ。
A shop facing the street stands out.
昔(むかし)はこの往来(おうらい)に沿(そ)って多(おお)くの商家(しょうか)が並(なら)んでいた。
In the past, many merchant houses lined this street.

USAGE:
往来(おうらい) combines 往(おう) (going) and 来(らい) (coming). It can function as a noun or as a suru verb (往来(おうらい)する = to come and go). Sense 2 (street) has a somewhat old-fashioned feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)の往来(おうらい) (foot traffic)
  • 車(くるま)の往来(おうらい) (vehicle traffic)
  • 往来(おうらい)が激(はげ)しい (heavy traffic)
  • 往来(おうらい)に面(めん)する (to face the street)

SIMILAR WORDS:
交通(こうつう) is the standard modern word for traffic and transportation. 往来(おうらい) has a more traditional feel and specifically emphasizes the back-and-forth movement of people.
