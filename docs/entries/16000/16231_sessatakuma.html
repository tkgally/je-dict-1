切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)

せっさたくま
noun / verb (suru)
friendly rivalry; mutual improvement through competition
1. friendly rivalry; mutual improvement through competition
The process of improving oneself through healthy competition and mutual encouragement with others. Originally refers to the polishing of gemstones, used metaphorically for self-improvement.
チームで切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)する。
To improve through friendly rivalry as a team.
仲間(なかま)と切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)しながら成長(せいちょう)した。
I grew by pushing each other with my peers.
ライバルと切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)することで、お互(たが)いの技術(ぎじゅつ)が向上(こうじょう)した。
By competing with our rivals, both sides improved their skills.

USAGE:
切磋琢磨(せっさたくま) is a four-character compound (yojijukugo) used as a noun or with する. It always implies a positive, constructive kind of competition — not hostile rivalry.

ETYMOLOGY:
From a passage in the Chinese classic "Book of Songs" (詩経(しきょう)). 切(せつ) (to cut), 磋(さ) (to file), 琢(たく) (to chisel), 磨(ま) (to polish) — four stages of working raw materials into fine objects.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)する: to improve through friendly competition
  • 切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)の精神(せいしん): the spirit of mutual improvement
  • お互(たが)いに切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)する: to push each other to improve
