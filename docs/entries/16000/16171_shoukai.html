照会(しょうかい)

しょうかい
noun
inquiry; query; reference check
1. inquiry; query; reference check
A formal inquiry or request for information, often directed at an organization or institution. Commonly used in business and administrative contexts.
残高(ざんだか)照会(しょうかい)をした。
I checked my account balance.
詳(くわ)しくはお電話(でんわ)にてお照会(しょうかい)ください。
For details, please inquire by telephone.
信用(しんよう)照会(しょうかい)の結果(けっか)、ローンの審査(しんさ)に通(とお)った。
As a result of the credit check, I passed the loan screening.

GRAMMAR:
Used with する: 照会(しょうかい)する (to inquire, to look up). Takes を or に depending on context.

USAGE:
照会(しょうかい) is a formal word used in business and administrative contexts. It differs from 紹介(しょうかい) (introduction), which is a common homophone. In banking, 残高(ざんだか)照会(しょうかい) (balance inquiry) is a standard term seen on ATM screens.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 残高(ざんだか)照会(しょうかい) (balance inquiry)
  • 信用(しんよう)照会(しょうかい) (credit check)
  • 照会(しょうかい)番号(ばんごう) (reference number)
  • 照会(しょうかい)先(さき) (inquiry destination, contact)
