箸箱(はしばこ)

はしばこ
noun
chopstick case
1. chopstick case; chopstick box
A container used to store or carry chopsticks. Comes in various forms — from decorative wooden boxes for home use to portable plastic cases for packed lunches.
箸箱(はしばこ)から箸(はし)を出(だ)した。
I took the chopsticks out of the case.
お弁当用(べんとうよう)の箸箱(はしばこ)を買(か)った。
I bought a chopstick case for my lunch box.
子(こ)どもが箸箱(はしばこ)を学校(がっこう)に忘(わす)れてきたので、明日(あした)使(つか)う分(ぶん)がない。
My child left the chopstick case at school, so there isn't one to use tomorrow.

USAGE:
箸箱(はしばこ) is a compound of 箸(はし) (chopsticks) and 箱(はこ) (box). In Japan, many people carry reusable chopsticks in a case as part of their lunch set. Traditional wooden 箸箱(はしばこ) are also used as tableware at home.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 箸箱(はしばこ)セット (chopstick case set — often includes spoon/fork)
  • 弁当(べんとう)の箸箱(はしばこ) (lunch box chopstick case)
  • 木製(もくせい)の箸箱(はしばこ) (wooden chopstick case)
