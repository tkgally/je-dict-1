評論家(ひょうろんか)

ひょうろんか
noun
critic; commentator; reviewer
1. critic; commentator; reviewer
A person who professionally analyzes and critiques works of art, literature, politics, or other fields. Can also be used dismissively to describe someone who only criticizes without taking action themselves.
有名(ゆうめい)な映画(えいが)評論家(ひょうろんか)がこの作品(さくひん)を絶賛(ぜっさん)した。
A famous film critic praised this work highly.
彼(かれ)は口(くち)ばかりの評論家(ひょうろんか)だ。
He's just an armchair critic who only talks.
政治(せいじ)評論家(ひょうろんか)たちがテレビで選挙(せんきょ)の結果(けっか)について議論(ぎろん)していた。
Political commentators were discussing the election results on television.

USAGE:
When used in its professional sense, 評論家(ひょうろんか) is neutral. However, when applied to an ordinary person, it often carries a negative connotation suggesting they criticize without contributing or acting themselves.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 映画(えいが)評論家(ひょうろんか) (film critic)
  • 文芸(ぶんげい)評論家(ひょうろんか) (literary critic)
  • 政治(せいじ)評論家(ひょうろんか) (political commentator)
  • 音楽(おんがく)評論家(ひょうろんか) (music critic)

WORD FORMATION:
評論(ひょうろん) (criticism, critique) + 家(か) (professional suffix). The suffix 家(か) indicates a person who practices a skill or profession.
