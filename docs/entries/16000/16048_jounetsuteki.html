情熱的(じょうねつてき)

じょうねつてき
na-adjective
passionate; fervent; ardent
1. passionate; fervent; ardent; fiery
Describes a person, action, or expression full of intense emotion and enthusiasm. Used for romantic passion, artistic fervor, or dedication to a cause.
情熱的(じょうねつてき)な演奏(えんそう)だった。
It was a passionate performance.
彼(かれ)は情熱的(じょうねつてき)に自分(じぶん)の夢(ゆめ)を語(かた)った。
He spoke passionately about his dreams.
情熱的(じょうねつてき)な指導(しどう)で多(おお)くの学生(がくせい)の心(こころ)を動(うご)かした先生(せんせい)だった。
The teacher moved many students' hearts with passionate teaching.

USAGE:
情熱的(じょうねつてき) is formed from 情熱(じょうねつ) (passion) + 的(てき) (-like). It is used as a na-adjective modifier (情熱的(じょうねつてき)な) or adverbially (情熱的(じょうねつてき)に).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情熱的(じょうねつてき)な演奏(えんそう) (passionate performance)
  • 情熱的(じょうねつてき)な恋愛(れんあい) (passionate love affair)
  • 情熱的(じょうねつてき)に語(かた)る (to speak passionately)
  • 情熱的(じょうねつてき)な人(ひと) (a passionate person)

SIMILAR WORDS:
熱心(ねっしん) means enthusiastic or eager and focuses on diligence and dedication. 情熱的(じょうねつてき) emphasizes intense emotional fervor and is more dramatic in tone.
