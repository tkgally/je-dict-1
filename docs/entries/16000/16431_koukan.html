好感(こうかん)

こうかん
noun
good impression; favorable feeling; liking
1. good impression; favorable feeling; liking
A positive feeling or impression toward someone or something. Often used to describe the initial positive reaction people have toward a person, brand, or behavior.
彼(かれ)に好感(こうかん)を持(も)った。
I took a liking to him.
誠実(せいじつ)な態度(たいど)が好感(こうかん)を持(も)たれる。
A sincere attitude earns a favorable impression.
あの芸能人(げいのうじん)は好感度(こうかんど)ランキングで常(つね)に上位(じょうい)にいる。
That celebrity is always near the top of the likability rankings.

USAGE:
The most common patterns are 好感(こうかん)を持(も)つ (to have a favorable feeling toward) and 好感(こうかん)が持(も)てる (to be able to feel favorable toward). The compound 好感度(こうかんど) (likability rating) is widely used in media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 好感(こうかん)を持(も)つ (to feel favorably toward)
  • 好感(こうかん)が持(も)てる (to find likable)
  • 好感(こうかん)を抱(いだ)く (to harbor a favorable feeling — more formal)
  • 好感度(こうかんど) (likability; favorability rating)
  • 好感度(こうかんど)が高(たか)い (to have high likability)

NOTE:
Do not confuse with the homophone 交換(こうかん) (exchange).
