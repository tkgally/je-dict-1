手鏡(てかがみ)

てかがみ
noun
hand mirror
1. hand mirror
A small mirror with a handle, designed to be held in the hand. Used for checking one's appearance while on the go.
手鏡(てかがみ)で顔(かお)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked my face in a hand mirror.
バッグの中(なか)にいつも手鏡(てかがみ)を入(い)れている。
I always keep a hand mirror in my bag.
美容師(びようし)が手鏡(てかがみ)で後(うし)ろの仕上(しあ)がりを見(み)せてくれた。
The hairdresser showed me the finished back with a hand mirror.

USAGE:
手鏡(てかがみ) specifically refers to a mirror with a handle, as opposed to a wall mirror (壁掛(かべか)け鏡(かがみ)) or a compact mirror (コンパクトミラー). It is a common item in personal grooming.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手鏡(てかがみ)で見(み)る (to look in a hand mirror)
  • 手鏡(てかがみ)を持(も)つ (to hold a hand mirror)
  • 手鏡(てかがみ)を取(と)り出(だ)す (to take out a hand mirror)
