着(き)せ替(か)え

きせかえ
noun
dress-up, changing clothes (for dolls etc.)
1. dress-up, changing outfits
Changing the clothes on a doll or character. Also used for changing the visual theme or appearance of a device, app, or interface.
着(き)せ替(か)え人形(にんぎょう)で遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play with a dress-up doll.
スマホの壁紙(かべがみ)を着(き)せ替(か)えた。
I changed the wallpaper theme on my smartphone.
娘(むすめ)は着(き)せ替(か)えゲームに夢中(むちゅう)になっている。
My daughter is absorbed in a dress-up game.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 着(き)せ替(か)え人形(にんぎょう): dress-up doll
  • 着(き)せ替(か)えゲーム: dress-up game
  • 着(き)せ替(か)えカバー: changeable cover (for phones, etc.)

USAGE NOTES:
Originally used for doll play, 着(き)せ替(か)え has expanded to digital contexts — changing phone themes, app skins, and avatar outfits in games.
