憶測(おくそく)

おくそく
noun / suru-verb
speculation; conjecture; guess
1. speculation; conjecture; guess
Forming an opinion or conclusion based on incomplete information or personal assumptions rather than evidence. Often carries a negative nuance, implying that the conclusion is unreliable.
憶測(おくそく)で物(もの)を言(い)うな。
Don't say things based on speculation.
様々(さまざま)な憶測(おくそく)が飛(と)び交(か)っている。
Various speculations are flying around.
事実(じじつ)を確認(かくにん)せずに憶測(おくそく)だけで判断(はんだん)するのは危険(きけん)だ。
It's dangerous to make judgments based solely on speculation without confirming the facts.

USAGE:
憶測(おくそく) generally has a negative connotation, implying that the conclusion drawn is not well-founded. It is common in news commentary and formal discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 憶測(おくそく)が飛(と)び交(か)う (speculation flies around)
  • 憶測(おくそく)で判断(はんだん)する (to judge based on speculation)
  • 憶測(おくそく)に過(す)ぎない (it's nothing more than speculation)
  • 憶測(おくそく)を呼(よ)ぶ (to invite speculation)
  • 憶測(おくそく)記事(きじ) (speculative article)

KANJI NOTE:
Also written as 臆測(おくそく) with a different first character. Both writings are accepted and have the same meaning.
