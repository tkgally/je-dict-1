幸福感(こうふくかん)

こうふくかん
noun
sense of happiness, feeling of well-being
1. sense of happiness, feeling of well-being, sense of fulfillment
A subjective feeling of happiness or contentment. Often used in discussions about quality of life, psychology, and social surveys.
幸福感(こうふくかん)に満(み)たされている。
I am filled with a sense of happiness.
日常(にちじょう)の小(ちい)さなことに幸福感(こうふくかん)を感(かん)じる。
I feel a sense of happiness in the small things of daily life.
お金(かね)だけでは本当(ほんとう)の幸福感(こうふくかん)は得(え)られないと言(い)われている。
It is said that money alone cannot bring a true sense of happiness.

幸福感(こうふくかん) is composed of 幸福(こうふく) (happiness) + 感(かん) (feeling, sense).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幸福感(こうふくかん)を感(かん)じる: to feel a sense of happiness
  • 幸福感(こうふくかん)に満(み)たされる: to be filled with happiness
  • 幸福感(こうふくかん)を得(え)る: to gain a sense of happiness
  • 幸福感(こうふくかん)が高(たか)い: to have a high level of happiness

SIMILAR WORDS: 幸(しあわ)せ is the everyday word for happiness. 幸福感(こうふくかん) is more analytical and abstract, focusing on the subjective perception rather than the state itself. It is common in news articles, academic writing, and surveys on well-being.
