戦慄(せんりつ)

せんりつ
noun
shudder; shiver; horror; thrill
1. shudder; shiver; trembling with fear or horror
An involuntary physical trembling caused by intense fear, horror, or shock. Used for situations that inspire deep dread.
戦慄(せんりつ)が走(はし)った。
A shudder ran through me.
その光景(こうけい)に戦慄(せんりつ)を覚(おぼ)えた。
I felt a shudder at the sight.
事件(じけん)の真相(しんそう)を知(し)って、背筋(せすじ)に戦慄(せんりつ)が走(はし)った。
When I learned the truth of the incident, a shiver ran down my spine.

USAGE:
戦慄(せんりつ) is a literary and dramatic word. In everyday speech, people are more likely to say 身震(みぶる)いする (to shudder) or ぞっとする (to be horrified). 戦慄(せんりつ) appears frequently in news, literature, and dramatic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 戦慄(せんりつ)が走(はし)る (a shudder runs through one)
  • 戦慄(せんりつ)を覚(おぼ)える (to feel a shudder)
  • 戦慄(せんりつ)する (to shudder, to tremble)
  • 戦慄(せんりつ)すべき (horrifying, shudder-inducing)

NOTE:
Do not confuse with the homophone 旋律(せんりつ) (melody).
