天袋(てんぶくろ)

てんぶくろ
noun
small cupboard above a closet; ceiling-level storage
1. small cupboard near the ceiling; overhead storage above a closet
A small storage compartment built high up on the wall, typically above a Japanese-style closet (押し入れ). Found in traditional Japanese rooms, it has small sliding doors and is used for storing seldom-used items.
天袋(てんぶくろ)に季節(きせつ)外(はず)れの物(もの)をしまった。
I put out-of-season items in the overhead cupboard.
天袋(てんぶくろ)は高(たか)すぎて踏(ふ)み台(だい)がないと届(とど)かない。
The overhead cupboard is too high to reach without a step stool.
天袋(てんぶくろ)を開(あ)けたら、昔(むかし)のアルバムが出(で)てきた。
When I opened the overhead cupboard, I found old photo albums inside.

CULTURE:
The 天袋(てんぶくろ) is a feature of traditional Japanese rooms (和室(わしつ)), located above the 押(お)し入(い)れ (closet) near the ceiling. The opposite is 地袋(じぶくろ), a low cupboard built at floor level. Both are characteristic of Japanese domestic architecture.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天袋(てんぶくろ)にしまう (to put away in the overhead cupboard)
  • 天袋(てんぶくろ)から出(だ)す (to take out from the overhead cupboard)
  • 天袋(てんぶくろ)を開(あ)ける (to open the overhead cupboard)

WORD FORMATION:
天(てん) (sky, ceiling, top) + 袋(ぶくろ) (bag, pouch — here meaning enclosed storage).
