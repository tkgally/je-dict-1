1. fine-grained; smooth-textured
Describes a surface or texture that is very smooth and fine, with no visible roughness.
きめ細(こま)かい肌(はだ)をしている。
She has fine-grained skin.
きめ細(こま)かい泡(あわ)が立(た)つ石鹸(せっけん)だ。
It's a soap that produces fine lather.
この陶器(とうき)はきめ細(こま)かい仕上(しあ)がりが美(うつく)しい。
This pottery has a beautiful fine-grained finish.
2. detailed; thorough; attentive; meticulous
Describes service, care, or work that pays attention to every small detail. A highly positive description of someone's approach.
きめ細(こま)かいサービスが評判(ひょうばん)だ。
The attentive service has a good reputation.
きめ細(こま)かい対応(たいおう)に感謝(かんしゃ)します。
I appreciate the thorough response.
一人一人(ひとりひとり)にきめ細(こま)かい指導(しどう)ができるのは、少人数(しょうにんずう)クラスの利点(りてん)だ。
Being able to give detailed guidance to each individual is an advantage of small classes.

USAGE:
きめ細(こま)かい combines きめ (木目(きめ), grain or texture) with 細(こま)かい (fine, detailed). It is often written partly in hiragana. The word works both as a literal description of texture and as a figurative description of thoroughness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • きめ細(こま)かい肌(はだ) (fine-grained skin)
  • きめ細(こま)かいサービス (attentive service)
  • きめ細(こま)かい対応(たいおう) (detailed response)
  • きめ細(こま)かい指導(しどう) (thorough instruction)

SIMILAR WORDS:
丁寧(ていねい) means polite or careful and focuses on manner and politeness. きめ細(こま)かい emphasizes paying attention to small details and leaving nothing overlooked.

Related Words

Synonym: 丁寧(ていねい) (polite, careful)
