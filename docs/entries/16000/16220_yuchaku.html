癒着(ゆちゃく)

ゆちゃく
noun, verb (suru)
adhesion; collusion; cozy relationship
1. adhesion (medical)
The abnormal joining of tissues or organs that are normally separate, often occurring after surgery or inflammation.
手術(しゅじゅつ)後(ご)に癒着(ゆちゃく)が起(お)きた。
Adhesion occurred after the surgery.
腸(ちょう)の癒着(ゆちゃく)で再手術(さいしゅじゅつ)が必要(ひつよう)になった。
Intestinal adhesion made another surgery necessary.
癒着(ゆちゃく)を防(ふせ)ぐために、術後(じゅつご)の経過(けいか)観察(かんさつ)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Post-operative monitoring is important to prevent adhesion.
2. collusion; cozy relationship; corrupt ties
An improperly close relationship between parties that should remain independent, especially between politicians and businesses or between government and industry.
政治家(せいじか)と業者(ぎょうしゃ)の癒着(ゆちゃく)が問題(もんだい)になった。
The collusion between politicians and contractors became an issue.
官民(かんみん)癒着(ゆちゃく)の体質(たいしつ)を改(あらた)めるべきだ。
We should reform the culture of government-business collusion.
長年(ながねん)の癒着(ゆちゃく)関係(かんけい)が明(あき)るみに出(で)て、大(おお)きなスキャンダルになった。
A long-standing collusive relationship came to light and became a major scandal.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is a medical term used frequently in clinical and health contexts. Sense 2 is a common political/journalistic term describing corrupt relationships, especially in news reports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 癒着(ゆちゃく)が起(お)きる: adhesion occurs
  • 癒着(ゆちゃく)関係(かんけい): collusive relationship
  • 官民(かんみん)癒着(ゆちゃく): government-business collusion
  • 癒着(ゆちゃく)体質(たいしつ): culture of collusion

WORD FORMATION:
癒(ゆ) (heal) + 着(ちゃく) (attach). Originally a medical term for tissues healing together.
