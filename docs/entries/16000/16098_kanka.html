感化(かんか)

かんか
noun
influence; inspiration; moral reform
1. influence; inspiration; being influenced (by someone's character or actions)
The act of being deeply influenced or changed by another person's character, behavior, or ideas, typically in a positive direction.
先生(せんせい)に感化(かんか)された。
I was influenced by my teacher.
友人(ゆうじん)に感化(かんか)されて読書(どくしょ)を始(はじ)めた。
I was inspired by a friend and started reading.
彼女(かのじょ)の情熱的(じょうねつてき)な生(い)き方(かた)に感化(かんか)されて、自分(じぶん)も新(あたら)しいことに挑戦(ちょうせん)しようと思(おも)った。
Inspired by her passionate way of living, I decided to try new things myself.

USAGE:
Used as a suru-verb (感化(かんか)する). Most often appears in the passive form 感化(かんか)される (to be influenced). Carries a positive or neutral nuance of being changed for the better through someone's example.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜に感化(かんか)される (to be influenced by ~)
  • 感化(かんか)を受(う)ける (to receive influence)
  • 感化(かんか)院(いん) (reformatory — historical term)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 影響(えいきょう): influence — more general and neutral, can be positive or negative
  • 触発(しょくはつ): inspiration, stimulus — being spurred to action by something
