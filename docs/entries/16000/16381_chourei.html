朝礼(ちょうれい)

ちょうれい
noun
morning assembly; morning meeting
1. morning assembly; morning meeting
A brief gathering held at the start of the day in schools, workplaces, or organizations. In schools, students typically line up in the schoolyard for announcements. In companies, it is a short meeting to share information and set the day's agenda.
毎朝(まいあさ)朝礼(ちょうれい)がある。
There's a morning assembly every day.
朝礼(ちょうれい)で校長(こうちょう)先生(せんせい)が話(はなし)をした。
The principal gave a talk at the morning assembly.
うちの会社(かいしゃ)では朝礼(ちょうれい)で一日(いちにち)の予定(よてい)を確認(かくにん)してから仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)める。
At our company, we start work after confirming the day's schedule at the morning meeting.

USAGE:
朝礼(ちょうれい) is a common practice in both Japanese schools and companies. School 朝礼(ちょうれい) typically involves the whole school gathering in the schoolyard or gym, while workplace 朝礼(ちょうれい) may be a brief stand-up meeting within a department.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 朝礼(ちょうれい)で話(はな)す (to speak at the morning assembly)
  • 朝礼(ちょうれい)に出(で)る (to attend the morning assembly)
  • 全校(ぜんこう)朝礼(ちょうれい) (whole-school morning assembly)
  • 朝礼(ちょうれい)の時間(じかん) (morning assembly time)

RELATED WORDS:
終礼(しゅうれい) is the end-of-day meeting or assembly, the counterpart to 朝礼(ちょうれい).
