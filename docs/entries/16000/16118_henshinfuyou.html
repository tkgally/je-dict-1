返信不要(へんしんふよう)

へんしんふよう
expression
no reply necessary
1. no reply necessary; no need to respond
A phrase added to messages or emails to let the recipient know that a response is not expected.
返信不要(へんしんふよう)です。
No reply is necessary.
ご確認(かくにん)のみで結構(けっこう)です。返信不要(へんしんふよう)。
Just for your confirmation. No reply needed.
メールの最後(さいご)に「返信不要(へんしんふよう)」と書(か)いてあったので、安心(あんしん)した。
It said 'no reply needed' at the end of the email, so I felt relieved.

USAGE:
返信不要(へんしんふよう) is a concise expression used mainly in emails and text messages. It combines 返信(へんしん) (reply) and 不要(ふよう) (unnecessary). Adding this to a message is considered considerate, as it saves the recipient from feeling obligated to respond.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 返信不要(へんしんふよう)です (no reply needed — polite)
  • 返信(へんしん)は不要(ふよう)です (a reply is not necessary — slightly more formal)
