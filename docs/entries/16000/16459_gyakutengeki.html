逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)

ぎゃくてんげき
noun
dramatic comeback; turnaround; reversal drama
1. dramatic comeback; thrilling reversal; turnaround
A dramatic reversal of fortune, especially a comeback victory in sports, an unexpected turnaround in business, or a surprising change of outcome. The word 劇(げき) (drama) emphasizes the dramatic or theatrical nature of the reversal.
見事(みごと)な逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)だった。
It was a splendid comeback.
後半(こうはん)に逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)が起(お)きた。
A dramatic comeback occurred in the second half.
誰(だれ)もが負(ま)けると思(おも)っていた試合(しあい)で、歴史(れきし)に残(のこ)る逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)が生(う)まれた。
In a match everyone thought they would lose, a historic comeback was born.

Compound noun: 逆転(ぎゃくてん) (reversal) + 劇(げき) (drama, play). Frequently used in sports journalism and can also be applied to business or political turnarounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)を演(えん)じる (to stage a dramatic comeback)
  • 逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)が起(お)きる (a turnaround occurs)
  • 劇的(げきてき)な逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき) (a dramatic comeback)
