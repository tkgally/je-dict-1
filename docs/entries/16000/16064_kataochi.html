型落(かたお)ち

かたおち
noun
outdated model; last year's model; discontinued model
1. outdated model; last year's model; previous-generation product
A product that is no longer the current model because a newer version has been released. Often sold at a discount. Common in discussions of electronics, cars, and appliances.
型落(かたお)ちの商品(しょうひん)は安(やす)く買(か)える。
You can buy outdated models cheaply.
このパソコンは型落(かたお)ちだが、十分(じゅうぶん)に使(つか)える。
This computer is a previous model, but it's perfectly usable.
新型(しんがた)が出(で)たので、型落(かたお)ちの冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)が大幅(おおはば)に値下(ねさ)げされていた。
Since the new model came out, the previous-generation refrigerator was heavily discounted.

USAGE:
型落(かたお)ち combines 型(かた) (model, type) and 落(お)ち (falling, dropping). The word implies that the product has dropped from being the current model. It is commonly used in consumer and retail contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 型落(かたお)ち品(ひん) (outdated-model product)
  • 型落(かたお)ちの家電(かでん) (previous-generation appliance)
  • 型落(かたお)ちを狙(ねら)う (to aim for last year's model)

SIMILAR WORDS:
旧型(きゅうがた) means old model and is used more broadly, including for industrial or military equipment. 型落(かたお)ち specifically implies a consumer product that has been superseded by a newer version and is often associated with discount shopping.
