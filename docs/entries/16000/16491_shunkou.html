1. completion of construction; finishing of a building project
The completion of a building, bridge, or other construction project. A formal term used in architecture, real estate, and civil engineering.
ビルが竣工(しゅんこう)した。
The building was completed.
新(あたら)しい橋(はし)は来年(らいねん)竣工(しゅんこう)の予定(よてい)だ。
The new bridge is scheduled for completion next year.
マンションの竣工(しゅんこう)予定日(よていび)が三(さん)か月(げつ)遅(おく)れると発表(はっぴょう)された。
It was announced that the condominium's completion date would be delayed by three months.

USAGE:
A formal and technical term common in real estate and construction. Often seen in property listings, construction announcements, and news about infrastructure projects. The 竣工(しゅんこう)式(しき) (completion ceremony) is held to celebrate the finishing of a major construction project.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 竣工(しゅんこう)予定(よてい) (scheduled completion)
  • 竣工(しゅんこう)式(しき) (completion ceremony)
  • 竣工(しゅんこう)年月(ねんげつ) (completion date)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完成(かんせい): completion — a more general term for finishing anything, not limited to construction
  • 着工(ちゃっこう): start of construction — the opposite; the beginning of a building project
  • 落成(らくせい): completion of a building — similar to 竣工(しゅんこう) but slightly more ceremonial

Related Words

Antonym: 着工(ちゃっこう) (start of construction)
Synonym: 完成(かんせい) (completion (general))
