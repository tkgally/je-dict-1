独(ひと)り立(だ)ち

ひとりだち
noun, verb (suru)
becoming independent; standing on one's own
1. becoming independent; standing on one's own; going solo
The act of becoming self-sufficient and no longer relying on parents, mentors, or others for support. Can refer to personal independence, professional independence, or a business becoming self-sustaining.
息子(むすこ)がようやく独(ひと)り立(だ)ちした。
My son has finally become independent.
師匠(ししょう)のもとを離(はな)れて独(ひと)り立(だ)ちする。
To leave one's master and go independent.
社会人(しゃかいじん)として独(ひと)り立(だ)ちするには、経済的(けいざいてき)にも精神的(せいしんてき)にも自立(じりつ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To become independent as a working adult, you need both financial and emotional self-reliance.

USAGE:
独(ひと)り立(だ)ち is used for young adults leaving home, apprentices going out on their own, or businesses becoming self-sustaining. It emphasizes the transition from dependence to independence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独(ひと)り立(だ)ちする: to become independent
  • 独(ひと)り立(だ)ちさせる: to help someone become independent
  • 経済的(けいざいてき)に独(ひと)り立(だ)ちする: to become financially independent

SIMILAR WORDS:
自立(じりつ) is a more formal word for independence/self-reliance. 独(ひと)り立(だ)ち emphasizes the moment or process of becoming independent, while 自立(じりつ) describes the ongoing state.

WORD FORMATION:
独(ひと)り (alone) + 立(だ)ち (standing). Literally "standing alone."
