口座開設(こうざかいせつ)

こうざかいせつ
noun, suru verb
opening a bank account
1. opening a bank account, account opening
The process of establishing a new bank account or other financial account.
口座開設(こうざかいせつ)の手続(てつづ)きをする。
To complete the procedures for opening an account.
ネットで口座開設(こうざかいせつ)ができるようになった。
It's now possible to open an account online.
口座開設(こうざかいせつ)には本人(ほんにん)確認(かくにん)書類(しょるい)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Identification documents are required to open an account.

口座開設(こうざかいせつ) is composed of 口座(こうざ) (account) + 開設(かいせつ) (establishment, opening).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 口座開設(こうざかいせつ)の手続(てつづ)き: account opening procedures
  • 口座開設(こうざかいせつ)申込書(もうしこみしょ): account opening application form
  • 口座開設(こうざかいせつ)する: to open an account

USAGE: A practical term frequently encountered when dealing with Japanese banks. Opening an account in Japan typically requires a personal seal (印鑑(いんかん)), identification, and proof of address (住所(じゅうしょ)確認(かくにん)書類(しょるい)).
