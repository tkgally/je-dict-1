驚嘆(きょうたん)

きょうたん
noun / suru verb
wonder; amazement; admiration
1. wonder; amazement; admiration
A feeling of astonishment or awe at something remarkable or impressive. Often used to describe being deeply moved by someone's skill, achievement, or a spectacle.
驚嘆(きょうたん)の声(こえ)が上(あ)がった。
Cries of wonder went up.
彼(かれ)の技術(ぎじゅつ)に驚嘆(きょうたん)した。
I was amazed by his skill.
観客(かんきゃく)は演奏(えんそう)の素晴(すば)らしさに驚嘆(きょうたん)し、拍手(はくしゅ)を送(おく)った。
The audience marveled at the magnificent performance and applauded.

USAGE:
A somewhat literary or formal word. Often used with する to form a suru verb meaning "to marvel at" or "to be amazed." Carries a positive nuance of being impressed, as opposed to simply being surprised.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 驚嘆(きょうたん)する (to marvel, to be amazed)
  • 驚嘆(きょうたん)の声(こえ) (cries of wonder)
  • 驚嘆(きょうたん)に値(あたい)する (to be worthy of admiration)
  • 驚嘆(きょうたん)すべき (admirable, marvelous)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 感嘆(かんたん): admiration with emotional depth — emphasizes being deeply moved
  • 驚愕(きょうがく): astonishment — stronger shock, not necessarily positive
