早期発見(そうきはっけん)

そうきはっけん
noun
early detection; early discovery
1. early detection; early discovery
Finding a disease, problem, or condition at an early stage. Most commonly used in medical contexts about detecting illnesses before they progress.
がんは早期発見(そうきはっけん)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Early detection is important for cancer.
定期(ていき)検診(けんしん)は病気(びょうき)の早期発見(そうきはっけん)につながる。
Regular check-ups lead to early detection of illness.
早期発見(そうきはっけん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう)のおかげで、完治(かんち)することができた。
Thanks to early detection and early treatment, a complete recovery was possible.

USAGE:
早期発見(そうきはっけん) combines 早期(そうき) (early stage) and 発見(はっけん) (detection, discovery). It is most frequently used in medical and health contexts, often paired with 早期治療(そうきちりょう) (early treatment) in the set phrase 早期発見(そうきはっけん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早期発見(そうきはっけん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう) (early detection and early treatment)
  • 病気(びょうき)の早期発見(そうきはっけん) (early detection of disease)
  • 早期発見(そうきはっけん)が重要(じゅうよう) (early detection is important)
