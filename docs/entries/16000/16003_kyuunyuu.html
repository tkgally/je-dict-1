吸入(きゅうにゅう)

きゅうにゅう
noun
inhalation; inhaling
1. inhalation; inhaling; breathing in
The act of breathing in air, medicine, gas, or other substances. Commonly used in medical contexts for inhaling medication through a device.
薬(くすり)を吸入(きゅうにゅう)する。
Inhale the medication.
喘息(ぜんそく)の治療(ちりょう)には吸入(きゅうにゅう)薬(やく)が使(つか)われる。
Inhaled medication is used to treat asthma.
有毒(ゆうどく)ガスを吸入(きゅうにゅう)しないように、マスクを着用(ちゃくよう)してください。
Please wear a mask so you don't inhale toxic gas.

USAGE:
Most commonly used with する as 吸入(きゅうにゅう)する. Frequently appears in medical and safety contexts. The related compound 吸入器(きゅうにゅうき) means "inhaler."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 吸入(きゅうにゅう)する (to inhale)
  • 吸入(きゅうにゅう)器(き) (inhaler)
  • 吸入(きゅうにゅう)薬(やく) (inhaled medication)
  • 酸素(さんそ)吸入(きゅうにゅう) (oxygen inhalation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 呼吸(こきゅう): breathing, respiration — the general process of breathing in and out
  • 吸引(きゅういん): suction, aspiration — drawing in by suction force
