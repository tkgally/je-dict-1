校則(こうそく)

こうそく
noun
school rules; school regulations
1. school rules; school regulations
The formal rules and regulations established by a school that students must follow. Covers dress code, behavior, hairstyle, and other aspects of student conduct.
校則(こうそく)を守(まも)る。
To follow the school rules.
うちの学校(がっこう)は校則(こうそく)が厳(きび)しい。
Our school has strict rules.
時代(じだい)に合(あ)わない校則(こうそく)を見直(みなお)すべきだという声(こえ)が上(あ)がっている。
There are calls to review school rules that are out of step with the times.

USAGE:
校則(こうそく) combines 校(こう) (school) and 則(そく) (rule). In Japan, school rules have been a topic of debate, with some critics calling overly strict rules ブラック校則(こうそく) (unreasonable school rules).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 校則(こうそく)を守(まも)る (to follow school rules)
  • 校則(こうそく)に違反(いはん)する (to violate school rules)
  • 校則(こうそく)を見直(みなお)す (to review school rules)
  • 厳(きび)しい校則(こうそく) (strict school rules)

SIMILAR WORDS:
規則(きそく) is the general word for rules or regulations in any context. 校則(こうそく) is specifically for school rules.
