封入(ふうにゅう)

ふうにゅう
noun / verb (suru)
enclosure; sealing inside
1. enclosure; sealing inside; inserting and sealing
The act of putting something inside an envelope, package, or container and sealing it. Also used for bonus items included in product packaging.
書類(しょるい)を封筒(ふうとう)に封入(ふうにゅう)する。
To enclose documents in an envelope.
封入(ふうにゅう)特典(とくてん)としてカードが付(つ)いてくる。
A card comes as an enclosed bonus.
返信(へんしん)用(よう)の封筒(ふうとう)に切手(きって)を貼(は)って封入(ふうにゅう)してください。
Please affix a stamp to the return envelope and enclose it.

USAGE:
封入(ふうにゅう) is used both for physical mail (putting documents in envelopes) and for product packaging (bonus items sealed inside). In the context of games, DVDs, and CDs, 封入(ふうにゅう)特典(とくてん) (enclosed bonus) refers to extras included in the package.

WORD FORMATION:
封(ふう) (seal) + 入(にゅう) (enter, put in).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 封入(ふうにゅう)する: to enclose, to seal inside
  • 封入(ふうにゅう)特典(とくてん): enclosed bonus/extra
  • 封入(ふうにゅう)物(ぶつ): enclosed items
