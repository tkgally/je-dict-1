ただいま

ただいま
expression
I'm home

Definitions

1. I'm home, I'm back
A set phrase said when returning home or to a place. Literally means 'just now' (from 只今(ただいま)). The response is お帰(かえ)りなさい.
2. right now, at this moment
In formal contexts, ただいま can mean 'at this moment' or 'right now.'

Examples

ただいま！
I'm home!
Said when returning home
「ただいま」「お帰(かえ)りなさい」
"I'm home." "Welcome back."
Standard exchange
ただいま会議中(かいぎちゅう)です。
We are currently in a meeting.
Meaning 'right now'

Notes

This is one of the most common daily expressions. It is said when returning home and is paired with お帰りなさい. The word literally means 'just now,' referring to the moment of arrival.
