種(たね)

たね
noun
seed, source, material

Definitions

1. seed
The unit of reproduction in plants.
2. source, cause
The origin or cause of something.
3. material, topic
Subject matter for discussion or creation.

Examples

花(はな)の種(たね)を蒔(ま)く。
To sow flower seeds.
悩(なや)みの種(たね)になる。
To become a source of worry.

Notes

種(たね) has both literal and figurative meanings.

LITERAL:

  • 種(たね)を蒔(ま)く: to sow seeds
  • 種(たね)を取(と)る: to save seeds

FIGURATIVE:

  • 話(はなし)の種(たね): topic of conversation
  • 笑(わら)いの種(たね): source of laughter
  • 心配(しんぱい)の種(たね): cause of worry
