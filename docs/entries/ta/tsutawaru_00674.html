伝(つた)わる

つたわる
verb (godan)
to be conveyed, to spread

Definitions

1. to be conveyed, to be communicated
For information to reach someone.
2. to be handed down, to be transmitted
For tradition to pass through generations.

Examples

気持(きも)ちが伝(つた)わった。
My feelings got through.
噂(うわさ)が伝(つた)わる。
Rumors spread.
昔(むかし)から伝(つた)わる話(はなし)。
A story passed down from old times.

Notes

伝(つた)わる means to be conveyed or spread.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 伝(つた)わる (intransitive): to be conveyed
  • 伝(つた)える (transitive): to convey

USAGE:

  • Information spreading
  • Traditions being passed down
  • Feelings being understood
