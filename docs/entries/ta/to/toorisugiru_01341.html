通(とお)り過(す)ぎる

とおりすぎる
ichidan verb
to pass by, to go past

Definitions

1. to pass by, to go past
To move past a place or point without stopping.

Examples

駅(えき)を通り過ぎてしまった。
I went past the station (by mistake).
車(くるま)が通り過ぎるのを待(ま)つ。
To wait for the car to pass by.

Notes

通り過ぎる is a compound verb (通る + 過ぎる).

COMMON USES:

  • 目(め)の前(まえ)を通り過ぎる: to pass before one's eyes
  • 時間(じかん)が通り過ぎる: time passes by

RELATED:

  • 通(とお)る: to pass through
  • 過(す)ぎる: to pass, to exceed
