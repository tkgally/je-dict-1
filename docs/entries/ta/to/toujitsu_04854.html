当日(とうじつ)

とうじつ
noun
that day, the day in question

Definitions

1. that day, the day in question, the appointed day
The specific day being discussed or the day when an event takes place.

Examples

当日(とうじつ)は晴(は)れるでしょう。
It will probably be sunny on that day.
当日(とうじつ)券(けん)を購入(こうにゅう)する。
I will purchase a same-day ticket.
当日(とうじつ)キャンセルは料金(りょうきん)がかかる。
Same-day cancellations incur a fee.

Notes

当日(とうじつ) refers to the specific day of an event.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 当日(とうじつ)券(けん): same-day ticket
  • 当日(とうじつ)予約(よやく): same-day reservation
  • 当日(とうじつ)キャンセル: same-day cancellation
  • イベント当日(とうじつ): the day of the event

CONTRAST:

  • 前日(ぜんじつ): the day before
  • 当日(とうじつ): that day, the day of
  • 翌日(よくじつ): the next day
