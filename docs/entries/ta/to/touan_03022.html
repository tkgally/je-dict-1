答案(とうあん)

とうあん
noun
answer sheet, exam paper

Definitions

1. answer sheet, examination paper
A paper or sheet on which answers are written for an examination or test. Can refer to both the blank sheet and the completed answers.

Examples

答案(とうあん)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit your answer sheets.
答案(とうあん)用紙(ようし)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name on the answer sheet.
答案用紙 = answer sheet (paper)
先生(せんせい)が答案(とうあん)を採点(さいてん)している。
The teacher is grading the exam papers.

Notes

USAGE: 答案(とうあん) specifically refers to written answers on examination papers. It's commonly used in school and testing contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 答案(とうあん)を書(か)く (to write answers)
  • 答案(とうあん)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit answer sheets)
  • 答案(とうあん)を採点(さいてん)する (to grade answer sheets)
  • 答案(とうあん)を返却(へんきゃく)する (to return graded papers)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 答案(とうあん)用紙(ようし) (answer sheet, answer paper)
  • 解答(かいとう) (answer, solution)
  • 回答(かいとう) (answer, reply)
  • 試験(しけん) (examination)
  • 採点(さいてん) (grading, scoring)
