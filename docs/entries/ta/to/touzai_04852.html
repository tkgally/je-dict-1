東西(とうざい)

とうざい
noun
east and west

Definitions

1. east and west
The two cardinal directions; or a comparison between Eastern and Western cultures.
2. attention! (interjection)
A call for attention, used at the beginning of announcements in traditional performances.

Examples

東西(とうざい)文化(ぶんか)の交流(こうりゅう)。
Cultural exchange between East and West.
東西(とうざい)南北(なんぼく)に広(ひろ)がる。
It spreads in all directions (east, west, south, north).
東西(とうざい)、東西(とうざい)！
Attention, attention!
Traditional call at kabuki or sumo events

Notes

東西(とうざい) means east and west, or is used as an attention-getting call.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 東西(とうざい)南北(なんぼく): all directions
  • 東西(とうざい)文化(ぶんか): Eastern and Western cultures
  • 古今(ここん)東西(とうざい): all times and places

TRADITIONAL USE:
「東西、東西！」is called out to get the audience's attention before announcements at traditional events like kabuki or sumo.

CONTRAST:

  • 東西(とうざい): east and west
  • 南北(なんぼく): north and south
