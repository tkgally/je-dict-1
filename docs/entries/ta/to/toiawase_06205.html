問(と)い合(あ)わせ

といあわせ
noun, suru verb
inquiry; contact

Definitions

1. inquiry; question; query
A request for information, typically addressed to a company, organization, or authority.
2. contacting; reaching out
The act of getting in touch with someone for information or assistance.

Examples

ご不明(ふめい)な点(てん)はお問(おと)い合(あ)わせください。
Please contact us if you have any questions.
電話(でんわ)で問(と)い合(あ)わせる。
To inquire by phone.
お問(おと)い合(あ)わせフォームから送信(そうしん)してください。
Please submit through the contact form.

Notes

USAGE:

Used as a noun or with する to form a verb.

  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire, to contact
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせをする: to make an inquiry

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お問(と)い合(あ)わせ: inquiry (polite, often seen in business contexts)
  • お問(と)い合(あ)わせ先(さき): contact information
  • お問(と)い合(あ)わせ窓口(まどぐち): inquiry counter/desk
  • お問(と)い合(あ)わせフォーム: contact form

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

  • Websites often have an 「お問(と)い合(あ)わせ」 page for customer inquiries
  • Customer service departments handle 問(と)い合(あ)わせ対応(たいおう)

POLITENESS:
The お prefix is almost always used in business and formal contexts.
