咎(とが)める

とがめる
ichidan verb
to blame; to reproach; to question

Definitions

1. to blame; to reproach; to find fault with
To criticize someone for their wrongdoing or mistake.
2. to question; to challenge
To question someone's actions or identity, often in a suspicious manner.

Examples

良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)める。
My conscience bothers me.
Common idiomatic expression
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)を咎(とが)めた。
I reproached him for his behavior.
警備員(けいびいん)に咎(とが)められた。
I was questioned by the security guard.

Notes

咎める is a literary/formal ichidan verb. The expression 良心が咎める (one's conscience bothers one) is a very common idiom.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 良心が咎める: to feel guilty, conscience bothers one
  • 〜を咎める: to blame/reproach someone for ~

RELATED WORDS:

  • 咎 (とが): fault, blame, sin
  • 咎め (とがめ): blame, censure
