峠(とうげ)

とうげ
noun
mountain pass, peak, crisis

Definitions

1. mountain pass
The highest point of a road crossing mountains.
2. peak, crisis point
The most critical or intense point of something.

Examples

峠(とうげ)を越(こ)える。
To cross a mountain pass.
病気(びょうき)の峠(とうげ)を越(こ)えた。
The illness passed its crisis point.

Notes

峠(とうげ) has both literal and figurative meanings.

LITERAL:
The highest point when crossing a mountain.

FIGURATIVE:
The most difficult or critical point of a situation.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 峠(とうげ)を越(こ)える: to pass the peak, to get over the worst
