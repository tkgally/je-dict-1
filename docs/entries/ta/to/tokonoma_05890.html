床(とこ)の間(ま)

とこのま
noun
alcove in Japanese room

Definitions

1. alcove, recessed area in traditional Japanese room
A recessed area in a Japanese-style room where art, flowers, or scrolls are displayed.

Examples

床(とこ)の間(ま)に掛(か)け軸(じく)を飾(かざ)る。
To display a hanging scroll in the alcove.
床(とこ)の間(ま)には花(はな)が生(い)けてある。
Flowers are arranged in the alcove.
床(とこ)の間(ま)のある和室(わしつ)。
A Japanese-style room with an alcove.

Notes

CULTURAL:
A raised alcove in a traditional Japanese room, considered the most important part of the room. Guests are traditionally seated with their back to the tokonoma as a sign of humility.
