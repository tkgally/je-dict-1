年寄(としよ)り

としより
noun
elderly person, old people

Definitions

1. elderly person, old person
A person of advanced age; senior citizens as a group.

Examples

年寄(としよ)りには席(せき)を譲(ゆず)りましょう。
Let's give up our seats to the elderly.
年寄(としよ)りの話(はなし)は勉強(べんきょう)になる。
Stories from the elderly are educational.
年寄(としよ)り扱(あつか)いされたくない。
I don't want to be treated like an old person.

Notes

KANJI: Can also be written as 年寄り(としより) with okurigana.

USAGE: While not inherently impolite, this term can feel casual or slightly blunt. More polite alternatives exist.

MORE POLITE ALTERNATIVES:

  • お年寄(としよ)り (adding お makes it more polite)
  • 高齢者(こうれいしゃ) (senior citizen - formal)
  • 老人(ろうじん) (old person - neutral/formal)
  • ご老人(ろうじん) (elderly person - respectful)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お年寄(としよ)りを大切(たいせつ)にする (to treat the elderly with care)
  • 年寄(としよ)り臭(くさ)い (acting/looking old - slightly negative)

CONTRAST:

  • 若者(わかもの) (young person) - opposite
