取(と)り出(だ)す

とりだす
verb (godan)
to take out; to extract

Definitions

1. to take out; to get out
To remove something from inside a container, bag, pocket, etc.
2. to extract; to pull out
To retrieve or extract data, information, or specific elements from a larger whole.

Examples

財布(さいふ)からお金(かね)を取(と)り出(だ)した。
I took out money from my wallet.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)から牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)を取(と)り出(だ)して、コップに注(そそ)いだ。
I took out the milk from the refrigerator and poured it into a glass.
このソフトを使(つか)えば、動画(どうが)から音声(おんせい)だけを取(と)り出(だ)すことができる。
If you use this software, you can extract just the audio from a video.

Notes

取(と)り出(だ)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 取(と)る (to take) + 出(だ)す (to put out). It emphasizes the action of removing something from an enclosed space.

ASPECT (ている):
取(と)り出(だ)している indicates the ongoing action of taking something out, while 取(と)り出(だ)した describes the completed action.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [container]から[object]を取(と)り出(だ)す — to take [object] out of [container]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 財布(さいふ)から取(と)り出(だ)す (take out from wallet)
  • 鞄(かばん)から取(と)り出(だ)す (take out from bag)
  • データを取(と)り出(だ)す (extract data)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出(だ)す: more general "to put out, to take out"
  • 取(と)り出(だ)す: emphasizes the extracting action from inside something
  • 引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to pull out (with more force or from a drawer)
