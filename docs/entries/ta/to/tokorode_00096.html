ところで

ところで
conjunction
by the way

Definitions

1. by the way, incidentally
A conjunction used to change the topic or introduce a new subject in conversation.

Examples

ところで、明日(あした)の予定(よてい)は何(なん)ですか。
By the way, what are your plans for tomorrow?
ところで、田中(たなか)さんは来(き)ますか。
By the way, is Tanaka-san coming?
ところで、聞(き)きたいことがあるんですが。
By the way, there's something I wanted to ask you.

Notes

ところで is used to change the subject in conversation. It signals that you're about to introduce a new topic that may or may not be related to what was being discussed. It's natural and commonly used in both casual and polite conversation.
