閉(と)じる

とじる
ichidan verb, transitive/intransitive
to close, to shut

Definitions

1. to close, to shut
To close something like eyes, a book, or a door.

Examples

目(め)を閉(と)じる。
To close one's eyes.
本(ほん)を閉(と)じる。
To close a book.
幕(まく)を閉(と)じる。
To bring down the curtain / to end.
Figurative

Notes

閉(と)じる is often used for eyes and books.

DISTINCTION:

  • 閉(と)じる: to close (eyes, books)
  • 閉(し)める: to close (doors, windows)
  • 綴(と)じる: to bind (pages)
