トイレ

といれ
noun
toilet, bathroom

Definitions

1. toilet, restroom, bathroom
A room containing a toilet. The most common word for toilet/restroom in modern Japanese.

Examples

トイレはどこですか。
Where is the toilet?
ちょっとトイレに行(い)ってきます。
I'll go to the restroom for a moment.
トイレを使(つか)ってもいいですか。
May I use the restroom?

Notes

トイレ is the most common and neutral term. Other terms: お手洗(てあら)い (polite), 化粧室けしょうしつ} (very polite, 'powder room'), {便所(べんじょ) (old-fashioned/crude). Japanese toilets often have advanced features like heated seats and bidets.
