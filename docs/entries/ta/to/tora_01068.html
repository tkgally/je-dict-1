虎(とら)

とら
noun
tiger

Definitions

1. tiger
A large striped wild cat.

Examples

虎(とら)は強(つよ)い動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Tigers are powerful animals.
動物園(どうぶつえん)で虎(とら)を見(み)た。
I saw a tiger at the zoo.

Notes

虎(とら) refers to tigers.

ZODIAC:
寅(とら) is the third animal of the Chinese zodiac.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 虎(とら)の威(い)を借(か)る狐(きつね): a fox borrowing the tiger's power
  • 虎穴(こけつ)に入(い)らずんば虎子(こじ)を得(え)ず: nothing ventured, nothing gained
