戸惑(とまど)う

とまどう
verb (godan)
to be bewildered, to be confused

Definitions

1. to be bewildered, to be confused, to be perplexed
To feel lost or confused, not knowing how to respond or act in an unexpected or unfamiliar situation. Often implies momentary disorientation.

Examples

突然(とつぜん)の質問(しつもん)に戸惑(とまど)った。
I was bewildered by the sudden question.
初(はじ)めての海外旅行(かいがいりょこう)で戸惑(とまど)うことが多(おお)かった。
I was often confused during my first trip abroad.
彼女(かのじょ)は戸惑(とまど)いながらも、笑顔(えがお)で答(こた)えた。
Though bewildered, she answered with a smile.
Uses noun form 戸惑い

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xに戸惑(とまど)う (be confused by X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state of confusion. 戸惑(とまど)っている = 'is confused' or 'is at a loss.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 変化(へんか)に戸惑(とまど)う (be confused by changes)
  • 対応(たいおう)に戸惑(とまど)う (be at a loss how to respond)
  • 戸惑(とまど)いを隠(かく)せない (cannot hide one's confusion)

RELATED: The noun form 戸惑(とまど)い (confusion, bewilderment) is also commonly used.

Related Words

Related: 迷(まよ)う
Related: 躊躇(ためら)う
