とても

とても
adverb
very, extremely

Definitions

1. very, extremely
To a great degree; used to intensify adjectives and verbs.

Examples

この料理(りょうり)はとてもおいしいです。
This dish is very delicious.
とても疲(つか)れました。
I'm very tired.
とても楽(たの)しかったです。
It was very fun.

Notes

Similar words: 大変(たいへん) (very, terribly), すごく (extremely, informal). とても is versatile and polite.

Related Words

See also: 大変(たいへん)
