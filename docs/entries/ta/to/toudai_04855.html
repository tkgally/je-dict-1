灯台(とうだい)

とうだい
noun
lighthouse

Definitions

1. lighthouse
A tower with a powerful light that guides ships at sea.

Examples

灯台(とうだい)の光(ひかり)が見(み)える。
I can see the lighthouse's light.
岬(みさき)の先(さき)に灯台(とうだい)がある。
There is a lighthouse at the tip of the cape.
灯台(とうだい)下(もと)暗(くら)し。
It's dark at the foot of the lighthouse. (Proverb: One fails to see what is close at hand.)

Notes

灯台(とうだい) refers to a lighthouse.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 灯台(とうだい)守(もり): lighthouse keeper
  • 灯台(とうだい)の光(ひかり): lighthouse light

PROVERB:
灯台(とうだい)下(もと)暗(くら)し (toudai moto kurashi): "It's dark at the foot of the lighthouse." Meaning: People often fail to notice things that are right in front of them.

NOTE:
Not to be confused with 東大(とうだい) (Tokyo University).
