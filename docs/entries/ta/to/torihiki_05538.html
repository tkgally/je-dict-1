取引(とりひき)

とりひき
noun
transaction

Definitions

1. transaction, business deal, trade
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

この会社(かいしゃ)とは長(なが)い取引(とりひき)がある。
We have a long business relationship with this company.
株(かぶ)の取引(とりひき)を始(はじ)めた。
I started trading stocks.
取引(とりひき)条件(じょうけん)について話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed the terms of the deal.

Notes

取引(とりひき) refers to business transactions, deals, or trading relationships.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 取引(とりひき)する (to do business, to trade)
  • 取引(とりひき)を始(はじ)める (to start doing business)
  • 取引(とりひき)先(さき) (business partner/client)
  • 取引(とりひき)条件(じょうけん) (transaction terms)
  • 取引(とりひき)銀行(ぎんこう) (bank one does business with)

TYPES OF TRANSACTIONS:

  • 株(かぶ)取引(とりひき) (stock trading)
  • 為替(かわせ)取引(とりひき) (foreign exchange trading)
  • 現金(げんきん)取引(とりひき) (cash transaction)
  • 信用(しんよう)取引(とりひき) (margin trading)
  • 電子(でんし)取引(とりひき) (electronic commerce)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 取引(とりひき)先(さき) (business partner)
  • 取引(とりひき)履歴(りれき) (transaction history)
