問(と)う

とう
godan verb, transitive
to ask, to question, to inquire

Definitions

1. to ask, to question
To seek an answer or information.
2. to hold responsible
To question someone's responsibility.

Examples

理由(りゆう)を問(と)う。
To ask the reason.
責任(せきにん)を問(と)う。
To hold someone responsible.
問(と)わず。
Regardless of...
Common pattern: ～を問(と)わず

Notes

問(と)う is a formal way to ask or question.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を問(と)わず: regardless of ~
  • 責任(せきにん)を問(と)う: to hold responsible
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire
