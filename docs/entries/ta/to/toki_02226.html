〜時(とき)

とき
noun
when, at the time of

Definitions

1. when, at the time of
Used after verbs, adjectives, or nouns to create temporal clauses meaning 'when' or 'at the time of.' Functions as a dependent noun that describes the timing of the main clause.

Examples

日本(にほん)に行(い)った時(とき)、富士山(ふじさん)を見(み)ました。
When I went to Japan, I saw Mt. Fuji.
Past tense + 時(とき)
暇(ひま)な時(とき)に来(き)てください。
Please come when you have free time.
Na-adjective + な + 時(とき)
困(こま)った時(とき)は連絡(れんらく)してください。
When you're in trouble, please contact me.

Notes

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • Verb (plain) + 時(とき): 食(た)べる時(とき) (when eating)
  • Verb (た form) + 時(とき): 食(た)べた時(とき) (when [I] ate)
  • い-adjective + 時(とき): 若(わか)い時(とき) (when young)
  • な-adjective + な + 時(とき): 暇(ひま)な時(とき) (when free)
  • Noun + の + 時(とき): 子供(こども)の時(とき) (when [I was] a child)

TENSE NUANCE:

  • 行(い)く時(とき) = when going (before/during)
  • 行(い)った時(とき) = when [I] went (completed action)

This tense distinction is important: dictionary form emphasizes the time before or during the action, while た form emphasizes after the action is complete.

Often followed by particles like は or に: 時(とき)は (when..., as for), 時(とき)に (at the time when).
