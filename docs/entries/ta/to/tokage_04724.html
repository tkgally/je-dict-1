蜥蜴(とかげ)

とかげ
noun
lizard

Definitions

1. lizard
A reptile with a long body, four legs, and a tail. Many species can shed their tails when threatened.

Examples

蜥蜴(とかげ)が石垣(いしがき)の隙間(すきま)から顔(かお)を出(だ)した。
A lizard peeked out from a gap in the stone wall.
蜥蜴(とかげ)は敵(てき)に襲(おそ)われると尻尾(しっぽ)を切(き)って逃(に)げる。
Lizards can drop their tails to escape when attacked by enemies.
子供(こども)が庭(にわ)で蜥蜴(とかげ)を見(み)つけて大喜(おおよろこ)びした。
The child found a lizard in the garden and was overjoyed.

Notes

蜥蜴(とかげ) refers to lizards in general.

Alternative writing: トカゲ (katakana)

Common expressions:

  • 蜥蜴(とかげ)の尻尾(しっぽ)切(き)り (cutting off a lizard's tail - used figuratively for sacrificing a subordinate to protect oneself)
  • 蜥蜴(とかげ)の尻尾(しっぽ) (lizard's tail - something expendable)

Counter: 匹(ひき)

Note:
蜥蜴(とかげ)の尻尾(しっぽ)切(き)り is commonly used in business and political contexts to describe scapegoating or letting a lower-ranking person take the blame.
