妥当(だとう)

だとう
na-adjective, noun
proper, appropriate, valid

Definitions

1. proper, appropriate, reasonable, valid
Something that is fitting, suitable, or acceptable given the circumstances. Often used to describe decisions, prices, or judgments.

Examples

この価格(かかく)は妥当(だとう)だと思(おも)う。
I think this price is reasonable.
妥当(だとう)な判断(はんだん)を下(くだ)してください。
Please make an appropriate judgment.
その結論(けつろん)の妥当(だとう)性(せい)を検証(けんしょう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to verify the validity of that conclusion.
妥当性 = validity, appropriateness

Notes

USAGE: 妥当(だとう) is used to describe something as reasonable, proper, or valid. It's commonly used in formal and business contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 妥当(だとう)な + noun (appropriate ~)
  • 妥当(だとう)だ/である (is appropriate)
  • 妥当(だとう)性(せい) (validity, appropriateness - noun form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 妥当(だとう)な価格(かかく) (reasonable price)
  • 妥当(だとう)な判断(はんだん) (appropriate judgment)
  • 妥当(だとう)な結論(けつろん) (valid conclusion)
  • 妥当(だとう)性(せい)を検討(けんとう)する (to consider validity)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 適切(てきせつ) (appropriate, suitable)
  • 適当(てきとう) (suitable; also: half-hearted, sloppy)
