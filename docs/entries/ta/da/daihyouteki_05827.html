代表的(だいひょうてき)

だいひょうてき
na-adjective
representative; typical

Definitions

1. representative; typical
Serving as a prime example or embodiment of a category; exemplary.

Examples

富士山(ふじさん)は日本(にほん)の代表的(だいひょうてき)な山(やま)だ。
Mount Fuji is a representative mountain of Japan.
これは代表的(だいひょうてき)な例(れい)の一(ひと)つです。
This is one of the representative examples.
寿司(すし)は日本(にほん)の代表的(だいひょうてき)な料理(りょうり)だ。
Sushi is a representative Japanese dish.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
代表(だいひょう) (representative) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

NUANCE:
代表的(だいひょうてき) emphasizes that something is a prime example or symbol of its category—not just typical, but exemplary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代表的(だいひょうてき)な例(れい): representative example
  • 代表的(だいひょうてき)な作品(さくひん): representative work
  • 代表的(だいひょうてき)な人物(じんぶつ): representative figure
  • 代表的(だいひょうてき)な料理(りょうり): representative dish

RELATED TERMS:

  • 代表(だいひょう): representative, symbol
  • 典型的(てんけいてき): typical, archetypal
