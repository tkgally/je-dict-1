妥当(だとう)

だとう
na-adjective
reasonable; appropriate; valid

Definitions

1. reasonable; appropriate; valid; proper
Being suitable, acceptable, or logically sound; meeting expected standards.

Examples

その判断(はんだん)は妥当(だとう)だと思(おも)う。
I think that judgment is reasonable.
妥当(だとう)な価格(かかく)を設定(せってい)する。
To set a reasonable price.
妥当性(だとうせい)を検証(けんしょう)する。
To verify the validity.

Notes

妥当(だとう) is used to describe decisions, prices, or conclusions that are sound and appropriate.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 妥当性(だとうせい): validity, appropriateness
  • 妥当(だとう)な結論(けつろん): reasonable conclusion
