抱(だ)く

だく
verb (godan)
to embrace, to hug, to hold

Definitions

1. to embrace, to hug
To hold someone in one's arms.
2. to hold, to carry
To hold something against one's body.

Examples

子供(こども)を抱(だ)く。
To hold a child.
彼女(かのじょ)を強(つよ)く抱(だ)いた。
I hugged her tightly.

Notes

抱(だ)く means to hold in arms.

READINGS:

  • 抱(だ)く: to embrace (spoken)
  • 抱(いだ)く: to harbor (feelings), more literary

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 抱(だ)く (transitive): to embrace
  • 抱(だ)かれる: to be embraced
