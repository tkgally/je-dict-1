断崖(だんがい)

だんがい
noun
cliff, precipice

Definitions

1. cliff, precipice, bluff
A steep rock face, especially at the edge of the sea or a mountain.

Examples

断崖(だんがい)の上(うえ)からの景色(けしき)は絶景(ぜっけい)だった。
The view from the top of the cliff was spectacular.
波(なみ)が断崖(だんがい)に打(う)ちつけていた。
Waves were crashing against the cliff.
断崖(だんがい)絶壁(ぜっぺき)の道(みち)を慎重(しんちょう)に歩(ある)いた。
I walked carefully along the path on the sheer cliff.

Notes

断崖(だんがい) refers to a steep cliff or precipice. 断(だん) means 'cut off/sheer' and 崖(がい) means 'cliff.'

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 断崖(だんがい)絶壁(ぜっぺき): sheer cliff, precipice
  • 断崖(だんがい)から落(お)ちる: to fall from a cliff
  • 海(うみ)の断崖(だんがい): sea cliff
  • 断崖(だんがい)を登(のぼ)る: to climb a cliff

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • 断崖(だんがい)絶壁(ぜっぺき)に立(た)つ: to stand on the edge (of disaster)
  • 崖(がけ)っぷち: on the brink/edge

RELATED WORDS:

  • 崖(がけ): cliff (more common in speech)
  • 絶壁(ぜっぺき): precipice, steep cliff
  • 岩壁(がんぺき): rock wall
