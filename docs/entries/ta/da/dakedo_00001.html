だけど

だけど
conjunction
but; however

Definitions

1. but; however
A casual conjunction used to introduce a contrasting statement or add reservation.

Examples

行(い)きたい。だけど、時間(じかん)がない。
I want to go. But I don't have time.
高(たか)い。だけど、品質(ひんしつ)はいい。
It's expensive. However, the quality is good.
だけど、それでいいの？
But is that okay?

Notes

だけど is a casual conjunction meaning "but" or "however."

REGISTER:

  • だけど: casual, conversational
  • だけれど/だけれども: slightly more formal
  • けど/けれど/けれども: after verbs/adjectives
  • でも: casual, very common in speech
  • しかし/だが: formal, written

USAGE:

  • Placed at the beginning of a sentence or between clauses
  • Common in everyday conversation
  • Can soften statements or add hesitation

COMPARISON:

  • だけど emphasizes contrast more clearly than けど
  • Often used when the speaker is about to say something contradictory

VARIATION:

  • {だ}けども is also used, slightly more emphatic

Related Words

Synonym: でも (but)
Related: だが (however (formal))
