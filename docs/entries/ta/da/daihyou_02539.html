代表(だいひょう)

だいひょう
noun, suru-verb
representative, representation

Definitions

1. representative, delegate
A person who speaks or acts on behalf of others or a group.
2. representation, representing
The act of speaking or acting on behalf of others; being a symbol or example of something.

Examples

彼(かれ)は日本(にほん)代表(だいひょう)として出場(しゅつじょう)した。
He participated as a representative of Japan.
Sense 1
この会社(かいしゃ)を代表(だいひょう)して挨拶(あいさつ)します。
I will give a greeting on behalf of this company.
Sense 2: suru-verb
富士山(ふじさん)は日本(にほん)を代表(だいひょう)する山(やま)だ。
Mt. Fuji is a mountain that represents Japan.
Sense 2: being symbolic

Notes

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 代表者(だいひょうしゃ) (representative person)
  • 代表取締役(だいひょうとりしまりやく) (representative director, CEO)
  • 代表チーム(だいひょうちーむ) (national team)
  • 代表作(だいひょうさく) (representative work, masterpiece)

GRAMMAR:

  • 〜を代表(だいひょう)する: to represent ~
  • 〜を代表(だいひょう)して: on behalf of ~

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代表(だいひょう)に選(えら)ばれる (to be chosen as representative)
  • 代表(だいひょう)を務(つと)める (to serve as representative)

Related Words
