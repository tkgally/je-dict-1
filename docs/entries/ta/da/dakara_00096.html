だから

だから
conjunction
therefore, so

Definitions

1. therefore, so, that's why
A conjunction indicating cause and effect. Casual form of ですから.

Examples

雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っています。だから、傘(かさ)を持(も)っていきます。
It's raining. So, I'll take an umbrella.
忙(いそが)しいです。だから、行(い)けません。
I'm busy. That's why I can't go.
だから言(い)ったでしょう。
That's why I told you. / I told you so.
Common expression

Notes

だから connects a reason to its result and is used in casual speech. The polite equivalent is ですから. だから can also be used at the beginning of a response to emphasize that something was already stated or known (だから言(い)ったでしょう - I told you so).
