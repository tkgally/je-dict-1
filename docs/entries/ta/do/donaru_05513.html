怒鳴(どな)る

どなる
godan verb
to shout (angrily); to yell

Definitions

1. to shout angrily; to yell
To speak loudly and aggressively, typically in anger.

Examples

上司(じょうし)に怒鳴(どな)られた。
I was yelled at by my boss.
怒鳴(どな)っても解決(かいけつ)しないよ。
Yelling won't solve anything.
「静(しず)かにしろ！」と怒鳴(どな)った。
He shouted, 'Be quiet!'

Notes

怒鳴る specifically implies angry shouting, as opposed to neutral loud speaking.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 叫(さけ)ぶ: to scream, to shout (neutral, not necessarily angry)
  • 喚(わめ)く: to yell, to scream (often hysterical or out of control)
  • 怒(おこ)る: to get angry (doesn't necessarily involve shouting)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に怒鳴る: to yell at ~
  • 怒鳴り声 (どなりごえ): yelling voice
  • 怒鳴りつける: to yell at fiercely
