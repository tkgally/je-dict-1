独立(どくりつ)

どくりつ
noun, suru-verb
independence

Definitions

1. independence (national)
The state of a country governing itself without control by another country. Political independence.
2. independence (personal), self-reliance
The state of being able to support oneself financially or emotionally without depending on others.

Examples

1776年(ねん)にアメリカが独立(どくりつ)した。
America gained independence in 1776.
親(おや)から独立(どくりつ)して一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしを始(はじ)めた。
I became independent from my parents and started living alone.
会社(かいしゃ)を辞(や)めて独立(どくりつ)した。
I quit my company job and started my own business.
独立する = to start one's own business

Notes

USAGE: 独立(どくりつ) has two main contexts:
1. Political independence of nations
2. Personal independence (financial, emotional, professional)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 独立(どくりつ)する (to become independent)
  • 独立(どくりつ)記念日(きねんび) (Independence Day)
  • 独立(どくりつ)国(こく) (independent nation)
  • 経済的(けいざいてき)に独立(どくりつ)する (to become financially independent)
  • 独立(どくりつ)開業(かいぎょう) (starting one's own business)
  • 独立(どくりつ)心(しん) (spirit of independence)

NUANCE: When someone says they 独立(どくりつ)した from a company, it often means they started their own business in the same field.

ANTONYM:

  • 依存(いそん) (dependence)

Related Words

Antonym: 依存(いそん) (dependence)
