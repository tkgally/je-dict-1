どうぞ

どうぞ
adverb
please, here you are

Definitions

1. please (go ahead)
Used when offering or inviting someone to do something.
2. here you are
Said when handing something to someone.

Examples

どうぞお入(はい)りください。
Please come in.
どうぞ座(すわ)ってください。
Please have a seat.
「これ、お土産(みやげ)です。」「どうぞ。」
"This is a souvenir." "Here you are."

Notes

どうぞ is a polite expression.

COMMON USES:

  • どうぞ + request (please do ~)
  • どうぞ (when offering something)
  • どうぞよろしく (nice to meet you)

SIMILAR:

  • どうか (please - more emphatic, pleading)
  • ぜひ (by all means, definitely)
