どんな

どんな
pre-noun adjectival
what kind of

Definitions

1. what kind of, what sort of
Used to ask about the nature or type of something.

Examples

どんな映画(えいが)が好(す)きですか。
What kind of movies do you like?
どんな人(ひと)ですか。
What kind of person is he/she?
どんな時(とき)でも頑張(がんば)る。
I'll do my best no matter what.

Notes

どんな asks about the nature or type of something.

KO-SO-A-DO SERIES:

  • こんな (like this)
  • そんな (like that - near you)
  • あんな (like that over there)
  • どんな (what kind of)

PATTERNS:

  • どんな + noun
  • どんなに〜ても (no matter how much)

SIMILAR:

  • どういう (what kind of - slightly more formal)
