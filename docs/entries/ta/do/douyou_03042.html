動揺(どうよう)

どうよう
noun, suru-verb
agitation, disturbance

Definitions

1. agitation, disturbance, emotional upset
A state of being emotionally shaken or disturbed. Can refer to mental agitation or being thrown off balance emotionally.
2. shaking, swaying, instability
Physical shaking or instability, though the emotional meaning is more common.

Examples

彼(かれ)は動揺(どうよう)を隠(かく)せなかった。
He couldn't hide his agitation.
その知(し)らせに動揺(どうよう)した。
I was shaken by that news.
動揺する = to be shaken/disturbed
動揺(どうよう)せずに落(お)ち着(つ)いて対応(たいおう)した。
I responded calmly without being flustered.

Notes

USAGE: 動揺(どうよう) primarily describes emotional disturbance or being mentally shaken. It's commonly used to describe reactions to shocking news or unexpected situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 動揺(どうよう)する (to be shaken/disturbed)
  • 動揺(どうよう)させる (to shake/disturb someone)
  • 動揺(どうよう)を隠(かく)す (to hide one's agitation)
  • 動揺(どうよう)を見(み)せる (to show one's agitation)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 童謡(どうよう) (children's song) - same reading, different meaning

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 狼狽(ろうばい) (panic, confusion)
  • 混乱(こんらん) (confusion)
  • うろたえる (to be flustered)
  • 慌(あわ)てる (to panic, be in a hurry)

Related Words

Contrast: 童謡(どうよう) (children's song (homophone))
Related: 混乱(こんらん) (confusion)
