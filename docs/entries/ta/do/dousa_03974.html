動作(どうさ)

どうさ
noun, suru-verb
movement, action, operation

Definitions

1. movement, motion, action, gesture
Physical movement or actions performed by a person or animal.
2. operation, functioning (of machines)
The working or running of a machine or system.

Examples

彼(かれ)の動作(どうさ)は機敏(きびん)だ。
His movements are agile.
この機械(きかい)は正常(せいじょう)に動作(どうさ)している。
This machine is operating normally.
動作(どうさ)を真似(まね)してください。
Please imitate the movement.

Notes

USAGE: 動作(どうさ) is used for both human/animal movements and machine operations.

FOR PEOPLE:

  • 動作(どうさ)が遅(おそ)い (slow in one's movements)
  • 動作(どうさ)が速(はや)い (quick in one's movements)
  • 基本(きほん)動作(どうさ) (basic movements)

FOR MACHINES/COMPUTERS:

  • 動作(どうさ)確認(かくにん) (operation check)
  • 動作(どうさ)環境(かんきょう) (operating environment, system requirements)
  • 動作(どうさ)しない (not working, not functioning)
  • 動作(どうさ){テスト} (operation test)

CONTRAST:

  • 動作(どうさ) - specific movements/operations
  • 動(うご)き - movement in general
  • 行動(こうどう) - behavior, conduct
