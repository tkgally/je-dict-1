手当(てあて)

てあて
noun
allowance (monetary)

Definitions

1. allowance (monetary), compensation, treatment (medical)
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

通勤(つうきん)手当(てあて)が支給(しきゅう)される。
Commuting allowance is provided.
怪我(けが)の手当(てあて)をした。
I treated the injury.
残業(ざんぎょう)手当(てあて)がつく仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a job that comes with overtime pay.

Notes

手当(てあて) has two main meanings: monetary allowances and medical treatment.

MONETARY ALLOWANCES:

  • 通勤(つうきん)手当(てあて) (commuting allowance)
  • 住宅(じゅうたく)手当(てあて) (housing allowance)
  • 残業(ざんぎょう)手当(てあて) (overtime pay)
  • 家族(かぞく)手当(てあて) (family allowance)
  • 役職(やくしょく)手当(てあて) (position allowance)
  • 児童(じどう)手当(てあて) (child allowance - government benefit)

MEDICAL TREATMENT:

  • 怪我(けが)の手当(てあて) (treating an injury)
  • 応急(おうきゅう)手当(てあて) (first aid)
  • 手当(てあて)をする (to treat, to provide care)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手当(てあて)がつく (allowance is attached/provided)
  • 手当(てあて)を支給(しきゅう)する (to provide allowance)
  • 手当(てあて)を受(う)ける (to receive allowance/treatment)
