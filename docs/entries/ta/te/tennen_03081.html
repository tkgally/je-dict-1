天然(てんねん)

てんねん
noun, no-adjective
natural, nature

Definitions

1. natural, nature
Existing in or produced by nature; not artificial or man-made.
2. natural, airhead (colloquial)
A person who is naturally scatterbrained or innocently oblivious.

Examples

この水(みず)は天然(てんねん)の湧(わ)き水(みず)だ。
This water is natural spring water.
天然(てんねん)素材(そざい)を使(つか)った製品(せいひん)。
Products made with natural materials.
彼女(かのじょ)は天然(てんねん)だから、悪気(わるぎ)はないよ。
She's just naturally airheaded, she doesn't mean any harm.
Colloquial usage

Notes

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 天然(てんねん)水(すい) (natural water)
  • 天然(てんねん)記念物(きねんぶつ) (natural monument, protected species)
  • 天然(てんねん)資源(しげん) (natural resources)
  • 天然(てんねん)素材(そざい) (natural materials)
  • 天然(てんねん)ガス (natural gas)
  • 天然(てんねん)パーマ (naturally curly hair)

COLLOQUIAL USAGE:
天然(てんねん) alone can mean "airhead" or a person who is naturally scatterbrained in an endearing way. This is different from 天然(てんねん)ボケ, which specifically means someone who unintentionally says funny things.

ANTONYM:

  • 人工(じんこう) (artificial, man-made)

Related Words

Antonym: 人工(じんこう) (artificial)
