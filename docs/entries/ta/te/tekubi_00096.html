手首(てくび)

てくび
noun
wrist

Definitions

1. wrist
The joint connecting the hand to the arm. Literally 'hand neck.'

Examples

手首(てくび)を回(まわ)してください。
Please rotate your wrist.
手首(てくび)を捻挫(ねんざ)しました。
I sprained my wrist.
手首(てくび)に時計(とけい)をしています。
I'm wearing a watch on my wrist.

Notes

手首(てくび) literally means 'hand-neck,' combining 手(て) (hand) and 首(くび) (neck). This pattern is common for body joints: 足首(あしくび) (ankle, literally 'foot-neck'). The pulse is often taken at the 手首(てくび).
