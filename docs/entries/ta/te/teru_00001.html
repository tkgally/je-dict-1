照(て)る

てる
godan verb
to shine

Definitions

1. to shine, to beam
For the sun or other light source to emit bright light.

Examples

太陽(たいよう)が照(て)っている。
The sun is shining.
日(ひ)が照(て)ると暑(あつ)い。
It's hot when the sun shines.
晴(は)れて日(ひ)が照(て)ってきた。
It cleared up and the sun came out.

Notes

照(て)る is an intransitive verb meaning to shine (of the sun, etc.).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 照(て)らす (transitive, to illuminate)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 照(て)っている means 'is shining' (ongoing state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 太陽(たいよう)が照(て)る: the sun shines
  • 日(ひ)が照(て)る: the sun shines
  • 照(て)り返(かえ)す: to reflect (light)

WEATHER EXPRESSION:
照(て)ったり降(ふ)ったり = sometimes sunny, sometimes rainy (changeable weather)

Related Words

Pair verb: 照(て)らす (transitive)
