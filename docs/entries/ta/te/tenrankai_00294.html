展覧会(てんらんかい)

てんらんかい
noun
exhibition, show

Definitions

1. exhibition, show, exposition
A public display of works of art or items of interest.

Examples

美術(びじゅつ)展覧会(てんらんかい)に行(い)った。
I went to an art exhibition.
写真(しゃしん)展覧会(てんらんかい)を開(ひら)く。
We're holding a photography exhibition.

Notes

Often abbreviated to 展(てん) in compound words like 美術展(びじゅつてん) (art exhibition).
