典型(てんけい)

てんけい
noun
type, model, typical example

Definitions

1. type, model, typical example, archetype
A person or thing that perfectly represents a particular type or category.

Examples

これは日本(にほん)家屋(かおく)の典型(てんけい)だ。
This is a typical Japanese house.
彼(かれ)は典型(てんけい)的|てき}なサラリーマンだ。
He is a typical company employee.
典型(てんけい)的|てき}な間違(まちが)いを避(さ)ける。
Avoid typical mistakes.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 典型(てんけい)的|てき}な〜 (typical ~, classic ~)
  • 〜の典型(てんけい) (a typical example of ~)

COMMON USES:

  • 典型(てんけい)的|てき}な例(れい) (typical example)
  • 典型(てんけい)的|てき}な日本人(にほんじん) (typical Japanese person)
  • 典型(てんけい)的|てき}な症状(しょうじょう) (typical symptoms)

USAGE NOTE:
典型(てんけい)的|てき} is very commonly used as a na-adjective to mean "typical" or "classic."
