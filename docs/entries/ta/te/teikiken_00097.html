定期券(ていきけん)

ていきけん
noun
commuter pass

Definitions

1. commuter pass, season ticket
A ticket that allows unlimited travel on a specific route for a set period (usually 1, 3, or 6 months).

Examples

定期券(ていきけん)を買(か)いました。
I bought a commuter pass.
定期券(ていきけん)は6ヶ月(かげつ)が一番(いちばん)お得(とく)です。
A 6-month commuter pass is the best value.
定期券(ていきけん)の期限(きげん)が切(き)れました。
My commuter pass has expired.

Notes

定期券 is essential for daily commuters in Japan. Companies often reimburse transportation costs (通勤費). Modern 定期券 are loaded onto IC cards like Suica or PASMO. 定期 means 'regular/periodic' and 券 means 'ticket.'
