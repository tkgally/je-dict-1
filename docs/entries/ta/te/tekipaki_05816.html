てきぱき

てきぱき
adverb, onomatopoeia
briskly; promptly; efficiently

Definitions

1. briskly; promptly; efficiently
Describes doing things quickly and efficiently, handling tasks one after another without delay.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)はてきぱき仕事(しごと)をこなす。
She handles her work briskly.
てきぱき動(うご)いて準備(じゅんび)を終(お)わらせた。
I moved briskly and finished the preparations.
てきぱきした対応(たいおう)で助(たす)かった。
The prompt response was helpful.

Notes

NUANCE:
てきぱき has a positive connotation, implying competence and efficiency. It describes someone who works without wasting time or being confused.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • てきぱき働(はたら)く: to work briskly
  • てきぱき動(うご)く: to move briskly
  • てきぱきした人(ひと): an efficient person
  • てきぱきと指示(しじ)を出(だ)す: to give instructions promptly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • さっさと: quickly (sometimes impatient tone)
  • きびきび: briskly, energetically
  • てきぱき implies more efficiency and capability than simple speed
