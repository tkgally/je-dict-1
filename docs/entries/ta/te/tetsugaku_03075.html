哲学(てつがく)

てつがく
noun
philosophy

Definitions

1. philosophy
The academic study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, and reason.
2. philosophy, outlook, principles
A person's fundamental beliefs or guiding principles.

Examples

大学(だいがく)で哲学(てつがく)を学(まな)んだ。
I studied philosophy at university.
彼(かれ)は独自(どくじ)の哲学(てつがく)を持(も)っている。
He has his own philosophy.
その会社(かいしゃ)の経営(けいえい)哲学(てつがく)に共感(きょうかん)する。
I resonate with that company's business philosophy.

Notes

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 哲学者(てつがくしゃ) (philosopher)
  • 哲学的(てつがくてき) (philosophical)
  • 人生(じんせい)哲学(てつがく) (philosophy of life)
  • 経営(けいえい)哲学(てつがく) (business philosophy, management philosophy)
  • 西洋(せいよう)哲学(てつがく) (Western philosophy)
  • 東洋(とうよう)哲学(てつがく) (Eastern philosophy)

USAGE NOTE:
In everyday speech, 哲学(てつがく) often refers to one's personal principles or approach to life/work rather than the academic discipline.
