哲学(てつがく)

てつがく
noun
philosophy

Definitions

1. philosophy
The study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, reason, and mind.
2. personal philosophy; principles; worldview
One's fundamental beliefs or approach to life.

Examples

大学(だいがく)で哲学(てつがく)を学(まな)ぶ。
To study philosophy at university.
彼(かれ)には独自(どくじ)の経営(けいえい)哲学(てつがく)がある。
He has his own management philosophy.
人生(じんせい)哲学(てつがく)について考(かんが)える。
To think about one's philosophy of life.

Notes

哲学(てつがく) is composed of 哲(てつ) (wise) + 学(がく) (study).

BRANCHES OF PHILOSOPHY:

  • 倫理(りんり)学(がく): ethics
  • 形而上(けいじじょう)学(がく): metaphysics
  • 認識論(にんしきろん): epistemology
  • 美学(びがく): aesthetics

RELATED:

  • 哲学(てつがく)者(しゃ): philosopher
  • 哲学(てつがく)的(てき): philosophical
