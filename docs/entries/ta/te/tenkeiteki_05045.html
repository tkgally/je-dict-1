典型的(てんけいてき)

てんけいてき
na-adjective
typical

Definitions

1. typical, representative
Exhibiting the essential characteristics of a particular type or category.

Examples

これは典型的(てんけいてき)な日本(にほん)の家(いえ)です。
This is a typical Japanese house.
典型的(てんけいてき)な例(れい)を挙(あ)げましょう。
Let me give a typical example.
彼(かれ)は典型的(てんけいてき)なサラリーマンだ。
He's a typical office worker.

Notes

典型(てんけい) means 'model' or 'type.' Used to describe something that represents the standard or norm for its category. Can have neutral or slightly negative connotation (stereotypical).
