点(てん)

てん
counter
point, score

Definitions

1. point, score
Counter for points or scores.
2. item (counter)
Counter for items or articles.

Examples

100点(てん)を取(と)った。
I got 100 points.
3点(てん)購入(こうにゅう)した。
I purchased three items.

Notes

Used for test scores, game points, and items purchased. Also means "point" or "aspect" as a noun.
