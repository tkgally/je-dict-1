艶(つや)

つや
noun
gloss, luster, sheen

Definitions

1. gloss, luster, sheen
A smooth, shiny appearance on a surface.
2. charm, allure
An attractive, alluring quality, especially in appearance.

Examples

髪(かみ)に艶(つや)がある。
Her hair has a nice sheen.
この漆器(しっき)は艶(つや)が美(うつく)しい。
This lacquerware has a beautiful luster.
彼女(かのじょ)の肌(はだ)は艶(つや)があって若(わか)く見(み)える。
Her skin has a glow that makes her look young.

Notes

艶(つや) describes a glossy, lustrous quality, often used for hair, skin, or polished surfaces.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 艶(つや)がある (to have luster/sheen)
  • 艶(つや)がない (to lack luster, look dull)
  • 艶(つや)を出(だ)す (to bring out the shine)
  • 艶(つや)やか (glossy, lustrous) [na-adjective]
  • 艶(つや)消(け)し (matte finish)

USAGE NOTES:
Often used to describe healthy-looking hair and skin, suggesting vitality and good health.
