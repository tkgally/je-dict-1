釣(つ)り

つり
noun
fishing

Definitions

1. fishing, angling
The activity of catching fish, typically with a rod and line.

Examples

週末(しゅうまつ)は釣(つ)りに行(い)く予定(よてい)です。
I plan to go fishing this weekend.
父(ちち)は釣(つ)りが趣味(しゅみ)だ。
My father's hobby is fishing.
海(うみ)釣(づ)りと川(かわ)釣(づ)りではどちらが好(す)きですか。
Which do you prefer, sea fishing or river fishing?
Note rendaku: つり → づり in compounds

Notes

KANJI: Can also be written as 釣り(つり) with okurigana.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 海(うみ)釣(づ)り (sea fishing)
  • 川(かわ)釣(づ)り (river fishing)
  • 釣(つ)り竿(ざお) (fishing rod)
  • 釣(つ)り針(ばり) (fishhook)
  • 釣(つ)り糸(いと) (fishing line)
  • 釣(つ)り船(ぶね) (fishing boat)
  • 釣(つ)り人(びと) (angler)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 釣(つ)りに行(い)く (to go fishing)
  • 釣(つ)りをする (to fish, to go fishing)

NOTE: Not to be confused with お釣(つ)り (change from payment), which is a different word.
