机(つくえ)

つくえ
noun
desk

Definitions

1. desk
A piece of furniture with a flat surface for writing, reading, or working. Common in offices, schools, and homes.

Examples

机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に本(ほん)があります。
There's a book on the desk.
机(つくえ)で勉強(べんきょう)します。
I study at my desk.
この机(つくえ)は大(おお)きいですね。
This desk is big, isn't it?

Notes

Different from テーブル (table) which is typically for dining or general use. 机(つくえ) is specifically for work or study. Counter: 台(だい). Common compound: 勉強(べんきょう)机(づくえ) (study desk).
