冷(つめ)たい

つめたい
adjective (i-adjective)
cold (to touch)

Definitions

1. cold (to the touch)
Describes cold objects, drinks, or things you can touch. Different from 寒い (samui), which describes cold weather.
2. cold-hearted, unfriendly
Figuratively describes a person who is cold, unfriendly, or indifferent.

Examples

冷(つめ)たいお水(みず)をください。
Please give me cold water.
この床(ゆか)は冷(つめ)たい。
This floor is cold.
彼(かれ)は冷(つめ)たい人(ひと)です。
He is a cold person.
Figurative meaning

Notes

冷たい describes cold things you can touch (drinks, objects, hands) and cold-hearted people. Use 寒い (samui) for cold weather. Conjugation: 冷たくない (negative), 冷たかった (past).
