遂(つい)に

ついに
adverb
finally, at last

Definitions

1. finally, at last
After a long time or effort.
2. in the end, never
Used with negatives for 'never in the end'.

Examples

ついに完成(かんせい)した。
It was finally completed.
ついに会(あ)えなかった。
In the end, we never met.

Notes

ついに emphasizes the long wait.

POSITIVE USE:

  • ついに + positive: finally achieved

NEGATIVE USE:

  • ついに + negative: never in the end

SIMILAR:

  • とうとう: finally (interchangeable)
  • やっと: finally (emphasizes relief)
