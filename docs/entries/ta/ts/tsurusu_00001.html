吊(つ)るす

つるす
godan verb
to hang; to suspend; to dangle

Definitions

1. to hang; to suspend
To attach something from above so that it hangs down freely.
2. to dangle; to let hang
To hold or attach something so that it swings or hangs loosely.

Examples

天井(てんじょう)からランプを吊(つ)るした。
I hung a lamp from the ceiling.
木(き)の枝(えだ)にブランコを吊(つ)るす。
To hang a swing from a tree branch.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を物干(ものほ)し竿(ざお)に吊(つ)るした。
I hung the laundry on the drying pole.

Notes

吊(つ)るす is transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive form is 吊(つ)る, but 吊(つ)るす is more commonly used for deliberate hanging actions.

ASPECT ({ている}):
吊(つ)るしている means something is currently hanging (resulting state) or the action of hanging is in progress.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 天井(てんじょう)から吊(つ)るす (to hang from the ceiling)
  • 首(くび)から吊(つ)るす (to hang from one's neck)
  • 木(き)に吊(つ)るす (to hang from/on a tree)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を吊(つ)るす: The object being hung
  • ～から吊(つ)るす: From where it's hung
  • ～に吊(つ)るす: On/to what it's attached

Also written as 釣(つ)るす in some contexts, but 吊(つ)るす is standard for "hanging."
