繋(つな)がる

つながる
verb (godan)
to be connected, to lead to

Definitions

1. to be connected, to be linked
To be joined together.
2. to lead to, to result in
To have a connection that leads somewhere.

Examples

電話(でんわ)が繋(つな)がらない。
The phone won't connect.
努力(どりょく)が成功(せいこう)に繋(つな)がる。
Effort leads to success.
インターネットに繋(つな)がった。
Connected to the internet.

Notes

繋(つな)がる means to be connected.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 繋(つな)がる (intransitive): to be connected
  • 繋(つな)ぐ (transitive): to connect

PATTERN:

  • Xに繋(つな)がる: to be connected to X
  • Xが繋(つな)がる: X connects
