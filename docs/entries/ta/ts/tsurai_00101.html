辛(つら)い

つらい
adjective (i-adjective)
hard, painful, difficult

Definitions

1. hard, painful, difficult to bear
Describing something emotionally or physically difficult to endure. Expresses suffering or hardship.

Examples

仕事(しごと)が辛(つら)いです。
Work is hard/painful.
Expressing that work is difficult to endure
別(わか)れるのは辛(つら)かった。
Parting was painful.
辛(つら)いことがあったら、話(はな)してください。
If something difficult happens, please talk to me.

Notes

辛い conjugates as an i-adjective: 辛くない (negative), 辛くて (te-form), 辛かった (past).

Note: The kanji 辛い can also be read からい (meaning spicy). Context usually makes the meaning clear. 辛い (つらい) expresses emotional/physical hardship, while 辛い (からい) describes spicy taste.
