つまらない

つまらない
i-adjective
boring, dull

Definitions

1. boring, dull
Not interesting or engaging.
2. trivial, worthless
Of little importance or value.

Examples

この映画(えいが)はつまらないです。
This movie is boring.
つまらない仕事(しごと)ですね。
This is tedious work.
つまらないものですが、どうぞ。
It's nothing special, but please accept it.
Humble expression when giving gifts

Notes

The humble use when giving gifts is a cultural set phrase, not literally saying the gift is boring. Antonym: 面白(おもしろ)い.
