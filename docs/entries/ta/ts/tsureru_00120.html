連(つ)れる

つれる
verb (ichidan)
to take, to bring (a person)

Definitions

1. to take along, to bring (a person)
To go somewhere accompanied by a person (or pet). Used when you are guiding or responsible for the person coming with you.

Examples

子供(こども)を公園(こうえん)に連(つ)れて行(い)く。
Take the children to the park.
連(つ)れて行(い)く = take (someone) somewhere
犬(いぬ)を連(つ)れて散歩(さんぽ)する。
Walk the dog (lit: walk taking the dog along).
友達(ともだち)を連(つ)れて来(き)てもいいですか。
May I bring a friend?
連(つ)れて来(く)る = bring (someone)

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを連(つ)れる (take/bring X along).

ASPECT: ている indicates state. 連(つ)れている = 'has (someone) with' or 'is accompanied by.'

KEY COMPOUND PATTERNS:

  • 連(つ)れて行(い)く (take someone somewhere—away from here)
  • 連(つ)れて来(く)る (bring someone—toward here)
  • 連(つ)れて帰(かえ)る (take/bring someone home)
  • 連(つ)れ出(だ)す (take out, lead out)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を連(つ)れる (take children)
  • 犬(いぬ)を連(つ)れる (take a dog)
  • お連(つ)れ様(さま) (your companion—polite)

CONTRAST WITH 持(も)って行(い)く:

  • 連(つ)れて行(い)く: take a person/animal
  • 持(も)って行(い)く: take an object
