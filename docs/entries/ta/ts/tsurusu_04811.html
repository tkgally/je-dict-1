吊(つ)るす

つるす
godan verb
to hang, to suspend

Definitions

1. to hang, to suspend
To attach something from above so that it hangs down freely.

Examples

天井(てんじょう)からランプを吊(つ)るす。
I hang a lamp from the ceiling.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を吊(つ)るして乾(かわ)かす。
I hang the laundry to dry.
クリスマスの飾(かざ)りを吊(つ)るした。
I hung Christmas decorations.

Notes

吊(つ)るす is a transitive verb meaning to hang something from above.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 吊(つ)る (intransitive, less common)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 天井(てんじょう)から吊(つ)るす: to hang from the ceiling
  • ロープで吊(つ)るす: to hang with a rope
  • 吊(つ)るし上(あ)げる: to string up, to grill someone (figurative)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 掛(か)ける: to hang (on a hook, wall)
  • ぶら下(さ)げる: to hang down, to dangle
