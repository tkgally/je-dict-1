継(つ)ぐ

つぐ
godan verb, transitive
to succeed, to inherit, to follow

Definitions

1. to succeed, to inherit
To take over a position, business, or tradition.
2. to follow, to continue
To come after something.

Examples

家業(かぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ。
To take over the family business.
父(ちち)の後(あと)を継(つ)ぐ。
To succeed one's father.

Notes

継(つ)ぐ implies succession or continuation.

DISTINCTION:

  • 継(つ)ぐ: to succeed, inherit
  • 注(つ)ぐ: to pour
  • 接(つ)ぐ: to join, graft
