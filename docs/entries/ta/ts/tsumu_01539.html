摘(つ)む

つむ
godan verb
to pluck, to pick, to trim

Definitions

1. to pluck, to pick
To remove something by pinching with fingers.
2. to trim, to nip
To cut off the tips of plants.

Examples

花(はな)を摘む。
To pick flowers.

Notes

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 摘(つ)む: to pluck, pick
  • 積(つ)む: to pile up
  • 詰(つ)む: to be stuck

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 芽(め)を摘む: to nip in the bud
  • 茶(ちゃ)を摘む: to pick tea leaves
