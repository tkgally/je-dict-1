土地(とち)

とち
noun
land, property, area

Definitions

1. land, property
A piece of ground owned by someone.
2. area, region
A particular geographical area.

Examples

土地(とち)を買(か)う。
To buy land.
この土地(とち)は高(たか)い。
Land here is expensive.

Notes

土地(とち) refers to land and property.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 土地勘(とちかん): familiarity with an area
  • 土地柄(とちがら): local characteristics

RELATED:

  • 土(つち): soil, earth
  • 地面(じめん): ground
  • 不動産(ふどうさん): real estate
