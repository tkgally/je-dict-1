退院(たいいん)

たいいん
noun (verbal)
leaving the hospital, discharge

Definitions

1. leaving the hospital, discharge
Being released from a hospital after treatment.

Examples

来週(らいしゅう)退院(たいいん)できます。
You can leave the hospital next week.
退院(たいいん)おめでとう。
Congratulations on being discharged.

Notes

退院(たいいん) means leaving the hospital.

OPPOSITE:

  • 入院(にゅういん): hospitalization

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 退院(たいいん)する: to leave the hospital
  • 退院(たいいん)日(び): discharge date
