出会(であ)い

であい
noun
encounter, meeting

Definitions

1. encounter, meeting
The act or occasion of meeting someone, especially for the first time or by chance.

Examples

素敵(すてき)な出会(であ)いがあった。
I had a wonderful encounter.
人生(じんせい)を変(か)える出会(であ)いだった。
It was an encounter that changed my life.
彼女(かのじょ)との出会(であ)いは偶然(ぐうぜん)だった。
My meeting with her was by chance.

Notes

FORMATION: Nominalized form of 出会(であ)う (to meet, to encounter).

KANJI VARIATIONS:

  • 出会(であ)い - most common
  • 出合(であ)い - alternative spelling

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 出会(であ)いがある (to have an encounter)
  • 出会(であ)いを求(もと)める (to seek encounters)
  • 運命(うんめい)の出会(であ)い (fateful encounter)
  • 一期一会(いちごいちえ) (once-in-a-lifetime encounter - idiom)

USAGE NOTES: Often used in romantic or meaningful contexts, implying an encounter that has significance.

RELATED:

  • 出会(であ)う (verb: to meet, to encounter)
  • 出会(であ)い系(けい) (dating site/app - modern usage)
