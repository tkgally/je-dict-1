伝染(でんせん)

でんせん
noun
contagion, infection

Definitions

1. contagion, infection, transmission
The spreading of disease from one person or organism to another.
2. spreading (of emotions, behaviors)
The figurative spreading of feelings or behaviors from one person to others.

Examples

伝染(でんせん)病(びょう)に気(き)をつける。
I am careful about infectious diseases.
あくびが伝染(でんせん)した。
The yawn was contagious.
笑(わら)いは伝染(でんせん)する。
Laughter is contagious.

Notes

伝染(でんせん) describes the spread of disease or behaviors.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 伝染(でんせん)する: to be transmitted, to spread
  • 伝染(でんせん)病(びょう): infectious disease
  • 伝染(でんせん)力(りょく): infectivity
  • 空気(くうき)伝染(でんせん): airborne transmission

RELATED WORDS:

  • 感染(かんせん): infection
  • 蔓延(まんえん): spread, rampancy
  • 流行(りゅうこう): epidemic, trend
