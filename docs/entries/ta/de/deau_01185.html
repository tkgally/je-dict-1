出会(であ)う

であう
godan verb, intransitive
to meet, to encounter, to come across

Definitions

1. to meet, to encounter
To meet someone, often by chance or for the first time.
2. to come across
To encounter something unexpected.

Examples

彼(かれ)とは大学(だいがく)で出会(であ)った。
I met him at university.
素晴(すば)らしい本(ほん)に出会(であ)った。
I came across a wonderful book.
運命(うんめい)の出会(であ)い。
A fateful encounter.

Notes

出会(であ)う often implies a meaningful or first-time meeting.

NOUN FORM:

  • 出会(であ)い: encounter, meeting

DISTINCTION:

  • 出会(であ)う: to meet (chance/meaningful)
  • 会(あ)う: to meet (general)
