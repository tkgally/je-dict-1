畳(たた)む

たたむ
verb (godan)
to fold, to close (business)

Definitions

1. to fold, to fold up
To bend something flat.
2. to close, to shut down (business)
To end a business or activity.

Examples

傘(かさ)を畳(たた)む。
To fold an umbrella.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を畳(たた)む。
To fold laundry.
店(みせ)を畳(たた)む。
To close down a shop.

Notes

畳(たた)む means to fold or close.

COMMON USES:

1. Folding:

  • 傘(かさ)を畳(たた)む: to fold an umbrella
  • 布団(ふとん)を畳(たた)む: to fold a futon

2. Closing business:

  • 店(みせ)を畳(たた)む: to close a shop
  • 会社(かいしゃ)を畳(たた)む: to shut down a company
