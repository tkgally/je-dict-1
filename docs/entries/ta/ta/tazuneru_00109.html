尋(たず)ねる

たずねる
verb (ichidan)
to ask, to inquire

Definitions

1. to ask, to inquire
To ask a question or seek information from someone. More formal than 聞(き)く.

Examples

道(みち)を尋(たず)ねた。
I asked for directions.
先生(せんせい)に質問(しつもん)を尋(たず)ねる。
Ask the teacher a question.
に marks the person asked
名前(なまえ)を尋(たず)ねてもいいですか。
May I ask your name?

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xに Yを尋(たず)ねる (ask X about Y).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing or completed action. 尋(たず)ねている = 'is asking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)を尋(たず)ねる (ask for directions)
  • 名前(なまえ)を尋(たず)ねる (ask someone's name)
  • 意見(いけん)を尋(たず)ねる (ask for an opinion)

HOMOPHONE: Don't confuse with 訪(たず)ねる (to visit), which has the same reading but different kanji and meaning.

  • 尋(たず)ねる (尋): to ask, inquire
  • 訪(たず)ねる (訪): to visit

CONTRAST WITH 聞(き)く:

  • 聞(き)く: general 'ask' or 'listen' (neutral)
  • 尋(たず)ねる: 'inquire' (slightly more formal)

REGISTER: Slightly formal. 聞(き)く is more casual.
