田(た)んぼ

たんぼ
noun
rice paddy; rice field

Definitions

1. rice paddy; rice field
A flooded field used for growing rice.

Examples

田(た)んぼに水(みず)を入(い)れる。
To fill the rice paddy with water.
Agricultural activity
夏(なつ)の田(た)んぼは緑(みどり)が美(うつく)しい。
Rice paddies in summer are beautifully green.
Describing scenery
子供(こども)たちが田(た)んぼで虫(むし)を捕(と)っている。
The children are catching bugs in the rice paddy.
Rural childhood activity

Notes

田(た)んぼ is a colloquial form combining 田(た) (rice field) with んぼ (a suffix). The formal term is 水田(すいでん).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 田(た)んぼ / 田(た) - rice paddy (flooded)
  • 畑(はたけ) / 畑(はた) - dry field (for vegetables, etc.)
  • 水田(すいでん) - rice paddy (formal)
  • 田園(でんえん) - rural area; countryside

SEASONAL CYCLE:
Rice paddies change appearance throughout the year: flooded in spring (田植(たう)え), lush green in summer, golden in autumn (稲刈(いねか)り), and dry in winter.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 田(た)んぼの畦道(あぜみち) (paths between paddies)
  • 田(た)んぼアート (rice paddy art - designs made by planting different rice varieties)

Related Words

Contrast: 畑(はたけ) (dry field (not flooded))
