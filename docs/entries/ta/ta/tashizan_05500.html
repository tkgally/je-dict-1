足(た)し算(ざん)

たしざん
noun
addition

Definitions

1. addition (mathematical operation)
The mathematical operation of adding numbers together.

Examples

足(た)し算(ざん)の練習(れんしゅう)をする。
To practice addition.
この問題(もんだい)は足(た)し算(ざん)で解(と)ける。
This problem can be solved with addition.
子供(こども)に足(た)し算(ざん)を教(おし)える。
To teach addition to children.

Notes

足(た)し算(ざん) is composed of 足(た)す (to add) + 算(さん) (calculation).

FOUR BASIC OPERATIONS:

  • 足(た)し算(ざん): addition (+)
  • 引(ひ)き算(ざん): subtraction (-)
  • 掛(か)け算(ざん): multiplication (×)
  • 割(わ)り算(ざん): division (÷)

Related Words

Related: 引(ひ)き算(ざん) (subtraction)
