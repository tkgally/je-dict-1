対(たい)する

たいする
verb (suru)
to face, to confront, regarding

Definitions

1. to face, to confront
To be opposite to or deal with directly.
2. regarding, toward
In relation to something or someone.

Examples

問題(もんだい)に対(たい)する。
To face a problem.
彼(かれ)に対(たい)する態度(たいど)。
Attitude toward him.

Notes

対(たい)する is often used with に.

PATTERN:

  • 〜に対(たい)する: toward ~, regarding ~
  • 〜に対(たい)して: toward ~, in contrast to ~

USAGE:

  • Formal/written style
  • Common in business and academic contexts
