退勤(たいきん)

たいきん
noun/suru-verb
leaving work

Definitions

1. leaving work; clocking out
leaving work; clocking out

Examples

5時(じ)に退勤(たいきん)した。
I left work at 5 o'clock.
退勤(たいきん)後(ご)、ジムに行(い)く。
I go to the gym after work.
退勤(たいきん)時間(じかん)を打刻(だこく)するのを忘(わす)れた。
I forgot to clock out.

Notes

退勤(たいきん) means leaving work for the day. As a suru-verb, it means "to leave work", "to clock out".

Common compounds:

  • 退勤(たいきん)時間(じかん) (time of leaving work)
  • 退勤(たいきん)後(ご) (after work)

Antonym: 出勤(しゅっきん) (going to work)

Related expressions:

  • タイムカードを押(お)す (punch the time card)
  • 打刻(だこく)する (to clock in/out)
  • 定時(ていじ)で上(あ)がる (leave at the regular time)

Related Words

Antonym: 出勤(しゅっきん) (going to work)
