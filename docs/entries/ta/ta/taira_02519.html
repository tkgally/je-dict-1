平(たい)ら

たいら
na-adjective, noun
flat, level, smooth

Definitions

1. flat, level, even
Having a surface with no curves, bumps, or inclines; horizontal.
2. calm, peaceful
Figuratively, being in a state of tranquility or stability (less common).

Examples

平(たい)らな道(みち)を歩(ある)く。
To walk on a flat road.
Sense 1
この土地(とち)は平(たい)らだ。
This land is flat.
テーブルの表面(ひょうめん)を平(たい)らにする。
To make the table surface flat.

Notes

GRAMMAR:

  • 平(たい)らな + noun: flat (noun)
  • 平(たい)らに + verb: flatly
  • 平(たい)らにする: to flatten, to level

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 平(たい)ら: Flat, level (general)
  • 平坦(へいたん): Flat, even (more formal)
  • 滑(なめ)らか: Smooth (texture)

NOTE: 平ら is also a common family name (Taira), associated with the famous Taira clan (平氏(へいし)) from Japanese history.

Related Words

Related: 平坦(へいたん) (flat, level)
