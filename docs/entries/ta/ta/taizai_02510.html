滞在(たいざい)

たいざい
noun, suru-verb
stay, sojourn

Definitions

1. stay, sojourn, residence
Remaining temporarily in a place away from one's home, especially for travel, business, or study purposes.

Examples

東京(とうきょう)に1週間(しゅうかん)滞在(たいざい)する予定(よてい)です。
I plan to stay in Tokyo for one week.
滞在期間(たいざいきかん)を延長(えんちょう)したい。
I want to extend my stay.
海外(かいがい)滞在中(たいざいちゅう)に病気(びょうき)になった。
I got sick while staying abroad.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 滞在(たいざい)する (to stay, to reside)
  • 滞在期間(たいざいきかん) (length of stay)
  • 滞在中(たいざいちゅう) (during one's stay)
  • 滞在先(たいざいさき) (place of stay, accommodation)
  • 滞在費(たいざいひ) (cost of stay)

VISA/IMMIGRATION TERMS:

  • 滞在許可(たいざいきょか) (permission to stay)
  • 滞在ビザ(たいざいびざ) (residence visa)
  • 長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい) (long-term stay)
  • 短期滞在(たんきたいざい) (short-term stay)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 滞在(たいざい): Temporary stay (travel, business)
  • 在住(ざいじゅう): Residence, living (more permanent)

Related Words

Related: 泊(と)まる (to stay overnight)
