溜息(ためいき)

ためいき
noun
sigh

Definitions

1. sigh
A long, deep breath expressing sadness, tiredness, relief, or longing.

Examples

疲(つか)れて溜息(ためいき)をついた。
I sighed from exhaustion.
彼女(かのじょ)の美(うつく)しさに溜息(ためいき)が出(で)た。
Her beauty made me sigh.
深(ふか)い溜息(ためいき)をつく。
I let out a deep sigh.

Notes

溜息(ためいき) is often written in hiragana as ため息(いき).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 溜息(ためいき)をつく: to sigh
  • 溜息(ためいき)が出(で)る: to sigh (involuntarily)
  • 深(ふか)い溜息(ためいき): a deep sigh
  • 溜息(ためいき)まじりに: with a sigh

USAGE NOTE:

Signs can express various emotions:

  • Tiredness, exhaustion
  • Sadness, disappointment
  • Relief
  • Admiration, being impressed
