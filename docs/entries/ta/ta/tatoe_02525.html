たとえ

たとえ
adverb
even if, even though

Definitions

1. even if, even though, no matter
Used to introduce a hypothetical or concessive condition, emphasizing that the outcome won't change regardless of the condition.

Examples

たとえ雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っても、行(い)きます。
Even if it rains, I will go.
たとえ〜ても pattern
たとえ失敗(しっぱい)しても、諦(あきら)めない。
Even if I fail, I won't give up.
たとえ時間(じかん)がかかっても、やり遂(と)げる。
No matter how long it takes, I will accomplish it.

Notes

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • たとえ〜ても: Even if ~ (with te-form + も)
  • たとえ〜でも: Even if ~ (with noun/na-adjective + でも)
  • たとえ〜としても: Even assuming that ~

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • たとえ: Even if (emphasizes hypothetical)
  • もし: If (neutral conditional)
  • 仮に: Supposing, even if (more formal)

NOTE: たとえ is typically paired with 〜ても/〜でも to form the complete pattern. Using たとえ emphasizes the speaker's determination or that the condition doesn't matter.

Related Words

Contrast: もし (if (neutral))
