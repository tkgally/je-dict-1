たっぷり

たっぷり
adverb, na-adjective
plenty, full, ample

Definitions

1. plenty, full, ample, generously
Having or giving more than enough; abundant in quantity or amount.

Examples

時間(じかん)はたっぷりある。
There's plenty of time.
クリームをたっぷり塗(ぬ)った。
I applied cream generously.
Adverb use
たっぷり寝(ね)たので元気(げんき)だ。
I slept plenty, so I feel great.

Notes

USAGE:

  • たっぷり + verb: generously, plenty (adverb)
  • たっぷりの + noun: plenty of, ample (adjectival)
  • 〜がたっぷりある: there is plenty of ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • たっぷり食(た)べる (to eat plenty)
  • たっぷり寝(ね)る (to sleep plenty)
  • たっぷり使(つか)う (to use generously)
  • たっぷりの野菜(やさい) (plenty of vegetables)

NUANCE: Conveys a sense of satisfaction and abundance. Often used when describing generous portions of food or having enough time/resources.

Related Words

Related: 十分(じゅうぶん) (enough, sufficient)
