探究心(たんきゅうしん)

たんきゅうしん
noun
spirit of inquiry; intellectual curiosity

Definitions

1. spirit of inquiry; intellectual curiosity
The desire to deeply investigate and understand things; a curious and questioning mindset.

Examples

子供(こども)の探究心(たんきゅうしん)を育(そだ)てる。
To nurture children's spirit of inquiry.
探究心(たんきゅうしん)が強(つよ)い人(ひと)だ。
They are a person with strong intellectual curiosity.
科学者(かがくしゃ)には探究心(たんきゅうしん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Scientists need a spirit of inquiry.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 探究心(たんきゅうしん)を育(そだ)てる: to foster a spirit of inquiry
  • 探究心(たんきゅうしん)が旺盛(おうせい): to have a strong curiosity
  • 探究心(たんきゅうしん)に満(み)ちた: filled with curiosity

RELATED TERMS:

  • 好奇心(こうきしん): curiosity
  • 向上心(こうじょうしん): desire for self-improvement
  • 探求(たんきゅう): quest, search
