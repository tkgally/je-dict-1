ただ

ただ
adverb, noun, conjunction
free; just, only; but

Definitions

1. free (of charge)
Without cost or payment.
2. just, only, merely
Nothing more than; simply.
3. but, however
Introduces a contrasting or qualifying statement.

Examples

この水(みず)はただです。
This water is free.
ただ見(み)ているだけです。
I'm just watching.
行(い)きたい。ただ、時間(じかん)がない。
I want to go. But I don't have time.
Conjunction use

Notes

ただ has multiple meanings depending on context.

MEANINGS:
1. Free: ただでもらう(もらう) (get for free)
2. Just/only: ただの友達(ともだち) (just a friend)
3. But/however: ただ、～ (but ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ただで (for free)
  • ただの + noun (just a ~, mere ~)
  • ただし (however - formal)
  • ただ～だけ (just ~ only)

KANJI NOTE:
Can be written as 只(ただ) but hiragana is more common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無料(むりょう): free of charge (formal)
  • だけ: only (particle)
  • しかし: but, however (more formal)
