戦(たたか)い

たたかい
noun
battle, fight, struggle

Definitions

1. battle, fight, combat
Armed conflict or physical confrontation.
2. struggle, competition
A determined effort or contest.

Examples

戦(たたか)いに勝(か)つ。
To win a battle.
病気(びょうき)との戦(たたか)い。
A fight against illness.
選挙(せんきょ)戦(たたか)いが始(はじ)まった。
The election battle has begun.

Notes

戦(たたか)い is the noun form of 戦(たたか)う.

USES:

  • Military: battle, war
  • Sports: match, competition
  • Figurative: struggle, fight

RELATED:

  • 戦(たたか)う: to fight (verb)
  • 戦争(せんそう): war
  • 闘(たたか)い: fight (alternative kanji)
