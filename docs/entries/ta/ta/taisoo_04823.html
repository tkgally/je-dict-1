大層(たいそう)

たいそう
adverb
very, greatly, extremely

Definitions

1. very, greatly, extremely
To a high degree; used to emphasize the extent of something.

Examples

大層(たいそう)お世話(せわ)になりました。
Thank you very much for your help.
大層(たいそう)立派(りっぱ)な建物(たてもの)だ。
It's a very magnificent building.
大層(たいそう)なことを言(い)う。
You say exaggerated things.

Notes

大層(たいそう) is an adverb meaning "very" or "greatly," often with a slightly formal or old-fashioned tone.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 大層(たいそう)な: exaggerated, grand
  • 大層(たいそう)お世話(せわ)になる: to receive great help

USAGE NOTE:
Can have a nuance of exaggeration or being excessive.

HOMOPHONE:
Not to be confused with 体操(たいそう) (gymnastics).
