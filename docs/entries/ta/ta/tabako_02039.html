たばこ

たばこ
noun
tobacco, cigarette

Definitions

1. tobacco, cigarette
Dried tobacco leaves or a cigarette for smoking.

Examples

たばこを吸(す)う。
To smoke a cigarette.
ここではたばこを吸(す)わないでください。
Please don't smoke here.
たばこを止(や)めたい。
I want to quit smoking.

Notes

たばこ can be written as 煙草 or タバコ.

COMMON PHRASES:

  • たばこを吸(す)う (to smoke)
  • たばこを止(や)める (to quit smoking)
  • 禁煙(きんえん) (no smoking)

RELATED:

  • 喫煙(きつえん) (smoking)
  • 喫煙所(きつえんじょ) (smoking area)
  • 灰皿(はいざら) (ashtray)
