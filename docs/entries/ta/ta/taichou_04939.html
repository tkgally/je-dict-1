体調(たいちょう)

たいちょう
noun
physical condition; health

Definitions

1. physical condition; state of health
One's overall physical well-being or state of health at a given time.

Examples

今日(きょう)は体調(たいちょう)があまり良(よ)くない。
I'm not feeling very well today.
体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)して会社(かいしゃ)を休(やす)んだ。
I got sick and took the day off from work.
体調(たいちょう)管理(かんり)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please take care of your health.

Notes

体調(たいちょう) refers to one's general physical condition, especially health status.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体調(たいちょう)が良(い)い/悪(わる)い - to be in good/bad condition
  • 体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)す - to become unwell
  • 体調(たいちょう)管理(かんり) - health management
  • 体調(たいちょう)が戻(もど)る/回復(かいふく)する - to recover one's health
  • 体調(たいちょう)に気(き)をつける - to take care of one's health
  • 体調(たいちょう)不良(ふりょう) - poor health condition

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 体調(たいちょう)はいかがですか - How are you feeling?
  • 体調(たいちょう)に気(き)をつけて - Take care of yourself

体調(たいちょう) VS 健康(けんこう):
体調(たいちょう) refers to one's current physical state (temporary), while 健康(けんこう) refers to health in a more general, ongoing sense.
