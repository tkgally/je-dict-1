例(たと)えば

たとえば
adverb
for example

Definitions

1. for example, for instance
Used to introduce an example to illustrate a point.

Examples

日本(にほん)の料理(りょうり)、例(たと)えば寿司(すし)や天(てん)ぷらが好(す)きです。
I like Japanese food, for example sushi and tempura.
例(たと)えば、どんなものがありますか。
For example, what kind of things are there?
例(たと)えば彼(かれ)のような人(ひと)
Someone like him, for example

Notes

例(たと)えば is essential for giving examples. It can appear at the beginning of a sentence or after a noun phrase to introduce examples. The kanji 例(れい) means 'example,' so 例(たと)えば literally means 'if we take an example.'
