正(ただ)しい

ただしい
adjective (i-adjective)
correct, right

Definitions

1. correct, right, accurate
Free from error, conforming to fact or truth. Also means morally right or proper.

Examples

この答(こた)えは正(ただ)しいです。
This answer is correct.
正(ただ)しい読(よ)み方(かた)を教(おし)えてください。
Please teach me the correct way to read it.
正(ただ)しいことをしましょう。
Let's do the right thing.
Moral sense

Notes

Can mean both 'factually correct' and 'morally right'. Opposite is 間違(まちが)った (wrong/mistaken) for factual errors. Related noun: 正(ただ)しさ (correctness/righteousness).
