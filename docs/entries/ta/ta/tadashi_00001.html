但(ただ)し

ただし
conjunction
but; however; provided that

Definitions

1. but; however
Used to introduce a contrasting or limiting statement.
2. provided that; on condition that
Used to specify a condition or exception.

Examples

参加(さんか)は自由(じゆう)です。但(ただ)し、事前(じぜん)に申(もう)し込(こ)みが必要(ひつよう)です。
Participation is free. However, advance registration is required.
明日(あした)届(とど)けます。但(ただ)し、天候(てんこう)によっては遅(おく)れることがあります。
We will deliver tomorrow. However, there may be delays depending on the weather.
入場無料(にゅうじょうむりょう)。但(ただ)し、小学生以下(しょうがくせいいか)に限(かぎ)る。
Free admission. However, limited to elementary school students and younger.

Notes

但(ただ)し is a conjunction used to introduce exceptions, conditions, or contrasting information.

USAGE:
但(ただ)し typically appears at the beginning of a new sentence to add a condition or exception to the previous statement. It's common in written notices, rules, and formal announcements.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～です。但(ただ)し、～ (~ However, ~)
  • 但(ただ)し書(が)き (proviso, caveat - written exception)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 但(ただ)し vs. しかし: しかし is a general "however" for contrast; 但(ただ)し specifically introduces conditions or exceptions.
  • 但(ただ)し vs. ただ: ただ (just, only, but) is more casual; 但(ただ)し is more formal.
  • 但(ただ)し vs. もっとも: もっとも (although, but then) introduces a secondary consideration; 但(ただ)し states a clear condition.

Register: Formal. Very common in written documents, rules, contracts, and official notices. Less common in casual conversation.

Related Words

Related: しかし (however)
Related: ただ (just, only, but)
