体操(たいそう)

たいそう
noun
gymnastics, exercise

Definitions

1. gymnastics
Athletic exercises involving physical agility, coordination, and strength, often performed on apparatus.
2. physical exercise, calisthenics
Routine physical exercises done for fitness and health.

Examples

毎朝(まいあさ)ラジオ体操(たいそう)をする。
I do radio exercises every morning.
Radio calisthenics is a popular tradition in Japan
体操(たいそう)選手(せんしゅ)になりたい。
I want to become a gymnast.
準備(じゅんび)体操(たいそう)をしてから運動(うんどう)する。
I do warm-up exercises before working out.

Notes

体操(たいそう) covers both competitive gymnastics and general physical exercises.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 体操(たいそう)をする: to do exercises/gymnastics
  • ラジオ体操(たいそう): radio calisthenics
  • 器械(きかい)体操(たいそう): apparatus gymnastics
  • 準備(じゅんび)体操(たいそう): warm-up exercises
  • 新体操(しんたいそう): rhythmic gymnastics

CULTURAL NOTE:
ラジオ体操(たいそう) (radio calisthenics) is broadcast on NHK every morning and is a beloved Japanese tradition, especially during summer vacation for children.
