鷹(たか)

たか
noun
hawk, falcon

Definitions

1. hawk
A bird of prey with sharp talons.

Examples

鷹(たか)が空(そら)を舞(ま)っている。
A hawk is soaring in the sky.
鷹狩(たかが)りは伝統的(でんとうてき)な狩猟(しゅりょう)だ。
Falconry is a traditional form of hunting.

Notes

鷹(たか) refers to hawks.

CULTURE:

  • 鷹狩(たかが)り: falconry
  • Hawks were prized by samurai and nobility

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 能(のう)ある鷹(たか)は爪(つめ)を隠(かく)す: a skilled hawk hides its talons (talented people don't show off)
  • 鳶(とび)が鷹(たか)を生(う)む: a kite giving birth to a hawk (exceptional child from ordinary parents)
