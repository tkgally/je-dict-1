態度(たいど)

たいど
noun
attitude, manner

Definitions

1. attitude, manner, behavior
The way a person behaves or carries themselves, especially in their demeanor toward others; one's stance or position on a matter.

Examples

彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)は失礼(しつれい)だった。
His attitude was rude.
態度(たいど)を改(あらた)めてください。
Please change your attitude.
政府(せいふ)は明確(めいかく)な態度(たいど)を示(しめ)すべきだ。
The government should show a clear stance.
態度 = stance/position on an issue

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 態度(たいど)を取(と)る (to take an attitude/stance)
  • 態度(たいど)を示(しめ)す (to show one's attitude/position)
  • 態度(たいど)を変(か)える / 改(あらた)める (to change one's attitude)
  • 態度(たいど)が悪(わる)い (to have a bad attitude)
  • 態度(たいど)がでかい (to be arrogant, to have a big attitude)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • どっちつかずの態度(たいど) (ambivalent attitude, sitting on the fence)
  • 曖昧(あいまい)な態度(たいど) (vague/noncommittal attitude)
  • 強硬(きょうこう)な態度(たいど) (tough stance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 態度(たいど): General attitude, manner, stance
  • 姿勢(しせい): Posture; also attitude (more formal)

Related Words

Related: 姿勢(しせい) (posture, attitude)
