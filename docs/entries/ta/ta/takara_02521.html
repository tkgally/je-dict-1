宝(たから)

たから
noun
treasure

Definitions

1. treasure, valuable
Something of great value or worth, either literally (gold, jewels) or figuratively (precious memories, beloved person).

Examples

海賊(かいぞく)が宝(たから)を探(さが)している。
The pirates are searching for treasure.
子供(こども)は私(わたし)の宝(たから)です。
My children are my treasure.
Figurative use
宝(たから)の持(も)ち腐(ぐさ)れ。
A pearl cast before swine. (Having treasure but not using it)
Proverb

Notes

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 宝物(たからもの) (treasure, prized possession)
  • 宝箱(たからばこ) (treasure chest)
  • 宝探(たからさが)し (treasure hunt)
  • 宝くじ(たからくじ) (lottery)
  • 宝石(ほうせき) (jewel, gem)

PROVERB:

  • 宝(たから)の持(も)ち腐(ぐさ)れ: Having valuable things but failing to use them; letting talent or resources go to waste.

CULTURAL NOTE: The Seven Lucky Gods (七福神(しちふくじん)) are often depicted with their treasure ship (宝船(たからぶね)).

Related Words
