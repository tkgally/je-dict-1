足(た)る

たる
godan verb
to be sufficient, to be worthy of

Definitions

1. to be sufficient, to be enough
To meet the requirements or standards needed.
2. to be worthy of, to deserve
To have the qualities needed to be considered something.

Examples

信頼(しんらい)に足(た)る人物(じんぶつ)だ。
He is a person worthy of trust.
この量(りょう)で足(た)りますか？
Is this amount sufficient?
[Note: 足(た)りる (ichidan verb form) is more common in modern Japanese]
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)は批判(ひはん)に足(た)る。
His actions deserve criticism.

Notes

足(た)る is a classical/literary form meaning 'to be sufficient' or 'to be worthy of.' In modern Japanese, the ichidan verb 足(た)りる is more commonly used for the meaning 'to be enough.'

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xに足(た)る (worthy of X)

MODERN USAGE:
足(た)る is mainly used in formal/literary expressions, especially in the pattern 「～に足(た)る」(worthy of ~).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ～に足(た)る (worthy of ~, deserving of ~)
  • 信頼(しんらい)に足(た)る (worthy of trust)
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)に足(た)る (worthy of attention)
  • 言及(げんきゅう)に足(た)る (worth mentioning)

COMPARISON:

  • 足(た)る: Classical/literary, used in 「～に足(た)る」
  • 足(た)りる: Modern, used for 'to be enough'

Related Words

Related: 足(た)りる (modern form)
