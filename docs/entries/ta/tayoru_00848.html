頼(たよ)る

たよる
verb (godan)
to rely on, to depend on

Definitions

1. to rely on, to depend on
To count on someone or something for help.
2. to resort to
To turn to as a means.

Examples

親(おや)に頼(たよ)る。
To rely on one's parents.
辞書(じしょ)に頼(たよ)る。
To depend on a dictionary.
人(ひと)に頼(たよ)らないで自分(じぶん)でやる。
I'll do it myself without relying on others.

Notes

頼(たよ)る means to rely on.

PATTERN:

  • 〜に頼(たよ)る: to rely on ~

RELATED:

  • 頼(たよ)り: reliance, dependence
  • 頼(たよ)りになる: to be reliable
  • 頼(たの)む: to ask, request
