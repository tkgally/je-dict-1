鳥類(ちょうるい)

ちょうるい
noun
bird (class)

Definitions

1. birds, avian species
Warm-blooded vertebrate animals with feathers, beaks, and wings. The class Aves in biological classification.

Examples

鳥類(ちょうるい)は羽毛(うもう)を持(も)っています。
Birds have feathers.
ペンギンも鳥類(ちょうるい)の一種(いっしゅ)です。
Penguins are also a type of bird.
鳥類(ちょうるい)の多(おお)くは空(そら)を飛(と)ぶことができます。
Most birds can fly.

Notes

鳥類(ちょうるい) is the scientific classification term for birds.

鳥(ちょう) means "bird" and 類(るい) means "class/category."

Note: In everyday speech, 鳥(とり) is used. 鳥類(ちょうるい) is more formal/scientific.

Characteristics of birds:

  • 羽毛(うもう) (feathers)
  • 嘴(くちばし) (beak)
  • 卵(たまご)を産(う)む (lay eggs)

Related classification terms:

  • 哺乳類(ほにゅうるい) (mammals)
  • 爬虫類(はちゅうるい) (reptiles)
  • 魚類(ぎょるい) (fish)
