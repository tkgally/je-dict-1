珍味(ちんみ)

ちんみ
noun
delicacy; rare or unusual food

Definitions

1. delicacy; rare or unusual food
A food item that is rare, exotic, or considered a special treat, often served as a accompaniment to alcohol.

Examples

各地(かくち)の珍味(ちんみ)を集(あつ)めた。
I collected delicacies from various regions.
珍味(ちんみ)を肴(さかな)に酒(さけ)を飲(の)む。
To drink sake with delicacies as accompaniment.
この地方(ちほう)の珍味(ちんみ)は海(うみ)の幸(さち)だ。
The delicacies of this region are seafood.

Notes

EXAMPLES OF JAPANESE DELICACIES:

  • 塩辛(しおから): fermented seafood
  • 明太子(めんたいこ): spicy cod roe
  • {からすみ}: dried mullet roe
  • {うに}: sea urchin
  • {くさや}: strong-smelling dried fish

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 珍味(ちんみ)を味(あじ)わう: to savor a delicacy
  • 土産(みやげ)の珍味(ちんみ): local delicacy as souvenir
  • 酒(さけ)の珍味(ちんみ): delicacies with sake

USAGE CONTEXT:
Often associated with alcohol, especially Japanese sake or beer. Available at izakaya and specialty shops.
