ちょうど

ちょうど
adverb
exactly, just, precisely

Definitions

1. exactly, precisely
Neither more nor less than a specific amount or time.
2. just, right now
At this very moment.

Examples

ちょうど3時(じ)です。
It's exactly 3 o'clock.
ちょうど千円(せんえん)です。
It's exactly 1000 yen.
ちょうど今(いま)出(で)かけるところです。
I'm just about to leave now.

Notes

ちょうど indicates exactness.

COMMON USES:

  • ちょうど + time (exactly at ~)
  • ちょうど + amount (exactly ~)
  • ちょうど今(いま) (just now)
  • ちょうどいい (just right)

SIMILAR:

  • ぴったり (exactly, perfectly fitting)
  • まさに (exactly, precisely - more emphatic)
