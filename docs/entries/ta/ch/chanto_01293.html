ちゃんと

ちゃんと
adverb
properly, correctly

Definitions

1. properly, correctly
In the right way.
2. reliably, without fail
Dependably or surely.

Examples

ちゃんと食(た)べなさい。
Eat properly.
ちゃんと聞(き)いている？
Are you listening properly?

Notes

ちゃんと is colloquial.

SIMILAR:

  • きちんと: properly, neatly (slightly more formal)
  • しっかり: firmly, properly

USAGE:

  • Often used by parents to children
  • Implies doing things the right way
