茶(ちゃ)

ちゃ
noun
tea

Definitions

1. tea
A beverage made from tea leaves. In Japanese, 茶(ちゃ) typically refers to Japanese green tea by default, though it can refer to tea in general.
2. brown (color)
The color brown, named after the color of tea. Used in compound words like 茶色(ちゃいろ) (brown color).

Examples

茶(ちゃ)を一杯(いっぱい)飲(の)みませんか。
Would you like to have a cup of tea?
茶色(ちゃいろ)の鞄(かばん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want a brown bag.
茶色 = brown (color)
日本(にほん)では食事(しょくじ)の後(あと)にお茶(ちゃ)を飲(の)む習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
In Japan, there's a custom of drinking tea after meals.

Notes

USAGE: The polite form お茶(ちゃ) is more commonly used in everyday speech. Without any modifier, 茶(ちゃ)/お茶(ちゃ) typically means Japanese green tea (緑茶(りょくちゃ)).

TYPES OF TEA:

  • 緑茶(りょくちゃ) (green tea)
  • 紅茶(こうちゃ) (black tea)
  • 麦茶(むぎちゃ) (barley tea)
  • 抹茶(まっちゃ) (matcha, powdered green tea)
  • ウーロン茶(ちゃ) (oolong tea)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お茶(ちゃ)を入(い)れる (to make/brew tea)
  • お茶(ちゃ)を出(だ)す (to serve tea)
  • 茶道(さどう)/茶(ちゃ)の湯(ゆ) (tea ceremony)

IDIOM: 茶(ちゃ)を濁(にご)す means 'to evade the issue' or 'to gloss over something'.
