近(ちか)い

ちかい
adjective (i-adjective)
near, close

Definitions

1. near, close, nearby
Located at a short distance. Used for physical proximity or closeness in relationships or time.

Examples

学校(がっこう)は家(いえ)から近(ちか)いです。
The school is close to home.
この近(ちか)くにコンビニはありますか？
Is there a convenience store nearby?
この近く = around here
試験(しけん)が近(ちか)いです。
The exam is coming up soon.
Close in time

Notes

An i-adjective. The noun/adverb form is 近(ちか)く. Opposite of 遠(とお)い (tooi, far).

Common expressions:

  • 近(ちか)くに (nearby)
  • この近(ちか)く (around here).
