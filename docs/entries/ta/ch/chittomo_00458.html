ちっとも

ちっとも
adverb
not at all, not a bit

Definitions

1. not at all, not a bit, not in the least
Used with negative predicates to emphasize complete absence or negation. Expresses stronger negation than あまり.

Examples

ちっとも分(わ)からない。
I don't understand at all.
ちっとも変(か)わっていない。
It hasn't changed at all.
ちっとも面白(おもしろ)くない。
It's not interesting at all.

Notes

ちっとも is always used with negative predicates to emphasize negation.

GRAMMAR:

  • ちっとも + negative verb/adjective
  • Cannot be used with positive statements

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS (with negatives):

  • 全然|ぜんぜん}〜ない: not at all (most common)
  • ちっとも〜ない: not at all (emphatic)
  • 少しも|すこしも}〜ない: not even a little
  • 全く|まったく}〜ない: not at all (formal)
