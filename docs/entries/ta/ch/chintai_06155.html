賃貸(ちんたい)

ちんたい
noun
rental, lease

Definitions

1. rental, lease, renting
The act of renting property, or property that is available for rent. Often used as a prefix for rental-related terms.

Examples

賃貸(ちんたい)マンションを探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a rental apartment.
賃貸(ちんたい)と分譲(ぶんじょう)、どちらがいいですか。
Which is better, renting or buying?
賃貸(ちんたい)契約(けいやく)を結(むす)ぶ。
To sign a rental contract.

Notes

COMPOUND: 賃(ちん) (rent, fee) + 貸(たい) (lending)

COMMON COMPOUND TERMS:

  • 賃貸(ちんたい)マンション (rental apartment building)
  • 賃貸(ちんたい)物件(ぶっけん) (rental property)
  • 賃貸(ちんたい)契約(けいやく) (rental contract)
  • 賃貸(ちんたい)住宅(じゅうたく) (rental housing)
  • 賃貸(ちんたい)アパート (rental apartment)

CONTRAST:

  • 賃貸(ちんたい) = rental (temporary)
  • 分譲(ぶんじょう) = for sale (ownership)

VERB FORM: 賃貸(ちんたい)する means 'to rent out' (from landlord's perspective).

Related Words

Contrast: 分譲(ぶんじょう)
