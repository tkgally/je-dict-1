小(ちい)さい

ちいさい
adjective (i-adjective)
small, little

Definitions

1. small, little (in size)
Describes something of less than average size. The opposite of 大(おお)きい (big). Used for physical dimensions of objects, spaces, and living things.
2. quiet, soft (of sound/voice)
When describing voices or sounds, 小(ちい)さい means quiet or soft. 小(ちい)さい声(こえ) means a quiet/soft voice.

Examples

このカバンは小(ちい)さいです。
This bag is small.
小(ちい)さい子供(こども)がいます。
There is a small child. / I have a small child.
小(ちい)さい声(こえ)で話(はな)してください。
Please speak in a quiet voice.

Notes

小(ちい)さい is an i-adjective. Like 大(おお)きい, it has a special attributive form 小(ちい)さな that can only be used directly before nouns. Both 小(ちい)さい猫(ねこ) and 小(ちい)さな猫(ねこ) mean 'small cat.' Conjugation: 小(ちい)さくない (negative), 小(ちい)さかった (past).
