近く(ちかく)

ちかく
noun
nearby, vicinity

Definitions

1. nearby, vicinity, neighborhood
The area close to something or someone.
2. nearly, almost
Close to a number or amount.

Examples

駅(えき)の近(ちか)くに住(す)んでいます。
I live near the station.
100人(にん)近(ちか)くが参加(さんか)した。
Nearly 100 people participated.

Notes

As a noun, often used with の: 近く(ちかく)の店(みせ) (nearby shop). Also functions as an adverb meaning "nearly."
