直線(ちょくせん)

ちょくせん
noun
straight line

Definitions

1. straight line
A line that extends in both directions without curving.

Examples

定規(じょうぎ)で直線(ちょくせん)を引(ひ)く。
Draw a straight line with a ruler.
二(ふた)つの点(てん)を結(むす)ぶ直線(ちょくせん)は一(ひと)つしかない。
There is only one straight line connecting two points.
この道(みち)は直線(ちょくせん)で10キロ続(つづ)く。
This road continues straight for 10 kilometers.

Notes

直線(ちょくせん) refers to a straight line in geometry.

CONTRAST:

  • 直線(ちょくせん): straight line
  • 曲線(きょくせん): curved line

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 直線(ちょくせん)を引(ひ)く: to draw a straight line
  • 直線(ちょくせん)コース: straight course
  • 直線(ちょくせん)距離(きょり): straight-line distance
