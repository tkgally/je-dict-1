地平線(ちへいせん)

ちへいせん
noun
horizon

Definitions

1. horizon (on land)
The apparent line where the earth's surface and the sky seem to meet, especially as seen from flat land or plains.

Examples

地平線(ちへいせん)に太陽(たいよう)が沈(しず)んだ。
The sun set on the horizon.
草原(そうげん)では地平線(ちへいせん)がよく見(み)える。
You can see the horizon clearly on the grassland.
地平線(ちへいせん)の向(む)こうに何(なに)があるのだろう。
I wonder what lies beyond the horizon.

Notes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 地平線(ちへいせん): The horizon as seen on land (plains, deserts)
  • 水平線(すいへいせん): The horizon as seen at sea

Both words refer to the horizon, but 地平線(ちへいせん) is used for land (地(ち) = earth/ground) while 水平線(すいへいせん) is used for the sea (水(すい) = water).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 地平線(ちへいせん)が見(み)える (the horizon is visible)
  • 地平線(ちへいせん)に沈(しず)む (to set on the horizon)

Related Words

Contrast: 水平線(すいへいせん) (sea horizon)
