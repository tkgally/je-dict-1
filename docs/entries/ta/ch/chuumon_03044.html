注文(ちゅうもん)

ちゅうもん
noun, suru-verb
order, request

Definitions

1. order (for goods or services)
A request to purchase or receive something, especially in commercial contexts like restaurants or online shopping.
2. special request, demand
A specific requirement or condition requested.

Examples

コーヒーを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered coffee.
ご注文(ちゅうもん)はお決(き)まりですか。
Are you ready to order?
Common phrase used by restaurant staff
注文(ちゅうもん)した本(ほん)が届(とど)いた。
The book I ordered arrived.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 注文(ちゅうもん)する (to order)
  • 注文(ちゅうもん)を取(と)る (to take an order)
  • 注文(ちゅうもん)を受(う)ける (to receive an order)
  • ご注文(ちゅうもん)は? (What would you like to order? - polite)

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 注文(ちゅうもん)書(しょ) (order form)
  • 注文(ちゅうもん)住宅(じゅうたく) (custom-built house)
  • 特別(とくべつ)注文(ちゅうもん) (special order)

USAGE NOTE:
In restaurants, ご注文(ちゅうもん)はお決(き)まりですか or ご注文(ちゅうもん)は? is the standard way staff ask if you're ready to order.
