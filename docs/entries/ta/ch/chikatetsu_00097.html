地下鉄(ちかてつ)

ちかてつ
noun
subway

Definitions

1. subway, underground railway
A railway system that runs underground in a city.

Examples

地下鉄(ちかてつ)で会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)きます。
I go to work by subway.
地下鉄(ちかてつ)の駅(えき)はどこですか。
Where is the subway station?
地下鉄(ちかてつ)は便利(べんり)ですが、朝(あさ)は混(こ)みます。
The subway is convenient, but it's crowded in the morning.

Notes

地下鉄 combines 地下 (underground) and 鉄 (iron/railway). Major cities like Tokyo, Osaka, and Nagoya have extensive subway networks. メトロ is also used in Tokyo (東京メトロ).
