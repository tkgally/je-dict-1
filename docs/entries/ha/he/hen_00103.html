変(へん)

へん
na-adjective
strange, odd, weird

Definitions

1. strange, odd, weird
Unusual or unexpected in a way that causes surprise or unease.
2. wrong, unusual
Not normal or correct; something that seems off.

Examples

変(へん)な音(おと)がする。
There's a strange sound.
このスープは変(へん)な味(あじ)がする。
This soup tastes weird.
何(なに)か変(へん)ですか。
Is something wrong/strange?

Notes

変 is a na-adjective: 変な (before nouns), 変だ/です (predicate). Very commonly used to describe something that doesn't seem right or normal.

Note: The same kanji 変 also means 'change' in words like 変える (かえる - to change) and 変わる (かわる - to change).
