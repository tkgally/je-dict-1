蛇(へび)

へび
noun
snake, serpent

Definitions

1. snake, serpent
A legless reptile.

Examples

蛇(へび)が草(くさ)の中(なか)にいた。
There was a snake in the grass.
蛇(へび)は苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not good with snakes.

Notes

蛇(へび) means snake.

ZODIAC:
巳(み) is the sixth animal of the Chinese zodiac.

TYPES:

  • 毒蛇(どくへび): venomous snake
  • 青大将(あおだいしょう): Japanese rat snake

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 蛇足(だそく): superfluous addition
  • 蛇(へび)の道(みち)は蛇(へび): it takes one to know one
