凹(へこ)む

へこむ
godan verb
to be dented; to be indented; to feel down

Definitions

1. to be dented; to be indented
To have a surface pushed inward, creating a depression.
2. to feel down; to be dejected
To feel discouraged or depressed (colloquial).
3. to be defeated; to give in
To lose or yield to someone.

Examples

車(くるま)のドアが凹(へこ)んでしまった。
The car door got dented.
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて凹(へこ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
地面(じめん)が少(すこ)し凹(へこ)んでいる。
The ground is slightly indented.

Notes

凹(へこ)む is intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 凹(へこ)ます (to dent, to make someone feel down).

ASPECT ({ている}):
凹(へこ)んでいる indicates a resulting state of being dented, or an ongoing state of feeling down.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドアが凹(へこ)む (door gets dented)
  • 凹(へこ)んでいる (to be dented; to be feeling down)
  • ちょっと凹(へこ)んだ (I'm a bit down)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～が凹(へこ)む: What is dented

COLLOQUIAL USAGE:
In casual speech, 凹(へこ)む is commonly used to describe feeling disappointed or dejected. This usage is very common among younger speakers.

The kanji {凹} visually represents a depression or dent, matching the meaning.

Related Words

Pair verb: 凹(へこ)ます (transitive)
Antonym: 出(で)っ張(ぱ)る (to protrude)
