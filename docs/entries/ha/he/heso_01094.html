臍(へそ)

へそ
noun
navel, belly button

Definitions

1. navel, belly button
The scar on the abdomen from the umbilical cord.

Examples

臍(へそ)を出(だ)す。
To expose one's belly button.
臍(へそ)が出(で)ている服(ふく)。
Clothes that show the navel.

Notes

臍(へそ) means belly button.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 臍(へそ)を曲(ま)げる: to get sulky
  • 臍(へそ)で茶(ちゃ)を沸(わ)かす: to find something absurdly funny

RELATED:

  • 臍(へそ)の緒(お): umbilical cord
  • お腹(なか): belly, stomach
