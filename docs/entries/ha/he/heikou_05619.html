平行(へいこう)

へいこう
noun / na-adjective
parallel

Definitions

1. parallel
Lines or surfaces that maintain equal distance and never intersect.
2. simultaneous; concurrent
Doing multiple things at the same time.

Examples

この二(ふた)つの線(せん)は平行(へいこう)だ。
These two lines are parallel.
線路(せんろ)は平行(へいこう)に走(はし)っている。
The railroad tracks run parallel to each other.
仕事(しごと)と勉強(べんきょう)を平行(へいこう)して進(すす)める。
To pursue work and studies simultaneously.

Notes

平行(へいこう) has both geometric and figurative meanings.

GEOMETRIC SENSE:

  • Lines that never meet regardless of how far they extend
  • 平行線(へいこうせん): parallel lines

FIGURATIVE SENSE:

  • Doing things at the same time
  • 平行(へいこう)して: simultaneously

RELATED TERMS:

  • 垂直(すいちょく): perpendicular, vertical
  • 平行四辺形(へいこうしへんけい): parallelogram
