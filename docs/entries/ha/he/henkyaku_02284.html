返却(へんきゃく)

へんきゃく
noun
return, returning (something borrowed)

Definitions

1. return, returning, giving back
The act of returning something that was borrowed or rented to its owner.

Examples

図書館(としょかん)の本(ほん)を返却(へんきゃく)した。
I returned the library books.
返却(へんきゃく)期限(きげん)は来週(らいしゅう)の金曜日(きんようび)です。
The return deadline is next Friday.
レンタカーを返却(へんきゃく)する場所(ばしょ)はどこですか。
Where do I return the rental car?

Notes

COMPOUND: 返(へん) (return) + 却(きゃく) (reject, return)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 返却(へんきゃく)する (to return)
  • 返却(へんきゃく)期限(きげん) (return deadline)
  • 返却(へんきゃく)日(び) (return date)
  • 返却(へんきゃく)口(ぐち) (return slot/counter)
  • 本(ほん)の返却(へんきゃく) (book return)

CONTEXTS:

  • Library books
  • Rental items (cars, equipment)
  • Borrowed money (formal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 返(かえ)す (to return - verb, more casual)
  • 返品(へんぴん) (return of merchandise for refund)
