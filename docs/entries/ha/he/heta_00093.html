下手(へた)

へた
adjective (na-adjective), noun
unskillful, bad at

Definitions

1. unskillful, bad at
Describes someone who lacks skill or ability in something. The opposite of 上手(じょうず) (skillful). Can be self-deprecating when used about oneself.

Examples

私(わたし)は歌(うた)が下手(へた)です。
I'm bad at singing.
Self-deprecating usage
字(じ)が下手(へた)ですみません。
Sorry, my handwriting is poor.
下手(へた)でもいいから、挑戦(ちょうせん)しよう。
Even if I'm not good at it, let's give it a try.

Notes

The opposite of 上手(じょうず). Uses the same grammar pattern: ～が下手(へた)です (bad at ~). It's considered polite in Japanese to be modest about your own abilities, so saying 下手(へた)です about yourself is common and not necessarily negative. Avoid using it directly about others as it can be rude.
