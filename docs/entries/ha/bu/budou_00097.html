ぶどう

ぶどう
noun
grapes

Definitions

1. grapes
Small round fruits that grow in clusters on vines. Used fresh, dried (raisins), and for wine.

Examples

秋(あき)はぶどうがおいしいです。
Grapes are delicious in autumn.
ぶどうを一房(ひとふさ)買(か)いました。
I bought a bunch of grapes.
房 is the counter for bunches
ワインはぶどうから作(つく)ります。
Wine is made from grapes.

Notes

ぶどう can also be written in kanji as 葡萄. Japanese grapes tend to be larger and sweeter than Western varieties. Famous varieties include 巨峰 (きょほう) and マスカット. The counter 房 (ふさ) is used for bunches.
