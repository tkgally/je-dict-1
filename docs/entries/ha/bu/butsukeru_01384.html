ぶつける

ぶつける
ichidan verb
to hit, to strike against

Definitions

1. to hit, to knock
To strike something against another thing.
2. to direct at, to take out on
To express feelings toward someone.

Examples

頭(あたま)をぶつけた。
I hit my head.
怒(いか)りをぶつける。
To vent one's anger.

Notes

ぶつける is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • ぶつける (transitive): to hit
  • ぶつかる (intransitive): to collide

FIGURATIVE USES:

  • 気持(きも)ちをぶつける: to express feelings
  • 不満(ふまん)をぶつける: to complain to
