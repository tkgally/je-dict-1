物価(ぶっか)

ぶっか
noun
prices (commodity)

Definitions

1. prices, commodity prices
The general level of prices of goods and services in an economy.

Examples

物価(ぶっか)が上(あ)がっている。
Prices are rising.
日本(にほん)は物価(ぶっか)が高(たか)い。
Japan has high prices.
物価(ぶっか)上昇(じょうしょう)が家計(かけい)を圧迫(あっぱく)している。
Rising prices are putting pressure on household budgets.
物価上昇 = price increase, inflation

Notes

USAGE: 物価(ぶっか) refers to the general price level of goods and services, often discussed in economic contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 物価(ぶっか)が上(あ)がる/下(さ)がる (prices rise/fall)
  • 物価(ぶっか)が高(たか)い/安(やす)い (prices are high/low)
  • 物価(ぶっか)上昇(じょうしょう) (price increase)
  • 物価(ぶっか)指数(しすう) (price index)
  • 物価(ぶっか)安定(あんてい) (price stability)
