ブレーキ

ぶれーき
noun
brake

Definitions

1. brake
A device for slowing or stopping a vehicle or machine.
2. brake, restraint
Something that slows down or stops progress or action (figurative).

Examples

ブレーキを踏(ふ)む。
I step on the brake.
ブレーキが効(き)かなくなった。
The brakes stopped working.
経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)にブレーキがかかった。
Economic growth has been slowed down.
Figurative usage

Notes

ブレーキ is essential vocabulary for driving and transportation.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ブレーキを踏(ふ)む: to step on the brake
  • ブレーキをかける: to apply the brake, to put the brakes on
  • ブレーキが効(き)く: the brake works
  • 急(きゅう)ブレーキ: sudden braking
  • サイドブレーキ: parking brake, handbrake

USAGE NOTE:
ブレーキをかける is often used figuratively to mean "to slow something down" or "to put a stop to something."
