卑怯(ひきょう)

ひきょう
na-adjective, noun
cowardly, unfair

Definitions

1. cowardly, unfair, mean, sneaky
Lacking courage or acting in a dishonest, underhanded way that takes unfair advantage.

Examples

後(うし)ろから襲(おそ)うなんて卑怯(ひきょう)だ。
Attacking from behind is cowardly.
卑怯(ひきょう)な手(て)を使(つか)うな。
Don't use underhanded tactics.
逃(に)げるのは卑怯(ひきょう)じゃない。
Running away isn't cowardly.

Notes

USAGE: 卑怯(ひきょう) carries a strong moral judgment, implying not just cowardice but also unfairness and lack of honor.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 卑怯(ひきょう)な + noun (cowardly/unfair ~)
  • 卑怯(ひきょう)だ (is cowardly)
  • 卑怯(ひきょう)な手(て)/やり方(かた) (underhanded tactics)
  • 卑怯者(ひきょうもの) (coward)

NUANCE: 卑怯(ひきょう) is stronger than 臆病(おくびょう) (timid). 臆病(おくびょう) simply means being fearful, while 卑怯(ひきょう) implies moral failing - being unfair, sneaky, or dishonorable.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 臆病(おくびょう) - timid, fearful (no moral judgment)
  • 狡(ずる)い - sly, cunning
  • 意気地(いくじ)なし - spineless
