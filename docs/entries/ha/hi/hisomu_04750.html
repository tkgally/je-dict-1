潜(ひそ)む

ひそむ
verb (godan)
to lurk, to hide

Definitions

1. to lurk, to hide, to lie concealed
To remain hidden or concealed, waiting or watching.
2. to be latent, to be hidden (within)
For something (often danger or potential) to exist in a hidden or dormant state.

Examples

猫(ねこ)が茂(しげ)みに潜(ひそ)んでいた。
The cat was lurking in the bushes.
この計画(けいかく)には危険(きけん)が潜(ひそ)んでいる。
Danger lurks within this plan.
Sense 2
彼(かれ)には大(おお)きな才能(さいのう)が潜(ひそ)んでいる。
Great talent lies hidden within him.
Sense 2 - positive

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No common transitive pair. Pattern: Xが潜(ひそ)む (X lurks/hides).

ASPECT: ている indicates state. 潜(ひそ)んでいる = 'is lurking/hiding' or 'lies hidden.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xに潜(ひそ)む (to lurk in X)
  • 危険(きけん)が潜(ひそ)む (danger lurks)
  • 問題(もんだい)が潜(ひそ)む (a problem lies hidden)
  • 可能性(かのうせい)が潜(ひそ)む (potential lies within)

NUANCE: Often implies something threatening or waiting to emerge, but can be used positively for hidden potential or talent.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 隠(かく)れる - to hide (more active)
  • 潜(もぐ)る - to dive, go underground
