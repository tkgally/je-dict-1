必要(ひつよう)

ひつよう
adjective (na-adjective)
necessary, needed

Definitions

1. necessary, needed, required
Essential or required for a purpose. Can function as a na-adjective or noun.

Examples

パスポートが必要(ひつよう)です。
A passport is necessary.
必要(ひつよう)な物(もの)を買(か)いました。
I bought the things I needed.
na-adjective modifying noun
それは必要(ひつよう)ありません。
That's not necessary.

Notes

Functions both as a na-adjective and a noun. 必要(ひつよう)がある (there is a need) and 必要(ひつよう)ない (not necessary) are common expressions. Opposite: 不必要(ふひつよう) (unnecessary).

Related Words

Antonym: 不必要(ふひつよう) (unnecessary)
