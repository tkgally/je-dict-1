日陰(ひかげ)

ひかげ
noun
shade, shadow (from sunlight)

Definitions

1. shade, shadow
An area protected from direct sunlight, typically cooler than surrounding areas.

Examples

暑(あつ)いから日陰(ひかげ)で休(やす)もう。
It's hot, so let's rest in the shade.
この植物(しょくぶつ)は日陰(ひかげ)でも育(そだ)ちます。
This plant can grow even in the shade.
日陰(ひかげ)と日向(ひなた)では温度(おんど)が全然(ぜんぜん)違(ちが)う。
The temperature is completely different in the shade versus the sun.

Notes

日陰(ひかげ) specifically refers to shade from sunlight, as opposed to general darkness.

Contrast with:

  • 日向(ひなた) (sunny spot, place in the sun) - opposite
  • 影(かげ) (shadow, silhouette) - shadow cast by an object
  • 陰(かげ) (shade, behind) - can also mean 'behind' or 'unseen'

Common expressions:

  • 日陰(ひかげ)に入(はい)る (to get into the shade)
  • 日陰(ひかげ)で育(そだ)つ (to grow in the shade)
  • 日陰(ひかげ)者(もの) (person who avoids the spotlight)
