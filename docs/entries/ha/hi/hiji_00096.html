肘(ひじ)

ひじ
noun
elbow

Definitions

1. elbow
The joint in the middle of the arm where it bends.

Examples

肘(ひじ)をテーブルにつかないでください。
Please don't put your elbows on the table.
Table manners
肘(ひじ)を曲(ま)げてください。
Please bend your elbow.
肘(ひじ)をぶつけてしまいました。
I bumped my elbow.

Notes

肘(ひじ) is a straightforward body part word. The kanji is fairly complex, so it's often written in hiragana. 肘(ひじ)掛(か)け refers to an armrest (on a chair). Putting elbows on the table (肘(ひじ)をつく) is considered bad manners in Japan, as in many cultures.
