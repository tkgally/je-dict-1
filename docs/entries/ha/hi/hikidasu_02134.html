引き出す

ひきだす
verb
to pull out, to withdraw

Definitions

1. to pull out, to withdraw
To take something out, especially money from a bank.

Examples

お金(かね)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す。
To withdraw money.
引(ひ)き出(だ)しから取(と)り出(だ)す。
To take out from the drawer.
能力(のうりょく)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す。
To draw out one's ability.
