引(ひ)き出(だ)す

ひきだす
verb
to pull out, to withdraw

Definitions

1. to pull out, to withdraw
To take something out, especially money from a bank.

Examples

お金(かね)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す。
To withdraw money.
引(ひ)き出(だ)しから取(と)り出(だ)す。
To take out from the drawer.
能力(のうりょく)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す。
To draw out one's ability.

Notes

引(ひ)き出(だ)す is a compound verb meaning to pull/draw out.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Related noun: 引(ひ)き出(だ)し (drawer)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お金(かね)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す (withdraw money)
  • 能力(のうりょく)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す (draw out ability)
  • 本音(ほんね)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す (draw out true feelings)

MEANINGS:
1. Physical: to pull something out
2. Banking: to withdraw money
3. Figurative: to elicit, bring out (potential, feelings)
