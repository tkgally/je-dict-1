広(ひろ)がる

ひろがる
verb (godan)
to spread, to extend

Definitions

1. to spread, to extend
To become wider or cover more area.
2. to expand, to grow
To increase in scope.

Examples

噂(うわさ)が広(ひろ)がる。
Rumors spread.
平野(へいや)が広(ひろ)がっている。
The plain extends.
被害(ひがい)が広(ひろ)がる。
The damage spreads.

Notes

広(ひろ)がる means to spread or extend.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 広(ひろ)がる (intransitive): to spread
  • 広(ひろ)げる (transitive): to spread

USAGE:

  • Rumors, news
  • Physical areas
  • Damage, influence
