否定(ひてい)

ひてい
noun / suru-verb
negation, denial

Definitions

1. negation, denial
The act of saying no or rejecting something as untrue.
2. negative (grammar)
In grammar, the negative form of a word or sentence.

Examples

彼(かれ)は関与(かんよ)を否定(ひてい)した。
He denied his involvement.
否定(ひてい)形(けい)を使(つか)って文(ぶん)を作(つく)りなさい。
Make sentences using the negative form.
Grammar instruction context
自分(じぶん)の価値(かち)を否定(ひてい)してはいけない。
You shouldn't deny your own worth.

Notes

Used both for denying claims and for grammatical negation.

FORMS:

  • 否定(ひてい)する (to deny, negate)
  • 否定(ひてい)的 (negative, in a denying manner)
  • 〜を否定(ひてい)する (to deny something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 否定(ひてい)形(けい) (negative form - grammar)
  • 否定(ひてい)文(ぶん) (negative sentence)
  • 全(ぜん)否定(ひてい) (complete denial)
  • 自己(じこ)否定(ひてい) (self-denial)
  • 否定(ひてい)的な意見(いけん) (negative opinion)

ANTONYM:

  • 肯定(こうてい) (affirmation)

Related Words

Antonym: 肯定(こうてい) (affirmation)
