筆者(ひっしゃ)

ひっしゃ
noun
writer, author

Definitions

1. writer, author
The person who wrote a particular text, article, or book. Often used by writers to refer to themselves in the third person.

Examples

筆者(ひっしゃ)は長年(ながねん)この問題(もんだい)を研究(けんきゅう)してきた。
The author has researched this issue for many years.
Self-reference in academic writing
筆者(ひっしゃ)の意見(いけん)に賛成(さんせい)だ。
I agree with the author's opinion.
この記事(きじ)の筆者(ひっしゃ)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is the author of this article?

Notes

USAGE: 筆者(ひっしゃ) is commonly used in academic and journalistic writing. Writers often use it to refer to themselves in the third person for a more objective tone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 筆者(ひっしゃ)は～ (the author/I [as writer])
  • 筆者(ひっしゃ)の意見(いけん)/考(かんが)え (the author's opinion)
  • 筆者(ひっしゃ)によると (according to the author)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 著者(ちょしゃ) - author (of a book)
  • 作者(さくしゃ) - author, creator (of literary/artistic works)
  • 記者(きしゃ) - journalist, reporter
  • 作家(さっか) - writer (professional novelist, etc.)

NOTE: 筆者(ひっしゃ) emphasizes the act of writing (筆(ふで) = brush/pen), while 著者(ちょしゃ) emphasizes authorship of a published work.
