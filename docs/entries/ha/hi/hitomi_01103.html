瞳(ひとみ)

ひとみ
noun
pupil (of eye), eye

Definitions

1. pupil
The dark circular opening in the center of the iris.
2. eye
The eye, especially poetically.

Examples

瞳(ひとみ)が大(おお)きい。
The eyes are big.
彼女(かのじょ)の瞳(ひとみ)は美(うつく)しい。
Her eyes are beautiful.

Notes

瞳(ひとみ) refers to the pupil or eyes.

POETIC:
Often used in poetry and literature to describe beautiful or expressive eyes.

NAME:
Common Japanese female name.
