響(ひび)く

ひびく
godan verb
to resound; to echo; to affect

Definitions

1. to resound; to echo; to reverberate
For a sound to carry and be heard over a distance or to bounce back.
2. to affect; to have an impact on
To influence or have consequences for something.
3. to be felt; to reach (emotions)
For something to touch one's heart or resonate emotionally.

Examples

鐘(かね)の音(おと)が町(まち)に響(ひび)いた。
The sound of the bell echoed through the town.
寝不足(ねぶそく)が仕事(しごと)に響(ひび)いている。
Lack of sleep is affecting my work.
その言葉(ことば)が心(こころ)に響(ひび)いた。
Those words resonated in my heart.

Notes

響(ひび)く is intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 響(ひび)かせる (to make resound).

ASPECT ({ている}):
響(ひび)いている indicates ongoing reverberation or an ongoing effect.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 音(おと)が響(ひび)く (sound echoes)
  • 心(こころ)に響(ひび)く (to resonate in one's heart)
  • ～に響(ひび)く (to affect ~)
  • 体(からだ)に響(ひび)く (to take a toll on one's body)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～が響(ひび)く: What is echoing
  • ～に響(ひび)く: What is affected, or where it echoes

The figurative sense of "affecting" something (often negatively) is very common in everyday use.

Related Words

Pair verb: 響(ひび)かせる (transitive)
Related: 響(ひび)き (noun form)
