非常(ひじょう)

ひじょう
noun, na-adjective
emergency; very, extremely

Definitions

1. emergency, extraordinary
An unusual or urgent situation requiring immediate attention.
2. very, extremely
Used adverbially (非常(ひじょう)に) to intensify adjectives or verbs.

Examples

非常(ひじょう)に難(むずか)しいです。
It is very difficult.
非常(ひじょう)に as intensifier
非常(ひじょう)口(ぐち)はどこですか。
Where is the emergency exit?
非常(ひじょう)な事態(じたい)が起(お)きました。
An emergency situation has occurred.

Notes

非常(ひじょう) has two main uses: 'emergency' as a noun/na-adjective, and 'very' as an adverb.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 非常(ひじょう)に (very, extremely)
  • Na-adjective: 非常(ひじょう)な + noun (emergency ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 非常(ひじょう)に + adjective (very ~)
  • 非常(ひじょう)口(ぐち) (emergency exit)
  • 非常(ひじょう)事態(じたい) (emergency situation)
  • 非常(ひじょう)ベル (emergency bell/alarm)

SIMILAR WORDS (as intensifier):

  • とても: very (neutral)
  • 非常(ひじょう)に: very (formal)
  • すごく: very (casual)
