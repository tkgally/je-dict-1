ひどい

ひどい
adjective (i-adjective)
terrible, awful

Definitions

1. terrible, awful, cruel
Very bad, unpleasant, or severe. Can describe situations, weather, behavior, or treatment.
2. excessive, extreme
Beyond what is normal or acceptable.

Examples

今日(きょう)は天気(てんき)がひどいですね。
The weather is terrible today, isn't it?
ひどいことを言(い)わないでください。
Please don't say such awful things.
風邪(かぜ)がひどくなった。
My cold got worse.
Using the adverbial form

Notes

ひどい conjugates as an i-adjective: ひどくない (negative), ひどくて (te-form), ひどかった (past). The kanji 酷い can be used but is less common. This word can express that something is bad, cruel, or extreme. Common expression: ひどい目に遭う (ひどいめにあう - to have a terrible experience).
