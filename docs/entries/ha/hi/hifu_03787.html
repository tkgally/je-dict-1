皮膚(ひふ)

ひふ
noun
skin

Definitions

1. skin
The outer covering of the human body. More technical/medical than 肌(はだ).

Examples

皮膚(ひふ)が乾燥(かんそう)している。
My skin is dry.
皮膚科(ひふか)に行(い)った。
I went to a dermatologist.
紫外線(しがいせん)は皮膚(ひふ)に悪(わる)い影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
Ultraviolet rays have a bad effect on the skin.

Notes

USAGE: Medical/scientific term for skin. More formal than 肌(はだ).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 皮膚科(ひふか) (dermatology)
  • 皮膚(ひふ)炎(えん) (dermatitis)
  • 皮膚(ひふ)病(びょう) (skin disease)
  • 皮膚(ひふ)癌(がん) (skin cancer)

CONTRAST:

  • 肌(はだ) - skin (everyday, often about texture/beauty)
  • 皮(かわ) - skin/hide (of animals, or as outer layer)

Related Words

Related: 肌(はだ) (everyday term)
