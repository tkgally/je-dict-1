檜(ひのき)

ひのき
noun
Japanese cypress

Definitions

1. Japanese cypress; hinoki
A coniferous tree native to Japan, valued for its fragrant, durable wood used in traditional architecture and bathtubs.

Examples

この神社(じんじゃ)は檜(ひのき)で建(た)てられている。
This shrine is built with hinoki cypress.
檜(ひのき)風呂(ぶろ)は香(かお)りが良(よ)い。
Hinoki cypress baths have a nice fragrance.
檜(ひのき)の香(かお)りにはリラックス効果(こうか)がある。
The scent of hinoki has a relaxing effect.

Notes

檜(ひのき) refers to Japanese cypress (Chamaecyparis obtusa).

Alternative writing: ヒノキ (katakana)

Common expressions:

  • 檜(ひのき)風呂(ぶろ) (hinoki cypress bathtub)
  • 檜(ひのき)舞台(ぶたい) (literally "cypress stage" - a big stage/opportunity to shine)
  • 檜(ひのき)造(づく)り (hinoki construction)

Counter: 本(ほん)

Cultural note:
檜(ひのき) is considered one of the finest building materials in Japan. It's used for shrines (including Ise Grand Shrine), temples, and traditional houses. The wood is prized for its durability, resistance to rot, and pleasant fragrance. 檜(ひのき)風呂(ぶろ) (hinoki baths) are a luxury feature in ryokan and high-end homes. The expression 檜(ひのき)舞台(ぶたい)に立(た)つ means to have one's moment in the spotlight.
