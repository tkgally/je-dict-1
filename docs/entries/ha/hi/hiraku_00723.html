開(ひら)く

ひらく
verb (godan)
to open, to hold (event)

Definitions

1. to open
To make something accessible.
2. to hold, to host
To organize an event.

Examples

本(ほん)を開(ひら)く。
To open a book.
会議(かいぎ)を開(ひら)く。
To hold a meeting.
新(あたら)しい店(みせ)を開(ひら)く。
To open a new shop.

Notes

開(ひら)く means to open or hold.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 開(ひら)く: to open (godan)
  • 開(あ)く: to open (intransitive, godan)
  • 開(あ)ける: to open (transitive, ichidan)

USAGE:

  • Books, meetings, events
  • New businesses
