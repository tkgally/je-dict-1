広(ひろ)める

ひろめる
ichidan verb
to spread, to propagate

Definitions

1. to spread, to disseminate
To make something known to many people.
2. to broaden, to widen
To expand knowledge or experience.

Examples

知識(ちしき)を広める。
To spread knowledge.
視野(しや)を広める。
To broaden one's horizons.

Notes

広める is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 広める (transitive): to spread
  • 広(ひろ)まる (intransitive): to spread

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名(な)を広める: to make a name known
  • 教(おし)えを広める: to spread teachings
