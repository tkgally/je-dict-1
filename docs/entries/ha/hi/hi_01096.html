火(ひ)

ひ
noun
fire, flame

Definitions

1. fire
The state of combustion producing heat and light.
2. flame
The visible part of fire.

Examples

火(ひ)をつける。
To light a fire.
火(ひ)に気(き)をつけて。
Be careful with fire.

Notes

火(ひ) means fire.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 火事(かじ): fire (conflagration)
  • 火曜日(かようび): Tuesday
  • 花火(はなび): fireworks
  • 火山(かざん): volcano
  • 消火(しょうか): firefighting

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 火(ひ)に油(あぶら)を注(そそ)ぐ: to add fuel to the fire
