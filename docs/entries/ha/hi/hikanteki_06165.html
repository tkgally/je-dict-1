悲観的(ひかんてき)

ひかんてき
na-adjective
pessimistic

Definitions

1. pessimistic
Having a negative outlook on life or situations, expecting unfavorable outcomes.

Examples

悲観的(ひかんてき)にならないでください。
Please don't be pessimistic.
彼女(かのじょ)は物事(ものごと)を悲観的(ひかんてき)に見(み)る傾向(けいこう)がある。
She tends to see things pessimistically.
経済(けいざい)の先行(せんこう)きに悲観的(ひかんてき)な予測(よそく)が多(おお)い。
There are many pessimistic forecasts about the economic outlook.

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 悲観的(ひかんてき)に (pessimistically)
  • Noun: 悲観(ひかん) (pessimism)
  • Verb: 悲観(ひかん)する (to be pessimistic)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 悲観的(ひかんてき)な見方(みかた)/考(かんが)え (pessimistic view/thinking)
  • 悲観的(ひかんてき)に考(かんが)える (think pessimistically)
  • 悲観的(ひかんてき)な予測(よそく) (pessimistic forecast)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ネガティブ (negative) - more casual, loanword
  • 後(うし)ろ向(む)き (backward-looking) - emphasizes reluctant attitude

Related Words

Antonym: 楽観的(らっかんてき) (optimistic)
