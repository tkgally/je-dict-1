紐(ひも)

ひも
noun
string, cord, strap

Definitions

1. string, cord
A thin piece of material for tying.
2. strap, lace
A strip of material for fastening.

Examples

紐(ひも)で結(むす)ぶ。
To tie with string.
靴(くつ)の紐(ひも)が解(ほど)けた。
My shoelace came untied.

Notes

紐(ひも) refers to strings and cords.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 靴紐(くつひも): shoelace
  • 首紐(くびひも): neck strap

SLANG:
紐(ひも) can mean a man who lives off a woman's earnings.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 紐(ひも)を解(と)く: to untie, to loosen restrictions
