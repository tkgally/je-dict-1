平仮名(ひらがな)

ひらがな
noun
hiragana

Definitions

1. hiragana
The cursive Japanese syllabary, used for native Japanese words and grammatical elements.

Examples

平仮名(ひらがな)で書(か)いてください。
Please write it in hiragana.
子供(こども)はまず平仮名(ひらがな)を習(なら)います。
Children first learn hiragana.
平仮名(ひらがな)は46文字(もじ)あります。
There are 46 hiragana characters.

Notes

平仮名(ひらがな) is one of the Japanese writing systems.

JAPANESE WRITING SYSTEMS:

  • 平仮名(ひらがな) (hiragana - cursive syllabary)
  • 片仮名(かたかな) (katakana - angular syllabary)
  • 漢字(かんじ) (kanji - Chinese characters)

ETYMOLOGY: 平(ひら) (ordinary) + 仮名(かな) (kana/syllabary)

USAGE: Used for grammatical particles, verb endings, and words without kanji.
