非常(ひじょう)に

ひじょうに
adverb
extremely; very; exceedingly

Definitions

1. extremely, very, exceedingly
A formal intensifier indicating a very high degree.

Examples

非常(ひじょう)に重要(じゅうよう)な問題(もんだい)です。
It's an extremely important issue.
Formal context
この料理(りょうり)は非常(ひじょう)においしいです。
This dish is extremely delicious.
結果(けっか)に非常(ひじょう)に満足(まんぞく)しています。
I'm extremely satisfied with the results.
非常(ひじょう)に申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I'm extremely sorry.
Very formal apology

Notes

非常(ひじょう)に is a formal adverb meaning 'extremely' or 'very.'

Position: Appears before adjectives or adverbs it modifies.

Modifies: Adjectives (both i-adjectives and na-adjectives), some adverbs, and verbal expressions.

[Register: Formal/Written]

Similar adverbs (from formal to casual):

  • 非常(ひじょう)に: Formal, written, business contexts
  • とても: Neutral, general use
  • すごく: Casual, spoken
  • めっちゃ: Very casual, slang

非常(ひじょう)に vs. とても:

  • 非常(ひじょう)に is more formal and stronger
  • Use 非常(ひじょう)に in presentations, formal writing, polite speech
  • Use とても in everyday conversation

Related word: 非常(ひじょう) as a noun means 'emergency' (非常口(ひじょうぐち) = emergency exit).
