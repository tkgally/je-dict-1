向日葵(ひまわり)

ひまわり
noun
sunflower

Definitions

1. sunflower
A tall plant with large yellow flowers that turn to follow the sun.

Examples

夏(なつ)になると向日葵(ひまわり)が咲(さ)く。
Sunflowers bloom in summer.
向日葵(ひまわり)の種(たね)を食(た)べる。
Eat sunflower seeds.
向日葵(ひまわり)畑(ばたけ)は写真(しゃしん)スポットとして人気(にんき)がある。
Sunflower fields are popular photo spots.

Notes

The name 向日葵(ひまわり) means 'facing the sun' (向(む)く + 日(ひ) + 葵(あおい)), referring to the flower's heliotropic behavior (following the sun). Also written as ヒマワリ.

Symbolism:

  • Summer (夏(なつ)の花(はな))
  • Brightness, energy, vitality
  • Admiration, loyalty (turning toward the sun)

Products from sunflowers:

  • 向日葵(ひまわり)の種(たね) (sunflower seeds - snack)
  • ひまわり油(あぶら) (sunflower oil)

Famous sunflower locations:

  • 北海道(ほっかいどう)北竜町(ほくりゅうちょう)
  • Various 向日葵(ひまわり)畑(ばたけ) (sunflower fields) across Japan

The 1970 film 「{ひまわり}」 (Sunflower) starring Sophia Loren features an iconic sunflower field scene shot in Ukraine.
