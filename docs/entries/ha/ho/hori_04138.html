堀(ほり)

ほり
noun
moat, canal, ditch

Definitions

1. moat
A defensive ditch filled with water surrounding a castle or fortification.
2. canal, waterway
An artificial waterway or channel.

Examples

城(しろ)の周(まわ)りには深(ふか)い堀(ほり)がある。
There is a deep moat around the castle.
江戸(えど)時代(じだい)の堀(ほり)が今(いま)も残(のこ)っている。
Moats from the Edo period still remain today.
外(そと)堀(ぼり)と内(うち)堀(ぼり)で守(まも)られていた。
It was protected by an outer and inner moat.
Note rendaku: ほり → ぼり in compounds

Notes

KANJI VARIATIONS:

  • 堀(ほり) - moat, canal (man-made waterway)
  • 濠(ほり) - moat (often used for castle moats)

HISTORICAL CONTEXT: Moats were essential defensive structures for Japanese castles (城(しろ)). Many castles had multiple moats (外(そと)堀(ぼり) outer moat, 内(うち)堀(ぼり) inner moat).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 外(そと)堀(ぼり) (outer moat)
  • 内(うち)堀(ぼり) (inner moat)
  • お堀(ほり) (moat - polite, often refers to Imperial Palace moat)
  • 堀(ほり)端(ばた) (moatside)

PLACE NAMES: Many Tokyo place names include 堀(ほり):

  • 日本橋(にほんばし)堀留(ほりどめ)
  • 虎ノ門(とらのもん)外堀(そとぼり)
