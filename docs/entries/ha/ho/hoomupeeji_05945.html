ホームページ

ほーむぺーじ
noun
homepage, website

Definitions

1. website
In Japanese, often refers to a website in general, not just the front page.
2. homepage (front page)
The main page of a website.

Examples

会社(かいしゃ)のホームページを見(み)てください。
Please look at the company's website.
ホームページを作成(さくせい)した。
I created a website.
詳(くわ)しくはホームページで確認(かくにん)できます。
You can check the details on our website.

Notes

WASEI EIGO (Japanese-English):
In Japanese, ホームページ is commonly used to mean "website" in general, unlike English where "homepage" typically refers only to the front page of a website. This is a case of semantic expansion.

ABBREVIATION:
HP (エイチピー) is sometimes used as an abbreviation.

ALTERNATIVES:

  • ウェブサイト: website (more technical usage)
