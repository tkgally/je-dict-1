方向(ほうこう)

ほうこう
noun
direction

Definitions

1. direction, course
The path along which something moves or points; the way something is oriented.
2. direction, orientation (figurative)
The general tendency or approach being taken.

Examples

駅(えき)はどの方向(ほうこう)ですか。
Which direction is the station?
風(かぜ)の方向(ほうこう)が変(か)わった。
The wind direction changed.
今後(こんご)の方向性(ほうこうせい)について話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed the future direction.
方向性 = direction, orientation (policy/approach)

Notes

USAGE: 方向(ほうこう) is used for both physical directions and figurative directions (policy, approach).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 方向(ほうこう)を示(しめ)す (to indicate direction)
  • 方向(ほうこう)を変(か)える (to change direction)
  • 方向(ほうこう)音痴(おんち) (having no sense of direction)
  • 方向性(ほうこうせい) (directionality, orientation)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 方向(ほうこう)転換(てんかん) (change of direction)
  • 方向(ほうこう)指示器(しじき) (turn signal, blinker)
