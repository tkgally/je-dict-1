堀(ほり)

ほり
noun
moat, canal

Definitions

1. moat
A water-filled ditch surrounding a castle or fortification.
2. canal, ditch
An artificial waterway or channel.

Examples

城(しろ)の周(まわ)りに堀(ほり)がある。
There is a moat around the castle.
堀(ほり)に鯉(こい)が泳(およ)いでいる。
Carp are swimming in the moat.
内堀(うちぼり)と外堀(そとぼり)が残(のこ)っている。
The inner and outer moats remain.

Notes

Commonly seen around Japanese castles and historical sites.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 内堀(うちぼり) (inner moat)
  • 外堀(そとぼり) (outer moat)
  • 空堀(からぼり) (dry moat)
  • 水堀(みずぼり) (water-filled moat)

FAMOUS EXAMPLES:

  • 皇居(こうきょ)の堀(ほり) (Imperial Palace moat in Tokyo)
  • 大阪城(おおさかじょう)の堀(ほり) (Osaka Castle moat)

IDIOM:

  • 外堀(そとぼり)を埋(う)める (to fill in the outer moat - to gradually corner someone or prepare the groundwork)
