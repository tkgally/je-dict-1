本当(ほんとう)に

ほんとうに
adverb
really, truly

Definitions

1. really, truly
Used to emphasize that something is genuine or to express sincere feelings.
2. indeed, certainly
Used to confirm or agree with something strongly.

Examples

本当(ほんとう)にありがとうございます。
Thank you so much. / I really appreciate it.
この映画(えいが)は本当(ほんとう)に面白(おもしろ)かった。
This movie was really interesting.
本当(ほんとう)にそう思(おも)いますか？
Do you really think so?

Notes

This is the adverbial form of 本当(ほんとう) (truth/reality). In casual speech, it's often shortened to ほんとに or even ほんと. Similar to 本当(ほんとう)の (true/real) as an adjective.

Related Words

Related: 確(たし)かに (certainly)
