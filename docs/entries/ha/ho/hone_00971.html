骨(ほね)

ほね
noun
bone

Definitions

1. bone
The hard tissue forming the skeleton.
2. frame, structure
The supporting framework of something.

Examples

魚(さかな)の骨(ほね)が喉(のど)に刺(さ)さった。
A fish bone got stuck in my throat.
骨(ほね)を折(お)る。
To break a bone; to make an effort.

Notes

骨(ほね) refers to bones.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 骨(ほね)を折(お)る: to break a bone; to work hard
  • 骨(ほね)が折(お)れる: to be laborious
  • 骨(ほね)を惜(お)しむ: to spare no effort
  • 骨抜(ほねぬ)きにする: to take the backbone out of

COMPOUNDS:

  • 骨格(こっかく): skeleton, framework
  • 背骨(せぼね): spine, backbone
