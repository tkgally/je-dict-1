埃(ほこり)

ほこり
noun
dust

Definitions

1. dust
Fine dry particles of matter, especially those found on surfaces or in the air.

Examples

本棚(ほんだな)に埃(ほこり)がたまっている。
Dust has accumulated on the bookshelf.
埃がたまる = dust accumulates
埃(ほこり)を払(はら)ってから使(つか)ってください。
Please dust it off before using.
埃を払う = to dust off
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)は埃(ほこり)っぽい。
The construction site is dusty.
埃っぽい = dusty

Notes

USAGE: 埃(ほこり) specifically refers to dust particles, not general dirt.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 埃(ほこり)がたまる (dust accumulates)
  • 埃(ほこり)を払(はら)う (to dust off)
  • 埃(ほこり)を被(かぶ)る (to be covered in dust)
  • 埃(ほこり)っぽい (dusty)
  • 埃(ほこり)だらけ (covered in dust)

HOMOPHONE: 誇(ほこ)り (pride) - same pronunciation but different meaning and kanji

RELATED:

  • 塵(ちり) (fine dust, litter)
  • 汚(よご)れ (dirt, stain)

Related Words

Contrast: 誇(ほこ)り (pride (homophone))
