保育園(ほいくえん)

ほいくえん
noun
nursery school

Definitions

1. nursery school, daycare center, childcare facility
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

子供(こども)を保育園(ほいくえん)に預(あず)けている。
I leave my child at the nursery school.
保育園(ほいくえん)の入園(にゅうえん)申(もう)し込(こ)みをした。
I applied for admission to the nursery school.
保育園(ほいくえん)は待機(たいき)児童(じどう)が多(おお)い。
There are many children on waiting lists for nursery schools.

Notes

保育園(ほいくえん) is primarily for childcare, typically for children ages 0-6, with longer hours than kindergarten.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保育園(ほいくえん)に預(あず)ける (leave [child] at nursery)
  • 保育園(ほいくえん)に入(はい)れる (get into nursery school)
  • 保育園(ほいくえん)に通(かよ)う (attend nursery school)
  • 保育園(ほいくえん)の保育士(ほいくし) (nursery school teacher)
  • 認可(にんか)保育園(ほいくえん) (licensed nursery)

WAITLIST ISSUE:
In urban areas, 待機(たいき)児童(じどう)問題(もんだい) (waitlisted children problem) is a major social issue. Many families struggle to find nursery spots.

CONTRAST WITH 幼稚園(ようちえん):

  • 保育園(ほいくえん): For working parents, accepts infants (0+), long hours (7am-7pm)
  • 幼稚園(ようちえん): Education-focused, ages 3+, shorter hours
