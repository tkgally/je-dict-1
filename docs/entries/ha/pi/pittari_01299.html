ぴったり

ぴったり
adverb, na-adjective
exactly, perfectly

Definitions

1. exactly, precisely
Without any deviation.
2. perfectly fitting
Matching exactly in size or suitability.
3. tightly, closely
With no gap or space.

Examples

ぴったり合(あ)う。
It fits perfectly.
ぴったり5時(じ)に着(つ)いた。
I arrived at exactly 5 o'clock.

Notes

ぴったり is onomatopoeia.

COMMON USES:

  • ぴったり合(あ)う: to fit perfectly
  • ぴったりくっつく: to stick closely
  • サイズがぴったり: perfect size
