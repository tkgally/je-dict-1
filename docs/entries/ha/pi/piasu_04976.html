ピアス

ぴあす
noun
pierced earrings; ear piercings

Definitions

1. pierced earrings
Earrings designed to go through pierced ears.
2. ear piercing; the pierced hole
The hole or piercing itself in the ear.

Examples

誕生日(たんじょうび)にピアスをもらった。
I received (pierced) earrings for my birthday.
ピアスを開(あ)けるのは痛(いた)かった。
Getting my ears pierced was painful.
ピアスの穴(あな)が塞(ふさ)がってしまった。
The piercing hole closed up.

Notes

ピアス comes from English 'pierce' and has a specific meaning in Japanese that differs from English usage.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION:

  • ピアス: Earrings for pierced ears / the piercing itself
  • イヤリング: Clip-on earrings (no piercing)

In English, 'earring' is the general term, but in Japanese, these are distinct categories.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ピアスをつける (to put on earrings)
  • ピアスを開(あ)ける (to get ears pierced)
  • ピアスの穴(あな) (piercing hole)
  • ピアスを外(はず)す (to remove earrings)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Many Japanese schools and workplaces prohibit or restrict ピアス. Some people get their ears pierced at 皮膚科(ひふか) (dermatology clinic) for safety.

Contrast: イヤリング (clip-on earrings)
