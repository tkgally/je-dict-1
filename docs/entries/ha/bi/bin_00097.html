瓶(びん)

びん
noun
bottle

Definitions

1. bottle
A container typically made of glass, used for liquids.

Examples

ワインの瓶(びん)を開(あ)けました。
I opened a bottle of wine.
空(から)の瓶(びん)を捨(す)ててください。
Please throw away the empty bottles.
瓶(びん)ビールをください。
I'll have a bottled beer.

Notes

瓶(びん) refers to glass bottles. 空(あ)き瓶(びん) means empty bottles. In Japan, bottles are often recycled, and glass bottle recycling is common. ペットボトル (PET bottle) refers to plastic bottles.
