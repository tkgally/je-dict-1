ベンチ

べんち
noun
bench

Definitions

1. bench
A long seat for multiple people, typically found in parks or public spaces.
2. bench (sports)
The seating area for players who are not currently playing in a game.

Examples

公園(こうえん)のベンチに座(すわ)る。
I sit on a bench in the park.
ベンチで休憩(きゅうけい)した。
I took a break on the bench.
選手(せんしゅ)はベンチから試合(しあい)を見(み)ていた。
The player was watching the game from the bench.

Notes

ベンチ is used for park benches and sports team benches.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ベンチに座(すわ)る: to sit on a bench
  • 公園(こうえん)のベンチ: park bench
  • ベンチ入(い)り: being on the bench (selected for the team)
  • ベンチを温(あたた)める: to warm the bench (not getting playing time)
  • ベンチプレス: bench press (exercise)

USAGE NOTE:
In sports, ベンチ入(い)り means being selected for the game roster but possibly not as a starter.
