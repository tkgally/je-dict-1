べたべた

べたべた
adverb
sticky; clingy

Definitions

1. sticky; gooey
Describes a surface or substance that is unpleasantly sticky.
2. clingy; overly affectionate
Describes behavior that is excessively close or touchy, often in a way that bothers others.

Examples

手(て)が飴(あめ)でべたべたする。
My hands are sticky from candy.
汗(あせ)で肌(はだ)がべたべたしている。
My skin is sticky from sweat.
カップルがべたべたしている。
The couple is being all clingy with each other.
Can have a slightly critical nuance

Notes

べたべた is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) describing stickiness or clinginess.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • べたべたする: to be sticky, to be clingy
  • べたべた触(さわ)る: to touch repeatedly (annoyingly)
  • べたべた甘(あま)える: to act clingy and spoiled

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ねばねば: sticky, gooey (like natto or okra)
  • ぬるぬる: slimy, slippery
