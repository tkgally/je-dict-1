別々(べつべつ)

べつべつ
na-adjective / adverb
separately; individually

Definitions

1. separately; individually
Acting or existing as distinct units, not together.

Examples

会計(かいけい)は別々(べつべつ)でお願(ねが)いします。
Separate checks, please.
Common phrase at restaurants
二人(ふたり)は別々(べつべつ)の道(みち)を歩(ある)いた。
The two walked separate paths.
Literal or figurative separation
別々(べつべつ)に送(おく)ってください。
Please send them separately.
Adverbial use with に

Notes

別々(べつべつ) is a reduplication of 別(べつ) (separate; different).

USAGE:

  • 別々(べつべつ)の + noun (separate ~)
  • 別々(べつべつ)に + verb (separately; do ~ separately)
  • 別々(べつべつ)だ (are separate)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 別々(べつべつ)に暮(く)らす (live separately)
  • 別々(べつべつ)に行動(こうどう)する (act separately)
  • 会計(かいけい)は別々(べつべつ)で (separate checks)
  • 別々(べつべつ)の道(みち) (separate paths)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 別(べつ)に (not particularly; separately)
  • 各自(かくじ) (each person; individually)
  • それぞれ (each; respectively)

別々(べつべつ) emphasizes the separateness of two or more things, while それぞれ emphasizes individual differences.
