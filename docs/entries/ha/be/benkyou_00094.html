勉強(べんきょう)

べんきょう
noun
study, learning

Definitions

1. study, studying
The act of learning or acquiring knowledge through reading, practice, or instruction. One of the most common words in Japanese education.
2. discount (in business)
In business contexts, 勉強(べんきょう)する can mean to give a discount or lower the price.

Examples

毎日(まいにち)日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I study Japanese every day.
勉強(べんきょう)が大変(たいへん)です。
Studying is tough.
試験(しけん)のために勉強(べんきょう)しなければなりません。
I have to study for the exam.

Notes

勉強(べんきょう)する is one of the most essential する-verbs. The kanji 勉(べん) means 'effort' and 強(きょう) means 'strong', so the word literally means 'to make strong effort'. In business, もう少(すこ)し勉強(べんきょう)してください means 'Please give me a better price'.
