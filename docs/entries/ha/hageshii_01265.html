激(はげ)しい

はげしい
i-adjective
intense, violent

Definitions

1. intense, violent
Very strong or forceful.
2. fierce, severe
Showing great energy or emotion.

Examples

激しい雨(あめ)
Heavy rain
激しい競争(きょうそう)
Fierce competition

Notes

激しい describes high intensity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 激しい運動(うんどう): strenuous exercise
  • 激しい感情(かんじょう): intense emotion
  • 気性(きしょう)が激しい: to have a violent temper

ADVERB FORM:

  • 激しく: intensely, violently
