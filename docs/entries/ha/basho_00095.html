場所(ばしょ)

ばしょ
noun
place, location

Definitions

1. place, location, spot
A particular position or area. Can refer to physical locations or more abstract positions.

Examples

待(ま)ち合(あ)わせの場所(ばしょ)はどこですか。
Where is the meeting place?
静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)で勉強(べんきょう)したいです。
I want to study in a quiet place.
この場所(ばしょ)は知(し)っています。
I know this place.

Notes

A very common word for 'place'. Different from 所(ところ) which is more general and can mean 'aspect' or 'point' in addition to 'place'. 場所(ばしょ) specifically refers to a physical location or spot.
