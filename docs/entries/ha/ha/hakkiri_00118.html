はっきり

はっきり
adverb
clearly; distinctly; plainly

Definitions

1. clearly; distinctly
In a way that is easy to see, hear, or understand.

Examples

はっきり言(い)ってください。
Please say it clearly.
理由(りゆう)がはっきりしない。
The reason is not clear.
はっきりする = to become clear
はっきり覚(おぼ)えていません。
I don't remember clearly.

Notes

はっきり can function as an adverb or with する to form a verb.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • はっきり言(い)う: to say clearly/frankly
  • はっきりする: to become clear
  • はっきり分(わ)かる: to understand clearly
