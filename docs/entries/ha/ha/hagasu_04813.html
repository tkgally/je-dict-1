剥(は)がす

はがす
godan verb
to peel off, to tear off

Definitions

1. to peel off, to tear off, to strip
To remove something that is attached or stuck to a surface.

Examples

シールを剥(は)がす。
I peel off the sticker.
壁紙(かべがみ)を剥(は)がして新(あたら)しいのを貼(は)る。
I peel off the wallpaper and put up new one.
仮面(かめん)を剥(は)がす。
I tear off the mask.
Can be used figuratively

Notes

剥(は)がす is a transitive verb meaning to peel or tear off something attached.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 剥(は)がれる (intransitive, to come off, to peel off)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シールを剥(は)がす: to peel off a sticker
  • 皮(かわ)を剥(は)がす: to peel off skin/bark
  • 化(ば)けの皮(かわ)を剥(は)がす: to unmask, expose someone's true nature

RELATED WORDS:

  • 剥(む)く: to peel (fruit, vegetables)
  • 取(と)る: to remove
