はず

はず
noun
should be, supposed to

Definitions

1. should be, ought to be, supposed to
Indicates expectation based on reasoning or evidence.

Examples

彼(かれ)は来(く)るはずだ。
He should come. / He's supposed to come.
ここにあるはずなのに。
It should be here (but it isn't).

Notes

Used after dictionary form, past form, or い-adjectives. Expresses logical expectation, not obligation.
