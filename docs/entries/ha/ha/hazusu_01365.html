外(はず)す

はずす
godan verb
to remove, to unfasten

Definitions

1. to remove, to take off
To detach something from where it was attached.
2. to miss, to fail to hit
To not strike the intended target.
3. to leave (a place)
To step away temporarily.

Examples

メガネを外す。
To take off one's glasses.
的(まと)を外す。
To miss the target.
席(せき)を外す。
To step away from one's seat.

Notes

外す is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 外す (transitive): to remove
  • 外(はず)れる (intransitive): to come off

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 席(せき)を外す: to be away from one's desk
  • ボタンを外す: to unbutton
