二十日(はつか)

はつか
noun
twenty days, twentieth day

Definitions

1. twenty days
A period of twenty days.
2. twentieth day of the month
The twentieth day of any month.

Examples

一月(いちがつ)二十日(はつか)
January 20th.
二十日(はつか)ぐらいかかる。
It takes about twenty days.
二十日(はつか)までにお届(とど)けします。
We will deliver it by the 20th.

Notes

二十日(はつか) is a special reading.

SPECIAL DAY READINGS:

  • 一日(ついたち) (1st)
  • 十日(とおか) (10th)
  • 二十日(はつか) (20th)
  • 二十四日(にじゅうよっか) (24th - regular + special)

NOTE: After 10, most days use regular Sino-Japanese numbers + 日(にち), but 14th, 20th, and 24th have special readings.
