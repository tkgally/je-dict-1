嵌(は)める

はめる
verb (ichidan)
to fit; to insert; to put on (ring)

Definitions

1. to fit; to insert
To put something into a frame, slot, or opening so it fits precisely.
2. to put on (ring, gloves)
To put jewelry or accessories on one's fingers or hands.
3. to trap; to set up
To deceive or frame someone.

Examples

窓(まど)にガラスを嵌(は)めた。
I fitted glass into the window.
指輪(ゆびわ)を嵌(は)めている。
She's wearing a ring.
彼(かれ)は誰(だれ)かに嵌(は)められたようだ。
It seems he was set up by someone.

Notes

嵌(は)める is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) with meanings ranging from fitting things to trapping people.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 嵌(は)まる (to fit into, to be stuck)

  • 嵌(は)める: I fit the glass in.
  • 嵌(は)まる: The glass fits in.

ASPECT (ている):
嵌(は)めている indicates something is fitted/worn, or someone has been set up.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を[opening]に嵌(は)める — to fit [thing] into [opening]
  • [ring/gloves]を嵌(は)める — to put on [ring/gloves]
  • [person]を嵌(は)める — to trap/set up [person]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 指輪(ゆびわ)を嵌(は)める (put on a ring)
  • 手袋(てぶくろ)を嵌(は)める (put on gloves)
  • 罠(わな)に嵌(は)める (trap someone)

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This verb is frequently written as はめる due to the kanji's rarity.

COLLOQUIAL USE:
嵌(は)められた (I was set up!) is common in casual speech when claiming to have been tricked or framed.
