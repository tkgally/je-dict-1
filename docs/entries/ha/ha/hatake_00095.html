畑(はたけ)

はたけ
noun
field, farm

Definitions

1. field, farm plot
A piece of land used for growing vegetables, fruits, or other non-rice crops. Different from a rice paddy.

Examples

畑(はたけ)で野菜(やさい)を作(つく)っています。
I grow vegetables in the field.
おじいさんは毎日(まいにち)畑(はたけ)に行(い)きます。
Grandfather goes to the field every day.
畑(はたけ)の仕事(しごと)は大変(たいへん)です。
Farm work is hard.

Notes

Different from 田(た)んぼ (rice paddy). 畑(はたけ) is used for vegetables and other crops that don't require flooding. Can also be read as 'hata' in some compounds. Common in rural Japan.
