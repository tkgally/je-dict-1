梯子(はしご)

はしご
noun
ladder, stepladder

Definitions

1. ladder, stepladder
A device with rungs or steps used for climbing up or down.
2. bar-hopping, going from place to place
The practice of visiting multiple bars, restaurants, or other establishments in succession (used in the expression はしご酒).

Examples

梯子(はしご)を使(つか)って屋根(やね)に登(のぼ)った。
I climbed onto the roof using a ladder.
消防士(しょうぼうし)が梯子(はしご)車(しゃ)で救助(きゅうじょ)に向(む)かった。
The firefighters headed to the rescue in a ladder truck.
昨夜(ゆうべ)は友達(ともだち)と梯子(はしご)酒(ざけ)をした。
Last night I went bar-hopping with friends.
梯子(はしご)酒(ざけ) = bar-hopping (literally 'ladder drinking')

Notes

梯子(はしご) refers to ladders of various types. The kanji 梯子(はしご) is often written in hiragana.

Common expressions:

  • 梯子(はしご)を登(のぼ)る／降(お)りる (to climb up/down a ladder)
  • 梯子(はしご)を掛(か)ける (to lean a ladder [against something])
  • 梯子(はしご)車(しゃ) (ladder truck, fire engine)
  • 梯子(はしご)酒(ざけ) (bar-hopping)

Idiomatic expression:

  • 梯子(はしご)を外(はず)す (to pull the ladder out from under someone - to withdraw support after leading someone on)
