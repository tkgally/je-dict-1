葉(は)

は
noun
leaf, leaves

Definitions

1. leaf, leaves
The flat green parts of a plant.

Examples

葉(は)が落(お)ちる。
Leaves are falling.
紅葉(もみじ)の葉(は)が赤(あか)い。
The maple leaves are red.

Notes

葉(は) means leaf.

SEASONS:

  • 新緑(しんりょく): fresh green leaves (spring)
  • 紅葉(こうよう): autumn leaves
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば): fallen leaves

COMPOUNDS:

  • 葉書(はがき): postcard
  • 言葉(ことば): word, language
  • 青葉(あおば): green leaves
