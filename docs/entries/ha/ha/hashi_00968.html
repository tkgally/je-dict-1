橋(はし)

はし
noun
bridge

Definitions

1. bridge
A structure built to span a physical obstacle such as a river or road.

Examples

橋(はし)を渡(わた)る。
To cross a bridge.
川(かわ)に橋(はし)がかかっている。
A bridge spans the river.

Notes

橋(はし) means bridge.

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:

  • 橋(はし): bridge
  • 箸(はし): chopsticks
  • 端(はし): edge, end

These are distinguished by pitch accent.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 石橋(いしばし): stone bridge
  • 歩道橋(ほどうきょう): pedestrian bridge
  • 吊り橋(つりばし): suspension bridge
