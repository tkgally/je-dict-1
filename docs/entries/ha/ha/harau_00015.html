払(はら)う

はらう
verb (godan)
to pay

Definitions

1. to pay
To give money in exchange for goods or services.

Examples

クレジットカードで払(はら)えますか？
Can I pay by credit card?
Potential form
電気代(でんきだい)を払(はら)いました。
I paid the electricity bill.
現金(げんきん)で払(はら)います。
I'll pay in cash.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを払(はら)う (pay X).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed state. 払(はら)っている = 'have paid' or 'am paying.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お金(かね)を払(はら)う (pay money)
  • 家賃(やちん)を払(はら)う (pay rent)
  • 現金(げんきん)で払(はら)う (pay in cash)
  • 払(はら)い戻(もど)し (refund)

Related:

  • 支払(しはら)う (pay - more formal)
  • 支払(しはら)い (payment).
