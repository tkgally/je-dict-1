灰皿(はいざら)

はいざら
noun
ashtray

Definitions

1. ashtray
A container for cigarette ash and butts.

Examples

灰皿(はいざら)はどこですか。
Where is the ashtray?
灰皿(はいざら)を空(から)にする。
To empty the ashtray.
テーブルに灰皿(はいざら)がある。
There is an ashtray on the table.

Notes

灰皿(はいざら) is used for cigarette ash.

COMPONENTS:

  • 灰(はい) (ash)
  • 皿(さら) (plate, dish)

RELATED WORDS:

  • たばこ (cigarette)
  • 喫煙(きつえん) (smoking)
  • 禁煙(きんえん) (no smoking)

NOTE: Less common now due to reduced smoking and smoking bans in many places.
