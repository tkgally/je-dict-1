省(はぶ)く

はぶく
godan verb
to omit, to eliminate

Definitions

1. to omit, to leave out
To not include something.
2. to reduce, to cut down
To decrease effort, cost, or time.

Examples

詳細(しょうさい)は省く。
I'll omit the details.
手間(てま)を省く。
To save effort.

Notes

省く is a transitive verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手間(てま)を省く: to save time/effort
  • 無駄(むだ)を省く: to cut waste
  • 説明(せつめい)を省く: to skip explanations

RELATED:

  • 省略(しょうりゃく)する: to abbreviate
