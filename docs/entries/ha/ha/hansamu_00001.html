ハンサム

はんさむ
na-adjective; noun
handsome

Definitions

1. handsome
Describing a man who is good-looking, with refined or attractive features.

Examples

彼(かれ)はハンサムな男性(だんせい)だ。
He is a handsome man.
ハンサムな俳優(はいゆう)
a handsome actor
若(わか)い頃(ころ)はハンサムだった。
He was handsome when he was young.

Notes

ハンサム describes a man with refined, attractive facial features. It is a somewhat dated term, less commonly used by younger generations.

USAGE NOTE:
ハンサム is typically used for men only. For women, use 美(うつく)しい, きれい, or 美人(びじん).

MODERN ALTERNATIVES:

  • イケメン is the modern slang for "handsome man" (very common)
  • かっこいい describes both appearance and general coolness
  • 二枚目(にまいめ) is a theatrical term for "leading man type"

ADJECTIVE FORMS:

  • ハンサムな + noun (attributive)
  • ハンサムだ (predicative)

Related Words

Related: かっこいい (cool/handsome)
