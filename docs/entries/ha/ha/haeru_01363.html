生(は)える

はえる
ichidan verb
to grow, to sprout

Definitions

1. to grow, to sprout
For plants, hair, or teeth to emerge and develop.

Examples

草(くさ)が生えている。
Grass is growing.
歯(は)が生えてきた。
Teeth have started to come in.

Notes

生える is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 生(は)やす (transitive): to grow (beard, etc.)
  • 生える (intransitive): to grow

HOMOPHONE:

  • 映(は)える: to look good, to shine
