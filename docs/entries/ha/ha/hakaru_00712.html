測(はか)る

はかる
verb (godan)
to measure

Definitions

1. to measure
To determine size, quantity, or degree.

Examples

長(なが)さを測(はか)る。
To measure the length.
体温(たいおん)を測(はか)る。
To take one's temperature.
時間(じかん)を測(はか)る。
To measure time.

Notes

測(はか)る means to measure.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 測(はか)る: to measure (physical)
  • 計(はか)る: to plan, measure
  • 量(はか)る: to weigh
  • 図(はか)る: to attempt

RELATED:

  • 測定(そくてい): measurement
  • 計測(けいそく): measurement
