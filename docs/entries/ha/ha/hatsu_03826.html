〜発(はつ)

はつ
suffix, counter
departing from; counter for shots

Definitions

1. departing from
Suffix indicating the point of departure for transportation.
2. counter for shots/bullets
Used to count gunshots, fireworks, or other projectiles fired.

Examples

東京(とうきょう)発(はつ)の電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)る。
To board a train departing from Tokyo.
10時(じゅうじ)発(はつ)のバスはもう出(で)ました。
The 10 o'clock bus has already left.
2発(にはつ)の銃声(じゅうせい)が聞(き)こえた。
Two gunshots were heard.

Notes

USAGE 1 - DEPARTURE:

  • Place + 発(はつ) = departing from [place]
  • Time + 発(はつ) = departing at [time]
  • Often paired with 着(ちゃく) (arriving)

EXAMPLES:

  • 東京(とうきょう)発(はつ)大阪(おおさか)着(ちゃく) (departing Tokyo, arriving Osaka)
  • 9時(くじ)発(はつ) (departing at 9:00)

USAGE 2 - COUNTER:

  • 一(いっ)発(ぱつ) (1 shot)
  • 二(に)発(はつ) (2 shots)
  • 花火(はなび)を10発(じゅっぱつ)打(う)ち上(あ)げる (to launch 10 fireworks)

NOTE: As a counter, the reading changes: いっぱつ, にはつ, さんぱつ...

Related Words

Antonym: 〜着(ちゃく) (arriving at)
