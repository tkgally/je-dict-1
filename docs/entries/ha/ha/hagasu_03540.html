剥(は)がす

はがす
verb (godan)
to peel off; to tear off; to strip

Definitions

1. to peel off; to tear off
To remove something that is stuck or attached to a surface.
2. to strip; to remove (covering)
To take off a layer or coating from something.

Examples

シールを剥(は)がした。
I peeled off the sticker.
Sense 1: removing something stuck
壁紙(かべがみ)を剥(は)がすのは大変(たいへん)だった。
Stripping the wallpaper was difficult.
Sense 2: removing a covering
彼(かれ)の仮面(かめん)を剥(は)がしてやった。
I exposed his true colors.
[Note: figuratively 'unmasking' someone]

Notes

剥(は)がす is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 剥(は)がれる (to come off, to peel off).

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form indicates ongoing action (今(いま)シールを剥(は)がしている = 'is peeling off the sticker now').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]を剥(は)がす (peel/tear off ~)
  • [surface]から剥(は)がす (peel off from ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シールを剥(は)がす (peel off a sticker)
  • 壁紙(かべがみ)を剥(は)がす (strip wallpaper)
  • 皮(かわ)を剥(は)がす (peel off skin/bark)
  • 仮面(かめん)を剥(は)がす (unmask; expose someone)

Related Words

Pair verb: 剥(は)がれる (intransitive)
