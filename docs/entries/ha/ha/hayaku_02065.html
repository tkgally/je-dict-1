早(はや)く

はやく
adverb
early, quickly, soon

Definitions

1. early
At an early time.
2. quickly, fast
At a rapid pace.
3. soon
In the near future.

Examples

早(はや)く起(お)きてください。
Please wake up early.
もっと早(はや)く歩(ある)いて。
Walk faster.
早(はや)く元気(げんき)になってね。
Get well soon.

Notes

早(はや)く is the adverb form of 早(はや)い.

BASE ADJECTIVE:

  • 早(はや)い (early, fast)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 早(はや)く起(お)きる (to wake up early)
  • 早(はや)く帰(かえ)る (to go home early)
  • できるだけ早(はや)く (as soon as possible)

DISTINCTION:

  • 早(はや)い can mean both "early" and "fast"
  • 速(はや)い specifically means "fast" (speed)
