早起(はやお)き

はやおき
noun
early rising, getting up early

Definitions

1. early rising, waking up early
The act of waking up early in the morning. Can also describe a person who habitually wakes up early. Functions as a noun and as a suru-verb.

Examples

早起(はやお)きは三文(さんもん)の徳(とく)。
The early bird catches the worm.
Famous proverb (lit. 'early rising is worth three mon')
明日(あした)は早起(はやお)きしなければならない。
I have to wake up early tomorrow.
Used as suru-verb
彼(かれ)は早起(はやお)きだ。
He's an early riser.
Describes a person

Notes

Can be used as a noun (早起(はやお)きが得意(とくい) = good at getting up early) or with する (早起(はやお)きする = to wake up early).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 早起(はやお)きする (wake up early)
  • 早起(はやお)きは三文(さんもん)の徳(とく) (proverb: early rising is beneficial)
  • 早起(はやお)きが苦手(にがて) (bad at getting up early)

Opposite: 朝寝坊(あさねぼう) (oversleeping, late riser)

Related Words

Antonym: 朝寝坊(あさねぼう) (oversleeping)
Related: 速(はや)い／早(はや)い (fast, early)
