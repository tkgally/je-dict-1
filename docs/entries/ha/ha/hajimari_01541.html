始(はじ)まり

はじまり
noun
beginning, start

Definitions

1. beginning, start, origin
The point at which something begins. Noun form of the verb 始(はじ)まる (to begin). Can refer to the start of events, stories, relationships, or abstract concepts.

Examples

物語(ものがたり)の始(はじ)まりです。
This is the beginning of the story.
始(はじ)まりがあれば終(お)わりがある。
Everything that has a beginning has an end.
Proverb-like expression
それが二人(ふたり)の友情(ゆうじょう)の始(はじ)まりだった。
That was the beginning of their friendship.

Notes

Noun form of the intransitive verb 始(はじ)まる (to begin). Compare with 始(はじ)め, which often means 'the beginning' as a point in time or 'at first.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物語(ものがたり)の始(はじ)まり (beginning of a story)
  • 新(あたら)しい始(はじ)まり (new beginning)
  • 全(すべ)ての始(はじ)まり (the beginning of everything)
  • 始(はじ)まりから終(お)わりまで (from start to finish)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 始(はじ)め - beginning, first (often temporal: 初(はじ)めに = at first)
  • 始(はじ)まる - to begin (intransitive verb)
  • 始(はじ)める - to start (transitive verb)
