早(はや)まる

はやまる
verb (godan)
to be brought forward; to quicken; to be hasty

Definitions

1. to be brought forward; to happen earlier
When a scheduled event or time occurs earlier than originally planned.
2. to quicken; to accelerate
When a pace, heartbeat, or process speeds up.
3. to be hasty; to act rashly
To make a premature or impulsive decision.

Examples

会議(かいぎ)の開始(かいし)時間(じかん)が早(はや)まった。
The meeting start time was moved earlier.
緊張(きんちょう)で脈(みゃく)が早(はや)まった。
My pulse quickened from nervousness.
早(はや)まったことをするな。
Don't do anything rash.

Notes

早(はや)まる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 早(はや)める (to hasten, to bring forward).

ASPECT ({ている}):
早(はや)まっている can indicate an ongoing quickened state or a resulting change in timing.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予定(よてい)が早(はや)まる - schedule is moved earlier
  • 脈(みゃく)/心拍(しんぱく)が早(はや)まる - pulse/heartbeat quickens
  • 早(はや)まった行動(こうどう) - hasty action
  • 早(はや)まるな - don't be hasty/rash

USAGE NOTE:
In the sense of "being hasty," 早(はや)まる often carries a negative nuance, implying an impulsive or regrettable action.
