ハンカチ

はんかち
noun
handkerchief

Definitions

1. handkerchief
A small cloth used for wiping hands or face.

Examples

ハンカチを持(も)っていますか。
Do you have a handkerchief?
ハンカチで汗(あせ)を拭(ふ)く。
To wipe sweat with a handkerchief.
ハンカチを忘(わす)れた。
I forgot my handkerchief.

Notes

ハンカチ is from English "handkerchief" (shortened).

FULL FORM:

  • ハンカチーフ (less commonly used)

COMMON USE:

  • Essential item carried by many Japanese people
  • Used for drying hands (many public restrooms have no paper towels)
  • Also used for wiping sweat in summer

RELATED:

  • ティッシュ (tissue paper)
  • タオル (towel)
