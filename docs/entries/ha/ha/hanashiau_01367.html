話(はな)し合(あ)う

はなしあう
godan verb
to discuss, to talk together

Definitions

1. to discuss, to talk over
To have a conversation to reach understanding or agreement.

Examples

よく話し合いましょう。
Let's discuss it thoroughly.
問題(もんだい)について話し合う。
To discuss a problem.

Notes

話し合う is a compound verb (話す + 合う).

The 〜合う pattern indicates mutual action.

RELATED NOUN:

  • 話(はな)し合(あ)い: discussion, talk

SIMILAR:

  • 相談(そうだん)する: to consult
  • 議論(ぎろん)する: to debate
