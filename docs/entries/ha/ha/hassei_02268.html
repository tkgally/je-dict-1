発生(はっせい)

はっせい
noun
occurrence, outbreak, generation

Definitions

1. occurrence, outbreak
Something (usually negative like an accident, disaster, or problem) happening or breaking out.
2. generation, production
The production or creation of something (heat, electricity, gas, etc.).

Examples

事故(じこ)が発生(はっせい)した。
An accident occurred.
Sense 1
火災(かさい)の発生(はっせい)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent the outbreak of fire.
熱(ねつ)が発生(はっせい)する。
Heat is generated.
Sense 2

Notes

COMPOUND: 発(はつ) (emit, occur) + 生(せい) (life, birth)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)が発生(はっせい)する (an accident occurs)
  • 問題(もんだい)が発生(はっせい)する (a problem arises)
  • 火災(かさい)/地震(じしん)が発生(はっせい)する (fire/earthquake occurs)
  • 電力(でんりょく)を発生(はっせい)させる (generate electricity)
  • 発生(はっせい)源(げん) (source of occurrence)
  • 発生(はっせい)率(りつ) (rate of occurrence)

OFTEN USED FOR:

  • Accidents, disasters, problems (negative events)
  • Scientific contexts (generation of heat, electricity, etc.)
