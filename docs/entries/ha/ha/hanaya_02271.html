花屋(はなや)

はなや
noun
flower shop, florist

Definitions

1. flower shop, florist
A shop that sells flowers and floral arrangements. Can also refer to the person who owns or works at such a shop.

Examples

花屋(はなや)で薔薇(ばら)を買(か)った。
I bought roses at the flower shop.
駅前(えきまえ)に花屋(はなや)がある。
There is a flower shop in front of the station.
彼女(かのじょ)は花屋(はなや)で働(はたら)いている。
She works at a florist.

Notes

COMPOUND: 花(はな) (flower) + 屋(や) (shop)

COMMON SHOP NAMES WITH 屋(や):

  • 本屋(ほんや) (bookstore)
  • 肉屋(にくや) (butcher shop)
  • 魚屋(さかなや) (fish shop)
  • 八百屋(やおや) (vegetable shop)
  • パン屋(や) (bakery)

RELATED:

  • 花束(はなたば) (bouquet)
  • 生(い)け花(ばな) (flower arrangement, ikebana)
  • 花(はな)を生(い)ける (to arrange flowers)
