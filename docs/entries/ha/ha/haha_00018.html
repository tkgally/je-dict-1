母(はは)

はは
noun
mother (humble)

Definitions

1. mother (my mother)
Humble term used when referring to one's own mother when speaking to others outside the family.

Examples

母(はは)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)です。
My mother is good at cooking.
母(はは)は教師(きょうし)です。
My mother is a teacher.
母(はは)の日(ひ)にプレゼントをあげました。
I gave a present on Mother's Day.
母の日 = Mother's Day

Notes

Use 母(はは) when speaking about your own mother to others. Use お母(かあ)さん when addressing your mother directly or speaking about someone else's mother.

Related: 父(ちち) (father - humble).
