ぺたぺた

ぺたぺた
adverb
sticking; pattering (footsteps)

Definitions

1. sticking; pasting repeatedly
The action or sound of sticking things on surfaces.
2. pattering; padding (footsteps)
The sound of light, flat footsteps, like walking barefoot.

Examples

シールをぺたぺた貼(は)る。
To stick on stickers one after another.
子供(こども)がぺたぺたと歩(ある)いている。
The child is padding along with bare feet.
手(て)がぺたぺたする。
My hands feel sticky.

Notes

ぺたぺた has two main uses: the action/sound of sticking things, and the sound of light barefoot walking.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • べたべた: sticky (more intensely sticky)
  • ぺたんと: flat, with a slap
  • ぱたぱた: pattering (lighter, flapping)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぺたぺた貼る: to stick on repeatedly
  • ぺたぺた歩く: to walk with padding footsteps
