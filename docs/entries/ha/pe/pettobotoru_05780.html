ペットボトル

ぺっとぼとる
noun
plastic bottle; PET bottle

Definitions

1. plastic bottle; PET bottle
A container made from polyethylene terephthalate (PET) plastic, commonly used for beverages.

Examples

ペットボトルをリサイクル(りさいくる)する。
To recycle plastic bottles.
ペットボトルの水(みず)を買(か)う。
To buy bottled water.
ペットボトルのキャップを外(はず)す。
To remove the cap from a plastic bottle.

Notes

ペットボトル is a Japanese-English compound (PET + bottle). PET stands for polyethylene terephthalate.

RECYCLING:
In Japan, PET bottles must be rinsed, labels removed, and caps separated for recycling.
