振(ふ)り

ふり
noun / suffix
manner, pretense; after (time)

Definitions

1. manner, way, style
The way something is done or appears.
2. pretense, act
Pretending or feigning something.
3. after (a period of time)
Used as a suffix to indicate the first time in a while.

Examples

知(し)らない振(ふ)りをした。
I pretended not to know.
Common expression
3年(ねん)振(ぶ)りに故郷(こきょう)に帰(かえ)った。
I returned to my hometown for the first time in three years.
Suffix usage indicating time elapsed
寝(ね)た振(ふ)りをして様子(ようす)を窺(うかが)った。
I pretended to be asleep and observed the situation.

Notes

Has multiple related meanings centered on appearance/manner.

MEANING 1 - MANNER:

  • 身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)り (gestures)
  • 振(ふ)る舞(ま)い (behavior, conduct)

MEANING 2 - PRETENSE:

  • 〜振(ふ)りをする (to pretend to ~)
  • 知(し)らん振(ぷ)り (pretending not to know)
  • 見(み)て見(み)ぬ振(ふ)り (pretending not to see)

MEANING 3 - TIME SUFFIX:

  • 〜振(ぶ)り (for the first time in ~)
  • 3年(ねん)振(ぶ)り (for the first time in 3 years)
  • 久(ひさ)し振(ぶ)り (after a long time)

NOTE:
As a time suffix, it's often read ぶり rather than ふり.

Related Words

Related: 久(ひさ)し振(ぶ)り (after a long time)
