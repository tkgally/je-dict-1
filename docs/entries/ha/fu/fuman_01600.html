不満(ふまん)

ふまん
na-adjective
dissatisfaction

Definitions

1. dissatisfaction, discontent, complaint
A feeling of not being satisfied or pleased with something.

Examples

給料(きゅうりょう)に不満(ふまん)がある。
I'm dissatisfied with my salary.
不満(ふまん)を言(い)う。
To complain.
不満(ふまん)そうな顔(かお)をしている。
They have a dissatisfied look on their face.

Notes

Functions as a na-adjective and noun. Formed by adding the negative prefix 不(ふ) to 満足(まんぞく).

FORMS:

  • 不満(ふまん)な + noun (不満(ふまん)な表情(ひょうじょう) = dissatisfied expression)
  • 不満(ふまん)がある (have dissatisfaction)
  • 不満(ふまん)を言(い)う (to complain)
  • 〜に不満(ふまん)だ (be dissatisfied with ~)

Related Words

Antonym: 満足(まんぞく) (satisfaction)
