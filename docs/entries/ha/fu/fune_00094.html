船(ふね)

ふね
noun
boat, ship

Definitions

1. boat, ship, vessel
A watercraft used for transportation on water. Includes small boats and large ships.

Examples

船(ふね)で島(しま)に行(い)きます。
I'm going to the island by boat.
大(おお)きい船(ふね)が見(み)えます。
I can see a big ship.
船(ふね)に乗(の)ると、酔(よ)います。
I get seasick when I ride a boat.
酔う means to get drunk/sick from motion

Notes

船(ふね) can refer to any size of watercraft. 船酔(ふなよ)い is 'seasickness'. For larger ships, the reading can be 船(せん) in compounds like 客船(きゃくせん) (passenger ship). 港(みなと) means 'port/harbor'.
