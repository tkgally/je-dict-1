振(ふ)る

ふる
verb (godan)
to wave, to shake

Definitions

1. to wave, to swing
To move something back and forth.
2. to shake, to sprinkle
To scatter by shaking.

Examples

手(て)を振(ふ)る。
To wave one's hand.
頭(あたま)を振(ふ)る。
To shake one's head.
塩(しお)を振(ふ)る。
To sprinkle salt.

Notes

振(ふ)る means to wave or shake.

COMMON USES:

  • 手(て)を振(ふ)る: to wave
  • 首(くび)を振(ふ)る: to shake one's head
  • 塩(しお)を振(ふ)る: to sprinkle salt

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 振(ふ)る: to wave, shake
  • 降(ふ)る: to fall (rain/snow)
