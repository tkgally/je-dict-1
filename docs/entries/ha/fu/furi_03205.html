不利(ふり)

ふり
noun, na-adjective
disadvantage

Definitions

1. disadvantage, drawback
A condition or circumstance that puts one in a worse position.

Examples

この条件(じょうけん)は我々(われわれ)に不利(ふり)だ。
These conditions are disadvantageous to us.
不利(ふり)な立場(たちば)に立(た)たされた。
I was put in a disadvantageous position.
被告(ひこく)に不利(ふり)な証拠(しょうこ)が出(で)てきた。
Evidence unfavorable to the defendant came out.

Notes

USAGE: 不利(ふり) describes situations, conditions, or evidence that work against someone's interests.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 不利(ふり)な条件(じょうけん) (unfavorable conditions)
  • 不利(ふり)な立場(たちば) (disadvantageous position)
  • 〜に不利(ふり)だ (to be disadvantageous to ~)
  • 不利益(ふりえき) (disadvantage, harm)

ANTONYM: 有利(ゆうり) (advantageous)

Related Words

Antonym: 有利(ゆうり) (advantageous)
