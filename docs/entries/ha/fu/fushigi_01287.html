不思議(ふしぎ)

ふしぎ
na-adjective, noun
strange, mysterious

Definitions

1. strange, mysterious
Difficult to explain or understand.
2. wonder, miracle
Something surprising or inexplicable (noun).

Examples

不思議な話(はなし)だ。
It's a strange story.
不思議に思(おも)う。
I find it strange.

Notes

不思議 implies wonder or puzzlement.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 不思議なことに: strangely enough
  • 不思議に思(おも)う: to find strange

CULTURAL NOTE:
不思議(ふしぎ)の国(くに)のアリス = Alice in Wonderland
