ふわふわ

ふわふわ
adverb
fluffy; soft; light

Definitions

1. fluffy; soft and light
Describes something with a soft, airy texture that feels pleasant to touch or has a light, floating quality.
2. floating lightly; drifting
Describes a light, floating or drifting motion, as if suspended in air.

Examples

ふわふわのパンケーキを作(つく)った。
I made fluffy pancakes.
この毛布(もうふ)はふわふわで気持(きも)ちいい。
This blanket is soft and feels nice.
風船(ふうせん)がふわふわと空(そら)に浮(う)かんでいる。
The balloon is floating lightly in the sky.

Notes

ふわふわ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) that evokes a soft, airy feeling.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ふわふわの〜: fluffy/soft (noun modifier)
  • ふわふわする: to feel soft and fluffy
  • ふわふわと浮(う)かぶ: to float lightly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • もこもこ: fluffy, puffy (thicker, puffier texture)
  • ふかふか: soft and cushy (like a mattress or cushion)
