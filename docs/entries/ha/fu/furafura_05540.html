ふらふら

ふらふら
adverb
unsteady; wobbly; aimlessly

Definitions

1. unsteady; wobbly; dizzy
Describes an unstable state, either physically or mentally.
2. aimlessly; without purpose
Wandering around without a clear destination.

Examples

熱(ねつ)でふらふらする。
I feel dizzy from the fever.
疲(つか)れてふらふらだ。
I'm so tired I can barely stand.
街(まち)をふらふら歩(ある)く。
To wander aimlessly around the town.

Notes

ふらふら can describe physical unsteadiness (dizziness) or purposeless wandering.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • よろよろ: tottering (from weakness/old age)
  • ぐらぐら: wobbling (more for objects)
  • うろうろ: wandering around (often negatively)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ふらふらする: to feel dizzy/unsteady
  • ふらふら歩く: to walk unsteadily/aimlessly
