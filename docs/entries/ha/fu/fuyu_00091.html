冬(ふゆ)

ふゆ
noun
winter

Definitions

1. winter
The coldest season of the year, typically from December to February in Japan.

Examples

冬(ふゆ)は寒(さむ)いです。
Winter is cold.
冬(ふゆ)に雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)ります。
It snows in winter.
冬(ふゆ)は鍋(なべ)がおいしいです。
Hot pot is delicious in winter.

Notes

冬 is associated with New Year (正月), skiing, hot springs, and warm foods like nabe (hot pot).

Related:

  • 冬休み (fuyuyasumi, winter vacation)
  • 冬至 (touji, winter solstice)
  • 真冬 (mafuyu, midwinter).
