筆(ふで)

ふで
noun
writing brush

Definitions

1. writing brush
A traditional brush used for writing or painting with ink, especially in East Asian calligraphy.
2. writing, penmanship
By extension, one's skill or style in writing.

Examples

筆(ふで)で漢字(かんじ)を書(か)く。
To write kanji with a brush.
祖父(そふ)は筆(ふで)が達者(たっしゃ)だ。
My grandfather is skilled at brush writing.
筆が達者 = skilled at calligraphy
書道(しょどう)では筆(ふで)の持(も)ち方(かた)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
In calligraphy, how you hold the brush is important.

Notes

USAGE: 筆(ふで) is primarily used for traditional writing brushes in calligraphy, but also extends to writing ability.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 筆(ふで)を執(と)る (to take up the pen, to start writing)
  • 筆(ふで)を置(お)く (to put down the pen, to stop writing)
  • 筆(ふで)が立(た)つ (to write well)
  • 筆(ふで)不精(ぶしょう) (reluctant to write letters)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 毛筆(もうひつ) (brush writing)
  • 鉛筆(えんぴつ) (pencil)
  • 筆記(ひっき) (writing, taking notes)
