吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす

ふきとばす
verb (godan)
to blow away; to blow off; to dispel

Definitions

1. to blow away; to blow off
To cause something to fly away by blowing.
2. to dispel; to drive away
Figuratively, to get rid of negative feelings or atmosphere.
3. to blow wide open; to blast
To destroy or break something apart with force.

Examples

強風(きょうふう)が屋根(やね)を吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばした。
The strong wind blew off the roof.
憂(ゆう)うつな気分(きぶん)を吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばそう。
Let's blow away this gloomy feeling.
爆発(ばくはつ)で建物(たてもの)の一部(いちぶ)が吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばされた。
Part of the building was blown apart by the explosion.

Notes

吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive form is 吹(ふ)き飛(と)ぶ (to be blown away).

ASPECT ({ている}):
吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばしている indicates an ongoing action of blowing something away.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 風(かぜ)が[noun]を吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす - wind blows [noun] away
  • 悩(なや)み/ストレス}を{吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす - to blow away worries/stress
  • 眠(ねむ)気|け}を吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす - to shake off sleepiness

COMPOUND VERB STRUCTURE:
吹(ふ)く (to blow) + 飛(と)ばす (to make fly) = 吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす

FIGURATIVE USE:
Commonly used to describe getting rid of negative emotions or states, especially in the phrase [feeling]を吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす.
