復習(ふくしゅう)

ふくしゅう
noun, verb (suru)
review, revision

Definitions

1. review, revision
Going over previously learned material again to reinforce understanding and memory.

Examples

毎日(まいにち)復習(ふくしゅう)しています。
I review every day.
試験(しけん)の前(まえ)に復習(ふくしゅう)しなければなりません。
I have to review before the exam.
今日(きょう)の授業(じゅぎょう)を復習(ふくしゅう)しよう。
Let's review today's lesson.

Notes

復習(ふくしゅう) means reviewing what you've already learned.

LEARNING CYCLE:

  • 予習(よしゅう): preparation before class
  • 授業(じゅぎょう): class/lesson
  • 復習(ふくしゅう): review after class

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 復習(ふくしゅう)する: to review
  • 復習(ふくしゅう)を忘(わす)れない: don't forget to review
  • 〜を復習(ふくしゅう)する: to review ~

OPPOSITE: 予習(よしゅう) (preparation/preview)

Note: Don't confuse with 復讐(ふくしゅう) (revenge) which uses different kanji but same reading.

Related Words

Antonym: 予習(よしゅう) (preparation/preview)
