服(ふく)

ふく
noun
clothes, clothing

Definitions

1. clothes, clothing
Garments worn on the body. A general term for articles of clothing.

Examples

新(あたら)しい服(ふく)を買(か)いました。
I bought new clothes.
この服(ふく)は高(たか)いです。
These clothes are expensive.
服(ふく)を着(き)替|が}えてください。
Please change your clothes.

Notes

A general term for clothing. Types include: 洋服(ようふく) (Western clothes), 和服(わふく) (Japanese clothes/kimono), 制服(せいふく) (uniform). Uses 着(き)る (to wear) and 脱(ぬ)ぐ (to take off). Counter: 着(ちゃく) or 枚(まい).
