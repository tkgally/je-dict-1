不可能(ふかのう)

ふかのう
noun
impossible, impossibility

Definitions

1. impossible, impossibility
Something that cannot be done or achieved; the state of being impossible.

Examples

それは不可能(ふかのう)です。
That is impossible.
一日(いちにち)で終(お)わらせるのは不可能(ふかのう)だ。
It's impossible to finish it in one day.
不可能(ふかのう)を可能(かのう)にする。
To make the impossible possible.

Notes

COMPOUND: 不(ふ) (not) + 可能(かのう) (possible)

USAGE:

  • 不可能(ふかのう)だ/です (it's impossible)
  • 不可能(ふかのう)な + Noun (impossible ~)
  • 不可能(ふかのう)に近(ちか)い (close to impossible)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 可能(かのう) (possible) - antonym
  • できない (cannot do)
  • 無理(むり) (impossible, unreasonable)

NUANCE: 不可能(ふかのう) is more formal and absolute than 無理(むり), which can mean 'unreasonable' or 'too much to ask' in addition to 'impossible.'

Related Words

Antonym: 可能(かのう)
Related: 無理(むり)
