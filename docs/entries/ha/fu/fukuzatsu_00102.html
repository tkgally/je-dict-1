複雑(ふくざつ)

ふくざつ
na-adjective
complicated, complex

Definitions

1. complicated, complex
Having many interconnected parts or aspects, difficult to understand or analyze.

Examples

この問題(もんだい)は複雑(ふくざつ)です。
This problem is complicated.
日本語(にほんご)の文法(ぶんぽう)は複雑(ふくざつ)だと思(おも)いますか。
Do you think Japanese grammar is complicated?
彼(かれ)は複雑(ふくざつ)な気持(きも)ちになった。
He had complicated feelings.
Describing emotional complexity

Notes

複雑 is a na-adjective: 複雑な (before nouns), 複雑だ/です (predicate), 複雑じゃない (negative). The kanji 複 means 'duplicate/multiple' and 雑 means 'miscellaneous' - together suggesting many mixed elements. Antonym: 簡単 (かんたん - simple), 単純 (たんじゅん - simple).
