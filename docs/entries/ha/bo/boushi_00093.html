帽子(ぼうし)

ぼうし
noun
hat, cap

Definitions

1. hat, cap
A covering for the head, worn for protection from sun or cold, or as a fashion accessory.

Examples

帽子(ぼうし)をかぶってください。
Please put on a hat.
赤(あか)い帽子(ぼうし)が好(す)きです。
I like red hats.
帽子(ぼうし)を脱(ぬ)いでください。
Please take off your hat.

Notes

Uses the verb かぶる (to wear on head) and 脱(ぬ)ぐ (to take off). Counter: 個(こ). Types include: 野球帽(ぼう) (baseball cap), 麦わら帽子(ぼうし) (straw hat).
