ボウル

ぼうる
noun
bowl (for cooking)

Definitions

1. bowl (for cooking); mixing bowl
bowl (for cooking); mixing bowl

Examples

ボウルに卵(たまご)を割(わ)り入(い)れて混(ま)ぜた。
I cracked eggs into a bowl and mixed them.
大(おお)きなボウルでサラダを和(あ)えた。
I tossed the salad in a large bowl.
ステンレスのボウルは軽(かる)くて割(わ)れない。
Stainless steel bowls are light and won't break.

Notes

ボウル (from English "bowl") refers to deep cooking bowls used for mixing, preparing, and serving food. In Japanese, it specifically refers to kitchen bowls rather than eating bowls.

Common types:

  • ステンレスボウル (stainless steel bowl)
  • ガラスボウル (glass bowl)
  • プラスチックボウル (plastic bowl)

Note: For eating bowls, Japanese uses 茶碗(ちゃわん) (rice bowl), 丼(どんぶり) (large bowl for rice dishes), or 汁椀(しるわん) (soup bowl).
