ボールペン

ぼーるぺん
noun
ballpoint pen

Definitions

1. ballpoint pen
A pen with a small rotating ball at the tip that dispenses ink. A common writing instrument for everyday use.

Examples

ボールペンで書(か)いてください。
Please write with a ballpoint pen.
ボールペンを持(も)っていますか。
Do you have a ballpoint pen?
黒(くろ)いボールペンがありますか。
Do you have a black ballpoint pen?

Notes

A loanword from English 'ball pen' (shortened from 'ballpoint pen'). Pronounced with a long 'o' sound. Counter: 本(ほん). Different from 万年筆(まんねんひつ) (fountain pen) and 鉛筆(えんぴつ) (pencil).
