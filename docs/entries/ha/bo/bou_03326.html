棒(ぼう)

ぼう
noun
stick, pole, rod

Definitions

1. stick, pole, rod
A long, thin piece of wood or other material.
2. line, bar
A straight line drawn on paper.

Examples

棒(ぼう)で地面(じめん)に線(せん)を引(ひ)いた。
I drew a line on the ground with a stick.
鉄(てつ)棒(ぼう)にぶら下(さ)がる。
To hang from an iron bar.
棒グラフ(ぼうぐらふ)を作成(さくせい)する。
To create a bar graph.

Notes

A basic word with many compound uses.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 鉄棒(てつぼう) (horizontal bar - gymnastics)
  • 棒(ぼう)グラフ (bar graph)
  • 泥棒(どろぼう) (thief)
  • 相棒(あいぼう) (partner, sidekick)
  • 棒読(ぼうよ)み (monotone reading)
  • 棒(ぼう)に振(ふ)る (to waste, throw away)

IDIOMS:

  • 棒(ぼう)に振(ふ)る (to waste something - effort, money)
  • 犬(いぬ)も歩(ある)けば棒(ぼう)に当(あ)たる (A dog that walks will bump into a stick - you'll encounter fortune/misfortune if you go out)
