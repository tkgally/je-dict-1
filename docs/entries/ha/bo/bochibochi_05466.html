ぼちぼち

ぼちぼち
adverb
so-so; little by little; soon

Definitions

1. so-so; not bad; getting by
Used to describe a moderate or acceptable state, neither good nor bad.
2. little by little; gradually
Doing something at a slow, steady pace.
3. soon; about time
Indicating that it's about time to do something.

Examples

調子(ちょうし)はどう？ぼちぼちだね。
How are you doing? So-so.
ぼちぼち仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めようか。
Shall we start working soon?
ぼちぼち帰(かえ)ろうか。
Shall we head home soon?

Notes

ぼちぼち is a casual expression with multiple nuances.

REGIONAL NOTE:

  • Particularly common in Kansai dialect
  • Standard Japanese equivalent: まあまあ (so-so)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 調子(ちょうし)はぼちぼち: doing okay
  • ぼちぼち行(い)こうか: shall we get going?
  • ぼちぼちやっている: getting by, managing
