パイプ

ぱいぷ
noun
pipe

Definitions

1. pipe; tube
A hollow cylinder for conveying liquids, gases, or other substances.
2. smoking pipe
A tube with a bowl at one end for smoking tobacco.
3. channel; connection
A means of communication or connection between parties.

Examples

水道(すいどう)パイプが破裂(はれつ)した。
The water pipe burst.
パイプを吸(す)う。
To smoke a pipe.
政治家(せいじか)とのパイプがある。
To have connections with politicians.

Notes

パイプ has three main uses from the English word "pipe."

PIPE/TUBE USAGE:

  • 水道(すいどう)パイプ (water pipe)
  • 排水(はいすい)パイプ (drainage pipe)
  • ガスパイプ (gas pipe)
  • パイプライン (pipeline)

SMOKING PIPE:

  • パイプを吸(す)う (to smoke a pipe)
  • パイプタバコ (pipe tobacco)

CONNECTION/CHANNEL (figurative):

  • パイプがある (to have connections/channels)
  • パイプ役(やく) (intermediary; go-between)
  • This usage often implies unofficial or behind-the-scenes connections
