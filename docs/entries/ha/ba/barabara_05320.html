ばらばら

ばらばら
adverb
scattered; in pieces; disjointed

Definitions

1. scattered; in pieces
Describes things that are separated, scattered, or not together.
2. varied; inconsistent
Describes opinions, times, or qualities that differ from each other.

Examples

書類(しょるい)がばらばらになっている。
The documents are scattered.
家族(かぞく)はばらばらに住(す)んでいる。
The family members live separately.
意見(いけん)がばらばらで決(き)まらない。
We can't decide because opinions vary so much.

Notes

ばらばら is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) describing separation or inconsistency.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ばらばらになる: to become scattered/separated
  • ばらばらに住(す)む: to live separately
  • 意見(いけん)がばらばら: opinions are divided

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • めちゃくちゃ: messed up, chaotic
  • ぐちゃぐちゃ: jumbled, mixed up
