不成功(ふせいこう)

ふせいこう
noun / na-adjective
failure, lack of success, unsuccessful
1. failure, lack of success, being unsuccessful
The state of not achieving the desired result or goal. A formal, somewhat literary word used to describe unsuccessful attempts, ventures, or outcomes.
実験(じっけん)は不成功(ふせいこう)に終(お)わった。
The experiment ended in failure.
交渉(こうしょう)は不成功(ふせいこう)だったが、次(つぎ)の機会(きかい)を待(ま)つ。
The negotiations were unsuccessful, but we'll wait for the next opportunity.
何度(なんど)も不成功(ふせいこう)を経験(けいけん)したが、あきらめずに挑戦(ちょうせん)し続(つづ)けた。
He experienced failure many times, but kept trying without giving up.

A formal word formed with the prefix 不(ふ) (non-, un-) + 成功(せいこう) (success). More literary and stiff than simply saying 失敗(しっぱい). Often appears in written reports, academic writing, and formal assessments.

The common set phrase is 不成功(ふせいこう)に終(お)わる (to end in failure).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不成功(ふせいこう)に終(お)わる: to end in failure
  • 不成功(ふせいこう)に終(お)わった試(こころ)み: an unsuccessful attempt
  • 不成功(ふせいこう)の原因(げんいん): cause of failure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 失敗(しっぱい): failure — the most common, everyday word for failure; can refer to both the act of failing and the resulting state
  • 不成立(ふせいりつ): failure to materialize — used when something (a deal, agreement, meeting) fails to come together
  • 未成功(みせいこう): not yet successful — implies the possibility of future success
