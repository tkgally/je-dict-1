1. putting on; equipping; wearing; attaching
The act of putting on, mounting, or fitting equipment, devices, accessories, or protective gear onto a person or object.
ヘルメットを装着(そうちゃく)する。
To put on a helmet.
シートベルトの装着(そうちゃく)は義務(ぎむ)です。
Wearing a seatbelt is mandatory.
新(あたら)しいレンズをカメラに装着(そうちゃく)してから撮影(さつえい)を始(はじ)めた。
I started shooting after attaching the new lens to the camera.

Composed of 装(そう) (wear, equip) + 着(ちゃく) (attach, arrive). Refers to the physical act of putting on or attaching equipment, devices, or gear. More formal and technical than simply 着(つ)ける or 付(つ)ける.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ヘルメットを装着(そうちゃく)する (to put on a helmet)
  • マスクを装着(そうちゃく)する (to wear a mask)
  • 部品(ぶひん)を装着(そうちゃく)する (to attach a part)
  • 装着(そうちゃく)方法(ほうほう) (method of attachment)
  • 装着(そうちゃく)完了(かんりょう) (attachment complete)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着用(ちゃくよう) — wearing (clothing, uniforms); focuses on clothing and accessories worn on the body
  • 取(と)り付(つ)ける — to install, attach; more everyday and less formal
  • 付(つ)ける — to attach; the most general everyday verb

USAGE:
装着(そうちゃく) is commonly used in technical contexts (equipment manuals, safety instructions) and for protective gear. It implies a deliberate, often mechanical act of fitting something into place, whereas 着用(ちゃくよう) is used more for clothing and uniforms.
