位置(いち)づけ

いちづけ
noun
positioning; status; role
1. positioning; status; role; place
The act of determining where something or someone fits within a framework, hierarchy, or context. Often used for defining the role or significance of something in a larger system.
この会社(かいしゃ)の業界(ぎょうかい)での位置(いち)づけは高(たか)い。
This company holds a high position in the industry.
この作品(さくひん)の文学史(ぶんがくし)における位置(いち)づけを考(かんが)える。
To consider the place of this work in literary history.
日本語(にほんご)教育(きょういく)における漢字(かんじ)学習(がくしゅう)の位置(いち)づけについて議論(ぎろん)が続(つづ)いている。
The debate continues about the role of kanji study within Japanese language education.

Describes the act of defining where something fits in a larger context or hierarchy. Derived from the verb 位置(いち)づける (to position, to define the role of). Often used in academic, business, and analytical writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 位置(いち)づけを明確(めいかく)にする: to clarify the positioning
  • 歴史的(れきしてき)な位置(いち)づけ: historical positioning/significance
  • 位置(いち)づけが変(か)わる: the status changes
  • 位置(いち)づけを与(あた)える: to assign a position/role

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立場(たちば): standpoint, position — focuses on one's stance or perspective
  • 地位(ちい): status, rank — emphasizes hierarchical standing in society or an organization
  • 役割(やくわり): role — the function someone or something plays
  • 位置(いち): position — the literal physical or abstract location

NOTE:
Also written 位置付(いちづ)け. The verb form 位置(いち)づける is also commonly used: この制度(せいど)をどう位置(いち)づけるかが問題(もんだい)だ (The question is how to position this system).
