気長(きなが)

きなが
na-adjective
patient; easygoing; leisurely
1. patient; easygoing; unhurried; leisurely
Having a calm, relaxed disposition that does not rush or become easily frustrated. Willing to wait without getting anxious.
気長(きなが)に待(ま)つ。
To wait patiently.
気長(きなが)な性格(せいかく)だから怒(おこ)らない。
Because of a patient personality, they don't get angry.
子育(こそだ)ては気長(きなが)に取(と)り組(く)むことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to approach child-rearing with patience.

A na-adjective composed of 気(き) (spirit/temperament) + 長(なが) (long), literally meaning 'long-tempered.' Used both as a modifier (気長(きなが)な) and adverbially (気長(きなが)に).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気長(きなが)に待(ま)つ: to wait patiently
  • 気長(きなが)な人(ひと): a patient person
  • 気長(きなが)に付(つ)き合(あ)う: to deal with something patiently over time
  • 気長(きなが)に構(かま)える: to take a relaxed approach

USAGE:
The adverbial form 気長(きなが)に is extremely common, often used as advice or encouragement to be patient. It implies a positive, calm attitude rather than passive resignation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 辛抱強(しんぼうづよ)い: persevering, enduring — emphasizes enduring hardship; stronger sense of effort
  • {のんびり}: laid-back, easygoing — more casual; describes a relaxed pace rather than patience specifically
  • 気短(きみじか): short-tempered, impatient — the direct opposite of 気長(きなが)

Related Words

Antonym: せっかち (impatient, hasty)
