1. to move something out of the way; to push aside; to clear away
To move an object or obstacle from a place so that the space is free. A common everyday transitive verb.
荷物(にもつ)をどけてください。
Please move your luggage out of the way.
邪魔(じゃま)な箱(はこ)をどけた。
I moved the box that was in the way.
テーブルの上(うえ)の物(もの)をどけて、広(ひろ)い作業(さぎょう)スペースを作(つく)った。
I cleared the things off the table and made a wide workspace.
通路(つうろ)に置(お)いてある自転車(じてんしゃ)をどけてもらえませんか。
Could you move the bicycle that's blocking the pathway?

A very common everyday verb for moving things out of the way. Written in hiragana in most contexts, though the kanji 退(ど)ける or 除(ど)ける exists. Typically used for clearing physical obstacles or clutter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)をどける: to move luggage out of the way
  • 邪魔(じゃま)な物(もの)をどける: to clear away obstructions
  • 脇(わき)にどける: to move to the side
  • 体(からだ)をどける: to move one's body out of the way

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The intransitive counterpart is どく (to get out of the way, to step aside). Example: どいてください (Please move out of the way).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どく: to get out of the way (intransitive counterpart)
  • 片付(かたづ)ける: to tidy up, to put away (implies organizing, not just clearing)
  • 退(ど)かす: to move aside (variant form, same meaning)
