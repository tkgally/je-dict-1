視覚化(しかくか)

しかくか
noun
visualization; making visible
1. visualization; making visible; rendering in visual form
The process of converting abstract data, concepts, or information into a visual representation such as charts, diagrams, or images.
データの視覚化(しかくか)。
Data visualization.
グラフを使(つか)って結果(けっか)を視覚化(しかくか)する。
To visualize the results using graphs.
複雑(ふくざつ)な統計(とうけい)データを視覚化(しかくか)することで、傾向(けいこう)が一目(ひとめ)でわかるようになる。
By visualizing complex statistical data, you can see the trends at a glance.

A compound of 視覚(しかく) (visual sense, vision) and 化(か) (-ization). Functions as a suru-verb: 視覚化(しかくか)する (to visualize).

USAGE:
Widely used in business, science, technology, and education. With the growth of data analytics and presentation tools, 視覚化(しかくか) has become increasingly common in everyday business Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • データの視覚化(しかくか) — data visualization
  • 情報(じょうほう)を視覚化(しかくか)する — to visualize information
  • 視覚化(しかくか)ツール — visualization tools
  • 視覚化(しかくか)して示(しめ)す — to show by visualizing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 可視化(かしか) — making visible, visualization; a close synonym that emphasizes making something previously invisible perceptible
  • 見(み)える化(か) — making visible (business jargon); a more casual, native-Japanese coinage popular in management contexts
  • 図式化(ずしきか) — diagramming, schematizing; specifically creating diagrams or schemas
