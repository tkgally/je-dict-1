太(ふと)め

ふとめ
na-adjective
somewhat thick; on the thick side; plumpish
1. somewhat thick; on the thick/heavy side; plumpish
Indicates something or someone is a little thicker or heavier than average. The め suffix softens the adjective, making it less direct than 太(ふと)い.
太(ふと)めの麺(めん)が好(す)きだ。
I like thicker noodles.
太(ふと)めのペンで書(か)いてください。
Please write with a thicker pen.
少(すこ)し太(ふと)めの体型(たいけい)だが、健康(けんこう)には問題(もんだい)ない。
He's slightly on the heavy side, but there are no health issues.

Formed by adding the suffix め to the adjective 太(ふと)い. The め suffix creates a softer, approximate expression. Can describe both objects (thick noodles, thick lines) and people's body types (plumpish), though when referring to people it serves as a more polite or indirect way of saying 太(ふと)い.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 太(ふと)めの麺(めん): thicker noodles
  • 太(ふと)めの糸(いと): thicker thread
  • 太(ふと)めの体型(たいけい): a heavier build
  • 太(ふと)めに切(き)る: to cut into thicker pieces

RELATED TERMS:

  • 細(ほそ)め: on the thin side — the opposite
  • 短(みじか)め: on the short side — same pattern
  • 大(おお)きめ: on the large side — same pattern
