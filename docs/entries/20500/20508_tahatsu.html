1. frequent occurrence; multiple outbreaks; repeated incidents
The occurrence of something many times or in large numbers. Used especially for negative events like accidents, crimes, or disasters.
交通(こうつう)事故(じこ)が多発(たはつ)している。
Traffic accidents are occurring frequently.
この地域(ちいき)では犯罪(はんざい)が多発(たはつ)している。
Crime is frequent in this area.
台風(たいふう)シーズンには土砂崩(どしゃくず)れが多発(たはつ)するため、早(はや)めの避難(ひなん)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Landslides occur frequently during typhoon season, so early evacuation is important.

Describes the frequent or repeated occurrence of events, especially negative ones. Composed of 多(た) (many) + 発(はつ) (occurrence/outbreak). Commonly used as a する verb: 多発(たはつ)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)多発(たはつ): frequent accidents
  • 事故(じこ)多発(たはつ)地帯(ちたい): accident-prone zone
  • 犯罪(はんざい)が多発(たはつ)する: crime occurs frequently
  • 多発(たはつ)する地域(ちいき): area with frequent occurrences
  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)が多発(たはつ)する: food poisoning breaks out frequently

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頻発(ひんぱつ): frequent occurrence — very close in meaning, slightly more formal
  • 続出(ぞくしゅつ): occurring one after another — emphasizes successive appearance
  • 頻繁(ひんぱん): frequently — an adjectival word describing general frequency

USAGE:
Almost always used for undesirable events. You would not typically use 多発(たはつ) for positive occurrences. The compound 事故(じこ)多発(たはつ)地帯(ちたい) (accident-prone zone) is commonly seen on road signs in Japan.
