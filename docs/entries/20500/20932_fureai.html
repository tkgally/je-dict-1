ふれあい

ふれあい
noun
interaction; contact; togetherness
1. interaction; contact; togetherness; human connection
Warm, personal interaction or contact between people, animals, or with nature. Emphasizes the emotional and human dimension of the encounter rather than mere physical contact.
動物(どうぶつ)とのふれあいを楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy interacting with animals.
地域(ちいき)の人(ひと)たちとのふれあいが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Interaction with people in the community is important.
この牧場(ぼくじょう)では、子供(こども)たちが羊(ひつじ)や馬(うま)とふれあうことができる。
At this farm, children can interact with sheep and horses.

Derived from the verb 触(ふ)れ合(あ)う (to touch each other, to come into contact). Written in hiragana when used as a noun referring to warm human interaction. Very common in compound names for community facilities, parks, and events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)とのふれあい: interaction with people
  • 動物(どうぶつ)とのふれあい: contact with animals
  • 自然(しぜん)とのふれあい: communing with nature
  • ふれあい広場(ひろば): interaction plaza (community gathering space)
  • ふれあい動物園(どうぶつえん): petting zoo

USAGE:
Often appears in names of public facilities and events, giving a warm, community-oriented tone: ふれあいセンター (community center), ふれあい祭(まつ)り (community festival).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交流(こうりゅう): exchange, interaction — more formal, often for cultural or international exchange
  • 触(ふ)れ合(あ)う: to touch each other, to interact — the verb form
  • コミュニケーション: communication — borrowed English word, broader in scope
