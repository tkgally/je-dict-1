出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)

しゅっせきしゃ
noun
attendee; participant; person present
1. attendee; participant; person present
A person who attends or is present at a meeting, event, or gathering.
出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)は全部(ぜんぶ)で十(じゅう)人(にん)だった。
There were ten attendees in total.
会議(かいぎ)の出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)リストを作成(さくせい)してください。
Please prepare a list of meeting attendees.
出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)の中(なか)から代表(だいひょう)を一人(ひとり)選(えら)ぶことになった。
It was decided that one representative would be chosen from among the attendees.

Compound of 出席(しゅっせき) (attendance) + 者(しゃ) (person). A straightforward agent noun for someone who attends.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)全員(ぜんいん): all attendees
  • 出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)数(すう): number of attendees
  • 出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)リスト: attendance list
  • 出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)の名前(なまえ): names of those present

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 参加者(さんかしゃ): participant — emphasizes active involvement, not just being present
  • 列席者(れっせきしゃ): person in attendance — more formal; used for ceremonies and official events
  • 来場者(らいじょうしゃ): visitor, attendee (at a venue) — used for events at specific venues
