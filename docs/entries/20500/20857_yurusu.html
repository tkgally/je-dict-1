1. to pardon; to forgive; to absolve
To formally forgive sins, crimes, or serious offenses. More solemn and weighty than the general-purpose 許す, often carrying religious, legal, or literary overtones.
罪(つみ)を赦(ゆる)す。
To forgive sins.
神(かみ)がすべての罪(つみ)を赦(ゆる)してくださる。
God forgives all sins.
死刑囚(しけいしゅう)が恩赦(おんしゃ)によって赦(ゆる)された。
The death row inmate was pardoned through an amnesty.
彼女(かのじょ)は長(なが)い時間(じかん)をかけて、ようやく過去(かこ)の自分(じぶん)を赦(ゆる)すことができた。
After a long time, she was finally able to forgive her past self.

A kanji variant of ゆるす that specifically conveys pardon and absolution of sins or serious offenses. While 許(ゆる)す is the general-purpose word for "to allow, to forgive, to permit," 赦(ゆる)す is reserved for the solemn, weighty act of forgiving wrongdoing.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 許(ゆる)す: general "to allow, to permit, to forgive" — the default spelling
  • 赦(ゆる)す: specifically "to pardon, to absolve" — used for sins, crimes, and deep personal forgiveness

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罪(つみ)を赦(ゆる)す: to forgive sins
  • 恩赦(おんしゃ): amnesty, pardon (contains the same kanji)
  • 自分(じぶん)を赦(ゆる)す: to forgive oneself

REGISTER:
Literary, religious, and formal. Common in Christian texts, legal contexts, and literary prose. In everyday conversation, 許(ゆる)す covers all meanings.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 許(ゆる)す: to allow, to permit, to forgive (general-purpose, much broader)
  • 容赦(ようしゃ)する: to pardon, to show mercy (formal)
  • 免(ゆる)す: to exempt, to release from obligation (another kanji variant)
