1. to understand; to be aware of
To have knowledge or understanding of a matter, especially rules, principles, or expected conduct.
事情(じじょう)は心得(こころえ)ている。
I understand the circumstances.
マナーを心得(こころえ)た人(ひと)だ。
That person understands proper etiquette.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の役割(やくわり)をよく心得(こころえ)ていて、的確(てきかく)に動(うご)いた。
He understood his role well and acted precisely.
2. to be skilled in; to be proficient at
To have acquired skill or expertise in a particular art, discipline, or practice.
茶道(さどう)を心得(こころえ)ている。
She is skilled in the tea ceremony.
武道(ぶどう)を心得(こころえ)た人物(じんぶつ)だ。
He is someone proficient in martial arts.
料理(りょうり)を心得(こころえ)ているので、急(きゅう)な来客(らいきゃく)にもすぐ対応(たいおう)できる。
Since she is skilled at cooking, she can handle unexpected guests right away.

A somewhat literary or formal verb meaning to have deep understanding or proficiency. Often used in the ている form to describe someone's established knowledge or skill. The noun form 心得(こころえ) (knowledge, guidelines, precautions) is also very common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事情(じじょう)を心得(こころえ)る: to understand the circumstances
  • マナーを心得(こころえ)る: to know proper manners
  • 茶道(さどう)を心得(こころえ)る: to be versed in tea ceremony
  • 心得(こころえ)たもので: being knowledgeable about

REGISTER:
Slightly formal and literary. In casual speech, 分(わ)かる or 知(し)っている are more common for sense 1, and 上手(じょうず)だ or できる for sense 2. 心得(こころえ)る implies a deeper, more internalized understanding.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 理解(りかい)する: to understand (more neutral and general)
  • 弁(わきま)える: to discern, to know one's place (similar formality, focuses on propriety)
  • 通(つう)じる: to be well-versed in (emphasizes familiarity through experience)
