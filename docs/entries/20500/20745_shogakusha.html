初学者(しょがくしゃ)

しょがくしゃ
noun
beginner; novice learner
1. beginner; novice learner
A person who has just started learning a subject or discipline. More formal and academic than 初心者, often used in educational and scholarly contexts.
初学者(しょがくしゃ)向(む)けの教材(きょうざい)。
Teaching materials for beginners.
この本(ほん)は初学者(しょがくしゃ)にもわかりやすい。
This book is easy to understand even for beginners.
初学者(しょがくしゃ)がつまずきやすいポイントを丁寧(ていねい)に解説(かいせつ)している。
It carefully explains the points where beginners tend to stumble.

A compound of 初(しょ) (first, beginning) and 学者(がくしゃ) (scholar, learner). Refers specifically to someone at the beginning stage of learning a particular field of study.

USAGE:
Most commonly appears in academic, educational, and publishing contexts — book titles, course descriptions, and study guides. It sounds more formal and scholarly than 初心者(しょしんしゃ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 初学者(しょがくしゃ)向(む)け — aimed at beginners
  • 初学者(しょがくしゃ)にもわかりやすい — easy for beginners to understand
  • 初学者(しょがくしゃ)のための入門書(にゅうもんしょ) — introductory book for beginners

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初心者(しょしんしゃ) — beginner; the most common general term, used broadly for hobbies, sports, and skills
  • 入門者(にゅうもんしゃ) — someone entering a field; emphasizes the act of starting
