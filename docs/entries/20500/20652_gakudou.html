学童(がくどう)

がくどう
noun
schoolchild; school-age child
1. schoolchild; school-age child
A child of elementary school age, roughly 6 to 12 years old. Often used in official or institutional contexts rather than casual conversation.
学童(がくどう)保育(ほいく)に申(もう)し込(こ)む。
To apply for after-school childcare.
学童(がくどう)の通学路(つうがくろ)を安全(あんぜん)にする。
To make the route that schoolchildren take to school safe.
共働(ともばたら)きの家庭(かてい)にとって、学童(がくどう)クラブは大(おお)きな助(たす)けになっている。
For dual-income households, after-school clubs are a great help.

Refers to children of elementary school age (roughly 6–12). While the literal meaning is simply 'school-age child,' in modern usage 学童(がくどう) is strongly associated with 学童(がくどう)保育(ほいく) — the after-school childcare programs that look after children while parents are at work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学童(がくどう)保育(ほいく): after-school childcare (for elementary school children)
  • 学童(がくどう)クラブ: after-school club
  • 学童(がくどう)疎開(そかい): wartime evacuation of schoolchildren (historical)
  • 学童(がくどう)の列(れつ): line of schoolchildren

USAGE:
In everyday conversation, people usually say 小学生(しょうがくせい) (elementary school student) rather than 学童(がくどう). The term 学童(がくどう) appears primarily in official contexts, policy discussions, and as shorthand for after-school care programs.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 小学生(しょうがくせい): elementary school student (the everyday term)
  • 児童(じどう): child (legal/official term, used in laws and regulations)
