色違(いろちが)い

いろちがい
noun
different color; color variation
1. different color; color variation; alternate color version
The same item in a different color. Widely used in shopping, fashion, and gaming (especially for rare alternate-colored creatures in games).
色違(いろちが)いも買(か)った。
I also bought it in a different color.
このシャツの色違(いろちが)いはありますか。
Do you have this shirt in another color?
気(き)に入(い)ったので色違(いろちが)いで三枚(さんまい)買(か)ってしまった。
I liked it so much that I ended up buying three in different colors.
色違(いろちが)いのポケモンを見(み)つけるのは非常(ひじょう)に難(むずか)しい。
Finding a shiny Pokémon is extremely difficult.

A practical compound noun used daily in shopping and fashion to refer to the same product in a different color option. Also extremely popular in gaming culture, especially Pokémon, where 色違(いろちが)い refers to rare alternate-colored creatures (called "shiny" in English).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色違(いろちが)いで買(か)う: to buy in different colors
  • 色違(いろちが)いの商品(しょうひん): the same product in a different color
  • 色違(いろちが)いのポケモン: shiny Pokémon
  • 色違(いろちが)いを集(あつ)める: to collect color variants

USAGE:
Often used with の as a modifier (色違(いろちが)いのスカート) or with で to indicate manner (色違(いろちが)いで揃(そろ)える — to get a matching set in different colors). In online shopping, 色違(いろちが)い is a standard filter option.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 別(べつ)の色(いろ): another color (descriptive phrase, not a set term)
  • カラーバリエーション: color variation (loanword, used in product catalogs)
