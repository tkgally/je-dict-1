樹海(じゅかい)

じゅかい
noun
sea of trees; dense forest
1. sea of trees; dense forest
A vast, dense forest that looks like a sea when viewed from above. Most famously associated with Aokigahara at the base of Mt. Fuji.
樹海(じゅかい)の中(なか)は暗(くら)い。
It's dark inside the sea of trees.
富士山(ふじさん)のふもとに広大(こうだい)な樹海(じゅかい)が広(ひろ)がっている。
A vast sea of trees stretches out at the foot of Mt. Fuji.
上空(じょうくう)から見(み)ると、山々(やまやま)を覆(おお)う樹海(じゅかい)がまるで緑(みどり)の海(うみ)のように見(み)えた。
Viewed from above, the sea of trees covering the mountains looked just like a green ocean.

A poetic compound of 樹(じゅ) (tree) and 海(かい) (sea), describing a forest so vast and dense that it resembles an ocean when seen from a high vantage point.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 樹海(じゅかい)に迷(まよ)い込(こ)む: to wander into the forest
  • 樹海(じゅかい)が広(ひろ)がる: a sea of trees stretches out
  • 青木ヶ原樹海(あおきがはらじゅかい): Aokigahara forest
  • 樹海(じゅかい)の奥(おく): deep within the forest

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
The word is strongly associated with 青木ヶ原樹海(あおきがはらじゅかい) (Aokigahara), the dense forest at the base of 富士山(ふじさん). This forest, formed on hardened lava, is known for its thick canopy and easy-to-get-lost terrain. It has unfortunately also become associated with suicide, a topic handled sensitively in Japanese media.
