絶景(ぜっけい)スポット

ぜっけいすぽっと
noun
scenic viewpoint; spectacular view spot
1. scenic viewpoint; spectacular view spot
A location known for offering a breathtaking or spectacular view. Widely used in travel guides, tourism websites, and social media to describe must-see locations.
絶景(ぜっけい)スポットを探(さが)す。
I'm looking for scenic viewpoints.
この展望台(てんぼうだい)は知(し)る人(ひと)ぞ知(し)る絶景(ぜっけい)スポットだ。
This observation deck is a scenic viewpoint known only to those in the know.
SNSで話題(わだい)になった絶景(ぜっけい)スポットに観光客(かんこうきゃく)が殺到(さっとう)している。
Tourists are flocking to a scenic spot that went viral on social media.

A compound of 絶景(ぜっけい) (superb view) and スポット (spot, location). Very common in travel writing and tourism promotion. Japan has many famous 絶景(ぜっけい)スポット, and travel magazines and websites regularly publish rankings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶景(ぜっけい)スポットランキング: scenic spot ranking
  • 絶景(ぜっけい)スポット巡(めぐ)り: touring scenic viewpoints
  • 隠(かく)れた絶景(ぜっけい)スポット: hidden scenic gem
  • 人気(にんき)の絶景(ぜっけい)スポット: popular scenic spot

RELATED TERMS:

  • 絶景(ぜっけい): superb view, breathtaking scenery
  • 景勝地(けいしょうち): scenic spot — more formal, often officially designated
  • 展望台(てんぼうだい): observation deck, lookout point
  • 名所(めいしょ): famous place, noted spot — broader than just views
