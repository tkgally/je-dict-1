1. art appreciation; appreciation of the arts
The act of viewing, enjoying, and understanding works of art, including visual art, music, theater, and other artistic forms.
芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)が趣味(しゅみ)です。
Art appreciation is my hobby.
学校(がっこう)で芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)の授業(じゅぎょう)がある。
There is an art appreciation class at school.
休日(きゅうじつ)は美術館(びじゅつかん)で芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)をして過(す)ごすのが好(す)きだ。
I like spending my days off appreciating art at museums.

Composed of 芸術(げいじゅつ) (art, the arts) and 鑑賞(かんしょう) (appreciation, viewing). Used for all forms of art — visual arts, music, theater, film, etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)の時間(じかん): art appreciation time
  • 芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)教室(きょうしつ): art appreciation class
  • 芸術鑑賞(げいじゅつかんしょう)会(かい): art appreciation event

RELATED TERMS:

  • 音楽鑑賞(おんがくかんしょう): music appreciation
  • 映画鑑賞(えいがかんしょう): film appreciation / watching movies
  • 観賞(かんしょう): appreciation (for natural beauty, flowers, fish) — note the different kanji from 鑑賞(かんしょう)

KANJI NOTE:
鑑賞(かんしょう) (with 鑑(かん)) is used for art and cultural works, while 観賞(かんしょう) (with 観(かん)) is used for natural beauty like flowers and fish. This is a common source of confusion.
