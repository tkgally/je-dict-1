先達(せんだつ)

せんだつ
noun
pioneer; predecessor; guide
1. pioneer; predecessor; forerunner
A person who has gone before and paved the way in a particular field, serving as a model or guide for those who follow.
先達(せんだつ)の教(おし)えに学(まな)ぶ。
To learn from the teachings of predecessors.
この分野(ぶんや)の先達(せんだつ)たちの努力(どりょく)があってこそ今(いま)がある。
It is thanks to the efforts of pioneers in this field that we are where we are today.
先達(せんだつ)が残(のこ)した業績(ぎょうせき)を引(ひ)き継(つ)ぎ、さらに発展(はってん)させていくことが私(わたし)たちの使命(しめい)だ。
Our mission is to build on the achievements our predecessors left behind and develop them further.

Composed of 先(せん) (ahead, before) + 達(だつ) (reach, attain). A respectful term for those who came before in a field or endeavor. Has a somewhat literary or formal tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先達(せんだつ)に倣(なら)う (follow the example of predecessors)
  • 先達(せんだつ)の知恵(ちえ) (wisdom of predecessors)
  • 先達(せんだつ)の教(おし)え (teachings of predecessors)
  • 先達(せんだつ)として (as a pioneer/guide)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先駆者(せんくしゃ) — pioneer, trailblazer (more emphasis on being the first)
  • 先輩(せんぱい) — senior (in school or workplace, closer social relationship)
  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ) — predecessor (specifically in a position or role)

CULTURAL NOTE:
The famous opening of 徒然草(つれづれぐさ) (Essays in Idleness) uses the phrase 先達(せんだつ)はあらまほしきことなり — "It is desirable to have a guide." This classical usage gave the word a lasting literary association.
