店先(みせさき)

みせさき
noun
storefront; in front of a shop
1. storefront; in front of a shop; shop front area
The area directly in front of or at the entrance of a shop, including any outdoor display space.
店先(みせさき)に花(はな)が並(なら)んでいる。
Flowers are lined up in front of the shop.
店先(みせさき)で野菜(やさい)を売(う)っている。
They sell vegetables at the storefront.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきたので、店先(みせさき)の軒下(のきした)で雨宿(あまやど)りした。
When it started raining, I took shelter under the eaves of a shop front.

Composed of 店(みせ) (shop) + 先(さき) (front/ahead). Refers to the area immediately in front of or at the entrance of a shop, often where goods are displayed outdoors.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 店先(みせさき)に並(なら)べる: to display in front of a shop
  • 店先(みせさき)で売(う)る: to sell at the storefront
  • 店先(みせさき)に立(た)つ: to stand at the shop front
  • 店先(みせさき)の看板(かんばん): a sign at the shop front

USAGE:
Evokes a traditional or small-business atmosphere — fruit stands, flower shops, and local stores that display goods outside. Less commonly used for modern chain stores or malls.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 店頭(てんとう): shop front, over the counter — more formal; often used in business/financial contexts (店頭販売(てんとうはんばい), over-the-counter sales)
  • 軒先(のきさき): under the eaves — specifically the area under the roof overhang, not the full storefront
