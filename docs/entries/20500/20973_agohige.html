あごひげ

あごひげ
noun
chin beard; goatee
1. chin beard; goatee; beard on the chin
Facial hair growing on the chin. Can refer to any beard on the chin area, including a goatee.
あごひげを生(は)やす。
To grow a chin beard.
彼(かれ)はあごひげを剃(そ)るのが嫌(きら)いだ。
He hates shaving his beard.
立派(りっぱ)なあごひげを蓄(たくわ)えた老人(ろうじん)が座(すわ)っていた。
An old man with a splendid beard was sitting there.

Compound of あご (chin/jaw) + ひげ (facial hair). Also written 顎鬚(あごひげ) or 顎髭(あごひげ) in kanji, though the kana spelling is more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • あごひげを生(は)やす: to grow a chin beard
  • あごひげを剃(そ)る: to shave one's beard
  • あごひげを蓄(たくわ)える: to grow/keep a beard (literary)
  • あごひげを整(ととの)える: to trim one's beard

RELATED WORDS:

  • ひげ: facial hair (general term) — can refer to any facial hair
  • 口(くち)ひげ: mustache — hair above the upper lip
  • ほおひげ: sideburns — hair on the cheeks
