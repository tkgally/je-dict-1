1. to detain; to take into custody; to hold
To physically confine or hold someone, especially by legal or police authority. Used for arrests, detentions, and custody.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を拘束(こうそく)する。
To detain the suspect.
警察(けいさつ)は犯人(はんにん)を現場(げんば)で拘束(こうそく)した。
The police detained the criminal at the scene.
彼(かれ)は不法入国(ふほうにゅうこく)の疑(うたが)いで拘束(こうそく)され、取(と)り調(しら)べを受(う)けた。
He was detained on suspicion of illegal entry and underwent questioning.
2. to restrain; to restrict; to bind
To limit someone's freedom of action or movement, whether physically or through rules, obligations, or agreements.
規則(きそく)に拘束(こうそく)される。
To be bound by rules.
契約(けいやく)は両者(りょうしゃ)を拘束(こうそく)する。
The contract is binding on both parties.
仕事(しごと)に拘束(こうそく)される時間(じかん)が長(なが)すぎて、自分(じぶん)の時間(じかん)がほとんどない。
I spend too many hours tied up with work and have almost no time for myself.

A する verb composed of 拘(こう) (seize, detain) + 束(そく) (bind, bundle). Has both a concrete legal/physical sense (detaining a person) and an abstract sense (being bound by rules or obligations).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を拘束(こうそく)する: to detain a suspect
  • 身柄(みがら)を拘束(こうそく)する: to take someone into custody
  • 拘束(こうそく)時間(じかん): hours of obligation (time one is tied up)
  • 法的(ほうてき)拘束力(こうそくりょく): legal binding force
  • 拘束(こうそく)される: to be detained / to be bound

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逮捕(たいほ)する: to arrest — formal legal arrest, stronger than detention
  • 束縛(そくばく)する: to restrain, to shackle — often for personal or emotional restraint
  • 制約(せいやく)する: to restrict, to constrain — for limitations on actions or choices
  • 監禁(かんきん)する: to confine, to imprison — longer-term physical confinement
