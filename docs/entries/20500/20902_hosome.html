細(ほそ)め

ほそめ
na-adjective
somewhat thin; on the slim side; slender
1. somewhat thin; on the thin/slim side; slender
Indicates something or someone is a little thinner or slimmer than average. The め suffix softens the adjective, creating a moderate, approximate expression.
細(ほそ)めのネクタイを選(えら)ぶ。
To choose a slimmer tie.
パスタは細(ほそ)めが好(す)みです。
I prefer thinner pasta.
彼女(かのじょ)は細(ほそ)めの体型(たいけい)で、どんな服(ふく)もよく似合(にあ)う。
She has a slim build, and any clothes look good on her.

Formed by adding the suffix め to the adjective 細(ほそ)い. The め suffix creates a softer, approximate expression. Can describe objects (thin noodles, slim pens) and people's body types (slender).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 細(ほそ)めの麺(めん): thinner noodles
  • 細(ほそ)めの体型(たいけい): a slender build
  • 細(ほそ)めに切(き)る: to cut into thinner pieces
  • 細(ほそ)めのパンツ: slim-fit pants

RELATED TERMS:

  • 太(ふと)め: on the thick side — the opposite
  • 短(みじか)め: on the short side — same pattern
  • 薄(うす)め: on the thin/light side — same pattern, for thickness or concentration
