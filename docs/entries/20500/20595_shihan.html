師範(しはん)

しはん
noun
master instructor; teacher; exemplar
1. master instructor; master teacher
A highly qualified teacher or instructor, especially in martial arts, traditional arts, or crafts. Also used as a title or rank.
剣道(けんどう)の師範(しはん)に教(おそ)わる。
To learn from a kendo master.
彼(かれ)は茶道(さどう)の師範(しはん)として長年(ながねん)活動(かつどう)している。
He has been active as a tea ceremony master for many years.
祖母(そぼ)は華道(かどう)の師範(しはん)の免許(めんきょ)を持(も)っており、自宅(じたく)で弟子(でし)に教(おし)えている。
My grandmother holds a master instructor's license in flower arrangement and teaches students at home.

Composed of 師(し) (teacher, master) + 範(はん) (model, pattern). Carries a sense of authority and mastery. In martial arts and traditional Japanese arts, 師範(しはん) is a formal rank indicating one is qualified to teach independently.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 師範(しはん)の免許(めんきょ) (master instructor's license)
  • 師範(しはん)代(だい) (assistant instructor, one rank below master)
  • 師範(しはん)学校(がっこう) (normal school, historical teacher training school)
  • 師範(しはん)に師事(しじ)する (study under a master)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先生(せんせい) — teacher (general, widely used for any teacher or respected professional)
  • 指導者(しどうしゃ) — instructor, leader (general term without the connotation of traditional mastery)
  • 名人(めいじん) — master, expert (emphasizes skill rather than teaching role)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In traditional arts like 茶道(さどう), 華道(かどう), and 書道(しょどう), receiving a 師範(しはん) license is a significant milestone that permits one to teach and award ranks to students.

Homophone: 市販(しはん)
