1. to take over; to assume (someone's burden, debt, or responsibility)
To shoulder another person's obligation, especially a financial debt or responsibility, on their behalf.
借金(しゃっきん)を肩代(かたが)わりする。
To take over someone's debt.
親(おや)が息子(むすこ)のローンを肩代(かたが)わりした。
The parents took over their son's loan.
費用(ひよう)を会社(かいしゃ)が肩代(かたが)わりしてくれることになった。
The company agreed to cover the costs on my behalf.

Composed of 肩(かた) (shoulder) + 代(か)わり (substitution) + する. The image is of placing someone else's burden on your own shoulders. Most commonly used for financial obligations but can extend to any responsibility.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 借金(しゃっきん)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to take over a debt
  • 費用(ひよう)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to cover costs (for someone)
  • 責任(せきにん)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to take over a responsibility
  • 損失(そんしつ)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to absorb a loss

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立(た)て替(か)える: to pay on someone's behalf (with expectation of repayment)
  • 引(ひ)き受(う)ける: to take on, to undertake — more general, not specifically about burdens
  • 負担(ふたん)する: to bear (a cost) — neutral, doesn't imply taking it from someone else
