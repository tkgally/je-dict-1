1. to be curly; to be frizzy; to be crinkled
For hair, noodles, fabric, or similar things to have a wavy, curled, or crinkled texture or shape.
髪(かみ)が縮(ちぢ)れている。
My hair is curly.
縮(ちぢ)れ麺(めん)のラーメンが好(す)きだ。
I like ramen with curly noodles.
湿気(しっけ)で髪(かみ)が縮(ちぢ)れてまとまらない。
My hair is frizzy from the humidity and won't stay in place.
熱(ねつ)で布(ぬの)の端(はし)が縮(ちぢ)れてしまった。
The edges of the fabric curled up from the heat.

An intransitive verb describing a curly, wavy, or crinkled state. Most commonly used for hair and noodles. The past participle 縮(ちぢ)れた functions as an adjective meaning "curly" or "crinkled."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 髪(かみ)が縮(ちぢ)れる: hair becomes curly/frizzy
  • 縮(ちぢ)れ毛(げ): curly hair (as a noun)
  • 縮(ちぢ)れ麺(めん): curly noodles
  • 縮(ちぢ)れた葉(は): crinkled leaves

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive form is 縮(ちぢ)らす (to make curly, to curl something).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 巻(ま)く: to curl, to wind (active curling action)
  • くせ毛(げ): naturally curly/wavy hair (noun describing hair type)
  • 縮(ちぢ)む: to shrink (focuses on getting smaller, not curling)
