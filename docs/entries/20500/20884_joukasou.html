浄化槽(じょうかそう)

じょうかそう
noun
septic tank, sewage treatment tank
1. septic tank, sewage treatment tank
An on-site wastewater treatment system that processes household sewage before discharging it. Common in areas of Japan not connected to the public sewage system. Regulated under the Septic Tank Law (浄化槽法(じょうかそうほう)).
浄化槽(じょうかそう)の点検(てんけん)を受(う)けた。
I had the septic tank inspected.
この地域(ちいき)は下水道(げすいどう)がないため、浄化槽(じょうかそう)を設置(せっち)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
Since this area has no sewage system, a septic tank needs to be installed.
浄化槽(じょうかそう)は定期的(ていきてき)に清掃(せいそう)しないと、悪臭(あくしゅう)の原因(げんいん)になる。
If the septic tank isn't cleaned regularly, it can cause bad odors.

A compound of 浄化(じょうか) (purification) + 槽(そう) (tank, vat). In Japan, areas without municipal sewage infrastructure use 浄化槽(じょうかそう) to treat household wastewater on-site. Homeowners with a 浄化槽(じょうかそう) are legally required to have it inspected and cleaned regularly.

There are two types:

  • 単独(たんどく)浄化槽(じょうかそう): treats only toilet waste (older type)
  • 合併(がっぺい)浄化槽(じょうかそう): treats all household wastewater including kitchen and bath (modern standard)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 浄化槽(じょうかそう)を設置(せっち)する: to install a septic tank
  • 浄化槽(じょうかそう)の清掃(せいそう): septic tank cleaning
  • 浄化槽(じょうかそう)の点検(てんけん): septic tank inspection
  • 浄化槽(じょうかそう)管理(かんり): septic tank maintenance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下水道(げすいどう): sewage system — public municipal sewage infrastructure
  • 汚水(おすい)処理(しょり): sewage treatment — the general process, not the specific equipment
