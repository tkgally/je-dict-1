1. to rub; to stroke; to massage gently
To move one's hand lightly and repeatedly over a surface, especially a part of the body, to soothe or comfort.
背中(せなか)をさすった。
I rubbed their back.
痛(いた)いところをやさしくさする。
To gently rub the place that hurts.
母(はは)が子(こ)どものお腹(なか)をさすって寝(ね)かしつけた。
The mother rubbed the child's tummy to get them to sleep.
彼女(かのじょ)は泣(な)いている友達(ともだち)の背中(せなか)をそっとさすってあげた。
She gently rubbed her crying friend's back.

A gentle, comforting action of rubbing or stroking a body part. Commonly used when soothing someone who is in pain, feeling sick, or crying. The motion is light and repetitive, distinct from a firm massage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 背中(せなか)をさする: to rub someone's back
  • お腹(なか)をさする: to rub one's tummy
  • 肩(かた)をさする: to rub one's shoulders
  • 膝(ひざ)をさする: to rub one's knees

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 撫(な)でる: to stroke, to pet (lighter touch, often for hair or animals)
  • 揉(も)む: to knead, to massage (firmer pressure)
  • こする: to rub (more vigorous, can imply friction or scrubbing)
