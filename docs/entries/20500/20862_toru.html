1. to gather; to pick; to collect; to harvest
To pick or gather natural things like plants, mushrooms, shellfish, or minerals from their environment.
山(やま)できのこを採(と)る。
To gather mushrooms in the mountains.
庭(にわ)で花(はな)を採(と)ってきた。
I picked some flowers from the garden.
潮干狩(しおひが)りで貝(かい)をたくさん採(と)った。
We gathered lots of shellfish while clamming.
2. to adopt; to choose; to employ
To select and adopt a plan, method, or person. Used for hiring, selecting candidates, or adopting approaches.
新(あたら)しい方法(ほうほう)を採(と)る。
To adopt a new method.
今年(ことし)は十名(じゅうめい)採(と)る予定(よてい)だ。
We plan to hire ten people this year.
会議(かいぎ)で多数決(たすうけつ)を採(と)って決(き)めた。
We decided by taking a majority vote at the meeting.

One of several とる verbs distinguished by kanji. 採(と)る specifically means to pick/gather from nature or to select/adopt. The kanji 採(さい) carries the sense of selecting or extracting.

KANJI DISTINCTIONS (とる):

  • 取(と)る: to take (general, most common)
  • 撮(と)る: to take (photos/video)
  • 採(と)る: to gather, to pick; to adopt, to hire
  • 捕(と)る: to catch (animals, criminals)
  • 盗(と)る: to steal

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • きのこを採(と)る: to gather mushrooms
  • 山菜(さんさい)を採(と)る: to gather wild vegetables
  • 血液(けつえき)を採(と)る: to draw blood
  • 多数決(たすうけつ)を採(と)る: to take a vote
  • 人材(じんざい)を採(と)る: to hire talent
  • 方針(ほうしん)を採(と)る: to adopt a policy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取(と)る: to take (general-purpose, broadest meaning)
  • 収穫(しゅうかく)する: to harvest (agriculture, more formal)
  • 採用(さいよう)する: to adopt, to employ (formal, common in business)
