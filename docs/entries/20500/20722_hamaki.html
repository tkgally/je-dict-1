葉巻(はまき)

はまき
noun
cigar
1. cigar
A roll of dried tobacco leaves for smoking, larger and thicker than a cigarette.
葉巻(はまき)を吸(す)う。
To smoke a cigar.
キューバ産(さん)の葉巻(はまき)は有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Cuban cigars are famous.
祖父(そふ)は毎晩(まいばん)食後(しょくご)に葉巻(はまき)を一本(いっぽん)楽(たの)しんでいた。
My grandfather used to enjoy one cigar every evening after dinner.

Composed of 葉(は) (leaf) + 巻(まき) (roll), literally 'rolled leaves.' Written in kanji as 葉巻(はまき) or sometimes 葉巻(はまき)き (with okurigana). The full form 葉巻(はまき)きタバコ is sometimes used to distinguish from cigarettes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 葉巻(はまき)を吸(す)う: to smoke a cigar
  • 葉巻(はまき)に火(ひ)をつける: to light a cigar
  • 高級(こうきゅう)葉巻(はまき): premium cigar
  • キューバ産(さん)葉巻(はまき): Cuban cigar

COUNTER:
Counted with 本(ほん) (一本(いっぽん), 二本(にほん)...).

CULTURAL NOTE:
Cigars are much less common in Japan than cigarettes and are associated with luxury, wealth, or foreign culture. They appear in depictions of businessmen, politicians, or characters with a Western lifestyle.
