1. grazing; pasturing; putting livestock out to pasture
The practice of letting livestock roam and feed freely on open land.
牛(うし)を放牧(ほうぼく)する。
To put cattle out to pasture.
広(ひろ)い草原(そうげん)で羊(ひつじ)が放牧(ほうぼく)されている。
Sheep are grazing on the wide grassland.
この牧場(ぼくじょう)では放牧(ほうぼく)で育(そだ)てた牛(うし)の乳(ちち)から作(つく)ったチーズが人気(にんき)だ。
This ranch is known for cheese made from the milk of pasture-raised cattle.

Composed of 放(ほう) (release, let go) + 牧(ぼく) (herding, pasture). Describes the agricultural practice of free-range grazing as opposed to keeping animals in enclosed spaces.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 放牧(ほうぼく)地(ち) (pasture land, grazing land)
  • 放牧(ほうぼく)で育(そだ)てる (raise by free-range grazing)
  • 自然(しぜん)放牧(ほうぼく) (natural grazing)
  • 放牧(ほうぼく)期間(きかん) (grazing period/season)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 牧畜(ぼくちく) — livestock farming (the broader industry of raising animals)
  • 飼育(しいく) — raising, breeding (keeping and caring for animals, not necessarily free-range)
  • 遊牧(ゆうぼく) — nomadic herding (moving with livestock across regions)
