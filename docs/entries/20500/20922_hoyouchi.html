保養地(ほようち)

ほようち
noun
health resort; retreat; convalescence destination
1. health resort; retreat; rest area; convalescence destination
A place where people go to rest, recuperate, or improve their health, typically in a natural setting with clean air, hot springs, or other therapeutic amenities.
山(やま)の保養地(ほようち)で過(す)ごす。
To spend time at a mountain retreat.
軽井沢(かるいざわ)は昔(むかし)から保養地(ほようち)として知(し)られている。
Karuizawa has been known as a health resort since long ago.
温泉(おんせん)のある保養地(ほようち)で心身(しんしん)をリフレッシュした。
I refreshed my body and mind at a resort with hot springs.

Composed of 保養(ほよう) (rest, recuperation) + 地(ち) (place). Refers to destinations valued for their restorative qualities — clean mountain air, hot springs, quiet seaside settings, etc. Many Japanese companies and organizations maintain 保養所(ほようじょ) (rest facilities) for employees.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 温泉(おんせん)保養地(ほようち): hot spring resort
  • 高原(こうげん)の保養地(ほようち): highland retreat
  • 海辺(うみべ)の保養地(ほようち): seaside resort
  • 保養地(ほようち)として発展(はってん)する: to develop as a health resort

RELATED TERMS:

  • 保養所(ほようじょ): rest facility, retreat house — a specific building or facility (often company-owned)
  • 避暑地(ひしょち): summer retreat — a place to escape summer heat
  • 湯治場(とうじば): hot spring cure site — a place specifically for therapeutic bathing
  • リゾート: resort — the loanword, more associated with leisure than health
