紳士的(しんしてき)

しんしてき
na-adjective
gentlemanly; courteous; refined
1. gentlemanly; courteous; refined; well-mannered
Describes behavior, attitude, or manner that is polite, dignified, and respectful in the way expected of a gentleman.
紳士的(しんしてき)な対応(たいおう)だった。
It was a gentlemanly response.
彼(かれ)はいつも紳士的(しんしてき)な態度(たいど)で人(ひと)に接(せっ)する。
He always treats people with a gentlemanly attitude.
相手(あいて)チームの選手(せんしゅ)は試合後(しあいご)も紳士的(しんしてき)に握手(あくしゅ)を求(もと)めてきた。
The opposing team's players courteously came to shake hands even after the match.

Composed of 紳士(しんし) (gentleman) + 的(てき) (suffix forming na-adjectives, meaning "-like" or "-esque"). Describes conduct befitting a gentleman.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 紳士的(しんしてき)な態度(たいど) (gentlemanly attitude)
  • 紳士的(しんしてき)な対応(たいおう) (courteous response)
  • 紳士的(しんしてき)な振(ふ)る舞(ま)い (gentlemanly behavior)
  • 紳士的(しんしてき)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う (to behave in a gentlemanly manner)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 礼儀正(れいぎただ)しい — polite, well-mannered; focuses on adherence to etiquette
  • 丁寧(ていねい) — polite, careful; more general and applicable to actions and speech
  • 上品(じょうひん) — elegant, refined; focuses on taste and refinement rather than conduct

USAGE:
Often used in sports and business contexts to praise someone's dignified and fair conduct. In sports, a 紳士的(しんしてき) player respects opponents and officials. In business, 紳士的(しんしてき)な対応(たいおう) means handling a situation with courtesy and fairness.
