志願者(しがんしゃ)

しがんしゃ
noun
applicant; volunteer; candidate
1. applicant; volunteer; candidate
A person who voluntarily applies or puts themselves forward for something, such as military service, a position, or a task. Implies willingness and initiative.
志願者(しがんしゃ)を募集(ぼしゅう)する。
To recruit volunteers.
今年(ことし)の志願者(しがんしゃ)数(すう)は過去最高(かこさいこう)だった。
The number of applicants this year was the highest ever.
危険(きけん)な任務(にんむ)にもかかわらず、多(おお)くの志願者(しがんしゃ)が名乗(なの)り出(で)た。
Despite the dangerous mission, many volunteers came forward.

Composed of 志願(しがん) (volunteering, application by choice) + 者(しゃ) (person). Emphasizes that the person acts of their own will, not under compulsion. Originally associated with military volunteering, now used broadly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 志願者(しがんしゃ)を募(つの)る: to solicit volunteers
  • 志願者(しがんしゃ)数(すう): number of applicants
  • 志願者(しがんしゃ)が殺到(さっとう)する: to be flooded with applicants
  • 志願者(しがんしゃ)が名乗(なの)り出(で)る: for a volunteer to come forward

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 応募者(おうぼしゃ): applicant — responding to a public call or advertisement
  • 候補者(こうほしゃ): candidate — someone nominated or standing for selection
  • ボランティア: volunteer — the loanword, more associated with community service
