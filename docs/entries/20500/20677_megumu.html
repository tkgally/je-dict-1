1. to give (charitably); to give alms; to show mercy
To give something to someone less fortunate out of compassion or charity.
貧(まず)しい人(ひと)に食(た)べ物(もの)を恵(めぐ)む。
To give food to a poor person.
道端(みちばた)で物乞(ものご)いに小銭(こぜに)を恵(めぐ)んだ。
I gave some coins to a beggar on the street.
どうかお恵(めぐ)みを。
Please show mercy. / Please give me alms.
2. to bless; to bestow; to be blessed with
For nature, fortune, or a higher power to grant favorable conditions or gifts. Often used in the passive form 恵まれる.
天候(てんこう)に恵(めぐ)まれた。
We were blessed with good weather.
才能(さいのう)に恵(めぐ)まれている。
To be blessed with talent.
自然(しぜん)に恵(めぐ)まれた土地(とち)で育(そだ)った野菜(やさい)は格別(かくべつ)だ。
Vegetables grown on land blessed with natural beauty are exceptional.

The passive form 恵(めぐ)まれる (to be blessed with) is extremely common and used even more frequently than the active form. It expresses being fortunate or gifted with favorable circumstances.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天候(てんこう)に恵(めぐ)まれる: to be blessed with good weather
  • 才能(さいのう)に恵(めぐ)まれる: to be blessed with talent
  • 環境(かんきょう)に恵(めぐ)まれる: to be blessed with a good environment
  • お恵(めぐ)み: alms, charity (noun form)
  • 恵(めぐ)みの雨(あめ): welcome rain, blessed rain

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 施(ほどこ)す: to give (charity), to perform — more formal, literary
  • 与(あた)える: to give, to grant — neutral, does not imply charity
  • 授(さず)ける: to bestow, to confer — more formal, implies granting from above
