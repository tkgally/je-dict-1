1. to surround; to encircle; to gather around
For people or things to form a ring or group around someone or something. Can describe a physical surrounding or a more figurative gathering around a central point.
記者(きしゃ)たちが選手(せんしゅ)を取(と)り囲(かこ)んだ。
Reporters surrounded the athlete.
テーブルを取(と)り囲(かこ)んで食事(しょくじ)をする。
To sit around the table and eat.
警察(けいさつ)が建物(たてもの)を取(と)り囲(かこ)んで犯人(はんにん)の逃走(とうそう)を防(ふせ)いだ。
The police surrounded the building to prevent the suspect from escaping.

A compound verb combining 取(と)り (intensifying prefix from 取(と)る) and 囲(かこ)む (to surround). The 取(と)り prefix adds a sense of completeness or thoroughness to the surrounding action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を取(と)り囲(かこ)む: to surround a person
  • テーブルを取(と)り囲(かこ)む: to gather around a table
  • 建物(たてもの)を取(と)り囲(かこ)む: to surround a building
  • 大勢(おおぜい)で取(と)り囲(かこ)む: to surround in large numbers

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 囲(かこ)む: to surround — simpler form, also used figuratively for enjoying things together (e.g., 鍋(なべ)を囲(かこ)む)
  • 包囲(ほうい)する: to besiege, to lay siege — more formal and military
  • 取(と)り巻(ま)く: to surround — often used for circumstances or environment
