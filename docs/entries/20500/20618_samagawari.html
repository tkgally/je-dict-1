1. complete change; transformation; dramatic shift
A thorough change in appearance, character, or situation, usually implying the result is noticeably different from before.
街(まち)がすっかり様変(さまが)わりした。
The town has completely transformed.
ここ十年(じゅうねん)で教育(きょういく)の現場(げんば)は様変(さまが)わりしている。
The education landscape has been transformed over the past ten years.
スマートフォンの普及(ふきゅう)で人(ひと)々の生活(せいかつ)は大(おお)きく様変(さまが)わりした。
People's lifestyles have changed dramatically with the spread of smartphones.

Composed of 様(さま) (appearance, manner) + 変(か)わり (change). Literally means the appearance or state of something has changed. Implies a visible, often dramatic transformation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すっかり様変(さまが)わりする (to change completely)
  • 大(おお)きく様変(さまが)わりする (to change dramatically)
  • 街(まち)が様変(さまが)わりする (for a town to transform)
  • 時代(じだい)とともに様変(さまが)わりする (to transform with the times)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一変(いっぺん) — a sudden, complete change; emphasizes the abruptness
  • 変貌(へんぼう) — transformation, metamorphosis; more literary and dramatic
  • 変化(へんか) — change; the most general and neutral term

USAGE:
様変(さまが)わり is commonly used in news and commentary to describe how places, industries, or customs have been transformed over time. It often carries a tone of observation or reflection rather than judgment.
