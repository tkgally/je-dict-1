1. disconnection; broken wire; severed line
The breaking or severing of a wire, cable, or electrical line, causing a loss of connection or power.
ケーブルが断線(だんせん)した。
The cable broke.
イヤホンの断線(だんせん)で片方(かたほう)から音(おと)が出(で)なくなった。
Due to a broken wire in the earphones, sound stopped coming from one side.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で送電線(そうでんせん)が断線(だんせん)し、広(ひろ)い範囲(はんい)で停電(ていでん)が発生(はっせい)した。
Due to the typhoon, power lines were severed, causing widespread power outages.

Composed of 断(だん) (sever/cut) + 線(せん) (line/wire). Refers to the physical breaking of a wire or cable, whether in small electronics or large infrastructure.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 断線(だんせん)する: to break/disconnect (as a verb with する)
  • ケーブルの断線(だんせん): cable breakage
  • 送電線(そうでんせん)の断線(だんせん): power line break
  • 断線(だんせん)箇所(かしょ): point of disconnection
  • 断線(だんせん)を修理(しゅうり)する: to repair a broken wire

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切断(せつだん): severance, cutting — a more general term for cutting, not limited to wires
  • 停電(ていでん): power outage — the result of power line 断線(だんせん), but can also be caused by other factors
  • 接触不良(せっしょくふりょう): poor contact — a different type of electrical problem where the wire is not broken but the connection is loose

NOTE:
In everyday life, 断線(だんせん) most commonly occurs with earphone/headphone cables and charging cables. It is also used in news reports about damage from natural disasters.
