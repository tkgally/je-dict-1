1. to conclude; to reach completion; to come to an end (of a series, story, or project)
For a work, series, or process to reach its natural and intended conclusion.
この漫画(まんが)は全(ぜん)30巻(かん)で完結(かんけつ)した。
This manga concluded at 30 volumes.
連載(れんさい)が完結(かんけつ)するのを楽(たの)しみにしている。
I'm looking forward to the serialization reaching its conclusion.
十年(じゅうねん)続(つづ)いたドラマシリーズがついに完結(かんけつ)し、多(おお)くのファンが涙(なみだ)を流(なが)した。
The drama series that continued for ten years finally concluded, and many fans shed tears.
2. to be complete in itself; to be self-contained
For something to form a complete whole without requiring anything additional.
この一冊(いっさつ)で完結(かんけつ)している。
It is complete in this single volume.
一話(いちわ)完結(かんけつ)のドラマが好(す)きだ。
I like dramas where each episode is self-contained.
この物語(ものがたり)は短編(たんぺん)ながら、見事(みごと)に完結(かんけつ)している。
Though this story is a short piece, it is admirably self-contained.

Especially common in discussions of manga, anime, novels, and TV series. 完結(かんけつ) emphasizes that something has reached its proper, intended ending — not just stopped, but concluded with finality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完結(かんけつ)済(ず)み: already concluded
  • 一話(いちわ)完結(かんけつ): self-contained per episode
  • 完結(かんけつ)編(へん): concluding arc/volume
  • 完結(かんけつ)巻(かん): final volume
  • 全(ぜん)X巻(かん)で完結(かんけつ): concluded in X volumes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完了(かんりょう)する: to complete — more procedural, used for tasks and processes
  • 終了(しゅうりょう)する: to end — simply stopping, without the nuance of a satisfying conclusion
  • 終結(しゅうけつ)する: to come to an end — used for conflicts, disputes, or negotiations
