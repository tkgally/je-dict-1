1. to imagine; to visualize; to picture; to form a mental image of
To create or hold a mental image of something. Used both for deliberate visualization and for the general impression one has of something.
成功(せいこう)した自分(じぶん)をイメージする。
To picture yourself succeeding.
完成(かんせい)形(けい)をイメージしてから作(つく)り始(はじ)める。
I start making it after visualizing the finished form.
日本(にほん)と聞(き)いて、どんなことをイメージしますか。
When you hear 'Japan,' what do you picture?

A する verb from the English loanword 'image.' Widely used in everyday Japanese, especially in contexts of mental visualization, branding, and impressions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～をイメージする: to imagine/picture ~
  • イメージしやすい: easy to imagine
  • イメージしにくい: hard to imagine
  • イメージ通(どお)り: just as imagined

USAGE:
Unlike English 'imagine,' イメージする often focuses on forming a concrete mental picture rather than abstract supposition. For hypothetical scenarios ('imagine if...'), 想像(そうぞう)する is more natural.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 想像(そうぞう)する: to imagine — broader; includes hypothetical and creative imagination
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる: to call to mind, to picture — native Japanese equivalent
  • 連想(れんそう)する: to associate (mentally) — triggered association rather than deliberate visualization
