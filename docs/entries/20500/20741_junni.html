順(じゅん)に

じゅんに
adverb
in order; one by one; in sequence
1. in order; one by one; in sequence; in turn
Following a set order or sequence, dealing with things one at a time from first to last.
順(じゅん)に並(なら)んでください。
Please line up in order.
番号(ばんごう)順(じゅん)に名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)ぶ。
To call names in numerical order.
一(ひと)つずつ順(じゅん)に説明(せつめい)しますので、質問(しつもん)は最後(さいご)にお願(ねが)いします。
I'll explain things one by one in order, so please save questions for the end.

The noun 順(じゅん) (order/sequence) combined with the particle に to function as an adverb meaning 'in order.' Extremely common in daily life, from queuing to following instructions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 順(じゅん)に並(なら)ぶ: to line up in order
  • 順(じゅん)に呼(よ)ぶ: to call in order
  • 順(じゅん)に説明(せつめい)する: to explain in order
  • 上(うえ)から順(じゅん)に: from the top, in order
  • 大(おお)きい順(じゅん)に: in order from largest

USAGE:
Often combined with a criterion: 番号(ばんごう)順(じゅん)に (in numerical order), 年齢(ねんれい)順(じゅん)に (in order of age), 到着(とうちゃく)順(じゅん)に (in order of arrival). The criterion + 順(じゅん)に pattern is very productive.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 順番(じゅんばん)に: in turn — emphasizes taking turns among people
  • 順々(じゅんじゅん)に: one after another — emphasizes the sequential, continuing nature
  • 次々(つぎつぎ)に: one after another — faster pace; less about order, more about rapid succession
