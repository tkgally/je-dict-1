1. to tell; to let someone hear; to read aloud to
To convey information or a story to someone by speaking. Often used for telling stories, reading aloud, or sharing news.
子供(こども)に絵本(えほん)を聞(き)かせる。
To read a picture book aloud to a child.
旅行(りょこう)の話(はなし)を聞(き)かせてください。
Please tell me about your trip.
寝(ね)る前(まえ)に子供(こども)たちに昔話(むかしばなし)を聞(き)かせるのが日課(にっか)だ。
It's my daily routine to tell the children folk tales before bed.
2. to play (music) for someone; to perform for someone to hear
To play music or sing so that someone can listen and appreciate it.
新曲(しんきょく)を聞(き)かせる。
To play a new song for someone.
彼女(かのじょ)はピアノで美(うつく)しい曲(きょく)を聞(き)かせてくれた。
She played a beautiful piece on the piano for us.
いつか自分(じぶん)の歌(うた)をたくさんの人(ひと)に聞(き)かせたい。
Someday I want to let lots of people hear my songs.

The causative form of 聞(き)く (to hear/listen), used as an independent verb meaning to let someone hear or to tell someone something. Unlike the grammatical causative, 聞(き)かせる is commonly treated as its own word with a nuance of sharing or performing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 話(はなし)を聞(き)かせる: to tell a story
  • 絵本(えほん)を聞(き)かせる: to read a picture book aloud
  • 歌(うた)を聞(き)かせる: to sing a song for someone
  • 意見(いけん)を聞(き)かせる: to share one's opinion

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 聞(き)かせてください: please tell me (polite request)
  • 聞(き)かせてくれる: to tell/play for someone (favor nuance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 話(はな)す: to speak, to tell — neutral, no emphasis on the listener's experience
  • 語(かた)る: to narrate, to tell — more literary, for longer stories
  • 伝(つた)える: to convey, to communicate — focuses on the information being transmitted
