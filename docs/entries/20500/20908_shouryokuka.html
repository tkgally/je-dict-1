1. labor-saving; mechanization; making more efficient
The process of reducing the amount of human labor needed through technology, automation, or improved processes. Common in business and manufacturing contexts.
工場(こうじょう)の省力化(しょうりょくか)を進(すす)める。
To promote labor-saving in the factory.
AIの導入(どうにゅう)で事務作業(じむさぎょう)の省力化(しょうりょくか)が可能(かのう)になった。
The introduction of AI has made it possible to reduce labor in office work.
人手不足(ひとでぶそく)に対応(たいおう)するため、農業(のうぎょう)の省力化(しょうりょくか)が急(いそ)がれている。
To cope with labor shortages, labor-saving measures in agriculture are being urgently pursued.

Composed of 省力(しょうりょく) (saving labor) + 化(か) (-ization, making into). A key concept in Japanese business and manufacturing, especially given Japan's aging population and labor shortages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 省力化(しょうりょくか)を進(すす)める: to promote labor-saving
  • 省力化(しょうりょくか)を図(はか)る: to aim for labor-saving
  • 省力化(しょうりょくか)技術(ぎじゅつ): labor-saving technology
  • 省力化(しょうりょくか)投資(とうし): investment in automation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自動化(じどうか): automation — specifically using machines to replace manual processes
  • 効率化(こうりつか): improving efficiency — broader, not limited to labor
  • 合理化(ごうりか): rationalization — streamlining processes, sometimes implies job cuts
