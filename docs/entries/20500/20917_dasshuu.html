1. deodorization; odor removal; deodorizing
The process of removing or neutralizing unpleasant odors. Used for products, appliances, and industrial processes.
脱臭(だっしゅう)フィルターを交換(こうかん)する。
To replace the deodorizing filter.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に脱臭剤(だっしゅうざい)を入(い)れる。
To put a deodorizer in the refrigerator.
活性炭(かっせいたん)には優(すぐ)れた脱臭(だっしゅう)効果(こうか)がある。
Activated charcoal has excellent deodorizing effects.

Composed of 脱(だつ) (removal, de-) + 臭(しゅう) (odor, smell). A practical term encountered in everyday life when discussing air purifiers, refrigerator products, and cleaning supplies.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脱臭剤(だっしゅうざい): deodorizer
  • 脱臭(だっしゅう)フィルター: deodorizing filter
  • 脱臭(だっしゅう)効果(こうか): deodorizing effect
  • 脱臭(だっしゅう)機能(きのう): deodorizing function
  • 脱臭(だっしゅう)炭(すみ): deodorizing charcoal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 消臭(しょうしゅう): deodorizing, odor elimination — very similar, but more common in product marketing (e.g., 消臭(しょうしゅう)スプレー = deodorizing spray)
  • 防臭(ぼうしゅう): odor prevention — preventing odors from occurring in the first place
  • 芳香(ほうこう): fragrance — adding a pleasant smell rather than removing a bad one
