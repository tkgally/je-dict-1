1. to be fed up; to be sick and tired of; to be weary of
To feel exhausted patience or strong boredom with something repetitive, tedious, or irritating.
もううんざりだ。
I've had enough.
長(なが)い会議(かいぎ)にうんざりした。
I was fed up with the long meeting.
彼(かれ)の自慢話(じまんばなし)にはうんざりさせられる。
His bragging really makes me sick and tired.

An onomatopoeic expression conveying strong weariness or boredom. Stronger than 飽(あ)きる and carries more exhaustion than 嫌(いや)になる. Often used when patience has been completely worn out.

USAGE:
Can be used as an adverb (うんざりする), a na-adjective-like form (うんざりだ), or in a causative pattern (うんざりさせる). The source of frustration is marked by に: Xにうんざりする.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xにうんざりする: to be fed up with X
  • うんざりするほど: to the point of being sick of it
  • うんざりさせる: to make someone fed up
  • うんざりした顔(かお): a fed-up expression

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 嫌(いや)になる: to become fed up — similar but slightly milder
  • 飽(あ)き飽(あ)きする: to be completely bored — emphasizes extreme tedium
  • げんなりする: to feel disheartened — similar weariness but with more deflation than irritation
