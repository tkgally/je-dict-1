1. becoming more sophisticated; growing more cunning or subtle
The process of something — typically methods, techniques, or schemes — becoming more elaborate, clever, or harder to detect. Almost always used in negative contexts like crime, fraud, or cyberattacks.
詐欺(さぎ)の手口(てぐち)が巧妙化(こうみょうか)している。
Fraud techniques are becoming more sophisticated.
サイバー攻撃(こうげき)は年々(ねんねん)巧妙化(こうみょうか)している。
Cyberattacks are becoming more sophisticated year by year.
犯罪(はんざい)の巧妙化(こうみょうか)に対応(たいおう)するため、警察(けいさつ)も新(あたら)しい捜査(そうさ)技術(ぎじゅつ)を導入(どうにゅう)している。
To respond to the growing sophistication of crime, the police are also adopting new investigation techniques.

A compound of 巧妙(こうみょう) (clever, ingenious, cunning) and 化(か) (-ization). Functions as a suru-verb: 巧妙化(こうみょうか)する.

USAGE:
Almost exclusively used in negative contexts — describing how criminal methods, scams, cyberattacks, or deceptive practices are becoming harder to detect and counter. Very common in news reports about crime and security.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手口(てぐち)の巧妙化(こうみょうか) — sophistication of methods
  • 巧妙化(こうみょうか)する詐欺(さぎ) — increasingly sophisticated fraud
  • 巧妙化(こうみょうか)・多様化(たようか) — becoming more sophisticated and diverse
  • 巧妙化(こうみょうか)に対応(たいおう)する — to respond to growing sophistication

RELATED TERMS:

  • 高度化(こうどか) — becoming more advanced; neutral, used for technology and systems
  • 悪質化(あくしつか) — becoming more malicious; focuses on increasing harm
  • 複雑化(ふくざつか) — becoming more complex; neutral, broader in application
