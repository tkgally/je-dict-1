1. bidding; tender; bid
The act of submitting a price or proposal in a competitive process, typically for government contracts, construction projects, or auctions.
公開(こうかい)入札(にゅうさつ)を行(おこな)う。
To hold an open bidding process.
この工事(こうじ)は入札(にゅうさつ)で業者(ぎょうしゃ)を決(き)める。
The contractor for this construction project will be decided through bidding.
三(さん)社(しゃ)が入札(にゅうさつ)に参加(さんか)したが、最(もっと)も安(やす)い価格(かかく)を提示(ていじ)した会社(かいしゃ)が落札(らくさつ)した。
Three companies participated in the bidding, and the company that offered the lowest price won the bid.

The formal process of submitting competitive bids, especially for public contracts. Composed of 入(にゅう) (enter/submit) + 札(さつ) (card/slip — here referring to the bid document). Often used as a する verb: 入札(にゅうさつ)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入札(にゅうさつ)する: to submit a bid
  • 入札(にゅうさつ)に参加(さんか)する: to participate in bidding
  • 公開(こうかい)入札(にゅうさつ): open/public bidding
  • 競争(きょうそう)入札(にゅうさつ): competitive bidding
  • 指名(しめい)入札(にゅうさつ): invited bidding (restricted to selected firms)
  • 入札(にゅうさつ)価格(かかく): bid price

RELATED TERMS:

  • 落札(らくさつ): winning a bid — the successful bidder
  • 開札(かいさつ): opening of bids
  • 談合(だんごう): bid-rigging — illegal collusion among bidders

CULTURAL NOTE:
Public 入札(にゅうさつ) is a key process in Japanese government procurement. 談合(だんごう) (bid-rigging) has been a recurring scandal in Japanese public works, leading to stricter regulations.
