1. to turn inside out; to be turned inside out
For clothing, fabric, or similar items to have their inner side exposed.
靴下(くつした)が裏返(うらがえ)っている。
The socks are inside out.
傘(かさ)が風(かぜ)で裏返(うらがえ)った。
The umbrella flipped inside out in the wind.
Tシャツが裏返(うらがえ)ったまま着(き)ていることに気(き)づかなかった。
I didn't notice I was wearing my T-shirt inside out.
2. to crack; to flip (of one's voice)
For a person's voice to suddenly jump to a higher pitch or break, often involuntarily.
緊張(きんちょう)して声(こえ)が裏返(うらがえ)った。
My voice cracked from nervousness.
歌(うた)の途中(とちゅう)で声(こえ)が裏返(うらがえ)った。
My voice cracked in the middle of the song.
大声(おおごえ)を出(だ)そうとしたら声(こえ)が裏返(うらがえ)って恥(は)ずかしかった。
I tried to shout but my voice cracked and it was embarrassing.

Composed of 裏(うら) (back side, reverse) + 返(かえ)る (to return). The physical "inside out" sense is the basic meaning, while the voice-cracking sense (sense 2) is very commonly heard in everyday conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 靴下(くつした)が裏返(うらがえ)る: socks turn inside out
  • 傘(かさ)が裏返(うらがえ)る: an umbrella flips inside out
  • 声(こえ)が裏返(うらがえ)る: one's voice cracks/flips

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive counterpart is 裏返(うらがえ)す (to turn something inside out, to flip something over).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裏返(うらがえ)す: to turn inside out (transitive)
  • ひっくり返(かえ)る: to flip over, to be overturned (more dramatic, physical)
  • 上擦(うわず)る: for one's voice to become shrill (from excitement or nervousness)
