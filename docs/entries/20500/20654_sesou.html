世相(せそう)

せそう
noun
social conditions; state of the world; public mood
1. social conditions; state of the world; state of society
The general atmosphere, trends, and conditions of society at a given time. Encompasses economic conditions, public sentiment, cultural trends, and the overall mood of an era.
世相(せそう)を反映(はんえい)する。
To reflect the state of society.
今年(ことし)の漢字(かんじ)は世相(せそう)をよく表(あらわ)している。
This year's kanji captures the state of society well.
小説(しょうせつ)を通(とお)して当時(とうじ)の世相(せそう)を知(し)ることができる。
Through novels, you can learn about the social conditions of the time.

Refers to the overall state of society at a particular point in time — its mood, trends, and conditions. Often used when discussing how art, media, or cultural events capture or reflect the spirit of an era.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世相(せそう)を反映(はんえい)する: to reflect the state of society
  • 世相(せそう)を映(うつ)す: to mirror social conditions
  • 世相(せそう)を表(あらわ)す: to represent the state of the world
  • 現代(げんだい)の世相(せそう): contemporary social conditions
  • 暗(くら)い世相(せそう): grim state of the world

USAGE:
Commonly appears in media commentary, literary criticism, and year-end reflections. Japan's annual '今年(ことし)の漢字(かんじ)' (kanji of the year) event explicitly aims to capture the 世相(せそう) of that year in a single character.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 世情(せじょう): the ways of the world, social conditions (more literary)
  • 時勢(じせい): the times, current trends (emphasizes temporal trends)
  • 風潮(ふうちょう): trend, tendency (focuses on a particular social movement or direction)
