企(くわだ)て

くわだて
noun
plan; plot; scheme; attempt
1. plan; scheme; plot; attempt
A plan or scheme, often with a nuance of something ambitious, secretive, or potentially underhanded. Can refer to both positive undertakings and negative plots.
企(くわだ)てが失敗(しっぱい)した。
The plan failed.
大胆(だいたん)な企(くわだ)てを実行(じっこう)に移(うつ)す。
To put a bold plan into action.
彼(かれ)の企(くわだ)ては事前(じぜん)に発覚(はっかく)して未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)がれた。
His plot was discovered beforehand and prevented.

The noun form of the verb 企(くわだ)てる (to plan, to scheme). While it can refer to neutral or positive plans, it often carries a nuance of ambition, secrecy, or cunning — closer to English "scheme" or "plot" than a simple "plan."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 企(くわだ)てを実行(じっこう)する: to carry out a plan
  • 企(くわだ)てが失敗(しっぱい)する: for a plan to fail
  • 企(くわだ)てが発覚(はっかく)する: for a plot to be discovered
  • 大胆(だいたん)な企(くわだ)て: a bold scheme

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 計画(けいかく): plan — neutral and general-purpose; the most common word for "plan"
  • 陰謀(いんぼう): conspiracy — stronger negative connotation, implies a hidden group effort
  • 企画(きかく): project/planning — used in business contexts for project proposals
