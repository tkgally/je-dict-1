すらり

すらり
adverb
slenderly; smoothly; in one swift motion
1. slenderly; tall and slim
Describes a person or object that is attractively tall and slender, with elegant proportions.
すらりとした体型(たいけい)。
A slender figure.
彼女(かのじょ)はすらりと背(せ)が高(たか)い。
She is tall and slender.
すらりと伸(の)びた脚(あし)が印象的(いんしょうてき)なモデルだった。
She was a model with impressively long, slender legs.
2. smoothly; in one swift motion
Describes something being drawn out, pulled, or accomplished smoothly and without resistance.
刀(かたな)をすらりと抜(ぬ)いた。
He smoothly drew his sword.
問題(もんだい)がすらりと解(と)けた。
The problem was solved smoothly.
難(むずか)しい質問(しつもん)にもすらりと答(こた)えて、面接官(めんせつかん)を驚(おどろ)かせた。
He answered even the difficult questions smoothly, surprising the interviewer.

A mimetic word (onomatopoeia/擬態語(ぎたいご)) describing elegantly slim proportions or smooth, effortless motion. Almost always used with the particle と as すらりと.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (slender) is most commonly used to describe people, especially women, and their limbs. Sense 2 (smoothly) often appears with actions like drawing a sword, answering questions, or completing tasks without hesitation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すらりとした体型(たいけい) — a slender figure
  • すらりと伸(の)びた脚(あし) — long, slender legs
  • すらりと抜(ぬ)く — to draw out smoothly
  • すらりと答(こた)える — to answer smoothly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すらっと — variant form with the same meanings; slightly more casual
  • ほっそり — slimly, delicately thin; focuses on thinness rather than height
  • すんなり — smoothly, without difficulty; focuses on lack of resistance
