1. to persist in; to insist on; to stick stubbornly to
To refuse to change one's opinion, position, or method despite pressure or reason to do so. Implies stubbornness and inflexibility.
自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)に固執(こしつ)する。
To insist on one's own opinion.
古(ふる)いやり方(かた)に固執(こしつ)していては進歩(しんぽ)がない。
There's no progress if you cling to the old way of doing things.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の立場(たちば)に固執(こしつ)して、誰(だれ)の話(はなし)も聞(き)こうとしなかった。
He stubbornly stuck to his position and refused to listen to anyone.

A する verb meaning to stubbornly persist in or cling to something. Composed of 固(こ) (solid, firm) + 執(しつ) (hold, grasp). Always negative in connotation, implying that the person is unreasonably rigid.

PRONUNCIATION:
Also read こしゅう, though こしつ is more standard in modern usage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意見(いけん)に固執(こしつ)する: to insist on one's opinion
  • 立場(たちば)に固執(こしつ)する: to cling to one's position
  • 方法(ほうほう)に固執(こしつ)する: to stick stubbornly to a method
  • 前例(ぜんれい)に固執(こしつ)する: to cling to precedent

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 執着(しゅうちゃく)する: to be attached to, to be obsessed with — broader, includes emotional attachment to things or people
  • こだわる: to be particular about — can be positive (pursuit of quality) or negative
  • 頑固(がんこ): stubborn — an adjective describing the person's character rather than a specific action
