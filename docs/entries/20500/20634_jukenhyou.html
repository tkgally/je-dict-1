受験票(じゅけんひょう)

じゅけんひょう
noun
examination admission ticket
1. examination admission ticket; exam entry pass
A document that a test-taker must bring to be admitted to an examination. It typically includes the candidate's name, photo, exam number, and venue information.
受験票(じゅけんひょう)を忘(わす)れないでください。
Please don't forget your exam admission ticket.
受験票(じゅけんひょう)が届(とど)いたので、いよいよ本番(ほんばん)だと実感(じっかん)した。
My exam ticket arrived, and it finally felt real that the exam was approaching.
当日(とうじつ)は受験票(じゅけんひょう)と写真付(しゃしんつ)きの身分証明書(みぶんしょうめいしょ)を持参(じさん)してください。
On the day, please bring your exam admission ticket and a photo ID.

Composed of 受験(じゅけん) (taking an exam) + 票(ひょう) (slip, ticket, ballot). An essential document for anyone sitting a formal examination in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受験票(じゅけんひょう)を持参(じさん)する (to bring one's exam ticket)
  • 受験票(じゅけんひょう)が届(とど)く (for the exam ticket to arrive)
  • 受験票(じゅけんひょう)を印刷(いんさつ)する (to print one's exam ticket)
  • 受験票(じゅけんひょう)を紛失(ふんしつ)する (to lose one's exam ticket)
  • 受験票(じゅけんひょう)に写真(しゃしん)を貼(は)る (to affix a photo to one's exam ticket)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 受験番号(じゅけんばんごう) — exam number; the unique identification number printed on the ticket
  • 入場券(にゅうじょうけん) — admission ticket; for general entry to venues, not exams

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, forgetting one's 受験票(じゅけんひょう) on exam day is a well-known source of panic. During major exam seasons (入試(にゅうし)), news programs often feature stories of students who forgot their tickets and the efforts made to accommodate them.
