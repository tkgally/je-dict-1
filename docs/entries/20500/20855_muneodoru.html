1. to be thrilling; to be exciting; to set one's heart racing
To feel excitement, anticipation, or exhilaration. Literally 'the chest dances.' Used to describe the feeling of eager anticipation or being thrilled.
胸躍(むねおど)る冒険(ぼうけん)が始(はじ)まる。
A thrilling adventure begins.
胸躍(むねおど)る体験(たいけん)をさせてもらった。
I was given an exciting experience.
海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)の計画(けいかく)を立(た)てていると、胸(むね)が躍(おど)る。
When I'm planning an overseas trip, my heart races with excitement.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)、遠足(えんそく)の前日(ぜんじつ)はいつも胸(むね)が躍(おど)って眠(ねむ)れなかった。
As a child, the night before a field trip I was always too excited to sleep.

Literally "the chest dances" — a vivid expression for excitement and anticipation. Often used as a modifier in the form 胸躍(むねおど)る + noun (胸躍(むねおど)る体験(たいけん), 胸躍(むねおど)る瞬間(しゅんかん)). Can also be used in the split form 胸(むね)が躍(おど)る.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 胸躍(むねおど)る冒険(ぼうけん): a thrilling adventure
  • 胸躍(むねおど)る体験(たいけん): an exciting experience
  • 胸躍(むねおど)る瞬間(しゅんかん): a thrilling moment
  • 胸(むね)が躍(おど)る: one's heart leaps with excitement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • わくわくする: to be excited, thrilled (more casual and colloquial)
  • 心躍(こころおど)る: to be excited (very close synonym, slightly more literary)
  • 胸(むね)がときめく: to have one's heart flutter (often romantic excitement)
  • ドキドキする: heart pounding (can be excitement or nervousness)
