1. to trap; to ensnare (literally)
To catch an animal or person using a trap or snare.
熊(くま)を罠(わな)にかけた。
They trapped a bear.
猟師(りょうし)が罠(わな)にかけた鹿(しか)を回収(かいしゅう)した。
The hunter retrieved the deer he had trapped.
野生動物(やせいどうぶつ)を罠(わな)にかけるには経験(けいけん)と忍耐(にんたい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Trapping wild animals requires experience and patience.
2. to set someone up; to trick into a trap
To deceive or manipulate someone into a disadvantageous situation through cunning or deception.
彼(かれ)は罠(わな)にかけられた。
He was set up.
敵(てき)を罠(わな)にかける作戦(さくせん)を立(た)てた。
They devised a plan to trap the enemy.
巧妙(こうみょう)な手口(てぐち)で詐欺師(さぎし)が被害者(ひがいしゃ)を罠(わな)にかけた。
The scammer trapped the victim with a clever scheme.

An expression combining 罠(わな) (trap/snare) with かける (to set/apply). Used both literally for animal trapping and figuratively for deceiving or setting people up.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 罠(わな)にはまる: to fall into a trap
  • 罠(わな)を仕掛(しか)ける: to set a trap
  • 罠(わな)から逃(のが)れる: to escape from a trap

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 騙(だま)す: to deceive — more general deception
  • 陥(おとしい)れる: to entrap — more formal, implies causing someone's downfall
  • はめる: to frame — colloquial, to set someone up
