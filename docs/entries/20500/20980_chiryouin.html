治療院(ちりょういん)

ちりょういん
noun
clinic; treatment center; therapy practice
1. clinic; treatment center; therapy practice
A medical or therapeutic facility, often specializing in treatments like acupuncture, massage, chiropractic, or other alternative therapies.
近所(きんじょ)の治療院(ちりょういん)に通(かよ)っている。
I go to a clinic in my neighborhood.
腰痛(ようつう)がひどいので、治療院(ちりょういん)を探(さが)している。
My back pain is bad, so I'm looking for a treatment clinic.
この治療院(ちりょういん)は鍼灸(しんきゅう)と整体(せいたい)の両方(りょうほう)を行(おこな)っている。
This clinic offers both acupuncture and chiropractic treatment.

Compound of 治療(ちりょう) (treatment, therapy) + 院(いん) (institution, clinic). In practice, 治療院(ちりょういん) most commonly refers to clinics providing traditional or alternative therapies rather than Western medical hospitals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 治療院(ちりょういん)に通(かよ)う: to go to a clinic regularly
  • 治療院(ちりょういん)を開(ひら)く: to open a clinic
  • 鍼灸(しんきゅう)治療院(ちりょういん): acupuncture clinic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 病院(びょういん): hospital — larger; provides Western medical care
  • 診療所(しんりょうじょ): medical clinic, doctor's office — smaller than a hospital; Western medicine
  • 接骨院(せっこついん): bone-setting clinic — specifically for bone and joint treatments
