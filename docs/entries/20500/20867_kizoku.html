1. belonging to, being attributed to
The state of something belonging to or being attributed to a particular person, group, or entity. Used in legal, business, and academic contexts for ownership, jurisdiction, or attribution of results.
この権利(けんり)は会社(かいしゃ)に帰属(きぞく)する。
This right belongs to the company.
著作権(ちょさくけん)は原則(げんそく)として著作者(ちょさくしゃ)に帰属(きぞく)する。
Copyright in principle belongs to the author.
成果(せいか)の帰属(きぞく)先(さき)について契約書(けいやくしょ)で明記(めいき)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
It is necessary to specify in the contract where the results will be attributed.
2. sense of belonging, affiliation
The psychological feeling of belonging to a group or organization. Common in discussions of identity, workplace culture, and social psychology.
帰属(きぞく)意識(いしき)が高(たか)い。
The sense of belonging is strong.
社員(しゃいん)の帰属(きぞく)意識(いしき)を高(たか)めることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to strengthen employees' sense of belonging.
リモートワークが増(ふ)えて、組織(そしき)への帰属(きぞく)意識(いしき)が薄(うす)れてきた。
With the increase in remote work, the sense of belonging to the organization has weakened.

A formal word used in legal, business, and psychological contexts. Sense 1 (attribution/ownership) appears frequently in contracts and legal documents. Sense 2 (帰属(きぞく)意識(いしき), sense of belonging) is common in HR and organizational discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に帰属(きぞく)する: to belong to, to be attributed to
  • 帰属(きぞく)意識(いしき): sense of belonging
  • 帰属(きぞく)先(さき): where something belongs/is attributed
  • 権利(けんり)の帰属(きぞく): attribution of rights
  • 領土(りょうど)の帰属(きぞく): territorial sovereignty

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 所属(しょぞく): affiliation, membership — concrete organizational membership (e.g., belonging to a department or team)
  • 隷属(れいぞく): subordination, servitude — being under someone's control in a subservient way
  • 所有(しょゆう): ownership, possession — focuses on having/owning something rather than attribution
