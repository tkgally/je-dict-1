月額制(げつがくせい)

げつがくせい
noun
monthly subscription system; monthly fee system
1. monthly subscription system; monthly fee plan
A pricing or payment system where a fixed amount is charged every month, typically for ongoing services such as streaming, gym memberships, or software.
このサービスは月額制(げつがくせい)です。
This service uses a monthly subscription system.
月額制(げつがくせい)のジムに通(かよ)い始(はじ)めた。
I started going to a gym with a monthly membership fee.
動画(どうが)サービスは月額制(げつがくせい)と年額制(ねんがくせい)が選(えら)べるが、年額制(ねんがくせい)のほうが割安(わりやす)だ。
For the video service, you can choose between monthly and annual plans, but the annual plan is cheaper.

Composed of 月額(げつがく) (monthly amount) + 制(せい) (system). Refers to a billing model where a fixed amount is charged per month.

USAGE:
Very common in modern Japanese for describing subscription services. Often used for streaming platforms, software services, gym memberships, and phone plans.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 月額制(げつがくせい)のサービス: a monthly subscription service
  • 月額制(げつがくせい)で利用(りよう)する: to use on a monthly subscription basis
  • 月額制(げつがくせい)に加入(かにゅう)する: to sign up for a monthly plan

RELATED TERMS:

  • 年額制(ねんがくせい): annual subscription system — charged once per year
  • 従量制(じゅうりょうせい): usage-based system — charged based on how much you use
  • サブスクリプション: subscription — the loanword equivalent, often shortened to サブスク
  • 定額制(ていがくせい): flat-rate system — a broader term for fixed-price plans
