悲哀(ひあい)

ひあい
noun
sorrow; grief; pathos
1. sorrow; grief; pathos; sadness
A deep, often quiet sadness or grief. A literary word conveying profound emotional pain, frequently used in writing and formal expression.
悲哀(ひあい)に満(み)ちた表情(ひょうじょう)。
An expression full of sorrow.
その音楽(おんがく)には深(ふか)い悲哀(ひあい)が込(こ)められている。
Deep sorrow is embedded in that music.
戦争(せんそう)の悲哀(ひあい)を描(えが)いた小説(しょうせつ)が心(こころ)に残(のこ)った。
A novel that depicted the grief of war stayed with me.

A literary word composed of 悲(ひ) (sad, sorrowful) + 哀(あい) (grief, pity). Both characters independently convey sadness; together they express profound, quiet grief. Common in literature, music criticism, and formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悲哀(ひあい)に満(み)ちた: filled with sorrow
  • 悲哀(ひあい)を感(かん)じる: to feel sorrow
  • 悲哀(ひあい)を帯(お)びた: tinged with sorrow
  • 人生(じんせい)の悲哀(ひあい): the sorrow of life

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悲(かな)しみ: sadness — the most common everyday word
  • 哀愁(あいしゅう): melancholy, pathos — similar literary register, but with a more wistful, nostalgic quality
  • 嘆(なげ)き: lamentation — active grieving, more vocal
  • 哀(あわ)れ: pathos, pity — a classical aesthetic concept in Japanese literature

REGISTER:
Primarily a written/literary word. In everyday conversation, 悲(かな)しみ or 悲(かな)しさ would be used instead.
