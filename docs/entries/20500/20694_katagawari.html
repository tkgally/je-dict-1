1. taking over (a debt, burden, or responsibility) for someone; shouldering someone else's obligation
Assuming another person's debt, financial obligation, or responsibility in their place. Literally means 'shoulder substitution.'
借金(しゃっきん)を肩代(かたが)わりする。
To take over someone's debt.
親(おや)が子供(こども)のローンを肩代(かたが)わりした。
The parents took over their child's loan.
会社(かいしゃ)が従業員(じゅうぎょういん)の引(ひ)っ越(こ)し費用(ひよう)を肩代(かたが)わりしてくれることになった。
The company agreed to cover the employees' moving expenses.

Composed of 肩(かた) (shoulder) + 代(か)わり (substitution). The image is of shifting a burden from one person's shoulders to another's.

USAGE:
Most commonly used with する to form the verb 肩代(かたが)わりする. Typically involves financial obligations — debts, loans, costs, or fees — but can extend to responsibilities in general.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 借金(しゃっきん)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to take over someone's debt
  • 費用(ひよう)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to cover someone's expenses
  • ローンを肩代(かたが)わりする: to take over a loan
  • 責任(せきにん)を肩代(かたが)わりする: to take on someone's responsibility

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立(た)て替(か)える: to pay on someone's behalf — with the expectation of being repaid later
  • 引(ひ)き受(う)ける: to take on, to undertake — broader, not limited to financial matters
  • 負担(ふたん)する: to bear (a cost) — focuses on the act of paying, not on replacing someone else
