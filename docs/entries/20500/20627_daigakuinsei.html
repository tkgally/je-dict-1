大学院生(だいがくいんせい)

だいがくいんせい
noun
graduate student
1. graduate student; postgraduate student
A student enrolled in a graduate school (大学院). Includes both master's and doctoral students.
姉(あね)は大学院生(だいがくいんせい)です。
My older sister is a graduate student.
大学院生(だいがくいんせい)として研究(けんきゅう)を続(つづ)けている。
I'm continuing my research as a graduate student.
この研究室(けんきゅうしつ)には大学院生(だいがくいんせい)が十人(じゅうにん)ほど在籍(ざいせき)している。
About ten graduate students are enrolled in this laboratory.

Composed of 大学院(だいがくいん) (graduate school) + 生(せい) (student). Often abbreviated to 院生(いんせい) in casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大学院生(だいがくいんせい)として研究(けんきゅう)する (to do research as a grad student)
  • 大学院生(だいがくいんせい)向(む)けの奨学金(しょうがくきん) (scholarships for grad students)
  • 大学院生(だいがくいんせい)の生活(せいかつ) (grad student life)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 院生(いんせい) — grad student; common abbreviation, used in casual and campus contexts
  • 学部生(がくぶせい) — undergraduate student
  • 研究生(けんきゅうせい) — research student; a non-degree status at Japanese universities, often for international students preparing for entrance exams
  • 博士課程(はくしかてい) — doctoral program; specifies the level within graduate school

USAGE:
In the Japanese education system, 大学院(だいがくいん) covers both the 修士課程(しゅうしかてい) (master's program, typically 2 years) and the 博士課程(はくしかてい) (doctoral program, typically 3 years). 大学院生(だいがくいんせい) refers to students at either level.
