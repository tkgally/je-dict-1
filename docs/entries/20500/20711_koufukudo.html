幸福度(こうふくど)

こうふくど
noun
level of happiness; happiness index
1. level of happiness; degree of happiness; happiness index
A measure or degree of how happy someone is, or how happy the people of a country or region are. Often used in surveys, rankings, and research on well-being.
幸福度(こうふくど)ランキングで上位(じょうい)に入(はい)る。
To rank high in the happiness index.
フィンランドは世界(せかい)幸福度(こうふくど)ランキングで常(つね)に上位(じょうい)だ。
Finland is always at the top of the world happiness rankings.
お金(かね)だけで幸福度(こうふくど)が決(き)まるわけではないという研究(けんきゅう)結果(けっか)がある。
There is research showing that happiness levels are not determined by money alone.

Composed of 幸福(こうふく) (happiness) + 度(ど) (degree/level). A widely used term in discussions of well-being, social policy, and international comparisons.

USAGE:
Frequently appears in news articles about the 世界(せかい)幸福度(こうふくど)ランキング (World Happiness Report) and in discussions of work-life balance, social welfare, and quality of life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幸福度(こうふくど)が高(たか)い: happiness level is high
  • 幸福度(こうふくど)ランキング: happiness ranking
  • 世界(せかい)幸福度(こうふくど)調査(ちょうさ): world happiness survey
  • 国民(こくみん)幸福度(こうふくど): national happiness level

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 満足度(まんぞくど): satisfaction level — focuses on contentment with a specific thing
  • 生活(せいかつ)の質(しつ): quality of life — broader concept of living standards
  • 幸福感(こうふくかん): sense of happiness — the subjective feeling rather than a measured index

NOTE:
The suffix 度(ど) is productive for creating 'degree of' compounds: 満足度(まんぞくど) (satisfaction level), 認知度(にんちど) (recognition level), 達成度(たっせいど) (achievement level).
