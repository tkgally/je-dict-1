防錆(ぼうせい)

ぼうせい
noun
rust prevention, corrosion prevention
1. rust prevention, corrosion prevention, anti-rust treatment
The act of preventing rust or corrosion on metal surfaces, or the property of being rust-resistant. Used in automotive, manufacturing, and construction contexts.
防錆(ぼうせい)処理(しょり)を施(ほどこ)す。
To apply rust-prevention treatment.
この塗料(とりょう)には防錆(ぼうせい)効果(こうか)がある。
This paint has anti-rust properties.
海(うみ)の近(ちか)くに住(す)んでいるので、車(くるま)の防錆(ぼうせい)対策(たいさく)は欠(か)かせない。
Since I live near the ocean, rust-prevention measures for my car are essential.

A compound of 防(ぼう) (prevention, protection) + 錆(せい) (rust, corrosion). Sometimes also read as ぼうさび, using the kun'yomi of 錆(さび). The on'yomi reading ぼうせい is more common in technical and industrial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 防錆(ぼうせい)処理(しょり): anti-rust treatment
  • 防錆(ぼうせい)塗料(とりょう): anti-rust paint
  • 防錆(ぼうせい)剤(ざい): rust inhibitor
  • 防錆(ぼうせい)対策(たいさく): rust-prevention measures
  • 防錆(ぼうせい)効果(こうか): anti-rust effect
  • 防錆(ぼうせい)加工(かこう): anti-rust processing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 耐食(たいしょく): corrosion resistance — broader term covering resistance to any chemical deterioration
  • 錆止(さびど)め: rust prevention — a more colloquial native Japanese word with the same meaning
