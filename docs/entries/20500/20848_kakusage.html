1. downgrade; demotion; relegation
Lowering something or someone in rank, status, rating, or classification. Used in business, finance, sports, and general contexts.
信用(しんよう)格付(かくづ)けが格下(かくさ)げされた。
The credit rating was downgraded.
成績(せいせき)不振(ふしん)で二軍(にぐん)に格下(かくさ)げになった。
Due to poor performance, he was demoted to the second team.
大型(おおがた)の台風(たいふう)が熱帯(ねったい)低気圧(ていきあつ)に格下(かくさ)げされた。
The large typhoon was downgraded to a tropical depression.
部長(ぶちょう)から課長(かちょう)への格下(かくさ)げは、社内(しゃない)で大(おお)きな話題(わだい)になった。
The demotion from department head to section chief became a big topic within the company.

A noun used with する (格下(かくさ)げする) or in passive (格下(かくさ)げされる). Common in financial news (credit rating downgrades), sports (team relegation), weather reports (storm reclassification), and workplace settings (demotions).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 格下(かくさ)げする: to downgrade
  • 格下(かくさ)げされる: to be downgraded
  • 格付(かくづ)けの格下(かくさ)げ: a rating downgrade
  • 格下(かくさ)げ処分(しょぶん): demotion as a disciplinary measure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 降格(こうかく): demotion (focuses on rank/position, especially in organizations)
  • 格上(かくあ)げ: upgrade, promotion (the opposite)
  • 左遷(させん): being transferred to a lesser post (implies punishment or sidelining)
