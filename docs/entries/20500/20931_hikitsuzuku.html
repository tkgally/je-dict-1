1. to continue; to follow on; to persist without interruption
For something to carry on or follow in succession without a break. Often used in the adverbial form 引(ひ)き続(つづ)き to mean 'continuing' or 'subsequently.'
雨(あめ)が三日間(みっかかん)引(ひ)き続(つづ)いた。
The rain continued for three days.
地震(じしん)の後(あと)、余震(よしん)が引(ひ)き続(つづ)いている。
After the earthquake, aftershocks are continuing.
好天(こうてん)が引(ひ)き続(つづ)いたおかげで、工事(こうじ)は予定(よてい)どおりに進(すす)んだ。
Thanks to the continued good weather, the construction progressed on schedule.

A compound verb combining 引(ひ)く (to pull) and 続(つづ)く (to continue). Emphasizes unbroken continuation or succession. Most frequently encountered in its adverbial form 引(ひ)き続(つづ)き, which functions as an adverb meaning "continuing" or "from here on."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 引(ひ)き続(つづ)き: continuously, from now on (adverb form — extremely common)
  • 好天(こうてん)が引(ひ)き続(つづ)く: good weather continues
  • 問題(もんだい)が引(ひ)き続(つづ)く: problems persist
  • 引(ひ)き続(つづ)きご愛顧(あいこ)ください: we hope for your continued patronage

USAGE:
The adverb 引(ひ)き続(つづ)き is far more common than the verb form. It appears frequently in business contexts, news, and formal writing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 続(つづ)く: to continue — simpler, more general
  • 持続(じぞく)する: to sustain, to last — more formal, often for states or conditions
  • 継続(けいぞく)する: to continue (something) — transitive, for deliberately maintaining an activity
