限度額(げんどがく)

げんどがく
noun
limit amount, credit limit, maximum amount
1. limit amount, credit limit, maximum amount
The maximum monetary amount allowed for a transaction, loan, credit card, tax deduction, or other financial matter. Common in banking, insurance, and tax contexts.
カードの限度額(げんどがく)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the credit limit on my card.
控除(こうじょ)の限度額(げんどがく)は年間(ねんかん)200万円(まんえん)です。
The maximum deduction amount is two million yen per year.
融資(ゆうし)の限度額(げんどがく)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げてもらえるか、銀行(ぎんこう)に相談(そうだん)した。
I consulted the bank about whether the loan limit could be raised.

A compound of 限度(げんど) (limit) + 額(がく) (amount). Very common in financial and administrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 限度額(げんどがく)を超(こ)える: to exceed the limit
  • 限度額(げんどがく)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げる: to raise the limit
  • 限度額(げんどがく)を設定(せってい)する: to set a limit
  • 控除(こうじょ)限度額(げんどがく): deduction limit
  • 融資(ゆうし)限度額(げんどがく): loan limit
  • 利用(りよう)限度額(げんどがく): usage limit (credit cards)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上限額(じょうげんがく): upper limit amount — synonymous, slightly more general
  • 限度(げんど): limit — the base word without the monetary nuance; can refer to any kind of limit
  • 枠(わく): frame, quota — colloquial for credit limit or allocation (e.g., カードの枠(わく))
