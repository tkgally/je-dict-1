1. to attract attention; to be in the spotlight; to draw notice
To become the focus of public attention or interest. Literally means to bathe in attention. Used for people, events, products, or topics that are drawing widespread notice.
世界中(せかいじゅう)から注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びている。
It is attracting attention from around the world.
彼女(かのじょ)のスピーチは大(おお)きな注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びた。
Her speech attracted a great deal of attention.
この技術(ぎじゅつ)は環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)の解決策(かいけつさく)として注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びている。
This technology is drawing attention as a solution to environmental problems.

Composed of 注目(ちゅうもく) (attention, notice) + を + 浴(あ)びる (to bathe in, to be showered with). The verb 浴(あ)びる conveys being drenched or showered in something, creating a vivid image of being surrounded by attention.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)きな注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる (to attract great attention)
  • 世界的(せかいてき)に注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる (to attract worldwide attention)
  • 再(ふたた)び注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる (to attract attention again)
  • メディアの注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる (to attract media attention)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)める — to gather attention; very similar, slightly less vivid
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)される — to be noted; more neutral and formal
  • 脚光(きゃっこう)を浴(あ)びる — to be in the limelight; more dramatic, used for performers and public figures

USAGE:
注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる is extremely common in news and media. It is more vivid and expressive than the neutral 注目(ちゅうもく)される, suggesting a flood of attention rather than merely being noticed.
