1. to rustle about; to rummage; to fumble around
To make rustling sounds while searching through things or moving around restlessly. An onomatopoeic word describing the sound and action of rummaging.
カバンの中(なか)をごそごそした。
I rummaged around in my bag.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)でごそごそ音(おと)がする。
I can hear rustling sounds from the next room.
彼(かれ)は押(お)し入(い)れの奥(おく)をごそごそと探(さが)して、古(ふる)いアルバムを引(ひ)っ張(ぱ)り出(だ)した。
He rummaged around in the back of the closet and pulled out an old album.

An onomatopoeic (giongo) word that imitates the sound of rustling or shuffling. Can be used as ごそごそする (verb), ごそごそと (adverb with と), or just ごそごそ (adverb without と).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ごそごそ探(さが)す: to rummage and search
  • ごそごそ音(おと)がする: to hear rustling sounds
  • ごそごそと動(うご)く: to move about restlessly
  • ポケットをごそごそする: to fumble in one's pocket

SIMILAR ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • がさがさ: rustling — louder, rougher rustling sound
  • もそもそ: mumbling/shuffling — slower, more muffled movement
  • ごろごろ: rolling/rumbling — heavier movement or thunder
