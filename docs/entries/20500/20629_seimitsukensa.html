1. detailed examination; thorough checkup; comprehensive testing
A thorough medical examination or inspection that goes beyond routine screening, typically ordered when an abnormality is suspected.
精密検査(せいみつけんさ)を受(う)ける。
To undergo a detailed examination.
健康診断(けんこうしんだん)で異常(いじょう)が見(み)つかり、精密検査(せいみつけんさ)を勧(すす)められた。
An abnormality was found during my health checkup, and I was advised to get a detailed examination.
精密検査(せいみつけんさ)の結果(けっか)、特(とく)に問題(もんだい)はないとのことで安心(あんしん)した。
I was relieved when the detailed examination results showed no particular problems.

Composed of 精密(せいみつ) (precise, detailed) + 検査(けんさ) (examination, inspection). Refers to a more thorough level of testing than a routine screening or checkup.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精密検査(せいみつけんさ)を受(う)ける (to undergo a detailed examination)
  • 精密検査(せいみつけんさ)の結果(けっか) (results of the detailed examination)
  • 精密検査(せいみつけんさ)を勧(すす)められる (to be advised to get a detailed exam)
  • 精密検査(せいみつけんさ)が必要(ひつよう) (a detailed examination is needed)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 健康診断(けんこうしんだん) — health checkup; the routine annual screening that may lead to 精密検査(せいみつけんさ)
  • 検査(けんさ) — examination, inspection; the general term
  • 人間(にんげん)ドック — comprehensive health screening; a voluntary, thorough checkup (from "human dock")

USAGE:
In Japan's healthcare system, annual 健康診断(けんこうしんだん) (health checkups) are standard for employees and residents. If something abnormal is found, the next step is a 精密検査(せいみつけんさ) — a follow-up with specialized testing such as MRI, CT scans, or endoscopy.
