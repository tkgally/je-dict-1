弾劾(だんがい)

だんがい
noun
impeachment; denunciation; accusation
1. impeachment; denunciation; formal accusation
The formal process of charging a public official with misconduct, or more broadly, the act of publicly denouncing someone's wrongdoing. Used primarily in legal and political contexts.
大統領(だいとうりょう)の弾劾(だんがい)裁判(さいばん)が始(はじ)まった。
The impeachment trial of the president has begun.
裁判官(さいばんかん)を弾劾(だんがい)する手続(てつづ)きが進(すす)んでいる。
Proceedings to impeach the judge are underway.
野党(やとう)は大臣(だいじん)の不正(ふせい)を弾劾(だんがい)する決議案(けつぎあん)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
The opposition party submitted a resolution to impeach the minister for corruption.

A formal legal and political term for impeachment or denunciation. Composed of 弾(だん) (impeach/accuse) + 劾(がい) (censure). Often used as a する verb: 弾劾(だんがい)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弾劾(だんがい)する: to impeach; to denounce
  • 弾劾(だんがい)裁判(さいばん): impeachment trial
  • 弾劾(だんがい)裁判所(さいばんしょ): impeachment court
  • 弾劾(だんがい)決議(けつぎ): impeachment resolution
  • 弾劾(だんがい)訴追(そつい): impeachment prosecution

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 告発(こくはつ): accusation, indictment — formally reporting a crime to authorities
  • 糾弾(きゅうだん): denunciation, censure — publicly condemning wrongdoing
  • 罷免(ひめん): dismissal from office — removing someone from their position

NOTE:
In Japan, 弾劾(だんがい)裁判所(さいばんしょ) (the Court of Impeachment) exists under the constitution to try judges accused of misconduct. The word gained broader public familiarity through international news coverage of presidential impeachment proceedings in other countries.
