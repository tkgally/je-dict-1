水浸(みずびた)し

みずびたし
noun
flooded; waterlogged; soaked
1. flooded; waterlogged; soaked; drenched
The state of being thoroughly soaked or submerged in water. Used for both large-scale flooding and smaller situations where something is thoroughly wet.
床(ゆか)が水浸(みずびた)しになった。
The floor got flooded.
大雨(おおあめ)で道路(どうろ)が水浸(みずびた)しだ。
The road is flooded from the heavy rain.
洗濯機(せんたくき)が壊(こわ)れて水(みず)が漏(も)れ、台所(だいどころ)が水浸(みずびた)しになってしまった。
The washing machine broke and water leaked out, leaving the kitchen flooded.

Composed of 水(みず) (water) + 浸(ひた)し (soaking). Describes a state where everything is thoroughly wet or underwater.

USAGE:
Typically used with になる (to become flooded) or as a predicate with だ. Common in news reports about flooding and in everyday situations like water spills or leaks.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水浸(みずびた)しになる: to become flooded/waterlogged
  • 水浸(みずびた)しの部屋(へや): a flooded room
  • 一面(いちめん)水浸(みずびた)し: flooded all over
  • 水浸(みずびた)しの被害(ひがい): flood damage

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 浸水(しんすい): flooding, inundation — a more formal, technical term used in disaster reports
  • 冠水(かんすい): submersion — specifically about roads or fields being submerged
  • びしょ濡(ぬ)れ: soaking wet — used for people or clothes, not for rooms or areas

NOTE:
The ～浸(びた)し pattern also appears in other words like 血(ち)まみれ and 汗(あせ)まみれ, though those use まみれ rather than 浸(びた)し.
