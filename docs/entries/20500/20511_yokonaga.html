横長(よこなが)

よこなが
noun
oblong; landscape-oriented; wider than tall
1. oblong; landscape-oriented; horizontally long; wider than tall
Describes a shape or object that is wider than it is tall — the horizontal dimension is longer than the vertical. Used for objects, screens, paper orientation, and architectural features.
横長(よこなが)の封筒(ふうとう)を使(つか)う。
To use a landscape-oriented envelope.
横長(よこなが)の画面(がめん)で動画(どうが)を見(み)る。
To watch videos on a wide screen.
リビングに横長(よこなが)の大(おお)きなソファを置(お)いたら、部屋(へや)がずいぶん狭(せま)くなった。
When I put a long, wide sofa in the living room, the room became quite cramped.

Describes something wider than it is tall. Composed of 横(よこ) (horizontal/side) + 長(なが) (long). Can be used as a noun or as a no-adjective modifier (横長(よこなが)の).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 横長(よこなが)の封筒(ふうとう): landscape envelope
  • 横長(よこなが)の画面(がめん): widescreen
  • 横長(よこなが)の建物(たてもの): horizontally elongated building
  • 横長(よこなが)の{テーブル}: oblong table

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 縦長(たてなが): portrait-oriented; taller than wide — the opposite of 横長(よこなが)
  • 細長(ほそなが)い: long and thin — emphasizes thinness rather than horizontal orientation

USAGE:
In digital contexts, 横長(よこなが) corresponds to "landscape orientation" (as opposed to 縦長(たてなが) for "portrait orientation"). In everyday use, it describes any object, shape, or space that extends more horizontally than vertically.
