離着陸(りちゃくりく)

りちゃくりく
noun
takeoff and landing
1. takeoff and landing
The combined process of an aircraft departing from and arriving at the ground. A suru-verb that can also describe the act of taking off and landing.
離着陸(りちゃくりく)の時間(じかん)。
Takeoff and landing times.
強風(きょうふう)のため離着陸(りちゃくりく)が遅(おく)れている。
Takeoffs and landings are delayed due to strong winds.
この空港(くうこう)では一日(いちにち)に約(やく)三百回(さんびゃっかい)の離着陸(りちゃくりく)が行(おこな)われている。
About 300 takeoffs and landings take place at this airport every day.

A compound abbreviation combining 離陸(りりく) (takeoff) and 着陸(ちゃくりく) (landing) into a single word. The 離(り) from 離陸(りりく) is placed before 着陸(ちゃくりく).

USAGE:
Common in aviation, airport announcements, news reports, and discussions about air travel. Can function as a suru-verb: 離着陸(りちゃくりく)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 離着陸(りちゃくりく)回数(かいすう) — number of takeoffs and landings
  • 離着陸(りちゃくりく)が遅(おく)れる — takeoffs and landings are delayed
  • 離着陸(りちゃくりく)禁止(きんし) — takeoff and landing prohibited
  • 垂直(すいちょく)離着陸(りちゃくりく) — vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 離陸(りりく) — takeoff (only)
  • 着陸(ちゃくりく) — landing (only)
  • 発着(はっちゃく) — departure and arrival; broader term used for trains, ships, and planes
