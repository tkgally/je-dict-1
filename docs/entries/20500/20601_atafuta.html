あたふた

あたふた
adverb
in a fluster; hurriedly; frantically
1. in a fluster; hurriedly; in a panic
An onomatopoeic adverb describing a state of flustered, panicky movement, rushing about without composure.
あたふたと準備(じゅんび)する。
To prepare in a fluster.
電車(でんしゃ)の時間(じかん)に遅(おく)れそうで、あたふた走(はし)った。
I was about to miss the train, so I ran about frantically.
急(きゅう)な来客(らいきゃく)に、母(はは)はあたふたと部屋(へや)を片付(かたづ)け始(はじ)めた。
Caught off guard by unexpected guests, my mother frantically started tidying up the room.

An onomatopoeic adverb (オノマトペ) conveying the image of someone rushing around in a flustered, disorganized manner. Often used with the particle と.

USAGE:

  • あたふたする (to be in a fluster — used as a suru verb)
  • あたふたと (in a fluster — used as an adverb modifying a verb)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • あたふたと逃(に)げる (flee in a panic)
  • あたふたと支度(したく)する (get ready in a rush)
  • あたふたと走(はし)り回(まわ)る (run around frantically)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おろおろ — being at a loss, confused and helpless (less action-oriented, more standing in bewilderment)
  • ばたばた — rushing about busily (can be neutral busy-ness, not necessarily panicked)
  • そわそわ — restlessly, fidgeting (anxiety-based rather than action-based)
  • 大慌(おおあわ)て — great panic (noun describing the same state)
