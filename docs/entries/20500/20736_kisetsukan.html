季節感(きせつかん)

きせつかん
noun
sense of the season; seasonal feeling
1. sense of the season; seasonal feeling; awareness of the season
The feeling or awareness evoked by seasonal characteristics — sights, foods, weather, and customs that mark a particular time of year.
季節感(きせつかん)のある料理(りょうり)を作(つく)る。
To make food that reflects the season.
都会(とかい)では季節感(きせつかん)を感(かん)じにくい。
In the city, it's hard to feel a sense of the seasons.
旬(しゅん)の食材(しょくざい)を使(つか)うことで、食卓(しょくたく)に季節感(きせつかん)を取(と)り入(い)れている。
By using seasonal ingredients, I bring a sense of the season to the dinner table.

Composed of 季節(きせつ) (season) + 感(かん) (sense/feeling). Reflects the deeply valued Japanese cultural concept of appreciating seasonal changes in food, clothing, decoration, and daily life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 季節感(きせつかん)がある: to have a seasonal feel
  • 季節感(きせつかん)を感(かん)じる: to feel the season
  • 季節感(きせつかん)を大切(たいせつ)にする: to value seasonality
  • 季節感(きせつかん)を取(と)り入(い)れる: to incorporate seasonal elements
  • 季節感(きせつかん)のない: lacking seasonal character

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese culture places great importance on seasonal awareness. This concept appears in cooking (旬(しゅん)の食材(しょくざい), seasonal ingredients), fashion (wearing appropriate colors and fabrics), interior decoration (季節(きせつ)の花(はな)), greetings (時候(じこう)の挨拶(あいさつ)), and traditional arts like haiku (季語(きご), seasonal words).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 風情(ふぜい): elegance, atmosphere — broader aesthetic appreciation, not limited to seasons
  • 情緒(じょうちょ): sentiment, mood — emotional quality of an experience or place
