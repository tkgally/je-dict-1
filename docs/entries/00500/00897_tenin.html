店員(てんいん)

てんいん
noun
store clerk, shop staff
1. store clerk, shop staff
A person who works in a store, helping customers and handling sales. Can refer to any retail employee.
店員(てんいん)に聞(き)いてください。
Please ask the store clerk.
店員(てんいん)さんがとても優(やさ)しかったです。
The store clerk was very kind.
さん added for politeness
店員(てんいん)として働(はたら)いています。
I work as a store clerk.
あの店(みせ)の店員(てんいん)は日本語(にほんご)と英語(えいご)が話(はな)せます。
The clerks at that store can speak Japanese and English.
店員(てんいん)さんに教(おし)えてもらったので、欲(ほ)しいものがすぐに見(み)つかりました。
The store clerk helped me, so I quickly found what I wanted.

店員(てんいん) refers to retail staff who serve customers directly. The word combines 店(みせ) (store) and 員(いん) (member/personnel).

ADDRESSING:
When speaking to or about a clerk politely, add さん: 店員(てんいん)さん. This is standard in customer interactions.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 店員(てんいん)に聞(き)く: to ask a store clerk
  • 店員(てんいん)を呼(よ)ぶ: to call a clerk over
  • 店員(てんいん)として働(はたら)く: to work as a store clerk

RELATED OCCUPATIONS:

  • 店長(てんちょう): store manager
  • 販売員(はんばいいん): sales staff
  • 従業員(じゅうぎょういん): employee (general)
  • レジ係(がかり): cashier

NOTE:
店員(てんいん) typically refers to retail workers. For restaurant staff, 店員(てんいん) works but ウェイター/ウェイトレス or スタッフ are also common.
