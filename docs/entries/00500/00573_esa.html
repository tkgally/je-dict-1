餌(えさ)

えさ
noun
feed, bait, food (for animals)

Definitions

1. feed, food (for animals)
Food given to animals.
2. bait
Food used to attract and catch animals or fish.
3. prey
An animal hunted by another for food.

Examples

犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる。
To feed the dog.
魚(さかな)釣(つ)りの餌(えさ)を買(か)った。
I bought bait for fishing.
鳥(とり)が餌(えさ)を探(さが)している。
The bird is looking for food.

Notes

餌(えさ) refers to food for animals or bait.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 餌(えさ)をやる/あげる (to feed, to give food)
  • 餌(えさ)を食(た)べる (to eat food - animals)
  • 餌(えさ)にする (to use as bait)
  • 餌食(えじき)になる (to fall prey to)

WRITING:

  • Can be written as 餌(えさ) or えさ
  • Kanji form is common
