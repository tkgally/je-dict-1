椅子(いす)

いす
noun
chair
1. chair
A piece of furniture for sitting, typically with a back and four legs. Can refer to any type of chair.
椅子(いす)に座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit on the chair.
椅子(いす)が足(た)りません。
There aren't enough chairs.
この椅子(いす)は高(たか)いですね。
This chair is tall, isn't it?

Usually written in hiragana (いす) or mixed (椅子(いす)). Counter: 脚(きゃく). Uses 座(すわ)る (to sit) and 立(た)つ (to stand). Types include: ソファー (sofa), ベンチ (bench).
