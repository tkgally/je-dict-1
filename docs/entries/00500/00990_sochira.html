そちら

そちら
pronoun
that way, you (polite)
1. that way, there (polite)
A polite demonstrative for direction or location near the listener.
そちらに行(い)く。
To go there (where you are).
そちらは何時(なんじ)ですか。
What time is it there?
そちらの物(もの)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me that thing (near you).
そちらへは電車(でんしゃ)で行(い)けますか。
Can I get there by train?
そちらの天気(てんき)はどうですか。こちらは今日(きょう)雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っています。
How is the weather there? It's raining here today.
2. you, your side (polite)
A polite way to refer to the listener or their group/company.
そちらはどなたですか。
Who is this? (on the phone, polite)
そちらはどちら様(さま)ですか。
Who are you? (very polite)
そちらの時間(じかん)はいかがですか。
How is your time? (polite)
そちらの会社(かいしゃ)ではどのようにされていますか。
How do you do it at your company?
そちらの意見(いけん)もお聞(き)きしたいと思(おも)います。ぜひ教(おし)えてください。
I would like to hear your opinion as well. Please tell me.

そちら is the polite equivalent of そこ and can also mean 'you' or 'your side' in formal contexts. In business, そちら is often used to refer to the other party's company or position. When asking about someone's identity or affiliation politely, そちらは is commonly used.
