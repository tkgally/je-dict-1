十一月(じゅういちがつ)

じゅういちがつ
noun
November
1. November
The eleventh month of the year. Late autumn with peak foliage in many areas and the Shichi-Go-San festival for children.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)は寒(さむ)いです。
November is cold.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)は秋(あき)の終(お)わりです。
November is the end of autumn.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)は紅葉(もみじ)がきれいです。
The autumn leaves are beautiful in November.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)になると、朝(あさ)はとても寒(さむ)くなります。
When November comes, mornings get very cold.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)の十五日(じゅうごにち)は七五三(しちごさん)で、家族(かぞく)と神社(じんじゃ)に行(い)きます。
November 15th is Shichi-Go-San, and families go to shrines.

十一月(じゅういちがつ) is November, the eleventh month of the year.

FORMATION:
十一(じゅういち) (eleven) + 月(がつ) (month)

SEASON:
Late autumn in Japan. 紅葉(もみじ) (autumn foliage) reaches its peak in many areas.

EVENTS:

  • 七五三(しちごさん) (November 15): festival celebrating children aged 3, 5, and 7. Families visit shrines.
  • 勤労感謝(きんろうかんしゃ)の日(ひ) (November 23): Labor Thanksgiving Day (national holiday)

RELATED:

  • 十月(じゅうがつ): October
  • 十二月(じゅうにがつ): December
