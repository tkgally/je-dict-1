女(おんな)

おんな
noun
woman, female

Definitions

1. woman, female
An adult female human. Also used as a prefix to indicate female gender of various things.

Examples

あの女(おんな)の人(ひと)は誰(だれ)ですか？
Who is that woman?
女の人 is more polite than 女 alone
女(おんな)の子(こ)が三人(さんにん)います。
There are three girls.
女の子 = girl
トイレは男(おとこ)と女(おんな)に分(わ)かれています。
The toilets are divided into male and female.

Notes

When referring to people politely, use 女(おんな)の人(ひと) (woman) or 女性(じょせい) (female, more formal). 女(おんな)の子(こ) means 'girl'. The kanji 女(おんな) is also read as じょ in compounds like 女性(じょせい). Opposite: 男(おとこ) (man).
