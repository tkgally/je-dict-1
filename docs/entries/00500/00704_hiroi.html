広(ひろ)い

ひろい
adjective (i-adjective)
wide, spacious
1. wide, broad, spacious
Describes something that has a large extent or area. The opposite of 狭(せま)い (narrow). Used for rooms, roads, areas, etc.
この部屋(へや)は広(ひろ)いですね。
This room is spacious, isn't it?
広(ひろ)い道(みち)を歩(ある)きましょう。
Let's walk on the wide road.
日本(にほん)は広(ひろ)いですか。
Is Japan large?

The opposite is 狭(せま)い (narrow, cramped). Can be used both for physical space (広(ひろ)い部屋(へや), spacious room) and in more abstract senses (広(ひろ)い心(こころ), broad-minded).
