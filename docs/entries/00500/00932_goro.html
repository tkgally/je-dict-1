頃(ごろ)

ごろ
suffix
around (time)
1. around, about (time)
A suffix attached to time expressions to indicate an approximate time.
三時(さんじ)頃(ごろ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet around three o'clock.
何時(なんじ)頃(ごろ)着(つ)きますか。
Around what time will you arrive?
昼(ひる)頃(ごろ)電話(でんわ)します。
I'll call around noon.

USAGE:
Attaches directly to time expressions without particles.

EXAMPLES:

  • 七時(しちじ)頃(ごろ): around 7 o'clock
  • 昼(ひる)頃(ごろ): around noon
  • 来週(らいしゅう)頃(ごろ): around next week
  • 子供(こども)の頃(ころ): when I was a child

COMPARISON:

  • 頃(ごろ): approximate time (suffix)
  • ぐらい/くらい: approximate amount/degree
  • 頃(ごろ) is more natural for clock times

NOTE:
Reading is ごろ as suffix, but ころ in 頃(ころ)に or standalone.

RELATED:

  • いつ頃(ごろ): around when?
  • その頃(ころ): around that time
  • この頃(ごろ): these days
