趣味(しゅみ)

しゅみ
noun
hobby
1. hobby, interest
An activity done regularly for enjoyment during leisure time. Something one likes to do as a pastime.
趣味(しゅみ)は何(なん)ですか。
What is your hobby?
Very common self-introduction question
私(わたし)の趣味(しゅみ)は読書(どくしょ)です。
My hobby is reading.
趣味(しゅみ)で写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)っています。
I take photos as a hobby.

A very common topic in Japanese self-introductions (自己紹介(じこしょうかい)). Popular hobbies include 読書(どくしょ) (reading), 映画鑑賞(えいがかんしょう) (watching movies), 旅行(りょこう) (travel), and 料理(りょうり) (cooking).
