1. to swim
To move through water using one's limbs. Used for recreational swimming, exercise, or competitive swimming.
夏(なつ)は毎日(まいにち)プールで泳(およ)ぎます。
I swim in the pool every day in summer.
海(うみ)で泳(およ)ぐのは楽(たの)しいです。
Swimming in the ocean is fun.
Nominalized with の
子供(こども)のとき、泳(およ)ぎ方(かた)を習(なら)いました。
I learned how to swim when I was a child.
泳(およ)ぎ方(かた) = way of swimming
川(かわ)で泳(およ)ぐのは危(あぶ)ないので、プールか海(うみ)で泳(およ)いでください。
Swimming in the river is dangerous, so please swim in the pool or the ocean.
彼(かれ)は50メートルを30秒(びょう)で泳(およ)げるので、水泳(すいえい)チームに入(はい)りました。
He can swim 50 meters in 30 seconds, so he joined the swimming team.

泳(およ)ぐ means to move through water using one's body. The location is marked with で: プールで泳(およ)ぐ (swim in a pool).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No transitive pair

ASPECT (ている):
泳(およ)いでいる means "is swimming" (ongoing action), not a resulting state.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • プールで泳(およ)ぐ: swim in the pool
  • 海(うみ)で泳(およ)ぐ: swim in the ocean
  • 泳(およ)ぎに行(い)く: go swimming (purpose)
  • 泳(およ)ぎ方(かた): way of swimming, swimming technique
  • 平泳(ひらおよ)ぎ: breaststroke
  • 背泳(せおよ)ぎ: backstroke

RELATED:

  • 水泳(すいえい): swimming (as a sport or competitive activity)
  • 泳(およ)ぎ: a swim; swimming (noun form)
