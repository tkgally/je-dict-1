近(ちか)づける

ちかづける
verb (ichidan)
to bring near, to let approach

Definitions

1. to bring near, to move closer
To make something come closer.

Examples

顔(かお)を近(ちか)づける。
To bring one's face closer.
火(ひ)を近(ちか)づけないで。
Don't bring fire near.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 近(ちか)づける (transitive): to bring near
  • 近(ちか)づく (intransitive): to approach
