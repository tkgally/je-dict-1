緑(みどり)

みどり
noun
green
1. green, greenery
The color green. Also refers to greenery, foliage, or verdure in nature.
緑(みどり)の木(き)がたくさんあります。
There are many green trees.
Using の to modify a noun
公園(こうえん)は緑(みどり)がいっぱいです。
The park is full of greenery.
Using the 'greenery' meaning
信号(しんごう)が緑(みどり)になったら渡(わた)ってください。
Please cross when the light turns green.
Note: traffic lights are called 青 (blue) in Japanese, but 緑 is also used
緑(みどり)のペンを使(つか)ってください。
Please use a green pen.
窓(まど)から見(み)える緑(みどり)がとても気持(きも)ちいいです。
The greenery visible from the window is very pleasant.

緑(みどり) is a noun, so to modify another noun, you use 緑(みどり)の (midori no). Interestingly, in traditional Japanese, the word 青(あお) (blue) covered both blue and green colors. Even today, green traffic lights and green vegetables are often called 青(あお). The distinct word 緑(みどり) for green became more common with Western influence.
