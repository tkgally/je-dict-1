幾(いく)ら

いくら
pronoun
how much (price)
1. how much (price)
Used to ask about the price or cost of something.
これは幾(いく)らですか。
How much is this?
Very common shopping phrase
全部(ぜんぶ)で幾(いく)らになりますか。
How much does it come to in total?
この本(ほん)は幾(いく)らでしたか。
How much was this book?
電車(でんしゃ)の切符(きっぷ)は幾(いく)らですか。
How much is the train ticket?
一(ひと)つ幾(いく)らですか。三(みっ)つください。
How much is each one? I'll take three, please.
この時計(とけい)は幾(いく)らしましたか。高(たか)そうですね。
How much did this watch cost? It looks expensive.
2. no matter how much
In the pattern いくら〜ても, means 'no matter how much.'
幾(いく)ら食(た)べても太(ふと)らない。
No matter how much I eat, I don't gain weight.
いくら〜ても pattern
幾(いく)ら待(ま)っても来(き)ませんでした。
No matter how long I waited, they didn't come.
幾(いく)ら話(はな)しても分(わ)かりません。
No matter how much I talk, they don't understand.
幾(いく)ら考(かんが)えても、いい考(かんが)えが思(おも)いつきません。
No matter how much I think, I can't come up with a good idea.
幾(いく)ら呼(よ)んでも、来(き)てくれませんでした。
No matter how much I called, they didn't come.

幾(いく)ら is one of the most essential question words for daily life in Japan, especially when shopping. The phrase これは幾(いく)らですか is one of the first phrases learners should master. The word is often written in hiragana. In the いくら〜ても pattern, it expresses 'no matter how much.'
