当(あ)たる

あたる
verb (godan)
to hit, to strike; to be correct

Definitions

1. to hit, to strike
To come into contact with something; to be hit by.
2. to be correct, to win
To be right; to win (a lottery, etc.).

Examples

ボールが頭(あたま)に当(あ)たった。
The ball hit my head.
宝(たから)くじが当(あ)たった！
I won the lottery!
予想(よそう)が当(あ)たった。
My prediction was correct.

Notes

当(あ)たる has multiple meanings.

KEY USES:
1. TO HIT: 〜に当(あ)たる (to hit against ~)
2. TO WIN: くじに当(あ)たる (to win lottery)
3. TO BE CORRECT: 予想(よそう)が当(あ)たる
4. TO APPLY TO: 〜に当(あ)たる (to correspond to)

RELATED:

  • 当(あ)てる (v.t.): to hit (something)
  • 当(あ)たり: hit, success
  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ): natural, obvious
