ヶ月(げつ)

かげつ
counter
months (duration)
1. months (duration)
A counter for counting the duration of time in months. Different from 月(がつ) which is used for naming specific months.
日本語(にほんご)を三(さん)ヶ月(げつ)勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I have been studying Japanese for three months.
六(ろっ)ヶ月(げつ)の赤(あか)ちゃん
A six-month-old baby
Note: ろっかげつ with small っ
あと何(なん)ヶ月(げつ)かかりますか。
How many more months will it take?
一(いっ)ヶ月(げつ)で五(ご)キロやせました。
I lost five kilos in one month.
Note: いっかげつ with small っ
十(じゅう)ヶ月(げつ)待(ま)っても、手紙(てがみ)が来(こ)ませんでした。
Even after waiting ten months, no letter came.

ヶ月(げつ) counts the duration in months. It's written with the small ヶ character. Common forms include 一(いっ)ヶ月(げつ) (one month), 二(に)ヶ月(げつ) (two months), 六(ろっ)ヶ月(げつ) (six months - note the っ), 十(じゅっ)ヶ月(げつ) (ten months). Compare with 月(がつ) (for naming months: 一(いち)月(がつ) = January).
