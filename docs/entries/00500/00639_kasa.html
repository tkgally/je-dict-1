傘(かさ)

かさ
noun
umbrella
1. umbrella
A device used to protect from rain or sun. In Japanese, the same word covers both rain umbrellas and parasols.
傘(かさ)を持(も)って行(い)きましょう。
Let's take an umbrella.
傘(かさ)を忘(わす)れました。
I forgot my umbrella.
雨(あめ)だから傘(かさ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
It's raining, so I need an umbrella.

傘 can refer to both rain umbrellas (雨傘, amagasa) and parasols (日傘, higasa). Umbrellas are commonly left at umbrella stands (傘立て, kasatate) at entrances in Japan. Transparent vinyl umbrellas are very common.
