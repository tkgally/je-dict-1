覚(おぼ)える

おぼえる
verb (ichidan)
to remember, to learn
1. to remember, to memorize
To commit something to memory, to memorize. The opposite of 忘(わす)れる (to forget).
2. to learn, to pick up
To acquire a skill or knowledge, often through practice or experience.
漢字(かんじ)を覚(おぼ)えています。
I'm memorizing kanji.
名前(なまえ)を覚(おぼ)えていますか。
Do you remember the name?
ピアノを覚(おぼ)えたい。
I want to learn piano.
Using the 'learn' meaning

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを覚(おぼ)える (memorize/learn X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 覚(おぼ)えている = 'remember' (state of having memorized). This is a key verb where ている means the result, not ongoing action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 漢字(かんじ)を覚(おぼ)える (memorize kanji)
  • 名前(なまえ)を覚(おぼ)える (remember a name)
  • 顔(かお)を覚(おぼ)える (remember a face)
  • 覚(おぼ)えている (remember - state)

Opposite: 忘(わす)れる (to forget). Different from 習(なら)う which emphasizes learning from a teacher.

Related Words

Antonym: 忘(わす)れる (to forget)
