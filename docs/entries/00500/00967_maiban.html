毎晩(まいばん)

まいばん
noun, adverb
every night
1. every night, every evening
Indicates something that happens regularly every night or evening.
毎晩(まいばん)本(ほん)を読(よ)みます。
I read books every night.
毎晩(まいばん)十一時(じゅういちじ)に寝(ね)ます。
I go to bed at eleven o'clock every night.
毎晩(まいばん)お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)ってから寝(ね)ます。
I take a bath every night before going to bed.
毎晩(まいばん)家族(かぞく)と一緒(いっしょ)に晩(ばん)ごはんを食(た)べて、テレビを見(み)ます。
Every evening, I eat dinner with my family and watch TV.
毎晩(まいばん)寝(ね)る前(まえ)に本(ほん)を読(よ)んだり、音楽(おんがく)を聞(き)いたりして、ゆっくり休(やす)みます。
Every night before bed, I read a book, listen to music, and relax.

毎晩(まいばん) means every night/evening, typically the time after dinner until bedtime.

WORD FORMATION:
毎(まい) (every) + 晩(ばん) (evening/night)

RELATED 毎(まい) WORDS:

  • 毎朝(まいあさ): every morning
  • 毎日(まいにち): every day
  • 毎週(まいしゅう): every week
  • 毎月(まいつき): every month
  • 毎年(まいとし/まいねん): every year

USAGE:

Like other 毎(まい) words, often used adverbially without particles:

  • 毎晩(まいばん)本(ほん)を読(よ)む (I read books every night)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 毎夜(まいよ): every night (more literary)
  • 夜(よる)ごとに: every night, nightly
