昼(ひる)

ひる
noun
noon, daytime
1. noon, midday
Around 12 o'clock in the day.
昼(ひる)ごはんを食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat lunch.
昼ごはん = lunch
昼休(ひるやす)みは何時(なんじ)からですか？
What time does lunch break start?
昼休み = lunch break
昼(ひる)の十二時(じゅうにじ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet at noon.
Sense 1: noon
昼(ひる)に何(なに)を食(た)べましたか。
What did you eat for lunch?
Sense 1: noon/lunchtime
昼(ひる)ごろ電話(でんわ)してください。
Please call around noon.
Sense 1: around noon
2. daytime
The hours when the sun is up, as opposed to night.
昼(ひる)は暑(あつ)いです。
It's hot during the day.
Sense 2
昼(ひる)は明(あか)るいです。
It's bright during the day.
Sense 2: daytime
昼間(ひるま)は仕事(しごと)をしています。
I work during the day.
Sense 2: 昼間(ひるま) = daytime
昼(ひる)でも星(ほし)が見(み)える時(とき)があります。
There are times when you can see stars even during the day.
Sense 2: daytime
夜(よる)より昼(ひる)のほうが好(す)きです。外(そと)で遊(あそ)べますから。
I like daytime more than nighttime. Because I can play outside.
Sense 2: daytime vs nighttime

Used for both noon specifically and daytime generally. Compounds: 昼(ひる)ごはん (lunch), 昼休(ひるやす)み (lunch break), 昼間(ひるま) (daytime).
