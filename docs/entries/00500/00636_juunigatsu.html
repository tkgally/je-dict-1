十二月(じゅうにがつ)

じゅうにがつ
noun
December
1. December
The twelfth and final month of the year. The year-end season with Christmas celebrations and preparation for New Year.
十二月(じゅうにがつ)は忙(いそが)しいです。
December is busy.
十二月(じゅうにがつ)二十五日(にじゅうごにち)はクリスマスです。
December 25th is Christmas.
十二月(じゅうにがつ)の終(お)わりに大掃除(おおそうじ)をします。
We do a big cleaning at the end of December.
十二月(じゅうにがつ)は一年(いちねん)で一番(いちばん)忙(いそが)しい月(つき)です。
December is the busiest month of the year.
十二月(じゅうにがつ)になると、町(まち)はクリスマスの飾(かざ)りでいっぱいになります。
When December comes, the town is full of Christmas decorations.

December is literally 'month twelve.' In Japan, Christmas is celebrated but is not a national holiday. The focus shifts to New Year preparations including 大掃除 (osouji, big cleaning), 年賀状 (nengajou, New Year cards), and おせち料理 (osechi ryouri, New Year food).
