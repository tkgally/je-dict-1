池(いけ)

いけ
noun
pond
1. pond
A small body of still water, smaller than a lake. Often found in gardens, parks, or natural settings.
池(いけ)に魚(さかな)がいる。
There are fish in the pond.
池(いけ)のまわりを歩(ある)きました。
I walked around the pond.
公園(こうえん)の池(いけ)に魚(さかな)がいます。
There are fish in the park pond.
庭(にわ)に小(ちい)さい池(いけ)を作(つく)りました。
I made a small pond in the garden.
子供(こども)たちは池(いけ)の近(ちか)くで遊(あそ)んでいました。でも、水(みず)に入(はい)らないように言(い)いました。
The children were playing near the pond. But I told them not to go in the water.

池(いけ) refers to a pond—a small body of still water, smaller than a lake.

SIZE COMPARISON:

  • 池(いけ): pond (small)
  • 湖(みずうみ): lake (large)
  • 沼(ぬま): marsh, swamp

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 池(いけ)の魚(さかな): fish in a pond
  • 池(いけ)のまわり: around the pond
  • 池(いけ)に落(お)ちる: to fall into the pond

COMPOUNDS:

  • 池袋(いけぶくろ): Ikebukuro (place name)
  • 電池(でんち): battery (electric + pond = cell)
  • 池田(いけだ): Ikeda (surname/place)

CULTURE:
Ponds are central to Japanese gardens (日本庭園(にほんていえん)). Koi ponds (鯉(こい)の池(いけ)) are popular and considered auspicious.
