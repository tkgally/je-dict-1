二(に)

に
number
two

Definitions

1. two
The number two. A fundamental counting number used throughout Japanese. Like other numbers, に combines with various counters and may change pronunciation in some combinations.

Examples

二(に)人(にん)で行(い)きましょう。
Let's go together (the two of us).
コーヒーを二(に)つください。
Two coffees, please.
二(に)月(がつ)は短(みじか)いです。
February is short.
二月 means February

Notes

The reading changes in some contexts: 二(ふた)つ (futatsu, two things), 二(ふた)人(り) (futari, two people). Note that 二(ふた)人(り) is a special reading. In most counter combinations, に is used: 二(に)冊(さつ) (nisatsu, two books), 二(に)台(だい) (nidai, two machines).
