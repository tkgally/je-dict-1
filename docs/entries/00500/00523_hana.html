花(はな)

はな
noun
flower
1. flower, blossom
The reproductive structure of flowering plants, often colorful and fragrant.
庭(にわ)にきれいな花(はな)が咲(さ)いています。
Beautiful flowers are blooming in the garden.
母(はは)に花(はな)をあげました。
I gave flowers to my mother.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)が好(す)きです。
I like cherry blossoms.
この花(はな)の名前(なまえ)は何(なん)ですか？
What is the name of this flower?
春(はる)になると、いろいろな花(はな)が咲(さ)いて、とてもきれいです。
When spring comes, various flowers bloom and it's very beautiful.

花(はな) is a fundamental nature word referring to flowers and blossoms. In Japanese culture, flowers hold deep significance.

READINGS:

  • はな: standalone and many compounds
  • か: in some compounds like 花瓶(かびん) (vase)
  • ばな: after certain words like 生(い)け花(ばな) (flower arrangement)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 花(はな)が咲(さ)く: flowers bloom
  • 花(はな)を生(い)ける: to arrange flowers
  • 花(はな)を贈(おく)る: to give flowers
  • 花見(はなみ): flower viewing (especially cherry blossoms)
  • 花火(はなび): fireworks

CULTURAL NOTE:
花見(はなみ) is a beloved spring tradition of viewing cherry blossoms. 花(はな) alone often implies 桜(さくら) (cherry blossoms) in spring contexts.
