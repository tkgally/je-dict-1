花(はな)

はな
noun
flower
1. flower, blossom
The reproductive structure of flowering plants, often colorful and fragrant.
庭(にわ)にきれいな花(はな)が咲(さ)いています。
Beautiful flowers are blooming in the garden.
母(はは)に花(はな)をあげました。
I gave flowers to my mother.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)が好(す)きです。
I like cherry blossoms.
この花(はな)の名前(なまえ)は何(なん)ですか？
What is the name of this flower?
春(はる)になると、いろいろな花(はな)が咲(さ)いて、とてもきれいです。
When spring comes, various flowers bloom and it's very beautiful.

A fundamental nature word. 花(はな) can also be read as か in compounds: 花瓶(かびん) (vase), 花火(はなび) (fireworks), 生(い)け花(ばな) (flower arrangement). 花見(はなみ) (flower viewing) is a popular spring activity.
