父(ちち)

ちち
noun
father (humble)
1. father (my father)
Humble term used when referring to one's own father when speaking to others outside the family.
父(ちち)は会社員(かいしゃいん)です。
My father is a company employee.
父(ちち)は今(いま)出張中(しゅっちょうちゅう)です。
My father is currently on a business trip.
父(ちち)の誕生日(たんじょうび)は5月(がつ)です。
My father's birthday is in May.
父(ちち)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)で、週末(しゅうまつ)はよく作(つく)ります。
My father is good at cooking and often cooks on weekends.
子供(こども)の時(とき)、父(ちち)に自転車(じてんしゃ)の乗(の)り方(かた)を教(おし)えてもらいました。
When I was a child, my father taught me how to ride a bicycle.

父(ちち) is the humble term for "father," used when referring to one's own father when speaking to people outside the family.

POLITENESS SYSTEM:

  • 父(ちち): my father (humble, to outsiders)
  • お父(とう)さん: father (neutral, direct address or others' fathers)
  • お父(とう)さま: father (very polite/formal)
  • パパ: dad (casual, childish)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 父(ちち)の日(ひ): Father's Day
  • 父(ちち)親(おや): father (formal/written)
  • 父(ちち)方(かた): paternal side (of family)

USAGE NOTE: In casual family settings, children may call their father お父(とう)さん or パパ. 父(ちち) is specifically for humble reference when talking to outsiders.

RELATED: 母(はは) (mother - humble)
