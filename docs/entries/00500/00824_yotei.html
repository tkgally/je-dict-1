予定(よてい)

よてい
noun
plan, schedule
1. plan, schedule, program
Something that is planned or scheduled to happen. Often used for future arrangements.
来週(らいしゅう)の予定(よてい)は何(なん)ですか。
What are your plans for next week?
予定(よてい)が変(か)わりました。
The plans changed.
三時(さんじ)に終(お)わる予定(よてい)です。
It's scheduled to end at 3 o'clock.

予定(よてい) refers to something planned or scheduled for the future.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜予定(よてい)です: It's scheduled to ~
  • 予定(よてい)どおり: as planned/scheduled
  • 予定(よてい)を入(い)れる: to schedule something
  • 予定(よてい)が入(はい)っている: to have plans
  • 予定(よてい)を変更(へんこう)する: to change plans

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 予定(よてい)表(ひょう): schedule, timetable
  • 予定(よてい)日(び): scheduled date
  • 予定(よてい)時刻(じこく): scheduled time
  • 出産(しゅっさん)予定(よてい)日(び): due date (for childbirth)

USAGE NOTE:
予定(よてい) implies something is tentatively planned but not guaranteed. For confirmed, official schedules, 日程(にってい) is often used.
