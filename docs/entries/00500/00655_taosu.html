倒(たお)す

たおす
verb (godan)
to knock down, to defeat

Definitions

1. to knock down, to knock over
To cause something to fall.
2. to defeat, to beat
To win against an opponent.

Examples

敵(てき)を倒(たお)す。
To defeat the enemy.
木(き)を倒(たお)す。
To fell a tree.
花瓶(かびん)を倒(たお)した。
I knocked over the vase.

Notes

倒(たお)す means to knock down or defeat.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 倒(たお)す (transitive): to knock down
  • 倒(たお)れる (intransitive): to fall down

USAGE:

  • Physical: knock over objects
  • Games/competition: defeat opponents
  • Overthrow: governments, powers
