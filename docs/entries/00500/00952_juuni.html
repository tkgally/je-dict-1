十二(じゅうに)

じゅうに
number
twelve
1. twelve
The number twelve. Formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 二(に) (two).
十二(じゅうに)時(じ)に昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べます。
I eat lunch at twelve o'clock.
一(いち)年(ねん)は十二(じゅうに)ヶ月(げつ)あります。
There are twelve months in a year.
十二(じゅうに)月(がつ)は冬(ふゆ)です。
December is winter.
December

Twelve is an important number in Japanese as there are twelve months and the traditional Chinese zodiac has twelve animals. 十二支(じゅうにし) refers to these zodiac animals.
