兄弟(きょうだい)

きょうだい
noun
siblings, brothers
See also: 鏡台(きょうだい) (dressing table)
1. siblings, brothers and sisters
A general term for siblings, which can include both brothers and sisters.
兄弟(きょうだい)は何人(なんにん)いますか。
How many siblings do you have?
Common question about family
私(わたし)には兄弟(きょうだい)が三人(さんにん)います。
I have three siblings.
兄弟(きょうだい)で旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きました。
I went on a trip with my siblings.
兄弟(きょうだい)の中(なか)で、私(わたし)が一番(いちばん)上(うえ)です。
Among my siblings, I'm the oldest.
兄弟(きょうだい)がいないので、一人(ひとり)っ子(こ)です。
I don't have any siblings, so I'm an only child.
兄弟(きょうだい)げんかはよくあることです。
Fights between siblings are common.
2. brothers
More specifically, male siblings or brothers.
彼(かれ)には兄弟(きょうだい)が二人(ふたり)いる。
He has two brothers.
兄弟(きょうだい)そろって野球(やきゅう)をしている。
All the brothers play baseball together.
双子(ふたご)の兄弟(きょうだい)はよく似(に)ています。
The twin brothers look very similar.
彼(かれ)ら二人(ふたり)は本当(ほんとう)の兄弟(きょうだい)のように仲(なか)がいい。
Those two get along like real brothers.
彼(かれ)の兄弟(きょうだい)はみんな男(おとこ)です。
His siblings are all boys.

MEANING AND GENDER:
兄弟(きょうだい) is written with the kanji for 'older brother' and 'younger brother,' but in modern usage it normally means siblings in general, regardless of gender. Sense 2 (male siblings only) is clear from context — for example when the number of brothers is contrasted with the number of sisters. For sisters specifically, use 姉妹(しまい).

ASKING ABOUT SOMEONE'S SIBLINGS:

  • 兄弟(きょうだい)は何人(なんにん)いますか (How many siblings do you have?)
  • ご兄弟(きょうだい)は？ - the honorific prefix ご is normal when asking about another person's family, and is left off when talking about your own.

COUNTING - AN EASY TRAP:

Two similar-looking patterns count differently:

  • 兄弟(きょうだい)が三人(さんにん)います = 'I have three siblings' (three besides yourself, four children in all).
  • 三人(さんにん)兄弟(きょうだい)です = 'we are three siblings' (three children in all, yourself included).

The compound form 三人(さんにん)兄弟(きょうだい) describes the size of the sibling group, so it counts the speaker as well.

COMMON COMPOUND:
兄弟(きょうだい)げんか (sibling quarrel) - note the rendaku: 喧嘩(けんか) becomes げんか in this compound, and it is usually written in kana.

RELATED WORDS:
兄(あに) (older brother), 弟(おとうと) (younger brother), 姉(あね) (older sister), 妹(いもうと) (younger sister), 一人(ひとり)っ子(こ) (only child).
