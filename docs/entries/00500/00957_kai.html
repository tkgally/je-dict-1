回(かい)

かい
counter
times (frequency)
1. times, occurrences
A counter for number of times or frequency of events. Used to count how many times something happens or has happened.
一(いち)日(にち)に三(さん)回(かい)薬(くすり)を飲(の)んでください。
Please take the medicine three times a day.
日本(にほん)には何(なん)回(かい)行(い)きましたか。
How many times have you been to Japan?
もう一(いっ)回(かい)言(い)ってください。
Please say it one more time.
Note: いっかい with small っ
今(いま)週(しゅう)はもう二(に)回(かい)映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I've already seen two movies this week.
一(いっ)回(かい)しか会(あ)ったことがないのに、友達(ともだち)になりました。
We became friends even though we only met once.

回(かい) is used to count occurrences or times. Common expressions include 一(いっ)回(かい) (once - note the small っ), 二(に)回(かい) (twice), 三(さん)回(かい) (three times), 何(なん)回(かい) (how many times). Note that 一回 is read いっかい (with gemination), not いちかい.
