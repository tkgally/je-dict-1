厚(あつ)い

あつい
adjective (i-adjective)
thick

Definitions

1. thick (not thin)
Describes something with considerable thickness or depth. The opposite of 薄(うす)い (thin). Used for books, walls, slices, etc. Different from 暑(あつ)い (hot weather) and 熱(あつ)い (hot to touch).

Examples

この本(ほん)は厚(あつ)いですね。
This book is thick, isn't it?
厚(あつ)い肉(にく)を焼(や)きます。
I'm grilling thick meat.
壁(かべ)が厚(あつ)いので音(おと)が聞(き)こえません。
The walls are thick so I can't hear any sound.

Notes

Be careful not to confuse with the other あつい words: 暑(あつ)い (hot weather), 熱(あつ)い (hot to touch). The kanji 厚(あつ)い specifically means 'thick.' The opposite is 薄(うす)い (thin).
