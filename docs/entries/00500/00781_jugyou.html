授業(じゅぎょう)

じゅぎょう
noun
class, lesson
1. class, lesson, instruction
A scheduled period of teaching or learning at a school or educational institution. Refers to the teaching session itself, not the subject or room.
授業(じゅぎょう)は何時(なんじ)に始(はじ)まりますか。
What time does class start?
今日(きょう)の授業(じゅぎょう)は難(むずか)しかったです。
Today's class was difficult.
授業(じゅぎょう)に遅(おく)れてしまいました。
I ended up being late to class.
午後(ごご)の授業(じゅぎょう)は何時(なんじ)から何時(なんじ)までですか。
What time is the afternoon class from and to?
先生(せんせい)の授業(じゅぎょう)はいつも面白(おもしろ)くて、時間(じかん)が早(はや)く過(す)ぎる。
The teacher's class is always interesting, so time passes quickly.

授業(じゅぎょう) refers to a class or lesson in an educational setting. It emphasizes the teaching session itself rather than the subject matter.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 授業(じゅぎょう)を受(う)ける: to attend/take a class (student's perspective)
  • 授業(じゅぎょう)をする: to teach a class (teacher's perspective)
  • 授業(じゅぎょう)に出(で)る: to attend class
  • 授業(じゅぎょう)を休(やす)む: to miss/skip class

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 授業(じゅぎょう)料(りょう): tuition fee
  • 授業(じゅぎょう)中(ちゅう): during class
  • 課外(かがい)授業(じゅぎょう): extracurricular lesson

RELATED WORDS:

  • 講義(こうぎ): lecture (university level)
  • 教室(きょうしつ): classroom
  • 科目(かもく): subject, course

Related Words

Synonym: 講義(こうぎ) (lecture (university level))
Related: 科目(かもく) (academic subject)
Related: 宿題(しゅくだい) (homework; also an unfinished task carried over)
