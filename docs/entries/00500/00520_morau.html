もらう

もらう
verb (godan)
to receive, to get
1. to receive, to get
To accept something given by someone. The receiver is typically the speaker or someone close to the speaker.
友達(ともだち)からプレゼントをもらいました。
I received a present from my friend.
から marks the giver
誕生日(たんじょうび)に何(なに)をもらいましたか？
What did you get for your birthday?
手伝(てつだ)ってもらえますか？
Could you help me?
てもらう = to have someone do something
先生(せんせい)に本(ほん)をもらいました。
I received a book from the teacher.
に marks the giver (alternative to から)
彼(かれ)から手紙(てがみ)をもらったとき、とても嬉(うれ)しかったです。
When I received a letter from him, I was very happy.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xに/からYをもらう (receive Y from X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. もらっている = 'has received.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 友達(ともだち)からもらう (receive from friend)
  • プレゼントをもらう (receive a present)
  • 〜てもらう (have someone do〜)
  • 〜てもらえますか (could you〜?)

Giving/receiving: もらう (receive), あげる (give), くれる (give to me). Humble: いただく. Auxiliary: 〜てもらう (receive favor of action).
