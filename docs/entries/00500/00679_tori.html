鳥(とり)

とり
noun
bird
1. bird
A warm-blooded vertebrate with feathers and wings. Also used as a general term for poultry.
鳥(とり)が飛(と)んでいます。
A bird is flying.
小(ちい)さい鳥(とり)がいますね。
There's a small bird, isn't there?
木(き)の上(うえ)に鳥(とり)がいます。
There's a bird in the tree.
朝(あさ)、窓(まど)の外(そと)に鳥(とり)が来(き)ていました。
In the morning, a bird had come outside the window.
この公園(こうえん)にはいろいろな鳥(とり)がいて、見(み)ていて楽(たの)しいです。
There are various birds in this park, and it's fun to watch them.
2. chicken, poultry (meat)
In food contexts, often refers to chicken meat.
鳥(とり)を食(た)べますか。
Do you eat chicken?
Refers to chicken meat
今日(きょう)の晩(ばん)ごはんは鳥(とり)です。
Tonight's dinner is chicken.
焼(や)き鳥(とり)を五(ご)本(ほん)ください。
Five grilled chicken skewers, please.
鳥(とり)は魚(さかな)より安(やす)いので、よく買(か)います。
Chicken is cheaper than fish, so I often buy it.
この店(みせ)の鳥(とり)はとても美味(おい)しいので、いつも買(か)いに来(き)ます。
This restaurant's chicken is very delicious, so I always come to buy it.

鳥 is the general word for 'bird' but in food contexts often specifically means chicken (as in 焼き鳥, yakitori—grilled chicken skewers). For the specific animal 'chicken,' 鶏 (niwatori) is used.

Related:

  • 鳥肉 (toriniku, chicken meat)
  • 小鳥 (kotori, small bird).
