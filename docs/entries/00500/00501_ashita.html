明日(あした)

あした
noun
tomorrow

Definitions

1. tomorrow
The day after today. Used as a time expression, typically without a particle.

Examples

明日(あした)、会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet tomorrow.
明日(あした)は休(やす)みです。
Tomorrow is a day off.
明日(あした)の天気(てんき)はどうですか。
How's the weather tomorrow?
明日(あした)の = tomorrow's

Notes

明日(あした) can be read as あした (common/casual), あす (slightly more formal), or みょうにち (formal/written). あした is by far the most common in everyday speech. Like 今日(きょう) and 昨日(きのう), it typically doesn't take the particle に.
