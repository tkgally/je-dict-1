万(まん)

まん
noun
ten thousand, 10000
1. ten thousand, 10000
The number ten thousand. A key unit in Japanese counting, as large numbers are grouped by 万 (ten thousands) rather than thousands.
一万(いちまん)円(えん)です。
It's 10,000 yen.
百万(ひゃくまん)円(えん)の車(くるま)を買(か)いました。
I bought a car for 1,000,000 yen.
100万 = one million
東京(とうきょう)には一千万人(いっせんまんにん)以上(いじょう)が住(す)んでいます。
More than 10 million people live in Tokyo.

Japanese groups large numbers by 万 (10,000): 十万(じゅうまん) (100,000), 百万(ひゃくまん) (1,000,000), 千万(せんまん) (10,000,000). 一万円(いちまんえん)札(さつ) is a 10,000-yen bill (the largest bill). 万一(まんいち) means 'by any chance/if by chance'.
