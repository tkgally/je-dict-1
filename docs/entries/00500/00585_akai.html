1. red
Having the color of blood or a ripe tomato.
赤(あか)い花(はな)がきれいです。
The red flowers are beautiful.
顔(かお)が赤(あか)いですよ。
Your face is red.
赤(あか)い車(くるま)を買(か)いました。
I bought a red car.
りんごが赤(あか)くなりました。食(た)べられます。
The apples have turned red. They're ready to eat.
信号(しんごう)が赤(あか)いです。青(あお)になったら、渡(わた)れます。
The light is red. When it turns green, you can cross.

USAGE:
赤(あか)い works both before a noun (赤(あか)い車(くるま)) and as a predicate (顔(かお)が赤(あか)い). It covers a wider range of shades than English "red," reaching into orange and pink: ripe fruit, a sunset, and a flushed face are all 赤(あか)い.

FORMS:

  • Noun form: 赤(あか) (the color red)
  • Adverbial: 赤(あか)く (赤(あか)くなる = to become red)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顔(かお)が赤(あか)くなる: to blush, to turn red (from embarrassment or anger)
  • 赤(あか)ちゃん: baby (literally "red one" — newborns look reddish)
  • 赤(あか)の他人(たにん): complete stranger
  • 赤字(あかじ): deficit, "in the red"
  • 赤信号(あかしんごう): red light

The last three take the noun 赤(あか), not the adjective. Set compounds of this kind normally use the noun form, so a traffic light is 赤信号(あかしんごう) rather than 赤(あか)い信号(しんごう), though the adjective is fine in a full sentence (信号(しんごう)が赤(あか)い).

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES:

  • 赤(あか)い: red
  • 青(あお)い: blue — but also "green" for traffic lights and fresh leaves
  • 黄色(きいろ)い: yellow
  • 黒(くろ)い: black

CULTURE:
Red symbolizes celebration and good fortune in Japan. 紅白(こうはく) (red and white) is the standard festive pairing, used for everything from ceremonial curtains to the New Year's Eve song contest.
