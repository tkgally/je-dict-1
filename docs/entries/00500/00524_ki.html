木(き)

き
noun
tree, wood
1. tree
A tall plant with a trunk, branches, and leaves. A fundamental nature word.
庭(にわ)に大(おお)きい木(き)があります。
There is a big tree in the garden.
木(き)に登(のぼ)るのは危(あぶ)ないです。
Climbing trees is dangerous.
公園(こうえん)にたくさんの木(き)があります。
There are many trees in the park.
木(き)の下(した)で休(やす)みましょう。
Let's rest under the tree.
秋(あき)になると、木(き)の葉(は)が赤(あか)くなります。
When autumn comes, the leaves on trees turn red.
Sense 1: tree
祖父(そふ)は毎日(まいにち)庭(にわ)の木(き)に水(みず)をやっています。
My grandfather waters the trees in the garden every day.
Sense 1: tree
2. wood, timber
The material obtained from trees, used for building and crafts.
このテーブルは木(き)で作(つく)られています。
This table is made of wood.
Sense 2: wood as material
日本(にほん)の家(いえ)は木(き)で作(つく)られることが多(おお)いです。
Japanese houses are often made of wood.
Sense 2: wood as building material
木(き)の椅子(いす)は座(すわ)り心地(ごこち)がいいです。
Wooden chairs are comfortable to sit on.
Sense 2: wood material
この箱(はこ)は木(き)でできています。
This box is made of wood.
Sense 2: wood material
木(き)のおもちゃ(おもちゃ)は子供(こども)に人気(にんき)があります。
Wooden toys are popular with children.
Sense 2: wood material

木(き) is one of the most basic nature words, meaning both 'tree' (the living plant) and 'wood' (the material).

KANJI READINGS:

  • き: native Japanese reading (kun'yomi)
  • もく: Sino-Japanese reading (e.g., 木曜日(もくようび))
  • ぼく: Sino-Japanese reading (e.g., 大木(たいぼく))

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 木(き)を植(う)える: to plant a tree
  • 木(き)に登(のぼ)る: to climb a tree
  • 木(き)の下(した): under the tree
  • 木(き)で作(つく)る: to make from wood

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 木曜日(もくようび): Thursday
  • 木造(もくぞう): wooden construction
  • 木の葉(このは): tree leaves
  • 並木(なみき): row of trees
  • 木々(きぎ): trees (plural)

IDIOM:
木(き)を見(み)て森(もり)を見(み)ず: 'Can't see the forest for the trees' - focusing on details while missing the big picture.
