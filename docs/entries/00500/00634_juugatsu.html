十月(じゅうがつ)

じゅうがつ
noun
October
1. October
The tenth month of the year. Autumn is in full swing with pleasant weather and autumn foliage.
十月(じゅうがつ)は秋(あき)です。
October is autumn.
十月(じゅうがつ)の天気(てんき)は気持(きも)ちがいいです。
The weather in October is pleasant.
十月(じゅうがつ)に紅葉(こうよう)を見(み)に行(い)きます。
I'll go see the autumn leaves in October.
十月(じゅうがつ)になると、朝(あさ)と夜(よる)は涼(すず)しくなります。
When October comes, mornings and evenings get cool.
十月(じゅうがつ)は旅行(りょこう)にいい季節(きせつ)なので、京都(きょうと)に行(い)こうと思(おも)っています。
October is a good season for travel, so I'm thinking of going to Kyoto.

十月(じゅうがつ) is October, the tenth month of the year.

FORMATION:
十(じゅう) (ten) + 月(がつ) (month)

SEASON:
Mid-autumn in Japan. One of the most pleasant months with comfortable temperatures and 紅葉(こうよう) (autumn foliage).

EVENTS:

  • 運動会(うんどうかい): Sports days are commonly held at schools and companies
  • 体育(たいいく)の日(ひ): Sports Day (national holiday, second Monday)
  • ハロウィン (Halloween): Increasingly popular, especially in cities

RELATED:

  • 九月(くがつ): September
  • 十一月(じゅういちがつ): November
