開(あ)く

あく
verb (godan)
to open (by itself)

Definitions

1. to open, to become open
For something to move from a closed to open position on its own. The intransitive pair of 開(あ)ける.

Examples

ドアが開(あ)きました。
The door opened.
店(みせ)は何(なん)時(じ)に開(あ)きますか。
What time does the store open?
窓(まど)が開(あ)いています。
The window is open.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 開(あ)ける (transitive, to open something). Pattern: Xが開(あ)く.

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 開(あ)いている = 'is open' (not 'is opening'). This is important: 窓(まど)が開(あ)いている means 'the window is open,' not 'the window is opening.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドアが開(あ)く (the door opens)
  • 店(みせ)が開(あ)く (the store opens)
  • 穴(あな)が開(あ)く (a hole forms)
  • 席(せき)が開(あ)く (a seat becomes available)

Opposite: 閉(し)まる (to close by itself).

Related Words

See also: 開(あ)ける
Antonym: 閉(し)まる (to close by itself)
