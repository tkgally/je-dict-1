九月(くがつ)

くがつ
noun
September
1. September
The ninth month of the year. The new school term resumes after summer vacation. Features the autumn equinox.
九月(くがつ)から学校(がっこう)が始(はじ)まります。
School starts in September.
九月(くがつ)は涼(すず)しくなります。
It gets cooler in September.
九月(くがつ)に台風(たいふう)が来(き)ます。
Typhoons come in September.

September uses く (9), not きゅう. The second semester begins in September in Japan. The autumn equinox (秋分の日) falls around September 23rd and is a national holiday.
