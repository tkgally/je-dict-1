1. dangerous, hazardous
Involving or causing risk of harm or injury.
ここは危(あぶ)ないです。
This place is dangerous.
危(あぶ)ない！車(くるま)が来(き)ます！
Watch out! A car is coming!
Used as a warning exclamation
夜(よる)一人(ひとり)で歩(ある)くのは危(あぶ)ない。
Walking alone at night is dangerous.
ナイフは危(あぶ)ないから、気(き)を付(つ)けてください。
Knives are dangerous, so please be careful.
子供(こども)が川(かわ)の近(ちか)くで遊(あそ)んでいて、危(あぶ)ないと思(おも)った。すぐに止(と)めました。
The children were playing near the river, and I thought it was dangerous. I stopped them right away.
2. risky, precarious
Uncertain or unstable, potentially leading to a bad outcome.
その考(かんが)えは危(あぶ)ないです。
That idea is risky.
この計画(けいかく)は危(あぶ)ないかもしれません。
This plan might be risky.
そのやり方(かた)は危(あぶ)ないと思(おも)います。
I think that approach is risky.
テストは危(あぶ)なかったけど、なんとか合格(ごうかく)した。
The test was close, but I somehow passed.
Meaning: was close to failing
今(いま)の会社(かいしゃ)は危(あぶ)ないかもしれません。仕事(しごと)を探(さが)した方(ほう)がいいと思(おも)います。
My current company might be in trouble. I think I should look for a new job.

危(あぶ)ない is an い-adjective meaning dangerous or risky. Often used as a warning exclamation.

USAGE AS EXCLAMATION:
危(あぶ)ない! (Watch out! Danger!) — used to warn someone of immediate danger.

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • 危(あぶ)なく〜するところだった (almost did something bad / had a close call)

Example: 危(あぶ)なく遅刻(ちこく)するところだった (I almost was late)

  • 危(あぶ)ないから〜 (because it's dangerous...)
  • 〜のは危(あぶ)ない (doing ~ is dangerous)

SENSE 2 (Risky/Precarious):
Used for unstable situations: 会社(かいしゃ)が危(あぶ)ない (the company is in trouble), テストが危(あぶ)なかった (the test was a close call).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 危険(きけん): danger/dangerous (more formal, na-adjective/noun)
  • 物騒(ぶっそう): unsafe, disturbing (for areas/situations)
  • ヤバい: risky, dangerous (slang, also means 'awesome')
