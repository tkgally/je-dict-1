嗅(か)ぐ

かぐ
verb (godan)
to smell, to sniff

Definitions

1. to smell, to sniff
To perceive odor through the nose.

Examples

花(はな)の匂(にお)いを嗅(か)ぐ。
To smell the flowers.
犬(いぬ)が地面(じめん)を嗅(か)いでいる。
The dog is sniffing the ground.
くんくん嗅(か)ぐ。
To sniff around.

Notes

嗅(か)ぐ means to smell or sniff.

COMMON USES:

  • 匂(にお)いを嗅(か)ぐ: to smell a scent
  • 香(かお)りを嗅(か)ぐ: to smell a fragrance

NOTE:

  • 嗅(か)ぐ is the action of smelling
  • 匂(にお)う/する is when something has a smell
