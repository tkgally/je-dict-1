味(あじ)

あじ
noun
taste, flavor
1. taste, flavor
The sensation perceived by the tongue when eating or drinking something. Can refer to sweet, salty, sour, bitter, or umami flavors.
この食(た)べ物(もの)は味(あじ)がいいです。
This food has good flavor.
味(あじ)はどうですか。
How does it taste?
Common way to ask about food
もう少(すこ)し塩(しお)の味(あじ)がほしいです。
I want a bit more salty taste.
母(はは)の作(つく)るスープは味(あじ)がとてもいいです。
The soup my mother makes has a very good taste.
このお茶(ちゃ)は少(すこ)し苦(にが)い味(あじ)がしますが、体(からだ)にいいですよ。
This tea has a slightly bitter taste, but it's good for your health.

味(あじ) refers to the sensation of flavor when eating or drinking.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 味(あじ)がする: to taste (of something)
  • 味(あじ)がいい: tastes good
  • 味(あじ)を見(み)る: to taste, to sample
  • 味(あじ)を付(つ)ける: to season, to add flavor

FLAVOR TYPES:

  • 甘(あま)い味(あじ): sweet taste
  • 辛(から)い味(あじ): spicy taste
  • 苦(にが)い味(あじ): bitter taste
  • 酸(す)っぱい味(あじ): sour taste
  • 旨味(うまみ): umami (savory taste)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 味見(あじみ): tasting, sampling
  • 調味料(ちょうみりょう): seasoning
  • 味付(あじつ)け: seasoning, flavoring

Figuratively used to describe character: 人生(じんせい)の味(あじ) (the flavor of life).
