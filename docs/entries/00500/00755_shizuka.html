静(しず)か

しずか
na-adjective
quiet, calm
1. quiet, silent
Describes a place, environment, or person that makes little or no noise. The opposite of うるさい (noisy).
図書館(としょかん)は静(しず)かです。
The library is quiet.
静(しず)かにしてください。
Please be quiet.
Polite request
静(しず)かな町(まち)に住(す)みたい。
I want to live in a quiet town.
Using the na-adjective form before a noun
夜(よる)は静(しず)かです。よく眠(ねむ)れます。
It's quiet at night. I can sleep well.
朝(あさ)早(はや)い時間(じかん)は駅(えき)も静(しず)かです。人(ひと)がいません。
The station is also quiet early in the morning. There's no one around.
2. calm, peaceful
Can also describe a tranquil atmosphere or a person with a calm demeanor.
静(しず)かな町(まち)に住(す)みたい。
I want to live in a quiet town.
Using the na-adjective form before a noun
彼(かれ)は静(しず)かです。
He is quiet.
赤(あか)ちゃんが静(しず)かに眠(ねむ)っています。
The baby is sleeping quietly.
静(しず)かな人(ひと)が好(す)きです。
I like calm/quiet people.
あの人(ひと)は静(しず)かな人(ひと)です。あまり話(はな)しません。
That person is a quiet person. They don't talk much.
祖父(そふ)は静(しず)かな人(ひと)でしたが、家族(かぞく)のことをとても大切(たいせつ)にしていました。
My grandfather was a calm person, but he cared about his family very much.

静(しず)か is a na-adjective meaning quiet, calm, or peaceful.

NA-ADJECTIVE FORMS:

  • 静(しず)かな + noun: 静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ) (a quiet place)
  • 静(しず)かに + verb: 静(しず)かに歩(ある)く (to walk quietly)
  • 静(しず)かです: it's quiet (polite)
  • 静(しず)かだ: it's quiet (plain)

OPPOSITES:

  • うるさい: noisy (for sound/volume)
  • 賑(にぎ)やか: lively, bustling (for atmosphere)
  • 騒(さわ)がしい: noisy, rowdy

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 静(しず)かにしてください: Please be quiet
  • 静(しず)かな夜(よる): a quiet night
  • 静(しず)かな生活(せいかつ): a peaceful life
  • 静(しず)かに暮(く)らす: to live quietly

DESCRIBING A PERSON:

Applied to a person, 静(しず)か describes someone who says little and keeps a low profile - it is about how much noise and fuss they make, not about obedience. Two neighbours differ:

  • 大人(おとな)しい: quiet and well-behaved, docile - often said of children and animals.
  • 穏(おだ)やか: even-tempered, mild - about temperament rather than volume, and also used of weather and the sea.

All three are complimentary in most contexts, but only 静(しず)か is the natural word for a place.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 静(しず)けさ: quietness, silence (noun)
  • 静寂(せいじゃく): silence, stillness (more literary)
  • 静(しず)まる: to become quiet (verb)
  • 静(しず)める: to quiet, calm down (verb)

Related Words

Antonym: うるさい (noisy, annoying)
Antonym: 賑(にぎ)やか (lively, bustling)
Related: 穏(おだ)やか (calm, even-tempered)
Related: 大人(おとな)しい (quiet and well-behaved)
