家(いえ)

いえ
noun
house, home
1. house, home
A building for dwelling; one's residence.
家(いえ)に帰(かえ)ります。
I'm going home.
大(おお)きい家(いえ)に住(す)んでいます。
I live in a big house.
家(いえ)で勉強(べんきょう)します。
I study at home.
友達(ともだち)の家(いえ)に遊(あそ)びに行(い)きました。
I went to my friend's house to hang out.
来年(らいねん)、新(あたら)しい家(いえ)を買(か)いたいと思(おも)っていますが、今(いま)はお金(かね)がありません。
I want to buy a new house next year, but I don't have money right now.

家(いえ) refers to a house (building) and home (place where one lives).

READINGS:

  • 家(いえ): house, home (standard reading)
  • うち: home, my house (more colloquial, can also mean "my family/company")

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 家(いえ)に帰(かえ)る: to go home
  • 家(いえ)を建(た)てる: to build a house
  • 家(いえ)を買(か)う: to buy a house
  • 家(いえ)で仕事(しごと)をする: to work from home

COMPOUNDS:

  • 家賃(やちん): rent
  • 家族(かぞく): family
  • 家事(かじ): housework
  • 家庭(かてい): household, home

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 住宅(じゅうたく): residence, housing (formal)
  • 自宅(じたく): one's own home (formal)
  • マンション: apartment building
