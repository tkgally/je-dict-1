巣(す)

す
noun
nest, den, hive

Definitions

1. nest
A structure built by birds or other animals to hold eggs or young.
2. den, hideout
A place where someone or something lives or hides.

Examples

鳥(とり)が巣(す)を作(つく)っている。
A bird is building a nest.
蜂(はち)の巣(す)に気(き)をつけて。
Be careful of the beehive.

Notes

巣(す) refers to nests and dens.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 鳥(とり)の巣(す): bird's nest
  • 蜂(はち)の巣(す): beehive
  • 蜘蛛(くも)の巣(す): spider web
  • 巣箱(すばこ): birdhouse, nesting box
