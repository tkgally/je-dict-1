速(はや)い／早(はや)い

はやい
adjective (i-adjective)
fast, early
1. fast, quick
Moving or happening at high speed. Written as 速(はや)い. Opposite of 遅(おそ)い (slow).
この電車(でんしゃ)は速(はや)いです。
This train is fast.
Sense 1: speed
速(はや)く歩(ある)いてください。
Please walk fast.
Adverb form: 速(はや)く
この車(くるま)は速(はや)くて、気持(きも)ちがいいです。
This car is fast and feels good.
Sense 1: speed
彼(かれ)は走(はし)るのが速(はや)いです。
He runs fast.
Sense 1: speed of running
時間(じかん)が経(た)つのは早(はや)いですね。
Time passes quickly, doesn't it?
Sense 1: speed of time passing
2. early
Happening before the expected or usual time. Written as 早(はや)い. Opposite of 遅(おそ)い (late).
朝(あさ)早(はや)く起(お)きました。
I woke up early in the morning.
Sense 2: early time
もう帰(かえ)るの？早(はや)いね！
You're leaving already? That's early!
Sense 2: sooner than expected
早(はや)く寝(ね)たほうがいいです。
You should go to bed early.
Adverb form: 早(はや)く
今日(きょう)は早(はや)く仕事(しごと)が終(お)わりました。
Work finished early today.
Sense 2: earlier than expected
明日(あした)は早(はや)いから、もう寝(ね)ます。
Tomorrow is early, so I'm going to bed now.
Sense 2: early start time

Two different kanji share this reading, each with distinct meaning.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 速(はや)い: fast, quick (relates to speed)
  • 早(はや)い: early (relates to time)

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 速(はや)く / 早(はや)く
  • Negative: 速(はや)くない / 早(はや)くない
  • Past: 速(はや)かった / 早(はや)かった

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 足(あし)が速(はや)い: to be fast (at running)
  • 仕事(しごと)が速(はや)い: to work quickly
  • 朝(あさ)早(はや)く: early in the morning
  • 〜するのが早(はや)い: to be quick to do ~

OPPOSITE:
遅(おそ)い covers both meanings: slow and late.
