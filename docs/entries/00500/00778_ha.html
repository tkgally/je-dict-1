歯(は)

は
noun
tooth, teeth
1. tooth, teeth
The hard structures in the mouth used for biting and chewing. Used for dental care, physical descriptions, and tooth-related expressions.
歯(は)を磨(みが)きましたか。
Did you brush your teeth?
歯(は)が痛(いた)いので、歯(は)医者(いしゃ)に行(い)きます。
My tooth hurts, so I'm going to the dentist.
子供(こども)の歯(は)が抜(ぬ)けました。
The child's tooth fell out.
寝(ね)る前(まえ)に歯(は)を磨(みが)いてね。
Brush your teeth before you go to bed, okay?
彼(かれ)は歯(は)が白(しろ)くてきれい(きれい)です。
His teeth are white and beautiful.

歯(は) refers to tooth/teeth, used in dental care, physical descriptions, and idioms.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 歯(は)を磨(みが)く: to brush one's teeth
  • 歯(は)が痛(いた)い: tooth hurts
  • 歯(は)が抜(ぬ)ける: tooth falls out

DENTAL VOCABULARY:

  • 歯(は)医者(いしゃ): dentist
  • 歯(は)ブラシ: toothbrush
  • 歯(は)磨(みが)き粉(こ): toothpaste
  • 虫歯(むしば): cavity, decayed tooth

IDIOM:
歯(は)が立(た)たない: to be no match for (lit. 'teeth can't stand')
