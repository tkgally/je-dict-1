北(きた)

きた
noun
north
1. north
The direction opposite to south, toward the Arctic in the Northern Hemisphere.
北(きた)に向(む)かって歩(ある)きます。
I'll walk toward the north.
北海道(ほっかいどう)は日本(にほん)の北(きた)にあります。
Hokkaido is in the north of Japan.
北口(きたぐち)で待(ま)っています。
I'll be waiting at the north exit.

北 is one of the four cardinal directions.

Related:

  • 北海道 (Hokkaido—'northern sea road')
  • 北極 (hokkyoku, North Pole)
  • 北口 (kitaguchi, north exit). In station announcements and signs
  • the cardinal directions are commonly used.
