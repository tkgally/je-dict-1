十三(じゅうさん) is the number thirteen, formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 三(さん) (three).

COMMON USES:

  • 十三(じゅうさん)歳(さい): thirteen years old
  • 十三(じゅうさん)人(にん): thirteen people
  • 十三(じゅうさん)日(にち): the 13th day (of the month)
  • 十三(じゅうさん)時(じ): 1 PM (13:00 in 24-hour time)

CULTURE:
Unlike in Western cultures where 13 is considered unlucky, there is no particular superstition around this number in Japan. However, the concept of 'Friday the 13th' (十三(じゅうさん)日(にち)の金曜日(きんようび)) has been introduced through Western media.
