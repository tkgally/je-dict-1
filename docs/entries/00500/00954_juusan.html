十三(じゅうさん)

じゅうさん
number
thirteen
1. thirteen
The number thirteen. Formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 三(さん) (three).
十三(じゅうさん)歳(さい)の誕生日(たんじょうび)おめでとう。
Happy thirteenth birthday.
十三(じゅうさん)日(にち)の金曜日(きんようび)
Friday the thirteenth
答(こた)えは十三(じゅうさん)です。
The answer is thirteen.

Unlike in Western cultures where 13 is considered unlucky, there is no particular superstition around this number in Japan. However, the concept of 'Friday the 13th' has been introduced through Western media.
