先週(せんしゅう)

せんしゅう
noun, adverb
last week
1. last week
The week before this one; the previous week. Used to refer to events or states in the week that just passed.
先週(せんしゅう)映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I watched a movie last week.
先週(せんしゅう)の金曜日(きんようび)に会議(かいぎ)がありました。
There was a meeting last Friday.
先週(せんしゅう)は雨(あめ)が多(おお)かったです。
There was a lot of rain last week.

Part of a time reference series: 先週(せんしゅう) (last week), 今週(こんしゅう) (this week), 来週(らいしゅう) (next week). The 先(せん) prefix means 'previous' or 'before' and also appears in 先月(せんげつ) (last month).
