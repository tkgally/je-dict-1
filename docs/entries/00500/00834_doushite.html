どうして

どうして
adverb
why, how come

Definitions

1. why, how come
Used to ask for the reason or cause of something. Slightly more casual than なぜ.
2. how
Used to ask about the method or way to do something.

Examples

どうして遅(おく)れましたか。
Why were you late?
Sense 1: asking for reason
どうして日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)していますか。
Why are you studying Japanese?
どうしてわからないの?
How come you don't understand?
Casual speech

Notes

More conversational than なぜ (why) which is more formal. Often followed by か in questions. Can express surprise or disbelief: どうして?! (Why?!/How come?!). Response typically uses から (because).
