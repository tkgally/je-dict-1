それ

それ
pronoun
that
1. that (thing)
A demonstrative pronoun referring to something close to the listener, or something just mentioned.
それは何(なん)?
What is that?
それを取(と)ってください。
Please pass me that.
それはいいですね。
That's good. / That sounds good.
それは私(わたし)のです。返(かえ)してください。
That's mine. Please give it back.
それを聞(き)いて安心(あんしん)しました。心配(しんぱい)していたので、よかったです。
I was relieved to hear that. I was worried, so I'm glad.

それ refers to things near the listener or to information just mentioned in conversation. It's part of the ko-so-a-do system. When someone tells you something, you can respond with それはいいですね (that's good) or それは大変(たいへん)ですね (that's tough). To modify nouns, use その instead.
