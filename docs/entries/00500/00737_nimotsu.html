荷物(にもつ)

にもつ
noun
luggage, baggage
1. luggage, baggage
Bags, suitcases, or packages that one carries when traveling. Can also refer to parcels or cargo.
荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ちましょうか。
Shall I carry your luggage?
荷物(にもつ)が多(おお)いですね。
You have a lot of luggage.
荷物(にもつ)はどこに置(お)きますか。
Where should I put the luggage?
荷物(にもつ)を車(くるま)に入(い)れてください。大(おお)きいですから、気(き)をつけて。
Please put the luggage in the car. It's big, so be careful.
旅行(りょこう)の時(とき)、荷物(にもつ)が多(おお)すぎて大変(たいへん)でした。次(つぎ)からはもっと少(すく)ない荷物(にもつ)で行(い)きたいです。
When I traveled, I had too much luggage and it was tough. Next time I want to go with less luggage.

Counter: 個(こ) for pieces of luggage.

Common expressions:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)ける (to check luggage)
  • 手(て)荷物(にもつ) (hand luggage/carry-on). At Japanese train stations
  • you'll often see coin lockers for storing 荷物(にもつ).
