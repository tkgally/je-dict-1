荷物(にもつ)

にもつ
noun
luggage, baggage
1. luggage, baggage
Bags, suitcases, or packages that one carries when traveling. Can also refer to parcels or cargo.
荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ちましょうか。
Shall I carry your luggage?
荷物(にもつ)が多(おお)いですね。
You have a lot of luggage.
荷物(にもつ)はどこに置(お)きますか。
Where should I put the luggage?
荷物(にもつ)を車(くるま)に入(い)れてください。大(おお)きいですから、気(き)をつけて。
Please put the luggage in the car. It's big, so be careful.
旅行(りょこう)の時(とき)、荷物(にもつ)が多(おお)すぎて大変(たいへん)でした。次(つぎ)からはもっと少(すく)ない荷物(にもつ)で行(い)きたいです。
When I traveled, I had too much luggage and it was tough. Next time I want to go with less luggage.

荷物(にもつ) means luggage, baggage, or parcels - items carried when traveling or packages being transported.

COUNTER:
個(こ) counts pieces of luggage: 荷物(にもつ)一(いっ)個(こ) (one piece of luggage)

COMMON TYPES:

  • 手(て)荷物(にもつ): hand luggage, carry-on
  • 預(あず)け荷物(にもつ): checked baggage
  • 宅配(たくはい)荷物(にもつ): delivery package

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を持(も)つ: to carry luggage
  • 荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)ける: to check luggage
  • 荷物(にもつ)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive luggage
  • 荷物(にもつ)を置(お)く: to put down luggage

TRAVEL IN JAPAN:
Train stations have コインロッカー (coin lockers) for storing 荷物(にもつ). Luggage delivery services (宅配便(たくはいびん)) can send bags ahead to your next hotel.
