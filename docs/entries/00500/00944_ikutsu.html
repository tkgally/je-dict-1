幾(いく)つ

いくつ
pronoun
how many; how old
1. how many
Used to ask about quantity, especially for items counted with the general counter つ.
りんごは幾(いく)つありますか。
How many apples are there?
幾(いく)つ欲(ほ)しいですか。
How many do you want?
卵(たまご)は幾(いく)つ買(か)いましたか。
How many eggs did you buy?
部屋(へや)には窓(まど)が幾(いく)つありますか。
How many windows are there in the room?
お菓子(かし)を幾(いく)つか買(か)って帰(かえ)りましょう。
Let's buy some sweets on our way home.
幾つか = some, several
2. how old
Used to ask about someone's age, particularly for children or in casual contexts.
お子(こ)さんは幾(いく)つですか。
How old is your child?
Asking about age
赤(あか)ちゃんは今(いま)幾(いく)つですか。
How old is the baby now?
お幾(いく)つですか。三十五(さんじゅうご)です。
How old are you? I'm thirty-five.
Polite form for adults
弟(おとうと)は幾(いく)つですか。五(いつ)つです。
How old is your younger brother? He's five.
来週(らいしゅう)、幾(いく)つになりますか。六(むっ)つになります。
How old will you be next week? I'll be six.

幾(いく)つ is one of the question words for quantity and is often written in hiragana. For asking age, it's commonly used for children or in casual situations. For adults, 何歳(なんさい)ですか or お幾(いく)つですか (polite) is more appropriate. The word corresponds to the counter つ (hitotsu, futatsu, etc.).
