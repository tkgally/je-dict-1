犬(いぬ)

いぬ
noun
dog
1. dog
A domesticated carnivorous mammal, commonly kept as a pet or working animal.
犬(いぬ)を飼(か)いたいです。
I want to have a dog.
犬(いぬ)が吠(ほ)えています。
The dog is barking.
大(おお)きい犬(いぬ)ですね。
That's a big dog.

犬(いぬ) is the word for 'dog.' Famous Japanese dog breeds include 秋田犬(あきたいぬ), 柴犬(しばいぬ). The story of Hachiko (ハチ公(ハチこう)), the loyal Akita dog, is famous in Japan.

Related:

  • 子犬(こいぬ): puppy
  • 番犬(ばんけん): guard dog
