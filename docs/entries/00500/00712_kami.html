紙(かみ)

かみ
noun
paper

Definitions

1. paper
Material made from wood pulp, used for writing, printing, and wrapping. A basic everyday item.

Examples

紙(かみ)をください。
Please give me some paper.
紙(かみ)に書(か)いてください。
Please write it on paper.
白(しろ)い紙(かみ)がありますか。
Do you have white paper?

Notes

Counter: 枚(まい) (一(いち)枚(まい), one sheet). Don't confuse with 髪(かみ) (hair), which has the same reading but different kanji.

Related words:

  • 折(お)り紙(がみ) (origami)
  • 新聞(しんぶん)紙(し) (newspaper).
