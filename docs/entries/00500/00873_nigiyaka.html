にぎやか

にぎやか
adjective (na-adjective)
lively, bustling

Definitions

1. lively, bustling, busy
Full of life and activity. Describes places with many people and activity, or atmospheres that are cheerful and animated.

Examples

この町(まち)はとてもにぎやかです。
This town is very lively.
にぎやかな場所(ばしょ)が好(す)きです。
I like bustling places.
na-adjective + な + noun
週末(しゅうまつ)は駅前(えきまえ)がにぎやかになります。
The area in front of the station becomes lively on weekends.

Notes

Can be written as 賑(にぎ)やか in kanji but hiragana is common. Opposite of 静(しず)か (quiet). Has a positive connotation unlike うるさい (noisy) which is negative. Describes pleasant liveliness.
