あまり

あまり
adverb
not very, not much
1. not very, not much
Used with negatives to express a low degree.
あまり好(す)きじゃないです。
I don't like it very much.
日本語(にほんご)があまりわかりません。
I don't understand Japanese very well.
あまり食(た)べませんでした。
I didn't eat much.
今日(きょう)はあまり寒(さむ)くなかったので、外(そと)で遊(あそ)びました。
It wasn't very cold today, so we played outside.
この店(みせ)は安(やす)いけど、あまりおいしくないので、友達(ともだち)にはすすめません。
This shop is cheap, but the food isn't very good, so I don't recommend it to friends.

あまり is used with negative forms to mean "not very" or "not much."

GRAMMAR:

  • あまり + negative: not very, not much
  • Must be followed by a negative predicate

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あまり好(す)きじゃない: don't really like
  • あまり分(わ)からない: don't really understand
  • あまり使(つか)わない: don't use much
  • あまり食(た)べない: don't eat much

DEGREE COMPARISON:

  • あまり〜ない: not very (soft negation)
  • 全然(ぜんぜん)〜ない: not at all (strong negation)
  • ほとんど〜ない: hardly, barely

Note: In casual speech, あんまり is also used.
