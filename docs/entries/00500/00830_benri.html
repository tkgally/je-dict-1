便利(べんり)

べんり
adjective (na-adjective)
convenient

Definitions

1. convenient, useful, handy
Easy to use or helpful in making things easier. Describes things, places, or services that save time or effort.

Examples

この道具(どうぐ)はとても便利(べんり)です。
This tool is very convenient.
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くで便利(べんり)です。
It's convenient because it's near the station.
スマホは便利(べんり)な物(もの)です。
Smartphones are convenient things.
na-adjective + な + noun

Notes

A very common na-adjective. Opposite is 不便(ふべん) (inconvenient). Often used to describe locations with good transportation access or tools that make life easier.
