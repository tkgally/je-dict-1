四月(しがつ)

しがつ
noun
April
1. April
The fourth month of the year. In Japan, April marks the beginning of the school year and fiscal year.
四月(しがつ)は春(はる)です。
April is spring.
四月(しがつ)は桜(さくら)がきれいです。
Cherry blossoms are beautiful in April.
四月(しがつ)から学校(がっこう)が始(はじ)まります。
School starts from April.
四月(しがつ)に友(とも)だちと花見(はなみ)に行(い)きました。
I went cherry blossom viewing with friends in April.
四月(しがつ)は暖(あたた)かくて、外(そと)で遊(あそ)ぶのが気持(きも)ちいいですね。
April is warm, and it's pleasant to play outside, isn't it?

April uses し (4) not よん. This is one of the months where the number reading changes: 四 is read し, not よん. April is when new employees join companies and students start new schools, making it a season of beginnings in Japan.
