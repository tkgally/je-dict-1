母(はは)

はは
noun
mother (humble)
1. mother (my mother)
Humble term used when referring to one's own mother when speaking to others outside the family.
母(はは)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)です。
My mother is good at cooking.
母(はは)は教師(きょうし)です。
My mother is a teacher.
母(はは)の日(ひ)にプレゼントをあげました。
I gave a present on Mother's Day.
母の日 = Mother's Day
母(はは)は毎朝(まいあさ)早(はや)く起(お)きて、朝(あさ)ごはんを作(つく)ってくれます。
My mother gets up early every morning and makes breakfast for me.
母(はは)が病気(びょうき)になったと聞(き)いて、心配(しんぱい)で仕事(しごと)が手(て)につきませんでした。
When I heard my mother was sick, I was so worried that I couldn't concentrate on work.

母(はは) is the humble term for "mother," used when referring to one's own mother when speaking to people outside the family.

POLITENESS SYSTEM:

  • 母(はは): my mother (humble, to outsiders)
  • お母(かあ)さん: mother (neutral, direct address or others' mothers)
  • お母(かあ)さま: mother (very polite/formal)
  • ママ: mom (casual, childish)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 母(はは)の日(ひ): Mother's Day
  • 母(はは)親(おや): mother (formal/written)
  • 母国(ぼこく): motherland, home country

USAGE NOTE: In casual family settings, children may call their mother お母(かあ)さん or ママ. 母(はは) is specifically for humble reference when talking to outsiders.

RELATED: 父(ちち) (father - humble)
