家内(かない)

かない
noun
wife (humble)
1. wife (humble)
A humble way to refer to one's own wife when speaking to others. This is a traditional term used by men when referring to their wives.
家内(かない)は今(いま)買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)っています。
My wife has gone shopping now.
家内(かない)と相談(そうだん)してからお返事(へんじ)します。
I'll reply after consulting with my wife.
家内(かない)が作(つく)った料理(りょうり)です。
This is a dish my wife made.

家内(かない) literally means 'inside the house' and is a humble term traditionally used by husbands to refer to their own wives. Like 主人(しゅじん), some consider it old-fashioned or implying a traditional gender role. Modern speakers often prefer 妻(つま). When referring to someone else's wife, use 奥(おく)さん (okusan).
