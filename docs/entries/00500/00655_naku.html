1. to cry, to weep
Used when someone cries or sheds tears, whether from sadness, pain, joy, or other strong emotions.
子供(こども)が泣(な)いている。
The child is crying.
泣(な)かないでください。
Please don't cry.
映画(えいが)を見(み)て泣(な)きました。
I cried watching the movie.
赤(あか)ちゃんが夜(よる)ずっと泣(な)いていました。
The baby was crying all night.
悲(かな)しい話(はなし)を聞(き)いて、泣(な)きたくなったけど、我慢(がまん)しました。
I heard a sad story and wanted to cry, but I held back.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xが泣(な)く (X cries).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 泣(な)いている = 'is crying.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 泣(な)いている (is crying)
  • 泣(な)かないで (don't cry)
  • 泣(な)き出(だ)す (burst into tears)
  • 嬉(うれ)し泣(な)き (tears of joy)

USAGE: 泣(な)く describes the act of crying itself; 涙(なみだ)が出(で)る ('tears come out') focuses on the tears and can happen without any sobbing. The potential form 泣(な)ける is used idiomatically of things, not people: この映画(えいが)は泣(な)ける = 'this movie is a tear-jerker.'

Kanji 泣(な)く contains the water radical (sanzui), a hint at its meaning. Different from 鳴(な)く (animal sounds) - homophones with different kanji.

Related Words

Antonym: 笑(わら)う (to laugh)
Related: 涙(なみだ) (tears)
Homophone: 鳴(な)く (of animals)
