それから

それから
conjunction
after that, and then
1. after that, and then
A conjunction indicating sequence in time. Emphasizes that events happen one after another.
買(か)い物(もの)をしました。それから、映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I went shopping. After that, I watched a movie.
それから、どうしましたか。
And then what happened?
2. and also, in addition
Used to add items to a list or add information.
りんごと、みかんと、それから、バナナをください。
Apples, oranges, and also bananas, please.
Adding to a list

それから combines それ (that) and から (from), literally meaning 'from that.' It indicates temporal sequence more explicitly than そして. It's also commonly used when listing items or adding to a list. Similar to 'and then' or 'after that' in English.
