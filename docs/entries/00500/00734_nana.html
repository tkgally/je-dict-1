七(なな)

なな
number
seven
1. seven
The number seven. Like four, seven has two readings: なな and しち. なな is more commonly used in everyday counting because しち can be confused with いち (one) or し (four) when spoken.
七(なな)時(じ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet at seven o'clock.
しちじ is also used
一(いっ)週間(しゅうかん)は七(なな)日(にち)です。
A week is seven days.
七(なな)月(がつ)に日本(にほん)へ行(い)きます。
I'm going to Japan in July.
しちがつ is also correct

Both なな and しち are correct readings, but なな is preferred in most spoken contexts for clarity. しち is used in 七(しち)月(がつ) (July) and sometimes 七(しち)時(じ) (seven o'clock), though なながつ and ななじ are increasingly common. The native counter is 七(なな)つ (nanatsu).
