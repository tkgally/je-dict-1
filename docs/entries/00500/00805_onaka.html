お腹(なか)

おなか
noun
stomach, belly

Definitions

1. stomach, belly, abdomen
The front part of the body between the chest and hips. Most commonly used when talking about hunger or stomach-related feelings.

Examples

お腹(なか)が空(す)きました。
I'm hungry.
The most common way to express hunger in Japanese
お腹(なか)がいっぱいです。
I'm full.
お腹(なか)が痛(いた)いです。
My stomach hurts.

Notes

お腹(なか) always includes the polite prefix お. The bare form 腹(はら) exists but sounds rough or masculine. お腹(なか)が空(す)く (to get hungry) and お腹(なか)がいっぱい (to be full) are essential everyday expressions.
