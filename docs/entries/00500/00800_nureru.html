1. to get wet, to become wet
For something or someone to become wet from water or another liquid. Intransitive verb.
雨(あめ)で服(ふく)が濡(ぬ)れました。
My clothes got wet from the rain.
傘(かさ)がなかったので、濡(ぬ)れてしまいました。
I didn't have an umbrella, so I got wet.
髪(かみ)が濡(ぬ)れています。
Your hair is wet.
靴(くつ)が濡(ぬ)れないように気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful not to get your shoes wet.
床(ゆか)が濡(ぬ)れているから、滑(すべ)らないでね。
The floor is wet, so don't slip.

濡(ぬ)れる means for something to become wet from contact with water or liquid. It describes the thing that gets wet, not the action of wetting.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 濡(ぬ)らす (transitive — to wet something)
  • Pattern: Xが濡(ぬ)れる (X gets wet)

ASPECT (ている):
濡(ぬ)れている means "is wet" (resulting state), not "is getting wet." This is one of the most common uses.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 雨(あめ)に濡(ぬ)れる: to get wet from rain
  • 汗(あせ)で濡(ぬ)れる: to be wet with sweat
  • びしょ濡(ぬ)れになる: to get soaking wet
  • 濡(ぬ)れた髪(かみ): wet hair

NOTE:
The particle に marks the cause (雨(あめ)に) while で marks the means or circumstance (汗(あせ)で).
