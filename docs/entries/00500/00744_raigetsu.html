来月(らいげつ)

らいげつ
noun, adverb
next month

Definitions

1. next month
The month after this one; the coming month. Used to refer to future events or plans in the month that follows the current one.

Examples

来月(らいげつ)旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きます。
I'm going on a trip next month.
来月(らいげつ)から新(あたら)しい仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めます。
I'm starting a new job from next month.
来月(らいげつ)の会議(かいぎ)に出席(しゅっせき)しますか。
Will you attend next month's meeting?

Notes

Part of a time reference series: 先月(せんげつ) (last month), 今月(こんげつ) (this month), 来月(らいげつ) (next month). The 来(らい) prefix means 'coming' or 'next' and also appears in 来週(らいしゅう) (next week) and 来年(らいねん) (next year).
