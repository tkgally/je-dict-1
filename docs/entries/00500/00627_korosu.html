殺(ころ)す

ころす
verb (godan)
to kill

Definitions

1. to kill
To cause death.
2. to suppress, to deaden
To restrain or hold back.

Examples

虫(むし)を殺(ころ)す。
To kill an insect.
時間(じかん)を殺(ころ)す。
To kill time.
Idiomatic expression
声(こえ)を殺(ころ)して泣(な)く。
To cry silently (suppressing one's voice).

Notes

殺(ころ)す means to kill or suppress.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)を殺(ころ)す: to kill time
  • 声(こえ)を殺(ころ)す: to suppress one's voice
  • 息(いき)を殺(ころ)す: to hold one's breath

RELATED:

  • 殺人(さつじん): murder
  • 殺(ころ)される: to be killed (passive)
