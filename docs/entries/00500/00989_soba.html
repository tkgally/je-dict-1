そば

そば
noun
beside, near

Definitions

1. beside, near, close to
A position close to something or someone. Not necessarily directly adjacent.

Examples

私(わたし)のそばに来(き)てください。
Please come to my side.
窓(まど)のそばに座(すわ)っています。
I'm sitting by the window.
母(はは)のそばにいたいです。
I want to be by my mother's side.
Emotional closeness

Notes

そば (also written as 側(そば)) indicates being close to or near something. It's less specific than 隣(となり) (which means directly adjacent). そば often implies a sense of proximity or being within reach. It can also suggest emotional closeness, as in staying 'by someone's side.' Note: This is different from そば (蕎麦), which is buckwheat noodles.
