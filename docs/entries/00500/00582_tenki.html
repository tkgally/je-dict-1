天気(てんき)

てんき
noun
weather

Definitions

1. weather
The state of the atmosphere at a particular time and place.

Examples

今日(きょう)の天気(てんき)はどうですか？
How's the weather today?
天気(てんき)がいいですね。
The weather is nice, isn't it?
明日(あした)の天気(てんき)は雨(あめ)です。
Tomorrow's weather will be rainy.

Notes

Common weather descriptions: いい天気(てんき) (nice weather), 悪(わる)い天気(てんき) (bad weather).

Related: 天気予報(てんきよほう) (weather forecast).
