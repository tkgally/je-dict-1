ここ

ここ
pronoun
here
1. here, this place
A demonstrative pronoun for a place near the speaker.
ここに座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit here.
ここは{どこ|}ですか。
Where is this place? / Where am I?
ここから駅(えき)まで歩(ある)いて十分(じゅっぷん)です。
It's a ten-minute walk from here to the station.
ここで待(ま)っていてください。すぐ(すぐ)戻(もど)りますから。
Please wait here. I'll be right back.
ここに名前(なまえ)と住所(じゅうしょ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name and address here.

ここ is a demonstrative pronoun meaning "here" or "this place" - a location near the speaker.

KO-SO-A-DO PLACE SERIES:

  • ここ: here (near speaker)
  • そこ: there (near listener)
  • あそこ: over there (far from both)
  • どこ: where (question)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ここに: here (location)
  • ここで: here (action takes place)
  • ここから: from here
  • ここまで: up to here, this far
  • この辺(へん): around here

GRAMMAR NOTE:
Unlike この (this), which must modify a noun, ここ can stand alone as a complete noun phrase.
