1. to think, to consider
To use the mind to process information, reflect on something, or come up with ideas. More deliberate than 思(おも)う which is more about feeling or believing.
よく考(かんが)えます。
I'll think carefully.
何(なに)を考(かんが)えていますか。
What are you thinking about?
もう少(すこ)し考(かんが)えさせてください。
Please let me think a bit more.
Causative form: 考えさせる
この問題(もんだい)についてよく考(かんが)えてください。
Please think carefully about this problem.
彼(かれ)は何(なに)も言(い)わないで、一人(ひとり)で静(しず)かに考(かんが)えています。
He's not saying anything, just quietly thinking by himself.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを考(かんが)える (think about/consider X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing thought. 考(かんが)えている = 'is thinking about' or 'is considering.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • よく考(かんが)える (think carefully)
  • 考(かんが)えさせてください (let me think)
  • 何(なに)を考(かんが)えている (what are you thinking about)
  • 考(かんが)え (noun: idea, thought)
  • 考(かんが)え方(かた) (way of thinking)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 思(おも)う expresses an opinion, impression, or feeling that is already formed; 考(かんが)える is the deliberate mental work of reasoning something out. 'I think it will rain' is 思(おも)う, while 'let me think about it' is 考(かんが)える.
  • Because 考(かんが)える takes effort, it pairs naturally with よく (carefully) and time expressions; 思(おも)う does not.

Related Words

Contrast: 思(おも)う (intuitive/opinion)
