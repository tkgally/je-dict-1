立(た)ち上(あ)がる

たちあがる
verb (godan)
to stand up, to rise

Definitions

1. to stand up
To rise from a sitting or lying position.
2. to rise, to take action
To start action or resistance.

Examples

椅子(いす)から立(た)ち上(あ)がる。
To stand up from a chair.
困難(こんなん)に立(た)ち上(あ)がる。
To rise against difficulties.

Notes

立(た)ち上(あ)がる is a compound verb.

COMPONENTS:

  • 立(た)つ: to stand
  • 上(あ)がる: to go up

USAGE:

  • Physical: rising from seated position
  • Figurative: taking action, standing up for something
