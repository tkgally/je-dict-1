軽(かる)い

かるい
adjective (i-adjective)
light (weight)
1. light (in weight)
Not heavy, easy to lift or carry. The opposite of 重(おも)い (heavy).
このかばんは軽(かる)いです。
This bag is light.
この本(ほん)は小(ちい)さくて軽(かる)いから、持(も)ち運(はこ)びやすい。
This book is small and light, so it's easy to carry around.
荷物(にもつ)が軽(かる)くなった。
The luggage became lighter.
Sense 1: light (weight)
このパソコンは軽(かる)いですね。
This laptop is light, isn't it?
Sense 1: light (weight)
この椅子(いす)は軽(かる)いです。
This chair is light.
Sense 1: light (weight)
2. slight, minor, casual
Can describe something that is not serious or intense, like a light meal or minor injury.
軽(かる)い食事(しょくじ)をしました。
I had a light meal.
けがは軽(かる)いです。
The injury is minor.
軽(かる)い運動(うんどう)をしましょう。
Let's do some light exercise.
軽(かる)い風邪(かぜ)をひきました。
I caught a minor cold.
Sense 2: slight/minor
軽(かる)い気持(きも)ちで始(はじ)めた。
I started it casually.
Sense 2: casual
問題(もんだい)を軽(かる)く考(かんが)えてはいけない。
You shouldn't take the problem lightly.
Sense 2: lightly/casually

軽(かる)い is an i-adjective meaning light (weight) or minor/casual.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 軽(かる)く
  • Negative: 軽(かる)くない
  • Past: 軽(かる)かった

TWO MAIN MEANINGS:
1. Light in weight: 荷物(にもつ)が軽(かる)い (luggage is light)
2. Minor/slight/casual: 軽(かる)い風邪(かぜ) (minor cold)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 軽(かる)い食事(しょくじ): light meal
  • 軽(かる)い運動(うんどう): light exercise
  • 軽(かる)く考(かんが)える: to take lightly
  • 口(くち)が軽(かる)い: loose-lipped (careless with secrets)

CONTRAST:

  • 軽(かる)い ⟷ 重(おも)い (heavy)
