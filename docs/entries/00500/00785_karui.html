軽(かる)い

かるい
adjective (i-adjective)
light (weight)
1. light (in weight)
Not heavy, easy to lift or carry. The opposite of 重(おも)い (heavy).
2. slight, minor, casual
Can describe something that is not serious or intense, like a light meal or minor injury.
このかばんは軽(かる)いです。
This bag is light.
軽(かる)い食事(しょくじ)をしました。
I had a light meal.
怪我(けが)は軽(かる)いです。
The injury is minor.

The opposite is 重(おも)い (heavy). 軽(かる)い can be used figuratively: 口(くち)が軽(かる)い (loose-lipped), 軽(かる)い気持(きも)ち (casual feeling/not taking seriously). 軽(かる)く考える means 'to take lightly'.
