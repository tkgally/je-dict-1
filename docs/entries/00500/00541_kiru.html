着(き)る

きる
verb (ichidan)
to wear, to put on (clothes)
1. to wear, to put on
To put on clothing worn on the upper body or full body (shirts, jackets, dresses). Different verbs are used for lower body, head, and accessories.
今日(きょう)は何(なに)を着(き)ますか？
What will you wear today?
コートを着(き)てください。
Please put on your coat.
彼女(かのじょ)は赤(あか)いドレスを着(き)ています。
She is wearing a red dress.
着ている = wearing (state)
寒(さむ)いので、もう一枚(いちまい)着(き)た方(ほう)がいいですよ。
It's cold, so you should put on one more layer.
兄(あに)のシャツを着(き)ていたら、「返(かえ)して」と言(い)われました。
When I was wearing my older brother's shirt, he said 'Give it back.'

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを着(き)る (wear/put on X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 着(き)ている = 'is wearing' (not 'is putting on').

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • コートを着(き)る (put on/wear a coat)
  • 着(き)ている (is wearing - state)
  • 着(き)替|か}える (change clothes)
  • 着物(きもの)を着(き)る (wear kimono)

CLOTHING VERBS:

  • 着(き)る: upper body/full body
  • はく: pants, shoes, skirts
  • かぶる: hats
  • する: accessories

Opposite: 脱(ぬ)ぐ (take off). Don't confuse with 切(き)る (to cut - godan).
