自転車(じてんしゃ)

じてんしゃ
noun
bicycle
1. bicycle, bike
A two-wheeled vehicle propelled by pedaling. Very common transportation in Japan.
自転車(じてんしゃ)で学校(がっこう)に行(い)きます。
I go to school by bicycle.
自転車(じてんしゃ)に乗(の)れますか。
Can you ride a bicycle?
自転車(じてんしゃ)を買(か)いたいです。
I want to buy a bicycle.

自転車 (literally 'self-rotating vehicle') is commonly used transportation in Japan. Bicycles must be registered and there are parking areas at stations. Related expressions: 自転車に乗る (to ride a bicycle), 自転車置き場 (bicycle parking area).
