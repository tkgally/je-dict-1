買(か)い物(もの)

かいもの
noun
shopping
1. shopping
The activity of going to stores to buy things. Often used with the verb する to form 'to go shopping'.
買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)きます。
I'm going shopping.
Common expression: 買い物に行く
昨日(きのう)買(か)い物(もの)をしました。
I went shopping yesterday.
今日(きょう)の買(か)い物(もの)は何(なに)ですか。
What are you buying today?
母(はは)と一緒(いっしょ)に買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)きました。
I went shopping with my mother.
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くのお店(みせ)で買(か)い物(もの)をするのが好(す)きです。
I like shopping at the stores near the station.

Used with に行(い)く to express going shopping, or with をする to express the action of shopping. 買(か)い物(もの)かご means 'shopping basket' and 買(か)い物(もの)リスト means 'shopping list'.
