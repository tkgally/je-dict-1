子供(こども)

こども
noun
child, children
1. child, children
A young human being, or one's own offspring regardless of age.
子供(こども)が3人(にん)います。
I have 3 children.
子供(こども)たちは公園(こうえん)で遊(あそ)んでいます。
The children are playing in the park.
たち = plural marker
子供(こども)の時(とき)、よく泳(およ)ぎました。
When I was a child, I swam often.
子供の時 = when (I was) a child
子供(こども)にも分(わ)かるように説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain it in a way that even a child can understand.
あの夫婦(ふうふ)には子供(こども)が五人(ごにん)いて、毎日(まいにち)大変(たいへん)そうです。
That couple has five children and seems to have a hard time every day.

子供(こども) can mean either 'children' in general or 'one's own children.' The word doesn't distinguish singular from plural.

POLITENESS LEVELS:

  • 子供(こども): neutral term
  • お子(こ)さん: polite (for others' children)
  • 子(こ): shorter/casual form

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 子供(こども)の時(とき): when (I was) a child
  • 子供(こども)たち: children (explicit plural)
  • 子供(こども)っぽい: childish
  • 子供(こども)向(む)け: for children

GENDERED TERMS:

  • 男(おとこ)の子(こ): boy
  • 女(おんな)の子(こ): girl

NOTE:
The kanji 供(とも) is sometimes considered offensive when written with kanji (implying 'attendant'), so こども or 子ども(こども) in mixed script is sometimes preferred in official contexts.
