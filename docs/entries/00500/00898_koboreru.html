こぼれる

こぼれる
verb (ichidan)
to spill, to overflow

Definitions

1. to spill, to overflow
For liquid or objects to fall out of a container.

Examples

水(みず)がこぼれた。
The water spilled.
涙(なみだ)がこぼれる。
Tears fall.

Notes

こぼれる is intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • こぼれる (intransitive): to spill
  • こぼす (transitive): to spill something

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 笑(え)みがこぼれる: to break into a smile
