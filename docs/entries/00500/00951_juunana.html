十七(じゅうなな)

じゅうなな
number
seventeen
1. seventeen
The number seventeen. Formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 七(なな) (seven). Can also be read as じゅうしち.
十七(じゅうなな)時(じ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet at seventeen o'clock (5 PM).
24-hour time
妹(いもうと)は十七(じゅうなな)歳(さい)になりました。
My younger sister turned seventeen.
十七(じゅうしち)日(にち)の予定(よてい)は何(なん)ですか。
What are your plans for the seventeenth?
Alternative reading じゅうしち
十七(じゅうなな)人(にん)の学生(がくせい)が教室(きょうしつ)にいます。
There are seventeen students in the classroom.
高校(こうこう)を卒業(そつぎょう)したのは十七(じゅうなな)年(ねん)前(まえ)です。
It's been seventeen years since I graduated from high school.

Seven in Japanese can be read as either なな or しち. For seventeen, both じゅうなな and じゅうしち are used. じゅうなな is more common in casual counting, while じゅうしち may be used in formal contexts. For dates, the seventeenth of the month is typically 十七(じゅうしち)日(にち).
