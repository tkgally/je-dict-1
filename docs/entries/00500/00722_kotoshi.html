今年(ことし)

ことし
noun, adverb
this year
1. this year
The current year; the year we are in now. Used to talk about events, plans, or states within the present year.
今年(ことし)は忙(いそが)しいです。
I'm busy this year.
今年(ことし)の夏(なつ)はどこに行(い)きますか。
Where are you going this summer?
今年(ことし)は雪(ゆき)が多(おお)かったです。
There was a lot of snow this year.
今年(ことし)は旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きたいので、毎月(まいつき)少(すこ)しずつお金(かね)を貯(た)めています。
I want to go on a trip this year, so I'm saving a little money each month.
今年(ことし)は去年(きょねん)より暖(あたた)かくて、桜(さくら)が早(はや)く咲(さ)きました。花見(はなみ)は三(さん)月(がつ)の終(お)わりにしましたが、天気(てんき)がよくてとてもきれいでした。
This year was warmer than last year, and the cherry blossoms bloomed early. We did hanami at the end of March, and the weather was good so it was very beautiful.

Part of a time reference series: 去年(きょねん) (last year), 今年(ことし) (this year), 来年(らいねん) (next year). Note the irregular reading: 今(こ) is not the usual reading of this kanji. A more formal equivalent is 本年(ほんねん).
