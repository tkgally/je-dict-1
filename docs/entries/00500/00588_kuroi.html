1. black
Having the darkest color, the opposite of white.
黒(くろ)い髪(かみ)がきれいです。
Your black hair is beautiful.
黒(くろ)い猫(ねこ)が好(す)きです。
I like black cats.
空(そら)が黒(くろ)くなりました。
The sky turned black.
黒(くろ)いスーツを着(き)て、会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)きます。
I wear a black suit to go to work.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうですね。空(そら)が黒(くろ)い雲(くも)でいっぱいです。
It looks like it's going to rain. The sky is full of black clouds.

黒(くろ)い is an i-adjective meaning black - the darkest color.

FORMS:

  • Adjective: 黒(くろ)い (black)
  • Noun: 黒(くろ) (black, the color black)
  • Adverb: 黒(くろ)く (黒(くろ)くなる - to become dark)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黒(くろ)い髪(かみ): black hair
  • 黒(くろ)い目(め): dark eyes
  • 黒(くろ)い服(ふく): black clothes
  • 黒(くろ)い猫(ねこ): black cat

RELATED COLORS:

  • 白(しろ)い: white (opposite)
  • 赤(あか)い: red
  • 青(あお)い: blue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 暗(くら)い is the word for a place or room that has little light. Japanese does not use 黒(くろ)い for 'dark' in that sense: 部屋(へや)が暗(くら)い (the room is dark), not 黒(くろ)い. 黒(くろ)い describes the color of a thing.

NOTE:
黒(くろ) can also have figurative meanings like "guilty" (黒(くろ)だ - guilty) in contrast to 白(しろ) (innocent).
