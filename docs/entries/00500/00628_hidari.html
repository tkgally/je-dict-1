左(ひだり)

ひだり
noun
left
1. left (direction)
The direction or side opposite to right.
左(ひだり)に曲(ま)がってください。
Please turn left.
左(ひだり)に郵便局(ゆうびんきょく)が見(み)えます。
You can see the post office on the left.
左手(ひだりて)で書(か)きます。
I write with my left hand.

左 is the opposite of 右 (migi, right).

Related:

  • 左手 (hidarite, left hand)
  • 左側 (hidarigawa, left side)
  • 左折 (sasetsu, left turn). In Japan
  • traffic drives on the left side.
