端(はし)

はし
noun
edge, end
1. edge, end, tip
The outermost part or boundary of something.
テーブルの端(はし)に座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit at the end of the table.
紙(かみ)の端(はし)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name at the edge of the paper.
道(みち)の端(はし)を歩(ある)いてください。
Please walk on the edge of the road.

端(はし) refers to the outermost edge or end of something. Don't confuse this with 橋(はし) (bridge) or 箸(はし) (chopsticks) - all three are pronounced 'hashi' but have different kanji and pitch accents. [noun]の端(はし) means 'the edge/end of [noun].'
