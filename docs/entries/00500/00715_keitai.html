携帯(けいたい)

けいたい
noun
mobile phone, cell phone
1. mobile phone, cell phone
A portable telephone. Short for 携帯(けいたい)電話(でんわ). One of the most commonly used words in modern Japanese.
携帯(けいたい)を忘(わす)れました。
I forgot my phone.
携帯(けいたい)の番号(ばんごう)は何(なん)ですか。
What's your cell phone number?
携帯(けいたい)で写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)りました。
I took a photo with my phone.

Short for 携帯(けいたい)電話(でんわ). Also commonly called スマホ (smartphone) in modern usage. 携帯(けいたい) literally means 'portable' but is almost always understood to mean 'mobile phone' in daily conversation.
