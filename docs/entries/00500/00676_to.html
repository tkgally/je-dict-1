戸(と)

と
noun
door, sliding door
1. door, sliding door
A door, especially a traditional Japanese sliding door. Often used in compounds.
戸(と)を閉(し)めてください。
Please close the door.
戸(と)が開(あ)いています。
The door is open.
雨戸(あまど)を閉(し)めましょう。
Let's close the storm shutters.
雨戸 = rain shutters
風(かぜ)が強(つよ)いので、戸(と)がガタガタ言(い)っています。
The wind is strong, so the door is rattling.
この古(ふる)い家(いえ)の戸(と)は木(き)でできていて、開(あ)けるのが大変(たいへん)です。
The sliding doors of this old house are made of wood, so they're hard to open.

戸 originally refers to traditional Japanese sliding doors (as opposed to hinged doors, which are ドア).

Common compounds:

  • 雨戸 (amado, storm shutters)
  • 戸棚 (todana, cupboard). For modern hinged doors
  • ドア is more commonly used.
