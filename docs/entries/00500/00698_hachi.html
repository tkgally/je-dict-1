八(はち)

はち
number
eight
1. eight
The number eight. Considered a lucky number in Japanese culture because the kanji 八(はち) widens at the bottom, symbolizing growing prosperity. Consistent in most counter combinations.
八(はち)時(じ)から仕事(しごと)です。
Work starts at eight o'clock.
たこには足(あし)が八(はち)本(ほん)あります。
An octopus has eight legs.
Note: はち + ほん = はっぽん
八(はち)月(がつ)は暑(あつ)いです。
August is hot.

The native counter is 八(やっ)つ (yattsu). When combined with counters starting with h-sounds, はち becomes はっ: 八(はっ)本(ぽん) (happon), 八(はっ)百(ぴゃく) (happyaku, 800). The number 8 is auspicious in Japan, and dates like 8/8 are considered lucky.
