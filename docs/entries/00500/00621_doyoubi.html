土曜日(どようび)

どようび
noun
Saturday
1. Saturday
The first day of the weekend. The kanji 土 means 'earth/soil' and relates to Saturn (the 'earth planet' in East Asian tradition).
土曜日(どようび)に買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)きます。
I go shopping on Saturday.
土曜日(どようび)は何(なに)をしますか。
What do you do on Saturday?
土曜日(どようび)の朝(あさ)は{ゆっくり}します。
I take it easy on Saturday morning.
今週(こんしゅう)の土曜日(どようび)は友達(ともだち)と映画(えいが)を見(み)ます。
This Saturday I'm going to watch a movie with a friend.
土曜日(どようび)と日曜日(にちようび)は会社(かいしゃ)が休(やす)みなので、家族(かぞく)と一緒(いっしょ)に過(す)ごします。
The company is closed on Saturday and Sunday, so I spend time with my family.

土曜日(どようび) means Saturday. The 土(ど) (earth/soil) relates to Saturn (the 'earth star' in East Asian astronomy).

FORMS:

  • 土曜日(どようび): Saturday (full form)
  • 土曜(どよう): Saturday (abbreviated)
  • 土(ど): Sat (very short, in schedules)

DAYS OF THE WEEK:

  • 日曜日(にちようび): Sunday (日(にち) = sun)
  • 月曜日(げつようび): Monday (月(げつ) = moon)
  • 火曜日(かようび): Tuesday (火(か) = fire/Mars)
  • 水曜日(すいようび): Wednesday (水(すい) = water/Mercury)
  • 木曜日(もくようび): Thursday (木(もく) = wood/Jupiter)
  • 金曜日(きんようび): Friday (金(きん) = metal/Venus)
  • 土曜日(どようび): Saturday (土(ど) = earth/Saturn)

USAGE:

  • "on Saturday" is 土曜日(どようび)に, but the particle is routinely dropped in casual speech: 土曜日(どようび)映画(えいが)見(み)に行(い)こう
  • 毎週(まいしゅう)土曜日(どようび): every Saturday
  • 今度(こんど)の土曜日(どようび): this coming Saturday
  • 来週(らいしゅう)の土曜日(どようび): next Saturday
  • In schedules and calendars the day is written with the bare kanji: (土(ど))

For most workers and students 土曜日(どようび) is part of the weekend, but many schools and some companies still hold classes or work on some Saturdays, so it is less uniformly a day off than Sunday.

NOTE:
Don't confuse with 土用(どよう) (18-day seasonal periods).
