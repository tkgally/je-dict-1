雑誌(ざっし)

ざっし
noun
magazine
1. magazine, periodical
A periodical publication containing articles, photographs, and advertisements on various topics.
雑誌(ざっし)を読(よ)んでいます。
I'm reading a magazine.
どんな雑誌(ざっし)が好(す)きですか。
What kind of magazines do you like?
ファッション雑誌(ざっし)を買(か)いました。
I bought a fashion magazine.

雑誌 covers various types of magazines and periodicals. Japan has a rich magazine culture with many specialized publications.

Related:

  • 週刊誌 (shuukanshi, weekly magazine)
  • 月刊誌 (gekkanshi, monthly magazine).
