引退(いんたい)

いんたい
noun/suru verb
retirement

Definitions

1. retirement
Withdrawal from one's profession, career, or public life.

Examples

来年(らいねん)引退(いんたい)する予定(よてい)だ。
I plan to retire next year.
選手(せんしゅ)が引退(いんたい)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The athlete announced their retirement.
引退(いんたい)試合(じあい)を行(おこな)う。
To hold a retirement match.

Notes

引退(いんたい) refers to retirement from a career or profession.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 引退(いんたい)する (to retire)
  • 引退(いんたい)を表明(ひょうめい)する (announce retirement)
  • 引退(いんたい)試合(じあい) (retirement match/game)
  • 引退(いんたい)後(ご) (after retirement)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引退(いんたい): retirement (from career/profession)
  • 退職(たいしょく): leaving a job, retirement from work
  • 定年(ていねん): mandatory retirement age
