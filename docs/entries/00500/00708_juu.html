十(じゅう)

じゅう
number
ten
1. ten
The number ten. A base number in Japanese counting, used to form larger numbers (11 = じゅういち, 20 = にじゅう, etc.). Very consistent in pronunciation across most combinations.
十(じゅう)分(ぷん)で着(つ)きます。
I'll arrive in ten minutes.
十(じゅう)人(にん)のクラスです。
It's a class of ten people.
十(じゅう)月(がつ)は秋(あき)なので涼(すず)しいです。
October is autumn, so it's cool.
今日(きょう)は十(じゅう)時間(じかん)以上(いじょう)仕事(しごと)をしたので、とても疲(つか)れました。
I worked more than ten hours today, so I'm very tired.
毎朝(まいあさ)十(じゅう)時(じ)から十二(じゅうに)時(じ)まで日本語(にほんご)の勉強(べんきょう)をして、午後(ごご)は図書館(としょかん)で本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいます。
Every morning I study Japanese from ten to twelve o'clock, and in the afternoon I hang out with friends.

十(じゅう) is the Sino-Japanese reading for ten, used in most counting and arithmetic contexts.

READINGS:

  • じゅう: standard Sino-Japanese reading, used in compounds and with counters
  • とお: native Japanese reading, used for counting objects without a counter (十(とお)つ)

SOUND CHANGES:

Before certain counters, じゅう becomes じゅっ or じっ:

  • 十(じゅっ)本(ぽん) (ten long objects)
  • 十(じっ)歳(さい) (ten years old)
  • 十(じゅっ)回(かい) (ten times)

BUILDING NUMBERS:

  • 二(に)十(じゅう) (20), 三(さん)十(じゅう) (30), etc.
  • 十(じゅう)一(いち) (11), 十(じゅう)二(に) (12), etc.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 十分(じゅうぶん): enough, sufficient (also read じゅっぷん for 'ten minutes')
  • 十(じゅう)人(にん)十(じゅう)色(いろ): to each their own (lit. ten people, ten colors)
