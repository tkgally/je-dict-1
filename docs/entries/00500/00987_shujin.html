主人(しゅじん)

しゅじん
noun
husband; master
1. husband (humble)
A humble way to refer to one's own husband when speaking to others. This is the traditional term used when women speak about their own husbands.
主人(しゅじん)は会社(かいしゃ)です。
My husband is at work.
主人(しゅじん)は今(いま)会社(かいしゃ)にいます。
My husband is at the company now.
うちの主人(しゅじん)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)です。
My husband is good at cooking.
主人(しゅじん)がお世話(せわ)になっております。
Thank you for taking care of my husband.
Polite business expression
主人(しゅじん)と相談(そうだん)してから、お返事(へんじ)します。少々(しょうしょう)お待(ま)ちください。
I will reply after consulting with my husband. Please wait a moment.
2. master, owner
The master or owner of a household, business, or establishment.
店(みせ)の主人(しゅじん)に聞(き)く。
To ask the shop owner.
この店(みせ)の主人(しゅじん)はとても親切(しんせつ)です。
The owner of this shop is very kind.
旅館(りょかん)の主人(しゅじん)が案内(あんない)してくれました。
The owner of the inn showed us around.
犬(いぬ)は主人(しゅじん)をよく覚(おぼ)えています。
Dogs remember their masters well.
あの店(みせ)は主人(しゅじん)が代(か)わってから、味(あじ)が変(か)わりました。前(まえ)の方(ほう)がおいしかったです。
That shop's taste changed after the owner changed. It was more delicious before.

主人(しゅじん) is a humble term traditionally used by wives to refer to their own husbands. Some modern Japanese women prefer using 夫(おっと) instead, as 主人(しゅじん) literally means 'master' and can feel hierarchical. When referring to someone else's husband, use ご主人(しゅじん). The term can also mean the owner or proprietor of a business.
