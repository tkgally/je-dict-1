乾(かわ)かす

かわかす
verb (godan)
to dry

Definitions

1. to dry, to dry off
To remove moisture from something.

Examples

髪(かみ)を乾(かわ)かす。
To dry one's hair.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を乾(かわ)かす。
To dry the laundry.
ドライヤーで乾(かわ)かす。
To dry with a dryer.

Notes

乾(かわ)かす means to dry.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 乾(かわ)かす (transitive): to dry something
  • 乾(かわ)く (intransitive): to dry, become dry

RELATED:

  • ドライヤー: hair dryer
  • 乾燥(かんそう): dryness
