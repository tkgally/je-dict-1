写真(しゃしん)

しゃしん
noun
photograph, picture
1. photograph, photo
An image captured by a camera. One of the most common words for talking about pictures.
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)ってもいいですか。
May I take a photo?
この写真(しゃしん)はどこで撮(と)りましたか。
Where did you take this photo?
写真(しゃしん)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me the photo.
家族(かぞく)の写真(しゃしん)を友達(ともだち)に見(み)せました。
I showed photos of my family to my friend.
日本(にほん)でたくさん}{写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)りました。全部(ぜんぶ)きれいです。
I took many photos in Japan. They're all beautiful.

Common collocations: 写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る (take a photo), 写真(しゃしん)を見(み)る (look at a photo). The verb 撮(と)る is specifically used for taking photos/videos. Don't confuse with 絵(え) (drawing/painting).
