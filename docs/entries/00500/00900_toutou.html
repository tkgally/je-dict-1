とうとう

とうとう
adverb
finally, at last

Definitions

1. finally, at last, in the end
After a long time or much effort, something finally happens. Often implies the speaker has been waiting for or expecting this outcome.

Examples

とうとう夢(ゆめ)が叶(かな)いました。
My dream finally came true.
とうとう雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り始(はじ)めました。
It finally started raining.
とうとう彼(かれ)は来(こ)なかった。
In the end, he didn't come.
Can be used for negative outcomes too

Notes

Can be written as 到頭(とうとう) in kanji but hiragana is more common. Similar to やっと but とうとう can be used for both positive and negative outcomes, while やっと is usually positive. とうとう often implies inevitability.
