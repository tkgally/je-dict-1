口(くち)

くち
noun
mouth

Definitions

1. mouth
The opening in the face used for eating and speaking.

Examples

口(くち)を開(あ)けてください。
Please open your mouth.
口(くち)に合(あ)いますか？
Does it suit your taste?
口に合う = to suit one's taste
口(くち)が堅(かた)い人(ひと)です。
They are a person who can keep secrets.
口が堅い = tight-lipped

Notes

Many idiomatic expressions: 口(くち)に合(あ)う (suit one's taste), 口(くち)が堅(かた)い (tight-lipped), 口(くち)が軽(かる)い (loose-lipped). Also used for openings: 入(い)り口(ぐち) (entrance), 出(で)口(ぐち) (exit).
