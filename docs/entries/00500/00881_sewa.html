世話(せわ)

せわ
noun (verbal)
care, looking after, help

Definitions

1. care, looking after
Taking care of someone or something.
2. help, assistance
Providing aid or support to someone.

Examples

子供(こども)の世話(せわ)をする。
To look after children.
お世話(せわ)になりました。
Thank you for your help. / I'm grateful for your care.
Common polite expression

Notes

世話(せわ) means care or help.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お世話(せわ)になる: to receive someone's help
  • 世話(せわ)を焼(や)く: to meddle, to be nosy

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世話(せわ)をする: to look after
  • 世話(せわ)になる: to be in someone's care
