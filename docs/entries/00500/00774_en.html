円(えん)

えん
noun
yen; circle
1. yen (Japanese currency)
The official currency of Japan. Symbol: ¥.
これは百(ひゃく)円(えん)です。
This is 100 yen.
五千(ごせん)円(えん)で足(た)りますか。
Is 5000 yen enough?
2. circle
A round shape. The kanji literally means 'round/circle'.
円(えん)を描(か)いてください。
Please draw a circle.

The yen is Japan's currency since 1871. Coins: 1円(えん), 5円(えん), 10円(えん), 50円(えん), 100円(えん), 500円(えん). Bills: 1000円(えん), 5000円(えん), 10000円(えん). The 5円(えん) coin is considered lucky (ご縁(えん) = connection/fate). 円高(えんだか) means 'strong yen', 円安(えんやす) means 'weak yen'.
