出(で)る

でる
verb (ichidan)
to go out, to leave, to appear
1. to go out, to leave, to exit
To move from inside to outside a place. The opposite of 入(はい)る (hairu).
8時(じ)に家(いえ)を出(で)ます。
I leave the house at 8 o'clock.
Note: を particle for leaving a place
部屋(へや)から出(で)ました。
I left the room.
Sense 1: から marks the origin
外(そと)に出(で)ましょう。
Let's go outside.
電車(でんしゃ)から出(で)るとき、気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful when getting off the train.
Sense 1: exiting a vehicle
大学(だいがく)を出(で)てから、この会社(かいしゃ)で働(はたら)いています。
Since graduating from university, I've been working at this company.
Sense 1: graduating (leaving school)
2. to appear, to come out
For something to become visible or available, such as results or products.
結果(けっか)が出(で)ました。
The results came out.
Sense 2: appearing
声(こえ)が出(で)ません。
I can't make a sound.
Sense 2: voice coming out
答(こた)えが出(で)ましたか。
Did you find the answer?
Sense 2: answer appearing
熱(ねつ)が出(で)たので、学校(がっこう)を休(やす)みました。
I got a fever, so I was absent from school.
Sense 2: fever appearing
新(あたら)しい本(ほん)が出(で)たので、買(か)いに行(い)きました。
A new book came out, so I went to buy it.
Sense 2: product release
3. to attend, to participate
To be present at meetings, events, or competitions.
会議(かいぎ)に出(で)てください。
Please attend the meeting.
Sense 3: attending (with に)
授業(じゅぎょう)に出(で)なければなりません。
I must attend class.
Sense 3: attending class
試合(しあい)に出(で)たいです。
I want to participate in the match.
Sense 3: participating in competition
毎週(まいしゅう)日曜日(にちようび)の会(かい)に出(で)ています。
I attend the meeting every Sunday.
Sense 3: regular attendance
来月(らいげつ)のパーティーに出(で)る予定(よてい)ですが、時間(じかん)があるかどうか分(わ)かりません。
I plan to attend next month's party, but I don't know if I'll have time.
Sense 3: planned attendance

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 出(だ)す (transitive). Pattern: Xを出(で)る (leave X) / Xに出(で)る (attend X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 出(で)ている = 'has left' or 'is out.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家(いえ)を出(で)る (leave home - uses を)
  • 会議(かいぎ)に出(で)る (attend a meeting - uses に)
  • 結果(けっか)が出(で)る (results come out)
  • 電話(でんわ)に出(で)る (answer the phone)

PAIR: 出(だ)す (transitive) - 本(ほん)を出(だ)した (took out the book). Opposite: 入(はい)る (to enter).
