どこ

どこ
pronoun
where
1. where
A question word asking about location or place.
トイレはどこですか？
Where is the restroom?
どこに住(す)んでいますか？
Where do you live?
どこへ行(い)きますか？
Where are you going?
どこへ for direction
夏休(なつやす)みはどこかに行(い)きましたか？
Did you go anywhere during summer vacation?
どこか = somewhere
この近(ちか)くでおいしいレストランはどこにあるか知(し)っていますか？
Do you know where there's a delicious restaurant near here?

どこ is an essential question word asking about location.

こそあど SERIES (PLACE):

  • ここ: here (near speaker)
  • そこ: there (near listener)
  • あそこ: over there (far from both)
  • どこ: where (question)

PARTICLE USAGE:

  • どこに: where (location/destination with に)
  • どこへ: where to (direction with へ)
  • どこで: where (location of action with で)
  • どこから: where from (origin)

DERIVED FORMS:

  • どこか: somewhere, anywhere (indefinite)
  • どこも: everywhere, nowhere (with negative)
  • どこでも: anywhere, wherever

COMMON PHRASES:

  • どこの国(くに): which country
  • どこが痛(いた)い: where does it hurt
