ご飯(はん)

ごはん
noun
rice; meal
1. cooked rice
Steamed white rice, the staple food of Japan. Served with most Japanese meals.
2. meal, food
More broadly refers to a meal or food in general. Often used to mean breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
ご飯(はん)を食(た)べましたか。
Have you eaten (a meal)?
Common greeting to check if someone has eaten
朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)は何(なに)を食(た)べましたか。
What did you eat for breakfast?
ご飯(はん)を炊(た)きました。
I cooked rice.

ご飯(はん) has the honorific prefix ご and is the polite way to say 'rice' or 'meal'. 朝(あさ)ご飯(はん) (breakfast), 昼(ひる)ご飯(はん) (lunch), and 晩(ばん)ご飯(はん)/夜(よる)ご飯(はん) (dinner) are common compounds. 飯(めし) is the casual/masculine form.
