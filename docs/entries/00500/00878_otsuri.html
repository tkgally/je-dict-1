お釣(つ)り

おつり
noun
change (money)
1. change (money returned)
The money returned to you when you pay more than the price of something. The お is an honorific prefix that is typically included.
お釣(つ)りはいりません。
Keep the change.
Polite way to let someone keep the change
お釣(つ)りをください。
Please give me my change.
お釣(つ)りは500円(えん)です。
Your change is 500 yen.
1000円(えん)出(だ)したら、お釣(つ)りが300円(えん)でした。
When I paid with 1000 yen, the change was 300 yen.
お釣(つ)りをもらうのを忘(わす)れたので、店(みせ)に戻(もど)らなければなりませんでした。
I forgot to get my change, so I had to go back to the store.

Almost always used with the honorific お prefix. The verb もらう (to receive) or 受(う)け取(と)る (to receive) is often used with お釣(つ)り. At stores, you might hear お釣(つ)りの確認(かくにん)をお願(ねが)いします (please check your change).
