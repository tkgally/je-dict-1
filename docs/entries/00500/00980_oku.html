奥(おく)

おく
noun
interior, back
1. interior, back, depths
The innermost or furthest part of a space, away from the entrance.
部屋(へや)の奥(おく)にある。
It's at the back of the room.
店(みせ)の奥(おく)にトイレがあります。
The restroom is at the back of the store.
もう少(すこ)し奥(おく)に進(すす)んでください。
Please go a little further back.
森(もり)の奥(おく)に湖(みずうみ)がありました。とても静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)でした。
There was a lake deep in the forest. It was a very quiet place.
引(ひ)き出(だ)しの奥(おく)を探(さが)したら、昔(むかし)の写真(しゃしん)が出(で)てきた。懐(なつ)かしい思(おも)い出(で)がよみがえった。
When I searched the back of the drawer, old photos came out. Nostalgic memories came back to me.

奥(おく) is the deep interior of a space — the part furthest from the entrance, or from where the speaker stands. It is a relative position word, so it almost always appears as Xの奥(おく) (the back of X) or as a destination with に or へ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)の奥(おく) (the back of the room)
  • 山(やま)の奥(おく) (deep in the mountains)
  • 心(こころ)の奥(おく) (the depths of one's heart)
  • 奥(おく)へ進(すす)む (to move further in)

CONTRAST: 手前(てまえ) is the near side, closest to the viewer; 奥(おく) is the far side. In a shop or a photograph the two are often paired to describe where something is.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 奥(おく)さん is a polite word for another person's wife. The connection is historical: the wife of the house was the person in its inner rooms.
  • 奥深(おくぶか)い (deep, profound) applies the same image to ideas and skills.

REGISTER: Neutral, common in everyday directions and descriptions.

Related Words

Antonym: 手前(てまえ) (this side)
