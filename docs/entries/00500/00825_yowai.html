弱(よわ)い

よわい
adjective (i-adjective)
weak
1. weak, feeble
Lacking physical strength or power.
2. not good at, susceptible to
Being vulnerable to or not skilled at something.
体(からだ)が弱(よわ)いです。
I have a weak body / I'm not very healthy.
お酒(さけ)に弱(よわ)いです。
I can't hold my alcohol well.
A common expression
暑(あつ)さに弱(よわ)いです。
I can't handle the heat well.

The opposite is 強(つよ)い (strong). ～に弱(よわ)い means 'weak against/susceptible to' or 'not good with'. 弱(よわ)くなる means 'to become weak'. 弱点(じゃくてん) means 'weak point/weakness'.
