弱(よわ)い

よわい
adjective (i-adjective)
weak
1. weak, feeble
Lacking physical strength or power.
体(からだ)が弱(よわ)いです。
I have a weak body / I'm not very healthy.
赤(あか)ちゃんは体(からだ)が弱(よわ)いので、気(き)をつけてください。
Babies have weak bodies, so please be careful.
風(かぜ)が弱(よわ)くなってきた。
The wind has gotten weaker.
Sense 1: weak (force)
電波(でんぱ)が弱(よわ)い。
The signal is weak.
Sense 1: weak
弱(よわ)い火(ひ)で煮(に)てください。
Please simmer over low heat.
Sense 1: weak/low
2. not good at, susceptible to
Being vulnerable to or not skilled at something.
お酒(さけ)に弱(よわ)いです。
I can't hold my alcohol well.
A common expression
暑(あつ)さに弱(よわ)いです。
I can't handle the heat well.
私(わたし)は朝(あさ)に弱(よわ)いです。毎朝(まいあさ)起(お)きるのが大変(たいへん)です。
I'm not a morning person. It's hard to get up every morning.
数学(すうがく)に弱(よわ)い。
I'm not good at math.
Sense 2: not good at
寒(さむ)さに弱(よわ)いので、冬(ふゆ)が苦手(にがて)だ。
I can't handle the cold, so I don't like winter.
Sense 2: susceptible to

弱(よわ)い is an i-adjective meaning weak or not good at something.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 弱(よわ)く
  • Noun form: 弱(よわ)さ (weakness)
  • Negative: 弱(よわ)くない

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に弱(よわ)い: weak against ~, susceptible to ~, not good with ~
  • お酒(さけ)に弱(よわ)い: can't hold alcohol well
  • 朝(あさ)に弱(よわ)い: not a morning person
  • 暑(あつ)さに弱(よわ)い: can't handle heat

COMPOUNDS:

  • 弱点(じゃくてん): weak point, weakness
  • 弱気(よわき): timidity, bearish (stock market)
  • 弱虫(よわむし): coward, weakling

OPPOSITE:
強(つよ)い: strong
