そこ

そこ
pronoun
there
1. there (near you)
A demonstrative pronoun for a place near the listener.
そこに何(なに)がありますか。
What is there?
そこで待(ま)っていてください。
Please wait there.
そこを右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がってください。
Please turn right there.
Giving directions

そこ refers to a place near the listener, contrasting with ここ (here, near speaker) and あそこ (over there). It's commonly used when giving directions or referring to a location in the listener's vicinity. そこで means 'there/at that place,' while そこに indicates direction toward that place.
