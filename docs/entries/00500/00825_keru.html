蹴(け)る

ける
verb (godan)
to kick

Definitions

1. to kick
To strike with the foot.
2. to refuse, to reject
To turn down an offer (figurative).

Examples

ボールを蹴(け)る。
To kick a ball.
犬(いぬ)を蹴(け)らないで。
Don't kick the dog.
申(もう)し出(で)を蹴(け)った。
I rejected the offer.

Notes

蹴(け)る means to kick.

COMMON USES:

  • ボールを蹴(け)る: to kick a ball
  • 蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす: to kick away

FIGURATIVE:

  • 申(もう)し出(で)を蹴(け)る: to reject an offer
  • 誘(さそ)いを蹴(け)る: to turn down an invitation
