六(ろく)

ろく
number
six
1. six
The number six. A straightforward number in Japanese with consistent pronunciation in most contexts.
六(ろく)時(じ)に起(お)きます。
I wake up at six o'clock.
ビールを六(ろっ)本(ぽん)ください。
Six beers, please.
Note: ろく becomes ろっ before ほん→ぽん
六(ろく)月(がつ)は梅雨(つゆ)の季節(きせつ)です。
June is the rainy season.
本(ほん)が六(ろく)冊(さつ)あります。全部(ぜんぶ)読(よ)みたいです。
There are six books. I want to read all of them.
毎日(まいにち)六(ろく)時間(じかん)勉強(べんきょう)しています。とても大変(たいへん)です。
I study for six hours every day. It's very tough.

The native Japanese counter is 六(むっ)つ (muttsu). When combined with counters starting with h-sounds, ろく often becomes ろっ and causes sound changes:

  • 六(ろっ)本(ぽん) (roppon)
  • 六(ろっ)百(ぴゃく) (roppyaku, 600). 六(ろく)時(じ) (rokuji) stays as ろく.
