遅(おそ)い

おそい
adjective (i-adjective)
slow, late
1. slow
Describing something that moves or progresses at a low speed. The opposite of 速(はや)い (hayai, fast).
このバスは遅(おそ)いですね。
This bus is slow, isn't it?
Sense 1: slow speed
歩(ある)くのが遅(おそ)いです。
I walk slowly.
Sense 1: slow pace
このパソコンは遅(おそ)いので、新(あたら)しいのが欲(ほ)しいです。
This computer is slow, so I want a new one.
Sense 1: slow speed
もう少(すこ)し遅(おそ)く話(はな)してください。
Please speak a little more slowly.
Sense 1: slow speech
食(た)べるのが遅(おそ)いので、いつも最後(さいご)まで食(た)べています。
I eat slowly, so I'm always the last one eating.
Sense 1: slow eating
2. late
Describing something that happens after the expected or scheduled time. Also used for late hours of the day/night.
遅(おそ)くなってすみません。
Sorry for being late.
Sense 2: late arrival
夜(よる)遅(おそ)くまで勉強(べんきょう)しました。
I studied until late at night.
Sense 2: late hours
返事(へんじ)が遅(おそ)くなってすみません。
Sorry for the late reply.
Sense 2: late response
遅(おそ)い時間(じかん)に電話(でんわ)してすみません。
Sorry for calling at a late hour.
Sense 2: late time
今日(きょう)は帰(かえ)りが遅(おそ)くなります。
I'll be home late today.
Sense 2: late return

遅(おそ)い is an i-adjective with two main meanings: slow (speed) and late (time).

FORMS:

  • Negative: 遅(おそ)くない
  • Past: 遅(おそ)かった
  • Adverb: 遅(おそ)く (slowly; late)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 遅(おそ)くなる: to become late, to be delayed
  • 遅(おそ)くても: at the latest
  • 夜(よる)遅(おそ)く: late at night
  • 遅(おそ)くまで: until late

ANTONYMS:

  • 速(はや)い: fast (speed)
  • 早(はや)い: early (time)

Note: For speed, the opposite is 速(はや)い. For time (late/early), the opposite is 早(はや)い. Both are read はやい but use different kanji.
