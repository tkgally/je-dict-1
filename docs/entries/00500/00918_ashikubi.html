足首(あしくび)

あしくび
noun
ankle

Definitions

1. ankle
The joint connecting the foot to the leg. Literally 'foot neck.'

Examples

足首(あしくび)を捻(ひね)ってしまいました。
I twisted my ankle.
足首(あしくび)が腫(は)れています。
My ankle is swollen.
足首(あしくび)を回(まわ)す運動(うんどう)をしましょう。
Let's do exercises rotating our ankles.

Notes

足首(あしくび) literally means 'foot-neck,' following the same pattern as 手首(てくび) (wrist, 'hand-neck'). Ankle injuries (足首(あしくび)の怪我(けが)) are common in sports. The word {くるぶし} is also used for the ankle bone specifically.
