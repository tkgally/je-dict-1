難(むずか)しい

むずかしい
adjective (i-adjective)
difficult, hard
1. difficult, hard
Requiring great effort, skill, or intelligence. The opposite of 易(やさ)しい (easy).
日本語(にほんご)は難(むずか)しいですか？
Is Japanese difficult?
この問題(もんだい)は難(むずか)しすぎます。
This problem is too difficult.
〜すぎる = too ~
難(むずか)しい漢字(かんじ)が読(よ)めません。
I can't read difficult kanji.

An i-adjective. Note the spelling: むずかしい (not むつかしい). The opposite is 易(やさ)しい or 簡単(かんたん)な. Can describe tasks, concepts, people (hard to please), and situations.
