電車(でんしゃ)

でんしゃ
noun
train, electric train
1. train, electric train
A railway vehicle powered by electricity, used for public transportation. The most common word for train in everyday Japanese.
電車(でんしゃ)で会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)きます。
I go to work by train.
電車で = by train
電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れています。
The train is delayed.
次(つぎ)の電車(でんしゃ)は何時(なんじ)ですか？
What time is the next train?
朝(あさ)の電車(でんしゃ)は人(ひと)が多(おお)いです。
Morning trains are crowded.
電車(でんしゃ)を降(お)りてから、歩(ある)いて十分(じっぷん)ぐらいです。
After getting off the train, it's about a 10-minute walk.

電車(でんしゃ) specifically means electric train, the most common type of train in urban Japan.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)る: to get on/ride a train
  • 電車(でんしゃ)を降(お)りる: to get off a train
  • 電車(でんしゃ)で行(い)く: to go by train
  • 終電(しゅうでん): last train
  • 始発(しはつ)電車(でんしゃ): first train

RELATED WORDS:

  • 列車(れっしゃ): train (generic/formal term)
  • 新幹線(しんかんせん): bullet train (shinkansen)
  • 地下鉄(ちかてつ): subway
  • 汽車(きしゃ): steam train (old/rural)

CULTURAL NOTE:
満員(まんいん)電車(でんしゃ) (packed train) is common during rush hour. Train punctuality in Japan is famous worldwide.
