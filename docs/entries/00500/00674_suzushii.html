涼(すず)しい

すずしい
adjective (i-adjective)
cool, refreshing
1. cool, refreshing
Describes pleasantly cool weather or temperature, especially refreshing coolness in summer or after hot weather.
今日(きょう)は涼(すず)しいですね。
It's cool today, isn't it?
秋(あき)は涼(すず)しくなります。
It gets cool in autumn.
涼(すず)しい風(かぜ)が気持(きも)ちいい。
The cool breeze feels good.

涼しい describes pleasant, refreshing coolness—the opposite of 暑い (atsui, hot). It has a positive connotation of comfort, unlike 寒い (samui, cold) which implies discomfort. Conjugation: 涼しくない (negative), 涼しかった (past).
