元気(げんき)

げんき
adjective (na-adjective), noun
healthy, energetic, well
1. healthy, well, fine
Describes a state of good health and vitality. Commonly used in greetings to ask how someone is doing.
2. energetic, lively, spirited
Describes someone who is full of energy and vigor.
お元気(げんき)ですか。
How are you? (Are you well?)
Common greeting
はい、元気(げんき)です。
Yes, I'm fine.
Standard response to お元気ですか
元気(げんき)な子供(こども)ですね。
What an energetic child!
na-adjective form

One of the most important words for basic conversation. お元気(げんき)ですか is the standard 'How are you?' greeting. Can be used as both a na-adjective and a noun. When someone looks down, you might encourage them with 元気(げんき)を出(だ)して (cheer up!).
