上手(じょうず)

じょうず
adjective (na-adjective), noun
skillful, good at
1. skillful, good at
Describes someone who has a high level of skill or ability in something. Often used to compliment someone's ability in language, sports, cooking, etc.
日本語(にほんご)が上手(じょうず)ですね。
You're good at Japanese!
Common compliment to language learners
彼(かれ)は歌(うた)が上手(じょうず)です。
He's good at singing.
上手(じょうず)に話(はな)せるようになりたい。
I want to be able to speak well.
Using 上手に as an adverb
妹(いもうと)は絵(え)を描(か)くのが上手(じょうず)です。
My younger sister is good at drawing pictures.
あの先生(せんせい)は説明(せつめい)が上手(じょうず)なので、学生(がくせい)はみんな分(わ)かりやすいと言(い)います。
That teacher is good at explaining, so all the students say it's easy to understand.

The opposite is 下手(へた) (unskillful, bad at). Note the grammar pattern: ～が上手(じょうず)です (good at ~). When used before a noun, it becomes 上手(じょうず)な: 上手(じょうず)な運転(うんてん) (skillful driving). The adverb form is 上手(じょうず)に (skillfully).
