1. that (over there)
A demonstrative pronoun referring to something far from both the speaker and listener.
あれは何(なん)ですか。
What is that over there?
あれを見(み)てください。
Please look at that (over there).
あれは富士山(ふじさん)ですか。
Is that Mt. Fuji (over there)?
昨日(きのう)話(はな)していたあれ、どうなった？
What happened with that thing we were talking about yesterday?
Shared knowledge usage
あれが終(お)わったら電話(でんわ)してください。私(わたし)はここで待(ま)っていますから。
Please call me when that is done. I'll be waiting here.

あれ is a demonstrative pronoun for things far from both the speaker and listener. Part of the ko-so-a-do system.

DEMONSTRATIVE SERIES (things):

  • これ: this (near speaker)
  • それ: that (near listener)
  • あれ: that (far from both)
  • どれ: which one (question)

SHARED KNOWLEDGE USE:
あれ can also refer to something both speaker and listener know about, even if not physically present: あれ、どうなった？ (What happened with that thing?)

MODIFYING NOUNS:
To modify a noun, use あの instead: あの本(ほん) (that book over there), not あれ本(ほん).
