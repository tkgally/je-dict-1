祖母(そぼ)

そぼ
noun
grandmother (humble)
1. grandmother (humble/formal)
The humble or formal term for one's own grandmother. Used when speaking about your grandmother to others in polite contexts.
祖母(そぼ)は元気(げんき)です。
My grandmother is well.
祖母(そぼ)は今年(ことし)八十歳(はちじゅっさい)になります。
My grandmother will turn 80 this year.
毎週(まいしゅう)祖母(そぼ)に電話(でんわ)します。
I call my grandmother every week.
祖母(そぼ)は遠(とお)くに住(す)んでいますので、夏休(なつやす)みによく会(あ)いに行(い)きます。
My grandmother lives far away, so I often go to see her during summer vacation.
私(わたし)の祖母(そぼ)は若(わか)い時(とき)、先生(せんせい)でした。今(いま)でも本(ほん)を読(よ)むのが好(す)きです。
My grandmother was a teacher when she was young. She still likes reading books.

祖母(そぼ) is the humble/formal term used when referring to your own grandmother in polite speech. In casual speech or when speaking directly to your grandmother, おばあさん or おばあちゃん is used. When referring to someone else's grandmother politely, use おばあさん or おばあさま.
