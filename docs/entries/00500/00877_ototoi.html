おととい

おととい
noun
day before yesterday
1. day before yesterday
The day that was two days ago. Part of the sequence: おととい (day before yesterday) → きのう (yesterday) → きょう (today).
おととい東京(とうきょう)に行(い)きました。
I went to Tokyo the day before yesterday.
おとといの夜(よる)、雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りました。
It rained the night before last.
おとといから風邪(かぜ)です。
I've had a cold since the day before yesterday.

Usually written in hiragana. Can also be written as 一昨日(おととい) in kanji. Sometimes pronounced as 'ototsui' in casual speech. Like other time words, it can be used without a particle when indicating when something happened.
