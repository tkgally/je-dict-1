コーヒー

こーひー
noun
coffee
1. coffee
The beverage made from roasted coffee beans. Very popular in Japan both hot and iced.
コーヒーを飲(の)みませんか。
Would you like to drink some coffee?
ブラックコーヒーをください。
Black coffee, please.
毎朝(まいあさ)コーヒーを飲(の)みます。
I drink coffee every morning.
コーヒーは熱(あつ)いのと冷(つめ)たいのとどちらがいいですか。
Which do you prefer, hot or cold coffee?
友達(ともだち)と一緒(いっしょ)にコーヒーを飲(の)みながら話(はな)すのが好(す)きです。
I like drinking coffee and chatting with friends.

Japan has a strong coffee culture with many 喫茶店 (kissaten, traditional coffee shops) and modern cafes. Common variations: ブラックコーヒー (black coffee), アイスコーヒー (iced coffee), カフェオレ (cafe au lait). Canned coffee from vending machines is also very popular.
