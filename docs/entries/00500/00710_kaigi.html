会議(かいぎ)

かいぎ
noun, verb (suru)
meeting, conference
1. meeting, conference
A gathering of people to discuss matters, make decisions, or exchange information. Common in business and organizational settings.
三(さん)時(じ)から会議(かいぎ)があります。
There's a meeting from 3 o'clock.
会議(かいぎ)に出席(しゅっせき)してください。
Please attend the meeting.
会議(かいぎ)は何時(なんじ)に終(お)わりますか。
What time does the meeting end?

Can be used with する: 会議(かいぎ)する (to hold a meeting).

Related words: 会議室(かいぎしつ) (meeting room/conference room). Often used in business Japanese: 会議(かいぎ)に出(で)る (attend a meeting), 会議(かいぎ)を開(ひら)く (hold a meeting).
