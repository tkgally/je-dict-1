十五(じゅうご)

じゅうご
number
fifteen

Definitions

1. fifteen
The number fifteen. Formed by combining 十(じゅう) (ten) and 五(ご) (five).

Examples

十五(じゅうご)分(ふん)待(ま)ってください。
Please wait fifteen minutes.
今日(きょう)は十五(じゅうご)日(にち)です。
Today is the fifteenth.
十五夜(じゅうごや)の月(つき)を見(み)ました。
I saw the full moon on the fifteenth night.
Traditional moon-viewing

Notes

十五夜(じゅうごや) (the fifteenth night) is culturally significant in Japan as it refers to the night of the full moon, particularly the harvest moon in autumn when people traditionally view the moon and eat rice dumplings.
