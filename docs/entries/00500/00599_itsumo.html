いつも

いつも
adverb
always

Definitions

1. always
At all times; on every occasion.
2. usually
In the typical or habitual way.

Examples

いつも朝(あさ)ごはんを食(た)べます。
I always eat breakfast.
いつもありがとうございます。
Thank you as always.
Common polite phrase
いつもの電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)ります。
I take the usual train.
いつもの = the usual

Notes

いつもの + noun = 'the usual'. Related time expressions: 時々(ときどき) (sometimes), たまに (occasionally).
