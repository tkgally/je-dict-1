いつも

いつも
adverb
always
1. always
At all times; on every occasion.
いつも元気(げんき)です。
I'm always well.
いつもありがとうございます。
Thank you as always.
Common polite phrase
母(はは)はいつも忙(いそが)しいです。
My mother is always busy.
いつも笑顔(えがお)でいてください。
Please always be smiling.
いつも同(おな)じことを言(い)っていますね。もう何度(なんど)も聞(き)きましたよ。
You always say the same thing. I've heard it many times already.
2. usually
In the typical or habitual way.
いつもの電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)る。
To take the usual train.
いつもの = the usual
いつも朝(あさ)ごはんを食(た)べます。
I usually eat breakfast.
いつも七時(しちじ)に起(お)きます。
I usually wake up at 7 o'clock.
いつもの席(せき)に座(すわ)ります。
I sit in my usual seat.
いつもの = the usual
いつもは電車(でんしゃ)で通(かよ)いますが、今日(きょう)は車(くるま)で来(き)ました。電車(でんしゃ)が止(と)まっていたからです。
I usually commute by train, but today I came by car. The trains were stopped.
いつもは = usually (with contrast)

いつも means "always" or "usually" depending on context.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • いつも + verb: always/usually do
  • いつもの + noun: the usual (noun)
  • いつもは: usually (with implied contrast)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • いつもありがとうございます: thank you as always
  • いつもの通(とお)り: as usual
  • いつもと違(ちが)う: different from usual

FREQUENCY SCALE:

  • いつも: always, usually (highest frequency)
  • よく: often
  • 時々(ときどき): sometimes
  • たまに: occasionally
  • あまり〜ない: not often
  • めったに〜ない: rarely
  • 全然(ぜんぜん)〜ない: never

NOTE:
いつも can mean both "always" (100%) and "usually" (habitually) - context determines the meaning.

Related Words

Contrast: 時々(ときどき) (sometimes)
Synonym: 始終(しじゅう) (constantly (slightly negative nuance))
Related: 毎度(まいど) (each time (also business greeting))
Contrast: ずっと (continuously; much more)
