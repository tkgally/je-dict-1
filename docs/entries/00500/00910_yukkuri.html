ゆっくり

ゆっくり
adverb
slowly, leisurely
1. slowly, at a slow pace
Not quickly. Taking one's time to do something.
ゆっくり話(はな)してください。
Please speak slowly.
ゆっくり歩(ある)こう。
Let's walk slowly.
ゆっくり食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat slowly.
もう少(すこ)しゆっくり読(よ)んでもらえますか。分(わ)かりませんでした。
Could you read a bit more slowly? I didn't understand.
道(みち)が暗(くら)いから、ゆっくり運転(うんてん)してください。急(いそ)がなくてもいいです。
The road is dark, so please drive slowly. You don't need to hurry.
2. leisurely, relaxed
In a relaxed manner without hurrying.
ゆっくり休(やす)んでください。
Please take it easy and rest.
ゆっくりしてね。
Take your time. / Relax.
週末(しゅうまつ)はゆっくりできた?
Were you able to relax on the weekend?
旅行(りょこう)でゆっくりしたいから、予定(よてい)は少(すく)なくしました。
I want to relax on the trip, so I reduced my plans.
今日(きょう)は仕事(しごと)が休(やす)みなので、家(いえ)でゆっくり本(ほん)を読(よ)むつもりです。
I have the day off work today, so I plan to relax at home and read a book.

ゆっくり is an adverb meaning "slowly" or "leisurely." Always written in hiragana.

MEANINGS:
1. Slowly (speed): ゆっくり話(はな)してください (Please speak slowly)
2. Leisurely (manner): ゆっくり休(やす)んでください (Please take it easy)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ゆっくりしてね: Take your time / Relax
  • ゆっくり休(やす)んでください: Please rest well (well-wishing)
  • ゆっくりできる: to be able to relax

GRAMMAR:
Can function as na-adjective: ゆっくりな時間(じかん) (leisurely time)

OPPOSITE:

  • 急(いそ)いで: hurriedly, quickly
  • 速(はや)く: fast, quickly
