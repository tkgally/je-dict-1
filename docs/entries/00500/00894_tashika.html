確(たし)か

たしか
adverb
certainly, if I remember correctly
1. certainly, surely
Expressing confidence that something is true or will happen.
2. if I remember correctly
Used when recalling something but not being 100% certain. Often at the beginning of a sentence.
確(たし)か明日(あした)は日曜日(にちようび)です。
If I remember correctly, tomorrow is Sunday.
Sense 2: uncertain recall
確(たし)かに聞(き)きました。
I certainly heard it.
Sense 1: with に for emphasis
確(たし)か彼女(かのじょ)は大阪(おおさか)から来(き)ました。
I believe she came from Osaka.

確(たし)かに (with に) emphasizes certainty more strongly. Without に, it often expresses uncertain recall. Also used as a na-adjective meaning 'certain/reliable': 確(たし)かな情報(じょうほう) (reliable information).
