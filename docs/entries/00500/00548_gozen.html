午前(ごぜん)

ごぜん
noun
morning, AM
1. morning, AM
The time from midnight until noon, or the AM hours when telling time.
午前(ごぜん)10時(じ)に来(き)てください。
Please come at 10 AM.
午前中(ごぜんちゅう)は忙(いそが)しいです。
I'm busy in the morning.
午前中 = during the morning
試験(しけん)は午前(ごぜん)9時(じ)からです。
The exam is from 9 AM.

Used before times to indicate AM: 午前(ごぜん)9時(じ) (9 AM). 午前中(ごぜんちゅう) means 'during the morning'. Opposite: 午後(ごご) (PM/afternoon).
