1. to take (a photo), to shoot (video)
To capture an image or video using a camera. Different kanji from 取(と)る (to take/grab).
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)りましょう。
Let's take a photo.
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)ってもいいですか。
May I take a photo?
いい写真(しゃしん)が撮(と)れました。
I was able to take a good photo.
山(やま)の上(うえ)から町(まち)の写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)りました。とてもきれいでした。
I took a photo of the town from the top of the mountain. It was very beautiful.
毎日(まいにち)朝(あさ)ごはんの写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)って、友達(ともだち)に送(おく)っています。楽(たの)しいです。
I take a photo of my breakfast every day and send it to my friend. It's fun.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを撮(と)る (take X [photo/video]).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 撮(と)っている = 'is taking photos/recording.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る (take a photo)
  • ビデオを撮(と)る (record a video)
  • 動画(どうが)を撮(と)る (shoot a video clip)
  • 撮(と)ってもいいですか (may I take a photo?)
  • 自撮(じど)りする (take a selfie)

ASKING SOMEONE TO TAKE A PHOTO: the て-form plus a giving-and-receiving verb is the standard way to handle this. 写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)ってもらえますか = 'could I have you take a photo?' (asking a stranger), and 撮(と)ってあげましょうか = 'shall I take one for you?' (offering).

POTENTIAL FORM: 撮(と)れる means 'can take' or 'comes out well', and what is photographed is marked with が rather than を: いい写真(しゃしん)が撮(と)れた = 'a good photo came out'.

Specifically for photography and video. Different from 取(と)る (general take/grab), which is a separate entry with the same reading.

Related: 撮影(さつえい) (filming/photography).
