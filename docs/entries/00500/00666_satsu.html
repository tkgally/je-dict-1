〜冊(さつ)

さつ
counter
counter for books
1. counter for books, volumes
Used to count bound publications such as books, magazines, notebooks, and other bound volumes.
本(ほん)を三冊(さんさつ)買(か)いました。
I bought three books.
本(ほん)が何冊(なんさつ)ありますか。
How many books are there?
一冊(いっさつ)ください。
One (book), please.
図書館(としょかん)で五冊(ごさつ)借(か)りることができます。
You can borrow five books at the library.
毎月(まいつき)二冊(にさつ)ぐらい本(ほん)を読(よ)むようにしています。
I try to read about two books every month.

〜冊(さつ) is the counter for bound volumes like books, magazines, and notebooks.

WHAT IT COUNTS:

  • 本(ほん): books
  • 雑誌(ざっし): magazines
  • ノート: notebooks
  • 辞書(じしょ): dictionaries

COUNTING FORMS:

  • 一冊(いっさつ): 1 book (sound change: っさつ)
  • 二冊(にさつ): 2 books
  • 三冊(さんさつ): 3 books
  • 四冊(よんさつ): 4 books
  • 五冊(ごさつ): 5 books
  • 六冊(ろくさつ): 6 books
  • 七冊(ななさつ): 7 books
  • 八冊(はっさつ): 8 books (sound change: っさつ)
  • 九冊(きゅうさつ): 9 books
  • 十冊(じゅっさつ): 10 books (sound change: っさつ)
  • 何冊(なんさつ): how many books?

十冊(じゅっさつ) is also read 十冊(じっさつ); both are correct, and じゅっさつ is the more common spoken form.

CONTRASTS:

  • 〜本(ほん) counts long cylindrical objects. The trap is the word 本(ほん) itself: a book is counted with 〜冊(さつ), never with 〜本(ほん), even though the two are written with the same character.
  • 〜枚(まい) counts the paper before it is bound. Loose printouts are 三枚(さんまい); the same pages stapled into a booklet become 一冊(いっさつ).
  • What matters is binding, not content or thickness: even a thin 雑誌(ざっし) is 一冊(いっさつ), while a 新聞(しんぶん), which is only folded, is counted with 〜部(ぶ) instead.
