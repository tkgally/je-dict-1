1. far, distant
Located at a great distance. Used for physical distance or metaphorical distance (like distant memories).
駅(えき)から遠(とお)いですか？
Is it far from the station?
彼(かれ)の家(いえ)は遠(とお)くにあります。
His house is far away.
遠(とお)く = far away (adverb/noun)
それは遠(とお)い昔(むかし)のことです。
That was a long time ago (in the distant past).
Metaphorical: distant in time
学校(がっこう)が遠(とお)いので、バスで行(い)きます。
The school is far, so I go by bus.
遠(とお)くに山(やま)が見(み)えます。
I can see mountains in the distance.
遠(とお)く = far away (used as noun)

遠(とお)い is an i-adjective meaning "far" or "distant," used for both physical and metaphorical distance.

FORMS:

  • 遠(とお)い: far (adjective)
  • 遠(とお)く: far away (adverb/noun)
  • 遠(とお)くに: in the distance
  • 遠(とお)くから: from far away

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜から遠(とお)い: far from ~
  • 遠(とお)い昔(むかし): distant past, long ago
  • 遠(とお)い親戚(しんせき): distant relative
  • 遠(とお)く離(はな)れた: far apart

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 遠(とお)くに見(み)える: can see in the distance
  • 遠(とお)いところから: from a far place
  • 耳(みみ)が遠(とお)い: hard of hearing (fixed idiom; the polite way to say someone cannot hear well)

OPPOSITE:
近(ちか)い: near, close

Related Words

Antonym: 近(ちか)い (near, close)
Related: 遠(とお)く (far away, the distance (noun/adverb form))
Related: 遠(とお)ざかる (to move away, to recede)
