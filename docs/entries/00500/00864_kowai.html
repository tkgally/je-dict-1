怖(こわ)い

1. scary, frightening
Describes something that causes fear or is frightening. Can also describe the feeling of being scared.
その映画(えいが)は怖(こわ)かったです。
That movie was scary.
暗(くら)いところが怖(こわ)いです。
I'm scared of dark places.
Expressing fear of something
怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ)を見(み)ました。
I had a scary dream.
犬(いぬ)が怖(こわ)くて、近(ちか)づけません。
I'm scared of dogs, so I can't go near them.
子供(こども)の時(とき)は夜(よる)一人(ひとり)で外(そと)に出(で)るのが怖(こわ)かったです。
When I was a child, I was scared of going outside alone at night.

怖(こわ)い covers both "scary" (describing what causes fear) and "scared" (describing the feeling); context decides which. The feared thing is marked with が: 犬(いぬ)が怖(こわ)い ("I'm afraid of dogs").

USAGE:

  • Use it of frightening things (a movie, a dog, the dark) and of one's own fear alike.
  • For someone else's fear as an ongoing action, switch to the verb 怖(こわ)がる ("to be/act afraid"), since a plain adjective most naturally describes the speaker's own feeling.

REGISTER:
Neutral and everyday. The slangy ビビる ("to be freaked out") is casual only.

FORMS:

  • 怖(こわ)くて: "scary and…" or "because it's scary"
  • 怖(こわ)くない: "not scary"
  • 怖(こわ)かった: "was scary"

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 怖(こわ)い話(はなし): scary story, ghost story
  • 怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ): nightmare
  • 怖(こわ)くて仕方(しかた)がない: to be terrified
  • 怖(こわ)いもの知(し)らず: fearless

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES:

  • 恐(おそ)ろしい: terrifying (stronger, more literary)
  • 気味(きみ)が悪(わる)い: creepy, eerie

Related Words

Synonym: 恐(おそ)ろしい (terrifying (stronger))
