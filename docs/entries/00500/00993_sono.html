その

その
pre-noun adjectival
that (before noun)
1. that (modifying a noun)
A demonstrative adjective used before nouns to indicate something close to the listener or previously mentioned.
その本(ほん)を貸(か)してください。
Please lend me that book.
その話(はなし)は本当(ほんとう)ですか。
Is that story true?
Referring to something mentioned
その時(とき)、電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)りました。
At that time, the phone rang.

その modifies nouns and must be followed by a noun. It refers to things near the listener or to previously mentioned information. その時(とき) (at that time), その後(あと) (after that), and その他(ほか) (others, the rest) are common expressions.
