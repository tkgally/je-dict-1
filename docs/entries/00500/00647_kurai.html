暗(くら)い

くらい
adjective (i-adjective)
dark, gloomy
1. dark
Describes a place or thing that lacks light, is dimly lit, or dark in color.
2. gloomy, depressed
Describes a gloomy mood or depressed personality.
この部屋(へや)は暗(くら)いです。
This room is dark.
外(そと)が暗(くら)くなりました。
It got dark outside.
暗(くら)い顔(かお)をしています。
You look gloomy.
Figurative meaning

暗い describes both physical darkness and gloomy personalities/moods. The opposite is 明るい (akarui, bright).

Note: Don't confuse with the particle くらい/ぐらい meaning 'about/approximately.' Conjugation: 暗くない (negative), 暗かった (past).
