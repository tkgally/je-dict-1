毎年(まいとし)

まいとし
noun, adverb
every year
1. every year
Each and every year; annually. Used to express something that happens on a yearly basis. Functions as both a noun and an adverb.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)に海(うみ)へ行(い)きます。
I go to the beach every summer.
毎年(まいとし)何(なに)をしますか。
What do you do every year?
毎年(まいとし)正月(しょうがつ)には家族(かぞく)に会(あ)います。
I see my family every New Year.
毎年(まいとし)十(じゅう)二(に)月(がつ)になると、来年(らいねん)のことを考(かんが)えます。
Every year when December comes, I think about the coming year.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)になると家族(かぞく)と海(うみ)に行(い)きます。子供(こども)たちはこの旅行(りょこう)をとても楽(たの)しみにしています。
Every summer we go to the beach with my family. The children really look forward to this trip.

毎年(まいとし) means "every year" and functions as both a noun and an adverb.

READINGS:
Can also be read まいねん. Both readings are correct; まいねん is slightly more formal and is the usual reading in news writing and announcements.

PATTERN:

The prefix まい- ("every") attaches to a time period to form frequency words:

  • 毎日(まいにち): every day
  • 毎週(まいしゅう): every week
  • 毎月(まいつき): every month
  • 毎年(まいとし): every year

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 毎年(まいとし)〜に: every year in ~
  • 毎年(まいとし)〜になると: every year when ~ comes around
  • 毎年(まいとし)恒例(こうれい)の: annual, customary (of an event held year after year)

RELATED TIME WORDS:

  • 去年(きょねん): last year
  • 今年(ことし): this year
  • 来年(らいねん): next year
  • 年々(ねんねん): year by year
