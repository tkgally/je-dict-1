悪(わる)い

わるい
adjective (i-adjective)
bad

Definitions

1. bad, poor
Of low quality, not good, or unsatisfactory.
2. sorry
Used colloquially to apologize or express regret.

Examples

天気(てんき)が悪(わる)いです。
The weather is bad.
Sense 1
悪(わる)い、遅(おく)れた。
Sorry, I'm late.
Sense 2: casual apology
体(からだ)の調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)いです。
I'm not feeling well.
Common health expression

Notes

Opposite of いい/よい (good). 悪(わる)い is one of the most common adjectives. As a casual apology, 悪(わる)い or 悪(わる)いね is like saying 'my bad' or 'sorry about that'. More formal apology: すみません or ごめんなさい.
