1. bad, poor
Of low quality, not good, or unsatisfactory.
天気(てんき)が悪(わる)いです。
The weather is bad.
目(め)が悪(わる)いです。
I have bad eyesight.
体(からだ)の調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)いです。
I'm not feeling well.
成績(せいせき)が悪(わる)くて、先生(せんせい)に注意(ちゅうい)されました。
My grades were bad, and the teacher scolded me.
この道(みち)は悪(わる)いので、ゆっくり運転(うんてん)してください。穴(あな)がたくさんあります。
This road is bad, so please drive slowly. There are many holes.

悪(わる)い is the general adjective of negative evaluation, the opposite of いい/よい (good). It covers poor quality, poor condition, and moral wrongness, and in casual speech it doubles as an admission of fault ('sorry').

USAGE:

  • As a predicate, what is bad is marked with が: 天気(てんき)が悪(わる)い (the weather is bad).
  • Before a noun, 悪(わる)い usually carries the moral sense: 悪(わる)い人(ひと) (a bad person), 悪(わる)いこと (a bad deed, wrongdoing).
  • 悪(わる)くない is literally 'not bad,' but in conversation it is mild praise, closer to 'pretty good' than to 'not good.'
  • 気(き)を悪(わる)くする means 'take offense,' not 'make something worse.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天気(てんき)が悪(わる)い: bad weather
  • 目(め)が悪(わる)い: poor eyesight
  • 調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)い: not feeling well / not working properly
  • 具合(ぐあい)が悪(わる)い: in poor condition (health or machinery)
  • 都合(つごう)が悪(わる)い: inconvenient, a bad time
  • 頭(あたま)が悪(わる)い: not smart (can be offensive)
  • 仲(なか)が悪(わる)い: on bad terms (relationship)

AS APOLOGY (casual):

In this use 悪(わる)い means 'I am in the wrong,' not 'bad in quality,' so it admits fault rather than describing something.

  • 悪(わる)い!: Sorry! My bad!
  • 悪(わる)いね: Sorry about that
  • 悪(わる)いけど...: Sorry, but...

These are casual and are used mainly by men and among close friends. For polite apologies, use すみません or ごめんなさい.
