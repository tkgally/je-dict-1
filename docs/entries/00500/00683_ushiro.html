後(うし)ろ

うしろ
noun
behind, back
1. behind, back, rear
The position at the back of someone or something; the opposite of the front.
後(うし)ろを見(み)てください。
Please look behind you.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の後(うし)ろにいます。
He is behind me.
後(うし)ろの席(せき)に座(すわ)ります。
I'll sit in the back seat.

後(うし)ろ indicates position at the back or rear of something. The opposite is 前(まえ) (mae, front). Common pattern: 〜の後(うし)ろに (behind ~).

Note: Different from 後(あと) (ato), which means 'after' in time.
