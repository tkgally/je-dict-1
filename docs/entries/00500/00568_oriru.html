降(お)りる

おりる
verb (ichidan)
to get off, to descend

Definitions

1. to get off, to alight
To exit a vehicle. Used for trains, buses, cars, etc.
2. to go down, to descend
To move from a higher position to a lower one.

Examples

次(つぎ)の駅(えき)で降(お)ります。
I'll get off at the next station.
Sense 1
バスを降(お)りてください。
Please get off the bus.
を can be used when leaving the vehicle
階段(かいだん)を降(お)りる。
To go down the stairs.
Sense 2

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 降(お)ろす (transitive, to let someone off/unload). Pattern: Xを/から降(お)りる.

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 降(お)りている = 'has gotten off' or 'is off (the vehicle).'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)を降(お)りる (get off the train)
  • バスから降(お)りる (get off the bus)
  • 階段(かいだん)を降(お)りる (go down the stairs)
  • 次(つぎ)の駅(えき)で降(お)りる (get off at the next station)

Opposite: 乗(の)る (to get on). Also written as 下(お)りる.
