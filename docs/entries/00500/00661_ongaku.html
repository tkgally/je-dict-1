音楽(おんがく)

おんがく
noun
music

Definitions

1. music
The art of organized sound; can refer to music in general or a specific piece of music.

Examples

音楽(おんがく)を聞(き)いています。
I'm listening to music.
どんな音楽(おんがく)が好(す)きですか。
What kind of music do you like?
音楽(おんがく)の先生(せんせい)になりたいです。
I want to become a music teacher.

Notes

音楽(おんがく) combines 音(おと) (sound) and 楽(がく) (pleasure/music).

Related words:

  • 音楽家(おんがくか) (ongakuka, musician)
  • 音楽会(おんがくかい) (ongakukai, concert). Used with 聞(き)く (to listen) rather than 見(み)る (to see).
