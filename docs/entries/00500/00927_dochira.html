どちら

どちら
pronoun
which (of two); where (polite)
1. which one (of two)
Used to ask about a choice between two options. The polite form of どっち.
2. where (polite)
A polite way to ask where someone is from or where they are going.
3. who (polite)
A polite way to ask who someone is, often used in business contexts.
紅茶(こうちゃ)とコーヒー(こーひー)、どちらがいいですか。
Which would you prefer, tea or coffee?
Choice between two
どちらからいらっしゃいましたか。
Where are you from?
Polite 'where' meaning
失礼(しつれい)ですが、どちら様(さま)ですか。
Excuse me, but who are you? / May I ask who you are?
Very polite 'who' meaning

どちら is a polite word with multiple uses. For asking 'which of two,' どっち is the casual equivalent. どちら is part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative series: こちら (this way), そちら (that way), あちら (over there), どちら (which way). In formal settings, どちら様(さま)ですか is used to politely ask someone's identity.
