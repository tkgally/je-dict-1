ちょっと

ちょっと
adverb
a little, just a moment
1. a little
A small amount or degree.
2. just a moment
A brief period of time.
3. excuse me (softener)
Used to soften requests or refusals.
ちょっと待(ま)ってください。
Please wait a moment.
Sense 2
ちょっと難(むずか)しいです。
It's a bit difficult.
Sense 1
ちょっと...（すみません）
Um... (I'm afraid I can't)
Sense 3: polite refusal

ちょっと alone can politely decline an invitation without completing the sentence. Similar: 少(すこ)し (a little - slightly more formal).
