〜台(だい)

だい
counter
counter for machines/vehicles
1. counter for machines and vehicles
Used to count machines, vehicles, and large equipment such as cars, computers, bicycles, TVs, and appliances.
車(くるま)が二台(にだい)あります。
There are two cars.
パソコンを一台(いちだい)買(か)いました。
I bought one computer.
自転車(じてんしゃ)が何台(なんだい)ありますか。
How many bicycles are there?
会社(かいしゃ)に新(あたら)しいパソコンが十台(じゅうだい)届(とど)きました。
Ten new computers arrived at the company.
家(いえ)にはテレビが三台(さんだい)あって、私(わたし)の部屋(へや)にも一台(いちだい)あります。
There are three TVs at home, and there's one in my room too.

〜台(だい) counts machines, vehicles, and large appliances.

COUNTING:

Regular pronunciation throughout — this counter has no sound changes:

  • 一台(いちだい), 二台(にだい), 三台(さんだい), 四台(よんだい), 五台(ごだい)
  • 六台(ろくだい), 七台(ななだい), 八台(はちだい), 九台(きゅうだい), 十台(じゅうだい)
  • 何台(なんだい): how many?

WHAT IT COUNTS:

  • Vehicles: 車(くるま), 自転車(じてんしゃ), バイク, バス
  • Electronics: パソコン, テレビ, 冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ), カメラ
  • Machines: エアコン, 洗濯機(せんたくき), 掃除機(そうじき), プリンター

The common thread is a self-contained 機械(きかい) — something with a motor, an engine, or a power supply — not the object's shape.

CONTRASTS:

  • 〜個(こ) counts small objects by shape rather than by function. Small devices sit on the border: a phone or a mouse takes either, with 台(だい) sounding more like equipment and 〜個(こ) more like a small item.
  • Long, thin things take 〜本(ほん) even when they are machine parts.
  • The independent noun 台(だい) ("stand, platform") is written with the same character but is a separate word, not this counter.

Related Words

Contrast: 〜個(こ) (counter for small objects)
Contrast: 〜本(ほん) (counter for long objects)
