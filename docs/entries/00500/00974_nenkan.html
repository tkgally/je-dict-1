年間(ねんかん)

ねんかん
counter
years (duration)
1. years (duration)
A counter for counting the duration of time in years. Emphasizes the span or period of time.
三(さん)年間(ねんかん)日本(にほん)に住(す)んでいました。
I lived in Japan for three years.
年間(ねんかん)売(う)り上(あ)げは百万(ひゃくまん)円(えん)です。
Annual sales are one million yen.
Using 年間 as 'annual'
十(じゅう)年間(ねんかん)の経験(けいけん)があります。
I have ten years of experience.

年間(ねんかん) emphasizes duration over a period of years. It can also mean 'annual' or 'yearly' when used as a prefix. Compare with 年(ねん) which is used for counting years more generally (三(さん)年(ねん) = three years) or for naming years (2024年(にせんにじゅうよねん) = the year 2024).
