おかしい

おかしい
adjective (i-adjective)
strange, funny
1. strange, odd
Something that seems wrong, unusual, or not quite right. Often implies suspicion that something is amiss.
これはおかしい。
This is strange.
この話(はなし)はおかしいですね。
This story is strange, isn't it?
何(なに)かおかしいと思(おも)いました。
I thought something was strange.
この時計(とけい)の音(おと)がおかしいから、見(み)てください。
The sound of this clock is strange, so please take a look.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)はおかしいと思(おも)ったので、もう一度(いちど)聞(き)いてみることにしました。
I thought his story was strange, so I decided to ask again.
2. funny, amusing
Something that makes you laugh or smile. Used when something is humorous or comical.
おかしい話(はなし)だね。
That's a funny story.
その映画(えいが)はとてもおかしかったです。
That movie was very funny.
友達(ともだち)がおかしい顔(かお)をして笑(わら)わせてくれた。
My friend made a funny face and made me laugh.
あの人(ひと)の話(はなし)し方(かた)がとてもおかしくて、みんな笑(わら)っていました。
The way that person talks is so funny, everyone was laughing.
子供(こども)たちはおかしい動画(どうが)を見(み)て、お腹(なか)を抱(かか)えて笑(わら)っていました。
The children watched a funny video and were laughing so hard they were holding their stomachs.

Context determines whether おかしい means 'strange' or 'funny'. When describing a person's behavior or a situation that seems wrong, it means 'strange'. When describing comedy or jokes, it means 'funny'. Can also be written 可笑(おか)しい in kanji, but hiragana is more common.
