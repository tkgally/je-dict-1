おかしい

おかしい
adjective (i-adjective)
strange, funny
1. strange, odd
Something that seems wrong, unusual, or not quite right. Often implies suspicion that something is amiss.
この話(はなし)はおかしいですね。
This story is strange, isn't it?
Sense 1: something seems off
体(からだ)の調子(ちょうし)がおかしいです。
Something is wrong with my body.
Common expression for feeling unwell
2. funny, amusing
Something that makes you laugh or smile. Used when something is humorous or comical.
その映画(えいが)はとてもおかしかったです。
That movie was very funny.
Sense 2: amusing

Context determines whether おかしい means 'strange' or 'funny'. When describing a person's behavior or a situation that seems wrong, it means 'strange'. When describing comedy or jokes, it means 'funny'. Can also be written 可笑(おか)しい in kanji, but hiragana is more common.
