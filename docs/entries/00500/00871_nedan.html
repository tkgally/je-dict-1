値段(ねだん)

ねだん
noun
price
1. price, cost
The amount of money required to buy something. The monetary value assigned to goods or services.
この値段(ねだん)は高(たか)いです。
This price is expensive.
値段(ねだん)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the price.
値段(ねだん)が下(さ)がりました。
The price went down.

More specific than just asking いくら (how much). 値段(ねだん)が高(たか)い/安{やす}い means expensive/cheap.

Related:

  • 定価(ていか) (fixed price)
  • 価格(かかく) (price, more formal).
