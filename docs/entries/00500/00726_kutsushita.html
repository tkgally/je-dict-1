靴下(くつした)

くつした
noun
socks

Definitions

1. socks
Cloth coverings for the feet, usually worn inside shoes. Literally means 'under shoes.'

Examples

靴下(くつした)を履(は)いてください。
Please put on socks.
白(しろ)い靴下(くつした)がありますか。
Do you have white socks?
靴下(くつした)に穴(あな)が開(あ)いています。
There's a hole in the sock.

Notes

Compound word: 靴(くつ) (shoes) + 下(した) (under). Counter: 足(そく) for pairs, 枚(まい) for individual socks. Uses 履(は)く (to put on) and 脱(ぬ)ぐ (to take off). When in Japan, socks should be clean and without holes since shoes are often removed!
