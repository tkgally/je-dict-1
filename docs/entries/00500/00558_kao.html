顔(かお)

かお
noun
face
1. face
The front part of the head, containing the eyes, nose, and mouth.
顔(かお)を洗(あら)いました。
I washed my face.
彼女(かのじょ)の顔(かお)が赤(あか)くなりました。
Her face turned red.
嬉(うれ)しい顔(かお)をしています。
They have a happy face/expression.
顔をする = to have an expression
彼(かれ)の顔(かお)はどこかで見(み)たことがあります。
I've seen his face somewhere before.
朝(あさ)、鏡(かがみ)で顔(かお)を見(み)たら、目(め)が赤(あか)くなっていて驚(おどろ)きました。
In the morning, when I looked at my face in the mirror, I was surprised to see my eyes were red.

顔(かお) refers to the face, and is used for both the physical face and facial expressions. Also used figuratively in many idioms.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 顔(かお)を洗(あら)う: to wash one's face
  • 顔(かお)を出(だ)す: to show up, make an appearance
  • 顔(かお)が広(ひろ)い: well-connected (lit. wide face)
  • 顔(かお)を立(た)てる: to save face, show respect
  • 顔(かお)がきく: to have influence

FACIAL EXPRESSIONS:

  • 嬉(うれ)しい顔(かお): happy face
  • 悲(かな)しい顔(かお): sad face
  • 怒(おこ)った顔(かお): angry face
  • 顔(かお)をする: to make a face, have an expression

RELATED BODY PARTS:

  • 目(め): eye
  • 鼻(はな): nose
  • 口(くち): mouth
  • 頭(あたま): head
