たぶん

たぶん
adverb
probably, maybe
1. probably, perhaps, maybe
Indicates likelihood or probability. Suggests the speaker thinks something is likely but isn't certain.
たぶん明日(あした)は雨(あめ)でしょう。
It will probably rain tomorrow.
たぶん大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)だと思(おも)います。
I think it's probably okay.
たぶん来(こ)ないと思(おも)います。
I don't think they'll probably come.

たぶん can be written in kanji as 多分(たぶん). It's often used with でしょう or と思(おも)う to express uncertainty. Similar to きっと (surely/certainly) but たぶん expresses less certainty. おそらく is a more formal synonym.
