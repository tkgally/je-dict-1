町(まち)

まち
noun
town, city
1. town, city
An urban area; a populated place.
この町(まち)は静(しず)かです。
This town is quiet.
町(まち)で買(か)い物(もの)をしました。
I went shopping in town.
新(あたら)しい町(まち)に引(ひ)っ越(こ)しました。
I moved to a new town.
この町(まち)には駅(えき)が二(ふた)つあります。
There are two stations in this town.
私(わたし)が生(う)まれた町(まち)は山(やま)の近(ちか)くにあって、空気(くうき)がとてもきれいでした。
The town where I was born was near the mountains, and the air was very clean.

町(まち) refers to a town or urban area, often used for smaller municipalities or neighborhoods.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 町(まち): town, municipality (administrative unit)
  • 街(まち): city streets, downtown area (emphasis on the urban atmosphere)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 町(まち)に出(で)る: to go into town
  • 町(まち)で買(か)い物(もの)をする: to shop in town
  • 町(まち)を歩(ある)く: to walk around town
  • 町(まち)並(な)み: townscape, row of houses

RELATED WORDS:

  • 村(むら): village (smaller than 町(まち))
  • 市(し): city (larger than 町(まち))
  • 都会(とかい): urban area, city
