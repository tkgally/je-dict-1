ほとんど

ほとんど
adverb
almost, mostly

Definitions

1. almost, nearly
Very close to completely. Used to indicate that something is almost at a certain state or level.
2. mostly, for the most part
The greater part of something. Nearly all.

Examples

仕事(しごと)はほとんど終(お)わりました。
The work is almost finished.
Sense 1: almost complete
ほとんどの人(ひと)が来(き)ました。
Most people came.
Sense 2: most of
ほとんど食(た)べられませんでした。
I could hardly eat anything.
With negative = hardly any

Notes

Can be written as 殆(ほとん)ど in kanji but hiragana is more common. With positive verbs: 'almost/mostly'. With negative verbs: 'hardly/barely'. ほとんどの + noun means 'most of the...'
