ごはん

ごはん
noun
rice, meal
1. cooked rice
Steamed white rice, the staple food of Japan.
ごはんを食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat (a meal/rice).
Can mean either
ごはんとみそ汁(しる)をください。
Rice and miso soup, please.
Sense 1: rice
ごはんを炊(た)く。
Cook rice.
Sense 1: cooked rice
ごはんのおかわりをください。
May I have another serving of rice?
Sense 1: cooked rice
白(しろ)いごはんが好(す)きだ。
I like plain white rice.
Sense 1: cooked rice
2. meal
Food in general, or a meal.
ごはんを食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat (a meal/rice).
Can mean either
朝(あさ)ごはんは何(なに)を食(た)べましたか？
What did you eat for breakfast?
Sense 2: meal
ごはんの時間(じかん)です。
It's time to eat.
Sense 2: meal
昼(ひる)ごはんに何(なに)を食(た)べよう。
What should I eat for lunch?
Sense 2: meal
晩(ばん)ごはんを作(つく)る。
Make dinner.
Sense 2: meal
もうごはんは食(た)べましたか。
Have you eaten yet?
Sense 2: meal

ごはん is one of the most important words in Japanese, meaning both "cooked rice" and "meal."

KANJI:
Written as ご飯(はん) with kanji. The ご is an honorific prefix.

MEAL NAMES:

  • 朝(あさ)ごはん: breakfast
  • 昼(ひる)ごはん: lunch
  • 晩(ばん)ごはん/夜(よる)ごはん: dinner

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ごはんを食(た)べる: to eat (a meal)
  • ごはんを炊(た)く: to cook rice
  • ごはんの時間(じかん): mealtime
  • ごはんのおかわり: another serving of rice

CULTURE:
Rice is so central to Japanese cuisine that ごはん came to mean "meal" in general. Asking ごはん食(た)べた? means "Have you eaten?" regardless of what was actually eaten.
