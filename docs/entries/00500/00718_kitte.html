切手(きって)

きって
noun
postage stamp

Definitions

1. postage stamp
A small adhesive label purchased from a post office and attached to mail as proof of postage payment.

Examples

切手(きって)を買(か)いたいです。
I want to buy stamps.
この手紙(てがみ)に切手(きって)を貼(は)ってください。
Please put a stamp on this letter.
八十(はちじゅう)円(えん)の切手(きって)をください。
Please give me an 80-yen stamp.

Notes

Counter: 枚(まい) (一(いち)枚(まい), 二(に)枚(まい), etc.). Common collocations: 切手(きって)を貼(は)る (to stick a stamp), 切手(きって)を買(か)う (to buy stamps). Can be bought at post offices and convenience stores in Japan.
