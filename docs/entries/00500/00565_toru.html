取(と)る

とる
verb (godan)
to take, to get

Definitions

1. to take, to pick up
To take hold of something with your hand.
2. to take (a photo)
To capture an image. Written as 撮(と)る for photos.
3. to get, to obtain
To acquire something like a reservation, license, or vacation time.

Examples

塩(しお)を取(と)ってください。
Please pass the salt.
Sense 1
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)りましょう。
Let's take a photo.
Sense 2 (different kanji)
休(やす)みを取(と)りたいです。
I want to take time off.
Sense 3

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを取(と)る (take X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state or ongoing action. 取(と)っている = 'have taken' or 'is taking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 塩(しお)を取(と)る (pass the salt)
  • 休(やす)みを取(と)る (take time off)
  • 予約(よやく)を取(と)る (make a reservation)
  • 年(とし)を取(と)る (get older)

Multiple kanji: 取(と)る (general take), 撮(と)る (photos), 採(と)る (harvest/collect). Very versatile verb.
