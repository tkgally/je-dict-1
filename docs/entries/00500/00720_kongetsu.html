今月(こんげつ)

こんげつ
noun, adverb
this month
1. this month
The current month; the month we are in now. Used to talk about events or plans within the present month.
今月(こんげつ)は忙(いそが)しいです。
I'm busy this month.
今月(こんげつ)の天気(てんき)はどうですか。
How's the weather this month?
今月(こんげつ)は誕生日(たんじょうび)です。
My birthday is this month.
今月(こんげつ)、新(あたら)しい仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めました。
I started a new job this month.
今月(こんげつ)は雨(あめ)が多(おお)かったですが、来月(らいげつ)は晴(は)れる日(ひ)が多(おお)いでしょう。
There was a lot of rain this month, but next month will probably have many sunny days.

Part of a time reference series: 先月(せんげつ) (last month), 今月(こんげつ) (this month), 来月(らいげつ) (next month). The 今(こん) prefix means 'this' or 'current' and also appears in 今週(こんしゅう) (this week).
