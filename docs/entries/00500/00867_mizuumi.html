湖(みずうみ)

みずうみ
noun
lake

Definitions

1. lake
A large body of water surrounded by land. Bigger than a pond (池(いけ)).

Examples

湖(みずうみ)で泳(およ)ぎました。
I swam in the lake.
きれいな湖(みずうみ)ですね。
It's a beautiful lake, isn't it?
湖(みずうみ)のまわりを歩(ある)きましょう。
Let's walk around the lake.

Notes

Composed of 水(みず) (water) and 海(うみ) (sea), literally 'water-sea'. Larger than 池(いけ) (pond). Famous lakes in Japan include 琵琶湖(びわこ) (Lake Biwa) and 河口湖(かわぐちこ) (Lake Kawaguchi).
