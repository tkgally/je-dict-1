品物(しなもの)

しなもの
noun
goods, article
1. goods, article, item
A physical item or product, especially one for sale or trade. General term for merchandise or objects.
この品物(しなもの)はいくらですか。
How much is this item?
品物(しなもの)を送(おく)りました。
I sent the goods.
高(たか)い品物(しなもの)は買(か)えません。
I can't buy expensive items.

A general term for merchandise or items. More formal than 物(もの) (thing). Often used in shopping and business contexts.

Related:

  • 品質(ひんしつ) (quality)
  • 商品(しょうひん) (product/merchandise).
