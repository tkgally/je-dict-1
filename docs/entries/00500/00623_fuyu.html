冬(ふゆ)

ふゆ
noun
winter
1. winter
The coldest season of the year, typically from December to February in Japan.
冬(ふゆ)は寒(さむ)いです。
Winter is cold.
冬(ふゆ)に雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)ります。
It snows in winter.
冬(ふゆ)は鍋(なべ)がおいしいです。
Hot pot is delicious in winter.
今年(ことし)の冬(ふゆ)は去年(きょねん)より寒(さむ)いと思(おも)います。
I think this winter is colder than last year.
冬(ふゆ)になると日(ひ)が短(みじか)くなって、五時(ごじ)ごろにはもう暗(くら)くなります。
When winter comes, the days get shorter and it's already dark around five o'clock.

冬 is associated with New Year (正月), skiing, hot springs, and warm foods like nabe (hot pot).

Related:

  • 冬休み (fuyuyasumi, winter vacation)
  • 冬至 (touji, winter solstice)
  • 真冬 (mafuyu, midwinter).
