どう

どう
adverb
how, in what way
1. how, in what way
A question word asking about manner, method, or condition.
日本(にほん)の生活(せいかつ)はどうですか？
How is life in Japan?
Asking about condition/impression
この漢字(かんじ)はどう読(よ)みますか？
How do you read this kanji?
Asking about method
映画(えいが)を見(み)に行(い)くのはどうですか？
How about going to see a movie?
Making a suggestion
どうして今日(きょう)は学校(がっこう)に来(こ)なかったのですか？
Why didn't you come to school today?
どうして = why
このパソコンの使(つか)い方(かた)がどうしても分(わ)からないので、教(おし)えてもらえませんか？
I just can't figure out how to use this computer, so could you teach me?
どうしても = no matter how/what

どう is a versatile question word asking "how" about manner, method, condition, or opinion.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • どうですか: How is it? / What do you think?
  • どう思(おも)いますか: What do you think?
  • どうしますか: What will you do?
  • どう読(よ)みますか: How do you read (this)?

MAKING SUGGESTIONS:
〜のはどうですか: How about ~?
映画(えいが)を見(み)るのはどうですか: How about watching a movie?

COMPOUNDS WITH どう:

  • どうして: why (also: how)
  • どうも: somehow; very much (thanks)
  • どうぞ: please, go ahead
  • どうしても: no matter what, by all means
  • どうやって: how (method)

PART OF KO-SO-A-DO:
どう corresponds to こう (this way), そう (that way), ああ (that way, far).
