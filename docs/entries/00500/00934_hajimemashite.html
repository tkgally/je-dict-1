初(はじ)めまして

はじめまして
expression
nice to meet you
1. nice to meet you, how do you do
A greeting used when meeting someone for the first time. Literally means 'for the first time.'
初(はじ)めまして。田中(たなか)です。
Nice to meet you. I'm Tanaka.
Standard self-introduction
初(はじ)めまして。どうぞよろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Nice to meet you. Please treat me favorably.
Full formal introduction
初(はじ)めまして。山田(やまだ)と申(もう)します。
Nice to meet you. My name is Yamada.
Humble/formal introduction style
初(はじ)めまして。私(わたし)は会社(かいしゃ)で働(はたら)いています。よろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Nice to meet you. I work at a company. I look forward to getting to know you.
初(はじ)めまして。隣(となり)に来(き)た鈴木(すずき)です。これからよろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Nice to meet you. I'm Suzuki, I just came next door. I look forward to getting to know you.
Neighbor introduction

初(はじ)めまして is the standard greeting when meeting someone for the first time. Derived from 初(はじ)めて (for the first time), it literally means 'it is the first time (we meet).'

TYPICAL INTRODUCTION PATTERN:
1. 初(はじ)めまして。(Nice to meet you.)
2. [Name]です / [Name]と申(もう)します。(I'm [Name]. / My name is [Name] — humble)
3. どうぞよろしくお願(ねが)いします。(Please treat me favorably.)

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • Casual: 初(はじ)めまして。[Name]です。よろしく。
  • Standard: 初(はじ)めまして。[Name]です。よろしくお願(ねが)いします。
  • Formal: 初(はじ)めまして。[Name]と申(もう)します。どうぞよろしくお願(ねが)いいたします。

IMPORTANT:
Only use 初(はじ)めまして on a genuine first meeting. Using it with someone you have met before is awkward and may come across as rude, implying you don't remember them.

Related Words

Related: よろしくお願(ねが)いします (the phrase that normally follows it)
Related: 初(はじ)めて (for the first time (the source of the greeting))
