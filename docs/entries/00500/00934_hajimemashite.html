初(はじ)めまして

はじめまして
expression
nice to meet you
1. nice to meet you, how do you do
A greeting used when meeting someone for the first time. Literally means 'for the first time.'
初(はじ)めまして。田中(たなか)です。
Nice to meet you. I'm Tanaka.
Standard self-introduction
初(はじ)めまして。どうぞよろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Nice to meet you. Please treat me favorably.
Full formal introduction

初(はじ)めまして is the standard greeting when meeting someone for the first time. It's typically followed by your name and よろしくお願(ねが)いします. This phrase should only be used on first meeting - using it with someone you've met before would be awkward or even rude.
