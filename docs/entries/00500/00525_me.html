目(め)

め
noun
eye
1. eye
The organ of sight.
目(め)が大(おお)きいですね。
You have big eyes.
目(め)を閉(と)じてください。
Please close your eyes.
目(め)が悪(わる)いので、めがねをかけています。
I wear glasses because I have poor eyesight.
目が悪い = poor eyesight
赤(あか)い目(め)をした猫(ねこ)がいます。
There is a cat with red eyes.
長(なが)い時間(じかん)パソコンを見(み)ていたので、目(め)が疲(つか)れました。
I was looking at the computer for a long time, so my eyes got tired.

目(め) is one of the most common body part words, appearing in many expressions and compounds.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)が悪(わる)い: to have poor eyesight
  • 目(め)が覚(さ)める: to wake up
  • 目(め)が疲(つか)れる: eyes get tired
  • 目(め)を閉(と)じる: to close one's eyes
  • 目(め)に見(み)える: visible
  • 目(め)を引(ひ)く: eye-catching

COMPOUNDS:

  • めがね: glasses (eye + metal)
  • 目薬(めぐすり): eye drops
  • 目覚(めざ)まし時計(どけい): alarm clock

COUNTER SUFFIX:
〜目(め) also serves as an ordinal suffix: 一(ひと)つ目(め) (first), 二(ふた)つ目(め) (second).
