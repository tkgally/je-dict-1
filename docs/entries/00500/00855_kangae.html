考(かんが)え

かんがえ
noun
thought, idea
1. thought, idea, opinion
A mental concept, plan, or point of view. The noun form of the verb 考(かんが)える (to think).
いい考(かんが)えがあります。
I have a good idea.
あなたの考(かんが)えを聞(き)かせてください。
Please tell me your thoughts.
私(わたし)の考(かんが)えは違(ちが)います。
My opinion is different.
その考(かんが)えはとてもいいと思(おも)います。
I think that idea is very good.
彼(かれ)の考(かんが)えを聞(き)いてから、私(わたし)たちも考(かんが)えを話(はな)しましょう。
After hearing his thoughts, let's also share our ideas.

The noun form derived from the verb 考(かんが)える. Often used with がある (to have) or を聞(き)く (to hear/ask). Similar to 意見(いけん) (opinion) but 考(かんが)え is more general and can include plans or ideas, not just opinions.
