技(わざ)

わざ
noun
technique, skill, move

Definitions

1. technique, skill
A specialized ability acquired through practice.
2. move, trick
A particular action in sports or martial arts.

Examples

技(わざ)を磨(みが)く。
To hone one's skills.
必殺技(ひっさつわざ)を繰(く)り出(だ)した。
He unleashed his special move.

Notes

技(わざ) refers to techniques and skills.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ): technique, technology
  • 必殺技(ひっさつわざ): special move
  • 大技(おおわざ): big move, major technique
  • 小技(こわざ): small trick

IN MARTIAL ARTS:
技(わざ) is used for moves in judo, sumo, wrestling, etc.
