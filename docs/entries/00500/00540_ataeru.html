与(あた)える

あたえる
verb (ichidan)
to give, to grant

Definitions

1. to give, to grant, to provide
To give something to someone, often implying the giver is in a superior position.

Examples

子供(こども)に教育(きょういく)を与(あた)える。
To give children an education.
影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えました。
It had an influence. / It gave an influence.
チャンスを与(あた)えてください。
Please give me a chance.

Notes

与(あた)える means to give or grant.

USAGE:

  • More formal than あげる or やる
  • Often implies giver has authority/superiority
  • Common in written/formal contexts

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える: to give influence
  • 印象(いんしょう)を与(あた)える: to give an impression
  • 機会(きかい)を与(あた)える: to give an opportunity
  • 許可(きょか)を与(あた)える: to grant permission

Note: Ichidan verb (る-verb).
