向(む)こう

むこう
noun
over there, opposite side
1. over there, the other side
A place that is across from or on the opposite side from where the speaker is.
向(む)こうに山(やま)が見(み)える。
Mountains are visible over there.
川(かわ)の向(む)こうに村(むら)があります。
There's a village on the other side of the river.
道(みち)の向(む)こうに店(みせ)が並(なら)んでいる。
Shops are lined up on the other side of the street.
海(うみ)の向(む)こうには外国(がいこく)があります。いつか}{行(い)ってみたいです。
There are foreign countries across the sea. I want to visit someday.
窓(まど)から見(み)ると、向(む)こうの山(やま)が白(しろ)くなっていた。雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)ったのだろう。
Looking from the window, the mountains over there had turned white. It must have snowed.
2. the opposite party, they
The other side in a negotiation or relationship.
向(む)こうに聞(き)く。
To ask them (the other party).
向(む)こうから連絡(れんらく)がありました。
We received contact from them (the other party).
向(む)こうの会社(かいしゃ)と話(はなし)をする。
To talk with the other company.
この問題(もんだい)について、向(む)こうは何(なに)も言(い)っていません。
They haven't said anything about this issue.
向(む)こうが悪(わる)いのだから、こちらから謝(あやま)る必要(ひつよう)はないと思(おも)います。
Since they're in the wrong, I don't think we need to apologize from our side.

向(む)こう indicates a place across from or opposite the speaker's position. It implies there's something (like a road, river, or distance) between the speaker and that place. [noun]の向(む)こう means 'on the other side of [noun].' It can also refer to 'them' or 'the other party' in business or relationship contexts.
