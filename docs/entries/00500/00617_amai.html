甘(あま)い

あまい
adjective (i-adjective)
sweet
1. sweet (taste)
Describes a sweet taste in food or drinks.
2. lenient, naive, optimistic
Figuratively means being too easy on someone, having naive expectations, or being overly optimistic.
このケーキは甘(あま)いです。
This cake is sweet.
甘(あま)いものが好(す)きです。
I like sweet things.
考(かんが)えが甘(あま)い。
Your thinking is naive.
Figurative meaning

甘(あま)い primarily means 'sweet' for taste but can also describe lenient parenting, naive thinking, or optimistic expectations. 甘(あま)いもの means 'sweets/desserts.' Conjugation: 甘(あま)くない (negative), 甘(あま)かった (past).
