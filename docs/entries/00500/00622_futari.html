二人(ふたり)

ふたり
noun
two people
1. two people
The counter for two people, using an irregular reading.
二人(ふたり)ください。
Table for two, please.
At a restaurant
2. together (as a pair)
Often used to mean 'the two of us/them together.'
二人(ふたり)でどこに行(い)きますか。
Where are the two of you going?
二人(ふたり)は仲(なか)がいいです。
The two of them get along well.

二人 uses the native Japanese reading ふたり, not ににん. Like 一人, this is an irregular counter that must be memorized. 二人で (futari de) means 'together (as a pair)' or 'the two of them.'
