五(ご)

ご
number
five
1. five
The number five. A central number in Japanese culture, found in many traditional concepts. Straightforward to use with most counters.
五(ご)時(じ)に起(お)きます。
I wake up at five o'clock.
五(ご)人(にん)家族(かぞく)です。
It's a family of five.
五(ご)月(がつ)は気持(きも)ちいい季節(きせつ)です。
May is a pleasant season.
友達(ともだち)と約束(やくそく)があるから、五(ご)時(じ)までに家(いえ)に帰(かえ)らなければなりません。
I have plans with a friend, so I have to be home by five o'clock.
日本(にほん)の会社(かいしゃ)では週(しゅう)に五(ご)日(にち)働(はたら)くのが普通(ふつう)で、土曜日(どようび)と日曜日(にちようび)は休(やす)みになっています。
In Japanese companies, it's normal to work five days a week, with Saturday and Sunday off.

The native Japanese counter reading is 五(いつ)つ (itsutsu). In most other cases, ご is used. The number five appears in cultural concepts like 五(ご)感(かん) (gokan, the five senses) and 五(ご)輪(りん) (gorin, the Olympic rings/five rings).
