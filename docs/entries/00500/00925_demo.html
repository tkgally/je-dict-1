でも

でも
conjunction
but, however
1. but, however
A casual conjunction used to contrast or introduce an exception. Used at the beginning of sentences.
行(い)きたい。でも、時間(じかん)がない。
I want to go. But I don't have time.
でも、それは本当(ほんとう)ですか。
But, is that true?
高(たか)いです。でも、おいしいです。
It's expensive. But it's delicious.
疲(つか)れました。でも、楽(たの)しかったです。
I'm tired. But it was fun.
あの店(みせ)は少(すこ)し遠(とお)いです。でも、料理(りょうり)がとてもおいしいので、週末(しゅうまつ)によく行(い)きます。
That shop is a bit far. But the food is very delicious, so I often go there on weekends.
2. even (with particle)
When attached to nouns, means 'even' (e.g., 子供(こども)でも - even a child).
子供(こども)でも分(わ)かる。
Even a child can understand.
何(なに)でもいいです。
Anything is fine.
Common expression
お茶(ちゃ)でも飲(の)みませんか。
Would you like to have some tea or something?
'Or something like that' nuance
誰(だれ)でも入(はい)れます。
Anyone can enter.
このゲームは簡単(かんたん)なので、初(はじ)めてでもすぐに上手(じょうず)になれますよ。
This game is simple, so even if it's your first time, you'll get good at it quickly.

でも covers two distinct jobs that happen to share a shape: a sentence-initial conjunction (sense 1) and a particle attached to a noun (sense 2). Learners meet the conjunction first, but the particle is the more productive of the two.

AS A CONJUNCTION (sense 1):

でも starts a new sentence and pushes back against what was just said. It is the everyday spoken choice — softer and far more conversational than しかし.

  • Position: sentence-initial only. Never use it to join two clauses inside one sentence; that job belongs to けど or が, which attach to the end of the first clause.
  • 高(たか)いですけど、おいしいです and 高(たか)いです。でも、おいしいです say the same thing; only the packaging differs.

AS A PARTICLE (sense 2):

Attached directly to a noun, 〜でも picks out something as an extreme or unremarkable case, and lets the rest of the set follow by implication.

  • "Even": 子供(こども)でも分(わ)かる — if even a child understands, so does everyone else.
  • "Or something": お茶(ちゃ)でも飲(の)みませんか softens an invitation by presenting tea as one casual option rather than a fixed demand. This is the standard polite way to propose an activity.

WITH QUESTION WORDS:

Attached to an interrogative, 〜でも produces a free-choice "any-" word. These are common enough to have their own entries:

  • 何(なに) → 何(なん)でも (anything)
  • 誰(だれ) → 誰(だれ)でも (anyone)
  • いつ → いつでも (anytime)
  • どこ → どこでも (anywhere)

Note the reading shift: 何(なに) becomes 何(なん) before でも.

REGISTER:
でも is informal. In writing, presentations, and business email, use しかし or ところが instead. The particle 〜でも carries no such restriction and is at home at any level of politeness.

Related Words

Synonym: しかし (however (formal))
Contrast: だから (therefore (causal))
Related: けど (but (clause-final, joins one sentence))
Related: 何(なん)でも (anything (〜でも with a question word))
