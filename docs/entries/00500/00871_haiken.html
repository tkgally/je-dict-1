拝見(はいけん)

はいけん
noun (verbal)
(humble) to look at, to see

Definitions

1. to look at, to see (humble)
Humble form of 見る, used when the speaker views something belonging to or offered by a superior.

Examples

お写真(しゃしん)を拝見(はいけん)しました。
I looked at your photo. (humble)
資料(しりょう)を拝見(はいけん)してもよろしいですか。
May I take a look at the documents? (humble)

Notes

拝見(はいけん)する is the humble form of 見(み)る.

POLITENESS:

  • 見(み)る: neutral
  • 拝見(はいけん)する: humble (lowers speaker)
  • ご覧(らん)になる: honorific (elevates other)

USAGE:
Used in formal/business situations when viewing something belonging to someone of higher status.
