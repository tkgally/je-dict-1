働(はたら)く

はたらく
verb (godan)
to work
1. to work, to labor
To engage in employment or productive activity. The most common verb for working at a job.
会社(かいしゃ)で働(はたら)いています。
I work at a company.
毎日(まいにち)8時間(じかん)働(はたら)きます。
I work 8 hours every day.
父(ちち)は銀行(ぎんこう)で働(はたら)いています。
My father works at a bank.
で marks the workplace

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xで働(はたら)く (work at X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 働(はたら)いている = 'is working' or 'works (habitually).'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)で働(はたら)く (work at a company)
  • 8時間(じかん)働(はたら)く (work 8 hours)
  • 働(はたら)いている (is working/employed)
  • 働(はたら)き方(かた) (way of working)

Uses で for workplace. Different from 勤(つと)める (employed at - focuses on organization).

Related: 仕事(しごと) (work/job).
