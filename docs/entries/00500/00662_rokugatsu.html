六月(ろくがつ)

ろくがつ
noun
June
1. June
The sixth month of the year. Known as the rainy season (梅雨, tsuyu) in most of Japan.
六月(ろくがつ)は雨(あめ)が多(おお)いです。
There's a lot of rain in June.
六月(ろくがつ)は暑(あつ)くなります。
It gets hot in June.
六月(ろくがつ)に梅雨(つゆ)が始(はじ)まります。
The rainy season starts in June.
六月(ろくがつ)の終(お)わりごろ、やっと晴(は)れの日(ひ)が多(おお)くなります。
Around the end of June, sunny days finally become more frequent.
六月(ろくがつ)に外(そと)へ出(で)かけるときは、傘(かさ)を持(も)っていったほうがいいですよ。
When going out in June, you'd better bring an umbrella.

六月(ろくがつ) is June, the sixth month of the year.

PATTERN:
Japanese months use number + 月(がつ): 一月(いちがつ), 二月(にがつ), etc.

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • Most of Japan (except Hokkaido) experiences 梅雨(つゆ) (rainy season) in June
  • Despite the rain, "June bride" (ジューンブライド) weddings are popular due to Western influence
  • 衣替(ころもが)え (seasonal clothing change) traditionally happens June 1st

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 六月(ろくがつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん): mid-June
  • 六月(ろくがつ)上旬(じょうじゅん): early June
  • 六月(ろくがつ)下旬(げじゅん): late June

RELATED:

  • 梅雨入(つゆい)り: start of rainy season
  • 梅雨明(つゆあ)け: end of rainy season
