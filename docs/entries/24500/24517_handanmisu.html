判断(はんだん)ミス

はんだんみす
noun
judgment error; misjudgment
1. judgment error; misjudgment
A mistake resulting from an incorrect assessment of a situation or from making a wrong decision.
審判(しんぱん)の判断(はんだん)ミスで試合(しあい)が荒(あ)れた。
The game got rough due to a judgment error by the referee.
あの事故(じこ)は運転手(うんてんしゅ)の判断(はんだん)ミスが原因(げんいん)だった。
That accident was caused by the driver's misjudgment.
経営陣(けいえいじん)の判断(はんだん)ミスにより会社(かいしゃ)は大(おお)きな損失(そんしつ)を被(こうむ)り、責任(せきにん)問題(もんだい)に発展(はってん)した。
Due to the management's misjudgment, the company suffered significant losses, and it escalated into a question of accountability.

A compound of 判断(はんだん) (judgment, decision) and ミス (mistake, from English "miss"). Commonly used in sports, driving, business, and everyday contexts to describe errors in decision-making.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判断(はんだん)ミスをする: to make a misjudgment
  • 判断(はんだん)ミスが命取(いのちと)りになる: a misjudgment proves fatal
  • 判断(はんだん)ミスを認(みと)める: to acknowledge a judgment error
  • 判断(はんだん)ミスを防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent judgment errors

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誤判断(ごはんだん): erroneous judgment (more formal)
  • 判断(はんだん)誤(あやま)り: judgment mistake (native-word variant)
  • 見込(みこ)み違(ちが)い: miscalculation; wrong estimate
