協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)

きょうどうくみあい
noun
cooperative; cooperative association
1. cooperative; cooperative association; co-op
An organization formed by individuals or businesses who pool resources and share profits for mutual benefit. Common in agriculture, fishing, consumer goods, and credit unions in Japan.
農業(のうぎょう)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)に加入(かにゅう)した。
I joined an agricultural cooperative.
この地域(ちいき)の漁業(ぎょぎょう)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)は百年以上(ひゃくねんいじょう)の歴史(れきし)がある。
The fishing cooperative in this region has a history of over a hundred years.
生活(せいかつ)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)では組合員(くみあいいん)が共同(きょうどう)で食品(しょくひん)や日用品(にちようひん)を安(やす)く購入(こうにゅう)できる仕組(しく)みになっている。
Consumer cooperatives are structured so that members can purchase food and daily goods at lower prices through collective buying.

Composed of 協同(きょうどう) (cooperation, working together) and 組合(くみあい) (union, association). Japan has a highly developed cooperative system, with organizations like JA (農協(のうきょう), agricultural cooperatives) playing a central role in rural economies.

USAGE:
Often abbreviated in compound names: 農業協同組合(のうぎょうきょうどうくみあい) becomes 農協(のうきょう) (JA), 漁業協同組合(ぎょぎょうきょうどうくみあい) becomes 漁協(ぎょきょう), and 生活協同組合(せいかつきょうどうくみあい) becomes 生協(せいきょう) (co-op). These abbreviations are far more common in everyday speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 農業(のうぎょう)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい): agricultural cooperative (JA)
  • 漁業(ぎょぎょう)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい): fishing cooperative
  • 生活(せいかつ)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい): consumer cooperative (co-op)
  • 協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)に加入(かにゅう)する: to join a cooperative

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 組合(くみあい): union, association — the general term for any organized group; cooperatives are one type
  • 農協(のうきょう): agricultural cooperative (JA) — the most well-known type of cooperative in Japan
  • 生協(せいきょう): consumer co-op — provides food, goods, and services to member households
  • 労働組合(ろうどうくみあい): labor union — a union of workers for collective bargaining, distinct from a cooperative
