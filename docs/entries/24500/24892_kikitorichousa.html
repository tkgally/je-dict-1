1. interview survey; hearing investigation; oral inquiry
A survey or investigation conducted by directly interviewing people. Used in government, academic research, journalism, and workplace contexts to gather firsthand information.
住民(じゅうみん)への聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)を行(おこな)った。
An interview survey of the residents was conducted.
被害者(ひがいしゃ)への聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)は慎重(しんちょう)に進(すす)められた。
The interviews with the victims were carried out carefully.
聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)の結果(けっか)をまとめて報告書(ほうこくしょ)を作成(さくせい)した。
I compiled the results of the interview survey and wrote a report.

Compound of 聞(き)き取(と)り (listening; hearing) + 調査(ちょうさ) (investigation; survey). A standard method of data collection where an interviewer asks questions face-to-face or by phone and records the responses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)を行(おこな)う: to conduct an interview survey
  • 聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)の結果(けっか): results of the hearing
  • 聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ)報告書(ほうこくしょ): interview survey report
  • 現地(げんち)聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ): on-site interview survey

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • アンケート調査(ちょうさ): questionnaire survey — written form, not oral
  • ヒアリング: hearing (loanword) — increasingly used in business and government as a synonym
  • 面接(めんせつ)調査(ちょうさ): interview survey (formal) — similar meaning with more formal nuance
  • 取材(しゅざい): news gathering; coverage — specifically journalistic interviews
