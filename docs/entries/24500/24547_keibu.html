A mid-ranking police officer title in the Japanese police hierarchy, roughly equivalent to 'inspector' in English. Very commonly encountered in crime fiction and detective dramas. Used as a title after a person's name (e.g., 田中(たなか)警部(けいぶ)).

JAPANESE POLICE RANKS (ascending):

  • 巡査(じゅんさ): patrol officer
  • 巡査部長(じゅんさぶちょう): sergeant
  • 警部補(けいぶほ): assistant inspector
  • 警部(けいぶ): inspector
  • 警視(けいし): superintendent

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 警部(けいぶ)補(ほ): assistant inspector (one rank below)
  • 主任(しゅにん)警部(けいぶ): senior inspector
  • 〜警部(けいぶ): Inspector ~ (as title)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Famous fictional inspectors include 古畑(ふるはた)任三郎(にんざぶろう) and 杉下(すぎした)右京(うきょう) from popular detective dramas.
