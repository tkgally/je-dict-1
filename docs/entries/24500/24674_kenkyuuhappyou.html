1. research presentation; academic presentation
A formal presentation of research findings, typically given at academic conferences, seminars, or within a university or research institution. A core activity of academic and scientific life.
来月(らいげつ)の学会(がっかい)で研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)をする。
I am giving a research presentation at next month's conference.
研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)の準備(じゅんび)で毎晩(まいばん)遅(おそ)くまでスライドを作(つく)っている。
I am making slides late every night in preparation for my research presentation.
彼女(かのじょ)の研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)は分(わ)かりやすく、質疑応答(しつぎおうとう)でも的確(てきかく)に答(こた)えていて高(たか)い評価(ひょうか)を受(う)けた。
Her research presentation was easy to follow, and she answered questions accurately during the Q&A, earning high praise.

Composed of 研究(けんきゅう) (research) and 発表(はっぴょう) (presentation, announcement). A fundamental term in academic Japanese, encountered by anyone studying or working at a university in Japan.

USAGE:
Can refer to both oral presentations (talks) and poster presentations at conferences. Also used for presentations within a research lab or seminar class. The term covers the entire process of presenting research, from the talk itself to the materials and preparation involved.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)をする: to give a research presentation
  • 研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)会(かい): research presentation session
  • 研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう)の要旨(ようし): abstract of a research presentation
  • 口頭(こうとう)研究発表(けんきゅうはっぴょう): oral research presentation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発表(はっぴょう): presentation, announcement — the general term; 研究(けんきゅう) specifies the academic context
  • 学会発表(がっかいはっぴょう): conference presentation — specifically at an academic conference
  • 論文(ろんぶん): academic paper, thesis — the written form of research output, as opposed to an oral presentation
  • 卒業研究(そつぎょうけんきゅう): graduation research — undergraduate thesis research, which culminates in a presentation
