1. permanent residence; always being present
The state of living or being present in a place permanently or continuously.
消防士(しょうぼうし)が常住(じょうじゅう)する。
Firefighters are stationed there permanently.
この島(しま)には常住(じょうじゅう)する住民(じゅうみん)が百人(ひゃくにん)ほどいる。
About a hundred residents live permanently on this island.
警備員(けいびいん)が常住(じょうじゅう)しているので安心(あんしん)だ。
It's reassuring because a security guard is always stationed there.
2. permanence; constancy; eternal existence
A Buddhist concept meaning unchanging or eternal existence, as opposed to impermanence.
仏教(ぶっきょう)の常住(じょうじゅう)の概念(がいねん)。
The Buddhist concept of permanence.
万物(ばんぶつ)は常住(じょうじゅう)ではなく無常(むじょう)であると説(と)かれた。
It was taught that all things are impermanent, not eternal.
常住(じょうじゅう)と無常(むじょう)の対比(たいひ)は仏教(ぶっきょう)思想(しそう)の根幹(こんかん)をなす。
The contrast between permanence and impermanence forms the foundation of Buddhist thought.

Has two distinct uses. In everyday language, 常住(じょうじゅう) means living somewhere permanently or being always stationed at a location. In Buddhist philosophy, it refers to the concept of eternal, unchanging existence — the opposite of 無常(むじょう) (impermanence).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 常住(じょうじゅう)する: to reside permanently; to be stationed
  • 常住(じょうじゅう)人口(じんこう): permanent population
  • 常住(じょうじゅう)不滅(ふめつ): eternal and indestructible (Buddhist term)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定住(ていじゅう): permanent settlement — focuses on the act of settling down in a place, while 常住(じょうじゅう) emphasizes continuous presence
  • 常駐(じょうちゅう): being permanently stationed — used for personnel assigned to a location, such as guards or IT staff
  • 無常(むじょう): impermanence — the Buddhist opposite of 常住(じょうじゅう)
