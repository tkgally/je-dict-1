主幹(しゅかん)

しゅかん
noun
chief editor; manager; person in charge
1. chief editor; managing editor; editorial director
The person who oversees and directs the editorial work of a publication such as a newspaper, magazine, or journal.
雑誌(ざっし)の主幹(しゅかん)を務(つと)める。
To serve as the chief editor of a magazine.
彼(かれ)は創刊(そうかん)から長年(ながねん)主幹(しゅかん)として紙面(しめん)を支(ささ)えてきた。
He has supported the publication as chief editor for many years since its founding.
論説(ろんせつ)主幹(しゅかん)が社説(しゃせつ)の方向性(ほうこうせい)を決(き)めている。
The editorial director determines the direction of the editorials.
2. manager; director; person in charge
A senior staff member or executive responsible for managing a project, department, or organization. Used as a job title in government and some companies.
プロジェクトの主幹(しゅかん)に任命(にんめい)された。
I was appointed as the project manager.
主幹(しゅかん)研究員(けんきゅういん)が報告(ほうこく)を行(おこな)った。
The senior researcher gave the report.
県(けん)庁(ちょう)の主幹(しゅかん)クラスの職員(しょくいん)が会議(かいぎ)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
Manager-level staff from the prefectural government office attended the meeting.

Compound of 主(しゅ) ('main; chief') and 幹(かん) ('trunk; core; key person'). Originally meaning the main trunk or pillar of something, the word extended to refer to the person who plays the central role in an organization or publication.

USAGE:
In journalism, 主幹(しゅかん) is a prestigious title, often higher than 編集長(へんしゅうちょう) ('editor-in-chief'). In government, it is a mid-to-senior rank between 課長補佐(かちょうほさ) and 課長(かちょう). Usage varies by organization.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 論説(ろんせつ)主幹(しゅかん): editorial director
  • 主幹(しゅかん)を務(つと)める: to serve as chief editor / manager
  • 主幹(しゅかん)研究員(けんきゅういん): senior researcher
  • 主幹(しゅかん)教諭(きょうゆ): senior teacher (government rank)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 編集長(へんしゅうちょう): editor-in-chief — the more common title for the head of a publication's editing team
  • 主観(しゅかん): subjectivity — homophone with different kanji; completely different meaning
  • 幹事(かんじ): organizer; secretary — a person who arranges events or meetings, not an editorial role
  • 責任者(せきにんしゃ): person in charge — a general term for whoever is responsible
