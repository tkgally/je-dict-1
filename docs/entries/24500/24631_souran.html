1. comprehensive survey; general overview; compendium
A broad, all-encompassing view or reference work that covers an entire subject area. Often used as part of a book title or publication name to indicate comprehensive coverage.
日本(にほん)美術(びじゅつ)総覧(そうらん)。
A Comprehensive Survey of Japanese Art.
地域(ちいき)産業(さんぎょう)総覧(そうらん)を参考(さんこう)にして調(しら)べた。
I researched using a comprehensive guide to regional industries.
この総覧(そうらん)は全国(ぜんこく)の大学(だいがく)・短期大学(たんきだいがく)の情報(じょうほう)を網羅(もうら)しており、受験生(じゅけんせい)にとって欠(か)かせない資料(しりょう)だ。
This compendium covers information on all universities and junior colleges nationwide and is an indispensable resource for exam candidates.

Compound of 総(そう) ('general; total') and 覧(らん) ('view; look over'). Often appears in the titles of reference books, directories, and encyclopedic surveys. The word implies that the work attempts to cover everything within a given field, not merely selected highlights.

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered as part of a publication title. The word can also be used as a suru-verb (総覧(そうらん)する, 'to survey comprehensively'), though this usage is less common. In contemporary usage, the word has a somewhat formal, reference-book feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全国(ぜんこく)大学(だいがく)総覧(そうらん): comprehensive guide to national universities
  • 美術(びじゅつ)総覧(そうらん): comprehensive art survey
  • 産業(さんぎょう)総覧(そうらん): industry compendium
  • 総覧(そうらん)する: to survey comprehensively

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 概覧(がいらん): general overview — a briefer, less comprehensive survey
  • 一覧(いちらん): list; overview — a quick-reference list or table, less detailed than 総覧(そうらん)
  • 便覧(びんらん): handbook; manual — a practical reference for quick consultation
  • 騒乱(そうらん): riot; disturbance — homophone with different kanji; completely unrelated
