1. planting; the act of planting trees and plants
The activity of planting trees, shrubs, and other plants, especially for landscaping, urban greening, or environmental purposes.
公園(こうえん)に新(あたら)しい植栽(しょくさい)を行(おこな)った。
New planting was carried out in the park.
道路(どうろ)沿(ぞ)いの植栽(しょくさい)工事(こうじ)が始(はじ)まった。
Planting work along the road has begun.
季節(きせつ)ごとに植栽(しょくさい)の手入(てい)れをしなければならない。
The planted vegetation needs to be maintained each season.
2. planted vegetation; landscaping plants
The trees, shrubs, and plants that have been planted in a particular area, especially as part of landscaping or urban design.
マンションの植栽(しょくさい)が美(うつく)しい。
The landscaping plants at the apartment complex are beautiful.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)の周(まわ)りに植栽(しょくさい)が施(ほどこ)されている。
Landscaping plants have been installed around the parking lot.
台風(たいふう)で敷地(しきち)内(ない)の植栽(しょくさい)が大(おお)きな被害(ひがい)を受(う)けた。
The landscaping on the property suffered major damage from the typhoon.

Compound of 植(しょく) (plant) + 栽(さい) (planting/cultivation). Used in both the verbal sense (the act of planting) and the nominal sense (the plants themselves). Common in real estate descriptions, municipal planning documents, and neighborhood notices.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 植栽(しょくさい)工事(こうじ): planting/landscaping work
  • 植栽(しょくさい)の手入(てい)れ: maintenance of planted vegetation
  • 植栽(しょくさい)帯(たい): planted strip; green belt
  • 植栽(しょくさい)計画(けいかく): planting plan
  • 街路(がいろ)植栽(しょくさい): street planting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 植樹(しょくじゅ): tree planting — specifically planting trees, often ceremonial
  • 造園(ぞうえん): landscape gardening — the broader design and creation of gardens
  • 緑化(りょくか): greening — making an area greener, often as urban policy
  • 植木(うえき): garden tree; potted plant — individual planted trees or potted plants
