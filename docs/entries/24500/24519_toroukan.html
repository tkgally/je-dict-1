徒労感(とろうかん)

とろうかん
noun
feeling of futility; sense of wasted effort
1. feeling of futility; sense of wasted effort
The emotional sense that one's efforts were pointless or produced no meaningful result.
何(なに)をしても徒労感(とろうかん)しか残(のこ)らない。
No matter what I do, all that remains is a feeling of futility.
長時間(ちょうじかん)働(はたら)いたのに成果(せいか)が出(で)ず、強(つよ)い徒労感(とろうかん)に襲(おそ)われた。
I worked long hours but got no results, and was struck by a strong sense of futility.
何度(なんど)説明(せつめい)しても相手(あいて)に伝(つた)わらず、徒労感(とろうかん)を覚(おぼ)えた。
No matter how many times I explained, the other person didn't understand, and I felt a sense of wasted effort.

A compound of 徒労(とろう) (futile effort, wasted labor) and 感(かん) (feeling, sense). Expresses the emotional weight of having put in effort that came to nothing — heavier and more literary than simply saying 無駄(むだ)だった (it was pointless).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 徒労感(とろうかん)を覚(おぼ)える: to feel a sense of futility
  • 徒労感(とろうかん)に襲(おそ)われる: to be struck by a sense of futility
  • 徒労感(とろうかん)が残(のこ)る: a feeling of futility remains
  • 深(ふか)い徒労感(とろうかん): deep sense of futility

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 虚(むな)しさ: emptiness; a broader feeling of meaninglessness
  • 徒労(とろう): wasted effort (the objective fact, without the emotional component)
  • 脱力感(だつりょくかん): feeling of exhaustion/lethargy — physical rather than emotional
