おどけ者(もの)

おどけもの
noun
joker; clown; jester
1. joker; clown; jester
A person who frequently acts silly or comical to amuse others.
彼(かれ)はクラスのおどけ者(もの)だ。
He is the class clown.
おどけ者(もの)の弟(おとうと)がみんなを笑(わら)わせてくれた。
My joker of a younger brother made everyone laugh.
いつもおどけ者(もの)のように振(ふ)る舞(ま)っている彼(かれ)だが、実(じつ)は誰(だれ)よりも真剣(しんけん)に仕事(しごと)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
He always acts like a clown, but in reality he approaches his work more seriously than anyone.

Derived from the verb おどける (to clown around, to act silly) plus 者(もの) (person). The nuance is generally affectionate or light — describing someone who is naturally funny and entertaining rather than someone being disruptive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おどけ者(もの)のように振(ふ)る舞(ま)う: to act like a clown
  • クラスのおどけ者(もの): the class clown
  • おどけ者(もの)を演(えん)じる: to play the role of the joker

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひょうきん者(もの): funny person; comedian — more colloquial and affectionate
  • 道化師(どうけし): clown; jester — refers to a professional entertainer or performer role
  • お調子者(ちょうしもの): person who gets carried away; show-off — can be more negative
