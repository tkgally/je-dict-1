ふくれっつら

ふくれっつら
noun
sulky face; pout
1. sulky face; pout; pouty expression
A facial expression showing displeasure or sulking, with puffed-out cheeks or a disgruntled look.
ふくれっつらをする。
To make a pouty face.
子供(こども)がふくれっつらで黙(だま)っている。
The child is sitting silently with a sulky face.
お菓子(かし)を買(か)ってもらえなくて、妹(いもうと)はふくれっつらで帰(かえ)ってきた。
My little sister came home pouting because she didn't get any snacks.

Derived from 膨(ふく)れる (to swell; to puff up) + 面(つら) (face, colloquial). The small っ before つら is a sound change typical of compound words. Describes the visual image of someone puffing out their cheeks in displeasure. Often used for children or in a slightly humorous tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ふくれっつらをする: to make a sulky face
  • ふくれっつらで黙(だま)る: to go silent with a pout

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不機嫌(ふきげん)な顔(かお): displeased face — a more neutral, adult description of looking unhappy
  • 膨(ふく)れる: to puff up; to sulk — the verb form that describes the act of sulking itself
  • むくれる: to sulk — colloquial verb with a similar meaning to 膨(ふく)れる in the emotional sense
