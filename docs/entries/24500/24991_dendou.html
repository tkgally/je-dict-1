1. conduction; transmission
The transfer of heat, electricity, or other forms of energy through a material, or the transfer of a stimulus through nerves.
金属(きんぞく)は熱(ねつ)伝導(でんどう)が良(よ)い。
Metals have good heat conduction.
神経(しんけい)伝導(でんどう)の速度(そくど)を測定(そくてい)する。
To measure the speed of nerve conduction.
木材(もくざい)は熱(ねつ)伝導(でんどう)率(りつ)が低(ひく)いため、鍋(なべ)の取(と)っ手(て)によく使(つか)われる。
Because wood has a low thermal conductivity, it is often used for pot handles.

A compound of 伝(でん) (to transmit/convey) and 導(どう) (to lead/conduct). Refers to the physical process by which energy passes through a medium. Not to be confused with the homophones 伝道(でんどう) (religious evangelism), 殿堂(でんどう) (hall of fame), or 電動(でんどう) (electric-powered).

The related verb 伝導(でんどう)する means "to conduct" (energy). The concept is fundamental in physics, appearing in the study of thermal conductivity, electrical conductivity, and nerve signal transmission.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 熱(ねつ)伝導(でんどう): heat conduction; thermal conduction
  • 神経(しんけい)伝導(でんどう): nerve conduction
  • 伝導(でんどう)率(りつ): conductivity rate
  • 伝導(でんどう)体(たい): conductor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電導(でんどう): electrical conduction (specifically electrical)
  • 対流(たいりゅう): convection (heat transfer through fluid movement)
  • 放射(ほうしゃ): radiation (heat transfer through electromagnetic waves)
