1. fulfillment of a long-cherished wish; realization of a dream
The achievement of something one has wished for or worked toward over a long period. A four-character compound with a celebratory and somewhat formal tone.
ついに念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)した。
My long-cherished wish has finally come true.
十年(じゅうねん)かけて念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)し、自分(じぶん)の店(みせ)を開(ひら)くことができた。
After ten years, my dream was fulfilled and I was able to open my own shop.
神社(じんじゃ)には念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)の御礼(おれい)に参拝(さんぱい)する人(ひと)が絶(た)えない。
There is no end of people visiting the shrine to give thanks for the fulfillment of their wishes.

Composed of 念願(ねんがん) (long-cherished wish) and 成就(じょうじゅ) (accomplishment, fulfillment). A classical four-character compound (yojijukugo) with roots in Buddhist terminology, where 成就(じょうじゅ) originally meant the completion of spiritual practice or prayer.

USAGE:
Often used with する to form a verb phrase. Common in contexts involving personal achievements, religious prayers, and celebrations. Appears on 絵馬(えま) (votive plaques) at shrines and temples. The tone is elevated and celebratory.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)する: to have one's wish fulfilled
  • 念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)の御礼(おれい): thanks for a fulfilled wish
  • 念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)を祈(いの)る: to pray for the fulfillment of a wish
  • 念願成就(ねんがんじょうじゅ)のお{守(まも)り}: charm for wish fulfillment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大願成就(たいがんじょうじゅ): fulfillment of a great ambition — a grander, more formal variant emphasizing the scale of the wish
  • 念願(ねんがん): long-cherished wish — the first half of the compound, usable on its own
  • 悲願達成(ひがんたっせい): achievement of a long-held aspiration — similar meaning with a slightly more solemn nuance of overcoming hardship
