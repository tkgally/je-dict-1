1. loading; loading cargo; shipment
the act of loading goods or cargo onto a vehicle, ship, or container
トラックへの荷積(にづ)みが始(はじ)まった。
Loading onto the truck has begun.
荷積(にづ)み作業(さぎょう)は午前中(ごぜんちゅう)に終(お)わる予定(よてい)だ。
The loading work is scheduled to finish by noon.
港(みなと)では大型(おおがた)船舶(せんぱく)への荷積(にづ)みが昼夜(ちゅうや)を問(と)わず行(おこな)われている。
At the port, cargo is loaded onto large ships around the clock.

Composed of 荷(に) (cargo/freight) and 積(づ)み (loading/stacking, from 積(つ)む). Refers specifically to the process of loading goods onto a transport vehicle. The opposite operation is 荷降(にお)ろし (unloading).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荷積(にづ)み作業(さぎょう): loading work
  • 荷積(にづ)みを行(おこな)う: to carry out loading
  • 荷積(にづ)み場所(ばしょ): loading area
  • トラックに荷積(にづ)みする: to load onto a truck

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 積(つ)み込(こ)み: loading, stowing — similar but emphasizes putting items into a container or vehicle
  • 荷降(にお)ろし: unloading — the reverse process of removing cargo
  • 積載(せきさい): loading, carrying — a more formal/technical term for carrying a load

Related Words

Antonym: 荷下(にお)ろし (unloading cargo)
