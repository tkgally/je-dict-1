クーポン券(けん)

くーぽんけん
noun
coupon; voucher; discount ticket
1. coupon; voucher; discount ticket
A ticket or certificate that entitles the holder to a discount, freebie, or special offer, commonly distributed by stores, restaurants, and online services.
クーポン券(けん)を使(つか)いますか。
Would you like to use a coupon?
アプリでクーポン券(けん)を配布(はいふ)しています。
We're distributing coupons through the app.
誕生日(たんじょうび)に届(とど)いたクーポン券(けん)を使(つか)って、ケーキを無料(むりょう)でもらえた。
I used a coupon I received for my birthday and got a cake for free.

Compound of クーポン (coupon, from French/English) and 券(けん) (ticket; certificate). Strictly speaking, クーポン alone already means coupon, so クーポン券(けん) is somewhat redundant, but this full form is extremely common in everyday Japanese, especially for physical paper coupons.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クーポン券(けん)を使(つか)う: to use a coupon
  • クーポン券(けん)を配(くば)る: to hand out coupons
  • 割引(わりびき)クーポン券(けん): discount coupon
  • 食事(しょくじ)クーポン券(けん): meal voucher

USAGE:
In casual speech and digital contexts, the shorter クーポン is increasingly common. The full form クーポン券(けん) is preferred when referring to physical paper coupons or in more formal commercial contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • クーポン: the shorter form, especially common for digital coupons
  • 商品券(しょうひんけん): gift certificate, exchangeable for merchandise
  • 割引券(わりびきけん): discount ticket, more generic term
