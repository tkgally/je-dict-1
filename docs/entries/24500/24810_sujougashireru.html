素性(すじょう)が知(し)れる

すじょうがしれる
expression
one's true background or origin becomes known

one's true background or origin becomes known; to be revealed for what one really is (often negative)
素性(すじょう)が知(し)れない人物(じんぶつ)だ。
That person's background is unknown.
調(しら)べていくうちに、犯人(はんにん)の素性(すじょう)が知(し)れてきた。
As the investigation progressed, the criminal's true identity came to light.
そんなことを言(い)うと、素性(すじょう)が知(し)れるよ。
If you say things like that, people will see what kind of person you really are.

素性(すじょう) means a person's true background, lineage, or origins — often implying something hidden or not immediately apparent. The expression 素性(すじょう)が知(し)れる means that this hidden background becomes visible, often carrying a negative or critical implication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 素性(すじょう)が知(し)れない — of unknown background
  • 素性(すじょう)が知(し)れてくる — one's background comes to light
  • 素性(すじょう)の知(し)れた人(ひと) — a person of known background (trustworthy)
  • 素性(すじょう)を隠(かく)す — to hide one's background

USAGE:
The negative form 素性(すじょう)が知(し)れない is extremely common, describing someone whose origins or character are suspicious or unverifiable. The positive form 素性(すじょう)の知(し)れた implies reliability and trustworthiness.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正体(しょうたい) — true identity (often of someone or something disguised)
  • 身元(みもと) — identity; personal details (more factual/administrative)
  • 出自(しゅつじ) — one's birth or origins (formal, literary)
