黒板消(こくばんけ)し

こくばんけし
noun
blackboard eraser
1. blackboard eraser; chalkboard eraser
A felt-padded tool used to wipe chalk from a blackboard. A standard item in Japanese classrooms.
黒板消(こくばんけ)しで消(け)してください。
Please erase it with the blackboard eraser.
黒板消(こくばんけ)しが汚(よご)れていて綺麗(きれい)に消(け)せない。
The eraser is dirty and I can't erase the board cleanly.
日直(にっちょく)の仕事(しごと)は黒板消(こくばんけ)しを叩(たた)いてチョークの粉(こな)を落(お)とすことだった。
The daily duty student's job was to clap the blackboard erasers together to knock out the chalk dust.

Compound of 黒板(こくばん) (blackboard) + 消(け)し (eraser, from 消(け)す 'to erase'). A familiar item in Japanese schools where chalkboards are still widely used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黒板消(こくばんけ)しで消(け)す: to erase with a blackboard eraser
  • 黒板消(こくばんけ)しを叩(たた)く: to clap erasers (to clean them)
  • 黒板消(こくばんけ)しクリーナー: eraser cleaning machine

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese schools, students take turns as 日直(にっちょく) (daily duty student), and one common task is cleaning the blackboard erasers by clapping them together outdoors. This is a widely shared memory of school life. As a prank, students sometimes balance a 黒板消(こくばんけ)し on top of a partially open door so it falls on whoever enters.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 消(け)しゴム: rubber eraser (for pencil) — for paper, not blackboards
  • 黒板(こくばん): blackboard — the board itself
  • ホワイトボードマーカー消(け)し: whiteboard eraser — for whiteboards, not chalkboards
