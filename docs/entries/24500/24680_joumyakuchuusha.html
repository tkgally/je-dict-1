1. intravenous injection; IV injection
A medical procedure in which medication or fluid is delivered directly into a vein using a needle or catheter. One of the most common medical procedures encountered at hospitals and clinics.
静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)で薬(くすり)を投与(とうよ)した。
The medication was administered by intravenous injection.
脱水症状(だっすいしょうじょう)がひどかったので、静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)で点滴(てんてき)を受(う)けた。
The dehydration was severe, so I received an IV drip through an intravenous injection.
看護師(かんごし)が丁寧(ていねい)に静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)の針(はり)を刺(さ)してくれたので、ほとんど痛(いた)みを感(かん)じなかった。
The nurse carefully inserted the IV needle, so I hardly felt any pain.

Composed of 静脈(じょうみゃく) (vein) and 注射(ちゅうしゃ) (injection). Often abbreviated to 静注(じょうちゅう) in medical settings. The English abbreviation IV (intravenous) is also used in Japanese medical contexts.

USAGE:
A practical medical term that anyone visiting a hospital in Japan may encounter. Distinguished from 皮下注射(ひかちゅうしゃ) (subcutaneous injection) and 筋肉注射(きんにくちゅうしゃ) (intramuscular injection) by the route of administration.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)を打(う)つ: to give an IV injection
  • 静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)を受(う)ける: to receive an IV injection
  • 静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)の針(はり): IV needle
  • 静脈注射(じょうみゃくちゅうしゃ)で投与(とうよ)する: to administer by IV

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 点滴(てんてき): IV drip, infusion — continuous slow delivery of fluid into a vein, as opposed to a single injection
  • 筋肉注射(きんにくちゅうしゃ): intramuscular injection — injection into muscle tissue, common for vaccines
  • 皮下注射(ひかちゅうしゃ): subcutaneous injection — injection under the skin, used for insulin and some vaccines
  • 注射(ちゅうしゃ): injection, shot — the general term for any injection
