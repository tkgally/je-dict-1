普及版(ふきゅうばん)

ふきゅうばん
noun
popular edition; affordable edition; mass-market version
1. popular edition; affordable edition; mass-market version
A lower-priced, widely available edition of a book or publication, designed for broad distribution rather than collectors or specialists. Often smaller in format, with simpler binding.
この辞典(じてん)の普及版(ふきゅうばん)が出(で)た。
A popular edition of this dictionary was released.
学生(がくせい)には普及版(ふきゅうばん)をお勧(すす)めします。
I recommend the affordable edition for students.
豪華版(ごうかばん)は高(たか)すぎたので、後(あと)から発売(はつばい)された普及版(ふきゅうばん)を買(か)った。
The deluxe edition was too expensive, so I bought the popular edition that came out later.

Composed of 普及(ふきゅう) (dissemination/spread) + 版(ばん) (edition). The emphasis is on making content accessible to a wide audience at a reasonable price. Common in publishing for books, dictionaries, and encyclopedias.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 普及版(ふきゅうばん)を出(だ)す: to release a popular edition
  • 普及版(ふきゅうばん)価格(かかく): affordable-edition pricing
  • 新書(しんしょ)普及版(ふきゅうばん): mass-market paperback edition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 豪華版(ごうかばん): deluxe edition — the higher-end counterpart
  • 文庫版(ぶんこばん): pocket paperback edition — specifically the small A6-format paperbacks common in Japan
  • 通常版(つうじょうばん): standard edition — neutral term without the "affordable" connotation
  • 廉価版(れんかばん): bargain edition — explicitly emphasizes low price
