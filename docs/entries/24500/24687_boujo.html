1. pest control; prevention and extermination of pests or diseases
The combined activities of preventing and eliminating harmful insects, animals, weeds, or plant diseases. A standard term in agriculture, forestry, and public health.
害虫(がいちゅう)の防除(ぼうじょ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Pest control is necessary.
農薬(のうやく)を使(つか)わない防除(ぼうじょ)方法(ほうほう)が注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Pest control methods that do not use pesticides are attracting attention.
地域(ちいき)全体(ぜんたい)で協力(きょうりょく)して松枯(まつが)れ病(びょう)の防除(ぼうじょ)に取(と)り組(く)んでいるが、被害(ひがい)の拡大(かくだい)を食(く)い止(と)めるのは難(むずか)しい。
The entire community is working together on pine wilt disease control, but it is difficult to halt the spread of damage.

Composed of 防(ぼう) (prevent, defend) and 除(じょ) (remove, eliminate). Covers both prevention (防(ぼう)) and elimination (除(じょ)) in a single term. Used across agriculture, forestry, and public health.

USAGE:
Frequently encountered in agricultural extension materials, municipal pest control notices, and environmental policy. Can refer to controlling insects, plant diseases, invasive species, or weeds. Often modified by the target: 害虫防除(がいちゅうぼうじょ) (insect pest control), 病害虫防除(びょうがいちゅうぼうじょ) (pest and disease control).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 害虫(がいちゅう)防除(ぼうじょ): insect pest control
  • 防除(ぼうじょ)対策(たいさく): pest control measures
  • 防除(ぼうじょ)を行(おこな)う: to carry out pest control
  • 生物的(せいぶつてき)防除(ぼうじょ): biological pest control

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 駆除(くじょ): extermination — focuses on eliminating pests that are already present, without the preventive aspect
  • 消毒(しょうどく): disinfection, sterilization — killing pathogens on surfaces or in environments
  • 除草(じょそう): weeding — specifically removing unwanted plants
  • 害虫対策(がいちゅうたいさく): pest countermeasures — a broader, less technical term for dealing with pest problems
