連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)

れんしょうきろく
noun
winning streak record; consecutive win record
1. winning streak record; consecutive win record
A record of consecutive victories in sports, games, or competitions. Refers both to an ongoing streak and to the historical record for most consecutive wins.
連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)が止(と)まった。
The winning streak was broken.
連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)を更新(こうしん)し続(つづ)けている。
They continue to extend the winning streak record.
この力士(りきし)は大相撲(おおずもう)の連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)に並(なら)ぶ勢(いきお)いだ。
This wrestler is on pace to match the consecutive win record in professional sumo.

A compound of 連勝(れんしょう) (consecutive victories, winning streak) and 記録(きろく) (record). This is a common term in sports journalism and everyday sports conversation.

In Japanese sports culture, 連勝記録(れんしょうきろく) is a closely watched statistic, particularly in baseball, sumo, and competitive board games like shogi and go. Famous winning streaks become part of sports lore.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)を更新(こうしん)する: to set a new winning streak record
  • 連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)が止(と)まる: for the winning streak to end
  • 連勝記録(れんしょうきろく)に並(なら)ぶ: to tie the winning streak record
  • 歴代(れきだい)連勝記録(れんしょうきろく): all-time winning streak record

RELATED TERMS:

  • 連勝(れんしょう): winning streak, consecutive wins
  • 連敗(れんぱい): losing streak, consecutive losses
  • 連敗記録(れんぱいきろく): consecutive loss record
  • 新記録(しんきろく): new record
