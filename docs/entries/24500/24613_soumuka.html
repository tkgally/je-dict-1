総務課(そうむか)

そうむか
noun
general affairs section; administrative department
1. general affairs section; administrative department
A department within a company or organization that handles administrative tasks such as facilities management, office supplies, internal communications, and other support functions that do not fall under a specific business division.
総務課(そうむか)に問(と)い合(あ)わせた。
I contacted the general affairs section.
備品(びひん)の注文(ちゅうもん)は総務課(そうむか)が担当(たんとう)している。
The general affairs section handles ordering supplies.
来月(らいげつ)から総務課(そうむか)に異動(いどう)になるので、引(ひ)き継(つ)ぎの準備(じゅんび)を始(はじ)めた。
Since I'm transferring to the general affairs section next month, I started preparing for the handover.

Compound of 総務(そうむ) ('general affairs') and 課(か) ('section; division'). This is the standard name for the administrative support section in Japanese companies and government offices. The 総務課(そうむか) typically handles building management, office equipment, internal events, and miscellaneous organizational tasks.

USAGE:
In larger organizations, the equivalent department may be called 総務部(そうむぶ) (using 部(ぶ) 'department' instead of 課(か) 'section'), reflecting a higher organizational level. The distinction between 課(か) and 部(ぶ) depends on company size and structure.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 総務課(そうむか)に配属(はいぞく)される: to be assigned to general affairs
  • 総務課(そうむか)の担当者(たんとうしゃ): the person in charge in general affairs
  • 総務課(そうむか)に届(とど)ける: to submit to general affairs
  • 総務課(そうむか)長(ちょう): section chief of general affairs

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 総務部(そうむぶ): general affairs department — the department-level equivalent; used in larger organizations
  • 人事課(じんじか): human resources section — handles hiring, personnel, and employee management
  • 経理課(けいりか): accounting section — handles financial records and payments
  • 庶務(しょむ): general office work — refers to the work itself rather than the department
