未発表(みはっぴょう)

みはっぴょう
noun
unpublished; unannounced; unreleased
1. unpublished; unannounced; unreleased
Describes something that has not yet been made public, whether a creative work, research findings, official information, or a product. Used as a noun or a pre-noun modifier with の.
未発表(みはっぴょう)の曲(きょく)が見(み)つかった。
An unreleased song was discovered.
この研究(けんきゅう)結果(けっか)はまだ未発表(みはっぴょう)です。
These research findings are still unpublished.
未発表(みはっぴょう)の新(しん)モデルに関(かん)する情報(じょうほう)がリークされた。
Information about an unannounced new model was leaked.

Formed from the prefix 未(み) (not yet) + 発表(はっぴょう) (announcement/publication). The 未(み) prefix indicates that an action has not yet been carried out, giving the word a sense of "pending" rather than permanent absence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 未発表(みはっぴょう)の作品(さくひん): unpublished work
  • 未発表(みはっぴょう)の曲(きょく): unreleased song
  • 未発表(みはっぴょう)の論文(ろんぶん): unpublished paper
  • 未発表(みはっぴょう)の{データ}: unreleased data

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 非公開(ひこうかい): private; not open to the public — emphasizes restricted access rather than timing
  • 未公表(みこうひょう): undisclosed — near-synonym but focuses more on official announcements
  • 未刊(みかん): unpublished (of books) — more literary, specifically about print publication
