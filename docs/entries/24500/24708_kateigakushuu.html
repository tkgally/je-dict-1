家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)

かていがくしゅう
noun
home study; studying at home
1. home study; studying at home; home learning
Academic study done at home, including homework, self-study, and review outside of school. Often discussed in the context of children's education and study habits.
家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)の習慣(しゅうかん)をつける。
To develop a habit of studying at home.
子(こ)どもの家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)を見守(みまも)るのは親(おや)の役割(やくわり)だ。
It's the parent's role to oversee their child's home study.
学校(がっこう)では毎日(まいにち)１時間(じかん)の家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)を推奨(すいしょう)している。
The school recommends one hour of home study every day.

Combines 家庭(かてい) (home, household) and 学習(がくしゅう) (study, learning). A standard term in Japanese education, frequently used by schools, parents, and in educational policy. Schools often distribute 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)ノート (home study notebooks) for students to record their daily study.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)ノート: home study notebook
  • 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)の時間(じかん): home study time
  • 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)の習慣(しゅうかん): home study habit
  • 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう)を推奨(すいしょう)する: to recommend home study

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 宿題(しゅくだい): homework — specific assignments given by school, while 家庭学習(かていがくしゅう) includes voluntary study
  • 自習(じしゅう): self-study — studying independently, can be done anywhere, not limited to home
  • 予習(よしゅう): preparation for class — studying upcoming material before it is taught
  • 復習(ふくしゅう): review — going over previously taught material
