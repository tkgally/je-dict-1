和英辞典(わえいじてん)

わえいじてん
noun
Japanese-English dictionary
1. Japanese-English dictionary
A dictionary that provides English translations and explanations for Japanese words. The counterpart to an English-Japanese dictionary (英和辞典(えいわじてん)).
和英辞典(わえいじてん)で調(しら)べた。
I looked it up in a Japanese-English dictionary.
英作文(えいさくぶん)を書(か)くときは和英辞典(わえいじてん)が役(やく)に立(た)つ。
A Japanese-English dictionary is useful when writing English compositions.
この和英辞典(わえいじてん)は用例(ようれい)が豊富(ほうふ)で、微妙(びみょう)なニュアンスの違(ちが)いもよく分(わ)かる。
This Japanese-English dictionary is rich in usage examples and helps you understand subtle nuance differences.

Composed of 和(わ) (Japanese), 英(えい) (English), and 辞典(じてん) (dictionary). The 和(わ) here is the same prefix seen in 和食(わしょく) (Japanese food) and 和風(わふう) (Japanese style). In the Japanese naming convention for bilingual dictionaries, the source language comes first: 和英(わえい) means Japanese-to-English, while 英和(えいわ) means English-to-Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 和英辞典(わえいじてん)を引(ひ)く: to look up in a J-E dictionary
  • 和英辞典(わえいじてん)で調(しら)べる: to check in a J-E dictionary
  • 電子(でんし)和英辞典(わえいじてん): electronic J-E dictionary
  • オンライン和英辞典(わえいじてん): online J-E dictionary

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 英和辞典(えいわじてん): English-Japanese dictionary — the reverse direction, used when reading English
  • 国語辞典(こくごじてん): Japanese-Japanese dictionary — a monolingual dictionary for native speakers
  • 辞書(じしょ): dictionary — the general, everyday word for any dictionary
  • 辞典(じてん): dictionary — more formal than 辞書(じしょ), often used in the titles of specific published dictionaries
