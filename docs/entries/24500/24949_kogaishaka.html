子会社化(こがいしゃか)

こがいしゃか
noun, verb-suru
subsidiarization; making into a subsidiary
1. subsidiarization; making into a subsidiary
The process of converting a company or business division into a subsidiary, typically through acquisition of a controlling stake or corporate restructuring.
A社(しゃ)がB社(しゃ)を子会社化(こがいしゃか)した。
Company A turned Company B into a subsidiary.
株式(かぶしき)の過半数(かはんすう)を取得(しゅとく)して子会社化(こがいしゃか)する方針(ほうしん)だ。
The plan is to acquire a majority stake and make it a subsidiary.
海外(かいがい)の企業(きぎょう)を子会社化(こがいしゃ���)することで、グローバル展開(てんかい)を加速(かそく)させる狙(ねら)いがある。
By making overseas companies into subsidiaries, they aim to accelerate their global expansion.

Compound of 子会社(こがいしゃ) (subsidiary company) and the suffix 化(か) (turning into; -ization). The suffix ~化(か) is extremely productive in business Japanese for describing transformation processes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完全(かんぜん)子会社化(こがいしゃか): making a wholly-owned subsidiary
  • 子会社化(こがいしゃか)を発表(はっぴょう)する: to announce subsidiarization
  • 子会社化(こがいしゃか)を検討(けんとう)する: to consider making into a subsidiary
  • 買収(ばいしゅう)による子会社化(こがいしゃか): subsidiarization through acquisition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 買収(ばいしゅう): acquisition, the broader act of purchasing a company
  • 合併(がっぺい): merger, combining two companies into one entity
  • 分社化(ぶんしゃか): spin-off, splitting off a division into its own company
  • 吸収合併(きゅうしゅうがっぺい): absorption merger, one company absorbs another
