陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)

りくじょうせんしゅ
noun
track and field athlete
1. track and field athlete; athletics competitor
A person who competes in track and field events such as sprints, distance running, hurdles, jumping, and throwing. Short for 陸上競技選手(りくじょうきょうぎせんしゅ).
彼女(かのじょ)は高校(こうこう)時代(じだい)から陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)だった。
She has been a track and field athlete since high school.
日本(にほん)の陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)がマラソンで金(きん)メダルを獲得(かくとく)した。
A Japanese track and field athlete won the gold medal in the marathon.
陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)にとって怪我(けが)の予防(よぼう)は練習(れんしゅう)と同(おな)じくらい大切(たいせつ)だ。
For track and field athletes, injury prevention is just as important as training.

Compound of 陸上(りくじょう) (track and field; short for 陸上競技(りくじょうきょうぎ)) + 選手(せんしゅ) (athlete; player). 陸上(りくじょう) literally means "on land" but in sports context refers specifically to track and field events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)として活躍(かつやく)する: to excel as a track athlete
  • プロ陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ): professional track athlete
  • 長距離(ちょうきょり)陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ): long-distance runner
  • 陸上選手(りくじょうせんしゅ)の体(からだ)づくり: physical training for track athletes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 短距離(たんきょり)走者(そうしゃ): sprinter — specifically a short-distance runner
  • マラソン選手(せんしゅ): marathon runner — specifically for marathon events
  • 競技者(きょうぎしゃ): competitor; athlete — formal, generic term for any sport
  • アスリート: athlete (loanword) — broader; any sport, increasingly common
