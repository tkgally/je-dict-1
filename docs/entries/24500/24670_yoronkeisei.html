1. formation of public opinion; shaping public opinion
The process by which collective attitudes and beliefs about social or political issues are formed, influenced, or changed. A key concept in media studies and political science.
メディアは世論形成(よろんけいせい)に大(おお)きな影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
The media has a major influence on the formation of public opinion.
SNSの普及(ふきゅう)により、世論形成(よろんけいせい)のスピードが格段(かくだん)に速(はや)くなった。
With the spread of social media, the speed of public opinion formation has dramatically increased.
政府(せいふ)は有識者会議(ゆうしきしゃかいぎ)を通(つう)じて世論形成(よろんけいせい)を図(はか)り、政策(せいさく)への理解(りかい)を得(え)ようとしている。
The government is trying to shape public opinion through expert panels and gain understanding for its policies.

Composed of 世論(よろん) (public opinion) and 形成(けいせい) (formation, shaping). Note that 世論(よろん) can also be read せろん, but よろん is more standard in modern usage.

USAGE:
Appears frequently in discussions about media, politics, advertising, and social movements. The concept is central to understanding how democracies function and how information environments shape collective attitudes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世論形成(よろんけいせい)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える: to influence the formation of public opinion
  • 世論形成(よろんけいせい)を図(はか)る: to aim to shape public opinion
  • 世論形成(よろんけいせい)の過程(かてい): the process of public opinion formation
  • 世論形成(よろんけいせい)に果(は)たす役割(やくわり): the role played in forming public opinion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 世論(よろん): public opinion — the first component on its own; the collective view itself rather than the process of forming it
  • 世論調査(よろんちょうさ): public opinion poll — the measurement of public opinion through surveys
  • 世論操作(よろんそうさ): manipulation of public opinion — has a negative connotation of deliberately misleading the public
  • 合意形成(ごういけいせい): consensus building — focuses on reaching agreement among stakeholders rather than broader public attitudes
