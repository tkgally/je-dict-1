姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)

しせいきょうせい
noun
posture correction
1. posture correction; postural alignment
The practice or process of correcting one's body posture, whether through exercises, devices, chiropractic treatment, or conscious habit changes.
姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)ベルトを買(か)った。
I bought a posture correction belt.
整体(せいたい)で姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)をしてもらった。
I had my posture corrected at a chiropractic clinic.
デスクワークが多(おお)い人(ひと)は、猫背(ねこぜ)になりやすいので、日頃(ひごろ)から姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)を意識(いしき)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
People who do a lot of desk work tend to develop a hunched back, so it's important to be mindful of posture correction in daily life.

Compound of 姿勢(しせい) (posture) and 矯正(きょうせい) (correction; straightening). With the rise of desk jobs and smartphone use, 姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい) has become a popular health topic in Japan. Products like posture belts, cushions, and 整体(せいたい) (chiropractic/bodywork) treatments are widely marketed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)ベルト: posture correction belt
  • 姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)クッション: posture correction cushion
  • 姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)ストレッチ: posture correction stretches
  • 姿勢矯正(しせいきょうせい)椅子(いす): posture correction chair

RELATED TERMS:

  • 猫背(ねこぜ): hunched back, rounded shoulders
  • 整体(せいたい): chiropractic treatment, bodywork
  • 骨盤矯正(こつばんきょうせい): pelvic alignment correction
  • 歯列矯正(しれつきょうせい): orthodontics (same 矯正(きょうせい), different domain)
