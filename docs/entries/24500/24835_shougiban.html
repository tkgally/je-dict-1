将棋盤(しょうぎばん)

しょうぎばん
noun
shogi board; Japanese chess board
1. shogi board; Japanese chess board
the board used to play shogi (Japanese chess), traditionally a thick wooden block with a 9x9 grid
将棋盤(しょうぎばん)に駒(こま)を並(なら)べる。
To line up the pieces on the shogi board.
祖父(そふ)から古(ふる)い将棋盤(しょうぎばん)をもらった。
I received an old shogi board from my grandfather.
高級(こうきゅう)な将棋盤(しょうぎばん)は榧(かや)の木(き)で作(つく)られ、脚(あし)付(つ)きで数十万円(すうじゅうまんえん)するものもある。
High-quality shogi boards are made from kaya wood, and leg-mounted ones can cost hundreds of thousands of yen.

The board used for 将棋(しょうぎ) (Japanese chess). Composed of 将棋(しょうぎ) (shogi) + 盤(ばん) (board). A standard 将棋盤(しょうぎばん) has a 9x9 grid of 81 squares. Professional and formal boards are thick blocks of wood, often made from 榧(かや) (Japanese torreya), and stand on four legs.

TYPES:

  • 脚(あし)付(つ)き将棋盤(しょうぎばん) — leg-mounted shogi board (formal, sits on the floor)
  • 卓上(たくじょう)将棋盤(しょうぎばん) — tabletop shogi board (thinner, placed on a table)
  • 折(お)り畳(たた)み将棋盤(しょうぎばん) — folding shogi board (portable, casual play)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 将棋盤(しょうぎばん)に向(む)かう — to sit down at the shogi board
  • 将棋盤(しょうぎばん)と駒(こま) — shogi board and pieces
  • 将棋盤(しょうぎばん)を挟(はさ)んで — across the shogi board (facing each other)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 碁盤(ごばん) — go board (19x19 grid for the game of go)
  • 盤面(ばんめん) — board position/state (the current arrangement of pieces)
  • チェス盤(ばん) — chess board
