1. restriction; confinement; localization to a limited scope or area
The state of being confined or restricted to a particular area, scope, or range. Often used in medical and technical contexts to describe something that has not spread beyond a limited zone.
被害(ひがい)は一部(いちぶ)の地域(ちいき)に局限(きょくげん)された。
The damage was confined to a limited area.
感染(かんせん)が局限(きょくげん)されている段階(だんかい)で対策(たいさく)を講(こう)じる必要(ひつよう)がある。
Measures need to be taken while the infection is still localized.
議論(ぎろん)を技術的(ぎじゅつてき)な問題(もんだい)に局限(きょくげん)せず、社会的(しゃかいてき)な影響(えいきょう)も考(かんが)えるべきだ。
We should not confine the discussion to technical issues but also consider the social impact.

Composed of 局(きょく) (bureau/limited area) + 限(げん) (limit). Functions both as a noun and as a する verb (局限(きょくげん)する: to confine/restrict). Commonly encountered in medical, military, and academic writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に局限(きょくげん)される: to be confined to...
  • ～に局限(きょくげん)する: to confine/restrict to...
  • 局限(きょくげん)された範囲(はんい): restricted range
  • 局限(きょくげん)性(せい): localized nature

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 極限(きょくげん): extreme limit; utmost — same pronunciation but completely different meaning; refers to the ultimate boundary of capacity
  • 限定(げんてい): limitation; restriction — more common in everyday use, often for products or offers
  • 制限(せいげん): restriction; limitation — broader, used for rules and regulations
