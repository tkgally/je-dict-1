1. treating as one's own property; personal appropriation; privatization for personal benefit
The act of treating something that belongs to an organization, the public, or others as if it were one's personal property. Strongly negative connotation, implying abuse of position or authority.
会社(かいしゃ)の備品(びひん)を私物化(しぶつか)する。
To treat company supplies as one's own.
公共(こうきょう)の施設(しせつ)を私物化(しぶつか)するのは許(ゆる)されない。
It is unacceptable to appropriate public facilities for personal use.
社長(しゃちょう)が会社(かいしゃ)の資金(しきん)を私物化(しぶつか)していたことが発覚(はっかく)し、大(おお)きなスキャンダルになった。
It came to light that the president had been appropriating company funds for personal use, and it became a major scandal.

Composed of 私物(しぶつ) (personal belongings) and 化(か) (transformation into). Describes the act of making something communal or organizational into one's personal property.

USAGE:
Almost always used critically. Common in news, politics, and corporate governance contexts. The word implies that someone in a position of power is abusing their authority by treating shared resources as their own. Often used with する to form a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)の私物化(しぶつか): appropriating company assets
  • 公金(こうきん)の私物化(しぶつか): misappropriation of public funds
  • 権力(けんりょく)の私物化(しぶつか): abuse of power for personal gain
  • 私物化(しぶつか)する: to treat as one's own / to appropriate
  • 組織(そしき)を私物化(しぶつか)する: to treat an organization as one's personal domain

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 横領(おうりょう): embezzlement — a legal term for misappropriating entrusted funds, while 私物化(しぶつか) is broader and includes non-monetary appropriation
  • 着服(ちゃくふく): pocketing; embezzlement — specifically taking money dishonestly
  • 流用(りゅうよう): diversion of funds — redirecting resources to a different purpose, not necessarily personal
