1. historical research; verification of historical accuracy
the process of researching and verifying historical facts, especially to ensure accuracy in creative works
時代(じだい)考証(こうしょう)がしっかりしたドラマだ。
It's a drama with solid period-accurate research.
衣装(いしょう)の考証(こうしょう)に何(なん)ヶ月(かげつ)もかけた。
They spent months on costume research for historical accuracy.
この映画(えいが)は考証(こうしょう)が甘(あま)く、歴史(れきし)ファン(ふぁん)から批判(ひはん)を受(う)けた。
This movie had poor historical accuracy and was criticized by history fans.

Composed of 考(こう) (to think/examine) and 証(しょう) (evidence/proof). Most commonly encountered as 時代(じだい)考証(こうしょう) (period verification), the process of checking historical accuracy in TV dramas, movies, and novels. NHK's 大河(たいが)ドラマ (historical drama series) always credits a 時代(じだい)考証(こうしょう) specialist.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時代(じだい)考証(こうしょう): period-accuracy research
  • 考証(こうしょう)が甘(あま)い: historically inaccurate (lit. "verification is loose")
  • 考証(こうしょう)を行(おこな)う: to conduct historical verification
  • 考証(こうしょう)担当(たんとう): person in charge of historical accuracy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 検証(けんしょう): verification — broader term for any kind of investigation or proof
  • 考察(こうさつ): consideration, analysis — intellectual examination of a topic
  • 実証(じっしょう): empirical proof — proving something through evidence or experiment
