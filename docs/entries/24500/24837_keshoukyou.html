化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう)

けしょうきょう
noun
vanity mirror; makeup mirror
1. vanity mirror; makeup mirror
a mirror designed specifically for applying makeup, often with magnification or built-in lighting
化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう)を覗(のぞ)き込(こ)む。
To peer into a vanity mirror.
ライト付(つ)きの化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう)をプレゼントにもらった。
I received a lighted makeup mirror as a gift.
洗面台(せんめんだい)の上(うえ)に大(おお)きな化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう)が取(と)り付(つ)けてあるので、朝(あさ)の身(み)じたくに便利(べんり)だ。
A large vanity mirror is mounted above the wash basin, which is convenient for getting ready in the morning.

A mirror used for grooming and applying makeup. Composed of 化粧(けしょう) (makeup, cosmetics) + 鏡(きょう) (mirror). Ranges from small portable hand mirrors to large wall-mounted mirrors in bathrooms and dressing rooms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ライト付(つ)き化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう) — lighted makeup mirror
  • 拡大(かくだい)化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう) — magnifying makeup mirror
  • 三面(さんめん)化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう) — three-panel vanity mirror
  • 卓上(たくじょう)化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう) — tabletop vanity mirror
  • 壁掛(かべか)け化粧(けしょう)鏡(きょう) — wall-mounted vanity mirror

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鏡(かがみ) — mirror (general term)
  • 手鏡(てかがみ) — hand mirror
  • 姿見(すがたみ) — full-length mirror
  • 鏡台(きょうだい) — dressing table with mirror
