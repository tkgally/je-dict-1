中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)

ちゅうにくちゅうせい
noun
medium build; average height and weight
1. medium build; average height and weight
A person of average physique, neither particularly tall nor short, neither thin nor heavy.
犯人(はんにん)は中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)の男(おとこ)だった。
The suspect was a man of average build.
彼(かれ)は中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)で、特徴(とくちょう)のない体型(たいけい)だ。
He has an average build with no distinguishing physical features.
目撃者(もくげきしゃ)の証言(しょうげん)によると、容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)で黒(くろ)い帽子(ぼうし)をかぶっていたという。
According to the witness's testimony, the suspect was of medium build and wearing a black hat.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) describing someone whose body type is unremarkable — average in both weight (中肉(ちゅうにく)) and height (中背(ちゅうぜい)). The word 背(せい) here refers to stature or height.

This expression is extremely common in physical descriptions, especially in police reports, news accounts of wanted persons, and character descriptions in fiction. It conveys the idea that the person would not stand out in a crowd.

USAGE:
Typically used as a noun modifying another noun (の construction) or in predicate position with だ/です. Often appears in descriptions where physical appearance matters but nothing distinctive can be identified.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)の男性(だんせい): a man of medium build
  • 中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)の体型(たいけい): an average body type
  • 中肉中背(ちゅうにくちゅうせい)で目立(めだ)たない: average build and inconspicuous

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 普通(ふつう)の体型(たいけい): normal body type (everyday expression, less precise)
  • 小柄(こがら): small build
  • 大柄(おおがら): large build
