町会(ちょうかい)

ちょうかい
noun
neighborhood association; town council
1. neighborhood association; town council
A local community organization at the town or block level that handles neighborhood affairs such as festivals, cleanup activities, and communication with local government.
町会(ちょうかい)に入(はい)る。
To join the neighborhood association.
町会(ちょうかい)の会合(かいごう)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended the neighborhood association meeting.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)してすぐに町会(ちょうかい)の回覧板(かいらんばん)が回(まわ)ってきた。
A neighborhood association circular came around right after I moved in.

A grassroots community organization found throughout Japan, typically organized at the block or neighborhood level. Members share responsibilities such as organizing local festivals, managing garbage collection schedules, and relaying information from the local government.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 町会(ちょうかい)費(ひ): neighborhood association dues
  • 町会(ちょうかい)長(ちょう): neighborhood association president
  • 町会(ちょうかい)の行事(ぎょうじ): neighborhood events
  • 町会(ちょうかい)に加入(かにゅう)する: to join the association

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自治会(じちかい): residents' association — essentially the same organization; the name varies by region, with 町会(ちょうかい) more common in eastern Japan and 自治会(じちかい) in western Japan
  • 町内会(ちょうないかい): neighborhood association — a near-synonym that emphasizes the geographic scope within a particular 町(ちょう)
