提携店(ていけいてん)

ていけいてん
noun
partner store; affiliated shop
1. partner store; affiliated shop
A store or business that has a partnership or affiliation agreement with another company, often offering special discounts, loyalty points, or shared services.
提携店(ていけいてん)でポイントが貯(た)まる。
You can earn points at partner stores.
このカードの提携店(ていけいてん)は全国(ぜんこく)に約(やく)2万(まん)店(てん)ある。
There are about 20,000 partner stores for this card nationwide.
提携店(ていけいてん)で買(か)い物(もの)をすると、割引(わりびき)が適用(てきよう)されるのでお得(とく)だ。
Shopping at partner stores is a good deal because you get a discount applied.

Compound of 提携(ていけい) (partnership; tie-up) and 店(てん) (store; shop). Commonly encountered in credit card programs, loyalty point systems, and corporate partnerships in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 提携店(ていけいてん)で使(つか)える: usable at partner stores
  • 提携店(ていけいてん)の一覧(いちらん): list of affiliated stores
  • 提携店(ていけいてん)を探(さが)す: to search for partner stores
  • 提携店(ていけいてん)募集(ぼしゅう): recruitment of partner stores

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加盟店(かめいてん): member store, often used for franchise or payment network members
  • 系列店(けいれつてん): chain-affiliated store, part of a corporate group
  • 協力店(きょうりょくてん): cooperating store, emphasis on voluntary cooperation
