発信履歴(はっしんりれき)

はっしんりれき
noun
outgoing call history; sent message log
1. outgoing call history; sent message log
A record of calls made or messages sent from a phone or device. A standard feature on mobile phones and communication apps.
発信履歴(はっしんりれき)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the outgoing call history.
発信履歴(はっしんりれき)から相手(あいて)の番号(ばんごう)をもう一度(いちど)かけ直(なお)した。
I called the number again from the outgoing call history.
スマホの発信履歴(はっしんりれき)を見(み)ると、昨日(きのう)の夜(よる)に知(し)らない番号(ばんごう)に何度(なんど)も電話(でんわ)をかけた形跡(けいせき)があった。
Looking at my phone's outgoing call history, there were traces of multiple calls to an unknown number last night.

Composed of 発信(はっしん) (transmission, sending) and 履歴(りれき) (history, log, record). A practical technology term that anyone with a smartphone encounters regularly.

USAGE:
Primarily used in the context of phone calls and messaging. On Japanese smartphones, the call log typically shows 発信履歴(はっしんりれき) (outgoing), 着信履歴(ちゃくしんりれき) (incoming), and 不在着信(ふざいちゃくしん) (missed calls) as separate views.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発信履歴(はっしんりれき)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the outgoing call log
  • 発信履歴(はっしんりれき)を削除(さくじょ)する: to delete the outgoing call history
  • 発信履歴(はっしんりれき)から電話(でんわ)する: to call from the outgoing log
  • 発信履歴(はっしんりれき)の一覧(いちらん): outgoing call list

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着信履歴(ちゃくしんりれき): incoming call history — the record of calls received
  • 通話履歴(つうわりれき): call history — a broader term covering both incoming and outgoing calls
  • 不在着信(ふざいちゃくしん): missed call — a call that was not answered
  • 履歴(りれき): history, log — the general term for any recorded history (browsing history, call history, etc.)
