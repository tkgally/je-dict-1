終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)

しゅうまついりょう
noun
end-of-life care; terminal care
1. end-of-life care; terminal care
medical care focused on comfort and quality of life for patients with terminal illnesses who are nearing death
終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)のあり方(かた)を考(かんが)える。
To think about the way end-of-life care should be.
終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)では患者(かんじゃ)の痛(いた)みを和(やわ)らげることが最(もっと)も重要(じゅうよう)だ。
In end-of-life care, relieving the patient's pain is the most important thing.
高齢化(こうれいか)社会(しゃかい)の進展(しんてん)に伴(ともな)い、終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)に対(たい)する関心(かんしん)が高(たか)まっている。
As the aging society progresses, interest in end-of-life care is growing.

Medical care provided to patients with incurable, terminal conditions, focusing on comfort rather than cure. Composed of 終末(しゅうまつ) (end, final stage) + 医療(いりょう) (medical care). This is a major topic in Japanese society given the rapidly aging population.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)のあり方(かた) — the way end-of-life care should be
  • 終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)を受(う)ける — to receive end-of-life care
  • 終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)に携(たずさ)わる — to be involved in end-of-life care
  • 終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)の現場(げんば) — the end-of-life care setting

KEY CONCEPTS:

  • 緩和(かんわ)ケア — palliative care (focuses on pain relief and comfort)
  • ホスピス — hospice (specialized facility for terminal patients)
  • 尊厳死(そんげんし) — death with dignity
  • 延命(えんめい)治療(ちりょう) — life-prolonging treatment
  • 看取(みと)り — watching over a dying person (attending to someone at the end of life)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ターミナルケア — terminal care (loanword synonym)
  • 緩和(かんわ)ケア — palliative care (broader; not limited to the very end of life)
  • 在宅(ざいたく)医療(いりょう) — home medical care (may overlap with end-of-life care at home)
