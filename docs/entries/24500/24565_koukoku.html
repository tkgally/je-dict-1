1. public notice; official announcement; proclamation
a formal announcement made to the public by a government body, court, or organization
官報(かんぽう)に公告(こうこく)が掲載(けいさい)された。
The public notice was published in the official gazette.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)が競売(けいばい)の公告(こうこく)を出(だ)した。
The court issued a public notice for the auction.
会社(かいしゃ)の解散(かいさん)にあたり、債権者(さいけんしゃ)に対(たい)する公告(こうこく)を行(おこな)わなければならない。
Upon dissolution of the company, a public notice to creditors must be issued.

Composed of 公(こう) (public/official) and 告(こく) (announce/tell). A formal legal and administrative term for official announcements made to the general public. Distinguished from 広告(こうこく) (advertisement), which is a homophone but uses a different first kanji (広(こう) meaning "wide/spread").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公告(こうこく)を出(だ)す: to issue a public notice
  • 公告(こうこく)を掲載(けいさい)する: to publish a public notice
  • 官報(かんぽう)公告(こうこく): notice in the official gazette
  • 決算(けっさん)公告(こうこく): financial statement disclosure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 広告(こうこく): advertisement — commercial promotion, not official notices
  • 告知(こくち): notification — informing someone of something specific
  • 公示(こうじ): official announcement — similar but often used for elections and official displays
  • 布告(ふこく): proclamation — a more authoritative decree, often historical
