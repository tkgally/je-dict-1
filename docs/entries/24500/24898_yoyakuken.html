予約券(よやくけん)

よやくけん
noun
reservation ticket; booking slip
1. reservation ticket; booking slip
A ticket or slip confirming a reservation, such as for a restaurant, event, or product pickup.
予約券(よやくけん)を見(み)せる。
To show a reservation ticket.
受付(うけつけ)で予約券(よやくけん)を提示(ていじ)してください。
Please present your reservation slip at the reception desk.
人気(にんき)のレストランでは予約券(よやくけん)がないと入(はい)れないことがある。
At popular restaurants, you sometimes can't get in without a reservation ticket.

A physical or digital ticket that proves a reservation has been made. Common at hospitals (as a numbered waiting slip), popular restaurants (for walk-in queuing systems), and events. Composed of 予約(よやく) (reservation) + 券(けん) (ticket).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予約券(よやくけん)を取(と)る: to get a reservation ticket
  • 予約券(よやくけん)を発行(はっこう)する: to issue a reservation ticket
  • 予約券(よやくけん)の番号(ばんごう): reservation ticket number

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 整理券(せいりけん): numbered ticket — a queuing ticket distributed in order of arrival, common on buses and at busy venues
  • 予約確認書(よやくかくにんしょ): reservation confirmation — a more formal document confirming a booking, often used for hotels or flights
  • 引換券(ひきかえけん): redemption ticket — a voucher for exchanging or picking up goods
