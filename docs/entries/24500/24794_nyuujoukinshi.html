入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)

にゅうじょうきんし
noun
no admittance
1. no admittance; entry prohibited; a sign or rule forbidding people from entering a venue or area
no admittance; entry prohibited; a sign or rule forbidding people from entering a venue or area
入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)の看板(かんばん)がある。
There is a 'no admittance' sign.
未成年者(みせいねんしゃ)は入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)です。
Minors are not permitted to enter.
工事(こうじ)中(ちゅう)のため、この先(さき)は入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)となっております。
Due to construction work, entry beyond this point is prohibited.

A compound of 入場(にゅうじょう) (entry to a venue) and 禁止(きんし) (prohibition). Commonly seen on signs at construction sites, restricted areas, event venues, and establishments with age or dress code restrictions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)区域(くいき) — restricted entry area
  • 関係者(かんけいしゃ)以外(いがい)入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし) — authorized personnel only
  • 入場(にゅうじょう)禁止(きんし)になる — to be barred from entry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立入禁止(たちいりきんし) — no trespassing (broader; for outdoor areas, not just venues)
  • 出入(でいり)り禁止(きんし) — banned from entering (often a permanent ban on a person)
  • 入場(にゅうじょう)制限(せいげん) — entry restriction (limits rather than a total ban)
