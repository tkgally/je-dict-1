1. contemplation; meditation; spiritual reflection
Deep, focused thought directed at a spiritual or philosophical subject, especially as a form of Buddhist or religious practice.
静(しず)かに観想(かんそう)にふける。
To quietly immerse oneself in contemplation.
僧侶(そうりょ)は仏(ほとけ)の姿(すがた)を心(こころ)に描(えが)き観想(かんそう)を行(おこな)った。
The monk visualized the figure of the Buddha and practiced contemplation.
観想(かんそう)とは単(たん)なる思索(しさく)ではなく、対象(たいしょう)を心(こころ)の中(なか)で鮮明(せんめい)に思(おも)い描(えが)くことで精神的(せいしんてき)な真理(しんり)に近(ちか)づこうとする修行(しゅぎょう)である。
Contemplation is not mere thinking — it is a spiritual practice of vividly visualizing an object in the mind in order to approach spiritual truth.

A compound of 観(かん) (to perceive/contemplate) and 想(そう) (to think/imagine). In Buddhist practice, 観想(かんそう) refers specifically to the meditation technique of visualizing a sacred image, deity, or concept in one's mind. It is a central practice in esoteric Buddhism (密教(みっきょう)).

Not to be confused with the homophones 感想(かんそう) (impression/thoughts about something), 乾燥(かんそう) (dryness), or 完走(かんそう) (completing a race). The suru-verb form 観想(かんそう)する means "to contemplate."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 観想(かんそう)にふける: to be absorbed in contemplation
  • 観想(かんそう)を行(おこな)う: to practice contemplation
  • 観想(かんそう)的(てき): contemplative

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 瞑想(めいそう): meditation (broader term, includes mindfulness practices)
  • 黙想(もくそう): silent meditation; silent reflection
  • 思索(しさく): speculation; deep thought (philosophical rather than religious)
