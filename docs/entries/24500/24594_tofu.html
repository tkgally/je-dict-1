1. application (of ointment, cream, paint, etc.); spreading on a surface
The act of applying a substance (typically medicine, ointment, or cream) to a surface, especially skin. A formal or technical term commonly found in medical instructions and pharmaceutical labeling.
薬(くすり)を患部(かんぶ)に塗布(とふ)する。
Apply the medicine to the affected area.
一日(いちにち)三回(さんかい)、軟膏(なんこう)を塗布(とふ)してください。
Please apply the ointment three times a day.
日焼(ひや)け止(ど)めは外出(がいしゅつ)の三十分前(さんじゅっぷんまえ)に塗布(とふ)するのが効果的(こうかてき)です。
Applying sunscreen thirty minutes before going out is most effective.

A formal term for applying or spreading a substance onto a surface, most commonly used in medical and pharmaceutical contexts. In everyday speech, 塗(ぬ)る is the standard verb for applying or spreading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薬(くすり)を塗布(とふ)する: to apply medicine
  • 軟膏(なんこう)を塗布(とふ)する: to apply ointment
  • 塗布(とふ)量(りょう): amount to apply
  • 塗布(とふ)面(めん): application surface

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 塗(ぬ)る: to paint, to apply — everyday verb for spreading substances on surfaces
  • 散布(さんぷ): spraying, scattering — applying a substance by spraying rather than direct contact
