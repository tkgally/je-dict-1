臓器(ぞうき)移植(いしょく)

ぞうきいしょく
noun
organ transplant
1. organ transplant
a surgical procedure to transfer an organ from a donor to a recipient
臓器(ぞうき)移植(いしょく)を受(う)ける。
To receive an organ transplant.
日本(にほん)では臓器(ぞうき)移植(いしょく)のドナーが不足(ふそく)している。
In Japan, there is a shortage of organ transplant donors.
臓器(ぞうき)移植(いしょく)法(ほう)の改正(かいせい)により、家族(かぞく)の同意(どうい)があれば本人(ほんにん)の意思(いし)表示(ひょうじ)がなくても提供(ていきょう)が可能(かのう)になった。
With the revision of the Organ Transplant Law, donation became possible with family consent even without the individual's prior declaration of intent.

A medical and news term for the surgical transfer of organs from one person to another. Composed of 臓器(ぞうき) (organ) + 移植(いしょく) (transplant). Japan has historically had low rates of organ transplantation compared to Western countries, partly due to cultural attitudes toward brain death and bodily integrity.

  • 臓器(ぞうき)移植(いしょく)待機(たいき)者(しゃ) — person on the transplant waiting list
