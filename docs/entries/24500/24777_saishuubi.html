最終日(さいしゅうび)

さいしゅうび
noun
final day, last day
1. final day, last day
The last day of an event, period, exhibition, sale, or scheduled activity.
今日(きょう)が最終日(さいしゅうび)です。
Today is the last day.
セールの最終日(さいしゅうび)に駆(か)け込(こ)んだ。
I rushed in on the last day of the sale.
展覧会(てんらんかい)の最終日(さいしゅうび)は大勢(おおぜい)の来場者(らいじょうしゃ)で混(こ)み合(あ)っていた。
The exhibition was crowded with many visitors on its final day.

A straightforward compound of 最終(さいしゅう) (final) and 日(び) (day). Very commonly seen on signs, advertisements, and schedules to indicate when something ends.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最終日(さいしゅうび)を迎(むか)える: to reach the final day
  • 開催(かいさい)最終日(さいしゅうび): last day of an event
  • 営業(えいぎょう)最終日(さいしゅうび): last day of business

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最後(さいご)の日(ひ): the last day (more conversational)
  • 千秋楽(せんしゅうらく): final day of a performance run (theater, sumo)
  • 初日(しょにち): first day, opening day (opposite)

Related Words

Antonym: 初日(しょにち) (first day, opening day)
