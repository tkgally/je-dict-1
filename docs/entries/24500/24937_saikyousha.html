最強者(さいきょうしゃ)

さいきょうしゃ
noun
the strongest person; the most powerful
1. the strongest person; the most powerful individual
The person who is the strongest or most powerful in a given group, field, or competition. Frequently used in gaming, martial arts, sports, and fiction.
彼(かれ)がこの大会(たいかい)の最強者(さいきょうしゃ)だ。
He is the strongest in this tournament.
最強者(さいきょうしゃ)を決(き)めるトーナメントが始(はじ)まった。
The tournament to decide the strongest has begun.
歴代(れきだい)の最強者(さいきょうしゃ)たちが一堂(いちどう)に会(かい)する夢(ゆめ)のような大会(たいかい)だった。
It was a dream-like tournament where the strongest competitors of all time gathered in one place.

A compound of 最強(さいきょう) (strongest) and 者(しゃ) (person). This word is heavily used in competitive and entertainment contexts: fighting games, anime, martial arts, and sports discussions.

The concept of 最強者(さいきょうしゃ) is a recurring theme in Japanese popular culture, from battle manga to competitive gaming. Characters described as the 最強者(さいきょうしゃ) often serve as the ultimate challenge or final boss.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最強者(さいきょうしゃ)を決(き)める: to determine the strongest
  • 歴代(れきだい)最強者(さいきょうしゃ): the strongest of all time
  • 真(しん)の最強者(さいきょうしゃ): the true strongest
  • 最強者(さいきょうしゃ)に挑(いど)む: to challenge the strongest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最強(さいきょう): strongest — the adjective/noun without the person suffix
  • 王者(おうじゃ): champion, champion title holder — emphasizes holding a title or throne
  • 覇者(はしゃ): champion, victor — emphasizes dominance through winning a competition
  • 達人(たつじん): master, expert — emphasizes skill and mastery rather than raw power
