A versatile noun derived from the verb 返(かえ)す (to return). Each sense has distinct usage patterns.

SENSE 1 — COMEBACK:
Very common in conversation about comedy (漫才(まんざい), variety shows) and everyday banter. A key skill in Japanese comedy is the 返(かえ)し — the ability to respond to a setup (ボケ) with a sharp retort (ツッコミ).

SENSE 2 — RETURNING:
Often used with the honorific お as お返(かえ)し, especially for return gifts. ホワイトデー (White Day, March 14) is built around the concept of お返(かえ)し — returning Valentine's chocolates.

SENSE 3 — COOKING:
A foundational element of Japanese noodle cuisine. 返(かえ)し is aged for days to mellow the flavors before being combined with dashi.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 返(かえ)しがうまい — good at retorts
  • お返(かえ)しに — in return
  • 本(ほん)返(かえ)し — fully heated base sauce (cooking)
  • 生(なま)返(かえ)し — unheated base sauce (cooking)
