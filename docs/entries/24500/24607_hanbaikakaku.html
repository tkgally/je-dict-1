販売価格(はんばいかかく)

はんばいかかく
noun
selling price; retail price; sale price
1. selling price; retail price; sale price
The price at which a product or service is offered to the end consumer. Distinguished from cost price and wholesale price, and commonly seen in retail, business, and consumer contexts.
販売価格(はんばいかかく)は税込(ぜいこ)み三千円(さんぜんえん)です。
The selling price is 3,000 yen including tax.
原材料(げんざいりょう)の高騰(こうとう)により販売価格(はんばいかかく)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げた。
The selling price was raised due to soaring raw material costs.
メーカーの希望(きぼう)販売価格(はんばいかかく)と実際(じっさい)の店頭(てんとう)価格(かかく)には差(さ)があることが多(おお)い。
There is often a gap between the manufacturer's suggested retail price and the actual in-store price.

The price at which a product or service is sold to the end consumer. Distinguished from the cost price (原価(げんか)) and the wholesale price (卸値(おろしね)). Commonly seen in business contexts, product listings, and retail.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 販売価格(はんばいかかく)を設定(せってい)する: to set the selling price
  • 販売価格(はんばいかかく)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる: to lower the selling price
  • 希望(きぼう)販売価格(はんばいかかく): manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP)
  • 販売価格(はんばいかかく)帯(たい): price range

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定価(ていか): list price, fixed price — the set price, especially for books
  • 原価(げんか): cost price — the cost to produce or acquire
  • 小売(こうり)価格(かかく): retail price — similar meaning, emphasizes retail context
  • 卸値(おろしね): wholesale price — price for bulk/wholesale transactions
