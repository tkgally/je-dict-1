お銚子(ちょうし)

おちょうし
noun
sake flask; sake decanter; tokkuri
1. sake flask; sake decanter
a small vessel used to serve warmed or room-temperature sake, typically narrow-necked and ceramic
お銚子(ちょうし)を一本(いっぽん)ください。
One flask of sake, please.
お銚子(ちょうし)が空(あ)いたので、もう一本(いっぽん)頼(たの)もう。
The sake flask is empty, so let's order another one.
熱燗(あつかん)をお銚子(ちょうし)に注(そそ)いで、ゆっくり晩酌(ばんしゃく)を楽(たの)しんだ。
I poured hot sake into the flask and enjoyed a leisurely evening drink.

The polite form of 銚子(ちょうし), with the honorific prefix お. In modern usage, お銚子(ちょうし) typically refers to the small ceramic flask (technically a とっくり/徳利(とくり)) used to serve sake at restaurants and bars. Historically, 銚子(ちょうし) referred to a long-handled metal vessel used in formal sake ceremonies, but this distinction has been lost in everyday language. Counted with 本(ほん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お銚子(ちょうし)を一本(いっぽん): one flask of sake
  • お銚子(ちょうし)が空(あ)く: the sake flask is empty
  • お銚子(ちょうし)をつける: to warm sake in the flask
  • お銚子(ちょうし)を回(まわ)す: to pass the sake flask around

RELATED TERMS:

  • 徳利(とっくり): tokkuri — the technically correct name for the narrow-necked sake flask
  • 猪口(ちょこ): ochoko — the small cup sake is poured into
  • 熱燗(あつかん): hot sake — sake warmed in a flask
  • 冷酒(れいしゅ): cold sake — sake served chilled
