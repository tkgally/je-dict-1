1. connection; interrelation; correlation; linkage
The mutual connection or structural relationship between two or more things. A formal, academic term frequently used in scholarly writing and research to describe how phenomena are linked.
二(ふた)つの事件(じけん)には連関(れんかん)がある。
There is a connection between the two incidents.
経済(けいざい)と政治(せいじ)は密接(みっせつ)に連関(れんかん)している。
Economics and politics are closely interrelated.
この研究(けんきゅう)は環境問題(かんきょうもんだい)と健康被害(けんこうひがい)の連関(れんかん)を明(あき)らかにしようとしている。
This research aims to clarify the interrelation between environmental problems and health damage.

A formal, academic term for the mutual connection or interrelation between things. Frequently used in scholarly writing, research, and analysis. More formal than 関係(かんけい) or 関連(かんれん), with an emphasis on the mutual or structural nature of the connection.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連関(れんかん)がある: to be connected
  • 連関(れんかん)を示(しめ)す: to show a correlation
  • 密接(みっせつ)な連関(れんかん): close interrelation
  • 相互(そうご)連関(れんかん): mutual interrelation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 関連(かんれん): relation, connection — more common and less formal
  • 関係(かんけい): relationship — broadest and most everyday term
  • 相関(そうかん): correlation — more specifically statistical or quantitative
