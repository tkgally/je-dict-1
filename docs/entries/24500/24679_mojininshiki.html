文字認識(もじにんしき)

もじにんしき
noun
character recognition; OCR
1. character recognition; optical character recognition (OCR)
The technology that enables computers to identify and convert printed or handwritten text into digital data. Widely used in document scanning, translation apps, and automated data entry.
このアプリは文字認識(もじにんしき)機能(きのう)が優(すぐ)れている。
This app has excellent character recognition capabilities.
手書(てが)きの文字認識(もじにんしき)はまだ完璧(かんぺき)ではない。
Handwriting recognition is still not perfect.
AIの進歩(しんぽ)により、文字認識(もじにんしき)の精度(せいど)は飛躍的(ひやくてき)に向上(こうじょう)し、古(ふる)い書類(しょるい)のデジタル化(か)が容易(ようい)になった。
With advances in AI, the accuracy of character recognition has dramatically improved, making it easier to digitize old documents.

Composed of 文字(もじ) (character, letter) and 認識(にんしき) (recognition). Often referred to by the English abbreviation OCR (optical character recognition). Particularly relevant for Japanese text, where the large number of characters (hiragana, katakana, and thousands of kanji) makes recognition technically challenging.

USAGE:
Commonly encountered in technology contexts, smartphone apps (especially translation and dictionary apps that use camera input), and office automation. The technology is especially valued in Japan for converting traditional hanko-stamped paper documents to digital formats.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文字認識(もじにんしき)機能(きのう): character recognition function
  • 文字認識(もじにんしき)の精度(せいど): character recognition accuracy
  • 文字認識(もじにんしき)ソフト: OCR software
  • 手書(てが)き文字認識(もじにんしき): handwriting recognition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • OCR: optical character recognition — the English abbreviation, commonly used even in Japanese technical contexts
  • 音声認識(おんせいにんしき): speech recognition — the audio equivalent, converting spoken language to text
  • 画像認識(がぞうにんしき): image recognition — broader technology that identifies objects in images, not just text
  • 光学式(こうがくしき)文字認識(もじにんしき): optical character recognition — the full Japanese term corresponding to OCR
