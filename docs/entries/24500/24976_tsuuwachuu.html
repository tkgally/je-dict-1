通話中(つうわちゅう)

つうわちゅう
noun
on a call; busy (phone line)
1. on a call; busy; in the middle of a phone call
The state of being engaged in a telephone conversation, or a phone line being in use.
今(いま)、通話中(つうわちゅう)です。
I'm on a call right now.
何度(なんど)かけても通話中(つうわちゅう)でつながらない。
No matter how many times I call, the line is busy and I can't get through.
通話中(つうわちゅう)に別(べつ)の電話(でんわ)が入(はい)ったので、いったん保留(ほりゅう)にして対応(たいおう)した。
Another call came in while I was on the phone, so I put the first one on hold and answered it.

A compound of 通話(つうわ) (telephone call/conversation) and 中(ちゅう) (in the middle of/during). Used both as a status indicator and in conversation to indicate someone is on the phone.

On older phones and landlines, calling a 通話中(つうわちゅう) number produces a busy signal (話中音(わちゅうおん)). On smartphones, the term appears as a screen indicator during active calls. The ~中(ちゅう) suffix is extremely productive and can attach to many nouns to mean "in the process of."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通話中(つうわちゅう)に: while on a call
  • 通話中(つうわちゅう)の表示(ひょうじ): in-call display
  • 通話中(つうわちゅう)でつながらない: can't get through because the line is busy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 話中(わちゅう): busy (phone line) — more technical, used in telecommunications
  • 使用中(しようちゅう): in use (broader, applies to anything being used)
  • 会議中(かいぎちゅう): in a meeting
