台所仕事(だいどころしごと)

だいどころしごと
noun
kitchen work; cooking chores
1. kitchen work; cooking and kitchen chores
Work done in the kitchen, including cooking, washing dishes, cleaning, and food preparation. Refers to the everyday domestic labor of meal-related tasks.
台所仕事(だいどころしごと)は毎日(まいにち)大変(たいへん)だ。
Kitchen work is tough every day.
母(はは)は朝(あさ)から台所仕事(だいどころしごと)に追(お)われている。
My mother has been busy with kitchen work since morning.
共働(ともばたら)きの家庭(かてい)では、台所仕事(だいどころしごと)を夫婦(ふうふ)で分担(ぶんたん)するのが当(あ)たり前(まえ)になってきた。
In dual-income households, it has become normal for couples to share kitchen duties.

Composed of 台所(だいどころ) (kitchen) and 仕事(しごと) (work, task). A compound noun referring to the full range of kitchen-related domestic labor. Has a slightly traditional or homey feel compared to more modern terms like 家事(かじ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 台所仕事(だいどころしごと)をする: to do kitchen work
  • 台所仕事(だいどころしごと)に追(お)われる: to be chased by kitchen duties
  • 台所仕事(だいどころしごと)を手伝(てつだ)う: to help with kitchen work
  • 台所仕事(だいどころしごと)を分担(ぶんたん)する: to share kitchen duties

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 家事(かじ): housework — broader term covering all domestic chores, not just kitchen-related ones
  • 炊事(すいじ): cooking — focuses specifically on the act of cooking meals, not cleaning or preparation
  • 料理(りょうり): cooking, cuisine — the act of preparing food, more focused on the creative/skill aspect
  • 水仕事(みずしごと): water work — kitchen and laundry tasks involving water; often used when discussing chapped hands in winter
