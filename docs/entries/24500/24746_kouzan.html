高山(こうざん)

こうざん
noun
high mountain; alpine mountain
1. high mountain; alpine mountain
A mountain of great height, typically above the tree line. Used in geography, ecology, and mountaineering contexts.
高山(こうざん)に登(のぼ)るのは危険(きけん)だ。
Climbing high mountains is dangerous.
高山(こうざん)植物(しょくぶつ)は厳(きび)しい環境(かんきょう)に適応(てきおう)している。
Alpine plants have adapted to harsh environments.
この地域(ちいき)には標高(ひょうこう)三千メートルを超(こ)える高山(こうざん)がいくつもある。
There are several high mountains exceeding 3,000 meters in altitude in this region.

A compound of 高(こう) (high) and 山(ざん) (mountain). Generally refers to mountains above approximately 2,500 meters in Japan, where the tree line gives way to alpine vegetation. Japan's highest peak, 富士山(ふじさん), stands at 3,776 meters.

Note: The reading こうざん refers to high mountains as a geographical category. The city name 高山(たかやま) in Gifu Prefecture uses a different reading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 高山(こうざん)植物(しょくぶつ): alpine plants
  • 高山(こうざん)病(びょう): altitude sickness
  • 高山(こうざん)地帯(ちたい): alpine zone
  • 高山(こうざん)気候(きこう): alpine climate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 山岳(さんがく): mountains, mountainous terrain — emphasizes rugged mountain landscape rather than height
  • 山(やま): mountain — the general everyday word
