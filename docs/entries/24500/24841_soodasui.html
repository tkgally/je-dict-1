ソーダ水(すい)

そーだすい
noun
soda water; carbonated water; sparkling water
1. soda water; carbonated water; sparkling water
water infused with carbon dioxide gas, producing bubbles
ウイスキーをソーダ水(すい)で割(わ)る。
To mix whisky with soda water.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)にはソーダ水(すい)が気持(きも)ちいい。
Soda water feels refreshing on a hot day.
最近(さいきん)は健康志向(けんこうしこう)でソーダ水(すい)をそのまま飲(の)む人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently, due to health consciousness, more people are drinking soda water straight.

Carbonated water, either plain or used as a mixer. Composed of ソーダ (soda, from English) + 水(すい) (water). In Japan, ソーダ水(すい) specifically refers to plain carbonated water without added sweeteners, distinguishing it from flavored sodas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ソーダ水(すい)で割(わ)る — to dilute/mix with soda water
  • ハイボール — highball (whisky + soda water, extremely popular in Japan)
  • 焼酎(しょうちゅう)のソーダ割(わ)り — shochu mixed with soda water
  • レモンソーダ水(すい) — lemon soda water

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 炭酸水(たんさんすい) — carbonated water (the more common everyday term)
  • 炭酸(たんさん)飲料(いんりょう) — carbonated beverage (broader category including cola, etc.)
  • サイダー — cider (in Japan, means lemon-lime soda, not apple cider)
  • ラムネ — ramune (traditional Japanese marble-sealed soda)
