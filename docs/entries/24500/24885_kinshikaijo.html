1. lifting a ban; removal of a prohibition; unbanning
The act of removing or canceling a previously imposed prohibition or restriction. Used in legal, regulatory, and general contexts.
輸入(ゆにゅう)禁止(きんし)が解除(かいじょ)された。
The import ban was lifted.
渡航(とこう)禁止(きんし)の解除(かいじょ)を求(もと)める声(こえ)が高(たか)まっている。
Calls to lift the travel ban are growing louder.
アカウントの禁止解除(きんしかいじょ)の手続(てつづ)きはどうすればいいですか。
How do I go about getting my account unbanned?

Compound of 禁止(きんし) (prohibition) + 解除(かいじょ) (cancellation/removal). Can be used as a single compound noun or as two separate words (禁止(きんし)を解除(かいじょ)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 禁止(きんし)を解除(かいじょ)する: to lift a ban (verb phrase)
  • 禁止解除(きんしかいじょ)を求(もと)める: to demand the lifting of a ban
  • 輸入(ゆにゅう)禁止解除(きんしかいじょ): lifting of an import ban
  • 全面(ぜんめん)禁止解除(きんしかいじょ): complete lifting of a ban

USAGE:
In modern usage, 禁止解除(きんしかいじょ) also appears in digital contexts such as account unbanning on social media, lifting of content restrictions, and unlocking features in games.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 解禁(かいきん): lifting of a ban — more concise synonym; often used for seasonal bans (fishing, hunting) and product release dates
  • 解除(かいじょ): cancellation; removal — broader term for removing any restriction, not just prohibitions
  • 撤廃(てっぱい): abolition; repeal — stronger; implies permanent removal of a law or system
  • 緩和(かんわ): relaxation; easing — partial loosening of restrictions rather than full removal
