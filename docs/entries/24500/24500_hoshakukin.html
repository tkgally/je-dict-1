保釈金(ほしゃくきん)

ほしゃくきん
noun
bail money; bail bond
1. bail money; bail bond
Money deposited with the court to secure the temporary release of a person awaiting trial.
保釈金(ほしゃくきん)を払(はら)った。
The bail money was paid.
保釈金(ほしゃくきん)は一千万円(いっせんまんえん)に設定(せってい)された。
Bail was set at ten million yen.
被告人(ひこくにん)が裁判(さいばん)に出廷(しゅってい)しなかった場合(ばあい)、保釈金(ほしゃくきん)は没収(ぼっしゅう)される。
If the defendant fails to appear in court, the bail money is forfeited.

A compound of 保釈(ほしゃく) (bail/release on bail) and 金(きん) (money). In Japan, bail is granted much less frequently than in many Western countries. Japan's criminal justice system has a very high conviction rate, and suspects are often detained throughout the investigation and trial process. When bail is granted, the amounts can be substantial — often tens of millions of yen for serious cases.

The 保釈金(ほしゃくきん) is returned if the defendant complies with all bail conditions and appears for trial.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保釈金(ほしゃくきん)を納(おさ)める: to deposit bail money
  • 保釈金(ほしゃくきん)が没収(ぼっしゅう)される: for bail to be forfeited
  • 保釈金(ほしゃくきん)の返還(へんかん): return of bail money

RELATED TERMS:

  • 保釈(ほしゃく): bail; release on bail
  • 保釈(ほしゃく)される: to be released on bail
  • 拘留(こうりゅう): detention
  • 勾留(こうりゅう): pretrial detention
