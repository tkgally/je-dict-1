1. unrewarded; unrecognized; going unappreciated
Describes effort, devotion, or hard work that does not receive the recognition or reward it deserves. The negative passive form of 報(むく)いる (to reward).
報(むく)われない努力(どりょく)はない。
No effort goes unrewarded.
どんなに頑張(がんば)っても報(むく)われない気(き)がする。
No matter how hard I try, I feel like it goes unrecognized.
長年(ながねん)の献身(けんしん)が報(むく)われない状況(じょうきょう)に彼女(かのじょ)は疲(つか)れていた。
She was tired of a situation where years of devotion went unappreciated.
2. futile; in vain; fruitless
Describes actions or attempts that produce no positive result, emphasizing the emotional sense of wasted effort.
報(むく)われない恋(こい)ほど辛(つら)いものはない。
There is nothing more painful than unrequited love.
報(むく)われないとわかっていても挑戦(ちょうせん)し続(つづ)けた。
Even knowing it was futile, I kept on trying.
報(むく)われない片思(かたおも)いを何年(なんねん)も続(つづ)けるのはやめたほうがいい。
You should stop carrying on with unrequited feelings for years.

Negative passive form of 報(むく)いる (to reward/repay). While grammatically derived, 報(むく)われない is extremely common as a set expression and functions almost as an independent adjective, especially in the collocation 報(むく)われない努力(どりょく).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 報(むく)われない努力(どりょく): unrewarded effort
  • 報(むく)われない恋(こい): unrequited love
  • 報(むく)われない思(おも)い: unreciprocated feelings
  • 報(むく)われない苦労(くろう): unappreciated hardship

USAGE:
Often used with the positive counterpart 報(むく)われる (to be rewarded) in motivational contexts: 努力(どりょく)は必(かなら)ず報(むく)われる (effort is always rewarded). The negative form carries a tone of resignation or emotional pain.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無駄(むだ)な: futile, useless — neutral and objective; lacks the emotional weight of unrecognized sacrifice
  • 徒労(とろう): wasted effort — formal; emphasizes the labor being pointless
  • 甲斐(かい)がない: not worth it, unrewarding — focuses on the lack of benefit from an action
