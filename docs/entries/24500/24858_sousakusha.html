創作者(そうさくしゃ)

そうさくしゃ
noun
creator; creative artist; author
1. creator; creative artist; originator of creative work
A person who creates original works, particularly in literature, art, music, or other creative fields. Emphasizes the act of original creation rather than mere production.
創作者(そうさくしゃ)の権利(けんり)を守(まも)る。
To protect the rights of creators.
AIの時代(じだい)でも創作者(そうさくしゃ)の役割(やくわり)は変(か)わらない。
Even in the age of AI, the role of creators remains unchanged.
この展覧会(てんらんかい)では若手(わかて)創作者(そうさくしゃ)による新(あたら)しい表現(ひょうげん)が紹介(しょうかい)されている。
This exhibition showcases new forms of expression by young creators.

Composed of 創作(そうさく) (creative work; creation) + 者(しゃ) (person). The emphasis is on originality and creative imagination, distinguishing it from words like 製作者(せいさくしゃ) (producer/manufacturer).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 若手(わかて)創作者(そうさくしゃ): young creator
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)の権利(けんり): creator's rights
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)支援(しえん): support for creators
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)側(がわ): from the creator's perspective

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 作者(さくしゃ): author; writer — focuses on a specific finished work
  • 製作者(せいさくしゃ): producer; manufacturer — emphasizes the production process rather than creative originality
  • クリエイター: creator — the English loanword, commonly used in digital and media industries
  • 作家(さっか): writer; novelist — specifically a professional writer of literature
