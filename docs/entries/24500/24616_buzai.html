部材(ぶざい)

ぶざい
noun
structural member; building material; component
1. structural member; building material; component
A piece of material that forms part of a larger structure or product. Used in construction, engineering, and manufacturing to refer to individual components such as beams, panels, or frames.
部材(ぶざい)を組(く)み立(た)てる。
To assemble the components.
木造(もくぞう)住宅(じゅうたく)の部材(ぶざい)は工場(こうじょう)で加工(かこう)される。
The structural members for wooden houses are processed at a factory.
老朽化(ろうきゅうか)した橋(はし)の部材(ぶざい)を一(ひと)つずつ点検(てんけん)して、交換(こうかん)が必要(ひつよう)な箇所(かしょ)を特定(とくてい)した。
We inspected the aging bridge's structural members one by one and identified the parts that needed replacement.

Compound of 部(ぶ) ('part; section') and 材(ざい) ('material; timber'). Refers to individual pieces that are combined to form a structure or product. The word sits between raw materials and finished products — a 部材(ぶざい) has already been shaped or prepared but has not yet been assembled into the final structure.

USAGE:
Common in construction, architecture, and manufacturing contexts. Often appears in technical documents, building regulations, and product catalogs. Can refer to materials of any kind — wood, steel, concrete, plastic — as long as they serve as components of a larger assembly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部材(ぶざい)を組(く)み立(た)てる: to assemble components
  • 部材(ぶざい)を加工(かこう)する: to process materials
  • 鉄骨(てっこつ)部材(ぶざい): steel structural members
  • 木(もく)部材(ぶざい): wooden components
  • 部材(ぶざい)の強度(きょうど): strength of the member

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 部品(ぶひん): parts — individual components, especially of machines and devices; more common in manufacturing and electronics
  • 資材(しざい): materials; supplies — raw or processed materials before they become specific components
  • 建材(けんざい): building materials — specifically for construction, a subset of 部材(ぶざい)
  • 素材(そざい): raw material — the unprocessed material before it is shaped into a component
