追悼式(ついとうしき)

ついとうしき
noun
memorial service; ceremony held to honor and remember the deceased

On the tenth anniversary of the earthquake, memorial services were held in various locations.

A formal ceremony to mourn and honor someone who has died, often held on anniversaries of a person's death or of disasters. Distinct from a funeral (葬儀(そうぎ)), which takes place shortly after death — a 追悼式(ついとうしき) can be held at any later date.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 追悼式(ついとうしき)を行(おこな)う — to hold a memorial service
  • 追悼式(ついとうしき)に参列(さんれつ)する — to attend a memorial service
  • 合同(ごうどう)追悼式(ついとうしき) — joint memorial service

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追悼(ついとう) — mourning; remembrance (the act itself, without 式(しき))
  • 慰霊祭(いれいさい) — memorial rite (often for war dead or spirits)
  • 法事(ほうじ) — Buddhist memorial service (family-centered, on specific anniversaries)
