梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)

つゆぜんせん
noun
seasonal rain front; baiu front
1. seasonal rain front; baiu front; rainy season front
The stationary weather front that brings Japan's rainy season, typically stretching across the country from June to mid-July as warm and cool air masses collide.
梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)が停滞(ていたい)している。
The rainy season front is stagnating.
梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)の影響(えいきょう)で西日本(にしにほん)は大雨(おおあめ)になる見込(みこ)みだ。
Due to the influence of the rainy season front, western Japan is expected to see heavy rain.
梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)が北上(ほくじょう)し、関東(かんとう)地方(ちほう)も来週(らいしゅう)には梅雨入(つゆい)りとなる見通(みとお)しだ。
The rainy season front is moving northward, and the Kanto region is expected to enter the rainy season next week.

A compound of 梅雨(つゆ) (the rainy season) and 前線(ぜんせん) (weather front). This is a key meteorological term that appears constantly in Japanese weather forecasts from late May through July.

The 梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん) forms where the warm, moist air mass from the Pacific (小笠原気団(おがさわらきだん)) meets the cool air from the north. It typically begins affecting Okinawa in May, gradually moves northward through Kyushu, Shikoku, and Honshu during June, and reaches the Tohoku region in July. When the front finally moves off the coast, it marks the end of the rainy season (梅雨明(つゆあ)け).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)が停滞(ていたい)する: the rainy season front stagnates
  • 梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)が活発化(かっぱつか)する: the rainy season front becomes active
  • 梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)の影響(えいきょう): influence of the rainy season front
  • 梅雨前線(つゆぜんせん)が北上(ほくじょう)する: the rainy season front moves northward

RELATED TERMS:

  • 梅雨(つゆ): rainy season
  • 梅雨入(つゆい)り: start of the rainy season
  • 梅雨明(つゆあ)け: end of the rainy season
  • 前線(ぜんせん): weather front
