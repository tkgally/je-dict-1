滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)

たいざいにっすう
noun
length of stay (in days)
1. length of stay (in days); the number of days spent staying at a place, especially for travel or immigration purposes
length of stay (in days); the number of days spent staying at a place, especially for travel or immigration purposes
滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)は何日(なんにち)ですか。
How many days is your stay?
ビザなしの滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)は最大(さいだい)九十(きゅうじゅう)日(にち)です。
The maximum stay without a visa is ninety days.
旅行(りょこう)の滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)に合(あ)わせて荷物(にもつ)の量(りょう)を調整(ちょうせい)した。
I adjusted the amount of luggage to match the length of my trip.

A practical compound used in travel, immigration, and hospitality contexts. Composed of 滞在(たいざい) (stay/sojourn) and 日数(にっすう) (number of days). This is one of the first things asked at immigration or when booking accommodations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)を延長(えんちょう)する — to extend one's stay
  • 最大(さいだい)滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう) — maximum length of stay
  • 平均(へいきん)滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう) — average length of stay
  • 滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう)を超(こ)える — to exceed the allowed stay

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 在留(ざいりゅう)期間(きかん) — period of stay (formal/immigration term; measured in months or years)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)日数(にっすう) — number of nights staying (hotel context; counted differently from 滞在(たいざい)日数(にっすう))
  • 滞在(たいざい)期間(きかん) — period of stay (interchangeable but 期間(きかん) can also cover weeks/months)
