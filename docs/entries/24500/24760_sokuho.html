速歩(そくほ)

そくほ
noun
fast walking; brisk walk; trot
1. fast walking; brisk walk
Walking at a speed faster than normal pace, often as a form of exercise.
毎朝(まいあさ)速歩(そくほ)で通勤(つうきん)している。
I commute by brisk walking every morning.
健康(けんこう)のために一日(いちにち)三十分(さんじゅっぷん)の速歩(そくほ)が推奨(すいしょう)されている。
Thirty minutes of brisk walking per day is recommended for health.
速歩(そくほ)とゆっくりした歩行(ほこう)を交互(こうご)に繰(く)り返(かえ)すインターバル速歩(そくほ)が注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Interval walking, which alternates between brisk walking and slow walking, is gaining attention.
2. trot (of a horse)
A gait of a horse between a walk and a canter, in which diagonal pairs of legs move together.
馬(うま)が速歩(そくほ)で走(はし)っている。
The horse is trotting.
乗馬(じょうば)レッスンでは、まず常歩(なみあし)を覚(おぼ)えてから速歩(そくほ)に進(すす)む。
In horseback riding lessons, you first learn the walk before progressing to the trot.
速歩(そくほ)のとき、騎手(きしゅ)は馬(うま)のリズムに合(あ)わせて体(からだ)を上下(じょうげ)させる。
During the trot, the rider moves their body up and down in rhythm with the horse.

A compound of 速(そく) (fast, speed) and 歩(ほ) (walk, step). Has two main domains of use: exercise/fitness and equestrian.

In the fitness context, 速歩(そくほ) refers to power walking or brisk walking, which has become popular as a moderate-intensity exercise. "Interval 速歩(そくほ)" is a well-known Japanese exercise method developed at Shinshu University.

In equestrian terminology, 速歩(そくほ) is the standard Japanese term for the trot, one of the basic horse gaits.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • インターバル速歩(そくほ): interval walking (exercise method)
  • 速歩(そくほ)で歩(ある)く: to walk briskly
  • 速歩(そくほ)訓練(くんれん): trot training (equestrian)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 早足(はやあし): fast pace, quick step — everyday word for walking quickly
  • ウォーキング: walking (as exercise) — loanword commonly used for fitness walking
  • 常歩(なみあし): walk (horse gait) — slower than a trot
