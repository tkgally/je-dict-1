1. proportional division; apportionment; prorating
Dividing or distributing something—typically money, costs, or quantities—proportionally according to a set ratio or standard.
費用(ひよう)を按分(あんぶん)する。
To prorate the costs.
家賃(やちん)は使用面積(しようめんせき)に応(おう)じて按分(あんぶん)される。
The rent is apportioned according to the floor area used.
自宅(じたく)を仕事(しごと)にも使(つか)っている場合(ばあい)、光熱費(こうねつひ)を按分(あんぶん)して経費(けいひ)に計上(けいじょう)できる。
If you also use your home for work, you can prorate utility costs and claim them as business expenses.

A practical term frequently encountered in accounting, tax filing, and shared-cost situations. The word implies a fair, mathematically proportional split rather than an equal division.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 按分(あんぶん)比率(ひりつ): apportionment ratio
  • 按分(あんぶん)計算(けいさん): prorating calculation
  • 経費(けいひ)を按分(あんぶん)する: to prorate expenses
  • 面積(めんせき)按分(あんぶん): area-based apportionment
  • 按分(あんぶん)票(ひょう): apportioned votes (in elections)

KANJI NOTE:
Also written as 案分(あんぶん) with a different first character. Both spellings are widely used, though 按分(あんぶん) is more common in tax and accounting contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配分(はいぶん): distribution, allocation — dividing something among parties, but not necessarily proportionally
  • 分配(ぶんぱい): distribution, sharing — general term for dividing and giving out
  • 割(わ)り勘(かん): splitting the bill — specifically for dividing a restaurant or bar bill equally
