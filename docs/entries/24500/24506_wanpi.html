ワンピ

わんぴ
noun
dress; one-piece dress
1. dress; one-piece dress
An informal abbreviation of ワンピース (one-piece), referring to a dress — a single garment covering the torso and extending down over the legs.
新(あたら)しいワンピを買(か)った。
I bought a new dress.
今日(きょう)は花柄(はながら)のワンピにした。
I went with a floral dress today.
デートだからかわいいワンピを着(き)て行(い)こうと思(おも)う。
Since it's a date, I'm thinking of wearing a cute dress.

A clipped form of ワンピース (one-piece), itself a Japanese-English word that refers specifically to dresses. In English, "one-piece" typically means a swimsuit, but in Japanese fashion, ワンピース and its abbreviation ワンピ always refer to dresses.

This abbreviation is casual and used mainly among young women in everyday conversation and fashion contexts. The full form ワンピース is used in more neutral or written contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ワンピを着(き)る: to wear a dress
  • 夏(なつ)ワンピ: summer dress
  • 花柄(はながら)ワンピ: floral dress
  • ワンピコーデ: dress outfit/coordination

RELATED TERMS:

  • ワンピース: one-piece; dress (full form)
  • ドレス: dress (loanword; often implies more formal attire)
  • スカート: skirt
