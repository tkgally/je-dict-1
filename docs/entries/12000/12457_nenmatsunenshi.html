年末年始(ねんまつねんし)

ねんまつねんし
noun
year-end and New Year period
1. year-end and New Year period
The period spanning late December through early January, covering both the end of the old year and the beginning of the new year. A major holiday season in Japan when most businesses close.
年末年始(ねんまつねんし)は実家(じっか)で過(す)ごす。
I spend the year-end and New Year period at my parents' house.
年末年始(ねんまつねんし)の休(やす)みは12月(がつ)29日(にち)から1月(がつ)3日(か)までです。
The year-end and New Year holiday is from December 29 to January 3.
年末年始(ねんまつねんし)は帰省(きせい)ラッシュで新幹線(しんかんせん)も高速道路(こうそくどうろ)も混雑(こんざつ)する。
During the year-end and New Year period, the bullet trains and expressways are crowded due to the rush of people returning home.

USAGE:
A compound of 年末(ねんまつ) (year-end) and 年始(ねんし) (beginning of the year). The typical 年末年始(ねんまつねんし) holiday for companies and government offices runs from December 29 to January 3. This is one of the three major holiday periods in Japan, along with Golden Week and Obon. Major activities include 大掃除(おおそうじ) (year-end cleaning), 年賀状(ねんがじょう) (New Year's cards), and 初詣(はつもうで) (first shrine visit).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年末年始(ねんまつねんし)の休(やす)み: year-end/New Year holiday
  • 年末年始(ねんまつねんし)休業(きゅうぎょう): closed for year-end/New Year
  • 年末年始(ねんまつねんし)を過(す)ごす: to spend the year-end/New Year period
  • 年末年始(ねんまつねんし)の営業(えいぎょう): year-end/New Year business hours
