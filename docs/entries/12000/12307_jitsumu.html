実務(じつむ)

じつむ
noun
practical work, actual business
1. practical work, actual business, hands-on work
The day-to-day practical tasks and operations of a job or organization, as opposed to planning, management, or theory.
実務(じつむ)経験(けいけん)が求(もと)められる。
Practical work experience is required.
実務(じつむ)を担当(たんとう)するのは若手(わかて)の社員(しゃいん)だ。
The junior employees are in charge of the practical work.
理論(りろん)だけでなく実務(じつむ)に強(つよ)い人材(じんざい)を育(そだ)てたい。
We want to develop people who are strong not just in theory but also in practical work.

実務(じつむ) combines 実(じつ) (actual, real) and 務(む) (duty, work).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実務(じつむ)経験(けいけん) (practical work experience)
  • 実務(じつむ)者(しゃ) (practitioner, person in charge of operations)
  • 実務(じつむ)能力(のうりょく) (practical ability)
  • 実務(じつむ)レベル (operational level)
  • 実務(じつむ)担当(たんとう) (person handling the actual work)

USAGE:
Commonly used in business and professional contexts. Often contrasted with 理論(りろん) (theory) or 管理(かんり) (management) to emphasize hands-on, operational work.
