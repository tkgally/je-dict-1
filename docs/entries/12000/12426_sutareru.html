廃(すた)れる

すたれる
ichidan verb
to fall into disuse, to decline
1. to fall into disuse, to go out of fashion, to decline, to become obsolete
For customs, practices, fashions, or things to gradually lose popularity or relevance and eventually fall out of use.
その風習(ふうしゅう)は廃(すた)れてしまった。
That custom has fallen into disuse.
かつて流行(りゅうこう)った言葉(ことば)も今(いま)では廃(すた)れている。
Words that were once trendy have now gone out of fashion.
伝統(でんとう)工芸(こうげい)が廃(すた)れないように、若(わか)い世代(せだい)に技術(ぎじゅつ)を伝(つた)える取(と)り組(く)みが進(すす)められている。
Efforts are being made to pass on skills to the younger generation so that traditional crafts do not die out.

CONJUGATION:
Ichidan verb: 廃(すた)れない / 廃(すた)れます / 廃(すた)れる / 廃(すた)れれば / 廃(すた)れた.

USAGE:
廃(すた)れる describes a gradual process of something losing its vitality, relevance, or popularity. It is commonly used for customs (風習(ふうしゅう)), traditions (伝統(でんとう)), fashions (流行(りゅうこう)), and practices. The nuance is of natural decline rather than active abolition (which would be 廃止(はいし)する). Often used in discussions about cultural preservation and changing times.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 風習(ふうしゅう)が廃(すた)れる: a custom falls into disuse
  • 伝統(でんとう)が廃(すた)れる: a tradition declines
  • 廃(すた)れた技術(ぎじゅつ): obsolete technology
  • 廃(すた)れることなく: without declining
