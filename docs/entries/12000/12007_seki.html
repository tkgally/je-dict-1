咳(せき)

せき
noun
cough
See also: 席(せき) (seat, place)
1. cough, coughing
The act of coughing, or the sound produced by coughing. A common symptom of colds and respiratory illness.
咳(せき)が出(で)る。
I have a cough.
咳(せき)が止(と)まらなくて辛(つら)い。
My cough won't stop and it's miserable.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で咳(せき)をするときは、マスクをするか手(て)で口(くち)を覆(おお)いましょう。
When coughing on the train, wear a mask or cover your mouth with your hand.

咳(せき) refers to the act or sound of coughing. A common symptom discussed when talking about colds and illness.

KEY EXPRESSIONS:

  • 咳(せき)が出(で)る: to have a cough (literally "a cough comes out" — the standard way to say it)
  • 咳(せき)をする: to cough (deliberate action)
  • 咳(せき)が止(と)まらない: can't stop coughing

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 咳(せき)止(ど)め: cough medicine/suppressant
  • 空咳(からぜき): dry cough (no phlegm)
  • 咳(せき)エチケット: cough etiquette (covering mouth, wearing mask)
  • 咳払(せきばら)い: clearing one's throat (often to get attention)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, wearing a mask when coughing in public is strongly expected as a matter of etiquette (咳(せき)エチケット). Not doing so can draw disapproval.

HOMOPHONE:
Do not confuse with 席(せき) (seat/place) — same reading, different kanji.
