好(す)き勝手(かって)

すきかって
na-adjective / noun
selfish, doing as one pleases
1. selfish, doing as one pleases, self-indulgent
Acting according to one's own desires without considering others. Always carries a negative nuance of selfishness or inconsiderate behavior.
好(す)き勝手(かって)なことを言(い)う。
To say whatever one pleases.
子(こ)どもに好(す)き勝手(かって)にさせてはいけない。
You shouldn't let children do whatever they want.
みんなが好(す)き勝手(かって)に行動(こうどう)したら、チームはまとまらない。
If everyone acts selfishly, the team won't come together.

USAGE:
Used as a な-adjective (好(す)き勝手(かって)な + noun) or adverbially (好(す)き勝手(かって)に + verb). Always negative — it implies the person is being inconsiderate or disregarding rules and others' feelings.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 好(す)き勝手(かって)なことを言(い)う: to say whatever one pleases
  • 好(す)き勝手(かって)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う: to behave selfishly
  • 好(す)き勝手(かって)にやる: to do as one likes

SIMILAR WORDS:
勝手(かって) alone can mean "selfish" or "at one's convenience." 好(す)き勝手(かって) intensifies this by adding 好(す)き (liking), emphasizing that the person is doing exactly what they want.
