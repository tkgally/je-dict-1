存分(ぞんぶん)

ぞんぶん
na-adjective / adverb
to one's heart's content, fully, as much as one likes
1. to one's heart's content, fully, as much as one likes
Doing something without restraint or limit, enjoying or engaging in an activity as much as one wishes. Conveys a sense of freedom and satisfaction in being unrestricted.
存分(ぞんぶん)に楽(たの)しんでください。
Please enjoy yourself to your heart's content.
休(やす)みの日(ひ)は存分(ぞんぶん)に寝(ね)たい。
On my days off, I want to sleep as much as I like.
才能(さいのう)を存分(ぞんぶん)に発揮(はっき)して、見事(みごと)な成績(せいせき)を収(おさ)めた。
By fully demonstrating their talent, they achieved splendid results.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy to one's heart's content
  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に発揮(はっき)する: to fully demonstrate/display
  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に味(あじ)わう: to savor to one's fill
  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう: to display one's skills fully

USAGE:
Most commonly used adverbially with に: 存分(ぞんぶん)に. Often appears in encouraging or inviting contexts ("go ahead and enjoy") and in descriptions of unrestricted action.

SIMILAR WORDS:
思(おも)う存分(ぞんぶん) is a common intensified form meaning "to one's heart's content," emphasizing doing exactly as one wishes without holding back.
