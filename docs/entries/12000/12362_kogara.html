小柄(こがら)

こがら
adjective (na), noun
small build, petite
1. small build, petite, small-framed
Having a small body frame. Describes a person who is short and slight in build. Generally used as a neutral or gentle description, not rude.
彼女(かのじょ)は小柄(こがら)な女性(じょせい)だ。
She is a petite woman.
小柄(こがら)だが力(ちから)が強(つよ)い。
He is small but strong.
小柄(こがら)な体(からだ)を生(い)かして、狭(せま)い場所(ばしょ)でもすばやく動(うご)ける。
Using her small frame to advantage, she can move quickly even in tight spaces.

USAGE:
小柄(こがら) is a polite and neutral way to describe someone who is small in stature. It is not rude or offensive. It is the opposite of 大柄(おおがら) (large build, big-framed).

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小柄(こがら)な人(ひと) — a small-framed person
  • 小柄(こがら)な体(からだ) — a small body
  • 小柄(こがら)な女性(じょせい) — a petite woman

SIMILAR WORDS:
背(せ)が低(ひく)い simply means short in height. 小柄(こがら) describes the overall frame being small, not just height. 華奢(きゃしゃ) means slender and delicate, emphasizing fragility rather than just small size.
