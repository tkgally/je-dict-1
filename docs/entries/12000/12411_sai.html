差異(さい)

さい
noun
difference, discrepancy, disparity
1. difference, discrepancy, disparity
A noticeable difference or gap between two or more things. More formal than 違い and often used in academic, analytical, or official contexts.
文化(ぶんか)の差異(さい)を認(みと)める。
To acknowledge cultural differences.
両者(りょうしゃ)の間(あいだ)には大(おお)きな差異(さい)がある。
There is a significant difference between the two.
調査(ちょうさ)の結果(けっか)、地域(ちいき)によって教育(きょういく)水準(すいじゅん)に大(おお)きな差異(さい)があることが明(あき)らかになった。
The survey results revealed that there are significant disparities in educational standards depending on the region.

STRUCTURE:
差(さ) (difference, gap) + 異(い) (different, unusual).

USAGE:
差異(さい) is a formal synonym of 違(ちが)い (difference). It is preferred in written, academic, and analytical contexts where precise or objective language is expected. Compared to 差(さ), which emphasizes a measurable gap, 差異(さい) focuses on qualitative differences between things being compared.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 差異(さい)がある: there is a difference
  • 差異(さい)を認(みと)める: to acknowledge differences
  • 差異(さい)を生(う)む: to create differences
  • 個人(こじん)差異(さい): individual differences
  • 文化的(ぶんかてき)差異(さい): cultural differences
