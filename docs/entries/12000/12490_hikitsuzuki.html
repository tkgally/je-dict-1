引(ひ)き続(つづ)き

ひきつづき
adverb
continuously, subsequently, going forward
1. continuously, without interruption
Indicates that a state or action continues without a break. Often used at the beginning of a sentence to signal that something ongoing will persist.
引(ひ)き続(つづ)きご注意(ちゅうい)ください。
Please continue to exercise caution.
引(ひ)き続(つづ)き調査(ちょうさ)を進(すす)めてまいります。
We will continue to carry out the investigation.
来年(らいねん)も引(ひ)き続(つづ)きよろしくお願(ねが)いいたします。
We look forward to your continued support next year as well.
2. subsequently, following that
Indicates that something will happen after the current event or situation. Common in formal announcements, business communication, and news.
引(ひ)き続(つづ)き、ニュースをお伝(つた)えします。
We will continue to bring you the news.
開会式(かいかいしき)に引(ひ)き続(つづ)き、記念(きねん)パーティーが行(おこな)われた。
Following the opening ceremony, a commemorative party was held.
午前中(ごぜんちゅう)の会議(かいぎ)に引(ひ)き続(つづ)き、午後(ごご)はワークショップが予定(よてい)されている。
Following the morning meeting, a workshop is scheduled for the afternoon.

USAGE:
Used both as an adverb modifying a verb (sense 1) and as a conjunctive expression connecting two events (sense 2). In sense 2, the pattern is often Noun に引(ひ)き続(つづ)き (following Noun). Very common in formal and business contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 引(ひ)き続(つづ)き〜する: to continue doing
  • 〜に引(ひ)き続(つづ)き: following, subsequent to
  • 引(ひ)き続(つづ)きよろしくお願(ねが)いします: set phrase for continued support

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Frequently heard in news broadcasts, business emails, and official announcements. In casual speech, そのまま or 続(つづ)けて are more natural.
