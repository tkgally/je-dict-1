夜通(よどお)し

よどおし
adverb, noun
all night long, throughout the night
1. all night long, throughout the night, the whole night through
From evening until morning without stopping. Describes an activity that continues for the entire duration of the night.
夜通(よどお)し歩(ある)いた。
I walked all night long.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)に夜通(よどお)し勉強(べんきょう)した。
I studied all night before the exam.
赤(あか)ちゃんが夜通(よどお)し泣(な)いていたので、両親(りょうしん)はほとんど眠(ねむ)れなかった。
The baby cried all night long, so the parents could hardly sleep.

USAGE:
Used adverbially to modify verbs describing activities that last the entire night. Can also function as a noun (夜通(よどお)しの〜). Implies endurance or persistence.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一晩中(ひとばんじゅう): all night long (more colloquial)
  • 夜通(よどお)し: all night long (slightly more literary)
  • 徹夜(てつや): staying up all night (emphasizes the act of not sleeping)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夜通(よどお)し働(はたら)く: to work all night
  • 夜通(よどお)しの作業(さぎょう): all-night work
  • 夜通(よどお)し語(かた)り明(あ)かす: to talk all through the night
