合(あ)わさる

あわさる
godan verb
to come together; to be combined; to merge
1. to come together, to be combined, to merge
For multiple things to join, combine, or come together into one. The intransitive counterpart of 合(あ)わせる.
二(ふた)つの川(かわ)が合(あ)わさる。
Two rivers merge.
色(いろ)が合(あ)わさって新(あたら)しい色(いろ)になった。
The colors combined to form a new color.
才能(さいのう)と努力(どりょく)が合(あ)わさって、素晴(すば)らしい結果(けっか)が生(う)まれた。
Talent and effort came together to produce a wonderful result.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive counterpart is 合(あ)わせる (to combine, to put together).

CONJUGATION:

Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)), る-ending (godan, not ichidan).

  • Negative: 合(あ)わさらない
  • Polite: 合(あ)わさります
  • Te-form: 合(あ)わさって
  • Past: 合(あ)わさった

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 力(ちから)が合(あ)わさる: for forces/efforts to combine
  • 条件(じょうけん)が合(あ)わさる: for conditions to align
  • 要素(ようそ)が合(あ)わさる: for elements to come together
