引(ひ)き渡(わた)す

ひきわたす
godan verb
to hand over, to deliver, to extradite
1. to hand over, to deliver, to turn over
To formally transfer possession of something (a building, property, goods) to another party. Often used in business and legal contexts for the completion of a transaction.
鍵(かぎ)を買主(かいぬし)に引(ひ)き渡(わた)した。
I handed over the keys to the buyer.
新築(しんちく)マンションの引(ひ)き渡(わた)しは3月(がつ)の予定(よてい)だ。
The handover of the newly built apartment is scheduled for March.
工事(こうじ)が完了(かんりょう)し、施設(しせつ)は予定通(よていどお)り発注者(はっちゅうしゃ)に引(ひ)き渡(わた)された。
Construction was completed, and the facility was handed over to the client on schedule.
2. to extradite, to hand over (a person)
To turn over a person, especially a criminal suspect, to another authority or country. Used in law enforcement and international legal contexts.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を警察(けいさつ)に引(ひ)き渡(わた)した。
The suspect was handed over to the police.
犯人(はんにん)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しを要求(ようきゅう)する。
Demand the extradition of the criminal.
両国(りょうこく)の間(あいだ)には犯罪者(はんざいしゃ)引(ひ)き渡(わた)し条約(じょうやく)が結(むす)ばれている。
An extradition treaty has been concluded between the two countries.

USAGE:
Godan verb conjugating as 引(ひ)き渡(わた)す・引(ひ)き渡(わた)して. The noun form 引(ひ)き渡(わた)し (handover, delivery, extradition) is extremely common. In real estate, 引(ひ)き渡(わた)し refers to the handover of keys and possession after a sale or lease. In international law, 引(ひ)き渡(わた)し条約(じょうやく) is an extradition treaty.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 引(ひ)き渡(わた)し日(び): handover date
  • 引(ひ)き渡(わた)し条約(じょうやく): extradition treaty
  • ～に引(ひ)き渡(わた)す: hand over to ~
  • 物件(ぶっけん)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)し: property handover
