呼(よ)び方(かた)

よびかた
noun
way of calling, how to address
1. way of calling, how to address, what to call something
The manner or term used to address or refer to a person or thing. Often used when discussing what name, title, or term is appropriate for someone or something.
先生(せんせい)の呼(よ)び方(かた)がわからない。
I don't know how to address the teacher.
日本(にほん)では名字(みょうじ)で呼(よ)ぶのが普通(ふつう)の呼(よ)び方(かた)です。
In Japan, calling people by their surname is the normal way of addressing them.
この魚(さかな)は地域(ちいき)によって呼(よ)び方(かた)が違(ちが)うので、旅行先(りょこうさき)で戸惑(とまど)うことがある。
This fish has different names depending on the region, which can be confusing when traveling.

USAGE:
呼(よ)び方(かた) is formed from 呼(よ)ぶ (to call) + 方(かた) (way of doing). It is a very common word in everyday conversation, especially when discussing appropriate forms of address or regional naming differences.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 呼(よ)び方(かた)がわからない: not know what to call (someone/something)
  • 呼(よ)び方(かた)が違(ちが)う: have different names for
  • 正(ただ)しい呼(よ)び方(かた): correct way to address
  • 呼(よ)び方(かた)を変(か)える: to change how one addresses (someone)
