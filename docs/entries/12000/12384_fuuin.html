封印(ふういん)

ふういん
noun, suru verb
seal; sealing away
1. seal, sealing
A seal placed on a container, document, or object to prevent unauthorized opening. Also the act of affixing such a seal.
手紙(てがみ)に封印(ふういん)をする。
To place a seal on a letter.
箱(はこ)の封印(ふういん)が破(やぶ)られていた。
The seal on the box had been broken.
証拠品(しょうこひん)には警察(けいさつ)の封印(ふういん)が貼(は)られている。
The evidence has a police seal affixed to it.
2. sealing away, locking away, suppressing
To deliberately lock away, suppress, or stop using something. Often used figuratively for abandoning a technique, hiding a memory, or sealing away a power (common in fiction and everyday speech).
辛(つら)い記憶(きおく)を封印(ふういん)する。
To seal away painful memories.
けがのせいで得意(とくい)な技(わざ)を封印(ふういん)せざるを得(え)なかった。
Due to injury, I had no choice but to stop using my signature technique.
古(ふる)い神社(じんじゃ)の奥(おく)に封印(ふういん)された邪悪(じゃあく)な力(ちから)が目覚(めざ)めた、という物語(ものがたり)だ。
It's a story about an evil power sealed away deep within an old shrine that awakens.

USAGE:
封印(ふういん)する is used both literally (sealing documents, containers) and figuratively (suppressing memories, abandoning techniques). The figurative sense is very common in everyday conversation and is a staple of fantasy fiction, manga, and anime where characters seal away powers or monsters.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 封印(ふういん)を解(と)く: to break a seal, to unseal
  • 封印(ふういん)を破(やぶ)る: to break a seal (forcibly)
  • 記憶(きおく)を封印(ふういん)する: to seal away memories
  • 技(わざ)を封印(ふういん)する: to stop using a technique
  • 封印(ふういん)された～: sealed ~
