1. opposition, rivalry, competition, resistance
The act of competing against or standing in opposition to someone or something. Implies actively matching or countering an opposing force.
大手(おおて)企業(きぎょう)に対抗(たいこう)する。
To compete against a major corporation.
値下(ねさ)げに対抗(たいこう)して、うちも価格(かかく)を下(さ)げた。
In response to their price cut, we also lowered our prices.
住民(じゅうみん)たちは再開発(さいかいはつ)計画(けいかく)に対抗(たいこう)するために署名(しょめい)活動(かつどう)を始(はじ)めた。
The residents started a petition drive to oppose the redevelopment plan.

USAGE:
対抗(たいこう) functions as a する verb (対抗(たいこう)する) and also appears frequently as a modifier: 対抗(たいこう)～ (counter-~, rival ~). The pattern ～に対抗(たいこう)する (to compete against ~, to counter ~) is very common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 対決(たいけつ): showdown, confrontation (more direct and climactic)
  • 対立(たいりつ): opposition, conflict (emphasis on the state of being opposed)
  • 競争(きょうそう): competition (neutral, ongoing rivalry)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に対抗(たいこう)する: to compete against ~, to counter ~
  • 対抗(たいこう)措置(そち): countermeasure
  • 対抗(たいこう)馬(ば): rival horse, strong contender
  • 対抗(たいこう)意識(いしき): sense of rivalry
  • 対抗(たいこう)戦(せん): rivalry match
