多岐(たき)

たき
noun
diverse, wide-ranging, manifold
1. diverse, wide-ranging, manifold, many-branched
Describes something that branches out into many different areas or directions. Almost always used in the fixed expression 多岐にわたる (to span many areas). Literally means 'many branches' (of a path).
議論(ぎろん)は多岐(たき)にわたった。
The discussion covered a wide range of topics.
彼(かれ)の活動(かつどう)は多岐(たき)にわたる。
His activities span a wide range of fields.
被害(ひがい)は多岐(たき)にわたっており、全容(ぜんよう)の把握(はあく)にはまだ時間(じかん)がかかる。
The damage is wide-ranging, and it will still take time to grasp the full picture.

多岐(たき) literally means "many branches" (of a road or path). It is almost exclusively used in the fixed expression 多岐(たき)にわたる.

KEY EXPRESSION:

多岐(たき)にわたる: to span many areas, to be wide-ranging

  • 多岐(たき)にわたる分野(ぶんや): fields spanning many areas
  • 多岐(たき)にわたる問題(もんだい): problems covering many areas

REGISTER:
Formal and written. Common in news articles, reports, academic writing, and business documents. In casual conversation, 幅広(はばひろ)い or いろいろな would be preferred.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 多岐(たき)にわたる: spanning many branches/areas (formal, fixed expression)
  • 幅広(はばひろ)い: wide-ranging, broad (more general, less formal)
  • 多方面(たほうめん): many directions/fields (similar formality)
