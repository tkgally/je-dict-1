学園(がくえん)

がくえん
noun
academy, school campus
1. academy, school, educational institution
A school or educational institution, often one that encompasses multiple levels (elementary through university) or has a prestigious or distinctive identity. Commonly used in school names and in fiction as a setting.
学園(がくえん)生活(せいかつ)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy school life.
この学園(がくえん)は創立(そうりつ)百年(ひゃくねん)の歴史(れきし)がある。
This academy has a hundred-year history since its founding.
学園(がくえん)祭(さい)の準備(じゅんび)で生徒(せいと)たちは毎日(まいにち)遅(おそ)くまで残(のこ)っている。
The students stay late every day preparing for the school festival.

USAGE:
Often appears in proper names of schools (e.g., 青山(あおやま)学院(がくいん), 学習院(がくしゅういん)). In fiction — anime, manga, and light novels — 学園(がくえん) is a standard term for the school setting, especially in the genre called 学園(がくえん)もの (school-themed stories).

SIMILAR WORDS:
学校(がっこう) is the general, everyday word for school. 学園(がくえん) has a slightly more formal or literary feel and often implies a larger institution or campus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学園(がくえん)祭(さい): school festival
  • 学園(がくえん)生活(せいかつ): school life
  • 学園(がくえん)ドラマ: school drama
  • 学園(がくえん)もの: school-themed genre
  • 学園(がくえん)都市(とし): academic city
