大惨事(だいさんじ)

だいさんじ
noun
catastrophe, disaster, calamity
1. catastrophe, disaster, calamity, major tragedy
A large-scale tragic event involving significant damage, casualties, or destruction. Used for accidents, natural disasters, and other events that cause widespread suffering.
列車(れっしゃ)事故(じこ)が大惨事(だいさんじ)になった。
The train accident turned into a catastrophe.
もう少(すこ)しで大惨事(だいさんじ)になるところだった。
It almost turned into a catastrophe.
迅速(じんそく)な避難(ひなん)誘導(ゆうどう)のおかげで、大惨事(だいさんじ)を免(まぬが)れることができた。
Thanks to the swift evacuation guidance, a catastrophe was avoided.

大惨事(だいさんじ) is a compound of 大(だい) (great) and 惨事(さんじ) (tragic incident).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大惨事(だいさんじ)になる: to turn into a catastrophe
  • 大惨事(だいさんじ)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす: to cause a catastrophe
  • 大惨事(だいさんじ)を免(まぬが)れる: to avert/avoid a catastrophe
  • 大惨事(だいさんじ)に至(いた)る: to lead to a catastrophe

USAGE:
Common in news reporting about accidents, natural disasters, and other large-scale tragic events. Also used in near-miss situations with expressions like 大惨事(だいさんじ)になるところだった ("it almost became a catastrophe").

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 惨事(さんじ): tragic incident (smaller scale)
  • 大惨事(だいさんじ): catastrophe (large-scale tragedy)
  • 災害(さいがい): disaster (often natural disasters, more general)
