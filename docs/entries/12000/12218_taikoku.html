大国(たいこく)

たいこく
noun
major power, great nation
1. major power, great nation
A country that has significant influence on global affairs due to its economic strength, military power, territory, or political clout. Used in geopolitical discussions about world powers.
経済(けいざい)大国(たいこく)としての責任(せきにん)がある。
There is a responsibility as a major economic power.
二(ふた)つの大国(たいこく)の対立(たいりつ)が世界(せかい)情勢(じょうせい)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えている。
The rivalry between the two great powers is affecting the world situation.
かつての軍事(ぐんじ)大国(たいこく)が今(いま)では平和(へいわ)国家(こっか)として知(し)られるようになった。
A country once known as a military great power has now come to be known as a peaceful nation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経済(けいざい)大国(たいこく): economic power
  • 軍事(ぐんじ)大国(たいこく): military power
  • 先進(せんしん)大国(たいこく): advanced major nation
  • 核(かく)大国(たいこく): nuclear power
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)大国(たいこく): technological power

SIMILAR WORDS:
大国(たいこく) vs 超大国(ちょうたいこく): 超大国(ちょうたいこく) means "superpower" and is reserved for the most dominant nations. 大国(たいこく) is a broader term for any influential major nation.

大国(たいこく) vs 強国(きょうこく): 強国(きょうこく) emphasizes military or competitive strength, while 大国(たいこく) emphasizes overall size and influence.
