名目(めいもく)

めいもく
noun
name; title; pretext; nominal
1. name, title, pretext
A name or label given to something, especially one that may not reflect the true nature or purpose. Often implies a gap between the stated reason and reality.
研修(けんしゅう)の名目(めいもく)で海外(かいがい)に行(い)った。
He went abroad under the pretext of training.
寄付(きふ)という名目(めいもく)で金(かね)を集(あつ)めていた。
They were collecting money under the name of donations.
視察(しさつ)という名目(めいもく)で出張(しゅっちょう)したが、実際(じっさい)にはほとんど観光(かんこう)だった。
He went on a business trip under the pretext of an inspection, but in reality it was mostly sightseeing.
2. nominal (in name only)
Existing in name but not necessarily in substance. Used in economics for values not adjusted for inflation.
名目(めいもく)上(じょう)は社長(しゃちょう)だ。
He is the president in name only.
名目(めいもく)賃金(ちんぎん)は上(あ)がったが、実質(じっしつ)賃金(ちんぎん)は下(さ)がっている。
Nominal wages have risen, but real wages have fallen.
名目(めいもく)GDP と実質(じっしつ)GDP の差(さ)は、物価(ぶっか)変動(へんどう)の影響(えいきょう)を表(あらわ)している。
The difference between nominal GDP and real GDP reflects the impact of price fluctuations.

USAGE:
Sense 1 often appears in the pattern ～の名目(めいもく)で or ～という名目(めいもく)で (under the name/pretext of), implying the stated reason may be a cover for the real purpose. Sense 2 is used in economics, contrasted with 実質(じっしつ) (real, actual).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～の名目(めいもく)で: under the pretext of
  • ～という名目(めいもく): in the name of
  • 名目(めいもく)上(じょう): nominally, in name
  • 名目(めいもく)賃金(ちんぎん): nominal wages
  • 名目(めいもく)GDP: nominal GDP

SIMILAR WORDS:
名目(めいもく) emphasizes the label or stated reason (often implying it differs from reality). 口実(こうじつ) (excuse, pretext) is more directly negative, meaning an excuse to justify an action. 建前(たてまえ) (public stance) is the socially presented position as opposed to one's true feelings (本音(ほんね)).
