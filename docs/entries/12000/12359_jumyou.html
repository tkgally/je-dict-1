寿命(じゅみょう)

じゅみょう
noun
lifespan, life expectancy
1. lifespan, life expectancy
The length of time a person or organism lives, or the expected duration of life.
日本人(にほんじん)の平均(へいきん)寿命(じゅみょう)は長(なが)い。
The average lifespan of Japanese people is long.
医学(いがく)の進歩(しんぽ)で寿命(じゅみょう)が延(の)びた。
Lifespans have increased due to advances in medicine.
健康(けんこう)寿命(じゅみょう)を延(の)ばすことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to extend healthy life expectancy.
2. service life, useful life
The expected duration of functionality for machines, products, or other non-living things before they wear out or need replacement.
バッテリーの寿命(じゅみょう)が短(みじか)い。
The battery life is short.
この製品(せいひん)の寿命(じゅみょう)は約(やく)十年(じゅうねん)だ。
The service life of this product is about ten years.
エアコンの寿命(じゅみょう)が来(き)たので買(か)い替(か)えた。
The air conditioner reached the end of its life, so I replaced it.

USAGE:
寿命(じゅみょう) is used for both living things and products/machines. When used for products, it functions similarly to English "lifespan" or "service life."

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 平均(へいきん)寿命(じゅみょう) — average lifespan
  • 健康(けんこう)寿命(じゅみょう) — healthy life expectancy
  • 寿命(じゅみょう)が延(の)びる — lifespan increases
  • 寿命(じゅみょう)が縮(ちぢ)む — lifespan shortens
  • 寿命(じゅみょう)が来(く)る — to reach end of life

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan has one of the highest average lifespans in the world. The concept of 健康(けんこう)寿命(じゅみょう) (healthy life expectancy) — years lived in good health, as opposed to total lifespan — has become an important topic in public health discussions.
