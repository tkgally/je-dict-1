学食(がくしょく)

がくしょく
noun
school cafeteria, university dining hall
1. school cafeteria, university dining hall
An abbreviation of 学校食堂 (school cafeteria). Refers to the on-campus dining facility at a university or college. A casual, everyday term used mainly by students.
学食(がくしょく)で昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べた。
I ate lunch at the school cafeteria.
この大学(だいがく)の学食(がくしょく)は安(やす)くておいしい。
This university's cafeteria is cheap and delicious.
昼休(ひるやす)みの学食(がくしょく)は学生(がくせい)でいっぱいになる。
The cafeteria gets packed with students during lunch break.

USAGE:
An abbreviation of 学校(がっこう)食堂(しょくどう). Used almost exclusively in reference to university or college dining halls, not elementary or middle school cafeterias. Very common in student conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学食(がくしょく)で食(た)べる: to eat at the cafeteria
  • 学食(がくしょく)のメニュー: cafeteria menu
  • 学食(がくしょく)が混(こ)む: the cafeteria is crowded

WORD FORMATION:
Abbreviation pattern: 学(がく) (school) + 食(しょく) (food/dining). This type of two-kanji abbreviation is common in Japanese campus slang.
