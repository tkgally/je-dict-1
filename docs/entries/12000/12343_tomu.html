富(と)む

とむ
verb (godan)
to be rich in, to abound in
1. to be rich in, to abound in, to be abundant
To possess something in large quantity or to be well-endowed with a particular quality. Often used with the particle に to indicate what is abundant.
この土地(とち)は自然(しぜん)に富(と)んでいる。
This land is rich in nature.
経験(けいけん)に富(と)んだ人(ひと)に相談(そうだん)したい。
I want to consult someone rich in experience.
この地域(ちいき)は鉱物(こうぶつ)資源(しげん)に富(と)んでいることで知(し)られている。
This region is known for being rich in mineral resources.

GRAMMAR:
Almost always used in the ている form (富(と)んでいる) to describe an ongoing state of abundance. The pattern is Xに富(と)む (to be rich in X).

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)に富(と)む — rich in experience
  • 変化(へんか)に富(と)む — full of variety
  • 才能(さいのう)に富(と)む — rich in talent
  • 栄養(えいよう)に富(と)む — rich in nutrition
  • 資源(しげん)に富(と)む — rich in resources

NOTE:
富(と)む is primarily used in written and formal contexts. In everyday speech, 豊(ゆた)かな is more common to express the same idea (e.g., 経験(けいけん)豊(ゆた)かな人(ひと)).
