売上高(うりあげだか)

うりあげだか
noun
total sales, gross revenue
1. total sales, gross revenue, turnover
The total amount of money received from selling goods or services over a given period. A key financial metric used in accounting and business reporting.
売上高(うりあげだか)が増加(ぞうか)した。
Total sales increased.
今年(ことし)の売上高(うりあげだか)は過去最高(かこさいこう)を記録(きろく)した。
This year's total sales set an all-time record.
連結(れんけつ)売上高(うりあげだか)は前年比(ぜんねんひ)で十(じゅう)パーセント増(ぞう)の一兆円(いっちょうえん)に達(たっ)した。
Consolidated revenue reached one trillion yen, a ten percent increase year on year.

STRUCTURE:
売上(うりあげ) (sales) + 高(だか) (amount, volume). The suffix 高(だか) indicates a total amount or volume.

USAGE:
売上高(うりあげだか) is a formal financial term used in corporate earnings reports, accounting statements, and business news. In everyday conversation, 売(う)り上(あ)げ is more common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)り上(あ)げ: sales, revenue (less formal, broader usage)
  • 収益(しゅうえき): earnings, profit
  • 利益(りえき): profit (what remains after expenses)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売上高(うりあげだか)が伸(の)びる: sales grow
  • 連結(れんけつ)売上高(うりあげだか): consolidated revenue
  • 年間(ねんかん)売上高(うりあげだか): annual sales
  • 売上高(うりあげだか)営業利益率(えいぎょうりえきりつ): operating profit margin
