完治(かんち)

かんち
noun, suru verb
complete recovery, full cure
1. complete recovery, full cure, complete healing
A disease or injury being fully cured with no remaining symptoms. Used in medical contexts to indicate that treatment has been completely successful.
病気(びょうき)が完治(かんち)した。
The illness was completely cured.
完治(かんち)するまでに半年(はんとし)かかった。
It took half a year to fully recover.
この病気(びょうき)は早期(そうき)発見(はっけん)すれば完治(かんち)できる可能性(かのうせい)が高(たか)い。
If this disease is detected early, there is a high possibility of a complete cure.

USAGE:
A medical term indicating that a condition has been fully resolved. Used as a suru verb: 完治(かんち)する (to be completely cured). Often contrasted with situations where symptoms are managed but not eliminated.

SIMILAR WORDS:
全快(ぜんかい) also means complete recovery but tends to emphasize the patient's return to full health, while 完治(かんち) emphasizes the disease or injury itself being fully cured.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完治(かんち)する: to be completely cured
  • 完治(かんち)が難(むずか)しい: difficult to cure completely
  • 完治(かんち)を目指(めざ)す: to aim for a complete cure
  • 完治(かんち)までの期間(きかん): period until full recovery
