差(さ)し金(がね)

さしがね
noun
instigation; pulling strings behind the scenes
1. instigation, manipulation, pulling strings behind the scenes
Secret manipulation or prompting by someone behind the scenes. Implies that a person's actions were actually directed or instigated by someone else. Almost always used with negative connotations.
これは誰(だれ)かの差(さ)し金(がね)だ。
Someone is pulling the strings behind this.
社長(しゃちょう)の差(さ)し金(がね)で動(うご)いているらしい。
It seems they're acting on the company president's instigation.
あの記事(きじ)は政治家(せいじか)の差(さ)し金(がね)で書(か)かれたのではないかと疑(うたが)われている。
It is suspected that that article was written at the instigation of a politician.

USAGE:
Almost always carries a negative nuance of covert manipulation. The most common pattern is ～の差(さ)し金(がね)で (at someone's instigation). Originally, 差(さ)し金(がね) referred to a carpenter's square or a device used in kabuki to manipulate stage props from offstage, hence the metaphorical meaning of hidden manipulation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～の差(さ)し金(がね)で: at the instigation of ~
  • 裏(うら)で差(さ)し金(がね)をする: pull strings behind the scenes
  • 差(さ)し金(がね)だと疑(うたが)う: suspect instigation
