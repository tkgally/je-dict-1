問(と)いかける

といかける
ichidan verb
to pose a question; to question; to ask
1. to pose a question, to ask, to question
To direct a question at someone, often with the nuance of prompting thought or seeking a deeper answer. Can also mean to address a question to the public or society at large.
自分(じぶん)に問(と)いかける。
To ask oneself.
先生(せんせい)が生徒(せいと)に問(と)いかけた。
The teacher posed a question to the students.
この映画(えいが)は、現代(げんだい)社会(しゃかい)のあり方(かた)を観客(かんきゃく)に問(と)いかけている。
This film poses questions to the audience about the state of modern society.

USAGE:
Often implies a question meant to provoke thought rather than simply seeking factual information. Common in intellectual, philosophical, and media contexts. The form 問(と)いかけ is used as a noun meaning "a question" or "an inquiry."

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)).

  • Negative: 問(と)いかけない
  • Polite: 問(と)いかけます
  • Te-form: 問(と)いかけて
  • Past: 問(と)いかけた
  • Noun form: 問(と)いかけ

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自分(じぶん)に問(と)いかける: to ask oneself
  • 社会(しゃかい)に問(と)いかける: to pose a question to society
  • 意味(いみ)を問(と)いかける: to question the meaning of
