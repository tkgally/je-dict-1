幼馴染(おさななじみ)

おさななじみ
noun
childhood friend
1. childhood friend, friend from one's early years
A person one has known and been close to since childhood. The relationship implies a deep, long-standing bond that began in one's formative years.
彼(かれ)は幼馴染(おさななじみ)だ。
He is my childhood friend.
幼馴染(おさななじみ)と久(ひさ)しぶりに再会(さいかい)した。
I was reunited with a childhood friend after a long time.
彼女(かのじょ)とは幼馴染(おさななじみ)で、幼稚園(ようちえん)の頃(ころ)から一緒(いっしょ)に遊(あそ)んでいた仲(なか)だ。
She and I are childhood friends — we've been playing together since kindergarten.

STRUCTURE:
幼(おさな) (young, childish) + 馴染(なじみ) (familiarity, acquaintance). Also written 幼(おさな)なじみ or おさななじみ in kana.

USAGE:
幼馴染(おさななじみ) specifically refers to a friend known since early childhood, not just any long-term friend. It carries a warm, nostalgic connotation and implies a uniquely close bond. The term is widely used in everyday conversation, literature, and is particularly prominent in manga and anime as a common character archetype — the childhood friend who may also be a romantic interest.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幼馴染(おさななじみ)と再会(さいかい)する: to reunite with a childhood friend
  • 幼馴染(おさななじみ)の仲(なか): a childhood friendship
  • 幼馴染(おさななじみ)と結婚(けっこん)する: to marry a childhood friend
