実証(じっしょう)

じっしょう
noun, verb (suru)
proof, verification, demonstration
1. proof, verification, demonstration
Proving or demonstrating something through evidence or experiment. Establishing the truth of a claim through concrete facts or testing.
理論(りろん)を実証(じっしょう)する。
To verify a theory.
実証(じっしょう)実験(じっけん)を行(おこな)って安全性(あんぜんせい)を確認(かくにん)した。
We conducted a verification experiment to confirm the safety.
科学的(かがくてき)に実証(じっしょう)されていない治療法(ちりょうほう)には注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Caution is needed with treatments that have not been scientifically verified.

USAGE:
実証(じっしょう) emphasizes proving through evidence or experiment, as opposed to theoretical reasoning. It is commonly used in academic, scientific, and technical contexts.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実証(じっしょう)実験(じっけん) — verification experiment, demonstration test
  • 実証(じっしょう)研究(けんきゅう) — empirical research
  • 科学的(かがくてき)に実証(じっしょう)する — to scientifically verify
  • 実証(じっしょう)済(ず)み — verified, proven

SIMILAR WORDS:
証明(しょうめい) is the general word for proof or certification. 検証(けんしょう) means examination or verification through review. 実証(じっしょう) specifically implies demonstrating through concrete evidence or experiments.
