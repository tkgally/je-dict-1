大病(たいびょう)

たいびょう
noun
serious illness, major disease
1. serious illness, major disease, grave sickness
A severe or life-threatening illness. Implies that the condition is or was significant enough to seriously impact one's life, requiring extended treatment or hospitalization.
大病(たいびょう)を患(わずら)った。
He suffered a serious illness.
大病(たいびょう)をしてから、健康(けんこう)のありがたみが分(わ)かった。
After going through a serious illness, I understood the value of good health.
祖父(そふ)は若(わか)い頃(ころ)に大病(たいびょう)を経験(けいけん)したが、無事(ぶじ)に回復(かいふく)して長生(ながい)きした。
My grandfather experienced a serious illness when he was young, but he recovered safely and lived a long life.

大病(たいびょう) refers to an illness serious enough to be life-altering or life-threatening.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大病(たいびょう)を患(わずら)う: to suffer from a serious illness
  • 大病(たいびょう)をする: to have a serious illness
  • 大病(たいびょう)を経験(けいけん)する: to experience a serious illness
  • 大病(たいびょう)から回復(かいふく)する: to recover from a serious illness

USAGE:
Often used when reflecting on a past illness or discussing life-changing health events. The phrase 大病(たいびょう)をしたことがない ("I've never had a serious illness") is a common way to express being in good health throughout one's life.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 病気(びょうき): illness, sickness (general)
  • 重病(じゅうびょう): serious/critical illness (more medical, often current)
  • 大病(たいびょう): serious illness (often reflective, life-changing event)

Related Words

Synonym: 重病(じゅうびょう) (critical illness)
Related: 病気(びょうき) (illness (general))
