多彩(たさい)

たさい
na-adjective
colorful, varied, diverse
1. colorful, varied, diverse, rich in variety
Having many colors, varieties, or aspects. Used both literally for colors and figuratively for talents, programs, and offerings.
多彩(たさい)なメニューがそろっている。
There is a varied menu available.
彼女(かのじょ)は多彩(たさい)な才能(さいのう)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
She is a person of diverse talents.
この地域(ちいき)は自然(しぜん)が豊(ゆた)かで、多彩(たさい)な動植物(どうしょくぶつ)が生息(せいそく)している。
This region is rich in nature, and a diverse array of plants and animals inhabit it.

USAGE:
Frequently used to praise the breadth or variety of something. Common in descriptions of events, performances, talents, and product lineups. Almost always carries a positive connotation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 多様(たよう): diverse (more neutral/academic; emphasizes variety of types)
  • 多彩(たさい): colorful, varied (more vivid; emphasizes richness and appeal)
  • 豊富(ほうふ): abundant, rich (emphasizes quantity)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多彩(たさい)な才能(さいのう): diverse talents
  • 多彩(たさい)なラインナップ: varied lineup
  • 多彩(たさい)なイベント: varied events
  • 多彩(たさい)な顔(かお)ぶれ: diverse cast of people
