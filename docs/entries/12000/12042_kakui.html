各位(かくい)

かくい
noun
everyone (formal address)
1. everyone, all of you (formal address)
A formal honorific term of address used to address a group of people respectfully. Commonly used in business emails, official notices, and formal announcements. Equivalent to "Dear all" or "To whom it may concern."
関係者(かんけいしゃ)各位(かくい)
To all concerned parties.
社員(しゃいん)各位(かくい)にお知(し)らせいたします。
We would like to inform all employees.
会員(かいいん)各位(かくい)におかれましては、ますますご健勝(けんしょう)のこととお慶(よろこ)び申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We are pleased to hope that all members are in good health.

USAGE:
各位(かくい) is itself an honorific, so adding さま or 殿(どの) after it is considered redundant, though 各位(かくい)さま is sometimes seen in practice. Typically placed after a group noun: 社員(しゃいん)各位(かくい), 会員(かいいん)各位(かくい), 関係者(かんけいしゃ)各位(かくい). Used primarily in written communication — business emails, notices, and circulars.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社員(しゃいん)各位(かくい): to all employees
  • 会員(かいいん)各位(かくい): to all members
  • 関係者(かんけいしゃ)各位(かくい): to all concerned parties
  • 保護者(ほごしゃ)各位(かくい): to all parents/guardians
