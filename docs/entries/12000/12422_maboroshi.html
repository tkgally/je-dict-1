幻(まぼろし)

まぼろし
noun
illusion, phantom, vision
1. illusion, phantom, vision, apparition
Something that appears to exist but does not, or that existed only briefly and is now gone. Used for visual hallucinations, fleeting experiences, and things that seem dreamlike or unreal.
幻(まぼろし)を見(み)る。
To see a vision.
あの幸(しあわ)せな日々(ひび)は幻(まぼろし)だったのだろうか。
Were those happy days just an illusion?
砂漠(さばく)で旅人(たびびと)が見(み)たオアシスは幻(まぼろし)にすぎなかった。
The oasis the traveler saw in the desert was nothing but an illusion.
2. legendary, elusive, rare beyond belief
Used as a modifier (幻の) to describe something so rare or fleeting that it seems almost mythical or impossible to obtain.
幻(まぼろし)の名酒(めいしゅ)を手(て)に入(い)れた。
I got hold of a legendary rare sake.
この魚(さかな)は幻(まぼろし)の魚(さかな)と呼(よ)ばれている。
This fish is called a legendary fish.
長年(ながねん)探(さが)し求(もと)めていた幻(まぼろし)のレコードが、古(ふる)い中古(ちゅうこ)レコード店(てん)でようやく見(み)つかった。
The legendary record I had been searching for over many years was finally found at an old secondhand record shop.

USAGE:
幻(まぼろし) as a standalone noun means an illusion or vision — something that appears real but is not. As a modifier (幻(まぼろし)の), it describes something so rare, elusive, or short-lived that it takes on a mythical quality. This modifier usage is very common in food and drink writing (幻(まぼろし)の日本酒(にほんしゅ), 幻(まぼろし)の食材(しょくざい)) and in media (幻(まぼろし)の名作(めいさく)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幻(まぼろし)を見(み)る: to see a vision
  • 幻(まぼろし)のように消(き)える: to vanish like a phantom
  • 幻(まぼろし)の～: legendary ~, phantom ~
  • 幻(まぼろし)に終(お)わる: to end as an illusion (to never materialize)
