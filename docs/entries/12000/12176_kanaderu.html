1. to play (a musical instrument), to produce music or melody
Describes performing music on an instrument. Has a literary, elegant tone compared to the more common 弾く. Often used for orchestral instruments, ensembles, or poetic descriptions of sound.
ピアノで美(うつく)しい曲(きょく)を奏(かな)でた。
She played a beautiful piece on the piano.
オーケストラが壮大(そうだい)なメロディーを奏(かな)でている。
The orchestra is playing a grand melody.
風(かぜ)が木々(きぎ)の葉(は)を揺(ゆ)らし、まるで自然(しぜん)が音楽(おんがく)を奏(かな)でているようだった。
The wind swayed the leaves of the trees, as if nature were playing music.

奏(かな)でる is a transitive verb with a literary, poetic nuance.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [instrument]で + [music/melody]を奏(かな)でる

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メロディーを奏(かな)でる (play a melody)
  • 曲(きょく)を奏(かな)でる (play a piece)
  • ハーモニーを奏(かな)でる (produce a harmony)
  • 音色(ねいろ)を奏(かな)でる (produce a tone)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 弾(ひ)く: the everyday word for playing piano, guitar, and other stringed/keyboard instruments
  • 吹(ふ)く: for wind instruments
  • 奏(かな)でる: more literary and elegant; used for poetic or formal descriptions of music-making, and can extend figuratively to natural sounds
