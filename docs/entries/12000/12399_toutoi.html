尊(とうと)い

とうとい
i-adjective
precious, noble, sacred
1. precious, valuable, important
Of great value and deserving of respect and protection. Used for abstract things like life, time, experiences, and sacrifices.
命(いのち)は尊(とうと)い。
Life is precious.
尊(とうと)い経験(けいけん)をさせてもらった。
I was given a precious experience.
多(おお)くの尊(とうと)い犠牲(ぎせい)の上(うえ)に今(いま)の平和(へいわ)がある。
Today's peace stands upon many precious sacrifices.
2. noble, sacred, holy, exalted
Having a dignified, sacred, or spiritually elevated quality. Used for deities, religious figures, and things of spiritual significance.
尊(とうと)い仏像(ぶつぞう)に手(て)を合(あ)わせる。
To put one's hands together before a sacred Buddhist statue.
この山(やま)は古(ふる)くから尊(とうと)い場所(ばしょ)とされてきた。
This mountain has been considered a sacred place since ancient times.
修行(しゅぎょう)を積(つ)んだ僧侶(そうりょ)の姿(すがた)は尊(とうと)く見(み)えた。
The figure of the monk who had undergone training appeared noble.

READING:
This word has two common readings: 尊(とうと)い and 尊(たっと)い. Both are correct and interchangeable, though 尊(とうと)い is more common in everyday speech. The kanji 貴(とうと)い can also be read the same way and has a similar meaning, though 貴(とうと)い leans more toward "valuable, high-ranking" while 尊(とうと)い leans toward "worthy of respect, sacred."

SLANG USAGE:
In internet and otaku culture, 尊(とうと)い (often written とうとい or 尊(とうと)い) is used as slang to express overwhelming admiration or emotional response to something cute, beautiful, or heartwarming — similar to English "precious" or "I can't even." This usage is very informal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 命(いのち)は尊(とうと)い: life is precious
  • 尊(とうと)い犠牲(ぎせい): precious sacrifice
  • 尊(とうと)い教(おし)え: sacred teachings
  • 尊(とうと)い経験(けいけん): valuable experience
