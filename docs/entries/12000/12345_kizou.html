寄贈(きぞう)

きぞう
noun, verb (suru)
donation, presentation, gift
1. donation, presentation, gift
The act of donating or presenting something, especially books, art, or other items of value, to an institution or organization. A formal term for giving.
図書館(としょかん)に本(ほん)を寄贈(きぞう)した。
I donated books to the library.
美術館(びじゅつかん)に絵画(かいが)が寄贈(きぞう)された。
Paintings were donated to the art museum.
この建物(たてもの)は地元(じもと)の企業(きぎょう)から寄贈(きぞう)されたものです。
This building was donated by a local company.

USAGE:
寄贈(きぞう) is a formal term used primarily for donating items (not money) to public institutions like libraries, museums, schools, or hospitals. For monetary donations, 寄付(きふ) is more appropriate.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本(ほん)を寄贈(きぞう)する — to donate books
  • 寄贈(きぞう)図書(としょ) — donated books
  • 寄贈(きぞう)を受(う)ける — to receive a donation
  • 寄贈(きぞう)品(ひん) — donated item

SIMILAR WORDS:
寄付(きふ) is the general term for donation, especially of money. 贈呈(ぞうてい) is a formal presentation or bestowal, often ceremonial. 寄贈(きぞう) specifically refers to donating physical items to institutions.
