1. acceptance, receipt (of official documents)
Formally accepting and processing an application, complaint, or official document. Used in legal, administrative, and bureaucratic contexts.
届(とどけ)を受理(じゅり)する。
To accept a notification form.
婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)が無事(ぶじ)に受理(じゅり)された。
The marriage registration was accepted without issue.
書類(しょるい)に不備(ふび)があったため、申請(しんせい)は受理(じゅり)されなかった。
The application was not accepted because there were deficiencies in the documents.

USAGE:
A formal, administrative term. The subject is typically a government office or official body that accepts documents from individuals. Passive form 受理(じゅり)される is very common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 申請(しんせい)を受理(じゅり)する: to accept an application
  • 届(とどけ)を受理(じゅり)する: to accept a registration/notification
  • 受理(じゅり)番号(ばんごう): acceptance/receipt number
  • 受理(じゅり)日(び): date of acceptance
