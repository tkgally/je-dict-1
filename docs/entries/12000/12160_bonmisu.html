凡(ぼん)ミス

ぼんみす
noun, suru verb
careless mistake, silly error
1. careless mistake, silly error, blunder
A mistake caused by carelessness or lack of attention rather than lack of knowledge or ability. Implies the error was easily avoidable.
凡(ぼん)ミスで点(てん)を落(お)とした。
I lost points due to a careless mistake.
計算(けいさん)の凡(ぼん)ミスが多(おお)いので気(き)をつけなさい。
You make a lot of careless calculation errors, so be careful.
大事(だいじ)な試合(しあい)で凡(ぼん)ミスを連発(れんぱつ)してしまい、チームに迷惑(めいわく)をかけた。
I made one careless mistake after another in an important match and caused trouble for the team.

USAGE:
A hybrid word combining the kanji 凡(ぼん) (ordinary, mediocre) with the English loanword ミス (miss/mistake). Very common in school, sports, and work contexts. Often used self-critically.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 凡(ぼん)ミスをする: to make a careless mistake
  • 凡(ぼん)ミスを減(へ)らす: to reduce careless mistakes
  • 凡(ぼん)ミスが目立(めだ)つ: careless mistakes stand out
  • 凡(ぼん)ミスを連発(れんぱつ)する: to make careless mistakes in succession
