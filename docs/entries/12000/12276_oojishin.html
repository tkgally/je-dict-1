大地震(おおじしん)

おおじしん
noun
major earthquake, great earthquake
1. major earthquake, great earthquake
A large-scale earthquake causing significant damage. Japan uses this term frequently due to its location on major fault lines and history of devastating earthquakes.
大地震(おおじしん)が起(お)きた。
A major earthquake occurred.
大地震(おおじしん)に備(そな)えて防災(ぼうさい)グッズを準備(じゅんび)した。
I prepared disaster supplies in case of a major earthquake.
専門家(せんもんか)は、今後(こんご)三十年(さんじゅうねん)以内(いない)に大地震(おおじしん)が発生(はっせい)する確率(かくりつ)が高(たか)いと警告(けいこく)している。
Experts warn that there is a high probability of a major earthquake occurring within the next thirty years.

READING:
Both おおじしん and だいじしん are used. おおじしん is the traditional reading and is used by NHK and most media. だいじしん is also widely accepted.

CULTURE:
Japan experiences frequent earthquakes, and 大地震(おおじしん) is a word that appears regularly in news and disaster preparedness materials. Major historical earthquakes include the 関東(かんとう)大震災(だいしんさい) (1923 Great Kanto Earthquake) and the 東日本(ひがしにほん)大震災(だいしんさい) (2011 Great East Japan Earthquake).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大地震(おおじしん)が起(お)きる: a major earthquake occurs
  • 大地震(おおじしん)に備(そな)える: to prepare for a major earthquake
  • 大地震(おおじしん)の被害(ひがい): damage from a major earthquake
  • 大地震(おおじしん)が発生(はっせい)する: a major earthquake strikes
