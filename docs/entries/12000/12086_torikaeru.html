取(と)り換(か)える

とりかえる
ichidan verb
to replace, to exchange, to swap
1. to replace, to exchange, to swap
To remove one thing and put another in its place, or to exchange two things with each other. Used for replacing worn-out items, swapping objects, and exchanging goods.
電池(でんち)を取(と)り換(か)える。
Replace the batteries.
古(ふる)くなったタイヤを新(あたら)しいのに取(と)り換(か)えた。
I replaced the worn-out tires with new ones.
部屋(へや)のカーテンを季節(きせつ)ごとに取(と)り換(か)えるようにしている。
I make it a point to swap out the curtains in my room each season.

USAGE:
Also written as 取(と)り替(か)える. Both kanji spellings (換(か)える and 替(か)える) are used, with 換(か) emphasizing technical replacement and 替(か) emphasizing general swapping. In practice they are often interchangeable.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電球(でんきゅう)を取(と)り換(か)える: replace a light bulb
  • フィルターを取(と)り換(か)える: replace a filter
  • 部品(ぶひん)を取(と)り換(か)える: replace a part
  • 新品(しんぴん)に取(と)り換(か)える: replace with a new one

SIMILAR WORDS:
取(と)り換(か)える focuses on removing one item and inserting another. 交換(こうかん)する is more formal and often implies a mutual exchange. 入(い)れ替(か)える emphasizes putting something new in place of the old.
