小(こ)さじ

こさじ
noun
teaspoon
1. teaspoon
A unit of measurement used in cooking, equal to 5 milliliters. Also refers to the small spoon itself used for measuring.
塩(しお)小(こ)さじ1を加(くわ)える。
Add one teaspoon of salt.
砂糖(さとう)を小(こ)さじ2杯(はい)入(い)れてください。
Please put in two teaspoons of sugar.
このレシピでは、しょうゆ小(こ)さじ1と酢(す)小(こ)さじ2を混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせます。
In this recipe, mix together one teaspoon of soy sauce and two teaspoons of vinegar.

USAGE:
A standard cooking measurement in Japan. 小(こ)さじ1 means one teaspoon (5 ml). Recipes typically write it directly after the ingredient: 塩(しお)小(こ)さじ1. The counterpart is 大(おお)さじ (tablespoon, 15 ml).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小(こ)さじ1: one teaspoon
  • 小(こ)さじ半分(はんぶん): half a teaspoon
  • 小(こ)さじ山盛(やまも)り: a heaped teaspoon
  • 小(こ)さじ摺(す)り切(き)り: a level teaspoon
