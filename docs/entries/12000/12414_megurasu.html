1. to encircle, to surround, to put around
To place something around an area so as to enclose or surround it, such as a fence, wall, or moat.
城(しろ)の周(まわ)りに堀(ほり)を巡(めぐ)らす。
To put a moat around the castle.
庭(にわ)の周(まわ)りに柵(さく)を巡(めぐ)らした。
I put a fence around the garden.
古(ふる)い屋敷(やしき)は高(たか)い石垣(いしがき)を巡(めぐ)らしていて、中(なか)の様子(ようす)はうかがえなかった。
The old mansion was surrounded by high stone walls, and one could not see what was inside.
2. to think over, to ponder, to turn over in one's mind
To consider various possibilities or think deeply about something. Often used with 思い (thoughts) or 考え (ideas).
思(おも)いを巡(めぐ)らす。
To turn thoughts over in one's mind.
将来(しょうらい)のことについて考(かんが)えを巡(めぐ)らせた。
I pondered about the future.
彼(かれ)は静(しず)かに椅子(いす)に座(すわ)り、事件(じけん)の真相(しんそう)について思(おも)いを巡(めぐ)らせていた。
He sat quietly in the chair, pondering the truth behind the incident.

Godan verb (す row): 巡(めぐ)らさない / 巡(めぐ)らします / 巡(めぐ)らす / 巡(めぐ)らせば / 巡(めぐ)らした.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (encircle) takes を for the barrier and の周(まわ)りに for the area being enclosed. Sense 2 (ponder) is most commonly used in the set phrases 思(おも)いを巡(めぐ)らす and 考(かんが)えを巡(めぐ)らす. The metaphor extends from physically going around to mentally circling through thoughts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 堀(ほり)を巡(めぐ)らす: to build a moat around
  • 柵(さく)を巡(めぐ)らす: to put a fence around
  • 思(おも)いを巡(めぐ)らす: to turn thoughts over
  • 策略(さくりゃく)を巡(めぐ)らす: to devise a scheme
