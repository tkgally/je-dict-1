寝床(ねどこ)

ねどこ
noun
bed, sleeping place
1. bed, sleeping place
The place where one sleeps, including futons, beds, or any spot used for sleeping. Has a warm, familiar tone compared to more formal alternatives.
寝床(ねどこ)に入(はい)る。
To get into bed.
猫(ねこ)が暖(あたた)かい寝床(ねどこ)を探(さが)している。
The cat is looking for a warm place to sleep.
母(はは)が寝床(ねどこ)を敷(し)いてくれた。
My mother laid out the bedding for me.

USAGE:
寝床(ねどこ) refers to the actual sleeping place, whether a futon or a bed. It has a warm, homey nuance and is used in everyday conversation. It can also refer to an animal's sleeping spot.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝床(ねどこ)に入(はい)る — to get into bed
  • 寝床(ねどこ)を敷(し)く — to lay out bedding
  • 寝床(ねどこ)から出(で)る — to get out of bed

SIMILAR WORDS:
ベッド refers specifically to a Western-style bed. 布団(ふとん) is the traditional Japanese bedding. 寝床(ねどこ) is a general term for any sleeping place regardless of type.
