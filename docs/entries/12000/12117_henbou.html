変貌(へんぼう)

へんぼう
noun, suru verb
transformation, transfiguration
1. transformation, transfiguration, dramatic change in appearance
A striking change in outward appearance or visible character. Often used for places, people, or things that have changed so dramatically they are almost unrecognizable. Emphasizes the visual or external aspect of change.
街(まち)が変貌(へんぼう)を遂(と)げた。
The town underwent a transformation.
再開発(さいかいはつ)で駅前(えきまえ)が大(おお)きく変貌(へんぼう)した。
The area in front of the station was dramatically transformed by redevelopment.
かつての静(しず)かな漁村(ぎょそん)は、観光地(かんこうち)として変貌(へんぼう)を遂(と)げ、今(いま)では多(おお)くの旅行者(りょこうしゃ)で賑(にぎ)わっている。
The once-quiet fishing village has transformed into a tourist destination and is now bustling with travelers.

USAGE:
変貌(へんぼう) focuses on visible, outward change — how something looks or appears. It is commonly paired with を遂(と)げる (to accomplish/undergo) in the set phrase 変貌(へんぼう)を遂(と)げる. Compared to 変容(へんよう) (qualitative, deep transformation) and 変化(へんか) (general change), 変貌(へんぼう) emphasizes the dramatic visual difference.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変貌(へんぼう)を遂(と)げる: to undergo a transformation
  • 大(おお)きく変貌(へんぼう)する: to be dramatically transformed
  • 街(まち)の変貌(へんぼう): transformation of a town
  • 著(いちじる)しい変貌(へんぼう): remarkable transformation
