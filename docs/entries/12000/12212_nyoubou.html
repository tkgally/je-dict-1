女房(にょうぼう)

にょうぼう
noun
wife (informal)
1. wife (informal, used by men)
An informal word for one's own wife, typically used by middle-aged and older men. It carries a casual, familiar tone and is used when referring to one's wife to others in relaxed conversation.
女房(にょうぼう)に叱(しか)られた。
I got scolded by my wife.
うちの女房(にょうぼう)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)だ。
My wife is a good cook.
長年(ながねん)連(つ)れ添(そ)った女房(にょうぼう)に先立(さきだ)たれて、彼(かれ)はすっかり元気(げんき)をなくした。
Having been predeceased by the wife who had been with him for so many years, he completely lost his spirits.

USAGE:
女房(にょうぼう) is used by men to refer to their own wife in casual speech. It has a slightly old-fashioned and down-to-earth feel. Younger men tend to prefer 妻(つま) or 嫁(よめ). It is not used to refer to someone else's wife (use 奥(おく)さん instead).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • うちの女房(にょうぼう): my wife (casual)
  • 女房(にょうぼう)役(やく): a supportive partner/right-hand person
  • 恐妻家(きょうさいか): henpecked husband (related concept)

SIMILAR WORDS:
妻(つま) is the neutral, standard term. 嫁(よめ) is colloquial and common among younger speakers. 家内(かない) is polite but somewhat dated. 女房(にょうぼう) has the most casual, unpretentious tone among these.

HISTORICAL NOTE:
Originally referred to ladies-in-waiting at the imperial court (女房(にょうぼう) literally means "woman's room/quarters"). The meaning shifted to "wife" during the Edo period.

Related Words

Synonym: 妻(つま) (neutral)
