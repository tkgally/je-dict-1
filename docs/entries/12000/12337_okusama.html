奥様(おくさま)

おくさま
noun
wife (polite), madam
1. wife (polite), madam, Mrs.
A polite and respectful way to refer to someone else's wife. Can also be used as a term of address for a married woman. Not used for one's own wife.
奥様(おくさま)はお元気(げんき)ですか。
Is your wife well?
社長(しゃちょう)の奥様(おくさま)にご挨拶(あいさつ)した。
I greeted the company president's wife.
奥様(おくさま)、こちらにどうぞ。
Madam, this way please.

奥様(おくさま) is the polite/honorific form used when referring to someone else's wife or addressing a married woman respectfully.

FORMS:

  • 奥様(おくさま): polite/honorific (most respectful)
  • 奥(おく)さん: polite but less formal (everyday use)
  • 奥方(おくがた): very formal, literary

IMPORTANT:
奥様(おくさま) and 奥(おく)さん are used only for someone else's wife, never for one's own. To refer to one's own wife, use 妻(つま) (neutral) or 家内(かない) (traditional).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 妻(つま): wife (neutral, used for one's own wife)
  • 家内(かない): my wife (humble, traditional)
  • 嫁(よめ): wife, bride (casual/regional)
