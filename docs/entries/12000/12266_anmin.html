安眠(あんみん)

あんみん
noun, suru verb
sound sleep, peaceful sleep
1. sound sleep, peaceful sleep, restful sleep
Sleeping soundly and peacefully without disturbance. Often used in contexts related to sleep quality, health, and products that promote good sleep.
安眠(あんみん)を妨(さまた)げる。
To disturb someone's sleep.
この枕(まくら)は安眠(あんみん)に効果(こうか)がある。
This pillow is effective for sound sleep.
工事(こうじ)の騒音(そうおん)で安眠(あんみん)できなくて困(こま)っている。
I'm troubled because the construction noise is keeping me from sleeping well.

USAGE:
Commonly appears in compound forms and product marketing. 安眠(あんみん)妨害(ぼうがい) (sleep disturbance) is a common complaint in neighborhood disputes. 安眠(あんみん)グッズ (sleep aids/products) is frequently seen in stores.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安眠(あんみん)を妨(さまた)げる: to disturb one's sleep
  • 安眠(あんみん)妨害(ぼうがい): sleep disturbance
  • 安眠(あんみん)効果(こうか): sleep-promoting effect
  • 安眠(あんみん)グッズ: sleep-aid products
  • 安眠(あんみん)枕(まくら): sleep pillow
