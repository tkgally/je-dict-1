全域(ぜんいき)

ぜんいき
noun
entire area, whole region
1. entire area, whole region, full extent
The complete area or range of a given territory, zone, or domain. Often used with geographic or administrative regions.
県(けん)の全域(ぜんいき)に警報(けいほう)が出(で)た。
A warning was issued for the entire prefecture.
停電(ていでん)は市内(しない)全域(ぜんいき)に及(およ)んだ。
The power outage affected the entire city.
関東(かんとう)全域(ぜんいき)で大雪(おおゆき)になる見込(みこ)みです。
Heavy snow is expected across the entire Kanto region.

USAGE:
Typically follows a place name or administrative unit: 県(けん)全域(ぜんいき), 市内(しない)全域(ぜんいき), 国内(こくない)全域(ぜんいき). Common in news broadcasts and official announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全域(ぜんいき)に及(およ)ぶ: to extend across the whole area
  • 全域(ぜんいき)で: throughout the entire area
  • 対象(たいしょう)全域(ぜんいき): the entire target area
