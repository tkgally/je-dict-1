寝(ね)たきり

ねたきり
noun / na-adjective
bedridden, confined to bed
1. bedridden, confined to bed
Unable to get out of bed due to illness, injury, or old age. Describes a state of being permanently or semi-permanently in bed.
祖母(そぼ)は寝(ね)たきりだ。
My grandmother is bedridden.
事故(じこ)の後(あと)、寝(ね)たきりの生活(せいかつ)が続(つづ)いている。
Life confined to bed has continued since the accident.
寝(ね)たきりの高齢者(こうれいしゃ)を介護(かいご)するのは体力的(たいりょくてき)にも精神的(せいしんてき)にも大(おお)きな負担(ふたん)だ。
Caring for a bedridden elderly person is a great burden both physically and mentally.

寝(ね)たきり is formed from 寝(ね)た (past tense of 寝(ね)る, to lie down) + きり (only, just — indicating a state that does not change). It literally means "lying down and staying that way."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝(ね)たきりの高齢者(こうれいしゃ) (bedridden elderly person)
  • 寝(ね)たきりの状態(じょうたい) (bedridden condition)
  • 寝(ね)たきりになる (to become bedridden)
  • 寝(ね)たきり防止(ぼうし) (prevention of becoming bedridden)

USAGE:
This is an important word in discussions of aging, healthcare, and nursing care (介護(かいご)) in Japan. 寝(ね)たきりにならない (not becoming bedridden) is a common health goal for the elderly. The word carries a serious, often somber tone.
