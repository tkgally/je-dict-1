尺度(しゃくど)

しゃくど
noun
scale, yardstick, measure
1. scale, yardstick, standard of measurement
A standard or criterion used to measure or evaluate something. Can refer to both physical measurement tools and abstract standards for judgment.
成功(せいこう)の尺度(しゃくど)は人(ひと)それぞれだ。
The measure of success varies from person to person.
お金(かね)だけを幸(しあわ)せの尺度(しゃくど)にすべきではない。
Money alone should not be the yardstick for happiness.
自分(じぶん)の尺度(しゃくど)で他人(たにん)を判断(はんだん)してはいけない。
You should not judge others by your own standards.

USAGE:
尺度(しゃくど) is most commonly used figuratively to mean a standard or criterion for evaluation. The literal meaning of a physical measuring tool is less common in modern usage.

ETYMOLOGY:
尺(しゃく) is a traditional unit of length (about 30.3 cm), and 度(ど) means degree or measure.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 評価(ひょうか)の尺度(しゃくど) — standard of evaluation
  • 価値(かち)の尺度(しゃくど) — measure of value
  • 尺度(しゃくど)で測(はか)る — to measure by a standard

SIMILAR WORDS:
基準(きじゅん) is the most common word for standard or criterion. 物差(ものさ)し is a physical ruler but is also used figuratively. 尺度(しゃくど) is more formal and often used in abstract or academic contexts.
