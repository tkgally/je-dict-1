工房(こうぼう)

こうぼう
noun
workshop, studio, atelier
1. workshop, studio, atelier
A place where craftspeople, artisans, or artists create their works. Implies hands-on creative or skilled work, often on a small scale.
陶芸(とうげい)工房(こうぼう)を見学(けんがく)する。
To tour a pottery workshop.
この工房(こうぼう)では手作(てづく)りのガラス製品(せいひん)を作(つく)っている。
At this workshop, they make handmade glass products.
彼(かれ)は古(ふる)い倉庫(そうこ)を改装(かいそう)して自分(じぶん)の工房(こうぼう)にし、そこで毎日(まいにち)家具(かぐ)を作(つく)っている。
He renovated an old warehouse into his own workshop and makes furniture there every day.

STRUCTURE:
工(こう) (work, craft) + 房(ぼう) (room, chamber).

USAGE:
工房(こうぼう) has a craft-oriented nuance, suggesting skilled handwork and artisanal production. It is commonly used for pottery, glasswork, woodworking, leather goods, and similar crafts. Compare with 工場(こうじょう) (factory), which implies larger-scale industrial production. Many 工房(こうぼう) are open to visitors and offer hands-on experiences.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 陶芸(とうげい)工房(こうぼう): pottery workshop
  • ガラス工房(こうぼう): glass workshop
  • 革(かわ)工房(こうぼう): leather workshop
  • 工房(こうぼう)見学(けんがく): workshop tour
  • 工房(こうぼう)を構(かま)える: to set up a workshop
