廃止(はいし)

はいし
noun, suru verb
abolition, discontinuation
1. abolition, discontinuation, repeal
The act of officially ending or abolishing a system, law, service, or practice that has been in effect. Implies a formal, deliberate decision to terminate something.
その制度(せいど)は廃止(はいし)された。
That system was abolished.
死刑(しけい)廃止(はいし)を求(もと)める声(こえ)が高(たか)まっている。
Calls for the abolition of the death penalty are growing louder.
利用者(りようしゃ)の減少(げんしょう)により、その路線(ろせん)のバスは来年度(らいねんど)廃止(はいし)される見込(みこ)みだ。
Due to declining ridership, the bus route is expected to be discontinued next fiscal year.

USAGE:
Used as a suru-verb: 廃止(はいし)する. Applies to systems, laws, regulations, services, and customs. Distinguish from 廃棄(はいき) (disposal of physical objects) and 撤廃(てっぱい) (abolition, often of restrictions or barriers). 廃止(はいし) emphasizes the official ending of something that was operational.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 制度(せいど)を廃止(はいし)する: abolish a system
  • 法律(ほうりつ)を廃止(はいし)する: repeal a law
  • 路線(ろせん)の廃止(はいし): discontinuation of a route
  • 死刑(しけい)廃止(はいし): abolition of the death penalty
