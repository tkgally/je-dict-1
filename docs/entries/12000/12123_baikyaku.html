売却(ばいきゃく)

ばいきゃく
noun, suru verb
sale, disposal (of assets)
1. sale, selling off, disposal (of property or assets)
The act of selling property, assets, or holdings, typically in a formal or business context. Implies a deliberate, often significant transaction such as selling real estate, company assets, or stocks. More formal than 売る.
土地(とち)を売却(ばいきゃく)する。
To sell off a piece of land.
会社(かいしゃ)は不採算(ふさいさん)事業(じぎょう)を売却(ばいきゃく)した。
The company sold off its unprofitable business.
保有(ほゆう)していた株式(かぶしき)をすべて売却(ばいきゃく)して、資金(しきん)を確保(かくほ)した。
I sold off all the stocks I held to secure funds.

USAGE:
売却(ばいきゃく) is a formal term used for the sale of significant assets such as real estate, businesses, stocks, or other property. It is common in news, business, and legal contexts. For everyday selling (e.g., selling at a market), 売(う)る is used instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 土地(とち)を売却(ばいきゃく)する: to sell land
  • 株式(かぶしき)を売却(ばいきゃく)する: to sell stocks
  • 資産(しさん)の売却(ばいきゃく): sale of assets
  • 売却(ばいきゃく)益(えき): profit from a sale
  • 売却(ばいきゃく)価格(かかく): selling price
