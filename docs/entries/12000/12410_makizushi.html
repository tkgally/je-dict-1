巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)

まきずし
noun
sushi roll, rolled sushi
1. sushi roll, rolled sushi
Sushi made by rolling vinegared rice and fillings in a sheet of seaweed (nori), then cutting it into rounds. A staple of Japanese home cooking and sushi restaurants.
巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)を作(つく)る。
To make sushi rolls.
節分(せつぶん)には恵方(えほう)巻(ま)きという巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)を食(た)べる。
On Setsubun, people eat a sushi roll called ehomaki.
母(はは)がお弁当(べんとう)に入(い)れてくれた巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)は、卵焼(たまごや)きとキュウリが入(はい)っていて美味(おい)しかった。
The sushi rolls my mother put in my lunch box had egg omelet and cucumber in them and were delicious.

STRUCTURE:
巻(ま)き (rolling) + 寿司(ずし) (sushi). Also written 巻(ま)きずし or 巻寿司(まきずし).

USAGE:

巻(ま)き寿司(ずし) has several varieties based on thickness:

  • 太巻(ふとま)き: thick roll with multiple fillings
  • 細巻(ほそま)き: thin roll with a single filling (e.g., かっぱ巻(ま)き for cucumber, 鉄火(てっか)巻(ま)き for tuna)
  • 裏巻(うらま)き: inside-out roll (rice on outside)

Rolling is done with a 巻(ま)きす (bamboo rolling mat). 巻(ま)き寿司(ずし) is commonly made at home for celebrations, parties, and 弁当(べんとう) (lunch boxes).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)を巻(ま)く: to roll sushi
  • 巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)を切(き)る: to cut sushi rolls
  • 手作(てづく)り巻(ま)き寿司(ずし): homemade sushi rolls
