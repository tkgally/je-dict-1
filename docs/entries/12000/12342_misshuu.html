密集(みっしゅう)

みっしゅう
noun, verb (suru)
crowding, dense concentration
1. crowding, dense concentration, clustering
A state where people, buildings, or objects are packed closely together in a limited area. Used both as a noun and as a suru verb.
住宅(じゅうたく)が密集(みっしゅう)している。
Houses are densely packed together.
密集(みっしゅう)した地域(ちいき)では火事(かじ)が広(ひろ)がりやすい。
Fires spread easily in densely packed areas.
人口(じんこう)が密集(みっしゅう)している都市(とし)では、交通(こうつう)渋滞(じゅうたい)が深刻(しんこく)だ。
In cities with dense populations, traffic congestion is a serious problem.

USAGE:
密集(みっしゅう) is typically used with する as a verb. The past participle 密集(みっしゅう)した functions as a modifier meaning "densely packed" or "concentrated."

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 住宅(じゅうたく)密集(みっしゅう)地(ち) — densely built residential area
  • 人口(じんこう)が密集(みっしゅう)する — population is densely concentrated
  • 密集(みっしゅう)した地域(ちいき) — densely packed area

SIMILAR WORDS:
密(みつ) refers to closeness or density in general and became widely known during COVID-19 (三密(さんみつ)). 過密(かみつ) means overcrowded and implies excessive density.
