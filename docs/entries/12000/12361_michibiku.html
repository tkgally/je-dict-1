導(みちび)く

みちびく
verb (godan)
to guide, to lead
1. to guide, to lead, to show the way
To guide someone physically or figuratively toward a destination, goal, or outcome. Often implies leading with authority or wisdom.
チームを勝利(しょうり)に導(みちび)く。
To lead the team to victory.
先生(せんせい)が生徒(せいと)を正(ただ)しい方向(ほうこう)に導(みちび)いた。
The teacher guided the students in the right direction.
彼(かれ)のリーダーシップがプロジェクトを成功(せいこう)に導(みちび)いた。
His leadership guided the project to success.
2. to derive, to arrive at (a conclusion or answer)
To reach a conclusion, answer, or result through reasoning, calculation, or investigation.
公式(こうしき)から答(こた)えを導(みちび)く。
To derive the answer from a formula.
実験(じっけん)データから結論(けつろん)を導(みちび)き出(だ)した。
We derived a conclusion from the experimental data.
論理的(ろんりてき)に考(かんが)えて正(ただ)しい答(こた)えを導(みちび)き出(だ)してください。
Please think logically and derive the correct answer.

GRAMMAR:
In sense 2, 導(みちび)く is often combined with 出(だ)す to form 導(みちび)き出(だ)す (to derive, to work out), which is very common in academic and scientific contexts.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勝利(しょうり)に導(みちび)く — to lead to victory
  • 成功(せいこう)に導(みちび)く — to guide to success
  • 結論(けつろん)を導(みちび)き出(だ)す — to derive a conclusion
  • 答(こた)えを導(みちび)く — to arrive at an answer

SIMILAR WORDS:
案内(あんない)する is to guide in the sense of showing around or giving directions. 率(ひき)いる means to lead or command a group. 導(みちび)く emphasizes guiding toward a positive outcome or correct answer.
