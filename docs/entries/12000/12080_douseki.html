同席(どうせき)

どうせき
noun / suru verb
being present together; sitting together; attending together
1. being present together, sitting together, attending together
Being present at the same place or event as someone else, especially at a meeting, meal, or formal occasion.
弁護士(べんごし)の同席(どうせき)を求(もと)めた。
I requested the presence of a lawyer.
上司(じょうし)が同席(どうせき)する会議(かいぎ)では緊張(きんちょう)する。
I get nervous at meetings where my boss is present.
保護者(ほごしゃ)の同席(どうせき)なしに、未成年者(みせいねんしゃ)への事情聴取(じじょうちょうしゅ)は行(おこな)わない方針(ほうしん)だ。
The policy is not to conduct questioning of minors without a guardian present.

USAGE:
Used as a suru verb: 同席(どうせき)する (to be present together, to attend together). Common in business and formal contexts. Can be used with を求(もと)める (to request someone's presence) or を許(ゆる)す (to allow someone to be present).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 同席(どうせき)する: to be present, to attend together
  • 同席(どうせき)を求(もと)める: to request someone's presence
  • 同席(どうせき)を許(ゆる)す: to allow someone to be present
  • ～の同席(どうせき)のもとで: in the presence of ~

SIMILAR WORDS:
同席(どうせき) emphasizes being in the same room or at the same table. 出席(しゅっせき) (attendance) focuses on attending an event. 立(た)ち会(あ)う (to witness, to be present at) implies being present to observe or oversee something.
