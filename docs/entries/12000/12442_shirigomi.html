尻込(しりご)み

しりごみ
noun, suru verb
flinching, shrinking back, hesitating
1. flinching, shrinking back, hesitating
Drawing back out of fear, reluctance, or intimidation. The image is of sitting back on one's heels (尻(しり) = buttocks) to avoid moving forward.
値段(ねだん)を見(み)て尻込(しりご)みした。
I flinched when I saw the price.
難(むずか)しそうで尻込(しりご)みしてしまう。
It looks difficult, so I end up shrinking back.
大勢(おおぜい)の前(まえ)で話(はな)すのは緊張(きんちょう)するが、尻込(しりご)みしていては成長(せいちょう)できない。
Speaking in front of a large audience is nerve-racking, but you can't grow if you keep shrinking back.

USAGE:
Almost always used as 尻込(しりご)みする. Implies a physical or psychological drawing back from something intimidating. The compound comes from 尻(しり) (buttocks) + 込(こ)む (to go into), evoking the image of sitting back in retreat.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 尻込(しりご)みする: to flinch, to shrink back
  • 尻込(しりご)みしてしまう: to end up hesitating
  • 尻込(しりご)みせずに: without flinching
