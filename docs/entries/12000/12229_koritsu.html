1. isolation, being cut off, standing alone
The state of being separated from others, either physically (cut off by a disaster, geographically remote) or socially (having no allies, being excluded). Can describe individuals, communities, or nations.
彼(かれ)はクラスで孤立(こりつ)している。
He is isolated in his class.
豪雪(ごうせつ)で村(むら)が孤立(こりつ)した。
The village was cut off by heavy snowfall.
国際(こくさい)社会(しゃかい)で孤立(こりつ)しないように、外交(がいこう)努力(どりょく)を続(つづ)ける必要(ひつよう)がある。
It is necessary to continue diplomatic efforts to avoid becoming isolated in the international community.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 孤立(こりつ)する: to become isolated
  • 孤立(こりつ)を深(ふか)める: to become increasingly isolated
  • 孤立(こりつ)無援(むえん): isolated and helpless (four-character compound)
  • 社会的(しゃかいてき)孤立(こりつ): social isolation
  • 国際的(こくさいてき)に孤立(こりつ)する: to be internationally isolated

USAGE:
Used across many contexts: social isolation of individuals, physical isolation of areas cut off by disasters, and diplomatic isolation of nations. The word carries a sense of vulnerability — being alone without support.

SIMILAR WORDS:
孤立(こりつ) vs 孤独(こどく): 孤独(こどく) (loneliness, solitude) focuses on the emotional experience of being alone. 孤立(こりつ) emphasizes the objective state of being separated or cut off from a group, whether one feels lonely or not.
