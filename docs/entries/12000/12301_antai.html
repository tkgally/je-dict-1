安泰(あんたい)

あんたい
noun / na-adjective
peace, security, safety
1. peace, security, safety, well-being
A state of being safe, stable, and free from danger or worry. Often used about positions, livelihoods, or organizations.
地位(ちい)は安泰(あんたい)だ。
The position is secure.
会社(かいしゃ)の経営(けいえい)は当分(とうぶん)安泰(あんたい)だろう。
The company's management will probably be stable for the time being.
レギュラーの座(ざ)は安泰(あんたい)とは言(い)えない状況(じょうきょう)だ。
The situation is such that his regular position cannot be called secure.

安泰(あんたい) combines 安(あん) (peace, safety) and 泰(たい) (calm, peaceful). It describes a stable, secure state, particularly of positions, livelihoods, or organizations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地位(ちい)が安泰(あんたい) (position is secure)
  • 身(み)の安泰(あんたい) (personal safety)
  • 安泰(あんたい)を祈(いの)る (to pray for well-being)
  • 国家(こっか)の安泰(あんたい) (national security/stability)

USAGE:
Often used as a predicate (安泰(あんたい)だ) or as a noun. Carries a sense of things being settled and worry-free. Frequently appears in discussions of job security, organizational stability, and future outlook.
