底辺(ていへん)

ていへん
noun
base (of a triangle); bottom of society
1. base (of a triangle or shape)
The bottom side of a geometric figure, especially a triangle. A standard term in mathematics education.
三角形(さんかくけい)の底辺(ていへん)の長(なが)さを求(もと)めなさい。
Find the length of the base of the triangle.
底辺(ていへん)×高(たか)さ÷2で面積(めんせき)が求(もと)められる。
The area can be calculated with base times height divided by 2.
底辺(ていへん)が5センチ、高(たか)さが3センチの三角形(さんかくけい)の面積(めんせき)を計算(けいさん)しなさい。
Calculate the area of a triangle with a base of 5 cm and a height of 3 cm.
2. bottom, lower strata, grassroots level
The lowest level or foundation of a society, organization, or hierarchy. Can refer neutrally to the grassroots or foundation, or negatively to the bottom rung of society.
社会(しゃかい)の底辺(ていへん)で働(はたら)く人(ひと)たち。
People who work at the bottom of society.
底辺(ていへん)から這(は)い上(あ)がった成功者(せいこうしゃ)の物語(ものがたり)。
A story of a successful person who climbed up from the bottom.
スポーツの底辺(ていへん)を広(ひろ)げることが将来(しょうらい)の強化(きょうか)につながる。
Broadening the grassroots base of a sport leads to future improvement.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is a standard math term learned in school. Sense 2 can be neutral (底辺(ていへん)を広(ひろ)げる, broaden the base/grassroots) or negative (社会(しゃかい)の底辺(ていへん), bottom of society). In recent internet slang, 底辺(ていへん) is sometimes used pejoratively to describe low-status jobs or lifestyles, which many consider offensive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 底辺(ていへん)の長(なが)さ: length of the base
  • 社会(しゃかい)の底辺(ていへん): bottom of society
  • 底辺(ていへん)を広(ひろ)げる: broaden the base
  • 底辺(ていへん)から這(は)い上(あ)がる: climb up from the bottom
