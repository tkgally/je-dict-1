席巻(せっけん)

せっけん
noun, suru verb
sweeping over, dominating
1. sweeping over, sweeping conquest, domination
Rapidly spreading over and dominating an area, market, or field. Originally a military term meaning to roll up enemy territory like a mat, now commonly used for trends, products, or cultural phenomena that rapidly take over.
そのブランドが世界(せかい)を席巻(せっけん)した。
That brand swept the world.
スマートフォンが市場(しじょう)を席巻(せっけん)している。
Smartphones are dominating the market.
日本(にほん)のアニメが海外(かいがい)の若者(わかもの)文化(ぶんか)を席巻(せっけん)していると言(い)われている。
It is said that Japanese anime is sweeping youth culture abroad.

USAGE:
Used as a suru-verb: 席巻(せっけん)する. Also written as 席捲(せっけん). Almost always used in the pattern ～を席巻(せっけん)する (sweep over ~). Carries a strong, dramatic tone and is common in news headlines and feature articles. Not to be confused with the homophone 石鹸(せっけん) (soap).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世界(せかい)を席巻(せっけん)する: sweep the world
  • 市場(しじょう)を席巻(せっけん)する: dominate the market
  • 業界(ぎょうかい)を席巻(せっけん)する: sweep through the industry
