固執(こしつ)

こしつ
noun
persistence; insistence; obstinacy
1. persistence, insistence, obstinacy, clinging
Stubbornly holding on to one's opinion, method, or position and refusing to change. Usually carries a negative nuance.
自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)に固執(こしつ)する。
To insist on one's own opinion.
古(ふる)いやり方(かた)に固執(こしつ)しすぎだ。
You're clinging too much to the old way of doing things.
一(ひと)つの方法(ほうほう)に固執(こしつ)せず、柔軟(じゅうなん)に対応(たいおう)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to respond flexibly rather than clinging to a single method.

READING:
Traditionally read こしゅう, but こしつ has become the dominant modern reading. Both are acceptable.

USAGE:
Almost always carries a negative nuance — the speaker views the persistence as stubborn or unreasonable. The pattern ～に固執(こしつ)する is standard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意見(いけん)に固執(こしつ)する: to insist on one's opinion
  • 前例(ぜんれい)に固執(こしつ)する: to cling to precedent
  • 固執(こしつ)せずに: without being stubborn about
  • ～に固執(こしつ)しすぎる: to cling too much to ~
