孕(はら)む

はらむ
godan verb
to conceive; to be fraught with
1. to conceive, to become pregnant
To become pregnant with a child. Can also be used for animals.
子(こ)を孕(はら)む。
To conceive a child.
猫(ねこ)が子(こ)を孕(はら)んでいるようだ。
The cat seems to be pregnant.
彼女(かのじょ)は双子(ふたご)を孕(はら)んでいることがわかった。
It was found that she was pregnant with twins.
2. to be fraught with, to contain, to harbor
To contain something within, especially something potentially dangerous or significant. Used figuratively for situations, plans, or ideas that carry risk or potential.
危険(きけん)を孕(はら)む。
To be fraught with danger.
この計画(けいかく)は問題(もんだい)を孕(はら)んでいる。
This plan harbors problems.
帆(ほ)が風(かぜ)を孕(はら)んで、船(ふね)は勢(いきお)いよく進(すす)んだ。
The sails caught the wind, and the boat moved forward briskly.

孕(はら)む is a godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)) with two main uses: a literal sense of becoming pregnant and a figurative sense of containing something latent.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを孕(はら)む

ASPECT (ている):
孕(はら)んでいる indicates a resulting state: being pregnant (sense 1) or currently containing/harboring (sense 2).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子(こ)を孕(はら)む (to conceive a child)
  • 危険(きけん)を孕(はら)む (to be fraught with danger)
  • 問題(もんだい)を孕(はら)む (to harbor problems)
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)を孕(はら)む (to contain contradictions)

USAGE:
Sense 1 is somewhat literary. In everyday conversation, 妊娠(にんしん)する is more common for human pregnancy. Sense 2 is used primarily in written or formal contexts.
