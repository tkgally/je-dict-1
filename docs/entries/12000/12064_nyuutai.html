入隊(にゅうたい)

にゅうたい
noun / suru verb
enlisting; joining the military
1. enlisting, joining the military
The act of entering military service or joining a military unit. Can also be used for other organized groups with a military-like structure.
十八歳(じゅうはっさい)で入隊(にゅうたい)した。
He enlisted at the age of eighteen.
入隊(にゅうたい)希望者(きぼうしゃ)は体力(たいりょく)検査(けんさ)を受(う)ける必要(ひつよう)がある。
Those wishing to enlist must undergo a physical fitness test.
祖父(そふ)は戦時中(せんじちゅう)に徴兵(ちょうへい)されて入隊(にゅうたい)し、終戦(しゅうせん)まで軍(ぐん)に所属(しょぞく)していた。
My grandfather was drafted and enlisted during the war, and remained in the military until the war ended.

USAGE:
Often used as a suru verb: 入隊(にゅうたい)する (to enlist). The word implies formally joining a military organization, whether voluntarily or through conscription.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入隊(にゅうたい)する: to enlist, to join the military
  • 入隊(にゅうたい)希望者(きぼうしゃ): enlistment applicant
  • 入隊(にゅうたい)式(しき): enlistment ceremony
  • 入隊(にゅうたい)検査(けんさ): enlistment examination

SIMILAR WORDS:
The opposite is 除隊(じょたい) (discharge from the military). 志願(しがん) (volunteering) emphasizes the voluntary nature of enlistment, while 徴兵(ちょうへい) (conscription) refers to compulsory military service.
