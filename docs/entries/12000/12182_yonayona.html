夜(よ)な夜(よ)な

よなよな
adverb
night after night, every night
1. night after night, every night, nightly
Describes something that happens repeatedly every night. Often implies secrecy, obsession, or something slightly unusual about the nighttime activity.
夜(よ)な夜(よ)な本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいる。
I read books night after night.
彼(かれ)は夜(よ)な夜(よ)な一人(ひとり)で出(で)かけるらしい。
Apparently he goes out alone night after night.
その古(ふる)い屋敷(やしき)からは夜(よ)な夜(よ)な不気味(ぶきみ)な音(おと)が聞(き)こえてくると噂(うわさ)されている。
It is rumored that eerie sounds can be heard night after night from that old mansion.

夜(よ)な夜(よ)な is an adverb formed by repeating 夜(よ)な. It implies recurring nighttime activity, often with a nuance of something secretive, mysterious, or obsessive.

POSITION:
Typically appears before the verb or at the beginning of the clause.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 毎晩(まいばん): every night (neutral, factual)
  • 夜(よ)な夜(よ)な: night after night (literary, implies something noteworthy or secretive about the repetition)

The word has a slightly dramatic or storytelling quality, making it common in novels, ghost stories, and descriptions of unusual habits.
