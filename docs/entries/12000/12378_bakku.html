1. background, backdrop, backing
The background or backdrop of a scene, or the support behind a person or project. From English "back."
バックに山(やま)が見(み)える。
You can see mountains in the background.
あの政治家(せいじか)には大(おお)きな企業(きぎょう)がバックについている。
That politician has a large corporation backing him.
夕焼(ゆうや)けをバックに写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)った。
I took a photo with the sunset in the background.
2. reversing, backing up (a vehicle)
The act of driving a vehicle in reverse. Used as a suru verb: バックする.
ゆっくりバックしてください。
Please reverse slowly.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)でバックするときは後方(こうほう)確認(かくにん)を忘(わす)れずに。
Don't forget to check behind you when backing up in a parking lot.
狭(せま)い道(みち)で対向車(たいこうしゃ)が来(き)たので、バックして道(みち)を譲(ゆず)った。
A car came from the opposite direction on a narrow road, so I backed up to let it pass.

USAGE:
バック has several distinct uses in Japanese. As a background/support word, it often appears in the pattern ～をバックに (with ~ as a backdrop) or バックがつく (to have backing). As a driving term, バックする is the standard way to say "to reverse" a vehicle.

NOTE ON MEANING:
バック does not mean "bag" in Japanese — that word is バッグ (baggu). The two are commonly confused by learners.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～をバックに: with ~ in the background
  • バックがつく: to gain backing/support
  • バックする: to reverse (a vehicle)
  • バックミラー: rearview mirror
  • バック駐車(ちゅうしゃ): reverse parking
