地価(ちか)

ちか
noun
land price, land value
1. land price, land value
The monetary value of a plot of land. Used in real estate, economics, and urban planning contexts. Japan publishes official land prices annually, and land price trends are a major economic indicator.
駅前(えきまえ)の地価(ちか)が上(あ)がった。
Land prices near the station went up.
都心(としん)の地価(ちか)は非常(ひじょう)に高(たか)い。
Land prices in the city center are extremely high.
バブル崩壊(ほうかい)のあと、全国的(ぜんこくてき)に地価(ちか)が大幅(おおはば)に下落(げらく)した。
After the bubble burst, land prices dropped significantly across the country.

USAGE:
地価(ちか) is a common economic term in Japan. Land prices have historically been a major factor in the Japanese economy, especially during the asset bubble of the late 1980s and its subsequent collapse.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地価(ちか)が上昇(じょうしょう)する: land prices rise
  • 地価(ちか)が下落(げらく)する: land prices fall
  • 公示(こうじ)地価(ちか): officially assessed land price
  • 地価(ちか)高騰(こうとう): soaring land prices

Related Words

Homophone: 地下(ちか) (underground)
