宿敵(しゅくてき)

しゅくてき
noun
archenemy, old rival
1. archenemy, old rival, longtime nemesis
An enemy or rival with whom one has had a long-standing conflict or rivalry. Implies a deep, enduring opposition.
宿敵(しゅくてき)を倒(たお)す。
To defeat one's archenemy.
宿敵(しゅくてき)との対決(たいけつ)がついに実現(じつげん)した。
The showdown with the longtime rival has finally come to pass.
二(ふた)つのチームは長年(ながねん)の宿敵(しゅくてき)で、試合(しあい)のたびにスタジアムは満員(まんいん)になる。
The two teams are longtime rivals, and the stadium is packed every time they play.

宿敵(しゅくてき) combines 宿(しゅく) (long-standing, old) and 敵(てき) (enemy). The 宿(しゅく) component implies something that has persisted over a long period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 宿敵(しゅくてき)を倒(たお)す (to defeat one's archenemy)
  • 宿敵(しゅくてき)との対決(たいけつ) (confrontation with a nemesis)
  • 宿敵(しゅくてき)に勝(か)つ (to triumph over a rival)
  • 長年(ながねん)の宿敵(しゅくてき) (longtime rival)

USAGE:
Used in sports, fiction, history, and politics to describe deeply rooted rivalries. It carries a dramatic, literary feel and is commonly seen in sports journalism and manga/anime narratives.
