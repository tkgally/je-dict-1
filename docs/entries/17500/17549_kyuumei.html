究明(きゅうめい)

きゅうめい
noun / verb (suru)
investigation; inquiry; getting to the bottom of
1. investigation; thorough inquiry; getting to the bottom of
The act of thoroughly investigating and clarifying the truth or cause of something, especially accidents, incidents, or problems.
事故(じこ)の原因(げんいん)を究明(きゅうめい)する。
To investigate the cause of the accident.
真相(しんそう)究明(きゅうめい)を求(もと)める声(こえ)が高(たか)まっている。
Calls for getting to the bottom of the truth are growing louder.
専門家(せんもんか)チームが原因(げんいん)究明(きゅうめい)にあたっている。
A team of experts is working on investigating the cause.

USAGE:
A formal word often used in news and official reports. Combines 究(きゅう) (to investigate thoroughly) and 明(めい) (to clarify). Commonly paired with 原因(げんいん) (cause) or 真相(しんそう) (truth) to express the goal of the investigation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原因(げんいん)究明(きゅうめい) (investigation of the cause)
  • 真相(しんそう)究明(きゅうめい) (getting to the truth)
  • 事実(じじつ)究明(きゅうめい) (fact-finding)
  • 究明(きゅうめい)にあたる (to work on investigating)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 調査(ちょうさ): investigation, survey — broader, more neutral
  • 解明(かいめい): elucidation, clarification — similar but emphasizes making clear
  • 捜査(そうさ): criminal investigation — specifically by police or authorities
