1. tie-up, promotional collaboration
A joint promotional partnership between companies, brands, or media properties. Common in entertainment, where a song or product is paired with a TV drama, anime, or film.
タイアップ曲(きょく)がヒットした。
The tie-up song became a hit.
あのアニメと飲料(いんりょう)メーカーがタイアップしている。
That anime has a promotional tie-up with a beverage company.
ドラマの主題歌(しゅだいか)に起用(きよう)されたタイアップ曲(きょく)が話題(わだい)になっている。
The tie-up song chosen as the drama's theme song is getting a lot of attention.

ORIGIN:
From English "tie-up." In English, "tie-up" usually means a delay or a connection; the Japanese meaning of promotional collaboration is a Japanese-specific usage.

USAGE:
Most commonly seen in the entertainment industry. A タイアップ曲(きょく) is a song used as a theme or insert song for a TV drama, anime, movie, or commercial. Brands also do タイアップ企画(きかく) (collaborative campaigns) with popular media properties.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タイアップ曲(きょく): tie-up song (theme song)
  • タイアップ企画(きかく): tie-up campaign
  • タイアップ商品(しょうひん): tie-up merchandise
  • タイアップする: to collaborate, to do a tie-up
