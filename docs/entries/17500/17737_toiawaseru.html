1. to inquire, to make an inquiry, to contact for information
To contact a person, company, or organization to ask about something. Used for formal or semi-formal information requests, customer service inquiries, and official communications.
詳(くわ)しくは窓口(まどぐち)に問(と)い合(あ)わせてください。
Please inquire at the counter for details.
在庫(ざいこ)があるかどうか、店(みせ)に電話(でんわ)で問(と)い合(あ)わせた。
I called the store to ask whether they had it in stock.
旅行(りょこう)の日程(にってい)について旅行会社(りょこうがいしゃ)に問(と)い合(あ)わせたところ、すぐに返事(へんじ)が来(き)た。
When I inquired with the travel agency about the itinerary, I received a reply right away.

USAGE:
A polite, semi-formal verb commonly used in business and customer service contexts. Often appears in the set phrase お問(と)い合(あ)わせ (noun form, with honorific お) on websites and signs.

GRAMMAR:
Ichidan verb. Takes に to mark the person or organization contacted, and について or に関(かん)して for the topic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電話(でんわ)で問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire by phone
  • メールで問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire by email
  • 窓口(まどぐち)に問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire at the counter
  • お問(と)い合(あ)わせ先(さき): contact information
