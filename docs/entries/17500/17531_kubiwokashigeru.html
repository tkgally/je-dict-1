首(くび)を傾(かし)げる

くびをかしげる
expression / verb (ichidan)
to tilt one's head in puzzlement; to wonder
1. to tilt one's head in puzzlement; to be doubtful; to wonder
To express doubt, puzzlement, or skepticism by tilting one's head to one side. Used both for the physical gesture and figuratively for feeling uncertain or unconvinced.
彼(かれ)の説明(せつめい)に首(くび)を傾(かし)げた。
I tilted my head in puzzlement at his explanation.
不思議(ふしぎ)に思(おも)って首(くび)を傾(かし)げた。
I tilted my head, finding it strange.
専門家(せんもんか)でさえ首(くび)を傾(かし)げるような難問(なんもん)だった。
It was such a difficult problem that even experts were puzzled.

USAGE:
A common idiomatic expression that combines the physical gesture of head-tilting with the mental state of doubt or confusion. Can describe the actual physical action or be used figuratively to mean "to have doubts about." The variant 首(くび)をひねる has a similar meaning but implies more active thinking or struggling with a problem.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～に首(くび)を傾(かし)げる (to be puzzled by ~)
  • 首(くび)を傾(かし)げざるを得(え)ない (cannot help but wonder)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 首(くび)をひねる: to puzzle over — implies more active deliberation
  • 疑問(ぎもん)に思(おも)う: to find doubtful — more straightforward
  • 腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない: to not make sense — stronger dissatisfaction
