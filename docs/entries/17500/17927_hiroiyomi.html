1. skimming; browsing; reading selectively
Reading only selected parts of a text rather than the whole thing, picking out what seems interesting or relevant. Literally 'picking-up reading.'
新聞(しんぶん)を拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みした。
I skimmed the newspaper.
時間(じかん)がなくて拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みしかできなかった。
I didn't have time and could only skim through it.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)を拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みして、重要(じゅうよう)な部分(ぶぶん)だけ確認(かくにん)した。
I skimmed the report and checked only the important parts.

USAGE:
Suggests a quick, selective style of reading rather than careful, thorough reading. Not necessarily negative — sometimes practical when short on time. Can be used with する as a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みする (to skim, to browse)
  • 拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みしかできない (can only skim)
  • ざっと拾(ひろ)い読(よ)みする (to quickly skim through)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 斜(なな)め読(よ)み: speed-reading, skimming — similar meaning, literally 'diagonal reading'
  • 流(なが)し読(よ)み: cursory reading — reading quickly without focusing
