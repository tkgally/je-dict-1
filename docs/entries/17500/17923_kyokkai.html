1. misinterpretation; distortion; misconstruction
Interpreting someone's words or intentions in a twisted or incorrect way, often to suit one's own purposes. Implies deliberate or biased distortion rather than innocent misunderstanding.
私(わたし)の発言(はつげん)を曲解(きょっかい)しないでほしい。
I don't want you to misinterpret what I said.
メディアが政治家(せいじか)の言葉(ことば)を曲解(きょっかい)して報道(ほうどう)した。
The media reported the politician's words in a distorted way.
相手(あいて)の意図(いと)を曲解(きょっかい)すると、不要(ふよう)なトラブルを招(まね)くことになる。
If you misinterpret the other person's intentions, it will lead to unnecessary trouble.

USAGE:
Stronger than 誤解(ごかい) (misunderstanding), which can be innocent. 曲解(きょっかい) implies a willful or biased twisting of meaning. The kanji 曲(きょく) means 'bend' or 'twist,' reflecting the distortion involved.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 曲解(きょっかい)する (to misinterpret)
  • 曲解(きょっかい)を招(まね)く (to invite misinterpretation)
  • 意味(いみ)を曲解(きょっかい)する (to distort the meaning)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誤解(ごかい): misunderstanding — neutral; can be accidental
  • 歪曲(わいきょく): distortion, falsification — even stronger; implies deliberate falsification of facts
