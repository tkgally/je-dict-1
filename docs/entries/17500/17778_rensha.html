1. continuous shooting; burst mode
Taking multiple photographs in rapid succession by holding down the shutter button. A standard feature on digital cameras and smartphones used to capture fast-moving subjects.
連写(れんしゃ)モードに切(き)り替(か)えた。
I switched to burst mode.
動(うご)きの速(はや)い被写体(ひしゃたい)には連写(れんしゃ)が便利(べんり)だ。
Burst mode is convenient for fast-moving subjects.
このカメラは一秒間(いちびょうかん)に最大(さいだい)二十枚(にじゅうまい)の連写(れんしゃ)が可能(かのう)だ。
This camera is capable of shooting up to twenty frames per second in burst mode.

USAGE:
A compound of 連(れん) (continuous) + 写(しゃ) (photograph). Used primarily in photography and camera discussions. Also used as a suru verb: 連写(れんしゃ)する (to shoot in burst mode).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連写(れんしゃ)モード (burst/continuous shooting mode)
  • 連写(れんしゃ)速度(そくど) (burst shooting speed)
  • 高速(こうそく)連写(れんしゃ) (high-speed burst shooting)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 単写(たんしゃ): single-shot mode — taking one photo at a time
  • 連続撮影(れんぞくさつえい): continuous shooting — a more formal synonym
