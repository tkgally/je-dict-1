大葉(おおば)

おおば
noun
green perilla leaf; shiso leaf
1. green perilla leaf; shiso leaf
The large green leaf of the perilla plant (Perilla frutescens), widely used as a garnish and flavoring in Japanese cuisine. In culinary contexts, the green variety is typically called ooba, while the botanical term is aojiso.
刺身(さしみ)に大葉(おおば)を添(そ)える。
To garnish sashimi with a perilla leaf.
大葉(おおば)の天(てん)ぷらはさくさくしておいしい。
Perilla leaf tempura is crispy and delicious.
鶏(とり)ささみに大葉(おおば)と梅(うめ)を巻(ま)いて焼(や)くと、さっぱりした味(あじ)に仕上(しあ)がる。
If you wrap chicken tenderloin with perilla leaves and pickled plum and grill it, it comes out with a refreshing flavor.

USAGE:
大葉(おおば) is the culinary name for the green perilla leaf, commonly used in supermarkets and recipes. The plant itself is called 紫蘇(しそ) (shiso), and the green variety is 青紫蘇(あおじそ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大葉(おおば)の天(てん)ぷら (perilla tempura)
  • 大葉(おおば)を刻(きざ)む (to shred perilla leaves)
  • 大葉(おおば)を巻(ま)く (to wrap with perilla leaves)

SIMILAR WORDS:
紫蘇(しそ) is the general/botanical name for the perilla plant. 大葉(おおば) specifically refers to the green leaf as a food ingredient.
