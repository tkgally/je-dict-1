爆走(ばくそう)

ばくそう
noun, verb (suru)
reckless driving; blazing run
1. reckless driving; tearing along at high speed
Driving or riding a vehicle at dangerously high speed, often recklessly. Associated with illegal street racing and biker gangs.
バイクが深夜(しんや)の道(みち)を爆走(ばくそう)していた。
A motorcycle was tearing down the road late at night.
住宅街(じゅうたくがい)を爆走(ばくそう)する車(くるま)に住民(じゅうみん)が困(こま)っている。
Residents are troubled by cars speeding recklessly through the neighborhood.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)を爆走(ばくそう)して警察(けいさつ)に捕(つか)まった。
He was caught by the police for speeding recklessly on the highway.
2. blazing run; unstoppable momentum
Used figuratively to describe someone or something going at full speed with unstoppable energy, such as a winning streak in sports or a hit product's sales.
チームは連勝(れんしょう)街道(かいどう)を爆走(ばくそう)している。
The team is on an unstoppable winning streak.
新商品(しんしょうひん)が発売(はつばい)直後(ちょくご)から爆走(ばくそう)し、売上(うりあげ)記録(きろく)を更新(こうしん)した。
The new product took off right after release and broke sales records.
あのお笑(わら)いコンビは今(いま)テレビで爆走中(ばくそうちゅう)だ。
That comedy duo is on a tear on TV right now.

USAGE:
爆走(ばくそう) literally means "explosive running." In sense 1, it is associated with 暴走族(ぼうそうぞく) (biker gangs) and reckless driving. In sense 2, it is used positively to describe impressive momentum.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 爆走(ばくそう)する (to tear along, to blaze through)
  • 爆走中(ばくそうちゅう) (in the middle of a blazing run)
  • 爆走(ばくそう)モード (full-speed mode)
