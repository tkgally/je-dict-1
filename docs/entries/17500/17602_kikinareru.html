1. to become used to hearing
to become familiar with a sound, voice, or expression through repeated exposure
聞(き)き慣(な)れない言葉(ことば)だ。
That's an unfamiliar word.
彼(かれ)の声(こえ)にも聞(き)き慣(な)れてきた。
I've gotten used to his voice too.
日本語(にほんご)の発音(はつおん)に聞(き)き慣(な)れるまで時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to get used to hearing Japanese pronunciation.

FORMATION:
聞(き)く (to hear) + 慣(な)れる (to become accustomed) → to become accustomed to hearing

COMMON USAGE:
Often used in the negative form 聞(き)き慣(な)れない to describe unfamiliar sounds or expressions.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れる (to become used to eating)
  • 見(み)慣(な)れる (to become used to seeing)
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れる (to become used to living)
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる (to become used to using)
