1. to explain; to give commentary; to provide analysis
To explain something in detail, often providing background information or expert analysis. Commonly used for media commentary, educational explanations, and expert analysis.
専門家(せんもんか)がニュースを解説(かいせつ)する。
An expert provides commentary on the news.
この本(ほん)は日本(にほん)の歴史(れきし)をわかりやすく解説(かいせつ)している。
This book explains Japanese history in an easy-to-understand way.
試合(しあい)の解説(かいせつ)を担当(たんとう)しているアナウンサーの話(はなし)がとても面白(おもしろ)い。
The announcer in charge of the game commentary is very interesting.

USAGE:
Used both as a suru verb and as a standalone noun. As a noun, it often refers to commentary or explanatory notes. Common in media, sports broadcasting, and educational contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 解説者(かいせつしゃ) (commentator)
  • 解説記事(かいせつきじ) (explanatory article)
  • 解説番組(かいせつばんぐみ) (commentary program)
  • わかりやすく解説(かいせつ)する (to explain clearly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 説明(せつめい)する: to explain — more general, everyday explanation
  • 解説(かいせつ)する: to explain with expert analysis or commentary — more detailed and authoritative
