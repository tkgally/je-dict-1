飲(の)み屋(や)

のみや
noun
bar; pub; drinking establishment
1. bar; pub; drinking establishment
A casual establishment where people go to drink alcohol, typically serving food as well. Covers a range of places from small standing bars to izakaya-style pubs.
駅前(えきまえ)の飲(の)み屋(や)に行(い)こう。
Let's go to the bar near the station.
この通(とお)りには飲(の)み屋(や)がたくさん並(なら)んでいる。
There are many bars lined up along this street.
昨日(きのう)は会社(かいしゃ)の同僚(どうりょう)と飲(の)み屋(や)で遅(おそ)くまで話(はな)していた。
Yesterday I was talking late at a bar with my coworkers.

USAGE:
A casual, everyday word for places where people drink alcohol. More colloquial than 居酒屋(いざかや), which specifically refers to Japanese-style pubs. 飲(の)み屋(や) can refer to any type of drinking establishment, from a tiny 立(た)ち飲(の)み (standing bar) to a larger pub.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飲(の)み屋(や)に行(い)く (to go to a bar)
  • 行(い)きつけの飲(の)み屋(や) (one's regular bar)
  • 飲(の)み屋(や)街(がい) (bar district)
  • 安(やす)い飲(の)み屋(や) (cheap bar)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 居酒屋(いざかや): Japanese-style pub — more specific, often used as a business name
  • バー: bar — suggests a Western-style establishment
  • 酒場(さかば): drinking place — slightly literary or old-fashioned
