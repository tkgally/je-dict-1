集積所(しゅうせきじょ)

しゅうせきじょ
noun
collection point; collection site
1. collection point; collection site; gathering place
A designated location where things are gathered or collected, most commonly used for garbage and recycling collection points in residential areas.
ごみは集積所(しゅうせきじょ)に出(だ)してください。
Please put your garbage at the collection point.
朝(あさ)八時(はちじ)までに集積所(しゅうせきじょ)にごみを出(だ)す決(き)まりだ。
The rule is to put garbage at the collection point by 8 a.m.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)してきたばかりなので、ごみの集積所(しゅうせきじょ)がどこか分(わ)からなかった。
Since I had just moved in, I didn't know where the garbage collection point was.

USAGE:
A compound of 集積(しゅうせき) (accumulation, collection) + 所(じょ) (place). Most commonly used in daily life to refer to the designated garbage collection spot in a neighborhood. In Japan, garbage must be taken to a specific community collection point, not left at the curb in front of one's own house.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ごみの集積所(しゅうせきじょ) (garbage collection point)
  • 集積所(しゅうせきじょ)に出(だ)す (to put out at the collection point)
  • 集積所(しゅうせきじょ)の掃除(そうじ) (cleaning the collection point)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ごみ置(お)き場(ば): garbage spot — a more casual/colloquial term
  • 集積場(しゅうせきば): collection site — similar meaning, sometimes used for larger-scale operations
