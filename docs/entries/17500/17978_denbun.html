伝聞(でんぶん)

でんぶん
noun, verb-suru
hearsay; rumor; secondhand information
1. hearsay; rumor; secondhand information
Information that one has heard from others rather than witnessed firsthand. Often used in formal or legal contexts to distinguish direct knowledge from reported information.
それは伝聞(でんぶん)にすぎない。
That's nothing more than hearsay.
伝聞(でんぶん)によると、来月(らいげつ)社長(しゃちょう)が交代(こうたい)するらしい。
According to what I've heard, the company president will be replaced next month.
裁判(さいばん)では伝聞(でんぶん)証拠(しょうこ)は原則(げんそく)として認(みと)められない。
In court, hearsay evidence is generally not admissible.

USAGE:
A formal word used in news, legal, and academic contexts. In grammar studies, 伝聞(でんぶん) also refers to the hearsay evidential form (〜そうだ, 〜とのことだ) — a way of marking that information comes from a secondary source.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伝聞(でんぶん)によると (according to what one has heard)
  • 伝聞(でんぶん)情報(じょうほう) (hearsay information)
  • 伝聞(でんぶん)証拠(しょうこ) (hearsay evidence)
  • 伝聞(でんぶん)にすぎない (nothing more than hearsay)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うわさ: rumor — more colloquial and informal
  • 風聞(ふうぶん): rumor — literary synonym, less common
  • 又聞(またぎ)き: secondhand information — specifically means hearing something through an intermediary
