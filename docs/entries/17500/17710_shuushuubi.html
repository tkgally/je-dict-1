収集日(しゅうしゅうび)

しゅうしゅうび
noun
collection day (for garbage)
1. collection day; pickup day
The designated day on which garbage, recyclables, or other waste is collected by the municipal service. An essential part of daily life in Japan, where garbage sorting and collection schedules are strictly followed.
燃(も)えるゴミの収集日(しゅうしゅうび)は火曜日(かようび)だ。
Burnable garbage collection day is Tuesday.
収集日(しゅうしゅうび)を確認(かくにん)してからゴミを出(だ)してください。
Please check the collection day before putting out your garbage.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)したばかりで、地域(ちいき)の収集日(しゅうしゅうび)カレンダーをまだもらっていない。
I just moved and haven't received the local garbage collection calendar yet.

USAGE:
収集日(しゅうしゅうび) is most commonly used for garbage and recycling pickup schedules. In Japan, each type of waste (燃(も)えるゴミ, 燃(も)えないゴミ, 資源(しげん)ゴミ, etc.) has its own designated collection day.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴミの収集日(しゅうしゅうび) (garbage collection day)
  • 収集日(しゅうしゅうび)カレンダー (collection day calendar)
  • 次(つぎ)の収集日(しゅうしゅうび) (next collection day)
