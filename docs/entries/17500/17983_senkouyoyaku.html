1. advance booking; advance reservation; pre-order
A reservation or booking made ahead of general availability, typically offered to members, fans, or priority customers before tickets or products go on sale to the public.
先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)が始(はじ)まった。
Advance bookings have started.
会員(かいいん)限定(げんてい)の先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)でチケットを取(と)った。
I got tickets through a members-only advance booking.
新作(しんさく)ゲームの先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)を受(う)け付(つ)けておりますので、お早(はや)めにお申(もう)し込(こ)みください。
We are accepting advance orders for the new game, so please apply early.

USAGE:
Common in entertainment (concert tickets, events) and retail (new product launches, limited editions). Often offered as a perk for fan club members or loyalty program participants.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)する (to pre-order / book in advance)
  • 先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)受付(うけつけ) (advance booking reception)
  • 先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)特典(とくてん) (advance booking bonus)
  • 先行(せんこう)予約(よやく)開始(かいし) (start of advance bookings)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予約(よやく): reservation — the general term for any booking
  • 先行(せんこう)販売(はんばい): advance sale — emphasizes the selling rather than the reserving
