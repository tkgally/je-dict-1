表向(おもてむ)き

おもてむき
noun, adverb
outwardly; officially; on the surface
1. outwardly; officially; on the surface
What is presented publicly or officially, as opposed to the real or hidden situation. Implies a gap between appearance and reality.
表向(おもてむ)きは賛成(さんせい)している。
On the surface, they agree.
表向(おもてむ)きの理由(りゆう)と本当(ほんとう)の理由(りゆう)は違(ちが)う。
The official reason and the real reason are different.
表向(おもてむ)きは友好(ゆうこう)関係(かんけい)だが、裏(うら)では対立(たいりつ)している。
On the surface it's a friendly relationship, but behind the scenes they're at odds.

USAGE:
Used as an adverb (表向(おもてむ)きは...) or as a noun modifier (表向(おもてむ)きの理由(りゆう)). Carries the implication that the reality differs from what is shown. Often contrasted with 裏(うら) (behind the scenes) or 本音(ほんね) (true feelings).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 表向(おもてむ)きは〜だが (outwardly it's... but)
  • 表向(おもてむ)きの理由(りゆう) (the official/stated reason)
  • 表向(おもてむ)きの態度(たいど) (outward attitude)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建前(たてまえ): public position, façade — focuses on social expectations vs. true feelings
  • 裏(うら): behind the scenes — the opposite, what is hidden from public view
