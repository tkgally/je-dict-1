1. wandering, roaming, drifting, vagrancy
Traveling from place to place without a fixed destination or home. Can be romantic (the wandering artist) or negative (homelessness), depending on context.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)、世界(せかい)を放浪(ほうろう)した。
When I was young, I wandered the world.
放浪(ほうろう)の旅(たび)に出(で)た詩人(しじん)がいた。
There was a poet who set out on a wandering journey.
仕事(しごと)を辞(や)めて一年間(いちねんかん)ヨーロッパを放浪(ほうろう)した経験(けいけん)が、人生観(じんせいかん)を変(か)えた。
The experience of quitting my job and wandering Europe for a year changed my outlook on life.

USAGE:
Carries a literary, romantic quality when describing travel with no fixed plans. In negative contexts, can imply vagrancy or aimlessness. Often used with の to modify nouns: 放浪(ほうろう)の旅(たび) (wandering journey), 放浪(ほうろう)の画家(がか) (wandering painter).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 放浪(ほうろう)の旅(たび): a wandering journey
  • 各地(かくち)を放浪(ほうろう)する: to wander from place to place
  • 放浪(ほうろう)生活(せいかつ): a nomadic life
  • 放浪(ほうろう)者(しゃ): a wanderer, vagrant
