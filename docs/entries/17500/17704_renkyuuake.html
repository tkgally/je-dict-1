連休明(れんきゅうあ)け

れんきゅうあけ
noun
end of a consecutive holiday; first day back after a long weekend
1. end of a consecutive holiday; first day back after a long weekend
The period immediately following a series of consecutive days off, typically referring to the first working day after a long weekend or holiday period like Golden Week or Obon.
連休明(れんきゅうあ)けは辛(つら)い。
The day after a long weekend is tough.
連休明(れんきゅうあ)けの月曜日(げつようび)は電車(でんしゃ)が混(こ)む。
The Monday after a long weekend is crowded on the trains.
連休明(れんきゅうあ)けに大量(たいりょう)のメールが溜(た)まっていて、処理(しょり)するのに半日(はんにち)かかった。
A huge number of emails had piled up after the holidays, and it took me half a day to get through them.

USAGE:
連休明(れんきゅうあ)け uses the suffix 明(あ)け, meaning "the period after" or "the end of." This pattern is productive: 正月明(しょうがつあ)け (after New Year), 夏休(なつやす)み明(あ)け (after summer break), 梅雨明(つゆあ)け (end of rainy season).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連休明(れんきゅうあ)けの仕事(しごと) (work after the holidays)
  • 連休明(れんきゅうあ)けに届(とど)く (to arrive after the long weekend)
  • ゴールデンウィーク明(あ)け (after Golden Week)
