1. to throw into
to toss or hurl something into a container, space, or situation
ゴミを箱(はこ)に投(な)げ込(こ)んだ。
I threw the garbage into the bin.
手紙(てがみ)をポストに投(な)げ込(こ)む。
I drop a letter into the mailbox.
池(いけ)に石(いし)を投(な)げ込(こ)んで遊(あそ)んだ。
We played by throwing stones into the pond.

A compound verb: 投(な)げる (to throw) + 込(こ)む (into). The 込(こ)む element adds the directional meaning of entering a space.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: XをYに投(な)げ込(こ)む (throw X into Y)

ASPECT (ている):
投(な)げ込(こ)んでいる can mean either 'is in the act of throwing in' or 'has thrown in (and it's still there).'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴミを箱(はこ)に投(な)げ込(こ)む (to toss garbage into the bin)
  • 手紙(てがみ)をポストに投(な)げ込(こ)む (to drop a letter in the mailbox)
  • 石(いし)を池(いけ)に投(な)げ込(こ)む (to throw stones into a pond)

RELATED 〜込(こ)む VERBS:

  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む (to rush into)
  • 迷(まよ)い込(こ)む (to wander into)
