新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)

しんきじぎょう
noun
new business venture; new enterprise
1. new business venture; new enterprise; new project
A newly launched business, project, or line of work within an existing company or as a startup. Common in corporate and business contexts.
新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)を立(た)ち上(あ)げる。
To launch a new business venture.
来年度(らいねんど)から新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)に参入(さんにゅう)する計画(けいかく)だ。
We plan to enter a new business venture starting next fiscal year.
社長(しゃちょう)は新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)の開発(かいはつ)に多額(たがく)の投資(とうし)を決定(けってい)した。
The president decided to invest a large sum in developing a new business venture.

USAGE:
A standard business term frequently seen in corporate strategy, news articles, and job postings. Often contrasted with 既存(きそん)事業(じぎょう) (existing business).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)を立(た)ち上(あ)げる (to launch a new venture)
  • 新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)開発(かいはつ) (new business development)
  • 新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)部(ぶ) (new business division)
  • 新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)に参入(さんにゅう)する (to enter a new business area)
