精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)

せいしんとういつ
noun / verb (suru)
mental concentration; focusing one's mind
1. mental concentration; focusing one's mind; composing oneself
The act of focusing all mental energy on a single point, clearing distractions. Used in martial arts, sports, meditation, and before important tasks.
精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)を図(はか)る。
To focus one's mind.
試合前(しあいまえ)に精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)して集中(しゅうちゅう)力(りょく)を高(たか)める。
I compose my mind before a match to heighten my concentration.
弓道(きゅうどう)では矢(や)を放(はな)つ前(まえ)に精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)し、心(こころ)を静(しず)めることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
In kyudo (Japanese archery), it is important to focus your mind and calm your heart before releasing the arrow.

USAGE:
Often associated with martial arts, traditional Japanese arts, and athletic performance. Can also be used humorously when someone is trying to concentrate intensely on an everyday task.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)する (to focus one's mind)
  • 精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)を図(はか)る (to seek mental composure)
  • 精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)を乱(みだ)す (to break one's concentration)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 集中(しゅうちゅう): concentration — more everyday and general
  • 瞑想(めいそう): meditation — focuses on the practice itself rather than the mental state
