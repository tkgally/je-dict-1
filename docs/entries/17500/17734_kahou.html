家宝(かほう)

かほう
noun
family heirloom; family treasure
1. family heirloom; family treasure
A valuable object passed down through generations within a family. Can be a physical item such as a sword, scroll, or piece of art, or used figuratively for something cherished.
この刀(かたな)は代々(だいだい)の家宝(かほう)だ。
This sword is a family heirloom passed down through generations.
祖父(そふ)から受(う)け継(つ)いだ掛(か)け軸(じく)を家宝(かほう)として大切(たいせつ)にしている。
I treasure the hanging scroll inherited from my grandfather as a family heirloom.
「子(こ)は家宝(かほう)」という言葉(ことば)のとおり、子供(こども)は何(なに)よりも大切(たいせつ)な存在(そんざい)だ。
As the saying 'children are a family's treasure' goes, children are the most precious thing of all.

USAGE:
家宝(かほう) combines 家(か) (house/family) + 宝(ほう) (treasure). It refers to items of significant value — sentimental, historical, or monetary — that are kept within a family across generations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家宝(かほう)を受(う)け継(つ)ぐ (to inherit a family heirloom)
  • 代々(だいだい)の家宝(かほう) (heirloom passed down through generations)
  • 子(こ)は家宝(かほう) (children are a family's treasure — proverb)

SIMILAR WORDS:
宝物(たからもの) is a more general word for treasure or prized possession. 家宝(かほう) specifically implies family inheritance and generational value.
