1. to project; to cast (an image)
To project an image, light, or shadow onto a surface such as a screen or wall.
スクリーンに映像(えいぞう)を投影(とうえい)する。
To project an image onto a screen.
プロジェクターで壁(かべ)に資料(しりょう)を投影(とうえい)して説明(せつめい)した。
I projected the materials on the wall with a projector and gave an explanation.
暗(くら)い部屋(へや)の天井(てんじょう)に星空(ほしぞら)を投影(とうえい)するプラネタリウムが人気(にんき)だ。
Planetariums that project a starry sky onto the ceiling of a dark room are popular.
2. to reflect; to mirror
To reflect or express inner feelings, thoughts, or circumstances in something external. Used figuratively for how personal experiences or emotions are expressed in art, behavior, or situations.
自分(じぶん)の不安(ふあん)を他人(たにん)に投影(とうえい)する。
To project one's own anxiety onto others.
この小説(しょうせつ)には作者(さくしゃ)の体験(たいけん)が色濃(いろこ)く投影(とうえい)されている。
The author's experiences are strongly reflected in this novel.
時代(じだい)の空気(くうき)が作品(さくひん)に投影(とうえい)されており、当時(とうじ)の社会(しゃかい)を知(し)る手(て)がかりになる。
The spirit of the era is reflected in the work, making it a clue to understanding the society of that time.

USAGE:
投影(とうえい) is used both as a noun and as a suru verb. Sense 1 is the physical/technical meaning, while sense 2 is the figurative/psychological meaning.

ASPECT (ている):
投影(とうえい)されている indicates a state where something is being reflected or projected.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を投影(とうえい)する (to project something)
  • [surface]に投影(とうえい)する (to project onto a surface)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 映像(えいぞう)を投影(とうえい)する (to project an image)
  • 心理(しんり)を投影(とうえい)する (to project one's psychology)
  • 自己(じこ)投影(とうえい) (self-projection)
