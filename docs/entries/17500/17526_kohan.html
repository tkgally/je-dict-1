湖畔(こはん)

こはん
noun
lakeside; lakeshore
1. lakeside; lakeshore; shore of a lake
The area along the edge of a lake. Often associated with scenic views, resorts, and recreational activities.
湖畔(こはん)でキャンプをした。
We camped by the lake.
湖畔(こはん)のホテルに泊(と)まった。
I stayed at a lakeside hotel.
静(しず)かな湖畔(こはん)を散歩(さんぽ)しながら、鳥(とり)の声(こえ)を聞(き)いた。
While strolling along the quiet lakeshore, I listened to the birdsong.

USAGE:
A somewhat literary word often used in travel writing and scenic descriptions. 湖畔(こはん) evokes a peaceful, natural setting and is commonly associated with vacation spots and resort areas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 湖畔(こはん)のホテル (lakeside hotel)
  • 湖畔(こはん)を散歩(さんぽ)する (to stroll along the lake)
  • 湖畔(こはん)に建(た)つ (to stand by the lake)
  • 湖畔(こはん)でキャンプする (to camp by the lake)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 湖岸(こがん): lakeshore — more technical, used in geography
  • 岸辺(きしべ): shore, waterside — can refer to any body of water
  • 畔(ほとり): beside, edge — poetic/literary term for the edge of water
