1. to lend; to loan; to provide on loan
To lend something formally, especially equipment, facilities, or funds. More formal than 貸す and commonly used in institutional, business, and legal contexts.
パソコンを社員(しゃいん)に貸与(たいよ)する。
To lend laptops to employees.
大学(だいがく)が学生(がくせい)に奨学金(しょうがくきん)を貸与(たいよ)する制度(せいど)がある。
There is a system where the university loans scholarships to students.
会社(かいしゃ)から貸与(たいよ)された携帯(けいたい)は私用(しよう)で使(つか)ってはいけない。
You must not use a mobile phone loaned by the company for personal purposes.

USAGE:
貸与(たいよ)する is the formal counterpart of 貸(か)す. It is commonly used in business, government, and educational contexts where lending is official or documented.

ASPECT (ている):
貸与(たいよ)されている indicates that the item is currently on loan.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を貸与(たいよ)する (to lend something)
  • [person]に貸与(たいよ)する (to lend to someone)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貸与(たいよ)品(ひん) (loaned item)
  • 貸与(たいよ)型(がた)奨学金(しょうがくきん) (loan-type scholarship)
  • 無償(むしょう)貸与(たいよ) (free loan)

SIMILAR WORDS:
貸(か)す is the everyday word for lending. 貸与(たいよ)する implies a formal, institutional loan.
