有名人(ゆうめいじん)

ゆうめいじん
noun
celebrity; famous person
1. celebrity; famous person; public figure
A well-known or famous person. Used broadly for anyone with public recognition, from entertainers and athletes to politicians and historical figures.
あの人(ひと)は有名人(ゆうめいじん)らしい。
That person seems to be a celebrity.
有名人(ゆうめいじん)にサインをもらった。
I got an autograph from a celebrity.
この町(まち)は多(おお)くの有名人(ゆうめいじん)を輩出(はいしゅつ)したことで知(し)られている。
This town is known for having produced many famous people.

USAGE:
有名人(ゆうめいじん) is a neutral, general term for any well-known person. It can be used in both positive and negative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有名人(ゆうめいじん)の私生活(しせいかつ) (a celebrity's private life)
  • 地元(じもと)の有名人(ゆうめいじん) (a local celebrity)
  • 有名人(ゆうめいじん)になる (to become famous)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 著名人(ちょめいじん): a more formal synonym, often used in written contexts
  • 芸能人(げいのうじん): specifically an entertainer or show business personality
  • セレブ: a loanword from "celebrity," often implying wealth and glamour

Related Words

Synonym: 著名人(ちょめいじん) (prominent person (formal))
Related: 有名(ゆうめい) (famous)
