即効性(そっこうせい)

そっこうせい
noun
immediate effectiveness, quick-acting quality
1. immediate effectiveness, quick-acting quality
The quality of producing results immediately or very quickly. Used for medicines, treatments, methods, and strategies that work right away.
この薬(くすり)は即効性(そっこうせい)がある。
This medicine is fast-acting.
即効性(そっこうせい)のある対策(たいさく)が求(もと)められている。
Measures with immediate effectiveness are being sought.
この方法(ほうほう)には即効性(そっこうせい)はないが、長期的(ちょうきてき)に見(み)れば効果(こうか)がある。
This method doesn't produce immediate results, but it is effective in the long term.

USAGE:
Commonly used to describe medicines, treatments, or strategies. The pattern 即効性(そっこうせい)がある/ない (has/lacks immediate effect) is the most frequent usage.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 即効(そっこう): immediate effect (the base word without ～性(せい))
  • 速効(そっこう): same meaning, alternate kanji

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即効性(そっこうせい)がある: to be fast-acting
  • 即効性(そっこうせい)のある薬(くすり): a quick-acting medicine
  • 即効性(そっこうせい)に欠(か)ける: to lack immediate effectiveness
