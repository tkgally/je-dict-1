1. reproduction; replica; copy; duplicate
A copy or reproduction of an original item, such as a work of art, document, or digital file. Implies a faithful reproduction rather than a rough copy.
これは名画(めいが)の複製(ふくせい)です。
This is a reproduction of a famous painting.
大切(たいせつ)なファイルを複製(ふくせい)しておいた。
I made a copy of the important file.
著作権(ちょさくけん)のある作品(さくひん)を無断(むだん)で複製(ふくせい)するのは違法(いほう)だ。
It is illegal to reproduce copyrighted works without permission.

USAGE:
Used both for physical reproductions (art replicas, document copies) and digital copies (file duplication). In legal and copyright contexts, 複製(ふくせい)権(けん) (reproduction rights) is a key concept.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 複製(ふくせい)を作(つく)る (to make a reproduction)
  • 複製(ふくせい)品(ひん) (replica, reproduction item)
  • 複製(ふくせい)権(けん) (reproduction rights)
  • 無断(むだん)複製(ふくせい) (unauthorized reproduction)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コピー: copy — more casual, everyday term
  • 模造(もぞう): imitation — often implies lower quality or intent to deceive
  • 複写(ふくしゃ): copying, duplication — more technical, used for documents
