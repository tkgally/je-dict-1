1. cherry blossom viewing; flower viewing party
The traditional Japanese custom of enjoying the beauty of cherry blossoms in spring, typically involving a picnic or party under the trees. The お prefix adds politeness.
今年(ことし)もお花見(はなみ)に行(い)きたい。
I want to go cherry blossom viewing again this year.
週末(しゅうまつ)に公園(こうえん)でお花見(はなみ)をする予定(よてい)です。
I'm planning to have a cherry blossom viewing party at the park this weekend.
お花見(はなみ)の季節(きせつ)になると、上野公園(うえのこうえん)は大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと)でにぎわう。
When cherry blossom season comes, Ueno Park bustles with crowds of people.

CULTURE:
お花見(はなみ) is one of Japan's most beloved spring traditions, typically taking place in late March to mid-April. Groups of friends, families, or coworkers gather under 桜(さくら) (cherry blossom) trees to eat, drink, and enjoy the flowers. Popular spots include parks, riverbanks, and temple grounds. It is common for someone to be sent early to reserve a spot by laying down a blue tarp (場所取(ばしょと)り).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お花見(はなみ)に行(い)く (go cherry blossom viewing)
  • お花見(はなみ)弁当(べんとう) (bento for flower viewing)
  • お花見(はなみ)スポット (popular viewing spots)
  • お花見(はなみ)の時期(じき) (cherry blossom season)

NOTE:
The form without お (花見(はなみ)) is also common and slightly less polite. Both refer to the same custom.
