案内図(あんないず)

あんないず
noun
guide map; information map
1. guide map; information map; directory map
A map displayed at a location to help visitors find their way around. Commonly found at stations, parks, shopping centers, tourist spots, and large buildings.
駅前(えきまえ)に案内図(あんないず)があった。
There was a guide map in front of the station.
案内図(あんないず)で現在地(げんざいち)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked my current location on the guide map.
公園(こうえん)の入口(いりぐち)にある案内図(あんないず)を見(み)て、散歩(さんぽ)コースを決(き)めた。
I looked at the guide map at the park entrance and decided on a walking route.

USAGE:
A compound of 案内(あんない) (guidance, information) + 図(ず) (map, diagram). Refers to the kind of map that is posted at a location to orient visitors, not a portable map you carry. Extremely common in Japan at train stations, parks, shopping malls, and tourist sites.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 案内図(あんないず)を見(み)る (to look at a guide map)
  • 館内(かんない)案内図(あんないず) (building directory/floor map)
  • 周辺(しゅうへん)案内図(あんないず) (area guide map)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 地図(ちず): map — a general portable map, not limited to on-site information boards
  • 見取(みと)り図(ず): floor plan, layout — shows the internal layout of a building
