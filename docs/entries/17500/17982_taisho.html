1. leaving an institution; discharge; departure from a facility
The act of leaving or being discharged from an institution such as a care facility, detention center, or research institute. The opposite of 入所.
退所(たいしょ)手続(てつづ)きをする。
To complete the discharge procedures.
祖母(そぼ)は来月(らいげつ)介護(かいご)施設(しせつ)を退所(たいしょ)する予定(よてい)だ。
My grandmother is scheduled to leave the care facility next month.
退所(たいしょ)後(ご)の生活(せいかつ)支援(しえん)について、担当者(たんとうしゃ)と相談(そうだん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to consult with the person in charge about support for living after discharge.

USAGE:
Pairs with 入所(にゅうしょ) (entering an institution). Used for care facilities, detention centers, research institutes, and similar organizations. Not typically used for hospitals (use 退院(たいいん)) or schools (use 退学(たいがく)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 退所(たいしょ)する (to leave/be discharged from a facility)
  • 退所(たいしょ)手続(てつづ)き (discharge procedures)
  • 退所(たいしょ)後(ご) (after leaving the facility)
  • 退所(たいしょ)日(び) (date of discharge)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 退院(たいいん): leaving a hospital — used specifically for medical discharge
  • 退学(たいがく): leaving a school — used for withdrawal from educational institutions
