1. to cry out; to wail; to scream and cry
To cry loudly while screaming or shouting, expressing extreme grief, pain, or distress. A compound verb combining 泣く (to cry) and 叫ぶ (to shout).
子(こ)どもが泣(な)き叫(さけ)んでいる。
The child is wailing.
痛(いた)みに耐(た)えきれず泣(な)き叫(さけ)んだ。
Unable to bear the pain, I cried out.
母親(ははおや)を亡(な)くした少女(しょうじょ)は泣(な)き叫(さけ)びながら地面(じめん)に崩(くず)れ落(お)ちた。
The girl who had lost her mother collapsed to the ground, wailing.

USAGE:
A compound verb that expresses more intense emotion than 泣(な)く alone. Often used in dramatic or literary descriptions of grief, pain, or desperation.

CONJUGATION:
Conjugates as a godan verb ending in ぶ: 泣(な)き叫(さけ)ばない, 泣(な)き叫(さけ)びます, 泣(な)き叫(さけ)んで, etc.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泣(な)く: to cry — general, less intense
  • 号泣(ごうきゅう)する: to bawl, cry loudly — emphasizes volume of crying
  • 喚(わめ)く: to scream, yell — emphasizes the shouting aspect
