1. to be done boiling, to be boiled through, to finish cooking (by boiling)
Used when food has finished boiling and is ready. An intransitive verb indicating the completion of the boiling process.
パスタが茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がった。
The pasta is done boiling.
卵(たまご)が茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がったら、すぐに冷水(れいすい)に入(い)れる。
Once the eggs are done boiling, put them in cold water right away.
野菜(やさい)が茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がるまでの間(あいだ)に、ソースの準備(じゅんび)をしておこう。
Let's prepare the sauce while the vegetables finish boiling.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (intransitive). The transitive counterpart is 茹(ゆ)で上(あ)げる (to finish boiling something). Part of the common ～上(あ)がる pattern indicating completion of an action.

USAGE:
Primarily a cooking term. Sometimes written in hiragana as ゆで上(あ)がる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パスタが茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がる: the pasta finishes boiling
  • 麺(めん)が茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がる: the noodles finish boiling
  • 卵(たまご)が茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がる: the eggs finish boiling
