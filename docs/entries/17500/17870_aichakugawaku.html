愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)く

あいちゃくがわく
expression
to grow fond of; to become attached to
1. to grow fond of; to become attached to; to develop affection for
To gradually develop feelings of attachment or fondness toward something or someone through familiarity and use over time.
この靴(くつ)には愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)いてきた。
I've grown fond of these shoes.
使(つか)い続(つづ)けるうちに道具(どうぐ)に愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)く。
You become attached to a tool as you keep using it.
最初(さいしょ)は興味(きょうみ)がなかったが、住(す)んでいるうちにこの町(まち)に愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)いてきた。
At first I wasn't interested, but as I lived here I grew fond of this town.

USAGE:
The noun 愛着(あいちゃく) means attachment or affection. Combined with 湧(わ)く (to well up, to spring forth), it expresses the gradual, natural development of fondness. Can be used for objects, places, people, and activities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)く (to grow fond of)
  • 愛着(あいちゃく)のある (having affection for)
  • 愛着(あいちゃく)を持(も)つ (to have attachment to)
  • 愛着(あいちゃく)を感(かん)じる (to feel attachment)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 親(した)しみが湧(わ)く: to feel familiarity/warmth — more about feeling comfortable with someone
  • 愛着(あいちゃく)が湧(わ)く: to become attached — deeper emotional bond, often with objects or places
