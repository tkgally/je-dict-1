1. to replant; to transplant; to repot
To move a plant from one location or container to another. Common in gardening contexts.
花(はな)を大(おお)きい鉢(はち)に植(う)え替(か)えた。
I replanted the flower in a bigger pot.
春(はる)になったら苗(なえ)を庭(にわ)に植(う)え替(か)える予定(よてい)だ。
I plan to transplant the seedlings to the garden in spring.
根(ね)が詰(つ)まってきたので、そろそろ植(う)え替(か)えた方(ほう)がいい。
The roots are getting crowded, so you should repot it soon.

A compound verb from 植(う)える (to plant) + 替(か)える (to replace/change). Primarily a gardening term.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鉢(はち)に植(う)え替(か)える: to repot
  • 庭(にわ)に植(う)え替(か)える: to transplant into the garden
  • 苗(なえ)を植(う)え替(か)える: to transplant seedlings
  • 植(う)え替(か)えの時期(じき): repotting season

USAGE:
The noun form is 植(う)え替(か)え. Spring is the most common season for repotting in Japan. Plants need repotting when 根(ね)が詰(つ)まる (roots become crowded) or 土(つち)が古(ふる)くなる (soil becomes old).
