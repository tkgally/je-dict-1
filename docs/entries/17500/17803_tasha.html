他社(たしゃ)

たしゃ
noun
other company; competitor
See also: 他者(たしゃ) (other people)
1. other company; another company; competitor
A company other than one's own. Used in business contexts to refer to rival companies, partner companies, or simply any other company.
他社(たしゃ)の製品(せいひん)と比較(ひかく)した。
I compared it with other companies' products.
他社(たしゃ)に先駆(さきが)けて新商品(しんしょうひん)を発売(はつばい)した。
We released the new product ahead of our competitors.
他社(たしゃ)との差別化(さべつか)を図(はか)るために、サービスの質(しつ)を向上(こうじょう)させた。
We improved service quality in order to differentiate ourselves from other companies.

USAGE:
A compound of 他(た) (other) + 社(しゃ) (company). A standard business term used to refer to any company other than one's own. Does not necessarily imply competition — can also refer to partner companies or companies in unrelated industries.

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:
Not to be confused with 他者(たしゃ) (another person, the other), which uses a different second kanji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 他社(たしゃ)製品(せいひん) (other companies' products)
  • 他社(たしゃ)との比較(ひかく) (comparison with other companies)
  • 他社(たしゃ)に負(ま)けない (not to lose to competitors)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 競合他社(きょうごうたしゃ): competing company — specifically a rival
  • 自社(じしゃ): one's own company — the opposite
