1. to join a club; to become a member of a club
To formally join a school club or extracurricular activity group. Primarily used in the context of Japanese schools, where club activities (部活動(ぶかつどう)) are a central part of student life.
野球部(やきゅうぶ)に入部(にゅうぶ)した。
I joined the baseball club.
友達(ともだち)に誘(さそ)われて吹奏楽部(すいそうがくぶ)に入部(にゅうぶ)した。
I was invited by a friend and joined the wind ensemble club.
中学(ちゅうがく)に入(はい)ったら、どの部活(ぶかつ)に入部(にゅうぶ)するか迷(まよ)っている。
I'm undecided about which club to join when I start middle school.

USAGE:
Used with the particle に to indicate which club one joins: ～部(ぶ)に入部(にゅうぶ)する. Primarily a school context word. The opposite is 退部(たいぶ)する (to quit a club).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～部(ぶ)に入部(にゅうぶ)する (to join ~ club)
  • 入部届(にゅうぶとどけ) (club membership application form)
  • 入部(にゅうぶ)希望(きぼう) (wish to join a club)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese schools, joining a club (部活(ぶかつ)) is considered an important part of education. Many students join in April at the start of the school year after attending trial sessions (体験入部(たいけんにゅうぶ)).

Related Words

Related: 部活(ぶかつ) (club activities)
