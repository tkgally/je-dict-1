1. one reading, a single perusal, reading through once
Reading something once, or giving something a single read-through. Often used as a recommendation — suggesting that something is worth reading.
ぜひ一読(いちどく)をお勧(すす)めします。
I highly recommend giving it a read.
契約書(けいやくしょ)を一読(いちどく)してからサインした。
I read through the contract once before signing.
一読(いちどく)しただけでは理解(りかい)できない難解(なんかい)な本(ほん)だ。
It's a difficult book that you can't understand with just one reading.

USAGE:
Often used in recommendations: 一読(いちどく)をお勧(すす)めします (I recommend reading it), 一読(いちどく)の価値(かち)がある (worth reading). Also used to mean a quick or cursory reading.

WORD FORMATION:
一(いち) (one) + 読(どく) (reading). Part of the 一(いち)～ pattern for single actions: 一見(いっけん) (a glance), 一聴(いっちょう) (a listen).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一読(いちどく)をお勧(すす)めする: to recommend reading
  • 一読(いちどく)の価値(かち)がある: to be worth reading
  • 一読(いちどく)する: to read through once
