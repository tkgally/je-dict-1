反応(はんのう)を見(み)る

はんのうをみる
expression (verb phrase)
to gauge a reaction; to see how someone responds
1. to gauge a reaction; to see how someone responds
To observe or assess someone's response to something before deciding on a next step. A common strategy in communication, business, and interpersonal interactions.
まず相手(あいて)の反応(はんのう)を見(み)てから判断(はんだん)しよう。
Let's assess their reaction first before deciding.
新商品(しんしょうひん)のサンプルを配(くば)って反応(はんのう)を見(み)ることにした。
We decided to hand out samples of the new product and gauge the reaction.
冗談(じょうだん)を言(い)って反応(はんのう)を見(み)たが、誰(だれ)も笑(わら)わなかった。
I told a joke to see the reaction, but nobody laughed.

USAGE:
A versatile expression used in everyday conversation and business. Can refer to observing facial expressions, verbal responses, market reactions, public opinion, etc. Often used as a deliberate strategy before committing to a course of action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 反応(はんのう)を見(み)てから～する (to do ~ after seeing the reaction)
  • 反応(はんのう)を見(み)る限(かぎ)り (as far as one can tell from the reaction)
  • まず反応(はんのう)を見(み)る (to check the reaction first)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 様子(ようす)を見(み)る: to see how things go — broader, not limited to reactions
  • 反応(はんのう)を伺(うかが)う: to gauge a reaction — more formal/cautious
