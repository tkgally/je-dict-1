1. to be utterly exhausted; to be completely worn out
To be exhausted to the absolute limit, both physically and mentally. The 果てる suffix intensifies 疲れる to mean total, complete exhaustion.
一日中(いちにちじゅう)歩(ある)いて疲(つか)れ果(は)てた。
I walked all day and was completely exhausted.
疲(つか)れ果(は)てて、家(いえ)に着(つ)いたらすぐ寝(ね)てしまった。
I was so exhausted that I fell asleep as soon as I got home.
長時間(ちょうじかん)の会議(かいぎ)と残業(ざんぎょう)が続(つづ)き、心身(しんしん)ともに疲(つか)れ果(は)てていた。
After long meetings and overtime one after another, I was completely exhausted both physically and mentally.

GRAMMAR:
Compound verb: 疲(つか)れる (to get tired) + 果(は)てる (to reach the end). The 果(は)てる suffix is a common intensifier meaning "to do something to completion" or "to the very end." Other examples: 使(つか)い果(は)たす (use up completely), 朽(く)ち果(は)てる (decay completely).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 疲(つか)れ果(は)てた顔(かお) (an utterly exhausted face)
  • 疲(つか)れ果(は)てた体(からだ) (a completely worn-out body)
  • 心身(しんしん)ともに疲(つか)れ果(は)てる (be exhausted both physically and mentally)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 疲(つか)れる: to get tired — basic form, milder degree
  • くたくたになる: to be worn out — colloquial, vivid expression
  • 消耗(しょうもう)する: to be depleted — more formal, can apply to resources as well as energy
