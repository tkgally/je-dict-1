手拍子(てびょうし)

てびょうし
noun
hand clapping, clapping to a beat
1. hand clapping, clapping to a rhythm or beat
Rhythmic clapping of hands, typically done in unison by an audience or group to accompany music, cheering, or traditional celebrations.
観客(かんきゃく)が手拍子(てびょうし)を打(う)った。
The audience clapped along to the beat.
手拍子(てびょうし)に合(あ)わせて歌(うた)った。
We sang along with the hand clapping.
コンサートの最後(さいご)に会場(かいじょう)全体(ぜんたい)が手拍子(てびょうし)で盛(も)り上(あ)がった。
At the end of the concert, the entire venue got fired up with rhythmic clapping.

USAGE:
Common at concerts, festivals, and traditional celebrations. In Japanese culture, a rhythmic three-clap pattern (三本締(さんぼんじ)め) is performed at the end of celebrations and business events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手拍子(てびょうし)を打(う)つ: to clap along
  • 手拍子(てびょうし)に合(あ)わせる: to match the clapping rhythm
  • 手拍子(てびょうし)で盛(も)り上(あ)がる: to get excited with clapping
