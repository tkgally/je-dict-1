退席(たいせき)

たいせき
noun, verb (suru)
leaving one's seat; withdrawing from a meeting
See also: 退席(たいせき)する (verb form), 体積(たいせき) (volume, capacity)
1. leaving one's seat; withdrawing from a meeting or gathering
Formally leaving one's seat or place during a meeting, class, ceremony, or other gathering. Often implies excusing oneself partway through.
会議(かいぎ)の途中(とちゅう)で退席(たいせき)した。
I left the meeting partway through.
体調(たいちょう)が悪(わる)くなり、授業(じゅぎょう)を退席(たいせき)させてもらった。
I felt unwell and was allowed to leave the class.
議長(ぎちょう)が退席(たいせき)を命(めい)じたため、その議員(ぎいん)は議場(ぎじょう)を離(はな)れた。
The chairman ordered the member to leave, so the legislator left the chamber.

USAGE:
退席(たいせき) is a formal word, often used in meetings, classes, and official proceedings. The opposite is 着席(ちゃくせき) (taking one's seat).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 途中(とちゅう)退席(たいせき) (leaving partway through)
  • 退席(たいせき)を求(もと)める (to request someone to leave)
  • 退席(たいせき)を命(めい)じる (to order someone to leave)

SIMILAR WORDS:
退出(たいしゅつ) means leaving a room or building entirely, while 退席(たいせき) specifically refers to leaving one's seat or place at a gathering.
