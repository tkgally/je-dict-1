懇親会(こんしんかい)

こんしんかい
noun
social gathering; mixer; get-together
1. social gathering; mixer; get-together
A gathering designed for people to socialize and deepen personal connections, typically held after a formal event such as a conference, seminar, or ceremony. Usually involves food and drinks.
セミナーの後(あと)に懇親会(こんしんかい)がある。
There is a social gathering after the seminar.
懇親会(こんしんかい)で名刺(めいし)を交換(こうかん)した。
I exchanged business cards at the mixer.
学会(がっかい)の懇親会(こんしんかい)では、研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)同士(どうし)が親睦(しんぼく)を深(ふか)める機会(きかい)になる。
The social gathering at the academic conference is an opportunity for researchers to deepen personal connections.

USAGE:
A formal term for socializing events that typically follow professional or academic gatherings. Unlike casual drinking parties (飲(の)み会(かい)), a 懇親会(こんしんかい) has a more structured, official character and is often organized by the host institution.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懇親会(こんしんかい)を開(ひら)く (to hold a social gathering)
  • 懇親会(こんしんかい)に参加(さんか)する (to attend a mixer)
  • 懇親会(こんしんかい)の費用(ひよう) (cost of the gathering)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲(の)み会(かい): drinking party — more casual and informal
  • 交流会(こうりゅうかい): networking event — emphasizes exchange and interaction
  • 歓迎会(かんげいかい): welcome party — specifically for welcoming someone

Related Words

Synonym: 飲(の)み会(かい) (drinking party (casual))
Synonym: 交流会(こうりゅうかい) (networking event)
Related: 歓迎会(かんげいかい) (welcome party)
