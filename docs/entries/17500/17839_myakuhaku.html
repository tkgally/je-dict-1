脈拍(みゃくはく)

みゃくはく
noun
pulse; heart rate
1. pulse; pulse rate; heart rate
The rhythmic beating of arteries as blood is pumped through them by the heart. Measured as beats per minute and used as a basic health indicator.
脈拍(みゃくはく)を測(はか)る。
To measure one's pulse.
運動(うんどう)すると脈拍(みゃくはく)が上(あ)がる。
Your pulse goes up when you exercise.
看護師(かんごし)が患者(かんじゃ)の脈拍(みゃくはく)と血圧(けつあつ)を確認(かくにん)した。
The nurse checked the patient's pulse and blood pressure.

USAGE:
脈拍(みゃくはく) is the standard medical/formal term for pulse. In casual speech, 脈(みゃく) alone is often sufficient.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脈拍(みゃくはく)を測(はか)る (to measure one's pulse)
  • 脈拍(みゃくはく)が速(はや)い / 遅(おそ)い (pulse is fast / slow)
  • 脈拍(みゃくはく)数(すう) (pulse rate)
  • 安静時(あんせいじ)脈拍(みゃくはく) (resting pulse)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脈(みゃく): pulse — shorter form, used more casually
  • 心拍(しんぱく): heartbeat — specifically the beating of the heart itself
  • 心拍数(しんぱくすう): heart rate — numerical measurement
