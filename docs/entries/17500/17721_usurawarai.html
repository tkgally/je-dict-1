薄(うす)ら笑(わら)い

うすらわらい
noun
faint smile; smirk; sneer
1. faint smile; smirk; sneer
A slight, often unsettling smile that suggests mockery, insincerity, or hidden intent. Almost always carries a negative connotation, unlike a genuine smile.
彼(かれ)は薄(うす)ら笑(わら)いを浮(う)かべた。
He let a smirk cross his face.
あの薄(うす)ら笑(わら)いが気味(きみ)悪(わる)くて仕方(しかた)がない。
That smirk is unbearably creepy.
謝罪会見(しゃざいかいけん)で薄(うす)ら笑(わら)いを見(み)せたことが批判(ひはん)を浴(あ)びた。
Showing a smirk during the apology press conference drew criticism.

USAGE:
薄(うす)ら笑(わら)い almost always has a negative connotation. It suggests the person is being insincere, mocking, or hiding something. It is not used for a genuine, warm smile.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薄(うす)ら笑(わら)いを浮(う)かべる (to show a smirk)
  • 薄(うす)ら笑(わら)いを見(み)せる (to display a smirk)
  • 不気味(ぶきみ)な薄(うす)ら笑(わら)い (an eerie smirk)

SIMILAR WORDS:
苦笑(くしょう)い is a wry or bitter smile at something awkward. 薄(うす)ら笑(わら)い is more sinister or unsettling.
