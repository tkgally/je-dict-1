1. to guess; to conjecture; to surmise; to infer
To form an opinion or conclusion based on incomplete evidence or reasoning. More formal and analytical than simply guessing.
原因(げんいん)を推測(すいそく)する。
Conjecture the cause.
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)から気持(きも)ちを推測(すいそく)した。
I inferred his feelings from his behavior.
限(かぎ)られたデータから結果(けっか)を推測(すいそく)するのは難(むずか)しいが、やってみよう。
It's difficult to surmise the results from limited data, but let's try.

USAGE:
A relatively formal word used in analytical, investigative, or academic contexts. Often used when drawing conclusions from indirect evidence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原因(げんいん)を推測(すいそく)する (surmise the cause)
  • 推測(すいそく)にすぎない (merely a guess)
  • 推測(すいそく)の域(いき)を出(で)ない (not go beyond conjecture)
  • 推測(すいそく)が当(あ)たる (one's guess is correct)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推定(すいてい)する: to presume — more authoritative; used when establishing something as likely true, often in legal or scientific contexts
  • 憶測(おくそく)する: to speculate — implies guessing without solid evidence, often with negative connotation
  • 予測(よそく)する: to predict — focuses on forecasting future events
