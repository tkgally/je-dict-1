1. to be combined; to interlock; to fit together
For multiple elements to come together and form a unified whole. The intransitive counterpart of 組み合わせる (to combine, to put together).
複数(ふくすう)の要素(ようそ)が組(く)み合(あ)わさっている。
Multiple elements are combined together.
色(いろ)と形(かたち)が組(く)み合(あ)わさって美(うつく)しいデザインになった。
Colors and shapes combined to create a beautiful design.
さまざまな条件(じょうけん)が組(く)み合(あ)わさって、今回(こんかい)の結果(けっか)につながった。
Various conditions combined to lead to this outcome.

USAGE:
The intransitive form of 組(く)み合(あ)わせる (to combine, transitive). Describes a state where multiple elements naturally come together to form something. Often used with the ている form to describe a resulting state.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 要素(ようそ)が組(く)み合(あ)わさる (elements combine)
  • 条件(じょうけん)が組(く)み合(あ)わさる (conditions come together)
  • うまく組(く)み合(あ)わさる (to combine well)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 組(く)み合(あ)わせる: to combine (transitive) — the transitive counterpart
  • 重(かさ)なる: to overlap — elements stacking on top of each other rather than interlocking
