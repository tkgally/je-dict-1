拝聴(はいちょう)

はいちょう
noun, verb (suru)
listening (humble)
1. listening (humble); hearing respectfully
A humble (kenjougo) expression meaning to listen. Used to show respect when referring to one's own act of listening to someone of higher status, such as a superior's speech or a teacher's lecture.
ご講演(こうえん)を拝聴(はいちょう)しました。
I had the honor of listening to your lecture.
先生(せんせい)のお話(はなし)を拝聴(はいちょう)して、大変(たいへん)勉強(べんきょう)になりました。
I learned a great deal from listening to your talk, sensei.
貴重(きちょう)なご意見(いけん)を拝聴(はいちょう)できる機会(きかい)をいただき、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。
Thank you very much for the opportunity to hear your valuable opinions.

USAGE:
拝聴(はいちょう) is a humble (謙譲語(けんじょうご)) form of 聞(き)く (to listen). It lowers the speaker's position relative to the person being listened to. Used in formal speeches, business settings, and polite correspondence.

The 拝(はい) prefix indicates humble respect and appears in other humble verbs: 拝見(はいけん) (to see, humble), 拝読(はいどく) (to read, humble), 拝借(はいしゃく) (to borrow, humble).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご講演(こうえん)を拝聴(はいちょう)する (to listen to a lecture)
  • お話(はなし)を拝聴(はいちょう)する (to listen to someone's talk)
  • ご意見(いけん)を拝聴(はいちょう)する (to hear someone's opinion)
