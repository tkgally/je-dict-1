凝視(ぎょうし)

ぎょうし
noun, verb-suru
staring; gazing intently
1. staring; gazing intently; fixed gaze
Looking at something or someone fixedly and intently without looking away. A formal, literary word for an intense, sustained gaze.
彼(かれ)は画面(がめん)を凝視(ぎょうし)していた。
He was staring intently at the screen.
遠(とお)くの一点(いってん)を凝視(ぎょうし)したまま動(うご)かなかった。
He didn't move, his gaze fixed on a single point in the distance.
刑事(けいじ)は容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の顔(かお)を凝視(ぎょうし)して、嘘(うそ)をついていないか見極(みきわ)めようとした。
The detective stared intently at the suspect's face, trying to determine if he was lying.

USAGE:
A formal, literary word. In everyday conversation, じっと見(み)る or 見(み)つめる are used instead. 凝視(ぎょうし) appears primarily in written narratives, news reports, and formal descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 凝視(ぎょうし)する (stare intently at)
  • 〜を凝視(ぎょうし)する (fix one's gaze on ~)
  • 凝視(ぎょうし)し続(つづ)ける (continue to stare)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)つめる: to gaze at — more natural and commonly used in daily conversation
  • じっと見(み)る: to stare — colloquial expression for looking at something without moving
  • 注視(ちゅうし)する: to watch closely — implies careful observation, less about intensity
