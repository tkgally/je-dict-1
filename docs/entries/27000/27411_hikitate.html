引(ひ)き立(た)て

ひきたて
noun
patronage; favor; support
1. patronage; favor; support
Formal expression of gratitude for ongoing business or social support from a customer, client, or benefactor. Used in business letters, opening remarks, and shop signage.
お引(ひ)き立(た)てありがとうございます。
Thank you for your patronage.
長年(ながねん)のお引(ひ)き立(た)てに心(こころ)より感謝(かんしゃ)いたします。
I sincerely thank you for your many years of patronage.
今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬお引(ひ)き立(た)てのほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We respectfully ask for your continued patronage in the future as well.

Noun form of 引(ひ)き立(た)てる (to support, promote, favor). Almost always appears with the honorific お and refers to support extended by a customer or superior. Common on signs and in stock business phrases.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てを賜(たまわ)る: to receive your patronage (very formal)
  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てのほど: (formal closing softener)
  • 長年(ながねん)のお引(ひ)き立(た)て: many years of patronage
  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てに感謝(かんしゃ)する: to be grateful for your support

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ご愛顧(あいこ): your patronage — slightly more elevated, common in retail and service
  • ご高配(こうはい): your kind consideration — even more formal, used in correspondence
  • ご支援(しえん): your support — broader and less stiff
