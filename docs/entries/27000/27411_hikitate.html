引(ひ)き立(た)て

ひきたて
noun
patronage; personal backing; favor — especially the support a customer or superior gives to a shop, performer, or junior person (often used in business and theatrical contexts)
1. patronage; personal backing; favor — the support, recommendation, or preferential treatment that a customer, superior, or established person gives to a shop, performer, junior, etc.
The noun form of 引(ひ)き立(た)てる ("to favor / support / recommend"). It refers to the act of singling someone or something out for support — patronizing a particular shop, repeatedly recommending a junior employee, or championing a young performer. 引(ひ)き立(た)て almost always appears with the honorific お (お引(ひ)き立(た)て) in a phrase the speaker addresses to a customer or benefactor, expressing gratitude for the addressee's continued support. The implication is that the speaker has been the beneficiary of the addressee's generosity.
お引(ひ)き立(た)てを賜(たまわ)る。
To receive your patronage. (formal letter phrase)
日頃(ひごろ)より格別(かくべつ)のお引(ひ)き立(た)てを頂(いただ)き、ありがとうございます。
Thank you for the special patronage you regularly extend to us.
今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬお引(ひ)き立(た)てのほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We humbly ask for your continued, unchanging patronage going forward.
師匠(ししょう)のお引(ひ)き立(た)てがあって、若手(わかて)役者(やくしゃ)は大(おお)きな役(やく)をもらうことができた。
Thanks to his master's patronage, the young actor was able to land a major role.

引(ひ)き立(た)て is the noun form of the verb 引(ひ)き立(た)てる ("to favor / single out for support"). It refers to the patronage, backing, or active support that a customer, superior, or established figure shows toward a shop, junior, or younger artist. It is almost always used with the honorific お — お引(ひ)き立(た)て.

WHO/WHAT IT REFERS TO:

  • The actions of a benefactor (customer, superior, master) toward the speaker or speaker's organization
  • The speaker is the beneficiary, not the giver
  • Cannot describe one's own act of supporting someone — for that, use 引(ひ)き立(た)てる as a verb

FIXED PHRASES (BUSINESS / SHOP CONTEXT):

  • 日頃(ひごろ)より格別(かくべつ)のお引(ひ)き立(た)てを賜(たまわ)り、ありがとうございます。 — Thank you for your special patronage as always.
  • 今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬお引(ひ)き立(た)てのほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。 — We humbly ask for your continued patronage.
  • 皆様(みなさま)のお引(ひ)き立(た)てに支(ささ)えられて、当店(とうてん)は創業(そうぎょう)五十年(ごじゅうねん)を迎(むか)えることができました。 — Sustained by all of your patronage, our shop has reached its fiftieth anniversary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てを賜(たまわ)る: to receive (your) patronage (very formal)
  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てを頂(いただ)く: to receive (your) patronage
  • お引(ひ)き立(た)てに{あずかる}: to be the beneficiary of patronage
  • 格別(かくべつ)のお引(ひ)き立(た)て: special / extraordinary patronage
  • 変(か)わらぬお引(ひ)き立(た)て: continuing / unchanging patronage
  • 師匠(ししょう)の引(ひ)き立(た)て: a master's patronage (theater / arts world)
  • 上司(じょうし)の引(ひ)き立(た)て: a superior's backing (workplace)

CONTEXTS OF USE:

  • Shops/businesses to customers: appears in greeting cards, year-end letters, opening of business correspondence
  • Theater / traditional arts (歌舞伎(かぶき), 落語(らくご)): a master singling out a junior for advancement
  • Workplace: a senior promoting or recommending a junior employee for opportunities

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • ご愛顧(あいこ): (your) patronage — very common in retail business letters; near synonym
  • ご高配(こうはい): (your) kind consideration — broader, more abstract
  • ご支援(しえん): (your) support — neutral and broader; less archaic
  • ご厚情(こうじょう): (your) deep goodwill — focuses on warm feelings
  • 引(ひ)き立(た)てる: the source verb — to favor / single out / make stand out
  • 挽(ひ)きたて: (homophone) freshly ground (e.g., coffee beans) — completely unrelated meaning

REGISTER:

  • Formal — appropriate for business letters, ceremonial speeches, theater-world contexts
  • Always honorific in reference (お~)
  • Inappropriate in casual conversation
