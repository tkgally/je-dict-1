埃(ほこり)まみれ

ほこりまみれ
noun
covered in dust; dusty; dust-covered
1. covered in dust; dusty; dust-covered
Describes something or someone that is thickly coated with dust. Uses the まみれ pattern meaning 'covered with' or 'smeared with' something undesirable.
倉庫(そうこ)の中(なか)は埃(ほこり)まみれだった。
The inside of the warehouse was covered in dust.
埃(ほこり)まみれの本(ほん)を棚(たな)から取(と)り出(だ)した。
I took out a dust-covered book from the shelf.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの作業(さぎょう)で埃(ほこり)まみれになったので、すぐにシャワーを浴(あ)びた。
I got covered in dust from the moving work, so I took a shower right away.

Compound of 埃(ほこり) (dust) + まみれ (covered with). The suffix まみれ attaches to nouns to indicate being covered or smeared with something, typically something unpleasant or messy.

THE まみれ PATTERN:

まみれ always implies a negative or unclean covering. It cannot be used for pleasant things.

  • 血(ち)まみれ: covered in blood
  • 泥(どろ)まみれ: covered in mud
  • 汗(あせ)まみれ: drenched in sweat
  • 借金(しゃっきん)まみれ: drowning in debt (figurative)

GRAMMAR:

  • As a noun modifier: 埃(ほこり)まみれの + noun (埃(ほこり)まみれの部屋(へや))
  • As a predicate: 埃(ほこり)まみれだ / 埃(ほこり)まみれになる

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 埃(ほこり)だらけ: full of dust — similar meaning but だらけ focuses on quantity (lots of dust everywhere) while まみれ focuses on being coated
  • 汚(よご)れた: dirty; soiled — general term for being dirty, not specific to dust
