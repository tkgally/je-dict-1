準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)

じゅんびぶそく
noun, adjective-na
lack of preparation; inadequate preparation
1. lack of preparation; inadequate preparation; being unprepared
The state of not having prepared sufficiently for something. Often used to explain or criticize a poor outcome.
準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)で失敗(しっぱい)した。
I failed due to lack of preparation.
プレゼンが準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)だったと反省(はんせい)している。
I regret that my presentation was insufficiently prepared.
準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)のまま本番(ほんばん)を迎(むか)えてしまい、大(おお)いに後悔(こうかい)した。
I went into the real event without enough preparation and deeply regretted it.

A compound of 準備(じゅんび) (preparation) + 不足(ぶそく) (insufficiency). Very commonly used in self-reflection and criticism. The ~不足(ぶそく) pattern is productive and creates many similar compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)で失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail due to lack of preparation
  • 準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)を痛感(つうかん)する: to keenly feel one's lack of preparation
  • 準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)を反省(はんせい)する: to reflect on one's lack of preparation
  • 準備不足(じゅんびぶそく)のまま: while still unprepared

PATTERN:

The ~不足(ぶそく) suffix attaches to many nouns to express insufficiency:

  • 経験不足(けいけんぶそく): lack of experience
  • 睡眠不足(すいみんぶそく): lack of sleep
  • 人手不足(ひとでぶそく): labor shortage
  • 勉強不足(べんきょうぶそく): insufficient study

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不十分(ふじゅうぶん): insufficient; inadequate — more general and can describe anything that falls short, not just preparation
  • 準備(じゅんび)が足(た)りない: not enough preparation — same meaning expressed as a full phrase rather than a compound
