こつこつ

こつこつ
adverb, onomatopoeia
steadily; diligently; persistently
1. steadily; diligently; persistently
Describes doing something with patient, sustained effort over time, often involving repetitive small actions that accumulate into significant results.
こつこつ勉強(べんきょう)する。
To study steadily.
毎日(まいにち)こつこつ貯金(ちょきん)している。
I save money little by little every day.
こつこつ努力(どりょく)を続(つづ)けた結果(けっか)、やっと合格(ごうかく)できた。
As a result of persistently continuing my efforts, I finally passed.
2. with a tapping or knocking sound
Mimics the sound of hard objects striking a surface repeatedly, such as knocking on a door, footsteps on a hard floor, or tapping with a tool.
ドアをこつこつ叩(たた)く。
To knock on the door with a tap-tap sound.
廊下(ろうか)にこつこつと足音(あしおと)が響(ひび)いた。
Footsteps echoed tap-tap-tap down the hallway.
杖(つえ)でこつこつと地面(じめん)を突(つ)きながら歩(ある)いていた。
He was walking along, tapping the ground with his cane.

Describes patient, sustained effort through small repeated actions. The diligence sense is far more common in everyday conversation than the sound sense. Strongly positive — implies admirable perseverance rather than drudgery.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • こつこつ勉強(べんきょう)する: to study steadily
  • こつこつ働(はたら)く: to work diligently
  • こつこつ貯(た)める: to save up bit by bit
  • こつこつ努力(どりょく)する: to make persistent effort
  • こつこつ積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる: to accumulate steadily

USAGE:
Often paired with 毎日(まいにち) (every day) to emphasize daily consistency. Can function as an adverb modifying a verb directly, or with the particle と (こつこつと) for a slightly more formal or literary feel. The sound sense typically uses と.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • こつこつ vs. 地道(じみち)に: Both mean "steadily," but こつこつ emphasizes the repetitive, incremental nature of the effort, while 地道(じみち)に emphasizes the unpretentious, unglamorous quality of the approach.
  • こつこつ vs. ちょこちょこ: ちょこちょこ means doing something in small amounts but implies irregularity, while こつこつ implies disciplined consistency.
