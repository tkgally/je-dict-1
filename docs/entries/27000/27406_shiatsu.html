1. shiatsu; finger-pressure massage
A Japanese form of massage in which the practitioner applies pressure with the fingers, thumbs, and palms to specific points on the body. Often used to relieve muscle stiffness (肩(かた)こり) and fatigue. Sometimes paired with する as a verb.
肩(かた)を指圧(しあつ)してもらった。
I had my shoulders massaged with shiatsu.
指圧(しあつ)師(し)に腰(こし)を治療(ちりょう)してもらう。
To have a shiatsu therapist treat my lower back.
長時間(ちょうじかん)のデスクワークで凝(こ)り固(かた)まった肩(かた)を、指圧(しあつ)で丁寧(ていねい)にほぐしてもらった。
I had my shoulders, stiffened from long hours of desk work, carefully loosened with shiatsu.

Compound of 指(ゆび)/指(し) ("finger") + 圧(あつ) ("pressure"). A traditional Japanese therapeutic practice that draws on Chinese medicine theory of meridians and pressure points but developed into a recognized profession in early-20th-century Japan. Practitioners are licensed nationally as あん{摩(ま)マッサージ指圧(しあつ)師(し)}.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指圧(しあつ)する: to give a shiatsu massage
  • 指圧(しあつ)を受(う)ける: to receive a shiatsu treatment
  • 指圧(しあつ)師(し): shiatsu practitioner
  • 指圧(しあつ)療法(りょうほう): shiatsu therapy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • マッサージ: massage — generic term; includes shiatsu, oil massage, etc.
  • 整体(せいたい): chiropractic-like body adjustment — focuses on skeletal alignment
  • 鍼灸(しんきゅう): acupuncture and moxibustion — uses needles and heat, not pressure
