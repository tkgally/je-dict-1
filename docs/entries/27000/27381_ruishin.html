累進(るいしん)

るいしん
noun
graduated, progressive (esp. of taxation, fees, or rates that increase by tier)
1. progressive / graduated (increase in steps, typically of taxation or fees)
A system in which a rate, charge, or punishment increases in stages or tiers as some underlying quantity (income, usage, severity) rises. Most commonly seen in compounds: 累進(るいしん)課税(かぜい) (progressive taxation), 累進(るいしん)税率(ぜいりつ) (progressive tax rate), 累進(るいしん)制(せい) (graduated/progressive system). Bare 累進(るいしん) on its own is rare in conversation; it almost always appears in such compounds in tax, finance, legal, and policy writing.
累進(るいしん)課税(かぜい)が採用(さいよう)されている。
Progressive taxation is in use.
所得税(しょとくぜい)は累進(るいしん)税率(ぜいりつ)が適用(てきよう)される。
Progressive tax rates are applied to income tax.
累進(るいしん)制(せい)のもとでは、所得(しょとく)が多(おお)い人(ひと)ほど税率(ぜいりつ)が高(たか)くなる。
Under a progressive system, the higher one's income, the higher the tax rate.
所得(しょとく)格差(かくさ)を是正(ぜせい)する手段(しゅだん)として、累進(るいしん)課税(かぜい)の強化(きょうか)を求(もと)める声(こえ)が高(たか)まっている。
Voices calling for stronger progressive taxation as a means to correct income inequality are growing louder.

累進(るいしん) expresses a graduated, step-by-step increase. It is used almost exclusively in fixed compounds, especially in tax and finance.

FORMATION:

  • 累(るい) ("to accumulate, to pile up") + 進(しん) ("to advance, progress") → 累進(るいしん)

KEY COMPOUNDS:

  • 累進(るいしん)課税(かぜい): progressive taxation
  • 累進(るいしん)税率(ぜいりつ): progressive tax rate
  • 累進(るいしん)制(せい): progressive / graduated system
  • 累進(るいしん)的(てき): progressive (in nature) — used adjectivally

USAGE PATTERN:

  • Bare 累進(るいしん) as a standalone noun is uncommon; it nearly always combines with 課税(かぜい), 税率(ぜいりつ), 制(せい), or 的(てき).
  • Frequently contrasted with 比例(ひれい) ("proportional, flat") and 逆進(ぎゃくしん)/逆累進(ぎゃくるいしん) ("regressive").

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 累進(るいしん)的(てき): progressive (adjectival form, e.g., 累進(るいしん)的(てき)な税制(ぜいせい) "a progressive tax system")
  • 累計(るいけい): cumulative total — a related word with 累(るい) but different meaning (the running total of accumulated amounts)
  • 比例(ひれい)税(ぜい): proportional / flat tax — the typical contrast in tax policy
  • 逆進(ぎゃくしん)的(てき): regressive (of taxation) — the opposite tendency
  • 段階(だんかい)的(てき): step-by-step — overlaps in spirit but is much more general

USAGE NOTES:

  • Outside of tax and policy writing, you may also see 累進(るいしん) in the phrase 累進(るいしん)処遇(しょぐう), an old term in Japanese prison administration referring to a graded incarceration system in which inmates' privileges progress with good behavior. This usage is technical and rare in everyday text.
  • Glasses with 累進(るいしん)レンズ refers to progressive (multifocal) lenses — a separate technical sense from optometry, where lens power increases gradually from top to bottom.
