てへん

てへん
noun
the "hand" radical (扌); left-side hand radical in kanji
1. the kanji radical {扌|てへん} ("hand"), as it appears on the left side of a character
The 扌(てへん) radical is a stylized form of 手(て) ("hand") used as the left-side (偏(へん)) component of many kanji. Most kanji that contain てへん relate in some way to actions of the hand — pushing, pulling, holding, hitting, etc. Examples include 打(う)つ ("to hit"), 持(も)つ ("to hold"), 押(お)す ("to push"), and 投(な)げる ("to throw").
「打(う)つ」はてへんだ。
"{打|う}つ" has the hand radical.
てへんの漢字(かんじ)は手(て)に関係(かんけい)がある。
Kanji with the hand radical are related to the hand.
「投(な)げる」はてへんに「投(とう)」の右側(みぎがわ)を書(か)く。
To write "{投|な}げる," you write the hand radical on the left and the right side of "{投|とう}."
先生(せんせい)は「てへんは手(て)という字(じ)を細(ほそ)くした形(かたち)だ」と説明(せつめい)した。
The teacher explained, "The hand radical (てへん) is a slimmed-down form of the character for 'hand'."

てへん is the name of the kanji radical 扌(てへん), which is a stylized form of 手(て) ("hand") used as the left-side component of many kanji. It is one of the most productive radicals in Japanese: most kanji built with てへん denote actions performed with the hands.

FORMATION:

  • 手(て) ("hand") + ~へん (偏(へん), "left-side radical")
  • The ん in てへん comes from the rendaku-flavored reading of 偏(へん).

COMMON KANJI WITH てへん:

  • 打(う)つ: to hit
  • 持(も)つ: to hold
  • 押(お)す: to push
  • 引(ひ)く: to pull (note: this kanji uses ゆみへん 弓(ゆみ) instead, so a useful contrast)
  • 投(な)げる: to throw
  • 振(ふ)る: to wave / shake
  • 折(お)る: to fold / break
  • 捨(す)てる: to throw away

RELATED RADICAL TERMS:

  • 偏(へん): left-side radical (the position-name suffix used in てへん, さんずい, きへん, etc.)
  • さんずい: "three drops of water" radical (氵(さんずい)), found in water-related kanji
  • きへん: "tree" radical (木(き)), found in wood/tree-related kanji
  • くさかんむり: "grass crown" radical (艹(くさかんむり)), found above the character
  • 部首(ぶしゅ): kanji radical (the general term)

USAGE NOTES:

  • てへん is often introduced very early in elementary-school kanji education as a memorable example of how radicals carry meaning. Teachers commonly say things like "てへんがあるから手(て)に関係(かんけい)がある" ("It has the hand radical, so it has something to do with the hand").
  • The radical 扌(てへん) is never written as a standalone character; it appears only as the left-side element of other kanji.
