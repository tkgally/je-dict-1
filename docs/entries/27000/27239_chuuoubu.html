中央部(ちゅうおうぶ)

ちゅうおうぶ
noun
central part; center; middle section
1. central part; center; middle section
The middle area or section of a physical space, region, object, or structure. Used for geographical locations, buildings, documents, and other things that have a discernible center.
島(しま)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ)に山(やま)がある。
There is a mountain in the central part of the island.
建物(たてもの)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ)にエレベーターがある。
There is an elevator in the center of the building.
アフリカ大陸(たいりく)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ)には広大(こうだい)な熱帯雨林(ねったいうりん)が広(ひろ)がっている。
A vast tropical rainforest stretches across the central part of the African continent.

Compound of 中央(ちゅうおう) (center) + 部(ぶ) (part/section). A straightforward spatial term that is common in descriptions of geography, architecture, and layout. Slightly more formal and precise than 真(ま)ん中(なか) (the very center).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 都市(とし)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ): the central part of a city
  • 日本(にほん)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ): central Japan
  • 胸(むね)の中央部(ちゅうおうぶ): the center of the chest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中心部(ちゅうしんぶ): the core/heart — emphasizes being the most important or active area, not just spatially central
  • 真(ま)ん中(なか): the very middle — more colloquial and emphatic
  • 中央(ちゅうおう): center — the base word without 部(ぶ), used more broadly including for abstract concepts like government
