1. to state or declare formally (to an authority, in writing, or in court); to lodge (a complaint, objection, or claim)
A transitive ichidan verb used when one formally states something to an authority or in an official setting — typically lodging a complaint, raising an objection, declaring an intention, or registering a claim. The compound 申(もう)し+立(た)てる literally evokes "to say and set up," and the verb has a firmly formal, often legal feel. It is the verb behind nouns like 申(もう)し立(た)て ("a formal statement, motion, application").
異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる。
To raise an objection.
被告(ひこく)は無罪(むざい)を申(もう)し立(た)てた。
The defendant stated that he was not guilty.
判決(はんけつ)に不服(ふふく)がある場合(ばあい)は、二週間(にしゅうかん)以内(いない)に異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てることができる。
If you are dissatisfied with the ruling, you can lodge an objection within two weeks.
労働(ろうどう)条件(じょうけん)の改善(かいぜん)を求(もと)めて、労働(ろうどう)組合(くみあい)は経営(けいえい)陣(じん)に正式(せいしき)に苦情(くじょう)を申(もう)し立(た)てた。
Seeking improvements to working conditions, the labor union formally lodged a grievance with the management.

申(もう)し立(た)てる is a formal transitive ichidan verb meaning to state something formally to an authority, especially to lodge a complaint, objection, or claim. It is overwhelmingly used in legal, administrative, and formal-business contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive. Takes を for the thing stated: 異議(いぎ)を}{申(もう)し立(た)てる ("to lodge {an objection}").
  • The party to whom one states it is marked by に: 裁判所(さいばんしょ)に}{申(もう)し立(た)てる ("to file with {the court}").

CONJUGATION:

  • ichidan verb (-える ending)
  • ます-form: 申(もう)し立(た)てます
  • past: 申(もう)し立(た)てた
  • te-form: 申(もう)し立(た)てて
  • negative: 申(もう)し立(た)てない

FORMATION:

  • 申(もう)す ("to say," humble) + 立(た)てる ("to set up, raise") → 申(もう)し立(た)てる ("to formally state, to raise (an objection)")
  • 申(もう)す lends the formal/humble register; 立(た)てる adds the sense of formally putting forward.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (を + 申(もう)し立(た)てる):

  • 異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to lodge an objection (set phrase, very common in legal and meeting contexts)
  • 苦情(くじょう)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to lodge a complaint
  • 不服(ふふく)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to lodge an appeal / express dissatisfaction
  • 無罪(むざい)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to plead not guilty
  • 破産(はさん)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to file for bankruptcy

RELATED NOUN:

  • 申(もう)し立(た)て: a formal statement, motion, application, or objection — the noun form derived from this verb, common in legal documents (e.g., 異議(いぎ)申(もう)し立(た)て "objection")

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 申(もう)し出(で)る: to offer, to volunteer (a statement, request, or willingness) — softer in tone; often used for proposals rather than formal complaints
  • 訴(うった)える: to appeal, to sue, to complain to — broader, can be emotional or legal; 申(もう)し立(た)てる is procedural
  • 主張(しゅちょう)する: to assert, to claim — emphasizes content of a position; 申(もう)し立(た)てる emphasizes the act of formally raising it
  • 陳述(ちんじゅつ)する: to state formally (in legal proceedings) — very technical legal term
  • 申告(しんこく)する: to declare officially (e.g., tax return, customs) — for routine official declarations rather than complaints

USAGE NOTES:

  • 異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる is so frequent that it functions as a near-fixed phrase; it shows up in court proceedings, parliamentary debate, and meeting minutes.
  • In casual conversation, speakers would use 文句(もんく)を言(い)う or クレームをつける} for the same idea; {申(もう)し立(た)てる is reserved for formal settings.
  • The kanji 立 in 立(た)てる is the same one used in "to stand (something) up"; the metaphor is of "setting up" a formal statement.
