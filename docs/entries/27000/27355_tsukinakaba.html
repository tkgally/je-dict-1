月(つき)半(なか)ば

つきなかば
noun
middle of the month; mid-month
1. middle of the month; mid-month
The middle portion of a calendar month, roughly the 10th to 20th. Combines 月(つき) ("month") with 半(なか)ば ("middle, halfway point"). Used both as a time expression in itself and to anchor schedules: "by mid-month," "around the middle of the month," etc.
月(つき)半(なか)ばに払(はら)う。
I'll pay in the middle of the month.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)は月(つき)半(なか)ばまでに提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the report by the middle of the month.
月初(つきはじ)めは忙(いそが)しいので、打(う)ち合(あ)わせは月(つき)半(なか)ばあたりにしましょう。
The beginning of the month is busy, so let's set the meeting for around mid-month.
月(つき)半(なか)ばを過(す)ぎると、月末(げつまつ)締切(しめきり)の仕事(しごと)が気(き)になり始(はじ)める。
Once we get past the middle of the month, I start worrying about the end-of-month deadlines.

月(つき)半(なか)ば is a fairly common time expression for the middle stretch of a calendar month. The 半(なか)ば element is the same word that appears in many "middle of X" expressions: 年(とし)半(なか)ば ("middle of the year"), 道(みち)半(なか)ば ("halfway along the road / partway done").

FORMATION:

  • 月(つき) ("month") + 半(なか)ば ("middle, midway point") → 月(つき)半(なか)ば

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 月(つき)半(なか)ばに: in mid-month
  • 月(つき)半(なか)ばまでに: by mid-month
  • 月(つき)半(なか)ばを過(す)ぎる: to pass the middle of the month
  • 月(つき)半(なか)ばあたり: around mid-month
  • 月(つき)半(なか)ば頃(ごろ): roughly mid-month

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 月初(つきはじ)め / 月初(げっしょ): beginning of the month
  • 月末(げつまつ): end of the month
  • 中旬(ちゅうじゅん): middle ten days of the month (a more formal/precise term, used in news and documents)
  • 上旬(じょうじゅん): first ten days of the month
  • 下旬(げじゅん): last ten days of the month
  • 年(とし)半(なか)ば: middle of the year

USAGE NOTES:

  • 月(つき)半(なか)ば is conversational and slightly soft compared to 中旬(ちゅうじゅん), which is more precise and used in business writing, schedules, and weather forecasts. "6月(がつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん)" is the natural way to write "mid-June" in a printed schedule, while "月(つき)半(なか)ば" is more typical in speech.
  • 半(なか)ば can also be used as a noun on its own meaning "halfway, in the middle of doing," as in 仕事(しごと)半(なか)ばで帰(かえ)る ("to leave with the work half-done").
