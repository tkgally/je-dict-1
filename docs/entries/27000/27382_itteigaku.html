一定額(いっていがく)

いっていがく
noun
a fixed amount; a set amount of money
1. a fixed amount of money; a set sum
A predetermined, set sum of money — for example, a fixed monthly allowance, a flat fee, a fixed deductible, or a threshold below which special rules apply. 一定額(いっていがく) appears constantly in financial, legal, tax, and contractual writing where a numeric amount is held constant or compared against a threshold.
毎月(まいつき)一定額(いっていがく)を貯金(ちょきん)している。
I save a fixed amount every month.
一定額(いっていがく)以上(いじょう)の支出(ししゅつ)には上司(じょうし)の承認(しょうにん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Expenditures above a fixed amount require the approval of a superior.
この{制度(せいど)}では、所得(しょとく)が一定額(いっていがく)を超(こ)えると支援(しえん)の対象(たいしょう)から外(はず)れる。
Under this system, you fall outside the scope of support if your income exceeds a fixed amount.
老後(ろうご)の備(そな)えとして、給料(きゅうりょう)から自動的(じどうてき)に一定額(いっていがく)を投資信託(とうししんたく)に回(まわ)している。
As preparation for old age, I automatically channel a fixed amount from my salary into mutual funds.

一定額(いっていがく) is a standard administrative and financial term meaning "a fixed amount" of money.

FORMATION:

  • 一定(いってい) ("fixed, set, constant") + 額(がく) (from 金額(きんがく) "amount of money") → 一定額(いっていがく)

TYPICAL CONTEXTS:

  • Personal finance and saving plans (一定額(いっていがく)を貯金(ちょきん)する "to save a fixed amount")
  • Tax and welfare thresholds (一定額(いっていがく)以上(いじょう)の所得(しょとく) "income above a certain amount")
  • Insurance deductibles, copay caps, and grant limits
  • Corporate expense-approval rules
  • Government subsidies and benefit programs

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一定額(いっていがく)以上(いじょう): a fixed amount or more (a threshold above which something applies)
  • 一定額(いっていがく)以下(いか): a fixed amount or less (a threshold below which something applies)
  • 一定額(いっていがく)を超(こ)える: to exceed a fixed amount
  • 一定額(いっていがく)に達(たっ)する: to reach a fixed amount
  • 毎月(まいつき)一定額(いっていがく): a fixed amount each month
  • 一定額(いっていがく)まで: up to a fixed amount

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 一定(いってい): fixed, set, constant — the broader adjective; modifies many nouns (一定(いってい)の条件(じょうけん) "certain conditions")
  • 定額(ていがく): fixed amount, flat rate — closely related and often interchangeable; 定額(ていがく)制(せい) "flat-rate plan" is a common service category. 定額(ていがく) feels more set-in-advance / contractual; 一定額(いっていがく) more often functions as "a (single) certain amount" used as a threshold.
  • 限度額(げんどがく): maximum amount, ceiling
  • 上限(じょうげん) / 下限(かげん): upper / lower limit
  • 金額(きんがく): monetary amount (general)

USAGE NOTES:

  • 一定額(いっていがく)以上(いじょう) is an extremely common phrase in administrative and policy writing, equivalent to "a certain (specified) amount or more" in English.
  • For consumer subscription pricing ("flat rate"), 定額(ていがく)制(せい) is the standard label, not 一定額(いっていがく)制(せい).
