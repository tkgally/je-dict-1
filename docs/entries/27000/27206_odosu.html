1. to threaten; to intimidate; to menace; to coerce
To use threats of harm or negative consequences to frighten someone or force them into compliance.
子供(こども)を脅(おど)してはいけない。
You must not threaten children.
彼(かれ)はナイフで店員(てんいん)を脅(おど)した。
He threatened the store clerk with a knife.
秘密(ひみつ)をばらすと脅(おど)されて、仕方(しかた)なく従(したが)った。
Threatened with having a secret exposed, I had no choice but to comply.

A godan verb conjugating with the す row (脅(おど)す → 脅(おど)して → 脅(おど)した). This is the more colloquial word for threatening, compared to the formal 脅迫(きょうはく)する.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: XがYを脅(おど)す

ASPECT:

  • Type: Atelic (ongoing action)
  • 脅(おど)している = is threatening / is in the process of threatening

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xを脅(おど)す: to threaten X (the person being threatened)
  • Xで脅(おど)す: to threaten with X (the weapon or means used)
  • ～と脅(おど)す: to threaten that... (content of the threat)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ナイフで脅(おど)す: to threaten with a knife
  • 金(かね)を出(だ)せと脅(おど)す: to threaten someone to hand over money
  • 殺(ころ)すと脅(おど)す: to threaten to kill
  • 脅(おど)して金(かね)を奪(うば)う: to take money by intimidation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脅(おびや)かす: to threaten; to endanger — focuses more on creating a sense of danger or insecurity; more literary
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)する: to threaten; to coerce — the formal/legal term for making threats
  • 威嚇(いかく)する: to intimidate; to menace — emphasizes a display of power to frighten
  • 恐喝(きょうかつ)する: to blackmail; to extort — specifically involves demanding money through threats
