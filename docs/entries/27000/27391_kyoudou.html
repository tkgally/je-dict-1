1. cooperation, collaboration — working together as partners, especially among organizations or institutions; close to 共同 but emphasizing partnership and shared effort
Working together with one or more partners toward a shared aim, with a particular emphasis on cooperative or partnership-style organization. 協同(きょうどう) overlaps heavily with 共同(きょうどう) (same reading) but tends to highlight the spirit of cooperation and joint effort, especially among institutions — schools and businesses, government and citizens, member organizations of a cooperative. It is the 協同(きょうどう) of 協同組合(きょうどうくみあい) ("cooperative," e.g., agricultural co-op), one of its most common compounds.
産業(さんぎょう)協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい)。
An industrial cooperative.
地域(ちいき)と学校(がっこう)が協同(きょうどう)して子(こ)どもを育(そだ)てる。
The community and schools cooperate to raise the children.
農業(のうぎょう)協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい)は、農家(のうか)の経営(けいえい)を支(ささ)える重要(じゅうよう)な役割(やくわり)を果(は)たしている。
Agricultural cooperatives play an important role in supporting farmers' operations.
企業(きぎょう)と大学(だいがく)が協同(きょうどう)して新(あたら)しい技術(ぎじゅつ)を開発(かいはつ)するプロジェクトが各地(かくち)で始(はじ)まっている。
Projects in which companies and universities collaborate to develop new technologies are starting up in various regions.

協同(きょうどう) is a noun (often used adverbially as 協同(きょうどう)して}, or with する as {協同(きょうどう)する) meaning cooperation or collaboration. It overlaps strongly with the homophone 共同(きょうどう) ("joint, in common") but carries a slightly different emphasis.

NUANCE VS 共同(きょうどう):

  • 共同(きょうどう): "in common, joint" — emphasizes that something is shared or done together. Common in everyday compounds: 共同(きょうどう)生活(せいかつ) (shared living), 共同(きょうどう)研究(けんきゅう) (joint research), 共同(きょうどう)経営(けいえい) (joint management).
  • 協同(きょうどう): "cooperative, partnership-based" — emphasizes the spirit of cooperation and partnership, especially institutional. Most prominent in 協同組合(きょうどうくみあい) ("cooperative society") and related cooperative-movement vocabulary.
  • In many contexts the two are interchangeable, but 協同(きょうどう) is the standard choice for cooperative-society vocabulary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい): cooperative society (the canonical compound; e.g., 農業(のうぎょう)協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい) "agricultural co-op," abbreviated 農協 (のうきょう))
  • 生活(せいかつ)協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい): consumer cooperative (often abbreviated 生協 (せいきょう))
  • 協同(きょうどう)作業(さぎょう): cooperative work, joint task
  • 協同(きょうどう)する: to cooperate, to work together
  • 協同(きょうどう)して}{取(と)り組(く)む: to tackle (something) cooperatively
  • 産学(さんがく)協同(きょうどう): industry-academia cooperation
  • 官民(かんみん)協同(きょうどう): public-private cooperation

GRAMMATICAL BEHAVIOR:

  • Often used in compounds before another noun: 協同(きょうどう)組合(くみあい), 協同(きょうどう)作業(さぎょう).
  • As a verb: 協同(きょうどう)する, 協同(きょうどう){して} ("in cooperation").
  • Used with と} for the cooperating partner: {学校(がっこう)と協同(きょうどう)して.

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 共同(きょうどう): in common, joint — much more frequent in everyday vocabulary; see nuance discussion above
  • 協力(きょうりょく): cooperation, support, help — a softer, more general word; everyday usage
  • 連携(れんけい): linking up, coordination — emphasizes coordinating actions between organizations
  • 提携(ていけい): tie-up, business partnership — formal business partnership between companies
  • 合同(ごうどう): joint, combined — joining of separate units (e.g., 合同チーム "combined team")

USAGE NOTES:

  • The compound 協同組合(きょうどうくみあい) is so central to 協同(きょうどう)'s usage that many learners encounter the word first in that legal/institutional context.
  • 協同(きょうどう) can take する to form a verb (協同(きょうどう)する), but 協力(きょうりょく)する is more common in everyday speech.
  • Pronunciation is identical to 共同(きょうどう): きょうどう (four morae).
