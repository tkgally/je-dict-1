そのもの

そのもの
expression
itself; the very thing; in itself
1. itself; the very thing; in itself
Attached to a noun or na-adjective stem to emphasize that the preceding word is exactly what is meant, with no qualification. Equivalent to English 'X itself' or 'X-ness itself'.
彼(かれ)は誠実(せいじつ)そのものだ。
He is sincerity itself.
問題(もんだい)そのものを見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reexamine the problem itself.
デザインは美(うつく)しいが、使(つか)いやすさそのものはあまり良(よ)くない。
The design is beautiful, but the usability itself isn't very good.

Compound of その (that) + もの (thing), but functions idiomatically as an emphatic 'X itself' rather than 'that thing'.

USAGE:

  • After a noun: 愛(あい)そのもの 'love itself'
  • After a na-adjective stem: 親切(しんせつ)そのもの 'kindness itself'
  • Often praises or characterizes someone/something as a pure embodiment

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 元気(げんき)そのもの: the picture of health
  • 真面目(まじめ)そのもの: seriousness itself
  • 存在(そんざい)そのもの: existence itself; the very existence

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自体(じたい): itself — more neutral and analytical; often used in formal/written discussion (問題(もんだい)自体(じたい))
  • そのまま: as it is — refers to keeping a state unchanged, not emphasis
