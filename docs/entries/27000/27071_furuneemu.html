フルネーム

ふるねーむ
noun
full name
1. full name; complete name including family name and given name
A person's complete name, as opposed to just their family name or given name alone. Used when specifying that the entire name is required.
フルネームで書(か)いてください。
Please write your full name.
彼(かれ)のフルネームを知(し)らない。
I don't know his full name.
申込書(もうしこみしょ)にはフルネームと住所(じゅうしょ)を記入(きにゅう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
You need to fill in your full name and address on the application form.

A wasei-eigo-style compound from English "full" + "name." While the component words are English, フルネーム is used naturally in Japanese and is well understood. In Japanese naming conventions, the full name consists of 姓(せい) (family name) followed by 名(めい) (given name).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フルネームで書(か)く: to write one's full name
  • フルネームで呼(よ)ぶ: to call someone by their full name
  • フルネームを記入(きにゅう)する: to fill in one's full name
  • フルネームで検索(けんさく)する: to search by full name

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 氏名(しめい): full name — the formal/official term used on documents and forms
  • 名前(なまえ): name — can mean either full name or given name depending on context
  • 本名(ほんみょう): real name — as opposed to a stage name, pen name, or username
