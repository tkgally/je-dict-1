1. to predict; to foresee; to foretell
To know or sense in advance that something will happen, especially natural disasters or future events.
地震(じしん)を予知(よち)するのは難(むずか)しい。
Predicting earthquakes is difficult.
動物(どうぶつ)は災害(さいがい)を予知(よち)する能力(のうりょく)があると言(い)われている。
It is said that animals have the ability to foresee disasters.
将来(しょうらい)のことを完全(かんぜん)に予知(よち)することは誰(だれ)にもできない。
No one can completely foresee the future.

Often used in the context of natural disasters, especially earthquakes (地震予知(じしんよち)). Implies knowing something before it happens through sensing or scientific means, not through deliberate calculation or analysis.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地震予知(じしんよち): earthquake prediction
  • 予知能力(よちのうりょく): ability to foresee; precognition
  • 予知(よち)できない: unpredictable
  • 予知夢(よちゆめ): prophetic dream

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予測(よそく)する: to predict; to forecast — based on data and analysis; more scientific and rational
  • 予想(よそう)する: to expect; to anticipate — based on reasoning or guessing; more everyday
  • 予言(よげん)する: to prophesy — often implies a supernatural or mystical prediction
  • 予報(よほう): forecast — used for weather and similar systematic predictions
