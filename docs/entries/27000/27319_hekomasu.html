1. to dent; to make a dent in (something); to push in
To physically deform something by pushing or striking it inward, creating a concave area. The transitive counterpart of 凹(へこ)む.
ボールが当(あ)たって車(くるま)の屋根(やね)を凹(へこ)ました。
The ball hit the car and dented the roof.
缶(かん)を足(あし)で踏(ふ)んで凹(へこ)ました。
I stomped on the can and crushed it flat.
自転車(じてんしゃ)を壁(かべ)にぶつけて、フレームを凹(へこ)ましてしまった。
I crashed my bike into the wall and ended up denting the frame.
2. to make (someone) feel down; to dishearten; to take down a peg
To cause someone to feel discouraged, embarrassed, or psychologically deflated — often by criticism, defeat, or a sharp comeback. The transitive counterpart of 凹(へこ)む in its emotional sense.
先輩(せんぱい)に言(い)い返(かえ)されて、彼(かれ)を凹(へこ)ますことができなかった。
My senior fired back at me, and I couldn't take him down a peg.
あんな鋭(するど)い反論(はんろん)をされて、すっかり彼(かれ)を凹(へこ)ませてしまった。
Hit with such a sharp counterargument, I completely deflated him.
そんな自慢(じまん)話(ばなし)ばかりしていると、誰(だれ)かに凹(へこ)まされるよ。
If you keep boasting like that, someone's going to take you down a peg.
Used in a passive form here (凹(へこ)まされる).

凹(へこ)ます is the transitive counterpart of 凹(へこ)む. The pair has both a literal sense (causing/being a dent) and a figurative sense (causing/feeling emotional deflation).

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • Transitive: ボールが屋根(やね)を凹(へこ)ます (The ball dents the roof)
  • Intransitive: 屋根(やね)が凹(へこ)む (The roof gets dented)

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • Sense 2 is informal/colloquial. The image is of someone's confidence being "dented" — often by a witty retort, defeat in argument, or harsh criticism.
  • Common pattern: 誰(だれ)かを凹(へこ)ます (to bring someone down emotionally). Often appears in sports/competition narratives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 缶(かん)を凹(へこ)ます: to dent / crush a can
  • 車(くるま)を凹(へこ)ます: to dent a car
  • 鼻(はな)っ柱(ぱしら)を凹(へこ)ます: to take down a peg (literally "dent the nose pillar" — the pride)
  • 彼(かれ)を凹(へこ)ましてやった: I really took him down a peg

RELATED TERMS:

  • 凹(へこ)む: intransitive counterpart — to be dented; to feel down
  • へこむ is also commonly used as a slang verb among younger speakers for feeling depressed or emotionally low.
  • 凹(へこ)み: the noun (a dent)
  • 潰(つぶ)す: to crush, smash (broader, more violent destruction)
  • 折(お)る: to break (a different kind of damage)

ORTHOGRAPHY NOTE:
The kanji 凹(おう) is one of the simplest pictographic kanji — its shape literally depicts a concave indentation. In casual writing, the verb is often written in hiragana (へこます) for readability.
