ナット

なっと
noun
nut — the threaded fastener that screws onto a bolt to hold pieces together (loanword from English; mechanical hardware sense only)
1. nut — a small block (typically metal) with an internal screw thread that pairs with a bolt to fasten objects together
Loanword from English "nut," used exclusively for the mechanical hardware item that screws onto a bolt (ボルト}). ナット is the standard term in Japanese hardware stores, machinery manuals, and DIY contexts. It is paired conceptually with ボルト, and the two are normally referenced together as ボルトとナット. Common shapes include the standard hexagonal nut ({六角(ろっかく)ナット), the wing nut (蝶(ちょう)ナット), the square nut (四角(しかく)ナット), and the cap nut (袋(ふくろ)ナット). NOTE: ナット in this sense has no connection to the food 納豆(なっとう), which is a longer, separately written word.
ナットを締(し)める。
To tighten the nut.
ボルトとナットで固定(こてい)する。
To fasten (it) with bolts and nuts.
緩(ゆる)んだナットをスパナで締(し)め直(なお)した。
I retightened the loose nut with a spanner.
自転車(じてんしゃ)の車輪(しゃりん)を外(はず)すには、中央(ちゅうおう)の六角(ろっかく)ナットを緩(ゆる)める必要(ひつよう)がある。
To remove a bicycle wheel, you need to loosen the hexagonal nut at the center.

ナット is the loanword for the mechanical fastener "nut" that pairs with a bolt. It is used in hardware, manufacturing, automotive, and DIY contexts.

KEY TERMINOLOGY:

  • ボルト: bolt (the threaded shaft)
  • ナット: nut (the threaded block that screws onto the bolt)
  • ねじ: screw (general term for any threaded fastener; can include both above)
  • ワッシャー: washer (the flat disc that goes between the nut and the surface)

COMMON COMPOUNDS / VARIETIES:

  • 六角(ろっかく)ナット: hexagonal nut (the standard six-sided nut)
  • 四角(しかく)ナット: square nut
  • 蝶(ちょう)ナット (or ウイングナット): wing nut (with two protrusions for hand tightening)
  • 袋(ふくろ)ナット: cap nut / acorn nut (closed at one end for a finished look)
  • 溝付(みぞつ)きナット: slotted nut (with slots for a cotter pin)
  • ロックナット: lock nut (designed to resist loosening)
  • ナイロンナット: nylon-insert lock nut
  • 高(たか)ナット: long / coupling nut (for joining two threaded rods end-to-end)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ナットを締(し)める: to tighten a nut
  • ナットを緩(ゆる)める: to loosen a nut
  • ナットを回(まわ)す: to turn a nut
  • ナットを外(はず)す: to remove a nut
  • ナットが緩(ゆる)む: a nut comes loose
  • ボルトとナット: bolt and nut (the standard pair)

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • ボルト: bolt — the partner fastener
  • ねじ: screw (broad category) / a screw
  • ビス: small screw (often for wood or sheet metal); sometimes interchangeable with ねじ
  • ワッシャー: washer
  • スパナ / レンチ: spanner / wrench (the tools used on ナット)
  • 工具(こうぐ): tool(s) — general

IMPORTANT — DO NOT CONFUSE WITH 納豆(なっとう):

  • 納豆(なっとう): fermented soybeans (the famous Japanese food)
  • ナット (loanword): mechanical nut for a bolt
  • The two are written and pronounced differently:
  • ナット (nat-to with a final short tsu sound and a clipped final to)
  • 納豆(なっとう) (nat-too with a long final ō)
  • The food is normally written in kanji 納豆(なっとう) or in hiragana, while the hardware is normally written in katakana ナット
  • Despite English/romaji overlap, native speakers do not confuse them in context

USAGE NOTES:

  • ナット by itself in isolation is unambiguous in a hardware context
  • In a culinary context, ナット is not a clipped form of any standard food name; the food is 納豆(なっとう)
  • The English "nut" meaning "a hard-shelled fruit" is rendered with 木(き)の実(み), ナッツ (nuts, plural-feeling, often roasted snack nuts), or specific names like クルミ (walnut), アーモンド (almond)
