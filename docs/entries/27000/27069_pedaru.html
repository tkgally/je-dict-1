ペダル

ぺだる
noun
pedal
1. pedal; a lever operated by the foot
A foot-operated control found on bicycles, cars, pianos, and other equipment.
ペダルを踏(ふ)む。
To press the pedal.
自転車(じてんしゃ)のペダルが重(おも)くなってきた。
The bicycle pedals have gotten stiff.
アクセルペダルとブレーキペダルを間違(まちが)えて事故(じこ)を起(お)こした。
They caused an accident by confusing the accelerator pedal with the brake pedal.

From English "pedal." Used for any foot-operated lever, including bicycle pedals, car pedals (accelerator, brake, clutch), and piano pedals. The most common context is bicycles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ペダルを踏(ふ)む: to step on / press a pedal
  • ペダルを漕(こ)ぐ: to pedal (a bicycle)
  • アクセルペダル: accelerator pedal; gas pedal
  • ブレーキペダル: brake pedal
  • ペダルの踏(ふ)み間違(まちが)い: pedal misapplication (pressing wrong pedal)

USAGE:
The compound ペダルの踏(ふ)み間違(まちが)い (pedal misapplication) is a frequently reported cause of traffic accidents in Japan, particularly involving elderly drivers. It appears regularly in news coverage.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • アクセル: accelerator — specifically the gas pedal or throttle
  • ブレーキ: brake — specifically the brake mechanism or pedal
