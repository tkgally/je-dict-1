講習会(こうしゅうかい)

こうしゅうかい
noun
workshop; training course; seminar
1. workshop; training course; seminar; class
An organized session where participants learn a specific skill or body of knowledge, typically over a short period.
講習会(こうしゅうかい)に参加(さんか)する。
To participate in a training course.
来月(らいげつ)、防災(ぼうさい)講習会(こうしゅうかい)が開(ひら)かれる。
A disaster preparedness workshop will be held next month.
会社(かいしゃ)が費用(ひよう)を出(だ)してくれるので、安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)の講習会(こうしゅうかい)を受(う)けてきた。
Since the company covered the cost, I attended a safety management training course.

A compound of 講習(こうしゅう) (training, instruction) + 会(かい) (gathering, meeting). Refers to organized training events that are typically shorter than full courses — often one day or a few sessions. Common in workplace training, community education, and certification programs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 講習会(こうしゅうかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold a workshop
  • 講習会(こうしゅうかい)を受(う)ける: to attend a training course
  • 講習会(こうしゅうかい)に申(もう)し込(こ)む: to apply for a workshop
  • 安全(あんぜん)講習会(こうしゅうかい): safety training workshop
  • 初心者(しょしんしゃ)向(む)け講習会(こうしゅうかい): beginners' workshop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • セミナー: seminar — often more academic or business-oriented; sometimes implies a lecture format
  • 研修(けんしゅう): training; professional development — broader term; can refer to longer training programs
  • ワークショップ: workshop — loanword increasingly used, especially for creative or hands-on sessions
  • 勉強会(べんきょうかい): study group — more informal; often self-organized among peers
