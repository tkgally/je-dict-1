整体(せいたい)

せいたい
noun
seitai — a Japanese form of bodywork that adjusts the body's musculoskeletal alignment; loosely "chiropractic/osteopathic treatment"
1. seitai — a category of Japanese manual-therapy techniques focused on correcting musculoskeletal alignment and bodily balance
A broad category of Japanese manual-therapy techniques aimed at adjusting the body's structure — typically the spine, pelvis, and joints — and rebalancing posture and muscle tension. 整体(せいたい) is loosely comparable to chiropractic or osteopathic care in the West and is often translated that way, though it is not a regulated medical specialty under Japanese law: most 整体(せいたい)師(し) (seitai practitioners) operate outside the licensed medical professions, drawing on a mix of traditional Japanese, Chinese, and Western bodywork techniques.
整体(せいたい)に通(かよ)う。
To go for seitai treatments regularly.
腰痛(ようつう)を治(なお)すために整体(せいたい)を受(う)けている。
I'm receiving seitai treatments to fix my back pain.
整体(せいたい)師(し)に骨盤(こつばん)の歪(ゆが)みを指摘(してき)された。
The seitai practitioner pointed out that my pelvis was misaligned.
月(つき)に一(いち)度(ど)整体(せいたい)に通(かよ)うようになってから、肩(かた)こり}や{頭痛(ずつう)が以前(いぜん)よりずいぶん楽(らく)になった。
Since I started going to seitai once a month, my stiff shoulders and headaches have become significantly easier than before.

整体(せいたい) is a broad term for Japanese manual-therapy techniques that aim to adjust musculoskeletal alignment and bodily balance. It overlaps with Western chiropractic and osteopathy but is not identical.

FORMATION:

  • 整(せい) ("to arrange, put in order, align") + 体(たい) ("body") → 整体(せいたい) "aligning the body"
  • The same 整(せい) appears in 整(ととの)える ("to put in order") and 調整(ちょうせい) ("adjustment").

SCOPE AND REGULATION:

  • 整体(せいたい) is an umbrella term: practitioners use a wide variety of techniques (pelvic alignment, joint mobilization, stretching, soft-tissue work), often blending traditional Japanese, Chinese, and Western chiropractic approaches.
  • In Japan, 整体(せいたい) is NOT a licensed medical profession: anyone can open a 整体(せいたい)院(いん). This contrasts with 柔道(じゅうどう)整復(せいふく)師(し) (judo-style bone-setters) and 鍼灸(しんきゅう)師(し) (acupuncturists), who are licensed.
  • For licensed musculoskeletal care, patients often see a 整形(せいけい)外科(げか) (orthopedics) clinic or a 接骨(せっこつ)院(いん) / 整骨(せいこつ)院(いん) (bone-setting clinic).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 整体(せいたい)を受(う)ける: to receive seitai treatment
  • 整体(せいたい)に通(かよ)う: to go regularly for seitai
  • 整体(せいたい)師(し): seitai practitioner
  • 整体(せいたい)院(いん): seitai clinic
  • 整体(せいたい)院(いん)に行(い)く: to go to a seitai clinic
  • 骨盤(こつばん)の歪(ゆが)みを整(ととの)える: to correct pelvic misalignment
  • 腰痛(ようつう)に整体(せいたい): seitai for back pain

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 指圧(しあつ): shiatsu — finger-pressure massage; distinct from but often offered alongside seitai
  • マッサージ: massage (general loanword) — broader; can include shiatsu, oil massage, etc.
  • 接骨(せっこつ)院(いん) / 整骨(せいこつ)院(いん): a clinic of a licensed 柔道(じゅうどう)整復(せいふく)師(し) (bone-setter); covers injuries and may be insurance-covered
  • カイロプラクティック: chiropractic — Western chiropractic; in Japan not a regulated medical profession either, often overlapping with 整体(せいたい)
  • オステオパシー: osteopathy — Western osteopathic technique
  • 整形(せいけい)外科(げか): orthopedic surgery / clinic — the licensed medical specialty
  • 鍼灸(しんきゅう): acupuncture and moxibustion — a separate licensed traditional-medicine specialty

USAGE NOTES:

  • 整体(せいたい) is a noun; 整体(せいたい)する is uncommon — speakers usually say 整体(せいたい)を受(う)ける ("to receive seitai") or 整体(せいたい)に行(い)く ("to go to a seitai clinic").
  • The English word that best fits in translation depends on context: "chiropractic," "bodywork," "manual therapy," or simply "seitai" with a brief gloss.
  • The word 整体(せいたい) is a homophone of 生態(せいたい) ("ecology") and 声帯(せいたい) ("vocal cords"); context distinguishes.
