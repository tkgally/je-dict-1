係員(かかりいん)

かかりいん
noun
person in charge; attendant; clerk; staff member
1. person in charge; attendant; clerk; staff member
Refers to a person responsible for or assigned to a particular task or area, often seen in public facilities, transportation, and customer service contexts.
係員(かかりいん)に聞(き)いてください。
Please ask a staff member.
駅(えき)の係員(かかりいん)が案内(あんない)してくれた。
A station attendant guided me.
入口(いりぐち)で係員(かかりいん)がチケットを確認(かくにん)している。
An attendant is checking tickets at the entrance.
お困(こま)りの際(さい)は、近(ちか)くの係員(かかりいん)にお声(こえ)がけください。
If you need help, please speak to a nearby staff member.

Refers to a worker or staff member responsible for a specific duty or area. Unlike 担当者(たんとうしゃ), which emphasizes being in charge of a specific case or project, 係員(かかりいん) emphasizes the role of attending to tasks at a particular post or station, typically in public-facing service positions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 係員(かかりいん)に尋(たず)ねる: to ask a staff member
  • 係員(かかりいん)の指示(しじ)に従(したが)う: to follow the attendant's instructions
  • 係員(かかりいん)を呼(よ)ぶ: to call a staff member

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 担当者(たんとうしゃ): person in charge — focuses on responsibility for a specific matter or case
  • 職員(しょくいん): employee/staff — broader term for institutional employees
  • 係(かかり): charge/duty — can refer to the role itself or the person informally
