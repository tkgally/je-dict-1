1. to give up on; to abandon; to write off
To conclude that something or someone is hopeless and decide to stop investing effort, money, or attention.
あの店(みせ)を見切(みき)った。
I gave up on that store.
在庫(ざいこ)を見切(みき)って安(やす)く売(う)ることにした。
We decided to write off the inventory and sell it cheaply.
将来性(しょうらいせい)がないと見切(みき)って、会社(かいしゃ)を辞(や)めた。
Having concluded there was no future in it, I quit the company.
2. to see through; to judge fully; to size up
To assess something or someone completely and reach a decisive conclusion about their true nature or value.
相手(あいて)の実力(じつりょく)を見切(みき)る。
To size up the opponent's ability.
彼(かれ)は一瞬(いっしゅん)で敵(てき)の動(うご)きを見切(みき)った。
He read the enemy's movements in an instant.
市場(しじょう)の動向(どうこう)を的確(てきかく)に見切(みき)るのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to accurately gauge market trends.

A compound verb from 見(み)る (to see) + 切(き)る (to cut/complete). The 切(き)る ending adds the nuance of doing something decisively or completely — here, seeing something through to a final conclusion.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: XがYを見切(みき)る

ASPECT:

  • Type: Telic (has endpoint)
  • 見切(みき)っている = has given up on / has already judged

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見切(みき)りをつける: to give up on; to cut one's losses (closely related set phrase)
  • 在庫(ざいこ)を見切(みき)る: to write off inventory
  • 見切(みき)り品(ひん): clearance items; marked-down goods
  • 見切(みき)り発車(はっしゃ): jumping the gun; starting before things are ready

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 諦(あきら)める: to give up — focuses on emotional resignation; 見切(みき)る implies a more rational, decisive judgment
  • 見極(みきわ)める: to ascertain; to discern — focuses on carefully determining the truth; less decisive than 見切(みき)る
  • 見限(みかぎ)る: to give up on; to abandon — very close synonym to sense 1, with a slightly stronger nuance of disgust or disappointment
