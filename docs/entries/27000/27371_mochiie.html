持(も)ち家(いえ)

もちいえ
noun
owned home; a house one owns (as opposed to a rental)
1. owned home; one's own house
A house owned by the resident, as contrasted with a rental property (賃貸(ちんたい)). The word appears regularly in housing surveys, real-estate advertisements, life-planning articles, and discussions of household finances. It implies a single-family home or detached house but is often extended to include owned condominiums.
持(も)ち家(いえ)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in a house I own.
将来(しょうらい)は持(も)ち家(いえ)を買(か)いたい。
In the future, I'd like to buy my own home.
賃貸(ちんたい)と持(も)ち家(いえ)、どちらが得(とく)か議論(ぎろん)されている。
There's a debate over whether renting or owning a home is more economical.
若(わか)い世代(せだい)の間(あいだ)では、無理(むり)をして持(も)ち家(いえ)を買(か)わずに賃貸(ちんたい)で暮(く)らし続(つづ)ける人(ひと)も増(ふ)えている。
Among the younger generation, more people are choosing to keep renting rather than stretching their finances to buy their own home.

持(も)ち家(いえ) refers to a home owned by the person who lives in it, as opposed to a rented one.

FORMATION:

  • 持(も)つ ("to own, hold") + 家(いえ) ("house") → 持(も)ち家(いえ)
  • The 持(も)ち- prefix attaches to many words to mean "owned, in one's possession" (cf. 持(も)ち物(もの) "belongings," 持(も)ち株(かぶ) "the stock one holds").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持(も)ち家(いえ)を買(か)う / 手(て)に入(い)れる: to buy / acquire one's own home
  • 持(も)ち家(いえ)に住(す)む: to live in an owned home
  • 持(も)ち家(いえ)派(は): people who prefer owning over renting
  • 持(も)ち家(いえ)率(りつ): home-ownership rate
  • 持(も)ち家(いえ)か賃貸(ちんたい)か: "owning or renting" — a stock phrase in lifestyle and finance writing

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 賃貸(ちんたい): rental, leased property — the standard antonym
  • 自宅(じたく): one's own home/residence — focuses on "my home" regardless of ownership; can refer to a rented apartment as long as it is where you live
  • 一戸建(いっこだ)て: detached house (single-family home) — often overlaps with 持(も)ち家(いえ) but specifies the building type rather than ownership
  • マイホーム: "my home" (loanword) — almost synonymous with 持(も)ち家(いえ) in marketing copy, with a warmer, aspirational nuance

USAGE NOTES:

  • The reading is {もちいえ}, not {もちか} or {もちや}. Some older or regional usage reads it as {もちや}, but {もちいえ} is now standard in mainstream media.
  • In housing statistics, 持(も)ち家(いえ) typically encompasses both detached homes and condominiums owned outright.
  • For the broader concept of owning vs. renting in real estate, the common stock phrase is 持(も)ち家(いえ)か賃貸(ちんたい)か ("to own or to rent"), seen in countless magazine articles and online debates.
