部員(ぶいん)

ぶいん
noun
member (of a club, team, or organizational department); club/team member
1. member of a club, team, or department; especially a member of a school club ({部活|ぶかつ}) or a corporate department ({部|ぶ})
A person who belongs to a 部(ぶ) — typically a school club (部活動(ぶかつどう) or 部活(ぶかつ), such as a sports team, band, or hobby circle) or a department within a company or organization. 部員(ぶいん) is the everyday word students and employees use to refer to fellow members of their group. In school contexts, it most often refers to club members; in corporate contexts, members of a particular department.
テニス部(ぶ)の部員(ぶいん)。
A member of the tennis club.
部員(ぶいん)が足(た)りなくて試合(しあい)に出(で)られない。
We don't have enough club members to enter the tournament.
新入(しんにゅう)部員(ぶいん)歓迎会(かんげいかい)が放課後(ほうかご)に行(おこな)われた。
A welcome party for the new club members was held after school.
営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ)の部員(ぶいん)全員(ぜんいん)が今月(こんげつ)の売上(うりあげ)目標(もくひょう)を達成(たっせい)するために遅(おそ)くまで残業(ざんぎょう)している。
All the members of the sales department are working late to achieve this month's sales target.

部員(ぶいん) is the everyday word for a member of a 部(ぶ) — most commonly a school club or a corporate department. It is the standard term used by students, athletes, and office workers when referring to fellow members of their group.

FORMATION:

  • 部(ぶ) ("section, department, club") + 員(いん) ("member, staff") → 部員(ぶいん) "member of a 部"
  • Parallel forms: 会員(かいいん) ("society/association member"), 社員(しゃいん) ("company employee"), 店員(てんいん) ("shop staff"), 委員(いいん) ("committee member").

CONTEXTS OF USE:

  • School clubs (部活動(ぶかつどう)): The most common context. Most Japanese middle and high schools have a wide range of 部(ぶ) (sports teams, music ensembles, cultural clubs), and members are called 部員(ぶいん).
  • Corporate departments: A 営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ) (sales department), 人事(じんじ)部(ぶ) (HR department), or similar group is staffed by 部員(ぶいん).
  • Universities sometimes use サークル員(いん) or 部員(ぶいん) for circle members.

ROLE WORDS WITHIN A 部:

  • 部長(ぶちょう): club president / department head (the top person)
  • 副(ふく)部長(ぶちょう): vice-president / deputy department head
  • マネージャー(まねーじゃー): manager (in sports clubs)
  • 新入(しんにゅう)部員(ぶいん): new (incoming) member
  • 一年生(いちねんせい)/二年生(にねんせい)/三年生(さんねんせい)の部員(ぶいん): first-/second-/third-year members
  • OB / OG: alumni members (Old Boy / Old Girl)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部員(ぶいん)を募集(ぼしゅう)する: to recruit members
  • 部員(ぶいん)が集(あつ)まる: members gather
  • 部員(ぶいん)が減(へ)る / 増(ふ)える: members decrease / increase
  • 全(ぜん)部員(ぶいん): all members
  • 新入(しんにゅう)部員(ぶいん): new (incoming) member
  • 部員(ぶいん)不足(ぶそく): shortage of members
  • 部員(ぶいん)名簿(めいぼ): membership list / roster

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • メンバー: member (loanword) — broader and more casual; covers any group's members (clubs, projects, bands, sports teams)
  • 会員(かいいん): member of an association, society, or membership service (e.g., a gym, professional society)
  • 社員(しゃいん): company employee — broader than 部員(ぶいん), refers to the whole company's staff
  • 委員(いいん): committee member
  • 団員(だんいん): troupe / corps member (used for performing groups, scout troops, etc.)
  • 選手(せんしゅ): player, athlete — used for athletes in sports; a 部員(ぶいん) may or may not be a 選手(せんしゅ)
  • 部活(ぶかつ): club activities (the activity itself, not the people)
  • 部(ぶ): the club or department itself

USAGE NOTES:

  • In casual conversation, students often refer to fellow members as メンバー or 仲間 (なかま}); {部員(ぶいん) is the more neutral institutional term.
  • The plural is unmarked: 部員(ぶいん) can mean one member or many depending on context; 部員(ぶいん)たち makes plurality explicit.
  • Reading: ぶいん} (two morae); the {員(いん) component shows up in many group-membership words.
