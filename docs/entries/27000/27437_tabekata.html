食(た)べ方(かた)

たべかた
noun
way of eating; eating manner; how to eat
1. way of eating; how to eat; eating manner
The manner in which someone eats — whether referring to instructions for a particular food, table manners, or an individual's eating habits. Formed by attaching the suffix 方(かた) ('way of doing') to 食(た)べる.
食(た)べ方(かた)が汚(きたな)い。
Their way of eating is messy.
この料理(りょうり)の食(た)べ方(かた)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me how to eat this dish.
子(こ)どもには箸(はし)の正(ただ)しい食(た)べ方(かた)を早(はや)いうちから教(おし)えたい。
I want to teach my child the proper way to eat with chopsticks from an early age.

Formed by the productive pattern verb-stem + 方(かた) ('way of Xing'). Applies to both manner (table manners) and method (instructions for an unfamiliar food).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食(た)べ方(かた)が上手(じょうず)・下手(へた): skilled / clumsy at eating
  • 食(た)べ方(かた)が綺麗(きれい)・汚(きたな)い: neat / messy eating manner
  • 正(ただ)しい食(た)べ方(かた): the proper way to eat
  • 食(た)べ方(かた)を教(おし)える: to show how to eat

RELATED PATTERN:

  • 作(つく)り方(かた): how to make
  • 読(よ)み方(かた): how to read; reading (of a kanji)
  • 使(つか)い方(かた): how to use

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食事(しょくじ)の作法(さほう): dining etiquette — specifically formal manners
  • 食(く)い方(かた): rougher/casual variant — masculine, colloquial, can sound coarse
