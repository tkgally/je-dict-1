モーニング

もーにんぐ
noun
morning service; morning coat
1. morning service; breakfast set offered at cafes at a discount
A special breakfast deal offered by cafes and coffee shops, typically in the morning hours. The customer orders a drink and receives toast, eggs, or other breakfast items for free or at a reduced price.
喫茶店(きっさてん)でモーニングを食(た)べた。
I had a morning set at the coffee shop.
この店(みせ)のモーニングはコーヒー一杯(いっぱい)でトーストと卵(たまご)がつく。
This shop's morning set comes with toast and an egg for the price of one coffee.
名古屋(なごや)のモーニング文化(ぶんか)は全国的(ぜんこくてき)に有名(ゆうめい)で、小倉(おぐら)トーストなど独自(どくじ)のメニューがある。
Nagoya's morning service culture is famous nationwide, with unique menu items like ogura toast.
2. morning coat; cutaway coat; men's formal daywear
A formal men's coat with tails tapering from the front waist to the back knee, worn at weddings, funerals, and formal daytime ceremonies.
結婚式(けっこんしき)でモーニングを着(き)た。
I wore a morning coat at the wedding.
父(ちち)のモーニングを借(か)りて式(しき)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I borrowed my father's morning coat to attend the ceremony.
新郎(しんろう)の父親(ちちおや)はモーニングを着用(ちゃくよう)するのが一般的(いっぱんてき)だ。
It's customary for the groom's father to wear a morning coat.

From English "morning." In Japanese, モーニング has developed specialized meanings beyond just "morning." The breakfast set meaning (sense 1) is a distinctly Japanese usage — abbreviated from モーニングサービス (morning service). The formal coat meaning (sense 2) is short for モーニングコート.

CULTURAL NOTE:
The モーニング breakfast culture is especially strong in the Nagoya/Aichi region, where elaborate breakfast sets (sometimes including full meals) are served with a coffee order. 名古屋(なごや)モーニング is a recognized cultural phenomenon.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • モーニングを食(た)べる: to have a morning set
  • モーニングサービス: morning service (the full term)
  • モーニング営業(えいぎょう): morning hours business
  • モーニングを着(き)る: to wear a morning coat
  • モーニングコート: morning coat (the full term)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • モーニングサービス: morning service — the full form of sense 1
  • タキシード: tuxedo — formal evening wear, whereas モーニング is formal daywear
  • 礼服(れいふく): formal wear — the general category that includes モーニング
