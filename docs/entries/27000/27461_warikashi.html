わりかし

わりかし
adverb
fairly; rather; relatively (casual)
1. fairly; rather; relatively
Casual spoken variant of 割(わり)と / 割(わり)に, meaning 'fairly' or 'comparatively (more than expected).' Most common in informal conversation.
この店(みせ)、わりかし安(やす)いね。
This shop is fairly cheap, isn't it.
テストはわりかし簡単(かんたん)だった。
The test was relatively easy.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っていたけど、わりかし早(はや)く着(つ)いた。
It was raining, but I got there fairly quickly.

Casual conversational variant of 割(わり)と and 割(わり)に. The し ending gives it a colloquial, slightly old-fashioned or down-to-earth feel. Avoid in formal writing or business contexts.

USAGE:

  • Like 割(わり)と, it expresses 'more than I had expected' — comparing reality to a prior expectation.
  • Modifies adjectives, adverbs, and verbs: わりかし美味(おい)しい, わりかし早(はや)く, わりかし歩(ある)く

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 割(わり)と: relatively; fairly — standard form, usable in writing
  • 割(わり)に: relatively; fairly — close to 割(わり)と, slightly more formal feel
  • 結構(けっこう): quite; fairly — similar meaning, broader register
