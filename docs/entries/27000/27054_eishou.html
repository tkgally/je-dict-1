1. chanting; recitation; intoning
The act of reciting or chanting aloud, particularly poems, prayers, or incantations. Also widely used in anime and gaming contexts for spell casting.
僧侶(そうりょ)が経(きょう)を詠唱(えいしょう)している。
The monk is chanting a sutra.
詩(し)の詠唱(えいしょう)コンテストに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a poetry recitation contest.
そのゲームでは魔法(まほう)を使(つか)うために長(なが)い詠唱(えいしょう)が必要(ひつよう)で、途中(とちゅう)で妨害(ぼうがい)されると失敗(しっぱい)する。
In that game, using magic requires a long chant, and if interrupted midway, it fails.

A compound of 詠(えい) (to recite/compose poetry) and 唱(しょう) (to chant/recite). Originally referred to the recitation of poems or religious texts, but has gained widespread modern use in anime, light novels, and RPG games where characters chant incantations to cast spells.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 詠唱(えいしょう)する — to chant; to recite
  • 詠唱(えいしょう)破棄(はき) — chant cancellation (skipping the incantation, common in fantasy media)
  • 無詠唱(むえいしょう) — chantless (casting without incantation)
  • 詠唱(えいしょう)時間(じかん) — chanting time

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 朗読(ろうどく) — reading aloud; recitation (neutral, used for reading prose or poetry aloud)
  • 読経(どきょう) — sutra chanting (specifically Buddhist scripture recitation)
  • 唱(とな)える — to chant/recite (verb form; more general)
