妹(いもうと)さん

いもうとさん
noun
younger sister (polite — someone else's)
1. younger sister (polite — referring to someone else's)
Polite term for the listener's or a third party's younger sister. The honorific さん makes it appropriate for talking about others; one's own younger sister is 妹(いもうと) (without さん).
妹(いもうと)さんはお元気(げんき)ですか。
Is your younger sister well?
妹(いもうと)さんは今(いま)、大学生(だいがくせい)ですか。
Is your younger sister a university student now?
妹(いもうと)さんが結婚(けっこん)されると聞(き)いて、とても嬉(うれ)しく思(おも)いました。
I was very glad to hear that your younger sister is getting married.

Polite reference form for someone else's younger sister — 妹(いもうと) + the honorific suffix さん. Do not use for one's own younger sister when speaking to outsiders.

USAGE:

  • Used when talking about the listener's or a third party's younger sister
  • For one's own younger sister (to outsiders): 妹(いもうと)
  • Within the family, older siblings call a younger sister by name, not by 妹(いもうと)さん

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 妹(いもうと)さんはおいくつですか: How old is your younger sister?
  • 妹(いもうと)さんによろしく: Say hi to your younger sister
  • 妹(いもうと)さんとよく似(に)ている: looks a lot like your younger sister

RELATED WORDS:

  • 妹(いもうと): one's own younger sister (humble; for outsiders)
  • お姉(ねえ)さん: older sister (polite)
  • 弟(おとうと)さん: someone else's younger brother (parallel form)
