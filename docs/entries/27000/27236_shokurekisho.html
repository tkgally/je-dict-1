職歴書(しょくれきしょ)

しょくれきしょ
noun
work history document; resume; CV
1. work history document; resume; CV
A document listing one's employment history, job responsibilities, and professional experience. In Japanese job hunting, this is typically submitted alongside a 履歴書(りれきしょ) (personal history form).
職歴書(しょくれきしょ)を書(か)く。
To write a resume.
面接(めんせつ)に職歴書(しょくれきしょ)を持参(じさん)してください。
Please bring your resume to the interview.
転職(てんしょく)活動(かつどう)では、履歴書(りれきしょ)と職歴書(しょくれきしょ)の両方(りょうほう)を準備(じゅんび)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
When job hunting, you need to prepare both a personal history form and a work history document.
職歴書(しょくれきしょ)には、これまでの業務(ぎょうむ)内容(ないよう)と実績(じっせき)を具体的(ぐたいてき)に記載(きさい)する。
In a resume, you describe your past job duties and accomplishments in concrete terms.

Compound of 職歴(しょくれき) (work history) + 書(しょ) (document). The more formal, complete name is 職務(しょくむ)経歴書(けいれきしょ), which is the standard term used in Japanese job applications. 職歴書(しょくれきしょ) is the commonly shortened form.

In Japanese job hunting, applicants typically submit two documents: a 履歴書(りれきしょ) (standardized personal data form with photo, education, and basic work history) and a 職務(しょくむ)経歴書(けいれきしょ) (detailed description of professional experience and accomplishments).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 職歴書(しょくれきしょ)を作成(さくせい)する: to prepare a resume
  • 職歴書(しょくれきしょ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a resume
  • 職歴書(しょくれきしょ)の書(か)き方(かた): how to write a resume

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 履歴書(りれきしょ): personal history form — the standardized Japanese form with photo, education, and personal details
  • 職務(しょくむ)経歴書(けいれきしょ): detailed work history — the full formal name for this document
  • レジュメ: resume — loanword, sometimes used in international or casual contexts
