無味乾燥(むみかんそう)

むみかんそう
adjective
dull; dry; uninteresting
1. dull; dry; uninteresting; bland
Lacking any flavor, charm, or emotional appeal; tediously uninteresting.
無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な文章(ぶんしょう)。
Dull, dry writing.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)は無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な内容(ないよう)だった。
The report had dry, uninteresting content.
毎日(まいにち)同(おな)じことの繰(く)り返(かえ)しで、無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)っている。
Every day is the same thing repeated, and I'm living a dull, colorless life.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) meaning literally 'without flavor and dried out.' Used figuratively — never literally about food. Describes writing, speeches, daily life, or anything that lacks emotional richness or interest. Used as a na-adjective.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な生活(せいかつ): a dull, uneventful life
  • 無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な話(はなし): a dry, boring talk
  • 無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な説明(せつめい): a bland explanation
  • 無味乾燥(むみかんそう)な日々(ひび): tedious days

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • つまらない: boring; uninteresting — everyday word; broader in application
  • 退屈(たいくつ): boredom; tedium — focuses on the feeling of being bored rather than a quality of the thing itself
  • 味気(あじけ)ない: dull; flat; dreary — similar literary register; implies a lack of warmth or emotional nourishment
  • 単調(たんちょう): monotonous — emphasizes repetitiveness rather than lack of substance
