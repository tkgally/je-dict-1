通気(つうき)

つうき
noun
ventilation; air circulation; airflow (passive)
1. ventilation; air passage; air circulation
The (typically passive) flow of air through a space, garment, or piece of equipment. 通気(つうき) emphasizes that air can pass through — for example, a room where air moves freely, fabric that breathes, or vents in a building's structure. Less active than 換気(かんき), which refers to deliberately exchanging stale air for fresh air.
通気(つうき)が悪(わる)い。
The airflow is poor.
この部屋(へや)は通気(つうき)性(せい)が良(よ)い。
This room has good airflow.
夏用(なつよう)の服(ふく)は通気(つうき)性(せい)を重視(じゅうし)して選(えら)ぶといい。
It's good to choose summer clothes with breathability in mind.
床下(ゆかした)に通気(つうき)口(こう)を設(もう)けることで、湿気(しっけ)による建物(たてもの)の劣化(れっか)を防(ふせ)ぐことができる。
By providing ventilation openings under the floor, you can prevent damage to the building from humidity.

通気(つうき) describes air being able to flow through a space or material. The most common compound by far is 通気(つうき)性(せい) ("breathability, ventilation properties"), used heavily in product descriptions for clothing, footwear, building materials, bedding, and bags.

FORMATION:

  • 通(つう) ("to pass through") + 気(き) ("air") → 通気(つうき)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通気(つうき)性(せい): breathability, ventilation properties
  • 通気(つうき)口(こう): vent, ventilation opening
  • 通気(つうき)孔(こう): ventilation hole
  • 通気(つうき)が良(よ)い / 悪(わる)い: airflow is good / poor
  • 通気(つうき)管(かん): vent pipe (plumbing)
  • 通気(つうき)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure ventilation

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 換気(かんき): ventilation in the sense of actively exchanging stale air for fresh air (e.g., opening a window, running a ventilation fan); more deliberate and active than 通気(つうき)
  • 通風(つうふう): ventilation; airflow through a space (close to 通気(つうき), but 通風(つうふう) more strongly suggests a draft moving through)
  • 空調(くうちょう): HVAC (mechanical climate control system)
  • 湿気(しっけ): humidity, moisture
  • 蒸(む)れる: to become stuffy, to get sweaty (used of clothes/spaces lacking 通気(つうき)性(せい))

USAGE NOTES:

  • 通気(つうき) is more about whether air can pass through than about anyone actively making it pass. A loose-weave shirt has good 通気(つうき)性(せい); opening a window for fresh air is 換気(かんき).
  • In product copy, 通気(つうき)性(せい) is a major selling point for sportswear, work boots, summer suits, mattresses, and even smartphone cases — anywhere stale or moist air would cause discomfort or damage.
  • For Japanese houses with raised wooden floors, 床下(ゆかした)通気(つうき) ("under-floor ventilation") is critical to prevent rot from humidity, and is a standard feature of building specifications.
