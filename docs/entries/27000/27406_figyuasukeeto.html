フィギュアスケート

ふぃぎゅあすけーと
noun
figure skating (the competitive winter sport in which skaters perform jumps, spins, and step sequences on ice to music)
1. figure skating — a winter sport in which skaters perform jumps, spins, and step sequences on ice, scored on technical execution and artistic presentation
A competitive winter sport in which skaters perform a choreographed routine on ice, combining jumps, spins, step sequences, and lifts. Scoring is based on both technical execution and artistic presentation. In Japan, フィギュアスケート has been an extremely popular sport since the 2000s and 2010s, with stars like 羽生(はにゅう)結弦(ゆづる) (Yuzuru Hanyu) and 浅田(あさだ)真央(まお) (Mao Asada) drawing huge television audiences. Competitive events include singles, pairs, ice dance, and synchronized skating.

フィギュアスケートの試合(しあい)では、ジャンプの技術(ぎじゅつ)点(てん)と演技(えんぎ)構成(こうせい)点(てん)の合計(ごうけい)で順位(じゅんい)が決(き)まる。
In a figure skating competition, the final ranking is determined by the total of the technical score for jumps and the program-components score for the performance.

フィギュアスケート is the standard Japanese term for figure skating. Originally a borrowing from English, it has been thoroughly naturalized and is the term used in sports broadcasting, journalism, and everyday speech.

ABBREVIATION:

  • Often shortened to フィギュア in headlines and conversation when context is clear: "フィギュアの試合(しあい)" ("a figure-skating competition")
  • Caution: フィギュア by itself can also mean "figurine" (model figures of anime characters, etc.) — context disambiguates.

CATEGORIES:

  • 男子(だんし)(シングル(しんぐる)): men's singles
  • 女子(じょし)(シングル(しんぐる)): women's / ladies' singles
  • ペア: pairs (one man + one woman, with lifts)
  • アイスダンス: ice dance (no lifts above the head; emphasis on dance and footwork)
  • シンクロナイズドスケーティング: synchronized skating (team event)
  • ジュニア / シニア: junior / senior age divisions

KEY JUMPS (often referenced in Japanese sports media):

  • アクセル: Axel jump (e.g., トリプルアクセル "triple Axel")
  • ルッツ: Lutz
  • フリップ: Flip
  • ループ: Loop
  • サルコウ: Salchow
  • トウループ: Toe loop
  • 4回転(かいてん)ジャンプ / クワド: quadruple jump

MAJOR COMPETITIONS:

  • 冬季(とうき)オリンピック: Winter Olympics
  • 世界(せかい)選手権(せんしゅけん): World Figure Skating Championships
  • グランプリ(シリーズ(しりーず)): Grand Prix series
  • グランプリファイナル: Grand Prix Final
  • 全日本(ぜんにほん)選手権(せんしゅけん): Japanese Nationals

FAMOUS JAPANESE SKATERS:

  • 羽生(はにゅう)結弦(ゆづる): Yuzuru Hanyu — two-time Olympic gold medalist (2014, 2018)
  • 浅田(あさだ)真央(まお): Mao Asada — three-time World Champion
  • 高橋(たかはし)大輔(だいすけ): Daisuke Takahashi
  • 宇野(うの)昌磨(しょうま): Shoma Uno
  • 紀平(きひら)梨花(りか): Rika Kihira

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フィギュアスケートの選手(せんしゅ): a figure skater
  • フィギュアスケートを習(なら)う: to learn figure skating
  • フィギュアスケートを見(み)る: to watch figure skating
  • フィギュアスケートの大会(たいかい): a figure-skating competition
  • 女子(じょし)フィギュア / 男子(だんし)フィギュア: women's / men's figure skating
  • フィギュアスケート連盟(れんめい): figure-skating federation
  • ショートプログラム / フリープログラム: short program / free skate

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • スケート: skating (general, including speed and figure)
  • スピードスケート: speed skating
  • 氷上(ひょうじょう)競技(きょうぎ): ice sports
  • アイスホッケー: ice hockey
  • リンク: ice rink
  • フィギュア: figurine (also short for fig. skating) — homograph
  • 演技(えんぎ): performance — used in figure skating to refer to a skater's program

USAGE NOTES:

  • Frequently abbreviated as フィギュア in casual speech: "今夜(こんや)フィギュアやるよね?" ("There's figure skating on tonight, right?")
  • Originally the English term "figure skating" referred to skaters tracing geometric figures on the ice (the 規定(きてい)演技(えんぎ) "compulsory figures" phase, removed from competition in the early 1990s); the name remained.
  • The katakana spelling has a small ィ in フィ and a small ュ in ギュ; the long vowel ー appears in スケート.
