行(い)き当(あ)たりばったり

いきあたりばったり
noun
haphazard; unplanned; playing it by ear
1. haphazard; unplanned; playing it by ear; random; improvised
Acting without a plan or preparation, just dealing with things as they come. Can describe either a carefree attitude or reckless disorganization.
行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりの旅(たび)が好(す)きだ。
I like unplanned trips.
計画(けいかく)を立(た)てず、行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりで行動(こうどう)した。
I acted without making a plan, just playing it by ear.
行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりの経営(けいえい)では、いずれ失敗(しっぱい)する。
Haphazard management will eventually lead to failure.

Can carry either a positive or negative nuance depending on context. When describing travel or leisure, it suggests a carefree, adventurous spirit. In work or business contexts, it implies irresponsible lack of planning. Functions as a noun modified by の, or as an adverbial modifier with で.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 行(い)き当(あ)たる (to come across by chance) + ばったり (suddenly encountering). The doubling creates an emphatic sense of doing whatever one happens upon.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりの旅(たび): an unplanned trip
  • 行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりの生活(せいかつ): an aimless lifestyle
  • 行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりで決(き)める: to decide on the fly
  • 行(い)き当(あ)たりばったりに行動(こうどう)する: to act haphazardly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出(で)たとこ勝負(しょうぶ): playing it by ear; taking one's chances — similar meaning but focuses more on the element of risk
  • 無計画(むけいかく): unplanned; without a plan — a more neutral, descriptive term
  • 場当(ばあ)たり: ad hoc; improvised — often negative, implying stopgap measures without thinking ahead
