狙(ねら)い目(め)

ねらいめ
noun
good opportunity; sweet spot; best bet
1. good opportunity; sweet spot; best bet
The most advantageous timing, target, or choice in a given situation. Compound of 狙(ねら)い (aim) + 目(め) (point/spot), often used in advice about shopping, travel, job hunting, and investment.
平日(へいじつ)の昼間(ひるま)が狙(ねら)い目(め)だ。
Weekday afternoons are the sweet spot.
セール初日(しょにち)の朝(あさ)が一番(いちばん)の狙(ねら)い目(め)だ。
The morning of the first day of the sale is the best bet.
中堅(ちゅうけん)企業(きぎょう)は大手(おおて)より採用(さいよう)されやすく、就活(しゅうかつ)の狙(ねら)い目(め)と言(い)える。
Mid-sized companies are easier to get hired by than major firms, so they can be called a sweet spot for job hunting.

Compound of 狙(ねら)い (aim, target) + 目(め) (spot, focal point). Common in journalism, travel guides, and casual advice for pointing out the smartest choice in a given context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)は〜だ: the sweet spot is ~
  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)の時期(じき): the best time to act
  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)の店(みせ): a shop that's a good bet
  • 一番(いちばん)の狙(ねら)い目(め): the best opportunity

USAGE:
Usually appears as 〜が狙(ねら)い目(め)だ or 〜の狙(ねら)い目(め), recommending a specific option. Rarely modified with adjectives.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • チャンス: chance, opportunity — broader; emphasizes the moment itself
  • 好機(こうき): good opportunity — formal, focuses on a timely moment
  • 穴場(あなば): hidden gem; little-known good spot — emphasizes obscurity, often about places
