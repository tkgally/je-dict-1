だけれど

だけれど
conjunction
but; although; however
1. but; although; however (slightly more formal than だけど)
A conjunction used to introduce a statement that contrasts with or qualifies what was just said. Sits between casual だけど and formal だけれども on the formality scale. Common in everyday writing such as essays, blog posts, and messages where the writer wants a slightly more careful tone than だけど without going as far as the literary しかし or だけれども.
欲(ほ)しい。だけれど、高(たか)い。
I want it. But it's expensive.
彼(かれ)は若(わか)い。だけれど、とても経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ)だ。
He's young. However, he is very experienced.
この本(ほん)は難(むずか)しいだけれど、読(よ)むと勉強(べんきょう)になる。
This book is difficult, but reading it is good for your studies.
今回(こんかい)のプロジェクトは予算(よさん)も時間(じかん)も限(かぎ)られているだけれど、チーム全員(ぜんいん)で協力(きょうりょく)すれば何(なん)とかなると思(おも)う。
We're short on both budget and time on this project, but I think we can manage somehow if the whole team pulls together.

だけれど is a conjunction used to express contrast, sitting between casual だけど and formal だけれども in register. Native speakers often choose it for blog posts, essays, and casual but considered writing where they want a softer, slightly more careful tone than だけど without sounding overly formal.

FORMALITY VARIANTS (most casual → most formal):

  • だけど: casual
  • だけども: casual, slightly more emphatic than だけど
  • だけれど: slightly more formal than だけど
  • だけれども: full, formal form
  • ですけれど: polite
  • ですけれども: polite formal

USAGE:

  • Often appears at the start of a sentence after a period: "…。だけれど、…" ("…. However, …").
  • Can also trail at the end of a clause to soften a statement: 〜のだけれど、… ("… though, …").

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • だけど: but (more casual; the everyday workhorse)
  • だけれども: but (the full form; slightly more formal still)
  • けれど: but (attaches to verb/adjective stems)
  • けれども: but (more formal version of けれど)
  • でも: but (general-purpose casual conjunction; can begin a sentence)
  • しかし: however (more formal, written)

USAGE NOTES:

  • The shorter form だけど and the longer form だけれども are both more frequent in raw speech; だけれど is comparatively most at home in writing and considered conversation.
  • Like its siblings, だけれど does not stand alone — it always introduces or links a clause, never appearing as a one-word answer.
