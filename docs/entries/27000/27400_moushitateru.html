1. to state; to assert; to lodge (a complaint, objection); to file
To formally state or put forward something — an opinion, complaint, objection, or claim — to an authority or in an official setting. Compound of 申(もう)す (humble "to say") + 立(た)てる (to put forward). Used in legal, administrative, and business contexts.
異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる。
To raise an objection.
被告人(ひこくにん)は無罪(むざい)を申(もう)し立(た)てた。
The defendant pleaded not guilty.
判決(はんけつ)に不服(ふふく)があれば、二週間以内(にしゅうかんいない)に控訴(こうそ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる必要(ひつよう)がある。
If you are dissatisfied with the judgment, you need to lodge an appeal within two weeks.

Compound of 申(もう)す (humble form of "to say") + 立(た)てる ("to set up, put forward"). The verb has a formal, often legal or administrative tone — not used for casual statements.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. The thing being asserted takes を: 意見(いけん)を申(もう)し立(た)てる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 異議(いぎ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to raise an objection
  • 不服(ふふく)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to file a complaint of dissatisfaction
  • 無罪(むざい)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to plead not guilty
  • 権利(けんり)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to assert a right
  • 控訴(こうそ)を申(もう)し立(た)てる: to lodge an appeal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主張(しゅちょう)する: to assert; to claim — broader, everyday register
  • 申(もう)し出(で)る: to come forward to say; to offer/propose — more for volunteering information or requests; less adversarial
