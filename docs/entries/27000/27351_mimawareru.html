1. to be struck by; to be afflicted with (a disaster, misfortune, illness, or unwelcome event)
The passive form of 見舞(みま)う ("to inquire after, to visit a sick person; to befall"). In its most common modern use, 見舞(みま)われる means to suffer or be hit by something unpleasant — typhoons, earthquakes, accidents, illness, financial troubles. Almost always used with {に} marking the misfortune.
台風(たいふう)に見舞(みま)われた。
We were hit by a typhoon.
その町(まち)は大雨(おおあめ)による洪水(こうずい)に見舞(みま)われた。
The town was struck by flooding from heavy rain.
帰国(きこく)の途中(とちゅう)、思(おも)わぬトラブルに見舞(みま)われた。
On my way home, I ran into unexpected trouble.
地震(じしん)と津波(つなみ)という二(ふた)つの災害(さいがい)に見舞(みま)われ、復興(ふっこう)には長(なが)い時間(じかん)がかかった。
Struck by the dual disasters of an earthquake and tsunami, the recovery took a long time.

見舞(みま)われる is the passive form of 見舞(みま)う and is by far the more common form in everyday and journalistic Japanese when describing being hit by misfortune. The pattern is essentially fixed: [victim/place]が[misfortune]に見舞(みま)われる.

GRAMMATICAL PATTERN:

  • ～が～に見舞(みま)われる: [subject] is struck by [misfortune]
  • 町(まち)が台風(たいふう)に見舞(みま)われた: "The town was struck by a typhoon."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 台風(たいふう)に見舞(みま)われる: to be hit by a typhoon
  • 地震(じしん)に見舞(みま)われる: to be struck by an earthquake
  • 病(やまい)に見舞(みま)われる: to fall ill (literary)
  • 不幸(ふこう)に見舞(みま)われる: to be visited by misfortune
  • トラブルに見舞(みま)われる: to run into trouble
  • 不景気(ふけいき)に見舞(みま)われる: to be hit by an economic downturn
  • 寒波(かんぱ)に見舞(みま)われる: to be hit by a cold snap

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 遭(あ)う: to encounter, to meet with (a misfortune); more colloquial. 事故(じこ)に遭(あ)う ("to be in an accident")
  • 襲(おそ)われる: to be attacked, to be assailed; stronger, often used for both attacks and sudden physical sensations (不安(ふあん)に襲(おそ)われる "to be overcome with anxiety")
  • 被(こうむ)る: to suffer (formal/literary; takes the misfortune as direct object: 被害(ひがい)を被(こうむ)る)

USAGE NOTES:

  • 見舞(みま)う itself originally meant "to inquire after, to visit (a sick person)," and that older sense survives in お{見舞(みま)い} ("a sympathy visit / get-well gift"). The modern "to befall" sense was extended from there: misfortune "visits" the victim.
  • The active form 見舞(みま)う in the "to befall" sense (e.g., 台風(たいふう)が町(まち)を見舞(みま)った) is heard mainly in literary or news writing; the passive 見舞(みま)われる is far more common in speech.
  • Almost always negative in connotation. To say someone was "visited" by a positive event, use 訪(おとず)れる or 恵(めぐ)まれる instead.
