ぞくぞく

ぞくぞく
adverb
shivering; thrilled; excited (mimetic)
1. shivering with cold; getting chills
Mimetic expression for a chilly, shivery sensation along the spine — typically from cold air or the early stages of a fever.
背中(せなか)がぞくぞくする。
I'm getting chills down my back.
風邪(かぜ)のせいかぞくぞくして眠(ねむ)れない。
I can't sleep because I'm shivering, probably from a cold.
夜中(よなか)に窓(まど)を開(あ)けたら、冷(つめ)たい風(かぜ)でぞくぞくと寒気(さむけ)がしてきた。
When I opened the window at night, the cold air gave me a shivering chill.
2. thrilled; tingling with excitement or fear
Used for an intense, tingling feeling of excitement, anticipation, or fear — the figurative 'shivers' down one's spine.
怖(こわ)い映画(えいが)を見(み)てぞくぞくした。
Watching the scary movie gave me thrills.
試合(しあい)の結果(けっか)を待(ま)つ間(あいだ)、ぞくぞくする気持(きも)ちが止(と)まらなかった。
While waiting for the match result, I couldn't stop feeling tingling excitement.
初(はじ)めて大舞台(おおぶたい)に立(た)つと思(おも)うと、ぞくぞくするような緊張感(きんちょうかん)があった。
Thinking that I'd stand on a big stage for the first time, I felt a thrilling tension.

Mimetic (giseigo) word describing a tingling, shivery sensation. Almost always used with する.

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 is physical: cold, fever, sudden chill
  • Sense 2 is emotional: thrill, anticipation, dread
  • Context usually makes the sense clear; with 寒気(さむけ)/風邪(かぜ)/冷(つめ)たい it is sense 1

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 背中(せなか)がぞくぞくする: to feel chills down one's back
  • ぞくぞくする思(おも)い: a thrilling feeling
  • ぞくぞくと鳥肌(とりはだ)が立(た)つ: to get tingling goosebumps

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぶるぶる: trembling; shaking — visible physical shaking, not internal tingling
  • わくわく: thrilled with anticipation — positive only; less intense than sense-2 ぞくぞく
  • どきどき: heart pounding — focus on the heartbeat, not the spine
