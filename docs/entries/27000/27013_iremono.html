入(い)れ物(もの)

いれもの
noun
container; receptacle
1. container; receptacle; vessel
A general term for any object used to hold or store things.
入(い)れ物(もの)がない。
There's no container.
この入(い)れ物(もの)に水(みず)を入(い)れてください。
Please put water in this container.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に入(い)れる前(まえ)に、ふたのある入(い)れ物(もの)に移(うつ)してください。
Before putting it in the fridge, please transfer it to a container with a lid.

A broad, everyday word for anything that serves as a container — boxes, jars, bags, bowls, cases, etc. Unlike more specific terms, it does not imply a particular material, shape, or purpose.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入(い)れ物(もの)に入(い)れる: to put into a container
  • 入(い)れ物(もの)を探(さが)す: to look for a container
  • 大(おお)きい入(い)れ物(もの): a large container
  • ふたつきの入(い)れ物(もの): a container with a lid

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 容器(ようき): container — more formal and technical; often used for food packaging and lab equipment
  • 器(うつわ): vessel; dish — implies a dish or bowl shape; also used figuratively for a person's capacity
  • 箱(はこ): box — specifically a box-shaped container
