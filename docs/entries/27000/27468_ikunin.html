幾人(いくにん)

いくにん
noun
how many people; several people
1. how many people; several people
Asks or refers to an unspecified number of people. Slightly formal or literary; in casual speech, 何人(なんにん) is far more common.
幾人(いくにん)もの学者(がくしゃ)がそう述(の)べている。
Many scholars have stated so.
幾人(いくにん)かの友人(ゆうじん)に相談(そうだん)した。
I consulted with several friends.
彼(かれ)の論文(ろんぶん)は、これまで(これまで)幾人(いくにん)もの研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)に引用(いんよう)されてきた。
His paper has been cited by many researchers up to now.

Compound of 幾(いく) (how many; some) + 人(にん) (people). Most often appears in 幾人(いくにん)も (many people) and 幾人(いくにん)か (several people). Has a literary feel; use 何人(なんにん) in casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幾人(いくにん)も: many people; a great number of people
  • 幾人(いくにん)か: several people; a few people
  • 幾人(いくにん)もの〜: many ~ (with a following noun phrase)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 何人(なんにん): how many people — everyday standard form
  • 数人(すうにん): several people — neutral, common in writing
  • 大勢(おおぜい): a crowd; many people
