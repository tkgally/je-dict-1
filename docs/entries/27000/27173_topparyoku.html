突破力(とっぱりょく)

とっぱりょく
noun
breakthrough power; ability to break through
1. breakthrough power; ability to break through obstacles
The force, ability, or drive needed to overcome barriers, whether physical, strategic, or figurative.
あのフォワードは突破力(とっぱりょく)がある。
That forward has great breakthrough ability.
現状(げんじょう)を変(か)えるには突破力(とっぱりょく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need breakthrough power to change the status quo.
このチーム(ちーむ)には個(こ)の突破力(とっぱりょく)で局面(きょくめん)を打開(だかい)できる選手(せんしゅ)がいない。
This team has no player who can break open the game with individual breakthrough ability.

Combines 突破(とっぱ) (breakthrough) with 力(りょく) (power; ability). Used both literally in sports and military contexts, and figuratively in business and personal development contexts to describe the ability to overcome difficult situations or barriers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 突破力(とっぱりょく)がある: to have breakthrough ability
  • 個人(こじん)の突破力(とっぱりょく): individual breakthrough ability
  • 突破力(とっぱりょく)に欠(か)ける: to lack breakthrough ability
  • 突破力(とっぱりょく)を発揮(はっき)する: to demonstrate breakthrough ability

USAGE:
Particularly common in sports commentary (especially soccer, rugby, and basketball) to describe a player's ability to dribble past defenders. Also used in business to describe leaders or teams that can push through organizational resistance or market barriers.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推進力(すいしんりょく): driving force — emphasizes sustained forward momentum rather than breaking through specific barriers
  • 打開力(だかいりょく): ability to break through a deadlock — focuses specifically on resolving stalemate situations
  • 突破(とっぱ): breakthrough — the base noun without the emphasis on ability or capacity
