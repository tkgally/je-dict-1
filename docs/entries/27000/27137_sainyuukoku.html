1. re-entry (into a country); returning to a country after leaving
Used in immigration and visa contexts, referring to the process of entering a country again after having left.
再入国(さいにゅうこく)許可(きょか)を取(と)る必要(ひつよう)がある。
You need to obtain a re-entry permit.
再入国(さいにゅうこく)手続(てつづ)きは空港(くうこう)で行(おこな)えます。
Re-entry procedures can be done at the airport.
ビザの種類(しゅるい)によって再入国(さいにゅうこく)の条件(じょうけん)が異(こと)なる。
Re-entry conditions differ depending on the type of visa.

Combines 再(さい) (again) and 入国(にゅうこく) (entering a country). An important term for foreign residents of Japan who need to leave and return. Japan's immigration system distinguishes between 再入国許可(さいにゅうこくきょか) (re-entry permit) and the newer みなし再入国(さいにゅうこく)許可(きょか) (deemed re-entry permit) introduced in 2012.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再入国(さいにゅうこく)許可(きょか): re-entry permit
  • 再入国(さいにゅうこく)手続(てつづ)き: re-entry procedures
  • 再入国(さいにゅうこく)ビザ: re-entry visa
  • みなし再入国(さいにゅうこく): deemed re-entry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 入国(にゅうこく): entering a country — the initial entry
  • 出国(しゅっこく): leaving a country — departing a country
  • 帰国(きこく): returning to one's home country — returning home specifically
