閣下(かっか)

かっか
noun
Your Excellency; His/Her Excellency
1. Your Excellency; His/Her Excellency
An honorific title used to address or refer to high-ranking officials such as heads of state, ambassadors, and ministers. Used in diplomatic and extremely formal contexts.
大使(たいし)閣下(かっか)にお会(あ)いする。
To meet His Excellency the Ambassador.
大統領(だいとうりょう)閣下(かっか)、ようこそ。
Welcome, Mr. President.
首相(しゅしょう)閣下(かっか)は式典(しきてん)でスピーチを行(おこな)った。
His Excellency the Prime Minister gave a speech at the ceremony.

A highly formal honorific (尊敬(そんけい)) title placed after a person's rank or title, never after a personal name alone. Used primarily in diplomatic contexts and official ceremonies. In modern Japan, it is rarely used in everyday conversation but appears frequently in news reports about foreign dignitaries and in historical or period dramas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大使(たいし)閣下(かっか) — His/Her Excellency the Ambassador
  • 大統領(だいとうりょう)閣下(かっか) — His/Her Excellency the President
  • 首相(しゅしょう)閣下(かっか) — His/Her Excellency the Prime Minister
  • 閣下(かっか)に申(もう)し上(あ)げる — to address His/Her Excellency

USAGE:

  • Follows the title, not the name: 大使(たいし)閣下(かっか) (correct), not ×スミス閣下(かっか)
  • In anime and manga, sometimes used humorously or satirically for characters with authority

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 陛下(へいか) — Your/His/Her Majesty; used exclusively for emperors and monarchs
  • 殿下(でんか) — Your/His/Her Highness; used for princes, princesses, and members of royal families
