くさかんむり

くさかんむり
noun
the "grass crown" radical (艹); top kanji radical for plants
1. the kanji radical {艹|くさかんむり} ("grass crown"), placed above the rest of a character
The 艹(くさかんむり) radical is a stylized form of 草(くさ) ("grass"), placed across the top (冠(かんむり)) of many kanji. It indicates a connection with grasses, plants, or vegetation. Examples include 花(はな) ("flower"), 草(くさ) ("grass"), 茶(ちゃ) ("tea"), and 葉(は) ("leaf").
「花(はな)」はくさかんむりだ。
"{花|はな}" has the grass-crown radical.
くさかんむりの漢字(かんじ)は植物(しょくぶつ)に関係(かんけい)がある。
Kanji with the grass-crown radical are related to plants.
「茶(ちゃ)」も「薬(くすり)」もくさかんむりが付(つ)いている。
Both "{茶|ちゃ}" and "{薬|くすり}" have the grass-crown radical.
先生(せんせい)は「くさかんむりが上(うえ)にあれば、たいてい植物(しょくぶつ)と関係(かんけい)があると覚(おぼ)えていい」と言(い)った。
The teacher said, "You can remember that if there's a grass-crown radical on top, it's usually related to plants."

くさかんむり is the name of the kanji radical 艹(くさかんむり), which is a stylized form of 草(くさ) ("grass") placed across the top of many kanji. Most kanji that carry くさかんむり have meanings tied to plants, herbs, flowers, or other vegetation, making it a useful clue for guessing meaning when learning new kanji.

FORMATION:

  • 草(くさ) ("grass") + ~かんむり (冠(かんむり), "top-of-character radical")

COMMON KANJI WITH くさかんむり:

  • 花(はな): flower
  • 草(くさ): grass
  • 葉(は): leaf
  • 茶(ちゃ): tea
  • 薬(くすり): medicine (originally derived from medicinal herbs)
  • 菜(さい): vegetable / greens
  • 若(わか)い: young (originally a plant-shoot image)
  • 苦(くる)しい: painful (using 古(こ) phonetic + 艹(くさかんむり))

RELATED RADICAL TERMS:

  • 冠(かんむり): top-of-character radical (the position name; also うかんむり 宀(うかんむり), あめかんむり 雨(あめ), etc.)
  • てへん: "hand" left-side radical (扌(てへん))
  • さんずい: "three drops of water" left-side radical (氵(さんずい))
  • きへん: "tree" left-side radical (木(き))
  • 部首(ぶしゅ): kanji radical (the general term)

USAGE NOTES:

  • The shape of くさかんむり is now usually written with three strokes (艹(くさかんむり)) in modern textbooks, although older forms wrote it with four strokes (艸(くさかんむり) / ⺿). Both are recognized as the same radical.
  • A common mnemonic for learners is to remember the meaning "plant" whenever they see くさかんむり on top of an unfamiliar kanji.
