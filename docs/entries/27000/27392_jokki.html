ジョッキ

じょっき
noun
beer mug; stein; tankard
1. beer mug; stein; tankard
A large glass or ceramic mug with a handle, used primarily for serving draft beer. Standard sizes in izakaya are 中(ちゅう)ジョッキ (medium, ~435 ml) and 大(だい)ジョッキ (large, ~700 ml or more).
ビールをジョッキで頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered the beer in a mug.
中(ちゅう)ジョッキを二(ふた)つください。
Two medium mugs of beer, please.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)に冷(ひ)えたジョッキで飲(の)むビールは格別(かくべつ)だ。
Beer drunk from a chilled mug on a hot day is something special.

From English "jug," reshaped in Japanese pronunciation. Refers specifically to a beer mug with a handle, not a pitcher. At izakaya, ジョッキ is the default vessel for draft beer (生(なま)ビール).

COMMON SIZES AND COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中(ちゅう)ジョッキ: medium mug (~435 ml; the standard size)
  • 大(だい)ジョッキ: large mug (~700 ml and up)
  • ジョッキビール: beer served in a mug
  • ジョッキで乾杯(かんぱい): to toast with mugs
  • 冷(ひ)えたジョッキ: a chilled mug (kept in the freezer)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • グラス: glass — generic term for a drinking glass
  • ピッチャー: pitcher — used for sharing; not a personal vessel
