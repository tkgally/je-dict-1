1. cloth — a piece of fabric used as a cover or wiping material, especially a tablecloth or wallpaper-class interior wall covering
Loanword from English "cloth." In everyday Japanese, this sense most often appears in compounds: テーブルクロス (tablecloth), 壁(かべ)クロス (wall covering / wallpaper-style fabric), 貼(は)りクロス (the same in interior-design contexts). It can also stand alone for a small cloth used to wipe glasses, lenses, or screens (メガネクロス, クリーニングクロス). The wallpaper sense is industry standard and broader than English "wallpaper" — it includes vinyl-faced and woven wall coverings.
テーブルにクロスを敷(し)く。
To put a cloth on the table.
メガネを専用(せんよう)のクロスで拭(ふ)いた。
I wiped my glasses with the dedicated cleaning cloth.
部屋(へや)の壁(かべ)クロスを白(しろ)から薄(うす)いベージュに張(は)り替(か)えた。
I redid the wall covering in the room from white to a pale beige.
2. cross — an X-shape, a crossing, a Christian cross, or a cross pass / cross punch in sports
Loanword from English "cross." Used for various senses: an X-shape or crossing pattern (クロスステッチ "cross stitch," クロスワード "crossword"); the Christian symbol (mostly in compounds like レッドクロス "Red Cross," though the religious cross is more often 十字架(じゅうじか) in standalone use); intersections and overlaps of lines or paths; and especially in sports — a cross pass in soccer, a cross punch in boxing. This sense is also productive as a verb-suru (クロスする) meaning to cross (one another) or to intersect.
二(ふた)つの線(せん)がクロスする。
The two lines cross.
サイドからクロスが上(あ)がる。
A cross comes in from the side. (soccer commentary)
ペナルティーエリアの中(なか)に鋭(するど)いクロスが送(おく)られたが、相手(あいて)ディフェンダーにクリアされた。
A sharp cross was sent into the penalty area, but the opposing defender cleared it.

クロス is a polysemous loanword that derives from two different English words — "cloth" and "cross" — both pronounced and written the same way in Japanese. Context disambiguates them clearly, and the two senses rarely appear in the same domain.

WHICH ENGLISH SOURCE WORD?

  • Sense 1 (fabric / cloth): from English "cloth" — used in interior design, household, optical, and electronics contexts
  • Sense 2 (cross / intersection / X-shape): from English "cross" — used in design, religion, sports, and as a verb-suru

COMMON COMPOUNDS — SENSE 1 (CLOTH):

  • テーブルクロス: tablecloth
  • 壁(かべ)クロス: wall covering / wallpaper (interior design industry standard term)
  • 貼(は)りクロス: applied wall covering (same domain)
  • メガネクロス: lens-cleaning cloth (for glasses)
  • クリーニングクロス: cleaning cloth (for screens, lenses, etc.)
  • マイクロファイバークロス: microfiber cloth
  • ティーマットクロス: tea-mat cloth

COMMON COMPOUNDS — SENSE 2 (CROSS):

  • クロスワード: crossword (puzzle)
  • クロスステッチ: cross stitch (embroidery)
  • クロスカントリー: cross-country (skiing or running)
  • クロスバイク: cross bike (a hybrid bicycle between road and mountain bike)
  • クロスゲーム: a closely contested game (sports)
  • クロスカウンター: cross counter (boxing — countering with a cross punch)
  • クロスファイア: crossfire
  • クロス集計(しゅうけい): cross tabulation (statistics)
  • クロスチェック: cross-check (verification)
  • レッドクロス: Red Cross (more often 赤十字(せきじゅうじ))

COMMON COLLOCATIONS — SENSE 1:

  • クロスを敷(し)く: to lay a cloth (e.g., on a table)
  • クロスを掛(か)ける: to drape a cloth
  • クロスで拭(ふ)く: to wipe with a cloth
  • クロスを張(は)り替(か)える: to redo / replace wall covering

COMMON COLLOCATIONS — SENSE 2:

  • クロスする: to cross / to intersect (verb-suru use)
  • クロスを上(あ)げる: to send in a cross (soccer)
  • 鋭(するど)いクロス: a sharp cross (soccer)
  • クロスを合(あ)わせる: to meet a cross (with a header etc., soccer)
  • クロスに 蹴(け)る: to kick crossways
  • 視線(しせん)がクロスする: gazes meet / lines of sight cross

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS — SENSE 1:

  • 布(ぬの): cloth, fabric (the native Japanese word)
  • 布巾(ふきん): dishcloth (kitchen)
  • 雑巾(ぞうきん): cleaning rag (more utilitarian than クロス)
  • 壁紙(かべがみ): wallpaper (everyday term; 壁(かべ)クロス is the trade-industry term that includes more material types)
  • ナフキン / ナプキン: napkin

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS — SENSE 2:

  • 十字(じゅうじ): cross-shape (the native term)
  • 十字架(じゅうじか): a Christian cross (the religious symbol)
  • 交差(こうさ): crossing, intersection
  • 交差点(こうさてん): intersection (of roads)
  • センタリング: centering pass (older soccer term, largely replaced by クロス)
  • パス: pass (general)

USAGE NOTES:

  • The two senses derive from different English source words and overlap only in spelling/pronunciation in Japanese — context always makes the meaning clear
  • Sense 1 (cloth) is mostly used in compounds; standalone クロス for "cloth" usually refers to a small wiping cloth
  • Sense 2 (cross) is also productive as a verb-suru (クロスする) meaning to intersect; the noun is common in sports and design vocabulary
  • The spelling does NOT distinguish the two — context, domain, and surrounding compounds disambiguate
  • For the food/grain "cross" (a religious cross used in baking, e.g., hot cross buns), Japanese tends to spell out the full 十字(じゅうじ) or borrow the full English compound name
