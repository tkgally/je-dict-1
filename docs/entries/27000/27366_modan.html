モダン

もだん
adjective-na
modern; stylish; contemporary (in design and aesthetic sense)
1. modern; stylish; contemporary
Of a stylish, contemporary aesthetic — clean lines, fashionable, up-to-date in look and feel. モダン applies most often to design, architecture, fashion, interiors, and art. Unlike English "modern," which can mean "of the present era" in general, モダン in Japanese is almost always evaluative — implying a sleek, refined, or cosmopolitan style rather than simply "of the current time." For the temporal sense, Japanese uses 現代的(げんだいてき) or 現代(げんだい).
モダンな家具(かぐ)。
Modern furniture.
部屋(へや)をモダンに仕上(しあ)げた。
I gave the room a modern finish.
古(ふる)い町並(まちな)みに建(た)つモダンなカフェ。
A stylish modern café standing in a historic streetscape.
和風(わふう)とモダンを融合(ゆうごう)させたインテリアが最近(さいきん)人気(にんき)だ。
Interiors that fuse Japanese style with modern design have been popular recently.

モダン is a な-adjective from English "modern," used almost exclusively to describe a stylish, contemporary aesthetic in design, architecture, fashion, and the arts.

GRAMMAR:

  • Predicate: モダンだ / モダンです
  • Modifier: モダンな + noun (e.g., モダンな建物(たてもの))
  • Adverbial: モダンに + verb

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • モダンな建築(けんちく): modern architecture
  • モダンな家具(かぐ) / インテリア: modern furniture / interior
  • モダンアート: modern art
  • モダンジャズ: modern jazz
  • モダンダンス: modern dance
  • 和(わ)モダン: Japanese-modern (a popular hybrid design style)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 現代的(げんだいてき): modern, contemporary — the closer match for "modern" in the sense of "of the present era," or "in line with current values." 現代的(げんだいてき)な考(かんが)え方(かた) = a modern way of thinking. モダン would be unnatural here.
  • スタイリッシュ: stylish — overlaps with the "stylish" reading of モダン, but emphasizes individual chic over period style
  • 古風(こふう): old-fashioned, classical — typical antonym
  • レトロ: retro — explicitly evokes a past era as a style choice

USAGE NOTES:

  • Beware of carrying English "modern" directly into Japanese for the time-period sense. "Modern Japan" as a historical era is 近代(きんだい)日本(にっぽん); "a modern smartphone" is 最新(さいしん)のスマートフォン or 現代(げんだい)のスマートフォン, not モダンなスマートフォン.
  • The term "モダンボーイ(ぼーい)/モダンガール(がーる)" (often abbreviated モボ・モガ) historically referred to fashionable young men and women of the 1920s-30s — a useful clue to the word's stylistic, cosmopolitan flavor.
