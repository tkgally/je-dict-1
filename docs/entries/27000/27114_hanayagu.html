1. to become bright and lively; to become gorgeous; to become festive
An intransitive verb describing a scene, atmosphere, or appearance becoming vibrant and glamorous.
会場(かいじょう)が華(はな)やいだ。
The venue became lively and festive.
春(はる)になると街(まち)が華(はな)やぐ。
The town brightens up when spring comes.
彼女(かのじょ)がいるだけで場(ば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)が華(はな)やぐ。
Just having her there makes the atmosphere brighter and more glamorous.
花(はな)を飾(かざ)ると部屋(へや)が華(はな)やいだ感(かん)じになる。
Decorating with flowers gives the room a brighter, more elegant feel.

An intransitive godan verb meaning to become bright, glamorous, or festive. Often used to describe how a place, event, or atmosphere gains vibrancy and elegance. The past participle 華(はな)やいだ is frequently used as a modifier meaning "vibrant" or "gorgeous."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 場(ば)が華(はな)やぐ: the atmosphere brightens up
  • 華(はな)やいだ雰囲気(ふんいき): a vibrant atmosphere
  • 華(はな)やいだ笑顔(えがお): a radiant smile
  • 華(はな)やいだ気分(きぶん): a festive mood

RELATED WORDS:

  • 華やか(はなやか): gorgeous, glamorous — the adjective form describing the same quality
  • 賑(にぎ)わう: to be lively/bustling — emphasizes crowds and activity rather than elegance
