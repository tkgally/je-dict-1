マイホーム

まいほーむ
noun
one's own home (especially a privately owned house)
1. one's own home; a house one has bought (as opposed to renting)
A house or condominium that one personally owns — typically used in the context of the dream of buying one's first home. Wasei-eigo: in English, "my home" simply means "my house," but in Japanese, マイホーム specifically connotes home ownership.
マイホームを買(か)いたい。
I want to buy my own home.
彼(かれ)らはようやくマイホームを手(て)に入(い)れた。
They finally got their own home.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)からこつこつ貯金(ちょきん)をして、四十代(よんじゅうだい)でマイホームを建(た)てた。
I saved up steadily from when I was young and built my own house in my forties.
都心(としん)でマイホームを購入(こうにゅう)するのは、多(おお)くの家庭(かてい)にとって大(おお)きな夢(ゆめ)であり、経済的(けいざいてき)な挑戦(ちょうせん)でもある。
Buying one's own home in the city center is a big dream for many families and also a financial challenge.

マイホーム is a wasei-eigo term combining English "my" + "home." Despite the literal meaning, it refers specifically to home ownership — buying one's own house or condominium. The word carries strong cultural overtones of life-stage achievement and adult responsibility.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:

  • Buying a マイホーム has long been a major life goal in Japan, on par with marriage and a stable job.
  • The phrase often appears in advertising for housing developers, mortgage companies, and home loans.
  • Recent trends include more flexibility — single-person マイホーム purchases, condos rather than detached houses, and second-home purchases in rural areas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マイホームを買(か)う / 購入(こうにゅう)する: to buy a home
  • マイホームを建(た)てる: to build a home
  • マイホームを手(て)に入(い)れる: to obtain a home
  • マイホーム購入(こうにゅう)資金(しきん): home-buying funds
  • マイホームの夢(ゆめ): the dream of home ownership
  • マイホームローン(ろーん): home loan / mortgage (also called 住宅(じゅうたく)ローン)

SCOPE NOTES:

  • Despite "my" being singular and first-person in English, マイホーム is used regardless of the speaker — "their" home, "his" home, etc. The phrase has become a fixed compound noun, not a possessive pronoun.
  • A renter's home is generally not called マイホーム — it's just 自宅(じたく) or 家(いえ).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 持(も)ち家(いえ): owned home (more neutral, technical term)
  • 自宅(じたく): one's home (whether owned or rented)
  • 一戸建(いっこだ)て: detached house
  • マンション: condominium / apartment building (in Japanese, ownership-style condos are commonly called マンション)
  • マイカー: one's own car (parallel wasei-eigo formation)
