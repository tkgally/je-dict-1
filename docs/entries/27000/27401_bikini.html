ビキニ

びきに
noun
bikini (a two-piece women's swimsuit consisting of separate top and bottom)
1. bikini — a two-piece women's swimsuit consisting of a separate top and bottom that expose the midriff
A two-piece women's swimsuit, with a separate top and bottom that expose the abdomen. ビキニ is the standard Japanese term, used essentially the same way as English "bikini." Compared with the one-piece 水着(みずぎ), a bikini is associated with beach and pool wear, summer fashion magazines, and a more revealing style; in everyday Japanese conversation, the choice between ビキニ and a one-piece swimsuit is a common topic of summer fashion talk.
新(あたら)しいビキニを買(か)う。
To buy a new bikini.
彼女(かのじょ)は白(しろ)いビキニを着(き)て海(うみ)に入(はい)った。
She put on a white bikini and got into the sea.
今年(ことし)の夏(なつ)は派手(はで)な柄(がら)のビキニが流行(はや)っている。
This summer, bikinis with flashy patterns are in fashion.
日焼(ひや)けが気(き)になる人(ひと)は、ビキニの上(うえ)にラッシュガードを羽織(はお)って海(うみ)を楽(たの)しむことが多(おお)くなった。
People worried about sunburn have increasingly come to enjoy the sea by throwing a rash guard over a bikini.

ビキニ is the standard Japanese loanword for a two-piece women's swimsuit. The katakana spelling and pronunciation closely follow the English/French original.

ORIGIN:

  • The name traces back to Bikini Atoll (ビキニ環礁(かんしょう)) in the Marshall Islands, where the United States conducted nuclear tests in 1946 — French designer Louis Réard named his new two-piece swimsuit after the atoll, evoking the explosive impact he expected the design to have.
  • The word entered Japanese via European fashion vocabulary in the post-war period.

DESIGN AND STYLES:

  • A two-piece consisting of a {ビキニトップ} (bikini top) and a {ビキニボトム} (bikini bottom).
  • Many sub-styles in Japanese fashion vocabulary: トライアングルビキニ (triangle bikini), バンドゥビキニ (bandeau bikini), ホルターネックビキニ (halter-neck bikini), ハイウエストビキニ (high-waist bikini), etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ビキニを着(き)る: to wear a bikini
  • ビキニを買(か)う / 選(えら)ぶ: to buy / choose a bikini
  • 柄(がら)のビキニ: a patterned bikini
  • 白(しろ)いビキニ / 黒(くろ)いビキニ: a white / black bikini
  • ビキニ姿(すがた): in (one's) bikini, bikini-clad appearance
  • 大胆(だいたん)なビキニ: a bold/revealing bikini
  • ビキニトップ / ビキニボトム: bikini top / bottom

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 水着(みずぎ): swimsuit (general) — the umbrella term covering ビキニ, ワンピース水着, タンキニ, etc.
  • ワンピース(水着(みずぎ)): one-piece swimsuit — the contrasting style
  • タンキニ: tankini — top extends to cover the midriff (less revealing than a bikini, less covering than one-piece)
  • モノキニ: monokini — one-piece swimsuit with cutouts at the waist
  • ラッシュガード: rash guard — long-sleeved swim top often worn over a bikini for sun protection
  • 海水(かいすい)パンツ(ぱんつ): men's swim trunks — the male counterpart in everyday vocabulary
  • ビーチウェア: beachwear — broader category covering swimsuits and coverups

USAGE NOTES:

  • ビキニ is pronounced with three light morae: ビ・キ・ニ.
  • Worn primarily for beach, pool, and resort settings; less common in public pools where many Japanese still favor one-piece swimsuits or rash guards.
  • The term is gender-marked: ビキニ is almost exclusively a women's garment in Japanese usage. Men's small swim briefs may be called ビキニパンツ or ビキニブリーフ but the bare term ビキニ is heard as referring to a women's swimsuit.
