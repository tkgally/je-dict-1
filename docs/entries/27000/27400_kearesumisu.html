ケアレスミス

けあれすみす
noun
careless mistake; avoidable error (a Japanese-coined loanword from English "careless" + "miss")
1. careless mistake; an avoidable error caused by inattention rather than lack of knowledge or skill
An error caused by inattention or carelessness rather than by a real lack of knowledge or ability — for example, miscopying a number on a test you knew how to solve, forgetting to attach a file you intended to send, or leaving out a step in a routine procedure. ケアレスミス is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) compound formed from "careless" + "miss," and it is one of the most common loanwords in school and workplace settings.
ケアレスミスをする。
To make a careless mistake.
試験(しけん)でケアレスミスを減(へ)らしたい。
I want to reduce careless mistakes on tests.
書類(しょるい)は提出(ていしゅつ)前(まえ)に必(かなら)ず見直(みなお)し、ケアレスミスがないか確認(かくにん)してください。
Be sure to review documents before submitting them and check for any careless mistakes.
知識(ちしき)は十分(じゅうぶん)なのに、時間(じかん)不足(ぶそく)とケアレスミスのせいで合格(ごうかく)点(てん)に届(とど)かなかった。
Although my knowledge was sufficient, between running out of time and making careless mistakes, I didn't reach the passing score.

ケアレスミス is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) compound that is one of the most common words for "careless mistake" in school and workplace settings.

WASEI-EIGO ORIGIN:

  • Formed from English "careless" + "miss."
  • Although the components are English, the full compound "careless miss" is not a fixed English phrase; English speakers would say "careless mistake" or "careless error." ケアレスミス should not be back-translated literally as "careless miss" when writing English.

TYPICAL CONTEXTS:

  • Schoolwork and exams: writing the wrong number, misreading a question, skipping a step
  • Office work: typos, attaching the wrong file, omitting a name from a list
  • Programming: missing a comma, off-by-one errors that you would have noticed if reviewing
  • Sports: dropped passes, simple errors in plays you can normally execute

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ケアレスミスをする: to make a careless mistake
  • ケアレスミスを犯(おか)す: to commit a careless mistake (slightly more formal)
  • ケアレスミスが多(おお)い: to make many careless mistakes
  • ケアレスミスを減(へ)らす: to reduce careless mistakes
  • ケアレスミスを防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent careless mistakes
  • ケアレスミスに気(き)をつける: to be careful of careless mistakes
  • ケアレスミスで失点(しってん)する: to lose points to a careless mistake

NUANCE:

  • ケアレスミス specifically implies that the error was avoidable — that the person had the knowledge or skill to do it correctly but slipped up because of inattention.
  • This is distinct from a mistake due to genuine ignorance or lack of skill (勉強(べんきょう)不足(ぶそく) "insufficient study") or a deeper error in reasoning (勘違(かんちが)い "misunderstanding").

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • ミス: mistake (general loanword) — the broader term; ケアレスミス is a specific type
  • 不注意(ふちゅうい): carelessness, inattention — the underlying cause; the native-Japanese parallel
  • うっかりミス: a slip / oversight due to inattention (うっかり "absent-mindedly" + ミス); near-synonym
  • 見落(みお)とし: oversight, something missed/overlooked — emphasizes the missed item rather than the error
  • 勘違(かんちが)い: misunderstanding, mistaken belief — implies a wrong understanding, not just inattention
  • ノーミス: "no mistakes," flawless (wasei-eigo) — the positive counterpart in test/game contexts
  • ヒューマンエラー: human error — broader and more formal; covers all human-caused errors

USAGE NOTES:

  • The verb form ケアレスミスする is grammatical but less common than ケアレスミスをする.
  • In strict English writing, prefer "careless mistake," "avoidable error," or "slip" — not "careless miss."
  • The word fits all registers from casual to relatively formal; in highly formal writing, 不注意(ふちゅうい)による誤(あやま)り ("errors due to carelessness") may be preferred.
