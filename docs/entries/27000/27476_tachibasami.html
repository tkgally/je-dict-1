裁(た)ちばさみ

たちばさみ
noun
fabric scissors; tailor's shears
1. fabric scissors; tailor's shears; dressmaking scissors
Large, heavy scissors designed for cutting cloth cleanly along a marked line. Used in sewing, dressmaking, and tailoring.
裁(た)ちばさみで布(ぬの)を切(き)る。
I cut the fabric with tailor's shears.
紙(かみ)を切(き)ると裁(た)ちばさみが切(き)れなくなる。
If you cut paper with them, fabric scissors lose their sharpness.
母(はは)は裁縫箱(さいほうばこ)の中(なか)から裁(た)ちばさみを取(と)り出(だ)した。
My mother took the fabric scissors out of the sewing box.

From 裁(た)つ (to cut out, to tailor) + はさみ (scissors). The verb 裁(た)つ specifically means cutting cloth or leather to a pattern.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 裁(た)ちばさみで布(ぬの)を切(き)る: to cut fabric with tailor's shears
  • 裁(た)ちばさみを研(と)ぐ: to sharpen the fabric scissors
  • 裁縫(さいほう)用(よう)の裁(た)ちばさみ: dressmaking scissors

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 糸切(いとき)りばさみ: thread snips — small spring-loaded scissors for cutting thread
  • ピンキングばさみ: pinking shears — zigzag-edged scissors for cloth
  • 爪切(つめき)りばさみ: nail scissors — small scissors for trimming nails

USAGE NOTE:
A traditional rule of sewing is never to use 裁(た)ちばさみ on paper, as paper dulls the blade and ruins the clean cut needed for cloth.
