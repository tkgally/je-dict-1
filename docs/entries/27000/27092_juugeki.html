1. shooting; gunfire; firing (a gun)
The act of firing a gun at a target, or the gunfire itself. Used both as a noun and as a suru verb.
銃撃(じゅうげき)事件(じけん)が起(お)きた。
A shooting incident occurred.
犯人(はんにん)は逃走(とうそう)中(ちゅう)に警察(けいさつ)から銃撃(じゅうげき)された。
The suspect was shot at by police while fleeing.
深夜(しんや)に住宅街(じゅうたくがい)で銃撃(じゅうげき)音(おん)が聞(き)こえたと住民(じゅうみん)が通報(つうほう)した。
Residents reported hearing gunfire in the residential area late at night.

A news vocabulary word frequently encountered in media reports about crime, conflict, and military operations. Composed of 銃(じゅう) (gun) + 撃(げき) (strike/attack).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 銃撃(じゅうげき)事件(じけん): shooting incident
  • 銃撃(じゅうげき)音(おん): sound of gunfire
  • 銃撃(じゅうげき)戦(せん): gun battle; firefight
  • 銃撃(じゅうげき)される: to be shot at
  • 銃撃(じゅうげき)を受(う)ける: to come under fire

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発砲(はっぽう): discharge of a firearm — focuses on the single act of firing a gun, while 銃撃(じゅうげき) can imply sustained or targeted shooting
  • 射撃(しゃげき): shooting (as a skill or sport) — often used for target practice and marksmanship, while 銃撃(じゅうげき) implies shooting at people or in a conflict
  • 狙撃(そげき): sniping — specifically refers to shooting from a concealed position at a distance
