許(ゆる)し

ゆるし
noun
forgiveness; pardon; permission; consent — the noun form of 許す, covering both "forgiving someone" and "granting permission"
1. forgiveness; pardon — the act of forgiving someone for a wrongdoing or offense
The act of releasing someone from blame for a fault or wrongdoing. The implicit relationship is between an offender and a wronged party (or an authority figure with the power to absolve). 許(ゆる)し in this sense often appears in religious, moral, or interpersonal contexts — asking for forgiveness from a person, a parent, or a deity. It tends to feel slightly literary or weighty; in everyday Japanese, people are more likely to say 謝(あやま)る ("to apologize") or use the verb 許(ゆる)す directly rather than the noun 許(ゆる)し.
許(ゆる)しを請(こ)う。
To beg for forgiveness.
彼(かれ)は母(はは)に許(ゆる)しを求(もと)めた。
He sought his mother's forgiveness.
自分(じぶん)の過(あやま)ちを深(ふか)く反省(はんせい)し、彼女(かのじょ)の許(ゆる)しを得(え)たいと思(おも)っている。
He deeply regrets his mistake and hopes to obtain her forgiveness.
2. permission; consent; authorization — the act of allowing something to be done
The act of granting permission or consent. Used when one party authorizes another to do something, or refrains from forbidding it. This sense overlaps with 許可(きょか) ("permission, authorization"), but 許(ゆる)し is softer, more personal, and somewhat literary or old-fashioned, while 許可(きょか) is the standard word for institutional or official permission (a license, an authorization). 許(ゆる)し often implies a personal relationship — a parent's permission, a master's consent — rather than a bureaucratic process.
父(ちち)の許(ゆる)しを得(え)る。
To obtain my father's permission.
師匠(ししょう)の許(ゆる)しがなければ、この技(わざ)を人(ひと)に教(おし)えることはできない。
Without my master's permission, I cannot teach this technique to others.
結婚(けっこん)するには、両親(りょうしん)の許(ゆる)しを得(え)る必要(ひつよう)があると彼女(かのじょ)は考(かんが)えていた。
She believed she needed to obtain her parents' consent in order to get married.

許(ゆる)し is the masu-stem (連用形(れんようけい)) noun form of the verb 許(ゆる)す ("to forgive; to permit"). It carries both senses of the source verb — forgiveness and permission — and the meaning is determined by context.

CORE SEMANTIC CONTRAST WITH 許可(きょか):

  • 許(ゆる)し: personal, interpersonal, often emotional or moral; softer; somewhat literary
  • 許可(きょか): institutional, bureaucratic, official; harder, more neutral; the everyday word for an issued permit, license, or authorization
  • A parent gives 許(ゆる)し to a child; a city gives 許可(きょか) to a builder
  • Asking a deity for 許(ゆる)し sounds natural; asking for 許可(きょか) from a deity sounds wrong

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (FORGIVENESS SENSE):

  • 許(ゆる)しを請(こ)う: to beg for forgiveness (formal/literary)
  • 許(ゆる)しを乞(こ)う: to plead for forgiveness (formal/literary; same word, alternative kanji)
  • 許(ゆる)しを求(もと)める: to seek forgiveness
  • 許(ゆる)しを得(え)る: to obtain forgiveness
  • 許(ゆる)しを与(あた)える: to grant forgiveness
  • 神(かみ)の許(ゆる)し: God's / the gods' forgiveness

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (PERMISSION SENSE):

  • 許(ゆる)しを得(え)る: to obtain permission
  • 許(ゆる)しなく: without permission
  • 許(ゆる)しがある: to have (someone's) permission
  • 親(おや)の許(ゆる)し: parental permission
  • 師匠(ししょう)の許(ゆる)し: master's permission (traditional arts)

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 許(ゆる)す: the source verb — to forgive; to permit
  • 許可(きょか): official permission; authorization (institutional)
  • 承諾(しょうだく): consent; agreement (more formal, often contractual)
  • 同意(どうい): agreement; assent
  • 了承(りょうしょう): acknowledgment / consent (used in business)
  • 謝罪(しゃざい): apology — paired with 許(ゆる)し in the forgiveness sense
  • 勘弁(かんべん): pardon, mercy (often colloquial; 勘弁(かんべん)してください = please forgive me / cut me a break)
  • 寛容(かんよう): tolerance, broad-mindedness

REGISTER:

  • Slightly literary or formal in feeling, especially in the forgiveness sense
  • In daily speech, the verb form (許(ゆる)してください, 許(ゆる)してくれる?) is far more common than the noun
  • The noun form is often seen in writing, song lyrics, religious texts, and serious dialogue

GRAMMAR / FORMATION:

  • Formed by the masu-stem of the godan verb 許(ゆる)す: 許(ゆる)し-(ます)
  • Other related deverbal nouns: 許可(きょか) (Sino-Japanese compound), お許(ゆる)し (honorific form, often used to a superior — お許(ゆる)しください = please grant me leave / forgive me)
