ノーミス

のーみす
noun
no mistakes; a flawless performance (wasei-eigo from English "no" + "miss")
1. no mistakes; flawless execution (used in sports, exams, performances, and games where errors can be counted)
A Japanese-coined English compound meaning "performing without any mistakes" — a flawless run through a test, game, performance, sport, or task. It is commonly used in figure skating, gymnastics, music recitals, exam-results talk, and gaming/esports contexts, where a clean performance with zero errors is the goal. ノーミス is typically used as a predicate (with だ) or in the adverbial form ノーミスで ("without any mistakes").
今日(きょう)の試験(しけん)はノーミスだった。
Today's exam was flawless (I made no mistakes).
ノーミスで演技(えんぎ)を終(お)える。
To finish a performance without any mistakes.
ノーミスでフリーを滑(すべ)り切(き)った選手(せんしゅ)に大(おお)歓声(かんせい)が送(おく)られた。
The skater who got through her free skate without a single mistake received huge cheers.
何度(なんど)も練習(れんしゅう)を重(かさ)ねたおかげで、本番(ほんばん)ではノーミスで曲(きょく)を弾(ひ)き終(お)えることができた。
Thanks to repeated practice, I was able to play through the piece without any mistakes during the actual performance.

ノーミス is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) noun meaning "no mistakes" or "a flawless performance." It is widely used in performance and sports contexts.

WASEI-EIGO STATUS:

  • Components: English "no" + "miss"
  • The full compound "no miss" is not idiomatic English; English speakers would say "flawless," "clean," "perfect," or "without any mistakes." ノーミス should not be back-translated literally as "no miss" when writing English.

TYPICAL CONTEXTS:

  • Figure skating, gymnastics, diving — sports where errors are noted and deducted
  • Music recitals — performing a piece without flubs
  • Exam talk — finishing a test without careless mistakes
  • Video games / rhythm games / esports — completing a level or song without errors (often abbreviated NM or ノーミス達成(たっせい))
  • Driving tests, certifications, demonstrations

GRAMMATICAL BEHAVIOR:

  • Predicate: 〜はノーミスだった ("~ was flawless")
  • Adverbial: ノーミスで〜 ("in a flawless manner / without any mistakes")
  • Compound modifier: ノーミス演技(えんぎ) ("a flawless performance"), ノーミス達成(たっせい) ("achieving a no-miss run")

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ノーミスで終(お)わる: to finish without a mistake
  • ノーミスで滑(すべ)り切(き)る: to skate through without a mistake (figure skating)
  • ノーミスで弾(ひ)き切(き)る: to play through without a mistake (music)
  • ノーミスを狙(ねら)う: to aim for a flawless run
  • ノーミス達成(たっせい): achieving a no-miss run
  • ノーミス演技(えんぎ): a flawless performance
  • 完全(かんぜん)ノーミス: "completely flawless" — for emphasis

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • ケアレスミス: careless mistake — the negative counterpart, a typical thing one tries to avoid
  • 完璧(かんぺき): perfect, flawless (general adjective) — broader; can describe more than just lack of errors
  • ミスなし: "no mistakes" (without the ノー prefix) — uses native Japanese 無(な)し; close in meaning
  • 一発(いっぱつ)合格(ごうかく): passing on the first try — related notion of clean success
  • パーフェクト: "perfect" (loanword) — broader; often used for perfect scores or runs
  • ミスゼロ: "zero mistakes" (less common than ノーミス but used similarly)
  • 完投(かんとう): complete game (baseball) — different but related notion of finishing without error

USAGE NOTES:

  • ノーミス implies measurable mistakes — there must be something one can count as a mistake. It is awkward for situations like a casual conversation where mistakes are not formally tracked.
  • In strict English writing, prefer "flawless," "clean," or "without any mistakes" — not "no miss."
  • Often heard in sports broadcasts: "ノーミスで演技(えんぎ)を滑(すべ)り切(き)りました!" ("She skated through without a single mistake!").
