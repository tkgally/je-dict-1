沼地(ぬまち)

ぬまち
noun
swamp; marshland; boggy ground
1. swamp; marsh; marshland; boggy ground
An area of low-lying, waterlogged land where the ground is soft and wet. May have standing water and characteristic wetland vegetation.
沼地(ぬまち)に足(あし)を取(と)られた。
My feet got stuck in the swamp.
この辺(あた)りはかつて沼地(ぬまち)だったらしい。
This area was apparently a swamp in the past.
沼地(ぬまち)を埋(う)め立(た)てて住宅地(じゅうたくち)にしたため、地盤(じばん)が弱(よわ)い。
Because they filled in the swamp to build a residential area, the ground is unstable.

A compound of 沼(ぬま) (swamp, pond) + 地(ち) (land, ground). Refers to terrain that is perpetually wet and soft, often with standing water. Relevant in Japan because many modern urban areas were built on reclaimed swampland, which affects building foundations and earthquake risk.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 沼地(ぬまち)に足(あし)を取(と)られる: to get one's feet stuck in a swamp
  • 沼地(ぬまち)を埋(う)め立(た)てる: to reclaim swampland (by filling)
  • 沼地(ぬまち)が広(ひろ)がる: swampland stretches out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 湿地(しっち): wetland; marshland — a broader, more scientific term that includes any kind of wet terrain. 沼地(ぬまち) specifically implies soft, muddy ground.
  • 沼(ぬま): swamp; pond — the base word, which can refer to a body of standing water rather than terrain. 沼地(ぬまち) emphasizes the land area.
  • 泥沼(どろぬま): mud swamp; quagmire — often used figuratively to mean a hopeless situation one cannot escape from.
