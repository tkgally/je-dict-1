係数(けいすう)

けいすう
noun
coefficient (mathematics, physics); a multiplying factor in an expression or formula
1. coefficient — a numerical or symbolic multiplying factor in a mathematical expression, physical formula, or statistical measure
A standard technical term in mathematics, science, and engineering for a multiplying factor in an expression. In an algebraic expression like 3x² + 5x − 2, the 係数(けいすう) of x² is 3 and the 係数(けいすう) of x is 5. The word is also widely used for named quantities in physics, statistics, and economics: 摩擦(まさつ)係数(けいすう) (friction coefficient), 相関(そうかん)係数(けいすう) (correlation coefficient), 弾性(だんせい)係数(けいすう) (modulus of elasticity), and so on.
摩擦(まさつ)係数(けいすう)を測(はか)る。
To measure the coefficient of friction.
この式(しき)のxの係数(けいすう)は2である。
The coefficient of x in this expression is 2.
二(ふた)つの変数(へんすう)の相関(そうかん)係数(けいすう)が高(たか)いほど、両者(りょうしゃ)の関係(かんけい)は強(つよ)いと言(い)える。
The higher the correlation coefficient between two variables, the stronger the relationship between them is said to be.
材料(ざいりょう)の熱(ねつ)膨張(ぼうちょう)係数(けいすう)が大(おお)きい場合(ばあい)、温度(おんど)変化(へんか)による寸法(すんぽう)の変化(へんか)を慎重(しんちょう)に設計(せっけい)に反映(はんえい)させる必要(ひつよう)がある。
If a material's coefficient of thermal expansion is large, dimensional changes due to temperature variation must be carefully reflected in the design.

係数(けいすう) is a technical term used widely in mathematics, science, and engineering for a multiplying factor in a formula or expression.

FORMATION:

  • 係(けい) ("related to, connected with") + 数(すう) ("number, quantity") → "a related/associated number" → coefficient
  • The same 係(けい) appears in 関係(かんけい) ("relation") and 係(かか)る ("to be involved").

CONTEXTS OF USE:

  • Mathematics: coefficient of a polynomial term (xの係数(けいすう) "coefficient of x")
  • Physics: named coefficients (摩擦(まさつ)係数(けいすう) friction coefficient, 膨張(ぼうちょう)係数(けいすう) expansion coefficient)
  • Statistics: 相関(そうかん)係数(けいすう) (correlation coefficient), 回帰(かいき)係数(けいすう) (regression coefficient), 決定(けってい)係数(けいすう) (coefficient of determination, R²)
  • Engineering: material properties such as 弾性(だんせい)係数(けいすう) (modulus of elasticity), 熱(ねつ)伝導(でんどう)係数(けいすう) (thermal conductivity)
  • Economics: ジニ}{係数(けいすう) (Gini coefficient)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 係数(けいすう)を求(もと)める: to find the coefficient (math problem)
  • 係数(けいすう)が大(おお)きい / 小(ちい)さい: the coefficient is large / small
  • 摩擦(まさつ)係数(けいすう): coefficient of friction
  • 相関(そうかん)係数(けいすう): correlation coefficient
  • 膨張(ぼうちょう)係数(けいすう): coefficient of expansion
  • 弾性(だんせい)係数(けいすう): elastic modulus / modulus of elasticity
  • 熱(ねつ)伝導(でんどう)係数(けいすう): thermal conductivity coefficient
  • 決定(けってい)係数(けいすう): coefficient of determination (statistics, R²)

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 定数(ていすう): constant — a fixed numerical value that does not change (vs. variable); coefficients are often constants but the words are not synonyms
  • 変数(へんすう): variable — a quantity that can take different values
  • 指数(しすう): index, exponent — used both for mathematical exponents (e.g., x³) and for indices like the consumer price index
  • 比率(ひりつ): ratio, proportion — a relationship between two quantities (different from coefficient)
  • 因子(いんし): factor — a contributing element; in statistics, a latent variable in factor analysis
  • 係(かかり)率(りつ): not a standard term; rarely used

USAGE NOTES:

  • 係数(けいすう) is firmly technical vocabulary; expect to encounter it in textbooks, technical writing, and STEM courses.
  • It is not used metaphorically; for an everyday "factor in life," use 要因(よういん) or 要素(ようそ) instead.
  • The pronunciation is {けいすう} (three morae); the けい is a long え-vowel.
