ゴムボート

ごむぼーと
noun
rubber dinghy; inflatable boat
1. inflatable boat; rubber dinghy
A small boat made of rubber or rubberized fabric and inflated with air. Used for recreation, fishing, life rafts, and emergency rescue.
川(かわ)でゴムボートに乗(の)った。
I rode in an inflatable boat on the river.
ゴムボートに空気(くうき)を入(い)れて海(うみ)に出(で)た。
We inflated the rubber dinghy and headed out to sea.
災害(さいがい)時(じ)にはゴムボートで避難(ひなん)者(しゃ)を救助(きゅうじょ)することがある。
In disasters, evacuees are sometimes rescued using inflatable boats.
家族(かぞく)でキャンプに行(い)って、湖(みずうみ)で小(ちい)さなゴムボートを浮(う)かべて遊(あそ)んだ。
We went camping with the family and floated a small inflatable boat on the lake to play.

ゴムボート is a Japanese compound of ゴム(ごむ) (rubber) + ボート (boat), referring to inflatable boats. The English equivalents include "inflatable boat," "rubber dinghy," or "raft," depending on size and use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴムボートに乗(の)る: to ride in an inflatable boat
  • ゴムボートに空気(くうき)を入(い)れる: to inflate a rubber dinghy
  • ゴムボートを膨(ふく)らませる: to blow up an inflatable boat
  • ゴムボートが破(やぶ)れる: an inflatable boat tears

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Recreation (river rafting, lake play, beach use)
  • Fishing
  • Disaster response and water rescue (災害(さいがい)救助(きゅうじょ))
  • Lifeboats on ships (救命(きゅうめい)ボート is the more specific term for life rafts)

RELATED TERMS:

  • ボート: boat (general)
  • カヤック: kayak
  • 筏(いかだ): raft (often wooden)
  • 救命(きゅうめい)ボート: lifeboat
  • 空気(くうき)入(い)れ: air pump
