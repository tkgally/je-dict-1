ボーイスカウト

ぼーいすかうと
noun
Boy Scouts (organization); a Boy Scout
1. Boy Scouts (organization); a Boy Scout
The international youth organization for boys focused on outdoor activities, citizenship, and character development. Also refers to an individual member of the organization.
息子(むすこ)はボーイスカウトに入(はい)っている。
My son is in the Boy Scouts.
ボーイスカウトの活動(かつどう)でキャンプに行(い)く。
We go camping as part of Boy Scout activities.
ボーイスカウトに入(はい)って、自然(しぜん)の中(なか)でいろいろなことを学(まな)んだ。
I joined the Boy Scouts and learned many things in nature.
兄(あに)は小学生(しょうがくせい)の頃(ころ)からボーイスカウトに所属(しょぞく)し、リーダーとして活躍(かつやく)している。
My older brother has been in the Boy Scouts since elementary school and is now active as a leader.

ボーイスカウト is a loanword from English "Boy Scout(s)," referring both to the organization and to individual members. It denotes the same international scouting movement as in English-speaking countries.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ボーイスカウトに入(はい)る: to join the Boy Scouts
  • ボーイスカウトの活動(かつどう): Boy Scout activities
  • ボーイスカウトの制服(せいふく): Boy Scout uniform
  • ボーイスカウトの隊長(たいちょう): Boy Scout leader

RELATED TERMS:

  • ガールスカウト: Girl Scouts
  • カブスカウト: Cub Scouts (younger boys)
  • ベンチャースカウト: Venture Scouts (older youth program)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, scouting is organized under ボーイスカウト日本連盟(ぼーいすかうとにほんれんめい) (Scout Association of Japan). Scouts wear standard scouting uniforms with neckerchiefs and earn merit badges. Membership is significantly smaller than in some Western countries but remains a recognizable youth activity, especially in suburban communities.
