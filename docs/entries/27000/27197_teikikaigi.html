定期会議(ていきかいぎ)

ていきかいぎ
noun
regular meeting; periodic meeting
1. regular meeting; periodic meeting; standing meeting
A meeting held at fixed, recurring intervals such as weekly, monthly, or quarterly, as part of an organization's routine operations.
毎週(まいしゅう)月曜日(げつようび)に定期会議(ていきかいぎ)がある。
There is a regular meeting every Monday.
今月(こんげつ)の定期会議(ていきかいぎ)は来週(らいしゅう)に延期(えんき)された。
This month's regular meeting was postponed to next week.
部署間(ぶしょかん)の定期会議(ていきかいぎ)を通(つう)じて、情報共有(じょうほうきょうゆう)と連携(れんけい)を強化(きょうか)している。
Through regular inter-departmental meetings, we are strengthening information sharing and coordination.

A compound of 定期(ていき) (regular, periodic) + 会議(かいぎ) (meeting, conference). Standard workplace vocabulary for scheduled recurring meetings, in contrast to 臨時会議(りんじかいぎ) (ad hoc/emergency meetings).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 定期会議(ていきかいぎ)を開(ひら)く: to hold a regular meeting
  • 定期会議(ていきかいぎ)に出席(しゅっせき)する: to attend a regular meeting
  • 月例(げつれい)定期会議(ていきかいぎ): monthly regular meeting
  • 定期会議(ていきかいぎ)の議題(ぎだい): agenda for the regular meeting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 臨時会議(りんじかいぎ): emergency/extraordinary meeting (unscheduled, called for urgent matters)
  • 定例会(ていれいかい): regular meeting (shorter synonym, commonly used in local government and organizations)
  • 朝礼(ちょうれい): morning assembly (brief daily gathering in Japanese companies)
