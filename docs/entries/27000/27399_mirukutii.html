ミルクティー

みるくてぃー
noun
milk tea — black tea served with milk (also used for various sweet milk-tea drinks including bubble tea)
1. milk tea — black tea prepared or served with milk; in cafe and beverage contexts, also sweet milk-tea drinks such as royal milk tea and bubble tea
Black tea (紅茶(こうちゃ)) served or prepared with milk. In Japan, ミルクティー can refer to a simple cup of tea with milk added, or to richer prepared varieties — most notably {ロイヤルミルクティー} (royal milk tea, in which the leaves are simmered in milk) and the wide range of sweet milk-tea beverages sold in cafes, convenience stores, and bubble-tea shops. The cold version (アイスミルクティー) is also widely sold.
温(あたた)かいミルクティー。
A warm milk tea.
砂糖(さとう)入(い)りのミルクティーを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered a milk tea with sugar.
紅茶(こうちゃ)専門店(せんもんてん)のロイヤルミルクティーは濃厚(のうこう)で香(かお)り豊(ゆた)かだ。
The royal milk tea at the specialty tea shop is rich and full of aroma.
コンビニで売(う)られているタピオカ入(い)りのミルクティーは、若(わか)い世代(せだい)に長年(ながねん)人気(にんき)が続(つづ)いている。
The bubble (tapioca) milk teas sold at convenience stores have remained popular with the younger generation for years.

ミルクティー is a loanword from English "milk tea," referring to black tea with milk and the range of sweet milk-based tea drinks popular in Japan.

LOANWORD ORIGIN AND USAGE SHIFT:

  • Coined from English "milk tea." In British English, the same drink is often just called "tea with milk"; the compound "milk tea" has gained ground in English partly via Asian café and bubble-tea culture.
  • In Japan, the term has expanded beyond simple tea with milk to cover a broad family of milk-based tea drinks (royal milk tea, bubble tea, chai-style spiced milk teas, etc.).

VARIETIES:

  • ロイヤルミルクティー: "royal milk tea" — black tea leaves simmered in milk (with little or no water), producing a thick, rich drink. A Japanese coinage that has spread to other parts of Asia.
  • アイスミルクティー: iced milk tea
  • タピオカミルクティー: bubble (tapioca) milk tea — Taiwanese-origin drink with chewy tapioca pearls
  • チャイミルクティー / マサラチャイ: Indian-style spiced milk tea

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ミルクティーを飲(の)む: to drink milk tea
  • ミルクティーを注文(ちゅうもん)する: to order a milk tea
  • 温(あたた)かいミルクティー: hot milk tea
  • アイスミルクティー: iced milk tea
  • 砂糖(さとう)入(い)りミルクティー: milk tea with sugar
  • ミルクティー味(あじ): milk-tea flavor (used for sweets, ice cream, candies)
  • ミルクティー色(いろ): milk-tea color (a beige-brown shade)

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 紅茶(こうちゃ): black tea — the base ingredient; ミルクティー is one way of serving 紅茶(こうちゃ)
  • レモンティー: lemon tea (black tea with lemon)
  • ストレートティー: straight tea (no milk, no lemon) — used in cafe ordering
  • ロイヤルミルクティー: royal milk tea (see above)
  • タピオカ({ティー}): bubble tea / boba — short for タピオカミルクティー
  • カフェオレ: café au lait — coffee-with-milk parallel to ミルクティー
  • チャイ: chai — Indian spiced milk tea

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japan, when ordering at a cafe, ミルクティー without specification usually means a basic black-tea-with-milk; ロイヤルミルクティー is a separate menu item.
  • Cold versions are clearly labeled アイスミルクティー (hot is the default in older shops, but bubble-tea shops have made cold the default for younger consumers).
  • The katakana spelling is ミルクティー (with a small ィ in ティ; older spellings sometimes used ミルクテー).
