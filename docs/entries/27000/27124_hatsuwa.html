1. utterance; speech; speaking (the act of producing speech)
Used in linguistics, education, and technical contexts to refer to the act or product of spoken language.
子(こ)どもの発話(はつわ)を記録(きろく)する。
To record children's utterances.
発話(はつわ)の速度(そくど)が速(はや)すぎて聞(き)き取(と)れなかった。
The speech was too fast and I couldn't catch it.
AIの音声(おんせい)認識(にんしき)は発話(はつわ)をテキストに変換(へんかん)する技術(ぎじゅつ)だ。
AI speech recognition is a technology that converts speech to text.

A formal/technical term for the act of speaking or an instance of spoken language. Combines 発(はつ) (emit/produce) and 話(わ) (speech/talk). Commonly used in linguistics, language education, speech therapy, and technology contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発話(はつわ)量(りょう): amount of speech output
  • 発話(はつわ)能力(のうりょく): speaking ability
  • 発話(はつわ)を分析(ぶんせき)する: to analyze speech
  • 自発的(じはつてき)な発話(はつわ): spontaneous speech

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発言(はつげん): statement, remark — emphasizes the content and context of what is said, often in public settings
  • 発音(はつおん): pronunciation — focuses on how sounds are produced
  • 会話(かいわ): conversation — interactive exchange between speakers
