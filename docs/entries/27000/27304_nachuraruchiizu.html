ナチュラルチーズ

なちゅらるちーず
noun
natural cheese
1. natural cheese (cheese that is not processed cheese)
Cheese made directly from milk by curdling, draining, and ripening, without further melting and reblending. Distinguished in Japanese supermarket labeling from プロセスチーズ (processed cheese).
ナチュラルチーズが好(す)きだ。
I like natural cheese.
ナチュラルチーズの種類(しゅるい)は多(おお)い。
There are many kinds of natural cheese.
ナチュラルチーズはプロセスチーズに比(くら)べて風味(ふうみ)が豊(ゆた)かだ。
Natural cheese has a richer flavor compared to processed cheese.
専門店(せんもんてん)には、世界中(せかいじゅう)から集(あつ)められた様々(さまざま)なナチュラルチーズが並(なら)んでいる。
At specialty shops, various natural cheeses gathered from around the world are lined up on display.

ナチュラルチーズ is a loanword phrase based on English "natural cheese." In Japanese, it serves as a marketing/labeling term to distinguish authentic cheeses from プロセスチーズ (processed cheese), which is melted and reblended from natural cheeses.

CATEGORY DETAILS:

  • ナチュラルチーズ includes most well-known cheese varieties: チェダー, モッツァレラ, カマンベール, ブリー, パルメザン, ゴーダ, etc.
  • The category is often subdivided by texture: 軟質(なんしつ) (soft), 半硬質(はんこうしつ) (semi-hard), 硬質(こうしつ) (hard).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ナチュラルチーズを選(えら)ぶ: to choose a natural cheese
  • ナチュラルチーズを食(た)べ比(くら)べる: to do a tasting comparison of natural cheeses
  • ナチュラルチーズの盛(も)り合(あ)わせ: a cheese plate / assortment

CONTRAST:

  • プロセスチーズ: processed cheese — melted and reblended natural cheeses, with longer shelf life and uniform flavor; the typical sliced cheese for sandwiches in Japan

USAGE NOTES:

  • This is more of a labeling/category term than a word used in casual conversation. Most people simply say チーズ in everyday speech and use ナチュラルチーズ when emphasizing the distinction (e.g., on packaging, in food magazines, or when shopping at a cheese counter).
  • Japan's food labeling regulations formally distinguish ナチュラルチーズ and プロセスチーズ.
