どこにも

どこにも
adverb
nowhere (with negative); everywhere; anywhere
1. nowhere; not anywhere (with negative verb)
When followed by a negative verb, means 'not in any place' or 'not to any place.' This is the most common usage.
鍵(かぎ)がどこにもない。
The key is nowhere to be found.
今日(きょう)はどこにも行(い)かない。
I'm not going anywhere today.
探(さが)したけど、どこにも見(み)つからなかった。
I searched, but I couldn't find it anywhere.
どこにも逃(に)げ場(ば)がない。
There is no place to escape to.
2. everywhere; anywhere; in every place
When followed by a positive verb, means 'in/to every place without exception.' Less common than the negative usage.
どこにもゴミが落(お)ちている。
There is litter everywhere.
この花(はな)はどこにも咲(さ)いている。
This flower blooms everywhere.

Composed of どこ (where) + に (location particle) + も (even/also). The meaning changes based on whether the verb is negative or affirmative:

  • Negative verb → nowhere: どこにもない (not anywhere)
  • Affirmative verb → everywhere: どこにもある (everywhere)

The negative pattern is far more common in everyday speech.

Note the difference from どこかに (somewhere), which uses か instead of も.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • どこにもない: not found anywhere; nowhere
  • どこにも行(い)かない: not going anywhere
  • どこにも見(み)つからない: can't be found anywhere
  • どこにも売(う)っていない: not sold anywhere

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どこかに: somewhere — indefinite location (with か instead of も)
  • どこへも: nowhere/everywhere (directional) — uses the directional particle へ instead of に
  • どこでも: anywhere; wherever — focuses on the place where an action occurs, without restriction
