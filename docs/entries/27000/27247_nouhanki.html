農繁期(のうはんき)

のうはんき
noun
busy farming season; peak agricultural period
1. busy farming season; peak agricultural period
The time of year when farming work is most intensive, particularly during planting (spring) and harvesting (autumn). The opposite of 農閑期(のうかんき) (slack farming season).
農繁期(のうはんき)は人手(ひとで)が足(た)りない。
There aren't enough workers during the busy farming season.
農繁期(のうはんき)には朝(あさ)から晩(ばん)まで田(た)んぼで働(はたら)く。
During the busy farming season, we work in the rice paddies from morning to night.
かつては農繁期(のうはんき)になると学校(がっこう)を休(やす)む子(こ)どもも多(おお)かった。
In the past, many children would miss school during the busy farming season.

Compound of 農(のう) (agriculture) + 繁(はん) (busy/flourishing) + 期(き) (period). In Japan, the main 農繁期(のうはんき) periods are spring (田植(たう)え, rice planting) and autumn (稲刈(いねか)り, rice harvesting). Historically, school schedules and local events were arranged around these periods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 農繁期(のうはんき)に入(はい)る: to enter the busy farming season
  • 農繁期(のうはんき)の手伝(てつだ)い: helping during busy farming season
  • 農繁期(のうはんき)休暇(きゅうか): farming season leave (historically given to school children)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 農閑期(のうかんき): slack farming season — the opposite, the quiet period between planting and harvesting
  • 繁忙期(はんぼうき): busy season — a general term for any industry's peak period, not limited to farming
  • 収穫期(しゅうかくき): harvest season — specifically the autumn harvest period
