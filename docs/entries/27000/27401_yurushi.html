許(ゆる)し

ゆるし
noun
forgiveness; pardon; permission
1. forgiveness; pardon
The act of forgiving someone for a wrongdoing, mistake, or offense; pardon. The noun form of 許(ゆる)す in the sense of "to forgive."
許(ゆる)しを求(もと)める。
To seek forgiveness.
彼女(かのじょ)の許(ゆる)しを得(え)た。
I received her forgiveness.
深(ふか)く反省(はんせい)し、被害者(ひがいしゃ)に許(ゆる)しを請(こ)うた。
He deeply regretted what he had done and begged the victim's forgiveness.
2. permission; consent; approval
Permission granted by someone with authority — a parent, teacher, employer, or institution. The noun form of 許(ゆる)す in the sense of "to allow."
親(おや)の許(ゆる)しを得(え)る。
To get one's parents' permission.
先生(せんせい)の許(ゆる)しなく教室(きょうしつ)を出(で)てはいけない。
You must not leave the classroom without the teacher's permission.
結婚(けっこん)するために、彼女(かのじょ)のお父(とう)さんに正式(せいしき)に許(ゆる)しをもらった。
In order to get married, I formally received her father's permission.

The noun form of 許(ゆる)す, covering both "to forgive" and "to permit." Context disambiguates the two senses, but they can overlap (e.g., a parent's 許(ゆる)し for a marriage involves both consent and acceptance).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 許(ゆる)しを請(こ)う: to beg for forgiveness (literary)
  • 許(ゆる)しを求(もと)める: to seek forgiveness / permission
  • 許(ゆる)しを得(え)る: to obtain forgiveness / permission
  • 許(ゆる)しを乞(こ)う: to plead for forgiveness
  • 親(おや)の許(ゆる)し: parents' permission/blessing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 許可(きょか): permission; authorization — institutional or formal permission (visa, license); doesn't cover "forgiveness"
  • 容赦(ようしゃ): mercy; leniency — formal; often in negative phrases like 容赦(ようしゃ)なく ("without mercy")
  • 勘弁(かんべん): pardoning; sparing — colloquial; often in 勘弁(かんべん)して "give me a break"
