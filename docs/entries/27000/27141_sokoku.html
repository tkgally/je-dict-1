祖国(そこく)

そこく
noun
homeland; fatherland; motherland
1. homeland; fatherland; motherland
The country where one was born or where one's ancestors originated. Carries a strong emotional and patriotic tone, often used in contexts of longing, loyalty, or sacrifice.
祖国(そこく)を離(はな)れた。
I left my homeland.
彼(かれ)は祖国(そこく)のために戦(たたか)った。
He fought for his homeland.
長(なが)い海外(かいがい)生活(せいかつ)を経(へ)て、ようやく祖国(そこく)の土(つち)を踏(ふ)むことができた。
After a long life overseas, I was finally able to set foot on the soil of my homeland.

Refers to one's country of origin with an emotional, often patriotic nuance. More literary and elevated than 国(くに) (country), which is the everyday word for one's home country.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 祖国(そこく)を愛(あい)する — to love one's homeland
  • 祖国(そこく)を離(はな)れる — to leave one's homeland
  • 祖国(そこく)に帰(かえ)る — to return to one's homeland
  • 祖国(そこく)の土(つち)を踏(ふ)む — to set foot on one's homeland
  • 祖国(そこく)のために — for one's homeland

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国(くに) — one's country; everyday, conversational word with no particular emotional weight
  • 母国(ぼこく) — mother country; similar meaning but slightly more neutral and often used in the context of language (母国語(ぼこくご), native language)
  • 故郷(ふるさと)/故郷(こきょう) — hometown; refers to a specific place of origin rather than a whole country
