1. grand opening; full-scale opening
The official, full-scale opening of a shop, mall, hotel, or other facility, often following a preliminary "soft opening" (プレオープン). Used in advertising and signage to mark the headline event.
来月(らいげつ)グランドオープンする。
It's having its grand opening next month.
グランドオープン記念(きねん)のセールが開催中(かいさいちゅう)だ。
A grand opening commemorative sale is going on.
プレオープンを経(へ)て、来月一日(らいげつついたち)にグランドオープンを迎(むか)える予定(よてい)だ。
After a soft opening, it is scheduled to have its grand opening on the first of next month.

Wasei-eigo from "grand" + "open." Used as a noun (and with する) to mark the official, full-scale opening of a store, mall, or facility. Distinguished from プレオープン (a soft/preview opening for testing operations).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • グランドオープンする: to have a grand opening
  • グランドオープンを迎(むか)える: to reach the grand opening
  • グランドオープン記念(きねん)セール: grand opening sale
  • グランドオープン日(び): grand opening day

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • プレオープン: soft opening; preview opening — limited operation before the grand opening, often invitation-only
  • 開店(かいてん): opening of a shop — native term; everyday register, doesn't carry the same promotional sense
  • 新装開店(しんそうかいてん): grand reopening after renovation
