フェイクファー

ふぇいくふぁー
noun
faux fur; fake fur — synthetic imitation of animal fur used in clothing and accessories (loanword from English 'fake fur')
1. faux fur; fake fur — a synthetic textile that imitates the look and feel of animal fur, used in clothing, accessories, and home furnishings
Loanword from English "fake fur," referring to a synthetic plush textile (typically acrylic, modacrylic, or polyester pile on a knit or woven backing) made to look and feel like animal fur. フェイクファー is a key term in modern Japanese fashion vocabulary, where it is contrasted with リアルファー (real fur). It has become the more socially acceptable choice as concerns about animal welfare and sustainability have grown. The marketing-friendly variant エコファー ("eco fur") refers to the same material with a more positive environmental framing.
フェイクファーのコート。
A faux-fur coat.
最近(さいきん)のファーはほとんどがフェイクファーだ。
Recent fur products are almost all faux fur.
動物愛護(どうぶつあいご)の観点(かんてん)から、フェイクファーを選(えら)ぶブランドが増(ふ)えている。
From an animal-welfare standpoint, more brands are choosing faux fur.
最近(さいきん)のフェイクファーは品質(ひんしつ)が高(たか)く、遠目(とおめ)には本物(ほんもの)の毛皮(けがわ)と見分(みわ)けがつかないものもある。
Recent faux fur is high in quality, and some types are indistinguishable from real fur at a distance.

フェイクファー is the loanword for "fake fur" — synthetic plush fabric that imitates animal fur. It is the standard term in contemporary Japanese fashion vocabulary, alongside near-synonyms with slightly different connotations.

MATERIAL:

  • Typically made of acrylic, modacrylic, or polyester pile fibers attached to a knit or woven backing
  • Designed to mimic the look, sheen, and softness of animal fur
  • Generally cheaper, easier to clean, and more durable than real fur

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • フェイクファーコート: faux-fur coat
  • フェイクファージャケット: faux-fur jacket
  • フェイクファーマフラー: faux-fur scarf
  • フェイクファーバッグ: faux-fur handbag
  • フェイクファー素材(そざい): faux-fur material
  • フェイクファー付(つ)き: trimmed with faux fur

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フェイクファーを使(つか)う: to use faux fur (in a product)
  • フェイクファーを選(えら)ぶ: to choose faux fur (over real fur)
  • フェイクファーに切(き)り替(か)える: to switch over to faux fur
  • フェイクファーで作(つく)られる: to be made of faux fur

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • ファー: fur (general fashion word; can mean either real or faux fur depending on context)
  • リアルファー: real fur — the explicit contrast term
  • エコファー: "eco fur" — Japanese marketing term for faux fur, emphasizing animal-friendliness
  • 毛皮(けがわ): animal fur (the general/formal Japanese word) — usually means real fur
  • 人工(じんこう)毛皮(けがわ): artificial fur — a more literal translation, sometimes used in technical contexts
  • フェイクレザー: faux leather — parallel construction in the same fashion vocabulary
  • 合成(ごうせい)皮革(ひかく): synthetic leather — the more formal Japanese equivalent of フェイクレザー

ETHICAL / FASHION CONTEXT:

  • Major Japanese and global brands have increasingly moved to use only faux fur
  • フェイクファー is often marketed positively as an ethical choice
  • The newer term エコファー emphasizes the environmental angle but refers to the same material
  • Real fur (リアルファー) is now associated more with vintage clothing and a smaller luxury market

USAGE NOTES:

  • Constructed in Japanese as フェイク ("fake") + ファー ("fur") — both elements are loanwords
  • Pronounced with two long vowels in the second element: ふぇいく + ふぁー
  • Although built from English elements, "fake fur" is a slightly less common phrasing in modern English than "faux fur" or "synthetic fur" — the Japanese reflects the older English term
