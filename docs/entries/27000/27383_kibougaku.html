希望額(きぼうがく)

きぼうがく
noun
desired amount; requested amount of money
1. desired amount; requested amount (of money)
The amount of money one wishes to receive, borrow, charge, or be paid. 希望額(きぼうがく) is a standard term in salary negotiations, loan applications, fundraising, donation requests, sales price negotiations, and similar contexts where one party states their preferred figure.
希望額(きぼうがく)を記入(きにゅう)する。
To fill in the desired amount.
融資(ゆうし)の希望額(きぼうがく)を聞(き)かれた。
I was asked the loan amount I wanted.
面接(めんせつ)では年収(ねんしゅう)の希望額(きぼうがく)も尋(たず)ねられた。
At the interview, I was also asked my desired annual salary.
売却(ばいきゃく)の希望額(きぼうがく)を相場(そうば)より高(たか)く設定(せってい)しすぎると、買(か)い手(て)がなかなか}つかない{恐(おそ)れがある。
If you set your asking price too far above market rates, there is a risk that buyers will be hard to find.

希望額(きぼうがく) is the amount of money one wishes to receive or pay. It appears most commonly on application forms, in negotiations, and in transaction listings.

FORMATION:

  • 希望(きぼう) ("hope, wish, desire") + 額(がく) ("amount of money") → 希望額(きぼうがく)

TYPICAL CONTEXTS:

  • 年収(ねんしゅう)の希望額(きぼうがく): desired annual salary (job applications, interviews)
  • 融資(ゆうし)の希望額(きぼうがく): requested loan amount
  • 売却(ばいきゃく)希望額(きぼうがく): desired sale price (real estate, used cars)
  • 寄付(きふ)の希望額(きぼうがく): suggested donation amount
  • 査定(さてい)希望額(きぼうがく): desired appraisal amount

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 希望額(きぼうがく)を記入(きにゅう)する: to fill in the desired amount (on a form)
  • 希望額(きぼうがく)を伝(つた)える: to communicate the desired amount
  • 希望額(きぼうがく)を下回(したまわ)る: to fall below the requested amount
  • 希望額(きぼうがく)を下(さ)げる / 上(あ)げる: to lower / raise one's requested amount
  • 希望額(きぼうがく)に届(とど)かない: to not reach the requested amount

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 希望(きぼう)価格(かかく): desired price — used in sales contexts; closely related but emphasizes the price tag of an item rather than a sum of money in general
  • 希望(きぼう)年収(ねんしゅう): desired annual income (a frequent collocation in job applications)
  • 見積(みつ)もり額(がく): estimated/quoted amount — what is offered, not what is requested
  • 提示(ていじ)額(がく): offered amount, the amount put forth (e.g., by an employer or buyer)
  • 目安(めやす): rough guideline, ballpark figure — softer than 希望額(きぼうがく)
  • 予算(よさん): budget — the amount one has set aside, not necessarily what one wants

USAGE NOTES:

  • On Japanese application and questionnaire forms, you will frequently see fields labeled simply 希望額(きぼうがく) with a numeric input.
  • 希望額(きぼうがく) is neutral in formality; it is used in business letters, contracts, and form labels alike.
