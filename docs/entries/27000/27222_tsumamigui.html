1. sneaking a taste; nibbling; pilfering food
Secretly eating small amounts of food, especially before a meal is ready or from food that isn't meant for you.
料理(りょうり)中(ちゅう)につまみ食(ぐ)いをした。
I snuck a taste while cooking.
ケーキのつまみ食(ぐ)いがばれて怒(おこ)られた。
I got scolded when I was caught sneaking bites of the cake.
子供(こども)たちが冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)の中(なか)のおかずをつまみ食(ぐ)いしていた。
The kids were sneaking bites of the side dishes in the fridge.
2. sampling superficially; dabbling; cherry-picking
Figuratively, trying things only partially or selectively without committing to any one thing.
知識(ちしき)のつまみ食(ぐ)いでは本当(ほんとう)の力(ちから)はつかない。
You can't develop real ability by just dabbling in knowledge.
いろいろな趣味(しゅみ)をつまみ食(ぐ)いしているだけだ。
I'm just dabbling in various hobbies.
情報(じょうほう)をつまみ食(ぐ)いするのではなく、体系的(たいけいてき)に学(まな)ぶべきだ。
Rather than cherry-picking information, you should study systematically.

From つまむ (to pinch/pick) + 食(く)い (eating). The image is of pinching small pieces of food and popping them in your mouth. Can be used with する as a suru-verb. The literal sense is playful and relatable, while the figurative sense carries a mildly critical tone about lacking depth or commitment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • つまみ食(ぐ)いする: to sneak a taste; to nibble
  • つまみ食(ぐ)いがばれる: to get caught sneaking food
  • 知識(ちしき)のつまみ食(ぐ)い: superficial sampling of knowledge
  • つまみ食(ぐ)い程度(ていど): only dabbling level

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 味見(あじみ): tasting; sampling — done openly and with purpose (e.g., a cook tasting a dish); no sneaking implied
  • 試食(ししょく): food sampling — formally offered samples at a store
  • 盗(ぬす)み食(ぐ)い: stealing food — more serious than つまみ食(ぐ)い
