油彩(ゆさい)

ゆさい
noun
oil painting
1. oil painting; painting in oils
A painting technique using oil-based pigments, or a work of art created using this technique. Refers to both the method and the finished work.
油彩(ゆさい)で風景(ふうけい)を描(えが)く。
To paint a landscape in oils.
この美術館(びじゅつかん)には油彩(ゆさい)の名作(めいさく)が多(おお)い。
This art museum has many masterpieces in oil painting.
彼女(かのじょ)は水彩(すいさい)よりも油彩(ゆさい)のほうが得意(とくい)だと言(い)っていた。
She said she's better at oil painting than watercolor.

A compound of 油(あぶら) (oil) and 彩(さい) (coloring, painting). Often contrasted with 水彩(すいさい) (watercolor). The longer form 油彩画(ゆさいが) specifically refers to an oil painting as a finished work, while 油彩(ゆさい) can refer to either the technique or the work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 油彩画(ゆさいが) — oil painting (the artwork)
  • 油彩(ゆさい)で描(えが)く — to paint in oils
  • 油彩(ゆさい)技法(ぎほう) — oil painting technique
  • 油彩(ゆさい)絵(え)の具(ぐ) — oil paint

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 油絵(あぶらえ) — oil painting; the everyday word, more common in casual conversation
  • 水彩(すいさい) — watercolor; painting with water-soluble pigments
  • 日本画(にほんが) — Japanese-style painting; traditional painting using mineral pigments and sumi ink
