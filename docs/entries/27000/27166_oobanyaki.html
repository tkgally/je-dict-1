大判焼(おおばんや)き

おおばんやき
noun
large round filled cake; imagawayaki
1. large round filled cake
A round, flat Japanese cake made from batter cooked in a circular mold and filled with sweet bean paste or other fillings.
大判焼(おおばんや)きを二(ふた)つください。
Two oobanyaki, please.
屋台(やたい)で焼(や)きたての大判焼(おおばんや)きを買(か)った。
I bought freshly baked oobanyaki at a street stall.
この店(みせ)の大判焼(おおばんや)きはクリーム入(い)りが一番(いちばん)人気(にんき)だ。
At this shop, the cream-filled oobanyaki is the most popular.

A popular Japanese street food consisting of a round cake made from a wheat flour batter, cooked in a special iron mold and filled with 餡(あん) (sweet red bean paste), custard cream, or savory fillings. The name 大判(おおばん) refers to a large oval gold coin from the Edo period, which the cake's shape resembles.

COMMON FILLINGS:

  • 小倉(おぐら)餡(あん): sweet red bean paste (classic)
  • カスタードクリーム: custard cream
  • 白(しろ)餡(あん): white bean paste
  • チョコレート: chocolate

REGIONAL NAMES:

This food is known by many names depending on the region. The variety of names is a well-known topic in Japanese culture:

  • 今川焼(いまがわや)き: the most widely recognized name, used in the Kanto region
  • 回転焼(かいてんや)き: used in Kansai and western Japan
  • おやき: used in parts of Hokkaido and Tohoku
  • 太鼓(たいこ)まんじゅう: used in some areas

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • たい焼(や)き: a fish-shaped filled cake — uses similar batter and filling but shaped like a sea bream
  • どら焼(や)き: a pancake-like cake with bean paste — two flat rounds sandwiching the filling rather than a single molded cake
