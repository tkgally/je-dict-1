だけども

だけども
conjunction
but; however; though
1. but; however; though (a slightly more emphatic variant of だけど)
A casual conjunction used to introduce a statement that contrasts with or qualifies what was just said. Functionally close to だけど, but with the extra も giving a slight feeling of "and yet…" or "…even so." Common in conversational and online writing; rare in formal documents.
安(やす)い。だけども、品質(ひんしつ)は悪(わる)くない。
It's cheap. Still, the quality isn't bad.
彼(かれ)は忙(いそが)しい。だけども、毎日(まいにち)連絡(れんらく)をくれる。
He's busy. But even so, he keeps in touch with me every day.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っていた。だけども、試合(しあい)は予定通(よていどお)り行(おこな)われた。
It was raining. But even so, the game went ahead as scheduled.
何度(なんど)も挑戦(ちょうせん)してうまくいかなかった。だけども、諦(あきら)めずに続(つづ)けていれば、いつかきっと結果(けっか)が出(で)ると信(しん)じている。
I've tried many times and it hasn't worked out. Even so, I believe that if I keep going without giving up, the results will come someday.

だけども is a casual conjunction used to introduce a contrasting statement, very close in meaning and feel to だけど. The extra も lends it a slightly stronger "…and yet," "…even so" nuance, similar to the relationship between けれど and けれども.

FORMALITY VARIANTS (most casual → most formal):

  • だけど: casual
  • だけども: casual, slightly more emphatic than だけど
  • だけれど: slightly more formal than だけど
  • だけれども: full, formal form
  • ですけれども: polite formal

USAGE:

  • Most often appears at the start of a new sentence after a period ("…。だけども、…") rather than mid-clause.
  • Can also trail at the end of a clause to soften the statement: 〜のだけども、… ("…though, but…").

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • だけど: but (more neutral/casual; the most common option in conversation)
  • けれども: but (attaches to verb/adjective stems; slightly more polite)
  • でも: but (general-purpose casual conjunction; can begin a sentence)
  • しかし: however (more formal, written)
  • ただし: however / provided that (introduces a qualification or condition)

USAGE NOTES:

  • だけども feels slightly more colloquial and warm than the longer だけれども; speakers may prefer it when they want a softer, more chatty tone in casual speech or messaging.
  • It is rarely seen in formal written contexts (essays, business documents); use しかし or だけれども there instead.
