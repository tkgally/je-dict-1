冬物(ふゆもの)

ふゆもの
noun
winter clothing; winter goods
1. winter clothing; winter wear; winter goods
Items, especially clothing, that are designed or sold for use in winter. Most commonly refers to coats, sweaters, and other warm clothes, but can also cover bedding and seasonal merchandise.
冬物(ふゆもの)のセールが始(はじ)まった。
The winter-clothing sale has started.
冬物(ふゆもの)のコートをクリーニングに出(だ)した。
I sent my winter coat out for dry cleaning.
春(はる)が近(ちか)づいたので、冬物(ふゆもの)を押(お)し入(い)れにしまった。
Since spring is approaching, I put away my winter clothes in the closet.

A general label for winter-season items, especially clothing. Often paired with verbs about storing, taking out, or shopping for them as the seasons change.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冬物(ふゆもの)を出(だ)す: to take out winter clothes
  • 冬物(ふゆもの)をしまう: to put winter clothes away
  • 冬物(ふゆもの)のコート: a winter coat
  • 冬物(ふゆもの)セール: winter clearance sale

RELATED FORMS:

  • 夏物(なつもの): summer clothing
  • 春物(はるもの): spring clothing
  • 秋物(あきもの): fall clothing
