クレーン

くれーん
noun
crane (machine)
1. crane; mechanical crane used for lifting and moving heavy objects
A machine with a long arm used for lifting, lowering, and moving heavy loads on construction sites, in factories, and at ports.
クレーンで荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ち上(あ)げた。
The cargo was lifted with a crane.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)に大(おお)きなクレーンが立(た)っている。
A large crane is standing at the construction site.
クレーンの操作(そうさ)には専門(せんもん)の免許(めんきょ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Operating a crane requires a specialized license.

From English "crane." Refers exclusively to the machine — not to the bird, which is 鶴(つる) in Japanese. Very common in contexts related to construction, ports, and factories.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クレーン車(しゃ): crane truck; mobile crane
  • クレーンゲーム: crane game; claw machine (arcade game)
  • タワークレーン: tower crane
  • クレーンで吊(つ)り上(あ)げる: to hoist with a crane
  • クレーンの操作(そうさ): crane operation

CULTURAL NOTE:
クレーンゲーム (crane game) is the standard Japanese term for the ubiquitous arcade claw machines found throughout Japan. Also called UFOキャッチャー after Sega's brand name.
