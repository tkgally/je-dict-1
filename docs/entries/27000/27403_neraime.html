狙(ねら)い目(め)

ねらいめ
noun
(good) target; the right thing/time/place to aim for; a sweet spot or good opportunity
1. (good) target; sweet spot; the option, timing, or place that is the smart choice to aim for
The point at which something is most worth aiming for — the sweet spot in an opportunity, a wise choice, or a strategically good time or place. 狙(ねら)い目(め) combines 狙(ねら)い ("aim") with 目(め) (here in its abstract "point, spot" sense, as in 境(さかい)目(め) "boundary point" or 見(み)せ場(ば)). It is a common everyday word in shopping, travel, dieting, investing, and exam-strategy contexts: "the smart move," "the angle to play," "the right time to go."
平日(へいじつ)の昼(ひる)が狙(ねら)い目(め)だ。
Weekday afternoons are the sweet spot.
セール初日(しょにち)が一番(いちばん)の狙(ねら)い目(め)。
The first day of the sale is the prime time to aim for.
観光地(かんこうち)は混雑(こんざつ)を避(さ)けたいなら、早朝(そうちょう)が狙(ねら)い目(め)だ。
For tourist spots, if you want to avoid the crowds, early morning is the time to aim for.
中古(ちゅうこ)車(しゃ)を安(やす)く買(か)いたい人(ひと)にとっては、新車(しんしゃ)のモデル}{チェンジ}{直後(ちょくご)が狙(ねら)い目(め)と言(い)われている。
For people who want to buy a used car cheaply, the time just after a new-car model change is said to be the sweet spot.

狙(ねら)い目(め) is an everyday noun meaning "the smart spot to aim for" — the timing, option, or place that offers the best opportunity. It is widely used in shopping, travel, exam-taking, dieting, and investing contexts.

FORMATION:

  • 狙(ねら)い ("aim, target") + 目(め) (here in the abstract sense of "point, spot, angle") → "a point worth aiming for"
  • The 目(め) here is the same suffix found in 境(さかい)目(め) ("boundary point"), 結(む)び目(め) ("knot point"), 見栄(みば)え ("appearance")

TYPICAL CONTEXTS:

  • Shopping: "X is the smart deal" — セール初日(しょにち)が狙(ねら)い目(め)
  • Travel: "X is the best time/place" — 早朝(そうちょう)が狙(ねら)い目(め)
  • Real estate: "X areas are the buying opportunity" — 駅(えき)近(ちか)物件(ぶっけん)が狙(ねら)い目(め)
  • Exams: "X questions are the angle to attack" — 過去(かこ)問(もん)頻出(ひんしゅつ)の単元(たんげん)が狙(ねら)い目(め)
  • Job hunting: "X industries / companies are the play" — 中堅(ちゅうけん)企業(きぎょう)が狙(ねら)い目(め)
  • Investing: "X stocks are the opportunity" — 成長(せいちょう)株(かぶ)が狙(ねら)い目(め)

GRAMMATICAL BEHAVIOR:

  • Most commonly as a predicate: 〜が狙(ねら)い目(め)だ ("~ is the sweet spot")
  • Modifier: 〜の狙(ねら)い目(め) ("the sweet spot of ~")
  • Asking question: 狙(ねら)い目(め)はどこ? / いつ? ("Where / when is the sweet spot?")

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)は〜だ: the sweet spot is ~
  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)を探(さが)す: to look for the sweet spot
  • 狙(ねら)い目(め)を見(み){つ}ける: to find the sweet spot
  • 一番(いちばん)の狙(ねら)い目(め): the best sweet spot, the prime opportunity
  • 買(か)い時(どき)の狙(ねら)い目(め): the sweet spot for when to buy
  • 〜が狙(ねら)い目(め): ~ is the sweet spot/the right play

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 買(か)い時(どき): the right time to buy — specific to purchasing decisions
  • 穴場(あなば): hidden gem, lesser-known good spot — emphasizes secrecy / uncrowdedness
  • チャンス: chance, opportunity (loanword) — broader and more neutral
  • 好機(こうき): good opportunity (formal/written)
  • ベスト(タイミング(たいみんぐ)): the best timing
  • 見(み)所(どころ): a point worth seeing, the highlight — different sense (something worth watching)
  • 目(め)星(ぼし): a guess, a target one has in mind — related compound with 目(め)

USAGE NOTES:

  • 狙(ねら)い目(め) carries a slightly strategic, savvy connotation — the speaker is suggesting that they have analyzed the situation and identified the smart angle.
  • It is common in lifestyle and consumer journalism ("5 watches that are the sweet spot for first-time buyers") and in conversation about shopping, travel, and timing.
  • Pronunciation: ねらいめ} (four morae). The verb {狙(ねら)う means "to aim, target."
