1. snatching away; stealing; intercepting; seizing what belongs to another
The act of taking something that rightfully belongs to someone else, especially by swooping in from the side. Implies unfairness and opportunism.
手柄(てがら)を横取(よこど)りされた。
Someone stole the credit for my work.
猫(ねこ)に魚(さかな)を横取(よこど)りされた。
The cat snatched my fish.
彼(かれ)はいつも人(ひと)のアイデアを横取(よこど)りして自分(じぶん)のものにする。
He always steals other people's ideas and claims them as his own.

From 横(よこ) (side/lateral) + 取(と)り (taking). The image is of someone coming from the side to snatch something away — an unexpected, unfair taking. Can be used with する as a suru-verb. Always carries a negative nuance of unfairness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手柄(てがら)を横取(よこど)りする: to steal credit
  • 獲物(えもの)を横取(よこど)りする: to steal someone's prey/prize
  • 客(きゃく)を横取(よこど)りする: to poach a customer
  • ～に横取(よこど)りされる: to have something snatched by...

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 奪(うば)う: to seize; to rob — a more forceful, direct taking
  • 掠(かす)め取(と)る: to snatch; to swipe — emphasizes speed and stealth
  • 盗(ぬす)む: to steal — the general word for stealing
  • 横領(おうりょう): embezzlement — a legal/formal term for misappropriating entrusted assets
