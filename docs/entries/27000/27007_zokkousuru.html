1. to continue; to carry on; to proceed with
To keep going with an activity or process, especially when there is reason to stop. Implies deliberate continuation despite obstacles or questions about whether to stop.
試合(しあい)は雨(あめ)の中(なか)続行(ぞっこう)された。
The game continued in the rain.
調査(ちょうさ)は引(ひ)き続(つづ)き続行(ぞっこう)する方針(ほうしん)だ。
The policy is to continue the investigation.
反対意見(はんたいいけん)があったが、計画(けいかく)は予定通(よていどお)り続行(ぞっこう)することになった。
Despite opposing opinions, it was decided to carry on with the plan as scheduled.

A more formal and deliberate word than 続(つづ)ける. Emphasizes the decision to keep going rather than the simple act of continuing. Often used in official contexts (meetings, games, investigations, operations).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 審議(しんぎ)を続行(ぞっこう)する: to continue deliberations
  • 作戦(さくせん)を続行(ぞっこう)する: to continue the operation
  • 裁判(さいばん)を続行(ぞっこう)する: to continue a trial
  • 営業(えいぎょう)を続行(ぞっこう)する: to continue business operations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 続(つづ)ける: to continue — the general, everyday word for continuing; no nuance of perseverance against obstacles
  • 継続(けいぞく)する: to continue; to maintain — focuses on sustained continuation over time rather than a deliberate decision to proceed
  • 遂行(すいこう)する: to carry out; to execute — focuses on completing a task, not on the decision to keep going
