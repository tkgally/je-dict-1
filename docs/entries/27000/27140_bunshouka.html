1. putting into writing; documenting; converting into written form
The act of expressing ideas, rules, or information in written form.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)を文章化(ぶんしょうか)する。
To put the meeting's contents into writing.
口頭(こうとう)での約束(やくそく)を文章化(ぶんしょうか)しておいた方(ほう)がいい。
It's better to put verbal agreements into writing.
暗黙(あんもく)のルールを文章化(ぶんしょうか)して全員(ぜんいん)に共有(きょうゆう)した。
We documented the unwritten rules and shared them with everyone.

Combines 文章(ぶんしょう) (writing/text) and 化(か) (transformation/conversion). The 化(か) suffix is highly productive and creates verbs meaning "to convert to" or "to make into." 文章化(ぶんしょうか) is commonly used in business and organizational contexts when formalizing rules, procedures, or agreements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文章化(ぶんしょうか)する: to put into writing, to document
  • ルールを文章化(ぶんしょうか)する: to document rules
  • 文章化(ぶんしょうか)された規則(きそく): documented rules

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 文書化(ぶんしょか): documentation — very close synonym, slightly more formal/bureaucratic
  • 書面化(しょめんか): putting into written form — emphasizes the official document
  • 言語化(げんごか): verbalization — putting thoughts into words (spoken or written)
