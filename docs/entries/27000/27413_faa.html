ファー

ふぁー
noun
fur
1. fur (typically for clothing or trim)
Loanword from English 'fur'. Used in fashion and retail contexts for animal fur or fur-like materials on coats, collars, hats, and accessories. Does not refer to fur on a live animal — that is 毛(け) or 毛皮(けがわ).
ファーの付(つ)いたコート。
A coat with fur on it.
襟(えり)のファーが暖(あたた)かい。
The fur on the collar is warm.
最近(さいきん)は動物(どうぶつ)愛護(あいご)の観点(かんてん)からフェイクファーを選(えら)ぶ人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently, more people are choosing faux fur from an animal welfare perspective.

Loanword from English "fur," used mainly in fashion contexts for trim, lining, or accessories made of fur or fur-like material. The native word 毛皮(けがわ) refers to fur pelts more broadly and is still common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ファーコート: fur coat
  • ファーの襟(えり): fur collar
  • ファー付(つ)き: with fur attached
  • リアルファー: real fur
  • フェイクファー / エコファー: faux fur / eco-fur

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 毛皮(けがわ): fur, pelt — native term, broader; emphasizes the whole hide
  • 毛(け): hair, fur — used for an animal's coat itself
  • ボア: boa (fluffy fabric) — synthetic plush, often used like ファー in casual fashion
