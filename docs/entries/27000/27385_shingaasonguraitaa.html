シンガーソングライター

しんがーそんぐらいたー
noun
singer-songwriter (a musician who writes and performs their own songs)
1. singer-songwriter; a musician who writes and performs their own songs
A musician who both composes and performs their own songs — typically writing their own lyrics and music and singing them themselves. The term entered Japanese music journalism in the 1970s with the rise of folk and adult-contemporary singer-songwriters and is now standard vocabulary for anyone whose performances feature their own original material.
有名(ゆうめい)なシンガーソングライター。
A famous singer-songwriter.
彼女(かのじょ)は自分(じぶん)で曲(きょく)を書(か)くシンガーソングライターだ。
She is a singer-songwriter who writes her own songs.
若手(わかて)のシンガーソングライターが大(おお)きな注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めている。
A young singer-songwriter is drawing major attention.
ライブハウスで演奏(えんそう)を続(つづ)けながら自作(じさく)の曲(きょく)を磨(みが)いてきたシンガーソングライターが、ついにメジャーデビューを果(は)たした。
A singer-songwriter who has been polishing her original songs while continuing to play at live music venues has finally made her major-label debut.

シンガーソングライター is the English compound "singer-songwriter" naturalized into Japanese. It is the standard term in Japanese music writing for an artist who writes and performs their own songs.

IMPLIED MEANING:

  • The artist writes both lyrics (作詞(さくし)) and music (作曲(さっきょく))
  • The artist also performs the songs themselves
  • Often associated with acoustic, folk, J-pop, and adult-contemporary genres, but used across all genres including rock, hip-hop, and electronic

ABBREVIATION:

  • Frequently abbreviated as SSW in Japanese music journalism (e.g., "若手(わかて)SSW" = "young singer-songwriters")

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 女性(じょせい)シンガーソングライター: female singer-songwriter
  • 若手(わかて)シンガーソングライター: young/up-and-coming singer-songwriter
  • ベテランシンガーソングライター: veteran singer-songwriter
  • シンガーソングライターとして活躍(かつやく)する: to be active as a singer-songwriter
  • シンガーソングライターを目指(めざ)す: to aim to become a singer-songwriter
  • 弾(ひ)き語(がた)りのシンガーソングライター: a singer-songwriter who accompanies herself on guitar/piano

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 作詞(さくし)家(か): lyricist — someone who writes lyrics (often for other performers)
  • 作曲(さっきょく)家(か): composer — someone who writes music
  • 歌手(かしゅ): singer (general) — does not imply songwriting
  • アーティスト: artist (loanword) — the broader, more flexible term covering all sorts of musicians and performers
  • ミュージシャン: musician (loanword) — emphasizes instrumental skill; partly overlaps with アーティスト
  • 弾(ひ)き語(がた)り: self-accompanied performance — describes the performance style often associated with singer-songwriters but is a separate concept

USAGE NOTES:

  • A pop idol who sings songs written by professional songwriting teams is not typically called a シンガーソングライター — the term implies meaningful authorship of one's material.
  • The katakana spelling is invariant; you will not see this written in kanji. Pronunciation reflects the English origin: しんがー・そんぐ・らいたー.
