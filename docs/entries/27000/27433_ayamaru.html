1. to err; to make a mistake; to be wrong
To do something incorrectly or to reach the wrong conclusion. Distinct from 謝(あやま)る (to apologize), which sounds identical but is written with a different kanji.
判断(はんだん)を誤(あやま)った。
I made a misjudgment.
道(みち)を誤(あやま)ると大変(たいへん)なことになる。
If you take the wrong path, things will turn serious.
選択(せんたく)を誤(あやま)らないように慎重(しんちょう)に考(かんが)えた方(ほう)がいい。
You should think carefully so as not to make the wrong choice.

Transitive godan verb (誤(あやま)る・誤(あやま)って・誤(あやま)った). More formal and written than 間違(まちが)える. The passive form 誤(あやま)られる is uncommon; the participial 誤(あやま)った is frequently used as a modifier ('mistaken X').

TRANSITIVITY:

Transitive: marks what is gotten wrong with を.

  • 判断(はんだん)を誤(あやま)る: to misjudge
  • 道(みち)を誤(あやま)る: to take the wrong path (also figurative: 'go astray')

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判断(はんだん)を誤(あやま)る: to misjudge
  • 選択(せんたく)を誤(あやま)る: to choose wrongly
  • 誤(あやま)った情報(じょうほう): incorrect information
  • 使(つか)い方(かた)を誤(あやま)る: to use incorrectly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 間違(まちが)える: to make a mistake — much more common in everyday speech; broader scope
  • 謝(あやま)る: to apologize — same reading, different kanji and meaning
  • 失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail — focuses on a failed outcome, not the wrong choice itself
