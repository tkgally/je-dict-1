ベンチプレス

べんちぷれす
noun
bench press (weight training)
1. bench press (a weight-training exercise performed lying on a bench)
A common upper-body strength exercise in which the lifter lies on their back on a bench and pushes a weighted barbell or dumbbells upward, primarily working the chest, shoulders, and triceps. Used both as the name of the exercise and, by extension, as a する verb meaning "to do bench presses."
ベンチプレスをする。
I do bench presses.
彼(かれ)はベンチプレスで百(ひゃく)キロ上(あ)げる。
He bench-presses 100 kilograms.
初心者(しょしんしゃ)はまずバーベルなしでベンチプレスのフォームを練習(れんしゅう)する。
Beginners first practice bench press form without a barbell.
毎週(まいしゅう)二回(にかい)ジムに通(かよ)って、ベンチプレスとスクワットを中心(ちゅうしん)に鍛(きた)えている。
I go to the gym twice a week and train mainly with bench presses and squats.

ベンチプレス is a direct loanword from English "bench press," used both for the exercise itself and (with する) as a verb meaning to perform the exercise. The word appears throughout fitness magazines, gym signage, sports broadcasts, and everyday conversation about working out.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ベンチプレスをする: to do (a) bench press
  • ベンチプレスで〜キロ上(あ)げる: to bench-press X kilograms
  • ベンチプレスの記録(きろく): bench press record / personal best
  • ベンチプレスのフォーム: bench press form / technique
  • 重量(じゅうりょう)挙(あ)げのベンチプレス: bench press as a weight-lifting event

RELATED TERMS:

  • スクワット: squat
  • デッドリフト: deadlift
  • バーベル: barbell
  • ダンベル: dumbbell
  • 筋(きん)トレ: strength / weight training (short for 筋肉(きんにく)訓練(くんれん))
  • ジム: gym

USAGE NOTES:

  • ベンチプレス is a noun in Japanese grammar; to express the action you usually say ベンチプレスをする ("do a bench press") rather than treating it as a stand-alone verb.
  • For talking about how much someone can lift, the typical pattern is ベンチプレスで〜キロ上(あ)げる ("to lift X kg in the bench press").
