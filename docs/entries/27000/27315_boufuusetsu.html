暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)

ぼうふうせつ
noun
blizzard; severe snowstorm
1. blizzard; severe snowstorm with strong winds
A meteorological term for a severe winter storm combining heavy snow with strong, often dangerous winds. The formal term used in weather warnings and news in Japan.
暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)警報(けいほう)が出(で)た。
A blizzard warning has been issued.
暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)のため、学校(がっこう)は休(やす)みになった。
Because of the blizzard, school was canceled.
北海道(ほっかいどう)の各地(かくち)では暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)により交通(こうつう)が乱(みだ)れている。
Across Hokkaido, transportation is being disrupted by blizzards.
気象庁(きしょうちょう)は、今夜(こんや)から明日(あす)にかけて暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)に注意(ちゅうい)するよう呼(よ)びかけている。
The Meteorological Agency is calling for caution against blizzards from tonight through tomorrow.

暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ) combines 暴風(ぼうふう) (violent wind, gale) and 雪(ゆき) (snow). It is the standard meteorological/news term for a blizzard — heavy snow accompanied by strong winds severe enough to cause poor visibility and travel hazards.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)警報(けいほう): blizzard warning (high-level alert)
  • 暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)注意(ちゅうい)報(ほう): blizzard advisory (lower-level alert)
  • 暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)に(に)見舞(みま)われる: to be hit by a blizzard
  • 暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ)の(の)ため: due to the blizzard
  • 猛烈(もうれつ)な暴風雪(ぼうふうせつ): a fierce blizzard

REGISTER:

  • Formal / news / official weather forecast
  • In casual conversation, the more general 吹雪(ふぶき) is far more common

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吹雪(ふぶき): blizzard, snowstorm — everyday term used in conversation, song lyrics, and literature
  • 大雪(おおゆき): heavy snowfall (heavy snow but not necessarily with strong winds)
  • 地吹雪(じふぶき): blowing snow (existing snow being blown across the ground by wind)
  • 暴風(ぼうふう): gale, violent wind (without snow)
  • 暴風雨(ぼうふうう): rainstorm (analogous formation with rain instead of snow)

DOMAIN: Used in weather forecasts, public-safety announcements, and news reports — most relevant to northern Japan, especially Hokkaido and the Sea of Japan coastal regions.
