1. major eruption; massive volcanic eruption
A large-scale volcanic eruption, typically one significant enough to be reported in the news or remembered historically. Formed by prefixing 大(だい)- ("big, major") to 噴火(ふんか) ("eruption"). Frequently used both for currently occurring large eruptions and for historical references such as 富士山(ふじさん)の大噴火(だいふんか).
富士山(ふじさん)の大噴火(だいふんか)が予想(よそう)されている。
A major eruption of Mt. Fuji is anticipated.
江戸時代(えどじだい)にこの火山(かざん)は大噴火(だいふんか)した。
This volcano underwent a major eruption during the Edo period.
大噴火(だいふんか)が起(お)きれば、広範囲(こうはんい)に火山灰(かざんばい)が降(ふ)り積(つ)もる。
If a major eruption occurs, volcanic ash will fall over a wide area.
過去(かこ)の大噴火(だいふんか)の記録(きろく)を調(しら)べることで、次(つぎ)にいつ発生(はっせい)しうるかを推測(すいそく)している。
By examining records of past major eruptions, scientists are estimating when the next one might occur.

大噴火(だいふんか) refers to a large-scale, often catastrophic volcanic eruption. The prefix 大(だい)- attaches to 噴火(ふんか) to mean "big eruption," and the resulting compound functions as both a noun and a する-verb.

FORMATION:

  • 大(だい)- ("big, major," prefix) + 噴火(ふんか) ("eruption") → 大噴火(だいふんか)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大噴火(だいふんか)が起(お)きる / 発生(はっせい)する: a major eruption occurs
  • 大噴火(だいふんか)する: to undergo a major eruption
  • 大噴火(だいふんか)の恐(おそ)れ: the risk of a major eruption
  • 歴史(れきし)的(てき)な大噴火(だいふんか): a historic major eruption
  • 大噴火(だいふんか)の被害(ひがい): damage from a major eruption

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 噴火(ふんか): eruption (the general term, regardless of scale)
  • 小規模(しょうきぼ)噴火(ふんか): small-scale eruption
  • 破局(はきょく)噴火(ふんか): catastrophic / cataclysmic eruption (specialist term for the very largest eruptions)
  • 噴火口(ふんかこう): volcanic vent
  • 火砕流(かさいりゅう): pyroclastic flow

USAGE NOTES:

  • The 大(だい)- prefix is highly productive in Japanese and attaches readily to many event nouns: 大地震(おおじしん) ("big earthquake"), 大津波(おおつなみ) ("big tsunami"), 大事故(だいじこ) ("major accident"). Note that the reading of 大(だい)- can vary (だい vs. おお) depending on the word.
  • As a する-verb, 大噴火(だいふんか)する takes the volcano as the subject: 火山(かざん)が大噴火(だいふんか)した.
  • In disaster-prevention contexts, 大噴火(だいふんか) is often paired with verbs like 備(そな)える ("to prepare for") and 予測(よそく)する ("to predict").
