1. prolonging; stalling; delaying; dragging out
Deliberately extending the duration of something, often to avoid a decision or outcome. Usually carries a negative nuance of unnecessary delay.
交渉(こうしょう)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばしは許(ゆる)されない。
Stalling the negotiations is unacceptable.
時間(じかん)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばしを図(はか)っているようだ。
It seems like they're trying to buy time.
これ以上(いじょう)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばしは事態(じたい)を悪化(あっか)させるだけだ。
Further delay will only make the situation worse.
2. enlargement; blowing up
Making something physically larger, especially enlarging a photograph or image.
写真(しゃしん)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばしを頼(たの)んだ。
I requested an enlargement of the photograph.
この画像(がぞう)を引(ひ)き延(の)ばしても画質(がしつ)が落(お)ちない。
Even when this image is enlarged, the quality doesn't degrade.
集合写真(しゅうごうしゃしん)を引(ひ)き延(の)ばしにして額(がく)に入(い)れた。
I had the group photo enlarged and framed.

The noun form of the compound verb 引(ひ)き延(の)ばす (to stretch out / to enlarge). The two senses reflect the literal meaning of "stretching" applied in different domains — stretching time (stalling) and stretching an image (enlarging).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばし: stalling for time
  • 引(ひ)き延(の)ばし作戦(さくせん): a stalling tactic
  • 引(ひ)き延(の)ばし工作(こうさく): a delaying maneuver
  • 写真(しゃしん)の引(ひ)き延(の)ばし: photo enlargement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先送(さきおく)り: postponement; putting off — focusing on deferring to a later date
  • 後回(あとまわ)し: putting off until later — similar to 先送(さきおく)り but more informal
  • 延長(えんちょう): extension; prolongation — neutral term for making something longer; lacks the negative "stalling" nuance
  • 拡大(かくだい): enlargement; expansion — more commonly used for digital image scaling
