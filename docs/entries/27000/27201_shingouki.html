信号機(しんごうき)

しんごうき
noun
traffic light; signal device
1. traffic light; traffic signal
A device installed at intersections and pedestrian crossings that uses colored lights to control traffic flow.
信号機(しんごうき)が赤(あか)になった。
The traffic light turned red.
あの交差点(こうさてん)には信号機(しんごうき)がない。
There is no traffic light at that intersection.
歩行者用(ほこうしゃよう)の信号機(しんごうき)が青(あお)に変(か)わるまで待(ま)ってください。
Please wait until the pedestrian signal turns green.
2. signal device; signaling equipment
More broadly, any mechanical or electronic device used to send signals, such as railway signals.
鉄道(てつどう)の信号機(しんごうき)が故障(こしょう)した。
The railway signal device broke down.
船(ふね)の信号機(しんごうき)で位置(いち)を知(し)らせる。
To communicate position using a ship's signaling device.
この区間(くかん)では自動(じどう)の信号機(しんごうき)が列車(れっしゃ)の運行(うんこう)を管理(かんり)している。
In this section, automatic signal devices manage train operations.

Refers specifically to the physical device, as opposed to 信号(しんごう) which refers to the signal itself or is used colloquially for traffic lights in general. In everyday speech, most people say 信号(しんごう) when talking about traffic lights, while 信号機(しんごうき) is the more precise, technical term for the actual equipment. Composed of 信号(しんごう) (signal) + 機(き) (machine/device).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信号機(しんごうき)の設置(せっち): installation of a traffic light
  • 信号機(しんごうき)の故障(こしょう): traffic light malfunction
  • 歩行者用(ほこうしゃよう)信号機(しんごうき): pedestrian signal
  • 押(お)しボタン式(しき)信号機(しんごうき): push-button traffic light
  • LED信号機(しんごうき): LED traffic light

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 信号(しんごう): signal; traffic light — the everyday word for traffic lights; 信号機(しんごうき) emphasizes the physical device
  • 標識(ひょうしき): sign; road sign — a fixed sign rather than a light-based signal

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, the green traffic light is traditionally called 青(あお) (blue) rather than 緑(みどり) (green), reflecting the broader Japanese color category where 青(あお) historically encompassed both blue and green.
