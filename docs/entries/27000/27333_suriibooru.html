スリーボール

すりーぼーる
noun
(baseball) three balls (count of 3-0, 3-1, or 3-2)
1. (baseball) a count of three balls
In baseball, the count when the batter has taken three pitches called as balls. From this count, one more ball results in a walk (フォアボール), so a スリーボール count puts the pitcher under pressure. Often used as part of the full count phrase スリーボール・ツーストライク (a 3-2 "full" count).
カウントはスリーボール。
The count is three balls.
スリーボールから投(な)げるのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to pitch from a three-ball count.
スリーボール・ツーストライクのフルカウントになった。
It became a full count of three balls and two strikes.
スリーボールに追(お)い込(こ)まれたピッチャーは慎重(しんちょう)にコースをついて投(な)げた。
Driven into a three-ball count, the pitcher carefully aimed his pitches at the corners of the strike zone.

スリーボール is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-made English) baseball term meaning a count of three balls. It is heard mainly in TV/radio commentary, in baseball video games, and in casual baseball talk. In English-language baseball, the count is simply expressed as "three balls" or as part of a count like "3-2."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • カウントはスリーボール: the count is three balls
  • スリーボールから投(な)げる: to pitch from a 3-ball count
  • スリーボール・ノーストライク: 3-0 count
  • スリーボール・ツーストライク: 3-2 count (the same as フルカウント, "full count")
  • スリーボールに追(お)い込(こ)まれる: to be cornered into a 3-ball count

RELATED TERMS:

  • ツーストライク: two strikes
  • フルカウント: full count (3 balls and 2 strikes)
  • フォアボール: walk / base on balls
  • ボールカウント: ball-strike count
  • ストライク: strike
  • ボール: ball (a pitch outside the strike zone)

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japanese broadcasts the count is conventionally given balls-first: スリーボール・ツーストライク (3-2). MLB-style English counts give strikes-first ("3 and 2" still works because it is balls-first too, but "two strikes, three balls" would sound odd).
  • スリーボール is also very common in baseball manga and anime as a way of building tension before the next pitch.
