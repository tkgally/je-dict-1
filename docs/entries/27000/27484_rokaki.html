ろ過器(かき)

ろかき
noun
filter; filtration device
1. filter; filtration device; strainer apparatus
A device that removes solids or impurities from a liquid or gas by passing it through a filtering medium. Used in chemistry labs, water treatment, and industrial processes.
ろ過器(かき)で水(みず)をきれいにする。
We purify the water with a filter.
実験(じっけん)用(よう)のろ過器(かき)を洗(あら)った。
I washed the laboratory filter.
古(ふる)くなったろ過器(かき)は交換(こうかん)しないと、ろ過(か)の性能(せいのう)が落(お)ちる。
If you don't replace an old filter, its filtration performance drops.

Compound of ろ過(か) (filtration) + 器(き) (apparatus, device). The first part is usually written in mixed kana-kanji because the kanji 濾(ろ) is outside the 常用漢字(じょうようかんじ) list.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ろ過器(かき)を使(つか)う: to use a filter
  • ろ過器(かき)を交換(こうかん)する: to replace the filter
  • 実験(じっけん)用(よう)のろ過器(かき): laboratory filter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • フィルター: filter — the everyday loanword, used for household filters (coffee, air, water)
  • ろ過(か)装置(そうち): filtration system — a larger system, often industrial
  • 浄水器(じょうすいき): water purifier — specifically for drinking water
