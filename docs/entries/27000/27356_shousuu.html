小数(しょうすう)

しょうすう
noun
decimal; decimal number
1. decimal; decimal number (a number with a fractional part written after the decimal point)
A mathematical term for a number expressed in decimal form — that is, with a decimal point and a fractional part, such as 0.5, 3.14, or 12.034. Combines 小(しょう) ("small") with 数(すう) ("number"). Beware the homophone 少数(しょうすう) ("small number, minority"), which is unrelated.
答(こた)えを小数(しょうすう)で書(か)いてください。
Please write the answer as a decimal.
小数(しょうすう)第(だい)二(に)位(い)を四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)しなさい。
Round to two decimal places.
分数(ぶんすう)を小数(しょうすう)に直(なお)す方法(ほうほう)を習(なら)った。
We learned how to convert fractions into decimals.
コンピュータ(コンピュータ)は小数(しょうすう)を扱(あつか)う際(さい)に丸(まる)め誤差(ごさ)が生(しょう)じることがある。
Computers can produce rounding errors when handling decimal numbers.

小数(しょうすう) is a basic mathematical term taught in Japanese elementary school for numbers written with a decimal point. It contrasts with 整数(せいすう) ("whole number, integer") and 分数(ぶんすう) ("fraction"), which together form the standard inventory of number representations introduced in primary math.

FORMATION:

  • 小(しょう) ("small") + 数(すう) ("number") → 小数(しょうすう)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小数点(しょうすうてん): decimal point
  • 小数(しょうすう)第(だい)一(いち)位(い) / 小数(しょうすう)第(だい)二(に)位(い): first / second decimal place
  • 小数(しょうすう)に直(なお)す: to convert into a decimal
  • 小数(しょうすう)で表(あらわ)す: to express as a decimal
  • 小数(しょうすう)部分(ぶぶん): the fractional part of a decimal
  • 循環(じゅんかん)小数(しょうすう): repeating / recurring decimal
  • 有限(ゆうげん)小数(しょうすう): terminating decimal

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 整数(せいすう): integer, whole number
  • 分数(ぶんすう): fraction (e.g., 1/2)
  • 実数(じっすう): real number
  • 小数点(しょうすうてん): decimal point
  • 四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう): rounding ("round 5 up, 4 down")

DISAMBIGUATION (HOMOPHONE):

  • 少数(しょうすう) "a small number, a minority" — same reading but completely different meaning. Compare:
  • 小数(しょうすう)で答(こた)える: "to answer in decimal form"
  • 少数(しょうすう)派(は): "the minority faction"
  • The kanji difference matters: 小(しょう) ("small in size, sub-unit") vs. 少(しょう) ("few in number").

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japanese math classrooms, the decimal point is normally read as {てん}: 3.14 → さんてんいちよん.
  • 小数(しょうすう) is strictly mathematical. To talk about "a few people" you need 少数(しょうすう) (different kanji) or 少(すこ)し.
