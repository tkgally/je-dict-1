1. homestay; staying with a host family while studying abroad or traveling
An arrangement in which a student or traveler — often abroad — stays in a private home with a host family rather than in a hotel or dormitory. Especially common for short-term language study programs.
夏休(なつやす)みにアメリカでホームステイをした。
I did a homestay in America during summer vacation.
ホームステイ先(さき)の家族(かぞく)はとても親切(しんせつ)だった。
The family at my homestay was very kind.
留学(りゅうがく)中(ちゅう)はホームステイをして、ホストファミリーと毎日(まいにち)英語(えいご)で話(はな)した。
While studying abroad I did a homestay and spoke English with my host family every day.
初(はじ)めてのホームステイは緊張(きんちょう)したが、家族(かぞく)が温(あたた)かく迎(むか)えてくれて、すぐに打(う)ち解(と)けることができた。
My first homestay was nerve-wracking, but the family welcomed me warmly and I was able to get comfortable quickly.

ホームステイ is a loanword from English "homestay," referring to the arrangement of staying with a host family — overwhelmingly used in the context of Japanese students going abroad, but also used in reverse for foreign students staying with Japanese families.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ホームステイをする: to do a homestay
  • ホームステイ先(さき): homestay destination / host family
  • ホストファミリー: host family
  • ホームステイプログラム(ぷろぐらむ): homestay program
  • ホームステイの受(う)け入(い)れ: hosting (a homestay student)
  • 短期(たんき)ホームステイ: short-term homestay

VERB USAGE:

  • As a する verb: ホームステイする, ホームステイをする (the latter is more common)
  • The destination is marked with で: アメリカでホームステイする
  • The host family is referred to with the particle のところ or 先(さき): ホームステイ先(さき)の家族(かぞく)

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • ホームステイ became a popular component of Japanese English-education programs starting in the 1980s.
  • Many Japanese high schools and universities run summer ホームステイ programs to English-speaking countries; Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the U.S. are particularly common destinations.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 留学(りゅうがく): study abroad
  • ホストファミリー: host family
  • 寮(りょう): dormitory (an alternative living arrangement during study abroad)
  • 民泊(みんぱく): vacation rental staying in a private home (like Airbnb — different concept; commercial)
