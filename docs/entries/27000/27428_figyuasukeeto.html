フィギュアスケート

ふぃぎゅあすけーと
noun
figure skating
1. figure skating
The competitive ice sport of performing jumps, spins, and choreographed movement on ice skates. Loanword from English. A widely followed winter sport in Japan, both at the Olympic level and on TV.
フィギュアスケートを見(み)るのが好(す)きだ。
I like watching figure skating.
娘(むすめ)は5歳(ごさい)からフィギュアスケートを習(なら)っている。
My daughter has been learning figure skating since she was five.
フィギュアスケートの大会(たいかい)でジャンプの回転(かいてん)が足(た)りず減点(げんてん)された。
At the figure skating competition, the jump rotations were under-rotated and points were deducted.

Loanword from English "figure skating." In casual conversation it is often shortened to フィギュア — context disambiguates between the sport and the model-figurine sense of フィギュア.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フィギュアスケートの選手(せんしゅ): figure skater
  • フィギュアスケートの大会(たいかい): figure skating competition
  • フィギュアスケートを習(なら)う: to take figure skating lessons
  • ペアフィギュア / アイスダンス: pairs / ice dance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スケート: skating — the general term, includes figure skating and speed skating
  • スピードスケート: speed skating — racing on a long oval track
  • アイスダンス: ice dance — a related discipline focusing on dance choreography
