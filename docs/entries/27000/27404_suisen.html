水仙(すいせん)

すいせん
noun
narcissus; daffodil (a bulbous flowering plant of the genus Narcissus, blooming in winter and early spring)
1. narcissus; daffodil — a bulbous flowering plant of the genus Narcissus, with white or yellow trumpet-shaped flowers, blooming in late winter and early spring
A bulbous flowering plant of the genus Narcissus, with slender green leaves and trumpet-shaped flowers (often white with a yellow center, or solid yellow). In Japan, 水仙(すいせん) blooms from late winter through early spring and is one of the iconic flowers of the new-year and early-spring seasons. The Echizen coast in Fukui and the cliffs of the Izu Peninsula are particularly famous for their wild 水仙(すいせん) colonies, which produce winter flower-viewing destinations.

水仙(すいせん)の香(かお)りは春(はる)の訪(おとず)れを感(かん)じさせる。
The scent of narcissus makes you feel the arrival of spring.
越前(えちぜん)海岸(かいがん)は冬(ふゆ)に水仙(すいせん)が一面(いちめん)に咲(さ)くことで有名(ゆうめい)だ。
The Echizen Coast is famous for narcissus blooming all over in winter.

Narcissus bulbs are poisonous, and accidentally eating them while mistaking them for chives can cause food poisoning, so caution is necessary.

水仙(すいせん) is the standard Japanese name for the narcissus / daffodil. The word covers the full genus Narcissus, including the trumpet-flowered daffodil and the smaller, multi-flowered Tazetta varieties common in Japan.

FORMATION:

  • 水(すい) ("water") + 仙(せん) ("hermit, sage") → 水仙(すいせん), literally "water hermit/sage"
  • The name comes from Chinese, where 水仙 refers to the same plant and evokes its preference for moist places and its elegant, slender appearance.

VARIETIES IN JAPAN:

  • 日本(にほん)水仙(すいせん): "Japanese narcissus" — the Tazetta type with small white flowers and a yellow cup; the most common wild and garden variety in Japan
  • ラッパ}{水仙(すいせん): trumpet daffodil — the large yellow trumpet type (Narcissus pseudonarcissus); a garden ornamental
  • 黄(き)水仙(すいせん): yellow narcissus

FLOWERING SEASON:

  • 日本(にほん)水仙(すいせん) blooms from December through February — one of the few flowers in midwinter, prized as a 冬(ふゆ)の花(はな) (winter flower).
  • Trumpet daffodils bloom slightly later, in March-April.
  • 水仙(すいせん) is a classical 季語(きご) (seasonal word) for winter.

FAMOUS VIEWING SPOTS:

  • 越前(えちぜん)海岸(かいがん) (Fukui Prefecture): wild Japanese narcissus on coastal cliffs; one of Japan's three great narcissus locations
  • 爪木崎(つめきざき) (伊豆(いず) Peninsula, Shizuoka): another famous wild colony
  • 灘黒岩(なだくろいわ)水仙(すいせん)郷(きょう) (Awaji Island, Hyogo): a famous narcissus-covered hillside

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水仙(すいせん)が咲(さ)く: narcissus blooms
  • 水仙(すいせん)の花(はな): narcissus flowers
  • 水仙(すいせん)畑(ばたけ): a field of narcissus
  • 水仙(すいせん)の球根(きゅうこん): narcissus bulb
  • 水仙(すいせん)の香(かお)り: the scent of narcissus
  • 一面(いちめん)の水仙(すいせん): narcissus blooming as far as the eye can see

CAUTION:

  • All parts of the 水仙(すいせん) plant, especially the bulbs, are poisonous (alkaloid lycorine). Each year there are food-poisoning incidents in Japan caused by people mistaking narcissus leaves for chives (ニラ) and bulbs for onions (玉(たま)ねぎ).

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 球根(きゅうこん): bulb (of a bulbous plant)
  • ラッパ水仙(すいせん): trumpet daffodil — see above
  • ヒヤシンス: hyacinth — another bulbous spring flower
  • チューリップ: tulip — another bulbous spring flower
  • 梅(うめ): plum blossom — another winter/early-spring icon
  • 花(はな)言葉(ことば): "flower language" / flower meanings — narcissus is associated in flower language with "self-love" and "respect" (from the Greek myth of Narcissus)

USAGE NOTES:

  • The reading すいせん} is also shared with several unrelated homophones: {推薦(すいせん) ("recommendation"), 垂線(すいせん) ("perpendicular line"), 水栓(すいせん) ("water tap"). Context disambiguates.
  • The flower's English name "narcissus" (the genus) and "daffodil" (the common English name for trumpet narcissi) both correspond to Japanese 水仙(すいせん) — Japanese does not lexically distinguish them as cleanly as English does.
