1. to massage thoroughly; to knead out (muscles); to loosen up
To rub and press a stiff muscle or knotted body part until the tension dissolves. Compound of 揉(も)む (to knead) + ほぐす (to loosen), implying both motions in one continuous action. Often used for shoulders, neck, and feet.
肩(かた)をもみほぐす。
To massage one's shoulders thoroughly.
固(かた)くなった筋肉(きんにく)を丁寧(ていねい)にもみほぐした。
I carefully massaged the stiffened muscles loose.
試合(しあい)の前(まえ)にトレーナーが選手(せんしゅ)の足(あし)をしっかりもみほぐしてくれた。
Before the match the trainer thoroughly massaged the athlete's legs.

Transitive godan verb (もみほぐす・もみほぐして・もみほぐした). Compound of 揉(も)む + ほぐす, common in massage and sports contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:

Transitive: marks the body part with を.

  • 肩(かた)をもみほぐす: to massage shoulders
  • 足(あし)をもみほぐす: to massage feet

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肩(かた)・首(くび)・腰(こし)をもみほぐす: to massage shoulders/neck/lower back
  • 筋肉(きんにく)をもみほぐす: to loosen the muscles
  • 凝(こ)りをもみほぐす: to knead out stiffness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 揉(も)む: to knead; to rub — broader, may not imply loosening as the goal
  • ほぐす: to loosen; to untangle — broader; not limited to massage
  • マッサージする: to massage — generic loanword, doesn't specify kneading motion
