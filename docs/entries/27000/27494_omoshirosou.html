面白(おもしろ)そう

おもしろそう
adjective-na
looks interesting; sounds fun
1. looks interesting; looks like fun; sounds fun
An appearance-based judgement that something seems interesting or enjoyable, based on what the speaker has seen or heard about it. Formed from 面白(おもしろ)い + the evidential suffix そう.
その映画(えいが)は面白(おもしろ)そうだ。
That movie looks interesting.
面白(おもしろ)そうな本(ほん)を見(み)つけた。
I found an interesting-looking book.
新(あたら)しい仕事(しごと)の内容(ないよう)を聞(き)いて、とても面白(おもしろ)そうだと思(おも)った。
After hearing what the new job involves, I thought it sounded really interesting.

Formed by attaching the evidential そう suffix to the stem of the i-adjective 面白(おもしろ)い (drop い, add そう). The result conjugates as a na-adjective: 面白(おもしろ)そうだ (predicate) / 面白(おもしろ)そうな (modifier) / 面白(おもしろ)そうに (adverbial).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 面白(おもしろ)そうな話(はなし): an interesting-sounding story
  • 面白(おもしろ)そうに聞(き)こえる: to sound interesting
  • 面白(おもしろ)そうにしている: to look like one is having fun

USAGE NOTE:
Used only when the speaker has not yet experienced the thing directly. After actually trying it, switch to plain 面白(おもしろ)い. Saying *面白(おもしろ)そうな映画(えいが)だった about a movie you have already watched sounds odd.
