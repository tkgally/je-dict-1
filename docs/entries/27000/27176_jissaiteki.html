実際的(じっさいてき)

じっさいてき
adjective
practical; pragmatic; realistic
1. practical; pragmatic; realistic
Focused on real-world application and usefulness rather than theory or ideals.
実際的(じっさいてき)な解決策(かいけつさく)を考(かんが)える。
Think of a practical solution.
もっと実際的(じっさいてき)な提案(ていあん)はありませんか。
Isn't there a more practical proposal?
理想(りそう)を語(かた)るだけでなく、実際的(じっさいてき)な行動(こうどう)に移(うつ)すべきだ。
Rather than just talking about ideals, you should move to practical action.

Describes an approach that prioritizes what works in practice over abstract theory. Derived from 実際(じっさい) (reality; actuality) plus 的(てき). Often used when contrasting pragmatic approaches with idealistic ones.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実際的(じっさいてき)な問題(もんだい): practical problem
  • 実際的(じっさいてき)な方法(ほうほう): practical method
  • 実際的(じっさいてき)な知識(ちしき): practical knowledge
  • 実際的(じっさいてき)な意味(いみ)で: in a practical sense

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実用的(じつようてき): practical; utilitarian — emphasizes usefulness and functionality of objects or skills
  • 現実的(げんじつてき): realistic — emphasizes accepting things as they are rather than being idealistic
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき): concrete; specific — focuses on detail and specificity rather than practicality
