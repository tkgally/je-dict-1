1. to take (with one); to carry away; to bring along
To take an object away from the speaker's location to somewhere else. Compound of 持(も)つ (to hold) + 行(い)く (to go). Used when the destination is away from the speaker; contrasts with 持(も)ってくる (to bring here).
傘(かさ)を持(も)っていく。
I'll take an umbrella.
お弁当(べんとう)を学校(がっこう)に持(も)っていきました。
I took a packed lunch to school.
旅行(りょこう)に持(も)っていく荷物(にもつ)をスーツケースに詰(つ)めた。
I packed the luggage I'm taking on the trip into a suitcase.

Transitive godan verb conjugating like 行(い)く: 持(も)っていく・持(も)っていって・持(も)っていった. Often written 持(も)って行(い)く; the hiragana form もっていく is also common in casual writing. Pairs with 持(も)ってくる for direction.

TRANSITIVITY:

Marks the object with を and the destination with に or へ.

  • お土産(みやげ)を家(いえ)に持(も)っていく: to take a souvenir home

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傘(かさ)を持(も)っていく: to take an umbrella
  • 昼食(ちゅうしょく)を持(も)っていく: to bring lunch
  • 忘(わす)れずに持(も)っていく: to remember to take

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 持(も)ってくる: to bring here — direction toward the speaker
  • 運(はこ)ぶ: to carry; transport — focuses on the act of conveying, not direction relative to speaker
