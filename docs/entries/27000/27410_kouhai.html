高配(こうはい)

こうはい
noun
your kind consideration / kind patronage / generous attention (very formal honorific noun used in business letters and formal speech)
1. (in formal correspondence) your kind consideration; your gracious attention; the generous attention or patronage that the addressee bestows on the speaker or speaker's organization
A respectful noun used almost exclusively in formal business letters, ceremonial speeches, and the like. It refers to the kindness, attentiveness, or favor that the addressee shows toward the speaker. The 高(こう) prefix means "high" / "esteemed" and acts as an honorific raising the addressee's actions; 配(はい) carries the sense of "showing care" or "distributing attention." 高配(こうはい) appears in set greeting phrases at the opening of business letters and is rarely used in everyday conversation.
御高配(ごこうはい)賜(たまわ)る。
To receive your kind consideration. (very formal letter phrase)
平素(へいそ)より格別(かくべつ)の御高配(ごこうはい)を賜(たまわ)り、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。
We are sincerely grateful for the special consideration you regularly extend to us. (standard opening of a business letter)
今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬ御高配(ごこうはい)のほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We humbly ask for your continued and unchanging support going forward.
皆様(みなさま)の温(あたた)かい御高配(ごこうはい)のおかげで、無事(ぶじ)に創立(そうりつ)二十周年(にじゅっしゅうねん)を迎(むか)えることができました。
Thanks to all of your warm consideration and support, we have been able to safely mark our twentieth anniversary.

高配(こうはい) is a very formal honorific noun referring to the addressee's kind consideration, generous attention, or patronage. It is almost always used with the honorific prefix 御(ご) as 御高配(ごこうはい) (ご}{高配(こうはい)), and is typical of opening and closing greetings in Japanese business letters and formal speeches.

WHO/WHAT IT REFERS TO:

  • The addressee's actions toward the speaker — never the speaker's own
  • Implicit subject is always the recipient of the letter or audience of the speech

FIXED PHRASES (BUSINESS LETTER OPENINGS AND CLOSINGS):

  • 平素(へいそ)より格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい)を賜(たまわ)り、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。 — Thank you sincerely for your special consideration extended to us as always.
  • 日頃(ひごろ)より格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい)にあずかり、厚(あつ)く御礼(おんれい)申(もう)し上(あ)げます。 — We extend our deep thanks for the special consideration you regularly extend to us.
  • 今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬご高配(こうはい)のほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。 — We respectfully request your continued, unchanged consideration going forward.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご高配(こうはい)を賜(たまわ)る: to receive (your) kind consideration (very formal)
  • ご高配(こうはい)にあずかる: to be the recipient of (your) gracious attention
  • ご高配(こうはい)を頂(いただ)く: to receive (your) kind consideration (slightly less archaic than 賜(たまわ)る)
  • 格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい): special / extraordinary consideration
  • 温(あたた)かいご高配(こうはい): warm consideration
  • 変(か)わらぬご高配(こうはい): unchanged / continuing consideration

SIMILAR FORMAL HONORIFIC NOUNS (BUSINESS-LETTER VOCABULARY):

  • ご愛顧(あいこ): (your) patronage — very common in retail business letters
  • ご厚情(こうじょう): (your) kindheartedness / deep goodwill
  • ご厚誼(こうぎ): (your) deep friendship / cordial relations (between organizations or individuals)
  • ご支援(しえん): (your) support — less archaic, broader use
  • ご協力(きょうりょく): (your) cooperation — neutral formal, not specifically epistolary
  • ご愛顧(あいこ)を賜(たまわ)る: to receive your patronage (parallel pattern)
  • 引(ひ)き立(た)て: backing / personal favor / patronage (often used in shop/business letters)

REGISTER AND USAGE NOTES:

  • Extremely formal — appropriate for: business correspondence, formal speeches, ceremonial occasions, anniversary announcements, customer letters
  • Inappropriate in: everyday conversation, casual writing, even most workplace internal email
  • Always honorific in reference: never used about one's own behavior
  • Modern business writing has begun to simplify this kind of language; younger writers may substitute ご支援(しえん) or お力添(ちからぞ)え for a less archaic feel
  • Distinct in meaning from the homophone 後輩(こうはい) (junior colleague/student) and 荒廃(こうはい) (devastation) — pure context distinguishes them in writing, but voice intonation alone is identical
