高配(こうはい)

こうはい
noun
kind consideration; gracious favor; patronage
1. kind consideration; gracious favor; patronage
A formal noun expressing gratitude for another's thoughtfulness or support. Almost always appears with the honorific prefix ご and is restricted to business correspondence and formal speeches.
ご高配(こうはい)を賜(たまわ)る。
To receive your kind consideration.
平素(へいそ)は格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい)を賜(たまわ)り、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。
We sincerely thank you for your special patronage as always.
今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬご高配(こうはい)のほど、よろしくお願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We humbly ask for your continued patronage going forward.

A set formal noun used almost exclusively in written business correspondence and ceremonial speeches. Always takes the honorific ご and is paired with verbs of receiving such as 賜(たまわ)る or いただく.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご高配(こうはい)を賜(たまわ)る: to receive your kind consideration
  • 格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい): your exceptional patronage
  • 変(か)わらぬご高配(こうはい): your unchanging patronage
  • ご高配(こうはい)のほど: (a softener closing the phrase)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ご愛顧(あいこ): your patronage — used by businesses thanking customers
  • ご厚情(こうじょう): your warm-hearted favor — slightly warmer in tone, used for personal gratitude
  • お引(ひ)き立(た)て: your support — slightly less stiff but similarly formal
