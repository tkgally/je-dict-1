重過失(じゅうかしつ)

じゅうかしつ
noun
gross negligence (legal term: a serious failure to exercise the care that a reasonable person would exercise)
1. gross negligence (a legal term for serious carelessness, distinguished from ordinary negligence)
A legal term referring to a serious lack of care that goes significantly beyond ordinary 過失(かしつ) (negligence) — a failure to exercise even the most basic level of caution that any reasonable person would observe. In Japanese civil and criminal law, the presence of 重過失(じゅうかしつ) (rather than mere 過失(かしつ)) often changes the legal consequences: it can defeat exemption-from-liability clauses in contracts, expand criminal liability, and shift damages calculations. Roughly parallel to "gross negligence" in Anglo-American legal systems.
重過失(じゅうかしつ)を認(みと)める。
To find gross negligence.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)は被告(ひこく)に重過失(じゅうかしつ)があったと判断(はんだん)した。
The court ruled that the defendant had been grossly negligent.
契約書(けいやくしょ)の免責(めんせき)条項(じょうこう)は、重過失(じゅうかしつ)の場合(ばあい)には適用(てきよう)されない。
The exemption clause in the contract does not apply in cases of gross negligence.
安全(あんぜん)基準(きじゅん)を完全(かんぜん)に無視(むし)した操業(そうぎょう)は、単(たん)なる過失(かしつ)ではなく重過失(じゅうかしつ)にあたると専門家(せんもんか)は指摘(してき)している。
Experts point out that operating in complete disregard of safety standards constitutes not mere negligence but gross negligence.

重過失(じゅうかしつ) is a legal-technical term meaning "gross negligence" — carelessness that is significantly worse than ordinary 過失(かしつ). It plays a central role in Japanese tort, contract, and criminal law.

FORMATION:

  • 重(じゅう) ("heavy, serious") + 過失(かしつ) ("negligence, error") → 重過失(じゅうかしつ)
  • Parallel construction to 軽過失(けいかしつ) ("slight negligence"), where 軽(けい) ("light") replaces 重(じゅう).

LEGAL SIGNIFICANCE:

  • In contracts, exemption-from-liability clauses (免責(めんせき)条項(じょうこう)) typically do not cover 重過失(じゅうかしつ) — a party cannot disclaim liability for gross negligence.
  • In criminal law, certain offenses are defined specifically in terms of 重過失(じゅうかしつ) (e.g., 重過失(じゅうかしつ)致死(ちし) "gross negligence resulting in death").
  • The standard for finding 重過失(じゅうかしつ) is that the conduct shows an extreme lack of attention — failing to notice what would be obvious to almost anyone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)を認(みと)める: to find/recognize gross negligence
  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)があった: there was gross negligence
  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)にあたる: to constitute gross negligence
  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)致死(ちし): causing death by gross negligence
  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)傷害(しょうがい): causing injury by gross negligence
  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ)失火(しっか): causing a fire through gross negligence

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 過失(かしつ): negligence — the general legal category; 重過失(じゅうかしつ) is a more severe sub-type
  • 軽過失(けいかしつ): slight negligence — minor lack of care; contrasted with 重過失(じゅうかしつ)
  • 故意(こい): intent, willfulness — a step beyond negligence (deliberate wrongdoing)
  • 不注意(ふちゅうい): carelessness — the everyday word; 重過失(じゅうかしつ) is the legal-technical counterpart
  • 業務上過失(ぎょうむじょうかしつ): occupational/professional negligence — negligence in the course of one's professional duties

USAGE NOTES:

  • 重過失(じゅうかしつ) is firmly legal vocabulary; it appears in statutes, judgments, contracts, and legal commentary, but rarely in casual speech.
  • For everyday "serious carelessness," speakers tend to use ひどい}{不注意(ふちゅうい) or 重大(じゅうだい)なミス} rather than {重過失(じゅうかしつ).
  • Often capitalized as a defined legal term in contracts: 「重過失(じゅうかしつ)とは…をいう」 ("'gross negligence' means…").
