先行(さきゆ)き

さきゆき
noun
future prospects; outlook
1. future prospects; outlook; what lies ahead
The way things are expected to develop in the future, especially regarding business, the economy, or a course of action. Often used to describe whether the future looks promising or uncertain.
先行(さきゆ)きが不安(ふあん)だ。
The outlook is uncertain.
会社(かいしゃ)の先行(さきゆ)きが心配(しんぱい)される。
There is concern about the company's prospects.
原材料(げんざいりょう)価格(かかく)の上昇(じょうしょう)で、業界(ぎょうかい)の先行(さきゆ)きは不透明(ふとうめい)になっている。
With raw-material prices rising, the industry's outlook has become unclear.

Very common in business and economic news. Almost always followed by a word about clarity or confidence (明(あか)るい, 不透明(ふとうめい), 不安(ふあん), etc.) rather than appearing alone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先行(さきゆ)きが明(あか)るい: the outlook is bright
  • 先行(さきゆ)きが暗(くら)い: the outlook is dark
  • 先行(さきゆ)き不透明(ふとうめい): unclear prospects
  • 先行(さきゆ)き不安(ふあん): worried about the future
  • 先行(さきゆ)きを見通(みとお)す: to predict what lies ahead

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見通(みとお)し: outlook; forecast — broader; can refer to visibility itself or to a forecast
  • 将来(しょうらい): the future — neutral and general, not specifically about how things will turn out
