諸条件(しょじょうけん)

しょじょうけん
noun
various conditions; multiple requirements
1. various conditions; multiple requirements; several terms
A collection of different conditions, requirements, or terms that must be considered together.
諸条件(しょじょうけん)を検討(けんとう)する。
To examine the various conditions.
契約(けいやく)の諸条件(しょじょうけん)に同意(どうい)してください。
Please agree to the various terms of the contract.
採用(さいよう)の諸条件(しょじょうけん)は応募者(おうぼしゃ)の経験(けいけん)によって異(こと)なる場合(ばあい)があります。
The various employment conditions may differ depending on the applicant's experience.

A formal compound combining the prefix 諸(しょ) (various; all) with 条件(じょうけん) (condition; requirement). Commonly used in business, legal, and academic contexts when referring to a set of conditions that need to be considered collectively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 諸条件(しょじょうけん)を満(み)たす: to meet the various conditions
  • 諸条件(しょじょうけん)を整(ととの)える: to arrange the various conditions
  • 諸条件(しょじょうけん)を比較(ひかく)する: to compare the various conditions
  • 勤務(きんむ)諸条件(しょじょうけん): various working conditions

THE PREFIX 諸(しょ):

The prefix 諸(しょ) means "various" or "all" and attaches to many nouns in formal language:

  • 諸問題(しょもんだい): various problems
  • 諸事情(しょじじょう): various circumstances
  • 諸費用(しょひよう): various expenses
  • 諸国(しょこく): various countries

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 条件(じょうけん): condition; requirement — the singular/general form
  • 要件(ようけん): requirement; prerequisite — focuses on what is necessary, often more specific
