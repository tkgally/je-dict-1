着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)

ちゃくちちてん
noun
landing point; landing spot
1. landing point; landing spot; point of touchdown
The location where something lands after jumping, falling, or flying. Used in sports (long jump, ski jump), aviation, and parachuting. Also used figuratively for where a negotiation or discussion will settle.
ジャンプの着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)を確認(かくにん)した。
We checked the landing point of the jump.
パラシュートの着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)がずれてしまった。
The parachute's landing point ended up off target.
交渉(こうしょう)の着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)が見(み)えてきたので、まとめに入(はい)ろう。
We can see where the negotiation will land, so let's start wrapping it up.

Compound of 着地(ちゃくち) (landing) + 地点(ちてん) (point/location).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)を確認(かくにん)する: to confirm the landing point
  • 着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)を予測(よそく)する: to predict where it will land
  • 着地地点(ちゃくちちてん)がずれる: the landing point is off

FIGURATIVE USE:
In business and political news, 着地地点(ちゃくちちてん) often refers to the final compromise position of a negotiation: "where the talks will land." This usage is increasingly common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着地点(ちゃくちてん): landing point — shorter form, identical in meaning
  • 落下地点(らっかちてん): point of impact (fall) — emphasizes uncontrolled falling rather than landing
