1. recovery from illness; complete healing; recuperation
Full recovery from an illness or injury. A formal word often used in written communication such as get-well cards and formal wishes for recovery.
ご快癒(かいゆ)をお祈(いの)りしております。
I am praying for your recovery.
手術後(しゅじゅつご)、快癒(かいゆ)に向(む)かっている。
After the surgery, recovery is progressing well.
社長(しゃちょう)の快癒(かいゆ)を願(ねが)い、社員(しゃいん)一同(いちどう)から花束(はなたば)を贈(おく)った。
Wishing for the company president's recovery, the employees sent a bouquet together.

Compound of 快(かい) (pleasant; refreshing) + 癒(ゆ) (healing; curing). A formal word primarily used in polite wishes for someone's recovery, especially in written communication like letters, cards, and formal messages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご快癒(かいゆ)をお祈(いの)りします: I pray for your recovery (formal)
  • 快癒(かいゆ)に向(む)かう: to be on the road to recovery
  • 一日(いちにち)も早(はや)いご快癒(かいゆ)を: wishing you the speediest recovery
  • 快癒(かいゆ)を願(ねが)う: to wish for recovery

REGISTER:
This is a formal, literary word. In everyday conversation, people would say 元気(げんき)になる (to get better), 治(なお)る (to heal), or 回復(かいふく)する (to recover).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 回復(かいふく): recovery — more common, used in both casual and formal contexts
  • 全快(ぜんかい): full recovery; complete cure — emphasizes being completely healed
  • 平癒(へいゆ): recovery; healing — even more literary, often seen in shrine prayers for health
