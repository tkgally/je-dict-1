1. to put on and take off; to attach and detach
The action of attaching/putting on and removing/taking off, especially clothing, accessories, or detachable parts. Often describes the ease or method of the process.
このジャケットは着脱(ちゃくだつ)しやすい。
This jacket is easy to put on and take off.
マスクの着脱(ちゃくだつ)は清潔(せいけつ)な手(て)で行(おこな)ってください。
Please put on and remove your mask with clean hands.
このカメラのレンズは簡単(かんたん)に着脱(ちゃくだつ)できるので便利(べんり)だ。
This camera's lens can be easily attached and detached, which is convenient.

A compound of 着(ちゃく) (put on/attach) and 脱(だつ) (take off/remove). Used both for clothing and for detachable parts/accessories. The noun form 着脱(ちゃくだつ) is common in product descriptions where ease of use is a selling point.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)が簡単(かんたん): easy to put on/take off
  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)しやすい: easy to attach/detach
  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)可能(かのう): detachable; removable
  • 着脱式(ちゃくだつしき): detachable type
  • ワンタッチで着脱(ちゃくだつ): attach/detach with one touch

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着(き)る/脱(ぬ)ぐ: to put on/take off (clothing) — the individual verbs for each action
  • 取(と)り外(はず)す: to remove; to detach — for removing a specific part
  • 装着(そうちゃく)する: to wear; to equip — focuses on the attaching/equipping action only
