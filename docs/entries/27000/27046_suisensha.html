推薦者(すいせんしゃ)

すいせんしゃ
noun
recommender; referee
1. recommender; referee; person who recommends
A person who provides a recommendation for someone or something, particularly in academic, professional, or organizational contexts.
推薦者(すいせんしゃ)の名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write the name of your recommender.
奨学金(しょうがくきん)の申請(しんせい)には推薦者(すいせんしゃ)が二人(ふたり)必要(ひつよう)です。
Two recommenders are required for the scholarship application.
指導(しどう)教員(きょういん)に推薦者(すいせんしゃ)になってもらえるようお願(おねが)いした。
I asked my academic advisor to serve as my recommender.

A compound of 推薦(すいせん) (recommendation) and 者(しゃ) (person). Most commonly encountered in academic and job application contexts, where a 推薦者(すいせんしゃ) provides a letter or verbal endorsement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 推薦者(すいせんしゃ)になる — to become a recommender
  • 推薦者(すいせんしゃ)を探(さが)す — to find a recommender
  • 推薦者(すいせんしゃ)の連絡先(れんらくさき) — recommender's contact information

RELATED TERMS:

  • 推薦(すいせん) — recommendation
  • 推薦状(すいせんじょう) — letter of recommendation
  • 推薦入試(すいせんにゅうし) — admission by recommendation (a common university entrance pathway in Japan)
  • 被推薦者(ひすいせんしゃ) — the person being recommended (nominee)
