チーズフォンデュ

ちーずふぉんでゅ
noun
cheese fondue
1. cheese fondue (a Swiss dish of melted cheese eaten communally with bread, vegetables, etc.)
A communal Swiss/French dish in which cheese is melted in a small pot (typically with white wine and seasonings) and kept warm at the table. Diners use long forks to dip pieces of bread, boiled potatoes, vegetables, or sausage into the molten cheese. In Japan, チーズフォンデュ is a popular dinner-party and restaurant dish, especially in winter.
今夜(こんや)はチーズフォンデュにしよう。
Let's have cheese fondue tonight.
パンを熱(あつ)いチーズフォンデュにつけて食(た)べる。
I dip bread into the hot cheese fondue and eat it.
家(いえ)でチーズフォンデュをするための専用(せんよう)の鍋(なべ)を買(か)った。
I bought a special pot for making cheese fondue at home.
冬(ふゆ)になると、家族(かぞく)でチーズフォンデュを囲(かこ)みながらゆっくり食事(しょくじ)を楽(たの)しむのが好(す)きだ。
In winter, I love sitting around a cheese fondue with my family and slowly enjoying the meal together.

チーズフォンデュ is a loanword based on French "fondue (au fromage)," referring to the Swiss/French communal dish of melted cheese into which diners dip bites of bread or vegetables. In Japan it is associated especially with romantic dinners, winter parties, and trendy restaurants.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チーズフォンデュにする: to make / decide on cheese fondue
  • チーズフォンデュを作(つく)る: to make cheese fondue
  • チーズフォンデュにつける / つけて食(た)べる: to dip (something) into cheese fondue and eat it
  • チーズフォンデュ鍋(なべ): cheese fondue pot
  • チーズフォンデュ専用(せんよう)の鍋(なべ): special pot for cheese fondue

RELATED TERMS:

  • フォンデュ: fondue (the general term, can also refer to chocolate fondue, etc.)
  • チョコフォンデュ / チョコレートフォンデュ: chocolate fondue
  • 鍋(なべ)料理(りょうり): hot-pot dish (a broader Japanese category that fondue is felt to belong to)
  • ラクレット: raclette (a related cheese-melting dish from the same region)
  • 溶(と)けるチーズ: melting cheese (often sold for fondue or pizza)

USAGE NOTES:

  • The standard verb for what diners do with the fondue is つける ("to dip") combined with 食(た)べる: パンをチーズフォンデュに付(つ)けて食(た)べる.
  • Although technically of Swiss origin, in Japan chocolate fondue (チョコフォンデュ) is so popular at dessert buffets that フォンデュ on its own is sometimes ambiguous; using the full term チーズフォンデュ avoids confusion.
