1. training; instruction (especially for skills like driving)
Formal instruction or training, most strongly associated in modern Japanese with driving schools (自動車(じどうしゃ)教習所(きょうしゅうじょ)). Combines 教(きょう) ("teach") with 習(しゅう) ("learn, practice"). Although it can in principle apply to any structured training, in everyday speech it almost always evokes driving instruction unless context specifies otherwise.
今日(きょう)は初(はじ)めての教習(きょうしゅう)だ。
Today is my first driving lesson.
兄(あに)は自動車(じどうしゃ)教習所(きょうしゅうじょ)に通(かよ)っている。
My older brother is attending driving school.
路上(ろじょう)教習(きょうしゅう)の前(まえ)に、教官(きょうかん)が注意(ちゅうい)事項(じこう)を説明(せつめい)してくれた。
Before the on-road lesson, the instructor explained the points to watch out for.
仕事(しごと)が忙(いそが)しい時期(じき)に教習(きょうしゅう)を始(はじ)めたので、卒業(そつぎょう)するまでに半年(はんとし)もかかった。
I started lessons during a busy stretch at work, so it took half a year to graduate from driving school.

教習(きょうしゅう) is a formal noun referring to structured instruction or training. In modern usage, the word is most strongly associated with driving instruction — to the point that 教習(きょうしゅう), 教習所(きょうしゅうじょ), and 教習生(きょうしゅうせい) are heard mainly in driving-school contexts.

FORMATION:

  • 教(きょう) ("teach") + 習(しゅう) ("learn, practice") → 教習(きょうしゅう)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 教習(きょうしゅう)所(じょ): training school, especially driving school
  • 自動車(じどうしゃ)教習所(きょうしゅうじょ): driving school
  • 教習(きょうしゅう)を受(う)ける: to take a lesson, to undergo training
  • 教習(きょうしゅう)に通(かよ)う: to attend lessons (regularly)
  • 路上(ろじょう)教習(きょうしゅう): on-the-road driving lesson
  • 仮免(かりめん)教習(きょうしゅう): instruction with a learner's permit
  • 教習(きょうしゅう)生(せい): a student (at a driving school)
  • 教習(きょうしゅう)車(しゃ): a driving-school car (marked with a green-and-yellow shoshinsha sign)

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 教育(きょういく): education (broad term covering schooling and upbringing)
  • 訓練(くんれん): training (often physical or skills-based; military, emergency drills, sports)
  • 研修(けんしゅう): professional training (used by companies and government bodies)
  • 講習(こうしゅう): short course or seminar (often a one-off or short series)
  • 指導(しどう): guidance, instruction (focuses on the act of guiding rather than the program)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Japan has a strong driving-school culture: most learners enroll in a 自動車(じどうしゃ)教習所(きょうしゅうじょ), complete classroom lectures and on-road lessons, and "graduate" before taking the licensing exam. Because of this, 教習(きょうしゅう) alone often means "driving instruction" in casual speech.
  • For non-driving training, 訓練(くんれん), 研修(けんしゅう), or 講習(こうしゅう) are usually more natural. For instance, a company workshop is a 研修(けんしゅう), not a 教習(きょうしゅう).
  • Note the homophone 郷愁(きょうしゅう) ("nostalgia") — same reading, completely different kanji and meaning.
