業務上(ぎょうむじょう)

ぎょうむじょう
noun
professional; occupational; in the course of one's duties (often used attributively before another noun)
1. professional; occupational; arising in the course of one's duties or business (used attributively, often in legal and formal contexts)
A compound formed from 業務(ぎょうむ) ("duties, business, professional activities") plus the suffix 上(じょう) ("on, in respect of"). The combination means "in the course of one's professional duties" or "arising from one's business activities," and is used overwhelmingly in attributive position — directly modifying a following noun. It carries a formal, legal, and slightly stiff register and is the standard prefix for many legal categories that distinguish professional conduct from private behavior.
業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の都合(つごう)。
Reasons related to the business.
業務上(ぎょうむじょう)知(し)り得(え)た情報(じょうほう)を漏(も)らしてはならない。
You must not disclose information learned in the course of your duties.
業務上(ぎょうむじょう)過失(かしつ)致死(ちし)罪(ざい)に問(と)われた運転手(うんてんしゅ)が出頭(しゅっとう)した。
The driver charged with professional negligence resulting in death turned himself in.
業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の必要(ひつよう)がある場合(ばあい)に限(かぎ)り、社員(しゃいん)は顧客(こきゃく)の個人(こじん)情報(じょうほう)にアクセスすることができる。
Employees may access customers' personal information only when it is necessary for business purposes.

業務上(ぎょうむじょう) is a formal compound that means "in the course of one's professional duties" or "for business reasons." It is overwhelmingly used attributively, directly modifying a following noun (or with {の}).

FORMATION:

  • 業務(ぎょうむ) ("duties, business, professional activity") + 上(じょう) (suffix: "on, in respect of, for the purposes of") → 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)
  • The 上(じょう) suffix is the same one seen in 法律上(ほうりつじょう) ("legally"), 歴史上(れきしじょう) ("historically"), 事実上(じじつじょう) ("in fact"), etc.

GRAMMATICAL BEHAVIOR:

  • Most commonly modifies a noun directly: 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)過失(かしつ), 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)横領(おうりょう), 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)災害(さいがい).
  • Can also take の} before a noun: {業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の都合(つごう), 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の秘密(ひみつ).
  • Rarely stands alone as a predicate.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)過失(かしつ): professional negligence (a legal term)
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)過失(かしつ)致死(ちし): professional negligence resulting in death
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)横領(おうりょう): embezzlement in the course of one's duties
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)災害(さいがい): work-related accident, industrial accident
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)知(し)り得(え)た情報(じょうほう): information obtained in the course of one's duties
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の都合(つごう): business reasons / for business purposes
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の秘密(ひみつ): professional secrets, business confidentiality
  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)の必要(ひつよう): business necessity

LEGAL USAGE:

  • 業務上(ぎょうむじょう) is a key term in Japanese criminal law for distinguishing offenses committed in the course of one's profession (which often carry heavier penalties than the same act committed in private). The classic example is 業務上(ぎょうむじょう)過失(かしつ)致死(ちし)罪(ざい) ("professional negligence resulting in death"), which formerly applied to traffic accidents.

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 業務(ぎょうむ): duties, business, work (the base noun)
  • 仕事上(しごとじょう): work-related, for work reasons — less formal; in everyday use
  • 職務上(しょくむじょう): official, in the course of one's official duties — emphasizes job role rather than business activity
  • 私的(してき)に: privately, personally — the natural opposite (private conduct, not business)
  • 公務(こうむ): public/official duties — used for civil servants; contrast 業務(ぎょうむ) which covers private business too

USAGE NOTES:

  • The register is formal-legal. In casual conversation, 仕事(しごと)で or 仕事(しごと)中(ちゅう)に is more natural than 業務上(ぎょうむじょう).
  • Pronunciation: {ぎょうむじょう} (four-mora compound), accent typically falls on {ぎょ}{う} (heiban or near-heiban patterns; varies by speaker).
