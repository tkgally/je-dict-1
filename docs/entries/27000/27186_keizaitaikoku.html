経済大国(けいざいたいこく)

けいざいたいこく
noun
economic superpower
1. economic superpower; major economic power
A country with a large, influential economy that plays a significant role in global markets and trade.
日本(にほん)は世界有数(せかいゆうすう)の経済大国(けいざいたいこく)だ。
Japan is one of the world's leading economic superpowers.
経済大国(けいざいたいこく)として国際社会(こくさいしゃかい)での責任(せきにん)も大(おお)きい。
As an economic superpower, it also bears great responsibility in the international community.
中国(ちゅうごく)は急速(きゅうそく)な成長(せいちょう)を遂(と)げ、世界第二位(せかいだいにい)の経済大国(けいざいたいこく)になった。
China achieved rapid growth and became the world's second-largest economic power.

A compound of 経済(けいざい) (economy) + 大国(たいこく) (great power/major nation). Frequently used in news and political discussions about Japan's postwar identity and international role.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世界(せかい)の経済大国(けいざいたいこく): world economic power
  • 経済大国(けいざいたいこく)としての責任(せきにん): responsibility as an economic superpower
  • 経済大国(けいざいたいこく)になる: to become an economic superpower
  • 経済大国(けいざいたいこく)の地位(ちい): status as an economic power

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Japan is often described as an 経済大国(けいざいたいこく) in contrast to a 軍事大国(ぐんじたいこく) (military power), reflecting its postwar constitution and the emphasis on economic development over military expansion.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 大国(たいこく): major power, great nation
  • 先進国(せんしんこく): developed country
  • 新興国(しんこうこく): emerging nation
