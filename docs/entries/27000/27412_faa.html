ファー

ふぁー
noun
fur — animal fur, or fur-look material used in clothing and fashion accessories (loanword)
1. fur — animal fur or imitation fur used as a fashion material, especially as trim or lining on coats, hats, gloves, and accessories
Loanword from English "fur," used almost exclusively in a fashion or clothing context. ファー can refer to either real animal fur (毛皮 in more general/serious vocabulary) or to synthetic imitation fur (フェイクファー / エコファー). In Japanese clothing-store and fashion-magazine vocabulary, ファー is the more everyday and trendy term, while 毛皮(けがわ) sounds more formal, technical, or evocative of luxury and the fur trade. ファー is most often seen in compound words such as ファーコート (fur coat), ファーマフラー (fur scarf), and ファー付(つ)き (fur-trimmed).
ファーのコート。
A fur coat.
襟(えり)にファーが付(つ)いている。
There's fur on the collar.
最近(さいきん)は動物愛護(どうぶつあいご)の観点(かんてん)から、フェイクファーを選(えら)ぶ人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently, more people are choosing faux fur for animal-welfare reasons.
冬(ふゆ)に向(む)けて、ファー付(つ)きのフードがついたダウンジャケットを買(か)いました。
For the winter season I bought a down jacket with a fur-trimmed hood.

ファー is the loanword for "fur," used mainly in fashion and clothing contexts. It covers both real animal fur and the more common synthetic look-alike materials, though context (and increasingly a qualifier like リアルファー or フェイクファー) makes the distinction explicit when it matters.

REAL VS FAKE:

  • リアルファー: real animal fur — used to disambiguate from synthetic
  • フェイクファー: faux/fake fur — synthetic imitation made from acrylic, polyester, etc.
  • エコファー: "eco fur" — Japanese marketing term for animal-friendly faux fur
  • 本(ほん)ファー: (informal) real fur, contrasted with fake
  • Without a qualifier, ファー increasingly defaults to fake fur in mainstream fashion, since real fur has fallen out of favor with many brands

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ファーコート: fur coat
  • ファージャケット: fur jacket
  • ファーマフラー: fur scarf
  • ファーベスト: fur vest
  • ファーバッグ: fur (or fur-look) handbag
  • ファーブーツ: fur-lined / fur-trimmed boots
  • ファースリッパ: fur slippers
  • ファー襟(えり): fur collar
  • ファー付(つ)き: fur-trimmed (e.g., ファー付(つ)きのフード = fur-trimmed hood)
  • もこもこファー: fluffy / plush fur (mimetic + ファー)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ファーを巻(ま)く: to wrap a fur (scarf or stole around oneself)
  • ファーが付(つ)く: to have fur attached / fur-trimmed
  • ファーを外(はず)す: to remove the fur (e.g., a detachable fur trim)
  • ファーを合(あ)わせる: to coordinate (an outfit) with fur

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • 毛皮(けがわ): the general/formal Japanese word for animal fur — more associated with the fur trade and traditional luxury
  • フェイクファー: faux fur (synthetic)
  • エコファー: eco-fur (faux fur, marketing term)
  • 毛(け): fur, hair (the general native word)
  • ボア: bouclé / boa-style fluffy fabric (often used for jacket linings)
  • ムートン: mouton (sheepskin) — a specific kind of real fur material
  • レザー: leather — a paired fashion category
  • フード: hood (often the part trimmed with ファー)

USAGE NOTES:

  • ファー is overwhelmingly used in fashion / clothing context; not used for fur on a living animal — use 毛(け) or 体毛(たいもう) for that
  • The distinction between ファー (everyday, trendy, often synthetic) and 毛皮(けがわ) (formal, often real) is similar to English "fur" in fashion copy vs. "furs" / "fur trade"
  • The pronunciation ファー uses the small ァ to write the English ah-vowel; do NOT confuse with ハー (a sigh) or ハイ
  • Pronounced with a long vowel (ファー, two morae) — short ファ would not be the loanword for "fur"
