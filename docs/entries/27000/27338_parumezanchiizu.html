パルメザンチーズ

ぱるめざんちーず
noun
Parmesan cheese
1. Parmesan cheese (a hard, aged Italian-style cheese)
A hard, granular cheese with a sharp, savory flavor, originally from Italy (Parmigiano-Reggiano). In Japan, パルメザンチーズ usually refers to the powdered version sold in green-topped shaker bottles for use on pasta, salads, and gratins, but it also covers genuine block Parmesan cheese.
パスタにパルメザンチーズをかける。
I sprinkle Parmesan cheese on the pasta.
サラダにすりおろしたパルメザンチーズをのせた。
I put grated Parmesan cheese on top of the salad.
粉(こな)のパルメザンチーズをスーパーで買(か)ってきた。
I bought powdered Parmesan cheese at the supermarket.
パルメザンチーズをたっぷりかけると、シンプルなパスタでも贅沢(ぜいたく)な味(あじ)になる。
Generously sprinkling on Parmesan cheese turns even a simple pasta into a luxurious-tasting dish.

パルメザンチーズ is the standard Japanese name for Parmesan-style cheese. Most Japanese consumers first encounter it as the powdered cheese sold in green-topped shaker containers, which is why パルメザンチーズ is often used loosely as a synonym for any sharp, dry, grated cheese sprinkled on pasta.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パルメザンチーズをかける: to sprinkle Parmesan cheese on (something)
  • パルメザンチーズをふる: to sprinkle / dust with Parmesan cheese
  • パルメザンチーズをすりおろす: to grate Parmesan cheese
  • 粉(こな)のパルメザンチーズ: powdered Parmesan cheese
  • パルメザンチーズの風味(ふうみ): Parmesan flavor

SIMILAR / RELATED TERMS:

  • パルミジャーノ・レッジャーノ: Parmigiano-Reggiano (the protected Italian-origin name)
  • 粉(こな)チーズ: powdered cheese (often used interchangeably with shaker-bottle parmesan)
  • ペコリーノ・ロマーノ: Pecorino Romano (a similar hard cheese, made from sheep's milk)
  • グラナ・パダーノ: Grana Padano (a closely related Italian hard cheese)
  • チーズ: cheese (general term)

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japanese supermarkets the powdered shaker-bottle product is labeled パルメザンチーズ even when, technically, it does not meet the EU's protected designation (which restricts the name "Parmigiano-Reggiano" to cheese from a specific region of Italy).
  • For block cheese, more specialty-oriented stores and restaurants tend to use the Italian name パルミジャーノ・レッジャーノ instead, signaling authenticity.
