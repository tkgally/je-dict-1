1. threat; intimidation; coercion; menace
The act of threatening someone with harm or negative consequences to force them to do something. Used in both everyday and legal contexts.
脅迫(きょうはく)電話(でんわ)がかかってきた。
A threatening phone call came in.
彼(かれ)は脅迫(きょうはく)されて金(かね)を渡(わた)した。
He was threatened and handed over the money.
脅迫(きょうはく)状(じょう)を送(おく)りつけた犯人(はんにん)が逮捕(たいほ)された。
The culprit who sent the threatening letter was arrested.

A serious word associated with criminal behavior and legal proceedings. In Japanese law, 脅迫罪(きょうはくざい) (the crime of intimidation) is defined under the Penal Code and involves threatening to harm someone's life, body, freedom, reputation, or property. Can be used with する as a suru-verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脅迫(きょうはく)電話(でんわ): threatening phone call
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)状(じょう): threatening letter
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)罪(ざい): crime of intimidation/threat
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)行為(こうい): act of intimidation
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)メール: threatening email
  • 脅迫(きょうはく)を受(う)ける: to receive a threat

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 恐喝(きょうかつ): blackmail; extortion — involves demanding money or valuables through threats; 脅迫(きょうはく) is the broader act of threatening
  • 脅(おど)し: threat; intimidation — a more colloquial word for threats
  • 威嚇(いかく): intimidation; menacing — emphasizes the display of force or power to frighten
  • 脅(おど)す: to threaten — the verb form; 脅迫(きょうはく)する is more formal
