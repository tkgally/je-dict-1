有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)

ゆうげんがいしゃ
noun
limited liability company; Ltd. company
1. limited liability company; Ltd. company
A type of Japanese small-business corporation in which the owners' liability is limited to their investment. New 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ) cannot be founded since the 2006 Companies Act, but existing ones continue to operate and the name is still common in business listings.
父(ちち)は有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)を経営(けいえい)している。
My father runs a limited liability company.
名刺(めいし)には有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)の社名(しゃめい)が書(か)かれていた。
The business card had the name of a limited liability company on it.
制度(せいど)が変(か)わったため、新(あら)たに有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)を設立(せつりつ)することはできない。
Because the law changed, it is no longer possible to newly establish a limited liability company.

Often abbreviated 有限(ゆうげん) or written as ㈲ before the company name. The 2006 Companies Act (会社法(かいしゃほう)) discontinued the category, but existing 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ) were grandfathered in and may still keep the title indefinitely.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)を設立(せつりつ)する: to establish a limited company
  • 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)を経営(けいえい)する: to run a limited company
  • 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ)○○(まるまる): "○○ Ltd." (in company names)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ): stock company; joint-stock corporation — much larger and more common form, with shares
  • 合同会社(ごうどうがいしゃ): LLC (Japanese-style) — the modern small-business form that replaced 有限会社(ゆうげんがいしゃ) after 2006
