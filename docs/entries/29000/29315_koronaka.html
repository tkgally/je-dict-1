コロナ禍(か)

ころなか
noun
the COVID-19 pandemic; the coronavirus crisis
1. the COVID-19 pandemic; the coronavirus crisis (as a period and its hardships)
The period of disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, together with the hardships it brought. The suffix 禍(か) means calamity or misfortune, so the word frames the pandemic as a time of trouble rather than just the disease itself.
コロナ禍(か)で生活(せいかつ)が変(か)わった。
Life changed during the coronavirus pandemic.
コロナ禍(か)で多(おお)くの店(みせ)が閉(し)まった。
Many shops closed during the COVID-19 pandemic.
コロナ禍(か)をきっかけに、在宅勤務(ざいたくきんむ)が一気(いっき)に広(ひろ)がった。
Spurred by the coronavirus pandemic, working from home spread rapidly.

A coinage from コロナ (corona, short for the coronavirus) plus the suffix 禍(か) (calamity, disaster). It refers to the pandemic as a difficult period and became extremely common from 2020 onward. Note that 禍(か) is read か here, not うず.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コロナ禍(か)で: amid the coronavirus pandemic
  • コロナ禍(か)の影響(えいきょう): the impact of the pandemic
  • コロナ禍(か)を経(へ)て: having gone through the pandemic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新型(しんがた)コロナ: COVID-19 (the novel coronavirus) — names the virus or disease itself, not the period
  • 感染(かんせん)拡大(かくだい): spread of infection — describes the spreading, not the era as a whole
