1. to collapse onto or into something; to fall and land heavily
To fall or collapse in a way that results in ending up on or inside something — a bed, a chair, a room, or a person. The ～込む suffix emphasizes the completeness of falling in or into a space.
疲(つか)れてベッドに倒(たお)れ込(こ)んだ。
I was so tired I collapsed onto the bed.
彼女(かのじょ)は部屋(へや)に入(はい)るなり、ソファに倒(たお)れ込(こ)んだ。
As soon as she entered the room, she collapsed onto the sofa.
走(はし)り終(お)えた選手(せんしゅ)はその場(ば)に倒(たお)れ込(こ)み、しばらく動(うご)けなかった。
The athlete who had finished running collapsed on the spot and couldn't move for a while.

Compound of 倒(たお)れる (to fall, to collapse) + 込(こ)む (directional suffix meaning 'into'). The ～込む suffix adds the sense of falling completely into or onto a space, often due to exhaustion or sudden loss of strength.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ベッドに倒(たお)れ込(こ)む: to collapse onto the bed
  • ソファに倒(たお)れ込(こ)む: to fall onto the sofa
  • その場(ば)に倒(たお)れ込(こ)む: to collapse right there
  • 疲(つか)れて倒(たお)れ込(こ)む: to collapse from exhaustion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 倒(たお)れる: to fall, to collapse — the base verb, without the implication of landing inside/onto something
  • ぶっ倒(たお)れる: to drop, to keel over — more sudden, can imply illness or shock
  • 崩(くず)れ落(お)ちる: to crumble and fall — usually of structures, not people
