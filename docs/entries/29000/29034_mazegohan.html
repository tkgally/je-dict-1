混(ま)ぜご飯(はん)

まぜごはん
noun
mixed rice; rice mixed with toppings after cooking
1. mixed rice; rice stirred together with cooked ingredients
A rice dish made by mixing cooked rice with separately prepared ingredients such as vegetables, mushrooms, seafood, or meat. Distinguished from 炊き込みご飯, where the ingredients are cooked together with the rice from the start.
鮭(さけ)の混(ま)ぜご飯(はん)を作(つく)る。
To make salmon mixed rice.
残(のこ)り野菜(やさい)で混(ま)ぜご飯(はん)にしよう。
Let's make mixed rice with the leftover vegetables.
炊(た)きたてのご飯(はん)に具(ぐ)を混(ま)ぜるだけで美味(おい)しい混(ま)ぜご飯(はん)ができる。
Just mix ingredients into freshly cooked rice and you have a delicious mixed rice dish.

A rice dish where plain cooked rice is tossed with ingredients and seasonings. The key distinction from similar dishes: the mixing happens after cooking.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 炊(た)き込(こ)みご飯(はん): seasoned rice cooked with ingredients from the start (the ingredients and broth are added to raw rice before cooking)
  • チャーハン: fried rice (stir-fried in a wok with oil)
  • 寿司(すし)めし / 酢飯(めし): sushi rice (vinegar-seasoned mixed rice)
