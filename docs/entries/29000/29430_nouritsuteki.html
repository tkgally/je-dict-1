能率的(のうりつてき)

のうりつてき
na-adjective
efficient; productive
1. efficient; productive
Getting a large amount of work done in a given time with little wasted effort. Emphasizes output and the pace of work.
能率的(のうりつてき)に働(はたら)く。
to work efficiently.
もっと能率的(のうりつてき)な方法(ほうほう)を探(さが)そう。
Let's look for a more efficient method.
道具(どうぐ)を上手(じょうず)に使(つか)えば、作業(さぎょう)がずっと能率的(のうりつてき)になる。
If you use your tools well, the work becomes much more efficient.

Written with 能率(のうりつ) (efficiency, output) + 的(てき) (-like, -al). Describes work or methods that produce a lot in little time.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 能率的(のうりつてき)に (efficiently)
  • Noun base: 能率(のうりつ) (efficiency, productivity)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 効率的(こうりつてき): efficient — focuses on achieving results with minimal waste of resources
  • 合理的(ごうりてき): rational — emphasizes sound, logical reasoning rather than speed of output
