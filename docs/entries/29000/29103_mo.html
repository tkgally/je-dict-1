喪(も)

も
noun
mourning; bereavement
1. mourning; period of mourning after a death
The period of grief and abstention from celebrations following the death of a family member or close relative. Traditionally lasting 49 days in Buddhism.
喪(も)に服(ふく)す。
To observe mourning.
祖父(そふ)が亡(な)くなり、喪(も)に服(ふく)している。
My grandfather passed away and I am in mourning.
喪(も)が明(あ)けるまでは祝(いわ)い事(ごと)を控(ひか)える習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
There is a custom of refraining from celebratory events until the mourning period ends.

喪(も)に服(ふく)す (to be in mourning) is the most common set phrase. The mourning period is called 喪中(もちゅう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喪(も)に服(ふく)す: to observe mourning, to be in mourning
  • 喪中(もちゅう): in mourning (period)
  • 喪服(もふく): mourning dress/clothes
  • 喪主(もしゅ): chief mourner
  • 喪(も)が明(あ)ける: for the mourning period to end

NOTE:
喪中(もちゅう) is written on postcards (喪中はがき) sent to notify people that the sender will not be sending New Year's greeting cards.
