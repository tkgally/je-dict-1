1. play; a move; performance (in a sport or game)
An action or move made by a player in a sport or game, or the overall manner of a player's performance. From English "play." Used as a verb with する.
ナイスプレー！
Nice play!
彼(かれ)のプレーは素晴(すば)らしい。
His play is wonderful.
選手(せんしゅ)たちは最後(さいご)まで全力(ぜんりょく)でプレーした。
The players played with all their might until the very end.

Loanword from English "play." Refers to action in sports and games; with する it means to play (a sport).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ナイスプレー: nice play (a good move)
  • ファインプレー: a fine play; a brilliant move
  • フェアプレー: fair play; sportsmanship
  • プレーする: to play (a sport or game)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 演技(えんぎ): performance, acting — used for figure skating and gymnastics routines, and for stage acting
  • 試合(しあい): match, game — the whole contest, not an individual play within it
