バチバチ

ばちばち
adverb
crackling; snapping; sparking
1. crackling; snapping; the sound of sparks or larger flames
The sound of something crackling sharply and repeatedly, such as a large fire, sparks from electricity, or oil splattering. Represents a louder, sharper crackling than ぱちぱち.
焚火(たきび)がバチバチと燃(も)えている。
The bonfire is crackling and burning.
電線(でんせん)がバチバチと火花(ひばな)を散(ち)らした。
The power line crackled and sent out sparks.
鉄板(てっぱん)の上(うえ)で油(あぶら)がバチバチとはねて、顔(かお)まで飛(と)んできた。
Oil was splattering noisily on the iron griddle and flying up to my face.

A mimetic/onomatopoeic word for sharp, repeated crackling sounds — louder and more intense than ぱちぱち.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 火(ひ)/焚火(たきび): crackling fire
  • 静電気(せいでんき): static electricity sparking
  • 油(あぶら)/揚(あ)げ物(もの): oil splattering when frying
  • 電気(でんき): electrical sparking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぱちぱち: lighter crackling (small fire, applause, flapping) — softer than バチバチ
  • バリバリ: sharp tearing or crunching — suggests more force
  • パチパチ: same word in katakana for emphasis or electrical connotation
