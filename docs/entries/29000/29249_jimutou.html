事務棟(じむとう)

じむとう
noun
office building; administration wing
1. office building; administration wing
The building or wing of a larger facility that houses the offices and administrative functions, as opposed to factory, classroom, or laboratory buildings.
受付(うけつけ)は事務棟(じむとう)にあります。
The reception is in the office building.
工場(こうじょう)の隣(となり)に事務棟(じむとう)が建(た)っている。
The office building stands next to the factory.
手続(てつづ)きをするには、まず事務棟(じむとう)の二階(にかい)に行(い)ってください。
To complete the paperwork, please first go to the second floor of the administration building.

Formed from 事務(じむ) (office work, administration) + 棟(とう) (a counter and word for a building or wing). Used on campuses, hospital grounds, and factory sites to name the administrative building.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事務棟(じむとう)の入口(いりぐち): the entrance to the office building
  • 本部(ほんぶ)事務棟(じむとう): headquarters office building

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 事務所(じむしょ): office — a workplace office in general, not specifically a separate building on a larger site
  • 本館(ほんかん): main building — the principal building of a complex, regardless of its function
