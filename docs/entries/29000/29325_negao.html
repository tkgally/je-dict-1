寝顔(ねがお)

ねがお
noun
sleeping face; face of someone asleep
1. sleeping face; face of someone asleep
The face or facial expression of a person (or animal) while asleep. Often used affectionately.
子(こ)どもの寝顔(ねがお)はかわいい。
A child's sleeping face is adorable.
彼女(かのじょ)の寝顔(ねがお)をそっと見(み)つめた。
I quietly gazed at her sleeping face.
赤(あか)ちゃんの寝顔(ねがお)を見(み)ていると、一日(いちにち)の疲(つか)れも吹(ふ)き飛(と)ぶ。
Watching the baby's sleeping face makes the whole day's tiredness melt away.

Composed of 寝(ね)る (to sleep) + 顔(かお) (face). Refers to how someone looks while asleep, and carries a warm, affectionate nuance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝顔(ねがお)を見(み)る: to look at someone's sleeping face
  • かわいい寝顔(ねがお): an adorable sleeping face
  • 寝顔(ねがお)を写真(しゃしん)に撮(と)る: to take a photo of a sleeping face

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寝相(ねぞう): sleeping posture — the position of the body while asleep, not the face.
