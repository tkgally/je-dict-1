〜ように言(い)う

ようにいう
expression
to tell (someone) to; to ask (someone) to; to instruct to
1. to tell (someone) to; to ask (someone) to; to instruct to
Used to report an indirect command or request. The speaker conveys what they told or asked someone else to do. Follows the dictionary or nai form of a verb.
先生(せんせい)は静(しず)かにするように言(い)った。
The teacher told us to be quiet.
彼(かれ)に早(はや)く来(く)るように言(い)ってください。
Please tell him to come soon.
医者(いしゃ)から、無理(むり)をしないように言(い)われた。
I was told by the doctor not to overdo it.

Used for indirect speech when reporting that someone was told or asked to do something.

GRAMMAR:

  • [Person に] Verb (dict form) + ように言(い)う: to tell/ask someone to do ~
  • [Person に] Verb (nai form) + ように言(い)う: to tell/ask someone not to do ~
  • 受身形(うけみけい) ように言(い)われる: to be told to do ~

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜するように言(い)う: tell someone to do ~
  • 〜しないように言(い)う: tell someone not to do ~
  • 〜ように言(い)われた: was told to ~
  • 〜ように頼(たの)む: to ask someone to ~ (softer than 言う)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜ように頼(たの)む: to ask someone to — more polite/indirect than 言う
  • 〜ように伝(つた)える: to pass along the message to — used when relaying through a third party
