1. to stifle; to suppress (a yawn, laugh, or impulse)
To suppress or hold back a physical or emotional response, especially a yawn or laugh. The image is of biting something to stop it from escaping.
あくび(あくび)を噛(か)み殺(ころ)す。
To stifle a yawn.
笑(わら)いを噛(か)み殺(ころ)して真剣(しんけん)な顔(かお)をした。
I suppressed a laugh and kept a serious face.
退屈(たいくつ)な会議(かいぎ)の中(なか)で、何度(なんど)もあくびを噛(か)み殺(ころ)さなければならなかった。
During the boring meeting, I had to stifle yawns many times.

A compound verb formed from 噛(か)む (to bite) + 殺(ころ)す (to kill/suppress). Used figuratively to mean holding back an involuntary response.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あくびを噛(か)み殺(ころ)す: to stifle a yawn
  • 笑(わら)いを噛(か)み殺(ころ)す: to suppress a laugh
  • 怒(いか)りを噛(か)み殺(ころ)す: to suppress anger

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 抑(おさ)える: to hold back, to suppress — more general; can apply to emotions or physical actions
  • 堪(こら)える: to endure, to hold back — implies effort; used for tears, laughter, pain
