銀屏風(ぎんびょうぶ)

ぎんびょうぶ
noun
silver folding screen
1. silver folding screen
A 屏風(びょうぶ) (folding screen) covered in silver leaf. Conceptually paired with the 金屏風(きんびょうぶ) (gold screen) but quieter in impression, used for more subdued or refined settings.
銀屏風(ぎんびょうぶ)を立(た)てる。
to set up a silver folding screen
茶室(ちゃしつ)に銀屏風(ぎんびょうぶ)が飾(かざ)られていた。
A silver folding screen was displayed in the tea room.
金屏風(きんびょうぶ)と銀屏風(ぎんびょうぶ)では受(う)ける印象(いんしょう)がだいぶ違(ちが)う。
A gold screen and a silver screen give quite different impressions.

The silver-leaf counterpart of the 金屏風(きんびょうぶ); calmer and more understated in feeling.

RELATED:

  • 屏風(びょうぶ): folding screen
  • 金屏風(きんびょうぶ): gold folding screen — brighter, more celebratory
