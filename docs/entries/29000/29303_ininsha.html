委任者(いにんしゃ)

いにんしゃ
noun
mandator; the entrusting party
1. mandator; party who entrusts a matter to another
In a contract of mandate or delegation, the person who entrusts a task, authority, or decision to another party. The one who accepts the entrusted matter is the 受任者(じゅにんしゃ).
委任者(いにんしゃ)の署名(しょめい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
The mandator's signature is required.
委任者(いにんしゃ)は代理人(だいりにん)に権限(けんげん)を与(あた)える。
The mandator grants authority to the agent.
契約書(けいやくしょ)には委任者(いにんしゃ)と受任者(じゅにんしゃ)の氏名(しめい)を記載(きさい)する。
The contract states the names of both the mandator and the mandatary.

A legal and business term. The 委任者(いにんしゃ) delegates authority, while the 受任者(じゅにんしゃ) accepts and carries it out.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 委任者(いにんしゃ)の意思(いし): the mandator's intent
  • 委任者(いにんしゃ)に代(か)わって: on behalf of the mandator

RELATED WORDS:

  • 受任者(じゅにんしゃ): mandatary — the party that accepts the entrusted task
  • 委任状(いにんじょう): power of attorney — the document granting the authority
