落ち込み(おちこみ)

おちこみ
noun
depression; slump; decline; low mood
1. emotional depression; low spirits; funk
A state of feeling down, depressed, or discouraged. The noun form of the verb 落ち込む. Used to describe temporary low moods rather than clinical depression.
失敗(しっぱい)して、一時(いちじ)落ち込み(おちこみ)があった。
After the failure, there was a period of feeling down.
落ち込み(おちこみ)から立(た)ち直(なお)るのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to recover from the slump in spirits.
2. slump; decline; drop (in figures, performance)
A period of reduced activity, performance, or numbers, especially in business or economic contexts. Equivalent to a slump or downturn.
今月(こんげつ)の売上(うりあげ)は先月(せんげつ)より落ち込み(おちこみ)が大きい(おおきい)。
This month's sales have seen a bigger slump than last month.
景気(けいき)の落ち込み(おちこみ)が予想(よそう)より深刻(しんこく)だった。
The economic slump was more serious than expected.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落ち込む(おちこむ): the base verb (to feel depressed; to slump)
  • {スランプ}: slump — mostly for performance (sports, work); loanword
  • 低迷(ていめい): prolonged slump — implies sustained stagnation at a low level

USAGE:
Sense 1 describes a temporary emotional state; it is milder than 鬱 (うつ, depression). Sense 2 describes quantitative decline (sales, production, performance).
