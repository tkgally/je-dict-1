1. surgical removal; excision; extraction
The medical act of removing an organ, tumor, or other tissue from the body through surgery. Also used more generally for extracting or removing objects.
腫瘍(しゅよう)の摘出(てきしゅつ)手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)けた。
I underwent surgery to remove a tumor.
盲腸(もうちょう)を摘出(てきしゅつ)することになった。
It was decided that my appendix would be removed.
医師(いし)は患者(かんじゃ)の体内(たいない)に残(のこ)っていた異物(いぶつ)を慎重(しんちょう)に摘出(てきしゅつ)した。
The doctor carefully removed the foreign object that had remained inside the patient's body.

摘(てき) originally means to pluck or pick; 出(しゅつ) means to take out. The compound is standard medical terminology for removing tissue or organs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜を摘出(てきしゅつ)する: to surgically remove 〜
  • 摘出(てきしゅつ)手術(しゅじゅつ): removal surgery
  • 臓器(ぞうき)摘出(てきしゅつ): organ removal
  • 腫瘍(しゅよう)摘出(てきしゅつ): tumor removal
  • 子宮(しきゅう)摘出(てきしゅつ): hysterectomy (uterus removal)
