聞(き)き応(ごた)えがある

ききごたえがある
expression
worth listening to; engaging to hear
1. worth listening to; engaging to hear
Said of a story, speech, music, or interview that has enough substance, skill, or depth to reward the listener. The opposite of dull or shallow.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)は聞(き)き応(ごた)えがある。
His stories are worth listening to.
聞(き)き応(ごた)えのある講演(こうえん)だった。
It was a lecture worth listening to.
ベテラン歌手(かしゅ)の歌唱力(かしょうりょく)はやはり聞(き)き応(ごた)えがある。
As expected, the vocal skill of a veteran singer is engaging to hear.

Compound of 聞(き)く (to listen) + 応(ごた)え (response, effect) + がある (there is). The 〜応(ごた)えがある pattern is productive: 食(た)べ応(ごた)えがある (filling), 読(よ)み応(ごた)えがある (worth reading), 見(み)応(ごた)えがある (worth watching).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 聞(き)き応(ごた)えのある話(はなし): a story worth hearing
  • 聞(き)き応(ごた)えのある演奏(えんそう): a performance worth listening to
  • 実(じつ)に聞(き)き応(ごた)えがある: truly worth listening to

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い: interesting — intellectual interest, doesn't imply substance
  • 面白(おもしろ)い: interesting, fun — broader and more casual
  • 見(み)応(ごた)えがある: worth watching — the visual counterpart
