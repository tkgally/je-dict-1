ホームシック

ほーむしっく
noun
homesickness
1. homesickness; longing for home
The feeling of longing for one's home, family, or familiar surroundings when living away from home. Often experienced by students, overseas workers, or anyone living far from their hometown.
ホームシックになった。
I got homesick.
留学(りゅうがく)してすぐ、ひどいホームシックにかかった。
Right after starting study abroad, I got a bad case of homesickness.
一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしを始(はじ)めて三(み)ヶ月(げつ)が経(た)ったが、時々(ときどき)ひどいホームシックに悩(なや)まされる。
Three months have passed since I started living alone, but I am sometimes troubled by severe homesickness.

From English "homesick." Used as a noun in Japanese, typically with になる (to become) or にかかる (to come down with).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ホームシックになる: to become homesick
  • ホームシックにかかる: to get homesick
  • ホームシックを感(かん)じる: to feel homesick
  • 激(はげ)しいホームシック: severe homesickness
  • ホームシックを克服(こくふく)する: to overcome homesickness
