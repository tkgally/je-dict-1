除毛(じょもう)クリーム

じょもうくりーむ
noun
hair removal cream; depilatory cream
1. hair removal cream; depilatory cream
A cream applied to the skin to chemically dissolve and remove body hair. Applied, left for a few minutes, then wiped off along with the hair. Used as an alternative to shaving or waxing.
除毛(じょもう)クリームで足(あし)の毛(け)を処理(しょり)した。
I removed the hair on my legs with depilatory cream.
除毛(じょもう)クリームは肌(はだ)が弱(よわ)い人(ひと)には合(あ)わない場合(ばあい)がある。
Hair removal cream may not be suitable for people with sensitive skin.
除毛(じょもう)クリームを使(つか)う前(まえ)に、必(かなら)ずパッチテストをしてください。
Before using hair removal cream, be sure to do a patch test.

Combines 除毛(じょもう) (hair removal, from 除(のぞ)く + 毛(け)) and クリーム (cream). A common consumer beauty product available at drugstores and convenience stores.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 除毛(じょもう)クリームを塗(ぬ)る: to apply hair removal cream
  • 除毛(じょもう)クリームで処理(しょり)する: to treat with depilatory cream

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脱毛(だつもう)クリーム: depilatory cream — similar term, slightly more clinical
  • ワックス脱毛(だつもう): waxing — removes hair at the root
