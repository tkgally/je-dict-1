預金利率

よきんりりつ
noun
deposit interest rate
1. deposit interest rate; rate paid on savings
The rate of interest a bank pays on money held in deposit accounts, expressed as an annual percentage. A standard term in personal finance and banking.
預金利率(よきんりりつ)が下(さ)がる。
for the deposit interest rate to fall.
この銀行(ぎんこう)は預金利率(よきんりりつ)が高(たか)い。
This bank offers a high deposit interest rate.
長(なが)く続(つづ)く低金利(ていきんり)で、預金利率(よきんりりつ)はほとんどゼロに近(ちか)い。
After a long stretch of low interest rates, the deposit rate is almost zero.

The rate earned by the depositor; set low in Japan during long periods of low interest rates.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 預金利率(よきんりりつ)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げる: to raise the deposit rate
  • 預金利率(よきんりりつ)を比較(ひかく)する: to compare deposit rates
  • 高(たか)い預金利率(よきんりりつ): a high deposit rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 金利(きんり): interest rate — the broad term, covering both deposits and loans
  • 貸出金利(かしだしきんり): lending rate — the rate a bank charges on loans, not pays on deposits
