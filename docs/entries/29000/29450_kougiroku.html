講義録

こうぎろく
noun
lecture notes; record of a lecture; transcript
1. written record of a lecture; lecture notes
A written or printed record of the content of a lecture or course, whether taken down by a student or published. Often compiled into booklets or course materials.
講義録(こうぎろく)を読(よ)む。
to read the lecture notes.
欠席(けっせき)した学生(がくせい)に講義録(こうぎろく)を渡(わた)した。
I gave the lecture notes to the student who was absent.
有名(ゆうめい)な教授(きょうじゅ)の講義録(こうぎろく)が一冊(いっさつ)の本(ほん)にまとめられた。
A famous professor's lectures were compiled into a single book.

Refers to the record itself, which may be handwritten notes, a transcript, or a published volume.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 講義録(こうぎろく)を取(と)る: to take lecture notes
  • 講義録(こうぎろく)をまとめる: to compile lecture notes
  • 講義録(こうぎろく)を配(くば)る: to hand out lecture notes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議事録(ぎじろく): meeting minutes — the record of a meeting rather than a lecture
  • 講義(こうぎ): lecture — the spoken lecture itself, not its written record
