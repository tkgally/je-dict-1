たわし

たわし
noun
scrubbing brush; scouring pad
1. scrubbing brush; scouring pad for kitchen use
A stiff brush or abrasive pad used for scrubbing pots, vegetables, and other items. Traditionally made from palm fibers; modern versions include metal and nylon varieties.
たわしで鍋(なべ)を磨(みが)く。
Scrub the pot with a scouring brush.
金属(きんぞく)たわしはフライパン(ふらいぱん)に傷(きず)がつく。
A metal scouring pad will scratch a frying pan.
台所(だいどころ)のたわしで野菜(やさい)の泥(どろ)を落(おと)とした。
I scrubbed the dirt off the vegetables with the kitchen brush.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 棕櫚(しゅろ)たわし: palm-fiber scrubbing brush (traditional type)
  • 金属(きんぞく)たわし: metal scouring pad (steel wool type)
  • たわしで洗(あら)う: to scrub with a scouring brush
  • たわしで磨(みが)く: to scour and polish

USAGE:
Usually written in kana alone. The object of scrubbing can be pots, root vegetables, or other sturdy surfaces. Softer objects like non-stick pans should use スポンジ (sponge) instead.
