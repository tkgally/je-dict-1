薄(うす)め

うすめ
adjective-na
slightly thin; on the light side; a bit diluted
1. somewhat thin, light, or diluted; on the weak side
Formed from 薄(うす)い (thin, light, weak, diluted) + め (suffix indicating 'somewhat' or 'on the _ side'). Describes something that is moderately thin, light in color, diluted, or sparse — slightly below average intensity.
コーヒーは薄(うす)めにしてください。
Please make the coffee on the weak side.
色(いろ)は薄(うす)めのブルーを選(えら)んだ。
I chose a lighter shade of blue.
今日(きょう)は薄(うす)めに塩(しお)を振(ふ)って、素材(そざい)の味(あじ)を生(い)かした料理(りょうり)にした。
Today I seasoned lightly with salt and made a dish that brings out the natural flavors.

Formed by attaching め to the adjective stem 薄(うす). This め suffix softens the adjective: '(a little) on the thin/light side.' It is used for color, liquid concentration, fabric thickness, and seasoning.

COMMON USES:

  • 味(あじ)は薄(うす)めに: with light seasoning
  • 色(いろ)は薄(うす)めに: in a light shade
  • 薄(うす)めに切(き)る: to cut thinly
  • 薄(うす)めに塗(ぬ)る: to apply thinly

NOTE: The め suffix can attach to many adjective stems: 厚(あつ)め (somewhat thick), 濃(こ)いめ/こいめ (somewhat strong/dark), 多(おお)め (somewhat much), etc.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 薄(うす)い: thin, light, diluted — the base adjective; stronger than うすめ
  • 淡(あわ)い: faint, pale — emphasizes delicateness of color or sensation
