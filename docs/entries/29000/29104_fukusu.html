1. to observe; to comply with; to submit to (a rule, period, or authority)
To follow or observe a prescribed duty, rule, or period. Most commonly used in the phrase 喪に服す (to observe mourning). Also written 服する, the longer form.
喪(も)に服(ふく)す。
To observe mourning.
親(おや)の死後(しご)、一年間(いちねんかん)喪(も)に服(ふく)した。
After my parent's death, I observed mourning for one year.
判決(はんけつ)に服(ふく)して刑務所(けいむしょ)に入(はい)った。
He submitted to the verdict and entered prison.

A formal verb most commonly encountered in the fixed phrase 喪(も)に服(ふく)す (to be in mourning). The longer form 服(ふく)する is equally correct and more common in writing.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 喪(も)に服(ふく)す: to observe mourning
  • 判決(はんけつ)に服(ふく)す: to submit to a court ruling
  • 命令(めいれい)に服(ふく)す: to comply with an order

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 従(したが)う: to follow, to obey — more general, everyday usage
  • 従事(じゅうじ)する: to engage in, to be involved in — used for occupations
