入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)

にゅうかんしゃ
noun
visitor; patron; person entering a building or facility
1. visitor; patron; person entering a building or facility
A person who enters a public building or facility such as a museum, library, government building, or sports arena. Used especially in formal or institutional contexts.
今日(きょう)の入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)は千人(せんにん)を超(こ)えた。
Today's visitors exceeded one thousand people.
入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)は受付(うけつけ)で名前(なまえ)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Visitors, please write your name at the reception desk.
その美術館(びじゅつかん)は開館(かいかん)から一週間(いっしゅうかん)で入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)が一万人(いちまんにん)に達(たっ)した。
That art museum reached ten thousand visitors within one week of opening.

Used in formal and institutional settings. The related compound 入館者数(にゅうかんしゃすう) (number of visitors) is very common in reports and announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)数(すう): number of visitors
  • 入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)を迎(むか)える: to receive visitors
  • 入館者(にゅうかんしゃ)記録(きろく): visitor record
