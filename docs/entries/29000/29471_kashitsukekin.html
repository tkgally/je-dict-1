貸付金(かしつけきん)

かしつけきん
noun
loan; loaned money (from the lender's side)
1. loan; loaned money (lender's perspective)
Money that has been lent out and is owed back, seen from the lender's side. On a balance sheet it is an asset; the same money viewed from the borrower's side is 借入金(かりいれきん).
会社(かいしゃ)の貸付金(かしつけきん)が増(ふ)えた。
The company's loans receivable increased.
貸付金(かしつけきん)は資産(しさん)として計上(けいじょう)される。
Loans receivable are recorded as an asset.
役員(やくいん)への貸付金(かしつけきん)の利息(りそく)が問題(もんだい)になった。
The interest on the loan to a company officer became an issue.

Compound of 貸付(かしつけ) (lending) + 金(きん) (money). An accounting and finance term taken from the lender's point of view.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 短期(たんき)貸付金(かしつけきん): short-term loans receivable
  • 貸付金(かしつけきん)の回収(かいしゅう): collection of a loan
  • 貸付金(かしつけきん)の残高(ざんだか): outstanding loan balance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 借入金(かりいれきん): borrowings — the same money from the borrower's side, recorded as a liability
  • 融資(ゆうし): financing, lending — the act of providing funds, often by a bank
