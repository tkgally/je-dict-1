有期(ゆうき)懲役(ちょうえき)

ゆうきちょうえき
noun
fixed-term imprisonment; definite-term prison sentence
1. fixed-term imprisonment; prison sentence of a specified duration
A prison sentence for a defined period, as opposed to 無期懲役(むきちょうえき) (life imprisonment). Under Japanese law, the range is 1 month to 30 years, typically stated as 懲役(ちょうえき)X年(ねん) (X years of imprisonment).
裁判所(さいばんしょ)は被告(ひこく)に有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)10年(ねん)を言(い)い渡(わた)した。
The court sentenced the defendant to ten years of imprisonment.
有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)の最長(さいちょう)は30年(ねん)とされている。
The maximum fixed-term imprisonment is 30 years.
無期懲役(むきちょうえき)と有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)では、釈放(しゃくほう)の見通し(みとおし)が大(おお)きく異(こと)なる。
Life imprisonment and fixed-term imprisonment differ greatly in terms of prospects for release.

有期(ゆうき) means "limited period," and 懲役(ちょうえき) means "imprisonment with labor." Together they describe a definite-length custodial sentence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)○年(ねん): X years of fixed-term imprisonment
  • 有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)を言(い)い渡(わた)す: to hand down a fixed-term sentence
  • 有期懲役(ゆうきちょうえき)の判決(はんけつ): a verdict of fixed-term imprisonment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無期懲役(むきちょうえき): life imprisonment — indefinite sentence
  • 禁錮(きんこ): imprisonment without labor — same duration range but without the labor component
