尾翼(びよく)

びよく
noun
tail (of an aircraft); tail fin
1. tail of an aircraft; tail assembly; tail fin
The tail section at the rear of an aircraft, including the vertical and horizontal stabilizers, which keeps the plane stable in flight.
飛行機(ひこうき)の尾翼(びよく)。
the tail of an airplane
尾翼(びよく)に航空会社(こうくうがいしゃ)のマークが描(えが)かれている。
The airline's logo is painted on the tail.
尾翼(びよく)は機体(きたい)の姿勢(しせい)を安定(あんてい)させる重要(じゅうよう)な部分(ぶぶん)だ。
The tail is an important part that keeps the aircraft's attitude stable.

The tail assembly of an aircraft. Built from 尾(び) (tail) and 翼(よく) (wing).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 垂直(すいちょく)尾翼(びよく): vertical stabilizer (vertical tail fin)
  • 水平(すいへい)尾翼(びよく): horizontal stabilizer
  • 尾翼(びよく)のマーク: the logo on the tail

RELATED:

  • 主翼(しゅよく): main wing
  • 機体(きたい): aircraft body; airframe
