1. to push down; to knock down; to shove over
To shove or thrust at a person so hard that they fall to the ground. Compound of 突(つ)く (to thrust) + 倒(たお)す (to knock down). The person pushed is marked with を.
相手(あいて)を突(つ)き倒(たお)す。
to push one's opponent down
彼(かれ)はぶつかってきた男(おとこ)を突(つ)き倒(たお)した。
He shoved down the man who bumped into him.
駅(えき)の混雑(こんざつ)で、後(うし)ろから人(ひと)に突(つ)き倒(たお)されそうになった。
In the crowd at the station, I was nearly shoved over from behind.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). The person pushed is marked with を. Compound of 突(つ)く (to thrust) + 倒(たお)す (to knock down).

USAGE:
Emphasizes a violent, forceful push that knocks someone off their feet, stronger than plain 押(お)す (to push).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 力(ちから)いっぱい突(つ)き倒(たお)す: to shove down with all one's force
  • 地面(じめん)に突(つ)き倒(たお)す: to knock down to the ground
