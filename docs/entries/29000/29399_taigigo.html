対義語(たいぎご)

たいぎご
noun
antonym; opposite word
1. antonym; opposite word
A word opposite in meaning to another — the standard term for an antonym (e.g., 高(たか)い vs 低(ひく)い). The opposite concept is 類義語(るいぎご) (synonym, near-synonym).
「大(おお)きい」の対義語(たいぎご)は「小(ちい)さい」だ。
The antonym of "big" is "small."
対義語(たいぎご)を覚(おぼ)えると語彙(ごい)が増(ふ)える。
Learning antonyms increases your vocabulary.
テストでは単語(たんご)の対義語(たいぎご)を答(こた)える問題(もんだい)が出(で)た。
The test had questions asking for the antonyms of words.

A grammar and vocabulary term used in language study.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対語(はんたいご): antonym — an everyday synonym of 対義語(たいぎご)
  • 類義語(るいぎご): synonym, near-synonym — the opposite concept
  • 同義語(どうぎご): synonym (identical meaning)
