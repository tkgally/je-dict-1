天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)

てんちばんぶつ
noun
all things in heaven and earth; all of creation
1. all things in heaven and earth; all of creation
Everything that exists in the universe — the whole of nature and all the things within it.
天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)を創造(そうぞう)する。
Create all things in heaven and earth.
天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)には命(いのち)が宿(やど)ると考(かんが)えられてきた。
It has long been believed that life dwells in all things of creation.
古代(こだい)の人々(ひとびと)は、天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)に神(かみ)が宿(やど)っていると信(しん)じていた。
The people of ancient times believed that gods dwelled in all things of heaven and earth.

A four-character idiom (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) made of 天地(てんち) (heaven and earth) + 万物(ばんぶつ) (all things). Literary and somewhat philosophical or religious in tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)の創造(そうぞう): the creation of all things
  • 天地万物(てんちばんぶつ)を支配(しはい)する: to rule over all of creation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 森羅万象(しんらばんしょう): all things in the universe — a close synonym, even more sweeping in scope
  • 万物(ばんぶつ): all things — the plainer component word, used on its own
