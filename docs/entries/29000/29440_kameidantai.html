加盟団体(かめいだんたい)

かめいだんたい
noun
member organization; affiliated body
1. member organization; affiliated body
An organization that has joined a larger federation, league, or alliance as a member.
加盟団体(かめいだんたい)が増(ふ)えた。
The number of member organizations increased.
各(かく)加盟団体(かめいだんたい)の代表(だいひょう)が集(あつ)まった。
Representatives of each member organization gathered.
この連盟(れんめい)には全国(ぜんこく)から百(ひゃく)を超(こ)える加盟団体(かめいだんたい)が参加(さんか)している。
This federation has over a hundred member organizations participating from across the country.

Written 加盟(かめい) (joining, affiliation) + 団体(だんたい) (organization, body). Common in contexts involving leagues, federations, and international bodies.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加盟国(かめいこく): member nation — the same 加盟(かめい) applied to countries (e.g. of the UN)
  • 会員(かいいん): member — refers to an individual member rather than an organization
