はじめに

はじめに
expression
to begin with; first of all; Introduction (section heading)
1. to begin with; first of all; at the outset
An adverbial phrase introducing the first thing said or done, often used to open a speech, explanation, or set of instructions.
はじめに自己紹介(じこしょうかい)をします。
To begin with, I'll introduce myself.
はじめに結論(けつろん)を述(の)べます。
First of all, let me state the conclusion.
はじめに、今日(きょう)の流(なが)れについて簡単(かんたん)に説明(せつめい)しておきます。
To begin with, let me briefly explain today's schedule.
2. Introduction; Preface (section heading)
A standard heading at the front of a book or document, especially non-fiction, marking the introductory section. Equivalent to an English "Introduction" or "Preface."
「はじめに」を読(よ)む。
To read the 'Introduction.'
この本(ほん)の「はじめに」に執筆(しっぴつ)の目的(もくてき)が書(か)いてある。
The aim of writing is stated in this book's 'Introduction.'
論文(ろんぶん)の「はじめに」では、研究(けんきゅう)の背景(はいけい)と目的(もくてき)を述(の)べるのが一般的(いっぱんてき)だ。
In a paper's 'Introduction,' it is common to state the background and aim of the research.

Sense 1 is the everyday adverbial use; sense 2 is the fixed section heading, usually written in kana and set in quotation marks or as a title.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • まず: first — a plainer adverb for the same opening function as sense 1.
  • 序文(じょぶん): preface — a more formal heading than はじめに for sense 2.
  • おわりに: in closing — the counterpart heading for the final section.
