ポルトガル語(ご)

ぽるとがるご
noun
Portuguese language
1. Portuguese language
The Romance language spoken in Portugal, Brazil, and several other countries. In Japan, Portuguese has historical significance as one of the first European languages introduced through contact with Portuguese missionaries and traders in the 16th century. Brazil's large Japanese diaspora also means Portuguese-Japanese bilingualism is notable.
ポルトガル語(ご)を勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I am studying Portuguese.
彼女(かのじょ)はポルトガル語(ご)とスペイン語(ご)の両方(りょうほう)が話(はな)せる。
She can speak both Portuguese and Spanish.
ポルトガル語(ご)は世界(せかい)で約(やく)2億(おく)5千万(せんまん)人(にん)に話(はな)されており、主(おも)にブラジルとポルトガルで使(つか)われている。
Portuguese is spoken by about 250 million people worldwide and is mainly used in Brazil and Portugal.

Portuguese has had notable influence on Japanese vocabulary since the 16th century. Many Japanese loanwords come from Portuguese, including 天麩羅(てんぷら) (tempura, from 'tempero'), パン (bread, from 'pão'), and タバコ(たばこ) (tobacco).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ポルトガル語(ご)圏(けん): Portuguese-speaking world
  • ポルトガル語(ご)で話(はな)す: to speak in Portuguese
  • ポルトガル語(ご)の通訳(つうやく): Portuguese interpreter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スペイン語(ご): Spanish — closely related Romance language also spoken in many countries
  • ポルトガル: Portugal — the country (not to be confused with ポルトガル語 the language)
