だったら

だったら
expression
if that's the case; if it is; then
1. if that's the case; if it is; in that case
Casual conditional expression formed from だった (past of だ) plus ら. Follows nouns and na-adjective stems. Often used as a discourse connector meaning 'in that case' or 'if so', and can open a new clause in response to something just said.
彼女(かのじょ)だったら知(し)ってるよ。
If it's her, she'd know.
そうだったら、もっと早(はや)く言(い)ってよ。
If that's the case, you should have said so sooner.
暇(ひま)だったら、一緒(いっしょ)に映画(えいが)でも観(み)に行(い)かない？
If you're free, want to go see a movie or something?

Casual conditional form of the copula だ, equivalent to 'if it is ~' or 'if that's the case'. Follows nouns and na-adjective stems directly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xだったら〜 — if X is the case/if it is X
  • そうだったら〜 — if that's so
  • 〜んだったら〜 — if (the situation is that) ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • なら: similar conditional meaning but slightly more formal/neutral; can also follow verb/i-adj directly
  • だとしたら: more formal equivalent, 'if it is the case that'
