配属先(はいぞくさき)

はいぞくさき
noun
place of assignment; posting; department one is assigned to
1. place of assignment; the department or post one is assigned to
The section, branch, or location to which a new employee or transferred worker is posted. Common in corporate and bureaucratic contexts when announcing where someone will work.
配属先(はいぞくさき)が決(き)まった。
My assignment has been decided.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)の配属先(はいぞくさき)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
The postings for the new employees were announced.
希望(きぼう)とは違(ちが)う配属先(はいぞくさき)になり、少(すこ)しがっかりした。
I ended up with a different posting than I had hoped for and felt a little disappointed.

Formed from 配属(はいぞく) (assignment, posting) plus the suffix 先(さき), which marks the destination or party on the other end of an action (as in 取引先(とりひきさき), a business contact).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 配属先(はいぞくさき)が決(き)まる: one's assignment is decided
  • 配属先(はいぞくさき)を希望(きぼう)する: to request a particular posting
  • 配属先(はいぞくさき)の部署(ぶしょ): the department one is assigned to

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勤務先(きんむさき): place of work — the company or office where one works, not the specific internal posting
  • 出向先(しゅっこうさき): place of secondment — where one is temporarily transferred to another organization
