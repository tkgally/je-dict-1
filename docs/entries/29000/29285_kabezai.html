壁材(かべざい)

かべざい
noun
wall material; walling material
1. wall material; walling material
Material used to build or finish walls — for example plaster, plasterboard, tile, or wood paneling. Built from 壁(かべ) (wall) + 材(ざい) (material).
自然(しぜん)な壁材(かべざい)を選(えら)んだ。
We chose a natural wall material.
この壁材(かべざい)は湿気(しっけ)に強(つよ)い。
This wall material is resistant to moisture.
部屋(へや)の雰囲気(ふんいき)は、選(えら)ぶ壁材(かべざい)によって大(おお)きく変(か)わる。
A room's atmosphere changes greatly depending on the wall material you choose.

A construction and interior-design term for any material that makes up or covers a wall.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁材(かべざい)を選(えら)ぶ: to choose a wall material
  • 内装(ないそう)壁材(かべざい): interior wall material

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 床材(ゆかざい): flooring material — the parallel '...}{材(ざい)' term for floors
  • 建材(けんざい): building material — the broad category covering all construction materials
