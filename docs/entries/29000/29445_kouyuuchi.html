公有地

こうゆうち
noun
public land; publicly owned land
1. public land; publicly owned land
Land owned by the national government or a local authority rather than by private individuals or companies. Contrasts with privately owned land.
ここは公有地(こうゆうち)だ。
This is public land.
公有地(こうゆうち)に建物(たてもの)を建(た)てる。
to put up a building on public land.
市(し)は公有地(こうゆうち)の一部(いちぶ)を公園(こうえん)として整備(せいび)した。
The city developed part of the public land into a park.

Used in administrative, legal, and urban-planning contexts for land held by the public sector.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公有地(こうゆうち)の管理(かんり): management of public land
  • 公有地(こうゆうち)を売却(ばいきゃく)する: to sell off public land
  • 公有地(こうゆうち)の有効(ゆうこう)利用(りよう): effective use of public land

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 私有地(しゆうち): privately owned land — the direct antonym
  • 国有地(こくゆうち): state-owned land — owned specifically by the national government
