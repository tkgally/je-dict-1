アカハラ

あかはら
noun
academic harassment
1. academic harassment; abuse of power in academic settings
Harassment or abuse of authority within academic institutions, typically by faculty, supervisors, or senior researchers against students, junior researchers, or staff. Abbreviated from アカデミックハラスメント. Includes unfair grading, withholding academic support as punishment, or demeaning treatment.
アカハラの被害(ひがい)を受(う)ける。
To suffer academic harassment.
アカハラは大学院(だいがくいん)の環境(かんきょう)で特(とく)に問題(もんだい)になりやすい。
Academic harassment tends to become a particular problem in graduate school environments.
大学(だいがく)では最近(さいきん)、アカハラ相談窓口(そうだんまどぐち)を設(もう)ける動(うご)きが進(すす)んでいる。
Universities have recently been moving to set up academic harassment consultation desks.

Abbreviation of アカデミックハラスメント (academic harassment). Part of the broader Japanese ハラスメント vocabulary for specific types of harassment.

RELATED TERMS:

  • パワハラ: power harassment (workplace abuse of authority)
  • セクハラ: sexual harassment
  • モラハラ: moral harassment (psychological/emotional abuse)
  • アルハラ: alcohol harassment (pressuring others to drink)

NOTE: All these abbreviated forms follow the pattern of cutting down a long loanword and adding ハラ from ハラスメント.
