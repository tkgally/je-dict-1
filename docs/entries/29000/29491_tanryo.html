短慮(たんりょ)

たんりょ
noun
rashness; imprudence; hasty thinking
1. rashness; imprudence; hasty thinking
Hasty, ill-considered thinking or action; making a decision without enough deliberation. A formal, somewhat literary word.
短慮(たんりょ)を慎(つつし)む。
to refrain from rash behavior.
短慮(たんりょ)な行動(こうどう)は後悔(こうかい)を招(まね)く。
Rash actions invite regret.
一時(いちじ)の短慮(たんりょ)から、彼(かれ)は大切(たいせつ)な仕事(しごと)を辞(や)めてしまった。
Out of a moment's rashness, he quit his important job.

Combines 短(たん) (short) + 慮(りょ) (thought, consideration); literally 'short thinking.' A formal, somewhat literary word for acting without enough forethought.

USAGE:

  • As a noun: 短慮(たんりょ)を慎(つつし)む (to refrain from rashness)
  • As a na-adjective: 短慮(たんりょ)な判断(はんだん) (a rash judgment)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 軽率(けいそつ): careless; hasty — more common, emphasizes carelessness
  • 浅慮(せんりょ): shallow thinking — close synonym, also formal
