受任者(じゅにんしゃ)

じゅにんしゃ
noun
person entrusted with a task; mandatary
1. person entrusted with a task; mandatary
In law, the party who accepts a commission to handle a matter on behalf of another; the one who is entrusted, as opposed to the one who entrusts.
受任者(じゅにんしゃ)として契約(けいやく)を結(むす)ぶ。
Enter into a contract as the entrusted party.
弁護士(べんごし)が受任者(じゅにんしゃ)となって手続(てつづ)きを進(すす)める。
A lawyer acts as the mandatary and carries out the procedures.
委任(いにん)契約(けいやく)では、受任者(じゅにんしゃ)は依頼人(いらいにん)の利益(りえき)のために誠実(せいじつ)に行動(こうどう)しなければならない。
Under a mandate contract, the entrusted party must act faithfully in the client's interest.

Composed of 受任(じゅにん) (accepting a commission) + the suffix 者(しゃ) (person). A legal term used mainly in connection with mandate (委任(いにん)) contracts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受任者(じゅにんしゃ)の義務(ぎむ): the duties of the entrusted party
  • 受任者(じゅにんしゃ)となる: to become the mandatary

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 委任者(いにんしゃ): the entrusting party — the counterpart who grants the commission
  • 代理人(だいりにん): agent; proxy — one authorized to act on another's behalf, often overlapping in practice
