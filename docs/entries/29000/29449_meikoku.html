1. inscription; engraving; engraved characters
Letters or designs carved into metal, stone, or other hard material, or the act of carving them. Also used figuratively for something engraved deeply in the heart or memory.
石碑(せきひ)に銘刻(めいこく)する。
to inscribe a stone monument.
古(ふる)い鐘(かね)に銘刻(めいこく)された文字(もじ)を読(よ)む。
to read the characters engraved on an old bell.
その言葉(ことば)は今(いま)も心(こころ)に銘刻(めいこく)されている。
Those words are engraved in my heart even now.

A formal word used both literally for carved inscriptions and figuratively for vivid, lasting impressions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 石(いし)に銘刻(めいこく)する: to engrave in stone
  • 心(こころ)に銘刻(めいこく)する: to engrave in one's heart
  • 銘刻(めいこく)された文字(もじ): engraved characters

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刻印(こくいん): stamp; engraved mark — emphasizes a stamped or imprinted mark
  • 銘記(めいき): to bear firmly in mind — figurative only, never a physical carving
