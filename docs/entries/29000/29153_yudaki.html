湯炊き(ゆだき)

ゆだき
noun
boil-and-drain cooking; cooking in hot water
1. boil-and-drain method; cooking by boiling in excess water
A cooking method in which food (especially rice) is cooked in a large volume of boiling water, then drained. For rice, it produces a different texture than the standard absorption method used in rice cookers.
湯炊き(ゆだき)で炊(た)いたご飯(はん)を食(た)べた。
I ate rice cooked by the boil-and-drain method.
湯炊き(ゆだき)は炊飯器(すいはんき)を使(つか)わずにご飯(はん)を炊(た)く伝統的(でんとうてき)な方法(ほうほう)だ。
The boil-and-drain method is a traditional way to cook rice without a rice cooker.
湯炊き(ゆだき)の場合(ばあい)、お米(おこめ)を大量(たいりょう)のお湯(ゆ)で茹(ゆ)でてから水(みず)を切(き)ります。
In the boil-and-drain method, the rice is boiled in a large amount of water, then drained.

Combines 湯(ゆ) (hot water) and 炊き(だき) (cooking, from 炊(た)く). Contrasts with the standard absorption method (吸水炊飯(きゅうすいすいはん)) used by modern rice cookers, where rice absorbs an exact amount of water with no draining.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 湯炊き(ゆだき)でご飯(はん)を炊(た)く: to cook rice by the boil-and-drain method
  • 湯炊き(ゆだき)の米(こめ): rice cooked in boiling water
