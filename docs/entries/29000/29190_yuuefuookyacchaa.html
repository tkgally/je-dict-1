UFOキャッチャー

ゆーえふおーきゃっちゃー
noun
claw machine; UFO catcher; crane game
1. claw machine; UFO catcher; crane game
An arcade game machine in which players use a mechanical claw to try to grab prizes such as stuffed animals or candy. Originally a Sega brand name, now used generically in Japan for any such machine.
UFOキャッチャーでぬいぐるみを取(と)った。
I grabbed a stuffed animal from the claw machine.
ゲームセンターでUFOキャッチャーに挑戦(ちょうせん)した。
I tried my luck at the claw machine at the arcade.
UFOキャッチャーは見(み)た目(め)より難(むずか)しく、何度(なんど)やっても景品(けいひん)が取(と)れなかった。
The claw machine was harder than it looked, and I couldn't grab a prize no matter how many times I tried.

UFO catcher is a registered trademark of Sega, but is used as a common noun for all claw machines in Japan. The name refers to the UFO-shaped housing of early machines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • UFOキャッチャーをやる: to play the claw machine
  • UFOキャッチャーで景品(けいひん)を取(と)る: to win a prize from the claw machine
  • UFOキャッチャーの達人(たつじん): claw machine expert
