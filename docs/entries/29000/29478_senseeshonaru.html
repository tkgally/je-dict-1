センセーショナル

せんせーしょなる
adjective-na
sensational; startling
1. sensational; startling; that causes a stir
From English "sensational." Describes news, a debut, or an event so striking that it draws wide attention and stirs strong public reaction. Used as a na-adjective, often of media coverage and dramatic developments.
センセーショナルな報道(ほうどう)が続(つづ)いた。
Sensational reporting continued.
彼(かれ)のデビューはセンセーショナルだった。
His debut was sensational.
その発表(はっぴょう)はセンセーショナルに取(と)り上(あ)げられ、大(おお)きな議論(ぎろん)を呼(よ)んだ。
The announcement was reported sensationally and sparked a major debate.

Loanword from English "sensational." Conjugates as a na-adjective and is common in media and entertainment contexts. Often carries a hint that the attention is exaggerated or stirred up.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • センセーショナルな見出(みだ)し: a sensational headline
  • センセーショナルな話題(わだい): a sensational topic
  • センセーショナルに報(ほう)じる: to report sensationally

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 衝撃的(しょうげきてき): shocking — emphasizes the impact, without the "stirring up attention" nuance
  • 扇情的(せんじょうてき): inflammatory, lurid — stronger sense of deliberately provoking reaction
