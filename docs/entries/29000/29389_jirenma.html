ジレンマ

じれんま
noun
dilemma
1. dilemma; quandary
A loanword from English 'dilemma.' A situation in which one must choose between two options, both of which have undesirable consequences.
ジレンマに陥(おちい)る。
to fall into a dilemma
仕事(しごと)と家庭(かてい)の間(あいだ)でジレンマを感(かん)じる。
to feel torn in a dilemma between work and family
環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)と経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)のジレンマは解決(かいけつ)が難(むずか)しい。
The dilemma between environmental protection and economic growth is hard to resolve.

A katakana loanword from English 'dilemma.' Refers to being caught between two options that are both unsatisfactory.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ジレンマに陥(おちい)る: to fall into a dilemma
  • ジレンマを抱(かか)える: to face a dilemma

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 板挟(いたばさ)み: being caught in the middle — emphasizes pressure from two opposing parties, rather than a choice between two bad options
