1. to suffer; to sustain; to experience (something negative)
To undergo or receive something unpleasant — typically defeat, a loss, or a setback. Used in formal and written contexts; the most common collocates are 敗北(はいぼく) (defeat) and 惨敗(ざんぱい) (crushing defeat).
敗北(はいぼく)を喫(きっ)した。
They suffered a defeat.
チーム(ちーむ)は惨敗(ざんぱい)を喫(きっ)してシーズン(しーずん)を終(お)えた。
The team ended the season having suffered a crushing defeat.
強豪校(きょうごうこう)に完封負(かんぷうま)けを喫(きっ)し、選手(せんしゅ)たちは言葉(ことば)を失(うしな)った。
Having suffered a shutout loss to a powerhouse school, the players were left speechless.

Primarily used in the fixed phrase ～を喫(きっ)する to mean "to suffer ~". Mostly seen in sports reporting, news, and formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敗北(はいぼく)を喫(きっ)する: to suffer a defeat
  • 惨敗(ざんぱい)を喫(きっ)する: to suffer a crushing defeat
  • 完封負(かんぷうま)けを喫(きっ)する: to suffer a shutout loss
  • 逆転負(ぎゃくてんま)けを喫(きっ)する: to suffer a come-from-behind loss

The kanji 喫(きっ) also appears in 喫茶(きっさ) (coffee shop) and 喫煙(きつえん) (smoking), where it carries the sense of "taking in" or "consuming".
