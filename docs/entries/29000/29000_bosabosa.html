ぼさぼさ

ぼさぼさ
adverb, onomatopoeia
messy; disheveled (hair); standing around idle
1. messy; disheveled; tousled (especially of hair)
Describes hair that is unkempt and sticking out in every direction; can also describe other fibrous things (a worn-out broom, dry grass) that have come apart.
髪(かみ)がぼさぼさだ。
My hair is a mess.
寝起(ねお)きで髪(かみ)がぼさぼさになっている。
My hair is all tousled from sleeping.
庭(にわ)の雑草(ざっそう)がぼさぼさに伸(の)びてしまった。
The weeds in the garden have grown out into a messy tangle.
2. standing around idle; spaced out
Describes someone hanging around blankly without doing what they should be doing. Often used in a scolding tone.
ぼさぼさしてないで早(はや)く手伝(てつだ)って。
Stop standing around and come help, quickly.
ぼさぼさしてたら電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れるよ。
If you keep spacing out, you'll miss the train.
何(なに)をぼさぼさ突(つ)っ立(た)っているんだ、早(はや)く動(うご)け。
What are you standing around blankly for? Get moving.

Sense 1 is the everyday meaning, usually about hair. Sense 2 uses the する form (ぼさぼさする) to scold someone for being idle or slow.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 髪(かみ)がぼさぼさ: messy hair
  • ぼさぼさの髪(かみ): tousled hair
  • ぼさぼさに伸(の)びる: to grow out into a messy tangle
  • ぼさぼさする: to stand around blankly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぐしゃぐしゃ: crumpled or rumpled — broader, also for paper and cloth
  • もじゃもじゃ: bushy, frizzy hair — emphasizes volume and curl
  • ぼうっと: spaced out — sense 2 equivalent without the visual messiness
