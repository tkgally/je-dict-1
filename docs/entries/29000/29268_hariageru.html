1. to raise one's voice; to shout out; to belt out
To produce a loud, forceful voice, straining it so that it carries. Almost always used with 声(こえ) as the object, describing shouting, calling out, or singing loudly.
大(おお)きな声(こえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げた。
He raised his voice loudly.
店員(てんいん)が元気(げんき)よく声(こえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げている。
The shop staff are calling out energetically.
応援団(おうえんだん)は試合(しあい)の間(あいだ)ずっと声(こえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げて歌(うた)っていた。
The cheering squad sang at the top of their voices throughout the match.

Transitive verb, almost always taking 声(こえ) as its object: 声(こえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げる (to raise one's voice). Implies a deliberate, forceful effort to be loud, often to be heard over noise or distance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 声(こえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げる: to raise one's voice; to shout
  • 大声(おおごえ)を張(は)り上(あ)げる: to yell loudly
  • 悲鳴(ひめい)を張(は)り上(あ)げる: to let out a scream

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 叫(さけ)ぶ: to shout — focuses on the cry itself; 張(は)り上(あ)げる stresses the effort of forcing the voice out
  • 怒鳴(どな)る: to yell (angrily) — usually carries anger, which 張(は)り上(あ)げる does not
