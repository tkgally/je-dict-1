内線(ないせん)

ないせん
noun
extension (phone), internal line

Definitions

1. phone extension, internal line
An internal telephone line within an organization, or the extension number used to reach a specific phone.
2. civil war, internal conflict
A war or armed conflict within a country.

Examples

内線(ないせん)番号(ばんごう)は何番(なんばん)ですか。
What is your extension number?
内線(ないせん)で担当者(たんとうしゃ)に繋(つな)ぎます。
I'll connect you to the person in charge via the internal line.
Business context
その国(くに)は長(なが)い内線(ないせん)に苦(くる)しんだ。
That country suffered through a long civil war.
Sense 2

Notes

USAGE: 内線(ないせん) most commonly refers to telephone extensions in offices and organizations. The second meaning (civil war) is used in historical and political contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 内線(ないせん)番号(ばんごう) (extension number)
  • 内線(ないせん)で電話(でんわ)する (to call on the internal line)
  • 内線(ないせん)XXXX番(ばん) (extension number XXXX)

CONTRAST:

  • 内線(ないせん) - internal line (within organization)
  • 外線(がいせん) - external line (outside calls)
