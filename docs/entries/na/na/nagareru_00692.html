流(なが)れる

ながれる
verb (ichidan)
to flow, to stream

Definitions

1. to flow, to stream
For liquid to move continuously.
2. to pass, to elapse
For time to pass.

Examples

川(かわ)が流(なが)れている。
The river is flowing.
時間(じかん)が流(なが)れる。
Time passes.
音楽(おんがく)が流(なが)れている。
Music is playing.

Notes

流(なが)れる means to flow or pass.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 流(なが)れる (intransitive): to flow
  • 流(なが)す (transitive): to let flow

USAGE:

  • Water, rivers flowing
  • Time passing
  • Music playing
