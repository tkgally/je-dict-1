ない

ない
adjective (i-adjective)
nonexistent, not have

Definitions

1. nonexistent, there is no
Indicates the absence or non-existence of something. The negative counterpart of ある.
2. to not have
Indicates that someone doesn't possess something.

Examples

時間(じかん)がない。
I don't have time. / There is no time.
お金(かね)がないので、買(か)えません。
I can't buy it because I don't have money.
問題(もんだい)はないですか。
Are there no problems? / Is there no issue?

Notes

ない conjugates as an i-adjective: なくない (negative - 'is it not nonexistent?'), なくて (te-form), なかった (past). ない is the negative form of ある (to exist/have for inanimate things). For animate things (people/animals), the negative of いる is いない. The kanji 無い is sometimes used but hiragana is more common.
