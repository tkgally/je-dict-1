為(な)す

なす
godan verb
to accomplish, to do, to make

Definitions

1. to accomplish, to achieve
To successfully complete or bring about something.
2. to form, to constitute
To make up or compose something.

Examples

彼(かれ)は偉業(いぎょう)を為(な)した。
He accomplished a great achievement.
為(な)すべきことを為(な)す。
Do what must be done.
この島(しま)は火山(かざん)によって為(な)されたものだ。
This island was formed by a volcano.

Notes

為(な)す is a literary/formal verb meaning 'to accomplish' or 'to do.' In modern speech, する is much more common.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを為(な)す

REGISTER: Literary/Formal
This verb is primarily used in formal writing, set expressions, and proverbs. It's rarely used in everyday conversation.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 為(な)すべきこと (what should be done)
  • 為(な)す術(すべ)がない (to have no way to do anything)
  • 志(こころざし)を為(な)す (to accomplish one's aspiration)
  • 「為(な)せば成(な)る」(Where there's a will, there's a way)

NOTE: Often written in hiragana as なす or in set expressions.
