撫(な)でる

なでる
verb (ichidan)
to stroke, to pat

Definitions

1. to stroke, to pat, to caress
To move one's hand gently over something or someone, often affectionately.

Examples

猫(ねこ)の頭(あたま)を撫(な)でる。
Pat the cat on the head.
子供(こども)の髪(かみ)を優(やさ)しく撫(な)でた。
I gently stroked the child's hair.
犬(いぬ)は撫(な)でられるのが好(す)きだ。
Dogs like being petted.
Passive form

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを撫(な)でる (to stroke X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 撫(な)でている = 'is stroking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 頭(あたま)を撫(な)でる (to pat on the head)
  • 背中(せなか)を撫(な)でる (to stroke one's back)
  • 髪(かみ)を撫(な)でる (to stroke hair)
  • 撫(な)で下(お)ろす (to stroke downward - also: to feel relieved)

IDIOM: 胸(むね)を撫(な)で下(お)ろす - to feel relieved (lit. 'stroke down one's chest').

CULTURAL NOTE: Stroking a child's head is a common sign of affection in Japan. The expression 頭(あたま)を撫(な)でる often appears in positive contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 触(さわ)る - to touch (neutral)
  • 叩(たた)く - to tap, pat (with more force)
