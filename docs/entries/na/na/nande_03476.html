何(なん)で

なんで
adverb
why, what for

Definitions

1. why, for what reason
Used to ask the reason or cause for something.
2. how, by what means
Used to ask about the means or method.

Examples

何(なん)で来(こ)なかったの？
Why didn't you come?
Asking for reason
何(なん)でそんなことを言(い)うの？
Why would you say something like that?
何(なん)で行(い)く？ — 電車(でんしゃ)で行(い)く。
How are you getting there? — I'm going by train.
Asking about means

Notes

USAGE: なんで is a casual way to ask "why" or "how." It's more informal than どうして or なぜ.

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • なんで (casual - most common in spoken Japanese)
  • どうして (neutral)
  • なぜ (formal/written)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • なんでだろう (I wonder why)
  • なんでもない (it's nothing)
  • なんでも (anything, whatever)

NOTE: なんで can also mean "by what means" (何で = with what), though context usually makes the meaning clear.

Related Words

Related: どうして (why (neutral))
Related: なぜ (why (formal))
