中指(なかゆび)

なかゆび
noun
middle finger

Definitions

1. middle finger
The third finger of the hand, located between the index finger and the ring finger.

Examples

中指(なかゆび)に指輪(ゆびわ)をはめる。
Put a ring on the middle finger.
中指(なかゆび)は五本(ごほん)の指(ゆび)の中(なか)で一番(いちばん)長(なが)い。
The middle finger is the longest of the five fingers.
中指(なかゆび)を怪我(けが)した。
I injured my middle finger.

Notes

USAGE: 中指(なかゆび) is the standard term for the middle finger.

FINGER NAMES:

  • 親指(おやゆび) (thumb)
  • 人差(ひとさ)し指(ゆび) (index finger)
  • 中指(なかゆび) (middle finger)
  • 薬指(くすりゆび) (ring finger)
  • 小指(こゆび) (little finger, pinky)

CULTURAL NOTE: Unlike in some Western countries, the middle finger gesture is not traditionally used as an obscene gesture in Japan, though awareness of its Western meaning has increased.
