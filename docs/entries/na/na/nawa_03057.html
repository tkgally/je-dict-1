縄(なわ)

なわ
noun
rope, cord

Definitions

1. rope, cord
A thick string made by twisting fibers together, traditionally made from straw or hemp in Japan.

Examples

荷物(にもつ)を縄(なわ)でしっかり縛(しば)った。
I tied the luggage tightly with rope.
子供(こども)たちが縄(なわ)跳(と)びをしている。
The children are jumping rope.
縄跳び = jump rope
犯人(はんにん)は縄(なわ)で縛(しば)られた。
The criminal was tied up with rope.

Notes

USAGE: 縄(なわ) traditionally refers to rope made from natural fibers like straw (藁(わら)) or hemp (麻(あさ)). It's used in various contexts from practical binding to cultural practices.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 縄(なわ)跳(と)び (jump rope)
  • 縄(なわ)張(ば)り (territory, turf)
  • 縄(なわ)を解(と)く (to untie rope)
  • 縄(なわ)で縛(しば)る (to tie with rope)

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • 注連縄(しめなわ) (shimenawa) - sacred rope used at Shinto shrines
  • 縄文(じょうもん)時代(じだい) (Jomon period) - named after rope-patterned pottery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ロープ - rope (loanword, general)
  • 紐(ひも) - string, cord (thinner)
  • 綱(つな) - thick rope, hawser
