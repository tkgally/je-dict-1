鍋(なべ)

なべ
noun
pot, pan

Definitions

1. pot, pan
A deep cooking vessel with handles, used for boiling, stewing, etc.
2. hot pot dish
A type of Japanese dish where ingredients are cooked together in a pot at the table.

Examples

大(おお)きい鍋(なべ)でスープを作(つく)ります。
I make soup in a large pot.
今日(きょう)の晩(ばん)ごはんは鍋(なべ)です。
Tonight's dinner is hot pot.
Referring to the dish
冬(ふゆ)は鍋料理(なべりょうり)がおいしいです。
Hot pot dishes are delicious in winter.

Notes

鍋 refers both to the cooking vessel and to hot pot dishes (鍋料理). Popular nabe dishes include すき焼き (sukiyaki), しゃぶしゃぶ (shabu-shabu), and 寄せ鍋 (yosenabe). Nabe is a popular winter meal often shared among family or friends.
