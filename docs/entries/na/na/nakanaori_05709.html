仲直(なかなお)り

なかなおり
noun
reconciliation; making up

Definitions

1. reconciliation; making up; patching up
The act of becoming friends again after a quarrel or disagreement.

Examples

友達(ともだち)と仲直(なかなお)りした。
I made up with my friend.
仲直(なかなお)りしよう。
Let's make up.
喧嘩(けんか)の後(あと)、仲直(なかなお)りした。
We made up after the fight.

Notes

仲直(なかなお)り means making up or reconciliation.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 仲直(なかなお)りする: to make up, to reconcile
  • 仲直(なかなお)りの握手(あくしゅ): handshake of reconciliation

USAGE:
Commonly used for personal relationships, especially among friends and family.
