なるべく

なるべく
adverb
as much as possible; if possible

Definitions

1. as much as possible; if possible
Indicates a desire to do something to the greatest extent circumstances allow.

Examples

なるべく早(はや)く来(き)てください。
Please come as early as possible.
なるべく静(しず)かにしてください。
Please be as quiet as possible.
野菜(やさい)をなるべく食(た)べるようにしています。
I try to eat vegetables as much as possible.

Notes

なるべく expresses a soft preference or request, less forceful than できるだけ.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • なるべく: as much as possible (softer, preference-based)
  • できるだけ: as much as possible (stronger, effort-based)
