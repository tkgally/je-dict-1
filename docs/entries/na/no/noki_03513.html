軒(のき)

のき
noun
eaves

Definitions

1. eaves
The part of a roof that projects beyond the wall of a building.

Examples

軒(のき)から雨(あま)だれが落(お)ちている。
Raindrops are falling from the eaves.
軒下(のきした)で雨宿(あまやど)りをした。
I took shelter from the rain under the eaves.
軒(のき)の深(ふか)い日本家屋(にほんかおく)は夏(なつ)涼(すず)しい。
Traditional Japanese houses with deep eaves are cool in summer.

Notes

軒(のき) refers to the overhanging edge of a roof. Deep eaves are a characteristic feature of traditional Japanese architecture.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 軒下(のきした) (under the eaves)
  • 軒先(のきさき) (edge of the eaves, front of a shop)
  • 軒(のき)を連(つら)ねる (to stand in a row, of houses/shops)
  • 雨宿(あまやど)り (taking shelter from rain)

ARCHITECTURAL NOTE:
Deep 軒(のき) in traditional Japanese architecture serve to shade interiors from summer sun while allowing winter sunlight to enter.

NOTE: 軒(けん) is also a counter for houses/buildings.
