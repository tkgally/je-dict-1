のどか

のどか
na-adjective
peaceful, tranquil

Definitions

1. peaceful, tranquil, idyllic
Calm and quiet, free from disturbance; often describing rural or relaxing settings.

Examples

のどかな田舎(いなか)の風景(ふうけい)だ。
It's a peaceful countryside scene.
のどかな春(はる)の午後(ごご)を過(す)ごした。
I spent a tranquil spring afternoon.
のどかな時間(じかん)が流(なが)れている。
Peaceful time is passing.

Notes

USAGE:

のどか is typically used to describe:

  • Rural, countryside settings
  • Slow-paced, relaxed time
  • Calm weather, especially spring weather

COLLOCATIONS:

  • のどかな日(ひ): a peaceful day
  • のどかな景色(けしき): idyllic scenery
