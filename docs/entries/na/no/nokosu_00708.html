残(のこ)す

のこす
verb (godan)
to leave behind

Definitions

1. to leave behind, to leave over
To not take or use something.
2. to save, to reserve
To keep something for later.

Examples

食(た)べ物(もの)を残(のこ)す。
To leave food behind.
記録(きろく)を残(のこ)す。
To leave a record.
名(な)を残(のこ)す。
To leave one's name (in history).

Notes

残(のこ)す means to leave behind.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 残(のこ)す (transitive): to leave behind
  • 残(のこ)る (intransitive): to remain

COMMON USES:

  • 遺産(いさん)を残(のこ)す: to leave an inheritance
  • 足跡(あしあと)を残(のこ)す: to leave footprints
