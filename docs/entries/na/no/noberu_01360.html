述(の)べる

のべる
ichidan verb
to state, to express

Definitions

1. to state, to express
To put thoughts or opinions into words.
2. to mention, to describe
To speak about something in detail.

Examples

意見(いけん)を述べる。
To express an opinion.
理由(りゆう)を述べる。
To state the reason.

Notes

述べる is formal and used in speeches or writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意見(いけん)を述べる: to state an opinion
  • 感想(かんそう)を述べる: to express impressions
  • 結論(けつろん)を述べる: to state a conclusion

REGISTER:

  • More formal than 言(い)う
