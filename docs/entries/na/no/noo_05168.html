ノー

のー
interjection
no

Definitions

1. no
An expression of refusal or denial, used in informal contexts or as part of compound words.

Examples

ノーと言(い)える勇気(ゆうき)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to have the courage to say no.
彼(かれ)の提案(ていあん)にノーを突(つ)きつけた。
I flatly refused his proposal.
ノーコメントでお願(ねが)いします。
No comment, please.

Notes

ノー is the English 'no' used as a loanword in Japanese, typically in specific contexts or compound expressions.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ノーと言(い)う: to say no
  • ノーコメント: no comment
  • ノーベル賞(しょう): Nobel Prize
  • ノーマル: normal
  • ノーネクタイ: no-tie (casual dress)
  • ノースリーブ: sleeveless

USAGE NOTE: For regular refusals in Japanese, いいえ, いや, or 違(ちが)う are more natural. ノー is often used emphatically or in set phrases borrowed from English.

CONTRAST:

  • イエス/ノー: yes/no (binary choice)
  • はい/いいえ: yes/no (standard Japanese)
