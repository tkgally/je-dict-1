乗換(のりかえ)

のりかえ
noun, suru-verb
transfer (transportation)

Definitions

1. transfer, change (of trains, buses, etc.)
The act of switching from one vehicle or transportation line to another during a journey.
2. switching, changing over
Changing from one thing to another (e.g., products, services, plans).

Examples

新宿(しんじゅく)で乗換(のりかえ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
You need to transfer at Shinjuku.
乗換(のりかえ)案内(あんない)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the transfer information.
携帯(けいたい)を別(べつ)の会社(かいしゃ)に乗換(のりかえ)ました。
I switched my mobile phone to a different carrier.
Sense 2

Notes

USAGE: 乗換(のりかえ) (also written as 乗(の)り換(か)え) is essential vocabulary for navigating Japanese public transportation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xで乗換(のりかえ)る (to transfer at X)
  • 乗換(のりかえ)なし (no transfer required, direct)
  • 乗換(のりかえ)時間(じかん) (transfer time)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 乗換(のりかえ)駅(えき) (transfer station)
  • 乗換(のりかえ)案内(あんない) (transfer guide)
  • 乗換(のりかえ)検索(けんさく) (route search)

RELATED VERB:

  • 乗(の)り換(か)える (to transfer) - godan verb
