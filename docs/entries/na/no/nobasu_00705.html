伸(の)ばす

のばす
verb (godan)
to stretch, to extend

Definitions

1. to stretch, to extend
To make something longer.
2. to grow, to develop
To increase or improve.

Examples

手(て)を伸(の)ばす。
To stretch out one's hand.
髪(かみ)を伸(の)ばす。
To grow one's hair.
能力(のうりょく)を伸(の)ばす。
To develop one's abilities.

Notes

伸(の)ばす means to stretch or extend.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 伸(の)ばす (transitive): to stretch
  • 伸(の)びる (intransitive): to stretch

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 伸(の)ばす: to stretch
  • 延(の)ばす: to extend (time)
