日曜日(にちようび)

にちようび
noun
Sunday

Definitions

1. Sunday
The second day of the weekend and traditionally a day of rest. The kanji 日 means 'sun,' similar to how 'Sunday' derives from 'Sun day' in English.

Examples

日曜日(にちようび)は家(いえ)でゆっくりします。
On Sunday, I relax at home.
日曜日(にちようび)に家族(かぞく)と出(で)かけます。
I go out with my family on Sunday.
毎週(まいしゅう)日曜日(にちようび)は休(やす)みです。
Every Sunday is a day off.

Notes

Sunday uses 日 (sun), directly parallel to the English naming convention. Can be abbreviated to 日曜 (nichiyou). In Japan, Sunday is often spent with family or resting before the work week.
