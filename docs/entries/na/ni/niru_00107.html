似(に)る

にる
verb (ichidan)
to resemble, to be similar

Definitions

1. to resemble, to look like
To have similar appearance or characteristics to something or someone else.

Examples

彼(かれ)は父親(ちちおや)に似(に)ている。
He looks like his father.
に marks who/what is resembled
この二(ふた)つはよく似(に)ている。
These two are very similar.
似(に)たような経験(けいけん)がある。
I have a similar experience.
似(に)た (た-form) used as modifier

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: XがYに似(に)る (X resembles Y) / XとYが似(に)る (X and Y are similar).

ASPECT: ている is almost always used. 似(に)ている = 'resembles' (state). The plain form 似(に)る is rarely used alone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xに似(に)ている (resemble X)
  • よく似(に)ている (look very similar)
  • 似(に)たような (similar—attributive)
  • 似合(にあ)う (to suit, look good on—related but different verb)
  • 似顔絵(にがおえ) (portrait, likeness)

GRAMMAR NOTE: Unlike many verbs, 似(に)る is almost exclusively used in the ている form in modern Japanese. Saying X は Y に似(に)る sounds unnatural; X は Y に似(に)ている is correct.

RELATED:

  • 似合(にあ)う: to suit (a different verb meaning 'to look good on')
