憎(にく)む

にくむ
godan verb
to hate; to detest; to loathe

Definitions

1. to hate; to detest
To feel intense dislike or hostility toward someone or something.
2. to loathe; to abhor
To feel extreme disgust or aversion.

Examples

罪(つみ)を憎(にく)んで人(ひと)を憎(にく)まず。
Hate the sin, not the sinner.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)を裏切(うらぎ)った友人(ゆうじん)を憎(にく)んでいる。
He hates the friend who betrayed him.
戦争(せんそう)を憎(にく)む気持(きも)ちは誰(だれ)にでもある。
Everyone has feelings of hatred toward war.

Notes

憎(にく)む is transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). It takes を for the object of hatred.

ASPECT ({ている}):
憎(にく)んでいる indicates an ongoing state of hatred.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)を憎(にく)む (to hate someone)
  • 罪(つみ)を憎(にく)む (to hate sin/crime)
  • 憎(にく)んでも憎(にく)みきれない (to hate but not be able to hate enough)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を憎(にく)む: The object of hatred

RELATED WORDS:

  • 憎(にく)い: I-adjective meaning "hateful" or (sarcastically) "admirably skilled"
  • 憎(にく)しみ: Noun meaning "hatred"
  • 恨(うら)む: To resent, bear a grudge (similar but with nuance of resentment)

Register: Neutral. A strong word expressing deep negative emotion.

Related Words

Related: 憎(にく)い (adjective)
Contrast: 恨(うら)む (to resent)
