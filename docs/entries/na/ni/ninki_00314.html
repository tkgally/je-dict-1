人気(にんき)

にんき
noun
popularity

Definitions

1. popularity, public favor
The state of being liked or admired by many people.

Examples

この歌手(かしゅ)は人気(にんき)がある。
This singer is popular.
人気(にんき)の商品(しょうひん)です。
It's a popular product.

Notes

Common patterns: 人気(にんき)がある (to be popular), 人気(にんき)の〜 (popular ~). Note: Different reading 人気(ひとけ) means "sign of people."
