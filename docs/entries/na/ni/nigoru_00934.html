濁(にご)る

にごる
verb (godan)
to become muddy, to become cloudy

Definitions

1. to become muddy, to become cloudy
For liquid to become unclear.
2. to be voiced (consonant)
For a consonant to become voiced (linguistics).

Examples

川(かわ)の水(みず)が濁(にご)っている。
The river water is muddy.
「か」が濁(にご)ると「が」になる。
When 'ka' is voiced, it becomes 'ga'.

Notes

濁(にご)る is intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 濁(にご)る (intransitive): to become muddy
  • 濁(にご)す (transitive): to muddy, to mumble

RELATED:

  • 濁点(だくてん): voicing mark (゛)
