二月(にがつ)

にがつ
noun
February

Definitions

1. February
The second month of the year and the shortest month. Features Setsubun (節分) and Valentine's Day.

Examples

二月(にがつ)は短(みじか)いです。
February is short.
二月(にがつ)十四日(じゅうよっか)はバレンタインデーです。
February 14th is Valentine's Day.
二月(にがつ)に雪(ゆき)がたくさん降(ふ)ります。
It snows a lot in February.

Notes

February is literally 'month two.' In Japan, Valentine's Day is when women give chocolate to men, with White Day (March 14) for reciprocation. Setsubun (February 3) marks the end of winter.
