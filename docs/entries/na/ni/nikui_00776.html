〜にくい

にくい
auxiliary adjective
hard to ~, difficult to ~

Definitions

1. hard to ~, difficult to ~
Attached to verb stems to indicate difficulty in doing something.

Examples

この漢字(かんじ)は読(よ)みにくい。
This kanji is hard to read.
食(た)べにくい魚(さかな)だ。
It's a fish that's hard to eat.
言(い)いにくいことがある。
There's something hard to say.

Notes

〜にくい attaches to verb stems.

PATTERN:

  • Verb stem + にくい
  • 書(か)き + にくい = 書(か)きにくい (hard to write)
  • 使(つか)い + にくい = 使(つか)いにくい (hard to use)

CONJUGATION (i-adjective):

  • にくい (present)
  • にくくない (negative)
  • にくかった (past)

OPPOSITE:

  • 〜やすい: easy to ~
