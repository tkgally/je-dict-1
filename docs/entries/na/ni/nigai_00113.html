苦(にが)い

にがい
adjective (i-adjective)
bitter

Definitions

1. bitter (taste)
Describes a sharp, unpleasant taste like coffee, certain vegetables, or medicine.
2. bitter, painful (experience)
Figuratively describes an unpleasant or painful experience or memory.

Examples

このコーヒーは苦(にが)い。
This coffee is bitter.
この薬(くすり)は苦(にが)くて飲(の)みにくい。
This medicine is bitter and hard to take.
苦(にが)い経験(けいけん)から学(まな)んだ。
I learned from a bitter experience.
Figurative usage
彼(かれ)は苦(にが)い顔(かお)をした。
He made a bitter/sour face.
Idiomatic expression

Notes

苦(にが)い is an i-adjective meaning 'bitter.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 苦(にが)く
  • Noun form: 苦(にが)さ (bitterness), 苦(にが)み (bitter taste, bitter element)

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 苦(にが)くない
  • Te-form: 苦(にが)くて
  • Past: 苦(にが)かった

Related expression: 苦(にが)い顔(かお)をする (to make a bitter/displeased face).

Basic tastes in Japanese: 甘(あま)い (sweet), 辛(から)い (spicy), 酸(す)っぱい (sour), 苦(にが)い (bitter), 塩辛(しおから)い (salty).
