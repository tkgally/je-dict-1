人参(にんじん)

にんじん
noun
carrot

Definitions

1. carrot
An orange root vegetable commonly used in cooking.

Examples

人参(にんじん)を細(ほそ)く切(き)る。
To cut the carrot into thin strips.
うさぎは人参(にんじん)が好(す)きだ。
Rabbits like carrots.
人参(にんじん)はビタミンAが豊富(ほうふ)だ。
Carrots are rich in vitamin A.

Notes

人参(にんじん) is a common vegetable in Japanese cooking.

COMMON USES:

  • カレー: curry
  • 肉(にく)じゃが: meat and potato stew
  • 煮物(にもの): simmered dishes
  • サラダ: salads

KANJI NOTE: The kanji 人参 literally means 'ginseng' in Chinese, but in Japanese it refers to carrots. Ginseng is called 朝鮮人参(ちょうせんにんじん).
