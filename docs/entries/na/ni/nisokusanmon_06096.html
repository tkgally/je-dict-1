二束三文(にそくさんもん)

にそくさんもん
noun, na-adjective
dirt cheap; a pittance

Definitions

1. dirt cheap; a pittance; next to nothing
An extremely low price, implying something is almost worthless. Used to describe goods sold for very little money.

Examples

骨董品(こっとうひん)を二束三文(にそくさんもん)で売(う)ってしまった。
I sold the antiques for a pittance.
こんな二束三文(にそくさんもん)の値段(ねだん)では売(う)れない。
I can't sell it at such a ridiculously low price.
大切(たいせつ)なものを二束三文(にそくさんもん)で手放(てばな)した。
I let go of something precious for next to nothing.

Notes

Literally "two bundles for three mon" (文(もん) was an old currency).

ORIGIN:
Originally referred to straw sandals (草鞋(わらじ)) that were sold two pairs for three mon - an extremely cheap price.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 二束三文(にそくさんもん)で売(う)る: to sell for a pittance
  • 二束三文(にそくさんもん)の値段(ねだん): a rock-bottom price

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 捨(す)て値(ね): throwaway price
  • 格安(かくやす): dirt cheap
