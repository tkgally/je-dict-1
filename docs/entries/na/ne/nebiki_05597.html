値引(ねび)き

ねびき
noun
discount; price reduction

Definitions

1. discount; price reduction
A reduction in the price of goods or services.

Examples

値引(ねび)きしてもらえますか。
Can you give me a discount?
20%の値引(ねび)き。
A 20% discount.
値引(ねび)き交渉(こうしょう)をする。
To negotiate a discount.

Notes

値引き is commonly used in shopping and business contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 値引きする: to discount
  • 値引き交渉: price negotiation
  • 大幅値引き: significant discount

RELATED:

  • 割引 (わりびき): discount (percentage-based)
  • セール: sale
  • 特価 (とっか): special price
