寝巻(ねまき)

ねまき
noun
sleepwear, pajamas, nightwear

Definitions

1. sleepwear, pajamas, nightwear
Clothes worn for sleeping, including both Western-style pajamas and traditional Japanese nightwear (yukata-style).

Examples

寝巻(ねまき)に着替(きが)えてからベッドに入(はい)った。
I changed into my pajamas before getting into bed.
旅館(りょかん)では浴衣(ゆかた)が寝巻(ねまき)として用意(ようい)されている。
At the ryokan, a yukata is provided as sleepwear.
寝巻(ねまき)のまま外(そと)に出(で)ないでください。
Please don't go outside in your sleepwear.

Notes

寝巻(ねまき) is a native Japanese word for sleepwear. It can refer to both traditional Japanese nightwear (like yukata) and Western-style pajamas.

Alternate forms:

  • 寝間着(ねまき) - alternate kanji spelling (same pronunciation)

Related words:

  • パジャマ - pajamas (Western-style, loanword)
  • 浴衣(ゆかた) - yukata (often used as sleepwear at ryokan)
  • ナイトウェア - nightwear (modern loanword)
