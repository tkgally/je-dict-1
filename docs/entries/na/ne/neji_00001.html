ねじ

ねじ
noun
screw

Definitions

1. screw
A metal fastener with a spiral thread.
2. winding mechanism
The mechanism for winding a clock or watch.

Examples

ねじを締(し)める。
I tighten the screw.
ねじが緩(ゆる)んでいる。
The screw is loose.
時計(とけい)のねじを巻(ま)く。
I wind the clock.

Notes

ねじ refers to screws and winding mechanisms. Can also be written as 螺子(ねじ) or ネジ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ねじを締(し)める: to tighten a screw
  • ねじを緩(ゆる)める: to loosen a screw
  • ねじを巻(ま)く: to wind (a clock)
  • ねじ回(まわ)し: screwdriver

IDIOMS:

  • ねじが外(はず)れている: to have a screw loose (be crazy)
  • ねじを巻(ま)く: to put pressure on, to discipline

RELATED:

  • ボルト: bolt
  • ナット: nut (for bolts)
