願(ねが)い

ねがい
noun
wish, desire, request

Definitions

1. wish, desire, hope
Something one hopes for or wants to happen.
2. request, petition
A formal or polite request for something.

Examples

願(ねが)いが叶(かな)いますように。
May your wish come true.
願いが叶う = wish comes true
お願(ねが)いがあります。
I have a request (favor to ask).
お願い = request, favor
退職(たいしょく)願(ねが)いを出(だ)した。
I submitted my resignation request.
〜願い = written request for ~

Notes

USAGE: 願(ねが)い is the noun form of 願(ねが)う (to wish). It's used for hopes, wishes, and formal requests.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 願(ねが)いが叶(かな)う (a wish comes true)
  • 願(ねが)いを込(こ)める (to put one's wish into)
  • お願(ねが)いします (please - polite request)
  • お願(ねが)いがある (to have a request/favor)

FORMAL REQUEST FORMS:

  • 休暇(きゅうか)願(ねが)い (leave request)
  • 退職(たいしょく)願(ねが)い (resignation request)
  • 入学(にゅうがく)願(ねが)書(しょ) (application for admission)

NOTE: お願(ねが)いします is one of the most important polite expressions in Japanese, used for requests and saying 'please'.

Related Words

Related: 願(ねが)う (verb: to wish)
