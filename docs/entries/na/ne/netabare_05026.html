ネタバレ

ねたばれ
noun
spoiler

Definitions

1. spoiler (plot reveal)
Revealing key plot details of a story, movie, or game before others have experienced it. From ネタ (material/story element) + バレる (to be exposed).

Examples

ネタバレ注意(ちゅうい)：以下(いか)は結末(けつまつ)を含(ふく)みます。
Spoiler warning: The following contains the ending.
ネタバレしないでください！
Please don't spoil it!
うっかりネタバレを見(み)てしまった。
I accidentally saw a spoiler.

Notes

Combination of ネタ (source material, story element) and バレる (to be exposed/revealed). Very common in online discussions about movies, anime, games, and books. Often written as 【ネタバレ】 or ネタバレ注意(ちゅうい) as a warning.
