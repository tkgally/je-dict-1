鼠(ねずみ)

ねずみ
noun
mouse, rat

Definitions

1. mouse, rat
A small rodent.

Examples

鼠(ねずみ)が出(で)た。
A mouse appeared.
猫(ねこ)が鼠(ねずみ)を追(お)いかけている。
The cat is chasing the mouse.

Notes

鼠(ねずみ) refers to mice and rats.

ZODIAC:
子(ね) is the first animal of the Chinese zodiac.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 窮鼠(きゅうそ)猫(ねこ)を噛(か)む: a cornered rat will bite a cat
  • 鼠(ねずみ)算: exponential growth

COLOR:
鼠色(ねずみいろ): gray
