年金(ねんきん)

ねんきん
noun
pension

Definitions

1. pension, annuity
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

65歳(ろくじゅうごさい)から年金(ねんきん)を受(う)け取(と)れる。
You can receive a pension from age 65.
年金(ねんきん)だけでは生活(せいかつ)が難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to live on pension alone.
毎月(まいつき)年金(ねんきん)保険料(ほけんりょう)を払(はら)っている。
I pay pension insurance premiums every month.

Notes

年金(ねんきん) is the pension system, crucial for retirement in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年金(ねんきん)を払(はら)う (pay pension premiums)
  • 年金(ねんきん)を受(う)け取(と)る (receive pension)
  • 年金(ねんきん)をもらう (receive pension - casual)
  • 年金(ねんきん)受給(じゅきゅう) (pension benefit receipt)
  • 年金(ねんきん)制度(せいど) (pension system)

TYPES OF PENSIONS:

  • 国民(こくみん)年金(ねんきん) (National Pension - basic, for all residents)
  • 厚生(こうせい)年金(ねんきん) (Employees' Pension - for company employees)
  • 企業(きぎょう)年金(ねんきん) (corporate pension)

SOCIAL ISSUE:
Japan's aging population has created concerns about the 年金(ねんきん) system's sustainability, often discussed as 年金(ねんきん)問題(もんだい) (pension problem).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 年金(ねんきん)保険料(ほけんりょう) (pension premiums)
  • 年金(ねんきん)受給者(じゅきゅうしゃ) (pension recipient)
