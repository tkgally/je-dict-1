濡(ぬ)らす

ぬらす
godan verb
to wet, to moisten

Definitions

1. to wet, to moisten
To make something damp with water or liquid.

Examples

タオルを濡らす。
To wet a towel.
手(て)を濡らさずに済(す)む。
To manage without getting one's hands wet.
Idiomatic: to avoid effort

Notes

濡らす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 濡らす (transitive): to wet
  • 濡(ぬ)れる (intransitive): to get wet

IDIOM:

  • 手(て)を濡らさずに: without lifting a finger
