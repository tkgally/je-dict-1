沼(ぬま)

ぬま
noun
swamp, marsh, bog

Definitions

1. swamp, marsh
An area of low-lying, waterlogged land.
2. bog, quagmire
Wet muddy ground that is difficult to cross.

Examples

沼(ぬま)に足(あし)を取(と)られた。
My foot got stuck in the swamp.
この沼(ぬま)には魚(さかな)がたくさんいる。
There are many fish in this marsh.

Notes

沼(ぬま) refers to wetlands.

SLANG:
In modern Japanese, 沼(ぬま) is slang for becoming deeply obsessed with something (like a hobby or fandom), similar to "falling down a rabbit hole."

COMPOUNDS:

  • 沼地(ぬまち): marshland
  • 泥沼(どろぬま): quagmire, muddy swamp
