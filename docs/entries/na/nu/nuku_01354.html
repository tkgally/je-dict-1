抜(ぬ)く

ぬく
godan verb
to extract, to pull out

Definitions

1. to extract, to pull out
To remove something from where it is fixed.
2. to omit, to leave out
To skip or exclude something.
3. to surpass, to outdo
To go past or exceed.

Examples

歯(は)を抜く。
To pull out a tooth.
朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)を抜く。
To skip breakfast.
先頭(せんとう)を抜く。
To overtake the leader.

Notes

抜く is a transitive verb with many uses.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 抜く (transitive): to pull out
  • 抜(ぬ)ける (intransitive): to come out

COMMON USES:

  • コルクを抜く: to uncork
  • 手(て)を抜く: to cut corners
