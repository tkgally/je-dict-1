ぬるぬる

ぬるぬる
adverb
slimy; slippery

Definitions

1. slimy; slippery
Describes a surface that is wet and slippery, often with an unpleasant slimy texture.

Examples

魚(さかな)がぬるぬるして持(も)ちにくい。
The fish is slimy and hard to hold.
お風呂(ふろ)の床(ゆか)がぬるぬるしている。
The bathroom floor is slimy.
ぬるぬるの海藻(かいそう)を触(さわ)った。
I touched the slimy seaweed.

Notes

ぬるぬる is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) describing a slimy, slippery texture.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぬるぬるする: to be slimy
  • ぬるぬるの〜: slimy (noun modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ねばねば: sticky, gooey (sticks to things)
  • つるつる: smooth and slippery (but not slimy)
  • べたべた: sticky (from sugar or adhesive)
