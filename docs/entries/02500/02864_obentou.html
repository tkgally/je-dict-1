お弁当(べんとう)

おべんとう
noun
boxed lunch, bento
1. boxed lunch, bento
A meal packed in a box, typically eaten for lunch.
お弁当(べんとう)を作(つく)る。
To make a boxed lunch.
お弁当(べんとう)を持(も)ってきた。
I brought a boxed lunch.
駅(えき)でお弁当(べんとう)を買(か)った。
I bought a boxed lunch at the station.
母(はは)が毎朝(まいあさ)お弁当(べんとう)を作(つく)ってくれる。
My mother makes a boxed lunch for me every morning.
遠足(えんそく)のときは、いつもお弁当(べんとう)を持(も)っていく。
I always bring a boxed lunch on field trips.

お弁当(べんとう) is a Japanese boxed lunch, a key part of Japanese food culture.

FORM:
The お prefix is optional and polite. 弁当(べんとう) is the plain form.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お弁当(べんとう)を作(つく)る: to make a bento
  • お弁当(べんとう)を持(も)っていく: to bring a bento
  • お弁当(べんとう)を買(か)う: to buy a bento

TYPES:

  • 駅弁(えきべん): train station bento (regional specialties)
  • 幕(まく)の内(うち)弁当(べんとう): traditional multi-item bento
  • キャラ弁(べん): character-themed bento (cute designs)
  • 日替(ひが)わり弁当(べんとう): daily special bento

CULTURE:
Making お弁当(べんとう) for family members, especially children, is a common practice in Japan.
