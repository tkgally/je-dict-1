1. cleaning, sweeping
The act of making a place clean and tidy.
部屋(へや)を掃除(そうじ)してください。
Please clean the room.
毎週(まいしゅう)土曜日(どようび)に掃除(そうじ)をします。
I clean every Saturday.
大掃除(おおそうじ)は年末(ねんまつ)にします。
We do a big cleaning at the end of the year.
掃除機(そうじき)をかけてから床(ゆか)を拭(ふ)いた。
I vacuumed and then wiped the floor.
学校(がっこう)では毎日(まいにち)昼休(ひるやす)みのあとに掃除(そうじ)の時間(じかん)がある。
At school, there's cleaning time after lunch break every day.

掃除(そうじ) refers to cleaning a space by sweeping, wiping, vacuuming, or tidying up. It covers general cleaning rather than specialized tasks like laundry or dishwashing.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を掃除(そうじ)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 掃除(そうじ)する / 掃除(そうじ)をする: to clean
  • 掃除機(そうじき)をかける: to vacuum
  • 掃除(そうじ)が行(い)き届(とど)いている: well-maintained, kept clean

TYPES OF CLEANING:

  • 大掃除(おおそうじ): major year-end cleaning (a Japanese tradition before the New Year)
  • 部屋(へや)の掃除(そうじ): room cleaning
  • トイレ掃除(そうじ): toilet cleaning
  • 掃除(そうじ)当番(とうばん): cleaning duty (common in schools and workplaces)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese schools, students clean their own classrooms and common areas daily (掃除(そうじ)の時間(じかん)). 大掃除(おおそうじ) in late December is a widespread tradition of thoroughly cleaning the home before the New Year.

Related Words

Synonym: 清掃(せいそう) (cleaning (formal/professional))
