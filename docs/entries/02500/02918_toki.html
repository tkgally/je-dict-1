時(とき)

とき
noun
time, when, occasion
1. time, moment
A point or period in time.
2. when, at the time of
Used to indicate when something happens.
子供(こども)の時(とき)、よく遊(あそ)んだ。
When I was a child, I played a lot.
困(こま)った時(とき)は言(い)ってください。
Please tell me when you're in trouble.
食(た)べる時(とき)は静(しず)かにして。
Be quiet when eating.
3. occasion, case
A particular situation or circumstance.

時(とき) is a fundamental time expression.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • verb plain form + 時(とき) (when ~)
  • noun + の時(とき) (at the time of ~)
  • adjective + 時(とき) (when ~)

COMPOUNDS:

  • その時(とき) (at that time)
  • 今(いま)の時(とき) (now, nowadays)
  • あの時(とき) (at that time)
