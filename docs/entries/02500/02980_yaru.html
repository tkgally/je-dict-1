1. to do, to perform
To carry out an action (casual form of する).
宿題(しゅくだい)をやる。
To do homework.
今日(きょう)は何(なに)をやるの？
What are you doing today?
やってみる。
I'll give it a try.
仕事(しごと)をやり終(お)えた。
I finished doing the work.
一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)やった。
I did my best.
2. to give (to inferiors/animals)
To give something to animals, plants, or those of lower status.
犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる。
To give food to the dog.
花(はな)に水(みず)をやる。
To water the flowers.
猫(ねこ)にご飯(はん)をやった。
I fed the cat.
庭(にわ)の木(き)に水(みず)をやる。
I water the trees in the garden.
弟(おとうと)に古(ふる)い本(ほん)をやった。
I gave my old books to my younger brother.

やる is one of the most common verbs in casual Japanese, with two distinct meanings.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

SENSE 1 — TO DO (casual する):

  • 仕事(しごと)をやる: to do work
  • 宿題(しゅくだい)をやる: to do homework
  • やってみる: to give it a try
  • やり直(なお)す: to redo
  • やり遂(と)げる: to accomplish, to see through

SENSE 2 — TO GIVE (downward):

Used for giving to animals, plants, or those of lower status:

  • 犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる: to feed the dog
  • 花(はな)に水(みず)をやる: to water the flowers
  • 弟(おとうと)に小遣(こづか)いをやる: to give one's younger brother pocket money

With people, sense 2 sounds blunt and can be rude if the listener is not clearly a social inferior or a close family junior. Many speakers now prefer あげる even for children and pets.

GIVING HIERARCHY:

  • やる: give (to animals/plants/inferiors — casual)
  • あげる: give (to equals — standard)
  • 差(さ)し上(あ)げる: give (to superiors — humble/polite)

REGISTER:
やる (sense 1) is informal. In formal contexts, use する instead.

Related Words

Synonym: する (to do (neutral))
Related: あげる (to give (polite))
