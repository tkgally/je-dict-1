やる

やる
verb
to do; to give

Definitions

1. to do, to perform
To carry out an action (casual form of する).
2. to give (to inferiors/animals)
To give something to animals, plants, or those of lower status.

Examples

宿題(しゅくだい)をやる。
To do homework.
犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる。
To give food to the dog.
花(はな)に水(みず)をやる。
To water the flowers.

Notes

やる has two main uses.

1. TO DO (casual):

  • やる = する (more casual)
  • 仕事(しごと)をやる (to do work)

2. TO GIVE (downward):

  • Used for animals, plants, younger siblings
  • 犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる (feed the dog)
  • 花(はな)に水(みず)をやる (water flowers)

NOTE: For giving to equals/superiors, use あげる.
