やる

やる
verb
to do; to give
1. to do, to perform
To carry out an action (casual form of する).
宿題(しゅくだい)をやる。
To do homework.
今日(きょう)は何(なに)をやるの？
What are you doing today?
Sense 1: to do
やってみる。
I'll give it a try.
Sense 1: to do
仕事(しごと)をやり終(お)えた。
I finished doing the work.
Sense 1: to do
一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)やった。
I did my best.
Sense 1: to do
2. to give (to inferiors/animals)
To give something to animals, plants, or those of lower status.
犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる。
To give food to the dog.
花(はな)に水(みず)をやる。
To water the flowers.
猫(ねこ)にご飯(はん)をやった。
I fed the cat.
Sense 2: to give (to animal)
庭(にわ)の木(き)に水(みず)をやる。
I water the trees in the garden.
Sense 2: to give (to plants)
金魚(きんぎょ)に餌(えさ)をやる。
I feed the goldfish.
Sense 2: to give (to animal)

やる has two main uses.

1. TO DO (casual):

  • やる = する (more casual)
  • 仕事(しごと)をやる (to do work)

2. TO GIVE (downward):

  • Used for animals, plants, younger siblings
  • 犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)をやる (feed the dog)
  • 花(はな)に水(みず)をやる (water flowers)

NOTE: For giving to equals/superiors, use あげる.
