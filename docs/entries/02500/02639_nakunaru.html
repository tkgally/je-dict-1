1. to pass away, to die
A polite, euphemistic way to say someone has died. More respectful than 死ぬ.
父(ちち)が亡(な)くなった。
My father passed away.
祖父(そふ)は去年(きょねん)亡(な)くなった。
My grandfather passed away last year.
事故(じこ)で多(おお)くの人(ひと)が亡(な)くなった。
Many people died in the accident.
お母様(かあさま)が亡(な)くなられたと聞(き)きました。お悔(く)やみ申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
I heard your mother passed away. My condolences.
Respectful form with honorific なられる
彼(かれ)は若(わか)くして亡(な)くなったが、その作品(さくひん)は今(いま)でも多(おお)くの人(ひと)に愛(あい)されている。
Although he passed away young, his works are still loved by many people today.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xが亡(な)くなる (X passes away).

亡(な)くなる is the polite/euphemistic word for dying.

USAGE:

  • More polite than 死(し)ぬ
  • Used when referring to someone's death respectfully
  • Common in news reports and formal situations
  • Often used when expressing condolences

HONORIFIC FORMS:

  • 亡(な)くなられる (honorific)
  • お亡(な)くなりになる (more honorific)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 無(な)くなる (to disappear, to run out) - different kanji

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 亡(な)くなる: to pass away (polite)
  • 死(し)ぬ: to die (direct, can be harsh)
  • 逝(い)く: to pass (literary)

Related Words

Contrast: 死(し)ぬ (to die (direct))
Contrast: 無(な)くなる (to disappear)
Synonym: 死去(しきょ) (formal/news)
