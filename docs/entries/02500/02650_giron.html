議論(ぎろん)

ぎろん
noun / suru-verb
discussion, argument, debate
1. discussion, debate, argument
An exchange of views on a topic, often involving different opinions.
この問題(もんだい)について議論(ぎろん)する。
To discuss this issue.
議論(ぎろん)が白熱(はくねつ)した。
The debate became heated.
議論(ぎろん)の余地(よち)がない。
There's no room for argument.

議論(ぎろん) refers to formal or serious discussions and debates.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)する (to discuss, to debate)
  • 議論(ぎろん)を呼(よ)ぶ (to spark debate)
  • 議論(ぎろん)が分(わ)かれる (opinions are divided)
  • 議論(ぎろん)の余地(よち)がない (no room for argument)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)好(ず)き (argumentative person)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん): discussion, debate (formal)
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)い: discussion (general)
  • 討論(とうろん): debate (formal, structured)
  • 口論(こうろん): quarrel, verbal fight
