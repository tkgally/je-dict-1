ポスト

ぽすと
noun
mailbox, post
1. mailbox, postbox
A public box for depositing outgoing mail.
手紙(てがみ)をポストに入(い)れる。
To put a letter in the mailbox.
赤(あか)いポストがあります。
There's a red mailbox.
2. post, position
A job or position (especially in business).
新(あたら)しいポストに就(つ)く。
To take up a new position.

ポスト is from English "post."

MEANINGS:
1. Mailbox (red boxes in Japan)
2. Position/post (business context)

RELATED (mailbox):

  • 郵便(ゆうびん)ポスト (mailbox)
  • 郵便局(ゆうびんきょく) (post office)

NOTE: Japanese mailboxes are typically red.
