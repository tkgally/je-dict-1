籠(かご)

かご
noun
basket, cage
1. basket
A container made of woven material such as bamboo or wicker.
籠(かご)に果物(くだもの)を入(い)れる。
To put fruit in a basket.
買(か)い物(もの)籠(かご)を持(も)っていく。
To take a shopping basket.
2. cage
An enclosure for keeping birds or small animals.
鳥(とり)籠(かご)から小鳥(ことり)が逃(に)げた。
The small bird escaped from the cage.
鳥籠 = birdcage

籠(かご) refers to both baskets and cages, depending on context.

TYPES:

  • 鳥(とり)籠(かご) (birdcage)
  • 買(か)い物(もの)籠(かご) (shopping basket)
  • 竹(たけ)籠(かご) (bamboo basket)
  • 洗濯(せんたく)籠(かご) (laundry basket)

KANJI NOTES:

  • Often written in hiragana (かご)
  • 篭(かご) is an alternative kanji

COUNTER:

  • Baskets: 個(こ) or 籠(かご)
  • Cages: 個(こ)

RELATED:

  • ザル (colander, strainer basket)
  • バスケット (basket - loanword)
