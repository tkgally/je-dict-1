気(き)の毒(どく)

きのどく
na-adjective
pitiful, unfortunate, regrettable
1. pitiful, unfortunate
Deserving of sympathy; in an unfortunate situation.
気(き)の毒(どく)な話(はなし)だ。
It's a pitiful story.
お気(き)の毒(どく)に。
I'm sorry to hear that. / My condolences.
Expression of sympathy
2. sorry, regrettable
Expressing regret for inconveniencing someone.
彼(かれ)を待(ま)たせて気(き)の毒(どく)だった。
I felt bad for making him wait.

気(き)の毒(どく) expresses sympathy for someone's misfortune or regret for causing inconvenience.

FORMS:

  • 気(き)の毒(どく)な + noun (pitiful ~)
  • 〜が気(き)の毒(どく)だ (feel sorry for ~)
  • お気(き)の毒(どく)に (expression: I'm sorry, my condolences)

USAGE:

  • Sense 1: Expressing sympathy for someone's situation
  • Sense 2: Feeling bad for troubling someone
  • More formal/polite than かわいそう

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お気(き)の毒(どく)様(さま)です/でした (formal condolences)
  • 気(き)の毒(どく)に思(おも)う (to feel sorry for)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • かわいそう: pitiful (more casual)
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ない: sorry for the inconvenience

Related Words

Synonym: かわいそう (pitiful (casual))
