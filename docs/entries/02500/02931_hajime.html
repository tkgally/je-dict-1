初(はじ)め

はじめ
noun
beginning, start
1. beginning, start
The first part or initial stage of something.
初(はじ)めから説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain from the beginning.
年(とし)の初(はじ)め
The beginning of the year.
初(はじ)めは難(むずか)しかった。
It was difficult at first.

初(はじ)め means the beginning.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 初(はじ)めに (at first, to begin with)
  • 初(はじ)めから (from the beginning)
  • 初(はじ)めは (at first, initially)

OPPOSITE:

  • 終(お)わり (end)

RELATED:

  • 始(はじ)める (to begin - verb)
  • 始(はじ)まる (to begin - intransitive)
