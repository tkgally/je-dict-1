1. construction, building
The process of constructing buildings or infrastructure.
建設(けんせつ)中(ちゅう)のビル
A building under construction.
建設(けんせつ)現場(げんば)で働(はたら)く。
To work at a construction site.
この地域(ちいき)に新(あたら)しい病院(びょういん)を建設(けんせつ)する計画(けいかく)がある。
There is a plan to construct a new hospital in this area.

建設(けんせつ) refers to large-scale construction of buildings, infrastructure, and public works. It emphasizes the overall project of building something substantial.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 建設(けんせつ)中(ちゅう): under construction
  • 建設(けんせつ)会社(がいしゃ): construction company
  • 建設(けんせつ)現場(げんば): construction site
  • 建設(けんせつ)業(ぎょう): construction industry
  • 建設(けんせつ)費(ひ): construction costs

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建設(けんせつ): large-scale construction (roads, dams, buildings)
  • 建築(けんちく): architecture, building design and construction (focuses on individual buildings)
  • 建造(けんぞう): construction of large structures (ships, bridges)
  • 工事(こうじ): construction work (the physical labor/process)

USAGE:
Can be used figuratively: 国家(こっか)建設(けんせつ) (nation-building), 建設的(けんせつてき)な意見(いけん) (constructive opinion).
