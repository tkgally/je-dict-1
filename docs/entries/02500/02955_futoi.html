太(ふと)い

ふとい
i-adjective
thick, fat

Definitions

1. thick, fat
Having a large diameter or girth.
2. bold (of text or lines)
Having thick strokes.

Examples

この木(き)は太(ふと)い。
This tree is thick.
太(ふと)いペンで書(か)いてください。
Please write with a thick pen.
太(ふと)い線(せん)を引(ひ)く。
To draw a thick line.

Notes

太(ふと)い describes thickness or girth.

OPPOSITE: 細(ほそ)い (thin, slender)

COMMON USES:

  • 足(あし)が太(ふと)い (thick legs)
  • 太(ふと)い声(こえ) (deep/loud voice)
  • 太(ふと)字(じ) (bold text)

NOTE: For people's weight, 太(ふと)っている is more common.
