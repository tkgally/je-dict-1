奥(おく)さん

おくさん
noun
wife (someone else's)
1. wife (of someone else)
A polite term to refer to another person's wife.
奥(おく)さんは元気(げんき)ですか。
How is your wife?
田中(たなか)さんの奥(おく)さん
Mr. Tanaka's wife.
奥(おく)さんによろしくお伝(つた)えください。
Please give my regards to your wife.
隣(となり)の奥(おく)さんとよく話(はな)す。
I often talk with the neighbor's wife.
山田(やまだ)さんの奥(おく)さんは料理(りょうり)がとても上手(じょうず)で、いつもおいしいものを作(つく)ってくれる。
Mr. Yamada's wife is a very good cook and always makes delicious food for us.

奥(おく)さん is a polite term used to refer to someone else's wife. Never use it for your own wife when speaking to others.

WIFE TERMS IN JAPANESE:

  • 奥(おく)さん / 奥様(おくさま): someone else's wife (polite / very polite)
  • 妻(つま): my wife (neutral, most standard)
  • 家内(かない): my wife (humble, traditional — some consider it old-fashioned)
  • かみさん: wife (casual, familiar)
  • 嫁(よめ): wife (casual); also means bride or daughter-in-law

ETYMOLOGY:
From 奥(おく) (inner part), originally referring to the person who stays in the inner rooms of the house.

USAGE:
Also used as a form of address for married women in general, similar to 'ma'am': 奥(おく)さん、これ落(お)としましたよ (Ma'am, you dropped this).

Related Words

Synonym: 妻(つま) (wife (neutral/formal))
