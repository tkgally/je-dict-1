押(お)す

おす
godan verb
to push, to press
1. to push, to press
To apply force to move something away or to press a button.
2. to stamp, to seal
To apply a stamp or seal to a document.
ボタンを押(お)す。
To press a button.
扉(とびら)を押(お)す。
To push the door.
ハンコを押(お)してください。
Please stamp your seal.

押(お)す means to push or press.

COMMON USES:

  • ボタンを押(お)す (press a button)
  • 扉(とびら)を押(お)す (push a door)
  • ハンコを押(お)す (stamp a seal)
  • 印鑑(いんかん)を押(お)す (put one's seal)

ANTONYM:

  • 引(ひ)く (to pull)

SIGNS:

  • 押(お)す (Push)
  • 引(ひ)く (Pull)
