平仮名(ひらがな)

ひらがな
noun

1. hiragana
The cursive Japanese syllabary, used for native Japanese words and grammatical elements.
平仮名(ひらがな)で書(か)いてください。
Please write it in hiragana.
子供(こども)はまず平仮名(ひらがな)を習(なら)います。
Children first learn hiragana.
平仮名(ひらがな)は46文字(もじ)あります。
There are 46 hiragana characters.
この漢字(かんじ)を平仮名(ひらがな)で読(よ)めますか。
Can you read this kanji in hiragana?
日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)する時(とき)は、まず平仮名(ひらがな)から始(はじ)めるのが普通(ふつう)です。
When studying Japanese, it's normal to start with hiragana.

平仮名(ひらがな) is one of the three main Japanese writing systems. It is the first script most learners study and the foundation of Japanese literacy.

ETYMOLOGY:
平(ひら) (ordinary, simple) + 仮名(かな) (phonetic script). Developed from simplified cursive forms of 漢字(かんじ).

JAPANESE WRITING SYSTEMS:

  • 平仮名(ひらがな): cursive syllabary (46 basic characters)
  • 片仮名(かたかな): angular syllabary (for loanwords, emphasis)
  • 漢字(かんじ): Chinese characters (meaning-based)

USES OF HIRAGANA:

  • Grammatical particles: は、が、を、に
  • Verb and adjective endings (送(おく)り仮名(がな))
  • Words without standard kanji
  • Furigana (reading aids above kanji)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 平仮名(ひらがな)で書(か)く: to write in hiragana
  • 平仮名(ひらがな)表(ひょう): hiragana chart
