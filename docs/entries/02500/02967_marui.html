1. round, circular, spherical
Having a curved shape like a circle or ball.
地球(ちきゅう)は丸(まる)い。
The Earth is round.
丸(まる)いテーブルがあります。
There is a round table.

He/She has a round face.

子供(こども)たちが丸(まる)い形(かたち)のクッキーを作(つく)っています。
The children are making round cookies.

丸(まる)い is an i-adjective meaning round, circular, or spherical.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 丸(まる)い: the default spelling, and the one to use when unsure. Covers both balls and circles.
  • 円(まる)い: reserved for flat circles — a disc, a circle drawn on paper, a full moon. Uncommon in modern writing, where 丸(まる)い has largely absorbed it.

FORMS:

  • 丸(まる)い: round (basic form)
  • 丸(まる)く: roundly (adverb)
  • 丸(まる)さ: roundness (noun)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 丸(まる)い顔(かお): round face
  • 丸(まる)いテーブル: round table
  • 丸(まる)い目(め): round eyes
  • 丸(まる)くなる: to become round

FIGURATIVE USES:

The figurative sense runs through the idea that a round thing has no corners to catch on, so roundness stands for harmony.

  • 丸(まる)く収(おさ)まる: to be settled peacefully, with nobody left aggrieved
  • 角(かど)が取(と)れて丸(まる)くなる: to mellow with age (of a person)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 丸(まる): the noun — a circle, or the ○ mark used for 'correct'
  • 四角(しかく)い: square
  • 三角(さんかく)の: triangular

Related Words
