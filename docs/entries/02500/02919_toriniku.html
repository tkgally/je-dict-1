鶏肉(とりにく)

とりにく
noun
chicken (meat)
1. chicken (meat)
The meat of a chicken used as food.
鶏肉(とりにく)を焼(や)く。
To grill chicken.
今日(きょう)は鶏肉(とりにく)を食(た)べたい。
I want to eat chicken today.
鶏肉(とりにく)のカレー
Chicken curry.

鶏肉(とりにく) specifically refers to chicken as a food ingredient. The kanji 鶏(にわとり) means the live bird, while 鶏肉(とりにく) is the meat used in cooking.

ALTERNATE TERMS:

  • チキン: used for Western-style dishes (チキンカツ, チキンナゲット)
  • 鳥肉(とりにく): alternate kanji using 鳥(とり) (bird) instead of 鶏(にわとり)
  • かしわ: regional term used mainly in Kansai

OTHER MEAT WORDS:

  • 牛肉(ぎゅうにく) (beef)
  • 豚肉(ぶたにく) (pork)
  • 羊肉(ようにく) (lamb/mutton)

COMMON CUTS AND DISHES:

  • 鶏(とり)むね肉(にく): chicken breast
  • 鶏(とり)もも肉(にく): chicken thigh
  • 焼(や)き鳥(とり): grilled chicken skewers
  • 唐揚(からあ)げ: deep-fried chicken
  • 親子丼(おやこどん): chicken and egg rice bowl
