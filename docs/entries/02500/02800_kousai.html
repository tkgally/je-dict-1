1. association, socializing, company
Having social relationships with others.
交際(こうさい)が広(ひろ)い。
To have a wide circle of friends.
交際(こうさい)費(ひ)
Entertainment expenses; socializing costs.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)たちとの交際(こうさい)を大切(たいせつ)にしている。
I value my social relationships with the neighbors.
2. dating, relationship
A romantic relationship between two people.
二人(ふたり)は交際(こうさい)している。
The two are dating.
彼(かれ)らは3年間(ねんかん)交際(こうさい)した後(あと)、先月(せんげつ)結婚(けっこん)した。
After dating for three years, they got married last month.
友人(ゆうじん)から交際(こうさい)を申(もう)し込(こ)まれて驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised when a friend asked me out.

交際(こうさい) covers both general social relationships and romantic dating, depending on context.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 交際(こうさい)する

SENSE 1 — SOCIALIZING:

Refers to maintaining relationships with others in a social or professional context.

  • 交際(こうさい)が広(ひろ)い: to have a wide social circle
  • 交際(こうさい)が狭(せま)い: to have a narrow social circle
  • 交際(こうさい)費(ひ): entertainment/socializing expenses

SENSE 2 — DATING:

Refers to a romantic relationship. This is the more common meaning in everyday conversation.

  • 交際(こうさい)する: to date, to go out with
  • 交際(こうさい)相手(あいて): romantic partner
  • 交際(こうさい)を申(もう)し込(こ)む: to ask someone out
  • 交際(こうさい)期間(きかん): length of relationship

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal. In casual speech, 付(つ)き合(あ)う is more common for "dating." 交際(こうさい) is preferred in news, formal announcements, and written contexts.
