交際(こうさい)

こうさい
noun / suru-verb
association, socializing, dating

Definitions

1. association, socializing, company
Having social relationships with others.
2. dating, relationship
A romantic relationship between two people.

Examples

交際(こうさい)が広(ひろ)い。
To have a wide circle of friends.
二人(ふたり)は交際(こうさい)している。
The two are dating.
交際(こうさい)費(ひ)
Entertainment expenses; socializing costs.
彼(かれ)らは3年間(ねんかん)交際(こうさい)した後(あと)、先月(せんげつ)結婚(けっこん)した。
After dating for three years, they got married last month.

Notes

交際(こうさい) covers both social and romantic relationships.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 交際(こうさい)する (to associate, to date)
  • 交際(こうさい)が広(ひろ)い/狭い (wide/narrow social circle)
  • 交際(こうさい)費(ひ) (entertainment expenses)
  • 交際(こうさい)相手(あいて) (partner, date)

NOTE: Can refer to both friendships and romantic relationships depending on context.
