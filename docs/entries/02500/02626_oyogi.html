泳(およ)ぎ

およぎ
noun
swimming, swim
1. swimming, swim
The activity or act of swimming. The noun form of 泳ぐ.
2. swimming style, stroke
A particular way of swimming.
彼(かれ)は泳(およ)ぎが上手(じょうず)だ。
He is good at swimming.
夏(なつ)は海(うみ)で泳(およ)ぎを楽(たの)しむ。
I enjoy swimming in the sea in summer.
犬(いぬ)かきの泳(およ)ぎ。
The doggy paddle swimming style.

泳(およ)ぎ is the noun form of 泳(およ)ぐ (to swim).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 泳(およ)ぎが上手(じょうず)/下手(へた) (good/bad at swimming)
  • 泳(およ)ぎを楽(たの)しむ (to enjoy swimming)
  • 〜の泳(およ)ぎ (~ style of swimming)

SWIMMING STYLES:

  • 平泳(ひらおよ)ぎ (breaststroke)
  • 背泳(せおよ)ぎ (backstroke)
  • 犬(いぬ)かき (doggy paddle)
  • クロール (freestyle/front crawl)
  • バタフライ (butterfly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泳(およ)ぎ: swimming (as activity or skill)
  • 水泳(すいえい): swimming (sport, formal)
