五(いつ)つ

いつつ
noun
five (things)
1. five (things)
The native Japanese counter for five objects.
りんごを五(いつ)つください。
Please give me five apples.
五(いつ)つの質問(しつもん)があります。
I have five questions.
子供(こども)が五(いつ)ついます。
There are five children.

五(いつ)つ is the native Japanese number 5 for counting things.

NATIVE COUNTING (1-10):

  • 一(ひと)つ (1)
  • 二(ふた)つ (2)
  • 三(みっ)つ (3)
  • 四(よっ)つ (4)
  • 五(いつ)つ (5)
  • 六(むっ)つ (6)
  • 七(なな)つ (7)
  • 八(やっ)つ (8)
  • 九(ここの)つ (9)
  • 十(とお) (10)

USAGE: Used for general counting of objects without specific counters.
