嫌(きら)い

きらい
na-adjective
dislike, hate
1. dislike, hate
To not like something or someone.
野菜(やさい)が嫌(きら)いです。
I don't like vegetables.
嫌(きら)いな食(た)べ物(もの)
Food I dislike.
勉強(べんきょう)が嫌(きら)いじゃない。
I don't dislike studying.
嫌(きら)いな人(ひと)はいません。
There's no one I dislike.
子供(こども)のとき魚(さかな)が嫌(きら)いだったが、今(いま)は好(す)きだ。
I disliked fish as a child, but now I like it.

嫌(きら)い is a na-adjective expressing dislike. Unlike English "hate," it covers mild dislikes as well as strong ones, so it is the ordinary word for "I don't like it."

FORMS:

  • 嫌(きら)いだ: (I) dislike
  • 嫌(きら)いな + noun: disliked ~
  • 嫌(きら)いではない / 嫌(きら)いじゃない: don't dislike

INTENSITY SCALE:

  • 好(す)き: like
  • 嫌(きら)いじゃない: don't dislike
  • 嫌(きら)い: dislike
  • 大嫌(だいきら)い: really hate

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が嫌(きら)い: dislike ~
  • 嫌(きら)いになる: to come to dislike
  • 嫌(きら)いではありません: I don't dislike (polite denial)

PARTICLES:
が marks what is disliked: 野菜(やさい)が嫌(きら)い (dislike vegetables). The topic marker は replaces が when the item is contrasted with something else: 野菜(やさい)は嫌(きら)いです (vegetables, at least, I don't like).

CAUTION — PEOPLE:
Saying 嫌(きら)い directly about a person is blunt and can sound harsh. In conversation speakers usually soften it: 苦手(にがて) (not comfortable with), あまり好(す)きではない (not very fond of), or 好(す)きじゃない.

CONTRAST WITH 苦手(にがて):
嫌(きら)い states an emotional dislike; 苦手(にがて) says the speaker is bad at something or ill at ease with it, and is the politer choice for foods, subjects, and people.

Related Words

Antonym: 好(す)き (like, fond of)
Antonym: 気(き)に入(い)る (to like)
Related: 嫌(きら)う (to dislike (verb form))
Contrast: 苦手(にがて) (poor at, not comfortable with (softer))
Related: 大嫌(だいきら)い (to really hate (stronger))
