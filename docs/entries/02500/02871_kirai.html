嫌(きら)い

きらい
na-adjective
dislike, hate

Definitions

1. dislike, hate
To not like something or someone.

Examples

野菜(やさい)が嫌(きら)いです。
I don't like vegetables.
嫌(きら)いな食(た)べ物(もの)
Food I dislike.
勉強(べんきょう)が嫌(きら)いじゃない。
I don't dislike studying.

Notes

嫌(きら)い expresses dislike.

PATTERNS:

  • 〜が嫌(きら)いだ (dislike ~)
  • 嫌(きら)いな + noun
  • 大(だい)嫌(きら)い (really hate)

OPPOSITE:

  • 好(す)き (like)
