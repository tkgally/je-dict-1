1. restriction, restraint, constraint
Limiting someone's freedom or actions.
時間(じかん)に拘束(こうそく)される。
To be constrained by time.
拘束(こうそく)時間(じかん)が長(なが)い。
The working hours are long.
[Note: 拘束時間 = time one must be present]
この契約(けいやく)には法的(ほうてき)拘束(こうそく)力(りょく)があるので、簡単(かんたん)には解除(かいじょ)できない。
This contract is legally binding, so it cannot be easily terminated.
2. detention, custody
Holding someone in custody, especially by legal authority.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を拘束(こうそく)する。
To detain a suspect.
警察(けいさつ)は身柄(みがら)を拘束(こうそく)した。
The police took the person into custody.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は48時間(じかん)にわたって拘束(こうそく)された後(あと)、釈放(しゃくほう)された。
The suspect was released after being detained for 48 hours.

拘束(こうそく) means to restrict freedom or to detain someone physically. It functions as both a noun and a する verb.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を拘束(こうそく)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 拘束(こうそく)される: to be restrained/detained
  • 拘束(こうそく)時間(じかん): hours of obligation (time one must be present at work)
  • 拘束(こうそく)力(りょく): binding force (legal or figurative)
  • 身柄(みがら)拘束(こうそく): physical custody, taking someone into custody

LEGAL USAGE:
In legal contexts, 拘束(こうそく) refers to formal detention by authorities. 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を拘束(こうそく)する means to detain a suspect. Distinct from 逮捕(たいほ) (arrest), which is the formal legal act of arresting.

WORKPLACE USAGE:
拘束(こうそく)時間(じかん) is commonly used for the total hours an employee must be present, including breaks — broader than 労働(ろうどう)時間(じかん) (actual working hours).

Related Words

Related: 逮捕(たいほ) (arrest)
Antonym: 釈放(しゃくほう) (release)
Related: 解放(かいほう) (liberation, release)
Synonym: 束縛(そくばく) (emotional/social restraint)
