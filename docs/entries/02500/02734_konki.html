根気(こんき)

こんき
noun
patience, perseverance
1. patience, perseverance
The ability to continue despite difficulties.
根気(こんき)よく続(つづ)ける。
To continue patiently.
根気(こんき)のいる仕事(しごと)
Work that requires patience.
根気(こんき)が続(つづ)かない。
I can't keep at it. / I lack perseverance.

根気(こんき) means "patience" or "perseverance"—the ability to persist through difficulties over time. It combines 根(こん) (root) + 気(き) (spirit).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 根気(こんき)よく: patiently, persistently
  • 根気(こんき)がいる: to require patience
  • 根気(こんき)が続(つづ)く: to have staying power
  • 根気(こんき)が続(つづ)かない: to lose patience, give up easily
  • 根気(こんき)強(づよ)い: persevering, patient

CONTRAST WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 根気(こんき): sustained patience for long-term effort
  • 忍耐(にんたい): endurance, bearing hardship
  • 辛抱(しんぼう): patience, putting up with something

USAGE: Often used when describing work or learning that requires persistent effort.
