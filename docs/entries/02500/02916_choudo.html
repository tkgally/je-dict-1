ちょうど

ちょうど
adverb
exactly, just, precisely
1. exactly, precisely
Neither more nor less than a specific amount or time.
ちょうど3時(じ)です。
It's exactly 3 o'clock.
ちょうど千円(せんえん)です。
It's exactly 1000 yen.
ちょうどいいサイズだ。
It's just the right size.
ちょうど半分(はんぶん)です。
It's exactly half.
ちょうど一年(いちねん)です。
It's exactly one year.
お金(かね)がちょうど足(た)りた。
I had exactly enough money.
2. just, right now
At this very moment.
ちょうど今(いま)出(で)かけるところです。
I'm just about to leave now.
ちょうど電話(でんわ)しようとしていた。
I was just about to call.
ちょうどその話(はなし)をしていた。
We were just talking about that.
ちょうど来(き)たところだ。
I just arrived.
ちょうど出(で)かけようとしたときに、友達(ともだち)から電話(でんわ)が来(き)た。
Just as I was about to leave, I got a call from a friend.

ちょうど is an adverb meaning 'exactly,' 'precisely,' or 'just (at this moment).'

SENSE 1 - EXACTNESS:

Indicates precise quantity, time, or amount.

  • ちょうど3時(じ): exactly 3 o'clock
  • ちょうど千円(せんえん): exactly 1000 yen
  • ちょうど半分(はんぶん): exactly half
  • ちょうど一年(いちねん): exactly one year

SENSE 2 - TIMING:

Indicates something happening at the right moment.

  • ちょうど今(いま): just now, right now
  • ちょうど来(き)たところ: just arrived
  • ちょうど〜しようとしていた: was just about to ~

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ちょうどいい: just right, perfect
  • ちょうど良(よ)い機会(きかい): a perfect opportunity

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぴったり: exactly (fits perfectly)
  • まさに: precisely (more emphatic/literary)
