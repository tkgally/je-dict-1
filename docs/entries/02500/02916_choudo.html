ちょうど

ちょうど
adverb
exactly, just, precisely
1. exactly, precisely
Neither more nor less than a specific amount or time.
ちょうど3時(じ)です。
It's exactly 3 o'clock.
ちょうど千円(せんえん)です。
It's exactly 1000 yen.
ちょうどいいサイズだ。
It's just the right size.
Sense 1: exactly (right)
ちょうど半分(はんぶん)です。
It's exactly half.
Sense 1: exactly
ちょうど一年(いちねん)です。
It's exactly one year.
Sense 1: exactly
お金(かね)がちょうど足(た)りた。
I had exactly enough money.
Sense 1: exactly/precisely
2. just, right now
At this very moment.
ちょうど今(いま)出(で)かけるところです。
I'm just about to leave now.
ちょうど電話(でんわ)しようとしていた。
I was just about to call.
Sense 2: just now
ちょうどその話(はなし)をしていた。
We were just talking about that.
Sense 2: just (at that moment)
ちょうど来(き)たところだ。
I just arrived.
Sense 2: just now

ちょうど indicates exactness.

COMMON USES:

  • ちょうど + time (exactly at ~)
  • ちょうど + amount (exactly ~)
  • ちょうど今(いま) (just now)
  • ちょうどいい (just right)

SIMILAR:

  • ぴったり (exactly, perfectly fitting)
  • まさに (exactly, precisely - more emphatic)
