粉(こな)

こな
noun
powder, flour
1. powder
A dry substance in the form of fine particles.
2. flour
Ground grain, especially wheat.
小麦(こむぎ)粉(こ)でパンを作(つく)る。
To make bread with wheat flour.
[Note: In compounds, read as こ]
粉(こな)ミルク
Powdered milk.
粉(こな)薬(ぐすり)は飲(の)みにくい。
Powdered medicine is hard to take.

粉(こな) means powder or flour.

TYPES OF FLOUR/POWDER:

  • 小麦(こむぎ)粉(こ) (wheat flour)
  • 米(こめ)粉(こ) (rice flour)
  • 片栗(かたくり)粉(こ) (potato starch)
  • 粉(こな)チーズ (powdered cheese)
  • 粉(こな)ミルク (powdered milk)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 粉(こな)にする (to grind into powder)
  • 粉(こな)をまぶす (to coat with powder)
  • 粉(こな)をふるう (to sift flour)

NOTE ON READINGS:

  • As a standalone word: こな
  • In compounds: often こ (e.g., 小麦(こむぎ)粉(こ))

RELATED:

  • 粉(こな)末(まつ) (powder, powdered form)
  • 粉(こな)状(じょう) (powdery, in powder form)
