お姉(ねえ)さん

おねえさん
noun
older sister (polite)
1. older sister (polite)
A polite way to refer to an older sister.
お姉(ねえ)さんは大学生(だいがくせい)ですか。
Is your older sister a university student?
お姉(ねえ)さんに借(か)りた。
I borrowed it from my older sister.
2. young woman
Used to address a young woman, similar to 'miss' in English.
お姉(ねえ)さん、これはいくらですか。
Excuse me, miss, how much is this?

お姉(ねえ)さん is the polite term for older sister.

FAMILY TERMS:

  • お姉(ねえ)さん: older sister (polite)
  • 姉(あね): my older sister (humble)
  • お姉(ねえ)ちゃん: older sister (casual/affectionate)

ALSO USED:

  • To address a young woman (like 'miss')
