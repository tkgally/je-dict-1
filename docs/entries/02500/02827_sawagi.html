騒(さわ)ぎ

さわぎ
noun
uproar, disturbance, commotion
See also: 騒(さわ)ぐ (verb form)
1. uproar, disturbance, commotion
A noisy or chaotic situation.
大(おお)騒(さわ)ぎになる。
To become a big commotion.
騒(さわ)ぎを起(お)こす。
To cause a disturbance.
なんの騒(さわ)ぎですか？
What's all the fuss about?
有名人(ゆうめいじん)が来店(らいてん)して、店内(てんない)は大(おお)騒(さわ)ぎになった。
A celebrity came to the store, causing a big commotion inside.

騒(さわ)ぎ is the noun form of 騒(さわ)ぐ (to make noise, to make a fuss). It describes both physical commotion and figurative uproar.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 大(おお)騒(さわ)ぎ: big commotion, uproar
  • 騒(さわ)ぎを起(お)こす: to cause a disturbance
  • 騒(さわ)ぎになる: to become an uproar
  • 〜騒(さわ)ぎ: ~ scandal/fuss (e.g., 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)騒(さわ)ぎ, a food poisoning scare)

USAGE:
The 〜騒(さわ)ぎ suffix is very productive. It attaches to the cause of the commotion to describe the whole incident: 地震(じしん)騒(さわ)ぎ (earthquake panic), 偽装(ぎそう)騒(さわ)ぎ (fraud scandal).

RELATED WORDS:

  • 騒(さわ)ぐ: to make noise, to cause a fuss (verb)
  • 騒動(そうどう): disturbance, turmoil (more formal, larger scale)
  • 騒音(そうおん): noise (unwanted sound)
