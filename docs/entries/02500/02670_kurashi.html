暮(く)らし

くらし
noun
living, livelihood, life
1. living, livelihood
The way one lives or earns a living.
2. life, lifestyle
One's daily life and how it is conducted.
田舎(いなか)暮(く)らしを楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy country living.
暮(く)らしが楽(らく)になった。
Life has become easier.
一人(ひとり)暮(ぐ)らしを始(はじ)める。
To start living alone.
[Note: Becomes ぐらし as suffix]

暮(く)らし describes how one lives day-to-day.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 暮(く)らしが楽(らく)だ/きつい (life is easy/hard)
  • 暮(く)らしを立(た)てる (to make a living)
  • 暮(く)らし向(む)き (living conditions)

AS SUFFIX (becomes ぐらし):

  • 一人(ひとり)暮(ぐ)らし (living alone)
  • 二人(ふたり)暮(ぐ)らし (living as a couple)
  • 田舎(いなか)暮(ぐ)らし (country living)
  • 都会(とかい)暮(ぐ)らし (city living)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 暮(く)らす (to live)
  • 生活(せいかつ) (life, livelihood - more formal)
