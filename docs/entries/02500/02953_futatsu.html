二(ふた)つ

ふたつ
noun
two (things)
1. two (things)
The number two when counting things using native Japanese numbers.
りんごを二(ふた)つください。
Two apples, please.
問題(もんだい)が二(ふた)つあります。
There are two problems.
二(ふた)つに分(わ)ける。
To divide into two.

二(ふた)つ is the native Japanese number "two" used for counting general objects.

NATIVE COUNTING SYSTEM (1-10):

  • 一(ひと)つ, 二(ふた)つ, 三(みっ)つ, 四(よっ)つ, 五(いつ)つ
  • 六(むっ)つ, 七(なな)つ, 八(やっ)つ, 九(ここの)つ, 十(とお)

WHEN TO USE:

Native numbers (一(ひと)つ, 二(ふた)つ, etc.) are used when:

  • No specific counter applies
  • Counting abstract things or general items
  • Casual, everyday counting

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 二(ふた)つください: Two, please
  • 二(ふた)つに分(わ)ける: to divide in two
  • 二(ふた)つとも: both (of them)
  • 二(ふた)つ目(め): the second one

NOTE:
Beyond 10, native numbers are rarely used. Use Sino-Japanese numbers (十一(じゅういち), etc.) instead.
