勘定(かんじょう)

かんじょう
noun / suru-verb
bill, check; calculation
1. bill, check (at restaurant)
The account for payment at a restaurant, bar, or shop.
お勘定(かんじょう)をお願(ねが)いします。
The bill, please.
At a restaurant
2. calculation, counting
The act of calculating numbers or counting.
勘定(かんじょう)が合(あ)わない。
The numbers don't add up.
3. consideration, account
Taking something into consideration or account.
経費(けいひ)を勘定(かんじょう)に入(い)れる。
To include expenses in the calculation.

勘定(かんじょう) has several related meanings around calculation and payment.

SENSE 1 - BILL:

  • お勘定(かんじょう) (the bill - polite)
  • 勘定(かんじょう)を払(はら)う (to pay the bill)
  • Traditional term; 会計(かいけい) is more common now

SENSE 2 - CALCULATION:

  • 勘定(かんじょう)する (to calculate)
  • 勘定(かんじょう)が合(あ)う (numbers match)
  • 勘定(かんじょう)が合(あ)わない (numbers don't match)

SENSE 3 - CONSIDERATION:

  • 〜を勘定(かんじょう)に入(い)れる (to take ~ into account)
  • 勘定(かんじょう)外(がい) (not taken into account)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会計(かいけい): check (modern term)
  • 計算(けいさん): calculation (general)

Related Words

Synonym: 会計(かいけい) (check (modern))
