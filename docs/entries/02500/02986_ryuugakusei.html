留学生(りゅうがくせい)

りゅうがくせい
noun
international student
1. international student, exchange student
A student studying abroad.
留学生(りゅうがくせい)として日本(にほん)に来(き)ました。
I came to Japan as an international student.
クラスに留学生(りゅうがくせい)が3人(にん)います。
There are 3 international students in the class.
留学生(りゅうがくせい)向(む)けのプログラム。
A program for international students.

留学生(りゅうがくせい) refers to international students or exchange students - those studying in a foreign country.

WORD FORMATION:
留学(りゅうがく) (studying abroad) + 生(せい) (student) = international student

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 留学(りゅうがく): study abroad (noun)
  • 留学(りゅうがく)する: to study abroad
  • 交換(こうかん)留学生(りゅうがくせい): exchange student
  • 短期(たんき)留学(りゅうがく): short-term study abroad
  • 留学先(りゅうがくさき): study abroad destination

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい)向(む)け: for international students
  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい)寮(りょう): international student dormitory
  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい)別科(べっか): international student program
  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい)支援(しえん): international student support

CONTRAST:

  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい): student from abroad (in Japan)
  • 海外(かいがい)留学(りゅうがく): studying overseas (Japanese going abroad)
