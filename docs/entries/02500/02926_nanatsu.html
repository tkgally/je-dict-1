七(なな)つ

ななつ
noun
seven (things)
1. seven (things)
The native Japanese counter for seven objects.
たまごを七(なな)つ買(か)った。
I bought seven eggs.
箱(はこ)の中(なか)に七(なな)つあります。
There are seven in the box.
問題(もんだい)が七(なな)つあります。
There are seven problems.
りんごを七(なな)つもらったから、友達(ともだち)に分(わ)けた。
I received seven apples, so I shared them with my friend.
この町(まち)には七(なな)つのお寺(てら)があって、全部(ぜんぶ)歩(ある)いて回(まわ)ることができる。
There are seven temples in this town, and you can walk to all of them.

七(なな)つ is the native Japanese counter for seven objects. It's part of the ひとつ counting system used for general objects.

THE NATIVE COUNTING SYSTEM (1-10):

  • 一(ひと)つ: one
  • 二(ふた)つ: two
  • 三(みっ)つ: three
  • 四(よっ)つ: four
  • 五(いつ)つ: five
  • 六(むっ)つ: six
  • 七(なな)つ: seven
  • 八(やっ)つ: eight
  • 九(ここの)つ: nine
  • 十(とお): ten (no つ)

WHEN TO USE:

  • General objects without specific counters
  • Age of children (under 10): 七(なな)つ = 7 years old
  • Informal counting

CONTRAST WITH SINO-JAPANESE:

  • 七(なな)つ (native): used alone or for general objects
  • 七(しち) (Sino-Japanese): used with specific counters (七人(しちにん), 七個(ななこ))

NOTE ON 七(なな):
The reading なな is preferred over しち in many cases to avoid confusion with 一(いち) (one) and 四(し) (four).
