お父(とう)さん

おとうさん
noun
father (polite)
1. father (polite/formal)
A polite way to refer to someone's father, including one's own when speaking to others.
お父(とう)さん、ただいま。
Dad, I'm home.
お父(とう)さんは会社員(かいしゃいん)です。
My father is a company employee.
お父(とう)さんに聞(き)いてみます。
I'll ask my father.

お父(とう)さん is the polite term for father.

FAMILY TERMS:

  • お父(とう)さん: father (polite, when addressing or referring to someone else's father)
  • 父(ちち): my father (humble, when referring to one's own father to others)
  • パパ: dad (casual)

USAGE:

  • Used to address one's own father
  • Used to refer to someone else's father
