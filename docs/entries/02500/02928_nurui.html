温(ぬる)い

ぬるい
i-adjective
lukewarm, tepid

Definitions

1. lukewarm, tepid
Neither hot nor cold; moderately warm.

Examples

お風呂(ふろ)が温(ぬる)い。
The bath is lukewarm.
コーヒーが温(ぬる)くなった。
The coffee has become lukewarm.
温(ぬる)いお湯(ゆ)
Lukewarm water.

Notes

温(ぬる)い describes something not hot enough.

TEMPERATURE ADJECTIVES:

  • 熱(あつ)い (hot - for objects)
  • 暖(あたた)かい/温(あたた)かい (warm)
  • 温(ぬる)い (lukewarm)
  • 冷(つめ)たい (cold)

USAGE: Often has a slightly negative nuance when something should be hot but isn't.

FIGURATIVE USE: Can describe a half-hearted attitude or effort.
