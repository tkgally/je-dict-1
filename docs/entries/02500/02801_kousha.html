校舎(こうしゃ)

こうしゃ
noun
school building

Definitions

1. school building
The building where classes are held at a school.

Examples

新(あたら)しい校舎(こうしゃ)
A new school building.
校舎(こうしゃ)の裏(うら)
Behind the school building.
古(ふる)い校舎(こうしゃ)を建(た)て替(か)える。
To rebuild the old school building.
地震(じしん)で校舎(こうしゃ)が損傷(そんしょう)したため、生徒(せいと)たちは仮設(かせつ)校舎(こうしゃ)で授業(じゅぎょう)を受(う)けている。
Because the school building was damaged in the earthquake, students are taking classes in a temporary building.

Notes

校舎(こうしゃ) refers to school buildings.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 校舎(こうしゃ)の中(なか)/外 (inside/outside the school building)
  • 新(あたら)しい/古い校舎(こうしゃ) (new/old school building)
  • 仮設(かせつ)校舎(こうしゃ) (temporary school building)
