九日(ここのか)

ここのか
noun
nine days, ninth day
1. nine days
A period of nine days.
旅行(りょこう)は九日(ここのか)かかった。
The trip took nine days.
2. ninth day of the month
The ninth day of any month.
九月(くがつ)九日(ここのか)は何曜日(なんようび)ですか。
What day of the week is September 9th?
九日(ここのか)に会議(かいぎ)があります。
There is a meeting on the 9th.

九日(ここのか) uses native Japanese counting.
