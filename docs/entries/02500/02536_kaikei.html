会計(かいけい)

かいけい
noun
payment, accounting

Definitions

1. payment, bill, check
The act of paying for something, especially at a restaurant or store.
2. accounting, finance
The management or recording of financial transactions.

Examples

お会計(かいけい)をお願(ねが)いします。
Check, please. / May I have the bill?
Very common phrase in restaurants
会計(かいけい)はレジでお願(ねが)いします。
Please pay at the register.
彼女(かのじょ)は会計(かいけい)事務所(じむしょ)で働(はたら)いている。
She works at an accounting firm.

Notes

会計(かいけい) has two main meanings: payment (at a store/restaurant) and accounting (as a profession/field).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お会計(かいけい)をお願(ねが)いします (Check, please)
  • 会計(かいけい)を済(す)ませる (to pay the bill)
  • 別(べつ)会計(かいけい)で (separate checks)
  • 会計(かいけい)士(し) (certified public accountant)
  • 会計(かいけい)年度(ねんど) (fiscal year)

USAGE NOTE:

  • In restaurants, お会計(かいけい) is polite; you can also just say 勘定(かんじょう)お願(ねが)いします
  • Signs at stores often say 会計(かいけい) or レジ for the checkout area

Related Words

Synonym: 勘定(かんじょう) (bill, calculation)
