温暖(おんだん)

おんだん
na-adjective / noun
warm (climate), mild
1. warm, mild (climate)
Having a pleasantly warm temperature, typically referring to climate.
この地域(ちいき)は一年(いちねん)中(じゅう)温暖(おんだん)だ。
This region is warm all year round.
温暖(おんだん)な気候(きこう)で過(す)ごしやすい。
The warm climate makes it easy to live here.
地球(ちきゅう)温暖(おんだん)化(か)が進(すす)んでいる。
Global warming is progressing.

温暖(おんだん) describes warm climate or temperature, especially for regions.

FORMS:

  • 温暖(おんだん)な + noun (warm ~)
  • 温暖(おんだん)に (warmly)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 温暖(おんだん)な気候(きこう) (warm climate)
  • 温暖(おんだん)な地域(ちいき) (warm region)

IMPORTANT COMPOUND:

  • 地球(ちきゅう)温暖(おんだん)化(か) (global warming)
  • 温暖(おんだん)化(か)対策(たいさく) (measures against warming)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 暖(あたた)かい: warm (general, casual)
  • 温暖(おんだん): warm climate (more technical/formal)
  • 穏(おだ)やか: mild, gentle (personality/weather)
