1. calculation, computation
Mathematical operations or figuring out numbers.
計算(けいさん)を間違(まちが)えた。
I made a calculation error.
電卓(でんたく)で計算(けいさん)する。
To calculate with a calculator.
暗算(あんざん)で計算(けいさん)するのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to calculate mentally.
2. consideration, planning
Taking something into account when planning.
計算(けいさん)高(だか)い人(ひと)
A calculating person.
計算(けいさん)に入(い)れる。
To take into account.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)には計算(けいさん)があると思(おも)う。
I think there's calculation behind his words.

計算(けいさん) has two main uses: (1) mathematical calculation, and (2) strategic thinking or taking something into account when planning.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 計算(けいさん)する: to calculate
  • 計算(けいさん)に入(い)れる: to take into account, to factor in
  • 計算(けいさん)が合(あ)う: the numbers add up
  • 計算(けいさん)を間違(まちが)える: to make a calculation error
  • 計算(けいさん)高(だか)い: calculating, scheming (describes a person who acts strategically for personal gain)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 計算(けいさん)機(き): calculator
  • 暗算(あんざん): mental calculation
  • 誤算(ごさん): miscalculation
  • 計算(けいさん)式(しき): mathematical formula/expression

The figurative sense appears in expressions like 計算(けいさん)がある (there is calculation behind something) and 計算(けいさん)高(だか)い人(ひと) (a calculating person), both carrying a somewhat negative nuance of self-interested scheming.
