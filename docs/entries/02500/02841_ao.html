青(あお)

あお
noun / no-adjective
blue, green (traffic lights)
1. blue
The color blue.
2. green (for traffic signals)
In Japanese, traffic lights that show 'go' are called 青 even though they appear green.
青(あお)い空(そら)
Blue sky.
信号(しんごう)が青(あお)になった。
The traffic light turned green.
青(あお)は私(わたし)の好(す)きな色(いろ)です。
Blue is my favorite color.

青(あお) is a basic color word.

USAGE:

  • As noun: 青(あお)が好(す)き (I like blue)
  • As no-adjective: 青(あお)の服(ふく) (blue clothes)
  • As i-adjective: 青(あお)い (blue)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese, 青(あお) traditionally covers both blue and green. Traffic lights use 青(あお) for 'go' even though they appear green.
