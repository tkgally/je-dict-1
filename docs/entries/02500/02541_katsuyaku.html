活躍(かつやく)

かつやく
noun / suru-verb
active role, great performance
1. activity, active role, great performance
Playing an active or prominent role; performing well and making significant contributions.
彼(かれ)は世界(せかい)で活躍(かつやく)している。
He is active on the world stage.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)で大(おお)活躍(かつやく)した。
He played a great role in today's game.
女性(じょせい)が活躍(かつやく)できる社会(しゃかい)を目指(めざ)す。
To aim for a society where women can play active roles.

活躍(かつやく) describes playing an active or prominent role, often with implications of success or excellent performance.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 活躍(かつやく)する (to play an active role, to be active)
  • 活躍(かつやく)の場(ば) (place/opportunity for active involvement)
  • 大(おお)活躍(かつやく)する (to play a great role)
  • 活躍(かつやく)が期待(きたい)される (expected to be active/perform well)

USAGE:

  • Often used in sports, business, and career contexts
  • Implies positive, noteworthy contribution
  • Common in news and formal writing

EXAMPLE CONTEXTS:

  • 海外(かいがい)で活躍(かつやく)する選手(せんしゅ) (athletes active overseas)
  • 若手(わかて)の活躍(かつやく) (active role of young people)
