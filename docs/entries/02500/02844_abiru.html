1. to bathe in, to shower
To pour water over oneself; to take a shower.
シャワーを浴(あ)びる。
To take a shower.
水(みず)を浴(あ)びる。
To pour water over oneself.
朝(あさ)シャワーを浴(あ)びてから会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)く。
I take a shower in the morning before going to work.
運動(うんどう)の後(あと)にシャワーを浴(あ)びた。
I took a shower after exercising.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)にホースで水(みず)を浴(あ)びて遊(あそ)んだ。
On a hot day, we played by spraying ourselves with water from a hose.
2. to be exposed to, to bask in
To be covered by or exposed to something like light or attention.
日光(にっこう)を浴(あ)びる。
To bask in sunlight.
注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる。
To attract attention.
拍手(はくしゅ)を浴(あ)びた。
I received applause.
新人(しんじん)歌手(かしゅ)が注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びている。
The new singer is attracting attention.
彼女(かのじょ)のスピーチは会場(かいじょう)中(じゅう)から大(おお)きな拍手(はくしゅ)を浴(あ)びた。
Her speech received a big round of applause from the entire venue.

浴(あ)びる means to pour or receive something over oneself. It has both a literal sense (water, shower) and a figurative sense (attention, criticism).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for what is received

ASPECT (ている):
浴(あ)びている describes the ongoing state of being showered with or exposed to something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シャワーを浴(あ)びる: to take a shower
  • 水(みず)を浴(あ)びる: to pour water over oneself
  • 日光(にっこう)を浴(あ)びる: to bask in sunlight
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる: to attract attention
  • 批判(ひはん)を浴(あ)びる: to receive criticism
  • 拍手(はくしゅ)を浴(あ)びる: to receive applause

CONTRAST:
For bathing in a tub, use お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)る, not 浴(あ)びる. 浴(あ)びる implies something coming down onto you, not immersion.
