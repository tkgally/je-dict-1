技師(ぎし)

ぎし
noun
engineer, technician
1. engineer, technician
A professional with technical expertise in a specialized field.
彼(かれ)は電気(でんき)技師(ぎし)だ。
He is an electrical engineer.
技師(ぎし)として働(はたら)く。
To work as an engineer.
技師(ぎし)に相談(そうだん)する。
To consult an engineer.

技師(ぎし) refers to engineers and technicians.

TYPES OF ENGINEERS:

  • 電気(でんき)技師(ぎし) (electrical engineer)
  • 機械(きかい)技師(ぎし) (mechanical engineer)
  • 土木(どぼく)技師(ぎし) (civil engineer)
  • 放射線(ほうしゃせん)技師(ぎし) (radiological technician)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 技師(ぎし): engineer/technician (formal, professional title)
  • エンジニア: engineer (more common, from English)
  • 技術者(ぎじゅつしゃ): technical person, engineer
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)者(しゃ): technician
