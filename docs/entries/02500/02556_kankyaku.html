観客(かんきゃく)

かんきゃく
noun
audience, spectator

Definitions

1. audience, spectator, viewer
People who watch a performance, game, or event.

Examples

観客(かんきゃく)が大(おお)きな拍手(はくしゅ)を送(おく)った。
The audience gave a big round of applause.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)には観客(かんきゃく)が多(おお)かった。
There were many spectators at today's game.
観客(かんきゃく)席(せき)は満員(まんいん)だった。
The audience seats were full.

Notes

観客(かんきゃく) refers to people who watch performances, sports, or events.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 観客(かんきゃく)席(せき) (spectator seats, audience area)
  • 観客(かんきゃく)動員(どういん) (audience turnout)
  • 観客(かんきゃく)を沸(わ)かせる (to excite the audience)

COUNTER: 人(にん) or 名(めい)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 聴衆(ちょうしゅう): audience (for lectures, speeches)
  • 視聴者(しちょうしゃ): viewer (TV, online)
  • 観客(かんきゃく): audience (live events, sports)
  • 観衆(かんしゅう): spectators (more literary)

USAGE NOTE:

  • Used for live performances, sports events, theater
  • Not typically used for TV viewers (use 視聴者(しちょうしゃ))

Related Words
