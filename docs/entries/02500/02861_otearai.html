お手洗(てあら)い

おてあらい
noun
restroom, toilet
1. restroom, toilet, bathroom
A polite term for the toilet or restroom.
お手洗(てあら)いはどこですか。
Where is the restroom?
お手洗(てあら)いに行(い)ってきます。
I'm going to the restroom.
お手洗(てあら)いを借(か)りてもいいですか。
May I use the restroom?

お手洗(てあら)い is a polite, euphemistic term for restroom or toilet, suitable for formal situations.

COMPONENTS:

  • お: honorific prefix
  • 手(て): hand
  • 洗(あら)い: washing (from 洗(あら)う)

LITERAL MEANING: 'place to wash hands' - a polite way to refer to the toilet.

POLITENESS LEVELS (toilet words):

  • お手洗(てあら)い: restroom (polite, safe for any situation)
  • トイレ: toilet (neutral, common in everyday speech)
  • 化粧室(けしょうしつ): powder room (formal, often used in announcements)
  • 洗面所(せんめんじょ): washroom (neutral, focuses on the sink area)
  • 便所(べんじょ): toilet (direct, can sound crude)
  • 厠(かわや): privy (old-fashioned, literary)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お手洗(てあら)いはどこですか: Where is the restroom?
  • お手洗(てあら)いに行(い)ってきます: I'm going to the restroom.
  • お手洗(てあら)いを借(か)りてもいいですか: May I use the restroom?
