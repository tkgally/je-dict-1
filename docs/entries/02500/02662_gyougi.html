行儀(ぎょうぎ)

ぎょうぎ
noun
manners, behavior
1. manners, behavior, deportment
The way one behaves, especially in social situations.
行儀(ぎょうぎ)よく座(すわ)る。
To sit properly with good manners.
行儀(ぎょうぎ)が悪(わる)い。
To have bad manners.
子供(こども)に行儀(ぎょうぎ)を教(おし)える。
To teach children manners.

行儀(ぎょうぎ) refers to manners and proper behavior.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)がいい/良(よ)い (to have good manners)
  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)が悪(わる)い (to have bad manners)
  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)よく (properly, with good manners)
  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)見習(みなら)い (apprenticeship for manners)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)作法(さほう) (manners and etiquette)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ): manners (behavior)
  • 礼儀(れいぎ): courtesy, etiquette
  • マナー: manners (loanword)
  • 作法(さほう): etiquette, manner
