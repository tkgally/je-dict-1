今晩(こんばん)

こんばん
noun
tonight, this evening
1. tonight, this evening
The evening or night of the current day.
今晩(こんばん)は何(なに)をしますか。
What are you doing tonight?
今晩(こんばん)映画(えいが)を見(み)に行(い)きませんか。
Would you like to go see a movie tonight?
今晩(こんばん)の夕食(ゆうしょく)は何(なに)ですか。
What's for dinner tonight?

今晩(こんばん) means tonight or this evening.

GREETING:

  • 今晩(こんばん)は (Good evening)

TIME EXPRESSIONS:

  • 今朝(けさ) (this morning)
  • 今日(きょう) (today)
  • 今夜(こんや) (tonight - more literary)
  • 昨晩(さくばん) (last night)
