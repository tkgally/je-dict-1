誰(だれ)か

だれか
pronoun
someone, somebody
1. someone, somebody
An unspecified person.
誰(だれ)か来(き)ましたか。
Did someone come?
誰(だれ)かに聞(き)いてみよう。
Let's ask someone.
誰(だれ)かが電話(でんわ)している。
Someone is making a phone call.

誰(だれ)か means "someone" or "somebody" - an unspecified person.

FORMATION:
誰(だれ) (who) + か (question particle) = someone

INDEFINITE PRONOUN PATTERN:

Question word + か = indefinite:

  • 誰(だれ)か: someone
  • 何(なに)か: something
  • どこか: somewhere
  • いつか: sometime
  • 何(なん)とか: somehow

NEGATIVE FORMS (with も + negative verb):

  • 誰(だれ)も〜ない: no one
  • 何(なに)も〜ない: nothing
  • どこも〜ない: nowhere

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 誰(だれ)かいますか: Is anyone there?
  • 誰(だれ)かに聞(き)く: to ask someone
  • 誰(だれ)か来(き)た: Someone came
