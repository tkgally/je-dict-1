誰(だれ)か

だれか
pronoun
someone, somebody
See also: 誰(だれ) (who (base form))
1. someone, somebody
An unspecified person.
誰(だれ)か来(き)ましたか。
Did someone come?
誰(だれ)かに聞(き)いてみよう。
Let's ask someone.
誰(だれ)かが電話(でんわ)している。
Someone is making a phone call.
誰(だれ)かいい人(ひと)を知(し)りませんか。
Do you know someone good?
外(そと)で誰(だれ)かが大(おお)きい声(こえ)で話(はな)しているのが聞(き)こえた。
I could hear someone talking loudly outside.

FORMATION:
誰(だれ) (who) + か (question particle) = someone

INDEFINITE PRONOUN PATTERN:

Question word + か = indefinite:

  • 誰(だれ)か: someone
  • 何(なに)か: something
  • どこか: somewhere
  • いつか: sometime
  • 何(なん)とか: somehow

NEGATIVE FORMS (with も + negative verb):

  • 誰(だれ)も〜ない: no one
  • 何(なに)も〜ない: nothing
  • どこも〜ない: nowhere

か VS も: か makes the question word indefinite and positive (someone, something); も with a negative verb makes it universally negative (no one, nothing). The pair is the standard way Japanese expresses English 'some-' and 'no-' words.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 誰(だれ)かいますか: Is anyone there?
  • 誰(だれ)かに聞(き)く: to ask someone
  • 誰(だれ)か来(き)た: Someone came

In questions, 誰(だれ)か is often best translated 'anyone' rather than 'someone': 誰(だれ)かいませんか is 'Is anyone there?'
