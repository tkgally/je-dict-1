小(ちい)さな

ちいさな
pre-noun adjectival
small, little
See also: 大(おお)きな (big (antonym))
1. small, little
Of limited size or extent.
小(ちい)さな犬(いぬ)がいる。
There is a small dog.
小(ちい)さな子供(こども)
A small child.
小(ちい)さな声(こえ)で話(はな)す。
To speak in a small voice.
庭(にわ)に小(ちい)さな花(はな)がたくさん咲(さ)いている。
Many small flowers are blooming in the garden.
小(ちい)さな町(まち)だけど、静(しず)かでとてもいいところです。
It's a small town, but it's quiet and a very nice place.

小(ちい)さな is a 連体詞(れんたいし) (pre-noun adjectival) — a special word class that can only modify nouns directly. It cannot be conjugated or used as a predicate.

COMPARISON WITH 小(ちい)さい:

  • 小(ちい)さな + noun: pre-noun form only (小(ちい)さな犬(いぬ))
  • 小(ちい)さい + noun: i-adjective form (小(ちい)さい犬(いぬ))
  • Both are interchangeable before nouns, but only 小(ちい)さい works as a predicate: この犬(いぬ)は小(ちい)さい (NOT 小(ちい)さな)

OPPOSITE:

  • 大(おお)きな (big — same pre-noun adjectival type)
  • 大(おお)きい (big — i-adjective)

Note: 小(ちい)さな and 大(おお)きな are the only common 連体詞(れんたいし) with corresponding い-adjective forms. Most 連体詞 (like この, あの) have no adjective counterpart.
