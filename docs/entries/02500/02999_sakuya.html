昨夜(さくや)

さくや
noun
last night
1. last night
The night before today. Can also be read as ゆうべ.
昨夜(さくや)はよく眠(ねむ)れた。
I slept well last night.
昨夜(さくや)のパーティーは楽(たの)しかった。
Last night's party was fun.
昨夜(さくや)から雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っている。
It's been raining since last night.
昨夜(さくや)は友人(ゆうじん)と遅(おそ)くまで話(はな)していた。
Last night, I was talking with a friend until late.
昨夜(さくや)見(み)た映画(えいが)がとても面白(おもしろ)かったので、友達(ともだち)にもすすめた。
The movie I saw last night was so interesting that I recommended it to my friends.

昨夜(さくや) means "last night." This word has two standard readings with different registers.

READINGS:

  • さくや: formal, written, news
  • ゆうべ: casual, spoken, everyday

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 昨夜(さくや)の出来事(できごと): last night's events
  • 昨夜(さくや)から: since last night
  • 昨夜(さくや)遅(おそ)く: late last night

SIMILAR TIME WORDS:

  • 昨日(きのう): yesterday
  • 今夜(こんや): tonight
  • 一昨夜(いっさくや): the night before last
  • 前夜(ぜんや): the previous night, the eve

REGISTER:
さくや is preferred in news reports, formal writing, and literature. In conversation, ゆうべ is more natural.
