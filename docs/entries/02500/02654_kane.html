鐘(かね)

かね
noun
bell, chime

Definitions

1. bell, chime
A large bell, especially those in temples or churches.

Examples

寺(てら)の鐘(かね)が鳴(な)る。
The temple bell rings.
除夜(じょや)の鐘(かね)を聞(き)く。
To listen to the New Year's Eve bell.
Traditional Japanese custom
鐘(かね)を突(つ)く。
To strike a bell.

Notes

鐘(かね) refers to large bells, especially temple bells.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 鐘(かね)が鳴(な)る (the bell rings)
  • 鐘(かね)を突(つ)く/撞(つ)く (to strike a bell)
  • 鐘(かね)の音(ね) (the sound of the bell)

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • 除夜(じょや)の鐘(かね) (New Year's Eve bell - rung 108 times)
  • Temple bells (梵鐘(ぼんしょう)) are important in Buddhism

HOMOPHONE:

  • 金(かね) (money) - different kanji

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鐘(かね): large bell (temple, church)
  • ベル: bell (small, electric)
  • 鈴(すず): small bell, jingle bell

Related Words

Contrast: 金(かね) (money)
