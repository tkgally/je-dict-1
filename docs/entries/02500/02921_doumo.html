どうも

どうも
adverb / interjection
thanks, somehow, very
1. thanks, thank you
A casual expression of gratitude.
どうも、久(ひさ)しぶり！
Hey, long time no see!
Sense 1: casual greeting/thanks
どうも、助(たす)かりました。
Thanks, that helped.
Sense 1: thanks
どうも、お待(ま)たせしました。
Sorry, thanks for waiting.
Sense 1: thanks/greeting
あ、どうも。
Oh, thanks.
Sense 1: casual thanks
どうも、お世話(せわ)になりました。
Thank you for everything.
Sense 1: thanks
2. somehow, for some reason
Indicates uncertainty about a reason.
どうも調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)い。
I somehow don't feel well.
どうもうまく(うまく)いかない。
Somehow it's not going well.
Sense 2: somehow
どうも彼(かれ)は嘘(うそ)を言(い)っているようだ。
It seems like he's lying for some reason.
Sense 2: somehow/it seems
どうも信(しん)じられない。
Somehow I can't believe it.
Sense 2: somehow
どうも気(き)になる。
It bothers me for some reason.
Sense 2: for some reason
3. very, quite
Used to emphasize an apology or greeting.
どうもありがとうございます。
Thank you very much.
どうもすみません。
I'm very sorry.
どうも失礼(しつれい)しました。
I'm very sorry for the rudeness.
Sense 3: very (emphasizer)
どうもご親切(しんせつ)に。
Thank you very much for your kindness.
Sense 3: very (emphasizer)
どうもお疲(つか)れ様(さま)でした。
Thank you very much for your hard work.
Sense 3: very (emphasizer)

どうも has multiple uses.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • どうも (casual thanks/greeting)
  • どうもありがとう (thank you very much)
  • どうもすみません (I'm very sorry)

USAGES:

  • As standalone greeting: どうも (Hi/Thanks)
  • Emphasizer: どうも + adjective
  • Uncertainty: どうも〜らしい (it seems that...)
