大好(だいす)き

だいすき
na-adjective
love, like very much
1. love, like very much
To have a strong liking for something or someone.
アイスクリームが大好(だいす)きです。
I love ice cream.
大好(だいす)きな映画(えいが)
A movie I love.
お母(かあ)さんが大好(だいす)き！
I love Mom!

大好(だいす)き is a na-adjective meaning 'love' or 'like very much' - an intensified form of 好(す)き.

FORMS:

  • 大好(だいす)きだ/です: I love (predicate)
  • 大好(だいす)きな + noun: beloved ~

INTENSITY SCALE:

  • 好(す)き: like
  • 大好(だいす)き: love, really like

OPPOSITES:

  • 嫌(きら)い: dislike
  • 大嫌(だいきら)い: hate, really dislike

PATTERNS:

  • Xが大好(だいす)き: I love X
  • 大好(だいす)きなX: X that I love

USAGE NOTES:
Used for things, activities, and people. When expressing romantic love, 愛(あい)している is stronger and more direct, while 大好(だいす)き is often used for affection in general.

COMMON USES:

  • 音楽(おんがく)が大好(だいす)き: I love music
  • 大好(だいす)きな人(ひと): a person I love/adore
