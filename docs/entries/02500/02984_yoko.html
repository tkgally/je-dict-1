横(よこ)

よこ
noun
side, beside, width

Definitions

1. side, beside
The area next to something.
2. width, horizontal
The horizontal dimension.

Examples

私(わたし)の横(よこ)に座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit beside me.
横(よこ)を向(む)く。
To face sideways.
横(よこ)に長(なが)い。
It's wide (long horizontally).

Notes

横(よこ) refers to the side or horizontal.

OPPOSITE: 縦(たて) (vertical, length)

COMMON USES:

  • 横(よこ)に並(なら)ぶ (to line up sideways)
  • 横(よこ)を見(み)る (to look to the side)
  • 横(よこ)書(が)き (horizontal writing)

POSITION:

  • 横(よこ)に (beside, to the side)
