決行(けっこう)

けっこう
noun / suru-verb
carrying out, going ahead with

Definitions

1. carrying out, execution, going ahead
Decisively proceeding with a plan despite obstacles or concerns.

Examples

計画(けいかく)を決行(けっこう)する。
To carry out the plan.
雨天(うてん)決行(けっこう)
Will be held rain or shine.
[Note: Common phrase for outdoor events]
強行(きょうこう)決行(けっこう)
Forcing through; pushing ahead.
反対(はんたい)の声(こえ)があったにもかかわらず、社長(しゃちょう)は新(あたら)しいプロジェクトの決行(けっこう)を決(き)めた。
Despite voices of opposition, the president decided to go ahead with the new project.

Notes

決行(けっこう) means proceeding resolutely with something.

COMMON USES:

  • 雨天(うてん)決行(けっこう) (rain or shine)
  • 計画(けいかく)を決行(けっこう)する (execute a plan)
  • 強行(きょうこう)決行(けっこう) (force through)

NOTE: Different from 結構 (けっこう - quite, fine, enough).
