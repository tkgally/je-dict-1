後者(こうしゃ)

こうしゃ
noun
the latter
See also: 校舎(こうしゃ) (school building)
1. the latter, the second one
The second of two things or people mentioned.
前者(ぜんしゃ)と後者(こうしゃ)
The former and the latter.
私(わたし)は後者(こうしゃ)を選(えら)ぶ。
I choose the latter.
後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)がいい。
The latter is better.
AとBの二(ふた)つの案(あん)があるが、費用(ひよう)の面(めん)から考(かんが)えると後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)が現実的(げんじつてき)だ。
There are two proposals, A and B, but considering cost, the latter is more realistic.
速度(そくど)と安全性(あんぜんせい)のどちらを重視(じゅうし)するかと聞(き)かれ、後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)が大切(たいせつ)だと答(こた)えた。
When asked which I value more, speed or safety, I answered that the latter is more important.

後者(こうしゃ) means "the latter" when referring to the second of two items previously mentioned.

REGISTER:
Formal/written style. More common in academic writing, business documents, and formal speech.

USAGE PATTERN:
前者(ぜんしゃ)は〜、後者(こうしゃ)は〜 (The former is ~, the latter is ~)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)が: the latter is more...
  • 後者(こうしゃ)を選(えら)ぶ: to choose the latter
  • 後者(こうしゃ)の場合(ばあい): in the case of the latter

ANTONYM:
前者(ぜんしゃ) (the former, the first one)

NOTE:
Only used when exactly two items are being compared. For more than two items, use 最後(さいご)の or ordinal numbers.

Related Words

Antonym: 前者(ぜんしゃ) (the former)
Homophone: 降車(こうしゃ) (getting off (a vehicle))
