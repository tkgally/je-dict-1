器用(きよう)

きよう
na-adjective
skillful, dexterous, handy

Definitions

1. skillful, dexterous, handy
Having skill in using one's hands or managing practical tasks.
2. clever, versatile
Able to handle many different things well.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は手先(てさき)が器用(きよう)だ。
She is good with her hands.
器用(きよう)に折(お)り紙(がみ)を作(つく)る。
To make origami skillfully.
彼(かれ)は何(なん)でも器用(きよう)にこなす。
He handles everything skillfully.

Notes

器用(きよう) describes skill with hands or general capability.

FORMS:

  • 器用(きよう)な (attributive form)
  • 器用(きよう)に (adverb: skillfully)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手先(てさき)が器用(きよう) (good with one's hands)
  • 器用(きよう)にこなす (to handle skillfully)

ANTONYM:

  • 不器用(ぶきよう) (clumsy, unskillful)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 器用(きよう): handy, dexterous (hands)
  • 上手(じょうず): good at, skilled
  • 巧(たく)み: skillful (craftsmanship)

Related Words

Antonym: 不器用(ぶきよう) (clumsy)
