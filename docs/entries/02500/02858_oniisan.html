お兄(にい)さん

おにいさん
noun
older brother (polite)
1. older brother (polite)
A polite way to refer to an older brother.
お兄(にい)さんは何歳(なんさい)ですか。
How old is your older brother?
お兄(にい)さんに聞(き)いてみる。
I'll ask my older brother.
お兄(にい)さんは会社員(かいしゃいん)です。
My older brother is an office worker.
Sense 1: older brother
お兄(にい)さんは大学(だいがく)を卒業(そつぎょう)した。
My older brother graduated from university.
Sense 1: older brother
お兄(にい)さんと仲(なか)がいい。
I get along well with my older brother.
Sense 1: older brother
お兄(にい)さんに似(に)ていますね。
You look like your older brother.
Sense 1: older brother
2. young man
Used to address a young man, similar to 'mister' in English.
お兄(にい)さん、道(みち)を教(おし)えてください。
Excuse me, young man, could you tell me the way?
お兄(にい)さん、手伝(てつだ)ってくれませんか。
Excuse me, young man, could you help me?
Sense 2: young man (addressing)
あのお兄(にい)さんに聞(き)いてみよう。
Let's ask that young man.
Sense 2: young man
お兄(にい)さん、これを落(お)としましたよ。
Excuse me, young man, you dropped this.
Sense 2: young man (addressing)

お兄(にい)さん is the polite term for older brother.

FAMILY TERMS:

  • お兄(にい)さん: older brother (polite)
  • 兄(あに): my older brother (humble)
  • お兄(にい)ちゃん: older brother (casual/affectionate)

ALSO USED:

  • To address a young man (like 'mister')
