さあ

さあ
interjection
well, come on, let's see
1. well, come on, now then
Used to encourage action or get started.
さあ、始(はじ)めましょう。
Well, let's begin.
さあ、行(い)こう！
Come on, let's go!
さあ、頑張(がんば)って！
Come on, do your best!
Sense 1: encouragement
さあ、食(た)べよう！
Now then, let's eat!
Sense 1: starting action
さあ、起(お)きて！
Come on, wake up!
Sense 1: encouragement
2. well, let's see, I wonder
Used when uncertain or thinking.
さあ、わかりません。
Well, I don't know.
「いつ届(とど)く？」「さあ...」
"When will it arrive?" "Well..."
Sense 2: uncertainty
さあ、どうでしょうね。
Well, I wonder about that.
Sense 2: uncertainty
「本当(ほんとう)？」「さあ...」
"Really?" "Well..."
Sense 2: uncertainty
さあ、どこに行(い)ったかな。
Well, I wonder where it went.
Sense 2: wondering

さあ is a versatile interjection.

USAGES:
1. Encouragement: さあ、頑張(がんば)って！(Come on, do your best!)
2. Uncertainty: 「いつ？」「さあ...」("When?" "Well...")
3. Starting something: さあ、食(た)べよう (Now, let's eat)

SIMILAR:

  • じゃあ (well then - transitional)
  • では (well then - more formal)
