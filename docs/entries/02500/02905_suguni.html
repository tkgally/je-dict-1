すぐに

すぐに
adverb
immediately, right away

Definitions

1. immediately, right away, at once
Without delay or very soon.

Examples

すぐに来(き)てください。
Please come right away.
電話(でんわ)があったら、すぐに知(し)らせてください。
If there's a phone call, please let me know immediately.
駅(えき)はすぐに見(み)つかった。
I found the station right away.

Notes

すぐに means immediately.

VARIANT:

  • すぐ (same meaning, can be used without に)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 直(ただ)ちに (immediately - more formal)
  • 今(いま)すぐ (right now)
  • もうすぐ (soon, shortly)

PATTERNS:

  • すぐに + verb (do ~ immediately)
  • すぐそこ (right there, nearby)
