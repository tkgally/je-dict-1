決(き)まり

きまり
noun
rule, settlement
1. rule, regulation, custom
An established practice or guideline that should be followed.
学校(がっこう)の決(き)まりを守(まも)りなさい。
Follow the school rules.
2. settlement, conclusion, decision
The state of something being decided or settled.
決(き)まりが悪(わる)い。
I feel embarrassed/awkward.
Common idiom expressing embarrassment
もう決(き)まりですか。
Has it been decided yet?

決(き)まり is a noun meaning rule or settlement. It comes from the verb 決(き)まる (to be decided).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 決(き)まりを守(まも)る (to follow rules)
  • 決(き)まりが悪(わる)い (to feel embarrassed/awkward)
  • 決(き)まり文句(もんく) (set phrase, cliché)

IDIOM:

  • 決(き)まりが悪(わる)い: feeling of embarrassment or awkwardness
  • Example: 遅刻(ちこく)して決(き)まりが悪(わる)かった (I felt embarrassed for being late)

RELATED VERB:

  • 決(き)まる (to be decided)
  • 決(き)める (to decide)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ルール (rule - loanword)
  • 規則(きそく) (regulation - more formal)
