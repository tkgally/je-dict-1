あっち

あっち
pronoun
over there, that way
See also: こっち (here/this way), そっち (there/that way), どっち (which way)
1. over there, that way
Casual form indicating a direction or place away from both speaker and listener.
あっちに行(い)ってください。
Please go over there.
あっちの方(ほう)が安(やす)い。
That one over there is cheaper.
トイレはあっちです。
The toilet is over there.
あっちの店(みせ)のほうがいいと思(おも)う。
I think the shop over there is better.
あっちに大(おお)きい公園(こうえん)があるから、そこで遊(あそ)ぼう。
There's a big park over there, so let's play there.

あっち is a casual pronoun meaning "over there" - referring to a place away from both speaker and listener.

KO-SO-A-DO SERIES (〜っち forms):

  • こっち: this way (near speaker)
  • そっち: that way (near listener)
  • あっち: over there (away from both)
  • どっち: which way (question)

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • あっち: casual/informal
  • あちら: polite/formal

USES:
1. Direction: あっちに行(い)って (Go over there)
2. Person/thing: あっちの人(ひと) (that person over there)
3. Comparison: あっちの方(ほう)がいい (that one is better)

NOTE:
In casual speech, can also refer vaguely to "the other side" or "them."
