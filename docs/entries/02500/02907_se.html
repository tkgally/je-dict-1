背(せ)

せ
noun
height, stature, back
1. height, stature
How tall a person is.
2. back (of body)
The rear part of the body.
背(せ)が高(たか)いですね。
You're tall, aren't you?
背(せ)が低(ひく)い。
To be short (in height).
背(せ)を伸(の)ばす。
To straighten one's back.

背(せ) relates to height and back.

COMMON PHRASES:

  • 背(せ)が高(たか)い (tall)
  • 背(せ)が低(ひく)い (short)
  • 背(せ)が伸(の)びる (to grow taller)
  • 背中(せなか) (back of body)

RELATED:

  • 身長(しんちょう) (height - measurement)
  • 背丈(せたけ) (stature)
