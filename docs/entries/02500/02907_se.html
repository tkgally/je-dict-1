背(せ)

せ
noun
height, stature, back
1. height, stature
How tall a person is.
背(せ)が高(たか)いですね。
You're tall, aren't you?
背(せ)が低(ひく)い。
To be short (in height).
背(せ)が伸(の)びた。
I grew taller.
Sense 1: height
兄(あに)より背(せ)が高(たか)い。
I'm taller than my older brother.
Sense 1: height
背(せ)の順(じゅん)に並(なら)ぶ。
Line up in order of height.
Sense 1: height
背(せ)はどのくらいですか。
How tall are you?
Sense 1: height
2. back (of body)
The rear part of the body.
背(せ)を伸(の)ばす。
To straighten one's back.
背中(せなか)が痛(いた)い。
My back hurts.
Sense 2: back
背(せ)を向(む)けた。
They turned their back.
Sense 2: back
背(せ)を押(お)してあげた。
I gave them a push (on their back).
Sense 2: back

背(せ) refers to height/stature or the back of the body.

HEIGHT EXPRESSIONS:

  • 背(せ)が高(たか)い: tall
  • 背(せ)が低(ひく)い: short
  • 背(せ)が伸(の)びる: to grow taller
  • 背(せ)の順(じゅん): in order of height

BACK EXPRESSIONS:

  • 背中(せなか): back (of body) - most common
  • 背(せ)を向(む)ける: to turn one's back
  • 背(せ)を伸(の)ばす: to straighten one's back
  • 背(せ)を押(お)す: to push from behind; to encourage

RELATED WORDS:

  • 身長(しんちょう): height (formal measurement)
  • 背丈(せたけ): stature, height
  • 背骨(せぼね): spine, backbone

IDIOM:
背(せ)に腹(はら)は代(か)えられない: necessity knows no law
