こっち

こっち
pronoun
this way, here
1. this way, this direction, here
Casual form indicating a direction or place near the speaker.
こっちに来(き)てください。
Please come this way.
こっちを見(み)て。
Look this way.
Sense 1: this direction
こっちへ来(き)て。
Come here.
Sense 1: here
こっちに座(すわ)って。
Sit over here.
Sense 1: here
こっちの道(みち)を歩(ある)こう。
Let's walk this way.
Sense 1: this direction
2. this one, me/us
Can refer to the speaker's side or group.
こっちの方(ほう)がいい。
This one is better.
こっちは準備(じゅんび)ができた。
We're ready on our end.
こっちの話(はなし)を聞(き)いて。
Listen to what I have to say.
Sense 2: this side/me
こっちは大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)です。
Things are fine on our end.
Sense 2: our side
こっちにしよう。
Let's go with this one.
Sense 2: this one

こっち is a casual pronoun meaning "this way" or "here" - referring to a place near the speaker.

KO-SO-A-DO SERIES (〜っち forms):

  • こっち: this way (near speaker)
  • そっち: that way (near listener)
  • あっち: over there (away from both)
  • どっち: which way (question)

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • こっち: casual/informal
  • こちら: polite/formal

MEANINGS:
1. Direction/place: こっちに来(き)て (Come here)
2. This one: こっちにしよう (Let's go with this one)
3. Speaker's side: こっちは大丈夫(だいじょうぶ) (We're fine on our end)

NOTE:
こちら in formal speech can also mean "I" or introduce someone.
