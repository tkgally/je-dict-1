ええ

ええ
interjection
yes, yeah

Definitions

1. yes, yeah
An affirmative response, slightly softer than はい.

Examples

「日本語(にほんご)がわかりますか。」「ええ、少(すこ)し。」
"Do you understand Japanese?" "Yes, a little."
「明日(あした)来(き)ますか。」「ええ、行(い)きます。」
"Will you come tomorrow?" "Yes, I will."
ええ、そうですね。
Yes, that's right.

Notes

ええ is an affirmative interjection.

COMPARISON:

  • はい (yes - formal, polite)
  • ええ (yes - softer, conversational)
  • うん (yeah - casual)

USAGE: Common in everyday conversation, slightly less formal than はい but more polite than うん.
