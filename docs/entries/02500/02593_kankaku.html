感覚(かんかく)

かんかく
noun
sense, sensation, feeling
1. sense, sensation
The physical ability to perceive through the senses (sight, hearing, touch, etc.).
痛(いた)みの感覚(かんかく)がない。
I have no sensation of pain.
Sense 1: physical
2. feeling, intuition, sense
An intuitive understanding or awareness; a knack for something.
彼(かれ)は音楽(おんがく)の感覚(かんかく)がいい。
He has a good sense for music.
Sense 2: intuition
時間(じかん)の感覚(かんかく)を失(うしな)う。
To lose one's sense of time.

感覚(かんかく) refers to both physical senses and intuitive understanding.

THE FIVE SENSES:

  • 視覚(しかく) (vision)
  • 聴覚(ちょうかく) (hearing)
  • 触覚(しょっかく) (touch)
  • 味覚(みかく) (taste)
  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく) (smell)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 感覚(かんかく)がいい/するどい (have good sense, be perceptive)
  • 感覚(かんかく)が鈍(にぶ)い (have dull senses)
  • 〜の感覚(かんかく) (sense of ~)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 感覚(かんかく)器官(きかん) (sensory organ)
  • 感覚(かんかく)的(てき) (sensory, intuitive)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 間隔(かんかく) (interval, space) - different meaning
