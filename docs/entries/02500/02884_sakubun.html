1. essay, composition
A written piece, typically for school assignments.
日本語(にほんご)で作文(さくぶん)を書(か)きました。
I wrote an essay in Japanese.
作文(さくぶん)の宿題(しゅくだい)があります。
I have an essay homework assignment.
夏休(なつやす)みの思(おも)い出(で)について作文(さくぶん)する。
To write an essay about summer vacation memories.

作文(さくぶん) refers to writing a composition or essay, particularly in a school setting. It combines 作(さく) (make) + 文(ぶん) (writing).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 作文(さくぶん)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 作文(さくぶん)を書(か)く: to write an essay
  • 作文(さくぶん)する: to compose, to write
  • 作文(さくぶん)用紙(ようし): essay writing paper (the ruled paper used in Japanese schools, typically 原稿(げんこう)用紙(ようし))

CONTEXT:
In Japanese schools, 作文(さくぶん) is a standard assignment where students write about topics like summer vacation, school events, or personal reflections. It is associated with elementary and middle school education.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 文章(ぶんしょう): text, writing (general term for written prose)
  • 論文(ろんぶん): thesis, academic paper (formal, scholarly)
  • 小論文(しょうろんぶん): short essay (often for entrance exams)
  • 感想文(かんそうぶん): book report, reflection essay
