小屋(こや)

こや
noun
hut, cabin, shed
1. hut, cabin, shed
A small, simple building.
山(やま)の小屋(こや)に泊(と)まった。
I stayed at a mountain hut.
物置(ものおき)小屋(こや)を建(た)てた。
I built a storage shed.
2. pen, coop
An enclosure for animals.
鶏(にわとり)小屋(ごや)から卵(たまご)を取(と)ってきた。
I brought eggs from the chicken coop.
[Note: Reading changes to ごや in compounds]

小屋(こや) is a small building or shelter.

TYPES OF SMALL BUILDINGS:

  • 山(やま)小屋(ごや) (mountain hut)
  • 物置(ものおき)小屋(こや) (storage shed)
  • 犬(いぬ)小屋(ごや) (doghouse)
  • 鶏(にわとり)小屋(ごや) (chicken coop)
  • 掘(ほ)っ立(た)て小屋(ごや) (shack, shanty)

NOTE ON READING:

  • Standalone: こや
  • In compounds: often ごや (rendaku)

HISTORICAL USE:

  • 芝居(しばい)小屋(ごや) (theater, playhouse - historical)
  • 見世物(みせもの)小屋(ごや) (sideshow tent - historical)
