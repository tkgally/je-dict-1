晴(は)れ

はれ
noun
clear weather, sunny
1. clear weather, sunny weather
Weather condition with clear skies.
明日(あした)は晴(は)れでしょう。
Tomorrow will be sunny.
晴(は)れの日(ひ)が続(つづ)く。
Sunny days continue.
天気(てんき)は晴(は)れです。
The weather is clear.
週末(しゅうまつ)は晴(は)れたら、公園(こうえん)に行(い)きましょう。
If it's sunny this weekend, let's go to the park.
今朝(けさ)は曇(くも)りだったけど、午後(ごご)から晴(は)れてきて気持(きも)ちがいい。
It was cloudy this morning, but it cleared up in the afternoon and feels nice.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

晴(は)れ is the standard word for clear weather in forecasts and in everyday conversation. As a forecast term it is combined with other conditions in a fixed order:

  • 晴(は)れのち曇(くも)り: sunny, later cloudy
  • 晴(は)れ時々(ときどき)曇(くも)り: sunny with occasional clouds
  • 晴(は)れ一時(いちじ)雨(あめ): sunny with brief rain

のち marks what follows, 時々(ときどき) a condition recurring through the day, and 一時(いちじ) a single short spell.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 晴(は)れ着(ぎ): formal or best clothes
  • 晴(は)れの舞台(ぶたい): a grand occasion, a moment in the spotlight

These preserve an older sense of 晴(は)れ meaning 'formal, public, ceremonial' — the opposite of ordinary everyday life. A learner meets this sense only in set phrases; the weather sense is the live one.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 晴(は)れる: the verb, to clear up
  • 快晴(かいせい): perfectly clear, stronger than 晴(は)れ and mostly written
  • 曇(くも)り: cloudy, the opposite condition

Related Words

Antonym: 曇(くも)り (cloudy)
Related: 晴(は)れる (to clear up (verb))
Related: 快晴(かいせい) (perfectly clear weather)
