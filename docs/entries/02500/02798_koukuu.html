International airfares are rising due to soaring fuel costs.

航空(こうくう) means aviation or air travel. It functions primarily as a prefix in compound words rather than standing alone. Composed of 航(こう) (navigate) + 空(くう) (sky).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 航空(こうくう)会社(がいしゃ): airline
  • 航空(こうくう)券(けん): airline ticket
  • 航空(こうくう)便(びん): airmail, flight service
  • 航空(こうくう)機(き): aircraft
  • 航空(こうくう)運賃(うんちん): airfare
  • 航空(こうくう)自衛隊(じえいたい): Air Self-Defense Force

RELATED WORDS:

  • 空港(くうこう): airport
  • 飛行機(ひこうき): airplane
  • 飛行(ひこう): flight

USAGE:
Japanese airline names typically end in 航空(こうくう): 日本(にほん)航空(こうくう) (JAL), 全日本(ぜんにほん)空輸(くうゆ) (ANA, often abbreviated).
