株(かぶ)

かぶ
noun
stock, shares; stump (of tree)
1. stock, shares
Financial securities representing ownership in a company.
株(かぶ)を買(か)う。
To buy stocks.
Sense 1: financial
株価(かぶか)が上(あ)がった。
The stock price went up.
2. stump, root (of plant)
The remaining part of a tree or plant after cutting; a plant with roots.
イチゴを3株(かぶ)植(う)えた。
I planted three strawberry plants.
Sense 2: plants

株(かぶ) has two main meanings: financial stocks and plant stumps/plants.

SENSE 1 - STOCKS:

  • 株(かぶ)を買(か)う/売(う)る (to buy/sell stocks)
  • 株(かぶ)に投資(とうし)する (to invest in stocks)
  • 株(かぶ)で儲(もう)ける (to profit from stocks)

COMPOUND WORDS (FINANCIAL):

  • 株価(かぶか) (stock price)
  • 株式(かぶしき) (stock, shares - formal)
  • 株式(かぶしき)会社(がいしゃ) (stock company, corporation)
  • 株主(かぶぬし) (stockholder, shareholder)

SENSE 2 - PLANTS:

  • Counter for plants: 〜株(かぶ)
  • 切(き)り株(かぶ) (tree stump)

IDIOMATIC:

  • 株(かぶ)が上(あ)がる (one's reputation rises)
  • 株(かぶ)が下(さ)がる (one's reputation falls)
