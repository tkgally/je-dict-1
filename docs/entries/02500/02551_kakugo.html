覚悟(かくご)

かくご
noun / suru-verb
resolution, readiness
1. resolution, preparedness, readiness
Mental preparation for something difficult or inevitable; being mentally ready to face consequences.
覚悟(かくご)を決(き)めて挑戦(ちょうせん)する。
To challenge with determination.
覚悟(かくご)を決(き)める is a common expression
死(し)ぬ覚悟(かくご)で戦(たたか)った。
They fought prepared to die.
2. resignation, acceptance
Acceptance of an unavoidable situation; being prepared for the worst.
最悪(さいあく)の事態(じたい)を覚悟(かくご)している。
I'm prepared for the worst-case scenario.

覚悟(かくご) means resolution or mental preparedness, especially for something difficult or unavoidable.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 覚悟(かくご)する (to be prepared, to resolve oneself)
  • 覚悟(かくご)を決(き)める (to make up one's mind, to be determined)
  • 覚悟(かくご)ができている (to be ready/prepared)
  • 〜の覚悟(かくご)で (with the resolution to ~)

USAGE:

  • Often implies readiness for sacrifice, difficulty, or consequences
  • Can express determination or acceptance of fate
  • Common in dramatic or serious contexts

FIXED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 覚悟(かくご)しろ！(Prepare yourself! - threat or warning)
  • それなりの覚悟(かくご)が必要(ひつよう) (requires a certain level of commitment)
