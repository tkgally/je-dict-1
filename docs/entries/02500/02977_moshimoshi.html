もしもし

もしもし
interjection
hello (on phone)
1. hello (on the phone)
A greeting used when answering or making phone calls.
もしもし、田中(たなか)です。
Hello, this is Tanaka.
もしもし、聞(き)こえますか。
Hello, can you hear me?
「もしもし」「はい、山田(やまだ)です。」
"Hello?" "Yes, this is Yamada."
Sense 1: phone greeting
もしもし、鈴木(すずき)さんのお宅(たく)ですか。
Hello, is this the Suzuki residence?
Sense 1: phone greeting
もしもし、電話(でんわ)が切(き)れそうです。
Hello? The call is about to drop.
Sense 1: phone (checking connection)
もしもし、そちらは何(なん)の会社(かいしゃ)ですか。
Hello, what company is this?
Sense 1: phone greeting
2. excuse me (to get attention)
Used to get someone's attention.
もしもし、すみません。
Excuse me (to get attention).
もしもし、落(お)としましたよ。
Excuse me, you dropped something.
Sense 2: getting attention
もしもし、そこの方(かた)。
Excuse me, you over there.
Sense 2: getting attention
もしもし、忘(わす)れ物(もの)ですよ。
Excuse me, you forgot something.
Sense 2: getting attention

もしもし is primarily used on the phone.

USAGE:

  • Answering the phone
  • Checking if the connection is working
  • Getting someone's attention (less common)

NOTE: In business contexts, one typically states their company name first rather than just saying もしもし.
