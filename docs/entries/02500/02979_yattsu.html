八(やっ)つ

やっつ
noun
eight (things)
1. eight (things)
The number eight when counting things using native Japanese numbers.
みかんを八(やっ)つ買(か)いました。
I bought eight mandarin oranges.
八(やっ)つに切(き)る。
To cut into eight pieces.
八(やっ)つ残(のこ)っています。
Eight are remaining.

八(やっ)つ is the native Japanese number for counting eight general items.

NATIVE COUNTING SYSTEM (1-10):

  • 一(ひと)つ (1), 二(ふた)つ (2), 三(みっ)つ (3), 四(よっ)つ (4), 五(いつ)つ (5)
  • 六(むっ)つ (6), 七(なな)つ (7), 八(やっ)つ (8), 九(ここの)つ (9), 十(とお) (10)

WHEN TO USE:

  • General objects without specific counters
  • Age up to 9: 八(やっ)つ = 8 years old
  • Abstract things: 問題(もんだい)が八(やっ)つ (eight problems)

CHINESE VS. NATIVE:

  • 八(はち): Sino-Japanese (used with counters)
  • 八(やっ)つ: Native Japanese (standalone counting)

EXAMPLE CONTRAST:

  • みかんを八(やっ)つ: eight oranges (native)
  • みかんを八(はち)個(こ): eight oranges (Sino-Japanese)
