煙(けむり)

けむり
noun
smoke, fumes
1. smoke, fumes
The visible vapor released from burning material.
タバコの煙(けむり)
Cigarette smoke.
煙(けむり)で目(め)が痛(いた)い。
My eyes hurt from the smoke.
煙(けむり)が立(た)ち上(のぼ)っている。
Smoke is rising.
工場(こうじょう)から煙(けむり)が出(で)ていた。
Smoke was coming from the factory.
火事(かじ)の煙(けむり)が空(そら)に広(ひろ)がり、遠(とお)くからでも見(み)えた。
The smoke from the fire spread across the sky and could be seen from far away.

煙(けむり) is the visible vapor produced by burning materials.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 煙(けむり)が出(で)る: smoke comes out
  • 煙(けむり)が立(た)ち上(のぼ)る: smoke rises
  • 煙(けむり)を吸(す)う: to inhale smoke
  • 煙(けむり)に巻(ま)く: to confuse, evade (idiom)

TYPES OF SMOKE:

  • タバコの煙(けむり): cigarette smoke
  • 排気(はいき)ガス: exhaust fumes
  • 煙霧(えんむ): smoky haze

RELATED WORDS:

  • 煙突(えんとつ): chimney
  • 煙(けむ)い: smoky (i-adjective)
  • 煙(けむ)たい: smoky; annoying (figurative)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 煙(けむり) can also be read as けむ in compounds like 煙(けむ)たがる (to find someone bothersome).
