観察(かんさつ)

かんさつ
noun / suru-verb
observation
1. observation, watching, study
The act of carefully watching or examining something to learn about it.
鳥(とり)を観察(かんさつ)するのが好(す)きだ。
I like observing birds.
観察(かんさつ)日記(にっき)をつける。
To keep an observation diary.
Common in school science classes
注意深(ちゅういぶか)く観察(かんさつ)してください。
Please observe carefully.

観察(かんさつ) means observation or the act of carefully watching something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 観察(かんさつ)する (to observe)
  • 観察(かんさつ)日記(にっき) (observation diary)
  • 観察(かんさつ)力(りょく) (power of observation)
  • 観察(かんさつ)眼(がん)/観察(かんさつ)眼(め) (observational eye)

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Science: 実験(じっけん)観察(かんさつ) (experimental observation)
  • Nature: 自然(しぜん)観察(かんさつ) (nature observation)
  • Medicine: 経過(けいか)観察(かんさつ) (progress monitoring)
  • Childcare: 保育(ほいく)観察(かんさつ) (childcare observation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 観察(かんさつ): careful, scientific observation
  • 見(み)る: general looking/watching
  • 監視(かんし): surveillance, monitoring
