会合(かいごう)

かいごう
noun / suru-verb
meeting, assembly, gathering
1. meeting, assembly, gathering
A formal or semi-formal gathering of people for discussion or a specific purpose.
今晩(こんばん)会合(かいごう)がある。
There's a meeting tonight.
定期(ていき)会合(かいごう)を開(ひら)く。
To hold a regular meeting.
会合(かいごう)に出席(しゅっせき)する。
To attend a meeting.

会合(かいごう) refers to gatherings or meetings, often more formal than casual get-togethers.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 会合(かいごう)を開(ひら)く (to hold a meeting)
  • 会合(かいごう)に出席(しゅっせき)する (to attend a meeting)
  • 会合(かいごう)を設(もう)ける (to arrange a meeting)

TYPES:

  • 定期(ていき)会合(かいごう) (regular meeting)
  • 緊急(きんきゅう)会合(かいごう) (emergency meeting)
  • 秘密(ひみつ)会合(かいごう) (secret meeting)

NUANCE:

  • More formal than 集(あつ)まり (gathering)
  • Less formal than 会議(かいぎ) (conference)
  • Often implies a purpose or agenda

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会議(かいぎ): meeting, conference (business)
  • 集会(しゅうかい): assembly, gathering
  • 集(あつ)まり: gathering (casual)
