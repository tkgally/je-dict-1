1. to be crowded, to be congested
For a place to be full of people or things; to be packed.
週末(しゅうまつ)は道(みち)が混(こ)む。
The roads get congested on weekends.
電車(でんしゃ)がとても混(こ)んでいる。
The train is very crowded.
このレストランはいつも混(こ)んでいる。
This restaurant is always crowded.

混(こ)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) describing a crowded state.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • No direct transitive pair
  • Pattern: [place]が混(こ)む

ASPECT (ている):

  • 混(こ)んでいる = is crowded (state, not action)
  • This is one of the verbs where ている indicates a state result

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)が混(こ)む (train is crowded)
  • 道(みち)が混(こ)む (road is congested)
  • 店(みせ)が混(こ)む (shop is crowded)

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 込(こ)み合(あ)う (to be packed together)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 空(す)く: to become empty (opposite)
  • 空(す)いている: not crowded (opposite state)

Related Words

Antonym: すく (to be empty)
