劇(げき)

げき
noun
drama, play

Definitions

1. drama, play
A theatrical performance or its script.

Examples

劇(げき)を見(み)に行(い)く。
To go see a play.
シェイクスピアの劇(げき)
A Shakespearean drama.
悲劇(ひげき)と喜劇(きげき)
Tragedy and comedy.

Notes

劇(げき) refers to theatrical drama.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 劇(げき)を演(えん)じる (to perform a play)
  • 劇(げき)を書(か)く (to write a play)

TYPES:

  • 悲劇(ひげき) (tragedy)
  • 喜劇(きげき) (comedy)
  • 歌劇(かげき) (opera)
  • 演劇(えんげき) (theatrical performance)
general
