故郷(こきょう)

こきょう
noun
hometown, birthplace
1. hometown, birthplace
The place where one was born or grew up.
故郷(こきょう)に帰(かえ)る。
To return to one's hometown.
故郷(こきょう)を離(はな)れて10年(ねん)になる。
It's been 10 years since I left my hometown.
故郷(こきょう)が懐(なつ)かしい。
I miss my hometown.

故郷(こきょう) is one's hometown.

ALTERNATE READINGS:

  • ふるさと (more poetic/emotional)
  • こきょう (more formal)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 故郷(こきょう)に帰(かえ)る (to return home)
  • 故郷(こきょう)を離(はな)れる (to leave one's hometown)
  • 第二(だいに)の故郷(こきょう) (second hometown)
