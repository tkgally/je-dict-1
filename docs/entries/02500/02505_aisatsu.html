挨拶(あいさつ)

あいさつ
noun
greeting, salutation
1. greeting, salutation
Words or actions used when meeting someone or on particular occasions.
2. address, speech
A formal speech or remarks, especially at ceremonies.
朝(あさ)の挨拶(あいさつ)をする。
I say good morning.
挨拶(あいさつ)もなしに帰(かえ)った。
They left without even saying goodbye.
社長(しゃちょう)の挨拶(あいさつ)があった。
There was a speech by the president.

Functions as a noun and with する as a verb (挨拶(あいさつ)する = to greet).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 挨拶(あいさつ)する (to greet)
  • 挨拶(あいさつ)を交(か)わす (exchange greetings)
  • ご挨拶(あいさつ) (greeting - polite)
  • 挨拶(あいさつ)回(まわ)り (making rounds to greet people)

COMMON GREETINGS:

  • おはようございます (good morning)
  • こんにちは (hello)
  • こんばんは (good evening)
  • さようなら (goodbye)
  • いただきます / ごちそうさま (meal greetings)
