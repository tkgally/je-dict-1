全部(ぜんぶ)

ぜんぶ
noun / adverb
all, entire, whole
1. all, entire, whole
The complete amount or every part of something.
全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べてください。
Please eat all of it.
宿題(しゅくだい)は全部(ぜんぶ)終(お)わりました。
I finished all of my homework.
全部(ぜんぶ)でいくらですか。
How much is it in total?
この本(ほん)を全部(ぜんぶ)読(よ)みました。
I read this entire book.
テストの問題(もんだい)は全部(ぜんぶ)答(こた)えることができた。
I was able to answer all the questions on the test.

全部(ぜんぶ) means 'all,' 'everything,' or 'the whole thing.' It functions as both a noun and an adverb.

USAGE AS NOUN:

  • 全部(ぜんぶ)ください: I'll take all of it
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)の本(ほん): all the books

USAGE AS ADVERB:

  • 全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べた: I ate all of it
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)終(お)わった: It's all finished

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 全部(ぜんぶ)で〜: ~ in total, altogether
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)で千(せん)円(えん): 1000 yen in total

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全(すべ)て: all, everything (slightly more formal)
  • みんな: everyone, all (for people/casual)
  • 全員(ぜんいん): all members, everyone (for groups)
  • 全体(ぜんたい): the whole, entirety
  • 合計(ごうけい): total (numerical sum)

NOTE:
全部(ぜんぶ) is used for things, while みんな is often used for people.

Related Words

Synonym: 全(すべ)て (all (slightly formal))
Antonym: 一部(いちぶ) (part)
