苦(くる)しい

くるしい
i-adjective
painful, difficult, hard
1. painful, agonizing
Causing physical or emotional pain or discomfort.
息(いき)が苦(くる)しい。
I'm having trouble breathing.
2. difficult, hard, tight
Being in a difficult situation, especially financially.
生活(せいかつ)が苦(くる)しい。
Life is hard (financially).
3. strained, far-fetched
Unconvincing or hard to justify.
その言(い)い訳(わけ)は苦(くる)しい。
That excuse is unconvincing.

苦(くる)しい expresses pain, difficulty, or distress.

FORMS:

  • 苦(くる)しい (dictionary form)
  • 苦(くる)しく (adverb: painfully)
  • 苦(くる)しさ (noun: pain, difficulty)
  • 苦(くる)しがる (to show signs of pain)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 息(いき)が苦(くる)しい (hard to breathe)
  • 生活(せいかつ)が苦(くる)しい (life is hard financially)
  • 苦(くる)しい立場(たちば) (difficult position)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 苦(くる)しい: painful, distressing
  • 辛(つら)い: hard, tough (emotionally)
  • 痛(いた)い: painful (physical)
  • 難(むずか)しい: difficult (intellectually)

Related Words

Related: 辛(つら)い (tough, hard)
Related: 苦(くる)しむ (to suffer (verb))
