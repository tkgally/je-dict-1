ざっと

ざっと
adverb
roughly, approximately
1. roughly, approximately
Without precise details; in round numbers.
ざっと100人(にん)
Roughly 100 people.
ざっと見積(みつ)もって50万(まん)円(えん)くらいかかる。
Roughly estimated, it will cost about 500,000 yen.
ざっと計算(けいさん)しても、費用(ひよう)は予算(よさん)を超(こ)えている。
Even with a rough calculation, the cost exceeds the budget.
2. quickly, briefly
Looking at or doing something in a superficial manner.
ざっと目(め)を通(とお)す。
To skim through.
ざっと説明(せつめい)する。
To explain briefly.
書類(しょるい)にざっと目(め)を通(とお)したが、特(とく)に問題(もんだい)はなさそうだ。
I skimmed through the documents, and there don't seem to be any particular problems.

ざっと indicates doing something roughly, quickly, or approximately — without going into detail.

TWO MAIN USES:

1. APPROXIMATION:

  • ざっと百(ひゃく)人(にん): roughly a hundred people
  • ざっと三万円: approximately 30,000 yen
  • ざっと見積(みつ)もって: roughly estimating

2. QUICK/CURSORY ACTION:

  • ざっと見(み)る: to glance at, to skim
  • ざっと目(め)を通(とお)す: to skim through
  • ざっと説明(せつめい)する: to explain briefly
  • ざっと洗(あら)う: to rinse quickly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ざっと: roughly, quickly (cursory)
  • だいたい: roughly, approximately (more common)
  • おおよそ: approximately (slightly formal)

Related Words

Synonym: だいたい (roughly, approximately)
