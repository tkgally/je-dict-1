ざっと

ざっと
adverb
roughly, approximately
1. roughly, approximately
Without precise details; in round numbers.
ざっと100人(にん)
Roughly 100 people.
ざっと見積(みつ)もって50万(まん)円(えん)くらいかかる。
Roughly estimated, it will cost about 500,000 yen.
ざっと計算(けいさん)しても、費用(ひよう)は予算(よさん)を超(こ)えている。
Even with a rough calculation, the cost exceeds the budget.
2. quickly, briefly
Looking at or doing something in a superficial manner.
ざっと目(め)を通(とお)す。
To skim through.
ざっと説明(せつめい)する。
To explain briefly.
書類(しょるい)にざっと目(め)を通(とお)したが、特(とく)に問題(もんだい)はなさそうだ。
I skimmed through the documents, and there don't seem to be any particular problems.

ざっと indicates approximation or quick action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ざっと〜人(にん)/〜個(こ) (roughly ~ people/items)
  • ざっと見(み)る (to glance at)
  • ざっと目(め)を通(とお)す (to skim through)
  • ざっと説明(せつめい)する (to explain briefly)

SIMILAR:

  • ざっと: roughly (casual)
  • およそ: approximately (formal)
  • 大体(だいたい): about, roughly
