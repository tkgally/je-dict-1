万年筆(まんねんひつ)

まんねんひつ
noun
fountain pen
1. fountain pen
A pen with an internal reservoir of ink.
万年筆(まんねんひつ)で手紙(てがみ)を書(か)く。
To write a letter with a fountain pen.
これは高(たか)い万年筆(まんねんひつ)です。
This is an expensive fountain pen.
万年筆(まんねんひつ)にインクを入(い)れる。
To fill a fountain pen with ink.

万年筆(まんねんひつ) literally means "ten-thousand-year brush," implying durability.

ETYMOLOGY:

  • 万年(まんねん): ten thousand years (long-lasting)
  • 筆(ひつ): writing brush/pen

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 万年筆(まんねんひつ)で書(か)く: to write with a fountain pen
  • 万年筆(まんねんひつ)にインクを入(い)れる: to fill with ink
  • 万年筆(まんねんひつ)のペン先(さき): fountain pen nib

RELATED WRITING TOOLS:

  • ボールペン: ballpoint pen
  • 鉛筆(えんぴつ): pencil
  • シャープペンシル: mechanical pencil
  • 筆(ふで)ペン: brush pen

USAGE:
万年筆(まんねんひつ) is often used for formal documents, signatures, and as luxury gifts. High-quality Japanese brands like Pilot and Sailor are internationally renowned.
