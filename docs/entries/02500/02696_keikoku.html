1. warning, caution
An alert about potential danger or consequences.
医者(いしゃ)から警告(けいこく)を受(う)けた。
I received a warning from the doctor.
台風(たいふう)の警告(けいこく)が出(で)ている。
A typhoon warning has been issued.
何度(なんど)も警告(けいこく)されていたのに、彼(かれ)は聞(き)かなかった。
Despite being warned many times, he didn't listen.

A formal warning about danger or consequences. Used in official, medical, legal, and everyday contexts where serious attention is needed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 警告(けいこく)する: to warn
  • 警告(けいこく)を受(う)ける: to receive a warning
  • 警告(けいこく)を発(はっ)する: to issue a warning
  • 警告(けいこく)を無視(むし)する: to ignore a warning

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 警告(けいこく)文(ぶん): warning message
  • 警告(けいこく)灯(とう): warning light
  • 警告(けいこく)処分(しょぶん): formal reprimand (disciplinary)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注意(ちゅうい): caution, heads-up (lighter, more everyday)
  • 忠告(ちゅうこく): advice, counsel (personal, well-meaning)
  • 警報(けいほう): alarm, alert (systematic, e.g., weather alerts)
