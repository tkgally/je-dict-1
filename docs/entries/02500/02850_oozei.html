大勢(おおぜい)

おおぜい
noun / adverb
many people, crowd

Definitions

1. many people, large number of people
A crowd or large group of people.

Examples

大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと)が来(き)た。
Many people came.
大勢(おおぜい)で行(い)きましょう。
Let's go with a large group.
駅(えき)に大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと)がいた。
There were many people at the station.

Notes

大勢(おおぜい) refers specifically to many people.

USAGE:

  • 大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと) (many people)
  • 大勢(おおぜい)で (with many people)

NOTE: Only used for people, not things.
For things, use 多(おお)くの or たくさんの.
