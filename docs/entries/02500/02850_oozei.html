大勢(おおぜい)

おおぜい
noun / adverb
many people, crowd
1. many people, large number of people
A crowd or large group of people.
大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと)が来(き)た。
Many people came.
大勢(おおぜい)で行(い)きましょう。
Let's go with a large group.
駅(えき)に大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと)がいた。
There were many people at the station.

大勢(おおぜい) refers specifically to a large number of people.

IMPORTANT RESTRICTION:

大勢(おおぜい) can ONLY be used for people, never for objects or animals.

  • ✓ 大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと): many people
  • ✗ 大勢(おおぜい)の本(ほん): (incorrect - use 多(おお)くの本(ほん))
  • ✗ 大勢(おおぜい)の猫(ねこ): (incorrect - use たくさんの猫(ねこ))

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 大勢(おおぜい)の人(ひと): many people
  • 大勢(おおぜい)で: with a large group, in large numbers
  • 大勢(おおぜい)集(あつ)まる: to gather in large numbers
  • 大勢(おおぜい)の観客(かんきゃく): a large audience
  • 大勢(おおぜい)の参加者(さんかしゃ): many participants

ALTERNATIVES FOR 'MANY':

  • 多(おお)くの: many (general, formal)
  • たくさんの: many, lots of (general, casual)
  • 数多(かずおお)くの: numerous (formal)
  • 大量(たいりょう)の: large quantity (for things)

READING NOTE:
The reading is おおぜい, not だいせい. This is an irregular reading.
