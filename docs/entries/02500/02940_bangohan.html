晩(ばん)ご飯(はん)

ばんごはん
noun
dinner, evening meal

Definitions

1. dinner, evening meal, supper
The main meal eaten in the evening.

Examples

晩(ばん)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べましたか。
Did you eat dinner?
今日(きょう)の晩(ばん)ご飯(はん)は何(なん)ですか。
What's for dinner today?
晩(ばん)ご飯(はん)を作(つく)る。
To make dinner.

Notes

晩(ばん)ご飯(はん) is the evening meal.

MEALS:

  • 朝(あさ)ご飯(はん) (breakfast)
  • 昼(ひる)ご飯(はん) (lunch)
  • 晩(ばん)ご飯(はん) (dinner)

ALTERNATIVES:

  • 夕飯(ゆうはん) (dinner - slightly formal)
  • 夕食(ゆうしょく) (dinner - formal)
  • 夜(よる)ご飯(はん) (evening meal)

PATTERN: ～ご飯(はん) = meal time + 飯(はん) (cooked rice/meal)
