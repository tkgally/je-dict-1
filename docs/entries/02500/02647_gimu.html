義務(ぎむ)

ぎむ
noun
duty, obligation

Definitions

1. duty, obligation, responsibility
Something one is required to do by law, morality, or social expectation.

Examples

税金(ぜいきん)を払(はら)う義務(ぎむ)がある。
I have an obligation to pay taxes.
義務(ぎむ)教育(きょういく)は6歳(さい)から始(はじ)まる。
Compulsory education starts from age 6.
権利(けんり)と義務(ぎむ)
Rights and obligations.

Notes

義務(ぎむ) refers to legal, moral, or social duties.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 義務(ぎむ)がある (to have an obligation)
  • 義務(ぎむ)を果(は)たす (to fulfill one's duty)
  • 義務(ぎむ)を負(お)う (to bear an obligation)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 義務(ぎむ)教育(きょういく) (compulsory education)
  • 義務(ぎむ)化(か) (making mandatory)
  • 義務(ぎむ)違反(いはん) (breach of duty)

RELATED PAIR:

  • 権利(けんり)と義務(ぎむ) (rights and obligations)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 義務(ぎむ): duty (formal, legal)
  • 責任(せきにん): responsibility
  • 務(つと)め: duty (literary)

Related Words

Related: 権利(けんり) (rights)
