私(わたくし)

わたくし
pronoun
I (formal)

Definitions

1. I, myself (formal)
First-person pronoun used in formal situations.
2. private, personal
Relating to one's private affairs.

Examples

私(わたくし)は田中(たなか)と申(もう)します。
I am (called) Tanaka.
Very formal self-introduction
私(わたくし)の意見(いけん)を述(の)べます。
I will state my opinion.
私(わたくし)どもの会社(かいしゃ)です。
It is our company.
Humble expression

Notes

私(わたくし) is the formal form of "I."

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • 私(わたくし) (very formal)
  • 私(わたし) (polite/neutral)
  • 僕(ぼく) (male, casual)
  • 俺(おれ) (male, very casual)

USAGE: Used in business, formal speeches, and polite situations.
