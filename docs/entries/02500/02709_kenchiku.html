建築(けんちく)

けんちく
noun / suru-verb
architecture, construction
1. architecture
The art and science of designing buildings.
2. construction, building
The process of constructing buildings.
日本(にほん)の伝統的(でんとうてき)な建築(けんちく)
Traditional Japanese architecture.
建築(けんちく)家(か)になりたい。
I want to become an architect.
家(いえ)を建築(けんちく)する。
To construct a house.

建築(けんちく) covers architecture and building.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 建築(けんちく)する (to build)
  • 建築(けんちく)家(か) (architect)
  • 建築(けんちく)物(ぶつ) (building, structure)

RELATED:

  • 建築(けんちく)設計(せっけい) (architectural design)
  • 建築(けんちく)基準(きじゅん)法(ほう) (building standards law)
