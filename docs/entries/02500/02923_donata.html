どなた

どなた
pronoun
who (polite)

Definitions

1. who (polite)
A polite way to ask about someone's identity.

Examples

どなたですか。
Who is it? / May I ask who you are?
どなたかいらっしゃいますか。
Is anyone there?
こちらはどなたですか。
Who is this person?

Notes

どなた is the polite form of 誰(だれ).

FORMALITY:

  • 誰(だれ) (who - casual/neutral)
  • どなた (who - polite)

PATTERNS:

  • どなたですか (Who are you?)
  • どなたか (someone - polite)
  • どなたでも (anyone - polite)

USAGE: Used in polite speech, customer service, and formal situations.
