そうして

そうして
conjunction
and, and then
1. and, and then
Used to connect sentences or clauses, indicating continuation or sequence.
そうして、どうなりましたか。
And then, what happened?
勉強(べんきょう)して、そうして寝(ね)ました。
I studied, and then went to bed.
朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べて、そうして学校(がっこう)に行(い)きました。
I ate breakfast, and then went to school.
買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)って、そうして友達(ともだち)の家(いえ)に行(い)きました。
I went shopping, and then went to my friend's house.
朝(あさ)起(お)きて顔(かお)を洗(あら)って、そうして朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べて学校(がっこう)に行(い)きました。
I got up in the morning, washed my face, and then ate breakfast and went to school.

そうして is a conjunction meaning 'and then' or 'in that way,' connecting events or actions in sequence.

COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • そして: and, and then (most common, neutral)
  • そうして: and then (slightly emphatic, shows consequence)
  • それから: after that (time sequence)
  • それで: so, therefore (causal)
  • そこで: so then, at that point (transitional)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Can start a sentence or connect clauses
  • Often implies the second action follows naturally from the first
  • More formal/literary than そして in some contexts

ETYMOLOGY:
From そう (that way) + して (doing), literally 'doing so.'
