禁煙(きんえん)

きんえん
noun / suru-verb
no smoking; quitting smoking
1. no smoking, smoking prohibited
A rule or area where smoking is not allowed.
2. quitting smoking, giving up smoking
The act of stopping the habit of smoking.
この店(みせ)は禁煙(きんえん)です。
This restaurant is non-smoking.
禁煙(きんえん)席(せき)をお願(ねが)いします。
A non-smoking seat, please.
禁煙(きんえん)を始(はじ)めた。
I started quitting smoking.

禁煙(きんえん) has two meanings: no-smoking zones and the act of quitting smoking.

NO-SMOKING MEANING:

  • 禁煙(きんえん)席(せき) (non-smoking seat)
  • 禁煙(きんえん)車両(しゃりょう) (non-smoking car)
  • 全面(ぜんめん)禁煙(きんえん) (completely smoke-free)

QUITTING MEANING:

  • 禁煙(きんえん)する (to quit smoking)
  • 禁煙(きんえん)に成功(せいこう)する (to succeed in quitting)
  • 禁煙(きんえん)中(ちゅう) (in the process of quitting)

OPPOSITE:

  • 喫煙(きつえん) (smoking, smoking allowed)

Related Words

Antonym: 喫煙(きつえん) (smoking)
