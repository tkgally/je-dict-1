差(さ)

さ
noun / suffix
difference, gap, margin

Definitions

1. difference, gap
The amount by which things differ.
2. (suffix) degree of, extent of
Added to adjective stems to express degree.

Examples

年齢(ねんれい)の差(さ)は10歳(さい)だ。
The age difference is 10 years.
1点(てん)差(さ)で負(ま)けた。
We lost by one point.
貧富(ひんぷ)の差(さ)が広(ひろ)がっている。
The gap between rich and poor is widening.
この高(たか)さは何(なん)メートルですか。
How many meters is this height?
[Note: Suffix さ = degree of height]

Notes

差(さ) expresses difference or, as a suffix, degree.

AS 'DIFFERENCE':

  • 年齢(ねんれい)差(さ) (age difference)
  • 個人(こじん)差(さ) (individual differences)
  • 時間(じかん)差(さ) (time difference)
  • 価格(かかく)差(さ) (price difference)
  • 貧富(ひんぷ)の差(さ) (gap between rich and poor)

AS SUFFIX (〜さ):

  • 高(たか)さ (height - from 高(たか)い)
  • 広(ひろ)さ (width - from 広(ひろ)い)
  • 美(うつく)しさ (beauty - from 美(うつく)しい)
  • 難(むずか)しさ (difficulty - from 難(むずか)しい)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 差(さ)がある (there is a difference)
  • 差(さ)をつける (to make a difference)
  • 差(さ)が縮(ちぢ)まる (the gap narrows)
