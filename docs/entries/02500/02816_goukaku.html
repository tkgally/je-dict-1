合格(ごうかく)

ごうかく
noun / suru-verb
passing (an exam), success
1. passing, success
Successfully meeting the required standards, especially in an examination.
試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)する。
To pass an exam.
合格(ごうかく)発表(はっぴょう)
Announcement of successful candidates.
合格(ごうかく)おめでとう！
Congratulations on passing!
彼女(かのじょ)は難関(なんかん)大学(だいがく)に合格(ごうかく)し、家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)が喜(よろこ)んだ。
She passed the entrance exam for a prestigious university, and the whole family was overjoyed.

合格(ごうかく) means passing or meeting standards.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)する (pass an exam)
  • 合格(ごうかく)発表(はっぴょう) (results announcement)
  • 合格(ごうかく)点(てん) (passing score)
  • 合格(ごうかく)率(りつ) (pass rate)
  • 合格(ごうかく)者(しゃ) (successful candidate)

ANTONYM:

  • 不合格(ふごうかく) (failure, not passing)
