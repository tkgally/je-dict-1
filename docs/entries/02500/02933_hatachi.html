二十歳(はたち)

はたち
noun
twenty years old
1. twenty years old
The age of twenty.
今年(ことし)二十歳(はたち)になります。
I turn twenty this year.
二十歳(はたち)の誕生日(たんじょうび)
Twentieth birthday.
二十歳(はたち)から選挙権(せんきょけん)がある。
You have voting rights from age twenty.
二十歳(はたち)になったら、お酒(さけ)が飲(の)めるようになる。
When you turn twenty, you can legally drink alcohol.
二十歳(はたち)のとき成人式(せいじんしき)に出(で)て、友達(ともだち)とたくさん写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)った。
At age twenty, I attended the coming-of-age ceremony and took lots of photos with friends.

二十歳(はたち) is a special native Japanese reading for 'twenty years old.' It is an irregular reading — the regular Sino-Japanese form would be 二十(にじゅう)歳(さい).

SPECIAL AGE READINGS:
Japanese has very few special age words. 二十歳(はたち) is the most common. The regular counter pattern ～歳(さい) is used for all other ages.

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:

  • Traditional age of adulthood in Japan
  • 成人式(せいじんしき) (Coming of Age ceremony) is held for those turning 20
  • Note: The legal adult age changed to 18 in April 2022, but 成人式(せいじんしき) is often still celebrated at 20

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 二十歳(はたち)になる: to turn twenty
  • 二十歳(はたち)の記念(きねん): twentieth birthday commemoration
