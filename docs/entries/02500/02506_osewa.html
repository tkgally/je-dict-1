お世話(せわ)

おせわ
noun
care, help, assistance
1. care, looking after, help
Taking care of someone or helping with their needs.
2. assistance, favor (in set phrases)
Used in polite expressions to acknowledge help received.
いつもお世話(せわ)になっています。
Thank you for always taking care of me.
Very common polite expression
子供(こども)の世話(せわ)をする。
To take care of children.
Without お = more casual
お世話(せわ)になりました。
Thank you for your help.
Said when leaving or concluding

お世話(せわ) is the polite form; 世話(せわ) alone is neutral.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お世話(せわ)になる (receive someone's care/help)
  • お世話(せわ)になっております (business greeting: 'Thank you for your continued support')
  • お世話(せわ)になりました (Thank you for everything)
  • 世話(せわ)をする/をやく (to take care of, to look after)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 世話(せわ)が焼(や)ける (to be troublesome to deal with)
  • 世話(せわ)がない (be no trouble)
