夏休(なつやす)み

なつやすみ
noun
summer vacation

Definitions

1. summer vacation, summer holiday
The break from school or work during summer.

Examples

夏休(なつやす)みに海(うみ)に行(い)きます。
I'm going to the beach during summer vacation.
夏休(なつやす)みの宿題(しゅくだい)
Summer vacation homework.
夏休(なつやす)みはいつからですか。
When does summer vacation start?

Notes

夏休(なつやす)み is the summer break.

SEASONAL VACATIONS:

  • 春休(はるやす)み (spring break)
  • 夏休(なつやす)み (summer vacation)
  • 冬休(ふゆやす)み (winter break)

COMMON ACTIVITIES:

  • 旅行(りょこう) (travel)
  • 海水浴(かいすいよく) (swimming in the sea)
  • 宿題(しゅくだい) (homework)
  • 自由研究(じゆうけんきゅう) (independent study project)
