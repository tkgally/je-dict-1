中学生(ちゅうがくせい)

ちゅうがくせい
noun
middle school student, junior high school student
1. middle school student, junior high school student
A student attending 中学校, which covers grades 7-9 (ages 12-15) in the Japanese education system.
娘(むすめ)は中学生(ちゅうがくせい)です。
My daughter is a middle school student.
中学生(ちゅうがくせい)のとき、部活(ぶかつ)でテニスをしていた。
When I was in middle school, I played tennis in my school club.
この映画(えいが)は中学生(ちゅうがくせい)でも分(わ)かる内容(ないよう)です。
This movie has content that even a middle school student can understand.

FORMATION:
中学(ちゅうがく) (middle school, abbreviation of 中学校(ちゅうがっこう)) + 生(せい) (student). The suffix ～生(せい) is used for students at all levels: 小学生(しょうがくせい) (elementary), 高校生(こうこうせい) (high school), 大学生(だいがくせい) (university).

JAPANESE SCHOOL SYSTEM:

  • 小学校(しょうがっこう) — elementary school (6 years, ages 6-12)
  • 中学校(ちゅうがっこう) — middle school (3 years, ages 12-15)
  • 高校(こうこう) — high school (3 years, ages 15-18)

中学生(ちゅうがくせい) is often abbreviated to 中学(ちゅうがく) in casual speech: 中学(ちゅうがく)のとき (when I was in middle school).
