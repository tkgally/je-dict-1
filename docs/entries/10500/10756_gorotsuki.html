ごろつき

ごろつき
noun
hooligan, thug, rogue
1. hooligan, thug, rogue
A rough, disreputable person who causes trouble or lives by intimidation. Often used to describe street toughs or petty criminals.
ごろつきに絡(から)まれた。
I was hassled by a thug.
町(まち)のごろつきが公園(こうえん)にたむろしている。
Town hooligans are loitering in the park.
あの店(みせ)の前(まえ)にはごろつきがいるから、気(き)をつけたほうがいい。
There are thugs in front of that shop, so you should be careful.

The word ごろつき originally comes from ごろごろ (rolling around idly), referring to people who loaf around without proper work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ごろつきに絡(から)まれる: to be hassled by thugs
  • 町(まち)のごろつき: town ruffians
  • ごろつきのような男(おとこ): a thug-like man
