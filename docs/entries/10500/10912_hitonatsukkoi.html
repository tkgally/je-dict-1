人懐(ひとなつ)っこい

ひとなつっこい
adjective (i)
friendly, sociable, approachable
1. friendly, sociable, approachable
Describes a person or animal that naturally warms up to people and is easy to get along with. Implies an endearing, open quality.
人懐(ひとなつ)っこい笑顔(えがお)だ。
That's a warm, friendly smile.
あの犬(いぬ)は人懐(ひとなつ)っこくて、誰(だれ)にでも尻尾(しっぽ)を振(ふ)る。
That dog is so friendly that it wags its tail at everyone.
彼女(かのじょ)は人懐(ひとなつ)っこい性格(せいかく)で、初対面(しょたいめん)の人(ひと)ともすぐに仲良(なかよ)くなる。
She has such a sociable personality that she quickly becomes friends with people she meets for the first time.

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 人懐(ひとなつ)っこくない
  • Past: 人懐(ひとなつ)っこかった
  • Te-form: 人懐(ひとなつ)っこくて
  • Adverbial: 人懐(ひとなつ)っこく

ETYMOLOGY:
人(ひと) (person) + 懐(なつ)く (to become attached to) + っこい (adjective suffix indicating tendency). Literally describes someone who tends to warm up to people.

USAGE:
Always positive. Used for both people and animals, especially pets. Common collocations include 人懐(ひとなつ)っこい笑顔(えがお) (a warm smile) and 人懐(ひとなつ)っこい性格(せいかく) (a friendly personality).
