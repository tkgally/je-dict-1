筍(たけのこ)

たけのこ
noun
bamboo shoot
1. bamboo shoot
The young, edible shoot of a bamboo plant. A prized spring ingredient in Japanese cuisine.
筍(たけのこ)を掘(ほ)る。
To dig up bamboo shoots.
春(はる)になると筍(たけのこ)が旬(しゅん)を迎(むか)える。
Bamboo shoots come into season when spring arrives.
採(と)れたての筍(たけのこ)を米(こめ)ぬかで茹(ゆ)でてアクを抜(ぬ)いた。
I boiled the freshly harvested bamboo shoots with rice bran to remove the bitterness.

WRITING:
Usually written in hiragana as たけのこ or in kanji as 筍(たけのこ). The kanji 竹(たけ)の子(こ) (literally "child of bamboo") is also seen but less common than the single-character form.

USAGE:
筍(たけのこ) is a quintessential spring ingredient in Japan. Fresh shoots require preparation — they must be boiled with 米(こめ)ぬか (rice bran) or 米(こめ)のとぎ汁(じる) (rice washing water) to remove their astringent flavor.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 筍(たけのこ)ご飯(はん): bamboo shoot rice
  • 筍(たけのこ)の煮物(にもの): simmered bamboo shoots
  • 筍(たけのこ)掘(ほ)り: bamboo shoot digging
  • 筍(たけのこ)の土佐煮(とさに): bamboo shoots simmered in bonito-flavored broth
