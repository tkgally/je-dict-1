なぞらえる

なぞらえる
verb (ichidan)
to liken, to compare to
1. to liken, to compare to, to model after
To treat or represent one thing as similar to another, often drawing an analogy or metaphor.
人生(じんせい)を旅(たび)になぞらえる。
To liken life to a journey.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の境遇(きょうぐう)を小説(しょうせつ)の主人公(しゅじんこう)になぞらえた。
He compared his own situation to the protagonist of a novel.
この庭園(ていえん)は富士山(ふじさん)の景色(けしき)になぞらえて造(つく)られたと言(い)われている。
This garden is said to have been designed to evoke the scenery of Mt. Fuji.

CONJUGATION (ichidan):

  • Negative: なぞらえない
  • Te-form: なぞらえて
  • Past: なぞらえた

USAGE:
The pattern is AをBになぞらえる (to liken A to B). Often used in literary, analytical, or artistic contexts.

WRITING:
Sometimes written as 準(なぞら)える or 擬(なぞら)える, but hiragana is most common.

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal and literary. More common in written language and speeches than in casual conversation.
