仕草(しぐさ)

しぐさ
noun
gesture, mannerism, behavior
1. gesture, mannerism, behavior
A person's physical movements, gestures, or habitual actions, often observed as characteristic or expressive.
かわいい仕草(しぐさ)だ。
What a cute gesture.
赤(あか)ちゃんの仕草(しぐさ)を見(み)ていると癒(いや)される。
Watching a baby's gestures is soothing.
彼女(かのじょ)は髪(かみ)を触(さわ)る仕草(しぐさ)が癖(くせ)になっている。
She has a habit of touching her hair — it's become one of her mannerisms.

WRITING:
Also written as 仕種(しぐさ). Both kanji forms are used, but 仕草(しぐさ) is more common.

USAGE:
Often describes small, characteristic movements that reveal personality or emotion. Frequently modified by adjectives like かわいい (cute), 上品(じょうひん) (elegant), or 自然(しぜん) (natural). Common in descriptions of people, animals, and acting/performance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • かわいい仕草(しぐさ): cute gesture
  • 何気(なにげ)ない仕草(しぐさ): casual gesture, unintentional mannerism
  • 仕草(しぐさ)が似(に)ている: to have similar mannerisms
