便宜(べんぎ)

べんぎ
noun
convenience, accommodation
1. convenience, expediency
The quality of being convenient or practical. Often used in the phrase 便宜上(べんぎじょう) (for convenience's sake).
便宜上(べんぎじょう)、AとBに分(わ)ける。
For convenience, we divide them into A and B.
便宜上(べんぎじょう)、この名前(なまえ)を使(つか)う。
For the sake of convenience, we'll use this name.
説明(せつめい)の便宜上(べんぎじょう)、簡単(かんたん)な例(れい)を使(つか)って話(はな)します。
For the convenience of explanation, I'll speak using a simple example.
2. special accommodation, favorable treatment
Giving someone special treatment or making an exception for their benefit.
便宜(べんぎ)を図(はか)る。
To arrange special accommodation.
知人(ちじん)に便宜(べんぎ)を図(はか)った。
I arranged favorable treatment for an acquaintance.
公務員(こうむいん)が特定(とくてい)の業者(ぎょうしゃ)に便宜(べんぎ)を図(はか)ったとして問題(もんだい)になった。
It became a problem when a public servant was found to have given special treatment to a specific contractor.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 便宜上(べんぎじょう) (for convenience's sake — very common set phrase)
  • 便宜(べんぎ)を図(はか)る (to arrange accommodation/favorable treatment)
  • 便宜(べんぎ)を供与(きょうよ)する (to provide accommodation — formal/legal)

USAGE:
Sense 1 (便宜上(べんぎじょう)) is neutral and widely used in academic, business, and everyday contexts. Sense 2 (便宜(べんぎ)を図(はか)る) often appears in news about favoritism or corruption, though it can also be used innocently.

CAUTION:
Do not confuse with 便利(べんり) (convenient). 便宜(べんぎ) is more about expediency or special treatment, while 便利(べんり) describes general usefulness.
