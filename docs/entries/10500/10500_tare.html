タレ

たれ
noun
sauce, dipping sauce
1. sauce, dipping sauce, glaze
A seasoning liquid used for dipping, marinating, or glazing food. Covers a wide range of sauces in Japanese cuisine.
タレをつける。
To dip in sauce.
焼(や)き鳥(とり)はタレと塩(しお)、どちらにしますか。
For the yakitori, would you like sauce or salt?
この店(みせ)の秘伝(ひでん)のタレは何十年(なんじゅうねん)も継(つ)ぎ足(た)して使(つか)っているらしい。
Apparently this shop has been adding to and using the same secret sauce for decades.

WRITING:
Usually written in katakana as タレ, sometimes in hiragana as たれ. The kanji 垂(た)れ exists but is rarely used for this meaning.

USAGE:
タレ is a general term for dipping sauces and glazes in Japanese cooking. It is distinct from ソース (Western-style sauce) and 醤油(しょうゆ) (soy sauce).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焼肉(やきにく)のタレ: yakiniku sauce
  • 焼(や)き鳥(とり)のタレ: yakitori glaze
  • タレをかける: to pour sauce over
  • 秘伝(ひでん)のタレ: secret sauce recipe
