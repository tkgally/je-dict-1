1. to hang, to hook, to catch on
To hang something on a hook or peg, or for something to catch on a protruding object.
コートをフックに引(ひ)っ掛(か)ける。
To hang a coat on a hook.
袖(そで)を枝(えだ)に引(ひ)っ掛(か)けてしまった。
I caught my sleeve on a branch.
タオルを肩(かた)に引(ひ)っ掛(か)けたまま外(そと)に出(で)た。
I went outside with a towel draped over my shoulder.
2. to trick, to deceive, to trap
To use a trick or ruse to deceive someone, often with a trick question or a scam.
その手(て)には引(ひ)っ掛(か)からないよ。
I won't fall for that trick.
Uses the intransitive 引(ひ)っ掛(か)かる (to be tricked).
詐欺師(さぎし)に引(ひ)っ掛(か)けられた。
I was tricked by a con artist.
ひっかけ問題(もんだい)に気(き)をつけて。
Watch out for trick questions.
3. to splash, to spill on
To splash or spill liquid on someone or something.
水(みず)を引(ひ)っ掛(か)けられた。
Water was splashed on me.
通(とお)りがかりの車(くるま)に泥水(どろみず)を引(ひ)っ掛(か)けられた。
A passing car splashed muddy water on me.
怒(おこ)った彼女(かのじょ)は彼(かれ)の顔(かお)にワインを引(ひ)っ掛(か)けた。
The angry woman threw wine in his face.

引(ひ)っ掛(か)ける is an ichidan verb with three distinct meanings: (1) to hang/hook something, (2) to trick/deceive someone, and (3) to splash liquid on someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 引(ひ)っ掛(か)かる (intransitive — to get caught on; to be tricked; to fall for)

ASPECT (ている):
引(ひ)っ掛(か)けている typically describes a resulting state: コートをフックに引(ひ)っ掛(か)けている (a coat is hanging on a hook).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • フックに引(ひ)っ掛(か)ける: to hang on a hook
  • 罠(わな)に引(ひ)っ掛(か)ける: to lure into a trap
  • 水(みず)を引(ひ)っ掛(か)ける: to splash water on
  • ひっかけ問題(もんだい): trick question

RELATED:
The compound ひっかけ is used as a noun meaning "trick" or "trap," as in ひっかけ問題(もんだい) (trick question on a test).
