がらくた

がらくた
noun
junk, rubbish, odds and ends
1. junk, rubbish, odds and ends
Useless or worthless objects; miscellaneous items of little value. Often used for old, broken, or unwanted things cluttering a space.
がらくたを捨(す)てる。
To throw out junk.
押入(おしい)れの中(なか)はがらくたでいっぱいだ。
The closet is full of junk.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に、長年(ながねん)たまったがらくたを整理(せいり)しなければならない。
Before moving, I have to sort through the junk that has accumulated over the years.

USAGE:
がらくた refers to a collection of miscellaneous worthless items. It often implies clutter or hoarding. Unlike ごみ (garbage), which is waste to be disposed of, がらくた suggests items that someone has kept but are essentially useless.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • がらくたの山(やま): a pile of junk
  • がらくたを片付(かたづ)ける: to clean up junk
  • がらくた市(いち): flea market, junk sale
