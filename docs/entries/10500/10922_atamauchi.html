頭打(あたまう)ち

あたまうち
noun
hitting a ceiling, plateauing, peaking
1. hitting a ceiling, plateauing, peaking
A state where growth, increase, or improvement has reached its limit and cannot go higher. Used for numbers, performance, prices, and similar metrics.
売上(うりあげ)が頭打(あたまう)ちだ。
Sales have plateaued.
人口(じんこう)の増加(ぞうか)が頭打(あたまう)ちになっている。
Population growth has hit a ceiling.
この業界(ぎょうかい)は成長(せいちょう)が頭打(あたまう)ちで、新(あたら)しい戦略(せんりゃく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Growth in this industry has plateaued, and new strategies are needed.

KEY PATTERN:
頭打(あたまう)ちになる (to plateau, to hit a ceiling) is the most common usage pattern. Also used as a predicate: ～が頭打(あたまう)ちだ.

ETYMOLOGY:
頭(あたま) (head) + 打(う)ち (hitting). The image of hitting one's head on a ceiling above, unable to go higher.

USAGE:
Primarily used in business, economics, and data contexts. Common subjects include 売上(うりあげ) (sales), 成長(せいちょう) (growth), 株価(かぶか) (stock prices), and 人口(じんこう) (population).
