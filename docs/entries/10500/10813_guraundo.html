グラウンド

ぐらうんど
noun
playing field, sports ground
1. playing field, sports ground, athletic field
An open outdoor area used for sports and athletic activities, typically at schools or public facilities. In Japanese, グラウンド specifically refers to an unpaved or dirt playing field.
グラウンドで走(はし)る。
To run on the field.
雨(あめ)でグラウンドが使(つか)えない。
The field can't be used because of rain.
放課後(ほうかご)、グラウンドで野球部(やきゅうぶ)が練習(れんしゅう)している。
After school, the baseball team is practicing on the field.

USAGE:
グラウンド in Japanese refers specifically to an outdoor playing field, often unpaved (dirt or sand surface). It is the standard word for school athletic fields and sports grounds. It differs from the English "ground" in that it does not mean the earth or floor in general.

The abbreviation グランド is also sometimes used, and 校庭(こうてい) (school yard) overlaps in meaning for school contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • グラウンド整備(せいび): field maintenance
  • グラウンドを走(はし)る: to run on the field
  • 学校(がっこう)のグラウンド: school athletic field
  • グラウンドがぬかるむ(ぬかるむ): the field gets muddy
