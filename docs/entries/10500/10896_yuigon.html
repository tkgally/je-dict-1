遺言(ゆいごん)

ゆいごん
noun
will, testament
1. will, testament, last wishes
A written or spoken statement declaring how one's property and affairs should be handled after death, or final words left for the living.
遺言(ゆいごん)を書(か)く。
To write a will.
祖父(そふ)は遺言(ゆいごん)で土地(とち)を孫(まご)に残(のこ)した。
My grandfather left the land to his grandchild in his will.
遺言(ゆいごん)の内容(ないよう)をめぐって親族(しんぞく)の間(あいだ)で争(あらそ)いが起(お)きた。
A dispute arose among the relatives over the contents of the will.

WORD FORMATION:
遺(ゆい) (leave behind) + 言(ごん) (words) = words left behind.

READING NOTE:
Also read as いごん, especially in legal contexts. The reading ゆいごん is standard in everyday use.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 遺言(ゆいごん)書(しょ): written will, testament document
  • 遺言(ゆいごん)状(じょう): letter of testament
  • 遺言(ゆいごん)執行(しっこう)者(しゃ): executor of a will

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 遺言(ゆいごん)を書(か)く: to write a will
  • 遺言(ゆいごん)を残(のこ)す: to leave a will
  • 遺言(ゆいごん)に従(したが)う: to follow the will
  • 遺言(ゆいごん)を公開(こうかい)する: to disclose the will
