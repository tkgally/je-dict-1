言及(げんきゅう)

げんきゅう
noun / verb (suru)
mention, reference
1. mention, reference, allusion
The act of referring to or touching upon a topic in speech or writing.
その件(けん)には言及(げんきゅう)しなかった。
I didn't mention that matter.
大臣(だいじん)は会見(かいけん)で辞任(じにん)の可能性(かのうせい)に言及(げんきゅう)した。
The minister made reference to the possibility of resignation at the press conference.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)では環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)についても言及(げんきゅう)されている。
The report also makes reference to environmental issues.

WORD FORMATION:
言(げん) (say, words) + 及(きゅう) (reach, extend to) = to extend one's words to (a topic).

GRAMMAR:
Pattern: 〜に言及(げんきゅう)する (to make reference to ~). The topic being mentioned is marked with に.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜に言及(げんきゅう)する: to refer to, to mention
  • 言及(げんきゅう)を避(さ)ける: to avoid mentioning
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき)に言及(げんきゅう)する: to refer to specifically
  • 一切(いっさい)言及(げんきゅう)しない: to make no mention whatsoever

USAGE NOTE:
言及(げんきゅう) is formal and commonly found in news, academic, and official contexts. In everyday speech, 触(ふ)れる (to touch upon) or 話(はな)す (to talk about) are more natural.
