1. output, production, expression
The act of producing or expressing information, ideas, or results. Widely used in business and education to mean actively creating or communicating rather than passively receiving.
アウトプットが大事(だいじ)だ。
Output is important.
学(まな)んだことをアウトプットする習慣(しゅうかん)をつけよう。
Let's build a habit of outputting what we've learned.
インプットだけでなくアウトプットも意識(いしき)しないと語学(ごがく)は上達(じょうたつ)しない。
Language skills won't improve unless you focus on output as well as input.

USAGE:
アウトプット is commonly paired with インプット (input). In education and self-improvement contexts, the pair emphasizes the importance of actively producing (writing, speaking, creating) rather than only passively absorbing (reading, listening).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アウトプットする: to output, to produce
  • アウトプットの量(りょう): amount of output
  • アウトプットを増(ふ)やす: to increase output
