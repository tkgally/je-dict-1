溜(た)まり場(ば)

たまりば
noun
hangout, gathering place
1. hangout, gathering place, haunt
A place where people regularly gather or hang out. Can be positive (a favorite spot) or negative (a place where troublesome people congregate).
あのカフェは学生(がくせい)の溜(た)まり場(ば)だ。
That cafe is a student hangout.
公園(こうえん)が若者(わかもの)の溜(た)まり場(ば)になっている。
The park has become a gathering spot for young people.
駅前(えきまえ)のコンビニが不良(ふりょう)の溜(た)まり場(ば)になっていると近所(きんじょ)から苦情(くじょう)が出(で)た。
There were complaints from the neighborhood that the convenience store in front of the station had become a hangout for delinquents.

FORMATION:
溜(た)まる (to accumulate, to gather) + 場(ば) (place). Literally a "gathering place."

NUANCE:
Can be neutral or carry a slightly negative connotation depending on context. When referring to delinquents or undesirable groups, it suggests an unwelcome gathering spot.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 若者(わかもの)の溜(た)まり場(ば): youth hangout
  • 不良(ふりょう)の溜(た)まり場(ば): delinquent hangout
  • いつもの溜(た)まり場(ば): usual hangout spot
