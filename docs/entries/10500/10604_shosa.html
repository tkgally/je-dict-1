所作(しょさ)

しょさ
noun
demeanor, bearing, movements
1. demeanor, bearing, conduct, movements
The way a person carries themselves, their physical movements and gestures, especially as reflecting character, upbringing, or training.
美(うつく)しい所作(しょさ)。
Graceful bearing.
茶道(さどう)では所作(しょさ)の一(ひと)つ一(ひと)つに意味(いみ)がある。
In the tea ceremony, every movement has meaning.
彼女(かのじょ)の上品(じょうひん)な所作(しょさ)は育(そだ)ちの良(よ)さを感(かん)じさせる。
Her refined demeanor gives the impression of a good upbringing.

USAGE:
Often used in contexts involving traditional arts (茶道(さどう), 舞踊(ぶよう)), formal etiquette, and descriptions of someone's overall bearing. Carries a slightly elevated, literary tone compared to 動作(どうさ) (movement) or 仕草(しぐさ) (gesture).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美(うつく)しい所作(しょさ): graceful bearing
  • 上品(じょうひん)な所作(しょさ): refined demeanor
  • 所作(しょさ)が美(うつく)しい: to have beautiful bearing
  • 所作(しょさ)事(ごと): action, conduct (in kabuki/theater)

DISTINCTION:
所作(しょさ) emphasizes the overall quality or elegance of movement, while 仕草(しぐさ) refers to specific gestures, and 動作(どうさ) is a neutral term for physical movement.
