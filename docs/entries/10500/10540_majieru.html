1. to mix in, to intersperse, to include
To include or mix different elements together. Often used for mixing things like humor into speech, or different items into a group.
冗談(じょうだん)を交(ま)じえて話(はな)す。
To speak with humor mixed in.
日本語(にほんご)に英語(えいご)を交(ま)じえて説明(せつめい)した。
I explained it mixing English into my Japanese.
2. to exchange (words, blows)
To exchange words, glances, or blows with someone.
二人(ふたり)は言葉(ことば)を交(ま)じえることなく別(わか)れた。
The two parted without exchanging a single word.

交(ま)じえる has two main uses: (1) to mix in or intersperse different elements, and (2) to exchange something with someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 交(ま)じる (intransitive — to be mixed in, to mingle)

ASPECT (ている):
交(ま)じえている means the mixing/interspersing is ongoing or the mixed state persists.

SENSE 1 — MIX IN:

  • 冗談(じょうだん)を交(ま)じえて話(はな)す: to speak with humor mixed in
  • 英語(えいご)を交(ま)じえる: to mix in English
  • 実例(じつれい)を交(ま)じえて説明(せつめい)する: to explain with real examples interspersed

SENSE 2 — EXCHANGE:

  • 言葉(ことば)を交(ま)じえる: to exchange words
  • 視線(しせん)を交(ま)じえる: to exchange glances
  • 杯(さかずき)を交(ま)じえる: to share drinks (literary)

REGISTER:
Slightly formal/literary. In casual speech, 混(ま)ぜる is more common for physical mixing.
