巡礼(じゅんれい)

じゅんれい
noun
pilgrimage
1. pilgrimage, visiting sacred sites
A journey to visit a series of temples, shrines, or other sacred places, often following an established route.
四国(しこく)巡礼(じゅんれい)に出(で)る。
To set out on the Shikoku pilgrimage.
八十八(はちじゅうはち)か所(しょ)の巡礼(じゅんれい)を達成(たっせい)した。
I completed the pilgrimage to all eighty-eight temples.
巡礼(じゅんれい)者(しゃ)が白装束(しろしょうぞく)で歩(ある)いていた。
Pilgrims were walking in white robes.
2. pilgrimage (anime/media), visiting real-life locations from fictional works
In modern usage, visiting real-world locations that appeared in anime, manga, films, or other media. Also called 聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい).
アニメの聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい)をする。
To make a pilgrimage to anime locations.
あの映画(えいが)のファンが聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい)に訪(おとず)れている。
Fans of that movie are visiting the filming locations on pilgrimage.
聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい)が地方(ちほう)の観光(かんこう)に貢献(こうけん)するケースが増(ふ)えている。
Cases where anime pilgrimages contribute to local tourism are increasing.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
The most famous Japanese pilgrimage is the 四国八十八(しこくはちじゅうはち)か所(しょ)巡(めぐ)り (Shikoku 88 Temple Pilgrimage), a roughly 1,200 km route visiting 88 Buddhist temples associated with 弘法大師(こうぼうだいし) (Kōbō Daishi).

MODERN USAGE:
聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい) ("sacred site pilgrimage") has become a major cultural phenomenon where fans visit real locations that appeared in anime, manga, or films. This has become a significant driver of regional tourism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 巡礼(じゅんれい)に出(で)る (to set out on a pilgrimage)
  • 巡礼(じゅんれい)者(しゃ) (pilgrim)
  • 聖地(せいち)巡礼(じゅんれい) (sacred site pilgrimage / anime location pilgrimage)
