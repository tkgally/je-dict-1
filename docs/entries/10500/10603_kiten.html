機転(きてん)

きてん
noun
quick wit, resourcefulness
1. quick wit, resourcefulness, presence of mind
The ability to think and respond quickly and cleverly in unexpected situations.
機転(きてん)が利(き)く人(ひと)だ。
That's someone with quick wit.
機転(きてん)を利(き)かせて危機(きき)を乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
Using quick thinking, I overcame the crisis.
あの場面(ばめん)で機転(きてん)を利(き)かせて嘘(うそ)をついたおかげで、大事(おおごと)にならずに済(す)んだ。
Thanks to the quick thinking of telling a white lie in that situation, it didn't turn into a big deal.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the set phrases 機転(きてん)が利(き)く (to be quick-witted) and 機転(きてん)を利(き)かせる (to use one's quick wit). Rarely used outside these collocations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 機転(きてん)が利(き)く: to be resourceful, to be quick-witted
  • 機転(きてん)を利(き)かせる: to use quick thinking, to think on one's feet
  • 機転(きてん)が利(き)かない: to lack resourcefulness

NOTE:
The verb is 利(き)く, not 聞(き)く. Both are read きく but have different meanings.
