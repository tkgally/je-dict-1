内訳(うちわけ)

うちわけ
noun
breakdown, itemization
1. breakdown, itemization, details
A detailed breakdown of figures, amounts, or components that make up a total.
費用(ひよう)の内訳(うちわけ)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the breakdown of the costs.
売上(うりあげ)の内訳(うちわけ)を見(み)ると、海外(かいがい)が半分(はんぶん)を占(し)めている。
Looking at the sales breakdown, overseas accounts for half.
予算(よさん)の内訳(うちわけ)を表(ひょう)にまとめて提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I compiled the budget breakdown into a table and submitted it.

KANJI:
内(うち) (inside/within) + 訳(わけ) (reason/breakdown). Literally "inner breakdown."

USAGE:
Commonly used in business, accounting, and administrative contexts. Appears on receipts, invoices, and financial reports. Often used with を見(み)る (to look at), を示(しめ)す (to show), or を教(おし)える (to tell).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内訳(うちわけ)を見(み)る: to look at the breakdown
  • 内訳(うちわけ)を示(しめ)す: to show the breakdown
  • 費用(ひよう)の内訳(うちわけ): cost breakdown
  • 予算(よさん)の内訳(うちわけ): budget breakdown
