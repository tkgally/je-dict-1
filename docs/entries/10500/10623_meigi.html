名義(めいぎ)

めいぎ
noun
name (on a title or account)
1. name (registered on an account, title, or document)
The name under which property, an account, or a legal document is officially registered.
この口座(こうざ)は誰(だれ)の名義(めいぎ)ですか？
Whose name is this account under?
不動産(ふどうさん)の名義(めいぎ)を変更(へんこう)する。
To change the name on the real estate title.
車(くるま)は父(ちち)の名義(めいぎ)で登録(とうろく)されているが、実際(じっさい)に使(つか)っているのは私(わたし)だ。
The car is registered under my father's name, but I'm the one who actually uses it.

USAGE:
Commonly used in legal, financial, and administrative contexts. The pattern 〜の名義(めいぎ)で (under the name of ~) is standard.

名義(めいぎ)変更(へんこう) (name change on a title) is a common administrative procedure for property, bank accounts, and vehicle registrations, especially after marriage or inheritance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名義(めいぎ)変更(へんこう): name change (on a document)
  • 名義(めいぎ)人(にん): the registered name holder
  • 〜の名義(めいぎ)で: under the name of
  • 名義(めいぎ)貸(か)し: lending one's name (often illegal)
