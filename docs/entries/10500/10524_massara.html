まっさら

まっさら
adjective (na)
brand new, blank, fresh
1. brand new, unused, fresh
Describes something that is completely new and has never been used.
まっさらなノート。
A brand new notebook.
まっさらな靴(くつ)を下(お)ろした。
I started wearing brand new shoes.
まっさらな気持(きも)ちで新(あたら)しい一年(いちねん)を始(はじ)めたい。
I want to start the new year with a fresh mindset.
2. blank, clean, empty
Describes something that is completely blank or clean with nothing on it.
まっさらな白紙(はくし)。
A completely blank sheet of paper.
頭(あたま)がまっさらになった。
My mind went completely blank.
まっさらな状態(じょうたい)からやり直(なお)すことにした。
I decided to start over from a clean slate.

FORMS:

  • Attributive: まっさらな + noun
  • Predicate: まっさらだ
  • Also used as a noun: まっさらの + noun

FORMATION:
まっ (emphatic prefix, from 真(ま)) + さら (新(さら), new). The prefix intensifies the meaning.

NUANCE:
Stronger than 新(あたら)しい (new). Emphasizes the completely pristine, untouched quality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • まっさらな気持(きも)ち: a fresh mindset
  • まっさらな状態(じょうたい): a clean slate
  • まっさらなノート: a brand new notebook
