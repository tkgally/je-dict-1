殺風景(さっぷうけい)

さっぷうけい
adjective (na)
bleak, dreary, bare
1. bleak, dreary, bare
Describes a place or scene that lacks warmth, interest, or decoration, giving a cold and uninviting impression.
殺風景(さっぷうけい)な部屋(へや)だ。
It's a dreary room.
壁(かべ)に何(なに)も飾(かざ)っていないので殺風景(さっぷうけい)に見(み)える。
It looks bleak because nothing is hung on the walls.
花(はな)を置(お)いたら、殺風景(さっぷうけい)だったオフィスが明(あか)るくなった。
Once I put flowers out, the dreary office brightened up.

FORMS:

  • Predicative: 殺風景(さっぷうけい)だ
  • Modifier: 殺風景(さっぷうけい)な + noun
  • Adverbial: not commonly used adverbially

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally "killing the scenery" (殺(さつ) kill + 風景(ふうけい) scenery). The word describes a scene that destroys any sense of aesthetic pleasure.

USAGE:
Typically describes rooms, buildings, landscapes, or views that feel empty, cold, or unwelcoming. It implies a lack of charm or personality rather than actual ugliness.
