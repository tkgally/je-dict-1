滅(ほろ)びる

ほろびる
verb (ichidan)
to perish, to be destroyed
1. to perish, to be destroyed, to fall to ruin
For a civilization, nation, species, or entity to come to an end, be destroyed, or cease to exist entirely.
文明(ぶんめい)が滅(ほろ)びる。
A civilization perishes.
その王国(おうこく)は戦争(せんそう)によって滅(ほろ)びた。
That kingdom was destroyed by war.
環境(かんきょう)を守(まも)らなければ、多(おお)くの生物(せいぶつ)が滅(ほろ)びてしまうだろう。
If we don't protect the environment, many species will likely perish.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xが滅(ほろ)びる.

TRANSITIVE PAIR: 滅(ほろ)ぼす (to destroy, to annihilate).

USAGE:
滅(ほろ)びる describes large-scale destruction or extinction — civilizations falling, species dying out, kingdoms collapsing. It is not used for small everyday things breaking. The word carries a literary, dramatic tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国(くに)が滅(ほろ)びる: a country falls
  • 文明(ぶんめい)が滅(ほろ)びる: a civilization perishes
  • 種(しゅ)が滅(ほろ)びる: a species goes extinct

RELATED EXPRESSION:
滅亡(めつぼう)する is the する-verb equivalent meaning "to perish/be annihilated," often used in news and historical writing.
