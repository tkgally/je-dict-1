1. to entertain, to host, to treat
To receive and treat a guest with hospitality, offering food, drink, or attentive service.
客(きゃく)をもてなす。
To entertain guests.
手料理(てりょうり)でもてなした。
I treated them to a home-cooked meal.
遠(とお)くから来(き)た友人(ゆうじん)を心(こころ)を込(こ)めてもてなした。
I wholeheartedly entertained a friend who came from far away.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xをもてなす.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 客(きゃく)をもてなす (to entertain guests)
  • 手料理(てりょうり)でもてなす (to treat with home cooking)
  • 心(こころ)を込(こ)めてもてなす (to host wholeheartedly)
  • 温(あたた)かくもてなす (to give a warm welcome)

RELATED:
The noun form おもてなし is widely known from the phrase 「おもてなしの心(こころ)」 (the spirit of hospitality). おもてなし became internationally known through the 2013 Tokyo Olympics bid presentation.
