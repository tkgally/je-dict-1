突(つ)きつける

つきつける
verb
to thrust at, to present forcefully
1. to thrust at, to point at
To thrust or point a weapon or object aggressively at someone.
ナイフを突(つ)きつけられた。
A knife was thrust at me.
2. to present forcefully, to confront with
To forcefully present evidence, demands, or facts to someone, leaving them no room to refuse or deny.
証拠(しょうこ)を突(つ)きつけた。
I presented the evidence forcefully.
辞表(じひょう)を突(つ)きつけて会社(かいしゃ)を辞(や)めた。
I slapped down my resignation letter and quit the company.

突(つ)きつける is a compound verb from 突(つ)く (to poke/thrust) and つける (to attach/direct toward). It conveys a forceful, aggressive action.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ナイフを突(つ)きつける (thrust a knife at someone)
  • 証拠(しょうこ)を突(つ)きつける (confront someone with evidence)
  • 辞表(じひょう)を突(つ)きつける (present a resignation letter forcefully)
  • 現実(げんじつ)を突(つ)きつける (confront someone with reality)
