生(い)ける

いける
verb (ichidan)
to arrange (flowers)
1. to arrange flowers, to do ikebana
To arrange cut flowers or branches in a vase or container, especially in the traditional Japanese art of flower arrangement (生け花).
花(はな)を生(い)ける。
To arrange flowers.
母(はは)は毎週(まいしゅう)、玄関(げんかん)に花(はな)を生(い)けている。
My mother arranges flowers in the entryway every week.
庭(にわ)で摘(つ)んだ花(はな)を和室(わしつ)の花瓶(かびん)に生(い)けた。
I arranged flowers I picked from the garden in the vase in the Japanese-style room.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for the flowers being arranged.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 生(い)け花(ばな) / 生(い)け花(はな): ikebana, the art of flower arrangement
  • 花瓶(かびん): flower vase

WRITING:
Usually written as 生(い)ける. Also sometimes written as 活(い)ける with the same meaning.

NOTE: This is a different word from the colloquial いける (to be doable, to be okay).
