サブカルチャー

さぶかるちゃー
noun
subculture
1. subculture
A cultural group or movement within a larger culture that has distinct interests, styles, and values. In Japan, often refers to manga, anime, games, and other otaku-related interests.
サブカルチャーに詳(くわ)しい。
To be well-versed in subculture.
秋葉原(あきはばら)は日本(にほん)のサブカルチャーの聖地(せいち)だ。
Akihabara is a mecca of Japanese subculture.
日本(にほん)のサブカルチャーは海外(かいがい)でも高(たか)い人気(にんき)を集(あつ)めている。
Japanese subculture is gaining high popularity overseas as well.

Often shortened to サブカル in casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • サブカルチャー / サブカル: subculture
  • サブカル系(けい): subculture-type, subcultural
  • サブカル好(ず)き: subculture fan/enthusiast
  • 日本(にほん)のサブカルチャー: Japanese subculture

USAGE:
In Japanese, サブカルチャー has a narrower connotation than the English "subculture." It primarily refers to manga, anime, video games, light novels, cosplay, and related interests — what might be called "otaku culture" or "nerd culture" in English. The broader sociological meaning of subculture (punk, hip-hop, etc.) exists but is less dominant.

The term サブカル系(けい) describes a person or aesthetic associated with indie, alternative, or niche cultural tastes.
