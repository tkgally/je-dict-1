緑茶(りょくちゃ)

りょくちゃ
noun
green tea
1. green tea
Tea made from unfermented tea leaves. The most commonly consumed type of tea in Japan, including varieties like sencha, matcha, and gyokuro.
緑茶(りょくちゃ)を飲(の)みますか。
Would you like some green tea?
食事(しょくじ)の後(あと)に緑茶(りょくちゃ)を入(い)れた。
I made green tea after the meal.
日本(にほん)の緑茶(りょくちゃ)は海外(かいがい)でも人気(にんき)があります。
Japanese green tea is also popular overseas.

TYPES OF 緑茶(りょくちゃ):

  • 煎茶(せんちゃ) — the most common variety
  • 抹茶(まっちゃ) — powdered green tea used in tea ceremony
  • 玉露(ぎょくろ) — high-grade shaded tea
  • ほうじ茶(ちゃ) — roasted green tea

In everyday Japanese, お茶(ちゃ) usually refers to 緑茶(りょくちゃ) by default. 緑茶(りょくちゃ) is used when distinguishing from other types like 紅茶(こうちゃ) (black tea) or ウーロン茶(ちゃ) (oolong tea).
