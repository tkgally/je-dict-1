ゆで卵(たまご)

ゆでたまご
noun
boiled egg
1. boiled egg
An egg cooked by boiling in water. Includes both soft-boiled and hard-boiled eggs.
ゆで卵(たまご)を作(つく)る。
To make boiled eggs.
ゆで卵(たまご)の殻(から)をむく。
To peel a boiled egg.
ラーメンに半熟(はんじゅく)のゆで卵(たまご)をトッピングした。
I added a soft-boiled egg as a topping on my ramen.

WRITING:
Also written as 茹(ゆ)で卵(たまご). The hiragana-kanji mixed form ゆで卵(たまご) is most common.

VARIETIES:

  • 半熟(はんじゅく)ゆで卵(たまご) — soft-boiled egg
  • 固(かた)ゆで卵(たまご) — hard-boiled egg
  • 味(あじ)付(つ)きゆで卵(たまご) — seasoned boiled egg (like 煮卵(にたまご))

CULTURAL NOTE:
Boiled eggs are a standard ramen topping (味玉(あじたま)) and a classic convenience store item in Japan. 温泉卵(おんせんたまご) (hot spring egg) is a related but different preparation.
