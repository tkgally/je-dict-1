アスファルト

あすふぁると
noun
asphalt, pavement
1. asphalt, paved surface
The dark material used to surface roads, or a road surface made of this material.
アスファルトの道(みち)。
An asphalt road.
夏(なつ)のアスファルトは熱(あつ)くなる。
Asphalt gets hot in summer.
雨(あめ)の日(ひ)はアスファルトが滑(すべ)りやすいので気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful because asphalt is slippery on rainy days.

USAGE:
アスファルト refers to both the material itself and paved surfaces in general. In everyday Japanese, it often simply means a paved road as opposed to a dirt or gravel path.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アスファルトを敷(し)く: to lay asphalt
  • アスファルトの上(うえ): on the asphalt
  • アスファルト舗装(ほそう): asphalt paving
