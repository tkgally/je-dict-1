1. to clear away, to dispel, to relieve
To get rid of negative feelings, suspicion, or gloom. Often used with abstract nouns like stress, doubt, or frustration.
ストレスを晴(は)らす。
To relieve stress.
疑(うたが)いを晴(は)らしたい。
I want to clear away the suspicion.
運動(うんどう)をして日(ひ)ごろの鬱憤(うっぷん)を晴(は)らした。
I exercised to vent my pent-up frustration.

晴(は)らす means to actively clear away or dispel something negative — emotions, suspicion, or gloom. The object is always something undesirable.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 晴(は)れる (intransitive — to clear up, as in weather or mood)
  • Pattern: Xを晴(は)らす

ASPECT (ている):
晴(は)らしている describes an ongoing effort to dispel something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ストレスを晴(は)らす (relieve stress)
  • 疑(うたが)いを晴(は)らす (clear suspicion)
  • 鬱憤(うっぷん)を晴(は)らす (vent frustration)
  • 恨(うら)みを晴(は)らす (settle a grudge)
  • 汚名(おめい)を晴(は)らす (clear one's name)

NUANCE:
Implies deliberate effort to remove something oppressive. Unlike 解消(かいしょう)する (to resolve/eliminate), 晴(は)らす has a more emotional, cathartic quality.
