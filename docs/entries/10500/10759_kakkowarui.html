かっこわるい

かっこわるい
i-adjective
uncool, lame, embarrassing
1. uncool, lame, unstylish
Describes something or someone that lacks style or looks bad. The opposite of かっこいい.
その服(ふく)、かっこわるいよ。
Those clothes are uncool.
かっこわるい車(くるま)には乗(の)りたくない。
I don't want to ride in an uncool car.
あの髪型(かみがた)はちょっとかっこわるいと思(おも)う。
I think that hairstyle is a bit lame.
2. embarrassing, shameful, awkward
Describes a situation or behavior that causes embarrassment or loss of face.
転(ころ)んで、かっこわるかった。
I fell over, and it was embarrassing.
人前(ひとまえ)で失敗(しっぱい)するのは、かっこわるい。
Failing in front of people is embarrassing.
約束(やくそく)を守(まも)れないのはかっこわるいから、絶対(ぜったい)に行(い)く。
It would be shameful not to keep my promise, so I'll definitely go.

FORMS:
かっこわるい comes from 格好(かっこう)悪(わる)い. The shortened form かっこわるい is standard in conversation. The full form 格好(かっこう)悪(わる)い sounds slightly more formal.

Also spelled かっこ悪(わる)い.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • かっこわるい姿(すがた): an uncool appearance
  • かっこわるいところを見(み)せる: to show an embarrassing side

Related Words

Antonym: かっこいい (cool, stylish)
