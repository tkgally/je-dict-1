ゲット

げっと
noun / verb (suru)
to get, to obtain, to score
1. to get, to obtain, to acquire
To successfully obtain or acquire something desirable. Colloquial and energetic in tone.
レアアイテムをゲットした。
I got a rare item.
チケットをゲットできた。
I managed to get tickets.
限定(げんてい)グッズを朝(あさ)から並(なら)んでゲットした。
I lined up from the morning and got the limited-edition goods.

ゲット comes from English "get" and is used colloquially, especially in casual speech, gaming, and shopping contexts. It conveys a sense of achievement or excitement about obtaining something.

USAGE:

  • Xをゲットする: to get/obtain X
  • ゲットした！: Got it! (exclamation of success)

REGISTER:
Casual. Not appropriate for formal or business contexts. Use 手(て)に入(い)れる, 獲得(かくとく)する, or 入手(にゅうしゅ)する in formal situations.

NOTE:
Popularized partly through gaming and anime (e.g., ポケモンゲットだぜ！ from Pokémon). Now widely used in everyday casual conversation.
