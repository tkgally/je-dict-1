何気(なにげ)ない

なにげない
adjective (i-adjective)
casual, nonchalant, unintentional
1. casual, nonchalant, offhand
Done without any particular intention or deep thought. Often describes words or actions that have an unintended impact.
何気(なにげ)ない一言(ひとこと)。
A casual remark.
何気(なにげ)ない会話(かいわ)が楽(たの)しい。
Casual conversation is enjoyable.
何気(なにげ)なく言(い)った言葉(ことば)が相手(あいて)を傷(きず)つけてしまった。
Words I said without thinking ended up hurting the other person.
The adverbial form 何気(なにげ)なく is very common.

FORMS:

  • Adnominal: 何気(なにげ)ない + noun
  • Adverbial: 何気(なにげ)なく
  • Predicate: 何気(なにげ)ない (rarely used predicatively)

USAGE:
Most commonly used as a modifier before a noun (何気(なにげ)ない一言(ひとこと), 何気(なにげ)ない仕草(しぐさ)) or in the adverbial form 何気(なにげ)なく. Often implies that a casual or unthinking action had significant consequences.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 何気(なにげ)ない一言(ひとこと): an offhand remark
  • 何気(なにげ)ない仕草(しぐさ): an unconscious gesture
  • 何気(なにげ)ない日常(にちじょう): ordinary daily life
  • 何気(なにげ)なく見(み)る: to glance casually

CAUTION:
何気(なにげ)に is widely used in casual speech as an adverb meaning "casually" or "actually," but it is considered nonstandard. The standard adverbial form is 何気(なにげ)なく.
