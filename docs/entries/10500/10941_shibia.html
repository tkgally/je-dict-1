シビア

しびあ
adjective (na)
severe, harsh
1. severe, strict, harsh
Describes conditions, standards, judgments, or situations that are demanding, unforgiving, or difficult.
シビアな条件(じょうけん)だ。
Those are harsh conditions.
先生(せんせい)の評価(ひょうか)はかなりシビアだった。
The teacher's evaluation was quite severe.
今(いま)の経済(けいざい)状況(じょうきょう)はシビアで、就職(しゅうしょく)が難(むずか)しくなっている。
The current economic situation is tough, making it hard to find a job.

FORMS:

  • Attributive: シビアな + noun
  • Predicate: シビアだ / シビアです
  • Adverbial: シビアに

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • シビアな条件(じょうけん): harsh conditions
  • シビアな評価(ひょうか): severe evaluation
  • シビアな現実(げんじつ): harsh reality
  • シビアな目(め)で見(み)る: to view with a critical eye
  • シビアな状況(じょうきょう): tough situation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 厳(きび)しい: strict, severe (native Japanese, more common in everyday speech)
  • 手厳(てきび)しい: harsh, scathing (specifically about criticism)
  • シビア tends to sound slightly more analytical or detached than 厳(きび)しい, and is often preferred in business and media contexts.
