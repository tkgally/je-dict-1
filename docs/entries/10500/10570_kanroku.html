貫禄(かんろく)

かんろく
noun
presence, gravitas, dignity
1. presence, gravitas, dignity, commanding air
An impressive, authoritative aura that comes from experience, age, or status. The quality of someone who naturally commands respect.
社長(しゃちょう)には貫禄(かんろく)がある。
The company president has a commanding presence.
彼(かれ)は若(わか)いのに貫禄(かんろく)がある。
He has gravitas despite being young.
横綱(よこづな)にふさわしい貫禄(かんろく)を見(み)せて、堂々(どうどう)とした相撲(すもう)を取(と)った。
He showed the gravitas befitting a yokozuna and wrestled with a commanding dignity.

KANJI:
貫(かん) (pierce/carry through) + 禄(ろく) (stipend/reward). Historically related to a samurai's stipend — someone with high 貫禄(かんろく) had the bearing of a high-ranking person.

USAGE:
貫禄(かんろく)がある (to have gravitas) is the most common pattern. Generally positive, but can also be used humorously — for example, when someone has gained weight, people might joke that they've gained 貫禄(かんろく).

The word is often used for leaders, experienced professionals, and athletes who project an air of authority.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貫禄(かんろく)がある: to have gravitas/presence
  • 貫禄(かんろく)が出(で)てきた: gravitas has developed
  • 貫禄(かんろく)十分(じゅうぶん): full of gravitas
  • 貫禄(かんろく)を見(み)せる: to show one's commanding presence
