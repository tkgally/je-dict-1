ギザギザ

ぎざぎざ
noun, na-adjective, onomatopoeia
jagged, serrated
1. jagged, serrated, zigzag
Having a rough, uneven edge with sharp points, like the teeth of a saw.
ギザギザの葉(は)っぱ。
A jagged leaf.
缶(かん)のふたがギザギザで手(て)を切(き)りそうだ。
The can lid is jagged and I might cut my hand.
ハサミで切(き)ったら、端(はし)がギザギザになってしまった。
When I cut it with scissors, the edge ended up jagged.

USAGE:
ギザギザ is an onomatopoeic word that describes a rough, uneven edge with sharp points. It modifies nouns with の (ギザギザの〜) or follows a noun with が as a predicate (〜がギザギザだ). It can also be used adverbially with に (ギザギザに切(き)る).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ギザギザの刃(は): serrated blade
  • ギザギザに切(き)る: to cut with a jagged edge
  • ギザギザした形(かたち): jagged shape
