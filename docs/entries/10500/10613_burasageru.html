1. to hang, to dangle, to suspend
To suspend something so that it hangs down and swings freely.
カバンを手(て)にぶら下(さ)げる。
To carry a bag dangling from one's hand.
首(くび)からカメラをぶら下(さ)げている。
He has a camera hanging from his neck.
天井(てんじょう)からカラフルな飾(かざ)りをぶら下(さ)げて、パーティーの準備(じゅんび)をした。
We prepared for the party by hanging colorful decorations from the ceiling.

CONJUGATION (ichidan):

  • Negative: ぶら下(さ)げない
  • Te-form: ぶら下(さ)げて
  • Past: ぶら下(さ)げた

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The intransitive counterpart is ぶら下(さ)がる (to hang down, to dangle).

USAGE:
Implies a casual or loose way of carrying or hanging something. Often suggests the object swings or sways.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 首(くび)からぶら下(さ)げる: to hang from the neck
  • 手(て)にぶら下(さ)げる: to dangle from the hand
  • 天井(てんじょう)からぶら下(さ)げる: to hang from the ceiling
