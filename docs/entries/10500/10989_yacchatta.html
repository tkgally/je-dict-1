やっちゃった

やっちゃった
expression
oops, I did it (by mistake)
1. oops, I did it (by accident), I messed up
A casual exclamation expressing that one has accidentally done something wrong or made a minor mistake. Lighter and more playful than やらかした. Contraction of やってしまった.
あ、やっちゃった。コーヒーこぼした。
Oops, I spilled the coffee.
また寝坊(ねぼう)しちゃった。やっちゃったなあ。
I overslept again. Whoops.
「ケーキ全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べたの？」「やっちゃった。止(と)まらなかったんだもん」
"You ate all the cake?" "Oops. I couldn't stop."

やっちゃった is a casual contraction of やってしまった (did and regret it). The 〜ちゃった / 〜じゃった pattern is the casual spoken form of 〜てしまった, which expresses an action done completely, often with a sense of regret.

CONTRACTION:
やってしまった → やっちゃった

TONE:

やっちゃった is lighter than やらかした. It suits minor slip-ups and has a self-deprecating, sometimes cute quality:

  • やっちゃった: "oops" — minor mistake, somewhat playful
  • やらかした: "I screwed up" — bigger blunder, more serious

〜ちゃった PATTERN:

This contraction is widely used beyond やる:

  • 食(た)べちゃった: I ate it (and maybe shouldn't have)
  • 忘(わす)れちゃった: I forgot (oops)
  • 壊(こわ)しちゃった: I broke it (accidentally)
  • 言(い)っちゃった: I said it (slipped out)

The pattern always implies the action happened unintentionally or against the speaker's better judgment.
