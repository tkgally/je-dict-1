憤(いきどお)り

いきどおり
noun
indignation, resentment
1. indignation, resentment, outrage
Strong anger or displeasure aroused by something perceived as unjust, wrong, or offensive.
憤(いきどお)りを感(かん)じる。
To feel indignation.
不正(ふせい)に対(たい)する憤(いきどお)りが広(ひろ)がった。
Outrage over the injustice spread.
住民(じゅうみん)は計画(けいかく)の突然(とつぜん)の変更(へんこう)に強(つよ)い憤(いきどお)りを示(しめ)した。
The residents showed strong indignation at the sudden change of plans.

DERIVED FROM:
The verb 憤(いきどお)る (to be indignant, to be outraged). The noun 憤(いきどお)り is the nominalized form.

USAGE:
憤(いきどお)り is a formal, literary word for righteous anger or moral outrage. It is stronger than 怒(いか)り (anger) in its implication of moral judgment — the anger comes from a sense of injustice.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 憤(いきどお)りを感(かん)じる: to feel indignation
  • 憤(いきどお)りを覚(おぼ)える: to feel outrage
  • 憤(いきどお)りを示(しめ)す: to show resentment
  • 憤(いきどお)りを抑(おさ)える: to suppress one's indignation
  • 憤(いきどお)りの声(こえ): voices of outrage
