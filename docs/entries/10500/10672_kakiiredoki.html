書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき)

かきいれどき
noun
busy season, peak business period
1. busy season, peak business period
The time of year when a business is at its busiest and earns the most revenue.
年末(ねんまつ)は書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき)だ。
Year-end is the peak business season.
クリスマスはケーキ屋(や)の書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき)だ。
Christmas is the busy season for cake shops.
書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき)に体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)すわけにはいかない。
I can't afford to get sick during the busy season.

ETYMOLOGY:
書(か)き入(い)れ refers to writing entries in an account book. The 時(どき) (time/period) when merchants were busiest writing in sales was the peak earning period, hence the meaning.

USAGE:
Used for any business or industry's peak earning period. Often appears in news and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年末(ねんまつ)の書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき): year-end busy season
  • 夏(なつ)の書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき): summer busy season
  • 書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき)を迎(むか)える: to enter the busy season
