ひき逃(に)げ

ひきにげ
noun
hit-and-run
1. hit-and-run, hit-and-run accident
A traffic incident where a driver strikes a person or vehicle and flees the scene without stopping to help or identify themselves.
ひき逃(に)げ事故(じこ)があった。
There was a hit-and-run accident.
ひき逃(に)げの犯人(はんにん)が逮捕(たいほ)された。
The hit-and-run perpetrator was arrested.
目撃者(もくげきしゃ)の情報(じょうほう)のおかげで、ひき逃(に)げの車(くるま)が特定(とくてい)された。
Thanks to witness information, the hit-and-run vehicle was identified.

FORMATION:
ひき (轢(ひ)き, from 轢(ひ)く "to run over") + 逃(に)げ (from 逃(に)げる "to flee"). Literally "run over and flee."

LEGAL CONTEXT:
ひき逃(に)げ is a serious crime in Japan. Penalties are more severe than for accidents where the driver remains at the scene. It is also called 救護(きゅうご)義務(ぎむ)違反(いはん) (failure to fulfill the duty to assist).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ひき逃(に)げ事故(じこ): hit-and-run accident
  • ひき逃(に)げ犯(はん): hit-and-run offender
  • ひき逃(に)げにあう: to be the victim of a hit-and-run
