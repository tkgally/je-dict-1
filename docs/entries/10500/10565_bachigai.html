場違(ばちが)い

ばちがい
noun, na-adjective
out of place, inappropriate
1. out of place, inappropriate for the setting
Not suited to the situation, atmosphere, or social context. Describes someone or something that doesn't fit in.
場違(ばちが)いな服装(ふくそう)で来(き)てしまった。
I came wearing something out of place for the occasion.
こんな高級(こうきゅう)レストランは場違(ばちが)いな気(き)がする。
I feel out of place in such a fancy restaurant.
真剣(しんけん)な会議(かいぎ)の中(なか)で場違(ばちが)いな冗談(じょうだん)を言(い)ってしまい、気(き)まずくなった。
I made an inappropriate joke during a serious meeting and it got awkward.

KANJI:
場(ば) (place/scene) + 違(ちが)い (difference/mistake). Literally "place-mismatch."

USAGE:
Used as a na-adjective: 場違(ばちが)いな + noun. Describes a mismatch between a person, action, or item and the social setting. Often used self-referentially when feeling uncomfortable in an environment.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 場違(ばちが)いな気(き)がする: to feel out of place
  • 場違(ばちが)いな発言(はつげん): an inappropriate remark
  • 場違(ばちが)いな服装(ふくそう): inappropriate attire for the occasion
  • 場違(ばちが)いな雰囲気(ふんいき): an atmosphere that doesn't fit the setting
