だよね

だよね
expression
right?, isn't it?, I know
1. right?, isn't it?
A sentence-ending expression seeking agreement or confirmation from the listener. Casual and friendly in tone.
明日(あした)、休(やす)みだよね？
Tomorrow's a day off, right?
これ、高(たか)いだよね。
This is expensive, isn't it.
あの映画(えいが)、面白(おもしろ)かったよね。また見(み)たいな。
That movie was good, wasn't it. I'd like to watch it again.
よね is used here after past tense
2. I know, that's right
Used as a response to express agreement with what someone just said.
「今日(きょう)、暑(あつ)いね。」「だよね。」
"It's hot today, huh." "I know, right."
「あの店(みせ)、美味(おい)しいよね。」「だよね！」
"That restaurant is good, isn't it." "Right?!"
「電車(でんしゃ)のほうが早(はや)いと思(おも)う。」「だよね、そうしよう。」
"I think the train would be faster." "You're right, let's do that."

GRAMMAR:
だよね breaks down as だ (copula) + よ (assertion) + ね (seeking agreement). After verbs and い-adjectives, the だ is dropped: 高(たか)いよね, 行(い)くよね.

Used predominantly by both male and female speakers. The variant だよな is more masculine.

As a standalone response, だよね functions like "I know, right" or "exactly" in English.

Related Words

Related: だよな (masculine variant)
Related: でしょ (similar function, softer tone)
