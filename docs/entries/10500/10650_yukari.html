ゆかり

ゆかり
noun
connection, affiliation
1. connection, relation, affiliation, ties
A personal or historical connection to a place, person, or thing. Often used to describe places associated with famous historical figures.
この土地(とち)にゆかりがある。
I have a connection to this land.
ゆかりのある人物(じんぶつ)を紹介(しょうかい)する。
To introduce a person with connections.
源氏物語(げんじものがたり)にゆかりのある場所(ばしょ)を巡(めぐ)るツアーに参加(さんか)した。
I joined a tour visiting places associated with The Tale of Genji.

WRITING:
Can be written as 縁(ゆかり), but hiragana is more common.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ゆかりのある場所(ばしょ) (a place with connections to)
  • ゆかりのある人物(じんぶつ) (a person with ties to)
  • ゆかりの地(ち) (a place of connection/association)

USAGE:
Frequently encountered in tourism and historical contexts, such as 「織田信長(おだのぶなが)ゆかりの地(ち)」 (places associated with Oda Nobunaga). Also used as a female given name.
