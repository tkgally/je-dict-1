募(つの)る

つのる
verb (godan)
to recruit; to intensify
1. to recruit, to solicit, to call for
To publicly seek participants, applicants, donations, or ideas from a group of people.
ボランティアを募(つの)る。
To recruit volunteers.
会社(かいしゃ)は新(あたら)しいアイデアを社員(しゃいん)から募(つの)った。
The company solicited new ideas from employees.
被災地(ひさいち)への寄付(きふ)を広(ひろ)く募(つの)っている。
They are broadly soliciting donations for the disaster-affected area.
2. to grow stronger, to intensify, to mount
For feelings, conditions, or situations to gradually become more intense or severe.
不安(ふあん)が募(つの)る。
Anxiety is mounting.
寒(さむ)さが募(つの)り、外出(がいしゅつ)するのがつらくなった。
The cold intensified and it became hard to go outside.
待(ま)てば待(ま)つほど、いらだちが募(つの)った。
The longer I waited, the more my frustration grew.

TRANSITIVITY: Sense 1 is 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive): Xを募(つの)る. Sense 2 is 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive): Xが募(つの)る.

USAGE:
The two senses have clearly different grammatical patterns. Sense 1 (recruit/solicit) takes a direct object with を. Sense 2 (intensify) takes a subject with が and is typically used with feelings or conditions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

Sense 1:

  • ボランティアを募(つの)る: to recruit volunteers
  • 寄付(きふ)を募(つの)る: to solicit donations
  • 参加者(さんかしゃ)を募(つの)る: to call for participants
  • アイデアを募(つの)る: to solicit ideas

Sense 2:

  • 不安(ふあん)が募(つの)る: anxiety mounts
  • 焦(あせ)りが募(つの)る: impatience grows
  • 寒(さむ)さが募(つの)る: the cold intensifies
  • 思(おも)いが募(つの)る: feelings deepen
