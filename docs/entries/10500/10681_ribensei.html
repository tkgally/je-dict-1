利便性(りべんせい)

りべんせい
noun
convenience, accessibility
1. convenience, accessibility
The quality of being convenient or easily accessible. Often used when evaluating locations, services, or systems.
利便性(りべんせい)が高(たか)い場所(ばしょ)。
A highly convenient location.
駅(えき)に近(ちか)いので利便性(りべんせい)がいい。
It's near the station, so the accessibility is good.
このアプリは利便性(りべんせい)を追求(ついきゅう)して設計(せっけい)されている。
This app was designed with a focus on convenience.

WORD FORMATION:
利便(りべん) (convenience) + 性(せい) (quality, nature)

USAGE:
A somewhat formal word frequently used in real estate descriptions, product reviews, urban planning, and business contexts. Unlike the more casual 便利(べんり) (convenient), 利便性(りべんせい) treats convenience as a measurable quality or attribute.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 便利(べんり): convenient (everyday adjective)
  • 利便性(りべんせい): convenience as a quality (more formal/analytical)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利便性(りべんせい)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い: high/low convenience
  • 利便性(りべんせい)を向上(こうじょう)させる: to improve convenience
  • 利便性(りべんせい)を追求(ついきゅう)する: to pursue convenience
  • 交通(こうつう)の利便性(りべんせい): transportation convenience
