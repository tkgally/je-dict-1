なにそれ

なにそれ
expression
what's that?, what do you mean?
1. what's that?, what is it?
A casual expression of curiosity when encountering something unfamiliar or unexpected.
なにそれ、見(み)せて！
What's that? Show me!
なにそれ、初(はじ)めて聞(き)いた。
What's that? I've never heard of it.
なにそれ、美味(おい)しそう。どこで買(か)ったの？
What's that? It looks delicious. Where did you buy it?
2. what do you mean?, that's weird
An expression of disbelief, confusion, or mild annoyance in response to something strange or unreasonable.
なにそれ、意味(いみ)わかんない。
What do you mean? I don't get it.
急(きゅう)に予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)？なにそれ。
A sudden schedule change? What's that about.
「明日(あした)のパーティーは中止(ちゅうし)だって。」「なにそれ、楽(たの)しみにしてたのに。」
"They say tomorrow's party is cancelled." "What? I was looking forward to it."

USAGE:
なにそれ is a very casual expression used among friends and family. The tone of voice determines the meaning: curious and upbeat for sense 1, flat or exasperated for sense 2.

RELATED:

  • なにこれ: "what's this?" (pointing at something close by)
  • なにそれ: "what's that?" (referring to something the other person has or said)
