クレジット

くれじっと
noun
credit (payment; attribution)
1. credit, credit payment
Payment by credit, especially using a credit card.
クレジットで払(はら)えますか。
Can I pay by credit?
クレジットカードの明細(めいさい)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the credit card statement.
この店(みせ)はクレジットも電子(でんし)マネーも使(つか)えて便利(べんり)だ。
This store is convenient because you can use both credit and e-money.
2. credits, attribution
A listing of contributors in a film, TV show, game, or other production.
映画(えいが)のクレジットに名前(なまえ)が載(の)った。
My name appeared in the movie credits.
エンドクレジットまで席(せき)を立(た)たないでほしい。
I'd like you not to leave your seat until the end credits.
監督(かんとく)はクレジットの順番(じゅんばん)にこだわるタイプだ。
The director is the type to care about the order of the credits.

From English "credit." Used in two main contexts in Japanese.

SENSE 1 — CREDIT PAYMENT:
In everyday conversation, クレジット usually refers to credit card payment.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • クレジットカード: credit card
  • クレジット払(ばら)い: credit payment

SENSE 2 — ATTRIBUTION:
Used in the entertainment industry for the listing of people who worked on a production.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • エンドクレジット: end credits
  • オープニングクレジット: opening credits
