遮(さえぎ)る

さえぎる
verb (godan)
to block, to intercept
1. to block, to obstruct, to shield
To physically block or cut off something such as light, wind, or a path.
カーテンで光(ひかり)を遮(さえぎ)る。
To block the light with curtains.
高(たか)いビルが視界(しかい)を遮(さえぎ)っている。
A tall building is blocking the view.
木(き)が風(かぜ)を遮(さえぎ)ってくれるので、ここは涼(すず)しい。
The trees block the wind, so it's cool here.
2. to interrupt, to cut off
To break into someone's speech or action, preventing them from continuing.
話(はなし)を遮(さえぎ)る。
To interrupt a conversation.
「ちょっと待(ま)って」と彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)の言葉(ことば)を遮(さえぎ)った。
"Wait a moment," she said, cutting off my words.
部長(ぶちょう)の発言(はつげん)を遮(さえぎ)るのは失礼(しつれい)だ。
It's rude to interrupt the department head while they're speaking.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを遮(さえぎ)る.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is used with physical objects like 光(ひかり) (light), 風(かぜ) (wind), 視界(しかい) (view), and 道(みち) (path). Sense 2 is used with speech-related words like 話(はなし) (conversation), 言葉(ことば) (words), and 発言(はつげん) (remarks).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 光(ひかり)を遮(さえぎ)る: to block the light
  • 視界(しかい)を遮(さえぎ)る: to obstruct the view
  • 話(はなし)を遮(さえぎ)る: to interrupt a conversation
  • 言葉(ことば)を遮(さえぎ)る: to cut someone off mid-sentence
  • 日差(ひざ)しを遮(さえぎ)る: to block the sunlight
