カオス

かおす
noun / na-adjective
chaos, chaotic
1. chaos, disorder, chaotic state
A state of utter confusion or disorder. In casual Japanese, used very broadly to describe anything absurd, messy, or unpredictable.
部屋(へや)がカオスだ。
The room is total chaos.
あの番組(ばんぐみ)の展開(てんかい)がカオスすぎる。
The way that show is going is way too chaotic.
渋谷(しぶや)のハロウィンは毎年(まいとし)カオスな状態(じょうたい)になる。
Shibuya turns into a chaotic scene every year on Halloween.

Originally from the Greek word "chaos," カオス entered Japanese through English. While it can refer to the philosophical or scientific concept of chaos, in everyday Japanese it is overwhelmingly used as casual slang meaning "absurd" or "a total mess."

USAGE:

  • Functions as a na-adjective: カオスな状況(じょうきょう) (a chaotic situation)
  • Used predicatively: カオスだ, カオスすぎる
  • Very common on social media and in casual speech

NUANCE:
In casual usage, カオス often carries a humorous or exaggerated tone rather than describing genuine danger or disorder.
