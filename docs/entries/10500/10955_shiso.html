シソ

しそ
noun
perilla, shiso
1. perilla, shiso
An aromatic herb in the mint family, widely used in Japanese cuisine. The leaves have a distinctive flavor that is fresh, slightly minty, and herbaceous.
シソの葉(は)を刻(きざ)む。
To chop shiso leaves.
刺身(さしみ)にシソが添(そ)えてある。
Shiso is served alongside the sashimi.
庭(にわ)でシソを育(そだ)てて、料理(りょうり)に使(つか)っている。
I grow shiso in the garden and use it in cooking.

Also written as 紫蘇(しそ) in kanji, but the katakana シソ is common.

VARIETIES:

  • 青(あお)じそ (大葉(おおば)): green shiso, the most common variety used as a garnish and in cooking
  • 赤(あか)じそ: red shiso, used for making 梅干(うめぼ)し and shiso juice

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • シソの葉(は): shiso leaves
  • 大葉(おおば): green shiso leaf (common name in supermarkets)
  • シソジュース: shiso juice (made from red shiso)
  • シソの天(てん)ぷら: shiso tempura

USAGE:
In supermarkets and recipes, green shiso is often sold under the name 大葉(おおば) rather than シソ. The two terms refer to the same thing, but 大葉(おおば) specifically means the leaf when used as food.
