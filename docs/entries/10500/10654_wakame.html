わかめ

わかめ
noun
wakame seaweed
1. wakame, a type of edible seaweed
A brown seaweed widely used in Japanese cuisine, especially in miso soup and salads.
わかめを水(みず)で戻(もど)す。
To rehydrate wakame in water.
味噌汁(みそしる)にわかめを入(い)れる。
To put wakame in miso soup.
わかめは栄養(えいよう)が豊富(ほうふ)で毎日(まいにち)の食事(しょくじ)に取(と)り入(い)れやすい。
Wakame is rich in nutrients and easy to incorporate into daily meals.

WRITING:
Usually written in hiragana. The kanji 若布(わかめ) or 和布(わかめ) exists but is uncommon.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • わかめの味噌汁(みそしる) (wakame miso soup)
  • わかめサラダ (wakame salad)
  • 乾燥(かんそう)わかめ (dried wakame)
  • わかめを水(みず)で戻(もど)す (to rehydrate dried wakame)

CULTURAL NOTE:
One of the most commonly eaten seaweeds in Japan. Dried wakame (乾燥(かんそう)わかめ) is a pantry staple that can be rehydrated quickly. Distinct from 昆布(こんぶ) (kelp) and 海苔(のり) (nori).
