おんぼろ

おんぼろ
adjective (na) / noun
shabby, dilapidated, worn-out
1. shabby, dilapidated, worn-out
Describes something old and in poor condition, falling apart from age or heavy use. Used for objects, buildings, and vehicles.
おんぼろの車(くるま)。
A beat-up car.
おんぼろの自転車(じてんしゃ)でも十分(じゅうぶん)走(はし)れる。
Even a worn-out bicycle can still ride just fine.
あのおんぼろアパートは来月(らいげつ)取(と)り壊(こわ)される予定(よてい)だ。
That dilapidated apartment building is scheduled to be demolished next month.

USAGE:
おんぼろ is a colloquial, slightly humorous word used to describe things that are old and falling apart. It modifies nouns directly with の (おんぼろの〜) and can also be used as a standalone noun (あのおんぼろ = that old wreck). It applies only to objects and structures, not to people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おんぼろの車(くるま): beat-up car
  • おんぼろの家(いえ): ramshackle house
  • おんぼろバス: rickety bus
