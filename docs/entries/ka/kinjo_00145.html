近所(きんじょ)

きんじょ
noun
neighborhood; vicinity

Definitions

1. neighborhood, vicinity
The area near one's home; nearby surroundings.
2. neighbors
The people who live nearby.

Examples

近所(きんじょ)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in the neighborhood.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)
Neighbors / People in the neighborhood
近所(きんじょ)に買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)く。
To go shopping in the neighborhood.
近所(きんじょ)付(づ)き合(あ)い
Neighborly relations

Notes

近所(きんじょ) means 'neighborhood' or 'vicinity.'

Common expressions:

  • 近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと): neighbors
  • 近所(きんじょ)付(づ)き合(あ)い: neighborly relations, getting along with neighbors
  • この近所(きんじょ)に: in this neighborhood

Related words:

  • 近(ちか)く: nearby (more general location)
  • 隣(となり): next door (immediate neighbor)
  • 周(まわ)り: surroundings
