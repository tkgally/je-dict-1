聞(き)き入(い)れる

ききいれる
ichidan verb
to accept, to grant, to comply with

Definitions

1. to accept, to grant
To accept a request, opinion, or advice and act accordingly.
2. to comply with, to heed
To listen to and follow someone's wishes or demands.

Examples

社長(しゃちょう)は社員(しゃいん)の要望(ようぼう)を聞(き)き入(い)れた。
The president accepted the employees' requests.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の忠告(ちゅうこく)を聞(き)き入(い)れなかった。
He didn't heed my advice.
願(ねが)いを聞(き)き入(い)れていただけませんか。
Would you please grant my request?
[Note: Polite request form using いただけませんか]

Notes

聞(き)き入(い)れる is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to accept or comply with a request.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 聞(き)く (to listen) + 入(い)れる (to put in, to accept) → to take in what one hears

ASPECT (ている):
聞(き)き入(い)れている is not commonly used; the simple form or past tense is more typical.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 要望(ようぼう)を聞(き)き入(い)れる (accept a request)
  • 意見(いけん)を聞(き)き入(い)れる (accept an opinion)
  • 願(ねが)いを聞(き)き入(い)れる (grant a wish)
  • 忠告(ちゅうこく)を聞(き)き入(い)れる (heed advice)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 受(う)け入(い)れる: to accept (more general)
  • 聞(き)き入(い)れる implies listening to a request and then accepting it
