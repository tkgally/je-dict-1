金(きん)

きん
noun
gold; Friday

Definitions

1. gold (metal)
The precious yellow metal.
2. gold (color)
The color of gold.
3. Friday
Abbreviation for 金曜日.

Examples

金(きん)の指輪(ゆびわ)
A gold ring.
金(きん)メダルを取(と)る。
To win a gold medal.
月(げつ)火(か)水(すい)木(もく)金(きん)土(ど)日(にち)
Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun.

Notes

金(きん) primarily means gold but also abbreviates Friday.

AS METAL:

  • 金(きん)メダル (gold medal)
  • 純金(じゅんきん) (pure gold)
  • 金(きん)色(いろ) (gold color)
  • 金(きん)貨(か) (gold coin)

AS DAY:

  • 金(きん) = 金曜日(きんようび) (Friday)

METAL COMPARISON:

  • 金(きん) (gold)
  • 銀(ぎん) (silver)
  • 銅(どう) (copper/bronze)

DIFFERENT READING:

  • 金(かね) = money (same kanji, different reading)

Related Words

Related: 銀(ぎん) (silver)
Contrast: 金(かね) (money)
