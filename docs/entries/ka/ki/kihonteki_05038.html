基本的(きほんてき)

きほんてき
na-adjective
basic, fundamental

Definitions

1. basic, fundamental
Forming or relating to the foundation or core of something.

Examples

基本的(きほんてき)なことから始(はじ)めましょう。
Let's start with the basics.
基本的(きほんてき)に賛成(さんせい)です。
I basically agree.
基本的(きほんてき)な知識(ちしき)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Basic knowledge is necessary.

Notes

基本的(きほんてき)に (basically, fundamentally) is very common as an adverb. 基本(きほん) means 'basis' or 'foundation'. Often used in business and educational contexts.
