生地(きじ)

きじ
noun
fabric, cloth; dough

Definitions

1. fabric, cloth, material
Woven or knitted material used to make clothing or other items.
2. dough, batter
A mixture of flour and liquid used for baking.
3. one's true nature, real character
A person's true personality without pretense.

Examples

この生地(きじ)は柔(やわ)らかい。
This fabric is soft.
パンの生地(きじ)をこねる(こねる)。
To knead bread dough.
生地(きじ)が出(で)る。
One's true nature shows.
Idiomatic expression

Notes

生地(きじ) has multiple meanings depending on context.

FABRIC MEANING:

  • 生地(きじ)を選(えら)ぶ (to choose fabric)
  • 綿(めん)生地(きじ) (cotton fabric)
  • 絹(きぬ)生地(きじ) (silk fabric)

DOUGH MEANING:

  • 生地(きじ)をこねる(こねる) (to knead dough)
  • パンの生地(きじ) (bread dough)
  • ピザの生地(きじ) (pizza dough)

TRUE NATURE MEANING:

  • 生地(きじ)が出(で)る (true nature comes out)
  • 生地(きじ)を見(み)せる (to show one's true colors)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生地(きじ): fabric, dough
  • 布(ぬの): cloth (general term)
  • 素材(そざい): material (broader term)

Related Words
