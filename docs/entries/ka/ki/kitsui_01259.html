きつい

きつい
i-adjective
tight, harsh, intense

Definitions

1. tight, close-fitting
Too snug or constricting.
2. harsh, severe
Strict or demanding.
3. intense, hard
Difficult to bear.

Examples

この靴(くつ)はきつい。
These shoes are too tight.
きつい仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a tough job.
きつい言(い)い方(かた)
A harsh way of speaking

Notes

きつい is versatile with multiple meanings.

COMMON USES:

  • 服(ふく)がきつい: clothes are tight
  • 坂(さか)がきつい: the slope is steep
  • 性格(せいかく)がきつい: personality is harsh
