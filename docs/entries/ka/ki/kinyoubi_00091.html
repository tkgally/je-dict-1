金曜日(きんようび)

きんようび
noun
Friday

Definitions

1. Friday
The last day of the typical work week. The kanji 金 means 'gold/metal' and relates to Venus (the 'metal planet' in East Asian tradition).

Examples

金曜日(きんようび)の夜(よる)は飲(の)みに行(い)きます。
On Friday night, I go out drinking.
金曜日(きんようび)が楽(たの)しみです。
I'm looking forward to Friday.
金曜日(きんようび)は早(はや)く帰(かえ)ります。
I go home early on Friday.

Notes

Friday uses 金 (gold/metal), corresponding to the planet Venus. In Japanese work culture, Friday evening ('kin'youbi no yoru') is often associated with after-work socializing. Can be abbreviated to 金曜 (kin'you).
