機構(きこう)

きこう
noun
mechanism, organization, structure

Definitions

1. mechanism, structure
The system or arrangement of parts that make something work.
2. organization, agency
A formal body or institution with a specific purpose.

Examples

政府(せいふ)機構(きこう)
Government organization.
機構(きこう)改革(かいかく)
Organizational reform.
国際(こくさい)機構(きこう)
International organization.
この機械(きかい)の機構(きこう)は非常(ひじょう)に複雑(ふくざつ)で、専門家(せんもんか)でなければ修理(しゅうり)できない。
The mechanism of this machine is extremely complex, and only an expert can repair it.

Notes

機構(きこう) refers to structures and organizations.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 政府(せいふ)機構(きこう) (government organization)
  • 行政(ぎょうせい)機構(きこう) (administrative structure)
  • 機構(きこう)改革(かいかく) (structural reform)
  • 国際(こくさい)機構(きこう) (international organization)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 機構(きこう): structure, mechanism (formal)
  • 組織(そしき): organization (general)
  • 仕組(しく)み: system, mechanism (conversational)
