気(き)に入(い)る

きにいる
expression; verb (godan)
to like, to be pleased with

Definitions

1. to like, to be pleased with
To find something or someone appealing.

Examples

この服(ふく)が気(き)に入(い)った。
I liked these clothes.
彼女(かのじょ)は気(き)に入(い)らなかった。
She didn't like it.
お気(き)に入(い)りの場所(ばしょ)。
A favorite place.

Notes

気(き)に入(い)る is a common expression meaning to like.

FORMS:

  • 気(き)に入(い)る: to like
  • 気(き)に入(い)らない: to not like
  • お気(き)に入(い)り: favorite (noun)

NOTE:

  • Subject is the person who likes
  • What is liked is marked with が
