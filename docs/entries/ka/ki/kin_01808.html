菌(きん)

きん
noun / suffix
germ, bacterium, fungus

Definitions

1. germ, bacterium, microbe
Microscopic organisms, often disease-causing.
2. fungus, mold
Organisms including mushrooms and molds.

Examples

菌(きん)が繁殖(はんしょく)する。
Germs multiply.
手(て)を洗(あら)って菌(きん)を落(お)とす。
To wash hands and remove germs.
乳酸(にゅうさん)菌(きん)
Lactic acid bacteria.

Notes

菌(きん) refers to microscopic organisms.

TYPES OF BACTERIA:

  • 細菌(さいきん) (bacteria - general term)
  • 大腸(だいちょう)菌(きん) (E. coli)
  • 乳酸(にゅうさん)菌(きん) (lactic acid bacteria)
  • 善玉(ぜんだま)菌(きん) (good bacteria)
  • 悪玉(あくだま)菌(きん) (bad bacteria)

FUNGI:

  • 菌(きん)(類(るい)) (fungi)
  • カビ (黴(かび)) (mold)
  • きのこ (茸(きのこ)) (mushroom)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 菌(きん)を殺(ころ)す (to kill germs)
  • 菌(きん)が入(はい)る (germs get in)
  • 抗菌(こうきん) (antibacterial)
