聞(き)き取(と)る

ききとる
godan verb
to catch, to make out, to hear clearly

Definitions

1. to catch, to make out
To manage to hear and understand what is being said, especially when it is difficult to hear.
2. to interview, to take a statement
To gather information by asking questions and listening to responses (formal/official context).

Examples

電話(でんわ)の声(こえ)が小(ちい)さくて聞(き)き取(と)れなかった。
The voice on the phone was too quiet and I couldn't make it out.
早口(はやくち)で話(はな)されると聞(き)き取(と)りにくい。
When someone speaks quickly, it's hard to catch what they're saying.
警察(けいさつ)が目撃者(もくげきしゃ)から話(はなし)を聞(き)き取(と)った。
The police took a statement from the witness.

Notes

聞(き)き取(と)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to hear and catch what is said.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 聞(き)く (to hear) + 取(と)る (to take) → to take in what one hears

ASPECT (ている):
聞(き)き取(と)っている indicates an ongoing process of listening and understanding.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 言葉(ことば)を聞(き)き取(と)る (catch someone's words)
  • うまく聞(き)き取(と)れない (can't make out clearly)
  • 聞(き)き取(と)り調査(ちょうさ) (interview survey)
  • 聞(き)き取(と)りテスト (listening comprehension test)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 聞(き)き取(と)り: listening comprehension; interview/hearing

LANGUAGE LEARNING:
Often used in the context of language learning, referring to listening comprehension skills.
