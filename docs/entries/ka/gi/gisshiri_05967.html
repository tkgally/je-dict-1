ぎっしり

ぎっしり
adverb
packed; crammed; tightly filled

Definitions

1. packed; crammed; tightly filled
Describes something being completely filled with no empty space. Emphasizes density and fullness.

Examples

本棚(ほんだな)に本(ほん)がぎっしり詰(つ)まっている。
The bookshelf is packed full of books.
スケジュールがぎっしり埋(う)まっている。
My schedule is completely packed.
弁当箱(べんとうばこ)にぎっしりおかずが入(はい)っている。
The lunch box is crammed full of side dishes.

Notes

ぎっしり emphasizes completeness and no wasted space.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぎっしり詰(つ)まる: to be packed full
  • ぎっしり埋(う)まる: to be completely filled
  • ぎっしり入(はい)っている: to be crammed in

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • びっしり: closely packed (often written things)
  • みっしり: tightly packed (less common)
  • ぱんぱん: stuffed full (casual)

Related Words

Synonym: びっしり (densely packed)
