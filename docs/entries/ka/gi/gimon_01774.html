疑問(ぎもん)

ぎもん
noun
question, doubt

Definitions

1. question, doubt, uncertainty
A feeling of uncertainty or skepticism about something.
2. interrogative (grammar)
In grammar, relates to question forms.

Examples

疑問(ぎもん)に思(おも)う。
To have doubts about something.
疑問(ぎもん)点(てん)があれば質問(しつもん)してください。
If you have any questions, please ask.
疑問(ぎもん)文(ぶん)の作(つく)り方(かた)
How to make interrogative sentences.
Grammar term

Notes

疑問(ぎもん) expresses doubt or questions.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 疑問(ぎもん)に思(おも)う (to have doubts)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)を持(も)つ (to have a question)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)を抱(いだ)く (to harbor doubts)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)が残(のこ)る (doubts remain)

GRAMMAR TERMS:

  • 疑問(ぎもん)文(ぶん) (interrogative sentence)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)詞(し) (interrogative word)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)形(けい) (question form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 疑問(ぎもん): doubt, question (abstract)
  • 質問(しつもん): question (concrete, asking)
  • 疑(うたが)い: doubt, suspicion

Related Words

Related: 質問(しつもん) (question (asking))
