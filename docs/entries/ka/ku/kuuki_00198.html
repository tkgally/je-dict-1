空気(くうき)

くうき
noun
air, atmosphere

Definitions

1. air
The invisible gaseous substance surrounding the earth.
2. atmosphere, mood
The pervading tone or mood of a place or situation.

Examples

山(やま)の空気(くうき)は新鮮(しんせん)だ。
The mountain air is fresh.
部屋(へや)の空気(くうき)が悪(わる)い。
The air in the room is bad. / The atmosphere in the room is awkward.
Can mean both literal air quality and figurative atmosphere

Notes

The phrase 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む (to read the air/atmosphere) is an important cultural concept in Japan, meaning to sense the unspoken mood or expectations in social situations.
