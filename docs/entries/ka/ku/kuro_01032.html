黒(くろ)

くろ
noun
black, dark color

Definitions

1. black
The darkest color, the absence of light.
2. guilty
Slang for being guilty or culpable.

Examples

黒(くろ)い服(ふく)を着(き)る。
To wear black clothes.
犯人(はんにん)は黒(くろ)だ。
The suspect is guilty.

Notes

黒(くろ) means black.

COLOR COMPOUNDS:

  • 黒髪(くろかみ): black hair
  • 真っ黒(まっくろ): pitch black
  • 黒字(くろじ): in the black (profit)

SLANG:

  • 黒(くろ): guilty
  • 白(しろ): innocent

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腹黒(はらぐろ)い: scheming, malicious
