クリスマス

くりすます
noun
Christmas

Definitions

1. Christmas
The annual Christian holiday on December 25th, widely celebrated in Japan as a secular holiday.

Examples

クリスマスに家族(かぞく)で集(あつ)まる。
We gather with family on Christmas.
クリスマスプレゼントを買(か)った。
I bought Christmas presents.
日本(にほん)ではクリスマスイブにケーキを食(た)べる。
In Japan, people eat cake on Christmas Eve.
A unique Japanese Christmas tradition

Notes

クリスマス is celebrated in Japan as a commercial and romantic holiday rather than a religious one. December 24th (Christmas Eve) is particularly popular for couples.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • クリスマスイブ: Christmas Eve
  • クリスマスツリー: Christmas tree
  • クリスマスプレゼント: Christmas present
  • クリスマスケーキ: Christmas cake
  • メリークリスマス: Merry Christmas

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, Christmas is primarily a commercial holiday. Christmas Eve is considered a romantic occasion for couples, similar to Valentine's Day in the West. Christmas cake (typically a strawberry shortcake) and fried chicken (especially KFC) are traditional Japanese Christmas foods.
