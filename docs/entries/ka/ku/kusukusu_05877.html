くすくす

くすくす
adverb
giggling, chuckling

Definitions

1. giggling, chuckling quietly
Laughing quietly in a suppressed manner, often secretly.

Examples

くすくす笑(わら)っている女(おんな)の子(こ)たち。
Girls giggling together.
後(うし)ろでくすくす笑(わら)い声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard giggling from behind me.
くすくす笑(わら)いをこらえた。
I suppressed a giggle.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes quiet, suppressed laughter, often implying secrecy or mischief.
