暮(く)らす

くらす
verb (godan)
to live, to make a living

Definitions

1. to live, to spend time
To pass one's life or time.
2. to make a living
To support oneself financially.

Examples

田舎(いなか)で暮(く)らす。
To live in the countryside.
一人(ひとり)で暮(く)らしている。
I'm living alone.
幸(しあわ)せに暮(く)らす。
To live happily.

Notes

暮(く)らす means to live or make a living.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 暮(く)らす: to live (lifestyle, daily life)
  • 住(す)む: to reside (location)
  • 生(い)きる: to be alive

RELATED:

  • 暮(く)らし: life, living
  • 一人暮(ひとりぐ)らし: living alone
