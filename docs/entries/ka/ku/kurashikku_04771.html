クラシック

くらしっく
noun
classical, classic

Definitions

1. classical music
Music in the European tradition, typically orchestral or chamber music.
2. classic, classical
Something traditional, timeless, or of the highest quality in its genre.

Examples

クラシック音楽(おんがく)を聴(き)くのが好(す)きだ。
I like listening to classical music.
この映画(えいが)はクラシックだ。
This movie is a classic.
クラシックなデザインの車(くるま)。
A car with a classic design.

Notes

クラシック is used both as a noun (especially for classical music) and as a na-adjective meaning "classic" or "classical."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • クラシック音楽(おんがく): classical music
  • クラシックコンサート: classical concert
  • クラシックカー: classic car
  • クラシックな雰囲気(ふんいき): classic atmosphere

USAGE NOTE:
When used alone, クラシック often refers specifically to classical music. For the adjective use, it functions as a na-adjective: クラシックな.
