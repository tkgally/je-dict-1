癖(くせ)

くせ
noun
habit, quirk

Definitions

1. habit, quirk, peculiarity
A behavioral tendency, often unconscious; can refer to good or bad habits, but frequently implies a bad or unusual habit.
2. curl, wave (in hair)
Natural wave or curl in hair.

Examples

爪(つめ)を噛(か)む癖(くせ)がある。
I have a habit of biting my nails.
〜癖がある = to have a habit of ~
彼(かれ)の口癖(くちぐせ)は「まあ(まあ)」だ。
His favorite phrase is 'well...'
口癖 = verbal habit, pet phrase
悪(わる)い癖(くせ)を直(なお)したい。
I want to fix my bad habit.
癖を直す = to fix a habit

Notes

USAGE: 癖(くせ) often has a negative connotation, referring to bad habits or peculiar behaviors. However, it can also be neutral.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 癖(くせ)がある (to have a habit of)
  • 癖(くせ)になる (to become a habit)
  • 癖(くせ)を直(なお)す (to fix a habit)
  • 口癖(くちぐせ) (verbal habit, catchphrase)
  • 悪(わる)い癖(くせ) (bad habit)

GRAMMAR:

  • 〜癖(くせ)に (despite ~, even though ~) - a conjunctive use expressing criticism

CONTRAST:

  • 癖(くせ) - habit (often negative or quirky)
  • 習慣(しゅうかん) - habit, custom (neutral, established routine)
