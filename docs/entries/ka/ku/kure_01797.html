暮(く)れ

くれ
noun
end of year; dusk, nightfall

Definitions

1. end of year, year-end
The final period of the year, especially December.
2. dusk, nightfall, sunset
The time when day ends and night begins.

Examples

年(とし)の暮(く)れは忙(いそが)しい。
The end of the year is busy.
日(ひ)暮(ぐ)れまでに帰(かえ)る。
To return home by nightfall.
暮(く)れも押(お)し迫(せま)る。
The year-end is fast approaching.

Notes

暮(く)れ refers to endings - of the year or the day.

YEAR-END USAGE:

  • 年(とし)の暮(く)れ (year end)
  • 暮(く)れの挨拶(あいさつ) (year-end greetings)
  • 暮(く)れも押(お)し迫(せま)る (year-end approaches)

DAY-END USAGE:

  • 日(ひ)暮(ぐ)れ (dusk, sunset)
  • 夕(ゆう)暮(ぐ)れ (evening, dusk)

SEASONAL EXPRESSIONS:

  • 暮(く)れから正月(しょうがつ) (from year-end through New Year)
  • お歳暮(せいぼ) (year-end gift)

CONTRAST:

  • 暮(く)れ (end of year/day)
  • 明(あ)け (beginning, dawn)

Related Words

Antonym: 明(あ)け (beginning)
