靴(くつ)

くつ
noun
shoes

Definitions

1. shoes
Footwear worn to protect and comfort the feet. A general term for shoes of all types.

Examples

靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)いでください。
Please take off your shoes.
Important when entering Japanese homes
新(あたら)しい靴(くつ)を買(か)いました。
I bought new shoes.
この靴(くつ)は小(ちい)さいです。
These shoes are small.

Notes

Counter: 足(そく) (一(いっ)足(そく), one pair). Uses 履(は)く (to put on) and 脱(ぬ)ぐ (to take off). In Japan, shoes are removed when entering homes, schools, and many traditional establishments.

Related: 靴下(くつした) (socks).
