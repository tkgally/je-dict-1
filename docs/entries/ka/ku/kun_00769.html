〜君(くん)

くん
suffix
Mr., Master (familiar)

Definitions

1. Mr., Master (for males)
An honorific suffix used for boys, younger men, or male subordinates.
2. (for females in workplace)
Sometimes used for female colleagues or subordinates.

Examples

田中(たなか)君(くん)、ちょっと来(き)て。
Tanaka, come here for a moment.
山田(やまだ)君(くん)は元気(げんき)ですか。
How is Yamada doing?
太郎(たろう)君(くん)、遊(あそ)ぼう。
Taro, let's play.

Notes

〜君(くん) is an honorific suffix.

USAGE:

  • For boys and young men
  • For male subordinates at work
  • Sometimes for female subordinates
  • Less formal than 〜さん

COMPARISON:

  • 〜さん: polite, gender-neutral
  • 〜君(くん): familiar, mainly male
  • 〜ちゃん: affectionate, mainly female/children
  • 〜様(さま): very formal/respectful
