食(く)う

くう
verb (godan)
to eat (vulgar/masculine)

Definitions

1. to eat (vulgar/masculine)
A rough, masculine way to say 'to eat.' More casual and direct than 食べる.
2. to make a living, to survive
To earn enough to live on.
3. to bite, to sting
For insects or animals to bite.

Examples

腹(はら)減(へ)った、何(なに)か食(く)おう。
I'm hungry, let's eat something.
Casual male speech
蚊(か)に食(く)われた。
I was bitten by a mosquito.
これで食(く)っていける。
I can make a living with this.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを食(く)う.

食(く)う is a rougher, more masculine way to say 'eat.'

REGISTER:

  • Very casual, masculine speech
  • Can sound vulgar in polite situations
  • Often used by men, especially older generations

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 飯(めし)を食(く)う (to eat a meal)
  • 食(く)っていく (to make a living)
  • 〜に食(く)われる (to be bitten by ~)

COMPARISON:

  • 食(た)べる: to eat (neutral, polite)
  • 食(く)う: to eat (vulgar, masculine)
  • 召(め)し上(あ)がる: to eat (honorific)

Related Words

Contrast: 食(た)べる (to eat (neutral))
