櫛(くし)

くし
noun
comb

Definitions

1. comb (for hair)
A flat piece with teeth used for arranging and styling hair.

Examples

櫛(くし)で髪(かみ)をとかす。
Comb one's hair with a comb.
彼女(かのじょ)は鏡(かがみ)の前(まえ)で櫛(くし)を通(とお)していた。
She was running a comb through her hair in front of the mirror.
この木製(もくせい)の櫛(くし)は祖母(そぼ)の形見(かたみ)です。
This wooden comb is a memento from my grandmother.

Notes

櫛(くし) refers to a traditional comb for styling hair. In modern Japanese, ブラシ (brush) is often used for everyday hair care, while 櫛(くし) tends to refer to traditional flat combs.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 櫛(くし)を入(い)れる (to comb, to put a comb through)
  • 櫛(くし)を通(とお)す (to run a comb through)
  • 櫛(くし)の歯(は)のように (like the teeth of a comb; in a row)

NOTE ON KANJI: The kanji 櫛(くし) is not commonly used in everyday writing. Often written in hiragana as くし.
