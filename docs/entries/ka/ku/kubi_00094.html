首(くび)

くび
noun
neck

Definitions

1. neck
The part of the body connecting the head to the shoulders. Used for physical descriptions and neck-related expressions.
2. dismissal, firing (from a job)
Colloquially, 首(くび)になる means to be fired from a job. This usage comes from the old practice of beheading.

Examples

首(くび)が痛(いた)いです。
My neck hurts.
首(くび)を長(なが)くして待(ま)っています。
I'm waiting eagerly.
Literally 'waiting with a long neck'
マフラーを首(くび)に巻(ま)きました。
I wrapped a scarf around my neck.

Notes

首(くび)を長(なが)くして待(ま)つ is an idiom meaning 'to wait eagerly/impatiently'. In business contexts, 首(くび)になる (to be fired) and 首(くび)にする (to fire someone) are common expressions.
