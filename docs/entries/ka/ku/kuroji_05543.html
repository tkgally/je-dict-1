黒字(くろじ)

くろじ
noun
surplus

Definitions

1. surplus, in the black, profit
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

今年(ことし)は黒字(くろじ)になった。
We went into the black this year.
黒字(くろじ)経営(けいえい)を続(つづ)けている。
We continue to operate in the black.
貿易(ぼうえき)黒字(くろじ)が拡大(かくだい)した。
The trade surplus expanded.

Notes

黒字(くろじ) literally means "black characters" because profits were traditionally written in black ink.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黒字(くろじ)になる (to become profitable)
  • 黒字(くろじ)を出(だ)す (to show a profit)
  • 黒字(くろじ)経営(けいえい) (profitable operation)
  • 黒字(くろじ)決算(けっさん) (profitable settlement)
  • 黒字(くろじ)転換(てんかん) (turning profitable)

CONTEXTS:

  • 企業(きぎょう)の黒字(くろじ) (corporate profit)
  • 貿易(ぼうえき)黒字(くろじ) (trade surplus)
  • 財政(ざいせい)黒字(くろじ) (fiscal surplus)
  • 経常(けいじょう)黒字(くろじ) (current account surplus)

ANTONYM:

  • 赤字(あかじ) (deficit, in the red)

Related Words

Antonym: 赤字(あかじ) (deficit)
