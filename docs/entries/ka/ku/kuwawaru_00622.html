加(くわ)わる

くわわる
verb (godan)
to join, to be added

Definitions

1. to join, to participate
To become part of a group.
2. to be added, to increase
For something to be added to an existing amount.

Examples

グループに加(くわ)わる。
To join the group.
新(あたら)しいメンバーが加(くわ)わった。
A new member joined.
力(ちから)が加(くわ)わる。
Force is added.

Notes

加(くわ)わる means to join or be added.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 加(くわ)わる (intransitive): to join, be added
  • 加(くわ)える (transitive): to add

PATTERN:

  • Xに加(くわ)わる: to join X
