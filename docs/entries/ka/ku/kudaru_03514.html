下(くだ)る

くだる
godan verb
to descend, to go down, to go downstream

Definitions

1. to descend, to go down
To move from a higher to a lower position; to go downhill or downstairs.
2. to go downstream, to go down (a river)
To travel in the direction of a river's flow toward the sea.
3. to be issued, to be handed down
For a decision, order, or judgment to be given from a higher authority.

Examples

坂(さか)を下(くだ)って駅(えき)に向(む)かった。
I went down the hill toward the station.
川(かわ)を下(くだ)ってボートで海(うみ)まで行(い)った。
We went down the river by boat to the sea.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)から判決(はんけつ)が下(くだ)った。
A verdict was handed down from the court.

Notes

下(くだ)る is an intransitive verb meaning to descend or go down.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pair: 下(くだ)す (transitive - to hand down, to give [a decision])
  • Pattern: Xが下(くだ)る

ASPECT:

  • 下(くだ)っている: In the process of descending or having descended

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 坂(さか)を下(くだ)る (to go down a hill)
  • 川(かわ)を下(くだ)る (to go down a river)
  • 判決(はんけつ)が下(くだ)る (a verdict is handed down)
  • 命令(めいれい)が下(くだ)る (an order is issued)

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 降(お)りる: to get off, to descend (from vehicles, stairs)
  • 下(さ)がる: to go down, to lower

Related Words

Pair verb: 下(くだ)す (transitive)
