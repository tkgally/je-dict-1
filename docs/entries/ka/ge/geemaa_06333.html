ゲーマー

げーまー
noun
gamer

Definitions

1. gamer, video game enthusiast
A person who plays video games regularly and enthusiastically, especially as a hobby or profession.

Examples

彼(かれ)は子供(こども)の頃(ころ)からゲーマーだ。
He's been a gamer since childhood.
プロゲーマーになるのが夢(ゆめ)だ。
My dream is to become a professional gamer.
ゲーマー向(む)けの商品(しょうひん)が増(ふ)えている。
Products aimed at gamers are increasing.

Notes

LOANWORD: From English "gamer."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • プロゲーマー (professional gamer)
  • ゲーマー向(む)け (for gamers, gamer-oriented)
  • ライトゲーマー (casual gamer)
  • ヘビーゲーマー/コアゲーマー (hardcore gamer)

MODERN USAGE:
With the rise of esports, being a ゲーマー has become more respected as a profession. プロゲーマー now compete in tournaments and can earn significant income.

RELATED TERMS:

  • eスポーツ (esports)
  • ゲーム実況(じっきょう) (game streaming/Let's Play)
  • ストリーマー (streamer)
  • ゲーム配信(はいしん) (game broadcast)
