月給(げっきゅう)

げっきゅう
noun
monthly salary

Definitions

1. monthly salary, monthly wage
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

月給(げっきゅう)は毎月(まいつき)25日(にじゅうごにち)に振(ふ)り込(こ)まれる。
My monthly salary is deposited on the 25th of every month.
月給(げっきゅう)制(せい)の仕事(しごと)を探(さが)している。
I am looking for a job with a monthly salary.
彼(かれ)の月給(げっきゅう)は30万円(さんじゅうまんえん)だ。
His monthly salary is 300,000 yen.

Notes

月給(げっきゅう) refers to a fixed monthly salary, typical for full-time employees.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 月給(げっきゅう)をもらう (to receive monthly salary)
  • 月給(げっきゅう)制(せい) (monthly salary system)
  • 額面(がくめん)月給(げっきゅう) (gross monthly salary)
  • 手取(てど)り月給(げっきゅう) (take-home monthly salary)

CONTRAST:

  • 月給(げっきゅう): Fixed monthly salary (typical for 正社員(せいしゃいん))
  • 時給(じきゅう): Hourly wage (typical for part-time)
  • 日給(にっきゅう): Daily wage
  • 年俸(ねんぽう): Annual salary

NOTE:
月給(げっきゅう) usually includes 基本給(きほんきゅう) (base pay) and various 手当(てあて) (allowances).
