月末(げつまつ)

げつまつ
noun
end of the month

Definitions

1. end of the month
The final days of a month, typically the last few days.

Examples

月末(げつまつ)までに提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit it by the end of the month.
月末(げつまつ)はいつも忙(いそが)しい。
The end of the month is always busy.
給料(きゅうりょう)は月末(げつまつ)払(ばら)いです。
Salary is paid at the end of the month.

Notes

月末(げつまつ) specifically refers to the end of the month.

Related time expressions:

  • 月初(げっしょ) (beginning of the month)
  • 月半(つきなか)ば (middle of the month)
  • 月末(げつまつ) (end of the month)

Common collocations:

  • 月末(げつまつ)までに (by the end of the month)
  • 月末(げつまつ)払(ばら)い (end-of-month payment)
  • 月末(げつまつ)処理(しょり) (end-of-month processing)
  • 月末(げつまつ)締(し)め (closing at month-end)

Business context:

  • Many deadlines and payments are set at 月末(げつまつ)
  • 月末(げつまつ)決算(けっさん) (month-end closing)
