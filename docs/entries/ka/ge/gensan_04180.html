原産(げんさん)

げんさん
noun
place of origin, native

Definitions

1. place of origin, native habitat
The original location or country where a plant, animal, or product comes from.

Examples

トマトは南米(なんべい)原産(げんさん)の野菜(やさい)です。
Tomatoes are vegetables native to South America.
原産地(げんさんち)を確認(かくにん)してから買(か)います。
I check the place of origin before buying.
この品種(ひんしゅ)は日本(にほん)原産(げんさん)です。
This variety is native to Japan.

Notes

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 原産(げんさん)地(ち) (place of origin)
  • 原産(げんさん)国(こく) (country of origin)
  • 〜原産(げんさん) (native to ~)

USAGE NOTE:
Often seen on food labels indicating where ingredients or products come from. 原産地(げんさんち)表示(ひょうじ) (origin labeling) is required by law for many food products in Japan.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 産地(さんち) - producing area (where something is currently produced)
  • 出身(しゅっしん) - origin (used for people)
