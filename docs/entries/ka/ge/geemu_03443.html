ゲーム

げーむ
noun
game

Definitions

1. video game
An electronic game played on a console, computer, or mobile device.
2. game, match
A competitive activity or single unit in a match (as in tennis).

Examples

子供(こども)がゲームばかりしている。
The child does nothing but play video games.
新(あたら)しいゲームを買(か)った。
I bought a new game.
このゲームは面白(おもしろ)くて、何時間(なんじかん)でも遊(あそ)べる。
This game is interesting and you can play it for hours.

Notes

USAGE: In Japanese, ゲーム most commonly refers to video games. For traditional games or board games, specific terms are often used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゲームをする/遊(あそ)ぶ (to play a game)
  • ゲームソフト (game software)
  • ゲーム機(き) (game console)
  • ゲームセンター (arcade)
  • オンラインゲーム (online game)
  • ボードゲーム (board game)
  • カードゲーム (card game)

RELATED:

  • 試合(しあい) (match, game in sports)
  • 遊(あそ)び (play, games in general)
