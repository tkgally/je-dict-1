げんなり

げんなり
adverb, verb (suru)
dispirited, exhausted

Definitions

1. dispirited, exhausted, deflated
Feeling drained or disappointed; losing energy or enthusiasm.

Examples

長(なが)い会議(かいぎ)でげんなりした。
The long meeting left me exhausted.
その知(し)らせを聞(き)いてげんなりした。
I was deflated hearing that news.
暑(あつ)さでげんなりしている。
I'm worn out from the heat.

Notes

Describes both physical and mental exhaustion or disappointment. Often used when one's enthusiasm is drained by circumstances. Similar to うんざり but emphasizes exhaustion more than annoyance.
