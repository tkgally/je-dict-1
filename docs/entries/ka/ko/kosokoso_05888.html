こそこそ

こそこそ
adverb, verb (suru)
sneakily, stealthily

Definitions

1. sneakily, stealthily, furtively
Acting secretly to avoid being noticed, often with guilty or suspicious connotation.

Examples

こそこそ逃(に)げる。
To sneak away.
こそこそ話(はな)している。
They're whispering secretively.
こそこそするな。
Stop being sneaky.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes secretive, furtive behavior. Usually has negative connotation implying something suspicious.
