転(ころ)がる

ころがる
godan verb
to roll, to tumble

Definitions

1. to roll, to tumble
To move by turning over and over; to roll along a surface.
2. to lie around, to be lying
To be scattered about in a casual manner; to lie around.

Examples

ボールが坂(さか)を転(ころ)がった。
The ball rolled down the slope.
Sense 1: physical rolling
子供(こども)が芝生(しばふ)の上(うえ)を転(ころ)がっている。
The children are rolling around on the lawn.
部屋(へや)に本(ほん)が転(ころ)がっている。
Books are lying around the room.
Sense 2: scattered about

Notes

転(ころ)がる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 転(ころ)がす.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pair: 転(ころ)がす (transitive - to roll something)
  • Pattern: Xが転(ころ)がる (X rolls)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 転(ころ)がっている can mean "is rolling" (ongoing) or "is lying around" (state)
  • Context determines the meaning

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 坂(さか)を転(ころ)がる (to roll down a slope)
  • 転(ころ)がり落(お)ちる (to tumble down)
  • 〜が転(ころ)がっている (~ is lying around)

RELATED:

  • 転(ころ)ぶ (to fall over, trip)
  • 転(ころ)げる (to roll, tumble - similar meaning)

Related Words

Pair verb: 転(ころ)がす (transitive)
Related: 転(ころ)ぶ (to fall over)
