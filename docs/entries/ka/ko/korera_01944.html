これら

これら
pronoun
these

Definitions

1. these
The plural form of これ, referring to multiple things near the speaker.

Examples

これらの本(ほん)
These books.
これらは全部(ぜんぶ)私(わたし)のです。
These are all mine.
これらの問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)する。
To solve these problems.
これらの研究(けんきゅう)結果(けっか)から、新(あたら)しい治療法(ちりょうほう)の可能性(かのうせい)が見(み)えてきた。
From these research results, the possibility of new treatment methods has become apparent.

Notes

これら is the plural of これ (this).

DEMONSTRATIVE PLURALS:

  • これら: these (near speaker)
  • それら: those (near listener)
  • あれら: those (far from both)

USAGE:

  • More formal/written than これ used plurally
  • Often used with の: これらの〜
  • Common in academic and formal contexts
