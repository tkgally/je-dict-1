今週(こんしゅう)

こんしゅう
noun, adverb
this week

Definitions

1. this week
The current week; the week we are in now. Used to talk about events or plans within the present week.

Examples

今週(こんしゅう)は忙(いそが)しいです。
I'm busy this week.
今週(こんしゅう)の日曜日(にちようび)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet this Sunday.
今週(こんしゅう)中(ちゅう)に終(お)わらせます。
I'll finish it within this week.

Notes

Part of a time reference series: 先週(せんしゅう) (last week), 今週(こんしゅう) (this week), 来週(らいしゅう) (next week). The 今(こん) prefix means 'this' or 'current' and also appears in 今月(こんげつ) (this month) and 今日(きょう) (today, with irregular reading).
