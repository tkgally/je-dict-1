答(こた)え

こたえ
noun
answer, reply

Definitions

1. answer, solution
The correct response to a question or problem. Used in academic contexts for test answers and solutions.
2. reply, response
A response to a question or inquiry in general conversation.

Examples

答(こた)えを教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the answer.
この問題(もんだい)の答(こた)えは何(なん)ですか。
What is the answer to this problem?
答(こた)えがわかりません。
I don't know the answer.

Notes

The verb form is 答(こた)える (to answer). 答(こた)えを出(だ)す means 'to give an answer' or 'to come up with an answer'. 正(せい)答(とう) (using the on'yomi reading) means 'correct answer'. 返(へん)答(とう) means 'reply' (to a letter or message).
