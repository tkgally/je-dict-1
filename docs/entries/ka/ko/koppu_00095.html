コップ

こっぷ
noun
glass, cup

Definitions

1. glass, cup (drinking vessel)
A drinking vessel, typically made of glass but can also be plastic. Used for water, juice, and other cold beverages.

Examples

コップに水(みず)を入(い)れてください。
Please put water in the glass.
コップが割(わ)れました。
The glass broke.
新(あたら)しいコップを買(か)いました。
I bought a new glass.

Notes

Borrowed from Dutch 'kop'. Usually written in katakana. Different from カップ (cup) which typically refers to cups with handles, like coffee cups. コップ usually refers to handleless drinking glasses.
