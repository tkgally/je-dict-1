焦(こ)げる

こげる
ichidan verb
to burn; to scorch; to char

Definitions

1. to burn; to scorch; to char
For food or other materials to become burned and blackened from heat.

Examples

トーストが焦(こ)げてしまった。
The toast got burned.
焦(こ)げた匂(にお)いがする。
I smell something burning.
焦げた = burned (adj.)
火(ひ)が強(つよ)すぎて肉(にく)が焦(こ)げた。
The heat was too high and the meat got charred.

Notes

焦げる is an intransitive ichidan verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 焦(こ)がす (transitive): to burn, to scorch
  • 焦げる (intransitive): to burn, to get scorched

RELATED WORDS:

  • 焦げ臭い (こげくさい): smelling of something burning
  • 焦げ付く (こげつく): to burn and stick to the pan
  • おこげ: scorched rice at the bottom of the pot (sometimes desired)
