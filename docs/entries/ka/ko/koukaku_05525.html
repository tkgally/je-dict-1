降格(こうかく)

こうかく
noun
demotion

Definitions

1. demotion, downgrade
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

失敗(しっぱい)の責任(せきにん)を取(と)って降格(こうかく)になった。
I was demoted for taking responsibility for the failure.
降格(こうかく)すると給料(きゅうりょう)も下(さ)がる。
When you get demoted, your salary also decreases.
降格(こうかく)処分(しょぶん)を受(う)けた。
I received a demotion as punishment.

Notes

降格(こうかく) means moving down to a lower rank or position.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 降格(こうかく)する (to demote)
  • 降格(こうかく)になる (to be demoted)
  • 降格(こうかく)させる (to demote someone)
  • 降格(こうかく)処分(しょぶん) (demotion as disciplinary action)
  • 降格(こうかく)人事(じんじ) (demotion personnel decision)

USAGE IN SPORTS:

  • In sports leagues, 降格(こうかく) refers to relegation to a lower division
  • サッカーで二部(にぶ)に降格(こうかく)した (was relegated to the second division in soccer)

ANTONYM:

  • 昇格(しょうかく) (promotion)
