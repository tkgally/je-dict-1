恋(こい)しい

こいしい
i-adjective
dear, beloved, yearned for

Definitions

1. dear, beloved, missed
Feeling longing or affection for someone or something that is absent; yearning for what one misses.

Examples

故郷(ふるさと)が恋(こい)しい。
I miss my hometown.
母(はは)の料理(りょうり)が恋(こい)しくなった。
I started to miss my mother's cooking.
遠(とお)く離(はな)れた恋人(こいびと)が恋(こい)しくてたまらない。
I miss my faraway lover unbearably.
～てたまらない = unbearably

Notes

恋(こい)しい expresses longing for someone or something absent.

FORMS:

  • 恋(こい)しい (dictionary form)
  • 恋(こい)しく (adverb: longingly)
  • 恋(こい)しさ (noun: longing)
  • 恋(こい)しがる (to show signs of missing)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～が恋(こい)しい (to miss ~)
  • 恋(こい)しくなる (to start missing)
  • 恋(こい)しくてたまらない (to miss unbearably)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 恋(こい)しい: yearning for someone/something absent (emotional)
  • 懐(なつ)かしい: nostalgic (for past experiences)
  • 会(あ)いたい: wanting to meet (direct desire)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often used for hometown, family, lovers, or familiar things
  • Has a romantic or sentimental nuance

Related Words

Related: 恋(こい) (romantic love)
Related: 懐(なつ)かしい (nostalgic)
