言葉(ことば)

ことば
noun
word, language, expression

Definitions

1. word, words
A unit of language that carries meaning.
2. language
A system of communication used by people.
3. expression, speech
The way something is said or expressed.

Examples

この言葉(ことば)の意味(いみ)がわかりません。
I don't understand the meaning of this word.
日本語(にほんご)は難(むずか)しい言葉(ことば)です。
Japanese is a difficult language.
彼女(かのじょ)の言葉(ことば)に感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by her words.

Notes

言葉(ことば) has multiple meanings related to language.

COMMON PHRASES:

  • 言葉(ことば)が通(つう)じる (to be understood)
  • 言葉(ことば)に詰(つ)まる (to be at a loss for words)
  • 言葉(ことば)を選(えら)ぶ (to choose one's words)
