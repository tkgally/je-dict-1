後者(こうしゃ)

こうしゃ
noun
the latter

Definitions

1. the latter, the second one
The second of two things or people mentioned.

Examples

前者(ぜんしゃ)と後者(こうしゃ)
The former and the latter.
私(わたし)は後者(こうしゃ)を選(えら)ぶ。
I choose the latter.
後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)がいい。
The latter is better.
AとBの二(ふた)つの案(あん)があるが、費用(ひよう)の面(めん)から考(かんが)えると後者(こうしゃ)の方(ほう)が現実的(げんじつてき)だ。
There are two proposals, A and B, but considering cost, the latter is more realistic.

Notes

後者(こうしゃ) refers to the second of two items.

USAGE:

  • Always used in contrast with 前者(ぜんしゃ) (the former)
  • Formal/written style
  • Used when comparing two options

PATTERN:

  • 前者(ぜんしゃ)は〜、後者(こうしゃ)は〜

ANTONYM:

  • 前者(ぜんしゃ) (the former)
