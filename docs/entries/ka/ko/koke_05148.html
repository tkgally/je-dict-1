苔(こけ)

こけ
noun
moss

Definitions

1. moss
Small, soft green plants that grow in dense mats on rocks, trees, and damp ground.

Examples

石(いし)に苔(こけ)が生(は)えている。
Moss is growing on the stone.
苔(こけ)庭(にわ)は京都(きょうと)の名所(めいしょ)です。
Moss gardens are famous attractions in Kyoto.
転(ころ)がる石(いし)には苔(こけ)が生(は)えない。
A rolling stone gathers no moss. (Proverb)
Japanese version of the English proverb

Notes

Moss holds special significance in Japanese aesthetics, representing age, tranquility, and the beauty of impermanence (侘(わ)び寂(さ)び).

Famous moss locations:

  • 西芳寺(さいほうじ) (苔寺(こけでら)) - Kyoto's famous 'Moss Temple'
  • 日本庭園(にほんていえん) (Japanese gardens)

苔(こけ)むす (to become covered with moss) suggests peaceful passage of time. Japan's national anthem includes the line 「苔(こけ)のむすまで」 (until stones become covered with moss).

Types:

  • 蘚類(せんるい) (true mosses)
  • 苔類(たいるい) (liverworts)
  • 地衣類(ちいるい) (lichens - often included colloquially)

Related:

  • 苔玉(こけだま) (moss balls - decorative plant art)
  • 苔(こけ)テラリウム (moss terrarium)
