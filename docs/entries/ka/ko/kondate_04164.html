献立(こんだて)

こんだて
noun
menu, meal plan

Definitions

1. menu, bill of fare
A list of dishes to be served or available at a meal.
2. meal plan, meal planning
The plan for what meals to prepare over a period of time.

Examples

今日(きょう)の献立(こんだて)は何(なん)ですか。
What's on the menu today?
一週間(いっしゅうかん)の献立(こんだて)を考(かんが)える。
I plan the menu for the week.
栄養(えいよう)バランスのいい献立(こんだて)を作(つく)りたい。
I want to create a menu with good nutritional balance.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 献立(こんだて)を考(かんが)える (to plan a menu)
  • 献立(こんだて)を決(き)める (to decide on a menu)
  • 献立(こんだて)表(ひょう) (menu chart)
  • 本日(ほんじつ)の献立(こんだて) (today's menu)

USAGE: More commonly used for home cooking or school lunches than restaurant menus (メニュー is often used for restaurants).
