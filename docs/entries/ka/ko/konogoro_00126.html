この頃(ごろ)

このごろ
adverb
these days; nowadays; recently

Definitions

1. these days, nowadays
Refers to the current period of time, implying a change from before.
2. recently, lately
Describes something that has been happening in the recent period.

Examples

この頃(ごろ)忙(いそが)しいです。
I've been busy these days.
この頃(ごろ)、天気(てんき)が悪(わる)いですね。
The weather has been bad lately, hasn't it?
この頃(ごろ)どうですか。
How have things been lately?
Common greeting
この頃(ごろ)彼(かれ)に会(あ)っていない。
I haven't seen him lately.

Notes

この頃(ごろ) is a time expression meaning 'these days' or 'recently.'

Position: Usually at the beginning of a sentence or clause.

[Register: Neutral]

Writing: Often written in hiragana as このごろ.

Similar expressions:

  • この頃(ごろ) vs. 最近(さいきん): Very similar; 最近(さいきん) is slightly more versatile and can be used in more formal contexts.
  • この頃(ごろ) vs. この間(あいだ): この間(あいだ) often refers to a specific past point ('the other day'); この頃(ごろ) refers to an ongoing recent period.
  • この頃(ごろ) vs. 近頃(ちかごろ): 近頃(ちかごろ) is more formal and literary.

Note: Often implies a change or contrast with how things were before.
