〜個(こ)

こ
counter
counter for small objects

Definitions

1. counter for small objects
A general-purpose counter used for small, compact objects like fruits, eggs, boxes, and other small items.

Examples

りんごを三(みっ)つ/三個(さんこ)ください。
Three apples, please.
Both つ and 個 work for apples
卵(たまご)が五個(ごこ)あります。
There are five eggs.
お菓子(かし)を何個(なんこ)食(た)べましたか。
How many sweets did you eat?

Notes

〜個 is used for small, compact, often round or box-shaped objects. Counting: 一個 (いっこ), 二個 (にこ), 三個 (さんこ), 四個 (よんこ), 五個 (ごこ), 六個 (ろっこ), etc. Note the sound changes: いっこ, ろっこ, はっこ, じゅっこ.
