考察(こうさつ)

こうさつ
noun / suru-verb
consideration, examination, study

Definitions

1. consideration, examination
Careful thought and analysis of a subject.

Examples

問題(もんだい)について考察(こうさつ)する。
To examine the problem.
深(ふか)い考察(こうさつ)
Deep consideration.
考察(こうさつ)を加(くわ)える。
To add analysis.

Notes

考察(こうさつ) is thoughtful analysis.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 考察(こうさつ)する (to examine)
  • 考察(こうさつ)を深(ふか)める (to deepen analysis)

USAGE:

  • Often used in academic or formal contexts
  • Common in research papers and reports
