これから

これから
adverb
from now on; from here

Definitions

1. from now on; after this
Indicates a point in time starting from the present moment.
2. from here; from this point
Can also refer to physical location or a starting point in a process.

Examples

これから気(き)をつけます。
I'll be careful from now on.
これからどこに行(い)くの？
Where are you going now?
これから会議(かいぎ)が始(はじ)まります。
The meeting is about to start now.

Notes

これから refers to actions or events starting from the present moment and continuing into the future.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • これから: from now on (present → future)
  • 今(いま)から: from now, starting now
  • この後(あと): after this
