鯉(こい)

こい
noun
carp, koi

Definitions

1. carp
A large freshwater fish.
2. koi
Ornamental carp kept in ponds.

Examples

池(いけ)に鯉(こい)がいる。
There are koi in the pond.
鯉のぼり(こいのぼり)を飾(かざ)る。
To display carp streamers.

Notes

鯉(こい) refers to carp.

CULTURE:

  • 鯉のぼり(こいのぼり): carp streamers flown on Children's Day (May 5)
  • Koi symbolize strength and perseverance
  • 錦鯉(にしきごい): colorful ornamental koi

NOTE:
Same pronunciation as 恋(こい) (love).
