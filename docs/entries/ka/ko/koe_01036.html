声(こえ)

こえ
noun
voice, sound

Definitions

1. voice
Sound produced by the vocal cords.
2. cry, call
A sound made by a person or animal.
3. opinion, feedback
Expressed views or comments.

Examples

大(おお)きな声(こえ)で話(はな)す。
To speak in a loud voice.
声(こえ)をかける。
To call out to someone.

Notes

声(こえ) refers to voice and sounds.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 声(こえ)をかける: to call out to
  • 声(こえ)を上(あ)げる: to raise one's voice
  • 声(こえ)を出(だ)す: to speak up

COMPOUNDS:

  • 声優(せいゆう): voice actor
  • 歌声(うたごえ): singing voice
  • 泣き声(なきごえ): crying voice
