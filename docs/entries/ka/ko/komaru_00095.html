困(こま)る

こまる
verb (godan)
to be troubled, to be in difficulty

Definitions

1. to be troubled, to have difficulty
To be in a difficult situation, to face a problem, or to not know what to do. Expresses distress or being at a loss.

Examples

お金(かね)がなくて困(こま)っています。
I'm in trouble because I don't have money.
ている form for ongoing state
困(こま)ったことがあったら、言(い)ってください。
If you have any trouble, please tell me.
道(みち)がわからなくて困(こま)りました。
I was troubled because I didn't know the way.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xに困(こま)る (be troubled by X) or Xで困(こま)る.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state. 困(こま)っている = 'is in trouble/difficulty.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 困(こま)っている (am in trouble)
  • 〜なくて困(こま)る (be troubled because there's no ~)
  • 困(こま)った (exclamation: this is a problem!)
  • 困(こま)ったことに (unfortunately)

困(こま)った can function as an adjective meaning 'troublesome.'
