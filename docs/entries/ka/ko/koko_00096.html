ここ

ここ
pronoun
here

Definitions

1. here, this place
A demonstrative pronoun for a place near the speaker.

Examples

ここに座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit here.
ここは{どこ|}ですか。
Where is this place? / Where am I?
ここから駅(えき)まで歩(ある)いて十分(じゅっぷん)です。
It's a ten-minute walk from here to the station.

Notes

ここ indicates a place near the speaker. It's part of the ko-so-a-do place series: ここ (here), そこ (there, near you), あそこ (over there). Unlike この, ここ can stand alone without a following noun. ここから means 'from here,' ここまで means 'up to here.'
