孤独(こどく)

こどく
noun, na-adjective
loneliness, solitude

Definitions

1. loneliness, solitude
The state of being alone; can be negative (lonely) or neutral (solitary).

Examples

孤独(こどく)を感(かん)じる。
To feel lonely.
孤独(こどく)な人生(じんせい)を送(おく)っている。
They're living a lonely life.
孤独(こどく)死(し)を防(ふせ)ぐ取(と)り組(く)み。
Efforts to prevent dying alone.

Notes

Can describe both unwanted loneliness and chosen solitude. 孤独死(こどくし) (dying alone) is a growing social concern in Japan. Related: 孤立(こりつ) (isolation).
