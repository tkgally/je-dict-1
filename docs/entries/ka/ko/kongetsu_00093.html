今月(こんげつ)

こんげつ
noun, adverb
this month

Definitions

1. this month
The current month; the month we are in now. Used to talk about events or plans within the present month.

Examples

今月(こんげつ)は出張(しゅっちょう)が多(おお)いです。
I have many business trips this month.
今月(こんげつ)の予定(よてい)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me your schedule for this month.
今月(こんげつ)は誕生日(たんじょうび)です。
My birthday is this month.

Notes

Part of a time reference series: 先月(せんげつ) (last month), 今月(こんげつ) (this month), 来月(らいげつ) (next month). The 今(こん) prefix means 'this' or 'current' and also appears in 今週(こんしゅう) (this week).
