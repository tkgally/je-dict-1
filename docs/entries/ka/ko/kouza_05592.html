口座(こうざ)

こうざ
noun
bank account

Definitions

1. bank account
A financial account held at a bank for depositing and withdrawing money.

Examples

口座(こうざ)を開(ひら)く。
To open a bank account.
口座(こうざ)番号(ばんごう)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me your account number.
普通(ふつう)口座(こうざ)と当座(とうざ)口座(こうざ)。
Savings account and checking account.

Notes

口座 is essential banking vocabulary.

TYPES:

  • 普通口座 (ふつうこうざ): savings account
  • 当座口座 (とうざこうざ): checking account
  • 定期預金口座 (ていきよきんこうざ): fixed deposit account

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 口座を開く: to open an account
  • 口座番号: account number
  • 口座振替 (こうざふりかえ): automatic bank transfer
