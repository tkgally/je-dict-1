解決(かいけつ)

かいけつ
noun, suru verb
solution, resolution

Definitions

1. solution, resolution, settlement
The act of solving a problem or resolving an issue.

Examples

問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)する。
To solve a problem.
解決(かいけつ)策(さく)を考(かんが)える。
To think of a solution.
やっと解決(かいけつ)した。
It's finally resolved.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)する (to solve a problem)
  • 解決(かいけつ)策(さく) (solution, resolution strategy)
  • 解決(かいけつ)に向(む)かう (to head toward a solution)

Related: 解(と)く (to solve - less formal), 解消(かいしょう) (elimination, resolution of tensions)
