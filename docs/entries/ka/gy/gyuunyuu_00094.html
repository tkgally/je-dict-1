牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)

ぎゅうにゅう
noun
milk

Definitions

1. milk, cow's milk
Milk from a cow. The standard term for milk as a beverage or cooking ingredient.

Examples

牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)を飲(の)みますか。
Do you drink milk?
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)がありません。
There's no milk in the refrigerator.
コーヒーに牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)を入(い)れてください。
Please put milk in the coffee.

Notes

The kanji literally means 'cow' 牛(ぎゅう) + 'milk/breast' 乳(にゅう). ミルク (miruku) is also commonly used, especially in coffee shops. Japanese schools traditionally serve milk with lunch (給食(きゅうしょく)).
