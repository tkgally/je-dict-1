結果(けっか)

けっか
noun
result, outcome

Definitions

1. result, outcome, consequence
What happens as a result of an action or event.

Examples

試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が出(で)た。
The test results came out.
努力(どりょく)した結果(けっか)、成功(せいこう)した。
As a result of my efforts, I succeeded.
〜た結果(けっか) = as a result of ~
結果(けっか)はどうでしたか。
How was the result?

Notes

Common word for discussing outcomes. Can also function as a conjunction meaning 'as a result.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 結果(けっか)が出(で)る (results come out)
  • 結果(けっか)を出(だ)す (produce results)
  • 良(よ)い結果(けっか) (good results)
  • 試験(しけん)の結果(けっか) (exam results)
  • 〜の結果(けっか) (as a result of ~)

GRAMMAR:

  • Verb(た)結果(けっか)、〜 = As a result of doing ~, ...
  • その結果(けっか) = as a result, consequently

Related Words

Related: 原因(げんいん) (cause)
