懸念(けねん)

けねん
noun
concern; apprehension; worry

Definitions

1. concern; apprehension; worry
A feeling of worry or anxiety about a potential problem or issue.

Examples

安全性(あんぜんせい)に懸念(けねん)がある。
There are concerns about safety.
環境(かんきょう)への影響(えいきょう)が懸念(けねん)される。
There are concerns about the environmental impact.
懸念(けねん)を表明(ひょうめい)する。
To express concern.

Notes

懸念(けねん) is a formal term often used in news, official statements, and business contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 懸念(けねん)される: to be a concern
  • 懸念(けねん)を抱(いだ)く: to have concerns
  • 懸念(けねん)材料(ざいりょう): matter of concern
