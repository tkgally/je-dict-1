肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)

けんこうこつ
noun
shoulder blade; scapula

Definitions

1. shoulder blade; scapula
The flat triangular bone at the back of the shoulder. An important bone connecting the arm to the body.

Examples

肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)のストレッチをする。
I do stretches for my shoulder blades.
肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)の周(まわ)りがこっている。
The area around my shoulder blades is stiff.
肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)を寄(よ)せるように動(うご)かす。
Move your shoulder blades as if bringing them together.

Notes

肩甲骨(けんこうこつ) is commonly mentioned in fitness and massage contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)を動(うご)かす: to move the shoulder blades
  • 肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)周(まわ)り: the area around the shoulder blades
  • 肩甲骨(けんこうこつ)はがし: shoulder blade release (massage technique)

FITNESS CONTEXT:
Proper 肩甲骨(けんこうこつ) mobility is emphasized in many exercise programs for posture improvement.
