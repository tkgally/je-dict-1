計(けい)

けい
noun / suffix
total, sum; plan; meter

Definitions

1. total, sum
The combined amount.
2. plan, scheme
A course of action.
3. (suffix) meter, gauge
A measuring instrument.

Examples

合計(ごうけい)で千(せん)円(えん)です。
The total is 1,000 yen.
体温(たいおん)計(けい)で測(はか)る。
To measure with a thermometer.
計(けい)10人(にん)
A total of 10 people.

Notes

計(けい) has multiple meanings based on context.

AS TOTAL:

  • 合計(ごうけい) (total, sum)
  • 小計(しょうけい) (subtotal)
  • 累計(るいけい) (cumulative total)
  • 計(けい)〜人 (a total of ~ people)

AS PLAN:

  • 計画(けいかく) (plan)
  • 設計(せっけい) (design)
  • 会計(かいけい) (accounting; bill)

AS METER (suffix):

  • 体温(たいおん)計(けい) (thermometer)
  • 時計(とけい) (watch, clock)
  • 温度(おんど)計(けい) (thermometer)
  • 血圧(けつあつ)計(けい) (blood pressure meter)
  • 万歩(まんぽ)計(けい) (pedometer)
