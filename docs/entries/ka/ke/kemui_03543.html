煙(けむ)い

けむい
i-adjective
smoky

Definitions

1. smoky; filled with smoke
Describing an atmosphere or environment with a lot of smoke.
2. irritating to the eyes (from smoke)
The sensation when smoke causes discomfort to the eyes.

Examples

この部屋(へや)は煙(けむ)い。
This room is smoky.
Sense 1: describing environment
たき火(び)の煙(けむり)で目(め)が煙(けむ)い。
My eyes are irritated by the smoke from the bonfire.
Sense 2: physical irritation
煙(けむ)くて息(いき)ができない。
It's so smoky I can't breathe.
Te-form connecting to consequence

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 煙(けむ)く
  • Noun form: 煙(けむ)さ (smokiness)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 煙(けむり) (smoke) - the noun form
  • 煙(けむ)たい - variant adjective with same meaning
  • 煙(けむ)たがる - to find someone/something bothersome (figurative)

The figurative expression 煙(けむ)たがる means to find someone annoying or uncomfortable to be around, as if they were smoke irritating your eyes.
