警察署(けいさつしょ)

けいさつしょ
noun
police station

Definitions

1. police station
A building where police officers work and where citizens can report crimes or seek assistance.

Examples

財布(さいふ)をなくしたので警察署(けいさつしょ)に届(とど)けました。
I lost my wallet so I reported it to the police station.
一番(いちばん)近(ちか)い警察署(けいさつしょ)はどこですか。
Where is the nearest police station?
警察署(けいさつしょ)でパスポートの届(とど)け出(で)をしました。
I filed a report about my passport at the police station.

Notes

警察署 is a formal police station. 交番 (こうばん) refers to the small police boxes found throughout Japanese neighborhoods, where officers handle local matters. Japan's police are known for being helpful with directions and lost items.
