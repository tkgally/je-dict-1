継続的(けいぞくてき)

けいぞくてき
na-adjective
continuous, sustained, ongoing

Definitions

1. continuous, sustained
Continuing without interruption over a period of time; ongoing.

Examples

継続的(けいぞくてき)な努力(どりょく)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Continuous effort is important.
継続的(けいぞくてき)に運動(うんどう)している。
I exercise continuously.
継続的(けいぞくてき)なサポートをお願(ねが)いします。
We ask for your ongoing support.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
Formed from 継続(けいぞく) (continuation) + 的 (suffix making na-adjectives).

ADVERBIAL FORM:
継続的(けいぞくてき)に: continuously, sustainedly

CONTRAST:

  • 一時的(いちじてき): temporary (opposite)

Related Words

Antonym: 一時的(いちじてき) (temporary)
