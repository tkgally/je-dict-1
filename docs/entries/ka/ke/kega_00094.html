けが

けが
noun
injury, wound

Definitions

1. injury, wound
Physical harm or damage to the body from an accident or attack.

Examples

けがをしました。
I got injured.
けがは大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですか。
Is your injury okay?
けがをしないように気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful not to get injured.

Notes

けがをする means 'to get injured'. Can be written in kanji as 怪我(けが) but hiragana is common. けが人(にん) means 'injured person'. 大(おお)けが (serious injury), 軽(かる)いけが (minor injury).
