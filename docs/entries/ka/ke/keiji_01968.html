掲示(けいじ)

けいじ
noun / suru-verb
notice, bulletin, posting

Definitions

1. notice, bulletin, posting
Public display of information.

Examples

掲示(けいじ)板(ばん)
Bulletin board.
掲示(けいじ)を見(み)る。
To look at the notice.
結果(けっか)を掲示(けいじ)する。
To post the results.
試験(しけん)結果(けっか)は明日(あした)掲示(けいじ)板(ばん)に掲示(けいじ)される予定(よてい)です。
The exam results are scheduled to be posted on the bulletin board tomorrow.

Notes

掲示(けいじ) means posting notices publicly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 掲示(けいじ)する (to post, display)
  • 掲示(けいじ)板(ばん) (bulletin board)
  • 掲示(けいじ)物(ぶつ) (posted notice)

NOTE: Different from 刑事(けいじ) (detective) - same reading.

DIGITAL:

  • 電子(でんし)掲示(けいじ)板(ばん) (electronic bulletin board, BBS)
