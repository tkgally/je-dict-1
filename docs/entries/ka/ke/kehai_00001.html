気配(けはい)

けはい
noun
indication, sign, presence

Definitions

1. indication, sign, hint
A subtle indication or sign of something, often sensed rather than directly observed.
2. presence, atmosphere
The sense that someone or something is present, even if not visible.

Examples

雨(あめ)の気配(けはい)がする。
There's a sign of rain.
誰(だれ)かの気配(けはい)を感(かん)じた。
I sensed someone's presence.
彼(かれ)が帰(かえ)ってくる気配(けはい)はなかった。
There was no indication that he would come back.

Notes

気配(けはい) describes a subtle indication or presence that is sensed rather than directly observed. It often implies an intuitive perception.

Common collocations:

  • 気配(けはい)がする (to sense a sign/presence)
  • 気配(けはい)を感(かん)じる (to feel a presence)
  • 気配(けはい)を消(け)す (to hide one's presence)
  • 人(ひと)の気配(けはい) (someone's presence)

Usage note: 気配(けはい) is often used when describing intuitive feelings about situations or presences. It's commonly found in mystery or suspense contexts.
