けれども

けれども
conjunction
but, however, although

Definitions

1. but, however, although
Formal conjunction meaning 'but' or 'however'. More polite than けど.

Examples

高(たか)いけれども、買(か)う。
It's expensive, but I'll buy it.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っているけれども、出(で)かける。
It's raining, but I'm going out.
難(むずか)しいけれども、頑張(がんば)る。
It's difficult, but I'll try hard.

Notes

けれども is a formal conjunction meaning "but" or "however".

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • けれども: formal
  • けれど: semi-formal
  • けど: casual

USAGE:

  • Attached after verbs, adjectives, or nouns+だ
  • Can be used mid-sentence or at sentence end

SENTENCE-FINAL USAGE:

Ending with けれども/けど softens the statement or implies there's more to say.

  • 行(い)きたいんですけど... (I want to go, but... [implying hesitation])

Note: In formal writing, が or しかし are preferred.
