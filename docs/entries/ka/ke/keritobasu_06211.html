蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす

けりとばす
verb (godan, transitive)
to kick away; to kick aside

Definitions

1. to kick away; to kick aside
To forcefully kick something, sending it flying away.
2. to kick over; to kick down
To knock something or someone down by kicking.

Examples

空(あ)き缶(かん)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす。
To kick an empty can away.
石(いし)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばしながら歩(ある)いた。
I walked while kicking stones out of my way.
怒(いか)りで椅子(いす)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばした。
In anger, I kicked the chair over.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
蹴(け)る (to kick) + 飛(と)ばす (to make fly, to send away)

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を to mark the object being kicked

ASPECT ({ている}):
蹴(け)り飛(と)ばしている indicates the action is in progress or habitual.

REGISTER:
Neutral to casual. Often used in informal contexts.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 突(つ)き飛(と)ばす: to push/shove away
  • 殴(なぐ)り飛(と)ばす: to punch and send flying
  • 吹(ふ)き飛(と)ばす: to blow away

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空(あ)き缶(かん)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす: to kick an empty can
  • 石(いし)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす: to kick a stone away
  • 人(ひと)を蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす: to kick someone away (violent)

Related Words
