けち

けち
noun / na-adjective
stingy, miser

Definitions

1. stingy, miserly, cheap
Being unwilling to spend money; a person who is tight with money.
2. petty, trivial
Being small-minded or making a fuss over small things.

Examples

けちな人(ひと)
A stingy person.
彼(かれ)はけちだ。
He is stingy.
けちをつける。
To find fault with; to nitpick.
[Note: Fixed expression meaning to criticize]
そんなけちなことを言(い)わないで、もう少(すこ)し出(だ)してよ。
Don't be so stingy—chip in a little more.

Notes

けち describes stinginess or pettiness.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • けちな人(ひと) (stingy person)
  • けちをつける (to find fault, nitpick)
  • けちくさい (cheap-looking, petty)

USAGE:

  • As noun: 彼(かれ)はけちだ
  • As na-adjective: けちな〜

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • けち: stingy (casual, slightly negative)
  • 倹約(けんやく): frugal (positive connotation)
  • 吝嗇(りんしょく): miserly (formal, literary)
