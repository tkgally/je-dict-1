学会(がっかい)

がっかい
noun
academic society, learned society, academic conference

Definitions

1. academic society, learned society
An organization of scholars or professionals in a particular field of study.
2. academic conference
A meeting or conference held by an academic society.

Examples

日本(にほん)言語(げんご)学会(がっかい)に入会(にゅうかい)した。
I joined the Linguistic Society of Japan.
来月(らいげつ)の学会(がっかい)で発表(はっぴょう)する。
I will present at the conference next month.
学会(がっかい)で論文(ろんぶん)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
I presented a paper at the academic conference.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学会(がっかい)発表(はっぴょう) (conference presentation)
  • 学会(がっかい)に参加(さんか)する (to participate in a conference)
  • 学会(がっかい)員(いん) (member of an academic society)

EXAMPLES OF SOCIETIES:

  • 日本(にほん)物理(ぶつり)学会(がっかい) (Physical Society of Japan)
  • 日本(にほん)医学(いがく)学会(がっかい) (Japan Medical Association)
