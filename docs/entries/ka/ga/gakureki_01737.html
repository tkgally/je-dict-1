学歴(がくれき)

がくれき
noun
educational background, academic record

Definitions

1. educational background, academic history
A person's history of formal education, including schools attended and degrees obtained.

Examples

履歴書(りれきしょ)に学歴(がくれき)を書(か)く。
To write one's educational background on a resume.
日本(にほん)は学歴(がくれき)社会(しゃかい)だと言(い)われている。
Japan is said to be an education-focused society.
学歴(がくれき)社会(しゃかい) = society that values educational credentials
高(たか)い学歴(がくれき)があっても仕事(しごと)が見(み)つからない。
Even with high educational credentials, I can't find a job.

Notes

学歴(がくれき) refers to one's formal educational credentials.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 学歴(がくれき)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い (high/low educational background)
  • 学歴(がくれき)を問(と)わない (regardless of educational background)
  • 学歴(がくれき)詐称(さしょう) (falsification of academic credentials)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 学歴(がくれき)社会(しゃかい) (credential-focused society)
  • 学歴(がくれき)差別(さべつ) (educational discrimination)
  • 最終(さいしゅう)学歴(がくれき) (highest level of education)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 学歴(がくれき): educational background
  • 経歴(けいれき): career history
  • 職歴(しょくれき): work history

Related Words
