がやがや

がやがや
adverb
noisily, clamorously (of a crowd)

Definitions

1. noisily, clamorously
Describes the loud, confused noise of many people talking at once; the din of a crowd.

Examples

教室(きょうしつ)の中(なか)はがやがやと騒(さわ)がしかった。
The classroom was noisy with chatter.
居酒屋(いざかや)でがやがやと話(はな)し声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the clamor of voices in the izakaya.
子供(こども)たちががやがやと集(あつ)まってきた。
The children gathered noisily.
市場(いちば)はがやがやとした活気(かっき)に満(み)ちていた。
The market was full of lively commotion.
がやがやとした + noun

Notes

SOUND SYMBOLISM: The voiced consonant が conveys a loud, somewhat rough or chaotic sound.

VERB FORMS:

  • がやがやする (to be noisy with chatter)
  • がやがや騒(さわ)ぐ (to make a noisy commotion)
  • がやがや言(い)う (to chatter noisily)

NUANCE: Often describes a lively atmosphere rather than unpleasant noise. Can be positive (bustling, lively) or slightly negative (too noisy).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ざわざわ: Buzzing, rustling (can be anxious undertone)
  • わいわい: Boisterously, merrily (more positive)
  • ぺちゃぺちゃ: Chattering (often trivial talk)
  • ひそひそ: Whispering (opposite - quiet)

Related Words
