葛藤(かっとう)

かっとう
noun
conflict; dilemma; inner struggle

Definitions

1. conflict; dilemma; inner struggle
A state of mental or emotional struggle between opposing forces or choices.

Examples

内心(ないしん)で葛藤(かっとう)している。
I'm struggling internally.
仕事(しごと)と家庭(かてい)の葛藤(かっとう)がある。
There's a conflict between work and family.
彼(かれ)との葛藤(かっとう)を乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
I overcame the conflict with him.

Notes

葛藤 can refer to both internal psychological conflict and interpersonal conflict.

ETYMOLOGY:
The characters 葛 and 藤 both refer to climbing vines that become tangled together, metaphorically representing entangled struggles.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 葛藤する: to struggle, to be conflicted
  • 内的葛藤: inner conflict
  • 〜との葛藤: conflict with ~
