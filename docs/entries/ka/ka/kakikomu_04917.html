書(か)き込(こ)む

かきこむ
godan verb
to write in, to fill in, to post (online)

Definitions

1. to write in, to fill in
To write information into a form, document, or blank space.
2. to post, to comment (online)
To write a comment or message on a website, bulletin board, or social media.

Examples

申込書(もうしこみしょ)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)き込(こ)んでください。
Please fill in your name on the application form.
教科書(きょうかしょ)にメモを書(か)き込(こ)んだ。
I wrote notes in the textbook.
SNSに毎日(まいにち)書(か)き込(こ)んでいる。
I post on social media every day.

Notes

書(か)き込(こ)む is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to write into something or post online.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 書(か)く (to write) + 込(こ)む (into) → to write into

ASPECT (ている):
書(か)き込(こ)んでいる indicates an ongoing action of writing/posting.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 書類(しょるい)に書(か)き込(こ)む (fill in a document)
  • フォームに書(か)き込(こ)む (fill in a form)
  • 掲示板に書(か)き込(こ)む (post on a bulletin board)
  • SNSに書(か)き込(こ)む (post on social media)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 書(か)き込(こ)み: a post, comment, entry (especially online)

MODERN USAGE:
In the internet age, 書(か)き込(こ)む is commonly used for posting comments or messages online. 書(か)き込(こ)み refers to such online posts or comments.
