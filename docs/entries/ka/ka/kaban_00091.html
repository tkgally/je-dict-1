かばん

かばん
noun
bag, briefcase

Definitions

1. bag, suitcase, briefcase
A general term for bags, including handbags, school bags, briefcases, and suitcases.

Examples

かばんを持(も)っていますか。
Do you have a bag?
この かばんは重(おも)いです。
This bag is heavy.
かばんに本(ほん)を入(い)れました。
I put the book in the bag.

Notes

かばん is usually written in hiragana, though the kanji 鞄 exists but is rarely used. It's a general term for bags. More specific terms include ハンドバッグ (handbag), リュック (backpack), and ブリーフケース (briefcase).
