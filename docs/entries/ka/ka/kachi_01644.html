勝(か)ち

かち
noun
win, victory

Definitions

1. win, victory, triumph
The result of winning a competition, game, or battle.

Examples

今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)は私(わたし)たちの勝(か)ちだ。
Today's game is our win.
3対(たい)2で勝(か)ちを収(おさ)めた。
We won 3 to 2.
勝(か)ちを収(おさ)める is a formal expression
勝(か)ちにこだわる。
To be obsessed with winning.

Notes

勝(か)ち is a noun meaning win or victory. It comes from the verb 勝(か)つ (to win).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 勝(か)ちを収(おさ)める (to achieve victory - formal)
  • 勝(か)ちにこだわる (to be obsessed with winning)
  • 〜の勝(か)ち (... wins / victory goes to ...)
  • 大勝(たいしょう)ち (big win)

ANTONYM:

  • 負(ま)け (loss, defeat)

RELATED VERB:

  • 勝(か)つ (to win)

COMPOUND EXPRESSIONS:

  • 勝(か)ち負(ま)け (winning and losing)
  • 勝(か)ち残(のこ)る (to survive by winning)

Related Words

Antonym: 負(ま)け (loss, defeat)
Related: 勝(か)つ (to win)
