かかる

かかる
verb (godan)
to take (time/money), to cost

Definitions

1. to take (time)
Describes the amount of time required to do something. Used with time expressions.
2. to cost (money)
Describes the amount of money required for something. Used with money/cost expressions.

Examples

駅(えき)まで十(じゅっ)分(ぷん)かかります。
It takes ten minutes to the station.
いくらかかりますか。
How much does it cost?
この仕事(しごと)は時間(じかん)がかかります。
This work takes time.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: かける (transitive). Pattern: Xがかかる.

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. かかっている = 'is taking time' or 'has been locked.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時間(じかん)がかかる (takes time)
  • お金(かね)がかかる (costs money)
  • 電話(でんわ)がかかる (receive a call)
  • 鍵(かぎ)がかかる (be locked)

MULTIPLE MEANINGS: This verb has many senses depending on context. Written with various kanji (掛かる, 係る, etc.).
