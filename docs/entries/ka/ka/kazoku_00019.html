家族(かぞく)

かぞく
noun
family

Definitions

1. family
A group of people related by blood, marriage, or adoption living together.

Examples

家族(かぞく)は4人(にん)です。
My family has 4 people.
家族(かぞく)と一緒(いっしょ)に旅行(りょこう)しました。
I traveled with my family.
ご家族(かぞく)はお元気(げんき)ですか？
Is your family well?
ご家族 = your family (polite)

Notes

A fundamental word for family. ご家族(かぞく) is the polite form for referring to someone else's family. Related terms include all the individual family member words.
