金持(かねも)ち

かねもち
noun
rich person, wealthy person

Definitions

1. rich person, wealthy person
A person who has a lot of money.

Examples

金持(かねも)ちになりたい。
I want to become rich.
金持(かねも)ちの家(いえ)。
A rich person's house.
あの人(ひと)は金持(かねも)ちだ。
That person is wealthy.

Notes

金持(かねも)ち combines 金(かね) (money) + 持(も)ち (having).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 金持(かねも)ちになる (become rich)
  • お金持(かねも)ち (rich person - polite/cute)
  • 大金持(おおがねも)ち (very rich person)

CONTRAST:

  • 金持(かねも)ち: colloquial, casual
  • 富裕層(ふゆうそう): wealthy class (formal)
  • 資産家(しさんか): wealthy person, property owner (formal)

OPPOSITE:

  • 貧乏(びんぼう): poor
