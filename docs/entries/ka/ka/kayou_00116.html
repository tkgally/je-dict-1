通(かよ)う

かよう
verb (godan)
to commute, to go regularly

Definitions

1. to commute, to go back and forth
To travel regularly between two places, especially for work or school.
2. to attend regularly
To go to a place regularly for lessons, treatment, or other purposes.

Examples

毎日(まいにち)電車(でんしゃ)で会社(かいしゃ)に通(かよ)っている。
I commute to work by train every day.
に marks the destination
日本語(にほんご)学校(がっこう)に通(かよ)う。
Attend Japanese language school.
病院(びょういん)に通(かよ)っています。
I go to the hospital regularly (for treatment).

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xに通(かよ)う (commute to X / attend X regularly).

ASPECT: ている indicates habitual action. 通(かよ)っている = 'commutes regularly' or 'has been attending.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)に通(かよ)う (commute to work)
  • 学校(がっこう)に通(かよ)う (go to school)
  • 病院(びょういん)に通(かよ)う (go to hospital regularly)
  • 塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)う (attend cram school)
  • 通(かよ)い (commuting—noun form)

CONTRAST WITH 行(い)く:

  • 行(い)く: general 'go' (one-time or general)
  • 通(かよ)う: regular, repeated going to the same place

RELATED:

  • 通勤(つうきん) (commuting to work—noun)
  • 通学(つうがく) (commuting to school—noun)
