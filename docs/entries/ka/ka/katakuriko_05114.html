片栗粉(かたくりこ)

かたくりこ
noun
potato starch

Definitions

1. potato starch
A fine white starch powder used for thickening sauces, coating foods for frying, and other culinary purposes.

Examples

片栗粉(かたくりこ)でとろみをつける。
Thicken with potato starch.
とろみをつける is the standard expression for thickening
鶏肉(とりにく)に片栗粉(かたくりこ)をまぶして揚(あ)げる。
Coat the chicken with potato starch and deep-fry it.
まぶす means to coat/dust with powder
水溶(みずと)き片栗粉(かたくりこ)を加(くわ)えてください。
Please add the potato starch mixed with water.
水溶き片栗粉 is starch dissolved in water, used for thickening

Notes

Originally made from the katakuri plant (dogtooth violet), but modern 片栗粉 is typically potato starch (馬鈴薯(ばれいしょ)でんぷん).

Common uses:

  • Thickening agent for 餡(あん)かけ (thick sauce dishes)
  • Coating for 唐揚(からあ)げ (creates crispier texture than flour)
  • Making わらび餅(もち) (warabi mochi)

Difference from 小麦粉(こむぎこ): 片栗粉 creates a crispier, lighter coating and clearer thickening compared to wheat flour.
