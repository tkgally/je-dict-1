火事(かじ)

かじ
noun
fire (destructive)

Definitions

1. fire, conflagration
A destructive fire, especially one that burns buildings.

Examples

火事(かじ)だ！
Fire!
Warning cry
隣(となり)の家(いえ)が火事(かじ)になった。
The neighboring house caught fire.
火事(かじ)で家(いえ)が焼(や)けた。
The house burned down in a fire.

Notes

火事(かじ) specifically refers to a destructive fire or house fire.

火事(かじ) vs. 火(ひ):

  • 火(ひ): fire (general - cooking fire, campfire, etc.)
  • 火事(かじ): fire (destructive, accidental)

Related:

  • 火事(かじ)になる: to catch fire
  • 火災(かさい): fire (formal/official term)
  • 消防車(しょうぼうしゃ): fire engine
  • 消防士(しょうぼうし): firefighter
