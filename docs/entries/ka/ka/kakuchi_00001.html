各地(かくち)

かくち
noun
various places; each place

Definitions

1. various places; each place; different regions
Multiple places across a country, region, or area. Often used in news reports and formal contexts.

Examples

全国(ぜんこく)各地(かくち)で桜(さくら)が咲(さ)き始(はじ)めた。
Cherry blossoms have started blooming in various places across the country.
日本(にほん)各地(かくち)に伝統的(でんとうてき)な祭(まつ)りがある。
There are traditional festivals in various parts of Japan.
世界(せかい)各地(かくち)から観光客(かんこうきゃく)が訪(おとず)れる。
Tourists visit from all over the world.

Notes

PATTERNS:

  • 全国(ぜんこく)各地(かくち) (various places across the country)
  • 日本(にほん)各地(かくち) (various parts of Japan)
  • 世界(せかい)各地(かくち) (various parts of the world)
  • 各地(かくち)で (in various places)
  • 各地(かくち)の (of various places)

REGISTER: Formal. Commonly used in news reports, announcements, and written Japanese.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 方々(ほうぼう) (here and there, everywhere - less formal)
  • あちこち (here and there - casual)
  • 各所(かくしょ) (various locations - similar formality)
  • 各国(かっこく) (various countries)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 産地(さんち) (producing area)
  • 現地(げんち) (local area, on-site)

Related Words
