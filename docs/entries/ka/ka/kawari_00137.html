代(か)わり

かわり
noun
substitute; replacement; instead of

Definitions

1. substitute, replacement
Something or someone that takes the place of another.
2. instead of, in exchange for
Used to indicate one thing replacing another.

Examples

彼(かれ)の代(か)わりに行(い)きます。
I'll go instead of him.
〜の代(か)わりに pattern
コーヒーの代(か)わりにお茶(ちゃ)を飲(の)んだ。
I drank tea instead of coffee.
代(か)わりを探(さが)す。
To look for a replacement.

Notes

代(か)わり means 'substitute' or 'replacement.'

Key pattern:

  • 〜の代(か)わりに: instead of ~, in place of ~

Related verb: 代(か)わる (to substitute, to take the place of)

Other forms:

  • 代(か)わりに: instead (adverbial)
  • その代(か)わり: in exchange, however
  • お代(か)わり: refill, second helping (polite)
