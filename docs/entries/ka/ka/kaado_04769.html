カード

かーど
noun
card

Definitions

1. card (payment, membership, ID)
A small flat piece of plastic used for payment, identification, or membership.
2. card (playing, message)
A piece of cardboard used for games or sending messages.

Examples

クレジットカードで支払(しはら)う。
I pay with a credit card.
カードを使(つか)えますか。
Can I use a card?
Common question when paying
誕生日(たんじょうび)カードをもらった。
I received a birthday card.

Notes

カード is a versatile word used for many types of cards.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • クレジットカード: credit card
  • キャッシュカード: bank/ATM card
  • ポイントカード: points card, loyalty card
  • 会員(かいいん)カード: membership card
  • カード決済(けっさい): card payment
  • カードで払(はら)う: to pay by card

RELATED WORDS:

  • 札(ふだ): tag, card (traditional)
  • トランプ: playing cards
  • 名刺(めいし): business card
