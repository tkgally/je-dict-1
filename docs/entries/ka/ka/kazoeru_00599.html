数(かぞ)える

かぞえる
verb (ichidan)
to count

Definitions

1. to count, to enumerate
To determine the number of something.

Examples

数(かず)を数(かぞ)える。
To count the number.
指(ゆび)で数(かぞ)える。
To count on one's fingers.
数(かぞ)え切(き)れない。
Too many to count.

Notes

数(かぞ)える means to count.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 数(かぞ)え切(き)れない: countless, too many to count
  • 指折(ゆびお)り数(かぞ)える: to count on fingers (eagerly await)
  • 日(ひ)を数(かぞ)える: to count the days
