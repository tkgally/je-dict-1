囲(かこ)む

かこむ
verb (godan)
to surround, to encircle

Definitions

1. to surround, to encircle
To form a circle around something.
2. to sit around (a table)
To gather around something together.

Examples

敵(てき)を囲(かこ)む。
To surround the enemy.
食卓(しょくたく)を囲(かこ)む。
To gather around the dining table.
塀(へい)で囲(かこ)まれている。
It's surrounded by a fence.

Notes

囲(かこ)む means to surround.

COMMON USES:

  • 食卓(しょくたく)を囲(かこ)む: to gather at the table
  • 鍋(なべ)を囲(かこ)む: to sit around a hot pot

RELATED:

  • 囲(かこ)い: enclosure, fence
  • 囲(かこ)まれる: to be surrounded (passive)
