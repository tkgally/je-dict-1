改善(かいぜん)

かいぜん
noun, suru verb
improvement

Definitions

1. improvement, betterment
Making something better, often through continuous small changes.

Examples

生活(せいかつ)を改善(かいぜん)したい。
I want to improve my lifestyle.
品質(ひんしつ)の改善(かいぜん)に取(と)り組(く)む。
To work on quality improvement.
状況(じょうきょう)は改善(かいぜん)している。
The situation is improving.

Notes

改善(かいぜん) is famous worldwide as a Japanese business philosophy of continuous improvement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を改善(かいぜん)する (to improve ~)
  • 改善(かいぜん)の余地(よち) (room for improvement)
  • 改善(かいぜん)点(てん) (points to improve)

Related: 改良(かいりょう) (improvement of products/systems)
