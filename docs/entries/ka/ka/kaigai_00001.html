海外(かいがい)

かいがい
noun
overseas, abroad

Definitions

1. overseas, abroad, foreign countries
Countries outside of Japan, typically accessed by crossing the sea.

Examples

海外(かいがい)に行(い)ったことがありますか。
Have you ever been abroad?
海外(かいがい)で働(はたら)きたい。
I want to work overseas.
海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)は初(はじ)めてです。
This is my first trip abroad.

Notes

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう) (overseas travel)
  • 海外(かいがい)出張(しゅっちょう) (business trip abroad)
  • 海外(かいがい)留学(りゅうがく) (studying abroad)

Note: 外国(がいこく) also means 'foreign country' but 海外(かいがい) emphasizes the concept of 'across the sea.'
