齧(かじ)る

かじる
verb (godan)
to gnaw, to nibble

Definitions

1. to gnaw, to nibble, to bite
To bite or chew repeatedly at something with the front teeth.
2. to dabble in, to have superficial knowledge
To have only partial or superficial knowledge of something (figurative usage).

Examples

りんごを齧(かじ)る。
To bite into an apple.
ネズミが電線(でんせん)を齧(かじ)った。
A mouse gnawed on the electrical wire.
学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)に哲学(てつがく)を齧(かじ)った。
I dabbled in philosophy during my student days.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 齧(かじ)らない (negative)
  • 齧(かじ)ります (polite)
  • 齧(かじ)って (te-form)
  • 齧(かじ)った (past)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Sense 2 is often used with studies or skills: 英語(えいご)を齧(かじ)る (to have a smattering of English).
  • Often written in hiragana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜を齧(かじ)る (to nibble on ~)
  • 爪(つめ)を齧(かじ)る (to bite one's nails)
