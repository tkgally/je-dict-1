肩(かた)

かた
noun
shoulder

Definitions

1. shoulder
The part of the body between the neck and upper arm.

Examples

肩(かた)が凝(こ)っている。
My shoulders are stiff.
肩(かた)を叩(たた)く。
To pat someone on the shoulder.

Notes

肩(かた) means shoulder.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 肩(かた)が凝(こ)る: to have stiff shoulders
  • 肩(かた)を並(なら)べる: to stand shoulder to shoulder
  • 肩(かた)を持(も)つ: to take someone's side
  • 肩(かた)の荷(に)が下(お)りる: to feel relieved of a burden

COMPOUNDS:

  • 肩書(かたがき): title, position
  • 肩車(かたぐるま): riding on shoulders
