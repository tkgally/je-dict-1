加減(かげん)

かげん
noun / suru-verb
adjustment; degree, extent; health condition

Definitions

1. adjustment, moderation
The act of adjusting or moderating something; adding and subtracting.
2. degree, extent, state
The condition or state of something; how something is.
3. health condition
One's physical condition, especially when unwell.

Examples

塩(しお)加減(かげん)がちょうどいい。
The amount of salt is just right.
Sense 2: degree/amount
いい加減(かげん)にして!
Cut it out! / Give it a rest!
Expression: stop being unreasonable
加減(かげん)が悪(わる)い。
I'm not feeling well.
Sense 3: health

Notes

加減(かげん) has multiple related meanings around adjustment and condition.

SENSE 1 - ADJUSTMENT:

  • 〜を加減(かげん)する (to adjust ~)
  • 加減(かげん)乗除(じょうじょ) (the four arithmetic operations)

SENSE 2 - DEGREE:

  • 塩(しお)加減(かげん) (amount of salt)
  • 湯(ゆ)加減(かげん) (water temperature)
  • いい加減(かげん) (moderate; also: irresponsible)

SENSE 3 - HEALTH:

  • 加減(かげん)が悪(わる)い (not feeling well)
  • お加減(かげん)はいかがですか (How are you feeling?)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • いい加減(かげん)にしろ (Cut it out!)
  • いい加減(かげん)な (half-hearted, irresponsible)
