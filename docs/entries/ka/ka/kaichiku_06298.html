改築(かいちく)

かいちく
noun / suru-verb
renovation; rebuilding

Definitions

1. renovation, rebuilding, reconstruction
To significantly modify or rebuild a building's structure, often involving major changes to the layout or basic framework.

Examples

古民家(こみんか)を改築(かいちく)してカフェにした。
We renovated an old traditional house and turned it into a café.
駅(えき)の改築(かいちく)工事(こうじ)が進(すす)んでいる。
The station renovation work is progressing.
家(いえ)を改築(かいちく)するか建(た)て替(か)えるか迷(まよ)っている。
I'm deciding whether to renovate or completely rebuild the house.

Notes

USAGE: 改築(かいちく) refers to significant structural changes to a building. It differs from 改装(かいそう) (interior renovation) and 修繕(しゅうぜん) (repairs).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改築(かいちく)工事(こうじ) (renovation work)
  • 改築(かいちく)費用(ひよう) (renovation costs)
  • 家(いえ)を改築(かいちく)する (to renovate a house)

DISTINCTION FROM SIMILAR TERMS:

  • 新築(しんちく) - new construction (building from scratch)
  • 改築(かいちく) - renovation with major structural changes
  • 改装(かいそう) - interior renovation, remodeling
  • 増築(ぞうちく) - building an addition/extension
  • 修繕(しゅうぜん) - repairs, fixing damage
  • 建(た)て替(か)え - complete rebuild (tear down and build new)

Related Words

Related: 解体(かいたい) (demolition)
