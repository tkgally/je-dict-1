唐揚(からあ)げ

からあげ
noun
fried chicken, deep-fried food

Definitions

1. Japanese fried chicken
Bite-sized pieces of meat (usually chicken) marinated, coated with flour or starch, and deep-fried.

Examples

鶏(とり)の唐揚(からあ)げを作(つく)りました。
I made fried chicken.
唐揚(からあ)げにレモンを絞(しぼ)る。
Squeeze lemon on the fried chicken.
A common but sometimes controversial practice
居酒屋(いざかや)の定番(ていばん)メニューは唐揚(からあ)げだ。
Fried chicken is a standard izakaya menu item.

Notes

Also written as 空揚(からあ)げ or から揚(あ)げ. The 唐(から) refers to China/foreign style, though the dish is distinctly Japanese.

Preparation typically involves:
1. Marinating in 醤油(しょうゆ), 酒(さけ), 生姜(しょうが), にんにく
2. Coating with 片栗粉(かたくりこ) or 小麦粉(こむぎこ)
3. Deep-frying until golden and crispy

大分(おおいた)県(けん)中津(なかつ)市 is famous as a karaage town. The debate over whether to squeeze lemon on shared karaage (レモン{論争(ろんそう)}) is a common topic.

Similar: 竜田揚(たつたあ)げ (uses potato starch only, often with soy sauce marinade).
