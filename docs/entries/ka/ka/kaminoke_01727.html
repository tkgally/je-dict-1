髪(かみ)の毛(け)

かみのけ
noun
hair (on head)

Definitions

1. hair, head hair
The hair growing on a person's head. More specific than 髪 alone.

Examples

髪(かみ)の毛(け)を切(き)る。
To cut one's hair.
髪(かみ)の毛(け)が長(なが)い。
To have long hair.
床(ゆか)に髪(かみ)の毛(け)が落(お)ちている。
There's hair on the floor.

Notes

髪(かみ)の毛(け) specifically refers to hair on the head.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 髪(かみ)の毛(け)を切(き)る (to cut hair)
  • 髪(かみ)の毛(け)を洗(あら)う (to wash hair)
  • 髪(かみ)の毛(け)が抜(ぬ)ける (hair falls out)
  • 髪(かみ)の毛(け)が伸(の)びる (hair grows)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 髪(かみ): hair (general, also means hairstyle)
  • 髪(かみ)の毛(け): individual hairs, or hair as substance
  • 毛(け): hair, fur (body hair, animal fur)

COUNTER: 本(ほん) for individual strands

Related Words

Related: 髪(かみ) (hair (general))
