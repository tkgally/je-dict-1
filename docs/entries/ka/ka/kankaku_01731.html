間隔(かんかく)

かんかく
noun
interval, gap, spacing

Definitions

1. interval, gap, space
The distance or space between two things.
2. time interval, frequency
The period of time between events or actions.

Examples

席(せき)の間隔(かんかく)を空(あ)ける。
To leave space between seats.
5分(ふん)間隔(かんかく)でバスが来(く)る。
Buses come at 5-minute intervals.
等(とう)間隔(かんかく)に並(なら)べる。
To arrange at equal intervals.

Notes

間隔(かんかく) refers to both spatial and temporal intervals.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 間隔(かんかく)を空(あ)ける (to leave an interval/gap)
  • 〜間隔(かんかく)で (at intervals of ~)
  • 等(とう)間隔(かんかく) (equal intervals)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 時間(じかん)間隔(かんかく) (time interval)
  • 運転(うんてん)間隔(かんかく) (service interval, headway)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 間隔(かんかく): interval, gap (neutral)
  • 隙間(すきま): gap, crack (smaller, often unintended)
  • 距離(きょり): distance (between two points)
  • 感覚(かんかく): sense, sensation (different word, same reading)

Related Words

Contrast: 感覚(かんかく) (sense, sensation)
