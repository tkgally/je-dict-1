変(か)える

かえる
verb (ichidan)
to change, to alter

Definitions

1. to change, to alter
To make something different from what it was before. This is transitive - you change something.
2. to replace, to exchange
To substitute one thing for another.

Examples

予定(よてい)を変(か)えてもいいですか。
May I change the schedule?
話題(わだい)を変(か)えましょう。
Let's change the subject.
髪(かみ)の色(いろ)を変(か)えた。
I changed my hair color.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 変(か)わる (intransitive, to change by itself). Pattern: Xを変(か)える (change X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 変(か)えている = 'have changed (it).'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予定(よてい)を変(か)える (change the schedule)
  • 話題(わだい)を変(か)える (change the subject)
  • 色(いろ)を変(か)える (change the color)
  • 変(か)えられる (can be changed - potential)

PAIR: 変(か)わる (intransitive) - 季節(きせつ)が変(か)わった (season changed).
Different from 帰(かえ)る (go home - godan).
