解説(かいせつ)

かいせつ
noun, suru-verb
explanation, commentary

Definitions

1. explanation, commentary, exposition
A detailed explanation or analysis of something, often by an expert.

Examples

専門家(せんもんか)が試合(しあい)の解説(かいせつ)をした。
An expert provided commentary on the match.
この本(ほん)には詳(くわ)しい解説(かいせつ)がついている。
This book comes with detailed explanations.
ニュース(にゅーす)解説(かいせつ)者(しゃ)がわかりやすく説明(せつめい)してくれた。
The news commentator explained it in an easy-to-understand way.

Notes

解説(かいせつ) implies expert or authoritative explanation, often used in media, academic, and professional contexts.

Common collocations:

  • 解説(かいせつ)者(しゃ) (commentator)
  • 解説(かいせつ)書(しょ) (commentary, explanatory book)
  • 解説(かいせつ)を加(くわ)える (to add commentary)
  • 試合(しあい)の解説(かいせつ) (match commentary)

Related terms:

  • 説明(せつめい) (explanation - more general)
  • コメンタリー (commentary - loanword)
  • 注釈(ちゅうしゃく) (annotation, notes)
