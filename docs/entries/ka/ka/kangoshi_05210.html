看護師(かんごし)

かんごし
noun
nurse

Definitions

1. nurse
A licensed healthcare professional who provides medical care and support to patients in hospitals, clinics, and other healthcare settings.

Examples

看護師(かんごし)になるには国家試験(こっかしけん)に合格(ごうかく)しなければならない。
To become a nurse, you must pass the national exam.
看護師(かんごし)さんが毎日(まいにち)血圧(けつあつ)を測(はか)ってくれる。
The nurse measures my blood pressure every day.
彼女(かのじょ)は大学病院(だいがくびょういん)で看護師(かんごし)として働(はたら)いている。
She works as a nurse at a university hospital.

Notes

看護師(かんごし) is the modern, gender-neutral term for nurse that replaced the older terms 看護婦(かんごふ) (female nurse) and 看護士(かんごし) (male nurse) in 2002.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 看護師(かんごし)になる (to become a nurse)
  • 看護師(かんごし)として働(はたら)く (to work as a nurse)
  • 看護師(かんごし)を目指(めざ)す (to aim to become a nurse)
  • 正看護師(せいかんごし) (registered nurse)
  • 准看護師(じゅんかんごし) (practical nurse)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often addressed or referred to as 看護師(かんごし)さん when speaking to or about a nurse respectfully.

Related Words

Related: 看護婦(かんごふ) (dated: female nurse)
