貸(か)し

かし
noun
lending, loan, rental

Definitions

1. lending, loan
The act of lending something to someone; credit extended to others.
2. rental, for rent
Something available for rent or hire.

Examples

彼(かれ)に貸(か)しがある。
He owes me (I have a loan out to him).
Figurative: he owes me a favor
貸(か)し自転車(じてんしゃ)を借(か)りた。
I rented a rental bicycle.
この本(ほん)は貸(か)し出(だ)し中(ちゅう)です。
This book is currently on loan.

Notes

貸(か)し is the noun form of 貸(か)す (to lend). It indicates lending or something for rent.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 貸(か)しがある (to have a loan out; someone owes you)
  • 貸(か)し借(か)り (lending and borrowing)
  • 貸(か)し出(だ)し (lending out, circulation)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 貸(か)し自転車(じてんしゃ) (rental bicycle)
  • 貸(か)し会議室(かいぎしつ) (meeting room for rent)
  • 貸(か)し衣装(いしょう) (costume rental)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)り (private charter, reserved)

IDIOMATIC:

  • 貸(か)しを作(つく)る (to create a debt of favor)
  • 貸(か)し倒(だお)れ (bad debt)

Related Words

Pair verb: 貸(か)す (to lend (verb))
Antonym: 借(か)り (borrowing)
