感(かん)じ

かんじ
noun
feeling, impression

Definitions

1. feeling, sense, impression
A perception or sensation about something; the way something appears or seems.

Examples

いい感(かん)じですね。
It has a nice feel to it.
Very common casual expression
彼(かれ)は優(やさ)しい感(かん)じの人(ひと)だ。
He seems like a kind person.
どんな感(かん)じですか。
What's it like? / How does it feel?

Notes

感(かん)じ means feeling, impression, or sense. It comes from the verb 感(かん)じる (to feel).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いい感(かん)じ (nice feeling, looking good)
  • こんな感(かん)じ (like this, this kind of feeling)
  • どんな感(かん)じ？ (what's it like?)
  • 〜な感(かん)じ (seems like ~, has a ~ feel)

CASUAL USAGE:

  • いい感(かん)じ is very common in casual conversation to express approval
  • Often used vaguely: こんな感(かん)じでいい？(Is this okay?)

DIFFERENCE FROM 感情(かんじょう):

  • 感(かん)じ: impression, how something feels/seems
  • 感情(かんじょう): emotion (like anger, sadness)

NOTE: Not to be confused with 漢字(かんじ) (Chinese characters).

Related Words

Related: 感(かん)じる (to feel)
