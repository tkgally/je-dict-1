課(か)

か
suffix
lesson, section, department

Definitions

1. lesson, chapter
A numbered section in a textbook or course.
2. section, department
A division within an organization.

Examples

第(だい)5課(か)を勉強(べんきょう)しましょう。
Let's study Lesson 5.
営業(えいぎょう)課(か)で働(はたら)いている。
I work in the sales department.

Notes

Used both for textbook lessons and organizational sections. Related: 課長(かちょう) (section chief).
