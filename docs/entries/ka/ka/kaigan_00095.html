海岸(かいがん)

かいがん
noun
seashore, coast

Definitions

1. seashore, coast, beach
The area of land along the edge of the sea. The boundary between land and ocean.

Examples

海岸(かいがん)を歩(ある)きました。
I walked along the seashore.
夏(なつ)は海岸(かいがん)に行(い)きます。
I go to the beach in summer.
海岸(かいがん)から海(うみ)を見(み)ました。
I looked at the sea from the shore.

Notes

Combines 海(うみ) (sea) and 岸(きし) (shore/bank). Similar to but more general than 砂浜 (すなはま, sandy beach). Japan has extensive coastlines, and many cities are located along the 海岸(かいがん).
