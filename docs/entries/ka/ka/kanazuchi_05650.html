金槌(かなづち)

かなづち
noun
hammer

Definitions

1. hammer
A tool with a heavy metal head used for hitting nails.
2. person who can't swim
Colloquial term for someone who sinks like a hammer in water.

Examples

金槌(かなづち)で釘(くぎ)を打(う)つ。
To hit a nail with a hammer.
金槌(かなづち)を取(と)ってきて。
Go get the hammer.
彼(かれ)は金槌(かなづち)だから、プールに入(はい)らない。
He can't swim, so he won't go in the pool.

Notes

金槌(かなづち) means hammer, but also has a secondary meaning.

LITERAL MEANING:
A hammer for hitting nails or other objects.

IDIOMATIC MEANING:
A person who cannot swim - because they sink like a hammer in water.

ALTERNATIVE:

  • ハンマー: hammer (loanword)
