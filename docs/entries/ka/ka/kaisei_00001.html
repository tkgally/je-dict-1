快晴(かいせい)

かいせい
noun
clear weather, fine weather

Definitions

1. clear weather, cloudless sky
Weather condition with a completely clear, blue sky and no clouds.

Examples

今日(きょう)は快晴(かいせい)で気持(きも)ちがいい。
It's clear and beautiful weather today, which feels great.
運動会(うんどうかい)の日(ひ)は快晴(かいせい)だった。
The day of the sports festival was perfectly clear.
快晴(かいせい)の空(そら)の下(もと)でピクニックを楽(たの)しんだ。
We enjoyed a picnic under the clear blue sky.

Notes

快晴(かいせい) specifically refers to completely clear weather with no clouds, stronger than just 'good weather.'

Weather vocabulary hierarchy:

  • 快晴(かいせい) (perfectly clear, cloudless)
  • 晴(は)れ (sunny, fine weather - may have some clouds)
  • 曇(くも)り (cloudy)
  • 雨(あめ) (rain)

In weather forecasts, 快晴(かいせい) indicates cloud cover of 10% or less.
