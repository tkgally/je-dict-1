勝手(かって)

かって
noun
one's own convenience, selfish

Definitions

1. one's own convenience, selfish
Acting according to one's own will without considering others.

Examples

勝手(かって)なことをしないで。
Don't do as you please.
勝手(かって)に入(はい)らないで。
Don't enter without permission.
自分(じぶん)勝手(かって)だ。
Self-centered.

Notes

勝手(かって) expresses acting according to one's own will, often selfishly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 勝手(かって)に〜する (do ~ without permission)
  • 勝手(かって)な〜 (selfish ~)
  • 自分(じぶん)勝手(かって) (self-centered)
  • お好(す)きに勝手(かって)に (as you like)

MEANINGS:
1. Selfishness: 勝手(かって)な人(ひと) (selfish person)
2. Without permission: 勝手(かって)に入(はい)る (enter without permission)
3. Kitchen area (archaic): お勝手(かって)

Note: 勝手(かって)にしろ means "do what you want" (I don't care).
