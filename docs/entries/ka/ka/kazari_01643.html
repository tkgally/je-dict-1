飾(かざ)り

かざり
noun
decoration, ornament

Definitions

1. decoration, ornament, embellishment
An object or arrangement used to make something look more attractive.

Examples

クリスマスの飾(かざ)りを出(だ)した。
I took out the Christmas decorations.
部屋(へや)に花(はな)の飾(かざ)りがある。
There is a flower decoration in the room.
お正月(しょうがつ)の飾(かざ)りを買(か)いに行(い)った。
I went to buy New Year decorations.

Notes

飾(かざ)り is a noun meaning decoration or ornament. It comes from the verb 飾(かざ)る (to decorate).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飾(かざ)りをつける (to put up decorations)
  • 飾(かざ)りを外(はず)す (to take down decorations)
  • 飾(かざ)り棚(だな) (display shelf)
  • 飾(かざ)り窓(まど) (display window)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 髪飾(かみかざ)り (hair ornament)
  • 首飾(くびかざ)り (necklace)
  • 正月飾(しょうがつかざ)り (New Year decorations)

RELATED VERB:

  • 飾(かざ)る (to decorate, to display)
