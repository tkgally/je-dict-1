乾杯(かんぱい)

かんぱい
noun, suru-verb, interjection
cheers!, toast

Definitions

1. cheers!, toast
An exclamation said when raising glasses together; the act of making a toast.

Examples

みんなで「乾杯(かんぱい)！」と言(い)ってグラスを合(あ)わせた。
Everyone said 'Cheers!' and clinked glasses together.
新年(しんねん)を祝(いわ)って乾杯(かんぱい)しましょう。
Let's toast to the new year.
乾杯(かんぱい)の音頭(おんど)を取(と)っていただけますか。
Would you lead the toast?

Notes

乾杯(かんぱい) literally means 'dry cup' (drinking until the cup is empty).

Common expressions:

  • 乾杯(かんぱい)！ (Cheers! - exclamation)
  • 乾杯(かんぱい)する (to make a toast)
  • 乾杯(かんぱい)の音頭(おんど)を取(と)る (to lead the toast)
  • 〜に乾杯(かんぱい)！ (Cheers to ~!)

Cultural note:

  • In Japanese drinking culture, the 乾杯(かんぱい) marks the official start of drinking
  • Often led by a senior person or guest of honor
  • It's polite to wait for the 乾杯(かんぱい) before drinking

Alternatives:

  • お疲(つか)れ様(さま)！ (Good work! - casual work-related toast)
