歓迎(かんげい)

かんげい
noun / suru-verb
welcome, reception

Definitions

1. welcome, welcoming, reception
The act of greeting someone warmly upon their arrival; showing pleasure at someone's presence.

Examples

ご来店(らいてん)を歓迎(かんげい)します。
We welcome your visit.
Common formal greeting
歓迎(かんげい)会(かい)を開(ひら)く。
To hold a welcome party.
この決定(けってい)を歓迎(かんげい)する。
We welcome this decision.
Can also mean to approve of something

Notes

歓迎(かんげい) means welcome or reception. It expresses pleasure at someone's arrival or approval of something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 歓迎(かんげい)する (to welcome)
  • 歓迎(かんげい)会(かい) (welcome party)
  • 歓迎(かんげい)の言葉(ことば) (words of welcome)
  • 〜を歓迎(かんげい)する (to welcome ~, to approve of ~)

COMMON SIGNS:

  • ようこそ！歓迎(かんげい)します (Welcome!)
  • ご来店(らいてん)歓迎(かんげい) (Visitors welcome)

EXTENDED MEANING:

  • Can express approval or positive reception of ideas, decisions, etc.
  • 皆(みな)さんの参加(さんか)を歓迎(かんげい)します (We welcome everyone's participation)

ANTONYM:

  • 拒否(きょひ) (rejection)
