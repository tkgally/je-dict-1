蝸牛(かたつむり)

かたつむり
noun
snail

Definitions

1. snail
A soft-bodied mollusk that carries a spiral shell on its back. Snails move slowly and are associated with rainy weather in Japan.

Examples

雨上(あめあ)がりに蝸牛(かたつむり)が出(で)てきた。
A snail came out after the rain stopped.
蝸牛(かたつむり)が葉(は)っぱの上(うえ)をゆっくり進(すす)んでいる。
A snail is slowly crawling on a leaf.
子供(こども)たちは蝸牛(かたつむり)の殻(から)に触(さわ)って遊(あそ)んでいた。
The children were playing by touching the snail's shell.

Notes

蝸牛(かたつむり) refers to land snails (not sea snails).

Alternative forms:

  • カタツムリ (katakana, common in scientific contexts)
  • でんでん虫(むし) (colloquial/children's term)
  • 蝸牛(まいまい) (alternative reading, dialectal)

Common expressions:

  • 蝸牛(かたつむり)の歩(あゆ)み (snail's pace)
  • 蝸牛(かたつむり)の殻(から) (snail's shell)

Counter: 匹(ひき)

Cultural note:
The children's song 「でんでん虫(むし)むし蝸牛(かたつむり)」is famous in Japan. Snails are associated with the rainy season (梅雨(つゆ)) and are a symbol of early summer.
