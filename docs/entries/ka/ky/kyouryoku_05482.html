強力(きょうりょく)

きょうりょく
na-adjective, noun
powerful; strong

Definitions

1. powerful; strong; mighty; potent
Having great strength, force, or effectiveness.

Examples

強力(きょうりょく)な洗剤(せんざい)で汚(よご)れを落(お)とす。
To remove dirt with a powerful detergent.
強力(きょうりょく)なリーダーシップ(りーだーしっぷ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Strong leadership is needed.
これは強力(きょうりょく)な武器(ぶき)だ。
This is a powerful weapon.

Notes

強力(きょうりょく) is composed of 強(きょう) (strong) + 力(りょく) (power).

NOTE: Do not confuse with 協力(きょうりょく) (cooperation), which has the same reading but different kanji and meaning.

USAGE:

  • 強力(きょうりょく)な + noun: powerful/strong ~
  • Often used for physical force, effectiveness of products, or abstract strength

Contrast: 協力(きょうりょく) (cooperation (different kanji))
