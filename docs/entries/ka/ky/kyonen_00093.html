去年(きょねん)

きょねん
noun, adverb
last year

Definitions

1. last year
The year before this one; the previous year. Used to refer to events or states in the year that just passed.

Examples

去年(きょねん)日本(にほん)に行(い)きました。
I went to Japan last year.
去年(きょねん)の夏(なつ)はとても暑(あつ)かったです。
Last summer was very hot.
去年(きょねん)から日本語(にほんご)を習(なら)っています。
I've been learning Japanese since last year.

Notes

Part of a time reference series: 去年(きょねん) (last year), 今年(ことし) (this year), 来年(らいねん) (next year). Can be used with の to modify nouns: 去年(きょねん)の冬(ふゆ) (last winter). A more formal word for last year is 昨年(さくねん).
