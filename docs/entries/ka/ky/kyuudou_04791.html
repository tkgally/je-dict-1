弓道(きゅうどう)

きゅうどう
noun
Japanese archery

Definitions

1. kyudo, Japanese archery
A traditional Japanese martial art of archery, emphasizing form, breathing, and spiritual discipline over accuracy.

Examples

高校(こうこう)で弓道(きゅうどう)を始(はじ)めた。
I started kyudo in high school.
弓道(きゅうどう)は精神(せいしん)統一(とういつ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Mental focus is important in kyudo.
弓道(きゅうどう)場(じょう)で練習(れんしゅう)する。
I practice at the archery hall.

Notes

COMPOUND: 弓(きゅう) (bow) + 道(どう) (way, path)

EQUIPMENT:

  • 弓(ゆみ) (bow) - Japanese bows are asymmetric
  • 矢(や) (arrow)
  • 的(まと) (target)
  • 弓道着(きゅうどうぎ) (kyudo uniform)
  • 袴(はかま) (traditional pants)
  • ゆがけ (glove)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弓道(きゅうどう)をする (to do kyudo)
  • 弓道(きゅうどう)部(ぶ) (archery club)
  • 弓道(きゅうどう)場(じょう) (archery hall)
  • 的(まと)に当(あ)たる (to hit the target)
  • 射法(しゃほう)八節(はっせつ) (eight stages of shooting)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Kyudo emphasizes the beauty of form and spiritual discipline (射即人生 "shooting is life"). Unlike Western archery, the goal is perfection of character through practice.
