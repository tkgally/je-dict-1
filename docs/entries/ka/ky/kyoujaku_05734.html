強弱(きょうじゃく)

きょうじゃく
noun
strength and weakness; intensity

Definitions

1. strength and weakness
The contrast between strong and weak; varying degrees of power or force.
2. intensity; dynamics
Variation in loudness or force, especially in music.

Examples

音(おと)の強弱(きょうじゃく)をつける。
To add dynamics (loud and soft) to the sound.
電波(でんぱ)の強弱(きょうじゃく)によって受信(じゅしん)状態(じょうたい)が変(か)わる。
Reception quality changes depending on signal strength.
強弱(きょうじゃく)をつけて演奏(えんそう)する。
To play with varying dynamics.

Notes

強弱(きょうじゃく) combines 強(きょう) (strong) and 弱(じゃく) (weak). It's commonly used in music to describe dynamics.

MUSICAL CONTEXT:
In music, 強弱(きょうじゃく) corresponds to dynamics (forte, piano, etc.).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 強弱(きょうじゃく)をつける: to vary the intensity
  • 強弱(きょうじゃく)記号(きごう): dynamic markings
