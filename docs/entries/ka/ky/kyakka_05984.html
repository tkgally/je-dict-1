却下(きゃっか)

きゃっか
noun, suru verb
rejection; dismissal; denial

Definitions

1. rejection; dismissal; denial
The formal rejection or dismissal of a request, proposal, application, or legal claim. Often used in official or legal contexts.

Examples

申請(しんせい)が却下(きゃっか)された。
The application was rejected.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)は訴(うった)えを却下(きゃっか)した。
The court dismissed the lawsuit.
提案(ていあん)は即座(そくざ)に却下(きゃっか)された。
The proposal was immediately rejected.

Notes

却下(きゃっか) is a formal term often used in legal, bureaucratic, or business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 申請(しんせい)を却下(きゃっか)する: to reject an application
  • 訴(うった)えを却下(きゃっか)する: to dismiss a lawsuit
  • 却下(きゃっか)される: to be rejected

CONTRAST:

  • 承認(しょうにん): approval (opposite)
  • 棄却(ききゃく): dismissal (legal, similar but more technical)
