競争(きょうそう)

きょうそう
noun (verbal)
competition, contest

Definitions

1. competition, contest, rivalry
The act of competing against others for a goal or prize.

Examples

会社(かいしゃ)の間(あいだ)で競争(きょうそう)が激(はげ)しい。
Competition between companies is fierce.
友達(ともだち)と競争(きょうそう)して走(はし)った。
I raced against my friend.

Notes

競争(きょうそう)する is the verbal form meaning 'to compete'. Common collocations:

  • 競争(きょうそう)に勝(か)つ - to win a competition
  • 競争(きょうそう)に負(ま)ける - to lose a competition
  • 競争(きょうそう)相手(あいて) - competitor, rival
