拠点(きょてん)

きょてん
noun
base; hub; foothold

Definitions

1. base; hub; foothold; stronghold
A main location from which operations or activities are conducted; a strategic point.

Examples

海外(かいがい)拠点(きょてん)を増(ふ)やす。
To increase overseas bases.
この都市(とし)は物流(ぶつりゅう)の拠点(きょてん)だ。
This city is a logistics hub.
研究(けんきゅう)拠点(きょてん)を設立(せつりつ)する。
To establish a research base.

Notes

拠点(きょてん) is commonly used in business and military contexts for strategic locations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 活動(かつどう)拠点(きょてん): activity base
  • 生産(せいさん)拠点(きょてん): production base
  • 拠点(きょてん)を置(お)く: to base (oneself)
