極端的(きょくたんてき)

きょくたんてき
na-adjective
extreme, radical

Definitions

1. extreme, radical
Going to the furthest possible point; far from moderate or average.

Examples

極端的(きょくたんてき)な考(かんが)え方(かた)は避(さ)けた方(ほう)がいい。
It's better to avoid extreme ways of thinking.
極端的(きょくたんてき)な例(れい)を挙(あ)げてみよう。
Let me give an extreme example.
極端的(きょくたんてき)に言(い)えば、失敗(しっぱい)だった。
Put extremely, it was a failure.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
Formed from 極端(きょくたん) (extreme) + 的 (suffix making na-adjectives).

ADVERBIAL FORM:
極端的(きょくたんてき)に: extremely, radically

NOTE:
The base word 極端(きょくたん) is also commonly used on its own as a na-adjective or in the adverbial form 極端(きょくたん)に.
