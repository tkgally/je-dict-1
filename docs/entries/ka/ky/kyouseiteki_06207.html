強制的(きょうせいてき)

きょうせいてき
na-adjective
compulsory; forced

Definitions

1. compulsory; forced; coercive
Done by force or obligation, not by choice; requiring compliance regardless of personal preference.

Examples

強制的(きょうせいてき)な退去(たいきょ)を命(めい)じられた。
We were ordered to evacuate compulsorily.
強制的(きょうせいてき)に参加(さんか)させられた。
I was forced to participate.
強制的(きょうせいてき)な措置(そち)は避(さ)けたい。
I want to avoid coercive measures.

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 強制的(きょうせいてき)に (compulsorily, forcibly)

ETYMOLOGY:
強制(きょうせい) (coercion, compulsion) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 強制的(きょうせいてき)な措置(そち): coercive measures
  • 強制的(きょうせいてき)な執行(しっこう): forced execution
  • 強制的(きょうせいてき)に中止(ちゅうし)する: to forcibly stop

LEGAL/OFFICIAL CONTEXT:
Often appears in legal and administrative contexts where compliance is mandatory.

CONTRAST:

  • 自発的(じはつてき): voluntary, spontaneous (opposite)
  • 任意(にんい): optional, voluntary

Related Words

Antonym: 自発的(じはつてき) (voluntary)
