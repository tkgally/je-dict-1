兄弟(きょうだい)

きょうだい
noun
siblings, brothers

Definitions

1. siblings, brothers and sisters
A general term for siblings, which can include both brothers and sisters.
2. brothers
More specifically, male siblings or brothers.

Examples

兄弟(きょうだい)は何人(なんにん)いますか。
How many siblings do you have?
Common question about family
私(わたし)には兄弟(きょうだい)が三人(さんにん)います。
I have three siblings.
兄弟(きょうだい)で旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きました。
I went on a trip with my siblings.

Notes

兄弟(きょうだい) is written with the kanji for 'older brother' and 'younger brother,' but in modern usage, it typically refers to siblings in general, regardless of gender. When asking about siblings, Japanese people often use 兄弟(きょうだい)は何人(なんにん)いますか or ご兄弟(きょうだい)は. For sisters specifically, you can say 姉妹(しまい).
