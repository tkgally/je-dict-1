ゴルフ

ごるふ
noun
golf

Definitions

1. golf
A sport in which players use clubs to hit a ball into a series of holes on a course.

Examples

父(ちち)は週末(しゅうまつ)にゴルフをする。
My father plays golf on weekends.
ゴルフは接待(せったい)でよく使(つか)われる。
Golf is often used for business entertainment.
ゴルフ場(じょう)を予約(よやく)した。
I made a reservation at a golf course.

Notes

Golf is popular in Japan, especially among business professionals. It is often used for 接待(せったい)ゴルフ (business entertainment golf).

Common expressions:

  • ゴルフをする (to play golf)
  • ゴルフ場(じょう) (golf course)
  • ゴルフクラブ (golf club)
  • 打(う)ちっ放(ぱな)し (driving range)
