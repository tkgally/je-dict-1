ご馳走

ごちそう
noun
feast; treat; delicious food

Definitions

1. feast; delicious food
Special, elaborate, or particularly good food.
2. treat; treating someone
The act of paying for someone's meal.

Examples

ごちそうさまでした。
Thank you for the meal.
Said after eating
これは本当(ほんとう)のごちそうですね。
This is a real feast.
今日(きょう)は私(わたし)がごちそうします。
Today is my treat.
Offering to pay

Notes

ごちそう combines the honorific ご with 馳走 (originally meaning 'running around' to prepare a meal).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ごちそうさまでした: thank you for the meal (after eating)
  • ごちそうする: to treat someone to a meal
  • ごちそうになる: to be treated to a meal

USAGE:

  • Used both as a noun (delicious food) and with する (to treat someone)
