〜号(ごう)

ごう
suffix, counter
number; issue (counter)

Definitions

1. number (in a series)
Used to indicate a numbered item in a series, such as train names, magazine issues, or room numbers.
2. name of a ship/train
The suffix used in naming vessels, trains, and other vehicles.

Examples

新幹線(しんかんせん)のぞみ3号(さんごう)に乗(の)る。
To ride the Nozomi No. 3 Shinkansen.
今月(こんげつ)号(ごう)の雑誌(ざっし)を買(か)った。
I bought this month's issue of the magazine.
会議(かいぎ)室(しつ)5号(ごごう)で待(ま)っています。
I'm waiting in meeting room No. 5.

Notes

COUNTING:

  • 1号(いちごう) (number 1)
  • 2号(にごう) (number 2)
  • 3号(さんごう) (number 3)
  • 何号(なんごう) (what number)

COMMON USES:

  • Train/bus names: のぞみ1号(いちごう)
  • Magazine issues: 5月号(ごがつごう)
  • Room numbers: 3号室(さんごうしつ)
  • Ships: タイタニック号(ごう)
  • Spacecraft: アポロ11号(じゅういちごう)

NOTE: Different from 〜番(ばん) (number/turn) - 号(ごう) is for named series, 番(ばん) for ordered sequences.

Related Words

Contrast: 〜番(ばん) (number/turn)
