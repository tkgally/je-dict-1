ゴール

ごーる
noun
goal

Definitions

1. goal (in sports)
The scoring area in sports like soccer, or the act of scoring.
2. goal, finish line
The end point of a race or a journey.
3. goal, objective
An aim or desired outcome that one works toward.

Examples

サッカーでゴールを決(き)めた。
I scored a goal in soccer.
マラソンのゴールに到着(とうちゃく)した。
I arrived at the marathon finish line.
人生(じんせい)のゴールは何(なん)ですか。
What is your goal in life?

Notes

ゴール is used for both sports goals and life objectives.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ゴールを決(き)める: to score a goal
  • ゴールを入(い)れる: to score a goal
  • ゴールイン: reaching the goal/finish line
  • ゴールキーパー: goalkeeper
  • ゴールを目指(めざ)す: to aim for a goal

USAGE NOTE:
For "goal" meaning objective, 目標(もくひょう) is often used in formal contexts, while ゴール is more casual.
