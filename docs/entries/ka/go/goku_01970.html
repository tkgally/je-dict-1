語句(ごく)

ごく
noun
words, phrases, wording

Definitions

1. words, phrases, wording
Individual words or short expressions in a text.

Examples

難(むずか)しい語句(ごく)
Difficult words.
語句(ごく)の意味(いみ)を調(しら)べる。
To look up the meaning of words.
語句(ごく)を覚(おぼ)える。
To memorize vocabulary.
論文(ろんぶん)を書(か)く際(さい)は、適切(てきせつ)な語句(ごく)を選(えら)ぶことが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
When writing a paper, it's important to choose appropriate wording.

Notes

語句(ごく) refers to words and phrases.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 語句(ごく)の意味(いみ) (meaning of words)
  • 語句(ごく)を調(しら)べる (look up words)
  • 語句(ごく)説明(せつめい) (word definitions)

NOTE: Different from ごく (極 - extremely).

SIMILAR:

  • 語句(ごく): words and phrases
  • 単語(たんご): word (individual)
  • 語彙(ごい): vocabulary (collection)
  • 表現(ひょうげん): expression
