頃(ごろ)

ごろ
suffix
around (time)

Definitions

1. around, about (time)
A suffix attached to time expressions to indicate an approximate time.

Examples

三時(さんじ)頃(ごろ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet around three o'clock.
何時(なんじ)頃(ごろ)着(つ)きますか。
Around what time will you arrive?
昼(ひる)頃(ごろ)電話(でんわ)します。
I'll call around noon.

Notes

頃(ごろ) is added directly after a time expression without a particle. It makes the time approximate: 七時(しちじ)頃(ごろ) (around 7 o'clock), 来週(らいしゅう)頃(ごろ) (around next week). Compare with {ぐらい} which can also mean 'about' but is used differently.
