強盗(ごうとう)

ごうとう
noun
robbery, robber

Definitions

1. robbery, burglary
The crime of stealing with the use of force or threats.
2. robber, burglar
A person who commits robbery.

Examples

強盗(ごうとう)に遭(あ)う。
To be robbed.
強盗(ごうとう)が銀行(ぎんこう)に入(はい)った。
A robber entered the bank.
強盗(ごうとう)事件(じけん)
A robbery case.
深夜(しんや)にコンビニで強盗(ごうとう)事件(じけん)が発生(はっせい)し、犯人(はんにん)は現金(げんきん)を奪(うば)って逃走(とうそう)した。
A robbery occurred at a convenience store late at night, and the perpetrator fled after stealing cash.

Notes

強盗(ごうとう) refers to robbery or robbers.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 強盗(ごうとう)に遭(あ)う (to be robbed)
  • 強盗(ごうとう)事件(じけん) (robbery case)
  • 強盗(ごうとう)犯(はん) (robbery offender)
  • 銀行(ぎんこう)強盗(ごうとう) (bank robbery)

RELATED CRIMES:

  • 窃盗(せっとう) (theft - without violence)
  • 空(あ)き巣(す) (burglary of empty house)
  • 万引(まんび)き (shoplifting)
