ぐらい

ぐらい
particle
about, approximately

Definitions

1. about, approximately
Indicates an approximate quantity, amount, or degree.

Examples

30分(ぷん)ぐらいかかります。
It takes about 30 minutes.
千円(せんえん)ぐらいです。
It's about 1000 yen.
どのぐらい時間(じかん)がかかりますか。
About how long does it take?

Notes

ぐらい indicates approximation.

VARIANT:

  • くらい (same meaning, interchangeable)

PATTERNS:

  • number + ぐらい (about ~)
  • どのぐらい (about how much/long)
  • これぐらい (about this much)

USAGE: Can follow numbers, quantity expressions, and demonstratives.
