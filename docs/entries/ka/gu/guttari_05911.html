ぐったり

ぐったり
adverb, verb (suru)
exhausted, limp, worn out

Definitions

1. exhausted, limp
Completely drained of energy, often to the point of being unable to move. Describes a state of total physical or mental exhaustion.

Examples

仕事(しごと)の後(あと)でぐったりしている。
I'm exhausted after work.
猫(ねこ)が暑(あつ)さでぐったりしていた。
The cat was lying limp from the heat.
マラソンを走(はし)った後(あと)、ぐったりと倒(たお)れ込(こ)んだ。
After running the marathon, I collapsed in exhaustion.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes complete exhaustion where one has no energy left. Often used for people or animals who are so tired they can barely move. Can also describe wilting plants.

CONTRAST:

  • へとへと: tired but still able to move
  • くたくた: worn out, exhausted (similar meaning)
