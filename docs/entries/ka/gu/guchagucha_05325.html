ぐちゃぐちゃ

ぐちゃぐちゃ
adverb
messy; chaotic; jumbled

Definitions

1. messy; chaotic; in disarray
Describes something that is disorganized, jumbled, or in complete disorder.
2. mushy; squashed
Describes something that has been crushed or mixed into a messy state.

Examples

部屋(へや)がぐちゃぐちゃだ。
The room is a mess.
カバンの中身(なかみ)がぐちゃぐちゃになった。
The contents of my bag got all jumbled up.
ケーキがぐちゃぐちゃに潰(つぶ)れた。
The cake got squashed into a mess.

Notes

ぐちゃぐちゃ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) expressing messiness or disorder.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぐちゃぐちゃになる: to become messy
  • ぐちゃぐちゃにする: to make a mess of
  • ぐちゃぐちゃの〜: messy (noun modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • めちゃくちゃ: messed up, destroyed (more extreme)
  • ばらばら: scattered, disjointed
