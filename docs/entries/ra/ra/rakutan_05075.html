落胆(らくたん)

らくたん
noun, verb (suru)
disappointment, dejection

Definitions

1. disappointment, dejection, discouragement
Loss of hope or enthusiasm; being discouraged by failure.

Examples

結果(けっか)に落胆(らくたん)した。
I was disappointed by the results.
落胆(らくたん)を隠(かく)せなかった。
I couldn't hide my disappointment.
落胆(らくたん)している場合(ばあい)ではない。
This is not the time to be dejected.

Notes

More formal than がっかり (disappointed). 落(らく) (fall) + 胆(たん) (courage/spirit). Often used in written language and news reports.

Related Words

Synonym: がっかり (colloquial)
