欄(らん)

らん
noun
column, section, field (in a form)

Definitions

1. column (in newspaper or publication)
A vertical section of a newspaper, magazine, or webpage.
2. field, box (in a form)
A designated space on a form for entering information.

Examples

新聞(しんぶん)のスポーツ欄(らん)を読(よ)む。
Read the sports section of the newspaper.
住所(じゅうしょ)欄(らん)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the address field.
コメント欄(らん)に意見(いけん)を書(か)いた。
I wrote my opinion in the comments section.

Notes

欄(らん) refers to designated sections or columns, whether in print media or on forms.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 住所(じゅうしょ)欄(らん) (address field)
  • 氏名(しめい)欄(らん) (name field)
  • 備考(びこう)欄(らん) (remarks column)
  • コメント欄(らん) (comments section)
  • 記入(きにゅう)欄(らん) (entry field)
  • 投書(とうしょ)欄(らん) (letters to the editor column)
