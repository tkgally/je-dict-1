来年(らいねん)

らいねん
noun, adverb
next year

Definitions

1. next year
The year after this one; the coming year. Used to refer to future events or plans in the year that follows the current one.

Examples

来年(らいねん)日本(にほん)に行(い)きたいです。
I want to go to Japan next year.
来年(らいねん)の春(はる)に結婚(けっこん)します。
I'm getting married next spring.
来年(らいねん)までに日本語(にほんご)を上手(じょうず)になりたい。
I want to become good at Japanese by next year.

Notes

Part of a time reference series: 去年(きょねん) (last year), 今年(ことし) (this year), 来年(らいねん) (next year). The 来(らい) prefix means 'coming' or 'next' and appears in other time words like 来週(らいしゅう) (next week) and 来月(らいげつ) (next month).
