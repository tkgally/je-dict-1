六月(ろくがつ)

ろくがつ
noun
June

Definitions

1. June
The sixth month of the year. Known as the rainy season (梅雨, tsuyu) in most of Japan.

Examples

六月(ろくがつ)は雨(あめ)が多(おお)いです。
There's a lot of rain in June.
六月(ろくがつ)に梅雨(つゆ)が始(はじ)まります。
The rainy season starts in June.
六月(ろくがつ)は結婚式(けっこんしき)が多(おお)いです。
There are many weddings in June.
June bride tradition from the West

Notes

June is literally 'month six.' Most of Japan (except Hokkaido) experiences the rainy season (梅雨, tsuyu) in June. Despite the rain, 'June bride' (ジューンブライド) is popular due to Western influence.
