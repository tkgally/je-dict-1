レジ

れじ
noun
cash register

Definitions

1. cash register, checkout counter
The machine used to process payments in stores, or the checkout area where payments are made. Short for 'register'.

Examples

レジで払(はら)ってください。
Please pay at the register.
レジに並(なら)んでください。
Please line up at the checkout.
レジが混(こ)んでいます。
The checkout is crowded.

Notes

Short for レジスター (register), borrowed from English. Common in everyday Japanese when shopping. At convenience stores and supermarkets, you'll often hear レジ袋 (shopping bag) at checkout.
