列(れつ)

れつ
noun
row, line, queue

Definitions

1. row, line
A series of objects or people arranged in a line.
2. queue
A line of people waiting for something.

Examples

列(れつ)に並(なら)ぶ。
To stand in line.
長(なが)い列(れつ)ができた。
A long line formed.

Notes

列(れつ) refers to rows and lines.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 行列(ぎょうれつ): procession, queue
  • 列車(れっしゃ): train
  • 整列(せいれつ): lining up
  • 前列(ぜんれつ): front row
  • 後列(こうれつ): back row

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 列(れつ)を作(つく)る: to form a line
  • 列(れつ)を乱(みだ)す: to break ranks
