零(れい)

れい
number
zero

Definitions

1. zero
The number zero. Used in formal contexts such as phone numbers, addresses, and mathematics.

Examples

電話番号(でんわばんごう)は零(れい)三(さん)の零(れい)零(れい)零(れい)零(れい)です。
The phone number is 03-0000.
Formal reading of phone numbers
気温(きおん)は零(れい)度(ど)です。
The temperature is zero degrees.
零(れい)点(てん)五(ご)
0.5 (zero point five)
Decimal numbers

Notes

零(れい) is the formal Japanese word for zero. In casual speech, ゼロ (from English 'zero') is more commonly used. When reading phone numbers or addresses formally, 零(れい) is preferred. In some contexts, 〇(まる) (circle) is also used for zero.
