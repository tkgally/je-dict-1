冷房(れいぼう)

れいぼう
noun
air conditioning

Definitions

1. air conditioning, cooling
A system that cools the air in a room or building.

Examples

冷房(れいぼう)をつけてください。
Please turn on the air conditioning.
冷房(れいぼう)が効(き)きすぎて寒(さむ)い。
The air conditioning is too strong and it's cold.
冷房(れいぼう)の温度(おんど)を上(あ)げてもらえますか。
Could you raise the air conditioning temperature?

Notes

冷房(れいぼう) means cooling/air conditioning.

OPPOSITE: 暖房(だんぼう) (heating)

COMMON VERBS:

  • 冷房(れいぼう)をつける: to turn on AC
  • 冷房(れいぼう)を消(け)す: to turn off AC
  • 冷房(れいぼう)が効(き)く: AC is effective

RELATED TERMS:

  • エアコン: air conditioner
  • クーラー: cooler, AC unit
  • 空調(くうちょう): climate control

Related Words

Antonym: 暖房(だんぼう) (heating)
