礼(れい)

れい
noun
bow, thanks, manners

Definitions

1. bow
A gesture of respect made by bending the body.
2. thanks, gratitude
An expression of appreciation.
3. manners, etiquette
Proper behavior and customs.

Examples

礼(れい)をする。
To bow.
お礼(れい)を言(い)う。
To express thanks.

Notes

礼(れい) covers bowing and gratitude.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • お礼(れい): thanks, reward
  • 礼(れい)を言(い)う: to thank
  • 礼(れい)を尽(つ)くす: to show proper respect

COMPOUNDS:

  • 礼儀(れいぎ): manners, courtesy
  • 失礼(しつれい): rudeness, excuse me
  • 敬礼(けいれい): salute
  • 無礼(ぶれい): rudeness
