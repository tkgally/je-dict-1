老人(ろうじん)

ろうじん
noun
elderly person, old person

Definitions

1. elderly person, old person, senior citizen
A person of advanced age; typically refers to those in their later years of life.

Examples

老人(ろうじん)に席(せき)を譲(ゆず)った。
I gave up my seat to an elderly person.
老人(ろうじん)ホームで働(はたら)いている。
I work at a nursing home.
老人ホーム = nursing home, retirement home
日本(にほん)は老人(ろうじん)人口(じんこう)が増(ふ)えている。
Japan's elderly population is increasing.

Notes

USAGE: 老人(ろうじん) is a neutral term for elderly people. In more polite or formal contexts, 高齢者(こうれいしゃ) (senior citizen) or お年寄(としよ)り (elderly person, polite) may be preferred.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 老人(ろうじん)ホーム (nursing home)
  • 老人(ろうじん)福祉(ふくし) (elderly welfare)
  • 老人(ろうじん)介護(かいご) (elderly care)

CONTRAST:

  • 老人(ろうじん) - neutral, somewhat clinical
  • お年寄(としよ)り - polite, softer
  • 高齢者(こうれいしゃ) - formal, administrative term

Related Words

Related: 年寄(としよ)り (polite form)
