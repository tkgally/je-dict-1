留年(りゅうねん)

りゅうねん
noun
repeating a year (in school)

Definitions

1. repeating a year (in school), being held back a grade
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

単位(たんい)が足(た)りなくて留年(りゅうねん)した。
I had to repeat a year because I didn't have enough credits.
留年(りゅうねん)しないように頑張(がんば)っている。
I am working hard so I don't have to repeat a year.
留年(りゅうねん)すると学費(がくひ)が増(ふ)える。
If you repeat a year, tuition increases.

Notes

留年(りゅうねん) means staying in the same grade for an additional year, typically due to poor grades or insufficient credits.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 留年(りゅうねん)する (to repeat a year)
  • 留年(りゅうねん)が決(き)まる (to have it decided that one repeats)
  • 留年(りゅうねん)を回避(かいひ)する (to avoid repeating a year)
  • 留年(りゅうねん)生(せい) (student who is repeating)

CAUSES:

  • 単位(たんい)不足(ふそく) (insufficient credits)
  • 出席(しゅっせき)日数(にっすう)不足(ふそく) (insufficient attendance)
  • 成績(せいせき)不良(ふりょう) (poor grades)

ALTERNATIVE TERM:

  • 落第(らくだい) - similar meaning but sounds more negative

CONTRAST:

  • 浪人(ろうにん): Gap year before entering university (to retake entrance exams)
  • 留年(りゅうねん): Repeating a year while enrolled
