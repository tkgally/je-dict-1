料金(りょうきん)

りょうきん
noun
fee, charge, fare

Definitions

1. fee, charge, fare, rate
Money charged for a service or use of something.

Examples

入場(にゅうじょう)料金(りょうきん)はいくらですか。
How much is the admission fee?
電話(でんわ)料金(りょうきん)が高(たか)すぎる。
The phone charges are too high.
料金(りょうきん)を支払(しはら)う。
To pay a fee.

Notes

USAGE: 料金(りょうきん) refers to charges for services, utilities, or usage fees. It's commonly combined with the type of service.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 入場(にゅうじょう)料金(りょうきん) (admission fee)
  • 電話(でんわ)料金(りょうきん) (phone charges)
  • 駐車(ちゅうしゃ)料金(りょうきん) (parking fee)
  • 基本(きほん)料金(りょうきん) (basic fee)
  • 追加(ついか)料金(りょうきん) (additional charge)
  • 料金(りょうきん)表(ひょう) (rate table, price list)

CONTRAST:

  • 料金(りょうきん) - fee, charge (for services)
  • 代金(だいきん) - price, payment (for goods)
  • 費用(ひよう) - cost, expense (general)
  • 運賃(うんちん) - fare (transportation)
