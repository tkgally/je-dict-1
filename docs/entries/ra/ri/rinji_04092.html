臨時(りんじ)

りんじ
noun, no-adjective
temporary, special, extraordinary

Definitions

1. temporary, provisional
Not permanent; lasting for only a limited time.
2. special, extraordinary, extra
Outside the regular schedule or arrangement; arranged specially for a particular occasion.

Examples

臨時(りんじ)休業(きゅうぎょう)のお知(し)らせです。
This is a notice of temporary closure.
臨時(りんじ)ニュースをお伝(つた)えします。
We bring you a special news bulletin.
台風(たいふう)のため臨時(りんじ)列車(れっしゃ)が運行(うんこう)された。
Extra trains were operated due to the typhoon.

Notes

USAGE: 臨時(りんじ) functions as a prefix-like modifier and combines with many nouns.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 臨時(りんじ)休業(きゅうぎょう) (temporary closure)
  • 臨時(りんじ)ニュース (special news bulletin)
  • 臨時(りんじ)列車(れっしゃ) (extra train)
  • 臨時(りんじ)収入(しゅうにゅう) (unexpected income)
  • 臨時(りんじ)国会(こっかい) (extraordinary Diet session)
  • 臨時(りんじ)雇用(こよう) (temporary employment)
  • 臨時(りんじ)職員(しょくいん) (temporary staff)

RELATED:

  • 一時的(いちじてき) - temporary (adjectival)
  • 特別(とくべつ) - special
