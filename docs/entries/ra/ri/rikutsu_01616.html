理屈(りくつ)

りくつ
noun
logic, reason

Definitions

1. logic, reasoning, theory
The principles or reasoning behind something.
2. excuse, argument (negative connotation)
Used negatively to describe someone who argues too much or makes excuses.

Examples

理屈(りくつ)は分(わ)かるけど...。
I understand the logic, but...
理屈(りくつ)を言(い)うな。
Don't argue. / Don't make excuses.
Negative usage
理屈(りくつ)っぽい人(ひと)だ。
They're an argumentative person.

Notes

Can be neutral (logic/reasoning) or negative (excessive argumentation).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 理屈(りくつ)を言(い)う (to argue, make excuses)
  • 理屈(りくつ)っぽい (argumentative, nitpicky)
  • 理屈(りくつ)に合(あ)う (make sense, be logical)
  • 理屈(りくつ)では (in theory)
  • 理屈(りくつ)抜|ぬ}きで (without reasoning, intuitively)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 論理(ろんり) - logic (more formal/academic)
