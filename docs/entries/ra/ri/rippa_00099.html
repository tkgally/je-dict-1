立派(りっぱ)

りっぱ
na-adjective
splendid, fine, admirable

Definitions

1. splendid, magnificent, impressive
Describes something grand, impressive in appearance or quality.
2. admirable, respectable, fine
Worthy of respect or praise; describes people or their achievements.

Examples

立派(りっぱ)な建物(たてもの)ですね。
What a splendid building!
彼(かれ)は立派(りっぱ)な人(ひと)になりました。
He became a fine person.
立派(りっぱ)に仕事(しごと)をしています。
You're doing a fine job.
Adverbial use

Notes

立派 is a na-adjective: 立派な (before nouns), 立派だ/です (predicate), 立派に (admirably, adverb). Can describe physical appearance (magnificent building), character (respectable person), or achievements (fine work). Often used as praise or encouragement.
