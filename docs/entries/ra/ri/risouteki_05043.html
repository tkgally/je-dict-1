理想的(りそうてき)

りそうてき
na-adjective
ideal

Definitions

1. ideal, perfect
Satisfying one's conception of what is best; most suitable.

Examples

理想的(りそうてき)な環境(かんきょう)で働(はたら)きたい。
I want to work in an ideal environment.
これが理想的(りそうてき)な解決策(かいけつさく)です。
This is the ideal solution.
理想的(りそうてき)には、毎日(まいにち)運動(うんどう)したい。
Ideally, I'd like to exercise every day.

Notes

理想(りそう) means 'ideal' or 'dream.' Can describe something that is perfect or something that may be unrealistic. 理想的(りそうてき)には (ideally) is common as an adverb.

Related Words

Contrast: 現実的(げんじつてき) (realistic)
