臨機応変(りんきおうへん)

りんきおうへん
na-adjective, noun
adaptable, flexible

Definitions

1. adaptable, flexible, playing by ear
Responding appropriately to circumstances as they arise, adapting to situations.

Examples

臨機応変(りんきおうへん)に対応(たいおう)してください。
Please respond flexibly to the situation.
彼(かれ)は臨機応変(りんきおうへん)な人(ひと)だ。
He is an adaptable person.
計画(けいかく)はあるが、臨機応変(りんきおうへん)に変更(へんこう)する。
There's a plan, but we'll adjust it as needed.

Notes

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語):
臨機(りんき) means "facing the occasion" and 応変(おうへん) means "responding to change." Together they describe the ability to adapt to changing circumstances.

COMMON USAGE:

  • Valued as a positive trait in Japanese business culture
  • Often used when discussing problem-solving or crisis management
