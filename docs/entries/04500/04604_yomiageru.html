読(よ)み上(あ)げる

よみあげる
ichidan verb
to read aloud, to read out
1. to read aloud, to read out
To read text out loud so that others can hear, often in a formal or official context.
先生(せんせい)が名簿(めいぼ)を読(よ)み上(あ)げた。
The teacher read out the roll call.
契約書(けいやくしょ)の内容(ないよう)を声(こえ)に出(だ)して読(よ)み上(あ)げてください。
Please read the contents of the contract out loud.
2. to recite, to announce
To formally announce or recite something, such as names or results.
受賞者(じゅしょうしゃ)の名前(なまえ)が読(よ)み上(あ)げられた。
The names of the award winners were announced.

読(よ)み上(あ)げる is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to read aloud or announce.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 読(よ)む (to read) + 上(あ)げる (to raise, to complete) → to read by raising one's voice

ASPECT (ている):
読(よ)み上(あ)げている indicates an ongoing action of reading aloud.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 名前(なまえ)を読(よ)み上(あ)げる (read out names)
  • 文章(ぶんしょう)を読(よ)み上(あ)げる (read a text aloud)
  • 声(こえ)に出(だ)して読(よ)み上(あ)げる (read out loud)

DIGITAL CONTEXT:

  • 読(よ)み上(あ)げ機能(きのう): text-to-speech function
  • 音声(おんせい)読(よ)み上(あ)げ: audio reading/text-to-speech

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 声(こえ)に出(だ)して読(よ)む: to read out loud (more casual)
  • 読(よ)み上(あ)げる is more formal, often used in official announcements
