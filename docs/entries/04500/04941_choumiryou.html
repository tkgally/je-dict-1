調味料(ちょうみりょう)

ちょうみりょう
noun
seasoning, condiment
1. seasoning, condiment
Substances added to food to enhance or modify its flavor, such as salt, soy sauce, or sugar.
基本(きほん)の調味料(ちょうみりょう)は「さしすせそ」です。
The basic seasonings are 'sa-shi-su-se-so'.
Mnemonic for sugar, salt, vinegar, soy sauce, miso
調味料(ちょうみりょう)を加(くわ)えて味(あじ)を調(ととの)える。
Add seasonings to adjust the taste.
調味料(ちょうみりょう)の入(い)れすぎに注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Be careful not to add too much seasoning.

The traditional Japanese seasoning order 「さしすせそ」:

  • さ = 砂糖(さとう) (sugar) - add first
  • し = 塩(しお) (salt)
  • す = 酢(す) (vinegar)
  • せ = 醤油(しょうゆ) (soy sauce)
  • そ = 味噌(みそ) (miso) - add last

This order is based on molecule size and how flavors penetrate food. Sugar's larger molecules need more time to absorb.

Other common 調味料(ちょうみりょう): みりん, 料理酒(りょうりしゅ), だしの素(もと), ソース, マヨネーズ.
