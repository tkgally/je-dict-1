胡麻油(ごまあぶら)

ごまあぶら
noun
sesame oil
1. sesame oil
Oil extracted from sesame seeds, used for flavoring and cooking in Asian cuisine.
仕上(しあ)げに胡麻油(ごまあぶら)を回(まわ)しかける。
Drizzle sesame oil over the dish as a finishing touch.
回しかける describes drizzling in a circular motion
胡麻油(ごまあぶら)の香(かお)りが食欲(しょくよく)をそそる。
The aroma of sesame oil stimulates the appetite.
中華料理(ちゅうかりょうり)には胡麻油(ごまあぶら)がよく使(つか)われる。
Sesame oil is commonly used in Chinese cuisine.

胡麻油(ごまあぶら) is oil extracted from sesame seeds, widely used in East Asian cooking. Also commonly written as ごま油(あぶら) or ゴマ油(あぶら).

TYPES:

  • 焙煎(ばいせん)ごま油(あぶら): roasted sesame oil — dark brown, strong nutty aroma, used as a finishing flavoring
  • 太白(たいはく)ごま油(あぶら): unroasted sesame oil — light, mild, used for general cooking and frying

USAGE:
The roasted variety is most common in Japanese home cooking. It is typically drizzled over dishes at the end of cooking to preserve its aroma (香(かお)り). A key seasoning in Chinese (中華料理(ちゅうかりょうり)) and Korean (韓国料理(かんこくりょうり)) cuisine as well.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 胡麻油(ごまあぶら)で炒(いた)める: to stir-fry in sesame oil
  • 胡麻油(ごまあぶら)を回(まわ)しかける: to drizzle sesame oil (in a circular motion)
