介護(かいご)

かいご
noun (verbal)
nursing care
1. nursing care, caregiving
Care provided to elderly or disabled people who need assistance with daily activities.
両親(りょうしん)の介護(かいご)をしている。
I am caring for my parents.
介護(かいご)サービスを利用(りよう)する。
Use nursing care services.
介護(かいご)施設(しせつ)に入(はい)る。
Enter a nursing care facility.

介護(かいご) refers to the care and assistance provided to elderly, disabled, or ill people who cannot fully manage daily activities on their own. This is a critically important concept in Japan due to its rapidly aging population.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 介護(かいご)する: to provide care
  • 介護(かいご)保険(ほけん): long-term care insurance (a national system in Japan)
  • 介護(かいご)施設(しせつ): care facility, nursing home
  • 介護(かいご)離職(りしょく): quitting work to care for a family member
  • 在宅(ざいたく)介護(かいご): home care
  • 介護(かいご)福祉(ふくし)士(し): certified care worker
  • 要(よう)介護(かいご): requiring nursing care (official designation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 看護(かんご): nursing (medical care, typically by trained nurses)
  • 世話(せわ): care, looking after (more general, less formal)
  • 福祉(ふくし): welfare (broader social welfare concept)

SOCIAL CONTEXT:
Japan's 介護(かいご)保険(ほけん)制度(せいど) (long-term care insurance system), introduced in 2000, requires citizens over 40 to pay premiums. 介護(かいご)離職(りしょく) (people leaving jobs to care for aging parents) is a growing social issue.
