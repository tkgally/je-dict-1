ベスト

べすと
noun
vest; best
1. vest; waistcoat
A sleeveless garment worn over a shirt, typically as part of formal attire or for warmth.
スーツにベストを合(あ)わせる。
Wear a vest with a suit.
寒(さむ)いのでダウンベストを着(き)る。
I'm wearing a down vest because it's cold.
2. best; one's best
The most excellent or favorable outcome; doing one's utmost.
ベストを尽(つ)くします。
I will do my best.

ベスト has two distinct meanings as a loanword:

1. CLOTHING (from English 'vest'):

TYPES:

  • ダウンベスト (down vest)
  • ニットベスト (knit vest)
  • スーツベスト (suit vest/waistcoat)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • ベストを着(き)る (to wear a vest)
  • ベストを羽織(はお)る (to put on a vest)

2. EXCELLENCE (from English 'best'):

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ベストを尽(つ)くす (to do one's best)
  • ベストな選択(せんたく) (the best choice)
  • 自己(じこ)ベスト (personal best)
  • ベスト10 (top 10)

Note: In British English, 'vest' means undershirt, but in Japanese ベスト refers to what Americans call a vest (British: waistcoat).
