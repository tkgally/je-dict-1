透(す)ける

すける
verb (ichidan)
to be see-through; to be transparent

Definitions

1. to be transparent; to be see-through
Describes when something can be seen through another material, or when underlying things become visible.
2. to show through; to be visible
When something beneath or behind becomes visible through a surface or covering.

Examples

白(しろ)い服(ふく)が透(す)けて下着(したぎ)が見(み)える。
The white clothes are see-through and the underwear is visible.
薄(うす)い紙(かみ)から光(ひかり)が透(す)けて見(み)える。
Light can be seen through the thin paper.
彼女(かのじょ)の肌(はだ)は白(しろ)くて、血管(けっかん)が透(す)けて見(み)えるほどだ。
Her skin is so pale that you can see her veins through it.

Notes

透(す)ける is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) describing when something is transparent or see-through. There is no direct transitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
透(す)けている indicates an ongoing state of being see-through or transparent.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 光(ひかり)が透(す)ける - light shows through
  • 肌(はだ)が透(す)ける - skin is translucent
  • 透(す)けて見(み)える - to be visible through (something)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 透明(とうめい) - transparent (adjective)
  • 透(す)かす - to look through, to hold up to light (transitive)
