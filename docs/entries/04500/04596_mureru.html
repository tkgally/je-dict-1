蒸(む)れる

むれる
verb (ichidan)
to be humid; to be stuffy; to steam
1. to be stuffy; to be humid and hot
When enclosed spaces or clothing become uncomfortably warm and humid.
夏(なつ)は靴(くつ)の中(なか)が蒸(む)れて気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い。
In summer, my shoes get stuffy and uncomfortable.
蒸(む)れた空気(くうき)で息苦(いきぐる)しい。
The humid air makes it hard to breathe.
窓(まど)を閉(し)めきった部屋(へや)は蒸(む)れて耐(た)えられなかった。
The room with all the windows shut was so stuffy I couldn't stand it.
2. to steam; to become overheated
When skin or body parts become hot and sweaty due to poor ventilation.
マスクをしていると顔(かお)が蒸(む)れる。
Wearing a mask makes my face get hot and stuffy.
長靴(ながぐつ)を履(は)いていたら足(あし)が蒸(む)れた。
My feet got sweaty from wearing rubber boots.
帽子(ぼうし)をかぶり続(つづ)けると頭(あたま)が蒸(む)れて痒(かゆ)くなる。
If you keep wearing a hat, your head gets sweaty and itchy.

蒸(む)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive form is 蒸(む)らす (to steam, to let something get humid).

ASPECT ({ている}):
蒸(む)れている indicates an ongoing state of being stuffy or humid.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 靴(くつ)が蒸(む)れる - shoes get stuffy
  • 部屋(へや)が蒸(む)れる - room becomes stuffy
  • 足(あし)が蒸(む)れる - feet get sweaty
  • 蒸(む)れた暑(あつ)さ - humid heat

RELATED WORDS:

  • 湿(しめ)る - to become damp (more general)
  • 蒸(む)し暑(あつ)い - muggy, sultry (adjective)
  • 蒸(む)れる specifically implies uncomfortable heat and humidity combined
