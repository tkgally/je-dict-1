漁(あさ)る

あさる
verb (godan)
to search for; to rummage; to hunt for
1. to search for; to rummage through
To search through things looking for something specific, often in a messy or thorough way.
2. to forage; to scavenge
When animals or people search for food.
古本屋(ふるほんや)で珍(めずら)しい本(ほん)を漁(あさ)った。
I rummaged through the used bookstore looking for rare books.
熊(くま)がゴミ箱(ごみばこ)を漁(あさ)っていた。
A bear was rummaging through the garbage.
ネットで情報(じょうほう)を漁(あさ)る。
To search the internet for information.

漁(あさ)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). There is no intransitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
漁(あさ)っている indicates an ongoing action of searching or rummaging.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • [place]を漁(あさ)る - to rummage through [place]
  • 情報(じょうほう)を漁(あさ)る - to hunt for information
  • 餌(えさ)を漁(あさ)る - to forage for food
  • 女(おんな)/男(おとこ)を漁(あさ)る - to pursue women/men (negative nuance)

NUANCE:
This verb often implies a somewhat indiscriminate or thorough search, sometimes with a slightly negative connotation of being greedy or indiscriminate.

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 漁(あさ) is the same as in 漁業(ぎょぎょう) (fishing industry), reflecting the original meaning of fishing/foraging.
