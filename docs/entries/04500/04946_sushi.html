寿司(すし)

すし
noun
sushi
1. sushi
A Japanese dish consisting of vinegared rice combined with various ingredients, typically raw fish, seafood, vegetables, or egg.
回転(かいてん)寿司(ずし)に行(い)きませんか。
Shall we go to a conveyor belt sushi restaurant?
Note the rendaku: すし becomes ずし in compounds
握(にぎ)り寿司(ずし)を十(じゅっ)貫(かん)注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered ten pieces of nigiri sushi.
Sushi is counted with 貫(かん) (kan)
新鮮(しんせん)なネタの寿司(すし)は格別(かくべつ)だ。
Sushi with fresh toppings is exceptional.
ネタ means the topping/ingredient on sushi
子(こ)どもたちは巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)が好(す)きだ。
The children like rolled sushi.
友達(ともだち)と一緒(いっしょ)に手巻(てま)き寿司(ずし)パーティーをした。
I had a hand-roll sushi party with friends.

Also written as 鮨(すし) or 鮓(すし), but 寿司(すし) is the usual spelling on signs and menus. In compounds the initial す regularly becomes ず (rendaku).

TYPES:

  • 握(にぎ)り寿司(ずし) (hand-pressed sushi)
  • 巻(ま)き寿司(ずし) (rolled sushi)
  • 押(お)し寿司(ずし) (pressed sushi)
  • 散(ち)らし寿司(ずし) (scattered sushi)
  • 手巻(てま)き寿司(ずし) (hand rolls)
  • 回転(かいてん)寿司(ずし) (conveyor-belt sushi)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寿司(すし)を握(にぎ)る - to make sushi (said of a chef)
  • 寿司(すし)を食(た)べに行(い)く - to go out for sushi
  • 寿司(すし)を注文(ちゅうもん)する - to order sushi
  • 寿司(すし)の出前(でまえ)を取(と)る - to have sushi delivered

SHOP TERMS:

  • シャリ (vinegared rice)
  • ネタ (topping)
  • ガリ (pickled ginger)
  • むらさき (soy sauce - sushi shop term)
  • あがり (green tea - sushi shop term)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刺身(さしみ) - raw fish served on its own, without the vinegared rice

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Sushi covers everything from inexpensive 回転(かいてん)寿司(ずし) chains to expensive counter restaurants where an 板前(いたまえ) prepares each piece to order. It is also standard celebration food, often ordered in on a platter for guests.

COUNTER:
Pieces of nigiri are counted with 貫(かん); at a 回転(かいてん)寿司(ずし) the bill is counted by 皿(さら) (plates).

Related Words

Related: 寿司屋(すしや) (sushi restaurant)
Related: 刺身(さしみ) (sashimi)
