転勤(てんきん)

てんきん
noun/suru-verb
job transfer

Definitions

1. job transfer; transfer (to another workplace)
job transfer; transfer (to another workplace)

Examples

大阪(おおさか)に転勤(てんきん)することになった。
I'm being transferred to Osaka.
転勤(てんきん)で引(ひ)っ越(こ)さなければならない。
I have to move because of a job transfer.
単身赴任(たんしんふにん)で転勤(てんきん)する人(ひと)も多(おお)い。
Many people transfer for work alone, leaving their family behind.

Notes

転勤(てんきん) means being transferred to a different office or branch within the same company. This is common in large Japanese corporations.

Related terms:

  • 転職(てんしょく) (changing jobs - different company)
  • 異動(いどう) (personnel change, reassignment)
  • 単身赴任(たんしんふにん) (living away from family for work)
  • 転校(てんこう) (school transfer - for children)

Common expressions:

  • 転勤(てんきん)族(ぞく) (people who frequently transfer for work)
  • 転勤(てんきん)先(さき) (new workplace after transfer)
  • 転勤(てんきん)命令(めいれい) (transfer order)

Related Words

Contrast: 転職(てんしょく) (changing companies)
