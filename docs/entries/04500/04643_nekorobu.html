寝転(ねころ)ぶ

ねころぶ
godan verb
to lie down, to sprawl
1. to lie down, to sprawl
To lie down in a relaxed, casual manner, often on the floor, sofa, or grass.
ソファに寝転(ねころ)んでテレビを見(み)た。
I lay on the sofa watching TV.
芝生(しばふ)の上(うえ)に寝転(ねころ)んで空(そら)を見上(みあ)げた。
I lay on the grass and looked up at the sky.
2. to lounge, to laze around
To lie down in a leisurely way while relaxing.
休日(きゅうじつ)は家(いえ)で寝転(ねころ)んで過(す)ごした。
I spent my day off lounging around at home.

寝転(ねころ)ぶ is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to lie down casually.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 寝(ね)る (to sleep/lie down) + 転(ころ)ぶ (to roll/tumble) → to roll into a lying position

ASPECT (ている):
寝転(ねころ)んでいる indicates a state of lying down.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ソファに寝転(ねころ)ぶ (lie on the sofa)
  • 畳(たたみ)に寝転(ねころ)ぶ (lie on the tatami)
  • 芝生(しばふ)に寝転(ねころ)ぶ (lie on the grass)
  • 寝転(ねころ)んで本(ほん)を読(よ)む (read while lying down)

VARIANT:

  • 寝転(ねころ)がる: same meaning, alternative form

NUANCE:
Casual, relaxed lying down—not the formal act of going to bed (寝(ね)る). Implies a leisurely, comfortable position.

[Register: Casual to Neutral]
