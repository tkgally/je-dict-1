キャプテン

キャプテン
noun
captain

Definitions

1. captain (team leader)
The leader of a sports team
2. captain (ship, aircraft)
The commander of a ship or aircraft

Examples

彼(かれ)はサッカーチームのキャプテンだ。
He is the captain of the soccer team.
キャプテンがチームを率(ひき)いて優勝(ゆうしょう)した。
The captain led the team to victory.
機長(きちょう)はキャプテンとも呼(よ)ばれる。
The pilot in command is also called the captain.

Notes

キャプテン is primarily used for sports team leaders in Japanese.

USAGE BY CONTEXT:

  • Sports teams: キャプテン is the standard term
  • Ships: 船長(せんちょう) is more common than キャプテン
  • Aircraft: 機長(きちょう) is the standard term

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • キャプテンを務(つと)める: to serve as captain
  • キャプテンに選(えら)ばれる: to be chosen as captain

In school sports clubs, the captain is often called 主将(しゅしょう) or 部長(ぶちょう) in more formal contexts.
