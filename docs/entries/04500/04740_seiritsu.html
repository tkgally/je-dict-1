成立(せいりつ)

せいりつ
noun, suru-verb
establishment, formation

Definitions

1. establishment, formation, coming into existence
The process of something being established, formed, or coming into being.
2. conclusion (of a deal), reaching agreement
When a contract, deal, or agreement is successfully concluded.

Examples

この法律(ほうりつ)は1950年(ねん)に成立(せいりつ)した。
This law was established in 1950.
契約(けいやく)が成立(せいりつ)した。
The contract was concluded.
条件(じょうけん)が整(ととの)わないと取引(とりひき)は成立(せいりつ)しない。
If the conditions aren't met, the deal won't go through.

Notes

成立(せいりつ) describes when something comes into existence or is formally established, or when a deal/agreement is successfully concluded.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Laws and regulations being enacted
  • Organizations being established
  • Contracts and deals being concluded
  • Theories being formed

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 法案(ほうあん)が成立(せいりつ)する: a bill is passed
  • 契約(けいやく)が成立(せいりつ)する: a contract is concluded
  • 取引(とりひき)が成立(せいりつ)する: a deal is made
  • 成立(せいりつ)条件(じょうけん): conditions for establishment

ANTONYM:

  • 不成立(ふせいりつ): failure to establish, breakdown of negotiations
