追(お)い越(こ)す

おいこす
godan verb
to overtake, to pass, to surpass
1. to overtake, to pass
To catch up with and move ahead of someone or something that was in front.
前(まえ)の車(くるま)を追(お)い越(こ)した。
I passed the car in front.
追(お)い越(こ)し禁止(きんし)。
No passing. / Overtaking prohibited.
[Note: Common traffic sign]
2. to surpass, to exceed
To go beyond someone in ability, achievement, or quantity.
弟子(でし)が師匠(ししょう)を追(お)い越(こ)した。
The student surpassed the master.

追(お)い越(こ)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to overtake or surpass.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 追(お)う (to chase) + 越(こ)す (to cross) → to chase and pass over

ASPECT (ている):
追(お)い越(こ)している indicates an ongoing action of passing or a state of having surpassed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 車(くるま)を追(お)い越(こ)す (pass a car)
  • 追(お)い越(こ)し禁止(きんし) (no passing)
  • 追(お)い越(こ)し車線(しゃせん) (passing lane)
  • ～を追(お)い越(こ)して一位(いちい)になる (pass ~ and take first place)

TRAFFIC TERMS:

  • 追(お)い越(こ)し: overtaking, passing (noun)
  • 追(お)い越(こ)し車線(しゃせん): passing lane
  • 追(お)い越(こ)し禁止(きんし): no passing zone
