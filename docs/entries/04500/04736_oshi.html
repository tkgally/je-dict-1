推(お)し

おし
noun
one's favorite (idol/character)
1. one's favorite person (especially idol or character)
A person, character, or thing that one strongly supports or is a fan of. From the verb 推(お)す (to recommend/support).
私(わたし)の推(お)しはこのアイドルです。
This idol is my favorite.
推(お)しのグッズを集(あつ)めています。
I collect merchandise of my favorite.
推(お)しの誕生日(たんじょうび)をお祝(いわ)いしました。
I celebrated my favorite's birthday.

Originally from idol fandom culture, now widely used. Related expressions: 推(お)し活(かつ) (fan activities), 推(お)しメン (favorite member), 推(お)し変(へん) (changing one's favorite). Also used as a prefix: 推(お)し色(いろ) (color associated with one's favorite).
