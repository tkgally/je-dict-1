言(い)い訳(わけ)

いいわけ
noun, suru-verb
excuse; explanation
1. excuse
A reason given to justify or explain one's actions, often implying it may not be entirely valid.
2. explanation; justification
An attempt to explain the circumstances behind something.
遅刻(ちこく)の言(い)い訳(わけ)ばかりするな。
Stop making excuses for being late.
彼(かれ)の言(い)い訳(わけ)は信(しん)じられない。
I can't believe his excuse.
言(い)い訳(わけ)をする暇(ひま)があったら行動(こうどう)しろ。
Instead of making excuses, take action.

言(い)い訳(わけ) often carries a negative nuance, implying the reason may not be genuine.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)をする - to make an excuse
  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)にならない - is no excuse
  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)ばかり - nothing but excuses
  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)無用(むよう) - no excuses accepted

NUANCE:
言(い)い訳(わけ) typically implies the speaker is trying to avoid responsibility or make themselves look better, whereas 説明(せつめい) (explanation) is more neutral.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)がましい - excuse-making, overly apologetic
  • 屁理屈(へりくつ) - sophistry, specious argument

SURU-VERB USAGE:
言(い)い訳(わけ)する can be used as a verb meaning "to make excuses."
