確定申告(かくていしんこく)

かくていしんこく
noun
tax return

Definitions

1. tax return, final tax return
The annual process of filing one's income tax declaration in Japan.

Examples

確定申告(かくていしんこく)の締(し)め切(き)りは３月(がつ)です。
The deadline for tax returns is March.
確定申告(かくていしんこく)を忘(わす)れずに済(す)ませてください。
Please don't forget to file your tax return.
今年(ことし)は初(はじ)めて確定申告(かくていしんこく)をした。
I filed a tax return for the first time this year.

Notes

Required for self-employed people, freelancers, and those with side income. Salaried employees usually have taxes withheld (源泉徴収(げんせんちょうしゅう)). Filing period is mid-February to mid-March.
