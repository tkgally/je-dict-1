福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)

ふくりこうせい
noun
employee benefits

Definitions

1. employee benefits, welfare programs
Benefits and services provided by an employer for employee welfare.

Examples

福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)が充実(じゅうじつ)している会社(かいしゃ)だ。
It's a company with comprehensive employee benefits.
福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)の一環(いっかん)として保養所(ほようじょ)がある。
As part of employee benefits, there's a company resort.
福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)を重視(じゅうし)する。
To prioritize employee benefits.

Notes

Common in job listings and HR contexts. Includes health insurance, retirement plans, company housing, childcare support, etc. Often shortened to 福利(ふくり).
