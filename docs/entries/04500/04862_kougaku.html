工学(こうがく)

こうがく
noun
engineering
1. engineering
The application of scientific principles to design and build machines, structures, and systems.
工学部(こうがくぶ)で学(まな)んでいる。
I'm studying in the engineering department.
電気(でんき)工学(こうがく)を専攻(せんこう)している。
I'm majoring in electrical engineering.
工学(こうがく)博士(はかせ)を取得(しゅとく)した。
I obtained a PhD in engineering.

工学(こうがく) refers to engineering, the application of science and math to design practical solutions.

COMPONENTS:
工(こう) (craft, industry) + 学(がく) (study) = engineering studies

COMMON BRANCHES:

  • 機械(きかい)工学(こうがく): mechanical engineering
  • 電気(でんき)工学(こうがく): electrical engineering
  • 土木(どぼく)工学(こうがく): civil engineering
  • 情報(じょうほう)工学(こうがく): information/computer engineering
  • 化学(かがく)工学(こうがく): chemical engineering
  • 建築(けんちく)工学(こうがく): architectural engineering

ACADEMIC TERMS:

  • 工学部(こうがくぶ): engineering department/faculty
  • 工学(こうがく)博士(はかせ): PhD in engineering
  • 工学(こうがく)修士(しゅうし): Master's in engineering

CONTRAST:
理学(りがく) (science) focuses on theoretical understanding, while 工学(こうがく) (engineering) focuses on practical applications.
