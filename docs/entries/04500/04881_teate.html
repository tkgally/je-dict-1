手当(てあて)

てあて
noun
allowance, treatment

Definitions

1. allowance, benefit
Extra payment or benefit provided in addition to salary.
2. medical treatment, first aid
Care or treatment given for an injury or illness.

Examples

住宅(じゅうたく)手当(てあて)をもらっている。
I receive a housing allowance.
怪我(けが)の手当(てあて)をする。
To treat an injury.
残業(ざんぎょう)手当(てあて)が付(つ)く。
Overtime pay is provided.

Notes

Two distinct meanings: 1) workplace benefits/allowances, and 2) medical treatment. Context determines meaning. Common allowances: 住宅(じゅうたく)手当(てあて), 通勤(つうきん)手当(てあて), 残業(ざんぎょう)手当(てあて).
