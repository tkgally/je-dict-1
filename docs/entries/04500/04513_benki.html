便器(べんき)

べんき
noun
toilet bowl
1. toilet bowl; toilet fixture
toilet bowl; toilet fixture
便器(べんき)を掃除(そうじ)した。
I cleaned the toilet bowl.
日本(にほん)の便器(べんき)は機能(きのう)が充実(じゅうじつ)している。
Japanese toilets have many features.
和式(わしき)便器(べんき)は最近(さいきん)少(すく)なくなった。
Japanese-style squat toilets have become rare recently.

便器(べんき) refers specifically to the toilet fixture itself, not the entire room (which is トイレ or 便所(べんじょ)).

Types:

  • 洋式(ようしき)便器(べんき) (Western-style toilet)
  • 和式(わしき)便器(べんき) (Japanese-style squat toilet)
  • 温水(おんすい)洗浄(せんじょう)便座(べんざ) (heated toilet seat with bidet)

Related terms:

  • 便座(べんざ) (toilet seat)
  • トイレ (toilet/bathroom)
  • 流(なが)す (to flush)

Cultural note:
Japanese toilets are famous worldwide for their advanced features including heated seats, bidets, and sound-masking functions (音姫(おとひめ)).
