吸(す)い物(もの)

すいもの
noun
clear soup
1. clear soup, Japanese broth
A clear Japanese soup made with dashi and typically containing a few refined ingredients. Distinguished from miso soup by its clear, delicate broth.
懐石料理(かいせきりょうり)では吸(す)い物(もの)が出(で)ます。
Clear soup is served in kaiseki cuisine.
蛤(はまぐり)の吸(す)い物(もの)はひな祭(まつ)りに食(た)べます。
Clam clear soup is eaten on Girls' Day.
吸(す)い物(もの)の出汁(だし)は透(す)き通(とお)っています。
The broth of clear soup is transparent.

吸(す)い物(もの) (literally "thing to sip") is a refined clear soup in Japanese cuisine.

Characteristics:

  • Clear, transparent broth
  • Delicate, subtle flavor
  • Few, high-quality ingredients
  • Served in lacquered bowls with lids

Typical ingredients (実(み)=ingredients, 椀種(わんだね)):

  • 豆腐(とうふ) (tofu)
  • 麩(ふ) (wheat gluten)
  • 海老(えび) (shrimp)
  • 蛤(はまぐり) (clam) - traditional for Girls' Day
  • Seasonal vegetables
  • 吸(す)い口(くち) (aromatic garnish like 木(き)の芽(め))

Differs from 味噌汁(みそしる):

  • 吸(す)い物(もの) = clear broth, more formal
  • 味噌汁(みそしる) = miso-based, everyday

Part of traditional Japanese meal structure.
