まな板(いた)

まないた
noun
cutting board, chopping board

Definitions

1. cutting board, chopping board
A flat board used as a surface for cutting food in the kitchen.

Examples

まな板(いた)の上(うえ)で肉(にく)を切(き)る。
Cut meat on the cutting board.
まな板(いた)を漂白剤(ひょうはくざい)で消毒(しょうどく)する。
Disinfect the cutting board with bleach.
まな板(いた)の鯉(こい)。
A carp on the cutting board. (Idiom: Someone who is at the mercy of others)
Describes helpless resignation to one's fate

Notes

Also written as 俎板(まないた) or 俎(まないた) (rare).

Materials:

  • 木製(もくせい) (wooden) - traditional, gentle on knives
  • プラスチック製(せい) (plastic) - easy to clean, affordable
  • ゴム製(せい) (rubber) - quiet, knife-friendly
  • 抗菌(こうきん)タイプ (antibacterial type)

Many Japanese kitchens have separate boards for:

  • 肉(にく)/魚(さかな)用(よう) (meat/fish)
  • 野菜(やさい)用(よう) (vegetables)

The idiom 「まな板(いた)の鯉(こい)」 describes accepting one's fate helplessly, like a fish waiting to be prepared.
