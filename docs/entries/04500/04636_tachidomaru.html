立(た)ち止(ど)まる

たちどまる
godan verb
to stop, to halt, to stand still
1. to stop, to halt
To stop walking or moving and remain standing in one place.
信号(しんごう)が赤(あか)になったので立(た)ち止(ど)まった。
I stopped because the light turned red.
美(うつく)しい景色(けしき)に思(おも)わず立(た)ち止(ど)まった。
I involuntarily stopped at the beautiful scenery.
2. to pause, to reflect
To stop and think or reconsider (figurative use).
たまには立(た)ち止(ど)まって人生(じんせい)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)ることも大切(たいせつ)だ。
Sometimes it's important to pause and look back on life.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to pause and reflect]

立(た)ち止(ど)まる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to stop or halt.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 立(た)つ (to stand) + 止(と)まる (to stop) → to stop while standing

ASPECT (ている):
立(た)ち止(ど)まっている indicates a state of standing still.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 思(おも)わず立(た)ち止(ど)まる (stop involuntarily)
  • ふと立(た)ち止(ど)まる (suddenly stop)
  • 足(あし)を止(と)める: similar meaning, more literary

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
Used metaphorically to describe pausing in life to think, reflect, or reconsider one's direction.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 止(と)まる: to stop (more general)
  • 立(た)ち止(ど)まる specifically implies stopping while on foot
