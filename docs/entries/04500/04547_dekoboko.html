凸凹(でこぼこ)

でこぼこ
noun
unevenness, bumpiness

Definitions

1. unevenness, bumpiness, rough surface
An irregular surface with bumps and depressions.
2. mismatched pair
Two things or people that are very different from each other.

Examples

道(みち)が凸凹(でこぼこ)で歩(ある)きにくい。
The road is bumpy and hard to walk on.
凸凹(でこぼこ)コンビで面白(おもしろ)い。
They're an odd couple and funny.
表面(ひょうめん)の凸凹(でこぼこ)を直(なお)す。
I fix the unevenness of the surface.

Notes

凸凹(でこぼこ) describes uneven surfaces or mismatched pairs.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 凸凹(でこぼこ)した道(みち): bumpy road
  • 凸凹(でこぼこ)コンビ: mismatched pair, odd couple
  • 凸凹(でこぼこ)がある: to have bumps

WRITING NOTE:
Also commonly written in hiragana (でこぼこ). The kanji 凸凹 visually represent the bumps (凸) and dips (凹).

RELATED WORDS:

  • ガタガタ: rattling, rough
  • 平(たい)ら: flat, even
