纏(まと)まる

まとまる
verb (godan)
to be settled; to come together; to be organized

Definitions

1. to come together; to be gathered
For scattered things to become collected or unified.
2. to be organized; to take shape
For ideas, plans, or work to become coherent and organized.
3. to be settled; to reach agreement
For negotiations or discussions to reach a conclusion.

Examples

意見(いけん)がやっと纏(まと)まった。
The opinions finally came together.
契約(けいやく)の話(はなし)が纏(まと)まった。
The contract negotiation was settled.
纏(まと)まったお金(かね)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
I need a substantial amount of money.

Notes

纏(まと)まる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning for things to come together or be resolved.

TRANSITIVE PAIR: 纏(まと)める (to put together, summarize)

  • 纏(まと)まる: The plan comes together.
  • 纏(まと)める: I put together the plan.

ASPECT (ている):
纏(まと)まっている indicates things are organized or a matter has been settled.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [opinion/plan]が纏(まと)まる — [opinion/plan] comes together
  • [negotiation]が纏(まと)まる — [negotiation] is concluded

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 話(はなし)が纏(まと)まる (discussion is settled)
  • 考(かんが)えが纏(まと)まる (thoughts become organized)
  • 纏(まと)まった金額(きんがく) (substantial amount)

ADJECTIVAL USE:

纏(まと)まった + noun means "substantial" or "considerable":

  • 纏(まと)まったお金(かね) (substantial money)
  • 纏(まと)まった時間(じかん) (significant chunk of time)
