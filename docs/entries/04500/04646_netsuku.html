寝付(ねつ)く

ねつく
godan verb
to fall asleep, to go to sleep
1. to fall asleep, to go to sleep
To transition from being awake to being asleep.
なかなか寝付(ねつ)けなかった。
I couldn't fall asleep easily.
子供(こども)がやっと寝付(ねつ)いた。
The child finally fell asleep.
暑(あつ)くて寝付(ねつ)きが悪(わる)い。
It's so hot that I have trouble falling asleep.
[Note: 寝付(ねつ)き is the noun form meaning 'ability to fall asleep']
2. to be bedridden
To become ill and confined to bed.

寝付(ねつ)く is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to fall asleep.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 寝(ね)る (to sleep) + 付(つ)く (to attach, to settle) → to settle into sleep

ASPECT (ている):
寝付(ねつ)いている indicates a state of being asleep (having fallen asleep).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • なかなか寝付(ねつ)けない (can't fall asleep easily)
  • やっと寝付(ねつ)いた (finally fell asleep)
  • 寝付(ねつ)きがいい/悪い (easy/hard to fall asleep)
  • すぐに寝付(ねつ)く (fall asleep quickly)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 寝付(ねつ)き: the ability to fall asleep; 寝付(ねつ)きがいい = falls asleep easily

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 眠(ねむ)る: to sleep (more general)
  • 寝(ね)る: to go to bed, to sleep
  • 寝付(ねつ)く specifically refers to the act of falling asleep

[Register: Neutral]

Related Words

Antonym: 目覚(めざ)める (to wake up)
