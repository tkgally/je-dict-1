ダサい

ださい
adjective (i-adjective)
uncool, lame
1. uncool, lame, unfashionable
Describing something or someone lacking style or sophistication. Slang.
その服(ふく)、ちょっとダサくない？
Isn't that outfit a bit uncool?
ダサいデザインだね。
That's a lame design.
昔(むかし)はダサかったけど、今(いま)はおしゃれになった。
They used to be uncool, but now they're stylish.

Slang word with disputed origins, possibly from 田舎(いなか) (countryside) + さい. Opposite of かっこいい (cool). Can refer to fashion, behavior, or ideas. Often written in katakana. Conjugates as i-adjective.
