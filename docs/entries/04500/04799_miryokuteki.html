魅力的(みりょくてき)

みりょくてき
na-adjective
attractive, charming
1. attractive, charming, appealing
Having qualities that arouse interest, admiration, or desire.
彼女(かのじょ)はとても魅力的(みりょくてき)な人(ひと)です。
She is a very attractive person.
魅力的(みりょくてき)な条件(じょうけん)を提示(ていじ)されました。
I was presented with attractive conditions.
この仕事(しごと)の魅力的(みりょくてき)なところは何(なん)ですか？
What's appealing about this job?

魅力(みりょく) means 'charm' or 'appeal.' Can describe people, offers, places, or ideas. More formal than かわいい or かっこいい when describing people.
