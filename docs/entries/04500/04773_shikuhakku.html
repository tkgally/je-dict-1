四苦八苦(しくはっく)

しくはっく
noun, verb (suru)
struggling, suffering
1. struggling, having a hard time
To struggle greatly with something difficult. Originally Buddhist term for life's sufferings.
数学(すうがく)の宿題(しゅくだい)に四苦八苦(しくはっく)している。
I'm really struggling with my math homework.
日本語(にほんご)の敬語(けいご)に四苦八苦(しくはっく)しました。
I had a hard time with Japanese honorifics.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)で四苦八苦(しくはっく)だ。
I'm struggling with moving preparations.

Originally a Buddhist term referring to the four sufferings (四苦(しく): birth, aging, sickness, death) and eight sufferings (four plus four more). Now commonly used to describe struggling with any difficult task. Often used with する.
