出(で)くわす

でくわす
godan verb
to come across, to run into, to encounter
1. to come across, to run into
To meet someone or encounter something unexpectedly and by chance.
偶然(ぐうぜん)友人(ゆうじん)に出(で)くわした。
I ran into a friend by chance.
駅(えき)で元(もと)カノに出(で)くわしてしまった。
I ran into my ex-girlfriend at the station.
[Note: しまった suggests it was an awkward encounter]
2. to encounter (a situation)
To find oneself in an unexpected situation, often unpleasant.
旅行中(りょこうちゅう)に思(おも)わぬトラブルに出(で)くわした。
I encountered unexpected trouble during my trip.

出(で)くわす is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to encounter unexpectedly.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 出(で)る (to go out) + くわす (to encounter) → to go out and encounter

ASPECT (ている):
出(で)くわしている is rare; the simple past 出(で)くわした is more common.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 偶然(ぐうぜん)出(で)くわす (come across by chance)
  • ～に出(で)くわす (run into~)
  • トラブルに出(で)くわす (encounter trouble)
  • 思(おも)いがけず出(で)くわす (unexpectedly run into)

NUANCE:
Implies an unexpected, unplanned encounter. Can be used for both pleasant surprises and awkward situations. More casual than 遭遇(そうぐう)する.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出会(であ)う: to meet, encounter (more neutral or positive)
  • 遭遇(そうぐう)する: to encounter (formal)
  • 出(で)くわす often implies surprise or coincidence
