入(はい)り込(こ)む

はいりこむ
godan verb
to enter, to get into, to slip into
1. to enter, to get into
To go into a place, often one that is not easily accessible or where one is not supposed to be.
2. to slip into, to infiltrate
To enter secretly or without permission.
3. to get deeply involved
To become immersed in something (figurative use).
猫(ねこ)が狭(せま)い隙間(すきま)に入(はい)り込(こ)んだ。
The cat got into the narrow gap.
不審者(ふしんしゃ)が敷地(しきち)に入(はい)り込(こ)んだ。
A suspicious person entered the premises.
物語(ものがたり)の世界(せかい)に入(はい)り込(こ)んで夢中(むちゅう)になった。
I got immersed in the world of the story.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to become absorbed]

入(はい)り込(こ)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to enter or get into.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 入(はい)る (to enter) + 込(こ)む (deeply into) → to go deep into

ASPECT (ている):
入(はい)り込(こ)んでいる indicates a state of being inside or immersed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)に入(はい)り込(こ)む (get into a room)
  • 隙間(すきま)に入(はい)り込(こ)む (get into a gap)
  • 世界(せかい)に入(はい)り込(こ)む (get immersed in a world)
  • 話(はなし)に入(はい)り込(こ)む (get into a conversation/story)

NUANCE:
Implies going deeper or more thoroughly than simple 入(はい)る. Often suggests entering somewhere one shouldn't be, or becoming deeply involved in something.
