十人十色(じゅうにんといろ)

じゅうにんといろ
noun, expression
to each their own
1. to each their own, different strokes for different folks
The idea that different people have different tastes, opinions, and preferences. Literally: ten people, ten colors.
好(す)みは十人十色(じゅうにんといろ)だから。
Preferences vary from person to person.
意見(いけん)は十人十色(じゅうにんといろ)で、それでいいと思(おも)う。
Everyone has different opinions, and I think that's fine.
十人十色(じゅうにんといろ)の考(かんが)え方(かた)がある。
There are as many ways of thinking as there are people.

A common 四字熟語 expressing diversity of opinions and tastes. Literally: ten people, ten colors. Used to acknowledge that differences in opinion are natural and acceptable. Similar to the English 'different strokes for different folks.'
