茸(きのこ)

きのこ
noun
mushroom
1. mushroom
The fleshy, spore-bearing fruiting body of a fungus, often umbrella-shaped, with some varieties being edible.
秋(あき)は茸(きのこ)狩(が)りの季節(きせつ)だ。
Autumn is the season for mushroom hunting.
茸狩り is the activity of gathering wild mushrooms
茸(きのこ)を鍋(なべ)に入(い)れる。
Put mushrooms in the pot.
毒(どく)茸(きのこ)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful of poisonous mushrooms.

Also written as キノコ. Common edible mushrooms:

  • しいたけ/椎茸(しいたけ) (shiitake)
  • しめじ (shimeji)
  • えのき/榎茸(えのきだけ) (enoki)
  • まいたけ/舞茸(まいたけ) (maitake)
  • エリンギ (king trumpet mushroom)
  • なめこ (nameko - slimy mushroom)
  • マッシュルーム (button mushroom)
  • 松茸(まつたけ) (matsutake - prized, expensive)

Dangerous varieties:

  • 毒(どく)茸(きのこ) (poisonous mushrooms)
  • ベニテングタケ (fly agaric)
  • ツキヨタケ (common in accidents)

Mushroom-related:

  • 茸(きのこ)狩(が)り (mushroom hunting)
  • 茸(きのこ)鍋(なべ) (mushroom hot pot)
  • 干(ほ)し椎茸(しいたけ) (dried shiitake)
