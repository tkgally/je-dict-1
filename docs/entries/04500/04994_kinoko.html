茸(きのこ)

きのこ
noun
mushroom
1. mushroom
The fleshy, spore-bearing fruiting body of a fungus, often umbrella-shaped, with some varieties being edible.
秋(あき)は茸(きのこ)がおいしい。
Mushrooms are delicious in autumn.
茸(きのこ)を鍋(なべ)に入(い)れる。
Put mushrooms in the pot.
毒(どく)茸(きのこ)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful of poisonous mushrooms.
山(やま)で茸(きのこ)を取(と)るときは、食(た)べられるかどうか確認(かくにん)してください。
When picking mushrooms in the mountains, please check whether they are edible.
茸(きのこ)はカロリーが低(ひく)くて栄養(えいよう)もあるので、毎日(まいにち)の料理(りょうり)に使(つか)いやすい食(た)べ物(もの)です。
Mushrooms are low in calories and nutritious, so they are an easy ingredient to use in everyday cooking.

Usually written キノコ in food writing and on packaging; 茸(きのこ) is the kanji form, and きのこ in hiragana is also common.

COMMON EDIBLE VARIETIES:

  • 椎茸(しいたけ) (shiitake)
  • しめじ (shimeji)
  • 榎茸(えのきだけ) (enoki)
  • 舞茸(まいたけ) (maitake)
  • エリンギ (king trumpet mushroom)
  • なめこ (nameko, a small slippery mushroom used in 味噌汁(みそしる))
  • マッシュルーム (button mushroom)
  • 松茸(まつたけ) (matsutake, prized for its 香(かお)り and very expensive)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 茸(きのこ)が生(は)える - mushrooms grow (in a wood, on a log)
  • 茸(きのこ)を採(と)る - to gather mushrooms
  • 茸(きのこ)を炒(いた)める - to stir-fry mushrooms

COMPOUNDS:

  • 茸(きのこ)狩(が)り (mushroom hunting, an 秋(あき) pastime)
  • 茸(きのこ)鍋(なべ) (mushroom hot pot)
  • 干(ほ)し椎茸(しいたけ) (dried shiitake, a basic stock ingredient)

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Mushrooms are strongly associated with 秋(あき) cooking. Wild-picking is popular but risky: 毒(どく)茸(きのこ) such as ツキヨタケ and ベニテングタケ closely resemble edible species, and poisonings are reported every autumn.
