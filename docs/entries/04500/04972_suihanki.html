炊飯器(すいはんき)

すいはんき
noun
rice cooker
1. rice cooker
An electrical kitchen appliance designed to cook rice automatically.
炊飯器(すいはんき)でご飯(はん)を炊(た)く。
Cook rice in a rice cooker.
この炊飯器(すいはんき)は三(さん)合(ごう)炊(だ)きです。
This rice cooker is a 3-cup model.
合(ごう) is the traditional unit for measuring rice
炊飯器(すいはんき)の保温(ほおん)機能(きのう)は便利(べんり)だ。
The keep-warm function of rice cookers is convenient.
新(あたら)しい炊飯器(すいはんき)を買(か)ったら、ご飯(はん)がとても美味(おい)しくなった。
After buying a new rice cooker, the rice became much more delicious.
うちの炊飯器(すいはんき)は予約(よやく)機能(きのう)がついているので、朝(あさ)起(お)きたらすぐに炊(た)きたてのご飯(はん)が食(た)べられます。
Our rice cooker has a timer function, so we can have freshly cooked rice as soon as we wake up in the morning.

An essential appliance in Japanese households. Types:

  • マイコン式(しき) (microcomputer-controlled) - basic, affordable
  • IH式(しき) (induction heating) - even heating, better quality
  • 圧力(あつりょく)IH式(しき) (pressure IH) - premium, fluffier rice
  • 土鍋(どなべ)IH (clay pot IH) - mimics traditional cooking

Features:

  • 保温(ほおん) (keep warm)
  • 予約(よやく)炊飯(すいはん) (timer/scheduled cooking)
  • 早炊(はやだ)き (quick cook)
  • 玄米(げんまい)モード (brown rice mode)
  • おかゆモード (porridge mode)

Major Japanese brands: 象印(ぞうじるし) (Zojirushi), タイガー (Tiger), パナソニック (Panasonic), 日立(ひたち) (Hitachi), 三菱(みつびし) (Mitsubishi).

Capacity measured in 合(ごう): 三合炊(さんごうだ)き (3 cups), 五合炊(ごごうだ)き (5 cups), etc. A 五合炊(ごごうだ)き model is the standard size for a family.
