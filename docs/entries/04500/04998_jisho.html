辞書(じしょ)

じしょ
noun
dictionary

Definitions

1. dictionary
A reference book or digital resource listing words and their meanings, often with translations or detailed explanations.

Examples

辞書(じしょ)で意味(いみ)を調(しら)べる。
Look up the meaning in a dictionary.
電子(でんし)辞書(じしょ)は便利(べんり)だ。
Electronic dictionaries are convenient.
和英(わえい)辞書(じしょ)を使(つか)って翻訳(ほんやく)する。
Translate using a Japanese-English dictionary.

Notes

辞書(じしょ) is a compound: 辞(じ) (words) + 書(しょ) (book).

TYPES OF 辞書(じしょ):

  • 国語(こくご)辞書(じしょ)/辞典(じてん) (Japanese dictionary)
  • 英和(えいわ)辞書(じしょ) (English-Japanese)
  • 和英(わえい)辞書(じしょ) (Japanese-English)
  • 漢字(かんじ)辞典(じてん) (kanji dictionary)
  • 電子(でんし)辞書(じしょ) (electronic dictionary)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 辞書(じしょ)を引(ひ)く (to look up in a dictionary)
  • 辞書(じしょ)で調(しら)べる (to check in a dictionary)
  • 辞書(じしょ)を使(つか)う (to use a dictionary)

NOTE:
辞書(じしょ) and 辞典(じてん) are often interchangeable, though 辞典(じてん) can feel slightly more formal or comprehensive (like an encyclopedia).
