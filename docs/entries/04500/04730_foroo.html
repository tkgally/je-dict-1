フォロー

ふぉろー
noun, verb (suru)
follow (social media), support
1. follow (social media)
To subscribe to someone's updates on social media platforms.
ぜひフォローしてください！
Please follow me!
フォロワーが1万人(いちまんにん)を超(こ)えました。
My followers exceeded 10,000.
2. follow-up, support
To provide assistance or supplementary help after an initial action.
彼(かれ)のフォローのおかげで助(たす)かりました。
I was saved thanks to his support.

A loanword from English that has developed distinct meanings in Japanese beyond the original 'follow' sense.

SENSE 1 (Social Media):

Very common in online contexts. Used with する to mean 'to follow (an account)'.

  • フォロワー (follower)
  • フォロー解除(かいじょ) (unfollow)
  • 相互(そうご)フォロー (mutual follow)
  • フォロバ (follow back, slang)

SENSE 2 (Support/Follow-up):

Used in business and everyday contexts for providing backup or assistance.

  • フォローに回(まわ)る (to take a supporting role)
  • フォローを入(い)れる (to provide backup/assistance)
  • 上司(じょうし)のフォロー (support from one's boss)

NUANCE:
In business Japanese, フォロー often implies helping someone who is struggling or covering for someone's mistake—a distinctly Japanese usage.
