駱駝(らくだ)

らくだ
noun
camel
1. camel
A large mammal with one or two humps on its back, adapted to desert conditions. Used for transportation and carrying goods.
砂漠(さばく)で駱駝(らくだ)に乗(の)りました。
I rode a camel in the desert.
駱駝(らくだ)は水(みず)なしで長(なが)く生(い)きられます。
Camels can survive for a long time without water.
駱駝(らくだ)の瘤(こぶ)には脂肪(しぼう)が蓄(たくわ)えられています。
Fat is stored in the camel's hump.
駱駝(らくだ)は砂漠(さばく)の暑(あつ)さにも寒(さむ)さにも強(つよ)い動物(どうぶつ)です。
Camels are animals that can withstand both the heat and cold of the desert.
昔(むかし)の商人(しょうにん)たちは駱駝(らくだ)に荷物(にもつ)を載(の)せて砂漠(さばく)を渡(わた)り、シルクロードを通(とお)って東(ひがし)と西(にし)の国々(くにぐに)を結(むす)んでいた。
Merchants of old loaded their goods onto camels and crossed the desert, connecting the countries of East and West through the Silk Road.

The kanji 駱駝(らくだ) is rarely used in ordinary writing; the katakana form ラクダ is standard in newspapers, textbooks, and zoo signage, and hiragana らくだ is also common.

TYPES:

  • 一瘤駱駝(ひとこぶらくだ) - dromedary (one hump), of Arabia and North Africa
  • 二瘤駱駝(ふたこぶらくだ) - Bactrian camel (two humps), of Central Asia

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駱駝(らくだ)に乗(の)る - to ride a camel
  • 駱駝(らくだ)の瘤(こぶ) - a camel's hump
  • 駱駝(らくだ)に荷物(にもつ)を運(はこ)ばせる - to have camels carry the load

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 馬(うま) - the riding and pack animal familiar in Japan; 駱駝(らくだ) fills the same role in desert regions
  • 家畜(かちく) - livestock, the category 駱駝(らくだ) belongs to in its home regions

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Camels are not native to Japan, so most Japanese meet them at a 動物園(どうぶつえん) or on the tourist rides at the Tottori sand dunes. In writing they appear mostly in connection with deserts and the Silk Road. Written in hiragana, らくだ is also an older word for warm knitted underwear, originally made of camel wool.

COUNTER:
Counted with 頭(とう), the counter for large animals.

Related Words

Related: 馬(うま) (horse)
