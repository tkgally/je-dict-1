朝焼(あさや)け

あさやけ
noun
morning glow; sunrise colors

Definitions

1. morning glow; sunrise colors
The red, orange, or pink colors in the sky during sunrise.

Examples

美(うつく)しい朝焼(あさや)けを見(み)ながらコーヒーを飲(の)んだ。
I drank coffee while watching the beautiful sunrise colors.
朝焼(あさや)けの空(そら)がオレンジ色(いろ)に染(そ)まっていた。
The sky was dyed orange with the morning glow.
朝焼(あさや)けは天気(てんき)が悪(わる)くなる前兆(ぜんちょう)だと言(い)われている。
They say a morning glow is a sign of worsening weather.

Notes

朝焼(あさや)け refers to the colorful sky at sunrise.

WORD FORMATION:
朝(あさ) (morning) + 焼(や)け (burning, glow) = 朝焼(あさや)け

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 朝焼(あさや)けの空(そら) - morning sky, sunrise sky
  • 朝焼(あさや)けが美(うつく)しい - the morning glow is beautiful
  • 朝焼(あさや)けに染(そ)まる - to be dyed with morning colors

WEATHER PROVERB:
朝焼(あさや)けは雨(あめ) - A red sky at morning means rain
(Similar to the English "Red sky at morning, sailor's warning")

朝焼(あさや)け VS 夕焼(ゆうや)け:
朝焼(あさや)け is morning glow (sunrise), while 夕焼(ゆうや)け is evening glow (sunset). The opposite weather prediction applies: 夕焼(ゆうや)けは晴(は)れ (evening glow means fair weather).
