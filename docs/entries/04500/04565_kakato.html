踵(かかと)

かかと
noun
heel

Definitions

1. heel (of foot)
The back part of the human foot below the ankle.
2. heel (of shoe)
The raised part at the back of a shoe.

Examples

踵(かかと)が痛(いた)い。
My heel hurts.
ハイヒールの踵(かかと)が折(お)れた。
The heel of my high heels broke.
靴(くつ)の踵(かかと)がすり減(へ)っている。
The heels of my shoes are worn down.

Notes

踵(かかと) refers to both the body part and the shoe part.

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This word is very frequently written as かかと due to the kanji's complexity.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 踵(かかと)が高(たか)い (high heels)
  • 踵(かかと)を返(かえ)す (turn on one's heel)
  • 踵(かかと)を上(あ)げる (raise one's heels, stand on tiptoe)

BODY PART CONTEXT:

Other foot parts:

  • 足首(あしくび) (ankle)
  • 足(あし)の裏(うら) (sole of foot)
  • つま先(さき) (toe/tiptoe)
  • 足(あし)の指(ゆび) (toes)

IDIOM:
踵(かかと)を接(せっ)して = in close succession, one after another (literally: heels touching)
