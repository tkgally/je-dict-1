1. to shake; to rock
To move something back and forth or up and down.
肩(かた)を揺(ゆ)すって起(お)こした。
I woke him by shaking his shoulder.
木(き)を揺(ゆ)すって実(み)を落(お)とした。
I shook the tree to make the fruit fall.
母(はは)が赤(あか)ちゃんをやさしく揺(ゆ)すっている。
The mother is gently rocking the baby.
2. to extort; to blackmail
Colloquial usage meaning to extort money by threatening.
金(かね)を揺(ゆ)すられた。
I was extorted for money.
弱(よわ)みを握(にぎ)られて揺(ゆ)すられている。
Someone has something on me and is blackmailing me.
秘密(ひみつ)をばらすと脅(おど)して大金(たいきん)を揺(ゆ)すり取(と)ろうとした。
He tried to extort a large sum of money by threatening to reveal the secret.

揺(ゆ)する is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 揺(ゆ)れる (to shake, to sway).

ASPECT (ている):
揺(ゆ)すっている indicates an ongoing action of shaking something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 肩(かた)を揺(ゆ)する - to shake someone's shoulder
  • 木(き)を揺(ゆ)する - to shake a tree
  • 揺(ゆ)り起(お)こす - to shake someone awake
  • 揺(ゆ)すり落(お)とす - to shake something off

揺(ゆ)する VS 揺(ゆ)れる:

  • 揺(ゆ)する (transitive): someone shakes something
  • 揺(ゆ)れる (intransitive): something shakes/sways on its own

NOTE ON SENSE 2:
The slang meaning "to extort" comes from the image of shaking someone down for money. This usage is informal.
