逃(のが)す

のがす
verb (godan)
to let escape; to miss; to lose

Definitions

1. to let escape; to set free
To allow someone or something to get away.
2. to miss; to let slip
To fail to take advantage of an opportunity or chance.

Examples

犯人(はんにん)を逃(のが)してしまった。
We let the criminal escape.
このチャンスを逃(のが)したくない。
I don't want to miss this chance.
電車(でんしゃ)を逃(のが)して遅刻(ちこく)した。
I missed the train and was late.

Notes

逃(のが)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 逃(のが)れる (to escape).

ASPECT ({ている}):
逃(のが)している is rarely used; the past tense 逃(のが)した (missed/let escape) is more common.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • チャンスを逃(のが)す - to miss a chance
  • 機会(きかい)を逃(のが)す - to miss an opportunity
  • 電車(でんしゃ)を逃(のが)す - to miss a train
  • 犯人(はんにん)を逃(のが)す - to let a criminal escape
  • 見(み)逃(のが)す - to overlook, to miss seeing

逃(のが)す VS 逃(に)がす:
Both readings exist with the same meaning, but 逃(のが)す is more common in standard speech. 逃(に)がす may be heard in some dialects.

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 見逃(みのが)す - to overlook, to let pass
  • 聞(き)き逃(のが)す - to miss hearing
