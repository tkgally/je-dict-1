萎(しお)れる

しおれる
verb (ichidan)
to wilt; to droop; to be dejected
1. to wilt; to droop
When plants or flowers lose their freshness and become limp, often due to lack of water.
暑(あつ)さで花(はな)が萎(しお)れてしまった。
The flowers wilted from the heat.
水(みず)をやったら萎(しお)れた葉(は)が元気(げんき)になった。
After watering, the wilted leaves perked up.
2. to be dejected; to be dispirited
Figuratively, when a person loses energy or becomes depressed.
失敗(しっぱい)して彼(かれ)は萎(しお)れている。
He's dejected after the failure.

萎(しお)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). There is no common transitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
萎(しお)れている indicates an ongoing or resulting state of being wilted or dejected.

萎(しお)れる VS 枯(か)れる:

  • 萎(しお)れる describes a temporary, often reversible state of drooping or wilting
  • 枯(か)れる describes permanent death or drying up of plants
  • A flower that 萎(しお)れる may recover with water, but one that 枯(か)れる is dead

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花(はな)が萎(しお)れる - flowers wilt
  • 野菜(やさい)が萎(しお)れる - vegetables go limp
  • 気持(きも)ちが萎(しお)れる - spirits droop

FIGURATIVE USE:
When used for people, 萎(しお)れる describes a deflated, dejected emotional state, similar to a wilting plant.
