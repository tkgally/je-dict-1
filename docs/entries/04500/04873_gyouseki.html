業績(ぎょうせき)

ぎょうせき
noun
business results, achievement
1. business results, performance
The financial or operational results of a company.
会社(かいしゃ)の業績(ぎょうせき)が好調(こうちょう)だ。
The company's performance is strong.
業績(ぎょうせき)を伸(の)ばす。
To improve business results.
今期(こんき)の業績(ぎょうせき)は前年(ぜんねん)を大(おお)きく上回(うわまわ)った。
This term's business results greatly exceeded the previous year's.
業績(ぎょうせき)不振(ふしん)により、会社(かいしゃ)は大規模(だいきぼ)なリストラを行(おこな)った。
Due to poor business performance, the company carried out large-scale restructuring.
2. achievement, accomplishment
Notable work or contributions, especially in academic or professional fields.
研究(けんきゅう)業績(ぎょうせき)が認(みと)められた。
Their research achievements were recognized.
長年(ながねん)の学術(がくじゅつ)業績(ぎょうせき)が評価(ひょうか)され、賞(しょう)を受(う)けた。
Their years of academic achievements were recognized, and they received an award.

業績(ぎょうせき) refers to results or achievements, whether in business (financial performance) or academia (scholarly contributions).

ETYMOLOGY:
業(ぎょう) (work, business) + 績(せき) (accomplishment).

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

  • 業績(ぎょうせき)が好調(こうちょう)だ: performance is strong
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)が低迷(ていめい)する: performance stagnates
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)を伸(の)ばす: to improve results
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)報告(ほうこく): performance report

ACADEMIC CONTEXT:

  • 研究(けんきゅう)業績(ぎょうせき): research achievements
  • 学術(がくじゅつ)業績(ぎょうせき): academic achievements

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 業績(ぎょうせき)評価(ひょうか): performance evaluation
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)悪化(あっか): deteriorating performance
  • 業績(ぎょうせき)見通(みとお)し: performance outlook

Related Words

Synonym: 実績(じっせき) (track record)
Synonym: 成果(せいか) (results, outcome)
