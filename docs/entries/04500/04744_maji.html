マジ

まじ
adverb, na-adjective
really, seriously
1. really, seriously (adverb)
Used to emphasize something is genuine or to express strong feelings. Casual/slang.
2. serious, for real (adjective)
Describing something as genuine or earnest.
マジで美味(おい)しい！
This is seriously delicious!
マジ？信(しん)じられない！
Seriously? I can't believe it!
マジな話(はなし)、転職(てんしょく)を考(かんが)えています。
Seriously though, I'm thinking about changing jobs.

Very common casual/slang term derived from 真面目(まじめ) (serious). Often used in マジで (really, seriously) or as a standalone exclamation. マジ卍 is an intensified youth slang expression. Considered informal and inappropriate in formal settings.
