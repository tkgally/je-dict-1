バレーボール

ばれーぼーる
noun
volleyball
1. volleyball
A sport in which two teams hit a ball over a high net, trying to make it land on the opponent's court.
バレーボールは6人(にん)でチームを作(つく)る。
A volleyball team is made up of six players.
日本(にほん)の女子(じょし)バレーボールは強(つよ)い。
Japan's women's volleyball team is strong.
ビーチバレーボールは砂浜(すなはま)でする。
Beach volleyball is played on the sand.

バレーボール is the sport of volleyball, often shortened to バレー in casual speech.

ETYMOLOGY:
From English "volleyball."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • バレー(ボール)をする: to play volleyball
  • バレーボールの試合(しあい): volleyball match/game
  • バレー部(ぶ): volleyball club (at school)
  • バレー選手(せんしゅ): volleyball player

TYPES:

  • ビーチバレー(ボール): beach volleyball
  • 室内(しつない)バレー: indoor volleyball
  • ママさんバレー: recreational volleyball for housewives

CULTURE:
Volleyball is popular in Japan, especially women's volleyball. School volleyball clubs (部活(ぶかつ)) are common. The Japanese women's national team has historically been competitive internationally.
