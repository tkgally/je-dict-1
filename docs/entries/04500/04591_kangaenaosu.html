考(かんが)え直(なお)す

かんがえなおす
godan verb
to reconsider, to think again
1. to reconsider, to think again
To think about something again, often with the possibility of changing one's mind or decision.
もう一度(いちど)考(かんが)え直(なお)してみてください。
Please think it over once more.
計画(けいかく)を考(かんが)え直(なお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reconsider the plan.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)を聞(き)いて、自分(じぶん)の行動(こうどう)を考(かんが)え直(なお)した。
After hearing his words, I reconsidered my actions.
2. to rethink, to reflect on
To examine one's thoughts or plans more carefully from a different perspective.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)を聞(き)いて、自分(じぶん)の行動(こうどう)を考(かんが)え直(なお)した。
After hearing his words, I reconsidered my actions.

考(かんが)え直(なお)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to reconsider or think again about something.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 考(かんが)える (to think) + 直(なお)す (to redo, to fix) → to think again

ASPECT (ている):
考(かんが)え直(なお)している indicates an ongoing process of reconsidering.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • もう一度(いちど)考(かんが)え直(なお)す (think it over once more)
  • 最初(さいしょ)から考(かんが)え直(なお)す (reconsider from the beginning)
  • よく考(かんが)え直(なお)す (think it over carefully)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見直(みなお)す: to review (more general, includes looking at documents)
  • 考(かんが)え直(なお)す focuses specifically on rethinking decisions or ideas
