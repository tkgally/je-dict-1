押(お)し込(こ)む

おしこむ
verb (godan)
to push in; to stuff; to break in

Definitions

1. to push in; to stuff in
To forcefully push something into a tight space.
2. to force one's way in; to break in
To enter a place forcefully, often illegally.

Examples

荷物(にもつ)を無理(むり)やり鞄(かばん)に押(お)し込(こ)んだ。
I forcefully stuffed the luggage into the bag.
泥棒(どろぼう)が家(いえ)に押(お)し込(こ)んできた。
A burglar broke into the house.
満員(まんいん)電車に押(お)し込(こ)まれた。
I was crammed into a packed train.

Notes

押(お)し込(こ)む is a verb formed from 押(お)す (to push) + 込(こ)む (into). It can be both transitive and intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Sense 1 (stuff in): transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Sense 2 (break in): intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))

ASPECT (ている):
押(お)し込(こ)んでいる can mean something is stuffed in, or someone has intruded and remains.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を[space]に押(お)し込(こ)む — to stuff [thing] into [space]
  • [place]に押(お)し込(こ)む — to force one's way into [place]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 引(ひ)き出(だ)しに押(お)し込(こ)む (stuff into a drawer)
  • 家(いえ)に押(お)し込(こ)む (break into a house)
  • 押(お)し込(こ)み強盗(ごうとう) (home invasion robbery)

PASSIVE USE:
押(お)し込(こ)まれる is commonly used for being crammed into crowded spaces like trains.
