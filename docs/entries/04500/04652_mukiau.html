向(む)き合(あ)う

むきあう
godan verb
to face each other, to confront
1. to face each other
To be positioned facing one another.
二人(ふたり)は向(む)き合(あ)って座(すわ)った。
The two sat facing each other.
2. to confront, to deal with
To face a problem, challenge, or difficult situation directly (figurative use).
問題(もんだい)と真剣(しんけん)に向(む)き合(あ)う必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to seriously confront the problem.
自分(じぶん)と向(む)き合(あ)う時間(じかん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want time to face myself (to reflect on myself).
[Note: Figurative use meaning self-reflection]

向(む)き合(あ)う is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to face each other or confront.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 向(む)く (to face) + 合(あ)う (each other) → to face each other

ASPECT (ている):
向(む)き合(あ)っている indicates a state of facing each other or confronting something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 互(たが)いに向(む)き合(あ)う (face each other mutually)
  • 問題(もんだい)に向(む)き合(あ)う (confront a problem)
  • 自分(じぶん)と向(む)き合(あ)う (face oneself, reflect)
  • 真剣(しんけん)に向(む)き合(あ)う (face seriously)

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
Commonly used in the figurative sense of dealing with or confronting problems, challenges, or oneself. Very common in self-help and psychological contexts.
