洋菓子(ようがし)

ようがし
noun
Western sweets
1. Western-style sweets, Western confectionery
Sweets and desserts that originated in Western countries, as opposed to traditional Japanese sweets. Includes cakes, cookies, chocolate, etc.
この店(みせ)は洋菓子(ようがし)で有名(ゆうめい)です。
This shop is famous for Western sweets.
洋菓子(ようがし)はバター(ばたー)やクリームを使(つか)います。
Western sweets use butter and cream.
誕生日(たんじょうび)に洋菓子(ようがし)のケーキを買(か)いました。
I bought a Western-style cake for my birthday.

洋菓子(ようがし) refers to Western-style sweets and desserts.

洋(よう) = Western, 菓子(かし) = sweets/confectionery

Common types:

  • ケーキ (cake)
  • クッキー (cookies)
  • チョコレート (chocolate)
  • シュークリーム (cream puff)
  • タルト (tart)
  • マカロン (macaron)
  • プリン (pudding/crème caramel)

Characteristics:

  • Uses dairy (butter, cream, milk)
  • Often uses wheat flour
  • Typically sweeter than Japanese sweets
  • Paired with coffee or tea

Opposite: 和菓子(わがし) (Japanese sweets)

  • Uses 餡子(あんこ), rice flour, etc.
  • Less dairy, more natural flavors
  • Paired with green tea
