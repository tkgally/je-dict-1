賞与(しょうよ)

しょうよ
noun
bonus; reward
1. bonus; year-end/mid-year bonus
Additional payment given to employees beyond regular salary, typically twice a year.
夏(なつ)の賞与(しょうよ)が出(で)た。
The summer bonus was paid out.
賞与(しょうよ)は年(ねん)2回(かい)、給料(きゅうりょう)の2か月(げつ)分(ぶん)だ。
Bonuses are paid twice a year, equivalent to two months' salary.
業績(ぎょうせき)によって賞与(しょうよ)の額(がく)が変(か)わる。
The bonus amount varies depending on company performance.

賞与(しょうよ) is a more formal term than ボーナス, often used in official documents.

SEASONAL BONUSES IN JAPAN:

  • 夏季(かき)賞与(しょうよ) (summer bonus - around July)
  • 冬季(とうき)賞与(しょうよ) or 年末(ねんまつ)賞与(しょうよ) (winter/year-end bonus - around December)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 賞与(しょうよ)が出(で)る (bonus is paid)
  • 賞与(しょうよ)を支給(しきゅう)する (to pay out bonus)
  • 賞与(しょうよ)の査定(さてい) (bonus evaluation)

SYNONYM:

  • ボーナス (bonus - more casual, commonly used in speech)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, bonuses are considered part of standard compensation. Many employees plan major purchases around bonus season.
