ヨガ

よが
noun
yoga
1. yoga
A physical, mental, and spiritual practice originating from India, involving poses, breathing, and meditation.
朝(あさ)、ヨガをする。
I do yoga in the morning.
ヨガは体(からだ)が柔(やわ)らかくなる。
Yoga makes your body more flexible.
ヨガ教室(きょうしつ)に通(かよ)い始(はじ)めた。
I started attending yoga classes.

Yoga has become very popular in Japan for health and relaxation. Also written as ヨーガ.

Common expressions:

  • ヨガをする (to do yoga)
  • ヨガ教室(きょうしつ) (yoga class)
  • ホットヨガ (hot yoga)
  • ヨガマット (yoga mat)
