1. to fly around
For birds, insects, or aircraft to fly in various directions.
蝶(ちょう)が花(はな)の周(まわ)りを飛(と)び回(まわ)っている。
Butterflies are flying around the flowers.
小鳥(ことり)が木(き)から木(き)へと飛(と)び回(まわ)っていた。
Small birds were flying from tree to tree.
夏(なつ)になると、トンボが池(いけ)の上(うえ)を低(ひく)く飛(と)び回(まわ)る姿(すがた)が見(み)られる。
When summer comes, you can see dragonflies flying low over the pond.
2. to rush about, to be busy going places
To move energetically from place to place (figurative use for people).
子(こ)どもたちが庭(にわ)を飛(と)び回(まわ)っている。
The children are running around the yard.
営業(えいぎょう)で世界中(せかいじゅう)を飛(と)び回(まわ)っている。
I fly around the world for business.
彼女(かのじょ)は取材(しゅざい)のために毎日(まいにち)あちこち飛(と)び回(まわ)っていて、家(いえ)にいる時間(じかん)がほとんどない。
She rushes around from place to place every day for reporting, and has almost no time at home.

飛(と)び回(まわ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to fly around.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 飛(と)ぶ (to fly) + 回(まわ)る (to go around) → to fly around

ASPECT (ている):
飛(と)び回(まわ)っている indicates an ongoing action of flying around.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 空(そら)を飛(と)び回(まわ)る (fly around the sky)
  • あちこち飛(と)び回(まわ)る (fly around everywhere)
  • 世界(せかい)を飛(と)び回(まわ)る (fly around the world - often figurative for traveling extensively)

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
When used for people, 飛(と)び回(まわ)る typically means to travel frequently, often by airplane, usually for business purposes.
