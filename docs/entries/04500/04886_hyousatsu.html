表札(ひょうさつ)

ひょうさつ
noun
nameplate
1. nameplate, door sign
A plate or sign displaying the resident's name at the entrance of a home.
新(あたら)しい表札(ひょうさつ)を取(と)り付(つ)けた。
I installed a new nameplate.
表札(ひょうさつ)を見(み)て住所(じゅうしょ)を確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed the address by looking at the nameplate.
木製(もくせい)の表札(ひょうさつ)が素敵(すてき)だ。
The wooden nameplate is lovely.

A traditional feature of Japanese homes, though less common on apartments. Can be made of wood, metal, or ceramic. Some people choose not to display one for privacy.
