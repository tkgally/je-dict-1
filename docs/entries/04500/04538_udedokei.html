Recently many people check the time on their smartphones, but there are still many people who wear wristwatches.

腕時計(うでどけい) is a compound of 腕(うで) (arm/wrist) and 時計(とけい) (clock/watch). It refers specifically to a watch worn on the wrist.

Common types:

  • アナログ腕時計(うでどけい) (analog watch)
  • デジタル腕時計(うでどけい) (digital watch)
  • スマートウォッチ (smartwatch)

Related terms:

  • 時計(とけい) (clock, watch - general)
  • 懐中時計(かいちゅうどけい) (pocket watch)
  • 置(お)き時計(どけい) (table clock)

Note: With smartphones, fewer young people wear 腕時計(うでどけい), though they remain popular as accessories and status symbols.
