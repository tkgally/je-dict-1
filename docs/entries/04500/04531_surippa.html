スリッパ

すりっぱ
noun
slippers

Definitions

1. slippers; indoor footwear
slippers; indoor footwear

Examples

玄関(げんかん)でスリッパに履(は)き替(か)えた。
I changed into slippers at the entrance.
トイレ用(よう)のスリッパがある。
There are slippers for the bathroom.
ホテルの部屋(へや)にはスリッパが用意(ようい)されている。
Slippers are provided in the hotel room.

Notes

スリッパ (from English "slipper") refers to indoor footwear worn inside homes, offices, and many indoor spaces in Japan.

Cultural significance:

  • Japanese homes require removing shoes at the entrance
  • スリッパ are typically provided for guests
  • Special トイレスリッパ (toilet slippers) are used only in bathrooms

Types:

  • 来客(らいきゃく)用(よう)スリッパ (guest slippers)
  • トイレスリッパ (bathroom slippers)
  • ルームシューズ (room shoes - more enclosed)
