賃金(ちんぎん)

ちんぎん
noun
wages
1. wages, pay
Money paid to workers for their labor.
賃金(ちんぎん)を上(あ)げる。
Raise wages.
最低(さいてい)賃金(ちんぎん)が引(ひ)き上(あ)げられた。
The minimum wage was raised.
賃金(ちんぎん)格差(かくさ)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
Wage disparities have become an issue.

賃金(ちんぎん) is a formal term for wages paid in exchange for labor, commonly used in economic, legal, and labor contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 最低(さいてい)賃金(ちんぎん): minimum wage
  • 賃金(ちんぎん)交渉(こうしょう): wage negotiations
  • 賃金(ちんぎん)水準(すいじゅん): wage level
  • 賃金(ちんぎん)格差(かくさ): wage disparity
  • 賃金(ちんぎん)を上(あ)げる: to raise wages

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 給料(きゅうりょう): salary (more common in everyday speech)
  • 給与(きゅうよ): salary/pay (formal, often for salaried employees)
  • 報酬(ほうしゅう): compensation, remuneration (broader, can include non-wage pay)

REGISTER:
賃金(ちんぎん) is used in formal contexts such as news reports, labor law, and economics. In casual conversation, 給料(きゅうりょう) is more natural.
