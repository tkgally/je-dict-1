哀愁(あいしゅう)

あいしゅう
noun
sorrow, pathos

Definitions

1. sorrow, pathos, melancholy
A feeling of sadness or longing; melancholic beauty.

Examples

哀愁(あいしゅう)を帯(お)びたメロディー。
A melody tinged with melancholy.
彼(かれ)の目(め)には哀愁(あいしゅう)が漂(ただよ)っていた。
There was a sense of sorrow in his eyes.
哀愁(あいしゅう)に満(み)ちた風景(ふうけい)。
A landscape filled with melancholy.

Notes

A literary word combining 哀(あい) (sorrow) and 愁(しゅう) (melancholy). Often used to describe artistic or aesthetic sadness rather than personal grief. Common in describing music, art, and atmosphere.
