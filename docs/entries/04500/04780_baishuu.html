買収(ばいしゅう)

ばいしゅう
noun (verbal)
acquisition
1. acquisition, buyout
The act of purchasing a company or business.
大手(おおて)企業(きぎょう)が買収(ばいしゅう)した。
A major company made the acquisition.
敵対的(てきたいてき)買収(ばいしゅう)から身(み)を守(まも)る。
Protect against a hostile takeover.
2. bribery
The act of paying someone to gain improper influence.
政治家(せいじか)が買収(ばいしゅう)で逮捕(たいほ)された。
The politician was arrested for bribery.

Has two distinct meanings: business acquisition and bribery.

Common expressions:

  • 買収(ばいしゅう)する (to acquire, to bribe)
  • 敵対的(てきたいてき)買収(ばいしゅう) (hostile takeover)
  • 買収(ばいしゅう)工作(こうさく) (bribery scheme)
