ゆらゆら

ゆらゆら
adverb, verb (suru)
swaying, wavering
1. swaying, wavering
Describing a slow, gentle swinging or rocking motion.
炎(ほのお)がゆらゆら揺(ゆ)れている。
The flame is wavering.
船(ふね)がゆらゆらと揺(ゆ)れた。
The boat swayed gently.
水面(すいめん)にゆらゆら映(うつ)る影(かげ)。
Shadows wavering on the water's surface.
ろうそくの火(ひ)がゆらゆらと光(ひか)っている。
The candle flame is flickering gently.
風(かぜ)に吹(ふ)かれて、木(き)の枝(えだ)がゆらゆらと揺(ゆ)れていた。
Blown by the wind, the tree branches were swaying gently.

ゆらゆら is an onomatopoeic (mimetic) word describing slow, gentle, rhythmic swaying or wavering motion.

USAGE:

  • As adverb: ゆらゆら揺(ゆ)れる (to sway gently)
  • With する: ゆらゆらする (to be swaying)
  • With と: ゆらゆらと (more literary)

TYPICAL SUBJECTS:

  • 炎(ほのお)がゆらゆら: flames flickering
  • 船(ふね)がゆらゆら: boat rocking gently
  • 影(かげ)がゆらゆら: shadows wavering
  • 水草(みずくさ)がゆらゆら: water plants swaying

RELATED:
揺(ゆ)れる (to shake/sway) is the related verb. ゆらゆら emphasizes the slow, gentle, back-and-forth quality of the motion.
