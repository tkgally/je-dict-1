浴槽(よくそう)

よくそう
noun
bathtub
1. bathtub
A large container in a bathroom that holds water for bathing.
浴槽(よくそう)にお湯(ゆ)を張(は)る。
Fill the bathtub with hot water.
湯を張る is the standard expression for filling a bath
浴槽(よくそう)に浸(つ)かってリラックスする。
Relax by soaking in the bathtub.
浴槽(よくそう)を洗(あら)ってから入(はい)る。
Clean the bathtub before getting in.

In Japanese bathing culture, the 浴槽 is for soaking after washing the body outside the tub (体を洗ってから入る). Family members often share the same bath water.

Types:

  • FRP製(せい) (fiberglass-reinforced plastic) - most common
  • 人工(じんこう)大理石(だいりせき) (artificial marble)
  • ホーロー (enamel)
  • 檜(ひのき)/木製(もくせい) (cypress/wooden) - traditional, luxury
  • ユニットバス (unit bath) - prefabricated bathroom

Colloquial terms:

  • お風呂(ふろ) (bath - general)
  • バスタブ (bathtub - loanword)
  • 湯船(ゆぶね) (bathtub - traditional term)

Related actions:

  • お湯(ゆ)を張(は)る (fill the bath)
  • 追(お)い焚(だ)きする (reheat the bath water)
