日没(にちぼつ)

にちぼつ
noun
sunset; sundown

Definitions

1. sunset; sundown
The time when the sun disappears below the horizon in the evening.

Examples

日没(にちぼつ)前(まえ)に帰(かえ)ろう。
Let's go home before sunset.
日没(にちぼつ)は午後(ごご)6時(じ)ごろです。
Sunset is around 6 PM.
日没(にちぼつ)から日(ひ)の出(で)までを「夜(よる)」という。
The period from sunset to sunrise is called 'night.'

Notes

日没(にちぼつ) is a formal/technical term for sunset.

WORD FORMATION:
日(にち) (sun, day) + 没(ぼつ) (sinking, setting) = 日没(にちぼつ)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日没(にちぼつ)前(まえ)/後(ご) - before/after sunset
  • 日没(にちぼつ)時刻(じこく) - sunset time
  • 日没(にちぼつ)と同時(どうじ)に - at sunset

日没(にちぼつ) VS 日(ひ)の入(い)り VS 夕暮(ゆうぐ)れ:

  • 日没(にちぼつ) - formal, technical term for the moment the sun sets
  • 日(ひ)の入(い)り - standard word for sunset
  • 夕暮(ゆうぐ)れ - dusk, twilight (the period around sunset)

OPPOSITE:

  • 日(ひ)の出(で) / 日出(にっしゅつ) - sunrise
