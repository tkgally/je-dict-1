脱線(だっせん)

だっせん
noun
derailment, digression
1. derailment
When a train or vehicle leaves the tracks.
2. digression, going off topic
Straying from the main subject in a conversation or discussion.
電車(でんしゃ)が脱線(だっせん)した。
The train derailed.
話(はなし)が脱線(だっせん)してしまった。
The conversation went off topic.
脱線(だっせん)事故(じこ)で遅延(ちえん)が発生(はっせい)した。
A derailment accident caused delays.

脱線(だっせん) has both literal (train derailment) and figurative (going off topic) meanings.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 脱線(だっせん)する: to derail, to go off topic
  • 脱線(だっせん)事故(じこ): derailment accident
  • 話(はなし)が脱線(だっせん)する: the conversation goes off topic

USAGE NOTE:
The figurative use is very common in casual conversation when someone strays from the main topic.
