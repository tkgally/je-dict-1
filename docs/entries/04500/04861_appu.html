アップ

アップ
noun, suru-verb
up, increase, upload

Definitions

1. increase, improvement
An increase in quantity, quality, or level
2. close-up (photography, film)
A close-up shot in photography or video
3. upload
Uploading content to the internet
4. warm-up
Warming up before sports or exercise

Examples

給料(きゅうりょう)が来月(らいげつ)からアップする。
My salary will increase starting next month.
写真(しゃしん)をSNSにアップした。
I uploaded a photo to social media.
試合(しあい)の前(まえ)にアップをしなければならない。
I have to warm up before the match.
俳優(はいゆう)の顔(かお)がアップで映(うつ)された。
The actor's face was shown in a close-up.

Notes

アップ is a versatile loanword with several distinct meanings depending on context.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ベースアップ: base salary increase
  • クローズアップ: close-up
  • スピードアップ: speeding up
  • レベルアップ: level up, skill improvement
  • ウォームアップ / ウォーミングアップ: warm-up

VERB USAGE:
アップする is commonly used as a suru-verb meaning to increase or to upload.

The opposite ダウン is used for decreases or downloads.
