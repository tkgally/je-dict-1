高齢化(こうれいか)

こうれいか
noun
aging population
1. aging population, population aging
The demographic shift toward an older average age in a population.
日本(にほん)は高齢化(こうれいか)社会(しゃかい)だ。
Japan is an aging society.
高齢化(こうれいか)が進(すす)むと医療費(いりょうひ)が増(ふ)える。
As the population ages, medical costs increase.
高齢化(こうれいか)率(りつ)が上昇(じょうしょう)している。
The aging rate is rising.

Japan has one of the highest rates of population aging in the world. The term is often combined with 少子化(しょうしか).

Common expressions:

  • 高齢化(こうれいか)社会(しゃかい) (aging society)
  • 超(ちょう)高齢化(こうれいか)社会(しゃかい) (super-aged society)
  • 少子(しょうし)高齢化(こうれいか) (declining birthrate and aging population)
