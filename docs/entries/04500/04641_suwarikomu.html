1. to sit down and stay
To sit down in a place and remain there, often for an extended time.
疲(つか)れて座(すわ)り込(こ)んだ。
I was tired and plopped down.
道(みち)に座(すわ)り込(こ)んでいる人(ひと)がいた。
There was someone sitting on the road.
子供(こども)が駄々(だだ)をこねて店(みせ)の前(まえ)で座(すわ)り込(こ)んでいる。
The child is throwing a tantrum and sitting down stubbornly in front of the store.
急(きゅう)にめまいがして、その場(ば)に座(すわ)り込(こ)んでしまった。
I suddenly felt dizzy and sank down right where I was.
長(なが)い時間(じかん)歩(ある)いた後(あと)、疲(つか)れて道端(みちばた)に座(すわ)り込(こ)んでしまい、しばらく動(うご)けなかった。
After walking for a long time, I was so tired that I sat down on the roadside and could not move for a while.
2. to stage a sit-in, to sit in protest
To occupy a place by sitting as a form of protest.
学生(がくせい)たちが座(すわ)り込(こ)んだ。
The students staged a sit-in.
抗議(こうぎ)のために座(すわ)り込(こ)んでいる。
They are sitting in protest.
市役所(しやくしょ)の前(まえ)で住民(じゅうみん)たちが座(すわ)り込(こ)んだ。
Residents staged a sit-in in front of city hall.
工事(こうじ)に反対(はんたい)する人(ひと)たちが朝(あさ)から道路(どうろ)に座(すわ)り込(こ)んでいる。
People opposed to the construction have been sitting in on the road since morning.
計画(けいかく)の中止(ちゅうし)を求(もと)めて、市民(しみん)たちは三日間(みっかかん)市役所(しやくしょ)の前(まえ)に座(すわ)り込(こ)み続(つづ)けた。
Demanding cancellation of the plan, citizens continued their sit-in in front of city hall for three days.

座(すわ)り込(こ)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to sit down and stay put.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 座(すわ)る (to sit) + 込(こ)む (to settle in) → to sit and settle in

ASPECT (ている):
座(すわ)り込(こ)んでいる indicates a state of being seated and staying.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道端(みちばた)に座(すわ)り込(こ)む (sit down on the roadside)
  • 抗議(こうぎ)で座(すわ)り込(こ)む (stage a sit-in protest)
  • 疲(つか)れて座(すわ)り込(こ)む (sit down from exhaustion)
  • 駄々(だだ)をこねて座(すわ)り込(こ)む (sit down stubbornly while throwing a tantrum)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 座(すわ)り込(こ)み: a sit-in (protest)

NUANCE:
Implies sitting down with the intention of staying, not just temporarily sitting. Can suggest exhaustion, protest, or stubbornness.
