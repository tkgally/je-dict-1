狼(おおかみ)

おおかみ
noun
wolf

Definitions

1. wolf
A wild canine known for living and hunting in packs. The Japanese wolf is now extinct, but wolves remain important in Japanese folklore.

Examples

狼(おおかみ)は群(む)れで行動(こうどう)する動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Wolves are animals that act in packs.
狼(おおかみ)の遠吠(とおぼ)えが山(やま)に響(ひび)いた。
The wolf's howl echoed through the mountains.
日本(にほん)狼(おおかみ)は明治時代(めいじじだい)に絶滅(ぜつめつ)した。
The Japanese wolf went extinct in the Meiji era.

Notes

狼(おおかみ) refers to wolves.

Common expressions:

  • 狼(おおかみ)少年(しょうねん) (the boy who cried wolf)
  • 一匹(いっぴき)狼(おおかみ) (lone wolf - someone who acts alone)
  • 狼狽(ろうばい)する (to be flustered - uses the kanji for wolf)
  • 送(おく)り狼(おおかみ) (a man who escorts a woman home with ulterior motives)

Counter: 匹(ひき) or 頭(とう)

Cultural note:
The 日本(にほん)狼(おおかみ) (Japanese wolf) went extinct around 1905. In Japanese folklore, wolves were revered as messengers of mountain gods (大口真神(おおくちのまかみ)) and protected crops from wild boars and deer. Several shrines still worship wolves, such as 三峯(みつみね)神社(じんじゃ) in Saitama.
