ぱさぱさ

ぱさぱさ
adverb, na-adjective, verb (suru)
dry, crumbly
1. dry, crumbly, parched
Describing something that lacks moisture; dry texture.
髪(かみ)がぱさぱさだ。
My hair is dry.
このパンはぱさぱさしている。
This bread is dry and crumbly.
肌(はだ)がぱさぱさになった。
My skin became dry.

ぱさぱさ is an onomatopoeia/mimetic word describing dry, rough, crumbly texture.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • ぱさぱさだ: is dry (na-adjective)
  • ぱさぱさしている: is dry (verb form)
  • ぱさぱさになる: to become dry

COMMONLY DESCRIBES:

  • 髪(かみ): dry hair
  • 肌(はだ): dry skin
  • パン: dry, crumbly bread
  • ケーキ: dry cake

CONTRAST:

  • ぱさぱさ: dry, lacking moisture
  • しっとり: moist, supple
  • ふわふわ: fluffy, soft
  • さらさら: smooth, silky (for hair)

NOTE:
The voiced version ばさばさ describes rustling sounds or rough/disheveled appearance.
