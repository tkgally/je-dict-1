ぱさぱさ

ぱさぱさ
adverb, na-adjective, verb (suru)
dry, crumbly

Definitions

1. dry, crumbly, parched
Describing something that lacks moisture; dry texture.

Examples

髪(かみ)がぱさぱさだ。
My hair is dry.
このパンはぱさぱさしている。
This bread is dry and crumbly.
肌(はだ)がぱさぱさになった。
My skin became dry.

Notes

A mimetic word describing dry, rough texture. Often used for hair, skin, bread, and other things that have lost moisture. Opposite of しっとり (moist).
