静(しず)まる

しずまる
verb (godan)
to become quiet; to calm down

Definitions

1. to become quiet; to become still
When noise, commotion, or activity subsides and becomes peaceful.
2. to calm down; to subside
When emotions, disturbances, or upheaval settle down.

Examples

夜(よる)が更(ふ)けて、辺(あた)りが静(しず)まった。
As the night deepened, the surroundings became quiet.
騒(さわ)ぎが静(しず)まるまで待(ま)った。
I waited until the commotion calmed down.
嵐(あらし)が過(す)ぎて、海(うみ)が静(しず)まった。
After the storm passed, the sea became calm.

Notes

静(しず)まる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 静(しず)める (to quiet, to calm).

ASPECT ({ている}):
静(しず)まっている indicates a resulting state of being quiet or calm.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 騒(さわ)ぎが静(しず)まる - commotion calms down
  • 辺(あた)りが静(しず)まる - surroundings become quiet
  • 心(こころ)が静(しず)まる - one's heart/mind calms
  • 静(しず)まり返(かえ)る - to become completely silent

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 静(しず)まり返(かえ)った - completely silent, dead quiet
  • 水(みず)を打(う)ったように静(しず)まる - to become as quiet as if water was poured (idiom for sudden silence)
