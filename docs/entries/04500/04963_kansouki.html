乾燥機(かんそうき)

かんそうき
noun
dryer; drying machine

Definitions

1. clothes dryer; tumble dryer
A machine used to dry laundry after washing.
2. drying machine; dehydrator
Equipment used to remove moisture from various materials.

Examples

洗濯物(せんたくもの)を乾燥機(かんそうき)に入(い)れた。
I put the laundry in the dryer.
乾燥機(かんそうき)を使(つか)うと電気代(でんきだい)がかかる。
Using the dryer increases the electricity bill.
このセーターは乾燥機(かんそうき)不可(ふか)です。
This sweater cannot be put in the dryer.

Notes

乾燥機(かんそうき) is a compound noun: 乾燥(かんそう) (drying) + 機(き) (machine).

TYPES:

  • 衣類(いるい)乾燥機(かんそうき) (clothes dryer)
  • 食器(しょっき)乾燥機(かんそうき) (dish dryer)
  • 布団(ふとん)乾燥機(かんそうき) (futon dryer)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乾燥機(かんそうき)にかける (to put in the dryer)
  • 乾燥機(かんそうき)使用(しよう)可(か)/不可(ふか) (dryer-safe / not dryer-safe)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, many people prefer air-drying laundry outdoors. Dryers are less common in homes compared to Western countries due to humidity concerns and electricity costs. 浴室(よくしつ)乾燥機(かんそうき) (bathroom dryer) is common in apartments.
