飛(と)び越(こ)える

とびこえる
ichidan verb
to jump over, to leap over

Definitions

1. to jump over, to leap over
To jump and pass over an obstacle such as a fence, hurdle, or gap.
2. to surpass, to transcend
To go beyond boundaries, limits, or expectations (figurative use).

Examples

犬(いぬ)が柵(さく)を飛(と)び越(こ)えた。
The dog jumped over the fence.
ハードルを飛(と)び越(こ)える。
To jump over a hurdle.
国境(こっきょう)を飛(と)び越(こ)えた友情(ゆうじょう)。
A friendship that transcends national borders.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to go beyond boundaries]

Notes

飛(と)び越(こ)える is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to jump over something.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 飛(と)ぶ (to fly/jump) + 越(こ)える (to cross over) → to jump across/over

ASPECT (ている):
飛(と)び越(こ)えている indicates an ongoing or repeated action of jumping over.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 塀(へい)を飛(と)び越(こ)える (jump over a wall)
  • 柵(さく)を飛(と)び越(こ)える (jump over a fence)
  • ハードルを飛(と)び越(こ)える (jump over a hurdle)
  • 壁(かべ)を飛(と)び越(こ)える (overcome a barrier - often figurative)

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
Used metaphorically to describe transcending boundaries, limits, or obstacles in abstract contexts like relationships, cultural differences, or limitations.
