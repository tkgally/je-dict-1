コンロ

こんろ
noun
stove; burner; cooking range
1. stove; burner; cooking range
A kitchen appliance with burners used for cooking on top of.
コンロで湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす。
Boil water on the stove.
ガスコンロの火(ひ)を消(け)し忘(わす)れた。
I forgot to turn off the gas stove.
台所(だいどころ)には３口(くち)コンロがある。
The kitchen has a three-burner stove.

コンロ derives from the Dutch word 'komfoor' (portable stove).

TYPES:

  • ガスコンロ (gas stove)
  • IHコンロ (induction cooktop)
  • 電気(でんき)コンロ (electric stove)
  • 卓上(たくじょう)コンロ (portable stove)
  • 七輪(しちりん) (traditional charcoal stove)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コンロの火(ひ)をつける (to turn on the stove)
  • コンロの火(ひ)を消(け)す (to turn off the stove)
  • コンロを掃除(そうじ)する (to clean the stove)
  • ～口(くち)コンロ (X-burner stove)

In Japan, ガスコンロ has been traditional, but IHコンロ (induction) is becoming popular due to safety and ease of cleaning.
