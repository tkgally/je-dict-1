鷺(さぎ)

さぎ
noun
heron
See also: 詐欺(さぎ) (homophone: fraud)
1. heron, egret
A long-legged wading bird with a long neck and pointed beak, commonly found near water.
川(かわ)で鷺(さぎ)が魚(さかな)を捕(と)っていました。
A heron was catching fish in the river.
白鷺(しらさぎ)が田(た)んぼに立(た)っています。
A white egret is standing in the rice paddy.
鷺(さぎ)は長(なが)い脚(あし)で水辺(みずべ)を歩(ある)きます。
Herons walk along the waterside on their long legs.

鷺(さぎ) is a general term for herons and egrets.

Common types in Japan:

  • 白鷺(しらさぎ) (white egret) - includes great egret, little egret
  • 青鷺(あおさぎ) (grey heron)
  • 五位鷺(ごいさぎ) (night heron)

白鷺城(しらさぎじょう) is another name for 姫路城(ひめじじょう) (Himeji Castle), due to its white walls resembling a white heron in flight.

Note: Not to be confused with 詐欺(さぎ) (fraud), which has the same reading.
