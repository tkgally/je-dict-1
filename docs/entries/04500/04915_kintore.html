1. strength training, weight training
Exercise focused on building muscle strength.
毎日(まいにち)筋(きん)トレをしている。
I do strength training every day.
ジムで筋(きん)トレする。
Do strength training at the gym.
筋(きん)トレの効果(こうか)が出(で)てきた。
The effects of strength training are showing.

筋(きん)トレ is an abbreviation of 筋力(きんりょく)トレーニング (muscle strength training).

USAGE:

  • 筋(きん)トレをする: to do strength training

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 筋(きん)トレメニュー: workout routine
  • 自宅(じたく)筋(きん)トレ: home workout
