早(はや)める

はやめる
verb (ichidan)
to hasten; to quicken; to bring forward
1. to bring forward; to move up
To reschedule something to an earlier time.
出発(しゅっぱつ)を一時間(いちじかん)早(はや)めた。
We moved up the departure by one hour.
2. to hasten; to speed up
To make something happen faster or accelerate a process.
足(あし)を早(はや)めて駅(えき)に向(む)かった。
I quickened my pace and headed to the station.
ストレスは老化(ろうか)を早(はや)める。
Stress hastens aging.

早(はや)める is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 早(はや)まる (to be brought forward, to quicken).

ASPECT ({ている}):
早(はや)めている indicates an ongoing action of hastening something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予定(よてい)を早(はや)める - to move up a schedule
  • 足(あし)を早(はや)める - to quicken one's pace
  • 歩(あゆ)みを早(はや)める - to quicken one's steps
  • 死(し)を早(はや)める - to hasten death

PARTICLE PATTERN:

  • [noun]を早(はや)める - to hasten/bring forward [noun]
