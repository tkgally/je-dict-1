孤独(こどく)

こどく
noun, na-adjective
loneliness, solitude
1. loneliness, solitude
The state of being alone; can be negative (lonely) or neutral (solitary).
孤独(こどく)を感(かん)じる。
To feel lonely.
孤独(こどく)な人生(じんせい)を送(おく)っている。
They're living a lonely life.
孤独(こどく)死(し)を防(ふせ)ぐ取(と)り組(く)み。
Efforts to prevent dying alone.

孤独(こどく) means loneliness or solitude, functioning as both a noun and na-adjective.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 孤独(こどく)な生活(せいかつ) (lonely life)
  • Noun: 孤独(こどく)を感(かん)じる (to feel lonely)
  • Adverb: 孤独(こどく)に生(い)きる (to live in solitude)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 孤独(こどく)を味(あじ)わう: to experience loneliness
  • 孤独(こどく)に耐(た)える: to endure solitude
  • 孤独(こどく)死(し): dying alone (social issue in Japan)
  • 孤独(こどく)感(かん): feeling of loneliness

NUANCE:
Can describe either unwanted loneliness or chosen solitude. Context determines whether negative (lonely) or neutral/positive (solitary).

RELATED:

  • 孤立(こりつ): isolation (often forced or circumstantial)
  • 寂(さび)しい: lonely (emotional feeling)
