着替(きが)える

きがえる
verb (ichidan)
to change clothes
1. to change clothes; to get changed
To take off current clothes and put on different ones.
汗(あせ)をかいたので着替(きが)えたい。
I'm sweaty so I want to change clothes.
仕事(しごと)から帰(かえ)ったら、すぐに楽(らく)な服(ふく)に着替(きが)える。
When I get home from work, I immediately change into comfortable clothes.
子供(こども)を着替(きが)えさせてから出(で)かけた。
I got the child changed before going out.

着替(きが)える is both transitive and intransitive. It can mean "to change one's own clothes" (intransitive use) or "to change [specific clothing]" (transitive use).

ASPECT ({ている}):
着替(きが)えている typically indicates an ongoing action of changing clothes.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 服(ふく)に着替(きが)える - to change into clothes
  • 着替(きが)えさせる - to change someone's clothes (causative)
  • 着替(きが)え - change of clothes (noun form)

PARTICLE USAGE:

  • [clothing]に着替(きが)える - to change into [clothing]
  • [clothing]を着替(きが)える - to change [specific clothing item]

RELATED WORDS:

  • 着替(きが)え - spare clothes, change of clothes
  • 更衣室(こういしつ) - changing room, locker room
