投資(とうし)

とうし
noun, suru verb
investment

Definitions

1. investment; investing
The act of putting money into financial schemes, property, or business ventures with the expectation of profit.
2. investment (of time, effort)
Devoting resources like time or effort to something with expectation of future benefit.

Examples

株式(かぶしき)に投資(とうし)する。
Invest in stocks.
不動産(ふどうさん)投資(とうし)で利益(りえき)を得(え)た。
Made a profit from real estate investment.
教育(きょういく)への投資(とうし)は重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Investment in education is important.

Notes

投資(とうし) is a compound: 投(とう) (throw/put in) + 資(し) (capital).

TYPES OF 投資(とうし):

  • 株式(かぶしき)投資(とうし) (stock investment)
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)投資(とうし) (real estate investment)
  • 設備(せつび)投資(とうし) (capital investment)
  • 海外(かいがい)投資(とうし) (overseas investment)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投資(とうし)する (to invest)
  • 投資(とうし)を行(おこな)う (to make an investment)
  • 投資家(とうしか) (investor)
  • 投資(とうし)信託(しんたく) (investment trust/mutual fund)
  • 投資(とうし)対象(たいしょう) (investment target)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 出資(しゅっし) (capital contribution)
  • 融資(ゆうし) (financing/loan)
