落(お)ち着(つ)く

おちつく
verb (godan)
to calm down; to settle; to compose oneself

Definitions

1. to calm down; to compose oneself
To become mentally calm and relaxed after being excited or anxious.
2. to settle; to become stable
For a situation, price, or condition to stabilize.
3. to be comfortable; to feel at home
To feel relaxed and comfortable in a place or situation.

Examples

落(お)ち着(つ)いて話(はな)してください。
Please calm down and speak.
騒(さわ)ぎが落(お)ち着(つ)いたら連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you once things settle down.
この喫茶店(きっさてん)は落(お)ち着(つ)く。
This café is relaxing / I feel comfortable in this café.
物価(ぶっか)がようやく落(お)ち着(つ)いてきた。
Prices have finally started to stabilize.

Notes

落(お)ち着(つ)く is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). There is no direct transitive pair, but 落(お)ち着(つ)かせる (causative) means 'to calm someone down'.

ASPECT ({ている}):

  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いている describes a calm state or composed demeanor
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いた as an adjective means 'calm, composed, mature'

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いて！ (Calm down!)
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いた雰囲気(ふんいき) (calm atmosphere)
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いた色(いろ) (subdued color)
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いた人(ひと) (composed person)
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)き (composure, as a noun)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 静(しず)まる (to become quiet)
  • 安定(あんてい)する (to stabilize)
  • 冷静(れいせい) (calm, cool-headed)
