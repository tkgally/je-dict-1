1. to tie up; to bundle
To bind or tie things together into a bundle.
髪(かみ)を後(うし)ろで括(くく)っている。
She has her hair tied back.
新聞(しんぶん)を紐(ひも)で括(くく)って捨(す)てた。
I tied up the newspapers with string and threw them away.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に段(だん)ボールを紐(ひも)で括(くく)って準備(じゅんび)した。
Before the move, I tied up the cardboard boxes with string to prepare.
2. to summarize; to lump together
To group or categorize things together.
全部(ぜんぶ)一(ひと)括(くく)りにしないでほしい。
I'd rather you not lump everything together.
同(おな)じカテゴリーに括(くく)ることはできない。
We can't lump them into the same category.
複雑(ふくざつ)な問題(もんだい)を一言(ひとこと)で括(くく)るのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to summarize a complex problem in a single word.

括(くく)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). There is no common intransitive pair.

ASPECT (ている):
括(くく)っている indicates a resulting state of being tied or bundled.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紐(ひも)で括(くく)る - to tie with string
  • 髪(かみ)を括(くく)る - to tie up one's hair
  • 一括(いっかつ)する - to bundle together, to lump together
  • 括(くく)り付(つ)ける - to tie firmly

COMPOUND EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腹(はら)を括(くく)る - to prepare oneself mentally, to steel oneself (idiom)
  • 一(ひと)括(くく)りにする - to lump together, to generalize

RELATED WORDS:

  • 縛(しば)る - to tie, to bind (more general, often tighter)
  • 束(たば)ねる - to bundle, to tie in a bunch
