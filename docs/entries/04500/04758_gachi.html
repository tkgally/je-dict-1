ガチ

がち
adverb, na-adjective, prefix
serious, for real

Definitions

1. serious, for real, genuine
Emphasizing that something is genuine, earnest, or done with full effort. Slang.

Examples

ガチで勉強(べんきょう)しないと合格(ごうかく)できない。
I have to study seriously to pass.
彼(かれ)はガチ勢(ぜい)だから。
He's a hardcore fan, you know.
ガチな話(はなし)、転職(てんしょく)しようと思(おも)っている。
Seriously though, I'm thinking about changing jobs.

Notes

Slang word meaning 'genuine' or 'serious'. Often used as ガチで (seriously), ガチな (serious), or as a prefix: ガチ勢(ぜい) (hardcore fans), ガチ恋(こい) (real romantic feelings). Similar to マジ but implies more commitment or intensity.

Related Words

Synonym: マジ (really)
