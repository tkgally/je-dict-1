立(た)ち寄(よ)る

たちよる
godan verb
to stop by, to drop in, to visit briefly
1. to stop by, to drop in
To visit a place briefly, usually on the way to somewhere else.
帰(かえ)りにコンビニに立(た)ち寄(よ)った。
I stopped by the convenience store on my way home.
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くの本屋(ほんや)に立(た)ち寄(よ)ってから会社(かいしゃ)に向(む)かった。
I stopped by the bookstore near the station before heading to the office.
観光(かんこう)のついでに有名(ゆうめい)な神社(じんじゃ)に立(た)ち寄(よ)ったら、思(おも)いがけず美(うつく)しい庭園(ていえん)を見(み)ることができた。
When I stopped by a famous shrine while sightseeing, I was able to see a beautiful garden unexpectedly.
2. to approach, to draw near
To move closer to someone or something, often with a sense of proximity or closeness.
猫(ねこ)がそっと立(た)ち寄(よ)ってきた。
A cat quietly drew near.
知(し)らない人(ひと)が立(た)ち寄(よ)ってきたので、少(すこ)し驚(おどろ)いた。
A stranger approached me, so I was a little surprised.
火(ひ)に立(た)ち寄(よ)ると危(あぶ)ないから、離(はな)れていなさい。
It's dangerous to go near the fire, so stay away.

立(た)ち寄(よ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)).

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 立(た)つ (to stand) + 寄(よ)る (to approach) → to stop and approach

SENSE 1 (to stop by):

ASPECT (ている): 立(た)ち寄(よ)っている indicates being at a place one has stopped by.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～に立(た)ち寄(よ)る (stop by~)
  • 帰(かえ)りに立(た)ち寄(よ)る (stop by on the way home)
  • ついでに立(た)ち寄(よ)る (stop by while you're at it)
  • お立(た)ち寄(よ)りください (please stop by - polite)

SENSE 2 (to approach, to draw near):

  • ～に立(た)ち寄(よ)る (approach~, draw near to~)
  • 立(た)ち寄(よ)ってくる (come closer)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 立(た)ち寄(よ)り: a stop, stopover; 立(た)ち寄(よ)り先(さき) = place one stops by

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寄(よ)る: to stop by (simpler, more casual); also to approach
  • 訪(たず)ねる: to visit (more formal, planned)
