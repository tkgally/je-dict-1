エモい

えもい
adjective (i-adjective)
emotional, moving
1. emotional, moving, nostalgic
Describing something that evokes strong emotions, often nostalgia or sentimentality. From English 'emo'.
この曲(きょく)、めっちゃエモい。
This song is so emotional.
夕焼(ゆうや)けがエモい写真(しゃしん)が撮(と)れた。
I took an emotional photo of the sunset.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)はエモかった。
The graduation ceremony was so moving.

ETYMOLOGY:
From English 'emotional' or 'emo'. Youth slang that became popular in late 2010s Japan.

USAGE:

  • Describes things evoking strong positive emotions
  • Often nostalgia, sentimentality, aesthetic beauty
  • Common for: 写真(しゃしん), 音楽(おんがく), 景色(けしき), 思(おも)い出(で)

CONJUGATION (i-adjective):

  • エモい (plain)
  • エモかった (past)
  • エモくない (negative)
  • エモすぎる (too emotional)

RELATED SLANG:

  • エモーショナル: emotional (formal loanword)
  • 懐(なつ)かしい: nostalgic (standard Japanese)
  • グッとくる: to be moved
