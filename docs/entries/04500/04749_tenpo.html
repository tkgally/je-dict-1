テンポ

てんぽ
noun
tempo

Definitions

1. tempo
The speed at which music is played.
2. pace, tempo
The speed or pace of an activity or conversation.

Examples

この曲(きょく)はテンポが速(はや)い。
This song has a fast tempo.
会話(かいわ)のテンポがいい。
The conversation flows at a good pace.
もう少(すこ)しテンポを落(お)としてください。
Please slow down the tempo a bit.

Notes

From Italian "tempo." Used for both musical and general speed/pace.

Common expressions:

  • テンポが速(はや)い/遅(おそ)い (fast/slow tempo)
  • テンポを上(あ)げる/落(お)とす (to speed up/slow down)
