動(うご)き回(まわ)る

うごきまわる
godan verb
to move around, to move about

Definitions

1. to move around, to move about
To change position or location repeatedly, moving in various directions.
2. to be active, to be on the move
To be physically active, not staying still.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんが部屋(へや)の中(なか)を動(うご)き回(まわ)っている。
The baby is moving around the room.
怪我(けが)をしたら、動(うご)き回(まわ)らないでください。
If you're injured, please don't move around.
猫(ねこ)が落(お)ち着(つ)かない様子(ようす)で動(うご)き回(まわ)っていた。
The cat was moving around restlessly.

Notes

動(うご)き回(まわ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to move around.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 動(うご)く (to move) + 回(まわ)る (to go around) → to move around

ASPECT (ている):
動(うご)き回(まわ)っている indicates an ongoing state of moving around, often restlessly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 落(お)ち着(つ)かず動(うご)き回(まわ)る (move around restlessly)
  • あちこち動(うご)き回(まわ)る (move around everywhere)
  • 自由(じゆう)に動(うご)き回(まわ)る (move around freely)

USAGE NOTE:
More general than 歩(ある)き回(まわ)る (walking) or 走(はし)り回(まわ)る (running). Can be used for any kind of movement including crawling, shuffling, etc.
