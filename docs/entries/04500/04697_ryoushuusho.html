領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)

りょうしゅうしょ
noun
receipt
1. receipt; proof of payment
An official document acknowledging that payment has been received.
領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)をください。
May I have a receipt, please?
経費(けいひ)精算(せいさん)には領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Receipts are needed for expense reimbursement.
領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)に宛名(あてな)を書(か)いてもらった。
I had them write a name/address on the receipt.

領収書(りょうしゅうしょ) is a compound: 領収(りょうしゅう) (receipt of payment) + 書(しょ) (document).

CONTRAST:

  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ) (formal receipt, often for business expenses)
  • レシート (cash register receipt, informal)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)をもらう (to get a receipt)
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)を発行(はっこう)する (to issue a receipt)
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)を切(き)る (to write/issue a receipt)

JAPANESE RECEIPT FORMAT:

  • 宛名(あてな) (recipient name - often 「上様(うえさま)」for anonymous)
  • 金額(きんがく) (amount)
  • 但(ただ)し書(が)き (description of goods/services)
  • 日付(ひづけ) (date)

CULTURAL NOTE:
「上様(うえさま)」is commonly written as recipient name when specific name isn't needed.
