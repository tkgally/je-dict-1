リモート

りもーと
noun, prefix
remote
1. remote (work/meeting)
Doing something from a distance, especially work or meetings conducted online rather than in person.
今日(きょう)はリモートで仕事(しごと)をします。
I'll work remotely today.
リモート会議(かいぎ)に参加(さんか)してください。
Please join the remote meeting.
リモートワークが普及(ふきゅう)しました。
Remote work has become widespread.

リモート refers to activities done from a distance, especially work conducted online rather than in-person.

ETYMOLOGY:
From English 'remote.' Became widespread after 2020 due to pandemic-related work changes.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • リモートワーク: remote work
  • リモート会議(かいぎ): remote meeting
  • リモート勤務(きんむ): remote work/attendance
  • リモート授業(じゅぎょう): remote classes
  • リモート面接(めんせつ): remote interview

USAGE:
Often shortened to リモ in casual speech. Can function as a noun or prefix. Contrast with 対面(たいめん) (in-person, face-to-face).
