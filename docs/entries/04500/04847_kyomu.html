虚無(きょむ)

きょむ
noun
nihility, emptiness
1. nihility, emptiness, void
The state of nothingness; philosophical emptiness or meaninglessness.
虚無感(きょむかん)に襲(おそ)われた。
I was overcome by a sense of emptiness.
虚無的(きょむてき)な人生観(じんせいかん)。
A nihilistic view of life.
虚無(きょむ)の中(なか)に生(い)きている。
Living in emptiness.

A philosophical term often used in existentialist contexts. Related: 虚無主義(きょむしゅぎ) (nihilism), 虚無感(きょむかん) (sense of emptiness). More literary than 空(から)っぽ.
