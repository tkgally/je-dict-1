憎悪(ぞうお)

ぞうお
noun, verb (suru)
hatred, animosity

Definitions

1. hatred, animosity, loathing
Intense dislike or hostility toward someone or something.

Examples

憎悪(ぞうお)を抱(いだ)く。
To harbor hatred.
憎悪(ぞうお)に満(み)ちた目(め)で見(み)つめた。
They gazed with eyes full of hatred.
憎悪(ぞうお)が憎悪(ぞうお)を生(う)む。
Hatred breeds hatred.

Notes

A formal word for intense hatred. Stronger and more literary than 嫌(きら)い or 憎(にく)む. Often used in dramatic or serious contexts. 憎悪犯罪(ぞうおはんざい) (hate crime).
