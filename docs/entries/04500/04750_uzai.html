ウザい

うざい
adjective (i-adjective)
annoying
1. annoying, irritating
Describing something or someone that is bothersome or gets on one's nerves. Slang.
あの人(ひと)、ウザいんだよね。
That person is so annoying.
ウザい広告(こうこく)が多(おお)すぎる。
There are too many annoying ads.
ウザいからやめてくれない？
It's annoying, can you stop?

Slang abbreviation of 鬱陶(うっとう)しい (depressing, annoying). Common among youth. Can be considered rude when directed at people. Often written in katakana for emphasis. Conjugates as i-adjective: ウザくない, ウザかった.
