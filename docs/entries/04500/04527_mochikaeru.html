持(も)ち帰(かえ)る

もちかえる
verb (godan)
to take home; to bring back

Definitions

1. to take home; to carry back
To bring something back to one's home or original location.
2. to take away (food)
To order food for takeout rather than eating at the restaurant.

Examples

会議(かいぎ)の資料(しりょう)を持(も)ち帰(かえ)って読(よ)んだ。
I took the meeting materials home and read them.
お持(も)ち帰(かえ)りですか、店内(てんない)でお召(め)し上(あ)がりですか。
Is that for takeout, or will you be eating here?
旅行先(りょこうさき)から素敵(すてき)な思(おも)い出(で)を持(も)ち帰(かえ)った。
I brought back wonderful memories from my trip.

Notes

持(も)ち帰(かえ)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 持(も)つ (to hold) + 帰(かえ)る (to return). It means to take something back with you.

ASPECT (ている):
持(も)ち帰(かえ)っている indicates the state of having brought something back.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を持(も)ち帰(かえ)る — to take [thing] home
  • [place]から[thing]を持(も)ち帰(かえ)る — to bring [thing] back from [place]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を持(も)ち帰(かえ)る (take work home)
  • お土産(みやげ)を持(も)ち帰(かえ)る (bring back souvenirs)
  • 持(も)ち帰(かえ)り弁当(べんとう) (takeout bento)

TAKEOUT USAGE:
お持(も)ち帰(かえ)り (with honorific お) is the standard term for takeout food. テイクアウト is also used but お持(も)ち帰(かえ)り is more traditional.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 持(も)ち込(こ)む: to bring in (opposite direction)
  • 持(も)って帰(かえ)る: same meaning, less compound-like
