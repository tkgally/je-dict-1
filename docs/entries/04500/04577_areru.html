荒(あ)れる

verb (ichidan)
to be rough; to be stormy; to become wild

1. to be rough; to be stormy
When weather, sea, or natural conditions become turbulent and violent.
台風(たいふう)で海(うみ)が荒(あ)れている。
The sea is rough because of the typhoon.
天気(てんき)が荒(あ)れる予報(よほう)だ。
The forecast says the weather will be stormy.
波(なみ)が荒(あ)れて船(ふね)が出(で)られない。
The waves are rough, so the boats can't go out.
嵐(あらし)で海(うみ)が荒(あ)れ狂(くる)う。
The sea rages wildly in the storm.
風(かぜ)が荒(あ)れて飛行機(ひこうき)が揺(ゆ)れた。
The wind was rough and the plane shook.
2. to become rough; to become chapped
When skin or hands become dry and rough.
冬(ふゆ)になると手(て)が荒(あ)れやすい。
In winter, my hands tend to get chapped.
肌(はだ)が荒(あ)れて痛(いた)い。
My skin is rough and it hurts.
唇(くちびる)が荒(あ)れている。
My lips are chapped.
乾燥(かんそう)で手(て)が荒(あ)れる。
Dryness makes my hands rough.
洗剤(せんざい)で肌(はだ)が荒(あ)れた。
My skin got rough from the detergent.
3. to fall into ruin; to become dilapidated
When a place becomes neglected and run-down.
空(あ)き家(や)が荒(あ)れている。
The vacant house has fallen into disrepair.
庭(にわ)が荒(あ)れ放題(ほうだい)だ。
The garden has become completely overgrown.
戦争(せんそう)で町(まち)が荒(あ)れた。
The town fell into ruin from the war.
畑(はたけ)が荒(あ)れて雑草(ざっそう)だらけだ。
The field has become overgrown with weeds.
廃墟(はいきょ)が荒(あ)れ果(は)ている。
The ruins have completely fallen into decay.
4. to become unruly; to get wild
When a situation, meeting, or behavior becomes chaotic or rough.
会議(かいぎ)が荒(あ)れて、収拾(しゅうしゅう)がつかなくなった。
The meeting became chaotic and got out of control.
生活(せいかつ)が荒(あ)れてきた。
Their lifestyle has become chaotic.
試合(しあい)が荒(あ)れた。
The match got rough.
教室(きょうしつ)が荒(あ)れている。
The classroom is in disorder.
討論(とうろん)が荒(あ)れて怒鳴(どな)り合(あ)いになった。
The debate got heated and turned into a shouting match.

荒(あ)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 荒(あ)らす (to lay waste, to devastate).

ASPECT (ている):
荒(あ)れている indicates a resulting state of being rough, stormy, or dilapidated.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 海(うみ)が荒(あ)れる - the sea becomes rough
  • 天気(てんき)が荒(あ)れる - the weather becomes stormy
  • 肌(はだ)が荒(あ)れる - skin becomes rough
  • 生活(せいかつ)が荒(あ)れる - lifestyle becomes unruly

荒(あ)れる VS 暴(あば)れる:
荒(あ)れる describes a state of roughness or turbulence, while 暴(あば)れる describes active violent behavior. The sea 荒(あ)れる (becomes rough), but a person 暴(あば)れる (acts violently).
