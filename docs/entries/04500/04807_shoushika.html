少子化(しょうしか)

しょうしか
noun
declining birthrate
1. declining birthrate, falling birthrate
The trend of decreasing numbers of children being born in a population.
少子化(しょうしか)が進(すす)んでいる。
The birthrate is declining.
少子化(しょうしか)対策(たいさく)が急務(きゅうむ)だ。
Measures against the declining birthrate are urgently needed.
少子化(しょうしか)で学校(がっこう)が統合(とうごう)された。
Schools were consolidated due to the declining birthrate.

A major social issue in Japan along with 高齢化(こうれいか) (aging population). Often mentioned together as 少子(しょうし)高齢化(こうれいか) (declining birthrate and aging population).

Common expressions:

  • 少子化(しょうしか)対策(たいさく) (measures against declining birthrate)
  • 少子(しょうし)高齢化(こうれいか) (declining birthrate and aging population)
  • 少子化(しょうしか)社会(しゃかい) (society with declining birthrate)
