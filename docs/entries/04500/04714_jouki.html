蒸気(じょうき)

じょうき
noun
steam, vapor

Definitions

1. steam, vapor
Water in its gaseous state, produced when water is heated to boiling point; also used for vapor in general.

Examples

やかんから蒸気(じょうき)が出(で)ている。
Steam is coming out of the kettle.
蒸気(じょうき)機関車(きかんしゃ)は今(いま)ではほとんど見(み)られない。
Steam locomotives are rarely seen nowadays.
蒸気機関車 = steam locomotive
お風呂(ふろ)の蒸気(じょうき)で鏡(かがみ)が曇(くも)った。
The mirror fogged up from the steam in the bath.

Notes

蒸気(じょうき) refers specifically to water vapor or steam. It played a crucial role in the Industrial Revolution and remains important in modern applications.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 蒸気(じょうき)機関(きかん): steam engine
  • 蒸気(じょうき)機関車(きかんしゃ): steam locomotive (often abbreviated SL)
  • 蒸気(じょうき)船(せん): steamship
  • 水蒸気(すいじょうき): water vapor

RELATED WORDS:

  • 湯気(ゆげ): steam (visible vapor rising from hot water, often used in everyday contexts like food or baths)
