Our company has an atmosphere where it's easy to take paid leave, which is a big help.

有給(ゆうきゅう) is short for 有給休暇(ゆうきゅうきゅうか) (paid vacation/leave). It refers to paid time off from work.

Full form: 有給休暇(ゆうきゅうきゅうか)

Common expressions:

  • 有給(ゆうきゅう)を取(と)る (take paid leave)
  • 有給(ゆうきゅう)を使(つか)う (use paid leave)
  • 有給(ゆうきゅう)消化(しょうか) (using up paid leave)
  • 有給(ゆうきゅう)残日数(ざんにっすう) (remaining paid leave days)

Cultural note:
Japanese workers often hesitate to use their paid leave due to workplace culture. Recent labor laws require companies to ensure employees take at least 5 days of paid leave annually.
