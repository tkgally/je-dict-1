ほかほか

ほかほか
adverb, na-adjective, verb (suru)
warm, steaming
1. warm, steaming
Pleasantly warm; describing freshly cooked food or warm feelings.
ほかほかの焼(や)きたてパンを食(た)べた。
I ate freshly baked, warm bread.
体(からだ)がほかほかしてきた。
My body started to feel warm.
ほかほかの御飯(ごはん)が食(た)べたい。
I want to eat steaming hot rice.

A pleasant onomatopoeia describing comfortable warmth. Often used for freshly cooked food. Related to the store name ほっかほっか亭(てい) (bento chain). Evokes a cozy, satisfying feeling.
