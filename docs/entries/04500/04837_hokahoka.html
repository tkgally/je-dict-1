1. warm, steaming
Pleasantly warm; describing freshly cooked food or warm feelings.
ほかほかの焼(や)きたてパンを食(た)べた。
I ate freshly baked, warm bread.
体(からだ)がほかほかしてきた。
My body started to feel warm.
ほかほかの御飯(ごはん)が食(た)べたい。
I want to eat steaming hot rice.

ほかほか is an onomatopoeia describing pleasant warmth, especially from freshly cooked food.

GRAMMAR:

  • Adverb: ほかほかと温(あたた)かい
  • Na-adjective: ほかほかの + noun
  • Suru verb: 体(からだ)がほかほかする

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ほかほかの御飯(ごはん): steaming hot rice
  • ほかほかの弁当(べんとう): hot/warm bento
  • ほかほかに温(あたた)まる: to get nice and warm

NUANCE:
Conveys pleasant, comforting warmth - not scorching hot. Associated with freshly cooked food, warm drinks, or the comfortable feeling after a bath.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あつあつ: piping hot (hotter than ほかほか)
  • ぽかぽか: warm and cozy (body warmth)

CULTURAL NOTE:
ほっかほっか亭(てい) is a popular bento chain whose name derives from this word.

Related Words

Synonym: ぽかぽか (warm and cozy)
Related: ぬくぬく (snugly warm)
