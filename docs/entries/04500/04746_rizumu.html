リズム

りずむ
noun
rhythm
1. rhythm
A regular pattern of beats or sounds in music.
この曲(きょく)はリズムがいい。
This song has a good rhythm.
リズムに合(あ)わせて踊(おど)る。
Dance to the rhythm.
2. rhythm, pace
A regular pattern or flow in life or activities.
生活(せいかつ)のリズムを整(ととの)える。
Establish a regular rhythm in your life.

リズム refers to both musical rhythm and the pace/pattern of activities or life.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • リズムに乗(の)る: to get into the rhythm
  • リズムを取(と)る: to keep rhythm
  • リズムを刻(きざ)む: to mark/beat out rhythm
  • リズム感(かん): sense of rhythm

COMPOUNDS:

  • 生活(せいかつ)リズム: daily rhythm/routine
  • 仕事(しごと)のリズム: work pace
  • 体内(たいない)リズム: biological rhythm

USAGE:
In the "pace of life" sense, リズムを整(ととの)える (to regulate one's rhythm) is commonly used when talking about establishing healthy routines.

Note: From English "rhythm."
