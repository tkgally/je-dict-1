軽蔑(けいべつ)

けいべつ
noun, verb (suru)
contempt, scorn

Definitions

1. contempt, scorn, disdain
Looking down on someone or something with disrespect.

Examples

彼(かれ)を軽蔑(けいべつ)している。
I despise him.
軽蔑(けいべつ)の眼差(まなざ)しで見(み)られた。
I was looked at with contempt.
嘘(うそ)つきは軽蔑(けいべつ)される。
Liars are looked down upon.

Notes

A strong negative word. 軽(けい) (light/trivial) + 蔑(べつ) (despise). Often used with する to show the action of despising. More formal than 馬鹿(ばか)にする.
