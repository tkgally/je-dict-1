具体的(ぐたいてき)

ぐたいてき
na-adjective
concrete, specific
1. concrete, specific, tangible
Clear and specific rather than vague or abstract. With actual details or examples.
もっと具体的(ぐたいてき)に説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain more specifically.
具体的(ぐたいてき)な例(れい)を挙(あ)げてもらえますか？
Could you give me a concrete example?
具体的(ぐたいてき)な計画(けいかく)を立(た)てましょう。
Let's make a concrete plan.

具体的(ぐたいてき) is a na-adjective meaning "concrete" or "specific," describing something with clear details rather than vague generalities.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 具体的(ぐたいてき)な例(れい) (a concrete example)
  • Adverb: 具体的(ぐたいてき)に (concretely, specifically)
  • Noun: 具体性(ぐたいせい) (concreteness)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 具体的(ぐたいてき)な例(れい): concrete example
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき)な計画(けいかく): specific plan
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき)な方法(ほうほう): specific method
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき)に説明(せつめい)する: to explain specifically

CONTRAST:

  • 具体的(ぐたいてき): concrete, specific (with details)
  • 抽象的(ちゅうしょうてき): abstract (theoretical, general)

USAGE CONTEXT:
Very common in business meetings, academic discussions, and everyday conversations when requesting or providing clearer information.

Related Words

Antonym: 抽象的(ちゅうしょうてき) (abstract)
