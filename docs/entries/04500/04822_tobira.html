扉(とびら)

とびら
noun
door

Definitions

1. door; gate (hinged, swinging)
door; gate (hinged, swinging)

Examples

扉(とびら)を開(あ)けると、広(ひろ)い庭(にわ)が見(み)えた。
When I opened the door, I saw a spacious garden.
重(おも)い扉(とびら)を押(お)して中(なか)に入(はい)った。
I pushed the heavy door and entered inside.
新(あたら)しい扉(とびら)を開(ひら)くことを恐(おそ)れないでください。
Don't be afraid to open new doors (figurative).

Notes

扉(とびら) specifically refers to a hinged door that swings open, as opposed to sliding doors. It often has a more literary or formal nuance than ドア.

Distinctions:

  • 扉(とびら): hinged door (literary, includes gates)
  • ドア: door (casual loanword, common in daily speech)
  • 戸(と): general term for door/sliding door
  • 障子(しょうじ): paper sliding door
  • 襖(ふすま): opaque sliding door

Figurative usage:

  • 新(あたら)しい扉(とびら)を開(ひら)く (open new doors/possibilities)
  • 心(こころ)の扉(とびら) (the door of one's heart)
