へらへら

へらへら
adverb, verb (suru)
grinning foolishly

Definitions

1. grinning foolishly, laughing emptily
Describing an insincere or inappropriate smile or laugh; being frivolous.

Examples

へらへら笑(わら)っていないで、真面目(まじめ)にしなさい。
Stop grinning foolishly and be serious.
彼(かれ)はいつもへらへらしている。
He's always acting frivolously.
へらへらした態度(たいど)が気(き)に入(い)らない。
I don't like his frivolous attitude.

Notes

Generally has a negative connotation, suggesting inappropriateness or lack of seriousness. Often used critically about someone's demeanor. Contrast with にこにこ (pleasant smile).
