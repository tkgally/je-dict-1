染(そ)める

そめる
verb (ichidan)
to dye; to color

Definitions

1. to dye; to color
To change the color of fabric, hair, or other materials using dye.
2. to tinge; to stain
For color or emotion to spread across something, often used poetically.

Examples

髪(かみ)を茶色(ちゃいろ)に染(そ)めた。
I dyed my hair brown.
夕日(ゆうひ)が空(そら)を赤(あか)く染(そ)めている。
The sunset is dyeing the sky red.
恥(は)ずかしさで頬(ほお)を染(そ)めた。
Her cheeks flushed with embarrassment.

Notes

染(そ)める is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to dye or color something.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 染(そ)まる (to be dyed, to be tainted)

  • 染(そ)める: I dye the cloth.
  • 染(そ)まる: The cloth gets dyed.

ASPECT (ている):
染(そ)めている indicates the dyeing process is ongoing, or something has been dyed.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を[color]に染(そ)める — to dye [thing] [color]
  • [subject]が[thing]を染(そ)める — [subject] dyes/tinges [thing]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 髪(かみ)を染(そ)める (dye hair)
  • 布(ぬの)を染(そ)める (dye cloth)
  • 空(そら)を染(そ)める (tinge the sky - poetic)
  • 頬(ほお)を染(そ)める (blush - literally "dye one's cheeks")

POETIC USE:
頬(ほお)を染(そ)める is a refined expression for blushing, often used in literature.
