羊(ひつじ)

ひつじ
noun
sheep
1. sheep
A domesticated ruminant animal kept for its wool and meat. Counter: 匹(ひき) or 頭(とう).
羊(ひつじ)の毛(け)でセーターを作(つく)ります。
Sweaters are made from sheep's wool.
羊(ひつじ)を数(かぞ)えると眠(ねむ)くなると言(い)われています。
They say counting sheep makes you sleepy.
羊(ひつじ)は群(む)れで行動(こうどう)する動物(どうぶつ)です。
Sheep are animals that move in flocks.

羊(ひつじ) refers to sheep, a domesticated animal raised for wool and meat.

COUNTERS:

  • 一匹(いっぴき), 二匹(にひき)... (small animals)
  • 一頭(いっとう), 二頭(にとう)... (larger animals, formal)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 羊毛(ようもう): wool
  • 羊肉(ようにく): mutton/lamb (meat)
  • ラム: lamb (meat, from English)
  • 牧羊(ぼくよう): sheep farming

ZODIAC:
One of the twelve zodiac animals. Written as 未(ひつじ) in zodiac context (未年(ひつじどし) - Year of the Sheep).

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 迷(まよ)える羊(ひつじ): lost sheep (metaphor for lost/confused person)
  • 羊(ひつじ)を数(かぞ)える: to count sheep (to fall asleep)
