すれすれ

すれすれ
noun, na-adjective
just barely, grazing
1. just barely, grazing
Almost touching; very close to something; by a narrow margin.
電車(でんしゃ)にすれすれで間(ま)に合(あ)った。
I just barely made the train.
頭(あたま)をすれすれでかわした。
I barely dodged it by a hair.
合格(ごうかく)点(てん)すれすれで受(う)かった。
I passed with barely a passing score.

すれすれ describes being just barely within limits or narrowly avoiding/achieving something.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 擦(す)れる (to rub against) - the nuance of just grazing or brushing past.

TWO MAIN USES:
1. Physical closeness: 頭(あたま)すれすれで通(とお)る (pass just over one's head)
2. Figurative: 合格(ごうかく)点(てん)すれすれ (barely passing score)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • すれすれで間(ま)に合(あ)う: to just barely make it
  • すれすれで避(さ)ける: to barely dodge
  • 法律(ほうりつ)すれすれ: borderline legal
  • 違反(いはん)すれすれ: on the edge of violation

NUANCE:
Implies a narrow margin - close to failure, contact, or crossing a line. Can describe both physical proximity and abstract limits.
