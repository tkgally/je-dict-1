すれすれ

すれすれ
noun, na-adjective
just barely, grazing

Definitions

1. just barely, grazing
Almost touching; very close to something; by a narrow margin.

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)にすれすれで間(ま)に合(あ)った。
I just barely made the train.
頭(あたま)をすれすれでかわした。
I barely dodged it by a hair.
合格(ごうかく)点(てん)すれすれで受(う)かった。
I passed with barely a passing score.

Notes

Describes narrowly avoiding or achieving something; being on the edge. Often used with で (by the margin of) or when describing close calls. Derived from 擦(す)れる (to rub against).
