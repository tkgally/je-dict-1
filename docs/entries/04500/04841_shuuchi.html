羞恥(しゅうち)

しゅうち
noun
shame, embarrassment
1. shame, embarrassment
A feeling of embarrassment or shame, especially regarding one's actions or appearance.
羞恥心(しゅうちしん)がない人(ひと)だ。
They have no sense of shame.
羞恥(しゅうち)に耐(た)えられなかった。
I couldn't bear the embarrassment.
彼女(かのじょ)は羞恥(しゅうち)で顔(かお)を赤(あか)くした。
She blushed with embarrassment.

More formal/literary than 恥(は)ずかしさ. Often appears in compound 羞恥心(しゅうちしん) (sense of shame). Used in psychological and formal contexts.
