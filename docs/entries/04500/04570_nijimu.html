滲(にじ)む

にじむ
verb (godan)
to blur; to run; to ooze

Definitions

1. to blur; to run; to spread
When ink, colors, or liquid spreads and blurs on a surface.
2. to ooze; to seep out
When liquid gradually comes out from something, such as sweat or blood.
3. to show through; to be tinged with
Figuratively, when emotions or qualities subtly show through someone's expression or words.

Examples

雨(あめ)で手紙(てがみ)のインクが滲(にじ)んでしまった。
The ink on the letter blurred because of the rain.
額(ひたい)から汗(あせ)が滲(にじ)み出(で)てきた。
Sweat started oozing out from my forehead.
彼(かれ)の声(こえ)には悲(かな)しみが滲(にじ)んでいた。
Sadness was showing through in his voice.

Notes

滲(にじ)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive form is 滲(にじ)ませる (to let something blur/ooze).

ASPECT ({ている}):
滲(にじ)んでいる indicates a resulting state where something has already blurred or oozed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • インクが滲(にじ)む - ink blurs/runs
  • 汗(あせ)が滲(にじ)む - sweat oozes
  • 涙(なみだ)が滲(にじ)む - tears well up
  • 感情(かんじょう)が滲(にじ)む - emotion shows through

USAGE NOTE:
Often used with 出(で)る in the compound 滲(にじ)み出(で)る to emphasize the gradual emergence of liquid.
