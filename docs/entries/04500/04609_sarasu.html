1. to expose; to leave out in the open
To expose something to natural elements like sun, wind, or rain.
布団(ふとん)を日(ひ)に晒(さら)す。
To air out the futon in the sun.
洗(あら)った米(こめ)を風(かぜ)に晒(さら)して乾(かわ)かした。
I dried the washed rice by exposing it to the wind.
木材(もくざい)を雨(あめ)や日光(にっこう)に長(なが)い間(あいだ)晒(さら)しておくと、色(いろ)が変(か)わってしまう。
If you leave lumber exposed to rain and sunlight for a long time, its color will change.
2. to bleach; to whiten
To whiten fabric by exposing it to sunlight or using bleaching agents.
布(ぬの)を日(ひ)に晒(さら)す。
To bleach cloth in the sun.
麻(あさ)の糸(いと)を川(かわ)の水(みず)で晒(さら)して白(しろ)くした。
I whitened the linen thread by bleaching it in river water.
昔(むかし)の人(ひと)は太陽(たいよう)の光(ひかり)を利用(りよう)して布(ぬの)を晒(さら)していたが、今(いま)では化学薬品(かがくやくひん)を使(つか)うことが多(おお)い。
People in the old days bleached cloth using sunlight, but nowadays chemical agents are more commonly used.
3. to expose to danger/ridicule; to make public
To put oneself or something at risk, or to make something publicly known.
危険(きけん)に身(み)を晒(さら)す。
To expose oneself to danger.
恥(はじ)を晒(さら)すことになった。
I ended up making a fool of myself.
彼(かれ)は不正(ふせい)の証拠(しょうこ)をネットに晒(さら)して、会社(かいしゃ)の秘密(ひみつ)を世間(せけん)に知(し)らしめた。
He posted evidence of fraud online and made the company's secrets known to the public.

晒(さら)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive/passive form is 晒(さら)される.

ASPECT (ている):
晒(さら)している indicates an ongoing state of exposure.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日(ひ)/風(かぜ)に晒(さら)す - to expose to sun/wind
  • 身(み)を晒(さら)す - to expose oneself (to danger)
  • 恥(はじ)を晒(さら)す - to disgrace oneself, to make a fool of oneself
  • 個人情報(こじんじょうほう)を晒(さら)す - to expose personal information (often online)

INTERNET USAGE:
In modern usage, 晒(さら)す is commonly used to mean exposing or posting someone's private information online, often with malicious intent.

RELATED KANJI:
曝(さら)す is an alternative kanji, sometimes used specifically for "exposing to elements."
