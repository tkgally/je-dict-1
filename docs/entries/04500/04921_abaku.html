暴(あば)く

あばく
verb (godan)
to expose; to reveal; to unmask

Definitions

1. to expose; to reveal; to unmask
To bring hidden wrongdoing, secrets, or lies to light.
2. to dig up; to exhume
To disturb or open something that was buried or concealed.

Examples

記者(きしゃ)が政治家(せいじか)の不正(ふせい)を暴(あば)いた。
The journalist exposed the politician's corruption.
嘘(うそ)を暴(あば)かれて、彼(かれ)は黙(だま)り込(こ)んだ。
When his lies were exposed, he fell silent.
真実(しんじつ)を暴(あば)くことが正義(せいぎ)だと信(しん)じている。
I believe that exposing the truth is justice.

Notes

暴(あば)く is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive counterpart is 暴(あば)かれる (passive: to be exposed).

ASPECT ({ている}):
暴(あば)いている indicates an ongoing process of exposing something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 不正(ふせい)を暴(あば)く - to expose corruption
  • 嘘(うそ)を暴(あば)く - to unmask lies
  • 真実(しんじつ)を暴(あば)く - to reveal the truth
  • 秘密(ひみつ)を暴(あば)く - to expose a secret
  • 正体(しょうたい)を暴(あば)く - to unmask someone's true identity

NUANCE:
This verb is typically used in serious contexts involving wrongdoing or hidden truths. It carries a sense of forcefully bringing something shameful or secret into the open.
