1. to compete with each other
To engage in competition with one another, each trying to outperform the other.
二人(ふたり)の選手(せんしゅ)が競(きそ)い合(あ)っている。
Two athletes are competing against each other.
企業(きぎょう)同士(どうし)が激(はげ)しく競(きそ)い合(あ)っている。
Companies are fiercely competing with each other.
お互(たが)いに競(きそ)い合(あ)うことで成長(せいちょう)できる。
We can grow by competing with each other.
2. to vie, to rival each other
To strive to surpass each other in achievement or quality.
各社(かくしゃ)が品質(ひんしつ)を競(きそ)い合(あ)っている。
The companies are vying with each other over quality.
二(ふた)つの店(みせ)が値段(ねだん)を競(きそ)い合(あ)う。
Two stores rival each other on price.
世界中(せかいじゅう)の研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)が新(あたら)しい発見(はっけん)を競(きそ)い合(あ)っている。
Researchers around the world are vying with each other for new discoveries.

競(きそ)い合(あ)う is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to compete with each other.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 競(きそ)う (to compete) + 合(あ)う (each other) → to compete with each other

ASPECT (ている):
競(きそ)い合(あ)っている indicates an ongoing competition.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 激(はげ)しく競(きそ)い合(あ)う (compete fiercely)
  • 互(たが)いに競(きそ)い合(あ)う (compete with each other)
  • 切磋琢磨(せっさたくま)して競(きそ)い合(あ)う (compete while improving each other)

NUANCE:
Implies mutual competition where both parties are actively trying to outdo each other. Often used in positive contexts where competition leads to improvement.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 競(きそ)う: to compete (not necessarily mutual)
  • 競争(きょうそう)する: to compete (more formal)

Related Words

Related: 競(きそ)う (to compete (base verb))
