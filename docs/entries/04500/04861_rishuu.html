履修(りしゅう)

りしゅう
noun (verbal)
course registration
1. course registration, taking a course
The act of registering for and completing academic courses.
来学期(らいがっき)の科目(かもく)を履修(りしゅう)登録(とうろく)する。
Register for courses for next semester.
必修(ひっしゅう)科目(かもく)を履修(りしゅう)する。
Take required courses.
履修(りしゅう)登録(とうろく)の期限(きげん)が迫(せま)っている。
The course registration deadline is approaching.

履修(りしゅう) refers to registering for and taking academic courses, common in university contexts.

USAGE:
Used with する: 履修(りしゅう)する (to take/register for a course)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 履修(りしゅう)登録(とうろく): course registration
  • 履修(りしゅう)科目(かもく): courses being taken
  • 必修(ひっしゅう)科目(かもく)を履修(りしゅう)する: to take required courses
  • 選択(せんたく)科目(かもく)を履修(りしゅう)する: to take elective courses

RELATED ACADEMIC VOCABULARY:

  • 単位(たんい): credit
  • 卒業(そつぎょう): graduation
  • 必修(ひっしゅう): required (course)
