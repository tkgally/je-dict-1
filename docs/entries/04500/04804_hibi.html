日々(ひび)

ひび
noun, adverb
days, daily
1. days, every day
Multiple days; can refer to the passing of time or daily activities.
2. daily (adverb)
Day by day; every day.
日々(ひび)の生活(せいかつ)を大切(たいせつ)にする。
To value daily life.
日々(ひび)成長(せいちょう)しています。
I'm growing day by day.
忙(いそが)しい日々(ひび)を送(おく)っている。
I'm spending busy days.

A reduplication of 日(ひ) (day). More literary/formal than 毎日(まいにち). Often used in written language and when reflecting on time passing. Common in phrases like 日々(ひび)の生活(せいかつ) (daily life).

Related Words

Synonym: 毎日(まいにち) (everyday)
