日々(ひび)

ひび
noun, adverb
days, daily
1. days, every day
Multiple days; can refer to the passing of time or daily activities.
日々(ひび)の生活(せいかつ)を大切(たいせつ)にする。
To value daily life.
忙(いそが)しい日々(ひび)を送(おく)っている。
I'm spending busy days.
2. daily (adverb)
Day by day; every day.
日々(ひび)成長(せいちょう)しています。
I'm growing day by day.

日々(ひび) is a literary/formal word meaning "days" or "daily," formed by reduplication of 日(ひ).

USAGE TYPES:
1. Noun: multiple days, the passing days
2. Adverb: day by day, daily

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 日々(ひび)の生活(せいかつ): daily life
  • 日々(ひび)成長(せいちょう)する: to grow day by day
  • 日々(ひび)を送(おく)る: to spend one's days
  • 忙(いそが)しい日々(ひび): busy days

REGISTER:
More literary and formal than 毎日(まいにち). Often used when reflecting on time passing or in written/poetic contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 毎日(まいにち): every day (neutral, everyday usage)
  • 日常(にちじょう): daily life, everyday (noun)
  • 年々(ねんねん): year by year (same pattern)

Related Words

Synonym: 毎日(まいにち) (everyday)
