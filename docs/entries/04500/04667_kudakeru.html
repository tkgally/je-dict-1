砕(くだ)ける

くだける
verb (ichidan)
to break into pieces; to become informal

Definitions

1. to break into pieces; to be smashed
For something hard to break apart or shatter into fragments.
2. to become informal; to relax
For an atmosphere or person to become more casual and relaxed.

Examples

氷(こおり)が細(こま)かく砕(くだ)けた。
The ice broke into small pieces.
波(なみ)が岩(いわ)に砕(くだ)ける。
Waves break against the rocks.
砕(くだ)けた表現(ひょうげん)で話(はな)してください。
Please speak in casual language.

Notes

砕(くだ)ける is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pair: 砕(くだ)く (transitive - to smash, crush)
  • Pattern: Xが砕(くだ)ける

ASPECT ({ている}):
砕(くだ)けている describes a result state (something is broken) or an ongoing casual atmosphere.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 波(なみ)が砕(くだ)ける (waves break)
  • 氷(こおり)が砕(くだ)ける (ice shatters)
  • 砕(くだ)けた雰囲気(ふんいき) (casual atmosphere)
  • 砕(くだ)けた表現(ひょうげん) (casual expression)

The figurative sense 2 is common in describing casual speech or relaxed social situations.
