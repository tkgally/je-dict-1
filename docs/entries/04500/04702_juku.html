塾(じゅく)

じゅく
noun
cram school; private tutoring school
1. cram school; private tutoring school
A private school offering supplementary education, often for exam preparation.
子供(こども)を塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)わせている。
I'm sending my child to cram school.
塾(じゅく)で英語(えいご)を習(なら)っている。
I'm studying English at cram school.
受験(じゅけん)のために塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)い始(はじ)めた。
I started going to cram school for exam preparation.
塾(じゅく)の先生(せんせい)の教(おし)え方(かた)がとても分(わ)かりやすい。
The cram school teacher's way of teaching is very easy to understand.
学校(がっこう)が終(お)わった後(あと)、週(しゅう)に3回(かい)塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)っているので、毎日(まいにち)忙(いそが)しい。
I go to cram school three times a week after school, so every day is busy.

TYPES OF 塾(じゅく):

  • 学習(がくしゅう)塾(じゅく) (general study cram school)
  • 進学(しんがく)塾(じゅく) (exam prep cram school)
  • 補習(ほしゅう)塾(じゅく) (supplementary tutoring)
  • 個別(こべつ)指導(しどう)塾(じゅく) (individual tutoring school)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)う (to attend cram school)
  • 塾(じゅく)に行(い)く (to go to cram school)
  • 塾(じゅく)の先生(せんせい) (cram school teacher)
  • 塾(じゅく)代(だい) (cram school fees)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 予備校(よびこう) (prep school - for university entrance)
  • 家庭教師(かていきょうし) (private tutor)

CULTURAL NOTE:
塾(じゅく) is integral to Japanese education. Many students attend after regular school hours to prepare for entrance exams or supplement school learning. The industry is significant, and famous 塾(じゅく) brands are well-known.
