1. to dance (traditional dance)
To perform a traditional or ceremonial dance, especially in Japanese performing arts.
巫女(みこ)が神前(しんぜん)で舞(ま)っている。
A shrine maiden is dancing before the altar.
能(のう)の舞台(ぶたい)で美(うつく)しく舞(ま)った。
She danced beautifully on the Noh stage.
祭(まつ)りで地元(じもと)の人(ひと)たちが伝統的(でんとうてき)な踊(おど)りを舞(ま)った。
Local people performed traditional dances at the festival.
2. to flutter; to whirl; to swirl
When something light moves through the air in a dancing motion, such as leaves, snow, or petals.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)びらが風(かぜ)に舞(ま)っている。
Cherry blossom petals are fluttering in the wind.
雪(ゆき)が舞(ま)い散(ち)る中(なか)を歩(ある)いた。
I walked while snow swirled and scattered around me.
落(お)ち葉(ば)が風(かぜ)に舞(ま)い上(あ)がって、空(そら)に消(き)えていった。
Fallen leaves swirled up in the wind and disappeared into the sky.

舞(ま)う is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). There is no direct transitive pair.

ASPECT (ている):
舞(ま)っている indicates an ongoing dancing or fluttering motion.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花(はな)びらが舞(ま)う - petals flutter
  • 雪(ゆき)が舞(ま)う - snow swirls
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)が舞(ま)う - fallen leaves flutter
  • 舞(ま)い上(あ)がる - to soar up, to be elated
  • 舞(ま)い散(ち)る - to flutter and scatter

舞(ま)う VS 踊(おど)る:
舞(ま)う refers to graceful, flowing movements often associated with traditional or ceremonial dances, while 踊(おど)る is the general word for dancing and covers more energetic, modern styles.
