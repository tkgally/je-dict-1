円高(えんだか)

えんだか
noun
strong yen
1. strong yen, yen appreciation
A situation where the Japanese yen increases in value relative to other currencies.
円高(えんだか)で輸出(ゆしゅつ)が減(へ)った。
Exports decreased due to the strong yen.
円高(えんだか)は海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)に有利(ゆうり)だ。
A strong yen is advantageous for overseas travel.
急激(きゅうげき)な円高(えんだか)が進(すす)んだ。
There was rapid yen appreciation.

円高(えんだか) refers to the appreciation of the Japanese yen against other currencies.

COMPONENTS:

  • 円(えん): yen (Japanese currency)
  • 高(たか): high, expensive

ECONOMIC EFFECTS:

  • Makes imports cheaper (good for consumers)
  • Makes exports more expensive (bad for exporters)
  • Favorable for overseas travel

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 円高(えんだか)が進(すす)む: yen appreciation advances
  • 円高(えんだか)傾向(けいこう): trend toward a stronger yen
  • 円高(えんだか)不況(ふきょう): strong-yen recession
  • 円高(えんだか)是正(ぜせい): yen appreciation correction

CONTRAST:
Opposite of 円安(えんやす) (weak yen, yen depreciation).

USAGE:
Frequently appears in news about economics, trade, and foreign exchange. Understanding this term is essential for following Japanese business news.

Related Words

Antonym: 円安(えんやす) (weak yen)
