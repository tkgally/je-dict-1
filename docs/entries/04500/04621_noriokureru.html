乗(の)り遅(おく)れる

のりおくれる
ichidan verb
to miss (a train, bus, etc.)
1. to miss (transportation)
To fail to get on a train, bus, plane, or other vehicle in time, usually arriving after it has departed.
2. to miss out on, to be left behind
To fail to take advantage of an opportunity or trend (figurative use).
朝(あさ)の電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れてしまった。
I missed the morning train.
寝坊(ねぼう)して飛行機(ひこうき)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れた。
I overslept and missed my flight.
時代(じだい)の流(なが)れに乗(の)り遅(おく)れないようにしたい。
I don't want to be left behind by the times.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to miss out on trends or changes]

乗(の)り遅(おく)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to miss transportation.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 乗(の)る (to ride) + 遅(おく)れる (to be late) → to be late for riding

ASPECT (ている):
乗(の)り遅(おく)れている is less common; the simple past 乗(の)り遅(おく)れた is typically used.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れる (miss a train)
  • バスに乗(の)り遅(おく)れる (miss a bus)
  • 飛行機(ひこうき)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れる (miss a flight)
  • 流行(りゅうこう)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れる (miss out on a trend)

SIMILAR EXPRESSION:

  • 乗(の)り損(そこ)なう: also means to miss (transportation), slightly more casual
