水筒(すいとう)

すいとう
noun
water bottle, canteen

Definitions

1. water bottle, canteen, flask
A container for carrying drinking water or other beverages, typically portable.

Examples

遠足(えんそく)に水筒(すいとう)を持(も)っていく。
I'm bringing a water bottle on the field trip.
水筒(すいとう)に麦茶(むぎちゃ)を入(い)れた。
I put barley tea in the water bottle.
保温(ほおん)水筒(すいとう)なら、飲(の)み物(もの)が冷(さ)めにくい。
With an insulated bottle, drinks don't cool down easily.
保温水筒 = thermos, insulated water bottle

Notes

水筒(すいとう) is a standard term for portable water containers. In Japan, children commonly bring 水筒(すいとう) to school and on outings.

TYPES:

  • 保温(ほおん)水筒(すいとう): thermos, insulated bottle
  • 保冷(ほれい)水筒(すいとう): cooling water bottle
  • ペットボトル: plastic bottle (disposable)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 魔法瓶（まほうびん）: thermos (literally 'magic bottle')
  • タンブラー: tumbler
