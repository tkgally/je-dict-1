跳(は)ね上(あ)がる

はねあがる
verb (godan)
to jump up; to spring up; to surge

Definitions

1. to jump up; to spring up
To leap upward suddenly or energetically.
2. to surge; to skyrocket
When prices, numbers, or statistics increase sharply.
3. to flip up; to fly up
When something hinged or loose springs upward.

Examples

驚(おどろ)いて椅子(いす)から跳(は)ね上(あ)がった。
I jumped up from my chair in surprise.
原油(げんゆ)価格(かかく)が急(きゅう)に跳(は)ね上(あ)がった。
Crude oil prices suddenly surged.
風(かぜ)で看板(かんばん)が跳(は)ね上(あ)がった。
The signboard flipped up in the wind.

Notes

跳(は)ね上(あ)がる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive form is 跳(は)ね上(あ)げる (to flip up, to jack up).

ASPECT ({ている}):
跳(は)ね上(あ)がっている indicates an ongoing or resulting state of being elevated or having jumped up.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 椅子(いす)から跳(は)ね上(あ)がる - to jump up from a chair
  • 値段(ねだん)/価格(かかく)が跳(は)ね上(あ)がる - prices surge
  • 跳(は)ね上(あ)がり式(しき) - flip-up type (of glasses, etc.)

COMPOUND VERB STRUCTURE:
跳(は)ねる (to jump) + 上(あ)がる (to go up) = 跳(は)ね上(あ)がる (to jump up)

RELATED:

  • 飛(と)び上(あ)がる - to jump up (similar meaning)
  • 跳(は)ね上(あ)げる - to flip up (transitive)
