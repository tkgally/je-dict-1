経費(けいひ)

けいひ
noun
expenses; costs
1. expenses; costs; expenditure
Money spent for business operations or official purposes, often reimbursable.
経費(けいひ)が足(た)りない。
There aren't enough funds.
タクシー代(だい)は経費(けいひ)で落(お)ちる。
The taxi fare can be covered as a business expense.
出張(しゅっちょう)の経費(けいひ)を申請(しんせい)する。
Submit expenses for a business trip.
経費(けいひ)を削減(さくげん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reduce expenses.
会議(かいぎ)のための交通費(こうつうひ)や宿泊費(しゅくはくひ)はすべて経費(けいひ)として処理(しょり)できる。
All transportation and accommodation costs for the meeting can be processed as business expenses.

経費(けいひ) is a compound: 経(けい) (manage) + 費(ひ) (expense).

COMMON TYPES:

  • 交通費(こうつうひ) (transportation expenses)
  • 交際費(こうさいひ) (entertainment expenses)
  • 通信費(つうしんひ) (communication costs)
  • 消耗品費(しょうもうひんひ) (consumable supplies)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経費(けいひ)で落(お)ちる/落(お)とす (to expense something, to charge as expense)
  • 経費(けいひ)を申請(しんせい)する (to submit expense claim)
  • 経費(けいひ)を削減(さくげん)する (to cut costs)
  • 経費(けいひ)精算(せいさん) (expense settlement/reimbursement)

NOTE:
経費(けいひ)で落(お)ちる is a common business expression meaning something can be claimed as a business expense.

Related Words

Synonym: 費用(ひよう) (cost, expense (general))
