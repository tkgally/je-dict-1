味醂(みりん)

みりん
noun
mirin, sweet rice wine
1. mirin, sweet cooking rice wine
A sweet Japanese rice wine used as a seasoning in cooking to add mild sweetness and shine to dishes.
醤油(しょうゆ)と味醂(みりん)を加(くわ)える。
Add soy sauce and mirin.
味醂(みりん)を入(い)れると照(て)りが出(で)ます。
Adding mirin gives the dish a nice glaze.
照りが出る refers to the glossy appearance mirin creates
煮物(にもの)には味醂(みりん)が欠(か)かせない。
Mirin is essential for simmered dishes.

A fundamental seasoning in Japanese cooking along with soy sauce and sake. Types include:

  • 本(ほん)みりん (true mirin, contains alcohol)
  • みりん風(ふう)調味料(ちょうみりょう) (mirin-like seasoning, low/no alcohol)

Common uses:

  • Adds sweetness and depth to sauces
  • Creates 照(て)り (glaze) on grilled foods
  • Reduces fishy odors in seafood dishes
  • Part of the classic 割(わ)り下(した) (sukiyaki sauce) ratio
