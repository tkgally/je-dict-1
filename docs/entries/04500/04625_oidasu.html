1. to drive out, to expel
To force someone or something to leave a place.
犬(いぬ)を外(そと)に追(お)い出(だ)した。
I drove the dog outside.
猫(ねこ)を部屋(へや)から追(お)い出(だ)した。
I chased the cat out of the room.
村人(むらびと)たちは熊(くま)を山(やま)の奥(おく)へ追(お)い出(だ)した。
The villagers drove the bear deep into the mountains.
2. to kick out, to throw out
To remove someone from a position, organization, or residence, often forcibly or against their will.
彼(かれ)は会社(かいしゃ)から追(お)い出(だ)された。
He was kicked out of the company.
家賃(やちん)を払(はら)わないと追(お)い出(だ)されるよ。
If you don't pay rent, you'll be kicked out.
不正(ふせい)が発覚(はっかく)して、社長(しゃちょう)は取締役会(とりしまりやくかい)から追(お)い出(だ)された。
After the fraud was discovered, the president was expelled from the board of directors.

追(お)い出(だ)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to drive out or expel.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 追(お)う (to chase) + 出(だ)す (to put out) → to chase out

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 追(お)い出(だ)す (他動詞(たどうし)): to drive someone out
  • There is no common intransitive pair

ASPECT (ている):
追(お)い出(だ)されている indicates a state of having been expelled.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)から追(お)い出(だ)す (drive out of a room)
  • 家(いえ)から追(お)い出(だ)す (kick out of the house)
  • 会社(かいしゃ)から追(お)い出(だ)す (kick out of the company)
  • 国(くに)から追(お)い出(だ)す (expel from a country)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 追放(ついほう): exile, banishment (more formal)
  • 排除(はいじょ): exclusion, removal (formal)
