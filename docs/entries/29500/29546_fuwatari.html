不渡(ふわた)り

ふわたり
noun
dishonored check; bounced bill
1. dishonored check; bounced bill
The failure of a check or promissory note to be paid because the account lacks sufficient funds, so the bill is returned unpaid.
小切手(こぎって)が不渡(ふわた)りになった。
The check bounced.
手形(てがた)が不渡(ふわた)りになると会社(かいしゃ)の信用(しんよう)は落(お)ちる。
When a bill is dishonored, a company's credit suffers.
二回(にかい)不渡(ふわた)りを出(だ)すと、銀行(ぎんこう)との取引(とりひき)が停止(ていし)される。
If a company dishonors bills twice, its bank transactions are suspended.

Formed from 渡(わた)る (to pass; to hand over) with the negative prefix 不(ふ): a check or 手形(てがた) that is not honored because the account lacks funds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不渡(ふわた)りになる: to bounce; to be dishonored
  • 不渡(ふわた)りを出(だ)す: to issue a check that bounces
  • 不渡(ふわた)り手形(てがた): a dishonored bill

USAGE:
In Japan, a business that records two dishonored bills within six months is effectively shut out of bank financing, so 不渡(ふわた)り is treated as a serious warning sign.
