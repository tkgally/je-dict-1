1. changing seats; seat reassignment
The rearranging of who sits where, especially the periodic reassignment of pupils' desks in a school classroom. Composed of 席(せき) (seat) and 替(か)え (changing). Takes する.
明日(あす)席替(せきが)えがある。
We have a seat change tomorrow.
くじで席替(せきが)えを決(き)めた。
We decided the new seats by drawing lots.
仲(なか)のいい友達(ともだち)と離(はな)れて、席替(せきが)えでがっかりした。
I was disappointed by the seat change because it separated me from my close friend.

Strongly associated with school life, where seats are reshuffled every month or term, often by lottery.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 席替(せきが)えをする: to change seats
  • 席替(せきが)えがある: to have a seat change
  • くじで席替(せきが)えを決(き)める: to decide seats by drawing lots

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 模様替(もようが)え: rearranging furniture in a room, not people's seats
  • 配置(はいち)換(が)え: reassignment of postings or placements, e.g. of staff
