1. exercise; workout
A loanword from English "exercise," used especially for fitness routines and workouts done for health or beauty. Often refers to lighter or trendier workouts than 運動(うんどう).
毎朝(まいあさ)エクササイズをする。
I exercise every morning.
軽(かる)いエクササイズで体(からだ)をほぐす。
to loosen up the body with light exercise.
動画(どうが)を見(み)ながら、家(いえ)でエクササイズしている。
I exercise at home while watching videos.

A loanword from English "exercise," used especially for fitness routines and workouts done for health or beauty. Often implies a lighter or trendier workout than 運動(うんどう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • エクササイズをする: to do a workout
  • 顔(かお)エクササイズ: facial exercises
  • ダイエットエクササイズ: weight-loss workout

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運動(うんどう): exercise, physical activity — broader native word
  • トレーニング: training — more goal-oriented, muscle-building nuance
