1. deposition; accumulation; piling up in layers
The piling up of material such as sediment, snow, or debris in layers over time. In geology, the deposition of sediment that forms strata. Usually intransitive: things 堆積(たいせき)する.
土砂(どしゃ)が堆積(たいせき)する。
Earth and sand accumulate.
川底(かわぞこ)に泥(どろ)が堆積(たいせき)している。
Mud has accumulated on the riverbed.
長(なが)い年月(ねんげつ)をかけて土砂(どしゃ)が堆積(たいせき)し、地層(ちそう)ができた。
Over long years, sediment was deposited and formed strata.

Composed of 堆(たい) (heap up) + 積(せき) (accumulate).

TRANSITIVITY:
Usually intransitive — material 堆積(たいせき)する on its own. Use 堆積(たいせき)させる when an agent causes the buildup.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 土砂(どしゃ)の堆積(たいせき): deposition of earth and sand
  • 堆積物(たいせきぶつ): sediment, deposits
  • 堆積岩(たいせきがん): sedimentary rock

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 侵食(しんしょく): erosion — the opposite process, wearing material away
  • 蓄積(ちくせき): accumulation — used of abstract things (knowledge, fatigue) as well as physical
