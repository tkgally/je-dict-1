1. mining (of cryptocurrency)
The computational process of validating cryptocurrency transactions and generating new units as a reward. Requires heavy computing power and electricity.
マイニングで暗号資産(あんごうしさん)を得(え)る。
You earn cryptocurrency through mining.
マイニングには大量(たいりょう)の電力(でんりょく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Mining requires a huge amount of electricity.
個人(こじん)でマイニングして利益(りえき)を出(だ)すのは難(むずか)しくなっている。
It's becoming difficult to turn a profit mining as an individual.

From English "mining." In cryptocurrency it names the process of confirming transactions and issuing new coins. Often used as a する-verb (マイニングする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マイニングする: to mine (cryptocurrency)
  • マイニングマシン: mining machine
  • マイニングプール: mining pool

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 採掘(さいくつ): mining; extraction — the literal digging of ore; マイニング is the crypto sense borrowed from it
