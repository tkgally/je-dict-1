1. mumbling; speaking indistinctly; mouthing
Mimetic word for speaking unclearly with the mouth barely moving, often from shyness or lack of confidence. Also describes chewing or moving something slowly inside a closed mouth.
もごもごと話(はな)す。
To speak in a mumble.
口(くち)の中(なか)でもごもご言(い)う。
To mumble under one's breath.
彼(かれ)は自信(じしん)がなさそうに、もごもごと言(い)い訳(わけ)をした。
He mumbled an excuse, seeming to lack confidence.

Mimetic word for speaking indistinctly with the mouth barely opening, typically from hesitation or embarrassment. Also describes slow movement inside a closed mouth.

USAGE:

  • As adverb: もごもご話(はな)す (to mumble)
  • With する: もごもごする (to mumble; to fidget with the mouth)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぼそぼそ: muttering — low, quiet, flat murmuring
  • もぐもぐ: munching; mumbling — emphasizes chewing/mouth movement
