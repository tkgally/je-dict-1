章末(しょうまつ)

しょうまつ
noun
end of a chapter
1. end of a chapter; chapter-end
The closing part of a chapter in a book, especially the summaries or practice problems placed there in textbooks and study guides.
章末(しょうまつ)に問題(もんだい)がある。
There are problems at the end of the chapter.
章末(しょうまつ)のまとめを読(よ)む。
I read the summary at the end of the chapter.
各(かく)章末(しょうまつ)に練習(れんしゅう)問題(もんだい)がついている参考書(さんこうしょ)を選(えら)んだ。
I chose a study guide with practice problems at the end of each chapter.

Compound of 章(しょう) (chapter) + 末(まつ) (end). Refers to the tail end of a chapter, most often in the context of textbook material placed there.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 章末(しょうまつ)問題(もんだい): end-of-chapter problems
  • 章末(しょうまつ)のまとめ: chapter-end summary

The opposite is 章(しょう)の初(はじ)め (the beginning of a chapter).
