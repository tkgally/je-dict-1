マネージャー

まねーじゃー
noun
manager
1. manager
A person who manages a business, team, or performer. In Japanese schools and clubs, also the student who handles a sports team's logistics and support rather than playing.
店(みせ)のマネージャーになる。
Become the manager of the shop.
野球部(やきゅうぶ)のマネージャーを務(つと)める。
Serve as the baseball club's manager.
新(あたら)しいマネージャーが、チームの予定(よてい)や道具(どうぐ)の管理(かんり)を担当(たんとう)している。
The new manager is in charge of the team's schedule and equipment.

From English "manager." Covers a business or store manager as well as, in schools, the club {マネージャー} who handles support tasks. Also used for the manager of an entertainer or musician.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • エリアマネージャー: area manager
  • マネージャーを務(つと)める: to serve as manager
  • 部(ぶ)のマネージャー: club manager

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 管理者(かんりしゃ): administrator — more formal, administrative
  • 監督(かんとく): coach; director — leads or directs a team or film
