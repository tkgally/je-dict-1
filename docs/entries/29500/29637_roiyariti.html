ロイヤリティ

ろいやりてぃ
noun
royalty; licensing fee
1. royalty; licensing fee
A fee paid for the use of intellectual property, a patent, a brand, or a franchise — usually a percentage of sales or a per-unit charge.
ロイヤリティを支払(しはら)う。
pay royalties
売上(うりあげ)の五(ご)パーセントをロイヤリティとして納(おさ)める。
pay 5 percent of sales as a royalty
フランチャイズ契約(けいやく)では、毎月(まいつき)一定(いってい)のロイヤリティが発生(はっせい)する。
Under a franchise contract, a fixed royalty accrues every month.

A loanword from English "royalty." Also written ロイヤルティ.

USAGE:
The separate meaning "loyalty" (as in customer loyalty) is usually written ロイヤルティ; context distinguishes the two.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ロイヤリティを支払(しはら)う: to pay royalties
  • ロイヤリティ収入(しゅうにゅう): royalty income

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 印税(いんぜい): royalty — specifically an author's royalty on published works
  • 使用料(しようりょう): usage fee — broader, not necessarily tied to intellectual property
