メレンゲ

めれんげ
noun
meringue
1. meringue
Egg whites whipped with sugar until stiff and foamy, used in baking and desserts. Also refers to baked confections made from this foam.
卵白(らんぱく)でメレンゲを作(つく)る。
I make meringue from egg whites.
メレンゲをしっかり泡立(あわだ)てる。
Whip the meringue until stiff.
砂糖(さとう)を少(すこ)しずつ加(くわ)えながら、つやのあるメレンゲに仕上(しあ)げる。
Adding the sugar little by little, whip it into a glossy meringue.

Loanword (from French "meringue," via the Japanese pronunciation メレンゲ). A staple of Western-style baking; also eaten as light baked meringue cookies.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メレンゲを作(つく)る: to make meringue
  • メレンゲを泡立(あわだ)てる: to whip meringue
  • メレンゲ菓子(がし): meringue confection

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生(なま)クリーム: whipped cream — also whipped, but from cream, not egg whites
  • スポンジ生地(きじ): sponge batter — uses whole beaten eggs rather than egg-white foam alone
