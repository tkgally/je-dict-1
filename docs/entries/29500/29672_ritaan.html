リターン

りたーん
noun
return; yield; reward
1. return; yield (on an investment or effort)
The profit, benefit, or result gained back from money, time, or effort that has been put in. Common in finance and business.
この投資(とうし)はリターンが大(おお)きい。
This investment has a big return.
リスクとリターンは比例(ひれい)する。
Risk and return are proportional.
努力(どりょく)に見合(みあ)うリターンが得(え)られるとは限(かぎ)らない。
You won't necessarily get a return that matches your effort.
2. reward; perk (in crowdfunding)
The goods, service, or benefit a backer receives in exchange for supporting a crowdfunding project. A fixed term of the クラウドファンディング world.
支援(しえん)のリターンはオリジナルグッズだ。
The reward for backing is original merchandise.
金額(きんがく)によってリターンの内容(ないよう)が変(か)わる。
The reward changes depending on the amount pledged.
魅力的(みりょくてき)なリターンを用意(ようい)すれば、支援者(しえんしゃ)が集(あつ)まりやすい。
If you prepare attractive rewards, it is easier to gather backers.

From English "return." Sense 1 is the general finance/business meaning of a yield or payoff; sense 2 is the fixed crowdfunding term for what a backer receives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ハイリスク・ハイリターン: high risk, high return
  • リターンを得(え)る: to gain a return
  • リターンを用意(ようい)する: to prepare rewards (crowdfunding)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 利回(りまわ)り: yield; rate of return — the technical financial ratio, not the general payoff
  • 見返(みかえ)り: return favor; something given in exchange — broader, often personal rather than financial
