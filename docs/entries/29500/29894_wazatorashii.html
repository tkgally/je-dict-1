1. obviously deliberate; affected; put-on; unnatural
Describing behavior, a reaction, or a manner that looks intentionally staged rather than genuine, so that it comes across as forced or insincere.
わざとらしい笑(わら)い方(かた)だ。
It's a put-on way of laughing.
彼女(かのじょ)のほめ方(かた)はわざとらしくて気(き)になる。
The way she compliments people feels affected, and it bothers me.
わざとらしい咳払(せきばら)いをして、彼(かれ)は話題(わだい)を変(か)えようとした。
With an obviously fake throat-clearing, he tried to change the subject.

Built on わざと (on purpose) plus the suffix ～らしい (seeming, looking like). It carries a critical tone: the action looks staged, exaggerated, or insincere rather than spontaneous.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • わざとらしい笑(わら)い: a forced, fake laugh
  • わざとらしく泣(な)く: to cry in an obviously put-on way
  • わざとらしい態度(たいど): an affected, insincere manner

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不自然(ふしぜん)な: unnatural — describes something that simply seems off, without the sense of deliberate pretense
  • 大袈裟(おおげさ)な: exaggerated, overblown — overstated, but not necessarily faked on purpose
