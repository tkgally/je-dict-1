電磁気学

でんじきがく
noun
electromagnetism (the field of study)
1. electromagnetism; electromagnetic theory
The branch of physics that studies electricity, magnetism, and the interactions between them. A standard subject in university science and engineering curricula.
電磁気学(でんじきがく)を学(まな)ぶ。
I study electromagnetism.
電磁気学(でんじきがく)は物理学(ぶつりがく)の基礎(きそ)の一(ひと)つだ。
Electromagnetism is one of the foundations of physics.
電磁気学(でんじきがく)の知識(ちしき)は、モーターや発電機(はつでんき)の仕組(しく)みを理解(りかい)するのに欠(か)かせない。
A knowledge of electromagnetism is essential for understanding how motors and generators work.

Formed from 電磁気(でんじき) (electromagnetism) + 学(がく) (the study of). Refers to the academic field, not the phenomenon itself.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電磁気学(でんじきがく)の授業(じゅぎょう): an electromagnetism class
  • 電磁気学(でんじきがく)を専攻(せんこう)する: to major in electromagnetism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電磁気(でんじき): electromagnetism — the phenomenon or property, without 学(がく).
  • 力学(りきがく): mechanics — a parallel branch of physics.
