1. marriage hunting; actively seeking a spouse
Deliberate effort to find a marriage partner, such as attending matchmaking events, using dating services, or being introduced to prospects. The full form of the common abbreviation 婚活(こんかつ).
妹(いもうと)は結婚活動(けっこんかつどう)を始(はじ)めた。
My sister has started marriage hunting.
結婚活動(けっこんかつどう)のために相談所(そうだんじょ)に登録(とうろく)した。
I registered with an agency for my marriage hunting.
仕事(しごと)が忙(いそが)しくて、なかなか結婚活動(けっこんかつどう)に時間(じかん)が取(と)れない。
I'm so busy with work that I can hardly find time for marriage hunting.

The full form of 婚活(こんかつ), formed from 結婚(けっこん) (marriage) + 活動(かつどう) (activity), on the model of 就職(しゅうしょく)活動(かつどう) (job hunting). In everyday use the abbreviation 婚活(こんかつ) is far more common; the full form sounds slightly more formal or explanatory.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 結婚活動(けっこんかつどう)を始(はじ)める: to start marriage hunting
  • 結婚活動(けっこんかつどう)に励(はげ)む: to work hard at finding a spouse

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 婚活(こんかつ): marriage hunting — the standard abbreviation; identical meaning
  • 就活(しゅうかつ): job hunting — the parallel abbreviation for finding employment
