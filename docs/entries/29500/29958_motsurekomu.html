1. to drag on into; to end up extending into (overtime, a later stage)
For a contest, negotiation, or dispute to fail to be settled and spill over into an additional stage such as extra time, a playoff, or a further round. Combines もつれる (to become tangled/deadlocked) with 込(こ)む (to move into).
試合(しあい)が延長(えんちょう)にもつれ込(こ)む。
The game drags on into extra time.
交渉(こうしょう)は最終日(さいしゅうび)までもつれ込(こ)んだ。
The negotiations dragged on all the way to the final day.
両者(りょうしゃ)譲(ゆず)らず、勝負(しょうぶ)はサドンデスにもつれ込(こ)んだ。
Neither side would give way, and the contest went into sudden death.

An intransitive verb. The thing that drags on takes に to mark what it extends into (extra innings, a playoff, a final day, etc.).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 延長(えんちょう)にもつれ込(こ)む: to go into overtime/extra innings
  • 最終(さいしゅう)戦(せん)にもつれ込(こ)む: to come down to the final match
  • 混戦(こんせん)にもつれ込(こ)む: to develop into a close, tangled contest

USAGE:
Most often used of sports, elections, and negotiations that stay unresolved and spill into an extra stage. Implies the outcome was hard-fought and undecided.
