1. humidity regulation; moisture control
Keeping indoor humidity at a comfortable level, either passively through absorbent materials or actively with equipment. Common in descriptions of building materials and air conditioners.
この素材(そざい)は調湿(ちょうしつ)効果(こうか)が高(たか)い。
This material has a strong humidity-regulating effect.
炭(すみ)は調湿(ちょうしつ)のために使(つか)われる。
Charcoal is used for moisture control.
この壁(かべ)は湿気(しっけ)を吸(す)って室内(しつない)を調湿(ちょうしつ)してくれる。
This wall absorbs moisture and regulates the humidity of the room.

Refers to keeping humidity at a comfortable level, either passively through materials or actively with equipment. Most often used attributively of building materials and appliances.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 調湿(ちょうしつ)機能(きのう): humidity-regulating function
  • 調湿(ちょうしつ)効果(こうか): moisture-regulating effect
  • 調湿(ちょうしつ)材(ざい): humidity-control material

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 除湿(じょしつ): dehumidification — removing moisture only
  • 加湿(かしつ): humidification — adding moisture only
