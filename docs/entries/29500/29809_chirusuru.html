1. to chill; to relax; to unwind
Youth slang meaning to relax and take it easy, from English "chill" plus する. Overlaps with the slang adjective チルい (chill, laid-back).
家(いえ)でチルしてる。
I'm chilling at home.
週末(しゅうまつ)は音楽(おんがく)を聞(き)きながらチルしたい。
On weekends I want to chill while listening to music.
カフェでコーヒーを飲(の)みながら友達(ともだち)とチルするのが好(す)きだ。
I like chilling with friends over coffee at a café.

Casual youth slang from English "chill" plus する, meaning to relax and do nothing in particular. Closely related to the slang adjective チルい (chill, laid-back).

REGISTER: Casual slang, mainly used by younger speakers since the 2010s–2020s. Avoid in formal writing or speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家(いえ)でチルする: to chill at home
  • まったりチルする: to chill out lazily
