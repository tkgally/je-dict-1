丈(たけ)

たけ
noun
length; height; stature
1. length (of clothing); height; stature
The length of a garment measured from top to bottom, such as the length of a skirt, sleeve, or trousers. By extension, a person's height or the full extent of something.
スカートの丈(たけ)が短(みじか)い。
The skirt's length is short.
ズボンの丈(たけ)を少(すこ)し詰(つ)めてもらった。
I had the trousers shortened a little.
袖(そで)の丈(たけ)が長(なが)すぎたので、お店(みせ)で直(なお)してもらった。
The sleeve length was too long, so I had it altered at the shop.

Most commonly refers to the length of clothing (skirts, sleeves, trousers). Also refers to a person's height or, in set phrases, the full extent of something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 丈(たけ)が長(なが)い / 短(みじか)い: to be long / short (in length)
  • 丈(たけ)を詰(つ)める: to shorten (a hem)
  • 背丈(せたけ): one's height; stature
  • 思(おも)いの丈(たけ): the full extent of one's feelings

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 長(なが)さ: length — general measurement of length; 丈(たけ) specifically suits clothing and height
  • 身長(しんちょう): height (of a person) — the technical term for a person's height
