飛(と)び石(いし)

とびいし
noun
stepping stone
1. stepping stone
A flat stone set in a garden path or across water at intervals, so that one can cross by stepping from one stone to the next. A characteristic feature of Japanese gardens.
飛(と)び石(いし)を渡(わた)って庭(にわ)を歩(ある)く。
You cross the garden by walking on the stepping stones.
池(いけ)に並(なら)んだ飛(と)び石(いし)を一(ひと)つずつ渡(わた)った。
I crossed the stepping stones set across the pond one by one.
日本庭園(にほんていえん)では、飛(と)び石(いし)が景観(けいかん)の一部(いちぶ)になっている。
In a Japanese garden, the stepping stones are part of the scenery.

Flat stones laid at intervals so one can cross a garden or stream step by step.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飛(と)び石(いし)を渡(わた)る: to cross on stepping stones
  • 飛(と)び石(いし)を置(お)く: to lay stepping stones

RELATED EXPRESSION:

  • 飛(と)び石(いし)連休(れんきゅう): a run of holidays broken up by workdays (e.g., days off on Monday and Wednesday but not Tuesday) — from the image of scattered stepping stones
