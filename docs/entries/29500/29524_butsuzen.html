仏前(ぶつぜん)

ぶつぜん
noun
before the Buddhist altar; in front of Buddha
1. before the Buddhist altar; in front of Buddha
The space in front of a Buddhist altar (仏壇(ぶつだん)) or image of the Buddha, where offerings are made and prayers are said. Also used in 御仏前(ごぶつぜん), the label on condolence money offered after the funeral.
仏前(ぶつぜん)に花(はな)を供(そな)える。
To offer flowers before the Buddhist altar.
毎朝(まいあさ)仏前(ぶつぜん)で手(て)を合(あ)わせる。
Every morning I put my hands together in prayer before the altar.
香典袋(こうでんぶくろ)には「御仏前(ごぶつぜん)」と書(か)いて法要(ほうよう)に持参(じさん)した。
I wrote 'go-butsuzen' on the condolence envelope and brought it to the memorial service.

Refers to the area before the household altar or a Buddha image, the place for offerings and prayer. The polite form 御仏前(ごぶつぜん) labels condolence money given at or after the 四十九日(しじゅうくにち) memorial, in contrast to 御霊前(ごれいぜん) used before it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仏前(ぶつぜん)に供(そな)える: to make an offering before the altar
  • 御仏前(ごぶつぜん): label for condolence money after the funeral

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仏壇(ぶつだん): Buddhist household altar — the altar itself
  • 御霊前(ごれいぜん): 'before the spirit' — condolence-money label used before the 49th-day rite
