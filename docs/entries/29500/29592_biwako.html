琵琶湖(びわこ)

びわこ
noun
Lake Biwa
1. Lake Biwa, the largest lake in Japan
Japan's largest freshwater lake, in Shiga Prefecture northeast of Kyoto. Named for its resemblance to the 琵琶(びわ) (biwa, a lute), it is a major water source for the Kansai region and a well-known scenic and historical site.
琵琶湖(びわこ)は日本(にほん)最大(さいだい)の湖(みずうみ)だ。
Lake Biwa is the largest lake in Japan.

Japan's largest lake, in 滋賀県(しがけん) (Shiga Prefecture). It is named after the 琵琶(びわ) (biwa lute) for its shape.
