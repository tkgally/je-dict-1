1. to turn into; to be transformed into
For something to change into a different state or thing, often a dramatic or unwelcome one. A literary variant of 化(か)する; the new state is marked with と or に.
町(まち)が廃墟(はいきょ)と化(か)す。
The town turns into ruins.
規則(きそく)が形式(けいしき)だけのものと化(か)してしまった。
The rule has turned into a mere formality.
災害(さいがい)で美(うつく)しい景色(けしき)が一面(いちめん)の焼(や)け野原(のはら)と化(か)した。
In the disaster, the beautiful scenery turned into a scorched wasteland as far as the eye could see.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive godan verb. The resulting state is marked with と (or に): Xと化(か)す. A literary, written-style variant of 化(か)する with the same meaning; it often conveys a striking or unwelcome transformation.

USAGE:

  • Frequently used with negative outcomes: 廃墟(はいきょ)と化(か)す (turn into ruins), 無(む)と化(か)す (come to nothing).
  • The past form 化(か)した and the て form 化(か)して are the most common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 廃墟(はいきょ)と化(か)す: to turn into ruins
  • 灰(はい)と化(か)す: to be reduced to ashes
