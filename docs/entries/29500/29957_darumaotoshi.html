だるま落(お)とし

だるまおとし
noun
daruma otoshi; block-knocking game
1. daruma otoshi; traditional block-knocking toy game
A traditional Japanese toy consisting of a stack of colored wooden blocks topped by a daruma figure. Players knock out the blocks one at a time with a small hammer without letting the daruma fall.
だるま落(お)としで遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play daruma otoshi.
だるま落(お)としは力(ちから)と正確(せいかく)さがいる。
Daruma otoshi takes both strength and precision.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、お正月(しょうがつ)によくだるま落(お)としをして遊(あそ)んだ。
As a child, I often played daruma otoshi at New Year's.

A classic wooden toy game. The player uses a small mallet (小(こ)づち) to knock out each block in turn while keeping the tower and the daruma on top upright.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • だるま落(お)としをする: to play daruma otoshi
  • だるま落(お)としの要領(ようりょう)で: in the manner of daruma otoshi (knocking out one layer at a time)

RELATED WORDS:

  • だるま: daruma doll (the figure that tops the stack)
  • 独楽(こま): spinning top — another traditional toy
