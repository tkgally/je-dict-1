スタッドレスタイヤ

すたっどれすたいや
noun
studless (winter) tire
1. winter tire that grips snow and ice without metal studs
A winter tire that grips snow and icy roads through soft rubber and a special tread pattern rather than metal studs. Standard in snowy regions of Japan, where studded tires are largely banned for damaging road surfaces.
冬(ふゆ)はスタッドレスタイヤに替(か)える。
In winter, I switch to studless tires.
雪道(ゆきみち)ではスタッドレスタイヤが必要(ひつよう)だ。
Studless tires are necessary on snowy roads.
早(はや)めにスタッドレスタイヤに交換(こうかん)しておくと安心(あんしん)だ。
Changing to studless tires early gives peace of mind.

From English "studless tire." Often shortened to スタッドレス.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Metal-studded tires (スパイクタイヤ) were banned in Japan in the early 1990s because they ground road surfaces into harmful dust, so スタッドレスタイヤ became the standard winter tire.
