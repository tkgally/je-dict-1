大器(たいき)

たいき
noun
person of great talent; great vessel
1. person of great talent; person of great capacity
A person of outstanding ability and breadth, likened to a large vessel. The literal sense of a large container survives mainly in set phrases.
彼(かれ)は大器(たいき)だ。
He is a person of great talent.
若手(わかて)選手(せんしゅ)の大器(たいき)ぶりが注目(ちゅうもく)される。
The young player's great promise is drawing attention.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は目立(めだ)たなかったが、今(いま)や球界(きゅうかい)を代表(だいひょう)する大器(たいき)となった。
He didn't stand out when young, but has become one of the baseball world's leading talents.

Composed of 大(たい) (great) + 器(き) (vessel, capacity). Most familiar from the idiom 大器晩成(たいきばんせい) (great talents mature late).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大器(たいき)の片鱗(へんりん): a glimpse of great talent
  • 大器晩成(たいきばんせい): great talents mature late

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逸材(いつざい): person of exceptional talent — emphasizes rarity and being hard to find
  • 人材(じんざい): talented person, human resource — a generally capable person, less exceptional
