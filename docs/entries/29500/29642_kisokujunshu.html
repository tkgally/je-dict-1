規則遵守

きそくじゅんしゅ
noun
rule compliance; observance of rules
1. rule compliance; observance of rules
The act of following established rules and regulations. Common in workplace, school, and safety contexts where adherence to rules is expected.
規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)は大切(たいせつ)だ。
Compliance with the rules is important.
会社(かいしゃ)は社員(しゃいん)に規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)を求(もと)める。
The company requires its employees to comply with the rules.
規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)を徹底(てってい)することで、事故(じこ)を防(ふせ)ぐことができる。
By thoroughly enforcing compliance with the rules, accidents can be prevented.

A compound of 規則(きそく) (rules, regulations) and 遵守(じゅんしゅ) (observance, compliance). Refers to following established rules and regulations, typically in an organizational or safety setting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)を徹底(てってい)する: to enforce strict compliance
  • 規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)の意識(いしき): awareness of compliance
  • 規則遵守(きそくじゅんしゅ)を求(もと)める: to demand compliance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遵守(じゅんしゅ): compliance, observance — the base word, which can apply to laws, rules, or agreements
  • コンプライアンス: compliance — loanword, especially for corporate and legal compliance
