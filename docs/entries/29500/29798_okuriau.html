1. to send to one another; to exchange back and forth
For two or more parties to send something to each other mutually, such as messages, photos, or gifts. A compound of 送(おく)る (to send) and 合(あ)う (to do mutually).
毎日(まいにち)メッセージを送(おく)り合(あ)う。
We send each other messages every day.
友達(ともだち)と写真(しゃしん)を送(おく)り合(あ)った。
I exchanged photos with a friend.
年末(ねんまつ)になると、家族(かぞく)や友人(ゆうじん)とプレゼントを送(おく)り合(あ)うのが習慣(しゅうかん)だ。
At the end of the year, it's a custom to exchange presents with family and friends.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive godan verb. The thing exchanged is marked with を and the other party with と: (person)と Xを送(おく)り合(あ)う. The 合(あ)う suffix adds the sense of mutual, reciprocal action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メッセージを送(おく)り合(あ)う: to exchange messages
  • 写真(しゃしん)を送(おく)り合(あ)う: to send each other photos
  • エールを送(おく)り合(あ)う: to cheer each other on
