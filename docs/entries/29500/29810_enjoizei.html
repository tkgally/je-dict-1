エンジョイ勢(ぜい)

えんじょいぜい
noun
casual player; those who play for fun (slang)
1. casual player; those who play for fun
In gaming and hobby communities, people who take part casually for enjoyment rather than competing seriously — the counterpart of ガチ勢(ぜい) (hardcore players).
私(わたし)はエンジョイ勢(ぜい)だ。
I'm a casual player.
このゲームはエンジョイ勢(ぜい)が多(おお)い。
This game has a lot of casual players.
ガチ勢(ぜい)とエンジョイ勢(ぜい)では楽(たの)しみ方(かた)が違(ちが)う。
Hardcore and casual players enjoy the game in different ways.

Formed from English "enjoy" plus the suffix 勢(ぜい) (a group or faction of people). Labels people who join a game or hobby for fun, without chasing top results — the opposite of ガチ勢(ぜい).

REGISTER: Casual slang, common in gaming, fan, and hobby communities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • エンジョイ勢(ぜい)として楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy it as a casual player

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ガチ勢(ぜい): hardcore players — those who play seriously and competitively
