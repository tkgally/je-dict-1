1. taking photographs; photo shoot
The act of taking photographs, whether casually or as an organized session. Often used with する as a verb and in signs stating whether photography is permitted.
ここでの写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)は禁止(きんし)です。
Photography is prohibited here.
結婚式(けっこんしき)の前(まえ)に写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)をした。
We had a photo shoot before the wedding.
美術館(びじゅつかん)では、作品(さくひん)の写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)が許可(きょか)されている場所(ばしょ)もある。
In the art museum there are also places where photographing the works is permitted.

Formed from 写真(しゃしん) (photograph) + 撮影(さつえい) (filming, shooting). Takes する to form a verb: 写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)する (to take photographs). Very common on signs about whether photography is allowed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)禁止(きんし): photography prohibited
  • 写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい)可(か): photography permitted
  • 記念(きねん)写真撮影(しゃしんさつえい): commemorative photo session

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 撮影(さつえい): filming; shooting — broader, covers both photos and video.
  • 撮(と)る: to take (a photo) — the everyday verb for snapping a picture.
