フルカラー

ふるからー
noun
full color
1. full color
Reproduction in the full range of colors, as opposed to black-and-white or a limited palette. Used of printing, displays, and images.
フルカラーで印刷(いんさつ)する。
To print in full color.
この冊子(さっし)はフルカラーで見(み)やすい。
This booklet is in full color and easy to read.
昔(むかし)は白黒(しろくろ)だった漫画(まんが)が、フルカラー版(ばん)で復刊(ふっかん)された。
A manga that used to be black-and-white was reissued in a full-color edition.

A loanword from English "full color," contrasting with 白黒(しろくろ) (black-and-white) or 単色(たんしょく) (monochrome). Frequently used of printing, screens, and illustrations. Often used attributively before another noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フルカラー印刷(いんさつ): full-color printing
  • フルカラー液晶(えきしょう): full-color LCD
  • フルカラーで表示(ひょうじ)する: to display in full color

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 白黒(しろくろ): black-and-white — the opposite
  • モノクロ: monochrome — single-color reproduction, another antonym
