Vチューバー

ぶいちゅーばー
noun
VTuber; virtual YouTuber
1. VTuber; virtual YouTuber
An online entertainer who streams or posts videos using an animated avatar instead of appearing in person. A blend of "virtual" + "YouTuber".
人気(にんき)のVチューバーを応援(おうえん)する。
I support a popular VTuber.
妹(いもうと)はVチューバーが大好(だいす)きだ。
My little sister loves VTubers.
最近(さいきん)はVチューバーとして活動(かつどう)する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently the number of people working as VTubers has been increasing.

A blend of "virtual" + "YouTuber", also written バーチャルYouTuber. The performer uses a digital avatar, but the voice and often the motion are their own.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 配信者(はいしんしゃ): streamer, broadcaster
  • 実況者(じっきょうしゃ): game commentator, streamer
  • 推(お)し: one's favorite performer
