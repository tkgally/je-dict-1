1. to start going out with someone; to begin a relationship or association
To begin a romantic relationship, or more generally to start keeping company with a person or group. A compound of 付(つ)き合(あ)う (to associate with, to date) and 始(はじ)める (to begin).
二人(ふたり)は先月(せんげつ)付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めた。
The two of them started dating last month.
彼女(かのじょ)と付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めてから半年(はんとし)になる。
It's been half a year since I started going out with her.
同(おな)じサークルの仲間(なかま)と付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めてから、週末(しゅうまつ)が楽(たの)しくなった。
Since I started spending time with friends from the same club, my weekends have become more fun.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive ichidan verb. The partner is marked with と: (person)と付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)める. A compound of 付(つ)き合(あ)う plus the auxiliary 始(はじ)める (to begin doing).

USAGE:

  • Most often means to enter a romantic relationship, but can also mean simply to begin associating with people or a group.
  • The plain past 付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めた is common when stating when a relationship began.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 彼(かれ)と付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)める: to start dating him
  • 付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めたばかり: to have only just started going out
