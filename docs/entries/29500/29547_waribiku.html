1. to discount; to reduce a price
To lower the price of something by a certain amount or percentage.
定価(ていか)を一割(いちわり)割(わ)り引(び)いた。
I discounted the list price by ten percent.
まとめて買(か)うと値段(ねだん)を割(わ)り引(び)いてくれる。
If you buy in bulk, they discount the price.
この店(みせ)は会員(かいいん)の料金(りょうきん)を二割(にわり)割(わ)り引(び)いている。
This shop discounts members' fees by twenty percent.
2. to take with a grain of salt; to make allowances for exaggeration
Figuratively, to mentally subtract exaggeration from what someone says and treat it as less than its face value.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)は割(わ)り引(び)いて聞(き)いたほうがいい。
You should take his story with a grain of salt.
自慢(じまん)話(ばなし)だったので、少(すこ)し割(わ)り引(び)いて考(かんが)えた。
Since it was a boast, I took it with some allowance.
宣伝(せんでん)の言葉(ことば)は割(わ)り引(び)いて受(う)け取(と)るべきだ。
You should take advertising claims with a grain of salt.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — takes を (値段(ねだん)を割(わ)り引(び)く). Composed of 割(わ)る (to divide) + 引(ひ)く (to subtract), read with rendaku as わりびく.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 値段(ねだん)を割(わ)り引(び)く: to discount the price
  • 一割(いちわり)割(わ)り引(び)く: to take ten percent off
  • 話(はなし)を割(わ)り引(び)いて聞(き)く: to take a story with a grain of salt

USAGE:
Sense 2 is figurative — picturing someone's words as a price to be marked down.
