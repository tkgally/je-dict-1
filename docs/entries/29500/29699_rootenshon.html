ローテンション

ろーてんしょん
adjective-na
low-energy; subdued; in low spirits
1. low-energy; subdued; in low spirits
A quiet, unexcited, low-energy mood or manner. Describes a person who seems flat or listless rather than lively. A Japanese coinage using テンション in its Japanese sense of "mood, energy level."
今日(きょう)は朝(あさ)からローテンションだ。
I've been low-energy since this morning.
彼(かれ)はいつもローテンションな話(はな)し方(かた)をする。
He always talks in a subdued way.
寝不足(ねぶそく)のせいか、みんなローテンションで会議(かいぎ)が盛(も)り上(あ)がらなかった。
Maybe from lack of sleep, everyone was low-energy and the meeting never got lively.

A casual Japanese coinage from English "low" + "tension." Note that テンション here means mood or energy level (a Japanese sense), not physical or nervous tension.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ローテンションな人(ひと): a low-energy person
  • ローテンションで話(はな)す: to speak in a subdued way
  • 妙(みょう)にローテンション: oddly listless

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ハイテンション: high-energy, excited — the direct antonym
  • テンションが低(ひく)い: in low spirits — the phrase this word is built from
