1. reconstruction; restructuring; reorganization
The act of putting something together again in a new arrangement, or reorganizing a system, structure, or set of information. Often used with する.
データを再構成(さいこうせい)する。
To reconstruct the data.
組織(そしき)の再構成(さいこうせい)が進(すす)む。
The reorganization of the organization is moving forward.
事件(じけん)の経緯(けいい)を証言(しょうげん)から再構成(さいこうせい)した。
We reconstructed the sequence of events from the testimony.

Composed of 再(さい) (re-, again) + 構成(こうせい) (composition, structure). Takes する to become a verb (再構成(さいこうせい)する). Applies to physical structures, organizations, data, and mentally piecing together events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 組織(そしき)を再構成(さいこうせい)する: to restructure an organization
  • 記憶(きおく)を再構成(さいこうせい)する: to piece memories back together

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 再編(さいへん): reorganization — often of organizations or systems on a large scale
  • 再建(さいけん): rebuilding — restoring something that has collapsed or failed
