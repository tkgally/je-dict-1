クラファン

くらふぁん
noun
crowdfunding (casual abbreviation)
1. crowdfunding
A casual clipped form of クラウドファンディング, referring to raising money for a project or cause by collecting small contributions from many people online. Common in everyday and social-media speech.
クラファンで資金(しきん)を集(あつ)めた。
We raised funds through crowdfunding.
その映画(えいが)はクラファンで制作(せいさく)された。
That film was produced with crowdfunding.
新(あたら)しい商品(しょうひん)を世(よ)に出(だ)すために、クラファンを立(た)ち上(あ)げることにした。
We decided to launch a crowdfunding campaign to bring the new product to market.

Casual abbreviation of クラウドファンディング (crowdfunding), formed by clipping and combining the first parts of each element. Common in conversation and online; the full form is preferred in formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クラファンで集(あつ)める: to raise (money) via crowdfunding
  • クラファンを立(た)ち上(あ)げる: to launch a crowdfunding campaign
  • クラファンに参加(さんか)する: to back a crowdfunding project
