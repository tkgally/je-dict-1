俵(たわら)

たわら
noun
straw bag; bale
1. straw bag; bale (for rice, charcoal, etc.)
A cylindrical sack woven from rice straw, traditionally used to hold and transport rice, charcoal, or potatoes. Also serves as a counter for such bagged goods (一(いち)俵(ぴょう), 二(に)俵(ひょう)).
米(こめ)を俵(たわら)に詰(つ)める。
Pack the rice into straw bags.
俵(たわら)を肩(かた)に担(かつ)いで運(はこ)んだ。
He carried the straw bale on his shoulder.
昔(むかし)の農家(のうか)は収穫(しゅうかく)した米(こめ)を俵(たわら)に詰(つ)めて蔵(くら)に保管(ほかん)した。
Farmers of old packed their harvested rice into straw bales and stored them in the storehouse.

A traditional agricultural container; the woven straw ring bordering a sumo ring (土俵(どひょう)) is also made of these bales.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 米俵(こめだわら): bag of rice
  • 炭俵(すみだわら): bag of charcoal
  • 俵(たわら)に詰(つ)める: to pack into a straw bag

USAGE:
Also works as a counter for bagged goods, with the sound shifting after some numbers: 一(いち)俵(ぴょう), 二(に)俵(ひょう), 三(さん)俵(びょう).
