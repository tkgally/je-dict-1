1. to get irritated; to get annoyed
To become irritated or annoyed, typically at something mildly frustrating. A casual, colloquial verb derived from the mimetic イライラ.
少(すこ)しイラつく。
I'm a little irritated.
長(なが)い間(あいだ)待(ま)たされてイラついた。
I got annoyed at being kept waiting so long.
彼(かれ)の偉(えら)そうな態度(たいど)にイラつくことがある。
Sometimes his arrogant attitude irritates me.

USAGE: {イラつく} is a casual, colloquial verb meaning to become irritated or annoyed. It is derived from the mimetic {イライラ} and is common in everyday speech and informal writing.

CONJUGATION: A godan verb — {イラつく} → イラついた (past), イラつかない (negative), イラついて (te-form).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • イライラする: to be irritated — the standard, slightly less blunt equivalent
  • むかつく: to be pissed off — stronger; can also mean to feel nauseous
