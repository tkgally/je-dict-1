尾根筋(おねすじ)

おねすじ
noun
ridge line; the line of a ridge
1. ridge line; the crest line of a ridge
The continuous high line of a mountain ridge, where the slopes on either side meet. 筋(すじ) adds the sense of a line or course running along the ridge.
尾根筋(おねすじ)を歩(ある)く。
To walk along the ridge.
登山道(とざんどう)は尾根筋(おねすじ)に沿(そ)って続(つづ)いている。
The trail runs along the ridge line.
尾根筋(おねすじ)に出(で)ると、風(かぜ)が強(つよ)く、両側(りょうがわ)の谷(たに)がよく見(み)えた。
When we reached the ridge line, the wind was strong and we could see the valleys on both sides.

The crest line of a mountain ridge. Common in hiking and mapping contexts, where trails and boundaries often follow it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 尾根筋(おねすじ)を歩(ある)く: to walk along the ridge
  • 尾根筋(おねすじ)に沿(そ)う: to follow the ridge line

RELATED:

  • 尾根(おね): ridge — the ridge itself, without the "line/course" nuance of 筋(すじ)
