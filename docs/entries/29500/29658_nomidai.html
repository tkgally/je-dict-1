飲(の)み代(だい)

のみだい
noun
drinking expenses; bar tab
1. drinking expenses; bar tab
Money spent on alcoholic drinks, especially at a bar, izakaya, or drinking party.
飲(の)み代(だい)をおごる。
To treat someone to the drinks.
今夜(こんや)の飲(の)み代(だい)は割(わ)り勘(かん)にしよう。
Let's split tonight's drinking bill.
給料日(きゅうりょうび)の前(まえ)は飲(の)み代(だい)を節約(せつやく)している。
Before payday I cut back on my drinking expenses.

Formed from 飲(の)む (to drink) plus 代(だい) (charge, fee). A colloquial word for the money spent on alcohol, distinct from the cost of food.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飲(の)み代(だい)をおごる: to treat someone to the drinks
  • 飲(の)み代(だい)を割(わ)り勘(かん)にする: to split the drinking bill
  • 飲(の)み代(だい)がかさむ: for the drinking tab to add up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲食代(いんしょくだい): food and drink bill — covers meals as well as drinks
  • ツケ: tab; deferred payment — an unpaid running account, not the amount itself
