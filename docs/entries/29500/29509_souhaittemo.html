そうは言(い)っても

そうはいっても
expression
having said that; even so; that said
1. having said that; even so; that said; but still
A conjunctive expression used to acknowledge what was just stated and then introduce a qualification or opposing point. It softens a shift to a contrary view, like English "that said" or "even so."
そうは言(い)っても、無理(むり)だよ。
Even so, it's impossible.
忙(いそが)しい。そうは言(い)っても、休(やす)みは必要(ひつよう)だ。
I'm busy. That said, I still need a break.
予算(よさん)は限(かぎ)られている。そうは言(い)っても、質(しつ)を下(さ)げるわけにはいかない。
The budget is limited. Having said that, we can't lower the quality.

Literally "even if (one) says so," used to grant a previous point before pushing back against it. Typically stands at the head of a sentence, followed by the speaker's reservation.

USAGE:

  • そうは言(い)っても、現実(げんじつ)は厳(きび)しい: that said, reality is harsh
  • そうは言(い)っても、やるしかない: even so, we have no choice but to do it

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • とはいえ: even so — more formal and written
  • それでも: even so; nevertheless — emphasizes persistence despite the obstacle
