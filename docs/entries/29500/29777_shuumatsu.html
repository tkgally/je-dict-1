終末

しゅうまつ
noun
end; conclusion; demise
1. end; final phase; demise
The final stage or ultimate end of something, especially of the world, of life, or of a long process. Carries a weightier, often apocalyptic or terminal nuance than a simple ending.
世界(せかい)の終末(しゅうまつ)を描(えが)く。
It depicts the end of the world.
この映画(えいが)は文明(ぶんめい)の終末(しゅうまつ)をテーマ(てーま)にしている。
This film takes the demise of civilization as its theme.
終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう)では、患者(かんじゃ)の苦痛(くつう)を和(やわ)らげることが重視(じゅうし)される。
In end-of-life care, easing the patient's suffering is given priority.

Refers to a grave or ultimate ending — of the world, of life, or of an era — not an everyday conclusion. A homophone of 週末(しゅうまつ) (weekend), distinguished only by context and kanji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終末(しゅうまつ)期(き): terminal stage (e.g. of an illness)
  • 終末(しゅうまつ)医療(いりょう): end-of-life (terminal) care
  • 終末(しゅうまつ)思想(しそう): eschatology; apocalyptic thought
  • 世界(せかい)の終末(しゅうまつ): the end of the world

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最後(さいご): the last; the end — ordinary, neutral word for the final point.
  • 終(お)わり: end; ending — the everyday word, without the grave nuance.
  • 末期(まっき): final period; terminal stage — the closing phase of an era or illness.
