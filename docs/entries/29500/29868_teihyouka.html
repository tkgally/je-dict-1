低評価

ていひょうか
noun
low rating; poor evaluation
1. low rating; poor evaluation; thumbs-down
An unfavorable assessment or rating given to something, indicating dissatisfaction or a judgment of poor quality. Common on review sites and video-sharing platforms.
この映画(えいが)は低評価(ていひょうか)だった。
This movie got a low rating.
動画(どうが)に低評価(ていひょうか)がつく。
The video gets a thumbs-down.
商品(しょうひん)の低評価(ていひょうか)レビューを読(よ)んでから買(か)うか決(き)める。
I decide whether to buy after reading the low-rated reviews of the product.

Composed of 低(てい) (low) + 評価(ひょうか) (evaluation, rating). Its natural antonym is 高評価(こうひょうか) (high rating).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 低評価(ていひょうか)を受(う)ける: to receive a poor evaluation
  • 低評価(ていひょうか)ボタン: the dislike button (on video sites)
  • 低評価(ていひょうか)レビュー: a negative review

USAGE:
Online, especially on video platforms, 低評価(ていひょうか) refers concretely to the "dislike" or thumbs-down button, as in 低評価(ていひょうか)を押(お)す (to hit dislike).
