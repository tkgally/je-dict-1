1. putting into practical use; practical application
The process of taking a technology, invention, or research result out of the experimental stage and making it usable in everyday life or on the market. Often used with する as a verb.
新技術(しんぎじゅつ)の実用化(じつようか)が進(すす)む。
The practical application of the new technology is advancing.
この電池(でんち)はまだ実用化(じつようか)されていない。
This battery has not yet been put into practical use.
自動運転(じどううんてん)の実用化(じつようか)には、まだ多(おお)くの課題(かだい)が残(のこ)っている。
Many challenges still remain before self-driving cars can be put into practical use.

Formed from 実用(じつよう) (practical use) + the suffix 化(か) (-ization). Takes する to form a verb: 実用化(じつようか)する (to put into practical use).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実用化(じつようか)が進(すす)む: practical use advances
  • 実用化(じつようか)される: to be put into practical use
  • 実用化(じつようか)に向(む)けて: toward practical application
  • 早期(そうき)実用化(じつようか): early commercialization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 商品化(しょうひんか): turning into a marketable product — emphasizes selling, not just usability.
  • 実現(じつげん): realization — making something a reality, broader and not limited to technology.
