1. filthy; grimy; disgusting-looking
Stronger and more emotive than 汚(きたな)い — describes something so dirty or unkempt that it looks repellent. The らしい suffix adds a "gives off a ... impression" nuance.
汚(きたな)らしい格好(かっこう)をしている。
He's dressed in a filthy, grimy way.
床(ゆか)が汚(きたな)らしくて座(すわ)れない。
The floor is so grimy I can't sit down.
汚(きたな)らしい食(た)べ方(かた)は周(まわ)りの人(ひと)を不快(ふかい)にさせる。
Eating in a disgusting way makes those around you uncomfortable.

An intensified, more emotional form of 汚(きたな)い (dirty). The らしい here means "looking/seeming," so 汚(きたな)らしい stresses a repellent, unclean impression rather than mere dirtiness.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 汚(きたな)らしく
  • Negative: 汚(きたな)らしくない
  • Past: 汚(きたな)らしかった

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 汚(きたな)い: dirty — plainer, less emotive
  • むさ苦(くる)しい: squalid, stuffy — filthy and cramped or shabby
