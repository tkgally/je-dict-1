1. serving food; setting out dishes; waiting at table
The act of arranging prepared dishes on a tray or table and bringing them to diners. Used in restaurants, banquets, hospitals, and traditional inns.
料理(りょうり)を配膳(はいぜん)する。
To serve the dishes.
宴会(えんかい)の配膳(はいぜん)を手伝(てつだ)った。
I helped serve the food at the banquet.
この店(みせ)では、配膳(はいぜん)ロボットが席(せき)まで料理(りょうり)を運(はこ)んでくれる。
At this restaurant, a serving robot brings the dishes to your table.

Refers to setting out and delivering dishes to diners. It covers both the arrangement of the food and the act of carrying it to the table. Takes する to form the verb 配膳(はいぜん)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料理(りょうり)を配膳(はいぜん)する: to serve the dishes
  • 配膳(はいぜん)ロボット: food-serving robot
  • 配膳(はいぜん)係(がかり): serving staff; waiter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 給仕(きゅうじ): waiting on; serving — emphasizes attending to diners throughout the meal
  • 盛(も)り付(つ)け: plating — arranging food attractively on the plate, before it is served
