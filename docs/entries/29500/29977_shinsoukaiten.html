1. grand reopening; reopening after renovation
The reopening of a shop or restaurant after it has been remodeled or refurbished. Composed of 新装(しんそう) (new fittings, refurbishment) and 開店(かいてん) (opening for business).
明日(あす)新装開店(しんそうかいてん)する。
It reopens after renovation tomorrow.
新装開店(しんそうかいてん)セールを行(おこな)っている。
They are holding a grand-reopening sale.
改装(かいそう)工事(こうじ)を終(お)えて、来月(らいげつ)新装開店(しんそうかいてん)の予定(よてい)だ。
Having finished the remodeling work, the store is scheduled for its grand reopening next month.

A set phrase on shop signage and flyers; takes する to form a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新装開店(しんそうかいてん)セール: grand-reopening sale
  • 新装開店(しんそうかいてん)する: to reopen after renovation
  • 祝(しゅく)新装開店(しんそうかいてん): "Celebrating our grand reopening" (banner wording)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新規(しんき)開店(かいてん): a brand-new store opening for the first time, not a reopening
  • リニューアルオープン: a common katakana equivalent for a renovation reopening
