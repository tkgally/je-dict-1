1. to distinguish by ear; to tell apart by listening
To tell sounds, voices, or words apart by listening carefully. A compound of 聞(き)く (to listen) and 分(わ)ける (to separate).
音(おと)で楽器(がっき)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける。
To tell instruments apart by their sound.
母(はは)は足音(あしおと)で子供(こども)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける。
My mother can tell which of her children it is just from the footsteps.
双子(ふたご)の声(こえ)を電話(でんわ)越(ご)しに聞(き)き分(わ)けるのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to tell the twins' voices apart over the phone.
2. to listen to reason; to be sensible; to understand and accept
To hear an explanation and accept it — to be reasonable rather than stubborn. Often said of children who behave when things are explained to them.
よく言(い)い聞(き)かせると、子供(こども)は素直(すなお)に聞(き)き分(わ)けた。
When I explained it carefully, the child understood and did as told.
彼(かれ)はなかなか道理(どうり)を聞(き)き分(わ)けない。
He is quite unwilling to listen to reason.
何度(なんど)も説明(せつめい)して、やっと彼(かれ)は事情(じじょう)を聞(き)き分(わ)けてくれた。
After I explained it many times, he finally understood and accepted the situation.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Compound verb: 聞(き)く + 分(わ)ける.

SENSE NOTE: Sense 1 is literal — telling sounds or voices apart by ear. Sense 2 is figurative — hearing an explanation and accepting it. The related noun 聞(き)き分(わ)け appears in 聞(き)き分(わ)けのいい子(こ) (a well-behaved, reasonable child).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音(おと)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける: to distinguish sounds
  • 声(こえ)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける: to tell voices apart
  • 道理(どうり)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける: to listen to reason

RELATED:

  • 見分(みわ)ける: to tell apart by sight
