貸(か)し出(だ)す

かしだす
verb, godan
to lend out; to rent out; to check out
1. to lend out; to rent out; to check out
To provide something temporarily to someone for their use, especially in institutional contexts like libraries, rental shops, or companies lending equipment.
図書館(としょかん)で本(ほん)を貸(か)し出(だ)す。
To check out books at the library.
この機材(きざい)は外部(がいぶ)に貸(か)し出(だ)すことができる。
This equipment can be lent out externally.
市(し)では災害(さいがい)時(じ)に避難所(ひなんじょ)として体育館(たいいくかん)を貸(か)し出(だ)している。
The city lends out gymnasiums as evacuation shelters during disasters.

貸(か)し出(だ)す is a transitive godan verb. It adds a nuance of sending something out to 貸(か)す (to lend), emphasizing that the item leaves the owner's possession.

ASPECT (ている):
貸(か)し出(だ)している indicates an ongoing state where something is currently lent out.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AをBに貸(か)し出(だ)す (lend A to B)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本(ほん)を貸(か)し出(だ)す (check out books)
  • 機材(きざい)を貸(か)し出(だ)す (lend out equipment)
  • 無料(むりょう)で貸(か)し出(だ)す (lend out for free)

The noun form 貸(か)し出(だ)し refers to the lending/checkout process itself, as in 貸(か)し出(だ)し期間(きかん) (lending period).
