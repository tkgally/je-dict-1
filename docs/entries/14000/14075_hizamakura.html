膝枕(ひざまくら)

ひざまくら
noun, verb-suru
lap pillow; resting one's head on someone's lap
1. lap pillow, resting one's head on someone's lap
The act of lying down and resting one's head on another person's lap, or offering one's lap as a pillow. An intimate gesture associated with closeness and comfort between romantic partners or family members.
彼女(かのじょ)に膝枕(ひざまくら)をしてもらった。
I had my girlfriend let me rest my head on her lap.
子供(こども)が母親(ははおや)の膝枕(ひざまくら)で眠(ねむ)っている。
The child is sleeping with their head on their mother's lap.
疲(つか)れた彼(かれ)はソファ(そふぁ)で妻(つま)の膝枕(ひざまくら)に甘(あま)え、そのまま寝(ね)てしまった。
Tired, he indulged in resting his head on his wife's lap on the sofa and fell asleep like that.

USAGE:
A compound of 膝(ひざ) (knee/lap) and 枕(まくら) (pillow). Can be used as a noun or with する. The person offering their lap is the subject of 膝枕(ひざまくら)をする, while the person resting is the subject of 膝枕(ひざまくら)をしてもらう. A common motif in Japanese popular culture, anime, and manga as a symbol of intimacy and comfort.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 膝枕(ひざまくら)をする: to offer one's lap as a pillow
  • 膝枕(ひざまくら)をしてもらう: to have someone let you rest on their lap
  • 膝枕(ひざまくら)で寝(ね)る: to sleep on someone's lap
