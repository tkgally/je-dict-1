選出(せんしゅつ)

せんしゅつ
noun, verb-suru
selection; election; choosing
1. selection; election; choosing
The act of selecting or electing someone from a group, typically through a formal process such as voting or deliberation. Often used for selecting representatives, committee members, or award recipients.
委員長(いいんちょう)が選出(せんしゅつ)された。
A committee chair was elected.
投票(とうひょう)によって代表(だいひょう)を選出(せんしゅつ)する。
Representatives are selected by vote.
各地区(かくちく)から一名(いちめい)ずつ候補者(こうほしゃ)が選出(せんしゅつ)され、全国(ぜんこく)大会(たいかい)に出場(しゅつじょう)する。
One candidate is selected from each district and participates in the national tournament.

A compound of 選(せん) (to choose, to select) and 出(しゅつ) (to bring forth, to put forward). Functions as both a noun and a suru-verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 選出(せんしゅつ)する (to select, to elect)
  • 選出(せんしゅつ)される (to be selected/elected)
  • 代表(だいひょう)を選出(せんしゅつ)する (to elect a representative)
  • 投票(とうひょう)で選出(せんしゅつ)する (to select by vote)
  • 全会一致(ぜんかいいっち)で選出(せんしゅつ)する (to select unanimously)

More formal than 選(えら)ぶ (to choose). 選出(せんしゅつ) implies a structured selection process with multiple candidates, while 選(えら)ぶ is a general word for choosing. Commonly used in news articles about elections, appointments, and award selections.
