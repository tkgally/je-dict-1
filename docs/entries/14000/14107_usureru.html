薄(うす)れる

うすれる
verb-ichidan
to fade, to weaken, to diminish
1. to fade, to thin out, to grow faint
For something to become physically thinner, lighter, or less distinct over time. Used for colors, fog, light, and other sensory qualities.
色(いろ)が薄(うす)れてきた。
The color has faded.
夕方(ゆうがた)になると日差(ひざ)しが薄(うす)れる。
The sunlight fades in the evening.
長年(ながねん)の風雨(ふうう)で看板(かんばん)の文字(もじ)がすっかり薄(うす)れてしまった。
The lettering on the sign has completely faded from years of wind and rain.
2. to weaken, to diminish, to wane
For feelings, memories, awareness, or abstract qualities to gradually decrease in intensity or strength.
記憶(きおく)が薄(うす)れる。
Memories fade.
危機感(ききかん)が薄(うす)れてきている。
The sense of crisis is weakening.
時間(じかん)がたつにつれて事故(じこ)の教訓(きょうくん)が薄(うす)れ、同(おな)じような失敗(しっぱい)が繰(く)り返(かえ)される。
As time passes, the lessons of the accident fade, and similar mistakes are repeated.

USAGE NOTE:
薄(うす)れる is an intransitive ichidan verb. The transitive counterpart is 薄(うす)める (to dilute, to thin). Both senses describe a gradual process of becoming less intense.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記憶(きおく)が薄(うす)れる (memories fade)
  • 意識(いしき)が薄(うす)れる (consciousness fades / awareness diminishes)
  • 存在感(そんざいかん)が薄(うす)れる (presence diminishes)
  • 影響力(えいきょうりょく)が薄(うす)れる (influence wanes)
  • 色(いろ)が薄(うす)れる (color fades)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 消(き)える: to disappear (complete, not gradual)
  • 衰(おとろ)える: to decline, to weaken (for strength, power, vitality)
  • 褪(あ)せる: to fade, to discolor (mainly for colors and dyes)
