補完(ほかん)

ほかん
noun, verb-suru
supplementation; completion; complementing
1. supplementation, completion, complementing, filling in gaps
The act of adding what is missing or insufficient to make something complete or whole. Used for filling in gaps in knowledge, data, systems, or relationships where each part completes the other.
不足(ふそく)分(ぶん)を補完(ほかん)する。
To supplement what is lacking.
二人(ふたり)の能力(のうりょく)は互(たが)いに補完(ほかん)し合(あ)っている。
Their abilities complement each other.
このデータベースは欠落(けつらく)した部分(ぶぶん)を別(べつ)の資料(しりょう)で補完(ほかん)することで、より正確(せいかく)な分析(ぶんせき)が可能(かのう)になる。
By supplementing the missing parts of this database with other materials, more accurate analysis becomes possible.

USAGE:
Commonly used in business, academic, and technical contexts. The pattern 互(たが)いに補完(ほかん)する (to complement each other) is especially frequent. Also appears in computing contexts such as 自動(じどう)補完(ほかん) (autocomplete).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 補足(ほそく): supplementary explanation, additional note
  • 補充(ほじゅう): replenishment, filling a vacancy
  • 補填(ほてん): compensation, making up a deficit

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 互(たが)いに補完(ほかん)する: to complement each other
  • 補完(ほかん)関係(かんけい): complementary relationship
  • 補完(ほかん)的(てき): complementary
  • 自動(じどう)補完(ほかん): autocomplete
