肥大(ひだい)

ひだい
noun, verb-suru
enlargement; hypertrophy; swelling
1. enlargement; hypertrophy
Abnormal increase in the size of an organ or tissue, typically used in medical contexts.
心臓(しんぞう)が肥大(ひだい)している。
The heart is enlarged.
前立腺(ぜんりつせん)の肥大(ひだい)は高齢(こうれい)の男性(だんせい)に多(おお)い。
Prostate enlargement is common in older men.
検査(けんさ)で左心室(さしんしつ)の肥大(ひだい)が見(み)つかり、治療(ちりょう)が必要(ひつよう)になった。
An examination found left ventricular hypertrophy, and treatment became necessary.
2. swelling; bloating; excessive growth
Figurative expansion beyond an appropriate or manageable size, used for organizations, budgets, or systems.
組織(そしき)が肥大(ひだい)しすぎた。
The organization has grown too large.
予算(よさん)が年々(ねんねん)肥大(ひだい)して手(て)に負(お)えなくなっている。
The budget has been ballooning year after year and is getting out of hand.
官僚(かんりょう)機構(きこう)が肥大(ひだい)し、意思(いし)決定(けってい)が遅(おそ)くなっている。
The bureaucracy has become bloated and decision-making has slowed down.

Often used as a suru-verb: 肥大(ひだい)する (to enlarge, to become hypertrophied).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心臓(しんぞう)肥大(ひだい) (cardiac hypertrophy)
  • 前立腺(ぜんりつせん)肥大(ひだい) (prostate enlargement)
  • 組織(そしき)が肥大(ひだい)する (an organization swells in size)

USAGE NOTE:
In medical contexts, 肥大(ひだい) is a neutral clinical term. In figurative use, it typically carries a negative connotation, implying something has grown beyond a desirable or functional size.
