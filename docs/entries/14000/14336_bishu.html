美酒(びしゅ)

びしゅ
noun
fine wine; fine sake; excellent liquor
1. fine wine; fine sake; excellent liquor
High-quality alcoholic beverage, especially sake or wine. Often used figuratively to describe the sweet taste of victory or success.
勝利(しょうり)の美酒(びしゅ)に酔(よ)う。
To savor the sweet taste of victory.
この蔵元(くらもと)は毎年(まいとし)美酒(びしゅ)を生(う)み出(だ)している。
This brewery produces fine sake every year.
長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)が実(みの)り、チームは優勝(ゆうしょう)の美酒(びしゅ)を味(あじ)わった。
Years of effort bore fruit, and the team savored the sweet taste of a championship victory.

Compound of 美(び) (beautiful, fine) + 酒(しゅ) (sake, liquor).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勝利(しょうり)の美酒(びしゅ) (the sweet taste of victory)
  • 美酒(びしゅ)に酔(よ)う (to savor fine sake; to revel in success)
  • 美酒(びしゅ)を味(あじ)わう (to taste fine sake; to enjoy a sweet victory)

USAGE NOTE:
The figurative expression 勝利(しょうり)の美酒(びしゅ)に酔(よ)う is extremely common in sports journalism and news. In literal use, 美酒(びしゅ) is a somewhat literary way to praise the quality of sake or wine.
