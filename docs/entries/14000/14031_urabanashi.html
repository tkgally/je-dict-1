裏話(うらばなし)

うらばなし
noun
behind-the-scenes story, inside story
1. behind-the-scenes story, inside story
A story or piece of information about the hidden, unofficial, or private side of an event, project, or situation. Often refers to things the public doesn't normally get to hear about.
映画(えいが)の裏話(うらばなし)を聞(き)いた。
I heard a behind-the-scenes story about the movie.
監督(かんとく)が制作(せいさく)の裏話(うらばなし)をインタビューで語(かた)った。
The director shared behind-the-scenes stories about the production in an interview.
あの有名(ゆうめい)な契約(けいやく)にはいろいろな裏話(うらばなし)があって、関係者(かんけいしゃ)しか知(し)らない事実(じじつ)が多(おお)い。
There are many inside stories behind that famous deal, with lots of facts known only to those involved.

USAGE:
A casual, conversational word. People enjoy hearing 裏話(うらばなし) because they reveal what really happened behind the public face of events. Common in entertainment, business, and politics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 裏話(うらばなし)を聞(き)く: to hear an inside story
  • 裏話(うらばなし)を語(かた)る: to tell behind-the-scenes stories
  • 裏話(うらばなし)を明(あ)かす: to reveal inside stories
