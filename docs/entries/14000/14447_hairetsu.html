配列(はいれつ)

はいれつ
noun, verb-suru
arrangement; array; sequence
1. arrangement; layout; sequence
The way items are arranged or ordered in a particular pattern or sequence. Used for physical arrangements and abstract orderings.
キーボードの配列(はいれつ)に慣(な)れない。
I can't get used to the keyboard layout.
座席(ざせき)の配列(はいれつ)を変(か)えて、グループ活動(かつどう)がしやすいようにした。
I changed the seating arrangement to make group activities easier.
五十音(ごじゅうおん)の配列(はいれつ)順(じゅん)に並(なら)べる。
Arrange them in the order of the Japanese syllabary.
2. array (computing/science)
A data structure consisting of a collection of elements arranged in order, each identified by an index. Also used in biology for sequences of amino acids or nucleotides.
配列(はいれつ)の要素(ようそ)にアクセスする。
Access the elements of an array.
DNAの塩基(えんき)配列(はいれつ)を解析(かいせき)する。
Analyze the DNA base sequence.
データを配列(はいれつ)に格納(かくのう)してから処理(しょり)する。
Store the data in an array and then process it.

A compound of 配(はい) (to distribute, to arrange) and 列(れつ) (row, line). Functions as both a noun and a suru-verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 配列(はいれつ)する (to arrange, to sequence)
  • キーボード配列(はいれつ) (keyboard layout)
  • 座席(ざせき)配列(はいれつ) (seating arrangement)
  • 塩基(えんき)配列(はいれつ) (base sequence — in genetics)
  • 配列(はいれつ)の要素(ようそ) (array element — in computing)
  • アミノ酸(さん)配列(はいれつ) (amino acid sequence)

In everyday use, sense 1 is more common (keyboard layout, seating arrangement). In technical contexts, sense 2 is standard terminology in both computing and molecular biology.
