野良猫(のらねこ)

のらねこ
noun
stray cat; feral cat
1. stray cat; feral cat
A cat that lives outdoors without an owner. May be a former pet that was abandoned or a cat born in the wild.
公園(こうえん)に野良猫(のらねこ)がいた。
There was a stray cat in the park.
野良猫(のらねこ)に餌(えさ)をやる人(ひと)がいる。
There are people who feed the stray cats.
近所(きんじょ)の野良猫(のらねこ)を保護(ほご)して、動物病院(どうぶつびょういん)に連(つ)れて行(い)った。
I rescued a neighborhood stray cat and took it to the animal hospital.

A compound of 野良(のら) (stray, ownerless — originally meaning "field") and 猫(ねこ) (cat).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 野良猫(のらねこ)に餌(えさ)をやる (to feed stray cats)
  • 野良猫(のらねこ)を保護(ほご)する (to rescue a stray cat)
  • 野良猫(のらねこ)の世話(せわ) (caring for stray cats)
  • 地域(ちいき)猫(ねこ) (community cat — managed stray cat)

In Japan, the issue of 野良猫(のらねこ) is a significant community topic. Many neighborhoods have volunteer groups that practice TNR (捕獲(ほかく)・不妊(ふにん)手術(しゅじゅつ)・返還(へんかん): trap-neuter-return) and manage stray cat populations as 地域(ちいき)猫(ねこ) (community cats). The related term 野良犬(のらいぬ) means "stray dog."
