遂(と)げる

とげる
verb, ichidan
to accomplish; to achieve; to carry out
1. to accomplish; to achieve; to carry out to completion
To see something through to the end, to bring an endeavor or process to completion. Often used for significant achievements or transformations.
目的(もくてき)を遂(と)げる。
To accomplish one's objective.
この街(まち)は大(おお)きな発展(はってん)を遂(と)げた。
This city achieved great development.
長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)の末(すえ)に、ついに夢(ゆめ)を遂(と)げることができた。
After years of effort, I was finally able to fulfill my dream.

遂(と)げる is a transitive ichidan verb with a literary, formal tone. It emphasizes completion and finality.

ASPECT (ている):
Rarely used in the ている form. The focus is on the completed result rather than an ongoing state.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Aを遂(と)げる (accomplish A)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目的(もくてき)を遂(と)げる (accomplish an objective)
  • 成長(せいちょう)を遂(と)げる (achieve growth)
  • 発展(はってん)を遂(と)げる (achieve development)
  • 変貌(へんぼう)を遂(と)げる (undergo a transformation)
  • 進化(しんか)を遂(と)げる (achieve an evolution)

Also appears in the compound verb 成(な)し遂(と)げる (to accomplish, to achieve), which is even more emphatic. The expression 死(し)を遂(と)げる means "to meet one's death" (literary).
