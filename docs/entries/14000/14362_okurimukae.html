送(おく)り迎(むか)え

おくりむかえ
noun, verb (suru)
picking up and dropping off
1. picking up and dropping off; transportation to and from
The act of taking someone to a destination and bringing them back, or more broadly, providing rides both ways. Very common in the context of children, elderly care, and daily commuting.
子(こ)どもの送(おく)り迎(むか)えをする。
To pick up and drop off the children.
毎日(まいにち)学校(がっこう)への送(おく)り迎(むか)えが大変(たいへん)だ。
The daily school run is exhausting.
駅(えき)まで車(くるま)で送(おく)り迎(むか)えしてくれる人(ひと)がいると助(たす)かる。
It would be a great help to have someone who can drive me to and from the station.

USAGE NOTES:
送(おく)り迎(むか)え combines 送(おく)り (sending/taking someone) and 迎(むか)え (picking up/meeting someone) into one word meaning round-trip transportation.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • の送(おく)り迎(むか)えをする (to do the pickup and drop-off for...)
  • への送(おく)り迎(むか)え (transportation to and from...)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子(こ)どもの送(おく)り迎(むか)え (school run for children)
  • 送(おく)り迎(むか)えバス (shuttle bus)
  • 送(おく)り迎(むか)えサービス (pickup and drop-off service)

This word is especially common among parents discussing daily routines with childcare and school.
