重点(じゅうてん)

じゅうてん
noun
emphasis; priority; focal point
1. emphasis; priority; focal point
The most important point or area to focus on. Used to indicate where effort, resources, or attention should be concentrated.
教育(きょういく)に重点(じゅうてん)を置(お)く。
To place emphasis on education.
今年度(こんねんど)の重点(じゅうてん)目標(もくひょう)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
They announced this fiscal year's priority goals.
防災(ぼうさい)対策(たいさく)に重点(じゅうてん)を置(お)いた予算(よさん)編成(へんせい)が求(もと)められている。
A budget plan that prioritizes disaster prevention measures is being called for.

重点(じゅうてん) is most commonly used with the pattern ...に重点(じゅうてん)を置(お)く (to place emphasis on).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重点(じゅうてん)を置(お)く (to place emphasis on)
  • 重点(じゅうてん)的(てき)に (with emphasis, intensively)
  • 重点(じゅうてん)項目(こうもく) (priority items)
  • 重点(じゅうてん)目標(もくひょう) (priority goals)
  • 重点(じゅうてん)分野(ぶんや) (priority field/area)

Commonly used in business, government, and education contexts to indicate where attention and resources should be concentrated. The adverbial form 重点(じゅうてん)的(てき)に (intensively, with priority) is also very frequent.
