裏腹(うらはら)

うらはら
noun, adjective-na
contrary, opposite
1. contrary, opposite, contradictory
Describes a situation where something is the opposite of what is expected, or where one's words and actions do not match. Most commonly used in the pattern ～とは裏腹に (contrary to ~).
言葉(ことば)とは裏腹(うらはら)に涙(なみだ)があふれた。
Contrary to her words, tears overflowed.
期待(きたい)とは裏腹(うらはら)に、結果(けっか)は散々(さんざん)だった。
Contrary to expectations, the results were terrible.
明(あか)るい笑顔(えがお)とは裏腹(うらはら)に、彼(かれ)の心(こころ)の中(なか)は不安(ふあん)でいっぱいだった。
Contrary to his bright smile, his heart was full of anxiety.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the pattern ～とは裏腹(うらはら)に (contrary to ~). The phrase sets up a contrast between an outward appearance or expectation and the actual reality.

PATTERN:

  • Noun + とは裏腹(うらはら)に: contrary to [noun]
  • 言葉(ことば)とは裏腹(うらはら)に: contrary to one's words
  • 期待(きたい)とは裏腹(うらはら)に: contrary to expectations
  • 本心(ほんしん)とは裏腹(うらはら)に: contrary to one's true feelings
