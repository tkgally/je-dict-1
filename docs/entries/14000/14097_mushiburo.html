蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)

むしぶろ
noun
steam bath, sauna
1. steam bath, sauna
A bath or room filled with steam for bathing or relaxation. Also used figuratively to describe an extremely hot and humid environment.
蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)に入(はい)る。
Take a steam bath.
真夏(まなつ)の電車(でんしゃ)は蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)のようだ。
The train in midsummer is like a steam bath.
エアコンが壊(こわ)れて、部屋(へや)の中(なか)が蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)のような状態(じょうたい)になってしまった。
The air conditioner broke down, and the room turned into something like a steam bath.

USAGE NOTE:
蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ) literally means "steaming bath." In everyday speech, it is very commonly used as a simile with のよう or みたい to describe unbearably hot and humid places, such as crowded trains, rooms without air conditioning, or outdoor spaces during the humid Japanese summer.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)のような暑(あつ)さ (heat like a steam bath)
  • 蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)に入(はい)る (take a steam bath)
  • 蒸(む)し風呂(ぶろ)状態(じょうたい) (steam-bath conditions)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サウナ: sauna (dry heat sauna, loanword from Finnish)
  • 岩盤浴(がんばんよく): hot stone bath (lying on heated stones)
