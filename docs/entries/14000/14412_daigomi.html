醍醐味(だいごみ)

だいごみ
noun
real pleasure; true delight; essence of enjoyment
1. real pleasure; true delight; the best part
The essential appeal or greatest enjoyment of something. Used to highlight what makes an experience truly worthwhile or deeply satisfying.
旅(たび)の醍醐味(だいごみ)は出会(であ)いだ。
The real pleasure of travel is the encounters.
ライブで聴(き)く音楽(おんがく)には独特(どくとく)の醍醐味(だいごみ)がある。
Listening to music live has a unique appeal.
自分(じぶん)で釣(つ)った魚(さかな)をその場(ば)で焼(や)いて食(た)べるのが、キャンプの醍醐味(だいごみ)だと思(おも)う。
I think the true delight of camping is catching your own fish and grilling it on the spot.

醍醐味(だいごみ) originally comes from Buddhist terminology. 醍醐(だいご) refers to the finest essence of milk (clarified butter or ghee), regarded as the most refined and purest substance. By extension, the word came to mean the ultimate or quintessential pleasure of something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ...の醍醐味(だいごみ) (the real pleasure of...)
  • 醍醐味(だいごみ)を味(あじ)わう (to savor the true delight)
  • 醍醐味(だいごみ)がある (to have a special appeal)

This is a culturally rich word that learners encounter frequently in descriptions of experiences, hobbies, and activities. It goes beyond simple enjoyment to express what makes something uniquely or deeply satisfying.
