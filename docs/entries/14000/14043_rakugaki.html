落書(らくが)き

らくがき
noun, verb-suru
graffiti, scribbling, doodle
1. graffiti, scribbling, doodling
Writing or drawing on walls, desks, or other surfaces where it is not intended, or casual, unplanned drawings and scribbles, such as doodling in a notebook.
壁(かべ)に落書(らくが)きをしてはいけない。
You must not scribble on the walls.
ノートの端(はし)に落書(らくが)きをしながら授業(じゅぎょう)を聞(き)いていた。
I was doodling in the margins of my notebook while listening to the lecture.
歴史的(れきしてき)な建物(たてもの)への落書(らくが)きが問題(もんだい)になっていて、市(し)は監視(かんし)カメラの設置(せっち)を検討(けんとう)している。
Graffiti on historic buildings has become a problem, and the city is considering installing surveillance cameras.

USAGE:
Covers a range from innocent doodling (in notebooks) to vandalism (on buildings and public property). The tone depends on context — a child's doodle is harmless, while graffiti on cultural heritage sites is a serious issue.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落書(らくが)きをする: to scribble, to doodle, to graffiti
  • 落書(らくが)きを消(け)す: to remove graffiti
  • 落書(らくが)き帳(ちょう): sketchbook, doodle pad
