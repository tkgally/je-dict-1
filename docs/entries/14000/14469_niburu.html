鈍(にぶ)る

にぶる
verb, godan
to become dull; to weaken; to slow down
1. to become dull; to lose sharpness
For a blade or edge to lose its sharpness through use or neglect.
包丁(ほうちょう)の切(き)れ味(あじ)が鈍(にぶ)った。
The kitchen knife has gone dull.
使(つか)い続(つづ)けるとハサミの刃(は)が鈍(にぶ)る。
Scissors get dull if you keep using them.
長(なが)い間(あいだ)研(と)いでいないので、刃(は)がだいぶ鈍(にぶ)っている。
Since I haven't sharpened it in a long time, the blade has become quite dull.
2. to weaken; to slow down; to deteriorate
For skills, senses, speed, or determination to decline or lose their edge. A figurative extension of the literal meaning.
勘(かん)が鈍(にぶ)る。
One's intuition weakens.
練習(れんしゅう)をサボると腕(うで)が鈍(にぶ)る。
If you skip practice, your skills deteriorate.
寒(さむ)さで手(て)の感覚(かんかく)が鈍(にぶ)って、うまくボタンが押(お)せなかった。
My hands were so numb from the cold that I couldn't press the buttons properly.

鈍(にぶ)る is an intransitive godan verb. The transitive form is 鈍(にぶ)らせる (to make dull, to cause to weaken).

ASPECT (ている):
鈍(にぶ)っている describes the resulting state of being dull or weakened.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 刃(は)が鈍(にぶ)る (a blade becomes dull)
  • 勘(かん)が鈍(にぶ)る (intuition weakens)
  • 腕(うで)が鈍(にぶ)る (skills deteriorate)
  • 動(うご)きが鈍(にぶ)る (movements slow down)
  • 感覚(かんかく)が鈍(にぶ)る (sensation becomes numb)

RELATED WORDS:
The adjective 鈍(にぶ)い (dull, slow) shares the same kanji. Compare with 鋭(するど)い (sharp) and 鋭(するど)くなる (to become sharp), which express the opposite direction.
