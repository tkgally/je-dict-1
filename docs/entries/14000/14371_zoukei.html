造形(ぞうけい)

ぞうけい
noun, verb (suru)
modeling; shaping; plastic arts
1. modeling; shaping; forming
The act of creating a three-dimensional form or shape, whether by sculpting, molding, or other methods. Also refers to the resulting form or design.
粘土(ねんど)で動物(どうぶつ)を造形(ぞうけい)する。
To sculpt animals out of clay.
この建物(たてもの)は造形(ぞうけい)が美(うつく)しい。
This building has beautiful design.
3Dプリンターで複雑(ふくざつ)な造形(ぞうけい)ができるようになった。
Complex shapes can now be formed using 3D printers.
2. plastic arts; formative arts
The visual and spatial arts that involve creating physical forms, including sculpture, architecture, and design. Used as a broad category in art and education.
子(こ)どもの造形(ぞうけい)活動(かつどう)を通(とお)して創造力(そうぞうりょく)を育(そだ)てる。
Fostering creativity through children's art-making activities.
大学(だいがく)で造形(ぞうけい)芸術(げいじゅつ)を学(まな)んでいる。
I'm studying formative arts at university.
日本(にほん)の伝統的(でんとうてき)な造形(ぞうけい)美(び)は海外(かいがい)でも高(たか)く評価(ひょうか)されている。
Japan's traditional formative beauty is highly regarded overseas as well.

USAGE NOTES:
造形(ぞうけい) covers both the process of creating forms and the aesthetic quality of the resulting shape. It appears frequently in art education, design criticism, and manufacturing contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 造形(ぞうけい)芸術(げいじゅつ) (plastic/formative arts)
  • 造形(ぞうけい)美(び) (formative beauty, aesthetic of form)
  • 造形(ぞうけい)活動(かつどう) (art-making activities)
  • 造形(ぞうけい)作家(さっか) (sculptor, formative artist)

東京(とうきょう)造形(ぞうけい)大学(だいがく) (Tokyo Zokei University) is a well-known art university in Japan.
