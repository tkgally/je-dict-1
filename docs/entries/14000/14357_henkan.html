返還(へんかん)

へんかん
noun, verb-suru
return; restitution; handover
1. return; restitution; giving back
The act of returning something to its rightful owner or original holder, especially in official, legal, or diplomatic contexts.
敷金(しききん)の返還(へんかん)を求(もと)める。
To request the return of a security deposit.
沖縄(おきなわ)の本土復帰(ほんどふっき)は領土(りょうど)の返還(へんかん)だった。
Okinawa's reversion was a return of territory.
美術館(びじゅつかん)は戦時中(せんじちゅう)に略奪(りゃくだつ)された作品(さくひん)を元(もと)の所有者(しょゆうしゃ)に返還(へんかん)することを決(き)めた。
The museum decided to return works looted during wartime to their original owners.

Compound of 返(へん) (return) + 還(かん) (to go back, to restore). Used as a suru-verb: 返還(へんかん)する (to return, to give back).

Not to be confused with the homophone 変換(へんかん) (conversion, transformation).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 領土(りょうど)返還(へんかん) (return of territory)
  • 敷金(しききん)の返還(へんかん) (return of a security deposit)
  • 返還(へんかん)を求(もと)める (to demand the return of something)
  • 返還(へんかん)請求(せいきゅう) (claim for restitution)

USAGE NOTE:
A formal word used in legal, diplomatic, and official contexts. For returning everyday items, 返(かえ)す is more natural. 返還(へんかん) implies returning something of significant value or official importance.
