近年(きんねん)

きんねん
noun, adverb
recent years; in recent years
1. recent years; in recent years; lately
Refers to the past several years up to the present, used to introduce trends or changes that have emerged over a period of time.
近年(きんねん)、観光客(かんこうきゃく)が増(ふ)えている。
In recent years, the number of tourists has been increasing.
近年(きんねん)の技術(ぎじゅつ)革新(かくしん)は目覚(めざ)ましい。
Technological innovation in recent years has been remarkable.
近年(きんねん)、異常気象(いじょうきしょう)による被害(ひがい)が世界各地(せかいかくち)で深刻(しんこく)になっている。
In recent years, damage from extreme weather has become severe in various parts of the world.

Compound of 近(きん) (near, recent) + 年(ねん) (year). Typically refers to a span of roughly 5-10 years. More formal than 最近(さいきん), which can mean "recently" on any timescale.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 近年(きんねん)の傾向(けいこう) (recent trends)
  • 近年(きんねん)まれに見(み)る (rarely seen in recent years)
  • 近年(きんねん)急増(きゅうぞう)している (rapidly increasing in recent years)

USAGE NOTE:
Often appears at the beginning of sentences to set a temporal frame. Primarily a written or formal-speech word. In casual conversation, 最近(さいきん) or ここ数年(すうねん) are preferred.
