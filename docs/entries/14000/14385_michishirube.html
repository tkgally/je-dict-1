道標(みちしるべ)

みちしるべ
noun
signpost; guidepost; guide
1. signpost; guidepost; waymark
A physical marker along a road or path indicating direction or distance to a destination.
山道(やまみち)に道標(みちしるべ)が立(た)っていた。
A signpost stood along the mountain path.
道標(みちしるべ)に従(したが)って進(すす)めば迷(まよ)わない。
If you follow the signposts, you won't get lost.
古(ふる)い街道(かいどう)には、江戸(えど)時代(じだい)の石(いし)の道標(みちしるべ)が残(のこ)っている。
Along the old highways, stone signposts from the Edo period still remain.
2. guide; guiding principle; compass
Figuratively, something or someone that serves as a guide in life, work, or decision-making.
恩師(おんし)の言葉(ことば)が人生(じんせい)の道標(みちしるべ)になった。
My mentor's words became a guiding light in my life.
この本(ほん)は若(わか)い研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)への道標(みちしるべ)となるだろう。
This book will serve as a guide for young researchers.
将来(しょうらい)の道標(みちしるべ)が見(み)つからず、悩(なや)んでいる学生(がくせい)は多(おお)い。
Many students are struggling because they can't find a guiding direction for their future.

USAGE NOTES:
道標(みちしるべ) can also be read as どうひょう, which is more formal and technical. The reading みちしるべ is more common in everyday and literary usage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道標(みちしるべ)に従(したが)う (to follow a signpost)
  • 人生(じんせい)の道標(みちしるべ) (guide/compass in life)
  • 石(いし)の道標(みちしるべ) (stone signpost)
  • 道標(みちしるべ)となる (to serve as a guide)

The figurative use (a guiding principle or inspiration) is poetic and common in titles of books, songs, and speeches.
