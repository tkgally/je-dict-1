郷土(きょうど)

きょうど
noun
homeland; native place; one's home region
1. homeland; native place; one's home region
The region or area where one was born and raised. Often used in compounds to describe things characteristic of or originating from a particular locality.
郷土(きょうど)料理(りょうり)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy local cuisine.
郷土(きょうど)愛(あい)の強(つよ)い人(ひと)が多(おお)い地域(ちいき)だ。
It is a region with many people who have strong love for their homeland.
郷土(きょうど)史(し)の研究(けんきゅう)を通(つう)じて、この土地(とち)の歴史(れきし)の奥深(おくぶか)さを知(し)った。
Through studying local history, I learned the depth of this land's history.

郷土(きょうど) emphasizes emotional attachment to one's native place and is frequently used as a modifier in compound words.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 郷土(きょうど)料理(りょうり) (local/regional cuisine)
  • 郷土(きょうど)愛(あい) (love for one's homeland)
  • 郷土(きょうど)史(し) (local history)
  • 郷土(きょうど)芸能(げいのう) (local performing arts)
  • 郷土(きょうど)資料館(しりょうかん) (local history museum)

Distinct from 故郷(ふるさと)/故郷(こきょう) (hometown, birthplace), which focuses on a specific place one has left behind. 郷土(きょうど) is more often used as a modifier and carries connotations of cultural identity and regional pride. The compound 郷土(きょうど)料理(りょうり) (local cuisine) is particularly common and refers to traditional dishes unique to a particular region.
