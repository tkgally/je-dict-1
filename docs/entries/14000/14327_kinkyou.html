近況(きんきょう)

きんきょう
noun
recent situation; how one has been lately
1. recent situation; how one has been lately; update on one's life
Information about what has been happening recently in someone's life or in a situation. Often used when catching up with someone after a period of no contact.
近況(きんきょう)を報告(ほうこく)する。
To report on one's recent situation.
久(ひさ)しぶりに会(あ)って、お互(たが)いの近況(きんきょう)を話(はな)した。
We met after a long time and talked about what we've each been up to.
SNSで友人(ゆうじん)の近況(きんきょう)を知(し)ることができるが、直接(ちょくせつ)会(あ)って話(はな)すのとは違(ちが)う。
You can learn about friends' recent situations on social media, but it's different from meeting in person to talk.

A compound of 近(きん) (recent) + 況(きょう) (situation). Common in letters, greetings, and reunion conversations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 近況(きんきょう)を報告(ほうこく)する (report on recent events)
  • 近況(きんきょう)を尋(たず)ねる (ask about someone's recent situation)
  • 近況(きんきょう)報告(ほうこく) (update, status report)
  • お互(たが)いの近況(きんきょう) (each other's recent news)

Often appears in letters and emails as a topic: 近況(きんきょう)をお知(し)らせします (Let me tell you about my recent situation). Also used in alumni newsletters and year-end greetings.
