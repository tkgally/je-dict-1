1. to become diluted, to become thinner, to weaken
For a liquid, color, flavor, or abstract quality to become weaker or less concentrated. Used for both physical dilution (soup becoming watery) and figurative weakening (interest fading, impressions dimming).
スープの味(あじ)が薄(うす)まった。
The soup's flavor became diluted.
時間(じかん)がたつにつれて記憶(きおく)が薄(うす)まっていく。
As time passes, memories gradually fade.
水(みず)を足(た)しすぎて紅茶(こうちゃ)の色(いろ)がすっかり薄(うす)まってしまった。
I added too much water, and the color of the tea became completely washed out.

Godan verb (五段(ごだん)). 薄(うす)まる → 薄(うす)まらない → 薄(うす)まって → 薄(うす)まった

USAGE:
The intransitive counterpart of 薄(うす)める (to dilute, to weaken). 薄(うす)まる describes the change happening on its own, while 薄(うす)める implies someone deliberately diluting something. Can be used for physical properties (flavor, color, concentration) and abstract qualities (memories, impressions, suspicion).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 味(あじ)が薄(うす)まる: flavor becomes diluted
  • 記憶(きおく)が薄(うす)まる: memories fade
  • 色(いろ)が薄(うす)まる: color fades
  • 印象(いんしょう)が薄(うす)まる: impression weakens
