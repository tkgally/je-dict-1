語(かた)らう

かたらう
verb, godan
to converse; to talk together
1. to converse; to talk together; to share stories
To engage in warm, unhurried conversation, often with a sense of intimacy or emotional depth.
友(とも)と語(かた)らう。
To talk with a friend.
夜遅(よるおそ)くまで将来(しょうらい)について語(かた)らった。
We talked about the future late into the night.
久(ひさ)しぶりに再会(さいかい)した旧友(きゅうゆう)と、昔(むかし)の思(おも)い出(で)を語(かた)らって楽(たの)しい時間(じかん)を過(す)ごした。
I spent an enjoyable time sharing old memories with an old friend I hadn't seen in a long time.

語(かた)らう is an intransitive godan verb that conveys a warm, leisurely quality of conversation. It is more literary and poetic than 話(はな)す or しゃべる.

ASPECT (ている):
語(かた)らっている indicates an ongoing conversation.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xと語(かた)らう (converse with X)
  • Xについて語(かた)らう (talk about X)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 友(とも)と語(かた)らう (talk with friends)
  • 夢(ゆめ)を語(かた)らう (share one's dreams)
  • 酒(さけ)を飲(の)みながら語(かた)らう (talk over drinks)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 語(かた)らう vs 話(はな)す: 語(かた)らう implies unhurried, heartfelt conversation, while 話(はな)す is a neutral, general word for speaking or talking.
  • 語(かた)らう vs 語(かた)る: 語(かた)る means to narrate or tell a story (often one-directional), while 語(かた)らう implies mutual, back-and-forth exchange.
