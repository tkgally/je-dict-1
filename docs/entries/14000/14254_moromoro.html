諸々(もろもろ)

もろもろ
noun, pre-noun adjectival
various; all sorts of
1. various; all sorts of; sundry
A collective reference to many different things, implying a wide range of items, factors, or matters.
諸々(もろもろ)の事情(じじょう)がある。
There are various circumstances.
諸々(もろもろ)の費用(ひよう)を含(ふく)めると、結構(けっこう)な金額(きんがく)になる。
When you include all the various costs, it comes to a considerable amount.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの際(さい)には、住所(じゅうしょ)変更(へんこう)の届(とど)け出(で)など諸々(もろもろ)の手続(てつづ)きが必要(ひつよう)だ。
When moving, various procedures such as address change notifications are required.

諸々(もろもろ) means "various" or "all sorts of." It has a slightly formal or mature tone.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 諸々(もろもろ)の + noun (various [things])
  • 諸々(もろもろ) as a standalone noun meaning "various things"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 諸々(もろもろ)の事情(じじょう) (various circumstances)
  • 諸々(もろもろ)の費用(ひよう) (various expenses)
  • 諸々(もろもろ)の手続(てつづ)き (various procedures)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 諸々(もろもろ) vs 色々(いろいろ): Both mean "various," but 諸々(もろもろ) is more formal and often used in written or business contexts, while 色々(いろいろ) is more conversational and widely used in everyday speech.
