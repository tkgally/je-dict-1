鑑(かんが)みる

かんがみる
verb, ichidan
to consider; to take into account; in light of
1. to consider; to take into account; in light of
To reflect upon past events, precedents, or circumstances and use them as a basis for judgment or action. A formal expression used in written and official contexts.
過去(かこ)の事例(じれい)に鑑(かんが)みる。
To take past cases into consideration.
現状(げんじょう)に鑑(かんが)みて、計画(けいかく)を見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
In light of the current situation, the plan needs to be reconsidered.
国際(こくさい)情勢(じょうせい)に鑑(かんが)み、政府(せいふ)は新(あら)たな対策(たいさく)を講(こう)じることを決定(けってい)した。
In light of the international situation, the government decided to implement new measures.

USAGE:
鑑(かんが)みる is a formal, literary verb. It almost always takes the particle に: ～に鑑(かんが)みる (in light of ~, considering ~). The conjunctive form ～に鑑(かんが)み is frequently used in official documents and formal announcements.

GRAMMAR NOTE:
Despite looking like it could be godan, 鑑(かんが)みる conjugates as an ichidan verb: 鑑(かんが)みる → 鑑(かんが)みて → 鑑(かんが)みた.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～に鑑(かんが)みる (in light of ~)
  • ～に鑑(かんが)みて (considering ~ — te-form)
  • ～に鑑(かんが)み (considering ~ — formal conjunctive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事情(じじょう)に鑑(かんが)みる (in view of circumstances)
  • 前例(ぜんれい)に鑑(かんが)みる (in light of precedent)
  • 時代(じだい)の変化(へんか)に鑑(かんが)みる (considering the changes of the times)
