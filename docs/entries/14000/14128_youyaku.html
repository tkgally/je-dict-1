要約(ようやく)

ようやく
noun, verb-suru
summary, abstract
1. summary, abstract, synopsis
A condensed version of a text, speech, or argument that presents the key points. Used in academic, business, and everyday contexts when distilling information to its essentials.
記事(きじ)の要約(ようやく)を書(か)く。
I write a summary of the article.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)を要約(ようやく)してください。
Please summarize the contents of the meeting.
論文(ろんぶん)の冒頭(ぼうとう)には要約(ようやく)をつけるのが一般的(いっぱんてき)だ。
It is standard to include an abstract at the beginning of an academic paper.

USAGE:
Do not confuse 要約(ようやく) (summary) with the adverb 漸(ようや)く (finally, at last), which is a homophone with a completely different meaning and kanji. 要約(ようやく) can be used as a noun or as a する verb meaning "to summarize."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 要約(ようやく)する: to summarize
  • 要約(ようやく)を書(か)く: to write a summary
  • 要約(ようやく)文(ぶん): summary text
  • 要約(ようやく)力(りょく): summarization ability
  • 三行(さんぎょう)要約(ようやく): three-line summary (internet slang for TL;DR)
