誹謗(ひぼう)

ひぼう
noun, verb (suru)
slander; defamation
1. slander; defamation; vilification
Speaking ill of someone or damaging their reputation through false or malicious statements.
誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう)は許(ゆる)されない。
Slander and defamation are not acceptable.
ネット上(じょう)での誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう)が社会(しゃかい)問題(もんだい)になっている。
Online slander has become a social problem.
有名人(ゆうめいじん)を誹謗(ひぼう)する書(か)き込(こ)みが増(ふ)えたため、法的(ほうてき)措置(そち)が取(と)られた。
Because posts slandering celebrities increased, legal action was taken.

誹謗(ひぼう) refers to slandering or speaking ill of someone. It is almost always used in the compound 誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう), which is the standard legal and media term for defamation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう) (slander and defamation)
  • 誹謗(ひぼう)する (to slander)
  • 誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう)対策(たいさく) (anti-defamation measures)

The word has become especially prominent in discussions about cyberbullying and online harassment. Japanese law treats 誹謗(ひぼう)中傷(ちゅうしょう) as a punishable offense, and recent legal reforms have made it easier to identify anonymous online slanderers.
