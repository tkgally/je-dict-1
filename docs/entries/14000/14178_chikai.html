誓(ちか)い

ちかい
noun
vow, oath, pledge
1. vow, oath, pledge, promise
A solemn promise or commitment, often made with deep personal conviction or in a formal ceremonial context. Carries more weight and formality than a simple promise.
二人(ふたり)は愛(あい)の誓(ちか)いを立(た)てた。
The two made a vow of love.
新年(しんねん)の誓(ちか)いを心(こころ)に刻(きざ)んだ。
I engraved my New Year's pledge in my heart.
選手(せんしゅ)たちは正々堂々(せいせいどうどう)と戦(たたか)うことを誓(ちか)いの言葉(ことば)として述(の)べた。
The athletes declared their pledge to compete fairly and squarely.

USAGE:
誓(ちか)い is the noun form of 誓(ちか)う (to swear, to vow). It carries a solemn, weighty tone — stronger than 約束(やくそく) (promise). Common contexts include wedding vows, oaths of office, and personal resolutions. The phrase 誓(ちか)いの言葉(ことば) (words of pledge) is used at ceremonies. It is a homophone of 近(ちか)い (near), which has a completely different meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誓(ちか)いを立(た)てる: to make a vow
  • 誓(ちか)いの言葉(ことば): pledge, oath (at ceremonies)
  • 愛(あい)の誓(ちか)い: vow of love
  • 誓(ちか)いを守(まも)る: to keep one's vow
