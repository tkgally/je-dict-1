達人(たつじん)

たつじん
noun
master; expert; virtuoso
1. master; expert; virtuoso
A person who has reached an exceptionally high level of skill or mastery in a particular field or art. Implies years of dedicated practice and deep expertise.
彼(かれ)は料理(りょうり)の達人(たつじん)だ。
He is a master of cooking.
節約(せつやく)の達人(たつじん)として本(ほん)を出(だ)した。
She published a book as a savings expert.
長年(ながねん)の修行(しゅぎょう)を経(へ)て、剣(けん)の達人(たつじん)と呼(よ)ばれるようになった。
After years of training, he came to be called a master swordsman.

USAGE NOTES:
達人(たつじん) implies a level of mastery beyond ordinary expertise. It can be used seriously (martial arts, traditional crafts) or more casually (cooking, saving money) in modern Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 達人(たつじん)の技(わざ) (a master's technique)
  • 達人(たつじん)に学(まな)ぶ (to learn from a master)
  • 〜の達人(たつじん) (a master of...)

The word appears in popular culture through the rhythm game 太鼓(たいこ)の達人(たつじん) (Taiko no Tatsujin, "Taiko Master").

SIMILAR WORDS:
名人(めいじん) (master, champion) is similar but is also used as a formal title, especially in traditional games like shogi and go. 達人(たつじん) emphasizes the person's skill level rather than a title.
