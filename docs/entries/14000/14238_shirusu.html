記(しる)す

しるす
verb-godan
to write down, to record, to note
1. to write down, to record, to note, to document
To put something into writing for the purpose of recording or preserving information. More formal and literary than 書く (to write), with a nuance of documenting or making a record.
日記(にっき)に思(おも)い出(で)を記(しる)した。
I recorded my memories in a diary.
歴史書(れきししょ)にはこう記(しる)されている。
It is recorded as follows in the history book.
名前(なまえ)と住所(じゅうしょ)を用紙(ようし)に記(しる)してください。
Please write your name and address on the form.

USAGE:
記(しる)す is a godan verb that is more formal and literary than 書(か)く (to write). It implies writing with the intent to document, preserve, or communicate important information. The passive form 記(しる)されている (is recorded/written) is very common, especially when citing historical or official documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日記(にっき)に記(しる)す: to record in a diary
  • 歴史(れきし)に記(しる)される: to be recorded in history
  • 名前(なまえ)を記(しる)す: to write one's name
  • 〜と記(しる)されている: it is written/recorded as ~
  • 心(こころ)に記(しる)す: to engrave in one's heart (to remember deeply)

SIMILAR WORDS:
書(か)く (to write) is the everyday, general word. 記(しる)す is more formal and emphasizes the act of recording or documenting. 記録(きろく)する (to record) is similar but focuses more on systematic data recording. 記(しる)す has a more literary, personal quality.
