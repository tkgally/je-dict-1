豪快(ごうかい)

ごうかい
adjective-na
bold, hearty, dynamic
1. bold, hearty, dynamic, uninhibited
Describes actions, people, or things that are impressively bold, vigorous, and unconstrained. Conveys admiration for large-scale, unrestrained behavior or presence.
豪快(ごうかい)に笑(わら)う人(ひと)だ。
He's the kind of person who laughs heartily.
魚(さかな)を丸(まる)ごと焼(や)く豪快(ごうかい)な料理(りょうり)だ。
It's a bold dish where the fish is grilled whole.
彼(かれ)は大金(たいきん)を豪快(ごうかい)に使(つか)う性格(せいかく)だ。
He has the kind of personality that spends large sums of money freely.

USAGE:
豪快(ごうかい) is a な-adjective often used adverbially with に. It is almost always positive, expressing admiration for boldness and vigor. It is commonly used to describe people with big personalities, dramatic cooking styles, or impressive physical feats.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 豪快(ごうかい)に笑(わら)う: to laugh heartily
  • 豪快(ごうかい)な食(た)べっぷり: hearty/impressive eating
  • 豪快(ごうかい)な料理(りょうり): bold/impressive cooking
  • 豪快(ごうかい)な性格(せいかく): bold personality
  • 豪快(ごうかい)に飲(の)む: to drink heartily

SIMILAR WORDS:
大胆(だいたん) (bold, daring) emphasizes courage in the face of risk. 豪快(ごうかい) emphasizes scale, vigor, and an impressive lack of inhibition. 豪快(ごうかい) is almost always positive, while 大胆(だいたん) can sometimes imply recklessness.
