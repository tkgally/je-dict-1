転載(てんさい)

てんさい
noun, verb (suru)
reposting; reprinting
1. reposting; reprinting; reproduction
Reproducing or reposting content (articles, images, text) that was originally published elsewhere. Often used regarding copyright and permission issues online.
無断(むだん)転載(てんさい)禁止(きんし)。
Unauthorized reproduction prohibited.
この記事(きじ)を転載(てんさい)してもいいですか。
May I repost this article?
許可(きょか)なく他人(たにん)の写真(しゃしん)を転載(てんさい)すると、著作権(ちょさくけん)の問題(もんだい)になる。
Reposting someone else's photos without permission can lead to copyright issues.

USAGE NOTES:
転載(てんさい) refers specifically to reproducing or reposting content from one medium or platform to another. It is commonly seen in copyright notices on websites and publications.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • を転載(てんさい)する (to repost/reprint something)
  • からの転載(てんさい) (reprint from...)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無断(むだん)転載(てんさい) (unauthorized reproduction)
  • 転載(てんさい)禁止(きんし) (reproduction prohibited)
  • 記事(きじ)を転載(てんさい)する (repost an article)

SIMILAR WORDS:
転載(てんさい) focuses on reproducing content across platforms. Compare with 引用(いんよう) (quotation), which refers to citing a portion of someone's work with attribution.
