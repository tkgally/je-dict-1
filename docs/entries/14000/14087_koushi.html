行使(こうし)

こうし
noun, verb-suru
exercise (of a right or power); use
1. exercise, use, exertion (of a right, power, or authority)
The act of putting a right, power, or authority into actual use or practice. A formal term used in legal, political, and institutional contexts. Implies the deliberate activation of something one is entitled to or capable of.
拒否権(きょひけん)を行使(こうし)する。
To exercise the right of veto.
警察(けいさつ)は必要(ひつよう)に応(おう)じて実力(じつりょく)を行使(こうし)することができる。
The police can use force as necessary.
武力(ぶりょく)の行使(こうし)は最後(さいご)の手段(しゅだん)であり、あらゆる外交(がいこう)努力(どりょく)を尽(つ)くした上(うえ)でなければならない。
The use of military force must be a last resort, employed only after all diplomatic efforts have been exhausted.

USAGE:
A formal term most commonly seen in legal, political, and diplomatic discourse. The object of 行使(こうし) is typically a right (権利(けんり)), power (権力(けんりょく)), or force (武力(ぶりょく)/実力(じつりょく)). Not used for casual, everyday actions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 権利(けんり)を行使(こうし)する: to exercise a right
  • 拒否権(きょひけん)を行使(こうし)する: to exercise veto power
  • 武力(ぶりょく)行使(こうし): use of military force
  • 実力(じつりょく)行使(こうし): use of force, direct action
  • 投票権(とうひょうけん)の行使(こうし): exercising one's right to vote
