進出(しんしゅつ)

しんしゅつ
noun, verb (suru)
advance; expansion; entry (into a market)
1. advance; expansion; entry into (a market, field, or area)
Moving forward into a new area, whether in business (entering a new market), sports (advancing to the next round), or other competitive contexts.
海外(かいがい)に進出(しんしゅつ)する企業(きぎょう)が増(ふ)えている。
More companies are expanding overseas.
チームは準決勝(じゅんけっしょう)に進出(しんしゅつ)した。
The team advanced to the semifinals.
日本(にほん)の自動車(じどうしゃ)メーカーはアジア市場(しじょう)への進出(しんしゅつ)を加速(かそく)させている。
Japanese automakers are accelerating their expansion into Asian markets.

USAGE NOTES:
進出(しんしゅつ) implies actively moving into a new sphere of activity. It is most commonly used in business (entering markets) and sports (advancing in tournaments).

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • に進出(しんしゅつ)する (to advance/expand into...)
  • への進出(しんしゅつ) (expansion into...)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 海外(かいがい)進出(しんしゅつ) (overseas expansion)
  • 市場(しじょう)進出(しんしゅつ) (market entry)
  • 決勝(けっしょう)進出(しんしゅつ) (advancing to the finals)
  • 進出(しんしゅつ)を果(は)たす (to achieve an advance/entry)

SIMILAR WORDS:
参入(さんにゅう) (market entry) focuses specifically on joining a market or industry. 進出(しんしゅつ) is broader and can cover geographic expansion, sports advancement, or entering new fields.
