解明(かいめい)

かいめい
noun, verb-suru
elucidation, clarification
1. elucidation, clarification, unraveling
The process of investigating and explaining something that was previously unclear or unknown. Often used in scientific, academic, and investigative contexts for uncovering causes, mechanisms, or truths.
原因(げんいん)の解明(かいめい)を急(いそ)ぐ。
To urgently work on clarifying the cause.
事件(じけん)の全容(ぜんよう)がようやく解明(かいめい)された。
The full picture of the case was finally elucidated.
この病気(びょうき)のメカニズムの解明(かいめい)には長年(ながねん)の研究(けんきゅう)が必要(ひつよう)だった。
Elucidating the mechanism of this disease required years of research.

USAGE:
解明(かいめい) implies a thorough, often systematic process of uncovering truth or causes. It is stronger than 説明(せつめい) (explanation), which simply conveys information, and more investigative than 解説(かいせつ) (commentary). 解明(かいめい) suggests that something was previously a mystery or unknown and has been figured out through effort.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原因(げんいん)を解明(かいめい)する: to elucidate the cause
  • 真相(しんそう)の解明(かいめい): uncovering the truth
  • 解明(かいめい)が進(すす)む: elucidation progresses
  • 科学的(かがくてき)解明(かいめい): scientific elucidation
