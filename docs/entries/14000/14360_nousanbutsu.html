農産物(のうさんぶつ)

のうさんぶつ
noun
agricultural products; farm produce
1. agricultural products; farm produce
Products grown or harvested through farming, including crops, vegetables, and fruits. Used in contexts ranging from everyday shopping to trade and economics.
地元(じもと)の農産物(のうさんぶつ)を買(か)う。
To buy local farm produce.
この地域(ちいき)は農産物(のうさんぶつ)の生産(せいさん)が盛(さか)んだ。
This region has thriving agricultural production.
輸入(ゆにゅう)農産物(のうさんぶつ)の増加(ぞうか)が国内(こくない)の農家(のうか)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えている。
The increase in imported agricultural products is affecting domestic farmers.

USAGE NOTES:
農産物(のうさんぶつ) is a formal compound noun used in news, trade discussions, and policy contexts. In everyday conversation, 野菜(やさい) (vegetables) or 果物(くだもの) (fruit) is more common for specific items.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国産(こくさん)農産物(のうさんぶつ) (domestic agricultural products)
  • 輸入(ゆにゅう)農産物(のうさんぶつ) (imported agricultural products)
  • 農産物(のうさんぶつ)直売所(ちょくばいじょ) (farm produce direct sales stand)
  • 農産物(のうさんぶつ)の輸出(ゆしゅつ) (export of agricultural products)
