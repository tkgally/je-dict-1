酢飯(すめし)

すめし
noun
vinegared sushi rice
1. vinegared sushi rice
Rice seasoned with a mixture of vinegar, sugar, and salt, used as the base for sushi. Also called シャリ in sushi restaurant terminology.
酢飯(すめし)を作(つく)る。
To make sushi rice.
炊(た)きたてのご飯(はん)に酢(す)を混(ま)ぜて酢飯(すめし)にする。
Mix vinegar into freshly cooked rice to make sushi rice.
おいしい寿司(すし)を作(つく)るには、酢飯(すめし)の味(あじ)つけが大切(たいせつ)だ。
To make delicious sushi, the seasoning of the vinegared rice is important.

USAGE:
酢飯(すめし) is the standard term used in cooking. At sushi restaurants, the same rice is called シャリ, which is the professional/insider term.

PREPARATION:
酢飯(すめし) is made by mixing 合(あ)わせ酢(ず) (seasoned vinegar — a blend of 酢(す), 砂糖(さとう), and 塩(しお)) into hot cooked rice while fanning it to cool.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 酢飯(すめし)を作(つく)る (to make sushi rice)
  • 酢飯(すめし)の味(あじ)つけ (seasoning of sushi rice)
  • 酢飯(すめし)を握(にぎ)る (to shape sushi rice by hand)
