落(お)ち葉(ば)

おちば
noun
fallen leaves
1. fallen leaves, dead leaves
Leaves that have fallen from trees, especially in autumn. A word closely associated with the autumn season in Japanese culture and literature.
落(お)ち葉(ば)を掃(は)く。
Sweep up the fallen leaves.
公園(こうえん)の道(みち)が落(お)ち葉(ば)で埋(う)め尽(つ)くされている。
The park path is completely covered with fallen leaves.
子(こ)どもたちが色(いろ)とりどりの落(お)ち葉(ば)を拾(ひろ)い集(あつ)めて、押(お)し葉(ば)の工作(こうさく)を楽(たの)しんでいた。
The children were gathering colorful fallen leaves and enjoying making pressed-leaf crafts.

USAGE NOTE:
落(お)ち葉(ば) is a seasonal word (季語(きご)) associated with autumn. It evokes the beauty and melancholy of the autumn season and appears frequently in poetry and literature.

落(お)ち葉(ば)焚(た)き (burning fallen leaves) was once a common autumn activity in Japan, though it is now restricted in many areas due to environmental regulations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)を掃(は)く (sweep fallen leaves)
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)を拾(ひろ)う (pick up fallen leaves)
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)焚(た)き (burning fallen leaves)
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)が舞(ま)う (fallen leaves dance in the air)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 枯(か)れ葉(は): dried/withered leaf (emphasizes the dry, dead state)
  • 紅葉(こうよう): autumn foliage (leaves still on the tree, or the act of changing color)
  • 落葉樹(らくようじゅ): deciduous tree
