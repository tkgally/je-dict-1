読(よ)み方(かた)

よみかた
noun
way of reading; pronunciation of a character
1. way of reading; how to read
The method or manner in which something is read, including the pronunciation assigned to a kanji character or the interpretation of a text.
この漢字(かんじ)の読(よ)み方(かた)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me how to read this kanji.
名前(なまえ)の読(よ)み方(かた)が難(むずか)しくて、よく間違(まちが)えられる。
The reading of my name is difficult, so people often get it wrong.
日本語(にほんご)の読(よ)み方(かた)には音読(おんよ)みと訓読(くんよ)みがある。
Japanese characters have two types of readings: on'yomi and kun'yomi.
2. interpretation; way of understanding
A particular way of interpreting or making sense of a situation, data, or written material.
データの読(よ)み方(かた)が違(ちが)う。
The interpretation of the data is different.
同(おな)じニュースでも人(ひと)によって読(よ)み方(かた)が変(か)わる。
Even for the same news, the interpretation varies depending on the person.
試合(しあい)の流(なが)れの読(よ)み方(かた)は経験(けいけん)を積(つ)まないと身(み)につかない。
Learning to read the flow of a match only comes with accumulated experience.

読(よ)み方(かた) follows the productive ～方(かた) (way of doing) pattern attached to the verb stem of 読(よ)む.

Sense 1 is especially common in language-learning contexts when asking about kanji pronunciation. Sense 2 extends to interpreting data, situations, or texts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 漢字(かんじ)の読(よ)み方(かた) (how to read a kanji)
  • 名前(なまえ)の読(よ)み方(かた) (reading of a name)
  • 地図(ちず)の読(よ)み方(かた) (how to read a map)
  • データの読(よ)み方(かた) (how to interpret data)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発音(はつおん): pronunciation in general, not limited to reading
  • 解釈(かいしゃく): interpretation, more formal and analytical
