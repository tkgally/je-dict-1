融合(ゆうごう)

ゆうごう
noun, verb-suru
fusion, merger, blending
1. fusion, merger, blending, integration
The process of two or more things combining or melting together into one unified whole. Used for physical processes like nuclear fusion, cultural blending, and organizational mergers.
東西(とうざい)の文化(ぶんか)が融合(ゆうごう)した料理(りょうり)だ。
This is cuisine that blends Eastern and Western cultures.
核(かく)融合(ゆうごう)エネルギーの実用化(じつようか)が期待(きたい)されている。
The practical application of nuclear fusion energy is anticipated.
伝統(でんとう)技術(ぎじゅつ)と最新(さいしん)テクノロジーを融合(ゆうごう)させた新(あたら)しい製品(せいひん)が話題(わだい)になっている。
A new product that fuses traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology has become a hot topic.

USAGE:
A versatile word used across many domains. In science, 核(かく)融合(ゆうごう) (nuclear fusion) is a key term. In culture and business, it describes the blending of different traditions, styles, or organizations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文化(ぶんか)の融合(ゆうごう): cultural fusion
  • 核(かく)融合(ゆうごう): nuclear fusion
  • 融合(ゆうごう)させる: to fuse, to merge (transitive)
  • 融合(ゆうごう)する: to fuse, to merge (intransitive)
