負(お)い目(め)

おいめ
noun
sense of guilt, feeling of indebtedness
1. sense of guilt, feeling of indebtedness, feeling of owing
A persistent feeling of guilt or moral debt toward someone, often because one feels they have done something wrong or have not done enough. Implies an emotional burden that weighs on the conscience.
彼(かれ)に負(お)い目(め)を感(かん)じている。
I feel a sense of guilt toward him.
負(お)い目(め)があるから、彼女(かのじょ)に強(つよ)く言(い)えない。
I can't be firm with her because I feel indebted.
助(たす)けてもらったことへの負(お)い目(め)が、ずっと心(こころ)に残(のこ)っている。
The feeling of indebtedness for being helped has stayed in my heart.

USAGE:
負(お)い目(め) is most commonly used with を感(かん)じる (to feel) or がある (to have). It describes an internal emotional state — not a financial debt, but a moral or emotional one. People with 負(お)い目(め) often feel they cannot assert themselves or refuse requests from the person they feel indebted to.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 負(お)い目(め)を感(かん)じる: to feel a sense of guilt
  • 負(お)い目(め)がある: to have a sense of indebtedness
  • 負(お)い目(め)を持(も)つ: to carry a sense of guilt
  • 負(お)い目(め)なく: without guilt

SIMILAR WORDS:
罪悪感(ざいあくかん) (guilt, guilty conscience) is stronger and implies wrongdoing. 負(お)い目(め) is softer — it can arise simply from receiving help or having an advantage, not necessarily from doing something wrong.
