良書(りょうしょ)

りょうしょ
noun
good book; quality reading
1. good book; worthwhile book; quality reading
A book considered to be of high quality or educational value. Often used in discussions about reading habits, recommendations, or education.
これは良書(りょうしょ)だから読(よ)んでみて。
This is a good book, so give it a read.
子(こ)どもには良書(りょうしょ)を与(あた)えたい。
I want to give children good books to read.
図書館(としょかん)の司書(ししょ)が選(えら)んだ良書(りょうしょ)のリストが公開(こうかい)された。
A list of quality books selected by the librarian was published.

A somewhat formal word used to describe a book of recognized quality or educational merit. Often appears in contexts related to reading recommendations, education, and children's literature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 良書(りょうしょ)を薦(すす)める (to recommend a good book)
  • 良書(りょうしょ)に親(した)しむ (to become familiar with good books)
  • 良書(りょうしょ)を選(えら)ぶ (to choose quality books)

The opposite is 悪書(あくしょ) (harmful or low-quality book). Both terms carry a value judgment and are commonly used in educational and literary contexts.
