輪郭(りんかく)

りんかく
noun
outline; contour; silhouette
1. outline; contour; silhouette
The outer edge or boundary line of a shape or figure. Used for physical outlines of objects, faces, and landscapes.
山(やま)の輪郭(りんかく)がはっきり見(み)える。
The outline of the mountain is clearly visible.
暗(くら)がりの中(なか)で人(ひと)の輪郭(りんかく)がぼんやりと浮(う)かんだ。
A person's silhouette faintly emerged in the darkness.
アイラインで目(め)の輪郭(りんかく)を強調(きょうちょう)する。
Emphasize the contour of the eyes with eyeliner.
2. outline; general framework
The broad structure or main points of a plan, idea, or concept, without going into fine detail.
計画(けいかく)の輪郭(りんかく)が見(み)えてきた。
The outline of the plan is becoming clear.
まずは全体(ぜんたい)の輪郭(りんかく)をつかんでから、細部(さいぶ)に入(はい)ろう。
Let's grasp the overall outline first, then get into the details.
議論(ぎろん)を重(かさ)ねるうちに、新(あたら)しい制度(せいど)の輪郭(りんかく)がだんだん明(あき)らかになってきた。
Through repeated discussions, the outline of the new system gradually became clear.

Compound of 輪(りん) (ring, circle) + 郭(かく) (enclosure, outline). Both senses share the idea of an outer boundary that defines a shape.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 輪郭(りんかく)がはっきりする (the outline becomes clear)
  • 輪郭(りんかく)がぼやける (the outline blurs)
  • 輪郭(りんかく)を描(えが)く (to draw an outline)
  • 輪郭(りんかく)をつかむ (to grasp the outline)
  • 顔(かお)の輪郭(りんかく) (contour of the face)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 概要(がいよう): summary, overview (focuses on content, not shape)
  • 外形(がいけい): external shape, form (more technical)
  • シルエット: silhouette (loanword, specifically a dark shape against light)
