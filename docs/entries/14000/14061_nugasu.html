脱(ぬ)がす

ぬがす
verb-godan
to undress someone; to take off (someone's clothes)
1. to undress someone, to take off someone's clothes
To remove clothing from another person. The transitive causative counterpart of 脱ぐ (to take off one's own clothes). Used when a parent undresses a child, a caregiver helps a patient, etc.
子供(こども)の靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)がす。
I take off the child's shoes.
赤(あか)ちゃんの服(ふく)を脱(ぬ)がしてお風呂(ふろ)に入(い)れた。
I undressed the baby and put her in the bath.
酔(よ)っぱらった友達(ともだち)の上着(うわぎ)を脱(ぬ)がして、ソファに寝(ね)かせた。
I took off my drunk friend's jacket and laid him down on the sofa.

CONJUGATION:
Godan verb (五段(ごだん)). 脱(ぬ)がす → 脱(ぬ)がさない → 脱(ぬ)がして → 脱(ぬ)がした

USAGE:
The transitive counterpart of 脱(ぬ)ぐ (to take off one's own clothes). While 脱(ぬ)ぐ is used for removing your own clothing, 脱(ぬ)がす is used for removing someone else's clothing. Commonly used in caregiving contexts such as parents undressing children or nurses helping patients.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 服(ふく)を脱(ぬ)がす: to take off someone's clothes
  • 靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)がす: to take off someone's shoes
  • 上着(うわぎ)を脱(ぬ)がす: to take off someone's jacket
