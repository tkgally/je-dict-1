診療(しんりょう)

しんりょう
noun, suru-verb
medical examination and treatment
1. medical examination and treatment, clinical practice
The combined act of examining a patient and providing treatment. Covers the whole process of a doctor seeing a patient, from diagnosis through treatment. Used broadly for medical consultations and clinical work.
午後(ごご)の診療(しんりょう)は三時(さんじ)からです。
Afternoon consultations start from three o'clock.
この病院(びょういん)は土曜日(どようび)も診療(しんりょう)している。
This hospital also sees patients on Saturdays.
診療(しんりょう)時間(じかん)が終(お)わっていたので、救急(きゅうきゅう)外来(がいらい)に行(い)った。
Since consultation hours were over, I went to the emergency outpatient department.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 診療(しんりょう)時間(じかん): consultation hours, office hours
  • 診療(しんりょう)科(か): medical department/specialty
  • 診療(しんりょう)所(じょ)/診療(しんりょう)所(しょ): clinic, medical office
  • 外来(がいらい)診療(しんりょう): outpatient care
  • 診療(しんりょう)報酬(ほうしゅう): medical fees (insurance-based reimbursement)

USAGE:
診療(しんりょう) combines 診(しん) (examine) and 療(りょう) (treat), covering the full scope of a medical visit. Signs reading 診療(しんりょう)中(ちゅう) (currently seeing patients) and 診療(しんりょう)時間(じかん) (consultation hours) are seen at every clinic in Japan. The term is neutral and formal, appropriate for signs, forms, and general discussion of medical services.
