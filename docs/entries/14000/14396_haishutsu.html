輩出(はいしゅつ)

はいしゅつ
noun, verb (suru)
producing (talented people) in succession
1. producing in succession; turning out (talented people)
The continuous production of notable or talented individuals from a school, region, or organization. Always refers to people, not things.
この大学(だいがく)は多(おお)くの学者(がくしゃ)を輩出(はいしゅつ)した。
This university has produced many scholars.
あの地方(ちほう)は政治家(せいじか)を多数(たすう)輩出(はいしゅつ)してきた。
That region has produced many politicians over the years.
この高校(こうこう)はオリンピック選手(せんしゅ)を輩出(はいしゅつ)することで全国(ぜんこく)に知(し)られている。
This high school is known throughout the country for producing Olympic athletes.

輩出(はいしゅつ) is a noun that can function as a suru verb (輩出(はいしゅつ)する). It is exclusively used for producing people of talent or distinction, not for objects or products.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [person type]を輩出(はいしゅつ)する (produce/turn out people of a type)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人材(じんざい)を輩出(はいしゅつ)する (produce talented people)
  • 学者(がくしゃ)を輩出(はいしゅつ)する (produce scholars)
  • 選手(せんしゅ)を輩出(はいしゅつ)する (produce athletes)

The subject is always an institution, organization, region, or era — something that serves as a source or training ground for people. The word carries a positive connotation, implying pride in the succession of talented individuals.
