詠(よ)む

よむ
verb (godan)
to compose (a poem), to recite
1. to compose a poem, to write verse
To create a poem, especially traditional Japanese poetry such as waka, tanka, or haiku. This is the primary meaning and is distinct from 読む (to read).
俳句(はいく)を詠(よ)む。
To compose a haiku.
彼女(かのじょ)は季節(きせつ)の移(うつ)り変(か)わりを詠(よ)んだ。
She composed a poem about the changing of the seasons.
万葉集(まんようしゅう)には恋(こい)を詠(よ)んだ歌(うた)が数多(かずおお)く収(おさ)められている。
The Man'yoshu contains a great many poems composed about love.
2. to recite, to chant (a poem)
To recite or chant a poem aloud, especially in a formal or ceremonial context.
声(こえ)に出(だ)して歌(うた)を詠(よ)む。
To recite a poem aloud.
歌会(うたかい)で和歌(わか)を詠(よ)む。
To recite waka at a poetry gathering.
新年(しんねん)の歌会始(うたかいはじめ)で天皇(てんのう)が詠(よ)まれた歌(うた)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
The poem composed by the Emperor at the New Year's poetry ceremony was announced.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を詠(よ)む: to compose/recite (a poem)
  • ～を詠(よ)んだ歌(うた): a poem composed about ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 俳句(はいく)を詠(よ)む: to compose a haiku
  • 和歌(わか)を詠(よ)む: to compose a waka
  • 短歌(たんか)を詠(よ)む: to compose a tanka
  • 歌(うた)を詠(よ)む: to compose a poem

SIMILAR WORDS:
Do not confuse with 読(よ)む (to read). 詠(よ)む specifically means composing or reciting poetry, while 読(よ)む is the general verb for reading. The kanji 詠(えい) contains the 言(ごんべん) radical (speech) plus 永(えい) (eternal), suggesting words that endure — fitting for poetry.
