要件(ようけん)

ようけん
noun
requirement, condition, matter of business
1. requirement, necessary condition, prerequisite
A condition that must be met or a qualification that is necessary. Commonly used in legal, business, and technical contexts.
応募(おうぼ)要件(ようけん)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the application requirements.
法律(ほうりつ)の要件(ようけん)を満(み)たしていない。
It does not meet the legal requirements.
システムの要件(ようけん)定義(ていぎ)を行(おこな)う。
We will carry out the system requirements definition.
2. matter of business, purpose of a visit or call
The reason or business purpose for contacting or visiting someone. A polite way to ask what someone needs.
ご要件(ようけん)は何(なん)でしょうか。
What is your business? / How can I help you?
要件(ようけん)だけ伝(つた)えて電話(でんわ)を切(き)った。
I conveyed just the business matter and hung up.
急(いそ)ぎの要件(ようけん)があるので、手短(てみじか)にお願(ねが)いします。
I have an urgent matter, so please keep it brief.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (要件(ようけん) as requirement) is common in formal, legal, and technical writing. In IT, 要件(ようけん)定義(ていぎ) (requirements definition) is a standard project phase. Sense 2 (要件(ようけん) as business matter) is polite and often heard in business phone calls and office settings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 要件(ようけん)を満(み)たす: to meet requirements
  • 要件(ようけん)定義(ていぎ): requirements definition
  • 応募(おうぼ)要件(ようけん): application requirements
  • ご要件(ようけん)は: what is your business (polite)
  • 必須(ひっす)要件(ようけん): mandatory requirement

SIMILAR WORDS:
用件(ようけん) (business, errand) is a homophone written with different kanji. 用件(ようけん) is exclusively sense 2 — the purpose of a call or visit. 条件(じょうけん) (condition, terms) is broader and less formal than 要件(ようけん) in the "requirement" sense.
