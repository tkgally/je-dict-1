辻褄(つじつま)

つじつま
noun
consistency; coherence; logic
1. consistency; coherence; logical connection
Whether the parts of a story, argument, or explanation fit together logically. Almost always used in the set phrase 辻褄(つじつま)が合(あ)う (to be consistent) or 辻褄(つじつま)を合(あ)わせる (to make things consistent).
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)は辻褄(つじつま)が合(あ)わない。
His story doesn't add up.
無理(むり)に辻褄(つじつま)を合(あ)わせようとしても、すぐにばれる。
Even if you try to force the story to be consistent, people will see through it right away.
証言(しょうげん)の辻褄(つじつま)が合(あ)っているかどうか、もう一度(いちど)確認(かくにん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to check once more whether the testimony is consistent.

Rarely used alone — almost always appears in set phrases. The word comes from sewing terminology: 辻(つじ) (crossroads/seam intersection) + 褄(つま) (hem), referring to whether seams line up properly at their meeting points.

KEY PATTERNS:

  • 辻褄(つじつま)が合(あ)う (to be consistent, to add up)
  • 辻褄(つじつま)が合(あ)わない (to be inconsistent, not add up)
  • 辻褄(つじつま)を合(あ)わせる (to make things consistent, to reconcile)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 話(はなし)の辻褄(つじつま) (consistency of a story)
  • 辻褄(つじつま)の合(あ)わない説明(せつめい) (an inconsistent explanation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 整合性(せいごうせい): consistency, coherence (formal, technical)
  • 筋(すじ)が通(とお)る: to make sense, to be logical (focuses on overall logic rather than specific matching)
