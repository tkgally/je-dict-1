行(い)き交(か)う

いきかう
verb, godan
to come and go; to pass each other
1. to come and go; to pass back and forth
To move back and forth in different directions, describing multiple people, vehicles, or things passing by one another. Evokes a scene of lively movement and traffic.
通(とお)りを人(ひと)が行(い)き交(か)う。
People come and go along the street.
港(みなと)には大小(だいしょう)さまざまな船(ふね)が行(い)き交(か)っていた。
Ships of all sizes were coming and going in the harbor.
年末(ねんまつ)の商店街(しょうてんがい)は、買(か)い物客(ものきゃく)が忙(せわ)しなく行(い)き交(か)っていた。
The shopping district at year's end was bustling with shoppers hurrying back and forth.

行(い)き交(か)う is an intransitive godan verb. A compound of 行(い)く (to go) and 交(か)う (to cross, to intersect).

ASPECT (ている):
行(い)き交(か)っている describes an ongoing scene of people or things moving back and forth.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [person/vehicle]が行(い)き交(か)う (people/vehicles pass back and forth)
  • [place]を行(い)き交(か)う (come and go through a place)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)が行(い)き交(か)う (people come and go)
  • 車(くるま)が行(い)き交(か)う (cars pass back and forth)
  • 情報(じょうほう)が行(い)き交(か)う (information is exchanged back and forth)

Often used to paint a vivid picture of a busy scene. Can also describe the exchange of messages, emails, or information flying back and forth between parties.
