説得(せっとく)

せっとく
noun, verb-suru
persuasion
1. persuasion, convincing
The act of using reasoning, arguments, or appeals to convince someone to do or believe something. Implies a rational, sustained effort rather than a single request.
何度(なんど)も説得(せっとく)してやっと同意(どうい)してもらった。
After persuading them many times, I finally got their agreement.
彼(かれ)の説得(せっとく)には説得力(せっとくりょく)がある。
His persuasion is convincing.
両親(りょうしん)を説得(せっとく)して留学(りゅうがく)の許可(きょか)をもらった。
I persuaded my parents and got their permission to study abroad.

USAGE:
説得(せっとく) is used as a noun or with する as a verb. The person being persuaded is marked with を (親(おや)を説得(せっとく)する). The very common compound 説得力(せっとくりょく) (persuasive power) describes arguments or presentations that are convincing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 説得(せっとく)する: to persuade
  • 説得(せっとく)される: to be persuaded
  • 説得力(せっとくりょく): persuasive power, convincingness
  • 説得力(せっとくりょく)がある: to be persuasive/convincing
  • 説得(せっとく)に応(おう)じる: to accept persuasion
  • 粘(ねば)り強(づよ)く説得(せっとく)する: to persistently persuade

SIMILAR WORDS:
説得(せっとく) implies a sustained, rational effort to change someone's mind. 勧(すす)める (to recommend) is lighter and less forceful. 口説(くど)く (to talk someone into something) can be more persistent or nagging, and also means to hit on someone romantically.
