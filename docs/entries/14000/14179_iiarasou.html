言(い)い争(あらそ)う

いいあらそう
verb (godan)
to argue, to quarrel
1. to argue, to quarrel verbally
To exchange heated words with someone over a disagreement. Implies a mutual verbal conflict where both sides are actively disputing. Stronger than a simple discussion but does not imply physical confrontation.
兄弟(きょうだい)で言(い)い争(あらそ)った。
The siblings argued with each other.
些細(ささい)なことで言(い)い争(あらそ)うのはやめよう。
Let's stop arguing over trivial things.
隣人(りんじん)と駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)の使(つか)い方(かた)について言(い)い争(あらそ)いになった。
I got into an argument with my neighbor about how the parking lot is used.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AとBが言(い)い争(あらそ)う: A and B argue (with each other)
  • ～について言(い)い争(あらそ)う: to argue about ~
  • ～のことで言(い)い争(あらそ)う: to argue over ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 激(はげ)しく言(い)い争(あらそ)う: to argue fiercely
  • 言(い)い争(あらそ)いになる: to turn into an argument (noun form)
  • 言(い)い争(あらそ)いが絶(た)えない: arguments never cease

USAGE:
The compound verb 言(い)い争(あらそ)う emphasizes verbal conflict — both parties are talking back at each other. The noun form 言(い)い争(あらそ)い is also very common. Compare with 喧嘩(けんか)する, which can include physical fighting, while 言(い)い争(あらそ)う is strictly verbal.
