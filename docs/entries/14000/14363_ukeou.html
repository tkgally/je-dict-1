請(う)け負(お)う

うけおう
verb, godan
to contract; to undertake
1. to contract; to undertake; to take on (a job)
To formally agree to take responsibility for a task or project, especially construction, manufacturing, or other professional work, usually under a contract.
その工事(こうじ)を請(う)け負(お)った。
We contracted to do the construction work.
大手(おおて)の建設(けんせつ)会社(がいしゃ)が駅(えき)の改修(かいしゅう)を請(う)け負(お)った。
A major construction company undertook the renovation of the station.
個人(こじん)で翻訳(ほんやく)の仕事(しごと)を請(う)け負(お)って生計(せいけい)を立(た)てている。
I make a living by taking on translation work as a freelancer.

請(う)け負(お)う is a transitive godan verb (conjugation: 請(う)け負(お)わない, 請(う)け負(お)って, etc.).

ASPECT (ている):
請(う)け負(お)っている indicates that someone currently has a contract or is in the process of carrying out contracted work.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • を請(う)け負(お)う (to contract for something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 工事(こうじ)を請(う)け負(お)う (to contract for construction)
  • 仕事(しごと)を請(う)け負(お)う (to take on a job)
  • 業務(ぎょうむ)を請(う)け負(お)う (to undertake operations)

RELATED WORDS:
The noun form is 請負(うけおい) (contract, contracting). A 請負(うけおい)業者(ぎょうしゃ) is a contractor.
