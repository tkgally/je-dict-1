鉢合(はちあ)わせ

はちあわせ
noun
running into someone; unexpected encounter
1. running into someone; bumping into; unexpected encounter
An unplanned meeting with someone, often in an awkward or unwelcome situation. Literally refers to two heads bumping together.
駅(えき)で元(もと)カノと鉢合(はちあ)わせした。
I ran into my ex-girlfriend at the station.
廊下(ろうか)で上司(じょうし)と鉢合(はちあ)わせして気(き)まずかった。
I bumped into my boss in the hallway and it was awkward.
泥棒(どろぼう)は逃(に)げようとしたところ、帰宅(きたく)した住人(じゅうにん)と鉢合(はちあ)わせしてしまった。
The burglar tried to escape but ran right into the returning resident.

An unexpected, often unwelcome encounter. The word comes from the image of two people bumping heads (鉢(はち) refers to the top of the head).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鉢合(はちあ)わせする (to run into someone)
  • 鉢合(はちあ)わせになる (to end up running into each other)
  • 偶然(ぐうぜん)鉢合(はちあ)わせする (to accidentally run into)

Often implies the encounter is inconvenient, embarrassing, or unwanted. Used more dramatically than 出(で)くわす (to come across) or 偶然(ぐうぜん)会(あ)う (to meet by chance).
