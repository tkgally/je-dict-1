重(おも)んじる

おもんじる
verb-ichidan
to value; to respect; to attach importance to
1. to value; to respect; to attach importance to
To regard something as important or worthy of respect. Used for abstract concepts like traditions, principles, and values rather than physical objects.
伝統(でんとう)を重(おも)んじる。
To value tradition.
この会社(かいしゃ)は社員(しゃいん)の意見(いけん)を重(おも)んじている。
This company values the opinions of its employees.
個人(こじん)の自由(じゆう)を重(おも)んじる社会(しゃかい)を目指(めざ)すべきだ。
We should aim for a society that respects individual freedom.

An ichidan verb derived from 重(おも)い (heavy, important). An older variant 重(おも)んずる also exists and conjugates as a mixed verb (ずる-verb), but 重(おも)んじる is the standard modern form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伝統(でんとう)を重(おも)んじる (to value tradition)
  • 礼儀(れいぎ)を重(おも)んじる (to value manners)
  • 人権(じんけん)を重(おも)んじる (to respect human rights)
  • 意見(いけん)を重(おも)んじる (to value opinions)
  • 名誉(めいよ)を重(おも)んじる (to value honor)

A formal, written-style verb. More elevated than 大切(たいせつ)にする (to treat as important) or 尊重(そんちょう)する (to respect). Typically used with abstract nouns like traditions, values, rights, and principles.
