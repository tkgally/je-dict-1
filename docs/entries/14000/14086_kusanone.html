草(くさ)の根(ね)

くさのね
noun
grassroots; the common people
1. grassroots, the common people, ordinary citizens
The ordinary people in a society or community, as opposed to leaders or elites. Used as a modifier to describe movements, activities, or efforts that originate from ordinary citizens rather than from institutions or authorities.
草(くさ)の根(ね)の活動(かつどう)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Grassroots activities are spreading.
草(くさ)の根(ね)レベルでの交流(こうりゅう)が両国(りょうこく)の関係(かんけい)を支(ささ)えている。
Grassroots-level exchanges are supporting the relationship between the two countries.
環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)は政府(せいふ)の政策(せいさく)だけでなく、草(くさ)の根(ね)の運動(うんどう)によっても大(おお)きく前進(ぜんしん)してきた。
Environmental protection has advanced greatly not only through government policy but also through grassroots movements.

USAGE:
Almost always used as a modifier (草(くさ)の根(ね)の + noun). Conveys the sense of bottom-up action by ordinary citizens. In Japanese politics and social discourse, 草(くさ)の根(ね) activities are contrasted with top-down government or corporate initiatives.

NOTE:
The concept became prominent in Japan partly through the influence of American "grassroots" political culture, but Japanese 草(くさ)の根(ね) movements have their own distinct history in community organizing and citizen activism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 草(くさ)の根(ね)運動(うんどう): grassroots movement
  • 草(くさ)の根(ね)活動(かつどう): grassroots activity
  • 草(くさ)の根(ね)交流(こうりゅう): people-to-people exchange
  • 草(くさ)の根(ね)外交(がいこう): grassroots diplomacy
