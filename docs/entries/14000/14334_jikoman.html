自己満(じこまん)

じこまん
noun
self-satisfaction (colloquial)
1. self-satisfaction; self-gratification
Doing something primarily for one's own satisfaction rather than for any practical benefit to others. Colloquial abbreviation of 自己満足.
それって自己満(じこまん)じゃない？
Isn't that just self-satisfaction?
自己満(じこまん)でもいいから続(つづ)けたい。
Even if it's just for my own satisfaction, I want to keep doing it.
人(ひと)に見(み)せるためじゃなくて、完全(かんぜん)に自己満(じこまん)で絵(え)を描(か)いている。
I'm not drawing to show anyone — it's entirely for my own satisfaction.

Abbreviated form of 自己満足(じこまんぞく). Very common in casual conversation and on social media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己満(じこまん)で終(お)わる (to end up being just self-satisfaction)
  • 自己満(じこまん)に過(す)ぎない (to be nothing more than self-satisfaction)
  • 完全(かんぜん)に自己満(じこまん) (completely self-indulgent)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used with a slightly self-deprecating or critical tone, implying that the effort or result benefits no one but the person doing it. Can also be used positively when someone acknowledges they enjoy something regardless of external validation.
