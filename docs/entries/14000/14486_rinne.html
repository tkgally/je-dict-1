輪廻(りんね)

りんね
noun
cycle of rebirth; samsara; reincarnation
1. cycle of rebirth; samsara; reincarnation
The Buddhist concept of the continuous cycle of birth, death, and rebirth that all living beings undergo until they attain enlightenment.
輪廻(りんね)から解脱(げだつ)する。
To be liberated from the cycle of rebirth.
仏教(ぶっきょう)では輪廻(りんね)の教(おし)えが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
In Buddhism, the teaching of reincarnation is important.
魂(たましい)は輪廻(りんね)を繰(く)り返(かえ)しながら、やがて悟(さと)りに至(いた)ると言(い)われている。
It is said that the soul goes through repeated cycles of rebirth and eventually reaches enlightenment.

A key concept in Buddhism and Hinduism. In Japanese Buddhism, 輪廻(りんね) describes the cycle of suffering through repeated births and deaths in the six realms (六道(ろくどう)). Attaining 悟(さと)り (enlightenment) allows one to escape this cycle.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 輪廻転生(りんねてんしょう) (transmigration of souls, metempsychosis)
  • 輪廻(りんね)から逃(のが)れる (to escape the cycle of rebirth)
  • 輪廻(りんね)を繰(く)り返(かえ)す (to go through cycles of rebirth)

Also read as りんえ in some classical Buddhist texts, though りんね is the standard modern reading. Frequently appears in literature, anime, and games as a theme.
