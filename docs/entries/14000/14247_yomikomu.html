読(よ)み込(こ)む

よみこむ
verb, godan
to load (data); to read thoroughly
1. to load; to read in (data)
To load data or a file into a computer or device for processing.
ファイルを読(よ)み込(こ)む。
To load a file.
アプリがデータを読(よ)み込(こ)んでいる。
The app is loading data.
画像(がぞう)ファイルの読(よ)み込(こ)みに時間(じかん)がかかっている。
Loading the image file is taking time.
2. to read thoroughly; to study deeply
To read something carefully and repeatedly until one has absorbed its content completely.
台本(だいほん)を読(よ)み込(こ)む。
To study a script thoroughly.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)に教科書(きょうかしょ)を何度(なんど)も読(よ)み込(こ)んだ。
I read the textbook over and over before the exam.
彼(かれ)は契約書(けいやくしょ)を隅(すみ)から隅(すみ)まで読(よ)み込(こ)んでから署名(しょめい)した。
He read the contract thoroughly from corner to corner before signing.

読(よ)み込(こ)む is a transitive godan verb that combines 読(よ)む (to read) and 込(こ)む (into, deeply). The 込(こ)む element adds the nuance of thoroughness or inward movement.

ASPECT (ている):
読(よ)み込(こ)んでいる can mean either "is currently loading" (ongoing action for sense 1) or "has studied thoroughly" (resulting state for sense 2).

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xを読(よ)み込(こ)む (load X / read X thoroughly)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • データを読(よ)み込(こ)む (load data)
  • ファイルを読(よ)み込(こ)む (load a file)
  • 台本(だいほん)を読(よ)み込(こ)む (study a script)

The noun form 読(よ)み込(こ)み is commonly used in computing to mean "loading" (e.g., 読(よ)み込(こ)み中(ちゅう) = now loading).
