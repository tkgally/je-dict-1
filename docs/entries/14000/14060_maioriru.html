舞(ま)い降(お)りる

まいおりる
verb-ichidan
to swoop down; to descend gracefully
1. to swoop down, to descend gracefully, to flutter down
To come down from above in a light, graceful manner. Used for birds landing, snow falling gently, or angels descending. Conveys elegance or lightness in the descent, unlike plain 降りる which is neutral.
鷹(たか)が舞(ま)い降(お)りてきた。
A hawk swooped down.
雪(ゆき)が静(しず)かに舞(ま)い降(お)りる夜(よる)だった。
It was a night when snow was gently falling.
白鳥(はくちょう)が湖(みずうみ)の水面(すいめん)にゆっくりと舞(ま)い降(お)りる姿(すがた)は美(うつく)しかった。
The sight of the swan gracefully descending onto the surface of the lake was beautiful.

CONJUGATION:
Ichidan verb (一段(いちだん)). 舞(ま)い降(お)りる → 舞(ま)い降(お)りない → 舞(ま)い降(お)りて → 舞(ま)い降(お)りた

USAGE:
A compound verb combining 舞(ま)う (to dance, to flutter) and 降(お)りる (to descend). The 舞(ま)う component adds a sense of lightness and grace to the descent. Often used in literary or poetic contexts. Common subjects include birds, snow, leaves, and angels.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鳥(とり)が舞(ま)い降(お)りる: a bird swoops down
  • 雪(ゆき)が舞(ま)い降(お)りる: snow flutters down
  • 天使(てんし)が舞(ま)い降(お)りる: an angel descends
