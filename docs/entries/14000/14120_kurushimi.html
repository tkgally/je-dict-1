苦(くる)しみ

くるしみ
noun
suffering, pain, anguish
1. suffering, pain, anguish
Physical or emotional distress. The noun form of the verb 苦しむ (to suffer). Covers a wide range from physical pain to deep emotional torment.
苦(くる)しみから逃(に)げたい。
I want to escape from the suffering.
彼(かれ)の苦(くる)しみは誰(だれ)にもわからない。
Nobody can understand his anguish.
長(なが)い病気(びょうき)の苦(くる)しみを乗(の)り越(こ)えて、やっと元気(げんき)になった。
After overcoming the suffering of a long illness, I finally got well.

USAGE:
苦(くる)しみ is the nominal form of 苦(くる)しむ (to suffer). It conveys deeper, more prolonged distress than 痛(いた)み (pain), which is primarily physical. 苦(くる)しみ encompasses both physical and emotional suffering.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 苦(くる)しみを味(あじ)わう: to experience suffering
  • 苦(くる)しみに耐(た)える: to endure suffering
  • 苦(くる)しみから逃(に)れる: to escape from suffering
  • 苦(くる)しみを乗(の)り越(こ)える: to overcome suffering
  • 苦(くる)しみを分(わ)かち合(あ)う: to share suffering
