道筋(みちすじ)

みちすじ
noun
route; course; path; line of reasoning
1. route; course; way
A physical path or route from one place to another.
目的地(もくてきち)までの道筋(みちすじ)を確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed the route to the destination.
パレードの道筋(みちすじ)に人(ひと)が集(あつ)まっていた。
People had gathered along the parade route.
祭(まつ)りの行列(ぎょうれつ)が通(とお)る道筋(みちすじ)には提灯(ちょうちん)が飾(かざ)られていた。
Lanterns were hung along the route of the festival procession.
2. course of action; roadmap; line of reasoning
Figuratively, the logical steps or process leading toward a goal or conclusion. Common in discussions of plans, policies, and arguments.
解決(かいけつ)への道筋(みちすじ)を示(しめ)す。
To show a path toward resolution.
話(はなし)の道筋(みちすじ)がよくわからない。
I can't follow the line of the story.
政府(せいふ)は経済(けいざい)回復(かいふく)に向(む)けた具体的(ぐたいてき)な道筋(みちすじ)をまだ示(しめ)していない。
The government has yet to present a concrete roadmap toward economic recovery.

USAGE NOTES:
The figurative use of 道筋(みちすじ) (course of action, reasoning) is more common than the literal use in modern Japanese. It often appears in news and political discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道筋(みちすじ)を示(しめ)す (to show a path/roadmap)
  • 道筋(みちすじ)を立(た)てる (to map out a course)
  • 道筋(みちすじ)が見(み)える (a path becomes visible)
  • 話(はなし)の道筋(みちすじ) (thread/line of a story)
  • 解決(かいけつ)への道筋(みちすじ) (path to resolution)
