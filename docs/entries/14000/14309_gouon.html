轟音(ごうおん)

ごうおん
noun
roar; thunderous sound; rumble
1. roar; thunderous sound; rumble
A very loud, deep, reverberating sound, such as that of engines, explosions, or thunder.
轟音(ごうおん)が響(ひび)いた。
A thunderous roar echoed.
飛行機(ひこうき)の轟音(ごうおん)で会話(かいわ)が聞(き)こえなかった。
The roar of the airplane drowned out the conversation.
爆発(ばくはつ)の轟音(ごうおん)とともに建物(たてもの)の窓(まど)ガラスが割(わ)れた。
The windowpanes of the building shattered with the thunderous sound of the explosion.

Describes an extremely loud, deep sound that can be felt physically. Stronger and more dramatic than 騒音(そうおん) (noise), which focuses on unwantedness rather than volume.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 轟音(ごうおん)が響(ひび)く (a roar reverberates)
  • 轟音(ごうおん)を立(た)てる (make a thunderous sound)
  • 轟音(ごうおん)とともに (with a roar)
  • すさまじい轟音(ごうおん) (a tremendous roar)

Often appears in literary and journalistic writing to convey dramatic impact. The kanji 轟(とどろ)く means "to roar" or "to rumble."
