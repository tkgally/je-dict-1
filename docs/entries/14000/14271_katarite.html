語(かた)り手(て)

かたりて
noun
narrator; storyteller
1. narrator; storyteller
A person who tells a story or narrates events. Used both for literary narrators (the voice telling a story in fiction) and for real people who pass on oral traditions or perform narrative arts.
この小説(しょうせつ)の語(かた)り手(て)は子供(こども)だ。
The narrator of this novel is a child.
戦争(せんそう)体験(たいけん)の語(かた)り手(て)が少(すく)なくなっている。
There are fewer and fewer people who can tell of their wartime experiences.
祖母(そぼ)は村(むら)の昔話(むかしばなし)の語(かた)り手(て)として知(し)られていた。
My grandmother was known as a storyteller of the village's old tales.

Compound of 語(かた)り (narrating) + 手(て) (person who does something). The ～手(て) suffix creates agent nouns similar to English "-er" (e.g., 聞(き)き手(て) listener, 書(か)き手(て) writer).

In literary criticism, 語(かた)り手(て) refers to the narrative voice, equivalent to English "narrator." In everyday use, it refers to someone who tells stories or passes on experiences orally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物語(ものがたり)の語(かた)り手(て) (narrator of a story)
  • 体験(たいけん)の語(かた)り手(て) (person who recounts experiences)
  • 伝統(でんとう)の語(かた)り手(て) (keeper of oral tradition)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ナレーター: narrator (for broadcasts, documentaries)
  • 話(はな)し手(て): speaker (in conversation, not necessarily storytelling)
