美肌(びはだ)

びはだ
noun
beautiful skin, clear complexion
1. beautiful skin, clear and healthy complexion
Skin that is smooth, clear, and healthy-looking. A common term in beauty and skincare contexts, referring to the ideal of having flawless, radiant skin.
美肌(びはだ)の秘訣(ひけつ)は保湿(ほしつ)だ。
The secret to beautiful skin is moisturizing.
彼女(かのじょ)は美肌(びはだ)で有名(ゆうめい)な女優(じょゆう)だ。
She's an actress famous for her beautiful skin.
美肌(びはだ)効果(こうか)があると言(い)われている食材(しょくざい)を積極的(せっきょくてき)に取(と)り入(い)れている。
I actively incorporate ingredients said to have skin-beautifying effects.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美肌(びはだ)効果(こうか): skin-beautifying effect
  • 美肌(びはだ)の秘訣(ひけつ): the secret to beautiful skin
  • 美肌(びはだ)ケア: skincare for beautiful skin
  • 美肌(びはだ)成分(せいぶん): beauty-skin ingredients

USAGE:
Extremely common in advertising, beauty magazines, and skincare discussions. Japan's beauty industry places great emphasis on skin quality, and 美肌(びはだ) appears constantly in product names, marketing copy, and lifestyle articles. The prefix 美(び) (beauty) combines with 肌(はだ) (skin) in a straightforward compound.
