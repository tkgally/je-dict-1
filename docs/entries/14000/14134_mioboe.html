見覚(みおぼ)え

みおぼえ
noun
recognition, remembrance (visual)
1. recognition, visual memory, remembrance of having seen
The feeling or memory of having seen something or someone before. Almost always used with ある or ない to express whether one recognizes something.
この顔(かお)には見覚(みおぼ)えがある。
I recognize this face.
見覚(みおぼ)えのない番号(ばんごう)から電話(でんわ)がかかってきた。
I got a call from a number I didn't recognize.
この場所(ばしょ)は見覚(みおぼ)えがあるけど、いつ来(き)たか思(おも)い出(だ)せない。
I recognize this place, but I can't remember when I came here.

USAGE:
見覚(みおぼ)え is almost always paired with がある (to have) or がない (to not have). The pattern 見覚(みおぼ)えのある／ない + noun is also very common as a modifier. Compare with 聞(き)き覚(おぼ)え (recognition of something heard).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 見覚(みおぼ)えがある: to recognize (having seen before)
  • 見覚(みおぼ)えがない: to not recognize
  • 見覚(みおぼ)えのある顔(かお): a face one recognizes
  • 見覚(みおぼ)えのない人(ひと): a person one doesn't recognize
