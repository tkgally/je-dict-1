転身(てんしん)

てんしん
noun, verb (suru)
career change; switching professions; turning around
1. career change; switching professions
Changing one's occupation or role, especially a significant shift to a different field or way of life.
会社員(かいしゃいん)から作家(さっか)に転身(てんしん)した。
He switched from being a company employee to a writer.
引退(いんたい)後(ご)、指導者(しどうしゃ)に転身(てんしん)する選手(せんしゅ)も多(おお)い。
Many athletes switch to coaching after retirement.
彼女(かのじょ)は銀行員(ぎんこういん)を辞(や)めて華麗(かれい)に転身(てんしん)し、今(いま)ではパティシエとして活躍(かつやく)している。
She left her bank job and made a brilliant career change — she is now active as a pastry chef.

転身(てんしん) is a noun that can function as a suru verb (転身(てんしん)する).

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [old role]から[new role]に転身(てんしん)する (switch from X to Y)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 華麗(かれい)な転身(てんしん) (a brilliant career change)
  • 大(おお)きな転身(てんしん) (a major career change)
  • 転身(てんしん)を図(はか)る (to aim for a career change)

Often implies a dramatic or noteworthy change rather than a routine job switch. The phrase 華麗(かれい)な転身(てんしん) (brilliant career change) is particularly common in media describing public figures who successfully reinvent themselves.
