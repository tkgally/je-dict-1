過(あやま)ち

あやまち
noun
mistake; error; wrongdoing
1. mistake; error; fault; wrongdoing
A mistake or transgression, often with moral or serious implications. Carries a heavier, more literary tone than simply 間違い and implies personal responsibility.
同(おな)じ過(あやま)ちを繰(く)り返(かえ)すな。
Don't repeat the same mistake.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)の過(あやま)ちを悔(く)やんでいる。
I regret the mistakes I made when I was young.
戦争(せんそう)という過(あやま)ちを二度(にど)と繰(く)り返(かえ)してはならないと、多(おお)くの人(ひと)が訴(うった)えている。
Many people are calling for the mistake of war to never be repeated.

USAGE NOTES:
過(あやま)ち has a more serious and literary tone than 間違(まちが)い (mistake). It implies moral weight — a failing, a transgression, or a grave error — rather than a simple factual mistake.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 過(あやま)ちを犯(おか)す (to commit a mistake/transgression)
  • 過(あやま)ちを繰(く)り返(かえ)す (to repeat a mistake)
  • 過(あやま)ちを認(みと)める (to acknowledge one's fault)
  • 若(わか)い頃(ころ)の過(あやま)ち (mistakes of youth)

SIMILAR WORDS:
間違(まちが)い is neutral and covers any kind of mistake. 過(あやま)ち implies moral responsibility and is often used in reflective, regretful, or admonitory contexts.
