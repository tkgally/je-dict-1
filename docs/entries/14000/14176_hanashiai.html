話(はな)し合(あ)い

はなしあい
noun
discussion, talk, conference
1. discussion, talk, conference, negotiation
A mutual conversation aimed at reaching understanding or agreement. Emphasizes the cooperative, two-way nature of the discussion rather than one-sided communication.
話(はな)し合(あ)いで解決(かいけつ)しよう。
Let's resolve it through discussion.
両者(りょうしゃ)の話(はな)し合(あ)いは深夜(しんや)まで続(つづ)いた。
The discussion between the two parties continued until late at night.
暴力(ぼうりょく)ではなく話(はな)し合(あ)いによって問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)するべきだ。
Problems should be resolved through discussion, not violence.

USAGE:
話(はな)し合(あ)い emphasizes mutual, cooperative dialogue. The 合(あ)い suffix indicates that the action is reciprocal — both sides are talking and listening. It is a fundamental concept in Japanese conflict resolution, where discussion and consensus-building are preferred over confrontation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 話(はな)し合(あ)いをする: to have a discussion
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)いで解決(かいけつ)する: to resolve through discussion
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)いの場(ば): a venue for discussion
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)いに応(おう)じる: to agree to negotiate
