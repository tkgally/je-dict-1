言(い)い合(あ)い

いいあい
noun
argument; quarrel
1. argument; quarrel; verbal fight
A heated verbal exchange where both parties express opposing views or complaints. Implies mutual back-and-forth rather than one-sided criticism.
兄弟(きょうだい)で言(い)い合(あ)いになった。
The siblings got into an argument.
くだらないことで言(い)い合(あ)いをするのはやめよう。
Let's stop arguing over trivial things.
会議(かいぎ)で意見(いけん)が対立(たいりつ)して、激(はげ)しい言(い)い合(あ)いに発展(はってん)してしまった。
Opinions clashed at the meeting, and it escalated into a heated argument.

言(い)い合(あ)い is the noun form derived from 言(い)い合(あ)う (to argue with each other). The 合(あ)い element indicates a mutual, reciprocal action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言(い)い合(あ)いになる (to get into an argument)
  • 言(い)い合(あ)いをする (to have an argument)
  • 激(はげ)しい言(い)い合(あ)い (a heated argument)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 言(い)い合(あ)い vs 喧嘩(けんか): 言(い)い合(あ)い is specifically a verbal disagreement, while 喧嘩(けんか) can include physical fighting as well as verbal arguments.
  • 言(い)い合(あ)い vs 口論(こうろん): 口論(こうろん) is a more formal term for a verbal dispute, while 言(い)い合(あ)い is more casual and everyday.
