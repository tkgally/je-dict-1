読(よ)み解(と)く

よみとく
verb, godan
to interpret; to decipher
1. to interpret; to decipher; to read and understand
To read something carefully and extract its deeper meaning, often applied to texts, data, or situations that require analysis.
古文(こぶん)を読(よ)み解(と)く。
To decipher classical text.
データを読(よ)み解(と)いて傾向(けいこう)を見(み)つける。
To interpret data and find trends.
歴史的(れきしてき)な文書(ぶんしょ)を正(ただ)しく読(よ)み解(と)くには、当時(とうじ)の文化(ぶんか)や社会(しゃかい)の背景(はいけい)を知(し)る必要(ひつよう)がある。
To correctly interpret historical documents, one needs to know the cultural and social background of the time.

読(よ)み解(と)く is a transitive godan verb that combines 読(よ)む (to read) and 解(と)く (to solve). It implies reading with analysis and interpretation, not just surface comprehension.

ASPECT (ている):
読(よ)み解(と)いている indicates an ongoing process of interpretation.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xを読(よ)み解(と)く (interpret/decipher X)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 古文(こぶん)を読(よ)み解(と)く (decipher classical text)
  • データを読(よ)み解(と)く (interpret data)
  • 暗号(あんごう)を読(よ)み解(と)く (decipher a code)
  • 行間(ぎょうかん)を読(よ)み解(と)く (read between the lines)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 読(よ)み解(と)く vs 読(よ)み取(と)る: Both involve reading with understanding, but 読(よ)み解(と)く emphasizes analytical deciphering, while 読(よ)み取(と)る emphasizes perceiving or grasping meaning from context.
