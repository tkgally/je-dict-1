1. to shave off; to pare away; to strip away
To remove something by shaving or cutting at an angle. Literally used for food preparation (shaving off thin slices), and figuratively for removing excess or unnecessary elements to achieve simplicity or efficiency.
ごぼうの皮(かわ)をそぎ落(お)とす。
Shave off the skin of the burdock root.
無駄(むだ)をそぎ落(お)としたデザインが美(うつく)しい。
The design, stripped of all excess, is beautiful.
脂肪(しぼう)をそぎ落(お)とすために毎日(まいにち)トレーニングをしている。
I train every day to strip away body fat.

USAGE:
The literal use involves shaving thin pieces from vegetables or other ingredients (a cutting technique called そぎ切(ぎ)り). The figurative use — removing unnecessary elements — is very common in discussions of design, writing, business processes, and fitness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無駄(むだ)をそぎ落(お)とす (to strip away excess)
  • 贅肉(ぜいにく)をそぎ落(お)とす (to trim off excess fat)
  • そぎ落(お)としたシンプルさ (pared-down simplicity)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 削(けず)り落(お)とす: to scrape off — uses more force, involves harder materials
  • 省(はぶ)く: to omit — focuses on leaving things out rather than physically removing
  • 切(き)り詰(つ)める: to cut back — often for budgets or expenses
