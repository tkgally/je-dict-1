百貨店(ひゃっかてん)

ひゃっかてん
noun
department store
1. department store
A large retail store carrying a wide variety of goods organized into departments. The formal term for what is more casually called デパート.
駅前(えきまえ)の百貨店(ひゃっかてん)で買(か)い物(もの)をした。
I shopped at the department store in front of the station.
老舗(しにせ)の百貨店(ひゃっかてん)が閉店(へいてん)することになった。
A long-established department store is going to close.
百貨店(ひゃっかてん)の地下(ちか)食品(しょくひん)売(う)り場(ば)は、いつも賑(にぎ)わっている。
The basement food floor of the department store is always bustling.

USAGE:
More formal than the commonly used loanword デパート. Often used in news, business contexts, and official names. Major Japanese department stores include 三越(みつこし), 伊勢丹(いせたん), 高島屋(たかしまや), and 大丸(だいまる). The 地下(ちか)食品(しょくひん)売(う)り場(ば) (basement food hall), nicknamed デパ地下(ちか), is a distinctive feature of Japanese department stores.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 百貨店(ひゃっかてん)業界(ぎょうかい) (department store industry)
  • 大手(おおて)百貨店(ひゃっかてん) (major department store)
  • 百貨店(ひゃっかてん)の売(う)り上(あ)げ (department store sales)
