疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い

うたがいぶかい
adjective-i
distrustful; skeptical; suspicious
1. distrustful; skeptical; suspicious; doubting
Describes a person who tends not to trust others or who is inclined to doubt what they are told. Formed from 疑い (doubt) + 深い (deep).
彼(かれ)は疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い性格(せいかく)だ。
He has a distrustful personality.
疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い目(め)でこちらを見(み)ている。
He's looking at us with suspicious eyes.
あの人(ひと)は疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)いから、簡単(かんたん)には信(しん)じてくれないだろう。
That person is so skeptical that they probably won't believe us easily.

FORMATION:
Follows the productive ～深(ぶか)い pattern meaning "deeply inclined toward ~": 用心(ようじん)深(ぶか)い (cautious), 信仰(しんこう)深(ぶか)い (devout), 嫉妬(しっと)深(ぶか)い (jealous).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い性格(せいかく) (a distrustful personality)
  • 疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い目(め) (suspicious eyes)
  • 疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い人(ひと) (a skeptical person)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 猜疑(さいぎ)心(しん)が強(つよ)い: suspicious-natured — more formal, emphasizes paranoia
  • 懐疑的(かいぎてき): skeptical — more formal/academic, used for intellectual doubt
  • 用心(ようじん)深(ぶか)い: cautious — focuses on carefulness rather than distrust
