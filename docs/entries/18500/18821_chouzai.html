1. dispensing medicine; compounding drugs; preparing a prescription
The professional act of preparing and providing medication according to a doctor's prescription. Done by pharmacists.
調剤(ちょうざい)薬局(やっきょく)で薬(くすり)を受(う)け取(と)った。
I picked up my medicine at the dispensing pharmacy.
処方箋(しょほうせん)に基(もと)づいて調剤(ちょうざい)する。
To dispense medicine based on a prescription.
調剤(ちょうざい)にかかる時間(じかん)は約(やく)十五分(じゅうごふん)です。
It takes about fifteen minutes to prepare the prescription.

調剤(ちょうざい) refers to the professional act of preparing medication according to a 処方箋(しょほうせん) (prescription).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 調剤(ちょうざい)薬局(やっきょく): dispensing pharmacy (fills prescriptions)
  • 調剤(ちょうざい)する: to dispense/compound medicine
  • 調剤(ちょうざい)報酬(ほうしゅう): dispensing fee

JAPANESE HEALTHCARE CONTEXT:
In Japan, 調剤(ちょうざい)薬局(やっきょく) (dispensing pharmacies) are distinct from ドラッグストア (drugstores). Most clinics and hospitals issue a 処方箋(しょほうせん) that patients take to a nearby 調剤(ちょうざい)薬局(やっきょく) to have their medication prepared. This separation of prescribing and dispensing (医薬分業(いやくぶんぎょう)) is standard practice.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 薬剤師(やくざいし): pharmacist (the professional who performs 調剤(ちょうざい))
  • 処方箋(しょほうせん): prescription
