麻薬(まやく)

まやく
noun
narcotic; drug
1. narcotic; illegal drug
Controlled substances such as heroin, cocaine, and opium. Used in legal, medical, and news contexts.
麻薬(まやく)は法律(ほうりつ)で禁止(きんし)されている。
Narcotics are prohibited by law.
麻薬(まやく)の密売(みつばい)で逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested for drug trafficking.
空港(くうこう)で麻薬(まやく)探知犬(たんちけん)が荷物(にもつ)を検査(けんさ)していた。
Drug-sniffing dogs were inspecting luggage at the airport.
2. something addictive; something irresistible
Used figuratively to describe something so appealing or habit-forming that it is hard to resist.
あのゲームは麻薬(まやく)のようだ。
That game is like a drug.
甘(あま)いものは麻薬(まやく)みたいにやめられない。
Sweets are impossible to quit, like a drug.
成功(せいこう)の快感(かいかん)は麻薬(まやく)のように人(ひと)を虜(とりこ)にする。
The thrill of success captivates people like a drug.

Common compounds include 麻薬(まやく)取締法(とりしまりほう) (Narcotics Control Act), 麻薬(まやく)中毒(ちゅうどく) (drug addiction), and 麻薬(まやく)密売人(みつばいにん) (drug dealer). Note the difference from 薬物(やくぶつ) (drugs in general, including legal ones) and 覚醒剤(かくせいざい) (stimulant drugs, specifically methamphetamine in Japanese legal usage).
