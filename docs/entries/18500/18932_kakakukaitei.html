価格改定(かかくかいてい)

かかくかいてい
noun, verb-suru
price revision; price adjustment
1. price revision; price adjustment; price change
An official change in the price of products or services. A business euphemism that often means a price increase, though it can refer to either direction.
来月(らいげつ)から価格改定(かかくかいてい)を行(おこな)います。
We will implement a price revision starting next month.
原材料費(げんざいりょうひ)の高騰(こうとう)により価格改定(かかくかいてい)のお知(し)らせ。
Notice of price revision due to rising raw material costs.
度重(たびかさ)なる価格改定(かかくかいてい)に消費者(しょうひしゃ)の不満(ふまん)が高(たか)まっている。
Consumer dissatisfaction is growing over repeated price revisions.

Composed of 価格(かかく) (price) + 改定(かいてい) (revision, amendment). In practice, 価格改定(かかくかいてい) almost always refers to a price increase — companies use this softer term instead of directly saying 値上(ねあ)げ (price hike). Common phrases: 価格改定(かかくかいてい)のお知(し)らせ (notice of price revision), 価格改定(かかくかいてい)を実施(じっし)する (to carry out a price revision). Related: 値上(ねあ)げ (price increase — more direct), 値下(ねさ)げ (price decrease).
