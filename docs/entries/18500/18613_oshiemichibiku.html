教(おし)え導(みちび)く

おしえみちびく
verb-godan
to teach and guide; to mentor
1. to teach and guide; to mentor; to lead and instruct
To both teach knowledge and guide someone in the right direction. Implies a deeper, more personal form of education than simply teaching facts. Often used for mentoring, parenting, and spiritual guidance.
子供(こども)たちを教(おし)え導(みちび)く。
To teach and guide children.
恩師(おんし)に教(おし)え導(みちび)かれて今(いま)の自分(じぶん)がある。
I am who I am today because my mentor taught and guided me.
若(わか)い世代(せだい)を正(ただ)しい方向(ほうこう)に教(おし)え導(みちび)くのは大人(おとな)の責任(せきにん)だ。
It is the responsibility of adults to teach and guide the younger generation in the right direction.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 教(おし)える (to teach) + 導(みちび)く (to lead/guide). The combination emphasizes both the instructional and directional aspects of guidance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子供(こども)を教(おし)え導(みちび)く (to teach and guide children)
  • 正(ただ)しく教(おし)え導(みちび)く (to guide correctly)
  • 教(おし)え導(みちび)く立場(たちば) (a position of mentoring)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 指導(しどう)する: to instruct, to coach — more formal and institutional
  • 導(みちび)く: to guide, to lead — emphasis on direction rather than teaching
  • 諭(さと)す: to admonish gently, to counsel — emphasis on moral persuasion
