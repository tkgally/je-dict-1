健康体(けんこうたい)

けんこうたい
noun
healthy body; state of good health
1. healthy body; state of good health; clean bill of health
A body that is in good physical health with no medical problems. Often used when describing someone who has been medically cleared or is in excellent physical condition.
検査(けんさ)の結果(けっか)、健康体(けんこうたい)だと言(い)われた。
After the examination, I was told I have a clean bill of health.
健康体(けんこうたい)を維持(いじ)するには運動(うんどう)が欠(か)かせない。
Exercise is essential to maintaining a healthy body.
彼(かれ)は70歳(さい)を過(す)ぎても健康体(けんこうたい)で、毎日(まいにち)ジョギングをしている。
Even past 70, he is in good health and jogs every day.

健康体(けんこうたい) refers to a body in good physical condition with no medical issues. A compound of 健康(けんこう) (healthy) + 体(たい) (body).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 健康体(けんこうたい)を維持(いじ)する: to maintain a healthy body
  • 健康体(けんこうたい)に戻(もど)る: to return to a healthy state
  • 健康体(けんこうたい)である: to be in good health

USAGE:
Often used in medical contexts, especially after checkups or physical examinations. A typical pattern is 検査(けんさ)の結果(けっか)、健康体(けんこうたい)だと分(わ)かった (the examination showed a clean bill of health).

CONTRAST:
Compare with 健康(けんこう) (general health), which is broader and can include mental well-being. 健康体(けんこうたい) focuses specifically on the physical body being free of illness.
