懸案(けんあん)

けんあん
noun
pending issue; unresolved matter
1. pending issue; unresolved matter; outstanding concern
A problem or issue that has remained unresolved over time and requires attention. Often used in formal, political, and business contexts for matters that have been put off or are proving difficult to settle.
懸案(けんあん)の問題(もんだい)がやっと解決(かいけつ)した。
The long-standing issue was finally resolved.
両国間(りょうこくかん)の懸案(けんあん)について協議(きょうぎ)が行(おこな)われた。
Discussions were held on pending issues between the two countries.
長年(ながねん)の懸案(けんあん)だった道路(どうろ)の拡張工事(かくちょうこうじ)がようやく始(はじ)まった。
The road expansion project, a long-standing concern, has finally begun.

Often used as a modifier: 懸案(けんあん)の問題(もんだい) (pending issue), 懸案事項(けんあんじこう) (outstanding matter). Commonly modified by 長年(ながねん)の (long-standing) or 最大(さいだい)の (biggest). Frequently appears in news reports about diplomacy, politics, and organizational management. More formal than 課題(かだい) (task/challenge), with a stronger nuance of something that has been left unresolved.
