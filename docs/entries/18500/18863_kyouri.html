郷里(きょうり)

きょうり
noun
hometown; home village; birthplace
1. hometown; home village; birthplace
The place where one was born and raised, or where one's family roots are. A somewhat formal or literary word for one's home region.
郷里(きょうり)に帰(かえ)る。
I'll return to my hometown.
郷里(きょうり)を離(はな)れてもう二十年(にじゅうねん)になる。
It's been twenty years since I left my hometown.
定年後(ていねんご)は郷里(きょうり)に戻(もど)って、のんびり暮(く)らしたいと思(おも)っている。
After retirement, I want to go back to my hometown and live a relaxed life.

A formal or literary synonym of 故郷(ふるさと)/故郷(こきょう). In everyday conversation, 地元(じもと) (local area) or 実家(じっか) (parents' home) are more commonly used. 郷里(きょうり) often appears in written language, biographical profiles, and formal introductions. Common collocations include 郷里(きょうり)に帰(かえ)る (to return to one's hometown) and 郷里(きょうり)を離(はな)れる (to leave one's hometown).
