静(しず)かに

しずかに
adverb
quietly; calmly; peacefully
1. quietly; silently; softly
In a way that makes little or no noise. Used as a command to tell someone to be quiet, or to describe actions done without making sound.
静(しず)かにしてください。
Please be quiet.
赤(あか)ちゃんが寝(ね)ているから、静(しず)かに歩(ある)いて。
The baby is sleeping, so walk quietly.
ドアを静(しず)かに閉(し)めた。
I closed the door quietly.
2. calmly; peacefully; gently
In a calm, undisturbed manner. Describes doing something without haste, excitement, or agitation.
彼(かれ)は静(しず)かに微笑(ほほえ)んだ。
He smiled quietly.
静(しず)かに暮(く)らしたい。
I want to live peacefully.
雪(ゆき)が静(しず)かに降(ふ)り積(つ)もっていた。
The snow was quietly piling up.

GRAMMAR:
Adverbial form of the na-adjective 静(しず)か. Functions as an adverb modifying verbs. Also used as a command: 静(しず)かに！(Be quiet!)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 静(しず)かにする (to be quiet; to keep quiet)
  • 静(しず)かに暮(く)らす (to live peacefully)
  • 静(しず)かに話(はな)す (to speak softly)
  • 静(しず)かに待(ま)つ (to wait quietly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おとなしく: obediently, meekly — implies compliance
  • そっと: softly, stealthily — emphasizes gentleness or secrecy
  • ひっそり: in seclusion, inconspicuously — emphasizes being unnoticed
