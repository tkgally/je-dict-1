1. persistent; tenacious; relentless; vindictive
Having an intense, unwavering attachment to something, often a grudge or goal. Can be neutral (admirably persistent) but more often carries a negative nuance of being obsessively fixated or holding grudges.
彼(かれ)は執念深(しゅうねんぶか)い性格(せいかく)だ。
He has a tenacious personality.
執念深(しゅうねんぶか)く追(お)いかけてくる相手(あいて)に恐怖(きょうふ)を感(かん)じた。
I felt fear toward the opponent who pursued me relentlessly.
あの刑事(けいじ)は執念深(しゅうねんぶか)い捜査(そうさ)で知(し)られており、何年(なんねん)も前(まえ)の未解決(みかいけつ)事件(じけん)を解決(かいけつ)した。
That detective is known for his relentless investigations and has solved cold cases from years ago.

Compound adjective formed from 執念(しゅうねん) (tenacity, obsessive determination) + 深(ふか)い (deep). The ～深(ぶか)い pattern creates adjectives meaning "deeply/intensely ~": 疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い (suspicious), 欲(よく)深(ぶか)い (greedy), 罪(つみ)深(ぶか)い (sinful). While 執念深(しゅうねんぶか)い can describe admirable persistence (e.g., in investigation or research), it often implies a negative obsessiveness, especially regarding grudges. Related: しつこい (persistent — more colloquial, often negative), 粘(ねば)り強(づよ)い (persevering — always positive), 根(ね)に持(も)つ (to hold a grudge).
