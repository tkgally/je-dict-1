1. costume; fancy dress; disguise
Dressing up in a costume to look like someone or something else, for entertainment, festivals, or events. Includes Halloween costumes, costume parties, and parade costumes.
ハロウィンに仮装(かそう)する。
To wear a costume for Halloween.
子供(こども)たちが魔女(まじょ)やお化(ば)けに仮装(かそう)して街(まち)を歩(ある)いた。
The children dressed up as witches and ghosts and walked through town.
仮装(かそう)行列(ぎょうれつ)はお祭(まつ)りの目玉(めだま)イベントだ。
The costume parade is the highlight event of the festival.
2. disguise; camouflage
Concealing one's true identity or the true nature of something by changing outward appearance.
犯人(はんにん)は警備員(けいびいん)に仮装(かそう)して建物(たてもの)に侵入(しんにゅう)した。
The culprit disguised himself as a security guard and entered the building.
密輸品(みつゆひん)は一般(いっぱん)貨物(かもつ)に仮装(かそう)されていた。
The smuggled goods were disguised as regular cargo.
仮装(かそう)した敵(てき)のスパイを見破(みやぶ)るのは難(むずか)しい。
It is difficult to see through a disguised enemy spy.

Used with する to form 仮装(かそう)する (to dress up in costume, to disguise). Common collocations: 仮装(かそう)大会(たいかい) (costume contest), 仮装(かそう)行列(ぎょうれつ) (costume parade), 仮装(かそう)パーティー (costume party). With the growing popularity of Halloween in Japan, 仮装(かそう) has become strongly associated with Halloween costumes. Homophones: 仮想(かそう) (virtual, hypothetical), 火葬(かそう) (cremation), 下層(かそう) (lower stratum) — all different words. Related: コスプレ (cosplay — dressing as specific characters), 変装(へんそう) (disguise — specifically to conceal identity).
