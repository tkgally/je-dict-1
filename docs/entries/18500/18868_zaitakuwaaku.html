在宅(ざいたく)ワーク

ざいたくわーく
noun
working from home; remote work
1. working from home; remote work
A work arrangement where employees perform their duties from home rather than commuting to an office. Became widespread in Japan during the COVID-19 pandemic.
今日(きょう)は在宅(ざいたく)ワークだ。
I'm working from home today.
在宅(ざいたく)ワークが増(ふ)えて、通勤(つうきん)しなくなった。
Remote work has increased, and I've stopped commuting.
在宅(ざいたく)ワークでは自己(じこ)管理(かんり)が大切(たいせつ)だと言(い)われている。
It's said that self-management is important when working from home.

USAGE:
Became an everyday term in Japan from 2020 onward. Used both as a noun and with する. Often abbreviated in casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 在宅(ざいたく)ワークをする (to work from home)
  • 在宅(ざいたく)ワークに切(き)り替(か)える (to switch to remote work)
  • 在宅(ざいたく)ワーク環境(かんきょう) (work-from-home environment)
  • 在宅(ざいたく)ワーク手当(てあて) (remote work allowance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • テレワーク: telework — a broader term covering any remote work, not only from home
  • リモートワーク: remote work — essentially synonymous, slightly more casual
