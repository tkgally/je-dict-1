目利(めき)き

めきき
noun
connoisseur; good judge
1. connoisseur; expert judge; discerning eye
A person with the ability to judge the quality or authenticity of things — especially art, antiques, food, or other specialized items. Also refers to the ability itself.
彼(かれ)は骨董品(こっとうひん)の目利(めき)きだ。
He is a connoisseur of antiques.
魚(さかな)の目利(めき)きができる人(ひと)は少(すく)ない。
Few people can judge the quality of fish.
あの店(みせ)の店主(てんしゅ)は目利(めき)きが確(たし)かで、良(い)いものばかり並(なら)んでいる。
The shop owner has a reliable eye for quality — only good items are on display.

USAGE:
Can refer either to the person (目利(めき)き = connoisseur) or to the skill of judging (目利(めき)きができる = to be able to appraise). Commonly used in contexts involving antiques, art, gemstones, fish, and other items where expert evaluation matters.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目利(めき)きが確(たし)か (having a reliable eye)
  • 目利(めき)きができる (to be able to judge quality)
  • 目利(めき)きの力(ちから) (the ability to appraise)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鑑定(かんてい): appraisal — more formal, often professional
  • 通(つう): connoisseur — broader term for someone knowledgeable
  • 見(み)る目(め)がある: to have a good eye — more casual expression
