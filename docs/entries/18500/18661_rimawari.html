利回(りまわ)り

りまわり
noun
yield; return on investment
1. yield; return; rate of return
The annual income from an investment expressed as a percentage of its cost or current value. Used in finance for bonds, stocks, real estate, and other investments.
利回(りまわ)りが高(たか)い。
The yield is high.
この債券(さいけん)の利回(りまわ)りは年(ねん)3%だ。
The yield on this bond is 3% per year.
不動産(ふどうさん)投資(とうし)では、表面(ひょうめん)利回(りまわ)りだけでなく実質(じっしつ)利回(りまわ)りも確認(かくにん)すべきだ。
In real estate investment, you should check not only the gross yield but also the net yield.

A key term in finance and investing. Common collocations: 利回(りまわ)りが高(たか)い/低(ひく)い (high/low yield), 表面(ひょうめん)利回(りまわ)り (gross yield), 実質(じっしつ)利回(りまわ)り (net yield), 配当(はいとう)利回(りまわ)り (dividend yield). Compound structure: 利(り) (profit, interest) + 回(まわ)り (rotation, return). Related: 利率(りりつ) (interest rate — for loans/deposits), 収益率(しゅうえきりつ) (rate of return — more formal).
