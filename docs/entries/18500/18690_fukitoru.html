1. to wipe off; to wipe away; to clean by wiping
To remove dirt, liquid, or other substances from a surface by wiping with a cloth or tissue.
汗(あせ)を拭(ふ)き取(と)る。
To wipe off sweat.
テーブルの汚(よご)れを拭(ふ)き取(と)った。
I wiped the dirt off the table.
濡(ぬ)れたタオルで床(ゆか)の汚(よご)れを拭(ふ)き取(と)ってください。
Please wipe the dirt off the floor with a wet towel.

A compound verb formed from 拭(ふ)く (to wipe) + 取(と)る (to take/remove). Unlike 拭(ふ)く alone, which simply means to wipe a surface, 拭(ふ)き取(と)る emphasizes removing something by wiping.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [object]を拭(ふ)き取(と)る
  • No intransitive counterpart

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 汗(あせ)を拭(ふ)き取(と)る: to wipe off sweat
  • 涙(なみだ)を拭(ふ)き取(と)る: to wipe away tears
  • 汚(よご)れを拭(ふ)き取(と)る: to wipe off dirt
  • 水気(みずけ)を拭(ふ)き取(と)る: to wipe off moisture

ASPECT (ている):
拭(ふ)き取(と)っている describes the action in progress (someone is wiping something off).
