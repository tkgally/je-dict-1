闇取引(やみとりひき)

やみとりひき
noun
illegal transaction; black market deal
1. illegal transaction; black market deal; underground dealing
A transaction conducted secretly and illegally, outside official or regulated channels. Includes black market sales, illicit trading, and under-the-table deals.
闇取引(やみとりひき)で逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested for illegal transactions.
武器(ぶき)の闇取引(やみとりひき)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
Illegal arms dealing has become a problem.
戦後(せんご)の日本(にほん)では食料品(しょくりょうひん)の闇取引(やみとりひき)が横行(おうこう)していた。
In postwar Japan, black market dealing of food was rampant.

Composed of 闇(やみ) (darkness, secrecy) + 取引(とりひき) (transaction, deal). The prefix 闇(やみ) is productive for illegal/underground activities: 闇(やみ)ルート (illegal channel), 闇(やみ)金融(きんゆう) (loan sharking), 闇(やみ)市(いち) (black market). Historically associated with the postwar black market (闇市(やみいち)). In modern usage, appears in news about organized crime, smuggling, and online illegal sales.
