役柄(やくがら)

やくがら
noun
role; character; part
1. role; character; part (in a play, film, etc.)
The nature or type of a role played by an actor. Refers not just to the role itself but to its character and qualities.
難(むずか)しい役柄(やくがら)に挑戦(ちょうせん)する。
I take on a difficult role.
彼女(かのじょ)はその役柄(やくがら)にぴったりだった。
She was a perfect fit for the role.
悪役(あくやく)の役柄(やくがら)を演(えん)じるために体重(たいじゅう)を増(ふ)やした。
He gained weight to play the villain's part.
2. nature of one's position; role one is expected to fill
By extension, the duties or behavior expected of someone given their position or status.
リーダーという役柄上(やくがらじょう)、厳(きび)しいことも言(い)わなければならない。
Given the nature of my role as leader, I have to say tough things too.
司会者(しかいしゃ)の役柄(やくがら)として場(ば)を盛(も)り上(あ)げた。
In his role as host, he livened up the event.
役柄上(やくがらじょう)、個人的(こじんてき)な意見(いけん)は控(ひか)えさせていただきます。
Given the nature of my position, I will refrain from expressing personal opinions.

役柄(やくがら) refers to the nature or character of a role, not just the role itself. It emphasizes what kind of role it is.

COMPOUND:
役(やく) (role) + 柄(がら) (nature, character, pattern). The suffix ～柄(がら) adds the sense of inherent nature or quality.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 役(やく): the role itself (what part you play)
  • 役柄(やくがら): the nature/character of the role (what kind of part it is)
  • 役割(やくわり): role, function (broader, used for social/organizational roles)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 難(むずか)しい役柄(やくがら): a difficult role
  • 明(あか)るい役柄(やくがら): a cheerful role
  • 役柄(やくがら)にぴったり: a perfect fit for the role
  • 役柄上(やくがらじょう): by virtue of one's role/position

NOTE:
役柄上(やくがらじょう) (sense 2) is a set expression meaning "given the nature of one's position" — used to explain behavior required by a role.
