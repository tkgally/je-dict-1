1. to come untied; to come undone; to unravel
For something that was tied, knotted, or bound to become loose and come apart on its own.
靴(くつ)ひもが解(ほど)けた。
My shoelace came untied.
リボンの結(むす)び目(め)が解(ほど)けてしまった。
The knot in the ribbon came undone.
マフラーの編(あ)み目(め)が一(ひと)つ解(ほど)けている。
One stitch in the scarf has come unraveled.
2. to relax; to soften
For tension, a stiff expression, or a guarded attitude to ease and become relaxed. Used figuratively.
緊張(きんちょう)が解(ほど)けた。
The tension eased.
彼(かれ)の表情(ひょうじょう)がようやく解(ほど)けた。
His expression finally softened.
話(はなし)をするうちに固(かた)い雰囲気(ふんいき)が解(ほど)けてきた。
As we talked, the stiff atmosphere gradually relaxed.

解(ほど)ける means for something tied or knotted to come undone on its own. Also used figuratively for tension or expressions softening.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 解(ほど)く (transitive — to untie, to undo)

ASPECT (ている):
解(ほど)けている means something is currently in an untied state, e.g. 靴(くつ)ひもが解(ほど)けている (the shoelace is untied).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 靴(くつ)ひもが解(ほど)ける: shoelace comes untied
  • 結(むす)び目(め)が解(ほど)ける: knot comes undone
  • 緊張(きんちょう)が解(ほど)ける: tension eases
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)が解(ほど)ける: expression softens

CAUTION:
Do not confuse with 解(と)ける (same kanji, different reading), which means "to be solved" or "to melt."
