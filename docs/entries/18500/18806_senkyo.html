1. occupation; seizure; taking possession of (a place)
Taking control of a building, territory, or area, often by force or without permission. Used in military, political, and protest contexts.
デモ隊(たい)が広場(ひろば)を占拠(せんきょ)した。
The demonstrators occupied the plaza.
武装(ぶそう)グループが大使館(たいしかん)を占拠(せんきょ)している。
An armed group is occupying the embassy.
不法(ふほう)占拠(せんきょ)されていた建物(たてもの)がようやく解放(かいほう)された。
The building that had been illegally occupied was finally freed.

Not to be confused with the homophone 選挙(せんきょ) (election). 占拠(せんきょ) uses 占(せん) (occupy/seize).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不法(ふほう)占拠(せんきょ): illegal occupation, squatting
  • 武力(ぶりょく)占拠(せんきょ): armed seizure
  • 市場(しじょう)を占拠(せんきょ)する: to dominate a market
  • 建物(たてもの)を占拠(せんきょ)する: to occupy a building

USAGE:
Often appears in news about protests, military actions, or territorial disputes. Can also be used figuratively for market dominance.

RELATED:

  • 占領(せんりょう): military occupation (larger scale, e.g. a country)
  • 占有(せんゆう): possession, occupancy (legal term)
  • 制圧(せいあつ): suppression, gaining control by force
