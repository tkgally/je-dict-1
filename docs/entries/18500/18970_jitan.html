時短(じたん)

じたん
noun
time-saving; shortened hours
1. time-saving; shortening of time
Reducing the time needed for a task or activity. Short for 時間短縮(じかんたんしゅく). Very commonly used in everyday life, especially about cooking, housework, and work tasks.
時短(じたん)レシピを探(さが)す。
I look for time-saving recipes.
時短(じたん)のために食洗機(しょくせんき)を買(か)った。
I bought a dishwasher to save time.
朝(あさ)の準備(じゅんび)を時短(じたん)するコツを教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me some tips for cutting down on morning preparation time.
2. shortened business hours
Reduced operating hours, especially of restaurants and shops. Became extremely common during COVID-19 when businesses were asked to close early.
時短営業(じたんえいぎょう)のお知(し)らせ。
Notice of shortened business hours.
あの店(みせ)は今(いま)時短(じたん)で夜(よる)8時(じ)に閉(し)まる。
That shop is on shortened hours and closes at 8 PM now.
時短要請(じたんようせい)に応(おう)じて、営業時間(えいぎょうじかん)を短縮(たんしゅく)しました。
In response to the request to shorten hours, we reduced our operating hours.

Abbreviation of 時間短縮(じかんたんしゅく). Can function as a noun modifier: 時短(じたん)レシピ (quick recipe), 時短(じたん)テク (time-saving technique), 時短(じたん)グッズ (time-saving goods). Also used with する: 時短(じたん)する (to save time). The sense of 'shortened business hours' became widespread during the COVID-19 pandemic, often appearing in 時短営業(じたんえいぎょう) (reduced-hours operation) and 時短要請(じたんようせい) (request to shorten hours).
