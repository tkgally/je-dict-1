1. enshrinement; placing a sacred object or statue
To place a Buddhist statue, sacred object, or religious artifact in a designated location for worship.
仏像(ぶつぞう)を安置(あんち)する。
To enshrine a Buddhist statue.
この寺(てら)には古(ふる)い仏像(ぶつぞう)が安置(あんち)されている。
An ancient Buddhist statue is enshrined at this temple.
本堂(ほんどう)に安置(あんち)されている観音像(かんのんぞう)は国宝(こくほう)に指定(してい)されている。
The Kannon statue enshrined in the main hall is designated a national treasure.
2. laying out a body; keeping a body before burial
To place a deceased person's body in a designated area before funeral rites are performed.
遺体(いたい)を安置(あんち)する。
To lay out a body.
故人(こじん)は葬儀場(そうぎじょう)に安置(あんち)された。
The deceased was laid to rest at the funeral home.
通夜(つや)まで遺体(いたい)を自宅(じたく)に安置(あんち)することにした。
We decided to keep the body at home until the wake.

安置(あんち) means to place something with reverence — used for enshrining sacred objects or laying out a body before a funeral.

USAGE:

  • Composed of 安(あん) (peace, calm) + 置(ち) (to place); literally, 'to place peacefully.'
  • Functions as a する verb and as a noun.
  • Two main contexts: religious (enshrining a statue, relic, or other sacred object) and funerary (laying out a body in a respectful place before the funeral service).
  • The word carries a tone of solemnity and reverence; it is never used for ordinary objects.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仏像(ぶつぞう)を安置(あんち)する: to enshrine a Buddha statue
  • 遺体(いたい)を安置(あんち)する: to lay out a body (for a wake or funeral)
  • 安置(あんち)所(しょ): a mortuary, a place where bodies are laid out
  • 安置(あんち)室(しつ): a viewing room (for the deceased)
  • 本尊(ほんぞん)を安置(あんち)する: to enshrine the principal Buddhist image

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 奉(ほう)る: to enshrine, to revere (more abstract; emphasizes the act of veneration).
  • 祀(まつ)る: to enshrine, to deify (used for Shinto kami and ancestors).
  • 置(お)く: to place (the everyday word, with no reverence implied).
