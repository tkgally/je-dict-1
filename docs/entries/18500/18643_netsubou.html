熱望(ねつぼう)

ねつぼう
noun
ardent desire; fervent wish
1. ardent desire; fervent wish; burning aspiration
An intense, passionate desire for something. Stronger than 希望(きぼう) (hope) or 願望(がんぼう) (wish), conveying urgency and emotional intensity.
平和(へいわ)を熱望(ねつぼう)する。
To fervently desire peace.
ファンが続編(ぞくへん)を熱望(ねつぼう)している。
Fans are eagerly hoping for a sequel.
長年(ながねん)熱望(ねつぼう)していた海外(かいがい)留学(りゅうがく)がついに実現(じつげん)した。
The study abroad that I had fervently wished for over many years finally came true.

Composed of 熱(ねつ) (heat, passion) + 望(ぼう) (desire, hope). Conveys intense, burning desire — stronger than ordinary wanting.

INTENSITY SCALE:

  • 希望(きぼう): hope, wish (mild)
  • 願望(がんぼう): desire, wish (moderate)
  • 熱望(ねつぼう): ardent desire, fervent wish (strong)
  • 渇望(かつぼう): craving, desperate longing (strongest)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 熱望(ねつぼう)する: to ardently desire
  • 熱望(ねつぼう)されている: to be eagerly awaited
  • 長年(ながねん)の熱望(ねつぼう): long-held ardent wish
  • ファンが熱望(ねつぼう): fans eagerly hope for

REGISTER:
Formal and literary. Common in news, essays, and formal speech. In casual conversation, すごく欲(ほ)しい or 待(ま)ち望(のぞ)む is more natural.
