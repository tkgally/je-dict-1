新古品(しんこひん)

しんこひん
noun
unused but second-hand item; like-new item
1. unused but second-hand item; like-new item
A product that has never been used but is sold as second-hand, typically because the packaging was opened, it was a display model, or the original owner never used it before reselling. Common in Japanese resale markets.
この靴(くつ)は新古品(しんこひん)です。
These shoes are unused but second-hand.
新古品(しんこひん)なら新品(しんぴん)より安(やす)く買(か)える。
If it's a like-new item, you can buy it cheaper than new.
フリマアプリで新古品(しんこひん)のブランドバッグを見(み)つけて、半額(はんがく)で手(て)に入(い)れた。
I found a like-new brand-name bag on a flea market app and got it for half price.

USAGE:
A compound of 新(しん) (new) and 古品(こひん) (used item), describing the in-between status of items that are technically second-hand but have never been used. Very common on Japanese auction and flea market sites like メルカリ and ヤフオク.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新古品(しんこひん)の状態(じょうたい) (like-new condition)
  • 新古品(しんこひん)を買(か)う (to buy a like-new item)
  • 新古品(しんこひん)として出品(しゅっぴん)する (to list as a like-new item)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新品(しんぴん): brand new — completely new, never sold before
  • 中古品(ちゅうこひん): used item — has been used by a previous owner
  • 未使用品(みしようひん): unused item — essentially synonymous but more formal
