入門書(にゅうもんしょ)

にゅうもんしょ
noun
introductory book; beginner's guide
1. introductory book; beginner's guide
A book written for beginners that covers the fundamentals of a subject.
プログラミングの入門書(にゅうもんしょ)を買(か)った。
I bought a beginner's guide to programming.
まずは入門書(にゅうもんしょ)から読(よ)み始(はじ)めるといい。
It's a good idea to start by reading an introductory book.
この入門書(にゅうもんしょ)は図(ず)が多(おお)くてわかりやすいので、初心者(しょしんしゃ)におすすめだ。
This introductory book has many illustrations and is easy to understand, so I recommend it for beginners.

Composed of 入門(にゅうもん) (introduction, initiation) and 書(しょ) (book, document). Often used with a topic preceding it: ～の入門書(にゅうもんしょ). Similar terms include 入門(にゅうもん)編(へん) (introductory volume/section) and 初心者(しょしんしゃ)向(む)け (aimed at beginners). In bookstores, 入門書(にゅうもんしょ) is a common shelf label for beginner-level materials.
