夏野菜(なつやさい)

なつやさい
noun
summer vegetables
1. summer vegetables
Vegetables that are in season during summer, such as tomatoes, cucumbers, eggplants, and bell peppers.
夏野菜(なつやさい)がおいしい季節(きせつ)だ。
It's the season when summer vegetables are delicious.
夏野菜(なつやさい)をたっぷり使(つか)ったカレーを作(つく)った。
I made a curry using lots of summer vegetables.
畑(はたけ)で育(そだ)てた夏野菜(なつやさい)を近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)にもおすそ分(わ)けした。
I shared some of the summer vegetables I grew in my garden with my neighbors.

Typical summer vegetables in Japan include トマト (tomato), きゅうり (cucumber), なす (eggplant), ピーマン (bell pepper), とうもろこし (corn), オクラ (okra), and ゴーヤ (bitter melon). Common in food writing and seasonal cooking. 夏野菜(なつやさい)カレー (summer vegetable curry) is a popular dish. Similar seasonal terms: 冬野菜(ふゆやさい) (winter vegetables), 春野菜(はるやさい) (spring vegetables).
