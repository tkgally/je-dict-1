理事会(りじかい)

りじかい
noun
board of directors; board meeting
1. board of directors; board of trustees; board meeting
The governing body of an organization, or a meeting of that body. Used for corporations, associations, condominiums, universities, and other organizations.
来週(らいしゅう)理事会(りじかい)が開(ひら)かれる。
A board meeting will be held next week.
マンションの理事会(りじかい)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended the condominium board meeting.
理事会(りじかい)で新(あたら)しい予算(よさん)案(あん)が承認(しょうにん)された。
The new budget proposal was approved at the board meeting.

USAGE:
Used for governing bodies of various organizations. In Japanese condominiums (分譲(ぶんじょう)マンション), the 理事会(りじかい) is the management board elected by unit owners. Also used for corporate boards, university governing bodies, and associations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 理事会(りじかい)を開(ひら)く (to hold a board meeting)
  • 理事会(りじかい)に出席(しゅっせき)する (to attend a board meeting)
  • 理事会(りじかい)の決定(けってい) (board decision)
  • 理事会(りじかい)役員(やくいん) (board member)
