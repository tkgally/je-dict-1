濃(こ)い目(め)

こいめ
noun
on the strong side; slightly dark/rich
1. on the strong/dark/rich side; somewhat concentrated
Describes something that is slightly stronger, darker, or richer than standard — typically flavor, color, or makeup. The suffix 目(め) indicates 'leaning toward' or 'on the ... side.'
コーヒーは濃(こ)い目(め)が好(す)きだ。
I like my coffee on the strong side.
味付(あじつ)けを濃(こ)い目(め)にしてください。
Please make the seasoning a bit stronger.
今日(きょう)は濃(こ)い目(め)のメイクで出(で)かけることにした。
I decided to go out with slightly heavier makeup today.

Uses the suffix 目(め) attached to adjective stems to mean 'somewhat' or 'on the ... side.' The pattern Adj-stem + 目(め) is productive: 薄(うす)め (on the light/thin side), 甘(あま)め (on the sweet side), 大(おお)きめ (on the large side). Written with kanji as 濃(こ)い目(め) or in kana as こいめ. Commonly used when ordering food or drinks to specify preference. Opposite: 薄(うす)め (on the weak/light side).
