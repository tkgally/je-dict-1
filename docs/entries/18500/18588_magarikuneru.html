1. to twist and turn; to wind; to meander; to zigzag
Describes a road, river, or path that bends repeatedly. A compound of 曲がる (to bend) and くねる (to twist), emphasizing continuous winding movement.
山道(やまみち)が曲(ま)がりくねっている。
The mountain road twists and turns.
川(かわ)は谷(たに)の間(あいだ)を曲(ま)がりくねって流(なが)れている。
The river meanders through the valley.
曲(ま)がりくねった細(ほそ)い路地(ろじ)を抜(ぬ)けると、小(ちい)さな神社(じんじゃ)が見(み)えてきた。
After passing through narrow winding alleys, a small shrine came into view.

USAGE:
Most commonly used in the ～ている form to describe the state of something winding, or as the past participle 曲(ま)がりくねった modifying a noun (曲(ま)がりくねった道(みち), a winding road).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 曲(ま)がりくねった道(みち) (a winding road)
  • 曲(ま)がりくねった川(かわ) (a meandering river)
  • 曲(ま)がりくねって進(すす)む (to proceed along a winding path)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 曲(ま)がる: to bend — a single turn, not repeated winding
  • くねくね: onomatopoeia for winding/twisting movement
  • 蛇行(だこう)する: to meander — more formal/technical, often for rivers
