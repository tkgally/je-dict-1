依頼人(いらいにん)

いらいにん
noun
client; person who makes a request
1. client; person who makes a request or commission
A person who hires or requests services from a professional such as a lawyer, detective, or consultant. More formal than simply saying 'customer.'
依頼人(いらいにん)と面談(めんだん)する。
I'll meet with the client.
弁護士(べんごし)は依頼人(いらいにん)の利益(りえき)を守(まも)る義務(ぎむ)がある。
A lawyer has a duty to protect the interests of their client.
探偵(たんてい)は依頼人(いらいにん)から調査(ちょうさ)の報告(ほうこく)を求(もと)められた。
The detective was asked by the client for an investigation report.

Composed of 依頼(いらい) (request, commission) + 人(にん) (person). Used mainly in legal, detective, and professional service contexts. Distinguished from 顧客(こきゃく) (customer, client in business) and 客(きゃく) (customer, guest), which are broader terms. 依頼人(いらいにん) specifically implies someone who has formally requested professional services.
