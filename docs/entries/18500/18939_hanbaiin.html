販売員(はんばいいん)

はんばいいん
noun
sales clerk; salesperson
1. sales clerk; salesperson; sales staff
A person whose job is to sell products, typically in a retail store. A neutral, professional term for someone working in sales.
販売員(はんばいいん)に聞(き)いてみよう。
Let's ask the sales clerk.
デパートの販売員(はんばいいん)として働(はたら)いている。
I work as a salesperson at a department store.
親切(しんせつ)な販売員(はんばいいん)が商品(しょうひん)の特徴(とくちょう)を丁寧(ていねい)に説明(せつめい)してくれた。
A friendly salesperson carefully explained the features of the product.

A compound of 販売(はんばい) (selling) + 員(いん) (member, staff). More formal and professional than 店員(てんいん) (shop staff), which is the everyday word for a store clerk. 販売員(はんばいいん) is commonly used in job listings and business contexts. Related terms: 営業(えいぎょう)マン (salesman, more business-to-business), 店員(てんいん) (shop clerk, everyday).
