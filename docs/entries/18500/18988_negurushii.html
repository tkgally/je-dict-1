1. hard to sleep; unable to sleep comfortably; restless
Describes a state where one cannot sleep well, typically because of heat, humidity, or uncomfortable conditions. Most often associated with hot summer nights.
寝苦(ねぐる)しい夜(よる)が続(つづ)く。
The sleepless nights continue.
暑(あつ)くて寝苦(ねぐる)しいので、エアコンをつけた。
It was so hot I couldn't sleep, so I turned on the air conditioner.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)は湿度(しつど)が高(たか)くて寝苦(ねぐる)しい日(ひ)が多(おお)い。
During the rainy season, there are many nights where it's too humid to sleep well.

Almost exclusively used to describe difficulty sleeping due to heat or humidity, especially during Japanese summers. The most common collocation is 寝苦(ねぐる)しい夜(よる) (a restless/sleepless night), frequently heard in weather forecasts: 今夜(こんや)も寝苦(ねぐる)しい夜(よる)になるでしょう (Tonight will be another uncomfortable night for sleeping). Part of the ～苦(ぐる)しい pattern meaning 'hard to do ~,' like 見苦(みぐる)しい (unsightly), 聞(きき)き苦(ぐる)しい (unpleasant to hear).
