変化(へんか)に富(と)む

へんかにとむ
expression
varied; full of variety; diverse
1. varied; full of variety; rich in change; diverse
Describes something that has abundant variation or diversity. Used for landscapes, climates, experiences, content, and other things characterized by richness and variety.
日本(にほん)は変化(へんか)に富(と)んだ地形(ちけい)を持(も)つ。
Japan has varied terrain.
変化(へんか)に富(と)んだメニューが人気(にんき)の秘密(ひみつ)だ。
The varied menu is the secret to its popularity.
この地域(ちいき)は四季(しき)を通(つう)じて変化(へんか)に富(と)んだ景色(けしき)が楽(たの)しめる。
This area offers diverse scenery to enjoy throughout the four seasons.

GRAMMAR:
The pattern ～に富(と)む means "to be rich in ~" and is productive: 栄養(えいよう)に富(と)む (rich in nutrition), 経験(けいけん)に富(と)む (rich in experience). Most commonly appears in the attributive form ～に富(と)んだ modifying a noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変化(へんか)に富(と)んだ景色(けしき) (varied scenery)
  • 変化(へんか)に富(と)んだ内容(ないよう) (varied content)
  • 変化(へんか)に富(と)んだ人生(じんせい) (a life full of changes)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • バラエティに富(と)む: rich in variety — uses the loanword バラエティ
  • 多彩(たさい)な: colorful, diverse — a single-word adjective alternative
  • 多様(たよう)な: diverse — more formal/academic
