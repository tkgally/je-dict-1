失策(しっさく)

しっさく
noun
blunder; mistake; error (in baseball)
1. blunder; miscalculation; mistake in strategy
A mistake in judgment, planning, or execution, especially one with significant consequences. More formal than ミス and implies a strategic or political error.
外交上(がいこうじょう)の失策(しっさく)だった。
It was a diplomatic blunder.
政府(せいふ)の経済(けいざい)政策(せいさく)は失策(しっさく)だったと批判(ひはん)されている。
The government's economic policy is being criticized as a blunder.
彼(かれ)の失策(しっさく)が会社(かいしゃ)に大(おお)きな損害(そんがい)を与(あた)えた。
His blunder caused significant damage to the company.
2. error (in baseball)
A fielding mistake in baseball that allows a batter or runner to advance. The official statistical term for an error in Japanese baseball.
ショートの失策(しっさく)で一点(いってん)入(はい)った。
A run scored due to an error by the shortstop.
今季(こんき)は失策(しっさく)数(すう)が多(おお)い。
There have been many errors this season.
守備(しゅび)の乱(みだ)れから失策(しっさく)が続(つづ)き、大量(たいりょう)失点(しってん)につながった。
Errors kept piling up due to sloppy fielding, leading to a flood of runs.

For sense 1, similar to 失敗(しっぱい) (failure) but implies a more strategic or consequential error, often in politics, diplomacy, or business. Common collocations: 外交(がいこう)失策(しっさく) (diplomatic blunder), 政策(せいさく)の失策(しっさく) (policy mistake). For sense 2, it is the standard baseball term corresponding to 'error' in English statistics. Abbreviation: E (失策(しっさく)).
