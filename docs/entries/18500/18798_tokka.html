特価(とっか)

とっか
noun
special price; bargain price
1. special price; bargain price
A price that is lower than usual, typically offered during a sale or promotion.
特価(とっか)セール開催中(かいさいちゅう)！
Bargain sale now on!
このパソコンは特価(とっか)で三万円(さんまんえん)です。
This computer is on special for 30,000 yen.
閉店(へいてん)セールで全品(ぜんぴん)特価(とっか)になっている。
Everything is at a special price for the closing sale.

A shortened form of 特別(とくべつ)価格(かかく) (special price). Commonly seen on signs, advertisements, and online shops.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 特価(とっか)品(ひん): bargain item, sale item
  • 特価(とっか)セール: special price sale
  • 特価(とっか)で販売(はんばい)する: to sell at a special price
  • 特価(とっか)で提供(ていきょう)する: to offer at a special price

RELATED PRICING TERMS:

  • 定価(ていか): list price, retail price
  • 半額(はんがく): half price
  • 割引(わりびき): discount
  • 激安(げきやす): extremely cheap
  • セール価格(かかく): sale price

HOMOPHONE:
特化(とっか) (specialization) is a completely different word with the same reading. Context makes the meaning clear.
