1. simmering; bubbling; boiling gently
An onomatopoeic word describing the sound and appearance of liquid simmering or boiling in a pot. Suggests steady, continuous bubbling at moderate heat.
スープがぐつぐつ煮(に)えている。
The soup is simmering away.
鍋(なべ)がぐつぐつと音(おと)を立(た)てている。
The pot is making bubbling sounds.
カレーをぐつぐつと弱火(よわび)で煮込(にこ)んでいると、いい香(かお)りがしてきた。
As I simmered the curry on low heat, a wonderful aroma wafted up.

An onomatopoeia for the sound of liquid simmering. Often used with と (ぐつぐつと) or directly before a verb (ぐつぐつ煮(に)る). Compare with related cooking sounds: ぐらぐら (rolling boil, more vigorous), ことこと (gentle simmering, lighter sound), ふつふつ (just starting to bubble). ぐつぐつ suggests a moderate, steady simmer — not as gentle as ことこと but not as violent as ぐらぐら.
