1. to beat down; to thrash; to pummel
To strike someone repeatedly and violently, knocking them down. Can also be used figuratively to mean thoroughly defeating someone in an argument, competition, or debate.
相手(あいて)を叩(たた)きのめした。
He beat his opponent down.
試合(しあい)で叩(たた)きのめされて、立(た)ち上(あ)がれなかった。
I was thrashed in the match and couldn't get back up.
議論(ぎろん)で完全(かんぜん)に叩(たた)きのめされて、何(なに)も言(い)い返(かえ)せなかった。
I was completely demolished in the argument and couldn't say anything back.

FORMATION:
叩(たた)く (to hit) + のめす (to knock down). The ～のめす suffix intensifies the action, implying the target is knocked flat. Compare 打(う)ちのめす, which has a similar meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相手(あいて)を叩(たた)きのめす (to beat one's opponent down)
  • 徹底的(てっていてき)に叩(たた)きのめす (to thoroughly thrash)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 打(う)ちのめす: to beat down — similar meaning but uses 打(う)つ instead of 叩(たた)く; slightly more literary
  • 叩(たた)く: to hit — basic action without the intensifying suffix
  • ぼこぼこにする: to beat up — colloquial slang
