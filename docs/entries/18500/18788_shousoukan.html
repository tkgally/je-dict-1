焦燥感(しょうそうかん)

しょうそうかん
noun
feeling of impatience; sense of restless frustration
1. feeling of impatience; sense of restless frustration; anxiety-driven restlessness
An uneasy, agitated feeling of being unable to wait or stay still, often arising from frustration at things not going as planned or from a sense of urgency.
焦燥感(しょうそうかん)に駆(か)られる。
To be driven by a sense of impatience.
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)づくにつれて焦燥感(しょうそうかん)が増(ま)してきた。
My sense of restless frustration grew as the deadline approached.
結果(けっか)が出(で)ないまま時間(じかん)だけが過(す)ぎていく焦燥感(しょうそうかん)は、誰(だれ)にでも経験(けいけん)があるだろう。
Everyone has probably experienced the frustration of time passing without results.

Common collocations: 焦燥感(しょうそうかん)に駆(か)られる (to be seized by restless frustration), 焦燥感(しょうそうかん)を覚(おぼ)える (to feel a sense of impatience), 焦燥感(しょうそうかん)が募(つの)る (for restless frustration to build up). The suffix ～感(かん) (feeling, sense) creates emotion nouns: 不安感(ふあんかん) (sense of unease), 達成感(たっせいかん) (sense of achievement), 疎外感(そがいかん) (sense of alienation). The base word 焦燥(しょうそう) (impatience, irritation) is less common on its own. Related: 苛立(いらだ)ち (irritation — more everyday), 焦(あせ)り (impatience — more colloquial).
