占(うらな)い

うらない
noun
fortune-telling; divination
1. fortune-telling; divination; horoscope
The practice of predicting the future or reading a person's fate through various methods such as astrology, palm reading, tarot cards, etc. Also refers to the result of such a reading.
占(うらな)いを信(しん)じますか。
Do you believe in fortune-telling?
今朝(けさ)のテレビの占(うらな)いで一位(いちい)だった。
I was ranked first in this morning's TV horoscope.
友達(ともだち)と原宿(はらじゅく)で占(うらな)いをしてもらった。
My friend and I had our fortunes told in Harajuku.

Derived from the verb 占(うらな)う (to tell fortunes). Types of 占(うらな)い include: 星占(ほしうらな)い (astrology/horoscope), 手相占(てそううらな)い (palm reading), タロット占(うらな)い (tarot reading), 血液型占(けつえきがたうらな)い (blood type fortune-telling, popular in Japan). A fortune-teller is 占(うらな)い師(し). Morning TV shows in Japan commonly feature daily horoscope segments (今日(きょう)の占(うらな)い).
