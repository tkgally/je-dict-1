1. being on a wanted list; wanted by police
A formal police designation where a specific individual is publicly sought for arrest in connection with a crime. The police issue a wanted notice with the person's name and photo.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は指名(しめい)手配(てはい)されている。
The suspect is on the wanted list.
指名(しめい)手配(てはい)犯(はん)がついに逮捕(たいほ)された。
The wanted criminal was finally arrested.
警察(けいさつ)は逃走(とうそう)した犯人(はんにん)を全国(ぜんこく)に指名(しめい)手配(てはい)した。
The police issued a nationwide wanted notice for the escaped criminal.

USAGE:
A standard term in crime news. Can be used as a noun or with する. The 指名(しめい)手配(てはい)書(しょ) (wanted poster) is posted in police stations and public places.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指名(しめい)手配(てはい)される (to be placed on a wanted list)
  • 指名(しめい)手配(てはい)犯(はん) (wanted criminal)
  • 全国(ぜんこく)指名(しめい)手配(てはい) (nationwide wanted notice)
  • 指名(しめい)手配(てはい)を受(う)ける (to be designated as wanted)
  • 国際(こくさい)指名(しめい)手配(てはい) (international wanted notice, e.g., via Interpol)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逮捕(たいほ): arrest — the actual act of detaining someone
  • 手配(てはい): arrangement, preparation — by itself has a different meaning; the crime sense requires 指名(しめい)
