腹持(はらも)ち

はらもち
noun
how filling food is; staying power of food
1. how filling food is; how long food keeps you full
The quality of food in terms of how long it keeps you feeling full after eating. Almost always used with がいい (filling, keeps you full) or がわるい (not filling, wears off quickly).
おにぎりは腹持(はらも)ちがいい。
Rice balls are filling.
パンは腹持(はらも)ちが悪(わる)いからすぐお腹(なか)が空(す)く。
Bread isn't very filling, so I get hungry again quickly.
朝(あさ)ごはんには腹持(はらも)ちのいいものを食(た)べた方(ほう)がいい。
It's better to eat something filling for breakfast.

A compound of 腹(はら) (stomach) + 持(も)ち (lasting/holding). Almost always appears in the fixed patterns 腹持(はらも)ちがいい (filling) or 腹持(はらも)ちが悪(わる)い (not filling). Also used as a modifier: 腹持(はらも)ちのいい食(た)べ物(もの) (filling food). There is no English word that corresponds exactly; the closest equivalents are 'filling' or 'satisfying' when talking about food.
