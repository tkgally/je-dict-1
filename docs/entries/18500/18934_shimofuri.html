霜降(しもふ)り

しもふり
noun
marbled (meat); frost pattern
1. marbled meat; marbling
Refers to beef or other meat with fine streaks of fat running through it, resembling frost. Highly prized in Japanese cuisine for its tenderness and flavor.
霜降(しもふ)りの肉(にく)を買(か)った。
I bought marbled meat.
この霜降(しもふ)り和牛(わぎゅう)はとても柔(やわ)らかい。
This marbled wagyu beef is very tender.
霜降(しもふ)りの入(はい)った高級(こうきゅう)な牛肉(ぎゅうにく)をすき焼(や)きにした。
We made sukiyaki with high-grade marbled beef.
2. frost pattern; frosted appearance
A pattern or appearance resembling frost, used to describe textiles, ceramics, or other surfaces with a speckled white-on-dark pattern.
霜降(しもふ)りの着物(きもの)を着(き)た。
She wore a kimono with a frost pattern.
この皿(さら)は霜降(しもふ)り模様(もよう)が美(うつく)しい。
This plate has a beautiful frosted pattern.
白(しろ)と灰色(はいいろ)の霜降(しもふ)りの生地(きじ)でコートを仕立(した)てた。
The coat was tailored from a white and gray heathered fabric.

When referring to meat, 霜降(しもふ)り describes the fine white fat marbling that runs through red meat, creating a pattern that looks like frost (霜(しも)). This marbling is a key quality indicator for Japanese beef, especially 和牛(わぎゅう). The word comes from 霜(しも) (frost) + 降(ふ)り (falling). In the textile sense, it refers to a heathered or speckled pattern. Common collocations include 霜降(しもふ)り肉(にく) (marbled meat) and 霜降(しもふ)り和牛(わぎゅう) (marbled wagyu).
