あざ

あざ
noun
bruise; birthmark
1. bruise
A discolored mark on the skin caused by an injury where blood vessels are damaged beneath the surface.
腕(うで)にあざができた。
I got a bruise on my arm.
ぶつけたところに大(おお)きなあざが残(のこ)っている。
A large bruise remains where I bumped it.
あざが消(き)えるまで一週間(いっしゅうかん)くらいかかった。
It took about a week for the bruise to disappear.
2. birthmark
A colored mark on the skin that is present from birth or appears shortly after.
生(う)まれつきのあざがある。
I have a birthmark.
背中(せなか)にあざがある赤(あか)ちゃんは多(おお)い。
Many babies have birthmarks on their backs.
蒙古斑(もうこはん)は青(あお)いあざのように見(み)えるが、成長(せいちょう)とともに消(き)える。
Mongolian spots look like blue birthmarks but fade as the child grows.

Can be written in kanji as 痣(あざ), but the kana form is more common. The typical verb pattern is あざができる (a bruise forms) rather than あざがつく. For bruises, 青(あお)あざ (blue bruise) is a common compound. In medical contexts, birthmarks may be further classified by color: 青(あお)あざ, 赤(あか)あざ, 茶(ちゃ)あざ, etc.
