誇(ほこ)らしげ

ほこらしげ
adjective (な)
proud-looking; triumphant
1. proud-looking; triumphant; showing pride
Describes someone's appearance or manner that conveys pride or satisfaction. Formed from 誇らしい (proud) + the suffix ～げ (appearing to be, looking like).
誇(ほこ)らしげな表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべた。
A proud expression appeared on their face.
息子(むすこ)が賞(しょう)を取(と)り、母親(ははおや)は誇(ほこ)らしげだった。
Her son won an award, and the mother looked proud.
選手(せんしゅ)たちは金(きん)メダルを誇(ほこ)らしげに掲(かか)げた。
The athletes proudly held up their gold medals.

The suffix ～げ creates な-adjectives from い-adjective or noun stems that describe an outward appearance of a feeling: 悲(かな)しげ (looking sad), 楽(たの)しげ (looking happy), 不安(ふあん)げ (looking anxious). Used both as a な-adjective (誇(ほこ)らしげな顔(かお)) and adverbially with に (誇(ほこ)らしげに話(はな)す). Conveys that the pride is visible in the person's demeanor.
