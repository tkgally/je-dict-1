横顔(よこがお)

よこがお
noun
profile; side view of a face
1. profile; side view of a face
The appearance of a person's face as seen from the side.
彼女(かのじょ)の横顔(よこがお)が美(うつく)しい。
Her profile is beautiful.
窓(まど)の外(そと)を見(み)ている彼(かれ)の横顔(よこがお)を描(えが)いた。
I drew his profile as he looked out the window.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で隣(となり)の人(ひと)の横顔(よこがお)がふと目(め)に入(はい)った。
On the train, the profile of the person next to me suddenly caught my eye.
2. another side; different aspect
A less well-known side or aspect of a person, organization, or thing. Used figuratively.
都市(とし)の知(し)られざる横顔(よこがお)を紹介(しょうかい)する。
We introduce a lesser-known side of the city.
企業(きぎょう)の横顔(よこがお)を特集(とくしゅう)した記事(きじ)を読(よ)んだ。
I read an article featuring the different sides of the company.
この番組(ばんぐみ)では選手(せんしゅ)たちの意外(いがい)な横顔(よこがお)に迫(せま)る。
This program explores the surprising other sides of the athletes.

In the literal sense, 横顔(よこがお) refers specifically to a side view of the face, as opposed to 正面(しょうめん) (front view). It is often used in aesthetic or romantic contexts to describe an attractive profile. The figurative sense (another aspect or side) is commonly seen in journalism and documentary-style writing.
