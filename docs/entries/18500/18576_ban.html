盤(ばん)

ばん
noun, suffix
board; tray; disc; base
1. board; game board
A flat surface used for playing board games such as go, shogi, or chess.
碁(ご)盤(ばん)の上(うえ)に石(いし)を並(なら)べる。
I place stones on the go board.
将棋(しょうぎ)盤(ばん)を挟(はさ)んで向(む)かい合(あ)う。
We sit facing each other across the shogi board.
盤(ばん)面(めん)の状況(じょうきょう)をよく見(み)て次(つぎ)の手(て)を考(かんが)える。
I examine the state of the board carefully and think about my next move.
2. tray; platter; disc; plate
A flat, broad object. Used in compounds to refer to various flat objects including trays, discs, and platforms.
レコード盤(ばん)を集(あつ)めている。
I collect vinyl records.
円盤(えんばん)を投(な)げる。
To throw a discus.
岩盤(がんばん)の上(うえ)に建物(たてもの)を建(た)てる。
To build a structure on bedrock.

盤(ばん) refers to a flat surface or board. As a standalone word, it most commonly means a game board. It is highly productive as a suffix in compounds.

GAME BOARD COMPOUNDS:

  • 碁(ご)盤(ばん): go board
  • 将棋(しょうぎ)盤(ばん): shogi board
  • チェス盤(ばん): chess board
  • 盤(ばん)面(めん): board surface; state of play

OTHER COMPOUNDS:

  • 円盤(えんばん): disc, discus
  • 岩盤(がんばん): bedrock
  • 基盤(きばん): foundation, basis
  • レコード盤(ばん): vinyl record
  • 配電(はいでん)盤(ばん): switchboard

NOTE:
The character 盤(ばん) conveys the idea of something flat and broad, which extends metaphorically to meanings like foundation (基盤(きばん)) and situation (局面(きょくめん) is related conceptually to 盤(ばん)面(めん)).
