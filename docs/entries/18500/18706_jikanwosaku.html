時間(じかん)を割(さ)く

じかんをさく
expression
to spare time; to find time for
1. to spare time; to make time for; to find time
To allocate or set aside time from a busy schedule for something. Implies that one is busy and making an effort to find time.
時間(じかん)を割(さ)いてくれた。
They spared their time for me.
お忙(いそが)しいところ時間(じかん)を割(さ)いていただきありがとうございます。
Thank you for taking the time out of your busy schedule.
毎日(まいにち)少(すこ)しでも読書(どくしょ)に時間(じかん)を割(さ)くようにしている。
I try to set aside at least a little time every day for reading.

The verb 割(さ)く means to split or divide, giving the image of carving out a portion from a busy schedule.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時間(じかん)を割(さ)く: to spare/make time
  • ～に時間(じかん)を割(さ)く: to devote time to ~
  • 時間(じかん)を割(さ)いていただく: to have someone spare their time (humble/polite)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勉強(べんきょう)に時間(じかん)を割(さ)く: to spare time for study
  • 趣味(しゅみ)に時間(じかん)を割(さ)く: to make time for hobbies

REGISTER:
Very common in polite business contexts, especially when thanking someone: お忙(いそが)しいところ時間(じかん)を割(さ)いていただきありがとうございます。

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)を作(つく)る: to make time (more casual)
  • 時間(じかん)を取(と)る: to take time (neutral)
