正体(しょうたい)を現(あらわ)す

しょうたいをあらわす
expression
to reveal one's true colors; to show one's real nature
1. to reveal one's true colors; to show one's real nature
For someone's hidden or disguised true nature, identity, or intentions to become apparent. Often implies the revealed truth is negative.
ついに正体(しょうたい)を現(あらわ)した。
They finally revealed their true colors.
優(やさ)しそうな人(ひと)だったが、正体(しょうたい)を現(あらわ)した。
They seemed kind, but they showed their true nature.
長年(ながねん)信頼(しんらい)していた相手(あいて)が正体(しょうたい)を現(あらわ)して裏切(うらぎ)った。
A person I had trusted for years revealed their true colors and betrayed me.

正体(しょうたい) means true identity or true nature. This expression is often used when someone who has been pretending finally shows who they really are. In fiction, it can literally mean unmasking a disguised character. The passive form 正体(しょうたい)が現(あらわ)れる is also used. Related: 本性(ほんしょう)を現(あらわ)す (to show one's true character), 化(ば)けの皮(かわ)が剥(は)がれる (to have one's mask ripped off).
