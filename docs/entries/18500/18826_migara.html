身柄(みがら)

みがら
noun
one's person; custody (of a suspect)
1. one's person; physical body (in a legal/official context)
Refers to a person's physical self, especially in the context of law enforcement and legal proceedings. Often used when discussing arrest, detention, or custody.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の身柄(みがら)を確保(かくほ)した。
The suspect was taken into custody.
身柄(みがら)を拘束(こうそく)されたまま取調(とりしら)べを受(う)けた。
He was questioned while being held in custody.
逃走(とうそう)していた犯人(はんにん)の身柄(みがら)が空港(くうこう)で拘束(こうそく)された。
The fugitive was apprehended at the airport.

Frequently appears in news reports about crime and law enforcement. Key collocations: 身柄(みがら)を確保(かくほ)する (take into custody), 身柄(みがら)を拘束(こうそく)する (detain), 身柄(みがら)引(ひき)渡(わた)し (extradition), 身柄(みがら)を釈放(しゃくほう)する (release from custody). 身柄(みがら)送検(そうけん) refers to sending the suspect (in person) to the prosecutor's office.
