1. propagation; spread; transmission
The spreading or transmission of something (disease, culture, ideas, waves) from one place or person to another. More formal and technical than 広(ひろ)がる.
感染症(かんせんしょう)が急速(きゅうそく)に伝播(でんぱ)した。
The infectious disease spread rapidly.
仏教(ぶっきょう)はインドから東(ひがし)アジアに伝播(でんぱ)した。
Buddhism spread from India to East Asia.
SNSを通(つう)じて情報(じょうほう)が瞬時(しゅんじ)に伝播(でんぱ)する時代(じだい)になった。
We live in an era where information spreads instantly through social media.

Not to be confused with the homophone 電波(でんぱ) (radio waves). 伝播(でんぱ) is used in formal and academic writing for the spread of diseases, cultures, technologies, and ideas. In scientific contexts, it also refers to wave propagation (音(おと)の伝播(でんぱ) — propagation of sound). The reading でんぱ is standard; the alternative reading でんぱん is also seen but less common.
