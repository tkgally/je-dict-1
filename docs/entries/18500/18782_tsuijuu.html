1. following blindly; tagging along; following suit
Following someone or something without independent thought or initiative. Often implies uncritical compliance or imitation.
大手(おおて)に追従(ついじゅう)する。
To follow the major companies' lead.
他社(たしゃ)の値下(ねさ)げに追従(ついじゅう)して価格(かかく)を下(さ)げた。
We followed other companies' lead and lowered our prices.
権力者(けんりょくしゃ)に追従(ついじゅう)するだけでは、本当(ほんとう)の改革(かいかく)はできない。
True reform cannot be achieved by merely following those in power.

Used with する to form 追従(ついじゅう)する (to follow, to tag along). Common collocations: 追従(ついじゅう)を許(ゆる)さない (to be unrivaled, to be beyond compare — literally "not allowing others to follow"). Note: the same kanji 追従(ついしょう) with a different reading means "flattery" or "sycophancy" — a completely different meaning. The reading 追従(ついじゅう) specifically means following/imitating. Related: 追随(ついずい) (following — very close synonym, slightly more formal), 模倣(もほう) (imitation — emphasizes copying).
