1. sold out; completely sold
The state of all available items or tickets having been sold, with none remaining. Used for merchandise, tickets, and limited-edition products.
チケットは完売(かんばい)した。
The tickets are sold out.
限定(げんてい)商品(しょうひん)は発売(はつばい)初日(しょにち)に完売(かんばい)した。
The limited-edition product sold out on the first day of release.
人気(にんき)のコンサートだったので、チケットは発売(はつばい)開始(かいし)数分(すうふん)で完売(かんばい)になった。
Since it was a popular concert, tickets sold out within minutes of going on sale.

Composed of 完(かん) (complete) + 売(ばい) (sell). Indicates that all stock or tickets have been sold with none remaining.

PATTERNS:

  • 完売(かんばい)する: to sell out (active)
  • 完売(かんばい)になる: to become sold out (change of state)
  • 完売(かんばい)済(ず)み: sold out (already completed)
  • 完売(かんばい)御礼(おんれい): thank you, sold out (seen on signs)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チケットが完売(かんばい)する: tickets sell out
  • 即日(そくじつ)完売(かんばい): sold out the same day
  • 発売(はつばい)開始(かいし)直後(ちょくご)に完売(かんばい): sold out immediately after going on sale

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)り切(き)れ: sold out (more colloquial, used in everyday speech and shop signs)
  • 品切(しなぎ)れ: out of stock (focuses on availability rather than sales)

REGISTER:
完売(かんばい) is more formal than 売(う)り切(き)れ and is commonly used in business, event, and media contexts.
