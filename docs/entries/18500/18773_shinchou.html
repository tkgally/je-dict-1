伸長(しんちょう)

しんちょう
noun
growth; extension; elongation
1. growth; extension; elongation; expansion
The act or process of stretching, extending, or growing longer or larger. Used for physical elongation and for the growth or expansion of abstract things like markets, abilities, or influence.
売上(うりあげ)が順調(じゅんちょう)に伸長(しんちょう)している。
Sales are growing steadily.
ゴムの伸長(しんちょう)率(りつ)を測定(そくてい)する。
To measure the elongation rate of rubber.
アジア市場(しじょう)の伸長(しんちょう)が会社(かいしゃ)の成長(せいちょう)を支(ささ)えている。
Growth in the Asian market is supporting the company's expansion.

Used with する to form the verb 伸長(しんちょう)する (to extend, to grow). Common collocations: 伸長(しんちょう)率(りつ) (growth/elongation rate), 市場(しじょう)の伸長(しんちょう) (market growth). Often used in business and technical writing. Homophones: 慎重(しんちょう) (cautious), 身長(しんちょう) (body height), 新調(しんちょう) (newly made) — all different words with distinct kanji. Related: 成長(せいちょう) (growth — more general, especially for people and organizations), 拡大(かくだい) (expansion — emphasizes widening in scale).
