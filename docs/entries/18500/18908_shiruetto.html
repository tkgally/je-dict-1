シルエット

しるえっと
noun
silhouette; outline
1. silhouette; outline; overall shape
The dark outline or shape of something seen against a lighter background. Also used in fashion to describe the overall shape or line of a garment or outfit.
夕日(ゆうひ)にシルエットが浮(う)かぶ。
A silhouette stands out against the sunset.
すっきりしたシルエットのドレス。
A dress with a clean silhouette.
山(やま)の稜線(りょうせん)が美(うつく)しいシルエットを描(えが)いていた。
The mountain ridgeline traced a beautiful silhouette.

From French 'silhouette.' Used for both the dark outline of a figure against light, and the overall shape of clothing in fashion contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • シルエットが浮(う)かぶ: a silhouette appears/stands out
  • シルエットを描(えが)く: to trace/form a silhouette
  • 美(うつく)しいシルエット: a beautiful silhouette

FASHION USAGE:

In fashion, シルエット describes the overall shape or line of a garment:

  • Aラインシルエット: A-line silhouette
  • タイトシルエット: tight/slim silhouette
  • すっきりしたシルエット: a clean silhouette

The fashion sense is very common in Japanese and appears frequently in clothing descriptions and magazines.
