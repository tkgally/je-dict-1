多人数(たにんずう)

たにんずう
noun
large number of people; many people
1. large number of people; many people; large group
A noun referring to a large group of people, often used when discussing group activities, seating capacity, or crowd management.
多人数(たにんずう)での利用(りよう)も可能(かのう)です。
It can also be used by large groups.
多人数(たにんずう)で遊(あそ)べるゲームを探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a game that can be played with many people.
多人数(たにんずう)に対応(たいおう)できる会議室(かいぎしつ)を予約(よやく)した。
I reserved a conference room that can accommodate a large group.

USAGE:
Often used with で to indicate the group context (多人数(たにんずう)で = with many people). Common in gaming, events, and facility descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多人数(たにんずう)で遊(あそ)ぶ (to play with many people)
  • 多人数(たにんずう)向(む)け (for large groups)
  • 多人数(たにんずう)に対応(たいおう)する (to accommodate large groups)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大人数(おおにんずう): large number of people — essentially the same meaning, slightly more common in casual speech
  • 少人数(しょうにんずう): small number of people — the opposite, a small group
