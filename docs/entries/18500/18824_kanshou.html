1. complete victory; decisive win
Winning convincingly without any doubt about the outcome. A thorough, one-sided victory.
五対(ごたい)ゼロで完勝(かんしょう)した。
We won 5 to 0 — a complete victory.
選挙(せんきょ)で完勝(かんしょう)を収(おさ)めた。
He achieved a decisive victory in the election.
相手(あいて)チームを圧倒(あっとう)して完勝(かんしょう)した試合(しあい)だった。
It was a match where we overwhelmed the opposing team for a complete victory.

完勝(かんしょう) means a complete victory — winning decisively with no room for doubt. A compound of 完(かん) (complete) + 勝(しょう) (victory).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完勝(かんしょう)を収(おさ)める: to achieve a complete victory
  • 完勝(かんしょう)する: to win completely
  • 圧倒的(あっとうてき)な完勝(かんしょう): an overwhelming complete victory

CONTRAST:

  • 完勝(かんしょう): complete victory
  • 完敗(かんぱい): complete defeat (the opposite)
  • 辛勝(しんしょう): narrow victory
  • 惜敗(せきはい): narrow defeat

USAGE:
Common in sports reporting, competitive contexts, and elections.
