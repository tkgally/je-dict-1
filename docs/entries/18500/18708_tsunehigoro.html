常日頃(つねひごろ)

つねひごろ
adverb
always; habitually; on a daily basis
1. always; habitually; on a daily basis; at all times
Describes something done regularly as a matter of course, or a habitual state of affairs. Somewhat formal.
常日頃(つねひごろ)思(おも)っていること。
Something I always think about.
常日頃(つねひごろ)から体(からだ)を鍛(きた)えている。
I train my body on a regular basis.
常日頃(つねひごろ)感(かん)じていた不満(ふまん)をようやく上司(じょうし)に伝(つた)えた。
I finally told my boss about the dissatisfaction I had always felt.

Means "always, habitually, on a regular basis." Emphasizes that something is a constant, ongoing state or habit. Somewhat formal in tone.

FORMATION:
常(つね) (always, constant) + 日頃(ひごろ) (usually, daily life). More emphatic than 日頃(ひごろ) alone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 常日頃(つねひごろ)から: from one's usual daily life, always
  • 常日頃(つねひごろ)思(おも)っている: something one always thinks
  • 常日頃(つねひごろ)の行(おこな)い: one's usual conduct

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日頃(ひごろ): usually, normally (less emphatic)
  • 普段(ふだん): ordinarily, normally (casual)
  • 常(つね)に: always, constantly (more literary)
