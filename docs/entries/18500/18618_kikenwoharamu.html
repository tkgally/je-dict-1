危険(きけん)を孕(はら)む

きけんをはらむ
expression
to involve danger; to be fraught with risk
1. to involve danger; to be fraught with risk; to carry risk within
To contain inherent danger or risk within something. The verb 孕む literally means 'to be pregnant with,' giving the sense that danger lurks within and may emerge. Used in formal or written contexts.
この計画(けいかく)は危険(きけん)を孕(はら)んでいる。
This plan involves danger.
新(あたら)しい技術(ぎじゅつ)は大(おお)きな可能性(かのうせい)とともに危険(きけん)も孕(はら)んでいる。
New technology carries great potential along with inherent risks.
安易(あんい)な判断(はんだん)は大(おお)きな危険(きけん)を孕(はら)むことがある。
Hasty decisions can be fraught with great danger.

USAGE:
The verb 孕(はら)む literally means "to be pregnant" or "to conceive," giving this expression a vivid metaphorical force — danger is "gestating" within the situation. It implies hidden, latent risk rather than obvious danger. Common in news, essays, and formal writing.

PATTERN:
The ～を孕(はら)む pattern is used with other abstract nouns: 矛盾(むじゅん)を孕(はら)む (to contain contradictions), 問題(もんだい)を孕(はら)む (to involve problems).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 危険(きけん)を孕(はら)んだ状況(じょうきょう) (a situation fraught with danger)
  • 大(おお)きな危険(きけん)を孕(はら)む (to carry great risk)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 危険(きけん)が伴(ともな)う: to be accompanied by danger — similar but less literary
  • リスクがある: to have risk — straightforward and neutral
  • 危険(きけん)をはらんでいる: same expression in ている form — most common usage
