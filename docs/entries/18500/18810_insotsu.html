1. leading a group; chaperoning; taking charge of a group
Guiding and being responsible for a group of people, especially students or children on an outing or trip.
先生(せんせい)が生徒(せいと)を引率(いんそつ)する。
The teacher leads the students.
遠足(えんそく)の引率(いんそつ)教員(きょういん)は三名(さんめい)だった。
There were three teachers chaperoning the field trip.
修学旅行(しゅうがくりょこう)で四十人(よんじゅうにん)の生徒(せいと)を引率(いんそつ)するのは大変(たいへん)だった。
Chaperoning forty students on the school trip was exhausting.

引率(いんそつ) means leading and being responsible for a group, especially on outings or trips. Most commonly used in educational settings.

COMPOSITION:
引(いん) (lead, pull) + 率(そつ) (lead, command).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を引率(いんそつ)する: to lead/chaperone ~
  • 引率(いんそつ)教員(きょういん): chaperoning teacher
  • 引率(いんそつ)者(しゃ): group leader, chaperone
  • 引率(いんそつ)のもとで: under the leadership of

USAGE:
The person or group being led takes the particle を: 生徒(せいと)を引率(いんそつ)する (to chaperone students). Primarily used for school excursions, field trips, and organized tours — not for everyday guidance or directions.
