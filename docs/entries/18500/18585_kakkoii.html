格好(かっこう)いい

かっこういい
adjective-i
cool; stylish; good-looking
1. cool; stylish; good-looking; attractive
Describes someone or something that looks impressive, attractive, or admirable. Can refer to physical appearance, behavior, or style. Very common in spoken Japanese.
その服(ふく)、格好(かっこう)いいね。
Those clothes look cool.
彼(かれ)はギターを弾(ひ)く姿(すがた)が格好(かっこう)いい。
He looks cool when he plays guitar.
格好(かっこう)いいことを言(い)うだけじゃなくて、実際(じっさい)に行動(こうどう)してほしい。
I don't just want you to say cool things — I want you to actually take action.

USAGE:
Often shortened to かっこいい in casual speech and writing. The full form 格好(かっこう)いい is the standard dictionary form. Can describe people, objects, actions, and attitudes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 格好(かっこう)いい人(ひと) (a cool/handsome person)
  • 格好(かっこう)いいデザイン (a stylish design)
  • 格好(かっこう)よく見(み)せる (to make something look cool)

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 格好(かっこう)よく
  • Negative: 格好(かっこう)よくない
  • Past: 格好(かっこう)よかった

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • かわいい: cute — focuses on charm and endearment rather than cool impressiveness
  • イケメン: good-looking man — specifically for male physical attractiveness
  • おしゃれ: fashionable — emphasizes fashion sense specifically
