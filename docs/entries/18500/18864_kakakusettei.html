価格(かかく)設定(せってい)

かかくせってい
noun
pricing; price setting
1. pricing; price setting
The act or process of determining the price of a product or service. Used in business, marketing, and everyday consumer contexts.
価格(かかく)設定(せってい)が高(たか)すぎる。
The pricing is too high.
この商品(しょうひん)の価格(かかく)設定(せってい)は適切(てきせつ)だと思(おも)う。
I think the pricing of this product is appropriate.
競合(きょうごう)他社(たしゃ)の動向(どうこう)を見(み)ながら価格(かかく)設定(せってい)を見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to review our pricing while watching the trends of our competitors.

USAGE:
A common business and consumer term. Often used when discussing whether something is expensive or cheap relative to its quality or the market.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 価格(かかく)設定(せってい)が高(たか)い／低(ひく)い (pricing is high/low)
  • 価格(かかく)設定(せってい)を見直(みなお)す (review pricing)
  • 適正(てきせい)な価格(かかく)設定(せってい) (appropriate pricing)
  • 強気(つよき)の価格(かかく)設定(せってい) (aggressive/bold pricing)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 値段(ねだん): the actual price of something — more everyday and concrete
  • 定価(ていか): list price, manufacturer's suggested retail price
