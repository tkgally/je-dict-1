1. to spread; to go viral; to share widely (online)
To spread information, images, or content widely, especially through social media. In modern usage, often means to repost or share content so it reaches many people.
この情報(じょうほう)を拡散(かくさん)してください。
Please spread this information.
動画(どうが)がSNSで一気(いっき)に拡散(かくさん)された。
The video went viral on social media all at once.
デマが拡散(かくさん)しないように注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
We need to be careful so that false rumors don't spread.
2. to diffuse; to disperse; to scatter
In scientific and technical contexts, the physical process of particles, substances, or energy spreading out through a medium.
光(ひかり)がレンズで拡散(かくさん)する。
Light diffuses through the lens.
汚染物質(おせんぶっしつ)が広範囲(こうはんい)に拡散(かくさん)した。
The contaminants dispersed over a wide area.
核兵器(かくへいき)の拡散(かくさん)を防(ふせ)ぐための条約(じょうやく)がある。
There is a treaty to prevent the proliferation of nuclear weapons.

Can be used both transitively (情報(じょうほう)を拡散(かくさん)する, to spread information) and intransitively (情報(じょうほう)が拡散(かくさん)する, information spreads). The online sense (sense 1) has become very common in daily usage — 拡散希望(かくさんきぼう) (please share/RT) is frequently seen on social media. In the political/military sense, 核拡散(かくかくさん) (nuclear proliferation) and 拡散防止(かくさんぼうし) (non-proliferation) are important terms.
