純度(じゅんど)

じゅんど
noun
purity; purity level
1. purity; purity level; degree of purity
The proportion of a substance that is pure, free from impurities or contamination. Used for metals, chemicals, water, and other substances.
純度(じゅんど)の高(たか)い金(きん)だ。
It's gold of high purity.
この水(みず)は純度(じゅんど)99%以上(いじょう)だ。
This water has a purity of over 99%.
半導体(はんどうたい)の製造(せいぞう)には極(きわ)めて純度(じゅんど)の高(たか)いシリコンが必要(ひつよう)とされる。
Extremely high-purity silicon is required for semiconductor manufacturing.

Common collocations: 純度(じゅんど)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い (high/low purity), 高(こう)純度(じゅんど) (high purity — as a compound modifier). The suffix ～度(ど) indicates degree or level, as in 濃度(のうど) (concentration), 精度(せいど) (precision), 鮮度(せんど) (freshness). Gold purity in Japan is expressed as 純度(じゅんど): 24金(きん) (純金(じゅんきん), pure gold), 18金(きん) (75% purity). Related: 純粋(じゅんすい) (pure — used more broadly, including figuratively for people).
