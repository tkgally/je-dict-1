1. closing time; closing of a facility for the day
The daily closing of a facility such as a library, museum, or public building at the end of operating hours.
閉館(へいかん)時間(じかん)は何時(なんじ)ですか。
What time does it close?
図書館(としょかん)は午後(ごご)五時(ごじ)に閉館(へいかん)する。
The library closes at 5 PM.
閉館(へいかん)まであと三十分(さんじゅっぷん)です。
There are thirty minutes until closing.
2. permanent closure; shutting down of a facility
The permanent closing of a building or facility that ceases operation entirely.
老朽化(ろうきゅうか)のため、来年(らいねん)閉館(へいかん)することが決(き)まった。
Due to deterioration, it was decided that the facility will close permanently next year.
長年(ながねん)親(した)しまれた映画館(えいがかん)が閉館(へいかん)して、惜(お)しむ声(こえ)が多(おお)かった。
The beloved cinema that had been enjoyed for many years closed, and many voiced their regret.
閉館(へいかん)した美術館(びじゅつかん)の跡地(あとち)にマンションが建(た)つらしい。
Apparently an apartment building will be built on the site of the closed museum.

Used as both a noun and a suru-verb (閉館(へいかん)する). The 館(かん) refers to a large building or facility — the word is used for libraries (図書館(としょかん)), museums (美術館(びじゅつかん)), cinemas (映画館(えいがかん)), and similar public facilities, but not for ordinary shops (use 閉店(へいてん)) or offices. The antonym is 開館(かいかん) (opening of a facility).
