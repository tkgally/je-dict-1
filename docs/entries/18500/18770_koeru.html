肥(こ)える

こえる
verb
to grow fat; to become fertile; to become refined
1. to grow fat; to become plump
To gain weight and become fat. Used for people and animals.
最近(さいきん)太(ふと)って肥(こ)えてきた。
I've been putting on weight lately.
よく肥(こ)えた豚(ぶた)が市場(いちば)に出(だ)された。
Well-fattened pigs were sent to market.
2. to become fertile; to become rich (of soil)
For land or soil to become nutrient-rich and productive.
肥(こ)えた土(つち)で育(そだ)てた野菜(やさい)はおいしい。
Vegetables grown in rich soil are delicious.
この畑(はたけ)は長年(ながねん)の堆肥(たいひ)で肥(こ)えている。
This field has been enriched by years of composting.
3. to become refined; to become discerning
For one's eye, palate, or ear to develop refined taste through experience.
目(め)が肥(こ)えている人(ひと)は本物(ほんもの)を見分(みわ)けられる。
A person with a discerning eye can tell the genuine article.
舌(した)が肥(こ)えているので、安(やす)いワインでは満足(まんぞく)できない。
I have a refined palate, so I can't be satisfied with cheap wine.

Ichidan verb (下一段(しもいちだん)). Key patterns by sense: Sense 1 (fat): 肥(こ)えた体(からだ) (a plump body), 肥(こ)え太(ふと)る (to fatten up). Sense 2 (soil): 肥(こ)えた土地(とち) (fertile land). Sense 3 (refined): 目(め)が肥(こ)える (to develop a discerning eye), 舌(した)が肥(こ)える (to develop a refined palate), 耳(みみ)が肥(こ)える (to develop a discerning ear). The kanji 肥(こ)える is also read こやす in the transitive form 肥(こ)やす (to fatten, to fertilize). Homophone: 超(こ)える (to exceed), 越(こ)える (to cross over) — different meanings entirely.
