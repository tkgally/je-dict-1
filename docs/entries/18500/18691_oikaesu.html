1. to drive back; to send away; to chase off
To force someone to leave or go back, often by refusing them entry or driving them away.
セールスマンを追(お)い返(かえ)す。
To send a salesman away.
野良猫(のらねこ)を追(お)い返(かえ)した。
I chased the stray cat away.
門(もん)の前(まえ)で不審者(ふしんしゃ)を追(お)い返(かえ)した。
I drove a suspicious person away at the gate.

追(お)い返(かえ)す is a compound verb from 追(お)う (to chase) + 返(かえ)す (to return/send back). It means to forcefully make someone leave or go back.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [person]を追(お)い返(かえ)す

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • セールスマンを追(お)い返(かえ)す: to turn away a salesman
  • 敵(てき)を追(お)い返(かえ)す: to drive back the enemy
  • 不審者(ふしんしゃ)を追(お)い返(かえ)す: to chase off a suspicious person
  • 野良猫(のらねこ)を追(お)い返(かえ)す: to chase away a stray cat

SIMILAR 追(お)い～ COMPOUNDS:

  • 追(お)い出(だ)す: to kick out, to expel (stronger)
  • 追(お)い払(はら)う: to drive away, to shoo
  • 追(お)いかける: to chase after

NUANCE:
追(お)い返(かえ)す implies sending someone back to where they came from. 追(お)い出(だ)す is stronger and means expelling someone entirely. 追(お)い払(はら)う focuses on shooing or brushing away.
