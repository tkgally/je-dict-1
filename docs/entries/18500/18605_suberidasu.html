滑(すべ)り出(だ)す

すべりだす
verb-godan
to start sliding; to get off to a start
1. to start sliding; to begin to slip
To begin sliding or slipping on a surface. Used for physical sliding motion.
車(くるま)が坂道(さかみち)で滑(すべ)り出(だ)した。
The car started sliding on the slope.
氷(こおり)の上(うえ)で足(あし)が滑(すべ)り出(だ)して転(ころ)んだ。
My feet started sliding on the ice and I fell.
雪(ゆき)がゆっくりと屋根(やね)から滑(すべ)り出(だ)した。
The snow slowly began to slide off the roof.
2. to get off to a start; to launch; to get underway
Figuratively, to begin a new venture, season, or period. Often used with adjectives like 好調 (strong) or 順調 (smooth) to describe how something has started.
新(しん)シーズンは好調(こうちょう)に滑(すべ)り出(だ)した。
The new season got off to a strong start.
新商品(しんしょうひん)の売(う)り上(あ)げが順調(じゅんちょう)に滑(すべ)り出(だ)した。
Sales of the new product got off to a smooth start.
新生活(しんせいかつ)は不安(ふあん)もあったが、思(おも)ったより順調(じゅんちょう)に滑(すべ)り出(だ)した。
I had some anxiety about my new life, but it got underway more smoothly than I expected.

USAGE:
Sense 2 is especially common in sports and business contexts. The noun form 滑(すべ)り出(だ)し means "the start" or "the beginning" and is frequently used: 滑(すべ)り出(だ)しは好調(こうちょう)だ (the start is going well).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 好調(こうちょう)な滑(すべ)り出(だ)し (a strong start)
  • 順調(じゅんちょう)に滑(すべ)り出(だ)す (to get off to a smooth start)
  • 滑(すべ)り出(だ)しが良(い)い (to have a good start)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スタートを切(き)る: to make a start — more general
  • 走(はし)り出(だ)す: to start running — physical movement only
  • 出足(であし)が良(い)い: to have a good start — often for sales or attendance
