1. to assign; to allocate; to set aside for a purpose
To designate something (money, time, resources, personnel) for a specific purpose or use.
予算(よさん)を研究(けんきゅう)に充(あ)てる。
To allocate the budget to research.
休日(きゅうじつ)を勉強(べんきょう)に充(あ)てた。
I devoted my day off to studying.
ボーナスの一部(いちぶ)を貯金(ちょきん)に充(あ)てることにした。
I decided to put part of my bonus toward savings.

充(あ)てる means to allocate or assign something to a specific purpose. Written with 充(あ) to distinguish from other あてる verbs.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

KANJI DISTINCTIONS:

  • 充(あ)てる: to allocate, to assign (resources to a purpose)
  • 当(あ)てる: to hit, to guess, to apply
  • 宛(あ)てる: to address (a letter)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を〜に充(あ)てる: to assign X to Y
  • 予算(よさん)を充(あ)てる: to allocate a budget
  • 時間(じかん)を充(あ)てる: to devote time
  • 人員(じんいん)を充(あ)てる: to assign personnel

REGISTER:
Common in business, financial, and planning contexts. Slightly formal in tone.
