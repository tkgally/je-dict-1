誰々(だれだれ)

だれだれ
pronoun
so-and-so; such-and-such a person
1. so-and-so; such-and-such a person; whoever
A placeholder pronoun used when referring to unspecified people or when listing names without specifying who exactly. Used like 'so-and-so' or 'Mr./Ms. X' in English.
誰々(だれだれ)さんが来(く)るらしい。
Apparently so-and-so is coming.
誰々(だれだれ)が何(なに)を担当(たんとう)するか決(き)めよう。
Let's decide who will be in charge of what.
誰々(だれだれ)と誰々(だれだれ)が参加(さんか)するのか教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me exactly who will be participating.

USAGE:
A reduplicated form of 誰(だれ). Used as a placeholder for actual names when the speaker doesn't want to specify or doesn't know exactly who. Similar patterns exist with other question words: 何々(なになに) (such-and-such thing), 何処々々(どこどこ) (such-and-such place).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誰々(だれだれ)さん (Mr./Ms. So-and-so)
  • 誰々(だれだれ)と誰々(だれだれ) (this person and that person)
