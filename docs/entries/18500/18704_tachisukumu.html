1. to stand frozen; to be rooted to the spot
To stand motionless, unable to move, due to fear, shock, or overwhelming emotion.
恐怖(きょうふ)で立(た)ちすくんだ。
I stood frozen with fear.
突然(とつぜん)の大(おお)きな音(おと)に立(た)ちすくんだ。
I froze in place at the sudden loud noise.
目(め)の前(まえ)に熊(くま)が現(あらわ)れて、立(た)ちすくむしかなかった。
A bear appeared right in front of me, and all I could do was stand frozen.

立(た)ちすくむ describes standing motionless, unable to move, typically due to fear, shock, or overwhelming emotion.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

WORD FORMATION:
立(た)つ (to stand) + すくむ (to cower, to stiffen). The verb すくむ means to become rigid or paralyzed.

ASPECT (ている):
立(た)ちすくんでいる means "is standing frozen" — a continuing state of paralysis.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 恐怖(きょうふ)で立(た)ちすくむ: to freeze with fear
  • ショックで立(た)ちすくむ: to stand frozen in shock
  • 圧倒(あっとう)されて立(た)ちすくむ: to be rooted to the spot in awe

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 足(あし)がすくむ: legs freeze (with fear)
  • 身(み)がすくむ: body freezes up
  • 動(うご)けなくなる: to become unable to move
