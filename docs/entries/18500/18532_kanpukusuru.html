1. to be deeply impressed; to admire greatly; to be filled with admiration
To feel deep admiration or respect for someone's skill, effort, character, or achievement. A somewhat formal expression that conveys genuine, heartfelt admiration.
彼(かれ)の努力(どりょく)に感服(かんぷく)した。
I was deeply impressed by his effort.
その職人(しょくにん)の技(わざ)には感服(かんぷく)するばかりだ。
I can only admire that craftsman's skill.
困難(こんなん)な状況(じょうきょう)でも前向(まえむ)きに取(と)り組(く)む姿勢(しせい)に感服(かんぷく)しました。
I was deeply impressed by the positive attitude of tackling things even in difficult situations.

感服(かんぷく)する expresses deep, heartfelt admiration for someone's skill, effort, or character. It is more formal and emphatic than 感心(かんしん)する, which conveys milder impressedness.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～に感服(かんぷく)する: to be deeply impressed by ~
  • 感服(かんぷく)するばかりだ: I can only admire
  • 感服(かんぷく)いたしました: I'm truly impressed (humble/keigo form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 感心(かんしん)する: to be impressed (milder, everyday)
  • 敬服(けいふく)する: to admire with respect (similarly formal)
  • 脱帽(だつぼう)する: to take one's hat off to (figurative admiration)

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal. Often used in written language, speeches, or polite conversation. The humble form 感服(かんぷく)いたします is common in business settings.
