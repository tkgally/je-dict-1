罰金(ばっきん)

ばっきん
noun
fine; monetary penalty
1. fine; monetary penalty
A sum of money imposed as punishment for breaking a law, rule, or regulation. Used in both legal and informal contexts.
罰金(ばっきん)を払(はら)う。
To pay a fine.
駐車違反(ちゅうしゃいはん)で罰金(ばっきん)を取(と)られた。
I was fined for a parking violation.
飲酒運転(いんしゅうんてん)には高額(こうがく)の罰金(ばっきん)が科(か)せられる可能性(かのうせい)がある。
Drunk driving can result in a heavy fine.

Common collocations: 罰金(ばっきん)を払(はら)う (to pay a fine), 罰金(ばっきん)を科(か)す / 罰金(ばっきん)が科(か)せられる (to impose a fine / to have a fine imposed), 罰金(ばっきん)刑(けい) (fine as a criminal penalty). In Japanese law, 罰金(ばっきん) is a formal criminal penalty, distinct from 反則金(はんそくきん) (infraction penalty — the administrative fine for minor traffic violations). Informally, 罰金(ばっきん) is used casually for any monetary penalty, even playful ones among friends. Related: 科料(かりょう) (minor fine — a lighter criminal penalty), 違約金(いやくきん) (penalty for breach of contract).
