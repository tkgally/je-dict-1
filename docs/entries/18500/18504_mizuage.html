1. landing a catch; unloading fish from a boat
The act of bringing caught fish or seafood from a fishing boat to the harbor. Also refers to the quantity or value of fish landed.
今朝(けさ)の水揚(みずあ)げは豊富(ほうふ)だった。
This morning's catch was plentiful.
港(みなと)でマグロが水揚(みずあ)げされた。
Tuna was landed at the harbor.
この漁港(ぎょこう)はサンマの水揚(みずあ)げ量(りょう)が日本一(にほんいち)だ。
This fishing port has the highest saury landings in Japan.
2. sales revenue; gross sales; proceeds
The total amount of money earned from sales, especially in retail or market contexts.
先月(せんげつ)の水揚(みずあ)げは好調(こうちょう)だった。
Last month's sales were strong.
年間(ねんかん)水揚(みずあ)げ高(だか)が過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)を記録(きろく)した。
The annual gross sales set a new record.
セールのおかげで、今月(こんげつ)の水揚(みずあ)げが伸(の)びた。
Thanks to the sale, this month's revenue increased.

The fishing sense (landing a catch) is the primary meaning and appears frequently in news about the fishing industry.

SENSE 1 — FISHING:

  • 水揚(みずあ)げ量(りょう): amount of catch landed
  • 水揚(みずあ)げ高(だか): value of the catch
  • 水揚(みずあ)げする: to land/unload fish
  • 水揚(みずあ)げ港(こう): landing port

SENSE 2 — SALES REVENUE:
Used for gross sales or proceeds, but 売(う)り上(あ)げ is more common in general business. 水揚(みずあ)げ高(だか) can refer to either fishing revenue or general sales depending on context.

FORMATION:
水(みず) (water) + 揚(あ)げ (raising up) — originally the act of lifting goods out of the water onto land.
