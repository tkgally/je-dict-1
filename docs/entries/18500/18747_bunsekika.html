分析家(ぶんせきか)

ぶんせきか
noun
analyst
1. analyst; person skilled at analysis
A person who analyzes information, data, or situations as a profession or by disposition. Used for financial analysts, political analysts, and also in psychology for psychoanalysts.
政治(せいじ)分析家(ぶんせきか)の見解(けんかい)を聞(き)く。
To hear the views of a political analyst.
彼女(かのじょ)は鋭(するど)い分析家(ぶんせきか)として知(し)られている。
She is known as a sharp analyst.
市場(しじょう)分析家(ぶんせきか)たちは来年(らいねん)の景気回復(けいきかいふく)を予測(よそく)している。
Market analysts are predicting an economic recovery next year.

Compound of 分析(ぶんせき) (analysis) + 家(か) (expert/professional suffix).

COMMON TYPES:

  • 政治(せいじ)分析家(ぶんせきか): political analyst
  • 市場(しじょう)分析家(ぶんせきか): market analyst
  • 精神(せいしん)分析家(ぶんせきか): psychoanalyst
  • 軍事(ぐんじ)分析家(ぶんせきか): military analyst

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • アナリスト: analyst (English loanword, common in business/finance)
  • 評論家(ひょうろんか): critic, commentator (more focused on opinions than data)
  • 研究者(けんきゅうしゃ): researcher (broader, academic focus)

The suffix 家(か) implies expertise or professional dedication. It appears in many occupation words: 作家(さっか) (author), 画家(がか) (painter), 専門家(せんもんか) (expert).
