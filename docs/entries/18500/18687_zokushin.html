1. continued rise; further advance; continued gain
A continued upward movement, especially in stock prices, market indexes, or other numerical measures. Used primarily in financial and economic reporting.
株価(かぶか)が続伸(ぞくしん)した。
Stock prices continued to rise.
日経(にっけい)平均(へいきん)は3日(にち)連続(れんぞく)で続伸(ぞくしん)している。
The Nikkei average has risen for three consecutive days.
好決算(こうけっさん)を受(う)けて、同社(どうしゃ)の株(かぶ)は大幅(おおはば)に続伸(ぞくしん)し、年初(ねんしょ)来高値(らいたかね)を更新(こうしん)した。
Following strong earnings, the company's stock continued to advance sharply, reaching a new high for the year.

Functions as a する verb: 続伸(ぞくしん)する (to continue rising). A financial journalism term. Compound: 続(ぞく) (continue) + 伸(しん) (extend/grow). The opposite is 続落(ぞくらく) (continued decline). Appears in headlines and financial news, e.g., 「日経(にっけい)平均(へいきん)、続伸(ぞくしん)」(Nikkei average continues to rise). Related: 続落(ぞくらく) (continued fall), 反発(はんぱつ) (rebound), 反落(はんらく) (pullback).
