耐久(たいきゅう)

たいきゅう
noun
durability; endurance
1. durability; endurance
The ability to withstand wear, pressure, or damage over time. Used for both physical objects and human endurance.
耐久性(たいきゅうせい)に優(すぐ)れた素材(そざい)だ。
It's a material with excellent durability.
この製品(せいひん)は耐久(たいきゅう)テストに合格(ごうかく)している。
This product has passed durability testing.
耐久(たいきゅう)レースでは体力(たいりょく)だけでなく精神力(せいしんりょく)も試(ため)される。
In endurance races, not only physical strength but also mental fortitude is tested.

Almost always appears in compounds or as a modifier: 耐久性(たいきゅうせい) (durability — the most common form), 耐久(たいきゅう)消費財(しょうひざい) (durable consumer goods), 耐久(たいきゅう)レース (endurance race), 耐久(たいきゅう)年数(ねんすう) (service life, expected lifespan). The prefix 耐(たい)～ means "resistant to" and appears in: 耐水(たいすい) (waterproof), 耐熱(たいねつ) (heat-resistant), 耐震(たいしん) (earthquake-resistant). Related: 丈夫(じょうぶ) (sturdy — everyday word for toughness), 頑丈(がんじょう) (robust).
