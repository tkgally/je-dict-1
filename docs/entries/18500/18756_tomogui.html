1. cannibalism among animals; eating one's own kind
The behavior of animals eating members of their own species. A natural phenomenon observed in many animal species.
ハムスターは共食(ともぐ)いをすることがある。
Hamsters sometimes engage in cannibalism.
ストレスが原因(げんいん)で魚(さかな)の共食(ともぐ)いが起(お)きることがある。
Stress can cause fish to eat each other.
2. infighting; undermining allies; competing destructively within the same group
Used figuratively for situations where members of the same group, party, or industry harm each other through competition, rather than cooperating against external rivals.
過度(かど)な価格競争(かかくきょうそう)は業界内(ぎょうかいない)の共食(ともぐ)いにつながる。
Excessive price competition leads to destructive infighting within the industry.
自社(じしゃ)ブランドの新商品(しんしょうひん)が既存(きそん)商品(しょうひん)と共食(ともぐ)いしている。
The company's new product is cannibalizing its existing products.
与党内(よとうない)の共食(ともぐ)いが続(つづ)いている。
Infighting within the ruling party continues.
同(おな)じ地域(ちいき)に店舗(てんぽ)を出(だ)しすぎると共食(ともぐ)いになる。
Opening too many stores in the same area leads to cannibalization.

A compound of 共(とも) (together/mutual) + 食(く)い (eating, from 食(く)う). Can function as a する verb: 共食(ともぐ)いする.

LITERAL SENSE:
Refers to animals eating members of their own species. Common in discussions of animal behavior, pet care (e.g., hamsters), and ecology.

FIGURATIVE/BUSINESS SENSE:
Corresponds to the English marketing term "cannibalization" — when a company's own new product takes sales from its existing products. Also used for destructive infighting within a political party or industry.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 共食(ともぐ)いをする: to cannibalize each other
  • 共食(ともぐ)いになる: to result in cannibalization
  • 共食(ともぐ)いが起(お)きる: cannibalization occurs
  • 業界内(ぎょうかいない)の共食(ともぐ)い: cannibalization within an industry
