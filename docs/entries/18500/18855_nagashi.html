流(なが)し

ながし
noun
kitchen sink
1. kitchen sink
The sink area in a kitchen, including the basin and the surrounding counter space used for washing dishes and food preparation.
流(なが)しに皿(さら)を置(お)く。
I put the plates in the sink.
流(なが)しの排水口(はいすいこう)が詰(つ)まってしまった。
The kitchen sink drain got clogged.
食(た)べ終(お)わった食器(しょっき)は流(なが)しに持(も)っていってね。
Please bring the used dishes to the sink when you're done eating.
2. cruising (for customers)
A taxi driving around without a passenger, looking to pick someone up. Also used for street musicians or performers who move from place to place.
流(なが)しのタクシーを拾(ひろ)う。
I'll hail a cruising taxi.
この辺(あた)りは流(なが)しのタクシーが多(おお)いので、すぐ捕(つか)まる。
There are many cruising taxis around here, so you can catch one quickly.
流(なが)しの演奏家(えんそうか)が店(みせ)を回(まわ)って歌(うた)っていた。
A roaming musician was going around shops singing.

Derived from the verb 流(なが)す (to let flow). For sense 1, the word originally referred to the drain channel in traditional Japanese kitchens. In modern usage, 台所(だいどころ)の流(なが)し specifically means the kitchen sink. The more technical term is 流(なが)し台(だい) (sink unit/counter). For sense 2, 流(なが)しのタクシー is the standard phrase for a taxi looking for fares on the street, as opposed to one waiting at a taxi stand (駅前(えきまえ)のタクシー乗(の)り場(ば)).
