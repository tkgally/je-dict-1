1. splendid; brilliant; spectacular; glorious
Describes achievements, careers, or events that are impressively successful and attract admiration. Implies public visibility and remarkable accomplishment.
華々(はなばな)しいデビューを飾(かざ)った。
She made a spectacular debut.
彼(かれ)は華々(はなばな)しい経歴(けいれき)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
He has a brilliant career history.
華々(はなばな)しい活躍(かつやく)の裏(うら)には、地道(じみち)な努力(どりょく)があった。
Behind the spectacular achievements was steady, quiet effort.

華々(はなばな)しい is an i-adjective meaning splendid or spectacular. Written with the repeated kanji 華(はな), emphasizing brilliance and splendor.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 華々(はなばな)しいデビュー: spectacular debut
  • 華々(はなばな)しい活躍(かつやく): brilliant performance/achievement
  • 華々(はなばな)しい経歴(けいれき): impressive career
  • 華々(はなばな)しい成果(せいか): splendid results

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 華々(はなばな)しく
  • 華々(はなばな)しく散(ち)る: to fall/die gloriously
  • 華々(はなばな)しく飾(かざ)る: to adorn splendidly

CONTRAST:

  • 華々(はなばな)しい: splendid, spectacular (public, attention-getting)
  • 地味(じみ): plain, unassuming (quiet, behind-the-scenes)
