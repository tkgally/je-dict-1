1. to awaken; to wake up; to become alert
To wake from sleep or become sharply alert and aware. Also used figuratively for becoming aware of a truth or reality.
コーヒーで覚醒(かくせい)する。
I wake up with coffee.
意識(いしき)が覚醒(かくせい)した。
Consciousness was restored.
麻酔(ますい)から覚醒(かくせい)するまで時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to wake up from the anesthesia.
2. to awaken to a talent or power; to be aroused
To discover or activate a hidden ability, potential, or awareness. Common in fiction and self-improvement contexts.
才能(さいのう)が覚醒(かくせい)した。
A talent was awakened.
危機(きき)に直面(ちょくめん)して、リーダーとしての資質(ししつ)が覚醒(かくせい)した。
Facing a crisis, their leadership qualities were awakened.
主人公(しゅじんこう)が隠(かく)された力(ちから)に覚醒(かくせい)する場面(ばめん)が一番(いちばん)好(す)きだ。
My favorite scene is when the protagonist awakens to their hidden power.

A suru verb (覚醒(かくせい)しない / 覚醒(かくせい)します / 覚醒(かくせい)して). Related compound: 覚醒剤(かくせいざい) (stimulant drug, amphetamine — a controlled substance in Japan). In medical contexts, 覚醒(かくせい) refers to a patient regaining consciousness. In anime and manga, 覚醒(かくせい) is a popular trope where a character unlocks hidden abilities. Different from 目覚(めざ)める, which is the everyday word for waking up from sleep.
