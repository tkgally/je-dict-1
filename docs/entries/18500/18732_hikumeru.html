1. to lower; to reduce; to bring down
To make something lower in level, degree, volume, or position. Used for physical height, sound volume, risk levels, standards, etc.
声(こえ)を低(ひく)めて話(はな)す。
To speak in a lowered voice.
姿勢(しせい)を低(ひく)めて進(すす)む。
To advance with a low posture.
事故(じこ)のリスクを低(ひく)めるために安全対策(あんぜんたいさく)を強化(きょうか)した。
Safety measures were strengthened to reduce the risk of accidents.

Ichidan verb. The transitive counterpart of 低(ひく)まる (to become lower). Common collocations: 声(こえ)を低(ひく)める (lower one's voice), リスクを低(ひく)める (reduce risk), ハードルを低(ひく)める (lower the bar/hurdle), 敷居(しきい)を低(ひく)める (lower the threshold, make something more accessible). Compare with 下(さ)げる, which is more general and everyday; 低(ひく)める specifically focuses on making something lower in height, level, or degree.
