1. change of plans; schedule change
Altering a previously set plan, appointment, or schedule. Common in both business and everyday contexts.
予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)のお知(し)らせです。
This is a notice of a schedule change.
急(きゅう)な予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)で申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません。
I apologize for the sudden change of plans.
天候(てんこう)の影響(えいきょう)で予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)を余儀(よぎ)なくされた。
We were forced to change our plans due to the weather.

USAGE:
A versatile compound used in announcements, apologies, and everyday conversation. Often used with する to mean "to change plans."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)をする (to change plans)
  • 急(きゅう)な予定(よてい)変更(へんこう) (sudden change of plans)
  • 予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)のお知(し)らせ (notice of schedule change)
  • 予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)を余儀(よぎ)なくされる (to be forced to change plans)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スケジュール変更(へんこう): schedule change — more casual, common in business settings
  • 日程(にってい)変更(へんこう): date change — specifically about changing the date rather than the plan itself
