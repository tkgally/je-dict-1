すごく

すごく
adverb
very; extremely; incredibly
1. very; extremely; incredibly; awfully
An intensifier used to emphasize the degree of an adjective or verb. Widely used in casual spoken Japanese. Originally the adverbial form of the adjective すごい, but now functions independently as an adverb.
すごく楽(たの)しかった。
It was really fun.
今日(きょう)はすごく寒(さむ)いね。
It's extremely cold today, isn't it.
すごく頑張(がんば)ったのに結果(けっか)が出(で)なかった。
I tried incredibly hard, but didn't get results.

The adverbial form of すごい (amazing/terrible). In casual speech, すごい is also widely used as an adverb (すごい楽(たの)しい instead of すごく楽(たの)しい), though this is grammatically nonstandard. すごく is the standard adverbial form. Compare with とても (very, neutral register) and 非常(ひじょう)に (extremely, formal). すごく is informal and used primarily in spoken language.
