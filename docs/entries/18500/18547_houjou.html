豊穣(ほうじょう)

ほうじょう
na-adjective / noun
abundant harvest; fertility; richness
1. abundant harvest; fertility; bountifulness; richness
Describes an abundant harvest or the state of being rich and productive. Used literally for agricultural abundance and figuratively for cultural or creative richness.
豊穣(ほうじょう)の秋(あき)を祝(いわ)う。
To celebrate an abundant autumn harvest.
豊穣(ほうじょう)な大地(だいち)がこの地域(ちいき)の誇(ほこ)りだ。
The fertile land is the pride of this region.
村(むら)の人(ひと)たちは毎年(まいとし)、五穀豊穣(ごこくほうじょう)を祈(いの)る祭(まつ)りを行(おこな)う。
The villagers hold a festival every year to pray for an abundant grain harvest.

A literary, somewhat elevated word. The compound 五穀豊穣(ごこくほうじょう) (abundant harvest of the five grains) is a very common set phrase used in Shinto prayers and harvest festivals. The five grains traditionally refer to 米(こめ) (rice), 麦(むぎ) (wheat/barley), 粟(あわ) (millet), 豆(まめ) (beans), and 黍(きび) (proso millet). Also used figuratively: 豊穣(ほうじょう)な文化(ぶんか) (rich culture), 豊穣(ほうじょう)な想像力(そうぞうりょく) (rich imagination).
