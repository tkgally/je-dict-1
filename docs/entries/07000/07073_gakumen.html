額面(がくめん)

がくめん
noun
face value; nominal value
1. face value; nominal value; par value
The value printed on a security, bond, or currency, as opposed to its market value.
この株(かぶ)の額面(がくめん)は100円(えん)だ。
The face value of this stock is 100 yen.
額面(がくめん)と市場(しじょう)価格(かかく)は異(こと)なる。
Face value and market price are different.
額面(がくめん)1万(まん)円(えん)の商品券(しょうひんけん)をもらった。
I received a gift certificate with a face value of 10,000 yen.
2. at face value; literally
Used figuratively to mean taking something at its stated or apparent value.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)を額面(がくめん)どおりに受(う)け取(と)ってはいけない。
Don't take his words at face value.
額面(がくめん)どおりに受(う)け取(と)ると、彼(かれ)は賛成(さんせい)しているように聞(き)こえる。
Taken at face value, it sounds like he agrees.
その発言(はつげん)を額面(がくめん)どおりに信(しん)じるのは危(あぶ)ない。
It's risky to believe that statement at face value.

額面(がくめん) literally means "the face/surface of an amount" — the value printed or written on something.

SENSE 1 — FINANCIAL:
The stated value on securities, bonds, stamps, gift certificates, and currency.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 額面(がくめん)金額(きんがく): face amount
  • 額面(がくめん)価格(かかく): par value
  • 額面(がくめん)以上(いじょう): above par
  • 額面(がくめん)以下(いか): below par

SENSE 2 — FIGURATIVE:
額面(がくめん)どおり means "at face value" or "literally" — accepting something as it appears without looking deeper.

KEY EXPRESSION:

  • 額面(がくめん)どおりに受(う)け取(と)る: to take at face value (often used with a warning not to)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 時価(じか): market value, current price
  • 定価(ていか): list price, retail price
