息(いき)を潜(ひそ)める

いきをひそめる
expression, verb phrase
to hold one's breath; to lie low; to keep quiet

Definitions

1. to hold one's breath; to breathe quietly
To breathe as quietly as possible, often while hiding or waiting tensely.
2. to lie low; to keep a low profile
To remain quiet and unnoticed, avoiding attention while waiting for a situation to pass.

Examples

物陰(ものかげ)に隠(かく)れて息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めていた。
I hid in the shadows, holding my breath.
全員(ぜんいん)が息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めて発表(はっぴょう)を待(ま)った。
Everyone held their breath waiting for the announcement.
騒動(そうどう)が収(おさ)まるまで息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めていよう。
Let's lie low until the commotion dies down.

Notes

IDIOM STRUCTURE:
息(いき) (breath) + を + 潜(ひそ)める (to conceal, to lower)

The verb 潜(ひそ)める means to suppress, conceal, or lower (one's voice, presence, etc.).

GRAMMAR:
〜して息(いき)を潜(ひそ)める - to hold one's breath while doing ~
息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めて + verb - doing ~ while holding one's breath

CONJUGATION:

  • 息(いき)を潜(ひそ)める (present)
  • 息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めた (past)
  • 息(いき)を潜(ひそ)めて (te-form, often used adverbially)

NUANCE:
Implies tension, fear, or deliberate concealment. Often used in suspenseful situations or when trying to avoid detection. The figurative sense (lie low) suggests strategic waiting.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 息(いき)を止(と)める - to stop breathing (more literal)
  • 息(いき)を殺(ころ)す - to hold one's breath (stronger, almost killing one's breath)
  • 身(み)を潜(ひそ)める - to hide oneself
  • 鳴(な)りを潜(ひそ)める - to keep quiet, to lie low
