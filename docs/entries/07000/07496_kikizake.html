利(き)き酒(ざけ)

ききざけ
noun
sake tasting

Definitions

1. sake tasting; sake sampling
The practice of tasting and evaluating different types of sake, often done professionally to assess quality or recreationally to appreciate varieties.

Examples

酒蔵(さかぐら)で利(き)き酒(ざけ)をした。
I did a sake tasting at a brewery.
利(き)き酒(ざけ)セットを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered a sake tasting set.
利(き)き酒(ざけ)で銘柄(めいがら)を当(あ)てる。
I identify the brand through sake tasting.
日本酒(にほんしゅ)の利(き)き酒(ざけ)イベントに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a sake tasting event.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
利(き)く (to taste/distinguish) + 酒(さけ) (sake/alcohol) → 酒(ざけ) (rendaku)

ALSO WRITTEN:
利(き)き酒(ざけ) is sometimes written as 唎酒(ききざけ) using a specialized kanji for tasting.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 利(き)き酒師(ざけし): sake sommelier
  • 酒蔵(さかぐら): sake brewery
  • 銘柄(めいがら): brand (of sake)
  • 日本酒(にほんしゅ): Japanese sake
  • 地酒(じざけ): local sake

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利(き)き酒(ざけ)をする: do sake tasting
  • 利(き)き酒(ざけ)セット: sake tasting set (flight)
  • 利(き)き酒(ざけ)イベント: sake tasting event
  • 利(き)き酒(ざけ)大会(たいかい): sake tasting competition

CULTURAL NOTE:
Sake tasting is a popular activity at Japanese breweries (酒蔵(さかぐら)) and sake festivals. Many regions have their own specialty 地酒(じざけ) (local sake), and tasting tours are a form of regional tourism.
