仕入(しい)れ

しいれ
noun
stocking; purchasing; procurement
1. stocking; purchasing; procurement
The act of purchasing goods or materials for resale or business use. Also refers to the wholesale price or cost of goods purchased.
毎朝(まいあさ)市場(いちば)で新鮮(しんせん)な魚(さかな)を仕入(しい)れる。
Every morning, I purchase fresh fish at the market.
仕入(しい)れ値(ね)が上(あ)がって利益(りえき)が減(へ)った。
The purchase price went up and profits decreased.
2. acquiring information; gathering material
Figuratively, the process of gathering information, knowledge, or material for a particular purpose.
記者(きしゃ)は情報(じょうほう)の仕入(しい)れに走(はし)り回(まわ)った。
The reporter ran around gathering information.

DERIVED FROM:
The verb 仕入(しい)れる (to stock up, to purchase for business).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕入(しい)れ値(ね) - purchase price, wholesale cost
  • 仕入(しい)れ先(さき) - supplier, source of goods
  • 仕入(しい)れ担当(たんとう) - purchasing manager
  • 仕入(しい)れコスト - procurement cost

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

  • 仕入(しい)れが入(はい)る - stock arrives
  • 仕入(しい)れを確保(かくほ)する - to secure supplies
  • 仕入(しい)れルート - supply route/channel

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 仕入(しい)れる - to purchase, stock up (verb)
  • 卸(おろし) - wholesale
  • 在庫(ざいこ) - inventory, stock
  • 発注(はっちゅう) - placing an order
  • 調達(ちょうたつ) - procurement

FIGURATIVE USE:
ネタの仕入(しい)れ - gathering material (for writing, comedy, etc.)

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in business contexts and everyday commercial activities.
