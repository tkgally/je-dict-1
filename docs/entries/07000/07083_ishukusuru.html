萎縮(いしゅく)する

いしゅくする
verb
to shrink; to feel intimidated

Definitions

1. to shrink; to atrophy; to wither
Physical shrinking, often used in medical or scientific contexts.
2. to feel intimidated; to cower; to lose confidence
Psychological shrinking, becoming timid or inhibited.

Examples

筋肉(きんにく)を使(つか)わないと萎縮(いしゅく)してしまう。
If you don't use your muscles, they will atrophy.
上司(じょうし)の前(まえ)で萎縮(いしゅく)してしまい、意見(いけん)が言(い)えなかった。
I felt intimidated in front of my boss and couldn't voice my opinion.
厳(きび)しい批判(ひはん)を受(う)けて、チームの雰囲気(ふんいき)が萎縮(いしゅく)した。
After receiving harsh criticism, the team's atmosphere became inhibited.

Notes

萎縮(いしゅく)する is an intransitive する verb (自動詞(じどうし)) with both physical and psychological meanings.

TWO MAIN USES:
1. Physical: Atrophy or shrinking of organs, muscles, or tissue
2. Psychological: Becoming timid, losing confidence due to pressure

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 筋肉(きんにく)が萎縮(いしゅく)する - muscles atrophy
  • プレッシャーで萎縮(いしゅく)する - to feel intimidated by pressure
  • 萎縮(いしゅく)せずに発言(はつげん)する - to speak up without being intimidated
