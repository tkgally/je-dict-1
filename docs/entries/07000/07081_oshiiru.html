押(お)し入(い)る

おしいる
verb
to break in; to force one's way in

Definitions

1. to break in; to force one's way in; to burst in
Used for unauthorized entry, often with criminal or aggressive implications. Intransitive verb formed from 押す (to push) + 入る (to enter).

Examples

強盗(ごうとう)が家(いえ)に押(お)し入(い)った。
A robber broke into the house.
不審者(ふしんしゃ)が店(みせ)に押(お)し入(い)り、現金(げんきん)を奪(うば)った。
A suspicious person broke into the store and stole cash.
警察(けいさつ)が容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の部屋(へや)に押(お)し入(い)った。
The police forced their way into the suspect's room.

Notes

押(お)し入(い)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) formed from 押(お)す (to push) + 入(い)る (to enter).

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [place]に押(お)し入(い)る - to break into [place]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家(いえ)に押(お)し入(い)る - to break into a house
  • 店(みせ)に押(お)し入(い)る - to break into a store
  • 強盗(ごうとう)が押(お)し入(い)る - a robber breaks in

RELATED:

  • 押(お)し込(こ)む - to force into
  • 侵入(しんにゅう)する - to intrude (more formal)
