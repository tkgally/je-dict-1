歯応(はごた)え

はごたえ
noun
chewiness; crunchy texture; bite
1. chewiness; crunchy texture; satisfying bite
The resistance or texture felt when biting into food; the satisfying sensation of chewing.
この野菜(やさい)は歯応(はごた)えがある。
These vegetables have a nice crunch.
アルデンテのパスタは歯応(はごた)えがいい。
Al dente pasta has a good bite.
2. challenge; satisfaction (figurative)
Something that provides resistance or challenge, making it worthwhile.
歯応(はごた)えのある相手(あいて)と対戦(たいせん)したい。
I want to compete against a challenging opponent.

WORD FORMATION:
歯(は) (tooth) + 応(こた)え (response) → response to teeth = chewiness

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 歯応(はごた)えがある (has a good bite/is crunchy)
  • 歯応(はごた)えがいい (has a nice texture)
  • 歯応(はごた)えのある (chewy, crunchy; challenging)

RELATED ～応(ごた)え WORDS:

  • 読(よ)み応(ごた)え (worth reading)
  • 見応(みごた)え (worth seeing)
  • 聞(き)き応(ごた)え (worth listening to)
  • 食(く)い応(ごた)え (filling, satisfying to eat)

USAGE NOTE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is common in competitive contexts, describing opponents or tasks that provide satisfying resistance.
