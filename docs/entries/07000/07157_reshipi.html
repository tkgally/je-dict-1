レシピ

れしぴ
noun
recipe
1. recipe; cooking instructions
A set of instructions for preparing a particular dish, including a list of ingredients and steps.
このレシピは簡単(かんたん)でおいしい。
This recipe is easy and delicious.
祖母(そぼ)のレシピを受(う)け継(つ)いだ。
I inherited my grandmother's recipe.
レシピ通(どお)りに作(つく)ったのに、なぜか失敗(しっぱい)した。
I made it exactly according to the recipe, but somehow it failed.

ORIGIN:
From English 'recipe'.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • レシピを見(み)る - to look at a recipe
  • レシピ通(どお)りに - according to the recipe
  • レシピを考(かんが)える - to think up a recipe
  • オリジナルレシピ - original recipe
  • 定番(ていばん)レシピ - standard/classic recipe
  • レシピサイト - recipe website

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 材料(ざいりょう) - ingredients
  • 分量(ぶんりょう) - quantities/measurements
  • 手順(てじゅん) - steps/procedure
  • 調理(ちょうり)時間(じかん) - cooking time
