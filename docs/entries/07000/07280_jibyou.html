持病(じびょう)

じびょう
noun
chronic illness; chronic condition

Definitions

1. chronic illness; chronic condition
An ongoing medical condition that one has had for a long time and may continue to have.

Examples

持病(じびょう)の喘息(ぜんそく)が悪化(あっか)した。
My chronic asthma got worse.
持病(じびょう)があるので定期的(ていきてき)に病院(びょういん)に通(かよ)っている。
I go to the hospital regularly because I have a chronic condition.
持病(じびょう)がなければ保険(ほけん)に入(はい)りやすい。
It's easier to get insurance if you don't have a chronic illness.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
持(も)つ (to have) + 病(びょう) (illness) → illness one has = chronic condition

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 持病(じびょう)がある - to have a chronic condition
  • 持病(じびょう)を抱(かか)える - to be living with a chronic illness
  • 持病(じびょう)が悪化(あっか)する - for a chronic condition to worsen

COMMON CHRONIC CONDITIONS:

  • 糖尿病(とうにょうびょう) - diabetes
  • 高血圧(こうけつあつ) - high blood pressure
  • 喘息(ぜんそく) - asthma
  • 腰痛(ようつう) - chronic back pain

USAGE NOTE:
Often mentioned when filling out medical forms, insurance applications, or when explaining one's health situation.
