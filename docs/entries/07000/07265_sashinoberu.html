差(さ)し伸(の)べる

さしのべる
verb (ichidan, transitive)
to extend; to reach out; to offer (help)
1. to extend; to stretch out (a hand)
To physically reach out one's hand or arm.
彼女(かのじょ)は手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べて握手(あくしゅ)した。
She extended her hand and shook hands.
2. to offer; to extend (help, support)
To provide assistance or support to someone in need.
困(こま)っている人(ひと)に救(すく)いの手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる。
To extend a helping hand to those in need.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)に支援(しえん)の手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べたい。
I want to extend support to the disaster victims.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを差(さ)し伸(の)べる

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 差(さ)す (to extend) + 伸(の)べる (to stretch)

CONJUGATION:

  • 差(さ)し伸(の)べる → 差(さ)し伸(の)べない (negative)
  • 差(さ)し伸(の)べる → 差(さ)し伸(の)べて (te-form)
  • 差(さ)し伸(の)べる → 差(さ)し伸(の)べた (past)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる - to extend one's hand
  • 救(すく)いの手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる - to extend a helping hand
  • 支援(しえん)の手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる - to offer support

REGISTER:
Slightly formal; often used in contexts involving aid, charity, or compassionate gestures.
