空(むな)しい

むなしい
i-adjective
empty; futile; vain

Definitions

1. empty; hollow; void
Describing something that lacks substance, meaning, or emotional fulfillment.
2. futile; vain; pointless
Describing efforts or actions that are meaningless or produce no results.

Examples

一人(ひとり)で過(す)ごす夜(よる)は空(むな)しい。
Nights spent alone feel empty.
努力(どりょく)が報(むく)われず、空(むな)しさを感(かん)じた。
My efforts went unrewarded, and I felt a sense of futility.
何(なに)を言(い)っても空(むな)しい抵抗(ていこう)だった。
No matter what I said, it was futile resistance.

Notes

FORMS:

  • 空(むな)しい (dictionary/attributive)
  • 空(むな)しさ (noun form: emptiness, futility)
  • 空(むな)しく (adverbial: in vain, emptily)

KANJI VARIATIONS:
Can also be written as 虚(むな)しい, though 空(むな)しい is more common.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 空(むな)しく終(お)わる - to end in vain
  • 空(むな)しさを感(かん)じる - to feel emptiness
  • 空(むな)しい努力(どりょく) - futile effort

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • やるせない - forlorn, helpless (more about suppressed emotions)
  • 物足(ものた)りない - unsatisfying, lacking (something is missing)
  • 儚(はかな)い - fleeting, ephemeral

REGISTER:
Neutral to literary. Common in both casual and written contexts.
