自由席(じゆうせき)

じゆうせき
noun
unreserved seat
1. unreserved seat; non-reserved seat
A seat that is available on a first-come, first-served basis without prior reservation.
自由席(じゆうせき)は早(はや)い者(もの)勝(が)ちだ。
Unreserved seats are first-come, first-served.
早(はや)い者(もの)勝(が)ち is a set phrase: "first come, first served."
自由席(じゆうせき)なら予約(よやく)なしで乗(の)れる。
With unreserved seats, you can ride without a reservation.
自由席(じゆうせき)車両(しゃりょう)は混(こ)んでいた。
The unreserved seat car was crowded.
自由席(じゆうせき)の方(ほう)が指定席(していせき)より安(やす)い。
Unreserved seats are cheaper than reserved seats.
朝(あさ)早(はや)い電車(でんしゃ)なら自由席(じゆうせき)でも座(すわ)れると思(おも)う。
If you take an early morning train, I think you can get a seat even in the unreserved cars.

FORMATION:
自由(じゆう) (free, unrestricted) + 席(せき) (seat)

CONTRAST:

  • 自由席(じゆうせき) - unreserved seat (cheaper, but no guaranteed seat)
  • 指定席(していせき) - reserved seat (a guaranteed seat, for an extra fee)
  • グリーン車(しゃ) - Green Car (the first-class car; all seats reserved)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自由席(じゆうせき)に座(すわ)る - to sit in an unreserved seat
  • 自由席(じゆうせき)車両(しゃりょう) - unreserved-seat car
  • 自由席(じゆうせき)券(けん) - unreserved-seat ticket

TRAIN CONTEXT:
On the Shinkansen, the unreserved cars are usually at one end of the train. At busy times passengers in them may have to stand for the whole journey.

COST:
自由席(じゆうせき) is cheaper than 指定席(していせき) because no 指定席(していせき)料金(りょうきん) (reserved-seat fee) is charged on top of the base fare.

Related Words

Antonym: 指定席(していせき) (reserved seat)
Related: グリーン車(しゃ) (Green Car (first-class train car))
