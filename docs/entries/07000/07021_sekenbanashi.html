世間話(せけんばなし)

せけんばなし
noun
small talk; chitchat; idle conversation
1. small talk; chitchat; idle conversation
Casual conversation about everyday topics, the weather, news, and other non-serious matters.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)と世間話(せけんばなし)をした。
I made small talk with a neighbor.
世間話(せけんばなし)ばかりで本題(ほんだい)に入(はい)らない。
We just keep chatting and don't get to the main topic.
コーヒーを飲(の)みながら世間話(せけんばなし)を楽(たの)しんだ。
We enjoyed chitchat over coffee.
久(ひさ)しぶりに会(あ)った友達(ともだち)と世間話(せけんばなし)に花(はな)が咲(さ)いた。
I got absorbed in chitchat with a friend I hadn't seen in a while.
世間話(せけんばなし)に花(はな)が咲(さ)く is a set phrase meaning to get absorbed in conversation.
会議(かいぎ)が始(はじ)まる前(まえ)に、みんなで天気(てんき)やテレビの話(はなし)など軽(かる)い世間話(せけんばなし)をしていた。
Before the meeting started, everyone was making light small talk about the weather and TV shows.

世間話(せけんばなし) is small talk: casual conversation about everyday things — the weather, the news, what is happening locally — carried on for its own sake rather than to settle anything.

Formation: 世間(せけん) 'society, the world at large' + 話(はなし) 'talk.' The name is apt: the topics are whatever is going on out in the world, nothing personal and nothing at stake.

Collocations:

  • 世間話(せけんばなし)をする: to make small talk
  • 世間話(せけんばなし)に花(はな)が咲(さ)く: for the chat to blossom — a set phrase for conversation that takes off and runs on happily
  • 軽(かる)い世間話(せけんばなし): light small talk
  • 世間話(せけんばなし)で盛(も)り上(あ)がる: for small talk to get lively

Similar words:

  • 雑談(ざつだん): casual chat. Broader — it can be about anything, including work or ideas — where 世間話(せけんばなし) suggests the ordinary topics of daily life.
  • おしゃべり: chatting, with the emphasis on the pleasure of talking a lot.
  • 井戸端会議(いどばたかいぎ): literally a 'meeting at the well,' where neighbours once gathered to draw water. Chat among neighbours, often with a hint of gossip about other people.

Usage: neutral, and socially useful. 世間話(せけんばなし) before a meeting, in a waiting room, or with a neighbour is how relationships are kept warm, so being able to sustain it is a genuine skill.

Related Words

Synonym: 雑談(ざつだん) (casual chat)
