腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう

うでをふるう
expression, verb phrase
to show one's skill; to demonstrate one's ability; to display one's talents
1. to show one's skill; to demonstrate one's ability; to display one's talents
To actively demonstrate or exercise one's skills, especially in a situation where those abilities can be fully utilized. Often used when someone gets an opportunity to use their expertise.
料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)を振(ふ)るってもてなした。
I showcased my cooking skills to entertain the guests.
今日(きょう)は腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう機会(きかい)だ。
Today is a chance to show what I can do.
新(あたら)しいプロジェクト(ぷろじぇくと)で思(おも)う存分(ぞんぶん)腕(うで)を振(ふ)るいたい。
I want to fully demonstrate my abilities in the new project.

IDIOM STRUCTURE:
腕(うで) (arm; skill) + を + 振(ふ)るう (to wield; to exercise)

腕(うで) here means skill/ability, not literally the arm. 振(ふ)るう means to wield or exercise something actively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう - to show off cooking skills
  • 腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう機会(きかい) - opportunity to show one's skill
  • 腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう場(ば) - place to demonstrate ability
  • 思(おも)う存分(ぞんぶん)腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう - to fully exercise one's skills

NUANCE:

Positive expression. Implies:

  • Having genuine skill to demonstrate
  • Having an appropriate opportunity
  • Enthusiasm to show one's abilities
  • Active engagement, not just passive display

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腕(うで)を見(み)せる - to show one's skill
  • 実力(じつりょく)を発揮(はっき)する - to demonstrate one's ability
  • 手腕(しゅわん)を発揮(はっき)する - to display one's capability
  • 才能(さいのう)を発揮(はっき)する - to show one's talent

RELATED EXPRESSION:
腕(うで)を磨(みが)く - to hone one's skills (complementary - first you polish, then you show)
