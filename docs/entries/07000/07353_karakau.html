1. to tease; to make fun of; to kid
To playfully mock or joke with someone, often in a good-natured way but sometimes crossing into mean-spirited territory.
友達(ともだち)をからかって遊(あそ)んでいた。
I was playing around teasing my friend.
からかわないでよ、本気(ほんき)なんだから。
Don't tease me - I'm being serious!
彼(かれ)は新人(しんじん)をからかうのが好(す)きだ。
He likes to make fun of new employees.
弟(おとうと)をからかったら泣(な)かれてしまった。
I teased my little brother and he ended up crying.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、名前(なまえ)のことでよくからかわれて嫌(いや)な思(おも)いをした。
When I was a child, I was often teased about my name and it was an unpleasant experience.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を to mark the person being teased.

USAGE:
からかう ranges from playful teasing between friends to more malicious mocking. Context and tone determine whether it's friendly or unkind.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)をからかう (to tease someone)
  • からかい半分(はんぶん)に (half-jokingly)
  • からかわれる (passive: to be teased)

REGISTER:
Casual to neutral.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いじる - to tease/poke fun at (often in comedy contexts)
  • ふざける - to fool around/joke (focuses on the joking behavior itself)
  • ひやかす - to tease/jeer (often about romantic interests)
  • 冷(ひ)やかす - to make fun of, to jeer
