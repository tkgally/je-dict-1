塩気(しおけ)

しおけ
noun
saltiness; salt content
1. saltiness; salt content; salty taste
The salty taste quality or the amount of salt in food.
このスープは塩気(しおけ)が足(た)りない。
This soup doesn't have enough salt.
塩気(しおけ)が強(つよ)すぎて喉(のど)が渇(かわ)く。
It's too salty and makes me thirsty.
健康(けんこう)のために塩気(しおけ)を控(ひか)えめにしている。
I'm keeping the salt content low for my health.

塩気(しおけ) is saltiness — how salty something tastes, or how much salt it contains. It belongs to the kitchen and the dinner table: this is the word for saying a soup needs more salt.

Formation: 塩(しお) 'salt' + 〜気(け), a suffix meaning 'a trace or presence of.' The same suffix gives 水気(みずけ) 'moisture' and 油気(あぶらけ) 'greasiness.'

Collocations:

  • 塩気(しおけ)が足(た)りない: not salty enough
  • 塩気(しおけ)が強(つよ)い: too salty
  • 塩気(しおけ)を控(ひか)える: to cut down on salt
  • 塩気(しおけ)のある: having some saltiness to it

Similar words:

  • 塩味(しおあじ): salty flavour. Closest to 塩気(しおけ), but it names the flavour as such, where 塩気(しおけ) is more about how much salt is present.
  • 塩分(えんぶん): salt content as a measurable quantity. This is the word of nutrition labels and medical advice — a doctor tells you to reduce your 塩分(えんぶん), a cook tastes the 塩気(しおけ).
