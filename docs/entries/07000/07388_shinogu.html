凌(しの)ぐ

しのぐ
verb (godan, transitive)
to endure; to surpass; to get through
1. to endure; to weather; to get through
To bear hardship or difficult conditions and survive through them.
寒(さむ)さを凌(しの)ぐためにコートを着(き)た。
I wore a coat to ward off the cold.
貯金(ちょきん)で当面(とうめん)の生活(せいかつ)を凌(しの)いだ。
I got by with my savings for the time being.
2. to surpass; to exceed; to outdo
To go beyond or exceed something in ability, quality, or quantity.
彼(かれ)の実力(じつりょく)は師匠(ししょう)を凌(しの)ぐほどだ。
His skill is enough to surpass even his master.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を.

USAGE:

Sense 1 (endure) is common in expressions about weathering difficult situations:

  • 寒(さむ)さを凌(しの)ぐ - to ward off/endure the cold
  • 暑(あつ)さを凌(しの)ぐ - to cope with the heat
  • 飢(う)えを凌(しの)ぐ - to stave off hunger

Sense 2 (surpass) is often used with ほど:

  • 〜を凌(しの)ぐほど - so much as to surpass ~

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 露(つゆ)を凌(しの)ぐ - to take shelter from rain/weather
  • 急場(きゅうば)を凌(しの)ぐ - to get through an emergency
  • 先輩(せんぱい)を凌(しの)ぐ - to surpass one's senior

KANJI NOTE:
Can also be written as 淩(しの)ぐ, but 凌(しの)ぐ is standard.

REGISTER:
Neutral to slightly literary. Often found in written Japanese.
