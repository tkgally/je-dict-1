予約制(よやくせい)

よやくせい
noun
reservation system; by appointment only
1. reservation system; appointment system; by reservation only
A system requiring advance booking or reservation before visiting or using a service.
このレストランは予約制(よやくせい)です。
This restaurant is by reservation only.
予約制(よやくせい)なので、事前(じぜん)に電話(でんわ)してください。
Since it's by reservation, please call in advance.
病院(びょういん)は完全(かんぜん)予約制(よやくせい)になった。
The hospital has switched to a complete appointment system.

予約制(よやくせい) says that a place or service is available only to people who have booked ahead. The suffix 制(せい) turns 予約(よやく) (reservation) into the name of a system, so the word describes the establishment's policy, not any one booking.

USAGE:

  • As a predicate: このレストランは予約制(よやくせい)です
  • Adopting the policy: 予約制(よやくせい)になる (to go over to appointments), 予約制(よやくせい)を導入(どうにゅう)する (to introduce the system)
  • 完全(かんぜん)予約制(よやくせい) means no walk-ins at all; 一部(いちぶ)予約制(よやくせい) means some capacity is held back for them

WHERE YOU MEET IT:
Clinics, 美容院(びよういん) (hair salons), small restaurants, and popular exhibitions at 美術館(びじゅつかん). Note that 病院(びょういん) (hospital) and 美容院(びよういん) (salon) differ by one mora, and both commonly carry this notice.

CONTRAST:
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) (first-come, first-served) and 抽選(ちゅうせん) (lottery) are the alternatives when demand outstrips places.
