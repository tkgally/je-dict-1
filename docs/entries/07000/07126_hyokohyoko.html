ひょこひょこ

ひょこひょこ
adverb
bobbing; nodding; with an uneven gait

Definitions

1. with a bobbing or nodding motion
Describes movement with repeated up-and-down motions, like a bird's head or walking with a limp.

Examples

鳩(はと)がひょこひょこと歩(ある)いている。
The pigeon is walking with a bobbing motion.
彼(かれ)は足(あし)を痛(いた)めてひょこひょこ歩(ある)いていた。
He was limping along because he hurt his foot.
人形(にんぎょう)がひょこひょこと動(うご)いておもしろい。
The puppet moves with a bobbing motion, which is fun to watch.

Notes

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo) describing manner of movement.

NUANCE: Describes uneven, bouncy movement with:

  • Up-and-down bobbing motion
  • Slightly comical or cute appearance
  • Often asymmetric or unsteady gait

TYPICAL SUBJECTS:

  • Birds walking (pigeons, chickens)
  • People walking with a limp
  • Puppets or toys
  • Small children just learning to walk

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ひょこひょこと歩(ある)く (to walk with a bobbing gait)
  • ひょこひょこ動(うご)く (to move with bobbing motion)
  • ひょこひょこ現(あらわ)れる (to pop up unexpectedly)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ちょこちょこ: quick small steps
  • ぴょんぴょん: hopping, jumping
  • よたよた: tottering, unsteady walk
  • びっこを引(ひ)く: to limp (more neutral/clinical)
