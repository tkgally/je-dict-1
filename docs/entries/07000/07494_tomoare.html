ともあれ

ともあれ
adverb
anyway; in any case; at any rate

Definitions

1. anyway; in any case; at any rate; be that as it may
Used to set aside or acknowledge previous points and move on to the main point or conclusion.

Examples

ともあれ、無事(ぶじ)でよかった。
Anyway, I'm glad you're safe.
理由(りゆう)はともあれ、結果(けっか)は良(よ)かった。
Whatever the reason, the result was good.
ともあれ、まず始(はじ)めてみよう。
In any case, let's try starting first.
細(こま)かいことはともあれ、大筋(おおすじ)は決(き)まった。
Setting aside the details, the general outline is decided.

Notes

POSITION:
Typically appears at the beginning of a sentence or clause.

VARIATIONS:

  • ともあれ (standard)
  • 何(なに)はともあれ: in any case; first of all
  • いずれにせよともあれ: regardless; in any event

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ともあれ vs. とにかく vs. いずれにせよ
  • ともあれ: setting aside previous points to make the main point (somewhat formal)
  • とにかく: anyway, at any rate (more emphatic/casual)
  • いずれにせよ: in any case (formal)

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. More commonly used in written language than in casual conversation.

GRAMMAR PATTERN:

～はともあれ: setting aside ~; regardless of ~

  • 結果(けっか)はともあれ: regardless of the result
  • 過去(かこ)はともあれ: setting aside the past

Related Words

Synonym: とにかく (anyway)
