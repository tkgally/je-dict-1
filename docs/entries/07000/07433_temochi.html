手持(ても)ち

てもち
noun
on hand; available; holdings

Definitions

1. on hand; available; in one's possession
Items, money, or resources that one currently has and can use immediately. Often used as a modifier before nouns.

Examples

手持(ても)ちの現金(げんきん)が足(た)りない。
I don't have enough cash on hand.
手持(ても)ち無沙汰(ぶさた)で何(なに)をしていいか分(わ)からない。
I'm at a loose end and don't know what to do with myself.
手持(ても)ちの株(かぶ)を売(う)った。
I sold the stocks I was holding.

Notes

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 手(て) - hand
  • 持(も)ち - holding, possession

KEY EXPRESSION:

  • 手持(ても)ち無沙汰(ぶさた) - having nothing to do, at a loose end, idle

This expression describes the uncomfortable feeling of having nothing to do with one's hands/time.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手持(ても)ちの + [noun] - [noun] on hand, available [noun]
  • 手持(ても)ちの現金(げんきん) - cash on hand
  • 手持(ても)ちの資料(しりょう) - available materials
  • 手持(ても)ちの株(かぶ) - stock holdings

USAGE:

Often used as a no-adjective modifying another noun:

  • 手持(ても)ち品(ひん) - items in one's possession
  • 手持(ても)ち資金(しきん) - available funds
  • 手持(ても)ちカメラ - handheld camera

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 在庫(ざいこ) - inventory, stock
  • 所持(しょじ) - possession
  • 保有(ほゆう) - holding, ownership
  • 手元(てもと) - at hand, nearby

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in everyday conversation and business contexts.
