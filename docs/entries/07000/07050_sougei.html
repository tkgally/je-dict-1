送迎(そうげい)

そうげい
noun
shuttle service; pickup and drop-off
1. pickup and drop-off; shuttle service
Transportation service that picks up and drops off people, commonly offered by hotels, airports, and various facilities.
駅(えき)から送迎(そうげい)バスが出(で)ている。
There's a shuttle bus from the station.
ホテルの送迎(そうげい)サービスを利用(りよう)した。
I used the hotel's shuttle service.
空港(くうこう)送迎(そうげい)は無料(むりょう)ですか。
Is the airport shuttle free?

FORMATION:
送(そう) (sending off) + 迎(げい) (going to meet). The word covers both directions of a trip — taking someone there and collecting them again.

VERB FORM:
送迎(そうげい)する - to provide pickup and drop-off

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送迎(そうげい)バス - shuttle bus
  • 送迎(そうげい)サービス - shuttle service
  • 送迎(そうげい)付(つ)き - with shuttle service included
  • 空港(くうこう)送迎(そうげい) - airport transfer
  • 無料(むりょう)送迎(そうげい) - free shuttle service

WHERE YOU SEE IT:

  • ホテル and 旅館(りょかん) - shuttles from the nearest station or airport
  • 結婚式場(けっこんしきじょう) - wedding venues, for guests
  • 幼稚園(ようちえん) - kindergarten buses
  • 介護(かいご)施設(しせつ) - nursing-care facilities, for day visits
