湯(ゆ)たんぽ

ゆたんぽ
noun
hot water bottle
1. hot water bottle
A container filled with hot water, used to warm the body, especially in bed.
冬(ふゆ)は湯(ゆ)たんぽを布団(ふとん)に入(い)れて寝(ね)る。
In winter, I put a hot water bottle in the futon when I sleep.
湯(ゆ)たんぽにお湯(ゆ)を入(い)れた。
I filled the hot water bottle with hot water.
最近(さいきん)の湯(ゆ)たんぽは電子(でんし)レンジで温(あたた)められる。
Modern hot water bottles can be heated in the microwave.

FORMATION:
湯(ゆ) (hot water) + たんぽ (Chinese: 湯婆(たんぽ), hand warmer)

ALTERNATE WRITING:
Also written as 湯湯婆(ゆたんぽ) or 湯婆(ゆたんぽ).

TYPES:

  • 金属製(きんぞくせい) - metal type (traditional)
  • 樹脂製(じゅしせい) - plastic/resin type
  • ゴム製(ごむせい) - rubber type
  • 電気(でんき)湯(ゆ)たんぽ - electric type
  • レンジで温(あたた)めるタイプ - microwave type

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 湯(ゆ)たんぽを入(い)れる - to put in a hot water bottle
  • 湯(ゆ)たんぽで足(あし)を温(あたた)める - to warm one's feet with a hot water bottle

SAFETY NOTE:
Direct skin contact can cause low-temperature burns (低温(ていおん)やけど). Always wrap in a cover.
