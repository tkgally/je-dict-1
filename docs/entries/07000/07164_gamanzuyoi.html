我慢強(がまんづよ)い

がまんづよい
i-adjective
patient; persevering; enduring

Definitions

1. patient; persevering; enduring; tolerant
Having the ability to endure hardship, pain, or difficulty without complaining.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は我慢強(がまんづよ)い人(ひと)だ。
She is a patient person.
我慢強(がまんづよ)く待(ま)っていたら、やっとチャンスが来(き)た。
By waiting patiently, my chance finally came.
この仕事(しごと)は我慢強(がまんづよ)くないとできない。
You can't do this job unless you're patient.

Notes

FORMATION:
我慢(がまん) (patience/endurance) + 強(つよ)い (strong)

FORMS:

  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)くない - not patient (negative)
  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)くて - patient and... (te-form)
  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)かった - was patient (past)
  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)く + verb - patiently (adverb)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)く待(ま)つ - to wait patiently
  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)く取(と)り組(く)む - to work on persistently
  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)い性格(せいかく) - patient personality

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 粘(ねば)り強(づよ)い - tenacious, persistent
  • 辛抱強(しんぼうづよ)い - patient, forbearing
  • 根気強(こんきづよ)い - persevering

NUANCE:
我慢強(がまんづよ)い emphasizes enduring hardship; 粘(ねば)り強(づよ)い emphasizes not giving up.

Related Words

Synonym: 粘(ねば)り強(づよ)い (tenacious)
Related: 我慢(がまん) (patience)
