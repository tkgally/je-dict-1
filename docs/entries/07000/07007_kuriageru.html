verb (ichidan, transitive)

1. to move up; to advance (a date or time)
To reschedule something to happen earlier than originally planned.
会議(かいぎ)の日程(にってい)を1週間(しゅうかん)繰(く)り上(あ)げた。
We moved up the meeting schedule by one week.
雨(あめ)の予報(よほう)なので運動会(うんどうかい)を繰(く)り上(あ)げて実施(じっし)する。
Since rain is forecast, we'll hold the sports day earlier.
出発(しゅっぱつ)時間(じかん)を三十分(さんじゅっぷん)繰(く)り上(あ)げることにした。
We decided to move the departure time up by thirty minutes.
2. to promote; to move up (in rank or position)
To advance someone to a higher position, often due to a vacancy.
欠員(けついん)が出(で)たので次点(じてん)候補(こうほ)が繰(く)り上(あ)げ当選(とうせん)した。
Due to a vacancy, the runner-up was promoted to winner.
繰(く)り上(あ)げ当選(とうせん) is a set term for winning by advancement.
上(うえ)の人(ひと)が辞(や)めたので、彼(かれ)が繰(く)り上(あ)げて部長(ぶちょう)になった。
Since the person above him quit, he was promoted to department head.
補欠(ほけつ)選手(せんしゅ)が繰(く)り上(あ)げでレギュラーに入(はい)った。
The substitute player was promoted to the regular lineup.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive: you 繰(く)り上(あ)げる a date, a time, or a person's position. The intransitive idea is expressed by the noun form 繰(く)り上(あ)げ, as in 繰(く)り上(あ)げ当選(とうせん).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日程(にってい)を繰(く)り上(あ)げる (to bring a schedule forward)
  • 時間(じかん)を繰(く)り上(あ)げる (to move a time earlier)
  • 繰(く)り上(あ)げ当選(とうせん) (winning a seat because someone above you withdrew)
  • 繰(く)り上(あ)げ返済(へんさい) (paying off a loan ahead of schedule)

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 繰(く)り下(さ)げる - the opposite operation, moving something later.
  • 早(はや)める - to make something happen sooner in general; 繰(く)り上(あ)げる specifically shifts a fixed slot in a sequence, so it suits schedules, rankings, and queues.

Related Words

Antonym: 繰(く)り下(さ)げる (to postpone/move down)
