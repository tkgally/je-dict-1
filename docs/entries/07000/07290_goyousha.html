ご容赦(ようしゃ)

ごようしゃ
noun
pardon; forgiveness; understanding

Definitions

1. pardon; forgiveness; understanding; forbearance
Formal/honorific expression asking for forgiveness or understanding.

Examples

ご不便(ふべん)をおかけしますが、ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください。
We apologize for the inconvenience; please bear with us.
品切(しなぎ)れの際(さい)はご容赦(ようしゃ)ください。
Please understand if items are out of stock.
何卒(なにとぞ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)のほど、お願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We humbly ask for your forgiveness.

Notes

STRUCTURE:
ご (honorific prefix) + 容赦(ようしゃ) (pardon)

REGISTER:

Very formal. Common in:

  • Customer service announcements
  • Business correspondence
  • Official apologies
  • Signs and notices

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください - please forgive/understand
  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)願(ねが)います - we ask your understanding
  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)のほど - (extra formal)
  • 何卒(なにとぞ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)... - most humbly ask...

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ご了承(りょうしょう)ください - please acknowledge/understand
  • ご理解(りかい)ください - please understand
  • お許(ゆる)しください - please forgive (slightly less formal)
