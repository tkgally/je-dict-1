1. to fold; to bend; to crease
To bend something by folding, typically paper, metal, or body parts like knees or fingers.
紙(かみ)を半分(はんぶん)に折(お)り曲(ま)げた。
I folded the paper in half.
膝(ひざ)を折(お)り曲(ま)げて座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit with your knees bent.
針金(はりがね)を折(お)り曲(ま)げて形(かたち)を作(つく)った。
I bent the wire and shaped it.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive, and it takes a physical object: paper, wire, a limb. It is not used for bending a rule or a fact.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紙(かみ)を折(お)り曲(ま)げる (to fold paper over)
  • 膝(ひざ)を折(お)り曲(ま)げる (to bend one's knees)
  • 指(ゆび)を折(お)り曲(ま)げる (to curl one's fingers)
  • 針金(はりがね)を折(お)り曲(ま)げる (to bend wire)

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 折(お)る - to fold or to snap something in two
  • 曲(ま)げる - to bend into a curve
  • 折(お)り曲(ま)げる combines the two: bending something so that it stays creased or held at an angle, rather than springing back.
