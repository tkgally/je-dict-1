手芸(しゅげい)

しゅげい
noun
handicraft; needlework
1. handicraft; needlework
Handmade crafts, particularly textile-based crafts like knitting, embroidery, and sewing.
母(はは)は手芸(しゅげい)が得意(とくい)だ。
My mother is good at handicrafts.
手芸(しゅげい)店(てん)で毛糸(けいと)を買(か)った。
I bought yarn at the craft store.
趣味(しゅみ)で手芸(しゅげい)を始(はじ)めた。
I started doing handicrafts as a hobby.

手芸(しゅげい) covers hand crafts worked in thread and fabric. It does not stretch to woodworking, pottery, or metalwork — those fall under 工芸(こうげい). Knowing where the line falls matters, because the two words are both translated "crafts" in English.

WHAT IT COVERS:

  • 編(あ)み物(もの): knitting and crochet
  • 刺繍(ししゅう): embroidery
  • 裁縫(さいほう): sewing
  • パッチワーク: patchwork; ビーズ細工(ざいく): beadwork

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手芸(しゅげい)をする: do handicrafts
  • 手芸(しゅげい)店(てん): craft shop
  • 手芸(しゅげい)用品(ようひん): craft supplies
  • 手芸(しゅげい)教室(きょうしつ): a handicraft class, the usual way adults take the hobby up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 工芸(こうげい) is the broad word for crafts and applied arts, including pottery and lacquer, and carries a whiff of professional or traditional production.
  • 手作(てづく)り describes any object made by hand rather than bought, food included; it says how something was made, not what kind of craft it is.
  • ハンドメイド is the loanword, used mostly of items sold online.

Related Words

Related: 木彫(きぼ)り (wood carving)
Related: 工芸(こうげい) (crafts in general, including non-textile work)
