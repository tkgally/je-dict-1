標準語(ひょうじゅんご)

ひょうじゅんご
noun
standard language; standard Japanese

Definitions

1. standard language; the official or prestige variety of a language
The variety of a language that is considered the norm for education, media, and official communication, as distinct from regional dialects.

Examples

学校(がっこう)では標準語(ひょうじゅんご)を使(つか)う。
We use standard Japanese at school.
テレビのアナウンサーは標準語(ひょうじゅんご)で話(はな)す。
TV announcers speak in standard Japanese.
地方(ちほう)から東京(とうきょう)に来(き)て標準語(ひょうじゅんご)を覚(おぼ)えた。
I learned standard Japanese after moving from the countryside to Tokyo.
標準語(ひょうじゅんご)と方言(ほうげん)を使(つか)い分(わ)ける。
I switch between standard Japanese and dialect depending on the situation.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 標準語(ひょうじゅんご)を話(はな)す: speak standard Japanese
  • 標準語(ひょうじゅんご)に直(なお)す: convert to standard Japanese
  • 標準語(ひょうじゅんご)と方言(ほうげん): standard language and dialect

SIMILAR TERMS:

  • 標準語(ひょうじゅんご): standard language (formal/academic term)
  • 共通語(きょうつうご): common language (emphasizes mutual intelligibility)
  • 東京弁(とうきょうべん): Tokyo dialect (technically not identical to 標準語(ひょうじゅんご))

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese 標準語(ひょうじゅんご) is largely based on the Tokyo dialect and became established as the national standard through education and mass media. While it serves as the lingua franca across Japan, many people are bidialectal, using 標準語(ひょうじゅんご) in formal settings and their regional 方言(ほうげん) in casual or family contexts.

LINGUISTIC NOTE:
Some scholars prefer the term 共通語(きょうつうご) (common language) over 標準語(ひょうじゅんご) to avoid implying that dialects are substandard.

Related Words

Contrast: 方言(ほうげん) (dialect)
Related: 訛(なま)り (accent)
