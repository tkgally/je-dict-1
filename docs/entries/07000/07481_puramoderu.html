プラモデル

ぷらもでる
noun
plastic model kit

Definitions

1. plastic model kit; scale model made of plastic
A kit for building a scale model made primarily of plastic parts, requiring assembly and often painting.

Examples

子供(こども)の頃(ころ)はよくプラモデルを作(つく)った。
I used to build a lot of plastic models when I was a kid.
ガンダムのプラモデルを集(あつ)めている。
I collect Gundam plastic model kits.
プラモデルを組(く)み立(た)てるのが趣味(しゅみ)だ。
Building plastic models is my hobby.
このプラモデルは細(こま)かいパーツが多(おお)くて難(むずか)しい。
This plastic model has many small parts and is difficult.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
Abbreviation of プラスチックモデル (plastic model). The term is wasei-eigo (Japanese-made English).

ABBREVIATION:
Often shortened to プラモ in casual speech.

POPULAR TYPES:

  • ガンプラ: Gundam plastic models (very popular in Japan)
  • 戦車(せんしゃ)モデル: tank models
  • 飛行機(ひこうき)モデル: airplane models
  • 車(くるま)モデル: car models
  • 艦船(かんせん)モデル: ship models

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プラモデルを作(つく)る: build a plastic model
  • プラモデルを組(く)み立(た)てる: assemble a plastic model
  • プラモデルを塗装(とそう)する: paint a plastic model

CULTURAL NOTE:
Plastic model building is a popular hobby in Japan, with major manufacturers like Bandai and Tamiya. Gunpla (Gundam plastic models) is particularly associated with Japanese hobbyist culture.
