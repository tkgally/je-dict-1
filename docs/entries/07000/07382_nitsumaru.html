煮詰(につ)まる

につまる
verb (godan, intransitive)
to boil down; to be stuck (in discussion)
1. to boil down; to be reduced by boiling
To become thickened or reduced in quantity through continued boiling. Used for cooking liquids.
ソースが煮詰(につ)まってきた。
The sauce has boiled down.
煮物(にもの)の汁(しる)が煮詰(につ)まって味(あじ)が濃(こ)くなった。
The simmered dish's broth boiled down and the flavor became stronger.
火(ひ)を弱(よわ)くしないと煮詰(につ)まってしまう。
If you don't lower the heat, it will boil down too much.
2. to be stuck; to reach a deadlock
For discussions or negotiations to reach a point where progress is no longer being made. Originally meant discussions that have been thoroughly deliberated but now often implies a stalemate.
会議(かいぎ)が煮詰(につ)まって、先(さき)に進(すす)めない。
The meeting has reached a deadlock and we can't move forward.
話(はな)し合(あ)いが煮詰(につ)まったら、休憩(きゅうけい)を取(と)ろう。
If the discussion gets stuck, let's take a break.
交渉(こうしょう)が煮詰(につ)まってしまい、第三者(だいさんしゃ)の助(たす)けが必要(ひつよう)になった。
The negotiations reached a stalemate and we needed a third party's help.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 煮詰(につ)める (to boil down something).

USAGE NOTE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) has undergone semantic shift. Originally, 「議論(ぎろん)が煮詰(につ)まる」 meant discussions had been thoroughly worked through to reach a conclusion (positive). Now it's more commonly understood as discussions being stuck in a deadlock (negative). Both usages exist, so context is important.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)が煮詰(につ)まる (discussions reach a point/get stuck)
  • 交渉(こうしょう)が煮詰(につ)まる (negotiations stall)
  • ソースが煮詰(につ)まる (sauce boils down)

REGISTER:
Neutral. Used in both casual and formal contexts.
