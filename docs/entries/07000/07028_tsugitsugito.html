次々(つぎつぎ)と

つぎつぎと
adverb
one after another; in succession; continuously
1. one after another; in succession; continuously
Indicates that things happen or appear repeatedly in quick succession, without pause.
問題(もんだい)が次々(つぎつぎ)と発生(はっせい)した。
Problems arose one after another.
新製品(しんせいひん)が次々(つぎつぎ)と発売(はつばい)される。
New products are released one after another.
お客(きゃく)さんが次々(つぎつぎ)と来店(らいてん)した。
Customers came in one after another.

次々(つぎつぎ)と describes events or arrivals following each other without a gap — one finishes or appears, and the next is already there. It modifies the verb, so it goes with verbs of happening, appearing, arriving and releasing rather than with states.

Formation: 次(つぎ) 'next' doubled + と, the particle that turns a manner word into an adverb.

Variants, all interchangeable in most contexts:

  • 次々(つぎつぎ)と: the standard adverbial form
  • 次々(つぎつぎ)に: equally common, slightly more colloquial
  • 次々(つぎつぎ): the bare form, used before a verb in brisk or written style

Collocations:

  • 次々(つぎつぎ)と起(お)こる: to happen one after another
  • 次々(つぎつぎ)と現(あらわ)れる: to appear in succession
  • 次々(つぎつぎ)と倒(たお)れる: to go down one after another

Similar words:

  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と: also 'one after another', but implying large numbers arriving
  • 立(た)て続(つづ)けに: in rapid succession, with the emphasis on how little time separates the events

Related Words

Synonym: 続々(ぞくぞく)と (one after another (in large numbers))
Related: 次々(つぎつぎ) (bare form of the same adverb)
Related: 立(た)て続(つづ)け (in rapid succession)
