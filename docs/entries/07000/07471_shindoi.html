しんどい

しんどい
i-adjective
tiring; exhausting; tough

Definitions

1. physically or mentally exhausting; draining
Describes something that requires a lot of physical or mental energy, or the state of being exhausted from such effort.

Examples

この仕事(しごと)、すごくしんどい。
This job is really tiring.
満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)での通勤(つうきん)はしんどい。
Commuting on packed trains is exhausting.
風邪(かぜ)で体(からだ)がしんどい。
I feel worn out because of a cold.
毎日(まいにち)残業(ざんぎょう)するのはしんどいけど、頑張(がんば)る。
Working overtime every day is tough, but I'll hang in there.

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: しんどく
  • Te-form: しんどくて
  • Negative: しんどくない

ORIGIN:
Originally from 関西弁(かんさいべん) (Kansai dialect) but now widely used throughout Japan, especially among younger speakers.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • しんどい vs. 疲(つか)れる vs. 大変(たいへん)
  • しんどい: emphasizes the draining, exhausting quality (casual)
  • 疲(つか)れる: being tired (verb, neutral)
  • 大変(たいへん): hard, difficult situation (more objective)

REGISTER: Casual
More formal alternatives: 辛(つら)い, 大変(たいへん), 疲(つか)れる

USAGE:
Can describe both physical exhaustion (体(からだ)がしんどい) and mental/emotional strain (精神的(せいしんてき)にしんどい).

Related Words

Synonym: 疲(つか)れる (to be tired)
Synonym: 辛(つら)い (hard, painful)
