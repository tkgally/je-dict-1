格上(かくうえ)

かくうえ
noun, no-adjective
higher rank; superior status; better quality
1. higher rank; superior status; higher class
Having a higher status, rank, or quality than something else being compared. Often used in competitive contexts like sports or business.
格上(かくうえ)の相手(あいて)に勝(か)った。
We beat a higher-ranked opponent.
彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)より格上(かくうえ)の存在(そんざい)だ。
She is of a higher status than me.
格上(かくうえ)のホテルに泊(と)まった。
I stayed at a higher-class hotel.

WORD FORMATION:
格(かく) (status, rank) + 上(うえ) (above)
Literally: "above in rank"

USAGE:

格上(かくうえ) is commonly used in:

  • Sports - comparing team rankings
  • Business - comparing company status
  • Quality - comparing product grades
  • Social status - comparing positions

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 格上(かくうえ)の相手(あいて) - higher-ranked opponent
  • 格上(かくうえ)に挑(いど)む - to challenge someone of higher rank
  • 格上(かくうえ)げ - upgrading (adding げ suffix)

ANTONYM:

  • 格下(かくした) - lower rank, inferior status

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in sports commentary and business contexts.

Related Words

Antonym: 格下(かくした) (lower rank)
