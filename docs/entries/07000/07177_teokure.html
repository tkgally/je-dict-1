手遅(ておく)れ

ておくれ
noun, na-adjective
too late; belated

Definitions

1. too late; belated; past the time when something could be done
A state where it is no longer possible to take effective action because the appropriate time has passed.

Examples

病気(びょうき)が見(み)つかったときには、もう手遅(ておく)れだった。
By the time the disease was discovered, it was already too late.
手遅(ておく)れになる前(まえ)に行動(こうどう)しよう。
Let's act before it's too late.
今(いま)からでも遅(おそ)くない、まだ手遅(ておく)れじゃない。
It's not too late even now; we can still do something.

Notes

FORMATION:
手(て) (hand/measure) + 遅(おく)れ (delay)

Literally 'the measures are late.'

GRAMMAR:

  • 手遅(ておく)れだ - it's too late (predicate)
  • 手遅(ておく)れになる - to become too late
  • 手遅(ておく)れの + noun - belated (rare modifier usage)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手遅(ておく)れになる前(まえ)に - before it's too late
  • もう手遅(ておく)れだ - it's already too late
  • まだ手遅(ておく)れではない - it's not too late yet
  • 手遅(ておく)れ気味(ぎみ) - somewhat too late

USAGE:
Often used in serious contexts like medical situations, business decisions, or environmental issues.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遅(おそ)すぎる - too late (more casual)
  • 後(あと)の祭(まつ)り - too late (idiom)

Related Words

Related: 遅(おく)れる (to be late)
