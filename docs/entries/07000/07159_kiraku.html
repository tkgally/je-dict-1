気楽(きらく)

きらく
na-adjective
carefree; easygoing; comfortable

Definitions

1. carefree; easygoing; at ease
Free from worries or stress; relaxed and comfortable.
2. easy; undemanding; comfortable
Describes a situation or condition that requires little effort or responsibility.

Examples

一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしは気楽(きらく)だ。
Living alone is carefree.
気楽(きらく)に構(かま)えてください。
Please take it easy.
Common expression meaning 'don't stress'
責任(せきにん)がない立場(たちば)は気楽(きらく)でいいね。
It must be nice having an easy position with no responsibility.
Can have a slightly ironic nuance

Notes

FORMATION:
気(き) (spirit/mind) + 楽(らく) (comfort/ease)

FORMS:

  • 気楽(きらく)な + noun - carefree (modifier)
  • 気楽(きらく)に + verb - in a carefree manner (adverb)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気楽(きらく)に構(かま)える - to take it easy
  • 気楽(きらく)な暮(く)らし - carefree life
  • 気楽(きらく)な仕事(しごと) - easy job
  • 気楽(きらく)に過(す)ごす - to spend time at ease

NUANCE:
Can be positive (relaxed, comfortable) or slightly negative (irresponsible, not taking things seriously).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • のんき - carefree, easygoing
  • 楽(らく) - easy, comfortable
  • リラックス - relaxed

Related Words

Synonym: のんき (carefree)
Related: 楽(らく) (easy)
