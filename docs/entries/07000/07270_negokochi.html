寝心地(ねごこち)

ねごこち
noun
sleeping comfort; how comfortable something is to sleep on

Definitions

1. sleeping comfort; quality of sleep comfort
The quality of comfort experienced when sleeping on a bed, futon, or other surface.

Examples

この布団(ふとん)は寝心地(ねごこち)がいい。
This futon is comfortable to sleep on.
寝心地(ねごこち)の良(よ)いマットレスを買(か)った。
I bought a comfortable mattress.
ホテルのベッドは寝心地(ねごこち)最高(さいこう)だった。
The hotel bed was extremely comfortable.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
寝(ね)る (to sleep) + 心地(ここち) (feeling, sensation) → feeling while sleeping

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寝心地(ねごこち)がいい / 良(よ)い (comfortable to sleep on)
  • 寝心地(ねごこち)が悪(わる)い (uncomfortable to sleep on)
  • 寝心地(ねごこち)抜群(ばつぐん) (exceptionally comfortable)

RELATED ～心地(ごこち) WORDS:

  • 居心地(いごこち) (comfort of a place)
  • 乗(の)り心地(ごこち) (ride comfort)
  • 着心地(きごこち) (wearing comfort)
  • 使(つか)い心地(ごこち) (ease of use)

USAGE NOTE:
Commonly used for describing mattresses, futons, pillows, and beds. Frequently seen in bedding product reviews.
