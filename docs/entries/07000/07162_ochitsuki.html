落(お)ち着(つ)き

おちつき
noun
composure; calmness; poise
1. composure; calmness; poise; presence of mind
A state of being calm and collected, especially in stressful situations.
彼(かれ)は落(お)ち着(つ)きがあるので、緊急(きんきゅう)時(じ)に頼(たよ)りになる。
He has composure, so he's reliable in emergencies.
この子(こ)は落(お)ち着(つ)きがなくて、じっとしていられない。
This child has no composure and can't sit still.
落(お)ち着(つ)きがない = restless, fidgety
2. stability; settledness
A state of being settled or steady, often referring to living situations or life circumstances.
転職(てんしょく)して一年(いちねん)、ようやく生活(せいかつ)に落(お)ち着(つ)きが出(で)てきた。
A year after changing jobs, my life is finally becoming stable.

FORMATION:
落(お)ち着(つ)く (to calm down, to settle) → 落(お)ち着(つ)き (noun form)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落(お)ち着(つ)きがある - to have composure
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)きがない - to lack composure, restless
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)きを取(と)り戻(もど)す - to regain composure
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)きを払(はら)う - to stay calm
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)きのある人(ひと) - a composed person

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冷静(れいせい) - calm, cool-headed
  • 沈着(ちんちゃく) - composure (literary)
  • 平静(へいせい) - calmness, equanimity
