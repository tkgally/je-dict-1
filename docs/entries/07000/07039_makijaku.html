巻尺(まきじゃく)

まきじゃく
noun
tape measure
1. tape measure; measuring tape
A flexible ruler that can be rolled up, used for measuring lengths.
巻尺(まきじゃく)で部屋(へや)の長(なが)さを測(はか)った。
I measured the length of the room with a tape measure.
引越(ひっこ)しの時(とき)、巻尺(まきじゃく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need a tape measure when moving.
巻尺(まきじゃく)を伸(の)ばして家具(かぐ)のサイズを確認(かくにん)する。
I extend the tape measure to check the size of the furniture.

FORMATION:
巻(まき) (rolling/coil) + 尺(じゃく) (measure/ruler)

ALTERNATE TERMS:

  • メジャー - measure (loanword, commonly used)
  • コンベックス - retractable tape measure (for construction)

TYPES:

  • 裁縫用(さいほうよう) - sewing tape measure (soft/flexible)
  • 工事用(こうじよう) - construction tape measure (stiff/retractable)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 巻尺(まきじゃく)で測(はか)る - to measure with a tape measure
  • 巻尺(まきじゃく)を伸(の)ばす - to extend the tape measure
  • 巻尺(まきじゃく)を巻(ま)き戻(もど)す - to retract the tape measure
general
