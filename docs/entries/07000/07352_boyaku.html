ぼやく

ぼやく
verb (godan, intransitive)
to complain; to grumble; to mutter
1. to complain; to grumble; to mutter
To express dissatisfaction or displeasure in a quiet, often mumbling manner. More subdued than formal complaints.
給料(きゅうりょう)が安(やす)いとぼやいていた。
He was grumbling that his salary is low.
疲(つか)れたとぼやきながら仕事(しごと)を続(つづ)けた。
I continued working while complaining that I was tired.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつも天気(てんき)のことでぼやいている。
She's always grumbling about the weather.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). No pair verb.

USAGE:
ぼやく is often used for everyday, minor complaints expressed in a muttering or grumbling tone. It's less formal than 文句(もんく)を言(い)う and implies a certain resignation or lack of expectation that the complaint will be addressed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜とぼやく (to grumble that ~)
  • 〜についてぼやく (to grumble about ~)
  • ぼやきながら (while grumbling)

REGISTER:
Casual to neutral. Often used in everyday conversation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 愚痴(ぐち)をこぼす - to complain/vent (more about emotional release)
  • 文句(もんく)を言(い)う - to complain (more direct, stronger)
  • つぶやく - to mutter (neutral, not necessarily complaining)
