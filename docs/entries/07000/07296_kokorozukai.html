心遣(こころづか)い

こころづかい
noun
thoughtfulness; consideration; kindness

Definitions

1. thoughtfulness; consideration
Kind attention to the feelings and needs of others; emotional care shown toward someone.

Examples

お心遣(こころづか)いに感謝(かんしゃ)します。
I appreciate your thoughtfulness/kindness.
彼(かれ)の心遣(こころづか)いが嬉(うれ)しかった。
I was happy about his thoughtfulness.
細(こま)やかな心遣(こころづか)いのある対応(たいおう)。
Service with delicate thoughtfulness.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
心(こころ) (heart) + 遣(つか)い (use, handling) → using one's heart = thoughtfulness

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お心遣(こころづか)いに感謝(かんしゃ)します - I appreciate your thoughtfulness
  • 細(こま)やかな心遣(こころづか)い - delicate thoughtfulness
  • 心遣(こころづか)いが嬉(うれ)しい - to be happy about someone's thoughtfulness

COMPARISON:

  • 気遣(きづか)い - concern (more about attentiveness and worry)
  • 心遣(こころづか)い - thoughtfulness (more about emotional warmth)
  • 心配(こころくば)り - care (more about attention to details)
