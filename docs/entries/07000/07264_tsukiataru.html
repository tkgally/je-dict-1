突(つ)き当(あ)たる

つきあたる
verb (godan, intransitive)
to run into; to hit; to come to a dead end

Definitions

1. to hit; to bump into; to run into (an obstacle)
To physically collide with something or reach an obstruction.
2. to come to a dead end; to reach the end of a road
To arrive at a point where one cannot proceed further.
3. to encounter; to face (a problem or difficulty)
To come up against an abstract obstacle like a problem or challenge.

Examples

道(みち)を真(ま)っすぐ行(い)くと壁(かべ)に突(つ)き当(あ)たる。
If you go straight down the road, you'll hit a wall.
T字路(じろ)を突(つ)き当(あ)たったら右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がってください。
When you reach the T-junction, turn right.
難(むずか)しい問題(もんだい)に突(つ)き当(あ)たった。
I encountered a difficult problem.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xに突(つ)き当(あ)たる

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 突(つ)く (to thrust/poke) + 当(あ)たる (to hit)

CONJUGATION:

  • 突(つ)き当(あ)たる → 突(つ)き当(あ)たらない (negative)
  • 突(つ)き当(あ)たる → 突(つ)き当(あ)たって (te-form)
  • 突(つ)き当(あ)たる → 突(つ)き当(あ)たった (past)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁(かべ)に突(つ)き当(あ)たる - to hit a wall (literal/figurative)
  • 行(い)き止(ど)まりに突(つ)き当(あ)たる - to reach a dead end
  • 問題(もんだい)に突(つ)き当(あ)たる - to encounter a problem

DIRECTIONS:
Commonly used when giving directions: 「突(つ)き当(あ)たり」 means "at the end of the road."

Related Words

Related: 突(つ)き進(すす)む (to push forward)
