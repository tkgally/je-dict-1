ぴえん

ぴえん
interjection, noun, internet slang
sad; crying; boo-hoo

Definitions

1. sad; crying; tears
Internet slang expressing mild sadness, disappointment, or sympathy, often used playfully.

Examples

推(お)しのライブ行(い)けなかった、ぴえん。
I couldn't go to my favorite artist's concert, so sad.
明日(あした)仕事(しごと)だ…ぴえん。
I have work tomorrow... boo-hoo.
彼氏(かれし)に振(ふ)られた。ぴえん超(こ)えてぱおん。
My boyfriend broke up with me. Beyond sad, I'm devastated.

Notes

ORIGIN:
泣(な)く sound effect "ぴえーん" → shortened to "ぴえん"
Popularized in 2019 through social media and the 🥺 (pleading face) emoji.

USAGE:

  • Usually written in hiragana
  • Often paired with 🥺 emoji
  • Used playfully for minor disappointments
  • Not typically used for serious sadness

INTENSIFIED FORMS:

  • ぴえん超(こ)えてぱおん - beyond ぴえん (even sadder)
  • 大(おお)ぴえん - big ぴえん

NOTE:
Primarily used by young people, especially on social media. May sound unnatural for older speakers.
