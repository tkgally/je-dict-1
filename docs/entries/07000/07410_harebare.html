晴(は)れ晴(ば)れ

はればれ
adverb, na-adjective
cheerfully; brightly; feeling refreshed
1. cheerfully; brightly; with a refreshed feeling
Describes a feeling of relief, joy, or lightness, as if clouds have cleared and the sky is bright. Often used after worries have been resolved or a burden has been lifted.
試験(しけん)が終(お)わって、晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした気分(きぶん)だ。
The exam is over, and I feel so relieved.
晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした笑顔(えがお)を見(み)せた。
She showed a bright, cheerful smile.
悩(なや)みが解決(かいけつ)して気持(きも)ちが晴(は)れ晴(ば)れした。
My worries were resolved and I felt refreshed.

WORD FORMATION:
晴(は)れ (clear weather, from 晴(は)れる) reduplicated.
The image is of clear, bright skies after clouds have passed.

FORMS:

  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れと (adverbial with と)
  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした + noun (adjectival)
  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れしい (i-adjective form, literary)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした気分(きぶん) - refreshed feeling
  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした顔(かお) - bright face
  • 晴(は)れ晴(ば)れとした表情(ひょうじょう) - cheerful expression
  • 気持(きも)ちが晴(は)れ晴(ば)れする - to feel refreshed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すっきり - refreshed, cleared up (more casual)
  • さっぱり - refreshed, clean (after bathing/eating)
  • 爽(さわ)やか - refreshing, fresh

REGISTER:
Neutral to slightly formal. Common in written and spoken Japanese.
