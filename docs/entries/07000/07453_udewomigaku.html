腕(うで)を磨(みが)く

うでをみがく
expression, verb phrase
to hone one's skills; to improve one's abilities; to polish one's craft

Definitions

1. to hone one's skills; to improve one's abilities; to polish one's craft
To work on improving one's skills or abilities through practice and effort. Implies dedicated, ongoing effort to become better at something.

Examples

毎日(まいにち)練習(れんしゅう)して腕(うで)を磨(みが)いている。
I practice every day to hone my skills.
料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)をもっと磨(みが)きたい。
I want to improve my cooking skills more.
海外(かいがい)で腕(うで)を磨(みが)いてきたシェフだ。
He's a chef who honed his skills overseas.

Notes

IDIOM STRUCTURE:
腕(うで) (arm; skill) + を + 磨(みが)く (to polish)

腕(うで) here means skill/ability. 磨(みが)く (to polish) creates the metaphor of polishing or refining a skill like one would polish a gem or blade.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)を磨(みが)く - to improve cooking skills
  • 語学(ごがく)の腕(うで)を磨(みが)く - to improve language skills
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)の腕(うで)を磨(みが)く - to hone technical skills
  • 日々(ひび)腕(うで)を磨(みが)く - to polish skills day by day

NUANCE:

Implies:

  • Deliberate effort to improve
  • Ongoing process, not one-time action
  • Recognition that one can always get better
  • Dedication and commitment to improvement

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 技(わざ)を磨(みが)く - to polish one's technique
  • 実力(じつりょく)を付(つ)ける - to build up ability
  • スキルアップ(すきるあっぷ)する - to skill up (modern)
  • 研鑽(けんさん)を積(つ)む - to accumulate training (formal)

RELATED EXPRESSION:
腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう - to show one's skill (complementary - first you polish, then you show)
