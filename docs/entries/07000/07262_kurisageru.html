繰(く)り下(さ)げる

くりさげる
verb (ichidan, transitive)
to postpone; to move back (in time); to defer
1. to postpone; to move back (a date or time)
To reschedule something to happen later than originally planned.
2. to defer; to push back (a deadline or age)
To delay something, especially a payment start date or retirement age.
会議(かいぎ)の日程(にってい)を2日(にち)繰(く)り下(さ)げた。
We postponed the meeting by two days.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で運動会(うんどうかい)を繰(く)り下(さ)げることにした。
Due to the typhoon, we decided to postpone the sports day.
年金(ねんきん)の受給(じゅきゅう)開始(かいし)年齢(ねんれい)を繰(く)り下(さ)げると月額(げつがく)が増(ふ)える。
If you defer the pension start age, the monthly amount increases.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを繰(く)り下(さ)げる

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 繰(く)る (to reel/wind) + 下(さ)げる (to lower)

CONJUGATION:

  • 繰(く)り下(さ)げる → 繰(く)り下(さ)げない (negative)
  • 繰(く)り下(さ)げる → 繰(く)り下(さ)げて (te-form)
  • 繰(く)り下(さ)げる → 繰(く)り下(さ)げた (past)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日程(にってい)を繰(く)り下(さ)げる - to postpone a schedule
  • 開始(かいし)時間(じかん)を繰(く)り下(さ)げる - to push back the start time
  • 年金(ねんきん)受給(じゅきゅう)を繰(く)り下(さ)げる - to defer pension receipt

ANTONYM:
繰(く)り上(あ)げる - to move up, advance (the opposite operation)

Related Words

Antonym: 繰(く)り上(あ)げる (to advance/move up)
