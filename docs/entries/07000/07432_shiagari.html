仕上(しあ)がり

しあがり
noun
finish; completion; final result
1. finish; completion; final result
The state or quality of something when it's completed. Refers to how something turns out after being made, processed, or prepared.
この料理(りょうり)は仕上(しあ)がりが美(うつく)しい。
This dish has a beautiful finish.
写真(しゃしん)の仕上(しあ)がりを確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the finished photos.
期待以上(きたいいじょう)の仕上(しあ)がりだった。
The result exceeded expectations.

DERIVED FROM:
The verb 仕上(しあ)がる (to be finished, to turn out).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕上(しあ)がりを確認(かくにん)する - to check the finish/result
  • 仕上(しあ)がりが良(よ)い/悪(わる)い - good/bad finish
  • 仕上(しあ)がり具合(ぐあい) - state of completion
  • 仕上(しあ)がり日(び) - completion date
  • 仕上(しあ)がりイメージ - finished image/visualization

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • 料理(りょうり) - cooking (appearance and taste when done)
  • 写真(しゃしん)・印刷(いんさつ) - photos/printing (final quality)
  • 作品(さくひん) - artwork (final state)
  • 建築(けんちく) - construction (completed appearance)
  • クリーニング - dry cleaning (condition when returned)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 仕上(しあ)がる - to be finished (intransitive verb)
  • 仕上(しあ)げる - to finish (transitive verb)
  • 仕上(しあ)げ - finishing touches
  • 出来(でき)上(あ)がり - completion, finished product
  • 完成(かんせい) - completion

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in everyday conversation, particularly when discussing the result of work or products.
