音色(ねいろ)

ねいろ
noun
tone; timbre; sound quality

Definitions

1. timbre; tone color; the distinctive quality of a sound
The characteristic quality of a musical sound that distinguishes different instruments or voices, even when playing the same pitch at the same volume.

Examples

このピアノは美(うつく)しい音色(ねいろ)だ。
This piano has a beautiful tone.
バイオリンの音色(ねいろ)に惹(ひ)かれた。
I was captivated by the violin's timbre.
楽器(がっき)によって音色(ねいろ)が違(ちが)う。
Each instrument has a different tone quality.
彼女(かのじょ)の声(こえ)は温(あたた)かい音色(ねいろ)がある。
Her voice has a warm tone.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美(うつく)しい音色(ねいろ): beautiful tone
  • 温(あたた)かい音色(ねいろ): warm tone
  • 澄(す)んだ音色(ねいろ): clear tone
  • 独特(どくとく)の音色(ねいろ): distinctive timbre
  • 音色(ねいろ)を奏(かな)でる: to produce a tone

READINGS:
Can also be read おんしょく in more technical/academic contexts, but ねいろ is more common in everyday usage.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 音色(ねいろ) vs. 音質(おんしつ)
  • 音色(ねいろ): timbre, the characteristic quality of a sound (often used for musical/aesthetic discussion)
  • 音質(おんしつ): sound quality, often used for audio equipment and recordings

USAGE:
Applies to both musical instruments and voices. Often used with adjectives describing warmth, clarity, or emotional quality.

Related Words

Related: 調律(ちょうりつ) (tuning)
Related: 楽器(がっき) (musical instrument)
