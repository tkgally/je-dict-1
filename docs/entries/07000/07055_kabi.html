カビ

かび
noun
mold; mildew
1. mold; mildew
A type of fungus that grows in damp conditions, appearing as fuzzy patches on food, walls, or other surfaces.
パンにカビが生(は)えた。
Mold grew on the bread.
浴室(よくしつ)のカビを取(と)る。
I remove the mold in the bathroom.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)はカビが発生(はっせい)しやすい。
Mold tends to grow during the rainy season.

カビ is the fungus that appears as fuzzy patches on damp food, walls, and fabric. Normally written in katakana; the kanji 黴(かび) exists but is rare.

VERB PATTERNS:

  • カビが生(は)える: mould grows (the everyday phrase)
  • カビが発生(はっせい)する: mould develops (more formal, used in notices)
  • カビを取(と)る: to remove mould

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黒(くろ)カビ, 青(あお)カビ: black mould, blue mould
  • カビ臭(くさ)い: musty, smelling of mould
  • カビ取(と)り剤(ざい): mould remover (a supermarket staple)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan's summer is humid, and the 梅雨(つゆ) (rainy season) in particular makes mould a routine household worry. Airing rooms, running 換気(かんき) fans in the 浴室(よくしつ), and using moisture absorbers are ordinary parts of the domestic calendar rather than emergency measures.
