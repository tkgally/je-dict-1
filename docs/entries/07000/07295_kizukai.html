気遣(きづか)い

きづかい
noun
concern; consideration; thoughtfulness

Definitions

1. concern; consideration
Thoughtful attention to others' needs or feelings; being considerate of others.
2. worry; anxiety
A feeling of unease or concern about something.

Examples

お気遣(きづか)いありがとうございます。
Thank you for your concern/thoughtfulness.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつも周(まわ)りへの気遣(きづか)いを忘(わす)れない。
She never forgets to be considerate of those around her.
お気遣(きづか)いなく。
Please don't worry about it./Don't trouble yourself.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お気遣(きづか)いありがとうございます - Thank you for your concern
  • お気遣(きづか)いなく - Please don't trouble yourself
  • 気遣(きづか)いのできる人(ひと) - a considerate person

NUANCE:
気遣(きづか)い refers to the act of being attentive and considerate toward others. Often used in polite exchanges to thank someone for their thoughtfulness or to tell them not to worry.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 心遣(こころづか)い - thoughtfulness (more about emotional consideration)
  • 心配(こころくば)り - care, attention (more about attentive behavior)
  • 目配(めくば)り - keeping an eye on things

Related Words
