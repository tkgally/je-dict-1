ひょいと

ひょいと
adverb
quickly; lightly; nimbly
1. with a quick, nimble movement
Describes a swift, effortless physical action, often involving jumping or picking something up.
猫(ねこ)がひょいと塀(へい)を飛(と)び越(こ)えた。
The cat jumped over the fence with ease.
ひょいと荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ち上(あ)げた。
I lifted the luggage up effortlessly.
子供(こども)がひょいと水溜(みずたま)りを飛(と)び越(こ)えた。
The child nimbly jumped over the puddle.
2. casually; suddenly; unexpectedly
Describes doing something without much thought or appearing somewhere unexpectedly.
友達(ともだち)がひょいと遊(あそ)びに来(き)た。
A friend dropped by unexpectedly.
ひょいと思(おも)い出(だ)した。
It suddenly came to mind.
散歩(さんぽ)の途中(とちゅう)でひょいと本屋(ほんや)に立(た)ち寄(よ)った。
I casually stopped by a bookstore during my walk.

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo/adverb) describing manner of action.

SENSE 1 - PHYSICAL MOVEMENT:

  • Emphasizes ease and swiftness
  • Often used with jumping, lifting, dodging
  • Implies agility or lightness

SENSE 2 - CASUAL/SUDDEN:

  • Dropping by casually
  • Remembering suddenly
  • Doing something without much planning

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ひょいと飛(と)び越(こ)える (to jump over easily)
  • ひょいと持(も)ち上(あ)げる (to pick up effortlessly)
  • ひょいと現(あらわ)れる (to appear suddenly)
  • ひょいと覗(のぞ)く (to take a quick peek)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぴょんと: with a hop (more bouncy)
  • さっと: quickly, swiftly (more deliberate)
  • ふらっと: casually, aimlessly (for visiting)
  • ひょっこり: suddenly appearing
