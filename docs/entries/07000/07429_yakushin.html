躍進(やくしん)

やくしん
noun, suru-verb
rapid advance; remarkable progress; leaping forward

Definitions

1. rapid advance; remarkable progress; leaping forward
Making rapid, impressive progress or advancement. Used for companies, countries, athletes, or any entity that achieves notable growth or success in a short time.

Examples

その会社(かいしゃ)は急速(きゅうそく)に躍進(やくしん)を遂(と)げた。
That company achieved rapid advancement.
新興国(しんこうこく)が世界経済(せかいけいざい)で躍進(やくしん)している。
Emerging nations are making rapid progress in the global economy.
若手(わかて)選手(せんしゅ)の躍進(やくしん)が目立(めだ)った。
The remarkable progress of young players stood out.

Notes

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 躍(やく) - leap, jump
  • 進(しん) - advance, progress

The compound literally captures the image of "leaping forward" in progress.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 躍進(やくしん)する - to make rapid progress
  • 躍進(やくしん)を遂(と)げる - to achieve rapid advancement
  • 目覚(めざ)ましい躍進(やくしん) - remarkable progress
  • 経済的(けいざいてき)躍進(やくしん) - economic leap forward

NUANCE:
躍進(やくしん) is always positive, implying impressive, noteworthy advancement that exceeds normal expectations.

CONTRASTING TERMS:

  • 停滞(ていたい) - stagnation (opposite)
  • 衰退(すいたい) - decline (opposite)
  • 後退(こうたい) - retreat, regression

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 飛躍(ひやく) - leap, great progress
  • 進歩(しんぽ) - progress, advancement
  • 発展(はってん) - development
  • 成長(せいちょう) - growth

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in news, business, and sports reporting.

Related Words

Antonym: 停滞(ていたい) (stagnation)
Antonym: 衰退(すいたい) (decline)
