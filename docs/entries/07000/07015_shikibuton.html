敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)

しきぶとん
noun
mattress futon; bottom futon
See also: 敷布団(しきぶとん) (mattress futon (敷布団))
1. mattress futon; bottom futon; sleeping mat
The futon placed on the floor to sleep on, providing cushioning from the hard floor. Contrasts with 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん) (top covering).
敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を敷(し)いて寝(ね)る準備(じゅんび)をした。
I laid out the mattress futon to prepare for bed.
朝(あさ)、敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を押(お)し入(い)れにしまう。
In the morning, I put away the mattress futon in the closet.
この敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)は厚(あつ)くて寝心地(ねごこち)がいい。
This mattress futon is thick and comfortable to sleep on.

敷(し)き布団(ぶとん) is the lower layer of Japanese bedding — the padded mattress futon laid on the floor to sleep on.

Formation: 敷(し)き, from 敷(し)く 'to spread out, to lay down,' + 布団(ぶとん). Literally the 'spread-out futon,' as against the 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん) draped over the sleeper.

Collocations:

  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を敷(し)く: to lay out the futon
  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)をたたむ: to fold it up
  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を干(ほ)す: to air it

Cultural note: in traditional Japanese sleeping the 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん) is laid directly on the 畳(たたみ) at night and stowed in the 押(お)し入(い)れ during the day. That daily cycle is the point: it lets one 部屋(へや) serve as both bedroom and living space, which matters in small homes. Airing the futon regularly is treated as basic housekeeping rather than a chore, since bedding on the floor absorbs moisture.
