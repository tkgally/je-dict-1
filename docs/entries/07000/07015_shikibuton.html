敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)

しきぶとん
noun
mattress futon; bottom futon
1. mattress futon; bottom futon; sleeping mat
The futon placed on the floor to sleep on, providing cushioning from the hard floor. Contrasts with 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん) (top covering).
敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を敷(し)いて寝(ね)る準備(じゅんび)をした。
I laid out the mattress futon to prepare for bed.
朝(あさ)、敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を押(お)し入(い)れにしまう。
In the morning, I put away the mattress futon in the closet.
この敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)は厚(あつ)くて寝心地(ねごこち)がいい。
This mattress futon is thick and comfortable to sleep on.

FORMATION:
敷(し)き (from 敷(し)く, to spread/lay) + 布団(ぶとん) (futon/bedding)

Literally 'spread-out futon.'

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を敷(し)く - to lay out a mattress futon
  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)をたたむ - to fold up a mattress futon
  • 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん)を干(ほ)す - to air out a mattress futon

CULTURAL NOTE:
In traditional Japanese sleeping, the 敷(し)き布団(ぶとん) is laid directly on the tatami floor at night and stored in a 押(お)し入(い)れ (closet) during the day. This allows the room to be used for other purposes.

Related Words

Contrast: 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん) (comforter)
