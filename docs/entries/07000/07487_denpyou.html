伝票(でんぴょう)

でんぴょう
noun
slip; voucher; chit; receipt
1. slip; voucher; chit; transaction slip
A small document used to record business transactions, orders, deliveries, or accounting entries. Common in retail, restaurants, and accounting.
売上(うりあげ)伝票(でんぴょう)を発行(はっこう)する。
Issue a sales slip.
レジで伝票(でんぴょう)をもらってください。
Please get the slip at the register.
伝票(でんぴょう)を整理(せいり)して帳簿(ちょうぼ)に記入(きにゅう)する。
Organize the slips and enter them in the ledger.

WORD STRUCTURE:
伝(でん) (transmit) + 票(ひょう) (slip, vote)

TYPES OF 伝票(でんぴょう):

  • 売上(うりあげ)伝票(でんぴょう) - sales slip
  • 仕入(しい)れ伝票(でんぴょう) - purchase slip
  • 入金(にゅうきん)伝票(でんぴょう) - receipt voucher
  • 出金(しゅっきん)伝票(でんぴょう) - payment voucher
  • 振替(ふりかえ)伝票(でんぴょう) - transfer slip
  • 納品(のうひん)伝票(でんぴょう) - delivery slip

RESTAURANT CONTEXT:
In restaurants, 伝票(でんぴょう) (also called "お会計(かいけい)」) is the bill/check that the customer receives.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 伝票(でんぴょう)を切(き)る - to write up a slip
  • 伝票(でんぴょう)を回(まわ)す - to pass along a slip
  • 伝票(でんぴょう)処理(しょり) - slip processing

ACCOUNTING USE:
In bookkeeping, 伝票(でんぴょう) serves as the source document for recording transactions in the 帳簿(ちょうぼ) (ledger).
