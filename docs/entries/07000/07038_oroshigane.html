おろし金(がね)

おろしがね
noun
grater
1. grater
A kitchen tool with a rough surface used to grate foods like ginger, daikon radish, or wasabi.
おろし金(がね)で大根(だいこん)をおろす。
I grate daikon radish with a grater.
生姜(しょうが)をおろすのに小(ちい)さいおろし金(がね)を使(つか)う。
I use a small grater to grate ginger.
おろし金(がね)は使(つか)った後(あと)すぐに洗(あら)う。
I wash the grater right after using it.

おろし金(がね) is a grater — in Japanese kitchens usually a flat plate or shallow dish covered in fine raised teeth, rather than a Western box grater. It is used for foods that are meant to come out as a wet pulp: daikon, ginger, wasabi, garlic.

Formation: おろし 'grating', from おろす, + 金(がね) 'metal'. Also written 卸(おろ)し金(がね) or 下(お)ろし金(がね); the kana-plus-kanji form above is the most common.

Types:

  • 鬼(おに)おろし: a coarse grater with large teeth, for a rough, flaky texture
  • 銅(どう)製(せい)おろし金(がね): a copper grater — the traditional professional tool, prized for a fine, non-bruising cut
  • セラミックおろし金(がね): ceramic, common in home kitchens

Collocations:

  • おろし金(がね)でおろす: to grate with a grater
  • 大根(だいこん)おろし: grated daikon — the standard accompaniment to grilled fish and tempura
  • 薬味(やくみ)をおろす: to grate a condiment

Related Words
