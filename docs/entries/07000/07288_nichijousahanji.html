日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)

にちじょうさはんじ
noun
everyday occurrence; commonplace event; routine matter

Definitions

1. everyday occurrence; routine matter
Something that happens so frequently that it is considered ordinary and unremarkable.

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)の遅延(ちえん)はこの路線(ろせん)では日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)だ。
Train delays are an everyday occurrence on this line.
残業(ざんぎょう)は日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)になっている。
Overtime has become a daily routine.
彼(かれ)にとって徹夜(てつや)は日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)らしい。
Apparently pulling all-nighters is routine for him.

Notes

WORD FORMATION (YOJIJUKUGO):
日常(にちじょう) (daily life) + 茶飯事(さはんじ) (tea and rice = everyday meals)
→ as ordinary as daily tea and rice = everyday occurrence

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜は日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)だ - ~ is an everyday occurrence
  • 日常茶飯事(にちじょうさはんじ)になる - to become routine

NUANCE:
Often used with slightly negative or resigned tone, suggesting something happens too frequently. Can imply that something that should be unusual has become normalized.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • よくあること - something that happens often
  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ) - natural, obvious
