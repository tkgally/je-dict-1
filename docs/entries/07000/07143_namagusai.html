生臭(なまぐさ)い

なまぐさい
i-adjective
fishy, raw-smelling

Definitions

1. having a fishy or raw smell
Smelling of raw fish, meat, or blood; having an unpleasant raw organic odor.

Examples

魚(さかな)を触(さわ)ったら手(て)が生臭(なまぐさ)くなった。
My hands became fishy after touching the fish.
市場(いちば)は朝(あさ)から生臭(なまぐさ)い匂(にお)いがする。
The market has a fishy smell from early morning.
レモンを絞(しぼ)ると生臭(なまぐさ)さが消(き)える。
Squeezing lemon removes the fishy smell.
生臭(なまぐさ)い血(ち)の匂(にお)いがした。
There was a raw, bloody smell.

Notes

生臭(なまぐさ)い is a compound i-adjective combining 生(なま) (raw) + 臭(くさ)い (smelling of).

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 生臭(なまぐさ)く
  • Noun form: 生臭(なまぐさ)さ

USAGE NOTE:
Primarily used for fish, but also applies to raw meat, blood, or any uncooked animal product. Often describes an unpleasant smell that people want to eliminate.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生臭(なまぐさ)い匂(にお)い - fishy smell
  • 生臭(なまぐさ)い手(て) - fishy-smelling hands
  • 生臭(なまぐさ)さを消(け)す - to remove the fishy smell

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生臭(なまぐさ)い vs. 青臭(あおくさ)い: 生臭(なまぐさ)い is for raw fish/meat; 青臭(あおくさ)い is for raw vegetables/grass
  • 生臭(なまぐさ)い vs. 臭(くさ)い: 臭(くさ)い is generic bad smell; 生臭(なまぐさ)い specifically means raw organic smell

Related Words

Related: 青臭(あおくさ)い (grassy smell)
Related: 焦(こ)げ臭(くさ)い (burnt smell)
