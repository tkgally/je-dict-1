甘味(あまみ)

あまみ
noun
sweetness; sweet taste; sweets
See also: 甘(あま)み (sweetness (variant))
1. sweetness; sweet taste
The sweet taste quality, one of the five basic tastes.
この桃(もも)は甘味(あまみ)が十分(じゅうぶん)ある。
This peach has plenty of sweetness.
砂糖(さとう)を入(い)れて甘味(あまみ)を足(た)した。
I added sugar to increase the sweetness.
このトマトは加熱(かねつ)すると甘味(あまみ)が増(ま)す。
This tomato gets sweeter when heated.
2. sweets; sweet foods
Sweet foods or confections. Also read as かんみ in this meaning.
食後(しょくご)に甘味(あまみ)を楽(たの)しむ。
I enjoy sweets after meals.
京都(きょうと)の甘味(かんみ)処(どころ)で抹茶(まっちゃ)パフェを食(た)べた。
I had a matcha parfait at a sweet shop in Kyoto.
Read as かんみどころ when referring to sweet shops.
あの店(みせ)は和(わ)の甘味(かんみ)が人気(にんき)だ。
That shop is popular for its Japanese sweets.

甘味(あまみ) is sweetness as a quality of taste (sense 1), and by extension sweet things to eat (sense 2).

Formation: 甘(あま)い 'sweet' + 味(み) 'taste.'

Two readings, and they split the two senses:

  • あまみ is the reading for sweetness as a flavour — what a 桃(もも) or a トマト has.
  • かんみ is used for sweets as food, and is fixed in the compounds below.

Collocations:

  • 甘味(あまみ)が強(つよ)い: strongly sweet
  • 甘味(あまみ)を加(くわ)える: to add sweetness
  • 甘味(かんみ)処(どころ): a traditional sweet shop serving things like anmitsu and matcha — read かんみどころ
  • 甘味(かんみ)料(りょう): sweetener, as listed on an ingredients label — read かんみりょう

Related words:

  • 酸味(さんみ): sourness, the taste most often balanced against sweetness
  • 旨味(うまみ): umami
  • 甘(あま)い: the adjective 'sweet.' As with the other taste nouns, 甘味(あまみ) names the quality while the adjective describes the food.
