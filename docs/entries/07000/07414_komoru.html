籠(こ)もる

こもる
verb (godan, intransitive)
to shut oneself in; to be filled with; to be muffled

Definitions

1. to shut oneself in; to seclude oneself; to stay indoors
To withdraw into a room, house, or other space and not come out. Often implies focusing intensely on something or avoiding the outside world.
2. to be filled with; to contain; to be imbued with
For a space or thing to be filled with something (heat, smoke, emotions, etc.).
3. to be muffled; to be stuffy
For sound or air to be trapped and not escape properly.

Examples

部屋(へや)に籠(こ)もって勉強(べんきょう)した。
I shut myself in my room and studied.
彼女(かのじょ)の手紙(てがみ)には愛情(あいじょう)が籠(こ)もっている。
Her letter is filled with affection.
部屋(へや)に熱気(ねっき)が籠(こ)もっている。
The room is stuffy with heat.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)).

CONJUGATION: Godan verb (-る type)

  • 籠(こ)もらない (negative)
  • 籠(こ)もります (polite)
  • 籠(こ)もって (te-form)
  • 籠(こ)もった (past)

KANJI NOTE:
Can be written as {籠}もる or {篭}もる. Also commonly written in hiragana.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)に籠(こ)もる - to shut oneself in a room
  • 家(いえ)に籠(こ)もる - to stay at home
  • 思(おも)いが籠(こ)もった - filled with feeling/emotion
  • 気持(きも)ちが籠(こ)もる - to be filled with feeling
  • 声(こえ)が籠(こ)もる - voice becomes muffled

MODERN USAGE:
引(ひ)き籠(こ)もり (hikikomori) - social withdrawal/recluse - is derived from this verb.

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in everyday conversation and writing.
