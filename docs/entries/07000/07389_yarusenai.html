やるせない

やるせない
i-adjective
helpless; forlorn; unbearable
1. helpless; forlorn; having no outlet for one's feelings
Describes a feeling of sadness, frustration, or longing with no way to relieve or express it. Often implies a sense of melancholy or emotional heaviness.
やるせない気持(きも)ちで胸(むね)がいっぱいになった。
My heart was filled with a helpless, forlorn feeling.
彼(かれ)の死(し)を聞(き)いて、やるせない思(おも)いだった。
Hearing of his death, I felt an unbearable sadness.
故郷(ふるさと)を離(はな)れるやるせなさを感(かん)じた。
I felt the forlorn sadness of leaving my hometown.

FORMS:

  • やるせない (dictionary/attributive)
  • やるせなさ (noun form: the feeling of helplessness/forlornness)
  • やるせなく (adverbial)

ETYMOLOGY:
From 遣(や)る瀬(せ) (やるせ, an outlet for emotions) + ない (without). Literally "without an outlet."

USAGE:

やるせない describes a deep emotional state where one feels sad, frustrated, or melancholy but has no way to express or relieve those feelings. Common collocations:

  • やるせない気持(きも)ち - helpless/forlorn feelings
  • やるせない思(おも)い - unbearable emotions
  • やるせない恋(こい) - unrequited/melancholy love

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(せつ)ない - painful, heartrending (more acute emotional pain)
  • 空(むな)しい - empty, futile (more about meaninglessness)
  • もどかしい - frustrating (more about impatience)

REGISTER:
Literary to neutral. Common in songs, poetry, and emotional writing.
