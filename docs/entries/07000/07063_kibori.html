木彫(きぼ)り

きぼり
noun
wood carving
1. wood carving
The art or craft of carving objects from wood, or an object made through this process.
祖父(そふ)は木彫(きぼ)りの熊(くま)を作(つく)った。
My grandfather made a wooden carved bear.
北海道(ほっかいどう)では木彫(きぼ)りの民芸品(みんげいひん)が有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Hokkaido is famous for its wood-carved folk crafts.
木彫(きぼ)りを趣味(しゅみ)にしている。
I do wood carving as a hobby.

木彫(きぼ)り names both the activity of carving wood and the carved object itself, so it can be the subject of a sentence or a modifier: 木彫(きぼ)りの + noun is the usual shape.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 木彫(きぼ)りの熊(くま): a carved wooden bear
  • 木彫(きぼ)りの像(ぞう): a wooden statue
  • 木彫(きぼ)り細工(ざいく): wood-carving work
  • 木彫(きぼ)り師(し): a wood carver by trade

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 彫刻(ちょうこく) is sculpture as an art form, in any material, and is the word for a museum piece. 木彫(きぼ)り names the material and the technique, and covers craft objects as readily as art.
  • 工芸(こうげい) is the umbrella term for crafts of this kind.

CULTURAL NOTE: the 木彫(きぼ)りの熊(くま) — a bear with a salmon in its mouth — is the standard souvenir of 北海道(ほっかいどう) and is instantly recognisable to any Japanese adult. It is associated with Ainu craft traditions, though the familiar form was developed for the tourist trade in the twentieth century.
