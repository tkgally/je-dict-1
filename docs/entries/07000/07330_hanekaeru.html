跳(は)ね返(かえ)る

はねかえる
verb (godan, intransitive)
to bounce back; to rebound; to ricochet

Definitions

1. to bounce back; to rebound; to spring back
For a physical object to bounce off a surface and return.
2. to backfire; to have a negative effect return
For negative consequences to return to the person who caused them, or for something to have an unintended rebound effect.

Examples

ボールが壁(かべ)に当(あ)たって跳(は)ね返(かえ)ってきた。
The ball hit the wall and bounced back.
弾丸(だんがん)が装甲(そうこう)に跳(は)ね返(かえ)った。
The bullet ricocheted off the armor.
悪口(わるくち)は結局(けっきょく)自分(じぶん)に跳(は)ね返(かえ)ってくる。
Bad-mouthing others will eventually come back to haunt you.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 跳(は)ね返(かえ)る → 跳(は)ね返(かえ)らない (negative)
  • 跳(は)ね返(かえ)る → 跳(は)ね返(かえ)って (te-form)
  • 跳(は)ね返(かえ)る → 跳(は)ね返(かえ)った (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 跳(は)ねる (to jump/splash) + 返(かえ)る (to return)

The combination means to jump/spring back.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • ボールが跳(は)ね返(かえ)る - ball bounces back
  • 光(ひかり)が跳(は)ね返(かえ)る - light reflects
  • 自分(じぶん)に跳(は)ね返(かえ)る - to come back to oneself

TRANSITIVITY:
跳(は)ね返(かえ)す is the transitive form meaning 'to repel, to bounce back (something)'.

Related Words

Pair verb: 跳(は)ね返(かえ)す (transitive)
Related: 跳(は)ねる (to jump/splash)
