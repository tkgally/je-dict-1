取消(とりけし)

とりけし
noun
cancellation; revocation
1. cancellation; revocation
The act of canceling or revoking something, such as a reservation, order, license, or statement.
予約(よやく)の取消(とりけし)をお願(ねが)いします。
I'd like to cancel my reservation.
注文(ちゅうもん)の取消(とりけし)は発送(はっそう)前(まえ)までです。
Order cancellation is only possible before shipping.
免許(めんきょ)の取消(とりけし)処分(しょぶん)を受(う)けた。
I received a license revocation.

FORMATION:
取(と)り (taking) + 消(け)し (erasing)

VERB FORM:

  • 取(と)り消(け)す (to cancel)

WRITING:
Also written as 取り消し(とりけし) with okurigana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 取消(とりけし)料(りょう) (cancellation fee)
  • 取消(とりけし)手続(てつづ)き (cancellation procedure)
  • 免許(めんきょ)取消(とりけし) (license revocation)
  • 予約(よやく)取消(とりけし) (reservation cancellation)

RELATED:

  • キャンセル (cancel - loanword, more casual)

Related Words

Synonym: キャンセル (cancellation (loanword))
