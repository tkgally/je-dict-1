押(お)し寄(よ)せる

おしよせる
verb (ichidan, intransitive)
to surge; to crowd in; to press forward

Definitions

1. to surge; to rush in; to press forward
To move forward in a mass or with force, like waves or a crowd.
2. to crowd in; to throng
For people or things to gather or arrive in large numbers.

Examples

大波(おおなみ)が岸(きし)に押(お)し寄(よ)せる。
Large waves surge toward the shore.
観客(かんきゃく)がステージに押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
The audience surged toward the stage.
注文(ちゅうもん)が押(お)し寄(よ)せて対応(たいおう)しきれない。
Orders are flooding in and we can't keep up.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 押(お)し寄(よ)せる → 押(お)し寄(よ)せない (negative)
  • 押(お)し寄(よ)せる → 押(お)し寄(よ)せて (te-form)
  • 押(お)し寄(よ)せる → 押(お)し寄(よ)せた (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 押(お)す (to push) + 寄(よ)せる (to bring near)

USAGE:

Often used to describe:

  • 波(なみ)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (waves surging)
  • 群衆(ぐんしゅう)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (crowd pressing in)
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせが押(お)し寄(よ)せる (inquiries flooding in)
  • 不安(ふあん)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (anxiety welling up)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 殺到(さっとう)する - to rush in, to flood (more intense)

Related Words

Related: 押(お)す (to push)
