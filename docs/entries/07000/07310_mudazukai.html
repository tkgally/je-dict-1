無駄遣(むだづか)い

むだづかい
noun
waste; wasteful spending; squandering
1. wasteful spending; squandering
Using money, time, or resources carelessly or on unnecessary things.
無駄遣(むだづか)いをやめて貯金(ちょきん)する。
To stop wasteful spending and start saving.
それは時間(じかん)の無駄遣(むだづか)いだ。
That's a waste of time.
税金(ぜいきん)の無駄遣(むだづか)いをなくすべきだ。
We should eliminate wasteful spending of tax money.

WORD FORMATION:
無駄(むだ) (waste, uselessness) + 遣(つか)い (use, spending) → wasteful use = squandering

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 無駄遣(むだづか)いをする - to waste (money, time, etc.)
  • 無駄遣(むだづか)いをやめる - to stop wasting
  • 時間(じかん)の無駄遣(むだづか)い - waste of time
  • 税金(ぜいきん)の無駄遣(むだづか)い - wasteful spending of taxes
  • お金(かね)の無駄遣(むだづか)い - wasteful spending of money

NUANCE:
Can apply to money, time, energy, resources, or talent. Often used critically when someone or something (like the government) is perceived as spending carelessly.
