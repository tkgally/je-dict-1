お節介(せっかい)

おせっかい
na-adjective
meddlesome; nosy; intrusive

Definitions

1. meddlesome; nosy; intrusive
Interfering in others' affairs uninvited; offering unwanted help or advice.

Examples

お節介(せっかい)かもしれませんが、一言(ひとこと)言(い)わせてください。
This might be meddling, but let me say one thing.
彼女(かのじょ)はお節介(せっかい)な性格(せいかく)だ。
She has a nosy/meddlesome personality.
お節介(せっかい)を焼(や)く。
To meddle; to stick one's nose in.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お節介(せっかい)を焼(や)く - to meddle, to interfere
  • お節介(せっかい)な人(ひと) - a meddlesome person
  • お節介(せっかい)かもしれませんが - this might be meddling, but...
  • 余計(よけい)なお節介(せっかい) - unwanted interference

NUANCE:
お節介(せっかい) describes someone who involves themselves in others' business, often with good intentions but without being asked. It can range from mildly annoying busybody behavior to genuinely helpful concern.

POLITE USAGE:
「お節介(せっかい)かもしれませんが…」 is a polite way to preface unsolicited advice, acknowledging that one might be overstepping.
