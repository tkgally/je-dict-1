目配(めくば)り

めくばり
noun
watchfulness; keeping an eye on things; vigilance

Definitions

1. watchfulness; keeping an eye on things
The act of carefully observing one's surroundings and paying attention to multiple things at once.

Examples

上司(じょうし)は部下(ぶか)への目配(めくば)りを怠(おこた)らない。
The boss doesn't neglect keeping an eye on subordinates.
店内(てんない)の目配(めくば)りが行(い)き届(とど)いている。
The store is thoroughly watched over.
会場(かいじょう)全体(ぜんたい)に目配(めくば)りをする。
To keep an eye on the entire venue.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
目(め) (eye) + 配(くば)り (distribution) → distributing one's eyes = watchfulness

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目配(めくば)りをする - to keep watch, to keep an eye on things
  • 目配(めくば)りを怠(おこた)らない - to not neglect watching
  • 目配(めくば)りが行(い)き届(とど)く - to be thoroughly vigilant

NUANCE:
目配(めくば)り emphasizes the visual aspect of attentiveness—scanning the environment, noticing details, and being aware of what's happening around you. Often used for managers, staff, or anyone responsible for overseeing a space or group.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)を配(くば)る (verb) - to keep watch, be vigilant
  • 気配(きくば)り - attentiveness to atmosphere/mood

Related Words

Related: 心配(こころくば)り (care)
Related: 気遣(きづか)い (concern)
