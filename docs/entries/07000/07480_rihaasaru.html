リハーサル

りはーさる
noun
rehearsal

Definitions

1. rehearsal; practice session before a performance
A practice session for a performance such as a concert, play, or broadcast, to prepare and refine the presentation before the actual event.

Examples

本番(ほんばん)前(まえ)にリハーサルをする。
We do a rehearsal before the actual performance.
リハーサルは午後(ごご)3時(じ)から始(はじ)まる。
The rehearsal starts at 3 PM.
舞台(ぶたい)のリハーサルに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a stage rehearsal.
番組(ばんぐみ)収録(しゅうろく)前(まえ)のリハーサルで流(なが)れを確認(かくにん)した。
We confirmed the flow during the rehearsal before the show recording.

Notes

ABBREVIATION:

Often shortened to リハ in casual speech.

  • リハ中(ちゅう): during rehearsal
  • リハ室(しつ): rehearsal room/studio

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リハーサルをする: to have a rehearsal
  • リハーサルに入(はい)る: to start rehearsals
  • 通(とお)しリハーサル: run-through rehearsal
  • ゲネプロ (総稽古(そうげいこ)): dress rehearsal (from German "Generalprobe")

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • リハーサル: rehearsal (general term, from English)
  • 稽古(けいこ): practice (especially for traditional arts)
  • 練習(れんしゅう): practice/training (general)
  • ゲネプロ: dress rehearsal

USAGE:
Used for music performances, theater, TV/radio broadcasts, ceremonies, and other events requiring preparation.

Related Words

Related: 練習(れんしゅう) (practice)
Related: 本番(ほんばん) (actual performance)
