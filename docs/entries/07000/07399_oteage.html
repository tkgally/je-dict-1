お手上(てあ)げ

おてあげ
noun
giving up; throwing in the towel; at a loss
1. giving up; being at a loss; throwing in the towel
A state of having exhausted all options and being unable to do anything more about a situation. Often expressed with a gesture of raising both hands in surrender.
もうお手上(てあ)げだ。
I give up.
この問題(もんだい)には、もうお手上(てあ)げだ。
I give up on this problem.
彼(かれ)の頑固(がんこ)さにはお手上(てあ)げだよ。
I'm at a complete loss with his stubbornness.
何(なに)をやっても直(なお)らなくて、お手上(てあ)げ状態(じょうたい)だ。
No matter what I try, I can't fix it - I'm completely stuck.
子供(こども)が全然(ぜんぜん)言(い)うことを聞(き)かなくて、親(おや)もお手上(てあ)げの様子(ようす)だった。
The child wouldn't listen at all, and the parents looked completely helpless.

WORD FORMATION:
お (honorific prefix) + 手(て) (hands) + 上(あ)げ (raising)
Literally "raising the hands" - the gesture of surrender. The お here is frozen into the word: there is no plain form 手上げ, so the prefix is not optional and adds no politeness.

USAGE:
Used when someone has tried everything and has no options left. It is a noun, and normally stands as a predicate with だ or です rather than taking a verb of its own. The thing that defeats you is marked with に, usually followed by は.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お手上(てあ)げだ - I give up; I am at a loss
  • お手上(てあ)げ状態(じょうたい) - a state of having run out of options
  • 〜にはお手上(てあ)げだ - I am at a loss with ~

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 万策尽(ばんさくつ)きる - to have exhausted every option (formal, written)
  • どうしようもない - there is nothing to be done (about the situation itself)
  • 降参(こうさん) - to give in, to surrender (to an opponent, or playfully in a game)
  • あきらめる - to give up, as an act of will; お手上(てあ)げ describes the helpless state rather than the decision

REGISTER:
Casual and common in everyday conversation. It is light rather than despairing - often said with a wry smile about a stubborn person, a broken appliance, or a problem beyond one's skill - so it is not the word for serious defeat or grief.

Related Words

Synonym: 万策尽(ばんさくつ)きる (to exhaust all options)
Related: 降参(こうさん) (surrender; giving in)
Related: あきらめる (to give up)
Related: どうしようもない (helpless; hopeless)
