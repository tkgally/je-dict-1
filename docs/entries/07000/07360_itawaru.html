いたわる

いたわる
verb (godan, transitive)
to care for; to treat with kindness; to show compassion

Definitions

1. to care for; to treat with kindness; to show compassion
To treat someone gently and with compassion, especially someone who is sick, elderly, weak, or in need of care.

Examples

病気(びょうき)の母(はは)をいたわる。
I care for my sick mother.
体(からだ)をいたわってください。
Please take care of your health.
彼(かれ)は部下(ぶか)をいたわる上司(じょうし)として知(し)られている。
He is known as a boss who treats his subordinates with kindness.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を to mark the person or thing being cared for.

KANJI:
Can be written as 労(いたわ)る, though hiragana is more common.

USAGE:
いたわる implies gentle, compassionate treatment, often directed toward those who are vulnerable, tired, or in need. It suggests warmth and consideration rather than mere assistance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体(からだ)をいたわる (to take care of one's body)
  • 心(こころ)をいたわる (to comfort/soothe)
  • 老人(ろうじん)をいたわる (to care for the elderly)
  • いたわりの気持(きも)ち (feelings of compassion)

RELATED NOUN:
いたわり - compassion, kindness, tender care

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気遣(きづか)う - to be considerate/concerned about (more about attention)
  • 世話(せわ)をする - to look after (more practical care)
  • 大切(たいせつ)にする - to value/treasure (broader meaning)
