年下(としした)

としした
noun
younger (in age); junior
1. younger; junior (in age)
Being younger than someone else; a person who is younger.
彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)より二(に)歳(さい)年下(としした)だ。
She is two years younger than me.
年下(としした)の同僚(どうりょう)に教(おし)えてもらった。
I was taught by a younger colleague.
夫(おっと)は五(ご)歳(さい)年下(としした)です。
My husband is five years younger than me.

WORD FORMATION:
年(とし) (age, year) + 下(した) (below) → below in years = younger

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜より年下(としした) - younger than ~
  • 年下(としした)の人(ひと) - younger person
  • 五(ご)歳(さい)年下(としした) - five years younger

USAGE:
Describes age relationships between people. In Japanese society, age differences often affect how people interact and what language they use with each other.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Dating or marrying someone 年下(としした) used to be less common, especially for women with younger men, but has become more accepted in modern Japan.

Related Words

Antonym: 年上(としうえ) (older)
