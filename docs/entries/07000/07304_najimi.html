馴染(なじ)み

なじみ
noun
familiarity; acquaintance; regular (customer)
1. familiarity; being used to
The state of being familiar with something or comfortable with it.
新(あたら)しい環境(かんきょう)に馴染(なじ)みがない。
I'm not familiar with the new environment.
2. acquaintance; regular
A person one knows well; a regular customer at a shop or establishment.
この店(みせ)は馴染(なじ)みの客(きゃく)が多(おお)い。
This shop has many regular customers.
幼(おさな)馴染(なじ)みと久(ひさ)しぶりに会(あ)った。
I met with my childhood friend after a long time.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 馴染(なじ)みの店(みせ) - a familiar shop, regular haunt
  • 馴染(なじ)みの客(きゃく) - regular customer
  • 幼(おさな)馴染(なじ)み - childhood friend (very common term)
  • 馴染(なじ)みがある - to be familiar with
  • 馴染(なじ)みがない - to be unfamiliar with

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 馴(な)じむ means "to get used to" or "to become familiar." The word is often written in hiragana as なじみ.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 顔馴染(かおなじ)み - familiar face, acquaintance
  • 馴染(な)じむ (verb) - to become familiar, to fit in
