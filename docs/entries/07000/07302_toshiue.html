年上(としうえ)

としうえ
noun
older (in age); senior

Definitions

1. older; senior (in age)
Being older than someone else; a person who is older.

Examples

彼(かれ)は私(わたし)より三(さん)歳(さい)年上(としうえ)だ。
He is three years older than me.
年上(としうえ)の人(ひと)に敬語(けいご)を使(つか)う。
To use polite language with older people.
妻(つま)は私(わたし)より年上(としうえ)です。
My wife is older than me.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
年(とし) (age, year) + 上(うえ) (above) → above in years = older

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜より年上(としうえ) - older than ~
  • 年上(としうえ)の人(ひと) - older person
  • 三(さん)歳(さい)年上(としうえ) - three years older

USAGE:
Used to describe age relationships between people. Important in Japanese culture where age often determines social hierarchy and language use.

NOTE ON RELATIONSHIPS:
年上(としうえ)の彼女(かのじょ)/彼氏(かれし) often refers to a romantic partner who is older, sometimes significantly so.

Related Words

Antonym: 年下(としした) (younger)
