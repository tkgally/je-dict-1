顔馴染(かおなじ)み

かおなじみ
noun
familiar face; acquaintance

Definitions

1. familiar face; acquaintance
A person one recognizes and has some level of familiarity with, though not necessarily a close friend.

Examples

彼(かれ)とは顔馴染(かおなじ)みだ。
I'm acquainted with him./He's a familiar face.
カフェで顔馴染(かおなじ)みの客(きゃく)と話(はな)した。
I talked with a familiar customer at the café.
この辺(へん)では顔馴染(かおなじ)みが多(おお)い。
I know many people by face around here.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
顔(かお) (face) + 馴染(なじ)み (familiarity) → familiar face = acquaintance

USAGE:
顔馴染(かおなじ)み implies a relationship where you recognize someone and have had some interaction, but they may not be a close friend. It's someone you would nod to or greet casually.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 顔馴染(かおなじ)みになる - to become acquainted
  • 顔馴染(かおなじ)みの店員(てんいん) - a familiar store clerk

CONTRAST:

  • 顔馴染(かおなじ)み - someone you recognize (casual acquaintance)
  • 知(し)り合(あ)い - acquaintance (more formal term)
  • 友人(ゆうじん) - friend (closer relationship)
