宿泊料(しゅくはくりょう)

しゅくはくりょう
noun
accommodation fee; lodging charge
1. accommodation fee; room charge
The fee charged for staying overnight at a hotel, inn, or other lodging facility.
宿泊料(しゅくはくりょう)には朝食(ちょうしょく)が含(ふく)まれています。
Breakfast is included in the accommodation fee.
宿泊料(しゅくはくりょう)は1泊(ぱく)1万円(まんえん)だ。
The accommodation fee is 10,000 yen per night.
繁忙期(はんぼうき)は宿泊料(しゅくはくりょう)が高(たか)くなる。
Accommodation fees go up during the busy season.

COMPOSITION: 宿泊(しゅくはく) (lodging, staying overnight) + 料(りょう) (fee).

SYNONYMS:

  • 宿泊費(しゅくはくひ): accommodation cost/expense
  • 室料(しつりょう): room charge
  • 部屋代(へやだい): room rate (colloquial)

INCLUSIONS/EXCLUSIONS:

  • 素泊(すど)まり: room only (no meals)
  • 朝食付(ちょうしょくつ)き: with breakfast
  • 二食付(にしょくつ)き: with two meals (dinner and breakfast)
  • 夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)き: with dinner

RELATED TERMS:

  • 宿泊税(しゅくはくぜい): accommodation tax
  • サービス料(りょう): service charge
  • 入湯税(にゅうとうぜい): bathing tax (hot spring areas)

PRICING MODELS:

  • 一人(ひとり)当(あ)たり: per person
  • 一部屋(ひとへや)当(あ)たり: per room
  • 1泊(ぱく): per night

Related Words
