叩(たた)き落(お)とす

たたきおとす
verb (godan)
to knock down, to beat off

Definitions

1. to knock down, to swat down
To strike something and cause it to fall; to hit and knock off.
2. to beat down (price), to force down
To aggressively negotiate a price downward.

Examples

蚊(か)を手(て)で叩(たた)き落(お)とした。
I swatted the mosquito down with my hand.
棒(ぼう)で果物(くだもの)を叩(たた)き落(お)とす。
To knock down fruit with a stick.
相手(あいて)の武器(ぶき)を叩(たた)き落(お)とした。
He knocked the opponent's weapon out of their hand.
値段(ねだん)を半額(はんがく)まで叩(たた)き落(お)とした。
I beat the price down to half.

Notes

叩(たた)き落(お)とす is a compound verb: 叩(たた)く (to strike) + 落(お)とす (to drop, to cause to fall).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを叩(たた)き落(お)とす

ASPECT (ている):

  • Telic verb: 叩(たた)き落(お)とした indicates completed action
  • 叩(たた)き落(お)としている describes ongoing/repeated action

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 虫(むし)を叩(たた)き落(お)とす - to swat an insect
  • 武器(ぶき)を叩(たた)き落(お)とす - to knock a weapon away
  • 値段(ねだん)を叩(たた)き落(お)とす - to beat down the price

COMPOUND VERB NOTES:
The ～落(お)とす pattern indicates causing something to fall. Combined with 叩(たた)く, it emphasizes the forceful striking that causes the fall.

Related Words

Related: 叩(たた)く (to strike)
Related: 落(お)とす (to drop)
