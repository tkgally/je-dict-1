パチンコ

ぱちんこ
noun
pachinko

Definitions

1. pachinko; Japanese pinball gambling machine
A mechanical game resembling vertical pinball, played in dedicated parlors. Players launch small steel balls that fall through a field of pins, with the goal of landing balls in winning pockets.

Examples

パチンコに行(い)く。
I'm going to play pachinko.
駅前(えきまえ)にパチンコ店(てん)がある。
There's a pachinko parlor in front of the station.
パチンコで大(おお)きく勝(か)った。
I won big at pachinko.
パチンコにハマって抜(ぬ)け出(だ)せない。
I got hooked on pachinko and can't quit.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パチンコをする / パチンコを打(う)つ: play pachinko
  • パチンコに行(い)く: go to a pachinko parlor
  • パチンコ店(てん) / パチンコ屋(や): pachinko parlor
  • パチンコ依存症(いぞんしょう): pachinko addiction

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • パチスロ (パチンコスロット): pachinko slot machine
  • 玉(たま): pachinko balls
  • 景品(けいひん): prizes (exchanged for balls)
  • 三店方式(さんてんほうしき): three-store system (legal loophole for cash exchange)

LEGAL STATUS:
Gambling for cash is illegal in Japan, but pachinko operates through a legal loophole: players win balls that can be exchanged for prizes, which can then be sold at nearby "exchange shops" for cash.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Pachinko is a major industry in Japan, with thousands of parlors nationwide. The distinctive noise of pachinko parlors and their bright neon signs are a familiar part of Japanese urban landscapes. The industry is often associated with gambling addiction concerns.

ETYMOLOGY:
Onomatopoeic, from the clicking sound (パチン) the balls make.
