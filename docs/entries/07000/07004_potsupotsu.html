ぽつぽつ

ぽつぽつ
adverb, onomatopoeia
bit by bit; sparsely; in drops; here and there

Definitions

1. bit by bit; little by little; gradually
Describes something happening slowly, one piece at a time, often in an unhurried or leisurely manner.
2. sparsely; scattered; here and there
Describes things that are spread out with spaces between them, appearing intermittently.
3. in drops; dripping
Describes rain or liquid falling in scattered drops.

Examples

ぽつぽつと話(はな)し始(はじ)めた。
They started talking little by little.
客(きゃく)がぽつぽつと集(あつ)まってきた。
Customers started gathering one by one.
雨(あめ)がぽつぽつと降(ふ)り始(はじ)めた。
Rain started falling in scattered drops.

Notes

USAGE:
ぽつぽつ(と) + verb

Sense 1 (bit by bit):

  • ぽつぽつ話(はな)す (to speak bit by bit)
  • ぽつぽつ始(はじ)まる (to start gradually)
  • ぽつぽつ集(あつ)まる (to gather one by one)

Sense 2 (sparse distribution):

  • 白髪(しらが)がぽつぽつ出(で)てきた (gray hairs are appearing here and there)
  • 星(ほし)がぽつぽつ見(み)える (stars are visible scattered about)

Sense 3 (drops of rain/liquid):

  • 雨(あめ)がぽつぽつ (rain in scattered drops)

NUANCE:
Conveys a leisurely, unhurried pace with gaps between occurrences. Often suggests something is just beginning or happening intermittently.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぽつりぽつり - Similar meaning (variant)
  • ちらほら - Here and there, scattered
  • ぼちぼち - Bit by bit, so-so (Kansai dialect)

Related Words

Synonym: ちらほら (here and there)
