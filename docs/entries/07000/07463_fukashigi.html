不可思議(ふかしぎ)

ふかしぎ
noun, na-adjective
mysterious; inexplicable; beyond comprehension; wonder
1. mysterious; inexplicable; beyond comprehension
Something that cannot be understood or explained through normal reasoning. Often describes phenomena or events that defy logical explanation.
不可思議(ふかしぎ)な体験(たいけん)をした。
I had a mysterious experience.
宇宙(うちゅう)には不可思議(ふかしぎ)なことが多(おお)い。
There are many inexplicable things in the universe.
2. wonder; marvel; mysterious phenomenon
A mysterious occurrence or thing worthy of wonder.
世界(せかい)の七(なな)不可思議(ふかしぎ)を巡(めぐ)る旅(たび)に出(で)た。
I set off on a journey to visit the Seven Wonders of the World.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:
不(ふ) (not) + 可(か) (possible) + 思議(しぎ) (to fathom/comprehend)

Literally "not possible to comprehend" - beyond the reach of thought or reason.

BUDDHIST ORIGIN:
Originally a Buddhist term referring to the ineffable nature of enlightenment and divine mysteries that transcend human understanding.

GRAMMAR:

  • 不可思議(ふかしぎ)な + noun - mysterious ~
  • 不可思議(ふかしぎ)に思(おも)う - to find mysterious
  • 〜は不可思議(ふかしぎ)だ - ~ is inexplicable

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 世界(せかい)の七(なな)不可思議(ふかしぎ) - Seven Wonders of the World
  • 不可思議(ふかしぎ)な現象(げんしょう) - mysterious phenomenon
  • 不可思議(ふかしぎ)な出来事(できごと) - inexplicable event

NUANCE:
Stronger than 不思議(ふしぎ) (strange, mysterious). 不可思議(ふかしぎ) implies something truly beyond human comprehension, while 不思議(ふしぎ) can be used for everyday curiosities.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 不思議(ふしぎ) - strange, mysterious (more common, lighter)
  • 神秘的(しんぴてき) - mystical
  • 謎(なぞ) - mystery, riddle
  • 怪奇(かいき) - bizarre, weird

REGISTER:
Slightly formal/literary. Often used in dramatic or philosophical contexts.
