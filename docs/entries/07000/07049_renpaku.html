連泊(れんぱく)

れんぱく
noun
consecutive nights stay
1. consecutive nights stay; staying multiple nights in a row
Staying at the same accommodation for two or more consecutive nights.
連泊(れんぱく)すると割引(わりびき)がある。
There's a discount if you stay multiple nights.
3泊(はく)連泊(れんぱく)で予約(よやく)した。
I made a reservation for three consecutive nights.
連泊(れんぱく)の場合(ばあい)、部屋(へや)の清掃(せいそう)はどうなりますか。
In the case of consecutive nights, what about room cleaning?

FORMATION:
連(れん) (consecutive, in a row) + 泊(ぱく) (an overnight stay; the counter for nights)

VERB FORM:
連泊(れんぱく)する - to stay several nights in a row

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連泊(れんぱく)割引(わりびき) - multi-night discount
  • 連泊(れんぱく)プラン - multi-night plan
  • 〜泊(はく)連泊(れんぱく) - staying ~ consecutive nights

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一泊(いっぱく) - a one-night stay
  • 二泊(にはく) - a two-night stay
  • 延泊(えんぱく) - extending a stay by an extra night

USAGE NOTE:
Many hotels and inns offer a 連泊(れんぱく)割引(わりびき), so booking several nights at once is often cheaper per night than booking them separately.

Related Words

Related: 一泊(いっぱく) (one-night stay)
