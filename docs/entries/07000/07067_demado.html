出窓(でまど)

でまど
noun
bay window
1. bay window
A window that projects outward from the wall, creating additional interior space and allowing more light.
出窓(でまど)に植木鉢(うえきばち)を置(お)いている。
I have potted plants on the bay window.
出窓(でまど)のある部屋(へや)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want a room with a bay window.
猫(ねこ)が出窓(でまど)で日向(ひなた)ぼっこしている。
The cat is sunbathing on the bay window.

USAGE:

出窓(でまど) projects from the wall, creating a small shelf-like space often used for:

  • 植物(しょくぶつ)を飾(かざ)る (displaying plants)
  • 小物(こもの)を置(お)く (placing small objects)
  • ペットの休憩(きゅうけい)場所(ばしょ) (pet resting spot)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出窓(でまど)のある家(いえ) (house with bay window)
  • 出窓(でまど)に座(すわ)る (to sit in the bay window)
  • 出窓(でまど)スペース (bay window space)
