呼(よ)び戻(もど)す

よびもどす
verb (godan, transitive)
to call back; to recall; to summon back
1. to call back; to summon back
To ask or order someone who has left to return.
出(で)て行(い)った息子(むすこ)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)した。
I called back my son who had left.
会社(かいしゃ)は海外(かいがい)にいた社員(しゃいん)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)した。
The company recalled employees who were overseas.
2. to recall; to bring back (memories, feelings)
To cause something from the past to come back to mind or existence.
その曲(きょく)は青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)の思(おも)い出(で)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)す。
That song brings back memories of my youth.

CONJUGATION:

  • 呼(よ)び戻(もど)す → 呼(よ)び戻(もど)さない (negative)
  • 呼(よ)び戻(もど)す → 呼(よ)び戻(もど)して (te-form)
  • 呼(よ)び戻(もど)す → 呼(よ)び戻(もど)した (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 呼(よ)ぶ (to call) + 戻(もど)す (to return/put back)

The combination means to call and make return.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)す - to call someone back
  • 記憶(きおく)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)す - to recall memories
  • 意識(いしき)を呼(よ)び戻(もど)す - to bring back to consciousness
  • 本国(ほんごく)に呼(よ)び戻(もど)す - to recall to one's home country

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 呼(よ)び返(かえ)す - to call back (similar meaning)
