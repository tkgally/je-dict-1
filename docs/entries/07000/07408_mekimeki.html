めきめき

めきめき
adverb, mimetic
remarkably; rapidly (improving)

Definitions

1. remarkably; rapidly; conspicuously
Describes rapid and noticeable improvement or progress. Often used for skills, abilities, or growth that advance quickly and visibly.

Examples

彼(かれ)の日本語(にほんご)はめきめき上達(じょうたつ)している。
His Japanese is improving remarkably.
子供(こども)がめきめき成長(せいちょう)している。
The child is growing rapidly.
練習(れんしゅう)のおかげでめきめき腕(うで)を上(あ)げた。
Thanks to practice, his skills improved rapidly.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA TYPE:
Gitaigo - mimetic word expressing manner or state.

USAGE:

めきめき is almost exclusively used in positive contexts to describe impressive, visible progress or improvement. It emphasizes that the change is:

  • Rapid
  • Noticeable to others
  • Impressive

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • めきめき上達(じょうたつ)する - to improve remarkably
  • めきめき成長(せいちょう)する - to grow rapidly
  • めきめき腕(うで)を上(あ)げる - to improve one's skills rapidly
  • めきめき頭角(とうかく)を現(あらわ)す - to rapidly distinguish oneself

NUANCE:
めきめき conveys admiration for how quickly someone or something is improving.

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in everyday conversation and writing.
