一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)

いっきいちゆう
noun
swinging between joy and worry
1. alternating between joy and worry; emotional ups and downs
The state of being emotionally affected by every small development, swinging between happiness and anxiety.
株価(かぶか)の変動(へんどう)に一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)している。
I'm swinging between joy and worry over the stock price fluctuations.
一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)せずに冷静(れいせい)に対応(たいおう)しよう。
Let's respond calmly without getting emotionally swayed.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)に一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)する必要(ひつよう)はない。
There's no need to get emotional about the exam results.

一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう) names the state of being lifted and cast down by every small turn of events. Literally "one joy, one worry".

USAGE:

  • Almost always used as a verb with する, and what one is reacting to takes に: 結果(けっか)に一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)する
  • The negative forms are the commonest of all, because the word usually appears in advice not to overreact: 一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)せずに, 一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)しない
  • Typical contexts are share prices, exam results, medical test numbers, and sports scores — anything that produces a stream of small readings

NUANCE:
The word carries mild disapproval of the swinging itself. Calling someone 一喜一憂(いっきいちゆう)している suggests they are letting short-term noise govern their mood, and the recommended alternative is usually 冷静(れいせい)に (calmly).
