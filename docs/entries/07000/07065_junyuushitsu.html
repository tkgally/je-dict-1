授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ)

じゅにゅうしつ
noun
nursing room; breastfeeding room
1. nursing room; breastfeeding room
A private room provided in public facilities for mothers to breastfeed or bottle-feed babies.
この施設(しせつ)には授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ)がありますか。
Does this facility have a nursing room?
授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ)は2階(かい)にあります。
The nursing room is on the second floor.
授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ)に給湯器(きゅうとうき)が設置(せっち)されている。
A hot water dispenser is installed in the nursing room.

授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ) are commonly found in shopping malls, department stores, train stations, and public facilities in Japan. Many are equipped with:

  • 給湯器(きゅうとうき) (hot water dispenser)
  • おむつ替(か)え台(だい) (diaper changing table)
  • 個室(こしつ) (private booths)

ALTERNATE TERMS:

  • ベビールーム (baby room)
  • おむつ替(か)え室(しつ) (diaper changing room)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 授乳(じゅにゅう) (breastfeeding/nursing)
  • 粉(こな)ミルク (formula milk)
