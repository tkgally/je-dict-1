調律(ちょうりつ)

ちょうりつ
noun
tuning (instruments)

Definitions

1. tuning; adjustment of pitch on musical instruments
The process of adjusting a musical instrument so that it produces the correct pitches, especially for keyboard and string instruments.

Examples

ピアノの調律(ちょうりつ)を頼(たの)んだ。
I requested a piano tuning.
年(ねん)に一度(いちど)は調律(ちょうりつ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Tuning is needed once a year.
調律(ちょうりつ)が狂(くる)っている。
The tuning is off.
調律師(ちょうりつし)を呼(よ)んでピアノを調律(ちょうりつ)してもらった。
I called a tuner to have the piano tuned.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 調律(ちょうりつ)する: to tune (an instrument)
  • 調律(ちょうりつ)を頼(たの)む: request tuning
  • 調律(ちょうりつ)が狂(くる)う: the tuning goes off
  • 調律(ちょうりつ)を合(あ)わせる: adjust the tuning

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 調律師(ちょうりつし): piano tuner (professional)
  • チューナー: tuner (electronic device)
  • チューニング: tuning (loanword, often for guitars)

NOTE:
調律(ちょうりつ) is most commonly used for pianos and similar keyboard instruments. For guitars and other string instruments, チューニング is often used instead.

VERB FORM:
調律(ちょうりつ)する functions as a する verb meaning "to tune."
