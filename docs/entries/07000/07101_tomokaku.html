ともかく

ともかく
adverb
anyhow; anyway; in any case

Definitions

1. anyhow; anyway; in any case; at any rate
Sets aside other considerations to focus on the main point.
2. setting aside; leaving aside
Used in the pattern ～はともかく to mean 'leaving ~ aside'.

Examples

ともかく、まず食(た)べよう。
Anyway, let's eat first.
ともかく連絡(れんらく)してみたら?
Why don't you try contacting them anyway?
結果(けっか)はともかく、全力(ぜんりょく)を尽(つ)くした。
Leaving the result aside, I gave it my all.

Notes

ともかく means "anyhow" or "in any case."

TWO MAIN USES:
1. As a sentence opener - Sets aside previous discussion
2. In ～はともかく pattern - Means "setting ~ aside"

SIMILAR:

  • とにかく - anyway, anyhow (more emphatic)
  • いずれにせよ - in any case (formal)

NUANCE:
ともかく suggests temporarily setting aside complications to focus on what should be done.

Related Words

Synonym: とにかく (more emphatic)
