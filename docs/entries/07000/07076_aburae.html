油絵(あぶらえ)

あぶらえ
noun
oil painting
1. oil painting
A painting created using oil-based paints, or the art form itself.
美術館(びじゅつかん)で油絵(あぶらえ)を鑑賞(かんしょう)した。
I viewed oil paintings at the art museum.
趣味(しゅみ)で油絵(あぶらえ)を描(か)いている。
I paint oil paintings as a hobby.
この油絵(あぶらえ)は有名(ゆうめい)な画家(がか)の作品(さくひん)だ。
This oil painting is a work by a famous painter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 油絵(あぶらえ)を描(か)く (to paint an oil painting)
  • 油絵(あぶらえ)の具(ぐ) (oil paints)
  • 油絵(あぶらえ)教室(きょうしつ) (oil painting class)
  • 油絵(あぶらえ)展(てん) (oil painting exhibition)

RELATED ART TERMS:

  • 水彩画(すいさいが) (watercolor painting)
  • 日本画(にほんが) (Japanese-style painting)
  • 版画(はんが) (printmaking)
  • 油彩(ゆさい) (oil painting - technical term)

MATERIALS:

  • イーゼル (easel)
  • 筆(ふで) (brush)
