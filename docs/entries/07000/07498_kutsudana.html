靴棚(くつだな)

くつだな
noun
shoe rack; shoe shelf

Definitions

1. shoe rack; shoe shelf; shoe storage
Furniture or a shelving unit for storing shoes, typically placed in the entryway of a home or building.

Examples

玄関(げんかん)に靴棚(くつだな)を置(お)いた。
I placed a shoe rack in the entryway.
靴棚(くつだな)がいっぱいになった。
The shoe rack is full.
新(あたら)しい靴棚(くつだな)を組(く)み立(た)てる。
I'm assembling a new shoe rack.
靴(くつ)を靴棚(くつだな)に戻(もど)してください。
Please put your shoes back on the shoe rack.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
靴(くつ) (shoes) + 棚(たな) (shelf)

ALTERNATIVE TERMS:

  • 靴(くつ)ラック: shoe rack (loanword version)
  • シューズラック: shoes rack
  • 下駄箱(げたばこ): traditional shoe cabinet (originally for geta)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 靴棚(くつだな)に並(なら)べる: line up on the shoe rack
  • 靴棚(くつだな)を整理(せいり)する: organize the shoe rack
  • 玄関(げんかん)の靴棚(くつだな): entryway shoe rack

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 玄関(げんかん): entryway
  • 下駄箱(げたばこ): shoe cabinet
  • 靴(くつ): shoes
  • 靴(くつ)ベラ: shoehorn

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, shoes are removed at the entrance of homes and many buildings. A 靴棚(くつだな) or 下駄箱(げたばこ) is a standard feature in Japanese entryways.

COUNTER: 台(だい)
