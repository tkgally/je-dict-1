思(おも)いがけず

おもいがけず
adverb
unexpectedly; by chance
1. unexpectedly; by chance; unintentionally
Something happens without anticipation or intention. Often carries a sense of pleasant or neutral surprise.
思(おも)いがけず旧友(きゅうゆう)に会(あ)った。
I unexpectedly ran into an old friend.
思(おも)いがけずプレゼントをもらって、とても嬉(うれ)しかった。
I was very happy to receive a gift unexpectedly.
思(おも)いがけず時間(じかん)がかかってしまった。
It unexpectedly took a long time.

FORMATION:
思(おも)い (thought) + がけ (expectation) + ず (negation)

Literally 'without expecting.'

RELATED FORMS:

  • 思(おも)いがけない - unexpected (adjective)
  • 思(おmo)いがけなく - unexpectedly (adverb form)

REGISTER:
Neutral to slightly formal. Literary flavor.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 意外(いがい)と/案外(あんがい) - unexpectedly (focuses on result being different than expected)
  • たまたま - by chance, coincidentally
  • ふと - suddenly, casually (for thoughts or actions)
