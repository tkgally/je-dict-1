日頃(ひごろ)

ひごろ
noun, adverb
usually; normally; on a daily basis
1. usually; normally; in everyday life
Refers to one's typical daily routine or habitual state.
日頃(ひごろ)からの努力(どりょく)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Consistent daily effort is important.
日頃(ひごろ)お世話(せわ)になっております。
Thank you for your continued support.
日頃(ひごろ)の運動(うんどう)不足(ぶそく)がたたった。
My usual lack of exercise caught up with me.

WORD FORMATION:
日(ひ) (day) + 頃(ころ) (around, about) → around the days = daily, usually

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 日頃(ひごろ)から - from day to day, habitually
  • 日頃(ひごろ)の - one's usual, everyday
  • 日頃(ひごろ)お世話(せわ)になっております - (polite) Thank you for your continued support

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 普段(ふだん) - usually, normally
  • いつも - always
  • 毎日(まいにち) - every day

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Often used in business correspondence and speeches.

Related Words

Synonym: 普段(ふだん) (usually)
