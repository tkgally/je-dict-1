雨樋(あまどい)

あまどい
noun
rain gutter; drainpipe
1. rain gutter; drainpipe
A channel attached to the edge of a roof to collect and drain rainwater away from the building.
雨樋(あまどい)に落(お)ち葉(ば)が詰(つ)まっている。
The rain gutter is clogged with fallen leaves.
台風(たいふう)で雨樋(あまどい)が壊(こわ)れた。
The rain gutter was damaged by the typhoon.
雨樋(あまどい)を掃除(そうじ)しないと水漏(みずも)れの原因(げんいん)になる。
If you don't clean the gutters, it can cause water leaks.

COMPOSITION: 雨(あま) (rain) + 樋(とい) (gutter, pipe). The 樋(とい) becomes {どい} through rendaku (sequential voicing).

ALTERNATE WRITING: Sometimes written as 雨(あま)どい or あまどい.

COMPONENTS OF GUTTER SYSTEM:

  • 軒樋(のきどい): horizontal gutter along the eave
  • 竪樋(たてどい)/縦樋(たてどい): vertical downspout
  • 集水器(しゅうすいき): funnel/collector
  • 這(は)い樋(どい): gutter running along the ground

MAINTENANCE:

  • 詰(つ)まる: to get clogged
  • 掃除(そうじ)する: to clean
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)を取(と)り除(のぞ)く: to remove fallen leaves

MATERIALS:

  • 塩(えん)ビ: PVC (most common)
  • 銅(どう): copper (traditional)
  • アルミ: aluminum

RELATED:

  • 軒(のき): eaves
  • 屋根(やね): roof
  • 排水(はいすい): drainage
