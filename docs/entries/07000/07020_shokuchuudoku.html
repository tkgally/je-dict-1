食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)

しょくちゅうどく
noun
food poisoning
1. food poisoning
Illness caused by eating contaminated food, typically causing symptoms like nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea.
生(なま)ガキを食(た)べて食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)になった。
I got food poisoning from eating raw oysters.
夏(なつ)は食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)が増(ふ)える季節(きせつ)だ。
Summer is the season when food poisoning increases.
食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)を防(ふせ)ぐには手洗(てあら)いが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Hand washing is important to prevent food poisoning.
肉(にく)をよく焼(や)かないと食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)の危険(きけん)がある。
If you don't cook meat thoroughly, there's a risk of food poisoning.
このレストランで食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)が発生(はっせい)して、何人(なんにん)もの客(きゃく)が病院(びょういん)に運(はこ)ばれた。
Food poisoning broke out at this restaurant, and several customers were taken to the hospital.

食中毒(しょくちゅうどく) is food poisoning — illness caused by eating contaminated food.

Formation: 食(しょく) 'food' + 中毒(ちゅうどく) 'poisoning.' Note that 中毒(ちゅうどく) on its own also means 'addiction,' but in this compound only the poisoning sense is present.

Collocations:

  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)になる: to get food poisoning (of the sufferer)
  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)を起(お)こす: to cause an outbreak (of a food or an establishment)
  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)が発生(はっせい)する: an outbreak occurs
  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent food poisoning
  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)警報(けいほう): a food-poisoning warning, issued by local authorities in hot, humid weather

Cultural note: 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく) is a live public-health topic in Japan, partly because raw and lightly cooked foods are common. Warnings peak in 夏(なつ) for bacterial causes and in winter for norovirus. Common culprits named in the news: サルモネラ菌(きん) (salmonella), ノロウイルス (norovirus), O157 (a strain of E. coli), and カンピロバクター (campylobacter, associated with undercooked chicken).
