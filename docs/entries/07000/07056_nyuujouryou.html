入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)

にゅうじょうりょう
noun
admission fee; entrance fee
1. admission fee; entrance fee
The fee charged to enter a venue such as a museum, amusement park, concert, or sporting event.
入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)は大人(おとな)1000円(えん)です。
The admission fee is 1000 yen for adults.
子供(こども)は入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)無料(むりょう)だ。
Children get free admission.
入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)を払(はら)って中(なか)に入(はい)った。
I paid the admission fee and went inside.

入場料(にゅうじょうりょう) is what you pay to get into a venue — an amusement park, a concert, a sports ground, an exhibition.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)を払(はら)う: to pay admission
  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)無料(むりょう): admission free
  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)込(こ)み / 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)別(べつ): admission included / not included

CHOOSING THE RIGHT FEE WORD:

Japanese uses a different compound depending on what is being entered, and signs use them precisely:

  • 入館料(にゅうかんりょう) for buildings whose name ends in 館(かん) — museums, libraries, halls
  • 拝観料(はいかんりょう) for temples and shrines, where you are paying to view something venerated
  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう) for open grounds and events

DISCOUNTS:
Ticket windows normally list 団体(だんたい)割引(わりびき) (group), 学生(がくせい)割引(わりびき) (student), and シニア割引(わりびき) (senior) rates.
