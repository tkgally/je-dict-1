突(つ)き落(お)とす

つきおとす
verb, godan
to push down; to thrust off; to knock down
1. to push down; to thrust off; to knock off (a ledge, etc.)
To forcefully push someone or something downward, causing them to fall from a height or elevated position.
階段(かいだん)から突(つ)き落(お)とされた。
I was pushed down the stairs.
崖(がけ)から突(つ)き落(お)とされそうになった。
I was almost pushed off a cliff.
犯人(はんにん)は被害者(ひがいしゃ)をホームから突(つ)き落(お)とした。
The culprit pushed the victim off the platform.
2. to plunge (into despair); to push (into misfortune)
Figuratively, to cause someone to fall into a negative state such as despair, misfortune, or a difficult situation.
絶望(ぜつぼう)に突(つ)き落(お)とされた。
I was plunged into despair.
その知(し)らせは彼女(かのじょ)を絶望(ぜつぼう)の淵(ふち)に突(つ)き落(お)とした。
That news plunged her into the depths of despair.
信(しん)じていた人(ひと)に裏切(うらぎ)られ、不幸(ふこう)の底(そこ)に突(つ)き落(お)とされた気分(きぶん)だった。
Betrayed by someone I trusted, I felt as if I'd been cast into the depths of misery.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: XがYを突(つ)き落(お)とす

COMPOUND VERB STRUCTURE:
突(つ)く (to thrust/push) + 落(お)とす (to drop/let fall) = to push down forcefully

ASPECT (ている):

突(つ)き落(お)としている can mean either:

  • In the process of pushing down (ongoing)
  • Has pushed down (resultant state, less common)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 階段(かいだん)から突(つ)き落(お)とす - to push down the stairs
  • 崖(がけ)から突(つ)き落(お)とす - to push off a cliff
  • 絶望(ぜつぼう)に突(つ)き落(お)とす - to plunge into despair
  • 不幸(ふこう)に突(つ)き落(お)とす - to thrust into misfortune
  • 地獄(じごく)に突(つ)き落(お)とす - to cast into hell

USAGE NOTE:
Often appears in crime reports, dramatic narratives, and figurative expressions about emotional devastation. The physical sense implies violent intent.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 突(つ)き飛(と)ばす - to push away (horizontally)
  • 押(お)し倒(たお)す - to push over
  • 落(お)とす - to drop (without the forceful pushing)
