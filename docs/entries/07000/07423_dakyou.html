妥協(だきょう)

だきょう
noun, suru-verb
compromise; settlement

Definitions

1. compromise; settlement; concession
Reaching an agreement by each side giving up some demands. Finding a middle ground between opposing positions.

Examples

両者(りょうしゃ)は妥協(だきょう)に達(たっ)した。
Both parties reached a compromise.
品質(ひんしつ)には妥協(だきょう)しない。
I don't compromise on quality.
妥協点(だきょうてん)を見(み)つけることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to find a point of compromise.

Notes

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 妥(だ) - peaceful settlement
  • 協(きょう) - cooperation

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 妥協(だきょう)する - to compromise
  • 妥協(だきょう)を許(ゆる)さない - to allow no compromise
  • 妥協点(だきょうてん) - point of compromise
  • 妥協案(だきょうあん) - compromise proposal
  • 妥協(だきょう)に達(たっ)する - to reach a compromise
  • 妥協(だきょう)の余地(よち) - room for compromise

NEGATIVE USE:

妥協(だきょう)しない is often used as a positive trait, meaning refusing to lower one's standards:

  • 品質(ひんしつ)に妥協(だきょう)しない - uncompromising on quality
  • 味(あじ)に妥協(だきょう)しない - no compromise on taste

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 譲歩(じょうほ) - concession, giving in
  • 折(お)り合(あ)い - settlement, understanding
  • 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)り - meeting halfway

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in business, politics, and everyday discussion.
