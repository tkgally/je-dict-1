励(はげ)み

はげみ
noun
encouragement; incentive; motivation

Definitions

1. encouragement; incentive; motivation
Something that gives one motivation or encouragement to continue efforts.

Examples

みんなの応援(おうえん)が励(はげ)みになる。
Everyone's support is encouraging.
子供(こども)の笑顔(えがお)が仕事(しごと)の励(はげ)みだ。
My children's smiles are my motivation at work.
励(はげ)みになる言葉(ことば)をありがとう。
Thank you for the encouraging words.

Notes

RELATED VERB:
励(はげ)む - to work hard, to strive
励(はげ)ます - to encourage (someone)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 励(はげ)みになる - to be encouraging/motivating
  • 励(はげ)みを与(あた)える - to give encouragement
  • 大(おお)きな励(はげ)み - great encouragement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 張(は)り合(あ)い - stimulation, motivation (from competition)
  • やりがい - sense of fulfillment
  • 動機(どうき) - motive

NUANCE:
励(はげ)み emphasizes the encouragement aspect, giving someone strength to continue.

Related Words

Related: 励(はげ)ます (to encourage)
Related: 張(は)り合(あ)い (motivation)
