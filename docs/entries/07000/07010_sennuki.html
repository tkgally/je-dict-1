栓(せん)抜(ぬ)き

せんぬき
noun
bottle opener
1. bottle opener
A tool used to remove bottle caps, especially from glass bottles with pry-off caps.
栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きでビール瓶(びん)を開(あ)けた。
I opened the beer bottle with a bottle opener.
栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きがなくて、歯(は)で開(あ)けようとした。
I didn't have a bottle opener, so I tried to open it with my teeth.
Not recommended!
この栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きは缶切(かんき)りも付(つ)いている。
This bottle opener also has a can opener attached.

A 栓(せん)抜(ぬ)き is specifically for prying off a crimped metal bottle cap (王冠(おうかん)). A corkscrew is a different tool, コルク抜(ぬ)き. The name is literally 'stopper-puller': 栓(せん) (stopper, cap) + 抜(ぬ)き, from 抜(ぬ)く (to pull out).

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きで開(あ)ける (to open with a bottle opener)
  • 栓(せん)抜(ぬ)き付(つ)き (with a built-in bottle opener, on a multi-tool or keyring)

WORD FORMATION:

Tool names are regularly built from a verb stem. 〜抜(ぬ)き marks something that pulls out, and 〜切(き)り something that cuts:

  • コルク抜(ぬ)き (corkscrew)
  • 毛抜(けぬ)き (tweezers)
  • 缶切(かんき)り (can opener)
  • 爪切(つめき)り (nail clippers)
