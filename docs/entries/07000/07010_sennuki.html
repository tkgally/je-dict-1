栓(せん)抜(ぬ)き

せんぬき
noun
bottle opener
1. bottle opener
A tool used to remove bottle caps, especially from glass bottles with pry-off caps.
栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きでビール瓶(びん)を開(あ)けた。
I opened the beer bottle with a bottle opener.
栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きがなくて、歯(は)で開(あ)けようとした。
I didn't have a bottle opener, so I tried to open it with my teeth.
Not recommended!
この栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きは缶切(かんき)りも付(つ)いている。
This bottle opener also has a can opener attached.

FORMATION:
栓(せん) (stopper/cork) + 抜(ぬ)き (from 抜(ぬ)く, to pull out)

Literally 'stopper-puller.'

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 栓(せん)抜(ぬ)きで開(あ)ける - to open with a bottle opener
  • 王冠(おうかん)を抜(ぬ)く - to remove a bottle cap

RELATED TOOLS:

  • 缶切(かんき)り - can opener
  • コルク抜(ぬ)き - corkscrew
  • 爪切(つめき)り - nail clippers
