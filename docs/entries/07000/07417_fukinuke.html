吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)け

ふきぬけ
noun
atrium; open ceiling

Definitions

1. atrium; open ceiling; vaulted space
An architectural feature where floors are open to create a multi-story vertical space, allowing light and air to flow through.

Examples

このビルのロビーは吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)けになっている。
The lobby of this building has an atrium design.
吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)けのリビングは開放感(かいほうかん)がある。
A living room with a vaulted ceiling feels spacious.
2階(かい)まで吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)けの家(いえ)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in a house with a two-story open ceiling.

Notes

ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT: 吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)け designs are popular in modern Japanese homes and commercial buildings for creating a sense of openness and allowing natural light to penetrate deeper into the building.

RELATED ARCHITECTURAL TERMS:

  • 天井(てんじょう) (ceiling)
  • 開放感(かいほうかん) (sense of openness)
  • 採光(さいこう) (natural lighting)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)けの玄関(げんかん) (entrance hall with atrium)
  • 吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)け構造(こうぞう) (atrium structure)
  • 吹(ふ)き抜(ぬ)け空間(くうかん) (atrium space)

NOTE: The literal meaning is 'blown through' - referring to wind being able to pass through the open space.
