抽選(ちゅうせん)

ちゅうせん
noun
lottery; drawing; raffle
1. lottery; drawing; raffle
A method of random selection, typically used for deciding winners in competitions, allocating tickets, or distributing prizes.
抽選(ちゅうせん)で当選者(とうせんしゃ)を決(き)める。
We'll decide the winner by lottery.
コンサートのチケットは抽選(ちゅうせん)で販売(はんばい)される。
Concert tickets will be sold through a lottery.
抽選(ちゅうせん)に申(もう)し込(こ)んだが、外(はず)れた。
I entered the lottery but didn't win.
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)ではなく抽選(ちゅうせん)なので、焦(あせ)る必要(ひつよう)はない。
It's by lottery, not first-come-first-served, so there's no need to rush.
人気(にんき)のイベントは抽選(ちゅうせん)倍率(ばいりつ)が高(たか)くて、なかなか当(あ)たらない。
Popular events have high lottery odds, so it's hard to win.

抽選(ちゅうせん) is selection by drawing lots. In Japan it is the standard way of rationing anything oversubscribed: concert tickets, popular restaurant slots, public housing, marathon entries, and school places.

USAGE:

  • The method takes で: 抽選(ちゅうせん)で決(き)める (decide by lottery)
  • The draw you enter or fail takes に: 抽選(ちゅうせん)に申(もう)し込(こ)む, 抽選(ちゅうせん)に外(はず)れる
  • Also used as a verb with する when an organiser holds the draw

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 抽選(ちゅうせん)で当(あ)たる: to win a draw
  • 抽選(ちゅうせん)販売(はんばい): sale by lottery
  • 抽選(ちゅうせん)結果(けっか): the result of the draw
  • 抽選(ちゅうせん)倍率(ばいりつ): the odds, expressed as applicants per place

CONTRAST:
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) (first-come, first-served) is the other common rationing method, and notices usually state which one applies. 抽選(ちゅうせん) rewards nobody for being quick; 先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) rewards nothing else.

Related Words

Contrast: 先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) (first-come-first-served)
Contrast: 予約制(よやくせい) (by reservation (third arrangement))
