寝言(ねごと)

ねごと
noun
sleep-talking; talking in one's sleep
1. sleep-talking; talking in one's sleep
Words spoken unconsciously while sleeping.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、よく寝言(ねごと)を言(い)っていた。
When I was a child, I often talked in my sleep.
彼(かれ)は寝言(ねごと)で何(なに)かつぶやいていた。
He was muttering something in his sleep.
2. nonsense; absurd talk
Ridiculous statements that make no sense, as if spoken while dreaming. Often used dismissively.
寝言(ねごと)を言(い)うな。そんなことできるわけがない。
Don't talk nonsense. There's no way that's possible.

WORD FORMATION:
寝(ね) (sleep) + 言(ごと/こと) (words)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寝言(ねごと)を言(い)う - to talk in one's sleep; to talk nonsense
  • 寝言(ねごと)を言(い)うな - don't talk nonsense (dismissive)
  • 寝言(ねごと)は寝(ね)て言(い)え - say your nonsense while sleeping (idiom meaning "stop talking nonsense")

RELATED 〜言(ごと) WORDS:

  • たわ言(ごと) - nonsense, drivel
  • 繰(く)り言(ごと) - repetitive complaints
  • 独(ひと)り言(ごと) - talking to oneself

REGISTER:
Casual to neutral. The dismissive usage (sense 2) is informal.
