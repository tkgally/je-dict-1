不用意(ふようい)

ふようい
na-adjective
careless; unguarded; thoughtless
1. careless; thoughtless; unguarded
Lacking proper preparation, caution, or thought. Often refers to words or actions done without considering the consequences.
不用意(ふようい)な発言(はつげん)で相手(あいて)を傷(きず)つけてしまった。
I hurt the other person with a careless remark.
不用意(ふようい)に個人情報(こじんじょうほう)を漏(も)らさないでください。
Please don't carelessly reveal personal information.
不用意(ふようい)にドアを開(あ)けたら、猫(ねこ)が逃(に)げた。
When I carelessly opened the door, the cat escaped.

FORMS:

  • 不用意(ふようい)な + noun (careless ~)
  • 不用意(ふようい)に + verb (carelessly ~)

WORD FORMATION:
不(ふ) (un-, non-) + 用意(ようい) (preparation)
Literally: "without preparation"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不用意(ふようい)な発言(はつげん) - careless remark
  • 不用意(ふようい)な行動(こうどう) - thoughtless action
  • 不用意(ふようい)に口(くち)を滑(すべ)らせる - to let something slip carelessly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 軽率(けいそつ) - rash, thoughtless (more about being imprudent)
  • 無防備(むぼうび) - defenseless, unguarded (more about vulnerability)
  • うかつ - careless (more colloquial)

REGISTER:
Neutral to formal. Common in news, official statements, and formal writing.
