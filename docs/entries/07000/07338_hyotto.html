ひょっと

ひょっと
adverb
possibly; by chance; perhaps
1. possibly; by chance; perhaps
Indicates possibility or chance occurrence, often used with conditional forms.
ひょっとしたら雨(あめ)が降(ふ)るかもしれない。
It might possibly rain.
ひょっとして、田中(たなか)さんですか。
By any chance, are you Mr./Ms. Tanaka?
ひょっとすると間(ま)に合(あ)わないかも。
There's a possibility we might not make it in time.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ひょっとしたら〜かもしれない (there's a possibility that ~)
  • ひょっとして〜？ (by any chance ~?)
  • ひょっとすると〜 (possibly ~)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • もしかしたら (perhaps, maybe - similar meaning)
  • ひょっとしたら vs もしかしたら: ひょっとしたら implies slightly lower probability

REGISTER:
Casual to neutral; common in everyday conversation.

Related Words

Synonym: もしかしたら (perhaps)
