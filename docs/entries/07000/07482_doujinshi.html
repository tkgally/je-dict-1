同人誌(どうじんし)

どうじんし
noun
doujinshi; fanzine; self-published work

Definitions

1. self-published magazine or book; fanzine; indie publication
A self-published work, typically a manga, novel, or magazine, created by amateurs or independent artists, often featuring original stories or fan-made content based on existing works.

Examples

コミケで同人誌(どうじんし)を買(か)った。
I bought doujinshi at Comiket.
自分(じぶん)で描(か)いた同人誌(どうじんし)を販売(はんばい)する。
I sell doujinshi that I drew myself.
同人誌(どうじんし)を作(つく)るのが趣味(しゅみ)だ。
Creating doujinshi is my hobby.
好(す)きなキャラクターの同人誌(どうじんし)を集(あつ)めている。
I collect doujinshi featuring my favorite characters.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
同人(どうじん) (coterie, group with shared interests) + 誌(し) (magazine/publication)

ORIGINAL MEANING:
Historically referred to literary magazines published by coterie groups. Now primarily associated with manga and fan-created content.

TYPES:

  • 二次創作(にじそうさく): derivative works (fan fiction/art based on existing works)
  • 一次創作(いちじそうさく): original works

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • コミケ / コミックマーケット: Comic Market (world's largest doujinshi event)
  • 即売会(そくばいかい): direct sales event
  • 同人活動(どうじんかつどう): doujin activities
  • サークル: circle (group of creators)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Doujinshi culture is a major part of Japanese fan communities. Many professional manga artists started in doujinshi circles. While some doujinshi involve fan works of copyrighted characters, this is generally tolerated in Japan as a form of fan expression, though it exists in a legal gray area.
