方言(ほうげん)

ほうげん
noun
dialect; regional speech

Definitions

1. regional dialect; local speech variety
A variety of a language spoken in a particular geographic region, with distinctive vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar.

Examples

関西(かんさい)方言(ほうげん)を話(はな)す。
I speak the Kansai dialect.
この地方(ちほう)には独特(どくとく)の方言(ほうげん)がある。
This region has a distinctive dialect.
方言(ほうげん)が強(つよ)くて聞(き)き取(と)りにくい。
The dialect is so strong that it's hard to understand.
祖母(そぼ)は方言(ほうげん)まじりの日本語(にほんご)を話(はな)す。
My grandmother speaks Japanese mixed with dialect.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 方言(ほうげん)を話(はな)す: speak a dialect
  • 方言(ほうげん)が出(で)る: one's dialect comes out
  • 方言(ほうげん)が強(つよ)い: have a strong dialect
  • お国(くに)言葉(ことば): one's regional speech (politer term)

MAJOR JAPANESE DIALECTS:

  • 関西弁(かんさいべん): Kansai dialect (Osaka, Kyoto area)
  • 東北弁(とうほくべん): Tohoku dialect
  • 九州弁(きゅうしゅうべん): Kyushu dialect
  • 沖縄(おきなわ)方言(ほうげん): Okinawan dialect
  • 北海道(ほっかいどう)弁(べん): Hokkaido dialect

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 方言(ほうげん) vs. 訛(なま)り
  • 方言(ほうげん): regional language variety (neutral/academic term)
  • 訛(なま)り: accent; dialectal influence in speech (may carry slight informal nuance)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, speaking in dialect can signal regional identity, intimacy, or informality. Many people use 標準語(ひょうじゅんご) (standard Japanese) in formal settings while switching to 方言(ほうげん) at home or with friends from the same region.

Related Words

Related: 訛(なま)り (accent)
Contrast: 標準語(ひょうじゅんご) (standard language)
