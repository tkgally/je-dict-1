敏腕(びんわん)

びんわん
na-adjective
capable; competent; skilled
1. capable; competent; skilled
Having excellent practical abilities, especially in business or professional contexts.
彼女(かのじょ)は敏腕(びんわん)な経営者(けいえいしゃ)だ。
She is a capable manager.
敏腕(びんわん)プロデューサーとして知(し)られている。
He is known as a skilled producer.
敏腕(びんわん)を振(ふ)るう。
To display one's abilities.

WORD FORMATION:
敏(びん) (quick, alert) + 腕(わん) (arm, skill) → quick-armed = capable

FORMS:

  • 敏腕(びんわん)な弁護士(べんごし) - capable lawyer
  • 敏腕(びんわん)プロデューサー - skilled producer
  • 敏腕(びんわん)を振(ふ)るう - to demonstrate one's abilities

USAGE:
敏腕(びんわん) is often used in business and professional contexts to describe someone who is highly effective at their job. It's more formal than 凄腕(すごうで) and implies professional competence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敏腕(びんわん)弁護士(べんごし) - capable lawyer
  • 敏腕(びんわん)経営者(けいえいしゃ) - capable manager
  • 敏腕(びんわん)プロデューサー - skilled producer
  • 敏腕(びんわん)エージェント - capable agent
