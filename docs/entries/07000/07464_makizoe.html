巻(ま)き添(ぞ)え

まきぞえ
noun
getting caught up in; being involved; collateral damage
1. getting caught up in; being involved in (something negative)
Being drawn into a troublesome situation, conflict, or accident without originally being a party to it. Often implies innocent involvement in someone else's problem.
喧嘩(けんか)の巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えを食(く)った。
I got caught up in their fight.
事故(じこ)で巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えになった通行人(つうこうにん)がいた。
There were passersby who got caught up in the accident.
巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えにならないよう、距離(きょり)を置(お)いている。
I'm keeping my distance so I don't get involved.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:
巻(ま)き (winding, wrapping - from 巻(ま)く) + 添(ぞ)え (accompanying - from 添(そ)える)

The image is of being "wound up" or "wrapped into" something.

KEY EXPRESSIONS:

  • 巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えを食(く)う - to get caught up in (passive involvement)
  • 巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えになる - to become involved
  • 巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えにする - to drag someone into
  • 巻(ま)き添(ぞ)えを避(さ)ける - to avoid getting involved

NUANCE:

Always negative - implies:

  • Unwilling involvement
  • Innocent third party affected
  • Unfortunate circumstance
  • Collateral damage

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 事故(じこ)の巻(ま)き添(ぞ)え - caught in an accident
  • 争(あらそ)いの巻(ま)き添(ぞ)え - caught in a dispute
  • 犯罪(はんざい)の巻(ま)き添(ぞ)え - implicated in a crime
  • リストラ(りすとら)の巻(ま)き添(ぞ)え - caught up in layoffs

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • とばっちり - splash damage, undeserved consequence
  • 巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる - to be drawn into
  • 飛(と)び火(び) - spreading (of trouble)
  • 累(るい)が及(およ)ぶ - to suffer consequences (formal)

REGISTER:
Neutral. Common in everyday conversation and news reports.
