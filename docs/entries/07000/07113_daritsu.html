打率(だりつ)

だりつ
noun
batting average
1. batting average
In baseball, a statistic measuring a batter's performance calculated by dividing the number of hits by the number of at-bats.
彼(かれ)の今(こん)シーズンの打率(だりつ)は3割(わり)を超(こ)えている。
His batting average this season is over .300.
打率(だりつ)が下(さ)がってスランプに陥(おちい)った。
His batting average dropped and he fell into a slump.
リーグ最高(さいこう)打率(だりつ)の選手(せんしゅ)が首位(しゅい)打者(だしゃ)になる。
The player with the highest batting average in the league becomes the batting champion.

READING BATTING AVERAGES IN JAPANESE:

  • .300 = 3割(わり) (san-wari)
  • .250 = 2割(わり)5分(ぶ) (ni-wari go-bu)
  • .333 = 3割(わり)3分(ぶ)3厘(りん) (san-wari san-bu san-rin)
