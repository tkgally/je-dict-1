LINE

らいん
noun
LINE (messaging app)

Definitions

1. LINE; popular messaging application
A free messaging and calling application widely used in Japan for personal and business communication. Developed by the South Korean company Naver's Japanese subsidiary.

Examples

LINEで連絡(れんらく)してね。
Contact me on LINE.
LINEを交換(こうかん)しませんか。
Would you like to exchange LINE IDs?
LINEで写真(しゃしん)を送(おく)る。
I'll send the photo via LINE.
グループLINEで予定(よてい)を決(き)めた。
We decided on the plans in the group LINE chat.

Notes

USAGE:

In Japan, LINE is often used as both a noun and verb:

  • LINEする: to message on LINE
  • LINEを送(おく)る: to send a LINE message
  • LINEが来(く)る: to receive a LINE message

FEATURES:

  • メッセージ: text messaging
  • スタンプ: stickers (very popular feature)
  • 無料通話(むりょうつうわ): free calls
  • グループトーク: group chats
  • LINE Pay: mobile payment

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • LINEを交換(こうかん)する: exchange LINE IDs
  • LINEを追加(ついか)する: add on LINE
  • LINEで連絡(れんらく)する: contact via LINE
  • 既読(きどく): "read" status (shows when message was seen)
  • 未読(みどく): "unread" status

CULTURAL NOTE:
LINE is the dominant messaging app in Japan, used by people of all ages. Exchanging LINE IDs has largely replaced exchanging phone numbers or email addresses in casual social situations. The app's "read" receipts (既読(きどく)) have even generated the term 既読(きどく)スルー (ignoring a message after reading it).
