払戻(はらいもど)し

はらいもどし
noun
refund; repayment

Definitions

1. refund; repayment
The return of money paid, such as when canceling a purchase, unused tickets, or overpayment.

Examples

切符(きっぷ)の払戻(はらいもど)しを受(う)けた。
I received a refund for the ticket.
払戻(はらいもど)し手数料(てすうりょう)がかかります。
A refund processing fee will be charged.
キャンセルした場合(ばあい)、払戻(はらいもど)しはできません。
In case of cancellation, refunds are not available.

Notes

FORMATION:
払(はら)い (paying) + 戻(もど)し (returning)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 払戻(はらいもど)しを受(う)ける (to receive a refund)
  • 払戻(はらいもど)しを請求(せいきゅう)する (to request a refund)
  • 払戻(はらいもど)し手数料(てすうりょう) (refund fee)
  • 払戻(はらいもど)し期限(きげん) (refund deadline)

CONTEXT:
Commonly used for transportation tickets, event tickets, insurance policies, and financial products. The term is more formal than 「返金(へんきん)」.

Related Words

Synonym: 返金(へんきん) (refund (casual))
general
