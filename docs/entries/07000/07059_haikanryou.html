拝観料(はいかんりょう)

はいかんりょう
noun
temple/shrine viewing fee
1. temple/shrine viewing fee; admission fee for religious sites
The fee charged to enter and view a temple, shrine, or other religious/historical site.
金閣寺(きんかくじ)の拝観料(はいかんりょう)は500円(えん)です。
The admission fee for Kinkaku-ji is 500 yen.
拝観料(はいかんりょう)を払(はら)って境内(けいだい)に入(はい)った。
I paid the admission fee and entered the temple grounds.
この神社(じんじゃ)は拝観料(はいかんりょう)無料(むりょう)だ。
This shrine has free admission.

FORMATION: 拝観(はいかん) (respectful viewing) + 料(りょう) (fee). The 拝(はい) is the same character as in 拝(おが)む (to pray to, to bow before), and it frames entry as a reverent act rather than the purchase of a ticket. That is why the word is confined to temples, shrines, and comparable historical sites.

RELATED FEE TYPES: Japanese picks the fee word by the kind of place, and the four are not interchangeable.

  • 拝観料(はいかんりょう): temples and shrines
  • 入館料(にゅうかんりょう): buildings whose name ends in 〜館(かん) — museums, galleries, halls
  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう): grounds, venues, and events
  • 観覧料(かんらんりょう): places you go to look at something — a garden, an observation deck, a zoo

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 拝観料(はいかんりょう)を払(はら)う: pay the viewing fee
  • 拝観料(はいかんりょう)無料(むりょう): admission free (sign wording; no particle)
  • 拝観料(はいかんりょう)込(こ)み: viewing fee included, e.g. in a tour price

VERB FORM: 拝観(はいかん)する is to view a temple or shrine. It is humble in origin — the speaker lowers their own act of looking — so it is not used of ordinary sightseeing.

CULTURAL NOTE: major temples in 京都(きょうと) and 奈良(なら) charge a 拝観料(はいかんりょう), commonly a few hundred yen, and it often covers only the main hall or the garden rather than the whole 境内(けいだい). Most neighbourhood 神社(じんじゃ) charge nothing at all.
