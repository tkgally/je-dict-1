落選(らくせん)

らくせん
noun, suru verb
losing (election); not being selected

Definitions

1. losing (an election)
Failing to win an election or not being elected to office.
2. not being selected (lottery/competition)
Not being chosen in a lottery, raffle, or selection process.

Examples

残念(ざんねん)ながら落選(らくせん)しました。
Unfortunately, you were not selected.
現職(げんしょく)議員(ぎいん)が落選(らくせん)した。
The incumbent lost the election.
チケットの抽選(ちゅうせん)で落選(らくせん)した。
I didn't win in the ticket lottery.

Notes

USAGE:
落選(らくせん) is the opposite of 当選(とうせん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落選(らくせん)する (to lose/not be selected)
  • 落選(らくせん)者(しゃ) (unsuccessful candidate; loser)
  • 落選(らくせん)通知(つうち) (notification of not being selected)
  • 落選(らくせん)運動(うんどう) (negative campaign)

NOTES:
落選(らくせん) carries a somewhat stronger sense of failure compared to simply "not winning." The kanji 落(らく) (fall) conveys the idea of falling short.

Related Words

Antonym: 当選(とうせん) (winning (election))
