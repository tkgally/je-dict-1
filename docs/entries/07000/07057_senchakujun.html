先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)

せんちゃくじゅん
noun
first-come, first-served
1. first-come, first-served; order of arrival
A system where people who arrive or apply first are given priority.
チケットは先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)で販売(はんばい)される。
Tickets will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis.
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)100名(めい)にプレゼントがあります。
The first 100 people will receive a gift.
予約(よやく)は先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)ですので、お早(はや)めに。
Reservations are first-come, first-served, so please hurry.
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)で整理券(せいりけん)を配(くば)っている。
They're distributing numbered tickets on a first-come, first-served basis.
先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)30名(めい)限定(げんてい)の特別(とくべつ)メニューがあったが、行(い)った時(とき)にはもう終(お)わっていた。
There was a special menu limited to the first 30 customers, but it was already sold out when I got there.

先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) means places are given out in order of arrival or application, until they run out. It is one of the two standard ways Japanese notices ration something limited.

USAGE:

  • The method takes で: 先着順(せんちゃくじゅん)で販売(はんばい) (sold first-come, first-served)
  • 先着(せんちゃく) without 順(じゅん) heads a number: 先着(せんちゃく)100名(めい) (the first 100 people)
  • Shop and event notices often add 様(さま) for politeness: 先着(せんちゃく)50名(めい)様(さま)限定(げんてい)

CONTRAST:
抽選(ちゅうせん) (lottery) is the alternative, and which one applies decides how you should behave: 先着順(せんちゃくじゅん) rewards arriving or applying early, 抽選(ちゅうせん) makes speed irrelevant. 予約制(よやくせい) (by reservation) is a third arrangement, where a booking rather than a queue secures the place.
