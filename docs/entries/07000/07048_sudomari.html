素泊(すど)まり

すどまり
noun
lodging without meals

Definitions

1. lodging without meals; room only
An accommodation option where only the room is provided, with no meals included.

Examples

素泊(すど)まりプランは安(やす)い。
The room-only plan is cheap.
食事(しょくじ)は外(そと)で食(た)べるので、素泊(すど)まりにした。
Since I'll eat out, I chose room-only lodging.
素泊(すど)まり一泊(いっぱく)5000円(えん)。
Room-only rate is 5000 yen per night.

Notes

FORMATION:
素(す) (plain/bare) + 泊(と)まり (staying)

ACCOMMODATION OPTIONS:

  • 素泊(すど)まり - room only (no meals)
  • 朝食付(ちょうしょくつ)き - with breakfast
  • 夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)き - with dinner
  • 一泊(いっぱく)二食付(にしょくつ)き - one night with two meals
  • 二食付(にしょくつ)き - with two meals (dinner and breakfast)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 素泊(すど)まりプラン - room-only plan
  • 素泊(すど)まり料金(りょうきん) - room-only rate
  • 素泊(すど)まりで泊(と)まる - to stay room-only

USAGE NOTE:
Common when booking ryokan (Japanese inns) or minshuku (guesthouses), where meals are traditionally included in the standard rate.

Related Words

Contrast: 一泊(いっぱく)二食付(にしょくつ)き (with two meals)
