程(ほど)よい

ほどよい
i-adjective
moderate; just right; appropriate

Definitions

1. moderate; just right; appropriate
Being at a suitable or comfortable level; neither too much nor too little.

Examples

程(ほど)よい距離感(きょりかん)を保(たも)つ。
To maintain an appropriate distance.
程(ほど)よい甘(あま)さで美味(おい)しい。
It's delicious with just the right amount of sweetness.
程(ほど)よい運動(うんどう)は健康(けんこう)に良(よ)い。
Moderate exercise is good for health.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
程(ほど) (degree, extent) + よい (good) → good degree = just right

FORMS:

  • 程(ほど)よい距離感(きょりかん) - appropriate distance (in relationships)
  • 程(ほど)よい甘(あま)さ - just the right sweetness
  • 程(ほど)よく (adverb) - moderately, appropriately

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 程(ほど)よい運動(うんどう) - moderate exercise
  • 程(ほど)よい緊張感(きんちょうかん) - appropriate level of tension
  • 程(ほど)よい塩加減(しおかげん) - just the right amount of salt

NUANCE:
程(ほど)よい emphasizes balance and moderation—the ideal middle ground. It's positive, suggesting something is perfectly suited to the situation.
