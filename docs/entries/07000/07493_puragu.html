プラグ

ぷらぐ
noun
plug (electrical)

Definitions

1. electrical plug; connector that fits into an outlet
The end piece of an electrical cord that is inserted into an outlet to connect a device to a power source.

Examples

プラグをコンセントに差(さ)す。
I plug it into the outlet.
プラグを抜(ぬ)いてから掃除(そうじ)する。
Unplug it before cleaning.
プラグの形状(けいじょう)が違(ちが)う。
The plug shape is different.
海外(かいがい)では変換(へんかん)プラグが必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need a plug adapter overseas.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
From English "plug"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プラグを差(さ)す / 挿(さ)す: plug in
  • プラグを抜(ぬ)く: unplug
  • 変換(へんかん)プラグ: plug adapter
  • 電源(でんげん)プラグ: power plug

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • コンセント: electrical outlet (wasei-eigo)
  • 延長(えんちょう)コード: extension cord
  • アダプター: adapter
  • 電源(でんげん)タップ: power strip

JAPAN'S ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:
Japan uses Type A plugs (two flat parallel prongs). Most Japanese electronics have two-prong plugs. Three-prong plugs (grounded) are less common and may require adapters.

NOTE:
差(さ)す is commonly used with プラグ. The outlet is called コンセント in Japanese (from English "concentric plug," a wasei-eigo term).
