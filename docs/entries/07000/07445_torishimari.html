取(と)り締(し)まり

とりしまり
noun, suru-verb
control; supervision; regulation; crackdown
1. control; supervision; regulation; enforcement
The act of maintaining order, enforcing rules, or supervising to ensure compliance with laws or regulations.
社内(しゃない)の規律(きりつ)取(と)り締(し)まりを担当(たんとう)している。
I'm in charge of maintaining discipline within the company.
2. crackdown; enforcement action
Intensified enforcement activity, especially by police or authorities against violations.
警察(けいさつ)が飲酒運転(いんしゅうんてん)の取(と)り締(し)まりを強化(きょうか)している。
The police are intensifying their crackdown on drunk driving.
違法駐車(いほうちゅうしゃ)の取(と)り締(し)まりが厳(きび)しくなった。
Enforcement against illegal parking has become stricter.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:
取(と)り締(し)まる (to control, supervise) → noun form 取(と)り締(し)まり

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 取(と)り締(し)まりを行(おこな)う - to conduct enforcement
  • 取(と)り締(し)まりを強化(きょうか)する - to intensify crackdowns
  • 取(と)り締(し)まりが厳(きび)しい - enforcement is strict
  • 取(と)り締(し)まりに遭(あ)う - to get caught in a crackdown
  • 取(と)り締(し)まりを逃(のが)れる - to escape enforcement

TYPES OF 取(と)り締(し)まり:

  • 交通(こうつう)取(と)り締(し)まり - traffic enforcement
  • 速度(そくど)取(と)り締(し)まり - speed enforcement
  • 不法投棄(ふほうとうき)の取(と)り締(し)まり - crackdown on illegal dumping

RELATED TERM:
取締役(とりしまりやく) - company director/board member (different meaning - uses the same kanji but refers to corporate governance role)

REGISTER:
Neutral to formal. Common in news reports and official announcements.
