飛(と)び込(こ)み

とびこみ
noun
diving (sport); jumping in

Definitions

1. diving (competitive sport)
The sport of jumping or diving into water from a platform or springboard, performing acrobatic movements.
2. jumping in; diving in
The act of suddenly jumping or diving into something, such as water.
3. unannounced visit; cold call
Visiting without an appointment, especially in sales contexts.

Examples

オリンピックの飛(と)び込(こ)み競技(きょうぎ)を見(み)た。
I watched the Olympic diving competition.
プールへの飛(と)び込(こ)みは禁止(きんし)されている。
Diving into the pool is prohibited.
飛(と)び込(こ)み営業(えいぎょう)で新規(しんき)顧客(こきゃく)を獲得(かくとく)した。
I acquired new customers through cold calling.

Notes

DIVING TYPES:

  • 高(た)か飛(と)び込(こ)み (platform diving - 10m platform)
  • 飛板飛(とびいたと)び込(こ)み (springboard diving)
  • シンクロナイズド飛(と)び込(こ)み (synchronized diving)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 飛(と)び込(こ)み台(だい) (diving platform/board)
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)み営業(えいぎょう) (cold calling/canvassing)
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)み客(きゃく) (walk-in customer)

VERB FORM:

  • 飛(と)び込(こ)む (to dive in, to jump into)

CAUTION: At many Japanese pools, 飛(と)び込(こ)み禁止(きんし) (no diving) signs are posted for safety reasons.
