一般(いっぱん)

いっぱん
noun/no-adjective
general, ordinary, public

Definitions

1. general, ordinary, common
What is typical or usual, not specialized or exceptional.
2. general public
Ordinary people as opposed to specialists or officials.

Examples

一般(いっぱん)の人(ひと)には難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult for ordinary people.
一般(いっぱん)公開(こうかい)されています。
It's open to the public.
一般的(いっぱんてき)に言(い)えば。
Generally speaking.
一般的(いっぱんてき) = general (na-adjective)

Notes

一般(いっぱん) means general or ordinary.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一般(いっぱん)の + noun (ordinary ~)
  • 一般(いっぱん)に (generally)
  • 一般的(いっぱんてき)に (generally - more common)
  • 一般(いっぱん)公開(こうかい) (open to public)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 一般的(いっぱんてき): general (na-adjective)
  • 一般人(いっぱんじん): ordinary person
  • 一般(いっぱん)常識(じょうしき): common knowledge
