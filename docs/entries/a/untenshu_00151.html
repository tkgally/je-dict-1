運転手(うんてんしゅ)

うんてんしゅ
noun
driver (professional)

Definitions

1. driver (professional)
A person who drives a vehicle as their occupation, such as a taxi, bus, or truck driver.

Examples

タクシーの運転手(うんてんしゅ)さん
The taxi driver
〜さん is commonly added
運転手(うんてんしゅ)に行(い)き先(さき)を伝(つた)えた。
I told the driver the destination.
バスの運転手(うんてんしゅ)になりたい。
I want to become a bus driver.

Notes

運転手(うんてんしゅ) specifically refers to professional drivers.

Components: 運転(うんてん) (driving) + 手(しゅ) (person/hand)

Usage:

  • Used for taxi drivers, bus drivers, truck drivers, chauffeurs, etc.
  • For casual/non-professional drivers, simply use 運転(うんてん)する人(ひと) or ドライバー

Polite address: Often called 運転手(うんてんしゅ)さん when addressing directly.

Related:

  • 運転(うんてん): driving
  • 運転(うんてん)する: to drive
  • 運転(うんてん)免許(めんきょ): driver's license
