上(あ)げる

あげる
ichidan verb
to raise, to give

Definitions

1. to raise, to lift up
To move something upward.
2. to give
To present to someone.

Examples

手(て)を上げる。
To raise one's hand.

Notes

上げる is versatile.

MEANINGS:

  • To raise: 手(て)を上げる
  • To give: プレゼントをあげる
  • Auxiliary: 〜てあげる (do for someone)
