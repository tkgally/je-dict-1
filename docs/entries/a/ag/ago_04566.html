顎(あご)

あご
noun
chin; jaw

Definitions

1. chin
The front part of the face below the mouth.
2. jaw
The bone structure that forms the framework of the mouth.

Examples

彼(かれ)は顎(あご)に髭(ひげ)を生(は)やしている。
He has a beard on his chin.
顎(あご)が外(はず)れそうなくらい笑(わら)った。
I laughed so hard I thought my jaw would dislocate.
顎(あご)を引(ひ)いて姿勢(しせい)を正(ただ)した。
I pulled my chin in and corrected my posture.

Notes

顎(あご) refers to both the chin and jaw area.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 顎(あご)を引(ひ)く (pull chin in, good posture)
  • 顎(あご)を上(あ)げる (raise chin, look up/act proudly)
  • 顎(あご)が外(はず)れる (jaw dislocates; figuratively: laugh hard)
  • 顎(あご)髭(ひげ) (chin beard, goatee)
  • 二重(にじゅう)顎(あご) (double chin)

BODY PART CONTEXT:

Face parts:

  • 頬(ほお) (cheek)
  • 額(ひたい) (forehead)
  • 唇(くちびる) (lips)
  • 眉(まゆ) (eyebrow)

IDIOMS:
顎(あご)で使(つか)う = to order someone around arrogantly (literally: direct with one's chin)
