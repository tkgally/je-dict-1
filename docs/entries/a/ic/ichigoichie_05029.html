一期一会(いちごいちえ)

いちごいちえ
noun, expression
once-in-a-lifetime encounter

Definitions

1. once-in-a-lifetime encounter
A concept from tea ceremony emphasizing that each meeting is unique and should be treasured. Every encounter is a singular, irreplaceable experience.

Examples

一期一会(いちごいちえ)の精神(せいしん)でお客様(おきゃくさま)をもてなす。
We serve customers with the spirit of treasuring each encounter.
旅(たび)での出会(であ)いは一期一会(いちごいちえ)だ。
Encounters during travel are once-in-a-lifetime experiences.
一期一会(いちごいちえ)を大切(たいせつ)にしたい。
I want to treasure every encounter.

Notes

A famous expression from Japanese tea ceremony (茶道(さどう)), attributed to Sen no Rikyū. Literally: one time, one meeting. Emphasizes mindfulness and appreciation of the present moment. Often used in hospitality contexts.
