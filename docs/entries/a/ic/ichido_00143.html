一度(いちど)

いちど
noun
once; one time

Definitions

1. once, one time
A single occurrence or instance.
2. one degree (temperature/angle)
A unit of measurement for temperature or angles.

Examples

一度(いちど)会(あ)ったことがある。
I've met them once.
もう一度(いちど)言(い)ってください。
Please say it one more time.
Very common expression
一度(いちど)日本(にほん)に行(い)ってみたい。
I'd like to go to Japan once (someday).
一度(いちど)に全部(ぜんぶ)はできない。
I can't do everything at once.
一度(いちど)に = at one time

Notes

一度(いちど) means 'once' or 'one time.'

Alternative reading: 一度(ひとたび) (more literary, means 'once' in the sense of 'if/when once')

Common expressions:

  • もう一度(いちど): one more time, again
  • 一度(いちど)も〜ない: not even once, never
  • 一度(いちど)に: at once, all at once
  • 一度(いちど)だけ: just once

Related counting:

  • 二度(にど): twice
  • 三度(さんど): three times
  • (But 四度(よんど) onward often uses 回(かい): 四回(よんかい))

Note: For counting occurrences, 回(かい) is more versatile: 一回(いっかい), 二回(にかい), etc.
