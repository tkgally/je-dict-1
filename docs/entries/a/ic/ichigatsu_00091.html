一月(いちがつ)

いちがつ
noun
January

Definitions

1. January
The first month of the year. In Japan, January is significant for New Year celebrations (お正月, oshōgatsu).

Examples

一月(いちがつ)に日本(にほん)へ行(い)きます。
I'm going to Japan in January.
一月(いちがつ)は寒(さむ)いです。
January is cold.
一月(いちがつ)一日(ついたち)は元日(がんじつ)です。
January 1st is New Year's Day.

Notes

Japanese month names are straightforward: the number + 月(がつ) (gatsu, month). January is literally 'month one.' Note that 一月(いちがつ) can also be read as ひとつき meaning 'one month (duration),' but for 'January' it's always いちがつ.
