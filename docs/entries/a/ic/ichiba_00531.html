市場(いちば)

いちば
noun
market, marketplace

Definitions

1. market, marketplace
A place where goods are bought and sold, especially food products.
2. market (economic)
The commercial activity of buying and selling goods.

Examples

市場(いちば)で新鮮(しんせん)な野菜(やさい)を買(か)う。
To buy fresh vegetables at the market.
朝(あさ)市場(いちば)は活気(かっき)がある。
The morning market is lively.
魚(さかな)市場(いちば)を見学(けんがく)した。
I visited the fish market.

Notes

市場(いちば) refers to physical marketplaces.

READINGS:

  • いちば: physical marketplace (shops, stalls)
  • しじょう: economic market (stock market, market forces)

COMMON TYPES:

  • 魚(さかな)市場(いちば) (fish market)
  • 青物(あおもの)市場(いちば) (vegetable market)
  • 朝(あさ)市(いち)/朝(あさ)市場(いちば) (morning market)
  • 闇(やみ)市場(いちば) (black market)

RELATED:

  • 市場(しじょう): market (economic sense)
