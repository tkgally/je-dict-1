老(お)いる

おいる
verb (ichidan, irregular)
to grow old; to age

Definitions

1. to grow old; to age
To become elderly or advanced in years. Often used for people and animals.
2. to become worn; to decline
Figuratively, when something loses its vitality or freshness.

Examples

親(おや)も老(お)いてきた。
My parents have also grown old.
老(お)いても元気(げんき)でいたい。
I want to stay healthy even when I grow old.
老(お)いた犬(いぬ)が日向(ひなた)ぼっこをしている。
An old dog is sunbathing.

Notes

老(お)いる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). It is classified as an ichidan verb but has some irregular features from classical Japanese. There is no transitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
老(お)いている indicates an ongoing or resulting state of being old.

CONJUGATION:

  • 老(お)いる (dictionary form)
  • 老(お)いて (te-form)
  • 老(お)いた (past)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 老(お)いてもなお - even in old age
  • 老(お)いた親(おや) - aged parents
  • 年(とし)を取(と)って老(お)いる - to grow old with age

LITERARY NOTE:
This verb has a somewhat literary or formal feel. In everyday speech, 年(とし)を取(と)る is more commonly used to express aging.
