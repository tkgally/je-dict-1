老(お)い

おい
noun
old age, aging

Definitions

1. old age, aging
The state of being old or the process of growing old.
2. the aged, the elderly
Old people collectively.

Examples

老(お)いを感(かん)じる。
To feel one's age.
老(お)いには勝(か)てない。
You can't fight old age.
老(お)いも若(わか)きも。
Both old and young.
Set phrase

Notes

老(お)い refers to old age or the elderly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 老(お)いを感(かん)じる (to feel one's age)
  • 老(お)いに負(ま)ける (to succumb to old age)
  • 老(お)いも若(わか)きも (old and young alike)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 老人(ろうじん): elderly person
  • 老(お)いる: to grow old (verb)
  • 老(ふ)ける: to age, look old
