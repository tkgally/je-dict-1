後(あと)で

あとで
expression
after, later

Definitions

1. after (doing something)
Indicates that something happens after another event.
2. later
At a future time, not now.

Examples

食(た)べた後(あと)で散歩(さんぽ)しましょう。
Let's take a walk after eating.
Past tense verb + 後で
後(あと)で電話(でんわ)します。
I'll call you later.
Using 'later' meaning
仕事(しごと)の後(あと)で飲(の)みに行(い)きませんか。
Won't you go drinking after work?
Noun + の後で

Notes

後(あと)で is used to indicate 'after' in time. With verbs, use [verb past tense] + 後(あと)で. With nouns, use [noun] + の後(あと)で. By itself, 後(あと)で means 'later.' The opposite is 前(まえ)に (before). Compare with 後(うしろ) which means 'behind' in location.
