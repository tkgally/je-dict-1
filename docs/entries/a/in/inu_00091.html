犬(いぬ)

いぬ
noun
dog

Definitions

1. dog
A domesticated carnivorous mammal, commonly kept as a pet or working animal.

Examples

犬(いぬ)を飼(か)いたいです。
I want to have a dog.
犬(いぬ)が吠(ほ)えています。
The dog is barking.
大(おお)きい犬(いぬ)ですね。
That's a big dog.

Notes

犬 is the word for 'dog.' Famous Japanese dog breeds include 秋田犬 (Akita-inu), 柴犬 (Shiba-inu). The story of Hachiko (ハチ公), the loyal Akita dog, is famous in Japan.

Related:

  • 子犬 (koinu, puppy)
  • 番犬 (banken, guard dog).
