命(いのち)

いのち
noun
life

Definitions

1. life
The state of being alive.
2. lifespan
The duration of one's life.

Examples

命(いのち)を守(まも)る。
To protect life.
命(いのち)がけで
Risking one's life

Notes

命 refers to life itself.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 命(いのち)がけ: at the risk of one's life
  • 命(いのち)拾(びろ)い: narrow escape from death
  • 命(いのち)を落(お)とす: to lose one's life

ALTERNATE READING:

  • 命(めい): life, fate, command
