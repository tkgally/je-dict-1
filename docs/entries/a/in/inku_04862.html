インク

インク
noun
ink

Definitions

1. ink
Colored liquid used for writing, printing, or drawing

Examples

ペンのインクが切(き)れた。
The pen ran out of ink.
プリンターのインクを交換(こうかん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
I need to replace the printer ink.
赤(あか)いインクで名前(なまえ)を書(か)かないでください。
Please don't write your name in red ink.
Writing names in red ink is traditionally avoided in Japan as it's associated with death.

Notes

インク refers to ink used in pens, printers, and other writing/printing tools.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • インクが切(き)れる: to run out of ink
  • インクを入(い)れる: to refill ink
  • インクを交換(こうかん)する: to replace ink

RELATED WORDS:

  • 墨(すみ): traditional Japanese/Chinese ink (sumi ink)
  • 朱肉(しゅにく): red ink pad (for seals/stamps)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, writing someone's name in red ink is considered inauspicious as it's associated with death and funerals.
