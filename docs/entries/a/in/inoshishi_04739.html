猪(いのしし)

いのしし
noun
wild boar

Definitions

1. wild boar
A wild pig with coarse hair and tusks. Wild boars are common in Japan's mountains and are also one of the twelve zodiac animals.

Examples

山(やま)で猪(いのしし)に遭(あ)ったら危険(きけん)だ。
It's dangerous if you encounter a wild boar in the mountains.
猪(いのしし)鍋(なべ)は冬(ふゆ)の味覚(みかく)だ。
Wild boar hot pot is a winter delicacy.
今年(ことし)は亥(い)年(どし)だ。
This year is the Year of the Boar.
亥(い) is the zodiac boar character

Notes

猪(いのしし) refers to wild boars.

Common expressions:

  • 猪(いのしし)突猛進(とつもうしん) (rushing headlong; reckless charge)
  • 猪(いのしし)鍋(なべ)/ぼたん鍋(なべ) (wild boar hot pot)
  • 猪(いのしし)肉(にく) (wild boar meat)

Counter: 頭(とう)

Zodiac:
猪(いのしし)/亥(い) is the 12th and last animal in the Japanese/Chinese zodiac. In China, the zodiac animal is a pig (豚(ぶた)), but in Japan it's specifically a wild boar.

Cultural note:
Wild boars have become a nuisance in rural Japan, damaging crops and occasionally entering urban areas. 猪(いのしし)突猛進(とつもうしん) describes someone who charges forward without thinking, like a boar.
