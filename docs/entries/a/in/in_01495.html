～員(いん)

いん
suffix
member, staff, personnel

Definitions

1. member, staff
Suffix indicating a person who belongs to an organization or performs a role.

Examples

会社(かいしゃ)員(いん)として働(はたら)く。
To work as a company employee.

Notes

EXAMPLES:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)員: company employee
  • 銀行(ぎんこう)員: bank employee
  • 店(てん)員: store clerk
  • 会(かい)員: member
  • 駅(えき)員: station staff
