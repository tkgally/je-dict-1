稲(いね)

いね
noun
rice plant, paddy

Definitions

1. rice plant
The plant that produces rice grains.
2. rice paddy, rice crop
Rice as it grows in the field.

Examples

稲(いね)が実(みの)る。
The rice plants bear grain.
稲(いね)を植(う)える。
To plant rice.
稲(いね)刈(か)りの季節(きせつ)だ。
It's rice harvesting season.

Notes

稲(いね) refers to rice as a growing plant.

RICE TERMINOLOGY:

  • 稲(いね): rice plant (growing)
  • 米(こめ): rice (grain, uncooked)
  • ご飯(はん): cooked rice, meal
  • 籾(もみ): unhulled rice

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 稲(いね)刈(か)り: rice harvesting
  • 稲穂(いなほ): ear of rice
  • 稲作(いなさく): rice cultivation
  • 水稲(すいとう): paddy rice
