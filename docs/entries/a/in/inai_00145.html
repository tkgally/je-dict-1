以内(いない)

いない
noun
within; less than; inside

Definitions

1. within, inside of (time/distance/amount)
Indicates that something is within a specified limit.

Examples

三日(みっか)以内(いない)に届(とど)きます。
It will arrive within three days.
Time limit
駅(えき)から五分(ごふん)以内(いない)です。
It's within five minutes from the station.
Distance/time
予算(よさん)以内(いない)で買(か)えた。
I was able to buy it within budget.
Amount limit
百(ひゃく)メートル以内(いない)
Within 100 meters

Notes

以内(いない) means 'within' or 'no more than.'

IMPORTANT: 以内(いない) INCLUDES the boundary:

  • 三日(みっか)以内(いない) = within 3 days (includes day 3)
  • 百円(ひゃくえん)以内(いない) = 100 yen or less

Common patterns:

  • [number] + [counter] + 以内(いない): within [number]
  • 〜以内(いない)に: within ~ (with time expressions)
  • 〜以内(いない)で: within ~ (with amount/range)

Related words:

  • 以下(いか): ~ or less (for amounts, rankings)
  • 未満(みまん): less than (excludes boundary)
  • 中(なか): inside (physical space)
