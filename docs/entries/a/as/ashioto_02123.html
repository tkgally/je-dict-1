足音(あしおと)

あしおと
noun
sound of footsteps

Definitions

1. sound of footsteps
The sound made by walking or stepping.

Examples

足音(あしおと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear footsteps.
足音(あしおと)を立(た)てないで。
Don't make noise with your footsteps.
静(しず)かな足音(あしおと)。
Quiet footsteps.

Notes

足音(あしおと) is a compound of 足(あし) (foot) + 音(おと) (sound).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 足音(あしおと)が聞(き)こえる (hear footsteps)
  • 足音(あしおと)を立(た)てる (make footstep sounds)
  • 足音(あしおと)を忍(しの)ばせる (muffle one's footsteps)

NOTE: Often used in context of detecting someone approaching or describing quiet/loud walking.
