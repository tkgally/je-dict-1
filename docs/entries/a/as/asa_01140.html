朝(あさ)

あさ
noun
morning

Definitions

1. morning
The early part of the day.

Examples

朝(あさ)起(お)きる。
To wake up in the morning.
朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べる。
To eat breakfast.

Notes

朝(あさ) means morning.

TIMES OF DAY:

  • 朝(あさ): morning
  • 昼(ひる): noon/day
  • 夕方(ゆうがた): evening
  • 夜(よる): night

COMPOUNDS:

  • 朝食(ちょうしょく): breakfast
  • 毎朝(まいあさ): every morning
  • 今朝(けさ): this morning
  • 朝日(あさひ): morning sun
