汗(あせ)

あせ
noun
sweat, perspiration

Definitions

1. sweat, perspiration
Moisture exuded through the skin.

Examples

汗(あせ)をかく。
To sweat.
汗(あせ)を拭(ふ)く。
To wipe off sweat.

Notes

汗(あせ) refers to sweat.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 汗(あせ)をかく: to sweat
  • 冷(ひ)や汗(あせ): cold sweat
  • 汗水(あせみず)垂(た)らす: to work hard (lit. dripping with sweat)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 発汗(はっかん): perspiration
  • 汗腺(かんせん): sweat gland
