甘(あま)い

あまい
adjective (i-adjective)
sweet

Definitions

1. sweet (taste)
Describes a sweet taste in food or drinks.
2. lenient, naive, optimistic
Figuratively means being too easy on someone, having naive expectations, or being overly optimistic.

Examples

このケーキは甘(あま)いです。
This cake is sweet.
甘(あま)いものが好(す)きです。
I like sweet things.
考(かんが)えが甘(あま)い。
Your thinking is naive.
Figurative meaning

Notes

甘い primarily means 'sweet' for taste but can also describe lenient parenting, naive thinking, or optimistic expectations. 甘いもの (amai mono) means 'sweets/desserts.' Conjugation: 甘くない (negative), 甘かった (past).
