あまり

あまり
adverb
not very, not much

Definitions

1. not very, not much
Used with negatives to express a low degree.

Examples

あまり好(す)きじゃないです。
I don't like it very much.
日本語(にほんご)があまりわかりません。
I don't understand Japanese very well.
あまり食(た)べませんでした。
I didn't eat much.

Notes

Must be used with negative verb forms. Softer than 全然(ぜんぜん) (not at all).

Related Words

See also: 全然(ぜんぜん)
