天(あま)の川(がわ)

あまのがわ
noun
Milky Way

Definitions

1. Milky Way
The galaxy containing our solar system, appearing as a faint band of light stretching across the night sky.

Examples

七夕(たなばた)の夜(よる)、天(あま)の川(がわ)を見(み)た。
On the night of Tanabata, I saw the Milky Way.
田舎(いなか)では天(あま)の川(がわ)がはっきり見(み)える。
In the countryside, you can see the Milky Way clearly.
天(あま)の川(がわ)には数千億(すうせんおく)の星(ほし)がある。
The Milky Way contains hundreds of billions of stars.

Notes

天(あま)の川(がわ) literally means 'River of Heaven,' the Japanese name for the Milky Way.

TANABATA LEGEND (七夕(たなばた)):
In the famous legend, 織姫(おりひめ) (Vega) and 彦星(ひこぼし) (Altair) are lovers separated by the 天(あま)の川(がわ), allowed to meet only once a year on July 7th when magpies form a bridge across the celestial river.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 天(あま)の川(がわ)が見(み)える: can see the Milky Way
  • 天(あま)の川(がわ)を渡(わた)る: to cross the Milky Way
  • 天(あま)の川(がわ)を仰(あお)ぐ: to look up at the Milky Way

SCIENTIFIC TERMS:

  • 銀河(ぎんが): galaxy (general term)
  • 銀河系(ぎんがけい): the Milky Way galaxy (scientific)
  • 天の川銀河(あまのがわぎんが): Milky Way Galaxy

BEST VIEWING:

  • Summer months in Japan
  • Away from city lights (光害(こうがい))
