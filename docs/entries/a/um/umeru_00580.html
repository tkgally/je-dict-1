埋(う)める

うめる
verb (ichidan)
to bury, to fill in

Definitions

1. to bury, to cover
To put something under the ground or cover it.
2. to fill in, to fill up
To make something full or complete.

Examples

穴(あな)を埋(う)める。
To fill in a hole.
空白(くうはく)を埋(う)める。
To fill in the blanks.
庭(にわ)に種(たね)を埋(う)めた。
I buried seeds in the garden.

Notes

埋(う)める means to bury or fill in.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 埋(う)める (transitive): to bury
  • 埋(う)まる (intransitive): to be buried

COMMON USES:

  • 穴(あな)を埋(う)める: to fill a hole
  • 空白(くうはく)を埋(う)める: to fill blanks
  • 差(さ)を埋(う)める: to close a gap
