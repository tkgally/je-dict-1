落(お)ち込(こ)む

おちこむ
verb (godan)
to feel down, to decline

Definitions

1. to feel down, to be depressed
To become sad, dejected, or emotionally low.
2. to fall into, to sink into
To drop or sink into something.
3. to decline, to slump
For business, economy, etc. to decline.

Examples

試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
そんなに落(お)ち込(こ)まないで。
Don't be so down.
売(う)り上(あ)げが落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
Sales are declining.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 落(お)ちる (to fall) + 込(こ)む (to go into). Intransitive verb.

USAGE:
Most commonly used for emotional depression (sense 1) in daily conversation.
