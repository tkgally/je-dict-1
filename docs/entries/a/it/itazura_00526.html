いたずら

いたずら
noun/na-adjective/suru verb
mischief, prank, trick

Definitions

1. mischief, prank, trick
Playful or annoying behavior, often by children.
2. tampering, meddling
Interfering with something inappropriately.
3. useless, pointless
Without purpose or effect (na-adjective use).

Examples

子供(こども)がいたずらをした。
The child played a prank.
いたずら電話(でんわ)がかかってきた。
I got a prank call.
いたずらに時間(じかん)を過(す)ごす。
To spend time uselessly.
Na-adjective/adverb usage

Notes

いたずら means mischief or pranks.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いたずらをする (to play a prank)
  • いたずら電話(でんわ) (prank call)
  • いたずら書(が)き (graffiti, scribbling)
  • いたずら好(ず)き (mischievous)

AS NA-ADJECTIVE:

  • いたずらな/いたずらに: useless, vain, idle
  • Example: いたずらに待(ま)つ (to wait in vain)

WRITING:

  • Usually hiragana
  • Can be written 悪戯(いたずら) (rare)
