いつ

いつ
adverb
when

Definitions

1. when
A question word asking about time. Used to inquire when something happens, happened, or will happen.

Examples

いつ日本(にほん)に来(き)ましたか？
When did you come to Japan?
会議(かいぎ)はいつですか？
When is the meeting?
いつでも大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)です。
Anytime is fine.
いつでも means 'anytime'

Notes

One of the essential question words (5W1H). Related expressions: いつも (always), いつか (someday), いつでも (anytime), いつまでも (forever).
