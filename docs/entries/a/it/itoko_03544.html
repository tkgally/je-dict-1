いとこ

いとこ
noun
cousin

Definitions

1. cousin
A child of one's aunt or uncle.

Examples

いとこが来週(らいしゅう)遊(あそ)びに来(く)る。
My cousin is coming to visit next week.
Basic usage
私(わたし)には三人(さんにん)のいとこがいる。
I have three cousins.
Using いる for family members
いとこの結婚式(けっこんしき)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended my cousin's wedding.
いとこの = my cousin's

Notes

KANJI VARIANTS:

いとこ can be written with various kanji depending on gender and age relative to one's parent:

  • 従兄(いとこ) - male cousin older than one's parent
  • 従弟(いとこ) - male cousin younger than one's parent
  • 従姉(いとこ) - female cousin older than one's parent
  • 従妹(いとこ) - female cousin younger than one's parent
  • 従兄弟(いとこ) - male cousin (general)
  • 従姉妹(いとこ) - female cousin (general)

In modern Japanese, hiragana いとこ is commonly used regardless of the cousin's gender or relative age.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • いとこ同士(どうし) (between cousins)
  • 従兄弟(いとこ)違(ちが)い / はとこ (second cousin)
