移転(いてん)

いてん
noun, する-verb
relocation; transfer

Definitions

1. relocation; moving (to a new location)
The act of moving a business, residence, or organization to a different location.
2. transfer (of rights, ownership)
The legal transfer of ownership, rights, or title from one party to another.

Examples

会社(かいしゃ)が新(あたら)しいビルに移転(いてん)した。
The company relocated to a new building.
所有権(しょゆうけん)の移転(いてん)手続(てつづ)きをする。
To complete the transfer of ownership procedures.
移転(いてん)先(さき)の住所(じゅうしょ)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the address of the new location.

Notes

USAGE: Used for official or formal relocation of businesses, organizations, or legal transfers.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 移転(いてん)先(さき) (new location/destination)
  • 移転(いてん)届(とどけ) (notice of relocation)
  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)移転(いてん) (transfer of ownership)
  • 本社(ほんしゃ)移転(いてん) (headquarters relocation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し (moving - usually for personal/household moves)
  • 移動(いどう) (movement - more general)
  • 移住(いじゅう) (migration - for people changing residence)

NOTE: 移転(いてん) is more formal and often used for businesses and legal contexts.

Related Words

Related: 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し (moving (household))
