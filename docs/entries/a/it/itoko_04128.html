いとこ

いとこ
noun
cousin

Definitions

1. cousin
A child of one's uncle or aunt; a relative of the same generation once removed.

Examples

いとこが来週(らいしゅう)遊(あそ)びに来(く)る。
My cousin is coming to visit next week.
私(わたし)にはいとこが五人(ごにん)いる。
I have five cousins.
いとこと小(ちい)さい頃(ころ)よく一緒(いっしょ)に遊(あそ)んだ。
I often played with my cousin when we were little.

Notes

KANJI VARIATIONS: Can be written with different kanji depending on gender and whether the cousin is older or younger:

  • 従兄弟(いとこ) - male cousin (general)
  • 従姉妹(いとこ) - female cousin (general)
  • 従兄(いとこ) - older male cousin
  • 従弟(いとこ) - younger male cousin
  • 従姉(いとこ) - older female cousin
  • 従妹(いとこ) - younger female cousin

USAGE: In everyday speech and writing, hiragana いとこ is most common and does not specify gender or relative age.

COUNTER: 人(にん) (いとこが三人(さんにん)いる)
