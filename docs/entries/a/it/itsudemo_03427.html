いつでも

いつでも
adverb
anytime, always

Definitions

1. anytime, at any time
At whatever time; without restriction on timing.
2. always, all the time
Constantly; at every occasion.

Examples

いつでも遊(あそ)びに来(き)てください。
Please come visit anytime.
彼(かれ)はいつでも元気(げんき)だ。
He is always cheerful.
いつでも連絡(れんらく)してね。
Contact me anytime.

Notes

USAGE: いつでも indicates freedom of timing (anytime) or constancy (always). It combines いつ (when) + でも (even).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いつでも + verb (anytime, you can ~)
  • いつでも + adjective (always ~)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いつでも - anytime, always
  • いつも - always, usually
  • どこでも - anywhere
  • だれでも - anyone
  • なんでも - anything

CONTRAST:

  • いつでも - anytime (flexible), always (constant)
  • いつも - always, usually (habitual)
