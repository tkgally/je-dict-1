仰向(あおむ)け

あおむけ
noun
lying face up; supine position

Definitions

1. lying face up; supine position
The position of lying with one's face and front of body facing upward.

Examples

仰向(あおむ)けに寝(ね)る。
To sleep on one's back.
空(そら)を見(み)ながら仰向(あおむ)けになった。
I lay on my back looking at the sky.
仰向(あおむ)けの姿勢(しせい)でストレッチをする。
To stretch in a supine position.

Notes

仰向(あおむ)け describes the supine position with face up. It contrasts with 俯(うつぶ)せ (face down).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 仰向(あおむ)けになる: to lie on one's back
  • 仰向(あおむ)けに寝(ね)る: to sleep on one's back
  • 仰向(あおむ)けに倒(たお)れる: to fall backward

KANJI NOTE:
The reading combines 仰(あお) (to look up) and 向(む)け (facing).

Related Words

Antonym: 俯(うつぶ)せ (face down)
