アウト

アウト
noun
out

Definitions

1. out (in baseball, sports)
When a player is eliminated or loses their turn, particularly in baseball
2. out (unacceptable, no good)
Used colloquially to indicate something is unacceptable, forbidden, or a failure

Examples

バッターはフライを打(う)ってアウトになった。
The batter hit a fly ball and was out.
遅刻(ちこく)は絶対(ぜったい)アウトだよ。
Being late is absolutely not acceptable.
ツーアウトで満塁(まんるい)だ。
There are two outs with bases loaded.

Notes

アウト is used in two main contexts.

SPORTS USAGE:
Primarily used in baseball for when a player is out. Common expressions include ワンアウト (one out), ツーアウト (two outs), スリーアウト (three outs), and アウトになる (to be out).

COLLOQUIAL USAGE:
Widely used in everyday speech to mean something is unacceptable, a deal-breaker, or fails to meet standards. The opposite is セーフ (safe/acceptable).

Common expressions:

  • アウトになる: to be out, to become unacceptable
  • 〜はアウト: ~ is not okay/unacceptable
