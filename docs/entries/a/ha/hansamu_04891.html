ハンサム

ハンサム
na-adjective, noun
handsome

Definitions

1. handsome, good-looking (for men)
Describes an attractive man with good features

Examples

彼(かれ)はとてもハンサムだ。
He is very handsome.
ハンサムな俳優(はいゆう)が人気(にんき)だ。
The handsome actor is popular.
背(せ)が高(たか)くてハンサムな男性(だんせい)が好(す)みだ。
I prefer tall, handsome men.

Notes

ハンサム describes men who are good-looking.

USAGE:

  • ハンサムな + noun: handsome ~
  • ハンサムだ: is handsome

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • イケメン: handsome guy (more casual, modern slang)
  • 男前(おとこまえ): handsome (traditional Japanese term)
  • かっこいい: cool, good-looking (broader usage)

NOTE:
ハンサム is primarily used for men. For women, 美(うつく)しい, 綺麗(きれい), or 可愛(かわい)い are typically used instead.

REGISTER:
ハンサム sounds somewhat dated compared to イケメン, which is the more common term among younger speakers.
