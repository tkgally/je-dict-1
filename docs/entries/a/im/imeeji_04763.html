イメージ

いめーじ
noun
image, impression

Definitions

1. image, impression
The mental picture or concept one has of something or someone.
2. image, visual representation
A visual representation or picture.

Examples

その会社(かいしゃ)のイメージが変(か)わった。
The image of that company has changed.
日本(にほん)のイメージはどうですか。
What is your impression of Japan?
頭(あたま)の中(なか)で完成(かんせい)したイメージを描(えが)く。
I draw the completed image I have in my mind.

Notes

イメージ is a versatile loanword used for both mental impressions and visual representations.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • イメージが良(い)い/悪(わる)い: to have a good/bad image
  • イメージアップ: image improvement
  • イメージダウン: damage to one's image
  • イメージチェンジ: image change, makeover
  • イメージどおり: as imagined, as expected

USAGE NOTE:
イメージする can be used as a suru-verb meaning "to imagine" or "to picture."
