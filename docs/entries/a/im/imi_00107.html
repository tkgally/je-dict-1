意味(いみ)

いみ
noun, suru verb
meaning, significance

Definitions

1. meaning, sense
What a word, phrase, or action signifies or represents.
2. to mean (suru verb)
When used as a suru verb, means 'to mean' or 'to signify.'

Examples

この言葉(ことば)の意味(いみ)は何(なん)ですか。
What is the meaning of this word?
意味(いみ)が分(わ)かりません。
I don't understand the meaning.
それは何(なに)を意味(いみ)しますか。
What does that mean?
Using 意味する as a verb

Notes

意味 is commonly used when asking about or explaining the meaning of words or concepts. Common question: 「～の意味は何ですか」or「～はどういう意味ですか」(What does ~ mean?). Can function as a suru verb: 意味する (to mean, to signify).
