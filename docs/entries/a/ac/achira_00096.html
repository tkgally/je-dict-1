あちら

あちら
pronoun
over there (polite)

Definitions

1. over there (polite)
A polite demonstrative for a direction or location far from both speaker and listener.
2. that person (polite)
A polite way to refer to a third party.

Examples

お手洗(てあら)いはあちらです。
The restroom is over there.
Common in service contexts
あちらでお待(ま)ちください。
Please wait over there.
あちらの方(かた)にお聞(き)きください。
Please ask that person over there.
Polite reference to a third party

Notes

あちら is the polite equivalent of あそこ, used in formal situations and customer service. It's commonly heard in department stores, hotels, and other service environments. When referring to people, あちらの方(かた) is a polite way to say 'that person over there.'
