暴(あば)れる

あばれる
verb (ichidan)
to act violently; to rage; to run wild

Definitions

1. to act violently; to behave wildly
To behave in an uncontrolled, violent, or destructive manner.
2. to rage; to run rampant
When natural forces or animals become violent and uncontrollable.

Examples

酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)いが店(みせ)の中(なか)で暴(あば)れている。
A drunk is acting violently inside the store.
台風(たいふう)が九州(きゅうしゅう)で暴(あば)れた。
The typhoon raged across Kyushu.
子供(こども)たちは公園(こうえん)で暴(あば)れ回(まわ)っていた。
The children were running wild around the park.

Notes

暴(あば)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). There is no direct transitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
暴(あば)れている indicates an ongoing state of violent or wild behavior.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 暴(あば)れ回(まわ)る - to run wild, to rampage
  • 暴(あば)れ出(だ)す - to start acting violently
  • 暴(あば)れん坊(ぼう) - a wild child, troublemaker

RELATED WORDS:

  • 暴力(ぼうりょく) - violence
  • 乱暴(らんぼう) - violent, rough (na-adjective)
  • 荒(あ)れる - to become rough/stormy (related but different nuance)
