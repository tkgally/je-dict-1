浴(あ)びる

あびる
ichidan verb
to bathe in, to shower

Definitions

1. to bathe in, to shower
To pour water over oneself; to take a shower.
2. to be exposed to, to bask in
To be covered by or exposed to something like light or attention.

Examples

シャワーを浴(あ)びる。
To take a shower.
日光(にっこう)を浴(あ)びる。
To bask in sunlight.
朝(あさ)シャワーを浴(あ)びてから会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)く。
I take a shower in the morning before going to work.

Notes

浴(あ)びる is used for showering and being exposed to things.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シャワーを浴(あ)びる (take a shower)
  • 日光(にっこう)を浴(あ)びる (bask in sunlight)
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を浴(あ)びる (attract attention)
  • 批判(ひはん)を浴(あ)びる (receive criticism)

NOTE: Different from 入(はい)る (お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)る - to take a bath).
