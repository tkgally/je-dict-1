危(あぶ)ない

あぶない
adjective (i-adjective)
dangerous

Definitions

1. dangerous, hazardous
Involving or causing risk of harm or injury.
2. risky, precarious
Uncertain or unstable, potentially leading to a bad outcome.

Examples

ここは危(あぶ)ないです。
This place is dangerous.
危(あぶ)ない！車(くるま)が来(き)ます！
Watch out! A car is coming!
Used as a warning exclamation
その考(かんが)えは危(あぶ)ないです。
That idea is risky.

Notes

危(あぶ)ない! is often used as a warning exclamation, similar to 'Watch out!' or 'Danger!' Related: 危険(きけん) (danger/dangerous - more formal noun/na-adjective). 危(あぶ)なく〜するところだった means 'almost did something bad'.
