親(おや)

おや
noun
parent

Definitions

1. parent, parents
One's father and/or mother.

Examples

親(おや)に相談(そうだん)する。
To consult with one's parents.
親(おや)の言(い)うことを聞(き)きなさい。
Listen to what your parents say.
親(おや)になる。
To become a parent.
親(おや)子(こ)で出(で)かけた。
We went out as parent and child.

Notes

親(おや) means 'parent' (can refer to one or both parents).

Family terms:

  • 父(ちち)/母(はは): father/mother (humble, about own)
  • お父(とう)さん/お母(かあ)さん: father/mother (polite, about others)
  • 両親(りょうしん): both parents (formal)

Common compounds:

  • 親(おや)子(こ): parent and child
  • 親(おや)友(とも): close friend
  • 親(おや)会社(がいしゃ): parent company
  • 親(おや)指(ゆび): thumb (parent finger)
  • 親(おや)切(きり): kindness (from 親切)

Note: 親(おや) is the generic/casual term. In formal contexts or when referring to someone else's parents, use ご両親(りょうしん).
