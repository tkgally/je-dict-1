受(う)け止(と)める

うけとめる
verb (ichidan)
to catch, to take seriously

Definitions

1. to catch, to stop
To physically catch or stop something coming toward you.
2. to accept, to take seriously
To mentally receive and process information, criticism, or feelings.

Examples

ボールを受(う)け止(と)める。
To catch the ball.
批判(ひはん)を真剣(しんけん)に受(う)け止(と)める。
I take the criticism seriously.
彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちを受(う)け止(と)めてあげて。
Please accept his feelings.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 受(う)ける (to receive) + 止(と)める (to stop). Transitive verb.
