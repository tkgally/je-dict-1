受付(うけつけ)

うけつけ
noun
reception; information desk; receptionist

Definitions

1. reception desk, front desk
The area where visitors are received and directed.
2. receptionist
The person who works at the reception desk.
3. acceptance, registration
The act of receiving applications, registrations, etc.

Examples

受付(うけつけ)で名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name at the reception desk.
受付(うけつけ)は何時(なんじ)までですか。
Until what time is registration open?
受付(うけつけ)の人(ひと)に聞(き)いてください。
Please ask the receptionist.
申(もう)し込(こ)みの受付(うけつけ)は終(お)わりました。
Registration/application acceptance is closed.

Notes

受付(うけつけ) can refer to the reception desk, the receptionist, or the act of accepting/registering.

Derived from: 受(う)け付(つ)ける (to accept, to receive)

Common collocations:

  • 受付(うけつけ)で: at the reception desk
  • 受付(うけつけ)時間(じかん): reception hours
  • 受付(うけつけ)開始(かいし): start of registration
  • 受付(うけつけ)終了(しゅうりょう): end of registration
  • 受付(うけつけ)番号(ばんごう): reception number (ticket number)

Usage: Found in hospitals, hotels, offices, event venues, etc.
