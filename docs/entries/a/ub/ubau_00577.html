奪(うば)う

うばう
verb (godan)
to snatch, to rob

Definitions

1. to snatch, to rob, to take by force
To take something away from someone forcibly.

Examples

命(いのち)を奪(うば)う。
To take someone's life.
バッグを奪(うば)われた。
My bag was snatched.
目(め)を奪(うば)われる。
To be captivated.
Literally: to have one's eyes stolen

Notes

奪(うば)う means to snatch or rob.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 命(いのち)を奪(うば)う: to take someone's life
  • 目(め)を奪(うば)う: to captivate
  • 心(こころ)を奪(うば)う: to steal someone's heart

RELATED:

  • 盗(ぬす)む: to steal (secretly)
  • 取(と)る: to take
