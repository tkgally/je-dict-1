お釣(つ)り

おつり
noun
change (money)

Definitions

1. change (money returned)
The money returned to you when you pay more than the price of something. The お is an honorific prefix that is typically included.

Examples

お釣(つ)りはいりません。
Keep the change.
Polite way to let someone keep the change
お釣(つ)りをください。
Please give me my change.
お釣(つ)りは500円(えん)です。
Your change is 500 yen.

Notes

Almost always used with the honorific お prefix. The verb もらう (to receive) or 受け取る (to receive) is often used with お釣(つ)り. At stores, you might hear お釣(つ)りの確認(かくにん)をお願(ねが)いします (please check your change).
