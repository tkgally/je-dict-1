落(お)とす

おとす
verb (godan)
to drop, to lose

Definitions

1. to drop, to let fall
To cause something to fall, either accidentally or intentionally. The transitive form—someone drops something.
2. to lose, to misplace
To lose an item by accidentally dropping it somewhere.
3. to remove, to wash off
To remove stains, dirt, or makeup from a surface.

Examples

コップを落(お)として割(わ)ってしまった。
I dropped the cup and it broke.
Accidental dropping—落(お)として + result
財布(さいふ)を落(お)としたかもしれない。
I might have lost my wallet.
'Lose' meaning—dropped and lost
化粧(けしょう)を落(お)とす。
Remove makeup.
Used for removing makeup, stains, etc.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 落(お)ちる (intransitive, to fall). Pattern: Xを落(お)とす (drop X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing or completed action with result. 落(お)としている = 'is dropping' or 'has dropped.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 物(もの)を落(お)とす (drop something)
  • 財布(さいふ)を落(お)とす (lose a wallet)
  • 化粧(けしょう)を落(お)とす (remove makeup)
  • 汚(よご)れを落(お)とす (remove dirt/stains)
  • スピードを落(お)とす (reduce speed)
  • 声(こえ)を落(お)とす (lower one's voice)

NOTE ON KANJI: The vocabulary list shows 落す but the standard form is 落(お)とす with the と included.

IDIOMS:

  • 気(き)を落(お)とす (to be discouraged)
  • 命(いのち)を落(お)とす (to lose one's life)
  • 穴(あな)に落(お)とす (to trick someone—lit. 'drop into a hole')

REGISTER: Neutral.
