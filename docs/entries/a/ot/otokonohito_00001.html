男(おとこ)の人(ひと)

おとこのひと
noun
man, male person

Definitions

1. man, male person
A polite or neutral way to refer to an adult male.

Examples

あの男(おとこ)の人(ひと)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is that man?
駅(えき)で男(おとこ)の人(ひと)に道(みち)を聞(き)かれた。
A man asked me for directions at the station.
背(せ)の高(たか)い男(おとこ)の人(ひと)が入(はい)ってきた。
A tall man came in.

Notes

男(おとこ)の人(ひと) is the polite, neutral way to say 'man' in Japanese, commonly used when referring to or describing men you don't know personally.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 男(おとこ): man (slightly more direct/casual)
  • 男性(だんせい): male (formal, often used in official contexts)
  • 紳士(しんし): gentleman (formal, respectful)

The opposite term is 女(おんな)の人(ひと) (woman).

Related Words

Antonym: 女(おんな)の人(ひと) (woman)
