およそ

およそ
adverb
about, approximately

Definitions

1. about, approximately, roughly
Used to indicate an approximate amount, number, or extent.
2. generally, on the whole
Used to speak in general terms about something.

Examples

およそ三十分(さんじゅっぷん)かかります。
It takes approximately thirty minutes.
参加者(さんかしゃ)はおよそ百人(ひゃくにん)だった。
There were about one hundred participants.
およそ想像(そうぞう)もつかない。
I can't even begin to imagine.
Fixed expression with negative

Notes

およそ means 'approximately' or 'about.' It is somewhat formal.

POSITION: Usually appears before numbers or quantities.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • およそ vs. だいたい: Both mean 'approximately,' but およそ is more formal/written; だいたい is more casual
  • およそ vs. 約(やく): 約(やく) is commonly used before numbers in formal writing
  • およそ vs. ほぼ: ほぼ means 'almost' and implies closer to the exact amount

FIXED EXPRESSIONS:

  • およそ〜ない: not at all, cannot possibly (emphatic negative)

Example: およそ考(かんが)えられない (utterly unthinkable)

KANJI: Can be written as 凡(およ)そ, but hiragana is more common.

Related Words

Synonym: だいたい (roughly (casual))
Synonym: 約(やく) (approximately)
