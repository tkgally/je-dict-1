ああ

ああ
adverb, interjection
that way, like that; oh, ah

Definitions

1. that way, like that (distant)
Refers to a manner or state distant from both speaker and listener. Part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system.
2. oh, ah (interjection)
An exclamation expressing various emotions like realization, satisfaction, or disappointment.

Examples

ああいう人(ひと)になりたい。
I want to become that kind of person.
ああいう = that kind of
ああ、そうですか。
Oh, is that so?
Interjection showing realization
ああ、疲(つか)れた。
Ah, I'm tired.
Expressing a feeling

Notes

ああ belongs to the ko-so-a-do system: こう (this way), そう (that way, near listener), ああ (that way, far from both). As an interjection, it can express realization, relief, disappointment, or other emotions depending on tone.

Common patterns:

  • ああいう (that kind of)
  • ああやって (like that, doing it that way).
