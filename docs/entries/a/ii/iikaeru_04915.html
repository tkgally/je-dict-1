言(い)い換(か)える

いいかえる
ichidan verb
to rephrase, to put differently

Definitions

1. to rephrase, to say in other words
To express the same meaning using different words, often to make something clearer.
2. in other words (when used as an expression)
Used as a transitional phrase to introduce a rephrased or clarified statement.

Examples

もっと簡単(かんたん)に言(い)い換(か)えてもらえますか。
Could you rephrase that more simply?
言(い)い換(か)えれば、彼(かれ)は断(ことわ)ったということだ。
In other words, he refused.
[Note: 言(い)い換(か)えれば is commonly used to mean 'in other words']
専門(せんもん)用語(ようご)を日常(にちじょう)的(てき)な言葉(ことば)に言(い)い換(か)えた。
I rephrased the technical terms into everyday language.

Notes

言(い)い換(か)える is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to rephrase or express differently.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 言(い)う (to say) + 換(か)える (to exchange, to replace) → to replace one's words with different ones

ASPECT (ている):
言(い)い換(か)えている is rarely used; the verb is typically used in non-progressive forms.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 言(い)い換(か)えれば (in other words)
  • 言(い)い換(か)えると (to put it another way)
  • ～に言(い)い換(か)える (rephrase as~)
  • 別(べつ)の言葉(ことば)で言(い)い換(か)える (rephrase in different words)

SET EXPRESSIONS:

  • 言(い)い換(か)えれば / 言(い)い換(か)えると: "in other words" - very common transitional phrase
