いい

いい
adjective (i-adjective)
good, fine, nice

Definitions

1. good, fine, nice
A general positive evaluation. Can describe quality, condition, character, or suitability. One of the most frequently used adjectives in Japanese.
2. okay, all right (permission/acceptance)
Used to give permission or indicate that something is acceptable. いいですよ (That's fine / Go ahead).
3. no thank you, I'm fine
Used to politely decline an offer. When someone offers you something, いいです can mean 'No, thank you' or 'I'm fine (without it).'

Examples

この映画はとてもいいです。
This movie is very good.
天気がいいですね。
The weather is nice, isn't it?
写真を撮ってもいいですか。
May I take a photo?
〜てもいい asking permission
コーヒーはいかがですか。—いいです。
Would you like coffee? —No, thank you. / I'm fine.
Declining an offer

Notes

いい has irregular conjugation. The negative, past, and other forms use the stem よ- from the alternate form 良い (よい): よくない (not good), よかった (was good), よく (well). The form いい is used only in the dictionary/plain present form. 良い (yoi) is more formal/literary. In the pattern 〜てもいい (may I...?), いい grants permission.
