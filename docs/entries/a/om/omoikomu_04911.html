思(おも)い込(こ)む

おもいこむ
godan verb
to be convinced, to assume, to believe firmly

Definitions

1. to be convinced, to be under the impression
To believe something strongly, often without sufficient evidence or despite being incorrect.
2. to assume mistakenly, to jump to conclusions
To believe something to be true based on assumptions rather than facts.

Examples

彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)が正(ただ)しいと思(おも)い込(こ)んでいる。
He's convinced that he's right.
会議(かいぎ)は来週(らいしゅう)だと思(おも)い込(こ)んでいた。
I was under the impression that the meeting was next week.
思(おも)い込(こ)みが激(はげ)しい人(ひと)だ。
He's a person who jumps to conclusions. / He has strong preconceptions.
[Note: 思(おも)い込(こ)み is the noun form meaning 'preconception' or 'assumption']

Notes

思(おも)い込(こ)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to be firmly convinced of something.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 思(おも)う (to think) + 込(こ)む (to go into deeply) → to think deeply and become fixed in belief

ASPECT (ている):
思(おも)い込(こ)んでいる is the most common form, indicating a state of being convinced or under an impression.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～だと思(おも)い込(こ)む (be convinced that~)
  • 思(おも)い込(こ)みが激(はげ)しい (prone to making assumptions)
  • 勝手に思(おも)い込(こ)む (assume on one's own)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 思(おも)い込(こ)み: preconception, assumption, belief

NUANCE:
Often implies that the belief is mistaken or at least unverified. Used when someone has convinced themselves of something that may not be accurate.
