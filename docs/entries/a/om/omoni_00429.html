主(おも)に

おもに
adverb
mainly, primarily

Definitions

1. mainly, primarily, chiefly
Indicates what constitutes the largest part or most significant aspect of something.

Examples

主(おも)に野菜(やさい)を食(た)べています。
I mainly eat vegetables.
主(おも)に学生(がくせい)が来(き)ます。
It's mainly students who come.
この店(みせ)では主(おも)に和食(わしょく)を出(だ)しています。
This restaurant mainly serves Japanese food.

Notes

主(おも)に indicates that something is the main or primary element.

POSITION: Usually placed before the element being emphasized.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 主(おも)に + noun (mainly ~)
  • 主(おも)に～ている (mainly doing ~)

RELATED WORD:

  • 主(おも)な: main, principal (adjective form)
  • 主(おも)な理由(りゆう) (main reason)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主(おも)に: mainly (what is primary)
  • たいてい: usually (frequency)
  • だいたい: mostly, approximately
