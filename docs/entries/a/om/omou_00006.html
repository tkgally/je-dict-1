思(おも)う

おもう
verb (godan)
to think, to feel

Definitions

1. to think, to believe
To have an opinion or belief about something. Often used with と思(おも)う to express one's thoughts.
2. to feel, to sense
To have an emotional response or impression. そう思(おも)う (I feel that way).
3. to expect, to suppose
To anticipate or assume something. 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ると思(おも)う (I think it will rain).

Examples

明日(あした)は晴(は)れると思(おも)います。
I think it will be sunny tomorrow.
〜と思(おも)う for expressing opinions
どう思(おも)いますか。
What do you think?
Common question
彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないと思(おも)う。
I don't think he will come.
Negative inside the quote, not on 思(おも)う

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: 〜と思(おも)う (think that ~) or Xを思(おも)う (think of X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing thought or opinion. 思(おも)っている = 'think/believe' (current opinion).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜と思(おも)う (think that ~)
  • どう思(おも)う (what do you think?)
  • そう思(おも)う (I think so)
  • 〜と思(おも)っている (believe/have the opinion that)

NOTE: Japanese often puts negatives inside the quote: 来(こ)ないと思(おも)う rather than 来(く)ると思(おも)わない.

Compare: 考(かんが)える implies more deliberate reasoning/consideration.
