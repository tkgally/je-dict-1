面白(おもしろ)い

おもしろい
i-adjective
interesting, funny

Definitions

1. interesting
Attracting attention or curiosity.
2. funny, amusing
Causing laughter or amusement.

Examples

この本(ほん)は面白(おもしろ)いです。
This book is interesting.
面白(おもしろ)い映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I watched an interesting movie.
あの人(ひと)は面白(おもしろ)いですね。
That person is funny, isn't he?
Sense 2

Notes

Antonym: つまらない (boring). Often written in hiragana: おもしろい.

Related Words

See also: つまらない
