おもちゃ

おもちゃ
noun
toy; plaything

Definitions

1. toy; plaything
An object for children to play with.

Examples

子供(こども)におもちゃを買(か)った。
I bought a toy for the child.
おもちゃで遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play with toys.
おもちゃを片付(かたづ)けなさい。
Put away your toys.
Common parent instruction

Notes

おもちゃ is typically written in hiragana. The kanji 玩具 exists but is rarely used in everyday writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おもちゃで遊(あそ)ぶ: to play with toys
  • おもちゃ屋(や): toy store
  • おもちゃ箱(ばこ): toy box
