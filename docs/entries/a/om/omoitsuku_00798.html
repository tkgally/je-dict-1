思(おも)い付(つ)く

おもいつく
verb (godan)
to think of, to hit upon

Definitions

1. to think of, to come up with
To have an idea suddenly come to mind.

Examples

いい考(かんが)えを思(おも)い付(つ)いた。
I thought of a good idea.
何(なに)か思(おも)い付(つ)いたら教(おし)えて。
Let me know if you think of something.
急(きゅう)に思(おも)い付(つ)いた。
It suddenly occurred to me.

Notes

思(おも)い付(つ)く means to think of or come up with.

RELATED:

  • 思(おも)い付(つ)き: idea, thought (noun)
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember
  • 考(かんが)え付(つ)く: to think up

SIMILAR:

  • 気(き)が付(つ)く: to notice
