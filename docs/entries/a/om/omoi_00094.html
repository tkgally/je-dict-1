重(おも)い

おもい
adjective (i-adjective)
heavy

Definitions

1. heavy (in weight)
Having great weight, difficult to lift or carry. The opposite of 軽(かる)い (light).
2. serious, severe
Can describe something serious or severe, like a serious illness or heavy responsibility.

Examples

この荷物(にもつ)は重(おも)いです。
This luggage is heavy.
重(おも)くて持(も)てません。
It's too heavy; I can't carry it.
責任(せきにん)が重(おも)いです。
The responsibility is heavy/serious.

Notes

The opposite is 軽(かる)い (light). 重(おも)い can be used figuratively: 重(おも)い病気(びょうき) (serious illness), 空気(くうき)が重(おも)い (heavy atmosphere, tense mood). 重(おも)すぎる means 'too heavy'.
