おっしゃる

おっしゃる
verb
to say; to speak (honorific)

Definitions

1. to say; to speak (honorific)
Honorific form of 言う, used to show respect toward the speaker being referenced.

Examples

先生(せんせい)がおっしゃったことを覚(おぼ)えていますか。
Do you remember what the teacher said?
おっしゃる通(とお)りです。
It's just as you say.
Common expression of agreement
何(なん)とおっしゃいましたか。
What did you say?
Polite way to ask someone to repeat

Notes

おっしゃる is an honorific (尊敬語) verb used when referring to what someone of higher status says.

CONJUGATION:

  • おっしゃいます (polite)
  • おっしゃって (te-form)
  • おっしゃった (past)

KEIGO TYPE:

  • 尊敬語 (sonkeigo): Honorific language
  • Humble equivalent: 申(もう)す, 申(もう)し上(あ)げる

Related Words

Keigo: 言(い)う (plain form)
Keigo: 申す (humble equivalent)
Keigo: 申(もう)し上(あ)げる (humble equivalent)
