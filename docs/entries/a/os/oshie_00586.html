教(おし)え

おしえ
noun
teaching, doctrine, lesson

Definitions

1. teaching, instruction
Knowledge or guidance passed on from a teacher.
2. doctrine, precept
Religious or philosophical teachings.
3. lesson, moral
A principle to be learned from an experience.

Examples

先生(せんせい)の教(おし)えを守(まも)る。
To follow the teacher's teachings.
仏(ほとけ)の教(おし)え。
The teachings of Buddha.
この物語(ものがたり)には大切(たいせつ)な教(おし)えがある。
This story has an important lesson.

Notes

教(おし)え is the noun form of 教(おし)える (to teach).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 教(おし)えを守(まも)る (to follow teachings)
  • 教(おし)えを受(う)ける (to receive teachings)
  • 教(おし)えに従(したが)う (to follow teachings)

RELATED:

  • 教(おし)える: to teach (verb)
  • 教訓(きょうくん): lesson, moral
  • 教義(きょうぎ): doctrine, dogma
