恐(おそ)らく

おそらく
adverb
perhaps, probably, likely

Definitions

1. perhaps, probably, likely
Indicates the speaker's conjecture or speculation about something uncertain but likely.

Examples

恐(おそ)らく彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないだろう。
He probably won't come.
恐(おそ)らく明日(あした)は雨(あめ)でしょう。
It will probably rain tomorrow.
この問題(もんだい)は恐(おそ)らく難(むずか)しすぎる。
This problem is probably too difficult.

Notes

USAGE: 恐らく is used to express the speaker's speculation or conjecture. It conveys a moderate to high degree of likelihood but acknowledges uncertainty.

POSITION: Can appear at the beginning of a sentence or before the predicate.

REGISTER: Slightly formal; common in both written and spoken Japanese.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • たぶん - probably (more casual)
  • おそらく - perhaps (slightly more formal than たぶん)
  • きっと - surely (expresses higher certainty)
