おしゃれ

おしゃれ
na-adjective
fashionable, stylish

Definitions

1. fashionable, stylish, chic
Describes someone or something that is stylish, well-dressed, or has good taste in fashion and appearance.
2. dressing up, making oneself stylish
As a noun or suru-verb, refers to the act of dressing up or paying attention to one's appearance.

Examples

おしゃれなカフェを見(み)つけた。
I found a stylish cafe.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつもおしゃれだ。
She's always fashionable.
今日(きょう)はおしゃれして出(で)かけよう。
Let's dress up and go out today.
おしゃれする = to dress up

Notes

Can function as a na-adjective, noun, or suru-verb.

FORMS:

  • おしゃれな + noun (おしゃれな服(ふく) = stylish clothes)
  • おしゃれする (to dress up, make oneself stylish)
  • おしゃれさん (a fashionable person - affectionate)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おしゃれな店(みせ) (stylish shop)
  • おしゃれな街(まち) (fashionable neighborhood)
  • おしゃれに気(き)を使(つか)う (to care about one's appearance)

Can be written with kanji as お洒落(おしゃれ), but hiragana is more common.
