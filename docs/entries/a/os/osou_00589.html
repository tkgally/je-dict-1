襲(おそ)う

おそう
verb (godan)
to attack, to strike

Definitions

1. to attack, to assault
To make a sudden violent action against.
2. to strike, to hit
For a disaster or feeling to come upon suddenly.

Examples

敵(てき)を襲(おそ)う。
To attack the enemy.
台風(たいふう)が町(まち)を襲(おそ)った。
A typhoon struck the town.
不安(ふあん)に襲(おそ)われた。
I was overcome with anxiety.

Notes

襲(おそ)う means to attack or strike.

USAGE:

  • Physical attacks: 敵(てき)を襲(おそ)う
  • Natural disasters: 地震(じしん)が襲(おそ)う
  • Emotions: 不安(ふあん)に襲(おそ)われる

PASSIVE FORM:

  • Xに襲(おそ)われる: to be attacked by X, to be struck by X
