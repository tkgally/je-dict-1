以後(いご)

いご
adverb
after this, from now on, hereafter

Definitions

1. after this, from now on
From this point forward in time.
2. after (a specific time/event)
Following a specified point in time or event.

Examples

以後(いご)気(き)をつけます。
I'll be careful from now on.
3時(じ)以後(いご)は会議(かいぎ)があります。
I have a meeting after 3 o'clock.
卒業(そつぎょう)以後(いご)、会(あ)っていない。
I haven't seen them since graduation.

Notes

以後(いご) indicates a time after a specified point.

USAGE:

  • Can stand alone to mean "from now on"
  • Can follow a time/event: X以後(いご) (after X)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 以後(いご): after, from now on (formal)
  • 以来(いらい): since, ever since (emphasizes continuation)
  • 以降(いこう): from ~ onward (often written contexts)
  • これから: from now (conversational)
