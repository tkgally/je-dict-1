意見(いけん)

いけん
noun
opinion; view; idea

Definitions

1. opinion, view, idea
A personal thought or judgment about something.
2. advice, suggestion
A recommendation or counsel given to someone.

Examples

ご意見(いけん)をお聞(き)かせください。
Please let me hear your opinion.
Polite request
意見(いけん)が合(あ)わない。
Our opinions don't match.
Common expression
自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)を言(い)う。
To express one's opinion.
意見(いけん)を求(もと)める。
To seek opinions/advice.

Notes

意見(いけん) means 'opinion' or 'view.'

Common collocations:

  • 意見(いけん)を言(い)う/述(の)べる: to state an opinion
  • 意見(いけん)を聞(き)く: to listen to/ask for opinions
  • 意見(いけん)が合(あ)う/一致(いっち)する: to agree (opinions match)
  • 意見(いけん)が違(ちが)う/分(わ)かれる: to disagree
  • 意見(いけん)を交換(こうかん)する: to exchange opinions

Similar words:

  • 意見(いけん) vs. 考(かんが)え: 意見(いけん) is more formal/official; 考(かんが)え is more casual/personal thinking
  • 意見(いけん) vs. 感想(かんそう): 感想(かんそう) is impression/feeling; 意見(いけん) is thought/judgment
