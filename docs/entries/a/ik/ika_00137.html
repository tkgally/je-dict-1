以下(いか)

いか
noun
below; less than; the following

Definitions

1. below, under, less than or equal to
Indicates a number/amount that is equal to or less than the specified value.
2. the following, as follows
Introduces what comes after in a list or explanation.
3. inferior, subordinate
Lower in rank or quality.

Examples

十八歳(じゅうはっさい)以下(いか)は入場(にゅうじょう)できません。
Those 18 and under cannot enter.
Includes 18
以下(いか)の点(てん)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please pay attention to the following points.
千円(せんえん)以下(いか)で買(か)える。
You can buy it for 1000 yen or less.

Notes

以下(いか) means 'below' or 'less than or equal to.'

IMPORTANT: In Japanese, 以下(いか) INCLUDES the stated number:

  • 十(じゅう)以下(いか) = 10 or less (includes 10)
  • This differs from English 'less than' which excludes the number

Contrast with 未満(みまん):

  • 十(じゅう)未満(みまん) = less than 10 (excludes 10)

Antonym: 以上(いじょう) (above, more than or equal to)

Common patterns:

  • [number]以下(いか): [number] or less
  • 以下(いか)の〜: the following ~
  • 以下(いか)同様(どうよう): same as below
