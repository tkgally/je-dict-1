いけない

いけない
adjective
no good, must not

Definitions

1. no good, won't do
Something is unacceptable or won't work.
2. must not
Used to express prohibition.

Examples

これではいけない。
This won't do.
ここで泳(およ)いではいけない。
You must not swim here.

Notes

いけない is often used in grammar patterns.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜てはいけない: must not ~
  • 〜なければいけない: must ~
  • 〜ないといけない: have to ~

VARIANT:

  • いけません (polite)
