勢(いきお)い

いきおい
noun/adverb
force, vigor, momentum

Definitions

1. force, vigor, energy
Physical or emotional strength and intensity.
2. momentum, impetus
The force or speed gained by movement or progress.
3. influence, power
The capacity to affect others or events.
4. naturally, inevitably
As a natural consequence (adverbial use).

Examples

勢(いきお)いよく走(はし)り出(だ)した。
He started running vigorously.
勢(いきお)いに乗(の)る。
To ride the momentum.
Common expression
勢(いきお)い余(あま)って転(ころ)んだ。
I fell over from the momentum.

Notes

勢(いきお)い expresses force, vigor, or momentum.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 勢(いきお)いがある (to have vigor)
  • 勢(いきお)いに乗(の)る (to ride the momentum)
  • 勢(いきお)いを増(ま)す (to gain momentum)
  • 勢(いきお)い余(あま)って (carried away by momentum)
  • 勢(いきお)いよく (vigorously)

ADVERBIAL USE:

  • 勢(いきお)い〜する: naturally, inevitably do ~
  • Example: 勢(いきお)い時間(じかん)がかかる (it naturally takes time)
