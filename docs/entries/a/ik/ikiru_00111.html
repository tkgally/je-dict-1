生(い)きる

いきる
verb (ichidan)
to live, to be alive

Definitions

1. to live, to be alive
To exist in a living state, as opposed to being dead. The fundamental sense of having life.
2. to make a living, to survive
To sustain one's existence, often with an implication of how one lives or supports oneself.

Examples

祖父(そふ)はまだ生(い)きています。
My grandfather is still alive.
生(い)きている = is alive (state)
一人(ひとり)で生(い)きていくのは大変(たいへん)です。
Living alone is hard.
生(い)きていく = to go on living
今(いま)を生(い)きる。
Live in the present.
A philosophical expression about mindful living

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No direct transitive pair.

Related: 生(い)かす (to make use of, to keep alive—different nuance). Pattern: Xが生(い)きる (X lives).

ASPECT: ている is crucial for this verb. 生(い)きている = 'is alive' (state), NOT 'is in the process of living.' This is a stative ている—it describes a continuing state of being alive.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 長(なが)く生(い)きる (live long)
  • 一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)生(い)きる (live with all one's might)
  • 今(いま)を生(い)きる (live in the moment)
  • 生(い)きていく (to go on living, to survive)
  • 生(い)き残(のこ)る (to survive—compound verb)

RELATED VERBS:

  • 住(す)む: to reside (focus on location)
  • 暮(く)らす: to make a living (focus on daily life)
  • 生(い)きる: to be alive (focus on existence itself)
