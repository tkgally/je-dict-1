後(うし)ろ

うしろ
noun
behind, back

Definitions

1. behind, back, rear
The position at the back of someone or something; the opposite of the front.

Examples

後(うし)ろを見(み)てください。
Please look behind you.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の後(うし)ろにいます。
He is behind me.
後(うし)ろの席(せき)に座(すわ)ります。
I'll sit in the back seat.

Notes

後ろ indicates position at the back or rear of something. The opposite is 前 (mae, front). Common pattern: 〜の後ろに (behind ~).

Note: Different from 後 (ato), which means 'after' in time.
