薄曇(うすぐも)り

うすぐもり
noun, verb (suru)
slightly overcast, thin clouds

Definitions

1. slightly overcast
Weather condition where the sky is partially covered by thin, light clouds.

Examples

今日(きょう)は薄曇(うすぐも)りだ。
It's slightly overcast today.
薄曇(うすぐも)りの空(そら)から日(ひ)が差(さ)した。
The sun came through the thin clouds.
天気予報(てんきよほう)は薄曇(うすぐも)りのち晴(は)れ。
The weather forecast is light clouds followed by clear skies.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
Formed from 薄(うす)い (thin) + 曇(くも)り (cloudy). Describes a sky with thin, light cloud coverage where some sun can still come through.

CONTRAST:

  • 曇(くも)り: overcast, cloudy (more complete coverage)
