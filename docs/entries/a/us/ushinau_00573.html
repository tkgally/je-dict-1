失(うしな)う

うしなう
verb (godan)
to lose

Definitions

1. to lose
To no longer have something that one previously had.

Examples

自信(じしん)を失(うしな)う。
To lose confidence.
事故(じこ)で命(いのち)を失(うしな)った。
Lost their life in an accident.
意識(いしき)を失(うしな)う。
To lose consciousness.

Notes

失(うしな)う means to lose something (abstract or concrete).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自信(じしん)を失(うしな)う: to lose confidence
  • 意識(いしき)を失(うしな)う: to lose consciousness
  • 命(いのち)を失(うしな)う: to lose one's life

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 失(うしな)う: to lose (abstract, permanent)
  • なくす: to lose (concrete objects)
