薄(うす)い

うすい
adjective (i-adjective)
thin, light, weak

Definitions

1. thin (not thick)
Describes something with little thickness. The opposite of 厚(あつ)い (thick). Used for paper, slices, walls, etc.
2. light, pale (color)
Describes colors that are not vivid or intense. The opposite of 濃(こ)い (dark, deep).
3. weak, diluted (taste/concentration)
Describes flavors or liquids that lack strength or intensity.

Examples

薄(うす)い紙(かみ)を使(つか)ってください。
Please use thin paper.
このコーヒーは薄(うす)いですね。
This coffee is weak, isn't it?
薄(うす)い青(あお)い色(いろ)が好(す)きです。
I like light blue colors.

Notes

Has multiple related meanings all involving lack of intensity or thickness. The opposite depends on context: 厚(あつ)い (thick) for physical thickness, 濃(こ)い (dark/strong) for colors and flavors.
