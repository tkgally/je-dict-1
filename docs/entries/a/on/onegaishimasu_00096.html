お願(ねが)いします

おねがいします
expression
please

Definitions

1. please (when requesting)
A polite expression used when making a request or asking someone to do something.
2. I'll have... / I'd like...
Used when ordering at a restaurant or shop.

Examples

コーヒーをお願(ねが)いします。
Coffee, please.
Ordering
手伝(てつだ)いをお願(ねが)いします。
Please help me.
Requesting help
よろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Please treat me well. / Thank you in advance.
Very common set phrase

Notes

お願(ねが)いします is one of the most essential polite expressions. It literally means 'I make a request.' The casual form is お願(ねが)い. よろしくお願(ねが)いします is extremely common when meeting someone, starting a project, or asking for continued cooperation.
