負(お)う

おう
verb (godan)
to bear, to owe, to sustain

Definitions

1. to bear, to carry on one's back
To carry something on the back.
2. to bear (responsibility), to owe
To have responsibility or debt.
3. to sustain (injury)
To receive an injury.

Examples

責任(せきにん)を負(お)う。
To bear responsibility.
怪我(けが)を負(お)った。
I sustained an injury.
借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)っている。
I'm in debt.

Notes

負(お)う means to bear or owe.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を負(お)う: to bear responsibility
  • 怪我(けが)を負(お)う: to sustain an injury
  • 借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)う: to owe money

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 負(お)う: to bear, owe
  • 追(お)う: to chase (different kanji)
