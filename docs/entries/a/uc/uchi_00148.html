内(うち)

うち
noun
inside; within; among; while

Definitions

1. inside, within
The interior part of something.
2. among, of (a group)
Within a specified group or set.
3. while, during
Within a time period; before something changes.
4. one's home, one's group
Refers to one's own home, family, or organization.

Examples

三人(さんにん)の内(うち)、一人(ひとり)が女性(じょせい)だ。
Of the three, one is a woman.
Among meaning
若(わか)い内(うち)に旅行(りょこう)したい。
I want to travel while I'm young.
While meaning
内(うち)の会社(かいしゃ)では...
At our company...
In-group reference
知(し)らない内(うち)に寝(ね)ていた。
I fell asleep without realizing it.
〜ない内(うち)に pattern

Notes

内(うち) has multiple meanings related to 'inside' or 'within.'

Grammar patterns:

  • 〜の内(うち): among ~, of ~
  • 〜内(うち)に: while ~, before ~ (changes)
  • 〜ない内(うち)に: before ~ happens

In-group usage:

  • 内(うち): my home, our family, our company (humble)
  • 内(うち)の + [person/thing]: my/our ~

Contrast:

  • 内(うち) (inside, in-group) vs. 外(そと) (outside, out-group)
  • 内(うち) can be humble; お宅(おたく) is the polite equivalent for others' homes

Note: The sense of 'inside' is abstract - for physical interiors, 中(なか) is more common.
