円(えん)

えん
noun
yen; circle

Definitions

1. yen (Japanese currency)
The official currency of Japan. Symbol: ¥.
2. circle
A round shape. The kanji literally means 'round/circle'.

Examples

これは百(ひゃく)円(えん)です。
This is 100 yen.
五千(ごせん)円(えん)で足(た)りますか。
Is 5000 yen enough?
円(えん)を描(か)いてください。
Please draw a circle.

Notes

The yen is Japan's currency since 1871. Coins: 1円, 5円, 10円, 50円, 100円, 500円. Bills: 1000円, 5000円, 10000円. The 5円 coin is considered lucky (ご縁(えん) = connection/fate). 円高(えんだか) means 'strong yen', 円安(えんやす) means 'weak yen'.
