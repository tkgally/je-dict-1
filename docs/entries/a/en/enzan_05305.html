演算(えんざん)

えんざん
noun / suru-verb
operation, computation

Definitions

1. operation, computation, calculation
A mathematical or logical operation performed by a computer or in mathematics, such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division.

Examples

コンピュータが演算(えんざん)を実行(じっこう)する。
The computer performs the operation.
四則(しそく)演算(えんざん)は基本(きほん)だ。
The four basic arithmetic operations are fundamental.
論理(ろんり)演算(えんざん)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn logical operations.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 演算(えんざん)する (to compute, to operate)
  • 四則(しそく)演算(えんざん) (four arithmetic operations: +, -, ×, ÷)
  • 論理(ろんり)演算(えんざん) (logical operation)
  • 演算子(えんざんし) (operator)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 計算(けいさん) (calculation)
  • 処理(しょり) (processing)
  • 算術(さんじゅつ) (arithmetic)
