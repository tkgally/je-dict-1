演説(えんぜつ)

えんぜつ
noun/suru verb
speech, address, oration

Definitions

1. speech, address
A formal talk given to an audience.
2. oration, lecture
A public speech, especially on a formal occasion.

Examples

演説(えんぜつ)を行(おこな)う。
To give a speech.
選挙(せんきょ)演説(えんぜつ)を聞(き)いた。
I listened to an election speech.
街頭(がいとう)演説(えんぜつ)をする。
To give a street speech.

Notes

演説(えんぜつ) refers to formal speeches.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 演説(えんぜつ)する (to give a speech)
  • 演説(えんぜつ)を行(おこな)う (to deliver a speech)
  • 演説(えんぜつ)を聞(き)く (to listen to a speech)

COMMON TYPES:

  • 選挙(せんきょ)演説(えんぜつ): election speech
  • 街頭(がいとう)演説(えんぜつ): street speech
  • 就任(しゅうにん)演説(えんぜつ): inaugural address

SIMILAR:

  • スピーチ: speech (loanword, less formal)
