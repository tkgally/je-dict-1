援助(えんじょ)

えんじょ
noun/suru verb
assistance, aid, support

Definitions

1. assistance, aid
Help or support given to someone in need.
2. financial aid, funding
Monetary support or grants.

Examples

経済(けいざい)援助(えんじょ)を行(おこな)う。
To provide economic aid.
被災地(ひさいち)への援助(えんじょ)。
Aid to the disaster area.
開発(かいはつ)援助(えんじょ)を受(う)ける。
To receive development aid.

Notes

援助(えんじょ) means assistance or aid.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 援助(えんじょ)する (to assist, to aid)
  • 援助(えんじょ)を受(う)ける (to receive aid)
  • 援助(えんじょ)を求(もと)める (to seek aid)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 経済(けいざい)援助(えんじょ): economic aid
  • 軍事(ぐんじ)援助(えんじょ): military aid
  • 人道(じんどう)援助(えんじょ): humanitarian aid
  • 開発(かいはつ)援助(えんじょ): development aid
