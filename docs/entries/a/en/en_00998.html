縁(えん)

えん
noun
fate, connection, bond

Definitions

1. fate, destiny
A connection or relationship determined by fate.
2. connection, bond
A tie or relationship between people.

Examples

縁(えん)があって出会(であ)った。
We met by fate.
縁(えん)を切(き)る。
To sever ties.

Notes

縁(えん) refers to fateful connections.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 縁(えん)がある: to be fated/connected
  • 縁(えん)がない: to have no connection
  • 縁(えん)を結(むす)ぶ: to form a bond

COMPOUNDS:

  • 縁談(えんだん): marriage proposal
  • 縁起(えんぎ): omen, luck
  • 無縁(むえん): unrelated

NOTE:
Also read as 縁(ふち) meaning edge or rim.
