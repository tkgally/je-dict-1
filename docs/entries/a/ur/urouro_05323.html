うろうろ

うろうろ
adverb
wandering about; loitering; pacing restlessly

Definitions

1. wandering about; loitering
Describes moving around restlessly without purpose or direction, often implying confusion or suspicion.

Examples

道(みち)に迷(まよ)ってうろうろした。
I got lost and wandered around.
不審(ふしん)者(しゃ)が家(いえ)の前(まえ)をうろうろしている。
A suspicious person is loitering in front of the house.
何(なに)をうろうろしているの？
What are you wandering around for?

Notes

うろうろ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) expressing restless, purposeless movement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • うろうろする: to wander about, pace around
  • うろうろ歩(ある)く: to walk around aimlessly

NUANCE: Can imply confusion, nervousness, or suspicious behavior. More restless than ぶらぶら.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぶらぶら: wandering aimlessly (more relaxed)
  • おろおろ: flustered, bewildered (emotional confusion)
