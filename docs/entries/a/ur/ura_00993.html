裏(うら)

うら
noun
back, reverse, behind

Definitions

1. back, reverse side
The opposite side from the front.
2. behind the scenes
What is hidden or not openly shown.

Examples

紙(かみ)の裏(うら)に書(か)く。
To write on the back of the paper.
裏(うら)から入(はい)る。
To enter from the back.

Notes

裏(うら) means back or reverse.

CONTRAST:

  • 表(おもて): front, surface
  • 裏(うら): back, reverse

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 裏(うら)を返(かえ)せば: conversely
  • 裏(うら)をかく: to outsmart
  • 裏目(うらめ)に出(で)る: to backfire

COMPOUNDS:

  • 裏切(うらぎ)る: to betray
  • 裏口(うらぐち): back door
