うるさい

うるさい
adjective (i-adjective)
noisy, annoying

Definitions

1. noisy, loud
Describes something that makes too much noise or is unpleasantly loud. Can refer to sounds, people, or environments.
2. annoying, bothersome
Can describe something or someone that is pestering, nagging, or being a nuisance. Often used to tell someone to be quiet or stop bothering you.

Examples

この部屋(へや)はうるさいですね。
This room is noisy, isn't it?
うるさい！静(しず)かにして！
Be quiet! Shut up!
Direct/rude way to tell someone to be quiet
隣(となり)の犬(いぬ)がうるさくて眠(ねむ)れない。
The neighbor's dog is so noisy I can't sleep.

Notes

This word is usually written in hiragana. When used as an exclamation (うるさい！), it's a fairly rude way to tell someone to be quiet—similar to 'Shut up!' in English. The kanji form 五月蠅(うるさ)い exists but is rarely used. The opposite is 静(しず)か (quiet).
