売上(うりあげ)

うりあげ
noun
sales, proceeds, turnover

Definitions

1. sales, amount sold, proceeds
The total amount of money received from selling goods or services.

Examples

今月(こんげつ)の売上(うりあげ)は先月(せんげつ)より増(ふ)えた。
This month's sales increased compared to last month.
売上(うりあげ)目標(もくひょう)を達成(たっせい)した。
We achieved our sales target.
この店(みせ)の一日(いちにち)の売上(うりあげ)は約(やく)百万円(ひゃくまんえん)だ。
This store's daily sales are about one million yen.

Notes

USAGE: 売上(うりあげ) is a fundamental business term used for revenue from sales.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 売上(うりあげ)高(だか) (sales amount, sales volume)
  • 売上(うりあげ)金(きん) (sales proceeds)
  • 売上(うりあげ)目標(もくひょう) (sales target)
  • 売上(うりあげ)報告(ほうこく) (sales report)
  • 売上(うりあげ)ランキング (sales ranking)

WRITING VARIATIONS: Can also be written as 売り上げ(うりあげ).

RELATED:

  • 収益(しゅうえき) - revenue, earnings
  • 利益(りえき) - profit (after costs)
  • 売れ行き(うれゆき) - sales performance, how well something sells
