嬉(うれ)しい

うれしい
adjective (i-adjective)
happy, glad

Definitions

1. happy, glad, pleased
Feeling or showing pleasure or contentment. Often used when something good happens to you.

Examples

プレゼントをもらって嬉(うれ)しいです。
I'm happy to receive a present.
会(あ)えて嬉(うれ)しいです。
I'm glad to meet you.
Common expression when meeting someone
合格(ごうかく)して嬉(うれ)しいです。
I'm happy that I passed.

Notes

嬉(うれ)しい describes personal happiness from something that happens to you, while 楽(たの)しい describes enjoying an activity. 嬉(うれ)し泣(な)き means 'tears of joy'. In casual speech, うれしい！ is often used as an exclamation.
