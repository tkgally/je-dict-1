羨(うらや)む

うらやむ
verb (godan)
to envy, to be jealous of

Definitions

1. to envy, to be jealous of
To wish you had something that someone else has.

Examples

彼(かれ)の才能(さいのう)を羨(うらや)む。
To envy his talent.
羨(うらや)ましい。
I'm jealous. / How envious.
人(ひと)を羨(うらや)んでも仕方(しかた)がない。
It's no use envying others.

Notes

羨(うらや)む means to envy.

RELATED:

  • 羨(うらや)ましい: envious, jealous (adjective)
  • 嫉妬(しっと): jealousy (stronger)

USAGE:

  • Often used in the form 羨(うらや)ましい (I'm jealous)
