占(うらな)う

うらなう
verb (godan)
to tell fortunes, to divine

Definitions

1. to tell fortunes, to divine
To predict the future using various methods.

Examples

運勢(うんせい)を占(うらな)う。
To tell someone's fortune.
手相(てそう)で占(うらな)う。
To tell fortunes by reading palms.
将来(しょうらい)を占(うらな)ってもらった。
I had my future told.

Notes

占(うらな)う means to tell fortunes or divine.

RELATED:

  • 占(うらな)い: fortune-telling
  • 占(うらな)い師(し): fortune-teller
  • 手相(てそう): palm reading
  • 星占(ほしうらな)い: astrology
