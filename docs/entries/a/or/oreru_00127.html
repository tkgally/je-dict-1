折(お)れる

おれる
verb (ichidan)
to break, to give in

Definitions

1. to break, to snap
For something to break by itself or as a result of pressure. The intransitive form—the object breaks on its own.
2. to be folded, to bend
To become bent or folded.
3. to give in, to yield
To compromise or yield in an argument or negotiation.
4. to turn (a corner)
To change direction, especially at a corner or intersection.

Examples

強(つよ)い風(かぜ)で木(き)の枝(えだ)が折(お)れた。
The tree branch broke in the strong wind.
Intransitive—the branch broke by itself
彼(かれ)は最後(さいご)に折(お)れた。
He finally gave in.
'Yield' meaning
次(つぎ)の角(かど)を右(みぎ)に折(お)れてください。
Please turn right at the next corner.
Direction meaning—can also use 曲(ま)がる

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 折(お)る (transitive, to break/fold). Pattern: Xが折(お)れる (X breaks).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 折(お)れている = 'is broken' (state).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 枝(えだ)が折(お)れる (branch breaks)
  • 骨(ほね)が折(お)れる (bone breaks)
  • 心(こころ)が折(お)れる (to lose heart, give up)
  • 右(みぎ)に折(お)れる (turn right)
  • 交渉(こうしょう)で折(お)れる (yield in negotiations)

NUANCE: When giving directions, 折(お)れる emphasizes making a turn at an angle, while 曲(ま)がる is more general for 'turn.'
