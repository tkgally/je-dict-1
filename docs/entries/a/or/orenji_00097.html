オレンジ

おれんじ
noun
orange

Definitions

1. orange (fruit)
A round citrus fruit with orange skin. From English 'orange.'
2. orange (color)
The color orange, between red and yellow.

Examples

オレンジジュースを飲(の)みたいです。
I want to drink orange juice.
オレンジを二個(にこ)買(か)いました。
I bought two oranges.
オレンジ色(いろ)のシャツを着(き)ています。
I'm wearing an orange shirt.
As a color

Notes

オレンジ can refer to both the fruit and the color. For the fruit, the native Japanese みかん (mandarin orange) is more common in daily life. オレンジジュース (orange juice) is a common drink.
