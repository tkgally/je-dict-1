赤字(あかじ)

あかじ
noun
deficit

Definitions

1. deficit, in the red, loss
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

今年(ことし)は赤字(あかじ)になった。
We went into the red this year.
赤字(あかじ)を減(へ)らす必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reduce the deficit.
家計(かけい)が赤字(あかじ)だ。
The household budget is in the red.

Notes

赤字(あかじ) literally means "red characters" because losses were traditionally written in red ink.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 赤字(あかじ)になる (to go into the red)
  • 赤字(あかじ)が出(で)る (to incur a loss)
  • 赤字(あかじ)を出(だ)す (to run a deficit)
  • 赤字(あかじ)経営(けいえい) (operating at a loss)
  • 赤字(あかじ)決算(けっさん) (deficit settlement)
  • 財政(ざいせい)赤字(あかじ) (fiscal deficit)

CONTEXTS:

  • 企業(きぎょう)の赤字(あかじ) (corporate loss)
  • 国(くに)の財政(ざいせい)赤字(あかじ) (national fiscal deficit)
  • 家計(かけい)の赤字(あかじ) (household budget deficit)
  • 貿易(ぼうえき)赤字(あかじ) (trade deficit)

ANTONYM:

  • 黒字(くろじ) (surplus, in the black)

Related Words

Antonym: 黒字(くろじ) (surplus)
