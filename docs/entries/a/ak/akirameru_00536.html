諦(あきら)める

あきらめる
verb (ichidan)
to give up

Definitions

1. to give up, to abandon
To stop trying or hoping for something; to accept that something cannot be achieved.

Examples

夢(ゆめ)を諦(あきら)めないで。
Don't give up on your dream.
もう諦(あきら)めました。
I've already given up.
簡単(かんたん)に諦(あきら)めてはいけない。
You shouldn't give up easily.

Notes

諦(あきら)める means to give up or resign oneself.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を諦(あきら)める: to give up on ~
  • 諦(あきら)めずに: without giving up
  • 諦(あきら)めが悪(わる)い: reluctant to give up

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • やめる: to quit, stop
  • 断念(だんねん)する: to give up (formal)
  • 手を引く: to withdraw

Note: Ichidan verb (る-verb).
