お母(かあ)さん

おかあさん
noun
mother (polite)

Definitions

1. mother (polite/formal)
A polite way to refer to someone's mother, including one's own when speaking to others.

Examples

お母(かあ)さん、おはよう。
Good morning, Mom.
お母(かあ)さんは元気(げんき)ですか。
How is your mother?
お母(かあ)さんに聞(き)いてみる。
I'll ask my mother.

Notes

お母(かあ)さん is the polite term for mother.

FAMILY TERMS:

  • お母(かあ)さん: mother (polite, when addressing or referring to someone else's mother)
  • 母(はは): my mother (humble, when referring to one's own mother to others)
  • ママ: mom (casual)

USAGE:

  • Used to address one's own mother
  • Used to refer to someone else's mother
