沖(おき)

おき
noun
open sea, offshore

Definitions

1. open sea, offshore
The area of the sea away from the shore.

Examples

船(ふね)が沖(おき)に出(で)た。
The ship went out to sea.
沖(おき)に島(しま)が見(み)える。
An island is visible offshore.

Notes

沖(おき) means the open sea.

USAGE:

  • 沖(おき)に出(で)る: to go out to sea
  • 沖合(おきあい): offshore area

NOTE:
沖縄(おきなわ) (Okinawa) uses the same kanji but has a different reading.
