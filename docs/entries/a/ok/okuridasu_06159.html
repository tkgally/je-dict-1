送(おく)り出(だ)す

おくりだす
verb (godan)
to send off, to see off

Definitions

1. to send off, to see off
To accompany someone who is leaving and bid them farewell, or to dispatch someone to a destination.
2. to produce, to turn out
To train and release into the world, typically referring to graduates, athletes, or professionals.

Examples

駅(えき)まで友達(ともだち)を送(おく)り出(だ)した。
I saw my friend off at the station.
空港(くうこう)で家族(かぞく)を笑顔(えがお)で送(おく)り出(だ)す。
To send off the family with smiles at the airport.
この大学(だいがく)は多(おお)くの優秀(ゆうしゅう)な人材(じんざい)を世(よ)に送(おく)り出(だ)してきた。
This university has produced many excellent talents for the world.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE: 送(おく)る (to send) + 出(だ)す (to put out). The ～出(だ)す suffix adds the meaning of dispatching outward.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 駅(えき)まで送(おく)り出(だ)す (see off at the station)
  • 笑顔(えがお)で送(おく)り出(だ)す (send off with a smile)
  • 世(よ)に送(おく)り出(だ)す (produce for the world)
  • 卒業生(そつぎょうせい)を送(おく)り出(だ)す (send off graduates)

ASPECT: ～ている indicates a habitual action or ongoing process of producing.

Related Words

Related: 送(おく)る (base verb)
