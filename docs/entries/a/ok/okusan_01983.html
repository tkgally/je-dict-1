奥(おく)さん

おくさん
noun
wife (someone else's)

Definitions

1. wife (of someone else)
A polite term to refer to another person's wife.

Examples

奥(おく)さんは元気(げんき)ですか。
How is your wife?
田中(たなか)さんの奥(おく)さん
Mr. Tanaka's wife.
奥(おく)さんによろしくお伝(つた)えください。
Please give my regards to your wife.

Notes

奥(おく)さん is used to refer to someone else's wife.

WIFE TERMS:

  • 奥(おく)さん: (someone else's) wife (polite)
  • 妻(つま): my wife (humble)
  • 家内(かない): my wife (humble, traditional)
  • かみさん: wife (casual)

NOTE: Don't use 奥(おく)さん for your own wife when speaking to others.
