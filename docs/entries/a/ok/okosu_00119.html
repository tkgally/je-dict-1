起(お)こす

おこす
verb (godan)
to wake (someone) up, to cause

Definitions

1. to wake (someone) up
To rouse someone from sleep. The transitive counterpart to 起(お)きる (to wake up oneself).
2. to raise, to help up
To help someone or something stand up from a lying or fallen position.
3. to cause, to bring about
To cause something to happen, often used for events, problems, or incidents.

Examples

朝(あさ)6時(じ)に起(お)こしてください。
Please wake me up at 6 in the morning.
起(お)こして is the te-form used for requests
子供(こども)を起(お)こさないように静(しず)かにしてください。
Please be quiet so as not to wake the children.
起(お)こさないように = so as not to wake
事故(じこ)を起(お)こしてしまった。
I caused an accident.
'Cause' meaning—often used with negative events

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 起(お)きる (intransitive, to wake up/get up). Pattern: Xを起(お)こす (wake X up / cause X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state or ongoing action. 起(お)こしている = 'has woken (someone) up' or 'is in the process of waking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)を起(お)こす (wake someone up)
  • 事故(じこ)を起(お)こす (cause an accident)
  • 問題(もんだい)を起(お)こす (cause a problem)
  • 奇跡(きせき)を起(お)こす (bring about a miracle)
  • 行動(こうどう)を起(お)こす (take action)

NUANCE: For 'wake up,' this verb focuses on the person doing the waking. 起(お)こす someone ≠ 起(お)きる (oneself). The pair is important:

  • 母(はは)が私(わたし)を起(お)こした (Mom woke me up)
  • 私(わたし)は6時(じ)に起(お)きた (I woke up at 6)

REGISTER: Neutral.
