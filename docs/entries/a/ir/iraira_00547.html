いらいら

いらいら
adverb/suru verb/noun
irritation, frustration, restlessness

Definitions

1. irritation, frustration
A feeling of annoyance or impatience.
2. restlessness, edginess
A nervous or agitated state.

Examples

いらいらする。
To feel irritated.
待(ま)たされていらいらした。
I got frustrated from being made to wait.
いらいらを抑(おさ)える。
To suppress one's irritation.

Notes

いらいら is an onomatopoeia expressing irritation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いらいらする (to be irritated)
  • いらいらしている (to be in an irritated state)
  • いらいらを感(かん)じる (to feel irritation)
  • いらいらさせる (to irritate someone)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いらいら: irritated, frustrated (common)
  • むかむか: feeling nauseated; angry
  • もやもや: feeling unsettled, uneasy
  • イライラ: also written in katakana
