医療(いりょう)

いりょう
noun
medical care, medical treatment

Definitions

1. medical care, medical treatment
The treatment of illness or injury by medical professionals.
2. healthcare, medicine
The field or system of medical services.

Examples

医療(いりょう)費(ひ)が高(たか)い。
Medical costs are high.
適切(てきせつ)な医療(いりょう)を受(う)ける。
To receive appropriate medical care.
医療(いりょう)従事者(じゅうじしゃ)への感謝(かんしゃ)。
Gratitude to healthcare workers.

Notes

医療(いりょう) refers to medical care and healthcare.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 医療(いりょう)費(ひ): medical expenses
  • 医療(いりょう)機関(きかん): medical institution
  • 医療(いりょう)保険(ほけん): health insurance
  • 医療(いりょう)従事者(じゅうじしゃ): healthcare worker
  • 救急(きゅうきゅう)医療(いりょう): emergency medicine
  • 先端(せんたん)医療(いりょう): advanced medicine

NOTE:

  • Different from 衣料(いりょう) (clothing) - same reading, different kanji
