渦(うず)

うず
noun
whirlpool, vortex, swirl

Definitions

1. whirlpool, vortex
A spiral movement of water or air.
2. swirl, spiral
A spiral pattern or movement.
3. turmoil, midst
Figuratively, being caught up in something.

Examples

渦(うず)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれた。
I got caught in a whirlpool.
戦争(せんそう)の渦(うず)の中(なか)にいた。
I was in the midst of war.
Figurative usage
渦(うず)巻(ま)き模様(もよう)。
A spiral pattern.

Notes

渦(うず) means whirlpool or spiral.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 渦(うず)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる (get caught in a vortex)
  • 渦(うず)の中(なか) (in the midst of)
  • 渦(うず)巻(ま)き (spiral)
  • 渦(うず)巻(ま)く (to swirl)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 渦巻(うずま)き: spiral
  • 渦潮(うずしお): whirlpool, tidal vortex
