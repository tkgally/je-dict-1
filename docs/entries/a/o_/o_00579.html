尾(お)

お
noun
tail

Definitions

1. tail
The rear appendage of an animal.
2. end, trail
The final or trailing part of something.

Examples

犬(いぬ)が尾(お)を振(ふ)っている。
The dog is wagging its tail.
尾(お)を引(ひ)く。
To leave a trail; to have lingering effects.
Idiom
頭(あたま)から尾(お)まで。
From head to tail.

Notes

尾(お) means tail.

READINGS:

  • お: standalone and some compounds
  • び: in compounds (e.g., 尾行(びこう))

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 尾(お)を振(ふ)る (to wag the tail)
  • 尾(お)を引(ひ)く (to leave a trail, have lingering effects)
  • 尾(お)ひれを付(つ)ける (to embellish a story)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 尾行(びこう): tailing, following someone
  • 末尾(まつび): end, conclusion
