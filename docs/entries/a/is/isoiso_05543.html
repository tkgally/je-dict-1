いそいそ

いそいそ
adverb
cheerfully; eagerly; excitedly

Definitions

1. cheerfully; eagerly; excitedly
Acting with visible joy and anticipation, often when preparing for something pleasant.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)はいそいそと出(で)かけた。
She went out cheerfully.
いそいそと準備(じゅんび)する。
To prepare eagerly.
デートの前(まえ)にいそいそしている。
Being excitedly busy before a date.

Notes

いそいそ describes visible happiness and eagerness, often when going somewhere pleasant or preparing for something enjoyable.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うきうき: cheerful, in high spirits
  • わくわく: excited, thrilled

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いそいそと出かける: to go out cheerfully
  • いそいそと準備する: to prepare eagerly
