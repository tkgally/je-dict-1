一斉(いっせい)

いっせい
adverb/no-adjective
all at once, simultaneously

Definitions

1. all at once, simultaneously
Many things or people doing something at the same time together.

Examples

一斉(いっせい)に立(た)ち上(あ)がった。
Everyone stood up at once.
一斉(いっせい)検査(けんさ)を行(おこな)います。
We will conduct a simultaneous inspection.
No-adjective usage
花(はな)が一斉(いっせい)に咲(さ)いた。
The flowers all bloomed at once.

Notes

一斉(いっせい) indicates simultaneous action by many.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 一斉(いっせい)に (all at once)
  • No-adjective: 一斉(いっせい)の + noun / noun + 一斉(いっせい)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一斉(いっせい)に + verb (do simultaneously)
  • 一斉(いっせい)検査(けんさ) (simultaneous inspection)
  • 一斉(いっせい)攻撃(こうげき) (simultaneous attack)
  • 一斉(いっせい)送信(そうしん) (mass transmission/broadcast)
