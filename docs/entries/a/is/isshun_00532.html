一瞬(いっしゅん)

いっしゅん
noun/adverb
instant, moment, split second

Definitions

1. instant, moment
An extremely brief period of time.
2. for an instant, momentarily
Used adverbially to describe something happening very briefly.

Examples

一瞬(いっしゅん)の出来事(できごと)だった。
It was a momentary event.
一瞬(いっしゅん)目(め)を離(はな)した隙(すき)に。
In the instant I looked away.
一瞬(いっしゅん)、何(なに)が起(お)きたか分(わ)からなかった。
For an instant, I didn't understand what happened.

Notes

一瞬(いっしゅん) refers to a very brief moment.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一瞬(いっしゅん)の + noun (a momentary ~)
  • 一瞬(いっしゅん)で (in an instant)
  • 一瞬(いっしゅん)にして (in a flash)
  • ほんの一瞬(いっしゅん) (just a moment)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 瞬間(しゅんかん): moment, instant
  • 一瞬(いっしゅん): instant (emphasizes brevity)
  • 一刻(いっこく): a moment (literary)
