磯(いそ)

いそ
noun
rocky shore; seashore rocks

Definitions

1. rocky shore; seashore with rocks
A rocky coastline area, typically exposed at low tide.

Examples

磯(いそ)で貝(かい)を拾(ひろ)った。
I picked up shells at the rocky shore.
磯(いそ)の香(かお)りがする。
There's a smell of the seashore.
磯の香り = ocean/seaweed scent
磯(いそ)遊(あそ)びを楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy playing at the rocky shore.
磯遊び = exploring tide pools

Notes

磯 specifically refers to rocky coastal areas, distinct from sandy beaches (砂浜, すなはま).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 磯の香り: scent of the sea/seaweed
  • 磯遊び: playing at tide pools, exploring rocky shores
  • 磯釣り (いそづり): rock fishing

RELATED WORDS:

  • 海岸 (かいがん): coast, seashore (general)
  • 砂浜 (すなはま): sandy beach
