エコ

えこ
noun
eco; eco-friendly; ecological

Definitions

1. eco-friendly; ecological; environmentally friendly
Related to environmental conservation or products that are good for the environment.

Examples

エコな生活(せいかつ)を心(こころ)がけている。
I try to live an eco-friendly lifestyle.
エコバッグを持参(じさん)する。
I bring my own eco bag.
エコカーに乗(の)り換(か)えた。
I switched to an eco-friendly car.

Notes

エコ is short for エコロジー (ecology) and is used as a prefix or adjective.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • エコバッグ: reusable shopping bag
  • エコカー: eco-friendly car (hybrid/electric)
  • エコ活動 (えこかつどう): eco activities
  • エコ商品: eco-friendly products

RELATED:

  • 環境にやさしい: environmentally friendly
