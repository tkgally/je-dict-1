帯(おび)

おび
noun
sash, belt, obi

Definitions

1. obi, sash (for kimono)
A wide sash worn around the waist to secure a kimono.
2. band, belt, strip
A strip or band wrapped around something.

Examples

着物(きもの)に帯(おび)を締(し)める。
To tie an obi around a kimono.
母(はは)の帯(おび)を借(か)りた。
I borrowed my mother's obi.
本(ほん)に帯(おび)が付(つ)いている。
The book has a promotional band.
Sense 2: paper band around books

Notes

帯(おび) primarily refers to the traditional sash worn with kimono, but also has broader meanings.

KIMONO USAGE:

  • 帯(おび)を締(し)める (to tie an obi)
  • 帯(おび)を結(むす)ぶ (to tie/knot an obi)
  • 帯(おび)締(じ)め (obi fastener)

TYPES OF OBI:

  • 袋帯(ふくろおび) (formal double-folded obi)
  • 名古屋帯(なごやおび) (Nagoya obi - semi-formal)
  • 半幅帯(はんはばおび) (half-width obi - casual)

OTHER MEANINGS:

  • 本(ほん)の帯(おび) (book band - promotional strip)
  • 周波数帯(しゅうはすうたい) (frequency band)

COUNTER:

  • 本(ほん) for long items

Related Words

Related: 着物(きもの) (kimono)
