グランド

グランド
noun
sports ground, athletic field

Definitions

1. sports ground, athletic field
An outdoor area for sports activities, particularly baseball or track and field
2. grand (in compounds)
Used in compound words to mean 'grand' or 'large-scale'

Examples

学校(がっこう)のグランドで野球(やきゅう)の練習(れんしゅう)をした。
We practiced baseball on the school grounds.
雨(あめ)でグランドの状態(じょうたい)が悪(わる)い。
The field conditions are poor due to rain.
グランドピアノを買(か)うのが夢(ゆめ)だ。
My dream is to buy a grand piano.

Notes

グランド has two main usages in Japanese.

SPORTS FIELD:
Short for グラウンド (ground), referring to outdoor athletic fields. Common at schools and sports facilities.

Common expressions:

  • グランドを整備(せいび)する: to maintain the field
  • グランドの状態(じょうたい): field conditions

GRAND (large-scale):
Used in compound words meaning "grand" or "large-scale."

Common compounds:

  • グランドピアノ: grand piano
  • グランドオープン: grand opening
  • グランドスラム: grand slam

NOTE:
The full form グラウンド is also commonly used for sports fields.
