グラス

グラス
noun
glass (drinking), grass

Definitions

1. drinking glass
A glass container used for drinking, especially for alcoholic beverages
2. grass (in compounds)
Grass, typically used in compound words

Examples

ワイングラスに赤(あか)ワインを注(そそ)いだ。
I poured red wine into the wine glass.
グラスに氷(こおり)を入(い)れてください。
Please put ice in the glass.
サングラスをかけて外出(がいしゅつ)した。
I went out wearing sunglasses.

Notes

グラス has two main meanings from different English words.

DRINKING GLASS:
Used for drinking glasses, especially for wine, cocktails, and other drinks. For everyday drinking glasses, コップ is more common.

COMMON COMPOUNDS (glass):

  • ワイングラス: wine glass
  • カクテルグラス: cocktail glass
  • ビールグラス: beer glass

COMMON COMPOUNDS (grass):

  • サングラス: sunglasses
  • グラスホッパー: grasshopper

VS コップ:

  • グラス: typically for stemmed or elegant glasses
  • コップ: general-purpose drinking cups/glasses

VS ガラス:

  • グラス: drinking vessel or grass
  • ガラス: glass as a material
