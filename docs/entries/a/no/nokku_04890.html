ノック

ノック
noun, suru-verb
knock; fungo (baseball)

Definitions

1. knock (on a door)
The act of rapping on a door to announce one's presence
2. fungo, batting practice (baseball)
Hitting practice balls to fielders during baseball training

Examples

ドアをノックしてから入(はい)ってください。
Please knock on the door before entering.
ノックの音(おと)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard a knocking sound.
監督(かんとく)がノックを打(う)っている。
The manager is hitting fungo balls.
練習(れんしゅう)で1000本(ぼん)ノックを受(う)けた。
I received 1000 fungo hits during practice.

Notes

ノック has two main uses: door knocking and baseball practice.

DOOR KNOCKING:

  • ノックする: to knock
  • ドアをノックする: to knock on the door
  • ノックの音(おと): knocking sound

BASEBALL PRACTICE:
ノック refers to hitting practice balls to fielders (fungo batting).

Common expressions:

  • ノックを打(う)つ: to hit fungo balls
  • ノックを受(う)ける: to receive fielding practice
  • 千本(せんぼん)ノック: 1000 fungo hits (intensive practice)
  • ノックバット: fungo bat

NOTE:
千本(せんぼん)ノック (1000 knock practice) is a famous Japanese training method representing intensive, repetitive practice.
