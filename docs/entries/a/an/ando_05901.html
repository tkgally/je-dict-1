安堵(あんど)

あんど
noun, verb (suru)
relief, reassurance

Definitions

1. relief, reassurance
The feeling of relief when worry or anxiety is removed.

Examples

安堵(あんど)の息(いき)をつく。
To breathe a sigh of relief.
Common expression
無事(ぶじ)を聞(き)いて安堵(あんど)した。
I was relieved to hear they were safe.
安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべる。
To show a relieved expression.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安堵の息をつく: sigh with relief
  • 安堵の表情: relieved expression
  • 安堵感: feeling of relief

REGISTER: Slightly formal; ほっとする is more casual.
