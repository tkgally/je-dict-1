運賃(うんちん)

うんちん
noun
fare; freight charge

Definitions

1. fare
The cost of transportation for a passenger.
2. freight charge; shipping cost
The cost of transporting goods.

Examples

バスの運賃(うんちん)は200円(にひゃくえん)だ。
The bus fare is 200 yen.
運賃(うんちん)を払(はら)う。
To pay the fare.
運賃(うんちん)表(ひょう)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the fare table.

Notes

運賃(うんちん) is composed of 運(うん) (transport) + 賃(ちん) (fee).

PASSENGER FARE TERMS:

  • 運賃(うんちん)表(ひょう): fare chart
  • 片道(かたみち)運賃(うんちん): one-way fare
  • 往復(おうふく)運賃(うんちん): round-trip fare
  • 子供(こども)運賃(うんちん): child fare

FREIGHT MEANING:

  • Used for shipping costs of packages and goods
  • 送料(そうりょう) is more common for online shopping shipping fees

Related Words

Related: 乗車券(じょうしゃけん) (train ticket)
