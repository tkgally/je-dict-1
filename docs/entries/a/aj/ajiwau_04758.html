味(あじ)わう

あじわう
verb (godan)
to taste, to savor

Definitions

1. to taste, to savor
To perceive the flavor of food or drink, often appreciatively.
2. to experience, to go through
To undergo or feel an experience, emotion, or sensation deeply.

Examples

料理(りょうり)をゆっくり味(あじ)わって食(た)べる。
Eat the food slowly and savor it.
このワイン}の{香(かお)りを味(あじ)わってください。
Please savor the aroma of this wine.
成功(せいこう)の喜(よろこ)びを味(あじ)わった。
I experienced the joy of success.
Sense 2

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを味(あじ)わう (to taste/experience X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing appreciation. 味(あじ)わっている = 'is savoring.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 料理(りょうり)を味(あじ)わう (to savor food)
  • 人生(じんせい)を味(あじ)わう (to experience life)
  • 苦(くる)しみを味(あじ)わう (to experience suffering)
  • 喜(よろこ)びを味(あじ)わう (to experience joy)
  • 味(あじ)わい深(ぶか)い (full of flavor/depth)

NUANCE: 味(あじ)わう implies careful, appreciative attention, not just casual tasting or experiencing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(た)べる - to eat (neutral)
  • 試食(ししょく)する - to sample food
  • 経験(けいけん)する - to experience (more neutral)
