エモい

えもい
adjective (i-adjective)
emotional, moving

Definitions

1. emotional, moving, nostalgic
Describing something that evokes strong emotions, often nostalgia or sentimentality. From English 'emo'.

Examples

この曲(きょく)、めっちゃエモい。
This song is so emotional.
夕焼(ゆうや)けがエモい写真(しゃしん)が撮(と)れた。
I took an emotional photo of the sunset.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)はエモかった。
The graduation ceremony was so moving.

Notes

Youth slang derived from 'emotional' or 'emo'. Used to describe things that evoke strong positive emotions, nostalgia, or a sense of aesthetic beauty. Became popular in the late 2010s. Often used for photography, music, and emotional moments.
