哀(あわ)れ

あわれ
na-adjective/noun
pitiful, pathetic, sad

Definitions

1. pitiful, pathetic
Deserving of pity or sympathy due to misfortune.
2. sad, touching, moving
Evoking feelings of sadness or deep emotion.
3. pity, pathos
A feeling of sympathy or sorrow (as a noun).

Examples

哀(あわ)れな姿(すがた)だった。
It was a pitiful sight.
彼(かれ)の哀(あわ)れを誘(さそ)う話(はなし)だ。
It's a story that evokes pity.
哀(あわ)れに思(おも)う。
To feel pity for.

Notes

哀(あわ)れ expresses pity, pathos, or a touching sadness.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 哀(あわ)れな (pitiful)
  • Noun: 哀(あわ)れ (pity, pathos)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 哀(あわ)れに思(おも)う (to feel pity)
  • 哀(あわ)れを誘(さそ)う (to evoke pity)
  • 哀(あわ)れを催(もよお)す (to feel compassion)

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • もののあわれ (物(もの)の哀(あわ)れ): a key concept in Japanese aesthetics meaning "the pathos of things" - sensitivity to the ephemeral nature of beauty
