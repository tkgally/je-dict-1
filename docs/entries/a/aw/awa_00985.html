泡(あわ)

あわ
noun
bubble, foam, froth

Definitions

1. bubble
A thin sphere of liquid enclosing air.
2. foam, froth
A mass of small bubbles on a liquid surface.

Examples

石鹸(せっけん)の泡(あわ)を立(た)てる。
To work up soap lather.
ビールの泡(あわ)が消(き)えた。
The beer foam disappeared.

Notes

泡(あわ) refers to bubbles and foam.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 泡(あわ)を食(く)う: to be flustered
  • 泡(あわ)と消(き)える: to vanish like bubbles
  • 水(みず)の泡(あわ): come to nothing

COMPOUNDS:

  • 泡立(あわだ)てる: to whip, to foam
  • 泡風呂(あわぶろ): bubble bath
