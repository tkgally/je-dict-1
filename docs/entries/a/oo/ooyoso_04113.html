大凡(おおよそ)

おおよそ
adverb, noun
about, approximately, roughly

Definitions

1. about, approximately, roughly
An estimate that is not exact; used when giving approximate numbers or amounts.
2. generally, on the whole
In general terms; for the most part.

Examples

大凡(おおよそ)三十分(さんじゅっぷん)で着(つ)きます。
It takes approximately 30 minutes to arrive.
大凡(おおよそ)の見積(みつも)もりを出(だ)してください。
Please give me a rough estimate.
大凡(おおよそ)の計画(けいかく)は決(き)まった。
The general plan has been decided.

Notes

USAGE: 大凡(おおよそ) is often written in hiragana (おおよそ) in modern usage.

POSITION: Usually appears before numbers or nouns.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 大凡(おおよそ)の見当(けんとう) (rough idea)
  • 大凡(おおよそ)の数(かず) (approximate number)
  • 大凡(おおよそ)想像(そうぞう)がつく (can roughly imagine)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 約(やく)〜 - about, approximately (more common for numbers)
  • だいたい - roughly, generally (more casual)
  • およそ - approximately (similar but can also mean 'at all' in negative contexts)
  • ざっと - roughly, at a glance
