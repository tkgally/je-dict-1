チャイム

チャイム
noun
chime, doorbell

Definitions

1. chime, doorbell
A ringing sound used as a signal, especially for a doorbell or school bell

Examples

チャイムが鳴(な)ったので玄関(げんかん)に行(い)った。
The doorbell rang, so I went to the entrance.
授業(じゅぎょう)終了(しゅうりょう)のチャイムが鳴(な)った。
The end-of-class chime rang.
チャイムを押(お)したが誰(だれ)も出(で)なかった。
I pressed the doorbell, but no one answered.

Notes

チャイム refers to chiming sounds used as signals.

COMMON USES:

  • 玄関(げんかん)のチャイム: doorbell
  • 学校(がっこう)のチャイム: school bell/chime

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チャイムが鳴(な)る: the chime rings
  • チャイムを押(お)す: to press the doorbell
  • チャイムを鳴(な)らす: to ring the bell

VS 呼(よ)び鈴(りん) / ベル:
チャイム usually refers to a melodic or electronic sound, while 呼(よ)び鈴(りん) and ベル can refer to traditional ringing bells.

NOTE:
School chimes in Japan often play the "Westminster Chime" melody at the start and end of class periods.
