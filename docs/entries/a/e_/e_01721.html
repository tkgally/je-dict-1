絵(え)

え
noun
picture, drawing, painting

Definitions

1. picture, drawing, painting
A visual representation created by drawing, painting, or other artistic means.
2. illustration, image
A picture in a book or document that explains or decorates the text.

Examples

子供(こども)が絵(え)を描(か)いている。
The child is drawing a picture.
この絵(え)は有名(ゆうめい)な画家(がか)の作品(さくひん)です。
This painting is a work by a famous artist.
絵本(えほん)を読(よ)む。
To read a picture book.
絵本 (えほん) = picture book

Notes

絵(え) is the everyday word for pictures and drawings.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 絵(え)を描(か)く (to draw/paint a picture)
  • 絵(え)を見(み)る (to look at a picture)
  • 絵(え)を飾(かざ)る (to display a picture)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 絵本(えほん) (picture book)
  • 絵(え)はがき (picture postcard)
  • 油絵(あぶらえ) (oil painting)
  • 水彩画(すいさいが) (watercolor painting)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 絵(え): picture (casual, everyday)
  • 絵画(かいが): painting (formal, art context)
  • 画(が): painting (in compounds like 漫画, 映画)

Related Words

Related: 絵画(かいが) (painting (formal))
Related: 画家(がか) (painter, artist)
