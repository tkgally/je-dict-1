おどおど

おどおど
adverb
nervously; timidly; fearfully

Definitions

1. nervously; timidly; fearfully
Acting with visible nervousness or fear, appearing intimidated.

Examples

おどおどしながら話(はな)した。
He spoke nervously.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)はおどおどしていた。
The new employee was acting timidly.
そんなにおどおどしないで。
Don't be so nervous.

Notes

おどおど describes visible nervousness or timidity, showing lack of confidence.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • びくびく: nervous, frightened (more fear-based)
  • おずおず: hesitantly, timidly
  • もじもじ: fidgeting, shy

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • おどおどする: to act nervously
  • おどおどしながら: while acting nervously
