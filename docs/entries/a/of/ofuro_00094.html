お風呂(ふろ)

おふろ
noun
bath, bathtub

Definitions

1. bath, bathtub
A tub filled with hot water for bathing. In Japan, the bath is for soaking after washing outside the tub.
2. bathing, taking a bath
The act of taking a bath, an important daily ritual in Japanese culture.

Examples

お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)りましたか。
Did you take a bath?
お風呂(ふろ)を沸(わ)かしてください。
Please heat the bath.
毎日(まいにち)お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)ります。
I take a bath every day.

Notes

Japanese bathing culture involves washing and rinsing the body outside the tub before soaking in the bath water. The same bath water is often shared by family members. 銭湯(せんとう) is a public bathhouse, and 温泉(おんせん) is a hot spring bath.
