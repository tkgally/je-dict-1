アヒル

あひる
noun
duck (domestic)

Definitions

1. domestic duck
A domesticated duck, typically white, raised for eggs, meat, or as a pet.

Examples

アヒルが池(いけ)で泳(およ)いでいる。
Ducks are swimming in the pond.
アヒルが「ガーガー」と鳴(な)く。
The duck quacks.
子供(こども)がアヒルに餌(えさ)をやっている。
The child is feeding the ducks.

Notes

アヒル refers specifically to domesticated ducks.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 鴨(かも): wild duck
  • アヒルの子(こ): duckling
  • 合鴨(あいがも): crossbreed of wild and domestic duck

ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • ガーガー: quacking sound
  • よちよち: waddling motion (アヒル歩(ある)き)

KANJI: Sometimes written as 家鴨 (domestic duck).
