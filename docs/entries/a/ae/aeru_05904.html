和(あ)える

あえる
verb (ichidan)
to dress (food), to mix with dressing

Definitions

1. to dress, to mix with seasoning/dressing
To mix vegetables or other foods with a dressing or sauce.

Examples

ほうれん草(そう)をごまで和(あ)える。
To dress spinach with sesame.
野菜(やさい)を味噌(みそ)で和(あ)える。
To dress vegetables with miso.
酢味噌(すみそ)で和(あ)えた刺身(さしみ)。
Sashimi dressed with vinegar miso.

Notes

COOKING:

Used for Japanese-style dressings/tossing. Common combinations:

  • ごま和え: sesame dressing
  • 酢味噌和え: vinegar-miso dressing
  • 白和え: tofu dressing
