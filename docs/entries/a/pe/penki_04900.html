ペンキ

ペンキ
noun
paint

Definitions

1. paint (oil-based)
A colored liquid applied to surfaces for decoration or protection, typically oil-based

Examples

壁(かべ)にペンキを塗(ぬ)った。
I painted the wall.
ペンキが乾(かわ)くまで触(さわ)らないでください。
Please don't touch until the paint dries.
白(しろ)いペンキで塗(ぬ)り直(なお)した。
I repainted it with white paint.
ペンキの匂(にお)いがする。
I can smell paint.

Notes

ペンキ is paint, particularly oil-based paint.

ETYMOLOGY:
From Dutch "pek" (pitch, tar), historically used for ship caulking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ペンキを塗(ぬ)る: to paint (apply paint)
  • ペンキが乾(かわ)く: paint dries
  • ペンキを剥(は)がす: to peel off paint
  • ペンキの匂(にお)い: paint smell

RELATED TERMS:

  • 塗料(とりょう): paint, coating (general/technical term)
  • スプレーペンキ: spray paint
  • 油性ペンキ: oil-based paint
  • 水性ペンキ: water-based paint

COMMON SIGNS:

  • ペンキ塗(ぬ)りたて: wet paint (freshly painted)
