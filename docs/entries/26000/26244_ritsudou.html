1. rhythm; rhythmic movement; pulsation
Regular, repeated movement or pattern, especially in music, dance, biology, or nature. Emphasizes the regularity and cyclical nature of the motion.
心臓(しんぞう)が律動(りつどう)する。
The heart beats rhythmically.
波(なみ)の律動(りつどう)に身(み)をまかせた。
I surrendered myself to the rhythm of the waves.
音楽(おんがく)に合(あ)わせて体(からだ)が自然(しぜん)と律動(りつどう)し始(はじ)めた。
My body naturally started moving rhythmically to the music.

Composed of 律(りつ) (law; rule; rhythm) and 動(どう) (movement). Describes movement characterized by regularity and repetition, whether biological, musical, or natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 律動的(りつどうてき): rhythmic; rhythmical
  • 律動(りつどう)運動(うんどう): rhythmic exercise
  • 心臓(しんぞう)の律動(りつどう): heartbeat rhythm
  • 律動(りつどう)体操(たいそう): rhythmic gymnastics/calisthenics

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • リズム: rhythm — the loanword is far more common in everyday speech and music contexts
  • 拍動(はくどう): pulsation; beating — specifically refers to the beating of the heart or pulse, more medical
  • 鼓動(こどう): heartbeat; throbbing — poetic or literary term for the beating of the heart
  • 韻律(いんりつ): meter; prosody — refers to rhythm in poetry and verse
