早飲(はやの)み

はやのみ
noun
quick drinking; chugging; drinking fast
1. quick drinking; chugging; drinking fast
The act of drinking a beverage quickly, whether in a competitive context, out of hurry, or as a social activity. Can refer to both alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks.
ビールの早飲(はやの)み競争(きょうそう)をした。
We had a beer-chugging contest.
早飲(はやの)みは体(からだ)に良(よ)くないから気(き)をつけて。
Drinking too fast isn't good for your body, so be careful.
朝(あさ)は時間(じかん)がないので、コーヒーを早飲(はやの)みして出(で)かける。
I don't have time in the morning, so I quickly drink my coffee and head out.

Composed of 早(はや) (fast, quick) and 飲(の)み (drinking), the noun form of 飲(の)む (to drink). Part of the productive 早(はや)+verb stem pattern: 早食(はやぐ)い (eating fast), 早起(はやお)き (getting up early), 早歩(はやある)き (walking fast), etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早飲(はやの)み競争(きょうそう): speed-drinking contest
  • 早飲(はやの)み早食(はやぐ)い: eating and drinking fast
  • 早飲(はやの)みする: to drink quickly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一気飲(いっきの)み: chugging all at once — drinking an entire drink in one go, often used in party contexts
  • 早食(はやぐ)い: fast eating — the eating counterpart; also considered unhealthy
  • 飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to drink up — to drain a glass completely
