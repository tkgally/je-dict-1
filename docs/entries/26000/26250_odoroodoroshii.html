1. eerie; ghastly; gruesome; horrifying
Describes something that evokes a sense of horror, dread, or the uncanny. Often used for atmospheres, appearances, sounds, or artistic styles that are intentionally creepy or macabre.
おどろおどろしい雰囲気(ふんいき)だ。
It's an eerie atmosphere.
おどろおどろしい音楽(おんがく)が流(なが)れてきた。
Eerie music started playing.
その映画(えいが)のポスターは、おどろおどろしい絵(え)が描(えが)かれていて、見(み)るだけで怖(こわ)くなる。
The movie poster has such a ghastly illustration that just looking at it is scary.

An emphatic reduplicative form related to 恐(おそ)ろしい (frightening). The repetition of the おどろ element intensifies the sense of horror and eeriness. Written entirely in hiragana; no standard kanji form is in common use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おどろおどろしい雰囲気(ふんいき): eerie atmosphere
  • おどろおどろしい声(こえ): ghastly voice
  • おどろおどろしい音(おと): creepy sound
  • おどろおどろしい絵(え): gruesome painting/illustration
  • おどろおどろしいタイトル: sensational/lurid title

USAGE:
Often used to describe horror media (movies, books, artwork), haunted house atmospheres, or anything deliberately designed to evoke dread. Can also be used critically to describe something overly melodramatic or exaggeratedly horrifying.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 恐(おそ)ろしい: frightening; terrifying — a general term for fear, broader in application
  • 不気味(ぶきみ): uncanny; creepy — focuses on the unsettling quality of something that feels "off"
  • 気味(きみ)が悪(わる)い: creepy; gives one the creeps — a more colloquial way to express unease
  • おぞましい: hideous; revolting — emphasizes disgust and moral repulsion more than supernatural dread
