会社名(かいしゃめい)

かいしゃめい
noun
company name; name of a company
1. company name; name of a company
The official name of a business or corporation.
会社名(かいしゃめい)を入力(にゅうりょく)してください。
Please enter the company name.
名刺(めいし)には会社名(かいしゃめい)と部署名(ぶしょめい)が記載(きさい)されている。
The business card shows the company name and department name.
合併(がっぺい)に伴(ともな)い会社名(かいしゃめい)が変更(へんこう)されたため、すべての書類(しょるい)を更新(こうしん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
Since the company name was changed due to a merger, all documents need to be updated.

Composed of 会社(かいしゃ) (company) and 名(めい) (name). Frequently encountered on forms, business cards, contracts, and official documents. In Japan, formal company names typically include a legal entity suffix such as 株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ) (kabushiki gaisha, often abbreviated K.K.) or 合同会社(ごうどうがいしゃ) (gōdō gaisha, LLC).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会社名(かいしゃめい)を記入(きにゅう)する — to fill in the company name
  • 会社名(かいしゃめい)を変更(へんこう)する — to change the company name
  • 正式(せいしき)会社名(かいしゃめい) — official company name
  • 旧(きゅう)会社名(かいしゃめい) — former company name

RELATED TERMS:

  • 社名(しゃめい) — company name (shorter form, common in news and business)
  • 商号(しょうごう) — trade name; business name (legal term)
  • 屋号(やごう) — trade name; shop name (for sole proprietors)
