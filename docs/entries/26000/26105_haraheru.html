1. to be hungry; to get hungry
A casual, direct expression meaning to feel hungry. Literally 'stomach diminishes.' More blunt and masculine-leaning than the polite form.
腹(はら)減(へ)った。
I'm hungry.
もう腹(はら)減(へ)ったから早(はや)く食(た)べよう。
I'm already hungry, so let's eat soon.
朝(あさ)から何(なに)も食(た)べてないから腹(はら)が減(へ)って仕方(しかた)がない。
I haven't eaten anything since morning, so I'm starving.

A casual way to say 'I'm hungry,' typically used in informal conversation. The past tense 腹(はら)減(へ)った is the most common form. Often used without the particle が between 腹(はら) and 減(へ)る in very casual speech, though 腹(はら)が減(へ)る is the grammatically complete form.

REGISTER:
Casual and somewhat rough. More common in male speech and among close friends. Women and speakers in polite contexts tend to prefer お腹(なか)が空(す)いた or お腹(なか)が空(す)きました.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腹(はら)減(へ)った: I'm hungry (most common form)
  • 腹(はら)が減(へ)って仕方(しかた)がない: to be starving; unbearably hungry
  • 腹(はら)が減(へ)っては戦(いくさ)はできぬ: you can't fight on an empty stomach (proverb)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お腹(なか)が空(す)く: to be hungry — polite, gender-neutral
  • 空腹(くうふく): hunger — formal noun form
