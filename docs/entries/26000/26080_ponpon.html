ぽんぽん

ぽんぽん
adverb, onomatopoeia
pop-pop; tapping; in rapid succession
1. pop-pop; with a popping or tapping sound
The sound or manner of light, repeated tapping, popping, or clapping.
肩(かた)をぽんぽんと叩(たた)いた。
I tapped their shoulder lightly.
花火(はなび)がぽんぽんと上(あ)がった。
Fireworks popped up one after another.
彼女(かのじょ)はぽんぽんと布団(ふとん)のほこりを払(はら)った。
She patted the dust off the futon with quick taps.
2. in rapid succession; one after another; without hesitation
Doing something quickly and smoothly, without pausing to think. Often used for speaking frankly or things happening in quick sequence.
言(い)いたいことをぽんぽん言(い)う人(ひと)だ。
She's the kind of person who says what's on her mind without hesitation.
仕事(しごと)がぽんぽん片(かた)づいて気持(きも)ちいい。
Work is getting done one thing after another — it feels great.
アイデアがぽんぽん出(で)てくる。
Ideas keep popping up one after another.
3. tummy (baby talk)
A childish word for stomach, used when speaking to or about young children.
ぽんぽんが痛(いた)いの？
Does your tummy hurt?
ぽんぽん出(で)てるね。食(た)べすぎたかな。
Your tummy is sticking out. Maybe you ate too much.
赤(あか)ちゃんのぽんぽんを優(やさ)しくさすってあげた。
I gently rubbed the baby's tummy.

A versatile onomatopoeia with multiple distinct uses. The sound-related senses describe light, repeated impacts or pops, while the figurative sense describes things happening in quick, effortless succession.

VARIANTS:

  • ポンポン — katakana form (also common)
  • ぽんぽん物(もの)を言(い)う — to speak frankly/bluntly
  • ぽんと — single action version (one tap, one pop)

SIMILAR ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • とんとん — lighter, more rhythmic tapping (like knocking on a door)
  • ぱんぱん — louder, sharper clapping or slapping sound
  • ぽんと — a single light pop or tap (non-repeated version)

REGISTER:
The onomatopoeia senses are neutral. The "tummy" sense (sense 3) is baby talk / childish and used only in casual family contexts.
