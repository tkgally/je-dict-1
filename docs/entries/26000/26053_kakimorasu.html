1. to omit writing; to fail to write down; to leave out in writing
To accidentally fail to include something that should have been written. Implies an unintentional omission rather than a deliberate exclusion.
名前(なまえ)を書(か)き漏(も)らしていた。
I had omitted writing the name.
大事(だいじ)なことを書(か)き漏(も)らさないように注意(ちゅうい)した。
I was careful not to leave out anything important.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)で数字(すうじ)をひとつ書(か)き漏(も)らしたせいで、やり直(なお)しになった。
Because I left out one number in the report, I had to redo it.

A compound verb from 書(か)く (to write) + 漏(も)らす (to let leak, to miss). Transitive. Describes the accidental omission of something in writing. The auxiliary 漏(も)らす adds the meaning of 'failing to do' or 'letting slip through' when attached to verb stems.

TRANSITIVITY: transitive

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書(か)き漏(も)らしがある: there is an omission in the writing
  • 書(か)き漏(も)らさないように: so as not to leave anything out
  • うっかり書(か)き漏(も)らす: to accidentally omit writing

SIMILAR PATTERNS WITH 漏(も)らす:

  • 聞(き)き漏(も)らす: to fail to hear, to miss hearing
  • 言(い)い漏(も)らす: to fail to mention, to leave unsaid
  • 見(み)漏(も)らす: to fail to notice, to overlook

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)き忘(わす)れる: to forget to write -- emphasis on forgetting rather than accidentally omitting
  • 書(か)き落(お)とす: to leave out in writing -- very similar, slightly more formal
