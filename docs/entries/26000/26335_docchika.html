どっちか

どっちか
pronoun
either one; one or the other
1. either one; one or the other; which one
A colloquial contraction of どちらか, used when choosing between two options. Can function as a question ('which one?') or as a statement ('one of the two').
どっちか選(えら)んで。
Pick one.
どっちかと言(い)えば、映画(えいが)のほうが好(す)きだ。
If I had to choose, I'd say I prefer movies.
赤(あか)と青(あお)のどっちかにしてくれない？どっちでもいいから早(はや)く決(き)めてよ。
Can you pick either the red one or the blue one? I don't care which, so just decide quickly.

A colloquial contraction of どちらか (which one of the two; either one). Very common in casual spoken Japanese. The full form どちらか is used in polite or formal speech.

The expression どっちかと言(い)えば (if I had to say which; if anything) is an extremely common hedging phrase used to express a mild preference.

USAGE:

  • どっちか選(えら)んで: pick one (of two)
  • どっちかと言(い)えば: if I had to choose; if anything
  • どっちかに決(き)める: to decide on one or the other

RELATED TERMS:

  • どちらか: either one — the polite/formal equivalent
  • どっち: which one (of two) — the informal question form without か
  • どれか: any one (of three or more) — for selecting from more than two options
  • どちら: which one (of two) — the polite form of どっち
