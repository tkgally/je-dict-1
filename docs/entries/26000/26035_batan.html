ばたん

ばたん
onomatopoeia
bang; slam; thud
1. bang; slam; thud
Mimics the sharp, heavy sound of something slamming shut or falling over. Used for doors slamming, objects toppling, or a person collapsing.
ドアがばたんと閉(し)まった。
The door slammed shut.
風(かぜ)でばたんと窓(まど)が閉(し)まった。
The window slammed shut from the wind.
疲(つか)れて家(いえ)に帰(かえ)ると、ベッドにばたんと倒(たお)れ込(こ)んだ。
Exhausted when I got home, I collapsed onto the bed with a thud.

An onomatopoeia representing a loud, heavy impact sound. Typically used with the particle と to form an adverbial phrase (ばたんと). The sound suggests something heavy or forceful, distinguishing it from lighter impact sounds.

FORMS:

  • ばたんと: adverbial form (with a bang)
  • ばたん！: exclamatory, representing the sound itself
  • ばたんばたん: repeated, for continuous banging sounds

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ドアをばたんと閉(し)める: to slam a door shut
  • ばたんと倒(たお)れる: to fall over with a thud
  • ばたんと閉(し)まる: to slam shut (intransitive)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • バタン: katakana variant, sometimes used for emphasis
  • がたん: a rattling impact sound, less clean and more rattly than ばたん
  • ドン: a deep thud or boom, often for a single heavy impact
  • パタン: a lighter, crisper closing sound (e.g., a book snapping shut)
