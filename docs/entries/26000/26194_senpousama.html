先方様(せんぽうさま)

せんぽうさま
noun
the other party (honorific); the other side
1. the other party (honorific); the other side
A respectful way to refer to the other person or party in a business or formal interaction. Used in polite speech when referring to clients, business partners, or the person one is dealing with.
先方様(せんぽうさま)にご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Please contact the other party.
先方様(せんぽうさま)のご都合(つごう)に合(あ)わせます。
We will accommodate the other party's schedule.
先方様(せんぽうさま)から返事(へんじ)がまだ届(とど)いていないので、もう一度(いちど)確認(かくにん)していただけますか。
We haven't received a reply from the other party yet, so could you please check again?

A keigo (polite language) term used primarily in business settings. The base word 先方(せんぽう) means "the other party" and is already polite; adding 様(さま) makes it more honorific. This form is standard in business email, phone conversations, and internal discussions when referring to a client, partner, or counterpart.

USAGE:

  • Used when speaking about (not to) the other party in the third person
  • Appropriate in business emails, memos, and internal meetings
  • More formal than simply saying 相手(あいて) (the other person)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先方様(せんぽうさま)のご意向(いこう) — the other party's wishes
  • 先方様(せんぽうさま)にお伝(つた)えする — to convey to the other party
  • 先方様(せんぽうさま)のご要望(ようぼう) — the other party's request

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先方(せんぽう) — the other party (less formal, without 様(さま))
  • 相手方(あいてがた) — the other side (legal or formal contexts)
  • お客様(きゃくさま) — customer (when the other party is specifically a client)
