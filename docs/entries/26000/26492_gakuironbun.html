学位論文(がくいろんぶん)

がくいろんぶん
noun
degree thesis; dissertation
1. degree thesis; dissertation; academic thesis
A formal written work submitted as a requirement for an academic degree. Encompasses both master's theses and doctoral dissertations.
学位論文(がくいろんぶん)のテーマが決(き)まった。
I've decided on the topic for my thesis.
学位論文(がくいろんぶん)の審査(しんさ)は来月(らいげつ)に行(おこな)われる。
The thesis defense will be held next month.
彼(かれ)は三年(さんねん)かけて学位論文(がくいろんぶん)を書(か)き上(あ)げ、博士号(はくしごう)を取得(しゅとく)した。
He spent three years writing his dissertation and earned his doctorate.

Composed of 学位(がくい) (academic degree) and 論文(ろんぶん) (thesis, paper). The umbrella term for theses written to fulfill degree requirements at any level. In practice, 修士論文(しゅうしろんぶん) (master's thesis) and 博士論文(はくしろんぶん) (doctoral dissertation) are more specific and frequently used when the level is known.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学位論文(がくいろんぶん)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a thesis
  • 学位論文(がくいろんぶん)の審査(しんさ): thesis examination/defense
  • 学位論文(がくいろんぶん)を執筆(しっぴつ)する: to write a thesis

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 修士論文(しゅうしろんぶん): master's thesis — specifically for the master's degree; often abbreviated as 修論(しゅうろん)
  • 博士論文(はくしろんぶん): doctoral dissertation — specifically for the doctoral degree; often abbreviated as 博論(はくろん)
  • 卒業論文(そつぎょうろんぶん): graduation thesis — the undergraduate thesis; abbreviated as 卒論(そつろん)
