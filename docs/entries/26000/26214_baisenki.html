焙煎機(ばいせんき)

ばいせんき
noun
roasting machine; roaster (for coffee or tea)
1. roasting machine; roaster
A machine used to roast raw materials, most commonly coffee beans, applying controlled heat to develop flavor and aroma.
店内(てんない)に焙煎機(ばいせんき)がある。
There is a roasting machine inside the shop.
自家(じか)焙煎(ばいせん)の店(みせ)では大(おお)きな焙煎機(ばいせんき)でコーヒー豆(まめ)を焼(や)いている。
At a self-roasting shop, they roast coffee beans in a large roasting machine.
最近(さいきん)は家庭用(かていよう)の小型(こがた)焙煎機(ばいせんき)も手(て)に入(はい)るようになった。
Recently, small home-use roasting machines have become available.

Composed of 焙煎(ばいせん) (roasting) and 機(き) (machine). Most commonly associated with coffee culture, where 自家焙煎(じかばいせん) (in-house roasting) cafés have become popular in Japan. Can also refer to machines for roasting tea leaves (茶(ちゃ)), barley (麦(むぎ)), or other foods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コーヒー焙煎機(ばいせんき) — coffee roasting machine
  • 家庭用(かていよう)焙煎機(ばいせんき) — home roasting machine
  • 小型(こがた)焙煎機(ばいせんき) — small roaster
  • 焙煎機(ばいせんき)で焼(や)く — to roast in a roasting machine

RELATED TERMS:

  • 焙煎(ばいせん) — roasting (the process)
  • 自家焙煎(じかばいせん) — in-house roasting; self-roasting
  • ロースター — roaster (loanword)
