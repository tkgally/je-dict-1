1. power outage; power cut; disconnection of electricity
The interruption or cutting off of electrical power supply, whether planned (for maintenance) or unplanned (due to a fault or disaster).
工事(こうじ)のため断電(だんでん)します。
Power will be cut for construction work.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で断電(だんでん)が発生(はっせい)した。
A power outage occurred due to the typhoon.
断電(だんでん)時(じ)には非常用(ひじょうよう)発電機(はつでんき)が作動(さどう)する。
The emergency generator activates during a power outage.

A somewhat technical word used in official notices, utility communications, and infrastructure contexts. More formal than the everyday 停電(ていでん), and emphasizes the deliberate or mechanical cutting of power rather than a general outage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 断電(だんでん)する: to cut power
  • 断電(だんでん)が発生(はっせい)する: a power cut occurs
  • 断電(だんでん)時(じ): during a power outage
  • 計画(けいかく)断電(だんでん): planned power cut

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 停電(ていでん): power outage; blackout — the everyday word for power going out
  • 送電(そうでん): power transmission — the delivery of electrical power
  • 通電(つうでん): energization; passing current — the opposite: electricity flowing through
