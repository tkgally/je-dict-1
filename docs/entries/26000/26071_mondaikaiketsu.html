1. problem solving; resolving issues
The act or process of finding solutions to problems or difficulties. Used both as a noun and with する as a verb.
問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)に取(と)り組(く)む。
To work on problem solving.
チームで問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)にあたることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to tackle problem solving as a team.
論理的(ろんりてき)な思考力(しこうりょく)は問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)能力(のうりょく)を高(たか)めるために不可欠(ふかけつ)だ。
Logical thinking skills are essential for improving problem-solving ability.

A compound of 問題(もんだい) (problem) and 解決(かいけつ) (resolution). It functions both as a standalone noun and as a suru-verb (問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)する). Frequently used in business, education, and academic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)に取(と)り組(く)む — to work on problem solving
  • 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)能力(のうりょく) — problem-solving ability
  • 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)手法(しゅほう) — problem-solving method
  • 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)を図(はか)る — to seek to solve a problem

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 解決(かいけつ) — resolution, solution (more general; can apply to any matter)
  • 対処(たいしょ) — coping, dealing with (focuses on handling rather than solving)
  • 打開(だかい) — breakthrough, breaking through a deadlock

USAGE:
Often used as a modifier before other nouns: 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)型(がた) (problem-solving type), 問題解決(もんだいかいけつ)学習(がくしゅう) (problem-based learning).
