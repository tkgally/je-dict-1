近況文(きんきょうぶん)

きんきょうぶん
noun
status update; personal news message
1. status update; personal news message; recent-activities write-up
A brief written message informing others about one's recent activities, life changes, or current situation. Common in letters, social media posts, and alumni newsletters.
同窓会(どうそうかい)のために近況文(きんきょうぶん)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a personal update for the class reunion.
年賀状(ねんがじょう)に近況文(きんきょうぶん)を添(そ)えた。
I included a personal update on my New Year's card.
SNSの近況文(きんきょうぶん)で転職(てんしょく)したことを報告(ほうこく)した。
I announced my job change in a status update on social media.

Composed of 近況(きんきょう) (recent situation, current state of affairs) and 文(ぶん) (writing, text). Writing 近況文(きんきょうぶん) is a common social obligation in Japan, especially for 年賀状(ねんがじょう) (New Year's cards), 暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)い (summer greeting cards), and alumni newsletters.

In the digital age, the concept extends to social media status updates and LinkedIn-style posts about career changes or life events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 近況文(きんきょうぶん)を書(か)く: to write a status update
  • 近況文(きんきょうぶん)を投稿(とうこう)する: to post a status update
  • 近況文(きんきょうぶん)を添(そ)える: to include a personal update

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 近況報告(きんきょうほうこく): status report — slightly more formal, often verbal
  • 近況(きんきょう): recent situation — the base noun without the 'writing' aspect
  • 自己紹介(じこしょうかい): self-introduction — introduces oneself rather than updating on recent events
