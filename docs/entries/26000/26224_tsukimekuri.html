月(つき)めくり

つきめくり
noun
monthly calendar; page-a-month calendar
1. monthly calendar; page-a-month calendar; wall calendar with one page per month
A calendar format where each month has its own page, typically hung on a wall and flipped or torn off at the end of each month. The most common type of wall calendar in Japan.
壁(かべ)に月(つき)めくりのカレンダーを掛(か)けた。
I hung a monthly calendar on the wall.
月(つき)めくりをめくって、新(あたら)しい月(つき)の予定(よてい)を確認(かくにん)した。
I turned the page of the monthly calendar and checked the schedule for the new month.
年末(ねんまつ)になると会社(かいしゃ)から月(つき)めくりのカレンダーが配(くば)られる。
At year's end, the company distributes monthly wall calendars.

Composed of 月(つき) (month) and めくり (turning/flipping pages, from 捲(めく)る). The word describes the format of the calendar rather than its content.

In Japan, companies and organizations commonly give out 月(つき)めくり calendars as year-end gifts to clients and employees. These are a ubiquitous part of Japanese office and home life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 月(つき)めくりのカレンダー: a page-a-month calendar
  • 壁掛(かべか)け月(つき)めくり: wall-hanging monthly calendar
  • 月(つき)めくりをめくる: to turn the page on the monthly calendar

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日(ひ)めくり: daily tear-off calendar — one page per day
  • 卓上(たくじょう)カレンダー: desk calendar — placed on a desk rather than hung
  • カレンダー: calendar — general term for any calendar
