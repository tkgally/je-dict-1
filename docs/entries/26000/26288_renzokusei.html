連続性(れんぞくせい)

れんぞくせい
noun
continuity; consecutiveness
1. continuity; consecutiveness; connectedness
the quality of being continuous or unbroken; the state of things being connected without interruption
ストーリーの連続性(れんぞくせい)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
The continuity of the story is important.
この政策(せいさく)には連続性(れんぞくせい)が欠(か)けている。
This policy lacks continuity.
歴史(れきし)の連続性(れんぞくせい)を理解(りかい)することで、現在(げんざい)の社会(しゃかい)がどのように形成(けいせい)されたかが見(み)えてくる。
By understanding the continuity of history, you can see how present-day society was formed.

Composed of 連続(れんぞく) (continuation; series) + 性(せい) (nature; quality). Describes the abstract quality of things being connected or ongoing without gaps. Used widely in academic, technical, and everyday contexts when discussing whether something maintains an unbroken flow.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連続性(れんぞくせい)がある: to have continuity
  • 連続性(れんぞくせい)が欠(か)ける: to lack continuity
  • 連続性(れんぞくせい)を保(たも)つ: to maintain continuity
  • 連続性(れんぞくせい)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure continuity
  • 事業(じぎょう)の連続性(れんぞくせい): business continuity

USAGE:
The suffix 性(せい) turns 連続(れんぞく) from "continuation" (a concrete process) into "continuity" (an abstract quality). This is a productive pattern in Japanese: 安全(あんぜん) (safety) → 安全性(あんぜんせい) (the quality of being safe), 重要(じゅうよう) (important) → 重要性(じゅうようせい) (importance).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連続(れんぞく): continuation; series — the base word, referring to the act or state of continuing
  • 一貫性(いっかんせい): consistency — emphasizes logical coherence rather than temporal continuity
  • 持続性(じぞくせい): sustainability; durability — emphasizes lasting over time
