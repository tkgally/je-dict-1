生豆(なままめ)

なままめ
noun
raw beans; green coffee beans
1. raw beans; uncooked beans
Beans that have not been cooked, roasted, or otherwise processed. Used when distinguishing raw beans from their prepared forms.
生豆(なままめ)は十分(じゅうぶん)に加熱(かねつ)してから食(た)べてください。
Please cook raw beans thoroughly before eating.
大豆(だいず)は生豆(なままめ)のまま食(た)べると消化(しょうか)に悪(わる)い。
Eating soybeans raw is bad for digestion.
2. green coffee beans; unroasted coffee beans
Coffee beans in their raw, unroasted state. A term commonly used in the specialty coffee world, where enthusiasts purchase green beans and roast them at home.
生豆(なままめ)は緑色(みどりいろ)をしている。
Green coffee beans are green in color.
自家(じか)焙煎(ばいせん)をするために、生豆(なままめ)を通販(つうはん)で取(と)り寄(よ)せた。
I ordered green coffee beans online to do my own home roasting.
この店(みせ)では世界(せかい)各地(かくち)の生豆(なままめ)を量(はか)り売(う)りしている。
This shop sells green coffee beans from around the world by weight.
生豆(なままめ)の品質(ひんしつ)が焙煎後(ばいせんご)の味(あじ)を大(おお)きく左右(さゆう)する。
The quality of the green beans greatly affects the taste after roasting.

Composed of 生(なま) (raw, unprocessed) and 豆(まめ) (beans). The word has two distinct usage domains: general cooking (raw beans of any kind) and specialty coffee (unroasted coffee beans).

In the coffee context, 生豆(なままめ) refers to the green, unroasted beans that are the starting material for roasting. The specialty coffee boom in Japan has made this sense increasingly common. Coffee enthusiasts and roasters use the term frequently when discussing bean origins, processing methods, and quality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生豆(なままめ)を焙煎(ばいせん)する: to roast green coffee beans
  • 生豆(なままめ)の産地(さんち): origin of green beans
  • 生豆(なままめ)を選(えら)ぶ: to select green beans
  • 生豆(なままめ)の状態(じょうたい): condition of raw/green beans

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 煎(い)り豆(まめ): roasted beans — beans that have been dry-roasted
  • 乾燥(かんそう)豆(まめ): dried beans — beans preserved by drying, but not necessarily raw
  • 焙煎豆(ばいせんまめ): roasted coffee beans — the result after green beans are roasted
