1. becoming enormous; enlargement; growing to a huge scale
The process of something growing to an extremely large size or scale, whether physically, organizationally, or figuratively. Often used with する.
台風(たいふう)が巨大化(きょだいか)している。
The typhoon is growing enormous.
企業(きぎょう)の巨大化(きょだいか)が進(すす)んでいる。
The growth of corporations to massive scale is progressing.
都市(とし)の巨大化(きょだいか)に伴(ともな)い、交通(こうつう)渋滞(じゅうたい)がひどくなった。
As cities have grown enormous, traffic congestion has worsened.

Formed from 巨大(きょだい) (enormous) + 化(か) (transformation suffix). Used for dramatic increases in size or scale — natural phenomena like typhoons, organizational growth like corporations, or social trends like urbanization. The nuance often implies that the growth is notable or problematic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 台風(たいふう)の巨大化(きょだいか): typhoons growing larger
  • 企業(きぎょう)の巨大化(きょだいか): corporations growing massive
  • 巨大化(きょだいか)する都市(とし): a city growing to enormous scale
  • 巨大化(きょだいか)が進(すす)む: enlargement is progressing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大型化(おおがたか): becoming larger — more neutral, used for products and equipment increasing in size
  • 拡大(かくだい): expansion — broader term for growth in scope, area, or influence
  • 肥大化(ひだいか): bloating; swelling — carries a negative nuance of unhealthy or excessive growth
