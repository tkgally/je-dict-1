印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)

いんさつがいしゃ
noun
printing company; print shop
1. printing company; print shop; printing firm
A company that provides printing services — from large commercial printers handling books, magazines, and packaging to smaller shops for business cards, flyers, and promotional materials.
印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)に名刺(めいし)を注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered business cards from a printing company.
父(ちち)は地元(じもと)の印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)で働(はたら)いている。
My father works at a local printing company.
デジタル化(か)が進(すす)んで、印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)は新(あたら)しいビジネスモデルを模索(もさく)している。
As digitalization progresses, printing companies are searching for new business models.

Composed of 印刷(いんさつ) (printing) + 会社(がいしゃ) (company). Japan has a long and significant printing industry, with major companies like 大日本印刷(だいにっぽんいんさつ) (DNP) and 凸版印刷(とっぱんいんさつ) (Toppan) being among the world's largest.

The 会社(がいしゃ) suffix combines with many industry terms to form company names: 建設(けんせつ)会社(がいしゃ) (construction company), 保険(ほけん)会社(がいしゃ) (insurance company), 運送(うんそう)会社(がいしゃ) (shipping company).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)に依頼(いらい)する: to commission a printing company
  • 大手(おおて)印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ): major printing company
  • 印刷会社(いんさつがいしゃ)の見積(みつも)もり: printing company estimate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 印刷所(いんさつじょ): print shop — often smaller-scale
  • 印刷屋(いんさつや): printer, print shop — more colloquial
  • 出版社(しゅっぱんしゃ): publisher — publishes content, may or may not print
