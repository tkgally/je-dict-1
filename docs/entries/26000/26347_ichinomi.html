一飲(いちの)み

いちのみ
noun
one gulp; drinking in a single swallow
1. one gulp; drinking in a single swallow; downing in one go
The act of drinking something all at once without pausing. Can also mean something is so easy it can be consumed or dealt with effortlessly.
ビールを一飲(いちの)みにした。
I downed the beer in one gulp.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)を一飲(いちの)みにして、生(い)き返(かえ)った気分(きぶん)になった。
I drank the cold water in one gulp and felt revived.
あの力士(りきし)なら、こんな相手(あいて)は一飲(いちの)みだろう。
For that sumo wrestler, an opponent like this would be a piece of cake.

Composed of 一(いち) (one) and 飲(の)み (drinking). The literal meaning is drinking something in a single gulp, but it extends figuratively to mean dealing with something effortlessly, as if swallowing it whole.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一飲(いちの)みにする: to gulp down; to make short work of
  • 一飲(いちの)みで飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to drain in a single gulp

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一気飲(いっきの)み: chugging — drinking without stopping, often in a social/party context
  • 一口(ひとくち): one sip, one bite — smaller quantity than 一飲(いちの)み
  • 丸呑(まるの)み: swallowing whole — used literally or for uncritical acceptance
