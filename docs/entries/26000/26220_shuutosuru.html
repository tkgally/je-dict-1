1. to shoot; to take a shot (in sports)
To kick, throw, or strike a ball toward the goal or target in sports such as soccer, basketball, or handball. Refers specifically to an attempt to score.
ゴール前(まえ)でシュートした。
He shot in front of the goal.
ペナルティエリア内(ない)から強烈(きょうれつ)なシュートを放(はな)った。
He fired a powerful shot from inside the penalty area.
残(のこ)り三(さん)分(ぷん)で逆転(ぎゃくてん)のシュートを決(き)めて、チームを勝利(しょうり)に導(みちび)いた。
With three minutes left, he scored the go-ahead shot and led his team to victory.

From the English word "shoot." In Japanese sports commentary and casual conversation, シュート is used both as a noun (a shot) and as a verb with する. The noun form is more common in commentary, while シュートする is common in general conversation.

In baseball, シュート refers to a specific pitch type (a screwball or shootball that moves toward the batter), which is a distinct usage from the goal-scoring meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • シュートを打(う)つ: to take a shot
  • シュートを放(はな)つ: to fire a shot (more dramatic)
  • シュートを決(き)める: to score a goal (with a shot)
  • 強烈(きょうれつ)なシュート: a powerful shot
  • ミドルシュート: a mid-range shot
  • ヘディングシュート: a header (heading shot)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 蹴(け)る: to kick — the physical action, not specifically an attempt to score
  • 投(な)げる: to throw — for basketball or handball shooting
  • 撃(う)つ: to shoot — for firearms, not sports
