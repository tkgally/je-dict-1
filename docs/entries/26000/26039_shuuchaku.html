終着(しゅうちゃく)

しゅうちゃく
noun
terminus; final stop; end of the line
1. terminus; final stop; last station
The last stop on a train, bus, or other transportation route. Most commonly encountered in the compound 終着駅 (terminal station).
この電車(でんしゃ)の終着(しゅうちゃく)は東京(とうきょう)です。
The final stop of this train is Tokyo.
終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)で全員(ぜんいん)降(お)りてください。
Please all disembark at the terminal station.
終着(しゅうちゃく)まで乗(の)っていたら、折(お)り返(かえ)し運転(うんてん)で戻(もど)される。
If you ride to the last stop, you'll be taken back on the return service.
2. final destination; end point
Used figuratively to refer to the ultimate destination or conclusion of a journey, career, or life path.
人生(じんせい)の終着(しゅうちゃく)はだれにもわからない。
Nobody knows the final destination of their life.
長(なが)い旅(たび)の終着点(しゅうちゃくてん)にようやく着(つ)いた。
I finally arrived at the end point of my long journey.
この研究(けんきゅう)の終着点(しゅうちゃくてん)は新薬(しんやく)の開発(かいはつ)だった。
The end point of this research was the development of a new drug.

Composed of 終(しゅう) (end) + 着(ちゃく) (arrival). Most frequently used in transportation contexts, particularly in the compound 終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき). Also used figuratively for the end point of any extended process or journey.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき): terminal station
  • 終着点(しゅうちゃくてん): end point, final destination
  • 終着(しゅうちゃく)まで: all the way to the last stop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 終点(しゅうてん): end point, last stop — more common in everyday speech for bus/train stops
  • 目的地(もくてきち): destination — where one intends to go, not necessarily the end of a route
  • 到着(とうちゃく): arrival — the act of arriving, not specific to a final stop
