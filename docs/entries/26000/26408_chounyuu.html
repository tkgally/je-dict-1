1. preparing baby formula; making up powdered milk
The process of mixing powdered infant formula with hot water to prepare a bottle for a baby. Involves specific temperature and hygiene requirements.
調乳(ちょうにゅう)には70℃以上(いじょう)のお湯(ゆ)を使(つか)う。
Use water at 70°C or above to prepare formula.
夜中(よなか)の調乳(ちょうにゅう)が大変(たいへん)で寝不足(ねぶそく)になる。
Preparing formula in the middle of the night is exhausting and causes sleep deprivation.
調乳(ちょうにゅう)ポットがあれば、すぐに適温(てきおん)のお湯(ゆ)が使(つか)えて便利(べんり)だ。
If you have a formula prep machine, you can use water at the right temperature immediately, which is convenient.

Composed of 調(ちょう) (prepare, mix) and 乳(にゅう) (milk). A specialized parenting term that new parents quickly learn. The WHO recommends preparing formula with water at 70°C or above to kill potentially harmful bacteria, then cooling to body temperature before feeding.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 調乳(ちょうにゅう)する: to prepare formula
  • 調乳(ちょうにゅう)ポット: formula prep kettle/machine
  • 調乳(ちょうにゅう)用(よう)のお湯(ゆ): water for preparing formula
  • 調乳(ちょうにゅう)温度(おんど): formula preparation temperature
  • 調乳(ちょうにゅう)スペース: formula preparation area (in public facilities)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Many Japanese shopping malls, department stores, and public facilities provide dedicated 調乳(ちょうにゅう)スペース or 授乳室(じゅにゅうしつ) (nursing rooms) where parents can prepare bottles with hot water provided.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 授乳(じゅにゅう): breastfeeding, nursing — feeding directly from the breast
  • 哺乳瓶(ほにゅうびん): baby bottle — the container used for feeding
  • 粉(こな)ミルク: powdered formula — the product that is mixed with water
