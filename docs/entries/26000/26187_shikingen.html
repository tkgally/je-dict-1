資金源(しきんげん)

しきんげん
noun
source of funds; funding source
1. source of funds; funding source
The origin or means by which money or capital is obtained for a particular purpose.
資金源(しきんげん)を確保(かくほ)する。
To secure a funding source.
そのNPOは寄付(きふ)を主(おも)な資金源(しきんげん)としている。
That NPO relies on donations as its main source of funds.
警察(けいさつ)は犯罪(はんざい)グループの資金源(しきんげん)を断(た)つための捜査(そうさ)を進(すす)めている。
The police are advancing their investigation to cut off the criminal group's funding source.

Frequently used in news and business contexts when discussing how organizations, projects, or even criminal operations obtain money. The word carries a neutral tone on its own but often appears in contexts involving accountability or investigation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金源(しきんげん)を確保(かくほ)する — to secure funding
  • 資金源(しきんげん)を断(た)つ — to cut off a funding source
  • 主(おも)な資金源(しきんげん) — main source of funds
  • 新(あたら)しい資金源(しきんげん) — new funding source
  • 不正(ふせい)な資金源(しきんげん) — illicit funding source

RELATED TERMS:

  • 財源(ざいげん) — financial resources (often used for government budgets)
  • 収入源(しゅうにゅうげん) — source of income (for individuals or organizations)
