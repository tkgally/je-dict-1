1. to color; to tint; to add color to
To apply color or pigment to something, giving it a particular hue.
絵(え)を色付(いろづ)ける。
To color a drawing.
食紅(しょくべに)でクリームを淡(あわ)く色付(いろづ)けた。
I lightly tinted the cream with food coloring.
陶芸(とうげい)では釉薬(ゆうやく)で作品(さくひん)を色付(いろづ)ける。
In pottery, works are colored using glaze.
2. to embellish; to add flair to
To add interest, beauty, or distinction to something in a figurative sense.
花(はな)が庭(にわ)を色付(いろづ)ける。
Flowers add color to the garden.
ジャズの演奏(えんそう)が夜(よる)の雰囲気(ふんいき)を色付(いろづ)けた。
The jazz performance added flair to the evening atmosphere.
季節(きせつ)の食材(しょくざい)が食卓(しょくたく)を美(うつく)しく色付(いろづ)けている。
Seasonal ingredients beautifully embellish the dining table.

An ichidan (lower-one-row) transitive verb composed of 色(いろ) (color) and 付(つ)ける (to attach, to apply). The intransitive counterpart is 色付(いろづ)く (to become colored, to turn color — often said of autumn leaves).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive: 色付(いろづ)ける (to color something)
  • Intransitive: 色付(いろづ)く (to become colored)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色付(いろづ)ける — to add color
  • 淡(あわ)く色付(いろづ)ける — to tint lightly
  • 鮮(あざ)やかに色付(いろづ)ける — to color vividly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着色(ちゃくしょく)する — to color, to dye (more technical/formal)
  • 彩(いろど)る — to color, to decorate (literary, emphasizes beauty)
