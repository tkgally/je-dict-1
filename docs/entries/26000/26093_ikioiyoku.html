勢(いきお)いよく

いきおいよく
adverb
vigorously; forcefully; with great energy
1. vigorously; forcefully; with great energy; with momentum
Doing something with strong force, speed, or enthusiasm. Describes energetic, dynamic movement or action.
水(みず)が勢(いきお)いよく流(なが)れている。
Water is flowing vigorously.
子(こ)どもたちが勢(いきお)いよく走(はし)り出(だ)した。
The children dashed out with great energy.
ドアを勢(いきお)いよく開(あ)けたら、壁(かべ)にぶつかってしまった。
I opened the door forcefully and it slammed into the wall.

The adverbial form of 勢(いきお)いがよい (having momentum/force). 勢(いきお)い is a noun meaning "momentum, force, vigor," and よく is the adverbial form of よい (good). Together they describe an action performed with noticeable force or energy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勢(いきお)いよく飛(と)び出(だ)す — to jump out energetically
  • 勢(いきお)いよく流(なが)れる — to flow strongly
  • 勢(いきお)いよく食(た)べる — to eat with gusto
  • 勢(いきお)いよく成長(せいちょう)する — to grow vigorously

SIMILAR ADVERBS:

  • 激(はげ)しく — violently, intensely (stronger; implies more extreme force)
  • 力強(ちからづよ)く — powerfully, strongly (focuses on strength)
  • 元気(げんき)よく — cheerfully, energetically (focuses on liveliness rather than physical force)
  • 一気(いっき)に — all at once (focuses on speed and continuity rather than force)
