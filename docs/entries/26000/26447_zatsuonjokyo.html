1. noise removal; noise cancellation; noise reduction
The process or technology of eliminating unwanted sound or electronic interference from audio signals, recordings, or transmissions. Common in audio engineering, telecommunications, and consumer electronics.
雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)機能(きのう)がついたイヤホンを買(か)った。
I bought earphones with a noise cancellation feature.
録音(ろくおん)データの雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)をソフトで行(おこな)った。
I used software to remove the noise from the recording data.
オンライン会議(かいぎ)では雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)の設定(せってい)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
In online meetings, the noise cancellation settings are important.

A compound of 雑音(ざつおん) (noise, static) and 除去(じょきょ) (removal, elimination). Has become an everyday term with the popularity of noise-cancelling headphones and online meeting software.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)機能(きのう): noise cancellation function
  • 雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)フィルター: noise removal filter
  • 雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)ソフト: noise removal software
  • 雑音(ざつおん)除去(じょきょ)を行(おこな)う: to perform noise removal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ノイズキャンセリング: noise cancelling — the loanword version, commonly used for headphone technology
  • 消音(しょうおん): muting, silencing — making something quiet, not specifically removing background noise
  • 防音(ぼうおん): soundproofing — preventing sound from entering or leaving a space
