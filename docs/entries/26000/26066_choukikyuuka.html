長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)

ちょうききゅうか
noun
long vacation; extended leave
1. long vacation; extended leave
A prolonged period away from work or school, typically lasting a week or more.
長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)を取(と)りたい。
I want to take a long vacation.
夏(なつ)の長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)に海外旅行(かいがいりょこう)を計画(けいかく)している。
I'm planning a trip abroad during summer's long vacation.
会社(かいしゃ)の制度(せいど)では、年(ねん)に一度(いちど)長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)を申請(しんせい)することができる。
Under the company's system, you can apply for extended leave once a year.

Refers to any vacation or leave period significantly longer than usual. In a workplace context, it often means taking a week or more off consecutively, which is uncommon in many Japanese companies.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)を取(と)る — to take a long vacation
  • 長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)を申請(しんせい)する — to apply for extended leave
  • 長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)に入(はい)る — to go on extended leave
  • 長期休暇(ちょうききゅうか)中(ちゅう) — during extended leave

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 有給休暇(ゆうきゅうきゅうか) — paid leave (specific to employment)
  • 連休(れんきゅう) — consecutive holidays (usually 2–5 days around national holidays)
  • 休職(きゅうしょく) — leave of absence from work (often for health or personal reasons)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Taking long vacations can be difficult in Japan due to workplace culture. Many workers feel pressure not to take extended time off (有休(ゆうきゅう)を取(と)りにくい). The government has promoted initiatives like "Premium Friday" to encourage more time off.
