隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)

いんきょにん
noun
retired person; recluse
1. retired person; person in retirement
A person who has withdrawn from active work or public life to live quietly. Traditionally referred to a household head who had handed over authority to a successor.
祖父(そふ)は隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)として静(しず)かに暮(く)らしている。
My grandfather lives quietly as a retired person.
会社(かいしゃ)を引退(いんたい)した父(ちち)は、すっかり隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)になった。
My father, who retired from the company, has become a complete retiree.
定年後(ていねんご)は隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)になるつもりはなく、ボランティアで活動(かつどう)したい。
After retirement age, I don't plan to become a recluse — I want to stay active through volunteer work.
江戸(えど)時代(じだい)の隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)は家督(かとく)を譲(ゆず)った後(あと)、趣味(しゅみ)に没頭(ぼっとう)する人(ひと)も多(おお)かった。
In the Edo period, many retirees devoted themselves to hobbies after handing over the family headship.
2. recluse; person living in seclusion
A person who has chosen to withdraw from society and live a quiet, secluded life, often with connotations of contentment and simplicity.
山奥(やまおく)に住(す)む隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)のような生活(せいかつ)に憧(あこが)れる。
I long for a life like a recluse living deep in the mountains.
彼(かれ)は世間(せけん)から離(はな)れた隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)のように見(み)えるが、実(じつ)はネットで活発(かっぱつ)に情報(じょうほう)発信(はっしん)している。
He looks like a recluse cut off from the world, but he's actually very active online sharing information.
江戸(えど)時代(じだい)の隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)は家督(かとく)を譲(ゆず)った後(あと)、趣味(しゅみ)に没頭(ぼっとう)する人(ひと)も多(おお)かった。
In the Edo period, many retirees devoted themselves to hobbies after handing over the family headship.

A compound of 隠居(いんきょ) (retirement, seclusion) and 人(にん) (person). In premodern Japan, 隠居(いんきょ) specifically meant the formal act of a household head (家長(かちょう)) transferring authority and property to a successor. The word 隠居人(いんきょにん) can carry either a positive nuance of peaceful retirement or a slightly negative implication of being idle and disengaged.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)のような生活(せいかつ): a reclusive lifestyle
  • 隠居(いんきょ)人(にん)になる: to become a retiree/recluse

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 隠居(いんきょ): retirement, seclusion — the act or state itself, not the person
  • 引退者(いんたいしゃ): retired person — a more modern, neutral term
  • 年金(ねんきん)生活者(せいかつしゃ): pensioner — focuses on living on a pension
