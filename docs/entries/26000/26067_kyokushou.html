極小(きょくしょう)

きょくしょう
noun, adjective-no
extremely small; minute; minimal
1. extremely small; minute; minimal
Describes something of the smallest possible size, degree, or amount. Used both as a noun and as a modifier with の.
極小(きょくしょう)のカメラが開発(かいはつ)された。
An extremely small camera was developed.
この部品(ぶひん)は極小(きょくしょう)サイズで肉眼(にくがん)では見(み)えにくい。
This component is so tiny that it's hard to see with the naked eye.
被害(ひがい)を極小(きょくしょう)に抑(おさ)えるための対策(たいさく)を講(こう)じた。
We took measures to keep the damage to an absolute minimum.

Can function as a noun meaning "the very smallest" or as a modifier (極小(きょくしょう)の) meaning "extremely small, minute." In mathematics, 極小(きょくしょう) refers to a local minimum (as opposed to 極大(きょくだい), local maximum).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 極小(きょくしょう)サイズ — extremely small size
  • 極小(きょくしょう)の粒子(りゅうし) — minute particles
  • 被害(ひがい)を極小(きょくしょう)にする — to minimize damage
  • 極小(きょくしょう)住宅(じゅうたく) — micro-house; tiny dwelling

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 微小(びしょう) — very small, minute (more technical/scientific)
  • 最小(さいしょう) — smallest, minimum (superlative; the absolute least)
  • 極(ごく)小(しょう)さい — an emphatic phrasing meaning "extremely tiny"

READING NOTE:
The reading きょくしょう is the standard on'yomi compound. The alternative reading 極小(ごくしょう) also exists and is sometimes used interchangeably, though きょくしょう is more common in formal and technical contexts.
