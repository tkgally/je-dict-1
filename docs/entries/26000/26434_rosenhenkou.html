1. change of route; rerouting
A physical change in the path or route of transportation such as a bus line, train line, or road.
工事(こうじ)のため路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)があります。
There will be a route change due to construction.
バスの路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)で通勤(つうきん)時間(じかん)が長(なが)くなった。
My commute got longer due to the bus route change.
新(あたら)しい道路(どうろ)が完成(かんせい)し、路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)が行(おこな)われた。
A new road was completed and the route was changed.
2. change of course; policy shift; change of direction
A figurative change in strategy, policy, or overall direction. Commonly used in business and politics to describe a significant shift in approach.
会社(かいしゃ)は大幅(おおはば)な路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The company announced a major policy shift.
選挙(せんきょ)の結果(けっか)を受(う)けて、党(とう)は路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)を迫(せま)られている。
In response to the election results, the party is being forced to change course.
経営(けいえい)陣(じん)の交代(こうたい)をきっかけに、路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)が進(すす)んでいる。
Triggered by a change in management, a shift in direction is underway.

A compound of 路線(ろせん) (route, line, course) and 変更(へんこう) (change, modification). Sense 1 is literal (transportation routes), while sense 2 is figurative and commonly used in news and business.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)を行(おこな)う: to carry out a route change
  • 大幅(おおはば)な路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう): major policy shift
  • 路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)を余儀(よぎ)なくされる: to be forced into a change of course
  • 路線(ろせん)変更(へんこう)を発表(はっぴょう)する: to announce a change of direction

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 方針(ほうしん)転換(てんかん): policy change — more explicitly about organizational/political strategy
  • 進路(しんろ)変更(へんこう): change of course — used for ships, aircraft, or career direction
  • 迂回(うかい): detour — a temporary rerouting, not a permanent route change
