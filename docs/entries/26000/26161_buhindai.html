部品代(ぶひんだい)

ぶひんだい
noun
parts cost; cost of replacement parts
1. parts cost; price of parts; cost of replacement components
The cost charged for physical parts or components, as distinct from labor charges. Commonly encountered when getting repairs done on cars, electronics, appliances, and other equipment.
部品代(ぶひんだい)は別途(べっと)かかります。
The parts cost is charged separately.
修理(しゅうり)の見積(みつも)もりで部品代(ぶひんだい)が高(たか)くて驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by the high parts cost in the repair estimate.
工賃(こうちん)は三千円(さんぜんえん)だが、部品代(ぶひんだい)を含(ふく)めると一万円(いちまんえん)を超(こ)える。
The labor charge is 3,000 yen, but including parts it exceeds 10,000 yen.

Composed of 部品(ぶひん) (parts, components) + 代(だい) (cost, charge). The suffix 代(だい) is one of the most common ways to express cost in Japanese, attaching to many nouns: 食事代(しょくじだい) (meal cost), 交通費(こうつうひ) (transport cost), 電気代(でんきだい) (electricity bill).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部品代(ぶひんだい)と工賃(こうちん): parts cost and labor charge
  • 部品代(ぶひんだい)込(こ)み: parts included (in the price)
  • 部品代(ぶひんだい)が高(たか)い: parts are expensive
  • 部品代(ぶひんだい)別途(べっと): parts charged separately

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 工賃(こうちん): labor charge — the cost of the work itself
  • 修理代(しゅうりだい): repair cost — the total cost of a repair
  • 材料費(ざいりょうひ): material cost — broader term covering raw materials
