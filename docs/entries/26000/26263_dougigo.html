同義語(どうぎご)

どうぎご
noun
synonym; word with the same meaning
1. synonym; word with the same meaning
「美(うつく)しい」と「きれいな」は同義語(どうぎご)です。
"Beautiful" and "pretty" are synonyms.
辞書(じしょ)で同義語(どうぎご)を調(しら)べる。
To look up synonyms in a dictionary.
完全(かんぜん)な同義語(どうぎご)はほとんどなく、多(おお)くの場合(ばあい)ニュアンスに違(ちが)いがある。
There are almost no perfect synonyms; in most cases there are differences in nuance.

A linguistic term for words that share the same or nearly the same meaning. In practice, true synonyms (words with identical meaning in all contexts) are rare; most so-called synonyms differ in register, nuance, or collocational range.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 同義語(どうぎご)辞典(じてん): thesaurus (synonym dictionary)
  • 同義語(どうぎご)を探(さが)す: to look for a synonym
  • 完全(かんぜん)な同義語(どうぎご): perfect synonym
  • 同義語(どうぎご)関係(かんけい): synonymous relationship

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 類義語(るいぎご): near-synonym, word with similar meaning (broader term; includes words that are close but not identical in meaning)
  • 対義語(たいぎご): antonym (word with opposite meaning)
  • 類語(るいご): similar word (casual abbreviation of 類義語(るいぎご))

USAGE:
同義語(どうぎご) implies words that are interchangeable in most contexts, while 類義語(るいぎご) includes words that are merely similar. In everyday conversation, 類義語(るいぎご) is used more broadly to encompass both categories.
