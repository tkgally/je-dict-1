届出書(とどけでしょ)

とどけでしょ
noun
notification form; official written notice
1. notification form; official written notice; registration document
A formal document submitted to a government office, employer, or institution to officially notify them of a change in status, a request, or a declaration.
届出書(とどけでしょ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する。
To submit a notification form.
転居(てんきょ)の届出書(とどけでしょ)を市役所(しやくしょ)に出(だ)した。
I submitted a change-of-address form to city hall.
届出書(とどけでしょ)の記入(きにゅう)に不備(ふび)があったため、再提出(さいていしゅつ)を求(もと)められた。
Because there were errors in filling out the notification form, I was asked to resubmit it.

Composed of 届出(とどけで) (notification, filing) and 書(しょ) (document). Japanese bureaucracy relies heavily on paper forms, and there are specific 届出書(とどけでしょ) for nearly every life event and administrative action.

Common types include 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ) (marriage registration), 出生届(しゅっしょうとどけ) (birth registration), 転居届(てんきょとどけ) (change of address), and 退職届(たいしょくとどけ) (resignation notice). The word 届出書(とどけでしょ) is the generic term covering all such documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 届出書(とどけでしょ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a notification form
  • 届出書(とどけでしょ)に記入(きにゅう)する: to fill out a notification form
  • 届出書(とどけでしょ)の様式(ようしき): notification form format
  • 届出書(とどけでしょ)の控(ひかえ): copy of a notification form

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 届(とどけ): notification; report — shorter, more general term
  • 申請書(しんせいしょ): application form — for requesting permission or approval
  • 申告書(しんこくしょ): declaration form — especially for tax returns
