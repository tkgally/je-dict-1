立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)

りっちじょうけん
noun
location conditions; site conditions
1. location conditions; site conditions; locational factors
The various characteristics and advantages of a particular location, especially as they relate to opening a business, building a home, or developing property. Includes factors like transportation access, surrounding amenities, and neighborhood environment.
この物件(ぶっけん)は立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)がいい。
This property has good location conditions.
駅(えき)から近(ちか)いという立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)が決(き)め手(て)になった。
The location factor of being close to the station was the deciding point.
新(あたら)しい店舗(てんぽ)を出(だ)すにあたって、立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)を慎重(しんちょう)に検討(けんとう)した。
When opening a new store, we carefully considered the site conditions.

A compound of 立地(りっち) (location, site) and 条件(じょうけん) (conditions, requirements). This term is essential in real estate, business planning, and urban development contexts. In Japan, where public transportation is heavily used, proximity to a train station is often the most important 立地条件.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)がいい/わるい: good/bad location conditions
  • 立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)に恵(めぐ)まれる: to be blessed with good location
  • 立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)を満(み)たす: to meet site requirements
  • 立地(りっち)条件(じょうけん)を重視(じゅうし)する: to prioritize location conditions

KEY FACTORS:

  • 駅(えき)からの距離(きょり): distance from the station
  • 周辺(しゅうへん)環境(かんきょう): surrounding environment
  • 交通(こうつう)の便(べん): transportation convenience
  • 人通(ひとどお)り: foot traffic
  • 治安(ちあん): public safety

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立地(りっち): location, site — the base noun without the 条件 component
  • 好立地(こうりっち): prime location — emphasizes desirability
  • 利便性(りべんせい): convenience — a broader concept of how convenient a location is
