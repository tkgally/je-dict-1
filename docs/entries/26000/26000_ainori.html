1. ride-sharing; sharing a vehicle; carpooling
Sharing a taxi, car, or other vehicle with others who are going in the same direction. A practical way to split transportation costs.
タクシーに相乗(あいの)りした。
We shared a taxi.
駅(えき)から空港(くうこう)まで相乗(あいの)りで行(い)きませんか。
Would you like to share a ride from the station to the airport?
料金(りょうきん)を節約(せつやく)するために、同(おな)じ方向(ほうこう)の人(ひと)と相乗(あいの)りすることにした。
To save on the fare, I decided to share the ride with someone going in the same direction.
2. jumping on the bandwagon; piggybacking
Figuratively, joining in on something already in progress for one's own benefit — such as a movement, trend, or someone else's initiative.
人気(にんき)に相乗(あいの)りする。
To ride on someone's popularity.
他社(たしゃ)のキャンペーンに相乗(あいの)りして売(う)り上(あ)げを伸(の)ばした。
They boosted sales by piggybacking on another company's campaign.
与党(よとう)の法案(ほうあん)に野党(やとう)が相乗(あいの)りする形(かたち)で、全会一致(ぜんかいいっち)で可決(かけつ)された。
With the opposition party jumping on the ruling party's bill, it was passed unanimously.

Composed of 相(あい) (mutual, together) and 乗(の)り (riding). The literal sense of sharing a vehicle is the most common everyday usage, especially with taxis. The figurative sense of piggybacking on someone else's efforts or momentum is frequently used in business and politics.

Also the name of a well-known Japanese reality TV show ({あいのり}) about people looking for love on a shared road trip.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タクシーに相乗(あいの)りする: to share a taxi
  • 相乗(あいの)り料金(りょうきん): shared ride fare
  • 人気(にんき)に相乗(あいの)りする: to ride on someone's popularity
  • 相乗(あいの)り候補(こうほ): joint candidate (politics — supported by multiple parties)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カープール: carpool — English loanword; more formal/modern
  • ライドシェア: ride-share — the modern app-based ride-sharing concept
  • 便乗(びんじょう): taking advantage of an opportunity; free ride — stronger nuance of exploiting a situation
