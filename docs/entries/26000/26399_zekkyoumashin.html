絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシン

ぜっきょうましん
noun
thrill ride; scream machine
1. thrill ride; scream machine; extreme amusement park ride
An amusement park ride designed to be thrilling and frightening, such as roller coasters, free-fall towers, and spinning rides. The name literally means 'screaming machine.'
絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンは苦手(にがて)です。
I'm not good with thrill rides.
この遊園地(ゆうえんち)には絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンが十(じゅう)もある。
This amusement park has as many as ten thrill rides.
友達(ともだち)に無理(むり)やり絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンに乗(の)せられて、泣(な)きそうになった。
My friend forced me onto a thrill ride, and I nearly cried.

A Japanese-coined compound combining 絶叫(ぜっきょう) (screaming) with the English loanword マシン (machine). This term is the standard way to refer to extreme amusement park rides in Japanese, encompassing roller coasters, drop towers, pendulum rides, and similar attractions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンに乗(の)る: to ride a thrill ride
  • 絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンが好(す)き: to like thrill rides
  • 絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシンが苦手(にがて): to be bad with thrill rides
  • 世界一(せかいいち)の絶叫(ぜっきょう)マシン: the world's greatest thrill ride

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan has world-renowned thrill rides, particularly at Fuji-Q Highland (富士急(ふじきゅう)ハイランド) and Universal Studios Japan. Discussing one's tolerance for 絶叫マシン is a common conversation topic, especially when planning group outings to amusement parks.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ジェットコースター: roller coaster — a specific type of thrill ride
  • アトラクション: attraction — a broader term for any amusement park ride or show
  • 乗(の)り物(もの): ride — generic term for things you ride
