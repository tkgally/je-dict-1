1. self-publishing
Publishing a book or other work independently, without going through a traditional publishing house. The author handles or arranges editing, design, printing, and distribution.
自己出版(じこしゅっぱん)で本(ほん)を出(だ)した。
I published a book through self-publishing.
電子書籍(でんししょせき)なら自己出版(じこしゅっぱん)のハードルが低(ひく)い。
The barrier to self-publishing is low with e-books.
SNSで人気(にんき)を集(あつ)めてから自己出版(じこしゅっぱん)で写真集(しゃしんしゅう)を出(だ)す人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
More and more people are building a following on social media and then self-publishing photo books.

Composed of 自己(じこ) (self) and 出版(しゅっぱん) (publishing). In Japan, self-publishing has traditionally been done through 自費出版(じひしゅっぱん) (paying out of pocket for a publisher's services), but 自己出版(じこしゅっぱん) has come to refer more specifically to the modern model where the author handles everything independently, especially through digital platforms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己出版(じこしゅっぱん)する: to self-publish
  • 自己出版(じこしゅっぱん)の本(ほん): self-published book
  • 電子書籍(でんししょせき)の自己出版(じこしゅっぱん): e-book self-publishing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自費出版(じひしゅっぱん): vanity publishing — paying a publisher to produce your book; the traditional Japanese model
  • 商業出版(しょうぎょうしゅっぱん): commercial publishing — the publisher bears costs and selects manuscripts
  • 同人誌(どうじんし): doujinshi — self-published fan works or indie magazines, a major Japanese subculture
