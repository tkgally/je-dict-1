1. defecation; bowel movement
The medical or formal term for the act of expelling feces from the body. Used in healthcare contexts, medical literature, and formal descriptions of digestive function.
毎朝(まいあさ)排便(はいべん)の習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
I have a habit of having a bowel movement every morning.
排便(はいべん)の回数(かいすう)や状態(じょうたい)を記録(きろく)してください。
Please keep a record of the frequency and condition of your bowel movements.
食物(しょくもつ)繊維(せんい)を多(おお)く摂(と)ることで排便(はいべん)がスムーズになり、便秘(べんぴ)の予防(よぼう)につながる。
By consuming plenty of dietary fiber, bowel movements become smoother, which helps prevent constipation.

Composed of 排(はい) (expel, discharge) and 便(べん) (stool, feces). Like 排尿(はいにょう), this is a medical/formal term. In everyday conversation, Japanese speakers say トイレに行(い)く or use euphemisms rather than 排便(はいべん) directly. The word appears on medical questionnaires, in health articles, and in conversations with doctors.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 排便(はいべん)習慣(しゅうかん): bowel habits
  • 排便(はいべん)障害(しょうがい): defecation disorder
  • 排便(はいべん)困難(こんなん): difficulty defecating
  • 排便(はいべん)回数(かいすう): bowel movement frequency
  • 排便(はいべん)を促(うなが)す: to promote bowel movements

RELATED TERMS:

  • 排尿(はいにょう): urination — the corresponding term for urinary function
  • 便秘(べんぴ): constipation — insufficient or difficult bowel movements
  • 下痢(げり): diarrhea — excessively frequent or loose bowel movements
  • お通(つう)じ: bowel movement (polite, everyday term)
