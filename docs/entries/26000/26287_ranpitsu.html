乱筆(らんぴつ)

らんぴつ
noun
poor handwriting; scrawl; hastily written letter
1. poor handwriting; scrawl; hastily written letter
messy or careless handwriting, or a letter written in haste; often used as a humble apology for one's own writing
乱筆(らんぴつ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください。
Please excuse my poor handwriting.
乱筆(らんぴつ)乱文(らんぶん)にて失礼(しつれい)いたします。
I apologize for the messy handwriting and rough composition.
急(いそ)いで書(か)いたので乱筆(らんぴつ)になってしまいましたが、内容(ないよう)はお伝(つた)えできたかと思(おも)います。
I wrote in a hurry so the handwriting is messy, but I believe I got the message across.

Composed of 乱(らん) (disorder; messy) + 筆(ひつ) (writing brush; writing). This word is most often used as a formulaic humble expression at the end of handwritten letters and notes to apologize for untidy writing. Even when the writer's handwriting is perfectly legible, it functions as a polite self-deprecation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乱筆(らんぴつ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください: please forgive my poor handwriting
  • 乱筆(らんぴつ)乱文(らんぶん): messy handwriting and rough composition (paired phrase)
  • 乱筆(らんぴつ)にて失礼(しつれい)いたします: I apologize for the poor handwriting
  • 乱筆(らんぴつ)で申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません: I'm sorry for the poor handwriting

REGISTER:
This is a formal, written expression. It belongs to the tradition of humble self-reference in Japanese letter-writing. It is rarely used in casual contexts and is almost exclusively found in handwritten correspondence.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乱文(らんぶん): rough or disorganized composition — often paired with 乱筆(らんぴつ)
  • 悪筆(あくひつ): bad handwriting — a more direct, less formal description of poor penmanship
