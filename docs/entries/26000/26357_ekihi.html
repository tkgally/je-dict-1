液肥(えきひ)

えきひ
noun
liquid fertilizer
1. liquid fertilizer; liquid plant food
Fertilizer in liquid form, diluted with water before application. Quickly absorbed by plants and commonly used for houseplants, container gardening, and hydroponics.
週(しゅう)に一回(いっかい)、液肥(えきひ)を与(あた)える。
Apply liquid fertilizer once a week.
液肥(えきひ)を水(みず)で薄(うす)めてから使(つか)ってください。
Please dilute the liquid fertilizer with water before using.
観葉植物(かんようしょくぶつ)には、成長期(せいちょうき)に液肥(えきひ)を定期的(ていきてき)に与(あた)えると元気(げんき)に育(そだ)つ。
If you regularly give liquid fertilizer to foliage plants during the growing season, they'll grow vigorously.

Composed of 液(えき) (liquid) and 肥(ひ) (fertilizer). A convenient and popular fertilizer type for home gardening. Typically sold as a concentrate that is diluted with water before application.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 液肥(えきひ)を薄(うす)める: to dilute liquid fertilizer
  • 液肥(えきひ)を与(あた)える: to apply liquid fertilizer
  • 液肥(えきひ)を撒(ま)く: to spread liquid fertilizer
  • 有機(ゆうき)液肥(えきひ): organic liquid fertilizer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 固形肥料(こけいひりょう): solid fertilizer — granular or pellet form
  • 元肥(もとごえ): base fertilizer — applied before planting
  • 追肥(ついひ): supplemental fertilizer — applied during growth, which 液肥(えきひ) is often used for
