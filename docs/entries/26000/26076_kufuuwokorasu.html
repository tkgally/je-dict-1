工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らす

くふうをこらす
expression
to devise cleverly; to use ingenuity
1. to devise cleverly; to use ingenuity; to put thought and effort into
To apply creativity and careful thought to come up with something effective or inventive. Implies dedicated effort in the planning or design of something.
工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした料理(りょうり)を作(つく)る。
To prepare food with creative ingenuity.
先生(せんせい)は授業(じゅぎょう)に工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らして生徒(せいと)を飽(あ)きさせない。
The teacher uses ingenuity in class to keep students from getting bored.
デザイナーが工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした展示(てんじ)は、来場者(らいじょうしゃ)から高(たか)い評価(ひょうか)を受(う)けた。
The cleverly devised exhibition by the designer received high praise from visitors.

A set expression combining 工夫(くふう) (ingenuity, device) with 凝(こ)らす (to concentrate, to elaborate). The verb 凝(こ)らす conveys putting concentrated effort into something, so the expression emphasizes dedicated creative thinking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした料理(りょうり) — creatively prepared food
  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らしたデザイン — ingeniously crafted design
  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした授業(じゅぎょう) — a well-thought-out lesson
  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした演出(えんしゅつ) — cleverly devised production/staging

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 工夫(くふう)する — to devise, to think up (simpler; less emphasis on elaborate effort)
  • 趣向(しゅこう)を凝(こ)らす — to exercise one's ingenuity (similar structure; focuses on creative taste)
  • 知恵(ちえ)を絞(しぼ)る — to rack one's brains (emphasizes the difficulty of thinking)

USAGE:
Often appears in the past participle form 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らした as a modifier before nouns, describing something that is the product of careful creative effort.
