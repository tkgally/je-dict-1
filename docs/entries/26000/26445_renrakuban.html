連絡板(れんらくばん)

れんらくばん
noun
message board; notice board; communication board
1. message board; notice board; communication board
A physical or digital board used to post messages, announcements, and notices for a group. Common in schools, workplaces, and community settings.
連絡板(れんらくばん)に予定(よてい)を書(か)いておいた。
I wrote the schedule on the message board.
大事(だいじ)なお知(し)らせが連絡板(れんらくばん)に貼(は)ってある。
An important notice is posted on the message board.
社内(しゃない)の連絡板(れんらくばん)を毎日(まいにち)確認(かくにん)するのが習慣(しゅうかん)になっている。
It has become a habit to check the company message board every day.

A compound of 連絡(れんらく) (communication, contact) and 板(ばん) (board). Can refer to a physical bulletin board or an online message board. In Japanese schools, information is often communicated to parents through a 連絡(れんらく)帳(ちょう) (communication notebook) rather than a board.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連絡板(れんらくばん)に書(か)く: to write on the message board
  • 連絡板(れんらくばん)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the message board
  • 連絡板(れんらくばん)に貼(は)る: to post on the message board
  • 社内(しゃない)連絡板(れんらくばん): company notice board

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 掲示板(けいじばん): bulletin board — a more general term that also refers to internet forums
  • 伝言板(でんごんばん): message board (for personal messages) — a board for leaving individual messages for specific people
  • 連絡帳(れんらくちょう): communication notebook — a school notebook for messages between teachers and parents
