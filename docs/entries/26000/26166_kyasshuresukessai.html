キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)

きゃっしゅれすけっさい
noun
cashless payment; electronic payment
1. cashless payment; non-cash payment; electronic payment
Any form of payment that does not involve physical cash — including credit cards, debit cards, electronic money (Suica, PayPay, etc.), QR code payments, and other digital payment methods.
キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)に対応(たいおう)していますか。
Do you accept cashless payment?
最近(さいきん)はキャッシュレス決済(けっさい)で支払(しはら)うことが多(おお)い。
Lately I often pay with cashless methods.
この店(みせ)はキャッシュレス決済(けっさい)専用(せんよう)で、現金(げんきん)は使(つか)えません。
This store is cashless-only — you can't use cash.

A compound of the English loanword キャッシュレス (cashless) and the Japanese word 決済(けっさい) (payment, settlement). The term has become extremely common in Japan since the late 2010s as the government promoted digital payment adoption.

Japan was historically a cash-heavy society, but キャッシュレス決済(けっさい) has spread rapidly through services like PayPay, Suica/PASMO, LINE Pay, and d-払(ばら)い. Many stores now display signs indicating which cashless methods they accept.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)に対応(たいおう)する: to support cashless payment
  • キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)比率(ひりつ): cashless payment ratio
  • キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)専用(せんよう): cashless-only
  • キャッシュレス決済(けっさい)を導入(どうにゅう)する: to introduce cashless payment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電子(でんし)マネー: electronic money — specific type (Suica, nanaco, etc.)
  • QRコード決済(けっさい): QR code payment — specific method
  • 現金払(げんきんばら)い: cash payment — the traditional alternative
  • 決済(けっさい): payment, settlement — the broader concept
