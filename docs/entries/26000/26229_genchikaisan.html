1. disbanding at the venue; free dismissal at the destination
The arrangement where a group disperses at the activity site rather than returning together to the starting point. Common for school excursions, company outings, and group tours where participants may want to continue on their own afterward.
今日(きょう)の遠足(えんそく)は現地解散(げんちかいさん)です。
Today's excursion will be free dismissal at the site.
午後(ごご)の見学(けんがく)のあと、現地解散(げんちかいさん)にしましょう。
After the afternoon tour, let's disband at the site.
会社(かいしゃ)の研修(けんしゅう)は現地解散(げんちかいさん)だったので、帰(かえ)りにそのまま買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)った。
Since the company training ended with free dismissal on site, I went shopping on the way home.

Composed of 現地(げんち) (the actual site, on location) and 解散(かいさん) (disbanding, breaking up). A standard term used by organizers of group events to announce that participants do not need to return to a meeting point.

Often contrasted with 集合場所(しゅうごうばしょ)に戻(もど)って解散(かいさん) (returning to the meeting point to disband). The phrase is commonly heard on school field trips, company outings, and tour groups.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現地解散(げんちかいさん)にする: to decide on free dismissal at the venue
  • 現地解散(げんちかいさん)になる: to end with on-site dismissal
  • 現地集合(げんちしゅうごう)・現地解散(げんちかいさん): meet at the venue, disband at the venue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 解散(かいさん): disbanding; dismissal — the general term
  • 自由解散(じゆうかいさん): free dismissal — similar meaning, less common
  • 現地集合(げんちしゅうごう): meeting at the venue — the counterpart for gathering
