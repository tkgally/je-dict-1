1. indulgence; addiction; being absorbed in
Losing oneself in a pleasure or activity to an excessive degree. Carries a negative connotation of being unable to exercise self-control, often used for addictive behaviors like alcohol, gambling, or sensual pleasures.
酒(さけ)に耽溺(たんでき)する。
To be addicted to alcohol.
快楽(かいらく)への耽溺(たんでき)は身(み)を滅(ほろ)ぼす。
Indulgence in pleasure leads to one's ruin.
彼(かれ)は若(わか)い頃(ころ)ギャンブルに耽溺(たんでき)し、多額(たがく)の借金(しゃっきん)を抱(かか)えることになった。
In his youth he was addicted to gambling and ended up with a huge amount of debt.

Composed of 耽(たん) (indulge, be absorbed in) and 溺(でき) (drown, be infatuated). The two characters together paint a vivid picture of drowning in indulgence. This is a literary, somewhat formal word that appears in essays, novels, and cultural criticism rather than casual conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 酒(さけ)に耽溺(たんでき)する: to be addicted to alcohol
  • 快楽(かいらく)に耽溺(たんでき)する: to indulge in pleasure
  • 耽溺(たんでき)的(てき)な生活(せいかつ): a dissipated lifestyle
  • ギャンブルに耽溺(たんでき)する: to be addicted to gambling

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中毒(ちゅうどく): addiction — more clinical and modern; used for substance addiction and figurative "addictions"
  • 溺(おぼ)れる: to drown in; to be infatuated with — more colloquial and versatile
  • 耽(ふけ)る: to be absorbed in; to indulge in — can be neutral or positive, unlike 耽溺(たんでき) which is almost always negative
  • 依存(いぞん): dependence; addiction — the standard modern term for clinical addiction
