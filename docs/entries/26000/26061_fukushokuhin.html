副食品(ふくしょくひん)

ふくしょくひん
noun
side dishes; supplementary food items
1. side dishes; supplementary food items
Foods eaten alongside the staple food (typically rice in Japan). Includes dishes such as fish, meat, vegetables, pickles, and soup that accompany the main grain.
給食(きゅうしょく)の副食品(ふくしょくひん)が充実(じゅうじつ)している。
The side dishes in the school lunch are excellent.
副食品(ふくしょくひん)の値段(ねだん)が上(あ)がっている。
The prices of side dish items are going up.
栄養(えいよう)バランスを考(かんが)えて、主食(しゅしょく)と副食品(ふくしょくひん)を組(く)み合(あ)わせることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to combine staple foods with side dishes while considering nutritional balance.

Composed of 副(ふく) (supplementary, secondary) + 食品(しょくひん) (food items). In traditional Japanese meal structure, food is divided into 主食(しゅしょく) (staple food, usually rice) and 副食(ふくしょく) (everything else). 副食品(ふくしょくひん) is the formal term for these accompanying items.

JAPANESE MEAL STRUCTURE:

  • 主食(しゅしょく): staple food (rice, noodles, bread)
  • 副食(ふくしょく)/副食品(ふくしょくひん): side dishes (fish, meat, vegetables)
  • 汁物(しるもの): soup (miso soup, clear soup)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 副食品(ふくしょくひん)を増(ふ)やす: to increase the variety of side dishes
  • 副食品(ふくしょくひん)の栄養(えいよう): nutrition from side dishes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おかず: side dishes -- the everyday, casual term
  • 副食(ふくしょく): side dishes -- slightly shorter form, same meaning
  • 惣菜(そうざい): prepared dishes -- ready-made side dishes sold at stores
