低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)

ていたいおんしょう
noun
hypothermia
1. hypothermia
A dangerous medical condition in which the body's core temperature drops below the normal range, typically below 35°C. Can occur from prolonged exposure to cold, wet conditions, or immersion in cold water.
遭難者(そうなんしゃ)は低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)で倒(たお)れていた。
The stranded person had collapsed from hypothermia.
冬(ふゆ)の海(うみ)に落(お)ちると、短時間(たんじかん)で低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)になる危険(きけん)がある。
If you fall into the winter sea, there is a risk of developing hypothermia in a short time.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)は室内(しつない)でも低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)にかかることがあるので、暖房(だんぼう)の管理(かんり)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Elderly people can develop hypothermia even indoors, so managing the heating is important.

Composed of 低(てい) (low), 体温(たいおん) (body temperature), and 症(しょう) (condition, disease). A medical term that appears frequently in news reports about outdoor accidents, natural disasters, and winter safety.

In Japan, 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう) is commonly discussed in the context of mountain climbing accidents, where sudden weather changes can catch hikers unprepared. It is also a concern for elderly people living alone, particularly during winter months when indoor heating may be insufficient.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)になる: to develop hypothermia
  • 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)にかかる: to suffer from hypothermia
  • 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)で死亡(しぼう)する: to die of hypothermia
  • 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent hypothermia
  • 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)の症状(しょうじょう): symptoms of hypothermia

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 凍傷(とうしょう): frostbite — localized damage to skin and tissue from freezing, distinct from the systemic cooling of 低体温症(ていたいおんしょう)
  • 低体温(ていたいおん): low body temperature — describes the condition of having a low temperature without necessarily implying a dangerous medical state
  • 熱中症(ねっちゅうしょう): heatstroke — the opposite condition, caused by overheating
