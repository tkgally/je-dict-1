赤(あか)ピーマン

あかぴーまん
noun
red bell pepper; red pepper
1. red bell pepper; red pepper
A mature bell pepper that has ripened to a red color, sweeter than green bell peppers.
サラダに赤(あか)ピーマンを入(い)れた。
I added red bell pepper to the salad.
赤(あか)ピーマンは緑(みどり)のピーマンより甘(あま)い。
Red bell peppers are sweeter than green ones.
赤(あか)ピーマンと黄(き)ピーマンを細切(ほそぎ)りにして彩(いろど)りよく炒(いた)めた。
I sliced the red and yellow bell peppers thinly and stir-fried them for a colorful dish.

Composed of 赤(あか) (red) and ピーマン (bell pepper, from French "piment"). In Japanese supermarkets, the standard green ピーマン is the most common and cheapest variety, while 赤(あか)ピーマン and 黄(き)ピーマン (yellow bell pepper) are often sold at a premium. Note that パプリカ (paprika) refers to the larger, thicker-walled colored bell peppers, while 赤(あか)ピーマン can refer to either a regular-sized red pepper or a paprika.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 赤(あか)ピーマンの千切(せんぎ)り — julienned red bell pepper
  • 赤(あか)ピーマンを炒(いた)める — to stir-fry red bell pepper
  • 赤(あか)ピーマンの肉詰(にくづ)め — stuffed red bell pepper

RELATED TERMS:

  • ピーマン — bell pepper (usually the green variety)
  • パプリカ — paprika; large colored bell pepper
  • 唐辛子(とうがらし) — chili pepper (spicy, not to be confused with bell pepper)
