国名(こくめい)

こくめい
noun
country name; name of a country
1. country name; name of a country
The official or commonly used name of a nation or country. Frequently encountered in forms, documents, maps, and educational materials.
国名(こくめい)を漢字(かんじ)で書(か)いてください。
Please write the country name in kanji.
この地図(ちず)には国名(こくめい)が英語(えいご)と日本語(にほんご)の両方(りょうほう)で表示(ひょうじ)されている。
On this map, country names are displayed in both English and Japanese.
独立後(どくりつご)に国名(こくめい)を変更(へんこう)した国(くに)は少(すく)なくない。
There are not a few countries that changed their country name after independence.

Composed of 国(こく) (country, nation) and 名(めい) (name). A straightforward compound used in contexts where countries are being listed, referenced, or compared by name.

In Japanese, many country names have both a kanji abbreviation (used in formal or traditional contexts) and a katakana rendering (based on the native-language pronunciation). For example, America is 米国(べいこく) in kanji and アメリカ in katakana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国名(こくめい)を書(か)く: to write a country name
  • 国名(こくめい)を変更(へんこう)する: to change a country name
  • 正式(せいしき)国名(こくめい): official country name
  • 略称(りゃくしょう)国名(こくめい): abbreviated country name
  • 国名(こくめい)一覧(いちらん): list of country names

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国号(こくごう): national title — more formal term often used in historical or official contexts for a country's formal designation
  • 国(くに)の名前(なまえ): name of a country — everyday phrasing with the same meaning but less formal than 国名(こくめい)
