ボーイズラブ

ぼーいずらぶ
noun
boys' love; BL; male-male romance genre
1. boys' love; BL; male-male romance genre
A genre of fiction, manga, anime, and other media depicting romantic or sexual relationships between male characters. Primarily created by and for women. Commonly abbreviated as BL.
ボーイズラブの漫画(まんが)を読(よ)んでいる。
I'm reading a boys' love manga.
最近(さいきん)はBLが原作(げんさく)のドラマも増(ふ)えてきた。
Recently, dramas based on BL source material have been increasing.
ボーイズラブは日本(にほん)発祥(はっしょう)のジャンルで、今(いま)では海外(かいがい)でも幅広(はばひろ)いファンを獲得(かくとく)している。
Boys' love is a genre that originated in Japan and has now gained a wide fan base overseas as well.

A wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) term. The English words 'boys' love' are not used this way in English-speaking countries, where the Japanese abbreviation BL is often used instead. The genre has roots in the 少年愛(しょうねんあい) (shōnen-ai) and やおい movements of the 1970s-80s manga scene.

The genre is distinct from real-life LGBTQ+ representation. BL is a fiction genre with its own conventions and is primarily produced by and consumed by women, though readership has diversified.

USAGE:
The abbreviation BL (ビーエル) is far more common in casual speech and writing than the full form ボーイズラブ. The full form appears in more formal or explanatory contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • BL漫画(まんが): BL manga
  • BL小説(しょうせつ): BL novel
  • BLドラマ: BL drama series
  • BL作品(さくひん): BL works

RELATED TERMS:

  • やおい: yaoi — an older term for explicit BL content
  • 腐女子(ふじょし): fujoshi — a female fan of BL (self-deprecating term that has been reclaimed)
  • 百合(ゆり): yuri — the female-female romance equivalent of BL
  • GL (ジーエル): girls' love — the abbreviation for the female-female genre
