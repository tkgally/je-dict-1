1. individual packaging; single-dose packaging; portioned packaging
The practice of dividing and packaging items into individual portions or doses. Most commonly used in pharmacy for dividing medication into single-dose packets, but also applies to food and other products.
薬(くすり)を分包(ぶんぽう)してもらった。
I had my medicine divided into individual packets.
お茶(ちゃ)が一杯(いっぱい)分(ぶん)ずつ分包(ぶんぽう)されていて便利(べんり)だ。
The tea is individually packaged per cup, which is convenient.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)が飲(の)み間違(まちが)えないように、薬局(やっきょく)で薬(くすり)を一回分(いっかいぶん)ずつ分包(ぶんぽう)してくれる。
The pharmacy packages medication into single doses so elderly patients don't take the wrong amount.

Composed of 分(ぶん) (divide, portion) and 包(ぽう) (wrapping, packaging). Most commonly encountered in pharmaceutical contexts, where pharmacists use a 分包機(ぶんぽうき) (dose-packaging machine) to divide medications into individual packets. Each packet is labeled with the time of day it should be taken. Also used in food packaging for single-serving portions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 分包(ぶんぽう)する: to package individually
  • 分包機(ぶんぽうき): dose-packaging machine
  • 一回分(いっかいぶん)分包(ぶんぽう): single-dose packaging

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:
Not to be confused with 文法(ぶんぽう) (grammar), which has the same reading but completely different meaning and kanji.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 個包装(こほうそう): individual wrapping — used more broadly for individually wrapped items like candy or snacks
  • 小分(こわ)け: dividing into small portions — more colloquial and used for food portioning at home
