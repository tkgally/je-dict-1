趣味人(しゅみじん)

しゅみじん
noun
hobbyist; person of refined tastes
1. hobbyist; enthusiast; person of refined tastes
A person who cultivates hobbies or cultural interests with genuine passion and appreciation, often implying good taste and a well-rounded lifestyle.
彼(かれ)は多趣味(たしゅみ)な趣味人(しゅみじん)だ。
He is a hobbyist with many interests.
趣味人(しゅみじん)が集(あつ)まるイベントに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in an event where hobbyists gathered.
祖父(そふ)は茶道(さどう)や陶芸(とうげい)を楽(たの)しむ趣味人(しゅみじん)で、退職後(たいしょくご)の生活(せいかつ)を満喫(まんきつ)している。
My grandfather is a man of refined tastes who enjoys tea ceremony and pottery, fully savoring his retirement.

A compound of 趣味(しゅみ) (hobby; taste) and 人(じん) (person). Carries a positive connotation — it suggests someone who enriches their life through cultural pursuits and hobbies, not merely someone who has a pastime. Often used to describe people who have developed refined aesthetic sensibilities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 趣味人(しゅみじん)の集(つど)い: gathering of hobbyists
  • 真(しん)の趣味人(しゅみじん): a true connoisseur
  • 趣味人(しゅみじん)として知(し)られる: known as a person of refined tastes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 愛好家(あいこうか): enthusiast; aficionado — emphasizes devotion to a specific interest
  • マニア: enthusiast; geek — loanword, sometimes implies obsessive dedication
  • 通(つう): connoisseur; expert — implies deep knowledge in a particular field
