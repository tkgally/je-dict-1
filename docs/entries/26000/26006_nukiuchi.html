抜(ぬ)き打(う)ち

ぬきうち
noun
surprise (inspection/test); unannounced; sudden
1. surprise; unannounced; without prior notice
An action carried out suddenly and without advance warning, typically an inspection, test, or check. Almost always used as a modifier with の or in compound form.
抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちテストがあった。
There was a surprise test.
抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちで検査(けんさ)が行(おこな)われた。
An unannounced inspection was carried out.
保健所(ほけんじょ)が抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちで飲食店(いんしょくてん)を調(しら)べることがある。
The health center sometimes inspects restaurants without prior notice.
先生(せんせい)は抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちの小(しょう)テストが好(す)きだ。
The teacher likes giving pop quizzes.

Originally a kendo term meaning to draw and strike in one motion, 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ち now primarily refers to doing something without advance notice. It is almost always used as a noun modifier: 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちの〜 or 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちで.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちテスト: surprise test; pop quiz
  • 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ち検査(けんさ): surprise inspection
  • 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ちで調(しら)べる: to investigate without notice
  • 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ち監査(かんさ): surprise audit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 突然(とつぜん): sudden — describes any abrupt event, not specifically planned surprise actions
  • 不意(ふい)に: unexpectedly — focuses on the recipient's lack of awareness
  • 予告(よこく)なし: without advance notice — more neutral and explanatory
