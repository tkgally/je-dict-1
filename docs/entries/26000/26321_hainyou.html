1. urination; passing urine
The medical or formal term for the act of urinating. Used in healthcare settings, medical literature, and formal descriptions of bodily functions.
排尿(はいにょう)の回数(かいすう)が増(ふ)えた。
The frequency of urination has increased.
排尿(はいにょう)時(じ)に痛(いた)みがある場合(ばあい)は受診(じゅしん)してください。
If you have pain during urination, please see a doctor.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)の排尿(はいにょう)障害(しょうがい)は生活(せいかつ)の質(しつ)に大(おお)きく影響(えいきょう)するため、早(はや)めに専門医(せんもんい)に相談(そうだん)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Urinary disorders in the elderly greatly affect quality of life, so it is important to consult a specialist early.

Composed of 排(はい) (expel, discharge) and 尿(にょう) (urine). A formal, medical term — in everyday conversation, Japanese speakers use トイレに行(い)く (to go to the bathroom) or 小(しょう)をする (to urinate, colloquial). 排尿(はいにょう) appears on medical questionnaires, in health articles, and in clinical discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 排尿(はいにょう)障害(しょうがい): urinary disorder
  • 排尿(はいにょう)困難(こんなん): difficulty urinating
  • 排尿(はいにょう)回数(かいすう): urination frequency
  • 排尿(はいにょう)痛(つう): pain during urination
  • 排尿(はいにょう)を促(うなが)す: to promote urination

RELATED TERMS:

  • 排便(はいべん): defecation — the corresponding term for bowel movements
  • 頻尿(ひんにょう): frequent urination — a common urological complaint
  • 尿(にょう): urine — the substance itself
