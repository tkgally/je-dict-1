時間制限(じかんせいげん)

じかんせいげん
noun
time limit; time restriction
1. time limit; time restriction
A constraint on how much time is allowed for an activity, stay, or process. Commonly applied to dining, parking, exams, and various services.
この試験(しけん)には時間制限(じかんせいげん)がある。
This exam has a time limit.
食(た)べ放題(ほうだい)は90分(ぷん)の時間制限(じかんせいげん)です。
The all-you-can-eat has a 90-minute time limit.
時間制限(じかんせいげん)がないと、だらだらと仕事(しごと)をしてしまいがちだ。
Without a time limit, people tend to work in a lazy, drawn-out way.

Composed of 時間(じかん) (time) and 制限(せいげん) (limit, restriction). A very practical term encountered frequently in everyday Japanese life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間制限(じかんせいげん)がある / ない — there is / is no time limit
  • 時間制限(じかんせいげん)を設(もう)ける — to set a time limit
  • 時間制限(じかんせいげん)付(つ)き — with a time limit
  • 時間制限(じかんせいげん)内(ない)に — within the time limit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 制限時間(せいげんじかん) — time limit (same meaning, reversed word order; more commonly used for exams and competitions)
  • 期限(きげん) — deadline, expiration date (for submissions, payments, products)
  • 締(し)め切(き)り — deadline (especially for work submissions)

NOTE ON WORD ORDER:
時間制限(じかんせいげん) and 制限時間(せいげんじかん) are often interchangeable, but 制限時間(せいげんじかん) tends to emphasize the specific allotted time ("the allotted time is 60 minutes"), while 時間制限(じかんせいげん) emphasizes the existence of the restriction itself ("there is a time limit").
