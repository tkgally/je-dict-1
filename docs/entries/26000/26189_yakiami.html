焼(や)き網(あみ)

やきあみ
noun
grilling net; grill rack; wire grill
1. grilling net; grill rack; wire grill
A wire mesh or metal grid used for grilling food over a flame or heat source.
焼(や)き網(あみ)の上(うえ)に肉(にく)を載(の)せた。
I placed the meat on the grilling net.
バーベキューの前(まえ)に焼(や)き網(あみ)を油(あぶら)で塗(ぬ)っておく。
Before the barbecue, oil the grilling net in advance.
使(つか)い終(お)わった焼(や)き網(あみ)は熱(あつ)いうちにブラシで汚(よご)れを落(お)とすとよい。
It's best to scrub the grill net with a brush while it's still hot after use.

A kitchen and outdoor cooking tool, essential for Japanese-style grilling such as 焼(や)き魚(ざかな) (grilled fish) and yakiniku. Also commonly used at barbecues and camping.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焼(や)き網(あみ)に載(の)せる — to place on the grill
  • 焼(や)き網(あみ)を洗(あら)う — to wash the grill net
  • 焼(や)き網(あみ)を交換(こうかん)する — to replace the grill rack
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)て焼(や)き網(あみ) — disposable grill net

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 網(あみ) — net (general term)
  • 鉄板(てっぱん) — iron plate (for teppanyaki-style grilling, flat surface rather than mesh)
