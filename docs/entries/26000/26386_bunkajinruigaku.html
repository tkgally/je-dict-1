文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)

ぶんかじんるいがく
noun
cultural anthropology
1. cultural anthropology
The branch of anthropology that studies human cultures, social structures, beliefs, and practices through fieldwork and comparative analysis.
文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)を専攻(せんこう)している。
I'm majoring in cultural anthropology.
文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)のフィールドワークでインドネシアに行(い)った。
I went to Indonesia for cultural anthropology fieldwork.
食文化(しょくぶんか)の違(ちが)いを文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)の視点(してん)から分析(ぶんせき)する論文(ろんぶん)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a paper analyzing differences in food culture from a cultural anthropology perspective.

Composed of 文化(ぶんか) (culture) and 人類学(じんるいがく) (anthropology). Cultural anthropology is a major academic discipline at Japanese universities. Key Japanese scholars include 梅棹忠夫(うめさおただお) and 川喜田二郎(かわきたじろう), whose KJ method for organizing fieldwork data is widely used in business.

In Japan, the term 民族学(みんぞくがく) (ethnology) was traditionally more common in academic circles, but 文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく) has become the standard label for university departments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)を学(まな)ぶ: to study cultural anthropology
  • 文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)者(しゃ): cultural anthropologist
  • 文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)の視点(してん): a cultural anthropology perspective
  • 文化人類学(ぶんかじんるいがく)入門(にゅうもん): introduction to cultural anthropology

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 社会人類学(しゃかいじんるいがく): social anthropology — closely related field, emphasis on social structures
  • 民族学(みんぞくがく): ethnology — older Japanese term for the comparative study of peoples
  • 人類学(じんるいがく): anthropology — the broader discipline
