醤油焼(しょうゆや)き

しょうゆやき
noun
soy sauce grilling; grilled with soy sauce
1. soy sauce grilling; grilled with soy sauce
A cooking method where ingredients are grilled or pan-fried with soy sauce as the primary seasoning. Also refers to dishes prepared this way. The soy sauce caramelizes during cooking, creating a savory, slightly sweet glaze.
餅(もち)の醤油焼(しょうゆや)きが好(す)きだ。
I like mochi grilled with soy sauce.
鶏肉(とりにく)を醤油焼(しょうゆや)きにして弁当(べんとう)に入(い)れた。
I grilled chicken with soy sauce and put it in my lunch box.
秋刀魚(さんま)の醤油焼(しょうゆや)きは、香(かお)ばしい匂(にお)いが食欲(しょくよく)をそそる。
The savory aroma of soy sauce-grilled sanma whets the appetite.

Composed of 醤油(しょうゆ) (soy sauce) and 焼(や)き (grilling, frying). One of the most basic and common Japanese cooking methods, found in home cooking, bento preparation, and restaurant menus.

The method is simple: brush or pour soy sauce over the ingredient during grilling or pan-frying. The heat causes the soy sauce to caramelize, producing the characteristic aromatic glaze. Sometimes 味醂(みりん) (mirin) or 砂糖(さとう) (sugar) is added for sweetness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 醤油焼(しょうゆや)きにする: to grill with soy sauce
  • 餅(もち)の醤油焼(しょうゆや)き: mochi grilled with soy sauce
  • 魚(さかな)の醤油焼(しょうゆや)き: fish grilled with soy sauce
  • 鶏(とり)の醤油焼(しょうゆや)き: chicken grilled with soy sauce

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 塩焼(しおや)き: salt grilling — grilling seasoned with salt instead of soy sauce; the most basic grilling method for fish
  • 照(て)り焼(や)き: teriyaki — soy sauce-based glaze with mirin and sugar, producing a thicker, shinier coating than plain 醤油焼(しょうゆや)き
  • 味噌焼(みそや)き: miso grilling — grilling with miso paste as the seasoning
  • 素焼(すや)き: plain grilling — grilling without any seasoning
