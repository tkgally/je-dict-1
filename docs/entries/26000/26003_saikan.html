1. republication; reissue; reprinting
Publishing a book, magazine, or other printed work again after it has gone out of print or after the original publication ceased. Differs from a simple reprint in that it often implies a gap in availability.
絶版(ぜっぱん)になった本(ほん)が再刊(さいかん)された。
The out-of-print book was republished.
読者(どくしゃ)の要望(ようぼう)に応(こた)えて再刊(さいかん)が決定(けってい)した。
In response to reader demand, republication was decided.
戦前(せんぜん)に廃刊(はいかん)となった雑誌(ざっし)が、新(あたら)しい編集部(へんしゅうぶ)のもとで再刊(さいかん)されることになった。
The magazine, which ceased publication before the war, is to be reissued under a new editorial team.

Composed of 再(さい) (again) and 刊(かん) (publish, print). Used in the publishing industry for works that return to availability after a period of being unavailable. This can apply to books that went out of print, magazines that ceased publication, or periodicals that were discontinued and later revived.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再刊(さいかん)する: to republish
  • 再刊(さいかん)が決(き)まる: republication is decided
  • 再刊(さいかん)を望(のぞ)む: to hope for republication
  • 再刊(さいかん)記念(きねん): republication commemoration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 復刊(ふっかん): revival of a publication — very similar, but often implies stronger demand or cultural significance behind the revival
  • 重版(じゅうはん): additional printing — a reprint of a book still in print due to demand; not the same as republication
  • 増刷(ぞうさつ): additional printing run — essentially the same as 重版(じゅうはん)
  • 絶版(ぜっぱん): out of print — the condition that precedes 再刊(さいかん)
  • 廃刊(はいかん): cessation of publication — permanent discontinuation of a periodical
