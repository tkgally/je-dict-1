懸案事項(けんあんじこう)

けんあんじこう
noun
pending matter; outstanding issue
1. pending matter; outstanding issue; unresolved item
A matter or issue that remains unresolved and requires attention or a decision. Commonly used in business, government, and organizational contexts for items that need to be addressed.
懸案事項(けんあんじこう)を確認(かくにん)する。
Let's review the pending matters.
会議(かいぎ)で懸案事項(けんあんじこう)について話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed the outstanding issues at the meeting.
長年(ながねん)の懸案事項(けんあんじこう)であった法案(ほうあん)が、ようやく可決(かけつ)された。
The bill, which had been a long-standing issue, was finally passed.

Composed of 懸案(けんあん) (pending problem; matter of concern) and 事項(じこう) (matter; item). A formal compound frequently used in meeting agendas, reports, and official documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懸案事項(けんあんじこう)の解決(かいけつ): resolution of outstanding issues
  • 懸案事項(けんあんじこう)リスト: list of pending matters
  • 長年(ながねん)の懸案事項(けんあんじこう): long-standing issue
  • 主(おも)な懸案事項(けんあんじこう): major outstanding issues
  • 懸案事項(けんあんじこう)を整理(せいり)する: to organize pending items

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 懸案(けんあん): pending matter — the shorter, more concise form; 懸案事項(けんあんじこう) is more formal and explicit
  • 課題(かだい): issue; challenge; task — broader in scope and less formal; can refer to problems, assignments, or goals
  • 問題点(もんだいてん): problem area; issue — focuses on what is problematic rather than what is unresolved
  • 未解決(みかいけつ): unresolved — emphasizes the lack of resolution rather than the need for action
