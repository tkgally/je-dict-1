牌(ぱい)

ぱい
noun
mahjong tile
1. mahjong tile; tile
A playing piece used in mahjong. A standard set contains 136 tiles divided into suits (萬子, 筒子, 索子) and honor tiles (字牌). Also written as 牌 in other tile-based games.
牌(ぱい)を切(き)る。
To discard a tile.
牌(ぱい)を混(ま)ぜてから配(くば)る。
Shuffle the tiles and then deal them.
手(て)牌(ぱい)の中(なか)から不要(ふよう)な牌(ぱい)を選(えら)んで捨(す)てる。
Choose an unnecessary tile from your hand and discard it.

In mahjong terminology, 牌(ぱい) appears in many compounds: 手牌(てはい) (tiles in one's hand), 字牌(じはい)/つーぱい} (honor tiles), {萬子(まんず)/筒子(ぴんず)/索子(そーず) (the three number suits). Key verbs used with tiles: 牌(ぱい)を切(き)る (to discard), 牌(ぱい)を引(ひ)く (to draw), 牌(ぱい)を鳴(な)く (to call a discarded tile). Mahjong is extremely popular in Japan both as a social game and as a subject of manga and anime.
