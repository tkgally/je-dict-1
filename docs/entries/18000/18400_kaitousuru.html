1. to reply; to answer; to respond (to a question, survey, or inquiry)
To provide an answer or response, especially to a formal question, survey, questionnaire, or official inquiry. More formal than 答える and implies a considered or official response.
アンケートに回答(かいとう)する。
To respond to a survey.
質問(しつもん)に正確(せいかく)に回答(かいとう)してください。
Please answer the questions accurately.
企業(きぎょう)は顧客(こきゃく)からの問(と)い合(あ)わせに対(たい)して迅速(じんそく)に回答(かいとう)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
Companies have an obligation to respond promptly to customer inquiries.

PATTERN:
～に回答(かいとう)する (to respond to ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アンケートに回答(かいとう)する (to respond to a survey)
  • 質問(しつもん)に回答(かいとう)する (to answer a question)
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせに回答(かいとう)する (to respond to an inquiry)
  • 回答(かいとう)を求(もと)める (to request a response)
  • 回答(かいとう)期限(きげん) (response deadline)

CONTRAST:

  • 回答(かいとう)する vs. 答(こた)える: 答(こた)える is the general word for answering and is used in everyday conversation. 回答(かいとう)する is more formal and implies an official, deliberate, or written response.
  • 回答(かいとう) vs. 返答(へんとう): 返答(へんとう) emphasizes the act of replying (returning an answer), while 回答(かいとう) emphasizes the content and correctness of the answer.
  • 回答(かいとう) vs. 解答(かいとう): Same pronunciation but different kanji. 解答(かいとう) means solving a problem or providing the solution to a test/quiz. 回答(かいとう) is a response to a question or inquiry.
