展望台(てんぼうだい)

てんぼうだい
noun
observation deck; lookout point
1. observation deck; lookout point; viewing platform
A platform or area, usually at a high elevation, designed for people to enjoy a panoramic view. Found on tall buildings, mountains, and scenic spots.
展望台(てんぼうだい)からの景色(けしき)は素晴(すば)らしかった。
The view from the observation deck was wonderful.
タワーの展望台(てんぼうだい)に上(のぼ)って、街(まち)を見下(みお)ろした。
I went up to the tower's observation deck and looked down at the city.
天気(てんき)がよければ、この展望台(てんぼうだい)から富士山(ふじさん)が見(み)えることもある。
On a clear day, you can sometimes see Mt. Fuji from this observation deck.

USAGE:
A compound of 展望(てんぼう) (view, outlook) + 台(だい) (platform, stand). A staple of sightseeing in Japan, found at landmarks like Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, and mountain summits.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 展望台(てんぼうだい)に上(のぼ)る (to go up to the observation deck)
  • 展望台(てんぼうだい)からの眺(なが)め (the view from the observation deck)
  • 展望台(てんぼうだい)が人気(にんき) (the observation deck is popular)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 展望(てんぼう)ロビー: observation lobby — an enclosed viewing area
  • 見晴(みは)らし台(だい): lookout — a simpler, often outdoor viewpoint
