1. to leave as is; to keep unchanged; to hold at the current level
To maintain something at its current state or level without changing it. Commonly used for prices, interest rates, taxes, and other quantitative measures that could be raised or lowered.
金利(きんり)を据(す)え置(お)く。
To keep interest rates unchanged.
今年度(こんねんど)は授業料(じゅぎょうりょう)を据(す)え置(お)くことが決(き)まった。
It was decided to keep tuition fees unchanged this fiscal year.
日本銀行(にほんぎんこう)は政策(せいさく)金利(きんり)を現行(げんこう)の水準(すいじゅん)に据(す)え置(お)くことを全会(ぜんかい)一致(いっち)で決定(けってい)した。
The Bank of Japan unanimously decided to keep the policy interest rate at its current level.

CONJUGATION:
Godan verb (か row): 据(す)え置(お)く・据(す)え置(お)きます
て-form: 据(す)え置(お)いて
た-form: 据(す)え置(お)いた

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 金利(きんり)を据(す)え置(お)く (to hold interest rates steady)
  • 価格(かかく)を据(す)え置(お)く (to keep prices unchanged)
  • 税率(ぜいりつ)を据(す)え置(お)く (to maintain the tax rate)
  • 据(す)え置(お)き期間(きかん) (grace period; deferment period)

RELATED FORMS:

  • 据(す)え置(お)き (noun form) — often used in compound phrases like 据(す)え置(お)き型(がた) (stationary type) or 据(す)え置(お)き期間(きかん) (deferment period).

USAGE:
Primarily used in financial, economic, and policy contexts. Very common in news reporting about central bank decisions and pricing.
