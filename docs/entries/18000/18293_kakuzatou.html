角砂糖(かくざとう)

かくざとう
noun
sugar cube
1. sugar cube
Sugar pressed into small cube shapes for convenient use in drinks like coffee and tea.
コーヒーに角砂糖(かくざとう)を入(い)れる。
I put a sugar cube in my coffee.
角砂糖(かくざとう)は何個(なんこ)入(い)れますか。
How many sugar cubes would you like?
ジュース一本(いっぽん)に角砂糖(かくざとう)十個分(じゅっこぶん)の砂糖(さとう)が入(はい)っていると聞(き)いて驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised to hear that one bottle of juice contains the equivalent of ten sugar cubes of sugar.

USAGE:
A compound of 角(かく) (square, cube) + 砂糖(さとう) (sugar). Sugar cubes are often used as a unit of measurement to illustrate how much sugar is in food and drinks. Counted with 個(こ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 角砂糖(かくざとう)を入(い)れる (to add a sugar cube)
  • 角砂糖(かくざとう)～個分(こぶん) (equivalent to ~ sugar cubes)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 黒砂糖(くろざとう): brown sugar — unrefined sugar with a dark color
  • 白砂糖(しろざとう): white sugar — standard refined granulated sugar
  • グラニュー糖(とう): granulated sugar — fine white sugar for baking
