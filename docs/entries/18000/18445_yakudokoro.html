役(やく)どころ

やくどころ
noun
role; part (in a play or situation)
1. role; part (in a play, film, or performance)
The specific character or part that an actor plays. Implies the nature and importance of the role.
難(むずか)しい役(やく)どころだ。
It's a difficult role.
今回(こんかい)の映画(えいが)では悪役(あくやく)の役(やく)どころだ。
In this movie, I play the villain's role.
あの俳優(はいゆう)は主人公(しゅじんこう)の親友(しんゆう)という重要(じゅうよう)な役(やく)どころを演(えん)じた。
That actor played the important role of the protagonist's best friend.
2. role; function; part to play (in a situation)
One's role or expected function in a group, project, or situation. Used figuratively beyond the performing arts.
チームでの自分(じぶん)の役(やく)どころを考(かんが)えた。
I thought about my role on the team.
この会議(かいぎ)での私(わたし)の役(やく)どころは進行(しんこう)だ。
My role in this meeting is to facilitate.
彼(かれ)はプロジェクトの中(なか)で調整役(ちょうせいやく)という役(やく)どころを担(にな)っている。
He plays the role of coordinator in the project.

役(やく)どころ originally comes from theater and film but is commonly used in everyday contexts to talk about one's function or expected contribution.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 役(やく)どころを演(えん)じる — to play a role
  • 役(やく)どころを担(にな)う — to take on a role
  • 重要(じゅうよう)な役(やく)どころ — an important role
  • 難(むずか)しい役(やく)どころ — a difficult role

RELATED WORDS:

  • 役割(やくわり) — role, function
  • 配役(はいやく) — casting, role assignment
  • 出番(でばん) — one's turn to appear
