1. presentation; giving (as a gift); offering
A humble expression for giving someone a gift or present. The speaker lowers themselves by using this word, making it appropriate when offering something to a superior, customer, or someone deserving respect.
粗品(そしな)を進呈(しんてい)いたします。
We would like to present you with a small gift.
ご来場(らいじょう)の方(かた)に記念品(きねんひん)を進呈(しんてい)します。
We will present a commemorative gift to those who attend.
アンケートに回答(かいとう)いただいた方(かた)に、もれなく図書(としょ)カードを進呈(しんてい)いたします。
We will present a book gift card to everyone who responds to the survey.

USAGE:
A humble (謙譲語(けんじょうご)) expression used by the giver. Very common in commercial and promotional contexts where a business gives something to customers. Not used when receiving a gift.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記念品(きねんひん)を進呈(しんてい)する (to present a commemorative gift)
  • 粗品(そしな)を進呈(しんてい)する (to present a small gift)
  • もれなく進呈(しんてい) (given to everyone without exception)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 贈呈(ぞうてい): presentation; bestowal — more formal, used for awards and ceremonies
  • 贈(おく)る: to give; to present — general term, less formal
  • 差(さ)し上(あ)げる: to give (humble) — spoken humble form, used in daily polite conversation
