噛(か)み砕(くだ)く

かみくだく
verb
to crunch; to break down into simple terms
1. to crunch; to chew up; to bite into pieces
To break food or other objects into small pieces by biting. The literal meaning of chewing something hard until it breaks apart.
氷(こおり)を噛(か)み砕(くだ)く。
To crunch ice.
硬(かた)いせんべいを噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて食(た)べた。
I crunched up the hard rice cracker and ate it.
薬(くすり)を噛(か)み砕(くだ)かずに水(みず)で飲(の)み込(こ)んでください。
Please swallow the medicine with water without chewing it.
2. to explain in simple terms; to break down (an explanation)
To make a complex topic easy to understand by simplifying it. A figurative extension of the literal meaning.
噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて説明(せつめい)する。
To explain in simple terms.
先生(せんせい)は難(むずか)しい理論(りろん)を噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて教(おし)えてくれた。
The teacher broke down the difficult theory into simple terms for us.
専門用語(せんもんようご)を使(つか)わず、子供(こども)にも分(わ)かるように噛(か)み砕(くだ)いた説明(せつめい)を心(こころ)がけている。
I try to give explanations broken down simply enough for even children to understand, without using technical terms.

CONJUGATION:
Godan verb (か row): 噛(か)み砕(くだ)く・噛(か)み砕(くだ)きます
て-form: 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて
た-form: 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いた

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて説明(せつめい)する (to explain in plain language)
  • 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いた表現(ひょうげん) (simplified expression)
  • 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて言(い)うと (to put it simply)

USAGE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is very common and is a natural metaphor in Japanese. The te-form 噛(か)み砕(くだ)いて is especially frequent as an adverbial modifier before verbs of explanation.
