1. application; filing (for admission, a patent, or a license)
The formal act of submitting an application, most commonly for university admission or patent registration. More formal and specific than 申し込み.
大学(だいがく)に出願(しゅつがん)した。
I applied to a university.
特許(とっきょ)の出願(しゅつがん)手続(てつづ)きを進(すす)めている。
We are proceeding with the patent application process.
出願(しゅつがん)締(し)め切(き)りは来月(らいげつ)の十五日(じゅうごにち)までです。
The application deadline is the fifteenth of next month.

USAGE:
Primarily used in two contexts: university/school admissions and patent/trademark filings. For general applications (job, membership), 申(もう)し込(こ)み is more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出願(しゅつがん)する (to apply, to file)
  • 出願(しゅつがん)書類(しょるい) (application documents)
  • 出願(しゅつがん)締(し)め切(き)り (application deadline)
  • 出願(しゅつがん)資格(しかく) (eligibility to apply)
  • 特許(とっきょ)出願(しゅつがん) (patent application)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み: application — broader and more general
  • 志願(しがん): volunteering, applying — implies personal desire, used for military or competitive contexts
  • 応募(おうぼ): applying (for a job, contest) — in response to a public call
