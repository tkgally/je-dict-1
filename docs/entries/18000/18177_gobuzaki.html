五分咲(ごぶざ)き

ごぶざき
noun
half-bloom; 50% in bloom
1. half-bloom; 50% in bloom
A stage in the blooming of flowers, especially cherry blossoms, where about half the buds have opened. Part of a scale used in Japanese weather reports and cherry blossom forecasts to describe the progress of blooming.
桜(さくら)はまだ五分咲(ごぶざ)きだ。
The cherry blossoms are only at half-bloom.
公園(こうえん)の桜(さくら)が五分咲(ごぶざ)きになったので花見(はなみ)に行(い)こう。
The cherry blossoms in the park have reached half-bloom, so let's go see them.
天気(てんき)予報(よほう)によると、今週末(こんしゅうまつ)には五分咲(ごぶざ)きから満開(まんかい)になる見込(みこ)みだ。
According to the weather forecast, it's expected to go from half-bloom to full bloom this weekend.

BLOOMING SCALE:
Japanese cherry blossom reports use a scale to describe bloom progress: 一分咲(いちぶざ)き (10%) → 三分咲(さんぶざ)き (30%) → 五分咲(ごぶざ)き (50%) → 七分咲(しちぶざ)き (70%) → 満開(まんかい) (full bloom) → 散(ち)り始(はじ)め (starting to fall). This scale is widely used in cherry blossom forecasts (桜(さくら)前線(ぜんせん)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 五分咲(ごぶざ)きの桜(さくら) (cherry blossoms at half-bloom)
  • 五分咲(ごぶざ)きを迎(むか)える (to reach half-bloom)
  • まだ五分咲(ごぶざ)き (still only half in bloom)
