～やすい

やすい
suffix
easy to ~; tend to ~
1. easy to ~
Attaches to the stem of a verb (masu-stem) to indicate that the action is easy to perform. The resulting word conjugates as an i-adjective.
この本(ほん)は読(よ)みやすい。
This book is easy to read.
説明(せつめい)が分(わ)かりやすくて助(たす)かった。
The explanation was easy to understand, which was helpful.
この道具(どうぐ)は子供(こども)でも使(つか)いやすいように設計(せっけい)されている。
This tool is designed to be easy to use even for children.
2. tend to ~; prone to ~
Indicates a natural tendency or susceptibility toward the action. Often used to describe inherent characteristics of people or things.
ガラスは割(わ)れやすい。
Glass breaks easily.
彼(かれ)は風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)きやすい体質(たいしつ)だ。
He has a constitution that's prone to catching colds.
この地域(ちいき)は地震(じしん)のとき液状化(えきじょうか)が起(お)きやすい土地(とち)だと言(い)われている。
This area is said to be land that is prone to liquefaction during earthquakes.

FORMATION:

Attaches to the masu-stem (連用形(れんようけい)) of verbs:

  • 読(よ)む → 読(よ)み + やすい → 読(よ)みやすい
  • 食(た)べる → 食(た)べ + やすい → 食(た)べやすい
  • する → し + やすい → しやすい

CONJUGATION:

The resulting word conjugates as an i-adjective:

  • 読(よ)みやすくない (not easy to read)
  • 読(よ)みやすかった (was easy to read)
  • 読(よ)みやすくて (easy to read, and...)

CONTRAST:

  • ～やすい vs. ～にくい: ～にくい is the opposite, meaning "hard to do." 読(よ)みやすい (easy to read) vs. 読(よ)みにくい (hard to read).
  • ～やすい vs. ～づらい: ～づらい also means "hard to do" but implies psychological or emotional difficulty, while ～にくい is more neutral.
