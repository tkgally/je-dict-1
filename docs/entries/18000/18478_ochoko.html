お猪口(ちょこ)

おちょこ
noun
small sake cup
1. small sake cup
A small, typically ceramic cup used for drinking sake. Usually holds about 30-50 ml. A staple item at izakaya and traditional Japanese restaurants.
お猪口(ちょこ)で日本酒(にほんしゅ)を飲(の)む。
To drink sake from an ochoko cup.
このお猪口(ちょこ)は有田焼(ありたやき)です。
This sake cup is Arita ware.
祖父(そふ)は毎晩(まいばん)お猪口(ちょこ)に二(に)杯(はい)だけ日本酒(にほんしゅ)を飲(の)むのが習慣(しゅうかん)だった。
My grandfather's habit was to drink just two small cups of sake every evening.

The お is an honorific prefix and is almost always included. Without it, 猪口(ちょこ) alone is rarely used. Distinct from 盃(さかずき) (a flat, saucer-shaped sake cup used in ceremonies) and 枡(ます) (a square wooden box sometimes used for sake). お猪口(ちょこ) comes in many styles and is a popular souvenir item.
