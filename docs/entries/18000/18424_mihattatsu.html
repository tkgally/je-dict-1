未発達(みはったつ)

みはったつ
noun, adjective (na)
undeveloped; immature; underdeveloped
1. undeveloped; immature; underdeveloped
Not yet fully developed or matured. Used for physical growth, technology, infrastructure, or cognitive/emotional development.
子供(こども)の脳(のう)はまだ未発達(みはったつ)だ。
A child's brain is still undeveloped.
交通(こうつう)インフラが未発達(みはったつ)な地域(ちいき)がある。
There are areas where transportation infrastructure is underdeveloped.
技術(ぎじゅつ)がまだ未発達(みはったつ)だった時代(じだい)は、多(おお)くのことが手作業(てさぎょう)で行(おこな)われていた。
In an era when technology was still undeveloped, many things were done by hand.

未発達(みはったつ) uses the prefix 未(み) (not yet) + 発達(はったつ) (development), indicating something that has not yet reached its full potential.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 未発達(みはったつ)な地域(ちいき) — underdeveloped area
  • 未発達(みはったつ)な段階(だんかい) — undeveloped stage
  • まだ未発達(みはったつ) — still undeveloped

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 未熟(みじゅく) — immature, inexperienced (often about skills or people)
  • 未発達(みはったつ) — undeveloped (neutral; about systems, organs, regions)
  • 低開発(ていかいはつ) — underdeveloped (older term, mainly for economies)
