加速器(かそくき)

かそくき
noun
accelerator; particle accelerator
1. accelerator; particle accelerator
A device that uses electromagnetic fields to propel charged particles to high speeds and energies. Used in physics research, medical treatment, and industrial applications.
加速器(かそくき)で粒子(りゅうし)を衝突(しょうとつ)させる。
To collide particles using an accelerator.
新(あたら)しい加速器(かそくき)の建設(けんせつ)が計画(けいかく)されている。
The construction of a new accelerator is being planned.
医療用(いりょうよう)加速器(かそくき)は、がんの放射線(ほうしゃせん)治療(ちりょう)にも使(つか)われている。
Medical accelerators are also used in radiation therapy for cancer treatment.

加速器(かそくき) is a technical term for a machine that accelerates charged particles to high speeds. It is most commonly encountered in physics and medical contexts.

FULL FORM:
The full term is 粒子(りゅうし)加速器(かそくき) (particle accelerator), but 加速器(かそくき) alone is commonly used.

TYPES:

  • 線形(せんけい)加速器(かそくき): linear accelerator (linac)
  • 円形(えんけい)加速器(かそくき): circular accelerator
  • 医療用(いりょうよう)加速器(かそくき): medical accelerator (used in radiation therapy)

WORD FORMATION:
加速(かそく) (acceleration) + 器(き) (device/instrument). The suffix 器(き) is productive for naming instruments and devices.

JAPAN CONTEXT:
Japan has major accelerator facilities, including KEK (高(こう)エネルギー加速器(かそくき)研究(けんきゅう)機構(きこう)) in Tsukuba.
