丙(へい)

へい
noun
third (in a ranking); C grade
1. third (in a series), C (in a grading system)
The third of the ten Heavenly Stems (天干), used as an ordinal marker meaning 'third' in traditional ranking systems. Equivalent to 'C' in Western A/B/C grading. Part of the sequence 甲 (A) → 乙 (B) → 丙 (C).
成績(せいせき)は丙(へい)だった。
The grade was C.
甲(こう)・乙(おつ)・丙(へい)の順(じゅん)に評価(ひょうか)される。
They are rated in order of A, B, and C.
契約書(けいやくしょ)では甲(こう)が売主(うりぬし)、乙(おつ)が買主(かいぬし)、丙(へい)が仲介者(ちゅうかいしゃ)を指(さ)す。
In the contract, Party A is the seller, Party B is the buyer, and Party C is the intermediary.

USAGE:
Part of the traditional ranking sequence: 甲(こう) (first/A), 乙(おつ) (second/B), 丙(へい) (third/C). Used in legal documents to label parties, in old grading systems, and in traditional contexts. Less common in modern everyday use, where A/B/C or numerical grades are preferred.

TEN HEAVENLY STEMS:
丙(へい) is the third of the 十干(じっかん) (Ten Heavenly Stems): 甲(こう)、乙(おつ)、丙(へい)、丁(てい)、戊(ぼ)、己(き)、庚(こう)、辛(しん)、壬(じん)、癸(き).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 甲(こう)乙(おつ)丙(へい): A, B, C (ranking series)
  • 丙(へい)種(しゅ): C-class, third category
