自制心(じせいしん)

じせいしん
noun
self-control; self-restraint
1. self-control; self-restraint; self-discipline
The ability to control one's emotions, impulses, and desires. A valued personal quality in both personal and professional settings.
自制心(じせいしん)が強(つよ)い人(ひと)だ。
He is a person with strong self-control.
怒(おこ)りそうになったが、自制心(じせいしん)を保(たも)った。
I was about to get angry, but I maintained my self-control.
ダイエット中(ちゅう)は自制心(じせいしん)が試(ため)されるが、甘(あま)いものを見(み)ると負(ま)けてしまう。
While on a diet, your self-control is tested, but I lose when I see sweets.

USAGE:
Often discussed in the context of personal development, education, and emotional management. A lack of 自制心(じせいしん) is seen as a character flaw.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自制心(じせいしん)が強(つよ)い (to have strong self-control)
  • 自制心(じせいしん)を保(たも)つ (to maintain self-control)
  • 自制心(じせいしん)を失(うしな)う (to lose self-control)
  • 自制心(じせいしん)が働(はたら)く (self-control kicks in)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 忍耐力(にんたいりょく): perseverance; endurance — focuses on tolerating hardship over time
  • 我慢(がまん): patience; endurance — more everyday term for holding back or tolerating something
