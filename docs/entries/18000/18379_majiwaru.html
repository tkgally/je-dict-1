1. to cross; to intersect
For lines, roads, or paths to cross each other at a point. Used for physical intersections.
ここで二(ふた)つの道(みち)が交(まじ)わる。
Two roads cross here.
平行線(へいこうせん)は決(けっ)して交(まじ)わらない。
Parallel lines never intersect.
視線(しせん)が交(まじ)わった瞬間(しゅんかん)、彼(かれ)は目(め)をそらした。
The moment their eyes met, he looked away.
2. to associate with; to have relations with
To interact or socialize with people. Often used in somewhat formal or literary contexts to describe personal or social relationships.
人(ひと)と交(まじ)わるのが苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm bad at socializing with people.
留学(りゅうがく)で様々(さまざま)な国(くに)の人(ひと)と交(まじ)わる機会(きかい)を得(え)た。
Through studying abroad, I got the chance to interact with people from various countries.
朱(しゅ)に交(まじ)われば赤(あか)くなる。
If you mix with vermilion, you turn red. (You become like those you associate with.)
Well-known proverb.

Godan verb (ら row): 交(まじ)わる・交(まじ)わります
て-form: 交(まじ)わって
た-form: 交(まじ)わった

PROVERB:
朱(しゅ)に交(まじ)われば赤(あか)くなる — "If you mix with vermilion, you become red." Equivalent to "You are the company you keep" or "Bad company corrupts good character."

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交差(こうさ)する: To intersect — more technical and objective. Used for roads and geometry.
  • 付(つ)き合(あ)う: To associate with, to date — more common in everyday speech for personal relationships.
  • 交(ま)じる: To mix in with, to be blended — emphasizes becoming part of a group rather than mutual interaction.
