1. cleaning by wiping; wiping down surfaces
The act of cleaning floors, tables, or other surfaces by wiping them with a cloth, usually a damp one. A traditional Japanese cleaning method, especially for wooden floors.
毎朝(まいあさ)拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ)をする。
I wipe down the floors every morning.
雑巾(ぞうきん)で廊下(ろうか)の拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ)をした。
I wiped the hallway floor with a rag.
子(こ)どもたちは教室(きょうしつ)の拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ)を担当(たんとう)している。
The children are in charge of wiping down the classroom.

USAGE:
In Japanese schools, students traditionally clean their own classrooms, and 拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ) of the floors with a 雑巾(ぞうきん) (cleaning rag) is a common task. This type of cleaning is contrasted with 掃(は)き掃除(そうじ) (sweeping).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 床(ゆか)の拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ) (wiping the floor)
  • 雑巾(ぞうきん)で拭(ふ)き掃除(そうじ)をする (to wipe-clean with a rag)
  • 水拭(みずぶ)き (wet wiping) / 乾拭(からぶ)き (dry wiping)
