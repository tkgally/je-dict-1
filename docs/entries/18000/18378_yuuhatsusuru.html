誘発(ゆうはつ)する

ゆうはつする
verb
to trigger; to induce; to cause
1. to trigger; to induce; to cause; to provoke
To cause something (usually negative) to occur as a consequence. Often used in medical, scientific, and news contexts for events that set off a chain reaction or bring about an undesirable outcome.
ストレスが病気(びょうき)を誘発(ゆうはつ)する。
Stress triggers illness.
大雨(おおあめ)が土砂崩(どしゃくず)れを誘発(ゆうはつ)する恐(おそ)れがある。
There is a risk that heavy rain will trigger landslides.
一(ひと)つの事故(じこ)が連鎖的(れんさてき)に別(べつ)の事故(じこ)を誘発(ゆうはつ)して、大規模(だいきぼ)な渋滞(じゅうたい)が発生(はっせい)した。
One accident triggered a chain of other accidents, causing a major traffic jam.

PATTERN:
A が B を誘発(ゆうはつ)する (A triggers/induces B)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)を誘発(ゆうはつ)する (to trigger an accident)
  • 病気(びょうき)を誘発(ゆうはつ)する (to induce illness)
  • アレルギーを誘発(ゆうはつ)する (to trigger allergies)
  • 地震(じしん)を誘発(ゆうはつ)する (to trigger an earthquake)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)き起(お)こす: To cause, to bring about — more general and common in everyday speech.
  • 招(まね)く: To invite, to bring upon oneself — implies the cause was avoidable or negligent.
  • 促(うなが)す: To urge, to promote — neutral, often positive, unlike 誘発(ゆうはつ) which tends toward negative outcomes.
