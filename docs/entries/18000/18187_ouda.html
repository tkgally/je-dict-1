殴打(おうだ)

おうだ
noun
striking; beating; assault
1. striking; beating; assault
The act of hitting or striking someone, especially as an act of violence. A formal word commonly used in legal, police, and news contexts to describe physical assault.
被害者(ひがいしゃ)は頭部(とうぶ)を殴打(おうだ)された。
The victim was struck on the head.
殴打(おうだ)による傷害(しょうがい)で逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested for assault causing injury.
防犯(ぼうはん)カメラの映像(えいぞう)に、容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が被害者(ひがいしゃ)を殴打(おうだ)する場面(ばめん)が映(うつ)っていた。
The security camera footage showed the suspect striking the victim.

USAGE:
A formal word that appears almost exclusively in news reports, police records, and legal documents. In everyday speech, 殴(なぐ)る (to punch) or 叩(たた)く (to hit) are used instead. Often seen in crime reporting alongside terms like 傷害(しょうがい) (injury/assault).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 殴打(おうだ)する (to strike, to beat)
  • 殴打(おうだ)による死亡(しぼう) (death by beating)
  • 頭部(とうぶ)を殴打(おうだ)する (to strike on the head)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 殴(なぐ)る: to punch, to hit — everyday word for striking someone
  • 暴行(ぼうこう): assault, violence — broader legal term covering various types of physical attack
