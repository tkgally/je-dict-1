1. literary work; written work; book
A work of writing, especially a book or scholarly publication. Refers to the finished product.
彼(かれ)の著作(ちょさく)を読(よ)んだ。
I read his written work.
この著作(ちょさく)は世界中(せかいじゅう)で翻訳(ほんやく)されている。
This work has been translated around the world.
有名(ゆうめい)な学者(がくしゃ)の著作(ちょさく)を図書館(としょかん)で借(か)りて研究(けんきゅう)に役立(やくだ)てた。
I borrowed a famous scholar's work from the library and used it for my research.
2. authorship; writing (the act of)
The act of writing and publishing written works. Often used in compounds related to intellectual property and copyright.
著作権(ちょさくけん)を侵害(しんがい)してはいけない。
You must not infringe on copyright.
この写真(しゃしん)の著作権(ちょさくけん)は撮影者(さつえいしゃ)にある。
The copyright for this photograph belongs to the photographer.
著作(ちょさく)活動(かつどう)を続(つづ)けながら、大学(だいがく)でも教(おし)えている。
While continuing to write, he also teaches at a university.

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered in the compound 著作権(ちょさくけん) (copyright). As a standalone word, it refers to a published work, particularly non-fiction or scholarly writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 著作権(ちょさくけん) (copyright)
  • 著作権(ちょさくけん)侵害(しんがい) (copyright infringement)
  • 著作(ちょさく)活動(かつどう) (writing activities)
  • 著作物(ちょさくぶつ) (copyrighted work; authored material)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 作品(さくひん): work — broader term covering any creative work including art, music, and film
  • 著書(ちょしょ): book by an author — specifically refers to books, not other types of writing
