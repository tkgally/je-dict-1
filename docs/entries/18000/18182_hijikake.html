肘掛(ひじか)け

ひじかけ
noun
armrest
1. armrest
The part of a chair, sofa, or seat designed to support the arm. Found on office chairs, airplane seats, theater seats, and sofas.
椅子(いす)の肘掛(ひじか)けに腕(うで)を乗(の)せた。
I rested my arm on the armrest of the chair.
飛行機(ひこうき)の肘掛(ひじか)けを隣(となり)の人(ひと)と取(と)り合(あ)った。
I fought over the armrest with the person sitting next to me on the plane.
この肘掛(ひじか)け椅子(いす)は座(すわ)り心地(ごこち)がよくて長時間(ちょうじかん)の作業(さぎょう)でも疲(つか)れにくい。
This armchair is comfortable and doesn't tire you out even during long work sessions.

A compound of 肘(ひじ) (elbow) and 掛(か)け (rest/support). Refers to the part of a chair or seat designed to support the arm.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肘掛(ひじか)け椅子(いす): armchair
  • 肘掛(ひじか)けの高(たか)さ: armrest height
  • 肘掛(ひじか)けを上(あ)げる: to raise the armrest
  • 肘掛(ひじか)けを下(さ)げる: to lower the armrest
  • 肘掛(ひじか)けに腕(うで)を乗(の)せる: to rest one's arm on the armrest

WHERE YOU ENCOUNTER ARMRESTS:

  • 飛行機(ひこうき)の座席(ざせき): airplane seats
  • 映画館(えいがかん)の席(せき): theater seats
  • オフィスチェア: office chairs
  • ソファ: sofas

The compound 肘掛(ひじか)け椅子(いす) specifically refers to an armchair — a chair with armrests, as opposed to a simple 椅子(いす).
