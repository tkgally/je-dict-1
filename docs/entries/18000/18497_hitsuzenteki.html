必然的(ひつぜんてき)

ひつぜんてき
adjective (な)
inevitable; necessary; bound to happen
1. inevitable; necessary; bound to happen
Describes something that is certain to occur as a natural or logical consequence. Implies that the outcome could not have been otherwise given the circumstances.
それは必然的(ひつぜんてき)な結果(けっか)だった。
It was an inevitable result.
技術(ぎじゅつ)の進歩(しんぽ)に伴(ともな)い、変化(へんか)は必然的(ひつぜんてき)に起(お)こる。
With advances in technology, change inevitably occurs.
人口(じんこう)が減(へ)れば、労働力(ろうどうりょく)不足(ぶそく)は必然的(ひつぜんてき)に深刻(しんこく)になる。
If the population declines, the labor shortage will inevitably become severe.

必然的(ひつぜんてき) describes something that is bound to happen as a logical or natural consequence. It implies that the outcome could not have been otherwise.

FORMS:

  • な-adjective: 必然的(ひつぜんてき)な結果(けっか) (an inevitable result)
  • Adverbial: 必然的(ひつぜんてき)に (inevitably)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 必然(ひつぜん): inevitability (base noun, also stands alone)
  • 偶然的(ぐうぜんてき): accidental, coincidental (opposite)
  • 偶然(ぐうぜん): coincidence, chance (opposite base noun)
  • 不可避(ふかひ): unavoidable (similar meaning, slightly more formal)

REGISTER:
Common in academic writing, essays, and formal discussion. Rarely used in casual conversation, where speakers would say 当然(とうぜん) (naturally) or しかたがない (it can't be helped) instead.
