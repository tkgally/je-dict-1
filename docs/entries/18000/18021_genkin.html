厳禁(げんきん)

げんきん
noun, verb-suru
strictly prohibited; forbidden
1. strictly prohibited; strictly forbidden
A strong prohibition, often seen on signs and in rules. Stronger than 禁止, conveying zero tolerance.
撮影(さつえい)厳禁(げんきん)。
Photography strictly prohibited.
この地域(ちいき)では火気(かき)の使用(しよう)が厳禁(げんきん)されている。
The use of open flames is strictly prohibited in this area.
関係者(かんけいしゃ)以外(いがい)の立(た)ち入(い)りは厳禁(げんきん)となっております。
Entry is strictly prohibited for anyone other than authorized personnel.

厳禁(げんきん) is stronger than 禁止(きんし) (prohibited). It implies zero tolerance and potentially severe consequences.

COMMON SIGN PATTERNS:

  • 撮影(さつえい)厳禁(げんきん): photography strictly prohibited
  • 飲酒(いんしゅ)厳禁(げんきん): alcohol strictly forbidden
  • 喫煙(きつえん)厳禁(げんきん): smoking strictly forbidden
  • 火気(かき)厳禁(げんきん): open flames strictly prohibited
  • 立(た)ち入(い)り厳禁(げんきん): entry strictly prohibited

CONTRAST:

  • 禁止(きんし): prohibited (standard level)
  • 厳禁(げんきん): strictly prohibited (stronger, zero tolerance)

HOMOPHONE NOTE:
Not to be confused with 現金(げんきん) (cash), which has the same reading but different kanji.
