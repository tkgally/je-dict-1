救(すく)いの手(て)

すくいのて
noun
helping hand; rescue; lifeline
1. helping hand; rescue; lifeline; salvation
Help or assistance offered to someone in trouble or distress. Often used figuratively to describe timely aid that saves a dire situation.
救(すく)いの手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる。
To extend a helping hand.
困(こま)っているときに救(すく)いの手(て)が現(あらわ)れた。
A helping hand appeared when I was in trouble.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)に救(すく)いの手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べるため、多(おお)くのボランティアが集(あつ)まった。
Many volunteers gathered to lend a helping hand to the disaster victims.

救(すく)いの手(て) literally means "hand of rescue" and refers to timely help given to someone in trouble. It is often used figuratively and carries a somewhat dramatic or literary tone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 救(すく)いの手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる: to extend a helping hand (the most common collocation)
  • 救(すく)いの手(て)を求(もと)める: to seek a helping hand
  • 救(すく)いの手(て)が現(あらわ)れる: a helping hand appears

USAGE:
Frequently appears in news reports about disaster relief, charitable aid, and personal crises. Also used in everyday speech to describe someone who helped at a critical moment.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 手(て)を貸(か)す: to lend a hand (more casual)
  • 助(たす)け舟(ぶね): a lifeboat, timely help (similar figurative use)
  • 援助(えんじょ): aid, assistance (more formal/official)
