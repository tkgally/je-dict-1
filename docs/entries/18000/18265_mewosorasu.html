1. to look away; to avert one's eyes
To deliberately turn one's gaze away from something. Often implies avoidance, embarrassment, or unwillingness to confront something.
彼(かれ)は目(め)を逸(そ)らした。
He looked away.
現実(げんじつ)から目(め)を逸(そ)らしてはいけない。
You must not look away from reality.
彼女(かのじょ)は恥(は)ずかしそうに目(め)を逸(そ)らして、何(なに)も答(こた)えなかった。
She looked away shyly and didn't answer anything.

USAGE:
The verb 逸(そ)らす conjugates as a godan verb (逸(そ)らさない, 逸(そ)らして). Often used figuratively to mean avoiding a difficult topic or refusing to face a problem. The expression 現実(げんじつ)から目(め)を逸(そ)らす (to look away from reality) is particularly common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現実(げんじつ)から目(め)を逸(そ)らす (to avert one's eyes from reality)
  • 問題(もんだい)から目(め)を逸(そ)らす (to look away from a problem)
  • 思(おも)わず目(め)を逸(そ)らす (to involuntarily look away)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目(め)をそむける: to turn one's eyes away — similar meaning, slightly more literary
  • 視線(しせん)をそらす: to avert one's gaze — uses 視線(しせん) instead of 目(め)
