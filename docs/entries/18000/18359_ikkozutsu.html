1. one by one; one at a time; one each
Indicates doing or distributing something one unit at a time. The counter 個 is used for small, discrete objects.
お菓子(かし)を一個(いっこ)ずつ配(くば)る。
To hand out sweets one each.
卵(たまご)を一個(いっこ)ずつ割(わ)って入(い)れてください。
Please crack and add the eggs one at a time.
問題(もんだい)を一個(いっこ)ずつ片付(かたづ)けていけば、いつか全部(ぜんぶ)終(お)わる。
If you tackle the problems one by one, you'll eventually finish them all.

Part of the ～ずつ pattern, which means "each" or "at a time" when attached to a quantity. Similar expressions with different counters: 一人(ひとり)ずつ (one person at a time), 一枚(いちまい)ずつ (one sheet at a time), 一本(いっぽん)ずつ (one stick/bottle at a time). In casual speech, 一個(いっこ)ずつ is very common since the counter 個(こ) is versatile and used for many small objects.
