水洗(すいせん)トイレ

すいせんといれ
noun
flush toilet
1. flush toilet
A toilet that uses water to flush waste into a sewage system. Distinguished from non-flush toilets (汲み取り式) that were common in rural Japan until recent decades.
この建物(たてもの)は水洗(すいせん)トイレだ。
This building has flush toilets.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)を水洗(すいせん)トイレに改修(かいしゅう)した。
We renovated the old house to have a flush toilet.
キャンプ場(じょう)に水洗(すいせん)トイレがあるかどうか事前(じぜん)に確認(かくにん)しておこう。
Let's check in advance whether the campsite has flush toilets.

In urban Japan today, nearly all toilets are 水洗(すいせん)トイレ, so the term is mainly used in contrast with non-flush alternatives, or when describing facilities at campsites, mountain huts, and older rural buildings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水洗(すいせん)トイレ完備(かんび) — equipped with flush toilets
  • 水洗(すいせん)トイレに改修(かいしゅう)する — to convert to a flush toilet

RELATED WORDS:

  • 水洗(すいせん) — water-flushing
  • 汲(く)み取(と)り式(しき) — non-flush/pit toilet system
  • 浄化槽(じょうかそう) — septic tank
