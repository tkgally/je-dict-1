丸(まる)み

まるみ
noun
roundness; mellowness
1. roundness; rounded shape
The quality of being round or having curved edges. Used for physical shapes.
この石(いし)は丸(まる)みがある。
This stone has a rounded shape.
デザインに丸(まる)みを持(も)たせた。
I gave the design a rounded feel.
角(かど)を削(けず)って丸(まる)みを出(だ)すと、やさしい印象(いんしょう)になる。
If you shave the corners to create roundness, it gives a softer impression.
2. mellowness; softness; gentleness
A figurative sense describing a softened or gentler quality in personality, tone, or flavor.
年(とし)を取(と)って性格(せいかく)に丸(まる)みが出(で)た。
With age, his personality has mellowed.
このワインは丸(まる)みのある味(あじ)わいだ。
This wine has a mellow flavor.
彼(かれ)の話(はな)し方(かた)には丸(まる)みがあって、聞(き)いていて心地(ここち)よい。
There's a gentleness to the way he speaks, and it's pleasant to listen to.

USAGE:
Formed from the adjective 丸(まる)い with the suffix ～み, which turns adjectives into nouns expressing the quality (like 甘(あま)み from 甘(あま)い). Can describe both physical roundness and figurative softness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 丸(まる)みがある (to have roundness; to be mellow)
  • 丸(まる)みを帯(お)びる (to take on a rounded shape)
  • 丸(まる)みを出(だ)す (to add roundness)
  • 性格(せいかく)に丸(まる)みが出(で)る (one's personality mellows)
