不評(ふひょう)

ふひょう
noun; na-adjective
bad reputation; unpopularity
1. bad reputation; unpopularity; poor reception
A negative evaluation or reception of something. Used when a product, decision, or action is poorly received by others.
その計画(けいかく)は不評(ふひょう)だった。
That plan was poorly received.
新(あたら)しいデザインが社員(しゃいん)に不評(ふひょう)で、変更(へんこう)された。
The new design was unpopular with employees and was changed.
値上(ねあ)げが消費者(しょうひしゃ)の間(あいだ)で大(おお)いに不評(ふひょう)を買(か)っている。
The price increase is drawing a great deal of criticism from consumers.

USAGE:
Often used with を買(か)う (to draw criticism/unpopularity) as a set expression. Can function as a な-adjective (不評(ふひょう)な映画(えいが) — an unpopular movie).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不評(ふひょう)を買(か)う (to draw criticism, to be poorly received)
  • 不評(ふひょう)だった (was unpopular)
  • 不評(ふひょう)な意見(いけん) (an unpopular opinion)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 好評(こうひょう): good reputation, popularity — the direct opposite
  • 評判(ひょうばん): reputation — neutral, can be good or bad
