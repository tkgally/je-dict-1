汚(けが)らわしい

けがらわしい
adjective-i
filthy; disgusting; defiling
1. filthy; disgusting; abhorrent; morally repugnant
Describes something that is morally or spiritually impure and offensive. Stronger than simply 'dirty' — implies deep revulsion and contamination.
汚(けが)らわしい行為(こうい)だ。
That is a disgusting act.
そんな汚(けが)らわしい金(かね)は受(う)け取(と)れない。
I can't accept such tainted money.
汚(けが)らわしいものを見(み)るような目(め)で彼(かれ)をにらんだ。
She glared at him as if looking at something repulsive.

Related to 穢(けが)れ (impurity, defilement), a concept rooted in Shinto beliefs about ritual purity. The word carries moral and sometimes spiritual disgust, not just physical dirtiness. Much stronger than 汚(きたな)い (dirty) — 汚(けが)らわしい expresses revulsion and moral condemnation. Often used in dramatic or literary contexts.
