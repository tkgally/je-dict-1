1. oath; pledge; vow; covenant
A solemn promise or formal commitment, often made publicly or in a binding context. Used for both personal vows and official agreements.
二人(ふたり)は愛(あい)の誓約(せいやく)を交(か)わした。
The two exchanged vows of love.
入社時(にゅうしゃじ)に秘密保持(ひみつほじ)の誓約書(せいやくしょ)に署名(しょめい)した。
I signed a confidentiality pledge when joining the company.
環境(かんきょう)を守(まも)るという誓約(せいやく)のもとに、各国(かっこく)が協力(きょうりょく)することになった。
Under a pledge to protect the environment, the countries agreed to cooperate.

USAGE:
More formal and solemn than 約束(やくそく). Often appears in legal, religious, and ceremonial contexts. The compound 誓約書(せいやくしょ) (written pledge) is very common in business and legal settings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誓約(せいやく)を交(か)わす (to exchange vows)
  • 誓約書(せいやくしょ) (written pledge; sworn statement)
  • 誓約(せいやく)を守(まも)る (to keep a pledge)
  • 誓約(せいやく)に違反(いはん)する (to violate a pledge)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 約束(やくそく): promise — everyday, less formal
  • 誓(ちか)い: oath; vow — literary or ceremonial, often used in personal and religious contexts
  • 契約(けいやく): contract — a legally binding agreement, more transactional than moral
