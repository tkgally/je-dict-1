1. to crowd together; to flock; to swarm; to gather in groups
For animals or people to gather together in large numbers. When used about people, it can carry a slightly negative nuance of mindlessly clustering together.
鳥(とり)が群(む)れている。
The birds are flocking together.
観光客(かんこうきゃく)が人気(にんき)スポットに群(む)れる。
Tourists crowd around popular spots.
池(いけ)に餌(えさ)をまくと、鯉(こい)がたくさん群(む)れてきた。
When I tossed feed into the pond, lots of koi came swarming over.

群(む)れる is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning to flock, swarm, or crowd together.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No transitive pair

ASPECT (ている):
群(む)れている describes the ongoing state of being gathered together: 鳥(とり)が群(む)れている (the birds are flocking together).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 群(む)れをなす: to form a group/flock
  • 群(む)れてくる: to come swarming over
  • 群(む)れるのが嫌(きら)い: to dislike running with the crowd

NUANCE:
When used about people, 群(む)れる can carry a negative connotation of mindlessly clustering or lacking individuality.

HOMOPHONE WARNING:
Do not confuse with 蒸(む)れる (to become stuffy/humid).

RELATED:

  • 群(む)れ (noun): a group, flock, herd, swarm
  • 群(むら)がる: to swarm, to crowd around (similar meaning, slightly more active)
