予防策(よぼうさく)

よぼうさく
noun
preventive measure; precaution
1. preventive measure; precaution; preventative measure
An action or plan taken in advance to prevent something undesirable from happening, such as disease, accidents, or problems.
予防策(よぼうさく)を講(こう)じる。
To take preventive measures.
感染症(かんせんしょう)の予防策(よぼうさく)として手洗(てあら)いが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Hand washing is important as a preventive measure against infectious diseases.
災害(さいがい)が起(お)きる前(まえ)に、しっかりと予防策(よぼうさく)を立(た)てておくべきだ。
You should establish thorough preventive measures before a disaster strikes.

Common collocations: 予防策(よぼうさく)を講(こう)じる (to take/implement preventive measures), 予防策(よぼうさく)を立(た)てる (to establish preventive measures). The suffix ～策(さく) (measure, plan) appears in many compound nouns: 対策(たいさく) (countermeasure), 解決策(かいけつさく) (solution), 改善策(かいぜんさく) (improvement measure). Related: 予防(よぼう) (prevention), 対策(たいさく) (countermeasure — reactive rather than preventive).
