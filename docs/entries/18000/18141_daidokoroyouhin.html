台所用品(だいどころようひん)

だいどころようひん
noun
kitchenware; kitchen supplies
1. kitchenware; kitchen supplies; kitchen utensils and goods
A general term for items used in the kitchen, including cooking utensils, pots, pans, cutting boards, and other kitchen tools and supplies.
台所用品(だいどころようひん)を買(か)いに行(い)った。
I went to buy kitchenware.
この店(みせ)は台所用品(だいどころようひん)の品揃(しなぞろ)えが豊富(ほうふ)だ。
This store has a wide selection of kitchenware.
一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしを始(はじ)めるとき、最低限(さいていげん)の台所用品(だいどころようひん)をそろえる必要(ひつよう)がある。
When you start living alone, you need to get at least the basic kitchenware.

USAGE:
A compound of 台所(だいどころ) (kitchen) and 用品(ようひん) (supplies/goods). The suffix ～用品(ようひん) is productive: 事務用品(じむようひん) (office supplies), 日用品(にちようひん) (daily necessities), 文房具(ぶんぼうぐ)・学用品(がくようひん) (school supplies).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 台所用品(だいどころようひん)売(う)り場(ば) (kitchenware section)
  • 台所用品(だいどころようひん)をそろえる (to stock up on kitchenware)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • キッチン用品(ようひん): same meaning using the English-derived word for kitchen
  • 調理器具(ちょうりきぐ): cooking utensils/tools — more specifically about tools used for cooking
