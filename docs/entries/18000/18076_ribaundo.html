1. weight regain, bounce-back after dieting
Regaining weight after a diet ends. This is the most common meaning in everyday Japanese. The word implies that restricting intake led to a bounce-back effect.
ダイエットの後(あと)にリバウンドした。
I gained the weight back after the diet.
急激(きゅうげき)なダイエットはリバウンドしやすい。
Extreme dieting tends to cause weight rebound.
リバウンドしないためには生活習慣(せいかつしゅうかん)を変(か)えることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
To avoid weight rebound, it's important to change your lifestyle habits.
2. rebound (in sports)
In basketball and other sports, catching the ball after a missed shot bounces off the backboard or rim.
リバウンドを取(と)る。
To grab a rebound.
彼(かれ)はリバウンドの強(つよ)い選手(せんしゅ)だ。
He's a player who's strong at rebounding.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)ではリバウンドで負(ま)けていた。
We were losing on rebounds in today's game.

USAGE:
In Japanese, the diet-related meaning (sense 1) is far more common in everyday conversation than the sports meaning. When Japanese people say リバウンド, they almost always mean gaining weight back after a diet.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リバウンドする: to rebound (gain weight back)
  • リバウンドを防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent rebound
  • リバウンドしやすい: prone to weight rebound
  • リバウンドを取(と)る: to grab a rebound (sports)
