1. to look after; to take care of; to attend to
To provide care, supervision, or assistance to someone. Often used for caring for children, elderly family members, or subordinates.
子(こ)どもの面倒(めんどう)を見(み)る。
I look after the children.
母(はは)が入院(にゅういん)している間(あいだ)、父(ちち)が家事(かじ)の面倒(めんどう)を見(み)た。
While my mother was in the hospital, my father took care of the housework.
先輩(せんぱい)がいつも新人(しんじん)の面倒(めんどう)を見(み)てくれるので、職場(しょくば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)がいい。
The senior staff always look after the new employees, so the workplace atmosphere is good.

USAGE:
A very common expression meaning to care for or look after someone or something. The verb 見(み)る conjugates normally as an ichidan verb. Often used with particles like の or が before 面倒(めんどう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子(こ)どもの面倒(めんどう)を見(み)る (to look after children)
  • 親(おや)の面倒(めんどう)を見(み)る (to look after one's parents)
  • 後輩(こうはい)の面倒(めんどう)を見(み)る (to look after junior colleagues)
  • 面倒(めんどう)を見(み)てもらう (to be looked after)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 世話(せわ)をする: to take care of — slightly more formal, can also mean to arrange or mediate
  • 介護(かいご)する: to provide nursing care — specifically for elderly or disabled persons
