1. uproar; commotion; big fuss
A noisy disturbance or a situation where people are making a big deal out of something. Can refer to both literal noise and figurative overreaction.
子供(こども)たちが大騒(おおさわ)ぎしている。
The children are making a big commotion.
そんなことで大騒(おおさわ)ぎするな。
Don't make such a fuss over something like that.
ニュースが流(なが)れると、ネット上(じょう)では大騒(おおさわ)ぎになった。
When the news broke, there was an uproar online.

USAGE:
Can be used as a noun (大騒(おおさわ)ぎになる — it became a commotion) or with する (大騒(おおさわ)ぎする — to make a fuss). Often implies that the reaction is disproportionate to the cause.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大騒(おおさわ)ぎになる (to become an uproar)
  • 大騒(おおさわ)ぎする (to make a big fuss)
  • 大騒(おおさわ)ぎするほどのことではない (it's not worth making a fuss over)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 騒(さわ)ぎ: commotion — a less intense version
  • 騒動(そうどう): disturbance, incident — more serious, often newsworthy
