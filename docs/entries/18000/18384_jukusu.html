1. to ripen; to mature (of fruit)
For fruit or crops to reach the stage where they are ready to eat or harvest. Used for the natural ripening process.
トマトが赤(あか)く熟(じゅく)した。
The tomatoes ripened red.
木(き)から落(お)ちるほど熟(じゅく)した柿(かき)を集(あつ)めた。
I gathered persimmons that had ripened enough to fall from the tree.
まだ熟(じゅく)していないバナナは青(あお)くて硬(かた)い。
Bananas that haven't ripened yet are green and hard.
2. to mature; to come to fruition (figurative)
For a plan, idea, skill, or situation to develop to the point where it is ready or complete. A figurative extension of the ripening metaphor.
機(き)が熟(じゅく)すのを待(ま)つ。
To wait for the right moment.
議論(ぎろん)が熟(じゅく)してから結論(けつろん)を出(だ)そう。
Let's reach a conclusion after the discussion has matured.
長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)で技術(ぎじゅつ)が熟(じゅく)し、独自(どくじ)のスタイルを確立(かくりつ)した。
Through years of experience, their skills matured, and they established their own style.

Godan verb (さ row): 熟(じゅく)す・熟(じゅく)します
て-form: 熟(じゅく)して
た-form: 熟(じゅく)した

Also read as 熟(う)れる (ichidan verb), which is more common for the literal ripening of fruit.

KEY EXPRESSION:
機(き)が熟(じゅく)す — "The time is ripe." A very common set phrase meaning the right moment has arrived.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 果実(かじつ)が熟(じゅく)す (fruit ripens)
  • 機(き)が熟(じゅく)す (the time is ripe)
  • 議論(ぎろん)が熟(じゅく)す (the discussion matures)
  • 計画(けいかく)が熟(じゅく)す (a plan comes to fruition)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 熟(う)れる: The native Japanese reading, specifically for fruit ripening. More colloquial than 熟(じゅく)す.
  • 成熟(せいじゅく)する: To mature — more formal, used for people, societies, or markets reaching maturity.
