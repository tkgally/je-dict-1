1. snap decision; prompt judgment; quick decision
Making a decision immediately without lengthy deliberation. Can be viewed positively (decisive action) or negatively (hasty judgment) depending on context.
即断(そくだん)即決(そっけつ)で決(き)めた。
I decided on the spot without hesitation.
この件(けん)は即断(そくだん)できない。
I can't make an immediate decision on this matter.
社長(しゃちょう)は即断(そくだん)する人(ひと)で、会議(かいぎ)が長引(ながび)くことはない。
The president is someone who makes quick decisions, so meetings never drag on.

USAGE:
Often paired with 即決(そっけつ) in the four-character expression 即断即決(そくだんそっけつ) (making snap decisions and acting on them immediately). Whether 即断(そくだん) is positive or negative depends on context — it can mean decisiveness or rashness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即断(そくだん)即決(そっけつ) (snap decision and immediate action)
  • 即断(そくだん)する (to decide on the spot)
  • 即断(そくだん)は禁物(きんもつ) (snap decisions are to be avoided)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 即決(そっけつ): immediate decision — very similar, often paired with 即断(そくだん)
  • 決断(けつだん): determination, resolution — a deliberate, weighty decision
