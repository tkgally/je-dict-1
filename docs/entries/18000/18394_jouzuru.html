1. to take advantage of; to exploit (an opportunity or weakness)
To seize upon a situation, moment of weakness, or confusion to act in one's favor. Often implies cunning or opportunism.
隙(すき)に乗(じょう)じて逃(に)げた。
I took advantage of an opening and escaped.
混乱(こんらん)に乗(じょう)じて泥棒(どろぼう)が侵入(しんにゅう)した。
A thief broke in, taking advantage of the confusion.
災害(さいがい)に乗(じょう)じた悪質(あくしつ)な詐欺(さぎ)が報告(ほうこく)されている。
Malicious scams exploiting the disaster have been reported.
2. to multiply (mathematics)
To perform multiplication. A formal/literary alternative to 掛ける in mathematical contexts.
3に5を乗(じょう)ずると15になる。
3 multiplied by 5 equals 15.
速度(そくど)に時間(じかん)を乗(じょう)じて距離(きょり)を求(もと)める。
Multiply speed by time to find distance.
係数(けいすう)を乗(じょう)じた値(あたい)を計算(けいさん)してください。
Please calculate the value multiplied by the coefficient.

This is a ずる-verb (a variant of する-verbs). Common conjugation uses 乗(じょう)じる (ichidan) rather than 乗(じょう)ずる:

  • 乗(じょう)じる・乗(じょう)じます
  • て-form: 乗(じょう)じて
  • た-form: 乗(じょう)じた

PATTERN (sense 1):
～に乗(じょう)じて (taking advantage of ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隙(すき)に乗(じょう)じる (to take advantage of an opening)
  • 混乱(こんらん)に乗(じょう)じる (to exploit confusion)
  • 機(き)に乗(じょう)じる (to seize the opportunity)

SIMILAR WORDS (sense 1):

  • 付(つ)け込(こ)む: To take advantage of — implies exploiting a weakness in a more calculated way.
  • 便乗(びんじょう)する: To take advantage of, to ride on — often implies riding on someone else's success or a trend.
