引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん)

ひっこみじあん
noun, adjective-na
shy; reserved; timid
1. shy; reserved; timid; withdrawn
A personality trait of being reluctant to step forward or assert oneself. Describes someone who holds back in social situations.
彼(かれ)は引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん)な性格(せいかく)だ。
He has a shy personality.
引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん)だった娘(むすめ)が、最近(さいきん)積極的(せっきょくてき)になってきた。
My daughter, who used to be withdrawn, has recently become more outgoing.
引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん)では商売(しょうばい)はうまくいかない。
You can't succeed in business if you're too timid.

Literally "pulling-back thinking" — the idea of hesitating and overthinking rather than stepping forward.

USAGE:
Often used with な to modify nouns: 引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん)な人(ひと) (a timid person). Describes a mild, everyday form of shyness rather than clinical anxiety.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 内気(うちき): introverted, shy (emphasizes quiet nature)
  • 消極的(しょうきょくてき): passive, negative (broader, not just social)
  • 人見知(ひとみし)り: shy around strangers (especially children)
  • 恥(は)ずかしがり屋(や): someone who gets embarrassed easily

CONTRAST:

  • 積極的(せっきょくてき): proactive, assertive (opposite quality)
