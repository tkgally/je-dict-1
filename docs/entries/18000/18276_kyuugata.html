旧型(きゅうがた)

きゅうがた
noun
old model; old type
1. old model; old type; outdated version
A previous version or model of a product, machine, or system that has been replaced by a newer one. Commonly used for electronics, vehicles, and equipment.
これは旧型(きゅうがた)のモデルだ。
This is an old model.
旧型(きゅうがた)のスマホはバッテリーの持(も)ちが悪(わる)い。
Old model smartphones have poor battery life.
旧型(きゅうがた)の機器(きき)を新型(しんがた)に入(い)れ替(か)える計画(けいかく)が進(すす)んでいる。
Plans to replace old equipment with new models are underway.

USAGE:
A compound of 旧(きゅう) (old, former) + 型(がた) (model, type). Often used as a noun modifier with の: 旧型(きゅうがた)の～. The opposite is 新型(しんがた) (new model).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旧型(きゅうがた)の車(くるま) (an old model car)
  • 旧型(きゅうがた)から新型(しんがた)に買(か)い替(か)える (to upgrade from the old model to the new model)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新型(しんがた): new model — the antonym
  • 型落(かたお)ち: discontinued model — specifically a model no longer in production, often sold at a discount
