1. to oversleep; to sleep past one's alarm or intended wake-up time
To sleep longer than planned, typically resulting in being late. A compound of 寝る (to sleep) and 過ごす (to pass/exceed).
今朝(けさ)寝過(ねす)ごしてしまった。
I overslept this morning.
目覚(めざ)ましが鳴(な)らなくて寝過(ねす)ごした。
My alarm didn't go off, so I overslept.
昨夜(ゆうべ)遅(おそ)くまで起(お)きていたせいで寝過(ねす)ごして、大事(だいじ)な会議(かいぎ)に遅刻(ちこく)してしまった。
Because I stayed up late last night, I overslept and was late for an important meeting.

CONJUGATION:
Godan verb (す row): 寝過(ねす)ごす・寝過(ねす)ごします
て-form: 寝過(ねす)ごして
た-form: 寝過(ねす)ごした

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • うっかり寝過(ねす)ごす (to accidentally oversleep)
  • 寝過(ねす)ごして遅刻(ちこく)する (to oversleep and be late)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寝坊(ねぼう)する: Also means to oversleep, but is more commonly used in everyday conversation. 寝過(ねす)ごす emphasizes sleeping past a specific point, while 寝坊(ねぼう)する simply means getting up late.
  • 乗(の)り過(す)ごす: To ride past one's stop — uses the same ～過(す)ごす pattern.
