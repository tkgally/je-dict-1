1. to pour into, to fill by pouring
To pour a liquid into a container or space.
コップに水(みず)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む。
Pour water into a glass.
鍋(なべ)にスープを注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)んだ。
I poured the soup into the pot.
川(かわ)の水(みず)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)んでいる。
The river water is flowing into the sea.
2. to invest, to devote, to channel into
To put a large amount of money, time, effort, or emotion into something. Implies concentrated, intensive investment.
研究(けんきゅう)に情熱(じょうねつ)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む。
Pour one's passion into research.
会社(かいしゃ)は新製品(しんせいひん)の開発(かいはつ)に莫大(ばくだい)な資金(しきん)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)んだ。
The company invested enormous funds into developing the new product.
子(こ)どもの教育(きょういく)に時間(じかん)と労力(ろうりょく)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)んできた。
I have been devoting time and effort to my children's education.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is the literal meaning of pouring liquid. Sense 2 is figurative and very common, used for investing money, time, effort, or emotion into something.

WORD FORMATION:
注(そそ)ぐ (to pour) + 込(こ)む (into). The 込(こ)む suffix emphasizes the action going deeply into something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金(しきん)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour in funds
  • 情熱(じょうねつ)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour in passion
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour in love
  • 全力(ぜんりょく)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour in all one's effort
  • 時間(じかん)を注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to invest time
