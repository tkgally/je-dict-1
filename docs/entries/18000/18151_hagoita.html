羽子板(はごいた)

はごいた
noun
battledore; decorated wooden paddle
1. battledore; wooden paddle used for hanetsuki
A wooden paddle used in the traditional New Year game hanetsuki (similar to badminton without a net). Also refers to elaborately decorated paddles sold as good-luck ornaments.
お正月(しょうがつ)に羽子板(はごいた)で遊(あそ)んだ。
We played with battledore paddles at New Year's.
浅草寺(せんそうじ)の羽子板(はごいた)市(いち)は毎年(まいとし)十二月(じゅうにがつ)に開(ひら)かれる。
The battledore fair at Sensoji Temple is held every December.
女(おんな)の子(こ)が生(う)まれると、初正月(はつしょうがつ)に羽子板(はごいた)を贈(おく)る風習(ふうしゅう)がある。
There is a custom of giving a battledore paddle when a baby girl is born, for her first New Year.

USAGE:
Decorated 羽子板(はごいた) are sold at year-end markets, most famously at the 浅草寺(せんそうじ)羽子板(はごいた)市(いち) (Sensoji Battledore Fair) in December. These ornamental paddles feature elaborate designs, often depicting kabuki actors, and are displayed as good-luck charms rather than used for play.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 羽子板(はごいた)市(いち) (battledore fair)
  • 飾(かざ)り羽子板(はごいた) (decorative battledore)
  • 羽根(はね)つき (hanetsuki — the game played with a battledore)
