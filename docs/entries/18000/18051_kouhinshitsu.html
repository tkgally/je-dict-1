高品質(こうひんしつ)

こうひんしつ
noun, adjective-na
high quality
1. high quality; premium quality
Of superior quality or craftsmanship. Used to describe products, services, and materials that meet a high standard.
高品質(こうひんしつ)な製品(せいひん)を提供(ていきょう)する。
To provide high-quality products.
この店(みせ)は高品質(こうひんしつ)で低価格(ていかかく)が売(う)りだ。
This store's selling point is high quality at low prices.
日本製(にほんせい)は高品質(こうひんしつ)だという評判(ひょうばん)が世界的(せかいてき)に広(ひろ)まっている。
The reputation that Japanese-made products are high quality has spread worldwide.

The opposite is 低品質(ていひんしつ) (low quality). Can function as a な-adjective to modify nouns: 高品質(こうひんしつ)な素材(そざい) (high-quality materials). Common collocations: 高品質(こうひんしつ)・低価格(ていかかく) (high quality, low price), 高品質(こうひんしつ)を保(たも)つ (to maintain high quality). Compare 上質(じょうしつ) (fine quality, elegant) which emphasizes refinement rather than technical superiority.
