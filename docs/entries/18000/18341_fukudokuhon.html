副読本(ふくどくほん)

ふくどくほん
noun
supplementary reader; supplementary textbook
1. supplementary reader; supplementary textbook
A book used alongside the main textbook in school education to provide additional material or different perspectives on a subject. Often produced by local governments or educational boards.
副読本(ふくどくほん)を配布(はいふ)する。
To distribute supplementary readers.
道徳(どうとく)の授業(じゅぎょう)で副読本(ふくどくほん)を使(つか)った。
We used a supplementary reader in the moral education class.
市(し)が作成(さくせい)した防災(ぼうさい)の副読本(ふくどくほん)が全校(ぜんこう)に配(くば)られた。
A disaster-preparedness supplementary reader created by the city was distributed to all schools.

Commonly used in Japanese elementary and junior high school education. 副読本(ふくどくほん) are often produced for subjects like moral education (道徳(どうとく)), local studies (郷土(きょうど)学習(がくしゅう)), and disaster preparedness (防災(ぼうさい)). Unlike the main textbook (教科書(きょうかしょ)), they do not require Ministry of Education approval and are often created by municipalities.
