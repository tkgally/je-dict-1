出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)

しゅっちょうてあて
noun
business trip allowance; travel per diem
1. business trip allowance; travel per diem; travel stipend
A monetary allowance paid to employees to cover expenses incurred during business trips, such as meals and incidental costs.
出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)は一日(いちにち)いくらですか。
How much is the business trip allowance per day?
海外出張(かいがいしゅっちょう)の場合(ばあい)、出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)は国内(こくない)より高(たか)い。
For overseas business trips, the travel allowance is higher than for domestic ones.
会社(かいしゃ)の規定(きてい)で出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)の金額(きんがく)が決(き)まっている。
The amount of the business trip allowance is determined by company policy.

USAGE:
A standard business term. 手当(てあて) (allowance) is a productive suffix in workplace vocabulary: 残業手当(ざんぎょうてあて) (overtime pay), 住宅手当(じゅうたくてあて) (housing allowance), 家族手当(かぞくてあて) (family allowance).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)が出(で)る (a travel allowance is paid)
  • 出張手当(しゅっちょうてあて)を申請(しんせい)する (to apply for a travel allowance)
  • 日当(にっとう) (daily allowance — shorter synonym)
