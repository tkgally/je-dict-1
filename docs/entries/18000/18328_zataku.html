座卓(ざたく)

ざたく
noun
low table (for sitting on the floor)
1. low table; floor table; Japanese-style table
A low table designed to be used while sitting on the floor (on tatami mats or cushions). A staple of traditional Japanese living rooms.
座卓(ざたく)を囲(かこ)んで食事(しょくじ)をする。
To sit around a low table and eat.
和室(わしつ)に座卓(ざたく)と座布団(ざぶとん)を置(お)いた。
I placed a low table and floor cushions in the Japanese-style room.
祖母(そぼ)の家(いえ)では、いつも座卓(ざたく)でお茶(ちゃ)を飲(の)んだ。
At my grandmother's house, we always drank tea at the low table.

USAGE:
The defining feature is that it is low enough to use while sitting on the floor. In winter, many families convert their 座卓(ざたく) into a 炬燵(こたつ) (heated table) by adding a heater underneath and a blanket over the top.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 座卓(ざたく)を囲(かこ)む (to sit around a low table)
  • 座卓(ざたく)に座(すわ)る (to sit at a low table)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ちゃぶ台(だい): small low dining table — more casual, round or square
  • 炬燵(こたつ): heated low table — a 座卓(ざたく) with a built-in heater
  • テーブル: table — Western-style table with legs for chairs
