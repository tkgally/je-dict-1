1. releasing fish or animals into the wild; stocking
The practice of releasing fish, fry, or other aquatic creatures into rivers, lakes, or the sea. Often done for conservation, fishing industry purposes, or as part of environmental restoration.
川(かわ)に稚魚(ちぎょ)を放流(ほうりゅう)する。
To release fry into the river.
毎年(まいとし)春(はる)にサケの放流(ほうりゅう)が行(おこな)われる。
Salmon are stocked every spring.
地域(ちいき)の子(こ)どもたちが放流(ほうりゅう)体験(たいけん)に参加(さんか)した。
Local children participated in a fish-release experience.
2. discharging water; releasing flow
The controlled release of water from a dam or reservoir.
ダムから水(みず)を放流(ほうりゅう)する。
To discharge water from a dam.
大雨(おおあめ)のため、緊急(きんきゅう)放流(ほうりゅう)が実施(じっし)された。
Due to heavy rain, an emergency water discharge was carried out.
ダムの放流(ほうりゅう)により下流(かりゅう)の水位(すいい)が上昇(じょうしょう)した。
The water level downstream rose due to the dam discharge.

放流(ほうりゅう) has two distinct uses: releasing fish/animals into the wild, and discharging water from dams.

WORD FORMATION:
放(ほう) (release) + 流(りゅう) (flow)

SENSE 1 — FISH STOCKING:

  • 稚魚(ちぎょ)の放流(ほうりゅう): releasing fry (young fish)
  • サケの放流(ほうりゅう): salmon stocking
  • 放流(ほうりゅう)体験(たいけん): fish-release experience (educational activity)

Commonly done for conservation and fisheries management.

SENSE 2 — WATER DISCHARGE:

  • ダムの放流(ほうりゅう): dam discharge
  • 緊急(きんきゅう)放流(ほうりゅう): emergency discharge
  • 放流(ほうりゅう)量(りょう): discharge volume

Seen in weather warnings during heavy rainfall. 緊急(きんきゅう)放流(ほうりゅう) alerts warn of sudden water level rises downstream.
