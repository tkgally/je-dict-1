1. to misjudge; to misgauge; to make a wrong assessment
To judge someone or something incorrectly, especially to underestimate or fail to see their true nature.
彼(かれ)の実力(じつりょく)を見損(みそこ)なっていた。
I had misjudged his ability.
相手(あいて)を見損(みそこ)なうと痛(いた)い目(め)に遭(あ)う。
If you misjudge your opponent, you'll pay for it.
状況(じょうきょう)を見損(みそこ)なって大(おお)きな損失(そんしつ)を出(だ)してしまった。
I misread the situation and ended up with a big loss.
2. to lose respect for; to think less of; to be disappointed in
To become disillusioned with someone after seeing their bad behavior or poor character. Often used when expressing strong disappointment.
あんなことをするなんて、見損(みそこ)なったよ。
I can't believe you'd do something like that — I've lost respect for you.
友人(ゆうじん)に裏切(うらぎ)られて、すっかり見損(みそこ)なった。
I was betrayed by my friend, and I completely lost respect for them.
彼女(かのじょ)のことを見損(みそこ)なっていたが、実(じつ)はとても優(やさ)しい人(ひと)だった。
I had thought poorly of her, but she was actually a very kind person.

USAGE:
Sense 2 is the more common everyday usage. The phrase 見損(みそこ)なったよ is a strong expression of disappointment, often said directly to the person. It carries an emotional weight similar to "I thought better of you."

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)). Conjugates as: 見損(みそこ)なわない, 見損(みそこ)なって, 見損(みそこ)なった.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を見損(みそこ)なう (to misjudge a person)
  • 見損(みそこ)なったよ (I've lost respect for you)
  • すっかり見損(みそこ)なう (to completely lose respect for)
