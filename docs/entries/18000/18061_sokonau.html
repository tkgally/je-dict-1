損(そこ)なう

そこなう
verb-godan
to harm, to impair, to damage
1. to harm, to impair, to damage
To cause damage or deterioration to something valuable — health, reputation, trust, beauty, etc. Often used for abstract damage rather than physical destruction.
健康(けんこう)を損(そこ)なう。
To harm one's health.
無理(むり)なダイエットは体(からだ)を損(そこ)なう。
Excessive dieting damages your body.
信頼(しんらい)を損(そこ)なうような行為(こうい)は避(さ)けるべきだ。
One should avoid actions that would damage trust.
2. to fail to do, to miss doing (as a verb suffix)
When attached to the stem of another verb (V-stem + 損(そこ)なう), it means failing to do the action or doing it incorrectly.
電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)り損(そこ)なった。
I missed the train.
言(い)い損(そこ)なったことがある。
There's something I failed to say.
せっかくのチャンスを掴(つか)み損(そこ)なった。
I missed the chance to seize a rare opportunity.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is a standalone verb meaning to harm or impair. Sense 2 is a grammatical suffix attached to verb stems, meaning to fail at the action. Both are common in everyday Japanese.

VERB SUFFIX PATTERN:

V-stem + 損(そこ)なう: fail to do V

  • 乗(の)り損(そこ)なう: to miss (a ride)
  • 言(い)い損(そこ)なう: to fail to say
  • 食(た)べ損(そこ)なう: to miss eating
  • 見(み)損(そこ)なう: to misjudge; to miss seeing

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 健康(けんこう)を損(そこ)なう: to harm one's health
  • 信頼(しんらい)を損(そこ)なう: to damage trust
  • 名誉(めいよ)を損(そこ)なう: to damage one's honor
  • 品位(ひんい)を損(そこ)なう: to compromise one's dignity
  • 景観(けいかん)を損(そこ)なう: to spoil the scenery
