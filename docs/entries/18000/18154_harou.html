波浪(はろう)

はろう
noun
waves; ocean waves; rough seas
1. waves; ocean waves; rough seas
Large waves on the ocean, especially those caused by wind. A formal or technical term commonly used in weather reports and maritime contexts.
波浪警報(はろうけいほう)が出(で)ている。
A high wave warning has been issued.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で波浪(はろう)が高(たか)くなっている。
The waves are high due to the typhoon.
波浪注意報(はろうちゅういほう)が発令(はつれい)されたため、漁船(ぎょせん)は出港(しゅっこう)を見合(みあ)わせた。
Because a wave advisory was issued, the fishing boats postponed their departure.

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered in weather reports as 波浪警報(はろうけいほう) (high wave warning) or 波浪注意報(はろうちゅういほう) (wave advisory). In everyday speech, 波(なみ) (waves) is more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 波浪警報(はろうけいほう) (high wave warning)
  • 波浪注意報(はろうちゅういほう) (wave advisory)
  • 波浪(はろう)が高(たか)い (waves are high)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 波(なみ): waves — general everyday term
  • 高波(たかなみ): high waves — more accessible than 波浪(はろう)
