～始(はじ)める

はじめる
suffix
to start ~ing; to begin to ~
1. to start ~ing; to begin to ~
Attaches to the masu-stem of a verb to indicate the beginning of an action. The resulting compound conjugates as an ichidan verb.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り始(はじ)めた。
It started raining.
最近(さいきん)、日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)し始(はじ)めました。
I recently started studying Japanese.
桜(さくら)が咲(さ)き始(はじ)めると、公園(こうえん)は花見(はなみ)の客(きゃく)でにぎわう。
When the cherry blossoms start blooming, the park bustles with hanami visitors.

FORMATION:

Attaches to the masu-stem (連用形) of verbs:

  • 降(ふ)る → 降(ふ)り + 始(はじ)める → 降(ふ)り始(はじ)める
  • 食(た)べる → 食(た)べ + 始(はじ)める → 食(た)べ始(はじ)める
  • する → し + 始(はじ)める → し始(はじ)める

The compound conjugates as an ichidan verb (始(はじ)める).

CONTRAST:

  • ～始(はじ)める vs. ～出(だ)す: Both mean "to start doing," but ～出(だ)す implies a more sudden or spontaneous onset. 泣(な)き出(だ)す (to burst out crying) feels more abrupt than 泣(な)き始(はじ)める (to start crying). ～出(だ)す is also used for involuntary actions more naturally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 読(よ)み始(はじ)める (start reading)
  • 走(はし)り始(はじ)める (start running)
  • 使(つか)い始(はじ)める (start using)
  • 動(うご)き始(はじ)める (start moving)
