立(た)ち見席(みせき)

たちみせき
noun
standing room; standing section
1. standing room; standing section; standing-room-only area
A section in a theater, concert venue, or stadium where audience members stand rather than sit. Often the cheapest ticket option.
座席(ざせき)は売(う)り切(き)れだが立(た)ち見席(みせき)ならまだある。
The seated tickets are sold out, but there's still standing room available.
立(た)ち見席(みせき)のチケットは安(やす)いけど、長時間(ちょうじかん)立(た)っているのは疲(つか)れる。
Standing room tickets are cheap, but standing for a long time is tiring.
人気(にんき)の公演(こうえん)だったので、立(た)ち見席(みせき)まで満員(まんいん)だった。
It was such a popular performance that even the standing section was packed.

USAGE:
The shorter form 立(た)ち見(み) is also commonly used. In sumo, the 立(た)ち見席(みせき) is a well-known feature of sold-out tournaments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立(た)ち見席(みせき)のチケット (standing room ticket)
  • 立(た)ち見席(みせき)で観(み)る (to watch from the standing section)
  • 立(た)ち見席(みせき)まで満員(まんいん) (standing room is full too)
