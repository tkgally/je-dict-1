1. copying verbatim; wholesale copying; transcribing in full
Copying something exactly as it is, word for word, without any changes or original input. Often carries a negative connotation, implying laziness, plagiarism, or lack of effort.
友達(ともだち)の宿題(しゅくだい)を丸写(まるうつ)しした。
I copied my friend's homework word for word.
ネットの記事(きじ)を丸写(まるうつ)しにしたレポートは不合格(ふごうかく)になる。
A report that's a wholesale copy of an online article will get a failing grade.
参考書(さんこうしょ)の丸写(まるうつ)しではなく、自分(じぶん)の言葉(ことば)でまとめることが大事(だいじ)だ。
It's important to summarize things in your own words rather than just copying the reference book verbatim.

USAGE:
Almost always used negatively, implying someone took a shortcut instead of doing their own work. Very common in school and academic contexts. Can be used as a noun (丸写(まるうつ)し) or with する (丸写(まるうつ)しにする / 丸写(まるうつ)しする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 丸写(まるうつ)しにする (to copy verbatim)
  • 丸写(まるうつ)しのレポート (a word-for-word copied report)
  • 答(こた)えを丸写(まるうつ)しする (to copy answers wholesale)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コピペ: copy-paste — modern slang for copying digital text
  • 盗用(とうよう): plagiarism — more formal and severe
