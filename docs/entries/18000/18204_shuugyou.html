1. end of work; end of the business day; closing
The conclusion of work or business hours for the day. Used in both workplace and school contexts. The opposite of 始業 (start of work).
終業(しゅうぎょう)時間(じかん)は午後(ごご)五時(ごじ)です。
The end of the workday is at 5 PM.
終業(しゅうぎょう)のベルが鳴(な)ったら帰(かえ)りの支度(したく)をする。
When the closing bell rings, I start getting ready to go home.
終業(しゅうぎょう)後(ご)に同僚(どうりょう)と食事(しょくじ)に行(い)くことになった。
I ended up going to dinner with a colleague after work.

USAGE:
Can function as a する verb (終業(しゅうぎょう)する). In schools, 終業(しゅうぎょう)式(しき) (closing ceremony) marks the end of a school term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終業(しゅうぎょう)時間(じかん) (closing time; end of work hours)
  • 終業(しゅうぎょう)後(ご) (after work)
  • 終業(しゅうぎょう)式(しき) (closing ceremony — at school)
  • 終業(しゅうぎょう)のチャイム (the end-of-work chime)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 始業(しぎょう): start of work — the opposite; the beginning of work or class hours
  • 退勤(たいきん): leaving work — the act of physically departing the workplace
