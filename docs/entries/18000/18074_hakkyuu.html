1. issuance, issuing (of passports, visas, certificates, etc.)
The official issuing of documents by a government or authorized body. Used specifically for formal documents like passports, visas, and certificates.
パスポートの発給(はっきゅう)を申請(しんせい)した。
I applied for the issuance of a passport.
ビザの発給(はっきゅう)には2週間(しゅうかん)かかる。
Visa issuance takes two weeks.
大使館(たいしかん)が査証(さしょう)を発給(はっきゅう)する。
The embassy issues the visa.

WORD FORMATION:
発(はつ) (issue, emit) + 給(きゅう) (supply, provide). Literally "issue and provide."

USAGE:
A formal, official term used almost exclusively for government-issued documents. For more general "issuing" (like tickets or receipts), 発行(はっこう) is more appropriate.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発行(はっこう): publication, issuance (broader use: books, tickets, cards)
  • 交付(こうふ): delivery, granting (of official documents)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パスポートの発給(はっきゅう): passport issuance
  • ビザの発給(はっきゅう): visa issuance
  • 発給(はっきゅう)を申請(しんせい)する: to apply for issuance
  • 発給(はっきゅう)手数料(てすうりょう): issuance fee
