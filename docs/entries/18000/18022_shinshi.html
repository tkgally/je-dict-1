紳士(しんし)

しんし
noun
gentleman
1. gentleman; man of refined manners
A man who behaves with courtesy, dignity, and good manners. Also used more broadly to refer to men in formal or polite contexts.
彼(かれ)は紳士(しんし)だ。
He is a gentleman.
紳士的(しんしてき)な態度(たいど)で接(せっ)する。
To treat someone in a gentlemanly manner.
あの店(みせ)は紳士(しんし)服(ふく)の品揃(しなぞろ)えがいい。
That store has a good selection of menswear.

紳士(しんし) means gentleman — a man of refined manners and dignity. It is used both as a character description and as a formal term for "men" in commercial contexts.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 紳士的(しんしてき): gentlemanly (na-adjective)
  • 紳士(しんし)服(ふく): menswear
  • 紳士(しんし)協定(きょうてい): gentleman's agreement
  • 紳士(しんし)用品(ようひん): men's accessories/goods

PAIR WORD:
淑女(しゅくじょ) (lady). The formal address 紳士(しんし)淑女(しゅくじょ)の皆(みな)さま means "ladies and gentlemen."

USAGE:
In department stores, 紳士(しんし) is the standard label for men's sections (紳士(しんし)服(ふく)フロア = menswear floor), parallel to 婦人(ふじん) (women's). As a character description, 紳士(しんし) implies courtesy, respectability, and good manners.
