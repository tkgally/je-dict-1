ダイヤ乱(みだ)れ

だいやみだれ
noun
train schedule disruption
1. train schedule disruption; timetable disruption
A disruption to train timetables causing delays or cancellations. ダイヤ is short for ダイヤグラム (diagram), referring to the graphical train schedule used in railway operations.
台風(たいふう)でダイヤが乱(みだ)れている。
The train schedule is disrupted because of the typhoon.
人身事故(じんしんじこ)の影響(えいきょう)でダイヤ乱(みだ)れが発生(はっせい)しています。
Schedule disruptions have occurred due to a passenger accident.
朝(あさ)のラッシュ時(じ)にダイヤが乱(みだ)れると、通勤(つうきん)する人(ひと)たちに大(おお)きな影響(えいきょう)が出(で)る。
When the train schedule is disrupted during the morning rush, it greatly affects commuters.

USAGE:
ダイヤ (short for ダイヤグラム) in this context means 'train timetable,' not 'diamond.' This usage is specific to railway operations. You will often hear station announcements saying ダイヤが乱(みだ)れております (the schedule is disrupted).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ダイヤが乱(みだ)れる (the schedule is disrupted)
  • ダイヤの乱(みだ)れ (schedule disruption)
  • ダイヤが回復(かいふく)する (the schedule returns to normal)
  • ダイヤ通(どお)り (on schedule)
