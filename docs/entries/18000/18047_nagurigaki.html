殴(なぐ)り書(が)き

なぐりがき
noun, verb-suru
scribbling; scrawl; hasty writing
1. scribbling; scrawl; hasty, messy writing
Writing done quickly and carelessly, resulting in messy, hard-to-read text. The image is of striking the paper rather than carefully forming characters.
メモを殴(なぐ)り書(が)きした。
I scribbled down a memo.
彼(かれ)の字(じ)は殴(なぐ)り書(が)きで読(よ)めない。
His handwriting is such a scrawl that I can't read it.
急(いそ)いでいたので、殴(なぐ)り書(が)きのメモだけ残(のこ)して出(で)かけた。
Since I was in a hurry, I left just a hastily scrawled note and went out.

Literally '殴(なぐ)る (to hit/punch) + 書(か)き (writing)' — as if one is punching the paper with a pen. Vivid and slightly self-deprecating. Can be used as a verb: 殴(なぐ)り書(が)きする. Often used when jotting something down quickly without caring about neatness.
