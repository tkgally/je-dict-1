郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)

ゆうびんぶつ
noun
mail; postal matter
1. mail; postal matter; postal item
Letters, packages, and other items sent through the postal system. The standard term for physical mail.
郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)が届(とど)いた。
The mail arrived.
不在(ふざい)で郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)を受(う)け取(と)れなかった。
I was out and couldn't receive the mail.
大量(たいりょう)の郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)が毎日(まいにち)届(とど)くので、仕分(しわ)けが大変(たいへん)だ。
A large volume of mail arrives every day, so sorting it is quite a task.

More formal than 手紙(てがみ) (letter) or 荷物(にもつ) (package), and covers all types of postal items. Common collocations: 郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)を届(とど)ける (to deliver mail), 郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)の転送(てんそう) (mail forwarding), 書留(かきとめ)郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ) (registered mail). Often seen on signs at apartment buildings: 郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)はこちらへ (mail goes here).
