採光(さいこう)

さいこう
noun
natural lighting; daylighting
1. natural lighting; daylighting
The design or practice of letting natural light into a building through windows, skylights, or other openings. An important consideration in architecture and interior design.
採光(さいこう)のいい部屋(へや)だ。
This room has good natural lighting.
大(おお)きな窓(まど)で採光(さいこう)を確保(かくほ)した。
Large windows ensure sufficient natural lighting.
この設計(せっけい)は採光(さいこう)と換気(かんき)を両立(りょうりつ)させている。
This design achieves both natural lighting and ventilation.

採光(さいこう) combines 採(さい) (to gather) and 光(こう) (light) — literally "gathering light."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 採光(さいこう)がいい/わるい — good/poor natural lighting
  • 採光(さいこう)を確保(かくほ)する — to secure natural lighting
  • 採光(さいこう)窓(まど) — window for natural lighting

In Japanese building regulations, residential rooms must meet minimum 採光(さいこう) standards. Real estate listings frequently mention 採光(さいこう) as a selling point, especially for south-facing rooms (南向(みなみむ)き).
