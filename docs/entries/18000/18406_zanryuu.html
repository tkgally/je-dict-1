1. remaining; staying behind
The act of staying in a place when others have left, or continuing to remain in a position or organization.
チームに残留(ざんりゅう)した。
I stayed on the team.
彼(かれ)は海外(かいがい)に残留(ざんりゅう)することを決(き)めた。
He decided to remain overseas.
選手(せんしゅ)の残留(ざんりゅう)か移籍(いせき)かが注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Whether the player will stay or transfer is attracting attention.
2. residual; lingering presence
Substances or effects that remain after a process, especially chemicals or contaminants that persist in food, soil, or the body.
残留(ざんりゅう)農薬(のうやく)が検出(けんしゅつ)された。
Residual pesticides were detected.
食品(しょくひん)に含(ふく)まれる残留(ざんりゅう)物質(ぶっしつ)の基準(きじゅん)が厳(きび)しくなった。
Standards for residual substances in food have become stricter.
体内(たいない)に残留(ざんりゅう)する化学(かがく)物質(ぶっしつ)の影響(えいきょう)が懸念(けねん)されている。
There are concerns about the effects of chemical substances remaining in the body.

残留(ざんりゅう) combines 残(ざん) (remain) and 留(りゅう) (stay), emphasizing staying in place.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 残留(ざんりゅう)農薬(のうやく) — residual pesticides
  • 残留(ざんりゅう)物質(ぶっしつ) — residual substance
  • チームに残留(ざんりゅう)する — to stay on a team
  • 残留(ざんりゅう)邦人(ほうじん) — Japanese nationals remaining abroad

In sports news, 残留(ざんりゅう) is commonly used when discussing whether a player will stay with their current team or transfer (移籍(いせき)). In science and food safety, it refers to chemical residues.
