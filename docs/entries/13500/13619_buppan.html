物販(ぶっぱん)

ぶっぱん
noun
merchandise sales; goods sales
1. merchandise sales; goods sales
The selling of physical goods, as opposed to services or digital products. Commonly refers to merchandise booths at concerts, events, and conventions where goods like T-shirts, keychains, and other items are sold.
物販(ぶっぱん)コーナーに行(い)こう。
Let's go to the merchandise area.
ライブの物販(ぶっぱん)でTシャツを買(か)った。
I bought a T-shirt at the concert merchandise booth.
人気(にんき)グループの物販(ぶっぱん)は開場(かいじょう)前(まえ)から長(なが)い列(れつ)ができる。
The merchandise line for popular groups forms long before the venue opens.

USAGE:
Short for 物品販売(ぶっぴんはんばい) (physical goods sales). While it has a general business meaning of selling physical goods (as opposed to services), in everyday usage it most commonly refers to merchandise sales at concerts, events, sports games, and fan conventions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物販(ぶっぱん)コーナー: merchandise area/booth
  • 物販(ぶっぱん)ブース: merchandise booth
  • 物販(ぶっぱん)列(れつ): merchandise line/queue
  • 物販(ぶっぱん)で並(なら)ぶ: to line up for merchandise
  • 物販(ぶっぱん)開始(かいし): start of merchandise sales
