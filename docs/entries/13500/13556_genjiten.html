現時点(げんじてん)

げんじてん
noun
the present moment, at this point
1. the present moment, at this point in time
Refers to the current point in time, emphasizing that a situation or assessment is based on the present circumstances and may change. Common in formal reports, news, and business communication.
現時点(げんじてん)では未定(みてい)です。
It is undecided at this point.
現時点(げんじてん)で分(わ)かっている情報(じょうほう)をお伝(つた)えします。
I will share the information that is known at this time.
現時点(げんじてん)での被害(ひがい)状況(じょうきょう)は把握(はあく)しきれていないが、今後(こんご)詳(くわ)しく調査(ちょうさ)する予定(よてい)だ。
The extent of the damage at this point has not been fully grasped, but a detailed investigation is planned going forward.

USAGE:
Almost always used with the particle で as 現時点(げんじてん)で or 現時点(げんじてん)では to mean "at this point in time." Implies that the situation may change in the future. Common in news reports, official statements, and business communication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現時点(げんじてん)では (at the present moment)
  • 現時点(げんじてん)での情報(じょうほう) (information as of now)
  • 現時点(げんじてん)で分(わ)かっていること (what is known at this point)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 現在(げんざい): currently, at present (more general, used in both formal and informal contexts)
  • 今(いま)のところ: for now, at this point (more conversational)
  • 目下(もっか): at present, currently (formal, literary)
