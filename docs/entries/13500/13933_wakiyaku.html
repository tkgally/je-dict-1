脇役(わきやく)

わきやく
noun
supporting role; supporting actor
1. supporting role, supporting actor, secondary part
A secondary or supporting role in a play, film, or other performance, as opposed to the lead. Also used figuratively for someone who plays a secondary or supporting part in any situation.
彼(かれ)は脇役(わきやく)ながら存在感(そんざいかん)がある。
Despite being in a supporting role, he has a strong presence.
この映画(えいが)は脇役(わきやく)の演技(えんぎ)が光(ひか)っている。
The supporting actors' performances shine in this film.
主役(しゅやく)を引(ひ)き立(た)てるのが脇役(わきやく)の仕事(しごと)だが、時(とき)には脇役(わきやく)が物語(ものがたり)を動(うご)かすこともある。
The supporting actor's job is to make the lead shine, but sometimes the supporting cast drives the story.

USAGE NOTE:
Also used figuratively outside of entertainment — for example, someone who plays a secondary role in a project or event. The figurative use is not necessarily negative.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脇役(わきやく)を演(えん)じる (play a supporting role)
  • 脇役(わきやく)に徹(てっ)する (commit fully to a supporting role)
  • 脇役(わきやく)ながら (though in a supporting role)

CONTRAST:

  • 主役(しゅやく): lead role, protagonist (opposite of 脇役(わきやく))
  • 端役(はやく): bit part, minor role (even smaller than 脇役(わきやく))
  • 助演(じょえん): supporting performance (used in award categories like 助演(じょえん)男優(だんゆう)賞(しょう))
