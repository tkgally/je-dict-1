石碑(せきひ)

せきひ
noun
stone monument, stele
1. stone monument, stele, inscribed stone
A stone slab or pillar with an inscription, erected to commemorate a person, event, or achievement. Found at historical sites, temples, shrines, and along old roads throughout Japan.
公園(こうえん)に石碑(せきひ)が建(た)っている。
There is a stone monument standing in the park.
石碑(せきひ)に刻(きざ)まれた文字(もじ)を読(よ)んだ。
I read the characters inscribed on the stone monument.
戦争(せんそう)の犠牲者(ぎせいしゃ)を追悼(ついとう)するために石碑(せきひ)が建(た)てられた。
A stone monument was erected to commemorate the war victims.

USAGE:
Commonly found at historical sites, temples, and shrines. The related word 碑(ひ) by itself also means monument/stele but is more literary. 記念碑(きねんひ) specifically means a commemorative monument.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 石碑(せきひ)を建(た)てる: to erect a stone monument
  • 石碑(せきひ)に刻(きざ)む: to inscribe on a monument
  • 石碑(せきひ)が建(た)つ: a monument stands
  • 古(ふる)い石碑(せきひ): an old stone monument
