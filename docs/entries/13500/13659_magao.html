真顔(まがお)

まがお
noun
serious face, straight face
1. serious face, straight face
A facial expression that is serious, earnest, and without a smile or laughter. Often used when someone says something funny or absurd with a completely straight face.
彼(かれ)は真顔(まがお)で言(い)った。
He said it with a straight face.
冗談(じょうだん)かと思(おも)ったが、彼女(かのじょ)は真顔(まがお)だった。
I thought it was a joke, but she was dead serious.
あんなおかしなことを真顔(まがお)で言(い)われると、笑(わら)っていいのか困(こま)る。
When someone says something that ridiculous with a straight face, I don't know whether to laugh or not.

USAGE:
Most commonly appears in the pattern 真顔(まがお)で (with a straight/serious face). Often creates a humorous or surprising effect when paired with absurd or unexpected statements. Can also simply describe genuine seriousness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真顔(まがお)で言(い)う: to say with a straight face
  • 真顔(まがお)になる: to become serious-faced
  • 真顔(まがお)で聞(き)く: to listen with a serious face

Related Words

Antonym: 笑顔(えがお) (smiling face)
