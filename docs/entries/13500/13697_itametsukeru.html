痛(いた)めつける

いためつける
verb (ichidan)
to torment, to beat up
1. to torment, to beat up, to punish severely
To inflict repeated or severe physical or emotional pain on someone. Can refer to physical violence, harsh treatment, or causing great suffering.
弱(よわ)い者(もの)を痛(いた)めつける。
To torment the weak.
相手(あいて)チームを痛(いた)めつけて大勝(たいしょう)した。
We trounced the opposing team and won by a large margin.
台風(たいふう)が町(まち)を痛(いた)めつけ、多(おお)くの家(いえ)が被害(ひがい)を受(う)けた。
The typhoon battered the town and many houses were damaged.

USAGE:
An intensified form of 痛(いた)める (to hurt, to damage). The ～つける ending adds the nuance of doing something forcefully and thoroughly. Can be used for physical violence, but also extends to non-human subjects like storms or economic hardship. In sports contexts, it means to inflict a heavy defeat.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相手(あいて)を痛(いた)めつける: to beat up one's opponent
  • ひどく痛(いた)めつける: to torment severely
  • 散々(さんざん)痛(いた)めつける: to beat up thoroughly
