1. rallying together, concentrating, uniting forces
To gather people, power, or resources together for a common purpose. Often used in political, military, or organizational contexts.
力(ちから)を結集(けっしゅう)して取(と)り組(く)む。
We unite our efforts and tackle the task.
全員(ぜんいん)の知恵(ちえ)を結集(けっしゅう)して問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)しよう。
Let's pool everyone's wisdom and solve the problem.
このプロジェクトは社内(しゃない)の技術(ぎじゅつ)力(りょく)を結集(けっしゅう)して完成(かんせい)させた大作(たいさく)です。
This project is a major achievement completed by pooling the company's technical expertise.

結集(けっしゅう) means to rally or concentrate forces, resources, or efforts for a common purpose. A compound of 結(けつ) (tie, bind) + 集(しゅう) (gather).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 力(ちから)を結集(けっしゅう)する: to unite forces
  • 知恵(ちえ)を結集(けっしゅう)する: to pool wisdom
  • 総力(そうりょく)を結集(けっしゅう)する: to concentrate all resources
  • 英知(えいち)を結集(けっしゅう)する: to pool collective wisdom

USAGE:
Implies purposeful, organized gathering rather than casual assembly. Common in speeches, news, and organizational contexts. Tends toward formal register.

CONTRAST:

  • 集合(しゅうごう): general gathering or assembling (neutral)
  • 集結(しゅうけつ): massing together, converging (often military/physical)
  • 結集(けっしゅう): pooling resources/efforts toward a goal (purposeful, abstract)
