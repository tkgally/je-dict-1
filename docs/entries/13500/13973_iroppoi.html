色(いろ)っぽい

いろっぽい
i-adjective
sexy, alluring, sensual
1. sexy, alluring, sensual
Describes someone or something that has a mature, sensual attractiveness. Implies an elegant, understated kind of sexiness rather than something vulgar. Can describe people, voices, expressions, and atmospheres.
彼女(かのじょ)は色(いろ)っぽい声(こえ)をしている。
She has a sensual voice.
その女優(じょゆう)は年(とし)を重(かさ)ねるほど色(いろ)っぽくなった。
That actress became more alluring as she aged.
着物(きもの)を着(き)た彼女(かのじょ)の後(うし)ろ姿(すがた)がとても色(いろ)っぽかった。
The view of her from behind in a kimono was very alluring.

USAGE:
Carries a positive, appreciative nuance of mature attractiveness. Unlike セクシー(せくしー), which is a more direct loanword, 色(いろ)っぽい implies a subtler, more Japanese-aesthetic sense of allure. Often used to compliment older women and conveys elegance alongside sensuality.

GRAMMAR:

  • Prenominal: 色(いろ)っぽい + noun (色(いろ)っぽい目(め)つき)
  • Adverbial: 色(いろ)っぽく
  • Negative: 色(いろ)っぽくない

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色(いろ)っぽい声(こえ): alluring voice
  • 色(いろ)っぽい目(め)つき: seductive look
  • 色(いろ)っぽい雰囲気(ふんいき): sensual atmosphere
  • 色(いろ)っぽい仕草(しぐさ): alluring gesture
