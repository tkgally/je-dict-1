生(う)まれ変(か)わる

うまれかわる
godan verb
to be reborn, to be reincarnated; to be completely renewed
1. to be reborn, to be reincarnated
To be born again in a new life after death, as in Buddhist or Hindu concepts of reincarnation.
鳥(とり)に生(う)まれ変(か)わりたい。
I want to be reborn as a bird.
もし生(う)まれ変(か)わったら、何(なに)になりたい？
If you were reborn, what would you want to be?
仏教(ぶっきょう)では、人(ひと)は死後(しご)に別(べつ)の姿(すがた)で生(う)まれ変(か)わると信(しん)じられている。
In Buddhism, it is believed that people are reborn in a different form after death.
2. to be completely renewed, to be transformed
To undergo such a thorough change that something appears entirely new, as if reborn. Used figuratively for people, places, or organizations.
街(まち)が生(う)まれ変(か)わった。
The town has been completely transformed.
改装(かいそう)して、店(みせ)が生(う)まれ変(か)わった。
After renovation, the shop was completely renewed.
古(ふる)い工場(こうじょう)が美術館(びじゅつかん)として生(う)まれ変(か)わり、多(おお)くの観光客(かんこうきゃく)が訪(おとず)れている。
The old factory has been reborn as an art museum, attracting many tourists.

USAGE:
A compound verb from 生(う)まれる (to be born) + 変(か)わる (to change). Sense 1 is the literal religious meaning of reincarnation, while sense 2 is the more common figurative usage meaning a dramatic transformation. The figurative use is very productive in modern Japanese.

GRAMMAR:

  • Godan verb (五段(ごだん)), intransitive
  • 生(う)まれ変(か)わり (noun form): rebirth, reincarnation
  • Often used with に to indicate what one is reborn as: 鳥(とり)に生(う)まれ変(か)わる
  • として生(う)まれ変(か)わる: to be reborn/transformed as

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に生(う)まれ変(か)わる: to be reborn as ~
  • ～として生(う)まれ変(か)わる: to be renewed as ~
  • 見違(みちが)えるほど生(う)まれ変(か)わる: to be transformed beyond recognition
