移籍(いせき)

いせき
noun, verb-suru
transfer, moving affiliation
1. transfer of affiliation, moving to a different team or organization
The act of transferring one's official membership or registration from one organization to another. Most commonly used for professional athletes changing teams, but also applies to entertainers changing agencies or members changing organizations.
海外(かいがい)のチームに移籍(いせき)した。
He transferred to an overseas team.
移籍(いせき)金(きん)は十億円(じゅうおくえん)と報道(ほうどう)されている。
The transfer fee is reported to be one billion yen.
あの選手(せんしゅ)の移籍(いせき)が決(き)まり、ファンの間(あいだ)で大(おお)きな話題(わだい)になっている。
That player's transfer has been finalized, and it's become a big topic among fans.

USAGE:
As a する verb, 移籍(いせき)する means "to transfer (to a different team/organization)." The destination is marked with に: ～に移籍(いせき)する. Most frequently heard in sports news.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に移籍(いせき)する: to transfer to ~
  • 移籍(いせき)金(きん): transfer fee
  • 移籍(いせき)が決(き)まる: the transfer is decided
  • 移籍(いせき)先(さき): destination (of the transfer)
  • 移籍(いせき)市場(しじょう): transfer market
  • 自由(じゆう)移籍(いせき): free transfer/free agency

SIMILAR WORDS:
転籍(てんせき) also means transfer of registration but is used more for administrative or family register transfers. 移籍(いせき) is the standard term for sports and entertainment industry transfers.
