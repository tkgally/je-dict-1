祈(いの)り

いのり
noun
prayer, wish
1. prayer, wish, supplication
A heartfelt wish or prayer directed to a deity, spirit, or higher power. Can also refer to a strong personal wish or hope. The nominal form of the verb 祈(いの)る.
祈(いの)りを捧(ささ)げる。
To offer a prayer.
平和(へいわ)への祈(いの)りを込(こ)めて鶴(つる)を折(お)った。
I folded cranes filled with prayers for peace.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)の安全(あんぜん)を願(ねが)う祈(いの)りが、全国(ぜんこく)から届(とど)いた。
Prayers for the safety of the disaster victims arrived from all over the country.

USAGE:
The noun form of 祈(いの)る (to pray). Used in both religious and secular contexts. Unlike 祈願(きがん) (formal supplication) or 祈祷(きとう) (ritual prayer), 祈(いの)り is a general, heartfelt word suitable for everyday use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 祈(いの)りを捧(ささ)げる: to offer a prayer
  • 祈(いの)りを込(こ)める: to put one's prayers into something
  • 祈(いの)りが届(とど)く: prayers reach (are answered)
  • 平和(へいわ)の祈(いの)り: prayer for peace
