総称(そうしょう)

そうしょう
noun, suru verb
general term, generic name
1. general term, collective name
A word or name used to refer to a group of related things collectively rather than individually. Often used when explaining that a single term covers multiple items or categories.
これらを総称(そうしょう)して何(なん)と言(い)いますか。
What do you collectively call these?
柑橘類(かんきつるい)は、みかんやレモンなどの総称(そうしょう)です。
Citrus is a general term for oranges, lemons, and the like.
日本(にほん)の伝統的(でんとうてき)な武道(ぶどう)を総称(そうしょう)して武術(ぶじゅつ)と呼(よ)ぶことがある。
Traditional Japanese martial arts are sometimes collectively called bujutsu.

USAGE:
Typically used in the pattern ～を総称(そうしょう)して…と呼(よ)ぶ (to collectively call ~ ...) or ～の総称(そうしょう) (a general term for ~). Common in explanatory and academic contexts when defining category names.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～を総称(そうしょう)する: to collectively call
  • ～の総称(そうしょう): a general term for
  • 総称(そうしょう)して…と呼(よ)ぶ: to collectively refer to as
