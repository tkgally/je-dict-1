疑(うたが)わしい

うたがわしい
i-adjective
doubtful, suspicious, questionable
1. doubtful, questionable, uncertain
Describes something whose truth, accuracy, or likelihood is uncertain. Used when there is reason to doubt a claim, outcome, or possibility.
成功(せいこう)するかどうか疑(うたが)わしい。
It's doubtful whether it will succeed.
その情報(じょうほう)の信頼性(しんらいせい)は疑(うたが)わしい。
The reliability of that information is questionable.
約束(やくそく)の時間(じかん)に間(ま)に合(あ)うかどうか疑(うたが)わしいので、先(さき)に始(はじ)めてください。
It's doubtful I'll make it in time, so please start without me.
2. suspicious, shady, suspect
Describes a person or situation that arouses suspicion. Implies something is not trustworthy or seems dishonest.
疑(うたが)わしい人物(じんぶつ)が目撃(もくげき)された。
A suspicious individual was spotted.
あの会社(かいしゃ)の経営(けいえい)は疑(うたが)わしいところがある。
There's something suspicious about that company's management.
警察(けいさつ)は疑(うたが)わしい取引(とりひき)がないか、口座(こうざ)の記録(きろく)を調(しら)べている。
The police are examining the account records for any suspicious transactions.

FORMATION:
Derived from the verb 疑(うたが)う (to doubt, to suspect) + しい (adjective-forming suffix). The adjective describes the quality of being doubt-worthy or suspicion-arousing.

USAGE:
Often used predicatively with かどうか疑(うたが)わしい (it's doubtful whether...) to express uncertainty about outcomes. In the suspicious sense, frequently modifies people or actions.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怪(あや)しい: suspicious in a strange or eerie way; broader in meaning
  • 疑(うたが)わしい: specifically suggests reason to doubt truthfulness or legitimacy

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 疑(うたが)わしい人物(じんぶつ): suspicious person
  • 疑(うたが)わしい行動(こうどう): suspicious behavior
  • かどうか疑(うたが)わしい: doubtful whether
