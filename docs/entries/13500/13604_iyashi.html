癒(いや)し

いやし
noun
healing, comfort, soothing
1. healing, comfort, soothing, solace
Emotional or spiritual healing and comfort. Refers to anything that soothes the mind, relieves stress, or provides emotional relief. A popular cultural concept in modern Japan, often associated with relaxation, cute things, nature, and wellness.
ペットは癒(いや)しの存在(そんざい)だ。
Pets are a source of comfort.
温泉(おんせん)に入(はい)ると心(こころ)の癒(いや)しになる。
Soaking in a hot spring is soothing for the mind.
忙(いそが)しい毎日(まいにち)の中(なか)で、自然(しぜん)の中(なか)を散歩(さんぽ)する時間(じかん)が一番(いちばん)の癒(いや)しだと感(かん)じている。
In my busy daily life, I feel that walking in nature is the greatest source of healing.

FORMATION:
Noun form of the verb 癒(いや)す (to heal, to soothe). The し ending creates a noun expressing the concept or experience of healing.

CULTURAL NOTE:
癒(いや)し became a buzzword in Japan from the late 1990s onward, reflecting a growing cultural focus on stress relief and emotional well-being. The concept extends to many areas: 癒(いや)し系(けい) (healing-type) is used to describe people, music, spaces, and products that have a calming or comforting effect.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 癒(いや)しの空間(くうかん): healing/relaxing space
  • 癒(いや)し系(けい): healing-type, soothing (as a personality or aesthetic)
  • 癒(いや)しの音楽(おんがく): healing music
  • 心(こころ)の癒(いや)し: emotional healing
