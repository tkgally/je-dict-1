研(と)ぎ澄(す)ます

とぎすます
verb (godan)
to hone, to sharpen
1. to hone, to sharpen, to refine
To sharpen something to a fine, keen edge. Most commonly used figuratively for sharpening senses, sensibilities, skills, or awareness to an extremely high degree.
神経(しんけい)を研(と)ぎ澄(す)ます。
To sharpen one's nerves.
研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた感覚(かんかく)で音(おと)を聞(き)き分(わ)ける。
To distinguish sounds with sharpened senses.
長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)で研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた職人(しょくにん)の技(わざ)は、見(み)る者(もの)を圧倒(あっとう)する。
The craftsman's skill, honed through years of experience, overwhelms those who watch.

USAGE:
Almost always used figuratively in modern Japanese. The passive form 研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた (honed, refined) is very common as a modifier: 研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた感性(かんせい) (refined sensibility), 研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた神経(しんけい) (sharpened nerves). Conveys a sense of extreme refinement through effort or experience.

KANJI:
研(と)ぐ means "to polish" or "to sharpen" and 澄(す)ます means "to make clear/pure." Together they convey the idea of sharpening something to crystalline clarity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 神経(しんけい)を研(と)ぎ澄(す)ます: to sharpen one's nerves
  • 感覚(かんかく)を研(と)ぎ澄(す)ます: to hone one's senses
  • 研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた感性(かんせい): refined sensibility
  • 研(と)ぎ澄(す)まされた美(うつく)しさ: a keen, refined beauty
