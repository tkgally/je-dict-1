病(やまい)

やまい
noun
illness; disease; bad habit
1. illness; disease
A physical or mental illness. More literary and formal than 病気, often used in set expressions and proverbs.
病(やまい)に倒(たお)れた。
He fell ill.
病(やまい)は気(き)からという言葉(ことば)がある。
There is a saying that illness comes from the mind.
長(なが)い病(やまい)と闘(たたか)いながら、彼女(かのじょ)は最後(さいご)まで前向(まえむ)きだった。
While battling a long illness, she remained positive until the end.
2. bad habit; weakness; obsession
A figurative usage referring to a persistent bad habit, character flaw, or unhealthy obsession that one cannot easily overcome.
浪費(ろうひ)は彼(かれ)の病(やまい)だ。
Spending money wastefully is his bad habit.
完璧(かんぺき)を求(もと)めるのは彼女(かのじょ)の病(やまい)だ。
Seeking perfection is her weakness.
収集(しゅうしゅう)の病(やまい)が始(はじ)まると、家中(いえじゅう)が物(もの)であふれてしまう。
Once the collecting bug takes hold, the whole house overflows with things.

USAGE:
More literary and formal than 病気(びょうき). Often found in proverbs, set phrases, and written language. In everyday conversation, 病気(びょうき) is the standard choice.

KEY PROVERBS:

  • 病(やまい)は気(き)から: illness comes from the mind (attitude affects health)
  • 病(やまい)膏肓(こうこう)に入(い)る: the illness has entered a critical stage; to be beyond help (often used figuratively for obsessions)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病(やまい)に伏(ふ)す: to be bedridden with illness
  • 病(やまい)に倒(たお)れる: to fall ill
  • 病(やまい)と闘(たたか)う: to fight an illness
  • 不治(ふじ)の病(やまい): incurable illness
