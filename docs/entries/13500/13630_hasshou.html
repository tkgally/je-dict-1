発祥(はっしょう)

はっしょう
noun; verb-suru
origin; birthplace
1. origin; birthplace; cradle
The place or point where something originated or began. Used for the origins of cultures, traditions, industries, and other phenomena, often with a sense of historical significance.
ここはラーメンの発祥(はっしょう)の地(ち)だ。
This is the birthplace of ramen.
柔道(じゅうどう)は日本(にほん)発祥(はっしょう)の武道(ぶどう)だ。
Judo is a martial art that originated in Japan.
この祭(まつ)りの発祥(はっしょう)は江戸(えど)時代(じだい)にまで遡(さかのぼ)ると言(い)われている。
The origin of this festival is said to date back to the Edo period.

USAGE:
Most commonly appears in the pattern 発祥(はっしょう)の地(ち) (place of origin, birthplace). Can also be used with する: 発祥(はっしょう)する (to originate).

NUANCE:
Carries a sense of historical or cultural significance. Not used for trivial beginnings — use 始(はじ)まり or 起源(きげん) for more general "origins."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発祥(はっしょう)の地(ち): birthplace, place of origin
  • 発祥(はっしょう)の国(くに): country of origin
  • ～発祥(はっしょう): originating from ~
  • 文化(ぶんか)の発祥(はっしょう): the origin of a culture
  • 発祥(はっしょう)に遡(さかのぼ)る: to trace back to the origins
