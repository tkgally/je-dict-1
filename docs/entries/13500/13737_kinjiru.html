禁(きん)じる

きんじる
verb (ichidan)
to prohibit, to forbid
1. to prohibit, to forbid, to ban
To officially or authoritatively forbid something. Used for rules, laws, and regulations.
喫煙(きつえん)を禁(きん)じる。
To prohibit smoking.
この地域(ちいき)では釣(つ)りが禁(きん)じられている。
Fishing is prohibited in this area.
法律(ほうりつ)で未成年者(みせいねんしゃ)の飲酒(いんしゅ)は禁(きん)じられている。
Drinking by minors is prohibited by law.
2. to suppress, to hold back (an emotion)
To restrain oneself from expressing a feeling. Most commonly appears in the set phrase ...を禁(きん)じ得(え)ない (cannot help but feel...).
涙(なみだ)を禁(きん)じ得(え)なかった。
I could not hold back my tears.
驚(おどろ)きを禁(きん)じ得(え)ない。
I cannot help but feel astonished.
その悲惨(ひさん)な状況(じょうきょう)を見(み)て、同情(どうじょう)の念(ねん)を禁(きん)じ得(え)なかった。
Seeing that tragic situation, I could not help but feel sympathy.

USAGE:
An ichidan verb. The variant 禁(きん)ずる (classical form) also exists but 禁(きん)じる is standard in modern Japanese.

Sense 2 almost always appears in the fixed pattern ～を禁(きん)じ得(え)ない (cannot suppress, cannot help but feel). This is a formal, written expression.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喫煙(きつえん)を禁(きん)じる: to prohibit smoking
  • 法律(ほうりつ)で禁(きん)じる: to prohibit by law
  • 涙(なみだ)を禁(きん)じ得(え)ない: cannot hold back tears
  • 驚(おどろ)きを禁(きん)じ得(え)ない: cannot help but be amazed
