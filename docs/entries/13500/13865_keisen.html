罫線(けいせん)

けいせん
noun
ruled line, grid line
1. ruled line, grid line
Lines printed or drawn on paper to guide writing, or lines used in tables and spreadsheets. Refers to both horizontal and vertical ruling lines.
罫線(けいせん)に沿(そ)って書(か)く。
Write along the ruled lines.
この用紙(ようし)には罫線(けいせん)が引(ひ)いてある。
This paper has ruled lines on it.
エクセルの表(ひょう)に罫線(けいせん)を追加(ついか)して見(み)やすくした。
I added grid lines to the Excel table to make it easier to read.

USAGE:
Used for both physical ruled lines on paper and digital grid lines in spreadsheets and documents. In office and computing contexts, adding or removing 罫線(けいせん) is a common formatting task.

WORD FORMATION:
罫(けい) (ruled line, square) + 線(せん) (line).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罫線(けいせん)を引(ひ)く: draw ruled lines
  • 罫線(けいせん)入(い)り: with ruled lines, lined
  • 罫線(けいせん)に沿(そ)って: along the ruled lines
  • 罫線(けいせん)を追加(ついか)する: add grid lines
