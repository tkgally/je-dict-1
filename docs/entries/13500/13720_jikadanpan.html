直談判(じかだんぱん)

じかだんぱん
noun / suru verb
direct negotiation, going straight to someone
1. direct negotiation, personal appeal
Negotiating or making a request directly with the person in charge, bypassing intermediaries or formal channels. Implies boldness or determination.
社長(しゃちょう)に直談判(じかだんぱん)した。
I went directly to the company president to negotiate.
上司(じょうし)を通(とお)さず、部長(ぶちょう)に直談判(じかだんぱん)するのは勇気(ゆうき)がいる。
It takes courage to go directly to the department head without going through your boss.
家賃(やちん)の値下(ねさ)げを求(もと)めて、大家(おおや)さんに直談判(じかだんぱん)しに行(い)った。
I went to negotiate directly with the landlord to ask for a rent reduction.

USAGE:
The 直(じか) here is the same as in 直(じか)に (directly, in person). This word carries a nuance of bypassing the usual process and going straight to someone with authority or responsibility. It can suggest boldness, resolve, or even slight impropriety depending on context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に直談判(じかだんぱん)する: to negotiate directly with ~
  • 直談判(じかだんぱん)しに行(い)く: to go and negotiate in person
  • 社長(しゃちょう)に直談判(じかだんぱん): to go directly to the president
  • 思(おも)い切(き)って直談判(じかだんぱん)する: to take the plunge and negotiate directly
