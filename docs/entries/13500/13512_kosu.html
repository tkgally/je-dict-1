漉(こ)す

こす
verb (godan)
to strain, to filter
1. to strain, to filter, to sieve
To pass a liquid or mixture through a cloth, sieve, or other filter to separate solids from liquids or to achieve a smoother texture. Common in cooking and traditional crafts.
だしを布(ぬの)で漉(こ)す。
To strain the dashi stock through a cloth.
スープを漉(こ)して滑(なめ)らかにする。
Strain the soup to make it smooth.
茶(ちゃ)こしで茶葉(ちゃば)を漉(こ)してからカップに注(そそ)いでください。
Please strain the tea leaves with a tea strainer before pouring into the cup.

USAGE:
A godan verb (漉(こ)す → 漉(こ)します). Often written in hiragana as こす since the kanji 漉(こ) is not in the standard-use kanji list. Primarily a cooking and craft term.

Note: The homophones 越(こ)す (to cross over) and 超(こ)す (to exceed) are completely different words.

Also used in the compound 紙(かみ)を漉(す)く (to make paper by hand), though this uses a different reading of the same kanji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 布(ぬの)で漉(こ)す: to strain through cloth
  • ざるで漉(こ)す: to strain through a colander
  • 裏(うら)漉(ご)し: straining through a sieve (noun form)
  • だしを漉(こ)す: to strain stock
