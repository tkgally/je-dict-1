直球(ちょっきゅう)

ちょっきゅう
noun
fastball; being direct
1. fastball, straight pitch
In baseball, a pitch thrown straight without spin or break. The most basic type of pitch.
速(はや)い直球(ちょっきゅう)を投(な)げる。
To throw a fast straight pitch.
あのピッチャーの直球(ちょっきゅう)は150キロを超(こ)える。
That pitcher's fastball exceeds 150 km/h.
変化球(へんかきゅう)ではなく直球(ちょっきゅう)で勝負(しょうぶ)した。
He challenged the batter with a fastball instead of a breaking ball.
2. directness, being straightforward, bluntness
A figurative extension from baseball: saying or doing something in a direct, unambiguous way without beating around the bush.
直球(ちょっきゅう)な質問(しつもん)をする。
To ask a direct question.
彼女(かのじょ)は直球(ちょっきゅう)な性格(せいかく)で、思(おも)ったことをそのまま言(い)う。
She has a direct personality and says exactly what she thinks.
直球(ちょっきゅう)で聞(き)くけど、私(わたし)のこと好(す)き？
I'll ask you straight out — do you like me?

USAGE:
Originally a baseball term, but the figurative sense 2 (directness, bluntness) is extremely common in everyday conversation. The na-adjective-like usage 直球(ちょっきゅう)な is widespread in colloquial speech. The adverbial 直球(ちょっきゅう)で means "straightforwardly" or "point-blank."

KANJI:
直(ちょく) means "straight" and 球(きゅう) means "ball." The compound literally means "straight ball."

CONTRAST:
変化球(へんかきゅう) (breaking ball, curveball) is the opposite in both baseball and figurative usage, meaning an indirect or unexpected approach.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 直球(ちょっきゅう)勝負(しょうぶ): straight pitch challenge; head-on approach
  • 直球(ちょっきゅう)な質問(しつもん): a direct question
  • 直球(ちょっきゅう)で聞(き)く: to ask directly

Related Words

Antonym: 変化球(へんかきゅう) (breaking ball; curveball)
