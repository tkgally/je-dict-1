確保(かくほ)

かくほ
noun, suru verb
securing, ensuring
1. securing, obtaining, ensuring
The act of making sure something necessary is obtained or maintained. Used for resources, safety, space, personnel, and other things that require deliberate effort to guarantee.
安全(あんぜん)を確保(かくほ)する。
To ensure safety.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)のスペースを確保(かくほ)しておいた。
I secured a parking space in advance.
人手不足(ひとでぶそく)の中(なか)、十分(じゅうぶん)な人員(じんいん)を確保(かくほ)するのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to secure enough personnel amid a labor shortage.

USAGE:
A very common word in business, news, and official contexts. Used with a wide range of objects: 安全(あんぜん) (safety), 予算(よさん) (budget), 人材(じんざい) (talent), スペース (space), 時間(じかん) (time), etc. The nuance is of actively obtaining and holding onto something that might otherwise not be available.

KANJI:
確(かく) means "certain" or "firm" and 保(ほ) means "to maintain" or "to protect." Together they convey the idea of firmly securing something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安全(あんぜん)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure safety
  • 予算(よさん)を確保(かくほ)する: to secure a budget
  • 人材(じんざい)を確保(かくほ)する: to secure talent/personnel
  • 場所(ばしょ)を確保(かくほ)する: to secure a place
  • 身柄(みがら)を確保(かくほ)する: to take someone into custody (police term)
