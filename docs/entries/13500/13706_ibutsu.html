異物(いぶつ)

いぶつ
noun
foreign body, foreign matter
1. foreign body, foreign matter
Something that does not belong in a particular place — a contaminant in food, a particle in the eye, or an unwanted object in a system. Used in medical, manufacturing, and food safety contexts.
目(め)に異物(いぶつ)が入(はい)った。
A foreign body got into my eye.
食品(しょくひん)に異物(いぶつ)が混入(こんにゅう)していた。
A foreign object was found mixed into the food product.
工場(こうじょう)では異物(いぶつ)混入(こんにゅう)を防(ふせ)ぐために厳(きび)しい管理(かんり)を行(おこな)っている。
The factory has strict controls in place to prevent foreign matter contamination.

USAGE:
Commonly encountered in news reports about food safety incidents and in medical descriptions. The compound 異物(いぶつ)混入(こんにゅう) (foreign matter contamination) is especially frequent in consumer safety discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 異物(いぶつ)混入(こんにゅう): foreign object contamination
  • 異物(いぶつ)を除去(じょきょ)する: to remove a foreign body
  • 異物(いぶつ)感(かん): sensation of a foreign body (e.g., in the throat)
  • 異物(いぶつ)が混(ま)じる: for a foreign object to be mixed in
