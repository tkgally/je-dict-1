現存(げんそん)

げんそん
noun / suru verb
existing, extant, surviving
1. existing, extant, surviving
Still in existence at the present time. Used for buildings, documents, species, and other things that have survived from the past to the present day.
現存(げんそん)する最古(さいこ)の木造(もくぞう)建築(けんちく)だ。
It is the oldest surviving wooden structure.
この文書(ぶんしょ)は現存(げんそん)する唯一(ゆいいつ)の写本(しゃほん)だ。
This document is the only extant manuscript.
江戸時代(えどじだい)に建(た)てられた城(しろ)のうち、天守(てんしゅ)が現存(げんそん)しているのは十二(じゅうに)だけだ。
Of the castles built during the Edo period, only twelve still have their original keeps surviving today.

USAGE:
Common in historical, archaeological, and cultural heritage contexts. The phrase 現存(げんそん)する最古(さいこ)の～ (the oldest surviving ~) is a frequent pattern, especially when discussing historical buildings and artifacts. Also used in biology for species that still exist (as opposed to extinct ones).

GRAMMAR:

  • 現存(げんそん)する + noun: surviving ~, extant ~
  • 現存(げんそん)している: is existing, still exists

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現存(げんそん)する最古(さいこ)の～: the oldest surviving ~
  • 現存(げんそん)する唯一(ゆいいつ)の～: the only surviving ~
  • 現存(げんそん)天守(てんしゅ): extant castle keep
  • 現存(げんそん)種(しゅ): extant species
