肌寒(はだざむ)い

はだざむい
adjective-i
chilly, slightly cold
1. chilly, slightly cold, cool enough to feel on the skin
A mild degree of cold felt on the skin, typically describing weather or air temperature. Conveys a sensation milder than 寒い (cold) — the kind of chill that makes you want a light jacket.
今朝(けさ)は肌寒(はだざむ)い。
It's chilly this morning.
秋(あき)になると朝晩(あさばん)は肌寒(はだざむ)くなる。
When autumn comes, mornings and evenings become chilly.
肌寒(はだざむ)い風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いていたので、カーディガンを羽織(はお)った。
A chilly breeze was blowing, so I threw on a cardigan.

READING NOTE:
This word has two readings: 肌寒(はだざむ)い and 肌寒(はださむ)い. Both are correct and widely used. This entry covers the はだざむい reading.

USAGE:
肌寒(はだざむ)い describes a subtle, skin-level chill — not deep cold, but enough to notice. It is a seasonal word often associated with early autumn or late spring, when daytime is warm but mornings and evenings are cool.

Compared to 寒(さむ)い (cold), 肌寒(はだざむ)い is milder and more specific to the sensation on the skin. Compared to 涼(すず)しい (cool, refreshing), 肌寒(はだざむ)い has a slightly unpleasant nuance — not refreshing, but uncomfortably cool.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肌寒(はだざむ)い朝(あさ): a chilly morning
  • 肌寒(はだざむ)い風(かぜ): a chilly breeze
  • 肌寒(はだざむ)い季節(きせつ): the chilly season
  • 肌寒(はだざむ)く感(かん)じる: to feel chilly
