町人(ちょうにん)

ちょうにん
noun
townsperson, merchant/artisan class
1. townsperson, merchant/artisan class
A member of the urban commoner class during the Edo period (1603-1868), comprising merchants (商人(しょうにん)) and artisans (職人(しょくにん)). In the four-tier social hierarchy, 町人(ちょうにん) ranked below samurai, farmers, and artisans in theory, but many accumulated great wealth and developed a vibrant urban culture.
江戸(えど)には多(おお)くの町人(ちょうにん)が暮(く)らしていた。
Many townspeople lived in Edo.
町人(ちょうにん)文化(ぶんか)は江戸時代(えどじだい)に大(おお)きく発展(はってん)した。
Townspeople's culture developed greatly during the Edo period.
裕福(ゆうふく)な町人(ちょうにん)たちは歌舞伎(かぶき)や浮世絵(うきよえ)といった芸術(げいじゅつ)のパトロンとなり、独自(どくじ)の文化(ぶんか)を育(はぐく)んだ。
Wealthy townspeople became patrons of arts such as kabuki and ukiyo-e, and cultivated a distinctive culture.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
In the Edo-period social hierarchy (士農工商(しのうこうしょう)), 町人(ちょうにん) formally occupied the lowest rung. In practice, however, wealthy merchants often wielded considerable economic power and influence. The 町人(ちょうにん) class drove the development of many cultural forms that define the Edo period, including 歌舞伎(かぶき), 浮世絵(うきよえ), 落語(らくご), and 俳句(はいく).

USAGE:
Primarily a historical term. In modern Japanese, it appears mainly in discussions of Edo-period history and culture. Not used to describe present-day city dwellers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 町人(ちょうにん)文化(ぶんか): townspeople's culture
  • 町人(ちょうにん)階級(かいきゅう): merchant/artisan class
  • 裕福(ゆうふく)な町人(ちょうにん): wealthy townspeople
