細工(さいく)

さいく
noun, suru verb
craftsmanship, handiwork, trick
1. craftsmanship, handiwork, workmanship
Skilled work done by hand, especially detailed or delicate work on objects. Also refers to the finished product of such work.
見事(みごと)な細工(さいく)だ。
That's splendid craftsmanship.
金(きん)の細工(さいく)が施(ほどこ)された箱(はこ)を買(か)った。
I bought a box with gold filigree work on it.
この人形(にんぎょう)は細工(さいく)が細(こま)かくて美(うつく)しい。
This doll has fine, detailed workmanship and is beautiful.
2. trick, scheme, manipulation
A clever or underhanded manipulation, tampering, or scheming done to achieve a desired outcome.
小(こ)細工(ざいく)はやめろ。
Stop playing tricks.
帳簿(ちょうぼ)に細工(さいく)をした疑(うたが)いがある。
There is suspicion that the books were tampered with.
いくら細工(さいく)をしても、いずれ真実(しんじつ)は明(あき)らかになる。
No matter how much you scheme, the truth will come to light eventually.

USAGE:
Used both as a standalone noun and in compounds. In sense 2, often appears with する to mean "to tamper with" or "to manipulate." The compound 小(こ)細工(ざいく) (petty tricks) is very common and has a strongly negative connotation. Note the rendaku: さいく → ざいく in compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 細工(さいく)が細(こま)かい: finely crafted
  • 細工(さいく)を施(ほどこ)す: to apply craftsmanship
  • 細工(さいく)をする: to tamper with, to manipulate
  • 小(こ)細工(ざいく): petty tricks, cheap tricks
  • 竹(たけ)細工(ざいく): bamboo craft
  • 金(きん)細工(ざいく): goldsmithing
