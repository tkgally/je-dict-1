策略(さくりゃく)

さくりゃく
noun
stratagem, scheme, ploy
1. stratagem, scheme, ploy, cunning plan
A clever or cunning plan designed to achieve a goal, often by outwitting others. Usually implies calculating, underhanded, or manipulative intent.
敵(てき)の策略(さくりゃく)にはまった。
I fell for the enemy's ploy.
あれは相手(あいて)の策略(さくりゃく)だと気(き)づいた。
I realized that was the other party's scheme.
巧(たく)みな策略(さくりゃく)を使(つか)って、彼(かれ)は交渉(こうしょう)を有利(ゆうり)に進(すす)めた。
Using a clever stratagem, he steered the negotiations in his favor.

USAGE:
策略(さくりゃく) usually carries a negative connotation, implying deception or manipulation. It is the kind of plan someone devises to trick or outmaneuver others.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 策略(さくりゃく)にはまる: to fall for a scheme
  • 策略(さくりゃく)を練(ね)る: to devise a stratagem
  • 策略(さくりゃく)を使(つか)う: to use a ploy
  • 策略(さくりゃく)を見抜(みぬ)く: to see through a scheme
  • 巧(たく)みな策略(さくりゃく): a clever stratagem

SIMILAR WORDS:
戦略(せんりゃく) means "strategy" and is neutral, used for military, business, and general planning. 策略(さくりゃく) implies cunning and often carries a negative undertone of deception. 計略(けいりゃく) is similar to 策略(さくりゃく) but even more strongly implies a deliberate trap.
