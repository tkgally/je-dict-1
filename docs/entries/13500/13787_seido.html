精度(せいど)

せいど
noun
precision, accuracy
1. precision, accuracy, degree of exactness
The degree to which something is exact, correct, or finely calibrated. Used for measurements, instruments, technology, and processes where accuracy matters.
精度(せいど)が高(たか)いセンサーだ。
It's a high-precision sensor.
AIの翻訳(ほんやく)精度(せいど)が年々(ねんねん)上(あ)がっている。
The accuracy of AI translation is improving year by year.
検査(けんさ)の精度(せいど)を上(あ)げるために、最新(さいしん)の機器(きき)を導入(どうにゅう)した。
We introduced the latest equipment to improve the accuracy of inspections.

USAGE:
精度(せいど) is a measurable, quantifiable concept — it describes how close something is to the true or intended value. Commonly modified by 高(たか)い (high) and 低(ひく)い (low).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精度(せいど)が高(たか)い: high precision
  • 精度(せいど)が低(ひく)い: low precision
  • 精度(せいど)を上(あ)げる: to improve accuracy
  • 精度(せいど)を保(たも)つ: to maintain precision
  • 測定(そくてい)精度(せいど): measurement accuracy
  • 認識(にんしき)精度(せいど): recognition accuracy

SIMILAR WORDS:
正確(せいかく)さ means "accuracy" as a quality (being correct). 精度(せいど) is more technical, referring to the measurable degree of precision, and is the standard term in scientific and engineering contexts.
