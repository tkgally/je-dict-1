管轄(かんかつ)

かんかつ
noun / verb-suru
jurisdiction, administrative control
1. jurisdiction, administrative control
The authority to govern, manage, or administer a particular area or matter. Used for government agencies, police districts, courts, and organizational authority.
この地域(ちいき)は東京都(とうきょうと)の管轄(かんかつ)だ。
This area is under Tokyo's jurisdiction.
管轄(かんかつ)外(がい)の問題(もんだい)なので、対応(たいおう)できません。
It's outside our jurisdiction, so we cannot handle it.
厚生労働省(こうせいろうどうしょう)が管轄(かんかつ)する法律(ほうりつ)は、医療(いりょう)や労働(ろうどう)に関(かん)するものが多(おお)い。
The laws under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare are mostly related to medicine and labor.

USAGE:
管轄(かんかつ) is used in legal, governmental, and organizational contexts. It describes the scope of authority an agency or body has. Can be used as a する verb: 管轄(かんかつ)する (to have jurisdiction over).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 管轄(かんかつ)区域(くいき): jurisdictional area
  • 管轄(かんかつ)外(がい): outside one's jurisdiction
  • 管轄(かんかつ)官庁(かんちょう): competent authority
  • 管轄(かんかつ)裁判所(さいばんしょ): court of jurisdiction
  • 管轄(かんかつ)警察署(けいさつしょ): police station with jurisdiction
