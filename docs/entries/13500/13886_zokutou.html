続投(ぞくとう)

ぞくとう
noun, suru verb
staying on, continuing in a role
1. continuing to pitch (baseball)
In baseball, when a pitcher remains in the game rather than being replaced by a relief pitcher.
エースが九回(きゅうかい)まで続投(ぞくとう)した。
The ace continued pitching through the ninth inning.
監督(かんとく)はピッチャーの続投(ぞくとう)を決(き)めた。
The manager decided to keep the pitcher in the game.
先発(せんぱつ)の投手(とうしゅ)は球数(きゅうすう)が多(おお)かったが、好調(こうちょう)だったため続投(ぞくとう)となった。
The starting pitcher had a high pitch count but was kept in because he was pitching well.
2. staying on in a position, remaining in office
Continuing in a leadership role, position, or job rather than stepping down or being replaced. Commonly used in politics and business.
社長(しゃちょう)が続投(ぞくとう)する方針(ほうしん)だ。
The policy is for the president to remain in office.
大臣(だいじん)は内閣改造(ないかくかいぞう)でも続投(ぞくとう)が決(き)まった。
The minister was confirmed to stay on even after the cabinet reshuffle.
監督(かんとく)は成績(せいせき)不振(ふしん)にもかかわらず続投(ぞくとう)が発表(はっぴょう)され、ファンの間(あいだ)で議論(ぎろん)になった。
Despite poor results, the manager's continuation was announced, sparking debate among fans.

USAGE:
Originally a baseball term, now widely used in politics and business to describe staying in a leadership position. The figurative sense (sense 2) is now more common in everyday news than the baseball sense.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 続投(ぞくとう)する: to continue in a role
  • 続投(ぞくとう)が決(き)まる: continuation is decided
  • 続投(ぞくとう)を表明(ひょうめい)する: to announce one's intention to stay on
  • 続投(ぞくとう)要請(ようせい): request to remain in office
