狭苦(せまくる)しい

せまくるしい
i-adjective
cramped, confined, stuffy
1. cramped, confined, uncomfortably narrow
Describes a space that is unpleasantly small and tight, causing physical or psychological discomfort. Stronger and more negative than 狭(せま)い (narrow), emphasizing the oppressive feeling of being in a small space.
狭苦(せまくる)しい部屋(へや)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in a cramped room.
狭苦(せまくる)しいオフィスで毎日(まいにち)働(はたら)くのはつらい。
Working every day in a cramped office is tough.
荷物(にもつ)が多(おお)すぎて、部屋(へや)が狭苦(せまくる)しくなってしまった。
There was too much luggage and the room became uncomfortably cramped.

USAGE:
An i-adjective (狭苦(せまくる)しい → 狭苦(せまくる)しくない). Formed from 狭(せま)い (narrow) + 苦(くる)しい (painful/uncomfortable), this compound adjective adds an element of discomfort or oppressiveness that plain 狭(せま)い lacks. Use 狭苦(せまくる)しい when emphasizing the unpleasant feeling, not just the physical dimensions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狭苦(せまくる)しい部屋(へや): cramped room
  • 狭苦(せまくる)しい空間(くうかん): confined space
  • 狭苦(せまくる)しい車内(しゃない): cramped vehicle interior
  • 狭苦(せまくる)しく感(かん)じる: to feel cramped
