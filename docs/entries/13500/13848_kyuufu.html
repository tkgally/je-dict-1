給付(きゅうふ)

きゅうふ
noun / verb-suru
benefit, allowance, payment, provision
1. benefit, allowance, payment, provision
Money or services provided by the government, an insurance system, or an organization to eligible individuals. Commonly used for welfare payments, insurance benefits, and government subsidies.
給付金(きゅうふきん)を申請(しんせい)する。
Apply for a benefit payment.
失業(しつぎょう)給付(きゅうふ)を受(う)けている。
I am receiving unemployment benefits.
政府(せいふ)は低所得(ていしょとく)世帯(せたい)に対(たい)して、一人(ひとり)あたり10万円(まんえん)の給付金(きゅうふきん)を支給(しきゅう)すると発表(はっぴょう)した。
The government announced that it would provide a benefit payment of 100,000 yen per person to low-income households.

USAGE:
A key term in discussions of government benefits, social security, and insurance in Japan. Became widely known during the COVID-19 pandemic when the government distributed 特別定額給付金(とくべつていがくきゅうふきん) (special fixed-amount benefits) to all residents.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 支給(しきゅう): provision, supply (from an employer or organization)
  • 手当(てあて): allowance, benefit (often for specific purposes like commuting)
  • 補助金(ほじょきん): subsidy, grant (for projects or organizations)

HOMOPHONES:
Not to be confused with 休符(きゅうふ) (musical rest).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 給付金(きゅうふきん): benefit payment, subsidy
  • 失業(しつぎょう)給付(きゅうふ): unemployment benefits
  • 給付(きゅうふ)を受(う)ける: to receive benefits
  • 給付(きゅうふ)対象(たいしょう): eligible for benefits
  • 現金(げんきん)給付(きゅうふ): cash benefit
