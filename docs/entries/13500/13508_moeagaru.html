燃(も)え上(あ)がる

もえあがる
verb (godan)
to flare up, to burst into flames
1. to flare up, to burst into flames, to blaze up
For fire or flames to suddenly rise up intensely. Describes the dramatic moment when something catches fire or when flames grow in intensity.
炎(ほのお)が燃(も)え上(あ)がった。
The flames flared up.
風(かぜ)で火(ひ)が一気(いっき)に燃(も)え上(あ)がった。
The fire flared up all at once in the wind.
乾燥(かんそう)した落(お)ち葉(ば)に火(ひ)がつき、あっという間(ま)に燃(も)え上(あ)がった。
The dry fallen leaves caught fire and blazed up in an instant.
2. to be inflamed, to burn with passion
For emotions such as anger, passion, or fighting spirit to surge intensely. A figurative extension of the fire imagery.
闘志(とうし)が燃(も)え上(あ)がる。
My fighting spirit is fired up.
観客(かんきゃく)の応援(おうえん)で選手(せんしゅ)たちが燃(も)え上(あ)がった。
The athletes were fired up by the cheering of the spectators.
不正(ふせい)に対(たい)する怒(いか)りが国中(くにじゅう)で燃(も)え上(あ)がり、大規模(だいきぼ)なデモに発展(はってん)した。
Anger against the injustice flared up across the country, developing into large-scale protests.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (燃(も)え上(あ)がる → 燃(も)え上(あ)がります). A compound verb formed from 燃(も)える (to burn) + 上(あ)がる (to rise). Intransitive — there is no direct transitive counterpart.

The 上(あ)がる component adds the nuance of upward movement and sudden intensification, distinguishing this from the more neutral 燃(も)える.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 炎(ほのお)が燃(も)え上(あ)がる: flames flare up
  • 闘志(とうし)が燃(も)え上(あ)がる: fighting spirit flares up
  • 怒(いか)りが燃(も)え上(あ)がる: anger flares up
  • 一気(いっき)に燃(も)え上(あ)がる: to blaze up all at once
