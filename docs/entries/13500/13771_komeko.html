米粉(こめこ)

こめこ
noun
rice flour
1. rice flour
Flour made from finely ground rice. Used widely in Japanese and Asian cooking for making sweets, noodles, bread, and as a gluten-free alternative to wheat flour.
米粉(こめこ)でパンを焼(や)く。
To bake bread with rice flour.
米粉(こめこ)は小麦(こむぎ)アレルギーの人(ひと)にも安心(あんしん)だ。
Rice flour is safe for people with wheat allergies.
最近(さいきん)は米粉(こめこ)を使(つか)ったお菓子(かし)やパスタの種類(しゅるい)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently, the variety of sweets and pasta made with rice flour has been increasing.

USAGE:
米粉(こめこ) has gained popularity as a gluten-free ingredient. In traditional Japanese sweets (和菓子(わがし)), specific types of rice flour are used: 上新粉(じょうしんこ) (from non-glutinous rice) and 白玉粉(しらたまこ) (from glutinous rice).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 米粉(こめこ)パン: rice flour bread
  • 米粉(こめこ)麺(めん): rice flour noodles
  • 米粉(こめこ)を使(つか)った: made with rice flour
  • 米粉(こめこ)のお菓子(かし): rice flour sweets
