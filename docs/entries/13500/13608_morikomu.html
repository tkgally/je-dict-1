盛(も)り込(こ)む

もりこむ
godan verb
to incorporate, to include, to pack in
1. to incorporate, to include, to work into
To include or incorporate content, ideas, or elements into a plan, document, proposal, or creative work. Often used in business and formal contexts when adding items to plans or policies.
新(あたら)しい提案(ていあん)を計画(けいかく)に盛(も)り込(こ)んだ。
I incorporated the new proposal into the plan.
予算(よさん)案(あん)に教育(きょういく)支援(しえん)の項目(こうもく)を盛(も)り込(こ)む予定(よてい)だ。
We plan to incorporate an education support item into the budget proposal.
契約書(けいやくしょ)には双方(そうほう)の権利(けんり)と義務(ぎむ)を明確(めいかく)に盛(も)り込(こ)む必要(ひつよう)がある。
It is necessary to clearly incorporate the rights and obligations of both parties into the contract.
2. to heap into, to pack into, to fill with
To pile or pack food or other items generously into a container. The literal, physical sense of filling something up.
お弁当(べんとう)箱(ばこ)におかずを盛(も)り込(こ)んだ。
I packed the side dishes into the lunch box.
大(おお)きな器(うつわ)にサラダをたっぷり盛(も)り込(こ)んだ。
I heaped a generous amount of salad into a large bowl.
丼(どんぶり)に具材(ぐざい)をこれでもかと盛(も)り込(こ)んで、豪快(ごうかい)な一品(いっぴん)に仕上(しあ)げた。
I packed the bowl to the brim with toppings and finished it as a hearty dish.

FORMATION:
盛(も)る (to heap, to serve) + 込(こ)む (into). The 込(こ)む suffix adds the nuance of putting something into or packing something in.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (incorporate) is more common in modern usage, especially in business, government, and media contexts. The passive form 盛(も)り込(こ)まれる (to be incorporated) is very frequent in news articles about policy and legislation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 計画(けいかく)に盛(も)り込(こ)む: incorporate into a plan
  • 予算(よさん)に盛(も)り込(こ)む: include in a budget
  • 内容(ないよう)を盛(も)り込(こ)む: incorporate content
  • 条件(じょうけん)を盛(も)り込(こ)む: include conditions
