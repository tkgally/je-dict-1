直通(ちょくつう)

ちょくつう
noun, suru verb
direct connection, nonstop service
1. direct connection, nonstop service
Going directly from one place to another without stopping, transferring, or passing through an intermediary. Used for transportation routes, phone lines, and similar connections.
空港(くうこう)への直通(ちょくつう)バスがある。
There is a direct bus to the airport.
この電話(でんわ)は社長室(しゃちょうしつ)に直通(ちょくつう)している。
This phone connects directly to the president's office.
新宿(しんじゅく)から箱根(はこね)まで直通(ちょくつう)運転(うんてん)の特急(とっきゅう)が走(はし)っているので乗(の)り換(か)えなしで行(い)ける。
A direct express train runs from Shinjuku to Hakone, so you can get there without transferring.

USAGE:
Used as a modifier (直通(ちょくつう)の or compound: 直通(ちょくつう)バス, 直通(ちょくつう)電話(でんわ)) or as a suru verb (直通(ちょくつう)する). Very common in transportation (trains, buses) and telecommunications (direct phone lines).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 直通(ちょくつう)バス: direct bus
  • 直通(ちょくつう)電車(でんしゃ): direct train
  • 直通(ちょくつう)電話(でんわ): direct phone line
  • 直通(ちょくつう)運転(うんてん): through-service (trains running across different lines)
  • 直通(ちょくつう)ダイヤル: direct dial
