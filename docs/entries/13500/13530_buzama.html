無様(ぶざま)

ぶざま
na-adjective / noun
unsightly, clumsy, disgraceful
1. unsightly, clumsy, disgraceful
Describes an appearance or action that is awkward, ungainly, or embarrassingly poor in form. Often implies that someone has failed to maintain dignity or composure.
無様(ぶざま)な姿(すがた)を見(み)せた。
I showed an unsightly figure.
転(ころ)んで無様(ぶざま)な格好(かっこう)になってしまった。
I fell and ended up in a disgraceful position.
大勢(おおぜい)の前(まえ)で無様(ぶざま)に負(ま)けるわけにはいかない。
I can't afford to lose disgracefully in front of so many people.

USAGE:
A strongly negative word that emphasizes the lack of grace, dignity, or composure. Often used about oneself in a self-deprecating way, or about someone who has failed publicly. The adverbial form 無様(ぶざま)に is common before verbs of failure or defeat.

KANJI NOTE:
無(ぶ) (without) + 様(ざま) (appearance, state). Literally "without [proper] form." The character 様(ざま) here refers to appearance or bearing, not the polite suffix ～様(さま).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無様(ぶざま)な姿(すがた): unsightly appearance
  • 無様(ぶざま)な負(ま)け方(かた): disgraceful way of losing
  • 無様(ぶざま)に倒(たお)れる: to fall ungracefully
