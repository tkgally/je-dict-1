脅(おびや)かす

おびやかす
verb-godan
to threaten, to endanger
1. to threaten, to menace, to endanger
To put something at risk or to pose a danger to something. Used for abstract threats to safety, stability, positions, or existence — not typically for direct verbal threats to a person.
地震(じしん)が安全(あんぜん)を脅(おびや)かす。
Earthquakes threaten safety.
環境(かんきょう)破壊(はかい)が多(おお)くの種(しゅ)の生存(せいぞん)を脅(おびや)かしている。
Environmental destruction is threatening the survival of many species.
テロの脅威(きょうい)が国際(こくさい)社会(しゃかい)の平和(へいわ)を脅(おびや)かし続(つづ)けている。
The threat of terrorism continues to endanger peace in the international community.
2. to challenge, to rival, to press hard
To approach closely in competition, threatening to overtake or surpass someone's position or achievement.
新人(しんじん)がベテランの地位(ちい)を脅(おびや)かしている。
The newcomer is threatening the veteran's position.
二位(にい)のチームが首位(しゅい)を脅(おびや)かす勢(いきお)いだ。
The second-place team has the momentum to challenge the leader.
急成長(きゅうせいちょう)する企業(きぎょう)が業界(ぎょうかい)トップの座(ざ)を脅(おびや)かしつつある。
A rapidly growing company is beginning to challenge the industry leader's top spot.

USAGE:
脅(おびや)かす describes abstract or systemic threats — endangering safety, peace, survival, or positions. It differs from 脅(おど)す, which means to directly intimidate or threaten a person (e.g., with words or force). 脅(おびや)かす is を-transitive (the thing threatened takes を).

Conjugation: godan verb (脅(おびや)かさない / 脅(おびや)かします / 脅(おびや)かして).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安全(あんぜん)を脅(おびや)かす: to threaten safety
  • 平和(へいわ)を脅(おびや)かす: to threaten peace
  • 地位(ちい)を脅(おびや)かす: to threaten someone's position
  • 生存(せいぞん)を脅(おびや)かす: to endanger survival
  • 首位(しゅい)を脅(おびや)かす: to challenge the top spot
