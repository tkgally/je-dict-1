秘(ひ)める

ひめる
ichidan verb
to keep secret, to harbor
1. to keep secret, to hide within oneself, to harbor
To conceal something — often feelings, thoughts, or intentions — within oneself rather than expressing or revealing them. Frequently used in literary and emotional contexts.
胸(むね)に秘(ひ)めた思(おも)い。
Feelings hidden in one's heart.
この土地(とち)は不思議(ふしぎ)な力(ちから)を秘(ひ)めている。
This land harbors a mysterious power.
あの選手(せんしゅ)は大(おお)きな可能性(かのうせい)を秘(ひ)めていると言(い)われている。
That athlete is said to harbor great potential.

USAGE:
秘(ひ)める often describes something concealed within a person or thing that has not yet been revealed. It has a literary, slightly elevated tone and is common in novels and formal speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 胸(むね)に秘(ひ)める: to keep in one's heart
  • 思(おも)いを秘(ひ)める: to harbor feelings
  • 可能性(かのうせい)を秘(ひ)める: to hold potential
  • 力(ちから)を秘(ひ)める: to harbor power
  • 秘(ひ)めた決意(けつい): hidden determination

SIMILAR WORDS:
隠(かく)す is a more everyday word for hiding or concealing things physically or metaphorically. 秘(ひ)める emphasizes keeping something deep within, often feelings or potential, and carries a more literary tone.
