秀(ひい)でる

ひいでる
verb (ichidan)
to excel, to surpass, to stand out
1. to excel, to surpass others, to stand out
To be superior to others in ability, skill, or quality. A literary and somewhat formal verb, often used in the form 秀(ひい)でた (excelling, outstanding) as a modifier.
彼(かれ)は数学(すうがく)に秀(ひい)でている。
He excels in mathematics.
秀(ひい)でた才能(さいのう)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
She is a person of outstanding talent.
多(おお)くの応募者(おうぼしゃ)の中(なか)で、彼女(かのじょ)の作品(さくひん)はひときわ秀(ひい)でていた。
Among the many applicants, her work stood out remarkably.

USAGE:
An ichidan verb often used in the te-form 秀(ひい)でている (excels, is outstanding) or as the prenominal modifier 秀(ひい)でた (outstanding, excellent). Takes the particle に to indicate the field of excellence.

More literary than 優(すぐ)れる (to be superior), which is the more common everyday equivalent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に秀(ひい)でる: to excel in...
  • 秀(ひい)でた才能(さいのう): outstanding talent
  • 秀(ひい)でた能力(のうりょく): exceptional ability
  • ひときわ秀(ひい)でる: to stand out remarkably
