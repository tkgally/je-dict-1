立(た)て続(つづ)け

たてつづけ
noun, adverb
in succession, one after another
1. in rapid succession, one after another, in a row
Describes events or actions occurring one right after another without pause. Most often used with the particle に as 立て続けに.
事故(じこ)が立(た)て続(つづ)けに起(お)きた。
Accidents happened one after another.
立(た)て続(つづ)けに三杯(さんばい)もコーヒーを飲(の)んだ。
I drank three cups of coffee in a row.
悪(わる)いニュースが立(た)て続(つづ)けに届(とど)いて、さすがに落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
Bad news kept arriving one after another, and I understandably felt down.

USAGE:
Almost always used with the particle に as 立(た)て続(つづ)けに. It emphasizes that events happen in rapid or immediate succession, often implying that the frequency is notable or surprising.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立(た)て続(つづ)けに起(お)きる: to happen in succession
  • 立(た)て続(つづ)けに飲(の)む: to drink one after another
  • 立(た)て続(つづ)けに質問(しつもん)する: to ask questions one after another
  • 立(た)て続(つづ)けに失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail repeatedly in a row

SIMILAR WORDS:
連続(れんぞく) means "consecutive" or "continuous" and is more neutral, often used for numbered sequences. 立(た)て続(つづ)けに emphasizes the rapid-fire, unexpected quality of repeated events.
