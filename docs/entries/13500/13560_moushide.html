申(もう)し出(で)

もうしで
noun
offer, proposal, request
1. offer, proposal, request, application
A formal or semi-formal statement expressing willingness to do something, making a request, or putting forward a proposal. Derived from the verb 申し出る (to offer, to propose). Used in business and official contexts.
彼(かれ)の申(もう)し出(で)を受(う)け入(い)れた。
I accepted his offer.
ボランティアの申(もう)し出(で)が多数(たすう)寄(よ)せられた。
Numerous offers to volunteer were received.
退職(たいしょく)の申(もう)し出(で)は、少(すく)なくとも一(いっ)か月(げつ)前(まえ)までに上司(じょうし)に伝(つた)えてください。
Please inform your supervisor of your resignation request at least one month in advance.

USAGE:
A formal noun derived from the verb 申(もう)し出(で)る. Used to refer to offers, proposals, and requests in business, administrative, and official settings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 申(もう)し出(で)を受(う)ける (receive an offer)
  • 申(もう)し出(で)を受(う)け入(い)れる (accept an offer)
  • 申(もう)し出(で)を断(ことわ)る (decline an offer)
  • 退職(たいしょく)の申(もう)し出(で) (resignation request)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 提案(ていあん): proposal, suggestion (more about ideas or plans)
  • 申請(しんせい): application, formal request (more bureaucratic, involves paperwork)
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み: application, entry (specifically for registering or signing up for something)
