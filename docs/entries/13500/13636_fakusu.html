ファクス

ふぁくす
noun
fax; fax machine
1. fax; fax machine; facsimile
A fax machine or a document sent by fax. Fax machines remain widely used in Japan, particularly in business, government offices, and medical settings, far more than in most other developed countries.
ファクスで送(おく)ってください。
Please send it by fax.
ファクスが届(とど)いたら確認(かくにん)の電話(でんわ)をください。
Please call to confirm when the fax arrives.
日本(にほん)では今(いま)でもファクスを使(つか)っている会社(かいしゃ)や役所(やくしょ)が多(おお)い。
In Japan, many companies and government offices still use fax machines.

USAGE:
Also written as ファックス. Both spellings are common. The shorter form ファクス is used in official JIS standards and some style guides, while ファックス is common in everyday writing.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Fax machines remain much more prevalent in Japan than in most other countries. Many businesses, hospitals, and government agencies still rely on fax for official documents, even well into the digital age. This is often noted by foreign visitors as a distinctive feature of Japanese business culture.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ファクスを送(おく)る: to send a fax
  • ファクスが届(とど)く: a fax arrives
  • ファクス番号(ばんごう): fax number
  • ファクス機(き): fax machine
