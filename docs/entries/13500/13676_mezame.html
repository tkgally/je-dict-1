目覚(めざ)め

めざめ
noun
awakening
See also: 目覚(めざ)める (verb form)
1. waking up, awakening
The act or moment of waking from sleep. Can describe the quality of how one wakes up.
今朝(けさ)は目覚(めざ)めがよかった。
I woke up feeling refreshed this morning.
春(はる)の目覚(めざ)めとともに花(はな)が咲(さ)き始(はじ)めた。
Flowers began to bloom with the awakening of spring.
コーヒーの香(かお)りで心地(ここち)よい目覚(めざ)めを迎(むか)えた。
I had a pleasant awakening to the aroma of coffee.
2. awakening, realization
A figurative awakening — becoming aware of something new, developing a new consciousness or interest.
芸術(げいじゅつ)への目覚(めざ)め。
An awakening to art.
あの旅(たび)が自然(しぜん)への目覚(めざ)めのきっかけだった。
That trip was what sparked my awakening to nature.
十代(じゅうだい)の頃(ころ)に政治(せいじ)への目覚(めざ)めを経験(けいけん)し、活動(かつどう)を始(はじ)めた。
I experienced a political awakening in my teens and started getting involved.

USAGE:
The nominal form of the verb 目覚(めざ)める (to wake up, to awaken). In sense 1, often used with adjectives describing how one wakes up: 目覚(めざ)めがいい (wakes up well), 目覚(めざ)めが悪(わる)い (wakes up poorly). In sense 2, the pattern ～への目覚(めざ)め (awakening to ~) is very common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目覚(めざ)めがいい: to wake up feeling good
  • 目覚(めざ)めが悪(わる)い: to wake up feeling groggy
  • ～への目覚(めざ)め: awakening to ~
  • 目覚(めざ)めを迎(むか)える: to experience an awakening
