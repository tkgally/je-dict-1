真骨頂(しんこっちょう)

しんこっちょう
noun
true worth, one's forte, true form
1. true worth, one's forte, true form, element
The quality or ability that represents someone's or something's best and most characteristic aspect. Used when highlighting what makes a person, group, or thing truly outstanding.
これが彼(かれ)の真骨頂(しんこっちょう)だ。
This is where he truly shines.
後半(こうはん)の逆転劇(ぎゃくてんげき)こそ、このチームの真骨頂(しんこっちょう)だ。
The second-half comeback is this team's true forte.
繊細(せんさい)な色使(いろづか)いに画家(がか)の真骨頂(しんこっちょう)が表(あらわ)れている。
The painter's true artistry is revealed in the delicate use of color.

USAGE:
Often used in the pattern ○○の真骨頂(しんこっちょう) or ○○こそ真骨頂(しんこっちょう) to highlight someone's defining strength or best quality. Common in sports commentary, arts reviews, and discussions of craftsmanship.

ETYMOLOGY:
真(しん) (true) + 骨頂(こっちょう) (peak, highest point). Originally from Buddhist terminology.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ○○の真骨頂(しんこっちょう): the true worth/forte of ○○
  • ○○こそ真骨頂(しんこっちょう): ○○ is truly where [one] shines
  • 真骨頂(しんこっちょう)を発揮(はっき)する: to demonstrate one's true worth
  • 真骨頂(しんこっちょう)が表(あらわ)れる: one's true form shows through
