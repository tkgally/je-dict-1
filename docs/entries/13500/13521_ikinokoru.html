生(い)き残(のこ)る

いきのこる
godan verb
to survive, to remain alive
1. to survive, to remain alive
To continue living through a dangerous or difficult situation such as a disaster, war, or illness.
奇跡的(きせきてき)に生(い)き残(のこ)った。
I miraculously survived.
地震(じしん)で家(いえ)は壊(こわ)れたが、家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)が生(い)き残(のこ)った。
The house was destroyed in the earthquake, but the entire family survived.
戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き残(のこ)った人(ひと)たちの証言(しょうげん)が記録(きろく)されている。
The testimonies of people who survived the war have been recorded.
2. to survive (in competition), to remain viable
To continue to exist or stay relevant in a competitive environment such as business, sports, or evolution.
厳(きび)しい競争(きょうそう)を生(い)き残(のこ)る。
To survive fierce competition.
この業界(ぎょうかい)で生(い)き残(のこ)るのは簡単(かんたん)ではない。
It's not easy to survive in this industry.
変化(へんか)に適応(てきおう)できた企業(きぎょう)だけが市場(しじょう)で生(い)き残(のこ)ることができる。
Only companies that can adapt to change will be able to survive in the market.

USAGE:
A compound verb formed from 生(い)きる (to live) + 残(のこ)る (to remain). Intransitive — the subject is the person or thing that survives. Sense 1 refers to physical survival, while sense 2 extends to figurative survival in competitive contexts.

GRAMMAR:

  • Godan verb (五段(ごだん)), intransitive
  • を生(い)き残(のこ)る: can take を to mark the situation survived (e.g., 戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き残(のこ)る)
  • 生(い)き残(のこ)り (noun form): survivor, survival

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き残(のこ)る: to survive a war
  • 競争(きょうそう)を生(い)き残(のこ)る: to survive competition
  • 奇跡的(きせきてき)に生(い)き残(のこ)る: to miraculously survive
