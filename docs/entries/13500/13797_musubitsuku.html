結(むす)びつく

むすびつく
verb (godan)
to be connected, to be linked
1. to be connected, to be linked, to be tied together
For two or more things to be joined or associated with each other, either physically or conceptually. Often implies a meaningful or significant connection.
努力(どりょく)が成功(せいこう)に結(むす)びついた。
Hard work led to success.
この地域(ちいき)は歴史的(れきしてき)に海上(かいじょう)貿易(ぼうえき)と結(むす)びついている。
This region is historically connected to maritime trade.
研究(けんきゅう)の成果(せいか)が新(あたら)しい製品(せいひん)の開発(かいはつ)に結(むす)びつくことが期待(きたい)されている。
It is hoped that the research results will lead to the development of new products.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (結(むす)びつく → 結(むす)びつきます). Intransitive. The pattern is AがBに結(むす)びつく (A connects to / leads to B) or AとBが結(むす)びつく (A and B are connected).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 成果(せいか)に結(むす)びつく: to lead to results
  • 成功(せいこう)に結(むす)びつく: to lead to success
  • 利益(りえき)に結(むす)びつく: to be linked to profit
  • 密接(みっせつ)に結(むす)びつく: to be closely connected

SIMILAR WORDS:
The transitive counterpart is 結(むす)びつける (to tie together, to connect). 繋(つな)がる is a more general word for being connected, while 結(むす)びつく emphasizes a meaningful or significant bond.
