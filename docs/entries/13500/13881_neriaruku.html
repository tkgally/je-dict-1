練(ね)り歩(ある)く

ねりあるく
godan verb
to parade, to march through
1. to parade, to march in procession
To walk slowly and deliberately in a group through streets or an area, typically as part of a festival, celebration, or demonstration.
神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)いで町(まち)を練(ね)り歩(ある)く。
They parade through town carrying the portable shrine.
仮装(かそう)した人(ひと)たちが通(とお)りを練(ね)り歩(ある)いた。
People in costumes paraded through the street.
祭(まつ)りの日(ひ)には山車(だし)が商店街(しょうてんがい)を練(ね)り歩(ある)き、沿道(えんどう)の観客(かんきゃく)がにぎやかに声(こえ)を上(あ)げた。
On the festival day, floats paraded through the shopping street, and spectators along the route cheered loudly.

CONJUGATION:

Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)), く-ending.

  • Negative: 練(ね)り歩(ある)かない
  • Polite: 練(ね)り歩(ある)きます
  • Te-form: 練(ね)り歩(ある)いて
  • Past: 練(ね)り歩(ある)いた

USAGE:
Often associated with festivals (祭(まつ)り), processions, and demonstrations. The 練(ね)り component implies a slow, deliberate, stately movement rather than ordinary walking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 町(まち)を練(ね)り歩(ある)く: to parade through town
  • 通(とお)りを練(ね)り歩(ある)く: to march along the street
  • 神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)いで練(ね)り歩(ある)く: to parade carrying a portable shrine
