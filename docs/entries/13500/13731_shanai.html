社内(しゃない)

しゃない
noun
within a company, in-house
1. within a company, in-house, internal
The inside of a company or organization. Used as a noun or as a prefix to describe things that are internal to a company, such as rules, events, or communication.
社内(しゃない)メールを送(おく)る。
To send an internal company email.
社内(しゃない)の規則(きそく)を守(まも)らなければならない。
You must follow the company's internal rules.
社内(しゃない)研修(けんしゅう)で新(あたら)しいシステムの使(つか)い方(かた)を学(まな)んだ。
I learned how to use the new system at an in-house training session.

USAGE:
Frequently used as a prefix modifying nouns to indicate something internal to a company. Not to be confused with 車内(しゃない) (inside a vehicle), which has the same reading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社内(しゃない)規定(きてい): company regulations
  • 社内(しゃない)研修(けんしゅう): in-house training
  • 社内(しゃない)恋愛(れんあい): office romance
  • 社内(しゃない)報告(ほうこく): internal report
