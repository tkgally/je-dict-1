絶好(ぜっこう)

ぜっこう
noun
best, ideal, perfect (opportunity or condition)
1. best, ideal, perfect, golden
The best possible condition or opportunity. Almost always modifies nouns with の, describing conditions, chances, timing, or locations that are perfectly suited for something.
絶好(ぜっこう)のチャンスだ。
It's a golden opportunity.
今日(きょう)は花見(はなみ)に絶好(ぜっこう)の天気(てんき)だ。
Today's weather is perfect for cherry blossom viewing.
この丘(おか)は街(まち)を見渡(みわた)す絶好(ぜっこう)のスポットだ。
This hill is the perfect spot for overlooking the city.

USAGE:
Almost always used as a modifier with の: 絶好(ぜっこう)の + noun. Common nouns it modifies include チャンス (chance), 機会(きかい) (opportunity), 天気(てんき)/日和(びより) (weather), 条件(じょうけん) (conditions), and スポット/場所(ばしょ) (spot/place). Rarely used as a standalone noun or predicate.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最高(さいこう): the best, the greatest (broader usage)
  • 最適(さいてき): most suitable, optimal (more analytical)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶好(ぜっこう)のチャンス: golden opportunity
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)の機会(きかい): perfect opportunity
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)の天気(てんき): perfect weather
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)の日和(びより): ideal weather (for an activity)
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)の条件(じょうけん): ideal conditions
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)の場所(ばしょ): perfect location
