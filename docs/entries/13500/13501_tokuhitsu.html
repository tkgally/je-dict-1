特筆(とくひつ)

とくひつ
noun, verb-suru
special mention, noteworthy
1. special mention, singling out for note
The act of highlighting something as particularly noteworthy or remarkable, worthy of being written about separately from ordinary matters.
特筆(とくひつ)すべき成果(せいか)だ。
It is a noteworthy achievement.
特筆(とくひつ)に値(あたい)する出来事(できごと)はなかった。
There were no events worth special mention.
この地域(ちいき)の歴史(れきし)の中(なか)で特筆(とくひつ)すべきは、江戸(えど)時代(じだい)の城下町(じょうかまち)としての発展(はってん)である。
What is particularly noteworthy in the history of this region is its development as a castle town in the Edo period.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the fixed patterns 特筆(とくひつ)すべき (noteworthy, worth mentioning) and 特筆(とくひつ)に値(あたい)する (worthy of special mention). Rarely used as a standalone verb. A formal, written-style word common in reports, reviews, and academic writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 特筆(とくひつ)すべき: noteworthy, deserving special mention
  • 特筆(とくひつ)に値(あたい)する: worthy of special note
  • 特筆(とくひつ)すべき点(てん): noteworthy point
  • 特筆(とくひつ)すべき成果(せいか): remarkable achievement
