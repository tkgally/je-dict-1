続報(ぞくほう)

ぞくほう
noun
follow-up report, update, further news
1. follow-up report, update, further news
Additional news or information that follows an initial report on the same topic. Common in journalism and news media.
続報(ぞくほう)が入(はい)り次第(しだい)お伝(つた)えします。
We will report as soon as further news comes in.
事件(じけん)の続報(ぞくほう)を待(ま)っている。
I'm waiting for a follow-up report on the incident.
台風(たいふう)の被害(ひがい)について続報(ぞくほう)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
A follow-up report on the typhoon damage was released.

USAGE:
A news and media term. The phrase 続報(ぞくほう)が入(はい)り次第(しだい) (as soon as further news comes in) is a standard journalistic expression heard on TV news and in online reporting.

WORD FORMATION:
続(ぞく) (continue, follow) + 報(ほう) (report, news).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 速報(そくほう): breaking news, flash report
  • 第一報(だいいっぽう): first report, initial news
  • 詳報(しょうほう): detailed report

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 続報(ぞくほう)が入(はい)る: further news comes in
  • 続報(ぞくほう)を待(ま)つ: wait for an update
  • 続報(ぞくほう)が入(はい)り次第(しだい): as soon as there is an update
