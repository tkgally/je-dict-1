特段(とくだん)

とくだん
adverb, noun
particularly, especially
1. particularly, especially, notably
Indicates that something stands out to a notable degree, or that there is nothing particularly noteworthy. Very commonly used with negatives to mean 'nothing in particular' or 'not especially.'
特段(とくだん)変(か)わったことはない。
Nothing particularly unusual is going on.
特段(とくだん)の理由(りゆう)がなければ、参加(さんか)してください。
Please participate unless you have a particular reason not to.
今回(こんかい)の検査(けんさ)結果(けっか)には特段(とくだん)問題(もんだい)は見(み)られなかったので、ご安心(あんしん)ください。
No particular problems were found in this examination, so please rest assured.

USAGE:
Used as an adverb (特段(とくだん)) or as a noun modifier (特段(とくだん)の). Very frequently used with negative expressions to mean "nothing in particular" or "not especially." It has a somewhat formal tone and is common in business, official, and written contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 特段(とくだん)の問題(もんだい)はない (no particular problems)
  • 特段(とくだん)の理由(りゆう) (particular reason)
  • 特段(とくだん)変(か)わりない (nothing particularly different)
  • 特段(とくだん)の支障(ししょう)はない (no particular hindrance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 特(とく)に: especially, particularly (more common in everyday speech)
  • 格別(かくべつ): exceptional, special (emphasizes standing out positively)
  • 別段(べつだん): (not) particularly (similar usage with negatives, slightly more literary)
