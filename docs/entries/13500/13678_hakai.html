破壊(はかい)

はかい
noun, suru verb
destruction
1. destruction, demolition
The act of physically breaking or destroying something such as buildings, structures, or objects.
建物(たてもの)を破壊(はかい)する。
To destroy a building.
地震(じしん)で多(おお)くの家屋(かおく)が破壊(はかい)された。
Many houses were destroyed by the earthquake.
戦争(せんそう)は都市(とし)を徹底的(てっていてき)に破壊(はかい)し、何(なに)も残(のこ)らなかった。
The war thoroughly destroyed the city, leaving nothing behind.
2. destruction, disruption
The figurative destruction or ruination of something abstract, such as relationships, trust, systems, or the environment.
信頼(しんらい)を破壊(はかい)する。
To destroy trust.
環境(かんきょう)破壊(はかい)が深刻(しんこく)な問題(もんだい)になっている。
Environmental destruction has become a serious problem.
その発言(はつげん)は二人(ふたり)の関係(かんけい)を完全(かんぜん)に破壊(はかい)してしまった。
That remark completely destroyed the relationship between the two of them.

USAGE:
Used as a suru verb (破壊(はかい)する = to destroy) or as a noun modifier (破壊(はかい)力(りょく) = destructive power). Very commonly appears in the passive (破壊(はかい)される = to be destroyed). The compound 環境(かんきょう)破壊(はかい) (environmental destruction) is an extremely frequent set phrase.

KANJI:
破(は) means "to break" and 壊(かい) means "to destroy." Both characters carry the meaning of destruction, making this a reinforcing compound.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)破壊(はかい): environmental destruction
  • 破壊(はかい)力(りょく): destructive power
  • 破壊(はかい)的(てき): destructive
  • 大量(たいりょう)破壊(はかい)兵器(へいき): weapons of mass destruction
  • 自己(じこ)破壊(はかい): self-destruction
