異国(いこく)

いこく
noun
foreign country, foreign land
1. foreign country, foreign land
A country or land that is different from one's own. More literary and evocative than 外国(がいこく), carrying a sense of exoticism, strangeness, or emotional distance.
異国(いこく)の地(ち)で暮(く)らす。
To live in a foreign land.
異国(いこく)情緒(じょうちょ)あふれる街並(まちな)みに魅了(みりょう)された。
I was captivated by the streets filled with an exotic atmosphere.
異国(いこく)の地(ち)で故郷(こきょう)を思(おも)い、涙(なみだ)を流(なが)した。
In a foreign land, I thought of home and shed tears.

USAGE:
More literary and emotional than 外国(がいこく). While 外国(がいこく) is the standard, neutral word for 'foreign country,' 異国(いこく) evokes a sense of distance, exoticism, or longing. It often appears in literary prose, travel writing, and historical narratives.

The compound 異国(いこく)情緒(じょうちょ) (exotic atmosphere) is especially common when describing places like 長崎(ながさき) or 神戸(こうべ) that have historical foreign influence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 異国(いこく)の地(ち): a foreign land
  • 異国(いこく)情緒(じょうちょ): exotic atmosphere
  • 異国(いこく)文化(ぶんか): foreign culture
  • 異国(いこく)の人(ひと): a foreigner (literary)
