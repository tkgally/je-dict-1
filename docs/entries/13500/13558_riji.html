理事(りじ)

りじ
noun
director, board member
1. director, board member, trustee
A person who serves on the governing board of an organization such as a corporation, university, nonprofit, or international body. Responsible for management decisions and organizational oversight.
彼女(かのじょ)は理事(りじ)に選(えら)ばれた。
She was elected as a board member.
理事会(りじかい)で新(あたら)しい方針(ほうしん)が決定(けってい)された。
A new policy was decided at the board meeting.
大学(だいがく)の理事(りじ)長(ちょう)が記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で今後(こんご)の経営(けいえい)計画(けいかく)について説明(せつめい)した。
The university's chairman of the board explained future management plans at a press conference.

USAGE:
理事(りじ) refers to a member of a governing board (理事会(りじかい)). Used for corporate boards, university boards of trustees, nonprofit organizations, and international organizations such as the UN Security Council (安全保障理事会(あんぜんほしょうりじかい)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 理事会(りじかい) (board of directors/trustees)
  • 理事長(りじちょう) (chairman of the board)
  • 常任理事国(じょうにんりじこく) (permanent member of the Security Council)
  • 理事(りじ)に就任(しゅうにん)する (assume a position as director)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取締役(とりしまりやく): corporate director (specifically for companies under the Companies Act)
  • 役員(やくいん): executive, officer (broader term for organizational leadership)
  • 委員(いいん): committee member (serves on a committee, not a governing board)
