しかし

しかし
conjunction
however, but

Definitions

1. however, but, nevertheless
A formal conjunction used to introduce contrast or contradiction. More formal than でも.

Examples

彼(かれ)は努力(どりょく)しました。しかし、成功(せいこう)しませんでした。
He made an effort. However, he did not succeed.
高(たか)いです。しかし、品質(ひんしつ)がいいです。
It's expensive. However, the quality is good.
しかし、問題(もんだい)があります。
However, there is a problem.

Notes

しかし is more formal than でも and is often used in written Japanese and formal speech. It introduces a contrasting statement or objection. In casual conversation, でも is more common. しかしながら is an even more formal variant.
