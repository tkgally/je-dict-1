十月(じゅうがつ)

じゅうがつ
noun
October

Definitions

1. October
The tenth month of the year. Autumn is in full swing with pleasant weather and autumn foliage.

Examples

十月(じゅうがつ)は秋(あき)です。
October is autumn.
十月(じゅうがつ)に紅葉(こうよう)を見(み)に行(い)きます。
I'll go see the autumn leaves in October.
十月(じゅうがつ)の天気(てんき)は気持(きも)ちがいいです。
The weather in October is pleasant.

Notes

October is literally 'month ten.' It's considered one of the most pleasant months in Japan with comfortable temperatures and beautiful autumn colors. Many school and company sports days (運動会) are held in October.
