遵守(じゅんしゅ)

じゅんしゅ
noun / suru verb
compliance, observance

Definitions

1. compliance, observance
The act of following and adhering to rules, laws, regulations, or guidelines.

Examples

法律(ほうりつ)を遵守(じゅんしゅ)する。
To comply with the law.
ルールの遵守(じゅんしゅ)を徹底(てってい)してください。
Please ensure thorough compliance with the rules.
社内(しゃない)規定(きてい)の遵守(じゅんしゅ)は義務(ぎむ)です。
Compliance with company regulations is mandatory.

Notes

USAGE:
遵守(じゅんしゅ) is a formal word commonly used in legal, business, and regulatory contexts. It implies strict adherence rather than casual following.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 法律(ほうりつ)を遵守(じゅんしゅ)する (comply with the law)
  • ルールを遵守(じゅんしゅ)する (observe the rules)
  • 遵守(じゅんしゅ)事項(じこう) (items to be observed)
  • コンプライアンス遵守(じゅんしゅ) (compliance observance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 順守(じゅんしゅ) - alternative kanji spelling (both are acceptable)
  • 守(まも)る - more casual, everyday usage
  • 従(したが)う - to follow, obey
