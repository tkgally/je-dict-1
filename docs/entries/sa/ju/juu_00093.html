十(じゅう)

じゅう
number
ten

Definitions

1. ten
The number ten. A base number in Japanese counting, used to form larger numbers (11 = じゅういち, 20 = にじゅう, etc.). Very consistent in pronunciation across most combinations.

Examples

十(じゅう)分(ぷん)で着(つ)きます。
I'll arrive in ten minutes.
十(じゅう)人(にん)のクラスです。
It's a class of ten people.
十(じゅう)月(がつ)は紅葉(こうよう)がきれいです。
The autumn leaves are beautiful in October.

Notes

The native counter is 十(とお) (too). じゅう is used to build larger numbers: 二(に)十(じゅう) (nijuu, 20), 三(さん)十(じゅう) (sanjuu, 30), etc. When combined with certain counters, じゅう may become じゅっ or じっ: 十(じゅっ)本(ぽん) (juppon), 十(じっ)歳(さい) (jissai, 10 years old).
