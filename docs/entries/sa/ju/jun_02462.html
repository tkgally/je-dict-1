順(じゅん)

じゅん
noun
order, turn, sequence

Definitions

1. order, sequence, turn
The arrangement or sequence of things; one's turn in a sequence.

Examples

順(じゅん)に並(なら)んでください。
Please line up in order.
次(つぎ)は私(わたし)の順(じゅん)だ。
It's my turn next.
あいうえお順(じゅん)で並(なら)べる。
Arrange in alphabetical (a-i-u-e-o) order.

Notes

順(じゅん) refers to order, sequence, or one's turn.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 順(じゅん)に (in order, one by one)
  • 順(じゅん)を待(ま)つ (to wait one's turn)
  • あいうえお順(じゅん) (alphabetical order in Japanese)
  • 年齢(ねんれい)順(じゅん) (in order of age)
  • 順(じゅん)を守(まも)る (to follow the order)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 順番(じゅんばん): turn, order (more common in everyday speech)
  • 順序(じゅんじょ): order, sequence (more formal)
  • 順位(じゅんい): rank, ranking
