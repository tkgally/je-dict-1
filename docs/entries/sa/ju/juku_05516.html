塾(じゅく)

じゅく
noun
cram school

Definitions

1. cram school, private tutoring school, prep school
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

子供(こども)を塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)わせている。
I am sending my child to cram school.
学校(がっこう)が終(お)わってから、塾(じゅく)に行(い)く。
I go to cram school after school ends.
受験(じゅけん)対策(たいさく)のために塾(じゅく)で勉強(べんきょう)している。
I am studying at cram school for entrance exam preparation.

Notes

塾(じゅく) is a private after-school tutoring institution, extremely common in Japan for exam preparation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塾(じゅく)に通(かよ)う (attend cram school)
  • 塾(じゅく)に行(い)く (go to cram school)
  • 塾(じゅく)の先生(せんせい) (cram school teacher)
  • 塾(じゅく)の宿題(しゅくだい) (cram school homework)

TYPES OF 塾(じゅく):

  • 学習(がくしゅう)塾(じゅく) (tutoring for general subjects)
  • 進学(しんがく)塾(じゅく) (exam prep school)
  • 英会話(えいかいわ)塾(じゅく) (English conversation school)
  • 個別(こべつ)指導(しどう)塾(じゅく) (individual tutoring school)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Most Japanese students attend 塾(じゅく) after regular school hours, especially before entrance exams. This is a major part of Japan's education-focused culture.
