十一月(じゅういちがつ)

じゅういちがつ
noun
November

Definitions

1. November
The eleventh month of the year. Late autumn with peak foliage in many areas and the Shichi-Go-San festival for children.

Examples

十一月(じゅういちがつ)は紅葉(もみじ)がきれいです。
The autumn leaves are beautiful in November.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)は寒(さむ)くなります。
It gets cold in November.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)に七五三(しちごさん)があります。
Shichi-Go-San is in November.

Notes

November is literally 'month eleven.' Shichi-Go-San (七五三, 'Seven-Five-Three') on November 15th is a festival celebrating children aged 3, 5, and 7. Autumn foliage reaches its peak in many areas during November.
