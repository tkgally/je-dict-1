順々(じゅんじゅん)

じゅんじゅん
adverb, の-adjective
in order; in turn

Definitions

1. in order; in turn; one by one
Following a proper sequence; each taking their turn without skipping.

Examples

順々(じゅんじゅん)に並(なら)んでください。
Please line up in order.
順々(じゅんじゅん)に名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)びます。
I will call names in order.
問題(もんだい)を順々(じゅんじゅん)に解(と)いていく。
To solve problems one by one in order.

Notes

USAGE: Emphasizes following proper order or sequence. Similar to 順番(じゅんばん)に but with more emphasis on the one-by-one aspect.

PATTERN: 順々(じゅんじゅん)に + verb

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 順番(じゅんばん)に (in order, by turns)
  • 次々(つぎつぎ)に (one after another - less emphasis on order)
  • 一(ひと)つずつ (one by one)

NOTE: The repetition (ABAB pattern) emphasizes the systematic nature of the sequence.

Related Words

Synonym: 順番(じゅんばん) (order, turn)
