ぞっと

ぞっと
adverb, suru verb
shuddering; with a chill; horrified

Definitions

1. shuddering; with a chill; horrified
Describes a sudden feeling of fear or horror that sends a chill down the spine.

Examples

その話(はなし)を聞(き)いてぞっとした。
I shuddered when I heard that story.
事故(じこ)現場(げんば)を見(み)てぞっとした。
I was horrified seeing the accident scene.
ぞっとするような怖(こわ)い映画(えいが)だ。
It's a horrifyingly scary movie.

Notes

ぞっと is an onomatopoeic word describing a sudden chill of fear.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぞっとする: to shudder, be horrified
  • ぞっとするような〜: horrifying, spine-chilling
  • ぞっとしない: not appealing (negative expression)

NOTE ON ぞっとしない:

  • This phrase means "not appealing" or "I don't care for it"
  • Not the same as "not scary" - it's an idiomatic expression

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひやっとする: to feel a sudden chill
  • 鳥肌が立つ: to get goosebumps
