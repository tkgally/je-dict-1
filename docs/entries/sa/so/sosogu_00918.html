注(そそ)ぐ

そそぐ
verb (godan)
to pour, to flow into

Definitions

1. to pour
To cause liquid to flow into a container.
2. to flow into
For a river to empty into a larger body of water.
3. to devote, to concentrate
To direct attention or effort toward something.

Examples

お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To pour tea.
川(かわ)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぐ。
The river flows into the sea.
愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To pour love into (devote love to).

Notes

注(そそ)ぐ has multiple meanings.

USAGE:

  • Literal: pouring liquid
  • Figurative: devoting attention/love

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour into
