外側(そとがわ)

そとがわ
noun
outside; outer side

Definitions

1. outside; outer side; exterior
The part that is on the outside or facing outward.

Examples

建物(たてもの)の外側(そとがわ)を塗(ぬ)り直(なお)す。
To repaint the outside of the building.
窓(まど)の外側(そとがわ)を拭(ふ)く。
To wipe the outside of the window.
カーブでは外側(そとがわ)のタイヤに負担(ふたん)がかかる。
On a curve, the stress is on the outside tire.

Notes

外側(そとがわ) refers to the outside or outer surface.

CONTRAST:

  • 外側(そとがわ): outside
  • 内側(うちがわ): inside

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 外側(そとがわ)から見(み)る: to look from the outside
  • 外側(そとがわ)に向(む)く: to face outward
