それなら

それなら
conjunction
if so; in that case; then

Definitions

1. if so; in that case; then
Used to introduce a response or suggestion based on what was just said; indicates a logical consequence or alternative.

Examples

「明日(あした)は雨(あめ)らしい」「それなら家(いえ)にいよう」
"It looks like rain tomorrow." "In that case, let's stay home."
それなら問題(もんだい)ないですね。
In that case, there's no problem.
時間(じかん)がないの？それなら手伝(てつだ)うよ。
You don't have time? Then I'll help you.

Notes

USAGE:
それなら connects what was said to a response or suggestion. It shows the speaker is acknowledging the situation and responding accordingly.

VARIATIONS:

  • それなら: standard
  • それならば: more formal
  • それじゃ: more casual
  • だったら: casual conditional

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • それでは: well then, in that case
  • じゃあ: then, well (very casual)
  • そうしたら: if that's the case
