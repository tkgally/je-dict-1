総合的(そうごうてき)

そうごうてき
na-adjective
comprehensive, overall

Definitions

1. comprehensive, overall, integrated
Combining or considering all aspects, parts, or factors as a whole.

Examples

総合的(そうごうてき)な判断(はんだん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A comprehensive judgment is necessary.
総合的(そうごうてき)に見(み)て、このプランが最(もっと)も良(よ)い。
Looking at it overall, this plan is the best.
総合的(そうごうてき)な学習(がくしゅう)の時間(じかん)が設(もう)けられた。
Time for integrated learning was established.
Refers to the Japanese school curriculum

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 総合的(そうごうてき)に (comprehensively, overall)
  • Noun: 総合(そうごう) (synthesis, comprehensive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 総合的(そうごうてき)な判断(はんだん)/評価(ひょうか) (comprehensive judgment/evaluation)
  • 総合的(そうごうてき)に考(かんが)える (to consider comprehensively)
  • 総合的(そうごうてき)な対策(たいさく) (comprehensive measures)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 総合(そうごう)病院(びょういん) (general hospital)
  • 総合的(そうごうてき)な学習(がくしゅう)の時間(じかん) (Period for Integrated Studies - part of Japanese school curriculum)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全体的(ぜんたいてき): Overall (emphasizes the whole)
  • 総合的(そうごうてき): Comprehensive (emphasizes integration of parts)
