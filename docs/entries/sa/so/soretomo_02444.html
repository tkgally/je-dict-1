それとも

それとも
conjunction
or, or else

Definitions

1. or, or else
Used to present an alternative option, typically in questions.

Examples

コーヒー(こーひー)にしますか、それとも紅茶(こうちゃ)にしますか。
Would you like coffee, or would you like tea?
電車(でんしゃ)で行(い)きますか、それとも車(くるま)で行(い)きますか。
Will you go by train, or will you go by car?
今日(きょう)会(あ)いますか、それとも明日(あした)にしますか。
Shall we meet today, or shall we make it tomorrow?

Notes

USAGE:
それとも is used between two options in questions to offer an alternative. It connects two complete question clauses.

PATTERN:
Option A + か、それとも + Option B + か

COMPARISON:

  • それとも: or (in questions, between two alternatives)
  • または: or (formal, in statements or questions)
  • あるいは: or, possibly (formal, literary)
  • か: or (simple alternative marker)

NOTE:
それとも is primarily used in questions. For statements, use または or あるいは.
