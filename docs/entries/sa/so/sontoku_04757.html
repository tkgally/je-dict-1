損得(そんとく)

そんとく
noun
profit and loss, advantage and disadvantage

Definitions

1. profit and loss, gain and loss
The combination of gains and losses; the overall balance of benefits and drawbacks.
2. advantage and disadvantage
The pros and cons of a situation; self-interest.

Examples

損得(そんとく)抜(ぬ)きで手伝(てつだ)ってくれた。
They helped me without considering personal gain.
損得抜きで = without regard to personal gain/loss
損得(そんとく)を考(かんが)えてから決(き)めた方(ほう)がいい。
You should consider the pros and cons before deciding.
損得(そんとく)勘定(かんじょう)ばかり考(かんが)える人(ひと)だ。
He's someone who only thinks about profit and loss.
損得勘定 = calculation of profit/loss, being calculating

Notes

損得(そんとく) combines 損(そん) (loss) and 得(とく) (gain) to express the concept of profit and loss or advantages and disadvantages.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 損得(そんとく)勘定(かんじょう): calculating profit/loss, being calculating
  • 損得(そんとく)抜(ぬ)きで: regardless of profit or loss
  • 損得(そんとく)を考(かんが)える: to consider the pros and cons

COMPONENTS:

  • 損(そん): loss, disadvantage
  • 得(とく): gain, profit, advantage

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 利害(りがい): interests, advantages and disadvantages
  • 損益(そんえき): profit and loss (accounting term)
