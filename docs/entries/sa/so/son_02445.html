損(そん)

そん
noun
loss, disadvantage

Definitions

1. loss, damage
Financial or material loss; the state of losing money or value.
2. disadvantage, drawback
An unfavorable situation or outcome; something that works against one's interests.

Examples

損(そん)をした。
I suffered a loss.
知(し)らないと損(そん)だよ。
It's a disadvantage not to know.
Common expression meaning "you'll miss out if you don't know."
損(そん)して得(とく)取(と)れ。
Lose a little to gain a lot.
Proverb meaning short-term losses can lead to long-term gains.
この投資(とうし)は損(そん)になった。
This investment resulted in a loss.

Notes

USAGE:
損(そん)する or 損(そん)をする means "to suffer a loss" or "to lose out."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 損(そん)をする (to suffer a loss)
  • 損(そん)して得(とく)取(と)れ (proverb: short-term loss for long-term gain)
  • 損(そん)は損(そん) (a loss is a loss)
  • 損(そん)得(とく) (profit and loss; pros and cons)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)損(そん) (big loss)
  • 丸(まる)損(そん) (complete loss)
  • 損(そん)失(しつ) (loss, formal)
  • 損(そん)害(がい) (damage, harm)

ANTONYM:

  • 得(とく): gain, profit, advantage

Related Words

Antonym: 得(とく) (gain)
