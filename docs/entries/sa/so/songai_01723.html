損害(そんがい)

そんがい
noun
damage, loss, injury

Definitions

1. damage, loss
Harm or loss, especially in financial or material terms. Often used in legal and business contexts.
2. injury, harm
Harm caused to a person or organization's interests.

Examples

台風(たいふう)で大(おお)きな損害(そんがい)が出(で)た。
The typhoon caused significant damage.
損害(そんがい)賠償(ばいしょう)を請求(せいきゅう)する。
To claim compensation for damages.
Legal terminology
会社(かいしゃ)に損害(そんがい)を与(あた)える。
To cause damage to the company.

Notes

損害(そんがい) emphasizes financial or material loss, often used in legal and business contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 損害(そんがい)を受(う)ける (to suffer damage)
  • 損害(そんがい)を与(あた)える (to cause damage)
  • 損害(そんがい)が出(で)る (damage occurs)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 損害(そんがい)賠償(ばいしょう) (compensation for damages)
  • 損害(そんがい)保険(ほけん) (property insurance)
  • 損害(そんがい)額(がく) (amount of damage)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 損害(そんがい): damage (financial/legal focus)
  • 被害(ひがい): damage received (victim perspective)
  • 害(がい): harm (general term)
  • 損(そん): loss (informal, often used alone)

Related Words

Related: 被害(ひがい) (damage (victim perspective))
Related: 損(そん) (loss)
