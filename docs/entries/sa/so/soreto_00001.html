それと

それと
conjunction
and; and also; besides that

Definitions

1. and; and also; besides that; in addition
A casual conjunction used to add items or information to what was previously mentioned. Often used when listing things or adding another point.

Examples

パンと牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)、それと卵(たまご)を買(か)ってきて。
Buy bread, milk, and also eggs.
宿題(しゅくだい)は終(お)わった。それと、明日(あした)の準備(じゅんび)もした。
I finished my homework. And I also prepared for tomorrow.
塩(しお)と砂糖(さとう)、それと醤油(しょうゆ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
We need salt, sugar, and also soy sauce.

Notes

REGISTER: Casual to neutral. Used in everyday conversation.

USAGE: Typically used to:

  • Add items when listing (and also, plus)
  • Add additional information in conversation
  • Introduce another topic

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • それから (and then, after that - for sequence)
  • そして (and, and then - slightly more formal)
  • あと (and, also - very casual)
  • 他(ほか)に (in addition - more formal)

NOTE: それと focuses on adding items, while それから emphasizes sequence or order.

Related Words

Contrast: それから (and then (sequence))
Related: そして (and (slightly formal))
