足(そく)

そく
counter
pairs of footwear

Definitions

1. pairs of footwear
A counter for pairs of shoes, socks, and other footwear.

Examples

靴(くつ)を二(に)足(そく)買(か)いました。
I bought two pairs of shoes.
靴下(くつした)は何(なん)足(ぞく)ありますか。
How many pairs of socks do you have?
なんぞく with voiced ぞ
一(いっ)足(そく)だけ残(のこ)っています。
Only one pair is left.
いっそく with small っ

Notes

足(そく) is used for counting pairs of footwear including shoes, socks, slippers, etc. Common forms:

  • 一(いっ)足(そく) (one pair - with っ)
  • 二(に)足(そく)
  • 三(さん)足(ぞく) (voiced)
  • 何(なん)足(ぞく). This counter emphasizes that footwear comes in pairs.
