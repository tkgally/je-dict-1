措置(そち)

そち
noun
measure; step; action

Definitions

1. measure; step; action
An official or formal action taken to deal with a situation.

Examples

適切(てきせつ)な措置(そち)を取(と)る。
To take appropriate measures.
緊急(きんきゅう)措置(そち)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Emergency measures are needed.
安全(あんぜん)措置(そち)を講(こう)じる。
To implement safety measures.

Notes

措置(そち) means measure or action.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 措置(そち)を取(と)る: to take measures
  • 措置(そち)を講(こう)じる: to implement measures
  • 応急(おうきゅう)措置(そち): emergency measures

USAGE:
Often used in formal, administrative, or legal contexts.
