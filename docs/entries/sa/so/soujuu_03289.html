操縦(そうじゅう)

そうじゅう
noun / suru-verb
operation, piloting, manipulation

Definitions

1. operation, piloting
The act of controlling a vehicle, aircraft, or machinery.
2. manipulation, control
Controlling or influencing someone or something, often with a negative connotation.

Examples

飛行機(ひこうき)を操縦(そうじゅう)するには免許(めんきょ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need a license to pilot an airplane.
彼(かれ)は操縦(そうじゅう)席(せき)に座(すわ)った。
He sat in the pilot's seat.
彼女(かのじょ)は巧(たく)みに人(ひと)を操縦(そうじゅう)する。
She skillfully manipulates people.
Negative nuance when used with people

Notes

Used for controlling vehicles (especially aircraft) and for manipulating people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 操縦(そうじゅう)する (to operate, pilot)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)席(せき) (cockpit, pilot's seat)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)士(し) (pilot)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)桿(かん) (control stick, joystick)
  • 遠隔(えんかく)操縦(そうじゅう) (remote control)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運転(うんてん) - driving (cars, trains)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう) - piloting (aircraft), manipulation

Related Words

Related: 運転(うんてん) (driving)
