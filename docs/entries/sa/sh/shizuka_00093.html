静(しず)か

しずか
adjective (na-adjective)
quiet, calm

Definitions

1. quiet, silent
Describes a place, environment, or person that makes little or no noise. The opposite of うるさい (noisy).
2. calm, peaceful
Can also describe a tranquil atmosphere or a person with a calm demeanor.

Examples

図書館(としょかん)は静(しず)かです。
The library is quiet.
静(しず)かにしてください。
Please be quiet.
Polite request
静(しず)かな町(まち)に住(す)みたい。
I want to live in a quiet town.
Using the na-adjective form before a noun

Notes

As a na-adjective, it takes な before nouns (静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)) and に when used adverbially (静(しず)かに歩(ある)く). The opposite is うるさい (noisy) for volume, or 賑(にぎ)やか (lively/bustling) for atmosphere.
