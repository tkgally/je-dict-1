素人(しろうと)

しろうと
noun
amateur, layperson

Definitions

1. amateur, layperson
Someone who is not a professional or expert in a particular field; a nonprofessional.

Examples

素人(しろうと)には難(むずか)しい仕事(しごと)だ。
This is a difficult job for an amateur.
彼(かれ)は素人(しろうと)とは思(おも)えないほど上手(じょうず)だ。
He is so good that you wouldn't think he's an amateur.
素人(しろうと)考(かんが)えで申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありませんが、この方法(ほうほう)はどうでしょうか。
Excuse my amateur thinking, but how about this method?
素人考え = amateur thinking/idea

Notes

素人(しろうと) refers to someone without professional training or expertise. It can describe both skill level and occupation status.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 素人(しろうと)考(かんが)え: amateur thinking/unprofessional idea
  • 素人(しろうと)目(め): an untrained eye
  • 素人(しろうと)判断(はんだん): layperson's judgment

ANTONYM:

  • 玄人(くろうと)/専門家(せんもんか): professional, expert

The word comes from the contrast with 玄人(くろうと) (professional), where 白(しろ) (white) suggests inexperience or blankness.
