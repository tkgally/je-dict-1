〜社(しゃ)

しゃ
suffix, counter
company (counter/suffix)

Definitions

1. company; firm (suffix)
A suffix attached to indicate a company or business entity.
2. counter for companies
Used to count the number of companies.

Examples

3社(しゃ)に応募(おうぼ)した。
I applied to three companies.
出版社(しゅっぱんしゃ)で働(はたら)いている。
I work at a publishing company.
競合(きょうごう)5社(しゃ)を分析(ぶんせき)した。
We analyzed five competing companies.

Notes

〜社(しゃ) is used both as a suffix to form company names and as a counter.

AS A SUFFIX:

Combines with nouns to indicate a type of company:

  • 出版社(しゅっぱんしゃ) - publishing company
  • 商社(しょうしゃ) - trading company
  • 会社(かいしゃ) - company (general)
  • 新聞社(しんぶんしゃ) - newspaper company
  • 広告代理店(こうこくだいりてん) uses 店(てん), not 社(しゃ)

AS A COUNTER:
COUNTING PATTERN:
1社(しゃ) (いっしゃ), 2社(しゃ) (にしゃ), 3社(しゃ) (さんしゃ)
4社(しゃ) (よんしゃ), 5社(しゃ) (ごしゃ)...

何社(なんしゃ) - how many companies?

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜社(しゃ)に応募(おうぼ)する - to apply to ~ companies
  • 〜社(しゃ)と取引(とりひき)する - to do business with ~ companies
