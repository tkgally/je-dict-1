執筆(しっぴつ)

しっぴつ
noun, suru-verb
writing, authoring

Definitions

1. writing, authoring
The act of writing, especially books, articles, or other substantial written works.

Examples

小説(しょうせつ)の執筆(しっぴつ)に専念(せんねん)している。
I'm dedicating myself to writing a novel.
この記事(きじ)は山田先生(やまだせんせい)が執筆(しっぴつ)した。
This article was written by Professor Yamada.
執筆(しっぴつ)活動(かつどう)を続(つづ)けている。
I'm continuing my writing activities.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 執筆(しっぴつ)する (to write, to author)
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)者(しゃ) (author, writer)
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)活動(かつどう) (writing activities)
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)中(ちゅう) (in the process of writing)

USAGE NOTE:
執筆(しっぴつ) is more formal than 書(か)く and implies serious writing work like books, articles, or academic papers. It's not used for casual writing like notes or text messages.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 著作(ちょさく) - written work, authorship
  • 著述(ちょじゅつ) - writing (formal/literary)
