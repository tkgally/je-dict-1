食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)

しょっきあらいき
noun
dishwasher

Definitions

1. dishwasher; dish-washing machine
An appliance that automatically washes dishes, utensils, and cookware.

Examples

食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)に食器(しょっき)を入(い)れる。
Put dishes in the dishwasher.
新(あたら)しい食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)を買(か)った。
I bought a new dishwasher.
この鍋(なべ)は食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)対応(たいおう)ですか。
Is this pot dishwasher-safe?

Notes

食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き) is often abbreviated to 食洗機(しょくせんき) in everyday speech.

ALTERNATIVE NAMES:

  • 食洗機(しょくせんき) (abbreviation, very common)
  • 皿洗(さらあら)い機(き) (less common)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)対応(たいおう) (dishwasher-safe)
  • 食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き)を回(まわ)す (to run the dishwasher)
  • ビルトインの食器洗(しょっきあら)い機(き) (built-in dishwasher)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Dishwashers are becoming more common in Japanese households but are still less prevalent than in Western countries. Many Japanese kitchens are small, so compact models or sink-mounted types are popular.
