車道(しゃどう)

しゃどう
noun
roadway, carriageway

Definitions

1. roadway, carriageway
The part of a road designated for vehicles, as opposed to sidewalks for pedestrians.

Examples

車道(しゃどう)を歩(ある)くのは危険(きけん)です。
It's dangerous to walk on the roadway.
自転車(じてんしゃ)は車道(しゃどう)を走(はし)らなければならない。
Bicycles must ride on the roadway.
子供(こども)が車道(しゃどう)に飛(と)び出(だ)した。
A child ran out onto the roadway.

Notes

車道(しゃどう) combines 車(しゃ) (vehicle) + 道(どう) (road/way).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 歩道(ほどう) (sidewalk) - antonym
  • 車線(しゃせん) (lane)
  • 道路(どうろ) (road)
  • 国道(こくどう) (national highway)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 車道(しゃどう)を走(はし)る (to drive on the roadway)
  • 車道(しゃどう)に出(で)る (to go onto the roadway)
  • 車道(しゃどう)と歩道(ほどう) (roadway and sidewalk)

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japan, bicycles are legally required to use 車道(しゃどう) in most cases
  • Important vocabulary for traffic safety and driving

Related Words

Antonym: 歩道(ほどう) (sidewalk)
Related: 道路(どうろ) (road)
