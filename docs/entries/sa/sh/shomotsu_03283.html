書物(しょもつ)

しょもつ
noun
books, written works

Definitions

1. books, written works
A formal or literary term for books, especially those of scholarly or literary value.

Examples

古(ふる)い書物(しょもつ)を集(あつ)めるのが趣味(しゅみ)だ。
My hobby is collecting old books.
この書物(しょもつ)には貴重(きちょう)な歴史(れきし)資料(しりょう)が含(ふく)まれている。
This book contains valuable historical materials.
図書館(としょかん)には数多(かずおお)くの書物(しょもつ)が所蔵(しょぞう)されている。
The library holds a large number of books in its collection.

Notes

A formal or literary word for books. More elevated in register than 本(ほん).

USAGE:

  • Often used when referring to books of historical, scholarly, or literary significance
  • Common in formal writing and academic contexts
  • Rarely used in everyday conversation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 本(ほん) - everyday word for book
  • 図書(としょ) - books (collective, often in compounds like 図書館(としょかん))
  • 書籍(しょせき) - formal term, often used in publishing contexts

Related Words

Related: 本(ほん) (everyday term)
