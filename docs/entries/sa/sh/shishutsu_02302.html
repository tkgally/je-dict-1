支出(ししゅつ)

ししゅつ
noun, suru-verb
expenditure, expenses, outlay

Definitions

1. expenditure, expenses
Money spent; the action of spending money.

Examples

毎月(まいつき)の支出(ししゅつ)を記録(きろく)している。
I record my monthly expenses.
収入(しゅうにゅう)と支出(ししゅつ)のバランスが大切(たいせつ)だ。
The balance between income and expenses is important.
政府(せいふ)は公共(こうきょう)事業(じぎょう)に多額(たがく)の資金(しきん)を支出(ししゅつ)した。
The government spent a large amount of funds on public works.
支出する = to spend/expend

Notes

支出(ししゅつ) refers to expenditure or expenses, often used in formal or financial contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 支出(ししゅつ)を抑(おさ)える (to reduce expenses)
  • 支出(ししゅつ)を削減(さくげん)する (to cut expenses)
  • 支出(ししゅつ)が増(ふ)える (expenses increase)

ANTONYM:

  • 収入(しゅうにゅう) (income) ↔ 支出(ししゅつ) (expenditure)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 出費(しゅっぴ) (expense, spending - slightly more casual)
  • 経費(けいひ) (operating expenses, costs)

Related Words

Antonym: 収入(しゅうにゅう) (income)
