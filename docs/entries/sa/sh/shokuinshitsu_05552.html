職員室(しょくいんしつ)

しょくいんしつ
noun
staff room; teachers' room

Definitions

1. staff room; teachers' room
The room in a school where teachers have their desks and work between classes.

Examples

先生(せんせい)は職員室(しょくいんしつ)にいます。
The teacher is in the staff room.
職員室(しょくいんしつ)に呼(よ)ばれた。
I was called to the staff room.
Often implies being in trouble
職員室(しょくいんしつ)の前(まえ)で待(ま)っていてください。
Please wait in front of the staff room.

Notes

職員室 is found in Japanese schools from elementary to high school. Unlike some countries where teachers have individual offices, Japanese teachers typically share one large room.

RELATED SCHOOL ROOMS:

  • 保健室 (ほけんしつ): nurse's office
  • 図書室 (としょしつ): library room
  • 校長室 (こうちょうしつ): principal's office
