知(し)り合(あ)い

しりあい
noun
acquaintance

Definitions

1. acquaintance
A person one knows but who is not a close friend; someone with whom one has become familiar.

Examples

彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の知(し)り合(あ)いです。
He is an acquaintance of mine.
知(し)り合(あ)いを通(とお)じて仕事(しごと)を見(み)つけた。
I found a job through an acquaintance.
この町(まち)には知(し)り合(あ)いが多(おお)い。
I have many acquaintances in this town.

Notes

FORMATION: From the verb 知(し)り合(あ)う (to get to know each other), nominalized.

KANJI VARIATION: Can also be written as 知合(しりあい).

CONTRAST WITH 友達(ともだち):

  • 知(し)り合(あ)い - someone you know (not necessarily close)
  • 友達(ともだち) - friend (implies closeness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 知(し)り合(あ)いになる (to become acquainted)
  • 知(し)り合(あ)いを作(つく)る (to make acquaintances)
  • ただの知(し)り合(あ)い (just an acquaintance)
