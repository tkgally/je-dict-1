四十九日(しじゅうくにち)

しじゅうくにち
noun
49th day memorial; forty-nine days

Definitions

1. 49th day memorial service
The most important Buddhist memorial service, held 49 days after death. Believed to be when the deceased's fate in the afterlife is determined.

Examples

四十九日(しじゅうくにち)が過(す)ぎるまで喪(も)に服(ふく)す。
We observe mourning until the 49th day has passed.
四十九日(しじゅうくにち)法要(ほうよう)に親戚(しんせき)が集(あつ)まった。
Relatives gathered for the 49th day memorial service.
四十九日(しじゅうくにち)を迎(むか)えて忌明(きあ)けとなる。
The mourning period ends when the 49th day arrives.

Notes

In Buddhist belief, the soul undergoes judgment every seven days for 49 days, after which its next rebirth is determined.

KEY CONCEPTS:

  • 忌明(きあ)け: end of mourning period (after 49 days)
  • 中陰(ちゅういん): the 49-day period between death and rebirth
  • 香典返(こうでんがえ)し: return gifts sent after the 49th day

CUSTOMS:

  • 仮祭壇(かりさいだん) (temporary altar) is removed
  • 本位牌(ほんいはい) (permanent memorial tablet) replaces temporary one
  • Mourning restrictions on celebration end
