市(し)

し
noun, suffix
city

Definitions

1. city (administrative unit)
A city as an administrative division in Japan, larger than a town (町(まち)) but smaller than a prefecture (県(けん)).
2. -shi (city suffix)
Used as a suffix attached to city names.

Examples

東京(とうきょう)は市(し)ではなく都(と)です。
Tokyo is not a city (shi) but a metropolis (to).
Tokyo has special administrative status
横浜(よこはま)市(し)に住(す)んでいます。
I live in Yokohama City.
市 as suffix
この市(し)の人口(じんこう)は百万人(ひゃくまんにん)です。
The population of this city is one million.

Notes

市(し) refers to cities as administrative units in Japan.

JAPANESE ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISIONS:

  • 都(と): metropolis (Tokyo only)
  • 道(どう): circuit (Hokkaido only)
  • 府(ふ): urban prefecture (Osaka, Kyoto)
  • 県(けん): prefecture
  • 市(し): city
  • 町(まち/ちょう): town
  • 村(むら/そん): village

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 市役所(しやくしょ): city hall
  • 市長(しちょう): mayor
  • 市民(しみん): citizen
  • 市内(しない): within the city

Note: When referring to the concept of a city in general, 都市(とし) or 町(まち) are often used instead.
