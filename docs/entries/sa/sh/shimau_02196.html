仕舞(しま)う

しまう
verb (godan)
to put away, to finish

Definitions

1. to put away, to store
To put something in its proper place; to store or tidy away.
2. to finish, to close
To bring something to an end; to close up (a shop).

Examples

本(ほん)を棚(たな)に仕舞(しま)う。
I put the book away on the shelf.
冬(ふゆ)の服(ふく)を仕舞(しま)った。
I put away my winter clothes.
店(みせ)を仕舞(しま)う時間(じかん)だ。
It's time to close the shop.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを仕舞(しま)う.

NOTE: This entry is for the main verb 仕舞(しま)う meaning "to put away." For the auxiliary verb ～てしまう, see the separate entry.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を仕舞(しま)う (to put ~ away)
  • 引(ひ)き出(だ)しに仕舞(しま)う (to put in a drawer)
  • 店(みせ)を仕舞(しま)う (to close shop)

WRITING NOTE:
Often written in hiragana (しまう) rather than kanji (仕舞(しま)う).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仕舞(しま)う: to put away (focus on tidying)
  • 片付(かたづ)ける: to tidy up (broader meaning)
  • 収納(しゅうのう)する: to store (formal)

Related Words

Related: 片付(かたづ)ける (to tidy up)
