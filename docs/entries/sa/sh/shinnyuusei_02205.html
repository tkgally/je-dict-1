新入生(しんにゅうせい)

しんにゅうせい
noun
new student, freshman

Definitions

1. new student, freshman, first-year student
A student who has just entered a school or university.

Examples

新入生(しんにゅうせい)歓迎会(かんげいかい)が開(ひら)かれた。
A welcome party for new students was held.
新入生(しんにゅうせい)は先輩(せんぱい)に挨拶(あいさつ)をする。
New students greet their seniors.
今年(ことし)の新入生(しんにゅうせい)は500人(にん)だ。
There are 500 new students this year.

Notes

新入生(しんにゅうせい) combines 新入(しんにゅう) (new entry) + 生(せい) (student).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 在校生(ざいこうせい) (current student)
  • 卒業生(そつぎょうせい) (graduate)
  • 先輩(せんぱい) (senior student)
  • 後輩(こうはい) (junior student)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 新入生(しんにゅうせい)歓迎会(かんげいかい) (freshman welcome party)
  • 新入生(しんにゅうせい){オリエンテーション} (freshman orientation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新入生(しんにゅうせい): new student (school context)
  • 新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん): new employee (company context)
  • 一年生(ねんせい): first-year student

USAGE NOTES:

  • Used for students at all levels: elementary through university
  • 新入生 is particularly emphasized during April, the start of the Japanese school year
