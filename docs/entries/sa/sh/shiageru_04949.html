仕上(しあ)げる

しあげる
ichidan verb
to finish, to complete

Definitions

1. to finish, to complete
To bring work to its final stage, adding final touches or completing the last steps.
2. to finish off, to put the finishing touches on
To complete something by doing the final work that makes it ready.

Examples

レポートを今日中(きょうじゅう)に仕上(しあ)げなければならない。
I have to finish the report by the end of today.
料理(りょうり)を美(うつく)しく仕上(しあ)げる。
I finish the dish beautifully.
作品(さくひん)が無事(ぶじ)に仕上(しあ)がった。
The work was successfully completed.
[Note: 仕上(しあ)がる is the intransitive form meaning 'to be finished']

Notes

仕上(しあ)げる is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to finish or complete.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 仕(し) (doing) + 上(あ)げる (to raise, to complete) → to complete the work

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 仕上(しあ)げる (他動詞(たどうし)): to finish (something)
  • 仕上(しあ)がる (自動詞(じどうし)): to be finished, to turn out

ASPECT (ている):
仕上(しあ)げている indicates an ongoing process of finishing work.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 作品(さくひん)を仕上(しあ)げる (finish a work)
  • きれい(きれい)に仕上(しあ)げる (finish nicely)
  • 完璧(かんぺき)に仕上(しあ)げる (finish perfectly)
  • 仕上(しあ)げの段階(だんかい) (final stage)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 仕上(しあ)げ: finishing, final touches; 仕上(しあ)げ作業(さぎょう) = finishing work
  • 仕上(しあ)がり: finish, result (how something turns out)
