敷(し)く

しく
godan verb
to spread, to lay out

Definitions

1. to spread out, to lay
To extend something flat over a surface.
2. to lay (rails, pipes)
To install or construct by laying down in position.

Examples

布団(ふとん)を敷(し)く。
I lay out the futon.
テーブルにクロスを敷(し)いた。
I spread a cloth on the table.
この地域(ちいき)に線路(せんろ)を敷(し)く計画(けいかく)がある。
There are plans to lay railroad tracks in this area.

Notes

敷(し)く means to spread or lay something flat over a surface.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 布団(ふとん)を敷(し)く

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 布団(ふとん)を敷(し)く: to lay out a futon
  • 線路(せんろ)を敷(し)く: to lay railway tracks
  • 敷(し)き布(ふ): fitted sheet, bottom sheet
  • 敷(し)き物(もの): mat, rug

CONTRAST:

  • 敷(し)く: to spread flat (futon, cloth)
  • 広(ひろ)げる: to spread open (newspaper, map)
