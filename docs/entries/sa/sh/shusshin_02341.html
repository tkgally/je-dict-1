出身(しゅっしん)

しゅっしん
noun
origin, hometown, alma mater

Definitions

1. one's origin, hometown
The place where someone was born or raised.
2. alma mater, graduate of
The school or organization one came from.

Examples

出身(しゅっしん)はどこですか。
Where are you from?
彼(かれ)は東京(とうきょう)出身(しゅっしん)だ。
He is from Tokyo.
同(おな)じ大学(だいがく)出身(しゅっしん)だと分(わ)かって、一気(いっき)に親近感(しんきんかん)が湧(わ)いた。世代(せだい)は違(ちが)うが、共通(きょうつう)の話題(わだい)で盛(も)り上(あ)がった。
When I found out we were from the same university, I immediately felt a sense of closeness. Although we're from different generations, we got excited talking about shared topics.

Notes

出身(しゅっしん) indicates where someone is from - their hometown, native place, or the institution they graduated from.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ○○出身(しゅっしん)だ (to be from XX)
  • 出身(しゅっしん)は○○だ (one's origin is XX)
  • ○○出身(しゅっしん)の人(ひと) (person from XX)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出身(しゅっしん)地(ち) (birthplace, hometown)
  • 出身(しゅっしん)校(こう) (alma mater)
  • 出身(しゅっしん)国(こく) (country of origin)
  • 出身(しゅっしん)大学(だいがく) (university one graduated from)

USAGE:

  • 東京(とうきょう)出身(しゅっしん) (from Tokyo)
  • 早稲田(わせだ)大学(だいがく)出身(しゅっしん) (Waseda University graduate)
  • アメリカ出身(しゅっしん) (from America)
  • 農家(のうか)出身(しゅっしん) (from a farming family)
