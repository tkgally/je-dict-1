賞与(しょうよ)

しょうよ
noun
bonus

Definitions

1. bonus, seasonal bonus payment
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

夏(なつ)と冬(ふゆ)に賞与(しょうよ)が出(で)る。
Bonuses are paid in summer and winter.
今年(ことし)は賞与(しょうよ)が増(ふ)えた。
This year the bonus increased.
業績(ぎょうせき)によって賞与(しょうよ)の額(がく)が変(か)わる。
The bonus amount changes depending on company performance.

Notes

賞与(しょうよ) is a formal term for bonus, typically paid twice a year in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 賞与(しょうよ)が出(で)る (bonus is paid out)
  • 賞与(しょうよ)を支給(しきゅう)する (to pay bonuses)
  • 賞与(しょうよ)を受(う)け取(と)る (to receive bonus)
  • 夏季(かき)賞与(しょうよ) (summer bonus)
  • 冬季(とうき)賞与(しょうよ) (winter bonus)
  • 年間(ねんかん)賞与(しょうよ) (annual bonus total)

BONUS CULTURE IN JAPAN:

Traditional Japanese companies typically pay 賞与(しょうよ) twice a year:

  • 夏(なつ)のボーナス (summer, usually June/July)
  • 冬(ふゆ)のボーナス (winter, usually December)

Amount often equals 1-4 months' salary depending on the company.

SYNONYM:

  • ボーナス (bonus - more casual)
  • 一時金(いちじきん) (lump-sum payment)
