主婦(しゅふ)

しゅふ
noun
housewife, homemaker

Definitions

1. housewife, homemaker
A woman who manages a household as her primary occupation.

Examples

母(はは)は専業(せんぎょう)主婦(しゅふ)だ。
My mother is a full-time homemaker.
主婦(しゅふ)の仕事(しごと)は大変(たいへん)だ。
A homemaker's work is tough.
彼女(かのじょ)は仕事(しごと)をしながら主婦(しゅふ)もこなしている。家事(かじ)と育児(いくじ)と仕事(しごと)の両立(りょうりつ)は容易(ようい)ではない。
She works while also being a homemaker. Balancing housework, childcare, and a job is not easy.

Notes

主婦(しゅふ) refers to a housewife or homemaker - typically a woman who manages the household.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 専業(せんぎょう)主婦(しゅふ) (full-time homemaker)
  • 兼業(けんぎょう)主婦(しゅふ) (working mother/homemaker with a job)
  • 主婦(しゅふ)業(ぎょう) (homemaking, housework as work)
  • 新米(しんまい)主婦(しゅふ) (new homemaker)

GENDER-NEUTRAL ALTERNATIVES:

  • 主夫(しゅふ): househusband (same reading, different kanji)
  • 家事(かじ)担当(たんとう)者(しゃ): person in charge of housework

CULTURAL NOTE:
While 主婦(しゅふ) traditionally referred to women, the term 主夫(しゅふ) (written with 夫 "husband") is increasingly used for men who stay home to manage the household.
