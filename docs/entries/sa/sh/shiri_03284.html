尻(しり)

しり
noun
buttocks, bottom, rear

Definitions

1. buttocks, bottom
The posterior part of the body that one sits on.
2. rear end, back part
The back or trailing part of something.

Examples

椅子(いす)に座(すわ)りすぎて尻(しり)が痛(いた)い。
My bottom hurts from sitting in a chair too long.
列(れつ)の尻(しり)に並(なら)んだ。
I lined up at the back of the queue.
彼(かれ)は尻(しり)が軽(かる)くて、すぐに転職(てんしょく)する。
He's restless and changes jobs quickly.
The idiom 尻(しり)が軽(かる)い means someone who doesn't stay put or is fickle.

Notes

Can be considered slightly crude in formal contexts. Alternative polite forms exist.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 尻(しり)が重(おも)い - reluctant to get up/act, slow to start
  • 尻(しり)が軽(かる)い - restless, fickle, doesn't stay in one place
  • 尻(しり)に敷(し)かれる - to be henpecked (lit. to be sat on)
  • 尻(しり)に火(ひ)がつく - to have a fire under one's seat (to be in a desperate hurry)
  • 尻尾(しっぽ)を出(だ)す - to show one's true colors (lit. to show one's tail)

POLITE ALTERNATIVES:

  • お尻(しり) (polite form)
  • 臀部(でんぶ) (medical/formal term)
