示(しめ)す

しめす
verb (godan)
to show, to indicate

Definitions

1. to show, to indicate
To make something visible or known.
2. to demonstrate, to prove
To provide evidence or proof.

Examples

方向(ほうこう)を示(しめ)す。
To indicate the direction.
例(れい)を示(しめ)す。
To show an example.
態度(たいど)で示(しめ)す。
To show through one's attitude.

Notes

示(しめ)す means to show or indicate.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを示(しめ)す: to show X
  • Xで示(しめ)す: to show by means of X

RELATED:

  • 指示(しじ): instruction
  • 表示(ひょうじ): display
