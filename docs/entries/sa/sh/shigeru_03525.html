茂(しげ)る

しげる
godan verb
to grow thick, to be overgrown

Definitions

1. to grow thick, to be overgrown
For plants, grass, or trees to grow densely.

Examples

夏(なつ)になると草(くさ)が茂(しげ)る。
The grass grows thick when summer comes.
木(き)が茂(しげ)って日陰(ひかげ)ができた。
The trees grew thick and created shade.
庭(にわ)に雑草(ざっそう)が茂(しげ)っている。
Weeds are growing thick in the garden.

Notes

茂(しげ)る means for vegetation to grow thickly or luxuriantly.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • No common transitive pair
  • Pattern: Xが茂(しげ)る

ASPECT:

  • 茂(しげ)っている: Is overgrown (resulting state)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 草(くさ)が茂(しげ)る (grass grows thick)
  • 木(き)が茂(しげ)る (trees grow thick)
  • うっそうと茂(しげ)る (to grow luxuriantly)
  • 生(お)い茂(しげ)る (to grow thickly, to be overgrown)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 生(は)える (to grow, sprout)
  • 伸(の)びる (to grow, extend)
