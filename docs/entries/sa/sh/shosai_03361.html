書斎(しょさい)

しょさい
noun
study (room), home office

Definitions

1. study, den, home office
A room in a home used for reading, writing, and intellectual work.

Examples

父(ちち)は書斎(しょさい)で仕事(しごと)をしている。
My father is working in his study.
書斎(しょさい)に本棚(ほんだな)がいっぱいある。
The study is full of bookshelves.
書斎(しょさい)にこもって執筆(しっぴつ)した。
I shut myself in my study and wrote.

Notes

Typically refers to a personal room for intellectual pursuits, often associated with writers, scholars, or professionals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書斎(しょさい)で仕事(しごと)をする (to work in one's study)
  • 書斎(しょさい)にこもる (to shut oneself in one's study)
  • 書斎(しょさい)の机(つくえ) (desk in the study)

RELATED ROOMS:

  • 居間(いま) (living room)
  • 寝室(しんしつ) (bedroom)
  • 和室(わしつ) (Japanese-style room)
  • オフィス (office - workplace)

NOTE:
In modern usage, 書斎(しょさい) often overlaps with "home office," but traditionally emphasizes intellectual or literary work.
