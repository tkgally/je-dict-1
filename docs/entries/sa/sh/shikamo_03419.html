しかも

しかも
conjunction
moreover, furthermore

Definitions

1. moreover, furthermore, besides
Used to add information that strengthens or supplements what was said before.

Examples

この店(みせ)は安(やす)い。しかも美味(おい)しい。
This restaurant is cheap. Moreover, it's delicious.
彼(かれ)は頭(あたま)がいい。しかも努力家(どりょくか)だ。
He's smart. What's more, he's hardworking.
遅刻(ちこく)した。しかも大事(だいじ)な会議(かいぎ)に。
I was late. And it was to an important meeting.
Used for emphasis

Notes

USAGE: しかも adds emphasis to additional information. It typically introduces something surprising or noteworthy that reinforces the previous statement.

CONTRAST:

  • しかも - moreover (adds emphasis, often positive or surprising)
  • その上(うえ) - on top of that, moreover
  • 更(さら)に - furthermore, even more
  • おまけに - on top of that (often negative additional point)

POSITION: Typically appears at the beginning of a new sentence or clause.
