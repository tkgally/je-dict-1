主体的(しゅたいてき)

しゅたいてき
na-adjective
autonomous; proactive; subjective

Definitions

1. autonomous; proactive; self-directed
Acting on one's own initiative rather than passively following others; taking ownership of one's actions.

Examples

主体的(しゅたいてき)に行動(こうどう)する。
To act proactively.
主体的(しゅたいてき)な学習(がくしゅう)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Self-directed learning is important.
主体的(しゅたいてき)に参加(さんか)する姿勢(しせい)を持(も)つ。
To have an attitude of proactive participation.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
主体(しゅたい) (subject, main body) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

EDUCATIONAL CONTEXT:
主体的(しゅたいてき) is often emphasized in Japanese educational reform, promoting "active learning" where students take initiative rather than passively receiving knowledge.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 主体的(しゅたいてき)に取(と)り組(く)む: to engage proactively
  • 主体的(しゅたいてき)な学(まな)び: self-directed learning
  • 主体的(しゅたいてき)な態度(たいど): proactive attitude

CONTRAST:

  • 受動的(じゅどうてき): passive
  • 受(う)け身(み): passive

Related Words

Antonym: 受動的(じゅどうてき) (passive)
