調(しら)べる

しらべる
verb (ichidan)
to investigate, to look up

Definitions

1. to investigate, to examine
To look into something carefully to find information or the truth.
2. to look up, to check
To search for information in a reference source like a dictionary or the internet.

Examples

辞書(じしょ)で言葉(ことば)を調(しら)べる。
Look up a word in the dictionary.
で marks the tool used
警察(けいさつ)が事件(じけん)を調(しら)べている。
The police are investigating the case.
インターネットで調(しら)べてみます。
I'll look it up on the internet.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを調(しら)べる (investigate/look up X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 調(しら)べている = 'is investigating/looking up.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 辞書(じしょ)で調(しら)べる (look up in a dictionary)
  • ネットで調(しら)べる (search online)
  • 原因(げんいん)を調(しら)べる (investigate the cause)
  • よく調(しら)べる (examine carefully)
  • 調(しら)べてみる (try looking up)

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Academic research
  • Police investigations
  • Looking things up in dictionaries/online
  • Checking facts

RELATED:

  • 調査(ちょうさ) (investigation, survey—noun, more formal)
  • 検索(けんさく) (search—especially computer searches)
