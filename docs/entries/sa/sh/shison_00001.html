子孫(しそん)

しそん
noun
descendant, offspring

Definitions

1. descendant, offspring, posterity
A person descended from a particular ancestor; future generations.

Examples

彼(かれ)は武士(ぶし)の子孫(しそん)だ。
He is a descendant of samurai.
子孫(しそん)に良(よ)い環境(かんきょう)を残(のこ)したい。
I want to leave a good environment for future generations.
この家系(かけい)は子孫(しそん)繁栄(はんえい)を願(ねが)って建(た)てられた。
This house was built with wishes for the prosperity of descendants.

Notes

子孫(しそん) refers to descendants or future generations of a family line.

Common collocations:

  • 子孫(しそん)を残(のこ)す (to leave descendants)
  • 子孫(しそん)繁栄(はんえい) (prosperity of descendants)
  • 子孫(しそん)に伝(つた)える (to pass down to descendants)
  • 〜の子孫(しそん) (descendant of ~)

Antonym: 先祖(せんぞ) / 祖先(そせん) (ancestor)

Related words:

  • 末裔(まつえい) (descendant - more literary)
  • 後裔(こうえい) (descendant - formal)
  • 子々孫々(ししそんそん) (descendants for generations)
