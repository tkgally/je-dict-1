食欲(しょくよく)

しょくよく
noun
appetite

Definitions

1. appetite, desire to eat
The natural desire or craving for food.

Examples

風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いて食欲(しょくよく)がない。
I have no appetite because I caught a cold.
秋(あき)は食欲(しょくよく)の秋(あき)と言(い)われる。
Fall is said to be the season of appetite.
Common Japanese expression associating autumn with good eating
運動(うんどう)したら食欲(しょくよく)が出(で)てきた。
After exercising, I developed an appetite.

Notes

食欲(しょくよく) specifically refers to appetite for food.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 食欲(しょくよく)がある (to have an appetite)
  • 食欲(しょくよく)がない (to have no appetite)
  • 食欲(しょくよく)が出(で)る (appetite returns/develops)
  • 食欲(しょくよく)をそそる (to whet one's appetite)
  • 食欲(しょくよく)が落(お)ちる (appetite decreases)
  • 食欲(しょくよく)の秋(あき) (autumn, the season of appetite)

RELATED:

  • 食欲(しょくよく)不振(ふしん) (loss of appetite, poor appetite)
