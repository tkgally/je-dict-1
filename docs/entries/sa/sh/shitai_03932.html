死体(したい)

したい
noun
corpse, dead body

Definitions

1. corpse, dead body, cadaver
The dead body of a human or animal. A neutral, factual term commonly used in news, legal, and medical contexts.

Examples

山(やま)で死体(したい)が発見(はっけん)された。
A dead body was discovered in the mountains.
警察(けいさつ)が死体(したい)を検視(けんし)した。
The police examined the corpse.
検視 = autopsy, post-mortem examination
死体(したい)遺棄(いき)は重大(じゅうだい)な犯罪(はんざい)だ。
Abandoning a dead body is a serious crime.
死体遺棄 = abandonment of a corpse (legal term)

Notes

REGISTER: 死体(したい) is a neutral, clinical term. In everyday speech, especially when talking about deceased loved ones, 遺体(いたい) (remains) is more respectful.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 死体(したい)発見(はっけん) (discovery of a body)
  • 死体(したい)遺棄(いき) (abandoning a corpse)
  • 死体(したい)解剖(かいぼう) (autopsy)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 遺体(いたい) (remains - more respectful)
  • 死骸(しがい) (carcass, remains - often for animals)
  • 亡骸(なきがら) (remains - literary/poetic)

Related Words
