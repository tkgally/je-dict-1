失望(しつぼう)

しつぼう
noun, suru-verb
disappointment, despair

Definitions

1. disappointment, despair
The feeling of sadness or displeasure caused by unfulfilled hopes or expectations.

Examples

試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて失望(しつぼう)した。
I was disappointed when I failed the exam.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)には失望(しつぼう)させられた。
I was disappointed by his attitude.
期待(きたい)が大(おお)きかっただけに、失望(しつぼう)も大(おお)きかった。何(なに)も言(い)えずに黙(だま)ってその場(ば)を離(はな)れた。
Because my expectations were high, my disappointment was equally great. Unable to say anything, I quietly left the place.

Notes

失望(しつぼう) expresses disappointment or loss of hope, typically when expectations are not met.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 失望(しつぼう)する (to be disappointed)
  • 失望(しつぼう)させる (to disappoint someone)
  • 失望(しつぼう)を感(かん)じる (to feel disappointment)
  • 〜に失望(しつぼう)する (to be disappointed in/by ~)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 失望(しつぼう): disappointment (formal, stronger emotion)
  • がっかり: disappointment (casual, everyday expression)
  • 落胆(らくたん): discouragement, dejection (written/formal)

REGISTER: Somewhat formal. In casual conversation, がっかりする is more common.
