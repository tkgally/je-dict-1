染(し)み

しみ
noun
stain; spot; blemish

Definitions

1. stain; spot (on fabric)
A mark or discoloration on fabric or other surfaces.
2. blemish; age spot (on skin)
Spots or marks on the skin, especially from sun exposure or aging.

Examples

シャツに染(し)みがついた。
I got a stain on my shirt.
染(し)みを落(お)とす。
To remove a stain.
顔(かお)に染(し)みができた。
I got blemishes on my face.

Notes

染み can refer to both fabric stains and skin spots.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 染みがつく: to get a stain
  • 染みを落とす: to remove a stain
  • 染み抜き (しみぬき): stain removal

SKIN USAGE:

  • シミ: commonly written in katakana for skin spots
  • シミ対策: anti-blemish measures
  • シミそばかす: spots and freckles
