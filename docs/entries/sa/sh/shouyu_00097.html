醤油(しょうゆ)

しょうゆ
noun
soy sauce

Definitions

1. soy sauce
A dark, salty liquid condiment made from fermented soybeans. Essential in Japanese cooking.

Examples

刺身(さしみ)に醤油(しょうゆ)をつけて食(た)べます。
I eat sashimi with soy sauce.
醤油(しょうゆ)を取(と)ってください。
Please pass the soy sauce.
この料理(りょうり)は醤油(しょうゆ)味(あじ)です。
This dish is flavored with soy sauce.

Notes

醤油 is one of the most important seasonings in Japanese cuisine. There are various types including 濃口 (こいくち, dark) and 薄口 (うすくち, light). It's used for cooking, as a dipping sauce, and as a table condiment.
