修理(しゅうり)

しゅうり
noun, suru-verb
repair, mending

Definitions

1. repair, mending, fixing
The act of restoring something that is broken or damaged to good condition.

Examples

車(くるま)を修理(しゅうり)に出(だ)した。
I sent my car in for repair.
時計(とけい)を修理(しゅうり)してもらった。
I had my watch repaired.
この古(ふる)いパソコンは修理(しゅうり)するより新(あたら)しいのを買(か)った方(ほう)が安(やす)いかもしれない。部品(ぶひん)がもう手(て)に入(はい)らないそうだ。
It might be cheaper to buy a new computer than to repair this old one. They say the parts are no longer available.

Notes

修理(しゅうり) means repair or fixing things that are broken or damaged.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 修理(しゅうり)する (to repair)
  • 修理(しゅうり)に出(だ)す (to send for repair)
  • 修理(しゅうり)してもらう (to have something repaired)
  • 修理(しゅうり)中(ちゅう) (under repair)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 修理(しゅうり)代(だい) / 修理(しゅうり)費(ひ) (repair cost)
  • 修理(しゅうり)工場(こうじょう) (repair shop)
  • 修理(しゅうり)業者(ぎょうしゃ) (repair service provider)
  • 修理(しゅうり)不能(ふのう) (beyond repair)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 修理(しゅうり): repair (general, machines, buildings)
  • 修繕(しゅうぜん): repair, restoration (buildings, older usage)
  • 直(なお)す: to fix (casual, everyday)
  • 補修(ほしゅう): maintenance, repair (roads, buildings)
