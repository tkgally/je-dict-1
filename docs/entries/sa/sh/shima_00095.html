島(しま)

しま
noun
island

Definitions

1. island
A piece of land surrounded by water. Japan itself is an island nation (島国) composed of many islands.

Examples

日本(にほん)は島国(しまぐに)です。
Japan is an island country.
あの島(しま)に行(い)きたいです。
I want to go to that island.
沖縄(おきなわ)にはたくさんの島(しま)があります。
There are many islands in Okinawa.

Notes

Japan has over 6,800 islands. Major islands include 本州(ほんしゅう) (Honshu), 北海道(ほっかいどう) (Hokkaido), 九州(きゅうしゅう) (Kyushu), and 四国(しこく) (Shikoku). The word appears in many place names.
