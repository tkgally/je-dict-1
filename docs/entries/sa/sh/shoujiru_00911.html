生(しょう)じる

しょうじる
verb (ichidan)
to occur, to arise, to be generated

Definitions

1. to occur, to arise
For something to come into existence or happen.
2. to be generated, to be produced
For something to be created as a result.

Examples

問題(もんだい)が生(しょう)じた。
A problem arose.
誤解(ごかい)が生(しょう)じる。
Misunderstandings occur.

Notes

生(しょう)じる is a formal verb.

ALTERNATE FORM:

  • 生(しょう)ずる: classical form

SIMILAR:

  • 生(しょう)じる: to arise (formal)
  • 起(お)こる: to occur (general)
  • 発生(はっせい)する: to occur (formal)
