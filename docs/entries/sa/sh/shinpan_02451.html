審判(しんぱん)

しんぱん
noun
referee, umpire; judgment

Definitions

1. referee, umpire
A person who officiates at a sporting event, making decisions on rules and play.
2. judgment, ruling
An official decision or verdict, especially in a legal or religious context.

Examples

審判(しんぱん)がファウルを取(と)った。
The referee called a foul.
審判(しんぱん)の判定(はんてい)に抗議(こうぎ)した。
They protested against the referee's decision.
最後(さいご)の審判(しんぱん)という宗教(しゅうきょう)的(てき)な概念(がいねん)がある。
There is a religious concept called the Last Judgment.

Notes

審判(しんぱん) can refer to both the person (referee/umpire) and the act of judging.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 審判(しんぱん)を務(つと)める (to serve as referee)
  • 審判(しんぱん)の判定(はんてい) (referee's decision)
  • 最後(さいご)の審判(しんぱん) (the Last Judgment)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 審判(しんぱん): general term for sports officials; also used for divine/legal judgment
  • レフェリー: referee (borrowed from English, mainly for boxing, soccer)
  • アンパイア: umpire (borrowed, mainly for baseball)
