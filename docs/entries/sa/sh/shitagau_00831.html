従(したが)う

したがう
verb (godan)
to follow, to obey, to comply

Definitions

1. to follow, to accompany
To go along with someone.
2. to obey, to comply with
To act according to rules or orders.
3. as, in accordance with
Used to indicate proportion or correlation.

Examples

規則(きそく)に従(したが)う。
To obey the rules.
指示(しじ)に従(したが)ってください。
Please follow the instructions.
年(とし)を取(と)るに従(したが)って忘(わす)れっぽくなる。
As you get older, you become more forgetful.

Notes

従(したが)う has multiple uses.

MEANINGS:
1. To follow (a person/path)
2. To obey (rules/orders)
3. As ~ (proportion/correlation)

PATTERN:

  • 〜に従(したが)って: as ~, in proportion to ~

EXAMPLE:

  • 経験(けいけん)を積(つ)むに従(したが)って: as one gains experience
