しゃきしゃき

しゃきしゃき
adverb, suru verb
crisply; briskly; fresh and crisp

Definitions

1. crisply; fresh and crisp
Describes the texture of fresh, crisp vegetables like lettuce, bean sprouts, or similar foods that make a light crunching sound when eaten.
2. briskly; energetically
Describes doing things in a brisk, efficient manner with energy and purpose.

Examples

このレタスはしゃきしゃきしていて美味(おい)しい。
This lettuce is crisp and delicious.
もやしはしゃきしゃき感(かん)を残(のこ)して炒(いた)めると良(よ)い。
Bean sprouts are best stir-fried while keeping their crisp texture.
しゃきしゃき動(うご)いてください。
Please work briskly.

Notes

しゃきしゃき has two main uses: describing the crisp texture of fresh vegetables (especially leafy ones and sprouts) and describing brisk, energetic action.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • さくさく: crispy (for fried food, cookies)
  • ぱりぱり: crispy/crunchy (for chips, nori)
  • きびきび: briskly (similar to sense 2)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • しゃきしゃきした野菜(やさい): crisp vegetables
  • しゃきしゃき感(かん): crisp texture
  • しゃきしゃき働(はたら)く: to work briskly

COOKING TIP:
When stir-frying vegetables, しゃきしゃき感(かん)を残(のこ)す (keeping the crisp texture) is often desired.

Related Words
