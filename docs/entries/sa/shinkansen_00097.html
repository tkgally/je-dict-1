新幹線(しんかんせん)

しんかんせん
noun
bullet train

Definitions

1. bullet train, Shinkansen
Japan's high-speed railway system, connecting major cities across the country.

Examples

新幹線(しんかんせん)で東京(とうきょう)から大阪(おおさか)まで2時間半(じかんはん)です。
It takes 2.5 hours from Tokyo to Osaka by Shinkansen.
新幹線(しんかんせん)の切符(きっぷ)を買(か)いました。
I bought a Shinkansen ticket.
新幹線(しんかんせん)は速(はや)くて便利(べんり)です。
The Shinkansen is fast and convenient.

Notes

新幹線 literally means 'new trunk line.' The first Shinkansen (東海道新幹線) opened in 1964 for the Tokyo Olympics. They are famous for speed (up to 320 km/h), punctuality, and safety. Different lines have different names (のぞみ, ひかり, こだま).
