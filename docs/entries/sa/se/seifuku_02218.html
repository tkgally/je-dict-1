制服(せいふく)

せいふく
noun
uniform

Definitions

1. uniform
A distinctive set of clothes worn by members of the same organization, school, or workplace.

Examples

学校(がっこう)の制服(せいふく)を着(き)る。
I wear the school uniform.
制服(せいふく)は毎日(まいにち)洗濯(せんたく)している。
I wash my uniform every day.
この会社(かいしゃ)は制服(せいふく)がない。
This company has no uniform.

Notes

制服(せいふく) combines 制(せい) (regulation) + 服(ふく) (clothes).

TYPES OF UNIFORMS:

  • 学校(がっこう)制服(せいふく) (school uniform)
  • 会社(かいしゃ)の制服(せいふく) (company uniform)
  • 警察(けいさつ)の制服(せいふく) (police uniform)
  • 軍服(ぐんぷく) (military uniform)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 制服(せいふく)を着(き)る (to wear a uniform)
  • 制服(せいふく)に着替(きが)える (to change into uniform)
  • 制服(せいふく)姿(すがた) (in uniform)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese school uniforms are an iconic part of student life. Most junior high and high schools require uniforms, and they have become a significant aspect of Japanese pop culture.
