正解(せいかい)

せいかい
noun / suru-verb
correct answer, right answer

Definitions

1. correct answer, right answer
The correct or right answer to a question or problem.
2. correct, right
Being correct or right about something.

Examples

正解(せいかい)は3番(ばん)です。
The correct answer is number 3.
あなたの判断(はんだん)は正解(せいかい)だった。
Your judgment was correct.
全問(ぜんもん)正解(せいかい)だった。
I got all the answers right.

Notes

正解(せいかい) combines 正(せい) (correct) + 解(かい) (answer/solution).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 正解(せいかい)を言(い)う/出(だ)す (to give the correct answer)
  • 正解(せいかい)する (to answer correctly)
  • 正解(せいかい)率(りつ) (accuracy rate)
  • 全問(ぜんもん)正解(せいかい) (all answers correct)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 不正解(ふせいかい) (incorrect answer) - antonym
  • 誤答(ごとう) (wrong answer)
  • 解答(かいとう) (answer, solution)
  • 答(こた)え (answer - general)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Common in quiz shows, tests, and educational contexts
  • Can also be used metaphorically for decisions and judgments

Related Words

Antonym: 不正解(ふせいかい) (incorrect)
Related: 解答(かいとう) (answer)
