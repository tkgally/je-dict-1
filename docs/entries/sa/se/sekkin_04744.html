接近(せっきん)

せっきん
noun, suru-verb
approach, drawing near

Definitions

1. approach, getting closer
The act of coming nearer or drawing close to something or someone.
2. becoming close (in relationship)
Developing a closer relationship with someone.

Examples

台風(たいふう)が日本(にほん)に接近(せっきん)している。
A typhoon is approaching Japan.
Common weather report expression
車(くるま)が接近(せっきん)してくるのが見(み)えた。
I could see a car approaching.
最近(さいきん)、彼女(かのじょ)と急(きゅう)に接近(せっきん)した。
Recently, I've gotten closer to her suddenly.
Refers to relationship closeness

Notes

接近(せっきん) describes physical approach or getting closer in a relationship. It's commonly used in weather reports for approaching storms.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Weather: 台風(たいふう)が接近(せっきん)する (typhoon approaching)
  • Military/aviation: 敵機(てっき)が接近(せっきん) (enemy aircraft approaching)
  • Relationships: 二人(ふたり)が接近(せっきん)する (two people getting closer)
  • Traffic: 車両(しゃりょう)が接近(せっきん) (vehicle approaching)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 近(ちか)づく: to approach, get close (more general)
  • 迫(せま)る: to approach, draw near (often with sense of pressure)
  • 離(はな)れる: to separate, move away (opposite)
