先輩(せんぱい)

せんぱい
noun
senior, upperclassman

Definitions

1. senior, upperclassman, senior colleague
Someone who has more experience in a group, school, or workplace.

Examples

先輩(せんぱい)に相談(そうだん)しました。
I consulted with my senior.
彼(かれ)は大学(だいがく)の先輩(せんぱい)です。
He's my senior from university.

Notes

Key concept in Japanese social hierarchy. Opposite of 後輩(こうはい) (junior). Also used as a form of address.
