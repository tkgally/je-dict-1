正確(せいかく)

せいかく
na-adjective
accurate, precise, exact

Definitions

1. accurate, precise, exact
Free from error; conforming exactly to truth or to a standard.

Examples

正確(せいかく)な情報(じょうほう)を伝(つた)えてください。
Please convey accurate information.
この時計(とけい)は正確(せいかく)だ。
This clock is accurate.
正確(せいかく)に言(い)うと、三時(さんじ)十五分(じゅうごふん)です。
To be precise, it's 3:15.

Notes

正確(せいかく) is a na-adjective meaning accurate or precise.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 正確(せいかく)に (accurately, precisely)
  • Noun form: 正確(せいかく)さ (accuracy, precision)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正確(せいかく)な情報(じょうほう) (accurate information)
  • 正確(せいかく)な数字(すうじ) (precise figures)
  • 正確(せいかく)に言(い)う (to be precise, to speak accurately)
  • 時間(じかん)に正確(せいかく) (punctual)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正確(せいかく): accurate, precise (objective correctness)
  • 精密(せいみつ): precise, detailed (high precision)
  • 的確(てきかく): precise, on point (hitting the mark)

HOMOPHONES:

  • 正確(せいかく): accurate, precise
  • 性格(せいかく): personality, character

Related Words

Contrast: 性格(せいかく) (homophone - personality)
