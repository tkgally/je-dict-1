背中(せなか)

せなか
noun
back (of body)

Definitions

1. back (of the body)
The rear surface of the human body from the shoulders to the waist. Used for physical descriptions and back-related expressions.

Examples

背中(せなか)が痒(かゆ)いです。
My back is itchy.
背中(せなか)を向(む)けないでください。
Please don't turn your back to me.
子供(こども)の背中(せなか)を押(お)しました。
I pushed the child's back (gave them a push/encouragement).
Can mean literal pushing or figurative encouragement

Notes

背中(せなか)を押(お)す can mean both 'to push someone's back' physically and 'to give someone a push' or 'to encourage someone'. 背中(せなか)を見(み)せる (to show one's back) means to set an example.
