赤道(せきどう)

せきどう
noun
equator

Definitions

1. equator
The imaginary line around the middle of the Earth, equidistant from the North and South Poles, dividing the Earth into Northern and Southern hemispheres.

Examples

赤道(せきどう)付近(ふきん)は一年中(いちねんじゅう)暑(あつ)い。
It's hot all year round near the equator.
赤道(せきどう)を越(こ)えると南半球(みなみはんきゅう)になる。
When you cross the equator, you enter the Southern Hemisphere.
エクアドルは赤道(せきどう)直下(ちょっか)にある国(くに)だ。
Ecuador is a country directly on the equator.
The country name Ecuador comes from Spanish for 'equator'

Notes

The 赤道(せきどう) is the line of 0° latitude that circles the Earth at its widest point. The name literally means 'red path' (赤(あか) = red, 道(みち) = road/path).

GEOGRAPHICAL TERMS:

  • 北半球(きたはんきゅう): Northern Hemisphere
  • 南半球(みなみはんきゅう): Southern Hemisphere
  • 北緯(ほくい): north latitude
  • 南緯(なんい): south latitude
  • 赤道(せきどう)直下(ちょっか): directly on/under the equator

CLIMATE ZONES:

  • 熱帯(ねったい): tropical zone (near the equator)
  • 温帯(おんたい): temperate zone
  • 寒帯(かんたい): frigid zone (polar regions)
