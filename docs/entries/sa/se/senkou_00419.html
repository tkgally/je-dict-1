専攻(せんこう)

せんこう
noun, suru verb
major, specialization

Definitions

1. major (academic), specialization
A primary field of study at a university or academic institution.
2. to major in, to specialize in
To focus one's studies on a particular field.

Examples

専攻(せんこう)は何(なん)ですか。
What is your major?
Common question for students
経済学(けいざいがく)を専攻(せんこう)しています。
I am majoring in economics.
大学(だいがく)で日本(にほん)文学(ぶんがく)を専攻(せんこう)しました。
I majored in Japanese literature at university.

Notes

専攻(せんこう) refers to one's academic major or specialization.

TRANSITIVITY (as suru verb):

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを専攻(せんこう)する (major in X)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 専攻(せんこう)は何(なん)ですか (What's your major?)
  • ～を専攻(せんこう)する (to major in ~)
  • 専攻(せんこう)分野(ぶんや) (field of specialization)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 専門(せんもん): specialty, expertise (broader)
  • 専攻(せんこう): major (academic context)
