生物(せいぶつ)

せいぶつ
noun
living thing, organism

Definitions

1. living thing, organism, creature
Any living entity, including animals, plants, and microorganisms.

Examples

海(うみ)には様々(さまざま)な生物(せいぶつ)がいる。
There are various organisms in the ocean.
生物(せいぶつ)多様性(たようせい)を守(まも)ることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to protect biodiversity.
微(び)生物(せいぶつ)は目(め)に見(み)えない。
Microorganisms cannot be seen with the naked eye.

Notes

生物(せいぶつ) is the scientific term for living things.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生物(せいぶつ)学(がく) (biology)
  • 微(び)生物(せいぶつ) (microorganism)
  • 生物(せいぶつ)多様性(たようせい) (biodiversity)
  • 海洋(かいよう)生物(せいぶつ) (marine life)
  • 生物(せいぶつ)兵器(へいき) (biological weapon)

COMPARISON:

  • 生物(せいぶつ): living thing (scientific)
  • 生(い)き物(もの): living thing (everyday, often animals)
  • 動物(どうぶつ): animal

Related Words

Related: 生(い)き物(もの) (living thing (casual))
