背広(せびろ)

せびろ
noun
business suit

Definitions

1. business suit, men's suit
A formal Western-style suit, typically worn for business. The term is somewhat dated and スーツ is now more common.

Examples

面接(めんせつ)には背広(せびろ)を着(き)ていきます。
I'll wear a suit to the interview.
父(ちち)は毎日(まいにち)背広(せびろ)を着(き)て会社(かいしゃ)に行(い)きます。
My father wears a suit to the office every day.
新(あたら)しい背広(せびろ)を買(か)いました。
I bought a new suit.

Notes

背広 is a traditional Japanese word for a Western-style business suit. The origin is debated - possibly from Savile Row (London) or 背が広い (broad back). Today, スーツ (from English 'suit') is more commonly used.
