生物(せいぶつ)

せいぶつ
noun
living thing; biology

Definitions

1. living thing; creature; organism
Any form of life, including animals, plants, and microorganisms.
2. biology (as a school subject)
The study of living organisms, commonly abbreviated in Japanese schools.

Examples

海(うみ)には様々(さまざま)な生物(せいぶつ)がいる。
There are various creatures in the sea.
生物(せいぶつ)の授業(じゅぎょう)
Biology class
微(び)生物(せいぶつ)を研究(けんきゅう)する。
To study microorganisms.

Notes

生物(せいぶつ) is composed of 生(せい) (life) + 物(ぶつ) (thing).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 生物(せいぶつ)学(がく): biology (formal)
  • 微(び)生物(せいぶつ): microorganism
  • 生物(せいぶつ)多様性(たようせい): biodiversity
  • 海洋(かいよう)生物(せいぶつ): marine life

VS. 生(い)き物(もの):

  • 生物(せいぶつ): technical/scientific term
  • 生(い)き物(もの): everyday term for living things
