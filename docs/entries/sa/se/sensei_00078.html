先生(せんせい)

せんせい
noun
teacher, doctor

Definitions

1. teacher
A person who teaches.
2. doctor, master
A respectful title for doctors, lawyers, and experts.

Examples

先生(せんせい)、質問(しつもん)があります。
Teacher, I have a question.
Sense 1
田中(たなか)先生(せんせい)はどこですか？
Where is Dr./Mr. Tanaka?
Title
日本語(にほんご)の先生(せんせい)はとても親切(しんせつ)です。
My Japanese teacher is very kind.

Notes

Used as a title after names (e.g., 田中(たなか)先生(せんせい)). Never use for yourself. Applies to teachers, doctors, lawyers, authors, etc.
