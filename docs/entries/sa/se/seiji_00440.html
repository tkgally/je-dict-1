政治(せいじ)

せいじ
noun
politics, government

Definitions

1. politics, political affairs
Activities associated with governance, policy-making, and the exercise of power in a state or organization.

Examples

政治(せいじ)に興味(きょうみ)がありますか。
Are you interested in politics?
政治(せいじ)の話(はなし)はしないほうがいい。
It's better not to talk about politics.
Common social advice
彼(かれ)は政治(せいじ)家(か)になりたいそうです。
He says he wants to become a politician.

Notes

政治(せいじ) covers all aspects of politics and governance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 政治(せいじ)家(か): politician
  • 政治(せいじ)的(てき): political
  • 政治(せいじ)問題(もんだい): political issue
  • 政治(せいじ)経済(けいざい): political economy
  • 国際(こくさい)政治(せいじ): international politics

RELATED TERMS:

  • 政府(せいふ): government
  • 選挙(せんきょ): election
  • 政党(せいとう): political party
  • 国会(こっかい): National Diet (parliament)
  • 首相(しゅしょう): prime minister
