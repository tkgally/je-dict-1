作者(さくしゃ)

さくしゃ
noun
author, writer, creator

Definitions

1. author, writer
A person who writes books, articles, or other written works.
2. creator, artist
A person who creates artistic works such as manga, plays, or other creative works.

Examples

この小説(しょうせつ)の作者(さくしゃ)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is the author of this novel?
作者(さくしゃ)不明(ふめい)の作品(さくひん)だ。
It is a work by an unknown author.
漫画(まんが)の作者(さくしゃ)に会(あ)いたい。
I want to meet the manga artist.

Notes

RELATED TERMS:

  • 著者(ちょしゃ) - author (more formal, typically for non-fiction)
  • 作家(さっか) - writer, author (profession)
  • 原作者(げんさくしゃ) - original author

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 作者(さくしゃ)不明(ふめい) (author unknown)
  • 原(げん)作者(さくしゃ) (original author/creator)
