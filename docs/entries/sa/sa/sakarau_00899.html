逆(さか)らう

さからう
verb (godan)
to go against, to oppose, to disobey

Definitions

1. to go against, to oppose
To act in opposition to something.
2. to disobey
To refuse to follow orders or rules.

Examples

親(おや)に逆(さか)らう。
To disobey one's parents.
流(なが)れに逆(さか)らう。
To go against the current.

Notes

逆(さか)らう means to go against.

PATTERN:

  • 〜に逆(さか)らう: to go against ~

SIMILAR:

  • 反対(はんたい)する: to oppose (formal)
  • 逆(さか)らう: to defy, to disobey
