昨夜(さくや)

さくや
noun
last night

Definitions

1. last night
The night before today. Can also be read as ゆうべ.

Examples

昨夜(さくや)はよく(よく)眠(ねむ)れた。
I slept well last night.
昨夜(さくや)のパーティー。
Last night's party.
昨夜(さくや)から雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っている。
It's been raining since last night.

Notes

昨夜(さくや) means "last night". This word has two readings.

READINGS:

  • さくや: formal/written
  • ゆうべ: casual/spoken

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 昨夜(さくや)の出来事(できごと) (events of last night)
  • 昨夜(さくや)から (since last night)

RELATED TIME WORDS:

  • 今夜(こんや): tonight
  • 昨日(きのう): yesterday
  • 夕べ(ゆうべ): last night (alternative writing)
