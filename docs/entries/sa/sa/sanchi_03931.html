産地(さんち)

さんち
noun
producing area, place of origin

Definitions

1. producing area, place of origin, production site
The region or area where a product (especially agricultural or natural products) originates or is produced.

Examples

この野菜(やさい)の産地(さんち)はどこですか。
Where is this vegetable from?
青森(あおもり)はりんご}の{産地(さんち)として有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Aomori is famous as an apple-producing area.
産地(さんち)直送(ちょくそう)の新鮮(しんせん)な魚(さかな)を仕入(しい)れています。
We stock fresh fish shipped directly from the source.
産地直送 = direct from the source

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 産地(さんち)直送(ちょくそう) (direct from producing area, farm-to-table)
  • 産地(さんち)偽装(ぎそう) (falsification of place of origin)
  • 産地(さんち)表示(ひょうじ) (origin labeling)
  • 特産(とくさん)品(ひん)の産地(さんち) (producing area of local specialty)

USAGE: 産地(さんち) is commonly used in food labeling and in discussions about where agricultural products, seafood, or manufactured goods come from. It's a key term in food safety and consumer protection contexts.
