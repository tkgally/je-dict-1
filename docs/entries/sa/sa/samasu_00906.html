冷(さ)ます

さます
verb (godan)
to cool, to let cool

Definitions

1. to cool, to let cool
To lower the temperature of something.

Examples

スープを冷(さ)ます。
To cool the soup.
熱(ねつ)を冷(さ)ます。
To bring down a fever / To cool one's passion.

Notes

冷(さ)ます is transitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 冷(さ)ます (transitive): to cool something
  • 冷(さ)める (intransitive): to cool down

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 冷(さ)ます: to cool
  • 覚(さ)ます: to wake (someone)
