最近(さいきん)

さいきん
noun
recently, lately

Definitions

1. recently, lately, these days
Refers to the recent past, from a few days to several weeks ago, up to the present. Can function as a noun or adverb.

Examples

最近(さいきん)、忙(いそが)しいです。
I've been busy recently.
最近(さいきん)、映画(えいが)を見(み)ましたか。
Have you seen any movies lately?
最近(さいきん)の天気(てんき)はどうですか。
How's the weather been lately?
Used as noun modifier with の

Notes

Can be used without a particle when used adverbially. 最近(さいきん)の + noun means 'recent (noun)'. Covers a broader time span than 'yesterday' but more recent than 'a long time ago'.
