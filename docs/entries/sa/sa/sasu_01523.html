注(さ)す

さす
godan verb
to pour (drinks), to add

Definitions

1. to pour, to add (liquid)
To pour a small amount of liquid, especially when serving drinks.

Examples

目薬(めぐすり)を注す。
To put in eye drops.

Notes

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 注(さ)す: to pour (drinks)
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab
  • 指(さ)す: to point
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目薬(めぐすり)を注す: to put in eye drops
  • 油(あぶら)を注す: to oil, lubricate
