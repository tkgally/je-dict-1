探(さぐ)る

さぐる
godan verb
to search, to probe, to explore; to investigate

Definitions

1. to search, to grope for
To search for something by feeling around, especially in the dark.
2. to probe, to investigate
To examine or look into something carefully to find information.
3. to sound out, to feel out
To try to discover someone's intentions or opinions indirectly.

Examples

暗(くら)い中(なか)でスイッチを探(さぐ)った。
I groped for the switch in the dark.
相手(あいて)の意図(いと)を探(さぐ)る。
Probe the other person's intentions.
真相(しんそう)を探(さぐ)るために調査(ちょうさ)した。
I investigated to find out the truth.

Notes

探(さぐ)る means to search or probe, often by touch or careful investigation.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを探(さぐ)る

COMPARISON WITH 探(さが)す:

  • 探(さが)す: To look for, to search (general)
  • 探(さぐ)る: To probe, to feel around, to investigate (more specific)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ポケットを探(さぐ)る (to search through one's pocket)
  • 意図(いと)を探(さぐ)る (to probe intentions)
  • 本音(ほんね)を探(さぐ)る (to find out one's true feelings)
  • 様子(ようす)を探(さぐ)る (to feel out the situation)
  • 手探(てさぐ)りで (by groping, fumblingly)

Related Words
