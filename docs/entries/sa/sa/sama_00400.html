様(さま)

さま
suffix
Mr., Mrs., Ms. (polite)

Definitions

1. Mr., Mrs., Ms., Sir, Madam (very polite)
Honorific suffix showing high respect.

Examples

田中(たなか)様(さま)、お電話(でんわ)です。
Mr./Ms. Tanaka, you have a phone call.
お客(きゃく)様(さま)がお待(ま)ちです。
The customer is waiting.

Notes

More polite than さん. Used in business, formal letters, and when addressing customers. Also used in expressions like お疲(つか)れ様(さま).
