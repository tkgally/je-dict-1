作詞(さくし)

さくし
noun
songwriting; lyric writing

Definitions

1. songwriting; lyric writing
The act of writing lyrics for a song.

Examples

この曲(きょく)の作詞(さくし)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who wrote the lyrics for this song?
作詞(さくし)作曲(さっきょく)は同(おな)じ人(ひと)だ。
The lyrics and music were written by the same person.
彼(かれ)は有名(ゆうめい)な作詞(さくし)家(か)だ。
He is a famous lyricist.

Notes

作詞(さくし) (作 = make + 詞 = words/lyrics)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 作詞(さくし)家(か): lyricist
  • 作曲(さっきょく): composition (music)
  • 編曲(へんきょく): arrangement
  • 作詞(さくし)作曲(さっきょく): written and composed by

COMMON FORMAT: Credits often appear as 作詞：〇〇 (Lyrics: 〇〇)
