砂糖(さとう)

さとう
noun
sugar

Definitions

1. sugar
A sweet crystalline substance obtained from sugarcane or sugar beets, used as a sweetener in food and drinks.

Examples

コーヒーに砂糖(さとう)を入(い)れますか。
Do you put sugar in your coffee?
砂糖(さとう)をもう少(すこ)しください。
Please give me a little more sugar.
この料理(りょうり)には砂糖(さとう)を使(つか)いません。
This dish doesn't use sugar.

Notes

One of the basic cooking ingredients along with 塩(しお) (salt). In Japanese cuisine, sugar is often used in savory dishes like teriyaki. 砂糖(さとう) can also be a common Japanese surname.
