裂(さ)く

さく
verb (godan)
to tear, to rip, to split

Definitions

1. to tear, to rip
To pull apart by force.
2. to split, to divide
To separate into parts.

Examples

紙(かみ)を裂(さ)く。
To tear paper.
二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)く。
To separate two people.

Notes

裂(さ)く means to tear.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 裂(さ)く (transitive): to tear
  • 裂(さ)ける (intransitive): to tear, to split

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 裂(さ)く: to tear
  • 咲(さ)く: to bloom (flowers)
