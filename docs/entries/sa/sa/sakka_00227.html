作家(さっか)

さっか
noun
writer, author

Definitions

1. writer, author, novelist
A person who writes books, especially fiction.

Examples

有名(ゆうめい)な作家(さっか)に会(あ)った。
I met a famous author.
将来(しょうらい)作家(さっか)になりたい。
I want to become a writer in the future.

Notes

Typically refers to literary writers. For other types of writers, more specific terms exist: 脚本家(きゃくほんか) (screenwriter), 漫画家(まんがか) (manga artist).
