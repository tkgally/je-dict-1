探(さが)す

さがす
verb (godan)
to search for, to look for

Definitions

1. to search for, to look for
To try to find something or someone. Also written as 捜(さが)す for searching for people.

Examples

鍵(かぎ)を探(さが)しています。
I'm looking for my keys.
仕事(しごと)を探(さが)しています。
I'm looking for a job.
どこを探(さが)しても見(み)つかりません。
No matter where I look, I can't find it.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを探(さが)す (search for X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 探(さが)している = 'is looking for' (active search in progress).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を探(さが)す (look for a job)
  • 鍵(かぎ)を探(さが)す (look for keys)
  • 部屋(へや)を探(さが)す (look for an apartment)
  • ネットで探(さが)す (search online)

Two kanji: 探(さが)す (search for things), 捜(さが)す (search for people/missing items).

Related: 見(み)つける (to find - the result of searching).
