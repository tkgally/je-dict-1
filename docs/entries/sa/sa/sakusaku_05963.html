さくさく

さくさく
adverb, na-adjective
crispy; crunchy; smoothly progressing

Definitions

1. crispy; crunchy
Describes a light, pleasant crunchiness, typically of food with a delicate texture like tempura, fresh vegetables, or pastries.
2. smoothly; efficiently; quickly
Describes something proceeding smoothly without hindrance, especially work or tasks.

Examples

この天(てん)ぷらはさくさくしておいしい。
This tempura is crispy and delicious.
新鮮(しんせん)な野菜(やさい)のさくさくした食感(しょっかん)が好(す)きだ。
I like the crispy texture of fresh vegetables.
仕事(しごと)がさくさく進(すす)む。
The work is progressing smoothly.

Notes

さくさく is a versatile onomatopoeia with both food-related and abstract meanings.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • さくさくした食感(しょっかん): crispy texture
  • さくさく進(すす)む: to progress smoothly
  • さくさく動(うご)く: to run smoothly (computers, devices)

CONTRAST WITH OTHER CRUNCHY SOUNDS:

  • ぱりぱり: crispy (dry, like chips)
  • ばりばり: hard crunching
  • しゃきしゃき: fresh crispness (vegetables)
