指(さ)す

さす
verb (godan)
to point, to indicate

Definitions

1. to point at, to indicate
To direct attention to something.
2. to refer to, to mean
To indicate a meaning or reference.

Examples

指(ゆび)で指(さ)す。
To point with one's finger.
それは何(なに)を指(さ)しますか。
What does that refer to?
針(はり)が北(きた)を指(さ)している。
The needle is pointing north.

Notes

指(さ)す means to point or indicate.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 指(さ)す: to point
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella), shine
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, pierce

COMMON USES:

  • 指(ゆび)を指(さ)す: to point
  • 〜を指(さ)す: to refer to ~
