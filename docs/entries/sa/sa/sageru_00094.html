下(さ)げる

さげる
verb (ichidan)
to lower, to hang

Definitions

1. to lower, to bring down
To move something to a lower position or to decrease something like price or temperature.
2. to hang, to carry
To hang or carry something, often from the shoulder or hand.

Examples

頭(あたま)を下(さ)げてください。
Please bow your head.
Bowing is common in Japanese culture
値段(ねだん)を下(さ)げてください。
Please lower the price.
かばんを手(て)に下(さ)げています。
I'm carrying a bag in my hand.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 下(さ)がる (intransitive). Pattern: Xを下(さ)げる (lower X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 下(さ)げている = 'have lowered' or 'is carrying (hanging).'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 頭(あたま)を下(さ)げる (bow one's head)
  • 値段(ねだん)を下(さ)げる (lower the price)
  • 温度(おんど)を下(さ)げる (lower the temperature)
  • かばんを下(さ)げる (carry a bag)

PAIR: 下(さ)がる (intransitive) - 気温(きおん)が下(さ)がった (temperature dropped). 頭(あたま)を下(さ)げる = bow (important in Japanese culture).
