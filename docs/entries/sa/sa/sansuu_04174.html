算数(さんすう)

さんすう
noun
arithmetic, math (elementary)

Definitions

1. arithmetic, elementary mathematics
Basic mathematics taught in elementary school, including addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Examples

算数(さんすう)が苦手(にがて)な子供(こども)が多(おお)い。
Many children are weak at arithmetic.
小学校(しょうがっこう)で算数(さんすう)を習(なら)った。
I learned arithmetic in elementary school.
算数(さんすう)のテストで満点(まんてん)を取(と)った。
I got a perfect score on the arithmetic test.

Notes

SCOPE NOTE:
算数(さんすう) specifically refers to elementary school mathematics (grades 1-6 in Japan). From middle school onward, mathematics is called 数学(すうがく).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 算数(さんすう)の授業(じゅぎょう) (arithmetic class)
  • 算数(さんすう)の問題(もんだい) (arithmetic problem)
  • 算数(さんすう)ドリル (arithmetic workbook/drill)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 数学(すうがく) - mathematics (middle school and beyond, more advanced)
  • 計算(けいさん) - calculation (the act of computing)
