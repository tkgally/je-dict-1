先程(さきほど)

さきほど
adverb
a little while ago, just now

Definitions

1. a little while ago, just now, a moment ago
Refers to a short time in the recent past, typically within the same day or conversation.

Examples

先程(さきほど)電話(でんわ)がありました。
There was a phone call a little while ago.
先程(さきほど)お話(はな)しした件(けん)ですが。
Regarding the matter we discussed earlier.
先程(さきほど)は失礼(しつれい)しました。
I apologize for earlier.

Notes

REGISTER: Polite and formal. Commonly used in business and formal contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • さっき - a little while ago (casual)
  • 先(さき)ほど - a little while ago (polite)
  • ついさっき - just a moment ago (casual)

USAGE: 先程 is the polite form of さっき.
