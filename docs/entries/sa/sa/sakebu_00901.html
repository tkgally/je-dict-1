叫(さけ)ぶ

さけぶ
verb (godan)
to shout, to cry out, to scream

Definitions

1. to shout, to cry out
To speak or call loudly.
2. to scream
To make a loud, high-pitched cry.

Examples

助(たす)けて！と叫(さけ)んだ。
I shouted "Help!"
彼(かれ)は大声(おおごえ)で叫(さけ)んだ。
He shouted loudly.

Notes

叫(さけ)ぶ means to shout.

SIMILAR:

  • 叫(さけ)ぶ: to shout, to cry out
  • 怒鳴(どな)る: to yell (angrily)

RELATED:

  • 叫(さけ)び: a shout, a cry
