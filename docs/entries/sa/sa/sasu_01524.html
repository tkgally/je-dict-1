射(さ)す

さす
godan verb
to shine, to strike (light)

Definitions

1. to shine, to strike
For light to come in or fall upon something.

Examples

日(ひ)が射す。
The sun shines in.

Notes

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 射(さ)す: to shine (light)
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab
  • 指(さ)す: to point
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日(ひ)が射す: sunlight shines in
  • 光(ひかり)が射す: light shines through
