再来年(さらいねん)

さらいねん
noun
year after next

Definitions

1. the year after next
Two years from the current year.

Examples

再来年(さらいねん)、大学(だいがく)を卒業(そつぎょう)します。
I'll graduate from university the year after next.
オリンピックは再来年(さらいねん)だ。
The Olympics are the year after next.

Notes

Time reference sequence: 去年(きょねん)/昨年(さくねん) (last year) → 今年(ことし) (this year) → 来年(らいねん) (next year) → 再来年(さらいねん) (year after next).
