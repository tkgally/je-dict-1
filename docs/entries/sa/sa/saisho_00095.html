最初(さいしょ)

さいしょ
noun
first, beginning

Definitions

1. first, beginning
The first one in a sequence, or the starting point of something. Can function as a noun or adverbial expression.

Examples

最初(さいしょ)から説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain from the beginning.
最初(さいしょ)は難(むずか)しかったです。
It was difficult at first.
最初(さいしょ)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name first.
最初に = first, to begin with

Notes

Opposite of 最後(さいご) (last/end). 最初(さいしょ)に is used adverbially to mean 'first' or 'at first'. Different from 初(はじ)めて which means 'for the first time'.
