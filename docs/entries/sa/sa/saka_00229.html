坂(さか)

さか
noun
slope, hill

Definitions

1. slope, hill, incline
A surface of ground that is not flat, especially one that goes up or down.

Examples

この坂(さか)は急(きゅう)だ。
This slope is steep.
坂(さか)を上(のぼ)って学校(がっこう)に行(い)く。
I go up the hill to school.

Notes

Common in place names throughout Japan, such as 赤坂(あかさか) or 道玄坂(どうげんざか).
