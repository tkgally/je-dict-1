盛(さか)ん

さかん
adjective (na-adjective)
prosperous; thriving; active; enthusiastic

Definitions

1. prosperous, thriving, flourishing
Describes something that is doing well and growing, such as an industry or business.
2. active, lively, vigorous
Describes active participation or energetic activity.
3. popular, prevalent
Describes something that is widely practiced or enthusiastically embraced.

Examples

この地域(ちいき)では農業(のうぎょう)が盛(さか)んです。
Agriculture is thriving in this region.
Predicate usage
盛(さか)んな議論(ぎろん)が行(おこな)われた。
A lively discussion took place.
Modifier usage
日本(にほん)では野球(やきゅう)が盛(さか)んだ。
Baseball is popular in Japan.
盛(さか)んに拍手(はくしゅ)する。
To applaud enthusiastically.
Adverbial form

Notes

盛(さか)ん is a na-adjective meaning 'prosperous,' 'thriving,' or 'active.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 盛(さか)んに
  • Modifier: 盛(さか)んな + noun

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 盛(さか)んではない / 盛(さか)んじゃない
  • Te-form: 盛(さか)んで
  • Past: 盛(さか)んだった

Usage pattern: More common as a predicate (〜が盛(さか)んだ) than as a modifier.

Common collocations: 盛(さか)んな活動(かつどう) (active efforts), 盛(さか)んな議論(ぎろん) (lively debate), 盛(さか)んに行(おこな)われる (to be actively conducted).
