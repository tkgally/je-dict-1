〜冊(さつ)

さつ
counter
counter for books

Definitions

1. counter for books, volumes
Used to count bound publications such as books, magazines, notebooks, and other bound volumes.

Examples

本(ほん)を三冊(さんさつ)買(か)いました。
I bought three books.
ノートが何冊(なんさつ)ありますか。
How many notebooks are there?
一冊(いっさつ)ください。
One (book), please.

Notes

〜冊 is specifically for bound materials. Sound changes:

  • 一冊 (いっさつ)
  • 八冊 (はっさつ)
  • 十冊 (じゅっさつ). Don't confuse with 〜本 (hon)
  • which counts the books as long objects—〜冊 counts them as bound volumes.
