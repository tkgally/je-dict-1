ゼミ

ぜみ
noun
seminar

Definitions

1. seminar, study group
A small class at a university where students discuss topics with a professor.

Examples

ゼミで発表(はっぴょう)する。
I give a presentation in the seminar.
来週(らいしゅう)のゼミは休(やす)みだ。
Next week's seminar is canceled.
田中(たなか)先生(せんせい)のゼミに入(はい)った。
I joined Professor Tanaka's seminar.

Notes

ゼミ (from German 'Seminar') refers to a university seminar class.

In Japanese universities, ゼミ (also written ゼミナール) is an important part of undergraduate education, especially in the humanities and social sciences. Students typically:

  • Choose a ゼミ in their 3rd year
  • Meet regularly with a professor and other students
  • Give presentations and participate in discussions
  • Write a graduation thesis under the professor's guidance

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ゼミに入(はい)る: to join a seminar
  • ゼミを取(と)る: to take a seminar
  • ゼミの発表(はっぴょう): seminar presentation
  • ゼミ合宿(がっしゅく): seminar retreat/camp
