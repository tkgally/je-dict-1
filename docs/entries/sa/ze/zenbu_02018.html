全部(ぜんぶ)

ぜんぶ
noun / adverb
all, entire, whole

Definitions

1. all, entire, whole
The complete amount or every part of something.

Examples

全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べてください。
Please eat all of it.
宿題(しゅくだい)は全部(ぜんぶ)終(お)わりました。
I finished all of my homework.
全部(ぜんぶ)でいくらですか。
How much is it in total?

Notes

全部(ぜんぶ) means all or everything.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 全部(ぜんぶ)で (in total, altogether)
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)の + noun (all of ~)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全(すべ)て (all, everything - more formal)
  • みんな (everyone, all)
  • 全員(ぜんいん) (everyone, all members)
