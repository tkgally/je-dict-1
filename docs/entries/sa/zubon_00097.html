ズボン

ずぼん
noun
pants, trousers

Definitions

1. pants, trousers
Garment covering the lower body with separate leg sections. From French 'jupon.'

Examples

ズボンをはいてください。
Please put on your pants.
このズボンは長(なが)すぎます。
These pants are too long.
黒(くろ)いズボンを買(か)いました。
I bought black pants.

Notes

ズボン comes from French 'jupon.' The verb はく is used for putting on pants (unlike 着る for upper body clothing). Recently, パンツ is also commonly used for pants.
