事故(じこ)

じこ
noun
accident

Definitions

1. accident
An unexpected and undesirable event, especially one causing damage or injury. Commonly refers to traffic accidents or industrial incidents.

Examples

交通(こうつう)事故(じこ)がありました。
There was a traffic accident.
事故(じこ)で電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れています。
The train is delayed due to an accident.
事故(じこ)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful of accidents.

Notes

Common types include 交通事故(こうつうじこ) (traffic accident), 自動車事故(じどうしゃじこ) (car accident). When trains are delayed, announcements often mention 事故(じこ). Different from 事件(じけん) (incident/crime).
