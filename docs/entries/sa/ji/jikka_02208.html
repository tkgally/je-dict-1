実家(じっか)

じっか
noun
parents' home, family home

Definitions

1. parents' home, family home
The home where one's parents live; one's original family home that one has left.

Examples

週末(しゅうまつ)は実家(じっか)に帰(かえ)る。
I go home to my parents' place on weekends.
実家(じっか)は大阪(おおさか)にある。
My parents' home is in Osaka.
実家(じっか)から野菜(やさい)が送(おく)られてきた。
Vegetables were sent from my parents' home.

Notes

実家(じっか) refers specifically to one's parents' or birth family's home.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 実家(じっか)に帰(かえ)る (to go back to one's parents' home)
  • 実家(じっか)から通(かよ)う (to commute from parents' home)
  • 実家(じっか)を出(で)る (to leave one's parents' home)
  • 実家(じっか)暮(ぐ)らし (living with parents)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実家(じっか): parents' home (where parents live)
  • 故郷(ふるさと)/郷里(きょうり): hometown (place of origin)
  • 本家(ほんけ): main family house (in extended family)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Used when someone has moved out but visits their parents
  • Common topic in adult conversations about family

Related Words

Related: 故郷(ふるさと) (hometown)
