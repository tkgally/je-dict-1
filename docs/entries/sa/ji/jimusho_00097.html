事務所(じむしょ)

じむしょ
noun
office

Definitions

1. office
A room or building where business or administrative work is conducted.

Examples

事務所(じむしょ)は3階(がい)にあります。
The office is on the third floor.
弁護士(べんごし)事務所(じむしょ)に相談(そうだん)しました。
I consulted with a law office.
事務所(じむしょ)で働(はたら)いています。
I work in an office.

Notes

事務所 refers to an office as a workplace or business location. 事務 means office work/clerical duties. オフィス (from English 'office') is also commonly used. 会社 refers to a company as a whole, while 事務所 is the physical office space.
