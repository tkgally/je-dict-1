自分(じぶん)

じぶん
noun / pronoun
oneself, myself

Definitions

1. oneself, myself, yourself
Refers to the speaker or the subject of the sentence.

Examples

自分(じぶん)でやってみてください。
Please try doing it yourself.
自分(じぶん)の部屋(へや)を掃除(そうじ)した。
I cleaned my own room.
自分(じぶん)のことは自分(じぶん)でする。
I do my own things myself.

Notes

自分(じぶん) is a reflexive pronoun meaning oneself.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 自分(じぶん)で (by oneself)
  • 自分(じぶん)の (one's own)
  • 自分(じぶん)自身(じしん) (oneself - emphasis)

USAGE:

  • Can refer to any person depending on context
  • Often used where English would use "my/your/his/her own"
