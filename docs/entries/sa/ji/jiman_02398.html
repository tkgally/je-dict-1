自慢(じまん)

じまん
noun
pride, boasting

Definitions

1. pride, boast, something to be proud of
The act of speaking with excessive pride about one's achievements or possessions; also, something one is proud of.

Examples

息子(むすこ)は私(わたし)の自慢(じまん)です。
My son is my pride.
自慢(じまん)ばかりする人(ひと)は嫌(きら)われる。
People who only brag are disliked.
この料理(りょうり)は店(みせ)の自慢(じまん)だ。
This dish is the pride of the restaurant.

Notes

自慢(じまん)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to boast" or "to brag."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自慢(じまん)する (to boast, to brag)
  • 自慢(じまん)の + noun (one's pride and joy ~)
  • 自慢(じまん)話(ばなし) (bragging, boastful talk)
  • 手前(てまえ)自慢(じまん) (self-praise)
  • 親(おや)馬鹿(ばか)/自慢(じまん) (parental bragging about one's child)

NUANCE:
自慢(じまん)する often has a slightly negative nuance (boasting), but 自慢(じまん)の + noun (something to be proud of) is neutral or positive.
