自殺(じさつ)

じさつ
noun
suicide

Definitions

1. suicide, self-destruction
The act of intentionally taking one's own life.

Examples

自殺(じさつ)予防(よぼう)のための相談(そうだん)窓口(まどぐち)がある。
There are counseling services for suicide prevention.
自殺(じさつ)率(りつ)を下(さ)げるための対策(たいさく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Measures to reduce the suicide rate are necessary.
彼(かれ)は経済的(けいざいてき)な問題(もんだい)で自殺(じさつ)を図(はか)った。
He attempted suicide due to financial problems.

Notes

自殺(じさつ)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to commit suicide."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自殺(じさつ)する (to commit suicide)
  • 自殺(じさつ)を図(はか)る (to attempt suicide)
  • 自殺(じさつ)未遂(みすい) (attempted suicide)
  • 自殺(じさつ)予防(よぼう) (suicide prevention)
  • 自殺(じさつ)率(りつ) (suicide rate)

Note: This is a sensitive topic. In Japan, there are help resources like "いのちの電話(でんわ)" (Inochi no Denwa - Lifeline).
