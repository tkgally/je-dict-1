財産(ざいさん)

ざいさん
noun
property, fortune, assets

Definitions

1. property, assets, wealth
Things of value that a person or organization owns, including money, land, and possessions.
2. fortune, estate
A large amount of money or valuable possessions.

Examples

彼(かれ)は財産(ざいさん)を全部(ぜんぶ)失(うしな)った。
He lost all his property.
財産(ざいさん)を子供(こども)たちに残(のこ)したい。
I want to leave my fortune to my children.
知識(ちしき)は一生(いっしょう)の財産(ざいさん)です。
Knowledge is a lifelong asset.
Figurative use

Notes

財産(ざいさん) refers to property, assets, or wealth.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 財産(ざいさん)を築(きず)く (build up one's fortune)
  • 財産(ざいさん)を相続(そうぞく)する (inherit property)
  • 財産(ざいさん)を管理(かんり)する (manage assets)
  • 知的(ちてき)財産(ざいさん) (intellectual property)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 資産(しさん): assets (more formal, business term)
  • 財産(ざいさん): property, wealth (general)
  • お金(かね): money
  • 富(とみ): wealth, fortune
