在学(ざいがく)

ざいがく
noun, suru-verb
enrolled in school, attending

Definitions

1. being enrolled in school, attending school
The state of being registered as a student at an educational institution.

Examples

大学(だいがく)に在学(ざいがく)している。
I am enrolled at university.
在学(ざいがく)中(ちゅう)にアルバイトをした。
I worked part-time while I was in school.
在学(ざいがく)証明書(しょうめいしょ)を発行(はっこう)してもらった。
I had a certificate of enrollment issued.

Notes

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 在学(ざいがく)中(ちゅう) (while enrolled in school)
  • 在学(ざいがく)生(せい) (enrolled student, current student)
  • 在学(ざいがく)証明書(しょうめいしょ) (certificate of enrollment)
  • 在学(ざいがく)期間(きかん) (period of enrollment)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 通学(つうがく) - commuting to school
  • 在籍(ざいせき) - being registered/enrolled (more general, also for organizations)
