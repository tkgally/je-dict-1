ざる

ざる
noun
colander

Definitions

1. colander; strainer; sieve
colander; strainer; sieve

Examples

ざるで麺(めん)の湯(ゆ)を切(き)った。
I drained the noodles with a colander.
ざるに野菜(やさい)を入(い)れて、水(みず)で洗(あら)ってください。
Please put the vegetables in a strainer and wash them with water.
蕎麦(そば)はざるに盛(も)って出(だ)された。
The soba was served on a bamboo strainer.

Notes

A ざる is a strainer or colander, traditionally made of bamboo but now also available in metal or plastic. It is essential in Japanese cooking for draining noodles, washing rice, and straining vegetables.

Common expressions:

  • ざるで水(みず)を切(き)る (drain water with a colander)
  • ざるに上(あ)げる (lift into a strainer)
  • ざる蕎麦(そば) (cold soba served on a bamboo strainer)

The term ざる also appears in the expression ざる耳(みみ), meaning "in one ear and out the other" (like water through a sieve).
