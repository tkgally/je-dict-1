じゃがいも

じゃがいも
noun
potato

Definitions

1. potato
A starchy root vegetable commonly used in cooking.

Examples

じゃがいもの皮(かわ)を剥(む)く。
To peel a potato.
カレーにじゃがいもを入(い)れる。
To add potatoes to curry.
北海道(ほっかいどう)はじゃがいもの産地(さんち)だ。
Hokkaido is a potato-producing region.

Notes

じゃがいも is one of the most common vegetables in Japanese home cooking.

ALTERNATE NAMES:

  • 馬鈴薯(ばれいしょ): formal/written term for potato

COMMON DISHES:

  • 肉(にく)じゃが: meat and potato stew
  • ポテトサラダ: potato salad
  • フライドポテト: French fries
  • コロッケ: croquette

ETYMOLOGY: From 'Jakarta' (ジャカルタ) + いも (potato), as potatoes were introduced via Jakarta.
