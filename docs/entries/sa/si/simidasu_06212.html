染(し)み出(だ)す

しみだす
verb (godan, intransitive)
to ooze out; to seep

Definitions

1. to ooze out; to seep through
To slowly leak or flow out through a surface, as liquid gradually penetrates or emerges.
2. to show through; to come to the surface
Figuratively, for emotions or inner qualities to gradually become visible or apparent.

Examples

傷口(きずぐち)から血(ち)が染(し)み出(だ)してきた。
Blood started to ooze from the wound.
地下(ちか)から水(みず)が染(し)み出(だ)している。
Water is seeping out from underground.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)から寂(さび)しさが染(し)み出(だ)していた。
A sense of loneliness was coming through in his words.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
染(し)みる (to soak in, to permeate) + 出(だ)す (to start, to begin to come out)

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) - the liquid or quality comes out on its own

ASPECT ({ている}):
染(し)み出(だ)している indicates an ongoing state of seeping or oozing.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 溢(あふ)れ出(だ)す: to overflow
  • 流(なが)れ出(だ)す: to flow out
  • 滲(にじ)み出(だ)す: to ooze out (similar meaning)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血(ち)が染(し)み出(だ)す: blood oozes out
  • 水(みず)が染(し)み出(だ)す: water seeps out
  • 油(あぶら)が染(し)み出(だ)す: oil seeps out

Related Words
