すっぱい

すっぱい
i-adjective
sour, acidic

Definitions

1. sour, acidic
Having a sharp, tart taste like vinegar or lemons.

Examples

レモンはすっぱい。
Lemons are sour.
このりんごはすっぱい。
This apple is sour.

Notes

すっぱい is usually written in hiragana.

KANJI:

  • 酸(す)っぱい

TASTE WORDS:

  • 甘(あま)い: sweet
  • 辛(から)い: spicy
  • 苦(にが)い: bitter
  • 塩辛(しおから)い: salty
