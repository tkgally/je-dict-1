好(す)き

すき
na-adjective
like, fond of

Definitions

1. like, fond of
Having favorable feelings toward something or someone.

Examples

音楽(おんがく)が好きです。
I like music.
あなたのことが好きです。
I like you. / I love you.

Notes

好き is a na-adjective, not a verb.

PATTERN:

  • 〜が好き: to like ~

LEVELS:

  • 好き: like
  • 大好き: love, really like
  • 好きじゃない: don't like
  • 嫌(きら)い: dislike

NOTE:
好きです can mean 'I love you' in romantic contexts.
