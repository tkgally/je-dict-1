すっかり

すっかり
adverb
completely; entirely; thoroughly

Definitions

1. completely; entirely
Indicates that something is done or changed to the fullest extent.

Examples

すっかり忘(わす)れていた。
I had completely forgotten.
風邪(かぜ)がすっかり治(なお)った。
My cold has completely recovered.
外(そと)はすっかり暗(くら)くなった。
It has gotten completely dark outside.

Notes

すっかり emphasizes completeness or thoroughness of a state or action.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すっかり: completely (emphasis on thoroughness)
  • 全部(ぜんぶ): all, everything (quantity)
  • 全然(ぜんぜん): not at all (with negatives)
