すやすや

すやすや
adverb
sleeping soundly

Definitions

1. sleeping soundly, peacefully
The manner of sleeping quietly and peacefully, often used for babies and children.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんがすやすや眠(ねむ)っている。
The baby is sleeping peacefully.
すやすやと寝息(ねいき)を立(た)てている。
Breathing softly in peaceful sleep.
猫(ねこ)がすやすや眠(ねむ)っている。
The cat is sleeping soundly.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
A gentle, positive word describing peaceful sleep. Often used to describe sleeping babies, children, or pets.
