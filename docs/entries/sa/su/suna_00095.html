砂(すな)

すな
noun
sand

Definitions

1. sand
Fine granular material found on beaches, in deserts, and riverbeds. Made of tiny particles of rock and minerals.

Examples

砂浜(すなはま)の砂(すな)は熱(あつ)いです。
The sand on the beach is hot.
子供(こども)が砂(すな)で遊(あそ)んでいます。
The children are playing in the sand.
靴(くつ)の中(なか)に砂(すな)が入(はい)りました。
Sand got into my shoes.

Notes

Related words:

  • 砂浜(すなはま) (sandy beach)
  • 砂漠(さばく) (desert)
  • 砂場(すなば) (sandbox). The kanji 砂(すな) is also used in 砂糖(さとう) (sugar).
