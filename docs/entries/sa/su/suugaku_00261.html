数学(すうがく)

すうがく
noun
mathematics

Definitions

1. mathematics, math
The study of numbers, quantities, and shapes.

Examples

数学(すうがく)が得意(とくい)です。
I'm good at math.
数学(すうがく)の問題(もんだい)を解(と)く。
I solve math problems.

Notes

Academic subject. Related fields: 算数(さんすう) (arithmetic, elementary math), 代数(だいすう) (algebra), 幾何学(きかがく) (geometry).
