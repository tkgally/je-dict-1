全(すべ)て

すべて
noun, adverb
all, everything

Definitions

1. all, everything, the whole
The entirety of something; every part or item without exception.

Examples

全(すべ)てが終(お)わった。
Everything is over.
全(すべ)ての準備(じゅんび)が整(ととの)った。
All preparations are complete.
すべての + noun = all ~
彼(かれ)は全(すべ)てを知(し)っている。
He knows everything.

Notes

USAGE: 全(すべ)て can function as a noun meaning "everything" or as an adverb/pre-noun adjectival meaning "all."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 全(すべ)ての + noun (all ~)
  • 全(すべ)てが (everything - subject)
  • 全(すべ)てを (everything - object)

ALTERNATIVE KANJI: Can also be written as 総(すべ)て, though 全(すべ)て is more common.

CONTRAST:

  • 全(すべ)て - all, everything (formal)
  • 皆(みな)/皆(みんな) - everyone (for people)
  • 全部(ぜんぶ) - all, everything (casual)

Related Words

Related: 全部(ぜんぶ) (casual equivalent)
