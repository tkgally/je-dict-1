吸(す)う

すう
verb (godan)
to inhale, to suck

Definitions

1. to inhale, to breathe in
To draw air or other gases into the lungs.
2. to smoke
To draw smoke from a cigarette into the mouth or lungs.
3. to suck, to sip
To draw liquid into the mouth, or for something to absorb liquid.

Examples

深(ふか)く息(いき)を吸(す)ってください。
Please take a deep breath.
タバコ}を{吸(す)いますか。
Do you smoke?
Sense 2
蚊(か)に血(ち)を吸(す)われた。
A mosquito sucked my blood.
Sense 3 - passive

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Intransitive pair: 吸われる (passive). Pattern: Xを吸(す)う (to inhale/suck X).

ASPECT: ている indicates habitual action. タバコ}を{吸(す)っている can mean 'smokes (habitually)' or 'is smoking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 息(いき)を吸(す)う (to inhale, breathe in)
  • タバコ}を{吸(す)う (to smoke)
  • 空気(くうき)を吸(す)う (to breathe air)
  • 血(ち)を吸(す)う (to suck blood)

CONTRAST:

  • 吸(す)う - to inhale/suck in
  • 吐(は)く - to exhale/spit out

NOTE: タバコ}を{吸(す)う is the standard expression for 'to smoke.'

Related Words

Antonym: 吐(は)く (to exhale)
