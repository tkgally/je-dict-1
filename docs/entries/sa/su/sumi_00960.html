角(すみ)

すみ
noun
corner, nook

Definitions

1. corner
The point or area where two lines, edges, or surfaces meet.
2. nook
A secluded or out-of-the-way place.

Examples

部屋(へや)の角(すみ)に置(お)く。
To put it in the corner of the room.
角(すみ)から角(すみ)まで探(さが)した。
I searched from corner to corner.

Notes

角(すみ) means corner.

NOTE ON READINGS:

  • 角(すみ): corner (of room, page)
  • 角(かど): corner (of street, building), angle
  • 角(つの): horn (of animal)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 隅(すみ)に置(お)けない: not to be taken lightly
  • 隅々(すみずみ): every corner
