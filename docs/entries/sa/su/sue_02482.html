末(すえ)

すえ
noun
end, tip, future

Definitions

1. end, tip
The final part or extremity of something.
2. future, end result
What will happen eventually; the outcome after a process.
3. end of a period
The final part of a time period (month, year, etc.).

Examples

月(つき)末(すえ)までに提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit by the end of the month.
長(なが)い議論(ぎろん)の末(すえ)、結論(けつろん)が出(で)た。
After a long discussion, a conclusion was reached.
指(ゆび)の末(すえ)まで冷(つめ)たい。
It's cold down to my fingertips.

Notes

末(すえ) indicates the end or conclusion of something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～の末(すえ)（に） (after..., at the end of...)
  • 月(つき)末(すえ) (end of the month)
  • 年(ねん)末(すえ) (end of the year)
  • 週(しゅう)末(すえ) (weekend) - note: pronounced まつ in this compound

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 将来(しょうらい)の末(すえ) (in the future, eventually)
  • 苦労(くろう)の末(すえ) (after much hardship)
  • 議論(ぎろん)の末(すえ) (after discussion)

READING NOTE:

  • 末(すえ): kun'yomi, used independently or in patterns like ～の末(すえ)
  • 末(まつ): on'yomi, used in compounds like 週末(しゅうまつ), 月末(げつまつ)
