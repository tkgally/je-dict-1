すぐ

すぐ
adverb
immediately, soon

Definitions

1. immediately, right away, at once
Without delay or very shortly after.
2. easily, readily
With little effort or difficulty.
3. right (nearby), just
Very close in distance.

Examples

すぐ行(い)きます。
I'll go right away.
駅(えき)はすぐそこです。
The station is right there.
すぐ分(わ)かりました。
I understood immediately.

Notes

すぐに is more emphatic than すぐ alone. すぐ近(ちか)く means 'very nearby'. すぐ後(あと)で means 'right after'. すぐそこ means 'right there' (very close).
