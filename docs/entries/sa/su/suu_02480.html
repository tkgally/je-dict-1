数(すう)

すう
noun
number, figure

Definitions

1. number, figure, quantity
A mathematical value; a count or amount.

Examples

参加者(さんかしゃ)の数(すう)が増(ふ)えた。
The number of participants increased.
数(すう)を数(かぞ)える。
To count the numbers.
偶数(ぐうすう)と奇数(きすう)に分(わ)ける。
Divide into even and odd numbers.

Notes

数(すう) is used both independently and as a component in compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 数(すう)が増(ふ)える/減(へ)る (number increases/decreases)
  • 数(すう)を数(かぞ)える (to count numbers)
  • 数(すう)十(じゅう) (tens, dozens)
  • 数(すう)百(ひゃく) (hundreds)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 人数(にんずう) (number of people)
  • 数字(すうじ) (numeral, digit)
  • 数学(すうがく) (mathematics)
  • 偶数(ぐうすう) (even number)
  • 奇数(きすう) (odd number)
  • 多数(たすう) (large number, majority)
  • 少数(しょうすう) (small number, minority)

READING NOTE:
数(すう) is the on'yomi reading used in compounds. The kun'yomi is 数(かず).
