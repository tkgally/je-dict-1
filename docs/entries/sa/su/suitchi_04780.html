スイッチ

すいっち
noun
switch

Definitions

1. switch (electrical)
A device for making or breaking an electrical connection.
2. switch, change
A change from one thing to another.

Examples

スイッチを入(い)れてください。
Please turn on the switch.
スイッチを切(き)るのを忘(わす)れた。
I forgot to turn off the switch.
気持(きも)ちのスイッチが入(はい)った。
Something clicked in my mind (my mental switch turned on).
Figurative usage

Notes

スイッチ is used for both physical switches and figurative mode changes.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • スイッチを入(い)れる: to turn on (a switch)
  • スイッチを切(き)る: to turn off (a switch)
  • スイッチが入(はい)る: (something) turns on, gets activated
  • スイッチを押(お)す: to press a switch
  • 電気(でんき)のスイッチ: light switch

USAGE NOTE:
スイッチが入(はい)る is often used figuratively to mean "getting into the zone" or "becoming motivated."
