すっと

すっと
adverb
straight, quickly, smoothly

Definitions

1. straight, directly
In a straight line without deviation.
2. quickly, swiftly, smoothly
Moving or happening in a quick and smooth manner.

Examples

すっと立(た)ち上(あ)がった。
He stood up quickly.
痛(いた)みがすっと消(き)えた。
The pain quickly disappeared.
すっと前(まえ)に進(すす)んだ。
I moved straight forward.

Notes

すっと expresses quick, smooth, or straight movement.

NUANCES:

  • Quick, effortless motion
  • Smooth transition
  • Direct, without hesitation

SIMILAR ADVERBS:

  • さっと: quickly (similar)
  • ぱっと: suddenly
  • すらすら: smoothly, fluently
  • すうっと: smoothly (longer sound)
