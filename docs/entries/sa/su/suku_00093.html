すく

すく
verb (godan)
to become empty, not crowded

Definitions

1. to become empty, to not be crowded
Describes a place that has few people, or something (like stomach) becoming empty. The opposite of 込(こ)む (to be crowded).
2. to become hungry
Used in the expression お腹(なか)がすく to mean 'to become hungry' (stomach becoming empty).

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)がすいています。
The train is not crowded.
お腹(なか)がすきました。
I'm hungry.
Literally: stomach has become empty
午後(ごご)は道(みち)がすいているでしょう。
The roads will probably be empty in the afternoon.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xがすく (X becomes empty).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. すいている = 'is empty/not crowded' (state, not process).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お腹(なか)がすく (become hungry)
  • 電車(でんしゃ)がすいている (the train is not crowded)
  • 道(みち)がすいている (the road is clear)
  • お腹(なか)がすいた (I'm hungry - past = current state)

Opposite: 込(こ)む/混(こ)む (to be crowded).

Note: すく can use different kanji (空(す)く for empty, 透(す)く for transparent).
