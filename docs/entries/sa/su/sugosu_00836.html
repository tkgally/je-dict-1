過(す)ごす

すごす
verb (godan)
to spend (time), to pass

Definitions

1. to spend, to pass (time)
To use time in a certain way.
2. to live, to get by
To lead one's life.

Examples

休日(きゅうじつ)を家(いえ)で過(す)ごす。
To spend the holiday at home.
楽(たの)しい時間(じかん)を過(す)ごした。
I had a good time.
静(しず)かに過(す)ごす。
To spend time quietly.

Notes

過(す)ごす means to spend time.

COMMON USES:

  • 時間(じかん)を過(す)ごす: to spend time
  • 日(ひ)を過(す)ごす: to spend days
  • 夏(なつ)を過(す)ごす: to spend the summer

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 過(す)ごす: to spend (time)
  • 過(す)ぎる: to pass, too much
