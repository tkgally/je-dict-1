酢(す)

す
noun
vinegar

Definitions

1. vinegar
A sour liquid made through fermentation, used as a condiment and in cooking. Essential for sushi rice.

Examples

寿司(すし)には酢(す)を使(つか)います。
Vinegar is used for sushi.
サラダに酢(す)をかけます。
I put vinegar on the salad.
酢(す)は体(からだ)にいいと言(い)われています。
It's said that vinegar is good for your health.

Notes

酢 is one of the basic seasonings in Japanese cooking. 米酢 (こめず, rice vinegar) is the most common type. 酢飯 (すめし) is the seasoned rice used for sushi. The word 酢の物 (すのもの) refers to vinegared dishes.
