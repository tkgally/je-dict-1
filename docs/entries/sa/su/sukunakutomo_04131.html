少(すく)なくとも

すくなくとも
adverb
at least, at the minimum

Definitions

1. at least, at the minimum
Indicates the minimum amount, degree, or extent; even if nothing else.

Examples

少(すく)なくとも一時間(いちじかん)はかかる。
It will take at least one hour.
少(すく)なくとも三回(さんかい)は読(よ)んだほうがいい。
You should read it at least three times.
少(すく)なくとも私(わたし)はそう思(おも)う。
At least I think so.
Used to express personal opinion as a minimum assertion

Notes

FORMATION: From 少(すく)ない (few, little) in its く-form + とも (even if).

WRITING: Often written in hiragana as すくなくとも.

POSITION: Usually appears at the beginning of a clause or before the element it modifies.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • せめて - at least (with nuance of hoping for minimum)
  • 最低(さいてい)でも - at the very least
  • どんなに少(すく)なくても - however little

CONTRAST:

  • 少(すく)なくとも - at least (minimum bound)
  • 多(おお)くても - at most (maximum bound)
