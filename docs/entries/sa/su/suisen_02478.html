推薦(すいせん)

すいせん
noun
recommendation

Definitions

1. recommendation, endorsement
The act of suggesting someone or something as suitable or good for a particular purpose.

Examples

先生(せんせい)の推薦(すいせん)で奨学金(しょうがくきん)をもらった。
I received a scholarship through my teacher's recommendation.
推薦(すいせん)状(じょう)を書(か)いてもらえますか。
Could you write a letter of recommendation for me?
この本(ほん)は専門家(せんもんか)に推薦(すいせん)されている。
This book is recommended by experts.

Notes

推薦(すいせん)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to recommend."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 推薦(すいせん)する (to recommend)
  • 推薦(すいせん)状(じょう) (letter of recommendation)
  • 推薦(すいせん)入試(にゅうし)/推薦(すいせん)入学(にゅうがく) (admission by recommendation)
  • 推薦(すいせん)図書(としょ) (recommended book)
  • 推薦(すいせん)を受(う)ける (to receive a recommendation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推薦(すいせん): recommendation (formal endorsement)
  • おすすめ: recommendation (casual, everyday)
  • 紹介(しょうかい): introduction, referral
