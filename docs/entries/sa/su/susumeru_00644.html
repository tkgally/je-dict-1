進(すす)める

すすめる
verb (ichidan)
to advance, to promote

Definitions

1. to advance, to move forward
To cause something to progress.
2. to promote, to carry on
To push forward with work or plans.

Examples

計画(けいかく)を進(すす)める。
To advance the plan.
話(はなし)を進(すす)める。
To move the discussion forward.
準備(じゅんび)を進(すす)めている。
We are proceeding with preparations.

Notes

進(すす)める means to advance or promote.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 進(すす)める (transitive): to advance
  • 進(すす)む (intransitive): to advance

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 進(すす)める: to advance
  • 勧(すす)める: to recommend
