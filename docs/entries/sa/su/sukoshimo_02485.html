少(すこ)しも

すこしも
adverb
not at all, not in the least

Definitions

1. not at all, not in the least
Used with negative verbs to emphasize that something is completely absent or does not happen at all.

Examples

少(すこ)しも分(わ)からない。
I don't understand at all.
彼(かれ)は少(すこ)しも変(か)わっていない。
He hasn't changed at all.
少(すこ)しも気(き)にしていない。
I'm not bothered in the slightest.

Notes

少(すこ)しも is always used with a negative verb to mean "not at all."

GRAMMAR PATTERN:
少(すこ)しも + [negative verb]

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 少(すこ)しも...ない: not at all (emphasizes zero amount)
  • 全然(ぜんぜん)...ない: not at all (common in speech)
  • 全(まった)く...ない: not at all (formal)
  • ちっとも...ない: not at all (casual)

COMMON MISTAKE:
Using 少(すこ)しも with affirmative verbs. This expression requires a negative ending.
