すてき

すてき
na-adjective
wonderful, lovely

Definitions

1. wonderful, lovely
Extremely attractive or pleasing.

Examples

すてきな服(ふく)ですね。
What a lovely outfit!
すてきな考(かんが)えだ。
That's a wonderful idea.

Notes

すてき expresses admiration.

KANJI:

  • 素敵(すてき) (often in hiragana)

USAGE:

  • Often used by women
  • Expresses genuine admiration

SIMILAR:

  • 素晴(すば)らしい: wonderful
  • きれい: beautiful
