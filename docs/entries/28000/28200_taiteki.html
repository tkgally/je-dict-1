大敵(たいてき)

たいてき
noun
great enemy; formidable foe; nemesis
1. great enemy; formidable foe; archenemy
A powerful enemy or opponent. Very often used figuratively for anything that poses a serious threat or obstacle to a goal or condition.
油断(ゆだん)は大敵(たいてき)だ。
Carelessness is your worst enemy.
乾燥(かんそう)は肌(はだ)の大敵(たいてき)だ。
Dryness is the enemy of the skin.
ダイエット中(ちゅう)の人(ひと)にとって、甘(あま)いものは大敵(たいてき)だ。
For someone on a diet, sweets are the great enemy.

Often used figuratively: not a literal foe, but anything that seriously threatens a goal or condition.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 油断(ゆだん)は大敵(たいてき): carelessness is your worst enemy (a common saying)
  • 肌(はだ)の大敵(たいてき): the enemy of healthy skin
  • 健康(けんこう)の大敵(たいてき): a threat to one's health

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 強敵(きょうてき): tough opponent — a strong rival, usually in a contest or fight
  • 天敵(てんてき): natural enemy — a predator in nature, or figuratively someone one can never beat
