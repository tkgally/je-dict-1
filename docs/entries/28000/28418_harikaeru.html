1. to re-cover; to replace a stretched surface (paper, fabric, leather)
To remove an existing paper, cloth, leather, or screen surface and replace it with a fresh one. Common with shoji screens, wallpaper, sofa upholstery, and tatami covers.
障子(しょうじ)を張(は)り替(か)える。
Re-paper a shoji screen.
壁紙(かべがみ)を白(しろ)に張(は)り替(か)えた。
We re-papered the walls in white.
古(ふる)くなったソファのカバーを張(は)り替(か)えてもらったら、新品(しんぴん)のように見(み)えた。
When I had the cover of the old sofa replaced, it looked like new.

Compound of 張(は)る (to stretch, to put up) + 替(か)える (to exchange, to replace).

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The surface being replaced is marked with を; the new material with に.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 障子(しょうじ)を張(は)り替(か)える: re-paper a shoji screen
  • 壁紙(かべがみ)を張(は)り替(か)える: re-paper the walls
  • 畳(たたみ)の表(おもて)を張(は)り替(か)える: replace the cover of tatami mats
  • ソファのカバーを張(は)り替(か)える: re-cover a sofa

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 貼(は)り替(か)える: replace something pasted (a sticker, poster) — uses the kanji 貼(は) for sticking
  • 取(と)り替(か)える: to swap; replace (general) — does not imply a stretched surface

NOTE: 張(は)り替(か)える and 貼(は)り替(か)える are often confused; for shoji, wallpaper, and tatami, 張(は)り替(か)える is conventional.
