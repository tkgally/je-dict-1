家庭内(かていない)

かていない
noun
within the home; domestic; intra-family
1. within the household; domestic; in-home
Refers to events, relationships, or conditions that occur inside the home or among family members, as opposed to those involving the outside world.
家庭内(かていない)の問題(もんだい)を抱(かか)えている。
I'm dealing with problems within the family.
家庭内(かていない)での役割(やくわり)分担(ぶんたん)を話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed the division of roles within the household.
家庭内(かていない)暴力(ぼうりょく)の相談件数(そうだんけんすう)は、ここ数年(すうねん)で大幅(おおはば)に増(ふ)えている。
The number of consultations about domestic violence has increased dramatically in recent years.

Compound of 家庭(かてい) (household, family) + 内(ない) (inside). Usually attaches to another noun as a prefix-like modifier.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 家庭内(かていない)暴力(ぼうりょく): domestic violence (often abbreviated DV)
  • 家庭内(かていない)離婚(りこん): "in-home divorce" — couple living together but emotionally separated
  • 家庭内(かていない)別居(べっきょ): living separately within the same home
  • 家庭内(かていない)感染(かんせん): in-home (disease) transmission
  • 家庭内(かていない)労働(ろうどう): household labor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 家庭(かてい): family, household — the base noun without the "inside" nuance
  • 家(いえ)の中(なか): literally "inside the house" — refers to physical space rather than family dynamics
