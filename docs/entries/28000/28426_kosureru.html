1. to rub against (something); to chafe; to become worn
For two surfaces to rub against each other, often resulting in friction, irritation, or wear. The intransitive counterpart of 擦(こす)る (to rub something).
靴(くつ)が擦(こす)れて痛(いた)い。
My shoes are rubbing and it hurts.
袋(ふくろ)が壁(かべ)に擦(こす)れて破(やぶ)れた。
The bag rubbed against the wall and tore.
長(なが)い間(あいだ)使(つか)っていたので、リュックの底(そこ)が擦(こす)れて薄(うす)くなってきた。
Since I'd been using it for a long time, the bottom of the backpack had become worn and thin.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 擦(こす)る (to rub something). The thing that rubs is marked with が; what it rubs against is marked with に.

PATTERN:

  • AがBに擦(こす)れる: A rubs against B

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 靴(くつ)が擦(こす)れる: the shoes chafe
  • 肌(はだ)が擦(こす)れる: the skin chafes
  • 生地(きじ)が擦(こす)れる: the fabric gets worn
  • 擦(こす)れて破(やぶ)れる: tear from friction

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 擦(こす)る: to rub (something) — the transitive pair; an actor deliberately rubs something
  • 擦(す)り切(き)れる: to wear through completely — endpoint of long-term 擦(こす)れる
