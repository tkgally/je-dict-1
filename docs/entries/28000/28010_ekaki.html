絵描き

えかき
noun
painter; artist (informal)
1. painter; artist (informal)
A person who paints or draws pictures, especially as a profession. A casual, everyday word compared with 画家(がか).
彼(かれ)は絵描(えか)きだ。
He is a painter.
子供(こども)のころから絵描(えか)きになりたかった。
I wanted to be a painter ever since I was a child.
売(う)れない絵描(えか)きとして長(なが)い間(あいだ)苦労(くろう)したそうだ。
Apparently he struggled for a long time as a painter who couldn't sell his work.

Formed from 絵(え) (picture) + 描(か)き (drawing, from 描(か)く). A plain, slightly informal word for a painter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絵描(えか)きになる: to become a painter
  • 売(う)れない絵描(えか)き: a struggling / unsuccessful painter
  • 絵描(えか)き歌(うた): a drawing song (a children's song that guides drawing a picture)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 画家(がか): painter — more formal and dignified, used for established artists
  • 芸術家(げいじゅつか): artist — broader, covers all the arts
