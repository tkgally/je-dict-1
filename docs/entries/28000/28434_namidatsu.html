1. (for a water surface) to ripple; to become choppy
For a body of water to develop waves or ripples, typically due to wind or other disturbance.
湖(みずうみ)が波立(なみだ)つ。
The lake becomes choppy.
風(かぜ)で水面(すいめん)が波立(なみだ)っている。
The water's surface is rippling in the wind.
台風(たいふう)が近(ちか)づき、海(うみ)は激(はげ)しく波立(なみだ)ち始(はじ)めた。
As the typhoon approached, the sea began to grow rough.
2. to be disturbed; to be in turmoil (figuratively)
Used figuratively for emotions, atmosphere, or a situation becoming agitated or unsettled, like a calm surface being disturbed.
心(こころ)が波立(なみだ)つ。
My heart is troubled.
そのニュースを聞(き)いて、会場(かいじょう)が波立(なみだ)った。
When they heard the news, the venue became unsettled.
彼(かれ)の一言(ひとこと)で、家族(かぞく)の関係(かんけい)が波立(なみだ)ち始(はじ)めた。
His single remark started to disturb the family's relationships.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). Compound of 波(なみ) (wave) + 立(た)つ (to rise, to stand).

ASPECT:
The ている form often describes the resulting state ('is rippling, is choppy') rather than a one-time action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水面(すいめん)が波立(なみだ)つ: the water's surface ripples
  • 海(うみ)が波立(なみだ)つ: the sea becomes choppy
  • 心(こころ)が波立(なみだ)つ: one's heart is troubled
  • 波立(なみだ)たない湖(みずうみ): a calm, unrippled lake

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 荒(あ)れる: to be rough (sea); to become wild — stronger; implies storm-level disturbance
  • ざわめく: to stir; to murmur — used for crowds or a rustling atmosphere
