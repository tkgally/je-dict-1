涼(すず)しげ

すずしげ
adjective-na
cool-looking; refreshing in appearance
1. looking cool; appearing refreshing
Describes something that gives an impression of coolness or refreshing breeziness to the eye, such as summer clothing, glassware, or wind chimes. The げ suffix means 'looking/seeming', so it conveys appearance rather than actual temperature.
涼(すず)しげな音(おと)がする。
It makes a sound that feels cool and refreshing.
彼女(かのじょ)は涼(すず)しげな浴衣(ゆかた)を着(き)ていた。
She was wearing a cool-looking yukata.
窓辺(まどべ)の風鈴(ふうりん)が涼(すず)しげな音色(ねいろ)を響(ひび)かせている。
The wind chime by the window is giving off a refreshing, cool tone.

Formed from 涼(すず)しい (cool) + the suffix げ ('appearing, seeming'). It describes the impression of coolness given to a viewer or listener, not the actual temperature.

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 涼(すず)しげではない
  • Te-form: 涼(すず)しげで
  • Past: 涼(すず)しげだった

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 涼(すず)しげな音(おと): a cool, refreshing sound
  • 涼(すず)しげな装(よそお)い: a cool-looking outfit
  • 涼(すず)しげな目元(めもと): cool, clear-looking eyes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 涼(すず)しい: cool — states actual coolness; 涼(すず)しげ is only about appearance.
