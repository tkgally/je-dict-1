大発見(だいはっけん)

だいはっけん
noun
great discovery; major find; breakthrough
1. great discovery; major find; breakthrough
A major or important discovery. The intensifying prefix 大(だい) emphasizes its significance; also used lightly in conversation for any exciting find.
それは大発見(だいはっけん)だ。
That's a major discovery!
科学者(かがくしゃ)たちが大発見(だいはっけん)をした。
The scientists made a great discovery.
この遺跡(いせき)の発掘(はっくつ)は歴史的(れきしてき)な大発見(だいはっけん)だった。
Excavating this site was a historic discovery.

Composed of the intensifying prefix 大(だい) (big, great) + 発見(はっけん) (discovery). It refers to a major or important discovery—scientific, historical, or, often playfully, an everyday revelation.

USAGE:

  • 歴史的(れきしてき)な大発見(だいはっけん): a historic discovery
  • Also used lightly in conversation for any exciting personal find.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 発見(はっけん): discovery — the base word
  • 発明(はつめい): invention — creating something new, as opposed to finding it
