1. self-denial; rejection of oneself; self-negation
The act or state of denying one's own value, abilities, identity, or past. Used in psychological, philosophical, and everyday contexts, often with a negative connotation of low self-esteem.
彼(かれ)は自己否定(じこひてい)が強(つよ)い。
He has a strong tendency toward self-denial.
過度(かど)な自己否定(じこひてい)は心(こころ)の健康(けんこう)に悪(わる)い。
Excessive self-denial is bad for mental health.
失敗(しっぱい)を反省(はんせい)するのは大切(たいせつ)だが、自己否定(じこひてい)に陥(おちい)る必要(ひつよう)はない。
It's important to reflect on failures, but there's no need to fall into self-denial.

Combines 自己(じこ) (self) + 否定(ひてい) (negation). Frequently appears in psychology, self-help, and personal-development contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己否定(じこひてい)に陥(おちい)る: to fall into self-denial
  • 自己否定(じこひてい)が強(つよ)い: to have strong self-denial
  • 自己否定(じこひてい)感(かん): feeling of self-rejection

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自己肯定(じここうてい): self-affirmation — the antonym, often paired in self-help contexts
  • 自己嫌悪(じこけんお): self-loathing — focuses on negative emotion toward oneself
  • 劣等感(れっとうかん): inferiority complex — feeling inferior to others
