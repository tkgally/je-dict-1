レプリカ

れぷりか
noun
replica; reproduction
1. replica; reproduction; copy
A faithful copy of an original object, especially of artwork, historical artifacts, sports jerseys, or trophies.
有名(ゆうめい)な彫刻(ちょうこく)のレプリカ。
A replica of a famous sculpture.
博物館(はくぶつかん)で展示(てんじ)されているのはレプリカだ。
What is displayed at the museum is a replica.
息子(むすこ)の誕生日(たんじょうび)にお気(き)に入(い)りのチームのレプリカユニフォームを買(か)ってあげた。
For my son's birthday I bought him a replica jersey of his favorite team.

Loanword from English 'replica'. Often implies an officially produced or high-quality copy, not a forgery or knockoff.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レプリカユニフォーム: replica jersey (fan version of a team uniform)
  • レプリカを作(つく)る: to make a replica
  • 精巧(せいこう)なレプリカ: a finely crafted replica

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 複製(ふくせい): reproduction; duplicate — the native Japanese term; broader in scope
  • 模型(もけい): model; scale model — typically a smaller-scale representation
  • 偽物(にせもの): fake; counterfeit — implies deception, unlike レプリカ
general
