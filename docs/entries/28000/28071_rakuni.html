楽(らく)に

らくに
adverb
easily; comfortably; with ease
1. easily; comfortably; with ease
The adverbial form of the na-adjective 楽(らく) (ease, comfort). Describes doing something without difficulty or strain, or being in a relaxed, comfortable state.
どうぞ楽(らく)にしてください。
Please make yourself comfortable.
この問題(もんだい)なら楽(らく)に解(と)ける。
I can solve this problem easily.
締(し)め切(き)りまでまだ時間(じかん)があるので、楽(らく)に間(ま)に合(あ)いそうだ。
There's still time before the deadline, so it looks like I'll make it easily.

Adverbial form of the na-adjective 楽(らく) (easy, comfortable). It covers two related senses: doing something without effort, and being physically or mentally relaxed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 楽(らく)に勝(か)つ: to win easily
  • 楽(らく)に息(いき)をする: to breathe easily
  • 楽(らく)にする: to relax, to make oneself comfortable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 簡単(かんたん)に: simply, easily — focuses on a task being uncomplicated
  • 気楽(きらく)に: in a relaxed way — emphasizes a carefree state of mind
