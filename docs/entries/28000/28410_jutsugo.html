術語(じゅつご)

じゅつご
noun
technical term; terminology
1. technical term; specialist terminology
A word used with a precise, specialized meaning within an academic discipline, profession, or art form. Often opaque to non-specialists.
専門(せんもん)の術語(じゅつご)を覚(おぼ)える。
Memorize the specialist terms of one's field.
この論文(ろんぶん)は術語(じゅつご)が多(おお)くて読(よ)みにくい。
This paper is hard to read because it contains many technical terms.
専門家以外(せんもんかいがい)にも分(わ)かるように、術語(じゅつご)を簡単(かんたん)に言(い)い換(か)える必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to paraphrase the technical terms in simple words so non-specialists can understand.

Compound of 術(じゅつ) (art, technique) + 語(ご) (word).

REGISTER:
Formal and somewhat academic. In everyday speech, 専門用語(せんもんようご) is more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 医学(いがく)の術語(じゅつご): medical terminology
  • 術語(じゅつご)を使(つか)う: use technical terms
  • 術語(じゅつご)を定義(ていぎ)する: define a technical term

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 専門用語(せんもんようご): technical term — everyday equivalent; broader and more common
  • 用語(ようご): term — general word for a name or label used in a field
  • 学術用語(がくじゅつようご): academic term — specifically scholarly vocabulary
