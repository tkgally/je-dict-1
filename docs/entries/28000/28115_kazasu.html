1. to hold up; to hold out over something
To hold something up or out, especially over or toward something — over a fire for warmth, over one's eyes to shield them, or over a card reader. Now commonly used for tapping an IC card to a reader.
手(て)を火(ひ)にかざす。
I hold my hands over the fire.
ICカードをかざして改札(かいさつ)を通(とお)る。
I tap my IC card and go through the ticket gate.
日差(ひざ)しが強(つよ)いので、手(て)を額(ひたい)にかざして遠(とお)くを見(み)た。
The sun was strong, so I held my hand to my forehead and looked into the distance.

かざす is a transitive verb meaning to hold something up or out, typically over or toward something. The thing held is marked with を and the target with に.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手(て)をかざす: to hold up one's hand (over a fire, eyes, etc.)
  • カードをかざす: to tap/hold up a card (to a reader)
  • 傘(かさ)をかざす: to hold an umbrella up over someone

USAGE:
With IC cards and smartphones, かざす is the standard verb for the touch-and-go gesture at ticket gates and payment terminals.
