授受

じゅじゅ
noun
giving and receiving; transfer; exchange
1. giving and receiving; transfer; exchange
The handing over of something from one party to another — money, goods, documents, or information. A formal word, common in legal, business, and grammar contexts. Takes する.
金銭(きんせん)の授受(じゅじゅ)があった。
There was an exchange of money.
書類(しょるい)の授受(じゅじゅ)は受付(うけつけ)で行(おこな)ってください。
Please hand over and receive documents at the reception desk.
違法(いほう)な資金(しきん)の授受(じゅじゅ)が行(おこな)われていた疑(うたが)いがある。
There is suspicion that an illegal transfer of funds was taking place.

Composed of 授(じゅ) (to give, confer) + 受(じゅ) (to receive). Refers to a two-way handover. A formal, written-register word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 金銭(きんせん)の授受(じゅじゅ): the exchange of money
  • 書類(しょるい)の授受(じゅじゅ): the handover of documents
  • 授受(じゅじゅ)動詞(どうし): giving-and-receiving verbs (a grammar term for あげる・くれる・もらう)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • やり取(と)り: exchange — far more casual and everyday
  • 受(う)け渡(わた)し: delivery / handover — emphasizes the physical passing of goods
