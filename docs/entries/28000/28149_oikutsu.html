1. how old?; how many? (polite)
The polite form of いくつ, made with the honorific お-. Used to ask someone's age, or sometimes a quantity, courteously.
失礼(しつれい)ですが、おいくつですか。
Excuse me, but how old are you?
お子(こ)さんはおいくつですか。
How old is your child?
おいくつのときに日本(にほん)へ来(こ)られたのですか。
At what age did you come to Japan?

The polite form of いくつ (how many / how old), made by adding the honorific お-. It is most often used to ask someone's age courteously.

USAGE:

  • Because asking age directly can be sensitive, おいくつ softens the question.
  • It can also politely ask a quantity, though this is less common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いくつ: how many / how old — the plain form
  • 何歳(なんさい): how old — direct and neutral, common with children or in casual speech
