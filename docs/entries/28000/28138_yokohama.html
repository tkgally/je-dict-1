横浜(よこはま)

よこはま
noun
Yokohama (city)
1. Yokohama (city)
A major port city in Kanagawa Prefecture just south of Tokyo, and Japan's second-largest city by population.
横浜(よこはま)に住(す)んでいます。
I live in Yokohama.
横浜(よこはま)の中華街(ちゅうかがい)は有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Yokohama's Chinatown is famous.
週末(しゅうまつ)に横浜(よこはま)の港(みなと)を散歩(さんぽ)した。
I strolled around Yokohama's harbor over the weekend.

A large port city in 神奈川(かながわ)県(けん), just south of Tokyo. With over three million residents, it is Japan's second-largest city by population after the Tokyo wards.

CONTEXT:
横浜(よこはま) opened as one of Japan's first international ports in 1859 and developed a strong foreign influence.

FAMOUS FOR:

  • 中華街(ちゅうかがい): Chinatown — the largest in Japan
  • みなとみらい: Minato Mirai, a modern waterfront district
  • 港(みなと): the harbor / port
