〜編(へん)

へん

volume; section; part (suffix)
1. volume; section; part
Suffix attaching to a noun to mark a part or installment of a book, course, video series, or themed collection.
基礎(きそ)編(へん)を先(さき)に読(よ)んだ。
I read the basics volume first.
応用(おうよう)編(へん)は来月(らいげつ)出版(しゅっぱん)される。
The advanced volume will be published next month.
文法(ぶんぽう)編(へん)が終(お)わったら、次(つぎ)は語彙(ごい)編(へん)に進(すす)みましょう。

Names a part, installment, or themed chapter of a larger work. Often paired in series (前(ぜん)編(へん) / 後(こう)編(へん), 基礎(きそ)編(へん) / 応用(おうよう)編(へん)).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 基礎(きそ)編(へん): basics volume
  • 応用(おうよう)編(へん): advanced/applied volume
  • 前(ぜん)編(へん): first part / earlier volume
  • 後(こう)編(へん): latter part / sequel volume
  • 完(かん)結(けつ)編(へん): final installment

USAGE:
Common in textbooks, anime/film series, manga arcs, and TV documentaries. Also written as a kanji that means 'to edit, compile', reflecting the sense of an editorial unit of a larger work.
