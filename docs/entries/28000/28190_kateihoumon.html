1. home visit (by a teacher to a pupil's family)
A Japanese school custom in which a teacher visits each pupil's home, usually early in the school year, to meet the family and learn about the home environment.
来週(らいしゅう)家庭訪問(かていほうもん)がある。
There will be a home visit next week.
先生(せんせい)が家庭訪問(かていほうもん)で家(いえ)に来(き)た。
The teacher came to our house for a home visit.
母(はは)は家庭訪問(かていほうもん)の前(まえ)に、急(いそ)いで部屋(へや)を片付(かたづ)けた。
My mother hurriedly tidied up the rooms before the home visit.

Compound of 家庭(かてい) (household) + 訪問(ほうもん) (visit). A familiar event in Japanese primary and junior-high schooling, typically held in spring. Takes する as a verb (家庭訪問(かていほうもん)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家庭訪問(かていほうもん)がある: there is a home visit
  • 家庭訪問(かていほうもん)の日(ひ): the day of the home visit
  • 担任(たんにん)の家庭訪問(かていほうもん): home visit by the homeroom teacher

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 面談(めんだん): interview; one-on-one meeting — teacher and parent meet, but usually at the school
  • 訪問(ほうもん): a visit — the general word, with no school connotation
