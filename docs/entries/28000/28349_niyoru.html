による

による
expression
due to; by means of; according to
1. due to; caused by; resulting from
Attaches to a noun to indicate the cause or origin of an event. The cause is presented as the agent or reason for the result that follows.
地震(じしん)による被害(ひがい)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Damage from the earthquake is spreading.
不注意(ふちゅうい)による事故(じこ)が多発(たはつ)している。
Accidents caused by carelessness are happening frequently.
2. by; by means of; through (a method or means)
Indicates the means, method, or instrument by which an action is performed.
オンラインによる授業(じゅぎょう)が始(はじ)まった。
Classes conducted online have begun.
投票(とうひょう)による選出(せんしゅつ)が基本(きほん)だ。
Selection by voting is the basic procedure.
3. according to; as reported by
Attributes information to a source. Typically used in news writing or formal reports.
気象庁(きしょうちょう)によると、明日(あした)は大雨(おおあめ)になるそうだ。
According to the Meteorological Agency, tomorrow will be heavy rain.
警察(けいさつ)の発表(はっぴょう)によれば、犯人(はんにん)はまだ捕(つか)まっていない。
According to the police announcement, the suspect has not yet been caught.

Plain present form of the verb 依(よ)る/因(よ)る (to depend on; to result from), used as a grammatical expression after a noun + に. The sense 3 form often appears as によると or によれば (according to).

FORM VARIATIONS:

  • Noun + による + Noun: noun-modifying form (地震(じしん)による被害(ひがい))
  • Noun + によって: te-form, joins to a verb clause (地震(じしん)によって家(いえ)が倒(たお)れた)
  • Noun + によると / によれば: according to ~ (sense 3)
  • Noun + により: formal/written equivalent of によって

USAGE NOTES:

  • Sense 1 (cause): the noun is the cause; the result follows.
  • Sense 2 (means): the noun is the method or instrument.
  • Sense 3 (source): used with verbs of reporting (〜そうだ, 〜らしい) for hearsay.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • で: by; with — broader; everyday particle
  • から: from — origin or cause in casual speech
  • 〜次第(しだい): depending on — for variability (場合(ばあい)次第(しだい))
