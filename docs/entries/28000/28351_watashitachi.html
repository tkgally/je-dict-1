私(わたし)たち

わたしたち
pronoun
we; us
1. we; us; our group
Standard first-person plural pronoun. Polite enough for most situations: classroom, workplace, and public-facing speech. Inclusive when the listener is part of the group; otherwise refers to the speaker and others.
私(わたし)たちは学生(がくせい)です。
We are students.
日本(にほん)の文化(ぶんか)は私(わたし)たちの誇(ほこ)りだ。
Japanese culture is our pride.
今度(こんど)の週末(しゅうまつ)、私(わたし)たちは京都(きょうと)に旅行(りょこう)に行(い)く予定(よてい)です。
We're planning to travel to Kyoto this coming weekend.

Standard plural of 私(わたし) (I). Politeness-neutral and safe in nearly any context. Choice of first-person plural depends on speaker register and group composition.

FIRST-PERSON PLURALS:

  • 私(わたし)たち: we (standard, neutral)
  • 私(わたくし)たち: we (very formal)
  • 僕(ぼく)たち / 僕(ぼく)ら: we (masculine, casual to neutral)
  • 俺(おれ)たち / 俺(おれ)ら: we (masculine, casual/rough)
  • うちら: we (feminine, casual)
  • 我々(われわれ): we (formal speeches, in-group identity)

USAGE NOTES:

  • INCLUSIVE: includes the listener when context allows (私(わたし)たちで決(き)めよう Let's decide together).
  • EXCLUSIVE: excludes the listener in most narrative contexts (私(わたし)たちは昨日(きのう)映画(えいが)を見(み)に行(い)きました We went to a movie yesterday).

GRAMMAR:
The possessive is 私(わたし)たちの (our). たち can attach to other personal nouns to mean 'and others' (先生(せんせい)たち the teachers).
