1. to discuss; to argue; to debate
To take up a topic and present reasoning, opinions, or analysis about it. The formal, classical-flavored variant of 論(ろん)じる; common in academic and editorial writing.
問題(もんだい)の本質(ほんしつ)を論(ろん)ずる。
Discuss the essence of the problem.
この論文(ろんぶん)では環境(かんきょう)と経済(けいざい)の関係(かんけい)を論(ろん)ずる。
In this paper I discuss the relationship between the environment and the economy.
結果(けっか)を急(いそ)いで論(ろん)ずるのは危険(きけん)だが、傾向(けいこう)についてはすでに議論(ぎろん)が始(はじ)まっている。
It is dangerous to debate the result hastily, but discussion of the trend has already begun.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The topic discussed is marked with を.

VARIANTS:
論(ろん)ずる is a zuru-verb (a classical sub-class of ichidan verbs). The everyday equivalent is 論(ろん)じる. Both are interchangeable in meaning; 論(ろん)ずる sounds more formal or literary and appears more in academic prose and titles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問題(もんだい)を論(ろん)ずる: discuss a problem
  • 本質(ほんしつ)を論(ろん)ずる: discuss the essence (of something)
  • 是非(ぜひ)を論(ろん)ずる: debate the merits and demerits
  • 一概(いちがい)に論(ろん)ぜられない: cannot be argued in a single, sweeping way

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 論(ろん)じる: same meaning — neutral, everyday register
  • 議論(ぎろん)する: to debate — implies an exchange between people
  • 述(の)べる: to state — broader; just expressing, not necessarily arguing
