反省会(はんせいかい)

はんせいかい
noun
review meeting; debriefing
1. review meeting; debriefing; post-mortem
A gathering held after an event or project to look back on what happened, discuss what went wrong, and draw lessons for next time.
試合(しあい)の後(あと)で反省会(はんせいかい)を開(ひら)いた。
We held a review meeting after the match.
イベントの反省会(はんせいかい)で改善点(かいぜんてん)を話(はな)し合(あ)った。
At the event debriefing, we discussed points to improve.
プロジェクトが終(お)わると、チームで反省会(はんせいかい)を開(ひら)いて次(つぎ)に生(い)かす課題(かだい)を整理(せいり)した。
When the project ended, the team held a review meeting and organized the issues to apply next time.

Compound of 反省(はんせい) (reflection, self-examination) + 会(かい) (meeting). Despite the word's literal sense, a 反省会(はんせいかい) can be lighthearted — among friends it sometimes just means gathering for drinks to rehash an event.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 反省会(はんせいかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold a review meeting
  • 反省会(はんせいかい)を行(おこな)う: to conduct a debriefing
  • 一人(ひとり)反省会(はんせいかい): privately replaying one's own mistakes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 打(う)ち上(あ)げ: celebration party (after an event) — festive, to mark completion rather than to reflect
  • 会議(かいぎ): meeting — the general word, with no reflective purpose implied
