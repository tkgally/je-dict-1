安定所(あんていじょ)

あんていじょ
noun
employment office; job placement office
1. employment office; job placement office
Short for 職業安定所(しょくぎょうあんていじょ), a public employment office where job seekers register, receive counseling, and apply for unemployment benefits. Commonly known as ハローワーク.
安定所(あんていじょ)に行(い)く。
I'm going to the employment office.
父(ちち)は安定所(あんていじょ)で仕事(しごと)を紹介(しょうかい)してもらった。
My father got a job referral at the employment office.
失業(しつぎょう)保険(ほけん)の手続(てつづ)きをするために安定所(あんていじょ)を訪(たず)ねた。
I visited the employment office to file for unemployment insurance.

Abbreviation of 職業安定所(しょくぎょうあんていじょ). In everyday speech, the English-derived name ハローワーク is more common; 安定所(あんていじょ) appears in formal documents and conversation among older speakers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安定所(あんていじょ)に通(かよ)う: to make regular visits to the employment office
  • 安定所(あんていじょ)で相談(そうだん)する: to consult at the employment office
  • 公共(こうきょう)職業安定所(しょくぎょうあんていじょ): public employment security office (full official name)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ハローワーク: the common, branded name for the same offices
  • 人材(じんざい)紹介(しょうかい)会社(がいしゃ): private staffing agency — fee-based, often for white-collar jobs
