食事券

しょくじけん
noun
meal voucher; meal ticket
1. meal voucher; meal ticket
A voucher or coupon exchangeable for a meal. Often given as a perk, prize, or welfare benefit, or included with hotel and event packages.
食事券(しょくじけん)をもらった。
I got a meal voucher.
このプランには朝食(ちょうしょく)の食事券(しょくじけん)が付(つ)いている。
This plan comes with a breakfast meal voucher.
商店街(しょうてんがい)の福引(ふくび)きで食事券(しょくじけん)が当(あ)たった。
I won a meal voucher in the shopping district's raffle.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食事券(しょくじけん)を使(つか)う: to use a meal voucher
  • 食事券(しょくじけん)付(つ)き: comes with a meal voucher
  • 食事券(しょくじけん)が当(あ)たる: to win a meal voucher

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 商品券(しょうひんけん): gift certificate — usable for goods at a store, broader than a meal
  • クーポン: coupon — typically a discount rather than a prepaid value
