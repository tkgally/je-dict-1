美肌効果

びはだこうか
noun
skin-beautifying effect; benefits for the skin
1. skin-beautifying effect; benefits for the skin
The effect of making skin smoother, clearer, or more attractive. A common marketing phrase for cosmetics, foods, and treatments.
このクリームには美肌効果(びはだこうか)がある。
This cream has a skin-beautifying effect.
美肌効果(びはだこうか)が期待(きたい)できる食(た)べ物(もの)。
Foods that can be expected to benefit the skin.
温泉(おんせん)には美肌効果(びはだこうか)があると言(い)われ、女性(じょせい)に人気(にんき)だ。
Hot springs are said to have a skin-beautifying effect and are popular with women.

Compound of 美肌(びはだ) (beautiful skin) and 効果(こうか) (effect). A frequent advertising term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美肌効果(びはだこうか)がある: to have a skin-beautifying effect
  • 美肌効果(びはだこうか)が高(たか)い: to be highly effective for the skin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 美容効果(びようこうか): beauty effect — broader, covering hair, body, and overall appearance
