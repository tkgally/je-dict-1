名場面(めいばめん)

めいばめん
noun
famous scene; memorable scene
1. famous scene; memorable scene; highlight
A celebrated, unforgettable scene in a film, drama, sports match, or other performance that audiences remember and replay.
この映画(えいが)には名場面(めいばめん)が多(おお)い。
This movie has many famous scenes.
あの試合(しあい)の逆転(ぎゃくてん)は名場面(めいばめん)として語(かた)り継(つ)がれている。
The comeback in that match is passed down as a memorable scene.
番組(ばんぐみ)の最後(さいご)に、ドラマの名場面(めいばめん)を集(あつ)めた特集(とくしゅう)が放送(ほうそう)された。
At the end of the program, a special collecting the drama's famous scenes was broadcast.

Compound of 名(めい) (famous, excellent) + 場面(ばめん) (scene). The prefix 名(めい) marks something as outstanding, as in 名(めい)作(さく) (masterpiece) or 名(めい)言(げん) (famous saying).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名場面(めいばめん)集(しゅう): collection of famous scenes
  • 数々(かずかず)の名場面(めいばめん): numerous memorable scenes
  • 名場面(めいばめん)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to look back on the highlights

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)せ場(ば): highlight; showpiece moment — the part meant to impress, from the performer's side
  • ハイライト: highlights — borrowed word, often for sports or recap footage
