1. writing; literary composition; (work of) authorship
The act of writing books or scholarly works, or the written work itself. Formal; primarily used for books, academic writing, and the profession of writing.
父(ちち)は著述(ちょじゅつ)を仕事(しごと)にしている。
My father makes a living by writing.
彼(かれ)の著述(ちょじゅつ)は広(ひろ)く読(よ)まれている。
His writings are widely read.
長年(ながねん)の研究(けんきゅう)を元(もと)に、著述(ちょじゅつ)活動(かつどう)を本格的(ほんかくてき)に始(はじ)めた。
Drawing on many years of research, he began writing in earnest.

Compound of 著(ちょ) (to write, to publish) + 述(じゅつ) (to state). Used as a する verb in 著述(ちょじゅつ)する (to write [a work]).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 著述(ちょじゅつ)業(ぎょう): the writing profession; author by trade
  • 著述(ちょじゅつ)家(か): author; writer (of books)
  • 著述(ちょじゅつ)活動(かつどう): writing activity
  • 著述(ちょじゅつ)を重(かさ)ねる: build up a body of writing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 執筆(しっぴつ): writing (the act) — focuses on the act of writing, often for a specific piece
  • 著作(ちょさく): written work — emphasizes the work as a product; basis of 著作権(ちょさくけん) (copyright)
  • 作品(さくひん): work — general term for any creative work, not specific to writing
