1. to get separated from; to lose sight of (one's companions)
To become separated from a group or companion one was with, typically by accident in a crowd or while moving. The person or animal one loses is marked with と or から.
子供(こども)が親(おや)とはぐれた。
The child got separated from its parents.
人混(ひとご)みで友達(ともだち)とはぐれてしまった。
I lost sight of my friend in the crowd.
登山中(とざんちゅう)にグループからはぐれて、一人(ひとり)で下山(げざん)した。
I got separated from the group while climbing and came down the mountain alone.

Usually written in kana. Often appears in the resultative はぐれている (have become separated, am lost).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)); has no transitive pair.
  • Pattern: AがBと/からはぐれる (A gets separated from B)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 親(おや)とはぐれる: get separated from one's parents
  • 群(む)れからはぐれる: stray from the herd/flock
  • はぐれた子犬(こいぬ): a stray puppy that lost its owner

Related Words

Related: 迷子(まいご) (a person who is lost)
