1. to get excited; to be worked up; to be on edge
Of emotions or nerves, to become heightened or aroused, often in a way that is hard to calm. Also written 昂(たか)ぶる.
気持(きも)ちが高(たか)ぶる。
To feel excited and worked up.
神経(しんけい)が高(たか)ぶって眠(ねむ)れない。
My nerves are on edge and I can't sleep.
大事(だいじ)な発表(はっぴょう)を前(まえ)に、気持(きも)ちが高(たか)ぶって落(お)ち着(つ)かなかった。
With an important presentation ahead, I was worked up and couldn't settle down.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive godan verb. Xが高(たか)ぶる.

Describes emotions or nerves becoming heightened, often excitement or agitation. Frequently in the て-form (高(たか)ぶって).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気持(きも)ちが高(たか)ぶる: to feel worked up
  • 神経(しんけい)が高(たか)ぶる: to be on edge; nerves heightened
  • 感情(かんじょう)が高(たか)ぶる: emotions run high

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 興奮(こうふん)する: to get excited — more general and neutral
  • 高揚(こうよう)する: to be elated, uplifted — positive, elevated mood
