畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)

ちくさんぎょう
noun
livestock industry; animal husbandry
1. livestock industry; animal husbandry (as an industry)
The industry of raising domestic animals such as cattle, pigs, and chickens for meat, milk, eggs, and other products. One branch of agriculture.
この地域(ちいき)は畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)が盛(さか)んだ。
The livestock industry is thriving in this region.
飼料(しりょう)の値上(ねあ)がりが畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)を圧迫(あっぱく)している。
Rising feed prices are squeezing the livestock industry.
家族(かぞく)は代々(だいだい)畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)を営(いとな)み、乳牛(にゅうぎゅう)を育(そだ)てている。
My family has run a livestock business for generations, raising dairy cows.

Composed of 畜産(ちくさん) (livestock raising) + 業(ぎょう) (industry, business).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)が盛(さか)んだ: the livestock industry is thriving
  • 畜産業(ちくさんぎょう)を営(いとな)む: to run a livestock business

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 農業(のうぎょう): agriculture — broader; growing crops as well as raising animals
  • 酪農(らくのう): dairy farming — a specific branch focused on milk-producing animals
