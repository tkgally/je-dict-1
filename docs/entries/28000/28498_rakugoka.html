落語家(らくごか)

らくごか
noun
rakugo performer; rakugo storyteller
1. rakugo performer; rakugo storyteller
A professional performer of 落語(らくご), the traditional Japanese art of comic storytelling. The performer kneels alone on stage in a kimono, narrates a long humorous story, and plays all characters by changing voice and direction of gaze.
有名(ゆうめい)な落語家(らくごか)が出演(しゅつえん)した。
A famous rakugo performer appeared.
彼(かれ)は落語家(らくごか)になりたくて弟子(でし)入(い)りした。
He wanted to become a rakugo performer, so he became an apprentice.
落語家(らくごか)は扇子(せんす)と手拭(てぬぐ)いだけで多(おお)くの登場人物(とうじょうじんぶつ)を演(えん)じ分(わ)ける。
Rakugo performers play many different characters using only a fan and a hand towel.

Compound of 落語(らくご) (rakugo) + 家(か) (practitioner, professional). 落語家(らくごか) follow a strict ranking system: 見習(みなら)い → 前座(ぜんざ) → 二(に)つ目(め) → 真打(しんうち).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落語家(らくごか)になる: to become a rakugo performer
  • 人気(にんき)落語家(らくごか): popular rakugo performer
  • 落語家(らくごか)に弟子入(でしい)りする: to apprentice oneself to a rakugo performer

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
A rakugo performer's stage name is given by their master and changes as they rise through the ranks. The two main professional associations are 落語協会(らくごきょうかい) and 落語芸術協会(らくごげいじゅつきょうかい) in Tokyo.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 芸人(げいにん): entertainer; comedian — broader category that includes modern stand-up
  • 噺家(はなしか): storyteller — an older, more traditional term for the same profession
