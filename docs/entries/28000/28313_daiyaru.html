1. dial; rotary control
A circular control that is rotated to make a selection — historically on telephones, and still on radios, safes, ovens, and other equipment. Also used as a verb (suru) for dialing a phone number.
ダイヤルを回(まわ)した。
I turned the dial.
番号(ばんごう)をダイヤルしてください。
Please dial the number.
昔(むかし)の電話(でんわ)はダイヤルを回(まわ)して電話(でんわ)をかけた。
Old telephones were used by turning the dial to make calls.

From English "dial." Can be used as a noun for the physical control or with する as a verb meaning to dial a number.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ダイヤルを回(まわ)す: to turn the dial
  • ダイヤルする: to dial (a number)
  • ダイヤル式(しき)電話(でんわ): rotary phone
  • 音量(おんりょう)のダイヤル: volume dial

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ボタン: button — press, not rotate
  • つまみ: knob — native term, often used for radio/oven controls
