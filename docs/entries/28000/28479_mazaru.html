1. to mingle; to join in; to be mixed in
Intransitive verb meaning one element joins or blends into a group. Uses 交(まじ)わる-related kanji to emphasize joining people or distinguishable elements, rather than the chemical-style blending of 混(ま)ざる.
大人(おとな)に交(ま)ざって遊(あそ)んだ。
I played mingled in with the adults.
外国(がいこく)人(じん)の中(なか)に日本(にほん)人(じん)が一人(ひとり)交(ま)ざっていた。
There was one Japanese person mixed in among the foreigners.
子供(こども)たちの歓声(かんせい)に、先生(せんせい)の笑(わら)い声(ごえ)も交(ま)ざって聞(き)こえた。
The teacher's laughter could be heard mixed in with the children's cheers.

Kanji variant of 混(ま)ざる. The 交(まじ)わる kanji ('to cross, to associate') is preferred when one thing joins a group of similar distinguishable things — especially people mingling — rather than substances blending uniformly.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: intransitive
  • Pair: 交(ま)ぜる (transitive — to mix in)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AがBに交(ま)ざる: A mingles with / is mixed into B

WHEN TO USE WHICH KANJI:

  • 混(ま)ざる: substances blending homogeneously (sugar in water)
  • 交(ま)ざる: distinguishable items or people joining a group

The two kanji forms are often interchangeable, and 混(ま)ざる is the more common spelling overall.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 混(ま)じる ／ 交(ま)じる: parallel form emphasizing the presence of something within a group; slightly more literary
