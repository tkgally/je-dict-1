コント

こんと
noun
(comedy) sketch; skit
1. (comedy) sketch; skit
A short comic skit performed by one or more comedians in character, typically with a clear setting (school, office, restaurant) and a punchline. Distinguished from 漫才(まんざい), which is performed by stand-up duos as themselves.
そのコントは面白(おもしろ)かった。
That sketch was funny.
彼(かれ)らはコントが得意(とくい)な芸人(げいにん)だ。
They are comedians who specialize in sketch comedy.
番組(ばんぐみ)のために新(あたら)しいコントの台本(だいほん)を書(か)いている。
I'm writing scripts for new comedy sketches for the program.

From French {conte} (short tale). On Japanese TV, comedy duos and groups regularly perform brief scripted コント of one to several minutes, often distinguishable by simple costumes and a single set.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コントを書(か)く: to write a sketch
  • コントを演(えん)じる: to perform a sketch
  • コント番組(ばんぐみ): sketch-comedy show
  • コント師(し): sketch comedian (less common; usually just 芸人(げいにん))

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 漫才(まんざい): stand-up comedy duo — two performers as themselves, fast-paced verbal back-and-forth
  • 落語(らくご): rakugo — solo seated storytelling in kimono; much longer than コント
