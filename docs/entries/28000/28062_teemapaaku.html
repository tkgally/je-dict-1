テーマパーク

てーまぱーく
noun
theme park
1. theme park
A large amusement park built around a unifying theme, with rides, shows, and attractions.
テーマパークに行(い)く。
To go to a theme park.
新(あたら)しいテーマパークが話題(わだい)になっている。
The new theme park is the talk of the town.
その人気(にんき)テーマパークは週末(しゅうまつ)になると全国(ぜんこく)から客(きゃく)が訪(おとず)れる。
On weekends that popular theme park draws visitors from all over the country.

A loanword from English "theme park," referring to large themed parks with rides, shows, and themed areas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • テーマパークを訪(おとず)れる: to visit a theme park
  • 人気(にんき)テーマパーク: a popular theme park

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遊園地(ゆうえんち): amusement park — older and smaller, ride-focused, usually without a single theme
  • 観光地(かんこうち): tourist spot — a broader sightseeing destination
