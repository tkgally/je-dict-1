1. to speak to; to address (someone)
To speak to someone, often gently or warmly and in a way that invites a response. Also used of works of art or media that 'speak to' the audience emotionally.
子(こ)どもに優(やさ)しく語(かた)りかける。
I speak gently to the child.
彼女(かのじょ)は赤(あか)ちゃんに語(かた)りかけた。
She talked to the baby.
この映画(えいが)は、観(み)る人(ひと)の心(こころ)に静(しず)かに語(かた)りかけてくる。
This film speaks quietly to the heart of the viewer.

語(かた)りかける is a compound verb (語(かた)る + かける) meaning to speak to or address someone, usually with warmth or care. The listener is marked with に. The かける ending adds the sense of an action directed outward toward someone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 優(やさ)しく語(かた)りかける: to speak gently to
  • 心(こころ)に語(かた)りかける: to speak to the heart (move emotionally)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 話(はな)しかける: to speak to; to strike up a conversation — more neutral and everyday
  • 語(かた)る: to tell; to narrate — to recount or relate at length
