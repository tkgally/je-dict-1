異存(いぞん)ない

いぞんない
expression
no objection; no different opinion
1. to have no objection; to have no different opinion
A formal expression used to indicate agreement or non-opposition to a proposal, decision, or arrangement. Composed of 異存(いぞん) (a differing opinion or objection) + ない (none).
その案(あん)に異存(いぞん)ない。
I have no objection to that proposal.
皆(みな)さんに異存(いぞん)がなければ、これで決定(けってい)します。
If no one has any objection, we will finalize it this way.
条件(じょうけん)に異存(いぞん)はございませんが、契約書(けいやくしょ)の文言(もんごん)は再確認(さいかくにん)させてください。
I have no objection to the terms, but please let me re-check the wording of the contract.

A formal, polite way to express agreement. Common in business meetings, contract discussions, and official correspondence.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に異存(いぞん)ない: to have no objection to ~
  • 異存(いぞん)がなければ: if there is no objection
  • 異存(いぞん)はございません: humble form, "I have no objection"
  • 異存(いぞん)を唱(とな)える: to raise an objection (the opposite)

REGISTER:
More formal and written-sounding than 反対(はんたい)しない (don't oppose) or 問題(もんだい)ない (no problem). Often used by superiors or in official settings to signal consent without enthusiasm — a neutral acceptance rather than positive endorsement.
