多(おお)め

おおめ
noun
a bit more; somewhat more; on the larger side
1. a bit more; somewhat more; on the larger side
An amount or quantity that is slightly larger than usual or than what is strictly required. The suffix め attaches to adjectives or quantity words to mean 'on the X side.'
ご飯(はん)を多(おお)めに盛(も)る。
Serve a bit more rice.
塩(しお)を多(おお)めに入(い)れてください。
Please add a little more salt.
週末(しゅうまつ)は客(きゃく)が増(ふ)えるので、食材(しょくざい)を多(おお)めに仕入(しい)れておいた。
Since customers increase on weekends, I stocked up on a bit more ingredients.

Formed from 多(おお)い (many, much) + the suffix め (tendency toward, slightly on the X side). Most often used adverbially as 多(おお)めに.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多(おお)めに入(い)れる: put in a bit more
  • 多(おお)めに作(つく)る: make a bit more
  • 多(おお)めに見積(みつ)もる: estimate on the high side
  • 多(おお)めの量(りょう): a somewhat larger amount

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 少(すく)なめ: a bit less — the opposite, used in the same way
  • たっぷり: plenty; a lot — emphasizes generous quantity, stronger than 多(おお)め
  • 大(おお)きめ: a bit larger (in size) — uses め with size rather than quantity
