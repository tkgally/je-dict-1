1. to crack; to become cracked; to split
For a surface or material to develop visible cracks, especially from dryness, age, or stress. Common for skin, walls, ceramics, and dried earth.
壁(かべ)がひび割(わ)れている。
The wall is cracked.
冬(ふゆ)になると唇(くちびる)がひび割(わ)れる。
When winter comes, my lips get cracked.
長年(ながねん)雨(あめ)が降(ふ)らず、田(た)んぼの土(つち)がひび割(わ)れていた。
After years without rain, the earth in the rice fields had cracked.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). Describes the surface itself developing cracks; the subject is marked with が. There is no widely used transitive pair — to actively crack something, use 割(わ)る.

ASPECT:
The ている form (ひび割(わ)れている) typically describes the resulting state — 'is cracked' — rather than an ongoing action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁(かべ)がひび割(わ)れる: the wall cracks
  • 唇(くちびる)がひび割(わ)れる: lips get chapped
  • 肌(はだ)がひび割(わ)れる: skin gets cracked
  • 地面(じめん)がひび割(わ)れる: the ground cracks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 割(わ)れる: to break, split — broader; for things breaking into pieces
  • 裂(さ)ける: to tear, split open — for things being torn along a line
  • ひび}が{入(はい)る: a crack forms — focuses on a single crack appearing
