〜年目(ねんめ)

ねんめ
suffix
the Nth year
1. the Nth year (since something began)
Ordinal counter attaching to a number plus 年(ねん) to mark the Nth year since something started, such as a job, a marriage, or any ongoing situation.
今年(ことし)で三年目(さんねんめ)だ。
This year is my third year.
入社(にゅうしゃ)五年目(ごねんめ)に昇進(しょうしん)した。
I was promoted in my fifth year at the company.
結婚(けっこん)十年目(じゅうねんめ)の記念(きねん)に、家族(かぞく)で旅行(りょこう)に出(で)かけた。
To mark our tenth year of marriage, our family went on a trip together.

Combines a counter (年(ねん) years) with the ordinal suffix 目(め) (Nth). The counted year is inclusive: 一年目(いちねんめ) is the first year, not the year after the first.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)三年目(さんねんめ): third year at the company
  • 結婚(けっこん)十年目(じゅうねんめ): tenth year of marriage
  • 留学(りゅうがく)二年目(にねんめ): second year of study abroad
  • 創業(そうぎょう)百年目(ひゃくねんめ): hundredth year since founding

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年間(ねんかん): a span of N years (duration) — 三年間(さんねんかん) "for three years"
  • 年(ねん)後(ご): N years later — 三年後(さんねんご) "three years from now"
