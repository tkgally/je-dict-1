旅行先(りょこうさき)

りょこうさき
noun
travel destination; place one is traveling
1. travel destination; place one travels to
The place one goes to or is staying at on a trip. The suffix 先(さき) marks the destination or place where an action occurs.
旅行先(りょこうさき)を決(き)めた。
We decided on a travel destination.
旅行先(りょこうさき)で財布(さいふ)をなくした。
I lost my wallet at my travel destination.
旅行先(りょこうさき)では、その土地(とち)でしか食(た)べられない料理(りょうり)を楽(たの)しみたい。
When traveling, I want to enjoy dishes you can only eat in that region.

Compound of 旅行(りょこう) (travel) + the suffix 先(さき) (destination, place where something happens). The same 先(さき) appears in 勤(つと)め先(さき) (place of work) and 連絡(れんらく)先(さき) (contact address).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旅行先(りょこうさき)を決(き)める: to decide on a destination
  • 旅行先(りょこうさき)で: while at one's travel destination
  • 人気(にんき)の旅行先(りょこうさき): a popular travel destination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目的地(もくてきち): destination — the specific place one is headed; more neutral and precise
  • 行(い)き先(さき): where one is going — broader, used for any outing, not only trips
