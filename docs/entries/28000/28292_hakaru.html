1. to measure (time, intervals)
To measure time, duration, or numerical intervals — typically using a clock, stopwatch, or counter.
ストップウォッチで時間(じかん)を計(はか)る。
To measure the time with a stopwatch.
試合(しあい)のタイムを計(はか)った。
I timed the match.
選手(せんしゅ)のスタートから走(はし)り終(お)わるまでの時間(じかん)を計(はか)った。
I timed how long it took the runner from the start to the finish.
2. to plan; to plot; to scheme
To devise or attempt something, often with a calculated or strategic intent. In set phrases this sense can carry either neutral or negative connotations.
解決(かいけつ)を計(はか)る。
To plan a resolution.
暗殺(あんさつ)を計(はか)ったとして逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested for plotting an assassination.
両者(りょうしゃ)の関係改善(かんけいかいぜん)を計(はか)る動(うご)きが進(すす)んでいる。
Efforts to improve relations between the two sides are moving forward.

Kanji variant of はかる. Use 計(はか)る for measuring time or intervals, or in set phrases meaning 'to plan / to scheme'. Distinguished from 測(はか)る (physical measurement of length, depth), 量(はか)る (weight, volume), and 図(はか)る (to attempt / plan an undertaking).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)を計(はか)る: to time something
  • タイムを計(はか)る: to measure a time
  • 解決(かいけつ)を計(はか)る: to seek a resolution
  • 暗殺(あんさつ)を計(はか)る: to plot an assassination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 測(はか)る: to measure length, height, depth, speed
  • 量(はか)る: to measure weight or volume
  • 図(はか)る: to attempt or plan (large undertaking)
