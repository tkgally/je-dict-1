〜ぶり

ぶり
suffix
for the first time in (a span of time); manner of doing
1. for the first time in; after an interval of
Suffix attached to a time expression to say that something has happened for the first time after that span. Highlights the gap between the previous occurrence and the present.
三年(さんねん)ぶりに会(あ)った。
We met for the first time in three years.
一週間(いっしゅうかん)ぶりの晴(は)れだ。
It's the first sunny day in a week.
半年(はんとし)ぶりに実家(じっか)に帰(かえ)って、家族(かぞく)みんなで食事(しょくじ)をした。
I went home for the first time in half a year, and the whole family had a meal together.
2. manner of doing; style; the way one does something
Suffix attached to a noun or a verb stem to name the observable manner in which an action is performed — how someone works, speaks, or lives. The resulting word is a noun and usually invites an evaluation of that manner.
彼(かれ)の仕事(しごと)ぶりは立派(りっぱ)だ。
The way he works is admirable.
落(お)ち着(つ)いた話(はな)しぶりに好感(こうかん)を持(も)った。
I warmed to his calm way of speaking.
番組(ばんぐみ)では現地(げんち)の人(ひと)たちの暮(く)らしぶりが細(こま)かく紹介(しょうかい)されていた。
The program gave a detailed look at how the local people live.

SENSE 1 — Attaches directly to a duration (三年(さんねん)ぶり, 一週間(いっしゅうかん)ぶり). The phrase 〜ぶりに modifies a verb; 〜ぶりの modifies a following noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 久(ひさ)しぶり: long time no see (idiomatic; lit. "for the first time in a long while")
  • 三年(さんねん)ぶりに会(あ)う: meet for the first time in three years
  • 一週間(いっしゅうかん)ぶりの晴(は)れ: the first sunny day in a week
  • 何年(なんねん)ぶりだろう: how many years has it been?

USAGE:
Use 〜ぶり when emphasizing the gap; use 〜以来(いらい) when emphasizing the starting point (去年(きょねん)以来(いらい) since last year). Cannot be used for spans shorter than about a day in normal speech.

SENSE 2 — Attaches to a noun or a verb stem to name a manner of acting:

  • 仕事(しごと)ぶり: the way one works
  • 話(はな)しぶり: one's way of speaking
  • 暮(く)らしぶり: one's way of living
  • 変(か)わりぶり: the extent of a change

After some verb stems the suffix becomes っぷり, which sounds livelier and more colloquial: 飲(の)みっぷり (the way one drinks), 食(た)べっぷり (the way one eats), 男(おとこ)っぷり (a man's bearing).

The two senses are told apart by what precedes the suffix: a duration gives sense 1, a noun or verb stem gives sense 2.
