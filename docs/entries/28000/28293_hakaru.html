1. to weigh; to measure (weight or volume)
To measure how much something weighs or its volume, typically using a scale or measuring vessel.
体重(たいじゅう)を量(はか)る。
To weigh oneself.
砂糖(さとう)を百(ひゃく)グラム量(はか)った。
I measured out 100 grams of sugar.
宅配便(たくはいびん)に出(だ)す前(まえ)に、荷物(にもつ)の重(おも)さを量(はか)っておく。
Weigh the package before sending it by parcel delivery.
米(こめ)をカップで量(はか)って炊(た)いた。
I measured the rice with a cup and cooked it.

Kanji variant of はかる reserved for weight and volume. For length, depth, or speed use 測(はか)る; for time or set-phrase 'planning' use 計(はか)る.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体重(たいじゅう)を量(はか)る: to weigh oneself
  • 重(おも)さを量(はか)る: to measure the weight
  • 分量(ぶんりょう)を量(はか)る: to measure out a quantity

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 測(はか)る: to measure length, depth, height, speed
  • 計(はか)る: to measure time; to plan
