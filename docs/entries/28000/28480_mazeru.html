1. to mix in; to include; to let join
Transitive verb meaning to add or include something within a larger group, while keeping it distinguishable. Uses the 交(まじ)わる kanji to emphasize joining or including, especially for people or visibly distinct items.
私(わたし)も交(ま)ぜてください。
Please let me join in too.
冗談(じょうだん)を交(ま)ぜて話(はな)した。
I spoke with jokes mixed in.
英語(えいご)を少(すこ)し交(ま)ぜながら話(はな)すと、子供(こども)たちが興味(きょうみ)を示(しめ)した。
When I spoke with a bit of English mixed in, the children showed interest.

Kanji variant of 混(ま)ぜる. The 交(まじ)わる kanji ('to cross, to associate') is preferred when adding or including something that remains distinguishable — especially people joining an activity, or visibly distinct elements (jokes, foreign words).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: transitive
  • Pair: 交(ま)ざる (intransitive — to mingle, be mixed in)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AをBに交(ま)ぜる: to mix A into B
  • 〜を交(ま)ぜて + verb: doing the action while incorporating ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仲間(なかま)に交(ま)ぜる: to let (someone) into the group
  • 冗談(じょうだん)を交(ま)ぜる: to mix in jokes
  • 英語(えいご)を交(ま)ぜて話(はな)す: to speak mixing in English

WHEN TO USE WHICH KANJI:

  • 混(ま)ぜる: stirring substances together into a homogeneous mixture
  • 交(ま)ぜる: including distinguishable items or letting someone join in

The two are often interchangeable, and 混(ま)ぜる is the more common spelling overall.
