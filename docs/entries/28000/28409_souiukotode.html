そういうことで

そういうことで
expression
so; well, that being the case; with that
1. well, that being the case; so; with that (set expression marking a wrap-up)
A set transition phrase used to draw a conclusion based on what has just been said, or to wrap up a topic and move on. Common at the end of an explanation or before saying goodbye.
そういうことで、よろしくお願(ねが)いします。
So with that, please look after this matter.
そういうことで、今日(きょう)の会議(かいぎ)は終(お)わりにします。
With that, we'll bring today's meeting to a close.
そういうことで、来週(らいしゅう)までに資料(しりょう)を準備(じゅんび)しておいてください。
That being the case, please have the materials ready by next week.

Literally 'in that kind of matter,' but functions as a discourse marker for wrapping up. Sounds slightly informal but is common in workplace conversation, meetings, and emails.

USAGE:
Typically followed by a request, a conclusion, or a farewell. Often paired with よろしくお願(ねが)いします or 終(お)わりにします.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • では: well then — more formal, neutral transition
  • それでは: with that — slightly more formal than そういうことで
  • というわけで: for that reason — emphasizes causal logic; close in nuance but more reasoning-oriented
