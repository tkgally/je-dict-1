1. to sell off; to sell out; to dispose of (a large quantity) by selling
To sell goods—often a large quantity, leftover stock, or illicitly obtained items—until they are gone. Implies skillful or aggressive distribution, and is frequently used for clearing inventory or fencing stolen items.
在庫(ざいこ)を売(う)りさばいた。
We sold off the inventory.
犯人(はんにん)は盗品(とうひん)をネットで売(う)りさばいていた。
The perpetrator was selling stolen goods online.
安(やす)く仕入(しい)れた商品(しょうひん)を短期間(たんきかん)で売(う)りさばいて利益(りえき)を上(あ)げた。
We made a profit by selling off cheaply procured goods in a short period.

Compound of 売(う)る (to sell) + さばく (to handle, dispose of, work through). The さばく element conveys efficiency and completeness — selling through everything.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. Takes a direct object marked with を: 商品(しょうひん)を売(う)りさばく.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 在庫(ざいこ)を売(う)りさばく: to sell off inventory
  • 盗品(とうひん)を売(う)りさばく: to fence stolen goods
  • 商品(しょうひん)を売(う)りさばく: to dispose of merchandise by selling
  • 安(やす)く売(う)りさばく: to sell off cheaply

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)る: to sell — neutral, no nuance of clearing or volume
  • 売(う)り切(き)る: to sell out — completion focus, often by seller's effort
  • 処分(しょぶん)する: to dispose of — broader, includes discarding, not just selling
