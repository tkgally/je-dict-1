1. to hit it off, to get along instantly
A four-character idiom describing the instant rapport and mutual understanding that develops between people when they first meet.
初対面(しょたいめん)で意気投合(いきとうごう)した。
We hit it off at first meeting.
趣味(しゅみ)が同(おな)じで意気投合(いきとうごう)した。
We bonded over having the same hobbies.
二人(ふたり)は意気投合(いきとうごう)して一緒(いっしょ)に事業(じぎょう)を始(はじ)めた。
The two hit it off and started a business together.
彼(かれ)らはすぐに意気投合(いきとうごう)して親友(しんゆう)になった。
They immediately hit it off and became best friends.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご))

LITERAL MEANING: 意気(いき) (spirit, will) + 投合(とうごう) (to come together, to harmonize) = Spirits coming together in harmony.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 意気投合(いきとうごう)する (to hit it off)
  • 意気投合(いきとうごう)して＋verb (after hitting it off, ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 初対面(しょたいめん)で意気投合(いきとうごう)する (to hit it off at first meeting)
  • すぐに意気投合(いきとうごう)する (to immediately get along)
  • 意気投合(いきとうごう)して友達(ともだち)になる (to become friends after hitting it off)

NUANCE: Always positive, describing a natural and mutual connection between people.

ENGLISH EQUIVALENTS:

  • To hit it off
  • To click
  • To connect immediately

RELATED TERMS:

  • 馬(うま)が合(あ)う - to get along well (literally: horses match)
  • 気(き)が合(あ)う - to be compatible, to get along
  • 仲良(なかよ)くなる - to become close/friendly
