つくづく

つくづく
adverb
deeply, keenly, thoroughly

Definitions

1. deeply, keenly, thoroughly
Feeling or realizing something deeply, often after careful reflection or repeated experience.

Examples

つくづく自分(じぶん)の無力(むりょく)さを感(かん)じた。
I deeply felt my own powerlessness.
つくづく嫌(いや)になった。
I became thoroughly fed up.
彼(かれ)の顔(かお)をつくづく見(み)て、変(か)わったなと思(おも)った。
Looking at his face intently, I thought he had changed.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • つくづく思(おも)う (to deeply feel/realize)
  • つくづく感(かん)じる (to keenly feel)
  • つくづく嫌(いや)になる (to become thoroughly fed up)
  • つくづく見(み)る (to look at intently/searchingly)

NUANCE:
Often used when reflecting on something and coming to a deep realization, frequently about negative aspects of oneself or a situation. Can express resignation, regret, or profound awareness.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • しみじみ: similar meaning but often warmer/more nostalgic
  • 痛切(つうせつ)に: more formal, emphasizing painful realization

KANJI FORM:
熟々(つくづく) exists but hiragana is standard.

Related Words

Related: しみじみ (deeply)
