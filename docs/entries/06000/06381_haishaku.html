拝借(はいしゃく)

はいしゃく
noun
borrowing (humble)
See also: 拝借(はいしゃく)する (verb form)
1. borrowing (humble)
A humble expression for borrowing something from a respected person. Used when referring to borrowing with respect toward the lender.
お知恵(ちえ)を拝借(はいしゃく)できますか。
May I ask for your wisdom?
Common set phrase for asking advice
少々(しょうしょう)お時間(じかん)を拝借(はいしゃく)できますでしょうか。
Could I have a moment of your time?
こちらのペンを拝借(はいしゃく)してもよろしいでしょうか。
May I borrow this pen?
Formal business context
先生(せんせい)のお力(ちから)を拝借(はいしゃく)したいのですが。
I would like to ask for your help.

KEIGO (敬語(けいご)): 謙譲語(けんじょうご) (humble language). The prefix 拝(はい) indicates humble respect toward the person from whom something is borrowed.

VERB FORM:

  • 拝借(はいしゃく)する (to borrow - humble)
  • 拝借(はいしゃく)いたす (more formal humble form)

COMMON SET PHRASES:

  • お知恵(ちえ)を拝借(はいしゃく)する (to ask for someone's wisdom/advice)
  • お時間(じかん)を拝借(はいしゃく)する (to take someone's time)
  • お力(ちから)を拝借(はいしゃく)する (to ask for someone's help)

CONTEXTS: Used in formal business settings, meetings, and when making requests of superiors or respected individuals.

RELATED 拝(はい) COMPOUNDS:

  • 拝見(はいけん) - to see/look at (humble)
  • 拝読(はいどく) - to read (humble)
  • 拝聴(はいちょう) - to listen (humble)
  • 拝受(はいじゅ) - to receive (humble)

PLAIN EQUIVALENT: 借(か)りる (to borrow - neutral)
