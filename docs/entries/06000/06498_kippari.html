きっぱり

きっぱり
adverb, suru verb
clearly; decisively; flatly

Definitions

1. clearly; decisively; flatly; firmly
In a decisive, unambiguous manner, often when refusing or cutting ties with something.

Examples

誘(さそ)いをきっぱり断(ことわ)った。
I flatly refused the invitation.
タバコ(たばこ)をきっぱりやめた。
I quit smoking for good.
きっぱりと「いいえ」と言(い)った。
I said "no" decisively.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA/MIMETIC WORD:
Expresses a clean, decisive action without hesitation or ambiguity.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • きっぱり断(ことわ)る: to flatly refuse
  • きっぱりやめる: to quit decisively
  • きっぱりと言(い)う: to say clearly/firmly

SIMILAR:

  • はっきり: clearly (more about clarity than decisiveness)
  • すっぱり: completely, clean (more about completeness)
