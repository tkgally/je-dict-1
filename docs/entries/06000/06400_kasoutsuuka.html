仮想通貨(かそうつうか)

かそうつうか
noun
cryptocurrency
1. cryptocurrency, virtual currency
Digital currency that uses cryptography for security and operates on decentralized networks, typically blockchain technology. Not issued by a central bank or government.
仮想通貨(かそうつうか)で支払(しはら)う。
To pay with cryptocurrency.
仮想通貨(かそうつうか)の価格(かかく)が急落(きゅうらく)した。
The price of cryptocurrency plummeted.
仮想通貨(かそうつうか)の取引所(とりひきじょ)で口座(こうざ)を開設(かいせつ)する。
To open an account at a cryptocurrency exchange.
仮想通貨(かそうつうか)の利益(りえき)には税金(ぜいきん)がかかる。
Profits from cryptocurrency are subject to taxes.

ETYMOLOGY: 仮想(かそう) (virtual) + 通貨(つうか) (currency). The term emphasizes that this currency exists only digitally, not as physical coins or bills.

TERMINOLOGY NOTE: Japan officially uses 暗号資産(あんごうしさん) (cryptographic assets) in legal and regulatory contexts since 2019, but 仮想通貨(かそうつうか) remains the common everyday term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)を購入(こうにゅう)する (to purchase cryptocurrency)
  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)に投資(とうし)する (to invest in cryptocurrency)
  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)取引所(とりひきじょ) (cryptocurrency exchange)
  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)ウォレット (cryptocurrency wallet)
  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)マイニング (cryptocurrency mining)

RELATED TERMS:

  • ビットコイン - Bitcoin
  • イーサリアム - Ethereum
  • ブロックチェーン - blockchain
  • 電子(でんし)マネー - electronic money (different: e-money is centrally managed)
