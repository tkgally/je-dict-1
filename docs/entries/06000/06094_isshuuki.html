一周忌(いっしゅうき)

いっしゅうき
noun
first death anniversary; first memorial

Definitions

1. first death anniversary; first yearly memorial
A Buddhist memorial service held one year after a person's death. An important milestone in the traditional mourning cycle.

Examples

父(ちち)の一周忌(いっしゅうき)を迎(むか)えた。
It's been one year since my father's passing.
一周忌(いっしゅうき)法要(ほうよう)でお寺(てら)に集(あつ)まった。
We gathered at the temple for the first anniversary memorial service.
一周忌(いっしゅうき)を期(き)に喪服(もふく)を着(き)なくなる。
After the first anniversary, mourning clothes are no longer worn.

Notes

一周忌(いっしゅうき) marks one full year since death and is the first of the annual memorials.

MEMORIAL ANNIVERSARY SEQUENCE:

  • 一周忌(いっしゅうき): 1 year (first death anniversary)
  • 三回忌(さんかいき): 2 years ("third" by traditional counting)
  • 七回忌(ななかいき): 6 years
  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき): 12 years
  • 三十三回忌(さんじゅうさんかいき): 32 years (often the final formal service)

NOTE: The counting includes the year of death, so 三回忌(さんかいき) is held 2 years after death.
