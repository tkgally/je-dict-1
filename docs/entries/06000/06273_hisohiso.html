ひそひそ

ひそひそ
adverb
in whispers, in hushed tones
1. in whispers, in hushed tones
Speaking very quietly so that others cannot hear; whispering secretly.
二人(ふたり)はひそひそと話(はな)していた。
The two were whispering to each other.
隣(となり)の席(せき)でひそひそ話(ばなし)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear whispered conversation from the next seat.
ひそひそ話(ばなし) is a common compound
彼(かれ)はひそひそ声(ごえ)で秘密(ひみつ)を教(おし)えてくれた。
He told me the secret in a whisper.
後(うし)ろでひそひそと何(なに)か言(い)っているのが気(き)になる。
It bothers me that they're whispering something behind me.
Implies suspicious or gossipy behavior

SOUND SYMBOLISM: The voiceless ひ sounds evoke the quiet, hushed quality of whispering.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ひそひそ話(ばなし) (whispered conversation, gossip)
  • ひそひそ声(ごえ) (whispered voice)

NUANCE: Often implies secrecy or gossip. Can have negative connotations when describing people whispering about others.

VERB FORMS:

  • ひそひそ話(はな)す (to whisper)
  • ひそひそ言(い)う (to say in a whisper)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • こそこそ: Stealthily, sneakily (about actions, not just speech)
  • ぼそぼそ: Muttering (unclear speech, not necessarily quiet)
  • がやがや: Noisily, clamorously (antonym)

Related Words

Contrast: こそこそ
Antonym: がやがや
