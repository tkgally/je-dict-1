砂場(すなば)

すなば
noun
sandbox, sandpit
1. sandbox, sandpit
A shallow box or pit filled with sand where children can play, typically found in playgrounds and parks.
子供(こども)たちが砂場(すなば)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing in the sandbox.
砂場(すなば)で砂(すな)のお城(しろ)を作(つく)った。
I made a sand castle in the sandbox.
砂場(すなば)で遊(あそ)んだ後(あと)は手(て)を洗(あら)いなさい。
Wash your hands after playing in the sandbox.

砂場(すなば) is a sandbox or sandpit for children's play.

COMMON ACTIVITIES:

  • 砂(すな)のお城(しろ)を作(つく)る (make a sand castle)
  • 砂(すな)を掘(ほ)る (dig in the sand)
  • 砂(すな)遊(あそ)び (sand play)
  • 泥団子(どろだんご)を作(つく)る (make mud balls)

COMMON TOOLS:

  • バケツ (bucket)
  • スコップ (small shovel)
  • 砂型(すながた) (sand mold)
  • 熊手(くまで) (rake)

HYGIENE NOTE:
Many Japanese parks cover sandboxes at night to prevent contamination from stray cats. Signs often remind children to wash their hands after playing.

RELATED PLAYGROUND EQUIPMENT:

  • ぶらんこ (swing)
  • 滑(すべ)り台(だい) (slide)
  • シーソー (seesaw)
