取(と)り返(かえ)す

とりかえす
verb (godan)
to take back, to recover

Definitions

1. to take back, to get back, to recover
To regain possession of something that was lost, taken, or given away.
2. to make up for, to recover
To compensate for lost time, ground, or disadvantage.

Examples

盗(ぬす)まれた財布(さいふ)を取(と)り返(かえ)した。
I got my stolen wallet back.
失(うしな)った時間(じかん)は取(と)り返(かえ)せない。
Lost time cannot be recovered.
Potential negative - common expression
遅(おく)れを取(と)り返(かえ)すために急(いそ)いだ。
I hurried to make up for the delay.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 取(と)る + 返(かえ)す. Pattern: Xを取(と)り返(かえ)す (take back X / recover X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 取(と)り返(かえ)している = 'has recovered' or 'is recovering.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 信頼(しんらい)を取(と)り返(かえ)す (regain trust)
  • 名誉(めいよ)を取(と)り返(かえ)す (restore one's honor)
  • 取(と)り返(かえ)しのつかない (irreparable, cannot be undone)

SET PHRASE: 「取(と)り返(かえ)しのつかない」 is a common expression meaning 'irreparable' or 'beyond recovery.'
