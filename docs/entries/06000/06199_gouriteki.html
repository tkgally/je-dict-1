合理的(ごうりてき)

ごうりてき
na-adjective
rational, reasonable, logical
1. rational, reasonable, logical
Based on reason or logic; sensible and practical.
合理的(ごうりてき)な判断(はんだん)をする。
Make a rational judgment.
それは合理的(ごうりてき)な説明(せつめい)だと思(おも)う。
I think that's a reasonable explanation.
2. efficient, streamlined
Practical and efficient, without waste.
もっと合理的(ごうりてき)なやり方(かた)を考(かんが)えよう。
Let's think of a more efficient method.

USAGE: 合理的(ごうりてき) is a ～的(てき) adjective used to describe decisions, explanations, and methods that are based on logic and reason.

FORMS:

  • 合理的(ごうりてき)な＋noun (rational/reasonable ~)
  • 合理的(ごうりてき)に (rationally - adverbial form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合理的(ごうりてき)な判断(はんだん) (rational judgment)
  • 合理的(ごうりてき)な説明(せつめい) (reasonable explanation)
  • 合理的(ごうりてき)な理由(りゆう) (rational reason)
  • 合理的(ごうりてき)に考(かんが)える (to think rationally)

CONTRAST:

  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき) - irrational, unreasonable (antonym)
  • 感情的(かんじょうてき) - emotional (often contrasted)

LEGAL CONTEXT: In legal documents, 合理的(ごうりてき) often corresponds to "reasonable" as in "合理的(ごうりてき)な配慮(はいりょ)" (reasonable accommodation).

Related Words

Antonym: 非合理的(ひごうりてき) (irrational)
