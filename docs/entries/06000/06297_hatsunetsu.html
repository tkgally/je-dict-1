発熱(はつねつ)

はつねつ
noun
fever; generating heat
1. fever, having a fever
An elevated body temperature, typically as a symptom of illness.
子供(こども)が発熱(はつねつ)したので病院(びょういん)に連(つ)れて行(い)った。
My child developed a fever, so I took them to the hospital.
発熱(はつねつ)がある場合(ばあい)は出勤(しゅっきん)しないでください。
Please don't come to work if you have a fever.
37.5度(ど)以上(いじょう)の発熱(はつねつ)は要注意(ようちゅうい)だ。
A fever of 37.5 degrees or higher requires attention.
2. heat generation, generating heat
The production of heat, used in technical and scientific contexts.
このヒーターは発熱(はつねつ)効率(こうりつ)が良(よ)い。
This heater has good heat generation efficiency.

MEDICAL USAGE:
発熱(はつねつ) is the medical/formal term for having a fever. It's commonly used in medical settings and official announcements.

FEVER THRESHOLDS IN JAPAN:

  • 37.5°C: Commonly used threshold for 'fever' in public health guidelines
  • 38°C: Generally considered moderate fever
  • 39°C+: High fever

VERB USAGE:

  • 発熱(はつねつ)する: to develop a fever / to generate heat
  • 発熱(はつねつ)がある: to have a fever

RELATED TERMS:

  • 熱(ねつ): fever, heat (casual term)
  • 高熱(こうねつ): high fever
  • 微熱(びねつ): low-grade fever
  • 解熱(げねつ): fever reduction
  • 解熱剤(げねつざい): fever reducer, antipyretic

TECHNICAL USAGE (sense 2):

In physics and engineering, 発熱(はつねつ) refers to heat generation:

  • 発熱(はつねつ)反応(はんのう): exothermic reaction
  • 発熱(はつねつ)体(たい): heating element
