粛々(しゅくしゅく)

しゅくしゅく
adverb, na-adjective (taru)
quietly and solemnly; steadily
1. quietly and solemnly; with gravity
In a solemn, dignified manner. Describes proceeding calmly and seriously without fanfare.
2. steadily; methodically; as planned
Proceeding in an orderly, systematic manner without being swayed by external factors.
式典(しきてん)は粛々(しゅくしゅく)と行(おこな)われた。
The ceremony was conducted solemnly.
計画(けいかく)を粛々(しゅくしゅく)と進(すす)める。
We steadily proceed with the plan.
法律(ほうりつ)に基(もと)づき粛々(しゅくしゅく)と対応(たいおう)する。
We will respond methodically in accordance with the law.

粛々(しゅくしゅく) is an ABAB-pattern adverb with a formal, literary tone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 粛々(しゅくしゅく)と進(すす)める: to proceed steadily
  • 粛々(しゅくしゅく)と行(おこな)う: to carry out solemnly
  • 粛々(しゅくしゅく)と対応(たいおう)する: to respond methodically

USAGE CONTEXT:

Often used in:

  • Official/government statements
  • Business contexts
  • Legal proceedings
  • Formal ceremonies

NUANCE:
Implies proceeding without being influenced by criticism or pressure.
