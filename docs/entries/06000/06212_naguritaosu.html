1. to knock down with a punch, to punch and knock over
To strike someone with enough force to make them fall down; to punch someone so hard they collapse.
彼(かれ)は相手(あいて)を一発(いっぱつ)で殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)した。
He knocked his opponent down with a single punch.
ボクサーは対戦相手(たいせんあいて)を殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)して勝利(しょうり)した。
The boxer knocked down his opponent and won.
酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)いに殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)されそうになった。
I was nearly knocked down by a drunk.

USAGE: 殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)す is a compound verb combining 殴(なぐ)る (to punch, to hit) and 倒(たお)す (to knock down). It emphasizes the result of the punch causing the person to fall.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (takes を)

ASPECT: 殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)している is rarely used since this is typically a momentary action.

COMPOUND VERB PATTERN: ～倒(たお)す indicates causing something to fall or collapse through the action.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 蹴(け)り倒(たお)す (to kick down)
  • 押(お)し倒(たお)す (to push down)
  • 突(つ)き倒(たお)す (to shove down)

REGISTER: This word can describe violence, so use with care. Common in sports commentary (especially combat sports), action narratives, and news reports.
