泣(な)き出(だ)す

なきだす
verb (godan)
to start crying; to burst into tears
1. to start crying; to burst into tears; to begin to cry
To suddenly begin to cry, often with emotion.
子供(こども)が急(きゅう)に泣(な)き出(だ)した。
The child suddenly started crying.
映画(えいが)の最後(さいご)で泣(な)き出(だ)してしまった。
I burst into tears at the end of the movie.
悲(かな)しい知(し)らせを聞(き)いて泣(な)き出(だ)した。
I started crying when I heard the sad news.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Compound verb: 泣(な)く + 出(だ)す.

NUANCE: The 出(だ)す component emphasizes the sudden onset of crying. It suggests that the crying begins abruptly, often triggered by something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 急(きゅう)に泣(な)き出(だ)す: to suddenly start crying
  • わあっと泣(な)き出(だ)す: to burst out crying loudly

RELATED:

  • 笑(わら)い出(だ)す: to start laughing
  • 怒(おこ)り出(だ)す: to start getting angry
