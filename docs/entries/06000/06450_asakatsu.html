1. morning activity, early morning self-improvement
The practice of waking up early to engage in productive activities before work or school, such as exercise, study, or hobbies.
朝活(あさかつ)で英語(えいご)の勉強(べんきょう)をしている。
I study English as part of my morning activity.
朝活(あさかつ)を始(はじ)めてから生活(せいかつ)リズムが整(ととの)った。
Since starting morning activities, my daily rhythm has improved.
カフェで朝活(あさかつ)をする人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
More people are doing morning activities at cafes.

ETYMOLOGY: Abbreviation of 朝(あさ)の活動(かつどう) (morning activity). Part of the ～活(かつ) word pattern.

CULTURAL NOTE:
朝活(あさかつ) became a trend in Japan in the 2010s as a way for busy professionals to make time for personal development. Cafes and coworking spaces began offering early morning hours to accommodate this trend. The concept emphasizes using the quiet early morning hours for focused, productive activities.
