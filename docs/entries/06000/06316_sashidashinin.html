差出人(さしだしにん)

さしだしにん
noun
sender
1. sender
The person who sends a letter, package, or other mail item. This information is typically written on the back of an envelope or on the return address label.
差出人(さしだしにん)の名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write the sender's name.
封筒(ふうとう)の裏(うら)に差出人(さしだしにん)を記入(きにゅう)する。
Write the sender's information on the back of the envelope.
In Japan, the sender's address is typically written on the back of the envelope
差出人(さしだしにん)不明(ふめい)の手紙(てがみ)が届(とど)いた。
A letter from an unknown sender arrived.

差出人(さしだしにん) refers to the sender of mail or packages.

POSTAL CONVENTIONS IN JAPAN:
In Japanese postal convention, the sender's information is written on the back (裏(うら)) of the envelope, while the recipient's address is on the front (表(おもて)).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 差出人(さしだしにん)住所(じゅうしょ) (sender's address)
  • 差出人(さしだしにん)氏名(しめい) (sender's name)
  • 差出人(さしだしにん)不明(ふめい) (unknown sender)

CONTRAST:

  • 差出人(さしだしにん): sender
  • 宛先(あてさき): recipient's address, destination
  • 受取人(うけとりにん): recipient, addressee
