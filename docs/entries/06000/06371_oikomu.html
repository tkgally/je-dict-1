追(お)い込(こ)む

おいこむ
verb (godan, transitive)
to corner, to drive into
1. to drive into, to chase into
To force someone or something into a confined space by chasing.
2. to corner, to push into a difficult situation
To force someone into a desperate or disadvantageous position, either literally or figuratively.
羊(ひつじ)を囲(かこ)いに追(お)い込(こ)む。
To drive the sheep into the pen.
犯人(はんにん)を袋小路(ふくろこうじ)に追(お)い込(こ)んだ。
The criminal was cornered in a dead end.
借金(しゃっきん)で彼(かれ)は追(お)い込(こ)まれた状況(じょうきょう)にある。
He's in a desperate situation due to debt.
自分(じぶん)を追(お)い込(こ)んでトレーニングする。
To push oneself hard during training.

COMPOUND VERB: 追(お)う (to chase) + 込(こ)む (to go into) = to chase into

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Takes を to mark what is being driven/cornered.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 窮地(きゅうち)に追(お)い込(こ)む (to drive into a tight spot)
  • 追(お)い込(こ)まれる (to be cornered - passive form)
  • 自分(じぶん)を追(お)い込(こ)む (to push oneself)
  • 崖(がけ)っぷちに追(お)い込(こ)む (to push to the brink)

USAGE NOTE: Often used in the passive form 追(お)い込(こ)まれる when describing being put in a difficult situation.

RELATED ～込(こ)む COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 呼(よ)び込(こ)む - to call in, to invite in
  • 誘(さそ)い込(こ)む - to lure into
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む - to rush into
