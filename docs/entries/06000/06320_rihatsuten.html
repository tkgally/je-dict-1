理髪店(りはつてん)

りはつてん
noun
barbershop, barber's shop
1. barbershop, barber's shop
A shop where men's hair is cut and styled, and shaving services may be offered. The formal term for a barber's establishment.
理髪店(りはつてん)で髪(かみ)を切(き)ってもらった。
I had my hair cut at the barbershop.
この理髪店(りはつてん)は三(さん)代(だい)続(つづ)いている。
This barbershop has been in the family for three generations.
昔(むかし)ながらの理髪店(りはつてん)が少(すく)なくなった。
Old-fashioned barbershops have become rare.

理髪店(りはつてん) is the formal term for a barbershop.

SERVICES OFFERED:

  • 散髪(さんぱつ) / カット (haircut)
  • シャンプー (shampoo)
  • 顔(かお)そり (face shaving)
  • 髭(ひげ)そり (beard shaving)

SYNONYMS:

  • 床屋(とこや): barbershop (colloquial, more common in casual speech)
  • 理容店(りようてん): barber shop (formal)
  • 理容室(りようしつ): barber shop/salon

CONTRAST:

  • 理髪店(りはつてん): barbershop (traditionally for men)
  • 美容院(びよういん): beauty salon (traditionally for women)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Traditional Japanese barbershops often display a red, white, and blue striped barber pole (サインポール) outside, similar to Western barbershops.
