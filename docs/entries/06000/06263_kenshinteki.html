献身的(けんしんてき)

けんしんてき
na-adjective
devoted, dedicated, selfless
1. devoted, dedicated, selfless
Giving oneself completely to a person, cause, or activity; showing self-sacrificing commitment.
彼女(かのじょ)は献身的(けんしんてき)に患者(かんじゃ)の世話(せわ)をした。
She devoted herself to caring for the patient.
献身的(けんしんてき)な愛情(あいじょう)で子供(こども)を育(そだ)てた。
She raised her children with devoted love.
ボランティア(ぼらんてぃあ)の献身的(けんしんてき)な活動(かつどう)に感謝(かんしゃ)します。
I appreciate the volunteers' dedicated efforts.
献身的(けんしんてき)なサポートのおかげで回復(かいふく)できた。
Thanks to the devoted support, I was able to recover.

USAGE: 献身的(けんしんてき) describes selfless devotion, often in the context of care, service, or love. It has a positive connotation implying admirable self-sacrifice.

NOUN/VERB: 献身(けんしん) (devotion, dedication)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 献身的(けんしんてき)に尽(つ)くす (serve devotedly)
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な介護(かいご) (devoted nursing care)
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な愛(あい) (selfless love)
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)に働(はたら)く (work devotedly)

CONTEXTS: Often used for caregiving, nursing, parenting, volunteer work, and religious service.
