三回忌(さんかいき)

さんかいき
noun
second death anniversary; third memorial
1. second death anniversary; third memorial service
A Buddhist memorial service held two years after a person's death. Called 'third' because it counts the year of death as the first.
祖母(そぼ)の三回忌(さんかいき)を営(いとな)んだ。
We held my grandmother's second-anniversary memorial service.
三回忌(さんかいき)には家族(かぞく)だけで集(あつ)まった。
Only family gathered for the second anniversary memorial.
三回忌(さんかいき)は亡(な)くなってから二(に)年目(ねんめ)に行(おこな)う。
The sankaiki is held two years after death.

Japanese Buddhist counting is different from Western counting:

JAPANESE COUNTING METHOD:

  • 一回忌(いっかいき) = year of death (not commonly used)
  • 三回忌(さんかいき) = 2 years after death (1 + 2 = 3)
  • 七回忌(ななかいき) = 6 years after death

This counting method (数え(かぞえ)) includes the starting point, unlike Western counting.

MEMORIAL SERVICES:
After 三回忌(さんかいき), memorials become less frequent and attendance typically decreases.
