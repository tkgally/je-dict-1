捏(こ)ねる

こねる
verb (ichidan)
to knead
1. to knead, to work (dough)
To press and fold dough or similar substances repeatedly to make them smooth and pliable.
パン生地(きじ)を捏(こ)ねる。
I knead the bread dough.
粘土(ねんど)をよく捏(こ)ねてから形(かたち)を作(つく)る。
Knead the clay well before shaping it.
2. to quibble, to nitpick
To make petty complaints or arguments (often in the form 理屈(りくつ)を捏(こ)ねる).
屁理屈(へりくつ)を捏(こ)ねないで。
Don't quibble with me.

USAGE: 捏(こ)ねる is an ichidan verb used primarily in cooking and crafting contexts.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (takes を)

ASPECT: 捏(こ)ねている indicates ongoing kneading action or habitual kneading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生地(きじ)を捏(こ)ねる (to knead dough)
  • 粘土(ねんど)を捏(こ)ねる (to knead clay)
  • 理屈(りくつ)を捏(こ)ねる (to quibble, make excuses)
  • 屁理屈(へりくつ)を捏(こ)ねる (to nitpick, make sophistic arguments)

KANJI NOTE: The kanji 捏(こ)ねる uses a relatively uncommon character. Often written in hiragana as こねる in casual contexts.
