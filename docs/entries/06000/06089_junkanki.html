循環器(じゅんかんき)

じゅんかんき
noun
circulatory system; cardiovascular system

Definitions

1. circulatory system; cardiovascular system
The organ system including the heart and blood vessels that circulates blood throughout the body.

Examples

循環器(じゅんかんき)内科(ないか)で検査(けんさ)を受(う)けた。
I had tests done at the cardiology department.
循環器(じゅんかんき)の病気(びょうき)に注意(ちゅうい)する。
Be careful of cardiovascular diseases.
循環器(じゅんかんき)系(けい)の健康(けんこう)を保(たも)つ。
Maintain the health of the circulatory system.

Notes

循環器(じゅんかんき) focuses on the heart and blood vessels.

MEDICAL TERMS:

  • 循環器(じゅんかんき)内科(ないか): cardiology
  • 循環器(じゅんかんき)疾患(しっかん): cardiovascular disease
  • 循環器(じゅんかんき)系(けい): circulatory system

ONE OF THE MAJOR ORGAN SYSTEMS:

  • 呼吸器(こきゅうき): respiratory system
  • 消化器(しょうかき): digestive system
  • 循環器(じゅんかんき): circulatory system
