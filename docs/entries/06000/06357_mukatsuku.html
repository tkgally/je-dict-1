ムカつく

むかつく
verb (godan)
to feel irritated; to be pissed off
1. to feel irritated; to be annoyed; to be pissed off
Slang expression for feeling strong annoyance or anger toward someone or something.
2. to feel nauseous; to feel sick
The original meaning: to feel queasiness or nausea, especially in the stomach.
あいつの態度(たいど)にムカつく。
His attitude pisses me off.
ムカつくことを言(い)われた。
Someone said something that annoyed me.
胃(い)がムカつく。
My stomach feels queasy.

SLANG/COLLOQUIAL:
Originally meant "to feel nauseous" (sense 2), but now primarily used as youth slang for "to be irritated/annoyed" (sense 1).

WRITING:
Often written in katakana (ムカつく) to emphasize its slang nature, though hiragana (むかつく) is also used.

RELATED:

  • ムカムカする: to feel nausea; to be irritated (onomatopoeia)
  • イラつく: to be irritated (similar slang)
  • 腹(はら)が立(た)つ: to be angry (standard expression)

REGISTER: Casual/informal. Avoid in formal situations.
