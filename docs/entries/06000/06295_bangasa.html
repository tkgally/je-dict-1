番傘(ばんがさ)

ばんがさ
noun
traditional Japanese umbrella; oiled paper umbrella

Definitions

1. traditional Japanese paper umbrella
A type of traditional Japanese umbrella made with bamboo and oiled Japanese paper (washi). Used for protection from rain.

Examples

時代劇(じだいげき)で番傘(ばんがさ)を差(さ)している場面(ばめん)をよく見(み)る。
You often see scenes with traditional umbrellas in period dramas.
京都(きょうと)のお{土産(みやげ)}に番傘(ばんがさ)を買(か)った。
I bought a traditional umbrella as a Kyoto souvenir.
番傘(ばんがさ)は竹(たけ)と和紙(わし)で作(つく)られている。
Traditional umbrellas are made from bamboo and Japanese paper.

Notes

TRADITIONAL JAPANESE ITEM:
番傘(ばんがさ) is a type of traditional Japanese umbrella (和傘(わがさ)) used for rain protection. The name comes from the numbering (番号(ばんごう)) used by umbrella makers.

CONSTRUCTION:

  • Frame: Bamboo (竹(たけ))
  • Canopy: Oiled Japanese paper (油紙(あぶらがみ)/和紙(わし))
  • Handle: Bamboo or wood

TYPES OF JAPANESE UMBRELLAS:

  • 番傘(ばんがさ): Everyday rain umbrella (sturdy, practical)
  • 蛇(じゃ)の目傘(めがさ): Decorative umbrella with bullseye pattern
  • 日傘(ひがさ): Parasol (for sun protection)

MODERN USAGE:

While largely replaced by Western-style umbrellas, 番傘(ばんがさ) are still used:

  • In 茶道(さどう) (tea ceremony)
  • At traditional inns (旅館(りょかん))
  • In festivals and traditional events
  • As decorative items and souvenirs
