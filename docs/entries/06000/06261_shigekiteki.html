刺激的(しげきてき)

しげきてき
na-adjective
stimulating, provocative, exciting
1. stimulating, exciting
Providing mental or physical stimulation; arousing interest or excitement.
2. provocative, sensational
Intentionally controversial or shocking; designed to provoke a strong reaction.
刺激的(しげきてき)な映画(えいが)を見(み)た。
I watched an exciting movie.
彼女(かのじょ)の話(はなし)はとても刺激的(しげきてき)だった。
Her talk was very stimulating.
刺激的(しげきてき)な見出(みだ)しで読者(どくしゃ)の注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)める。
The provocative headline attracts readers' attention.
新(あたら)しい環境(かんきょう)は刺激的(しげきてき)で毎日(まいにち)が楽(たの)しい。
The new environment is stimulating and every day is enjoyable.

USAGE: 刺激的(しげきてき) can be used as a な-adjective or adverbially with に. It can be positive (exciting, thought-provoking) or negative (too provocative, sensational).

NOUN/VERB: 刺激(しげき) (stimulation), 刺激(しげき)する (to stimulate)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 刺激的(しげきてき)な内容(ないよう) (stimulating content)
  • 刺激的(しげきてき)な体験(たいけん) (exciting experience)
  • 刺激的(しげきてき)な発言(はつげん) (provocative statement)
  • 刺激的(しげきてき)な味(あじ) (stimulating flavor - spicy/strong)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 興奮(こうふん)する: To get excited (verb form)
  • エキサイティング: Exciting (loanword, casual)
  • センセーショナル: Sensational (often negative, media context)
