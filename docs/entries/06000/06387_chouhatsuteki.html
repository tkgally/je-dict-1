挑発的(ちょうはつてき)

ちょうはつてき
na-adjective
provocative, defiant

Definitions

1. provocative, defiant
Intentionally causing anger, annoyance, or a strong reaction; challenging or defiant in nature.
2. provocative (sexually suggestive)
Deliberately sexually suggestive in appearance or behavior.

Examples

彼(かれ)の挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な態度(たいど)が問題(もんだい)になった。
His provocative attitude became a problem.
挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な発言(はつげん)で批判(ひはん)を浴(あ)びた。
He faced criticism for his provocative remarks.
挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な服装(ふくそう)だと言(い)われた。
She was told her outfit was provocative.
相手(あいて)を挑発的(ちょうはつてき)に見(み)つめた。
He stared at the other person provocatively.

Notes

FORMATION: 挑発(ちょうはつ) (provocation) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

FORMS:

  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な＋noun (attributive)
  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)に＋verb (adverbial)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な態度(たいど) (provocative attitude)
  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な発言(はつげん) (provocative statement)
  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な行動(こうどう) (provocative behavior)
  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な服装(ふくそう) (provocative clothing)
  • 挑発的(ちょうはつてき)な視線(しせん) (provocative gaze)

CONTEXT: Often used in news and political contexts to describe deliberately inflammatory actions or statements.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 挑発(ちょうはつ) - provocation
  • 挑発(ちょうはつ)する - to provoke
  • 刺激的(しげきてき) - stimulating, exciting (milder nuance)
