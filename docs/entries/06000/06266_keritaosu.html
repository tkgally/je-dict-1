蹴(け)り倒(たお)す

けりたおす
verb (godan)
to kick down, to kick over

Definitions

1. to kick down, to kick over
To knock someone or something down by kicking; to topple with a kick.

Examples

空手(からて)の技(わざ)で相手(あいて)を蹴(け)り倒(たお)した。
I kicked the opponent down with a karate technique.
怒(おこ)った馬(うま)が柵(さく)を蹴(け)り倒(たお)した。
The angry horse kicked down the fence.
ドアを蹴(け)り倒(たお)して中(なか)に入(はい)った。
I kicked the door down and went inside.
子供(こども)がゴミ箱(ばこ)を蹴(け)り倒(たお)してしまった。
The child accidentally kicked over the trash can.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 蹴(け)る + 倒(たお)す.

PATTERN: Xを蹴(け)り倒(たお)す (kick X down)

COMPOUND VERB PATTERN: ～倒(たお)す compounds indicate knocking something down through the action of the first verb. Similar compounds:

  • 殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)す (to knock down by punching)
  • 押(お)し倒(たお)す (to push down)
  • 突(つ)き倒(たお)す (to shove down)
  • 蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす (to kick away)

NUANCE: Often implies forceful or violent action. Common in martial arts, action scenes, and descriptions of accidents.
