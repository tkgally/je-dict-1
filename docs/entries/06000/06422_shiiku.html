1. breeding, raising, keeping
The act of keeping, raising, and caring for animals, whether livestock, pets, or animals in captivity such as zoos.
動物園(どうぶつえん)でパンダを飼育(しいく)する。
To raise pandas at a zoo.
飼育(しいく)係(がかり)が動物(どうぶつ)の世話(せわ)をする。
The zookeeper takes care of the animals.
牛(うし)の飼育(しいく)には広(ひろ)い土地(とち)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Raising cattle requires a large amount of land.

COMPOSITION: 飼(し) (to raise/keep) + 育(いく) (to nurture/raise). Combines the concepts of keeping and nurturing animals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 動物(どうぶつ)を飼育(しいく)する (to raise/keep animals)
  • 飼育(しいく)係(がかり)/飼育(しいく)員(いん) (zookeeper, animal keeper)
  • 飼育(しいく)環境(かんきょう) (breeding environment)
  • 飼育(しいく)方法(ほうほう) (method of raising)
  • 飼育(しいく)下(か)にある (to be kept in captivity)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 飼(か)う (to keep/raise - more casual, for pets)
  • 畜産(ちくさん) (animal husbandry - industry focus)
  • 養殖(ようしょく) (fish farming, aquaculture)
  • 繁殖(はんしょく) (breeding, reproduction)
  • 家畜(かちく) (livestock)

USAGE NOTE: 飼育(しいく) is more formal than 飼(か)う. Use 飼育(しいく) for professional or scientific contexts (zoos, farms), while 飼(か)う is used for keeping pets at home.
