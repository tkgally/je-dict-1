じっくり

じっくり
adverb
slowly and carefully, thoroughly, taking one's time
1. slowly and carefully, thoroughly
Taking sufficient time to do something carefully and thoroughly without rushing.
じっくり考(かんが)えてから返事(へんじ)をする。
I'll think it over carefully before replying.
肉(にく)をじっくり煮込(にこ)む。
Simmer the meat slowly.
じっくり話(はな)し合(あ)う時間(じかん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want time to have a thorough discussion.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • じっくり考(かんが)える (to think carefully)
  • じっくり話(はな)し合(あ)う (to have a thorough discussion)
  • じっくり煮込(にこ)む (to simmer slowly)
  • じっくり読(よ)む (to read carefully)
  • じっくり取(と)り組(く)む (to work on something thoroughly)

NUANCE:
Emphasizes taking adequate time without rushing. Often implies a positive approach that leads to better results through patience and care.

CONTRAST:

  • じっくり emphasizes taking time carefully
  • ゆっくり emphasizes slow speed or relaxation
  • 念入(ねんい)りに emphasizes meticulousness

COOKING USAGE:
Very common in cooking contexts for slow-cooking methods that develop flavor.
