1. to scrape together, to rake up
To physically gather scattered things by scraping or raking them into one place.
砂(すな)を掻(か)き集(あつ)めた。
I scraped the sand together.
落(お)ち葉(ば)を掻(か)き集(あつ)めて袋(ふくろ)に入(い)れた。
I raked up the fallen leaves and put them in a bag.
散(ち)らばった書類(しょるい)を掻(か)き集(あつ)めて机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に戻(もど)した。
I gathered up the scattered documents and put them back on the desk.
2. to scrape together, to round up
To gather with effort from various sources, often implying difficulty or urgency.
資金(しきん)をなんとか掻(か)き集(あつ)めた。
I somehow scraped together the funds.
ボランティアを掻(か)き集(あつ)めてイベントを開催(かいさい)した。
We rounded up volunteers and held the event.
締(し)め切(き)り前(まえ)に各部署(かくぶしょ)から情報(じょうほう)を掻(か)き集(あつ)めて報告書(ほうこくしょ)をまとめた。
I gathered information from each department before the deadline and compiled the report.

USAGE: 掻(か)き集(あつ)める is a compound verb combining 掻(か)く (to scrape, to scratch) and 集(あつ)める (to gather). The 掻(か)き～ prefix adds a nuance of hasty or vigorous action.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for what is being gathered.

ASPECT: 掻(か)き集(あつ)めている indicates ongoing gathering activity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金(しきん)を掻(か)き集(あつ)める (to scrape together funds)
  • 情報(じょうほう)を掻(か)き集(あつ)める (to gather information)
  • 人手(ひとで)を掻(か)き集(あつ)める (to round up workers)
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)を掻(か)き集(あつ)める (to rake up fallen leaves)

NUANCE: Implies effort, urgency, or gathering from disparate sources. More emphatic than simple 集(あつ)める.
