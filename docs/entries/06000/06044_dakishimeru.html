抱(だ)きしめる

だきしめる
verb (ichidan)
to embrace, to hug tightly
1. to embrace, to hug tightly, to hold in one's arms
To wrap one's arms around someone or something and hold them close. Often implies affection, comfort, or strong emotion.
母(はは)は子供(こども)を強(つよ)く抱(だ)きしめた。
The mother hugged her child tightly.
再会(さいかい)を喜(よろこ)んで抱(だ)きしめ合(あ)った。
They embraced each other, overjoyed at their reunion.
With 合う for mutual action
ぬいぐるみを抱(だ)きしめて眠(ねむ)った。
I fell asleep hugging my stuffed animal.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 抱(だ)く + 締(し)める. Pattern: Xを抱(だ)きしめる (embrace X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 抱(だ)きしめている = 'is embracing' or 'is holding tight.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 強(つよ)く抱(だ)きしめる (hug tightly)
  • ぎゅっと抱(だ)きしめる (squeeze in an embrace)
  • 抱(だ)きしめ合(あ)う (embrace each other)

REGISTER: Neutral. A warm, affectionate word commonly used in both spoken and written Japanese.

WRITING: May also be written as 抱(だ)き締(し)める with kanji for しめる.
