創意工夫(そういくふう)

そういくふう
noun, suru verb
creative ingenuity; inventive thinking
1. creative ingenuity; original ideas and clever solutions; inventive thinking
The combination of original thinking and practical problem-solving to create something new or improve an existing process.
創意工夫(そういくふう)を凝(こ)らした料理(りょうり)だ。
This is a dish made with creative ingenuity.
限(かぎ)られた予算(よさん)の中(なか)で創意工夫(そういくふう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
We need creative thinking within our limited budget.
社員(しゃいん)の創意工夫(そういくふう)が会社(かいしゃ)の成長(せいちょう)につながる。
The creative ingenuity of employees leads to company growth.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)):
創意(そうい) means "originality" or "creative ideas," and 工夫(くふう) means "devising" or "ingenuity." Together, they describe the combination of original thinking with practical implementation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 創意工夫(そういくふう)を凝(こ)らす: to put creative effort into
  • 創意工夫(そういくふう)が必要(ひつよう)だ: creative thinking is needed
  • 創意工夫(そういくふう)に富(と)む: to be rich in creative ideas

USAGE:
Commonly used in business, education, and everyday contexts to encourage original thinking and innovative solutions rather than following established patterns.
