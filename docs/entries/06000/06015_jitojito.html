じとじと

じとじと
adverb, suru verb
damp; humid; clammy; sticky

Definitions

1. damp; humid; clammy
Describes an uncomfortably damp or sticky feeling, either from humidity, sweat, or moisture. Often implies an unpleasant sensation.

Examples

汗(あせ)でシャツがじとじとしている。
My shirt is damp with sweat.
じとじとした湿気(しっけ)で不快(ふかい)だ。
The clammy humidity is uncomfortable.
雨(あめ)に濡(ぬ)れて髪(かみ)がじとじとになった。
My hair became damp from the rain.

Notes

じとじと describes an unpleasant damp or clammy feeling, often from sweat or humidity. It emphasizes the sticky, uncomfortable sensation more than just wetness.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • むしむし: muggy (heat + humidity)
  • じめじめ: damp (environmental moisture)
  • べたべた: sticky (adhesive quality)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • じとじとする: to be damp/clammy
  • じとじとした汗(あせ): clammy sweat

COMPARISON:

  • じとじと emphasizes the uncomfortable clammy feeling
  • じめじめ emphasizes the dampness of an environment
  • Both are negative, but じとじと is more about personal discomfort

Related Words

Related: むしむし (muggy)
Related: じめじめ (damp)
