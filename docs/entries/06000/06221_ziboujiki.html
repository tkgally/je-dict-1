自暴自棄(じぼうじき)

じぼうじき
na-adjective, noun (four-character idiom)
self-destructive; reckless despair; giving up on oneself

Definitions

1. self-destructive; reckless despair
A state of having lost hope and acting recklessly without caring about consequences, often damaging oneself in the process.

Examples

失恋(しつれん)して自暴自棄(じぼうじき)になる。
To become self-destructive after a broken heart.
自暴自棄(じぼうじき)になっても何(なに)も解決(かいけつ)しない。
Nothing is solved by giving in to despair.
彼(かれ)は仕事(しごと)を失(うしな)って自暴自棄(じぼうじき)な行動(こうどう)を取(と)った。
He lost his job and started acting recklessly out of despair.

Notes

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)):
自暴(じぼう) (self-abandonment) + 自棄(じき) (giving up on oneself)

COMPONENTS:

  • 自(じ): self
  • 暴(ぼう): violence, recklessness
  • 棄(き): to abandon, to discard

GRAMMAR:

Used as a na-adjective with になる or as a noun:

  • 自暴自棄(じぼうじき)になる (to become self-destructive)
  • 自暴自棄(じぼうじき)な行動(こうどう) (self-destructive behavior)

NUANCE:
Describes someone who has lost all hope and no longer cares about the consequences of their actions, often leading to harmful behavior.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • {やけになる}: to become desperate/reckless
  • 投(な)げやりになる}: to become careless/indifferent
  • 諦(あきら)める: to give up
