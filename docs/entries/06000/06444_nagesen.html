1. online tipping, virtual tip
Monetary donations given to content creators during live streams or to support their work, typically through platforms like YouTube, Twitch, or Japanese services.
配信者(はいしんしゃ)に投(な)げ銭(せん)をした。
I tipped the streamer.
投(な)げ銭(せん)で月(つき)に10万円(じゅうまんえん)稼(かせ)ぐ配信者(はいしんしゃ)もいる。
Some streamers earn 100,000 yen a month from tips.
人気(にんき)の配信者(はいしんしゃ)には一回(いっかい)の配信(はいしん)で数十万円(すうじゅうまんえん)→noentryの投(な)げ銭(せん)が集(あつ)まることもある。
Popular streamers can sometimes receive hundreds of thousands of yen in tips from a single stream.
2. thrown coins (traditional)
The original meaning: coins thrown to street performers or into offering boxes at shrines and temples.
大道芸人(だいどうげいにん)→noentryに投(な)げ銭(せん)を入(い)れた。
I threw some coins to the street performer.
路上(ろじょう)ライブの前(まえ)にギターケース→noentryを開(あ)けて投(な)げ銭(せん)を待(ま)った。
I opened my guitar case in front of the street performance and waited for tips.
お寺(てら)の前(まえ)で投(な)げ銭(せん)をして手(て)を合(あ)わせた。
I tossed a coin in front of the temple and put my hands together in prayer.

ETYMOLOGY: 投(な)げる (to throw) + 銭(せん)→noentry (coin/money). Originally referred to coins thrown to performers or into offering boxes.

MODERN USAGE:

In the 2010s-2020s, 投(な)げ銭(せん) became the standard Japanese term for online tipping on streaming platforms. It covers features like:

  • スーパーチャット→noentry (Super Chat on YouTube)
  • 投(な)げ銭(せん) features on Japanese platforms
  • Donations on Twitch and similar services

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投(な)げ銭(せん)をする (to tip)
  • 投(な)げ銭(せん)を送(おく)る (to send a tip)
  • 投(な)げ銭(せん)を貰(もら)う (to receive tips)
  • 投(な)げ銭(せん)文化(ぶんか) (tipping culture)

RELATED TERMS:

  • スーパーチャット→noentry/スパチャ→noentry (Super Chat)
  • 配信(はいしん) (live streaming)
  • 配信者(はいしんしゃ) (streamer)
  • 投(な)げる (to throw, also used alone for tipping)
  • 課金(かきん)する (to spend money on digital content)
