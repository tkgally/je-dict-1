1. to pile up, to stack
To physically place objects on top of each other in layers.
本(ほん)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねた。
I stacked the books.
段(だん)ボール箱(ばこ)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねて収納(しゅうのう)した。
I stacked the cardboard boxes for storage.
レンガを丁寧(ていねい)に積(つ)み重(かさ)ねて壁(かべ)を作(つく)った。
I carefully stacked the bricks to build a wall.
2. to accumulate, to build up
To gradually gather or amass things over time, especially abstract things like experience, effort, or achievements.
経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to accumulate experience.
長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねて、やっと成功(せいこう)した。
After accumulating years of effort, I finally succeeded.
小(ちい)さな実績(じっせき)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねることで、周囲(しゅうい)の信頼(しんらい)を得(え)た。
By building up small achievements, I earned the trust of those around me.

USAGE: 積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる is a compound verb combining 積(つ)む (to pile) and 重(かさ)ねる (to stack, to repeat). The figurative use (sense 2) is more common than the literal use.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for the object being accumulated.

ASPECT: 積(つ)み重(かさ)ねている indicates ongoing accumulation or a state of having accumulated over time.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 努力(どりょく)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる (to accumulate effort)
  • 経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる (to build up experience)
  • 実績(じっせき)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる (to build up achievements)
  • 練習(れんしゅう)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる (to accumulate practice)

RELATED: The intransitive form 積(つ)み重(かさ)なる means "to pile up" or "to accumulate" naturally without an agent.
