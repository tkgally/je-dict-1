逃(に)げ出(だ)す

にげだす
verb (godan)
to flee, to run away

Definitions

1. to flee, to run away, to escape
To start running away from danger, a threatening situation, or something unpleasant. Emphasizes the sudden start of the escape action.

Examples

犯人(はんにん)は警察(けいさつ)から逃(に)げ出(だ)した。
The criminal fled from the police.
怖(こわ)くなって逃(に)げ出(だ)したくなった。
I got scared and wanted to run away.
With たくなる (want to)
火事(かじ)になり、全員(ぜんいん)が建物(たてもの)から逃(に)げ出(だ)した。
A fire broke out, and everyone fled from the building.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Compound verb: 逃(に)げる + 出(だ)す. Pattern: Xから逃(に)げ出(だ)す (flee from X).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed action. 逃(に)げ出(だ)している = 'has fled.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 現実(げんじつ)から逃(に)げ出(だ)す (run away from reality)
  • 責任(せきにん)から逃(に)げ出(だ)す (flee from responsibility)
  • 一目散(いちもくさん)に逃(に)げ出(だ)す (flee at full speed)

NUANCE: The 出(だ)す component emphasizes the start of the action - the moment of beginning to flee.

Related Words

Related: 逃(に)げる
Related: 抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す
