序文(じょぶん)

じょぶん
noun
preface, foreword
1. preface, foreword, introduction
The introductory section at the beginning of a book, typically written by the author or another person.
序文(じょぶん)を読(よ)んでから本文(ほんぶん)に進(すす)んだ。
I read the preface before proceeding to the main text.
著者(ちょしゃ)が序文(じょぶん)で執筆(しっぴつ)の動機(どうき)を語(かた)っている。
The author talks about the motivation for writing in the preface.
有名(ゆうめい)な教授(きょうじゅ)が序文(じょぶん)を書(か)いた。
A famous professor wrote the foreword.

USAGE: 序文(じょぶん) is used for academic books, novels, and other written works.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 序文(じょぶん)を書(か)く (to write a preface)
  • 序文(じょぶん)を読(よ)む (to read the preface)

BOOK STRUCTURE TERMS:

  • 序文(じょぶん) / まえがき - preface, foreword
  • 目次(もくじ) - table of contents
  • 本文(ほんぶん) - main text
  • 付録(ふろく) - appendix
  • あとがき - afterword

SIMILAR TERMS:

  • まえがき - preface (less formal than 序文(じょぶん))
  • はじめに - introduction (common in non-fiction)
  • 緒言(しょげん) - preface (formal, academic)
