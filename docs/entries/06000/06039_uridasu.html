売(う)り出(だ)す

うりだす
verb (godan)
to put on sale, to launch
1. to put on sale, to start selling
To begin selling a product, especially at a special price or for a limited time.
新商品(しんしょうひん)を来月(らいげつ)売(う)り出(だ)す予定(よてい)だ。
We plan to launch the new product next month.
セールで半額(はんがく)で売(う)り出(だ)している。
They're selling it at half price during the sale.
2. to launch, to promote
To actively promote someone or something to gain recognition or popularity.
彼(かれ)は若手俳優(わかてはいゆう)として売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう)だ。
He's being promoted as an up-and-coming young actor.
Uses 売り出し中 (in the process of being promoted)

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 売(う)る + 出(だ)す. Pattern: Xを売(う)り出(だ)す (put X on sale / promote X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 売(う)り出(だ)している = 'is selling' or 'is on sale.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)を売(う)り出(だ)す (launch a new product)
  • 特価(とっか)で売(う)り出(だ)す (put on sale at a special price)
  • 売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう) (now on sale; being promoted)

RELATED: The noun form 売(う)り出(だ)し refers to a sale event or the act of launching/promoting.
