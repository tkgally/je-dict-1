押(お)し流(なが)す

おしながす
verb (godan, transitive)
to wash away, to sweep away
1. to wash away, to sweep away
For water, flood, or other force to carry something away by pushing.
洪水(こうずい)で橋(はし)が押(お)し流(なが)された。
The bridge was washed away by the flood.
津波(つなみ)が家(いえ)を押(お)し流(なが)した。
The tsunami swept away the houses.
激流(げきりゅう)に押(お)し流(なが)されないように気(き)をつけて。
Be careful not to be swept away by the rapids.
2. to overwhelm, to sweep aside
Figuratively, to overwhelm something with force, to brush aside or disregard.
反対(はんたい)意見(いけん)を押(お)し流(なが)して強行(きょうこう)した。
They pushed through despite opposition, sweeping aside objections.

COMPOUND VERB: 押(お)す (to push) + 流(なが)す (to let flow) = to push and let flow away

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Takes を to mark what is being washed/swept away. Often used in passive form 押(お)し流(なが)される.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洪水(こうずい)に押(お)し流(なが)される (to be washed away by a flood)
  • 津波(つなみ)に押(お)し流(なが)される (to be swept away by a tsunami)
  • 濁流(だくりゅう)に押(お)し流(なが)される (to be swept away by muddy torrents)

USAGE NOTE: Very commonly used in disaster news reporting. The passive form 押(お)し流(なが)される is particularly frequent.

RELATED ～流(なが)す COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 流(なが)し込(こ)む - to pour into
  • 洗(あら)い流(なが)す - to wash away
  • 吹(ふ)き流(なが)す - to blow away
