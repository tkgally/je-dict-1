1. to hold back, to check, to stop
To prevent something from advancing, spreading, or getting worse by firmly stopping it.
敵(てき)の攻撃(こうげき)を食(く)い止(と)める。
To hold back the enemy's attack.
感染(かんせん)拡大(かくだい)を食(く)い止(と)める対策(たいさく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Measures to stop the spread of infection are needed.
被害(ひがい)を最小限(さいしょうげん)に食(く)い止(と)めた。
The damage was kept to a minimum.
赤字(あかじ)の拡大(かくだい)を何(なん)とか食(く)い止(と)めたい。
I want to somehow stop the deficit from growing.

COMPOUND VERB: 食(く)う (to bite/eat into) + 止(と)める (to stop) = to bite into and stop (i.e., to firmly hold in place)

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Takes を to mark what is being stopped or checked.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 攻撃(こうげき)を食(く)い止(と)める (to check an attack)
  • 感染(かんせん)拡大(かくだい)を食(く)い止(と)める (to stop the spread of infection)
  • 被害(ひがい)を食(く)い止(と)める (to prevent damage)
  • 浸水(しんすい)を食(く)い止(と)める (to stop flooding)
  • 赤字(あかじ)を食(く)い止(と)める (to check losses)

USAGE NOTE: Often used in news contexts for disasters, epidemics, military situations, and economic crises. Implies urgent action to prevent something from getting worse.
