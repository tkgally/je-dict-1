嵌(は)め込(こ)む

はめこむ
verb (godan, transitive)
to fit into, to set into; to frame

Definitions

1. to fit into, to set into, to embed
To insert or place something precisely into a space or frame designed for it.
2. to frame, to entrap, to set up
To trick someone into a disadvantageous situation, often by deception or manipulation.

Examples

窓(まど)にガラスを嵌(は)め込(こ)む。
To fit glass into a window frame.
指輪(ゆびわ)に宝石(ほうせき)を嵌(は)め込(こ)んだ。
A gemstone was set into the ring.
パズルのピースを嵌(は)め込(こ)む。
To fit a puzzle piece into place.
彼(かれ)は詐欺師(さぎし)に嵌(は)め込(こ)まれた。
He was set up by a con artist.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB: 嵌(は)める (to fit, to insert) + 込(こ)む (to go into) = to fit firmly into place

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Takes を to mark what is being fitted in, and に to mark where it's being fitted.

KANJI NOTE: 嵌(は)める can also be written in hiragana as はめる in everyday use. The kanji 嵌(はま) is not commonly known.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 宝石(ほうせき)を嵌(は)め込(こ)む (to set a gemstone)
  • ガラスを嵌(は)め込(こ)む (to fit glass into a frame)
  • 罠(わな)に嵌(は)め込(こ)む (to entrap)
  • 嵌(は)め込(こ)まれる (to be framed - passive)

SENSE 2 NUANCE: The figurative meaning of "framing" or "trapping" someone is colloquial and often used when discussing schemes or fraud. The passive form 嵌(は)め込(こ)まれる is common for victims of such schemes.

RELATED ～込(こ)む COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 追(お)い込(こ)む - to corner
  • 誘(さそ)い込(こ)む - to lure into
  • 押(お)し込(こ)む - to push into
