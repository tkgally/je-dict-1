出社(しゅっしゃ)

しゅっしゃ
noun, suru verb
going to work; coming to the office

Definitions

1. going to work; coming to the office
The act of going to one's workplace, typically an office.

Examples

毎朝(まいあさ)9時(じ)に出社(しゅっしゃ)する。
I go to work at 9 o'clock every morning.
今日(きょう)は出社(しゅっしゃ)せずに在宅(ざいたく)で働(はたら)く。
I'll work from home today instead of going to the office.
出社(しゅっしゃ)時間(じかん)が早(はや)くなった。
The time I go to work has become earlier.

Notes

COMPONENTS:
出(しゅつ) (to go out) + 社(しゃ) (company)

CONTRAST WITH 退社(たいしゃ):

  • 出社(しゅっしゃ): going to work/office
  • 退社(たいしゃ): leaving work or resigning from a company

REGISTER:
Neutral. Standard workplace vocabulary.

MODERN USAGE:
With the rise of remote work, 出社(しゅっしゃ) is often contrasted with 在宅勤務(ざいたくきんむ) (working from home) or リモートワーク (remote work).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出社(しゅっしゃ)する: to go to work
  • 出社(しゅっしゃ)時間(じかん): arrival time at work
  • 出社(しゅっしゃ)日(び): days in the office
  • 週(しゅう)に何日(なんにち)出社(しゅっしゃ): how many days a week in the office

Related Words

Antonym: 退社(たいしゃ) (leaving work)
