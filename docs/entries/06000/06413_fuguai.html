不具合(ふぐあい)

ふぐあい
noun / na-adjective
defect, malfunction

Definitions

1. defect, malfunction, bug
A problem or flaw in a product, system, or process that prevents it from working correctly. Often used as a softer alternative to stronger words like 'failure' or 'defect' in customer-facing communications.
2. inconvenience, trouble
An undesirable situation or condition that causes problems or difficulties.

Examples

ソフトウェアに不具合(ふぐあい)が見(み)つかった。
A bug was found in the software.
製品(せいひん)の不具合(ふぐあい)でリコールになった。
There was a recall due to a product defect.
不具合(ふぐあい)を修正(しゅうせい)するアップデートを配信(はいしん)した。
We released an update to fix the bug.
何(なに)か不具合(ふぐあい)があればご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Please contact us if you experience any problems.

Notes

COMPOSITION: 不(ふ) (not, un-) + 具合(ぐあい) (condition, state). Literally "bad condition."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不具合(ふぐあい)が発生(はっせい)する (a malfunction occurs)
  • 不具合(ふぐあい)が見(み)つかる (a defect is found)
  • 不具合(ふぐあい)を修正(しゅうせい)する (to fix a bug)
  • 不具合(ふぐあい)を報告(ほうこく)する (to report a problem)
  • 不具合(ふぐあい)対応(たいおう) (defect handling)
  • システムの不具合(ふぐあい) (system malfunction)

USAGE NOTE: 不具合(ふぐあい) is often preferred in business and customer communication because it sounds less severe than 故障(こしょう) (breakdown), 欠陥(けっかん) (defect), or 不良(ふりょう) (defective). This is sometimes called euphemistic corporate language.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 故障(こしょう) - breakdown, malfunction (more severe)
  • バグ - bug (in software)
  • 欠陥(けっかん) - defect, flaw
