ふわっと

ふわっと
adverb
softly, lightly, gently floating
1. softly, lightly, gently floating
Describes something moving or resting in a soft, light, airy manner, like floating or drifting.
カーテンがふわっと揺(ゆ)れた。
The curtain swayed gently.
いい香(かお)りがふわっと漂(ただよ)ってきた。
A pleasant scent drifted over softly.
風船(ふうせん)がふわっと空(そら)に浮(う)かんだ。
The balloon floated softly up into the sky.
2. fluffy, soft and puffy
Describes a soft, fluffy texture or appearance.
パンケーキがふわっと焼(や)けた。
The pancakes turned out fluffy.
ふわっとした食感(しょっかん)のケーキだ。
It's a cake with a fluffy texture.
卵(たまご)をよく泡立(あわだ)てると、オムレツがふわっと仕上(しあ)がる。
If you beat the eggs well, the omelet will turn out nice and fluffy.

ONOMATOPOEIA TYPE:
Mimetic word (擬態語(ぎたいご)) describing light, soft movement or texture.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ふわっと浮(う)く (to float lightly)
  • ふわっと香(かお)る (to waft fragrantly)
  • ふわっと揺(ゆ)れる (to sway gently)
  • ふわっと仕上(しあ)がる (to turn out fluffy)

VARIATIONS:

  • ふわふわ: repeated form emphasizing fluffy texture
  • ふんわり: similar meaning, slightly more deliberate softness

COOKING USAGE:
Very common for describing desired texture of baked goods, eggs, etc.
