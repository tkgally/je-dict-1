喪中(もちゅう)

もちゅう
noun
in mourning
1. period of mourning, in mourning
The period following the death of a close family member during which one refrains from celebrations and New Year greetings.
喪中(もちゅう)なので年賀状(ねんがじょう)は出(だ)しません。
I won't send New Year's cards because I'm in mourning.
喪中(もちゅう)ハガキを十一月(じゅういちがつ)に送(おく)った。
I sent mourning notification postcards in November.
祖母(そぼ)が亡(な)くなったので、今年(ことし)は喪中(もちゅう)です。
My grandmother passed away, so I'm in mourning this year.

USAGE: 喪中(もちゅう) refers to the period of mourning, typically lasting about one year after a close family member's death. During this time, people refrain from celebratory activities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喪中(もちゅう)ハガキ/喪中(もちゅう)葉書(はがき) (mourning notification postcard)
  • 喪中(もちゅう)欠礼(けつれい) (formal term for not sending New Year greetings due to mourning)
  • 喪中(もちゅう)につき (due to being in mourning)

CUSTOMS:

  • 喪中(もちゅう)ハガキ is sent in November-early December to notify friends that you won't be sending 年賀状(ねんがじょう)
  • During 喪中(もちゅう), one avoids 正月(しょうがつ)飾(かざ)り (New Year decorations), お節料理(せちりょうり), and other celebratory activities
  • The mourning period length varies by relationship (spouse/parents: 12-13 months; grandparents: 3-6 months)

RELATED:

  • 忌中(きちゅう) (immediate mourning period, typically 49 days)
