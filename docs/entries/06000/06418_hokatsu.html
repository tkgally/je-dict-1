保活(ほかつ)

ほかつ
noun
daycare hunting

Definitions

1. daycare hunting, nursery school search
The process of searching for and securing a spot at a nursery school (保育園(ほいくえん)) or daycare facility for one's child. Often involves considerable effort due to long waiting lists in urban areas.

Examples

保活(ほかつ)を始(はじ)める。
To start daycare hunting.
保活(ほかつ)に成功(せいこう)して入園(にゅうえん)できた。
I succeeded in daycare hunting and got my child enrolled.
都市部(としぶ)では保活(ほかつ)が大変(たいへん)だ。
Daycare hunting is tough in urban areas.
保活(ほかつ)のために引(ひ)っ越(こ)しを考(かんが)えている。
We're considering moving for the sake of daycare hunting.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY: Abbreviation of 保育園(ほいくえん)活動(かつどう) (nursery school activities/search). Part of a pattern of ～活(かつ) words for various "hunting" or "search" activities.

RELATED ～活(かつ) WORDS:

  • 就活(しゅうかつ) - job hunting (就職活動(しゅうしょくかつどう))
  • 婚活(こんかつ) - spouse hunting (結婚活動(けっこんかつどう))
  • 朝活(あさかつ) - morning activities (朝活動(あさかつどう))
  • 終活(しゅうかつ) - end-of-life preparation

SOCIAL CONTEXT: 保活(ほかつ) became a major social issue in Japan, particularly in urban areas like Tokyo where daycare spots are scarce. The phrase 待機児童(たいきじどう) (children on waiting lists) is commonly heard in this context. Parents often need to apply to multiple facilities and may need to consider factors like:

  • 認可(にんか)保育園(ほいくえん) (licensed nurseries)
  • 認可外(にんかがい)保育園(ほいくえん) (unlicensed nurseries)
  • 点数(てんすう) (point system for admission priority)
  • 入園(にゅうえん)申(もう)し込(こ)み (enrollment applications)
