突(つ)き飛(と)ばす

つきとばす
verb (godan, transitive)
to push away; to shove
1. to push away; to shove
To forcefully push someone or something away from oneself.
相手(あいて)を突(つ)き飛(と)ばす。
To shove the opponent away.
彼(かれ)は邪魔者(じゃまもの)を突(つ)き飛(と)ばして逃(に)げた。
He pushed the person blocking his way aside and ran away.
2. to knock down
To push with enough force to make someone fall or stagger.
怒(いか)りで彼女(かのじょ)を突(つ)き飛(と)ばしてしまった。
I ended up shoving her in anger.

COMPOUND VERB:
突(つ)く (to thrust, to poke) + 飛(と)ばす (to make fly, to send away)

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を to mark the person/thing being pushed

ASPECT ({ている}):
突(つ)き飛(と)ばしている indicates the action is in progress.

REGISTER:
Neutral. Can be used in both casual and formal contexts.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 突(つ)き刺(さ)す: to stab
  • 蹴(け)り飛(と)ばす: to kick away
  • 殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)す: to knock down with a punch

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を突(つ)き飛(と)ばす: to push a person away
  • 力(ちから)いっぱい突(つ)き飛(と)ばす: to shove with all one's strength
