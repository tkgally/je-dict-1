ぶらんこ

ぶらんこ
noun
swing (playground equipment)
1. swing (playground equipment)
A seat suspended by ropes or chains from a frame, used for swinging back and forth.
公園(こうえん)でぶらんこに乗(の)った。
I rode on the swing in the park.
子供(こども)がぶらんこを漕(こ)いでいる。
The child is swinging on the swing.
ぶらんこから飛(と)び降(お)りないで。
Don't jump off the swing.

ぶらんこ is a playground swing.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぶらんこに乗(の)る (get on a swing)
  • ぶらんこを漕(こ)ぐ (swing on a swing)
  • ぶらんこから降(お)りる (get off a swing)
  • ぶらんこを押(お)す (push a swing)

ETYMOLOGY:
The word is believed to come from the onomatopoeia ぶらぶら (swinging/dangling motion) + こ (child-friendly suffix).
