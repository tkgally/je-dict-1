にこやか

にこやか
na-adjective
smiling, cheerful, beaming
1. smiling, cheerful, beaming
Having a pleasant, smiling expression; radiating warmth and friendliness.
店員(てんいん)さんがにこやかに対応(たいおう)してくれた。
The shop clerk served me with a smile.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつもにこやかな笑顔(えがお)をしている。
She always has a cheerful smile.
にこやかに挨拶(あいさつ)をする。
To greet someone with a smile.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: にこやかに (with a smile, cheerfully)
  • Noun form: にこやかさ (cheerfulness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • にこやかな笑顔(えがお)/表情(ひょうじょう) (smiling face/expression)
  • にこやかに迎(むか)える (to welcome with a smile)
  • にこやかに対応(たいおう)する (to respond cheerfully)

ETYMOLOGY:
Related to にこにこ (smiling onomatopoeia).

NUANCE:
Describes a warm, pleasant demeanor rather than just a smile. Often used in service contexts or to describe people with approachable personalities.

CONTRAST:

  • にこやか: warm, pleasant smile (personality trait or demeanor)
  • にこにこ: onomatopoeia for smiling (descriptive of action)
  • 明(あか)るい: bright, cheerful (more general)
