泡立(あわだ)てる

あわだてる
verb (ichidan)
to whip, to beat
1. to whip, to beat, to whisk
To vigorously stir a liquid (such as cream or eggs) to incorporate air and create foam or increase volume. A key cooking technique.
生(なま)クリームを泡立(あわだ)てる。
To whip cream.
卵白(らんぱく)を泡立(あわだ)ててメレンゲを作(つく)る。
To beat egg whites to make meringue.
泡立(あわだ)て器(き)で卵(たまご)を泡立(あわだ)てる。
To beat eggs with a whisk.
2. to lather, to foam up
To create foam or lather from soap, shampoo, or similar products.
シャンプーを手(て)で泡立(あわだ)てる。
To lather shampoo with your hands.

COMPOSITION: 泡(あわ) (bubble, foam) + 立(た)てる (to make stand/rise). Literally "to make foam rise."

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • 泡立(あわだ)てる (他動詞(たどうし)): to whip (someone whips something)
  • 泡立(あわだ)つ (自動詞(じどうし)): to foam up (something foams by itself)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生(なま)クリームを泡立(あわだ)てる (to whip cream)
  • 卵白(らんぱく)を泡立(あわだ)てる (to beat egg whites)
  • 卵(たまご)を泡立(あわだ)てる (to beat eggs)
  • しっかり泡立(あわだ)てる (to whip thoroughly)
  • 軽(かる)く泡立(あわだ)てる (to whip lightly)

COOKING TOOLS:

  • 泡立(あわだ)て器(き) (whisk)
  • ハンドミキサー (hand mixer)
  • 電動(でんどう)泡立(あわだ)て器(き) (electric whisk)

COOKING TIPS:

  • 七(しち)分(ぶ)立(だ)て (whipped to soft peaks)
  • 八(はち)分(ぶ)立(だ)て (whipped to firm peaks)
  • 角(つの)が立(た)つまで (until peaks form)

Related Words
