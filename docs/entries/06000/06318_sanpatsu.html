散髪(さんぱつ)

さんぱつ
noun
haircut
1. haircut
The act of cutting hair, typically at a barber shop or hair salon.
散髪(さんぱつ)に行(い)ってくる。
I'm going to get a haircut.
Common expression
散髪(さんぱつ)したばかりだ。
I just got a haircut.
月(つき)に一度(いちど)散髪(さんぱつ)している。
I get a haircut once a month.

散髪(さんぱつ) refers to a haircut, typically for men at a barbershop.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 散髪(さんぱつ)に行(い)く (go to get a haircut)
  • 散髪(さんぱつ)する (get a haircut)
  • 散髪(さんぱつ)してもらう (have someone cut one's hair)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 理髪店(りはつてん): barbershop (traditional term)
  • 床屋(とこや): barbershop (colloquial)
  • 美容院(びよういん): beauty salon (typically for women)
  • 美容室(びようしつ): beauty salon/hair salon

USAGE NOTE:
散髪(さんぱつ) is typically used for simple haircuts, especially for men. For styling, coloring, or women's hair services, terms like 美容院(びよういん) or カット are more common.
