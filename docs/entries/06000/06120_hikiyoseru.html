引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる

ひきよせる
verb (ichidan)
to draw near, to pull toward

Definitions

1. to draw near, to pull toward oneself
To physically pull something or someone closer.
2. to attract, to draw
To attract or bring something toward oneself, often used for attracting luck, success, or attention.

Examples

椅子(いす)を引(ひ)き寄(よ)せて座(すわ)った。
I pulled a chair closer and sat down.
彼女(かのじょ)を腕(うで)の中(なか)に引(ひ)き寄(よ)せた。
He pulled her into his arms.
幸運(こううん)を引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる方法(ほうほう)。
Ways to attract good luck.
Figurative - attracting abstract things

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 引(ひ)く + 寄(よ)せる. Pattern: Xを引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる (pull X closer / attract X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action or resulting state. 引(ひ)き寄(よ)せている = 'is pulling closer' or 'is attracting.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手(て)を引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる (pull someone's hand toward oneself)
  • 運(うん)を引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる (attract luck)
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる (attract attention)

POPULAR CULTURE: The phrase 「引(ひ)き寄(よ)せの法則(ほうそく)」 (law of attraction) has become popular in self-help contexts.
