1. to lure into, to entice into
To cleverly or deceptively draw someone into a place, situation, or state, often with hidden intentions.
罠(わな)に誘(さそ)い込(こ)んだ。
They lured them into a trap.
犯人(はんにん)は被害者(ひがいしゃ)を罠(わな)に誘(さそ)い込(こ)んだ。
The criminal lured the victim into a trap.
詐欺師(さぎし)は巧(たく)みな話術(わじゅつ)で客(きゃく)を誘(さそ)い込(こ)んで高額(こうがく)な商品(しょうひん)を売(う)りつけた。
The con artist lured customers in with skillful talk and sold them expensive products.
2. to invite into, to draw into
To naturally or gradually cause someone to enter a particular state or activity (such as sleep).
眠(ねむ)りに誘(さそ)い込(こ)む音楽(おんがく)。
Music that lulls you to sleep.
心地(ここち)よい音楽(おんがく)が眠(ねむ)りに誘(さそ)い込(こ)む。
The soothing music lulls you into sleep.
暖(あたた)かい部屋(へや)と静(しず)かな雨音(あまおと)が、いつの間(ま)にか深(ふか)い眠(ねむ)りに誘(さそ)い込(こ)んでいた。
The warm room and quiet sound of rain had lulled me into a deep sleep before I knew it.

USAGE: 誘(さそ)い込(こ)む is a compound verb combining 誘(さそ)う (to invite, to tempt) and 込(こ)む (into). It often implies drawing someone into something with a degree of intention or manipulation.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (takes を for the person/thing being lured, に for the destination)

ASPECT: 誘(さそ)い込(こ)んでいる indicates ongoing enticement or habitual luring behavior.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罠(わな)に誘(さそ)い込(こ)む (to lure into a trap)
  • 眠(ねむ)りに誘(さそ)い込(こ)む (to lull into sleep)
  • 世界(せかい)に誘(さそ)い込(こ)む (to draw into a world)

COMPOUND VERB PATTERN: ～込(こ)む indicates movement into or being drawn into something.
