1. to grate, to grind
To reduce food such as vegetables or spices to fine pieces using a grater or similar tool, typically moving in a back-and-forth or circular motion.
大根(だいこん)をすり下(お)ろす。
I grate daikon radish.
生姜(しょうが)をすり下(お)ろして薬味(やくみ)にする。
I grate ginger to use as a condiment.
リンゴをすり下(お)ろして離乳食(りにゅうしょく)を作(つく)った。
I grated an apple to make baby food.

USAGE: すり下(お)ろす is a godan verb commonly used in cooking contexts. It is a compound verb combining すり (from 擦(す)る, to rub) and 下(お)ろす (to move downward), describing the downward rubbing motion of grating.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (takes を)

ASPECT: すり下(お)ろしている indicates the action of grating in progress.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大根(だいこん)をすり下(お)ろす (to grate daikon radish)
  • 生姜(しょうが)をすり下(お)ろす (to grate ginger)
  • 山葵(わさび)をすり下(お)ろす (to grate wasabi)
  • 人参(にんじん)をすり下(お)ろす (to grate carrot)
  • 林檎(りんご)をすり下(お)ろす (to grate apple)

KANJI NOTE: Often written in hiragana as すりおろす. Can also be written as 擦(す)り下(お)ろす.

RELATED NOUN: すり下(お)ろし refers to the grated food itself, especially 大根(だいこん)おろし (grated daikon).
