突(つ)き刺(さ)す

つきさす
verb (godan)
to stab, to pierce

Definitions

1. to stab, to pierce
To thrust a sharp object into something, penetrating it.
2. to pierce emotionally
For words or gaze to cut deeply, affecting someone's emotions.

Examples

フォークで肉(にく)を突(つ)き刺(さ)す。
To stab the meat with a fork.
鋭(するど)いナイフが壁(かべ)に突(つ)き刺(さ)さっていた。
A sharp knife was stuck in the wall.
Using the intransitive form 突(つ)き刺(さ)さる
その言葉(ことば)が胸(むね)に突(つ)き刺(さ)さった。
Those words pierced my heart.
Figurative usage with intransitive form

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair verb: 突(つ)き刺(さ)さる (intransitive, to be stuck/pierced).

COMPOUND STRUCTURE: 突(つ)く (to poke/thrust) + 刺(さ)す (to pierce). The combination emphasizes the forceful penetrating action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ナイフで突(つ)き刺(さ)す (stab with a knife)
  • フォークで突(つ)き刺(さ)す (pierce with a fork)
  • 心(こころ)に突(つ)き刺(さ)さる (pierce the heart - figurative)
  • 胸(むね)に突(つ)き刺(さ)さる (pierce the chest/heart - figurative)

ASPECT: The intransitive form 突(つ)き刺(さ)さっている indicates a resultant state (something remains stuck).
