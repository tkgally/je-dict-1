間取(まど)り

まどり
noun
floor plan, layout
1. floor plan, room layout, arrangement of rooms
The layout showing how rooms are arranged in a house or apartment, typically expressed using abbreviations like 1LDK or 2DK.
この部屋(へや)の間取(まど)りは2LDKです。
The floor plan of this apartment is 2LDK.
2LDK = 2 rooms + Living/Dining/Kitchen
間取(まど)り図(ず)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me the floor plan.
使(つか)いやすい間取(まど)りの家(いえ)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a house with a practical layout.

COMPOUND: 間(ま) (space, room) + 取(と)り (arrangement)

JAPANESE HOUSING ABBREVIATIONS:

  • 1K = 1 room + Kitchen
  • 1DK = 1 room + Dining Kitchen
  • 1LDK = 1 room + Living Dining Kitchen
  • 2LDK = 2 rooms + Living Dining Kitchen
  • 3LDK = 3 rooms + Living Dining Kitchen

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 間取(まど)り図(ず) (floor plan diagram)
  • 広(ひろ)い間取(まど)り (spacious layout)
  • 間取(まど)りを変(か)える (change the layout)

NOTE: 間取(まど)り is essential vocabulary when apartment hunting in Japan.
