1. wild joy, ecstasy, rapture
Extreme and unrestrained happiness or excitement, often to the point of losing self-control.
ファンは狂喜(きょうき)乱舞(らんぶ)した。
The fans went into a frenzy of joy.
優勝(ゆうしょう)に狂喜(きょうき)する選手(せんしゅ)たち。
Players going wild with joy at the championship victory.
宝(たから)くじで一等(いっとう)が当(あ)たって、家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)で狂喜(きょうき)した。
We won first prize in the lottery, and the whole family went wild with joy.

COMPOSITION: 狂(きょう) (mad, crazy) + 喜(き) (joy, happiness). Literally "mad with joy."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狂喜(きょうき)する (to be ecstatic, to go wild with joy)
  • 狂喜(きょうき)乱舞(らんぶ) (dancing with wild joy - a set phrase)
  • 狂喜(きょうき)の余(あま)り (out of sheer ecstasy)

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM:

  • 狂喜(きょうき)乱舞(らんぶ): To dance with wild joy. Describes extreme excitement where one loses control. Often used for sports victories or major celebrations.

REGISTER: Formal/literary. Used in written language and formal speech more than casual conversation.

CONTRAST:

  • 狂喜(きょうき): wild, uncontrolled joy (intense)
  • 歓喜(かんき): great joy, delight (strong but more composed)
  • 喜(よろこ)び: joy, happiness (neutral)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 歓喜(かんき) (great joy, jubilation)
  • 有頂天(うちょうてん) (being on cloud nine)
  • 大喜(おおよろこ)び (great happiness)
