論理(ろんり)

ろんり
noun
logic; reasoning

Definitions

1. logic; reasoning; rationale
The principles of valid reasoning and argumentation. The systematic study of correct inference.

Examples

その論理(ろんり)は通(とお)らない。
That logic doesn't hold up.
論理的(ろんりてき)に考(かんが)える。
Think logically.
論理(ろんり)に飛躍(ひやく)がある。
There's a logical leap in the argument.

Notes

論理(ろんり) is fundamental to academic and philosophical discourse.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical
  • 論理(ろんり)が通(とお)る: the logic holds
  • 論理(ろんり)に飛躍(ひやく)がある: there's a logical leap
  • 論理(ろんり)を組(く)み立(た)てる: to construct an argument

RELATED TERMS:

  • 論理学(ろんりがく): logic (as a field of study)
  • 論理性(ろんりせい): logicality
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき): illogical
