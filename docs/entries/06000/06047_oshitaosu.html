押(お)し倒(たお)す

おしたおす
verb (godan)
to push down, to knock over
1. to push down, to knock over, to push someone to the ground
To forcefully push something or someone so that they fall over or are pinned down.
風(かぜ)で木(き)が押(お)し倒(たお)された。
The tree was knocked over by the wind.
Passive form
相手(あいて)を押(お)し倒(たお)して勝(か)った。
I won by pushing my opponent down.
Sports/wrestling context
群衆(ぐんしゅう)が押(お)し倒(たお)されそうになった。
The crowd was about to be knocked down.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 押(お)す + 倒(たお)す. Pattern: Xを押(お)し倒(たお)す (push X down).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 押(お)し倒(たお)されている = 'is knocked down' or 'is pinned.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 相手(あいて)を押(お)し倒(たお)す (push one's opponent down)
  • 力(ちから)ずくで押(お)し倒(たお)す (forcefully push down)

SUMO: In sumo wrestling, 「押(お)し倒(たお)し」 is an official winning technique where you push your opponent down onto the ground.
