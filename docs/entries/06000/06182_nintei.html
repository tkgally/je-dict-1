認定(にんてい)

にんてい
noun / suru verb
certification, authorization

Definitions

1. certification, authorization
Official recognition or approval that someone or something meets certain standards or qualifications.
2. acknowledgment, recognition
Formally recognizing or determining that something is the case.

Examples

資格(しかく)認定(にんてい)試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)した。
I passed the certification exam.
この製品(せいひん)はISO認定(にんてい)を受(う)けている。
This product has received ISO certification.
労災(ろうさい)として認定(にんてい)された。
It was recognized as a work-related injury.

Notes

USAGE:
認定(にんてい) is used in formal contexts for official recognition, certification, or authorization. It often appears in compound words related to qualifications and standards.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 認定(にんてい)を受(う)ける (receive certification)
  • 認定(にんてい)試験(しけん) (certification exam)
  • 認定(にんてい)機関(きかん) (certification body)
  • として認定(にんてい)される (be recognized as)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 認定(にんてい)こども園(えん) (certified childcare center)
  • 国家(こっか)認定(にんてい) (nationally certified)
  • 認定(にんてい)医(い) (certified physician)
