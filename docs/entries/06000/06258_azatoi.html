あざとい

あざとい
i-adjective
cunning, calculating; cutely manipulative
1. cunning, calculating, crafty
Acting in a scheming or manipulative way to achieve one's goals. Traditional meaning with negative connotation.
2. cutely manipulative, knowingly cute
Deliberately acting cute or charming to get what one wants. Modern usage, often used semi-positively or playfully.
あざとい手口(てぐち)で客(きゃく)を騙(だま)す。
To deceive customers with cunning tactics.
あざとい女(おんな)だと思(おも)われたくない。
I don't want to be seen as a calculating woman.
あざといポーズで写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る。
To take a photo with a knowingly cute pose.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: あざとく (cunningly)
  • Noun form: あざとさ (cunning quality)

SEMANTIC SHIFT:

Traditionally negative (sense 1), but since around 2010s, sense 2 emerged in youth culture, particularly regarding deliberately cute behavior in fashion, poses, and social media. This newer usage can be:

  • Negative: criticizing calculated behavior
  • Neutral/Positive: acknowledging skillful self-presentation

MODERN EXPRESSIONS:

  • あざとかわいい: cunningly cute (blend of あざとい + かわいい)
  • あざとい仕草(しぐさ): knowingly cute gestures

KANJI FORM:
小聡明(あざと)い is the historical kanji form but almost never used.
