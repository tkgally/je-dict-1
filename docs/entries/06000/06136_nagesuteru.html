1. to throw away; to toss aside
To discard something by throwing it, often carelessly.
ゴミを投(な)げ捨(す)てるな。
Don't throw trash away (on the ground).
空(あ)き缶(かん)を道端(みちばた)に投(な)げ捨(す)てる。
To toss an empty can on the roadside.
読(よ)み終(お)わった新聞(しんぶん)を投(な)げ捨(す)てて部屋(へや)を出(で)た。
He tossed aside the newspaper he had finished reading and left the room.
2. to abandon; to give up on
Figuratively, to completely give up or discard something abstract like hopes, dreams, or responsibilities.
夢(ゆめ)を投(な)げ捨(す)てる。
To give up on one's dreams.
彼(かれ)は仕事(しごと)を投(な)げ捨(す)てて旅(たび)に出(で)た。
He abandoned his job and went traveling.
全(すべ)てを投(な)げ捨(す)てたくなることがあるが、踏(ふ)みとどまっている。
There are times when I want to throw everything away, but I hold on.

COMPOUND VERB:
投(な)げる (to throw) + 捨(す)てる (to discard)

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を to mark the object being thrown away

ASPECT (ている):
投(な)げ捨(す)てている indicates the ongoing state after throwing away or a habitual action.

REGISTER:
Neutral. Used in both casual and formal contexts.

NUANCE:
Implies a careless or deliberate act of discarding. The figurative sense (sense 2) carries emotional weight, suggesting frustration or a decisive break.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴミを投(な)げ捨(す)てる: to throw away garbage
  • 責任(せきにん)を投(な)げ捨(す)てる: to abandon responsibility
  • 夢(ゆめ)を投(な)げ捨(す)てる: to give up on one's dreams
