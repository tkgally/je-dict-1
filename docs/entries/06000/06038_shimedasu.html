閉(し)め出(だ)す

しめだす
verb (godan)
to lock out, to shut out
1. to lock out, to shut out
To prevent someone from entering by closing and locking a door. Can refer to accidentally locking oneself out.
鍵(かぎ)を忘(わす)れて家(いえ)から閉(し)め出(だ)されてしまった。
I forgot my keys and got locked out of the house.
Passive form - common usage
猫(ねこ)を外(そと)に閉(し)め出(だ)さないでください。
Please don't lock the cat out.
2. to exclude, to shut out
To exclude someone from participation, membership, or involvement in something.
彼(かれ)はグループから閉(し)め出(だ)された。
He was shut out of the group.
Figurative usage - exclusion

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 閉(し)める + 出(だ)す. Pattern: Xを閉(し)め出(だ)す (lock X out).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 閉(し)め出(だ)されている = 'is locked out.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家(いえ)から閉(し)め出(だ)される (get locked out of the house)
  • 会議(かいぎ)から閉(し)め出(だ)す (shut out from a meeting)
  • オートロックで閉(し)め出(だ)される (get locked out by the auto-lock)

NOTE: Often used in passive form (閉(し)め出(だ)される) when one is the victim of being locked out.
