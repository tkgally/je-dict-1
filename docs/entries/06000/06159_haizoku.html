1. assignment; placement
The act of assigning someone to a particular department, location, or position within an organization.
営業部(えいぎょうぶ)に配属(はいぞく)された。
I was assigned to the sales department.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)の配属(はいぞく)が決(き)まった。
The assignments for new employees have been decided.
配属先(はいぞくさき)は希望(きぼう)どおりになった。
My assignment turned out to be what I had hoped for.

COMPONENTS:
配(はい) (to distribute, to assign) + 属(ぞく) (to belong to)

JAPANESE CORPORATE CULTURE:
In Japanese companies, new graduates (新卒(しんそつ)) are often hired through mass recruitment and then assigned (配属(はいぞく)) to different departments. This assignment is often made by the company rather than by the employee's choice.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 異動(いどう): job transfer, personnel change
  • 転勤(てんきん): relocation, transfer to another office
  • 人事(じんじ): personnel, HR
  • 部署(ぶしょ): department

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に配属(はいぞく)される: to be assigned to ~
  • 配属先(はいぞくさき): place of assignment
  • 配属(はいぞく)が決(き)まる: assignment is decided
  • 初期(しょき)配属(はいぞく): initial assignment
