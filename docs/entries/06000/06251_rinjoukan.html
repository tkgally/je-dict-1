臨場感(りんじょうかん)

りんじょうかん
noun
sense of presence; sense of being there
1. sense of presence; sense of being there
The feeling of actually being present at or in a scene, especially when experiencing media like movies, games, or audio systems.
この映画(えいが)は臨場感(りんじょうかん)がすごい。
This movie has an amazing sense of presence.
VRゲームは臨場感(りんじょうかん)が高(たか)い。
VR games have a high sense of immersion.
臨場感(りんじょうかん)あふれる実況(じっきょう)に興奮(こうふん)した。
I got excited by the commentary that was full of vivid realism.

FORMATION:
臨場(りんじょう) (being on the scene) + 感(かん) (feeling)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 臨場感(りんじょうかん)がある: to have a sense of presence
  • 臨場感(りんじょうかん)を味(あじ)わう: to experience the sense of being there
  • 臨場感(りんじょうかん)あふれる: full of vivid realism
  • 臨場感(りんじょうかん)が高(たか)い: having high immersion

CONTEXT:

Frequently used when discussing:

  • Movies, especially with surround sound or 3D
  • Video games, especially VR
  • Audio equipment (speakers, headphones)
  • Live broadcasts and sports commentary
  • Theme park attractions

ENGLISH EQUIVALENTS:
Often translated as "sense of presence," "immersion," "realism," or "you-are-there feeling" depending on context.
