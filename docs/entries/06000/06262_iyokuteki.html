意欲的(いよくてき)

いよくてき
na-adjective
ambitious, eager, motivated
1. ambitious, eager, motivated
Showing strong motivation and enthusiasm; actively seeking to achieve or improve.
彼(かれ)は意欲的(いよくてき)に仕事(しごと)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
He is tackling work with great motivation.
意欲的(いよくてき)な学生(がくせい)を求(もと)めています。
We are looking for motivated students.
新(あたら)しいプロジェクトに意欲的(いよくてき)に参加(さんか)した。
I eagerly participated in the new project.
意欲的(いよくてき)な姿勢(しせい)が評価(ひょうか)された。
The motivated attitude was highly evaluated.

USAGE: 意欲的(いよくてき) is commonly used in work and academic contexts to describe someone who actively engages with tasks. Can be used as な-adjective or adverbially with に.

NOUN: 意欲(いよく) (motivation, enthusiasm, will)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 意欲的(いよくてき)に取(と)り組(く)む (tackle enthusiastically)
  • 意欲的(いよくてき)な姿勢(しせい) (motivated attitude)
  • 意欲的(いよくてき)な作品(さくひん) (ambitious work)
  • 意欲的(いよくてき)に挑戦(ちょうせん)する (eagerly take on challenges)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 積極的(せっきょくてき): Proactive, assertive (emphasis on taking initiative)
  • 意欲的(いよくてき): Ambitious, motivated (emphasis on strong desire)
  • やる気(き)がある: To be motivated (casual expression)
