張(は)り付(つ)く

はりつく
verb (godan)
to stick to, to cling
1. to stick to, to cling to, to adhere
To become firmly attached to a surface. Used for physical adhesion.
汗(あせ)でシャツが背中(せなか)に張(は)り付(つ)いた。
My shirt stuck to my back from sweat.
ポスターが壁(かべ)に張(は)り付(つ)いている。
A poster is stuck to the wall.
2. to stay close to, to be glued to
To remain constantly in one place or position, often watching something intently.
一日中(いちにちじゅう)パソコンの前(まえ)に張(は)り付(つ)いている。
I'm glued to my computer all day.
Figurative - staying in one place

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Compound verb: 張(は)る + 付(つ)く. Pattern: Xに張(は)り付(つ)く (stick to X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 張(は)り付(つ)いている = 'is stuck' or 'is clinging.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 肌(はだ)に張(は)り付(つ)く (stick to the skin)
  • 画面(がめん)に張(は)り付(つ)く (be glued to the screen)
  • ぴったり張(は)り付(つ)く (stick closely)

PAIR: 張(は)り付(つ)ける (他動詞(たどうし)) means 'to stick something on.'

Related Words

Pair verb: 張(は)り付(つ)ける (transitive)
