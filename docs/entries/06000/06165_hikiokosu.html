引(ひ)き起(お)こす

ひきおこす
verb (godan)
to cause, to give rise to
1. to cause, to give rise to, to trigger
To bring about an event, situation, or condition, often something problematic.
不注意(ふちゅうい)が事故(じこ)を引(ひ)き起(お)こした。
Carelessness caused the accident.
この薬(くすり)は副作用(ふくさよう)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす可能性(かのうせい)がある。
This medicine may cause side effects.
経済危機(けいざいきき)が社会不安(しゃかいふあん)を引(ひ)き起(お)こしている。
The economic crisis is causing social unrest.
2. to help up, to raise up
To physically help someone who has fallen to stand up.
転(ころ)んだ子供(こども)を引(ひ)き起(お)こしてあげた。
I helped the child who had fallen get up.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 引(ひ)く + 起(お)こす. Pattern: Xを引(ひ)き起(お)こす (cause X / help X up).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing situation or resulting state. 引(ひ)き起(お)こしている = 'is causing' or 'has caused.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 問題(もんだい)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす (cause a problem)
  • 事故(じこ)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす (cause an accident)
  • 反発(はんぱつ)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす (provoke opposition)
  • 副作用(ふくさよう)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす (cause side effects)

NOTE: Sense 1 (to cause) is much more common than sense 2 (to help up). Often used for negative events or consequences.
