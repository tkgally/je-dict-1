ぶーぶー

ぶーぶー
adverb, noun
honking; oinking; complaining
1. honking sound; car horn
The sound of a car horn or similar noise. Also used as baby talk for 'car.'
2. oinking; pig sound
The sound a pig makes.
3. complaining; grumbling
The action of complaining or expressing dissatisfaction repeatedly.
車(くるま)がぶーぶー鳴(な)っている。
The car is honking.
豚(ぶた)さんはぶーぶー鳴(な)くよ。
Piggies go oink oink.
ぶーぶー文句(もんく)を言(い)うな。
Stop complaining.

ぶーぶー has multiple meanings depending on context.

BABY TALK:

  • ぶーぶー: car (child's word)
  • ぶーぶーに乗(の)る: to ride in the car (baby talk)

COMPLAINING:

  • ぶーぶー言(い)う: to grumble, to complain
  • ぶーたれる: to sulk and complain (variant)

OTHER ANIMAL SOUNDS:

  • ワンワン: dog bark
  • ニャーニャー: cat meow
  • モーモー: cow moo
