切(せつ)ない

1. painfully bittersweet, heartrending, poignant
A complex emotional state mixing sadness with sweetness, often related to longing, nostalgia, or unrequited feelings.
彼女(かのじょ)のことを思(おも)い出(だ)すと切(せつ)ない気持(きも)ちになる。
When I think of her, I feel a bittersweet ache.
この映画(えいが)の結末(けつまつ)は切(せつ)なくて泣(な)いてしまった。
The ending of this movie was so heartrending that I cried.
切(せつ)ない恋(こい)の歌(うた)が流(なが)れていた。
A song of bittersweet love was playing.
胸(むね)が切(せつ)なくなるほど会(あ)いたい。
I miss you so much it makes my heart ache.
胸(むね)が切(せつ)ない is a common collocation

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 切(せつ)なく
  • Noun form: 切(せつ)なさ (the feeling of bittersweet longing)

切(せつ)ない describes a nuanced emotional state that combines sadness with a bittersweet quality. It is often used for:

  • Unrequited or lost love
  • Nostalgic longing for the past
  • Poignant moments in stories or films

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 胸(むね)が切(せつ)ない (heartache)
  • 切(せつ)ない思(おも)い (bittersweet feelings)
  • 切(せつ)ない恋(こい) (bittersweet love)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悲(かな)しい: Pure sadness, less nuanced
  • 切(せつ)ない: Sadness mixed with longing or sweetness
  • 寂(さび)しい: Loneliness, missing someone
