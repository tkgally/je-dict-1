ガジェット

がじぇっと
noun
gadget, device
1. gadget, electronic device
A small electronic device or piece of technology, especially one that is innovative, clever, or useful. Often refers to smartphones, smartwatches, portable speakers, and similar devices.
新(あたら)しいガジェットが出(で)るとすぐ買(か)ってしまう。
I end up buying new gadgets as soon as they come out.
このガジェットは旅行(りょこう)に便利(べんり)だ。
This gadget is convenient for traveling.
ガジェット好(ず)きな彼(かれ)は最新(さいしん)の機器(きき)に詳(くわ)しい。
He's a gadget enthusiast and knows a lot about the latest devices.

SCOPE:
In Japanese, ガジェット typically refers to portable electronic devices like smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, wireless earbuds, portable chargers, etc. It emphasizes novelty and technological innovation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ガジェット好(ず)き (gadget enthusiast)
  • 最新(さいしん)ガジェット (latest gadget)
  • ガジェットレビュー (gadget review)
  • ガジェット系(けい) (gadget-related)

RELATED TERMS:

  • デバイス: device (more technical/formal)
  • 機器(きき): equipment, device (formal)
  • 端末(たんまつ): terminal, device (technical)
