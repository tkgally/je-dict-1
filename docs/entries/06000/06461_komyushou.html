コミュ障(しょう)

こみゅしょう
noun
socially awkward person, poor communicator (slang)
1. socially awkward person
Slang term for someone who has difficulty communicating or socializing with others. Abbreviation of コミュニケーション障害(しょうがい) (communication disorder), but used casually and often self-deprecatingly.
私(わたし)はコミュ障(しょう)だから、初対面(しょたいめん)の人(ひと)と話(はな)すのが苦手(にがて)。
I'm socially awkward, so I have trouble talking to people I've just met.
コミュ障(しょう)でも働(はたら)きやすい仕事(しごと)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a job that's easy to work at even if you're socially awkward.
「コミュ障(しょう)」は自虐(じぎゃく)ネタとしてよく使(つか)われる。
"Komyushou" is often used as self-deprecating humor.

ETYMOLOGY: Abbreviation of コミュニケーション障害(しょうがい) (communication disorder). The clinical term has been adopted as casual slang with a much broader, less clinical meaning.

REGISTER: Casual/Slang
Primarily used among young people. Often used self-deprecatingly. The clinical origin means this term should be used carefully, as it can be insensitive to people with actual communication disorders.

USAGE NOTES:

  • Very commonly used for self-description
  • Can be seen as insensitive when used to describe others
  • Often appears in discussions about social anxiety or introversion
  • Sometimes written as コミュしょう with hiragana

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コミュ障(しょう)気味(ぎみ) (somewhat socially awkward)
  • 重度(じゅうど)のコミュ障(しょう) (severely socially awkward - humorous)
  • コミュ障(しょう)を克服(こくふく)する (to overcome social awkwardness)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 人見知(ひとみし)り: shy around strangers (more neutral)
  • 内向的(ないこうてき): introverted (formal, clinical)
  • コミュ力(りょく): communication skills (abbreviated)
