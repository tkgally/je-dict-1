1. to jump across, to leap to
To move from one place to another by jumping, often across a gap or between elevated surfaces.
猿(さる)が木(き)から木(き)へ飛(と)び移(うつ)る。
Monkeys leap from tree to tree.
船(ふね)から岸(きし)に飛(と)び移(うつ)った。
I jumped from the boat to the shore.
リスが屋根(やね)から屋根(やね)へ飛(と)び移(うつ)っていた。
Squirrels were leaping from roof to roof.
2. to spread (fire, disease), to jump to
For something like fire or disease to spread by jumping from one place or host to another.
火(ひ)が隣(となり)の家(いえ)に飛(と)び移(うつ)った。
The fire spread to the neighboring house.
ウイルスが新(あたら)しい宿主(しゅくしゅ)に飛(と)び移(うつ)る。
The virus jumps to a new host.
強風(きょうふう)で火(ひ)の粉(こ)が飛(と)び移(うつ)り、次々(つぎつぎ)と家(いえ)が燃(も)えた。
Sparks spread from house to house in the strong wind, and homes burned one after another.

COMPOUND VERB: 飛(と)ぶ (to fly, jump) + 移(うつ)る (to move, transfer) = to jump and move to another place

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive. Uses に or へ to mark the destination, and から to mark the origin.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 木(き)から木(き)へ飛(と)び移(うつ)る (to leap from tree to tree)
  • 火(ひ)が飛(と)び移(うつ)る (fire spreads)
  • 屋根(やね)から屋根(やね)へ飛(と)び移(うつ)る (to jump from roof to roof)

USAGE NOTE: In sense 2, this verb is commonly used in news and scientific contexts to describe the spread of fire or infectious diseases.

RELATED 飛(と)び～ COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 飛(と)び上(あ)がる - to jump up
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)む - to jump into, to dive in
  • 飛(と)び出(だ)す - to jump out, to rush out
  • 飛(と)び越(こ)える - to jump over
