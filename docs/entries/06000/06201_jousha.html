1. boarding, getting on (a train, bus, car)
The act of getting on a ground vehicle such as a train, bus, or car.
乗車(じょうしゃ)拒否(きょひ)された。
I was refused boarding.
乗車(じょうしゃ)位置(いち)は黄色(きいろ)い線(せん)の内側(うちがわ)です。
The boarding position is inside the yellow line.
乗車(じょうしゃ)する前(まえ)に切符(きっぷ)を買(か)ってください。
Please buy a ticket before boarding.

USAGE: 乗車(じょうしゃ) is used for getting on trains, buses, and cars. For aircraft, use 搭乗(とうじょう) instead.

AS A SURU VERB: 乗車(じょうしゃ)する (to board)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乗車券(じょうしゃけん) (train ticket, boarding ticket)
  • 乗車(じょうしゃ)位置(いち) (boarding position)
  • 乗車(じょうしゃ)拒否(きょひ) (refusal of boarding)
  • 乗車(じょうしゃ)時間(じかん) (boarding time)
  • 乗車(じょうしゃ)定員(ていいん) (passenger capacity)

ANTONYM: 降車(こうしゃ) (getting off, alighting)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 搭乗(とうじょう) - boarding (aircraft, ship)
  • 下車(げしゃ) - alighting, getting off
