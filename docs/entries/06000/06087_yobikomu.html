1. to call in, to invite in
To summon someone to come inside or to a specific place.
中(なか)に呼(よ)び込(こ)む。
To call someone in.
部屋(へや)に友達(ともだち)を呼(よ)び込(こ)んだ。
I called my friend into the room.
店員(てんいん)が客(きゃく)を店(みせ)の中(なか)に呼(よ)び込(こ)んでいる。
The store clerk is calling customers into the shop.
医者(いしゃ)が次(つぎ)の患者(かんじゃ)を診察室(しんさつしつ)に呼(よ)び込(こ)んだ。
The doctor called the next patient into the examination room.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り出(だ)したので、外(そと)で遊(あそ)んでいた子(こ)どもたちを家(いえ)の中(なか)に呼(よ)び込(こ)んだ。
It started to rain, so I called the children who were playing outside into the house.
2. to attract, to draw in
To bring in customers, visitors, or attention through active promotion or appeal.
客(きゃく)を呼(よ)び込(こ)む。
To attract customers.
イベントで多(おお)くの観光客(かんこうきゃく)を呼(よ)び込(こ)む。
To attract many tourists with an event.
新(あたら)しいサービスで若(わか)い世代(せだい)を呼(よ)び込(こ)みたい。
We want to attract the younger generation with new services.
この地域(ちいき)は自然(しぜん)を生(い)かした観光(かんこう)で人(ひと)を呼(よ)び込(こ)もうとしている。
This area is trying to attract people through nature-based tourism.
駅前(えきまえ)に新(あたら)しい商業施設(しょうぎょうしせつ)ができて、周辺(しゅうへん)の店(みせ)にも客(きゃく)を呼(よ)び込(こ)んでいる。
A new commercial facility opened in front of the station, attracting customers to the surrounding shops as well.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE: 呼(よ)ぶ (to call) + 込(こ)む (into). The ～込(こ)む suffix adds the meaning of drawing inward.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 客(きゃく)を呼(よ)び込(こ)む (attract customers)
  • 観光客(かんこうきゃく)を呼(よ)び込(こ)む (attract tourists)
  • 投資(とうし)を呼(よ)び込(こ)む (attract investment)

Usage note: Often used in business contexts for marketing and sales activities. The related noun 呼(よ)び込(こ)み refers to the act of soliciting customers on the street.
