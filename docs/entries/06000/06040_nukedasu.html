抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す

ぬけだす
verb (godan)
to slip out, to escape
1. to slip out, to sneak out
To leave a place quietly or secretly without being noticed.
会議(かいぎ)の途中(とちゅう)でこっそり抜(ぬ)け出(だ)した。
I slipped out quietly in the middle of the meeting.
2. to escape, to get out of
To free oneself from a difficult situation, bad habit, or state.
不況(ふきょう)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)すのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to escape from the recession.
Figurative usage
悪循環(あくじゅんかん)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)せない。
I can't break out of the vicious cycle.
Potential negative form

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Compound verb: 抜(ぬ)ける + 出(だ)す. Pattern: Xから抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す (slip out of X / escape from X).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed action or resulting state. 抜(ぬ)け出(だ)している = 'has escaped' or 'is out.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す (slip out of the room)
  • スランプから抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す (break out of a slump)
  • こっそり抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す (sneak out)

SIMILAR WORDS: 逃(に)げ出(だ)す emphasizes fleeing from danger, while 抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す emphasizes quietly leaving or escaping a state.
