切(き)り抜(ぬ)く

きりぬく
verb (godan)
to cut out, to pull through

Definitions

1. to cut out, to clip
To cut something out from paper, fabric, or other material.
2. to pull through, to get through, to survive
To overcome a difficult situation or crisis through perseverance.

Examples

新聞(しんぶん)の記事(きじ)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)いた。
I clipped out a newspaper article.
困難(こんなん)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)けた。
I pulled through the difficulties.
Figurative - overcoming challenges
なんとかこの危機(きき)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)こう。
Let's somehow get through this crisis.
Volitional form

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 切(き)る + 抜(ぬ)く. Pattern: Xを切(き)り抜(ぬ)く (cut out X / get through X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 切(き)り抜(ぬ)いている = 'has cut out' or 'has survived.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 写真(しゃしん)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)く (cut out a photo)
  • 困難(こんなん)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)く (pull through difficulties)
  • 切(き)り抜(ぬ)き (clipping; noun form)

MODERN USAGE: 「切(き)り抜(ぬ)き動画(どうが)」 refers to video clips extracted from longer content, popular on social media.

NOTE: Sometimes written/pronounced as 切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける (ichidan) with similar meaning for sense 2.
