波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)

はらんばんじょう
na-adjective, noun, expression
eventful, turbulent
1. eventful, turbulent, full of ups and downs
A four-character idiom describing a life or story full of dramatic changes, difficulties, and unexpected events.
彼女(かのじょ)は波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)の人生(じんせい)を送(おく)った。
She led an eventful life full of ups and downs.
波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)な展開(てんかい)のドラマだ。
It's a drama with a turbulent plot.
その会社(かいしゃ)は波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)の歴史(れきし)を持(も)つ。
That company has a turbulent history.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご))

LITERAL MEANING: 波乱(はらん) (turbulence, upheaval) + 万丈(ばんじょう) (ten thousand 丈(じょう) - an extremely high measure) = Waves of turbulence as high as mountains.

FORMS:

  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)な＋noun (attributive)
  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)の＋noun (attributive - more common)
  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)だ (predicative)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)の人生(じんせい) (an eventful life)
  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)な展開(てんかい) (turbulent developments)
  • 波乱万丈(はらんばんじょう)の生涯(しょうがい) (a turbulent lifetime)

NUANCE: Often used with a somewhat positive connotation, suggesting a life that was interesting and full of experiences, even if difficult. Frequently used for biographies, autobiographies, and dramatic narratives.

RELATED IDIOM: 波瀾万丈(はらんばんじょう) is an alternative spelling using 瀾(らん) instead of 乱(らん).
