壊滅的(かいめつてき)

かいめつてき
na-adjective
devastating, catastrophic
1. devastating, catastrophic, annihilating
Causing complete destruction or ruin; so severe as to make recovery extremely difficult.
台風(たいふう)は壊滅的(かいめつてき)な被害(ひがい)をもたらした。
The typhoon caused devastating damage.
経済(けいざい)に壊滅的(かいめつてき)な打撃(だげき)を与(あた)えた。
It dealt a catastrophic blow to the economy.
壊滅的(かいめつてき)な状況(じょうきょう)から復興(ふっこう)した。
They recovered from a devastating situation.
敵(てき)軍(ぐん)に壊滅的(かいめつてき)な敗北(はいぼく)を喫(きっ)した。
They suffered a crushing defeat at the hands of the enemy.

FORMATION: 壊滅(かいめつ) (annihilation, destruction) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

FORMS:

  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な＋noun (attributive)
  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)に＋verb (adverbial)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な被害(ひがい) (devastating damage)
  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な打撃(だげき) (catastrophic blow)
  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な影響(えいきょう) (devastating impact)
  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な状況(じょうきょう) (catastrophic situation)
  • 壊滅的(かいめつてき)な敗北(はいぼく) (crushing defeat)

CONTEXT: Frequently used in news reporting about natural disasters, military conflicts, economic crises, and other severe situations.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 壊滅(かいめつ) - annihilation, destruction
  • 壊滅(かいめつ)する - to be destroyed/annihilated
  • 破壊的(はかいてき) - destructive
  • 致命的(ちめいてき) - fatal, critical
