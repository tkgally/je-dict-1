心機一転(しんきいってん)

しんきいってん
noun, adverb, suru verb
turning over a new leaf; making a fresh start
1. turning over a new leaf; making a fresh start; with renewed determination
A change in one's mental attitude or outlook, often accompanied by a determination to start anew.
心機一転(しんきいってん)、新(あたら)しい仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めることにした。
I decided to turn over a new leaf and start a new job.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しを機(き)に心機一転(しんきいってん)した。
I made a fresh start after moving to a new place.
失敗(しっぱい)から学(まな)び、心機一転(しんきいってん)して頑張(がんば)ろう。
Let's learn from our failures and start fresh with renewed determination.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)):
心機(しんき) means "mental state" or "feelings," and 一転(いってん) means "complete change" or "turning around." Together, the phrase describes a complete change in one's mindset.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 心機一転(しんきいってん)する: to make a fresh start
  • 心機一転(しんきいってん)、〜: with renewed determination, ...
  • 〜を機(き)に心機一転(しんきいってん)する: to make a fresh start on the occasion of ~

USAGE:
Often used when someone decides to change their attitude or start something new after a significant life event such as a job change, move, or failure.
