自発的(じはつてき)

じはつてき
na-adjective
spontaneous; voluntary

Definitions

1. spontaneous; voluntary
Acting of one's own initiative without being prompted, ordered, or forced by others.

Examples

自発的(じはつてき)に参加(さんか)する。
To participate voluntarily.
社員(しゃいん)の自発的(じはつてき)な行動(こうどう)を促(うなが)す。
To encourage spontaneous action from employees.
自発的(じはつてき)な学習(がくしゅう)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Self-motivated learning is important.

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 自発的(じはつてき)に (voluntarily, spontaneously)

ETYMOLOGY:
自発(じはつ) (spontaneity) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自発的(じはつてき)な参加(さんか): voluntary participation
  • 自発的(じはつてき)な行動(こうどう): spontaneous action
  • 自発的(じはつてき)な意志(いし): voluntary will

NUANCE:
Emphasizes acting without external pressure or compulsion. Valued in contexts like education and workplace culture.

CONTRAST:

  • 強制的(きょうせいてき): compulsory, forced (opposite)
  • 自主的(じしゅてき): self-directed (similar, emphasizes independence)

Related Words

Antonym: 強制的(きょうせいてき) (compulsory)
Synonym: 自主的(じしゅてき) (self-directed)
