華(はな)やか

はなやか
na-adjective
gorgeous, glamorous

Definitions

1. gorgeous, glamorous, brilliant, showy
Attractively bright, colorful, and impressive. Often describes appearance, events, or lifestyles that are dazzling and splendid.

Examples

華(はな)やかなドレスを着(き)ている。
She's wearing a gorgeous dress.
華(はな)やかな舞台(ぶたい)に立(た)つ。
To stand on a glamorous stage.
彼女(かのじょ)の周(まわ)りは華(はな)やかな雰囲気(ふんいき)がある。
There's a glamorous atmosphere around her.

Notes

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 華(はな)やかに (gorgeously)
  • Noun: 華(はな)やかさ (gorgeousness, glamour)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 華(はな)やかな世界(せかい) (glamorous world)
  • 華(はな)やかな経歴(けいれき) (brilliant career)
  • 華(はな)やかに飾(かざ)る (decorate gorgeously)

NUANCE: Has positive connotations of beauty and brilliance. Unlike 派手(はで) (flashy), 華(はな)やか implies tasteful splendor rather than gaudiness.

KANJI: May also be written as 花(はな)やか, though 華(はな)やか is more common for the figurative sense.

Related Words

Contrast: 地味(じみ)
Related: 派手(はで)
