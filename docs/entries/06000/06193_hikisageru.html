引(ひ)き下(さ)げる

ひきさげる
verb (ichidan)
to lower, to reduce, to cut
1. to lower, to reduce
To decrease a price, rate, level, or standard.
価格(かかく)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる。
Lower the price.
政府(せいふ)は消費税(しょうひぜい)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げた。
The government lowered the consumption tax.
2. to withdraw, to pull back
To withdraw troops, people, or proposals.
提案(ていあん)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げるしかない。
We have no choice but to withdraw the proposal.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 引(ひ)き下(さ)がる (to step back, to withdraw)

ASPECT: 引(ひ)き下(さ)げている can indicate an ongoing price reduction or a state of having reduced something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 価格(かかく)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる (to lower the price)
  • 金利(きんり)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる (to lower interest rates)
  • 税率(ぜいりつ)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる (to lower the tax rate)
  • 水準(すいじゅん)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる (to lower the standard)
  • 提案(ていあん)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる (to withdraw a proposal)

ANTONYM: 引(ひ)き上(あ)げる (to raise, to pull up)

NOTE: Often used in business, economic, and political contexts.

Related Words

Pair verb: 引(ひ)き下(さ)がる (intransitive)
Antonym: 引(ひ)き上(あ)げる (to raise)
