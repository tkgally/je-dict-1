ぱたぱた

ぱたぱた
adverb, suru verb
flapping; pattering

Definitions

1. flapping; fluttering
The sound or action of something light flapping repeatedly, such as wings, fabric, or sandals.
2. pattering; light quick steps
The sound of light, quick footsteps, often of children or small animals.

Examples

鳥(とり)がぱたぱたと羽(はね)ばたいた。
The bird flapped its wings.
子供(こども)がぱたぱたと走(はし)ってきた。
The child came running with pattering footsteps.
旗(はた)がぱたぱたと風(かぜ)になびいている。
The flag is flapping in the wind.

Notes

ぱたぱた describes light, rapid, repetitive movement or sound.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぱたぱた走(はし)る: to run with pattering steps
  • ぱたぱた羽(はね)ばたく: to flap wings
  • ぱたぱたする: to bustle about

CONTRAST:

  • ばたばた: heavier, more frantic flapping or bustling
  • ひらひら: light, graceful fluttering
  • どたどた: heavy, clumsy footsteps
