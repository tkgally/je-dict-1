新規(しんき)

しんき
noun
new; newcomer; new fan

Definitions

1. new, newly created
Something newly established or created. Often used with other nouns to indicate newness.
2. newcomer, new member, new fan
A person who has recently joined a group, community, or started following someone. Common in online fan communities.

Examples

新規(しんき)顧客(こきゃく)を獲得(かくとく)する。
To acquire new customers.
新規(しんき)登録(とうろく)は無料(むりょう)です。
New registration is free.
新規(しんき)なので分(わ)からないことが多(おお)い。
Since I'm new, there's a lot I don't understand.
新規(しんき)にも優(やさ)しいコミュニティだ。
It's a community that's friendly to newcomers.

Notes

REGISTER: Neutral to formal in business contexts (sense 1); casual in online/fan community contexts (sense 2).

BUSINESS/FORMAL USAGE (sense 1):

Common compounds include:

  • 新規(しんき)開拓(かいたく): new development/pioneering
  • 新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう): new business venture
  • 新規(しんき)参入(さんにゅう): new market entry
  • 新規(しんき)採用(さいよう): new hiring

FAN COMMUNITY USAGE (sense 2):

In online communities, 新規(しんき) refers to new fans:

  • 新規(しんき)ファン: new fan
  • 新規(しんき)さん: newcomer (polite)
  • 新規(しんき)を歓迎(かんげい)する: to welcome newcomers

CONTRAST WITH 古参(こさん):

  • 新規(しんき): new fan (recently joined)
  • 古参(こさん): longtime fan (since early days)

NOTE: In fan communities, healthy communities embrace 新規(しんき), while toxic ones may gatekeep or look down on newcomers.

Related Words

Antonym: 古参(こさん)
