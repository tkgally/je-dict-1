1. to stretch, to extend
To pull something to make it longer or larger.
ゴムを引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす。
To stretch a rubber band.
生地(きじ)を薄(うす)く引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす。
To stretch the dough out thin.
針金(はりがね)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばして形(かたち)を作(つく)った。
I stretched the wire out and shaped it.
2. to enlarge (photo)
To make a photograph larger, to print an enlargement.
この写真(しゃしん)をA4サイズに引(ひ)き伸(の)ばしてください。
Please enlarge this photo to A4 size.
小(ちい)さい写真(しゃしん)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばすと画質(がしつ)が落(お)ちる。
Enlarging a small photo reduces the image quality.
ネガから引(ひ)き伸(の)ばした写真(しゃしん)を額(がく)に入(い)れた。
I framed the photo that was enlarged from the negative.
3. to delay, to postpone
To stretch out the time of something, to drag out a process.
交渉(こうしょう)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばして時間(じかん)を稼(かせ)いだ。
We bought time by dragging out the negotiations.
返事(へんじ)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばさないでほしい。
I'd like you to not delay your reply.
会議(かいぎ)がだらだらと引(ひ)き伸(の)ばされて、予定(よてい)より1時間(じかん)も長(なが)くなった。
The meeting was dragged out and ran an hour longer than planned.

COMPOUND VERB: 引(ひ)く (to pull) + 伸(の)ばす (to extend) = to pull and extend

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. The intransitive counterpart is 引(ひ)き伸(の)びる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 写真(しゃしん)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす (to enlarge a photo)
  • 期間(きかん)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす (to extend the period)
  • 決定(けってい)を引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす (to delay a decision)
  • 引(ひ)き伸(の)ばし (noun form: enlargement; delay)

PHOTOGRAPHY USAGE: In the context of film photography, 引(ひ)き伸(の)ばす refers to making an enlargement print from a negative.

RELATED ～伸(の)ばす COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 書(か)き伸(の)ばす - to extend in writing
  • 押(お)し伸(の)ばす - to spread by pressing
