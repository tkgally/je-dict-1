とはいうものの

とはいうものの
conjunction
having said that, although, nevertheless
1. having said that, although
Introduces a contrasting point while fully acknowledging the preceding statement. Emphasizes that despite what was said, there is still an important counter-point.
約束(やくそく)したとはいうものの、実行(じっこう)できるか不安(ふあん)だ。
Although I promised, I'm worried whether I can actually do it.
経験(けいけん)があるとはいうものの、まだまだ未熟(みじゅく)だ。
Although I have experience, I'm still inexperienced.
春(はる)だとはいうものの、まだ寒(さむ)い日(ひ)が続(つづ)く。
Although it's spring, cold days continue.
賛成(さんせい)だとはいうものの、条件(じょうけん)がある。
Although I agree, there are conditions.

GRAMMAR: とはいうものの follows nouns, adjectives, or verb plain forms. It acknowledges the preceding statement more emphatically than とはいえ before introducing a contrasting point.

PATTERNS:

  • [noun] + だとはいうものの
  • [i-adjective] + とはいうものの
  • [na-adjective] + だとはいうものの
  • [verb plain] + とはいうものの

REGISTER: Formal, common in written language and speeches.

COMPARISON WITH とはいえ:

  • とはいえ: More concise, slightly less emphatic
  • とはいうものの: More emphatic, acknowledges the first point more fully before contrasting

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • とはいえ: Although (shorter, slightly less formal)
  • それにしても: Even so, nevertheless
  • そうはいっても: Having said that
