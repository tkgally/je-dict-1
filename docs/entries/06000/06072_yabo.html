野暮(やぼ)

やぼ
na-adjective
unsophisticated, uncouth

Definitions

1. unsophisticated, uncouth, boorish
Lacking refinement, elegance, or social grace. Describes someone or something that is awkward or lacking in tact.
2. tactless, unrefined (of actions or words)
Used to describe actions or questions that are inappropriate, rude, or break social norms.

Examples

そんな野暮(やぼ)なことを聞(き)くな。
Don't ask such a tactless question.
Common expression
野暮(やぼ)な服装(ふくそう)だと思(おも)われたくない。
I don't want to be seen as having unsophisticated fashion.
金額(きんがく)を聞(き)くのは野暮(やぼ)だ。
It's tactless to ask about the price.
Social context

Notes

FORMS:

  • Noun: 野暮(やぼ) (used as noun meaning 'boor' or 'philistine')
  • Set phrase: 野暮(やぼ)なこと (tactless thing)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 野暮(やぼ)なことを言(い)う (say something tactless)
  • 野暮(やぼ)を承知(しょうち)で (knowing it's tactless, but...)
  • 野暮(やぼ)天(てん) (boor, philistine - noun)

CULTURAL NOTE: Originated in Edo-period culture where it contrasted with 粋(いき) (stylishness, chic). Someone who doesn't understand the unwritten rules of social elegance was called 野暮(やぼ).

ANTONYM: 粋(いき) (chic, sophisticated).

Related Words

Antonym: 粋(いき)
