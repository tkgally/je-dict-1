1. clearly; decisively; flatly; firmly
In a decisive, unambiguous manner, often when refusing or cutting ties with something.
誘(さそ)いをきっぱり断(ことわ)った。
I flatly refused the invitation.
タバコをきっぱりやめた。
I quit smoking for good.
きっぱりと「いいえ」と言(い)った。
I said "no" decisively.

きっぱり expresses doing something in a clean, decisive manner without hesitation or ambiguity. It conveys firm resolve, especially when refusing, quitting, or stating a position.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • きっぱり断(ことわ)る: to flatly refuse
  • きっぱりやめる: to quit for good
  • きっぱりと言(い)う: to say clearly and firmly
  • きっぱり別(わか)れる: to make a clean break

GRAMMAR:
Often followed by と (きっぱりと) before verbs for emphasis. Can also be used with する: きっぱりした態度(たいど) (a firm attitude).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • はっきり: clearly (focuses on clarity of expression rather than decisiveness)
  • すっぱり: cleanly, completely (emphasizes completeness of a cut or break)
  • きちんと: properly, neatly (focuses on correctness rather than resolve)

Contrast: はっきり (clearly (clarity))
