見過(みす)ごす

みすごす
verb (godan)
to overlook, to miss

Definitions

1. to overlook, to miss, to fail to notice
To not notice something that should have been seen or caught. Can be unintentional.
2. to let pass, to turn a blind eye
To deliberately ignore or tolerate something, often a problem or wrongdoing.

Examples

重要(じゅうよう)なエラーを見過(みす)ごしてしまった。
I overlooked an important error.
この問題(もんだい)は見過(みす)ごせない。
This problem cannot be ignored.
Potential negative - common expression
今回(こんかい)は見過(みす)ごしてあげよう。
I'll let it slide this time.
Deliberately overlooking

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 見(み)る + 過(す)ごす. Pattern: Xを見過(みす)ごす (overlook X / ignore X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 見過(みす)ごしている = 'has overlooked' or 'is ignoring.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 見過(みす)ごすわけにはいかない (cannot let it slide)
  • 見過(みす)ごせない問題(もんだい) (a problem that cannot be ignored)
  • うっかり見過(みす)ごす (accidentally overlook)

SIMILAR WORDS: 見落(みお)とす emphasizes failing to notice, while 見過(みす)ごす can include deliberately ignoring.
