突(つ)き詰(つ)める

つきつめる
verb (ichidan)
to investigate thoroughly, to think through completely
1. to investigate thoroughly, to pursue to the end
To examine or investigate something exhaustively until reaching a conclusion.
問題(もんだい)の原因(げんいん)を突(つ)き詰(つ)めて調(しら)べた。
I investigated the cause of the problem thoroughly.
真相(しんそう)を突(つ)き詰(つ)めるまで諦(あきら)めない。
I won't give up until I get to the bottom of the truth.
2. to think through completely, to carry to its logical conclusion
To follow a line of reasoning to its ultimate conclusion.
突(つ)き詰(つ)めて考(かんが)えると、答(こた)えは一(ひと)つしかない。
When you think it through completely, there's only one answer.
理論(りろん)を突(つ)き詰(つ)めていくと矛盾(むじゅん)が見(み)えてくる。
As you pursue the theory to its end, contradictions become visible.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Compound verb: 突(つ)く + 詰(つ)める.

PATTERN: Xを突(つ)き詰(つ)める (pursue/investigate X thoroughly)

COMPOUND VERB PATTERN: ～詰(つ)める compounds indicate doing something exhaustively or to completion:

  • 考(かんが)え詰(つ)める (to think exhaustively - rare)
  • 追(お)い詰(つ)める (to corner, to drive into a corner)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 突(つ)き詰(つ)めて考(かんが)える (think through thoroughly)
  • 突(つ)き詰(つ)めれば (ultimately, when you get down to it)
  • 突(つ)き詰(つ)めると (in the final analysis)
