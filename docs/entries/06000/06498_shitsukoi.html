しつこい

しつこい
i-adjective
persistent, insistent; heavy (taste)

Definitions

1. persistent, insistent, tenacious
Describes someone who keeps repeating the same request, action, or question in an annoying or stubborn way. Often has a negative connotation.
2. heavy, greasy, rich (of taste)
Describes food or flavors that are too rich, oily, or heavy, making one feel full or uncomfortable.

Examples

しつこく質問(しつもん)されて困(こま)った。
I was troubled by persistent questioning.
しつこい勧誘(かんゆう)は迷惑(めいわく)だ。
Persistent solicitation is a nuisance.
この料理(りょうり)はちょっとしつこい。
This dish is a bit too heavy/rich.
年(とし)を取(と)るとしつこい味(あじ)が苦手(にがて)になる。
As you get older, you tend to dislike heavy/rich flavors.

Notes

ADJECTIVE FORMS:

  • しつこい (dictionary form)
  • しつこく (adverbial form)
  • しつこくない (negative)
  • しつこかった (past)
  • しつこさ (noun form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しつこい勧誘(かんゆう) - persistent solicitation
  • しつこい{セールス} - pushy sales
  • しつこく質問(しつもん)する - to question persistently
  • しつこく付(つ)きまとう - to persistently follow/stalk

FOOD CONTEXT:

  • しつこい味(あじ) - heavy/rich taste
  • 油(あぶら)がしつこい - greasy, oily
  • しつこくない/あっさりした - light, refreshing (antonym)

NUANCE: When describing people's behavior, しつこい is typically negative, implying annoyance. However, in some contexts it can suggest tenacity in a neutral way.

SYNONYMS/RELATED:

  • 執拗(しつよう) - persistent (more formal)
  • くどい - repetitive, long-winded
  • あっさり - light (antonym for taste)
