生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)

せいせんしょくひん
noun
fresh food; produce
1. fresh food, fresh produce, perishables
Foods that are fresh and have not been processed, preserved, or frozen. Includes fresh vegetables, fruits, meat, and fish.
生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)は冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)で保管(ほかん)してください。
Please store fresh food in the refrigerator.
このスーパーは生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)が新鮮(しんせん)で安(やす)い。
This supermarket has fresh produce that's fresh and inexpensive.
生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)売(う)り場(ば)で野菜(やさい)を買(か)った。
I bought vegetables at the fresh food section.

USAGE: 生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん) refers to perishable foods that are sold fresh, without processing. This is a common term seen in supermarkets and food retail.

CATEGORIES OF 生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん):

  • 野菜(やさい) (vegetables)
  • 果物(くだもの) (fruits)
  • 肉(にく)/精肉(せいにく) (meat)
  • 魚(さかな)/鮮魚(せんぎょ) (fish)
  • 卵(たまご) (eggs)

SUPERMARKET TERMS:

  • 生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)売(う)り場(ば) (fresh food section)
  • 生鮮(せいせん)コーナー (fresh produce corner)

CONTRAST:

  • 加工食品(かこうしょくひん) (processed food) - food that has been processed, preserved, or packaged
  • 冷凍食品(れいとうしょくひん) (frozen food)

Related Words

Antonym: 加工食品(かこうしょくひん) (processed food)
