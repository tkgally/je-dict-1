1. to ask again
To repeat a question or request because one didn't hear or understand the first time.
もう一度(いちど)聞(き)き直(なお)した。
I asked again.
すみません、もう一度(いちど)聞(き)き直(なお)してもいいですか。
Sorry, may I ask you again?
名前(なまえ)がよく聞(き)こえなかったので、聞(き)き直(なお)した。
I couldn't hear the name well, so I asked again.
電話(でんわ)で住所(じゅうしょ)を聞(き)き直(なお)すのは大変(たいへん)だった。
It was hard to ask for the address again over the phone.
何度(なんど)も聞(き)き直(なお)すのは申(もう)し訳(わけ)ないと思(おも)ったが、大事(だいじ)な話(はなし)だったので確認(かくにん)した。
I felt bad about asking again so many times, but it was important, so I confirmed.
2. to re-listen, to listen again
To listen to something again, especially to music or audio recordings, to hear it better or with fresh ears.
昔(むかし)の曲(きょく)を聞(き)き直(なお)した。
I listened to an old song again.
インタビューの録音(ろくおん)を聞(き)き直(なお)した。
I listened to the interview recording again.
昔(むかし)の曲(きょく)を聞(き)き直(なお)すと懐(なつ)かしくなる。
When I re-listen to old songs, I feel nostalgic.
授業(じゅぎょう)の録音(ろくおん)を聞(き)き直(なお)して大事(だいじ)なところをメモした。
I re-listened to the class recording and took notes on the important parts.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)に好(す)きだったアルバムを何年(なんねん)ぶりかに聞(き)き直(なお)したら、当時(とうじ)の気持(きも)ちが蘇(よみがえ)ってきた。
When I re-listened to an album I loved when I was young after many years, the feelings from that time came flooding back.

COMPOUND VERB: 聞(き)く (to listen/ask) + 直(なお)す (to redo, to do again) = to listen/ask again

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive. Takes を when there is a specific object being listened to again.

CONJUGATION (godan verb):

  • 聞(き)き直(なお)して (te-form)
  • 聞(き)き直(なお)した (past)
  • 聞(き)き直(なお)せる (potential)
  • 聞(き)き直(なお)される (passive)

RELATED ～直(なお)す COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 言(い)い直(なお)す - to say again, to rephrase
  • 書(か)き直(なお)す - to rewrite
  • 見(み)直(なお)す - to reconsider, to look at again
  • 読(よ)み直(なお)す - to reread
  • やり直(なお)す - to redo

USAGE NOTE: When asking someone to repeat what they said, 聞(き)き返(かえ)す is also used and emphasizes the interactive exchange. 聞(き)き直(なお)す can be used both for asking someone to repeat and for listening to recordings again.
