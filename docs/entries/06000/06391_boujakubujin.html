傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)

ぼうじゃくぶじん
noun, na-adjective
arrogant, behaving as if no one else exists
1. arrogant, behaving as if no one else exists
A four-character idiom describing someone who acts selfishly and inconsiderately, as if they were completely alone and others don't matter.
彼(かれ)は傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)った。
He behaved arrogantly, as if no one else mattered.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な態度(たいど)を取(と)る人(ひと)がいる。
There are people who behave inconsiderately on trains.
傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な運転(うんてん)で事故(じこ)を起(お)こした。
The reckless driving caused an accident.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語(よじじゅくご))

LITERAL MEANING: 傍(かたわ)ら (beside) + 若(ごと)し (as if) + 無人(むじん) (no people) = Acting as if there were no one beside you.

ORIGIN: From the Chinese historical text 《史記(しき)》 (Records of the Grand Historian), describing the behavior of the assassin 荊軻(けいか) (Jing Ke).

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な＋noun (arrogant ~)
  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)に＋verb (arrogantly)
  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な振(ふ)る舞(ま)い (arrogant behavior)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な態度(たいど) (arrogant attitude)
  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)な行動(こうどう) (inconsiderate behavior)
  • 傍若無人(ぼうじゃくぶじん)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う (to behave arrogantly)

NUANCE: Strongly negative, implying selfishness and complete disregard for others.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 横柄(おうへい) - arrogant, haughty
  • 尊大(そんだい) - arrogant, self-important
  • 厚顔無恥(こうがんむち) - shameless, brazen

Related Words
