チルい

ちるい
i-adjective
chill, relaxed (slang)

Definitions

1. chill, relaxed, mellow
Modern slang describing a relaxed, calm, or laid-back atmosphere or feeling. Derived from English "chill" with the Japanese adjective ending い.

Examples

このカフェはチルい雰囲気(ふんいき)でいいね。
This cafe has a nice chill vibe.
週末(しゅうまつ)は家(いえ)でチルく過(す)ごしたい。
I want to spend the weekend chilling at home.
チルく is the adverbial form
チルい音楽(おんがく)を聴(き)きながら作業(さぎょう)する。
I work while listening to chill music.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY: From English "chill" + Japanese adjective suffix い. Part of the trend of creating Japanese adjectives from English loanwords.

REGISTER: Casual/Slang
Used primarily by young people. A relatively recent addition to youth slang vocabulary (2010s-2020s).

CONJUGATION:

Conjugates as a regular i-adjective:

  • チルい (plain)
  • チルくない (negative)
  • チルかった (past)
  • チルく (adverbial form)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often describes music, atmosphere, or spending time relaxing
  • Related to the verb チルする (to chill, to relax)
  • Associated with lo-fi music, cafe culture, and relaxation

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チルい雰囲気(ふんいき) (chill atmosphere)
  • チルい音楽(おんがく) (chill music)
  • チルく過(す)ごす (to spend time chilling)

RELATED TERMS:

  • チルする: to chill, to relax (verb)
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)いた: calm, settled (more standard)
  • まったり: leisurely, relaxed (established slang)
