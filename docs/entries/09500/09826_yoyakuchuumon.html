予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)

よやくちゅうもん
noun
pre-order; advance order
1. pre-order; advance order
An order placed before a product is released or available, securing the item for future delivery.
予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)を受(う)け付(つ)けています。
We are accepting pre-orders.
新作(しんさく)ゲームの予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)をした。
I pre-ordered the new game.
予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)すると発売日(はつばいび)に届(とど)く。
If you pre-order, it arrives on the release date.

予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん) is common for games, books, limited edition items, and products with high demand. Pre-ordering often comes with bonuses or guarantees availability.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)を入(い)れる (to place a pre-order)
  • 予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)受付(うけつけ)中(ちゅう) (now accepting pre-orders)
  • 予約(よやく)注文(ちゅうもん)特典(とくてん) (pre-order bonus)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 発売日(はつばいび): release date
  • 入荷(にゅうか)待(ま)ち: waiting for stock
  • お取(と)り寄(よ)せ: special order
