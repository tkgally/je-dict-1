他人事(ひとごと)

ひとごと
noun
other people's affairs, someone else's problem
1. other people's affairs, someone else's problem
Something that concerns others but not oneself; treating something as if it doesn't affect you.
他人事(ひとごと)ではない。
This is not someone else's problem.
他人事(ひとごと)のように言(い)う。
To speak as if it's someone else's problem.
彼(かれ)は他人事(ひとごと)だと思(おも)っている。
He thinks it's someone else's problem.

他人事(ひとごと) refers to matters that concern other people but not oneself. It's often used when criticizing someone for not taking something seriously.

READINGS:

  • ひとごと: standard reading
  • たにんごと: also acceptable, less common

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 他人事(ひとごと)ではない (it's not someone else's problem; it concerns me too)
  • 他人事(ひとごと)のように (as if it were someone else's problem)
  • 他人事(ひとごと)と思(おも)う (to think of something as not one's concern)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used negatively to describe someone who doesn't show appropriate concern or empathy.
