二度(にど)

にど
adverb
twice, two times; (with negative) never again
1. twice, two times
An action or event occurring two times.
二度(にど)会(あ)った。
I met them twice.
2. never again (with negative)
Used with と and a negative verb to mean 'never again' - emphasizes a strong resolve not to repeat something.
二度(にど)と行(い)かない。
I'll never go again.
二度(にど)と嘘(うそ)をつかないと誓(ちか)う。
I swear I'll never tell a lie again.

二度(にど) means 'twice' or 'two times.'

IMPORTANT PATTERN:

  • 二度(にど)と + negative = never again
  • Example: 二度(にど)と行(い)かない (I'll never go again)
  • Expresses strong determination not to repeat

RELATED WORDS:

  • 一度(いちど): once
  • 三度(さんど): three times
  • 何度(なんど): how many times
