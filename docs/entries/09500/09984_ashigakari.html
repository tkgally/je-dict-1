足掛(あしが)かり

あしがかり
noun
foothold; stepping stone, starting point
1. foothold, toehold
A place to put one's foot when climbing or a secure position from which to advance.
岩(いわ)の足掛(あしが)かりを探(さが)す。
To look for a foothold on the rock.
崖(がけ)を登(のぼ)るには足掛(あしが)かりが必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need footholds to climb the cliff.
2. stepping stone, starting point, springboard
Something that helps one make progress or achieve a goal figuratively.
この資格(しかく)を足掛(あしが)かりにキャリアアップを目指(めざ)す。
I aim to advance my career using this qualification as a stepping stone.
今回(こんかい)の成功(せいこう)を足掛(あしが)かりに、さらに飛躍(ひやく)したい。
I want to use this success as a springboard to achieve even more.
インターンシップは就職(しゅうしょく)への足掛(あしが)かりになる。
Internships serve as a stepping stone to employment.
海外(かいがい)進出(しんしゅつ)の足掛(あしが)かりとして、まず現地(げんち)法人(ほうじん)を設立(せつりつ)した。
As a foothold for overseas expansion, we first established a local subsidiary.

足掛(あしが)かり can be physical or figurative.

WRITING:
Also written as 足がかり} or {足懸(あしが)かり.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 足掛(あしが)かりになる: to serve as a stepping stone
  • 足掛(あしが)かりにする: to use as a stepping stone
  • 足掛(あしが)かりを得(え)る: to gain a foothold
  • 足掛(あしが)かりを築(きず)く: to establish a foothold

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 手掛(てが)かり: handhold; clue
  • 足場(あしば): scaffolding; footing
