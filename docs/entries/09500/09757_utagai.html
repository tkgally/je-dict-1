疑(うたが)い

うたがい
noun
doubt, suspicion
1. doubt, suspicion, uncertainty
A feeling of uncertainty or lack of conviction about something.
疑(うたが)いを挟(はさ)む。
To harbor doubt.
疑(うたが)いの余地(よち)はない。
There is no room for doubt.
彼(かれ)に対(たい)する疑(うたが)いが消(き)えた。
My suspicion toward him disappeared.

疑(うたが)い is the noun form of the verb 疑(うたが)う (to doubt).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 疑(うたが)いを挟(はさ)む: to harbor doubt
  • 疑(うたが)いを持(も)つ: to have doubts
  • 疑(うたが)いの余地(よち)がない: no room for doubt
  • 疑(うたが)いを晴(は)らす: to clear up doubt

RELATED WORDS:

  • 疑(うたが)う: to doubt (verb)
  • 疑問(ぎもん): question, doubt
