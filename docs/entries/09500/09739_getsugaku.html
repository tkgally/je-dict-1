月額(げつがく)

げつがく
noun
monthly fee; monthly amount
1. monthly fee; monthly charge; monthly amount
A recurring payment or charge that is billed every month, commonly used for subscriptions, memberships, and services.
月額(げつがく)500円(えん)で利用(りよう)できる。
You can use it for 500 yen per month.
月額(げつがく)料金(りょうきん)はいくらですか？
How much is the monthly fee?
サブスクの月額(げつがく)を見直(みなお)したら、結構(けっこう)節約(せつやく)できた。
When I reviewed my monthly subscription fees, I was able to save quite a bit.

月額(げつがく) is commonly used in pricing and subscription contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 月額(げつがく)料金(りょうきん) (monthly fee)
  • 月額(げつがく)プラン (monthly plan)
  • 月額(げつがく)制(せい) (monthly billing system)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 年額(ねんがく): annual fee
  • 定額(ていがく): flat rate
  • サブスク: subscription
  • 日割(ひわ)り: daily rate/proration
