転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)

てんしゅつとどけ
noun
moving-out notification
1. moving-out notification
An official form submitted to the local ward or city office when moving to a different municipality in Japan.
転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)を出(だ)してきた。
I submitted my moving-out notification.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A moving-out notification is required before moving.
市役所(しやくしょ)で転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)の手続(てつづ)きをする。
I'll complete the moving-out notification procedures at the city hall.

転出届(てんしゅつとどけ) is filed at your current municipality's office when moving to a different city, ward, or town. You receive a 転出(てんしゅつ)証明書(しょうめいしょ) (moving-out certificate) which you then submit along with 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ) at your new location.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit a moving-out notification)
  • 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ)の届出(とどけで) (filing a moving-out notification)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ): moving-in notification
  • 転居届(てんきょとどけ): change of address notice
  • 住民票(じゅうみんひょう): certificate of residence
