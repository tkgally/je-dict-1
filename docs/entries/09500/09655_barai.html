払(はら)い

ばらい
noun, suffix
payment
1. payment; paying
The act of paying money, often used as a suffix to indicate a payment method or timing.
給料(きゅうりょう)は月末(げつまつ)払(ばら)いです。
Salaries are paid at the end of the month.
現金(げんきん)払(ばら)いでお願(ねが)いします。
Cash payment, please.
分割(ぶんかつ)払(ばら)いは可能(かのう)ですか。
Is installment payment possible?

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 月末(げつまつ)払(ばら)い (end-of-month payment)
  • 現金(げんきん)払(ばら)い (cash payment)
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)払(ばら)い (installment payment)
  • カード払(ばら)い (card payment)
  • 一括(いっかつ)払(ばら)い (lump-sum payment)
  • 後(あと)払(ばら)い (deferred payment)

RELATED:

  • 払(はら)う (to pay - verb)
  • 支払(しはら)い (payment - more formal)
