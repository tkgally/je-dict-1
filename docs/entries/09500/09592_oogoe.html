大声(おおごえ)

おおごえ
noun
loud voice
1. loud voice, shouting
Speaking or calling out with high volume.
大声(おおごえ)で笑(わら)うのは下品(げひん)だと思(おも)われることもある。
Laughing loudly can sometimes be considered vulgar.
大声(おおごえ)で呼(よ)んだ。
I called out in a loud voice.
大声(おおごえ)を出(だ)さないでください。
Please don't speak loudly.

大声(おおごえ) means speaking or calling out loudly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 大声(おおごえ)で〜: loudly, in a loud voice
  • 大声(おおごえ)を出(だ)す: to raise one's voice, to shout
  • 大声(おおごえ)で叫(さけ)ぶ: to shout loudly
  • 大声(おおごえ)で笑(わら)う: to laugh loudly

CONTRAST:

  • 大声(おおごえ): loud voice
  • 小声(こごえ): soft/quiet voice, whisper
