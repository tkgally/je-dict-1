1. to scoop, to ladle, to dip up
To lift or take up something (usually liquid or small items) using a tool like a ladle, net, or one's hands.
金魚(きんぎょ)をすくう。
To scoop goldfish.
金魚(きんぎょ)すくい is a popular festival game
スープを皿(さら)にすくった。
I ladled soup into the bowl.
夏祭(なつまつ)りで金魚(きんぎょ)をすくったが、すぐに死(し)んでしまった。
I scooped goldfish at the summer festival, but they died right away.

すくう is a godan verb meaning to scoop or ladle.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを すくう

ASPECT (ている):

  • すくっている: in the process of scooping

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 金魚(きんぎょ)をすくう (to scoop goldfish)
  • スープをすくう (to ladle soup)
  • 水(みず)をすくう (to scoop up water)
  • 手(て)ですくう (to scoop with one's hands)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 金魚(きんぎょ)すくい: goldfish scooping (festival game)
  • 玉(たま)すくい: ball scooping game

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 救(すく)う (to save, rescue) - different kanji, different meaning
