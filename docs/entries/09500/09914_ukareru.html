浮(う)かれる

うかれる
ichidan verb
to be in high spirits, to be elated, to get carried away
1. to be in high spirits, to be elated
To be excited and happy, often in a festive or celebratory mood.
祭(まつ)りで町(まち)が浮(う)かれている。
The town is in high spirits for the festival.
春(はる)が来(き)て、心(こころ)が浮(う)かれる。
Spring has come, and my heart is elated.
合格(ごうかく)の知(し)らせに浮(う)かれて、つい飲(の)みすぎた。
I got carried away with the news of passing and drank too much.
2. to get carried away, to be too carefree
To become overly excited or careless, often with a slightly negative nuance suggesting one should be more careful.
浮(う)かれてばかりいないで仕事(しごと)しなさい。
Stop being so carefree and get to work.
成功(せいこう)に浮(う)かれるのは早(はや)い。
It's too early to get carried away with success.
彼(かれ)は昇進(しょうしん)に浮(う)かれて、足元(あしもと)が見(み)えなくなっている。
He's so carried away with his promotion that he's lost sight of what's at his feet.

Can have either a positive (joyful) or slightly negative (careless) nuance depending on context.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 浮(う)かれ気分(きぶん) (festive/elated mood)
  • 〜に浮(う)かれる (to be elated by ~)
  • 浮(う)かれている場合(ばあい)じゃない (this is no time to be celebrating)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 浮(う)く: to float
  • 浮(う)かれ騒(さわ)ぐ: to make merry
