代引(だいび)き

だいびき
noun
cash on delivery; COD
1. cash on delivery; COD; payment on delivery
A payment method where the recipient pays the delivery person upon receiving the package, typically with cash or card.
代引(だいび)きで注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered with cash on delivery.
代引(だいび)きは手数料(てすうりょう)がかかります。
There is a fee for cash on delivery.
クレジットカードがない場合(ばあい)は代引(だいび)きが便利(べんり)だ。
Cash on delivery is convenient when you don't have a credit card.

代引(だいび)き is short for 代金引換(だいきんひきかえ) (payment on delivery).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代引(だいび)きで払(はら)う (to pay by COD)
  • 代引(だいび)き手数料(てすうりょう) (COD fee)
  • 代引(だいび)き可能(かのう) (COD available)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 着払(ちゃくばら)い: collect delivery (recipient pays shipping)
  • 前払(まえばら)い: prepayment
  • 後払(あとばら)い: deferred payment
