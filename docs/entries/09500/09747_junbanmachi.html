順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ち

じゅんばんまち
noun
waiting in line; waiting one's turn
1. waiting in line; waiting one's turn; queuing
The act of waiting for one's turn in a queue, whether at a restaurant, store, or other establishment.
順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ちをしている。
I'm waiting in line.
順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ちの人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
There are a lot of people waiting in line.
アプリで順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ちの登録(とうろく)ができるから、店(みせ)で並(なら)ばなくていい。
You can register for a spot in line on the app, so you don't have to wait at the store.

順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ち is common at popular restaurants and services.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ちをする (to wait in line)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ちシステム (queue management system)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)待(ま)ち時間(じかん) (waiting time)

MODERN SYSTEMS:

  • Many restaurants use digital queue systems.
  • You can often register via apps and receive notifications.
  • 呼(よ)び出(だ)し (calling your number/name)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 行列(ぎょうれつ): queue, line
  • 整理券(せいりけん): numbered ticket
