蛍光灯(けいこうとう)

けいこうとう
noun
fluorescent light, fluorescent lamp
1. fluorescent light, fluorescent lamp
A type of light that uses fluorescent tubes, common in offices, schools, and homes in Japan.
蛍光灯(けいこうとう)が切(き)れた。
The fluorescent light burned out.
蛍光灯(けいこうとう)を交換(こうかん)する。
To replace the fluorescent light.
蛍光灯(けいこうとう)からLEDに替(か)える家庭(かてい)が増(ふ)えている。
More households are switching from fluorescent lights to LEDs.

蛍光灯(けいこうとう) is the standard term for fluorescent lighting.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 蛍光灯(けいこうとう)が切(き)れる: the fluorescent light burns out
  • 蛍光灯(けいこうとう)を交換(こうかん)する: to replace the fluorescent light
  • 蛍光灯(けいこうとう)の光(ひかり): fluorescent lighting

USAGE NOTE:
Still widely used in Japan, though being gradually replaced by LED lighting. The tubes come in various shapes including 丸型(まるがた) (circular) and 直管(ちょっかん) (straight tube).
