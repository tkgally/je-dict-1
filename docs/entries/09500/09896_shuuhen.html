周辺(しゅうへん)

しゅうへん
noun
surroundings; vicinity; periphery
1. surroundings; vicinity; area around
The area surrounding or near something.
オリンピック期間(きかん)中(ちゅう)は、会場(かいじょう)周辺(しゅうへん)の警備(けいび)が強化(きょうか)された。
Security around the venue was strengthened during the Olympics.
駅(えき)周辺(しゅうへん)には店(みせ)が多(おお)い。
There are many shops in the area around the station.
2. periphery; peripheral
Related or supplementary items; the outer edges.
周辺(しゅうへん)機器(きき)を買(か)い揃(そろ)えた。
I bought all the peripheral devices.
Computing context

周辺(しゅうへん) refers to the surrounding area or periphery.

  • 周辺(しゅうへん)に: in the vicinity
