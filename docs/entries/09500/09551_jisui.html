1. cooking for oneself
Preparing one's own meals at home rather than eating out or buying ready-made food.
自炊(じすい)している。
I cook for myself.
自炊(じすい)は節約(せつやく)になる。
Cooking for yourself saves money.
一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしを始(はじ)めてから自炊(じすい)するようになった。
I started cooking for myself after I began living alone.

自炊(じすい) means cooking for oneself at home, as opposed to eating out or buying ready-made food. Especially associated with students and people living alone.

KANJI:
自(じ) (self) + 炊(すい) (cook rice). Literally "cooking (rice) for oneself."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自炊(じすい)する: to cook for oneself
  • 自炊(じすい)生活(せいかつ): life of cooking for oneself
  • 自炊(じすい)を始(はじ)める: to start cooking for oneself
  • 自炊(じすい)派(は): someone who prefers cooking at home

CONTRASTS:

  • 外食(がいしょく): eating out at restaurants
  • コンビニ弁当(べんとう): convenience store bento
  • 中食(なかしょく): buying prepared food to eat at home

CULTURAL NOTE:
自炊(じすい) is a common topic when discussing 一人暮(ひとりぐ)らし (living alone). University students often start 自炊(じすい) for the first time when they move away from home.

Related Words

Antonym: 外食(がいしょく) (eating out)
