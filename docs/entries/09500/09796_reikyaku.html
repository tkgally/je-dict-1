1. cooling; refrigeration
The process of reducing temperature, often used in technical or industrial contexts.
冷却(れいきゃく)する。
To cool down.
冷却(れいきゃく)装置(そうち)を使(つか)う。
To use a cooling device.
冷却(れいきゃく)期間(きかん)を置(お)く。
To allow a cooling-off period.

冷却(れいきゃく) combines 冷(れい) (cold) + 却(きゃく) (withdraw), referring to the process of reducing temperature. Primarily a technical/scientific term.

USAGE:
Most common in technical and industrial contexts. Also used figuratively: 冷却(れいきゃく)期間(きかん) means a "cooling-off period" for emotions or negotiations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冷却(れいきゃく)する: to cool
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)装置(そうち): cooling system/device
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)水(すい): coolant water
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)期間(きかん): cooling-off period
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)ファン: cooling fan

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冷(ひ)やす: to cool (everyday, e.g. food/drinks)
  • 冷凍(れいとう): freezing (below 0°C)
  • 冷蔵(れいぞう): refrigeration (above 0°C)
  • 加熱(かねつ): heating (opposite process)
