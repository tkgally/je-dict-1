冷却(れいきゃく)

れいきゃく
noun
cooling; refrigeration
1. cooling; refrigeration
The process of reducing temperature, often used in technical or industrial contexts.
冷却(れいきゃく)する。
To cool down.
冷却(れいきゃく)装置(そうち)を使(つか)う。
To use a cooling device.
冷却(れいきゃく)期間(きかん)を置(お)く。
To allow a cooling-off period.

冷却(れいきゃく) refers to the process of cooling, often in technical contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冷却(れいきゃく)する (to cool)
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)装置(そうち) (cooling system)
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)水(すい) (coolant water)
  • 冷却(れいきゃく)期間(きかん) (cooling-off period)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 加熱(かねつ): heating (opposite)
  • 冷(ひ)やす: to cool (everyday)
  • 冷凍(れいとう): freezing
  • 冷蔵(れいぞう): refrigeration
