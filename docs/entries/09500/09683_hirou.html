披露(ひろう)

ひろう
noun, suru verb
announcement; unveiling; display
1. announcement; unveiling; display; exhibition
Publicly presenting or revealing something, especially for the first time.
新曲(しんきょく)を披露(ひろう)した。
They performed their new song.
結婚(けっこん)披露(ひろう)宴(えん)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended the wedding reception.
長年(ながねん)の研究(けんきゅう)成果(せいか)を披露(ひろう)した。
They unveiled the results of years of research.

披露(ひろう) implies formal presentation to others.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 披露(ひろう)宴(えん) (wedding reception, announcement party)
  • 結婚(けっこん)披露(ひろう)宴(えん) (wedding reception)
  • お披露(ひろ)め (debut, unveiling - polite form)
  • 腕前(うでまえ)を披露(ひろう)する (display one's skill)

NOTE:
お披露(ひろ)め is a common polite variant meaning 'debut' or 'first public showing.'
