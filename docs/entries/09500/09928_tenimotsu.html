手荷物(てにもつ)

てにもつ
noun
hand luggage, carry-on baggage
1. hand luggage, carry-on baggage
Bags and belongings that passengers carry with them onto a plane, train, or other transportation, as opposed to checked luggage.
手荷物(てにもつ)は一(ひと)つまでです。
You can have only one piece of carry-on luggage.
手荷物(てにもつ)検査(けんさ)を受(う)けてください。
Please go through the baggage inspection.
手荷物(てにもつ)は座席(ざせき)の下(した)か上(うえ)の棚(たな)に収納(しゅうのう)してください。
Please store your hand luggage under your seat or in the overhead bin.

A common travel term used at airports and train stations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手荷物(てにもつ)検査(けんさ) (baggage inspection/security check)
  • 手荷物(てにもつ)預(あず)かり所(じょ) (baggage storage/left luggage)
  • 手荷物(てにもつ)制限(せいげん) (carry-on restrictions)
  • 手荷物(てにもつ)を持(も)ち込(こ)む (to bring carry-on luggage)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 預(あず)け荷物(にもつ): checked baggage
  • 機内(きない)持(も)ち込(こ)み: carry-on (for flights)
  • 荷物(にもつ): luggage, baggage
