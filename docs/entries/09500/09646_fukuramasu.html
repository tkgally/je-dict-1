1. to inflate; to swell; to puff up; to expand
To cause something to increase in size by filling it with air or gas, or to make something expand or swell.
風船(ふうせん)を膨(ふく)らます。
I inflate a balloon.
頬(ほほ)を膨(ふく)らませて怒(おこ)った。
She puffed out her cheeks in anger.
想像(そうぞう)を膨(ふく)らませて物語(ものがたり)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a story by letting my imagination run wild.

膨(ふく)らます means to cause something to expand, inflate, or swell. Used both literally (balloons, cheeks) and figuratively (imagination, expectations).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 膨(ふく)らむ (intransitive — to swell on its own)
  • Pattern: Xを膨(ふく)らます

ASPECT (ている):

  • 膨(ふく)らませている: is in the process of inflating (ongoing action)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 風船(ふうせん)を膨(ふく)らます: to inflate a balloon
  • 頬(ほほ)を膨(ふく)らます: to puff out one's cheeks (often showing displeasure)

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • 想像(そうぞう)を膨(ふく)らませる: to let one's imagination run wild
  • 期待(きたい)を膨(ふく)らませる: to raise one's expectations/hopes
  • 夢(ゆめ)を膨(ふく)らませる: to expand one's dreams
