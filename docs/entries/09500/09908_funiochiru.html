腑(ふ)に落(お)ちる

ふにおちる
ichidan verb
to make sense, to be convincing, to understand clearly
1. to make sense, to be convincing
To feel that something is logically understandable or emotionally acceptable; to be fully convinced.
その説明(せつめい)は腑(ふ)に落(お)ちた。
That explanation made sense to me.
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)が腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない。
His behavior doesn't make sense to me.
何度(なんど)聞(き)いても腑(ふ)に落(お)ちなかった理由(りゆう)が、やっと理解(りかい)できた。
I finally understood the reason that hadn't made sense no matter how many times I heard it.

An idiomatic expression where 腑(ふ) refers to the internal organs, particularly the stomach/gut, which in Japanese is associated with deep understanding and intuition.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 腑(ふ)に落(お)ちる (to make sense)
  • 腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない (to not make sense)
  • なかなか腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない (hard to accept/understand)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used in the negative form 腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない to express that something doesn't sit right or feels unconvincing.
