税込(ぜいこみ)

ぜいこみ
noun
tax included
1. tax included, including tax
Indicates that the displayed price includes consumption tax. The standard phrase seen on price tags and menus.
税込(ぜいこみ)百円(ひゃくえん)です。
It's 100 yen including tax.
価格(かかく)は税込(ぜいこみ)表示(ひょうじ)です。
Prices include tax.
税込(ぜいこみ)価格(かかく)で表示(ひょうじ)されているので分(わ)かりやすい。
It's easy to understand because prices are shown including tax.

COMMON USAGE:

  • 税込(ぜいこみ)価格(かかく): price including tax
  • 税込(ぜいこみ)表示(ひょうじ): tax-inclusive display
  • 税込(ぜいこみ)○○円(えん): ○○ yen including tax

ABBREVIATION:
Often written as 込(こみ) on price tags.

RELATED:

  • 税抜(ぜいぬき): tax not included
  • 消費税(しょうひぜい): consumption tax
