取(と)り扱(あつか)い

とりあつかい
noun
handling; treatment; dealing
1. handling; treatment
The way something is handled, managed, or treated, often referring to products, equipment, or services.
取(と)り扱(あつか)いに注意(ちゅうい)する。
To be careful with handling.
取(と)り扱(あつか)い説明書(せつめいしょ)を読(よ)む。
To read the instruction manual.
2. dealing with; carrying (products)
The act of selling or providing certain products or services.
当店(とうてん)では取(と)り扱(あつか)いが{ない。
We don't carry this product at our store.

取(と)り扱(あつか)い refers to handling or dealing with something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)い注意(ちゅうい) (handle with care)
  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)い説明書(せつめいしょ) (instruction manual)
  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)い店(てん) (authorized dealer)
  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)いがある/ない (to carry / not carry a product)

RELATED VERB:

  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)う: to handle, to deal with

RELATED TERMS:

  • 使(つか)い方(かた): how to use
  • 管理(かんり): management
  • 対応(たいおう): response, handling
