塔(とう)

とう
noun
tower; pagoda
1. tower; pagoda
A tall, narrow building or structure, either freestanding or part of a larger building.
塔(とう)が右(みぎ)に傾(かたむ)いている。
The tower is leaning to the right.
東京(とうきょう)タワーは有名(ゆうめい)な塔(とう)だ。
Tokyo Tower is a famous tower.
お寺(てら)には五重(ごじゅう)の塔(とう)がある。
The temple has a five-story pagoda.

塔(とう) refers to a tall structure, including both modern towers and traditional pagodas.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 管制(かんせい)塔(とう): control tower
  • 時計(とけい)塔(とう): clock tower
  • 五重(ごじゅう)の塔(とう): five-story pagoda

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese Buddhism, pagodas (塔(とう)) originally served as reliquaries for Buddha's ashes and are iconic features of temple architecture.
