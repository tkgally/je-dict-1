掻(か)く

かく
verb-godan
to scratch
1. to scratch; to scrape
To rub or scratch a surface with fingernails or claws, often to relieve an itch or to scrape something away.
頭(あたま)を掻(か)く。
I scratch my head.
かさぶたを掻(か)いてはいけない。
You shouldn't scratch the scab.
蚊(か)に刺(さ)されたところが痒(かゆ)くて掻(か)いてしまった。
The mosquito bite was so itchy that I ended up scratching it.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを掻(か)く

IDIOMS:

  • 頭(あたま)を掻(か)く (to scratch one's head - showing embarrassment or confusion)
  • 汗(あせ)を掻(か)く (to sweat)
  • 恥(はじ)を掻(か)く (to be embarrassed)

Note: Not to be confused with 書(か)く (to write) or 描(か)く (to draw).
