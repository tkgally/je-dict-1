品質(ひんしつ)

ひんしつ
noun
quality (of a product)
1. quality (of a product or item)
The standard or grade of excellence of a product or manufactured good.
品質(ひんしつ)がいい。
The quality is good.
品質(ひんしつ)を保(たも)つ。
To maintain quality.
高(たか)い。だけど、品質(ひんしつ)はいい。
It's expensive. But the quality is good.

品質(ひんしつ) refers to the quality of products or goods.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 品質(ひんしつ)管理(かんり): quality control
  • 品質(ひんしつ)保証(ほしょう): quality assurance
  • 高(こう)品質(ひんしつ): high quality

CONTRAST WITH 質(しつ):

  • 品質(ひんしつ): specifically product quality
  • 質(しつ): quality in general (sleep, life, etc.)
