1. to do laundry; to wash (clothes)
To clean clothes, linens, or other fabric items, typically using water and detergent.
毎日(まいにち)洗濯(せんたく)する。
I do laundry every day.
この服(ふく)は手(て)で洗濯(せんたく)してください。
Please wash these clothes by hand.
雨(あめ)で洗濯(せんたく)ができなかった。
I couldn't do laundry because of the rain.

洗濯(せんたく)する means to wash clothes or do laundry. One of the most common household verbs in Japanese.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを洗濯(せんたく)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 洗濯(せんたく)をする: to do laundry
  • 洗濯(せんたく)物(もの)を干(ほ)す: to hang laundry to dry
  • 洗濯(せんたく)物(もの)を取(と)り込(こ)む: to bring in the laundry
  • 手(て)洗(あら)いで洗濯(せんたく)する: to hand-wash

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 洗濯(せんたく)機(き): washing machine
  • 洗濯(せんたく)物(もの): laundry, clothes to wash
  • 洗濯(せんたく)洗剤(せんざい): laundry detergent
  • コインランドリー: laundromat

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, hanging laundry outside to dry (外干(そとぼ)し) is still very common. Many people check the 洗濯(せんたく)指数(しすう) (laundry drying index) in weather forecasts.
