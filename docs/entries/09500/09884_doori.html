どおり

どおり
suffix
as; in accordance with; just like
1. as; in accordance with; just like
Suffix indicating that something happens or is done according to a certain standard, expectation, or manner.
希望(きぼう)どおりの結果(けっか)になった。
The result turned out as I had hoped.
予定(よてい)どおりに進(すす)んでいる。
Things are proceeding as scheduled.
説明書(せつめいしょ)どおりに組(く)み立(た)てた。
I assembled it according to the instructions.

どおり is a suffix meaning "as" or "in accordance with."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 希望(きぼう)どおり: as hoped/wished
  • 予定(よてい)どおり: as scheduled/planned
  • 期待(きたい)どおり: as expected
  • 思(おも)ったどおり: as I thought
  • 言(い)ったどおり: as (I) said

OTHER USES:

  • そのどおり: exactly, that's right
  • このどおり: like this, as you can see
  • ～のどおりに: in the manner of ~

GRAMMAR:

  • Attaches to nouns: 計画(けいかく)どおり
  • Attaches to verb た-form: 言(い)ったどおり
  • Followed by に for adverbial use: どおりに進(すす)む
  • Followed by の for noun modification: どおりの結果(けっか)

SPELLING NOTE:
Written as どおり (long vowel), not どうり. Can also be written as 通(とお)り when attached to a noun using kanji.
