日用品(にちようひん)

にちようひん
noun
daily necessities, everyday goods
1. daily necessities, everyday goods
Items used in daily life, such as toiletries, cleaning supplies, and household basics.
日用品(にちようひん)を買(か)いに行(い)く。
To go buy daily necessities.
日用品(にちようひん)売(う)り場(ば)はどこですか。
Where is the daily necessities section?
ドラッグストアで日用品(にちようひん)と食品(しょくひん)を買(か)った。
I bought daily necessities and food at the drugstore.
