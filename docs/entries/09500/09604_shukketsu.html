出欠(しゅっけつ)

しゅっけつ
noun
attendance, presence and absence
1. attendance, presence and absence
The record or status of being present or absent, typically in schools or meetings.
出欠(しゅっけつ)を取(と)る。
To take attendance.
出欠(しゅっけつ)の確認(かくにん)をお願(ねが)いします。
Please confirm your attendance.
会議(かいぎ)への出欠(しゅっけつ)を週末(しゅうまつ)までに連絡(れんらく)してください。
Please let us know whether you will attend the meeting by the weekend.

出欠(しゅっけつ) combines 出席(しゅっせき) (presence) and 欠席(けっせき) (absence).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 出欠(しゅっけつ)を取(と)る: to take attendance
  • 出欠(しゅっけつ)の確認(かくにん): confirmation of attendance
  • 出欠(しゅっけつ)を連絡(れんらく)する: to inform of attendance status
  • 出欠(しゅっけつ)簿(ぼ): attendance register

USAGE NOTE:
Used in schools, meetings, and events. When responding to invitations, this refers to whether someone will attend or not.
