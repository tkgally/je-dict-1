集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく)

しゅうごうじゅうたく
noun
apartment building; multi-unit housing
1. apartment building; multi-unit housing; collective housing
A building containing multiple dwelling units, such as apartments, condominiums, or public housing.
集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in an apartment building.
集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく)ではゴミの分別(ぶんべつ)ルールが厳(きび)しい。
Apartment buildings have strict garbage sorting rules.
この地域(ちいき)には高層(こうそう)集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく)がたくさん建(た)っている。
There are many high-rise apartment buildings in this area.

集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく) is the general term for multi-unit housing.

TYPES INCLUDED:

  • マンション (condominium)
  • アパート (apartment)
  • 団地(だんち) (housing complex)
  • 社宅(しゃたく) (company housing)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく)の管理(かんり) (apartment building management)
  • 高層(こうそう)集合住宅(しゅうごうじゅうたく) (high-rise apartment building)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一戸建(いっこだ)て: detached house
  • マンション: condominium
  • 賃貸(ちんたい): rental
