不得手(ふえて)

ふえて
na-adjective
weak point, not good at, unskillful
1. weak point, not good at, unskillful
An area or skill where one lacks proficiency; something one is not adept at.
数学(すうがく)が不得手(ふえて)だ。
I'm not good at math.
人前(ひとまえ)で話(はな)すことが不得手(ふえて)だ。
I'm not good at speaking in front of people.
彼(かれ)は不得手(ふえて)な仕事(しごと)も逃(に)げずに取(と)り組(く)む。
He tackles even work he's not good at without running away.

不得手(ふえて) is a na-adjective meaning weak at or not skilled in something.

OPPOSITE:

  • 得手(えて): strong point, specialty

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 苦手(にがて): bad at, weak point (more common)
  • 不得意(ふとくい): not one's forte
  • 下手(へた): unskillful, poor at

USAGE:
不得手(ふえて) is somewhat more formal or literary than 苦手(にがて). Often used in expressions like 不得手(ふえて)な分野(ぶんや) (weak field) or 不得手(ふえて)な科目(かもく) (weak subject).
