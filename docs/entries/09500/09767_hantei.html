判定(はんてい)

はんてい
noun / suru verb
judgment, decision, ruling
1. judgment, decision, ruling
The act of making a judgment or decision, especially in competitions or official contexts.
偶数(ぐうすう)か奇数(きすう)かを判定(はんてい)するプログラム(ぷろぐらむ)を作(つく)った。
I created a program that determines whether a number is even or odd.
審判(しんぱん)の判定(はんてい)に従(したが)う。
To follow the referee's ruling.
判定(はんてい)勝(が)ちで優勝(ゆうしょう)した。
Won the championship by decision.

判定(はんてい) is used for making judgments or determinations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判定(はんてい)を下(くだ)す: to make a ruling
  • 判定(はんてい)勝(が)ち: win by decision
  • ビデオ判定(はんてい): video review

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 判断(はんだん): judgment, decision (general)
  • 裁定(さいてい): arbitration, ruling
