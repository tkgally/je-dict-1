免税(めんぜい)

めんぜい
noun
tax-free; duty-free
1. tax-free; tax exemption; duty-free
The exemption from taxes, particularly consumption tax for foreign visitors or customs duties at airports.
免税(めんぜい)で買(か)い物(もの)ができる。
You can shop tax-free.
免税(めんぜい)手続(てつづ)きはどこでできますか。
Where can I complete the tax-free procedure?
空港(くうこう)の免税(めんぜい)店(てん)でお土産(みやげ)を買(か)った。
I bought souvenirs at the duty-free shop in the airport.

免税(めんぜい) refers to tax exemption, especially for tourists.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 免税(めんぜい)店(てん) (duty-free shop)
  • 免税(めんぜい)手続(てつづ)き (tax-free procedure)
  • 免税(めんぜい)対象(たいしょう) (eligible for tax exemption)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Available to foreign tourists in Japan
  • Requires passport and minimum purchase amount
  • Look for "Tax Free" signs in stores

RELATED TERMS:

  • 消費税(しょうひぜい): consumption tax
  • 税込(ぜいこ)み: tax included
  • 税抜(ぜいぬ)き: tax not included
