転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)

てんにゅうとどけ
noun
moving-in notification
1. moving-in notification
An official form submitted to the local ward or city office when moving into a new municipality in Japan.
転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)は早(はや)めに出(だ)そう。
Let's submit the moving-in notification early.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し後(ご)14日(じゅうよっか)以内(いない)に転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)を出(だ)す。
Submit the moving-in notification within 14 days after moving.
転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)には転出(てんしゅつ)証明書(しょうめいしょ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
A moving-out certificate is required for the moving-in notification.

転入届(てんにゅうとどけ) must be submitted within 14 days of moving to a new municipality. You need to bring your 転出(てんしゅつ)証明書(しょうめいしょ) from your previous location.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit a moving-in notification)
  • 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)の届出(とどけで) (filing a moving-in notification)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ): moving-out notification
  • 転居届(てんきょとどけ): change of address notice
  • 住民票(じゅうみんひょう): certificate of residence
