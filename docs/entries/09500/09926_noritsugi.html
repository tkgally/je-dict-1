乗(の)り継(つ)ぎ

のりつぎ
noun
transfer, transit, connection
1. transfer, transit, connection
Changing from one mode of transportation to another, or from one flight/train to another, during a journey.
シンガポールで乗(の)り継(つ)ぎがある。
There's a transfer in Singapore.
乗(の)り継(つ)ぎ時間(じかん)は2時間(じかん)です。
The layover time is two hours.
乗(の)り継(つ)ぎに失敗(しっぱい)して、次(つぎ)の便(びん)を待(ま)つことになった。
I missed my connection and had to wait for the next flight.

Used for both air travel (connecting flights) and ground transportation (transferring trains or buses).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乗(の)り継(つ)ぎ便(びん) (connecting flight)
  • 乗(の)り継(つ)ぎ時間(じかん) (layover/transfer time)
  • 乗(の)り継(つ)ぎ客(きゃく) (transit passenger)
  • 乗(の)り継(つ)ぎをする (to make a transfer)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 乗(の)り換(か)え: changing trains (often used for trains within a city)
  • 直行便(ちょっこうびん): direct flight
  • 経由(けいゆ): via, by way of
