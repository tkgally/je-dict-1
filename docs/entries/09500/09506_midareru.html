乱(みだ)れる

みだれる
verb (ichidan)
to be disordered, to be disturbed, to be disrupted
1. to be disordered, to be in disarray, to be disturbed
To become disorganized, confused, or thrown into disorder. Can refer to physical things, schedules, emotions, or social order.
髪(かみ)が乱(みだ)れている。
My hair is messy.
ダイヤが乱(みだ)れている。
The (train) schedule is disrupted.
心(こころ)が乱(みだ)れる。
To feel emotionally disturbed.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xが乱(みだ)れる.
Transitive pair: 乱(みだ)す (to disturb, to throw into disorder)

ASPECT (ている):
乱(みだ)れている indicates a resulting state: "is in disorder/disrupted."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ダイヤが乱(みだ)れる: train schedule is disrupted
  • 髪(かみ)が乱(みだ)れる: hair becomes messy
  • 心(こころ)が乱(みだ)れる: to feel disturbed
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)が乱(みだ)れる: order is disrupted
