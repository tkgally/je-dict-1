駅前(えきまえ)

えきまえ
noun
in front of the station, station area
1. in front of the station, station square, station area
The area immediately in front of a train/subway station.
駅前(えきまえ)広場(ひろば)でイベントが開(ひら)かれている。
An event is being held in the station square.
駅前(えきまえ)のコンビニで待(ま)ち合(あ)わせしよう。
Let's meet at the convenience store in front of the station.
駅前(えきまえ)に新(あたら)しいホテルができた。
A new hotel was built in front of the station.

駅前(えきまえ) refers to the area in front of a train station.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駅前(えきまえ)広場(ひろば): station square/plaza
  • 駅前(えきまえ)通(どお)り: street in front of the station
  • 駅前(えきまえ)開発(かいはつ): station-front development

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 駅(えき): station
  • 改札(かいさつ): ticket gate
  • 駅(えき)ビル: station building

NOTE: Station areas in Japan are often commercial hubs with shops, restaurants, and meeting spots.
