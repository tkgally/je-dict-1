信(しん)じ込(こ)む

しんじこむ
godan verb
to firmly believe, to be convinced
1. to firmly believe, to be convinced, to be under the impression
To believe something completely without doubt, often regardless of whether it is actually true.
彼女(かのじょ)は噂(うわさ)を信(しん)じ込(こ)んでいる。
She firmly believes the rumor.
自分(じぶん)が正(ただ)しいと信(しん)じ込(こ)んでいた。
I was convinced that I was right.
詐欺師(さぎし)の話(はなし)を信(しん)じ込(こ)んでお金(かね)を騙(だま)し取(と)られた。
I believed the scammer's story completely and was cheated out of money.

信(しん)じ込(こ)む is a compound verb with ～込(こ)む adding the sense of thoroughness or completion.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを信(しん)じ込(こ)む / Xと信(しん)じ込(こ)む

ASPECT (ている):

  • 信(しん)じ込(こ)んでいる: is firmly convinced (state)

NUANCE:
Often implies blind or possibly mistaken belief. More emphatic than 信(しん)じる alone.

COMPOSITION:

  • 信(しん)じる: to believe
  • ～込(こ)む: suffix adding completeness/thoroughness
