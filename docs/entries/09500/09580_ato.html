後(あと)

あと
noun
after, later, remaining
1. after, later
The time following an event or action.
昼食(ちゅうしょく)の後(あと)、少(すこ)し昼寝(ひるね)をした。
After lunch, I took a short nap.
後(あと)で連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you later.
後(あと)から来(く)る。
I'll come later. / I'll follow behind.
2. rest, remainder, remaining
What is left over; the remaining portion.
後(あと)五分(ごふん)で終(お)わる。
It will be done in five more minutes.
後(あと)は任(まか)せて。
Leave the rest to me.
3. behind, back
The position or location at the rear.
後(あと)から来(く)る。
I'll come later. / I'll follow behind.

後(あと) is a common word meaning "after" or "remaining."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 後(あと)で (later)
  • 〜の後(あと) (after ~)
  • 後(あと)から (afterward, from behind)
  • もう少(すこ)しの後(あと)で (in a little while)
  • あと〜 (~ more, remaining ~)

"REMAINING" USAGE:

  • あと五分(ごふん) (five more minutes)
  • あと三(みっ)つ (three more)
  • あとは〜 (as for the rest...)

CONTRAST:

  • 後(あと): everyday/spoken style
  • 後(のち): formal/written style
  • 後(ご): in compounds (食後(しょくご), 午後(ごご))

NOTE: Not to be confused with 跡(あと) (trace, mark).
