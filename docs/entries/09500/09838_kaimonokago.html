買(か)い物(もの)かご

かいものかご
noun
shopping basket
1. shopping basket
A basket provided by stores for customers to carry items while shopping, typically placed near the entrance.
買(か)い物(もの)かごを取(と)って。
Grab a shopping basket.
買(か)い物(もの)かごがいっぱいになった。
The shopping basket got full.
買(か)い物(もの)かごをカートに乗(の)せる。
Put the shopping basket on the cart.

In Japanese supermarkets, there's often a system where you use one 買(か)い物(もの)かご while shopping, then transfer items to a clean basket at checkout for the cashier to pack.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 買(か)い物(もの)かごを持(も)つ (to carry a shopping basket)
  • 買(か)い物(もの)かごに入(い)れる (to put in the shopping basket)
  • 買(か)い物(もの)かご置(お)き場(ば) (shopping basket station)

RELATED TERMS:

  • カート: shopping cart
  • レジかご: checkout basket
  • マイバッグ: reusable bag
