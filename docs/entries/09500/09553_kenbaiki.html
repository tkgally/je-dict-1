券売機(けんばいき)

けんばいき
noun
ticket vending machine
1. ticket vending machine
A machine for purchasing tickets, commonly found at train stations and restaurants.
券売機(けんばいき)で切符(きっぷ)を買(か)う。
To buy a ticket at the ticket machine.
券売機(けんばいき)は現金(げんきん)のみです。
The ticket machine accepts cash only.
ラーメン屋(や)の券売機(けんばいき)で食券(しょっけん)を買(か)った。
I bought a meal ticket at the ramen shop's ticket machine.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 駅(えき): train stations
  • ラーメン屋(や): ramen shops (order at machine first)
  • 食堂(しょくどう): cafeterias

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 券売機(けんばいき)で買(か)う: to buy at the ticket machine
  • 食券(しょっけん): meal ticket

RELATED:

  • 切符(きっぷ): ticket
  • 食券(しょっけん): meal ticket
  • 精算機(せいさんき): fare adjustment machine
