1. annotation, commentary, notes
Explanatory notes or comments added to a text to clarify its meaning.
注釈(ちゅうしゃく)を括弧(かっこ)に入(い)れる。
To put annotations in parentheses.
この本(ほん)には詳(くわ)しい注釈(ちゅうしゃく)が付(つ)いている。
This book has detailed annotations.
難(むずか)しい言葉(ことば)に注釈(ちゅうしゃく)を付(つ)ける。
To add annotations to difficult words.

注釈(ちゅうしゃく) refers to explanatory notes or comments added to a text to help the reader understand difficult words, historical context, or technical terms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注釈(ちゅうしゃく)を付(つ)ける: to add annotations
  • 注釈(ちゅうしゃく)を入(い)れる: to insert notes
  • 注釈(ちゅうしゃく)付(つ)き: annotated, with notes
  • 注釈(ちゅうしゃく)書(しょ): annotated edition, commentary

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 解説(かいせつ): explanation, commentary (more detailed and interpretive)
  • 脚注(きゃくちゅう): footnote (specifically at the bottom of a page)
  • 注(ちゅう): note (shorter form, often used in academic writing)
  • 補足(ほそく): supplement, additional explanation
