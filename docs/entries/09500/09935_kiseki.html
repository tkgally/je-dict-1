奇跡(きせき)

きせき
noun
miracle
1. miracle
An extraordinary event that seems impossible to explain by natural or scientific laws.
これは奇跡(きせき)だ。
This is a miracle.
奇跡(きせき)が起(お)きた。
A miracle happened.
奇跡(きせき)的(てき)に助(たす)かった。
He/She miraculously survived.

奇跡(きせき) refers to an extraordinary event beyond normal explanation.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 奇跡(きせき)が起(お)きる: a miracle happens
  • 奇跡(きせき)的(てき)に: miraculously
  • 奇跡(きせき)を信(しん)じる: to believe in miracles

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不思議(ふしぎ): mystery, wonder (less dramatic than 奇跡(きせき))
