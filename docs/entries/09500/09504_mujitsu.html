無実(むじつ)

むじつ
noun / na-adjective
innocence, not guilty
1. innocence, not guilty
The state of being innocent of a crime or accusation; having not committed the alleged offense.
彼(かれ)は無実(むじつ)だ。
He is innocent.
無実(むじつ)を証明(しょうめい)する。
To prove one's innocence.
神(かみ)に誓(ちか)って、私(わたし)は無実(むじつ)です。
I swear to God, I am innocent.

無実(むじつ) means innocence or being not guilty.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 無実(むじつ)の罪(つみ): false accusation, being wrongly accused
  • 無実(むじつ)を証明(しょうめい)する: to prove one's innocence
  • 無実(むじつ)を主張(しゅちょう)する: to claim innocence

CONTRAST:

  • 無実(むじつ): innocent (not guilty of crime)
  • 無罪(むざい): not guilty (legal verdict)
