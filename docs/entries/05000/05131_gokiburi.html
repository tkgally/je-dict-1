ゴキブリ

ごきぶり
noun
cockroach
1. cockroach
A common household insect pest known for its flat body, long antennae, and ability to survive in various conditions.
台所(だいどころ)でゴキブリを見(み)た。
I saw a cockroach in the kitchen.
ゴキブリが苦手(にがて)だ。
I can't stand cockroaches.
ゴキブリ退治(たいじ)のスプレーを買(か)った。
I bought cockroach spray.

ゴキブリ is one of the most feared household pests in Japan, especially during the hot and humid summer months.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴキブリが出(で)る: cockroaches appear (in the house)
  • ゴキブリを退治(たいじ)する: to exterminate cockroaches
  • ゴキブリ対策(たいさく): cockroach countermeasures
  • ゴキブリホイホイ: cockroach trap (brand name used generically)
  • ゴキブリ駆除(くじょ): cockroach extermination

CULTURE:
Many Japanese people have a strong aversion to cockroaches. In casual speech and online, people often refer to them simply as "G" (ジー) to avoid saying the word directly.

SEASON:
Most commonly seen in 夏(なつ) (summer) when heat and humidity create ideal conditions. Prevention products sell strongly before summer begins.
