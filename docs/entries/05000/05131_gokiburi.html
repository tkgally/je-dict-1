ゴキブリ

ごきぶり
noun
cockroach
1. cockroach
A common household insect pest known for its flat body, long antennae, and ability to survive in various conditions.
台所(だいどころ)でゴキブリを見(み)た。
I saw a cockroach in the kitchen.
ゴキブリが苦手(にがて)だ。
I can't stand cockroaches.
ゴキブリ退治(たいじ)のスプレーを買(か)った。
I bought cockroach spray.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴキブリを見(み)る (to see a cockroach)
  • ゴキブリが出(で)る (cockroaches appear)
  • ゴキブリを退治(たいじ)する (to exterminate cockroaches)
  • ゴキブリホイホイ (cockroach trap - brand name often used generically)

NICKNAMES:

  • G - Sometimes written as just "G" in casual Japanese to avoid saying the word directly.
