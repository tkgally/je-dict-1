ざらざら

ざらざら
adverb
rough; coarse; grainy
1. rough; coarse
Describes a surface that is rough to the touch, like sandpaper or dry skin.
紙(かみ)やすりはざらざらしている。
Sandpaper is rough.
冬(ふゆ)になると肌(はだ)がざらざらになる。
My skin becomes rough in winter.
ざらざらした感触(かんしょく)の壁(かべ)。
A wall with a rough texture.

ざらざら is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) describing rough texture.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ざらざらする: to feel rough
  • ざらざらした〜: rough (adj. modifier)
  • ざらざらになる: to become rough

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ごつごつ: rugged, bumpy (larger irregularities)
  • かさかさ: dry and rough (specifically dry skin)
  • すべすべ: smooth (opposite)
general
