日向(ひなた)

ひなた
noun
sunny place, sunshine

Definitions

1. sunny place, sunshine, sunlit spot
A place exposed to direct sunlight, as opposed to shade.

Examples

猫(ねこ)が日向(ひなた)で気持(きも)ちよさそうに寝(ね)ている。
The cat is sleeping contentedly in the sunny spot.
冬(ふゆ)は日向(ひなた)で本(ほん)を読(よ)むのが好(す)きだ。
In winter, I like reading books in the sunshine.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を日向(ひなた)に干(ほ)す。
I hang the laundry out in the sun.

Notes

日向(ひなた) refers to a sunny spot or place in the sun. 日(ひ) means 'sun' and 向(なた) relates to direction/facing.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 日向(ひなた)で干(ほ)す: to dry in the sun
  • 日向(ひなた)ぼっこ: sunbathing, basking in the sun
  • 日向(ひなた)の匂(にお)い: the smell of sunshine (on dried clothes/bedding)
  • 日向(ひなた)と日陰(ひかげ): sunny and shady spots

OPPOSITE:

  • 日陰(ひかげ): shady spot, shade

AS NAME:

  • 日向(ひなた) is a common given name (unisex)
  • 日向(ひゅうが): old name for Miyazaki Prefecture

IDIOMATIC:

  • 日向(ひなた)に出(で)る: to come into the light/spotlight
