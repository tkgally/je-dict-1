玉葱(たまねぎ)

たまねぎ
noun
onion
1. onion
A round bulb vegetable with layers, used widely in cooking.
玉葱(たまねぎ)を切(き)ると涙(なみだ)が出(で)る。
When I cut onions, tears come out.
玉葱(たまねぎ)を炒(いた)めて飴色(あめいろ)にする。
To sauté onions until caramelized.
カレーには玉葱(たまねぎ)が欠(か)かせない。
Onion is essential for curry.
新(しん)玉葱(たまねぎ)は甘(あま)くて生(なま)でも食(た)べられる。
New onions are sweet and can be eaten raw.
ハンバーグを作(つく)る時(とき)に玉葱(たまねぎ)をみじん切(ぎ)りにして混(ま)ぜる。
When making hamburger steaks, I dice onions and mix them in.

玉葱(たまねぎ) is one of the most commonly used vegetables in Japanese cooking, appearing in curries, stir-fries, soups, and salads. Often written in katakana as タマネギ or hiragana as たまねぎ.

WORD FORMATION:
玉(たま) (ball) + 葱(ねぎ) (green onion) — literally 'ball onion,' distinguishing it from 長(なが)葱(ねぎ) (long green onion).

COMMON PREPARATIONS:

  • みじん切(ぎ)り: diced/minced
  • 薄切(うすぎ)り: thinly sliced
  • 飴色(あめいろ)玉葱(たまねぎ): caramelized onion
  • 酢(す)玉葱(たまねぎ): pickled onion

VARIETIES:

  • 新(しん)玉葱(たまねぎ): new/spring onion (sweeter, available in spring)
  • 赤(あか)玉葱(たまねぎ): red onion
  • 紫(むらさき)玉葱(たまねぎ): purple onion (same as red onion)
