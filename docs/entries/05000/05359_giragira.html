ぎらぎら

ぎらぎら
adverb
glaring; dazzling (harshly)
1. glaring; dazzling; blindingly bright
Describes intense, harsh brightness that is uncomfortable to look at.
2. greedy; hungry (eyes)
Describes eyes that show intense desire or greed.
夏(なつ)の太陽(たいよう)がぎらぎら照(て)りつける。
The summer sun is blazing down harshly.
ぎらぎらした目(め)で見(み)ていた。
He was watching with greedy eyes.
ぎらぎら光(ひか)るネオンサイン。
Glaringly bright neon signs.

ぎらぎら suggests harsh, uncomfortable brightness, unlike きらきら which is pleasant sparkling.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きらきら: sparkling, glittering (pleasant)
  • ぴかぴか: shiny, sparkling (clean/polished)
  • てかてか: shiny, glossy (surface shine)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぎらぎら光る: to glare, to shine harshly
  • ぎらぎらした目: greedy/hungry eyes
