与党(よとう)

よとう
noun
ruling party; party in power

Definitions

1. ruling party; party in power
The political party or coalition that holds government power.

Examples

与党(よとう)が法案(ほうあん)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
The ruling party submitted the bill.
与党(よとう)と野党(やとう)の対立(たいりつ)が続(つづ)いている。
The conflict between the ruling and opposition parties continues.
与党(よとう)が選挙(せんきょ)で勝(か)った。
The ruling party won the election.

Notes

与党(よとう) (与 = give/participate + 党 = party)

CONTRAST:

  • 野党(やとう): opposition party
  • 連立(れんりつ)与党(よとう): coalition government

RELATED:

  • 政権(せいけん): administration, government
  • 内閣(ないかく): cabinet
  • 首相(しゅしょう): prime minister
