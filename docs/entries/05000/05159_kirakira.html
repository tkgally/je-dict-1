きらきら

きらきら
adverb
sparkling; twinkling; glittering
1. sparkling; twinkling
Describes small points of light shining or flickering, like stars, jewels, or reflections on water.
星(ほし)がきらきら輝(かがや)いている。
The stars are twinkling brightly.
ダイヤモンドがきらきら光(ひか)っている。
The diamond is sparkling.
彼女(かのじょ)の目(め)がきらきらしていた。
Her eyes were sparkling.
Often used to describe bright, lively eyes

きらきら is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語(ぎたいご)) describing gentle, beautiful sparkling light.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • きらきら光(ひか)る: to sparkle, twinkle
  • きらきら輝(かがや)く: to shine brilliantly
  • きらきらした〜: sparkling (adj. modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぴかぴか: shiny, polished (continuous shine, not twinkling)
  • ぎらぎら: glaring, dazzling (harsh, intense light)
  • てかてか: shiny, glossy (oily or plastic-like shine)
