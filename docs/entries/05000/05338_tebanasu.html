手放(てばな)す

てばなす
godan verb
to let go of; to part with
1. to let go of; to release one's hold
To physically release something from one's hand.
2. to part with; to give up
To sell, dispose of, or give away something one has owned.
子供(こども)の手(て)を手放(てばな)さないで。
Don't let go of the child's hand.
古(ふる)い車(くるま)を手放(てばな)すことにした。
I decided to part with my old car.
この本(ほん)はどうしても手放(てばな)せない。
I just can't part with this book.
手放せない = can't let go of (emotionally attached)

手放す is a transitive godan verb. The compound comes from 手 (hand) + 放す (to release).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 手放す (transitive): to let go of

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を手放す: to let go of ~, to part with ~
  • 手放せない: can't let go of (emotionally attached)
  • 手放しで: unreservedly, wholeheartedly (as an adverb)
