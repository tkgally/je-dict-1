出金(しゅっきん)

しゅっきん
noun / suru verb
withdrawal; disbursement
1. withdrawal; disbursement; cash withdrawal
Money going out of an account or being withdrawn.
ATMで出金(しゅっきん)する。
To withdraw money from an ATM.
出金(しゅっきん)履歴(りれき)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the withdrawal history.
一日(いちにち)の出金(しゅっきん)限度額(げんどがく)は50万(まん)円(えん)です。
The daily withdrawal limit is 500,000 yen.

出金(しゅっきん) refers to money being withdrawn or going out.

CONTRAST:

  • 出金(しゅっきん): money going out (withdrawal)
  • 入金(にゅうきん): money coming in (deposit)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 出金(しゅっきん)手数料(てすうりょう): withdrawal fee
  • 出金(しゅっきん)限度額(げんどがく): withdrawal limit
  • 出金(しゅっきん)履歴(りれき): withdrawal history
