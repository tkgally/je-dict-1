ひやひや

ひやひや
adverb
nervously; anxiously; on edge

Definitions

1. nervously; anxiously; on edge
Feeling nervous or anxious while watching something dangerous or uncertain.
2. feeling chilly
A cool or slightly cold sensation.

Examples

試合(しあい)を見(み)ながらひやひやした。
I watched the match nervously.
ひやひやする場面(ばめん)が多(おお)かった。
There were many nerve-wracking scenes.
ひやひやした風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
A chilly wind is blowing.

Notes

ひやひや often describes the nervous feeling of watching something risky or uncertain.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • はらはら: anxiously watching (similar)
  • どきどき: heart pounding (excitement or nervousness)
  • びくびく: nervously, fearfully

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ひやひやする: to feel nervous/anxious
  • ひやひやしながら見る: to watch nervously
