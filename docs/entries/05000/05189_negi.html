葱(ねぎ)

ねぎ
noun
green onion; leek; scallion
1. green onion; scallion; leek
A type of onion with long green stalks, commonly used as a garnish and ingredient in Japanese cooking.
味噌汁(みそしる)に葱(ねぎ)を入(い)れる。
To add green onion to miso soup.
葱(ねぎ)を小口切(こぐちぎ)りにする。
To slice green onion into thin rounds.
ラーメンに葱(ねぎ)をたっぷり乗(の)せる。
To put lots of green onion on ramen.
葱(ねぎ)を買(か)うのを忘(わす)れた。
I forgot to buy green onions.
鍋(なべ)に葱(ねぎ)と豆腐(とうふ)と肉(にく)を入(い)れて煮(に)た。
I put green onion, tofu, and meat into the pot and simmered it.

葱(ねぎ) is an essential ingredient in Japanese cuisine, used both as a main ingredient and as a garnish. The word covers several varieties that English distinguishes as separate vegetables.

TYPES:

  • 長(なが)葱(ねぎ)/白(しろ)葱(ねぎ): Japanese leek (thick, white part prominent)
  • 青(あお)葱(ねぎ)/万能(ばんのう)葱(ねぎ): green onion/scallion (thin, mostly green)
  • 小(こ)葱(ねぎ): thin green onion (for garnishing)
  • わけぎ: Welsh onion

COMMON USES:

  • 薬味(やくみ) (garnish): for ラーメン, うどん, 味噌汁(みそしる), 蕎麦(そば)
  • 鍋(なべ) (hot pot): cut into thick pieces
  • 焼(や)き鳥(とり): grilled between pieces of chicken (ねぎま)

REGIONAL NOTE:
In eastern Japan (Kanto), 葱(ねぎ) typically refers to 長(なが)葱(ねぎ) (the thick white type). In western Japan (Kansai), it usually means 青(あお)葱(ねぎ) (the thin green type).
