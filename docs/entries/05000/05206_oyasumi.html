おやすみ

おやすみ
expression
good night (casual)
1. good night (casual)
A casual greeting used when parting for the night or before going to bed.
じゃあね、おやすみ。
See you, good night.
おやすみ、いい夢(ゆめ)見(み)てね。
Good night, sweet dreams.
子供(こども)に「おやすみ」と言(い)った。
I said 'good night' to the child.

おやすみ is the casual form of the nighttime/bedtime greeting.

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • おやすみ: casual (friends, family, children)
  • おやすみなさい: polite (general use)

USAGE:

  • Said before going to sleep
  • Said when parting at night
  • Can be used as a farewell in late evening

KANJI: From お休(やす)み (rest). Same stem as 休(やす)む (to rest).

TEXTING: Often shortened to おやすみ〜 or おやすー in casual messages.
