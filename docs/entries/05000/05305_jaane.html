じゃあね

じゃあね
expression
see you; bye (casual)
1. see you; bye; later
A casual way to say goodbye to friends or peers.
じゃあね、また明日(あした)！
See you, see you tomorrow!
じゃあね、気(き)をつけてね。
Bye, take care.
もう行(い)くね。じゃあね！
I'm going now. Bye!

じゃあね is a casual farewell expression used among friends.

VARIATIONS:

  • じゃあ: even more casual
  • じゃあな: masculine
  • じゃ: very casual abbreviation

FORMALITY SCALE (casual → formal):

  • じゃあね (casual, friends)
  • またね (casual, friendly)
  • では (semi-formal)
  • 失礼(しつれい)します (formal)
  • さようなら (formal, less common daily)
