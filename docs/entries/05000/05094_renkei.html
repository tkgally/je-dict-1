連携(れんけい)

れんけい
noun / suru-verb
cooperation, coordination

Definitions

1. cooperation, coordination, collaboration
Working together in a coordinated manner; linking up or integrating efforts between individuals, organizations, or systems.

Examples

他(た)部署(ぶしょ)との連携(れんけい)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Cooperation with other departments is important.
警察(けいさつ)と消防(しょうぼう)が連携(れんけい)して対応(たいおう)した。
The police and fire department responded in coordination.
このアプリはSNSと連携(れんけい)できる。
This app can link with social media.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連携(れんけい)する (to cooperate, to link)
  • 連携(れんけい)を取(と)る (to coordinate)
  • 連携(れんけい)を強化(きょうか)する (to strengthen cooperation)
  • 連携(れんけい)プレー (coordinated play, teamwork)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Used both for human cooperation and technical integration (e.g., software linking).
  • Common in business and organizational contexts.
