1. to mutter; to murmur
To speak in a low voice, often to oneself.
彼(かれ)は何(なに)か呟(つぶや)いていた。
He was muttering something.
「疲(つか)れた」と呟(つぶや)いた。
I murmured, 'I'm tired.'
小(ちい)さな声(こえ)で文句(もんく)を呟(つぶや)いた。
I muttered a complaint in a small voice.
隣(となり)の席(せき)の人(ひと)が何(なに)か呟(つぶや)いているのが聞(き)こえた。
I could hear the person in the next seat muttering something.
2. to tweet; to post (on social media)
Modern usage referring to posting short messages on social media platforms.
SNSで日常(にちじょう)のことを呟(つぶや)いている。
I tweet about daily life on social media.
Modern usage for social media posting
彼女(かのじょ)は思(おも)ったことをすぐSNSに呟(つぶや)く。
She immediately tweets whatever she's thinking on social media.
友達(ともだち)が面白(おもしろ)い動画(どうが)を見(み)つけてSNSに呟(つぶや)いた。
My friend found a funny video and tweeted about it on social media.

呟(つぶや)く originally means to speak quietly, almost to oneself. With the rise of social media, it gained a second meaning of posting short messages online.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) / 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Can take a direct object (文句(もんく)を呟(つぶや)く) or be used alone (何(なに)か呟(つぶや)いている)

ASPECT (ている):
呟(つぶや)いている means 'is muttering' (ongoing action). Often describes someone speaking under their breath.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぼそぼそ言(い)う: to mumble (emphasizes unclear speech)
  • 独(ひと)り言(ごと)を言(い)う: to talk to oneself
  • ささやく: to whisper (directed at someone)

MODERN USAGE:
Associated with Twitter/X, where "tweet" was rendered as ツイート or つぶやき (noun form). SNSに呟(つぶや)く means 'to post on social media.'

Related Words

Synonym: 囁(ささや)く (to whisper (to someone))
Related: 独(ひと)り言(ごと) (talking to oneself)
