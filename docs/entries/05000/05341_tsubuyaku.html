呟(つぶや)く

つぶやく
godan verb
to mutter; to murmur; to tweet
1. to mutter; to murmur
To speak in a low voice, often to oneself.
彼(かれ)は何(なに)か呟(つぶや)いていた。
He was muttering something.
「疲(つか)れた」と呟(つぶや)いた。
I murmured, 'I'm tired.'
2. to tweet; to post (on social media)
Modern usage referring to posting short messages on social media platforms.
SNSで日常(にちじょう)のことを呟(つぶや)いている。
I tweet about daily life on social media.
Modern usage for social media posting

呟(つぶや)く originally means to speak quietly to oneself. With the rise of Twitter, it gained a second meaning related to posting on social media.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぼそぼそ言(い)う: to mumble
  • 独(ひと)り言(ごと)を言(い)う (ひとりごとをいう): to talk to oneself

MODERN USAGE:
The verb became associated with Twitter (ツイッター) posts, as the Japanese name for "tweet" is ツイート or つぶやき (noun form).
