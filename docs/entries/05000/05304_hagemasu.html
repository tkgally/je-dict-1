1. to encourage; to cheer up; to hearten
To give someone support, confidence, or motivation.
友達(ともだち)を励(はげ)ます。
To encourage a friend.
落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる彼(かれ)を励(はげ)ました。
I cheered up my depressed friend.
応援(おうえん)の言葉(ことば)で励(はげ)まされた。
I was encouraged by words of support.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を励(はげ)ます: to encourage someone
  • 励(はげ)ましの言葉(ことば): words of encouragement
  • 互(たが)いに励(はげ)まし合(あ)う: to encourage each other

ASPECT (ている):
励(はげ)ましている describes the ongoing act of encouraging someone.

PASSIVE:
励(はげ)まされる (to be encouraged) is very common — often more natural than the active form in Japanese.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 励(はげ)み: encouragement, incentive (noun)
  • 励(はげ)む: to strive, to work hard (different verb — intransitive)
  • 応援(おうえん)する: to cheer on, to support (broader)
  • 元気(げんき)づける: to cheer up (more casual)

NUANCE:
励(はげ)ます implies emotional support directed at someone who is struggling or discouraged.

Related Words

Synonym: 応援(おうえん) (support, cheering)
Synonym: 元気(げんき)づける (to cheer up (casual))
Synonym: 鼓舞(こぶ) (formal, rallying)
