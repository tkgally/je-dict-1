キャベツ

きゃべつ
noun
cabbage
1. cabbage
A leafy green vegetable commonly used in salads, stir-fries, and other dishes.
キャベツの千切(せんぎ)りを作(つく)る。
To make shredded cabbage.
とんかつにはキャベツが付(つ)いている。
Tonkatsu comes with cabbage.
キャベツと豚肉(ぶたにく)を炒(いた)める。
To stir-fry cabbage and pork.

キャベツ comes from English "cabbage" and is one of the most widely used vegetables in Japanese cooking.

COMMON USES:

  • とんかつ/コロッケの付(つ)け合(あ)わせ: shredded raw cabbage served with fried foods
  • 野菜(やさい)炒(いた)め: stir-fried vegetables
  • 餃子(ぎょうざ)の具(ぐ): gyoza filling
  • ロールキャベツ: stuffed cabbage rolls
  • お好(この)み焼(や)き: a key ingredient in okonomiyaki

VARIETIES:

  • 春(はる)キャベツ: spring cabbage (softer, sweeter leaves)
  • 紫(むらさき)キャベツ: red/purple cabbage

CULTURE:
Shredded raw cabbage (千切(せんぎ)りキャベツキャベツ) is the standard accompaniment for tonkatsu and other fried dishes at restaurants across Japan.
