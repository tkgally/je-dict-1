預金(よきん)

よきん
noun
deposit; savings
1. deposit; savings
Money deposited in a bank account.
預金(よきん)を下(お)ろす。
To withdraw a deposit.
定期(ていき)預金(よきん)にする。
To put it in a fixed deposit.
預金(よきん)残高(ざんだか)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the account balance.

預金 is essential banking vocabulary for money held in accounts.

TYPES:

  • 普通預金 (ふつうよきん): ordinary deposit
  • 定期預金 (ていきよきん): fixed deposit
  • 当座預金 (とうざよきん): checking deposit

VERB USAGE:

  • 預金する: to deposit money
  • 預金を下ろす: to make a withdrawal

Related Words

Related: 口座(こうざ) (bank account)
