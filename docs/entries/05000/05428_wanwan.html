わんわん

わんわん
adverb
bow-wow; woof (dog barking)

Definitions

1. bow-wow; woof
The sound a dog makes when barking.
2. doggie (baby talk)
A child's word for dog.

Examples

犬(いぬ)がわんわん吠(ほ)えている。
The dog is barking loudly.
わんわん、来(き)て！
Doggie, come here!
Baby talk
わんわん泣(な)く。
To cry loudly (like a child).
Figurative usage

Notes

わんわん is a common onomatopoeia for dog barking and also baby talk for dog.

OTHER ANIMAL SOUNDS:

  • にゃんにゃん: cat meow
  • もうもう: cow moo
  • ぴよぴよ: chick chirp
  • コケコッコー: rooster crow

USAGE NOTE:
わんわん泣く means crying loudly like a child.
