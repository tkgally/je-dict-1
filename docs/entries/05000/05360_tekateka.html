てかてか

てかてか
adverb
shiny; glossy; oily
1. shiny; glossy; gleaming
Describes a smooth, reflective surface shine.
床(ゆか)をてかてかに磨(みが)いた。
I polished the floor until it shone.
てかてかの新車(しんしゃ)。
A shiny new car.
2. oily; greasy (skin)
Describes skin or surfaces that are shiny due to oil.
顔(かお)がてかてかしている。
Your face is oily/shiny.
Often refers to oily skin

てかてか describes surface shine, often from polish or oil.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぴかぴか: sparkling clean/new
  • つやつや: glossy, lustrous (healthy shine)
  • ぎらぎら: glaring (harsh brightness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • てかてかする: to be shiny/oily
  • てかてかに磨く: to polish until shiny
