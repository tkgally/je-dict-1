にゃんにゃん

にゃんにゃん
adverb
meow; mew (cat sound)

Definitions

1. meow; mew
The sound a cat makes.
2. kitty (baby talk)
A child's word for cat.

Examples

猫(ねこ)がにゃんにゃん鳴(な)いている。
The cat is meowing.
にゃんにゃん、かわいいね。
The kitty is cute, isn't it?
Baby talk
にゃんにゃん大好(だいす)き！
I love kitties!
Child's speech

Notes

にゃんにゃん is the doubled form of にゃん (meow).

VARIATIONS:

  • にゃー: meow (single)
  • にゃーにゃー: meowing repeatedly
  • にゃお: mew (softer)

BABY TALK:
Like わんわん for dogs, にゃんにゃん is used by children to refer to cats.

Related Words

Related: わんわん (dog sound)
