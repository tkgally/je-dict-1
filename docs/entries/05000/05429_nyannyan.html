にゃんにゃん

にゃんにゃん
adverb
meow; mew (cat sound)
1. meow; mew
The sound a cat makes.
猫(ねこ)がにゃんにゃん鳴(な)いている。
The cat is meowing.
2. kitty (baby talk)
A child's word for cat.
にゃんにゃん、かわいいね。
The kitty is cute, isn't it?
Baby talk
にゃんにゃん大好(だいす)き！
I love kitties!
Child's speech

にゃんにゃん is an onomatopoeia representing a cat's meow, and also functions as baby talk for 'cat' or 'kitty.'

USAGES:
1. Sound: The sound a cat makes, often used with 鳴(な)く
2. Baby talk: A child's word for cat (like 'kitty' in English)

RELATED SOUNDS:

  • にゃー: single meow
  • にゃーにゃー: repeated meowing
  • にゃお: softer mew
  • ゴロゴロ: purring

OTHER ANIMAL BABY TALK:

  • わんわん: doggy (from the barking sound)
  • もーもー: cow
  • ぶーぶー: piggy
  • ぴよぴよ: chick

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • にゃんにゃん鳴(な)く: to meow
  • にゃんにゃんがいるよ: There's a kitty! (child's speech)

Related Words
