1. to wilt; to shrivel; to wither
For flowers or plants to lose freshness and droop.
花(はな)が萎(しぼ)んでしまった。
The flowers have wilted.
水(みず)をやらないと花(はな)はすぐ萎(しぼ)んでしまう。
If you don't water them, the flowers will wilt quickly.
暑(あつ)さで庭(にわ)の野菜(やさい)が萎(しぼ)んでしまった。
The vegetables in the garden wilted from the heat.
2. to deflate; to go flat
For something inflated to lose air and become smaller.
風船(ふうせん)が萎(しぼ)んだ。
The balloon deflated.
タイヤが萎(しぼ)んでいた。
The tire was flat.
子供(こども)が持(も)っていた風船(ふうせん)が次(つぎ)の日(ひ)にはもう萎(しぼ)んでいた。
The balloon the child was holding had already deflated by the next day.
3. to dwindle; to fade (abstract)
For enthusiasm, hopes, or spirits to diminish.
期待(きたい)が萎(しぼ)んだ。
My expectations faded.
Abstract usage
朝(あさ)はやる気(き)があったのに、午後(ごご)になったら気持(きも)ちが萎(しぼ)んでしまった。
I was motivated in the morning, but by the afternoon my spirits had deflated.
結果(けっか)を聞(き)いて、みんなの熱意(ねつい)が一気(いっき)に萎(しぼ)んだ。
After hearing the results, everyone's enthusiasm deflated all at once.

萎(しぼ)む describes something losing its fullness — flowers wilting, balloons deflating, or spirits fading.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 萎(しぼ)ませる (transitive, causative)

ASPECT (ている):
萎(しぼ)んでいる describes the resulting state: the flower has already wilted, the balloon is already flat.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 花(はな)が萎(しぼ)む: flowers wilt
  • 風船(ふうせん)が萎(しぼ)む: a balloon deflates
  • 気持(きも)ちが萎(しぼ)む: spirits sink
  • やる気(き)が萎(しぼ)む: motivation fades
  • 期待(きたい)が萎(しぼ)む: expectations fade

OPPOSITE:

  • 膨(ふく)らむ: to swell, to inflate, to expand
