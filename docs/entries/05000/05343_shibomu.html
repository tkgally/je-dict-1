萎(しぼ)む

しぼむ
godan verb
to wilt; to deflate; to shrivel
1. to wilt; to shrivel; to wither
For flowers or plants to lose freshness and droop.
2. to deflate; to go flat
For something inflated to lose air and become smaller.
3. to dwindle; to fade (abstract)
For enthusiasm, hopes, or spirits to diminish.
花(はな)が萎(しぼ)んでしまった。
The flowers have wilted.
風船(ふうせん)が萎(しぼ)んだ。
The balloon deflated.
期待(きたい)が萎(しぼ)んだ。
My expectations faded.
Abstract usage

萎む is an intransitive godan verb describing the process of something losing its fullness or vitality.

OPPOSITE:

  • 膨(ふく)らむ: to swell, to inflate

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 花が萎む: flowers wilt
  • 風船が萎む: balloon deflates
  • 気持ちが萎む: spirits sink
  • やる気が萎む: motivation fades
