抓(つね)る

つねる
godan verb
to pinch
1. to pinch
To squeeze skin between the thumb and finger.
頬(ほほ)を抓(つね)ってみて。
Try pinching your cheek.
夢(ゆめ)じゃないか確(たし)かめるために腕(うで)を抓(つね)った。
I pinched my arm to make sure I wasn't dreaming.
弟(おとうと)に抓(つね)られた。
I got pinched by my younger brother.

抓る is a transitive godan verb specifically for pinching with fingers.

USAGE NOTE:
The kanji 抓 is relatively uncommon, so this word is often written in hiragana (つねる).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頬をつねる: to pinch one's cheek
  • 腕をつねる: to pinch one's arm
