微分(びぶん)

びぶん
noun / suru-verb
differentiation, differential calculus
1. differentiation, differential calculus
A mathematical operation that finds the rate of change of a function; a core concept in calculus.
この関数(かんすう)を微分(びぶん)する。
To differentiate this function.
微分(びぶん)積分(せきぶん)は高校(こうこう)で習(なら)う。
Differential and integral calculus is taught in high school.
微分(びぶん)方程式(ほうていしき)を解(と)くのは難(むずか)しい。
Solving differential equations is difficult.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 微分(びぶん)する (to differentiate)
  • 微分(びぶん)積分(せきぶん) (differential and integral calculus)
  • 微分(びぶん)方程式(ほうていしき) (differential equation)
  • 偏(へん)微分(びぶん) (partial differentiation)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 積分(せきぶん) (integration)
  • 微分(びぶん)係数(けいすう) (derivative)
  • 導関数(どうかんすう) (derivative function)
