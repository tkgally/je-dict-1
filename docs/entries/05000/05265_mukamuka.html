むかむか

むかむか
adverb, suru verb
nauseous; queasy; irritated

Definitions

1. nauseous; queasy
Describes feeling sick to one's stomach, as if about to vomit.
2. irritated; angry
Describes rising feelings of anger or irritation.

Examples

乗(の)り物(もの)酔(よ)いで胃(い)がむかむかする。
I feel nauseous from motion sickness.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)にむかむかする。
His attitude irritates me.
食(た)べ過(す)ぎてむかむかしてきた。
I ate too much and started feeling sick.

Notes

むかむか is an onomatopoeic word with two main uses: physical nausea and emotional irritation.

PHYSICAL USES:

  • 胃(い)がむかむかする: stomach feels queasy
  • むかむかして吐(は)きそう: feeling like vomiting

EMOTIONAL USES:

  • むかむかする: to feel irritated/angry
  • むかつく: casual verb form meaning "annoying"

RELATED WORDS:

  • 吐き気がする: to feel nauseous
  • イライラする: to be irritated
