1. nauseous; queasy
Describes feeling sick to one's stomach, as if about to vomit.
胃(い)がむかむかする。
My stomach feels queasy.
乗(の)り物(もの)酔(よ)いでむかむかする。
I feel nauseous from motion sickness.
食(た)べ過(す)ぎてむかむかしてきた。
I ate too much and started feeling sick.
朝(あさ)からむかむかして何(なに)も食(た)べられない。
I've been feeling nauseous since morning and can't eat anything.
薬(くすり)を飲(の)んだら胃(い)のむかむかが治(おさ)まった。
After taking the medicine, the queasiness in my stomach settled down.
2. irritated; angry
Describes rising feelings of anger or irritation.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)にむかむかする。
His attitude irritates me.
嘘(うそ)をつかれてむかむかした。
I was irritated at being lied to.
あの言(い)い方(かた)を思(おも)い出(だ)すとむかむかする。
I get angry whenever I remember how they said it.
待(ま)たされてむかむかしている。
I'm irritated from being kept waiting.
理不尽(りふじん)なことを言(い)われてむかむかしたが、我慢(がまん)した。
I was furious at the unfair things said to me, but I held it in.

むかむか is an onomatopoeic word describing either physical nausea (a churning stomach) or rising anger/irritation. The word mimics the sensation of something welling up from inside.

SENSE 1 - PHYSICAL NAUSEA:

  • 胃(い)がむかむかする: stomach feels queasy
  • むかむかして吐(は)きそう: feeling like vomiting
  • Common triggers: 乗(の)り物(もの)酔(よ)い (motion sickness), 食(た)べ過(す)ぎ (overeating), 二日酔(ふつかよ)い (hangover)

SENSE 2 - EMOTIONAL IRRITATION:

  • むかむかする: to feel angry/irritated
  • Often triggered by someone's 態度(たいど) (attitude) or 言動(げんどう) (words and actions)

RELATED FORMS:

  • むかつく: casual verb form widely used among younger speakers (very informal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吐(は)き気(け)がする: to feel nauseous (more clinical)
  • イライラする: to be irritated (sustained frustration, not a sudden surge)
