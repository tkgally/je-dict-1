1. immigrant; emigrant
A person who moves to live permanently in a foreign country.
彼(かれ)の祖父(そふ)はアメリカへの移民(いみん)だった。
His grandfather was an immigrant to America.
この町(まち)には移民(いみん)が多(おお)い。
There are many immigrants in this town.
移民(いみん)として新(あたら)しい国(くに)で生活(せいかつ)を始(はじ)める。
To start a new life in a new country as an immigrant.
2. immigration; emigration
The act of moving to a foreign country to live.
移民(いみん)政策(せいさく)について議論(ぎろん)する。
To discuss immigration policy.
カナダに移民(いみん)する。
To immigrate to Canada.
若(わか)いころに家族(かぞく)で移民(いみん)して、もう20年(ねん)になる。
My family immigrated when I was young, and it's been 20 years now.

移民(いみん) means both the person (immigrant/emigrant) and the act (immigration/emigration). It can be used with する as a verb.

WORD FORMATION:
移(い) (move) + 民(みん) (people)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 移民(いみん)政策(せいさく): immigration policy
  • 移民(いみん)ビザ: immigration visa
  • 不法(ふほう)移民(いみん): illegal immigrant
  • 移民(いみん)を受(う)け入(い)れる: to accept immigrants
  • 〜に移民(いみん)する: to immigrate to ~

DISTINCTION:

  • 移民(いみん): immigrant (voluntary relocation)
  • 難民(なんみん): refugee (forced displacement)
  • 帰化(きか): naturalization (becoming a citizen)

HISTORICAL NOTE:
Japan has a history of 移民(いみん) to Brazil, Hawaii, and the Americas in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
