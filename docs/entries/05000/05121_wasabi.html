山葵(わさび)

わさび
noun
wasabi

Definitions

1. wasabi, Japanese horseradish
A pungent green condiment made from the grated rhizome of the wasabi plant, commonly served with sushi and sashimi.

Examples

刺身(さしみ)に山葵(わさび)をつけて食(た)べる。
I eat sashimi with wasabi.
山葵(わさび)が効(き)きすぎて涙(なみだ)が出(で)た。
The wasabi was too strong and I got teary-eyed.
本(ほん)山葵(わさび)は値段(ねだん)が高(たか)い。
Real wasabi is expensive.

Notes

USAGE NOTES:

  • Most "wasabi" served in restaurants outside Japan (and even in many Japanese restaurants) is actually colored horseradish (西洋(せいよう)わさび).
  • 本(ほん)わさび refers to authentic wasabi from the wasabi plant.
  • Fresh wasabi is grated on a 鮫皮(さめかわ)おろし (sharkskin grater).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 山葵(わさび)をつける (to add wasabi)
  • 山葵(わさび)が効(き)く (wasabi is hot/strong)
  • 山葵(わさび)抜(ぬ)き (without wasabi)
  • 練(ね)り山葵(わさび) (wasabi paste)
