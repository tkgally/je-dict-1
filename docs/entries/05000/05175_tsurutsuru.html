つるつる

つるつる
adverb
smooth; slippery; bald
1. smooth; slippery
Describes a surface that is very smooth and can be slippery.
氷(こおり)の上(うえ)はつるつる滑(すべ)る。
It's slippery on the ice.
つるつるの卵(たまご)の殻(から)。
A smooth eggshell.
ワックスを塗(ぬ)ったら床(ゆか)がつるつるになった。
The floor became slippery after applying wax.
2. slurping (noodles)
Describes the action or sound of slurping noodles.
うどんをつるつる食(た)べる。
To slurp up udon noodles.
そばをつるつると音(おと)を立(た)てて食(た)べた。
I ate the soba noodles with a slurping sound.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)は冷(つめ)たいそうめんをつるつる食(た)べるのが一番(いちばん)だ。
On hot days, there's nothing better than slurping cold somen noodles.

つるつる is a mimetic word that works as both 擬態語(ぎたいご) (describing smoothness) and 擬音語(ぎおんご) (describing the sound of slurping noodles).

SENSE 1 — SMOOTH/SLIPPERY:

  • つるつる滑(すべ)る: to slide/slip smoothly
  • つるつるの肌(はだ): smooth skin
  • つるつるの頭(あたま): bald head
  • つるつるになる: to become smooth/slippery

SENSE 2 — SLURPING:

  • つるつる食(た)べる: to slurp (noodles)
  • つるつると啜(すす)る: to slurp up

Used specifically for noodle-like foods: うどん, そば, ラーメン, そうめん.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すべすべ: smooth to the touch (pleasant texture, e.g., skin)
  • ぬるぬる: slimy, slippery (unpleasant, e.g., seaweed)
  • さらさら: smooth and flowing (e.g., hair, sand)

Related Words

Synonym: すべすべ (smooth (pleasant))
Synonym: さらさら (smooth and flowing)
Antonym: ざらざら (rough, gritty)
Related: ぬるぬる (slimy (unpleasant))
