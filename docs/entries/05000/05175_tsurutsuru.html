つるつる

つるつる
adverb
smooth; slippery; bald
1. smooth; slippery
Describes a surface that is very smooth and can be slippery.
2. slurping (noodles)
Describes the action or sound of slurping noodles.
氷(こおり)の上(うえ)はつるつる滑(すべ)る。
It's slippery on the ice.
つるつるの卵(たまご)の殻(から)。
A smooth eggshell.
うどんをつるつる食(た)べる。
To slurp up udon noodles.

つるつる is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語/擬音語) describing smoothness or slurping.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • つるつる滑(すべ)る: to slide smoothly/be slippery
  • つるつる食(た)べる: to slurp (noodles)
  • つるつるの肌(はだ): smooth skin

CAN ALSO DESCRIBE: A bald head (つるつるの頭(あたま)).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すべすべ: smooth (to touch, pleasant)
  • ぬるぬる: slimy, slippery (unpleasant)
