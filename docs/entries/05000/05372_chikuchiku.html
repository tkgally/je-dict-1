1. prickling; stinging; pricking
Describes a sharp, prickling sensation, like being poked repeatedly.
セーターがちくちくする。
The sweater feels prickly.
喉(のど)がちくちく痛(いた)い。
My throat has a stinging pain.
このセーターはちくちくする。
This sweater is prickly.
2. sewing; stitching (sound)
The sound or action of continuous small stitches.
ちくちくと縫(ぬ)い物(もの)をする。
To sew with small stitches.
ちくちくと嫌味(いやみ)を言(い)う。
To make snide remarks.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)がちくちく胸(むね)に刺(さ)さった。
His words stung my heart.

ちくちく is an onomatopoeic word describing repeated small, sharp sensations — like being pricked by needles.

PHYSICAL SENSATIONS:

  • ちくちくする: to feel prickly (a sweater, grass)
  • ちくちく痛(いた)む: to have a stinging pain (throat, wound)

SEWING:

  • ちくちく縫(ぬ)う: to sew with small stitches
  • ちくちくと縫(ぬ)い物(もの)をする: to do needlework

FIGURATIVE USE:
Also describes biting or hurtful words: ちくちく言(い)う (to make snide remarks), 言葉(ことば)がちくちく刺(さ)さる (words that sting). This figurative meaning comes from the sense of being pricked repeatedly.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひりひり: stinging, burning (like a sunburn or scrape)
  • じんじん: tingling, numbness
  • ずきずき: throbbing (deeper, pulsing pain)
