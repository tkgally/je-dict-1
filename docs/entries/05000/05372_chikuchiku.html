ちくちく

ちくちく
adverb
prickling; stinging; stabbing
1. prickling; stinging; pricking
Describes a sharp, prickling sensation, like being poked repeatedly.
2. sewing; stitching (sound)
The sound or action of continuous small stitches.
セーターがちくちくする。
The sweater feels prickly.
喉(のど)がちくちく痛(いた)い。
My throat has a stinging pain.
ちくちくと縫(ぬ)い物(もの)をする。
To sew with small stitches.

ちくちく primarily describes prickling sensations, and can also represent the action of sewing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひりひり: stinging (like a burn)
  • じんじん: tingling, numbing
  • ずきずき: throbbing (deeper pain)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ちくちくする: to feel prickly
  • ちくちく痛む: to have a stinging pain
