流(なが)れ星(ぼし)

ながれぼし
noun
shooting star, meteor

Definitions

1. shooting star, meteor, falling star
A streak of light in the night sky caused by a small piece of space debris burning up as it enters Earth's atmosphere.

Examples

流(なが)れ星(ぼし)を見(み)たら願(ねが)い事(ごと)をする。
If you see a shooting star, make a wish.
昨夜(さくや)、流(なが)れ星(ぼし)が空(そら)を横切(よこぎ)るのを見(み)た。
Last night, I saw a shooting star streak across the sky.
流星群(りゅうせいぐん)の夜(よる)は流(なが)れ星(ぼし)がたくさん見(み)える。
On nights of meteor showers, you can see many shooting stars.

Notes

流(なが)れ星(ぼし) refers to shooting stars or meteors. 流(なが)れ means 'flowing' and 星(ぼし) means 'star.'

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 流(なが)れ星(ぼし)を見(み)る: to see a shooting star
  • 流(なが)れ星(ぼし)に願(ねが)いをかける: to make a wish on a shooting star
  • 流(なが)れ星(ぼし)が流(なが)れる: a shooting star falls

METEOR SHOWERS (流星群(りゅうせいぐん)):

  • ペルセウス座(ざ)流星群(りゅうせいぐん): Perseids (August)
  • ふたご座(ざ)流星群(りゅうせいぐん): Geminids (December)
  • しぶんぎ座(ざ)流星群(りゅうせいぐん): Quadrantids (January)

SCIENTIFIC TERMS:

  • 流星(りゅうせい): meteor (scientific)
  • 隕石(いんせき): meteorite (if it reaches ground)

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • In Japan, it's believed that if you make a wish before a shooting star disappears, it will come true
