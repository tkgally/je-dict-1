さらさら

さらさら
adverb
smooth; silky; rustling
1. smooth; silky; flowing
Describes something that feels smooth and flows easily, often used for hair, fabric, or liquids.
2. rustling; murmuring
The soft sound of leaves, water, or paper moving gently.
彼女(かのじょ)の髪(かみ)はさらさらだ。
Her hair is silky smooth.
川(かわ)がさらさらと流(なが)れている。
The river is flowing with a murmuring sound.
このシャンプーを使(つか)うと髪(かみ)がさらさらになる。
Using this shampoo makes your hair silky smooth.

さらさら is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語/擬音語) with both tactile and auditory uses.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • さらさらの髪(かみ): silky hair
  • さらさら流(なが)れる: to flow smoothly
  • さらさらした手触(てざわ)り: smooth texture

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すべすべ: smooth (skin texture)
  • つるつる: slippery, smooth
  • ざらざら: rough (opposite texture)

Related Words

Antonym: ざらざら (rough)
