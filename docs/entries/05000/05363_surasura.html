すらすら

すらすら
adverb
smoothly; fluently; without hesitation

Definitions

1. smoothly; fluently; without hesitation
Describes speaking, reading, or doing something without stumbling or pausing.

Examples

彼(かれ)は英語(えいご)をすらすら話(はな)す。
He speaks English fluently.
すらすらと答(こた)えられた。
I was able to answer smoothly.
この本(ほん)はすらすら読(よ)める。
This book is easy to read smoothly.

Notes

すらすら implies smooth flow without obstacles, particularly in speech, reading, or writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すらすら話す: to speak fluently
  • すらすら読む: to read smoothly
  • すらすら書く: to write smoothly
  • すらすら答える: to answer without hesitation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぺらぺら: fluently (often implies showing off)
  • さらさら: smoothly flowing (more for physical movement/texture)
