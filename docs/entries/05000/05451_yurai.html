1. origin; history; derivation
The source or beginning of something; where something came from.
この言葉(ことば)の由来(ゆらい)を調(しら)べる。
To research the origin of this word.
その祭(まつ)りには古(ふる)い由来(ゆらい)がある。
That festival has an ancient history.
名前(なまえ)の由来(ゆらい)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the origin of the name.

由来(ゆらい) refers to the origin, history, or story behind something — how a name, custom, place, or thing came to be.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 由来(ゆらい)する: to derive from, to originate from
  • 〜に由来(ゆらい)する: to originate in/from ~
  • 名前(なまえ)の由来(ゆらい): the origin of a name
  • 地名(ちめい)の由来(ゆらい): the origin of a place name
  • 由来(ゆらい)を持(も)つ: to have an origin/history

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 由来(ゆらい): origin, derivation (how something came to be)
  • 起源(きげん): origin (more scientific/formal, e.g., origin of life)
  • 語源(ごげん): etymology (specifically word origins)
  • 来歴(らいれき): history, career (of a person or thing)

NOTE:
Can also function as an adverb meaning "originally" or "by nature": 由来(ゆらい)日本(にほん)は島国(しまぐに)である (Japan is by nature an island nation). This usage is literary.

Related Words

Synonym: 起源(きげん) (origin (formal))
