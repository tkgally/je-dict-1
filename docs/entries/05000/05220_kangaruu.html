カンガルー

かんがるー
noun
kangaroo

Definitions

1. kangaroo
A large Australian marsupial that moves by hopping on its powerful hind legs and carries its young in a pouch.

Examples

カンガルーは袋(ふくろ)で赤(あか)ちゃんを育(そだ)てる。
Kangaroos raise their babies in their pouches.
動物園(どうぶつえん)でカンガルーを見(み)た。
I saw kangaroos at the zoo.
カンガルーは後(うし)ろ足(あし)で跳(は)ねる。
Kangaroos hop on their hind legs.

Notes

カンガルー is the Japanese word for kangaroo, native to Australia.

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • 有袋類(ゆうたいるい) (marsupial)
  • Known for hopping locomotion
  • Mothers carry babies (joeys) in pouches

KANJI: Sometimes written 袋鼠 in kanji, but katakana is far more common.
