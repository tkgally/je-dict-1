のろのろ

のろのろ
adverb
slowly; sluggishly
1. slowly; sluggishly
Describes movement or action that is frustratingly slow or sluggish.
渋滞(じゅうたい)で車(くるま)がのろのろ進(すす)んでいる。
The cars are moving slowly due to traffic.
のろのろ歩(ある)いていたら遅刻(ちこく)するよ。
If you walk so slowly, you'll be late.
仕事(しごと)をのろのろやらないで。
Don't do your work so slowly.

のろのろ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態{語(ご)|ぎたいご}) that expresses frustratingly slow movement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • のろのろ運転(うんてん): slow driving
  • のろのろ進(すす)む: to proceed slowly
  • のろのろする: to dawdle, move sluggishly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ゆっくり: slowly (neutral, not negative)
  • もたもた: slow and clumsy (implies awkwardness)
  • ぐずぐず: dawdling (implies hesitation)
