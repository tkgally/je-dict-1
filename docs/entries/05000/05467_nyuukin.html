入金(にゅうきん)

にゅうきん
noun / suru verb
deposit; payment received

Definitions

1. deposit; payment received
Money coming into an account or being deposited.

Examples

入金(にゅうきん)を確認(かくにん)する。
To confirm the deposit.
給料(きゅうりょう)の入金(にゅうきん)があった。
My salary was deposited.
入金(にゅうきん)日(び)は毎月(まいつき)25日(にち)です。
The payment date is the 25th of every month.

Notes

入金(にゅうきん) refers to money being deposited or received.

CONTRAST:

  • 入金(にゅうきん): money coming in
  • 出金(しゅっきん): money going out (withdrawal)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 入金(にゅうきん)確認(かくにん): deposit confirmation
  • 入金(にゅうきん)日(び): payment/deposit date
  • 入金(にゅうきん)通知(つうち): deposit notification
