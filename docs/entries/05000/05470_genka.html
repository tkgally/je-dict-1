原価(げんか)

げんか
noun
cost price; production cost
1. cost price; production cost
The actual cost to produce or acquire something before profit margin.
原価(げんか)を下(さ)げる努力(どりょく)をする。
To make efforts to reduce the cost price.
原価(げんか)割(わ)れで販売(はんばい)している。
They are selling below cost.
原価(げんか)は100円(えん)だが、500円(えん)で売(う)っている。
The cost is 100 yen, but it's sold for 500 yen.

原価(げんか) means the cost price or production cost — the actual expense of making or acquiring something before any profit margin is added. The kanji combine 原(げん) (original) + 価(か) (price).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 原価(げんか)計算(けいさん): cost accounting
  • 原価(げんか)割(わ)れ: below cost (selling at a loss)
  • 原価(げんか)率(りつ): cost ratio
  • 原価(げんか)を下(さ)げる: to reduce costs

RELATED PRICE TERMS:

  • 単価(たんか): unit price
  • 売価(ばいか): selling price
  • 定価(ていか): list price / retail price
  • 仕入(しい)れ値(ね): purchase price (what a retailer pays)
  • 利益(りえき): profit

NOTE:
原価(げんか) is a business/accounting term. In casual conversation, people are more likely to say 元値(もとね) (original price) or simply いくらで仕入(しい)れた (how much it was bought for).
