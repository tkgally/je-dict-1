吐(は)き気(け)

はきけ
noun
nausea
1. nausea, feeling of sickness
A sensation of unease and discomfort in the stomach with an urge to vomit.
船(ふね)に乗(の)ったら吐(は)き気(け)がしてきた。
I started feeling nauseous when I got on the boat.
吐(は)き気(け)がひどくて何(なに)も食(た)べられない。
The nausea is so bad that I can't eat anything.
薬(くすり)の副作用(ふくさよう)で吐(は)き気(け)がする。
I feel nauseous as a side effect of the medication.
朝(あさ)から吐(は)き気(け)が止(と)まらなくて、会社(かいしゃ)を休(やす)むことにした。
I couldn't stop feeling nauseous since morning, so I decided to take the day off work.
妊娠(にんしん)初期(しょき)はつわりで吐(は)き気(け)がひどく、何(なに)を食(た)べても気持(きも)ち悪(わる)くなった。
In early pregnancy, the morning sickness caused severe nausea, and I felt sick no matter what I ate.

吐(は)き気(け) refers to the feeling of nausea. It comes from 吐(は)く (to vomit) + 気(け) (feeling/tendency).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 吐(は)き気(け)がする: to feel nauseous
  • 吐(は)き気(け)を催(もよお)す: to feel the urge to vomit
  • 吐(は)き気(け)を抑(おさ)える: to suppress nausea
  • 吐(は)き気(け)を止(と)める: to stop nausea
  • 激(はげ)しい吐(は)き気(け): severe nausea

COMMON CAUSES:

  • 乗(の)り物(もの)酔(よ)い: motion sickness
  • つわり: morning sickness
  • 食(しょく)あたり: food poisoning
  • 二日酔(ふつかよ)い: hangover
  • 副作用(ふくさよう): medication side effects

RELATED WORDS:

  • 吐(は)く: to vomit
  • 嘔吐(おうと): vomiting (medical term)
  • 気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い: feeling sick/unwell
