カブトムシ

かぶとむし
noun
rhinoceros beetle; Japanese rhinoceros beetle
1. rhinoceros beetle; Japanese rhinoceros beetle
A large beetle with a horn-like projection, popular among Japanese children as a pet.
夏(なつ)にカブトムシを捕(つか)まえた。
I caught a rhinoceros beetle in summer.
カブトムシを飼(か)っている。
I'm keeping a rhinoceros beetle as a pet.
カブトムシとクワガタは人気(にんき)がある。
Rhinoceros beetles and stag beetles are popular.

カブトムシ is one of Japan's most popular insects among children.

NAME ORIGIN: 兜(かぶと) (samurai helmet) + 虫(むし) (insect), named after the horn resembling a helmet.

CULTURE:

  • Popular summer activity for children to catch them
  • Often kept as pets in plastic cases
  • Sold at pet stores and department stores
  • Featured in games, anime, and media

RELATED: クワガタ (stag beetle) - equally popular
