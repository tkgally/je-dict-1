もちもち

もちもち
adverb, na-adjective
chewy; springy; soft and elastic

Definitions

1. chewy; springy; soft and elastic
Describes a pleasantly chewy or bouncy texture, often used for rice, bread, or skin.

Examples

このパンはもちもちしておいしい。
This bread is chewy and delicious.
赤(あか)ちゃんのもちもちの肌(はだ)。
A baby's soft, supple skin.
もちもちの食感(しょっかん)がたまらない。
I can't resist the chewy texture.

Notes

もちもち is derived from 餅(もち) (mochi rice cake) and describes that characteristic bouncy texture.

COMMON USES:

  • もちもちのパン: chewy bread
  • もちもちの肌(はだ): soft, supple skin
  • もちもち食感(しょっかん): chewy texture

FOODS DESCRIBED AS もちもち:

  • 餅(もち) (mochi)
  • うどん (udon noodles)
  • タピオカ (tapioca pearls)
  • もち米(ごめ) (glutinous rice)
  • ポン・デ・リング (pon de ring doughnuts)
