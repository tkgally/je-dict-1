こめかみ

こめかみ
noun
temple (of the head)
1. temple
The flat area on each side of the head between the forehead and the ear.
こめかみが痛(いた)い。
My temples hurt.
こめかみを押(お)さえる。
To press one's temples.
こめかみに白髪(しらが)が生(は)えてきた。
Gray hair has started to grow at my temples.

こめかみ likely comes from 米噛み (rice chewing), as this area moves when chewing.

RELATED BODY PARTS:

  • ひたい/額(ひたい): forehead
  • 耳(みみ): ear
  • ほほ/頬(ほほ): cheek

HEADACHE CONTEXT:

  • Often associated with tension headaches and migraines
  • 偏頭痛(へんずつう): migraine
