保健室(ほけんしつ)

ほけんしつ
noun
nurse's office; school infirmary
1. nurse's office; school infirmary
A room in Japanese schools where students can rest when sick or receive first aid.
気分(きぶん)が悪(わる)くて保健室(ほけんしつ)に行(い)った。
I felt sick so I went to the nurse's office.
保健室(ほけんしつ)で休(やす)んでいる。
I'm resting in the nurse's office.
保健室(ほけんしつ)の先生(せんせい)に相談(そうだん)した。
I consulted with the school nurse.
保健室の先生 = school nurse

保健室(ほけんしつ) is staffed by a {保健室(ほけんしつ)の先生(せんせい)|ほけんしつのせんせい} or 養護教諭(ようごきょうゆ) (ようごきょうゆ, school nurse/health teacher). Students can rest there when feeling unwell.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 保健室(ほけんしつ)に行(い)く: to go to the nurse's office
  • 保健室(ほけんしつ)で休(やす)む: to rest in the nurse's office

CULTURAL NOTE:
The school nurse often provides emotional support as well as physical first aid.
