広(ひろ)さ

ひろさ
noun
width; area; extent

Definitions

1. width; area; extent; spaciousness
The measurement of how wide or spacious something is.

Examples

この部屋(へや)の広(ひろ)さは20畳(じょう)だ。
The size of this room is 20 tatami mats.
土地(とち)の広(ひろ)さを測(はか)る。
To measure the area of the land.
庭(にわ)の広(ひろ)さに驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by the size of the garden.

Notes

広(ひろ)さ is derived from the adjective 広(ひろ)い (wide/spacious) + さ (nominalizing suffix).

ROOM SIZE:

In Japan, room size is often measured in 畳(じょう) (tatami mats):

  • 1畳(じょう) ≈ 1.65 m²
  • 6畳(じょう) is a standard small room
  • 8畳(じょう) is a standard bedroom
