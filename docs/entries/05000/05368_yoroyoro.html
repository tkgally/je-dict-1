よろよろ

よろよろ
adverb
tottering; staggering; unsteady
1. tottering; staggering; unsteady
Walking unsteadily due to weakness, age, or intoxication.
酔(よ)っぱらいがよろよろ歩(ある)いている。
The drunk is staggering along.
よろよろと立(た)ち上(あ)がった。
He stood up unsteadily.
足(あし)がよろよろしている。
My legs are unsteady.

よろよろ specifically describes tottering movement, often due to physical weakness.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ふらふら: unsteady (can be from dizziness or aimless wandering)
  • おぼつかない: unsteady, uncertain

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • よろよろ歩く: to totter, to stagger
  • よろよろと: totteringly
