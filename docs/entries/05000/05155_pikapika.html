ぴかぴか

ぴかぴか
adverb
sparkling; glittering; shiny

Definitions

1. sparkling; glittering; shiny
Describes something that shines brightly or gleams, often with reflected light. Used for polished surfaces, stars, or clean objects.

Examples

靴(くつ)をぴかぴかに磨(みが)いた。
I polished my shoes until they were shiny.
星(ほし)がぴかぴか光(ひか)っている。
The stars are twinkling brightly.
ぴかぴかの新車(しんしゃ)を買(か)った。
I bought a brand-new shiny car.
ぴかぴかの + noun = shiny/brand-new

Notes

ぴかぴか is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) that mimics the visual impression of sparkling or gleaming.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぴかぴかに磨(みが)く: to polish until shiny
  • ぴかぴか光(ひか)る: to sparkle, shine brightly
  • ぴかぴかの〜: brand-new, sparkling clean (noun modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きらきら: twinkling, sparkling (often for smaller, softer lights like stars)
  • ぎらぎら: glaring, dazzling (harsh, intense light)
