惨(みじ)め

みじめ
na-adjective
miserable, wretched

Definitions

1. miserable, wretched, pathetic
Pitifully bad; causing or deserving pity; very unhappy.

Examples

惨(みじ)めな気持(きも)ちになった。
I felt miserable.
惨(みじ)めな失敗(しっぱい)をした。
I suffered a miserable failure.
こんな惨(みじ)めな生活(せいかつ)は嫌(いや)だ。
I hate living such a wretched life.

Notes

A strongly negative adjective describing pitiful or wretched conditions. Can describe situations, feelings, or people. Often used reflexively about oneself during difficult times.
