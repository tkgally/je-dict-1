納品(のうひん)

のうひん
noun / suru-verb
delivery (of goods)
1. delivery, supplying goods
The act of delivering ordered goods or products to a customer or client.
明日(あした)納品(のうひん)する予定(よてい)です。
We are scheduled to deliver tomorrow.
納品(のうひん)日(び)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please confirm the delivery date.
納品(のうひん)書(しょ)にサインをお願(ねが)いします。
Please sign the delivery note.

納品(のうひん) means delivering ordered goods to a client or customer. The kanji break down as 納(のう) (deliver/supply) + 品(ひん) (goods). It functions as a noun and する verb, and is primarily used in business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 納品(のうひん)する: to deliver goods
  • 納品(のうひん)日(び)/納品(のうひん)期日(きじつ): delivery date/deadline
  • 納品(のうひん)書(しょ): delivery slip/invoice
  • 納品(のうひん)先(さき): delivery destination
  • 納品(のうひん)期限(きげん)に間(ま)に合(あ)う: to make the delivery deadline

CONTRAST:

  • 配達(はいたつ): general delivery (mail, packages to individuals)
  • 出荷(しゅっか): shipping (sending goods from a warehouse)
  • 納品(のうひん): formal delivery of ordered goods to a business client

納品(のうひん) implies a business transaction where ordered goods are formally handed over.
