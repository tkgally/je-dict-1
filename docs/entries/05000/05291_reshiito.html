レシート

れしーと
noun
receipt
1. receipt
A printed proof of purchase given at the time of a transaction.
レシートは要(い)りますか。
Do you need a receipt?
Common phrase asked at stores
レシートを保管(ほかん)しておく。
To keep the receipt.
返品(へんぴん)にはレシートが必要(ひつよう)です。
A receipt is required for returns.

レシート is a loanword from English "receipt," referring to the printed slip given at checkout.

KEY DISTINCTION:

  • レシート: cash register receipt (printed automatically)
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ): formal receipt (handwritten or stamped, for business expenses and tax deductions)

You may need to specifically request a 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ) at the register if you need one for business purposes. Cashiers typically ask レシートは要(い)りますか (Do you need a receipt?).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レシートを保管(ほかん)する: to keep the receipt
  • レシートを捨(す)てる: to throw away the receipt
  • レシートを確認(かくにん)する: to check the receipt
  • レシートをもらう: to get a receipt
