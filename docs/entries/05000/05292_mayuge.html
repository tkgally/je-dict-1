眉毛(まゆげ)

まゆげ
noun
eyebrow
1. eyebrow
The hair that grows on the brow ridge above the eye.
眉毛(まゆげ)を整(ととの)える。
To groom one's eyebrows.
眉毛(まゆげ)が濃(こ)い。
To have thick eyebrows.
眉毛(まゆげ)を描(か)く。
To draw on eyebrows.

眉毛(まゆげ) is composed of 眉(まゆ) (brow) + 毛(げ) (hair).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 眉(まゆ): brow/eyebrow (also used alone)
  • 眉間(みけん): between the eyebrows

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 眉毛(まゆげ)を剃(そ)る: to shave eyebrows
  • 眉毛(まゆげ)を抜(ぬ)く: to pluck eyebrows
  • 眉(まゆ)をひそめる: to frown (show displeasure)
