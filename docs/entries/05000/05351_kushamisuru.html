1. to sneeze
To involuntarily expel air from the nose and mouth due to irritation.
花粉症(かふんしょう)でくしゃみが止(と)まらない。
I can't stop sneezing due to hay fever.
くしゃみが止まらない = can't stop sneezing
胡椒(こしょう)でくしゃみが出(で)た。
The pepper made me sneeze.
くしゃみをする時(とき)は口(くち)を覆(おお)って。
Cover your mouth when you sneeze.
朝(あさ)から何回(なんかい)もくしゃみをしている。風邪(かぜ)かもしれない。
I've been sneezing many times since this morning. I might have a cold.
静(しず)かな図書館(としょかん)で大(おお)きなくしゃみをしてしまい、恥(は)ずかしかった。
I let out a big sneeze in the quiet library and was embarrassed.

くしゃみ is an onomatopoeic word representing the sound of sneezing. It can be used with する or as くしゃみが出(で)る.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • くしゃみをする: to sneeze
  • くしゃみが出(で)る: to sneeze (lit. a sneeze comes out)
  • くしゃみが止(と)まらない: can't stop sneezing

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, there's a saying that if you sneeze once, someone is talking about you. Twice means someone is talking badly about you. Three times and you might be catching a cold.
