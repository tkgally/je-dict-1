苦(くる)しむ

くるしむ
godan verb
to suffer; to struggle

Definitions

1. to suffer; to be in pain; to struggle; to be troubled
To experience physical or emotional pain, or to have difficulty with something.

Examples

病気(びょうき)で苦(くる)しんでいる。
To be suffering from illness.
借金(しゃっきん)に苦(くる)しむ。
To struggle with debt.
彼(かれ)は罪悪(ざいあく)感(かん)に苦(くる)しんでいた。
He was suffering from guilt.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 苦(くる)しむ (dictionary form)
  • 苦(くる)しみます (polite)
  • 苦(くる)しんで (te-form)
  • 苦(くる)しんだ (past)

PATTERNS:

  • ～で苦(くる)しむ: to suffer from ~
  • ～に苦(くる)しむ: to struggle with ~

RELATED:

  • 苦(くる)しい: painful, difficult (adjective)
  • 苦(くる)しみ: suffering, pain (noun)
  • 苦(く)しめる: to torment (transitive)

Related Words

Related: 苦(くる)しい (painful (adjective))
