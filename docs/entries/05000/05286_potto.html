ポット

ぽっと
noun
pot; thermos; hot water dispenser
1. pot; thermos; hot water dispenser
A container for keeping water hot, or an electric hot water dispenser.
ポットにお湯(ゆ)を入(い)れておく。
To keep hot water in the pot.
電気(でんき)ポットでお茶(ちゃ)を入(い)れる。
To make tea with an electric hot water pot.
ポットのお湯(ゆ)が冷(さ)めた。
The water in the pot has cooled down.

In Japanese, ポット most commonly refers to an electric hot water dispenser — a kitchen appliance found in nearly every Japanese home and office. Unlike in English, it does not usually refer to a cooking pot.

TYPES:

  • 電気(でんき)ポット: electric hot water dispenser (keeps water hot at a set temperature)
  • 魔法瓶(まほうびん): vacuum flask/thermos (no electricity)
  • コーヒーポット: coffee pot
  • ティーポット: teapot

DIFFERENCE FROM ケトル:

  • ケトル (kettle): boils water, then turns off
  • ポット (pot): keeps water hot continuously at a set temperature

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ポットにお湯(ゆ)を入(い)れる: to fill the pot with hot water
  • ポットのお湯(ゆ): hot water from the pot
