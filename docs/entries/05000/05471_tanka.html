単価(たんか)

たんか
noun
unit price
1. unit price
The price of a single item or unit.
単価(たんか)が上(あ)がった。
The unit price went up.
客(きゃく)単価(たんか)を上(あ)げる戦略(せんりゃく)を考(かんが)える。
To think of strategies to increase customer spending.
一(ひと)つあたりの単価(たんか)は200円(えん)だ。
The unit price is 200 yen each.

単価(たんか) refers to the price per unit.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 客(きゃく)単価(たんか): average spending per customer
  • 単価(たんか)が高(たか)い/安(やす)い: high/low unit price
  • 単価(たんか)を下(さ)げる: to lower the unit price

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
Frequently used in retail and business contexts.
