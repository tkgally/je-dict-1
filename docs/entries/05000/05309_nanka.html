なんか

なんか
adverb, particle
something; kind of; like; things like (dismissive)
1. something; somehow
Used when unable to specify exactly what.
なんか変(へん)だな。
Something seems off.
なんか嫌(いや)な予感(よかん)がする。
I have a bad feeling about something.
なんかあった？
Did something happen?
なんか食(た)べたい。
I want to eat something.
なんか聞(き)いたことがある気(き)がするけど、思(おも)い出(だ)せない。
I feel like I've heard it somewhere, but I can't remember.
2. kind of; sort of; like
A filler word adding vagueness to statements.
なんか疲(つか)れた。
I'm kind of tired.
なんか違(ちが)う気(き)がする。
It kind of feels different.
なんか難(むずか)しいね。
It's kind of difficult, isn't it?
なんか楽(たの)しくない。
It's not really fun.
なんか、今日(きょう)は調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)い。
I'm kind of off today.
3. things like; such as (dismissive)
Used to downplay or dismiss something.
私(わたし)なんかには無理(むり)だ。
Someone like me couldn't do it.
Self-deprecating usage
そんなことなんか知(し)らない。
I don't know about things like that.
彼(かれ)なんか大嫌(だいきら)いだ。
I hate someone like him.
お金(かね)なんかいらない。
I don't need things like money.
勉強(べんきょう)なんかしたくない。
I don't want to do things like studying.
こんなところなんか来(き)たくなかった。
I didn't want to come to a place like this.

なんか is a very common casual word with multiple functions.

FILLER WORD:

  • Often used unconsciously in casual speech
  • Similar to English "like" or "kind of"
  • なんか、今日(きょう)は暑(あつ)いね: It's kind of hot today, isn't it?

DISMISSIVE PARTICLE:

  • 〜なんか: things like ~, someone like ~ (often self-deprecating)
  • More casual form of など

FORMAL EQUIVALENT:

  • 何(なに)か (なにか): something (more formal)

REGISTER:

  • なんか belongs to casual speech. In a 面接(めんせつ), a report, or anything you 書(か)く formally, use 何(なに)か for sense 1 and など for sense 3.
  • The dismissive sense 3 sounds rude when aimed at another person; used about oneself it reads as modesty or self-pity instead.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • なんて: close to sense 3 and also usable before a verb or a whole clause, where なんか cannot go
  • など: the neutral written equivalent of sense 3
  • 何(なに)か: the plain "something" without the vagueness or dismissal
