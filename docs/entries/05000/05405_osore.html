恐(おそ)れ

おそれ
noun
fear; anxiety; risk
1. fear; dread; fright
A feeling of being afraid of something.
2. risk; possibility (of something bad)
The possibility that something undesirable might happen.
失敗(しっぱい)への恐(おそ)れがある。
I have a fear of failure.
地震(じしん)の恐(おそ)れがある。
There's a risk of an earthquake.
恐(おそ)れを克服(こくふく)する。
To overcome one's fears.

恐(おそ)れ can express both emotional fear and the possibility of something negative happening.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 恐(おそ)ろしい (おそろしい): frightening, terrible
  • 恐(おそ)れる (おそれる): to fear (verb)
  • 恐怖(きょうふ) (きょうふ): fear, terror

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の恐(おそ)れがある: there is a risk of ~
  • 恐(おそ)れを抱(いだ)く: to harbor fear
  • 恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが: excuse me, but... (polite)
