蠅(はえ)

はえ
noun
fly

Definitions

1. fly, housefly
A common flying insect with two wings, often found around food and waste.

Examples

蠅(はえ)がうるさい。
The flies are annoying.
蠅(はえ)を追(お)い払(はら)う。
Shoo away the flies.
蠅(はえ)も殺(ころ)さぬ顔(かお)をしている。
He has a face like he wouldn't hurt a fly. (Looks innocent)
Common expression for someone who looks harmless

Notes

Also written as ハエ. Common species:

  • イエバエ (housefly)
  • キンバエ (blowfly)
  • ショウジョウバエ (fruit fly)

Expressions:

  • 蠅(はえ)も殺(ころ)さぬ (wouldn't hurt a fly - looks innocent)
  • 蠅叩(はえたた)き (fly swatter)
  • 蠅取(はえと)り紙(がみ) (fly paper)

Associated with:

  • Unsanitary conditions
  • Summer nuisance
  • Quick, erratic movement

Related vocabulary:

  • ブンブン (buzzing sound)
  • 虫除(むしよ)け (insect repellent)
  • 殺虫剤(さっちゅうざい) (insecticide)
  • 蠅叩(はえたた)き (fly swatter)

The kanji 蠅(はえ) is complex; ハエ (katakana) is often used instead.
