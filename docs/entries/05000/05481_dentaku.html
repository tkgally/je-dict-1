電卓(でんたく)

でんたく
noun
calculator
1. calculator
An electronic device for performing mathematical calculations.
電卓(でんたく)で計算(けいさん)する。
To calculate with a calculator.
試験(しけん)には電卓(でんたく)を持(も)ち込(こ)めない。
You can't bring a calculator to the exam.
スマホの電卓(でんたく)アプリを使(つか)う。
To use the calculator app on my smartphone.

電卓(でんたく) is short for 電子卓上計算機(でんしたくじょうけいさんき).

ETYMOLOGY:

  • 電(でん): electronic
  • 卓(たく): table/desktop

NOTE:
Although phone calculator apps are common, physical calculators are still used in business and accounting.
