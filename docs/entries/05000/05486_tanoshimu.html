楽(たの)しむ

たのしむ
godan verb
to enjoy

Definitions

1. to enjoy; to have fun; to take pleasure in
To find pleasure or satisfaction in an activity or experience.

Examples

旅行(りょこう)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy traveling.
人生(じんせい)を楽(たの)しんでいる。
I am enjoying life.
食事(しょくじ)を楽(たの)しみながら話(はなし)をする。
To talk while enjoying a meal.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 楽(たの)しむ (dictionary form)
  • 楽(たの)しみます (polite)
  • 楽(たの)しんで (te-form)
  • 楽(たの)しんだ (past)
  • 楽(たの)しめる (potential)

PATTERNS:

  • ～を楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy ~
  • ～を楽(たの)しみにする: to look forward to ~

RELATED:

  • 楽(たの)しい: fun, enjoyable (adjective)
  • 楽(たの)しみ: enjoyment, pleasure (noun)
