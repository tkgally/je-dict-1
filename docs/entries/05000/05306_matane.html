またね

またね
expression
see you later; bye
1. see you later; see you again
A friendly, casual way to say goodbye with the implication of meeting again.
またね！楽(たの)しかったよ。
See you later! It was fun.
今日(きょう)はありがとう。またね。
Thanks for today. See you later.
バイバイ、またね！
Bye-bye, see you!

またね comes from また (again) + ね (sentence-ending particle).

NUANCE:

  • Implies you'll meet again
  • Warm and friendly tone
  • Common in daily conversation

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • じゃあね: see you (casual)
  • また会(あ)おうね: let's meet again
  • また今度(こんど)ね: next time
  • バイバイ: bye-bye (casual, often used by younger speakers)
