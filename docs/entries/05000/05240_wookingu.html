ウォーキング

うぉーきんぐ
noun
walking (as exercise)
1. walking (as exercise)
Walking done regularly as a form of physical exercise for health and fitness.
毎朝(まいあさ)30分(ぷん)ウォーキングをしている。
I walk for 30 minutes every morning.
ウォーキングは健康(けんこう)にいい。
Walking is good for your health.
ウォーキングシューズを買(か)った。
I bought walking shoes.

ウォーキング specifically means walking done as deliberate exercise for health, distinct from ordinary walking (歩(ある)く) or strolling (散歩(さんぽ)).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ウォーキングシューズ: walking shoes
  • ウォーキングコース: walking course/trail
  • ウォーキングマシン: treadmill

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 散歩(さんぽ): walk, stroll (leisurely, for enjoyment)
  • 歩(ある)く: to walk (the basic verb, no exercise nuance)
  • ジョギング: jogging (faster pace)
  • ランニング: running
  • ハイキング: hiking (usually in nature, longer distance)

USAGE:
Very popular among middle-aged and older Japanese as a low-impact exercise. ウォーキングをする (to do walking exercise) is the typical verb pattern.
