数量(すうりょう)

すうりょう
noun
quantity; amount
1. quantity; amount; number
The countable amount of something.
数量(すうりょう)を入力(にゅうりょく)してください。
Please enter the quantity.
数量(すうりょう)限定(げんてい)の商品(しょうひん)だ。
This is a limited quantity product.
注文(ちゅうもん)数量(すうりょう)を変更(へんこう)する。
To change the order quantity.

数量(すうりょう) refers to a countable quantity or amount, combining 数(すう) (number) and 量(りょう) (amount). It is somewhat formal and commonly appears in business, commerce, and official contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 数量(すうりょう)限定(げんてい): limited quantity
  • 数量(すうりょう)割引(わりびき): quantity discount
  • 注文(ちゅうもん)数量(すうりょう): order quantity
  • 数量(すうりょう)を確認(かくにん)する: to confirm the quantity

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 量(りょう): amount (general, everyday — can be countable or uncountable)
  • 分量(ぶんりょう): portion, amount (especially for ingredients or dosage)
  • 個数(こすう): number of items (discrete, countable pieces)
  • 数(かず): number, count (native word, more general)

REGISTER:
数量(すうりょう) is more formal than 量(りょう) or 数(かず). It is typical of order forms, invoices, and written instructions.

Related Words

Synonym: 量(りょう) (amount (general))
Related: 分量(ぶんりょう) (portion)
Related: 個数(こすう) (number of items)
Related: 数(かず) (number, count)
