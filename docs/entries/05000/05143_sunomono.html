酢(す)の物(もの)

すのもの
noun
vinegared dish

Definitions

1. vinegared dish, food dressed with vinegar
A category of Japanese dishes made with vinegar-based dressing, often served cold as a refreshing side dish.

Examples

胡瓜(きゅうり)と若布(わかめ)の酢(す)の物(もの)を作(つく)りました。
I made a cucumber and wakame vinegared dish.
酢(す)の物(もの)は食欲(しょくよく)を増進(ぞうしん)させます。
Vinegared dishes stimulate the appetite.
夏(なつ)は酢(す)の物(もの)がさっぱりして美味(おい)しいです。
In summer, vinegared dishes taste refreshingly light.

Notes

酢(す)の物(もの) refers to dishes dressed with vinegar-based seasonings.

Common types:

  • 酢(す)の物(もの) (basic vinegar dressing)
  • 三杯酢(さんばいず) (soy sauce, vinegar, mirin mixture)
  • 二杯酢(にはいず) (soy sauce and vinegar mixture)
  • 甘酢(あまず) (sweet vinegar)
  • 土佐酢(とさず) (vinegar with dashi)

Common ingredients:

  • 胡瓜(きゅうり) (cucumber)
  • 若布(わかめ) (wakame seaweed)
  • 蛸(たこ) (octopus)
  • もずく (mozuku seaweed)
  • 海老(えび) (shrimp)

Characteristics:

  • Light and refreshing
  • Often served as a palate cleanser
  • Typically cold dishes
  • Common in summer meals
