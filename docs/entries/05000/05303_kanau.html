1. to come true; to be fulfilled; to be realized
For a wish, dream, or hope to become reality.
夢(ゆめ)が叶(かな)った。
My dream came true.
願(ねが)いが叶(かな)いますように。
May your wish come true.
長年(ながねん)の希望(きぼう)がやっと叶(かな)った。
A long-held wish was finally fulfilled.
努力(どりょく)すれば夢(ゆめ)は叶(かな)う。
If you work hard, your dreams will come true.
海外(かいがい)で働(はたら)きたいという夢(ゆめ)が叶(かな)って、とてもうれしい。
I'm so happy that my dream of working abroad came true.
2. to match; to be equal to
To be a match for someone in ability (often used in negative).
彼(かれ)には叶(かな)わない。
I'm no match for him.
先生(せんせい)にはとても叶(かな)わない。
I can't compare to the teacher at all.
料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)では母(はは)には叶(かな)わない。
When it comes to cooking skills, I'm no match for my mother.
力(ちから)では叶(かな)わないが、知恵(ちえ)では負(ま)けない。
I can't match them in strength, but I won't lose in wits.
経験(けいけん)では先輩(せんぱい)に叶(かな)わないが、熱意(ねつい)なら負(ま)けない。
I can't match my senior in experience, but I won't lose in enthusiasm.

叶(かな)う has two distinct meanings: (1) for wishes/dreams to come true, and (2) to be a match for someone (usually negative).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 叶(かな)える (transitive — to make come true, to grant)
  • Pattern (sense 1): Xが叶(かな)う
  • Pattern (sense 2): Xには叶(かな)わない

ASPECT (ている):
叶(かな)っている means the wish/dream has already been realized and the state continues.

SENSE 1 PATTERNS (come true):

  • 夢(ゆめ)が叶(かな)う: a dream comes true
  • 願(ねが)いが叶(かな)う: a wish is granted
  • 希望(きぼう)が叶(かな)う: a hope is fulfilled

SENSE 2 PATTERNS (be a match for):

Almost always used in the negative:

  • 〜には叶(かな)わない: to be no match for ~
  • 力(ちから)では叶(かな)わない: can't match in strength

WRITING:
The kanji 叶(かな) is common but not universal — sometimes written in kana as かなう.

Related Words

Related: 願(ねが)う (to wish)
