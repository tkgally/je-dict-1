たんぽぽ

たんぽぽ
noun
dandelion
1. dandelion
A common weed with yellow flowers that turn into white fluffy seed heads.
たんぽぽの綿毛(わたげ)を吹(ふ)く。
To blow dandelion fluff.
春(はる)になるとたんぽぽが咲(さ)く。
Dandelions bloom in spring.
庭(にわ)にたんぽぽがたくさん生(は)えている。
Many dandelions are growing in the garden.

たんぽぽ is the dandelion flower.

KANJI:
Can be written as 蒲公英(たんぽぽ) but hiragana is more common.

PARTS:

  • 花(はな): yellow flower
  • 綿毛(わたげ): fluffy seed head

CULTURE:
Blowing dandelion seeds is a common childhood activity in Japan.
