皺(しわ)

しわ
noun
wrinkle

Definitions

1. wrinkle (on skin or fabric)
A line or crease on skin caused by aging, or a crease in fabric or paper.

Examples

年(とし)を取(と)ると顔(かお)に皺(しわ)ができる。
Wrinkles appear on the face as you age.
シャツの皺(しわ)をアイロンで伸(の)ばす。
I iron out the wrinkles in my shirt.
目尻(めじり)の皺(しわ)が気(き)になる。
I'm concerned about the wrinkles at the corners of my eyes.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 皺(しわ)ができる (wrinkles form)
  • 皺(しわ)を伸(の)ばす (to smooth out wrinkles)
  • 皺(しわ)が寄(よ)る (wrinkles appear)
  • 笑(え)い皺(じわ) (laugh lines)
  • 眉間(みけん)の皺(しわ) (wrinkles between the eyebrows)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often written in hiragana (しわ) in casual contexts.
  • Can refer to both skin wrinkles and fabric creases.
