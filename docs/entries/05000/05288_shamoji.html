杓文字(しゃもじ)

しゃもじ
noun
rice paddle; rice scoop
1. rice paddle; rice scoop
A flat paddle used for scooping and serving rice from a rice cooker or container.
杓文字(しゃもじ)でご飯(はん)をよそう。
To scoop rice with a rice paddle.
炊飯器(すいはんき)に杓文字(しゃもじ)が付(つ)いている。
A rice paddle comes with the rice cooker.
木(き)の杓文字(しゃもじ)とプラスチックの杓文字(しゃもじ)がある。
There are wooden and plastic rice paddles.

杓文字(しゃもじ) is an essential kitchen tool in Japanese homes.

MATERIALS:

  • 木(き)の杓文字(しゃもじ): wooden rice paddle
  • プラスチックの杓文字(しゃもじ): plastic rice paddle
  • Non-stick versions are common with modern rice cookers

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • 宮島(みやじま)（広島県(ひろしまけん)）is famous for large decorative しゃもじ as souvenirs
  • Historically associated with good luck and prayers
