めらめら

めらめら
adverb
blazing; flaring; burning fiercely

Definitions

1. blazing; flaring; burning fiercely
Describes flames burning intensely with visible flickering.
2. burning (emotions)
Intense emotions like anger or jealousy rising up.

Examples

炎(ほのお)がめらめらと燃(も)えている。
The flames are blazing fiercely.
闘志(とうし)がめらめらと燃(も)え上(あ)がる。
Fighting spirit is burning fiercely.
Figurative usage
嫉妬(しっと)の炎(ほのお)がめらめらと燃(も)える。
The flames of jealousy burn fiercely.
Figurative usage

Notes

めらめら vividly describes actively burning flames, and is often used metaphorically for intense emotions.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぼうぼう: burning/blazing (large fire)
  • ちろちろ: flickering weakly
  • ごうごう: roaring (large fire)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • めらめら燃える: to blaze, to burn fiercely
  • めらめらと燃え上がる: to flare up
