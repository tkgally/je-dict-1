1. recognition, awareness, cognition
The act or ability of perceiving, understanding, or recognizing something; awareness or comprehension.
問題(もんだい)の深刻(しんこく)さを認識(にんしき)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to recognize the seriousness of the problem.
彼(かれ)と私(わたし)では認識(にんしき)が違(ちが)う。
He and I have different perceptions.
AIによる顔(かお)認識(にんしき)の技術(ぎじゅつ)が進歩(しんぽ)している。
AI facial recognition technology is advancing.

認識(にんしき) covers both the act of recognizing or perceiving something and the resulting awareness or understanding. As a noun it refers to one's perception or understanding of a situation; as a suru verb it means to recognize or become aware of something.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) — takes を to mark what is recognized.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 認識(にんしき)する: to recognize, to be aware of
  • 認識(にんしき)を持(も)つ: to have an awareness of
  • 認識(にんしき)が甘(あま)い: to have a naive/insufficient understanding
  • 認識(にんしき)を深(ふか)める: to deepen one's understanding
  • 顔(かお)認識(にんしき): facial recognition
  • 音声(おんせい)認識(にんしき): voice recognition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 理解(りかい): understanding (deeper comprehension)
  • 自覚(じかく): self-awareness (awareness of one's own state)
  • 意識(いしき): consciousness, awareness (broader, more general)
