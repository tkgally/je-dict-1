配送(はいそう)

はいそう
noun / suru verb
delivery; shipping
1. delivery; shipping; distribution
The act of transporting and delivering goods.
配送(はいそう)は無料(むりょう)ですか？
Is delivery free?
配送(はいそう)日(び)を指定(してい)できます。
You can specify the delivery date.
全国(ぜんこく)に配送(はいそう)します。
We deliver nationwide.

配送(はいそう) refers to delivery or shipping of goods.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 配送(はいそう)料(りょう): delivery fee
  • 配送(はいそう)先(さき): delivery destination
  • 配送(はいそう)状況(じょうきょう): delivery status
  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう): same-day delivery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配達(はいたつ): delivery (often smaller scale)
  • 発送(はっそう): shipping (from sender's perspective)
  • 出荷(しゅっか): shipment (business term)
