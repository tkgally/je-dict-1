ジーパン

じーぱん
noun
jeans
1. jeans, denim pants
Casual pants made of denim fabric, originally designed for work but now worn as everyday casual wear.
新(あたら)しいジーパンを買(か)った。
I bought new jeans.
ジーパンにTシャツという格好(かっこう)だった。
I was wearing jeans and a T-shirt.
このジーパンはスキニータイプだ。
These jeans are skinny type.

ジーパン is a Japanese-coined word for jeans, combining "G" (from "jeans") + パン(ツ) (pants).

ALTERNATIVE TERMS:

  • ジーンズ: jeans (closer to English, increasingly common)
  • デニム: denim (trendy, fashion-industry term)

ジーパン is the most traditional Japanese term and is still widely understood, though younger speakers may prefer ジーンズ or デニム.

COMMON TYPES:

  • スキニージーンズ: skinny jeans
  • ストレートジーンズ: straight-leg jeans
  • ダメージジーパン: distressed jeans

NOTE:
ジーパン was especially popular in the 1970s-80s. The term has a slightly older or more casual feel compared to ジーンズ/デニム.

Related Words

Synonym: ジーンズ (jeans (modern))
Related: デニム (denim)
