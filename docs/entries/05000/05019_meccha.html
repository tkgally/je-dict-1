めっちゃ

めっちゃ
adverb
very, extremely

Definitions

1. very, extremely
Casual intensifier meaning 'very much' or 'extremely'. Originally Kansai dialect.

Examples

めっちゃ楽(たの)しかった！
It was super fun!
めっちゃ疲(つか)れた。
I'm extremely tired.
この映画(えいが)、めっちゃ面白(おもしろ)いよ。
This movie is really interesting.

Notes

Originally from Kansai dialect (滅茶(めちゃ)), now used throughout Japan, especially by younger speakers. More casual than とても or すごく. Variations include めちゃ, めちゃくちゃ (even stronger). Very common in everyday casual speech.

Related Words

Synonym: すごく (standard)
