もこもこ

もこもこ
adverb
fluffy; puffy; bulky
1. fluffy; puffy; soft and bulky
Describes something soft, thick, and cloud-like in appearance.
もこもこの毛布(もうふ)で暖(あたた)まる。
I warm up with a fluffy blanket.
もこもこした雲(くも)が浮(う)かんでいる。
Fluffy clouds are floating in the sky.
冬(ふゆ)はもこもこの服(ふく)を着(き)る。
I wear fluffy clothes in winter.

もこもこ is an onomatopoeic word describing soft, thick, puffy textures — things that look rounded and pillowy. Often associated with warmth and comfort.

USAGE:
Can be used as a modifier (もこもこの + noun) or with する (もこもこした + noun) to describe the texture.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • もこもこの毛布(もうふ): fluffy blanket
  • もこもこの雲(くも): puffy clouds
  • もこもこの服(ふく): bulky/fluffy clothes
  • もこもこした素材(そざい): puffy material

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ふわふわ: fluffy, light (more airy, less dense)
  • もふもふ: fluffy (especially animal fur; internet/casual speech)
  • ふかふか: soft and springy (beds, cushions)
  • もこもこ: soft, thick, and puffy (emphasizes volume and roundness)
