ケトル

けとる
noun
kettle
1. kettle
A container for boiling water, often electric.
ケトルでお湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす。
To boil water in a kettle.
電気(でんき)ケトルは便利(べんり)だ。
Electric kettles are convenient.
ケトルが沸(わ)いた。
The kettle has boiled.

ケトル is from English "kettle," referring to a container for boiling water.

TYPES:

  • 電気(でんき)ケトル: electric kettle (most common in modern Japan)
  • やかん: traditional stovetop kettle (Japanese style)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ケトルでお湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす: to boil water in a kettle
  • ケトルが沸(わ)く: the kettle boils
  • ケトルに水(みず)を入(い)れる: to fill the kettle with water

RELATED ITEMS:

  • ポット: pot/thermos (keeps water hot)
  • 湯沸(ゆわ)かし器(き): water heater

USAGE NOTE:
In Japan, 電気(でんき)ケトル (electric kettles) are extremely popular for quickly boiling water for tea, instant noodles, and coffee.
