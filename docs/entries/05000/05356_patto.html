ぱっと

ぱっと
adverb
suddenly; in a flash; brightly

Definitions

1. suddenly; in a flash; quickly
Describes something happening instantaneously.
2. brightly; flashily
Describes something becoming bright or standing out vividly.

Examples

電気(でんき)がぱっとついた。
The light suddenly came on.
ぱっと見(み)ただけで分(わ)かる。
You can tell just at a glance.
ぱっと見 = at a glance
花火(はなび)がぱっと開(ひら)いた。
The fireworks burst open brightly.

Notes

ぱっと is an onomatopoeia expressing sudden, quick actions or bright appearances.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぱっと見 (ぱっとみ): at a glance
  • ぱっとしない: not impressive, dull

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • さっと: quickly, swiftly (smooth motion)
  • はっと: with a start (surprise)
