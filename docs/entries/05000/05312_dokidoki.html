どきどき

どきどき
adverb
heart pounding; throbbing with excitement

Definitions

1. heart pounding; throbbing
Describes the sound or sensation of one's heart beating fast due to excitement, nervousness, fear, or anticipation.

Examples

緊張(きんちょう)で心臓(しんぞう)がどきどきする。
My heart is pounding from nervousness.
初(はじ)めてのデートでどきどきした。
I was nervous and excited on my first date.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)を待(ま)つ間(あいだ)、どきどきしていた。
I was anxiously waiting for the exam results.

Notes

どきどき is an onomatopoeic word (擬音語) that imitates the sound of a rapidly beating heart.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • どきどきする: to feel one's heart pounding
  • 胸(むね)がどきどきする: one's heart is pounding
  • どきどきしながら: while feeling nervous/excited

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • わくわく: excited, thrilled (positive anticipation)
  • はらはら: anxious, nervous (worry about outcome)
  • ばくばく: heart beating very fast (stronger intensity)
