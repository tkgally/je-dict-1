タイパ

たいぱ
noun
time-performance, time efficiency

Definitions

1. time-performance, time efficiency
The relationship between time invested and results gained. Short for タイムパフォーマンス.

Examples

タイパを重視(じゅうし)して倍速(ばいそく)で動画(どうが)を見(み)ます。
I watch videos at double speed to prioritize time efficiency.
この方法(ほうほう)はタイパがいいです。
This method is time-efficient.
タイパを考(かんが)えて行動(こうどう)する若者(わかもの)が増(ふ)えています。
More young people are acting with time efficiency in mind.

Notes

A newer abbreviation modeled after コスパ, from タイムパフォーマンス (time performance). Gained popularity especially among younger generations in the 2020s. Reflects a cultural shift toward valuing efficiency in time use.

Related Words

Related: コスパ (cost-performance)
