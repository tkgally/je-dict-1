麦(むぎ)

むぎ
noun
wheat; barley; grain
1. wheat; barley; grain
A general term for cereal grains including wheat, barley, oats, and rye.
麦(むぎ)畑(ばたけ)。
A wheat field.
麦茶(むぎちゃ)を飲(の)む。
To drink barley tea.
麦(むぎ)ご飯(はん)。
Rice mixed with barley.

麦 is a general term for grains, with specific types distinguished by prefix.

TYPES:

  • 小麦 (こむぎ): wheat
  • 大麦 (おおむぎ): barley
  • 燕麦 (えんばく): oats
  • ライ麦: rye

COMMON PRODUCTS:

  • 麦茶 (むぎちゃ): barley tea (popular summer drink)
  • 麦わら (むぎわら): straw
  • 麦わら帽子: straw hat
