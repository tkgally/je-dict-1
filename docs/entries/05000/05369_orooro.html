おろおろ

おろおろ
adverb
flustered; bewildered; at a loss

Definitions

1. flustered; bewildered; at a loss
Being so confused or panicked that one doesn't know what to do.

Examples

火事(かじ)でおろおろしてしまった。
I was flustered because of the fire.
おろおろしていても始(はじ)まらない。
Being flustered won't help anything.
彼女(かのじょ)はおろおろするばかりだった。
She could only stand there bewildered.

Notes

おろおろ describes a state of confusion and panic where one cannot act properly.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あたふた: in a panic, hurriedly
  • おどおど: nervously, timidly
  • まごまご: bewildered, confused

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • おろおろする: to be flustered
  • おろおろするばかり: can only stand bewildered
