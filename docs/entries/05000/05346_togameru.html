1. to blame; to reproach; to find fault with
To criticize someone for their wrongdoing or mistake.
良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)める。
My conscience bothers me.
Common idiomatic expression
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)を咎(とが)めた。
I reproached him for his behavior.
嘘(うそ)をついたことが良心(りょうしん)に咎(とが)められる。
My conscience nags me for having told a lie.
遅刻(ちこく)を咎(とが)められて何(なに)も言(い)えなかった。
I was reproached for being late and couldn't say anything.
2. to question; to challenge
To question someone's actions or identity, often in a suspicious manner.
警備員(けいびいん)に咎(とが)められた。
I was questioned by the security guard.
不審(ふしん)な人(ひと)がいたので、「何(なに)をしているんですか」と咎(とが)めた。
There was a suspicious person, so I challenged them with 'What are you doing?'
夜(よる)遅(おそ)く帰(かえ)った息子(むすこ)を咎(とが)めるように見(み)つめた。
She stared at her son who came home late at night in a reproachful way.

咎(とが)める is a somewhat literary/formal verb with two main uses: reproaching someone for wrongdoing, and questioning someone's actions or identity.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 良心(りょうしん)が咎(とが)める: one's conscience bothers one (very common idiom)
  • 〜を咎(とが)める: to blame/reproach someone for ~
  • 〜に咎(とが)められる: to be questioned/challenged by ~

ASPECT (ている):
咎(とが)めている describes an ongoing state of reproach or nagging guilt.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 咎(とが): fault, blame, sin (noun)
  • 責(せ)める: to blame, to criticize (more common, everyday)
  • 非難(ひなん)する: to condemn, to censure (formal)

REGISTER:
More literary than 責(せ)める. Often seen in written language and formal contexts.

Related Words

Synonym: 責(せ)める (to blame (everyday))
Synonym: 非難(ひなん) (condemnation (formal))
