真夜中(まよなか)

まよなか
noun
middle of the night; midnight
1. middle of the night; midnight
The period of time around 12 o'clock at night, or the deepest part of the night.
真夜中(まよなか)に起(お)きた。
I woke up in the middle of the night.
真夜中(まよなか)まで仕事(しごと)した。
I worked until midnight.
真夜中(まよなか)に電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)った。
The phone rang in the middle of the night.

真{夜中(よなか)|まよなか} can refer to midnight specifically or the late night hours generally.

RELATED TIME WORDS:

  • 深夜(しんや) (しんや): late at night
  • 夜中(よなか) (よなか): during the night
  • 午前零時(ごぜんれいじ) (ごぜんれいじ): 12:00 AM exactly
  • 未明(みめい) (みめい): before dawn
