現実的(げんじつてき)

げんじつてき
na-adjective
realistic

Definitions

1. realistic, practical
Based on what is practical or achievable rather than idealistic.

Examples

もっと現実的(げんじつてき)に考(かんが)えましょう。
Let's think more realistically.
それは現実的(げんじつてき)な目標(もくひょう)ではない。
That's not a realistic goal.
現実的(げんじつてき)な解決策(かいけつさく)を提案(ていあん)してください。
Please propose a realistic solution.

Notes

The opposite of 理想的(りそうてき) (ideal) in the sense of being grounded in reality. 現実(げんじつ) means 'reality.' Common in business planning and problem-solving discussions.

Related Words

Contrast: 理想的(りそうてき) (ideal)
