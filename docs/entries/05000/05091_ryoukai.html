1. understanding, comprehension, consent
Understanding and accepting something; giving one's agreement or acknowledgment.
了解(りょうかい)を得(え)る。
To obtain consent.
上司(じょうし)の了解(りょうかい)を得(え)てから進(すす)める。
I'll proceed after getting my supervisor's approval.
本人(ほんにん)の了解(りょうかい)なしに写真(しゃしん)を使(つか)ってはいけない。
You must not use the photo without the person's consent.
関係者(かんけいしゃ)全員(ぜんいん)の了解(りょうかい)を得(え)るのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to get the consent of all the people involved.
計画(けいかく)を変更(へんこう)する場合(ばあい)は、事前(じぜん)に全員(ぜんいん)の了解(りょうかい)を得(え)なければならない。
If you change the plan, you must obtain everyone's consent in advance.
2. roger, understood, copy that
An acknowledgment that a message has been received and understood, commonly used in professional/military contexts.
「了解(りょうかい)」
"Roger." / "Got it."
「了解(りょうかい)です。すぐ(すぐ)やります」
"Understood. I'll do it right away."
「明日(あした)10時(じ)に来(き)てください」「了解(りょうかい)です」
"Please come at 10 o'clock tomorrow." "Understood."
了解(りょうかい)しました。すぐ(すぐ)対応(たいおう)します。
Understood. I'll handle it right away.
メールで「了解(りょうかい)しました」と返事(へんじ)したが、上司(じょうし)には「承知(しょうち)しました」の方(ほう)がいいと言(い)われた。
I replied "understood" in an email, but my boss told me it's better to use the more formal expression.
承知(しょうち)しました is considered more appropriate when speaking to superiors.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is an ordinary noun: the consent or approval of a person who has authority over the matter. It is what you 了解(りょうかい)を得(え)る (obtain) before acting.

Sense 2 is the one-word reply "understood / got it", heard in workplaces, on radio, and in messaging apps. As a reply it is a complete utterance on its own.

POLITENESS LEVELS:

Replies of acknowledgment form a clear ladder, and choosing the wrong rung is a common learner mistake:

  • 了解(りょうかい) - bare, plain; between colleagues and friends, or to a subordinate
  • 了解(りょうかい)です - lightly polite; the everyday chat and messaging form
  • 了解(りょうかい)しました - polite, but see the caution below
  • 承知(しょうち)しました - humble; the safe choice toward a superior or a customer
  • かしこまりました - the most deferential; service-industry standard

CAUTION:
了解(りょうかい) is not itself a humble word. Many Japanese workplaces treat even 了解(りょうかい)しました as too casual toward a superior or a client, and expect 承知(しょうち)しました instead. The rule is not universal - some companies find it fussy - but a learner is safer defaulting to 承知(しょうち)しました upward and keeping 了解(りょうかい) for peers.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 了解(りょうかい)する (to understand, to consent)
  • 了解(りょうかい)を得(え)る (to obtain consent)
  • 了解(りょうかい)を求(もと)める (to seek consent)
  • 了解(りょうかい)なしに (without consent)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 承知(しょうち) - understanding, with the added sense of accepting what is asked; the humble counterpart
  • 分(わ)かりました - neutral "I understand"; safe in most situations but weaker as an acknowledgment of an instruction
  • かしこまりました - very formal; a shop assistant or hotel clerk answering a customer
