ぎゅっと

ぎゅっと
adverb
tightly; firmly; with a strong squeeze
1. tightly; firmly; with a strong squeeze
Describes gripping, squeezing, or holding something very tightly with strength.
子供(こども)をぎゅっと抱(だ)きしめた。
I hugged the child tightly.
目(め)をぎゅっと閉(と)じた。
I squeezed my eyes shut.
レモンをぎゅっと絞(しぼ)る。
Squeeze the lemon hard.

ぎゅっと is an onomatopoeia describing a strong, tight squeeze. It's more forceful than きゅっと.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きゅっと: tightly (lighter/quicker squeeze)
  • ぎゅーっと: tightly (prolonged squeeze)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぎゅっと抱(だ)きしめる: to hug tightly
  • ぎゅっと握(にぎ)る: to grip tightly
  • ぎゅっと詰(つ)める: to pack tightly
