ぱりぱり

ぱりぱり
adverb, na-adjective
crispy; crunchy; energetic
1. crispy; crunchy
Describes food with a light, crisp texture that makes a cracking sound when bitten.
ぱりぱりの海苔(のり)で巻(ま)く。
Wrap with crispy seaweed.
ぱりぱりに焼(や)けた餃子(ぎょうざ)がおいしい。
The crispy fried dumplings are delicious.
春巻(はるま)きをぱりぱりに揚(あ)げた。
I deep-fried the spring rolls until crispy.
2. energetic; vigorous; ambitious
Describes someone who works with energy and drive.
彼女(かのじょ)はぱりぱりの仕事人(しごとにん)だ。
She is a very ambitious businesswoman.
ぱりぱり働(はたら)く若(わか)い社員(しゃいん)。
An energetic young employee who works hard.
彼(かれ)はぱりぱりの新人(しんじん)だ。
He is an ambitious newcomer.

ぱりぱり is a 擬態語(ぎたいご)/擬音語(ぎおんご) (mimetic/onomatopoeic word) describing a thin, crispy texture or the sound of something crispy being eaten or cracked.

COMMON FOOD USES:

  • ぱりぱりの海苔(のり): crispy seaweed
  • ぱりぱりの餃子(ぎょうざ): crispy dumplings
  • ぱりぱりに揚(あ)げる: to fry until crispy
  • ぱりぱりの皮(かわ): crispy skin (e.g., roast chicken)

OTHER USES:

  • ぱりぱりの新札(しんさつ): crisp new bills
  • 糊(のり)でぱりぱりのシャツ: a shirt stiff with starch

SIMILAR TEXTURE WORDS:

  • さくさく: light and crispy (cookies, tempura)
  • かりかり: hard and crunchy (harder than ぱりぱり)
  • ぽりぽり: crunching sound (snacking)
  • ばりばり: breaking/crunching (harder, louder)
