平行(へいこう)

へいこう
noun / na-adjective
parallel
See also: 並行(へいこう) (parallel, concurrent)
1. parallel
Lines or surfaces that maintain equal distance and never intersect.
この二(ふた)つの線(せん)は平行(へいこう)だ。
These two lines are parallel.
線路(せんろ)は平行(へいこう)に走(はし)っている。
The railroad tracks run parallel to each other.
平行(へいこう)な二(ふた)つの線(せん)はどこまで伸(の)ばしても交(まじ)わらない。
Two parallel lines never intersect no matter how far they are extended.
2. simultaneous; concurrent
Doing multiple things at the same time.
仕事(しごと)と勉強(べんきょう)を平行(へいこう)して進(すす)める。
To pursue work and studies simultaneously.
二(ふた)つのプロジェクトを平行(へいこう)して進(すす)めている。
We are working on two projects simultaneously.
話(はな)し合(あ)いは平行線(へいこうせん)のままで、なかなか結論(けつろん)が出(で)なかった。
The discussion remained at a stalemate, and no conclusion was reached.
平行線(へいこうせん) (parallel lines) is used figuratively to mean that two sides cannot reach agreement.

平行(へいこう) has both a geometric meaning (parallel lines) and a figurative meaning (doing things simultaneously).

GEOMETRIC SENSE:

  • 平行線(へいこうせん): parallel lines
  • 平行四辺形(へいこうしへんけい): parallelogram

FIGURATIVE SENSE:

  • 平行(へいこう)して進(すす)める: to pursue simultaneously
  • 平行線(へいこうせん)をたどる: to remain at a stalemate (a discussion where two sides never meet, like parallel lines)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 垂直(すいちょく): perpendicular, vertical
  • 並行(へいこう): concurrent, simultaneous (the variant spelling preferred for the "simultaneous" meaning)

NOTE:
平行(へいこう) and 並行(へいこう) are homophones. 平行(へいこう) emphasizes the geometric sense; 並行(へいこう) is preferred for "concurrent" or "alongside."

Related Words

Homophone: 平衡(へいこう) (balance)
Antonym: 垂直(すいちょく) (perpendicular)
