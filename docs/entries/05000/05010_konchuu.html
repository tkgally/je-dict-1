昆虫(こんちゅう)

こんちゅう
noun
insect
1. insect, bug
A small invertebrate animal with six legs and typically one or two pairs of wings, such as beetles, butterflies, and ants.
子供(こども)は昆虫(こんちゅう)採集(さいしゅう)が好(す)きだ。
Children like collecting insects.
日本(にほん)には多(おお)くの種類(しゅるい)の昆虫(こんちゅう)がいる。
There are many kinds of insects in Japan.
昆虫(こんちゅう)図鑑(ずかん)で調(しら)べる。
Look it up in an insect encyclopedia.

Japan has a rich tradition of appreciating insects, including keeping them as pets and listening to their sounds.

Major insect groups:

  • 甲虫(こうちゅう) (beetles) - カブトムシ, クワガタ
  • 蝶(ちょう) (butterflies)
  • 蛾(が) (moths)
  • 蜂(はち) (bees, wasps)
  • 蟻(あり) (ants)
  • 蝉(せみ) (cicadas)
  • トンボ (dragonflies)
  • バッタ (grasshoppers)

昆虫採集(こんちゅうさいしゅう) (insect collecting) is a popular summer activity for children.

Cultural notes:

  • 虫(むし)の音(ね) (insect sounds) - appreciated in autumn
  • 鈴虫(すずむし) (bell crickets) kept for their song
  • カブトムシ and クワガタ are prized pets
