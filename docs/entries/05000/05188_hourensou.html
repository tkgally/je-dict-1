ほうれん草(そう)

ほうれんそう
noun
spinach
1. spinach
A leafy green vegetable known for its nutritional value.
ほうれん草(そう)の胡麻(ごま)和(あ)えを作(つく)った。
I made spinach with sesame dressing.
A classic Japanese side dish
ほうれん草(そう)は鉄分(てつぶん)が多(おお)い。
Spinach is high in iron.
ほうれん草(そう)を茹(ゆ)でて水(みず)にさらす。
To boil spinach and rinse in cold water.
Common cooking technique

ほうれん草(そう) is a nutritious vegetable commonly used in Japanese cooking.

COMMON DISHES:

  • ほうれん草(そう)の胡麻(ごま)和(あ)え: spinach with sesame dressing
  • ほうれん草(そう)のおひたし: boiled spinach in dashi
  • ほうれん草(そう)の白(しろ)和(あ)え: spinach with tofu dressing

BUSINESS USAGE: ほうれんそう is also an acronym for 報告(ほうこく)・連絡(れんらく)・相談(そうだん) (報(ほう)連(れん)相(そう)), meaning 'report, contact, consult' - key business communication practices.
