手数料(てすうりょう)

てすうりょう
noun
handling fee; commission; service charge
1. handling fee; commission; service charge
A fee charged for processing a transaction or providing a service.
手数料(てすうりょう)がかかります。
There's a handling fee.
手数料(てすうりょう)無料(むりょう)。
No handling fee.
ATMの手数料(てすうりょう)は高(たか)い。
ATM fees are high.

手数料 is a common fee term in financial and service contexts.

COMMON TYPES:

  • 振込手数料: transfer fee
  • ATM手数料: ATM fee
  • 代行手数料: agency fee
  • 仲介手数料 (ちゅうかいてすうりょう): brokerage fee

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手数料がかかる: a fee is charged
  • 手数料無料: free of charge

Related Words

Related: 振込(ふりこみ) (bank transfer)
