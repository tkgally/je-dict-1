1. slope; inclination; tilt
The state of being angled or slanted from horizontal or vertical.
この坂(さか)は傾斜(けいしゃ)が急(きゅう)だ。
This slope has a steep incline.
屋根(やね)に傾斜(けいしゃ)をつける。
To give the roof a slope.
地面(じめん)が傾斜(けいしゃ)しているので歩(ある)きにくい。
The ground is sloped, making it hard to walk.

傾斜(けいしゃ) means a slope, tilt, or inclination from horizontal or vertical. Combines 傾(けい) (lean, tilt) + 斜(しゃ) (slant). Also used as a する verb: 傾斜(けいしゃ)する (to slope, to incline).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 傾斜(けいしゃ)が急(きゅう)だ: the slope is steep
  • 傾斜(けいしゃ)が緩(ゆる)やかだ: the slope is gentle
  • 傾斜(けいしゃ)角度(かくど): angle of inclination
  • 傾斜(けいしゃ)地(ち): sloping land

FIGURATIVE USE:
Can also describe a figurative leaning or bias: 予算(よさん)の傾斜(けいしゃ)配分(はいぶん) (weighted budget allocation).

RELATED:

  • 坂(さか): hill, slope (everyday)
  • 斜面(しゃめん): slope surface
  • 勾配(こうばい): gradient (more technical)
