振込(ふりこみ)

ふりこみ
noun
bank transfer; remittance
See also: 振(ふ)り込(こ)む (verb form)
1. bank transfer; remittance
The act of transferring money from one bank account to another.
振込(ふりこみ)で支払(しはら)う。
To pay by bank transfer.
振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)がかかる。
There's a transfer fee.
ATMで振込(ふりこみ)をした。
I made a transfer at the ATM.

振込(ふりこみ) refers to a bank transfer, one of the most common payment methods in Japan for bills, rent, and business transactions.

WORD FORMATION:
Noun form of 振(ふ)り込(こ)む (to transfer money into an account).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 振込(ふりこみ)をする: to make a transfer
  • 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう): transfer fee
  • 振込先(ふりこみさき): transfer destination (account)
  • 振込(ふりこみ)期限(きげん): transfer deadline

TRANSFER METHODS:

  • ATM振込(ふりこみ): ATM transfer
  • ネット振込(ふりこみ): online transfer
  • 窓口(まどぐち)振込(ふりこみ): over-the-counter transfer

CAUTION:
振込(ふりこみ)詐欺(さぎ) (wire fraud / transfer scam) is a well-known type of scam in Japan targeting elderly people.

Related Words

Related: 口座(こうざ) (bank account)
Related: 手数料(てすうりょう) (transfer/handling fee)
Contrast: 振替(ふりかえ) (auto debit / rescheduling)
