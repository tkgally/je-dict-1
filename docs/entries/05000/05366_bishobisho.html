びしょびしょ

びしょびしょ
adverb
soaking wet; drenched
1. soaking wet; drenched
Describes being completely wet, thoroughly soaked with water.
雨(あめ)でびしょびしょに濡(ぬ)れた。
I got soaking wet from the rain.
服(ふく)がびしょびしょだ。
My clothes are soaking wet.
汗(あせ)でシャツがびしょびしょになった。
My shirt got drenched with sweat.

びしょびしょ is an onomatopoeic word describing being thoroughly soaked with water. It functions as a na-adjective and adverb.

FORMS:

  • びしょびしょだ: is soaking wet (predicate)
  • びしょびしょに濡(ぬ)れる: to get soaking wet
  • びしょびしょになる: to become soaking wet
  • びしょびしょの服(ふく): soaking wet clothes (modifier)

DEGREES OF WETNESS:

  • しっとり: slightly moist (pleasant)
  • じっとり: damp, clammy (unpleasant)
  • びしょびしょ: soaking wet
  • ずぶ濡(ぬ)れ: completely drenched (noun)

REGISTER:
Casual and vivid. Common in everyday speech. The variant びしょ濡(ぬ)れ (noun) means "soaking wet" and is slightly more literary.

Related Words

Related: ずぶ濡(ぬ)れ (completely drenched (noun))
Contrast: しっとり (slightly moist (pleasant))
Related: 濡(ぬ)れる (to get wet)
