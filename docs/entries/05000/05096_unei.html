1. operation, management, administration
The act of running or managing an organization, business, event, or system.
イベントの運営(うんえい)を担当(たんとう)している。
I'm in charge of event operations.
会社(かいしゃ)の運営(うんえい)がうまくいっていない。
The company's management isn't going well.
ボランティアが運営(うんえい)を支(ささ)えている。
Volunteers support the operations.

運営(うんえい) refers to the ongoing management and operation of an organization, event, or system. It emphasizes the day-to-day running of something rather than strategic direction.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 運営(うんえい)する: to operate, to manage
  • 運営(うんえい)を担当(たんとう)する: to be in charge of operations
  • 運営(うんえい)委員会(いいんかい): steering committee
  • 運営(うんえい)費(ひ): operating expenses
  • 円滑(えんかつ)な運営(うんえい): smooth operation
  • 運営(うんえい)方針(ほうしん): management policy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経営(けいえい): business management (focuses on the business as a whole, including profit/strategy)
  • 管理(かんり): management, supervision (focuses on controlling and maintaining)
  • 運用(うんよう): operation, utilization (focuses on putting resources or systems to practical use)
