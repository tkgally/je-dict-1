レタス

れたす
noun
lettuce
1. lettuce
A leafy green vegetable commonly used in salads and sandwiches.
レタスを手(て)でちぎる。
To tear lettuce by hand.
Common preparation method
サンドイッチにレタスを挟(はさ)む。
To put lettuce in a sandwich.
シャキシャキのレタスが美味(おい)しい。
Crisp lettuce is delicious.

From English "lettuce." One of the most commonly eaten leafy vegetables in Japan, used in salads, sandwiches, and as a garnish for various dishes.

VARIETIES:

  • 玉(たま)レタス: iceberg/head lettuce (most common)
  • サニーレタス: loose-leaf lettuce (red-tipped)
  • ロメインレタス: romaine lettuce
  • サラダ菜(な): butterhead lettuce

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レタスを手(て)でちぎる: to tear lettuce by hand
  • レタスを洗(あら)う: to wash lettuce
  • レタスを敷(し)く: to line with lettuce (as a bed for food)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, lettuce is commonly torn by hand rather than cut with a knife to prevent browning and preserve crispness.
