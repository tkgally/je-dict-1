レタス

れたす
noun
lettuce
1. lettuce
A leafy green vegetable commonly used in salads and sandwiches.
レタスを手(て)でちぎる。
To tear lettuce by hand.
Common preparation method
サンドイッチにレタスを挟(はさ)む。
To put lettuce in a sandwich.
シャキシャキのレタスが美味(おい)しい。
Crisp lettuce is delicious.

レタス is commonly used in salads and as a garnish.

VARIETIES:

  • 玉(たま)レタス: iceberg/head lettuce
  • サニーレタス: loose-leaf lettuce (red-tipped)
  • ロメインレタス: romaine lettuce
  • サラダ菜(な): butterhead lettuce

USAGE TIP: Japanese often tear lettuce by hand rather than cutting with a knife to prevent browning.
