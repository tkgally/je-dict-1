お疲(つか)れ様(さま)

おつかれさま
expression
good work; thank you for your hard work
1. good work; thank you for your hard work
An expression used to acknowledge someone's effort, commonly used when parting after work or finishing a task.
今日(きょう)もお疲(つか)れ様(さま)でした。
Thank you for your hard work today.
Formal version with でした
お疲(つか)れ様(さま)、先(さき)に帰(かえ)ります。
Good work, I'm heading home first.
「お疲(つか)れ様(さま)」「お疲(つか)れ」
"Good work." "You too."
Casual exchange

お疲(つか)れ様(さま) is one of the most frequently used expressions in Japanese workplace culture.

FORMS:

  • お疲(つか)れ様(さま)です: polite (colleagues, general)
  • お疲(つか)れ様(さま)でした: past tense (end of work/meeting)
  • お疲(つか)れ: casual (friends, peers)
  • お疲(つか)れ〜: very casual

USAGE:

  • As a greeting when meeting colleagues
  • As a farewell after work
  • To acknowledge someone's effort
  • When finishing a project or meeting

NOTE: Often abbreviated to お疲(つか)れ or おつ in casual contexts.
