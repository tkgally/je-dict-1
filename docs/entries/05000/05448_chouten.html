頂点(ちょうてん)

ちょうてん
noun
peak; vertex; apex

Definitions

1. vertex; apex (geometry)
The point where two or more lines meet, such as the corner of a polygon.
2. peak; summit; pinnacle
The highest point or greatest level of something.

Examples

三角形(さんかくけい)には三(みっ)つの頂点(ちょうてん)がある。
A triangle has three vertices.
彼(かれ)は人気(にんき)の頂点(ちょうてん)にいる。
He is at the peak of his popularity.
興奮(こうふん)が頂点(ちょうてん)に達(たっ)した。
The excitement reached its climax.

Notes

頂点(ちょうてん) has both geometric and figurative meanings.

GEOMETRIC SENSE:

  • The point where edges meet in a polygon or polyhedron
  • 頂点(ちょうてん)角(かく): vertex angle

FIGURATIVE SENSE:

  • The highest point of achievement, fame, or intensity
  • 頂点(ちょうてん)に達(たっ)する: to reach the peak
  • 頂点(ちょうてん)を極(きわ)める: to attain the summit
