化粧品(けしょうひん)

けしょうひん
noun
cosmetics
1. cosmetics, makeup products
Products used to enhance or alter the appearance of the face or body, including makeup, skincare, and beauty products.
化粧品(けしょうひん)を買(か)う。
To buy cosmetics.
新(あたら)しい化粧品(けしょうひん)を試(ため)した。
I tried new cosmetics.
化粧品(けしょうひん)にアレルギーがある。
I'm allergic to cosmetics.
日本(にほん)の化粧品(けしょうひん)は海外(かいがい)でも人気(にんき)がある。
Japanese cosmetics are popular overseas too.
化粧品(けしょうひん)売(う)り場(ば)で店員(てんいん)に肌(はだ)に合(あ)うものを選(えら)んでもらった。
I had the staff at the cosmetics counter choose products that suit my skin.

化粧品(けしょうひん) is a broad term covering makeup, skincare, and beauty products. In Japan, the word encompasses everything from foundation and lipstick to lotions and creams. Japanese 化粧品(けしょうひん) are highly regarded internationally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 化粧品(けしょうひん)会社(がいしゃ): cosmetics company
  • 化粧品(けしょうひん)売(う)り場(ば): cosmetics counter/section
  • 化粧品(けしょうひん)を使(つか)う: to use cosmetics
  • 基礎(きそ)化粧品(けしょうひん): basic skincare products
  • 化粧品(けしょうひん)メーカー: cosmetics manufacturer

RELATED TERMS:

  • 化粧(けしょう): makeup (the act or the result)
  • コスメ: cosmetics (casual abbreviation, common in magazines and advertising)
  • スキンケア: skincare
