残高(ざんだか)

ざんだか
noun
balance (of an account)
1. balance (of an account)
The amount of money currently in a bank account or on a card.
残高(ざんだか)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the balance.
残高(ざんだか)不足(ふそく)で引(ひ)き落(お)としできなかった。
The withdrawal failed due to insufficient balance.
カードの残高(ざんだか)はいくら？
What's the balance on the card?

残高(ざんだか) means the current balance in a financial account. Combines 残(ざん) (remaining) + 高(だか) (amount/quantity). Used for bank accounts, prepaid cards, and electronic money.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 残高(ざんだか)確認(かくにん): balance inquiry
  • 残高(ざんだか)不足(ぶそく): insufficient balance/funds
  • 残高(ざんだか)照会(しょうかい): balance check (formal)
  • 預金(よきん)残高(ざんだか): deposit balance
  • 口座(こうざ)残高(ざんだか): account balance

MODERN USAGE:

  • チャージ残高(ざんだか): charged balance (for IC cards like Suica, PASMO)
  • ポイント残高(ざんだか): points balance
  • 残高(ざんだか)不足(ぶそく)で引(ひ)き落(お)としできない: unable to withdraw due to insufficient balance

RELATED WORDS:

  • 預金(よきん): deposit, savings
  • 口座(こうざ): bank account
  • 引(ひ)き落(お)とし: automatic debit/withdrawal
