残高(ざんだか)

ざんだか
noun
balance (of an account)

Definitions

1. balance (of an account)
The amount of money currently in a bank account or on a card.

Examples

残高(ざんだか)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the balance.
残高(ざんだか)不足(ふそく)で引(ひ)き落(お)としできなかった。
The withdrawal failed due to insufficient balance.
カードの残高(ざんだか)はいくら？
What's the balance on the card?

Notes

残高 is commonly used for bank accounts and prepaid cards.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 残高確認: balance inquiry
  • 残高不足: insufficient balance
  • 残高照会 (ざんだかしょうかい): balance check

USAGE:

  • 預金残高: deposit balance
  • チャージ残高: charged balance (for IC cards)

Related Words

Related: 預金(よきん) (deposit/savings)
