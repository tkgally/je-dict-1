貼(は)り付(つ)け

はりつけ
noun
paste; pasting
1. paste; pasting (computing)
The action of inserting copied or cut content at a new location, especially in computing contexts.
コピーした文章(ぶんしょう)を貼(は)り付(つ)けする。
To paste the copied text.
右(みぎ)クリックで貼(は)り付(つ)けを選(えら)ぶ。
Choose paste from the right-click menu.
貼(は)り付(つ)けのショートカットキーはCtrl+Vです。
The shortcut key for paste is Ctrl+V.

貼(は)り付(つ)け is the standard Japanese computing term for 'paste,' referring to inserting copied or cut content.

ORIGIN:
Derived from the verb 貼(は)り付(つ)ける (to stick, to paste), which originally referred to physically gluing or attaching something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 貼(は)り付(つ)けする: to paste
  • 貼(は)り付(つ)けを選(えら)ぶ: to choose paste (from a menu)
  • コピー&貼(は)り付(つ)け: copy and paste
  • コピーして貼(は)り付(つ)ける: to copy and paste

RELATED COMPUTING TERMS:

  • コピー: copy
  • 切(き)り取(と)り: cut
  • 元(もと)に戻(もど)す: undo
  • 選択(せんたく): select

NOTE:
In casual speech, the English loanword ペースト is also used, especially among younger speakers.
