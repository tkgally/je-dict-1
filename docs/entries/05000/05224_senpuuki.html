扇風機(せんぷうき)

せんぷうき
noun
electric fan

Definitions

1. electric fan
An electrically powered device with rotating blades that creates airflow to cool a room or provide ventilation.

Examples

暑(あつ)いので扇風機(せんぷうき)をつけた。
It's hot, so I turned on the electric fan.
扇風機(せんぷうき)の風(かぜ)が気持(きも)ちいい。
The breeze from the fan feels nice.
夏(なつ)は扇風機(せんぷうき)とエアコンを併用(へいよう)している。
In summer, I use both the fan and air conditioner together.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 扇風機(せんぷうき)をつける/かける (to turn on a fan)
  • 扇風機(せんぷうき)を消(け)す (to turn off a fan)
  • 扇風機(せんぷうき)の風(かぜ) (the breeze from a fan)
  • 首振(くびふ)り扇風機(せんぷうき) (oscillating fan)

RELATED TERMS:

  • エアコン (air conditioner)
  • 換気扇(かんきせん) (ventilation fan)
  • 団扇(うちわ) (hand fan)
