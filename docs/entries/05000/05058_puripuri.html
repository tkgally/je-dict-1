ぷりぷり

ぷりぷり
adverb, verb (suru), na-adjective
angrily; plump and firm

Definitions

1. angrily, huffily
In an obviously angry or annoyed manner.
2. plump and firm
Describing a fresh, bouncy texture (often food like shrimp).

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)はぷりぷり怒(おこ)って出(で)て行(い)った。
She left in a huff.
ぷりぷりした海老(えび)が美味(おい)しい。
The plump shrimp is delicious.
ぷりぷりしないで、話(はな)を聞(き)いて。
Don't be huffy; listen to what I have to say.

Notes

Has two distinct meanings: 1) being visibly angry or annoyed (often cute/mild annoyance), and 2) describing fresh, bouncy texture in food. Context determines meaning.
