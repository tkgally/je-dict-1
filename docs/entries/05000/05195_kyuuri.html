胡瓜(きゅうり)

きゅうり
noun
cucumber

Definitions

1. cucumber
A long green vegetable commonly eaten raw or pickled.

Examples

胡瓜(きゅうり)を薄切(うすぎ)りにする。
To slice cucumber thinly.
胡瓜(きゅうり)の漬物(つけもの)が好(す)きだ。
I like pickled cucumber.
冷(つめ)たい胡瓜(きゅうり)は夏(なつ)に美味(おい)しい。
Cold cucumber is delicious in summer.

Notes

胡瓜(きゅうり) is commonly eaten raw or pickled in Japanese cuisine.

COMMON PREPARATIONS:

  • 漬物(つけもの): pickled cucumber
  • サラダ: in salads
  • 酢(す)の物(もの): vinegared dishes
  • かっぱ巻(ま)き: cucumber sushi roll

KANJI NOTE: Often written in hiragana (きゅうり). The kanji 胡瓜 literally means 'foreign gourd.'

CULTURE: Associated with kappa (河童), mythical creatures said to love cucumbers.
