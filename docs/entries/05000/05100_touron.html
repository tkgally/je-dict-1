1. debate, discussion
A formal discussion or argument on a particular topic, especially one in which opposing views are put forward.
討論(とうろん)が始(はじ)まった。
The debate started.
この問題(もんだい)について討論(とうろん)する。
To debate this issue.
選挙(せんきょ)前(まえ)にテレビで討論(とうろん)会(かい)が行(おこな)われた。
A televised debate was held before the election.
学生(がくせい)たちが環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)について熱心(ねっしん)に討論(とうろん)していた。
The students were passionately debating environmental issues.
国会(こっかい)では新(あたら)しい法案(ほうあん)について何時間(なんじかん)も討論(とうろん)が続(つづ)いたが、結論(けつろん)は出(で)なかった。
In the Diet, debate on the new bill continued for hours, but no conclusion was reached.

討論(とうろん) refers to a formal debate where opposing views are presented and examined. Composed of 討(とう) (attack/discuss) + 論(ろん) (argument).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 討論(とうろん)する: to debate
  • 討論(とうろん)会(かい): debate meeting, panel discussion
  • 討論(とうろん)を行(おこな)う: to hold a debate
  • 活発(かっぱつ)な討論(とうろん): lively debate
  • 公開(こうかい)討論(とうろん): public debate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん): discussion, argument (broader — can be informal or heated)
  • ディベート: debate (loanword, often used for formal competitive debate)
  • 論争(ろんそう): controversy, dispute (implies ongoing disagreement)
  • 審議(しんぎ): deliberation (formal, legislative)

REGISTER:
A written and formal-spoken word, at home in politics, journalism, and academic settings. In everyday conversation about disagreements, speakers reach for 議論(ぎろん) or 話(はな)し合(あ)い instead; 討論(とうろん) implies a structured exchange with sides.

Related Words

Synonym: 議論(ぎろん) (discussion, argument)
Related: 討論(とうろん)会(かい) (debate meeting, panel discussion)
Contrast: 論争(ろんそう) (controversy, dispute)
