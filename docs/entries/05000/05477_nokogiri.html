鋸(のこぎり)

のこぎり
noun
saw

Definitions

1. saw (tool)
A cutting tool with a toothed blade for cutting wood or other materials.

Examples

鋸(のこぎり)で木(き)を切(き)る。
To cut wood with a saw.
鋸(のこぎり)の刃(は)が錆(さ)びている。
The saw blade is rusty.
電動(でんどう)鋸(のこぎり)を使(つか)う。
To use an electric saw.

Notes

鋸(のこぎり) is a saw for cutting.

TYPES:

  • 手(て)鋸(のこぎり): handsaw
  • 電動(でんどう)鋸(のこぎり): electric saw
  • 糸(いと)鋸(のこぎり): coping saw

NOTE:
Japanese saws traditionally cut on the pull stroke, unlike Western saws.
