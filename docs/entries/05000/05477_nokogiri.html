鋸(のこぎり)

のこぎり
noun
saw
1. saw (tool)
A cutting tool with a toothed blade for cutting wood or other materials.
鋸(のこぎり)で木(き)を切(き)る。
To cut wood with a saw.
鋸(のこぎり)の刃(は)が錆(さ)びている。
The saw blade is rusty.
電動(でんどう)鋸(のこぎり)を使(つか)う。
To use an electric saw.

鋸(のこぎり) is a saw—a cutting tool with a toothed blade, used primarily for wood.

WRITING:
Often written in hiragana (のこぎり) as the kanji is rarely used in everyday writing.

TYPES:

  • 手(て)のこぎり/手(て)鋸(のこぎり): handsaw
  • 電動(でんどう)のこぎり: electric saw, power saw
  • 糸(いと)のこぎり: coping saw

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • のこぎりで切(き)る: to cut with a saw
  • のこぎりを使(つか)う: to use a saw
  • のこぎりの刃(は): saw blade

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese saws traditionally cut on the pull stroke (引(ひ)く), unlike Western saws that cut on the push stroke. This design allows for thinner, more precise blades.
