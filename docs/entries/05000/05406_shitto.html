嫉妬(しっと)

しっと
noun
jealousy; envy

Definitions

1. jealousy; envy
A feeling of resentment toward someone because of their advantages, possessions, or relationships.

Examples

嫉妬(しっと)に燃(も)えている。
I'm burning with jealousy.
彼女(かのじょ)の成功(せいこう)に嫉妬(しっと)した。
I felt jealous of her success.
嫉妬深(しっとぶか)い人(ひと)だ。
He's a jealous person.

Notes

嫉妬 often implies jealousy in romantic contexts but can also mean envy of others' success.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 嫉妬する: to be jealous (verb)
  • 嫉妬深い (しっとぶかい): jealous (personality)
  • 羨望 (せんぼう): envy (more literary)
  • やきもち: jealousy (colloquial, especially romantic)

NUANCE:
嫉妬 tends to be stronger and more negative than simply being envious.
