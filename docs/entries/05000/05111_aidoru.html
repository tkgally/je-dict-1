アイドル

あいどる
noun
idol (entertainer)
1. idol, pop star
A young, attractive entertainer who is marketed for their image and personality, typically performing as a singer or dancer. Japanese idols are often members of groups and cultivate close relationships with fans.
彼女(かのじょ)は人気(にんき)アイドルグループのメンバーだ。
She is a member of a popular idol group.
アイドルのコンサートに行(い)った。
I went to an idol concert.
アイドルを目指(めざ)している女(おんな)の子(こ)がたくさんいる。
There are many girls who aspire to become idols.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アイドルグループ (idol group)
  • アイドルを応援(おうえん)する (to support an idol)
  • 地下(ちか)アイドル (underground idol)
  • ご当地(とうち)アイドル (local/regional idol)
  • 推(お)しのアイドル (one's favorite idol)

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • Japanese idol culture emphasizes accessibility, purity, and fan engagement.
  • Idols often participate in handshake events (握手会(あくしゅかい)) and other fan meetings.
  • Major groups include AKB48, 乃木坂(のぎざか)46, etc.
