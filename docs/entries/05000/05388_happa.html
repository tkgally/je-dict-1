葉(は)っぱ

はっぱ
noun
leaf
1. leaf
The flat, green part of a plant. A casual/childish variant of 葉 (は).
葉(は)っぱが落(お)ちている。
Leaves have fallen.
子供(こども)が葉(は)っぱで遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing with leaves.
緑(みどり)の葉(は)っぱがきれいだ。
The green leaves are beautiful.

葉っぱ is a casual/colloquial word for leaf. The standard word is 葉 (は).

USAGE:

  • 葉っぱ: casual, often used by/with children
  • 葉 (は): standard/formal

RELATED:

  • 落ち葉 (おちば): fallen leaves
  • 青葉 (あおば): green leaves (spring/summer)
