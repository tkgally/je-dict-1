倫理(りんり)

りんり
noun
ethics
1. ethics, morals
A system of moral principles governing appropriate conduct; the study of right and wrong behavior.
医療(いりょう)倫理(りんり)について学(まな)ぶ。
To study medical ethics.
AIの倫理(りんり)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
AI ethics has become an issue.
企業(きぎょう)倫理(りんり)を守(まも)る。
To uphold business ethics.

倫理(りんり) refers to a system of moral principles that govern right and wrong conduct. Common in academic, medical, and business contexts.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 倫理(りんり)観(かん): ethical view, sense of ethics
  • 職業(しょくぎょう)倫理(りんり): professional ethics
  • 医療(いりょう)倫理(りんり): medical ethics
  • 企業(きぎょう)倫理(りんり): business/corporate ethics
  • 倫理(りんり)委員会(いいんかい): ethics committee
  • 倫理(りんり)的(てき): ethical (adjective)
  • 生命(せいめい)倫理(りんり): bioethics

CONTRAST:

  • 倫理(りんり): ethics (systematic principles, often institutional or professional)
  • 道徳(どうとく): morality, morals (more personal and everyday)
  • 良心(りょうしん): conscience (internal moral sense)

USAGE NOTE:
倫理(りんり) is a formal, academic word. In high school, 倫理(りんり) is also the name of a social studies subject covering philosophy and ethics.

Related Words

Related: 正義(せいぎ) (justice)
Synonym: 道徳(どうとく) (morality (personal))
Related: 良心(りょうしん) (conscience)
