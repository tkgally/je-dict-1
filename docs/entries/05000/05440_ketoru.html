ケトル

けとる
noun
kettle

Definitions

1. kettle
A container for boiling water, often electric.

Examples

ケトルでお湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす。
To boil water in a kettle.
電気(でんき)ケトルは便利(べんり)だ。
Electric kettles are convenient.
ケトルが沸(わ)いた。
The kettle has boiled.

Notes

ケトル is a loanword from English "kettle."

TYPES:

  • 電気(でんき)ケトル: electric kettle (most common in Japan)
  • やかん: traditional stovetop kettle

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ケトルでお湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす: to boil water in a kettle
  • ケトルが沸(わ)く: the kettle boils

Related Words

Related: ポット (pot/thermos)
