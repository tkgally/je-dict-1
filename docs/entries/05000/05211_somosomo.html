そもそも

そもそも
adverb
in the first place; to begin with; originally
1. in the first place; to begin with
Used to question or refer back to fundamental premises or origins of a matter.
そもそも、なぜそんなことをしたの？
Why did you do such a thing in the first place?
そもそもこの計画(けいかく)には無理(むり)があった。
This plan was unreasonable from the start.
そもそも彼(かれ)は来(く)る気(き)がなかった。
He had no intention of coming in the first place.

そもそも is used to go back to basics or question fundamental assumptions.

KANJI: Can be written as 抑(そもそ)も or 抑々(そもそも), though hiragana is standard.

USAGE:

  • Often used to point out a fundamental problem
  • Can express criticism or skepticism
  • Common in logical arguments and debates

SIMILAR:

  • 元々(もともと): originally (more neutral)
  • 本来(ほんらい): originally, by nature
