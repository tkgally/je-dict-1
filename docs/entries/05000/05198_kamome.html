かもめ

かもめ
noun
seagull
1. seagull; gull
A white and gray seabird commonly seen at coasts and harbors.
かもめが海(うみ)の上(うえ)を飛(と)んでいる。
Seagulls are flying over the sea.
港(みなと)にはたくさんのかもめがいる。
There are many seagulls at the harbor.
かもめに餌(えさ)をやった。
I fed the seagulls.

かもめ is a common seabird often seen at Japanese coastal areas.

KANJI: Can be written as 鷗(かもめ), though hiragana is more common.

LITERARY: Seagulls often appear in Japanese poetry and songs as symbols of the sea and freedom.

TRAIN: The 特急(とっきゅう)かもめ is a limited express train in Kyushu named after the seagull.
