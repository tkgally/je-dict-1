山々(やまやま)

やまやま
noun, adverb
mountains; greatly

Definitions

1. mountains, many mountains
Multiple mountains; a range of mountains.
2. greatly, very much (wanting to)
Used to express strong desire, often followed by 'but...' to indicate constraint.

Examples

遠(とお)くに山々(やまやま)が見(み)えます。
You can see mountains in the distance.
行(い)きたいのは山々(やまやま)ですが、時間(じかん)がありません。
I'd love to go, but I don't have time.
山々(やまやま)に囲(かこ)まれた村(むら)。
A village surrounded by mountains.

Notes

Has two distinct uses: 1) literal plural of 山(やま), and 2) adverbial expression meaning 'very much' (often with ～のは山々(やまやま)だが... pattern expressing that one wants to do something but can't).
