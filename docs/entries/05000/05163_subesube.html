すべすべ

すべすべ
adverb
smooth (to the touch); silky

Definitions

1. smooth; silky
Describes a surface that is pleasantly smooth to the touch, like skin after moisturizing or polished wood.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんの肌(はだ)はすべすべだ。
A baby's skin is smooth.
このクリームを塗(ぬ)ると肌(はだ)がすべすべになる。
When you apply this cream, your skin becomes smooth.
すべすべした生地(きじ)のシャツが好(す)きだ。
I like shirts made of smooth fabric.

Notes

すべすべ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語) expressing a pleasant smooth texture.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • すべすべの肌(はだ): smooth skin
  • すべすべになる: to become smooth
  • すべすべした〜: smooth (adj. modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • つるつる: smooth and slippery (can be difficult to grip)
  • さらさら: smooth and flowing (like hair or powder)
