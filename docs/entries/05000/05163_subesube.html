すべすべ

すべすべ
adverb
smooth (to the touch); silky
1. smooth; silky
Describes a surface that is pleasantly smooth to the touch, like skin after moisturizing or polished wood.
赤(あか)ちゃんの肌(はだ)はすべすべだ。
A baby's skin is smooth.
このクリームを塗(ぬ)ると肌(はだ)がすべすべになる。
When you apply this cream, your skin becomes smooth.
すべすべした生地(きじ)のシャツが好(す)きだ。
I like shirts made of smooth fabric.

すべすべ is an onomatopoeic word (擬態語(ぎたいご)) expressing a pleasant smooth texture. It always carries a positive connotation, describing surfaces that feel good to touch.

USAGE:

すべすべ functions as a na-adjective and can also be used with する:

  • すべすべな肌(はだ) / すべすべの肌(はだ): smooth skin
  • すべすべした生地(きじ): smooth fabric (した form as modifier)
  • すべすべする: to feel smooth
  • すべすべになる: to become smooth

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

Most often used to describe skin or fabric:

  • 肌(はだ)がすべすべ: skin is smooth
  • すべすべの手触(てざわ)り: smooth feel/texture

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • つるつる: smooth and slippery (can imply difficulty gripping; also used for bald heads)
  • さらさら: smooth and flowing (like dry hair or fine powder)
  • すべすべ focuses specifically on pleasant tactile smoothness

Related Words
