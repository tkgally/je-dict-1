がんがん

がんがん
adverb
pounding; throbbing; vigorously
1. pounding; throbbing
Describes a pounding sensation, especially headaches or loud repetitive sounds.
頭(あたま)ががんがんする。
My head is pounding.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)からがんがん音楽(おんがく)が聞(き)こえる。
Loud music is blaring from the next room.
2. vigorously; intensively
Describes doing something with great intensity or energy.
がんがん勉強(べんきょう)して試験(しけん)に受(う)かった。
I studied intensively and passed the exam.

がんがん is an onomatopoeic word mimicking pounding or banging sounds.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 頭(あたま)ががんがんする: to have a pounding headache
  • がんがん働(はたら)く: to work intensively
  • がんがん鳴(な)る: to ring/pound loudly

SIMILAR PAIN WORDS:

  • ずきずき: throbbing (pain)
  • きりきり: sharp, piercing (pain)
  • がんがん is typically used for headaches with a pounding quality
