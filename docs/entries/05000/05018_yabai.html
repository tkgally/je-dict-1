やばい

やばい
adjective (i-adjective)
amazing; terrible; risky

Definitions

1. amazing, awesome
Slang use expressing strong positive emotion about something impressive.
2. terrible, bad
Original meaning indicating a dangerous or problematic situation.
3. risky, dangerous
Describing something potentially harmful or illegal.

Examples

このラーメン、やばい！超(ちょう)美味(おい)しい！
This ramen is amazing! So delicious!
やばい、電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)り遅(おく)れそう！
Oh no, I'm going to miss the train!
あの場所(ばしょ)はやばいから行(い)かない方(ほう)がいい。
That place is sketchy, so you shouldn't go there.

Notes

Originally meant 'dangerous/risky' but evolved among youth to mean 'amazing/cool'. Context determines meaning. Very informal. The positive sense became dominant in the 2000s. Common variations: やべえ (masculine), ヤバい (katakana for emphasis). One of the most versatile slang words.
