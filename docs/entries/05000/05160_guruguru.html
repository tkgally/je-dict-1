ぐるぐる

ぐるぐる
adverb
spinning; whirling; round and round
1. spinning; whirling around
Describes a continuous rotating or circular motion, or something wrapped around repeatedly.
目(め)がぐるぐる回(まわ)っている。
My head is spinning. (lit. My eyes are spinning.)
Used for dizziness
ロープをぐるぐる巻(ま)いた。
I wound the rope round and round.
犬(いぬ)が尻尾(しっぽ)をぐるぐる振(ふ)っている。
The dog is wagging its tail in circles.

ぐるぐる is an onomatopoeic word (擬態{語(ご)|ぎたいご}) describing continuous circular motion.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぐるぐる回(まわ)る: to spin around
  • ぐるぐる巻(ま)く: to wrap around repeatedly
  • 頭(あたま)がぐるぐるする: to feel dizzy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • くるくる: spinning, whirling (lighter, faster motion)
  • ぐらぐら: wobbling, unsteady
