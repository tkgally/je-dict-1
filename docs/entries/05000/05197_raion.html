ライオン

らいおん
noun
lion

Definitions

1. lion
A large wild cat native to Africa, known as the 'king of beasts.'

Examples

ライオンは百獣(ひゃくじゅう)の王(おう)だ。
The lion is the king of beasts.
動物園(どうぶつえん)でライオンを見(み)た。
I saw lions at the zoo.
ライオンが吠(ほ)えている。
The lion is roaring.

Notes

ライオン is known as the 'king of beasts' in Japanese too.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 雄(おす)ライオン: male lion
  • 雌(めす)ライオン: female lion (lioness)
  • たてがみ: mane
  • 群(む)れ: pride (group of lions)

KANJI: Can be written as 獅子 ({しし}), though this is more literary/classical.
