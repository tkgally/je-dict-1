さっと

さっと
adverb
quickly; swiftly; lightly

Definitions

1. quickly; swiftly; in one smooth motion
Describes a quick, smooth action performed without hesitation.
2. lightly; briefly
Describes doing something in a quick, light manner.

Examples

さっと手(て)を洗(あら)った。
I quickly washed my hands.
野菜(やさい)をさっと炒(いた)める。
Stir-fry the vegetables quickly.
彼(かれ)はさっと立(た)ち上(あ)がった。
He stood up swiftly.

Notes

さっと expresses quick, smooth, effortless motion. It has a clean, crisp feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • さっと読む: to skim through, to read quickly
  • さっと済ませる: to finish quickly
  • さっと炒める: to stir-fry briefly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぱっと: suddenly, in a flash (more sudden/bright)
  • すっと: smoothly, quietly (more gentle)
