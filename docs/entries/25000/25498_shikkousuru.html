1. to execute; to carry out
To implement or put into effect a plan, decision, duty, or order.
予算(よさん)を執行(しっこう)する。
To execute the budget.
計画(けいかく)どおりに業務(ぎょうむ)を執行(しっこう)した。
The duties were carried out according to plan.
市長(しちょう)は公務(こうむ)を厳正(げんせい)に執行(しっこう)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
The mayor has an obligation to carry out public duties impartially.
2. to enforce; to execute (a legal judgment)
To carry out a court ruling, sentence, or legal order, including capital punishment.
判決(はんけつ)が執行(しっこう)された。
The sentence was carried out.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)は差(さ)し押(おさ)さえを執行(しっこう)した。
The court enforced the seizure of assets.
死刑(しけい)が執行(しっこう)されたというニュースに衝撃(しょうげき)を受(う)けた。
I was shocked by the news that the death sentence had been carried out.

Composed of 執行(しっこう) (execution; enforcement) + する. A formal, administrative word often seen in government and legal contexts. The noun 執行(しっこう) alone frequently appears in compound terms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予算(よさん)を執行(しっこう)する: to execute a budget
  • 刑(けい)を執行(しっこう)する: to carry out a sentence
  • 強制(きょうせい)執行(しっこう): compulsory enforcement (of a court order)
  • 執行(しっこう)猶予(ゆうよ): suspended sentence
  • 執行(しっこう)役員(やくいん): executive officer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実行(じっこう)する: to carry out; to put into practice — more general; used for plans, ideas, and personal actions
  • 施行(しこう)する: to enforce; to put into effect — specifically for laws and regulations taking effect
  • 遂行(すいこう)する: to accomplish; to carry through — emphasizes completing a mission or duty
