1. to record; to document; to write down
To preserve information by writing it down, filming it, or storing it in some form for future reference.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)を記録(きろく)した。
I recorded the contents of the meeting.
毎日(まいにち)の体重(たいじゅう)をノートに記録(きろく)している。
I'm recording my daily weight in a notebook.
戦争(せんそう)の体験(たいけん)を映像(えいぞう)で記録(きろく)し、後世(こうせい)に伝(つた)えることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to record wartime experiences on film and pass them on to future generations.
2. to set a record; to register a figure
To achieve or register a particular number, score, or measurement, especially a notable one.
最高(さいこう)気温(きおん)を記録(きろく)した。
A record high temperature was registered.
売上(うりあげ)が過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)を記録(きろく)した。
Sales registered an all-time high.
今年(ことし)の夏(なつ)は観測(かんそく)史上(しじょう)最(もっと)も暑(あつ)い気温(きおん)を記録(きろく)し、各地(かくち)で熱中症(ねっちゅうしょう)の患者(かんじゃ)が増(ふ)えた。
This summer registered the hottest temperatures on record, and heatstroke patients increased across the country.

A versatile suru-verb with two distinct uses. Sense 1 (to document) covers any act of preserving information — writing notes, filming, logging data, etc. Sense 2 (to register a figure) is commonly seen in news about weather records, economic figures, and sports achievements.

The noun form 記録(きろく) means "record" or "documentation" and appears in many compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記録(きろく)に残(のこ)す: to leave on record
  • 記録(きろく)を更新(こうしん)する: to break/update a record
  • 記録(きろく)を保持(ほじ)する: to hold a record
  • 映像(えいぞう)に記録(きろく)する: to record on video
  • 世界(せかい)記録(きろく): world record
  • 記録(きろく)的(てき)な: record-breaking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 録音(ろくおん)する: to record (audio) — specifically for sound recording
  • 録画(ろくが)する: to record (video) — specifically for video recording
  • 記載(きさい)する: to describe; to state in writing — formal, used for official documents
