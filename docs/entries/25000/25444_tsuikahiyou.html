追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)

ついかひよう
noun
additional cost; extra charge
1. additional cost; extra charge; supplementary expense
A cost that is added on top of the base price or original budget.
追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)はかかりません。
There is no additional cost.
追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)が発生(はっせい)する場合(ばあい)があります。
Additional costs may arise.
工事(こうじ)の途中(とちゅう)で設計(せっけい)変更(へんこう)があり、追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)が大幅(おおはば)に膨(ふく)らんだ。
There were design changes midway through construction, and the additional costs ballooned significantly.

A compound of 追加(ついか) (addition) and 費用(ひよう) (cost, expense). Frequently encountered in business contracts, service agreements, travel bookings, and construction projects.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)が発生(はっせい)する: additional costs arise
  • 追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)なし: no extra charge
  • 追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)を請求(せいきゅう)する: to charge an additional fee
  • 追加(ついか)費用(ひよう)を負担(ふたん)する: to bear the additional cost

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追加(ついか)料金(りょうきん): surcharge; extra fee — specifically for fees and service charges
  • 別途(べっと)費用(ひよう): separate cost — implies a cost that is billed independently
  • 経費(けいひ): expenses — general term for costs incurred in business or operations
