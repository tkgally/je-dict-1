1. to imitate; to mimic; to copy
To reproduce or closely follow the style, manner, or actions of someone or something, often with the aim of replicating it.
先生(せんせい)の技(わざ)を模倣(もほう)する。
To imitate the teacher's technique.
子(こ)どもは大人(おとな)の行動(こうどう)を模倣(もほう)して学(まな)ぶ。
Children learn by imitating adults' behavior.
他社(たしゃ)の成功(せいこう)モデルを模倣(もほう)するだけでは、長期的(ちょうきてき)な成長(せいちょう)は難(むずか)しい。
Just imitating another company's success model won't lead to long-term growth.
有名(ゆうめい)ブランドの模倣品(もほうひん)が市場(しじょう)に出回(でまわ)っている。
Counterfeit goods imitating famous brands are circulating in the market.

A formal, somewhat academic word. Can be neutral (learning through imitation) or negative (unauthorized copying). In academic and artistic contexts, it's a standard term for discussing how works or behaviors are replicated.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 模倣品(もほうひん): imitation product; counterfeit
  • 模倣(もほう)犯罪(はんざい): copycat crime
  • 模倣(もほう)行為(こうい): act of imitation
  • 自然(しぜん)を模倣(もほう)する: to imitate nature
  • 模倣(もほう)から創造(そうぞう)へ: from imitation to creation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 真似(まね)する: to imitate, to copy — everyday word; can be playful or critical
  • 物真似(ものまね): impersonation — specifically imitating a person for entertainment
  • 模写(もしゃ)する: to copy (artwork) — specifically reproducing visual art
  • 偽造(ぎぞう)する: to forge, to counterfeit — criminal connotation, creating fakes with intent to deceive
