1. to stay still; to remain motionless; to keep quiet
To hold one's body still without moving, often requiring patience or self-control.
じっとしていてね。
Stay still, okay?
子(こ)どもにじっとしていろと言(い)っても無理(むり)だ。
It's impossible to tell children to stay still.
痛(いた)みをじっと我慢(がまん)して、治療(ちりょう)が終(お)わるのを待(ま)った。
I endured the pain without moving and waited for the treatment to finish.
2. to stare; to gaze fixedly
To look at something or someone steadily and intently without looking away.
彼女(かのじょ)をじっと見(み)た。
I stared at her fixedly.
猫(ねこ)が窓(まど)の外(そと)をじっと見(み)ている。
The cat is gazing fixedly out the window.
彼(かれ)は何(なに)も言(い)わず、写真(しゃしん)をじっと見(み)つめていた。
He said nothing and stared intently at the photograph.

An onomatopoeic suru-verb where じっと expresses the idea of being fixed in place or focused without movement. In sense 1, it describes physical stillness — holding one's body motionless. In sense 2, it describes visual fixation — staring without looking away. Both senses share the core idea of sustained, unbroken focus or immobility.

じっと can also function as an adverb without する: じっと待(ま)つ (to wait patiently without moving), じっと耐(た)える (to endure without flinching).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • じっとしている: to be staying still
  • じっと見(み)る: to stare at
  • じっと見(み)つめる: to gaze at intently
  • じっと待(ま)つ: to wait patiently
  • じっと我慢(がまん)する: to endure without moving
  • じっとしていられない: to be unable to stay still

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 動(うご)かない: to not move — neutral description of absence of movement, without the nuance of effort or intent
  • 黙(だま)っている: to be silent — focuses on not speaking rather than not moving
  • 凝視(ぎょうし)する: to stare; to gaze — formal/literary equivalent of the staring sense
