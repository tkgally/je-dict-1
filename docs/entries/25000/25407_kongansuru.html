1. to implore; to entreat; to plead earnestly
To earnestly and desperately beg someone for something, often with deep emotion or humility. A stronger and more desperate form of asking than ordinary requests.
命(いのち)だけは助(たす)けてほしいと懇願(こんがん)した。
He implored them to spare his life.
母親(ははおや)は涙(なみだ)ながらに医師(いし)に懇願(こんがん)した。
The mother pleaded with the doctor through her tears.
何度(なんど)も懇願(こんがん)した結果(けっか)、ようやく許可(きょか)が下(お)りた。
After pleading many times, permission was finally granted.

A formal, literary word conveying deep emotional pleading. Implies that the speaker is in a position of need and is asking with great earnestness and humility.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 必死(ひっし)に懇願(こんがん)する: to plead desperately
  • 涙(なみだ)ながらに懇願(こんがん)する: to implore through tears
  • 懇願(こんがん)の目(め): pleading eyes
  • 懇願(こんがん)を聞(き)き入(い)れる: to grant someone's plea

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頼(たの)む: to ask, to request — everyday and versatile, without the desperate emotional weight of 懇願(こんがん)
  • 嘆願(たんがん)する: to petition, to appeal — more formal and often used for official petitions to authorities, while 懇願(こんがん) is more personal and emotional
  • 哀願(あいがん)する: to beg pitifully — similar intensity but emphasizes evoking pity, while 懇願(こんがん) emphasizes earnestness and sincerity
