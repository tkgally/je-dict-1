1. to register; to sign up; to enroll
to register; to sign up; to enroll
サイトに会員(かいいん)登録(とうろく)する。
I register as a member on the site.
新(あたら)しいアドレスを登録(とうろく)してください。
Please register your new address.
商標(しょうひょう)を特許庁(とっきょちょう)に登録(とうろく)する手続(てつづ)きを進(すす)めた。
I proceeded with the process of registering the trademark with the Patent Office.
アプリをダウンロードしたら、まず個人(こじん)情報(じょうほう)を登録(とうろく)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
After downloading the app, you first need to register your personal information.

Refers to formally recording information in an official or organized system. Used for both official registrations (trademark, residency, vehicle) and everyday digital contexts (creating accounts, signing up for services).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会員(かいいん)登録(とうろく): member registration
  • 住所(じゅうしょ)を登録(とうろく)する: register an address
  • 登録(とうろく)番号(ばんごう): registration number
  • 商標(しょうひょう)登録(とうろく): trademark registration
  • ユーザー登録(とうろく): user registration
  • 登録(とうろく)済(ず)み: registered; already registered

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 届(とど)け出(で)る: to notify; to file (a report) — used for submitting official notices to government offices, such as a marriage registration or change of address
  • 申請(しんせい)する: to apply — implies requesting approval or permission, while 登録(とうろく) simply records information
  • 記録(きろく)する: to record — focuses on keeping a record of information or events, not necessarily in an official registry
