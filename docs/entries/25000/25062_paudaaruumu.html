パウダールーム

ぱうだーるーむ
noun
powder room; women's restroom; vanity area
1. powder room; women's restroom; vanity area
a restroom or vanity area, typically in department stores, hotels, or commercial facilities, designed for women to freshen up
パウダールームはどこですか。
Where is the powder room?
パウダールームで化粧(けしょう)を直(なお)した。
I touched up my makeup in the powder room.
このデパートのパウダールームは広(ひろ)くてきれいだと評判(ひょうばん)だ。
The powder room in this department store has a reputation for being spacious and clean.

Refers to a designated area, usually in department stores, hotels, or commercial buildings, where women can fix their hair, apply makeup, or freshen up. Unlike a standard restroom, a powder room typically includes mirrors, counter space, and seating.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パウダールームで化粧(けしょう)を直(なお)す: to fix one's makeup in the powder room
  • パウダールームを利用(りよう)する: to use the powder room
  • 完備(かんび)されたパウダールーム: a fully equipped powder room

USAGE:
In Japanese, パウダールーム is not a euphemism for a toilet the way "powder room" sometimes is in English. It specifically refers to the makeup and grooming area, which is often a separate space from the toilet stalls in Japanese department stores and commercial facilities. The term 化粧室(けしょうしつ) is a closer equivalent to the English euphemistic use of "powder room" meaning restroom.
