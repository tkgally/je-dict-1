サービス業(ぎょう)

さーびすぎょう
noun
service industry; service sector
1. service industry; service sector
the sector of the economy that provides services rather than manufactured goods
サービス業(ぎょう)で働(はたら)いている。
I work in the service industry.
サービス業(ぎょう)は休日(きゅうじつ)が不規則(ふきそく)になりがちだ。
In the service industry, days off tend to be irregular.
日本(にほん)の経済(けいざい)はサービス業(ぎょう)を中心(ちゅうしん)に成長(せいちょう)してきた。
Japan's economy has grown centering on the service industry.

Composed of サービス (service) + 業(ぎょう) (industry, business). Covers a wide range of businesses including hospitality, retail, food service, healthcare, and entertainment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • サービス業(ぎょう)に従事(じゅうじ)する: to be engaged in the service industry
  • サービス業(ぎょう)で働(はたら)く: to work in the service industry
  • サービス業(ぎょう)の人手不足(ひとでぶそく): labor shortage in the service sector
  • サービス業(ぎょう)全般(ぜんぱん): service industry as a whole

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 接客業(せっきゃくぎょう): customer-facing work — specifically jobs involving direct interaction with customers
  • 製造業(せいぞうぎょう): manufacturing industry — produces physical goods, in contrast to services
  • 第三次産業(だいさんじさんぎょう): tertiary industry — the economic classification that includes services

NOTE: In Japan, サービス業(ぎょう) workers often work weekends and holidays since those are peak customer times. This is a common topic in discussions about work-life balance.
