1. conception; pregnancy; becoming pregnant
王妃(おうひ)が懐妊(かいにん)された。
The queen has become pregnant.
懐妊(かいにん)の知(し)らせを受(う)けて、家族(かぞく)は大(おお)いに喜(よろこ)んだ。
Upon receiving the news of the pregnancy, the family was overjoyed.
宮内庁(くないちょう)は皇室(こうしつ)の懐妊(かいにん)を正式(せいしき)に発表(はっぴょう)した。
The Imperial Household Agency officially announced the imperial pregnancy.

A formal, respectful term for pregnancy. Composed of 懐(かい) (bosom, to carry) + 妊(にん) (pregnancy). Primarily used in formal announcements, historical texts, and when speaking about royalty or the imperial family. Not used in everyday medical or casual contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懐妊(かいにん)の報(ほう): news of pregnancy
  • 懐妊(かいにん)を発表(はっぴょう)する: to announce a pregnancy
  • ご懐妊(かいにん): pregnancy (honorific)

USAGE:
Most commonly seen in news about the Japanese imperial family or in historical/literary contexts. The respectful prefix ご is almost always attached when referring to royalty: ご懐妊(かいにん).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 妊娠(にんしん): pregnancy — the standard, everyday medical term
  • 身(み)ごもる: to conceive, to become pregnant — a native Japanese word, somewhat literary
  • おめでた: pregnancy — informal, euphemistic, used when congratulating
