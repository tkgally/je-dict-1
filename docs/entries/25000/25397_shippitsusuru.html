1. to write; to author; to pen
To write literary works, articles, or other texts as an author. Implies serious, professional, or scholarly writing rather than casual everyday writing.
小説(しょうせつ)を執筆(しっぴつ)する。
To write a novel.
彼女(かのじょ)は現在(げんざい)、新(あたら)しい論文(ろんぶん)を執筆(しっぴつ)中(ちゅう)だ。
She is currently writing a new academic paper.
この教科書(きょうかしょ)は大学(だいがく)の教授(きょうじゅ)たちが共同(きょうどう)で執筆(しっぴつ)したものだ。
This textbook was co-authored by university professors.

A formal word for writing that implies authorship of substantial works. Not used for casual writing like notes, text messages, or shopping lists.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 執筆(しっぴつ)中(ちゅう): currently writing, in the process of writing
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)活動(かつどう): writing activities
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)者(しゃ): writer, author
  • 共同(きょうどう)執筆(しっぴつ): co-authoring
  • 執筆(しっぴつ)依頼(いらい): writing commission/request

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)く: to write — the most general and everyday word for writing, applicable to anything from notes to novels
  • 著(あらわ)す: to author, to publish — emphasizes the act of producing a published work, while 執筆(しっぴつ) focuses on the process of writing itself
  • 記(しる)す: to write down, to record — focuses on documenting or recording information, rather than creative or scholarly composition
