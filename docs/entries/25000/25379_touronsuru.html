1. to debate; to discuss formally; to argue a point
To engage in a structured exchange of differing opinions on a topic, typically with the aim of reaching a conclusion or persuading others.
この問題(もんだい)について討論(とうろん)する。
To debate this issue.
国会(こっかい)で法案(ほうあん)について討論(とうろん)が行(おこな)われた。
A debate on the bill was held in the Diet.
学生(がくせい)たちは環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)について活発(かっぱつ)に討論(とうろん)した。
The students debated environmental issues vigorously.
賛成派(さんせいは)と反対派(はんたいは)が討論(とうろん)番組(ばんぐみ)で対決(たいけつ)した。
The proponents and opponents faced off on a debate program.

Implies a formal, structured exchange with opposing viewpoints. More confrontational and formal than 議論(ぎろん), which can be more collaborative. Often associated with parliamentary debate, academic discussion, and TV debate shows.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 討論会(とうろんかい): debate meeting, forum
  • 討論(とうろん)番組(ばんぐみ): debate program
  • 党首(とうしゅ)討論(とうろん): party leaders' debate
  • 活発(かっぱつ)な討論(とうろん): lively debate
  • 公開(こうかい)討論(とうろん): public debate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)する: to discuss, to argue — broader; can be collaborative discussion or heated argument
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)う: to talk it over — implies cooperative discussion to reach agreement
  • 論争(ろんそう)する: to dispute, to be in controversy — implies prolonged, unresolved disagreement
