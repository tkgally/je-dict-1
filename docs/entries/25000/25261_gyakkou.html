1. going backwards; moving against the flow; retrogression
moving in the opposite direction from what is expected or normal, either physically or figuratively
時代(じだい)に逆行(ぎゃっこう)する政策(せいさく)だ。
It's a policy that goes against the times.
一方通行(いっぽうつうこう)の道(みち)を逆行(ぎゃっこう)してしまった。
I accidentally drove the wrong way down a one-way street.
この改正(かいせい)は民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)への逆行(ぎゃっこう)だと批判(ひはん)された。
This revision was criticized as a step backwards for democracy.
水星(すいせい)が逆行(ぎゃっこう)する時期(じき)はトラブルが多(おお)いと言(い)われている。
The period when Mercury is in retrograde is said to bring many troubles.

Describes movement in the opposite or wrong direction, whether physical (driving the wrong way) or figurative (going against progress or trends). The figurative usage is particularly common in news and political discourse.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時代(じだい)に逆行(ぎゃっこう)する: to go against the times
  • 流(なが)れに逆行(ぎゃっこう)する: to go against the current/trend
  • 逆行(ぎゃっこう)運転(うんてん): driving the wrong way
  • 水星(すいせい)の逆行(ぎゃっこう): Mercury retrograde

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 後退(こうたい): retreat or regression; emphasizes moving backward from a position, while 逆行(ぎゃっこう) emphasizes going against the expected direction
  • 反する(はんする): to be contrary to; describes opposition to rules or principles rather than direction of movement

ETYMOLOGY:
From 逆(ぎゃく) (reverse, opposite) + 行(こう) (going, movement).
