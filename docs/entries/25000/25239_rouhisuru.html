1. to waste; to squander; to be extravagant with
to waste; to squander; to be extravagant with
お金(かね)を浪費(ろうひ)してはいけない。
You shouldn't waste money.
彼(かれ)は給料(きゅうりょう)を一晩(ひとばん)で浪費(ろうひ)した。
He squandered his salary in one night.
貴重(きちょう)な時間(じかん)を浪費(ろうひ)するのはもったいない。
It's a shame to waste precious time.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は才能(さいのう)を浪費(ろうひ)していたと、今(いま)になって後悔(こうかい)している。
I now regret having wasted my talent when I was young.

Describes using money, time, energy, or other resources carelessly and without value. Always carries a strongly negative nuance — the spending is seen as wasteful and irresponsible.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お金(かね)を浪費(ろうひ)する: waste money
  • 時間(じかん)を浪費(ろうひ)する: waste time
  • 浪費(ろうひ)癖(ぐせ): habit of wasteful spending
  • 浪費(ろうひ)家(か): spendthrift; big spender
  • 資源(しげん)を浪費(ろうひ)する: waste resources

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無駄遣(むだづか)いする: to waste (money) — a more colloquial term, often used for everyday overspending
  • 節約(せつやく)する: to economize — the antonym, meaning to save or cut back
  • 消費(しょうひ)する: to consume — neutral term for using up resources, without the negative nuance of wastefulness
  • 散財(さんざい)する: to splurge — implies spending a lot of money, but can be used either negatively or self-deprecatingly
