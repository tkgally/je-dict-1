2. word coinage; the act of creating new words
word coinage; the act of creating new words
明治(めいじ)時代(じだい)には西洋(せいよう)の概念(がいねん)を表(あらわ)すために多(おお)くの漢語(かんご)が造語(ぞうご)された。
In the Meiji era, many Sino-Japanese words were coined to express Western concepts.
その作家(さっか)は独自(どくじ)の造語(ぞうご)を作品(さくひん)に多用(たよう)する。
That author frequently uses self-coined words in their works.
科学(かがく)分野(ぶんや)では新(あたら)しい発見(はっけん)のたびに造語(ぞうご)が必要(ひつよう)になる。
In scientific fields, coining new words becomes necessary with each new discovery.

Can refer both to the resulting new word and to the process of creating it. Japanese is highly productive in word coinage, particularly through combining kanji compounds, abbreviation of katakana words, and blending. Many words that are now standard Japanese were originally 造語(ぞうご) from the Meiji era.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 造語(ぞうご)を作(つく)る: create a coined word
  • 造語(ぞうご)力(りょく): word-coining ability/productivity
  • 和製(わせい)造語(ぞうご): Japanese-made coined word

RELATED TERMS:

  • 新語(しんご): new word — any word recently entering use, not necessarily coined
  • 略語(りゃくご): abbreviation — a shortened form of an existing word
  • 和製英語(わせいえいご): Japanese-made English — English-sounding words coined in Japanese
