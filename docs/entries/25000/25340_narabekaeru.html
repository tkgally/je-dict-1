1. to rearrange; to reorder; to sort
To change the order or arrangement of items, putting them in a different sequence or configuration.
名前(なまえ)をあいうえお順(じゅん)に並(なら)べ替(か)えた。
I rearranged the names in alphabetical order.
家具(かぐ)を並(なら)べ替(か)えて部屋(へや)の模様替(もようが)えをした。
I rearranged the furniture and redecorated the room.
優先度(ゆうせんど)の高(たか)い順(じゅん)にタスクを並(なら)べ替(か)えて、効率(こうりつ)よく仕事(しごと)を進(すす)めよう。
Let's rearrange the tasks in order of priority and work efficiently.

A compound verb formed from 並(なら)べる (to line up, to arrange) + 替(か)える (to change, to replace). The combination means to change the existing arrangement into a new one. Very common in both physical contexts (rearranging objects) and digital contexts (sorting lists, reordering data).

This is an ichidan verb that conjugates like 替(か)える.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 順番(じゅんばん)を並(なら)べ替(か)える: to change the order
  • あいうえお順(じゅん)に並(なら)べ替(か)える: to sort in Japanese alphabetical order
  • 日付(ひづけ)順(じゅん)に並(なら)べ替(か)える: to sort by date
  • 大(おお)きさ順(じゅん)に並(なら)べ替(か)える: to sort by size

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 並(なら)べる: to line up; to arrange — placing items in order, without the nuance of rearranging
  • 整理(せいり)する: to organize; to tidy — broader, includes discarding and categorizing
  • ソートする: to sort — loanword used mainly in computing and data contexts
