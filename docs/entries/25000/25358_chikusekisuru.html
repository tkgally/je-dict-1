1. to accumulate; to store up; to build up
To gradually collect and store something over time, whether tangible (wealth, data) or intangible (experience, fatigue).
疲労(ひろう)が蓄積(ちくせき)している。
Fatigue has been building up.
長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)を蓄積(ちくせき)してきた。
I have accumulated years of experience.
データを蓄積(ちくせき)して分析(ぶんせき)することで、新(あたら)しい知見(ちけん)が得(え)られる。
By accumulating and analyzing data, new insights can be obtained.
ストレスを蓄積(ちくせき)させないように、定期的(ていきてき)に休息(きゅうそく)を取(と)ろう。
Let's take regular breaks so stress doesn't build up.

Emphasizes gradual, layered accumulation over time. Used in both positive contexts (knowledge, experience, wealth) and negative ones (fatigue, stress, waste). More formal than 溜(た)まる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 疲労(ひろう)の蓄積(ちくせき): accumulation of fatigue
  • データの蓄積(ちくせき): accumulation of data
  • 知識(ちしき)を蓄積(ちくせき)する: to build up knowledge
  • 資本(しほん)蓄積(ちくせき): capital accumulation
  • 蓄積(ちくせき)された経験(けいけん): accumulated experience

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 溜(た)まる: to accumulate, to pile up — more casual, often implies unwanted buildup
  • 貯(た)める: to save up — focuses on deliberate saving, especially money
  • 積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる: to pile up — emphasizes the layering process; often used for efforts or experiences
