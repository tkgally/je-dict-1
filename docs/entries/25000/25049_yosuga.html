よすが

よすが
noun
means of support; connection; link; something to rely on; memento; keepsake; reminder (of a person or time)
1. means of support; connection; link; something to rely on
友人(ゆうじん)が心(こころ)のよすがだった。
My friend was my emotional support.
生(い)きるよすがを失(うしな)った。
I lost my reason for living.
音楽(おんがく)だけが孤独(こどく)な日々(ひび)のよすがだった。
Music alone was my solace during lonely days.
2. memento; keepsake; reminder (of a person or time)
亡(な)き母(はは)のよすがの品(しな)。
A memento of my late mother.
この手紙(てがみ)は青春(せいしゅん)時代(じだい)のよすがである。
This letter is a keepsake from my youth.
古(ふる)い写真(しゃしん)を眺(なが)めて、過(す)ぎ去(さ)った日々(ひび)のよすがとした。
I gazed at old photographs, using them as a connection to days gone by.

An elegant, somewhat literary word with deep emotional resonance. Can be written in kanji as 縁(よすが) or 便(よすが), but is most commonly written in hiragana. Appears frequently in literature, poetry, and formal or reflective writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心(こころ)のよすが: emotional support, something for the heart to cling to
  • 生(い)きるよすが: reason for living, something to live for
  • よすがとする: to take as one's support or keepsake
  • よすがの品(しな): a memento, keepsake item

USAGE:
Used when speaking of something deeply important that provides emotional support, comfort, or connection — whether to a person, to the past, or to hope. Carries a poetic, often bittersweet nuance. Uncommon in casual speech but valued in literary and formal contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頼(たよ)り: reliance, support — more everyday and practical
  • 縁(えん): connection, fate — broader concept of karmic or social ties
  • 形見(かたみ): keepsake of a deceased person — specifically a memento left by someone who has died
