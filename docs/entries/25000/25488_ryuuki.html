1. uplift; elevation; rising
The geological process of land rising or being pushed upward, or the resulting elevated landform.
地殻(ちかく)の隆起(りゅうき)によって山(やま)が形成(けいせい)された。
Mountains were formed by the uplift of the earth's crust.
この海岸(かいがん)は隆起(りゅうき)サンゴ礁(しょう)でできている。
This coastline is made of uplifted coral reef.
能登半島(のとはんとう)地震(じしん)では海底(かいてい)が隆起(りゅうき)し、海岸線(かいがんせん)が大(おお)きく変化(へんか)した。
In the Noto Peninsula earthquake, the seabed was uplifted, dramatically changing the coastline.
2. protuberance; swelling; bump
A raised area on a surface, including on the body. Used in medical and general descriptive contexts.
皮膚(ひふ)に小(ちい)さな隆起(りゅうき)がある。
There is a small bump on the skin.
骨(ほね)の隆起(りゅうき)部分(ぶぶん)を触(さわ)ると痛(いた)い。
It hurts when I touch the protruding part of the bone.
地面(じめん)の隆起(りゅうき)のせいで道路(どうろ)にひびが入(はい)っている。
The road has cracks due to the ground swelling up.

A compound of 隆(りゅう) (to rise; prominent) and 起(き) (to rise; to occur). Most commonly encountered in geology and earth science, but also used in medicine and anatomy. Japan's volcanic and seismic geography makes this a relatively common word in news reporting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隆起(りゅうき)する: to be uplifted; to rise; to swell
  • 地殻(ちかく)隆起(りゅうき): crustal uplift
  • 隆起(りゅうき)サンゴ礁(しょう): uplifted coral reef
  • 海底(かいてい)の隆起(りゅうき): seabed uplift
  • 隆起(りゅうき)部(ぶ): protuberance; raised area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 沈降(ちんこう): subsidence — the opposite process; land sinking down
  • 膨(ふく)らみ: swelling; bulge — a softer, everyday term for something that puffs up
  • 突起(とっき): protrusion; projection — a sharp or pointed raised part
