1. cutting in line; jumping the queue
entering a line from the side without waiting one's turn
横入(よこい)りしないでください。
Please don't cut in line.
列(れつ)に横入(よこい)りする人(ひと)がいて腹(はら)が立(た)った。
I got angry because someone cut into the line.
ラーメン屋(や)の行列(ぎょうれつ)に横入(よこい)りしたら、後(うし)ろの人(ひと)に注意(ちゅうい)された。
When I cut into the line at the ramen shop, the person behind me called me out.

Composed of 横(よこ) (side, lateral) + 入(い)り (entering). Describes the act of entering a queue from the side rather than joining at the end. Considered rude behavior in Japan, where orderly queuing is a strong social norm.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 横入(よこい)りする: to cut in line
  • 横入(よこい)りされる: to have someone cut in front of you
  • 横入(よこい)り禁止(きんし): no cutting in line
  • 列(れつ)に横入(よこい)りする: to cut into a line

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 割(わ)り込(こ)み: cutting in — similar meaning, also used for interrupting conversations or traffic
  • 割(わ)り込(こ)む: to cut in — verb form; broader usage including cutting into traffic
  • 順番(じゅんばん)を守(まも)る: to wait one's turn — the expected behavior

NOTE: 横入(よこい)り is particularly common in children's speech and in casual contexts. Adults more often use 割(わ)り込(こ)み.
