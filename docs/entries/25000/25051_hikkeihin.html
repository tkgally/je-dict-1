必携品(ひっけいひん)

ひっけいひん
noun
essential item; must-have item; indispensable thing to carry
1. essential item; must-have item; indispensable thing to carry
旅行(りょこう)の必携品(ひっけいひん)リスト。
A list of travel essentials.
折(お)りたたみ傘(かさ)は梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)の必携品(ひっけいひん)だ。
A folding umbrella is an essential item during the rainy season.
海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)の必携品(ひっけいひん)として、パスポートのコピーや常備薬(じょうびやく)を忘(わす)れないようにしましょう。
As essentials for overseas travel, make sure not to forget copies of your passport and your regular medications.

Composed of 必携(ひっけい) (must-carry, indispensable) + 品(ひん) (item, goods). Often used in travel guides, packing lists, and product recommendations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旅行(りょこう)の必携品(ひっけいひん): travel essentials
  • 登山(とざん)の必携品(ひっけいひん): essential mountaineering gear
  • 冬(ふゆ)の必携品(ひっけいひん): winter must-haves
  • 必携品(ひっけいひん)リスト: essentials list

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 必需品(ひつじゅひん): necessities, daily essentials — things needed for living, not necessarily portable
  • 持(も)ち物(もの): belongings, things to bring — neutral term without the "essential" nuance
  • 携行品(けいこうひん): items carried on one's person — formal, often used in official or insurance contexts
