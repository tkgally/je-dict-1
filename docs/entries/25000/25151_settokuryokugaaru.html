説得力(せっとくりょく)がある

せっとくりょくがある
expression
to be persuasive; to be convincing
1. to be persuasive; to be convincing; to carry weight
Describes an argument, explanation, person, or presentation that has the power to persuade or convince others.
彼(かれ)の意見(いけん)には説得力(せっとくりょく)がある。
His opinion is convincing.
データに基(もと)づいた説明(せつめい)は説得力(せっとくりょく)がある。
An explanation based on data is persuasive.
実際(じっさい)に経験(けいけん)した人(ひと)の話(はなし)は、教科書(きょうかしょ)の知識(ちしき)よりもはるかに説得力(せっとくりょく)がある。
The words of someone who has actually experienced it are far more convincing than textbook knowledge.

A combination of 説得力(せっとくりょく) (persuasive power) and がある (to have, to exist). This is the standard way to say something is persuasive or convincing in Japanese.

The negative form 説得力(せっとくりょく)がない means "not convincing, unpersuasive." 説得力(せっとくりょく)に欠(か)ける (to lack persuasiveness) is a slightly more formal alternative.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 説得力(せっとくりょく)のある議論(ぎろん): a convincing argument
  • 説得力(せっとくりょく)のある説明(せつめい): a persuasive explanation
  • 説得力(せっとくりょく)が増(ま)す: persuasiveness increases
  • 説得力(せっとくりょく)に欠(か)ける: to lack persuasiveness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 納得(なっとく)できる: to be able to accept, to find convincing — focuses on the listener's acceptance
  • {もっともらしい}: plausible, seemingly reasonable — can imply something only appears convincing on the surface
  • 信憑性(しんぴょうせい)がある: to have credibility — focuses on trustworthiness of information
