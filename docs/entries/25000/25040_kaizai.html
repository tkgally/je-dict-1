1. mediation; intervention; being interposed between
第三者(だいさんしゃ)が介在(かいざい)する。
A third party intervenes.
両者(りょうしゃ)の間(あいだ)に誤解(ごかい)が介在(かいざい)している。
A misunderstanding lies between the two parties.
政治的(せいじてき)な思惑(おもわく)が介在(かいざい)しているのではないかと疑(うたが)われている。
It is suspected that political motives are at play behind the scenes.
この取引(とりひき)には仲介業者(ちゅうかいぎょうしゃ)が介在(かいざい)しているため、手数料(てすうりょう)がかかる。
Because a broker is involved in this transaction, there is a commission fee.

Describes something or someone positioned between two things, acting as an intermediary or intervening force. Often used in formal writing and news to describe hidden factors or middlemen in a situation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 第三者(だいさんしゃ)が介在(かいざい)する: a third party intervenes
  • 利害(りがい)が介在(かいざい)する: interests are involved
  • 感情(かんじょう)が介在(かいざい)する: emotions come into play
  • 仲介者(ちゅうかいしゃ)の介在(かいざい): involvement of a mediator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仲介(ちゅうかい): mediation, brokerage — focuses on actively facilitating between parties
  • 介入(かいにゅう): intervention — implies more forceful or deliberate involvement
  • 媒介(ばいかい): transmission, mediation — often used for disease vectors or agents that convey something
