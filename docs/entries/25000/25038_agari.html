上(あ)がり

あがり
noun
rise; increase; income; earnings
1. rise; increase; income; earnings
売(う)り上(あ)がりが増(ふ)えた。
Sales revenue increased.
今月(こんげつ)の上(あ)がりは先月(せんげつ)より多(おお)い。
This month's earnings are more than last month's.
物価(ぶっか)の上(あ)がりが止(と)まらないので家計(かけい)が苦(くる)しい。
The rise in prices won't stop, so our household budget is tight.
2. completion; finish; result (of work or a game)
きれいな上(あ)がりだね。
That's a nice finish.
写真(しゃしん)の上(あ)がりを確認(かくにん)する。
I'll check how the photos turned out.
この料理(りょうり)は上(あ)がりが早(はや)いから、忙(いそが)しい日(ひ)にぴったりだ。
This dish finishes quickly, so it's perfect for busy days.
3. stepping up into a house; entryway; green tea (at a sushi restaurant)
上(あ)がりをどうぞ。
Here's your tea (at a sushi restaurant).
靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)いで上(あ)がりにお上(あ)がりください。
Please take off your shoes and step up into the entryway.
お寿司屋(すしや)さんで最後(さいご)に上(あ)がりを頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered tea at the end of my meal at the sushi restaurant.

The noun form of the verb 上(あ)がる. Has a wide range of meanings depending on context, from financial gains to the quality of a finished product.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売(う)り上(あ)がり: sales, revenue
  • 出来(でき)上(あ)がり: completion, finished product
  • 仕上(しあ)がり: finishing, final result
  • 物価(ぶっか)の上(あ)がり: rise in prices
  • 上(あ)がり框(がまち): entryway step (in a Japanese house)

SUSHI RESTAURANT USAGE:
At sushi restaurants, 上(あ)がり is the traditional term for green tea. This is part of the specialized vocabulary (sushi terminology) used at the counter. Customers may use this word to request tea at the end of their meal.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上昇(じょうしょう): rise, ascent — more formal and used for measurable increases
  • 収入(しゅうにゅう): income — specifically refers to money earned
  • 完成(かんせい): completion — emphasizes the finished state of a project or work
