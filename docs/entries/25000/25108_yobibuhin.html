予備部品(よびぶひん)

よびぶひん
noun
spare part; replacement part
1. spare part; replacement part
a component kept in reserve to replace a broken or worn-out part in a machine, vehicle, or device
予備部品(よびぶひん)を注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered a spare part.
予備部品(よびぶひん)がないと修理(しゅうり)できない。
Without spare parts, the repair can't be done.
古(ふる)い車(くるま)は予備部品(よびぶひん)の入手(にゅうしゅ)が難(むずか)しくなるので、維持(いじ)するのが大変(たいへん)だ。
Old cars are hard to maintain because spare parts become difficult to obtain.

Combines 予備(よび) (reserve, spare) and 部品(ぶひん) (parts, components). Used in automotive, industrial, electronics, and general maintenance contexts. The shortened form スペア部品(ぶひん) or スペアパーツ is also used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予備部品(よびぶひん)を確保(かくほ)する: to secure spare parts
  • 予備部品(よびぶひん)の在庫(ざいこ): spare parts inventory
  • 予備部品(よびぶひん)を交換(こうかん)する: to replace with a spare part
  • 純正(じゅんせい)予備部品(よびぶひん): genuine spare parts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交換部品(こうかんぶひん): replacement part — a part specifically for swapping out
  • 消耗品(しょうもうひん): consumable — items that wear out with regular use
  • スペアパーツ: spare parts — the English loanword version
  • 部品(ぶひん): parts, components — the general term
