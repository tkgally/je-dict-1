A widely used verb covering two main domains: making information public (announcements, publications, releases) and giving oral presentations in academic or business settings. The noun form 発表(はっぴょう) is equally common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 研究(けんきゅう)発表(はっぴょう): research presentation
  • 記者(きしゃ)発表(はっぴょう): press conference
  • 発表(はっぴょう)会(かい): presentation event; recital
  • 新(しん)製品(せいひん)を発表(はっぴょう)する: announce a new product
  • 声明(せいめい)を発表(はっぴょう)する: issue a statement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 公表(こうひょう)する: to make public; to disclose — emphasizes revealing information that was previously undisclosed, often official data or statistics
  • 発信(はっしん)する: to send out; to communicate — focuses on transmitting information or messages, especially in media or online contexts
  • 報告(ほうこく)する: to report — involves reporting findings or progress to a specific audience, typically superiors or stakeholders
