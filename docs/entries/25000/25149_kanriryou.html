管理料(かんりりょう)

かんりりょう
noun
management fee; maintenance charge
1. management fee; maintenance charge; administration fee
A fee charged for the management, maintenance, or administration of a property, facility, or service.
管理料(かんりりょう)は月額(げつがく)一万円(いちまんえん)だ。
The management fee is 10,000 yen per month.
マンションの管理料(かんりりょう)が値上(ねあ)げされた。
The apartment management fee was raised.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)の管理料(かんりりょう)には清掃費(せいそうひ)や設備(せつび)の維持費(いじひ)が含(ふく)まれている。
The parking lot management fee includes cleaning costs and equipment maintenance expenses.

A compound of 管理(かんり) (management, administration) and 料(りょう) (fee, charge). Commonly encountered in housing, real estate, and facility usage. In apartment living, 管理料(かんりりょう) or 管理費(かんりひ) covers shared costs like elevator maintenance, cleaning of common areas, and building security.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 管理料(かんりりょう)を払(はら)う: to pay a management fee
  • 管理料(かんりりょう)の値上(ねあ)げ: management fee increase
  • 月額(げつがく)管理料(かんりりょう): monthly management fee
  • 管理料(かんりりょう)込(こ)み: management fee included

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 管理費(かんりひ): management expenses — nearly synonymous; 費(ひ) emphasizes cost/expense while 料(りょう) emphasizes a set fee
  • 手数料(てすうりょう): handling fee, commission — a fee for processing or service
  • 維持費(いじひ): maintenance costs — focuses specifically on keeping something in working condition
