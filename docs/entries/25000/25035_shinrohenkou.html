1. change of course; direction change
Changing the physical direction of travel, such as when driving, sailing, or flying.
進路変更(しんろへんこう)するときはウインカーを出(だ)す。
Use your turn signal when changing lanes.
台風(たいふう)が進路変更(しんろへんこう)して東(ひがし)に向(む)かった。
The typhoon changed course and headed east.
安全(あんぜん)を確認(かくにん)してから進路変更(しんろへんこう)してください。
Please confirm it is safe before changing direction.
2. career path change; change of future plans
Changing one's planned course in life, education, or career — such as switching from a university track to vocational school, or changing intended fields of study.
高校(こうこう)三年(さんねん)の夏(なつ)に進路変更(しんろへんこう)を決(き)めた。
I decided to change my career path in the summer of my third year of high school.
留学(りゅうがく)をきっかけに進路変更(しんろへんこう)した。
Studying abroad led me to change my career plans.
親(おや)に進路変更(しんろへんこう)のことを相談(そうだん)したら、応援(おうえん)してくれた。
When I consulted my parents about changing my career path, they were supportive.

Composed of 進路(しんろ) (course; route; career path) + 変更(へんこう) (change). The word has two distinct spheres of usage: physical navigation and life planning. In the driving context, it specifically refers to lane changes and turns. In the life-planning context, it is closely tied to the Japanese education system, where students must choose their 進路(しんろ) (future path) during high school.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 進路変更(しんろへんこう)する: change course / change career path
  • 進路変更(しんろへんこう)の合図(あいず): signal for a direction change
  • 進路変更(しんろへんこう)を決(き)める: decide on a change of course

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 方向転換(ほうこうてんかん): change of direction — more dramatic; implies a major shift
  • 転職(てんしょく): job change — specifically about switching jobs, not the broader life-course meaning
  • 志望変更(しぼうへんこう): change of desired school/career — more specific to application targets
