1. to record (audio); to make a recording
To capture sound onto a medium for later playback. Applies to recording music, voice, conversations, or any audio.
会議(かいぎ)を録音(ろくおん)する。
To record a meeting.
スマホで授業(じゅぎょう)の内容(ないよう)を録音(ろくおん)した。
I recorded the class content on my smartphone.
このアルバムは有名(ゆうめい)なスタジオで録音(ろくおん)されたものだ。
This album was recorded at a famous studio.

Specifically refers to audio recording, as distinct from video recording. The kanji literally combine 録(ろく) (record) and 音(おん) (sound).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 録音(ろくおん)機能(きのう): recording function
  • 録音(ろくおん)データ: recording data
  • 録音(ろくおん)スタジオ: recording studio
  • 録音(ろくおん)禁止(きんし): recording prohibited
  • ボイス録音(ろくおん): voice recording

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 録画(ろくが)する: to record video — captures both image and sound, while 録音(ろくおん) is audio only
  • 収録(しゅうろく)する: to record (for broadcast) — typically used for professional recording of TV/radio programs, while 録音(ろくおん) is more general
  • 撮影(さつえい)する: to film, to photograph — captures visual images, possibly with sound
