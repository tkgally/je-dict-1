お昼寝(ひるね)

おひるね
noun
afternoon nap; daytime nap
1. afternoon nap; daytime nap
A nap taken during the day, typically in the afternoon. The お prefix adds politeness or softness, and the word is especially associated with children's naptime.
赤(あか)ちゃんがお昼寝(ひるね)している。
The baby is taking a nap.
日曜日(にちようび)はお昼寝(ひるね)するのが楽(たの)しみだ。
I look forward to taking a nap on Sundays.
保育園(ほいくえん)では、お昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)の後(あと)にお昼寝(ひるね)の時間(じかん)がある。
At daycare, there is naptime after lunch.

The polite prefix お makes this word softer and more colloquial than the plain form 昼寝(ひるね). It is especially common when talking about children's naps (at daycare, kindergarten, etc.) and in casual adult conversation about taking a relaxing daytime rest.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お昼寝(ひるね)する: to take a nap
  • お昼寝(ひるね)の時間(じかん): naptime
  • お昼寝(ひるね)から起(お)きる: to wake up from a nap
  • お昼寝(ひるね)させる: to put someone down for a nap

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 昼寝(ひるね): daytime nap — the plain form without お; used in neutral or written contexts
  • 仮眠(かみん): short sleep; power nap — more formal; used for brief strategic rest, often at work
  • うたた寝(ね): dozing off; nodding off — falling asleep unintentionally

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, 保育園(ほいくえん) (daycare) typically has a scheduled お昼寝(ひるね) period for young children. The concept of short naps at work (昼寝(ひるね)) has also been gaining acceptance, with some companies introducing nap rooms.
