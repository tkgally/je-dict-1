1. to do piecework at home; to do a side job
to earn extra money by doing work at home, typically simple manual tasks paid by the piece
母(はは)は家(いえ)で内職(ないしょく)をしている。
My mother does piecework at home.
家計(かけい)を助(たす)けるために内職(ないしょく)を始(はじ)めた。
I started doing side work at home to help with the household finances.
封筒(ふうとう)の糊付(のりづ)けや部品(ぶひん)の組(く)み立(た)てなどの内職(ないしょく)で月(つき)に数万円(すうまんえん)稼(かせ)いでいる。
I earn tens of thousands of yen a month from piecework like gluing envelopes and assembling parts.
2. to do something unrelated during class or a meeting
to secretly engage in a personal activity when one should be paying attention
授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)に内職(ないしょく)するな。
Don't do other things during class.
会議中(かいぎちゅう)にメールを打(う)つのは内職(ないしょく)だ。
Typing emails during a meeting is slacking off.
先生(せんせい)の目(め)を盗(ぬす)んでこっそり内職(ないしょく)していたら、見(み)つかってしまった。
I was secretly doing something else behind the teacher's back, but got caught.

Composed of 内(ない) (inside, home) + 職(しょく) (work). Originally referred specifically to piecework done at home for extra income. The secondary meaning of secretly doing unrelated activities during class or meetings is very common in spoken Japanese, especially among students.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内職(ないしょく)で稼(かせ)ぐ: to earn money from side work
  • 内職(ないしょく)を見(み)つける: to find piecework
  • 授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)の内職(ないしょく): doing other things during class
  • 内職(ないしょく)がバレる: to get caught doing something else

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 副業(ふくぎょう): side business — broader term for any secondary job or income source
  • 在宅(ざいたく)ワーク: work from home — modern term for remote work, not necessarily piecework
  • サボる: to slack off — to skip or be lazy about one's duties
