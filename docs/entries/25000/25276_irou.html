1. appreciation for hard work; recognition of effort
expressing thanks and comfort to someone for their labor or hardship
社員(しゃいん)を慰労(いろう)する。
To show appreciation to the employees for their hard work.
慰労(いろう)会(かい)でスタッフの労(ろう)をねぎらった。
We thanked the staff for their hard work at the appreciation party.
長年(ながねん)の功績(こうせき)に対(たい)して慰労(いろう)金(きん)が支給(しきゅう)された。
A recognition bonus was given for years of distinguished service.

Composed of 慰(い) (comfort, console) + 労(ろう) (labor, toil). Expresses gratitude and comfort to those who have worked hard or endured hardship. Commonly used in workplace and organizational contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 慰労(いろう)会(かい): appreciation party; thank-you gathering
  • 慰労(いろう)金(きん): recognition bonus; gratuity for service
  • 慰労(いろう)の言葉(ことば): words of appreciation
  • 慰労(いろう)旅行(りょこう): appreciation trip (reward trip for employees)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ねぎらう: to thank/appreciate someone's effort — a verb with similar meaning, used more in everyday speech
  • 慰安(いあん): comfort, consolation — focuses more on providing relief and pleasure than recognizing effort
  • 労(ろう)をねぎらう: to appreciate someone's labor — a common expression with overlapping meaning
