1. to be on guard; to be vigilant; to watch out for
To remain cautious and alert against potential danger, threats, or problems.
不審者(ふしんしゃ)に警戒(けいかい)する。
To be on guard against suspicious individuals.
台風(たいふう)の接近(せっきん)に警戒(けいかい)してください。
Please be on the alert for the approaching typhoon.
野生(やせい)の動物(どうぶつ)は人間(にんげん)を警戒(けいかい)している。
Wild animals are wary of humans.
大雨(おおあめ)による土砂(どしゃ)災害(さいがい)に厳重(げんじゅう)に警戒(けいかい)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
There is a need to be strictly on guard against landslides caused by heavy rain.

Frequently used in weather warnings and security contexts. The pattern is 〜に警戒(けいかい)する (to be on guard against). Also used for animals being wary and for military/police vigilance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厳重(げんじゅう)警戒(けいかい): strict vigilance; high alert
  • 警戒(けいかい)態勢(たいせい): alert status; state of readiness
  • 警戒(けいかい)区域(くいき): restricted/alert zone
  • 警戒(けいかい)レベル: alert level
  • 警戒(けいかい)を強(つよ)める: to heighten vigilance
  • 警戒(けいかい)を怠(おこた)る: to let one's guard down

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注意(ちゅうい)する: to be careful, to pay attention — more general caution, less intensity
  • 用心(ようじん)する: to be cautious — everyday precaution against known risks
  • 監視(かんし)する: to monitor, to watch — implies continuous observation, often with equipment
