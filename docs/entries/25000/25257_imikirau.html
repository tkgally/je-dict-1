1. to detest; to abhor; to loathe
to hate something so strongly that one avoids it or finds it repulsive
嘘(うそ)を忌(い)み嫌(きら)う。
I detest lies.
暴力(ぼうりょく)は忌(い)み嫌(きら)われるべきだ。
Violence should be abhorred.
彼(かれ)は不正(ふせい)を忌(い)み嫌(きら)う人(ひと)だ。
He is a person who abhors injustice.
かつて忌(い)み嫌(きら)われていた食材(しょくざい)が、今(いま)では珍味(ちんみ)として人気(にんき)だ。
An ingredient that was once loathed is now popular as a delicacy.

A compound verb combining 忌(い)む (to shun as taboo) and 嫌(きら)う (to dislike), creating a word that expresses an intense, visceral aversion. Stronger than simply 嫌(きら)う, it implies that the object of hatred is seen as morally wrong or deeply offensive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嘘(うそ)を忌(い)み嫌(きら)う: to detest lies
  • 不正(ふせい)を忌(い)み嫌(きら)う: to abhor injustice
  • 暴力(ぼうりょく)を忌(い)み嫌(きら)う: to loathe violence
  • 忌(い)み嫌(きら)われる: to be loathed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 嫌(きら)う: to dislike; much milder than 忌(い)み嫌(きら)う
  • 憎(にく)む: to hate; focuses on hostility toward a specific person or thing
  • 毛嫌(けぎら)いする: to have an irrational dislike or prejudice against; implies the dislike is not based on reason

ETYMOLOGY:
From 忌(い)む (to avoid as taboo or inauspicious) + 嫌(きら)う (to dislike). The combination intensifies the hatred and adds a dimension of moral rejection.
