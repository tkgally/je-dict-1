乗合(のりあい)バス

のりあいばす
noun
public bus; shared bus; fixed-route bus
1. public bus; shared bus; fixed-route bus
乗合(のりあい)バスで通勤(つうきん)している。
I commute by public bus.
この地域(ちいき)では乗合(のりあい)バスが一日(いちにち)に三本(さんぼん)しかない。
In this area, there are only three public buses a day.
過疎地(かそち)では乗合(のりあい)バスの路線(ろせん)が廃止(はいし)されるケースが増(ふ)えており、住民(じゅうみん)の移動手段(いどうしゅだん)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
In depopulated areas, public bus routes are increasingly being discontinued, making transportation a problem for residents.

The formal term for a public bus that runs on a fixed route and picks up multiple passengers. 乗合(のりあい) means "shared ride" or "riding together." While バス alone is the everyday word, 乗合(のりあい)バス is used in official, legal, and administrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乗合(のりあい)バスの路線(ろせん): public bus route
  • 乗合(のりあい)バス事業(じぎょう): public bus service (business term)
  • 乗合(のりあい)タクシー: shared taxi (a related concept for rural areas)

USAGE:
In everyday speech, people simply say バス. The term 乗合(のりあい)バス appears in transportation regulations, news articles about public transit, and administrative documents. It distinguishes fixed-route public buses from chartered buses (貸切(かしきり)バス) and tour buses.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • バス: bus — the common everyday word
  • 路線(ろせん)バス: route bus — another term for fixed-route public buses, more commonly used than 乗合(のりあい)バス
  • 貸切(かしきり)バス: chartered bus — a bus hired for private group use
  • 高速(こうそく)バス: highway bus — long-distance express bus
