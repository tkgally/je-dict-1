その他(た)

そのた
noun
others; the rest; miscellaneous; et cetera
1. others; the rest; miscellaneous; et cetera
その他(た)の質問(しつもん)はありますか。
Are there any other questions?
本(ほん)やノート、その他(た)の文房具(ぶんぼうぐ)を買(か)った。
I bought books, notebooks, and other stationery.
アンケートの選択肢(せんたくし)に「その他(た)」と書(か)いてある欄(らん)に自由(じゆう)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please write freely in the box labeled 'Other' on the survey choices.
交通費(こうつうひ)、食費(しょくひ)、その他(た)もろもろの費用(ひよう)がかかった。
Transportation costs, food expenses, and various other expenses were incurred.

A very common expression meaning "others" or "the rest," used to refer to remaining items in a category. Often appears in lists, forms, and menus as a catch-all category.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • その他(た)の + noun: other (noun), the remaining (noun)
  • その他(た)もろもろ: various other things
  • その他(た)いろいろ: and various others

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • その他(た)の費用(ひよう): other expenses
  • その他(た)の条件(じょうけん): other conditions
  • その他(た)の事項(じこう): other matters
  • その他(た)大勢(おおぜい): the masses, the rest of the crowd

USAGE:
Often written as その他(た) or simply その他(ほか) (read as そのほか), which is a near-synonym. The そのた reading is more formal and commonly appears in written documents, forms, and official categories, while そのほか is more conversational.
