司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)

しほうかいぼう
noun
forensic autopsy; judicial autopsy
1. forensic autopsy; judicial autopsy; medico-legal autopsy
An autopsy ordered by a court or prosecutor to determine the cause of death in cases involving suspected crime or unnatural death. Performed by a forensic pathologist as part of a criminal investigation.
警察(けいさつ)は司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)を行(おこな)った。
The police conducted a forensic autopsy.
司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)の結果(けっか)、死因(しいん)は窒息(ちっそく)と判明(はんめい)した。
As a result of the forensic autopsy, the cause of death was determined to be asphyxiation.
不審(ふしん)な点(てん)があったため、検察(けんさつ)は裁判所(さいばんしょ)の許可(きょか)を得(え)て司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)を実施(じっし)することにした。
Because there were suspicious points, the prosecution obtained court permission and decided to carry out a forensic autopsy.

A compound of 司法(しほう) (judicial, legal) and 解剖(かいぼう) (dissection, autopsy). In Japan, 司法解剖(しほうかいぼう) requires a warrant from a judge and is specifically for criminal investigations, unlike 行政解剖(ぎょうせいかいぼう) (administrative autopsy) which does not require a warrant.

A common term in crime news, detective fiction, and legal dramas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)を行(おこな)う: to perform a forensic autopsy
  • 司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)の結果(けっか): results of the forensic autopsy
  • 司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)を依頼(いらい)する: to request a forensic autopsy
  • 司法解剖(しほうかいぼう)で死因(しいん)を特定(とくてい)する: to determine cause of death through forensic autopsy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 行政解剖(ぎょうせいかいぼう): administrative autopsy — autopsy for non-criminal unnatural deaths
  • 病理解剖(びょうりかいぼう): pathological autopsy — autopsy to study disease, performed in hospitals
  • 解剖(かいぼう): autopsy, dissection — the general term
