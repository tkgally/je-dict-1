未処理(みしょり)

みしょり
noun, adjective-no
unprocessed; untreated; unresolved
1. unprocessed; untreated; unresolved
not yet having been dealt with, processed, or treated
未処理(みしょり)の書類(しょるい)がたまっている。
Unprocessed paperwork has piled up.
未処理(みしょり)の排水(はいすい)を川(かわ)に流(なが)してはいけない。
You must not discharge untreated wastewater into the river.
未処理(みしょり)の案件(あんけん)が三十件(さんじゅっけん)も残(のこ)っているので、今日中(きょうじゅう)に片付(かたづ)けたい。
There are thirty unresolved cases remaining, so I want to get them done by today.

Composed of 未(み) (not yet) + 処理(しょり) (processing, treatment). The prefix 未(み) is productive and indicates something has not yet been done: 未完成(みかんせい) (unfinished), 未確認(みかくにん) (unconfirmed), 未解決(みかいけつ) (unsolved).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 未処理(みしょり)の書類(しょるい): unprocessed documents
  • 未処理(みしょり)のデータ: raw/unprocessed data
  • 未処理(みしょり)排水(はいすい): untreated wastewater
  • 未処理(みしょり)案件(あんけん): unresolved cases
  • 未処理(みしょり)のまま放置(ほうち)する: to leave unprocessed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 未解決(みかいけつ): unsolved, unresolved — specifically for problems or cases that haven't been solved
  • 処理済(しょりず)み: processed, handled — the opposite state, indicating completion
  • 保留(ほりゅう): pending, on hold — deliberately put aside for later decision
