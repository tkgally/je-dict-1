1. to breathe; to respire
To take air into the lungs and expel it, either as an involuntary bodily function or as a deliberate action.
深(ふか)く呼吸(こきゅう)してください。
Please breathe deeply.
緊張(きんちょう)したときはゆっくり(ゆっくり)呼吸(こきゅう)するといい。
When you're nervous, it's good to breathe slowly.
患者(かんじゃ)は自力(じりき)で呼吸(こきゅう)することができなくなり、人工(じんこう)呼吸器(こきゅうき)が必要(ひつよう)になった。
The patient became unable to breathe on their own and needed a ventilator.

Covers both the involuntary act of breathing and conscious breathing exercises. The noun form 呼吸(こきゅう) is more common than the verb form and appears in many compounds.

Literally composed of 呼(こ) (to exhale) and 吸(きゅう) (to inhale), though the compound refers to the full breathing cycle.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 深(ふか)呼吸(こきゅう): deep breathing
  • 呼吸(こきゅう)が荒(あら)い: to be breathing heavily
  • 呼吸(こきゅう)が止(と)まる: to stop breathing
  • 呼吸(こきゅう)を整(ととの)える: to regulate one's breathing
  • 人工(じんこう)呼吸(こきゅう): artificial respiration
  • 腹式(ふくしき)呼吸(こきゅう): abdominal breathing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 息(いき)をする: to breathe — the everyday, colloquial way to say "breathe"
  • 吸(す)う: to inhale; to breathe in — refers only to the inhalation phase
  • 吐(は)く: to exhale; to breathe out — refers only to the exhalation phase
