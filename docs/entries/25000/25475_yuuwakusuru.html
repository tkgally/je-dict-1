1. to tempt; to lure; to entice
To attract or entice someone toward something, often something they should resist. The temptation may involve food, money, pleasure, or other desires.
ケーキに誘惑(ゆうわく)された。
I was tempted by the cake.
甘(あま)い言葉(ことば)で誘惑(ゆうわく)してくる。
They try to lure you with sweet words.
ダイエット中(ちゅう)なのに、目(め)の前(まえ)のスイーツに誘惑(ゆうわく)されて食(た)べてしまった。
Even though I was on a diet, I was tempted by the sweets in front of me and ended up eating them.
2. to seduce; to lead astray
To attract someone romantically or sexually, often with the implication of leading them away from proper behavior.
彼女(かのじょ)を誘惑(ゆうわく)しようとした。
He tried to seduce her.
映画(えいが)の中(なか)で悪魔(あくま)が主人公(しゅじんこう)を誘惑(ゆうわく)する場面(ばめん)が印象的(いんしょうてき)だった。
The scene in the movie where the devil tempts the protagonist was memorable.
彼(かれ)は巧(たく)みな話術(わじゅつ)で相手(あいて)を誘惑(ゆうわく)し、大金(たいきん)を騙(だま)し取(と)った。
He seduced his target with skillful rhetoric and swindled a large sum of money.

A compound of 誘(ゆう) (to invite; to lead) and 惑(わく) (to be confused; to go astray). The noun form 誘惑(ゆうわく) is also very common, used in phrases like 誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)ける (to give in to temptation).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)ける: to give in to temptation
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に勝(か)つ: to resist temptation
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に抗(あらが)う: to fight against temptation
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に駆(か)られる: to be driven by temptation
  • 甘(あま)い誘惑(ゆうわく): sweet temptation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誘(さそ)う: to invite; to lure — more neutral; covers friendly invitations as well as enticement
  • 惑(まど)わす: to lead astray; to bewitch — emphasizes confusion and deception
  • 口説(くど)く: to woo; to come on to — specifically romantic/sexual persuasion, more direct
