上白糖(じょうはくとう)

じょうはくとう
noun
refined white sugar; superior white sugar
1. refined white sugar; superior white sugar
The most common type of granulated white sugar used in Japan, finer and slightly more moist than Western granulated sugar.
上白糖(じょうはくとう)を大(おお)さじ二杯(にはい)加(くわ)える。
Add two tablespoons of refined white sugar.
日本(にほん)の家庭(かてい)では上白糖(じょうはくとう)が最(もっと)もよく使(つか)われる砂糖(さとう)だ。
In Japanese households, refined white sugar is the most commonly used sugar.
上白糖(じょうはくとう)はグラニュー糖(とう)と比(くら)べて粒(つぶ)が細(こま)かく、料理(りょうり)に溶(と)けやすいのが特徴(とくちょう)だ。
Compared to granulated sugar, refined white sugar has finer grains and dissolves more easily in cooking.

Literally "superior white sugar," from 上(じょう) (superior), 白(はく) (white), and 糖(とう) (sugar). This is the default sugar in Japan — when Japanese recipes simply call for 砂糖(さとう) (sugar), they typically mean 上白糖(じょうはくとう).

Unlike the coarser granulated sugar (グラニュー糖(とう)) common in Western countries, 上白糖(じょうはくとう) has a finer texture and contains a small amount of inverted sugar, giving it a slightly moist quality and milder sweetness. It accounts for roughly half of all sugar consumption in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上白糖(じょうはくとう)大(おお)さじ一杯(いっぱい): one tablespoon of white sugar
  • 上白糖(じょうはくとう)を加(くわ)える: to add white sugar
  • 上白糖(じょうはくとう)の代(か)わりに: instead of white sugar

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • グラニュー糖(とう): granulated sugar — coarser crystals, commonly used in Western baking
  • 三温糖(さんおんとう): light brown sugar — made by further heating white sugar, with a richer flavor
  • 黒糖(こくとう): brown sugar, raw sugar — unrefined sugar from Okinawa with a strong molasses flavor
