川上(かわかみ)

かわかみ
noun
upstream; upper reaches of a river
1. upstream; upper reaches of a river
The part of a river closer to its source, or the direction from which the water flows.
川上(かわかみ)から流(なが)れてきた。
It came flowing from upstream.
川上(かわかみ)に向(む)かって歩(ある)いた。
I walked toward the upper reaches of the river.
大雨(おおあめ)で川上(かわかみ)のダムが放流(ほうりゅう)した。
The dam upstream released water due to heavy rain.
2. upstream (in a supply chain or process)
The earlier stages of a production or supply chain, closer to raw materials or initial creation.
川上(かわかみ)産業(さんぎょう)から川下(かわしも)産業(さんぎょう)まで一貫(いっかん)して管理(かんり)する。
To manage consistently from upstream industries to downstream industries.
製造(せいぞう)の川上(かわかみ)で品質(ひんしつ)を管理(かんり)することが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
It is important to control quality at the upstream stage of manufacturing.
川上(かわかみ)から川下(かわしも)へ、情報(じょうほう)が正確(せいかく)に伝(つた)わる仕組(しく)みが必要(ひつよう)だ。
A system is needed for information to flow accurately from upstream to downstream.

The literal meaning refers to the upper part of a river, near its source. In business, 川上(かわかみ) and 川下(かわしも) are commonly used as a metaphor for stages in a supply chain or value chain.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 川上(かわかみ)産業(さんぎょう): upstream industry (raw materials, manufacturing)
  • 川上(かわかみ)から川下(かわしも)まで: from upstream to downstream
  • 川上(かわかみ)に遡(さかのぼ)る: to go upstream; to trace back to the source

ANTONYM:

  • 川下(かわしも): downstream — the lower part of a river, or the later stages of a supply chain (distribution, retail)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 上流(じょうりゅう): upper stream — can be used similarly for rivers and also figuratively for upper class
  • 源流(げんりゅう): headwaters, source — the very origin of a river or tradition
