1. name change; renaming
The act of officially changing the name of an organization, product, place, or system. Implies a formal, deliberate decision rather than a casual nickname change.
駅(えき)の名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)が決(き)まった。
The station's name change was decided.
会社(かいしゃ)は来月(らいげつ)から名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)する予定(よてい)です。
The company is scheduled to change its name starting next month.
名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)に伴(ともな)い、ロゴやウェブサイトも新(あたら)しくなった。
Along with the name change, the logo and website were also updated.

Composed of 名称(めいしょう) (name; designation) + 変更(へんこう) (change; alteration). Used for official renamings of institutions, places, products, and services. Common in news and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)する: change the name
  • 名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)に伴(ともな)い: accompanying the name change
  • 名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)の届出(とどけで): notification of name change
  • 名称変更(めいしょうへんこう)の理由(りゆう): reason for the name change

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 改名(かいめい): renaming — used for people and smaller entities; less formal
  • 改称(かいしょう): name change — a more literary synonym, often used for historical renamings
  • 変更(へんこう): change; alteration — the general word for changes of any kind
