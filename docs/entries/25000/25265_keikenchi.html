経験値(けいけんち)

けいけんち
noun
experience points (in games); level of experience
1. experience points (in games); level of experience
a numerical value representing accumulated experience, originally from video games but now widely used figuratively to describe real-life experience and skill
経験値(けいけんち)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To earn experience points.
彼(かれ)はまだ経験値(けいけんち)が低(ひく)い。
He still has a low level of experience.
海外(かいがい)で働(はたら)くことで経験値(けいけんち)が上(あ)がった。
Working abroad raised my experience level.
失敗(しっぱい)も経験値(けいけんち)だと思(おも)えば気(き)が楽(らく)になる。
If you think of failures as experience points, it's easier to accept them.

Originally a gaming term (often abbreviated as EXP), 経験値(けいけんち) has crossed over into everyday Japanese to describe accumulated real-world experience. The gaming metaphor is alive in everyday speech — people talk about "leveling up" their experience.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験値(けいけんち)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to earn experience (points)
  • 経験値(けいけんち)が上(あ)がる: experience level goes up
  • 経験値(けいけんち)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い: to have high/low experience
  • 経験値(けいけんち)を積(つ)む: to build up experience

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経験(けいけん): experience (the general word); 経験値(けいけんち) adds the nuance of quantifiable, accumulated skill
  • 実績(じっせき): track record or achievements; focuses on concrete results rather than accumulated learning
  • 熟練度(じゅくれんど): proficiency level; more formal and focused on skill mastery

WORD FORMATION:
経験(けいけん) (experience) + 値(ち) (value, numerical value).
