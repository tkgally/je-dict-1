1. to break through; to penetrate
To force one's way through a barrier, defense, or obstacle.
敵(てき)の防衛(ぼうえい)線(せん)を突破(とっぱ)した。
They broke through the enemy's defense line.
困難(こんなん)を突破(とっぱ)して目標(もくひょう)を達成(たっせい)した。
They overcame the difficulties and achieved their goal.
予選(よせん)を突破(とっぱ)して決勝(けっしょう)に進(すす)んだ。
They made it through the preliminaries to the finals.
2. to surpass; to exceed
To go beyond a numerical limit, milestone, or threshold.
売(う)り上(あ)げが一億円(いちおくえん)を突破(とっぱ)した。
Sales surpassed 100 million yen.
登録者(とうろくしゃ)数(すう)が百万人(ひゃくまんにん)を突破(とっぱ)した。
The number of subscribers exceeded one million.
気温(きおん)が四十度(よんじゅうど)を突破(とっぱ)する猛暑(もうしょ)が続(つづ)いている。
A severe heat wave with temperatures exceeding 40 degrees continues.

A dynamic, forceful word. In sense 1, it implies forceful or determined action to get past an obstacle. In sense 2, it's commonly used in news to report milestones being reached. Always implies momentum and energy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁(かべ)を突破(とっぱ)する: to break through a wall/barrier
  • 難関(なんかん)を突破(とっぱ)する: to get through a difficult challenge
  • 突破口(とっぱこう): breakthrough; opening
  • 〜を突破(とっぱ): exceeding (a number)
  • 現状(げんじょう)突破(とっぱ): breaking through the status quo

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 超(こ)える: to exceed, to surpass — more general and neutral
  • 乗(の)り越(こ)える: to overcome — emphasizes enduring and getting past a hardship
  • 打破(だは)する: to break down, to smash — focuses on destroying an obstacle or convention
