フェイスタオル

ふぇいすたおる
noun
face towel; hand towel
1. face towel; hand towel
a medium-sized towel, typically about 34×80 cm, used for drying the face and hands
フェイスタオルを新(あたら)しく買(か)った。
I bought new face towels.
洗面所(せんめんじょ)にフェイスタオルを掛(か)けておいた。
I hung a face towel in the washroom.
このフェイスタオルは肌触(はだざわ)りがよくて、顔(かお)を拭(ふ)くのに気持(きも)ちいい。
This face towel feels nice against the skin and is pleasant to dry your face with.

The standard medium-sized towel in Japanese households, used primarily for drying the face and hands. In Japan, towels are categorized by size more precisely than in many Western countries.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フェイスタオルで顔(かお)を拭(ふ)く: to dry one's face with a face towel
  • フェイスタオルを交換(こうかん)する: to replace a face towel
  • 今治(いまばり)フェイスタオル: Imabari face towel (a famous towel-producing region)

JAPANESE TOWEL SIZES:

  • ハンドタオル / タオルハンカチ: hand towel (approx. 25×25 cm) — pocket-sized
  • フェイスタオル: face towel (approx. 34×80 cm) — the most common household towel
  • バスタオル: bath towel (approx. 60×120 cm) — large towel for after bathing

USAGE:
In Japanese daily life, フェイスタオル is the most frequently used towel size. Many Japanese people prefer using a フェイスタオル even after bathing, rather than a full-sized バスタオル, as it dries faster and takes up less space in the laundry.
