1. crop rotation; rotating crops on the same field across seasons
輪作(りんさく)で土(つち)を守(まも)る。
Protect the soil through crop rotation.
輪作(りんさく)すると連作障害(れんさくしょうがい)を防(ふせ)ぐことができる。
Crop rotation can prevent continuous cropping damage.
この農家(のうか)では三年(さんねん)周期(しゅうき)の輪作(りんさく)を取(と)り入(い)れて、稲(いね)、大豆(だいず)、麦(むぎ)を順番(じゅんばん)に作(つく)っている。
This farm has adopted a three-year crop rotation cycle, growing rice, soybeans, and wheat in turn.

Composed of 輪(りん) (ring, cycle) + 作(さく) (cultivation, crop). The practice of growing different crops in sequence on the same field to maintain soil health and reduce pest problems.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 輪作(りんさく)体系(たいけい): crop rotation system
  • 三年(さんねん)輪作(りんさく): three-year crop rotation
  • 輪作(りんさく)周期(しゅうき): rotation cycle
  • 輪作(りんさく)を取(と)り入(い)れる: to adopt crop rotation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連作(れんさく): continuous cropping — growing the same crop repeatedly on the same land, the practice that crop rotation aims to avoid
  • 混作(こんさく): mixed cropping — growing different crops simultaneously on the same field
  • 二毛作(にもうさく): double cropping — growing two different crops in one year on the same field
