1. to return; to come back; to be reinstated
To return to a previous position, role, or state after a period of absence. Commonly used for returning to work after leave, an athlete coming back from injury, or territory being returned to a nation.
育休(いくきゅう)から職場(しょくば)に復帰(ふっき)する。
To return to the workplace after parental leave.
けがから復帰(ふっき)した選手(せんしゅ)がゴールを決(き)めた。
The player who came back from injury scored a goal.
長期(ちょうき)入院(にゅういん)を経(へ)て、ようやく社会(しゃかい)復帰(ふっき)を果(は)たした。
After a long hospital stay, he finally achieved his return to society.

Implies returning to a role or status that one previously held, after an interruption. Carries a sense of formal reinstatement rather than casual return.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 職場(しょくば)復帰(ふっき): return to the workplace
  • 社会(しゃかい)復帰(ふっき): return to society (after illness, incarceration, etc.)
  • 現役(げんえき)復帰(ふっき): return to active duty/service
  • 復帰(ふっき)戦(せん): comeback match
  • 本土(ほんど)復帰(ふっき): reversion to the mainland

HISTORICAL NOTE:
沖縄(おきなわ)返還(へんかん) (the reversion of Okinawa) in 1972 is often referred to as 本土(ほんど)復帰(ふっき), marking the return of Okinawa to Japanese sovereignty from American administration.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 戻(もど)る: to return, to go back — more general and everyday, while 復帰(ふっき) implies formal reinstatement to a previous role or status
  • 回復(かいふく)する: to recover — focuses on regaining health or a previous condition, while 復帰(ふっき) focuses on returning to a position or role
