1. to demote; to downgrade; to relegate
To lower someone or something in rank, status, or grade. Used in workplace hierarchies, sports leagues, and classification systems.
部長(ぶちょう)から課長(かちょう)に降格(こうかく)された。
He was demoted from department head to section chief.
チームが二部(にぶ)リーグに降格(こうかく)した。
The team was relegated to the second division.
成績(せいせき)不振(ふしん)を理由(りゆう)に降格(こうかく)処分(しょぶん)を受(う)けた。
He received a demotion due to poor performance.

Used both transitively (to demote someone) and intransitively (to be demoted / to drop in rank). In sports, equivalent to English "relegation" when a team drops to a lower division.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 降格(こうかく)処分(しょぶん): demotion (as disciplinary action)
  • 降格(こうかく)人事(じんじ): demotion in personnel decisions
  • 二軍(にぐん)に降格(こうかく)する: to be sent down to the minor league
  • 降格(こうかく)圏(けん): relegation zone (in sports leagues)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 昇格(しょうかく)する: to be promoted in rank — the antonym of 降格(こうかく), meaning to move up in status or division
  • 左遷(させん)する: to be transferred to a lesser position — implies a sideways move to a less desirable post as a de facto punishment, while 降格(こうかく) is an explicit rank reduction
  • 格下(かくさ)げする: to downgrade — more general, can apply to ratings, classifications, and evaluations, not just personnel
