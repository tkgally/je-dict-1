A formal word for structured discussion or debate, typically in institutional settings such as parliament, committees, or conferences. Composed of 討(とう) (attack, debate) and 議(ぎ) (deliberation). Implies a thorough examination of issues from multiple perspectives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 討議(とうぎ)する: to discuss, to deliberate
  • 討議(とうぎ)を行(おこな)う: to hold a discussion
  • 討議(とうぎ)事項(じこう): items for discussion, agenda
  • 自由(じゆう)討議(とうぎ): open discussion, free debate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん): argument, discussion — the most common word for debate, less formal
  • 討論(とうろん): debate — more adversarial, opposing sides argue their positions
  • 審議(しんぎ): deliberation — specifically examining and judging, used in parliament
  • 協議(きょうぎ): consultation, negotiation — aims to reach agreement
