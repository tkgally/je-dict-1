温(あたた)かみ

あたたかみ
noun
warmth; warm feeling
1. warmth; warm feeling; human touch
A quality of gentleness, kindness, or emotional warmth that a person, thing, or atmosphere possesses. Often refers to an intangible feeling of comfort or human connection.
手作(てづく)りの料理(りょうり)には温(あたた)かみがある。
Homemade cooking has a warm touch to it.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)には温(あたた)かみが感(かん)じられた。
I could feel warmth in his words.
木(き)の家具(かぐ)は金属(きんぞく)にはない温(あたた)かみがあって、部屋(へや)の雰囲気(ふんいき)が柔(やわ)らかくなる。
Wooden furniture has a warmth that metal lacks, and it softens the atmosphere of a room.

Formed from the i-adjective 温(あたた)かい + the suffix み, which converts adjectives into nouns expressing the quality or feeling of that adjective. Unlike 温(あたた)かさ (which measures degree of warmth), 温(あたた)かみ emphasizes the subjective, emotional quality — a sense of gentleness, comfort, or human touch.

Primarily used for emotional or aesthetic warmth rather than physical temperature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 温(あたた)かみがある: to have warmth; to feel warm
  • 温(あたた)かみを感(かん)じる: to feel warmth
  • 温(あたた)かみのある声(こえ): a warm voice
  • 温(あたた)かみのある色(いろ): a warm color
  • 人(ひと)の温(あたた)かみ: human warmth

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 温(あたた)かさ: warmth (degree) — more objective, can refer to physical temperature
  • 優(やさ)しさ: kindness; gentleness — focuses on character rather than atmosphere
  • ぬくもり: warmth; coziness — more physical and intimate, often body warmth or the warmth of being held
