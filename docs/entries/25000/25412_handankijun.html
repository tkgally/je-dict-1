判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)

はんだんきじゅん
noun
judgment criteria; standard of judgment
1. judgment criteria; standard of judgment; basis for deciding
The standards or criteria used to make a decision or form a judgment about something.
判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)を決(き)める。
We establish the judgment criteria.
何(なに)を判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)にすればいいですか。
What should I use as the criteria for my decision?
採用(さいよう)の判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)は会社(かいしゃ)によって大(おお)きく異(こと)なる。
The criteria for hiring decisions differ greatly from company to company.

判断基準(はんだんきじゅん) is a compound of 判断(はんだん) (judgment, decision) + 基準(きじゅん) (standard, criterion). It refers to the yardstick or benchmark by which decisions are made.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)を設(もう)ける: establish judgment criteria
  • 判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)に基(もと)づく: based on the judgment criteria
  • 明確(めいかく)な判断基準(はんだんきじゅん): clear criteria
  • 判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)を示(しめ)す: present the criteria

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 基準(きじゅん): standard; criterion — more general, not limited to decision-making
  • 評価基準(ひょうかきじゅん): evaluation criteria — specifically for assessing performance or quality
  • 目安(めやす): rough guide; benchmark — less formal and less precise than 判断基準(はんだんきじゅん)
