単純(たんじゅん)

たんじゅん
na-adjective
simple; uncomplicated; straightforward
1. simple; uncomplicated; straightforward
simple; uncomplicated; straightforward
単純(たんじゅん)な作業(さぎょう)を繰(く)り返(かえ)す。
I repeat simple tasks.
この問題(もんだい)は見(み)た目(め)ほど単純(たんじゅん)ではない。
This problem is not as simple as it looks.
構造(こうぞう)が単純(たんじゅん)なので、修理(しゅうり)も簡単(かんたん)にできる。
Since the structure is simple, repairs can be done easily too.
2. naive; simple-minded; gullible
naive; simple-minded; gullible
彼(かれ)は単純(たんじゅん)だからすぐ信(しん)じる。
He's naive, so he believes things right away.
そんな嘘(うそ)に騙(だま)されるなんて、単純(たんじゅん)すぎる。
Falling for a lie like that is too naive.
褒(ほ)められると単純(たんじゅん)に喜(よろこ)んでしまう性格(せいかく)だ。
I have a simple personality that just gets happy when praised.

Describes something lacking complexity, either in a neutral/positive sense (uncomplicated, straightforward) or in a negative sense (simplistic, naive). When applied to people, it often implies being easily influenced or gullible.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 単純(たんじゅん)な作業(さぎょう): simple task
  • 単純(たんじゅん)な構造(こうぞう): simple structure
  • 単純(たんじゅん)な計算(けいさん): simple calculation
  • 単純(たんじゅん)明快(めいかい): simple and clear
  • 単純(たんじゅん)労働(ろうどう): unskilled labor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 簡単(かんたん): easy — focuses on the ease of doing something, while 単純(たんじゅん) focuses on the lack of complexity in structure
  • 素朴(そぼく): simple; unsophisticated — carries a positive nuance of naturalness or lack of artifice
  • 安易(あんい): easy-going; simplistic — has a negative nuance of being overly casual or not thinking things through
