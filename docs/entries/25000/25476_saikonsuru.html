1. to remarry; to marry again
To marry again after a previous marriage has ended through divorce or the death of a spouse.
母(はは)は去年(きょねん)再婚(さいこん)した。
My mother remarried last year.
離婚(りこん)して三年後(さんねんご)に再婚(さいこん)した。
She remarried three years after her divorce.
子供(こども)がいる場合(ばあい)、再婚(さいこん)する際(さい)には家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)の気持(きも)ちを大切(たいせつ)にする必要(ひつよう)がある。
When there are children, it is necessary to consider the feelings of the whole family when remarrying.

A compound of 再(さい) (again; re-) and 婚(こん) (marriage). The noun form 再婚(さいこん) is equally common. In Japan, remarriage has become increasingly common and socially accepted, though it still carries some social considerations, especially when children from previous marriages are involved (連(つ)れ子(こ)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再婚(さいこん)相手(あいて): remarriage partner
  • 再婚(さいこん)家庭(かてい): remarried family; blended family
  • 再婚(さいこん)を考(かんが)える: to consider remarrying
  • バツイチで再婚(さいこん): to remarry after one divorce

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 結婚(けっこん)する: to marry — first or subsequent marriage; the general term
  • 離婚(りこん)する: to divorce — the end of a marriage; often mentioned alongside 再婚(さいこん)
  • 初婚(しょこん): first marriage — contrasts with 再婚(さいこん)
