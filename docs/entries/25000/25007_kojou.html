湖上(こじょう)

こじょう
noun
on the lake; over the lake
1. on the lake; over the lake; the lake surface
The surface of a lake or the area above it. Used to describe things happening on, over, or across a lake.
湖上(こじょう)に霧(きり)がかかっている。
Mist hangs over the lake.
湖上(こじょう)をボートで横切(よこぎ)った。
We crossed the lake by boat.
夕暮(ゆうぐ)れの湖上(こじょう)に浮(う)かぶ小舟(こぶね)の影(かげ)が、水面(すいめん)に映(うつ)る夕焼(ゆうや)けの光(ひかり)と重(かさ)なって美(うつく)しかった。
The silhouette of a small boat floating on the evening lake, overlapping with the sunset light reflected on the water's surface, was beautiful.

A compound of 湖(こ) (lake) and 上(じょう) (above, on). Follows the same pattern as other location compounds: 海上(かいじょう) (at sea), 山上(さんじょう) (on the mountain), 地上(ちじょう) (on the ground). Often appears in literary, scenic, and travel descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 湖上(こじょう)に浮(う)かぶ: to float on the lake
  • 湖上(こじょう)の風景(ふうけい): scenery on the lake
  • 湖上(こじょう)クルーズ: lake cruise
  • 湖上(こじょう)を渡(わた)る風(かぜ): wind blowing across the lake

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 海上(かいじょう): at sea; on the ocean — the equivalent term for the sea
  • 湖面(こめん): lake surface — specifically the water surface itself
  • 湖畔(こはん): lakeside; lakeshore — the land surrounding a lake, not on the water
