1. to flap; to flutter
To make repeated flapping or fluttering movements, as wings or fabric in the wind.
鳥(とり)が羽(はね)をぱたぱたさせた。
The bird flapped its wings.
旗(はた)が風(かぜ)でぱたぱたしている。
The flag is fluttering in the wind.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)がベランダでぱたぱたと音(おと)を立(た)てていた。
The laundry was making flapping sounds on the balcony.
2. to fan; to wave back and forth
To wave something back and forth rapidly, such as a fan or a piece of cloth.
暑(あつ)いのでうちわでぱたぱたした。
It was hot so I fanned myself with a fan.
新聞紙(しんぶんし)でぱたぱたと虫(むし)を追(お)い払(はら)った。
I shooed the bug away by waving a newspaper back and forth.
タオルをぱたぱたして埃(ほこり)を落(お)とした。
I shook out the towel to remove the dust.

Mimetic word representing the sound and motion of light, repeated flapping. The light voiceless ぱ conveys a crisp, light flapping sound, in contrast to the voiced ばたばた which suggests heavier, noisier movement.

USAGE:

  • ぱたぱたする — to flap, to fan
  • ぱたぱたと — adverbial use ("with a flapping motion/sound")
  • ぱたぱたさせる — to make something flap

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 羽(はね)をぱたぱたさせる: to flap wings
  • うちわでぱたぱたする: to fan with a round fan
  • 旗(はた)がぱたぱたする: a flag flutters

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ばたばた: heavier, more vigorous flapping — also means rushing around busily
  • ひらひら: light, graceful fluttering — used for thin, delicate things like petals or butterflies
  • はためく: to flutter, to flap — a standard verb for flags and banners
