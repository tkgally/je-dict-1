和食店(わしょくてん)

わしょくてん
noun
Japanese restaurant; Japanese food restaurant
1. Japanese restaurant; Japanese food restaurant
A restaurant that serves traditional Japanese cuisine.
和食店(わしょくてん)で食事(しょくじ)する。
To dine at a Japanese restaurant.
駅前(えきまえ)に新(あたら)しい和食店(わしょくてん)がオープンした。
A new Japanese restaurant opened in front of the station.
海外(かいがい)でも和食店(わしょくてん)の数(かず)が増(ふ)えており、日本食(にほんしょく)の人気(にんき)の高(たか)さがうかがえる。
The number of Japanese restaurants is increasing overseas as well, showing the high popularity of Japanese food.

A compound of 和食(わしょく) (Japanese cuisine) and 店(てん) (shop, restaurant). 和食(わしょく) was recognized as a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2013, and the term 和食店(わしょくてん) has become increasingly common both in Japan and abroad.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 和食店(わしょくてん)で食(た)べる: to eat at a Japanese restaurant
  • 高級(こうきゅう)和食店(わしょくてん): upscale Japanese restaurant
  • 和食店(わしょくてん)を開(ひら)く: to open a Japanese restaurant
  • 人気(にんき)の和食店(わしょくてん): popular Japanese restaurant

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日本(にほん)料理(りょうり)店(てん): Japanese cuisine restaurant — more formal, essentially synonymous
  • 料亭(りょうてい): traditional Japanese restaurant — upscale establishment with private rooms and full-course meals
  • 居酒屋(いざかや): izakaya; Japanese pub — a more casual drinking establishment that also serves food
  • 洋食店(ようしょくてん): Western-style restaurant — serving Western-influenced Japanese cuisine
