1. to burst; to pop; to split open
to break apart suddenly with force, scattering contents outward
豆(まめ)が弾(はじ)けた。
The beans popped open.
ポップコーンが次々(つぎつぎ)と弾(はじ)ける。
Popcorn kernels are popping one after another.
熟(じゅく)した実(み)が弾(はじ)けて種(たね)が飛(と)び散(ち)った。
The ripe fruit burst open and seeds scattered.
2. to be lively; to be bursting with energy
to overflow with energy, joy, or youthful vitality
笑顔(えがお)が弾(はじ)ける。
Smiles are beaming.
子供(こども)たちが弾(はじ)けるように遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing with bursting energy.
夏(なつ)の海(うみ)で弾(はじ)けるような若(わか)さを感(かん)じた。
I felt an explosive youthfulness at the summer beach.

An ichidan verb conveying the image of something bursting apart with energy. The physical sense of popping or splitting extends naturally to describe lively, radiant energy in people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ポップコーンが弾(はじ)ける: popcorn pops
  • 笑顔(えがお)が弾(はじ)ける: smiles burst forth
  • 弾(はじ)けるような笑(え)み: a radiant smile
  • 水(みず)しぶきが弾(はじ)ける: water splashes burst

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 弾(はじ)く: the transitive counterpart — to flick, to repel, to play (a stringed instrument)
  • 爆(は)ぜる: to pop or burst, especially from heat (e.g., chestnuts in a fire); more limited to physical bursting than 弾(はじ)ける
  • 割(わ)れる: to crack or break, but implies splitting into pieces rather than bursting outward

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive pair is 弾(はじ)く.
