室温(しつおん)

しつおん
noun
room temperature
1. room temperature
room temperature
室温(しつおん)は25度(ど)に設定(せってい)してある。
The room temperature is set to 25 degrees.
バターを室温(しつおん)に戻(もど)してから使(つか)ってください。
Please bring the butter to room temperature before using it.
冬(ふゆ)は室温(しつおん)が下(さ)がりやすいので、断熱(だんねつ)対策(たいさく)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
In winter, room temperature tends to drop, so insulation measures are important.

The temperature inside a room or enclosed space. Used in everyday contexts such as cooking instructions and home comfort, as well as scientific contexts where experiments are conducted at room temperature (typically around 20-25°C).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 室温(しつおん)に戻(もど)す: bring to room temperature
  • 室温(しつおん)で保存(ほぞん)する: store at room temperature
  • 室温(しつおん)を調整(ちょうせい)する: adjust the room temperature
  • 室温(しつおん)管理(かんり): room temperature management

RELATED TERMS:

  • 気温(きおん): air temperature — outdoor temperature
  • 体温(たいおん): body temperature
  • 水温(すいおん): water temperature
