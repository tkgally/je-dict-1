砂(すな)まみれ

すなまみれ
noun
covered in sand; sandy all over
1. covered in sand; sandy all over
the state of being thoroughly coated or smeared with sand
子供(こども)たちが砂(すな)まみれで遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing covered in sand.
砂浜(すなはま)で転(ころ)んで全身(ぜんしん)砂(すな)まみれになった。
I fell on the beach and got sand all over me.
砂(すな)まみれのユニフォームを洗濯機(せんたくき)に入(い)れる前(まえ)に、外(そと)ではたいて砂(すな)を落(お)とした。
Before putting the sand-covered uniform in the washing machine, I shook the sand off outside.

Uses the suffix まみれ, which attaches to nouns to mean 'covered in' or 'smeared with' something, typically something undesirable or messy. The pattern is noun}まみれ. Unlike {砂(すな)だらけ, which emphasizes that sand is everywhere in a location, 砂(すな)まみれ focuses on sand clinging to a person or object.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 砂(すな)まみれになる: to become covered in sand
  • 砂(すな)まみれの手(て): sand-covered hands
  • 砂(すな)まみれの服(ふく): sand-covered clothes

RELATED まみれ EXPRESSIONS:

  • 泥(どろ)まみれ: covered in mud
  • 血(ち)まみれ: covered in blood
  • 汗(あせ)まみれ: drenched in sweat
  • 借金(しゃっきん)まみれ: drowning in debt (figurative)
