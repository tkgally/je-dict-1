レンガ造(づく)り

れんがづくり
noun
brick construction; brick-built
1. brick construction; brick-built; made of brick
A building or structure constructed primarily from bricks, or the method of building with bricks.
レンガ造(づく)りの建物(たてもの)が並(なら)んでいる。
Brick buildings line the street.
横浜(よこはま)の赤(あか)レンガ倉庫(そうこ)はレンガ造(づく)りの代表的(だいひょうてき)な建築物(けんちくぶつ)だ。
The Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse is a representative example of brick construction.
明治時代(めいじじだい)に建(た)てられたレンガ造(づく)りの洋館(ようかん)が、今(いま)も歴史的(れきしてき)建造物(けんぞうぶつ)として大切(たいせつ)に保存(ほぞん)されている。
A Western-style brick building constructed in the Meiji era is still carefully preserved as a historic structure.

A compound of レンガ (brick) and 造(づく)り (construction, making). The ～造(づく)り suffix is a productive pattern in Japanese for describing construction types.

Brick buildings are not native to Japanese architecture but were introduced during the 明治時代(めいじじだい) (Meiji era) as part of Western-style modernization. Many iconic brick buildings from this period survive as cultural landmarks, such as 東京駅(とうきょうえき) (Tokyo Station) and the 横浜(よこはま)赤(あか)レンガ倉庫(そうこ) (Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レンガ造(づく)りの家(いえ): a brick house
  • レンガ造(づく)りの壁(かべ): a brick wall
  • レンガ造(づく)りの倉庫(そうこ): a brick warehouse
  • 赤(あか)レンガ造(づく)り: red brick construction

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 木造(もくぞう): wooden construction — the traditional building method in Japan
  • 鉄筋(てっきん)コンクリート造(づく)り: reinforced concrete construction — modern standard for buildings
  • 石造(せきぞう)り: stone construction — built from stone
