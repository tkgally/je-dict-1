1. to giggle; to snicker; to titter
to laugh quietly in a suppressed way, often behind one's hand
子供(こども)たちがくすくす笑(わら)っている。
The children are giggling.
彼女(かのじょ)は口(くち)を手(て)で隠(かく)しながらくすくす笑(わら)った。
She giggled while covering her mouth with her hand.
先生(せんせい)の言(い)い間違(まちが)いに、クラス中(じゅう)がくすくす笑(わら)いを漏(も)らした。
The whole class let out stifled giggles at the teacher's slip of the tongue.

Combines the onomatopoeia くすくす (the sound of stifled laughter) with 笑(わら)う (to laugh). Describes quiet, suppressed laughing — lighter and more restrained than outright laughter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • くすくす笑(わら)いを漏(も)らす: to let out a giggle
  • くすくす笑(わら)い出(だ)す: to start giggling
  • くすくす笑(わら)い声(ごえ): the sound of giggling

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • にやにや笑(わら)う: to grin — smiling in a knowing or smug way, without sound
  • げらげら笑(わら)う: to guffaw — loud, unrestrained laughter
  • くすっと笑(わら)う: to chuckle — a single brief, quiet laugh
  • 忍(しの)び笑(わら)い: stifled laugh — trying to hold back laughter

NOTE: くすくす can have a slightly negative nuance when it implies laughing at someone behind their back.
