1. to join a company; to enter a firm
To begin employment at a company, typically as a full-time hire.
来月(らいげつ)入社(にゅうしゃ)します。
I'm joining the company next month.
大学(だいがく)を卒業(そつぎょう)してすぐに大手(おおて)メーカーに入社(にゅうしゃ)した。
I joined a major manufacturer right after graduating from university.
彼女(かのじょ)は新卒(しんそつ)で入社(にゅうしゃ)してから十年(じゅうねん)、今(いま)では部長(ぶちょう)になっている。
She joined the company as a new graduate, and now, ten years later, she is a department head.

入社(にゅうしゃ)する refers specifically to joining a company (not a school, club, or other organization). It is closely tied to the Japanese employment system, where mass hiring of new graduates (新卒採用(しんそつさいよう)) occurs each April.

USAGE:
The company is marked with に: ～に入社(にゅうしゃ)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)に入社(にゅうしゃ)する: join a company
  • 新卒(しんそつ)で入社(にゅうしゃ)する: join as a new graduate
  • 中途(ちゅうと)入社(にゅうしゃ): mid-career hire
  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)試験(しけん): company entrance exam
  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)式(しき): company entrance ceremony

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 就職(しゅうしょく)する: to get a job — broader term for finding employment, not limited to companies
  • 転職(てんしょく)する: to change jobs — moving from one company to another
  • 退社(たいしゃ)する: to leave a company — the antonym; also means to leave the office for the day
