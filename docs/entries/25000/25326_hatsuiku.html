1. growth; development (physical)
growth; development (physical)
子供(こども)の発育(はついく)は早(はや)い。
Children's physical growth is fast.
栄養(えいよう)バランスのよい食事(しょくじ)が発育(はついく)には大切(たいせつ)だ。
A nutritionally balanced diet is important for growth.
定期(ていき)健診(けんしん)で赤(あか)ちゃんの発育(はついく)状態(じょうたい)を確認(かくにん)する。
The baby's growth is checked at regular health checkups.

Specifically refers to physical growth and development of the body, especially of children, infants, and organisms. Composed of 発(はつ) (emit, develop) and 育(いく) (grow, raise). Primarily used in medical, childcare, and biological contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発育(はついく)が良(よ)い/悪(わる)い: good/poor development
  • 発育(はついく)状態(じょうたい): growth condition
  • 発育(はついく)不良(ふりょう): poor growth, failure to thrive
  • 発育(はついく)段階(だんかい): stage of development

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 成長(せいちょう): growth — broader; includes physical, mental, and figurative growth
  • 発達(はったつ): development — emphasizes functional and mental development
  • 生育(せいいく): growth — used for plants and organisms in natural environments
