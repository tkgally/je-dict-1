1. to dismiss; to remove from office; to relieve of duties
To officially remove someone from a position of responsibility, typically a managerial, executive, or public role.
監督(かんとく)が解任(かいにん)された。
The manager was dismissed.
取締役(とりしまりやく)を解任(かいにん)する決議(けつぎ)が採択(さいたく)された。
A resolution to remove the director was adopted.
成績(せいせき)不振(ふしん)を理由(りゆう)に監督(かんとく)が解任(かいにん)されたが、ファンからは同情(どうじょう)の声(こえ)もあった。
The manager was dismissed due to poor results, but there were also sympathetic voices from the fans.

Composed of 解任(かいにん) (dismissal; removal from office) + する. A formal term used primarily in corporate governance and sports management. In news headlines, the passive form 解任(かいにん)される is very common, especially for sports managers and corporate executives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 監督(かんとく)を解任(かいにん)する: to dismiss a manager/coach
  • 取締役(とりしまりやく)を解任(かいにん)する: to remove a company director
  • 解任(かいにん)要求(ようきゅう): demand for dismissal
  • 解任(かいにん)動議(どうぎ): motion for removal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 解雇(かいこ)する: to fire; to dismiss — used for regular employees, while 解任(かいにん) is for people in appointed positions
  • 罷免(ひめん)する: to impeach and dismiss — more formal, used for government officials removed through official proceedings
  • 更迭(こうてつ)する: to replace; to reshuffle — emphasizes the replacement aspect rather than the removal
