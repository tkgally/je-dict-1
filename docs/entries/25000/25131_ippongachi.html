一本勝(いっぽんが)ち

いっぽんがち
noun
win by ippon; decisive victory (in martial arts)
1. win by ippon; decisive victory
a victory achieved by scoring a full point (ippon) in martial arts such as judo or kendo, awarded for a clean, decisive technique
見事(みごと)な一本勝(いっぽんが)ちだった。
It was a magnificent ippon victory.
決勝(けっしょう)で一本勝(いっぽんが)ちを収(おさ)めて優勝(ゆうしょう)した。
She won the championship with an ippon victory in the final.
彼(かれ)は大外刈(おおそとが)りで一本勝(いっぽんが)ちし、会場(かいじょう)を沸(わ)かせた。
He won by ippon with an osoto-gari throw, electrifying the venue.

Combines 一本(いっぽん) (one point; ippon) and 勝(か)ち (victory). In judo, an ippon is the highest score, awarded for a clean throw where the opponent lands largely on their back, a hold-down lasting 20 seconds, or a submission. In kendo, it is awarded for a valid strike to a scoring target. An 一本勝(いっぽんが)ち is considered the most decisive and admired way to win.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一本勝(いっぽんが)ちを収(おさ)める: to achieve an ippon victory
  • 一本勝(いっぽんが)ちで決(き)める: to settle it with an ippon
  • 豪快(ごうかい)な一本勝(いっぽんが)ち: a spectacular ippon victory
  • 開始(かいし)早々(そうそう)の一本勝(いっぽんが)ち: an ippon victory right from the start

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 判定勝(はんていが)ち: decision victory — winning by judges' scores rather than a clean point
  • 合(あ)わせ技(わざ): combined techniques — winning by accumulating two half-points (waza-ari)
  • 一本(いっぽん): ippon — the full point itself, not the act of winning by it
