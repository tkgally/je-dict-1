1. to emit; to discharge; to expel
To release or push out substances such as gases, waste, or pollutants from a system, body, or process.
工場(こうじょう)が有害(ゆうがい)物質(ぶっしつ)を排出(はいしゅつ)している。
The factory is discharging harmful substances.
CO2の排出量(はいしゅつりょう)を削減(さくげん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reduce CO2 emissions.
体(からだ)は汗(あせ)や尿(にょう)で老廃物(ろうはいぶつ)を排出(はいしゅつ)する。
The body expels waste products through sweat and urine.
排出(はいしゅつ)基準(きじゅん)を超(こ)えた企業(きぎょう)には罰則(ばっそく)がある。
There are penalties for companies that exceed emission standards.

A key term in environmental discussions. Most frequently encountered in the context of greenhouse gas emissions and pollution. Also used in biology for bodily excretion processes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 排出量(はいしゅつりょう): emission volume
  • 排出(はいしゅつ)基準(きじゅん): emission standards
  • 排出(はいしゅつ)規制(きせい): emission regulations
  • 排出(はいしゅつ)削減(さくげん): emission reduction
  • 温室(おんしつ)効果(こうか)ガスの排出(はいしゅつ): greenhouse gas emissions
  • 排出(はいしゅつ)ゼロ: zero emissions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 放出(ほうしゅつ)する: to release, to emit — broader; can include intentional releases of energy, heat, or goods
  • 排気(はいき)する: to exhaust (gas) — specifically for exhaust fumes from engines
  • 廃棄(はいき)する: to discard, to dispose of — focuses on discarding waste or unwanted items
