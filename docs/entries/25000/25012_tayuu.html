太夫(たゆう)

たゆう
noun
tayuu; high-ranking performer or courtesan
1. highest-ranking courtesan; oiran of the top rank
The most elite rank of courtesan in Edo-period pleasure quarters, distinguished by exceptional beauty, cultural refinement, and artistic accomplishments.
太夫(たゆう)は教養(きょうよう)と美貌(びぼう)を兼(か)ね備(そな)えていた。
The tayuu combined refinement and beauty.
吉原(よしわら)の太夫(たゆう)は最高位(さいこうい)の遊女(ゆうじょ)として特別(とくべつ)な待遇(たいぐう)を受(う)けた。
Tayuu of Yoshiwara received special treatment as the highest-ranking courtesans.
太夫(たゆう)になるには和歌(わか)や茶道(さどう)、書道(しょどう)など多(おお)くの芸事(げいごと)に通(つう)じていなければならなかった。
To become a tayuu, one had to be well-versed in many arts including waka poetry, tea ceremony, and calligraphy.
2. lead performer; master narrator or puppeteer
A title for high-ranking performers in traditional Japanese arts, especially the lead chanter in bunraku puppet theater and certain Noh performers.
文楽(ぶんらく)の太夫(たゆう)が語(かた)り始(はじ)めた。
The bunraku narrator began to chant.
人間国宝(にんげんこくほう)の太夫(たゆう)の語(かた)りは圧巻(あっかん)だった。
The performance of the narrator designated as a Living National Treasure was magnificent.

Bunraku is a performing art in which the tayuu narrates the story, the shamisen player accompanies, and the puppeteers manipulate the puppets — a trinity of arts.

An important term in Japanese cultural history with two distinct but related meanings. Both senses share the idea of being at the top of a hierarchy.

Also written as 大夫(たゆう). The alternate reading だゆう exists but たゆう is standard.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
The courtesan sense dates to the Edo period (1603-1868). 太夫(たゆう) were the elite of the pleasure quarters, particularly 吉原(よしわら) in Edo and 島原(しまばら) in Kyoto. Unlike lower-ranking courtesans, they could refuse clients and were celebrated for their cultural accomplishments. The 太夫(たゆう) rank gradually disappeared by the mid-Edo period as the 花魁(おいらん) system replaced it.

The performer sense remains alive in 文楽(ぶんらく) (bunraku puppet theater), where the 太夫(たゆう) is the chanter who narrates the entire story.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 花魁(おいらん): oiran — high-ranking courtesans who succeeded the 太夫(たゆう) system
  • 遊女(ゆうじょ): courtesan; prostitute — the general term for women in the pleasure quarters
  • 語(かた)り手(て): narrator — a general term for a storyteller in any context
