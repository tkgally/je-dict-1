1. to develop; to unfold; to expand (a business, argument, story)
to develop; to unfold; to expand (a business, argument, story)
事業(じぎょう)を海外(かいがい)に展開(てんかい)する。
I expand the business overseas.
議論(ぎろん)が予想外(よそうがい)の方向(ほうこう)に展開(てんかい)した。
The discussion developed in an unexpected direction.
物語(ものがたり)は後半(こうはん)からスピード感(かん)を増(ま)して展開(てんかい)していく。
The story unfolds with increasing pace from the second half.
2. to deploy; to spread out (troops, equipment)
to deploy; to spread out (troops, equipment)
部隊(ぶたい)を前線(ぜんせん)に展開(てんかい)した。
Troops were deployed to the front line.
救援(きゅうえん)チームが被災地(ひさいち)に展開(てんかい)した。
Relief teams deployed to the disaster area.
警察(けいさつ)は広(ひろ)い範囲(はんい)に捜査網(そうさもう)を展開(てんかい)した。
The police deployed their investigation network across a wide area.

A versatile verb describing the process of something spreading out, unfolding, or progressing. In business and narrative contexts, it means to develop or expand. In military or emergency contexts, it means to deploy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事業(じぎょう)を展開(てんかい)する: expand a business
  • 議論(ぎろん)を展開(てんかい)する: develop an argument
  • 活動(かつどう)を展開(てんかい)する: carry out activities
  • 物語(ものがたり)の展開(てんかい): the development of a story
  • 急(きゅう)展開(てんかい): sudden turn of events

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発展(はってん)する: to develop; to grow — emphasizes growth or progress toward maturity, often used for economies, relationships, or technologies
  • 拡大(かくだい)する: to expand; to enlarge — focuses on increasing in size or scale, such as markets or influence
  • 進展(しんてん)する: to make progress — emphasizes forward movement or advancement of a situation
