空色(そらいろ)

そらいろ
noun
sky blue; azure
1. sky blue; azure; light blue like the sky
A light, clear blue color resembling the sky on a sunny day. One of the traditional Japanese color names.
空色(そらいろ)のワンピースを着(き)た。
I wore a sky-blue dress.
壁(かべ)を空色(そらいろ)に塗(ぬ)り替(か)えた。
I repainted the wall sky blue.
窓(まど)から見(み)える空色(そらいろ)のグラデーションが美(うつく)しく、しばらく見(み)とれていた。
The gradation of sky blue visible from the window was so beautiful that I gazed at it for a while.

A compound of 空(そら) (sky) and 色(いろ) (color). One of the traditional Japanese color names (和色(わしょく)), referring to the clear light blue of a fair-weather sky. It can function as an adjective with の (e.g., 空色(そらいろ)の花(はな)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空色(そらいろ)の花(はな): sky-blue flowers
  • 空色(そらいろ)の目(め): sky-blue eyes
  • 空色(そらいろ)に染(そ)まる: to be dyed sky blue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 水色(みずいろ): light blue — very similar shade; 水色(みずいろ) is the more common everyday term for light blue
  • 青(あお): blue — the general term for blue; covers a wider range of shades
  • 紺色(こんいろ): dark blue; navy — a much darker blue than 空色(そらいろ)
  • 藍色(あいいろ): indigo — a deep blue-purple traditional color
