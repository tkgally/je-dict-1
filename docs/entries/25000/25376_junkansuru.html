1. to circulate; to cycle; to go around
To move in a continuous loop or circuit, or to pass through a recurring sequence and return to the starting point.
血液(けつえき)が体(からだ)を循環(じゅんかん)する。
Blood circulates through the body.
循環(じゅんかん)バスが駅(えき)と病院(びょういん)を結(むす)んでいる。
A loop bus connects the station and the hospital.
資源(しげん)を循環(じゅんかん)させる社会(しゃかい)を目指(めざ)す。
We aim for a society that recycles resources.
経済(けいざい)が{うまく|}循環(じゅんかん)するには、消費(しょうひ)と投資(とうし)のバランスが大切(たいせつ)だ。
For the economy to circulate well, a balance between consumption and investment is important.

Describes continuous, cyclical movement. Used across biology (blood circulation), transportation (loop routes), economics (circulation of money/goods), and environmental science (recycling). The noun 循環(じゅんかん) is used as frequently as the verb form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血液(けつえき)循環(じゅんかん): blood circulation
  • 循環(じゅんかん)バス: loop bus, shuttle bus
  • 循環(じゅんかん)型(がた)社会(しゃかい): recycling-oriented society
  • 悪(あく)循環(じゅんかん): vicious cycle
  • 好(こう)循環(じゅんかん): virtuous cycle
  • 自然(しぜん)循環(じゅんかん): natural cycle

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 回(まわ)る: to go around — more everyday; physical rotation or visiting places in turn
  • 流通(りゅうつう)する: to distribute, to circulate — focuses on goods or money moving through a market
  • 巡回(じゅんかい)する: to patrol, to make the rounds — implies visiting multiple points in order
