キッチンペーパー

きっちんぺーぱー
noun
kitchen paper towel; paper towel (for cooking)
1. kitchen paper towel; paper towel (for cooking)
キッチンペーパーで油(あぶら)を拭(ふ)く。
Wipe up the oil with a paper towel.
揚(あ)げ物(もの)はキッチンペーパーの上(うえ)に置(お)いて油(あぶら)を切(き)る。
Place fried food on kitchen paper to drain the oil.
豆腐(とうふ)をキッチンペーパーで包(つつ)んで電子(でんし)レンジで温(あたた)めると、簡単(かんたん)に水切(みずき)りができる。
If you wrap tofu in kitchen paper and microwave it, you can easily drain the water.

A wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) term. In English, these are usually called "paper towels," while "kitchen paper" is more common in British English. In Japan, キッチンペーパー specifically refers to absorbent paper used in cooking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • キッチンペーパーで拭(ふ)く: to wipe with kitchen paper
  • キッチンペーパーで油(あぶら)を切(き)る: to drain oil with kitchen paper
  • キッチンペーパーで水気(みずけ)を取(と)る: to pat dry with kitchen paper
  • キッチンペーパーで包(つつ)む: to wrap in kitchen paper

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ペーパータオル: paper towel — sometimes used interchangeably, but can also refer to hand-drying towels in restrooms
  • ティッシュ: tissue — thinner and used for wiping the face or nose, not for cooking
  • クッキングシート: cooking sheet, parchment paper — used for baking, not absorbing
