1. to recognize; to identify
To identify or distinguish something, especially through sensory or technological means.
顔(かお)認識(にんしき)でロックを解除(かいじょ)する。
To unlock with facial recognition.
パソコンが新(あたら)しいデバイスを認識(にんしき)しない。
The computer doesn't recognize the new device.
音声(おんせい)認識(にんしき)の精度(せいど)が年々(ねんねん)向上(こうじょう)している。
The accuracy of voice recognition improves year by year.
2. to perceive; to be aware of; to understand
To grasp or become conscious of a fact, situation, or problem.
問題(もんだい)の深刻(しんこく)さを認識(にんしき)する。
To recognize the seriousness of the problem.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の立場(たちば)を正(ただ)しく認識(にんしき)していない。
He doesn't properly understand his own position.
国民(こくみん)が環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)の重要性(じゅうようせい)を認識(にんしき)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important for citizens to be aware of the importance of environmental issues.

A versatile word used in both technical and abstract contexts. In computing and technology, it refers to identification and detection. In everyday and formal speech, it describes awareness and understanding of situations or facts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顔(かお)認識(にんしき): facial recognition
  • 音声(おんせい)認識(にんしき): voice recognition
  • 現状(げんじょう)認識(にんしき): understanding of the current situation
  • 認識(にんしき)が甘(あま)い: to underestimate; to have insufficient awareness
  • 認識(にんしき)を改(あらた)める: to revise one's understanding

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 理解(りかい)する: to understand — focuses on comprehension of meaning or content
  • 把握(はあく)する: to grasp — implies firm understanding of details and scope
  • 意識(いしき)する: to be conscious of — implies deliberate attention to something
