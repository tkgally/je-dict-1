内情(ないじょう)

ないじょう
noun
internal affairs; inside story; true circumstances
1. internal affairs; inside story; true circumstances
internal affairs; inside story; true circumstances
会社(かいしゃ)の内情(ないじょう)は複雑(ふくざつ)だ。
The company's internal affairs are complicated.
内情(ないじょう)を知(し)っている人(ひと)は少(すく)ない。
Few people know the true circumstances.
元社員(もとしゃいん)が組織(そしき)の内情(ないじょう)をメディアに暴露(ばくろ)した。
A former employee exposed the organization's internal affairs to the media.

Refers to the true state of affairs within an organization, household, or situation that is not publicly known. Composed of 内(ない) (inside) and 情(じょう) (circumstances). Often carries a nuance of hidden or behind-the-scenes reality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内情(ないじょう)を知(し)る: know the inside story
  • 内情(ないじょう)を暴露(ばくろ)する: expose internal affairs
  • 内情(ないじょう)に詳(くわ)しい: be well-informed about internal matters
  • 内情(ないじょう)を探(さぐ)る: probe the true circumstances

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 事情(じじょう): circumstances — more neutral, not necessarily hidden
  • 内幕(うちまく): the inside story — emphasizes what goes on behind the scenes
  • 実情(じつじょう): actual conditions — focuses on the real situation vs. appearances
