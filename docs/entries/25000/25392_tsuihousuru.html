1. to banish; to exile; to expel
To force someone to leave a place, group, or country, typically as a punishment or for political reasons. Also used for eliminating undesirable things from society.
国(くに)から追放(ついほう)された。
He was banished from the country.
その選手(せんしゅ)はドーピングで大会(たいかい)から追放(ついほう)された。
The athlete was expelled from the competition for doping.
戦後(せんご)、多(おお)くの政治家(せいじか)が公職(こうしょく)から追放(ついほう)された。
After the war, many politicians were purged from public office.

A formal word often used in historical, political, and legal contexts. Describes forceful removal from a position, organization, or territory.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公職(こうしょく)追放(ついほう): purge from public office
  • 国外(こくがい)追放(ついほう): deportation, exile abroad
  • 永久(えいきゅう)追放(ついほう): permanent banishment
  • 追放(ついほう)処分(しょぶん): banishment/expulsion as punishment

HISTORICAL NOTE:
A historically significant term in Japan, most notably associated with the postwar 公職(こうしょく)追放(ついほう) (public office purge) carried out during the Allied Occupation, which removed wartime leaders from positions of power.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 除名(じょめい)する: to expel (from membership) — specifically removing someone's name from a roster or organization
  • 追(お)い出(だ)す: to drive out, to kick out — more colloquial and physical, while 追放(ついほう) is formal and often institutional
