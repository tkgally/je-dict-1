1. information sharing; sharing of information
The act or practice of making information available to multiple people within a group, team, or organization so that everyone has access to the same knowledge.
チーム内(ない)で情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)する。
We share information within the team.
情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)が不足(ふそく)しているとミスが増(ふ)える。
When information sharing is insufficient, mistakes increase.
部署間(ぶしょかん)の情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)を円滑(えんかつ)にするため、週(しゅう)に一度(いちど)会議(かいぎ)を開(ひら)いている。
To facilitate information sharing between departments, we hold a meeting once a week.

A compound of 情報(じょうほう) (information) and 共有(きょうゆう) (sharing; joint possession). This is a staple term in Japanese workplaces, especially in discussions about teamwork, project management, and organizational efficiency. It can function as a suru-verb: 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)する.

In modern workplaces, 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう) is seen as essential for preventing errors and maintaining smooth operations. Digital tools like Slack, Teams, and shared documents are often described as 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)ツール.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)する: to share information
  • 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)ツール: information sharing tool
  • 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)の場(ば): a forum for sharing information
  • 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)が大切(たいせつ): information sharing is important
  • 情報(じょうほう)共有(きょうゆう)を徹底(てってい)する: to ensure thorough information sharing

RELATED TERMS:

  • 報連相(ほうれんそう): report-contact-consult — the Japanese business acronym for essential communication practices
  • 共有(きょうゆう): sharing — the general term, used for files, spaces, and resources as well
  • 伝達(でんたつ): communication; transmission — one-directional passing of information, unlike mutual sharing
