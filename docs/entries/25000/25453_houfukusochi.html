報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)

ほうふくそち
noun
retaliatory measures; reprisal
1. retaliatory measures; reprisal; retaliation
Actions taken in response to hostile or harmful actions by another party, especially between nations or organizations.
報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)を取(と)る。
To take retaliatory measures.
報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)として関税(かんぜい)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げた。
Tariffs were raised as a retaliatory measure.
両国(りょうこく)の報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)の応酬(おうしゅう)が貿易(ぼうえき)戦争(せんそう)に発展(はってん)する恐(おそ)れがある。
There is a risk that the exchange of retaliatory measures between the two countries will escalate into a trade war.

A compound of 報復(ほうふく) (retaliation, revenge) and 措置(そち) (measure, action). Frequently used in news reporting on international relations, trade disputes, and diplomatic conflicts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)を取(と)る: to take retaliatory measures
  • 報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)を発動(はつどう)する: to invoke retaliatory measures
  • 報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)として: as a retaliatory measure
  • 報復(ほうふく)措置(そち)の応酬(おうしゅう): exchange of retaliatory measures

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 対抗(たいこう)措置(そち): countermeasure — a response that may or may not be retaliatory
  • 制裁(せいさい): sanctions — punitive measures, often multilateral
  • 報復(ほうふく)関税(かんぜい): retaliatory tariff — a specific type of retaliatory measure in trade
