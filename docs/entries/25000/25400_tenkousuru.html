1. to transfer schools; to change schools
To leave one school and enroll at another, typically due to the family moving to a new area.
来月(らいげつ)転校(てんこう)することになった。
I'm going to transfer schools next month.
父(ちち)の転勤(てんきん)で、三回(さんかい)転校(てんこう)した。
I transferred schools three times because of my father's job transfers.
転校(てんこう)してきたばかりで、まだ友達(ともだち)がいない。
I've only just transferred here, so I don't have any friends yet.

A common word in school life, often appearing in anime, manga, and fiction where the 転校生(てんこうせい) (transfer student) is a classic character trope. In real life, school transfers are frequently caused by a parent's 転勤(てんきん) (job transfer).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転校生(てんこうせい): transfer student
  • 転校(てんこう)手続(てつづ)き: school transfer procedures
  • 転校(てんこう)届(とど)け: school transfer notification
  • 転校(てんこう)してくる: to transfer to (this school)
  • 転校(てんこう)していく: to transfer away (from this school)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 転入(てんにゅう)する: to transfer in (to a new school)
  • 転出(てんしゅつ)する: to transfer out (of the current school)
  • 編入(へんにゅう)する: to transfer mid-course (often at university level)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 転勤(てんきん)する: to be transferred (at work) — applies to job transfers, not school transfers, though 転勤(てんきん) is often the cause of 転校(てんこう)
