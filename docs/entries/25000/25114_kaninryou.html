缶飲料(かんいんりょう)

かんいんりょう
noun
canned drink; canned beverage
1. canned drink; canned beverage
any beverage sold in a metal can, including soft drinks, coffee, tea, beer, and juice
缶飲料(かんいんりょう)を買(か)った。
I bought a canned drink.
自動販売機(じどうはんばいき)で缶飲料(かんいんりょう)が売(う)られている。
Canned drinks are sold in vending machines.
日本(にほん)では缶飲料(かんいんりょう)の種類(しゅるい)が非常(ひじょう)に多(おお)く、コーヒーやお茶(ちゃ)も缶(かん)で手軽(てがる)に買(か)える。
In Japan, there is an enormous variety of canned drinks — even coffee and tea can be easily bought in cans.

Combines 缶(かん) (can, tin) and 飲料(いんりょう) (beverage, drink). Japan has a particularly rich canned beverage culture, with vending machines offering everything from hot and cold coffee to soup and tea.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 缶飲料(かんいんりょう)の自動販売機(じどうはんばいき): canned drink vending machine
  • 缶飲料(かんいんりょう)を冷(ひ)やす: to chill a canned drink
  • 缶飲料(かんいんりょう)の空(あ)き缶(かん): empty canned drink container

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 缶(かん)ジュース: canned juice — casual term often used for any canned soft drink, not just juice
  • 缶(かん)コーヒー: canned coffee — a specific and very popular category in Japan
  • ペットボトル飲料(いんりょう): PET bottle drink — beverages in plastic bottles rather than cans
  • 瓶(びん)飲料(いんりょう): bottled drink — beverages in glass bottles
