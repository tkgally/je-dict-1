1. collapse; destruction (of a building or structure)
collapse; destruction (of a building or structure)
地震(じしん)で建物(たてもの)が倒壊(とうかい)した。
The building collapsed in the earthquake.
倒壊(とうかい)した家屋(かおく)から生存者(せいぞんしゃ)が救出(きゅうしゅつ)された。
Survivors were rescued from the collapsed houses.
老朽化(ろうきゅうか)した橋(はし)が倒壊(とうかい)する危険性(きけんせい)があると専門家(せんもんか)が警告(けいこく)した。
Experts warned that the aging bridge is in danger of collapsing.

Specifically describes the collapse or destruction of buildings and structures. Used extensively in disaster reporting, particularly for earthquakes and typhoons. Composed of 倒(とう) (topple) and 壊(かい) (destroy).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 建物(たてもの)が倒壊(とうかい)する: a building collapses
  • 全壊(ぜんかい)/半壊(はんかい): total/partial collapse
  • 倒壊(とうかい)の危険(きけん): danger of collapse
  • 倒壊(とうかい)家屋(かおく): collapsed buildings

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 崩壊(ほうかい): collapse, breakdown — broader; used for systems, regimes, structures
  • 崩落(ほうらく): cave-in, collapse — used for cliffs, tunnels, roofs falling in
  • 倒れる(たおれる): fall over — more general; people or objects falling
