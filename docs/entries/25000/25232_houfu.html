豊富(ほうふ)

ほうふ
na-adjective
abundant; plentiful; rich (in resources, experience, variety)
1. abundant; plentiful; rich (in resources, experience, variety)
abundant; plentiful; rich (in resources, experience, variety)
この地域(ちいき)は水資源(みずしげん)が豊富(ほうふ)だ。
This region is rich in water resources.
経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ)なスタッフが対応(たいおう)します。
Experienced staff will assist you.
メニューの種類(しゅるい)が豊富(ほうふ)で、何(なに)を注文(ちゅうもん)するか迷(まよ)ってしまう。
The menu has such a wide variety that it's hard to decide what to order.
野菜(やさい)にはビタミンやミネラルが豊富(ほうふ)に含(ふく)まれている。
Vegetables are rich in vitamins and minerals.

Describes having a large amount or wide variety of something desirable. Always carries a positive nuance — something is not just plentiful but valuably so. Often used with に as an adverb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ): rich in experience
  • 知識(ちしき)が豊富(ほうふ): knowledgeable
  • 資源(しげん)が豊富(ほうふ): rich in resources
  • 栄養(えいよう)が豊富(ほうふ): nutritious; nutrient-rich
  • 品揃(しなぞろ)えが豊富(ほうふ): wide selection (of products)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 豊(ゆた)か: abundant; rich — a broader term also used for emotional or cultural richness, while 豊富(ほうふ) focuses on quantity or variety
  • 多(おお)い: many; much — a neutral quantitative word without the positive nuance of 豊富(ほうふ)
  • 潤沢(じゅんたく): ample; in abundance — a more formal or literary word, often used for financial resources or supplies
