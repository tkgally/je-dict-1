製鉄所(せいてつじょ)

せいてつじょ
noun
ironworks; steel mill; steelworks
1. ironworks; steel mill; steelworks
a factory where iron ore is smelted and processed into iron or steel
この町(まち)には大(おお)きな製鉄所(せいてつじょ)がある。
There is a large steel mill in this town.
製鉄所(せいてつじょ)から白(しろ)い煙(けむり)が上(あ)がっている。
White smoke is rising from the steelworks.
祖父(そふ)は若(わか)いころ北九州(きたきゅうしゅう)の製鉄所(せいてつじょ)で働(はたら)いていたそうだ。
I hear my grandfather worked at a steelworks in Kitakyushu when he was young.

Composed of 製鉄(せいてつ) (steelmaking, iron production) + 所(じょ) (place, facility). Japan has a long history of steel production, with major steelworks in cities like 北九州(きたきゅうしゅう) (八幡製鉄所(やはたせいてつじょ)) and 室蘭(むろらん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 製鉄所(せいてつじょ)で働(はたら)く: to work at a steel mill
  • 製鉄所(せいてつじょ)の跡地(あとち): former site of a steelworks
  • 高炉(こうろ): blast furnace (key equipment in a steelworks)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 製鋼所(せいこうじょ): steel refinery — focuses on the steel refining process specifically
  • 鋳造所(ちゅうぞうじょ): foundry — a facility for casting metals
  • 工場(こうじょう): factory — general term for any manufacturing facility

CULTURAL NOTE:
The 八幡製鉄所(やはたせいてつじょ) in 北九州(きたきゅうしゅう) was Japan's first modern steel mill, established in 1901. It played a central role in Japan's industrialization and is now part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
