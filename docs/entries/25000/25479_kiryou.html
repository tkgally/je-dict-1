器量(きりょう)

きりょう
noun
looks; facial features; ability; caliber
1. looks; facial features; appearance
Physical attractiveness, especially of the face. Traditionally used more often for women's looks.
器量(きりょう)のいい人(ひと)だ。
She is a good-looking person.
器量(きりょう)だけで人(ひと)を判断(はんだん)してはいけない。
You shouldn't judge people by their looks alone.
昔(むかし)は女性(じょせい)の価値(かち)が器量(きりょう)で決(き)まると言(い)われた時代(じだい)もあった。
There was a time when a woman's worth was said to be determined by her looks.
2. ability; caliber; capacity
The ability or capacity to handle important tasks or leadership. Refers to someone's overall competence and character.
彼(かれ)にはリーダーとしての器量(きりょう)がある。
He has the caliber to be a leader.
社長(しゃちょう)の器量(きりょう)が問(と)われている。
The president's ability is being called into question.
大(おお)きな組織(そしき)をまとめるには、それなりの器量(きりょう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A certain level of caliber is needed to lead a large organization.

A compound of 器(き) (vessel; capacity) and 量(りょう) (measure; amount). The two senses are quite different: sense 1 (looks) is somewhat old-fashioned and more commonly applied to women; sense 2 (ability) applies to anyone and is still actively used in business and leadership contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 器量(きりょう)がいい: good-looking (sense 1); capable (sense 2)
  • 器量(きりょう)が悪(わる)い: plain-looking; unattractive
  • 器量(きりょう)が問(と)われる: one's caliber is tested
  • 器量(きりょう)負(ま)け: a woman whose looks outshine her other qualities

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 容姿(ようし): appearance; looks — more neutral and modern term for physical appearance
  • 器(うつわ): caliber; vessel — the character standing alone; used metaphorically for a person's capacity (器(うつわ)が大(おお)きい = magnanimous)
  • 才能(さいのう): talent; ability — focuses on specific skills rather than overall character and leadership capacity
