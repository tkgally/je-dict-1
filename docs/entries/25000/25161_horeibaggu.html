保冷(ほれい)バッグ

ほれいばっぐ
noun
cooler bag; insulated bag
1. cooler bag; insulated bag
A bag with insulated lining designed to keep food and drinks cool during transport.
保冷(ほれい)バッグに飲(の)み物(もの)を入(い)れた。
I put drinks in the cooler bag.
ピクニックに保冷(ほれい)バッグを持(も)っていく。
I'll bring a cooler bag to the picnic.
夏場(なつば)の買(か)い物(もの)では、生鮮食品(せいせんしょくひん)を傷(いた)めないように保冷(ほれい)バッグと保冷剤(ほれいざい)を持参(じさん)している。
When shopping in summer, I bring a cooler bag and ice packs to keep fresh foods from spoiling.

A compound of 保冷(ほれい) (keeping cool, cold storage) and バッグ (bag). A practical everyday item in Japan, especially popular during the hot summer months for grocery shopping and outdoor activities.

Many Japanese supermarkets sell or provide 保冷(ほれい)バッグ, and eco-conscious shoppers often bring their own reusable ones. They are also essential for 弁当(べんとう) transport in warm weather.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保冷(ほれい)バッグに入(い)れる: to put in a cooler bag
  • 折(お)りたたみ保冷(ほれい)バッグ: foldable cooler bag
  • 大容量(だいようりょう)保冷(ほれい)バッグ: large-capacity cooler bag
  • 保冷(ほれい)バッグと保冷剤(ほれいざい): cooler bag and ice packs

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • クーラーボックス: cooler box — a rigid, larger container for keeping items cold
  • 保冷剤(ほれいざい): ice pack, cold pack — the gel packs placed inside cooler bags
  • エコバッグ: eco bag, reusable shopping bag — a regular shopping bag without insulation
