物品(ぶっぴん)

ぶっぴん
noun
goods; articles; commodities
1. goods; articles; commodities; items
A formal term for physical objects or merchandise. Used in administrative, legal, and business contexts.
救援(きゅうえん)物品(ぶっぴん)を届(とど)ける。
To deliver relief supplies.
会社(かいしゃ)の物品(ぶっぴん)を私的(してき)に使用(しよう)してはいけない。
You must not use company property for personal purposes.
被災地(ひさいち)に食料(しょくりょう)や衣類(いるい)などの物品(ぶっぴん)を送(おく)るボランティアに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in volunteer work sending supplies like food and clothing to the disaster-stricken area.

A compound of 物(ぶつ) (thing; object) and 品(ぴん) (article; item). A formal word used in official, administrative, and business contexts. In everyday conversation, 物(もの) or 品物(しなもの) is more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 救援(きゅうえん)物品(ぶっぴん): relief supplies
  • 支援(しえん)物品(ぶっぴん): support goods; aid supplies
  • 物品(ぶっぴん)管理(かんり): inventory management; property management
  • 物品(ぶっぴん)購入(こうにゅう): purchasing goods
  • 物品(ぶっぴん)税(ぜい): commodity tax

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 品物(しなもの): goods; merchandise — everyday term for items, especially in shopping
  • 物(もの): thing; object — the most general, everyday word
  • 商品(しょうひん): merchandise; product — specifically items for sale
  • 備品(びひん): equipment; fixtures — items belonging to an organization, kept for regular use
