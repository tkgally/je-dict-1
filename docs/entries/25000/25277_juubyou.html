重病(じゅうびょう)

じゅうびょう
noun
serious illness; grave disease
1. serious illness; grave disease
a severe or life-threatening illness or medical condition
父(ちち)は重病(じゅうびょう)にかかった。
My father fell seriously ill.
重病(じゅうびょう)患者(かんじゃ)は集中治療室(しゅうちゅうちりょうしつ)に入(い)れられた。
The critically ill patient was placed in the intensive care unit.
重病(じゅうびょう)を克服(こくふく)して社会(しゃかい)復帰(ふっき)を果(は)たした彼女(かのじょ)の姿(すがた)に感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by the sight of her overcoming a serious illness and returning to society.

Composed of 重(じゅう) (heavy, serious) + 病(びょう) (illness). Describes a condition that is severe enough to be life-threatening or to require intensive medical care.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重病(じゅうびょう)にかかる: to fall seriously ill
  • 重病(じゅうびょう)患者(かんじゃ): critically ill patient
  • 重病(じゅうびょう)を患(わずら)う: to suffer from a serious illness
  • 重病(じゅうびょう)を克服(こくふく)する: to overcome a serious illness
  • 重病(じゅうびょう)から回復(かいふく)する: to recover from a serious illness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重症(じゅうしょう): serious condition — describes the severity of symptoms or injuries, not the disease itself
  • 大病(たいびょう): major illness — similar meaning but slightly less formal; emphasizes the scale of the illness
  • 難病(なんびょう): intractable disease — a disease that is difficult to cure, regardless of current severity
