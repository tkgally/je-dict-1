身(み)を捧(ささ)げる

みをささげる
expression
to devote oneself; to dedicate oneself
1. to devote oneself; to dedicate oneself (to a cause or pursuit)
To commit one's entire being to a purpose, cause, or activity, often at the expense of personal comfort or desires.
研究(けんきゅう)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げる。
I devote myself to research.
彼女(かのじょ)は音楽(おんがく)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げてきた。
She has devoted herself to music.
医療(いりょう)の発展(はってん)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げた医師(いし)として知(し)られている。
He is known as a doctor who devoted himself to the advancement of medicine.
2. to sacrifice oneself; to give one's life
To give up one's life or safety for a cause, person, or ideal.
国(くに)のために身(み)を捧(ささ)げた。
They sacrificed themselves for their country.
仲間(なかま)を守(まも)るために身(み)を捧(ささ)げる覚悟(かくご)がある。
I am prepared to sacrifice myself to protect my comrades.
平和(へいわ)のために身(み)を捧(ささ)げた人(ひと)々の犠牲(ぎせい)を忘(わす)れてはならない。
We must not forget the sacrifice of those who gave their lives for peace.

身(み)を捧(ささ)げる literally means "to offer one's body/self" (身(み) = body/self, 捧(ささ)げる = to offer/dedicate). It is a literary and somewhat elevated expression conveying deep commitment or sacrifice.

USAGE:
The cause or pursuit is marked with に: ～に身(み)を捧(ささ)げる. Sense 1 (devotion) is more common in modern usage than sense 2 (sacrifice).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げる: devote oneself to one's work
  • 愛(あい)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げる: devote oneself to love
  • 神(かみ)に身(み)を捧(ささ)げる: dedicate oneself to God
  • 国(くに)のために身(み)を捧(ささ)げる: sacrifice oneself for one's country

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一生(いっしょう)を捧(ささ)げる: to devote one's whole life — emphasizes the lifetime duration
  • 打(う)ち込(こ)む: to devote oneself to; to throw oneself into — more colloquial and active
  • 尽(つ)くす: to serve; to exhaust oneself for — focuses on giving one's all in service
