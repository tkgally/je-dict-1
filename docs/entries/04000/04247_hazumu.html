1. to bounce; to spring
For a ball or elastic object to rebound when striking a surface.
ボールがよく弾(はず)む。
The ball bounces well.
ゴムのボールは高(たか)く弾(はず)む。
A rubber ball bounces high.
2. to be lively; to be animated
For conversation, mood, or breathing to become energetic or excited.
会話(かいわ)が弾(はず)んで楽(たの)しかった。
The conversation was lively and fun.
子供(こども)たちが弾(はず)んだ声(こえ)で話(はな)している。
The children are talking in lively voices.
走(はし)った後(あと)で息(いき)が弾(はず)んでいた。
I was out of breath after running.
久(ひさ)しぶりに会(あ)ったので話(はなし)が弾(はず)んだ。
Since we hadn't seen each other in a while, the conversation was lively.
旅行(りょこう)のことを考(かんが)えると心(こころ)が弾(はず)む。
My heart leaps when I think about the trip.
3. to be generous; to splurge
To be unusually generous with money, tips, or gifts.
今日(きょう)は給料日(きゅうりょうび)だから、チップを弾(はず)んだ。
Since it's payday, I gave a generous tip.
お祝(いわ)いだからと言(い)って、いつもより弾(はず)んでくれた。
Saying it was a celebration, he was more generous than usual.

弾(はず)む is primarily an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) but can be transitive in sense 3.

ASPECT (ている):
弾(はず)んでいる indicates something is bouncing or a lively state continues.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]が弾(はず)む — [object] bounces
  • [conversation/mood]が弾(はず)む — [conversation/mood] becomes lively
  • [tip/money]を弾(はず)む — to be generous with [money]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ボールが弾(はず)む (ball bounces)
  • 会話(かいわ)が弾(はず)む (conversation becomes animated)
  • 息(いき)が弾(はず)む (breathing becomes heavy from exertion/excitement)
  • 心(こころ)が弾(はず)む (heart leaps with excitement)

IDIOMATIC EXPRESSION:
息(いき)を弾(はず)ませて (声(こえ)を弾(はず)ませて) means "breathlessly" or "with an excited voice."

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 跳(は)ねる: to jump, leap (more active)
  • 弾(はず)む: to bounce, be lively (includes liveliness)
