幾分(いくぶん)

いくぶん
adverb, noun
somewhat, to some extent
1. somewhat, to some extent, a little
To a moderate or limited degree; not completely but noticeably.
2. some portion, a part
A portion or share of something (less common).
今日(きょう)は幾分(いくぶん)暖(あたた)かい。
It's somewhat warm today.
薬(くすり)を飲(の)んで幾分(いくぶん)楽(らく)になった。
I feel somewhat better after taking the medicine.
彼女(かのじょ)の態度(たいど)は幾分(いくぶん)冷(つめ)たかった。
Her attitude was somewhat cold.

USAGE: 幾分(いくぶん) is a slightly formal adverb indicating a moderate degree. It's softer than 少(すこ)し and more formal.

POSITION: Usually appears before the word it modifies.

REGISTER: Slightly formal; more common in written language.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 少(すこ)し - a little (more casual, common)
  • やや - slightly (similar formality)
  • 多少(たしょう) - more or less, somewhat
  • いくらか - somewhat (similar meaning)
