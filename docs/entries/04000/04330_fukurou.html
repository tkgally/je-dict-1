梟(ふくろう)

ふくろう
noun
owl
1. owl
A nocturnal bird of prey with large forward-facing eyes, a flat face, and silent flight. Owls are associated with wisdom and good luck in Japan.
夜(よる)になると梟(ふくろう)の鳴(な)き声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえる。
When night falls, you can hear the hooting of owls.
梟(ふくろう)は首(くび)を大(おお)きく回(まわ)すことができる。
Owls can rotate their heads extensively.
お土産(みやげ)に梟(ふくろう)の置物(おきもの)を買(か)った。
I bought an owl figurine as a souvenir.

梟(ふくろう) refers to owls.

Alternative writing: フクロウ (katakana, common)

Common expressions:

  • 梟(ふくろう)カフェ (owl café)
  • 梟(ふくろう)の置物(おきもの) (owl figurine)

Counter: 羽(わ)

Cultural note:

In Japan, 梟(ふくろう) is considered a lucky symbol. The word can be written with different kanji to create puns:

  • 不苦労(ふくろう) ("no hardship")
  • 福来郎(ふくろう) ("good fortune comes")

Owl figurines and goods are popular as good luck charms. Owl cafés where visitors can interact with owls have become tourist attractions in Japan.
