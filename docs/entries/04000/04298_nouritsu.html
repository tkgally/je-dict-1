能率(のうりつ)

のうりつ
noun
efficiency
1. efficiency, productivity
The ratio of output to input, or the effectiveness of achieving results with minimal wasted effort or resources.
仕事(しごと)の能率(のうりつ)を上(あ)げる方法(ほうほう)を考(かんが)えている。
I'm thinking of ways to increase work efficiency.
このシステムは能率(のうりつ)が良(い)い。
This system is efficient.
能率(のうりつ)的(てき)に働(はたら)くことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to work efficiently.

USAGE: 能率(のうりつ) focuses on productivity and effectiveness, especially in work contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 能率(のうりつ)が良(い)い/悪(わる)い (efficient/inefficient)
  • 能率(のうりつ)を上(あ)げる (to increase efficiency)
  • 能率(のうりつ)が上(あ)がる (efficiency increases)
  • 能率(のうりつ)的(てき)に (efficiently)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 能率(のうりつ)向上(こうじょう) (efficiency improvement)
  • 作業(さぎょう)能率(のうりつ) (work efficiency)
  • 生産(せいさん)能率(のうりつ) (production efficiency)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 効率(こうりつ) - efficiency (more technical, mathematical)
  • 生産性(せいさんせい) - productivity
