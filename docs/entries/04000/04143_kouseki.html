功績(こうせき)

こうせき
noun
achievements, merits, distinguished service
1. achievements, merits, distinguished service, accomplishments
Notable accomplishments or contributions that deserve recognition.
彼(かれ)の功績(こうせき)は高(たか)く評価(ひょうか)されている。
His achievements are highly valued.
科学(かがく)への功績(こうせき)でノーベル賞(しょう)を受賞(じゅしょう)した。
He received the Nobel Prize for his contributions to science.
会社(かいしゃ)の発展(はってん)に大(おお)きな功績(こうせき)がある。
He has made great contributions to the company's development.

功績(こうせき) refers to notable achievements, accomplishments, or contributions deserving recognition.

WORD FORMATION:
功(こう) (merit) + 績(せき) (achievements) = meritorious achievements

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 功績(こうせき)を残(のこ)す: to leave behind achievements
  • 功績(こうせき)を認(みと)める: to acknowledge achievements
  • 功績(こうせき)を称(たた)える: to praise achievements
  • 偉大(いだい)な功績(こうせき): great achievements
  • 功績(こうせき)を挙(あ)げる: to make contributions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 業績(ぎょうせき): work achievements, performance
  • 実績(じっせき): actual results, track record
  • 貢献(こうけん): contribution
