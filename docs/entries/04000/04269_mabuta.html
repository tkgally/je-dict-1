瞼(まぶた)

まぶた
noun
eyelid
1. eyelid
The movable fold of skin that covers and protects the eye.
瞼(まぶた)が重(おも)くなってきた。
My eyelids are getting heavy.
瞼(まぶた)を閉(と)じて深呼吸(しんこきゅう)した。
I closed my eyes and took a deep breath.
一重(ひとえ)瞼(まぶた)と二重(ふたえ)瞼(まぶた)、どちらが好(す)きですか。
Which do you prefer, single or double eyelids?

瞼(まぶた) (eyelid) is commonly written in hiragana as まぶた.

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 瞼(まぶた) is complex and often replaced with hiragana.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 瞼(まぶた)が重(おも)い (eyelids are heavy - sleepy)
  • 瞼(まぶた)を閉(と)じる (close one's eyes)
  • 瞼(まぶた)を開(ひら)く (open one's eyes)
  • 一重(ひとえ)瞼(まぶた) (single eyelid - monolid)
  • 二重(ふたえ)瞼(まぶた) (double eyelid)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, 一重(ひとえ) (single eyelid) vs 二重(ふたえ) (double eyelid) is a common topic in beauty discussions. Some people use tape or glue to create a double-eyelid crease.

IDIOM:
瞼(まぶた)に浮(う)かぶ = to come to mind, to visualize (literally: to float before one's eyelids)
