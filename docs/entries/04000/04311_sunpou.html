寸法(すんぽう)

すんぽう
noun
measurement, dimensions, size
1. measurement, dimensions
The measured size of something, including length, width, and height.
家具(かぐ)の寸法(すんぽう)を測(はか)ってください。
Please measure the dimensions of the furniture.
寸法(すんぽう)が合(あ)わなくて、棚(たな)に入(はい)らなかった。
The dimensions didn't match, so it didn't fit on the shelf.
寸法(すんぽう)通(どお)りに作(つく)ったのに、なぜか合(あ)わない。
Even though I made it according to the measurements, it somehow doesn't fit.
寸法通り = according to the measurements
2. plan, scheme
A plan or way of doing something (often in the expression 寸法通り).

寸法(すんぽう) refers to measurements or dimensions, especially for clothing, furniture, or construction. The word comes from 寸(すん) (an old unit of measurement) and 法(ほう) (method).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寸法(すんぽう)を測(はか)る: to take measurements
  • 寸法(すんぽう)が合(あ)う: measurements match
  • 寸法(すんぽう)通(どお)り: according to measurements
  • 採寸(さいすん): taking body measurements

RELATED WORDS:

  • サイズ: size (loanword)
  • 大(おお)きさ: size (general)
  • 長(なが)さ/幅(はば)/高(たか)さ: length/width/height
