水分(すいぶん)

すいぶん
noun
moisture, water content
1. moisture, water content
The amount of water contained in something; hydration level.
水分(すいぶん)を十分(じゅうぶん)に取(と)ってください。
Please drink plenty of fluids.
Common health advice, especially in summer
この食品(しょくひん)は水分(すいぶん)が少(すく)ないので長(なが)く保存(ほぞん)できる。
This food has low moisture content, so it can be stored for a long time.
熱中症(ねっちゅうしょう)予防(よぼう)のため、こまめに水分(すいぶん)補給(ほきゅう)をしましょう。
Let's stay hydrated frequently to prevent heat stroke.
水分補給 = hydration, fluid intake

水分(すいぶん) refers to water content or moisture level, whether in food, soil, or the human body. It's commonly used in health and food contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 水分(すいぶん)を取(と)る: to take in fluids, to hydrate
  • 水分(すいぶん)補給(ほきゅう): hydration, fluid replacement
  • 水分(すいぶん)不足(ふそく): dehydration, lack of fluids
  • 水分(すいぶん)量(りょう): moisture content

HEALTH NOTE:
In Japan, reminders to drink 水分(すいぶん) are common during hot summer months to prevent 熱中症(ねっちゅうしょう) (heat stroke).
