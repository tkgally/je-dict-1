漕(こ)ぐ

こぐ
verb (godan)
to row; to pedal
1. to row; to paddle
To propel a boat through water using oars or paddles.
湖(みずうみ)でボートを漕(こ)いだ。
I rowed a boat on the lake.
2. to pedal
To operate a bicycle or swing by moving one's legs.
自転車(じてんしゃ)を一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)漕(こ)いで坂(さか)を上(のぼ)った。
I pedaled hard and climbed the hill.
子供(こども)がブランコを漕(こ)いでいる。
The child is swinging on the swing.

漕(こ)ぐ is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) used for rowing boats or pedaling bicycles/swings.

ASPECT (ている):
漕(こ)いでいる indicates rowing or pedaling is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [boat]を漕(こ)ぐ — to row [boat]
  • [bicycle]を漕(こ)ぐ — to pedal [bicycle]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 舟(ふね)を漕(こ)ぐ (row a boat)
  • 自転車(じてんしゃ)を漕(こ)ぐ (pedal a bicycle)
  • ブランコを漕(こ)ぐ (swing on a swing)
  • オールを漕(こ)ぐ (row with oars)

IDIOMATIC:
船(ふね)を漕(こ)ぐ when describing someone's head nodding while dozing (head moves like rowing motion).

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This verb is frequently written as こぐ.
