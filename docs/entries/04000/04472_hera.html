へら

へら
noun
spatula
1. spatula; paddle; scraper
spatula; paddle; scraper
へらで生地(きじ)を混(ま)ぜてください。
Please mix the batter with a spatula.
木(き)のへらで炒(いた)めると、フライパンを傷(きず)つけない。
Using a wooden spatula for stir-frying won't scratch the frying pan.
ゴムへらで鍋(なべ)の底(そこ)をきれいに取(と)った。
I scraped the bottom of the pot clean with a rubber spatula.

へら is a flat utensil used for mixing, stirring, flipping, and scraping. There are many types depending on material and purpose.

Common types:

  • 木(き)べら / 木(き)のへら (wooden spatula)
  • ゴムへら (rubber/silicone spatula)
  • フライ返(がえ)し (metal turner for flipping)

Common expressions:

  • へらで混(ま)ぜる (mix with a spatula)
  • へらで返(かえ)す (flip with a spatula)
