濯(すす)ぐ

すすぐ
verb (godan)
to rinse; to wash out
1. to rinse; to wash out
To wash something with clean water to remove soap, dirt, or impurities.
洗剤(せんざい)をよく濯(すす)いでください。
Please rinse out the detergent thoroughly.
米(こめ)を濯(すす)いでから炊(た)く。
Rinse the rice before cooking it.
2. to gargle
To rinse one's mouth or throat with liquid.
口(くち)を水(みず)で濯(すす)いだ。
I rinsed my mouth with water.

濯(すす)ぐ is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) specifically for rinsing with water.

ASPECT (ている):
濯(すす)いでいる indicates rinsing is in progress.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を濯(すす)ぐ — to rinse [thing]
  • [water]で濯(すす)ぐ — to rinse with [water]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 洗濯物(せんたくもの)を濯(すす)ぐ (rinse laundry)
  • 口(くち)を濯(すす)ぐ (rinse mouth)
  • 米(こめ)を濯(すす)ぐ (rinse rice)
  • 汚名(おめい)を濯(すす)ぐ (clear one's name - figurative)

WASHING PROCESS:
In laundry: 洗(あら)う (wash) → 濯(すす)ぐ (rinse) → 絞(しぼ)る (wring) → 干(ほ)す (hang to dry)

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This verb is frequently written as すすぐ due to the kanji's rarity.

RELATED:

  • うがいをする (to gargle - more common for gargling)
