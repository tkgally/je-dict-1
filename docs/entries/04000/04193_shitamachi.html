下町(したまち)

したまち
noun
downtown, old town

Definitions

1. downtown, old town, low-lying area of a city
The older, traditional commercial and residential areas of a city, typically in lower-lying areas, associated with working-class culture and traditional atmosphere.

Examples

東京(とうきょう)の下町(したまち)には古(ふる)い街並(まちな)みが残(のこ)っている。
Old townscapes remain in Tokyo's downtown area.
下町(したまち)の雰囲気(ふんいき)が好(す)きだ。
I like the atmosphere of the old downtown.
下町(したまち)育(そだ)ちの人(ひと)は人情(にんじょう)に厚(あつ)いと言(い)われる。
People raised in the old downtown are said to be warm-hearted.

Notes

CULTURAL NOTE:

下町(したまち) refers to the traditional working-class areas of Japanese cities, particularly Tokyo. Areas like 浅草 (Asakusa), 上野 (Ueno), and 両国 (Ryogoku) are considered classic 下町. It's associated with:

  • 人情(にんじょう) (warmth, humanity)
  • 職人(しょくにん) (craftspeople)
  • 伝統(でんとう)的(てき)な店(みせ) (traditional shops)
  • 祭(まつ)り (festivals)

OPPOSITE:

  • 山(やま)の手(て) (uptown, residential hillside areas)
