押(お)し付(つ)ける

おしつける
verb (ichidan)
to press against; to force upon
1. to press against; to push onto
To physically press something firmly against a surface.
スタンプを紙(かみ)に押(お)し付(つ)けた。
I pressed the stamp onto the paper.
2. to force upon; to push off onto
To impose unwanted tasks, responsibilities, or opinions on someone.
自分(じぶん)の考(かんが)えを人(ひと)に押(お)し付(つ)けてはいけない。
You shouldn't force your ideas on other people.
面倒(めんどう)な仕事(しごと)を新人(しんじん)に押(お)し付(つ)けないでください。
Please don't push the troublesome work onto the newcomers.

押(お)し付(つ)ける is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 押(お)す (to push) + 付(つ)ける (to attach). The figurative meaning of forcing things on people is very common.

ASPECT (ている):
押(お)し付(つ)けている indicates ongoing pressure or that something has been pressed/forced.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を[surface]に押(お)し付(つ)ける — to press [thing] against [surface]
  • [person]に[thing]を押(お)し付(つ)ける — to force [thing] on [person]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を押(お)し付(つ)ける (push responsibility onto)
  • 仕事(しごと)を押(お)し付(つ)ける (push work onto)
  • 意見(いけん)を押(お)し付(つ)ける (force one's opinion on)
  • 自分(じぶん)の価値観(かちかん)を押(お)し付(つ)ける (impose one's values)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 強制(きょうせい)する: to force, compel (more formal)
  • 押(お)しつけがましい: pushy, overbearing (adjective form)
