地名(ちめい)

ちめい
noun
place name, geographical name
1. place name, geographical name
The name of a geographical location such as a city, town, region, mountain, or river.
この地名(ちめい)の由来(ゆらい)を知(し)っていますか。
Do you know the origin of this place name?
日本(にほん)には読(よ)みにくい地名(ちめい)が多(おお)い。
There are many place names in Japan that are difficult to read.
地名(ちめい)辞典(じてん)で調(しら)べた。
I looked it up in a place name dictionary.

INTERESTING FACTS:

  • Many Japanese place names use unusual kanji readings (難読(なんどく)地名(ちめい))
  • Examples of difficult readings: 五十嵐(いがらし), 御徒町(おかちまち), 酒々井(しすい)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地名(ちめい)の由来(ゆらい) (origin of a place name)
  • 地名(ちめい)辞典(じてん) (place name dictionary)
  • 難読(なんどく)地名(ちめい) (place names that are hard to read)
  • 珍(ちん)地名(ちめい) (unusual place names)

CULTURAL NOTE: Japanese place names often have historical, topographical, or religious origins. Many come from Ainu language in Hokkaido, or reflect local features (rivers, mountains, temples).
