眉(まゆ)

まゆ
noun
eyebrow

Definitions

1. eyebrow
The strip of hair growing on the ridge above each eye.

Examples

眉(まゆ)を整(ととの)えた。
I shaped my eyebrows.
彼女(かのじょ)は眉(まゆ)をひそめて考(かんが)え込(こ)んだ。
She furrowed her brow and thought deeply.
太(ふと)い眉(まゆ)が印象的(いんしょうてき)だ。
His thick eyebrows are impressive.

Notes

眉(まゆ) refers to the eyebrow. It's often used in expressions describing facial expressions.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 眉(まゆ)をひそめる (furrow one's brow, frown)
  • 眉(まゆ)を上(あ)げる (raise eyebrows)
  • 眉(まゆ)を描(か)く (draw/apply eyebrows with makeup)
  • 眉(まゆ)を剃(そ)る (shave eyebrows)
  • 眉毛(まゆげ) (eyebrow hair - synonym)

EYE AREA:

  • 眉(まゆ) / 眉毛(まゆげ) (eyebrow)
  • 睫毛(まつげ) (eyelash)
  • 瞼(まぶた) (eyelid)
  • 瞳(ひとみ) (pupil)

IDIOMS:

  • 眉唾(まゆつば) = dubious, questionable (from the belief that putting saliva on eyebrows protects against being deceived by foxes)
  • 目(め)鼻(はな)立(だ)ち (facial features - includes 眉(まゆ))
