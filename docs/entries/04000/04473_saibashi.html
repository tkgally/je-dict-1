菜箸(さいばし)

さいばし
noun
cooking chopsticks
1. cooking chopsticks; kitchen chopsticks
cooking chopsticks; kitchen chopsticks
菜箸(さいばし)で天(てん)ぷらを揚(あ)げた。
I fried tempura using cooking chopsticks.
菜箸(さいばし)は普通(ふつう)の箸(はし)より長(なが)い。
Cooking chopsticks are longer than regular chopsticks.
熱(あつ)い油(あぶら)に手(て)を近(ちか)づけないように、菜箸(さいばし)を使(つか)った。
I used cooking chopsticks to keep my hands away from the hot oil.

菜箸(さいばし) are extra-long chopsticks (usually 30-40cm) designed for cooking. They keep hands at a safe distance from heat and allow precise handling of ingredients.

Common uses:

  • 揚(あ)げ物(もの)をする (frying)
  • 炒(いた)め物(もの)を作(つく)る (stir-frying)
  • 茹(ゆ)でた麺(めん)を取(と)り出(だ)す (removing boiled noodles)

菜箸(さいばし) are typically made of bamboo, wood, or heat-resistant plastic. Some have silicone tips for use with non-stick cookware.
