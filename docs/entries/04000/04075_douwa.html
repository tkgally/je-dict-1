童話(どうわ)

どうわ
noun
fairy tale, children's story
1. fairy tale, children's story, nursery tale
A story written or told for children, often featuring magical elements, moral lessons, or fantastical characters.
子供(こども)に童話(どうわ)を読(よ)み聞(き)かせた。
I read a fairy tale to the children.
グリム}{童話(どうわ)は世界中(せかいじゅう)で有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Grimm's Fairy Tales are famous around the world.
童話(どうわ)作家(さっか)になりたい。
I want to become a children's story writer.
小(ちい)さいころ、毎晩(まいばん)母(はは)に童話(どうわ)を読(よ)んでもらっていた。
When I was little, my mother would read me fairy tales every night.
この童話(どうわ)には動物(どうぶつ)たちが助(たす)け合(あ)うという大切(たいせつ)なメッセージが込(こ)められている。
This fairy tale contains an important message about animals helping each other.

FAMOUS COLLECTIONS:

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 童話(どうわ)を読(よ)む (to read a fairy tale)
  • 童話(どうわ)作家(さっか) (children's story writer)
  • 童話(どうわ)集(しゅう) (collection of fairy tales)

HOMOPHONE: 動揺(どうよう) (agitation, disturbance) sounds similar but different meaning.

RELATED:
