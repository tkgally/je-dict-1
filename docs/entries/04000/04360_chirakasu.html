散(ち)らかす

ちらかす
verb (godan)
to scatter, to make messy
1. to scatter, to make a mess, to clutter
To cause disorder by spreading things around untidily.
子供(こども)がおもちゃ}を{散(ち)らかした。
The child made a mess with toys.
部屋(へや)を散(ち)らかさないでください。
Please don't make a mess of the room.
紙(かみ)を床(ゆか)に散(ち)らかすな。
Don't scatter papers on the floor.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Intransitive pair: 散(ち)らかる (to be messy).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state or ongoing action. 散(ち)らかしている = 'has made messy' or 'is making messy.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを散(ち)らかす (to scatter X, make X messy)
  • 部屋(へや)を散(ち)らかす (to mess up a room)
  • 散(ち)らかさないで (don't make a mess)

INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 散(ち)らかす (transitive) - to make messy
  • 散(ち)らかる (intransitive) - to be messy

NOTE: Often used in negative requests: 散(ち)らかさないで！ (Don't make a mess!)
