舐(な)める

なめる
verb (ichidan)
to lick

Definitions

1. to lick
To pass the tongue over something.
2. to underestimate, to look down on
Colloquially, to not take someone or something seriously.

Examples

犬(いぬ)が顔(かお)を舐(な)めた。
The dog licked my face.
飴(あめ)を舐(な)めている。
I'm sucking on a candy.
俺(おれ)を舐(な)めるな！
Don't underestimate me!
Sense 2 - colloquial/rough

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを舐(な)める (to lick X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 舐(な)めている = 'is licking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを舐(な)める (to lick X)
  • 飴(あめ)を舐(な)める (to suck on candy)
  • 傷(きず)を舐(な)める (to lick a wound)
  • 人を舐(な)める (to look down on someone)

COLLOQUIAL USAGE (Sense 2):

  • 舐(な)めるな！ (Don't underestimate me!)
  • 舐(な)めてる (underestimating, not taking seriously)
  • 舐(な)められている (being looked down on)

NOTE: The 'underestimate' meaning is very colloquial and often used in confrontational contexts.
