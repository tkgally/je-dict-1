受取(うけとり)

うけとり
noun
receipt, receiving
1. receipt
A written acknowledgment that something has been received, especially payment.
受取(うけとり)をください。
Please give me a receipt.
受取(うけとり)金額(きんがく)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please verify the amount received.
2. receiving, acceptance
The act of receiving something.
荷物(にもつ)の受取(うけとり)は窓口(まどぐち)でお願(ねが)いします。
Please pick up your package at the counter.
商品(しょうひん)の受取(うけとり)は本人(ほんにん)のみとなっています。
Only the person themselves can receive the item.
受取(うけとり)の日時(にちじ)を指定(してい)できますか。
Can I specify the date and time for receiving it?

USAGE: 受取(うけとり) can refer to both the document (receipt) and the act of receiving.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 受取(うけとり)を書(か)く (to write a receipt)
  • 受取(うけとり)を発行(はっこう)する (to issue a receipt)
  • 受取(うけとり)証(しょう) (receipt, acknowledgment)
  • 受取(うけとり)人(にん) (recipient, addressee)

WRITING VARIATIONS: Can also be written as 受け取り(うけとり).

RELATED:

  • レシート - receipt (from English, for store purchases)
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ) - official receipt (more formal)
