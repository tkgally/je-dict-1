生存(せいぞん)

せいぞん
noun, suru-verb
existence, survival
1. survival, being alive
The state of continuing to live, especially in difficult or dangerous circumstances.
2. existence
The fact of existing or being real.
地震(じしん)から72時間(じかん)後(ご)に生存(せいぞん)者(しゃ)が発見(はっけん)された。
Survivors were discovered 72 hours after the earthquake.
生存者 = survivor
生存(せいぞん)率(りつ)は早期(そうき)発見(はっけん)で大(おお)きく上(あ)がる。
The survival rate greatly increases with early detection.
生存率 = survival rate (often used in medical contexts)
厳(きび)しい環境(かんきょう)でも生存(せいぞん)できる生物(せいぶつ)がいる。
There are organisms that can survive even in harsh environments.

生存(せいぞん) means survival or the state of being alive. It's commonly used in serious contexts like disasters, medical situations, and biology.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 生存(せいぞん)者(しゃ): survivor
  • 生存(せいぞん)率(りつ): survival rate
  • 生存(せいぞん)競争(きょうそう): survival competition, struggle for existence
  • 生存(せいぞん)確認(かくにん): confirmation of survival (checking if someone is alive)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 生(い)きる: to live (verb)
  • 生命(せいめい): life (biological)
  • サバイバル: survival (loanword, often for survival activities/shows)
