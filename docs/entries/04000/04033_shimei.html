氏名(しめい)

しめい
noun
full name

Definitions

1. full name, family name and given name
A person's complete name consisting of both surname and given name. Used in formal and official contexts.

Examples

氏名(しめい)をフルネームで記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please write your full name.
応募者(おうぼしゃ)の氏名(しめい)と住所(じゅうしょ)を確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed the applicant's name and address.
氏名(しめい)不詳(ふしょう)の男性(だんせい)が病院(びょういん)に運(はこ)ばれた。
An unidentified man was brought to the hospital.
氏名不詳 = name unknown, unidentified

Notes

USAGE: 氏名(しめい) is a formal term used on official forms, documents, and in administrative contexts. In everyday conversation, 名前(なまえ) is more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 氏名(しめい)を記入(きにゅう)する (to fill in one's name)
  • 氏名(しめい)不詳(ふしょう) (name unknown)
  • 本人(ほんにん)氏名(しめい) (name of the person in question)

HOMOPHONE NOTE: Be careful not to confuse with:

  • 使命(しめい) (mission, vocation)
  • 指名(しめい) (nomination, designation)

Related Words

Related: 姓名(せいめい) (full name (formal))
