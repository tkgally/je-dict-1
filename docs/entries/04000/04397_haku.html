吐(は)く

はく
verb (godan)
to exhale, to vomit
1. to exhale, to breathe out
To expel air from the lungs.
息(いき)をゆっくり吐(は)いてください。
Please exhale slowly.
2. to vomit, to throw up
To eject the contents of the stomach through the mouth.
気分(きぶん)が悪(わる)くて吐(は)きそうだ。
I feel sick and might throw up.
Sense 2
3. to spit out, to emit
To forcefully expel something from the mouth, or for something to emit.
嘘(うそ)を吐(は)くな。
Don't tell lies.
Idiom: 嘘を吐く = to tell a lie

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを吐(は)く (to exhale/vomit X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 吐(は)いている = 'is exhaling/vomiting.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 息(いき)を吐(は)く (to exhale)
  • 吐(は)きそう (feels like vomiting)
  • 嘘(うそ)を吐(は)く (to tell a lie)
  • 弱音(よわね)を吐(は)く (to complain, show weakness)
  • 本音(ほんね)を吐(は)く (to reveal true feelings)

CONTRAST:

  • 吐(は)く - to exhale/spit out
  • 吸(す)う - to inhale/suck in

IDIOMS:

  • 嘘(うそ)を吐(は)く (to lie)
  • 弱音(よわね)を吐(は)く (to whine, complain)
  • 本音(ほんね)を吐(は)く (to speak honestly)

Related Words

Antonym: 吸(す)う (to inhale)
