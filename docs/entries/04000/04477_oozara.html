大皿(おおざら)

おおざら
noun
large plate
1. large plate; serving platter
large plate; serving platter
唐揚(からあ)げを大皿(おおざら)に盛(も)った。
I served fried chicken on a large plate.
パーティーでは大皿(おおざら)に料理(りょうり)を出(だ)して、みんなで取(と)り分(わ)ける。
At parties, we serve food on large platters and everyone helps themselves.
この大皿(おおざら)は祖母(そぼ)から受(う)け継(つ)いだものだ。
This large platter was handed down from my grandmother.

大皿(おおざら) are large plates or platters used for serving food family-style. In Japanese dining, dishes are often placed in the center of the table for everyone to share.

Common uses:

  • 大皿(おおざら)料理(りょうり) (food served family-style)
  • 大皿(おおざら)に盛(も)る (to arrange on a large platter)

Size note: 大皿(おおざら) are typically 25cm or larger in diameter. They are common in 居酒屋(いざかや) and for home entertaining.
