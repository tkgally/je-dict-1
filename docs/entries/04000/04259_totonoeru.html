整(ととの)える

ととのえる
verb (ichidan)
to arrange; to prepare; to put in order
1. to arrange; to put in order
To organize things neatly or make adjustments for proper alignment.
2. to prepare; to get ready
To make necessary preparations or gather requirements.
3. to regulate; to condition
To bring into a balanced or healthy state.
部屋(へや)を整(ととの)えてからお客(きゃく)さんを迎(むか)えた。
I tidied up the room before welcoming the guests.
書類(しょるい)を整(ととの)えて提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I organized the documents and submitted them.
体調(たいちょう)を整(ととの)えることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to regulate your physical condition.

整(ととの)える is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to put things in proper order.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 整(ととの)う (to be arranged, to be ready)

  • 整(ととの)える: I arrange the room.
  • 整(ととの)う: The preparations are complete.

ASPECT (ている):
整(ととの)えている indicates the arranging process is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を整(ととの)える — to arrange/prepare [thing]
  • [condition]を整(ととの)える — to regulate/condition [condition]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 身(み)だしなみを整(ととの)える (groom oneself)
  • 環境(かんきょう)を整(ととの)える (prepare the environment)
  • 呼吸(こきゅう)を整(ととの)える (regulate breathing)
  • 体調(たいちょう)を整(ととの)える (condition one's health)

SAUNA CULTURE:
整(ととの)う has become popular in sauna culture, referring to the blissful state achieved after alternating hot and cold bathing.
