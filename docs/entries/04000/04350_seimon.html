正門(せいもん)

せいもん
noun
main gate, main entrance
1. main gate, front gate
The principal entrance to a building, institution, or grounds; the front or formal entrance.
学校(がっこう)の正門(せいもん)の前(まえ)で待(ま)ち合(あ)わせしよう。
Let's meet in front of the school's main gate.
正門(せいもん)から入(はい)ってまっすぐ進(すす)んでください。
Please enter through the main gate and go straight.
会社(かいしゃ)の正門(せいもん)には警備員(けいびいん)がいる。
There are security guards at the company's main gate.

正門(せいもん) is the main or front gate of an institution, as opposed to side entrances or back gates.

CONTRAST WITH:

  • 裏門(うらもん): back gate
  • 通用門(つうようもん): service entrance, side entrance
  • 南門(なんもん)/北門(ほくもん)/etc.: south gate, north gate, etc.

COMMON LOCATIONS:

  • Schools and universities
  • Shrines and temples
  • Corporate buildings
  • Government facilities
  • Parks and gardens
general
