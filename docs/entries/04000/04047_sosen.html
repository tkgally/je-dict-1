祖先(そせん)

そせん
noun
ancestor, forefather
1. ancestor, forefather, progenitor
A person from whom one is descended, often referring to distant ancestors. More formal than 先祖(せんぞ) and often used in scientific or historical contexts.
人類(じんるい)の祖先(そせん)はアフリカ}で{誕生(たんじょう)した。
The ancestors of humanity originated in Africa.
祖先(そせん)から受(う)け継(つ)いだ伝統(でんとう)を守(まも)る。
We preserve traditions inherited from our ancestors.
日本人(にほんじん)の祖先(そせん)について研究(けんきゅう)している。
I am researching the ancestors of the Japanese people.

USAGE: 祖先(そせん) is more formal and literary than 先祖(せんぞ). It's commonly used in:

  • Scientific contexts (evolution, anthropology)
  • Historical discussions
  • Formal/literary writing

CONTRAST:

  • 祖先(そせん) - formal, used for distant/collective ancestors, scientific contexts
  • 先祖(せんぞ) - everyday, used for family ancestors, religious contexts

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 祖先(そせん)崇拝(すうはい) (ancestor worship)
  • 共通(きょうつう)祖先(そせん) (common ancestor)
  • 祖先(そせん)から受(う)け継(つ)ぐ (to inherit from ancestors)

Related Words

Synonym: 先祖(せんぞ) (ancestor (everyday))
