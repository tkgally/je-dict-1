零(こぼ)れる

こぼれる
verb (ichidan)
to spill; to overflow; to slip out
1. to spill; to overflow
For liquid or small items to accidentally fall out of a container.
2. to slip out; to escape
For something like a smile, tears, or words to emerge unintentionally.
グラスから水(みず)が零(こぼ)れた。
Water spilled from the glass.
彼女(かのじょ)の顔(かお)に笑(え)みが零(こぼ)れた。
A smile escaped onto her face.
思(おも)わず涙(なみだ)が零(こぼ)れた。
Tears fell before I could stop them.

零(こぼ)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning for something to spill out or escape.

TRANSITIVE PAIR: 零(こぼ)す (to spill something)

  • 零(こぼ)れる: Water spills.
  • 零(こぼ)す: I spill water.

ASPECT (ている):
零(こぼ)れている indicates something has spilled and remains, or is continuously spilling.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [liquid]が零(こぼ)れる — [liquid] spills
  • [container]から零(こぼ)れる — to spill from [container]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 涙(なみだ)が零(こぼ)れる (tears fall)
  • 笑(え)みが零(こぼ)れる (a smile escapes)
  • 光(ひかり)が零(こぼ)れる (light spills through)
  • 木漏(こも)れ日(び) (sunlight filtering through trees - related compound)

POETIC USE:
零(こぼ)れる is often used poetically for involuntary expressions of emotion (tears, smiles) that "slip out."
