習字(しゅうじ)

しゅうじ
noun
penmanship, calligraphy practice
1. penmanship, handwriting practice
The practice of writing characters neatly, especially with brush and ink as taught in Japanese schools.
小学校(しょうがっこう)で習字(しゅうじ)を習(なら)った。
I learned penmanship in elementary school.
習字(しゅうじ)の時間(じかん)は好(す)きだった。
I liked penmanship class.
娘(むすめ)を習字(しゅうじ)教室(きょうしつ)に通(かよ)わせている。
I have my daughter attend calligraphy class.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書道(しょどう) - calligraphy as an art form, more formal/artistic
  • 習字(しゅうじ) - penmanship, learning to write properly (school subject)

習字(しゅうじ) focuses on learning proper character formation, while 書道(しょどう) emphasizes artistic expression.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 習字(しゅうじ)を習(なら)う (to learn penmanship)
  • 習字(しゅうじ)教室(きょうしつ) (penmanship/calligraphy class)
  • 習字(しゅうじ)道具(どうぐ) (calligraphy tools)
  • 習字(しゅうじ)セット (calligraphy set)
