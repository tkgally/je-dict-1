徐々(じょじょ)に

じょじょに
adverb
gradually, slowly, little by little
1. gradually, slowly, little by little
Describes a slow, steady progression or change over time.
空(そら)が徐々(じょじょ)に暗(くら)くなってきた。
The sky gradually became dark.
病気(びょうき)は徐々(じょじょ)に回復(かいふく)している。
The illness is gradually recovering.
彼(かれ)の日本語(にほんご)は徐々(じょじょ)に上達(じょうたつ)している。
His Japanese is gradually improving.

USAGE: 徐々(じょじょ)に emphasizes a slow, steady pace of change without sudden movements or shifts.

POSITION: Typically appears before the verb it modifies.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • だんだん - gradually (slightly more casual)
  • しだいに - gradually, increasingly
  • ゆっくり - slowly (emphasizes pace rather than progression)
