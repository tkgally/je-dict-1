王女(おうじょ)

おうじょ
noun
princess
1. princess
A daughter of a king or queen; a female member of a royal family.
王女(おうじょ)は美(うつく)しいドレスを着(き)ていた。
The princess was wearing a beautiful dress.
物語(ものがたり)の中(なか)で王女(おうじょ)は魔法使(まほうつか)いに助(たす)けられる。
In the story, the princess is saved by a wizard.
ダイアナ王女(おうじょ)は世界中(せかいじゅう)で愛(あい)されていた。
Princess Diana was loved around the world.
王女(おうじょ)は王子(おうじ)と結婚(けっこん)して幸(しあわ)せに暮(く)らした。
The princess married the prince and lived happily.
その国(くに)の王女(おうじょ)は国民(こくみん)から深(ふか)く敬(うやま)われていた。
The princess of that country was deeply respected by the people.

USAGE: 王女(おうじょ) specifically refers to a king's daughter (princess by birth), used for foreign royalty.

JAPANESE IMPERIAL FAMILY:

For Japanese imperial princesses, different terms are used:

  • 皇女(こうじょ) - imperial princess (daughter of Emperor)
  • 内親王(ないしんのう) - princess (official title)
  • 女王(じょおう) - princess (lower rank); also means 'queen'

RELATED:

  • 王子(おうじ) - prince (male counterpart)
  • 女王(じょおう) - queen
  • 国王(こくおう) - king
  • 王(おう) - king
  • 姫(ひめ) - princess (literary, fairy tales)
