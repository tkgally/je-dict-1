養(やしな)う

やしなう
godan verb
to support, to nourish, to cultivate
1. to support, to provide for
To financially or materially support someone, especially family members.
2. to nourish, to nurture
To provide sustenance or care for growth and development.
3. to cultivate, to develop
To develop a skill, ability, or quality over time.
家族(かぞく)を養(やしな)うために働(はたら)く。
I work to support my family.
体力(たいりょく)を養(やしな)う。
I build up my physical strength.
読書(どくしょ)で教養(きょうよう)を養(やしな)う。
I cultivate my education through reading.

養(やしな)う has meanings related to support, nourishment, and cultivation.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家族(かぞく)を養(やしな)う: to support one's family
  • 力(ちから)を養(やしな)う: to build up one's strength
  • 教養(きょうよう)を養(やしな)う: to cultivate one's education
  • 目(め)を養(やしな)う: to develop an eye for (something)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 育(そだ)てる: to raise, bring up
  • 扶養(ふよう)する: to support (formal)
  • 栄養(えいよう): nutrition
