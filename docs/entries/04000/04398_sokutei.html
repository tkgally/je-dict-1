測定(そくてい)

そくてい
noun, suru-verb
measurement, measuring
1. measurement, measuring
The act of determining the size, amount, or degree of something using instruments or calculations.
気温(きおん)を測定(そくてい)する。
To measure the temperature.
血圧(けつあつ)の測定(そくてい)結果(けっか)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the blood pressure measurement results.
正確(せいかく)な測定(そくてい)には精密(せいみつ)な機器(きき)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Precise instruments are needed for accurate measurement.

測定(そくてい) refers to scientific or technical measurement using instruments. It's commonly used in scientific, medical, and industrial contexts.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 測定(そくてい)器(き)/測定(そくてい)機器(きき): measuring instrument
  • 測定(そくてい)結果(けっか): measurement results
  • 測定(そくてい)値(ち): measured value
  • 測定(そくてい)方法(ほうほう): measurement method

TYPES OF MEASUREMENT:

  • 温度(おんど)測定(そくてい): temperature measurement
  • 距離(きょり)測定(そくてい): distance measurement
  • 体重(たいじゅう)測定(そくてい): weight measurement
  • 放射線(ほうしゃせん)測定(そくてい): radiation measurement

RELATED WORDS:

  • 計測(けいそく): measurement (similar meaning)
  • 計量(けいりょう): weighing, measuring
  • 測(はか)る: to measure (verb)
