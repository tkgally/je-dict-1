概論(がいろん)

がいろん
noun
introduction, general remarks, outline
1. introduction, general remarks, outline
A general overview or introductory discussion of a subject, especially in academic contexts.
経済学(けいざいがく)概論(がいろん)の授業(じゅぎょう)を取(と)っている。
I'm taking an Introduction to Economics class.
まず概論(がいろん)を読(よ)んでから詳細(しょうさい)を学(まな)ぼう。
Let's first read the introduction and then study the details.
この本(ほん)は日本(にほん)史(し)概論(がいろん)として最適(さいてき)だ。
This book is ideal as an introduction to Japanese history.

USAGE: 概論(がいろん) is commonly used in academic contexts for introductory courses and texts.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 経済学(けいざいがく)概論(がいろん) (Introduction to Economics)
  • 法学(ほうがく)概論(がいろん) (Introduction to Law)
  • 心理学(しんりがく)概論(がいろん) (Introduction to Psychology)
  • 哲学(てつがく)概論(がいろん) (Introduction to Philosophy)

ACADEMIC USE: University introductory courses often have 概論(がいろん) in their titles, indicating they provide a general overview suitable for beginners.

RELATED:

  • 入門(にゅうもん) - introduction (for beginners)
  • 序論(じょろん) - introduction (to a text)
  • 総論(そうろん) - general discussion
