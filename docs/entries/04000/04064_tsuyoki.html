強気(つよき)

つよき
noun, na-adjective
bullish, confident, firm
1. bullish, optimistic (finance)
In financial markets, the expectation that prices will rise. The opposite of bearish.
2. confident, assertive, firm
A confident or assertive attitude; not easily intimidated or discouraged.
市場(しじょう)は強気(つよき)の姿勢(しせい)を見(み)せている。
The market is showing a bullish stance.
Financial context
彼女(かのじょ)は強気(つよき)な態度(たいど)で交渉(こうしょう)した。
She negotiated with a confident attitude.
強気(つよき)の発言(はつげん)が話題(わだい)になった。
The assertive statement became a topic of conversation.

USAGE CONTEXTS:

1. FINANCIAL/BUSINESS:

  • 強気(つよき)相場(そうば) (bull market)
  • 強気(つよき)の見通(みとお)し (bullish outlook)
  • 強気(つよき)で買(か)う (to buy bullishly)

2. PERSONALITY/ATTITUDE:

  • 強気(つよき)な態度(たいど) (confident attitude)
  • 強気(つよき)に出(で)る (to take a firm stance)
  • 強気(つよき)な発言(はつげん) (assertive statement)

ANTONYM: 弱気(よわき) (bearish; timid, lacking confidence)

NOTE: 強気(つよき) can describe someone who doesn't back down, but in some contexts may have a slightly negative nuance of being overconfident or aggressive.

Related Words

Antonym: 弱気(よわき) (bearish, timid)
