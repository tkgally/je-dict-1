おたま

おたま
noun
ladle
1. ladle
ladle
おたまで味噌汁(みそしる)をよそった。
I served miso soup with a ladle.
このおたまは深(ふか)くて使(つか)いやすい。
This ladle is deep and easy to use.
おたまを使(つか)って、スープを鍋(なべ)から器(うつわ)に移(うつ)した。
I used a ladle to transfer soup from the pot to the bowl.

おたま is a ladle used for serving soup, stew, and other liquid dishes. The formal name is お玉杓子(たまじゃくし), but おたま is the common everyday form.

Common expressions:

  • おたまでよそう (serve with a ladle)
  • おたまで掬(すく)う (scoop with a ladle)

The word おたまじゃくし also means "tadpole" due to the similar shape, which is where the kitchen utensil likely got its name.
