振(ふ)り向(む)く

ふりむく
verb (godan)
to turn around; to look back
1. to turn around; to turn one's head
To physically turn to face another direction, especially behind oneself.
後(うし)ろから声(こえ)をかけられて振(ふ)り向(む)いた。
Someone called out from behind me and I turned around.
彼女(かのじょ)は振(ふ)り向(む)きもせずに歩(ある)いて行(い)った。
She walked away without even looking back.
2. to pay attention to; to take notice of
To show interest in someone or something, often used in the negative.
いくらアピールしても、彼(かれ)は振(ふ)り向(む)いてくれない。
No matter how much I try to get his attention, he won't give me a second glance.

振(ふ)り向(む)く is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) formed from 振(ふ)る (to swing) + 向(む)く (to face). It primarily describes the physical action of turning around.

ASPECT (ている):
振(ふ)り向(む)いている indicates someone has turned around and is facing that direction.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [direction]を振(ふ)り向(む)く — to turn toward [direction]
  • [person]が振(ふ)り向(む)く — [person] turns around

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 後(うし)ろを振(ふ)り向(む)く (turn to look behind)
  • 振(ふ)り向(む)きもしない (doesn't even turn around; ignores completely)
  • 振(ふ)り向(む)いてくれない (won't pay attention to me)

IDIOMATIC USAGE:
振(ふ)り向(む)いてくれない in romantic contexts means someone won't show interest or return one's feelings.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to look back (physical + figurative reflection)
  • 振(ふ)り向(む)く: only physical turning
