打(う)ち明(あ)ける

うちあける
verb (ichidan)
to confide; to reveal; to confess
1. to confide; to reveal
To share a secret, personal matter, or hidden feelings with someone.
友人(ゆうじん)に悩(なや)みを打(う)ち明(あ)けた。
I confided my worries to my friend.
彼(かれ)は長年(ながねん)の秘密(ひみつ)をついに打(う)ち明(あ)けた。
He finally revealed a secret he had kept for many years.
2. to confess; to come clean
To admit something one has been hiding, often reluctantly.
実(じつ)を打(う)ち明(あ)けると、私(わたし)もよく分(わ)からないんです。
To be honest with you, I don't really understand it either.

打(う)ち明(あ)ける is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 打(う)つ (to strike) + 明(あ)ける (to open). The image is of opening one's heart to reveal what's inside.

ASPECT (ている):
打(う)ち明(あ)けている is uncommon; the completed form 打(う)ち明(あ)けた is typically used.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [person]に[secret]を打(う)ち明(あ)ける — to confide [secret] to [person]
  • 実(じつ)を打(う)ち明(あ)けると — to be honest, frankly speaking

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 秘密(ひみつ)を打(う)ち明(あ)ける (reveal a secret)
  • 本当(ほんとう)のことを打(う)ち明(あ)ける (tell the truth)
  • 気持(きも)ちを打(う)ち明(あ)ける (reveal one's feelings)

FIXED EXPRESSION:
実(じつ)を打(う)ち明(あ)けると or 打(う)ち明(あ)けて言(い)うと means "to tell the truth" or "frankly speaking" — used to introduce an honest admission.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 告白(こくはく)する: to confess (especially romantic feelings)
  • 白状(はくじょう)する: to confess (often wrongdoing)
