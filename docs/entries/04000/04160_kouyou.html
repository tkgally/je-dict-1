紅葉(こうよう)

こうよう
noun
autumn leaves, fall colors

Definitions

1. autumn leaves, fall colors, autumn foliage
The changing of leaf colors in autumn, particularly to red, orange, and yellow.
2. viewing autumn leaves
The activity of going to see autumn foliage.

Examples

紅葉(こうよう)が美(うつく)しい季節(きせつ)になった。
It has become the season when autumn leaves are beautiful.
京都(きょうと)へ紅葉(こうよう)を見(み)に行(い)った。
I went to Kyoto to see the autumn leaves.
山(やま)の紅葉(こうよう)が見頃(みごろ)だ。
The mountain's autumn colors are at their peak.

Notes

READING NOTE: Can also be read as もみじ, which specifically refers to Japanese maple leaves or the tree itself.

CULTURAL NOTE: 紅葉狩り (もみじがり) is the traditional Japanese custom of visiting scenic areas to view autumn leaves.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 紅葉(こうよう)を楽(たの)しむ (to enjoy autumn leaves)
  • 紅葉(こうよう)が見頃(みごろ) (autumn leaves are at their peak)
  • 紅葉(こうよう)狩(が)り (autumn leaf viewing)
