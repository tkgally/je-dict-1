絡(から)む

からむ
verb (godan)
to get tangled, to be involved
1. to get tangled, to become entangled
For threads, ropes, or similar things to become twisted together.
糸(いと)が絡(から)んでほどけない。
The thread got tangled and I can't undo it.
ツルが木(き)に絡(から)んでいる。
Vines are wrapped around the tree.
Sense 1: to get tangled
コードが足(あし)に絡(から)んだ。
The cord got tangled around my feet.
Sense 1: to become entangled
髪(かみ)が絡(から)んで梳(と)かせない。
My hair is tangled and I can't comb it.
Sense 1: to get tangled
イヤホンのコードが絡(から)まっている。
The earphone cord is tangled.
Sense 1: to become entangled
2. to be connected with, to be involved in
For something to be related to or associated with a matter or issue.
この問題(もんだい)には複雑(ふくざつ)な事情(じじょう)が絡(から)んでいる。
Complex circumstances are involved in this problem.
Sense 2
利益(りえき)が絡(から)んでいる。
Interests are involved.
Sense 2: to be connected with
金(かね)が絡(から)む問題(もんだい)だ。
It's a matter involving money.
Sense 2: to be involved in
政治(せいじ)が絡(から)んでいる。
Politics is involved.
Sense 2: to be connected with
人間関係(にんげんかんけい)が絡(から)んで複雑(ふくざつ)だ。
Human relationships are involved, making it complicated.
Sense 2: to be involved
3. to pick a quarrel, to pester
To persistently bother someone, often when drunk.
酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)いに絡(から)まれた。
I was pestered by a drunk.
Sense 3
しつこく絡(から)んでくる。
They persistently bother me.
Sense 3: to pester
知(し)らない人(ひと)に絡(から)まれた。
I was harassed by a stranger.
Sense 3: to pick a quarrel
なぜ絡(から)んでくるの。
Why are you picking on me?
Sense 3: to pester
いちいち絡(から)まないで。
Don't nitpick at everything.
Sense 3: to pick a quarrel

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Transitive pair: 絡(から)める (to entwine).

ASPECT: ている indicates state. 絡(から)んでいる = 'is tangled/involved.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xが絡(から)む (X gets tangled)
  • Xに絡(から)む (to be involved in X)
  • Xに絡(から)まれる (to be pestered by X - passive)

MULTIPLE MEANINGS:
1. Physical tangling (threads, vines)
2. Abstract involvement (issues, circumstances)
3. Pestering behavior (especially by drunks)

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 絡(から)む (intransitive) - to get tangled
  • 絡(から)める (transitive) - to entwine, involve
