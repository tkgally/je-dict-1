図形(ずけい)

ずけい
noun
figure, shape, diagram
1. figure, shape, geometric form
A shape or figure, particularly in mathematical or geometric contexts. Includes circles, triangles, squares, and other geometric forms.
三角形(さんかくけい)は最(もっと)も基本的(きほんてき)な図形(ずけい)の一(ひと)つだ。
A triangle is one of the most basic geometric shapes.
この図形(ずけい)の面積(めんせき)を求(もと)めなさい。
Find the area of this figure.
Common in math problems
子供(こども)たちは様々(さまざま)な図形(ずけい)を使(つか)って絵(え)を描(か)いた。
The children drew pictures using various shapes.

USAGE: 図形(ずけい) is commonly used in mathematical and educational contexts. It refers specifically to geometric shapes and figures.

COMMON GEOMETRIC SHAPES:

  • 三角形(さんかくけい) (triangle)
  • 四角形(しかくけい) (quadrilateral)
  • 円(えん) (circle)
  • 長方形(ちょうほうけい) (rectangle)
  • 正方形(せいほうけい) (square)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 図形(ずけい)を描(えが)く (to draw a figure)
  • 平面(へいめん)図形(ずけい) (plane figure)
  • 立体(りったい)図形(ずけい) (solid figure, 3D shape)
general
