歯車(はぐるま)

はぐるま
noun
gear, cogwheel
1. gear, cogwheel, cog
A rotating machine part with teeth that mesh with another toothed part to transmit motion.
時計(とけい)の歯車(はぐるま)が壊(こわ)れている。
The gear in the clock is broken.
歯車(はぐるま)が噛(か)み合(あ)ってスムーズに動(うご)く。
The gears mesh and move smoothly.
2. cog (in a system)
Figuratively, a small part of a larger mechanism or organization.
彼(かれ)は大(おお)きな組織(そしき)の一(ひと)つの歯車(はぐるま)に過(す)ぎない。
He's just a cog in a large organization.
Figurative usage

USAGE: 歯車(はぐるま) combines 歯(は) (tooth) and 車(くるま) (wheel). It's used both literally for mechanical gears and figuratively for people as parts of larger systems.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 歯車(はぐるま)が噛(か)み合(あ)う (gears mesh - also figurative: things work well together)
  • 歯車(はぐるま)が狂(くる)う (gears go wrong - things stop working smoothly)
  • 歯車(はぐるま)の一(ひと)つ (one gear/cog - small part of system)

FIGURATIVE USAGE: Often used to describe feeling like an insignificant part of a large organization or system.
