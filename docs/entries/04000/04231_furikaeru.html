1. to look back; to turn around
To physically turn one's head or body to look behind.
ふと振(ふ)り返(かえ)った。
I suddenly looked back.
名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)ばれて振(ふ)り返(かえ)った。
I turned around when my name was called.
歩(ある)きながら何度(なんど)も振(ふ)り返(かえ)った。
I kept looking back as I walked.
彼女(かのじょ)は駅(えき)のホームで手(て)を振(ふ)りながら振(ふ)り返(かえ)った。
She waved and looked back from the station platform.
後(うし)ろで大(おお)きな音(おと)がしたので、思(おも)わず振(ふ)り返(かえ)ると、猫(ねこ)が棚(たな)から落(お)ちていた。
There was a loud noise behind me, so I instinctively looked back and saw a cat had fallen from the shelf.
2. to reflect on; to look back on
To think about or review past events, experiences, or one's own actions.
過去(かこ)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る。
To reflect on the past.
一年(いちねん)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)ると、いろいろなことがあった。
Looking back on the year, a lot happened.
過去(かこ)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)っても仕方(しかた)がない。
There's no point in dwelling on the past.
学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)ると、もっと勉強(べんきょう)しておけばよかったと思(おも)う。
Looking back on my student days, I wish I had studied more.
今(いま)までの人生(じんせい)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)ってみると、あの出会(であ)いが一番(いちばん)大(おお)きな転機(てんき)だった。
Looking back on my life so far, that encounter was the biggest turning point.

振(ふ)り返(かえ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) formed from 振(ふ)る (to swing) + 返(かえ)る (to return). It means to turn back physically or mentally.

ASPECT (ている):
振(ふ)り返(かえ)っている indicates the action of looking back is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (physical) — to turn around
  • [period/event]を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (figurative) — to reflect on [period/event]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 過去(かこ)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (look back on the past)
  • 人生(じんせい)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (reflect on one's life)
  • 一年(いちねん)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (look back on the year)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 振(ふ)り向(む)く: to turn around (only physical, not reflective)
  • 顧(かえり)みる: to reflect on, consider (more literary)
  • 回顧(かいこ)する: to look back on (formal, written)

Related Words

Synonym: 省(かえり)みる (to reflect on (literary))
