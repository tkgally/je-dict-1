早口(はやくち)

はやくち
noun, no-adjective
fast-talking
1. fast-talking, speaking quickly
The habit or manner of speaking very rapidly.
彼女(かのじょ)は早口(はやくち)で聞(き)き取(と)りにくい。
She speaks so fast that it's hard to catch what she says.
早口言葉(はやくちことば)を練習(れんしゅう)している。
I'm practicing tongue twisters.
もう少(すこ)しゆっくり話(はな)してください。早口(はやくち)で分(わ)かりません。
Please speak a little more slowly. You're speaking too fast for me to understand.

USAGE: 早口(はやくち) describes rapid speech, often seen as a characteristic of a person or a temporary manner of speaking.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 早口(はやくち)で話(はな)す (to speak quickly)
  • 早口(はやくち)になる (to start speaking quickly)
  • 早口(はやくち)の人(ひと) (fast talker)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 早口言葉(はやくちことば) (tongue twister)
  • 早口(はやくち)で説明(せつめい)する (to explain quickly)

CONTRAST:

  • 早口(はやくち) - fast-talking
  • ゆっくり - slowly
  • 口(くち)が重(おも)い - slow to speak, reticent
