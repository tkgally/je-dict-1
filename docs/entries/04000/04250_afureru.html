溢(あふ)れる

あふれる
verb (ichidan)
to overflow; to be filled with; to brim with
1. to overflow; to spill over
For liquid or contents to exceed a container's capacity and spill out.
お風呂(ふろ)のお湯(ゆ)が溢(あふ)れた。
The bathwater overflowed.
2. to be filled with; to brim with
To be full of an emotion, quality, or abundance of something.
彼女(かのじょ)は自信(じしん)に溢(あふ)れている。
She is brimming with confidence.
街(まち)には観光客(かんこうきゃく)が溢(あふ)れていた。
The town was overflowing with tourists.

溢(あふ)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) describing overflow or abundance.

ASPECT (ている):
溢(あふ)れている indicates something is currently overflowing or full of something.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [container]から[liquid]が溢(あふ)れる — [liquid] overflows from [container]
  • [quality]に溢(あふ)れる — to be full of [quality]
  • [place]に[things]が溢(あふ)れる — [place] is overflowing with [things]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 涙(なみだ)が溢(あふ)れる (tears overflow)
  • 愛情(あいじょう)に溢(あふ)れる (full of affection)
  • 活気(かっき)に溢(あふ)れる (full of vitality)
  • 笑顔(えがお)に溢(あふ)れる (full of smiles)

OFTEN WRITTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This verb is frequently written as あふれる rather than 溢(あふ)れる due to the kanji's complexity.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 零(こぼ)れる: to spill (smaller amounts, drops)
  • 溢(あふ)れる: to overflow (larger amounts, abundance)
