縮(ちぢ)める

ちぢめる
verb (ichidan)
to shorten; to shrink; to reduce
1. to shorten; to make shorter
To reduce the length or duration of something.
会議(かいぎ)の時間(じかん)を縮(ちぢ)めてください。
Please shorten the meeting time.
距離(きょり)を縮(ちぢ)めるために努力(どりょく)している。
I'm working hard to close the gap.
2. to shrink; to contract
To make something smaller by compressing or contracting it.
首(くび)を縮(ちぢ)めて寒(さむ)さに耐(た)えた。
I hunched my neck and endured the cold.

縮(ちぢ)める is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to make something shorter or smaller.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 縮(ちぢ)む (to shrink)

  • 縮(ちぢ)む (intransitive): The sweater shrinks.
  • 縮(ちぢ)める (transitive): I shorten the time.

ASPECT (ている):
縮(ちぢ)めている indicates the action of shortening is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を縮(ちぢ)める — to shorten/shrink [thing]
  • [distance/gap]を縮(ちぢ)める — to close [distance/gap]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時間(じかん)を縮(ちぢ)める (shorten time)
  • 距離(きょり)を縮(ちぢ)める (close the distance)
  • 差(さ)を縮(ちぢ)める (narrow the gap)
  • 身(み)を縮(ちぢ)める (shrink back, cringe)

FIGURATIVE USE:
距離(きょり)を縮(ちぢ)める can mean to become closer emotionally or to catch up in a competition.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 短(みじか)くする: to make short (more neutral)
  • 縮小(しゅくしょう)する: to reduce, scale down (formal)
