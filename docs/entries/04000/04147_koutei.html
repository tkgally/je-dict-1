校庭(こうてい)

こうてい
noun
schoolyard, school grounds, playground
1. schoolyard, school grounds, playground
The outdoor area of a school used for physical education, recess, and other activities.
子供(こども)たちが校庭(こうてい)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing in the schoolyard.
校庭(こうてい)で運動会(うんどうかい)が開(ひら)かれた。
A sports day was held in the schoolyard.
校庭(こうてい)は広(ひろ)くて緑(みどり)が多(おお)い。
The schoolyard is spacious with lots of greenery.

校庭(こうてい) refers to the outdoor grounds of a school, typically used for physical education, recess, and school events.

COMPONENTS:

  • 校(こう) (school) + 庭(てい) (yard, garden)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 校庭(こうてい)で遊(あそ)ぶ: to play in the schoolyard
  • 校庭(こうてい)を走(はし)る: to run around the schoolyard
  • 校庭(こうてい)に集(あつ)まる: to gather in the schoolyard

RELATED TERMS:

  • 運動場(うんどうじょう): playground/athletic field (can be larger or more formal)
  • 中庭(なかにわ): courtyard (enclosed garden area)
  • グラウンド: sports ground/field (from English 'ground')

USAGE:
校庭(こうてい) specifically refers to school grounds. For parks or public playgrounds, 公園(こうえん) is used instead.
