鼻水(はなみず)

はなみず
noun
runny nose; nasal discharge
1. runny nose; nasal mucus
Liquid discharge from the nose, typically due to a cold, allergies, or irritation.
風邪(かぜ)をひいて鼻水(はなみず)が止(と)まらない。
I caught a cold and can't stop my runny nose.
鼻水(はなみず)をティッシュで拭(ふ)いた。
I wiped my runny nose with a tissue.
花粉症(かふんしょう)で鼻水(はなみず)と目(め)の痒(かゆ)みがひどい。
My runny nose and itchy eyes are bad due to hay fever.
子(こ)どもが鼻水(はなみず)を垂(た)らしている。
The child has a runny nose.
鼻水(はなみず)がひどいときは薬(くすり)を飲(の)んで早(はや)めに休(やす)んだほうがいい。
When you have a bad runny nose, it's better to take medicine and rest early.

鼻水(はなみず) refers to nasal discharge (runny nose).

Common expressions:

  • 鼻水(はなみず)が出(で)る (to have a runny nose)
  • 鼻水(はなみず)が止(と)まらない (can't stop one's runny nose)
  • 鼻水(はなみず)を拭(ふ)く (to wipe one's nose)
  • 鼻水(はなみず)を垂(た)らす (to have a dripping nose)
  • 鼻水(はなみず)をすする (to sniff/sniffle)
  • 鼻(はな)をかむ (to blow one's nose)

Related symptoms:

  • くしゃみ (sneezing)
  • 鼻(はな)づまり (stuffy nose)
  • 咳(せき) (cough)

Note:
鼻水(はなみず) is a common symptom of both colds (風邪(かぜ)) and allergies (花粉症(かふんしょう)). The word literally means "nose water." More formal or medical terms include 鼻汁(びじゅう).
