真空(しんくう)

しんくう
noun
vacuum
1. vacuum
A space entirely devoid of matter, or a space with greatly reduced air pressure.
宇宙(うちゅう)は真空(しんくう)の状態(じょうたい)だ。
Space is in a vacuum state.
真空(しんくう)パックで食品(しょくひん)を保存(ほぞん)する。
I preserve food in vacuum packs.
真空パック is a common compound
真空(しんくう)状態(じょうたい)では音(おと)が伝(つた)わらない。
Sound doesn't travel in a vacuum.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 真空(しんくう)パック (vacuum pack/packaging)
  • 真空(しんくう)管(かん) (vacuum tube)
  • 真空(しんくう)掃除機(そうじき) (vacuum cleaner) - though this is different from scientific vacuum
  • 真空(しんくう)状態(じょうたい) (vacuum state/condition)

SCIENTIFIC NOTE:
In physics, a "perfect vacuum" is theoretical. In everyday use, 真空(しんくう) often refers to low-pressure environments or vacuum packaging for food preservation.
