汁椀(しるわん)

しるわん
noun
soup bowl
1. soup bowl; lacquered bowl for soup
soup bowl; lacquered bowl for soup
味噌汁(みそしる)を汁椀(しるわん)に注(そそ)いだ。
I poured miso soup into a soup bowl.
この汁椀(しるわん)は漆塗(うるしぬ)りで高級感(こうきゅうかん)がある。
This soup bowl has a luxurious feel because it's lacquered.
汁椀(しるわん)を両手(りょうて)で持(も)って飲(の)むのがマナーだ。
It's good manners to hold the soup bowl with both hands when drinking.

汁椀(しるわん) is a bowl specifically designed for soup, particularly 味噌汁(みそしる) and 吸(す)い物(もの). Traditional ones are made of wood with lacquer coating, though modern versions may be plastic or ceramic.

Features:

  • Often comes with a lid (蓋(ふた))
  • Has a foot (高台(こうだい)) to lift it off the table
  • Designed to be held in the hand while eating

In Japanese dining, soup is drunk directly from the bowl rather than eaten with a spoon.
