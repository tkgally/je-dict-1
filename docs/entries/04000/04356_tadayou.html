漂(ただよ)う

ただよう
verb (godan)
to drift, to float

Definitions

1. to drift, to float
To move slowly on water or in the air without direction or purpose.
2. to hang in the air, to pervade
For a scent, atmosphere, or feeling to be present and noticeable in a space.

Examples

雲(くも)が空(そら)を漂(ただよ)っている。
Clouds are drifting across the sky.
花(はな)の香(かお)りが部屋(へや)に漂(ただよ)っている。
The fragrance of flowers is wafting through the room.
Sense 2
不安(ふあん)な雰囲気(ふんいき)が漂(ただよ)っていた。
An uneasy atmosphere was hanging in the air.
Sense 2 - figurative

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No transitive pair. Pattern: Xが漂(ただよ)う (X drifts/floats).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state. 漂(ただよ)っている = 'is drifting/floating.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 海(うみ)を漂(ただよ)う (to drift on the sea)
  • 空気(くうき)が漂(ただよ)う (an atmosphere pervades)
  • 香(かお)りが漂(ただよ)う (a scent wafts)
  • 霧(きり)が漂(ただよ)う (mist drifts)

NUANCE: 漂(ただよ)う implies aimless, passive movement without control, often creating a poetic or atmospheric effect.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 浮(う)く - to float (stationary on surface)
  • 流(なが)れる - to flow (with direction)
  • 舞(ま)う - to flutter, dance in air
