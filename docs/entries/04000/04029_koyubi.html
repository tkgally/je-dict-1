小指(こゆび)

こゆび
noun
little finger, pinky
1. little finger, pinky (of the hand)
The smallest finger on the hand, located on the outer edge opposite the thumb.
約束(やくそく)するとき、小指(こゆび)を絡(から)める。
When making a promise, we link our little fingers.
Refers to 'pinky promise'
彼女(かのじょ)は小指(こゆび)に指輪(ゆびわ)をしていた。
She was wearing a ring on her little finger.
小指の指輪 = pinky ring
2. little toe, pinky toe
The smallest toe on the foot. Often specified as 足(あし)の小指(こゆび).
小指(こゆび)をぶつけて痛(いた)い。
I bumped my little toe and it hurts.
Context usually makes clear whether finger or toe

CULTURAL NOTE: In Japanese culture, linking little fingers (指切(ゆびき)り) is a traditional way of sealing a promise, similar to a 'pinky promise' in English. The full phrase is 指切(ゆびき)り拳万(げんまん).

RELATED FINGER NAMES:

  • 親指(おやゆび) (thumb)
  • 人差(ひとさ)し指(ゆび) (index finger)
  • 中指(なかゆび) (middle finger)
  • 薬指(くすりゆび) (ring finger)
  • 小指(こゆび) (little finger)

For the toe, it's common to say 足(あし)の小指(こゆび) to distinguish from the finger.
