逆様(さかさま)

さかさま
na-adjective
upside down, reverse
1. upside down, inverted
With the top and bottom positions reversed.
絵(え)が逆様(さかさま)に掛(か)かっている。
The picture is hanging upside down.
本(ほん)を逆様(さかさま)に持(も)っていた。
I was holding the book upside down.
2. reverse, opposite
In the opposite order or direction.
順番(じゅんばん)が逆様(さかさま)になった。
The order became reversed.

逆様(さかさま) can be used as a na-adjective or adverbially.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 逆様(さかさま)な
  • Adverbial: 逆様(さかさま)に

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 逆様(さかさま)にする: to turn upside down
  • 逆様(さかさま)になる: to become upside down
  • 逆様(さかさま)に読(よ)む: to read in reverse

RELATED WORDS:

  • 逆(ぎゃく): reverse, opposite
  • 反対(はんたい): opposite
  • 裏返(うらがえ)し: inside out
