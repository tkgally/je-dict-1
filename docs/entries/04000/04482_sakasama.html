逆様(さかさま)

さかさま
na-adjective
upside down, reverse
See also: 逆(さか)さま (upside down (逆さま))
1. upside down, inverted
With the top and bottom positions reversed.
絵(え)が逆様(さかさま)に掛(か)かっている。
The picture is hanging upside down.
本(ほん)を逆様(さかさま)に持(も)っていた。
I was holding the book upside down.
カップを逆様(さかさま)にして乾(かわ)かす。
I dry the cup by turning it upside down.
2. reverse, opposite
In the opposite order or direction.
順番(じゅんばん)が逆様(さかさま)になった。
The order became reversed.
靴(くつ)を左右(さゆう)逆様(さかさま)に履(は)いていた。
I was wearing my shoes on the wrong feet.
話(はなし)の順番(じゅんばん)が逆様(さかさま)で、最初(さいしょ)に結論(けつろん)を言(い)ってしまった。
The order of the story was reversed, and I stated the conclusion first.

逆様(さかさま) describes something being upside down, inverted, or in reverse order. It functions as a na-adjective and is often used adverbially with に.

KANJI:
More commonly written in kana as さかさま or 逆(さか)さま. The kanji 逆様(さかさま) is less frequent.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 逆(さか)さまな状態(じょうたい) (an upside-down state)
  • Adverbial: 逆(さか)さまに持(も)つ (to hold upside down)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 逆(さか)さまにする: to turn upside down
  • 逆(さか)さまになる: to become upside down/reversed
  • 左右(さゆう)逆(さか)さま: left and right reversed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆(ぎゃく): reverse, opposite (more abstract/general)
  • 反対(はんたい): opposite (direction, opinion)
  • 裏返(うらがえ)し: inside out, turned over
