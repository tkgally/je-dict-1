絨毯(じゅうたん)

じゅうたん
noun
carpet

Definitions

1. carpet; rug
carpet; rug

Examples

絨毯(じゅうたん)の上(うえ)に座(すわ)って本(ほん)を読(よ)んだ。
I sat on the carpet and read a book.
ペルシャ絨毯(じゅうたん)は高価(こうか)で美(うつく)しい。
Persian carpets are expensive and beautiful.
絨毯(じゅうたん)に飲(の)み物(もの)をこぼしてしまった。
I spilled a drink on the carpet.

Notes

絨毯(じゅうたん) refers to thick woven floor coverings, typically wall-to-wall carpets or large decorative rugs. Also commonly written in katakana as じゅうたん.

Related terms:

  • カーペット (carpet - loanword, more casual)
  • ラグ (smaller rug)
  • マット (mat)
  • 敷物(しきもの) (general term for floor coverings)

Common expressions:

  • 絨毯(じゅうたん)を敷(し)く (lay/spread a carpet)
  • 絨毯(じゅうたん)を掃除機(そうじき)で掃除(そうじ)する (vacuum the carpet)
  • 赤(あか)い絨毯(じゅうたん) (red carpet)
