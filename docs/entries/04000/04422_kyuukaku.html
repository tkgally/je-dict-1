嗅覚(きゅうかく)

きゅうかく
noun
sense of smell
1. sense of smell; olfaction
The ability to perceive odors through the nose. One of the five senses.
犬(いぬ)は嗅覚(きゅうかく)が非常(ひじょう)に発達(はったつ)している。
Dogs have a highly developed sense of smell.
風邪(かぜ)をひいて嗅覚(きゅうかく)が鈍(にぶ)くなった。
I caught a cold and my sense of smell became dull.
嗅覚(きゅうかく)で食(た)べ物(もの)の新鮮(しんせん)さが分(わ)かる。
You can tell the freshness of food by smell.

嗅覚(きゅうかく) refers to the sense of smell.

The five senses (五感(ごかん)):

  • 視覚(しかく) (sight)
  • 聴覚(ちょうかく) (hearing)
  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく) (smell)
  • 味覚(みかく) (taste)
  • 触覚(しょっかく) (touch)

Common expressions:

  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく)が鋭(するど)い (to have a keen sense of smell)
  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく)が鈍(にぶ)い (to have a dull sense of smell)
  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく)障害(しょうがい) (olfactory dysfunction)
  • 嗅覚(きゅうかく)を失(うしな)う (to lose one's sense of smell)

Note:
Loss of 嗅覚(きゅうかく) was recognized as a COVID-19 symptom and brought attention to this often-overlooked sense. The Japanese word for smell as a noun is 匂(にお)い/臭(にお)い, while 嗅覚(きゅうかく) refers specifically to the sensory ability.
