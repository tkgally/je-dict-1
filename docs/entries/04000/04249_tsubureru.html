1. to be crushed; to be squashed
For something to be flattened or deformed by pressure.
空(あ)き缶(かん)が潰(つぶ)れた。
The empty can got crushed.
トマトが潰(つぶ)れてしまった。
The tomato got squashed.
箱(はこ)が潰(つぶ)れていた。
The box was crushed.
ボールが潰(つぶ)れた。
The ball got flattened.
目(め)が潰(つぶ)れるほど眩(まぶ)しい。
It's so bright it could blind you.
2. to collapse; to fail
For a business, plan, or organization to fail or go bankrupt.
あの店(みせ)は去年(きょねん)潰(つぶ)れた。
That store went out of business last year.
不況(ふきょう)で会社(かいしゃ)が潰(つぶ)れた。
The company went bankrupt due to the recession.
計画(けいかく)が潰(つぶ)れてしまった。
The plan fell through.
銀行(ぎんこう)が潰(つぶ)れる心配(しんぱい)がある。
There's concern the bank might collapse.
商談(しょうだん)が潰(つぶ)れた。
The business deal fell through.
3. to be wasted; to be ruined
For time, a day, or opportunity to be lost or wasted.
雨(あめ)で休日(きゅうじつ)が潰(つぶ)れてしまった。
The rain ruined my day off.
午後(ごご)が潰(つぶ)れた。
My afternoon was wasted.
予定(よてい)が潰(つぶ)れた。
My plans were ruined.
週末(しゅうまつ)が仕事(しごと)で潰(つぶ)れた。
My weekend was consumed by work.
声(こえ)が潰(つぶ)れている。
My voice is hoarse.

潰(つぶ)れる is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to be crushed or to fail.

TRANSITIVE PAIR: 潰(つぶ)す (to crush, to destroy)

  • 潰(つぶ)れる: The can gets crushed.
  • 潰(つぶ)す: I crush the can.

ASPECT (ている):
潰(つぶ)れている indicates something has been crushed/has failed and remains so.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]が潰(つぶ)れる — [thing] gets crushed/fails
  • [business]が潰(つぶ)れる — [business] goes bankrupt

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 会社(かいしゃ)が潰(つぶ)れる (company goes bankrupt)
  • 予定(よてい)が潰(つぶ)れる (plans fall through)
  • 声(こえ)が潰(つぶ)れる (voice becomes hoarse)
  • お酒(さけ)で潰(つぶ)れる (to pass out drunk)

COLLOQUIAL USE:
潰(つぶ)れる is commonly used for businesses closing, but also casually for getting too drunk (お酒(さけ)で潰(つぶ)れる).
