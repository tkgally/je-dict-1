1. to inhale, to breathe in
To draw air or other gases into the lungs.
深(ふか)く息(いき)を吸(す)ってください。
Please take a deep breath.
山(やま)の新鮮(しんせん)な空気(くうき)を吸(す)った。
I breathed in the fresh mountain air.
鼻(はな)から息(いき)を吸(す)って、口(くち)から吐(は)いてください。
Breathe in through your nose and out through your mouth.
2. to smoke
To draw smoke from a cigarette into the mouth or lungs.
タバコを吸(す)いますか。
Do you smoke?
ここは禁煙(きんえん)なのでタバコは吸(す)えません。
This is a non-smoking area, so you can't smoke here.
彼(かれ)は一日(いちにち)に一箱(ひとはこ)タバコを吸(す)う。
He smokes a pack of cigarettes a day.
3. to suck, to sip
To draw liquid into the mouth, or for something to absorb liquid.
蚊(か)に血(ち)を吸(す)われた。
A mosquito sucked my blood.
スポンジが水(みず)を吸(す)った。
The sponge absorbed the water.
赤(あか)ちゃんが指(ゆび)を吸(す)っている。
The baby is sucking its finger.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Intransitive pair: 吸(す)われる (passive). Pattern: Xを吸(す)う (to inhale/suck X).

ASPECT: ている indicates habitual action. タバコを吸(す)っている can mean 'smokes (habitually)' or 'is smoking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 息(いき)を吸(す)う (to inhale, breathe in)
  • タバコを吸(す)う (to smoke)
  • 空気(くうき)を吸(す)う (to breathe air)
  • 血(ち)を吸(す)う (to suck blood)

CONTRAST:

  • 吸(す)う - to inhale/suck in
  • 吐(は)く - to exhale/spit out

NOTE: タバコを吸(す)う is the standard expression for 'to smoke.'

Antonym: 吐(は)く (to exhale)
