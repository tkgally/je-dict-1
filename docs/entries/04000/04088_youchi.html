幼稚(ようち)

ようち
na-adjective, noun
childish, infantile, immature
1. childish, infantile, immature
Characteristic of or appropriate for a young child; lacking sophistication or maturity.
2. early childhood, infancy
Relating to the stage of early childhood development (neutral usage).
そんな幼稚(ようち)な言(い)い訳(わけ)は通(とお)らない。
Such a childish excuse won't work.
彼(かれ)の考(かんが)え方(かた)は幼稚(ようち)だ。
His way of thinking is immature.
子供(こども)は幼稚(ようち)園で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing at kindergarten.
Neutral usage in 幼稚(ようち)園(えん) (kindergarten)

USAGE: 幼稚(ようち) has two main contexts:
1. Negative: describing immature or childish behavior in adults (pejorative)
2. Neutral: relating to early childhood education

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 幼稚(ようち)な考(かんが)え (childish thinking)
  • 幼稚(ようち)な行動(こうどう) (immature behavior)

COMPOUND:

  • 幼稚(ようち)園(えん) (kindergarten) - very common word using neutral sense

RELATED:

  • 子供(こども)っぽい - childish (more colloquial)
  • 未熟(みじゅく) - immature, inexperienced
  • 大人(おとな)げない - unbecoming of an adult
