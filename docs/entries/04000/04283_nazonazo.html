謎々(なぞなぞ)

なぞなぞ
noun
riddle
1. riddle, puzzle
A question or statement phrased to require clever thinking to answer, often used as a game or entertainment, especially for children.
子供(こども)たちと謎々(なぞなぞ)をして遊(あそ)んだ。
I played riddles with the children.
この謎々(なぞなぞ)の答(こた)えが分(わ)かる？
Do you know the answer to this riddle?
謎々(なぞなぞ)を出(だ)すから当(あ)ててみて。
I'll give you a riddle, try to guess it.

USAGE: 謎々(なぞなぞ) is the reduplicated form of 謎(なぞ) (mystery/riddle). It specifically refers to playful riddles, often word-play based, typically enjoyed by children.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 謎々(なぞなぞ)を出(だ)す (to give a riddle)
  • 謎々(なぞなぞ)を解(と)く (to solve a riddle)
  • 謎々(なぞなぞ)の答(こた)え (the answer to a riddle)

WRITING: Can also be written as なぞなぞ in hiragana.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 謎(なぞ) - mystery, enigma (more serious)
  • クイズ - quiz (knowledge-based)
