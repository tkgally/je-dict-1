早速(さっそく)

さっそく
adverb
at once, immediately, right away
1. at once, immediately, right away, promptly
Indicates that an action is taken without delay, often in response to something.
早速(さっそく)始(はじ)めましょう。
Let's get started right away.
メールを受(う)け取(と)って早速(さっそく)返事(へんじ)した。
I received the email and replied immediately.
新(あたら)しいレシピを早速(さっそく)試(ため)してみた。
I tried out the new recipe right away.

早速(さっそく) conveys acting promptly, often in eager response to new information or an opportunity. It carries a positive nuance of enthusiasm, not just speed.

POSITION:
Typically appears at the beginning of a sentence or directly before the verb.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 早速(さっそく)ですが…: Getting right to the point... (polite transition in business)
  • 早速(さっそく)試(ため)す: to try right away
  • 早速(さっそく)使(つか)ってみる: to try using it right away

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すぐに: immediately (neutral, no eagerness nuance)
  • 直(ただ)ちに: immediately (formal, urgent)
  • 即座(そくざ)に: instantly (no delay at all)

NOTE:
早速(さっそく) implies willing, eager action. It would sound odd for reluctant or forced immediate action.
