早速(さっそく)

さっそく
adverb
at once, immediately, right away

Definitions

1. at once, immediately, right away, promptly
Indicates that an action is taken without delay, often in response to something.

Examples

早速(さっそく)始(はじ)めましょう。
Let's get started right away.
メールを受(う)け取(と)って早速(さっそく)返事(へんじ)した。
I received the email and replied immediately.
新(あたら)しいレシピを早速(さっそく)試(ため)してみた。
I tried out the new recipe right away.

Notes

POSITION: Typically appears at the beginning of a sentence or before the verb.

USAGE: Often used when taking action in response to new information or an opportunity. Conveys eagerness or promptness.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すぐに - immediately, at once
  • 直(ただ)ちに - immediately (more formal)
  • 即座(そくざ)に - instantly
