農村(のうそん)

のうそん
noun
farming village, rural area
1. farming village, agricultural community, rural area
A village or community where agriculture is the main occupation and way of life.
祖父母(そふぼ)は農村(のうそん)に住(す)んでいます。
My grandparents live in a farming village.
農村(のうそん)から都市(とし)への人口(じんこう)移動(いどう)が続(つづ)いている。
The migration from rural areas to cities continues.
日本(にほん)の農村(のうそん)は高齢化(こうれいか)が進(すす)んでいる。
Aging is advancing in Japan's rural farming communities.
Social issue

USAGE: 農村(のうそん) specifically refers to communities centered on agriculture, distinct from general countryside.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 農村(のうそん)に住(す)む (to live in a farming village)
  • 農村(のうそん)地域(ちいき) (rural agricultural area)
  • 農村(のうそん)の生活(せいかつ) (rural life)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 農村(のうそん)部(ぶ) (rural farming district)
  • 農村(のうそん)地帯(ちたい) (agricultural zone)
  • 農村(のうそん)振興(しんこう) (rural development)

CONTRAST:

  • 農村(のうそん) - farming village (agriculture-focused)
  • 田舎(いなか) - countryside (general)
  • 漁村(ぎょそん) - fishing village
