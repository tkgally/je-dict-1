為替(かわせ)

かわせ
noun
exchange; money order
1. exchange (currency/foreign)
The system of exchanging one currency for another; foreign exchange.
為替(かわせ)レートを確認(かくにん)する。
To check the exchange rate.
円(えん)安(やす)で為替(かわせ)差損(さそん)が出(で)た。
The weak yen caused a foreign exchange loss.
2. money order; draft
A method of transferring money without using cash, through banks or postal services.
郵便(ゆうびん)為替(かわせ)で送金(そうきん)する。
To send money by postal money order.

READING: This is an irregular reading (ateji).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 外国(がいこく)為替(かわせ) (foreign exchange)
  • 為替(かわせ)レート (exchange rate)
  • 為替(かわせ)市場(しじょう) (exchange market)
  • 為替(かわせ)相場(そうば) (exchange rate)
  • 郵便(ゆうびん)為替(かわせ) (postal money order)
  • 為替(かわせ)差益(さえき) (exchange gain)
  • 為替(かわせ)差損(さそん) (exchange loss)

BUSINESS/FINANCE: Essential term for international trade and financial markets.
