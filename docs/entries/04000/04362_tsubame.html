燕(つばめ)

つばめ
noun
swallow (bird)
1. swallow (bird)
A small, agile bird with a forked tail, known for its swift flight and seasonal migration. Swallows are harbingers of spring in Japan.
春(はる)になると燕(つばめ)が戻(もど)ってくる。
Swallows return when spring comes.
燕(つばめ)が軒下(のきした)に巣(す)を作(つく)った。
Swallows built a nest under the eaves.
燕(つばめ)は虫(むし)を捕(と)りながら飛(と)ぶ。
Swallows catch insects while flying.

燕(つばめ) refers to swallows.

Alternative writing: ツバメ (katakana)

Common expressions:

  • 燕(つばめ)の巣(す) (swallow's nest - also a Chinese delicacy)
  • 燕(つばめ)返(がえ)し (a sword technique; also a quick reversal)
  • 燕(つばめ) (slang: a younger boyfriend of an older woman)

Counter: 羽(わ)

Cultural note:
Swallows nesting under the eaves of a house are considered good luck in Japan. People protect swallow nests and watch the chicks grow. The proverb 「燕(つばめ)が低(ひく)く飛(と)ぶと雨(あめ)が降(ふ)る」 (when swallows fly low, rain will fall) is a traditional weather prediction.

The Tsubame Sanjo area of Niigata is famous for metalwork, and the Shinkansen "Tsubame" serves Kyushu.
