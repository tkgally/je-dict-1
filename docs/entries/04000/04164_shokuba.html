職場(しょくば)

しょくば
noun
workplace, office
1. workplace, place of work
The location where one works, including offices, factories, stores, and other work environments.
職場(しょくば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)はとてもいいです。
The atmosphere at my workplace is very good.
職場(しょくば)で人間関係(にんげんかんけい)に悩(なや)んでいる。
I'm troubled by interpersonal relationships at work.
新(あたら)しい職場(しょくば)に慣(な)れるまで時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to get used to my new workplace.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 職場(しょくば)の環境(かんきょう) (work environment)
  • 職場(しょくば)の同僚(どうりょう) (coworkers)
  • 職場(しょくば)に通(かよ)う (to commute to work)
  • 職場(しょくば)を変(か)える (to change jobs/workplace)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仕事場(しごとば) - similar but often implies a physical space where work is done (studio, workshop)
  • 勤務先(きんむさき) - formal term for one's place of employment
  • 会社(かいしゃ) - company (more specific to corporate employment)
