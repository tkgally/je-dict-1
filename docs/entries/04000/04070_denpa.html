電波(でんぱ)

でんぱ
noun
radio waves, electromagnetic waves
1. radio waves, electromagnetic waves
Electromagnetic radiation used for communication, broadcasting, and other technological applications.
2. signal, reception (informal)
In casual usage, refers to the quality of wireless signal reception.
ここは電波(でんぱ)が悪(わる)い。
The reception is bad here.
Common casual usage
電波(でんぱ)で情報(じょうほう)を送信(そうしん)する。
Information is transmitted via radio waves.
電波(でんぱ)時計(どけい)は正確(せいかく)だ。
Radio-controlled clocks are accurate.
電波時計 = radio-controlled clock

TECHNICAL USE:

  • 電波(でんぱ)を発信(はっしん)する (to transmit radio waves)
  • 電波(でんぱ)を受信(じゅしん)する (to receive radio waves)
  • 電波(でんぱ)障害(しょうがい) (radio interference)
  • 電波(でんぱ)法(ほう) (Radio Law)

EVERYDAY USE:

  • 電波(でんぱ)が悪(わる)い (poor signal/reception)
  • 電波(でんぱ)が入(はい)らない (no signal)
  • 電波(でんぱ)時計(どけい) (radio-controlled clock)

SLANG (dated): 電波(でんぱ)系 was used to describe someone who seems disconnected from reality or has strange ideas (not recommended to use).

SCIENCE: 電波(でんぱ) is part of the electromagnetic spectrum, with frequencies lower than infrared.
