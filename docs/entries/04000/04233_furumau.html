1. to behave; to conduct oneself
To act in a certain manner, especially in social situations.
大人(おとな)らしく振(ふ)る舞(ま)いなさい。
Behave like an adult.
何(なに)も知(し)らないふりをして振(ふ)る舞(ま)った。
I acted as if I knew nothing.
初(はじ)めての場所(ばしょ)でも堂々(どうどう)と振(ふ)る舞(ま)える人(ひと)を尊敬(そんけい)する。
I admire people who can carry themselves confidently even in unfamiliar places.
2. to treat to; to serve
To offer food or drink to others as a host.
料理(りょうり)を振(ふ)る舞(ま)う。
To serve food to guests.
友人(ゆうじん)が手作(てづく)りのケーキを振(ふ)る舞(ま)ってくれた。
My friend treated us to homemade cake.
正月(しょうがつ)に親戚(しんせき)が集(あつ)まると、祖母(そぼ)はいつも手料理(てりょうり)を振(ふ)る舞(ま)ってくれた。
Whenever relatives gathered for New Year's, my grandmother would always serve us her home cooking.

振(ふ)る舞(ま)う has two distinct meanings: behavior and hospitality. Both are common in everyday usage.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Sense 1 (behave): intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Sense 2 (treat to): transitive (他動詞(たどうし))

ASPECT (ている):
振(ふ)る舞(ま)っている indicates ongoing behavior or acting a certain way.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [manner]に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う — to behave in [manner]
  • [person]に[food]を振(ふ)る舞(ま)う — to treat [person] to [food]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 普通(ふつう)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う (behave normally)
  • 紳士的(しんしてき)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う (conduct oneself like a gentleman)
  • 料理(りょうり)を振(ふ)る舞(ま)う (serve food to guests)

NOUN FORM:
振(ふ)る舞(ま)い means "behavior" or "conduct," and also "treat" or "feast."
