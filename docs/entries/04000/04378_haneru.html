跳(は)ねる

はねる
verb (ichidan)
to jump, to bounce
1. to jump, to leap, to hop
To spring up from the ground or a surface.
2. to bounce, to splash
For something to spring back or splatter.
兎(うさぎ)がぴょんぴょん}{跳(は)ねている。
The rabbit is hopping around.
油(あぶら)が跳(は)ねて火傷(やけど)をした。
The oil splattered and I got burned.
Sense 2
ボールが跳(は)ねて取(と)りにくかった。
The ball bounced and was hard to catch.
Sense 2

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Transitive pair: 跳(は)ねる can also be used transitively in some contexts. Pattern: Xが跳(は)ねる (X jumps/bounces).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 跳(は)ねている = 'is jumping/bouncing.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぴょんぴょん}{跳(は)ねる (to hop around)
  • 油(あぶら)が跳(は)ねる (oil splatters)
  • 水(みず)が跳(は)ねる (water splashes)
  • 髪(かみ)が跳(は)ねる (hair sticks out)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 撥(は)ねる - to reject, hit (different kanji)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 跳(と)ぶ - to jump (higher, more distance)
  • 弾(は)む - to bounce, spring back
