零(こぼ)す

こぼす
verb (godan)
to spill; to complain; to let slip
1. to spill; to drop
To accidentally cause liquid or small items to fall out of a container.
コーヒーを零(こぼ)してしまった。
I spilled the coffee.
涙(なみだ)を零(こぼ)しながら話(はな)した。
She spoke while shedding tears.
2. to complain; to grumble
To express dissatisfaction, often repeatedly.
彼(かれ)はいつも愚痴(ぐち)を零(こぼ)している。
He's always complaining.

零(こぼ)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) with both literal and figurative meanings.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 零(こぼ)れる (to spill, overflow)

  • 零(こぼ)す: I spill water.
  • 零(こぼ)れる: Water spills.

ASPECT (ている):
零(こぼ)している can mean currently spilling or habitually complaining.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [liquid]を零(こぼ)す — to spill [liquid]
  • 愚痴(ぐち)を零(こぼ)す — to complain, grumble
  • 涙(なみだ)を零(こぼ)す — to shed tears

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 水(みず)を零(こぼ)す (spill water)
  • 食(た)べ物(もの)を零(こぼ)す (drop food)
  • 愚痴(ぐち)を零(こぼ)す (complain, grumble)
  • 弱音(よわね)を零(こぼ)す (show weakness, complain about hardship)

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
Both 零(こぼ)す and こぼす are common. The kanji is the same as "zero" but has this separate native reading.
