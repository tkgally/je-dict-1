仕込(しこ)む

しこむ
verb (godan)
to train; to prepare; to stock up

Definitions

1. to train; to teach (skills)
To thoroughly train someone in a skill, trade, or discipline.
2. to prepare (food/drinks); to brew
To prepare ingredients or ferment products like sake, miso, or soy sauce.
3. to stock up; to lay in
To purchase and store goods for future sale or use.

Examples

師匠(ししょう)に芸(げい)を仕込(しこ)まれた。
I was trained in the art by my master.
味噌(みそ)を仕込(しこ)むのは冬(ふゆ)がいい。
Winter is the best time to prepare miso.
新(あたら)しい商品(しょうひん)を仕込(しこ)んでおいた。
I stocked up on new products.

Notes

仕込(しこ)む is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) with origins in traditional apprenticeship and food preparation. It implies thorough preparation over time.

ASPECT (ている):
仕込(しこ)んでいる indicates preparation is in progress. For fermentation, 仕込(しこ)んである indicates something has been prepared and is now aging.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [person]に[skill]を仕込(しこ)む — to train [person] in [skill]
  • [product]を仕込(しこ)む — to prepare/brew [product]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 味噌(みそ)を仕込(しこ)む (prepare miso)
  • 酒(さけ)を仕込(しこ)む (brew sake)
  • 弟子(でし)を仕込(しこ)む (train an apprentice)
  • 商品(しょうひん)を仕込(しこ)む (stock up on merchandise)

TRADITIONAL CONTEXT:
Originating from traditional crafts and brewing, 仕込(しこ)む implies patient, thorough preparation and often strict discipline in training.

NOUN FORM:
仕込(しこ)み (preparation, training, stock)
