夜行(やこう)

やこう
noun
traveling by night, night train
1. night train, overnight travel
Travel that takes place during nighttime hours, or a train/bus that operates overnight.
夜行(やこう)バスで大阪(おおさか)に行(い)った。
I went to Osaka by overnight bus.
夜行(やこう)列車(れっしゃ)は安(やす)いが疲(つか)れる。
Night trains are cheap but tiring.
2. nocturnal activity
Being active at night, especially referring to animals that are nocturnal.
フクロウは夜行(やこう)性(せい)の鳥(とり)です。
Owls are nocturnal birds.

USAGE: 夜行(やこう) often appears in compound words related to overnight transportation.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 夜行(やこう)バス (overnight bus)
  • 夜行(やこう)列車(れっしゃ) (night train)
  • 夜行(やこう)性(せい) (nocturnal - for animals)
  • 夜行(やこう)便(びん) (overnight flight/service)

CONTRAST:

  • 夜行(やこう) vs 日帰(ひがえ)り - overnight travel vs day trip
