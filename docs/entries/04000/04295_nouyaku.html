農薬(のうやく)

のうやく
noun
agricultural chemicals, pesticide

Definitions

1. agricultural chemicals, pesticide, agrochemicals
Chemicals used in farming to protect crops from pests, diseases, and weeds.

Examples

この野菜(やさい)は農薬(のうやく)を使(つか)っていません。
These vegetables were grown without pesticides.
農薬(のうやく)の使(し)い過(す)ぎは環境(かんきょう)に悪(わる)い。
Overuse of pesticides is bad for the environment.
有機(ゆうき)栽培(さいばい)は農薬(のうやく)を使(つか)わない。
Organic farming doesn't use pesticides.

Notes

USAGE: 農薬(のうやく) is a general term for all agricultural chemicals, including pesticides, herbicides, and fungicides.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 農薬(のうやく)を使(つか)う (to use pesticides)
  • 農薬(のうやく)を撒(ま)く (to spray pesticides)
  • 無(む)農薬(のうやく) (pesticide-free)
  • 減(げん)農薬(のうやく) (reduced pesticides)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 農薬(のうやく)散布(さんぷ) (pesticide spraying)
  • 残留(ざんりゅう)農薬(のうやく) (pesticide residue)
  • 有機(ゆうき)農薬(のうやく) (organic pesticides)

NOTE: Consumer awareness about 農薬(のうやく) has increased, leading to growth in organic (有機(ゆうき)) and pesticide-free (無(む)農薬(のうやく)) produce.
