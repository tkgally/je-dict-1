観念(かんねん)

かんねん
noun, suru-verb
idea, concept, notion; resignation
1. idea, concept, notion
An abstract thought or general understanding about something.
2. resignation, acceptance of one's fate
The act of giving up and accepting an inevitable outcome.
時間(じかん)の観念(かんねん)がない人(ひと)は困(こま)る。
People with no sense of time are troublesome.
Sense 1
固定(こてい)観念(かんねん)にとらわれてはいけない。
You shouldn't be bound by fixed ideas.
Sense 1
もう逃(に)げられない、観念(かんねん)しろ。
You can't escape anymore. Give up.
Sense 2 - often in imperative

USAGE: 観念(かんねん) has two distinct meanings depending on context.

SENSE 1 - CONCEPT:

  • 時間(じかん)の観念(かんねん) (sense of time)
  • 固定(こてい)観念(かんねん) (fixed idea, preconception)
  • 先入観(せんにゅうかん)/観念(かんねん) (preconceived notion)
  • 道徳(どうとく)観念(かんねん) (moral sense)

SENSE 2 - RESIGNATION:

  • 観念(かんねん)する (to give up, resign oneself)
  • 観念(かんねん)しろ (Give up! - imperative)

PHILOSOPHICAL USE: In philosophy, 観念(かんねん) translates 'idea' in the philosophical sense (e.g., 観念論(かんねんろん) = idealism).

RELATED:

  • 概念(がいねん) - concept (more formal/technical)
  • 考(かんが)え - thought, idea (more casual)
