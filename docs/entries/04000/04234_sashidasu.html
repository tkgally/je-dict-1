差(さ)し出(だ)す

さしだす
verb (godan)
to hold out; to present; to submit
1. to hold out; to extend
To stretch out one's hand or an object toward someone.
彼女(かのじょ)は笑顔(えがお)で手(て)を差(さ)し出(だ)した。
She held out her hand with a smile.
2. to present; to offer
To formally offer or present something to someone.
名刺(めいし)を差(さ)し出(だ)して自己紹介(じこしょうかい)した。
I presented my business card and introduced myself.
3. to submit; to send
To submit documents or send mail.
差出人(さしだしにん)の住所(じゅうしょ)が書(か)いてなかった。
The sender's address wasn't written.

差(さ)し出(だ)す is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 差(さ)す (to hold up) + 出(だ)す (to put out). It suggests a polite or formal gesture of offering.

ASPECT (ている):
差(さ)し出(だ)している indicates the ongoing action of holding something out.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を差(さ)し出(だ)す — to hold out/present [thing]
  • [person]に[thing]を差(さ)し出(だ)す — to present [thing] to [person]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手(て)を差(さ)し出(だ)す (hold out one's hand)
  • 名刺(めいし)を差(さ)し出(だ)す (present a business card)
  • 手紙(てがみ)を差(さ)し出(だ)す (send a letter)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 差出人(さしだしにん): sender (of mail)
  • 差(さ)し入(い)れ: gift brought when visiting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出(だ)す: to take out (neutral)
  • 提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit (formal documents)
