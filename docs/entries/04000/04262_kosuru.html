擦(こす)る

こする
verb (godan)
to rub; to scrub
1. to rub; to scrub
To move something back and forth against a surface with pressure.
目(め)を擦(こす)ってはいけません。
You shouldn't rub your eyes.
たわしで鍋(なべ)を擦(こす)って汚(よご)れを落(お)とした。
I scrubbed the pot with a scrub brush to remove the stains.
2. to graze; to scrape against
To brush or scrape lightly against something while passing.
壁(かべ)に車(くるま)を擦(こす)ってしまった。
I scraped the car against the wall.

擦(こす)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to rub or scrub with friction.

INTRANSITIVE: 擦(こす)れる (to rub against, to become worn)

ASPECT (ている):
擦(こす)っている indicates the rubbing action is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [body part]を擦(こす)る — to rub [body part]
  • [object]を[surface]に擦(こす)る — to scrape [object] against [surface]
  • [thing]で擦(こす)る — to scrub with [thing]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 目(め)を擦(こす)る (rub one's eyes)
  • 背中(せなか)を擦(こす)る (scrub one's back)
  • マッチを擦(こす)る (strike a match)
  • 車(くるま)を擦(こす)る (scrape a car)

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
This verb is frequently written as こする.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 揉(も)む: to knead, massage (more pressing)
  • 擦(こす)る: to rub, scrub (more friction)
  • 撫(な)でる: to stroke (gentle, one direction)
