一旦(いったん)

いったん
adverb
once, for a moment, temporarily
1. once, when once
Used to indicate that once something happens, a consequence follows.
一旦(いったん)決(き)めたら最後(さいご)までやる。
Once I've decided, I see it through to the end.
2. for the time being, temporarily
For a short period before continuing or changing.
一旦(いったん)休憩(きゅうけい)しましょう。
Let's take a break for now.
一旦(いったん)帰(かえ)ってから出直(でなお)します。
I'll go home first and then come back.

USAGE: 一旦(いったん) has two main uses:

1. CONDITIONAL (once...): Often followed by と or ば

  • 一旦(いったん)〜と/ば (once ~ happens)
  • Emphasizes that something triggers an irreversible or significant change

2. TEMPORARY (for now): Indicates a pause before continuing

  • 一旦(いったん)止(と)める (to stop temporarily)
  • 一旦(いったん)保留(ほりゅう)にする (to put on hold for now)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一旦(いったん)〜したら (once you do ~)
  • 一旦(いったん)家(いえ)に帰(かえ)る (to go home first)
  • 一旦(いったん)落(お)ち着(つ)く (to calm down for a moment)
