打(う)ち合(あ)わせる

うちあわせる
verb (ichidan)
to arrange; to discuss; to have a meeting
1. to have a meeting; to discuss
To meet and discuss plans, schedules, or details in advance.
イベントの詳細(しょうさい)について打(う)ち合(あ)わせた。
We had a meeting about the details of the event.
明日(あした)、クライアントと打(う)ち合(あ)わせがある。
I have a meeting with the client tomorrow.
2. to arrange; to coordinate
To coordinate timing or actions with another person.
二人(ふたり)は事前(じぜん)に打(う)ち合(あ)わせていたようだ。
It seems the two of them had arranged things beforehand.

打(う)ち合(あ)わせる is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 打(う)つ (to strike) + 合(あ)わせる (to match). Originally meaning to strike things together, it now means to coordinate or meet for discussion.

ASPECT (ている):
打(う)ち合(あ)わせている indicates a meeting is currently in progress.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [topic]について打(う)ち合(あ)わせる — to discuss [topic]
  • [person]と打(う)ち合(あ)わせる — to have a meeting with [person]

NOUN FORM:

打(う)ち合(あ)わせ is the extremely common noun form meaning "meeting" or "briefing" in business contexts.

  • 打(う)ち合(あ)わせがある (have a meeting)
  • 打(う)ち合(あ)わせに行(い)く (go to a meeting)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 事前(じぜん)に打(う)ち合(あ)わせる (arrange in advance)
  • スケジュールを打(う)ち合(あ)わせる (coordinate schedules)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会議(かいぎ): formal meeting (larger, more structured)
  • ミーティング: meeting (borrowed from English)
  • 打(う)ち合(あ)わせ: smaller, working-level discussion
