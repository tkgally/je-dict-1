小皿(こざら)

こざら
noun
small plate

Definitions

1. small plate; small dish
small plate; small dish

Examples

醤油(しょうゆ)を小皿(こざら)に入(い)れた。
I poured soy sauce into a small dish.
取(と)り皿(ざら)として小皿(こざら)を使(つか)ってください。
Please use a small plate as a serving plate.
小皿(こざら)に薬味(やくみ)を盛(も)り付(つ)けた。
I arranged the condiments on a small plate.

Notes

小皿(こざら) are small dishes used for serving condiments, sauces, and small portions of food. They are essential in Japanese table settings.

Common uses:

  • 醤油皿(しょうゆざら) (for soy sauce)
  • 取(と)り皿(ざら) (individual serving plate)
  • 薬味(やくみ)皿(ざら) (for condiments like ginger or wasabi)

Size note: 小皿(こざら) are typically 6-12cm in diameter. Slightly larger plates (12-18cm) are called 中皿(ちゅうざら).
