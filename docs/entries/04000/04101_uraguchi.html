裏口(うらぐち)

うらぐち
noun
back door, rear entrance

Definitions

1. back door, rear entrance
A door or entrance at the back of a building, as opposed to the main entrance.
2. backdoor, illicit means
Figuratively, an unofficial or illegitimate way of achieving something, often implying bribery or unfair advantage.

Examples

裏口(うらぐち)から入(はい)ってください。
Please enter through the back door.
裏口(うらぐち)入学(にゅうがく)は問題(もんだい)になっている。
Backdoor admissions have become a problem.
Refers to gaining admission through bribery or connections
正面(しょうめん)玄関(げんかん)が閉(し)まっていたので裏口(うらぐち)を使(つか)った。
The front entrance was closed, so I used the back door.

Notes

USAGE: 裏口(うらぐち) has both literal and figurative meanings.

LITERAL:

  • 裏口(うらぐち)から入(はい)る (to enter from the back)
  • 裏口(うらぐち)を開(あ)ける (to open the back door)

FIGURATIVE (illicit means):

  • 裏口(うらぐち)入学(にゅうがく) (backdoor admission - through bribery/connections)
  • 裏口(うらぐち)営業(えいぎょう) (illicit business)

CONTRAST:

  • 表口(おもてぐち)/正面(しょうめん)玄関(げんかん) - front entrance
  • 勝手口(かってぐち) - kitchen entrance, service entrance
