雑巾(ぞうきん)

ぞうきん
noun
cleaning rag, dust cloth
1. cleaning rag, dust cloth, floor cloth
A cloth used for cleaning floors, wiping surfaces, or general cleaning tasks.
雑巾(ぞうきん)で床(ゆか)を拭(ふ)いた。
I wiped the floor with a cloth.
雑巾(ぞうきん)がけをする。
To clean/wipe (the floor) with a rag.
雑巾がけ = floor wiping
古(ふる)いタオルを雑巾(ぞうきん)にして使(つか)っている。
I use old towels as cleaning rags.

雑巾(ぞうきん) is a cloth used for cleaning, especially floors. In Japanese schools, students clean their own classrooms using 雑巾(ぞうきん).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 雑巾(ぞうきん)がけ: wiping floors with a rag
  • 雑巾(ぞうきん)を絞(しぼ)る: to wring out a rag
  • 雑巾(ぞうきん)掛(か)け: floor wiping, mopping

SCHOOL CLEANING:
In Japanese schools, students participate in daily cleaning (掃除(そうじ)). Using 雑巾(ぞうきん) to wipe floors on hands and knees is a traditional cleaning method.

RELATED CLEANING TOOLS:

  • ほうき: broom
  • ちりとり: dustpan
  • モップ: mop
  • バケツ: bucket
