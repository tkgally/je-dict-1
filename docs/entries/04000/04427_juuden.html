1. charging (battery)
The process of restoring electrical energy to a battery.
スマホを充電(じゅうでん)する。
I charge my smartphone.
充電(じゅうでん)が完了(かんりょう)した。
Charging is complete.
充電(じゅうでん)が切(き)れそうだ。
The battery is about to die.
寝(ね)る前(まえ)にスマホを充電(じゅうでん)しておく。
I charge my phone before going to bed.
ワイヤレス充電(じゅうでん)に対応(たいおう)しているスマホが増(ふ)えてきた。
More and more smartphones support wireless charging.
2. recharging (figurative)
Figuratively, resting and recovering one's energy.
旅行(りょこう)で充電(じゅうでん)してきた。
I recharged (myself) on the trip.
Figurative: refreshing oneself
週末(しゅうまつ)は充電(じゅうでん)期間(きかん)にしたい。
I want to make this weekend a recharging period.
温泉(おんせん)でゆっくり充電(じゅうでん)した。
I recharged by relaxing at a hot spring.
仕事(しごと)が忙(いそが)しすぎて、そろそろ充電(じゅうでん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Work has been too busy, and I need to recharge soon.
半年(はんとし)働(はたら)いたら一か月(げつ)休(やす)んで充電(じゅうでん)するつもりだ。
After working for half a year, I plan to take a month off and recharge.

COMPOUND: 充(じゅう) (fill) + 電(でん) (electricity)

USAGE:

  • 充電(じゅうでん)する takes the device or battery as its object with を: スマホを充電(じゅうでん)する.
  • The noun also names the stored charge itself, not just the act of charging. That is why it appears with が in 充電(じゅうでん)が切(き)れる ("the battery dies") and 充電(じゅうでん)が少(すく)ない ("the charge is low"). Learners who read 充電(じゅうでん) only as "charging" find these puzzling.
  • 充電(じゅうでん)中(ちゅう) labels the ongoing state, as on a device screen.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 充電(じゅうでん)する (to charge)
  • 充電(じゅうでん)が切(き)れる (charging runs out)
  • 充電(じゅうでん)中(ちゅう) (charging)
  • 充電(じゅうでん)器(き) (charger)
  • ワイヤレス充電(じゅうでん) (wireless charging)
  • 急速(きゅうそく)充電(じゅうでん) (fast charging)

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • Sense 2 applies the battery image to a person. Here there is no を object — you simply 充電(じゅうでん)する, and the implied thing being charged is yourself.
  • 充電(じゅうでん)期間(きかん) (recharging period) — time off to rest and recover. Often used of a performer or athlete on hiatus, where it is a softer way of saying they have stopped working.
  • The figurative sense is casual and positive; it is not used of recovering from illness, which takes 回復(かいふく) instead.

ANTONYM:

  • 放電(ほうでん) (discharge)

Related Words

Related: バッテリー (battery)
