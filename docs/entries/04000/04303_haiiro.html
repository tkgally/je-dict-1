灰色(はいいろ)

はいいろ
noun, no-adjective
gray, grey
1. gray, grey, ashen
The color between black and white, like the color of ash.
空(そら)が灰色(はいいろ)で雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうだ。
The sky is gray and it looks like it will rain.
灰色(はいいろ)のスーツを着(き)ている。
He is wearing a gray suit.
2. unclear, ambiguous
Figuratively, something that is neither black nor white, uncertain or suspect.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は灰色(はいいろ)の存在(そんざい)だ。
That company is a gray-area entity.
Figurative: suspicious but not proven

USAGE: 灰色(はいいろ) literally means 'ash color.' It can describe actual gray things or figuratively refer to ambiguous, suspicious situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 灰色(はいいろ)の + noun (gray ~)
  • 灰色(はいいろ)っぽい (grayish)

FIGURATIVE USAGE:

  • 灰色(はいいろ)高官(こうかん) (gray official - suspected of corruption)
  • 灰色(はいいろ)地帯(ちたい) (gray zone - legal ambiguity)
  • 白(しろ)でも黒(くろ)でもない (neither white nor black)

RELATED COLORS:

  • 白(しろ) (white)
  • 黒(くろ) (black)
  • グレー (gray - loanword)
