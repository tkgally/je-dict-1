仕掛(しか)ける

しかける
verb (ichidan)
to set up; to start; to challenge
1. to set up; to lay (a trap)
To prepare a device, trap, or mechanism.
2. to start; to begin (something against someone)
To initiate an attack, challenge, or aggressive action.
3. to be in the middle of
Combined with other verbs to indicate an action has started but is incomplete.
森(もり)に罠(わな)を仕掛(しか)けた。
I set a trap in the forest.
相手(あいて)チームが攻撃(こうげき)を仕掛(しか)けてきた。
The opposing team launched an attack.
仕事(しごと)を仕掛(しか)けたまま帰(かえ)ってしまった。
I went home leaving the work half-done.

仕掛(しか)ける is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to set something in motion or prepare something, often with strategic intent.

ASPECT (ている):
仕掛(しか)けている indicates something is set up or an action has been initiated.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [trap/device]を仕掛(しか)ける — to set up [trap/device]
  • [person]に[action]を仕掛(しか)ける — to start [action] against [person]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 罠(わな)を仕掛(しか)ける (set a trap)
  • 喧嘩(けんか)を仕掛(しか)ける (pick a fight)
  • 攻撃(こうげき)を仕掛(しか)ける (launch an attack)
  • 仕掛(しか)け (mechanism, gimmick)

COMPOUND FORM:

〜仕掛(しか)ける/〜仕掛(しか)け means "in the middle of doing" or "half-done":

  • 読(よ)みかけの本(ほん) (book one is in the middle of reading)
  • 食(た)べかけ (half-eaten)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 仕掛(しか)け: mechanism, trick, gimmick (noun)
  • 仕掛(しか)け花火(はなび): fireworks display
