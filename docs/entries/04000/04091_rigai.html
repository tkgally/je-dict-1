利害(りがい)

りがい
noun
advantages and disadvantages, interests

Definitions

1. advantages and disadvantages, pros and cons
The beneficial and detrimental aspects of something considered together.
2. interests, stake
Personal or collective benefits at stake in a situation.

Examples

利害(りがい)関係(かんけい)のない第三者(だいさんしゃ)に判断(はんだん)してもらった。
We had a disinterested third party make the judgment.
この問題(もんだい)では両者(りょうしゃ)の利害(りがい)が対立(たいりつ)している。
The interests of both parties are in conflict on this issue.
利害(りがい)を超(こ)えて協力(きょうりょく)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to cooperate beyond our individual interests.

Notes

USAGE: 利害(りがい) is commonly used in business, legal, and political contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 利害(りがい)関係(かんけい) (interests, stake; having an interest in something)
  • 利害(りがい)関係者(かんけいしゃ) (interested party, stakeholder)
  • 利害(りがい)が一致(いっち)する (interests align)
  • 利害(りがい)が対立(たいりつ)する (interests conflict)

CONTRAST:

  • 利(り) alone means benefit/advantage
  • 害(がい) alone means harm/damage
  • Together, 利害(りがい) refers to both sides or overall interests
