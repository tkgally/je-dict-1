1. to grasp tightly; to grip firmly
To hold something very tightly in one's hand.
彼女(かのじょ)は手紙(てがみ)を握(にぎ)りしめたまま泣(な)いていた。
She was crying while clutching the letter tightly.
子供(こども)は母親(ははおや)の手(て)を握(にぎ)りしめて離(はな)さなかった。
The child gripped their mother's hand tightly and wouldn't let go.
合格(ごうかく)通知(つうち)を握(にぎ)りしめて、嬉(うれ)しさのあまり涙(なみだ)が出(で)た。
Clutching the acceptance letter, tears came from sheer happiness.
2. to clench (fist)
To close one's hand tightly into a fist.
拳(こぶし)を握(にぎ)りしめた。
I clenched my fist.
拳(こぶし)を握(にぎ)りしめて怒(いか)りをこらえた。
I clenched my fist and held back my anger.
悔(くや)しくて両手(りょうて)を握(にぎ)りしめたが、何(なに)も言(い)えなかった。
I clenched both hands in frustration, but couldn't say anything.

握(にぎ)りしめる is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 握(にぎ)る (to grip) + しめる (to tighten). It emphasizes holding with force and determination.

ASPECT (ている):
握(にぎ)りしめている indicates continuous tight gripping, often suggesting emotional intensity.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を握(にぎ)りしめる — to grip [thing] tightly

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 拳(こぶし)を握(にぎ)りしめる (clench one's fist)
  • 手(て)を握(にぎ)りしめる (grip someone's hand tightly)
  • 希望(きぼう)を握(にぎ)りしめる (hold onto hope - figurative)

EMOTIONAL NUANCE:
This verb often appears in emotional contexts, suggesting determination, fear, anxiety, or desperate attachment.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 握(にぎ)る: to grip (neutral)
  • 掴(つか)む: to grab, seize
  • 握(にぎ)りしめる: to grip tightly (emphatic)
