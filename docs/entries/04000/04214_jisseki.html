実績(じっせき)

じっせき
noun
achievements, track record
1. achievements, accomplishments, results
Actual accomplishments or proven results, often in work or business.
彼(かれ)は営業(えいぎょう)で素晴(すば)らしい実績(じっせき)を上(あ)げた。
He achieved excellent results in sales.
2. track record, performance history
A documented history of past performance and achievements.
実績(じっせき)のある会社(かいしゃ)に依頼(いらい)したい。
I want to hire a company with a proven track record.
過去(かこ)の実績(じっせき)を評価(ひょうか)して採用(さいよう)を決(き)めた。
We decided to hire based on an evaluation of past achievements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実績(じっせき)を上(あ)げる (to achieve results)
  • 実績(じっせき)がある (to have a track record)
  • 実績(じっせき)を積(つ)む (to build up achievements)
  • 実績(じっせき)報告(ほうこく) (performance report)

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

実績(じっせき) is frequently used in business to refer to:

  • Sales results (営業(えいぎょう)実績(じっせき))
  • Past performance (過去(かこ)の実績(じっせき))
  • Proven abilities (実績(じっせき)のある〜)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 成果(せいか) - results, achievements (output)
  • 業績(ぎょうせき) - business results, achievements
  • 功績(こうせき) - meritorious service, distinguished achievements
