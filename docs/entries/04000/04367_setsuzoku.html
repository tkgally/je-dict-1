接続(せつぞく)

せつぞく
noun, suru-verb
connection, linking

Definitions

1. connection, joining
The act of connecting or linking things together, such as cables, devices, or transportation lines.
2. transfer, connection (transportation)
Changing from one train/bus line to another; connecting service.

Examples

インターネットに接続(せつぞく)する。
To connect to the internet.
この駅(えき)で地下鉄(ちかてつ)に接続(せつぞく)できます。
You can transfer to the subway at this station.
接続(せつぞく)が不安定(ふあんてい)で動画(どうが)が止(と)まってしまう。
The connection is unstable, so the video keeps stopping.

Notes

接続(せつぞく) is commonly used for both technical connections (internet, devices) and transportation transfers.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 接続(せつぞく)詞(し): conjunction (grammar)
  • 接続(せつぞく)駅(えき): connecting station, transfer station
  • Wi-Fi接続(せつぞく): Wi-Fi connection
  • USB接続(せつぞく): USB connection

TECHNICAL USES:

  • インターネットに接続(せつぞく)する: connect to the internet
  • 機器(きき)を接続(せつぞく)する: connect devices
  • 接続(せつぞく)状態(じょうたい): connection status

TRANSPORTATION:

  • 乗(の)り換(か)え: transfer (more common in everyday speech)
  • 接続(せつぞく): connection (more formal, schedule-focused)
