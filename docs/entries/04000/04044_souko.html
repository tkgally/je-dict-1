倉庫(そうこ)

そうこ
noun
warehouse, storehouse
1. warehouse, storehouse, storage facility
A building used for storing goods, merchandise, or materials. Can range from small storage sheds to large commercial warehouses.
商品(しょうひん)は倉庫(そうこ)に保管(ほかん)されている。
The goods are stored in the warehouse.
倉庫(そうこ)から在庫(ざいこ)を出(だ)してください。
Please take the stock out of the warehouse.
古(ふる)い倉庫(そうこ)がカフェに改装(かいそう)された。
An old warehouse was renovated into a café.
Common trend in urban redevelopment

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 倉庫(そうこ)に保管(ほかん)する (to store in a warehouse)
  • 倉庫(そうこ)業(ぎょう) (warehousing industry)
  • 物流(ぶつりゅう)倉庫(そうこ) (distribution warehouse, logistics center)
  • 冷凍(れいとう)倉庫(そうこ) (cold storage warehouse)

MODERN USAGE:

  • In e-commerce, 倉庫(そうこ) often refers to fulfillment centers
  • 倉庫(そうこ)カフェ / 倉庫(そうこ)レストラン = café/restaurant in a converted warehouse (trendy)

TRADITIONAL: 蔵(くら) is a traditional Japanese storehouse, often with thick walls for fire protection.
