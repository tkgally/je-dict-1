切(き)り替(か)える

きりかえる
verb (ichidan)
to switch; to change over; to shift
1. to switch; to change over
To change from one mode, system, or state to another.
2. to shift (mindset); to change one's attitude
To change one's way of thinking or mental state.
チャンネルを切(き)り替(か)えてください。
Please change the channel.
気持(きも)ちを切(き)り替(か)えて、次(つぎ)の試合(しあい)に臨(のぞ)もう。
Let's shift our mindset and face the next match.
会社(かいしゃ)は新(あたら)しいシステムに切(き)り替(か)える予定(よてい)だ。
The company plans to switch to a new system.

切(き)り替(か)える is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 切(き)る (to cut) + 替(か)える (to replace). It implies a decisive change from one thing to another.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 切(き)り替(か)わる (to be switched, to switch automatically)

ASPECT (ている):
切(き)り替(か)えている indicates the process of switching is ongoing or a switch has been made.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を切(き)り替(か)える — to switch [thing]
  • AからBに切(き)り替(か)える — to switch from A to B

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 画面(がめん)を切(き)り替(か)える (switch screens)
  • 気持(きも)ちを切(き)り替(か)える (shift one's mindset)
  • モードを切(き)り替(か)える (change modes)
  • 話題(わだい)を切(き)り替(か)える (change the subject)

IDIOMATIC USAGE:
気持(きも)ちを切(き)り替(か)える is a very common expression meaning to mentally move on, especially after a setback.
