〜済(ず)み

ずみ
suffix
finished; completed

Definitions

1. finished; completed; done
Attached to verb stems or nouns to indicate that something has been completed or is already done.

Examples

支払(しはら)い済(ず)みです。
Payment has been completed.
使用(しよう)済(ず)みのチケット。
A used ticket.
確認(かくにん)済(ず)みの書類(しょるい)。
Documents that have been verified.

Notes

PATTERN: Verb stem / Noun + 済(ず)み

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 支払(しはら)い済(ず)み (paid)
  • 確認(かくにん)済(ず)み (confirmed/verified)
  • 使用(しよう)済(ず)み (used/spent)
  • 予約(よやく)済(ず)み (reserved)
  • 処理(しょり)済(ず)み (processed)
  • 登録(とうろく)済(ず)み (registered)

USAGE: Very common in business and administrative contexts to indicate status.

NOTE: Comes from the verb 済(す)む (to be finished). Functions like a の-adjective or noun.

Related Words
