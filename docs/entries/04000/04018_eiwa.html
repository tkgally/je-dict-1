英和(えいわ)

えいわ
noun, の-adjective
English-Japanese
1. English-Japanese
Relating to translation or reference from English to Japanese, especially for dictionaries.
英和(えいわ)辞典(じてん)を使(つか)う。
To use an English-Japanese dictionary.
英和(えいわ)翻訳(ほんやく)のアルバイトをしている。
I have a part-time job doing English-Japanese translation.
大(おお)きい英和(えいわ)辞典(じてん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want a big English-Japanese dictionary.

英和(えいわ) combines 英(えい) (English) and 和(わ) (Japanese) to indicate the direction from English to Japanese. Almost always used as a modifier before another noun.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 英和(えいわ)辞典(じてん)/辞書(じしょ) (English-Japanese dictionary)
  • 英和(えいわ)翻訳(ほんやく) (English-to-Japanese translation)

DICTIONARY NAMING PATTERN:

Japanese dictionary names follow the pattern {Language A}{Language B} = A-to-B direction:

  • 英和(えいわ)辞典(じてん): English → Japanese
  • 和英(わえい)辞典(じてん): Japanese → English
  • 漢和(かんわ)辞典(じてん): kanji → Japanese
  • 国語(こくご)辞典(じてん): Japanese → Japanese (monolingual)
  • 仏和(ふつわ)辞典(じてん): French → Japanese

NOTE:
和(わ) in this context means "Japanese" (from 大和(やまと)). It appears in many language-pair terms.

Related Words

Antonym: 和英(わえい) (Japanese-English)
