差(さ)し込(こ)む

さしこむ
verb (godan)
to insert; to plug in; to shine in
1. to insert; to plug in
To put something into a slot, socket, or opening.
2. to shine in; to stream in
For light to come in through an opening, especially sunlight through a window.
コンセントにプラグを差(さ)し込(こ)んでください。
Please plug the plug into the outlet.
窓(まど)から朝日(あさひ)が差(さ)し込(こ)んでいた。
Morning sunlight was streaming in through the window.
カードを差(さ)し込(こ)んでパスワードを入力(にゅうりょく)してください。
Insert your card and enter your password.

差(さ)し込(こ)む has both transitive and intransitive uses depending on the meaning.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Sense 1 (insert): transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Sense 2 (shine in): intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))

ASPECT (ている):

  • 差(さ)し込(こ)んでいる (plugged in): something remains inserted
  • 差(さ)し込(こ)んでいる (light): light is streaming in continuously

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]を[slot]に差(さ)し込(こ)む — to insert [thing] into [slot]
  • [light]が差(さ)し込(こ)む — [light] shines in

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • プラグを差(さ)し込(こ)む (plug in)
  • 鍵(かぎ)を差(さ)し込(こ)む (insert a key)
  • 光(ひかり)が差(さ)し込(こ)む (light streams in)
  • 木漏(こも)れ日(び)が差(さ)し込(こ)む (sunlight filtering through trees)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 差(さ)し込(こ)み口(ぐち): outlet, socket
  • コンセント: electrical outlet (from "concentric plug")
