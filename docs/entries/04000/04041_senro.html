線路(せんろ)

せんろ
noun
railway track, railroad line
1. railway track, railroad line
The rails and supporting structures on which trains run. The physical infrastructure of a railway.
線路(せんろ)に入(はい)らないでください。
Please do not enter the railway track.
Common warning sign
線路(せんろ)の工事(こうじ)で電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れている。
The train is delayed due to track construction.
子供(こども)のころ、線路(せんろ)沿(ぞ)いの道(みち)をよく歩(ある)いた。
As a child, I often walked along the road by the railway tracks.
線路沿い = along the railway tracks

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 線路(せんろ)に入(はい)る (to enter the tracks - dangerous)
  • 線路(せんろ)沿(ぞ)い (along the railway line)
  • 線路(せんろ)工事(こうじ) (track construction/maintenance)
  • 線路(せんろ)を渡(わた)る (to cross the tracks)

SAFETY WARNING: 線路(せんろ)への立(た)ち入(い)りは禁止(きんし)されています (Entering the railway tracks is prohibited).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 路線(ろせん) (route, train line)
  • 軌道(きどう) (track, orbit)
  • 踏切(ふみきり) (railroad crossing)
