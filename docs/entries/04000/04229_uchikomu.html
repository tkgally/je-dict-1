1. to drive in; to hammer in
To strike something so that it goes into a surface.
壁(かべ)に釘(くぎ)を打(う)ち込(こ)んだ。
I drove a nail into the wall.
杭(くい)を地面(じめん)に深(ふか)く打(う)ち込(こ)んだ。
I drove the stake deep into the ground.
ゴルフボールを遠(とお)くまで打(う)ち込(こ)んだ。
I hit the golf ball far out.
2. to devote oneself to; to be absorbed in
To dedicate oneself wholeheartedly to an activity or pursuit.
仕事(しごと)に打(う)ち込(こ)む。
To devote oneself to work.
彼女(かのじょ)は研究(けんきゅう)に打(う)ち込(こ)んでいる。
She is devoted to her research.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は音楽(おんがく)に打(う)ち込(こ)んでいて、毎日(まいにち)練習(れんしゅう)していた。
When I was young, I was devoted to music and practiced every day.
3. to type in; to input
To enter data or text into a computer or device.
パスワードを打(う)ち込(こ)む。
To type in a password.
パスワードを打(う)ち込(こ)んでください。
Please type in your password.
大量(たいりょう)のデータをパソコンに打(う)ち込(こ)む作業(さぎょう)は時間(じかん)がかかる。
The task of typing a large amount of data into the computer takes time.

打(う)ち込(こ)む is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) formed from 打(う)つ (to strike) + 込(こ)む (into). It has evolved from physical striking to include figurative devotion and modern computer input.

ASPECT (ている):

  • 打(う)ち込(こ)んでいる (physical): something is embedded
  • 打(う)ち込(こ)んでいる (devotion): is absorbed in, devoted to

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [nail]を[surface]に打(う)ち込(こ)む — to drive [nail] into [surface]
  • [activity]に打(う)ち込(こ)む — to devote oneself to [activity]
  • [data]を打(う)ち込(こ)む — to type in [data]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仕事(しごと)に打(う)ち込(こ)む (devote oneself to work)
  • 練習(れんしゅう)に打(う)ち込(こ)む (throw oneself into practice)
  • データを打(う)ち込(こ)む (input data)
  • 杭(くい)を打(う)ち込(こ)む (drive in a stake)

NOTE: For the "devote oneself" meaning, the particle is に (intransitive use), while for physical/typing meanings, it takes を (transitive).
