創作(そうさく)

そうさく
noun, suru-verb
creation, creative work
1. creation, creative work
The act of creating original artistic or literary works; original creative output.
小説(しょうせつ)の創作(そうさく)に励(はげ)んでいる。
I'm working hard on writing novels.
この料理(りょうり)はシェフ}の{創作(そうさく)です。
This dish is the chef's original creation.
2. fabrication, fiction
Something made up or invented; not based on fact.
その話(はなし)は創作(そうさく)で、事実(じじつ)ではない。
That story is fiction, not fact.

創作(そうさく) emphasizes original creation, particularly in artistic contexts. It can also mean something fabricated or fictional.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 創作(そうさく)活動(かつどう): creative activities
  • 創作(そうさく)料理(りょうり): creative cuisine, fusion cooking
  • 創作(そうさく)ダンス: creative dance
  • 創作(そうさく)意欲(いよく): creative motivation

CONTEXTS:

  • Literature: writing original fiction/poetry
  • Art: creating original artwork
  • Cooking: creating original dishes
  • Dance/Music: original choreography/compositions

VS. OTHER 'CREATION' WORDS:

  • 制作(せいさく): production (of media, art)
  • 製作(せいさく): manufacture (of goods)
  • 創作(そうさく): original creation (emphasis on originality)
