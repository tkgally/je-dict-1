蘇(よみがえ)る

よみがえる
verb (godan)
to be revived; to come back to life
1. to be resurrected; to come back to life
To return from death or apparent death; to be revived.
心肺(しんぱい)蘇生(そせい)で彼(かれ)は蘇(よみがえ)った。
He was revived through CPR.
2. to be revived; to be restored
For something dormant, forgotten, or declining to regain vitality or prominence.
記憶(きおく)が蘇(よみがえ)った。
The memories came flooding back.
古(ふる)い街(まち)が蘇(よみがえ)った。
The old town was revived.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No transitive pair.

WRITING: Also written as 甦(よみがえ)る or in hiragana.

ETYMOLOGY: From 黄泉(よみ) (the underworld in Japanese mythology) + 帰(かえ)る (to return).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 記憶(きおく)が蘇(よみがえ)る (memories come back)
  • 活気(かっき)が蘇(よみがえ)る (vitality is restored)
  • 思(おも)い出(で)が蘇(よみがえ)る (memories revive)

RELATED:

  • 蘇生(そせい) (resuscitation)
  • 復活(ふっかつ) (revival, resurrection)
