社説(しゃせつ)

しゃせつ
noun
editorial
1. editorial
An article in a newspaper or magazine expressing the opinion of the editors or publisher.
今日(きょう)の社説(しゃせつ)は環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)について書(か)かれていた。
Today's editorial was written about environmental issues.
社説(しゃせつ)を読(よ)んで考(かんが)えさせられた。
I was made to think after reading the editorial.
この新聞(しんぶん)の社説(しゃせつ)は保守的(ほしゅてき)だ。
This newspaper's editorials are conservative.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社説(しゃせつ)を書(か)く (to write an editorial)
  • 社説(しゃせつ)を読(よ)む (to read an editorial)
  • 社説(しゃせつ)欄(らん) (editorial column)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 論説(ろんせつ) - editorial, opinion piece (similar meaning)
  • 記事(きじ) - article (neutral, factual)
  • {コラム} - column (regular opinion piece by a specific writer)

社説(しゃせつ) represents the official stance of the newspaper/magazine, while コラム represents an individual writer's opinion.
