温室(おんしつ)

おんしつ
noun
greenhouse, hothouse
1. greenhouse, hothouse, conservatory
A building with glass walls and roof for growing plants that need protection from cold weather.
温室(おんしつ)でトマトを育(そだ)てている。
I'm growing tomatoes in a greenhouse.
植物園(しょくぶつえん)の温室(おんしつ)には熱帯(ねったい)の花(はな)がある。
The botanical garden's greenhouse has tropical flowers.
温室(おんしつ)効果(こうか)ガスが問題(もんだい)になっている。
Greenhouse gases have become a problem.

USAGE: 温室(おんしつ) refers to structures for growing plants, and also appears in environmental terminology.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 温室(おんしつ)栽培(さいばい) (greenhouse cultivation)
  • 温室(おんしつ)効果(こうか) (greenhouse effect)
  • 温室(おんしつ)効果(こうか)ガス (greenhouse gas)
  • 温室(おんしつ)育(そだ)ち (raised in a greenhouse - figuratively, sheltered/pampered)

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • 温室(おんしつ)育(そだ)ち describes someone raised in an overprotected environment.

RELATED:

  • ビニールハウス - plastic greenhouse (vinyl house)
  • ハウス栽培(さいばい) - greenhouse farming
