伝記(でんき)

でんき
noun
biography
See also: 電気(でんき) (electricity (homophone))
1. biography, life story
A written account of a person's life, typically focusing on their achievements, character, and historical significance.
偉人(いじん)の伝記(でんき)を読(よ)むのが好(す)きだ。
I like reading biographies of great people.
彼(かれ)の伝記(でんき)が出版(しゅっぱん)された。
His biography was published.
伝記(でんき)映画(えいが)が賞(しょう)を受(う)けた。
The biographical film won an award.
伝記(でんき)映画(えいが) = biopic

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伝記(でんき)を書(か)く (to write a biography)
  • 伝記(でんき)を読(よ)む (to read a biography)
  • 伝記(でんき)小説(しょうせつ) (biographical novel)
  • 伝記(でんき)映画(えいが) (biographical film, biopic)
  • 自伝(じでん) (autobiography - written by oneself)

HOMOPHONE NOTE: Be careful not to confuse with:

  • 電気(でんき) (electricity)
  • 電器(でんき) (electrical appliances)
  • 伝奇(でんき) (romance, legend)

RELATED:

  • 自伝(じでん) (autobiography)
  • 評伝(ひょうでん) (critical biography)
