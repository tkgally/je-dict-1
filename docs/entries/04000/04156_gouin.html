強引(ごういん)

ごういん
na-adjective
forcible, pushy, assertive
1. forcible, pushy, high-handed, overbearing
Acting in a forceful manner without considering others' feelings or objections.
彼(かれ)は強引(ごういん)に契約(けいやく)させようとした。
He tried to force me into signing the contract.
そんな強引(ごういん)なやり方(かた)はよくない。
Such a forceful approach is not good.
強引(ごういん)に話(はなし)を進(すす)めた。
He pushed the discussion forward forcefully.

USAGE: Often has a negative connotation, implying that someone is being too forceful or inconsiderate.

GRAMMAR: Used as a な-adjective (強引(ごういん)な) or adverb (強引(ごういん)に).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無理(むり)やり - by force, forcibly
  • 押(お)しが強(つよ)い - pushy, assertive
general
