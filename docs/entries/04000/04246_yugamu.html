歪(ゆが)む

ゆがむ
verb (godan)
to warp; to be distorted; to become crooked
1. to warp; to bend out of shape
For something to become bent, twisted, or misshapen.
湿気(しっけ)で木(き)が歪(ゆが)んでしまった。
The wood warped because of the humidity.
彼(かれ)の顔(かお)が苦痛(くつう)で歪(ゆが)んだ。
His face contorted with pain.
2. to be distorted; to be warped
For something abstract like perception, facts, or personality to become twisted or corrupted.
事実(じじつ)が歪(ゆが)んで伝(つた)わっている。
The facts are being conveyed in a distorted way.

歪(ゆが)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) describing something becoming warped, twisted, or distorted.

TRANSITIVE PAIR: 歪(ゆが)める (to distort, warp something)

ASPECT (ている):
歪(ゆが)んでいる indicates something is currently in a warped/distorted state.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [thing]が歪(ゆが)む — [thing] becomes warped

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 形(かたち)が歪(ゆが)む (shape becomes distorted)
  • 顔(かお)が歪(ゆが)む (face contorts - from pain/emotion)
  • 事実(じじつ)が歪(ゆが)む (facts become distorted)
  • 性格(せいかく)が歪(ゆが)む (personality becomes warped)

FIGURATIVE USE:
歪(ゆが)む is often used figuratively for distorted perceptions, reports, or personalities that have developed in unhealthy ways.

ALTERNATE READING:
Also read as ひずむ with similar meaning, especially in technical contexts (audio distortion).
