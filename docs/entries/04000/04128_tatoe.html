例(たと)え

たとえ
noun, adverb
example; even if
1. example, simile, metaphor
A comparison or illustration used to explain something.
分(わ)かりやすい例(たと)えを使(つか)って説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain using an easy-to-understand example.
Sense 1: example
2. even if, even though (adverb)
Used to introduce a hypothetical or concessive condition.
例(たと)え雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っても行(い)きます。
Even if it rains, I will go.
Sense 2: even if - often followed by ても
例(たと)え失敗(しっぱい)しても諦(あきら)めない。
Even if I fail, I won't give up.

TWO DISTINCT USES:

1. AS A NOUN (example):

  • 例(たと)えを出(だ)す (to give an example)
  • 例(たと)えが悪(わる)い (a bad analogy)
  • 例(たと)えて言(い)えば (to use an analogy)

2. AS AN ADVERB (even if):

  • 例(たと)え〜ても (even if ~)
  • 例(たと)え〜でも (even though ~)
  • Usually written in hiragana: たとえ

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 例(れい) - example (more formal, numbered examples)
  • 比喩(ひゆ) - metaphor, figure of speech
  • もし - if (neutral conditional)
