水産(すいさん)

すいさん
noun
marine products, fisheries
1. marine products, aquatic products
Products obtained from oceans, rivers, and other bodies of water, including fish, shellfish, and seaweed.
日本(にほん)は水産(すいさん)資源(しげん)が豊富(ほうふ)な国(くに)だ。
Japan is a country rich in marine resources.
水産(すいさん)加工(かこう)品(ひん)を輸出(ゆしゅつ)している。
We export processed marine products.
水産加工品 = processed seafood products
2. fisheries, fishing industry
The industry related to catching, farming, and processing aquatic products.
彼(かれ)は水産(すいさん)大学(だいがく)で漁業(ぎょぎょう)を学(まな)んだ。
He studied fisheries at a marine sciences university.

水産(すいさん) encompasses all products from aquatic sources and the industry surrounding them. Japan, being an island nation, has a significant fisheries sector.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 水産(すいさん)業(ぎょう): fishing industry
  • 水産(すいさん)物(ぶつ): marine products, seafood
  • 水産(すいさん)加工(かこう): seafood processing
  • 水産(すいさん)資源(しげん): marine resources
  • 水産(すいさん)庁(ちょう): Fisheries Agency (government)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 漁業(ぎょぎょう): fishing industry
  • 養殖(ようしょく): aquaculture, fish farming
  • 海産物(かいさんぶつ): marine products (specifically from the sea)
