西暦(せいれき)

せいれき
noun
Western calendar, A.D., C.E.
1. Western calendar, Christian Era, Common Era
The calendar system based on years counted from the traditional birth year of Jesus Christ; the Gregorian calendar dating system.
西暦(せいれき)2024年(ねん)は令和(れいわ)6年(ねん)です。
The year 2024 (Western calendar) is Reiwa 6.
書類(しょるい)には西暦(せいれき)で日付(ひづけ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write the date using the Western calendar on the documents.
西暦(せいれき)と和暦(われき)、どちらで書(か)けばいいですか。
Should I write it in the Western calendar or Japanese era?
和暦 = Japanese era calendar

Japan uses two calendar systems: 西暦(せいれき) (Western/Gregorian calendar) and 和暦(われき) (Japanese era calendar based on imperial reigns).

CALENDAR SYSTEMS IN JAPAN:

  • 西暦(せいれき): Western calendar (2024, 2025, etc.)
  • 和暦(われき): Japanese era calendar (令和(れいわ), 平成(へいせい), 昭和(しょうわ), etc.)

USAGE:

  • International contexts, business, younger generations often prefer 西暦(せいれき)
  • Government documents, traditional contexts often use 和暦(われき)
  • Many forms accept either

ABBREVIATIONS:

  • A.D. / C.E. correspond to 西暦(せいれき) (though B.C./B.C.E. would be 紀元前(きげんぜん))
