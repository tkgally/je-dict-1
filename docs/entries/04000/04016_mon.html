〜問(もん)

もん
counter
counter for questions

Definitions

1. counter for questions or problems
Used to count questions on tests, quizzes, or in problem sets.

Examples

テストは全部(ぜんぶ)で20問(にじゅうもん)ある。
The test has 20 questions in total.
第(だい)1問(いちもん)に答(こた)えてください。
Please answer question 1.
5問(ごもん)中(ちゅう)3問(さんもん)正解(せいかい)だった。
I got 3 out of 5 questions correct.

Notes

COUNTING:

  • 一(いち)問(もん)/1問(いちもん) (1 question)
  • 二(に)問(もん)/2問(にもん) (2 questions)
  • 第(だい)〜問(もん) (question number ~)
  • 何問(なんもん) (how many questions)

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Tests and exams
  • Quizzes
  • Homework problems
  • Interview questions

NOTE: For general questions in conversation, 質問(しつもん) (question) is used as a noun, not counted with 〜問.
