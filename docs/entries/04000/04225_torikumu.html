取(と)り組(く)む

とりくむ
verb (godan)
to tackle; to grapple with; to work on

Definitions

1. to tackle; to work on
To engage seriously with a problem, task, or project.
2. to grapple; to wrestle
Originally from sumo, meaning to engage in a match. Now mostly used figuratively.

Examples

環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)に真剣(しんけん)に取(と)り組(く)む必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to seriously tackle environmental issues.
新(あたら)しいプロジェクトに取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
I'm working on a new project.
会社(かいしゃ)は働(はたら)き方(かた)改革(かいかく)に積極的(せっきょくてき)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
The company is actively working on work-style reform.

Notes

取(と)り組(く)む is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) formed from 取(と)る (to take) + 組(く)む (to grapple). Originally a sumo term, it now mainly means to engage seriously with tasks or problems.

ASPECT (ている):
取(と)り組(く)んでいる indicates ongoing engagement with a task or problem. This is the most common form.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [problem/task]に取(と)り組(く)む — to tackle/work on [problem/task]
  • [problem]に真剣(しんけん)に取(と)り組(く)む — to seriously tackle [problem]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 課題(かだい)に取(と)り組(く)む (work on a task/challenge)
  • 問題(もんだい)に取(と)り組(く)む (tackle a problem)
  • 研究(けんきゅう)に取(と)り組(く)む (engage in research)
  • 改革(かいかく)に取(と)り組(く)む (work on reform)

NOTE: Takes the particle に, not を, because it's intransitive.
