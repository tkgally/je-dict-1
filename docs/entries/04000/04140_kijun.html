規準(きじゅん)

きじゅん
noun
standard, criterion, norm
1. standard, criterion, norm, basis
A measure or principle used as a basis for judgment or comparison.
合格(ごうかく)の規準(きじゅん)は何(なん)ですか。
What are the criteria for passing?
この規準(きじゅん)に基(もと)づいて判断(はんだん)する。
I will judge based on this standard.
国際(こくさい)規準(きじゅん)を満(み)たす必要(ひつよう)がある。
It needs to meet international standards.

KANJI NOTE: Also commonly written as 基準(きじゅん) with a different first kanji but the same meaning and pronunciation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 規準(きじゅん)を設(もう)ける (to establish a standard)
  • 規準(きじゅん)を満(み)たす (to meet a standard)
  • 判断(はんだん)の規準(きじゅん) (criterion for judgment)
general
