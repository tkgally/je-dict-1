湯呑(ゆの)み (full form: 湯呑(ゆの)み茶碗(ぢゃわん)) is a cylindrical cup without a handle, used primarily for drinking Japanese green tea. Unlike Western teacups, 湯呑(ゆの)み are held with both hands.

Characteristics:

  • Typically ceramic or porcelain
  • Cylindrical shape with no handle
  • Often larger for men (大(おお)ぶり) and smaller for women (小(こ)ぶり)
  • Many households have personalized ones for each family member

Related:

  • 茶碗(ちゃわん) (rice bowl; also used for matcha)
