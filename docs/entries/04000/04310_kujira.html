鯨(くじら)

くじら
noun
whale
1. whale
A large marine mammal that breathes air through blowholes. Whales have significant cultural and historical importance in Japan.
船(ふね)から鯨(くじら)が潮(しお)を吹(ふ)くのが見(み)えた。
We could see a whale spouting water from the boat.
鯨(くじら)は海(うみ)で最(もっと)も大(おお)きな動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Whales are the largest animals in the ocean.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)は給食(きゅうしょく)に鯨(くじら)の肉(にく)が出(で)ることがあった。
When I was a child, whale meat was sometimes served in school lunches.

鯨(くじら) refers to whales in general.

Common expressions:

  • 鯨(くじら)肉(にく) (whale meat)
  • 鯨(くじら)油(あぶら) (whale oil)
  • 捕鯨(ほげい) (whaling)
  • 鯨(くじら)を飲(の)む (to swallow a whale - to do something impossible)

Types of whales:

  • シロナガス鯨(くじら) (blue whale)
  • ザトウ鯨(くじら) (humpback whale)
  • マッコウ鯨(くじら) (sperm whale)

Counter: 頭(とう)

Cultural note:
Whale meat has been consumed in Japan for centuries, and some coastal towns have whale festivals. However, international controversy over whaling has made this a sensitive topic. Many younger Japanese have never eaten whale meat.
