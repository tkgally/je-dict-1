バッテリー

ばってりー
noun
battery
1. battery (rechargeable)
A device that stores electrical energy and powers electronics, especially rechargeable batteries in phones and laptops.
スマホのバッテリーが切(き)れた。
My phone's battery died.
バッテリーの持(も)ちがいい。
The battery life is good.
Common expression
2. battery (baseball)
In baseball, the combination of pitcher and catcher.
彼(かれ)らは最強(さいきょう)のバッテリーだ。
They are the strongest battery (pitcher-catcher duo).
Baseball term

From English "battery."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • バッテリーが切(き)れる (battery dies)
  • バッテリーを充電(じゅうでん)する (to charge the battery)
  • バッテリーの残量(ざんりょう) (battery level)
  • バッテリーの持(も)ち (battery life)
  • モバイルバッテリー (portable battery/power bank)

NOTE: For disposable batteries, 乾電池(かんでんち) is typically used.

BASEBALL: バッテリー refers to the pitcher (投手(とうしゅ)) and catcher (捕手(ほしゅ)) as a pair.
