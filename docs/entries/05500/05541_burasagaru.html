ぶら下(さ)がる

ぶらさがる
verb (godan)
to hang; to dangle
1. to hang; to dangle; to suspend oneself
To be suspended from something, or to hang oneself from a support.
鉄棒(てつぼう)にぶら下(さ)がる。
To hang from a horizontal bar.
電車(でんしゃ)の吊(つ)り革(かわ)にぶら下(さ)がる。
To hang onto the train strap.
木(き)の枝(えだ)から果物(くだもの)がぶら下(さ)がっている。
Fruit is hanging from the tree branch.

ぶら下(さ)がる is an intransitive verb meaning to hang or dangle. It can describe both people hanging from something and objects suspended from a support.

CONJUGATION (godan verb):

  • ぶら下(さ)がる (dictionary form)
  • ぶら下(さ)がらない (negative)
  • ぶら下(さ)がった (past)
  • ぶら下(さ)がって (te-form)

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • ぶら下(さ)げる: to hang (something), to dangle (something)
