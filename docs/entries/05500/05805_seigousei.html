整合性(せいごうせい)

せいごうせい
noun
consistency; coherence; integrity
1. consistency; coherence; integrity
The quality of being logically connected and free from contradictions; often used in technical contexts like data integrity.
データ(でーた)の整合性(せいごうせい)を保(たも)つ。
To maintain data integrity.
説明(せつめい)に整合性(せいごうせい)がない。
The explanation lacks consistency.
整合性(せいごうせい)のある計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる。
To make a coherent plan.

整合性(せいごうせい) means consistency, coherence, or integrity — the quality of being logically connected and free from contradictions. Commonly used in technical and formal contexts.

WORD FORMATION:
整合(せいごう) (alignment, matching) + 性(せい) (quality, nature).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 整合性(せいごうせい)を保(たも)つ: to maintain consistency
  • 整合性(せいごうせい)がある/ない: to have/lack consistency
  • 整合性(せいごうせい)をチェックする: to check for consistency
  • 整合性(せいごうせい)が取(と)れている: to be consistent
  • データの整合性(せいごうせい): data integrity

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一貫性(いっかんせい): consistency (over time, in behavior)
  • 矛盾(むじゅん): contradiction (opposite concept)
  • 論理性(ろんりせい): logic, logicality

REGISTER:
Formal. Commonly used in IT, business, academic, and legal contexts.

Related Words

Synonym: 一貫性(いっかんせい) (consistency (over time))
Antonym: 矛盾(むじゅん) (contradiction)
Related: 論理性(ろんりせい) (logicality)
