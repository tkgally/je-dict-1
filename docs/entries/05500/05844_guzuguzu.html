1. slowly, dawdling
Taking too much time, being slow to act. Often implies procrastination or lack of motivation.
ぐずぐずするな。
Don't dawdle.
ぐずぐずしないで早(はや)くしなさい。
Stop dawdling and hurry up.
ぐずぐずしていると間(ま)に合(あ)わないよ。
If you keep dawdling, you won't make it in time.
子供(こども)がぐずぐずして学校(がっこう)に行(い)きたがらない。
The child is dawdling and doesn't want to go to school.
朝(あさ)からぐずぐずしていたら、約束(やくそく)の時間(じかん)に遅(おく)れてしまった。
I was dawdling since morning and ended up being late for my appointment.
2. indecisively, hesitantly
Being unable to make a decision or take action promptly.
ぐずぐず迷(まよ)っている。
I'm hesitating indecisively.
ぐずぐず言(い)わないで決(き)めて。
Stop hemming and hawing and just decide.
決断(けつだん)をぐずぐずと先延(さきの)ばしにした。
I kept putting off making a decision.
ぐずぐずと返事(へんじ)を出(だ)さないでいたら、相手(あいて)に怒(おこ)られた。
I kept putting off giving a reply, and the other person got angry with me.
いつまでもぐずぐずしていないで、自分(じぶん)で考(かんが)えて答(こた)えを出(だ)しなさい。
Stop being indecisive forever and think for yourself and come up with an answer.

ぐずぐず is an onomatopoeic adverb describing slow, dawdling, or indecisive behavior.

USAGE:

  • ぐずぐずする: to dawdle, procrastinate
  • ぐずぐず + verb: doing something slowly/hesitantly

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぐずぐずしないで: stop dawdling
  • ぐずぐず言(い)う: to complain/whine
  • ぐずぐず先延(さきの)ばしにする: to keep putting off

CONNOTATION:
Usually negative - implies laziness, reluctance, or inability to act decisively.

CONTRAST:

  • てきぱき: efficiently, briskly
  • さっさと: quickly, promptly
  • きびきび: energetically, snappily

Related Words

Synonym: だらだら (idle, sluggish)
Antonym: さっさと (quickly, promptly)
Antonym: てきぱき (briskly, efficiently)
