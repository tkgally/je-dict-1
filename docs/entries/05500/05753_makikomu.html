巻(ま)き込(こ)む

まきこむ
verb (godan)
to involve, to wrap up
1. to roll up, to wrap up in
To wind or wrap something into something else.
布(ぬの)を巻(ま)き込(こ)む。
To roll up the cloth.
髪(かみ)がドライヤーに巻(ま)き込(こ)まれた。
My hair got caught in the hair dryer.
機械(きかい)にスカーフが巻(ま)き込(こ)まれないように気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful not to let your scarf get caught in the machine.
2. to involve, to drag into
To get someone involved in a situation, often against their will.
事件(じけん)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれた。
I got caught up in an incident.
周(まわ)りを巻(ま)き込(こ)まないでください。
Please don't involve others.
友人(ゆうじん)の借金(しゃっきん)の問題(もんだい)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれて、とても困(こま)っている。
I've gotten dragged into my friend's debt problems and I'm really struggling.

巻(ま)き込(こ)む is a compound verb meaning to roll/wrap something in, or to involve someone in a situation.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Formed from 巻(ま)く (to wind) + 込(こ)む (into)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を巻(ま)き込(こ)む: to involve someone in ~
  • 〜に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる: to get caught up in ~ (passive)
  • 周(まわ)りを巻(ま)き込(こ)む: to involve those around you
  • 事件(じけん)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる: to get caught up in an incident

USAGE NOTE:
The passive form 巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる is very common and often implies being involuntarily dragged into a situation, whether a conflict, accident, or trouble.
