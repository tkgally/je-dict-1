善悪(ぜんあく)

ぜんあく
noun
good and evil; right and wrong
1. good and evil; right and wrong
The moral distinction between what is good and what is bad.
善悪(ぜんあく)の判断(はんだん)ができる。
To be able to judge right from wrong.
子供(こども)に善悪(ぜんあく)を教(おし)える。
To teach children right from wrong.
善悪(ぜんあく)の区別(くべつ)がつかない。
To be unable to distinguish between good and evil.

善悪(ぜんあく) combines 善(ぜん) (good) and 悪(あく) (evil). It's a fundamental concept in ethics and moral philosophy.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 善悪(ぜんあく)の判断(はんだん): moral judgment
  • 善悪(ぜんあく)の区別(くべつ): distinction between right and wrong
  • 善悪(ぜんあく)を問(と)わず: regardless of good or bad
