煮込(にこ)む

にこむ
verb (godan)
to simmer, stew
1. to simmer, to stew, to cook slowly
To cook food slowly in liquid over low heat for an extended time.
カレーを煮込(にこ)む。
To simmer curry.
肉(にく)を柔(やわ)らかくなるまで煮込(にこ)む。
To simmer the meat until tender.
よく煮込(にこ)んだスープ。
Well-simmered soup.

煮込(にこ)む means to simmer or stew food slowly, allowing flavors to blend thoroughly.

COMPOUND VERB:
煮(に)る (to boil/cook) + 込(こ)む (into, thoroughly) - the 込(こ)む adds the nuance of cooking deeply or for an extended time.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: カレーを煮込(にこ)む

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 弱火(よわび)で煮込(にこ)む: to simmer over low heat
  • じっくり煮込(にこ)む: to simmer thoroughly
  • 味(あじ)が染(し)みるまで煮込(にこ)む: to simmer until flavors soak in

COMMON DISHES:

  • 煮込(にこ)みうどん: stewed udon
  • 煮込(にこ)みハンバーグ: hamburger steak in sauce
  • 牛(ぎゅう)すじ(すじ)の煮込(にこ)み: simmered beef tendon
