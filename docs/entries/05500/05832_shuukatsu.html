就活(しゅうかつ)

しゅうかつ
noun, verb (suru)
job hunting
1. job hunting, job search
Abbreviation of 就職活動. The process of seeking employment, especially for new graduates.
就活(しゅうかつ)を始(はじ)める。
To start job hunting.
就活(しゅうかつ)中(ちゅう)の学生(がくせい)。
A student in the middle of job hunting.
就活(しゅうかつ)スーツを買(か)う。
To buy a job-hunting suit.
Standard dark suit worn for interviews

ABBREVIATION:
Short for 就職活動 (shuushoku katsudou).

CULTURAL:
In Japan, university students typically begin job hunting in their third year. Companies hold unified recruitment periods.

RELATED:

  • 婚活 (konkatsu): marriage hunting
  • 終活 (shuukatsu, different kanji): end-of-life preparation
