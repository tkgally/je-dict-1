肌寒(はださむ)い

はださむい
i-adjective
chilly, slightly cold

Definitions

1. chilly, slightly cold
Cold enough to feel on one's skin; uncomfortably cool but not freezing.

Examples

今日(きょう)は肌寒(はださむ)い。
It's chilly today.
朝晩(あさばん)は肌寒(はださむ)くなってきた。
It's getting chilly in the mornings and evenings.
肌寒(はださむ)いので上着(うわぎ)を羽織(はお)った。
I put on a jacket because it was chilly.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
Formed from 肌(はだ) (skin) + 寒(さむ)い (cold). Literally "cold to the skin."

ADVERBIAL FORM:
肌寒(はださむ)く

SEASONAL:
Often used to describe the cool weather of spring or autumn, when it's not quite cold but noticeably cooler than warm.
