ざっくり

ざっくり
adverb, onomatopoeia
roughly; loosely; in broad strokes
1. roughly; in broad strokes
Describes doing or explaining something in a rough, approximate manner without going into details.
ざっくり説明(せつめい)すると、こういうことだ。
To explain roughly, it's like this.
ざっくりした計算(けいさん)で百万円(ひゃくまんえん)くらいだ。
By rough calculation, it's about one million yen.
2. loosely; coarsely
Describes a rough texture or loose fit, often in relation to knitted items or chopped ingredients.
野菜(やさい)をざっくり切(き)る。
Chop the vegetables roughly.

USAGE:
ざっくり emphasizes approximation and a lack of precision, often intentionally. It's useful when details aren't necessary or when being casual.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ざっくり言(い)うと: to put it roughly
  • ざっくりした説明(せつめい): rough explanation
  • ざっくり切(き)る: to chop roughly/coarsely
  • ざっくりしたニット(にっと): loosely knit sweater

CONTRAST:

  • きっちり: precisely, exactly
  • 細(こま)かく: finely, in detail
  • 詳(くわ)しく: in detail
