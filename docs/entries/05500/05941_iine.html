いいね

いいね
noun, interjection
like (social media), sounds good

Definitions

1. like (social media)
A positive reaction on social media platforms, typically indicated by a heart or thumbs-up icon.
2. sounds good, nice
Expression of agreement or approval in conversation.

Examples

投稿(とうこう)にいいねを押(お)した。
I liked the post.
いいねが1000件(けん)を超(こ)えた。
The likes exceeded 1000.
「今度(こんど)飲(の)みに行(い)こう」「いいね！」
"Let's go drinking sometime." "Sounds good!"

Notes

SOCIAL MEDIA:
The social media meaning comes from the Japanese phrase いい+ね ("that's good, right?"). It became associated with the "like" function on various platforms.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • いいねを押(お)す: to press like
  • いいねを付(つ)ける: to give a like
  • いいね数(すう): number of likes
