若干(じゃっかん)

じゃっかん
adverb
some; a few; slightly
1. some; a few; several
An unspecified small number or amount.
若干(じゃっかん)名(めい)募集(ぼしゅう)。
Recruiting a few people.
若干(じゃっかん)の変更(へんこう)があった。
There were some changes.
2. somewhat; slightly
To a small degree.
若干(じゃっかん)遅(おく)れている。
Running slightly behind.

若干(じゃっかん) means some, a few, or slightly.

USAGE:

  • As a quantity: 若干(じゃっかん)名(めい) (a few people)
  • As a degree: 若干(じゃっかん)高(たか)い (somewhat expensive)

FORMALITY:
More formal than 少し or ちょっと.
