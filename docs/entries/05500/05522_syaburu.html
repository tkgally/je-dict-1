しゃぶる

しゃぶる
godan verb
to suck; to lick; to chew on

Definitions

1. to suck; to lick; to chew on
To put something in the mouth and suck or lick it.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんが指(ゆび)をしゃぶっている。
The baby is sucking its finger.
犬(いぬ)が骨(ほね)をしゃぶっている。
The dog is chewing on a bone.
あめをしゃぶる。
To suck on candy.

Notes

しゃぶる is a colloquial godan verb. It often has childish or informal connotations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指をしゃぶる: to suck one's finger
  • 骨をしゃぶる: to chew/gnaw on a bone
  • あめをしゃぶる: to suck on candy

RELATED WORDS:

  • 舐める (なめる): to lick (more general)
  • 吸う (すう): to suck, to inhale
