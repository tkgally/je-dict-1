凭(もた)れる

もたれる
verb (ichidan)
to lean on; to feel heavy (stomach)
1. to lean on; to rest against
To support one's body by leaning against something or someone.
椅子(いす)の背(せ)もたれに凭(もた)れる。
To lean against the back of a chair.
彼(かれ)は壁(かべ)に凭(もた)れて立(た)っていた。
He was standing leaning against the wall.
2. to feel heavy; to sit heavily (stomach)
To have a heavy, uncomfortable feeling in the stomach after eating.
油(あぶら)っこいものを食(た)べて胃(い)が凭(もた)れている。
My stomach feels heavy from eating greasy food.

凭(もた)れる has two distinct meanings: the physical action of leaning and the sensation of stomach heaviness.

CONJUGATION (ichidan verb):

  • 凭(もた)れる (dictionary form)
  • 凭(もた)れない (negative)
  • 凭(もた)れた (past)
  • 凭(もた)れて (te-form)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 凭(もた) is rarely used in modern writing; this word is often written in hiragana as もたれる.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 胃(い)にもたれる: to sit heavily on the stomach
  • 背(せ)もたれ: backrest (of a chair)

Related Words

Synonym: 寄(よ)り掛(か)かる (similar)
