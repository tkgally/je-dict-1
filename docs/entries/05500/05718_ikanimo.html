如何(いか)にも

いかにも
adverb
indeed, truly, as expected
1. indeed, truly, really
Used to emphasize that something is exactly as described or expected.
如何(いか)にも日本(にほん)らしい風景(ふうけい)だ。
It's a truly Japanese-looking landscape.
如何(いか)にも彼(かれ)らしい発言(はつげん)だ。
That's such a typical thing for him to say.
2. seemingly, as if
Suggesting something appears to be a certain way, sometimes implying it's not genuine.
如何(いか)にも怪(あや)しい男(おとこ)だ。
He looks really suspicious.

如何(いか)にも is an adverb meaning "truly," "indeed," or "seemingly," emphasizing typicality.

KANJI NOTE:
Usually written in hiragana (いかにも). The kanji 如何(いか) is rarely used.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 如何(いか)にも〜らしい: so typical of ~, very ~ -like
  • 如何(いか)にも〜そうだ: really looks like ~
  • 如何(いか)にも怪(あや)しい: really suspicious-looking

NUANCE:

Emphasizes that something perfectly matches expectations or stereotypes. Can be:

  • Positive: 如何(いか)にも日本(にほん)らしい (quintessentially Japanese)
  • Negative: 如何(いか)にも嘘(うそ)っぽい (looks obviously fake)
  • Neutral: 如何(いか)にも彼(かれ)らしい (so typical of him)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • まさに: exactly, precisely
  • 本当(ほんとう)に: really, truly
  • いかにも (without も): how, in what way
