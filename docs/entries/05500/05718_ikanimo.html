如何(いか)にも

いかにも
adverb
indeed, truly, as expected

Definitions

1. indeed, truly, really
Used to emphasize that something is exactly as described or expected.
2. seemingly, as if
Suggesting something appears to be a certain way, sometimes implying it's not genuine.

Examples

如何(いか)にも日本(にほん)らしい風景(ふうけい)だ。
It's a truly Japanese-looking landscape.
如何(いか)にも怪(あや)しい男(おとこ)だ。
He looks really suspicious.
如何(いか)にも彼(かれ)らしい発言(はつげん)だ。
That's such a typical thing for him to say.

Notes

USAGE:
如何(いか)にも emphasizes that something matches expectations or appears to be exactly a certain type. Can be positive or negative depending on context.
