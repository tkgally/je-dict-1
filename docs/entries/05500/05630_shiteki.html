指摘(してき)

してき
noun
pointing out; indication
1. pointing out; indication; identification
The act of drawing attention to something, especially a problem, error, or important point.
問題点(もんだいてん)を指摘(してき)する。
To point out the problems.
専門家(せんもんか)から指摘(してき)を受(う)けた。
Received feedback from an expert.
ご指摘(してき)の通(とお)りです。
It is as you pointed out.

指摘(してき) is commonly used in professional and academic settings for constructive criticism or observations. Often implies pointing out errors or issues.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 指摘(してき)する: to point out
  • 指摘(してき)を受(う)ける: to receive feedback/criticism
  • ご指摘(してき)の通(とお)り: as you pointed out (formal acknowledgment)
  • 問題点(もんだいてん)を指摘(してき)する: to point out problems

REGISTER:
ご指摘(してき) is the polite/honorific form, commonly used in business and formal writing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 批判(ひはん): criticism (more negative)
  • 注意(ちゅうい): warning, attention

Related Words
