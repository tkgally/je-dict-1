いいね

いいね
noun, interjection
like (social media), sounds good
1. like (social media)
A positive reaction on social media platforms, typically indicated by a heart or thumbs-up icon.
投稿(とうこう)にいいねを押(お)した。
I liked the post.
いいねが1000件(けん)を超(こ)えた。
The likes exceeded 1000.
写真(しゃしん)にいいねをたくさんもらえてうれしい。
I'm happy I got a lot of likes on my photo.
2. sounds good, nice
Expression of agreement or approval in conversation.
「今度(こんど)飲(の)みに行(い)こう」「いいね！」
"Let's go drinking sometime." "Sounds good!"
「明日(あした)映画(えいが)を見(み)に行(い)かない？」「いいね、行(い)こう！」
"Want to go see a movie tomorrow?" "Sounds good, let's go!"
「このケーキ、おいしいよ」「いいね、私(わたし)も食(た)べたい」
"This cake is delicious." "Nice, I want to try it too."

いいね has two uses: as a conversational interjection meaning "sounds good!" and as a noun for the "like" button/action on social media.

SOCIAL MEDIA USE:
The noun meaning comes from the conversational phrase いい + ね ("that's good, right?"). Platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook use いいね for their like feature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • いいねを押(お)す: to press like
  • いいねを付(つ)ける: to give a like
  • いいね数(すう): number of likes
  • いいねが多(おお)い: has many likes
  • いいねをもらう: to receive likes

CONVERSATIONAL USE:

As an interjection, いいね expresses agreement or approval:

  • 「明日(あした)映画(えいが)に行(い)かない？」「いいね！」: "Wanna go to a movie tomorrow?" "Sounds good!"
