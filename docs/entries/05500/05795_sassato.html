さっさと

さっさと
adverb
quickly, promptly
1. quickly, promptly, without delay
Doing something swiftly without wasting time. Often implies impatience.
さっさと片付(かたづ)けなさい。
Hurry up and clean up.
さっさと帰(かえ)ろう。
Let's hurry and go home.
彼(かれ)はさっさと仕事(しごと)を終(お)わらせた。
He finished the work quickly.

さっさと expresses doing something quickly without wasting time, often with a nuance of impatience.

USAGE:
Typically used when urging someone to hurry up or describing efficient action. Can sound pushy or impatient in commands.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • さっさと片付(かたづ)ける: to clean up quickly
  • さっさと済(す)ませる: to finish up quickly
  • さっさと帰(かえ)る: to hurry home
  • さっさと行(い)く: to go quickly

REGISTER:
Informal. Using さっさと in commands (さっさとしなさい) sounds demanding or frustrated.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 素早(すばや)く: quickly, swiftly (neutral)
  • てきぱき: briskly, efficiently (positive)
  • ぐずぐず: slowly, dawdling (negative, opposite meaning)
