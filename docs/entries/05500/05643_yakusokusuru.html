1. to promise, to make a commitment
To give one's word to do or not do something.
秘密(ひみつ)を守(まも)ると約束(やくそく)した。
I promised to keep the secret.
約束(やくそく)は守(まも)らなければならない。
Promises must be kept.
子供(こども)に毎日(まいにち)本(ほん)を読(よ)むと約束(やくそく)した。
I promised my child that I would read a book every day.
約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)ってしまったので友達(ともだち)に謝(あやま)らなければならない。
I broke my promise, so I have to apologize to my friend.
2. to make an appointment, to arrange to meet
To agree on a time and place to meet someone.
友達(ともだち)と映画(えいが)を見(み)る約束(やくそく)をした。
I made plans to see a movie with my friend.
明日(あした)会(あ)う約束(やくそく)をした。
We made plans to meet tomorrow.
先生(せんせい)と三時(さんじ)に約束(やくそく)がある。
I have an appointment with the teacher at three o'clock.
来週(らいしゅう)の土曜日(どようび)に会(あ)う約束(やくそく)をしていたのに急(きゅう)に仕事(しごと)が入(はい)ってしまった。
We had made plans to meet next Saturday, but work suddenly came up.
約束(やくそく)の時間(じかん)に遅(おく)れないでね。
Don't be late for the time we agreed on, okay?
約束(やくそく)の場所(ばしょ)に着(つ)いたけど相手(あいて)がまだ来(き)ていなかった。
I arrived at the meeting place we agreed on, but the other person hadn't come yet.

約束(やくそく)する covers a broader range than English "promise." It is used for both solemn commitments and casual plans to meet.

SENSE 1 — PROMISE:

  • 約束(やくそく)を守(まも)る: to keep a promise
  • 約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)る: to break a promise
  • 〜と約束(やくそく)する: to promise (someone) that ~

SENSE 2 — APPOINTMENT/PLANS:

  • 約束(やくそく)がある: to have plans/an appointment
  • 約束(やくそく)の時間(じかん): the agreed-upon time
  • 約束(やくそく)の場所(ばしょ): the agreed-upon place
  • 友達(ともだち)と約束(やくそく)する: to make plans with a friend

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 約束(やくそく)を取(と)り付(つ)ける: to secure a commitment/appointment
  • 約束(やくそく)どおり: as promised, as agreed

NOTE:
For formal or business appointments, 予約(よやく) (reservation) or アポ (from English "appointment") is more appropriate. 約束(やくそく) is typically used for informal agreements between individuals.
