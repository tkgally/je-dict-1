1. reply; response (social media)
A response to someone's post or tweet on social media platforms. Often abbreviated as リプ in casual usage.
彼女(かのじょ)の投稿(とうこう)にリプライした。
I replied to her post.
たくさんのリプライが来(き)て嬉(うれ)しかった。
I was happy to receive many replies.
リプライ欄(らん)で議論(ぎろん)が盛(も)り上(あ)がっている。
A lively discussion is happening in the replies section.

From English "reply." Used almost exclusively in social media contexts (X/Twitter, Instagram, etc.), not for email or formal correspondence.

ABBREVIATIONS:

  • リプ: casual abbreviation (most common in practice)
  • リプる: to reply (slang verb, adding る to the abbreviation)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • リプライを送(おく)る: to send a reply
  • リプライが来(く)る: to receive a reply
  • リプライ欄(らん): the replies section

CONTRAST WITH 返信(へんしん):
返信(へんしん) is the general word for "reply" used in email, messaging, and formal contexts. リプライ is specific to social media platforms.

RELATED SOCIAL MEDIA TERMS:

  • コメント: comment
  • いいね: like
  • リツイート / リポスト: retweet/repost
  • フォロー: follow
