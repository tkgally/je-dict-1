油断大敵(ゆだんたいてき)

ゆだんたいてき
expression
carelessness is one's greatest enemy
1. carelessness is one's greatest enemy, overconfidence is dangerous
A warning that letting one's guard down or becoming complacent can lead to failure.
油断大敵(ゆだんたいてき)だから、気(き)を引(ひ)き締(し)めよう。
Carelessness is the enemy, so let's stay focused.
試合(しあい)は最後(さいご)まで油断大敵(ゆだんたいてき)だ。
In a match, you can't let your guard down until the end.
合格(ごうかく)したと思(おも)っても油断大敵(ゆだんたいてき)だよ。
Even if you think you passed, don't get overconfident.

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語):
油断(ゆだん) means "carelessness" or "letting one's guard down," and 大敵(たいてき) means "great enemy." This is a common proverb warning against complacency.

USAGE:

  • Often used as a reminder to stay vigilant
  • Common in sports, work, and study contexts
