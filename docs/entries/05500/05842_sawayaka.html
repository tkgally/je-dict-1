爽(さわ)やか

さわやか
na-adjective
refreshing, fresh

Definitions

1. refreshing, fresh, clear
Pleasantly clean, clear, and invigorating, often describing weather, air, or a person's demeanor.

Examples

爽(さわ)やかな朝(あさ)だ。
It's a refreshing morning.
彼(かれ)は爽(さわ)やかな笑顔(えがお)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
He has a refreshing smile.
爽(さわ)やかな風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
A refreshing breeze is blowing.

Notes

USAGE:

爽(さわ)やか is often used to describe:

  • Weather: cool, crisp, clear weather
  • People: those with a clean, pleasant appearance or personality
  • Feelings: a clean, refreshed feeling

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 爽(さわ)やかな印象(いんしょう): refreshing impression
  • 爽(さわ)やかな香(かお)り: refreshing scent
