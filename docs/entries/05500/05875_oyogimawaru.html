1. to swim around
To swim in various directions; to swim about an area.
魚(さかな)が水槽(すいそう)の中(なか)を泳(およ)ぎ回(まわ)っている。
The fish are swimming around in the tank.
子供(こども)たちがプールで泳(およ)ぎ回(まわ)っていた。
The children were swimming around in the pool.
イルカが船(ふね)の周(まわ)りを泳(およ)ぎ回(まわ)った。
Dolphins swam around the boat.

A compound verb meaning to swim around freely or in various directions.

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 泳(およ)ぐ (to swim) + 回(まわ)る (to go around). The ～回(まわ)る pattern indicates movement in various directions or throughout an area.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • The subject (fish, person, etc.) moves freely through water

ASPECT (ている):
泳(およ)ぎ回(まわ)っている describes the ongoing action of swimming around, emphasizing continuous movement in multiple directions.

SIMILAR ～回(まわ)る COMPOUNDS:

  • 走(はし)り回(まわ)る: to run around
  • 歩(ある)き回(まわ)る: to walk around
  • 飛(と)び回(まわ)る: to fly around
  • 動(うご)き回(まわ)る: to move around
