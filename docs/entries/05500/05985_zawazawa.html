1. rustling; murmuring
The sound of many things moving or people talking at once, creating a low, continuous noise. Typical of leaves in wind or a crowded room.
木(き)の葉(は)がざわざわ鳴(な)っている。
The leaves are rustling.
会場(かいじょう)がざわざわしている。
The venue is buzzing with noise.
風(かぜ)で木(き)の葉(は)がざわざわと音(おと)を立(た)てた。
The wind made the leaves rustle.
教室(きょうしつ)がざわざわしていて、先生(せんせい)の声(こえ)が聞(き)こえなかった。
The classroom was so noisy that I couldn't hear the teacher.
発表(はっぴょう)が始(はじ)まると観客席(かんきゃくせき)がざわざわとざわめき、皆(みな)驚(おどろ)いた様子(ようす)だった。
When the announcement began, the audience started murmuring, and everyone looked surprised.
2. feeling uneasy; unsettled
A psychological sensation of unease, anxiety, or foreboding. Often used to describe a gut feeling that something is wrong.
胸(むね)がざわざわする。
I have an uneasy feeling.
胸(むね)がざわざわして落(お)ち着(つ)かない。
I feel uneasy and can't calm down.
何(なに)か悪(わる)いことが起(お)きそうで、心(こころ)がざわざわしている。
I feel unsettled, as if something bad is about to happen.
あのニュースを聞(き)いてから、ずっと気持(きも)ちがざわざわしている。
Ever since I heard that news, I've been feeling uneasy.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が出(で)る前(まえ)は、いつも胸(むね)がざわざわして夜(よる)も眠(ねむ)れなくなる。
Before exam results come out, I always feel so anxious that I can't sleep at night.

ざわざわ is an onomatopoeia with two distinct uses: a physical sound and a psychological state.

SENSE 1 — PHYSICAL SOUND:

Describes a continuous, low-level noise from many sources — leaves in wind, a crowd murmuring, a restless audience.

  • ざわざわする / ざわざわしている: to be noisy, rustling
  • ざわざわと音(おと)を立(た)てる: to make a rustling sound

SENSE 2 — PSYCHOLOGICAL UNEASE:

Describes a churning, unsettled feeling in the chest or heart — a sense that something is wrong.

  • 胸(むね)がざわざわする: to feel anxious, uneasy
  • 気持(きも)ちがざわざわする: to feel unsettled

CONTRAST:

  • がやがや: noisier, more chaotic crowd noise (louder than ざわざわ)
  • そわそわ: restless, fidgety (visible physical anxiety, not a sound)
  • ざわめく: to stir, to murmur (related verb, not onomatopoeia)
