えぐい

えぐい
i-adjective
harsh; extreme; disgusting
1. harsh; severe; extreme
Describing something intense, extreme, or difficult to handle (modern slang).
2. acrid; disgusting (taste)
Having an unpleasant, bitter, or harsh taste that causes discomfort.
その映画(えいが)のシーンはえぐかった。
That scene in the movie was intense/disturbing.
残業(ざんぎょう)がえぐい。
The overtime is extreme/brutal.
この野菜(やさい)はえぐみがある。
This vegetable has an acrid taste.

えぐい originally described unpleasant tastes but has evolved into slang meaning "extreme" or "harsh."

MODERN USAGE:
In contemporary Japanese, it often means "intense," "brutal," or "disturbing," similar to やばい.

CONJUGATION:

  • えぐい (dictionary form)
  • えぐくない (negative)
  • えぐかった (past)
  • えぐくて (te-form)
