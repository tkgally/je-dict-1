和(なご)やか

なごやか
na-adjective
harmonious, peaceful
1. harmonious, peaceful, friendly
Having a pleasant, relaxed, and friendly atmosphere.
和(なご)やかな雰囲気(ふんいき)の中(なか)で話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed in a harmonious atmosphere.
会議(かいぎ)は和(なご)やかに進(すす)んだ。
The meeting proceeded peacefully.
和(なご)やかな家庭(かてい)を築(きず)きたい。
I want to build a harmonious home.

USAGE:
和(なご)やか describes situations or atmospheres where people get along well without tension or conflict.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 和(なご)やかな雰囲気(ふんいき): harmonious atmosphere
  • 和(なご)やかな食事(しょくじ): pleasant meal
  • 和(なご)やかに過(す)ごす: to spend time peacefully
general
