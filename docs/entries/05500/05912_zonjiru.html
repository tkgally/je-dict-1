1. to know (humble)
A humble expression for knowing something. Used when speaking to someone of higher status to lower oneself.
はい、存(ぞん)じております。
Yes, I know (humble).
ご存(ぞん)じですか。
Do you know? (honorific to listener)
その件(けん)は存(ぞん)じません。
I don't know about that matter.
お名前(なまえ)は以前(いぜん)から存(ぞん)じ上(あ)げておりました。
I have known your name for a long time.
社長(しゃちょう)のことはよく存(ぞん)じております。
I know the president well.
2. to think; to believe (humble)
A humble way of expressing one's thoughts or opinions.
大変(たいへん)光栄(こうえい)に存(ぞん)じます。
I feel very honored.
ぜひお力(ちから)になりたいと存(ぞん)じます。
I would very much like to be of help.
問題(もんだい)ないと存(ぞん)じます。
I believe there is no problem.
この計画(けいかく)は大変(たいへん)有意義(ゆういぎ)であると存(ぞん)じます。
I believe this plan is very meaningful.
皆様(みなさま)のご理解(りかい)をいただければ幸(さいわ)いに存(ぞん)じます。
I would be grateful for everyone's understanding.

存(ぞん)じる is a humble verb (謙譲語(けんじょうご)) for "to know" or "to think."

KEIGO USAGE:

  • 存(ぞん)じております: I know (ている form, humble)
  • 存(ぞん)じません: I don't know (humble)
  • ご存(ぞん)じ: honorific form when asking if someone knows

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 存(ぞん)じ上(あ)げる: to know (very humble)
  • …と存(ぞん)じます: I think that... (formal humble)

ALTERNATE FORM:
存(ぞん)ずる is a classical variant still seen in formal contexts.
