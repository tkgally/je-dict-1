炎上(えんじょう)

えんじょう
noun, verb (suru)
going up in flames; online controversy, flaming

Definitions

1. going up in flames
A building or object being engulfed in fire.
2. online controversy, flaming
A situation where a person or organization receives massive criticism on social media, often due to a controversial statement or action.

Examples

建物(たてもの)が炎上(えんじょう)した。
The building went up in flames.
あの発言(はつげん)でSNSが炎上(えんじょう)した。
That comment caused a firestorm on social media.
炎上(えんじょう)を恐(おそ)れて投稿(とうこう)を控(ひか)える。
I refrain from posting for fear of getting flamed.

Notes

INTERNET SLANG:
The social media meaning (sense 2) developed from the original meaning of "going up in flames." It describes situations where negative comments flood in rapidly, like a fire spreading.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • SNSで炎上(えんじょう)する: to be flamed on social media
  • 炎上(えんじょう)を招(まね)く: to invite controversy
  • 炎上(えんじょう)商法(しょうほう): controversy marketing
