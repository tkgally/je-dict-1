さっさと

さっさと
adverb
quickly, promptly

Definitions

1. quickly, promptly, without delay
Doing something swiftly without wasting time. Often implies impatience.

Examples

さっさと片付(かたづ)けなさい。
Hurry up and clean up.
さっさと帰(かえ)ろう。
Let's hurry and go home.
彼(かれ)はさっさと仕事(しごと)を終(お)わらせた。
He finished the work quickly.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Expresses swift action. Often used in commands with impatient nuance.
