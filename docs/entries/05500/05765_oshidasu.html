押(お)し出(だ)す

おしだす
verb (godan)
to push out, to force out
1. to push out, to force out
To push something or someone out from a place.
競争(きょうそう)から押(お)し出(だ)された。
I was pushed out of the competition.
勇気(ゆうき)を振(ふ)り絞(しぼ)って一歩(いっぽ)押(お)し出(だ)す。
I summon my courage and push forward a step.
2. to squeeze out, to extrude
To push out a substance from a container.
歯磨(はみが)き粉(こ)を押(お)し出(だ)す。
To squeeze out toothpaste.

押(お)し出(だ)す means to push something outward or squeeze out a substance.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the thing being pushed out

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 押(お)す (to push) + 出(だ)す (to put out, to begin).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 歯磨(はみが)き粉(こ)を押(お)し出(だ)す: squeeze out toothpaste
  • 市場(しじょう)から押(お)し出(だ)される: be pushed out of the market
  • 前(まえ)に押(お)し出(だ)す: push forward

ASPECT (ている):
押(お)し出(だ)している indicates the action is ongoing or habitual.

NOTE:
In baseball, 押(お)し出(だ)し refers to a "walk with bases loaded" (scoring a run by walking a batter).
