専(もっぱ)ら

もっぱら
adverb
exclusively, solely
1. exclusively, solely, mainly
Focusing on one thing only, to the exclusion of others.
2. widely, generally (in rumors)
Something that is widely said or believed.
最近(さいきん)は専(もっぱ)ら家(いえ)で仕事(しごと)をしている。
Recently, I've been working exclusively from home.
専(もっぱ)らの噂(うわさ)だ。
That's what everyone's saying.
Set expression
専(もっぱ)ら読書(どくしょ)に時間(じかん)を費(つい)やしている。
I spend my time mainly on reading.

USAGE:
専(もっぱ)ら indicates focusing on or dedicating oneself to one thing.

SET EXPRESSION:

  • 専(もっぱ)らの噂(うわさ): what everyone's saying, the word on the street
