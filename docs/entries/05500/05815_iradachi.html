苛立(いらだ)ち

いらだち
noun
irritation, frustration
See also: 苛立(いらだ)つ (verb form)
1. irritation, frustration, annoyance
A feeling of being annoyed or frustrated.
苛立(いらだ)ちを隠(かく)せない。
Unable to hide one's irritation.
苛立(いらだ)ちを感(かん)じる。
To feel frustrated.
苛立(いらだ)ちが募(つの)る。
The frustration builds up.

苛立(いらだ)ち is the noun form of 苛立(いらだ)つ, expressing irritation or frustration.

RELATED FORMS:

  • 苛立(いらだ)つ (verb): to be irritated
  • 苛立(いらだ)たしい (adjective): irritating, frustrating
  • 苛立(いらだ)ち (noun): irritation

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 苛立(いらだ)ちを覚(おぼ)える: to feel irritation
  • 苛立(いらだ)ちを感(かん)じる: to feel frustration
  • 苛立(いらだ)ちが募(つの)る: frustration builds up
  • 苛立(いらだ)ちを隠(かく)す: to hide one's irritation
  • 苛立(いらだ)ちを抑(おさ)える: to suppress irritation

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • イライラ: irritation (onomatopoeia, more casual)
  • 焦(あせ)り: frustration, impatience
  • もどかしさ: frustration (at inability to do something)

Related Words

Synonym: 焦燥(しょうそう) (anxious impatience (formal))
Synonym: いらいら (irritation (casual))
Related: もどかしい (frustrating)
