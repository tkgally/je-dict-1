横(よこ)になる

よこになる
expression
to lie down

Definitions

1. to lie down; to recline
To move from a sitting or standing position to lying down horizontally.

Examples

疲(つか)れたので少(すこ)し横(よこ)になりたい。
I'm tired so I want to lie down for a bit.
ソファに横(よこ)になって本(ほん)を読(よ)む。
To lie down on the sofa and read a book.
頭(あたま)が痛(いた)いので、横(よこ)になってもいいですか。
I have a headache, so may I lie down?

Notes

横(よこ)になる is a common expression for lying down, literally meaning "to become horizontal."

CONJUGATION:

  • 横(よこ)になる (dictionary form)
  • 横(よこ)にならない (negative)
  • 横(よこ)になった (past)
  • 横(よこ)になって (te-form)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寝転(ねころ)ぶ: to lie down, to sprawl
  • 寝(ね)る: to sleep, to go to bed

USAGE NOTE:
This expression specifically refers to the action of reclining, without implying sleep. It's often used when someone wants to rest briefly.
