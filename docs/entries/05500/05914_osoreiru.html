恐(おそ)れ入(い)る

おそれいる
verb (godan)
to be obliged; to be sorry; much obliged

Definitions

1. to be obliged; to be grateful
A humble expression of gratitude or appreciation when receiving kindness or favors from others.
2. to be sorry for troubling; excuse me
A polite way to apologize for troubling someone or to preface a request.

Examples

恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、もう一度(いちど)説明(せつめい)していただけますか。
I'm sorry to trouble you, but could you explain again?
ご親切(しんせつ)に恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります。
I'm much obliged for your kindness.
恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、お名前(なまえ)を伺(うかが)えますか。
Excuse me, may I ask your name?

Notes

恐(おそ)れ入(い)る is a very polite humble expression used in formal situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが…: Excuse me, but... (preface to request)
  • 恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります: I'm much obliged / Thank you

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Business situations
  • Customer service
  • Formal telephone calls
  • Making polite requests

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • すみませんが: Excuse me, but... (less formal)
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありませんが: I'm sorry, but... (apologetic)
