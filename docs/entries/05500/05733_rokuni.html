碌(ろく)に

ろくに
adverb
(not) properly; (not) satisfactorily
1. (not) properly; (not) satisfactorily
Used with negative expressions to mean not doing something properly or adequately.
碌(ろく)に食(た)べていない。
I haven't eaten properly.
碌(ろく)に眠(ねむ)れなかった。
I couldn't sleep properly.
碌(ろく)に説明(せつめい)もしないで帰(かえ)った。
He left without even explaining properly.

GRAMMAR NOTE:

碌(ろく)に is almost always used with NEGATIVE forms:

  • 碌(ろく)に〜ない (doesn't do properly)
  • 碌(ろく)に〜できない (can't do properly)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 碌(ろく)に食(た)べていない: haven't eaten properly
  • 碌(ろく)に寝(ね)ていない: haven't slept properly
  • 碌(ろく)に話(はな)せない: can't speak properly
  • 碌(ろく)に知(し)らない: don't really know

WRITING:
Often written in hiragana as ろくに.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ちゃんと〜ない: don't do properly
  • まともに〜ない: don't do decently
