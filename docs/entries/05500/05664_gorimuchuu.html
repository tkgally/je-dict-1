五里霧中(ごりむちゅう)

ごりむちゅう
noun
totally lost, in the dark

Definitions

1. being totally lost, in the dark, groping in the fog
A state of confusion where one cannot see the way forward or understand the situation.

Examples

捜査(そうさ)は五里霧中(ごりむちゅう)の状態(じょうたい)だ。
The investigation is completely in the dark.
将来(しょうらい)のことは五里霧中(ごりむちゅう)だ。
The future is completely unclear.
問題(もんだい)の解決策(かいけつさく)が五里霧中(ごりむちゅう)だった。
The solution to the problem was completely unclear.

Notes

FOUR-CHARACTER IDIOM (四字熟語):
Literally means "in fog for five ri" (a ri is an old Japanese unit of distance, about 4 km). The imagery is of being lost in thick fog where you cannot see anything around you.

ORIGIN:
From a Chinese legend about a Taoist who could create fog that extended for five ri.

USAGE:

  • Often used when describing situations with no clear solution or direction
