簡潔(かんけつ)

かんけつ
na-adjective
concise; brief; succinct

Definitions

1. concise; brief; succinct
Expressing much in few words; clear and to the point without unnecessary detail.

Examples

簡潔(かんけつ)に説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain briefly.
簡潔(かんけつ)な文章(ぶんしょう)を書(か)くのは難(むずか)しい。
Writing concise sentences is difficult.
報告(ほうこく)は簡潔(かんけつ)にまとめた。
I summarized the report concisely.

Notes

簡潔(かんけつ) is valued in Japanese business and academic writing.

COMMON USAGE:

  • 簡潔(かんけつ)に: briefly, concisely
  • 簡潔(かんけつ)な表現(ひょうげん): concise expression
