ひたすら

ひたすら
adverb
earnestly, single-mindedly

Definitions

1. earnestly, intently, single-mindedly
Doing something with complete focus and dedication, without distraction.

Examples

ひたすら練習(れんしゅう)を続(つづ)けた。
I kept practicing single-mindedly.
ひたすら謝(あやま)るしかなかった。
All I could do was keep apologizing.
ひたすら走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
I just kept running.

Notes

USAGE:
ひたすら describes doing something with complete dedication, often for a long time, without getting distracted or giving up.
