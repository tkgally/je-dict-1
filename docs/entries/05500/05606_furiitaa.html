フリーター

ふりーたー
noun
freeter; part-time worker

Definitions

1. freeter; part-time worker
A person who makes a living from temporary or part-time jobs rather than full-time employment.

Examples

卒業後(そつぎょうご)フリーターになった。
I became a freeter after graduating.
フリーターから正社員(せいしゃいん)になりたい。
I want to become a full-time employee from being a freeter.
フリーター生活(せいかつ)を続(つづ)けている。
I'm continuing to live as a freeter.

Notes

フリーター is a Japanese coinage from "free" + "arbeiter" (German for worker).

SOCIAL CONTEXT:
While some choose this lifestyle for flexibility, it can also indicate difficulty finding regular employment. The term emerged in the 1980s.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 正社員(せいしゃいん): full-time employee
  • 派遣(はけん)社員(しゃいん): temporary employee
