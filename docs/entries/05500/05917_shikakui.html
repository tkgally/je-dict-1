1. square-shaped; rectangular
Describes something having a square or rectangular shape with four corners.
四角(しかく)い箱(はこ)に入(い)れてください。
Please put it in the square box.
顔(かお)が四角(しかく)い。
His face is angular/square.
四角(しかく)いテーブルが部屋(へや)の真(ま)ん中(なか)にある。
There is a rectangular table in the middle of the room.
2. angular; stiff; formal
Describes a personality or manner that is rigid, formal, or inflexible.
そんなに四角(しかく)い考(かんが)え方(かた)をしないで。
Don't think in such a rigid way.
彼(かれ)は四角(しかく)い性格(せいかく)で冗談(じょうだん)が通(つう)じない。
He has a rigid personality and doesn't get jokes.
もう少(すこ)し四角(しかく)くない話(はな)し方(かた)をした方(ほう)がいいよ。
You should speak in a less stiff way.

四角(しかく)い is the everyday adjective for describing square or rectangular shapes. It covers both squares and rectangles — any shape with four corners.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 四角(しかく)い箱(はこ): square/rectangular box
  • 四角(しかく)い顔(かお): square/angular face
  • 四角(しかく)いテーブル: rectangular table
  • 四角(しかく)く切(き)る: to cut into a square shape

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
Describes a rigid, overly formal personality: 四角(しかく)い考(かんが)え方(かた) (rigid thinking). The four-character compound 四角四面(しかくしめん) means "extremely stiff and formal."
