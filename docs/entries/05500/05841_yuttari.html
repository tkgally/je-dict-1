ゆったり

ゆったり
adverb, verb (suru)
relaxed, loose, spacious
1. relaxed, at ease
In a calm, relaxed manner without rushing or feeling stressed.
2. loose, spacious
Having plenty of room or space; not tight or constrained.
休日(きゅうじつ)はゆったり過(す)ごしたい。
I want to spend my day off relaxing.
ゆったりした服(ふく)が好(す)きだ。
I like loose-fitting clothes.
ゆったりとソファに座(すわ)って本(ほん)を読(よ)んだ。
I sat comfortably on the sofa and read a book.

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes relaxation and spaciousness. Can refer to a relaxed pace/manner or to physical looseness (clothing, space).

CONTRAST:

  • きつきつ: tight, cramped
  • ばたばた: busy, hectic
