盆踊(ぼんおど)り

ぼんおどり
noun
Bon festival dance
1. Bon festival dance
A traditional dance performed during the Obon festival in summer to honor the spirits of ancestors.
夏(なつ)になると町(まち)で盆踊(ぼんおど)りがある。
There's a Bon dance in town when summer comes.
浴衣(ゆかた)を着(き)て盆踊(ぼんおど)りに参加(さんか)した。
I wore a yukata and participated in the Bon dance.
盆踊(ぼんおど)りの輪(わ)に加(くわ)わった。
I joined the circle of the Bon dance.

TIMING:
盆踊(ぼんおど)り is performed during お盆(おぼん) (Obon), typically in mid-August (or mid-July in some regions).

STYLE:
Dancers form a circle around a raised platform (櫓(やぐら)) and perform synchronized movements to traditional folk songs. Each region has its own distinctive dance style.

FAMOUS DANCES:

  • 阿波踊(あわおど)り (Tokushima)
  • 郡上踊(ぐじょうおど)り (Gifu)
  • 西馬音内盆踊(にしもないぼんおど)り (Akita)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 盆踊(ぼんおど)りを踊(おど)る: to dance the Bon dance
  • 盆踊(ぼんおど)り大会(たいかい): Bon dance festival
  • 盆踊(ぼんおど)りの曲(きょく): Bon dance music
