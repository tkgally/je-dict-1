お越(こ)しになる

おこしになる
verb (godan)
to come; to go (honorific)
1. to come; to visit (honorific)
An honorific expression used to respectfully describe someone coming to a place. Used when speaking about or to people of higher status.
先生(せんせい)がお越(こ)しになりました。
The teacher has arrived.
何時(なんじ)にお越(こ)しになりますか。
What time will you be coming?
またお越(こ)しくださいませ。
Please come again.

お越(こ)しになる is a formal honorific (尊敬語(そんけいご)) for "to come."

KEIGO LEVEL:
This is a respectful expression used to honor the person coming. It elevates the subject's action.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • いらっしゃる: another honorific for come/go/be
  • お見(み)えになる: to arrive (honorific)
  • 参(まい)る: to come/go (humble)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お越(こ)しください: please come
  • お越(こ)しくださいませ: please come (more polite)
  • お越(こ)しいただく: to have someone come (humble-honorific)

Related Words

Keigo: 来(く)る (plain form)
