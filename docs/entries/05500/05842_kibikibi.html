1. briskly, energetically
Acting or moving in a quick, lively, and efficient manner. Suggests sharp, decisive movements.
きびきびと動(うご)く店員(てんいん)さんだ。
That store clerk moves briskly.
きびきびした動作(どうさ)で仕事(しごと)をこなす。
She handles work with quick, efficient movements.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)がきびきびと働(はたら)いている。
The new employee is working energetically.
朝(あさ)からきびきびと行動(こうどう)すると、一日(いちにち)が気持(きも)ちよく始(はじ)まる。
If you act briskly from the morning, the day starts off pleasantly.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつもきびきびしていて、周(まわ)りの人(ひと)にも良(よ)い影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えている。
She is always brisk and energetic, and has a positive influence on those around her.

きびきび is an onomatopoeic adverb describing quick, energetic, and efficient actions or movements. It has a positive connotation.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • きびきびと + verb: to do ~ briskly
  • きびきびした + noun: brisk, efficient ~
  • きびきび動(うご)く: to move briskly
  • きびきび働(はたら)く: to work energetically

EXAMPLES:

  • きびきびとした動作(どうさ): brisk, efficient movements
  • きびきびした対応(たいおう): quick, efficient response
  • きびきびと指示(しじ)を出(だ)す: to give instructions crisply

CONTRAST (antonyms):

  • だらだら: sluggishly, lazily
  • ぐずぐず: slowly, indecisively
  • のろのろ: slowly, sluggishly

SIMILAR (positive movement):

  • てきぱき: efficiently, briskly (similar meaning)
  • しゃきしゃき: crisply, briskly

USAGE CONTEXT:
Commonly used to praise employees, service staff, or anyone who works/moves efficiently.
