1. eating greedily; ravenously
Describes eating food quickly and greedily, as if starving. The manner suggests lack of manners or extreme hunger.
がつがつ食(た)べるな。
Don't eat so greedily.
子供(こども)たちはおやつをがつがつ食(た)べた。
The children wolfed down their snacks.
お腹(なか)が空(す)いていたので、がつがつ食(た)べてしまった。
I was hungry, so I devoured the food.
もっとゆっくり食(た)べなさい。がつがつしないで。
Eat more slowly. Don't be so ravenous.
久(ひさ)しぶりの家庭(かてい)料理(りょうり)を、みんながつがつ食(た)べていた。
Everyone was wolfing down the home cooking they hadn't had in a long time.
2. being greedy; eager for money or status
Describes an aggressive, greedy pursuit of something, especially money, success, or material things.
お金(かね)にがつがつするな。
Don't be so greedy for money.
お金(かね)にがつがつしている人(ひと)は信用(しんよう)できない。
I can't trust people who are greedy for money.
がつがつ働(はたら)いてばかりいないで、少(すこ)し休(やす)んだら？
Instead of working so obsessively, why don't you take a break?
出世(しゅっせ)にがつがつしている同僚(どうりょう)がいる。
There's a colleague who is aggressively pursuing promotion.
もう若(わか)くないから、がつがつせずにのんびり生(い)きたい。
I'm not young anymore, so I want to live at ease without being so driven.

がつがつ has two related meanings: eating greedily (sense 1) and being greedy for money/status (sense 2). Both carry negative connotations of lacking restraint.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぱくぱく: eating with mouth opening and closing (neutral)
  • もぐもぐ: munching with mouth closed (neutral/cute)
  • ばくばく: eating quickly in large bites

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • がつがつ食(た)べる: to eat greedily
  • お金(かね)にがつがつする: to be greedy for money
  • がつがつ働(はたら)く: to work obsessively for money
