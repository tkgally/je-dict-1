崖(がけ)

がけ
noun
cliff; precipice

Definitions

1. cliff; precipice
A steep rock face, especially at the edge of the sea or a mountain.

Examples

崖(がけ)から落(お)ちそうになった。
I almost fell off the cliff.
崖(がけ)の上(うえ)からの景色(けしき)は絶景(ぜっけい)だった。
The view from the top of the cliff was spectacular.
崖崩(がけくず)れに注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful of landslides.

Notes

崖 refers to steep cliff faces.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 崖っぷち (がけっぷち): cliff edge; brink (figuratively: desperate situation)
  • 崖崩れ (がけくずれ): cliff collapse, landslide

FIGURATIVE USE:
崖っぷち is commonly used figuratively to mean being in a desperate or critical situation.
