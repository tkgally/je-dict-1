感覚的(かんかくてき)

かんかくてき
na-adjective
sensory; intuitive

Definitions

1. sensory; related to the senses
Pertaining to perception through the five senses.
2. intuitive; based on feeling rather than logic
Relying on instinct or feeling rather than rational analysis.

Examples

感覚的(かんかくてき)に理解(りかい)できる。
I can understand it intuitively.
感覚的(かんかくてき)な表現(ひょうげん)が多(おお)い詩(し)だ。
It's a poem with many sensory expressions.
感覚的(かんかくてき)に選(えら)んだ。
I chose it intuitively.

Notes

WORD FORMATION:
感覚(かんかく) (sense, sensation) + 的(てき) (adjectival suffix)

TWO NUANCES:
1. SENSORY: Related to physical senses (sight, sound, etc.)
2. INTUITIVE: Based on gut feeling rather than analysis

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 感覚的(かんかくてき)な判断(はんだん): intuitive judgment
  • 感覚的(かんかくてき)に分(わ)かる: to understand intuitively
  • 感覚的(かんかくてき)な快楽(かいらく): sensory pleasure

CONTRAST:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical
  • 理性的(りせいてき): rational
