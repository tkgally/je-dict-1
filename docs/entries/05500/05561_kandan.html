寒暖(かんだん)

かんだん
noun
cold and warmth; temperature variation
1. cold and warmth; temperature variation
The fluctuation between cold and warm temperatures.
寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ)が激(はげ)しい。
The temperature variation is extreme.
寒暖(かんだん)計(けい)で温度(おんど)を測(はか)る。
To measure the temperature with a thermometer.
春(はる)は寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ)が大(おお)きい季節(きせつ)だ。
Spring is a season with large temperature differences.

寒暖(かんだん) combines 寒(かん) (cold) + 暖(だん) (warm) to describe the contrast between cold and warm temperatures.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ): temperature difference/variation
  • 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ)が激(はげ)しい: extreme temperature variation
  • 寒暖(かんだん)計(けい): thermometer
  • 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ)が大(おお)きい: large temperature difference

USAGE:
Most commonly appears in the phrase 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ). Used when talking about weather, seasonal changes, and health. Large 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ) is often cited as a cause of 病気(びょうき) or discomfort.

SEASONAL CONTEXT:

  • 春(はる) and 秋(あき) are known for significant 寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ)
  • 朝晩(あさばん)の寒暖(かんだん)の差(さ): morning-evening temperature difference

RELATED:

  • 気温(きおん): air temperature
  • 温度差(おんどさ): temperature difference (more general)
