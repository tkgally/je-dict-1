紅葉狩(もみじが)り

もみじがり
noun
autumn leaf viewing
1. autumn leaf viewing; maple leaf viewing
The activity of going out to view autumn foliage, especially maple leaves.
秋(あき)に紅葉狩(もみじが)りに行(い)く。
To go autumn leaf viewing in fall.
友達(ともだち)と紅葉狩(もみじが)りを楽(たの)しんだ。
I enjoyed autumn leaf viewing with friends.
京都(きょうと)は紅葉狩(もみじが)りの名所(めいしょ)だ。
Kyoto is a famous spot for autumn leaf viewing.
紅葉狩(もみじが)りで山(やま)を散策(さんさく)するのが好(す)きだ。
I like strolling in the mountains for autumn leaf viewing.
家族(かぞく)で紅葉狩(もみじが)りに出(で)かけて、赤(あか)や黄色(きいろ)に色(いろ)づいた山(やま)の景色(けしき)を楽(たの)しんだ。
We went out with the family for autumn leaf viewing and enjoyed the mountain scenery colored in red and yellow.

紅葉狩(もみじが)り is a traditional autumn activity — going to parks, mountains, or temples to enjoy the colorful fall foliage. It is the autumn counterpart of 花見(はなみ) (cherry blossom viewing) in spring.

WORD ORIGIN:
紅葉(もみじ) (autumn leaves) + 狩(か)り (hunting). 狩(か)り originally meant "hunting" but evolved to mean "going out to enjoy" nature. The same pattern appears in いちご狩(が)り (strawberry picking).

SEASON:
Typically late October through November, with the peak moving from north to south across Japan.

FAMOUS SPOTS:
京都(きょうと), 日光(にっこう), and 箱根(はこね) are among the most popular 紅葉狩(もみじが)り destinations.
