健(すこ)やか

すこやか
na-adjective
healthy, sound
1. healthy, sound, vigorous
In good health, physically and mentally well.
健(すこ)やかに成長(せいちょう)してほしい。
I hope you grow up healthy.
Often used in well-wishes for children
健(すこ)やかな毎日(まいにち)を送(おく)る。
I live a healthy daily life.
お子(こ)さんの健(すこ)やかな成長(せいちょう)をお祈(いの)りします。
I pray for your child's healthy growth.
Formal blessing/well-wish

健(すこ)やか is a na-adjective describing good health and vitality, often with a nuance of wholesome well-being.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 健(すこ)やかな + noun
  • Adverbial: 健(すこ)やかに

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 健(すこ)やかに育(そだ)つ: to grow up healthy
  • 健(すこ)やかな成長(せいちょう): healthy growth
  • 健(すこ)やかな毎日(まいにち): healthy daily life

USAGE:

This is a somewhat formal or literary word often used:

  • In well-wishes for children's health and growth
  • In formal greetings, blessings, and ceremonial contexts
  • On cards for births, coming-of-age, and other celebrations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 元気(げんき): energetic, healthy (more casual)
  • 健康(けんこう): healthy (more neutral/common)
