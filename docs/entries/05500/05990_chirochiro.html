ちろちろ

ちろちろ
adverb
flickering; glancing sideways

Definitions

1. flickering; wavering
Describes small flames or lights flickering gently, with a soft, delicate movement.
2. glancing; stealing looks
Describes the action of looking at someone or something with quick, furtive glances.

Examples

ろうそくの炎(ほのお)がちろちろと揺(ゆ)れている。
The candle flame is flickering gently.
彼女(かのじょ)はちろちろとこちらを見(み)ていた。
She kept stealing glances at me.
焚(た)き火(び)がちろちろ燃(も)えている。
The bonfire is burning with a flickering flame.

Notes

ちろちろ is a gentle onomatopoeia, often associated with small flames or shy/furtive glances.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ちろちろ燃(も)える: to burn flickeringly
  • ちろちろ見(み)る: to steal glances

CONTRAST:

  • めらめら: burning intensely
  • ゆらゆら: swaying gently (larger movements)
  • じろじろ: staring rudely (more direct, impolite)
