淡々(たんたん)

たんたん
adverb, taru-adjective, na-adjective
calmly; dispassionately; matter-of-factly
1. calmly; dispassionately; matter-of-factly
Describes doing or saying something without showing emotion, in a detached or composed manner.
彼(かれ)は淡々(たんたん)と事実(じじつ)を述(の)べた。
He stated the facts matter-of-factly.
淡々(たんたん)と仕事(しごと)をこなす。
To handle work calmly and steadily.
淡々(たんたん)とした口調(くちょう)で話(はな)した。
She spoke in a dispassionate tone.

淡々(たんたん) conveys emotional detachment and composure, which can be positive (professional calm) or neutral.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 淡々(たんたん)と話(はな)す: to speak dispassionately
  • 淡々(たんたん)とした態度(たいど): matter-of-fact attitude
  • 淡々(たんたん)と日々(ひび)を過(す)ごす: to live each day calmly

GRAMMAR NOTE:
Can be used as たる-adjective (淡々(たんたん)たる) in formal contexts.
