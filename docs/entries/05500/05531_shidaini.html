次第(しだい)に

しだいに
adverb
gradually; little by little
1. gradually; little by little; step by step
Happening slowly over time; progressing in small increments.
次第(しだい)に慣(な)れてきた。
I gradually got used to it.
空(そら)が次第(しだい)に暗(くら)くなった。
The sky gradually darkened.
次第(しだい)に状況(じょうきょう)が改善(かいぜん)した。
The situation gradually improved.

次第(しだい)に means "gradually" or "progressively," describing change that occurs slowly over time.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • だんだん: gradually (casual, everyday)
  • 徐々(じょじょ)に: gradually (formal, written)
  • 少(すこ)しずつ: little by little (emphasis on small increments)
  • 段階的(だんかいてき)に: step by step, in stages

NUANCE DIFFERENCES:

  • 次第(しだい)に: neutral to formal; emphasizes continuous progression
  • だんだん: casual; most common in spoken Japanese
  • 徐々(じょじょ)に: formal/literary; often in written contexts

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 次第(しだい)に慣(な)れる: to gradually get used to
  • 次第(しだい)に増(ふ)える: to gradually increase
  • 次第(しだい)に変(か)わる: to gradually change
  • 次第(しだい)に分(わ)かってくる: to gradually come to understand

REGISTER:
More formal than だんだん, appropriate for both spoken and written Japanese.
