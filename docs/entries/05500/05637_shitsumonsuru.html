1. to ask a question, to inquire
To pose a question to someone in order to obtain information or clarification.
先生(せんせい)に質問(しつもん)してもいいですか。
May I ask the teacher a question?
何(なに)か質問(しつもん)はありますか。
Are there any questions?
Common phrase used after presentations or lectures
彼(かれ)は記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で鋭(するど)い質問(しつもん)をした。
He asked a sharp question at the press conference.

質問(しつもん)する means to ask a question or inquire. The noun 質問(しつもん) combines with する to form the verb.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 質問(しつもん)に答(こた)える: to answer a question
  • 質問(しつもん)を受(う)ける: to receive questions
  • 質問(しつもん)を投(な)げかける: to pose a question
  • 質問(しつもん)がある: to have a question
  • 何(なに)か質問(しつもん)はありますか: any questions?

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 聞(き)く: to ask (casual, everyday)
  • 尋(たず)ねる: to inquire (slightly formal)
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to make an inquiry (business/customer service)

NOTE:
質問(しつもん)する is neutral in register and works in both casual and formal contexts.
