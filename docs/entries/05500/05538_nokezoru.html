仰(の)け反(ぞ)る

のけぞる
verb (godan)
to lean back; to bend backward

Definitions

1. to lean back; to bend backward
To tilt one's body backward, often in surprise, to avoid something, or to look up.

Examples

驚(おどろ)いて思(おも)わず仰(の)け反(ぞ)った。
I involuntarily leaned back in surprise.
椅子(いす)に座(すわ)って大(おお)きく仰(の)け反(ぞ)る。
To lean far back in a chair.
彼(かれ)のパンチを仰(の)け反(ぞ)って避(よ)けた。
I dodged his punch by leaning back.

Notes

仰(の)け反(ぞ)る describes the action of bending or leaning backward. It often implies a sudden or reactive movement.

CONJUGATION (godan verb):

  • 仰(の)け反(ぞ)る (dictionary form)
  • 仰(の)け反(ぞ)らない (negative)
  • 仰(の)け反(ぞ)った (past)
  • 仰(の)け反(ぞ)って (te-form)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used to describe reactions to surprise, shock, or physical impact.
