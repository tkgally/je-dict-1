ぎゅうぎゅう

ぎゅうぎゅう
adverb, na-adjective, onomatopoeia
packed; crammed; squeezed tightly
1. packed; crammed
Describes being tightly packed or crammed together with little or no space.
2. squeezed tightly
Describes the action of squeezing or pressing something firmly.
満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)でぎゅうぎゅう詰(づ)めだった。
It was packed tight on the crowded train.
荷物(にもつ)をぎゅうぎゅう押(お)し込(こ)んだ。
I crammed the luggage in tightly.
スーツケースがぎゅうぎゅうで閉(し)まらない。
The suitcase is so packed it won't close.

USAGE:
ぎゅうぎゅう describes tight compression or packing. It conveys a sense of discomfort or strain from being squeezed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぎゅうぎゅう詰(づ)め: packed tight, crammed
  • ぎゅうぎゅうに押(お)し込(こ)む: to cram in tightly
  • ぎゅうぎゅうに詰(つ)まる: to be tightly packed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぎゅっと: (single action) tightly, firmly
  • ぱんぱん: stretched full, about to burst
  • 満杯(まんぱい): completely full
