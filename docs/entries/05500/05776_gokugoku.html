ごくごく

ごくごく
adverb
gulping, drinking in big gulps
1. gulping, drinking in big gulps
The sound or manner of drinking thirstily in large swallows.
水(みず)をごくごく飲(の)んだ。
I gulped down water.
ごくごく音(おと)を立(た)てて飲(の)む。
To drink making gulping sounds.
喉(のど)が渇(かわ)いてごくごく飲(の)んだ。
I was thirsty and gulped it down.

ごくごく is an onomatopoeia representing the sound and action of gulping liquid thirstily.

USAGE:
Typically used with 飲(の)む (to drink) to express drinking in large swallows.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ごくごく飲(の)む: to gulp down
  • ごくごくと飲(の)む: to drink gulp by gulp
  • ごくごく音(おと)を立(た)てる: to make gulping sounds

CONTRAST:

  • ちびちび: sipping little by little (opposite)
  • ごくんと: with one gulp (single swallow)

RELATED ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • がぶがぶ: also gulping, but emphasizes greediness

Related Words

Antonym: ちびちび (sipping little by little)
