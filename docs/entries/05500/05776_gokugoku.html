ごくごく

ごくごく
adverb
gulping, drinking in big gulps
1. gulping, drinking in big gulps
The sound or manner of drinking thirstily in large swallows.
水(みず)をごくごく飲(の)んだ。
I gulped down water.
ごくごく音(おと)を立(た)てて飲(の)む。
To drink making gulping sounds.
喉(のど)が渇(かわ)いてごくごく飲(の)んだ。
I was thirsty and gulped it down.

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Represents the sound and action of drinking thirstily.

CONTRAST:

  • ちびちび: sipping little by little (opposite manner)
general
