みっしり

みっしり
adverb
tightly packed; densely
1. tightly packed; densely; without gaps
Describes things packed so tightly together that there is no space between them. Emphasizes complete fullness.
本(ほん)が本棚(ほんだな)にみっしり詰(つ)まっている。
Books are tightly packed on the bookshelf.
スケジュールがみっしり埋(う)まっている。
My schedule is completely packed.
箱(はこ)の中(なか)にみっしりと荷物(にもつ)が入(はい)っている。
The box is tightly filled with luggage.

みっしり is an onomatopoeic adverb that emphasizes complete density with no wasted space or gaps. It conveys a sense of things being crammed together so thoroughly that nothing more could fit.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • みっしり詰(つ)まる: to be packed tightly
  • みっしり埋(う)まる: to be completely filled
  • みっしりと入(はい)っている: to be tightly packed inside
  • みっしり並(なら)ぶ: to be lined up densely

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぎっしり: tightly packed (more common, nearly interchangeable)
  • びっしり: densely covered/written (emphasizes surface coverage)
  • ぎゅうぎゅう: crammed, squeezed (often for people in tight spaces)
  • すかすか: sparse, with gaps (opposite meaning)

みっしり often takes the particle と (みっしりと) in more formal or written contexts. In casual speech, と is frequently dropped.
