みっしり

みっしり
adverb
tightly packed; densely
1. tightly packed; densely; without gaps
Describes things packed so tightly together that there is no space between them. Emphasizes complete fullness.
本(ほん)が本棚(ほんだな)にみっしり詰(つ)まっている。
Books are tightly packed on the bookshelf.
スケジュールがみっしり埋(う)まっている。
My schedule is completely packed.
箱(はこ)の中(なか)にみっしりと荷物(にもつ)が入(はい)っている。
The box is tightly filled with luggage.

みっしり emphasizes complete density with no wasted space.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • みっしり詰(つ)まる: to be packed tightly
  • みっしり埋(う)まる: to be completely filled

CONTRAST:

  • ぎっしり: also tightly packed (similar, slightly more common)
  • びっしり: densely covered/written
  • すかすか: sparse, with gaps (opposite)
