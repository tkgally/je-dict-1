優劣(ゆうれつ)

ゆうれつ
noun
superiority and inferiority; relative merit
1. superiority and inferiority; relative merit
The comparison between better and worse; differences in quality or ability.
優劣(ゆうれつ)をつけがたい。
It's hard to decide which is better.
成績(せいせき)の優劣(ゆうれつ)で判断(はんだん)する。
To judge based on differences in performance.
両者(りょうしゃ)の優劣(ゆうれつ)を比較(ひかく)する。
To compare the relative merits of both.

優劣(ゆうれつ) combines 優(ゆう) (superior) and 劣(れつ) (inferior) to express relative merit or the difference between better and worse.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 優(ゆう): superior, excellent
  • 劣(れつ): inferior, lesser

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 優劣(ゆうれつ)をつける: to rank, determine which is better
  • 優劣(ゆうれつ)をつけがたい: hard to determine which is superior
  • 優劣(ゆうれつ)を決(き)める: to decide the ranking
  • 優劣(ゆうれつ)を比較(ひかく)する: to compare relative merits

USAGE:
Used in formal comparisons of quality, performance, or ability. Common in academic, business, and competitive contexts.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 勝敗(しょうはい): victory and defeat
  • 長短(ちょうたん): pros and cons
