骨盤(こつばん)

こつばん
noun
pelvis

Definitions

1. pelvis
The bony structure at the base of the spine that supports the torso and connects to the legs. Important for posture, walking, and in women, childbirth.

Examples

骨盤(こつばん)の歪(ゆが)みを矯正(きょうせい)した。
I had my pelvic misalignment corrected.
産後(さんご)は骨盤(こつばん)が開(ひら)いている。
After childbirth, the pelvis is widened.
骨盤(こつばん)ベルトで腰痛(ようつう)を予防(よぼう)する。
I use a pelvic belt to prevent lower back pain.

Notes

ANATOMY:

The 骨盤(こつばん) consists of:

  • 腸骨(ちょうこつ): ilium
  • 恥骨(ちこつ): pubis
  • 坐骨(ざこつ): ischium
  • 仙骨(せんこつ): sacrum

COMMON TERMS:

  • 骨盤矯正(こつばんきょうせい): pelvic adjustment
  • 骨盤底筋(こつばんていきん): pelvic floor muscles
  • {骨盤ベルト}: pelvic belt/support

HEALTH CONTEXT:

  • Often discussed in relation to posture and back pain
  • Important in prenatal and postnatal care
  • Popular topic in Japanese health/beauty media

GENDER DIFFERENCES:
Women's 骨盤(こつばん) is typically wider and shallower than men's to accommodate childbirth.
