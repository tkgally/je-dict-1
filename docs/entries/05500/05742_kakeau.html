1. to negotiate; to bargain
To discuss terms or make a request to someone with authority; to go back and forth in discussion.
値段(ねだん)を掛(か)け合(あ)う。
To negotiate the price.
上司(じょうし)に掛(か)け合(あ)って休暇(きゅうか)をもらった。
I negotiated with my boss and got vacation time.
市役所(しやくしょ)に掛(か)け合(あ)って、許可(きょか)をもらうことにした。
I decided to negotiate with city hall to get permission.
2. to call to each other; to exchange words
For two or more people to speak to each other, often taking turns in dialogue.
声(こえ)を掛(か)け合(あ)う。
To call out to each other.
役者(やくしゃ)たちが台詞(せりふ)を掛(か)け合(あ)う。
The actors exchange dialogue.
試合中(しあいちゅう)、選手(せんしゅ)たちは互(たが)いに声(こえ)を掛(か)け合(あ)っていた。
During the game, the players were calling out to each other.

A compound verb from 掛(か)ける (to engage) + 合(あ)う (to do together/mutually). Has two distinct senses: negotiation and reciprocal speech.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Uses に for the person negotiated with: 上司(じょうし)に掛(か)け合(あ)う

ASPECT (ている):
掛(か)け合(あ)っている means "is in the process of negotiating" or "are exchanging words" (ongoing action).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に掛(か)け合(あ)う (to negotiate with ~)
  • 声(こえ)を掛(か)け合(あ)う (to call out to each other)
  • 台詞(せりふ)を掛(か)け合(あ)う (to exchange lines — theater)
  • 掛(か)け合(あ)って許可(きょか)をもらう (to negotiate and get permission)

RELATED:

  • 掛(か)け合(あ)い: the noun form — negotiation; dialogue; comic back-and-forth
  • 掛(か)け合(あ)い漫才(まんざい): manzai comedy dialogue
  • 交渉(こうしょう): negotiation (more formal/official)

Related Words
