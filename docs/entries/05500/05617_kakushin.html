革新(かくしん)

かくしん
noun
innovation; reform

Definitions

1. innovation; reform; renewal
Making significant changes or improvements; introducing new ideas or methods.

Examples

技術(ぎじゅつ)革新(かくしん)が進(すす)んでいる。
Technological innovation is advancing.
革新的(かくしんてき)なアイデアを生(う)み出(だ)す。
To come up with innovative ideas.
制度(せいど)の革新(かくしん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Reform of the system is necessary.

Notes

革新(かくしん) implies significant change or modernization, often with positive connotations.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)革新(かくしん): technological innovation
  • 革新的(かくしんてき): innovative
  • 革新(かくしん)政党(せいとう): progressive party
