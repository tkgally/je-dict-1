生(い)き返(かえ)る

いきかえる
verb (godan)
to revive, to come back to life
1. to revive, to come back to life
To return to life or consciousness after appearing to be dead or exhausted.
2. to be refreshed, to feel revitalized
To regain energy and feel rejuvenated.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)を飲(の)んで生(い)き返(かえ)った。
I felt revived after drinking cold water.
昔(むかし)の伝統(でんとう)が生(い)き返(かえ)った。
Old traditions came back to life.
シャワーを浴(あ)びて生(い)き返(かえ)った気分(きぶん)だ。
I feel refreshed after taking a shower.

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 生(い)きる (to live) + 返(かえ)る (to return). Literally "return to living."

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive verb.
