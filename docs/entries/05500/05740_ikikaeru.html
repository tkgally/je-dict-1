1. to revive, to come back to life
To return to life or consciousness after appearing to be dead or exhausted.
花(はな)が生(い)き返(かえ)った。
The flower came back to life.
昔(むかし)の伝統(でんとう)が生(い)き返(かえ)った。
Old traditions came back to life.
水(みず)をやったら、枯(か)れかけた木(き)が生(い)き返(かえ)った。
The tree that was nearly dead came back to life after I watered it.
この町(まち)は再開発(さいかいはつ)で生(い)き返(かえ)った。
This town was revived through redevelopment.
長(なが)い間(あいだ)忘(わす)れられていた文化(ぶんか)が、若(わか)い人(ひと)たちの力(ちから)で生(い)き返(かえ)った。
A culture long forgotten was revived through the efforts of young people.
2. to be refreshed, to feel revitalized
To regain energy and feel rejuvenated.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)で生(い)き返(かえ)る。
To feel revived with cold water.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)を飲(の)んで生(い)き返(かえ)った。
I felt revived after drinking cold water.
シャワーを浴(あ)びて生(い)き返(かえ)った気分(きぶん)だ。
I feel refreshed after taking a shower.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)に冷(つめ)たいビールを飲(の)むと、生(い)き返(かえ)る気(き)がする。
Drinking a cold beer on a hot day makes me feel revived.
一日中(いちにちじゅう)歩(ある)いて疲(つか)れていたが、温泉(おんせん)に入(はい)ったら生(い)き返(かえ)った。
I was tired from walking all day, but I felt revived after getting into the hot spring.

生(い)き返(かえ)る means to revive or feel refreshed, literally "return to living."

WORD FORMATION:
生(い)きる (to live) + 返(かえ)る (to return)

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Transitive pair: 生(い)き返(かえ)らせる (to revive something)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 冷(つめ)たい水(みず)で生(い)き返(かえ)る: to feel revived by cold water
  • 生(い)き返(かえ)った気分(きぶん): feeling refreshed
  • 伝統(でんとう)が生(い)き返(かえ)る: traditions come back to life

SIMILAR:

  • 蘇(よみがえ)る: to be revived, resurrected (more literary)
