却(かえ)って

かえって
adverb
on the contrary; rather; all the more
1. on the contrary; rather; all the more
Used when the result is opposite of what was expected, or when something intensifies rather than lessens.
説明(せつめい)したら却(かえ)って混乱(こんらん)した。
When I explained, it only made things more confusing.
休(やす)んだのに却(かえ)って疲(つか)れた。
Even though I rested, I ended up more tired.
シンプルな方(ほう)が却(かえ)って良(よ)い。
The simpler option is actually better.

USAGE PATTERN:
却(かえ)って is used when:
1. An action produces the opposite of its intended effect
2. Something unexpected happens that goes against common sense
3. A simpler or different approach turns out better

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜したら却(かえ)って〜: when I did ~, it actually ~
  • 〜の方(ほう)が却(かえ)って〜: actually, ~ is more ~
  • 却(かえ)って悪(わる)くなる: to become worse instead

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • むしろ: rather (neutral comparison)
  • 逆(ぎゃく)に: on the contrary
