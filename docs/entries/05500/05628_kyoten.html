拠点(きょてん)

きょてん
noun
base; hub; foothold
1. base; hub; foothold; stronghold
A main location from which operations or activities are conducted; a strategic point.
海外(かいがい)拠点(きょてん)を増(ふ)やす。
To increase overseas bases.
この都市(とし)は物流(ぶつりゅう)の拠点(きょてん)だ。
This city is a logistics hub.
研究(けんきゅう)拠点(きょてん)を設立(せつりつ)する。
To establish a research base.

拠点(きょてん) means a base, hub, or strategic location from which activities are conducted.

COMPONENTS:

  • 拠(きょ): to depend on, base
  • 点(てん): point, location

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 活動(かつどう)拠点(きょてん): activity base, center of operations
  • 生産(せいさん)拠点(きょてん): production base
  • 研究(けんきゅう)拠点(きょてん): research center
  • 海外(かいがい)拠点(きょてん): overseas base
  • 物流(ぶつりゅう)拠点(きょてん): logistics hub

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 拠点(きょてん)を置(お)く: to establish a base
  • 拠点(きょてん)を構(かま)える: to set up headquarters
  • 拠点(きょてん)として: as a base

USAGE:
Commonly used in business, military, and organizational contexts. Implies a strategic importance beyond just "location" (場所(ばしょ)).

Related Words

Synonym: 本拠地(ほんきょち) (stronghold, home base)
Related: 本部(ほんぶ) (headquarters)
