天秤(てんびん)

てんびん
noun
balance scale; Libra
1. balance scale; balance
A weighing device with two pans suspended from a horizontal beam.
2. Libra (zodiac sign)
The seventh sign of the zodiac, represented by scales.
天秤(てんびん)で重(おも)さを量(はか)る。
To weigh something with a balance scale.
両方(りょうほう)を天秤(てんびん)にかける。
To weigh both options (figuratively).
私(わたし)は天秤(てんびん)座(ざ)です。
I'm a Libra.

天秤(てんびん) is used both for the weighing device and the zodiac sign.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 天秤(てんびん)にかける: to weigh (options), to compare
  • 天秤(てんびん)座(ざ): Libra (constellation/zodiac)

SCIENCE:
Used in chemistry and physics for precise measurements.
