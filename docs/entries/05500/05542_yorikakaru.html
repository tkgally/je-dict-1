1. to lean against; to rest against
To support one's body by leaning against something.
壁(かべ)に寄(よ)り掛(か)かって休(やす)む。
To rest by leaning against the wall.
彼女(かのじょ)は彼(かれ)の肩(かた)に寄(よ)り掛(か)かった。
She leaned against his shoulder.
疲(つか)れて電車(でんしゃ)のドアに寄(よ)り掛(か)かっていた。
I was tired and leaning against the train door.
2. to depend on; to rely on
To depend on someone for support or assistance.
親(おや)に寄(よ)り掛(か)かってばかりいてはいけない。
You shouldn't just depend on your parents all the time.
他人(たにん)に寄(よ)り掛(か)からず、自分(じぶん)の力(ちから)でやってみよう。
Without relying on others, let's try doing it ourselves.
困(こま)った時(とき)に寄(よ)り掛(か)かれる人(ひと)がいるのは心強(こころづよ)い。
It's reassuring to have someone you can rely on when in trouble.

寄(よ)り掛(か)かる has both physical (leaning against) and figurative (depending on) meanings.
