1. to bend over; to stoop; to crouch
To bend one's body forward or downward, typically at the waist.
腰(こし)を屈(かが)めて靴(くつ)を履(は)く。
To bend over and put on shoes.
低(ひく)い入口(いりぐち)で身(み)を屈(かが)めた。
I stooped at the low entrance.
背中(せなか)を屈(かが)めて歩(ある)く老人(ろうじん)。
An elderly person walking with a stooped back.

屈(かが)む describes bending the upper body forward or downward — stooping, crouching, or hunching.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 屈(かが)める (transitive: to bend something)

ASPECT (ている):
屈(かが)んでいる means being in a stooped or crouched position.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 腰(こし)を屈(かが)める: to bend at the waist (uses transitive form)
  • 身(み)を屈(かが)める: to stoop, to duck
  • 前(まえ)屈(かが)み: bending forward (noun)
  • 背中(せなか)が屈(かが)んでいる: having a hunched back

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • しゃがむ: to squat, to crouch down (legs bend, body lowers)
  • 屈(かが)む: to stoop, to bend (upper body bends forward)

Related Words

Synonym: しゃがむ (to squat/crouch)
Related: 跪(ひざまず)く (to kneel)
