ころころ

ころころ
adverb, suru verb
rolling; frequently changing

Definitions

1. rolling; tumbling
Describes something small and round rolling along. Often used for balls, marbles, or small round objects.
2. frequently changing; fickle
Describes something that changes often, such as opinions, moods, or weather. Often carries a slightly negative connotation of inconsistency.

Examples

ビー玉(だま)がころころ転(ころ)がった。
The marble rolled along.
猫(ねこ)がころころ丸(まる)まって寝(ね)ている。
The cat is curled up in a ball sleeping.
彼(かれ)は意見(いけん)がころころ変(か)わる。
His opinions change constantly.

Notes

ころころ can describe physical rolling motion or figurative "rolling" of opinions/situations. The repetition suggests continuous or repeated action.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • ごろごろ: rolling (larger objects); lounging around; rumbling (thunder)
  • くるくる: spinning/rotating
  • ぐるぐる: going around in circles

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ころころ転(ころ)がる: to roll along
  • ころころ変(か)わる: to change frequently
  • ころころした: round and cute

SIZE DISTINCTION:

  • ころころ: small round things rolling
  • ごろごろ: larger things rolling

Related Words
