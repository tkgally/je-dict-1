万全(ばんぜん)

ばんぜん
noun, na-adjective
perfect; thorough; flawless

Definitions

1. perfect; complete; thorough
A state of being completely prepared with nothing lacking. Often used for preparations, measures, or conditions.

Examples

万全(ばんぜん)の準備(じゅんび)を整(ととの)えた。
We made thorough preparations.
万全(ばんぜん)の態勢(たいせい)で臨(のぞ)む。
To face something with complete readiness.
万全(ばんぜん)を期(き)す。
To ensure everything is perfect.

Notes

万全(ばんぜん) literally means "ten thousand complete" - completely perfect.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 万全(ばんぜん)の準備(じゅんび): thorough preparation
  • 万全(ばんぜん)の態勢(たいせい)/体制(たいせい): complete readiness
  • 万全(ばんぜん)を期(き)す: to make sure everything is perfect
  • 万全(ばんぜん)ではない: not perfect, not complete

CONTEXT:
Often used in formal or business settings when discussing preparations, security measures, or conditions.
