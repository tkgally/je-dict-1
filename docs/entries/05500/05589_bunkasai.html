文化祭(ぶんかさい)

ぶんかさい
noun
cultural festival; school festival
1. cultural festival; school festival
An annual festival at schools where students showcase performances, exhibits, and food stalls.
文化祭(ぶんかさい)で劇(げき)を発表(はっぴょう)する。
To present a play at the school festival.
文化祭(ぶんかさい)の準備(じゅんび)で忙(いそが)しい。
I'm busy preparing for the school festival.
文化祭(ぶんかさい)で模擬(もぎ)店(てん)を出(だ)す。
To run a booth at the school festival.

文化祭(ぶんかさい) is an important event in Japanese schools, typically held in autumn.

COMMON ACTIVITIES:

  • 模擬(もぎ)店(てん): food/game stalls run by students
  • 演劇(えんげき): theatrical performances
  • 展示(てんじ): exhibits and displays
  • バンド演奏(えんそう): band performances

TIMING:
Usually held in October or November.
