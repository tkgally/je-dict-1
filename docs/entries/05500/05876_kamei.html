加盟(かめい)

かめい
noun, suru verb
joining; affiliation; membership

Definitions

1. joining; affiliation; membership
The act of becoming a member of an organization, alliance, or agreement.

Examples

新(あたら)しい国(くに)がEUに加盟(かめい)した。
A new country joined the EU.
加盟(かめい)店(てん)で使(つか)えるクーポンだ。
It's a coupon usable at member stores.
組合(くみあい)に加盟(かめい)する。
To join a union.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 加盟(かめい)国(こく): member nation
  • 加盟(かめい)店(てん): member store, affiliated store
  • 加盟(かめい)する: to join, affiliate
  • 加盟(かめい)企業(きぎょう): member company
  • 加盟(かめい)を申(もう)し込(こ)む: to apply for membership

CONTRAST:

  • 脱退(だったい): withdrawal
  • 離脱(りだつ): withdrawal, secession

RELATED TERMS:

  • 参加(さんか): participation
  • 入会(にゅうかい): joining (an organization)

Related Words

Antonym: 脱退(だったい) (withdrawal)
