ぺちゃぺちゃ

ぺちゃぺちゃ
adverb, suru verb
chattering; smacking lips
1. chattering; talking noisily
Describes continuous, trivial, or annoying chatter. Often implies gossiping or talking too much.
2. smacking lips; eating noisily
Describes the sound of eating messily or smacking one's lips while eating.
授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)にぺちゃぺちゃしゃべらないで。
Don't chatter during class.
彼女(かのじょ)たちはぺちゃぺちゃと噂話(うわさばなし)をしていた。
They were chattering away, gossiping.
ぺちゃぺちゃ音(おと)を立(た)てて食(た)べるのは行儀(ぎょうぎ)が悪(わる)い。
Eating with smacking sounds is bad manners.

ぺちゃぺちゃ generally carries a negative nuance, implying that the chattering or eating sounds are annoying or impolite.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぺちゃぺちゃしゃべる: to chatter noisily
  • ぺちゃぺちゃ食(た)べる: to eat with smacking sounds

CONTRAST:

  • べらべら: talking too much (spilling secrets)
  • くちゃくちゃ: chewing noisily
  • ぱくぱく: eating eagerly (more neutral/positive)
