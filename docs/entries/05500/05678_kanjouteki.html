感情的(かんじょうてき)

かんじょうてき
na-adjective
emotional
1. emotional, driven by feelings
Acting based on emotions rather than reason, or relating to emotions.
感情的(かんじょうてき)にならないでください。
Please don't get emotional.
彼(かれ)は感情的(かんじょうてき)な反応(はんのう)をした。
He had an emotional reaction.
感情的(かんじょうてき)な判断(はんだん)は避(さ)けるべきだ。
You should avoid emotional judgments.

WORD FORMATION:
Formed from 感情(かんじょう) (emotion) + 的 (suffix making na-adjectives).

USAGE:
Often carries a slightly negative connotation, implying that someone is being irrational or letting emotions override reason.

CONTRAST:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical (often contrasted with 感情的)

Related Words

Contrast: 論理的(ろんりてき) (logical)
