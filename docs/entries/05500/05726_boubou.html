ぼうぼう

ぼうぼう
adverb, na-adjective, onomatopoeia
overgrown; unkempt; blazing

Definitions

1. overgrown; unkempt
Describes hair, grass, or vegetation that has grown wild and uncontrolled.
2. blazing; burning fiercely
The sound or manner of flames burning intensely.

Examples

庭(にわ)の草(くさ)がぼうぼうに生(は)えている。
The grass in the garden has grown wild.
髭(ひげ)がぼうぼうだ。
His beard is unkempt.
火(ひ)がぼうぼう燃(も)えている。
The fire is blazing fiercely.

Notes

DUAL USAGE:
1. GROWTH: Describes untidy, overgrown vegetation or hair
2. FIRE: Describes fierce, roaring flames

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 草(くさ)がぼうぼう: grass has grown wild
  • 髭(ひげ)がぼうぼう: unkempt beard
  • 髪(かみ)がぼうぼう: disheveled hair
  • 火(ひ)がぼうぼう: blazing fire

RELATED WORDS:

  • ぼさぼさ: messy, disheveled (hair)
  • めらめら: flames flickering
  • ごうごう: roaring (fire, water)
