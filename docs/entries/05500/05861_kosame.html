小雨(こさめ)

こさめ
noun
light rain, drizzle

Definitions

1. light rain, drizzle
Light, gentle rainfall; not heavy enough to need shelter.

Examples

小雨(こさめ)が降(ふ)っている。
It's drizzling.
小雨(こさめ)なら傘(かさ)はいらない。
If it's just a light rain, I don't need an umbrella.
小雨(こさめ)程度(ていど)なら試合(しあい)は行(おこな)われる。
The game will be held if it's only light rain.

Notes

CONTRAST:

  • 豪雨(ごうう): heavy rain (opposite intensity)
  • 霧雨(きりさめ): misty rain, fine drizzle

REGISTER:
小雨(こさめ) is the standard term. 霧雨(きりさめ) refers to even finer, mist-like rain.
