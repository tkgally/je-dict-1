顕著(けんちょ)

けんちょ
na-adjective
remarkable; conspicuous; notable
1. remarkable; conspicuous; notable; striking
Standing out clearly; easily noticeable or prominent.
顕著(けんちょ)な成果(せいか)を上(あ)げた。
Achieved remarkable results.
効果(こうか)が顕著(けんちょ)に現(あらわ)れた。
The effect became conspicuously apparent.
人口(じんこう)減少(げんしょう)が顕著(けんちょ)だ。
The population decline is notable.

顕著(けんちょ) is a na-adjective meaning "remarkable" or "conspicuous," describing something that stands out clearly and is easily noticeable.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 顕著(けんちょ)な + noun
  • Adverbial: 顕著(けんちょ)に

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顕著(けんちょ)な傾向(けいこう): notable trend
  • 顕著(けんちょ)な成果(せいか): remarkable results
  • 顕著(けんちょ)な変化(へんか): conspicuous change
  • 顕著(けんちょ)な効果(こうか): notable effect

REGISTER: This is a formal, written-style word commonly used in academic, scientific, and business contexts to describe data, trends, or outcomes. Less common in casual conversation.
