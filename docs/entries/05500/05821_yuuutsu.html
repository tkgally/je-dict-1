憂鬱(ゆううつ)

ゆううつ
na-adjective, noun
depressed, melancholy

Definitions

1. depressed, melancholy, gloomy
A state of feeling sad, pessimistic, or emotionally down.

Examples

憂鬱(ゆううつ)な気分(きぶん)だ。
I'm feeling depressed.
月曜日(げつようび)は憂鬱(ゆううつ)だ。
Mondays are depressing.
憂鬱(ゆううつ)になる。
To get depressed.

Notes

The kanji 鬱 is one of the most complex characters in common use (29 strokes). Often written in hiragana (ゆううつ) or with 欝 as a simplified alternative.

CONTRAST:

  • うつ病: clinical depression (medical term)
