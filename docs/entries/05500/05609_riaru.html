リアル

りある
na-adjective
real; realistic; relatable

Definitions

1. real; realistic
Lifelike, authentic, or based on reality.
2. relatable (slang)
Something that resonates or feels true to one's experience.
3. in real life; offline
Referring to the physical world as opposed to online.

Examples

このゲームのグラフィックはリアルだ。
The graphics in this game are realistic.
その話(はなし)、リアルだね。
That story is so relatable.
リアルで会(あ)おう。
Let's meet in real life.

Notes

リアル has multiple meanings in modern Japanese, from "realistic" to the slang "relatable."

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • リアル友達(ともだち): real-life friend (vs. online friend)
  • リアルタイム: real-time
