必然(ひつぜん)

ひつぜん
noun
inevitability; necessity
1. inevitability; necessity; certainty
Something that must happen and cannot be avoided.
必然(ひつぜん)の結果(けっか)だ。
It's an inevitable result.
必然的(ひつぜんてき)に起(お)こる。
It happens inevitably.
偶然(ぐうぜん)か必然(ひつぜん)か。
Is it coincidence or inevitability?

必然(ひつぜん) means inevitability.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 必然的(ひつぜんてき)(な): inevitable
  • 必然(ひつぜん)性(せい): inevitability

CONTRAST:

  • 偶然(ぐうぜん): chance, coincidence
general
