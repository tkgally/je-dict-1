切(き)り離(はな)す

きりはなす
verb (godan)
to cut off, to separate

Definitions

1. to cut off, to sever
To physically separate something by cutting.
2. to separate, to detach
To separate things or concepts that were previously connected.

Examples

紐(ひも)を切(き)り離(はな)す。
To cut off the string.
仕事(しごと)と私生活(しせいかつ)を切(き)り離(はな)す。
To separate work and private life.
この問題(もんだい)は切(き)り離(はな)して考(かんが)えよう。
Let's consider this issue separately.

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 切(き)る (to cut) + 離(はな)す (to separate). Transitive verb.
