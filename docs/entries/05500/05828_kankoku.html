勧告(かんこく)

かんこく
noun
recommendation; advice; counsel
1. recommendation; advice; counsel
Formal advice or suggestion, often from an authority or official body urging a certain course of action.
政府(せいふ)が避難(ひなん)勧告(かんこく)を出(だ)した。
The government issued an evacuation advisory.
勧告(かんこく)を無視(むし)する。
To ignore the recommendation.
辞職(じしょく)勧告(かんこく)を受(う)けた。
I received advice to resign.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勧告(かんこく)を出(だ)す: to issue a recommendation
  • 避難(ひなん)勧告(かんこく): evacuation advisory
  • 辞職(じしょく)勧告(かんこく): advice to resign
  • 勧告(かんこく)に従(したが)う: to follow advice

RELATED TERMS:

  • 忠告(ちゅうこく): advice, warning
  • 助言(じょげん): advice
  • 提言(ていげん): proposal, suggestion
