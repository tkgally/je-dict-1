婚活(こんかつ)

こんかつ
noun, verb (suru)
marriage hunting
1. marriage hunting, spouse searching
Active efforts to find a marriage partner, including attending matchmaking events and using dating services.
婚活(こんかつ)パーティーに参加(さんか)する。
To attend a matchmaking party.
婚活(こんかつ)中(ちゅう)です。
I'm looking for a marriage partner.
婚活(こんかつ)アプリを使(つか)う。
To use a matchmaking app.

ABBREVIATION:
Short for 結婚活動 (kekkon katsudou). Coined in 2007, modeled after 就活.

CULTURAL:

Reflects modern Japanese society where traditional matchmaking (お見合い) has declined. Common activities include:

  • 婚活パーティー: matchmaking parties
  • 婚活アプリ: dating apps
  • 結婚相談所: marriage agencies
