1. marriage hunting, spouse searching
Active efforts to find a marriage partner, including attending matchmaking events and using dating services.
婚活(こんかつ)パーティーに参加(さんか)する。
To attend a matchmaking party.
婚活(こんかつ)中(ちゅう)です。
I'm looking for a marriage partner.
婚活(こんかつ)アプリを使(つか)う。
To use a matchmaking app.

婚活(こんかつ) refers to active efforts to find a marriage partner. Short for 結婚(けっこん)活動(かつどう).

WORD FORMATION:
Abbreviated from 結婚(けっこん) (marriage) + 活動(かつどう) (activities). Coined around 2007, modeled after 就活(しゅうかつ) (就職(しゅうしょく)活動(かつどう), job hunting).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 婚活(こんかつ)パーティー: matchmaking party
  • 婚活(こんかつ)アプリ: dating/matchmaking app
  • 婚活(こんかつ)中(ちゅう): currently looking for a partner
  • 婚活(こんかつ)疲(つか)れ: marriage-hunting fatigue

RELATED 〜活(かつ) WORDS:

  • 就活(しゅうかつ): job hunting
  • 朝活(あさかつ): early-morning activities
  • 終活(しゅうかつ): end-of-life preparation
  • 妊活(にんかつ): trying to conceive

CULTURAL NOTE:
As traditional matchmaking (お見合(みあ)い) has declined, 婚活(こんかつ) fills the gap with modern methods: dating apps, organized parties, and 結婚(けっこん)相談所(そうだんじょ) (marriage agencies).
