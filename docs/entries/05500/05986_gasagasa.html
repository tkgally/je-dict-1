1. rustling sound
The sound of dry, rough objects rubbing together or moving through vegetation. Often suggests careless or clumsy movement.
茂(しげ)みの中(なか)でがさがさと音(おと)がした。
There was a rustling sound in the bushes.
紙袋(かみぶくろ)をがさがさと開(あ)けた。
I rustled open a paper bag.
何(なに)かが落(お)ち葉(ば)の上(うえ)をがさがさと歩(ある)いている音(おと)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard the sound of something walking noisily over fallen leaves.
2. rough; dry; coarse
Describes rough, dry texture, especially of skin, hair, or other surfaces that should be smooth but have become dried out.
冬(ふゆ)になると肌(はだ)ががさがさになる。
My skin gets rough and dry in winter.
ハンドクリームを塗(ぬ)らないと手(て)ががさがさだ。
My hands are rough if I don't apply hand cream.
唇(くちびる)ががさがさに荒(あ)れて、リップクリームが手放(てばな)せない。
My lips are so dry and chapped that I can't do without lip balm.

がさがさ is a mimetic word with two related meanings: a rustling sound and a rough/dry texture. Both senses share the image of dry, coarse surfaces.

SOUND MEANING:

  • がさがさと音(おと)がする: to make a rustling sound
  • 茂(しげ)みでがさがさする: rustling in the bushes

TEXTURE MEANING:

  • がさがさした肌(はだ): rough, dry skin
  • 手(て)ががさがさになる: hands become rough and dry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • かさかさ: lighter rustling; drier texture (milder than がさがさ)
  • ごそごそ: rummaging sound (deliberate searching movement)
  • ざらざら: rough, gritty (texture only, no sound meaning)
  • すべすべ: smooth (opposite texture)
