るんるん

るんるん
adverb, na-adjective
cheerfully; in high spirits; humming happily

Definitions

1. cheerfully; in high spirits; humming happily
Describes being in an extremely good mood, often to the point of humming or skipping. Conveys carefree happiness.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)はるんるん気分(きぶん)で出(で)かけた。
She went out in high spirits.
給料日(きゅうりょうび)はるんるんで買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)く。
On payday, I go shopping in a cheerful mood.
子供(こども)たちはるんるんしながら遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing happily.

Notes

るんるん is a playful, somewhat childish expression for being in high spirits. It evokes the image of someone humming a tune while walking.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • るんるん気分(きぶん): in high spirits
  • るんるんで〜する: to do something cheerfully
  • るんるんしている: to be in a happy mood

USAGE NOTE:
This expression is casual and somewhat cute or feminine. It's commonly used in informal speech and manga/anime.
