脾臓(ひぞう)

ひぞう
noun
spleen
1. spleen
An organ in the upper left abdomen that filters blood, recycles old red blood cells, and plays a role in immune function.
脾臓(ひぞう)は古(ふる)い赤血球(せっけっきゅう)を処理(しょり)する。
The spleen processes old red blood cells.
事故(じこ)で脾臓(ひぞう)を損傷(そんしょう)した。
The spleen was damaged in the accident.
脾臓(ひぞう)がなくても生(い)きていける。
You can live without a spleen.

FUNCTIONS:

  • Filters blood and removes old/damaged red blood cells
  • Stores platelets and white blood cells
  • Plays a role in immune response

MEDICAL TERMS:

  • 脾腫(ひしゅ): splenomegaly (enlarged spleen)
  • 脾臓摘出(ひぞうてきしゅつ): splenectomy (spleen removal)

LOCATION:
Upper left abdomen, behind the 胃(い) (stomach) and under the ribs.

INTERESTING FACT:
The spleen is not essential for survival; other organs can take over its functions if it's removed.

RELATED ORGANS:

  • 膵臓(すいぞう): pancreas
  • 肝臓(かんぞう): liver
