論理性(ろんりせい)

ろんりせい
noun
logic; logical consistency
1. logic; logical consistency
The quality of being logically ordered and coherent; the presence of clear reasoning.
論理性(ろんりせい)のある文章(ぶんしょう)を書(か)く。
To write a logically coherent text.
その議論(ぎろん)は論理性(ろんりせい)に欠(か)ける。
That argument lacks logical consistency.
論理性(ろんりせい)を重視(じゅうし)する。
To place importance on logic.

論理性(ろんりせい) refers to logical consistency or the quality of being logical. Common in academic and analytical contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 論理性(ろんりせい)がある: to have logic/be logical
  • 論理性(ろんりせい)に欠(か)ける: to lack logic
  • 論理性(ろんりせい)を重視(じゅうし)する: to emphasize logic
  • 論理性(ろんりせい)を高(たか)める: to improve logical consistency

WORD FORMATION:
論理(ろんり) (logic) + 性(せい) (nature/quality).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical (na-adjective)
  • 合理性(ごうりせい): rationality
  • 整合性(せいごうせい): consistency, coherence
