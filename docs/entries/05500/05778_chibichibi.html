ちびちび

ちびちび
adverb
sipping, little by little
1. sipping, drinking little by little
Drinking slowly in small amounts, savoring.
日本酒(にほんしゅ)をちびちび飲(の)む。
To sip sake slowly.
ちびちび飲(の)んでいたら冷(さ)めちゃった。
It got cold because I was sipping it slowly.
ちびちび使(つか)う。
To use sparingly.
Can also be used for careful use of resources

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes slow, small-amount drinking or using. Often associated with enjoying alcohol leisurely.

CONTRAST:

  • ごくごく: gulping down (opposite manner)
general
