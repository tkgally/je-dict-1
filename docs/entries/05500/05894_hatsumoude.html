初詣(はつもうで)

はつもうで
noun, verb (suru)
first shrine visit of the year

Definitions

1. first shrine/temple visit of the new year
The traditional practice of visiting a Shinto shrine or Buddhist temple for the first time in the new year.

Examples

初詣(はつもうで)に行(い)く。
To go for the first shrine visit of the year.
初詣(はつもうで)で今年(ことし)の願(ねが)い事(ごと)をした。
I made a wish for this year at the new year shrine visit.
明治神宮(めいじじんぐう)の初詣(はつもうで)は混雑(こんざつ)する。
Meiji Shrine gets crowded during hatsumode.

Notes

CULTURAL:
A major New Year custom in Japan. Many people visit shrines at midnight on New Year's Eve or within the first three days of January. Popular activities include drawing omikuji (fortune slips) and buying omamori (charms).
