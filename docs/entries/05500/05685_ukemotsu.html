1. to be in charge of; to take charge of
To have responsibility for a task, class, area, or group of people.
彼女(かのじょ)は三年生(さんねんせい)のクラスを受(う)け持(も)っている。
She is in charge of the third-grade class.
このプロジェクトは誰(だれ)が受(う)け持(も)つのですか。
Who will be in charge of this project?
関西(かんさい)地区(ちく)を受(う)け持(も)っています。
I'm in charge of the Kansai region.
新人(しんじん)の教育(きょういく)を受(う)け持(も)つことになった。
I've been assigned to handle training the new employees.
一人(ひとり)の先生(せんせい)が二(ふた)つのクラスを受(う)け持(も)っているので、とても忙(いそが)しい。
One teacher is in charge of two classes, so they're very busy.

受(う)け持(も)つ means to have something as one's assigned responsibility — a class, a subject, a district, a set of patients. It describes an ongoing assignment given to you rather than a task you take on by choice, so it is most at home in schools, hospitals, and workplaces where duties are divided up among people. 受(う)け持(も)っている (the ている form) is the usual way to say what someone is currently in charge of.

COMPOUND VERB:
受(う)ける (to receive) + 持(も)つ (to hold)

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クラスを受(う)け持(も)つ: to be in charge of a class
  • 担当(たんとう)を受(う)け持(も)つ: to take on a responsibility
  • 地域(ちいき)を受(う)け持(も)つ: to be in charge of an area

RELATED NOUN:

  • 受(う)け持(も)ち: one's charge, assignment

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 担当(たんとう)する: to be in charge of (more formal)
  • 任(まか)される: to be entrusted with

Related Words

Related: 受(う)け持(も)ち (noun form: one's charge or assignment)
Related: 担当(たんとう) (more formal word for being in charge)
