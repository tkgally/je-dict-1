ばっちり

ばっちり
adverb, onomatopoeia
perfectly; completely; spot-on

Definitions

1. perfectly; completely; spot-on
Describes something done perfectly or matching exactly as intended, with no problems or mistakes.

Examples

準備(じゅんび)はばっちりだ。
The preparations are perfect.
化粧(けしょう)がばっちり決(き)まっている。
Her makeup looks perfect.
今日(きょう)のプレゼン、ばっちりうまくいった。
Today's presentation went perfectly.

Notes

USAGE:
ばっちり is an informal, expressive word conveying confidence that something is exactly right or will work out perfectly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ばっちり決(き)まる: to look perfect, to be decided perfectly
  • 準備(じゅんび)はばっちり: preparations are perfect
  • ばっちりOK: totally okay
  • ばっちり合(あ)う: to fit perfectly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完璧(かんぺき)に: perfectly (more formal)
  • きっちり: precisely, exactly
  • ぴったり: exactly fitting
