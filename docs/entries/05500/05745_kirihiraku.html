1. to clear, to open up (land)
To clear land by cutting down trees and vegetation.
山(やま)を切(き)り開(ひら)く。
To clear a mountain.
森(もり)を切(き)り開(ひら)いて農地(のうち)にした。
They cleared the forest and made it farmland.
先人(せんじん)たちが荒(あ)れ地(ち)を切(き)り開(ひら)いて、この村(むら)を作(つく)った。
Our ancestors cleared the wasteland and built this village.
2. to pioneer, to forge (a path)
To create a new way or opportunity where none existed before.
自分(じぶん)で道(みち)を切(き)り開(ひら)く。
To forge one's own path.
未来(みらい)を切(き)り開(ひら)く力(ちから)がある。
You have the power to pioneer your future.
彼女(かのじょ)は新(あたら)しい分野(ぶんや)を切(き)り開(ひら)いて、多(おお)くの後輩(こうはい)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えた。
She pioneered a new field and influenced many of those who came after her.

切(き)り開(ひら)く is a compound verb meaning to clear or pioneer, used literally and figuratively.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the object being cleared or pioneered

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:
切(き)る (to cut) + 開(ひら)く (to open) = to cut open, clear away

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 森(もり)を切(き)り開(ひら)く: to clear a forest
  • 道(みち)を切(き)り開(ひら)く: to forge a path
  • 未来(みらい)を切(き)り開(ひら)く: to pioneer one's future
  • 新(あたら)しい分野(ぶんや)を切(き)り開(ひら)く: to pioneer a new field

USAGE:
Literally used for clearing land or vegetation. Figuratively used for breaking new ground, creating opportunities, or overcoming obstacles to achieve something new.

Related Words

Synonym: 開拓(かいたく) (cultivate, pioneer (noun/verb))
