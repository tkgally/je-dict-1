豪雨(ごうう)

ごうう
noun
heavy rain, downpour

Definitions

1. heavy rain, downpour
Very heavy rainfall, often causing flooding or other damage.

Examples

豪雨(ごうう)で川(かわ)が氾濫(はんらん)した。
The river flooded due to the heavy rain.
豪雨(ごうう)警報(けいほう)が発令(はつれい)された。
A heavy rain warning was issued.
記録的(きろくてき)な豪雨(ごうう)に見舞(みま)われた。
We were hit by record-breaking heavy rain.

Notes

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 豪雨(ごうう)警報(けいほう): heavy rain warning
  • 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう): localized heavy rain
  • 豪雨(ごうう)災害(さいがい): heavy rain disaster

CONTRAST:

  • 小雨(こさめ): light rain (opposite intensity)
