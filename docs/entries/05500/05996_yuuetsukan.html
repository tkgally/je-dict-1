優越感(ゆうえつかん)

ゆうえつかん
noun
sense of superiority; superiority complex
1. sense of superiority; superiority complex
The feeling of being better than others. Can be a healthy self-confidence or an unhealthy arrogance depending on context.
彼(かれ)は優越感(ゆうえつかん)に浸(ひた)っているようだ。
He seems to be basking in a sense of superiority.
優越感(ゆうえつかん)を持(も)つのは危険(きけん)だ。
Having a superiority complex is dangerous.
他人(たにん)より優(すぐ)れているという優越感(ゆうえつかん)を感(かん)じる。
I feel a sense of superiority, as if I'm better than others.

優越感(ゆうえつかん) is often used in psychological contexts and typically carries a negative connotation suggesting arrogance.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 優越感(ゆうえつかん)を感(かん)じる: to feel a sense of superiority
  • 優越感(ゆうえつかん)に浸(ひた)る: to bask in superiority
  • 優越感(ゆうえつかん)を持(も)つ: to have a superiority complex

Related Words

Antonym: 劣等感(れっとうかん) (inferiority complex)
