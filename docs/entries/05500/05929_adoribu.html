アドリブ

あどりぶ
noun, na-adjective
ad-lib; improvisation
1. ad-lib; improvisation
Speaking or performing spontaneously without preparation or a script. Common in music, comedy, and acting.
このシーンはアドリブで演(えん)じた。
This scene was performed ad-lib.
ジャズはアドリブが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Improvisation is important in jazz.
彼(かれ)はアドリブが得意(とくい)だ。
He's good at improvising.

アドリブ is borrowed from Latin "ad libitum" (at pleasure), via English.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • アドリブで〜する: to do something ad-lib
  • アドリブが利(き)く: to be good at improvising
  • アドリブ演奏(えんそう): improvised performance

CONTEXTS:

  • Music (especially jazz)
  • Comedy (manzai, variety shows)
  • Acting and theater
  • Public speaking
