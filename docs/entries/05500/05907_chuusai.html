1. arbitration; mediation
The process of intervening between two disputing parties to help resolve their conflict. Can refer to formal legal arbitration or informal mediation.
国際(こくさい)仲裁(ちゅうさい)裁判所(さいばんしょ)に訴(うった)えた。
They appealed to the international arbitration court.
友人(ゆうじん)が二人(ふたり)の喧嘩(けんか)を仲裁(ちゅうさい)してくれた。
A friend mediated the argument between the two.
仲裁(ちゅうさい)に入(はい)って紛争(ふんそう)を解決(かいけつ)した。
They stepped in to mediate and resolved the dispute.

仲裁(ちゅうさい) means intervening between disputing parties to help resolve a conflict. It covers both formal legal arbitration and informal mediation between people.

WORD FORMATION:
仲(ちゅう) (between, intermediary) + 裁(さい) (judge, decide)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい)に入(はい)る: to step in as mediator
  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい)を求(もと)める: to seek arbitration
  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい)裁判所(さいばんしょ): arbitration court/tribunal
  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい)人(にん): arbitrator
  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい)判断(はんだん): arbitral decision

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仲裁(ちゅうさい): arbitration (a third party helps decide the outcome)
  • 調停(ちょうてい): mediation, conciliation (a third party facilitates discussion)
  • 和解(わかい): settlement, reconciliation (the parties reach agreement)
  • 仲直(なかなお)り: making up (informal, between friends/family)

REGISTER:
The legal sense is formal. In everyday speech, 仲裁(ちゅうさい)に入(はい)る is used casually to mean stepping in to break up an argument.

Related Words

Synonym: 調停(ちょうてい) (mediation, conciliation)
Synonym: 和解(わかい) (settlement, reconciliation)
