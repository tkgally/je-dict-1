概(おおむ)ね

おおむね
adverb
mostly, generally
1. mostly, generally, on the whole
For the most part, with minor exceptions.
計画(けいかく)は概(おおむ)ね順調(じゅんちょう)だ。
The plan is going mostly well.
概(おおむ)ね同意(どうい)できる。
I can generally agree.
結果(けっか)は概(おおむ)ね予想通(よそうどお)りだった。
The results were mostly as expected.

概(おおむ)ね is a formal adverb meaning "mostly" or "generally," indicating something is largely true with minor exceptions.

USAGE:
Common in business reports, news, and formal writing. Less common in casual speech.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 概(おおむ)ね順調(じゅんちょう): mostly going well
  • 概(おおむ)ね予想通(よそうどお)り: mostly as expected
  • 概(おおむ)ね賛成(さんせい): generally in agreement
  • 概(おおむ)ね良好(りょうこう): generally favorable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • だいたい: roughly, approximately (casual)
  • ほぼ: almost, nearly
  • 大体(だいたい): generally (neutral)
  • 概(おおむ)ね: mostly (formal)

概(おおむ)ね is more formal than だいたい or ほぼ.
