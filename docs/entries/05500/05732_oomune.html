概(おおむ)ね

おおむね
adverb
mostly, generally
1. mostly, generally, on the whole
For the most part, with minor exceptions.
計画(けいかく)は概(おおむ)ね順調(じゅんちょう)だ。
The plan is going mostly well.
概(おおむ)ね同意(どうい)できる。
I can generally agree.
結果(けっか)は概(おおむ)ね予想通(よそうどお)りだった。
The results were mostly as expected.

USAGE:
概(おおむ)ね is a somewhat formal word indicating that something is largely true with minor exceptions. Common in business and formal writing.
