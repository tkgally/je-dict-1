獲得(かくとく)

かくとく
noun
acquisition; obtaining
1. acquisition; obtaining; gaining
The act of gaining or obtaining something, often through effort or achievement.
金メダル(きんメダル)を獲得(かくとく)した。
Won a gold medal.
新規(しんき)顧客(こきゃく)の獲得(かくとく)に力(ちから)を入(い)れる。
To focus on acquiring new customers.
資格(しかく)を獲得(かくとく)するために勉強(べんきょう)する。
To study to obtain a qualification.

獲得(かくとく) means actively obtaining something through effort, competition, or achievement.

GRAMMAR:
Functions as a noun or する verb: 獲得(かくとく)する (to acquire)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 獲得(かくとく)する: to acquire, to obtain
  • 票(ひょう)を獲得(かくとく)する: to win votes
  • 金(きん)メダルを獲得(かくとく)する: to win a gold medal
  • 顧客(こきゃく)を獲得(かくとく)する: to acquire customers
  • 資格(しかく)を獲得(かくとく)する: to obtain a qualification

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取得(しゅとく): acquisition (formal, documents/qualifications)
  • 入手(にゅうしゅ): obtaining (getting hold of something)
  • もらう: to receive (casual, passive receiving)

NUANCE:
獲得(かくとく) emphasizes active effort and often implies competition or achievement.
