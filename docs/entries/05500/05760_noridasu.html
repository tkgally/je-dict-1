乗(の)り出(だ)す

のりだす
verb (godan)
to lean forward, to embark on
1. to lean forward
To move the upper body forward, showing interest or eagerness.
身(み)を乗(の)り出(だ)して話(はなし)を聞(き)いた。
I leaned forward and listened to the story.
2. to embark on, to venture into
To start participating in or launching into a new activity or field.
新(あたら)しい事業(じぎょう)に乗(の)り出(だ)す。
To venture into a new business.
政府(せいふ)が対策(たいさく)に乗(の)り出(だ)した。
The government has embarked on countermeasures.

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 乗(の)る (to ride) + 出(だ)す (to start). The suffix 出す adds the meaning of beginning an action.

COMMON PATTERN:

  • 身(み)を乗(の)り出(だ)す: to lean forward (literally "lean one's body out")
