ぱくぱく

ぱくぱく
adverb, onomatopoeia
munching; gobbling; opening and closing (mouth)

Definitions

1. munching; gobbling
Eating eagerly by repeatedly opening and closing the mouth; wolfing down food.
2. opening and closing repeatedly
The motion of a mouth (or mouth-like thing) repeatedly opening and closing.

Examples

子(こ)どもがケーキをぱくぱく食(た)べた。
The child gobbled up the cake.
金魚(きんぎょ)がぱくぱく口(くち)を開(あ)けている。
The goldfish is opening and closing its mouth.
おにぎりをぱくぱく頬張(ほおば)る。
To stuff one's cheeks with rice balls.

Notes

SOUND REPRESENTATION:
The voiceless "ぱ" suggests light, quick movements of the mouth, giving a cute or childlike impression.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぱくぱく食(た)べる: to munch, gobble
  • 口(くち)をぱくぱく: opening and closing mouth
  • ぱくぱく頬張(ほおば)る: to stuff one's cheeks

RELATED WORDS:

  • もぐもぐ: chewing (mouth closed)
  • がつがつ: eating greedily (rougher image)
  • ぱくっと: taking one big bite
