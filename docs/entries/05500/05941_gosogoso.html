ごそごそ

ごそごそ
adverb, suru verb
rustling; rummaging around

Definitions

1. rustling; rummaging
The sound of rustling or the action of rummaging through things, often when searching for something. Implies somewhat clumsy or persistent searching.

Examples

カバンの中(なか)をごそごそ探(さが)した。
I rummaged through my bag.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)からごそごそという音(おと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear rustling sounds from the next room.
彼女(かのじょ)は押(お)し入(い)れの中(なか)をごそごそしていた。
She was rummaging around in the closet.

Notes

ごそごそ describes the sound of rustling or the action of searching through things in a somewhat clumsy or thorough manner. Often used when someone is looking for something.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • がさがさ: similar rustling (rougher sound)
  • ごちゃごちゃ: messy/cluttered
  • がさごそ: rummaging (variant)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ごそごそ探(さが)す: to rummage/search
  • ごそごそする: to rustle/rummage
  • ごそごそと音(おと)がする: to make rustling sounds

NUANCE:
ごそごそ often implies discrete or secretive searching, or searching that disturbs the surrounding area.

Related Words

Related: がさがさ (rustling)
