しょんぼり

しょんぼり
adverb, verb (suru)
dejectedly, sadly, downcast

Definitions

1. dejectedly, sadly
Looking sad and dejected, often with drooping posture or expression. Suggests being discouraged or dispirited.

Examples

しょんぼりした顔(かお)をしている。
You have a dejected look on your face.
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちてしょんぼりしている。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
彼女(かのじょ)に振(ふ)られて、彼(かれ)はしょんぼりと帰(かえ)っていった。
After being rejected by her, he went home looking dejected.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes a sad, dejected state with visible discouragement. Often implies a drooping posture or downcast appearance.

CONTRAST:

  • がっくり: more sudden disappointment
  • るんるん: opposite - cheerful and merry

Related Words

Related: がっくり (sudden disappointment)
