しょんぼり

しょんぼり
adverb, verb (suru)
dejectedly, sadly, downcast
1. dejectedly, sadly
Looking sad and dejected, often with drooping posture or expression. Suggests being discouraged or dispirited.
しょんぼりした顔(かお)をしている。
You have a dejected look on your face.
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちてしょんぼりしている。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
彼女(かのじょ)に振(ふ)られて、彼(かれ)はしょんぼりと帰(かえ)っていった。
After being rejected by her, he went home looking dejected.

しょんぼり is a mimetic word (擬態語(ぎたいご)) describing a dejected, downcast state.

USAGE:

  • しょんぼりする: to feel dejected
  • しょんぼりした顔(かお): a dejected face
  • しょんぼり帰(かえ)る: to go home dejectedly

NUANCE:
Implies visible sadness—drooping shoulders, downcast eyes, dispirited manner. Often used for temporary disappointment rather than deep grief.

CONTRAST WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • しょんぼり: dejected, visibly sad (mild)
  • がっくり: sudden disappointment, crestfallen
  • しょげる: to be depressed, lose spirit
  • 落(お)ち込(こ)む: to feel down, depressed

OPPOSITE:

  • るんるん: cheerful, merry
  • うきうき: excited, light-hearted

Related Words

Related: がっくり (sudden disappointment)
