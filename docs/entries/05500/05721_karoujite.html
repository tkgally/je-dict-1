辛(かろ)うじて

かろうじて
adverb
barely, narrowly
1. barely, narrowly, just
Managing to do something by a very small margin, with difficulty.
辛(かろ)うじて合格(ごうかく)した。
I barely passed.
辛(かろ)うじて間(ま)に合(あ)った。
I just barely made it in time.
事故(じこ)を辛(かろ)うじて避(さ)けた。
I narrowly avoided the accident.

辛(かろ)うじて is an adverb meaning "barely" or "narrowly," emphasizing that something was achieved with great difficulty.

NUANCE:
Implies that failure/disaster was very close and success came only by a small margin. Often used for near-misses or last-minute saves.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 辛(かろ)うじて間(ま)に合(あ)う: to barely make it in time
  • 辛(かろ)うじて合格(ごうかく)する: to barely pass
  • 辛(かろ)うじて逃(のが)れる: to narrowly escape
  • 辛(かろ)うじて助(たす)かる: to barely survive

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ぎりぎり: just barely (more casual)
  • やっと: finally, at last (emphasizes effort)
  • なんとか: somehow, barely
  • 辛(かろ)うじて: barely (emphasizes small margin)
