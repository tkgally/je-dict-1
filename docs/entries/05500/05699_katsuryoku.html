活力(かつりょく)

かつりょく
noun
vitality; energy

Definitions

1. vitality; energy; vigor
The state of being strong, active, and full of energy.

Examples

活力(かつりょく)に満(み)ちている。
To be full of vitality.
経済(けいざい)に活力(かつりょく)を与(あた)える。
To give energy to the economy.
活力(かつりょく)を取(と)り戻(もど)す。
To regain vitality.

Notes

活力(かつりょく) means vitality or energy.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 活力(かつりょく)に満(み)ちた: full of vitality
  • 活力(かつりょく)がある: to have energy
  • 活力(かつりょく)を失(うしな)う: to lose vitality

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 元気(げんき): health, energy
  • 精力(せいりょく): energy, vigor
