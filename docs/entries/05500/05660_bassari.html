ばっさり

ばっさり
adverb, onomatopoeia
drastically; completely; with one clean cut
1. drastically; completely
Describes cutting something off completely or making a decisive, drastic change.
髪(かみ)をばっさり切(き)った。
I cut my hair drastically short.
予算(よさん)がばっさり削(けず)られた。
The budget was drastically cut.
彼(かれ)の提案(ていあん)はばっさり却下(きゃっか)された。
His proposal was flatly rejected.
2. with one clean cut
Describes the sound or manner of cutting something cleanly and decisively.
髪(かみ)をばっさり切(き)った。
I cut my hair drastically short.
庭(にわ)の木(き)の枝(えだ)をばっさり切(き)り落(お)とした。
I cleanly cut off the tree branches in the garden.
刀(かたな)でばっさりと斬(き)られた。
He was cut down with one clean stroke of the sword.

NUANCE:
ばっさり conveys decisiveness and finality—a clean, complete cut or elimination without hesitation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ばっさり切(き)る: to cut drastically/decisively
  • ばっさり削(けず)る: to cut drastically (budget, etc.)
  • ばっさり断(ことわ)る: to flatly refuse
  • ばっさり捨(す)てる: to throw away completely

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すっぱり: cleanly, completely (for quitting habits)
  • きっぱり: flatly, decisively (for statements)
  • さっぱり: refreshingly (after cleaning up)

Related Words

Synonym: きっぱり (flatly, decisively)
Synonym: さっぱり (refreshingly; cleanly)
