慣習(かんしゅう)

かんしゅう
noun
custom; convention; practice

Definitions

1. custom; convention; established practice
A traditional way of doing things that has become established over time.

Examples

古(ふる)い慣習(かんしゅう)を守(まも)る。
To preserve old customs.
社会的(しゃかいてき)慣習(かんしゅう)に従(したが)う。
To follow social conventions.
慣習(かんしゅう)を破(やぶ)る。
To break with convention.

Notes

慣習(かんしゅう) means custom or convention.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 慣習(かんしゅう)法(ほう): customary law
  • 慣習(かんしゅう)に従(したが)う: to follow custom

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 習慣(しゅうかん): habit (personal)
  • 風習(ふうしゅう): customs, manners
