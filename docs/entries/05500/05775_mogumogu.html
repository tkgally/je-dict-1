1. munching, chewing
The sound or manner of chewing food, especially with the mouth closed.
もぐもぐ食(た)べている。
Munching away at food.
ハムスターがもぐもぐしている。
The hamster is munching away.
子供(こども)がパンをもぐもぐ食(た)べている。
The child is munching on bread.
口(くち)いっぱいにもぐもぐしながら話(はな)さないで。
Don't talk while munching with your mouth full.
赤(あか)ちゃんがおやつをもぐもぐ食(た)べている姿(すがた)がかわいい。
The baby munching on a snack is so cute.
2. mumbling
Speaking unclearly, often with food in one's mouth.
口(くち)の中(なか)でもぐもぐ言(い)っている。
He's mumbling something.
もぐもぐ言(い)わないで、はっきり言(い)って。
Stop mumbling and say it clearly.
何(なに)かもぐもぐ言(い)っていたが、聞(き)き取(と)れなかった。
He was mumbling something, but I couldn't catch it.
先生(せんせい)に叱(しか)られて、もぐもぐと言(い)い訳(わけ)をしていた。
After being scolded by the teacher, he was mumbling excuses.
彼(かれ)はもぐもぐと何(なに)か言(い)いながら、下(した)を向(む)いて歩(ある)いていた。
He was walking with his head down, mumbling something.

もぐもぐ is an onomatopoeia for the sound/manner of chewing, especially with the mouth closed.

USAGE:

  • As adverb: もぐもぐ食(た)べる (to munch)
  • With する: もぐもぐする (to munch away)
  • For mumbling: 口(くち)の中(なか)でもぐもぐ言(い)う (to mumble)

NUANCE:

Conveys a cute, gentle impression. Often used for:

  • Children eating
  • Small animals (hamsters, rabbits) eating
  • Eating something soft or chewy

SIMILAR ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • ぱくぱく: eating quickly with big bites
  • むしゃむしゃ: eating heartily, chomping
  • もごもご: mumbling (speaking)

もぐもぐ vs. もごもご: both can describe unclear speech, but もぐもぐ keeps its link to the chewing motion (mouth working, often with food in it), while もごもご is purely about indistinct, hesitant speech and carries no eating sense.
