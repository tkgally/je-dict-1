安堵(あんど)

あんど
noun, verb (suru)
relief, reassurance
1. relief, reassurance
The feeling of relief when worry or anxiety is removed.
安堵(あんど)の息(いき)をつく。
To breathe a sigh of relief.
Common expression
無事(ぶじ)を聞(き)いて安堵(あんど)した。
I was relieved to hear they were safe.
安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべる。
To show a relieved expression.

安堵(あんど) expresses the feeling of relief when worry or anxiety is lifted. It has a somewhat literary or formal tone.

USAGE:

  • 安堵(あんど)する: to feel relieved (verb)
  • 安堵(あんど)の + noun: relief + noun (attributive)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 安堵(あんど)の息(いき)をつく: to breathe a sigh of relief
  • 安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう): relieved expression
  • 安堵感(あんどかん): feeling of relief
  • 安堵(あんど)の色(いろ)を浮(う)かべる: to show relief (on one's face)

CONTRAST:

  • ほっとする: to feel relieved (casual, everyday)
  • 安心(あんしん)する: to feel at ease, relieved (neutral)
  • 安堵(あんど)する: to feel relieved (literary, formal)

NOTE:
安堵(あんど) is often used in written Japanese, news, and formal speech rather than casual conversation.
