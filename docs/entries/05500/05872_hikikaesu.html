引(ひ)き返(かえ)す

ひきかえす
verb (godan)
to turn back, to go back
1. to turn back, to go back
To return the way one came; to go back to the starting point.
道(みち)を間違(まちが)えたので引(ひ)き返(かえ)した。
I turned back because I took the wrong road.
忘(わす)れ物(もの)に気(き)づいて引(ひ)き返(かえ)した。
I turned back when I realized I forgot something.
天気(てんき)が悪(わる)くなったので山頂(さんちょう)から引(ひ)き返(かえ)した。
We turned back from the summit because the weather got bad.

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 引(ひ)く (to pull) + 返(かえ)す (to return). Emphasizes the action of physically going back.

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive verb)

CONTRAST:

  • 引(ひ)き下(さ)がる: to step back, to withdraw (often from a position or argument)

Related Words

Contrast: 引(ひ)き下(さ)がる (to withdraw)
