1. to look around; to survey
To turn one's gaze in various directions to observe the surroundings.
部屋(へや)を見回(みまわ)して何(なに)かを探(さが)した。
I looked around the room searching for something.
辺(あた)りを見回(みまわ)すと、誰(だれ)もいなかった。
When I looked around, there was no one there.
彼女(かのじょ)は不安(ふあん)そうに周囲(しゅうい)を見回(みまわ)した。
She looked around anxiously.
教室(きょうしつ)を見回(みまわ)すと、全員(ぜんいん)席(せき)に着(つ)いていた。
When I looked around the classroom, everyone was in their seats.
山(やま)の上(うえ)から景色(けしき)を見回(みまわ)すと、遠(とお)くに海(うみ)が見(み)えた。
When I surveyed the scenery from the top of the mountain, I could see the sea in the distance.

見回(みまわ)す is a compound verb: 見(み)る (to see) + 回(まわ)す (to turn around). It means to look around in all directions, scanning the surroundings.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the area being surveyed: 部屋(へや)を見回(みまわ)す

ASPECT (ている):
見回(みまわ)している means "is looking around" (ongoing action).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 辺(あた)りを見回(みまわ)す: to look around the area
  • 部屋(へや)を見回(みまわ)す: to look around the room
  • きょろきょろ見回(みまわ)す: to look around restlessly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見渡(みわた)す: to survey, to look out over (wider panoramic view)
  • 見回(みまわ)る: to patrol, to make rounds (walking around to check)
  • 見(み)つめる: to gaze at (focused on one point, not scanning)
