しっとり

しっとり
adverb, verb (suru)
moist, damp, subdued

Definitions

1. moist, pleasantly damp
Having a soft, gentle moisture. Often used positively to describe skin, hair, food, or atmosphere.
2. subdued, calm, elegant
Having a quiet, refined elegance. Used for atmosphere, manner, or appearance.

Examples

この化粧水(けしょうすい)は肌(はだ)がしっとりする。
This toner makes your skin feel moist.
しっとりとした髪(かみ)になりたい。
I want my hair to be smooth and moisturized.
しっとりとした雰囲気(ふんいき)のバーで飲(の)んだ。
We had drinks at a bar with a subdued atmosphere.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Can describe pleasant moisture (skin, food) or a calm, elegant atmosphere. Generally has positive connotations.

CONTRAST:

  • じめじめ: unpleasantly damp
  • ぱさぱさ: dry, lacking moisture (opposite)

Related Words
