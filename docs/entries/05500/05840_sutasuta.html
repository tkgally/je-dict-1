すたすた

すたすた
adverb
briskly, with quick steps
1. briskly, with quick steps
Walking quickly with purpose and energy. Suggests determination or urgency in one's gait.
彼女(かのじょ)はすたすたと歩(ある)いていった。
She walked away briskly.
怒(おこ)ってすたすたと去(さ)っていった。
He stormed off angrily.
時間(じかん)がないので、すたすたと駅(えき)に向(む)かった。
Since I was short on time, I walked briskly toward the station.

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes walking with quick, purposeful steps. Often suggests determination, being in a hurry, or leaving in a huff.

CONTRAST:

  • とぼとぼ: walking wearily (opposite energy)
  • てくてく: walking steadily (moderate pace)
