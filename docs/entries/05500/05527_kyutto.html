きゅっと

きゅっと
adverb
tightly; with a squeeze

Definitions

1. tightly; with a squeeze
Describes gripping, squeezing, or tightening something firmly but briefly.

Examples

ネジをきゅっと締(し)める。
Tighten the screw firmly.
蓋(ふた)をきゅっと閉(し)めた。
I closed the lid tightly.
彼(かれ)の手(て)をきゅっと握(にぎ)った。
I squeezed his hand.

Notes

きゅっと is an onomatopoeia describing a quick, tight squeeze or grip. It's lighter than ぎゅっと.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぎゅっと: tightly, firmly (stronger/longer squeeze)
  • きゅうっと: variant spelling with longer vowel

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • きゅっと締める: to tighten
  • きゅっと握る: to squeeze/grip
