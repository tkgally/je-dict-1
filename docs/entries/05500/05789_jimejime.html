じめじめ

じめじめ
adverb, verb (suru)
damp, humid, gloomy

Definitions

1. damp, humid, clammy
Unpleasantly moist atmosphere or condition.
2. gloomy, depressing
Dark and cheerless atmosphere or mood.

Examples

梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)はじめじめする。
It gets damp during the rainy season.
じめじめした場所(ばしょ)にカビが生(は)える。
Mold grows in damp places.
じめじめした話(はなし)はやめよう。
Let's stop talking about depressing things.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes unpleasant dampness or gloominess. Always has negative connotation.

CONTRAST:

  • からっと: dry and refreshing (opposite)
