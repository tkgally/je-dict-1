締(し)め切(き)る

しめきる
verb (godan)
to close, to set a deadline

Definitions

1. to close tightly, to shut completely
To close doors, windows, etc. completely to seal off a space.
2. to set a deadline, to close (applications)
To establish a final date for accepting applications, submissions, etc.

Examples

窓(まど)を締(し)め切(き)って冷房(れいぼう)をつけた。
I closed all the windows and turned on the AC.
応募(おうぼ)は明日(あした)締(し)め切(き)ります。
Applications close tomorrow.
締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)わなかった。
I didn't make the deadline.
締(し)め切(き)り is the noun form

Notes

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 締(し)める (to close) + 切(き)る (to cut/complete). The suffix 切る adds the meaning of doing something completely.

RELATED NOUN:
締(し)め切(き)り: deadline
