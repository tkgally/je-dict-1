とぼとぼ

とぼとぼ
adverb
trudging wearily, plodding along

Definitions

1. trudging wearily, plodding along
Walking slowly and wearily, often suggesting exhaustion, sadness, or discouragement. Has a tired, heavy quality.

Examples

疲(つか)れてとぼとぼと家(いえ)に帰(かえ)った。
I trudged home wearily.
彼(かれ)はとぼとぼと歩(ある)いていった。
He walked away wearily.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けた選手(せんしゅ)たちがとぼとぼと去(さ)っていった。
The players who lost the game trudged away dejectedly.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes walking slowly with a heavy, tired quality. Often implies emotional state as well as physical tiredness - sadness, disappointment, or exhaustion.

CONTRAST:

  • てくてく: walking steadily (neutral)
  • すたすた: walking briskly (energetic)

Related Words
