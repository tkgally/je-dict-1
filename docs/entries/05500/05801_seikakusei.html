正確性(せいかくせい)

せいかくせい
noun
accuracy; precision
1. accuracy; precision
The quality of being correct and exact; freedom from errors or mistakes.
データ(でーた)の正確性(せいかくせい)を確認(かくにん)する。
To verify the accuracy of the data.
正確性(せいかくせい)が求(もと)められる仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a job that requires accuracy.
情報(じょうほう)の正確性(せいかくせい)に欠(か)ける。
The information lacks accuracy.

正確性(せいかくせい) refers to the quality of being correct, exact, and free from errors. Common in technical, academic, and business contexts.

WORD FORMATION:
正確(せいかく) (accurate) + 性(せい) (quality/nature) = accuracy

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正確性(せいかくせい)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure accuracy
  • 正確性(せいかくせい)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い: high/low accuracy
  • 正確性(せいかくせい)に欠(か)ける: to lack accuracy
  • 正確性(せいかくせい)を検証(けんしょう)する: to verify accuracy

RELATED TERMS:

  • 正確(せいかく): accurate (na-adjective)
  • 精度(せいど): precision (often interchangeable)
  • 確実性(かくじつせい): certainty, reliability
  • 信頼性(しんらいせい): reliability, trustworthiness

USAGE:
Often used when discussing data, measurements, information quality, or work performance.
