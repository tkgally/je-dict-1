精力(せいりょく)

せいりょく
noun
energy; vigor; stamina

Definitions

1. energy; vigor; vitality
The physical and mental strength to do things actively.

Examples

精力(せいりょく)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To devote one's energy to.
精力的(せいりょくてき)に活動(かつどう)する。
To work energetically.
精力(せいりょく)が衰(おとろ)える。
One's energy declines.

Notes

精力(せいりょく) means energy or vigor.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 精力的(せいりょくてき): energetic
  • 精力(せいりょく)を傾(かたむ)ける: to devote energy to
  • 精力(せいりょく)旺盛(おうせい): full of energy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 活力(かつりょく): vitality
  • 体力(たいりょく): physical strength
