跨(また)ぐ

またぐ
verb (godan)
to straddle; to step over
1. to straddle; to step over
To stand or walk with legs on either side of something, or to step over an obstacle.
柵(さく)を跨(また)いで向(む)こう側(がわ)に行(い)く。
To step over the fence and go to the other side.
自転車(じてんしゃ)に跨(また)ぐ。
To straddle a bicycle.
水(みず)たまりを跨(また)いで歩(ある)く。
To walk by stepping over puddles.

跨(また)ぐ describes stepping over something or straddling it with legs apart.

CONJUGATION (godan verb):

  • 跨(また)ぐ (dictionary form)
  • 跨(また)がない (negative)
  • 跨(また)いだ (past)
  • 跨(また)いで (te-form)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese culture, it is considered rude to step over people or their belongings. The concept of 跨(また)ぐ carries cultural significance regarding respect for others.
