明暗(めいあん)

めいあん
noun
light and darkness; contrast
1. light and darkness; brightness and shadow
The contrast between light and dark areas.
2. fortune and misfortune; fate
The contrast between good and bad outcomes.
この絵(え)は明暗(めいあん)のコントラストが美(うつく)しい。
This painting has beautiful contrast between light and dark.
人生(じんせい)の明暗(めいあん)を分(わ)ける。
To determine one's fate (lit: to divide light and dark of life).
明暗(めいあん)をはっきりつける。
To create clear contrast between light and shadow.

明暗(めいあん) combines 明(めい) (bright) and 暗(あん) (dark). Used both literally (in art/photography) and figuratively (fate/fortune).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 明暗(めいあん)を分(わ)ける: to determine fate/outcome
  • 明暗(めいあん)をつける: to add contrast
