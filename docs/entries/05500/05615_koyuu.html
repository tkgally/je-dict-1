固有(こゆう)

こゆう
na-adjective
inherent; peculiar; unique
1. inherent; peculiar; unique to
Being distinctive to or characteristic of something; not shared with others.
日本(にほん)固有(こゆう)の文化(ぶんか)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn about culture unique to Japan.
この地域(ちいき)固有(こゆう)の動物(どうぶつ)がいる。
There are animals endemic to this region.
固有(こゆう)名詞(めいし)は大文字(おおもじ)で始(はじ)まる。
Proper nouns begin with a capital letter.

固有(こゆう) indicates something distinctive or characteristic of a particular thing or place.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 固有(こゆう)名詞(めいし): proper noun
  • 固有(こゆう)種(しゅ): endemic species
  • 固有(こゆう)の問題(もんだい): inherent problem
