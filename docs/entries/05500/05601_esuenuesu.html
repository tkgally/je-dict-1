SNS

えすえぬえす
noun
social media; social networking service
1. social media; social networking service
Online platforms that allow users to create profiles, share content, and interact with others.
SNSで友達(ともだち)と繋(つな)がる。
To connect with friends on social media.
SNSに写真(しゃしん)を投稿(とうこう)する。
To post photos on social media.
SNSの使(つか)いすぎに注意(ちゅうい)する。
To be careful not to overuse social media.

SNS is an abbreviation for "Social Networking Service," commonly used in Japan.

POPULAR SNS IN JAPAN:

  • LINE: messaging app
  • Twitter/X: microblogging
  • Instagram: photo sharing
  • TikTok: short videos

RELATED TERMS:

  • 炎上(えんじょう): online controversy/flaming
  • 投稿(とうこう): post/posting
  • フォロー: follow
