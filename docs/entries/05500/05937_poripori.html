ぽりぽり

ぽりぽり
adverb, suru verb
crunching lightly; scratching
1. crunching lightly
The sound or action of eating something crunchy with a light, crisp texture, like pickles, crackers, or nuts.
漬物(つけもの)をぽりぽり食(た)べる音(おと)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard the sound of someone crunching on pickles.
おせんべいをぽりぽりかじりながらテレビを見(み)た。
I watched TV while munching on rice crackers.
2. scratching lightly
The action of scratching one's head or body lightly, often when puzzled or embarrassed.
彼(かれ)は困(こま)った顔(かお)で頭(あたま)をぽりぽり掻(か)いた。
He scratched his head with a troubled expression.

ぽりぽり describes either a light crunching sound when eating crispy foods or the action of scratching lightly. The soft 'p' sound suggests gentleness compared to がりがり (harder crunching).

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • がりがり: harder, louder crunching
  • さくさく: crispy texture
  • ぼりぼり: similar crunching (variant)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぽりぽり食(た)べる: to munch/crunch
  • 頭(あたま)をぽりぽり掻(か)く: to scratch one's head (when puzzled)

The scratching sense often appears in manga to show embarrassment or confusion.
