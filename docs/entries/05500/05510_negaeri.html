寝返(ねがえ)り

ねがえり
noun
turning over (in bed); roll over
1. turning over (in bed)
Rolling from one position to another while sleeping.
寝返(ねがえ)りを打(う)つ。
To roll over in bed.
赤(あか)ちゃんが寝返(ねがえ)りを打(う)った。
The baby rolled over.
寝返(ねがえ)りがしやすいベッドが欲(ほ)しい。
I want a bed that's easy to turn over in.
夜中(よなか)に何度(なんど)も寝返(ねがえ)りを打(う)って、よく眠(ねむ)れなかった。
I rolled over many times in the middle of the night and couldn't sleep well.
赤(あか)ちゃんが初(はじ)めて寝返(ねがえ)りをした時(とき)、家族(かぞく)みんなで喜(よろこ)んだ。
When the baby rolled over for the first time, the whole family celebrated.
2. betrayal; defection
Switching sides or betraying one's allies.
味方(みかた)の寝返(ねがえ)りで戦況(せんきょう)が逆転(ぎゃくてん)した。
The betrayal of our allies reversed the battle situation.
部下(ぶか)の寝返(ねがえ)りが発覚(はっかく)した。
A subordinate's betrayal was discovered.
大(おお)きな軍勢(ぐんぜい)の寝返(ねがえ)りが決定的(けっていてき)な打撃(だげき)となった。
The defection of a large army was a decisive blow.
信頼(しんらい)していた仲間(なかま)の寝返(ねがえ)りに、深(ふか)く傷(きず)ついた。
I was deeply hurt by the betrayal of a trusted companion.
有利(ゆうり)な条件(じょうけん)を提示(ていじ)されて、寝返(ねがえ)りが続出(ぞくしゅつ)した。
Because favorable conditions were offered, betrayals came one after another.

寝返(ねがえ)り has both a literal physical meaning and a figurative meaning of betrayal.

PHYSICAL MEANING:
Rolling over in bed while sleeping. An important developmental milestone for babies (赤(あか)ちゃんの寝返(ねがえ)り).

FIGURATIVE MEANING:
Betray, defect, switch sides. The image is of "turning over" one's allegiance.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 寝返(ねがえ)りを打(う)つ: to roll over (physical) / to defect (figurative)
  • 寝返(ねがえ)りができる: able to roll over (baby milestone)
  • 敵(てき)に寝返(ねがえ)る: to defect to the enemy

RELATED WORDS:

  • 裏切(うらぎ)り: betrayal (more direct/harsh)
  • 寝(ね)る: to sleep (component verb)
  • 返(かえ)る: to return (component verb)

Related Words

Related: 寝返(ねがえ)る (to roll over; to defect (verb form))
Related: 裏切(うらぎ)り (betrayal (stronger))
