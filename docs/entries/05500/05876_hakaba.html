墓場(はかば)

はかば
noun
graveyard, cemetery
1. graveyard, cemetery
A place where the dead are buried; a burial ground.
墓場(はかば)で幽霊(ゆうれい)を見(み)た。
I saw a ghost at the graveyard.
先祖(せんぞ)の墓場(はかば)を訪(おとず)れた。
I visited my ancestors' graveyard.
古(ふる)い墓場(はかば)は不気味(ぶきみ)だ。
Old graveyards are eerie.

ALTERNATIVES:

  • 墓地(ぼち): cemetery (more formal)
  • 霊園(れいえん): memorial park (modern euphemism)

IDIOM:

  • 墓場(はかば)まで持(も)っていく: to take to the grave (keep secret until death)
