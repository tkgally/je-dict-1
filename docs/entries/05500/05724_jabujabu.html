じゃぶじゃぶ

じゃぶじゃぶ
adverb, onomatopoeia
splashing; wading

Definitions

1. splashing; wading
The sound of splashing through water or washing something vigorously in water.

Examples

子(こ)どもたちがじゃぶじゃぶ川(かわ)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are splashing around in the river.
服(ふく)をじゃぶじゃぶ洗(あら)う。
To wash clothes vigorously.
じゃぶじゃぶ池(いけ)で遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play in a wading pool.

Notes

SOUND REPRESENTATION:
The voiced "じゃ" captures the heavy splashing sound of water being disturbed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • じゃぶじゃぶ洗(あら)う: to wash vigorously
  • じゃぶじゃぶ遊(あそ)ぶ: to splash around playing
  • じゃぶじゃぶ池(いけ): wading pool (children's play area)

RELATED WORDS:

  • ざぶざぶ: similar splashing (slightly rougher)
  • ばしゃばしゃ: splashing (lighter)
  • ぴちゃぴちゃ: light splashing or spattering
