延々(えんえん)

えんえん
adverb, taru-adjective
endlessly; on and on; interminably

Definitions

1. endlessly; on and on; interminably
Describes something continuing for an extremely long time without end. Often implies tedium or exhaustion.

Examples

会議(かいぎ)が延々(えんえん)と続(つづ)いた。
The meeting went on endlessly.
彼(かれ)は延々(えんえん)と愚痴(ぐち)を言(い)い続(つづ)けた。
He kept complaining on and on.
延々(えんえん)と続(つづ)く道(みち)を歩(ある)いた。
I walked along the seemingly endless road.

Notes

延々(えんえん) emphasizes excessive length or duration, often with a negative connotation of tedium.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 延々(えんえん)と続(つづ)く: to continue endlessly
  • 延々(えんえん)と話(はな)す: to talk on and on

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 永遠(えいえん)に: eternally (neutral/positive)
  • だらだら: dragging on (more casual)
  • いつまでも: forever
