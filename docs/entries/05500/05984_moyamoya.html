もやもや

もやもや
adverb, suru verb, noun
hazy feeling; vague unease; unclear
1. hazy; unclear; vague
Describes something that is unclear, foggy, or not well-defined visually.
霧(きり)でもやもやしていて前(まえ)が見(み)えない。
It's so foggy that I can't see ahead.
2. vague unease; nagging feeling
A feeling of emotional discomfort, anxiety, or frustration that is difficult to articulate. Often used to describe unresolved feelings.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)にもやもやする。
His attitude makes me feel uneasy.
もやもやした気持(きも)ちを言葉(ことば)にできない。
I can't put my uneasy feelings into words.

VISUAL MEANING (Sense 1):

Describes foggy, hazy, or unclear vision:

  • もやもやした空気(くうき): hazy air
  • もやもやした視界(しかい): blurry vision

EMOTIONAL MEANING (Sense 2):

Describes internal emotional state that is:

  • Difficult to identify or articulate
  • A mix of frustration, anxiety, or dissatisfaction
  • Often unresolved or lingering

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • もやもやする: to feel uneasy/frustrated
  • もやもやした気持(きも)ち: vague uncomfortable feeling
  • もやもやを解消(かいしょう)する: to resolve the unease

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • すっきりしない: not feeling refreshed/clear
  • 腑(ふ)に落(お)ちない: not fully convinced
  • 引(ひ)っかかる: to bother (me)
