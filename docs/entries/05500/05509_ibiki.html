いびき

いびき
noun
snore; snoring
1. snore; snoring
The breathing sound made during sleep.
いびきがうるさくて眠(ねむ)れない。
I can't sleep because the snoring is loud.
いびきをかく。
To snore.
いびき防止(ぼうし)グッズを買(か)った。
I bought anti-snoring products.

いびき means snoring.

KANJI:
Can be written as 鼾(いびき) but hiragana is common.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • いびきをかく: to snore
  • いびきが大(おお)きい: snoring loudly

HEALTH:
Chronic loud snoring may indicate 睡眠(すいみん)時(じ)無呼吸(むこきゅう)症候群(しょうこうぐん) (sleep apnea).
