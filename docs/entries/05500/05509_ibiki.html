いびき

いびき
noun
snore; snoring
1. snore; snoring
The breathing sound made during sleep.
いびきをかく。
To snore.
いびきがうるさくて眠(ねむ)れない。
I can't sleep because the snoring is loud.
いびき防止(ぼうし)グッズを買(か)った。
I bought anti-snoring products.
父(ちち)のいびきが大(おお)きくて、家族(かぞく)みんなが起(お)きてしまった。
My father's snoring was so loud that everyone in the family woke up.
疲(つか)れている時(とき)は、いつもよりいびきが大(おお)きくなるとと言(い)われている。
It's said that when you're tired, your snoring gets louder than usual.

いびき refers to snoring, the breathing sound made during sleep.

KANJI:
Can be written as 鼾(いびき) but hiragana is more common.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • いびきをかく: to snore
  • いびきが大(おお)きい: snoring is loud
  • いびきがうるさい: snoring is noisy
  • いびきが止(と)まらない: snoring won't stop

RELATED TERMS:

  • いびき防止(ぼうし): anti-snoring
  • いびき対策(たいさく): snoring countermeasures
  • 寝息(ねいき): sleeping breath (quiet breathing while asleep)

HEALTH NOTE:
Chronic loud snoring may indicate 睡眠(すいみん)時(じ)無呼吸(むこきゅう)症候群(しょうこうぐん) (sleep apnea syndrome), a condition where breathing repeatedly stops during sleep.

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Snoring sounds are often written as グーグー or ゴーゴー.
