無論(むろん)

むろん
adverb
of course; needless to say

Definitions

1. of course; needless to say; naturally
Used to state something that is obvious or goes without saying.

Examples

無論(むろん)参加(さんか)します。
Of course I'll participate.
無論(むろん)のこと。
Needless to say.
無論(むろん)、反対(はんたい)する人(ひと)もいる。
Naturally, there are people who oppose it.

Notes

無論(むろん) means of course or needless to say.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • もちろん: of course (more common)
  • 当然(とうぜん): naturally

FORMALITY:
More formal and written than もちろん.
