ぽっかり

ぽっかり
adverb
gaping open; floating lightly
1. gaping; wide open
Describes something opening up or appearing suddenly, creating a distinct void or opening. Often suggests unexpectedness.
2. floating lightly; drifting
Describes something floating gently in the air or water, especially clouds or the moon appearing serenely.
地面(じめん)にぽっかり穴(あな)が開(あ)いていた。
There was a gaping hole in the ground.
心(こころ)にぽっかり穴(あな)が空(あ)いた気(き)がする。
I feel like there's a gaping hole in my heart.
空(そら)にぽっかりと月(つき)が浮(う)かんでいる。
The moon is floating serenely in the sky.

ぽっかり often conveys a sense of sudden appearance or unexpected emptiness/openness.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぽっかり(穴(あな)が)開(あ)く: a hole opens up
  • ぽっかり浮(う)かぶ: to float gently
  • 心(こころ)にぽっかり穴(あな)が空(あ)く: to feel empty (emotionally)

CONTRAST:

  • ぱっくり: gaping wide (often for mouths or wounds)
  • ふわふわ: light and fluffy floating
  • ぽつんと: alone and isolated
general
