ぽたぽた

ぽたぽた
adverb, onomatopoeia
dripping

Definitions

1. dripping; drip by drip
The sound or manner of liquid dripping slowly in individual drops.

Examples

蛇口(じゃぐち)から水(みず)がぽたぽた落(お)ちている。
Water is dripping from the faucet.
汗(あせ)がぽたぽた垂(た)れる。
Sweat is dripping down.
雨(あめ)がぽたぽたと屋根(やね)に当(あ)たる。
Rain is dripping onto the roof.

Notes

SOUND REPRESENTATION:
ぽたぽた represents slow, individual drops falling—not a stream or flow. The soft "ぽ" sound suggests liquid drops.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぽたぽた落(お)ちる: to drip
  • ぽたぽた垂(た)れる: to drip down
  • ぽたぽたと滴(したた)る: to trickle

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぽつぽつ: sparse drops (rain beginning)
  • ぼたぼた: larger drops falling
  • たらたら: thin liquid dripping continuously
  • ざあざあ: heavy rain pouring
