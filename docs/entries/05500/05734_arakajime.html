予(あらかじ)め

あらかじめ
adverb
beforehand, in advance
1. beforehand, in advance
Done before the time when something else happens.
予(あらかじ)め予約(よやく)が必要(ひつよう)です。
You need to make a reservation in advance.
予(あらかじ)め準備(じゅんび)しておいた。
I prepared beforehand.
予(あらかじ)めご了承(りょうしょう)ください。
Please understand in advance.
Formal expression often used in notices

予(あらかじ)め is an adverb meaning "in advance" or "beforehand," emphasizing preparation before the main event.

KANJI NOTE:
Written with 予(あらかじ) (beforehand/in advance). Also commonly written entirely in hiragana: あらかじめ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予(あらかじ)め + verb: to do something in advance
  • 予(あらかじ)め + noun + が必要(ひつよう): X is required in advance

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予(あらかじ)め予約(よやく)する: to reserve in advance
  • 予(あらかじ)め準備(じゅんび)する: to prepare beforehand
  • 予(あらかじ)めご了承(りょうしょう)ください: Please understand in advance (formal notice)
  • 予(あらかじ)め確認(かくにん)する: to confirm in advance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 前(まえ)もって: beforehand (casual)
  • 事前(じぜん)に: in advance (formal)

REGISTER:
Slightly formal; common in business, notices, and instructions.
