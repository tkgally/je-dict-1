タピオカ

たぴおか
noun
tapioca; bubble tea
1. tapioca; tapioca pearls
Starch balls made from cassava root, used in drinks and desserts.
2. bubble tea
A drink containing tapioca pearls, typically milk tea.
タピオカミルクティーを飲(の)む。
To drink bubble milk tea.
タピオカがもちもちしている。
The tapioca pearls are chewy.
タピオカブームが到来(とうらい)した。
A bubble tea boom arrived.

タピオカ became extremely popular in Japan around 2019, leading to a "タピオカブーム."

POPULAR TYPES:

  • タピオカミルクティー: bubble milk tea
  • タピオカドリンク: tapioca drink

TEXTURE:
Often described as もちもち (chewy/bouncy).
