変遷(へんせん)

へんせん
noun
transition; change over time
1. transition; change over time; evolution
The process of gradual change and development through history.
言葉(ことば)の変遷(へんせん)を研究(けんきゅう)する。
To research the evolution of language.
時代(じだい)の変遷(へんせん)とともに変(か)わった。
It changed with the passage of time.
歴史(れきし)の変遷(へんせん)をたどる。
To trace the changes through history.

変遷(へんせん) describes gradual historical or temporal changes, often used in academic or formal contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 時代(じだい)の変遷(へんせん): changes through the ages
  • 歴史(れきし)の変遷(へんせん): historical transition
  • 変遷(へんせん)をたどる: to trace changes
