受(う)け付(つ)ける

うけつける
verb (ichidan, transitive)
to accept; to receive (applications)
1. to accept; to receive (applications/requests)
To officially accept applications, requests, or submissions; to handle reception of documents or visitors.
申(もう)し込(こ)みは来週(らいしゅう)まで受(う)け付(つ)けています。
We are accepting applications until next week.
苦情(くじょう)は受(う)け付(つ)けません。
We do not accept complaints.
2. to tolerate; to accept (physically/mentally)
To be able to stomach or tolerate something; often used in negative form to indicate incompatibility.
体(からだ)がアルコールを受(う)け付(つ)けない。
My body can't tolerate alcohol.

COMPOUND VERB:
受(う)ける (to receive) + 付(つ)ける (to attach, to apply)

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予約(よやく)を受(う)け付(つ)ける: to accept reservations
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)みを受(う)け付(つ)ける: to accept applications
  • 相談(そうだん)を受(う)け付(つ)ける: to accept consultations
  • 体(からだ)が受(う)け付(つ)けない: body can't tolerate

RELATED NOUN:

  • 受付(うけつけ): reception desk, receptionist
