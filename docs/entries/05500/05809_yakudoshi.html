厄年(やくどし)

やくどし
noun
unlucky year, climacteric year

Definitions

1. unlucky year, year of calamity
Specific ages believed to bring bad luck or misfortune in Japanese tradition.

Examples

今年(ことし)は厄年(やくどし)だ。
This year is my unlucky year.
厄年(やくどし)の厄払(やくばら)いをする。
To have a purification ceremony for the unlucky year.
男性(だんせい)の大厄(たいやく)は42歳(さい)だ。
The great unlucky year for men is age 42.

Notes

CULTURAL:

Traditional belief in years when one is susceptible to misfortune. Main unlucky ages differ by gender:

  • Men: 25, 42 (大厄 major), 61
  • Women: 19, 33 (大厄 major), 37

People often visit shrines for 厄払い (purification rituals) during these years.
