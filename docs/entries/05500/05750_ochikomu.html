1. to feel down, to be depressed
To become sad, dejected, or emotionally low.
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
そんなに落(お)ち込(こ)まないで。
Don't be so down.
失恋(しつれん)して落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
I'm depressed after being rejected.
何(なに)があったの？落(お)ち込(こ)んでるね。
What happened? You seem down.
失敗(しっぱい)して落(お)ち込(こ)む必要(ひつよう)はない。
There's no need to feel down about failing.
彼女(かのじょ)はまだ落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
She's still feeling down.
2. to fall into, to sink into
To drop or sink into something.
ボールが穴(あな)に落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
The ball fell into the hole.
車(くるま)が溝(みぞ)に落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
The car sank into a ditch.
地盤(じばん)が落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
The ground is subsiding.
大雨(おおあめ)で道路(どうろ)の一部(いちぶ)が落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
Part of the road caved in from the heavy rain.
工事(こうじ)の影響(えいきょう)で周辺(しゅうへん)の地面(じめん)が少(すこ)しずつ落(お)ち込(こ)んでいるようだ。
The ground around the area seems to be gradually sinking due to the construction.
3. to decline, to slump
For business, economy, etc. to decline.
売(う)り上(あ)げが落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
Sales are declining.
景気(けいき)が落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
The economy is in a slump.
株価(かぶか)が大(おお)きく落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
Stock prices dropped significantly.
成績(せいせき)が落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
My grades are declining.
視聴率(しちょうりつ)が落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
Viewership ratings dropped.
需要(じゅよう)が落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる。
Demand is declining.

落(お)ち込(こ)む is a compound godan verb formed from 落(お)ちる (to fall) + 込(こ)む (into).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No transitive pair

ASPECT (ている):
落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる describes an ongoing state of being depressed or a continuing decline.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて落(お)ち込(こ)む: to feel down after failing an exam
  • 売(う)り上(あ)げが落(お)ち込(こ)む: sales decline
  • ひどく落(お)ち込(こ)む: to be deeply depressed

USAGE:
Most commonly used for emotional state (sense 1) in daily conversation. For physical falling, 落(お)ちる alone is more common.

Related Words

Related: がっくり (dejected; knees buckling)
