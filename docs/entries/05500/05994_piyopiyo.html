ぴよぴよ

ぴよぴよ
adverb
chirp chirp; peep peep
1. chirping; peeping
The sound made by baby chicks or small birds. A cute, high-pitched chirping sound.
ひよこがぴよぴよ鳴(な)いている。
The chicks are chirping.
巣(す)の中(なか)からぴよぴよと声(こえ)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear chirping sounds from inside the nest.
ぴよぴよと鳴(な)く小鳥(ことり)がかわいい。
The little birds chirping are so cute.

ぴよぴよ is specifically associated with baby chicks (ひよこ) and small birds. It has a cute, childlike connotation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ぴよぴよ鳴(な)く: to chirp (like a chick)

CONTRAST:

  • チュンチュン: sparrow chirping
  • ホーホケキョ: nightingale call
  • コケコッコー: rooster crowing
