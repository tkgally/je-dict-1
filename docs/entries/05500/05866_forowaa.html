フォロワー

ふぉろわー
noun
follower (social media)

Definitions

1. follower (social media)
A person who subscribes to another user's account on social media to see their posts.

Examples

フォロワーが1万人(まんにん)を超(こ)えた。
My followers exceeded 10,000.
フォロワーを増(ふ)やしたい。
I want to increase my followers.
フォロワー数(すう)が多(おお)いインフルエンサーだ。
They're an influencer with a large number of followers.

Notes

SOCIAL MEDIA VOCABULARY:
From English "follower." Used across various social media platforms.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • フォロワー数(すう): follower count
  • フォロワーを増(ふ)やす: to increase followers
  • フォロワーを獲得(かくとく)する: to gain followers

RELATED:

  • フォローする: to follow (someone)
