がっくり

がっくり
adverb, suru verb
dejected; disappointed; crestfallen
1. dejected; disappointed; crestfallen
Describes a sudden loss of energy or spirits due to disappointment or shock. Often accompanied by physical drooping of the shoulders or head.
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちてがっくりした。
I was dejected after failing the exam.
彼(かれ)はがっくりと肩(かた)を落(お)とした。
He dropped his shoulders in disappointment.
結果(けっか)を聞(き)いてがっくりした。
I was dejected when I heard the results.
期待(きたい)していた店(みせ)が休(やす)みで、がっくり来(き)た。
The shop I'd been looking forward to was closed, and I was hit with disappointment.
せっかく準備(じゅんび)したのに中止(ちゅうし)になって、みんながっくりしていた。
Even though we had prepared, it was canceled, and everyone was dejected.
2. suddenly weakening; collapsing
Describes a sudden physical weakening or giving way, such as knees buckling.
膝(ひざ)ががっくりと折(お)れた。
My knees suddenly gave way.
疲(つか)れて足(あし)ががっくりした。
My legs gave out from exhaustion.
長(なが)い階段(かいだん)を上(のぼ)ったら、体(からだ)ががっくりした。
After climbing the long staircase, my body gave out.
病気(びょうき)のあと、急(きゅう)に体力(たいりょく)ががっくり落(お)ちた。
After the illness, my stamina dropped suddenly.
立(た)ち上(あ)がろうとしたら膝(ひざ)ががっくりときて、倒(たお)れそうになった。
When I tried to stand up, my knees buckled and I almost fell over.

がっくり conveys a sudden, visible loss of spirits or strength.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • がっくりする: to feel dejected
  • がっくり来(く)る: to be hit with disappointment
  • 肩(かた)をがっくり落(お)とす: to drop one's shoulders in disappointment

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • しょんぼり: dejected, downcast (more passive)
  • がっかり: disappointed (similar but less physical)
  • 落(お)ち込(こ)む: to feel down
