めそめそ

めそめそ
adverb, verb (suru)
whimpering, sniveling

Definitions

1. whimpering, sniveling, crying softly
Crying in a weak, pitiful manner.

Examples

めそめそ泣(な)いてばかりいるな。
Stop whimpering all the time.
めそめそしないで頑張(がんば)ろう。
Stop crying and let's do our best.
子供(こども)がめそめそしている。
The child is sniveling.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes weak, pitiful crying. Often carries a slightly negative or impatient tone toward the person crying.
