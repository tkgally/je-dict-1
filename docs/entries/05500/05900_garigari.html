1. crunching; scraping
Describes a hard, scratching or crunching sound, like scraping ice or chewing something hard.
氷(こおり)をがりがり削(けず)る。
To scrape the ice with a crunching sound.
犬(いぬ)がドアをがりがりと引(ひ)っ掻(か)いている。
The dog is scratching at the door.
煎餅(せんべい)をがりがり噛(か)んで食(た)べた。
I crunched on the rice cracker.
2. extremely thin; bony; skinny
Describes a person or animal being unhealthily thin, with bones visible.
ダイエットしすぎてがりがりに痩(や)せてしまった。
I dieted too much and became extremely thin.
あの猫(ねこ)はがりがりで心配(しんぱい)だ。
That cat is so skinny that I'm worried.
病気(びょうき)で食(た)べられなくて、がりがりの体(からだ)になってしまった。
Unable to eat because of illness, I became skin and bones.

がりがり is an onomatopoeic word (擬音語(ぎおんご)/擬態語(ぎたいご)) with two distinct meanings.

SENSE 1 — CRUNCHING/SCRAPING SOUND:

Describes a hard, rough scraping or crunching noise.

  • がりがり削(けず)る: to scrape with a crunching sound
  • がりがり噛(か)む: to crunch (hard food)
  • 氷(こおり)をがりがり食(た)べる: to crunch on ice

SENSE 2 — EXTREMELY THIN:

Describes unhealthy thinness where bones are visible. Carries a negative, concerning connotation.

  • がりがりに痩(や)せる: to become skin and bones
  • がりがりの体(からだ): bony, emaciated body
  • がりがりだ: to be very skinny

SIMILAR WORDS (SOUND):

  • ぽりぽり: light, gentle crunching (like crackers)
  • ばりばり: energetic crunching or tearing
  • ごりごり: grinding, grating (harder/deeper sound)

SIMILAR WORDS (THINNESS):

  • 痩(や)せている: thin (neutral)
  • ほっそり: slender (positive nuance)
