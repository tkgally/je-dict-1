遂行(すいこう)

すいこう
noun
accomplishment; execution
1. accomplishment; execution; carrying out
Successfully completing or carrying out a task, duty, or mission.
任務(にんむ)を遂行(すいこう)する。
To carry out a mission.
計画(けいかく)を遂行(すいこう)する能力(のうりょく)がある。
To have the ability to execute a plan.
職務(しょくむ)遂行(すいこう)に全力(ぜんりょく)を尽(つ)くす。
To devote full effort to carrying out one's duties.

遂行(すいこう) means to carry out or execute a task, duty, or plan to completion. It's formal and emphasizes successful accomplishment.

REGISTER:
Formal. Common in business, military, and official contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 任務(にんむ)を遂行(すいこう)する: to carry out a mission
  • 職務(しょくむ)遂行(すいこう): performance of duties
  • 計画(けいかく)を遂行(すいこう)する: to execute a plan
  • 業務(ぎょうむ)遂行(すいこう): carrying out operations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実行(じっこう): execution (more general)
  • 達成(たっせい): achievement (focus on reaching goals)
  • 遂行(すいこう): execution (formal, focus on carrying through)

WORD FORMATION:
遂(すい) (accomplish) + 行(こう) (carry out) = execution/accomplishment

Related Words

Synonym: 実行(じっこう) (execution (general))
Synonym: 達成(たっせい) (achievement)
Synonym: 完遂(かんすい) (carrying through to completion)
