遂行(すいこう)

すいこう
noun
accomplishment; execution
1. accomplishment; execution; carrying out
Successfully completing or carrying out a task, duty, or mission.
任務(にんむ)を遂行(すいこう)する。
To carry out a mission.
計画(けいかく)を遂行(すいこう)する能力(のうりょく)がある。
To have the ability to execute a plan.
職務(しょくむ)遂行(すいこう)に全力(ぜんりょく)を尽(つ)くす。
To devote full effort to carrying out one's duties.

遂行(すいこう) is a formal term for accomplishing or executing tasks, often used in business and military contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 任務(にんむ)遂行(すいこう): mission execution
  • 職務(しょくむ)遂行(すいこう): duty performance
  • 遂行(すいこう)する: to accomplish/execute
