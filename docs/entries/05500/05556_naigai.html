内外(ないがい)

ないがい
noun
inside and outside; domestic and foreign
1. inside and outside
Both the interior and exterior of something.
建物(たてもの)の内外(ないがい)を点検(てんけん)する。
To inspect both the inside and outside of a building.
家(いえ)の内外(ないがい)を掃除(そうじ)した。
I cleaned both the inside and outside of the house.
Sense 1: inside and outside
車(くるま)の内外(ないがい)を洗(あら)った。
I washed both the inside and outside of the car.
Sense 1: inside and outside
会社(かいしゃ)の内外(ないがい)で評判(ひょうばん)が良(よ)い。
They have a good reputation both inside and outside the company.
Sense 1: inside and outside
2. domestic and foreign; home and abroad
Both within a country and internationally.
国(くに)内外(ないがい)から注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めている。
It's attracting attention from both home and abroad.
内外(ないがい)の情勢(じょうせい)を分析(ぶんせき)する。
Analyze domestic and international conditions.
Sense 2: domestic and foreign
内外(ないがい)の企業(きぎょう)が参加(さんか)した。
Both domestic and foreign companies participated.
Sense 2: home and abroad
内外(ないがい)の投資家(とうしか)に人気(にんき)がある。
It's popular among domestic and foreign investors.
Sense 2: domestic and foreign
内外(ないがい)から批判(ひはん)を受(う)けた。
It received criticism from both home and abroad.
Sense 2: home and abroad
3. approximately
About (a certain number); roughly.
参加者(さんかしゃ)は100人(にん)内外(ないがい)だった。
There were approximately 100 participants.
費用(ひよう)は50万円(ごじゅうまんえん)内外(ないがい)だ。
The cost is approximately 500,000 yen.
Sense 3: approximately
1時間(いちじかん)内外(ないがい)で着(つ)く。
It takes approximately one hour to arrive.
Sense 3: approximately
30歳(さんじゅっさい)内外(ないがい)の人(ひと)。
People around 30 years old.
Sense 3: approximately
重(おも)さは1キロ(いちきろ)内外(ないがい)だ。
The weight is approximately one kilogram.
Sense 3: approximately
200名(にひゃくめい)内外(ないがい)が集(あつ)まった。
Approximately 200 people gathered.
Sense 3: approximately

内外(ないがい) is a compound of 内(ない) (inside) and 外(がい) (outside), used in various contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 国(くに)内外(ないがい): domestic and international
  • 社(しゃ)内外(ないがい): inside and outside the company
  • 〜内外(ないがい): approximately (with numbers)
