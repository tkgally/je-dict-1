1. approval, authorization
Official permission or sanction from authorities to do something.
新薬(しんやく)が認可(にんか)された。
The new drug was approved.
認可(にんか)を申請(しんせい)する。
I apply for authorization.
建設(けんせつ)計画(けいかく)がようやく認可(にんか)を受(う)けた。
The construction plan finally received approval.
2. licensing, accreditation
Official recognition that something meets required standards.
認可(にんか)保育園(ほいくえん)に入(はい)れた。
I got into a licensed daycare.
無認可(むにんか)の施設(しせつ)は安全性(あんぜんせい)に問題(もんだい)がある場合(ばあい)がある。
Unlicensed facilities may have safety issues.
この学校(がっこう)は文部科学省(もんぶかがくしょう)の認可(にんか)を受(う)けている。
This school is accredited by the Ministry of Education.

認可(にんか) means official approval or authorization from authorities or governing bodies.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 認可(にんか)する: to approve, to authorize
  • 認可(にんか)を受(う)ける: to receive authorization
  • 認可(にんか)を申請(しんせい)する: to apply for approval
  • 認可(にんか)保育園(ほいくえん): licensed daycare (meets government standards)
  • 無認可(むにんか): unlicensed, unauthorized

JAPANESE CONTEXT:
The distinction between 認可(にんか)保育園(ほいくえん) (licensed daycare) and 無認可(むにんか)保育園(ほいくえん) (unlicensed daycare) is very important for Japanese parents. Licensed facilities meet national standards and receive government subsidies.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 許可(きょか): permission (for specific actions, e.g., building permits)
  • 承認(しょうにん): approval (more general, also used for agreements)
  • 認定(にんてい): certification, official recognition
