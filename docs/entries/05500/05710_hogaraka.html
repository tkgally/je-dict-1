朗(ほが)らか

ほがらか
na-adjective
cheerful, bright

Definitions

1. cheerful, bright, sunny
Having a bright, positive personality; open and pleasant.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は朗(ほが)らかな性格(せいかく)だ。
She has a cheerful personality.
朗(ほが)らかな笑(わら)い声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえる。
I hear cheerful laughter.
朗(ほが)らかに話(はな)す。
She speaks cheerfully.

Notes

USAGE:
朗(ほが)らか describes people with bright, open, optimistic personalities.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 朗(ほが)らかな性格(せいかく): cheerful personality
  • 朗(ほが)らかな笑顔(えがお): bright smile
