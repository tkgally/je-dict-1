1. to catch, to stop
To physically catch or stop something coming toward you.
ボールを受(う)け止(と)める。
To catch the ball.
相手(あいて)のパンチを受(う)け止(と)めた。
I blocked the opponent's punch.
落(お)ちてきた荷物(にもつ)を受(う)け止(と)めた。
I caught the luggage that was falling.
壁(かべ)が水(みず)の力(ちから)を受(う)け止(と)めている。
The wall is holding back the force of the water.
キャッチャーが速(はや)い球(たま)をしっかり受(う)け止(と)めた。
The catcher firmly caught the fast pitch.
2. to accept, to take seriously
To mentally receive and process information, criticism, or feelings.
批判(ひはん)を受(う)け止(と)める。
To take criticism to heart.
彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちを受(う)け止(と)めてあげて。
Please accept his feelings.
この問題(もんだい)を真剣(しんけん)に受(う)け止(と)めるべきだ。
We should take this problem seriously.
子(こ)どもの悩(なや)みを受(う)け止(と)めてあげることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to accept and listen to your child's worries.
先生(せんせい)のアドバイスを素直(すなお)に受(う)け止(と)めて、自分(じぶん)の行動(こうどう)を変(か)えることにした。
I honestly took the teacher's advice to heart and decided to change my behavior.

受(う)け止(と)める means to catch/stop something physically or to accept/process something mentally.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the object

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 受(う)ける (to receive) + 止(と)める (to stop).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ボールを受(う)け止(と)める: to catch a ball
  • 批判(ひはん)を受(う)け止(と)める: to take criticism to heart
  • 現実(げんじつ)を受(う)け止(と)める: to accept reality
  • 真剣(しんけん)に受(う)け止(と)める: to take seriously

ASPECT (ている):
受(う)け止(と)めている indicates an ongoing state of acceptance or understanding.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 受(う)け取(と)る: to receive, to interpret
  • 受(う)け入(い)れる: to accept, to take in

Related Words

Contrast: 受(う)け入(い)れる (to accept, to receive)
