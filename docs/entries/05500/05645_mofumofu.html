もふもふ

もふもふ
adverb, na-adjective, onomatopoeia
fluffy; soft and fuzzy
1. fluffy; soft and fuzzy
Describes something with soft, thick fur or texture that feels pleasant to touch.
この猫(ねこ)、もふもふしている。
This cat is so fluffy.
もふもふの毛布(もうふ)が気持(きも)ちいい。
The fluffy blanket feels nice.
犬(いぬ)のもふもふな毛(け)を触(さわ)りたい。
I want to touch the dog's fluffy fur.

USAGE:
もふもふ is a relatively modern onomatopoeia that became popular on the internet, particularly among pet owners. It describes the pleasant, soft texture of fur or fabric.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Pets (cats, dogs, rabbits)
  • Blankets and soft fabrics
  • Stuffed animals

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • もふもふする: to pet/stroke something fluffy
  • もふもふ感(かん): fluffy feeling
  • もふもふ系(けい): fluffy type (of animals)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ふわふわ: fluffy, light and airy
  • もこもこ: thick and puffy

Related Words
