処置(しょち)

しょち
noun
treatment; measure; action
1. treatment; medical procedure
Medical care or procedure given to a patient.
応急(おうきゅう)処置(しょち)を行(おこな)う。
To perform first aid treatment.
傷(きず)の処置(しょち)をする。
To treat a wound.
2. measure; action; step
Action taken to deal with a situation or problem.
適切(てきせつ)な処置(しょち)を取(と)る。
To take appropriate measures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 処置(しょち)を取(と)る: to take measures
  • 応急(おうきゅう)処置(しょち): first aid, emergency treatment
  • 適切(てきせつ)な処置(しょち): appropriate measures
  • 処置(しょち)室(しつ): treatment room

RELATED TERMS:

  • 措置(そち): measure, step
  • 対処(たいしょ): coping, dealing with
  • 治療(ちりょう): medical treatment
