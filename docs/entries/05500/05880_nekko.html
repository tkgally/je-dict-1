根(ね)っこ

ねっこ
noun
root (of a plant)
1. root (of a plant)
The underground part of a plant that absorbs water and nutrients. Slightly informal/colloquial.
木(き)の根(ね)っこが土(つち)から出(で)ている。
The roots of the tree are sticking out of the ground.
根(ね)っこから引(ひ)き抜(ぬ)いた。
I pulled it out from the root.
雑草(ざっそう)の根(ね)っこを取(と)る。
I remove the roots of the weeds.

根(ね)っこ is a colloquial term for the root of a plant.

WORD FORMATION:
根(ね) (root) + っこ (diminutive/casual suffix)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 根(ね)っこから引(ひ)き抜(ぬ)く: to pull out from the root
  • 根(ね)っこを取(と)る: to remove the roots
  • 木(き)の根(ね)っこ: tree roots

REGISTER:
Slightly informal/colloquial. Use 根(ね) in more formal contexts.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 根(ね): root (standard)
  • 根元(ねもと): base, root area
  • 根(ね)を張(は)る: to take root (figurative)

FIGURATIVE USE:
Can imply the fundamental/deep-rooted part: 問題(もんだい)の根(ね)っこ (the root of a problem).
