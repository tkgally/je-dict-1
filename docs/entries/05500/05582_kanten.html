観点(かんてん)

かんてん
noun
viewpoint; perspective
1. viewpoint; perspective; standpoint
A particular way of considering or looking at something.
この観点(かんてん)は大切(たいせつ)だ。
This perspective is important.
新(あたら)しい観点(かんてん)から見(み)てみよう。
Let's try looking at it from a new perspective.
異(こと)なる観点(かんてん)から考(かんが)える。
To think from a different perspective.
経済(けいざい)の観点(かんてん)から見(み)ると、この計画(けいかく)には問題(もんだい)がある。
From an economic perspective, this plan has problems.
いろいろな観点(かんてん)から問題(もんだい)を考(かんが)えることで、今(いま)までとは違(ちが)う答(こた)えが見(み)つかることがある。
By considering the problem from various perspectives, you may find answers that differ from before.

観点(かんてん) means a viewpoint or perspective from which something is considered or evaluated.

WORD FORMATION:
観(かん) (view, observe) + 点(てん) (point) = viewpoint

KEY PATTERNS:

  • 〜の観点(かんてん)から: from the viewpoint/perspective of ~
  • 〜という観点(かんてん)で: from the perspective that ~
  • 観点(かんてん)から見(み)ると: looking at it from the perspective of

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 観点(かんてん)を変(か)える: to change perspective
  • 新(あたら)しい観点(かんてん): new perspective
  • 異(こと)なる観点(かんてん): different viewpoint
  • 多角的(たかくてき)な観点(かんてん): multiple perspectives
  • 客観的(きゃっかんてき)な観点(かんてん): objective viewpoint

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 視点(してん): point of view (often more personal/subjective)
  • 見地(けんち): standpoint (more formal)
  • 立場(たちば): position, standpoint (emphasizes one's situation)
  • 角度(かくど): angle (literal or figurative)

USAGE:
Commonly used in academic, business, and analytical discussions when examining issues from various angles.
