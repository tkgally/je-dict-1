1. to kneel; to go down on one's knees
To lower oneself onto one's knees, often as a gesture of respect, reverence, or submission.
神様(かみさま)の前(まえ)で跪(ひざまず)く。
To kneel before God.
王様(おうさま)に跪(ひざまず)いて許(ゆる)しを乞(こ)う。
To kneel before the king and beg for forgiveness.
彼(かれ)は彼女(かのじょ)の前(まえ)で跪(ひざまず)いてプロポーズした。
He knelt before her and proposed.

跪(ひざまず)く is a formal verb indicating the act of kneeling, typically associated with showing respect, worship, or making formal requests.

CONJUGATION (godan verb):

  • 跪(ひざまず)く (dictionary form)
  • 跪(ひざまず)かない (negative)
  • 跪(ひざまず)いた (past)
  • 跪(ひざまず)いて (te-form)

USAGE NOTE:
This word is more literary/formal than simply saying 膝(ひざ)をつく (to put one's knees down). It emphasizes the reverent or submissive nature of the action.
