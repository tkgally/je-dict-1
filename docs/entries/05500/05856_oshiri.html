お尻(しり)

おしり
noun
buttocks, bottom
1. buttocks, bottom
The fleshy part of the body that one sits on.
お尻(しり)が痛(いた)い。
My bottom hurts.
赤(あか)ちゃんのお尻(しり)を拭(ふ)く。
I wipe the baby's bottom.
長時間(ちょうじかん)座(すわ)っていたらお尻(しり)が痺(しび)れた。
My bottom went numb from sitting for a long time.

お尻(しり) is the standard word for buttocks in everyday Japanese.

REGISTER:
The お prefix makes this a polite form. The plain form 尻(しり) exists but sounds rougher. お尻(しり) is appropriate in most situations.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お尻(しり)を拭(ふ)く: to wipe one's bottom
  • お尻(しり)を叩(たた)く: to spank
  • お尻(しり)が痛(いた)い: my bottom hurts
  • お尻(しり)に火(ひ)がつく: to be in a tight spot (lit. fire on one's rear)

ALTERNATIVES:

  • 尻(しり): plain form (rougher)
  • 臀部(でんぶ): medical/formal term
  • ヒップ: hips (often used for shape/fashion contexts)
