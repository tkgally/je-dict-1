押(お)し入(い)れる

おしいれる
verb (ichidan)
to push into, to break into, to force one's way in
1. to push into
To forcefully push something or someone into a space.
2. to break into, to force one's way in
To enter a place by force, especially illegally.
荷物(にもつ)を無理(むり)やり押(お)し入(い)れた。
I forcibly pushed the luggage in.
強盗(ごうとう)が家(いえ)に押(お)し入(い)れた。
Robbers broke into the house.
群衆(ぐんしゅう)が建物(たてもの)に押(お)し入(い)れた。
The crowd forced their way into the building.

COMPOUND VERB:
Formed from 押(お)す (to push) + 入(い)れる (to put in). Indicates forceful entry or insertion.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive verb) for sense 1
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive verb) for sense 2 (person as subject)

NOTE:
Do not confuse with 押(お)し入(い)れ (おしいれ), which means "closet" (noun).
