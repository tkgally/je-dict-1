民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)

みんしゅしゅぎ
noun
democracy

Definitions

1. democracy
A political system in which power is vested in the people, who rule either directly or through elected representatives.

Examples

民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)を守(まも)る。
To protect democracy.
民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)国家(こっか)。
A democratic nation.
民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)の基本(きほん)原則(げんそく)。
The basic principles of democracy.

Notes

民主主義 is the standard term for democracy as a political ideology.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 民主的 (みんしゅてき): democratic (adjective)
  • 民主化 (みんしゅか): democratization
  • 直接民主主義: direct democracy
  • 間接民主主義: representative democracy
