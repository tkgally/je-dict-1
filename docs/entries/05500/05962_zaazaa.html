ざあざあ

ざあざあ
adverb
pouring; heavily (rain)

Definitions

1. pouring; heavily falling
Describes the sound and manner of heavy rain falling forcefully. Conveys intensity and volume.

Examples

雨(あめ)がざあざあ降(ふ)っている。
The rain is pouring down.
ざあざあ降(ふ)りの中(なか)を走(はし)って帰(かえ)った。
I ran home through the pouring rain.
外(そと)はざあざあ降(ふ)りだから、出(で)かけるのはやめよう。
It's pouring outside, so let's not go out.

Notes

ざあざあ is an onomatopoeia for heavy, intense rain. The voiced consonant ざ creates a sense of force and volume.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ざあざあ降(ふ)る: to pour (rain)
  • ざあざあ降(ふ)り: pouring rain (noun form)

CONTRAST WITH OTHER RAIN SOUNDS:

  • しとしと: gentle drizzle
  • ぽつぽつ: scattered drops
  • 土砂降(どしゃぶ)り: downpour (more intense)

Related Words

Contrast: しとしと (gentle rain)
