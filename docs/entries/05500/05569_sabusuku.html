サブスク

さぶすく
noun
subscription (service)

Definitions

1. subscription; subscription service
A business model where customers pay a recurring fee to access a product or service.

Examples

音楽(おんがく)のサブスクを使(つか)っている。
I use a music subscription service.
サブスクを解約(かいやく)した。
I canceled my subscription.
最近(さいきん)はサブスクが増(ふ)えてきた。
Subscription services have been increasing recently.

Notes

サブスク is an abbreviation of サブスクリプション (subscription). It became a widely used term with the rise of streaming services.

COMMON SERVICES:

  • 音楽サブスク: music streaming (Spotify, Apple Music)
  • 動画サブスク: video streaming (Netflix, Amazon Prime)
  • 食品サブスク: food subscription boxes
