紹介(しょうかい)する

しょうかいする
verb (suru)
to introduce
1. to introduce (a person)
To present one person to another, typically in a social or professional context.
友達(ともだち)を紹介(しょうかい)します。
Let me introduce my friend.
自己紹介(じこしょうかい)をしてください。
Please introduce yourself.
Common request in Japanese settings
2. to introduce, to present (something)
To present or explain something new, such as a product, concept, or information.
今日(きょう)は新商品(しんしょうひん)を紹介(しょうかい)します。
Today, I will introduce our new product.

紹介(しょうかい)する is used for introducing people to each other or presenting information/products.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for the person/thing being introduced and に for the recipient.

PATTERN:

  • AをBに紹介(しょうかい)する: to introduce A to B
  • Example: 友人(ゆうじん)を両親(りょうしん)に紹介(しょうかい)する (introduce a friend to one's parents)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 自己紹介(じこしょうかい): self-introduction
  • 紹介状(しょうかいじょう): letter of introduction
  • 紹介料(しょうかいりょう): referral fee
  • ご紹介(しょうかい): introduction (polite)

NOTE:
自己紹介(じこしょうかい) is expected in many Japanese social and business situations. It typically includes name, affiliation, and a brief personal detail.
