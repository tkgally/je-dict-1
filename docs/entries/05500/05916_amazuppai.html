1. sweet and sour (taste)
Describes a flavor that combines sweetness and sourness, like certain fruits or sauces.
この苺(いちご)は甘酸(あまず)っぱくておいしい。
These strawberries are sweet and sour and delicious.
甘酸(あまず)っぱいソースで鶏肉(とりにく)を食(た)べた。
I ate chicken with sweet and sour sauce.
このみかんは甘酸(あまず)っぱくて美味(おい)しい。
This mandarin orange is sweet and sour and delicious.
2. bittersweet (emotion)
Describes a nostalgic or sentimental feeling that combines happiness and sadness, often associated with youthful memories.
甘酸(あまず)っぱい青春(せいしゅん)の思(おも)い出(で)。
Bittersweet memories of youth.
初恋(はつこい)の甘酸(あまず)っぱい記憶(きおく)が蘇(よみがえ)った。
The bittersweet memories of my first love came back.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)の写真(しゃしん)を見(み)ると、甘酸(あまず)っぱい気持(きも)ちになる。
When I look at graduation photos, I get a bittersweet feeling.

甘酸(あまず)っぱい is an i-adjective combining 甘(あま)い (sweet) and 酸(す)っぱい (sour). It has both a literal taste meaning and a figurative emotional meaning.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 甘酸(あまず)っぱく
  • Past: 甘酸(あまず)っぱかった

TASTE USAGE:

  • 甘酸(あまず)っぱいソース: sweet and sour sauce
  • 甘酸(あまず)っぱい果物(くだもの): sweet and sour fruit
  • Common with berries, citrus, and vinegar-based dishes

EMOTIONAL USAGE:

Describes bittersweet or nostalgic feelings, especially related to:

  • 青春(せいしゅん): youth
  • 初恋(はつこい): first love
  • 思(おも)い出(で): memories

This figurative usage is very common in literature and everyday conversation. The emotional sense evokes a mix of fondness and wistfulness.
