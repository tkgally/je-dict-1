信頼性(しんらいせい)

しんらいせい
noun
reliability; trustworthiness
1. reliability; trustworthiness
The quality of being reliable and dependable; the degree to which something can be trusted.
このデータの信頼性(しんらいせい)は高(たか)い。
The reliability of this data is high.
製品(せいひん)の信頼性(しんらいせい)を向上(こうじょう)させる。
To improve the reliability of products.
情報(じょうほう)の信頼性(しんらいせい)を確認(かくにん)する。
To verify the reliability of information.

信頼性(しんらいせい) is formed from 信頼(しんらい) (trust) + 性(せい) (nature, quality). It's commonly used in technical and academic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信頼性(しんらいせい)が高(たか)い/低い: high/low reliability
  • 信頼性(しんらいせい)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure reliability
  • 信頼性(しんらいせい)試験(しけん): reliability test
