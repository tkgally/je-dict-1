にやにや

にやにや
adverb, verb (suru)
grinning, smirking

Definitions

1. grinning, smirking
Smiling in a knowing, often mischievous or self-satisfied way.

Examples

何(なに)をにやにやしているの？
What are you smirking about?
にやにやしながら話(はな)している。
He's talking with a grin on his face.
にやにや笑(わら)いが気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い。
That smirk is creepy.

Notes

ONOMATOPOEIA:
Describes a sly or knowing smile, often with negative connotations.

CONTRAST:

  • にこにこ: smiling pleasantly (positive)
  • にたにた: grinning unpleasantly (more negative than にやにや)
