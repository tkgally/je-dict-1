地元紙(じもとし)

じもとし
noun
local newspaper
1. local newspaper; regional paper
A newspaper that covers news of a particular local area — a prefecture, city, or community. Contrasted with national newspapers (全国紙(ぜんこくし)). Used in news, media, and journalism contexts.
地元紙(じもとし)を読(よ)む。
To read the local newspaper.
この事件(じけん)は地元紙(じもとし)で大(おお)きく報(ほう)じられた。
This incident was prominently reported in the local newspaper.
地元紙(じもとし)には、全国紙(ぜんこくし)には載(の)らない細(こま)かな情報(じょうほう)が載(の)っている。
Local papers carry detailed information you won't find in the national newspapers.
祖母(そぼ)は毎朝(まいあさ)地元紙(じもとし)を読(よ)むのが日課(にっか)で、地域(ちいき)のイベントはすべてそこで知(し)るらしい。
My grandmother's daily routine is reading the local paper every morning, and she apparently learns about all community events that way.

Composed of 地元(じもと) (local area, home area) and 紙(し) (paper — here, newspaper). The 紙(し) of this word and many other newspaper names is the same character used for "paper" but refers specifically to a newspaper publication.

Japan has a strong local newspaper tradition. Prefecture-level papers such as the 北海道新聞(ほっかいどうしんぶん) and the 中日新聞(ちゅうにちしんぶん) have very high readership within their regions and are the main source of local news.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地元紙(じもとし)に載(の)る: to appear in the local paper
  • 地元紙(じもとし)で取(と)り上(あ)げられる: to be covered by the local paper
  • 地元紙(じもとし)の記者(きしゃ): a reporter for a local paper
  • 地元紙(じもとし)を購読(こうどく)する: to subscribe to a local paper

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全国紙(ぜんこくし): national newspaper — the direct antonym; circulated throughout Japan (e.g., 朝日新聞(あさひしんぶん), 読売新聞(よみうりしんぶん))
  • 地方紙(ちほうし): regional newspaper — a near-synonym of 地元紙(じもとし); slightly more formal and used in media-industry contexts
  • 業界紙(ぎょうかいし): trade paper, industry paper — covers a specific industry rather than a region
  • 機関紙(きかんし): organ paper, house organ — published by an organization for its members
