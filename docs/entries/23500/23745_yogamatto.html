ヨガマット

よがまっと
noun
yoga mat
1. yoga mat
A thin, non-slip mat used during yoga practice to provide cushioning for the body and grip for the hands and feet. Also widely used for general home stretching, pilates, and core exercises.
ヨガマットを床(ゆか)に広(ひろ)げた。
I spread out the yoga mat on the floor.
毎朝(まいあさ)ヨガマットの上(うえ)でストレッチをしている。
I stretch on a yoga mat every morning.
厚(あつ)めのヨガマットを使(つか)うと膝(ひざ)への負担(ふたん)が軽減(けいげん)される。
Using a thicker yoga mat reduces the strain on your knees.

Widely sold in Japan at sports shops, 100-yen stores, and online retailers. Commonly used at home even by people who don't practice yoga formally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ヨガマットを敷(し)く: to lay out a yoga mat
  • ヨガマットを広(ひろ)げる: to spread out a yoga mat
  • ヨガマットを丸(まる)める: to roll up a yoga mat
  • 厚(あつ)めのヨガマット: a thicker yoga mat
  • 滑(すべ)り止(ど)めのヨガマット: a non-slip yoga mat

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • マット: mat — the generic term; can refer to any kind of mat (doormat, bath mat, exercise mat)
  • トレーニングマット: training mat — a broader term for exercise mats, often thicker than ヨガマット
  • ピラティスマット: pilates mat — typically thicker than a yoga mat for spine support

CULTURAL NOTE:
ヨガ has become a mainstream fitness activity in Japan, with many studios and home-practice followers, so ヨガマット is a household item for a significant portion of the population.
