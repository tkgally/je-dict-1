総務部(そうむぶ)

そうむぶ
noun
general affairs department; administration department
1. general affairs department; administration department
The department within a company or organization responsible for general administrative matters — office management, facilities, company-wide events, supplies, and other functions not handled by specialized departments.
総務部(そうむぶ)で働(はたら)く。
To work in the general affairs department.
備品(びひん)の購入(こうにゅう)は総務部(そうむぶ)が担当(たんとう)している。
The general affairs department is in charge of purchasing office supplies.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)の歓迎会(かんげいかい)は総務部(そうむぶ)が企画(きかく)した。
The welcome party for new employees was organized by the general affairs department.
総務部(そうむぶ)は、人事(じんじ)、労務(ろうむ)、施設(しせつ)管理(かんり)など幅広(はばひろ)い業務(ぎょうむ)を担当(たんとう)している。
The general affairs department handles a wide range of duties including personnel, labor relations, and facilities management.

Compound of 総務(そうむ) ('general affairs') and 部(ぶ) ('department; division'). The 部(ぶ) suffix is standard for major departmental divisions within companies: 営業部(えいぎょうぶ) ('sales department'), 人事部(じんじぶ) ('HR department'), 経理部(けいりぶ) ('accounting department').

USAGE:
A standard department name in Japanese companies, government offices, schools, and other organizations. The scope of the department varies by organization, but typically covers general administrative functions that don't fall under a specialized department. In smaller companies it may also absorb HR and accounting duties.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 総務部(そうむぶ)で働(はたら)く: to work in the general affairs department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ)長(ちょう): head of the general affairs department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ)に配属(はいぞく)される: to be assigned to the general affairs department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ)所属(しょぞく): affiliated with the general affairs department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ)の担当(たんとう): in charge on behalf of the general affairs department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ)に問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to make an inquiry to the general affairs department

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 総務(そうむ): general affairs — the field itself; can also refer to the department in abbreviation
  • 庶務(しょむ): general office work — similar but more clerical; in older or smaller organizations
  • 人事部(じんじぶ): HR department — handles hiring, evaluation, personnel records
  • 経理部(けいりぶ): accounting department — handles bookkeeping and finance
  • 管理部(かんりぶ): management / administration department — sometimes used for an umbrella of administrative functions

CULTURAL NOTE:
The scope of 総務(そうむ) in Japanese companies often includes tasks that in Western companies might be split among HR, facilities, and administration. It frequently plans company events, handles relations with shareholders and auditors, and manages internal communications.
