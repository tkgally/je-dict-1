集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)

しゅうちゅうごうう
noun
torrential downpour, localized heavy rainfall
1. torrential downpour — extremely heavy rain concentrated in a small area over a short time, often causing floods and landslides
Very heavy rain that falls over a limited area for a short period of time, often a few hours. Common in Japan during the rainy season (梅雨(つゆ)) and typhoon season, and a frequent cause of flash floods, landslides, and disaster warnings.
集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)で道路(どうろ)が冠水(かんすい)した。
The torrential downpour flooded the roads.
九州(きゅうしゅう)地方(ちほう)で集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)が発生(はっせい)した。
A torrential downpour occurred in the Kyushu region.
昨夜(さくや)の集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)で土砂崩(どしゃくず)れが起(お)きた。
Last night's torrential downpour caused a landslide.
近年(きんねん)、温暖化(おんだんか)の影響(えいきょう)で集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)が増(ふ)えているとされている。
In recent years, torrential downpours are said to be increasing due to global warming.

Compound of 集中(しゅうちゅう) ('concentration') and 豪雨(ごうう) ('heavy rain, downpour'). Literally 'concentrated heavy rain.'

USAGE:

  • Emphasizes that the rain is concentrated in both time and space — falling very hard on a limited area for a short stretch, rather than spreading out over a wide region.
  • A frequent term in weather forecasts, news reports, and disaster alerts; often appears in headlines about flash floods (鉄砲水(てっぽうみず)) and landslides (土砂災害(どしゃさいがい)).
  • The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) uses the related term 線状(せんじょう)降水帯(こうすいたい) ('linear rainband') for a specific pattern that causes repeated 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)が発生(はっせい)する: for a torrential downpour to occur
  • 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)に見舞(みま)われる: to be hit by a torrential downpour
  • 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう)による被害(ひがい): damage caused by a torrential downpour
  • 局地的(きょくちてき)な集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう): a localized torrential downpour

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 豪雨(ごうう): heavy rain, downpour — the broader term, not necessarily localized.
  • 大雨(おおあめ): heavy rain — an everyday term, less intense in connotation than 豪雨(ごうう).
  • ゲリラ{豪雨(ごうう)|げりらごうう}: 'guerrilla downpour' — a journalistic term for a sudden, unpredictable, intense rainfall; overlaps heavily with 集中豪雨(しゅうちゅうごうう).
  • 夕立(ゆうだち): evening shower — a brief summer afternoon/evening shower, typically much lighter.
