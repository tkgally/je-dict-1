広報活動(こうほうかつどう)

こうほうかつどう
noun
public relations activities; PR activities; publicity work
1. public relations activities; publicity work carried out by a company, government agency, or organization
The range of activities an organization undertakes to communicate information about itself or its services to the public — press releases, press conferences, social media posts, information pamphlets, advertising campaigns, and community outreach. The focus is on building understanding and a favorable image rather than on direct sales.
広報活動(こうほうかつどう)に力(ちから)を入(い)れている。
We are putting effort into our public relations activities.
市(し)は子育(こそだ)て支援(しえん)制度(せいど)の広報活動(こうほうかつどう)を強化(きょうか)している。
The city is stepping up its publicity activities for the childcare support program.
SNSの普及(ふきゅう)によって、中小企業(ちゅうしょうきぎょう)でも少(すく)ない予算(よさん)で効果的(こうかてき)な広報活動(こうほうかつどう)ができるようになった。
With the spread of social media, even small and medium-sized companies have become able to conduct effective PR activities on a modest budget.

Compound of 広報(こうほう) ('public relations; publicity') and 活動(かつどう) ('activity; activities'). 広報(こうほう) alone is often used as a department name (広報{部(ぶ)|こうほうぶ} 'PR department'); 広報活動(こうほうかつどう) refers to the activities carried out by such a department.

USAGE:

  • Contrast with 宣伝(せんでん) ('advertising; publicity') and マーケティング ('marketing'): 広報活動(こうほうかつどう) emphasizes neutral information dissemination and image-building, while 宣伝(せんでん) and マーケティング more directly aim at sales or persuasion.
  • Used by both private companies and public organizations. Local governments in particular refer to their newsletter distribution, town-hall postings, and information campaigns as 広報活動(こうほうかつどう).
  • Often paired with purposes: 認知度(にんちど)向上(こうじょう) ('raising awareness'), イメージ戦略(せんりゃく) ('image strategy'), 情報(じょうほう)発信(はっしん) ('information dissemination').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 広報活動(こうほうかつどう)を行(おこな)う: to conduct PR activities
  • 広報活動(こうほうかつどう)に力(ちから)を入(い)れる: to put effort into PR
  • 広報活動(こうほうかつどう)を強化(きょうか)する: to strengthen/expand PR activities
  • 積極的(せっきょくてき)な広報活動(こうほうかつどう): proactive PR activities
  • 企業(きぎょう)の広報活動(こうほうかつどう): corporate PR activities

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 広報(こうほう): public relations; PR — the shorter form; also refers to the PR department itself.
  • 宣伝(せんでん): advertising; publicity — more sales-oriented and persuasive in tone.
  • PR: PR — the loanword/abbreviation, common in company slogans and recruitment ('自己(じこ)PR' = 'self-promotion').
  • 広告(こうこく): advertisement; advertising — a paid message with clear commercial intent; narrower than 広報活動(こうほうかつどう).
