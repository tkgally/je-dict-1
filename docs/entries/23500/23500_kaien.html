1. start of a performance; curtain up; showtime
The moment when a theatrical show, concert, or other live performance begins. Used for scheduled start times and the actual commencement of performances.
開演(かいえん)は午後(ごご)7時(じ)です。
The performance starts at 7 PM.
開演(かいえん)時間(じかん)に遅(おく)れないように早(はや)めに出(で)かけた。
I left early so I wouldn't be late for the start of the show.
開演(かいえん)のベルが鳴(な)って、客席(きゃくせき)が静(しず)かになった。
The bell rang for the start of the performance, and the audience fell silent.
開演(かいえん)後(ご)の入場(にゅうじょう)はご遠慮(えんりょ)ください。
Please refrain from entering after the performance has started.

Composed of 開(かい) (open, begin) and 演(えん) (performance, play). The word is specific to live performances — theater, concerts, dance recitals, and similar events. You will encounter it on tickets, venue announcements, and event listings.

Japanese tickets and programs typically distinguish between 開場(かいじょう) (doors open) and 開演(かいえん) (showtime), often listed as a pair: e.g., 開場(かいじょう) 18:00 / 開演(かいえん) 18:30.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 開演(かいえん)時間(じかん): showtime, curtain time
  • 開演(かいえん)前(まえ): before the performance starts
  • 開演(かいえん)後(ご): after the performance starts
  • 開演(かいえん)のベル: the curtain-up bell

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開場(かいじょう): doors open — when the venue opens for seating, before the performance itself
  • 開幕(かいまく): raising of the curtain; opening (of a season/tournament) — can overlap with 開演(かいえん) but also applies to sports seasons and festivals
  • 終演(しゅうえん): end of a performance — the antonym of 開演(かいえん)
