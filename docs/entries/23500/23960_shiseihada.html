脂性肌(しせいはだ)

しせいはだ
noun
oily skin; greasy skin type
1. oily skin; a skin type that produces excess sebum
One of the standard skin type categories in Japanese cosmetics and dermatology. Describes skin that secretes a large amount of sebum, giving it a shiny appearance and making it prone to clogged pores and breakouts. Contrasts with 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ) ('dry skin') and 混合肌(こんごうはだ) ('combination skin').
私(わたし)は脂性肌(しせいはだ)だ。
I have oily skin.
脂性肌(しせいはだ)の人(ひと)に向(む)いた化粧水(けしょうすい)を使(つか)っている。
I'm using a toner designed for people with oily skin.
脂性肌(しせいはだ)の方(かた)は、洗顔後(せんがんご)に保湿(ほしつ)を怠(おこた)らないことが大切(たいせつ)だと美容(びよう)雑誌(ざっし)に書(か)いてあった。
A beauty magazine said that for people with oily skin, it's important not to neglect moisturizing after washing your face.

Compound of 脂性(しせい) ('oily; greasy disposition') and 肌(はだ) ('skin'). One of the five standard skin types commonly cited in Japanese beauty and skincare contexts: 普通肌(ふつうはだ) (normal), 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ) (dry), 脂性肌(しせいはだ) (oily), 混合肌(こんごうはだ) (combination), 敏感肌(びんかんはだ) (sensitive).

USAGE:

  • Frequently appears in skincare product labels and reviews: 脂性肌(しせいはだ)用(よう) ('for oily skin'), 脂性肌(しせいはだ)に向(む)く ('suited to oily skin').
  • Can describe a part of the body (especially the T-zone) or the whole face.
  • Also read as {あぶらしょうはだ} in some dictionaries; the on-reading {しせいはだ} is the standard in cosmetics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脂性肌(しせいはだ)用(よう)の化粧品(けしょうひん): cosmetics for oily skin
  • 脂性肌(しせいはだ)に向(む)く: suited to oily skin
  • 脂性肌(しせいはだ)対策(たいさく): oily skin care measures
  • 脂性肌(しせいはだ)の悩(なや)み: concerns of those with oily skin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ): dry skin — the opposite skin type.
  • 混合肌(こんごうはだ): combination skin — oily in some areas (often the T-zone) and dry in others.
  • 普通肌(ふつうはだ): normal skin — balanced skin, neither oily nor dry.
  • 敏感肌(びんかんはだ): sensitive skin — skin that reacts easily to irritation.
  • オイリー肌(おいりーはだ): oily skin (loanword-based variant) — common in fashion magazines and cosmetic marketing.
