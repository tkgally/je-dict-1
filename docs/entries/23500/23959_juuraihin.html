従来品(じゅうらいひん)

じゅうらいひん
noun
previous model; existing product; conventional product
1. previous model; existing product; conventional product
A product that has been on sale or in use until now, implicitly being contrasted with a new, improved, or different version. Typical in product marketing, press releases, and technical comparisons where a manufacturer emphasizes what the new product does better than the previous lineup.
従来品(じゅうらいひん)より軽(かる)くなった。
It has become lighter than the previous model.
新製品(しんせいひん)は従来品(じゅうらいひん)と互換性(ごかんせい)がある。
The new product is compatible with the previous model.
今回(こんかい)の改良(かいりょう)モデルは、従来品(じゅうらいひん)と比(くら)べて消費(しょうひ)電力(でんりょく)を約(やく)30パーセント削減(さくげん)することに成功(せいこう)した。
This improved model has succeeded in reducing power consumption by about 30 percent compared to the previous model.

Compound of 従来(じゅうらい) ('up to now; conventional; existing') and 品(ひん) ('product; item'). Implies a comparison — the word is almost always used when contrasting a previous product with a new, different, or improved one.

USAGE:

  • Standard vocabulary in advertising copy, product brochures, and technical documents.
  • Often appears with 比(くら)べて ('compared to') or より ('than'): 従来品(じゅうらいひん)と比(くら)べて, 従来品(じゅうらいひん)より.
  • Not inherently negative about the older product; simply positions it as the baseline.
  • For 'conventional methods' or 'traditional approaches' more generally, use 従来(じゅうらい)の方法(ほうほう) / 従来(じゅうらい)のやり{方(かた)}, not 従来品(じゅうらいひん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 従来品(じゅうらいひん)と比(くら)べて: compared to the previous model
  • 従来品(じゅうらいひん)より軽(かる)い: lighter than the previous model
  • 従来品(じゅうらいひん)との違(ちが)い: the difference from the previous model
  • 従来品(じゅうらいひん)の後継(こうけい)モデル: successor to the previous model
  • 従来品(じゅうらいひん)と互換性(ごかんせい)がある: to be compatible with the previous model

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 既存品(きそんひん): existing product — emphasizes that the item is already on the market; overlaps with 従来品(じゅうらいひん) but focuses on current existence rather than implicit comparison.
  • 旧型(きゅうがた): old model — similar in meaning but sounds more colloquial and can feel more clearly 'outdated'.
  • 新製品(しんせいひん): new product — the counterpart that 従来品(じゅうらいひん) is typically contrasted with.
  • 最新型(さいしんがた): latest model — emphasizes being the newest version.
