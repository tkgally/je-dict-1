七夕祭(たなばたまつ)り

たなばたまつり
noun
Tanabata festival; Star Festival
1. Tanabata festival; Star Festival
A Japanese summer festival held around July 7 (or August 7 in regions following the lunar calendar) to celebrate the once-a-year meeting of the legendary weaver-star 織姫(おりひめ) and cowherd-star 彦星(ひこぼし) across the Milky Way. People write wishes on colorful strips of paper (短冊(たんざく)) and hang them from bamboo branches.
明日(あした)は七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りだ。
Tomorrow is the Tanabata festival.
保育園(ほいくえん)で七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りをした。
We held a Tanabata festival at the preschool.
仙台(せんだい)の七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りは日本三大(にほんさんだい)七夕(たなばた)の一(ひと)つとして知(し)られている。
The Sendai Tanabata Festival is known as one of the three great Tanabata festivals of Japan.

Composed of 七夕(たなばた) (the Tanabata day/legend) + 祭(まつ)り ("festival"). The name 七夕(たなばた) itself is an ancient irregular reading: the characters literally mean "seventh evening," but the reading comes from an older Japanese weaving-goddess ritual called 棚機(たなばた).

USAGE:

  • Celebrated nationwide, but dates vary: most areas hold it on July 7 (solar calendar), while Sendai and parts of Tohoku hold it on August 7 (roughly matching the old lunar-calendar date).
  • Typical customs include writing wishes on 短冊(たんざく) (colorful paper strips) and tying them to bamboo branches, eating seasonal foods, and watching parades with elaborate streamers.
  • Famous large-scale celebrations include the Sendai, Hiratsuka (Kanagawa), and Anjō (Aichi) festivals, which attract millions of visitors.
  • Commonly celebrated in small ways at preschools, elementary schools, and shopping streets, where children write wishes on 短冊(たんざく).

CULTURAL BACKGROUND:

  • The legend: the weaver-princess 織姫(おりひめ) (the star Vega) and the cowherd 彦星(ひこぼし) (Altair) were banished to opposite sides of the 天(あま)の川(がわ) (Milky Way) and are allowed to meet only once a year on the seventh night of the seventh month.
  • If it rains on Tanabata, the two are said to be unable to meet — rain on this day is called 催涙雨(さいるいう) ("tears of sorrow rain").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りが開(ひら)かれる: a Tanabata festival is held
  • 七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りに行(い)く: to go to the Tanabata festival
  • 仙台(せんだい)七夕祭(たなばたまつ)り: Sendai Tanabata Festival
  • 七夕祭(たなばたまつ)りの飾(かざ)り: Tanabata festival decorations
  • 短冊(たんざく)に願(ねが)い事(ごと)を書(か)く: to write a wish on a paper strip (typical Tanabata activity)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 七夕(たなばた): Tanabata (the day/holiday)
  • 短冊(たんざく): strips of colorful paper for wishes
  • 織姫(おりひめ): Orihime (weaver-star, Vega)
  • 彦星(ひこぼし): Hikoboshi (cowherd-star, Altair)
  • 天(あま)の川(がわ): Milky Way
  • 笹(ささ): bamboo grass (used for hanging the wishes)
