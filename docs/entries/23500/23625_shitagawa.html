下側(したがわ)

したがわ
noun
underside; lower side; bottom side
1. underside; lower side; bottom side
The lower side or underside of something — the surface, region, or portion that is oriented downward, or that lies below a reference point. Contrasts with 上側(うわがわ) (upper side).
箱(はこ)の下側(したがわ)を見(み)て。
Look at the underside of the box.
スイッチは本体(ほんたい)の下側(したがわ)についている。
The switch is on the underside of the unit.
写真(しゃしん)の下側(したがわ)に撮影日(さつえいび)が小(ちい)さく印字(いんじ)されている。
The date the photo was taken is printed in small characters on the bottom side of the picture.

Composed of 下(した) ("lower, below") + 側(がわ) ("side"). The 〜側(がわ) suffix forms "side" nouns from direction/position words: 上側(うわがわ) (upper side), 右側(みぎがわ) (right side), 左側(ひだりがわ) (left side), 内側(うちがわ) (inside), 外側(そとがわ) (outside).

USAGE:

  • Describes either the lower surface of a single object (the underside of a box, the bottom of a shelf) or the lower region of a larger area (the bottom part of a page or image).
  • Typically used with の to link to what it is the underside of: テーブル(てーぶる)の下側(したがわ) (the underside of the table).
  • Also used relatively, to describe what lies below a reference line or object: 道路(どうろ)の下側(したがわ) (below the road).
  • Near-synonym of 下面(かめん) (lower surface, lower face), but 下側(したがわ) is more conversational and more about orientation than surface geometry.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下側(したがわ)にある(ある): to be on the underside / lower side
  • 下側(したがわ)を見(み)る: to look at the underside
  • 下側(したがわ)から: from the underside / from below
  • 下側(したがわ)の面(めん): the lower surface
  • 画面(がめん)の下側(したがわ): the lower part of the screen

ANTONYM:

  • 上側(うわがわ): upper side, top side — the opposite position or surface.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下(した): below, bottom — the basic positional noun; more general and can be used where 下側(したがわ) would feel heavy.
  • 裏側(うらがわ): back side, reverse side — emphasizes "the other side / behind," not "lower."
  • 底(そこ): bottom — the innermost/lowest interior, as of a container.
  • 下面(かめん): lower surface, underside — more technical/written register.
