増幅器(ぞうふくき)

ぞうふくき
noun
amplifier (electronic)
1. amplifier — an electronic device that increases the strength of a signal, especially an audio or radio-frequency signal
An electronic device or circuit that increases the power, voltage, or current of a signal. Used in audio equipment, radio receivers and transmitters, instrumentation, and many other devices. In everyday audio contexts, the loanword アンプ is far more common, but 増幅器(ぞうふくき) remains the standard technical term.
音声(おんせい)信号(しんごう)を増幅器(ぞうふくき)で強(つよ)める。
To boost the audio signal with an amplifier.
高(たか)性能(せいのう)の増幅器(ぞうふくき)を使(つか)っている。
I'm using a high-performance amplifier.
この測定(そくてい)装置(そうち)には小型(こがた)の増幅器(ぞうふくき)が内蔵(ないぞう)されている。
This measuring instrument has a small amplifier built in.
増幅器(ぞうふくき)は弱(よわ)い電気(でんき)信号(しんごう)を必要(ひつよう)な大(おお)きさまで増幅(ぞうふく)するための装置(そうち)である。
An amplifier is a device for boosting a weak electrical signal up to a required level.

Compound of 増幅(ぞうふく) ('amplification') and 器(き) ('device, instrument'). Literally 'amplifying device.'

USAGE:

  • The standard technical term in electronics and physics for an amplifying device or circuit.
  • In everyday audio/hi-fi contexts, the katakana loanword アンプ (from 'amplifier') is much more common; 増幅器(ぞうふくき) appears mainly in textbooks, spec sheets, and technical writing.
  • Often modified by type: 音声(おんせい)増幅器(ぞうふくき) ('audio amplifier'), 電力(でんりょく)増幅器(ぞうふくき) ('power amplifier'), 高周波(こうしゅうは)増幅器(ぞうふくき) ('radio-frequency amplifier').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信号(しんごう)を増幅器(ぞうふくき)で増幅(ぞうふく)する: to amplify a signal with an amplifier
  • 増幅器(ぞうふくき)を内蔵(ないぞう)する: to have a built-in amplifier
  • 高(こう)利得(りとく)の増幅器(ぞうふくき): a high-gain amplifier
  • 音声(おんせい)増幅器(ぞうふくき): audio amplifier

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • アンプ: amp, amplifier — the everyday katakana term, especially for audio.
  • 増幅(ぞうふく): amplification (as a noun / suru-verb) — the action or process rather than the device.
  • 拡声器(かくせいき): loudspeaker, megaphone — a related but distinct device; contains an amplifier but also speakers.
  • 受信機(じゅしんき): receiver — often contains amplifier circuits but is a complete device for receiving signals.
