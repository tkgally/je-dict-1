輸液(ゆえき)

ゆえき
noun
intravenous infusion; IV drip; infusion fluid
1. intravenous infusion; IV drip; infusion fluid
The medical procedure of administering large volumes of fluid (saline, glucose, electrolytes, nutrients, or medication) into a patient's vein over a sustained period, or the fluid itself that is administered. Used to correct dehydration, restore electrolyte balance, deliver drugs, or provide nutrition to patients who cannot eat or drink.
患者(かんじゃ)に輸液(ゆえき)を始(はじ)めた。
We started an IV infusion on the patient.
脱水(だっすい)症状(しょうじょう)があったので輸液(ゆえき)治療(ちりょう)を受(う)けた。
I had dehydration symptoms, so I received IV therapy.
食事(しょくじ)が取(と)れない間(あいだ)は、栄養(えいよう)を輸液(ゆえき)で補(おぎな)う必要(ひつよう)がある。
While the patient cannot eat, nutrition has to be supplemented through IV infusion.
術後(じゅつご)管理(かんり)では、輸液(ゆえき)の種類(しゅるい)と速度(そくど)を患者(かんじゃ)の状態(じょうたい)に合(あ)わせて細(こま)かく調整(ちょうせい)する。
In postoperative care, the type and rate of IV infusion are finely adjusted to match the patient's condition.

Composed of 輸(ゆ) (to transport; to convey, as in 輸送(ゆそう) 'transport') and 液(えき) (liquid). Literally 'transported liquid,' the term covers both the act of infusion and the bagged solution itself.

USAGE:
A technical medical term used by doctors, nurses, pharmacists, and in patient charts. The everyday word a patient is more likely to use is 点滴(てんてき) (drip), which refers to the same procedure from the patient's point of view. 輸液(ゆえき) is preferred when discussing the formulation, volume, or rate (輸液(ゆえき)速度(そくど) 'infusion rate'), while 点滴(てんてき) is preferred when describing the bedside experience. Distinct from 輸血(ゆけつ), which is the transfusion of blood specifically.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 輸液(ゆえき)を行(おこな)う: to perform an infusion
  • 輸液(ゆえき)療法(りょうほう): infusion therapy
  • 輸液(ゆえき)ポンプ: infusion pump
  • 維持(いじ)輸液(ゆえき): maintenance infusion (to maintain fluid balance)
  • 高(こう)カロリー輸液(ゆえき): high-calorie (parenteral) nutrition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 点滴(てんてき): IV drip — the everyday word; same procedure, less technical
  • 輸血(ゆけつ): blood transfusion — infusion of blood specifically, not general fluid
  • 注射(ちゅうしゃ): injection — a one-time push of medication, much smaller volume than 輸液(ゆえき)
  • 生理食塩水(せいりしょくえんすい): physiological saline — the most common type of IV fluid
