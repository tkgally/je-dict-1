1. ventilation; sending air; blowing air (without heating or cooling)
The act of sending a stream of air through a space or machine — for ventilation, cooling, or drying. In household contexts, particularly the "fan" (air-circulation only) mode on an air conditioner, which moves air without heating or cooling it.
送風(そうふう)モードにする。
To switch it to fan mode.
エアコンを送風(そうふう)にして部屋(へや)の空気(くうき)を回(まわ)す。
I set the air conditioner to fan mode to circulate the air in the room.
工場(こうじょう)では大型(おおがた)のファンで常(つね)に送風(そうふう)している。
In the factory, large fans are constantly blowing air for ventilation.
冷房(れいぼう)を切(き)った後(あと)に少(すこ)し送風(そうふう)すると、エアコンの内部(ないぶ)の湿気(しっけ)が減(へ)ってカビが防(ふせ)げる。
Running the fan briefly after turning off the cooling reduces humidity inside the air conditioner and prevents mold.

Composed of 送(そう) (send, transmit) and 風(ふう) (wind, air). Refers to sending a current of air — either for ventilation, cooling, drying, or driving a process such as combustion. Common in product manuals, HVAC contexts, and factory operations.

USAGE:

  • As a noun: 送風(そうふう)モード (fan mode, ventilation mode)
  • As a suru-verb: 部屋(へや)に送風(そうふう)する (to blow air into the room)
  • Often paired with 冷房(れいぼう) (cooling) and 暖房(だんぼう) (heating) in discussions of air conditioning

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送風(そうふう)モード: fan / ventilation mode
  • 送風(そうふう)機(き): a blower, fan unit
  • 送風(そうふう)ファン: a ventilation fan
  • 送風(そうふう)口(ぐち): air outlet, vent

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 換気(かんき): ventilation (exchanging air) — emphasizes replacing stale air with fresh air; 送風(そうふう) can just recirculate existing air
  • 通気(つうき): air passage, airflow — focuses on air being able to move through a space; more passive
  • 冷房(れいぼう): air cooling — opposite of 暖房(だんぼう); produces cooled air, not plain airflow
  • 排気(はいき): exhaust, expelling air — sending air out rather than in
