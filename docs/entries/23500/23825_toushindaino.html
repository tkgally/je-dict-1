等身大(とうしんだい)の

とうしんだいの
no-adjective
life-size; life-sized; true-to-life
1. life-size; life-sized; in the same dimensions as a real human body
Describes an object — most often a statue, doll, poster, or mannequin — that is made in the exact proportions and size of a real human. Literally 'in the size of one's (whole) body'.
等身大(とうしんだい)の人形(にんぎょう)。
A life-size doll.
店内(てんない)に等身大(とうしんだい)の銅像(どうぞう)が立(た)っている。
A life-size bronze statue stands inside the shop.
駅前(えきまえ)に設置(せっち)された等身大(とうしんだい)のパネルと記念(きねん)写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る観光客(かんこうきゃく)でにぎわっていた。
The area in front of the station was crowded with tourists taking commemorative photos with the life-size panel set up there.
2. true-to-life; realistic; unembellished
Figurative use: describes a portrayal or self-image that presents a person honestly, without exaggeration or idealization. Common in talking about fiction, memoirs, and public personas.
等身大(とうしんだい)の主人公(しゅじんこう)。
A true-to-life protagonist.
彼女(かのじょ)は等身大(とうしんだい)の自分(じぶん)を語(かた)った。
She spoke about herself as she really is.
この小説(しょうせつ)は著者(ちょしゃ)が若(わか)い頃(ころ)の等身大(とうしんだい)の姿(すがた)をありのままに描(えが)いた作品(さくひん)である。
This novel is a work in which the author honestly portrays himself as he was in his youth, without any embellishment.

Compound of 等身(とうしん) ('proportional to one's body') and 大(だい) ('size'), plus the attributive particle の. Literally 'of a size equal to one's body', hence 'life-size'.

USAGE:

  • Normally used attributively with の: 等身大(とうしんだい)の人形(にんぎょう) ('life-size doll'). It is not naturally used as a predicate (✗ この人形(にんぎょう)は等身大(とうしんだい)だ is dispreferred; say の人形(にんぎょう)だ instead).
  • Sense 2 is a productive metaphor meaning 'life-size' in the sense of 'undistorted by idealization'. Frequent in book reviews, interviews, and essays.
  • 等身(とうしん) alone is rarely used in modern Japanese outside this fixed form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 等身大(とうしんだい)の人形(にんぎょう): life-size doll
  • 等身大(とうしんだい)の像(ぞう): life-size statue
  • 等身大(とうしんだい)のパネル: life-size cardboard cutout / standee
  • 等身大(とうしんだい)の自分(じぶん): one's true self, unembellished self
  • 等身大(とうしんだい)の姿(すがた)を描(えが)く: to portray (someone) as they really are
  • 等身大(とうしんだい)のヒーロー: a hero the audience can relate to

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実物大(じつぶつだい): life-size; actual size — used for any object (cars, animals, buildings), not limited to the human body.
  • 原寸大(げんすんだい): full-scale; original-size — emphasizes 'same dimensions as the original' and is common in printing, design, and architectural drawings.
  • {ありのまま}: as it is; unembellished — close in spirit to sense 2, but an adverb/noun rather than a modifier.
