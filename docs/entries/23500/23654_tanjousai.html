誕生祭(たんじょうさい)

たんじょうさい
noun
birthday celebration; birthday festival (esp. for an idol or fictional character)
1. birthday celebration; birthday festival (esp. for an idol or fictional character)
A formal-sounding word for a birthday celebration, used especially in fan culture for events that honor the birthday of an idol, anime/manga/game character, or other beloved figure. Often accompanied by hashtags, fan art, special merchandise, and themed cafés or events.
推(お)しの誕生祭(たんじょうさい)に参加(さんか)した。
I attended my favorite idol's birthday event.
今日(きょう)は大切(たいせつ)なキャラクターの誕生祭(たんじょうさい)だ。
Today is the birthday celebration of a character dear to me.
誕生祭(たんじょうさい)記念(きねん)のイラストをSNSに投稿(とうこう)した。
I posted commemorative birthday illustrations on social media.
毎年(まいとし)この時期(じき)になると、ファンたちが力(ちから)を合(あ)わせて誕生祭(たんじょうさい)企画(きかく)を盛(も)り上(あ)げる。
Every year around this time, fans join forces to make the birthday celebration project a huge success.

Composed of 誕生(たんじょう) (birth) and 祭(さい) (festival; celebration). The 祭(さい) suffix lifts the term above the everyday 誕生日(たんじょうび) (birthday), giving it a ceremonial flavor that suits fan-organized events.

USAGE:
While the literal meaning is simply 'birthday festival,' in contemporary Japanese the word is overwhelmingly associated with otaku and idol fan culture. Fans use it on social media (notably as the hashtag #○○誕生祭(たんじょうさい)) to organize coordinated posts of fan art, photos, and messages on the day of an idol's, voice actor's, or character's birthday. It can also refer to the official ticketed birthday concerts and events held by idol groups for individual members. For an ordinary person's birthday, 誕生日(たんじょうび) or 誕生日(たんじょうび)会(かい) is far more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誕生祭(たんじょうさい)を祝(いわ)う: to celebrate the birthday event
  • 生誕(せいたん)祭(さい): alternate term, often used for fictional characters
  • 誕生祭(たんじょうさい)企画(きかく): a birthday-celebration fan project
  • 誕生祭(たんじょうさい)記念(きねん)グッズ: commemorative birthday merchandise

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誕生日(たんじょうび): birthday — the everyday word, used for ordinary people
  • 生誕(せいたん)祭(さい): birth festival — near-synonym, sometimes preferred for fictional characters
  • 生誕(せいたん)記念日(きねんび): birth anniversary — formal, used for historical figures
  • 誕生日(たんじょうび)会(かい): birthday party — the everyday gathering with friends and family
