利用限度額(りようげんどがく)

りようげんどがく
noun
credit limit; usage limit (amount)
1. credit limit; usage limit (amount); spending cap
The maximum amount that a user is permitted to charge, withdraw, or spend — most commonly the credit limit on a credit card, but also used for debit cards, prepaid cards, point services, and other usage-based accounts.
利用限度額(りようげんどがく)は50万円(まんえん)です。
The credit limit is 500,000 yen.
クレジットカードの利用限度額(りようげんどがく)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げたい。
I want to raise the credit limit on my credit card.
今月(こんげつ)はもう利用限度額(りようげんどがく)に達(たっ)してしまったので、カードが使(つか)えない。
I've already hit my credit limit for this month, so I can't use the card.
カード会社(がいしゃ)に連絡(れんらく)すれば、利用限度額(りようげんどがく)を一時的(いちじてき)に増額(ぞうがく)してもらえる場合(ばあい)もあります。
If you contact the card company, they may be able to temporarily raise your credit limit.

Composed of 利用(りよう) (use, utilization), 限度(げんど) (limit), and 額(がく) (amount, sum). The standard term used on credit card statements, banking apps, and service contracts in Japan to refer to the maximum chargeable or usable amount.

USAGE:
Most commonly seen in the context of credit cards ({クレジットカード}), where it refers to the cardholder's credit limit. Also used for debit cards, prepaid cards, electronic money services, and subscription-style services that have a usage cap.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利用限度額(りようげんどがく)を超(こ)える: to exceed the credit limit
  • 利用限度額(りようげんどがく)に達(たっ)する: to reach the credit limit
  • 利用限度額(りようげんどがく)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げる / 増額(ぞうがく)する: to raise the limit
  • 利用限度額(りようげんどがく)を引(ひ)き下(さ)げる: to lower the limit
  • 利用限度額(りようげんどがく)の設定(せってい): setting of the limit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 限度額(げんどがく): limit amount — the more general term; 利用限度額(りようげんどがく) specifies that it is a use/spending limit
  • 与信枠(よしんわく): credit line — the bank-side term for the credit extended to a cardholder; more technical
  • 利用可能額(りようかのうがく): available credit — the amount currently available to spend, i.e., the limit minus what has already been used
