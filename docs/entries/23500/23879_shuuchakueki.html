終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)

しゅうちゃくえき
noun
terminal station, last stop, terminus
1. terminal station — the final station on a train or bus line; also used figuratively for a final destination or end point
The last stop on a rail or bus line, where the service ends and passengers must disembark. Also used figuratively in song lyrics and prose to evoke the end of a journey, a relationship, or a stage of life.
この電車(でんしゃ)の終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)は東京(とうきょう)です。
The terminal station of this train is Tokyo.
終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)まで乗(の)ってください。
Please ride all the way to the last stop.
深夜(しんや)の終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)は人(ひと)がほとんどいなかった。
There was almost no one at the terminal station late at night.
長(なが)い旅(たび)もついに終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)にたどり着(つ)いた。
After a long journey, we finally reached the last stop.

Compound of 終着(しゅうちゃく) ('arrival at the end point') and 駅(えき) ('station').

USAGE:

  • Refers literally to the last station on a line — where a train or bus ends its run and cannot go further.
  • Frequently used figuratively in songs, novels, and essays to suggest an ending or final destination, often with a melancholy or nostalgic tone.
  • On station announcements, the related verb 終着(しゅうちゃく) appears in phrases like '当(とう)駅(えき)が終着(しゅうちゃく)です' ('this is the last stop').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)に着(つ)く: to arrive at the terminal station
  • 終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき)まで乗(の)る: to ride all the way to the last stop
  • 人生(じんせい)の終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき): the final destination of one's life
  • 夜(よる)の終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき): the terminal station at night

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 始発駅(しはつえき): starting station — the direct antonym, the first station on a line.
  • 終点(しゅうてん): terminus, end point — a broader term applicable to any route (bus, train, road), or used figuratively.
  • 終着(しゅうちゃく): (final) arrival — the noun component, used attributively as in 終着(しゅうちゃく)列車(れっしゃ) ('the last train').
