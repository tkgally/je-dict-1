誤飲(ごいん)

ごいん
noun
accidental swallowing (of a non-food object); accidental ingestion
1. accidental swallowing; accidental ingestion
The act of swallowing something that was not meant to be swallowed — typically a coin, button battery, small toy, medication, or household chemical. Most often discussed in the context of small children or elderly people with swallowing difficulties, and prominently featured in poison-control and pediatric-safety advice.
子(こ)どもが硬貨(こうか)を誤飲(ごいん)した。
My child accidentally swallowed a coin.
誤飲(ごいん)の恐(おそ)れがあるので、小(ちい)さな部品(ぶひん)は手(て)の届(とど)かない所(ところ)に置(お)いてください。
There is a risk of accidental swallowing, so please keep small parts out of reach.
ボタン電池(でんち)を誤飲(ごいん)した場合(ばあい)は、すぐに医師(いし)の診察(しんさつ)を受(う)ける必要(ひつよう)があります。
If someone accidentally swallows a button battery, they need to be examined by a doctor immediately.

Composed of 誤(ご) ("mistakenly, in error") + 飲(いん) ("drink, swallow"). The 誤(ご)〜 prefix forms a productive set of "mistakenly/accidentally 〜" compounds: 誤解(ごかい) (misunderstanding), 誤作動(ごさどう) (malfunction), 誤用(ごよう) (misuse).

USAGE:

  • Used as a する verb or as the direct object of する: 誤飲(ごいん)する, 誤飲(ごいん)してしまう.
  • Primarily refers to swallowing non-food items — coins, small toys, button batteries, magnets, medicines taken by the wrong person, and so on. It is distinguished from 誤食(ごしょく), which covers accidentally eating the wrong food (e.g., eating something you're allergic to).
  • Commonly appears on product warning labels, children's-safety pamphlets, and poison-control hotline information.
  • Button batteries (ボタン電池(ぼたんでんち)) and strong magnets are singled out as especially dangerous 誤飲(ごいん) hazards because they can cause serious internal injury.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誤飲(ごいん)する: to swallow (something) by mistake
  • 誤飲(ごいん)事故(じこ): an accidental-swallowing incident
  • 誤飲(ごいん)の恐(おそ)れ: risk of accidental swallowing (frequent warning-label phrasing)
  • 誤飲(ごいん)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent accidental swallowing
  • 誤飲(ごいん)した場合(ばあい)は: in case of accidental swallowing
  • 子(こ)どもの誤飲(ごいん): accidental swallowing by children

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誤食(ごしょく): eating by mistake — applied when the object is a food item but the wrong one (an allergen, spoiled food, wrong person's meal).
  • 誤嚥(ごえん): aspiration (into the airway) — food or liquid going down the windpipe instead of the esophagus; a major concern for the elderly.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 誤(ご) 〜(〜): mistakenly / erroneously (productive prefix)
  • 中毒(ちゅうどく): poisoning
  • ボタン電池(ぼたんでんち): button battery (a common 誤飲(ごいん) hazard)
