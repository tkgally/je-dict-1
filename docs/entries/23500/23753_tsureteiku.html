1. to take someone along; to bring someone with you (to a destination)
To accompany someone by taking them from the current location to another place. The subject physically leads or brings the other person, who is normally a human or animal (not an inanimate object — for inanimate objects use 持(も)っていく). Direction is away from the speaker's reference point.
子(こ)どもを公園(こうえん)に連(つ)れていく。
I'll take the kids to the park.
犬(いぬ)を散歩(さんぽ)に連(つ)れていきます。
I take the dog for a walk.
今度(こんど)の週末(しゅうまつ)、彼女(かのじょ)を新(あたら)しいレストランに連(つ)れていくつもりだ。
I'm planning to take my girlfriend to the new restaurant this weekend.
会議(かいぎ)には通訳(つうやく)を連(つ)れていったほうがいいだろう。
You should probably take an interpreter with you to the meeting.

Compound of the て form of 連(つ)れる ('to take/lead someone along') + いく ('to go'). The combination expresses motion away from the speaker's reference point while accompanying another person. Often written 連(つ)れて行(い)く with kanji; the kana form 連(つ)れていく is also very common, especially in casual writing.

The object is almost always a person or animal, marked with を. For inanimate objects, use 持(も)っていく instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子(こ)どもを連(つ)れていく: to take the children along
  • 犬(いぬ)を散歩(さんぽ)に連(つ)れていく: to take the dog for a walk
  • 病院(びょういん)に連(つ)れていく: to take (someone) to the hospital
  • 一緒(いっしょ)に連(つ)れていく: to take along together
  • 無理(むり)やり連(つ)れていく: to take (someone) by force

DIRECTION:

  • 連(つ)れていく: take someone away from here (speaker's viewpoint stays here)
  • 連(つ)れてくる: bring someone here (speaker's viewpoint is at destination)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連(つ)れる: to accompany/lead — the base verb, often used in attributive forms like 連(つ)れの (人(ひと)) 'accompanying (person)'
  • 持(も)っていく: to take along — used for inanimate objects, not people
  • 案内(あんない)する: to guide, show around — emphasizes showing the way rather than accompanying
  • 同行(どうこう)する: to accompany — more formal; used in written or business contexts

USAGE:
The て form of いく follows standard godan-ku conjugation: 連(つ)れていって (連(つ)れて + いって), 連(つ)れていった (past), 連(つ)れていかない (negative). Note the irregular いく→いって contraction.
