学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)

がくえんせいかつ
noun
school life; campus life
1. school life; campus life
Life as a student, including studies, friendships, club activities, and school events. Typically refers to the overall experience at a high school or college, especially in the context of fiction or nostalgia.
学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy school life.
この漫画(まんが)は高校生(こうこうせい)の学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)を描(えが)いている。
This manga depicts the school life of high school students.
部活動(ぶかつどう)や文化祭(ぶんかさい)など、充実(じゅうじつ)した学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)を送(おく)った。
I had a fulfilling school life with club activities, the culture festival, and more.

Compound of 学園(がくえん) (academy, school) and 生活(せいかつ) (life, daily living). Carries a softer, more nostalgic feel than the neutral 学校(がっこう)生活(せいかつ), and is especially common as a genre label in manga, anime, and light novels (学園(がくえん)もの).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)を送(おく)る: to live a school life; to have (a particular kind of) school life
  • 楽(たの)しい学園生活(がくえんせいかつ): enjoyable school life
  • 充実(じゅうじつ)した学園生活(がくえんせいかつ): fulfilling school life
  • 学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)の思(おも)い出(で): memories of school life

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 学校(がっこう)生活(せいかつ): school life — neutral and more commonly used in everyday speech, often about elementary or junior high school
  • キャンパスライフ: campus life — loanword focused on university life
  • 青春(せいしゅん): youth; the springtime of life — the emotional / nostalgic associations often implied by 学園生活(がくえんせいかつ)
