管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)

かんりがいしゃ
noun
management company; property management company
1. management company; property management company
A company that manages residential or commercial properties on behalf of the owner. Handles maintenance, tenant relations, rent collection, and building upkeep.
管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)に電話(でんわ)する。
To call the management company.
水漏(みずも)れがあったので管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)に連絡(れんらく)した。
I contacted the management company because there was a water leak.
マンションの管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)が変(か)わって、サービスが良(よ)くなった。
The apartment building's management company changed, and the service improved.

A compound of 管理(かんり) (management) and 会社(がいしゃ) (company). In the context of Japanese housing, the 管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ) is the first point of contact for tenants when something goes wrong — from broken appliances to noise complaints.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)に連絡(れんらく)する: to contact the management company
  • 管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)に相談(そうだん)する: to consult the management company
  • 管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ)の担当者(たんとうしゃ): management company representative
  • マンションの管理(かんり)会社(がいしゃ): apartment building management company

RELATED TERMS:

  • 大家(おおや): landlord — the property owner (may hire a management company)
  • 管理人(かんりにん): building manager/superintendent — the person who is physically present at the building
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)会社(がいしゃ): real estate company — handles buying, selling, and renting
