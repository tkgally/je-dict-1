孵化場(ふかじょう)

ふかじょう
noun
hatchery
1. hatchery; hatching facility
A facility where eggs — most commonly those of fish, chickens, or other birds — are incubated and hatched under controlled conditions. Used in fisheries, poultry farming, and conservation programs for releasing young fish or birds into the wild.
この町(まち)には鮭(さけ)の孵化場(ふかじょう)がある。
This town has a salmon hatchery.
孵化場(ふかじょう)で育(そだ)てた稚魚(ちぎょ)を川(かわ)に放流(ほうりゅう)した。
We released young fish raised at the hatchery into the river.
地元(じもと)の漁業(ぎょぎょう)協同組合(きょうどうくみあい)が孵化場(ふかじょう)を運営(うんえい)している。
The local fisheries cooperative operates the hatchery.
絶滅(ぜつめつ)の危機(きき)にある魚(さかな)を守(まも)るため、専門(せんもん)の孵化場(ふかじょう)が各地(かくち)に作(つく)られている。
Specialized hatcheries are being built around the country to protect fish that are in danger of extinction.

Composed of 孵化(ふか) (hatching, incubation) and the suffix -場(じょう) (place, site, facility). Refers to a physical site where eggs are hatched on an industrial or large scale, most often in the context of fisheries (水産(すいさん)) or poultry.

USAGE:
Most commonly heard in news reports and technical writing about fisheries and conservation, where salmon and trout hatcheries are a familiar institution in Japan. It can also describe bird and insect breeding facilities, but for household pets or small-scale hobby breeding, speakers usually prefer 孵卵器(ふらんき) (incubator) or simply describe the setup. The -場(じょう) suffix emphasizes the facility as a whole rather than a single piece of equipment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鮭(さけ)の孵化場(ふかじょう): salmon hatchery
  • 孵化場(ふかじょう)を運営(うんえい)する: to operate a hatchery
  • 孵化場(ふかじょう)で育(そだ)てる: to raise at a hatchery
  • 稚魚(ちぎょ)を孵化場(ふかじょう)から放流(ほうりゅう)する: to release young fish from a hatchery
  • 国営(こくえい)孵化場(ふかじょう): state-run hatchery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 孵卵器(ふらんき): incubator — the machine or apparatus used to incubate eggs, not a facility
  • 養殖場(ようしょくじょう): aquaculture farm — where fish are farmed to maturity, often downstream of a hatchery
  • 種苗(しゅびょう){センター}: fry/seedling center — an official term for a government-run fish-breeding center
  • 養鶏場(ようけいじょう): poultry farm — raises chickens, sometimes including a hatching operation
