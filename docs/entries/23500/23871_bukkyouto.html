仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)

ぶっきょうと
noun
Buddhist; follower of Buddhism
1. Buddhist — a person who follows or practices Buddhism
A person who professes Buddhism as their religion or practices it as a way of life. The suffix 徒(と) attaches to religions to designate an adherent (as in キリスト{教徒(きょうと)} 'Christian', イスラム{教徒(きょうと)} 'Muslim'). In Japan, formal religious affiliation is often loose, so 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと) is more commonly heard in news, surveys, and descriptions of other countries than as a personal self-identification.
彼(かれ)は仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)だ。
He is a Buddhist.
タイは仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)が多(おお)い国(くに)です。
Thailand is a country with many Buddhists.
世界(せかい)には約(やく)五億人(ごおくにん)の仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)がいると言(い)われている。
It is said that there are about 500 million Buddhists in the world.

Compound of 仏教(ぶっきょう) ('Buddhism') and the suffix 徒(と) ('adherent, follower').

USAGE:

  • Used to refer to adherents of Buddhism in general, whether in Japan or abroad. The word is neutral and descriptive.
  • In Japan, many people are culturally and ritually connected to Buddhism (through family temples, funerals, and holidays) but do not self-identify as 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと) in daily speech. The word tends to appear in formal, academic, or international contexts.
  • The pattern X{教徒(きょうと)} is used for adherents of other religions as well: キリスト{教徒(きょうと)} (Christian), イスラム{教徒(きょうと)} (Muslim), ヒンドゥー{教徒(きょうと)} (Hindu).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敬虔(けいけん)な仏教徒(ぶっきょうと): a devout Buddhist
  • 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)の家庭(かてい): a Buddhist household
  • 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)人口(じんこう): Buddhist population
  • 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)になる: to become a Buddhist

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仏教(ぶっきょう): Buddhism — the religion itself, not the adherent.
  • 信者(しんじゃ): believer, follower of a religion — a more general word that can be combined with a religion's name (仏教(ぶっきょう)の信者(しんじゃ)).
  • お{坊(ぼう)さん}: Buddhist monk — a member of the clergy, not a lay follower.

RELATED TERMS:

  • キリスト{教徒(きょうと)}: Christian
  • イスラム{教徒(きょうと)}: Muslim
  • 無宗教(むしゅうきょう): non-religious, without a faith
