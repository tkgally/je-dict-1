先駆(さきが)け

さきがけ
noun
forerunner; pioneer; harbinger; the first of its kind
1. forerunner; pioneer; the first of its kind
The earliest example of something — a person, work, event, or phenomenon — that precedes and paves the way for others. Commonly used of pioneers in a field, early signs of a trend, or seasonal firsts such as the first flowers of spring.
春(はる)の先駆(さきが)け。
A harbinger of spring.
彼(かれ)はその分野(ぶんや)の先駆(さきが)けとなった。
He became a pioneer in that field.
この会社(かいしゃ)は、日本(にほん)における電気(でんき)自動車(じどうしゃ)開発(かいはつ)の先駆(さきが)けだ。
This company is a pioneer in electric vehicle development in Japan.
梅(うめ)の花(はな)は春(はる)の先駆(さきが)けとして古(むかし)から詩(し)や和歌(わか)に詠(よ)まれてきた。
Plum blossoms, as a harbinger of spring, have been celebrated in poems and waka from ancient times.

Nominalization of the verb 先駆(さきが)ける ('to precede; to lead the way'), which is itself a compound of 先(さき) ('ahead; earlier') and 駆(か)ける ('to gallop; to run'). The original image is of galloping ahead of others in battle.

USAGE:
Used in both literal and figurative senses. Most often seen in three patterns: (1) 'pioneer/forerunner' of a movement or field (〜の先駆(さきが)け), (2) 'harbinger' of a season or change (春(はる)の先駆(さきが)け), and (3) as an adverbial expression 〜に先駆(さきが)けて ('ahead of; before; leading the way in'). Register is slightly formal and literary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜の先駆(さきが)けとなる: to become a pioneer/forerunner of ~
  • 〜の先駆(さきが)けである: to be a pioneer of ~
  • 春(はる)の先駆(さきが)け: a harbinger of spring
  • 時代(じだい)の先駆(さきが)け: a forerunner of an era
  • 業界(ぎょうかい)の先駆(さきが)け: a pioneer in the industry
  • 〜に先駆(さきが)けて: ahead of ~; before ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先駆者(せんくしゃ): pioneer (person) — same meaning but specifically a person; on-yomi reading
  • パイオニア: pioneer — loanword; common in business and technology contexts
  • 草分(くさわ)け: pioneer; founder — emphasizes being the one who 'cleared the grass,' i.e., opened up a new area
  • 先(さき)がけて: the adverbial form; used in set expressions to mean 'ahead of'
  • 魁(さきがけ): alternative kanji spelling, more literary
