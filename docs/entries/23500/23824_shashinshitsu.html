写真室(しゃしんしつ)

しゃしんしつ
noun
photo studio; photography room
1. photo studio; room equipped for taking photographs
A dedicated room or small studio for taking photographs, typically equipped with backgrounds, lighting, and a camera setup. Found inside department stores, hotels, schools, hospitals, and government offices, 写真室(しゃしんしつ) is often used for ID photos, portraits, and commemorative pictures. Distinct from the freestanding commercial 写真館(しゃしんかん) ('photo studio'), which is a whole shop rather than a room within a larger facility.
写真室(しゃしんしつ)で記念(きねん)写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る。
Take a commemorative photo at the photo studio.
パスポートの写真(しゃしん)は駅前(えきまえ)の写真室(しゃしんしつ)で撮(と)ってもらった。
I had my passport photo taken at the photo studio in front of the station.
入学式(にゅうがくしき)のあと、家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)で大学(だいがく)内(ない)の写真室(しゃしんしつ)に寄(よ)って記念(きねん)撮影(さつえい)をした。
After the entrance ceremony, the whole family stopped by the photo studio on campus to take a commemorative portrait.

Compound of 写真(しゃしん) ('photograph') and 室(しつ) ('room'). The suffix 〜室(しつ) names rooms dedicated to a particular purpose: 会議室(かいぎしつ) ('meeting room'), 研究室(けんきゅうしつ) ('laboratory; research office'), 保健室(ほけんしつ) ('school nurse's office'), 資料室(しりょうしつ) ('reference room').

USAGE:

  • Common verbs: で撮(と)る / してもらう ('to take / have taken'), を利用(りよう)する ('to use'), を予約(よやく)する ('to book').
  • Often found inside larger institutions: 病院(びょういん)の写真室(しゃしんしつ), 大学(だいがく)の写真室(しゃしんしつ), 市役所(しやくしょ)の写真室(しゃしんしつ).
  • The purpose is usually specified by context: 証明(しょうめい)写真(しゃしん) ('ID photo'), 家族(かぞく)写真(しゃしん) ('family portrait'), 記念(きねん)写真(しゃしん) ('commemorative photo').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 写真室(しゃしんしつ)で撮影(さつえい)する: to take photos at the studio
  • 写真室(しゃしんしつ)を予約(よやく)する: to book the photo studio
  • 写真室(しゃしんしつ)付(つ)き: with a photo studio attached
  • 院内(いんない)の写真室(しゃしんしつ): the in-hospital photo studio
  • 写真室(しゃしんしつ)の受付(うけつけ): the photo studio reception

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 写真館(しゃしんかん): photo studio — a standalone commercial studio, typically a family-run shop specializing in portraits.
  • スタジオ: studio — loanword; broader, covering photo, video, and music studios.
  • 暗室(あんしつ): darkroom — a room for developing photographs, not for taking them.
  • 撮影(さつえい)室(しつ): photography/filming room — close synonym; slightly more formal and often used for industrial or medical imaging contexts.
