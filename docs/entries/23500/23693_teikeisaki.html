提携先(ていけいさき)

ていけいさき
noun
business partner; affiliated company; tie-up partner
1. business partner; affiliated company; tie-up partner; partner organization
A company, organization, or store with which one has a formal business alliance or tie-up — the "other side" of a business partnership. Common in corporate, financial, and travel-industry contexts.
提携先(ていけいさき)の企業(きぎょう)。
A partner company.
このカードは提携先(ていけいさき)の店(みせ)でも使(つか)える。
You can use this card at affiliated stores as well.
新(あたら)しい提携先(ていけいさき)を探(さが)して、販路(はんろ)を広(ひろ)げたい。
We want to find new business partners and expand our sales channels.
海外(かいがい)の提携先(ていけいさき)との打(う)ち合(あ)わせのため、来週(らいしゅう)ヨーロッパに出張(しゅっちょう)することになった。
I will go on a business trip to Europe next week for a meeting with our overseas partner.

Composed of 提携(ていけい) (tie-up, alliance, cooperation) and the suffix ～先(さき) (the other party; the destination or counterpart of an action). The suffix ～先(さき) productively turns verbs and verbal nouns into nouns meaning "the party/place on the receiving or counterpart side": 取引先(とりひきさき) (business client), 連絡先(れんらくさき) (contact information / person to contact), 勤務先(きんむさき) (place of employment).

USAGE:
Common in business, finance, retail, and travel contexts. Frequently seen in press releases, company profiles, and points/credit-card program descriptions ("affiliated stores").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 提携先(ていけいさき)を探(さが)す: to look for a partner
  • 提携先(ていけいさき)を決(き)める: to decide on a partner
  • 提携先(ていけいさき)企業(きぎょう): partner company
  • 提携先(ていけいさき)店舗(てんぽ): affiliated store

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取引先(とりひきさき): business client/customer — emphasizes ongoing transactional relationships rather than formal alliance
  • 協力先(きょうりょくさき): cooperating party — emphasizes cooperation on a specific project rather than a standing partnership
  • 関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ): affiliated company — a legal/corporate term for companies connected by ownership
  • 業務(ぎょうむ)提携(ていけい): business tie-up — the relationship itself, not the counterpart
