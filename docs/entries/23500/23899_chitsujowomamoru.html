秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)る

ちつじょをまもる
expression
to maintain order; to observe the rules
1. to maintain order, to keep a peaceful and orderly state, to observe the rules or social norms that keep a group or society functioning
A common collocation of 秩序(ちつじょ) ('order, orderly arrangement') with 守(まも)る ('to protect, to observe, to keep'). Used in contexts of law, public safety, classrooms, lines and queues, international order, and any situation where a shared system of rules needs to be upheld.
秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)ろう。
Let's keep order.
警察(けいさつ)は町(まち)の秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)っている。
The police maintain order in the town.
災害時(さいがいじ)でも人々(ひとびと)が秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)って行動(こうどう)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Even in times of disaster, it's important for people to act in an orderly way.
国際社会(こくさいしゃかい)の秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)るためには、各国(かっこく)の協力(きょうりょく)が欠(か)かせない。
Cooperation among nations is essential to maintain order in the international community.

A standard collocation for the idea of upholding a stable, rule-governed state, whether in everyday public spaces or in larger social and political arenas.

USAGE:

  • The subject is typically an institution (police, government, school) or a group of people acting collectively; can also be a single person acting with restraint.
  • Negated form 秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)らない describes disruptive or lawless behavior (cutting in line, rioting, ignoring rules).
  • The related opposite action is 秩序(ちつじょ)を乱(みだ)す ('to disturb order, to cause disorder').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公共(こうきょう)の秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)る: to maintain public order
  • 社会(しゃかい)の秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)る: to maintain social order
  • 法(ほう)と秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)る: to uphold law and order
  • 列(れつ)の秩序(ちつじょ)を守(まも)る: to keep the order of the line / queue
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)を乱(みだ)す: to disturb order (opposite action)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 規則(きそく)を守(まも)る: to observe the rules — focus on specific regulations, not on overall orderliness.
  • ルールを守(まも)る: to follow the rules — casual, everyday counterpart using the loanword.
  • 治安(ちあん)を守(まも)る: to maintain public safety — emphasizes safety and policing more than orderly conduct.
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)を保(たも)つ: to preserve / sustain order — very close synonym, slightly more formal; 守(まも)る implies active defense, 保(たも)つ implies maintenance.

REGISTER:
Neutral to formal. Common in news reporting, public signage, civics education, and speeches.
