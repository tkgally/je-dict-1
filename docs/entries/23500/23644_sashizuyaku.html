指図役(さしずやく)

さしずやく
noun
person who gives orders; director; person in charge
1. person who gives orders; director; person in charge
The person whose job is to direct others — issuing instructions, assigning tasks, and overseeing how work is carried out. Can describe a formal supervisory role, but more often appears in a slightly critical or teasing tone, suggesting someone who bosses others around without doing the hands-on work themselves.
彼(かれ)はいつも指図役(さしずやく)だ。
He's always the one giving orders.
指図役(さしずやく)ばかりで自分(じぶん)では動(うご)かない。
He just gives orders and never moves a finger himself.
現場(げんば)の指図役(さしずやく)として若手(わかて)が抜擢(ばってき)された。
A young employee was selected as the person in charge on-site.
指図役(さしずやく)に徹(てっ)するより、自分(じぶん)で手(て)を動(うご)かした方(ほう)が早(はや)いこともある。
Sometimes it's faster to get your own hands moving than to stick to just giving orders.

Composed of 指図(さしず) (instruction; order given to others) and the suffix 役(やく) (role; part). The 役(やく) suffix names a functional role, as in 案内役(あんないやく) (guide) or 仲介役(ちゅうかいやく) (mediator).

USAGE:
Often carries a negative or sarcastic nuance, especially with adverbs like ばかり or いつも — "always the one giving orders (but never doing the work)." When used neutrally, it simply refers to the person assigning tasks or coordinating a group. In formal work contexts, 指揮者(しきしゃ), 責任者(せきにんしゃ), or 監督(かんとく) are preferred for the same role without the dismissive overtones.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指図役(さしずやく){ばかり}: nothing but giving orders
  • 指図役(さしずやく)に回(まわ)る: to take on the role of order-giver
  • 指図役(さしずやく)に徹(てっ)する: to devote oneself solely to directing others
  • 現場(げんば)の指図役(さしずやく): the on-site person in charge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 指揮者(しきしゃ): conductor; commander — a more formal, positive term for a leader who directs
  • 責任者(せきにんしゃ): person in charge; the one responsible — emphasizes accountability, not order-giving
  • 監督(かんとく): supervisor; director — used for film directors, sports coaches, and general supervisors
  • 仕切(しき)り役(やく): the one who runs the show — similar but more about organizing an event than giving orders
