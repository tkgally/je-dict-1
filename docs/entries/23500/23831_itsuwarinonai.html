偽(いつわ)りのない

いつわりのない
expression (prenominal phrase)
truthful; genuine; unfeigned; free of falsehood
1. truthful; genuine; without falsehood; unfeigned
A set prenominal phrase used to describe a statement, feeling, attitude, or person as completely honest and free of any deception or exaggeration. Literally 'without falsehood'. It is somewhat formal and often occurs in written language, testimony, declarations, and heartfelt personal statements.
偽(いつわ)りのない気持(きも)ち。
My genuine feelings.
偽(いつわ)りのない事実(じじつ)を伝(つた)えたい。
I want to convey the unvarnished truth.
彼(かれ)は記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で、偽(いつわ)りのない思(おも)いを自分(じぶん)の言葉(ことば)で語(かた)ろうとした。
At the press conference he tried to speak, in his own words, of his genuine, unfeigned feelings.

Literally 偽(いつわ)り ('falsehood; lie') + の + ない ('absent; nonexistent') = 'with no falsehood'. A productive pattern of the form NOUN + の + ない, used as a prenominal modifier.

USAGE:

  • Used attributively before a noun, almost never as a predicate on its own. Compare:
  • ✓ 偽(いつわ)りのない気持(きも)ち ('genuine feelings')
  • ✗ 彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちは偽(いつわ)りのない (unnatural; say 〜に偽(いつわ)りはない instead).
  • Slightly formal; suits written statements, public apologies or declarations, speeches, and emotionally weighty contexts. In casual conversation speakers usually say 本当(ほんとう)の or 本心(ほんしん)の.
  • Often combined with abstract nouns about inner states or information: 気持(きも)ち ('feelings'), 思(おも)い ('thoughts'), 言葉(ことば) ('words'), 告白(こくはく) ('confession'), 証言(しょうげん) ('testimony'), 事実(じじつ) ('facts').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 偽(いつわ)りのない気持(きも)ち: genuine feelings
  • 偽(いつわ)りのない言葉(ことば): sincere words
  • 偽(いつわ)りのない事実(じじつ): the unvarnished truth
  • 偽(いつわ)りのない告白(こくはく): an honest confession
  • 偽(いつわ)りのない愛情(あいじょう): genuine affection
  • 偽(いつわ)りのない証言(しょうげん): truthful testimony

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 本当(ほんとう)の: true; real — everyday equivalent; more neutral and much more common in speech.
  • 本物(ほんもの)の: real; authentic — emphasizes genuineness of an object, person, or skill rather than honesty of feeling.
  • 真(しん)の: true — formal and written; can modify a wide range of nouns (真(しん)の友人(ゆうじん), 真(しん)の意味(いみ)).
  • 嘘(うそ)のない: free of lies — near-synonymous and often interchangeable, but slightly more conversational.
  • 飾(かざ)らない: unadorned; straightforward — describes manner of expression or personality rather than truth content.
