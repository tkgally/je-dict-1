潤滑(じゅんかつ)

じゅんかつ
noun
lubrication; smoothness
1. lubrication — reducing friction between moving parts, or (figuratively) the smooth operation of a system
The act or state of making motion smooth by reducing friction. Used literally for machinery (oil between moving parts) and figuratively for the smooth running of organizations, communication, or relationships. Rarely appears as a standalone predicate — almost always found in compounds or in the phrase 潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ) ('lubricating oil').
潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ)を差(さ)す。
To apply lubricating oil.
このベアリングには定期的(ていきてき)な潤滑(じゅんかつ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
This bearing needs regular lubrication.
挨拶(あいさつ)は人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ)だとよく言(い)われる。
It is often said that greetings are the lubricant of human relationships.

Compound of 潤(じゅん) ('moisten/enrich') and 滑(かつ) ('slide/smooth'). Literally 'moistening and sliding'.

USAGE:

  • Almost never used as a standalone noun. The most common form is the compound 潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ) or 潤滑剤(じゅんかつざい) ('lubricant').
  • The figurative phrase 潤滑油(じゅんかつゆ) (or ...の}{潤滑油(じゅんかつゆ){になる}) is idiomatic — used to describe something or someone that helps interactions or systems run smoothly, such as a mediator, a helpful custom, or small talk.
  • The する-verb form 潤滑(じゅんかつ)する exists but is rare; 潤滑(じゅんかつ){させる} ('to lubricate / smooth the working of') appears in technical writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ): lubricating oil
  • 潤滑(じゅんかつ)剤(ざい): lubricant
  • 潤滑(じゅんかつ)作用(さよう): lubricating action
  • 人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ): lubricant of human relationships (figurative)
  • 組織(そしき)の潤滑(じゅんかつ)油(ゆ){となる}: to become the lubricant of an organization

RELATED TERMS:

  • 摩擦(まさつ): friction — the opposite effect that lubrication reduces.
  • 円滑(えんかつ): smooth (running) — very close in meaning, but more often used as an adjective-na to describe the smooth course of events or communication (e.g., 円滑(えんかつ)な}{進行(しんこう) 'smooth progress'), while 潤滑(じゅんかつ) emphasizes the agent that makes things smooth.
  • 滑(なめ)らか: smooth, slick — describes surfaces or motion directly.
