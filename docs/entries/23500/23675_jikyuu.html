1. self-sufficiency; self-supply; producing for one's own use
Supplying something — especially food, energy, or resources — by oneself rather than depending on outside sources. Often used in economic and policy contexts about a country or household producing what it consumes.
食料(しょくりょう)の自給(じきゅう)。
Self-sufficiency in food.
野菜(やさい)はすべて自給(じきゅう)している。
We grow all our vegetables ourselves.
エネルギーの自給(じきゅう)率(りつ)を高(たか)めることが国(くに)の課題(かだい)だ。
Raising the energy self-sufficiency rate is a challenge for the country.
田舎(いなか)に引(ひ)っ越(こ)して、野菜(やさい)や米(こめ)を自給(じきゅう)する生活(せいかつ)を始(はじ)めた。
I moved to the countryside and began a lifestyle of self-sufficiency with vegetables and rice.

Composed of 自(じ) (self) and 給(きゅう) (supply, provide). Refers to meeting one's own needs through one's own production rather than by purchasing or importing. The term is especially common in discussions of national food policy, energy security, and rural or back-to-the-land lifestyles.

USAGE:

  • As a noun: 自給(じきゅう)が難(むずか)しい (self-sufficiency is difficult)
  • As a suru-verb: 野菜(やさい)を自給(じきゅう)する (to grow one's own vegetables)
  • Commonly combined with 率(りつ) (rate): 食料(しょくりょう)自給率(じきゅうりつ) (food self-sufficiency rate)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食料(しょくりょう)自給(じきゅう): food self-sufficiency
  • 自給(じきゅう)自足(じそく): self-sufficiency (four-character idiom — supplying and meeting one's own needs)
  • エネルギー自給(じきゅう): energy self-sufficiency
  • 自給(じきゅう)率(りつ): self-sufficiency rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自足(じそく): self-sufficiency (meeting one's needs) — close in meaning; almost always paired with 自給(じきゅう) in the phrase 自給自足(じきゅうじそく)
  • 自活(じかつ): supporting oneself, being financially independent — focuses on earning one's own living rather than producing goods
  • 自前(じまえ): at one's own expense, doing it oneself — emphasizes personal funding or provision rather than production
