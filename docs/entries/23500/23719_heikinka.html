1. averaging; leveling (out); smoothing out; standardization (toward an average)
The process of reducing variation across items, people, or values so that they become closer to a common average. Used both in technical/statistical contexts (smoothing data, leveling workload) and in social ones (standardization of wages, service quality, or abilities).
データを平均化(へいきんか)する。
To average out the data.
業務(ぎょうむ)の負担(ふたん)を平均化(へいきんか)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to even out the workload.
生産(せいさん)ラインの作業量(さぎょうりょう)を平均化(へいきんか)することで、効率(こうりつ)が上(あ)がる。
By leveling out the workload on the production line, efficiency improves.
評価(ひょうか)の平均化(へいきんか)が進(すす)むと、本当(ほんとう)に優秀(ゆうしゅう)な社員(しゃいん)が正(ただ)しく評価(ひょうか)されなくなる恐(おそ)れがある。
If the leveling of evaluations progresses, there is a risk that truly outstanding employees will no longer be properly appraised.

Composed of 平均(へいきん) (average) and the suffix -化(か) (-ization, turning into). A productive -化(か) noun that refers to the act or process of making things averaged or leveled.

USAGE:

Used across technical, business, and social contexts:

  • Statistics / engineering: smoothing of measurements, data, or loads.
  • Business / operations: evening out workload, production volume, or service quality.
  • Social / educational: leveling of abilities, grades, or outcomes — sometimes with a negative connotation of suppressing individuality or excellence.

Can be used as a する} verb: {平均化(へいきんか)する 'to average out, to level'.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 負荷(ふか)を平均化(へいきんか)する: to level out the load (computing, engineering)
  • 作業量(さぎょうりょう)を平均化(へいきんか)する: to even out the workload
  • データ}を{平均化(へいきんか)する: to average / smooth data
  • 能力(のうりょく)の平均化(へいきんか): the leveling of abilities
  • 平均化(へいきんか)が進(すす)む: leveling progresses / becomes more pronounced

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 均一化(きんいつか): uniformization — making things uniform or identical; stronger than 平均化(へいきんか), which only brings values closer to an average
  • 平滑化(へいかつか): smoothing — a technical term in data processing, particularly in signal processing or image processing
  • 標準化(ひょうじゅんか): standardization — making things conform to a standard, not necessarily an average
  • 一律化(いちりつか): uniform application (across cases) — emphasizes treating everyone the same
