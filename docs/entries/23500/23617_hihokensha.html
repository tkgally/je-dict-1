被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)

ひほけんしゃ
noun
the insured; insured person; policyholder (of an insurance policy)
1. the insured; insured person
The person covered by an insurance policy — that is, the person whose life, health, property, or circumstances are the subject of the contract. Distinguished from the 契約者(けいやくしゃ) (the policyholder who signs the contract and pays the premiums) and the 受取人(うけとりにん) (the beneficiary who receives any payout).
被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)の氏名(しめい)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the name of the insured.
被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)が入院(にゅういん)した場合(ばあい)、給付金(きゅうふきん)が支払(しはら)われる。
If the insured is hospitalized, benefits will be paid.
生命保険(せいめいほけん)では契約者(けいやくしゃ)と被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)が同一人物(どういつじんぶつ)でない場合(ばあい)もある。
In life insurance, the policyholder and the insured are not always the same person.

Composed of 被(ひ) (passive prefix, "the one receiving") + 保険(ほけん) ("insurance") + 者(しゃ) ("person"), literally "the person who is insured." A standard legal/insurance term appearing on policy documents, enrollment forms, and social-insurance cards (e.g., 健康保険被保険者証(けんこうほけんひほけんしゃしょう), the health-insurance card).

USAGE:

  • In life insurance, the 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ) is the person whose life is covered; their death or illness triggers the payout.
  • In health insurance, the 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ) is the enrolled employee; their dependents are called 被扶養者(ひふようしゃ).
  • Forms often ask for 被保険者氏名(ひほけんしゃしめい) (name of the insured) separately from the applicant.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)証(しょう): insurance card / certificate of the insured
  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)の氏名(しめい): name of the insured
  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)番号(ばんごう): insured person's ID number
  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)本人(ほんにん): the insured party themselves

RELATED TERMS:

  • 保険者(ほけんしゃ): insurer — the company or body providing the insurance
  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ): policyholder — the person who signs the contract and pays premiums
  • 受取人(うけとりにん): beneficiary — the person who receives the payout
  • 被扶養者(ひふようしゃ): dependent — a family member covered under the insured's policy
  • 保険料(ほけんりょう): insurance premium
  • 給付金(きゅうふきん): benefit payment
