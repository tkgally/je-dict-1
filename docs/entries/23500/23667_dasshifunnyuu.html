脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)

だっしふんにゅう
noun
skim milk powder; nonfat dry milk
1. skim milk powder; nonfat dry milk
Powdered milk produced by removing the fat from cow's milk and then dehydrating it. Used as an inexpensive long-shelf-life dairy product, in baking, in food processing, and historically as a key ingredient in postwar Japanese school lunches.
脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)を水(みず)に溶(と)かす。
Dissolve skim milk powder in water.
昔(むかし)の給食(きゅうしょく)には脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)が出(で)ていた。
School lunches in the old days used to include skim milk powder.
祖母(そぼ)は脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)の独特(どくとく)の味(あじ)が今(いま)でも忘(わす)れられないと言(い)う。
My grandmother says she still cannot forget the distinctive taste of skim milk powder.
脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)は保存(ほぞん)性(せい)が高(たか)く、パンや菓子(かし)の材料(ざいりょう)としても広(ひろ)く使(つか)われている。
Skim milk powder has a long shelf life and is widely used as an ingredient in bread and confectionery.

Composed of 脱脂(だっし) (defatted; with the fat removed), 粉(ふん) (powder), and 乳(にゅう) (milk). Reads with sokuon ({だっ}し) and the nasal-doubling typical when {ふん} meets {にゅう} ({ふんにゅう}).

USAGE:
In modern Japan, this word carries strong nostalgic and sometimes negative associations because of its prominent role in postwar school lunches. From the late 1940s through the early 1970s, donated American skim milk powder was reconstituted with hot water and served in school lunches; many older Japanese remember the smell and taste with distaste, and 脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう) is often invoked as a symbol of postwar privation. It is still used today as an industrial ingredient, but consumers more often encounter the term スキムミルク on supermarket shelves. 脱脂乳(だっしにゅう) (without the 粉(ふん)) refers to liquid skim milk before drying.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)を溶(と)かす: to reconstitute skim milk powder
  • 脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)を使(つか)う: to use skim milk powder
  • 給食(きゅうしょく)の脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう): school-lunch skim milk powder
  • 脱脂粉乳(だっしふんにゅう)入(い)り: containing skim milk powder

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スキムミルク: skim milk — modern consumer term, same product
  • 粉乳(ふんにゅう): powdered milk — broader, includes whole-milk powder
  • 全脂粉乳(ぜんしふんにゅう): whole-milk powder — the full-fat counterpart
  • 脱脂乳(だっしにゅう): liquid skim milk — the same product before being dried
  • コンデンスミルク: condensed milk — concentrated, sweetened liquid form
