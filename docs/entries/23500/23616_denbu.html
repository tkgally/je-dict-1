臀部(でんぶ)

でんぶ
noun
buttocks; gluteal region (anatomical)
1. buttocks; gluteal region
The buttocks or gluteal region of the body. A clinical/anatomical term used in medicine, fitness, and formal writing. In everyday speech Japanese speakers use the much more common お尻(しり) (or plain 尻(しり)).
臀部(でんぶ)に痛(いた)みがある。
I have pain in the buttocks.
医師(いし)は臀部(でんぶ)に注射(ちゅうしゃ)を打(う)った。
The doctor gave an injection in the buttocks.
この筋(きん)トレは臀部(でんぶ)と太(ふと)ももの筋肉(きんにく)を同時(どうじ)に鍛(きた)えられる。
This strength exercise trains the gluteal and thigh muscles at the same time.

Composed of 臀(でん) ("buttocks") + 部(ぶ) ("part, region"). A Sino-Japanese medical/anatomical term found mainly in clinical contexts, anatomy textbooks, pharmaceutical instructions (e.g., "apply to the 臀部(でんぶ)"), and fitness writing. The kanji 臀(でん) is not commonly written by hand and is often left in kana (でんぶ) in informal anatomy references.

REGISTER:

  • Formal and clinical; avoid in casual speech.
  • In everyday conversation, use お尻(しり) (polite/neutral) or 尻(しり) (blunt).
  • In fitness and bodybuilding contexts, お尻(しり), ヒップ, and 臀部(でんぶ) coexist; 臀部(でんぶ) sounds the most technical.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 臀部(でんぶ)の筋肉(きんにく): the gluteal muscles
  • 臀部(でんぶ)に注射(ちゅうしゃ)する: to inject into the buttocks
  • 臀部(でんぶ)痛(つう): pain in the buttocks
  • 臀部(でんぶ)を鍛(きた)える: to train/strengthen the glutes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お尻(しり): buttocks — the everyday, all-purpose word; appropriate in almost any non-clinical context
  • 尻(しり): buttocks — blunt/rough register; also used figuratively (e.g., 尻(しり)が重(おも)い)
  • {ヒップ}: hips/buttocks — loanword, common in fashion and fitness
  • 大殿筋(だいでんきん): gluteus maximus — a specific muscle within the 臀部(でんぶ)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 腰(こし): lower back, hips
  • 骨盤(こつばん): pelvis
  • 太(ふと)もも: thigh
