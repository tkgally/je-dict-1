口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)く

くちぶえをふく
expression
to whistle (with one's lips)
1. to whistle (with one's lips); to whistle a tune
To make a whistling sound by blowing air through puckered lips, often while doing something casually or carefree. Can be whistling a tune or just whistling for expression, for calling, or absentmindedly. The standard Japanese way to describe whistling.
口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)きながら歩(ある)く。
To walk along whistling.
彼(かれ)は機嫌(きげん)がいいとき、いつも口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)く。
He always whistles when he's in a good mood.
父(ちち)は車(くるま)を洗(あら)いながら、好(す)きな曲(きょく)の口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)いていた。
My father was whistling his favorite tune while washing the car.
夜(よる)に口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)くと蛇(へび)が来(く)るという言(い)い伝(つた)えを、子供(こども)のころ祖母(そぼ)から聞(き)いたことがある。
When I was a child, I heard from my grandmother the folk belief that if you whistle at night, snakes will come.

Composed of 口笛(くちぶえ) (whistle, literally "mouth flute") and 吹(ふ)く (to blow). The standard way to say "to whistle" in Japanese — the verb 吹(ふ)く (to blow) is used because whistling is produced by blowing air, and 口笛(くちぶえ) is treated as the instrument being played.

USAGE:

This phrase is almost always used as 口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)く with the particle を. It can describe:

  • whistling a tune: 好(す)きな歌(うた)の口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)く
  • whistling absentmindedly or cheerfully
  • whistling to call someone or a dog (though this is less common in Japan than in some cultures)

CULTURAL NOTE:
There is a well-known folk superstition that whistling at night brings snakes, thieves, or other misfortune — 夜(よる)に口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)くと蛇(へび)が来(く)る (or どろぼう — thieves). Many older Japanese were told this as children and still avoid whistling after dark out of habit.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)きながら: while whistling
  • 口笛(くちぶえ)で呼(よ)ぶ: to call (someone/a dog) by whistling
  • 口笛(くちぶえ)が上手(じょうず)だ: to be good at whistling
  • 口笛(くちぶえ)を吹(ふ)くのが下手(へた)だ: to be bad at whistling

RELATED TERMS:

  • 口笛(くちぶえ): whistle (the sound or act) — the noun on its own
  • 指笛(ゆびぶえ): finger whistle — whistling by putting fingers in the mouth for a louder sound
  • 笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く: to play a flute/whistle — uses the same verb 吹(ふ)く
