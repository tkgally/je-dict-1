一般用(いっぱんよう)

いっぱんよう
noun
for general (public) use; for general audiences
1. for general (public) use; for ordinary consumers; for general audiences
Intended for the general public or ordinary consumers, rather than for professionals, specialists, children, or any other restricted group. Labels a product, service, or category that anyone may use or purchase.
一般用(いっぱんよう)の薬(くすり)を買(か)った。
I bought an over-the-counter medicine.
このソフトは一般用(いっぱんよう)ではなく、業務用(ぎょうむよう)だ。
This software is not for general users — it's for business use.
病院(びょういん)で処方(しょほう)される薬(くすり)と一般用(いっぱんよう)の市販薬(しはんやく)では、成分(せいぶん)や量(りょう)が異(こと)なります。
The medicines prescribed at the hospital and those for general use sold over the counter differ in their ingredients and dosage.

Composed of 一般(いっぱん) ("general, ordinary, the general public") + 用(よう) ("-use, for use"). The 〜用(よう) suffix is highly productive: 業務用(ぎょうむよう) (for business/commercial use), 家庭用(かていよう) (for home use), 医療用(いりょうよう) (for medical use), 子供用(こどもよう) (for children).

USAGE:

  • Functions as a noun but almost always modifies a following noun, directly or via の: 一般用(いっぱんよう)医薬品(いやくひん), 一般用(いっぱんよう)のソフト.
  • Most distinctive in the pharmaceutical industry: 一般用(いっぱんよう)医薬品(いやくひん) is the official Japanese regulatory category for over-the-counter (OTC) medicines, as opposed to 医療用(いりょうよう)医薬品(いやくひん) (prescription drugs).
  • Also appears on product packaging to distinguish consumer-grade versions from professional versions (cooking equipment, cleaning supplies, printers, etc.).
  • A close but distinct label in media is 一般向(いっぱんむ)け, which means "aimed at a general audience" (e.g., for films and books suitable for all ages), and partly overlaps with 一般用(いっぱんよう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一般用(いっぱんよう)医薬品(いやくひん): over-the-counter drugs (regulatory term)
  • 一般用(いっぱんよう)のソフト: consumer-grade software
  • 一般用(いっぱんよう)電気(でんき)製品(せいひん): general-use electrical appliances
  • 一般用(いっぱんよう)と業務用(ぎょうむよう): consumer-use vs. business-use
  • 一般用(いっぱんよう)の入口(いりぐち): entrance for general visitors

ANTONYM / CONTRAST:

  • 業務用(ぎょうむよう): for commercial/business use — professional grade, typically sold to shops, restaurants, factories.
  • 医療用(いりょうよう): for medical/clinical use — prescription-grade, handled by medical professionals.
  • 専門用(せんもんよう): for specialist / professional use.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一般向(いっぱんむ)け: aimed at general audiences — overlapping term, preferred for films, books, and other content; emphasizes "target audience" rather than "intended use."
  • 市販(しはん): commercially available, retail — focuses on being sold in ordinary stores rather than on the target user.
