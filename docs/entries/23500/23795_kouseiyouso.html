構成要素(こうせいようそ)

こうせいようそ
noun
component; constituent element; building block
1. component; constituent element; building block
The individual elements that together make up a whole. Used across fields — grammar, chemistry, software, systems engineering, social theory — to refer to the parts of which something is composed.
重要(じゅうよう)な構成要素(こうせいようそ)。
An important component.
家族(かぞく)は社会(しゃかい)の基本的(きほんてき)な構成要素(こうせいようそ)だ。
The family is a fundamental component of society.
タンパク質(しつ)は生命(せいめい)の最(もっと)も重要(じゅうよう)な構成要素(こうせいようそ)の一(ひと)つである。
Proteins are one of the most important constituent elements of life.
このシステムは複数(ふくすう)の構成要素(こうせいようそ)から成(な)り立(た)っており、それぞれが特定(とくてい)の役割(やくわり)を担(にな)っている。
This system consists of multiple components, each of which plays a specific role.

Compound of 構成(こうせい) ('composition; structure') and 要素(ようそ) ('element; factor'). A set expression meaning 'the elements that make up the composition' — i.e., the parts of a whole.

USAGE:
Standard technical and academic vocabulary. Common across natural sciences, social sciences, engineering, and grammar. Often used with から成(な)り立(た)つ ('to be composed of') or から構成(こうせい)される ('to be constituted by').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重要(じゅうよう)な構成要素(こうせいようそ): an important component
  • 基本的(きほんてき)な構成要素(こうせいようそ): a fundamental component
  • 主(おも)な構成要素(こうせいようそ): the main components
  • 構成要素(こうせいようそ)を分析(ぶんせき)する: to analyze the components
  • 構成要素(こうせいようそ)に分解(ぶんかい)する: to break down into components
  • 構成要素(こうせいようそ)の一(ひと)つ: one of the components
  • 〜の構成要素(こうせいようそ)となる: to constitute a component of ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 要素(ようそ): element; factor — broader; can include abstract factors
  • 成分(せいぶん): ingredient; chemical component — specifically for substances, food, medicine
  • 部品(ぶひん): part; component part — physical parts of machines or products
  • コンポーネント: component — English loanword; used in IT and engineering
  • 構成(こうせい): composition; structure — the act or result of composing
  • 因子(いんし): factor — used in mathematics, statistics, genetics
