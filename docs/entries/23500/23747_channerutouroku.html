チャンネル登録(とうろく)

ちゃんねるとうろく
noun
channel subscription (e.g., YouTube)
1. channel subscription; subscribing to a channel
Subscribing to a content creator's channel on a video platform such as YouTube, so that the viewer is notified of new uploads and the channel appears in their subscription feed. Heard constantly in YouTuber videos in the standard appeal to viewers.
チャンネル登録(とうろく)をお願(ねが)いします。
Please subscribe to the channel.
面白(おもしろ)い動画(どうが)だったので、チャンネル登録(とうろく)した。
The video was interesting, so I subscribed to the channel.
チャンネル登録(とうろく)者数(しゃすう)が10万人(まんにん)を超(こ)えると銀(ぎん)の盾(たて)がもらえる。
When the number of channel subscribers exceeds 100,000, you receive a silver play button.

A set phrase coined with the spread of YouTube in Japan. The full appeal phrase チャンネル登録(とうろく)よろしく}お{願(ねが)いします is one of the most-heard sentences in Japanese YouTube videos.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)する: to subscribe to the channel
  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)を解除(かいじょ)する: to unsubscribe from the channel
  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)者(しゃ): subscriber(s) (of a channel)
  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)者数(しゃすう): subscriber count
  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)ボタン: subscribe button
  • チャンネル登録(とうろく)よろしく}お{願(ねが)いします: 'Please subscribe!' (standard YouTuber closing line)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 購読(こうどく): subscription (reading/content) — often used for paid subscriptions to magazines, newspapers, or online publications
  • 定期購読(ていきこうどく): regular subscription (print or digital) — emphasizes recurring payment
  • フォロー: follow — used on social media (Twitter, Instagram); a lighter, non-notification-based version of following

USAGE:
チャンネル登録(とうろく) is specifically used for video channels (YouTube and similar platforms). For social media following, フォロー is used instead. For paid subscriptions, サブスク (short for サブスクリプション) or 定期購読(ていきこうどく) is more common.
