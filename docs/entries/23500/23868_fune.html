舟(ふね)

ふね
noun
small boat; rowboat
1. small boat — a small watercraft, typically propelled by oars, poles, or a small sail, used on rivers, lakes, or along the coast
A small watercraft such as a rowboat, canoe, or skiff. 舟(ふね) evokes a traditional, human-powered vessel and is contrasted with the larger character 船(ふね), which is used for ships and larger modern boats. Both are read ふね, but the choice of kanji conveys a difference in scale and register.
舟(ふね)に乗(の)る。
To get on a small boat.
川(かわ)に小(ちい)さな舟(ふね)が浮(う)かんでいる。
A small boat is floating on the river.
漁師(りょうし)は毎朝(まいあさ)舟(ふね)をこいで湖(みずうみ)に出(で)ていく。
The fisherman rows out onto the lake on his small boat every morning.

舟(ふね) and 船(ふね) share the same reading but are written with different kanji depending on the size and type of vessel.

USAGE:

  • 舟(ふね) is used for small, often traditional craft — rowboats, canoes, sampans, and the like — especially when they are human-powered or very modest in size.
  • 船(ふね) is the general and modern term, used for ships, ferries, and larger boats of any kind.
  • In ambiguous cases or everyday writing, 船(ふね) is the default. 舟(ふね) is chosen to evoke a smaller, more rustic, or literary image.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 舟(ふね)に乗(の)る: to get on a (small) boat
  • 舟(ふね)をこぐ: to row a boat (also idiomatic for nodding off to sleep)
  • 舟(ふね)を出(だ)す: to launch a boat; to set out in a boat
  • 小(ちい)さな舟(ふね): a small boat
  • 渡(わた)し舟(ぶね): a ferry boat (across a river)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 船(ふね): ship, boat (general) — preferred for larger modern vessels such as ferries, cargo ships, and passenger ships.
  • {ボート}: boat — the English loanword, common for rowboats or pleasure craft such as those in parks.
  • 舟艇(しゅうてい): small craft — a technical term, used in military or maritime contexts.

CULTURAL NOTE:
The idiom 舟(ふね)をこぐ literally means 'to row a boat' but is commonly used to describe someone nodding off to sleep, since the up-and-down motion of the head resembles the motion of rowing.
