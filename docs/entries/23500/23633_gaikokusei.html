外国製(がいこくせい)

がいこくせい
noun
foreign-made; of foreign manufacture
1. foreign-made; of foreign manufacture
Made in a country other than Japan — used to indicate that a product was manufactured abroad. Functions either as a noun or, more often, as a modifier attached directly to a product name or with の.
これは外国製(がいこくせい)の車(くるま)だ。
This is a foreign-made car.
外国製(がいこくせい)の部品(ぶひん)を使(つか)っている。
It uses foreign-made parts.
外国製(がいこくせい)の家電(かでん)は、日本(にほん)の電圧(でんあつ)に対応(たいおう)しているか確認(かくにん)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
With foreign-made appliances, you need to check whether they are compatible with Japan's voltage.

Composed of 外国(がいこく) ("foreign country") + 製(せい) ("-made, manufactured by"). The 〜製(せい) suffix is extremely productive and attaches to country names, company names, and material names: 日本製(にほんせい) (Japan-made), ドイツ製(どいつせい) (German-made), 革製(かわせい) (leather-made), 手製(てせい) (hand-made).

USAGE:

  • Modifies a following noun either directly or via の: 外国製(がいこくせい)車(しゃ) or 外国製(がいこくせい)の車(くるま).
  • Used when the specific country of origin is unknown, unimportant, or being generalized. When the country is known, the specific form (アメリカ製(あめりかせい), 韓国製(かんこくせい), etc.) is strongly preferred.
  • Often appears in product labeling, customs/tax discussions, and consumer advice about compatibility, warranties, and repair availability.
  • Carries connotations depending on context: sometimes suggests higher quality or prestige (外国製(がいこくせい)の高級車(こうきゅうしゃ), a luxury foreign car), sometimes caution (concerns about voltage, spare-parts availability, or food-safety standards).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の車(くるま): a foreign-made car
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の部品(ぶひん): foreign-made parts
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の家電(かでん): foreign-made appliances
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)品(ひん): foreign-made goods
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)タバコ(たばこ): foreign-made cigarettes
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の薬(くすり): foreign-made medicines

ANTONYM / CONTRAST:

  • 国産(こくさん): domestic-made — produced in one's own country (Japan, from a Japanese speaker's perspective).
  • 日本製(にほんせい): made in Japan — the explicit positive counterpart of 外国製(がいこくせい).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 舶来品(はくらいひん): imported goods — an older, somewhat old-fashioned word with overtones of "prestige import"; far less common today.
  • 輸入品(ゆにゅうひん): imported goods — emphasizes the importation process rather than the origin; partly overlaps with 外国製(がいこくせい).
  • 海外製(かいがいせい): made overseas — near-synonym; preferred in some marketing contexts, slightly softer sounding.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 〜製(せい): -made (productive suffix)
  • 原産国(げんさんこく): country of origin
  • メーカー(めーかー): manufacturer
