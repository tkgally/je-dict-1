言語能力(げんごのうりょく)

げんごのうりょく
noun
language ability; linguistic competence
1. language ability; linguistic competence; proficiency in understanding and using language
A person's capacity to understand, produce, and use language — whether their native language or a foreign one. The word is common in educational, psychological, medical (language development or impairment), and linguistic discussions; it also appears in job postings about required foreign-language skills.
彼(かれ)は言語能力(げんごのうりょく)が高(たか)い。
He has strong language ability.
子(こ)どもの言語能力(げんごのうりょく)は読書(どくしょ)で伸(の)びる。
Children's language ability grows through reading.
留学(りゅうがく)を通(つう)じて、彼女(かのじょ)の英語(えいご)の言語能力(げんごのうりょく)は大(おお)きく向上(こうじょう)した。
Through studying abroad, her English language ability improved significantly.

Compound of 言語(げんご) (language) and 能力(のうりょく) (ability, capacity). It is a formal, somewhat academic word — more technical than the everyday expression "日本語(にほんご)が上手(じょうず)" (good at Japanese). It covers four classical skill areas — listening, speaking, reading, and writing — and is often used to discuss testing, child development, and brain function.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言語能力(げんごのうりょく)が高(たか)い: to have high language ability
  • 言語能力(げんごのうりょく)を伸(の)ばす: to build up one's language ability
  • 言語能力(げんごのうりょく)を向上(こうじょう)させる: to improve language ability
  • 英語(えいご)の言語能力(げんごのうりょく): English language ability
  • 言語能力(げんごのうりょく)の発達(はったつ): the development of language ability

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 語学力(ごがくりょく): foreign language ability — typically refers to learned foreign languages, not one's native language
  • 国語力(こくごりょく): Japanese language ability — used especially in education about a child's command of Japanese
  • コミュニケーション{能力(のうりょく)|こみゅにけーしょんのうりょく}: communication ability — broader, covers nonverbal skills too
  • 言語運用(げんごうんよう)能力(のうりょく): linguistic performance — a more technical term from linguistics, close to Chomsky's "performance"
