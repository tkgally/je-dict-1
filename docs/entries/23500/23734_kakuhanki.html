攪拌器(かくはんき)

かくはんき
noun
mixer; agitator; stirrer
1. mixer; agitator; stirrer
A device or appliance that mixes liquids, pastes, or powders by rotating blades or a paddle. Found in kitchens (food mixers, blenders), laboratories (magnetic stirrers, vortex mixers), construction (mortar mixers), and industrial plants (large tanks with powered impellers).
攪拌器(かくはんき)で混(ま)ぜる。
Mix it with a mixer.
実験室(じっけんしつ)の攪拌器(かくはんき)が故障(こしょう)した。
The stirrer in the laboratory is broken.
生(なま)クリームは小型(こがた)の攪拌器(かくはんき)を使(つか)うと短時間(たんじかん)で泡立(あわだ)つ。
Using a small mixer, you can whip fresh cream in a short time.
工場(こうじょう)では大型(おおがた)の攪拌器(かくはんき)が液体(えきたい)を一定(いってい)の速度(そくど)で回(まわ)し続(つづ)けている。
In the factory a large agitator keeps rotating the liquid at a constant speed.

Composed of 攪拌(かくはん) (stirring, agitating) and the suffix -器(き) (instrument, apparatus). Sometimes written 撹拌器(かくはんき) with the simplified form of the first character. The same word covers small kitchen mixers and large industrial agitators; the specific kind is usually clear from context or from a modifier such as 小型(こがた) (small) or 業務用(ぎょうむよう) (commercial-use).

USAGE:
Appears in technical writing (chemistry, food engineering, construction) and on product labels. In casual everyday conversation about the kitchen, speakers more often use the katakana loanwords ミキサー (mixer/blender), ハンドミキサー (hand mixer), or ブレンダー (blender); 攪拌器(かくはんき) sounds more formal or technical.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 攪拌器(かくはんき)で混(ま)ぜる: to mix with a mixer
  • 攪拌器(かくはんき)を使(つか)う: to use a mixer / stirrer
  • 磁気(じき)攪拌器(かくはんき): magnetic stirrer
  • 電動(でんどう)攪拌器(かくはんき): electric mixer
  • 工業用(こうぎょうよう)攪拌器(かくはんき): industrial-use agitator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ミキサー: mixer / blender — the common kitchen-appliance term
  • ハンドミキサー: hand(-held) mixer — a small, handheld kitchen mixer
  • ブレンダー: blender — typically a jar-type appliance for smoothies and purees
  • 泡立(あわだ)て器(き): whisk — a non-powered hand tool for whipping
  • 撹拌機(かくはんき): agitator / mixing machine — a variant using -機(き) (machine); often used for larger, industrial equipment
