受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)

うけとめかた
noun
way of taking something (mentally); interpretation; reaction
1. way of taking (something said or done); way of interpreting; way of reacting to
How a person mentally or emotionally takes in something — news, criticism, an event, or another person's words. Emphasizes that different people may react or interpret things in different ways.
受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)が違(ちが)う。
We take it differently.
人(ひと)によってニュースの受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)はさまざまだ。
People take the news in all sorts of different ways.
同(おな)じ言葉(ことば)でも、受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)次第(しだい)で傷(きず)つくこともある。
Even the same words can hurt depending on how you take them.
先生(せんせい)の注意(ちゅうい)を素直(すなお)に受(う)け止(と)められるかどうかは、その子(こ)の受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)次第(しだい)だ。
Whether a child can take the teacher's advice in good faith depends on how that child reacts to it.

Composed of 受(う)け止(と)める (to catch, receive; to take in or come to terms with) and the suffix ～方(かた) (way of, manner of). The verb 受(う)け止(と)める originally means to catch a ball or other physical thing, but is overwhelmingly used figuratively for taking in news, criticism, reality, or emotions.

USAGE:
Commonly used in discussions about communication, interpretation, and differing perspectives. Emphasizes the subjective side — how the listener or observer takes something, rather than the objective message.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)が違(ちが)う: the way of taking it differs
  • 受(う)け止(と)め方(かた)次第(しだい): depending on how one takes it
  • 人(ひと)それぞれの受(う)け止(と)め方(かた): each person's own way of taking it
  • 国民(こくみん)の受(う)け止(と)め方(かた): the public's reaction (often in news commentary)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 受(う)け取(と)り方(かた): way of receiving/interpreting — very close in meaning; slightly more neutral and often used for physical or literal interpretation
  • 解釈(かいしゃく): interpretation — emphasizes analytical, deliberate interpretation
  • 捉(とら)え方(かた): way of grasping/perceiving — emphasizes how one conceptualizes something
  • 感(かん)じ方(かた): way of feeling — emphasizes emotional reaction rather than mental interpretation
