録音機(ろくおんき)

ろくおんき
noun
recording device; audio recorder
1. recording device; (audio) recorder; tape recorder
A device for making audio recordings. Historically referred to tape and reel-to-reel machines; today the word is still used generically for audio recorders, though in casual conversation about handheld digital recorders speakers usually say {ボイスレコーダー} or ICレコーダー instead.
録音機(ろくおんき)をオンにする。
To turn on the recorder.
父(ちち)は古(ふる)い録音機(ろくおんき)をずっと使(つか)っている。
My father has been using an old recorder for a long time.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)を録音機(ろくおんき)で記録(きろく)しておいた。
I recorded the contents of the meeting on an audio recorder.
昔(むかし)は大(おお)きなテープの録音機(ろくおんき)を使(つか)っていたが、今(いま)はスマホ1台(だい)で十分(じゅうぶん)だ。
In the past we used big tape recorders, but today a single smartphone is enough.

Composed of 録音(ろくおん) (sound recording) and the suffix -機(き) (machine, device). The native Sino-Japanese term for a sound-recording device, contrasting with the katakana loanword レコーダー.

USAGE:
In writing and more formal contexts, 録音機(ろくおんき) is often preferred, particularly when discussing audio recording in general terms or historical equipment. In everyday spoken Japanese, the more specific loanwords ボイスレコーダー} or ICレコーダー are more common when referring to modern handheld digital recorders. {録音機(ろくおんき) can sound slightly old-fashioned when used for very current consumer devices.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 録音機(ろくおんき)を使(つか)う: to use a recorder
  • 録音機(ろくおんき)を作動(さどう)させる: to operate / run a recorder
  • 録音機(ろくおんき)で録音(ろくおん)する: to make a recording on a recorder
  • 小型(こがた)の録音機(ろくおんき): a small / compact recorder
  • 携帯(けいたい)録音機(ろくおんき): a portable recorder

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • レコーダー: recorder — the katakana loanword; covers both audio and video recorders, and is the more common everyday term for modern devices
  • {ボイスレコーダー}: voice recorder — a handheld audio recorder for speech (interviews, notes)
  • ICレコーダー: IC recorder; digital voice recorder — the solid-state successor to cassette voice recorders; often overlaps with {ボイスレコーダー}
  • テープレコーダー: tape recorder — specifically a cassette tape recorder
  • 録画機(ろくがき): video recorder — parallel formation with 画(が) (image) instead of 音(おん) (sound)
