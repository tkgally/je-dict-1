正三角形(せいさんかくけい)

せいさんかくけい
noun
equilateral triangle
1. equilateral triangle
A triangle in which all three sides are of equal length and, equivalently, all three interior angles are equal at 60°. The most symmetric of the triangle types and a foundational figure in elementary and middle-school geometry.
正三角形(せいさんかくけい)を描(か)く。
Draw an equilateral triangle.
正三角形(せいさんかくけい)の内角(ないかく)はすべて60度(ろくじゅうど)だ。
All interior angles of an equilateral triangle are 60 degrees.
コンパスと定規(じょうぎ)があれば正三角形(せいさんかくけい)は簡単(かんたん)に作図(さくず)できる。
With a compass and a ruler, you can easily construct an equilateral triangle.
一辺(いっぺん)の長(なが)さが分(わ)かれば、正三角形(せいさんかくけい)の面積(めんせき)や高(たか)さは公式(こうしき)を使(つか)って求(もと)められる。
If you know the length of one side, you can find the area and height of an equilateral triangle using a formula.

Composed of 正(せい) (regular; correct, used as a geometric prefix meaning 'regular' as in 正方形(せいほうけい) 'square'), 三角(さんかく) (triangle), and 形(けい) (shape). The 正(せい) prefix indicates that all sides and angles are equal, paralleling 'regular polygon' in English.

USAGE:
A core term in elementary and middle-school mathematics. The reading せいさんかくけい} is the standard textbook reading, but {せいさんかっけい} (with sokuon) is also widely heard and is often listed as an alternative reading. Often appears alongside {直角三角形(ちょっかくさんかくけい) (right triangle) and 二等辺三角形(にとうへんさんかくけい) (isosceles triangle) when classifying triangles. The 正(せい)〜形(けい) pattern extends to 正方形(せいほうけい) (regular square) and 正多角形(せいたかくけい) (regular polygon).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正三角形(せいさんかくけい)を描(か)く: to draw an equilateral triangle
  • 正三角形(せいさんかくけい)を作図(さくず)する: to construct an equilateral triangle (in geometry)
  • 正三角形(せいさんかくけい)の面積(めんせき): area of an equilateral triangle
  • 正三角形(せいさんかくけい)の一辺(いっぺん): one side of an equilateral triangle

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 三角形(さんかくけい): triangle — the general term
  • 二等辺三角形(にとうへんさんかくけい): isosceles triangle — two sides equal, the next-most-symmetric type
  • 直角三角形(ちょっかくさんかくけい): right triangle — has one 90° angle
  • 正方形(せいほうけい): square (regular quadrilateral) — built from the same 正(せい)〜形(けい) pattern
  • 正多角形(せいたかくけい): regular polygon — the broader category to which 正三角形(せいさんかくけい) belongs
