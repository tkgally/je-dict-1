在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)

ざいたくいりょう
noun
home healthcare, home medical care
1. home healthcare — medical care provided to patients at their own home, by visiting doctors, nurses, or other medical professionals
Medical care delivered at a patient's home rather than in a hospital or clinic, typically through scheduled visits by doctors (往診(おうしん)) and nurses (訪問(ほうもん)看護(かんご)). Especially important for elderly people with chronic illnesses, patients recovering after discharge, and terminal patients receiving palliative care.
在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)を受(う)ける。
To receive home medical care.
高齢(こうれい)の両親(りょうしん)のために在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)を選(えら)んだ。
We chose home healthcare for our elderly parents.
地域(ちいき)の在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)の体制(たいせい)が整(ととの)ってきた。
The home healthcare system in the local area has been improving.
在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)では、医師(いし)と看護師(かんごし)が定期的(ていきてき)に患者(かんじゃ)の家(いえ)を訪問(ほうもん)する。
In home healthcare, doctors and nurses regularly visit patients at their homes.

Compound of 在宅(ざいたく) ('being at home') and 医療(いりょう) ('medical care').

USAGE:

  • Refers to a care model in which medical professionals visit patients at home, as opposed to the patient going to a hospital or clinic.
  • A key concept in Japan's aging-society healthcare policy; closely tied to 地域(ちいき)包括(ほうかつ)ケア ('community-based integrated care').
  • Distinct from 在宅(ざいたく)介護(かいご) ('home care/nursing'), which focuses on daily-living assistance rather than medical treatment, though the two are often combined in practice.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)を受(う)ける: to receive home medical care
  • 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)を提供(ていきょう)する: to provide home medical care
  • 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)制度(せいど): the home healthcare system
  • 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう)に切(き)り替(か)える: to switch to home healthcare

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 往診(おうしん): house call (by a doctor) — a component of 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう); a single visit rather than an ongoing model of care.
  • 訪問(ほうもん)看護(かんご): visiting nursing — a related nursing service; also often part of 在宅医療(ざいたくいりょう).
  • 在宅(ざいたく)介護(かいご): home care, home nursing care — focuses on daily-living support (bathing, feeding, etc.) rather than medical treatment.
  • 通院(つういん): going to a hospital/clinic for treatment — the usual contrast.
