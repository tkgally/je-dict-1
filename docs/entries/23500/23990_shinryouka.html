診療科(しんりょうか)

しんりょうか
noun
medical department; clinical specialty
1. medical department; clinical specialty
A division of a hospital or clinic organized around a medical specialty, such as internal medicine, pediatrics, or orthopedics. The term appears on hospital directories, signage, and insurance paperwork to direct patients to the appropriate doctor.
診療科(しんりょうか)を選(えら)ぶ。
To choose a medical department.
この病院(びょういん)には二十(にじゅう)の診療科(しんりょうか)がある。
This hospital has twenty medical departments.
症状(しょうじょう)に合(あ)った診療科(しんりょうか)を受診(じゅしん)してください。
Please consult the medical department that matches your symptoms.

Compound of 診療(しんりょう) ('medical examination and treatment') and 科(か) ('department; section'). The 科(か) element is the same suffix seen in school subject names like 理科(りか) and in other institutional divisions; here it designates the medical specialty each department covers.

USAGE:

  • When patients make an appointment or visit reception, they are usually asked which 診療科(しんりょうか) they wish to see.
  • Hospital websites list available departments under a 診療科(しんりょうか)一覧(いちらん) ('list of departments') menu.
  • 総合(そうごう)診療科(しんりょうか) ('general medicine department') exists at larger hospitals for patients who are unsure which specialty fits their symptoms.

COMMON DEPARTMENTS:

  • 内科(ないか): internal medicine
  • 外科(げか): surgery
  • 小児科(しょうにか): pediatrics
  • 産婦人科(さんふじんか): obstetrics and gynecology
  • 眼科(がんか): ophthalmology
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか): otolaryngology (ENT)
  • 整形外科(せいけいげか): orthopedics
  • 皮膚科(ひふか): dermatology
  • 精神科(せいしんか): psychiatry

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 診療科(しんりょうか)一覧(いちらん): list of departments
  • 診療科(しんりょうか)別(べつ): by department
  • 診療科(しんりょうか)を受診(じゅしん)する: to consult a department
  • 該当(がいとう)の診療科(しんりょうか): the relevant department

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 科(か): department; field — used as a suffix (e.g., 内科(ないか)), rarely standing alone in the medical sense.
  • 病棟(びょうとう): hospital ward — a physical ward within a hospital, not a clinical specialty.
  • 専門医(せんもんい): specialist doctor — a doctor certified in a specific 診療科(しんりょうか).
