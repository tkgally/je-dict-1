営業所(えいぎょうしょ)

えいぎょうしょ
noun
sales office; business office; branch office
1. sales office; business office; (local) branch office
A local office of a company that handles sales, customer service, or regional operations — typically smaller than a headquarters or 支店(してん) (branch), and often focused on logistics, pickups, deliveries, or regional clients. Common for shipping companies, utilities, and sales-driven businesses.
近(ちか)くの営業所(えいぎょうしょ)に電話(でんわ)した。
I called the nearest branch office.
荷物(にもつ)は最寄(もよ)りの営業所(えいぎょうしょ)で受(う)け取(と)れます。
You can pick up the package at your nearest branch office.
新規(しんき)取引(とりひき)のため、大阪(おおさか)の営業所(えいぎょうしょ)で打(う)ち合(あ)わせを行(おこな)った。
For the new business deal, we held a meeting at the Osaka branch office.

Composed of 営業(えいぎょう) (business, sales) and 所(しょ) (place). Most Japanese learners first encounter the word with delivery companies — 宅配(たくはい)便(びん) companies like ヤマト{運輸(うんゆ)} or 佐川急便(さがわきゅうびん) call their local pickup/drop-off centers 営業所(えいぎょうしょ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最寄(もよ)りの営業所(えいぎょうしょ): the nearest branch office
  • 営業所(えいぎょうしょ)に問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to make an inquiry at the branch office
  • 営業所(えいぎょうしょ)止(ど)め: "hold at branch office" (for package delivery)
  • 大阪(おおさか)営業所(えいぎょうしょ): the Osaka branch office
  • 営業所(えいぎょうしょ)長(ちょう): branch office manager

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 支店(してん): branch (of a chain store, bank, etc.) — typically larger and more full-service than a 営業所(えいぎょうしょ)
  • 本社(ほんしゃ): head office, headquarters
  • 事業所(じぎょうしょ): business establishment — a broader legal/accounting term covering any workplace of a company
  • 出張所(しゅっちょうじょ): sub-office, local outlet — usually smaller than a 営業所(えいぎょうしょ)
