禁固刑(きんこけい)

きんこけい
noun
imprisonment without labor; confinement
1. imprisonment without labor (as a criminal sentence)
A form of imprisonment under Japanese criminal law in which the convict is confined to a prison but, unlike 懲役刑(ちょうえきけい), is not required to perform compulsory labor. Historically given for crimes considered to involve no moral turpitude, such as some negligence offenses. Under criminal-law reforms that take effect from 2025, 禁固刑(きんこけい) is being unified with 懲役刑(ちょうえきけい) into a single sentence called 拘禁刑(こうきんけい).
被告(ひこく)は禁固刑(きんこけい)を受(う)けた。
The defendant received a sentence of imprisonment without labor.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)は禁固刑(きんこけい)3年(ねん)を言(い)い渡(わた)した。
The court handed down three years' imprisonment without labor.
過失(かしつ)運転(うんてん)致死(ちし)罪(ざい)で禁固刑(きんこけい)が科(か)される場合(ばあい)もある。
In cases of negligent driving causing death, imprisonment without labor may also be imposed.
法律(ほうりつ)の改正(かいせい)により、懲役刑(ちょうえきけい)と禁固刑(きんこけい)は将来(しょうらい)的(てき)に一本化(いっぽんか)される予定(よてい)だ。
Due to changes in the law, imprisonment with labor and imprisonment without labor are scheduled to be unified in the future.

Composed of 禁固(きんこ) (confinement, being locked up) and -刑(けい) (punishment, sentence). Sometimes also written 禁錮刑(きんこけい) using the original jōyō-kanji form {錮}, especially in legal documents. Refers specifically to the criminal-law sentence, not to detention in general.

USAGE:
A technical legal term; appears in court news, legal textbooks, and novels involving crime or trials. In everyday speech, people often say 刑務所(けいむしょ)に入(はい)る (to go to prison) without distinguishing the type of sentence. Journalists frequently use 禁固(きんこ) (without -刑(けい)) as a modifier, for example 禁固(きんこ)3年(ねん) (three years' imprisonment without labor).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 禁固刑(きんこけい)を科(か)す: to impose a sentence of imprisonment without labor
  • 禁固刑(きんこけい)を受(う)ける: to receive a sentence of imprisonment without labor
  • 禁固(きんこ)3年(ねん)の判決(はんけつ): a verdict of three years' imprisonment without labor
  • 執行(しっこう)猶予(ゆうよ)付(つ)きの禁固刑(きんこけい): imprisonment without labor with suspended execution
  • 禁固刑(きんこけい)に処(しょ)される: to be sentenced to imprisonment without labor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 懲役刑(ちょうえきけい): imprisonment with labor — inmates are required to perform prison labor
  • 拘禁刑(こうきんけい): confinement sentence — the new unified sentence that will replace both 懲役刑(ちょうえきけい) and 禁固刑(きんこけい)
  • 罰金刑(ばっきんけい): fine (as a sentence) — a monetary penalty imposed by the court
  • 拘留(こうりゅう): short-term detention — a minor confinement sentence of 1-30 days
  • 執行(しっこう)猶予(ゆうよ): suspended sentence — the sentence is not carried out immediately, pending good behavior
