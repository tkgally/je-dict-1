神経痛(しんけいつう)

しんけいつう
noun
neuralgia; nerve pain
1. neuralgia; nerve pain
Sharp, often shooting pain that runs along the course of a nerve, typically triggered by cold weather, pressure, or nerve damage. A common complaint associated with aging, and often discussed in everyday conversation about aches and pains.
神経痛(しんけいつう)がひどい。
My neuralgia is bad.
寒(さむ)くなると神経痛(しんけいつう)が出(で)る。
When it gets cold, I get neuralgia.
祖父(そふ)は長年(ながねん)腰(こし)の神経痛(しんけいつう)に悩(なや)まされている。
My grandfather has suffered from neuralgia in his lower back for many years.

Compound of 神経(しんけい) ('nerve') and 痛(つう) ('pain'). Refers specifically to pain radiating along a nerve pathway, as distinct from muscle pain or joint pain. In casual speech, older speakers often use 神経痛(しんけいつう) to describe any persistent, weather-sensitive pain, even when the medical diagnosis is something else.

USAGE:

  • Commonly paired with body-part modifiers: 坐骨(ざこつ)神経痛(しんけいつう) (sciatica), 肋間(ろっかん)神経痛(しんけいつう) (intercostal neuralgia), 三叉(さんさ)神経痛(しんけいつう) (trigeminal neuralgia).
  • Often described as appearing in cold weather or on rainy days; the folk association with weather is strong enough that people joke about using their neuralgia as a weather forecast.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 神経痛(しんけいつう)がする: to have neuralgic pain
  • 神経痛(しんけいつう)が出(で)る: neuralgia flares up
  • 神経痛(しんけいつう)に悩(なや)まされる: to suffer from neuralgia
  • 神経痛(しんけいつう)を和(やわ)らげる: to ease neuralgia
  • 坐骨(ざこつ)神経痛(しんけいつう): sciatica

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頭痛(ずつう): headache — pain in the head specifically.
  • 腰痛(ようつう): lower back pain — a more general term that does not specify nerve involvement.
  • 筋肉痛(きんにくつう): muscle pain; soreness — typically from exercise, unrelated to nerves.
  • 関節痛(かんせつつう): joint pain — pain in the joints rather than along a nerve.
