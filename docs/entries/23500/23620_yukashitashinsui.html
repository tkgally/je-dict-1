床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)

ゆかしたしんすい
noun
below-floor flooding; flooding that does not rise above the floor level
1. below-floor flooding; flooding where water enters the space under the floor but does not rise above floor level
A category of flood damage, defined in Japanese disaster statistics, in which floodwater has entered the crawl space or ground beneath a building but has not risen above the floor. Less severe than 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい) (above-floor flooding), but still a significant form of damage.
この地域(ちいき)では床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)が多(おお)かった。
There was a lot of below-floor flooding in this area.
台風(たいふう)で近所(きんじょ)の家(いえ)が床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)の被害(ひがい)を受(う)けた。
The neighbor's house suffered below-floor flood damage from the typhoon.
床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)でも、放(ほう)っておくと湿気(しっけ)やカビの原因(げんいん)になる。
Even below-floor flooding can lead to dampness and mold if left untreated.
市内(しない)で床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)が報告(ほうこく)された住宅(じゅうたく)は百軒(ひゃっけん)を超(こ)えるという。
More than one hundred homes in the city were reported to have suffered below-floor flooding.

Composed of 床下(ゆかした) (under the floor) and 浸水(しんすい) (flooding, water damage). A set phrase used in official disaster reports and news coverage.

USAGE:
In Japanese disaster statistics, flooding damage to houses is officially classified into 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい) (above-floor flooding) and 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) (below-floor flooding). You will see these two categories in municipal damage reports, weather news, and insurance documents. 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) is the less severe category: water has entered under the house but the living space above is still dry.

Even though floors remain dry, 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) should not be ignored. Residents typically need to drain, clean, and disinfect the crawl space to prevent mold, rot, and pest problems later.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)の被害(ひがい): below-floor flood damage
  • 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)が発生(はっせい)する: below-floor flooding occurs
  • 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい)○{○軒(けん)}: N homes affected by below-floor flooding

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい): above-floor flooding — the more severe category, where water rises above the floor
  • 浸水(しんすい): flooding; water intrusion — the general term
  • 冠水(かんすう): submergence (of roads, fields) — flooding of an area rather than a specific building
  • 水害(すいがい): flood disaster; water damage — the broadest term for damage caused by water
