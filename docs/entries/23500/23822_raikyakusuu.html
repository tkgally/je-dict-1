来客数(らいきゃくすう)

らいきゃくすう
noun
number of visitors; customer count; attendance
1. number of visitors; customer count; number of people who come to a place
A numerical count of visitors, guests, or customers coming to a business, event, facility, or home. Common in retail, hospitality, and event management to track attendance. Can describe shop customers, exhibition attendees, or household visitors depending on context.
来客数(らいきゃくすう)が増(ふ)えた。
The number of customers increased.
今月(こんげつ)の来客数(らいきゃくすう)は先月(せんげつ)より多(おお)い。
This month's customer count is higher than last month's.
週末(しゅうまつ)のイベントでは来客数(らいきゃくすう)が予想(よそう)を大(おお)きく上回(うわまわ)り、スタッフ全員(すたっふぜんいん)が対応(たいおう)に追(お)われた。
At the weekend event, the number of visitors greatly exceeded expectations, and the entire staff was kept busy handling them.

Compound of 来客(らいきゃく) ('visitor; guest; customer') and 数(すう) ('number; count'). The suffix 〜数(すう) is productive in business and statistics: 人数(にんずう) ('number of people'), 会員数(かいいんすう) ('membership count'), 閲覧数(えつらんすう) ('pageviews').

USAGE:

  • Treated as a noun; does not take する.
  • Common verbs: が増(ふ)える / 減(へ)る ('increases / decreases'), を数(かぞ)える ('to count'), を集計(しゅうけい)する ('to tally'), を予想(よそう)する ('to forecast').
  • Used in retail reports, restaurant daily logs, tourism statistics, and event planning.
  • For household visitors, 来客(らいきゃく) alone is usually more natural; 来客数(らいきゃくすう) implies counting or tracking.
  • Very often paired with comparison expressions: より多(おお)い ('more than'), を上回(うわまわ)る / 下回(したまわ)る ('exceeds / falls below').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 来客数(らいきゃくすう)が増(ふ)える: the number of visitors increases
  • 来客数(らいきゃくすう)を集計(しゅうけい)する: to tally the customer count
  • 来客数(らいきゃくすう)の推移(すいい): trends in visitor numbers
  • 来客数(らいきゃくすう)を予想(よそう)する: to forecast the customer count
  • 一日(いちにち)の来客数(らいきゃくすう): daily customer count

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 来場者数(らいじょうしゃすう): number of attendees — preferred for events, exhibitions, and venues rather than shops.
  • 来店客数(らいてんきゃくすう): number of store customers — narrower; specifically for retail shops.
  • 集客数(しゅうきゃくすう): number of customers attracted — focuses on how many were drawn in, often used in marketing.
  • 入場者数(にゅうじょうしゃすう): number of admissions — for ticketed venues like stadiums, museums, and amusement parks.
