同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)

どうぞくきぎょう
noun
family business; family-owned company
1. family business; family-owned company
A company whose ownership and/or management is concentrated in the hands of a single family or a small number of related families. The term covers everything from small neighborhood shops to large publicly listed corporations in which the founding family retains a controlling stake or holds key executive posts.
うちは同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)だ。
Ours is a family business.
日本(にほん)には老舗(しにせ)の同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)が多(おお)い。
Japan has many long-established family-owned companies.
同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)には、意思(いし)決定(けってい)が速(はや)いという利点(りてん)がある。
Family-owned companies have the advantage of being able to make decisions quickly.
同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)では、後継者(こうけいしゃ)問題(もんだい)や親族(しんぞく)間(かん)の対立(たいりつ)が経営(けいえい)上(じょう)の大(おお)きな課題(かだい)となりやすい。
In family-owned companies, succession problems and conflicts among relatives easily become major management challenges.

Composed of 同族(どうぞく) (same family; same clan) and 企業(きぎょう) (enterprise; company). The 族(ぞく) here refers to the family lineage rather than ethnicity.

USAGE:
A neutral, descriptive term used in business news, accounting and tax contexts, and corporate governance discussions. Note the specialized tax-law sense: under Japan's Corporation Tax Act, a 同族会社(どうぞくがいしゃ) is a company in which three or fewer shareholders together own more than 50% of the shares — a status that triggers special anti-tax-avoidance rules. Outside of tax law, the everyday meaning is broader and synonymous with 'family business.' The reading どうぞく} is fixed; do not confuse with {同属(どうぞく) 'of the same genus.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)を経営(けいえい)する: to run a family business
  • 同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ: to inherit/take over a family business
  • 大手(おおて)同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう): a major family-owned company
  • 同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)の後継者(こうけいしゃ): successor in a family business

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 同族会社(どうぞくがいしゃ): family company — narrower technical term used in tax law
  • 家族経営(かぞくけいえい): family management — emphasizes management by family members; often a smaller scale
  • 老舗(しにせ): long-established business — emphasizes age and tradition; many 老舗(しにせ) are also 同族企業(どうぞくきぎょう)
  • ファミリービジネス: family business — katakana loanword, common in MBA-style writing
