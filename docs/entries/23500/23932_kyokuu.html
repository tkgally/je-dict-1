極右(きょくう)

きょくう
noun
far right; extreme right wing
1. far right; extreme right wing (in politics)
A political label for the extreme right end of the ideological spectrum — parties, groups, or individuals associated with strong nationalism, anti-immigration positions, or hardline conservatism. Used in news reporting, commentary, and academic writing about politics worldwide.
彼(かれ)は極右(きょくう)の政治家(せいじか)だ。
He is a far-right politician.
選挙(せんきょ)で極右(きょくう)政党(せいとう)が票(ひょう)を伸(の)ばした。
A far-right party gained votes in the election.
ヨーロッパでは近年(きんねん)、極右(きょくう)勢力(せいりょく)の台頭(たいとう)が懸念(けねん)されている。
In Europe there has been growing concern in recent years about the rise of far-right forces.

Literally "extreme right," from 極(きょく) (extreme, pole) and 右(う) (right). The word is neutral in form but almost always critical in context — speakers who use it are typically distancing themselves from the groups they describe. In Japanese political discourse it is sometimes also used for hardline nationalist or street-activist groups.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 極右(きょくう)政党(せいとう): far-right political party
  • 極右(きょくう)勢力(せいりょく): far-right forces
  • 極右(きょくう)思想(しそう): far-right ideology
  • 極右(きょくう)の台頭(たいとう): the rise of the far right
  • 極右(きょくう)と呼(よ)ばれる: to be called far-right

RELATED TERMS:

  • 極左(きょくさ): far left — direct antonym
  • 右翼(うよく): right wing — broader term covering the full right of the spectrum
  • 保守(ほしゅ): conservative — the mainstream right
  • ネオナチ: neo-Nazi — a specific variety of far-right group
