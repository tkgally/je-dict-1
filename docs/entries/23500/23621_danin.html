団員(だんいん)

だんいん
noun
member (of a group, troupe, corps, or team)
1. member (of a group, troupe, corps, or team)
A member of an organization whose name ends in 団(だん) — such as a musical ensemble, a theatrical company, a scout troop, a fire brigade, a delegation, or a sports supporter club. The word is general, and its specific meaning depends on what kind of 団(だん) is being discussed.
新(あたら)しい団員(だんいん)を募集(ぼしゅう)しています。
We are recruiting new members.
彼(かれ)は消防団(しょうぼうだん)の団員(だんいん)です。
He is a member of the volunteer fire brigade.
劇団(げきだん)の団員(だんいん)たちは毎日(まいにち)遅(おそ)くまで練習(れんしゅう)している。
The members of the theater company practice every day until late.
楽団(がくだん)の団員(だんいん)に選(えら)ばれるためには、厳(きび)しいオーディションに合格(ごうかく)しなければならない。
To be selected as a member of the orchestra, you have to pass a rigorous audition.

Composed of 団(だん) (group, troupe, body) and 員(いん) (member). Used for members of organizations whose names end in 団(だん). The word itself is neutral about what kind of group — context or a preceding compound tells you what sort of member is meant.

USAGE:
Most commonly used in fixed compounds or paired with the specific type of group, as in 消防団員(しょうぼうだんいん) (fire brigade member), 楽団員(がくだんいん) (orchestra member), or 劇団員(げきだんいん) (theater troupe member). Standalone 団員(だんいん) generally refers back to a group that has already been mentioned.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 団員(だんいん)になる: to become a member
  • 団員(だんいん)を募集(ぼしゅう)する: to recruit members
  • 団員(だんいん)全員(ぜんいん): all the members
  • 新(しん)団員(だんいん): new member

RELATED TERMS:

  • 消防団員(しょうぼうだんいん): volunteer firefighter
  • 楽団員(がくだんいん): orchestra member
  • 劇団員(げきだんいん): member of a theater company
  • 選手団(せんしゅだん): team (of athletes, e.g., for the Olympics)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会員(かいいん): member — of a 会(かい) (association, club, society)
  • 社員(しゃいん): employee, company member — of a 会社(かいしゃ)
  • 組合員(くみあいいん): union member — of a 組合(くみあい)
  • メンバー: member — general loanword, less formal
