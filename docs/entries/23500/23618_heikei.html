閉経(へいけい)

へいけい
noun
menopause; cessation of menstruation
1. menopause; the end of menstruation
The point at which menstrual periods permanently cease, typically occurring in women in their late forties to mid-fifties. Commonly used both as a noun ('menopause') and with する to describe entering that stage (閉経(へいけい)する: 'to enter menopause').
母(はは)は50歳(さい)で閉経(へいけい)した。
My mother went through menopause at fifty.
閉経(へいけい)の時期(じき)には個人差(こじんさ)がある。
The timing of menopause varies from person to person.
閉経(へいけい)前後(ぜんご)はホルモンバランスが大(おお)きく変化(へんか)する。
Hormonal balance changes dramatically around the time of menopause.
閉経(へいけい)後(ご)は骨粗(こつそ)しょう症(しょう)のリスクが高(たか)まるので、カルシウムの摂取(せっしゅ)に気(き)をつけたほうがよい。
After menopause, the risk of osteoporosis rises, so it is wise to pay attention to calcium intake.

Composed of 閉(へい) (close, shut) and 経(けい) (here short for 月経(げっけい), menstruation). Literally 'the closing of menstruation.'

USAGE:
閉経(へいけい) is used both as a noun and combined with する (閉経(へいけい)する) to mean 'to reach menopause / to undergo menopause.' It is a neutral medical term appropriate for conversations with doctors, health articles, and everyday discussion among adults. Japanese also has the broader term 更年期(こうねんき) (the climacteric, perimenopause), which refers to the whole transitional period rather than just the cessation of periods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 閉経(へいけい)する: to reach menopause
  • 閉経(へいけい)を迎(むか)える: to enter menopause
  • 閉経(へいけい)前(まえ)/後(ご): pre-/postmenopausal
  • 閉経(へいけい)期(き): menopausal period
  • 閉経(へいけい)年齢(ねんれい): age of menopause

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 更年期(こうねんき): the climacteric / menopausal transition — the broader transitional years, including symptoms before and after 閉経(へいけい) itself
  • 月経(げっけい): menstruation — the regular monthly period that 閉経(へいけい) brings to an end
  • 生理(せいり): period — the everyday conversational term for menstruation
  • 初経(しょけい): menarche; first menstruation — the opposite life-stage event
