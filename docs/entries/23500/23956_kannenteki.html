観念的(かんねんてき)

かんねんてき
na-adjective
notional; theoretical; ideational; divorced from reality
1. notional; theoretical; ideational; based on abstract ideas rather than concrete reality
Describes thinking, argument, or planning that stays at the level of ideas without reference to concrete facts or practical considerations. Often carries a negative nuance of being 'armchair' or 'out of touch'. Contrasts with 具体的(ぐたいてき) ('concrete') and 実際的(じっさいてき) ('practical').
彼(かれ)の意見(いけん)は観念的(かんねんてき)だ。
His opinion is too abstract / armchair.
議論(ぎろん)が観念的(かんねんてき)になりすぎている。
The discussion has become too abstract.
観念的(かんねんてき)な議論(ぎろん)ばかりしていても、実際(じっさい)の問題(もんだい)は何一(なにひと)つ解決(かいけつ)しない。
No matter how much we engage in purely theoretical debate, not a single real problem gets solved.

Compound of 観念(かんねん) ('idea; notion; conception') and the adjectival suffix 〜的(てき) ('-ish; -like; of the nature of'). Literally 'of the nature of a notion'.

USAGE:

  • Frequently critical in tone: 観念的(かんねんてき)だ often implies that something is removed from reality or from practical concerns.
  • Used for arguments, plans, theories, and people's thinking styles.
  • Common in the adverbial form 観念的(かんねんてき)に ('in the abstract; notionally').
  • Often paired with すぎる ('too much'): 観念的(かんねんてき)すぎる ('too abstract').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 観念的(かんねんてき)な議論(ぎろん): abstract / armchair debate
  • 観念的(かんねんてき)な考(かんが)え: a notional idea; an abstract view
  • 観念的(かんねんてき)な理解(りかい): a theoretical understanding
  • 観念的(かんねんてき)になる: to become abstract / detached from reality
  • 観念的(かんねんてき)に捉(とら)える: to grasp in the abstract

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 概念的(がいねんてき): conceptual — close in meaning, but more neutral; focuses on the level of concepts without the 'out of touch' implication.
  • 抽象的(ちゅうしょうてき): abstract — emphasizes the opposite of concreteness; broader and less evaluative.
  • 理論的(りろんてき): theoretical — emphasizes a systematic framework; can be positive (rigorous) rather than negative.
  • 具体的(ぐたいてき): concrete; specific — the practical antonym.
  • 実際的(じっさいてき): practical — another antonym, emphasizing usefulness in real situations.
