口(くち)を開(あ)ける

くちをあける
expression (verb phrase)
to open one's mouth; to open (a container)
1. to open one's mouth (to speak, yawn, or receive food, medicine, or a dental exam)
The basic, literal sense: to physically open the mouth. Commonly heard at the dentist (口(くち)を大(おお)きく開(あ)けてください), when feeding a baby or an animal, or when describing someone yawning or singing. Note: for the sense 'to start talking; to speak up', Japanese usually prefers 口(くち)を開(ひら)く with the reading ひらく.
口(くち)を開(あ)けてください。
Please open your mouth.
赤(あか)ちゃんが口(くち)を開(あ)けてあくびをした。
The baby opened its mouth and yawned.
歯医者(はいしゃ)さんに「もう少(すこ)し大(おお)きく口(くち)を開(あ)けて」と言(い)われて、必死(ひっし)であごを広(ひろ)げた。
When the dentist said 'Please open your mouth a little wider,' I spread my jaws open for all I was worth.
2. to open (a bottle, package, or other container); to unseal
By extension from the 'mouth' metaphor for openings: to remove the cap or seal of a bottle, jar, bag, or similar container, often for the first time. Common with foods, drinks, cosmetics, and medicines.
ワインの口(くち)を開(あ)ける。
To open a bottle of wine.
新(あたら)しい醤油(しょうゆ)の口(くち)を開(あ)けたら、香(かお)りが立(た)った。
When I opened the new bottle of soy sauce, the aroma wafted up.
お土産(みやげ)にもらったジャムの瓶(びん)の口(くち)を開(あ)けたら、甘(あま)い香(かお)りが部屋(へや)いっぱいに広(ひろ)がった。
When I opened the jar of jam that I had received as a souvenir, a sweet aroma filled the entire room.

The construction is noun 口(くち) + を + verb 開(あ)ける. 口(くち) here refers literally to the mouth (sense 1) or to the opening/mouth of a container (sense 2); 開(あ)ける is the transitive verb 'to open'.

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 is nearly always concrete and physical. The metaphorical 'open one's mouth = start speaking' sense is normally carried by 口(くち)を開(ひら)く (same kanji, but read ひらく). Beginning learners often conflate them.
  • For the intransitive counterpart (a mouth/container opens by itself or is open), use 口(くち)が開(あ)く or the state form 口(くち)が開(あ)いている.
  • In sense 2, 口(くち)を切(き)る is a near-synonym, especially for unsealing a new package or bottle for the very first time.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 口(くち)を大(おお)きく開(あ)ける: to open one's mouth wide
  • 口(くち)を開(あ)けて笑(わら)う: to open one's mouth and laugh
  • あくびをして口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to open one's mouth in a yawn
  • ワインの口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to open a bottle of wine
  • 瓶(びん)の口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to open the mouth of a bottle
  • 袋(ふくろ)の口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to open the mouth of a bag

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 口(くち)を開(ひら)く: to open one's mouth (and speak); to break one's silence — figurative use, focused on speech.
  • 口(くち)を切(き)る: to be the first to speak; also to unseal a new container — overlaps with sense 2 when referring to opening something for the first time.
  • 口(くち)を閉(と)じる / 閉(し)める: to close one's mouth — the antonym.
  • 開(あ)ける: to open — the general transitive verb; combines freely with many objects (door, window, box).
