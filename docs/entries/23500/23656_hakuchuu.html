白昼(はくちゅう)

はくちゅう
noun
broad daylight; daytime
1. broad daylight; the middle of the day
Daytime, especially thought of as bright and fully public. The word is almost always used to draw attention to something unusual happening when it would normally not — typically a crime, bold act, or strange incident that occurs in full public view rather than under cover of darkness.
白昼(はくちゅう)堂々(どうどう)と犯行(はんこう)におよんだ。
He committed the crime in broad daylight and without hesitation.
白昼(はくちゅう)の繁華街(はんかがい)で事件(じけん)が起(お)きた。
The incident occurred in a busy downtown area in broad daylight.
白昼(はくちゅう)に銀行(ぎんこう)が襲(おそ)われるという大胆(だいたん)な事件(じけん)が報道(ほうどう)された。
A bold incident in which a bank was robbed in broad daylight was reported in the news.

Composed of 白(はく) (white, bright) and 昼(ちゅう) (daytime). Literally "bright day." Although the dictionary meaning is simply "daytime," in actual use the word carries a strong nuance of "in plain view, when everyone can see," and is overwhelmingly attached to crimes, shocking incidents, or brazen acts. It is a written / news-register word; everyday conversation uses 昼間(ひるま).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 白昼(はくちゅう)堂々(どうどう): in broad daylight, brazenly
  • 白昼(はくちゅう)夢(む): daydream
  • 白昼(はくちゅう)の犯行(はんこう): a crime committed in broad daylight
  • 白昼(はくちゅう)の街(まち): the city by daylight
  • 白昼(はくちゅう)に及(およ)ぶ: (typically of a crime) to take place in broad daylight

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 昼間(ひるま): daytime — the neutral everyday word; no nuance of shocking exposure
  • 真昼(まひる): high noon, midday — emphasizes the middle of the day; more literary but less crime-associated
  • 日中(にっちゅう): during the day — factual, often used in forecasts and schedules
  • 昼日中(ひるひなか): broad daylight — old-fashioned, similar nuance to 白昼(はくちゅう) but rarer
