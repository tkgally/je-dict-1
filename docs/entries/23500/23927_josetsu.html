序説(じょせつ)

じょせつ
noun
introduction; introductory treatise; prolegomena
1. an introductory chapter or book that lays out the groundwork of a subject — used especially in academic titles and in the openings of scholarly works
Found most often in book titles in the humanities, sciences, and philosophy — e.g., 「哲学(てつがく)序説(じょせつ)」 ("An Introduction to Philosophy"). More formal and more scholarly than 入門(にゅうもん) or 概論(がいろん), and typically emphasizes foundational concepts or preliminary arguments that prepare the reader for fuller treatment.
「経済(けいざい)学(がく)序説(じょせつ)」を読(よ)んだ。
I read "An Introduction to Economics."
この本(ほん)は言語学(げんごがく)の序説(じょせつ)として定評(ていひょう)がある。
This book has an established reputation as an introduction to linguistics.
序説(じょせつ)で著者(ちょしゃ)は研究(けんきゅう)の目的(もくてき)と方法(ほうほう)を明(あき)らかにしている。
In the introduction, the author sets out the aims and methods of the study.
カントの書(しょ)いた「将来(しょうらい)の形而上学(けいじじょうがく)への序説(じょせつ)」は、哲学(てつがく)史(し)における重要(じゅうよう)な著作(ちょさく)である。
Kant's "Prolegomena to Any Future Metaphysics" is an important work in the history of philosophy.

Composed of 序(じょ) ("order, preface, introductory") + 説(せつ) ("theory, discourse"). Literally "introductory discourse," close to the Latin term "prolegomena" in scholarly usage.

USAGE:

  • Most commonly appears in book titles and chapter titles of academic works: X学(がく)序説(じょせつ) or 「Xへの序説(じょせつ)」.
  • Signals a formal, scholarly register — not suitable for casual writing or everyday introductions.
  • Can also refer to the introductory chapter of a book or treatise that lays out its scope, questions, and methods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 哲学(てつがく)序説(じょせつ): an introduction to philosophy
  • X学(がく)への序説(じょせつ): a prolegomenon to (field) X
  • 序説(じょせつ)を書(か)く: to write an introductory treatise
  • 序説(じょせつ)に当(あ)たる章(しょう): the chapter that serves as the introduction

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 入門(にゅうもん): introduction, primer — aimed at beginners; much more accessible in tone.
  • 概論(がいろん): general survey — overview of a field, less foundational than 序説(じょせつ).
  • 序論(じょろん): introductory chapter/argument — often the first chapter of an academic paper or thesis.
  • 序文(じょぶん): preface — the opening remarks of a book, often by the author or editor.
  • 前書(まえが)き: foreword — a short prefatory note, usually more personal or practical.
