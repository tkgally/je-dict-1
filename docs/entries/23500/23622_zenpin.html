全品(ぜんぴん)

ぜんぴん
noun
all items; every product (in a store or lineup)
1. all items; every product
Every product or item in a given lineup — the entire stock of a shop, the whole range of a product series, or all dishes on a menu. Extremely common in retail advertising, especially sale announcements.
本日(ほんじつ)、全品(ぜんぴん)半額(はんがく)。
Everything is half price today.
全品(ぜんぴん)送料(そうりょう)無料(むりょう)キャンペーン中(ちゅう)です。
We're running a free-shipping-on-everything campaign.
この店(みせ)は全品(ぜんぴん)税込(ぜいこ)み価格(かかく)で表示(ひょうじ)されているので、わかりやすい。
This shop shows every item at its tax-included price, which makes things easy to understand.

Composed of 全(ぜん) ("all, entire") + 品(ひん) ("article, product"). The 全〜(ぜん〜) prefix forms many "all / every" nouns, such as 全員(ぜんいん) (everyone), 全国(ぜんこく) (the whole country), and 全員参加(ぜんいんさんか) (participation by all).

USAGE:

  • Ubiquitous in retail advertising, where it signals that a discount, campaign, or promotion applies to the entire product lineup with no exclusions.
  • Functions as a noun; it typically modifies a following price phrase with no particle — 全品(ぜんぴん)半額(はんがく) ("all items half off"), 全品(ぜんぴん)送料(そうりょう)無料(むりょう) ("free shipping on all items") — in the same compact style used on shop signs and price tags.
  • When a particle is used, が or の are most natural: 全品(ぜんぴん)が対象(たいしょう)です (all items are eligible); 全品(ぜんぴん)の内容(ないよう) (the contents of every item).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全品(ぜんぴん)半額(はんがく): all items half price
  • 全品(ぜんぴん)送料(そうりょう)無料(むりょう): free shipping on everything
  • 全品(ぜんぴん)税込(ぜいこ)み: all items tax-included
  • 全品(ぜんぴん)対象(たいしょう): all items eligible (for the offer)
  • 全品(ぜんぴん)〇〇(まるまる)%オフ: __% off every item
  • 全品(ぜんぴん)完売(かんばい): every item sold out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全商品(ぜんしょうひん): all products — near-synonym; slightly more formal and common in online-shop contexts.
  • 全(すべ)ての商品(しょうひん): all products — the plain-language version of the same idea.
  • 一部(いちぶ)商品(しょうひん): some items — the opposite, used in fine print to signal that a promotion has exclusions.
