暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)い

しょちゅうみまい
noun
summer greeting card; midsummer greeting
1. summer greeting card; midsummer greeting
A seasonal greeting card or message sent during the hottest part of summer (roughly mid-July to early August) to inquire after someone's well-being. One of several traditional seasonal greeting customs in Japan, along with New Year's cards.
暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)いを出(だ)した。
I sent out summer greeting cards.
先生(せんせい)に暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)いのハガキを書(か)いた。
I wrote a summer greeting postcard to my teacher.
暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
I extend my midsummer greetings to you. (standard opening phrase)
立秋(りっしゅう)を過(す)ぎたら暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)いではなく残暑見舞(ざんしょみま)いになる。
After the start of autumn on the traditional calendar, it becomes a late-summer greeting rather than a midsummer greeting.

Composed of 暑中(しょちゅう) (the heat of midsummer; the hottest period) and 見舞(みま)い (inquiry after someone's health; a visit or greeting of sympathy). Literally "midsummer health inquiry."

This is one of Japan's important seasonal correspondence customs. Cards are sent during the period called 暑中(しょちゅう), roughly from mid-July until 立秋(りっしゅう) (start of autumn, around August 7). After 立秋(りっしゅう), the same type of greeting shifts to 残暑見舞(ざんしょみま)い (late-summer greeting).

The standard opening phrase on these cards is 暑中(しょちゅう)お見舞(みま)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます, a highly formal expression of seasonal well-wishing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)いを出(だ)す: to send summer greeting cards
  • 暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)いのハガキ: summer greeting postcard
  • 暑中見舞(しょちゅうみま)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます: standard summer greeting phrase

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年賀状(ねんがじょう): New Year's card — the other major seasonal greeting card tradition
  • 残暑見舞(ざんしょみま)い: late-summer greeting — sent after 立秋(りっしゅう), when the summer heat lingers
  • 寒中見舞(かんちゅうみま)い: midwinter greeting — the winter equivalent
