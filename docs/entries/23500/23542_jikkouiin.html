実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)

じっこういいん
noun
organizing committee member; executive committee member
1. organizing committee member; executive committee member
A member of a committee responsible for planning and carrying out an event, festival, or project. Common in schools, universities, and community organizations.
文化祭(ぶんかさい)の実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)になった。
I became a member of the cultural festival organizing committee.
実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)が会議(かいぎ)を開(ひら)いた。
The organizing committee held a meeting.
イベントの準備(じゅんび)は実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)を中心(ちゅうしん)に進(すす)められた。
The event preparations were led by the organizing committee members.

A compound of 実行(じっこう) (execution/implementation) and 委員(いいん) (committee member). In Japanese schools and universities, students often serve on organizing committees for events like 文化祭(ぶんかさい) (school festivals), 体育祭(たいいくさい) (sports days), and 卒業式(そつぎょうしき) (graduation ceremonies).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)になる: to become an organizing committee member
  • 実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)を務(つと)める: to serve as an organizing committee member
  • 実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)長(ちょう): head of the organizing committee
  • 実行(じっこう)委員(いいん)会(かい): organizing committee (the body/organization)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 委員(いいん): committee member — a broader term for any committee member
  • 幹事(かんじ): organizer, secretary — typically for social gatherings like parties
