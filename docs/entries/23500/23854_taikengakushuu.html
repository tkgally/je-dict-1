体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)

たいけんがくしゅう
noun
experiential learning; hands-on learning
1. experiential learning; hands-on learning — education in which students learn by directly experiencing an activity
An educational approach in which students acquire knowledge and skills through direct, practical experience rather than only through lectures or textbooks. In Japan, it is widely used for elementary and junior high school field programs — farming experiences, workplace visits, traditional craft workshops, nature programs, and similar activities.
来月(らいげつ)農業(のうぎょう)の体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)がある。
Next month there will be a hands-on learning program in farming.
小学生(しょうがくせい)たちが陶芸(とうげい)の体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)を楽(たの)しんでいた。
The elementary school students were enjoying a hands-on learning activity in pottery.
地元(じもと)の工場(こうじょう)を訪問(ほうもん)する体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)を通(つう)じて、生徒(せいと)たちはものづくりの大変(たいへん)さを知(し)ったそうだ。
Through the hands-on learning program of visiting a local factory, the students apparently came to understand how demanding manufacturing work is.

Compound of 体験(たいけん) ('first-hand experience') and 学習(がくしゅう) ('learning; study'). Emphasizes gaining knowledge by doing rather than by reading or listening.

USAGE:

  • Very common in school curricula. Public elementary and junior high schools regularly organize 体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう) in cooperation with local farms, factories, traditional-craft studios, and civic facilities.
  • Adult learning contexts also use the word for workshops, trial lessons, and corporate training that involve a practical component.
  • Overlaps with 体験(たいけん)授業(じゅぎょう) ('trial/experience class'), but 体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう) focuses on the learning process while 体験(たいけん)授業(じゅぎょう) typically refers to a sample lesson used to introduce prospective students to a school.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)を行(おこな)う: to conduct experiential learning
  • 体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう)に参加(さんか)する: to participate in experiential learning
  • 農業(のうぎょう)体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう): farming experience program
  • 職場(しょくば)体験学習(たいけんがくしゅう): workplace experience program

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 体験(たいけん)授業(じゅぎょう): trial lesson — sample class for prospective students.
  • 実習(じっしゅう): practical training; practicum — usually tied to vocational or professional training with specific skill goals.
  • 校外学習(こうがいがくしゅう): off-campus learning — broader term for lessons held outside school grounds.
  • 見学(けんがく): observational tour — visiting a facility mainly to look, with less hands-on participation.
