1. accidental death; death by accident; death in an accident
Dying as a result of an accident — a traffic accident, a workplace accident, a fall, or any similar unintended event. Contrasted with natural death, disease death, suicide, or homicide. Used in news reports, legal contexts, and insurance documents.
交通(こうつう)事故死(じこし)。
A traffic-accident death.
父(ちち)は若(わか)くして事故死(じこし)した。
My father died in an accident at a young age.
山(やま)での事故死(じこし)を防(ふせ)ぐため、登山(とざん)の届(とど)け出(で)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To prevent accidental deaths in the mountains, you are required to submit a climbing notification.
保険(ほけん)の契約書(けいやくしょ)には、病死(びょうし)と事故死(じこし)で保険金(ほけんきん)が違(ちが)うと書(か)いてあった。
The insurance contract stated that the payout differs between death by illness and accidental death.

Composed of 事故(じこ) (accident) and 死(し) (death). Refers to death caused by an accident, such as a traffic accident, fall, drowning, fire, or industrial accident. It is the standard term in news reports, police announcements, and insurance documents.

USAGE:

  • As a noun: 事故死(じこし)が多(おお)い (there are many accidental deaths)
  • As a suru-verb: 若(わか)くして事故死(じこし)する (to die accidentally at a young age)
  • Often preceded by a qualifier: 交通(こうつう)事故死(じこし) (traffic-accident death), 労働(ろうどう)事故死(じこし) (workplace-accident death)

REGISTER: Neutral, but matter-of-fact and somewhat blunt. In eulogies or sympathy letters, softer phrasing such as 亡(な)くなる (pass away) is preferred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故死(じこし)する: to die in an accident
  • 事故死(じこし)者(しゃ): a person who died in an accident
  • 事故死(じこし)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent accidental deaths
  • 交通(こうつう)事故死(じこし): death from a traffic accident

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 病死(びょうし): death from illness — contrasted with accidental death on death certificates and insurance
  • 変死(へんし): unnatural death — a broader legal term covering accidents, suicide, and homicide
  • 急死(きゅうし): sudden death — emphasizes suddenness, not necessarily accident
  • 即死(そくし): instant death — dying immediately at the scene of an accident
