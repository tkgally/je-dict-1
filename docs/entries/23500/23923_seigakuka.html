声楽家(せいがくか)

せいがくか
noun
classical vocalist; classical singer; vocal artist
1. a professional singer in the classical or art-music tradition — typically an opera singer, lieder singer, or trained soloist, as distinguished from a popular or rock singer
Refers specifically to someone trained in the art of 声楽(せいがく) (classical vocal music) — opera, art song, oratorio, or church music. The word carries an image of formal concert halls, Western classical training, and sustained academic study, and would not be applied to a J-pop singer or street musician.
彼女(かのじょ)は有名(ゆうめい)な声楽家(せいがくか)だ。
She is a famous classical vocalist.
声楽家(せいがくか)を目指(めざ)して音楽(おんがく)大学(だいがく)に入(はい)った。
I entered a music college aiming to become a classical vocalist.
イタリアで修行(しゅぎょう)を積(つ)んだ声楽家(せいがくか)が出演(しゅつえん)する。
A classical vocalist who trained in Italy will be performing.
彼(かれ)は長年(ながねん)大学(だいがく)で教(おし)える一方(いっぽう)、現役(げんえき)の声楽家(せいがくか)としても活躍(かつやく)している。
While he has taught at the university for many years, he is also active as a working classical vocalist.

Composed of 声楽(せいがく) ("vocal music," specifically in the classical tradition) + 家(か) ("specialist, -ist"). Denotes a trained singer of Western classical repertoire — not a pop or folk singer.

USAGE:

  • Often modified by voice type in more detailed descriptions: ソプラノ歌手(かしゅ) / バリトン歌手(かしゅ) are common alternatives when naming a specific voice part.
  • Typical verbs: 目指(めざ)す ("to aspire to become"), 活躍(かつやく)する ("to be active as"), {デビューする} ("to debut").
  • Career context: 声楽家(せいがくか) almost always implies formal training through a conservatory or music university.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有名(ゆうめい)な声楽家(せいがくか): a famous classical vocalist
  • 声楽家(せいがくか)を目指(めざ)す: to aspire to become a classical vocalist
  • 現役(げんえき)の声楽家(せいがくか): an active/working classical vocalist
  • 声楽家(せいがくか)として活躍(かつやく)する: to be active as a classical vocalist

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 歌手(かしゅ): singer — the most general word; includes pop, rock, enka, and classical.
  • オペラ歌手(かしゅ): opera singer — a specific kind of classical vocalist.
  • 音楽家(おんがくか): musician — broader term covering performers and composers.
  • 作曲家(さっきょくか): composer — parallel formation (家(か) = specialist).
