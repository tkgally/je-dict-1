何日(なんにち)

なんにち
noun
how many days; what day (of the month)
1. how many days — asking about or referring to an unspecified number of days
Used to ask about the duration of something in days, or to vaguely refer to 'some number of days'. Formed from the question word 何(なん) and the counter 日(にち).
何日(なんにち)かかりますか。
How many days will it take?
旅行(りょこう)は何日(なんにち)間(かん)ですか。
How many days is your trip?
東京(とうきょう)に何日(なんにち)か泊(と)まるつもりです。
I'm planning to stay in Tokyo for several days.
2. what day (of the month) — asking the date of the month
When paired with the date, 何日(なんにち) asks for the day of the month (the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.). Context or an accompanying word such as 今日(きょう) makes this meaning clear.
今日(きょう)は何日(なんにち)ですか。
What's today's date?
会議(かいぎ)は何月(なんがつ)何日(なんにち)ですか。
What month and day is the meeting?
来月(らいげつ)の何日(なんにち)に引(ひ)っ越(こ)しますか。
On what day of next month will you move?

Question word formed from 何(なん) ('what, how many') + 日(にち) (counter for days / day of the month). Which sense applies depends on context.

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 (duration): often paired with 間(かん) to explicitly mean 'for how many days', as in 何日(なんにち)間(かん).
  • Sense 1 (vague): 何日(なんにち)か means 'several / a few days' when not a direct question — the か turns it into an indefinite expression.
  • Sense 2 (date): asks the day of the month. The answer uses the standard day-of-month readings (一日(ついたち), 二日(ふつか), ..., 二十日(はつか), 三十一日(さんじゅういちにち), etc.).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 何日(なんにち)かかる: to take how many days
  • 何日(なんにち)間(かん): for how many days
  • 何日(なんにち)か: several days; a few days (indefinite)
  • 何日(なんにち)前(まえ): how many days ago
  • 今日(きょう)は何日(なんにち): what day (of the month) is today

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 何曜日(なんようび): what day of the week — asks the weekday (Monday, Tuesday, etc.), not the date.
  • {いつ}: when — a more general 'when', without specifying duration or date.
  • 何月(なんがつ): what month.

COMMON MISTAKES:
Learners sometimes confuse 何日(なんにち) with 何曜日(なんようび). If you want to know 'Monday or Tuesday?', use 何曜日(なんようび). If you want to know 'the 5th or the 6th?', use 何日(なんにち).
