鼻炎(びえん)

びえん
noun
rhinitis; inflammation of the nasal mucosa
1. rhinitis — inflammation of the mucous membrane of the nose, causing sneezing, a runny nose, or congestion; includes allergic rhinitis and hay fever
A medical term used for inflammation of the nose, whether caused by allergy, infection, or other irritation. The most common compound encountered by learners is アレルギー{性(せい)鼻炎(びえん)} (allergic rhinitis), which overlaps heavily with 花粉症(かふんしょう) (hay fever).
鼻炎(びえん)がひどい。
My rhinitis is bad.
慢性(まんせい)の鼻炎(びえん)に悩(なや)まされている。
I've been suffering from chronic rhinitis.
アレルギー性(せい)の鼻炎(びえん)で、毎年(まいとし)春(はる)になると辛(つら)い。
I have allergic rhinitis, so every spring is rough for me.
医者(いしゃ)に鼻炎(びえん)と診断(しんだん)され、薬(くすり)を処方(しょほう)してもらった。
The doctor diagnosed me with rhinitis and prescribed some medication.

A medical term composed of 鼻(はな) ("nose") + 炎(えん) ("inflammation"). Covers any inflammation of the nasal mucosa, whether allergic, viral, or chronic.

USAGE:

  • Commonly modified by type: アレルギー{性(せい)鼻炎(びえん)} (allergic rhinitis), 慢性(まんせい)鼻炎(びえん) (chronic rhinitis), 急性(きゅうせい)鼻炎(びえん) (acute rhinitis).
  • Takes the verbs なる} ("to develop"), {ひどい} ("to be severe"), or {悩(なや)まされる ("to be troubled by").
  • In everyday speech, many people simply say 鼻(はな)がつまる or 花粉症(かふんしょう) rather than using 鼻炎(びえん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アレルギー性(せい)鼻炎(びえん): allergic rhinitis
  • 慢性(まんせい)鼻炎(びえん): chronic rhinitis
  • 鼻炎(びえん)の薬(くすり): medicine for rhinitis
  • 鼻炎(びえん)の症状(しょうじょう): symptoms of rhinitis

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 花粉症(かふんしょう): hay fever — a specific type of allergic rhinitis caused by pollen; much more common in everyday speech.
  • 風邪(かぜ): a cold — often causes temporary rhinitis-like symptoms but refers to the illness as a whole.
  • 鼻詰(はなづ)まり: nasal congestion — describes the symptom, not the medical condition.
