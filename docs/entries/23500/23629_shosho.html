諸所(しょしょ)

しょしょ
noun
various places; here and there; several places
1. various places; here and there; several places
A formal, written-sounding word meaning "various places" or "here and there." Used in written reports, formal announcements, and literary prose to refer to multiple locations without specifying them. Adverbial use with {に} ("in various places") or {から} ("from various places") is common.
諸所(しょしょ)に問題(もんだい)がある。
There are problems in various places.
地震(じしん)で建物(たてもの)の諸所(しょしょ)にひび割(わ)れが生(しょう)じた。
The earthquake caused cracks in various parts of the building.
この資料(しりょう)は諸所(しょしょ)に誤(あやま)りがあるため、全面的(ぜんめんてき)に見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
Since there are errors in various places in this document, it needs to be fully reviewed.
古(ふる)い寺院(じいん)の柱(はしら)や壁(かべ)の諸所(しょしょ)に、修復(しゅうふく)の跡(あと)がはっきりと残(のこ)っているのが見(み)て取(と)れる。
You can clearly see the traces of restoration work remaining on various parts of the pillars and walls of the old temple.

Composed of 諸(しょ) (various, several) and 所(しょ) (place). A formal word belonging to written and careful spoken Japanese.

USAGE:
諸所(しょしょ) is used mainly in writing — news reports, formal documents, literary prose — to refer to "several places" without naming them. In casual conversation, speakers are much more likely to say あちこち or いろいろなところ.

It is often paired with another word in the compound 諸所(しょしょ)方々(ほうぼう) ("all over the place, here and there"), a set phrase that emphasizes a wide and scattered distribution.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 諸所(しょしょ)に: in various places
  • 諸所(しょしょ)から: from various places
  • 諸所(しょしょ)方々(ほうぼう): all over the place; here, there, and everywhere
  • 諸所(しょしょ)に問題(もんだい)がある: to have problems in various places
  • 諸所(しょしょ)に見(み)られる: to be seen in various places

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あちこち: here and there — everyday, conversational
  • あちらこちら: here and there — slightly more formal than あちこち, still conversational
  • いろいろなところ: various places — neutral
  • 各所(かくしょ): each place, various places — similar meaning, also formal; often used for specific known locations (venues, branches)
  • 方々(ほうぼう): everywhere, many places — somewhat old-fashioned on its own

REGISTER:
Written, formal. Avoid in casual conversation; use あちこち instead.
