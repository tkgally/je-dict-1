送付先(そうふさき)

そうふさき
noun
mailing address; destination address (for shipping or forwarding)
1. mailing address; shipping address; recipient's address
The address to which documents, parcels, or other materials are to be sent. Standard term on shipping labels, order forms, and business correspondence.
送付先(そうふさき)を入力(にゅうりょく)する。
To enter the mailing address.
送付先(そうふさき)を間違(まちが)えてしまった。
I ended up entering the wrong mailing address.
書類(しょるい)の送付先(そうふさき)は下記(かき)のとおりです。
The address for sending the documents is as follows.
注文(ちゅうもん)時(じ)に、送付先(そうふさき)を請求先(せいきゅうさき)と別(べつ)に指定(してい)することができる。
At the time of ordering, you can specify a shipping address separate from the billing address.

Compound of 送付(そうふ) ('sending; dispatch') and the suffix 先(さき) ('destination; recipient'). The 先(さき) suffix is highly productive and forms 'destination/target' nouns: 連絡先(れんらくさき) ('contact address'), 請求先(せいきゅうさき) ('billing address'), 取引先(とりひきさき) ('business partner'), 勤務先(きんむさき) ('place of employment').

USAGE:
Appears on shipping and order forms, in business letters, and in email signatures. In e-commerce it typically refers to the physical shipping address; in business correspondence it may refer to the address to which documents should be mailed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送付先(そうふさき)を入力(にゅうりょく)する: to enter the mailing address
  • 送付先(そうふさき)を指定(してい)する: to specify the mailing address
  • 送付先(そうふさき)を変更(へんこう)する: to change the mailing address
  • 送付先(そうふさき)を間違(まちが)える: to get the mailing address wrong
  • 送付先(そうふさき)住所(じゅうしょ): the mailing address (line)
  • 送付先(そうふさき)の確認(かくにん): confirmation of the mailing address

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 宛先(あてさき): addressee; destination — for any kind of communication, including email
  • 住所(じゅうしょ): address — the address itself, not specifically a destination
  • 配送先(はいそうさき): delivery address — specifically for physical delivery, common in e-commerce
  • 請求先(せいきゅうさき): billing address — contrasted with 送付先(そうふさき) on order forms
  • 届(とど)け先(さき): delivery destination — more conversational
