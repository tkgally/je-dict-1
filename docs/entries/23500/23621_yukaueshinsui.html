床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)

ゆかうえしんすい
noun
above-floor flooding; flooding that rises above the floor level
1. above-floor flooding; flooding where water rises above the floor level of a building
A category of flood damage in Japanese disaster statistics in which floodwater rises above the floor of a building, inundating the living space itself. It is the more severe of the two official flood-damage categories, the other being 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) (below-floor flooding).
家(いえ)が床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)してしまった。
Our house was flooded above floor level.
床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)の被害(ひがい)を受(う)けた家屋(かおく)は修復(しゅうふく)に時間(じかん)がかかる。
Houses that suffer above-floor flooding take time to repair.
豪雨(ごうう)の影響(えいきょう)で、川沿(かわぞ)いの住宅(じゅうたく)が広(ひろ)い範囲(はんい)で床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)した。
Due to the heavy rain, homes along the river suffered above-floor flooding over a wide area.
床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)になった場合(ばあい)、畳(たたみ)や家具(かぐ)、家電(かでん)などの多(おお)くが使(つか)えなくなってしまうことが多(おお)い。
When above-floor flooding occurs, tatami, furniture, and home appliances often become unusable.

Composed of 床上(ゆかうえ) (above the floor) and 浸水(しんすい) (flooding). A set phrase used in official disaster classifications, news coverage, and insurance documentation.

USAGE:
Japanese disaster statistics officially separate house flooding into two categories: 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) (water only below the floor) and 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい) (water above the floor). 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい) is the more severe category: water enters the living space itself, typically ruining tatami, flooring, furniture, appliances, and electrical outlets. Recovery usually requires much more extensive cleaning, replacement, and repair than 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい) and often triggers larger insurance payouts and government disaster aid.

Like 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい), this word can be used both as a noun and with する (床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)する: 'to be flooded above floor level').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)する: to suffer above-floor flooding
  • 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)の被害(ひがい): above-floor flood damage
  • 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)○{○軒(けん)}: N homes affected by above-floor flooding
  • 床上浸水(ゆかうえしんすい)の恐(おそ)れ: risk of above-floor flooding

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 床下浸水(ゆかしたしんすい): below-floor flooding — the less severe category, where water stays under the floor
  • 浸水(しんすい): flooding; water intrusion — the general term covering both categories
  • 冠水(かんすう): submergence (of roads, fields) — used more for areas than specific buildings
  • 水害(すいがい): flood disaster — the broad term for damage caused by water
