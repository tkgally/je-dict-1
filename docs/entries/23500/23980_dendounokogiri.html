電動(でんどう)のこぎり

でんどうのこぎり
noun
power saw; electric saw
1. power saw; electric saw
A saw powered by an electric motor, typically used for cutting wood, metal, or other materials. An everyday household/DIY term encompassing handheld reciprocating saws, jigsaws, and similar tools.
電動(でんどう)のこぎりで木(き)を切(き)る。
To cut wood with a power saw.
ホームセンターで電動(でんどう)のこぎりを買(か)った。
I bought a power saw at the home center.
電動(でんどう)のこぎりを使(つか)うときは必(かなら)ず保護(ほご)メガネを着用(ちゃくよう)してください。
Be sure to wear safety goggles when using a power saw.

Compound of 電動(でんどう) ('electrically powered') and のこぎり ('saw'). The word のこぎり is almost always written in hiragana, even though the kanji 鋸(のこぎり) exists. A common DIY and hardware-store term.

USAGE:

  • Covers most handheld electric saws in everyday speech. For specialized tools, more specific terms are used: 丸(まる)のこ (circular saw), ジグソー (jigsaw), チェーンソー (chainsaw), レシプロソー (reciprocating saw).
  • Frequently seen in DIY magazines, construction contexts, and product listings.
  • Contrasts with 手(て)のこぎり (hand saw), operated by arm motion alone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電動(でんどう)のこぎりで切(き)る: to cut with a power saw
  • 電動(でんどう)のこぎりを使(つか)う: to use a power saw
  • 電動(でんどう)のこぎりの刃(は): the blade of a power saw
  • コードレス電動(でんどう)のこぎり: cordless power saw

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手(て)のこぎり: hand saw — manually operated, non-electric.
  • 丸(まる)のこ: circular saw — saw with a round rotating blade.
  • チェーンソー: chainsaw — specifically for felling trees and heavy cutting.
  • ジグソー: jigsaw — for cutting curves in wood.
