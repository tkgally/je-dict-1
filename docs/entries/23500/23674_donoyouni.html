どのように

どのように
expression
how; in what way; in what manner
1. how; in what way; in what manner
A somewhat formal way to ask "how?" or "in what way?" Built from どの (which) + ように (like, in the manner of). Used to ask about the manner, method, or process by which something is done. More polite and written-style than the casual どう.
どのように使(つか)いますか。
How do you use it?
この問題(もんだい)をどのように解決(かいけつ)しますか。
How will you solve this problem?
留学(りゅうがく)の経験(けいけん)は、あなたの人生(じんせい)をどのように変(か)えましたか。
In what ways did your study-abroad experience change your life?
新(あたら)しいシステムを導入(どうにゅう)する際(さい)に、社員(しゃいん)にどのように説明(せつめい)すればよいかを考(かんが)えています。
We are thinking about how to explain to employees when we introduce the new system.

A formal or written equivalent of どう. Composed of どの (which, what kind of) and ように (like, in the manner of). Asks about the manner, method, process, or degree of something.

USAGE:

  • Used in surveys, interviews, essays, business communication, and polite spoken Japanese
  • Typically followed by a verb: どのように + verb
  • Can also precede a noun as どのような (what kind of), though どのように is the adverbial form

REGISTER: Formal / neutral-polite. More formal than どう; slightly less stiff than いかに (archaic/literary).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • どのように考(かんが)えますか: how do you think about it?
  • どのように説明(せつめい)しますか: how would you explain it?
  • どのように使(つか)うか: how to use it
  • どのように変(か)わったか: how it changed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どう: how — casual, spoken equivalent; much more common in conversation
  • いかに: how, to what degree — literary or archaic; more formal than どのように
  • どんなふうに: how, in what way — a casual spoken equivalent that is slightly softer than どう
  • どのような: what kind of — the adnominal form; modifies a noun rather than a verb
