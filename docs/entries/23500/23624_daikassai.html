大喝采(だいかっさい)

だいかっさい
noun
thunderous applause; loud cheering; great ovation
1. thunderous applause; loud cheering; great ovation
Very loud, enthusiastic applause, cheering, or shouts of praise, typically given to a performer, speaker, athlete, or winner after an impressive showing. Stronger than ordinary 拍手(はくしゅ) (clapping): implies a crowd reacting with energy and approval, often standing or roaring.
会場(かいじょう)から大喝采(だいかっさい)が起(お)こった。
A great ovation rose from the audience.
見事(みごと)な演奏(えんそう)に観客(かんきゃく)は大喝采(だいかっさい)を送(おく)った。
The audience gave thunderous applause for the splendid performance.
優勝(ゆうしょう)が決(き)まった瞬間(しゅんかん)、スタジアム全体(ぜんたい)が大喝采(だいかっさい)に包(つつ)まれた。
The moment the championship was decided, the entire stadium was enveloped in thunderous cheers.
新人(しんじん)ピアニストの堂々(どうどう)とした演奏(えんそう)に、満員(まんいん)の観客(かんきゃく)は総立(そうだ)ちで大喝采(だいかっさい)を送(おく)り、カーテンコールが何度(なんど)も続(つづ)いた。
At the new pianist's commanding performance, the packed audience rose to their feet with thunderous applause, and the curtain calls went on and on.

Composed of 大(だい) (great, big) and 喝采(かっさい) (applause, cheers). The intensifying prefix 大(だい) strengthens 喝采(かっさい) to emphasize scale and enthusiasm. Often translated as "thunderous applause" or "great ovation."

USAGE:
Typically appears as the object of 送(おく)る (to give/send applause to) or the subject of 起(お)こる / 沸(わ)き起(お)こる (to arise, break out). It describes applause from a sizeable audience — a theater, concert hall, sports venue, or public event — not just clapping from a few people. Written and spoken registers; also frequent in reports about performances or sports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大喝采(だいかっさい)を送(おく)る: to give thunderous applause
  • 大喝采(だいかっさい)を浴(あ)びる: to receive thunderous applause (from the performer's perspective)
  • 大喝采(だいかっさい)が起(お)こる: thunderous applause breaks out
  • 大喝采(だいかっさい)に包(つつ)まれる: to be enveloped in thunderous applause

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 喝采(かっさい): applause, cheering — the base word; 大喝采(だいかっさい) is the intensified form
  • 拍手(はくしゅ): applause, clapping — neutral, physical clapping; can be small or large
  • 歓声(かんせい): cheer, shout of joy — focuses on voices, not clapping
  • 拍手喝采(はくしゅかっさい): applause and cheers — set phrase for enthusiastic reception
  • 万雷(ばんらい)の拍手(はくしゅ): thunderous applause (literally "applause of a thousand thunders") — dramatic literary synonym
