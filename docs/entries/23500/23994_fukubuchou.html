副部長(ふくぶちょう)

ふくぶちょう
noun
deputy department head; assistant manager; vice-captain (of a club)
1. deputy department head; assistant manager; vice-captain (of a club)
The person who supports and acts as deputy to a 部長(ぶちょう) — a department head in a company, or the captain of a school or university club. Responsibilities typically include deputizing for the head when absent and handling day-to-day coordination within the unit.
副部長(ふくぶちょう)に選(えら)ばれた。
I was chosen as deputy department head.
営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ)の副部長(ふくぶちょう)が会議(かいぎ)を仕切(しき)った。
The deputy manager of the sales department ran the meeting.
部長(ぶちょう)が出張(しゅっちょう)中(ちゅう)は副部長(ふくぶちょう)が代(か)わりに判断(はんだん)する。
While the department head is on a business trip, the deputy makes decisions in his place.

Formed with the prefix 副(ふく)- ('vice-; assistant; deputy') attached to 部長(ぶちょう) ('department head; club captain'). The same pattern produces 副社長(ふくしゃちょう) ('vice president') and 副会長(ふくかいちょう) ('vice chair').

USAGE:

  • In corporate contexts: the second-in-command of a department (部(ぶ)), ranking below the 部長(ぶちょう) and above section chiefs (課長(かちょう)).
  • In school/club contexts: the vice-captain of a school club (also called 部(ぶ)), supporting the student-elected 部長(ぶちょう).
  • Often abbreviated to 副(ふく) within the organization when context is clear: 「副(ふく)にやらせる」('have the deputy do it').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 副部長(ふくぶちょう)に任命(にんめい)する: to appoint as deputy head
  • 副部長(ふくぶちょう)に昇進(しょうしん)する: to be promoted to deputy head
  • 副部長(ふくぶちょう)職(しょく): the position of deputy head
  • 副部長(ふくぶちょう)代理(だいり): acting deputy head

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 部長(ぶちょう): department head; club captain — the superior whom the 副部長(ふくぶちょう) supports.
  • 次長(じちょう): deputy director; assistant manager — a similar-rank position, often more senior in large organizations; typically used one level up from 副部長(ふくぶちょう).
  • 課長(かちょう): section chief — the rank below 部長(ぶちょう) in the standard corporate hierarchy.
  • 副会長(ふくかいちょう): vice chair; vice president (of a society) — same 副(ふく)- prefix applied to 会長(かいちょう).
  • 副社長(ふくしゃちょう): vice president (of a company) — senior corporate officer, not a department-level role.
