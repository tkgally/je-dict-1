1. safety management; safety oversight; safety administration
The systematic effort by an organization to identify hazards, prevent accidents, and protect workers, users, and the public. In Japan, 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり) is a legally required activity in construction, manufacturing, transportation, schools, and many other fields, typically coordinated by a designated safety manager.
安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)を徹底(てってい)する。
To enforce thorough safety management.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)では安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)が最(もっと)も重要(じゅうよう)だ。
At construction sites, safety management is most important.
会社(かいしゃ)は従業員(じゅうぎょういん)の健康(けんこう)と安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)に責任(せきにん)を負(お)う。
The company is responsible for the health and safety management of its employees.
事故(じこ)防止(ぼうし)のため、定期的(ていきてき)に安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)の研修(けんしゅう)を行(おこな)っている。
To prevent accidents, we conduct safety management training on a regular basis.

Formed from 安全(あんぜん) (safety) + 管理(かんり) (management, administration). A standard term in workplace safety, disaster prevention, and regulatory compliance.

USAGE:

  • In workplaces with 50 or more employees in certain industries, Japanese law requires the appointment of an 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)者(しゃ) (safety manager).
  • Used in formal and professional contexts — training materials, company policies, government documents. Rare in everyday conversation.
  • Can take する to form a verb: 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)する (to manage safety).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)者(しゃ): safety manager
  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)体制(たいせい): safety management system
  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)規定(きてい): safety management regulations
  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)を徹底(てってい)する: to enforce strict safety management
  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり)の責任(せきにん): responsibility for safety management

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 危機(きき)管理(かんり): crisis management — focuses on responding to emergencies
  • 健康(けんこう)管理(かんり): health management — focuses on employee health
  • 品質(ひんしつ)管理(かんり): quality management — focuses on product/service quality
  • 労務(ろうむ)管理(かんり): labor management — broader administrative oversight of workers
