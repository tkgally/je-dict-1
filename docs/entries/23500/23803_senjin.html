先人(せんじん)

せんじん
noun
predecessor; forerunner; those who came before
1. predecessor; forerunner; those who came before; our forebears
People who lived or worked in the past, especially those whose achievements, wisdom, or example serve as a legacy to later generations. Typically collective rather than pointing to one individual, and carries a tone of respect. Often appears in essays, speeches, and commemorative writing in phrases like '先人(せんじん)の知恵(ちえ)' ('the wisdom of our forebears') or '先人(せんじん)の努力(どりょく)' ('the efforts of those before us').
先人(せんじん)の知恵(ちえ)。
The wisdom of our forebears.
先人(せんじん)の教(おし)えを大切(たいせつ)にする。
To treasure the teachings of those who came before us.
今日(こんにち)の繁栄(はんえい)は先人(せんじん)たちの努力(どりょく)の上(うえ)に成(な)り立(た)っている。
Today's prosperity stands upon the efforts of those who came before us.
研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)としては、先人(せんじん)の残(のこ)した業績(ぎょうせき)に謙虚(けんきょ)に学(まな)ぶ姿勢(しせい)が欠(か)かせない。
As a researcher, an attitude of humbly learning from the achievements left by one's predecessors is indispensable.

Compound of 先(せん) ('earlier; before') and 人(じん) ('person'). Pattern 先(せん)-: 先輩(せんぱい) ('senior'), 先祖(せんぞ) ('ancestor'), 先生(せんせい) ('teacher').

USAGE:

  • Usually collective and abstract: 'people of earlier times' in general, not one specific predecessor. For a specific named predecessor in a role, use 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ) or 先代(せんだい).
  • Carries a respectful tone and belongs to formal written and spoken registers (speeches, essays, academic prose). Rare in casual conversation.
  • Commonly followed by たち for a plural sense: 先人(せんじん)たち.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先人(せんじん)の知恵(ちえ): the wisdom of our forebears
  • 先人(せんじん)の教(おし)え: the teachings of those before us
  • 先人(せんじん)の努力(どりょく): the efforts of predecessors
  • 先人(せんじん)の業績(ぎょうせき): the achievements of predecessors
  • 先人(せんじん)に学(まな)ぶ: to learn from those who came before
  • 先人(せんじん)の足跡(あしあと)をたどる: to follow in the footsteps of our predecessors

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先輩(せんぱい): senior (same school, company, field) — a living, often personal relationship.
  • 先祖(せんぞ): ancestor — one's own family/lineage forebears.
  • 先駆者(せんくしゃ): pioneer — emphasizes being first in a new field.
  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ): one's immediate predecessor in a specific role.
  • 先代(せんだい): the previous generation (of a head of household, shop owner, etc.).

ANTONYM:

  • 後人(こうじん) / 後世(こうせい)の人(ひと): those who come after; future generations.
