臀部(でんぶ)

でんぶ
noun
buttocks; gluteal region
1. buttocks; gluteal region; rear; bottom
The buttocks or gluteal area of the body, treated as an anatomical region. This is the formal, technical term used in medical, anatomical, and fitness contexts, as opposed to everyday words like お尻(しり).
臀部(でんぶ)の筋肉(きんにく)を鍛(きた)える。
To train the muscles of the buttocks.
注射(ちゅうしゃ)は臀部(でんぶ)に打(う)ちます。
The injection is given in the buttocks.
長時間(ちょうじかん)座(すわ)っていると臀部(でんぶ)に痛(いた)みが出(で)ることがある。
Sitting for long periods can cause pain in the buttocks.
このトレーニングは臀部(でんぶ)と太(ふと)もも裏側(うらがわ)の筋肉(きんにく)を同時(どうじ)に刺激(しげき)する効果(こうか)があります。
This exercise has the effect of stimulating the muscles of the buttocks and the back of the thighs at the same time.

Composed of 臀(でん) (buttocks — a kanji used almost exclusively in this compound and a few medical terms) and 部(ぶ) (part, region).

REGISTER:
臀部(でんぶ) is a clinical, anatomical word. You will encounter it in medical documents, fitness and exercise articles, anatomy textbooks, and physical therapy contexts. In ordinary conversation, Japanese speakers say お尻(しり) (or simply 尻(しり)) instead. Using 臀部(でんぶ) in casual speech would sound oddly formal or euphemistic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 臀部(でんぶ)の筋肉(きんにく): the gluteal muscles
  • 臀部(でんぶ)痛(つう): pain in the buttocks
  • 臀部(でんぶ)に注射(ちゅうしゃ)する: to inject into the buttocks
  • 臀部(でんぶ)下垂(かすい): sagging of the buttocks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お尻(しり): bottom, butt — the everyday, conversational word
  • 尻(しり): butt — blunter, sometimes vulgar without the お
  • 大臀筋(だいでんきん): gluteus maximus — the specific muscle name
  • 腰(こし): lower back, hip area — overlapping but distinct region
