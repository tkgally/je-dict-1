立面図(りつめんず)

りつめんず
noun
elevation drawing; elevation view
1. elevation drawing; elevation view; front/side elevation
A drawing that shows a building as seen from the front, back, or side — representing the exterior as a flat, two-dimensional image. Used in architecture alongside plan views and cross-sections.
ビルの立面図(りつめんず)。
The elevation drawing of a building.
立面図(りつめんず)を見(み)れば、建物(たてもの)の外観(がいかん)が分(わ)かる。
You can tell what the building will look like from the elevation drawing.
正面(しょうめん)の立面図(りつめんず)と側面(そくめん)の立面図(りつめんず)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the front elevation and side elevation drawings.
建築(けんちく)の図面(ずめん)には、平面図(へいめんず)・立面図(りつめんず)・断面図(だんめんず)の三種類(さんしゅるい)がある。
Architectural drawings come in three main types: plan, elevation, and cross-section.

Composed of 立面(りつめん) (vertical face, elevation) and 図(ず) (diagram, drawing). A 立面図(りつめんず) depicts a building's exterior from a particular direction — typically the front, rear, or side — showing walls, windows, doors, and the overall shape without perspective distortion.

USAGE:
Standard term in architecture, construction, and civil engineering documents. Often specified by direction: 正面(しょうめん)立面図(りつめんず) (front elevation), 背面(はいめん)立面図(りつめんず) (rear elevation), 側面(そくめん)立面図(りつめんず) (side elevation).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立面図(りつめんず)を描(か)く: to draw an elevation
  • 立面図(りつめんず)を作成(さくせい)する: to prepare an elevation drawing
  • 正面(しょうめん)立面図(りつめんず): front elevation
  • 立面図(りつめんず)と平面図(へいめんず): elevation and plan views

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 平面図(へいめんず): floor plan, plan view — shows the building from above
  • 断面図(だんめんず): cross-section drawing — shows the interior as if sliced
  • 外観図(がいかんず): exterior drawing — similar but may include perspective or 3D rendering
  • 透視図(とうしず): perspective drawing — shows the building with depth and vanishing points
