季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)

きせつぎょうじ
noun
seasonal event; seasonal observance
1. seasonal event; seasonal observance; seasonal tradition
A recurring event, custom, or observance tied to a particular time of year — such as 花見(はなみ) in spring, 花火(はなび)大会(たいかい) in summer, 月見(つきみ) in autumn, or 正月(しょうがつ) in winter. Includes both traditional cultural observances and modern seasonal promotions at schools, shops, and community groups.
季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy seasonal events.
日本(にほん)には様々(さまざま)な季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)がある。
There are various seasonal events in Japan.
子供(こども)たちと一緒(いっしょ)に季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)を体験(たいけん)することは大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to experience seasonal events together with children.
商店街(しょうてんがい)では、季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)に合(あ)わせて飾(かざ)り付(つ)けやイベントが行(おこな)われる。
In the shopping district, decorations and events are put on in line with the seasonal occasions.

Composed of 季節(きせつ) (season) and 行事(ぎょうじ) (event, function, observance). A broad term covering both traditional calendar-based observances and contemporary seasonal programming at kindergartens, schools, community centers, and retail businesses.

USAGE:
Often used in the plural sense to refer to 'seasonal events' as a category — for example in kindergarten curricula, community-center programming, or department-store marketing. Individual events like 七夕(たなばた) or 節分(せつぶん) are more specifically called 年中行事(ねんちゅうぎょうじ) (annual events), but the two terms overlap.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)を行(おこな)う: to hold a seasonal event
  • 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)に参加(さんか)する: to take part in a seasonal event
  • 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)を楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy seasonal events
  • 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)を大切(たいせつ)にする: to value / cherish seasonal traditions
  • 日本(にほん)の季節行事(きせつぎょうじ): Japanese seasonal events

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年中行事(ねんちゅうぎょうじ): annual event — traditional observances that recur at a fixed time each year (正月(しょうがつ)、節分(せつぶん)、etc.); a major subset of 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)
  • 伝統行事(でんとうぎょうじ): traditional event — specifically cultural and heritage events, not modern seasonal promotions
  • 祭(まつ)り: festival — a specific, typically local, festive event, often a type of 季節行事(きせつぎょうじ)
  • {イベント}: event — the general modern loanword, used for any event regardless of seasonal tie-in
