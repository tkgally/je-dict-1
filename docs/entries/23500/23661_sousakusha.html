創作者(そうさくしゃ)

そうさくしゃ
noun
creator; author (of a creative work)
1. creator; author; originator of a creative work
The person who creates an original work — a novel, illustration, song, game, video, or other artistic or creative product. Increasingly common in online and publishing contexts to refer to individual creators (authors, artists, designers, content makers) and to distinguish them from mere reproducers or users.
創作者(そうさくしゃ)の名前(なまえ)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the name of the creator.
この作品(さくひん)の創作者(そうさくしゃ)には著作権(ちょさくけん)が認(みと)められている。
The creator of this work is granted copyright.
インターネットの普及(ふきゅう)によって、誰(だれ)でも自由(じゆう)に作品(さくひん)を発表(はっぴょう)できる創作者(そうさくしゃ)になれる時代(じだい)になった。
With the spread of the internet, we've entered an era in which anyone can become a creator who freely publishes their work.

Composed of 創作(そうさく) (creation, original work) and the suffix 〜者(しゃ) (person). A somewhat formal, written-register word. Often appears in copyright and intellectual-property contexts (the person whose rights attach to a work), and in online discussions of original content — for example, contrasting 創作者(そうさくしゃ) with 模倣者(もほうしゃ) (imitator) or 消費者(しょうひしゃ) (consumer).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)の権利(けんり): the creator's rights
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)本人(ほんにん): the creator themselves
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)の意図(いと): the creator's intent
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)として活動(かつどう)する: to work/be active as a creator
  • 創作者(そうさくしゃ)を尊重(そんちょう)する: to respect the creator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 作者(さくしゃ): author — the everyday word for the author of a specific work (of literature, art, etc.)
  • 著者(ちょしゃ): author (of a book) — specifically the writer of a published book
  • クリエイター: creator — the borrowed word; especially common for online/social-media content creators
  • 作家(さっか): writer, artist — a professional whose job is creative work
  • 創作家(そうさくか): creative artist — very close in meaning; less common than 創作者(そうさくしゃ)
