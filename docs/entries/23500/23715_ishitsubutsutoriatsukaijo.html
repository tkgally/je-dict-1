遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)

いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ
noun
lost-and-found office; lost property office
1. lost-and-found office; lost property office
An office or counter at a station, airport, public facility, or police jurisdiction where items lost by the public are received, held, and returned to their owners. The formal term seen on signs at train stations and on railway websites.
遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)はどこですか。
Where is the lost-and-found office?
駅(えき)の遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)に問(と)い合(あ)わせてみてください。
Please try contacting the station's lost-and-found office.
電車(でんしゃ)に傘(かさ)を忘(わす)れてしまったので、遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)に電話(でんわ)した。
I left my umbrella on the train, so I called the lost-and-found office.
空港(くうこう)の遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)で、なくした財布(さいふ)が見(み)つかったという連絡(れんらく)があった。
The airport lost-and-found office contacted me to say that the wallet I had lost had been found.

Composed of 遺失物(いしつぶつ) (lost article) and 取扱所(とりあつかいじょ) (handling office, counter). The formal, signage-style term for what is commonly called a 'lost and found' in English.

USAGE:
Seen on signage and on official websites of railway companies (JR}、{東京(とうきょう)メトロ、etc.), airports, and police stations. In spoken Japanese, speakers often use shorter variants like 遺失物(いしつぶつ)センター or 忘(わす)れ物(もの)センター.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駅(えき)の遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ): the station lost-and-found
  • 遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)に問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire at the lost-and-found office
  • 遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)に届(とど)ける: to hand in / turn in (a found item) at the lost-and-found
  • 遺失物取扱所(いしつぶつとりあつかいじょ)に保管(ほかん)される: to be kept at the lost-and-found

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 忘(わす)れ物(もの)センター: lost-and-found center — the more everyday term, common at railway companies' consumer-facing websites
  • 遺失物(いしつぶつ)センター: lost-property center — near-synonym; same scope, slightly more formal register
  • 落(お)とし物(もの)保管所(ほかんじょ): found-items storage — emphasizes holding items until claimed
  • 拾得物(しゅうとくぶつ): found article — the legal counterpart of 遺失物(いしつぶつ), referring to the item from the finder's perspective
