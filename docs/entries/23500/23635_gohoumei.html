ご芳名(ほうめい)

ごほうめい
noun
your (esteemed) name; your name (polite, written)
1. your (esteemed) name (very polite)
A highly polite way to refer to someone else's name — used almost exclusively in writing, on forms, and at formal ceremonies. Common on wedding guest books, condolence registers, reception sign-in sheets, and formal invitations.
ご芳名(ほうめい)をお書(か)きください。
Please write your name.
受付(うけつけ)でご芳名(ほうめい)をご記入(きにゅう)ください。
Please write your name at the reception desk.
披露宴(ひろうえん)の会場(かいじょう)には、ご芳名(ほうめい)帳(ちょう)が置(お)かれ、出席者(しゅっせきしゃ)が順番(じゅんばん)に名前(なまえ)を記(しる)していた。
A guest book was placed at the wedding reception, and the attendees were writing their names in turn.

Composed of the beautifying / honorific prefix ご〜 + 芳名(ほうめい) ("fragrant/esteemed name"), a classical-feeling compound of 芳(ほう) (fragrant; a polite adjective for another's things) and 名(めい) (name). The literal "fragrant name" metaphor signals deep respect for the person whose name is being asked for.

USAGE:

  • Used to refer to the addressee's name respectfully in very formal written contexts. Ordinary polite conversation uses お名前(なまえ) (your name) instead.
  • Appears most often on forms and registers where guests are asked to write their names: 芳名(ほうめい)帳(ちょう) (guest book), 芳名(ほうめい)録(ろく) (register of names).
  • Common at weddings, funerals, opening ceremonies, exhibition openings, and other formal events. On an invitation, ご芳名(ほうめい) may be used to respectfully refer to the invitee's name.
  • When using ご芳名(ほうめい) about your own name, you must drop the ご〜 and ideally avoid 芳名(ほうめい) altogether — use お名前(おなまえ) or 名前(なまえ) for your own.

FORMALITY NOTE:

  • This word belongs to highly formal written Japanese; it is out of place in ordinary conversation. In most spoken settings, お名前(なまえ) is the natural polite choice.
  • Common invitation-card politeness rule: when RSVPing, guests often cross out the ご before 芳名(ほうめい) on their returned card as a gesture of humility toward their own name.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご芳名(ほうめい)をお書(か)きください: please write your name
  • ご芳名(ほうめい)をご記入(きにゅう)ください: please fill in your name
  • ご芳名(ほうめい)帳(ちょう): guest book / register
  • ご芳名(ほうめい)録(ろく): register of names (formal, for ceremonial events)
  • ご芳名(ほうめい)を賜(たまわ)る: to kindly receive (someone's) name (very formal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お名前(なまえ): your name — the standard polite term; used in everyday polite speech and on most forms.
  • ご氏名(しめい): your full name — formal; asks specifically for the full family-and-given name, commonly on official documents.
  • 御名前(おなまえ): polite, written variant of お名前(なまえ); essentially the same in meaning.
