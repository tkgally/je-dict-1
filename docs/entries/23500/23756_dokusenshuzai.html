1. exclusive interview; exclusive coverage; scoop
News coverage or an interview obtained by a single media outlet with no access granted to competitors. A common selling point on magazine covers and in TV news promotion, signaling that the content cannot be seen or read elsewhere.
これは独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)です。
This is an exclusive interview.
有名(ゆうめい)な監督(かんとく)に独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)を行(おこな)った。
We conducted an exclusive interview with the famous director.
週刊(しゅうかん)誌(し)が事件(じけん)の当事者(とうじしゃ)への独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)に成功(せいこう)し、話題(わだい)を集(あつ)めた。
The weekly magazine landed an exclusive interview with the person at the center of the incident and drew a lot of attention.

Compound of 独占(どくせん) ('monopoly; exclusive possession') + 取材(しゅざい) ('news gathering, reporting; interview'). Widely used by newspapers, TV news programs, and weekly magazines to promote content that cannot be found in competing outlets.

Can function grammatically as both a noun and a する verb: 独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)する ('to get an exclusive interview').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)に成功(せいこう)する: to succeed in getting an exclusive
  • 独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)を行(おこな)う: to conduct an exclusive interview
  • 独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)を受(う)ける: to grant an exclusive interview
  • 独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)映像(えいぞう): exclusive footage
  • 緊急(きんきゅう)独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい): urgent exclusive interview

RELATED TERMS:

  • 取材(しゅざい): news gathering; reporting; interview (the general term)
  • 独占(どくせん): monopoly; exclusive control
  • スクープ: scoop — an exclusive story, especially a surprising one
  • 独占(どくせん)放送(ほうそう): exclusive broadcast
  • 独占(どくせん)配信(はいしん): exclusive streaming/distribution
  • 会見(かいけん): press conference — contrasts with an exclusive in that many outlets attend

USAGE:
Strongly associated with media and journalism. On magazine covers it often appears as a large headline: 「独占(どくせん)取材(しゅざい)!」
