来訪者(らいほうしゃ)

らいほうしゃ
noun
visitor; caller; guest
1. visitor; caller; person who comes to call on someone
A person who comes to visit a place — typically a home, office, institution, or website. A formal-sounding word most often used in business, official, or written contexts; in everyday conversation, words like お客(きゃく)さん are far more common.
来訪者(らいほうしゃ)を受付(うけつけ)で確認(かくにん)する。
Check visitors in at the reception desk.
来訪者(らいほうしゃ)には必(かなら)ず名札(なふだ)をお渡(わた)ししています。
We always issue a name badge to visitors.
最近(さいきん)、このサイトの来訪者(らいほうしゃ)が急激(きゅうげき)に増(ふ)えている。
Recently, visitors to this site have been increasing rapidly.
大統領(だいとうりょう)の執務室(しつむしつ)には、毎日(まいにち)国内外(こくないがい)から多(おお)くの来訪者(らいほうしゃ)があり、対応(たいおう)に追(お)われている。
The president's office has many visitors from Japan and abroad every day, and he is kept busy attending to them.

Composed of 来訪(らいほう) (visit, call) and 者(しゃ) (person). Literally "a person who comes to visit."

USAGE:

来訪者(らいほうしゃ) is a fairly formal, written-sounding word. It appears frequently in:

  • business and official contexts (building security, reception procedures, government offices)
  • news and reports ("来訪者(らいほうしゃ)の数(かず)", number of visitors)
  • web analytics — used for "visitors" to a website, though ユーザー or アクセス数(すう) are also common

In everyday conversation, Japanese speakers rarely say 来訪者(らいほうしゃ). Words like お客(きゃく)さん (customer, guest) or just 人(ひと) are used instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 来訪者(らいほうしゃ)を迎(むか)える: to receive a visitor
  • 来訪者(らいほうしゃ)の受付(うけつけ): reception of visitors
  • 来訪者(らいほうしゃ)名簿(めいぼ): visitor log / register
  • 来訪者(らいほうしゃ)数(すう): number of visitors
  • 来訪者(らいほうしゃ)がある: to have a visitor / for a visitor to come

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 訪問者(ほうもんしゃ): visitor — very close synonym; slightly more active (the visitor is actively going to the place)
  • お客(きゃく)さん: guest, customer — everyday word
  • 来客(らいきゃく): caller, incoming guest (often in a business setting)
  • 参観者(さんかんしゃ): observer, visitor (to an event, class, or open house)
  • ビジター: visitor — loanword, often used in sports and at hotels/clubs

REGISTER:
Formal, written. For conversation, prefer お客(きゃく)さん or 来客(らいきゃく).
