社交性(しゃこうせい)

しゃこうせい
noun
sociability; social skill; being good with people
1. sociability; sociable nature; ease in interacting with others
The personality trait of being comfortable around other people and skilled at interacting with them — enjoying company, making conversation easily, and getting along smoothly in social settings. Treated as a character strength in self-descriptions, job interviews, and personality discussions.
彼女(かのじょ)は社交性(しゃこうせい)がある。
She is sociable.
仕事(しごと)がら、社交性(しゃこうせい)が求(もと)められる。
Because of the nature of the job, sociability is required.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)は内気(うちき)だったが、大人(おとな)になるにつれて社交性(しゃこうせい)が身(み)についてきた。
I was shy as a child, but I've gradually developed social skills as I've grown up.

Composed of 社交(しゃこう) (social interaction, socializing) and the suffix 〜性(せい) (-ness, -ity, the quality of being). Describes a personal trait rather than a skill learned in a specific context: someone with high 社交性(しゃこうせい) enjoys and is good at mingling, small talk, and forming new connections.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)がある: to be sociable
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)がない: to be unsociable, reserved
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)に欠(か)ける: to lack sociability
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)が高(たか)い: to be highly sociable
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)を身(み)につける: to acquire social skills
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい)が求(もと)められる: sociability is required / called for

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 社交的(しゃこうてき): sociable (na-adjective) — the adjectival form used to describe people directly
  • 協調性(きょうちょうせい): cooperativeness — the ability to work harmoniously with others; emphasized in Japanese group settings
  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく): communication ability — broader professional skill, not limited to friendly interaction
  • 人(ひと)づきあいのよさ: being good with people — a more colloquial equivalent
