発足式(ほっそくしき)

ほっそくしき
noun
inauguration ceremony; launch ceremony; kickoff ceremony
1. inauguration ceremony; launch ceremony; kickoff ceremony
A formal ceremony held to mark the start of a new organization, committee, project, or system. Common for new government agencies, research institutes, associations, and corporate initiatives.
新(あたら)しい委員会(いいんかい)の発足式(ほっそくしき)が開(ひら)かれた。
An inauguration ceremony for the new committee was held.
市長(しちょう)が発足式(ほっそくしき)であいさつを述(の)べた。
The mayor gave a speech at the inauguration ceremony.
研究(けんきゅう)プロジェクトの発足式(ほっそくしき)には関係(かんけい)者(しゃ)が多(おお)く集(あつ)まった。
Many stakeholders gathered for the launch ceremony of the research project.

Compound of 発足(ほっそく) ('launch; founding; starting operation') and 式(しき) ('ceremony'). The verb 発足(ほっそく)する means 'to be launched; to start operating', and 発足式(ほっそくしき) is the formal ceremony that marks that moment. Commonly seen in news reports about new committees, departments, councils, and projects.

USAGE:

  • A formal term found in official announcements, press releases, and news reports.
  • Typical events at a 発足式(ほっそくしき) include speeches by the chairperson and guests, an introduction of members, and sometimes a symbolic action such as a ribbon cutting ({テープカット}) or a group photo.
  • The word 発足(ほっそく) is read ほっそく in standard usage, though はっそく is occasionally heard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発足式(ほっそくしき)を開(ひら)く: to hold an inauguration ceremony
  • 発足式(ほっそくしき)を行(おこな)う: to carry out an inauguration ceremony
  • 発足式(ほっそくしき)に出席(しゅっせき)する: to attend an inauguration ceremony
  • 発足式(ほっそくしき)であいさつを述(の)べる: to give an address at the inauguration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開所式(かいしょしき): opening ceremony (of a facility) — for the opening of a physical office or institution.
  • 開会式(かいかいしき): opening ceremony (of an event) — for sporting events, conferences, etc.
  • 就任式(しゅうにんしき): inauguration (of a person taking office) — centers on an individual assuming a post, not an organization being founded.
  • 創立(そうりつ)記念(きねん)式(しき): founding anniversary ceremony — held to commemorate an already-founded organization.
