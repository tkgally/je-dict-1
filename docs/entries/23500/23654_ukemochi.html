受(う)け持(も)ち

うけもち
noun
charge; responsibility; being in charge (of)
1. charge; responsibility; the task or area someone is in charge of
A duty, task, class, territory, or area that a particular person is responsible for. Frequently used for teachers' assigned classes, salespeople's assigned territories, nurses' assigned patients, and workers' designated sections. Can refer to either the duty itself or, by extension, the person in charge.
私(わたし)の受(う)け持(も)ちです。
That's my responsibility.
田中(たなか)先生(せんせい)は3年(ねん)2組(くみ)の受(う)け持(も)ちだ。
Mr. Tanaka is in charge of the 3rd-year class 2.
営業(えいぎょう)の受(う)け持(も)ち地域(ちいき)が来月(らいげつ)から変(か)わることになった。
My assigned sales territory will change starting next month.

The noun form of 受(う)け持(も)つ (to take charge of; to be assigned). Refers to whatever falls within a particular person's area of responsibility — a class, a patient, a customer, a territory, a topic, a section of work. In schools, the 受(う)け持(も)ちの先生(せんせい) is specifically the homeroom teacher assigned to a class.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受(う)け持(も)ちの先生(せんせい): one's homeroom teacher
  • 受(う)け持(も)ちの患者(かんじゃ): the patient one is assigned to
  • 受(う)け持(も)ち地域(ちいき): assigned territory
  • 受(う)け持(も)ち区域(くいき): assigned area/district
  • 受(う)け持(も)ちの生徒(せいと): the pupils in one's charge
  • 受(う)け持(も)ちになる: to become the person in charge
  • 受(う)け持(も)ちを外(はず)れる: to be removed from a charge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 担当(たんとう): being in charge — more formal and very common in workplace contexts (担当者(たんとうしゃ) "person in charge")
  • 担任(たんにん): homeroom teacher (a specific school usage); also "being in charge"
  • 責任(せきにん): responsibility — the abstract concept of accountability, not the duty itself
  • 仕事(しごと): work, job — broader; 受(う)け持(も)ち specifies the allocated portion
