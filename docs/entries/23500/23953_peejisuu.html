ページ数(すう)

ぺーじすう
noun
number of pages; page count
1. number of pages; page count; total pages
The total count of pages in a book, document, report, thesis, or printed material. Used when referring to the length or volume of written content, often in contexts like publishing, academic writing, printing, and office work.
この本(ほん)のページ数(すう)は何(なん)ページですか。
How many pages does this book have?
レポートのページ数(すう)が足(た)りない。
My report doesn't have enough pages.
論文(ろんぶん)のページ数(すう)が規定(きてい)を超(こ)えてしまったので、内容(ないよう)を削(けず)る必要(ひつよう)がある。
My paper has exceeded the page limit, so I need to cut some of the content.

Compound of the loanword ページ ('page') and the suffix 〜数(すう) ('number of; count of'), which attaches productively to many nouns to mean 'number of X': 人数(にんずう) ('number of people'), 回数(かいすう) ('number of times'), 件数(けんすう) ('number of cases'), and so on.

USAGE:

  • Refers to a count, not to individual page numbers. For 'page number' (e.g., 'page 12'), use ページ{番号(ばんごう)} or simply specify the page with "○○ページ".
  • Frequently appears with verbs of quantity: 多(おお)い ('large'), 少(すく)ない ('few'), 足(た)りる / 足(た)りない ('enough / not enough'), 超(こ)える ('to exceed'), 制限(せいげん)する ('to limit').
  • Very common in academic, publishing, and office contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ページ数(すう)が多(おお)い: to have many pages
  • ページ数(すう)が少(すく)ない: to have few pages
  • ページ数(すう)を数(かぞ)える: to count the number of pages
  • ページ数(すう)制限(せいげん): page limit
  • 総(そう)ページ数(すう): total number of pages
  • ページ数(すう)が増(ふ)える: the page count grows

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ページ番号(ばんごう): page number — the numeric label on a specific page, not the total count.
  • 文字数(もじすう): character count — another measure of document length, tied to characters rather than pages.
  • 枚数(まいすう): number of sheets — used for loose paper, documents, or tickets, not for bound pages.
  • 分量(ぶんりょう): amount; volume — a more general word for the overall size or quantity of content.
