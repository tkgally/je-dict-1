投稿者(とうこうしゃ)

とうこうしゃ
noun
poster; contributor; person who submits a post or article
1. poster; contributor; person who submits a post, comment, or article
The person who writes and submits a post, comment, photo, or article to a magazine, newspaper, bulletin board, blog, or social media platform. Originally used of readers contributing to print publications; now the default word for someone who posts content online.
投稿者(とうこうしゃ)の名前(なまえ)は伏(ふ)せられている。
The poster's name has been withheld.
この写真(しゃしん)の投稿者(とうこうしゃ)は北海道(ほっかいどう)在住(ざいじゅう)らしい。
The person who posted this photo apparently lives in Hokkaido.
運営(うんえい)側(がわ)は悪質(あくしつ)なコメントを繰(く)り返(かえ)す投稿者(とうこうしゃ)のアカウントを凍結(とうけつ)することにした。
The management decided to freeze the accounts of posters who repeatedly made malicious comments.

Compound of 投稿(とうこう) ('to post; to submit a contribution') and 者(しゃ) ('person'). A 投稿者(とうこうしゃ) is literally 'one who posts'.

USAGE:

  • Neutral term with no positive or negative connotation on its own; context usually supplies the evaluation (e.g., 悪質(あくしつ)な投稿者(とうこうしゃ) 'malicious poster', 常連(じょうれん)投稿者(とうこうしゃ) 'regular contributor').
  • Appears on websites and in apps as a generic label for the author of a piece of user-generated content (photo, review, comment, question).
  • When referring to one's own post, speakers use 自分(じぶん) or 私(わたし) rather than calling themselves 投稿者(とうこうしゃ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投稿者(とうこうしゃ)の名前(なまえ): the poster's name
  • 投稿者(とうこうしゃ)本人(ほんにん): the poster himself/herself
  • 投稿者(とうこうしゃ)を特定(とくてい)する: to identify the poster
  • 匿名(とくめい)の投稿者(とうこうしゃ): anonymous poster
  • 常連(じょうれん)投稿者(とうこうしゃ): regular contributor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 投稿(とうこう)する: to post; to submit — the verb the noun is derived from.
  • 寄稿者(きこうしゃ): contributor (to a magazine or publication) — more formal; used of writers invited to submit articles to a specific publication.
  • 発信者(はっしんしゃ): sender; originator (of a message or information) — broader, covering anyone who sends or emits a communication.
  • ユーザー: user — covers anyone using a service; narrower than 投稿者(とうこうしゃ) since not all users post content.
