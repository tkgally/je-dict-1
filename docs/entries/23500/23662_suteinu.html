捨(す)て犬(いぬ)

すていぬ
noun
abandoned dog; stray dog that has been abandoned by its owner
1. abandoned dog; dog that has been thrown away by its owner
A pet dog that has been abandoned by its owner — left on a street, dumped in the countryside, or tied up outside a shelter. The term emphasizes the owner's act of abandoning the animal and carries a strongly negative emotional charge; it is the standard word used in discussions of pet welfare and adoption.
公園(こうえん)で捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を見(み)つけた。
I found an abandoned dog in the park.
家族(かぞく)で捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を引(ひ)き取(と)ることにした。
Our family decided to take in an abandoned dog.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)になると、旅行(りょこう)先(さき)などで捨(す)て犬(いぬ)にされるケースが増(ふ)えると言(い)われている。
Every year when summer comes, it is said that cases of dogs being abandoned — at travel destinations and so on — increase.

Compound of the verb stem 捨(す)て (from 捨(す)てる, "to throw away, abandon") and 犬(いぬ) (dog). The verb stem acts as a modifier meaning "thrown away," so 捨(す)て犬(いぬ) is literally "a thrown-away dog." The parallel word 捨(す)て猫(ねこ) exists for cats.

The word distinguishes animals that were someone's pet and then abandoned from other kinds of strays; a dog born in the wild would more naturally be 野犬(やけん) (wild/feral dog) rather than 捨(す)て犬(いぬ). Because abandoning a pet is a crime in Japan under the Animal Welfare Act, the term often appears in news stories and animal-welfare campaigns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を拾(ひろ)う: to pick up an abandoned dog
  • 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を保護(ほご)する: to rescue an abandoned dog
  • 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を引(ひ)き取(と)る: to take in an abandoned dog
  • 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)にする: to abandon a dog
  • 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)を減(へ)らす: to reduce the number of abandoned dogs

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 捨(す)て猫(ねこ): abandoned cat — the parallel word for cats
  • 野良犬(のらいぬ): stray dog — broader; a dog living on the streets regardless of origin
  • 野犬(やけん): feral/wild dog — a dog never socialized as a pet
  • 保護犬(ほごけん): rescue dog — a dog currently being cared for at a shelter, often a former 捨(す)て犬(いぬ)
  • 迷(まよ)い犬(いぬ): lost dog — a dog that is missing, not intentionally abandoned
