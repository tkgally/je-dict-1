非論理的(ひろんりてき)

ひろんりてき
na-adjective
illogical; not logical
1. illogical; lacking logical coherence
Describes reasoning, arguments, statements, or actions that do not follow logical principles. Typically used negatively to criticize reasoning that is inconsistent, unreasoned, or based on emotion rather than analysis. The direct antonym is 論理的(ろんりてき) ('logical').
非論理的(ひろんりてき)な主張(しゅちょう)。
An illogical argument.
彼(かれ)の説明(せつめい)は非論理的(ひろんりてき)で納得(なっとく)できない。
His explanation is illogical and unconvincing.
感情(かんじょう)に流(なが)されて非論理的(ひろんりてき)な判断(はんだん)をしてしまうことは誰(だれ)にでもある。
Anyone can end up making illogical judgments when swayed by emotion.

Formed with the negating prefix 非(ひ) ('non-; un-') attached to 論理的(ろんりてき) ('logical'). The prefix 非〜(ひ) is productive: 非公式(ひこうしき) ('unofficial'), 非常識(ひじょうしき) ('unreasonable; outrageous'), 非科学的(ひかがくてき) ('unscientific').

USAGE:

  • Used as a な-adjective: 非論理的(ひろんりてき)な結論(けつろん) ('an illogical conclusion').
  • Adverbially: 非論理的(ひろんりてき)に考(かんが)える ('to think illogically').
  • Usually evaluative and negative — used when criticizing someone's reasoning.
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき) ('irrational') overlaps in meaning; see distinctions below.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき)な主張(しゅちょう): illogical claim
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき)な判断(はんだん): illogical judgment
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき)な結論(けつろん): illogical conclusion
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき)に感(かん)じる: to feel illogical
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき)な思考(しこう): illogical thinking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical — the direct antonym.
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき): irrational — stresses being contrary to reason; often covers economic or emotional irrationality rather than a broken argument.
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)した: contradictory — points to specific contradictions, not overall illogic.
  • 無茶(むちゃ)な: unreasonable; reckless — more colloquial, stresses going beyond what is sensible.
