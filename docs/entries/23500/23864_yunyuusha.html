輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)

ゆにゅうしゃ
noun
imported car; foreign-made car
1. imported car — a car manufactured overseas and brought into Japan for sale
A car manufactured outside Japan and imported for sale domestically, contrasted with 国産車(こくさんしゃ) (domestically produced cars). In Japan, 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ) is often associated with European brands such as BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, and Volkswagen, and carries connotations of higher price and prestige.
父(ちち)は輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)に乗(の)っている。
My father drives an imported car.
輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)は修理代(しゅうりだい)が高(たか)いとよく言(い)われる。
It's often said that repairs for imported cars are expensive.
最近(さいきん)、国産車(こくさんしゃ)よりも輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)を選(えら)ぶ若者(わかもの)が増(ふ)えているそうだ。
It seems that more young people are choosing imported cars over domestic ones lately.

Compound of 輸入(ゆにゅう) ('import') and 車(しゃ) ('vehicle'). Read as しゃ, not くるま, in this compound.

USAGE:

  • Refers to passenger cars imported into Japan. In Japanese usage, the term is most often associated with European luxury and sports brands, because domestic (Japanese) cars dominate the local market.
  • In news and industry contexts, 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ) is contrasted with 国産車(こくさんしゃ) when discussing market share, sales trends, or consumer preferences.
  • Near-synonym 外車(がいしゃ) is more colloquial and has the same practical meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)を買(か)う: to buy an imported car
  • 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)に乗(の)る: to drive / ride in an imported car
  • 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)販売(はんばい): sale of imported cars
  • 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ){ディーラー}: imported car dealer
  • 輸入車(ゆにゅうしゃ)市場(しじょう): imported car market

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 外車(がいしゃ): foreign car — colloquial near-synonym; slightly more casual and more common in everyday speech.
  • 国産車(こくさんしゃ): domestic car — the direct antonym, referring to cars made in Japan.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 輸入(ゆにゅう): import (goods from abroad)
  • 輸出車(ゆしゅつしゃ): exported car
  • 中古車(ちゅうこしゃ): used car
  • 新車(しんしゃ): new car
