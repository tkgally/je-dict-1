魚肉(ぎょにく)

ぎょにく
noun
fish meat; fish flesh (as food product)
1. the flesh of fish, considered as a food ingredient — especially used as a generic or categorical term, or in processed-food names
A slightly technical or categorical word for fish as meat, in contrast to 肉(にく) when it refers to livestock meat. Commonly seen on food labels and in processed-food names like 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ (fish sausage) or 魚肉(ぎょにく)ハム. Much less common in everyday cooking speech, where people simply say 魚(さかな).
魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージが好(す)きだ。
I like fish sausages.
魚肉(ぎょにく)は高(たか)タンパクで低(てい)カロリーだ。
Fish meat is high in protein and low in calories.
この練(ね)り物(もの)は魚肉(ぎょにく)を主(しゅ)原料(げんりょう)としている。
This processed product is made primarily from fish meat.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)は、お弁当(べんとう)に魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージがよく入(はい)っていた。
When I was a kid, fish sausages were often in my lunchbox.

Compound of 魚(ぎょ) ("fish") + 肉(にく) ("meat, flesh"). Treats fish as a category of meat. The word is somewhat technical and appears more in food-industry, nutritional, and processed-product contexts than in everyday speech.

USAGE:

  • In everyday conversation, people normally say 魚(さかな) when talking about eating fish — not 魚肉(ぎょにく).
  • Extremely common as the first half of compound product names: 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ, 魚肉(ぎょにく)ハム, 魚肉(ぎょにく)練製品(ねりせいひん) (fish paste products).
  • Used in nutrition and cooking-science discussions to classify food types.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ: fish sausage
  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)ハム: fish ham
  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)加工(かこう)品(ひん): processed fish products
  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)練製品(ねりせいひん): fish paste products (kamaboko, chikuwa, etc.)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 魚(さかな): fish — the everyday word, used whether alive, raw, or cooked.
  • 肉(にく): meat — typically livestock meat (beef, pork, chicken); contrasts with 魚肉(ぎょにく).
  • 畜肉(ちくにく): livestock meat — the technical counterpart to 魚肉(ぎょにく).
  • 白身(しろみ)/赤身(あかみ): white-fleshed / red-fleshed (fish) — describe types of fish flesh.
