教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)

きょういくかてい
noun
curriculum; educational program; course of study
1. curriculum; educational program; course of study
The overall plan of subjects, content, and activities that make up a school's instructional program. Used for both compulsory education and higher education in Japan. Organized by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) for public schools, with each school adapting the national curriculum to its own context.
教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)を見直(みなお)す。
To review the curriculum.
新(あたら)しい教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)が来年度(らいねんど)から始(はじ)まる。
The new curriculum will begin next academic year.
この大学(だいがく)の教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)は実習(じっしゅう)を重視(じゅうし)している。
This university's curriculum places emphasis on practical training.
文部科学省(もんぶかがくしょう)は十年(じゅうねん)ごとに教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)の基準(きじゅん)を改訂(かいてい)している。
The Ministry of Education revises the curriculum standards every ten years.

Formed from 教育(きょういく) (education) + 課程(かてい) (course, program). A formal term used in education administration, school management, and academic writing.

USAGE:

  • In Japan, the national standard for public school curricula is the 学習(がくしゅう)指導(しどう)要領(ようりょう) (Course of Study), and individual schools design their own 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい) based on it.
  • At the university level, 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい) refers to the program of courses leading to a degree. It is also used for 教員(きょういん)養成(ようせい)課程(かてい) (teacher training program).
  • More formal than カリキュラム (curriculum, the loanword), which is used in everyday conversation and in some academic writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)を編成(へんせい)する: to organize / design a curriculum
  • 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)の改訂(かいてい): revision of the curriculum
  • 新(しん)教育(きょういく)課程(かてい): the new curriculum
  • 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)の基準(きじゅん): curriculum standards
  • 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)表(ひょう): curriculum chart

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カリキュラム: curriculum — loanword, more conversational, same basic meaning
  • 学習(がくしゅう)指導(しどう)要領(ようりょう): MEXT's national course of study — the framework that guides 教育(きょういく)課程(かてい)
  • 授業(じゅぎょう)計画(けいかく): lesson plan — a narrower, individual-lesson-level plan
  • シラバス: syllabus — a single course's outline
