中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)

ちゅうしんぶ
noun
central part; center; downtown
1. central part; center; downtown area
The central or innermost portion of a geographical area, city, object, or region. Often used of the downtown core of a city, the inner zone of a typhoon, or the central part of a body or structure.
都市(とし)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)。
The downtown area of the city.
台風(たいふう)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)が上陸(じょうりく)した。
The center of the typhoon made landfall.
東京(とうきょう)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)は家賃(やちん)が非常(ひじょう)に高(たか)い。
Rent is extremely high in the central areas of Tokyo.
地震(じしん)の被害(ひがい)は市(し)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)に集中(しゅうちゅう)していた。
The earthquake damage was concentrated in the central part of the city.

Compound of 中心(ちゅうしん) ('center; middle') and the suffix 部(ぶ) ('part; section'). The 部(ぶ) suffix is productive and produces area/zone nouns such as 上部(じょうぶ) ('upper part'), 下部(かぶ) ('lower part'), and 内部(ないぶ) ('interior').

USAGE:
Refers to the central zone of something that has spatial extent — a city, a region, a storm system, an organ. Slightly more technical or formal than plain 中心(ちゅうしん), which can mean 'center point' or 'focal person.' Often translated as 'downtown,' 'city center,' or simply 'center' depending on context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 都市(とし)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ): downtown of the city
  • 中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)に位置(いち)する: to be located in the center
  • 中心部(ちゅうしんぶ)を通(とお)る: to pass through the central area
  • 台風(たいふう)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ): the center of a typhoon
  • 都心(としん)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ): the very heart of the city
  • 街(まち)の中心部(ちゅうしんぶ): the town center

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中心(ちゅうしん): center; focus — abstract 'center'; can refer to a point or person
  • 都心(としん): urban center; downtown (specifically of a metropolis like Tokyo)
  • 中央(ちゅうおう): central; middle — emphasizes axis or national center (中央政府(ちゅうおうせいふ))
  • 中部(ちゅうぶ): central region (of Japan) — proper geographic division
  • 内部(ないぶ): interior — inside, not necessarily central
