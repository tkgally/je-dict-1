覗(のぞ)き

のぞき
noun
peeping; voyeurism; peek
1. peeping (Tom); voyeurism (illegal act of secretly watching someone)
The act of secretly watching someone — typically through a window, gap, or small hole — especially in a sexual or invasive context. Used as a legal and news term for the crime of voyeurism, and also as a noun for the person who does this (a 'Peeping Tom').
覗(のぞ)きを働(はたら)く。
To commit an act of voyeurism.
覗(のぞ)きの被害(ひがい)にあった。
I was the victim of a Peeping Tom.
警察(けいさつ)はアパートの周辺(しゅうへん)で覗(のぞ)きを繰(く)り返(かえ)していた男(おとこ)を逮捕(たいほ)した。
The police arrested a man who had been repeatedly peeping around the apartment building.
2. a peek; a quick glance (neutral sense)
A brief or partial look at something through a small opening or from around a corner. Neutral usage, often appearing in compounds like 覗(のぞ)き穴(あな) ('peephole') or 覗(のぞ)き見(み) ('a peek').
ドアの覗(のぞ)き穴(あな)から外(そと)を見(み)た。
I looked outside through the peephole in the door.
子(こ)どもが塀(へい)の隙間(すきま)から覗(のぞ)きをしていた。
The child was peeking through a gap in the fence.
カーテンの隙間(すきま)からの覗(のぞ)きで、部屋(へや)の中(なか)の様子(ようす)が少(すこ)し見(み)えた。
By peeking through a gap in the curtain, I could see a little of what was happening inside the room.

The noun form of the verb 覗(のぞ)く ('to peek; to peer in'). Meaning shifts sharply depending on context: in crime/news contexts it refers to voyeurism, while in neutral contexts it simply means 'a peek' or appears in everyday compounds.

USAGE:
In isolation, especially with verbs like 働(はたら)く ('to engage in [a bad act]') or 被害(ひがい)にあう ('to be a victim of'), it almost always carries the voyeurism sense. In compounds such as 覗(のぞ)き穴(あな), 覗(のぞ)き見(み), or 覗(のぞ)き込(こ)む-related contexts, the neutral meaning is normal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 覗(のぞ)き穴(あな): peephole (in a door)
  • 覗(のぞ)き見(み): a peek; peeking (can be neutral or invasive)
  • 覗(のぞ)きを働(はたら)く: to commit voyeurism
  • 覗(のぞ)き魔(ま): a Peeping Tom (strong term)
  • 覗(のぞ)き行為(こうい): the act of voyeurism (legal/news term)
  • 覗(のぞ)きの被害(ひがい)にあう: to be a victim of a Peeping Tom

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 覗(のぞ)く: to peek; to peer into — the base verb
  • 盗(ぬす)み見(み): secretly looking at something; peeking at someone's private info (e.g. a screen, a letter)
  • 盗撮(とうさつ): secret filming/photography — a related but distinct crime
  • 覗(のぞ)き込(こ)む: to peer into; lean in to look (neutral, not a crime)

REGISTER:
The voyeurism sense is mildly taboo; news reports often prefer the more formal 覗(のぞ)き行為(こうい). The neutral 'peek' sense is everyday Japanese.
