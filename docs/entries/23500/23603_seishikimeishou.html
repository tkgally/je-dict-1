正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)

せいしきめいしょう
noun
official name; formal name; full official designation
1. official name; formal name; full official designation
The full, official name of an organization, law, product, or other entity, as opposed to a common abbreviation or nickname. Often used when introducing a new item or when clarifying what a commonly used short name actually stands for.
正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)は何(なん)ですか。
What is the official name?
「コンビニ」の正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)は「コンビニエンスストア」だ。
The official name of "konbini" is "convenience store."
書類(しょるい)には必(かなら)ず会社(かいしゃ)の正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please be sure to enter the official name of the company on the documents.

Composed of 正式(せいしき) (formal, official) and 名称(めいしょう) (name, designation). Used especially when contrasting a formal name with a nickname, abbreviation, or everyday term — a very common rhetorical move in Japanese quiz shows, trivia, and news explanations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)は〜: the official name is...
  • 正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)で呼(よ)ぶ: to call (something) by its official name
  • 正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)を記入(きにゅう)する: to write in the official name
  • 会社(かいしゃ)の正式名称(せいしきめいしょう): the official name of the company

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 略称(りゃくしょう): abbreviated name, short name — the opposite of 正式名称(せいしきめいしょう)
  • 通称(つうしょう): common name, popular name — what something is usually called in practice
  • 名称(めいしょう): name, designation (the general word, without the "official" nuance)
  • 本名(ほんみょう): real name — used for people, not organizations
