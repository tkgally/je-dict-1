基準金利(きじゅんきんり)

きじゅんきんり
noun
benchmark interest rate; reference rate
1. benchmark interest rate; reference rate
The reference interest rate used as a baseline for setting the rates on loans, mortgages, and other financial products. A bank's advertised loan rate is typically expressed as 'the benchmark rate plus/minus X percentage points'.
基準金利(きじゅんきんり)が上(あ)がった。
The benchmark interest rate has risen.
住宅(じゅうたく)ローンの金利(きんり)は基準金利(きじゅんきんり)によって決(き)まる。
Mortgage rates are determined by the benchmark interest rate.
日銀(にちぎん)が政策(せいさく)を変更(へんこう)したので、各(かく)銀行(ぎんこう)の基準金利(きじゅんきんり)も見直(みなお)された。
Because the Bank of Japan changed its policy, each bank's benchmark interest rate was also revised.
優良(ゆうりょう)顧客(こきゃく)には、基準金利(きじゅんきんり)から一定(いってい)の割引(わりびき)を適用(てきよう)して融資(ゆうし)を行(おこな)う。
For top-rated customers, loans are offered at a set discount below the benchmark interest rate.

Composed of 基準(きじゅん) (standard, basis) and 金利(きんり) (interest rate). A financial term referring to the reference rate that banks and other lenders publish and use as the baseline for pricing their loan products.

USAGE:
The headline rate that banks display for mortgages and business loans is typically the 基準金利(きじゅんきんり); the rate a customer actually pays is that rate adjusted by a discount (優遇(ゆうぐう)) based on creditworthiness and the type of loan. Changes in this rate ripple through to mortgages, consumer loans, and corporate borrowing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 基準金利(きじゅんきんり)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げる / 引(ひ)き下(さ)げる: to raise / lower the benchmark interest rate
  • 基準金利(きじゅんきんり)が上昇(じょうしょう)する / 下落(げらく)する: the benchmark rate rises / falls
  • 基準金利(きじゅんきんり)に連動(れんどう)する: to be linked to the benchmark interest rate
  • 店頭(てんとう)表示(ひょうじ)金利(きんり): the posted (over-the-counter) rate, essentially the same as 基準金利(きじゅんきんり) for retail banking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 政策(せいさく)金利(きんり): policy interest rate — the rate set by the central bank (日銀(にちぎん)), which drives the 基準金利(きじゅんきんり) of commercial banks
  • 優遇(ゆうぐう)金利(きんり): preferential interest rate — a discounted rate below the benchmark, offered to specific customers
  • 適用(てきよう)金利(きんり): applied interest rate — the rate actually applied to a given loan, after any adjustments
  • 市場(しじょう)金利(きんり): market interest rate — the rate determined by financial markets, which influences the benchmark
