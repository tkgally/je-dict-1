肩掛(かたか)け

かたかけ
noun
shoulder wrap; shawl; shoulder bag (item slung over the shoulder)
1. a shawl or wrap worn over the shoulders; (also) something designed to be slung over the shoulder, such as a shoulder bag
Originally a piece of cloth such as a shawl draped over the shoulders for warmth. Extended in compounds to mean 'worn over the shoulder,' as in 肩掛(かたか)けカバン ('shoulder bag').
肩掛(かたか)けを羽織(はお)る。
To throw on a shawl.
寒(さむ)いので肩掛(かたか)けを貸(か)してもらった。
It was cold, so I was lent a shawl.
彼女(かのじょ)は肩掛(かたか)けカバンを提(さ)げて出(で)かけた。
She went out carrying a shoulder bag.
祖母(そぼ)は編(あ)みかけの肩掛(かたか)けを、孫(まご)のために丁寧(ていねい)に仕上(しあ)げた。
Grandmother carefully finished the half-knitted shawl for her grandchild.

Nominalization of the verb 肩(かた)に掛(か)ける ('to hang on the shoulder'). Usable both as a standalone noun (a shawl) and as the first element of compounds meaning 'shoulder-worn' (肩掛(かたか)けカバン, 肩掛(かたか)けバッグ).

USAGE:
As a standalone noun, 肩掛(かたか)け refers to a shawl — a rectangular or wrap-style piece of cloth draped over the shoulders for warmth. The ショール (shawl) loanword has largely taken over in fashion contexts, so 肩掛(かたか)け is now somewhat old-fashioned when used alone. In compound form (肩掛(かたか)けカバン), it remains current.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肩掛(かたか)けを羽織(はお)る: to throw on a shawl
  • 肩掛(かたか)けを掛(か)ける: to put a shawl over one's shoulders
  • 肩掛(かたか)けカバン: shoulder bag
  • 肩掛(かたか)けバッグ: shoulder bag (using katakana loanword)
  • 毛織(けお)りの肩掛(かたか)け: a woolen shawl
  • 編(あ)みかけの肩掛(かたか)け: a half-knitted shawl

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ショール: shawl — loanword, more common in modern fashion vocabulary
  • 肩(かた)布(ぬの): shoulder cloth — rare, literal term
  • 膝掛(ひざか)け: lap blanket — same pattern for the lap
  • 首(くび)巻(ま)き: muffler; scarf — worn around the neck
  • マフラー: muffler; scarf — loanword
  • 肩(かた)紐(ひも): shoulder strap — the strap itself

REGISTER:
As a standalone noun for a shawl, 肩掛(かたか)け has a slightly old-fashioned, homely feel and is often associated with hand-knitted items. The compound 肩掛(かたか)けカバン remains a fully current word in everyday speech.
