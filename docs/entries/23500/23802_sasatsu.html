1. (official) inspection; on-site inspection; audit
A formal, on-site check conducted by an authority to verify compliance with laws, regulations, treaties, or internal rules. Typical contexts include tax audits by the National Tax Agency, nuclear non-proliferation inspections by the IAEA, workplace safety inspections, and internal-audit visits by a company headquarters to a branch. The nuance is of an authority coming in to look, not merely reviewing documents from afar.

An unannounced inspection uncovered one deficiency after another in the on-site safety management.

Compound of 査(さ) ('investigate; examine') and 察(さつ) ('observe; perceive'). Used as a verbal noun with する (査察(ささつ)する 'to inspect'), but the noun form with verbs like 入(はい)る, 行(おこな)う, 受(う)ける is far more common.

USAGE:

  • The inspecting side: 査察(ささつ)を行(おこな)う / 実施(じっし)する ('to carry out an inspection').
  • The inspected side: 査察(ささつ)を受(う)ける ('to be inspected'), 査察(ささつ)が入(はい)る ('to have inspectors come in' — idiomatic, often about tax audits).
  • Reserved for official, legally grounded inspections. For casual 'checking' use 点検(てんけん) ('routine check') or 検査(けんさ) ('examination'); for a quiet investigation use 調査(ちょうさ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 査察(ささつ)を受(う)ける: to be inspected
  • 査察(ささつ)が入(はい)る: for inspectors to arrive (often used of tax raids)
  • 査察(ささつ)を行(おこな)う: to conduct an inspection
  • 査察(ささつ)を実施(じっし)する: to carry out an inspection
  • 抜(ぬ)き打(う)ち査察(ささつ): unannounced inspection
  • 税務(ぜいむ)査察(ささつ): tax audit
  • 査察(ささつ)官(かん): inspector (official title)
  • 査察(ささつ)チーム: inspection team

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 検査(けんさ): examination; inspection — broader; includes medical tests, product quality checks, and routine inspections. 査察(ささつ) specifically implies a regulatory on-site visit.
  • 監査(かんさ): audit — typically financial/document review; can be on-site but the focus is books and records.
  • 点検(てんけん): routine check; inspection — self-inspection or maintenance check.
  • 調査(ちょうさ): investigation; survey — research-oriented, not enforcement-oriented.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In news headlines, 査察(ささつ) often evokes either 税務(ぜいむ)査察(ささつ) (tax raids on suspected tax evaders, famously dramatized in the film 'A Taxing Woman' / マルサの{女(おんな)|}) or IAEA nuclear inspections of North Korea and Iran.
