棋譜(きふ)

きふ
noun
game record (shogi, go, chess); score sheet of a board game
1. game record (of shogi, go, or chess); notation of moves
A written or digital record of the moves played in a game of shogi, go, or (less often) chess. Kept for later study and analysis, and a central part of how professional and amateur players learn. Published for major professional matches in magazines, newspapers, and online databases.
棋譜(きふ)を取(と)る。
To record a game.
プロの棋譜(きふ)を並(なら)べて勉強(べんきょう)する。
I study by playing through professional game records.
名人戦(めいじんせん)の棋譜(きふ)がオンラインで公開(こうかい)された。
The game record of the Meijin title match has been published online.

Compound of 棋(き) ('board game', especially shogi, go, and chess) and 譜(ふ) ('record; score; notation' — the same character used in 楽譜(がくふ), 'sheet music'). Both elements are literary kanji rarely used outside such compounds.

USAGE:

  • A specialized but standard term among shogi and go players; almost every serious player reads 棋譜(きふ).
  • The verbs used with 棋譜(きふ) reflect how records are handled:
  • 棋譜(きふ)を取(と)る: to record the moves as a game is played.
  • 棋譜(きふ)を並(なら)べる: to reproduce/play through a recorded game on a board.
  • 棋譜(きふ)を残(のこ)す: to leave a game record (for posterity).
  • Note the homophone 寄付(きふ) ('donation') — context and kanji distinguish them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 棋譜(きふ)を取(と)る: to record a game
  • 棋譜(きふ)を並(なら)べる: to play through a game record
  • 棋譜(きふ)を残(のこ)す: to leave a record of the game
  • プロの棋譜(きふ): professional game records
  • 棋譜(きふ)解析(かいせき): game record analysis

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽譜(がくふ): sheet music — uses the same 譜(ふ) element; 'score' in the musical sense.
  • 譜面(ふめん): score; notation — general term for written records of music; shares the 'notation' sense.
  • 記録(きろく): record — generic word for any record; 棋譜(きふ) is a specific kind of 記録(きろく).
  • 寄付(きふ): donation — a homophone with different kanji; easy to confuse in speech.
