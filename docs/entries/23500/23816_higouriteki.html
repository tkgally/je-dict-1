非合理的(ひごうりてき)

ひごうりてき
na-adjective
irrational; contrary to reason
1. irrational; contrary to reason; not conforming to rational principles
Describes behavior, beliefs, systems, or decisions that go against reason or rational judgment. Frequently used in economics, psychology, and philosophy to label actions that maximize neither efficiency nor logic. The direct antonym is 合理的(ごうりてき) ('rational; reasonable').
非合理的(ひごうりてき)な行動(こうどう)。
Irrational behavior.
人(ひと)はときに非合理的(ひごうりてき)な選択(せんたく)をする。
People sometimes make irrational choices.
この制度(せいど)は非合理的(ひごうりてき)なので、根本(こんぽん)から見直(みなお)すべきだ。
This system is irrational and should be fundamentally reviewed.

Formed with the negating prefix 非(ひ) ('non-; un-') attached to 合理的(ごうりてき) ('rational'). It points to a lack of reasoned grounds rather than to simple wrongness or illegality.

USAGE:

  • Used as a な-adjective: 非合理的(ひごうりてき)な制度(せいど) ('an irrational system').
  • Adverbially: 非合理的(ひごうりてき)に振(ふ)る舞(ま)う ('to act irrationally').
  • Common in academic and journalistic writing; in casual speech, おかしい} ('weird') or {意味(いみ)がない ('meaningless') may be more natural.
  • In behavioral economics, the phrase 人間(にんげん)の非合理性(ひごうりせい) ('human irrationality') is frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき)な行動(こうどう): irrational behavior
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき)な判断(はんだん): irrational judgment
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき)な制度(せいど): irrational system
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき)な信念(しんねん): irrational belief
  • 非合理的(ひごうりてき)に感(かん)じる: to feel unreasonable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 合理的(ごうりてき): rational; reasonable — the direct antonym.
  • 非論理的(ひろんりてき): illogical — focuses on reasoning that does not follow valid logical steps, rather than on overall unreasonableness.
  • 不合理(ふごうり): unreasonable; irrational — very close in meaning; 不合理(ふごうり) often functions as a noun or な-adjective stem and tends to be slightly more formal.
  • 理不尽(りふじん): unreasonable; unjust — emphasizes unfairness and treatment of others, not cognitive irrationality.
