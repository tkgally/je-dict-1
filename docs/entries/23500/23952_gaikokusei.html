外国製(がいこくせい)

がいこくせい
noun
foreign-made; made overseas; imported
1. foreign-made; made overseas; of foreign manufacture
Indicates that a product was manufactured outside of Japan. Used on labels, catalogs, and in conversation to contrast with 国産(こくさん) ('domestic / Japanese-made'). Often functions as a noun modifier ('foreign-made car', 'imported watch'), and can also take の to attach to another noun.
これは外国製(がいこくせい)の時計(とけい)です。
This is a foreign-made watch.
外国製(がいこくせい)の車(くるま)は部品(ぶひん)が手(て)に入(はい)りにくいこともある。
Parts for foreign-made cars can sometimes be hard to get.
外国製(がいこくせい)の家電(かでん)は、日本(にほん)の電圧(でんあつ)に対応(たいおう)していない場合(ばあい)があるので注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Foreign-made home appliances sometimes do not support Japanese voltage, so caution is needed.

Compound of 外国(がいこく) ('foreign country') and the suffix 〜製(せい) ('made in; manufactured by'), which combines productively with country names and other nouns to form origin-of-manufacture labels: 日本製(にほんせい) ('made in Japan'), 中国製(ちゅうごくせい) ('made in China'), 手(て)製(せい) ('handmade'), 木製(もくせい) ('wooden').

USAGE:

  • Most commonly attaches directly to a noun without の: 外国製(がいこくせい)家電(かでん), 外国製(がいこくせい)車(くるま).
  • The の form — 外国製(がいこくせい)の時計(とけい) — is also fully standard and slightly more explicit.
  • Can stand alone as a predicate: この{靴(くつ)は外国製(がいこくせい)だ}.
  • Not a positive or negative word in itself, but in advertising contexts it is often associated with premium or specialty goods, while 国産(こくさん) carries a 'trusted local' image.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の車(くるま): foreign-made car
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)家電(かでん): imported home appliances
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の部品(ぶひん): foreign-made parts
  • 外国製(がいこくせい)の時計(とけい): foreign-made watch

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国産(こくさん): domestically produced; Japan-made — the direct antonym.
  • 舶来(はくらい): imported from overseas — older, slightly literary word with a nostalgic, high-class nuance.
  • 輸入品(ゆにゅうひん): imported goods — focuses on the customs / trade side rather than on place of manufacture.
  • 海外製(かいがいせい): made overseas — near-synonym of 外国製(がいこくせい), with 海外(かいがい) sounding a touch softer than 外国(がいこく).
