1. drawing a figure; geometric construction; drafting (a diagram or plan)
The act of producing a geometric figure, diagram, technical drawing, or plan — typically following a set of rules or requirements. Most often encountered in (a) school mathematics, where it refers to classical ruler-and-compass constructions, and (b) engineering/architecture, where it refers to producing technical drawings. Used as a verbal noun with する: 作図(さくず)する ('to draw a figure; to draft').
作図(さくず)の問題(もんだい)。
A geometric construction problem.
コンパスと定規(じょうぎ)だけで正三角形(せいさんかっけい)を作図(さくず)する。
To construct an equilateral triangle with only a compass and straightedge.
設計(せっけい)図(ず)の作図(さくず)にはCADソフトが使用(しよう)される。
CAD software is used to draft design drawings.
中学校(ちゅうがっこう)の数学(すうがく)では、垂直(すいちょく)二等分線(にとうぶんせん)や角(かく)の二等分線(にとうぶんせん)の作図(さくず)方法(ほうほう)を学(まな)ぶ。
In junior high school mathematics, students learn how to construct perpendicular bisectors and angle bisectors.

Compound of 作(さく) ('make; create') and 図(ず) ('figure; diagram'). Literally 'making a figure'. Used both as a noun (作図(さくず)問題(もんだい) 'construction problem') and as a verbal noun with する.

USAGE:

  • School math sense: classical geometric constructions using only a straightedge and compass (定規(じょうぎ)とコンパス). Typical Japanese middle-school curriculum topics.
  • Engineering / drafting sense: producing technical drawings, often now on a computer with CAD. 作図(さくず)ソフト ('drafting software') is a common phrase.
  • Does not refer to drawing pictures or art; for that use 絵(え)を描(か)く or 描画(びょうが).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 作図(さくず)する: to draw a figure; to draft
  • 作図(さくず)問題(もんだい): construction problem (in math)
  • 作図(さくず)方法(ほうほう): method of construction
  • 作図(さくず)ツール: drawing/drafting tool
  • 作図(さくず)ソフト: drafting/CAD software
  • 設計(せっけい)図(ず)の作図(さくず): drafting of design drawings
  • コンパスと定規(じょうぎ)で作図(さくず)する: to construct with compass and straightedge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 製図(せいず): drafting; technical drawing — near-synonym emphasizing professional/engineering drawings. 作図(さくず) is broader and more common in math contexts.
  • 描画(びょうが): drawing (a picture) — artistic/graphical rendering.
  • 設計(せっけい): design — the full process of design, of which drafting is one part.
  • 作成(さくせい): creation; preparation — generic 'making' of documents; less specific than 作図(さくず).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 図形(ずけい): geometric figure
  • 定規(じょうぎ): straightedge; ruler
  • コンパス: (geometric) compass
  • 垂直(すいちょく)二等分線(にとうぶんせん): perpendicular bisector
  • CAD: computer-aided design
