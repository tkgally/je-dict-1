法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)

ほうてきせきにん
noun
legal responsibility; legal liability
1. legal responsibility; legal liability
Responsibility imposed by law — contrasted with moral or social responsibility. Used when discussing who can be held accountable under a legal system, typically in civil, criminal, or administrative contexts. Often appears in news, legal writing, and business discussion.
法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を問(と)う。
To hold someone legally liable.
この事故(じこ)について、会社(かいしゃ)は法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を負(お)う可能性(かのうせい)がある。
The company may bear legal responsibility for this accident.
道義的(どうぎてき)な責任(せきにん)はあるが、法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)までは問(と)えないとの見解(けんかい)が示(しめ)された。
The view was presented that while there is moral responsibility, legal responsibility cannot be pursued.
弁護士(べんごし)に相談(そうだん)したうえで、法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)の有無(うむ)と、損害賠償(そんがいばいしょう)の請求(せいきゅう)が可能(かのう)かどうかを検討(けんとう)することにした。
After consulting a lawyer, we decided to examine whether there is legal liability and whether claiming damages is possible.

Composed of 法的(ほうてき) (legal, of the law) and 責任(せきにん) (responsibility). Contrasts with 道義的(どうぎてき)責任(せきにん) (moral responsibility) and 社会的(しゃかいてき)責任(せきにん) (social responsibility).

USAGE:
Standard phrase in news reporting, legal texts, corporate statements, and everyday discussions of fault. The structure 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を負(お)う means "to bear legal responsibility," while 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を問(と)う means "to hold (someone) legally responsible" or "to pursue legal accountability."

A typical pattern of argument in Japanese public discourse is to distinguish moral/social responsibility from legal responsibility — a person or organization may be criticized on ethical grounds while still being cleared of legal liability, or vice versa.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を負(お)う: to bear legal responsibility
  • 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を問(と)う: to hold legally accountable; to pursue legal responsibility
  • 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を認(みと)める: to acknowledge legal responsibility
  • 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)を免(まぬか)れる: to escape legal responsibility
  • 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)の有無(うむ): the presence or absence of legal liability

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 責任(せきにん): responsibility — general
  • 道義的(どうぎてき)責任(せきにん): moral responsibility — contrasted with legal responsibility
  • 社会的(しゃかいてき)責任(せきにん): social responsibility
  • 刑事(けいじ)責任(せきにん): criminal responsibility — a narrower category within 法的(ほうてき)責任(せきにん)
  • 民事(みんじ)責任(せきにん): civil responsibility (liability) — another narrower category
