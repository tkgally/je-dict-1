混合肌(こんごうはだ)

こんごうはだ
noun
combination skin; mixed skin type
1. combination skin; a mixed skin type (oily in some areas, dry in others)
A skin type in which different parts of the face have different characteristics — most commonly oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and dry or normal on the cheeks. Standard category in Japanese skincare, alongside 脂性肌(しせいはだ), 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ), and 敏感肌(びんかんはだ).
彼女(かのじょ)は混合肌(こんごうはだ)だ。
She has combination skin.
混合肌(こんごうはだ)のスキンケアは難(むずか)しい。
Skincare for combination skin is tricky.
混合肌(こんごうはだ)の場合(ばあい)、Tゾーンには皮脂(ひし)対策(たいさく)の化粧水(けしょうすい)を、頬(ほお)には保湿力(ほしつりょく)の高(たか)いクリームを使(つか)い分(わ)けるとよい。
For combination skin, it's a good idea to use separate products — a sebum-control toner for the T-zone and a highly moisturizing cream for the cheeks.

Compound of 混合(こんごう) ('mixture; combination') and 肌(はだ) ('skin'). Widely used in cosmetics marketing and skincare columns to describe faces that have both oily and dry areas at the same time.

USAGE:

  • Japanese skincare routines often encourage 'zone-based' care for 混合肌(こんごうはだ), applying different products to different parts of the face.
  • Frequently appears in labels and recommendations: 混合肌(こんごうはだ)用(よう) ('for combination skin'), 混合肌(こんごうはだ)におすすめ ('recommended for combination skin').
  • Considered one of the most common skin types, especially in humid climates.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 混合肌(こんごうはだ)用(よう)の化粧水(けしょうすい): toner for combination skin
  • 混合肌(こんごうはだ)のスキンケア: skincare for combination skin
  • 混合肌(こんごうはだ)向(む)けの製品(せいひん): products aimed at combination skin
  • 混合肌(こんごうはだ)の悩(なや)み: concerns of those with combination skin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脂性肌(しせいはだ): oily skin — oily across the whole face.
  • 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ): dry skin — dry across the whole face.
  • 普通肌(ふつうはだ): normal skin — balanced, without obvious oily or dry areas.
  • 敏感肌(びんかんはだ): sensitive skin — skin that reacts easily to products, environment, or stress.
  • Tゾーン: T-zone — the forehead–nose–chin area, often the oily part of a 混合肌(こんごうはだ) face.
