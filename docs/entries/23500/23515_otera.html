お寺(てら)

おてら
noun
temple (Buddhist)
1. Buddhist temple
A Buddhist temple, used with the polite prefix お. The standard everyday form when referring to temples in conversation.
お寺(てら)に行(い)く。
To go to a temple.
京都(きょうと)にはお寺(てら)がたくさんある。
There are many temples in Kyoto.
家族(かぞく)でお寺(てら)にお参(まい)りした。
I visited a temple with my family.
あのお寺(てら)は桜(さくら)の名所(めいしょ)として有名(ゆうめい)だ。
That temple is famous as a cherry blossom viewing spot.

The polite form of 寺(てら), using the honorific prefix お. In everyday conversation, お寺(てら) is much more common than the plain form 寺(てら). The お prefix is not strictly honorific here — it has become a natural part of the word in spoken Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お寺(てら)に行(い)く: to go to a temple
  • お寺(てら)にお参(まい)りする: to visit a temple (for worship)
  • お寺(てら)を回(まわ)る: to visit several temples
  • お寺(てら)の鐘(かね): a temple bell

TEMPLE VS. SHRINE:

  • お寺(てら): Buddhist temple — features a 本堂(ほんどう) (main hall), 仏像(ぶつぞう) (Buddha statues), and often a 墓地(ぼち) (cemetery)
  • 神社(じんじゃ): Shinto shrine — features a 鳥居(とりい) (torii gate) and 狛犬(こまいぬ) (guardian lion-dogs)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寺院(じいん): temple — a more formal, written-style word for Buddhist temples
  • 寺(てら): temple — the plain form, used mainly in compounds and written contexts
