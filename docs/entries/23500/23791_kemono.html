獣(けもの)

けもの
noun
beast; (wild) animal; four-legged mammal
1. beast; wild animal; four-legged mammal
A wild, typically four-legged mammal — a deer, boar, bear, fox, and the like. Carries a wilder, more primitive connotation than the neutral 動物(どうぶつ) ('animal'), and is often used in figurative or literary contexts.
山(やま)の獣(けもの)。
Wild beasts of the mountains.
夜(よる)の森(もり)で獣(けもの)の声(こえ)が聞(き)こえた。
The cries of beasts could be heard in the nighttime forest.
この山道(やまみち)には獣(けもの)の足跡(あしあと)が点々(てんてん)と残(のこ)っている。
There are animal tracks scattered along this mountain path.
飢(う)えに追(お)い詰(つ)められた彼(かれ)は、まるで獣(けもの)のように食(た)べ物(もの)にかぶりついた。
Driven by hunger, he fell upon the food like a beast.

Native Japanese word, thought to derive from 毛(け)の物(もの) ('thing with fur'). Written with the kanji 獣(けもの), which also has the on-yomi reading じゅう (as in 獣医(じゅうい) 'veterinarian' or 野獣(やじゅう) 'wild beast').

USAGE:
Literary and evocative. Used in descriptions of wildlife (山(やま)の獣(けもの) = 'mountain beasts'), in metaphors about human behavior reverting to an animalistic state, and in classical and folkloric contexts. In everyday speech, 動物(どうぶつ) is the neutral word; 獣(けもの) is chosen for its earthy, wild nuance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 山(やま)の獣(けもの): mountain beasts
  • 獣(けもの)道(みち): animal trail (a track made by wild animals through the forest)
  • 獣(けもの)のように: like a beast
  • 獣(けもの)の足跡(あしあと): animal tracks
  • 獣(けもの)臭(くさ)い: smelling like an animal
  • 獣(けもの)の皮(かわ): the hide/pelt of a beast

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 動物(どうぶつ): animal — neutral, standard term; covers all animals including pets and sea life
  • 野獣(やじゅう): wild beast — more literary, emphasizes ferocity
  • 野生(やせい)動物(どうぶつ): wildlife; wild animal — modern, scientific term
  • 四足(よつあし)獣(じゅう): four-legged beast — specifically quadrupeds
  • 猛獣(もうじゅう): ferocious beast — used of tigers, lions, etc.

REGISTER:
Somewhat literary and old-fashioned in everyday registers, but standard in outdoor, folklore, hunting, and agricultural contexts, where 'wild animals causing trouble' is often expressed using 獣(けもの) or 獣害(じゅうがい) ('animal damage to crops').
