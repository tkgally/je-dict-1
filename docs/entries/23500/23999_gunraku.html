群落(ぐんらく)

ぐんらく
noun
community; stand; colony (of plants)
1. plant community; stand; colony (of plants)
In botany and ecology, a group of plants of the same or related species growing together in a specific location and forming a distinct natural grouping. Roughly equivalent to the English terms 'plant community' or 'stand', depending on context.
群落(ぐんらく)を形成(けいせい)する。
To form a community (of plants).
高山(こうざん)植物(しょくぶつ)の群落(ぐんらく)が広(ひろ)がっていた。
A community of alpine plants spread out before us.
この湿原(しつげん)には絶滅(ぜつめつ)危惧種(きぐしゅ)の群落(ぐんらく)が自然(しぜん)に残(のこ)っている。
In this wetland, a community of endangered species remains in its natural state.

Compound of 群(ぐん) ('group; flock') and 落(らく) (here meaning 'settlement; cluster', as in 集落(しゅうらく) 'village'). Primarily a botanical and ecological term: it refers to the coherent natural groupings of plants that grow together in a particular habitat, rather than a random scatter.

USAGE:

  • Common in nature writing, hiking guides, and environmental conservation contexts.
  • Often qualified by a species or habitat: ブナ群落(ぐんらく) ('beech stand'), ヨシ群落(ぐんらく) ('reed community'), 高山(こうざん)植物(しょくぶつ)群落(ぐんらく) ('alpine plant community').
  • Designated protected 群落(ぐんらく) may appear on signs at national parks and nature reserves.
  • The related compound 大(だい)群落(ぐんらく) emphasizes particularly large or dense communities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 群落(ぐんらく)を形成(けいせい)する: to form a community/stand
  • 大(だい)群落(ぐんらく): a large community/stand
  • ブナ群落(ぐんらく): beech forest stand
  • 天然(てんねん)記念物(きねんぶつ)の群落(ぐんらく): a designated natural monument plant community
  • 保護(ほご)対象(たいしょう)の群落(ぐんらく): a protected plant community

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 群生(ぐんせい): growing in groups — describes the behavior of plants growing together; overlaps with 群落(ぐんらく) but is used as a verbal noun (e.g., 群生(ぐんせい)する).
  • 群(む)れ: group; flock; herd — general word for any group of animals or people, not specifically plants.
  • 集落(しゅうらく): settlement; village; (of bacteria) colony — used for human settlements or microbial colonies, not plant stands.
  • コロニー: colony — loanword used for bacterial or biological colonies in scientific writing.
