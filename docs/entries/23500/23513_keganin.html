怪我人(けがにん)

けがにん
noun
injured person; casualty
1. injured person; casualty; the injured
A person who has been physically injured, whether from an accident, disaster, or other incident. Used in news reports, emergency contexts, and everyday conversation about accidents.
怪我人(けがにん)はいませんか。
Is anyone injured?
事故(じこ)で3人(にん)の怪我人(けがにん)が出(で)た。
Three people were injured in the accident.
怪我人(けがにん)は救急車(きゅうきゅうしゃ)で病院(びょういん)に運(はこ)ばれた。
The injured person was taken to the hospital by ambulance.
幸(さいわ)い怪我人(けがにん)は出(で)なかったが、建物(たてもの)の被害(ひがい)は大(おお)きかった。
Fortunately there were no injuries, but the damage to the building was extensive.

Composed of 怪我(けが) (injury) and 人(にん) (person). The 人(にん) suffix is read にん (not じん) in this compound. The word is standard in news reports and emergency situations to describe anyone who has been physically hurt.

The phrase 怪我人(けがにん)が出(で)る (injuries occurred; someone was hurt) is extremely common in news and accident reports. Its opposite — 怪我人(けがにん)は出(で)なかった (no one was injured) — is equally standard when reporting the fortunate outcome of an incident.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 怪我人(けがにん)が出(で)る: there are injuries / someone is injured
  • 怪我人(けがにん)は出(で)なかった: no one was injured
  • 怪我人(けがにん)を救助(きゅうじょ)する: to rescue the injured
  • 怪我人(けがにん)を運(はこ)ぶ: to transport the injured
  • 重傷(じゅうしょう)の怪我人(けがにん): a seriously injured person

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 負傷者(ふしょうしゃ): injured person — a more formal synonym, common in official reports and news
  • 被害者(ひがいしゃ): victim — broader term including injury, crime, or disaster victims
  • 死傷者(ししょうしゃ): casualties (dead and injured) — used when reporting both fatalities and injuries together
