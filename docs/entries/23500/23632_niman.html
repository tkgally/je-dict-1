1. twenty thousand; 20,000
The number 20,000, expressed as 二(に) (two) × 万(まん) (10,000) in the Japanese numbering system. A common round figure in everyday contexts — mid-range yen amounts, attendance figures, and so on.
二万(にまん)円(えん)。
Twenty thousand yen.

二万(にまん) is the number 20,000. In the Japanese number system, it is formed as 二(に) (2) × 万(まん) (10,000).

JAPANESE NUMBER SYSTEM:

Japanese groups large numbers in units of 10,000 (万(まん)), not 1,000 as in English. For twenties of thousands:

  • 一万(いちまん): 10,000
  • 二万(にまん): 20,000
  • 三万(さんまん): 30,000
  • 十万(じゅうまん): 100,000

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 二万(にまん)円(えん): twenty thousand yen (roughly the price of a mid-range jacket or a nice dinner for two)
  • 二万(にまん)人(にん): twenty thousand people
  • 二万(にまん)部(ぶ): twenty thousand copies (of a book, magazine)
  • 二万(にまん)円(えん)札(さつ): (colloquially) twenty-thousand-yen note — note: there is no such bill; the highest is 一万(いちまん)円(えん)

LEARNER TIP:
二万(にまん)円(えん) is roughly the threshold between "moderately expensive" and "noticeably expensive" in everyday Japanese consumer contexts (a nice meal, a mid-range clothing item, a budget-level appliance).
