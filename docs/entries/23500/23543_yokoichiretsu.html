横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)

よこいちれつ
noun
in a horizontal line; in a row side by side
1. horizontal line; single row; side by side in a line
A formation where people or things are arranged in one line from left to right (as opposed to a vertical column). Used for physical arrangements and figuratively for equal status.
横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)に並(なら)ぶ。
To line up in a horizontal row.
椅子(いす)を横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)に並(なら)べた。
I arranged the chairs in a single row.
子供(こども)たちが横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)に手(て)をつないで歩(ある)いている。
The children are walking in a line, holding hands side by side.

A compound of 横(よこ) (horizontal, side) and 一列(いちれつ) (single row/line). Describes a formation where people or objects are arranged in a single horizontal line, side by side.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)に並(なら)ぶ: to line up in a horizontal row
  • 横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)に並(なら)べる: to arrange in a horizontal row
  • 横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)になる: to form a horizontal line

FIGURATIVE USE:
Sometimes used to describe a situation where everyone is on equal footing with no one ahead or behind: 横(よこ)一列(いちれつ)のスタート (everyone starting on equal terms).

OPPOSITE:

  • 縦(たて)一列(いちれつ): in a vertical column / single file — arranged front to back

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一列(いちれつ): single line — without specifying horizontal or vertical orientation
  • 横並(よこなら)び: side by side; keeping pace with others — often figurative
