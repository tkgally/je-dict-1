1. to be driven into a corner; to be in a desperate situation; to run out of options
To reach a point where there is no more time or no way out, and one must act urgently. Describes deadlines, financial trouble, personal crises, and similar situations where all easier options have been exhausted.
切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まっている。
I'm in a real bind.
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)づいて切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まってきた。
With the deadline approaching, I'm getting desperate.
切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まると人間(にんげん)は思(おも)わぬ行動(こうどう)に出(で)るものだ。
When people are cornered, they often take unexpected actions.
生活(せいかつ)が切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まって、彼(かれ)はついに親(おや)に頭(あたま)を下(さ)げて金(かね)を借(か)りることになった。
Pressed to the wall financially, he finally swallowed his pride and borrowed money from his parents.

Originally a term from swordsmanship: 切羽(せっぱ) is the metal washer between the blade and the guard (鍔(つば)) of a Japanese sword. When it 'gets jammed' (詰(つ)まる), the sword cannot be drawn, leaving the wielder helpless — hence the figurative meaning of being cornered.

USAGE:

  • Almost always used in the progressive 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まっている or the past 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった, describing a current state rather than a one-time event.
  • The modifier form 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった is common: 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった状況(じょうきょう) (a desperate situation), 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった声(こえ) (a desperate voice).
  • Subjects include people, as well as abstract subjects like 生活(せいかつ) (life), 経営(けいえい) (business management), or 状況(じょうきょう) (situation).

ASPECT:
The て}いる form has the stative meaning 'be in a cornered state,' not the progressive meaning 'be becoming cornered.' To express the latter, use {切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まってくる or 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まりつつある.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive. The subject is the person or situation that is being cornered. The pressure or cause is normally expressed with a cause clause or ~で: 借金(しゃっきん)で切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まる (to be cornered by debt).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった状況(じょうきょう): a desperate situation
  • 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まった声(こえ)で: in a desperate voice
  • 切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まって助(たす)けを求(もと)める: to be cornered and ask for help
  • 生活(せいかつ)が切羽(せっぱ)詰(つ)まる: one's livelihood is in dire straits

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追(お)い詰(つ)められる: to be driven into a corner — more literal, often by an opponent or circumstance
  • 窮(きゅう)する: to be in distress — formal and literary
  • 困(こま)り果(は)てる: to be at wit's end — emphasizes emotional exhaustion
  • 進退(しんたい)窮(きゅう)まる: to have no way to advance or retreat — very formal, often written
