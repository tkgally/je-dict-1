電気工事(でんきこうじ)

でんきこうじ
noun
electrical work; electrical construction or installation
1. electrical work — construction, installation, or repair of electrical wiring and equipment
The trade and work of installing, maintaining, or repairing electrical systems in buildings, including wiring, outlets, lighting, and major appliances. In Japan, most 電気工事(でんきこうじ) must be performed by a licensed 電気工事士(でんきこうじし) ('licensed electrician').
あしたは電気工事(でんきこうじ)で停電(ていでん)になる。
Tomorrow the power will be off due to electrical work.
電気工事(でんきこうじ)は資格(しかく)がないとできない。
You can't do electrical work without a license.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)を買(か)ったので、リフォームのついでに配線(はいせん)の電気工事(でんきこうじ)もお願(ねが)いすることにした。
Since we bought an old house, we decided to have wiring work done as part of the renovation.

Compound of 電気(でんき) ('electricity') and 工事(こうじ) ('construction work'). A general term for any construction or installation task involving electrical systems.

USAGE:

  • Used both for major projects (wiring a new building) and small jobs (installing a new outlet or light fixture).
  • Because Japanese law restricts much electrical work to holders of the 電気工事士(でんきこうじし) license, signage and paperwork in this field frequently mention the word. Homeowners hire a 電気工事屋(でんきこうじや) ('electrical contractor') for such work.
  • Planned power outages due to building maintenance are commonly announced as 電気工事(でんきこうじ)のため停電(ていでん)いたします 'There will be a power outage due to electrical work'.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電気工事(でんきこうじ)を行(おこな)う: to carry out electrical work
  • 電気工事(でんきこうじ)を依頼(いらい)する: to request electrical work
  • 電気工事(でんきこうじ)のため停電(ていでん): power outage due to electrical work
  • 電気工事(でんきこうじ)会社(がいしゃ): electrical contractor company

RELATED TERMS:

  • 電気工事士(でんきこうじし): licensed electrician — the national qualification required to perform most electrical work.
  • 工事(こうじ): construction work — general term.
  • 配線(はいせん): wiring — the specific task of routing electrical cables.
  • 停電(ていでん): power outage — common consequence of 電気工事(でんきこうじ).
