風(かぜ)よけ

かぜよけ
noun
windbreak; wind shield; protection against the wind
1. windbreak; wind shield; something used to block or shelter from the wind
A fence, wall, row of trees, tarp, or other barrier placed to block wind. Also used of clothing or gear designed to protect the body from wind.
風(かぜ)よけの塀(へい)。
A windbreak wall.
テントの周(まわ)りに風(かぜ)よけを立(た)てた。
We set up a windbreak around the tent.
海岸(かいがん)の松林(まつばやし)は風(かぜ)よけの役目(やくめ)を果(は)たしている。
The pine grove along the coast serves as a windbreak.
このジャケットは軽(かる)くて、風(かぜ)よけにちょうどいい。
This jacket is light and just right as protection against the wind.

Compound of 風(かぜ) ('wind') and the nominalized verb stem of 避(よ)ける ('to avoid; to ward off'). The pattern noun + よけ} 'something to keep X away' is productive: {虫(むし)よけ ('insect repellent'), 日(ひ)よけ ('sunshade; awning'), 雨(あめ)よけ ('rain shelter').

USAGE:
Used for both permanent structures (walls, tree rows designed as windbreaks) and temporary items (tarps, camping gear). Can also describe clothing: 風(かぜ)よけのジャケット ('a windbreaker jacket'). Often appears in の-phrases modifying other nouns, e.g. 風(かぜ)よけの塀(へい) ('windbreak wall').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 風(かぜ)よけの塀(へい): windbreak wall
  • 風(かぜ)よけの林(はやし): windbreak forest / row of trees
  • 風(かぜ)よけを立(た)てる: to set up a windbreak
  • 風(かぜ)よけになる: to serve as a windbreak
  • 風(かぜ)よけの役目(やくめ)を果(は)たす: to function as a windbreak

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 防風林(ぼうふうりん): windbreak forest — formal term, specifically a forest planted to block wind
  • 防風(ぼうふう): wind protection — formal; 防風(ぼうふう)壁(へき) = 'windproof wall'
  • ウィンドブレーカー: windbreaker jacket
  • 日(ひ)よけ: sunshade; awning — same {よけ} pattern, for sun
  • 雨(あめ)よけ: rain shelter — same pattern, for rain
