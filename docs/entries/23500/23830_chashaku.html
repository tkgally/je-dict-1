茶杓(ちゃしゃく)

ちゃしゃく
noun
tea scoop; bamboo scoop for matcha
1. tea scoop; slender bamboo scoop used to measure matcha powder
A narrow, slightly curved utensil — usually carved from a single piece of bamboo — used in the Japanese tea ceremony to scoop powdered green tea (抹茶(まっちゃ)) from the caddy into the tea bowl. Although simple in appearance, 茶杓(ちゃしゃく) are valued as art objects, often given poetic names (銘(めい)) by their makers or owners.
茶杓(ちゃしゃく)で抹茶(まっちゃ)をすくう。
Scoop matcha with the tea scoop.
師匠(ししょう)から銘(めい)のついた茶杓(ちゃしゃく)を譲(ゆず)り受(う)けた。
I received a tea scoop with a poetic name from my teacher.
茶杓(ちゃしゃく)は一本(いっぽん)の竹(たけ)から削(けず)り出(だ)される小(ちい)さな道具(どうぐ)だが、その銘(めい)や作者(さくしゃ)によって高価(こうか)に取引(とりひき)されることもある。
A tea scoop is a small tool carved from a single piece of bamboo, but depending on its poetic name and maker it can sometimes be traded at a high price.

Compound of 茶(ちゃ) ('tea') and 杓(しゃく) ('ladle; scoop'). Historically, tea was measured with ivory or metal scoops; the bamboo 茶杓(ちゃしゃく) was popularized by 千(せん)利休(のりきゅう) and remains the standard utensil today.

USAGE:

  • A core tea-ceremony utensil, ranked among the 茶道具(ちゃどうぐ) alongside 茶筅(ちゃせん) ('tea whisk') and 茶碗(ちゃわん) ('tea bowl').
  • Each 茶杓(ちゃしゃく) is often given a 銘(めい), a poetic name chosen to match the season or theme of the gathering (茶会(ちゃかい)).
  • One scoop is traditionally taken as one serving of matcha; adjustments are made for strong tea (濃茶(こいちゃ)) vs. thin tea (薄茶(うすちゃ)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 茶杓(ちゃしゃく)で抹茶(まっちゃ)をすくう: to scoop matcha with the tea scoop
  • 茶杓(ちゃしゃく)の銘(めい): the poetic name of a tea scoop
  • 茶杓(ちゃしゃく)を削(けず)る: to carve a tea scoop
  • 竹(たけ)の茶杓(ちゃしゃく): a bamboo tea scoop
  • 利休(りきゅう)作(さく)の茶杓(ちゃしゃく): a tea scoop made by Rikyū

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 茶筅(ちゃせん): tea whisk — the companion bamboo utensil used to whisk matcha after it is scooped.
  • 棗(なつめ): tea caddy — the lacquered container that holds the powdered tea from which the 茶杓(ちゃしゃく) scoops.
  • 杓子(しゃくし): ladle — a general-purpose scoop or ladle, especially for rice or soup; related but much larger and not tea-specific.
  • {スプーン}: spoon — the everyday alternative for measuring tea in a non-ceremonial setting.
