猥褻(わいせつ)

わいせつ
adjective-na
obscene; indecent; lewd
1. obscene; indecent; lewd (especially in a legally or socially unacceptable way)
Describes sexual content, acts, or depictions considered a violation of public decency. In Japanese, 猥褻(わいせつ) is primarily a legal and news-media term: it appears in criminal-law names of offenses (強制(きょうせい)猥褻(わいせつ)罪(ざい) 'indecent assault', 猥褻(わいせつ)物頒布(ぶつはんぷ)罪(ざい) 'distribution of obscene materials') and in reports of arrests for indecent exposure, groping, or sexual molestation. Much more formal and more strongly negative than everyday words like エッチ or いやらしい.
猥褻(わいせつ)な表現(ひょうげん)。
Obscene expression.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は猥褻(わいせつ)行為(こうい)の疑(うたが)いで逮捕(たいほ)された。
The suspect was arrested on suspicion of indecent conduct.
この法律(ほうりつ)は猥褻(わいせつ)物(ぶつ)の頒布(はんぷ)を禁(きん)じている。
This law prohibits the distribution of obscene materials.
表現(ひょうげん)の自由(じゆう)と猥褻(わいせつ)の境界線(きょうかいせん)をどう引(ひ)くかは、古(ふる)くから議論(ぎろん)されてきた難(むずか)しい問題(もんだい)である。
Where to draw the line between freedom of expression and obscenity has long been a difficult issue of debate.

Compound of 猥(わい) ('disordered; lewd') and 褻(せつ) ('familiar; vulgar'). Both kanji are outside the general-use list (non-常用(じょうよう)); news media often writes the word in hiragana (わいせつ) as a result — わいせつ}{事件(じけん), わいせつ}{行為(こうい).

USAGE:

  • Used as a na-adjective: 猥褻(わいせつ)な画像(がぞう) ('obscene image'), 猥褻(わいせつ)な言葉(ことば) ('lewd language').
  • Used as a bare prefix in legal and news compounds: 猥褻(わいせつ)行為(こうい) ('indecent conduct'), 猥褻(わいせつ)物(ぶつ) ('obscene material'), 猥褻(わいせつ)事件(じけん) ('indecency case'), 強制(きょうせい)猥褻(わいせつ) ('indecent assault').
  • Much stronger and more formal than いやらしい ('lewd; creepy') or エッチ ('naughty'); carries the weight of moral and legal condemnation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 猥褻(わいせつ)行為(こうい): indecent conduct / indecent act (esp. in criminal context)
  • 猥褻(わいせつ)物(ぶつ): obscene material
  • 猥褻(わいせつ)画像(がぞう): obscene image / photograph
  • 猥褻(わいせつ)文書(ぶんしょ): obscene writing / publication
  • 強制(きょうせい)猥褻(わいせつ)罪(ざい): crime of indecent assault
  • 公然(こうぜん)猥褻(わいせつ)罪(ざい): crime of public indecency
  • 猥褻(わいせつ)容疑(ようぎ): suspicion of indecency

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いやらしい: lewd; creepy; nasty — everyday, subjective reaction; can apply to suggestive jokes or a 'creepy' manner, not limited to illegal conduct.
  • エッチ: naughty; sexual — colloquial and often light-hearted.
  • 淫(みだ)ら: lewd; indecent — literary, moral-judgment tone.
  • 卑(ひ)わい: obscene; vulgar — close synonym, often applied to language specifically.
  • 下品(げひん): vulgar; crude — broader; covers non-sexual bad taste as well.

LEGAL NOTE:
日本(にほん)の刑法(けいほう) (Japanese Penal Code) uses 猥褻(わいせつ) as a legal technical term. In modern statutes, some offense names have been revised (e.g. '強制(きょうせい)わいせつ罪(ざい)' was restructured into '不同意(ふどうい)わいせつ罪(ざい)' in the 2023 revision), but 猥褻(わいせつ)/わいせつ remains the core word.
