耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)

じびいんこうか
noun
ENT (ear, nose, and throat) department; otolaryngology
1. ENT (ear, nose, and throat) department; otolaryngology
The medical specialty and the corresponding department of a hospital or clinic that treats diseases of the ear, nose, throat, and adjoining areas of the head and neck. Patients visit it for problems such as ear infections, hearing loss, sinusitis, sore throat, hoarseness, dizziness, and hay fever.
耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)に行(い)ってきた。
I went to the ENT clinic.
花粉症(かふんしょう)がひどいので耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)を受診(じゅしん)した。
My hay fever was severe, so I saw an ENT specialist.
耳(みみ)の聞(き)こえが悪(わる)くなったら、早(はや)めに耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)で診(み)てもらうべきだ。
If your hearing gets worse, you should be examined at an ENT clinic as soon as possible.
耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)は耳(みみ)・鼻(はな)・のどだけでなく、めまいや首(くび)まわりの腫瘍(しゅよう)なども扱(あつか)う専門科(せんもんか)だ。
Otolaryngology is a specialty that handles not only the ears, nose, and throat but also dizziness and tumors around the neck area.

Composed of 耳(じ) (ear), 鼻(び) (nose), 咽喉(いんこう) (throat; pharynx and larynx), and 科(か) (department; specialty). The English equivalent ENT (ear-nose-throat) is structurally identical, just shorter.

USAGE:
The full official name of the medical specialty and the corresponding hospital department, used on signage, prescriptions, insurance cards, and referral letters. In casual conversation it is very often shortened to 耳鼻科(じびか) ('ear-nose department'), which patients find easier to say. The shortened form is informal but completely standard. Some clinics now use even broader names like 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)・頭頚部外科(とうけいぶげか) (otolaryngology and head & neck surgery) on their nameplates.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)に行(い)く: to go to the ENT clinic
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)を受診(じゅしん)する: to see an ENT doctor
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)医(い): ENT physician (otolaryngologist)
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)クリニック: ENT clinic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 耳鼻科(じびか): short, casual everyday form of the same word
  • 内科(ないか): internal medicine — a parallel medical department
  • 眼科(がんか): ophthalmology — another sense-organ specialty
  • 頭頚部外科(とうけいぶげか): head and neck surgery — a related, more surgical specialty often combined with ENT
