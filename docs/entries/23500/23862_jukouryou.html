受講料(じゅこうりょう)

じゅこうりょう
noun
course fee; tuition for a class or lecture
1. course fee; the fee paid to attend a class, lecture, seminar, or training program
The fee charged to attend a non-degree class, lecture series, seminar, workshop, or other training course. Distinct from university tuition (授業料(じゅぎょうりょう), 学費(がくひ)), which typically refers to fees for formal schooling. Common in the context of adult education, cram schools, online courses, and professional training.
受講料(じゅこうりょう)はいくらですか。
How much is the course fee?
受講料(じゅこうりょう)は当日(とうじつ)現金(げんきん)でお支払(しはら)いください。
Please pay the course fee in cash on the day.
オンライン講座(こうざ)の受講料(じゅこうりょう)が値上(ねあ)げされたので、申(もう)し込(こ)みを迷(まよ)っている。
The fee for the online course has been raised, so I'm hesitating about whether to sign up.

Compound of 受講(じゅこう) ('attending a course/lecture') and 料(りょう) ('fee'). Literally 'course-attendance fee'.

USAGE:

  • Most commonly seen in the context of cram schools (塾(じゅく)), cultural centers, professional training, language classes, and online learning platforms.
  • Often appears on brochures and websites in phrases like 受講料(じゅこうりょう)無料(むりょう) ('free of charge') and 受講料(じゅこうりょう)込(こ)み ('course fee included').
  • Payment verbs: pair with 払(はら)う ('pay'), 納(おさ)める ('pay, submit'), or the humble お支払(しはら)い{いただく} in formal/business writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)を払(はら)う: to pay the course fee
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)を納(おさ)める: to pay/submit the course fee (formal)
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)無料(むりょう): free course / no fee required
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)別(べつ): course fee charged separately
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)込(こ)み: course fee included
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう)の振(ふ)り込(こ)み: transfer of the course fee

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 授業料(じゅぎょうりょう): tuition — primarily used for schools and universities, where instruction takes place over a full term or year.
  • 学費(がくひ): school fees / educational expenses — broader term including tuition and associated costs at formal institutions.
  • 参加費(さんかひ): participation fee — used for events, conferences, and workshops rather than courses of study.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 受講(じゅこう): attending a course; taking a class.
  • 受講生(じゅこうせい): course participant; student of the class.
