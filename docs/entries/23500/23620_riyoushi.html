理容師(りようし)

りようし
noun
barber; licensed barber
1. barber; licensed barber
A person licensed to cut and style hair, shave faces, and groom beards — primarily for male clients. In Japan, 理容師(りようし) is a legally regulated profession distinct from 美容師(びようし) (beautician/hairdresser): only licensed 理容師(りようし) may perform a straight-razor shave with a lather.
父(ちち)は理容師(りようし)だ。
My father is a barber.
理容師(りようし)の免許(めんきょ)を取(と)った。
I got my barber's license.
この店(みせ)の理容師(りようし)さんは、昔風(むかしふう)の髭剃(ひげそ)りがとても上手(じょうず)だ。
The barber at this shop is really skilled at old-fashioned straight-razor shaves.

Composed of 理容(りよう) ("grooming, barbering") + 師(し) ("qualified practitioner"), the suffix used for many licensed professions like 医師(いし), 看護師(かんごし), and 美容師(びようし).

USAGE:

  • Traditionally a male-oriented profession, though both men and women work as 理容師(りようし) today.
  • In everyday speech, customers usually call the practitioner 床屋(とこや)さん (the old shop name) or simply the shop's staff; 理容師(りようし) itself is the formal, occupational label used on licenses, signs, and résumés.
  • Becoming a 理容師(りようし) in Japan requires graduating from an accredited training school and passing a national exam.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 理容師(りようし)になる: to become a barber
  • 理容師(りようし)の免許(めんきょ): barber's license
  • 理容師(りようし)試験(しけん): barber's qualifying exam
  • 理容師(りようし)会(かい): barbers' association
  • ベテランの理容師(りようし): a veteran barber

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 美容師(びようし): hairstylist, beautician — holds a separate license; focuses on cutting, perming, and coloring, mainly for female clients, and may not do razor shaves.
  • 床屋(とこや): barbershop / barber — the colloquial word for the shop itself, and often used for the person working there.
  • 散髪屋(さんぱつや): colloquial term for a barbershop.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 理容院(りよういん) / 理容室(りようしつ): barbershop (formal)
  • 髭剃(ひげそ)り: shaving
  • 散髪(さんぱつ): haircut
