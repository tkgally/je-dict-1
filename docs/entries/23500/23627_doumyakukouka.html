動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)

どうみゃくこうか
noun
arteriosclerosis; hardening of the arteries
1. arteriosclerosis; hardening of the arteries
A medical condition in which the walls of the arteries thicken, lose elasticity, and narrow due to the buildup of cholesterol, calcium, and other deposits. It is a major risk factor for heart attack, stroke, and other cardiovascular diseases, and is strongly linked to aging, high blood pressure, diabetes, and lifestyle factors.
動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)が進(すす)んでいる。
The arteriosclerosis is advancing.
塩分(えんぶん)の取(と)りすぎは動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)の原因(げんいん)になる。
Too much salt can cause arteriosclerosis.
運動不足(うんどうぶそく)や喫煙(きつえん)は動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)のリスクを高(たか)めるといわれている。
Lack of exercise and smoking are said to raise the risk of arteriosclerosis.

Composed of 動脈(どうみゃく) ("artery") + 硬化(こうか) ("hardening, stiffening"). Literally "artery hardening," which closely matches the English "arteriosclerosis" (from Greek arterio- "artery" + sklerosis "hardening").

USAGE:

  • A standard medical term used in both professional and popular health contexts — common in health check-up (健康診断(けんこうしんだん)) results, news articles on lifestyle diseases, and TV programs on senior health.
  • Often appears with verbs of progression like 進(すす)む (advance) or 悪化(あっか)する (worsen), since the condition develops gradually over years.
  • Frequently mentioned alongside related lifestyle diseases (生活習慣病(せいかつしゅうかんびょう)) like 高血圧(こうけつあつ) (hypertension) and 糖尿病(とうにょうびょう) (diabetes).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)が進(すす)む: arteriosclerosis progresses
  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)を引(ひ)き起(お)こす: to cause arteriosclerosis
  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)の予防(よぼう): prevention of arteriosclerosis
  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)の原因(げんいん): the cause of arteriosclerosis
  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)のリスク: the risk of arteriosclerosis
  • 動脈硬化(どうみゃくこうか)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent arteriosclerosis

RELATED TERMS:

  • 動脈(どうみゃく): artery
  • 静脈(じょうみゃく): vein
  • 高血圧(こうけつあつ): high blood pressure, hypertension
  • コレステロール(これすてろーる): cholesterol
  • 心筋梗塞(しんきんこうそく): myocardial infarction, heart attack
  • 脳梗塞(のうこうそく): cerebral infarction, stroke
  • 生活習慣病(せいかつしゅうかんびょう): lifestyle disease
