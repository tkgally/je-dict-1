先駆的(せんくてき)

せんくてき
na-adjective
pioneering; trailblazing
1. pioneering; groundbreaking; ahead of its time
Describes a person, work, idea, or initiative that leads the way in a field, doing something before others do. Usually carries positive evaluation: praises originality and vision. Common in reviews of art, research, technology, and social movements.
先駆的(せんくてき)な研究(けんきゅう)。
Pioneering research.
彼女(かのじょ)は女性(じょせい)作家(さっか)として先駆的(せんくてき)な存在(そんざい)だった。
She was a pioneering figure as a female writer.
この作品(さくひん)は、当時(とうじ)としては極(きわ)めて先駆的(せんくてき)な試(こころ)みだったと評価(ひょうか)されている。
This work is regarded as an extremely pioneering attempt for its time.

Compound of 先駆(せんく) ('pioneer; forerunner', literally 'riding ahead') and the adjectival suffix 〜的(てき) ('-ical'). Written-language feel; common in formal writing, reviews, and obituaries.

USAGE:

  • Used as a な-adjective: 先駆的(せんくてき)な試(こころ)み ('a pioneering attempt').
  • Often combined with 存在(そんざい) ('figure; presence'), 役割(やくわり) ('role'), 業績(ぎょうせき) ('achievements'), 作品(さくひん) ('work').
  • The related noun 先駆者(せんくしゃ) ('pioneer; trailblazer') is used for people.
  • Adverbial use 先駆的(せんくてき)に is less common than with many 〜的(てき) adjectives; prefer 先駆(せんく)けて ('ahead of') for the adverbial idea.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先駆的(せんくてき)な研究(けんきゅう): pioneering research
  • 先駆的(せんくてき)な役割(やくわり)を果(は)たす: to play a pioneering role
  • 先駆的(せんくてき)な試(こころ)み: pioneering attempt
  • 先駆的(せんくてき)な存在(そんざい): a pioneering figure
  • 先駆的(せんくてき)な業績(ぎょうせき): pioneering achievements

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 画期的(かっきてき): epoch-making; revolutionary — stresses a decisive break with the past rather than being the first.
  • 革新的(かくしんてき): innovative — emphasizes novelty of method or technology.
  • 先進的(せんしんてき): advanced; progressive — emphasizes being at the forefront of development rather than being first.
  • 独創的(どくそうてき): original; creative — emphasizes uniqueness of ideas, not necessarily being first.
