1. inhalation; breathing in
The act of breathing in — drawing air into the lungs. Used in medical, physiological, and exercise contexts. The opposite is 呼気(こき) ('exhalation').
深(ふか)く吸気(きゅうき)する。
To inhale deeply.
吸気(きゅうき)と呼気(こき)のバランスが大切(たいせつ)だ。
The balance between inhalation and exhalation is important.
運動中(うんどうちゅう)は鼻(はな)からの吸気(きゅうき)を意識(いしき)するとよい。
During exercise, it is good to be mindful of breathing in through the nose.
2. intake air; air intake
Air drawn into an engine, ventilation system, or other mechanical device. Common in automotive and HVAC contexts, often combined with 口(こう) or 弁(べん) to name specific parts.
エンジンの吸気口(きゅうきこう)を掃除(そうじ)した。
I cleaned the engine's air intake.
吸気(きゅうき)フィルターが詰(つ)まっている。
The air intake filter is clogged.
この車(くるま)は吸気(きゅうき)システムを改良(かいりょう)して燃費(ねんぴ)を向上(こうじょう)させた。
This car improved fuel efficiency by enhancing its air intake system.

Compound of 吸(きゅう) ('to inhale; to suck in') and 気(き) ('air; breath'). Works as both a biological/medical term and a mechanical engineering term.

USAGE:

  • In medicine and physiology, refers to the inhalation phase of breathing; contrasted with 呼気(こき) ('exhalation').
  • In engineering, refers to air drawn into an engine or ventilation system; compounds freely: 吸気口(きゅうきこう) ('air intake port'), 吸気弁(きゅうきべん) ('intake valve'), 吸気管(きゅうきかん) ('intake pipe').
  • Sounds technical or clinical — for everyday 'breathing in' in casual speech, 息(いき)を吸(す)う is more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 吸気(きゅうき)する: to inhale
  • 吸気(きゅうき)と呼気(こき): inhalation and exhalation
  • 吸気口(きゅうきこう): air intake (port)
  • 吸気(きゅうき)フィルター: air intake filter
  • 吸気(きゅうき)システム: intake system

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 呼気(こき): exhalation; breath going out — the direct opposite.
  • 息(いき)を吸(す)う: to breathe in — the everyday, non-technical phrasing.
  • 通気(つうき): ventilation — emphasizes airflow through a space rather than air being drawn in.
  • 排気(はいき): exhaust — the counterpart in engine contexts, referring to air being pushed out.
