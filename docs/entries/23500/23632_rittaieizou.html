立体映像(りったいえいぞう)

りったいえいぞう
noun
3D image; stereoscopic image; three-dimensional video
1. 3D image; stereoscopic image; three-dimensional video/visual
A visual image or moving image that appears to have depth — that is, one that gives the viewer an impression of three-dimensional space. Includes stereoscopic images viewed with 3D glasses, holograms, and volumetric displays, as well as 3D video content in general.
立体映像(りったいえいぞう)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy 3D images.
専用(せんよう)のメガネをかけると立体映像(りったいえいぞう)が見(み)える。
When you put on the special glasses, you can see the 3D image.
最新(さいしん)の技術(ぎじゅつ)で、メガネなしでも立体映像(りったいえいぞう)が見(み)られるディスプレイが開発(かいはつ)された。
With the latest technology, a display that allows you to see 3D images without glasses has been developed.
医療(いりょう)の現場(げんば)では、CTやMRIのデータから作(つく)られた立体映像(りったいえいぞう)を使(つか)って、手術(しゅじゅつ)の計画(けいかく)を立(た)てることが増(ふ)えている。
In medical practice, 3D images constructed from CT and MRI data are increasingly being used to plan surgeries.

Composed of 立体(りったい) (solid body, three-dimensional object) and 映像(えいぞう) (image, video, visual). The word covers both still images with a 3D effect and moving 3D video.

USAGE:

立体映像(りったいえいぞう) is a technical/descriptive term that appears most often in:

  • consumer electronics advertising (3D TVs, VR headsets, glasses-free 3D displays)
  • medicine and science (3D visualization from scans, molecular models)
  • entertainment (3D films, theme-park attractions)

In casual conversation, many Japanese speakers just say 3D (pronounced スリーディー) — for example, 3D 映画(えいが) (a 3D movie). 立体映像(りったいえいぞう) is the more formal or technical term used in writing and in careful descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立体映像(りったいえいぞう)を見(み)る: to view a 3D image
  • 立体映像(りったいえいぞう)を楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy 3D images/video
  • 立体映像(りったいえいぞう)技術(ぎじゅつ): 3D imaging technology
  • 立体映像(りったいえいぞう)ディスプレイ: 3D display

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 3D 映像(えいぞう): 3D image/video — everyday word, same meaning
  • 立体視(りったいし): stereoscopic vision, stereopsis — the perceptual/technical term
  • ホログラム: hologram — a specific kind of 3D image made with interference patterns
  • VR 映像(えいぞう): VR video — virtual-reality content, a related but distinct category

RELATED TERMS:

  • 立体(りったい): solid, three-dimensional object
  • 映像(えいぞう): image, video, visual
  • 平面(へいめん): flat, two-dimensional — opposite direction
