売(う)り手(て)

うりて
noun
seller; vendor; the selling side (of a transaction)
1. seller; vendor; the selling side
The party that sells in a transaction — a person, company, or side of the market offering goods, services, or assets for sale. Contrasts with 買(か)い手(て) (buyer). Common in business, real estate, stock market, and labor market contexts.
今(いま)は売(う)り手(て)市場(しじょう)だ。
Right now it's a seller's market.
売(う)り手(て)と買(か)い手(て)の条件(じょうけん)が合(あ)わない。
The seller's and buyer's terms don't match.
不動産(ふどうさん)取引(とりひき)では、売(う)り手(て)が物件(ぶっけん)の欠陥(けっかん)を説明(せつめい)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
In a real-estate transaction, the seller has a duty to disclose any defects in the property.

Composed of the verb stem 売(う)る ("to sell") + 手(て) ("hand; doer, party"). The 〜手(て) suffix forms agent nouns meaning "the one who does 〜" — compare 買(か)い手(て) (buyer), 聞(き)き手(て) (listener), 書(か)き手(て) (writer).

USAGE:

  • Refers to the selling side of a transaction in business, real estate, stocks, or the labor market. Often used in contrast with 買(か)い手(て).
  • Widely used in fixed expressions about market conditions, especially 売(う)り手(て)市場(しじょう) (seller's market — when supply is scarce and sellers have the advantage) and its opposite 買(か)い手(て)市場(しじょう) (buyer's market).
  • In the labor market, 売(う)り手(て)市場(しじょう) describes a situation favorable to job-seekers, when companies are competing to hire.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売(う)り手(て)市場(しじょう): seller's market
  • 売(う)り手(て)と買(か)い手(て): seller and buyer
  • 売(う)り手(て)側(がわ): the seller's side
  • 売(う)り手(て)の責任(せきにん): the seller's responsibility
  • 売(う)り手(て)が強(つよ)い: the seller has the upper hand

ANTONYM:

  • 買(か)い手(て): buyer — the purchasing party in a transaction.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 販売者(はんばいしゃ): seller, vendor — more formal/legal; common on contracts and product labeling.
  • 出品者(しゅっぴんしゃ): seller — specifically in auctions or online marketplaces.
  • 売主(うりぬし): seller/vendor — formal term used especially in real estate and legal documents.
