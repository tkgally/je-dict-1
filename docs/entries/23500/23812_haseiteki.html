派生的(はせいてき)

はせいてき
na-adjective
derivative; derived; secondary
1. derivative; derived (from something else); secondary
Describes something that arises or is produced from a more fundamental thing, rather than existing in its own right. Used of concepts, meanings, products, effects, and problems that branch off from a primary source. Often contrasts with 本来(ほんらい)の ('original; intrinsic') or 主(おも)な ('main; primary').
派生的(はせいてき)な意味(いみ)。
A derived meaning.
この問題(もんだい)は派生的(はせいてき)に発生(はっせい)した。
This problem arose as a by-product.
主(おも)な商品(しょうひん)のほかに、派生的(はせいてき)な商品(しょうひん)もいくつか販売(はんばい)している。
In addition to the main product, we also sell several spin-off products.

Compound of 派生(はせい) ('derivation; arising from') and the adjectival suffix 〜的(てき), yielding 'of the nature of a derivation'. Frequent in academic, technical, legal, and business writing.

USAGE:

  • Very commonly used in its adverbial form 派生的(はせいてき)に ('as a derivative; as a by-product').
  • Often paired with words like 問題(もんだい) ('problem'), 効果(こうか) ('effect'), 意味(いみ) ('meaning'), 商品(しょうひん) ('product').
  • Typically contrasts with 本来的(ほんらいてき) ('intrinsic; essential') or 主要(しゅよう)な ('primary').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 派生的(はせいてき)な意味(いみ): derived meaning
  • 派生的(はせいてき)な問題(もんだい): secondary problem; knock-on issue
  • 派生的(はせいてき)な効果(こうか): derivative effect; spillover effect
  • 派生的(はせいてき)に生(しょう)じる: to arise as a derivative
  • 派生的(はせいてき)な商品(しょうひん): spin-off product

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 副次的(ふくじてき): secondary; incidental — emphasizes being a side effect rather than directly derived.
  • 二次的(にじてき): secondary — stresses being of second order or not primary.
  • 間接的(かんせつてき): indirect — focuses on lack of direct connection rather than on origin.
  • 本来的(ほんらいてき): intrinsic; original — the common antonym.
