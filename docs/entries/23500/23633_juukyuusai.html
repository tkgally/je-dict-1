十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)

じゅうきゅうさい
noun
nineteen years old; age 19
1. nineteen years old; age 19
The age of nineteen. In Japan, this is the final year before legal adulthood (成人(せいじん)) at age 20, though the legal age of majority for most civil-law purposes was lowered to 18 in 2022. Nineteen-year-olds are usually first- or second-year university students, or young workers in their second post–high-school year.
息子(むすこ)は十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)です。
My son is nineteen years old.
十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)で大学(だいがく)に入(はい)った。
I entered university at nineteen.
十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)の誕生日(たんじょうび)に家族(かぞく)でレストランに行(い)った。
For my nineteenth birthday, we went to a restaurant as a family.
十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)の時(とき)、初(はじ)めて一人(ひとり)で海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)に行(い)って、世界(せかい)が広(ひろ)がった気(き)がした。
At nineteen, I traveled abroad alone for the first time, and it felt like my world had widened.

Composed of 十九(じゅうきゅう) (nineteen) and the age counter 〜歳(さい) (years of age).

READING NOTES:
Note the special reading 十九(じゅうきゅう) — in isolation, 19 can also be read じゅうく}, but for the age compound {十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい), じゅうきゅう} is the standard reading. The {歳(さい) counter can also be written 才(さい) in informal contexts.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In Japan, traditional coming-of-age age is 20, celebrated at 成人式(せいじんしき) (the Coming-of-Age Ceremony). Since the 2022 civil-code change lowering the age of majority to 18, nineteen-year-olds are adults for most legal purposes, but drinking and smoking remain restricted until 20. In many families, the nineteenth birthday still feels like the last year of "being a kid."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)の誕生日(たんじょうび): nineteenth birthday
  • 十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)の時(とき): when I was nineteen
  • 十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)で: at nineteen (at the age of)
  • 十九歳(じゅうきゅうさい)の若者(わかもの): a young person of nineteen

RELATED AGES:

  • 十八歳(じゅうはっさい): eighteen years old — now the legal age of adulthood in Japan
  • 二十歳(はたち): twenty years old — special non-compositional reading; the traditional coming-of-age
  • 二十一歳(にじゅういっさい): twenty-one years old
