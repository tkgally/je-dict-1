人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)

じんぶつびょうしゃ
noun
characterization; character description; portrayal of a person
1. characterization; depiction of a character; portrayal of a person
The way a person is portrayed in a novel, film, drama, or other work — covering appearance, personality, background, and behavior. Standard in literary criticism, film reviews, and writing guides; used to praise a work for its vivid portrayal of characters or to criticize it as flat or shallow.
人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)が巧(たく)みだ。
The characterization is skillful.
この小説(しょうせつ)は人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)が細(こま)やかだ。
This novel has delicate, nuanced characterization.
脚本家(きゃくほんか)は人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)を深(ふか)めるために役者(やくしゃ)と話(はな)し合(あ)った。
The screenwriter discussed the script with the actors in order to deepen the characterization.

Compound of 人物(じんぶつ) ('a person; character') and 描写(びょうしゃ) ('description; depiction; portrayal'). A standard term in literary, theater, and film criticism.

USAGE:

  • Typically appears as the subject of evaluative predicates like 巧(たく)みだ ('skillful'), 細(こま)やかだ ('nuanced'), 深(ふか)い ('deep'), 薄(うす)い ('thin; shallow'), 平板(へいばん)だ ('flat').
  • Used by critics, reviewers, and creative writing teachers; also by fans discussing novels, manga, anime, and dramas.
  • Not usually used for non-fiction profiles — those would be 人物(じんぶつ)紹介(しょうかい) ('profile; introduction of a person').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)が巧(たく)みだ: the characterization is skillful
  • 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)が細(こま)やか: nuanced, detailed characterization
  • 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)が薄(うす)い: the characterization is thin/shallow
  • 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ)を深(ふか)める: to deepen the characterization
  • リアルな人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ): realistic characterization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 情景(じょうけい)描写(びょうしゃ): description of a scene or setting — the counterpart dealing with scenery rather than people.
  • 心理(しんり)描写(びょうしゃ): psychological description — focuses on a character's inner thoughts and feelings; a subset of 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ).
  • キャラクター造形(ぞうけい): character design; character construction — used especially in manga, anime, and game contexts, with a production-side nuance.
  • 人物像(じんぶつぞう): character profile; image of a person — the resulting picture of the character rather than the act of depicting.

Related Words

Contrast: 心理(しんり)描写(びょうしゃ) (psychological description)
