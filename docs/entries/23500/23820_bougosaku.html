防護柵(ぼうごさく)

ぼうごさく
noun
protective fence; guardrail; safety barrier
1. protective fence; guardrail; safety barrier
A fence or barrier installed to protect people, vehicles, or property from danger. The term covers a wide range of structures, from steel guardrails along roads ({ガードレール}) to fencing around construction sites, power substations, train platforms, animal enclosures, and restricted areas. The key nuance is purpose: the barrier exists to prevent harm or intrusion, not merely to mark a boundary.
防護柵(ぼうごさく)を設置(せっち)する。
Install a safety barrier.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)の周(まわ)りに防護柵(ぼうごさく)が張(は)られている。
Protective fencing has been put up around the construction site.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)の防護柵(ぼうごさく)は、事故(じこ)の際(さい)に車両(しゃりょう)の飛(と)び出(だ)しを防(ふせ)ぐ役割(やくわり)を果(は)たす。
Guardrails on expressways play the role of preventing vehicles from flying off the road in the event of an accident.

Compound of 防護(ぼうご) ('protection; safeguarding') and 柵(さく) ('fence; palisade'). The noun 防護(ぼうご) appears in many safety-related compounds: 防護服(ぼうごふく) ('protective clothing; hazmat suit'), 防護(ぼうご)マスク ('protective mask'), 防護(ぼうご)壁(かべ) ('protective wall').

USAGE:

  • Often used in traffic, construction, and industrial contexts.
  • Common verbs: を設置(せっち)する ('install'), を設(もう)ける ('provide'), を撤去(てっきょ)する ('remove'), を越(こ)える ('go over').
  • For roadside guardrails specifically, the loanword ガードレール} is also widely used and sometimes more natural in everyday speech; {防護柵(ぼうごさく) has a more formal, official tone and covers a broader range of structures.
  • Municipal and transportation documents distinguish specific types like 横断(おうだん)防止(ぼうし)柵(さく) ('pedestrian-crossing-prevention fence') and 転落(てんらく)防止(ぼうし)柵(さく) ('fall-prevention fence').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 防護柵(ぼうごさく)を設置(せっち)する: to install a protective fence
  • 防護柵(ぼうごさく)を設(もう)ける: to put up a safety barrier
  • 防護柵(ぼうごさく)を越(こ)える: to climb over the barrier
  • 道路(どうろ)の防護柵(ぼうごさく): roadside guardrail
  • 工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)の防護柵(ぼうごさく): construction-site fencing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • {ガードレール}: guardrail — loanword, typically limited to roadside guardrails; more colloquial.
  • 柵(さく): fence — the bare noun, without the 'protective' nuance; can simply delimit space.
  • 塀(へい): wall; fence — typically a solid wall around a property, not designed for safety.
  • 防護(ぼうご)壁(かべ): protective wall — a solid wall rather than an open fence.
