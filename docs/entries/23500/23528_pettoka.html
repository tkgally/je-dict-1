ペット可(か)

ぺっとか
noun
pets allowed; pet-friendly
1. pets allowed; pet-friendly; pet-OK
A label used in housing listings, hotels, and other facilities to indicate that pets are permitted. Very commonly seen in real estate advertisements and apartment search websites.
ペット可(か)の物件(ぶっけん)を探(さが)す。
To look for a pet-friendly property.
このマンションはペット可(か)です。
This apartment building allows pets.
ペット可(か)のホテルを予約(よやく)した。
I booked a pet-friendly hotel.

A common real estate and hospitality term combining ペット (pet) and 可(か) (permitted/allowed). Frequently appears as a filter option on apartment search websites and in property listings. Note that even in ペット可(か) buildings, there are often restrictions on pet size, number, or species.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ペット可(か)の物件(ぶっけん): pet-friendly property
  • ペット可(か)のマンション: pet-friendly apartment building
  • ペット可(か)のホテル: pet-friendly hotel
  • ペット可(か)の賃貸(ちんたい): pet-friendly rental

OPPOSITE:

  • ペット不可(ふか): no pets allowed — the standard restriction in most Japanese apartments
