転覆(てんぷく)

てんぷく
noun
capsizing; overturning; overthrow (of a government)
1. capsizing; overturning
A vessel, vehicle, or large object turning over or upside down — most commonly used of boats capsizing at sea, but also of trains derailing on their side and of cars or carriages overturning in an accident.
船(ふね)が転覆(てんぷく)した。
The boat capsized.
列車(れっしゃ)転覆(てんぷく)事故(じこ)が起(お)きた。
A train derailment-and-overturning accident occurred.
強風(きょうふう)と高波(たかなみ)により、漁船(ぎょせん)が転覆(てんぷく)し、乗組員(のりくみいん)が海(うみ)に投(な)げ出(だ)された。
Strong winds and high waves caused the fishing boat to capsize, and the crew were thrown into the sea.
2. overthrow; toppling (of a government or regime)
The toppling or overturning of a government, regime, or established order through force, insurrection, or subversion. Used in news, history, and legal writing about coups, rebellions, and state-security cases.
政府(せいふ)の転覆(てんぷく)を企(くわだ)てた。
They plotted to overthrow the government.
革命(かくめい)により王政(おうせい)が転覆(てんぷく)した。
The monarchy was overthrown by the revolution.
国家(こっか)転覆(てんぷく)を目的(もくてき)とする組織(そしき)の活動(かつどう)は、法律(ほうりつ)で厳(きび)しく禁(きん)じられている。
The activities of organizations aiming at the overthrow of the state are strictly prohibited by law.

Composed of 転(てん) ("roll, turn over") + 覆(ぷく) ("overturn, cover"). Both characters carry the sense of "turning upside down," reinforcing each other; the word is used for both physical overturning (sense 1) and the metaphorical toppling of a regime (sense 2).

USAGE:

  • Functions as a する-verb (転覆(てんぷく)する) in both senses. Also widely used as a plain noun, especially in news-style compound nouns: 転覆(てんぷく)事故(じこ) (overturning accident), 転覆(てんぷく)事件(じけん) (overthrow incident).
  • In sense 1, the typical subject is a vehicle or vessel; the verb is intransitive — 船(ふね)が転覆(てんぷく)する (the boat capsizes). The transitive counterpart uses 転覆(てんぷく)させる.
  • In sense 2, common targets are 政府(せいふ) (government), 政権(せいけん) (administration), 国家(こっか) (state), 王政(おうせい) (monarchy), and 体制(たいせい) (regime).
  • The set phrase 国家(こっか)転覆(てんぷく) (overthrow of the state) is standard in legal and political discussion.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive: something capsizes / is overthrown — 船(ふね)が転覆(てんぷく)する.
  • Transitive (using the causative): 転覆(てんぷく)させる — to capsize / overthrow something — 政府(せいふ)を転覆(てんぷく)させる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (sense 1):

  • 船(ふね)が転覆(てんぷく)する: a boat capsizes
  • 転覆(てんぷく)事故(じこ): capsizing / overturning accident
  • 列車(れっしゃ)転覆(てんぷく): train derailment and overturning
  • 転覆(てんぷく)現場(げんば): the scene of the accident

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (sense 2):

  • 政府(せいふ)の転覆(てんぷく): overthrow of the government
  • 政権(せいけん)転覆(てんぷく): regime change (by force)
  • 国家(こっか)転覆(てんぷく): overthrow of the state
  • 転覆(てんぷく)を企(くわだ)てる: to plot an overthrow
  • 転覆(てんぷく)罪(ざい): crime of state-overthrow

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 横転(おうてん): rolling onto the side — similar to sense 1, but specifically describes a vehicle ending up on its side rather than fully upside down.
  • 打倒(だとう): overthrowing, defeating — emphasizes "bringing down by force"; often used as a slogan (政府(せいふ)打倒(だとう)!).
  • 崩壊(ほうかい): collapse — a regime falling apart, not necessarily overthrown by someone.
  • クーデター(くーでたー): coup d'état — the specific means by which a regime might be 転覆(てんぷく)させられる.
