1. coma; deep unconscious state from which a person cannot be awakened
A medical term for the most severe level of impaired consciousness: the patient does not open their eyes, cannot speak, and does not respond even to strong painful stimuli. Distinct from ordinary sleep or fainting. Frequently appears in medical reports and news about serious illness, injury, or overdose. Used as a verbal noun with する (昏睡(こんすい)する 'to fall into a coma'), but the phrase 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい) ('comatose state') is by far the most common form.
昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)に陥(おちい)る。
To fall into a coma.
患者(かんじゃ)は数日間(すうじつかん)昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)が続(つづ)いている。
The patient has been in a coma for several days.
事故(じこ)の衝撃(しょうげき)で昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)となったが、一週間(いっしゅうかん)後(ご)に意識(いしき)を取(と)り戻(もど)した。
She fell into a coma from the shock of the accident, but regained consciousness a week later.
睡眠薬(すいみんやく)を大量(たいりょう)に服用(ふくよう)した男性(だんせい)が昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)で発見(はっけん)され、病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)された。
A man who had taken a large dose of sleeping pills was found in a comatose state and rushed to the hospital.

Compound of 昏(こん) ('darkness; dim') and 睡(すい) ('sleep'). Literally 'dark sleep'. A medical Sino-Japanese term; the everyday synonym-like phrase for 'pass out' is 意識(いしき)を失(うしな)う ('to lose consciousness'), which is broader.

USAGE:

  • The most natural form is the set phrase 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい) ('comatose state').
  • Typical verbs: 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)に陥(おちい)る / になる ('fall into a coma'), 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)が続(つづ)く ('remain comatose'), 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)から目覚(めざ)める / 脱(だっ)する ('emerge from a coma').
  • 昏睡(こんすい)する is grammatical but much less frequent than the 状態(じょうたい) phrase.
  • Also appears in the criminal-law term 昏睡(こんすい)強盗(ごうとう) ('knockout robbery' — drugging a victim to rob them).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい): comatose state
  • 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)に陥(おちい)る: to fall into a coma
  • 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)が続(つづ)く: to remain in a coma
  • 昏睡(こんすい)状態(じょうたい)から目覚(めざ)める: to wake from a coma
  • 深(ふか)い昏睡(こんすい): deep coma
  • 昏睡(こんすい)強盗(ごうとう): knockout robbery (legal term)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 意識(いしき)不明(ふめい): unconsciousness — broader news-style term, common in press reports.
  • 気絶(きぜつ): fainting; passing out — sudden, typically brief.
  • 失神(しっしん): fainting; syncope — medical term for transient loss of consciousness.
  • 植物(しょくぶつ)状態(じょうたい): persistent vegetative state — chronic and severe, different clinical category.

MEDICAL NOTE:
In Japanese medical settings, level of consciousness is commonly graded with the Japan Coma Scale (JCS); the Glasgow Coma Scale is also used. 昏睡(こんすい) corresponds to the most severe grades in both systems.
