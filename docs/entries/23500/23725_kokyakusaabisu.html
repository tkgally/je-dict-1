顧客(こきゃく)サービス

こきゃくさーびす
noun
customer service
1. customer service
The service provided to customers by a company — both the department or staff dedicated to handling customer inquiries and complaints, and the broader concept of how a company treats its customers before, during, and after a purchase.
顧客(こきゃく)サービスを強化(きょうか)する。
To strengthen customer service.
この会社(かいしゃ)は顧客(こきゃく)サービスの質(しつ)が高(たか)い。
This company has high-quality customer service.
問(と)い合(あ)わせがあったら、顧客(こきゃく)サービス部門(ぶもん)にご連絡(れんらく)ください。
If you have any inquiries, please contact the customer service department.
競合(きょうごう)他社(たしゃ)との差別化(さべつか)を図(はか)るためには、商品(しょうひん)力(りょく)だけでなく顧客(こきゃく)サービスの充実(じゅうじつ)も重要(じゅうよう)だ。
In order to differentiate ourselves from competitors, strengthening customer service is just as important as product quality.

Composed of 顧客(こきゃく) (customer, client) and サービス (service). A formal, business-register term for what is called 'customer service' in English. The corresponding department within a company is often called 顧客(こきゃく)サービス部(ぶ) or 顧客(こきゃく)サービス部門(ぶもん).

USAGE:

Refers both to:

  • **The service itself**: how a company handles inquiries, complaints, and after-sales support.
  • **The department**: the staff or unit responsible for these activities.

In casual speech or shop-floor contexts, speakers often use 接客(せっきゃく) (serving / attending to customers in person) or the shorter お客様(きゃくさま)サービス instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顧客(こきゃく)サービスを提供(ていきょう)する: to provide customer service
  • 顧客(こきゃく)サービスを向上(こうじょう)させる / 強化(きょうか)する: to improve / strengthen customer service
  • 顧客(こきゃく)サービスの質(しつ): the quality of customer service
  • 顧客(こきゃく)サービス部(ぶ): customer service department
  • 顧客(こきゃく)サービスセンター: customer service center

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お客様(きゃくさま)サービス: customer service — a more customer-facing label commonly used on websites and signage; softer register
  • 接客(せっきゃく): serving customers; customer-facing service — the in-person interaction side (waitstaff, shop clerks); not used for phone or email support
  • {カスタマーサービス}: customer service — direct katakana equivalent; common in IT and e-commerce
  • アフターサービス: after-sales service — service provided after the sale (repairs, support); a subset of 顧客(こきゃく)サービス
  • 顧客(こきゃく)対応(たいおう): customer handling / response — the process of responding to a particular customer or inquiry
