共和国(きょうわこく)

きょうわこく
noun
republic
1. republic
A state governed as a republic — a country in which power is held by the people or their elected representatives rather than by a monarch. The word is the standard rendering of "Republic" in the official names of foreign countries (e.g., フランス共和国(ふらんすきょうわこく), French Republic).
フランスは共和国(きょうわこく)だ。
France is a republic.
大統領(だいとうりょう)は共和国(きょうわこく)の元首(げんしゅ)である。
The president is the head of state of the republic.
正式(せいしき)な国名(こくめい)は「大韓民国(だいかんみんこく)」だが、「韓国(かんこく)」は共和国(きょうわこく)の略称(りゃくしょう)としてよく使(つか)われる。
The official name is "the Republic of Korea," but the abbreviated form "Korea" is commonly used to refer to the republic.

Composed of 共和(きょうわ) ("republican, joint harmony") + 国(こく) ("country"). 共和(きょうわ) was coined in the 19th century as a Japanese/Chinese translation of the Western political concept "republic," drawing on a classical Chinese phrase referring to joint rule.

USAGE:

  • Appears most often as a suffix on the formal names of foreign nations that officially call themselves republics: 中華人民共和国(ちゅうかじんみんきょうわこく) (People's Republic of China), 大韓民国(だいかんみんこく) (Republic of Korea), ドイツ連邦共和国(どいつれんぽうきょうわこく) (Federal Republic of Germany).
  • Also used as a standalone noun to contrast with 王国(おうこく) (kingdom) or 君主国(くんしゅこく) (monarchy) in political and historical discussions.
  • In everyday conversation, the short-form country names (中国(ちゅうごく), 韓国(かんこく), ドイツ(どいつ)) are far more common than the full 〜共和国(きょうわこく) forms, which appear mainly in formal, official, or news contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 共和国(きょうわこく)を建設(けんせつ)する / 樹立(じゅりつ)する: to establish a republic
  • 共和国(きょうわこく)の大統領(だいとうりょう): the president of the republic
  • 共和国(きょうわこく)政府(せいふ): the government of the republic
  • 人民共和国(じんみんきょうわこく): people's republic
  • 連邦共和国(れんぽうきょうわこく): federal republic
  • イスラム(いすらむ)共和国(きょうわこく): Islamic republic

ANTONYM / CONTRAST:

  • 王国(おうこく): kingdom — a country ruled by a monarch.
  • 君主国(くんしゅこく): monarchy — any state headed by a sovereign, whether king, emperor, or sultan.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 共和制(きょうわせい): republican system (of government)
  • 共和主義(きょうわしゅぎ): republicanism
  • 大統領(だいとうりょう): president
  • 国家(こっか): state, nation
