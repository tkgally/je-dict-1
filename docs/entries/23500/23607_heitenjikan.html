閉店時間(へいてんじかん)

へいてんじかん
noun
closing time (of a shop or restaurant)
1. closing time; the time at which a shop, restaurant, or other business closes for the day
The time at which a retail shop, restaurant, or similar business stops operating for the day. Posted on shop signs and websites, and commonly discussed when planning visits.
閉店時間(へいてんじかん)は何時(なんじ)ですか。
What time do you close?
閉店時間(へいてんじかん)が近(ちか)づいたので急(いそ)いで会計(かいけい)を済(す)ませた。
Closing time was approaching, so I hurried to pay the bill.
このレストランは平日(へいじつ)と週末(しゅうまつ)で閉店時間(へいてんじかん)が違(ちが)うので注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please note that this restaurant's closing time is different on weekdays and weekends.

Composed of 閉店(へいてん) (closing of a shop) and 時間(じかん) (time). Used for the closing time of retail establishments — shops, restaurants, cafes, banks, and similar businesses that have daily opening and closing hours.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 閉店時間(へいてんじかん)が近(ちか)づく: closing time approaches
  • 閉店時間(へいてんじかん)間際(まぎわ): right before closing time
  • 閉店時間(へいてんじかん)を過(す)ぎる: to go past closing time
  • 閉店時間(へいてんじかん)を延長(えんちょう)する: to extend closing time
  • 閉店時間(へいてんじかん)は午後(ごご)9時(じ): the closing time is 9 PM

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開店時間(かいてんじかん): opening time — the antonym
  • 営業時間(えいぎょうじかん): business hours — covers the whole range from opening to closing
  • 閉館(へいかん)時間(じかん): closing time of a museum, library, or other public facility (not a shop)
  • 終業(しゅうぎょう)時間(じかん): end-of-work time — for offices/workplaces, not for shops serving customers
