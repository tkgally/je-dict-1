厄除(やくよ)け

やくよけ
noun
warding off misfortune; protective charm against bad luck
1. warding off misfortune; protection against bad luck; (good-luck) charm
The act of warding off misfortune, calamity, or bad luck through prayer, ritual, or a protective charm — a central concept in Japanese folk belief and at Shinto shrines and Buddhist temples. Also used as an attributive noun for items or rituals that serve this purpose, such as 厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)り (a protective amulet).
厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)りを買(か)った。
I bought a protective amulet to ward off bad luck.
神社(じんじゃ)で厄除(やくよ)けのお祓(はら)いを受(う)けた。
I received a purification ritual for warding off misfortune at the shrine.
厄年(やくどし)には厄除(やくよ)けのために寺(てら)や神社(じんじゃ)にお参(まい)りする人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
During one's unlucky year, many people visit temples and shrines to ward off misfortune.
母(はは)は毎年(まいとし)初詣(はつもうで)で厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)りを新(あたら)しくして、古(ふる)いものは神社(じんじゃ)に返(かえ)すようにしている。
Every year at the New Year's shrine visit, my mother replaces her protective amulet with a new one and returns the old one to the shrine.

Composed of 厄(やく) (misfortune, calamity) and 除(よ)け (warding off, keeping away — from 除(よ)ける). 厄除(やくよ)け refers both to the abstract idea of warding off bad luck and, attributively, to items or rituals that do so.

CULTURAL NOTE:
厄除(やくよ)け is closely tied to the concept of 厄年(やくどし) — "unlucky years" at certain ages (traditionally 25, 42, and 61 for men, 19, 33, and 37 for women, counted in 数(かぞ)え年(どし)). During one's 厄年(やくどし), it is customary to visit a shrine or temple for a 厄除(やくよ)けのお祓(はら)い (purification ritual) or a 厄除(やくよ)け祈祷(きとう) (prayer). Protective amulets called 厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)り are sold at shrines and temples year-round but are especially popular at New Year's.

USAGE:
Often used attributively before another noun: 厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)り (protective amulet), 厄除(やくよ)け祈願(きがん) (prayer for protection), 厄除(やくよ)けの神社(じんじゃ) (shrine famous for warding off misfortune). Can also be used as a standalone noun: 厄除(やくよ)けに行(い)く (to go for a misfortune-warding ritual).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)り: amulet for warding off bad luck
  • 厄除(やくよ)け祈願(きがん): prayer for protection from misfortune
  • 厄除(やくよ)けに行(い)く: to go (to a shrine/temple) for a protection ritual
  • 厄除(やくよ)けの神社(じんじゃ) / お寺(てら): shrine / temple known for warding off misfortune

RELATED TERMS:

  • 厄年(やくどし): unlucky year — a traditionally inauspicious year of one's life
  • 厄払(やくばら)い: exorcism of bad luck — a purification ritual, close in meaning
  • お祓(はら)い: Shinto purification ritual
  • お守(まも)り: (protective) amulet — general term
  • 祈祷(きとう): prayer, invocation (at a shrine or temple)
