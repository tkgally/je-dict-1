音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)

おんきょうせつび
noun
sound equipment; audio system; PA system
1. sound equipment; audio system; PA system
The collection of equipment — speakers, amplifiers, microphones, mixers, and related devices — installed in a venue to handle sound production and amplification. Used for concerts, theaters, conference rooms, wedding halls, schools, and public facilities.
音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)を整(ととの)える。
To set up the sound equipment.
このホールは音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)が充実(じゅうじつ)している。
This hall has well-equipped sound facilities.
イベントの前(まえ)に、音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)の動作(どうさ)確認(かくにん)を行(おこな)った。
Before the event, we checked the operation of the sound equipment.
新(あたら)しく建(た)てられたコンサートホールは、最新(さいしん)の音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)を備(そな)えている。
The newly built concert hall is equipped with the latest sound equipment.

Formed from 音響(おんきょう) (sound, acoustics) + 設備(せつび) (facilities, equipment). Refers to installed or semi-permanent equipment, as opposed to individual devices.

USAGE:

  • Typically used when describing a venue's facilities: concert halls, theaters, churches, conference rooms, school auditoriums.
  • In event planning and rental listings, 音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび) is commonly listed alongside lighting (照明(しょうめい)設備(せつび)) and stage (舞台(ぶたい)設備(せつび)) equipment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)を整(ととの)える: to set up / prepare the sound equipment
  • 音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)が充実(じゅうじつ)している: the sound equipment is well-stocked/high quality
  • 音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)を備(そな)える: to be equipped with sound equipment
  • 音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび)の点検(てんけん): inspection of sound equipment
  • 最新(さいしん)の音響(おんきょう)設備(せつび): state-of-the-art sound equipment

RELATED TERMS:

  • 音響(おんきょう): acoustics, sound — the broader concept of sound
  • オーディオ: audio — often for home or personal systems, rather than installed equipment
  • PA (ピーエー): public address system — a subset of sound equipment focused on voice amplification
  • 照明(しょうめい)設備(せつび): lighting equipment — commonly paired with sound equipment in venue listings
  • 音響(おんきょう)効果(こうか): sound effects / acoustics — the effect produced rather than the equipment itself
