1. direct proportion; proportional relationship
A mathematical relationship in which one quantity increases (or decreases) in the same ratio as another — if one doubles, the other doubles. Contrasted with 反比例(はんぴれい) (inverse proportion). Also used in everyday Japanese to describe any situation where two things rise and fall together.
正比例(せいひれい)の関係(かんけい)。
A relationship of direct proportion.
努力(どりょく)と成果(せいか)は正比例(せいひれい)するとは限(かぎ)らない。
Effort and results are not always directly proportional.
距離(きょり)は時間(じかん)に正比例(せいひれい)して増(ふ)えていく。
The distance increases in direct proportion to the time.
給料(きゅうりょう)は働(はたら)いた時間(じかん)に正比例(せいひれい)するので、残業(ざんぎょう)すればその分(ぶん)増(ふ)える。
The salary is directly proportional to the hours worked, so it increases by the amount of overtime.

Composed of 正(せい) (direct, positive, correct) and 比例(ひれい) (proportion). In mathematics, two quantities x and y are in 正比例(せいひれい) when y = kx for some constant k — as x increases, y increases by the same ratio.

Beyond math class, the word appears in everyday discussion of cause-and-effect relationships, especially when people want to say that one thing rises and falls with another.

USAGE:

  • As a noun: 正比例(せいひれい)の関係(かんけい) (a directly proportional relationship)
  • As a suru-verb: AはBに正比例(せいひれい)する (A is directly proportional to B)
  • Often used with に (the standard of comparison is marked by に)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正比例(せいひれい)する: to be directly proportional
  • 正比例(せいひれい)関係(かんけい): proportional relationship
  • Aに正比例(せいひれい)してBが増(ふ)える: B increases in direct proportion to A
  • 正比例(せいひれい)のグラフ: a graph of direct proportion (a straight line through the origin)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反比例(はんぴれい): inverse proportion — the direct antonym; as one increases, the other decreases proportionally
  • 比例(ひれい): proportion (general) — often used when the direction (direct or inverse) is clear from context
  • 連動(れんどう): moving together, linked movement — less mathematical; describes things that change together without specifying a ratio
