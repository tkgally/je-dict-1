1. passbook update; printing transactions in a bankbook
Having recent deposits, withdrawals, and the running balance printed into a bank passbook. Typically done at an ATM, which has a dedicated slot for feeding in the passbook.
ATMで通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)をする。
To update the passbook at an ATM.
久(ひさ)しぶりに通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)したら記録(きろく)がたくさん出(で)てきた。
When I updated my passbook after a long time, a lot of entries came out at once.
通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)は窓口(まどぐち)ではなく専用(せんよう)のATMで無料(むりょう)でできます。
Passbook updates can be done free of charge at dedicated ATMs, not at the counter.

Compound of 通帳(つうちょう) (passbook) and 記入(きにゅう) (filling in, entering). The label appears on Japanese ATM menus and is a routine banking task. When too many transactions have accumulated while the passbook was not updated, the ATM may print a summary line instead of every transaction.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)をする: to update the passbook
  • 通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)を済(す)ませる: to finish updating the passbook
  • 通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう)機(き): dedicated passbook-update machine
  • 未記帳(みきちょう)分(ぶん)の通帳記入(つうちょうきにゅう): updating with not-yet-printed transactions

RELATED TERMS:

  • 通帳(つうちょう): passbook — the booklet being updated
  • 記帳(きちょう): bookkeeping; making an entry in a book — a near-synonym that also appears on ATM menus as 記帳(きちょう)
  • 預金通帳(よきんつうちょう): bank passbook — the more formal full form
