途中(とちゅう)から

とちゅうから
expression
from partway through; from the middle (of)
1. from partway through; starting from the middle; from partway along
A set phrase meaning 'starting from some point partway through' something — an event, a story, a route, a process, or a period. Combines the noun 途中(とちゅう) (on the way; partway) with the starting-point particle から (from).
途中(とちゅう)から雨(あめ)になった。
It started raining partway through.
映画(えいが)は途中(とちゅう)から面白(おもしろ)くなった。
The movie got interesting partway through.
話(はなし)を途中(とちゅう)から聞(き)いたので、意味(いみ)がよく分(わ)からなかった。
I started listening to the story partway through, so I couldn't really follow it.
会議(かいぎ)に遅(おく)れて、途中(とちゅう)から参加(さんか)することになった。
I was late to the meeting, so I ended up joining partway through.

A common fixed expression meaning 'from partway through (something)'. Formed by attaching the starting-point particle から to 途中(とちゅう) (on the way, midway). Used for anything with a temporal or spatial progression — stories, events, journeys, processes.

USAGE:
When speakers want to indicate that some change or action began at a point after the start of something rather than at the beginning. Frequently used with change-of-state verbs (雨(あめ)になる, 面白(おもしろ)くなる) and with verbs of joining or watching (参加(さんか)する, 見(み)る, 聞(き)く).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 途中(とちゅう)から参加(さんか)する: to join partway through
  • 途中(とちゅう)から見(み)る / 聞(き)く: to start watching / listening partway through
  • 途中(とちゅう)から雨(あめ)になる: to start raining partway through
  • 途中(とちゅう)から道(みち)が狭(せま)くなる: the road gets narrower partway along
  • 途中(とちゅう)から話(はなし)が変(か)わる: the topic shifts partway through

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 途中(とちゅう)で: on the way; in the middle (of doing) — marks a point within the duration; contrasts with から, which marks a starting point. 'I dropped by partway there' would be 途中(とちゅう)で寄(よ)る, not 途中(とちゅう)から寄(よ)る.
  • 途中(とちゅう)まで: up to partway; as far as partway — marks an ending point rather than a starting point
  • 最初(さいしょ)から: from the beginning — the natural antonym
  • 始(はじ)めから: from the start — near-synonym of 最初(さいしょ)から
