1. refueling; fuel supply; act of supplying fuel to a vehicle, aircraft, or ship
Supplying fuel — gasoline, jet fuel, bunker oil, and so on — to a vehicle or vessel. Common in aviation, shipping, military, and road-transport contexts. The word can also be used metaphorically for refilling other "reserves."
飛行機(ひこうき)は燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)のため着陸(ちゃくりく)した。
The plane landed to refuel.
艦船(かんせん)への燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)は海上(かいじょう)でも可能(かのう)だ。
Refueling ships is possible even at sea.
長距離(ちょうきょり)トラックの運転手(うんてんしゅ)は、決(き)まったサービスエリアで燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)を行(おこな)う。
Long-haul truck drivers refuel at designated service areas.

Compound of 燃料(ねんりょう) (fuel) and 補給(ほきゅう) (replenishment, supply). It behaves as a noun and often takes する to form a verb (燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)する, "to refuel"). Register is neutral-formal and the word is standard in news, military reporting, and technical writing; in casual speech speakers are more likely to say 給油(きゅうゆ) for cars at a gas station.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)する: to refuel
  • 燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)を行(おこな)う: to carry out refueling
  • 空中(くうちゅう)燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう): in-flight refueling
  • 燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)基地(きち): refueling base
  • 燃料補給(ねんりょうほきゅう)機(き): refueling aircraft (tanker plane)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 給油(きゅうゆ): refueling (with oil or gasoline) — the everyday word for filling up a car
  • 補給(ほきゅう): replenishment, resupply — general term, not limited to fuel
  • ガソリンを入(い)れる: to put in gas — the most casual conversational phrasing
  • 満(まん)タン: full tank — casual; what you ask for at a gas station
