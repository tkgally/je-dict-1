1. a drop in water level; decrease in water volume (of a river, reservoir, lake, etc.)
A fall or reduction in the water level or stored volume of a body of water. Used of rivers, reservoirs (ダム}), lakes, and ponds, typically during droughts or dry seasons. Often appears in news about water shortages alongside {渇水(かっすい) ('water shortage') and 節水(せっすい) ('water conservation'). The antonym is 増水(ぞうすい) ('rising water').
ダムが減水(げんすい)している。
The reservoir water level is dropping.
長(なが)い日(ひ)照(で)りで川(かわ)の水量(すいりょう)が減水(げんすい)した。
After a long dry spell, the river's water volume dropped.
梅雨(つゆ)の雨(あめ)が少(すく)なかったため、夏(なつ)に入(はい)ってから関東(かんとう)の主要(しゅよう)ダム}で{減水(げんすい)が進(すす)んでいる。
Because rainfall during the rainy season was scarce, water levels in major reservoirs in the Kantō region have been dropping since the start of summer.

Compound of 減(げん) ('decrease; reduction') and 水(すい) ('water'). Both characters are active in many related compounds: 減少(げんしょう) ('decrease'), 減(へ)る ('to decrease'), 増水(ぞうすい) ('rise in water level'), 洪水(こうずい) ('flood').

USAGE:

  • Functions as a noun and also as a する-verb: 減水(げんすい)する ('to decrease in water level / volume').
  • Common subjects are bodies of water: 川(かわ) ('river'), ダム ('reservoir'), 湖(みずうみ) ('lake'), 貯水池(ちょすいち) ('reservoir').
  • Frequent in weather reports, environmental news, and agricultural bulletins during dry seasons.
  • Also used in the fisheries industry: 減水期(げんすいき) ('low-water season').
  • Does not usually describe the water in a cup or container — for that use 減(へ)る or 少(すく)なくなる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ダム}の{減水(げんすい): drop in reservoir level
  • 川(かわ)が減水(げんすい)する: the river's water level falls
  • 減水(げんすい)が進(すす)む: the water level keeps falling
  • 減水(げんすい)傾向(けいこう): a trend of falling water levels
  • 深刻(しんこく)な減水(げんすい): serious drop in water level

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 増水(ぞうすい): rise in water level — the direct antonym.
  • 渇水(かっすい): water shortage — focuses on the resulting scarcity for human use rather than on the physical drop in level.
  • 干上(ひあ)がる: to dry up — describes completely drying out; more extreme than 減水(げんすい).
  • 水位(すいい)が下(さ)がる: the water level drops — a more descriptive phrasing that covers the same phenomenon in everyday speech.
