仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)

しだしべんとう
noun
catered boxed meal; delivered bento
1. a bento box delivered from a caterer or restaurant — typically ordered in bulk for meetings, funerals, film shoots, or other events rather than individual consumption
Refers to bento meals prepared by a catering service and delivered to the customer, rather than bento bought at a store or made at home. Usually ordered by offices, event organizers, or families for gatherings, and often more elaborate than convenience-store bento.
会議(かいぎ)に仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)を頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered catered bento for the meeting.
昼食(ちゅうしょく)は仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)が用意(ようい)されています。
Catered bento will be provided for lunch.
葬儀(そうぎ)の後(あと)、参列者(さんれつしゃ)に仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)が配(くば)られた。
After the funeral, catered bento were handed out to the attendees.
近所(きんじょ)の老舗(しにせ)に仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)を三十(さんじゅう)個(こ)注文(ちゅうもん)しておいた。
I went ahead and ordered thirty catered bento from the long-established shop nearby.

Composed of 仕出(しだ)し ("catering; preparing and delivering food") + 弁当(べんとう) ("bento, boxed lunch"). Refers to bento that comes from a catering service rather than something homemade or bought off the shelf.

USAGE:

  • Typical occasions: business meetings (会議(かいぎ)), funerals and memorial services (葬儀(そうぎ), 法事(ほうじ)), film shoots, family gatherings, and sporting events.
  • Usually ordered in bulk; quality and price vary from simple boxed meals to elaborate multi-tiered presentations.
  • Common verbs: 頼(たの)む, 注文(ちゅうもん)する ("to order"), 配(くば)る ("to distribute"), 用意(ようい)する ("to prepare/provide").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)を頼(たの)む: to order catered bento
  • 仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)を注文(ちゅうもん)する: to place an order for catered bento
  • 仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)を配(くば)る: to hand out catered bento
  • 仕出(しだ)し弁当(べんとう)の予約(よやく): a catering order reservation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 弁当(べんとう): bento — any boxed meal, whether homemade, store-bought, or catered.
  • 宅配弁当(たくはいべんとう): home-delivery bento — routinely delivered, often a subscription service for elderly or busy customers.
  • 駅弁(えきべん): train-station bento — sold at stations for travel.
  • ケータリング: catering — loanword; often implies Western-style buffet catering rather than boxed meals.
