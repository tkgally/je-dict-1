高層建築(こうそうけんちく)

こうそうけんちく
noun
high-rise building, high-rise construction
1. high-rise building — a tall building with many stories; also, the construction of such buildings
A tall building with many floors — typically an office tower, condominium, hotel, or public building. Also refers to the activity or industry of constructing such buildings. In Japan the term is often used more or less interchangeably with 高層(こうそう)ビル, though 高層建築(こうそうけんちく) has a slightly more technical or formal ring.
都心(としん)には高層建築(こうそうけんちく)が多(おお)い。
There are many high-rise buildings in the city center.
高層建築(こうそうけんちく)は地震(じしん)に強(つよ)く設計(せっけい)されている。
High-rise buildings are designed to be earthquake-resistant.
駅前(えきまえ)の再開発(さいかいはつ)で新(あたら)しい高層建築(こうそうけんちく)が次々(つぎつぎ)と建(た)てられている。
With the redevelopment in front of the station, new high-rise buildings are being built one after another.
高層建築(こうそうけんちく)の技術(ぎじゅつ)は戦後(せんご)急速(きゅうそく)に発展(はってん)した。
High-rise construction technology developed rapidly after the war.

Compound of 高層(こうそう) ('many-storied, high-rise') and 建築(けんちく) ('building, architecture, construction').

USAGE:

  • Used both for the buildings themselves and for the act or field of constructing them, e.g., 高層建築(こうそうけんちく)技術(ぎじゅつ) ('high-rise construction technology').
  • In Japanese legal and technical usage, buildings over 60 meters are often formally classed as 超高層(ちょうこうそう)建築(けんちく) ('super-high-rise'); the boundary between 高層(こうそう) and 超高層(ちょうこうそう) is not universally fixed.
  • Widely used in news, architecture, urban planning, and real estate contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 高層建築(こうそうけんちく)が建(た)つ: a high-rise is built/goes up
  • 高層建築(こうそうけんちく)を建設(けんせつ)する: to construct a high-rise
  • 高層建築(こうそうけんちく)物(ぶつ): high-rise structure (another, more formal variant)
  • 都市(とし)の高層建築(こうそうけんちく): urban high-rise buildings

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 高層(こうそう)ビル: high-rise (office) building — the everyday conversational equivalent.
  • 超高層(ちょうこうそう)建築(けんちく): super-high-rise / skyscraper — for especially tall buildings, often 100 m+.
  • 低層(ていそう)建築(けんちく): low-rise building — the direct antonym.
  • 中層(ちゅうそう)建築(けんちく): mid-rise building — for buildings of intermediate height.
  • 建築(けんちく)物(ぶつ): building, structure — the general term for any building.
