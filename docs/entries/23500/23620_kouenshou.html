抗炎症(こうえんしょう)

こうえんしょう
noun
anti-inflammatory
1. anti-inflammatory (action, drug, or effect)
Describes a substance, drug, or effect that reduces inflammation in the body. Used mainly in medical, pharmaceutical, and health-food contexts, often as a modifier in compounds like 抗炎症薬(こうえんしょうやく) (anti-inflammatory drug) or 抗炎症作用(こうえんしょうさよう) (anti-inflammatory action).
抗炎症(こうえんしょう)作用(さよう)がある。
It has an anti-inflammatory effect.
抗炎症(こうえんしょう)薬(やく)を処方(しょほう)された。
I was prescribed an anti-inflammatory drug.
このハーブには抗炎症(こうえんしょう)効果(こうか)があると言(い)われている。
This herb is said to have anti-inflammatory properties.
医師(いし)は、腫(は)れを抑(おさ)えるために抗炎症(こうえんしょう)剤(ざい)を使(つか)うよう勧(すす)めた。
The doctor recommended using an anti-inflammatory agent to suppress the swelling.

Composed of 抗(こう) (resist, counter) and 炎症(えんしょう) (inflammation). Almost always appears as a modifier in compounds rather than as a standalone noun, describing drugs, foods, or biological actions that reduce inflammation.

USAGE:
This is a technical term used in medical, pharmaceutical, and nutritional contexts. It does not normally stand alone; speakers combine it with a following noun such as 薬(やく), 剤(ざい), 作用(さよう), or 効果(こうか).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 抗炎症(こうえんしょう)作用(さよう): anti-inflammatory action
  • 抗炎症(こうえんしょう)薬(やく): anti-inflammatory drug
  • 抗炎症(こうえんしょう)剤(ざい): anti-inflammatory agent
  • 抗炎症(こうえんしょう)効果(こうか): anti-inflammatory effect
  • 抗炎症(こうえんしょう)成分(せいぶん): anti-inflammatory ingredient

RELATED TERMS:

  • 炎症(えんしょう): inflammation — the condition this word counters
  • 消炎(しょうえん): reducing inflammation — a near-synonym used in drug names like 消炎鎮痛剤(しょうえんちんつうざい)
  • 抗生物質(こうせいぶっしつ): antibiotic — a different category of medication
  • 解熱剤(げねつざい): fever reducer — often paired with anti-inflammatories in over-the-counter medicines
