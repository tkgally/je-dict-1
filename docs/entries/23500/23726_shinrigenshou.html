心理現象(しんりげんしょう)

しんりげんしょう
noun
psychological phenomenon
1. psychological phenomenon; psychological effect
An observable pattern of thought, feeling, perception, or behavior studied by psychology — including cognitive biases, optical illusions, group dynamics, and other regular effects that appear across people under similar conditions.
興味深(きょうみぶか)い心理現象(しんりげんしょう)だ。
It's an interesting psychological phenomenon.
これは誰(だれ)にでも起(お)こる心理現象(しんりげんしょう)だ。
This is a psychological phenomenon that can happen to anyone.
群衆(ぐんしゅう)の中(なか)では、個人(こじん)の判断(はんだん)が弱(よわ)くなる心理現象(しんりげんしょう)が知(し)られている。
It is known that in crowds, individual judgment weakens — this is a well-documented psychological phenomenon.
消費者(しょうひしゃ)が最初(さいしょ)に提示(ていじ)された価格(かかく)に影響(えいきょう)を受(う)けるのは、アンカリングと呼(よ)ばれる心理現象(しんりげんしょう)の一例(いちれい)である。
Consumers being influenced by the first price they see is an example of a psychological phenomenon called anchoring.

Composed of 心理(しんり) (mental state, psychology) and 現象(げんしょう) (phenomenon). A slightly formal academic term used in psychology textbooks, popular science articles, marketing books, and news explainers about cognitive biases and human behavior.

USAGE:
Used as an umbrella term for effects that psychology has named and studied — for example アンカリング (anchoring), バイアス} (biases), {錯視(さくし) (optical illusions), バイスタンダー{効果(こうか)} (the bystander effect). Often appears in the pattern 'Xという}{心理現象(しんりげんしょう)' ('a psychological phenomenon called X').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心理現象(しんりげんしょう)が起(お)こる: a psychological phenomenon occurs
  • 心理現象(しんりげんしょう)を説明(せつめい)する: to explain a psychological phenomenon
  • 心理現象(しんりげんしょう)を研究(けんきゅう)する: to study a psychological phenomenon
  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い心理現象(しんりげんしょう): an interesting psychological phenomenon
  • Xという}{心理現象(しんりげんしょう): a psychological phenomenon called X

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 心理効果(しんりこうか): psychological effect — emphasizes causal impact (Y causes Z); more common in marketing contexts
  • 心理(しんり)作用(さよう): psychological action / workings — the inner mental process underlying behavior
  • 認知(にんち)バイアス: cognitive bias — a specific subtype of 心理現象(しんりげんしょう) involving systematic errors in reasoning
  • 心理学(しんりがく): psychology — the field of study, not an individual phenomenon
  • 社会現象(しゃかいげんしょう): social phenomenon — a parallel formation at the societal rather than individual level
