1. to spin round and round; to rotate continuously; to go round in circles
To turn repeatedly in a circular motion, whether literally (a wheel, a top, a carousel) or figuratively (one's thoughts, a conversation that gets nowhere). The mimetic adverb ぐるぐる suggests multiple continuous rotations rather than a single turn.
目(め)がぐるぐる回(まわ)る。
My head is spinning.
観覧車(かんらんしゃ)がゆっくりぐるぐる回(まわ)っている。
The Ferris wheel is slowly spinning round and round.
同(おな)じ考(かんが)えが頭(あたま)の中(なか)をぐるぐる回(まわ)って、なかなか眠(ねむ)れなかった。
The same thought kept going round and round in my head, and I couldn't fall asleep.

Combines the mimetic adverb ぐるぐる ('round and round, spinning repeatedly') with the intransitive godan verb 回(まわ)る ('to turn, rotate'). Refers to sustained circular motion — not a single rotation but many. Used both literally for rotating objects and figuratively for restless thoughts or unproductive discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目(め)がぐるぐる回(まわ)る: one's head/eyes spin (dizzy, overwhelmed)
  • 頭(あたま)の中(なか)でぐるぐる回(まわ)る: (a thought) goes round and round in one's mind
  • 同(おな)じところをぐるぐる回(まわ)る: to keep going in circles (same place)
  • 話(はなし)がぐるぐる回(まわ)る: the conversation goes in circles
  • 洗濯(せんたく)機(き)がぐるぐる回(まわ)る: the washing machine spins

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 回(まわ)る: to turn, rotate — the base verb; a single turn or unmarked rotation
  • くるくる回(まわ)る: to spin lightly/gracefully — faster, lighter, and smaller than ぐるぐる
  • ぐるっと回(まわ)る: to turn once all the way around — a single complete rotation
  • 回転(かいてん)する: to rotate — more formal/technical, used for machines and physics

CONTRAST:

  • ぐるぐる: repeated, heavy or continuous rotation (often big or anxious)
  • くるくる: lighter, quicker, smaller circles (often playful or pleasant)
  • ぐるっと: one complete turn, then stop

TRANSITIVITY:
回(まわ)る is intransitive. The transitive counterpart is 回(まわ)す — so 'to spin something round and round' is ぐるぐる回(まわ)す.
