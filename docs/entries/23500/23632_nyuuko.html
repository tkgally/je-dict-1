入庫(にゅうこ)

にゅうこ
noun
entering a warehouse or garage; receiving into storage; arriving at the depot
1. placing (goods) into a warehouse; receiving stock into storage
The arrival or receipt of goods into a warehouse, stockroom, or storage facility — a standard logistics term for inventory coming in. Contrasts with 出庫(しゅっこ) (shipping out of storage).
新(あたら)しい商品(しょうひん)が入庫(にゅうこ)した。
The new stock has come in.
入庫(にゅうこ)の予定(よてい)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the incoming-stock schedule.
発注(はっちゅう)した部品(ぶひん)がまだ入庫(にゅうこ)していないため、生産(せいさん)ラインが止(と)まっている。
The parts we ordered still haven't arrived in the warehouse, so the production line is halted.
2. (of a vehicle) pulling into a garage, parking lot, or depot
A vehicle entering its garage, depot, or parking space — used of cars entering a parking facility, trains returning to the rail yard, and buses going back to the depot at the end of service.
最終(さいしゅう)電車(でんしゃ)が車庫(しゃこ)に入庫(にゅうこ)した。
The last train pulled into the depot.
立体(りったい)駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)に入庫(にゅうこ)する際(さい)は、ゆっくり進(すす)んでください。
Please drive slowly when entering the multi-story parking garage.
バスは運行(うんこう)終了後(しゅうりょうご)、営業所(えいぎょうしょ)に入庫(にゅうこ)して点検(てんけん)を受(う)けます。
After its route is finished, the bus pulls into the depot and is given an inspection.

Composed of 入(にゅう) ("enter") + 庫(こ) ("storehouse, garage"). Literally "entering the storehouse," and applies to both goods going into a warehouse (sense 1) and vehicles going into a garage or depot (sense 2).

USAGE:

  • Used as a する-verb (入庫(にゅうこ)する) and as a plain noun, often in logistics compounds: 入庫(にゅうこ)日(び) (date of receipt), 入庫(にゅうこ)数(すう) (number of items received), 入庫(にゅうこ)予定(よてい) (scheduled arrival).
  • Sense 1 is standard in retail, manufacturing, and supply-chain contexts — store staff talk about when stock "comes in," and inventory systems record 入庫(にゅうこ) events.
  • Sense 2 is most visible on parking-garage signage and train/bus schedules. The last scheduled service of the day is often described as entering its depot — 車庫(しゃこ)入庫(にゅうこ) — and late-night trains/buses may be labeled 「入庫(にゅうこ)」 on destination boards.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive: stock / a vehicle enters storage — 商品(しょうひん)が入庫(にゅうこ)する.
  • Transitive: to place stock into storage — 入庫(にゅうこ)させる, or in plain form 〜を{入庫(にゅうこ)する} (also widely used in logistics writing).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (sense 1):

  • 商品(しょうひん)が入庫(にゅうこ)する: stock comes in
  • 入庫(にゅうこ)予定(よてい): scheduled inbound
  • 入庫(にゅうこ)日(び): date of receipt
  • 入庫(にゅうこ)検査(けんさ): incoming inspection

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (sense 2):

  • 駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)に入庫(にゅうこ)する: to pull into the parking garage
  • 車庫(しゃこ)入庫(にゅうこ): returning to the depot
  • 入庫(にゅうこ)時間(じかん): time of entry
  • 最終(さいしゅう)入庫(にゅうこ): last train/bus to the depot

ANTONYM:

  • 出庫(しゅっこ): shipping out of storage / leaving the garage — the complementary term for both senses.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 在庫(ざいこ): inventory, stock on hand
  • 倉庫(そうこ): warehouse
  • 車庫(しゃこ): garage, depot
  • 納品(のうひん): delivery (of goods to a customer) — the upstream act that precedes 入庫(にゅうこ) on the receiving end
