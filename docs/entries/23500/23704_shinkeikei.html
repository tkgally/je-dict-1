神経系(しんけいけい)

しんけいけい
noun
nervous system
1. nervous system
The biological system consisting of the brain, spinal cord, and nerves, which transmits signals throughout the body and coordinates sensation, movement, and thought.
神経系(しんけいけい)の病気(びょうき)。
A disease of the nervous system.
神経系(しんけいけい)は脳(のう)と脊髄(せきずい)を中心(ちゅうしん)とする。
The nervous system is centered on the brain and spinal cord.
ストレスは神経系(しんけいけい)に大(おお)きな影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
Stress has a major effect on the nervous system.
神経系(しんけいけい)は中枢(ちゅうすう)神経(しんけい)と末梢(まっしょう)神経(しんけい)の二(ふた)つに大(おお)きく分(わ)けられる。
The nervous system is broadly divided into the central and peripheral nervous systems.

Composed of 神経(しんけい) (nerve) and 系(けい) (system). A standard biological/medical term, used in both technical writing and general educated conversation.

USAGE:

  • Used as a plain noun, most often with の in attributive position: 神経系(しんけいけい)の病気(びょうき).
  • Regularly compounded with other terms: 中枢(ちゅうすう)神経系(しんけいけい) (central nervous system), 自律(じりつ)神経系(しんけいけい) (autonomic nervous system).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 神経系(しんけいけい)の疾患(しっかん): nervous system disorder
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)に作用(さよう)する: to act on the nervous system
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)が発達(はったつ)する: the nervous system develops
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)の異常(いじょう): abnormality in the nervous system

RELATED TERMS:

  • 中枢(ちゅうすう)神経(しんけい): central nerves / central nervous system — brain and spinal cord
  • 末梢(まっしょう)神経(しんけい): peripheral nerves — nerves outside the brain and spinal cord
  • 自律(じりつ)神経(しんけい): autonomic nerves — regulate involuntary functions
  • 脳(のう): brain — main command center of the nervous system
