次第(しだい)で

しだいで
expression
depending on; subject to
1. depending on — used after a noun to say that an outcome varies according to that factor
A fixed pattern [noun] + 次第(しだい)で, meaning 'depending on [noun]' or 'according to [noun]'. It introduces a factor that will determine or influence the result of something. The related form 次第(しだい)だ ends a sentence ('it depends on X'), and 次第(しだい)では adds a stronger conditional nuance ('depending on the circumstances').
天気(てんき)次第(しだい)で決(き)めます。
I'll decide depending on the weather.
結果(けっか)はあなたの努力(どりょく)次第(しだい)です。
The result depends on your effort.
使(つか)い方(かた)次第(しだい)で、この道具(どうぐ)はとても便利(べんり)になる。
Depending on how you use it, this tool can be very useful.
値段(ねだん)次第(しだい)では、買(か)ってもいいと思(おも)っている。
Depending on the price, I'm willing to buy it.

Fixed pattern: [noun] + 次第(しだい)で / 次第(しだい)です / 次第(しだい)では. The element 次第(しだい) is a noun meaning 'order' or 'circumstances', and attaching で turns the whole phrase into 'depending on X'.

USAGE:

  • [noun] + 次第(しだい)で: depending on [noun] (used mid-sentence). The outcome varies with the noun.
  • [noun] + 次第(しだい)だ/です: it depends on [noun] (sentence-ending). Common as a complete statement: '結果(けっか)は君(きみ)次第(しだい)だ' (the result depends on you).
  • [noun] + 次第(しだい)では: depending on [noun] (with a stronger conditional nuance, often introducing a contingency).
  • Attaches only to NOUNS, not directly to verbs or adjectives. To follow a verb, use a nominalized form such as 使(つか)い方(かた) or similar nouns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天気(てんき)次第(しだい)で: depending on the weather
  • あなた次第(しだい)だ: it depends on you; it's up to you
  • 結果(けっか)次第(しだい)で: depending on the result
  • 努力(どりょく)次第(しだい)で: depending on one's effort
  • 場合(ばあい)次第(しだい)で: depending on the situation
  • 値段(ねだん)次第(しだい)では: depending on the price

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • [noun] + によって: by / depending on — very close in meaning; more neutral and more common overall. 次第(しだい)で emphasizes that the factor will decisively determine the outcome.
  • [noun] + による: depends on — verb form, used in expressions like 'それはあなたによる' ('that depends on you').
  • [noun] + 如何(いかん)で: depending on — more formal and literary, common in written Japanese.

REGISTER:
Neutral to slightly formal. Common in both speech and writing. 次第(しだい)です as a sentence ending often appears in business or polite contexts when accepting that an outcome rests with another party.
