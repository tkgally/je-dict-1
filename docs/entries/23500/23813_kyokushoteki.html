局所的(きょくしょてき)

きょくしょてき
na-adjective
local; localized; limited to a small area
1. localized; limited to a particular place or part
Describes a phenomenon, problem, or effect that is confined to a small or specific area rather than spread widely. Common in weather reports, medicine, engineering, and discussions of systems or regions. Usually contrasts with 全体的(ぜんたいてき) ('overall; general') or 広範(こうはん)な ('wide-ranging').
局所的(きょくしょてき)な雨(あめ)。
Localized rain.
局所的(きょくしょてき)に停電(ていでん)が発生(はっせい)した。
Power outages occurred in limited areas.
今日(きょう)の午後(ごご)は局所的(きょくしょてき)に激(はげ)しい雷雨(らいう)となる恐(おそ)れがあります。
This afternoon there is a risk of severe localized thunderstorms.

Compound of 局所(きょくしょ) ('local area; particular part') and the adjectival suffix 〜的(てき). Written-language register; heard daily in weather forecasts and in medical or technical explanations.

USAGE:

  • As a な-adjective: 局所的(きょくしょてき)な現象(げんしょう) ('a localized phenomenon').
  • Extremely common adverbially: 局所的(きょくしょてき)に降(ふ)る ('to rain in limited areas').
  • Weather forecasts very often combine it with words such as 雨(あめ) ('rain'), 雷雨(らいう) ('thunderstorm'), 大雨(おおあめ) ('heavy rain').
  • In medicine, pairs with 麻酔(ますい) ('anesthesia') and 治療(ちりょう) ('treatment'): 局所的(きょくしょてき)な麻酔(ますい) ('local anesthesia').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 局所的(きょくしょてき)な雨(あめ): localized rain
  • 局所的(きょくしょてき)な痛(いた)み: localized pain
  • 局所的(きょくしょてき)な麻酔(ますい): local anesthesia
  • 局所的(きょくしょてき)に発生(はっせい)する: to occur in a limited area
  • 局所的(きょくしょてき)に見(み)れば: looking at it locally / in isolation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 部分的(ぶぶんてき): partial — emphasizes being only a part of the whole, not necessarily a geographic area.
  • 限定的(げんていてき): limited; restricted — stresses deliberate limitation rather than natural localization.
  • 地域的(ちいきてき): regional — points to a specific region or district.
  • 全体的(ぜんたいてき): overall; general — the common antonym.
