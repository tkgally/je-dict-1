孵卵器(ふらんき)

ふらんき
noun
incubator (for eggs)
1. incubator; egg incubator
A device that keeps eggs at the right temperature and humidity so that they will hatch. Used on poultry farms, in schools for educational observation, and by hobbyists who breed birds or reptiles.
卵(たまご)を孵卵器(ふらんき)に入(い)れる。
Put the eggs in the incubator.
小学校(しょうがっこう)で孵卵器(ふらんき)を使(つか)ってひよこを育(そだ)てた。
At elementary school we raised chicks using an incubator.
孵卵器(ふらんき)の温度(おんど)を38度(ど)に設定(せってい)する。
Set the incubator's temperature to 38 degrees.
家庭用(かていよう)の小型(こがた)孵卵器(ふらんき)を買(か)って、ウズラの卵(たまご)を孵化(ふか)させてみた。
I bought a small home-use incubator and tried to hatch quail eggs.

Composed of 孵卵(ふらん) (egg incubation) and the suffix -器(き) (instrument, apparatus). Refers to the device itself, in contrast with 孵化場(ふかじょう), which refers to the whole facility.

USAGE:
Commonly encountered in agricultural contexts (poultry, game birds), elementary-school science projects (hatching chicken eggs in the classroom), and among hobbyists who breed exotic birds or reptiles. The term is slightly technical; in casual conversation, people may instead describe the box by function, for example 卵(たまご)を温(あたた)める機械(きかい) (a machine that warms eggs).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 孵卵器(ふらんき)に卵(たまご)を入(い)れる: to place eggs in the incubator
  • 孵卵器(ふらんき)の温度(おんど)を保(たも)つ: to maintain the incubator's temperature
  • 小型(こがた)孵卵器(ふらんき): small / compact incubator
  • 自動(じどう)孵卵器(ふらんき): automatic incubator (with automatic egg-turning)
  • 孵卵器(ふらんき)で孵化(ふか)させる: to hatch using an incubator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 孵化場(ふかじょう): hatchery — the building or facility, not the machine
  • 保育器(ほいくき): (baby) incubator — a medical device for newborn humans; not for eggs
  • 育雛器(いくすうき): brooder — a device for keeping newly hatched chicks warm after hatching
  • インキュベーター: incubator — the katakana loanword, used for laboratory/medical incubators or, figuratively, business incubators
