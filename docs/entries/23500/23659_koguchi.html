小口(こぐち)

こぐち
noun
small lot; small amount; small-scale (transaction)
1. small lot; small amount; small-scale (transaction or order)
A small quantity or low-value unit, especially in business and finance — a small transaction, a small order, a small loan, or a small investment. Used attributively with nouns such as 取引(とりひき) (transaction), 融資(ゆうし) (loan), and 注文(ちゅうもん) (order) to indicate the small-scale version.
小口(こぐち)の注文(ちゅうもん)も受(う)け付(つ)けます。
We also accept small orders.
個人(こじん)向(む)けに小口(こぐち)の融資(ゆうし)を行(おこな)っている。
We offer small-scale loans aimed at individuals.
最近(さいきん)は海外(かいがい)から小口(こぐち)の荷物(にもつ)を送(おく)る人(ひと)が増(ふ)えているそうだ。
These days, the number of people sending small parcels from abroad is apparently increasing.

Composed of 小(こ) (small) and 口(くち/ぐち) (here meaning "an individual unit or lot," as in 一口(ひとくち) "one share, one unit"). Very common in commerce and finance as the opposite of 大口(おおぐち) (large lot, bulk). Used attributively: 小口(こぐち)の～, 小口(こぐち)～.

A separate specialized sense exists in bookbinding, where 小口(こぐち) refers to the three cut edges of a bound book (top, bottom, and the edge opposite the spine). This usage is technical and rarely encountered outside printing and library contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小口(こぐち)取引(とりひき): small-lot transaction
  • 小口(こぐち)融資(ゆうし): small-scale lending, microloan
  • 小口(こぐち)注文(ちゅうもん): small-lot order
  • 小口(こぐち)現金(げんきん): petty cash
  • 小口(こぐち)預金(よきん): small-amount deposit
  • 小口(こぐち)配送(はいそう): small-parcel delivery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大口(おおぐち): large lot; big-ticket (transaction) — direct antonym
  • 少額(しょうがく): small sum (of money) — emphasizes amount of money rather than lot size
  • 小額(しょうがく): small denomination — specifically small-denomination bills or coins
  • 零細(れいさい): very small scale (of a business) — describes a business, not individual transactions
