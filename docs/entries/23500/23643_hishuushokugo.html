被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)

ひしゅうしょくご
noun
modified word; head word (the word that a modifier modifies)
1. modified word; head word (the word that a modifier modifies)
A linguistic term referring to the word that receives modification from another word (the modifier). In the phrase "red apple," "apple" is the modified word and "red" is the modifier. Used in grammar textbooks, linguistics papers, and Japanese-language teaching.
修飾語(しゅうしょくご)と被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)。
The modifier and the modified word.
被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)は通常(つうじょう)、修飾語(しゅうしょくご)の後(あと)に来(く)る。
In Japanese, the modified word usually comes after the modifier.
この文(ぶん)では「本(ほん)」が被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)で、「赤(あか)い」が修飾語(しゅうしょくご)である。
In this sentence, "book" is the modified word and "red" is the modifier.
日本語(にほんご)の文法(ぶんぽう)では、修飾語(しゅうしょくご)と被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)の関係(かんけい)を正(ただ)しく把握(はあく)することが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
In Japanese grammar, it is important to grasp correctly the relationship between the modifier and the modified word.

Composed of the passive prefix 被(ひ) (receiving; undergoing), 修飾(しゅうしょく) (modification; qualification), and 語(ご) (word). Literally "the word that receives modification" — the prefix 被(ひ) marks the receiving side, just as in 被害者(ひがいしゃ) (victim) and 被告(ひこく) (defendant).

USAGE:
A grammar term. You will see it in Japanese-language textbooks, school grammar lessons, linguistic papers, and Japanese-as-a-foreign-language teaching materials. It is almost always paired with 修飾語(しゅうしょくご) (modifier). In Japanese, the 被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご) comes AFTER the modifier, which is the opposite of the order in many European languages but the same as English attributive order.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 修飾語(しゅうしょくご)と被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご): modifier and modified word
  • 被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)を修飾(しゅうしょく)する: to modify the head word
  • 被修飾語(ひしゅうしょくご)の関係(かんけい): the modifier-head relationship

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 修飾語(しゅうしょくご): modifier — the counterpart; the word doing the modifying
  • 主語(しゅご): subject — a different grammatical role; not about modification
  • 連体修飾語(れんたいしゅうしょくご): nominal modifier — a more specific term for a modifier that attaches to a noun
  • 係(かか)る・受(う)ける: to modify / to be modified (as verbs) — describes the same relationship in action
