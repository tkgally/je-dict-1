老年期(ろうねんき)

ろうねんき
noun
old age; one's later years; senescence
1. old age; later years; senescence
The stage of life corresponding to old age, typically beginning around age 65 in Japanese usage. Used in medical, welfare, psychological, and developmental contexts as one of the named life stages (幼年期(ようねんき), 少年期(しょうねんき), 青年期(せいねんき), 壮年期(そうねんき), 老年期(ろうねんき)).
老年期(ろうねんき)に入(はい)る。
To enter old age.
老年期(ろうねんき)の健康(けんこう)管理(かんり)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Managing one's health is important in old age.
老年期(ろうねんき)のうつ病(びょう)は見逃(みのが)されやすい病気(びょうき)の一(ひと)つだ。
Depression in old age is one of the illnesses most easily overlooked.

Compound of 老年(ろうねん) ('old age') and 期(き) ('period; phase; stage'). Names the final stage in the standard progression of life phases used in Japanese medicine, psychology, and welfare policy.

USAGE:

  • A technical or semi-formal term; in casual conversation about 'when you get old' speakers are more likely to say 年(とし)を取(と)ってから or お年寄(としよ)りの時期(じき).
  • Frequent in healthcare contexts: 老年期(ろうねんき)医学(いがく) ('geriatric medicine'), 老年期(ろうねんき)のうつ病(びょう) ('late-life depression'), 老年期(ろうねんき)認知症(にんちしょう) ('late-onset dementia').
  • Typically defined as age 65 and above, though boundaries vary by source.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 老年期(ろうねんき)に入(はい)る: to enter old age
  • 老年期(ろうねんき)を迎(むか)える: to reach old age
  • 老年期(ろうねんき)医学(いがく): geriatric medicine
  • 老年期(ろうねんき)の健康(けんこう): health in old age
  • 老年期(ろうねんき)認知症(にんちしょう): late-onset dementia

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 壮年期(そうねんき): prime of life (roughly 30s to 50s) — the preceding life stage.
  • 高齢期(こうれいき): old age — near-synonym; 高齢(こうれい) is often used in policy documents and statistics.
  • 晩年(ばんねん): one's later years — more literary; emphasizes the last part of a particular person's life rather than a biological stage.
  • 老後(ろうご): life after retirement; one's years in old age — everyday word focused on the practical phase after working life.
