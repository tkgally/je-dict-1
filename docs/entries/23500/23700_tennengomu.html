天然(てんねん)ゴム

てんねんごむ
noun
natural rubber
1. natural rubber; caoutchouc
Elastic material obtained from the latex of rubber trees (mainly パラゴムノキ}, Hevea brasiliensis). Contrasts with synthetic rubber ({合成(ごうせい)ゴム) made from petrochemicals.
天然(てんねん)ゴムの手袋(てぶくろ)。
Natural rubber gloves.
このタイヤは天然(てんねん)ゴムでできている。
This tire is made of natural rubber.
天然(てんねん)ゴムは弾力性(だんりょくせい)にとても優(すぐ)れている。
Natural rubber has excellent elasticity.
天然(てんねん)ゴムは東南(とうなん)アジアの熱帯(ねったい)雨林(うりん)で育(そだ)つゴムの木(き)から採(と)れる。
Natural rubber is harvested from rubber trees that grow in the tropical rainforests of Southeast Asia.

Composed of 天然(てんねん) (natural) and {ゴム} (rubber, from Dutch {gom}). Written with the second element in katakana because {ゴム} is a loanword.

USAGE:

  • Used as a material noun, typically in attributive form with の: 天然(てんねん)ゴムの手袋(てぶくろ) (natural rubber gloves).
  • Contrasted almost automatically with 合成(ごうせい)ゴム (synthetic rubber) in technical and commercial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天然(てんねん)ゴムの{タイヤ}: natural rubber tire
  • 天然(てんねん)ゴム製(せい): made of natural rubber
  • 天然(てんねん)ゴム{アレルギー}: natural rubber latex allergy
  • 天然(てんねん)ゴムを採取(さいしゅ)する: to harvest natural rubber

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 合成(ごうせい)ゴム: synthetic rubber — made from petrochemicals
  • {ラテックス}: latex — the milky sap from which natural rubber is made, or thin rubber film products
  • {ゴム}: rubber — the general term; may refer to either natural or synthetic
