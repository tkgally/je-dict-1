合成(ごうせい)ゴム

ごうせいごむ
noun
synthetic rubber
1. synthetic rubber
An elastic polymer made artificially from petrochemicals, used as a substitute or supplement for natural rubber. Comes in many varieties (SBR, nitrile, silicone, etc.) tailored to different heat, oil, or weather requirements.
合成(ごうせい)ゴムの部品(ぶひん)。
Synthetic rubber parts.
このホースは合成(ごうせい)ゴムでできている。
This hose is made of synthetic rubber.
合成(ごうせい)ゴムは油(あぶら)や熱(ねつ)に強(つよ)い種類(しゅるい)が多(おお)い。
Many kinds of synthetic rubber are resistant to oil and heat.
現代(げんだい)の自動車(じどうしゃ)タイヤ}には{天然(てんねん)ゴムと合成(ごうせい)ゴムの両方(りょうほう)が使(つか)われている。
Modern car tires use both natural and synthetic rubber.

Composed of 合成(ごうせい) (synthesis, artificial combination) and {ゴム} (rubber, from Dutch {gom}).

USAGE:

  • Used as a material noun, most often in attributive form with の: 合成(ごうせい)ゴムの部品(ぶひん).
  • Regularly contrasted with 天然(てんねん)ゴム (natural rubber). In industry, the two are often mixed in a single product.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合成(ごうせい)ゴム製(せい): made of synthetic rubber
  • 合成(ごうせい)ゴムの{タイヤ}: synthetic rubber tire
  • 合成(ごうせい)ゴム工業(こうぎょう): the synthetic rubber industry
  • 合成(ごうせい)ゴムを使用(しよう)する: to use synthetic rubber

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 天然(てんねん)ゴム: natural rubber — derived from rubber tree latex
  • {エラストマー}: elastomer — a broader technical term for any rubber-like elastic polymer
  • {プラスチック}: plastic — a different class of synthetic polymer, generally less elastic
