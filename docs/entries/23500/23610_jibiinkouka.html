耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)

じびいんこうか
noun
otolaryngology; ENT (ear, nose, and throat) department
1. otolaryngology; ENT department; ear, nose, and throat clinic
The medical specialty dealing with disorders of the ears, nose, and throat, or the department or clinic that provides such care. Patients are referred here for issues like ear infections, hearing problems, sinusitis, tonsillitis, hoarseness, allergies, and related conditions.
耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)に通(かよ)っている。
I'm going to an ENT clinic regularly.
花粉症(かふんしょう)なので耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)で薬(くすり)をもらった。
I have hay fever, so I got medicine from the ENT clinic.
耳(みみ)の調子(ちょうし)がおかしいので、明日(あした)耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)を受診(じゅしん)しようと思(おも)う。
My ear feels off, so I'm thinking of seeing an ENT doctor tomorrow.
子(こ)どもが中耳炎(ちゅうじえん)を繰(く)り返(かえ)すので、近所(きんじょ)の耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)に定期的(ていきてき)に通(かよ)っています。
Our child keeps getting middle-ear infections, so we regularly visit the neighborhood ENT clinic.

Composed of 耳(じ) (ear), 鼻(び) (nose), 咽喉(いんこう) (throat, pharynx), and 科(か) (department, section). This is the formal name for the ENT (ear, nose, and throat) medical specialty and the clinic or hospital department that practices it.

USAGE:
Japanese signage and hospital directories usually write out the full four-kanji term. In casual speech, many people shorten it to 耳鼻科(じびか), which refers to essentially the same specialty. Smaller neighborhood clinics often use either form on their signs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)に行(い)く: to go to an ENT clinic
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)を受診(じゅしん)する: to see an ENT doctor; to consult the ENT department
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)の医師(いし): ENT doctor, otolaryngologist
  • 耳鼻咽喉科(じびいんこうか)クリニック: ENT clinic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 耳鼻科(じびか): a common shortened form of the same specialty — more conversational
  • 内科(ないか): internal medicine — for general, non-surgical medical problems
  • 眼科(がんか): ophthalmology — for eye-related problems
  • 皮膚科(ひふか): dermatology — for skin conditions
