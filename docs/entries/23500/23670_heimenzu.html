平面図(へいめんず)

へいめんず
noun
floor plan; plan view
1. floor plan; plan view; plan drawing
A drawing that shows a building, room, or site as seen from directly above — with walls, doors, furniture, or other features drawn flat on a two-dimensional plane. Used in architecture, real estate, and interior design.
家(いえ)の平面図(へいめんず)。
The floor plan of a house.
この平面図(へいめんず)を見(み)ると、部屋(へや)の配置(はいち)がよく分(わ)かる。
Looking at this floor plan, you can clearly see the layout of the rooms.
建築士(けんちくし)が新(あたら)しいマンションの平面図(へいめんず)を描(か)いた。
The architect drew up the floor plan for the new apartment building.
不動産(ふどうさん)のサイトでは、物件(ぶっけん)ごとに詳(くわ)しい平面図(へいめんず)が掲載(けいさい)されている。
On real-estate websites, a detailed floor plan is posted for each property.

Composed of 平面(へいめん) (flat surface, plane) and 図(ず) (diagram, drawing). A 平面図(へいめんず) shows a structure or area from a bird's-eye (top-down) perspective, as if the roof has been removed. It is one of the standard types of architectural drawings, alongside 立面図(りつめんず) (elevation) and 断面図(だんめんず) (cross-section).

USAGE:
Frequently seen in real-estate listings, house-plan magazines, and building-construction documents. Also used in geometry classes when depicting flat figures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 平面図(へいめんず)を描(か)く: to draw a floor plan
  • 平面図(へいめんず)を見(み)る: to look at a floor plan
  • 平面図(へいめんず)に示(しめ)す: to indicate on the floor plan
  • 詳細(しょうさい)な平面図(へいめんず): a detailed floor plan

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 間取(まど)り: room layout — less technical; refers to the arrangement of rooms rather than the drawing itself
  • 立面図(りつめんず): elevation drawing — shows the exterior as seen from the front or side
  • 断面図(だんめんず): cross-section drawing — shows the interior as if sliced vertically
  • 見取(みと)り図(ず): sketch map, rough layout drawing — a simpler, less precise version often drawn by hand
