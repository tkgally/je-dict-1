搬送(はんそう)

はんそう
noun
conveyance; transport (especially of an injured or sick person, or of goods)
1. transport; conveyance (of a person, especially to a hospital)
The act of moving a person to another location by vehicle — most commonly used for moving an injured or ill person to a hospital by ambulance, but also for transferring patients between facilities. A news-register word; in everyday speech, 運(はこ)ぶ (to carry) is more common.
救急車(きゅうきゅうしゃ)で病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)された。
He was transported to the hospital by ambulance.
事故(じこ)の負傷者(ふしょうしゃ)は近(ちか)くの病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)された。
The victims of the accident were transported to a nearby hospital.
高齢(こうれい)の男性(だんせい)が意識不明(いしきふめい)の状態(じょうたい)で病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)され、現在(げんざい)治療(ちりょう)を受(う)けている。
An elderly man was transported to the hospital unconscious and is currently receiving treatment.
2. conveyance; transport (of goods, materials, or equipment)
The act of moving goods, materials, or equipment from one place to another within a facility or between facilities. Common in manufacturing, logistics, and technical contexts.
工場内(こうじょうない)では、部品(ぶひん)は自動(じどう)の搬送(はんそう)システムで各(かく)ラインに運(はこ)ばれる。
Within the factory, parts are conveyed to each line by an automated transport system.
倉庫(そうこ)から店舗(てんぽ)への商品(しょうひん)搬送(はんそう)は、毎朝(まいあさ)定時(ていじ)に行(おこな)われている。
The transport of goods from the warehouse to the store is carried out at a fixed time every morning.
重(おも)い機材(きざい)の搬送(はんそう)には専用(せんよう)の台車(だいしゃ)とフォークリフトが必要(ひつよう)になり、作業(さぎょう)には複数(ふくすう)の人手(ひとで)がかかる。
Transporting heavy equipment requires dedicated carts and forklifts, and the operation takes several workers.

Composed of 搬(はん) (to transport, carry) and 送(そう) (to send). A formal word used in news reports, medical contexts, and technical/industrial writing. Takes する to form a verb: 搬送(はんそう)する (to transport).

USAGE:

The two senses reflect two quite different contexts:

  • **Medical/emergency use (sense 1)** — By far the most common use in news. Seen constantly in the standard construction 病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)された ("was transported to the hospital"), typically in the passive, when reporting on accidents or sudden illness.
  • **Industrial/logistical use (sense 2)** — Used for moving goods, materials, or equipment, especially in contexts like factories, warehouses, and construction sites. Often appears in compounds such as 搬送(はんそう)システム (conveyor/transport system) or 搬送(はんそう)装置(そうち) (conveying device).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病院(びょういん)に搬送(はんそう)される: to be taken to the hospital
  • 救急(きゅうきゅう)搬送(はんそう): emergency transport (by ambulance)
  • 搬送(はんそう)先(さき): the destination of transport; the hospital to which someone is being taken
  • 搬送(はんそう)中(ちゅう): during transport
  • 搬送(はんそう)システム: conveyor / transport system

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運搬(うんぱん): transportation, haulage — broader word, usually for goods/materials; less common for people
  • 輸送(ゆそう): transport, shipping — long-distance or large-scale; freight, logistics
  • 運(はこ)ぶ: to carry, transport — everyday word
  • 移送(いそう): transfer (of a person or object to another location) — often between institutions, including prisoners or patients between hospitals

REGISTER:
Formal, written. Not used in casual conversation. In casual speech, prefer 運(はこ)ぶ (for goods) or specify by context (e.g., 病院(びょういん)に連(つ)れて行(い)った).
