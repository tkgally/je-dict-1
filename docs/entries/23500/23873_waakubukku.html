ワークブック

わーくぶっく
noun
workbook; exercise book
1. workbook — a book containing exercises and activities for a student or trainee to work through, often used alongside a main textbook
Loanword from English 'workbook'. Refers to a study book made up of exercises, practice questions, and fill-in activities, typically used as a companion to a main textbook. It may also refer to self-study books for language learning, business skills, or personal development.
ワークブックを開(ひら)いてください。
Please open your workbook.
教科書(きょうかしょ)とワークブックを持(も)ってきてください。
Please bring the textbook and workbook.
このワークブックには各(かく)章(しょう)の最後(さいご)に練習問題(れんしゅうもんだい)が付(つ)いている。
This workbook has practice problems at the end of each chapter.

Loanword from English 'workbook'. Most commonly refers to exercise books used in school or language learning, but also covers self-study and training books with writing-based activities.

USAGE:

  • Used especially for language-learning and school-subject workbooks where students write answers directly in the book.
  • Often paired with 教科書(きょうかしょ) ('textbook') as the companion exercise volume.
  • Also extended to self-improvement and skills-training books that guide the reader through written exercises.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ワークブックを使(つか)う: to use a workbook
  • ワークブックに書(か)き込(こ)む: to write in a workbook
  • 英語(えいご)のワークブック: an English workbook
  • ワークブック形式(けいしき): workbook format
  • 問題集(もんだいしゅう)とワークブック: problem sets and workbooks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 問題集(もんだいしゅう): problem collection — focused on practice problems (especially for exams), usually with answers provided but without the instructional scaffolding of a workbook.
  • 練習帳(れんしゅうちょう): practice book / exercise notebook — a native equivalent, often used for children's writing and kanji practice.
  • 教科書(きょうかしょ): textbook — the main instructional book the workbook supplements.
  • 参考書(さんこうしょ): reference book, study guide — explanatory, not exercise-based.

REGISTER:
Modern and common in educational publishing. 練習帳(れんしゅうちょう) feels more traditional and is more associated with elementary school and children's materials.
