論客(ろんきゃく)

ろんきゃく
noun
pundit; commentator; skilled debater
1. pundit; commentator; skilled debater; opinion-maker
A person known for publicly putting forward strong, clearly reasoned opinions on political, social, or cultural issues. A 論客(ろんきゃく) is someone whom others recognize as articulate and hard to argue against — whether a newspaper columnist, a TV commentator, or an active participant in academic debate. The term is mildly honorific in tone but can be used with a touch of irony when the person is overly fond of arguing.
有名(ゆうめい)な論客(ろんきゃく)。
A well-known pundit.
保守派(ほしゅは)の論客(ろんきゃく)として知(し)られている。
He is known as a commentator on the conservative side.
番組(ばんぐみ)には各(かく)分野(ぶんや)の論客(ろんきゃく)が集(あつ)まり、白熱(はくねつ)した議論(ぎろん)が展開(てんかい)された。
Pundits from various fields gathered on the show, and a heated debate unfolded.
彼(かれ)は若手(わかて)ながら、経済(けいざい)政策(せいさく)について政府(せいふ)を鋭(するど)く批判(ひはん)する論客(ろんきゃく)として注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Although young, he has drawn attention as a commentator who sharply criticizes the government's economic policy.

Compound of 論(ろん) ('argument; discourse') and 客(きゃく) (here in the classical sense of 'person; fellow', as in 刺客(しかく) 'assassin' or 剣客(けんきゃく) 'swordsman'). So literally 'a person of argument'. Despite the kanji 客(きゃく) normally meaning 'guest', it does not mean a 'guest debater' in this word.

USAGE:

  • Refers to a public figure, not an ordinary person who enjoys arguing. For that nuance use 口(くち)の立(た)つ人(ひと) ('someone good with words') or 議論好(ぎろんず)き ('one who likes to argue').
  • Typically modified by adjectives or qualifying phrases: 有力(ゆうりょく)な論客(ろんきゃく) ('influential pundit'), 若手(わかて)の論客(ろんきゃく) ('young commentator'), 保守派(ほしゅは)の論客(ろんきゃく) ('conservative commentator').
  • Often appears in TV, newspaper, and magazine contexts; less common in ordinary conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 有力(ゆうりょく)な論客(ろんきゃく): influential pundit
  • 若手(わかて)論客(ろんきゃく): young commentator
  • 保守派(ほしゅは)論客(ろんきゃく) / リベラル論客(ろんきゃく): conservative / liberal pundit
  • 論客(ろんきゃく)が集(あつ)まる: pundits gather
  • 論客(ろんきゃく)として知(し)られる: to be known as a commentator
  • 論客(ろんきゃく)ぞろい: a gathering full of sharp debaters

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 評論家(ひょうろんか): critic; commentator — a formal professional title; many 評論家(ひょうろんか) are also 論客(ろんきゃく).
  • コメンテーター: (TV) commentator — the loanword, neutral in tone.
  • オピニオンリーダー: opinion leader — emphasizes influence, not debating skill.
  • 論者(ろんしゃ): proponent; advocate (of a specific view) — more limited; often 'one who argues for X'.

REGISTER:
A slightly literary/journalistic word. Using it in casual speech about a friend (e.g. 彼(かれ)は論客(ろんきゃく)だ) tends to sound half-joking — praising or teasing them for being argumentative.
