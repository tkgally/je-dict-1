茶筅(ちゃせん)

ちゃせん
noun
tea whisk (for matcha); chasen
1. tea whisk; bamboo whisk used to prepare matcha
A small whisk carved from a single piece of bamboo, with fine tines splayed outward, used in the Japanese tea ceremony to whisk powdered green tea (抹茶(まっちゃ)) into hot water. The splitting of the bamboo into many thin tines is what gives the whisk its characteristic shape and frothing ability.
茶筅(ちゃせん)で抹茶(まっちゃ)を点(た)てる。
Whisk the matcha with a tea whisk.
茶筅(ちゃせん)は使(つか)う前(まえ)にお湯(ゆ)で湿(しめ)らせておく。
Moisten the tea whisk with hot water before using it.
奈良(なら)県(けん)の高山(たかやま)は茶筅(ちゃせん)づくりの産地(さんち)として知(し)られ、職人(しょくにん)が一本(いっぽん)の竹(たけ)から丁寧(ていねい)に穂先(ほさき)を割(わ)り出(だ)す。
Takayama in Nara Prefecture is known as a production center for tea whisks, where artisans carefully split the tines out of a single piece of bamboo.

Compound of 茶(ちゃ) ('tea') and 筅(せん) ('whisk; brush'). The character {筅} is used almost exclusively in this word.

USAGE:

  • Tightly associated with the tea ceremony (茶道(さどう)). The whisk is one of the core utensils alongside 茶碗(ちゃわん) ('tea bowl'), 茶杓(ちゃしゃく) ('tea scoop'), and 棗(なつめ) ('tea caddy').
  • The verb for using a tea whisk to prepare matcha is 点(た)てる, as in お{茶(ちゃ)を点(た)てる} ('to make tea [in the ceremonial way]'). Using 入(い)れる ('to brew') with 茶筅(ちゃせん) sounds unnatural.
  • Number of tines varies by school and style: 数穂(かずほ) (many tines, ~80) for everyday use vs. fewer-tined whisks for thicker 濃茶(こいちゃ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 茶筅(ちゃせん)で点(た)てる: to whisk (matcha) with a chasen
  • 茶筅(ちゃせん)を振(ふ)る: to move the whisk (in the whisking motion)
  • 茶筅(ちゃせん)を湿(しめ)らせる: to moisten the whisk
  • 茶筅通(ちゃせんとお)し: the preparatory step of warming and inspecting the whisk
  • 新品(しんぴん)の茶筅(ちゃせん): a brand-new tea whisk

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 茶杓(ちゃしゃく): tea scoop — the bamboo scoop used to measure matcha powder out of the caddy.
  • 茶碗(ちゃわん): tea bowl — in the tea ceremony, the bowl in which the matcha is whisked; in everyday life, also 'rice bowl'.
  • 抹茶(まっちゃ): matcha — the powdered green tea that is whisked with a 茶筅(ちゃせん).
  • 泡立(あわだ)て器(き): (ordinary) whisk — a Western-style wire whisk used in cooking; not a substitute for 茶筅(ちゃせん) in the tea ceremony.
