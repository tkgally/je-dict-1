薄紅色(うすべにいろ)

うすべにいろ
noun
light pink; pale rose color
1. light pink; pale rose; soft pink color
A soft, pale pink color — the shade evoked by things like light cherry blossoms, a faint blush, or watered-down rouge. 紅(べに) refers to the traditional red dye or rouge made from safflower, so the name literally means 'light crimson/rouge color'.
薄紅色(うすべにいろ)のドレス。
A light pink dress.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)びらが薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まる。
The cherry petals are tinted a pale pink.
彼女(かのじょ)のほおが恥(は)ずかしさで薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まった。
Her cheeks flushed a light pink with embarrassment.
夕焼(ゆうや)けの空(そら)が薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まり、山(やま)の輪郭(りんかく)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がって見(み)えた。
The sunset sky was tinged a soft pink, and the outline of the mountains stood out against it.

Composed of 薄(うす) (thin, light, pale), 紅(べに) (crimson, rouge — the traditional safflower dye), and 色(いろ) (color).

REGISTER:
薄紅色(うすべにいろ) carries a literary, slightly elegant nuance. It is common in descriptive prose about nature (cherry blossoms, sunsets, flowers), in fashion and cosmetics marketing, and in classical or poetic writing. In casual conversation, Japanese speakers more often just say ピンク(ぴんく) or 薄(うす)いピンク.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の桜(さくら): pale pink cherry blossoms
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まる: to be tinged with light pink
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)のほお: rosy cheeks; lightly flushed cheeks
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の夕(ゆう)空(ぞら): pale pink evening sky

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ピンク: pink — the everyday, general loanword
  • 桜色(さくらいろ): cherry-blossom pink — a very similar poetic color word
  • 桃色(ももいろ): peach pink — a slightly warmer pink shade
  • 紅色(べにいろ): crimson, deep rouge red — the darker, fuller version of the same hue family
