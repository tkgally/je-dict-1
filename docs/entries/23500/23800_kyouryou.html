狭量(きょうりょう)

きょうりょう
adjective-na
narrow-minded; intolerant; petty
1. narrow-minded; small-minded; intolerant; petty
Unable or unwilling to accept views, behaviors, or people different from one's own; lacking generosity of spirit. Describes a person's disposition or an institution's attitude, often in critical or reflective writing. More formal and literary than 心(こころ)が狭(せま)い, which is the everyday equivalent.
狭量(きょうりょう)な人間(にんげん)。
A narrow-minded person.
彼(かれ)の狭量(きょうりょう)な態度(たいど)にはうんざりした。
I was fed up with his small-minded attitude.
異(こと)なる意見(いけん)を一切(いっさい)認(みと)めないのは狭量(きょうりょう)というものだ。
Refusing to accept any differing opinion is simply narrow-mindedness.
社会(しゃかい)の多様性(たようせい)を受(う)け入(い)れられない狭量(きょうりょう)な姿勢(しせい)は、国際(こくさい)社会(しゃかい)で通用(つうよう)しない。
A narrow-minded stance that cannot accept social diversity will not be accepted in the international community.

Compound of 狭(きょう) ('narrow') and 量(りょう) ('capacity; measure'), literally 'narrow capacity' — i.e., small capacity of mind. A formal, bookish adjective that conveys a measured, critical judgment of character.

USAGE:

  • Almost always used as a na-adjective before nouns describing people or their attitudes: 狭量(きょうりょう)な人(ひと), 狭量(きょうりょう)な考(かんが)え, 狭量(きょうりょう)な政治家(せいじか).
  • Used as a predicate noun to name the quality itself: それは狭量(きょうりょう)だ ('that's narrow-mindedness').
  • Rare in casual conversation; belongs to essays, editorials, and thoughtful criticism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狭量(きょうりょう)な人(ひと): a narrow-minded person
  • 狭量(きょうりょう)な考(かんが)え: narrow-minded thinking
  • 狭量(きょうりょう)な態度(たいど): a petty attitude
  • 狭量(きょうりょう)な政治家(せいじか): a small-minded politician
  • 狭量(きょうりょう)さ: narrow-mindedness (the noun form via さ)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 心(こころ)が狭(せま)い: narrow-hearted; petty — the everyday equivalent; less formal.
  • 偏狭(へんきょう): parochial; bigoted — stronger, implies prejudice and rigidity, not just pettiness.
  • 度量(どりょう)が狭(せま)い: small-minded — idiom with 度量(どりょう) ('tolerance; magnanimity').
  • 寛容(かんよう): tolerant — direct antonym.

ANTONYMS:

  • 寛容(かんよう): tolerant; broad-minded
  • 度量(どりょう)が大(おお)きい: magnanimous; big-hearted
