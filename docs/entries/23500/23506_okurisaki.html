Composed of 送(おく)り (sending, from the verb 送(おく)る) and 先(さき) (destination, place ahead). The 先(さき) suffix is productive in Japanese for indicating the destination or counterpart of an action: 届(とど)け先(さき) (delivery address), 連絡先(れんらくさき) (contact information), 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(��き) (place one is moving to).

You will encounter this word when sending packages at the post office or a convenience store, filling out delivery forms (伝票(でんぴょう)), or shopping online. Japanese delivery slips typically have fields for both 送(おく)り先(さき) (recipient) and 依頼主(いらいぬし) or 送(おく)り主(ぬし) (sender).
