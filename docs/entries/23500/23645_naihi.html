内皮(ないひ)

ないひ
noun
endothelium; inner lining; inner skin
1. endothelium; inner skin (anatomy)
In anatomy and medicine, the thin layer of cells lining the inside of blood vessels, the heart, and other hollow organs. Contrasted with 外皮(がいひ) (outer skin) and 中皮(ちゅうひ) (mesothelium).
血管(けっかん)内皮(ないひ)。
Vascular endothelium.
内皮(ないひ)細胞(さいぼう)が傷(きず)つく。
The endothelial cells are damaged.
高血圧(こうけつあつ)は血管(けっかん)の内皮(ないひ)に悪(わる)い影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
High blood pressure has a negative impact on the endothelium of blood vessels.
2. inner rind; inner skin (of fruit, seeds, etc.)
In botany and cooking, the thin white or translucent membrane found just inside the outer peel of citrus fruits, chestnuts, and similar foods. Often removed for a better texture or flavor.
栗(くり)の内皮(ないひ)を剥(む)く。
Peel the inner skin of the chestnut.
みかん}の{内皮(ないひ)には食物繊維(しょくもつせんい)が多(おお)く含(ふく)まれている。
The inner skin of a mandarin orange contains a lot of dietary fiber.
料理人(りょうりにん)は丁寧(ていねい)に栗(くり)の内皮(ないひ)を取(と)り除(のぞ)いてから煮(に)る。
The chef carefully removes the inner skin of the chestnuts before simmering them.

Composed of 内(ない) (inner) and 皮(ひ) (skin). A technical compound with parallel counterparts in 外皮(がいひ) (outer skin), 中皮(ちゅうひ) (mesothelium), and 表皮(ひょうひ) (epidermis).

USAGE:
The anatomical sense (sense 1) is the most common in writing — it appears in medical journals, health articles, and biology textbooks, often in combinations like 血管(けっかん)内皮(ないひ) (vascular endothelium) or 内皮(ないひ)細胞(さいぼう) (endothelial cells). The culinary/botanical sense (sense 2) appears in cooking instructions and describes the thin inner membrane that must be removed from chestnuts, citrus fruits, and similar foods. Context makes clear which sense is intended.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血管(けっかん)内皮(ないひ): vascular endothelium
  • 内皮(ないひ)細胞(さいぼう): endothelial cells
  • 内皮(ないひ)機能(きのう): endothelial function
  • 内皮(ないひ)を剥(む)く: to peel off the inner skin (of a fruit)
  • 栗(くり)の内皮(ないひ): the inner skin of a chestnut

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 外皮(がいひ): outer skin; peel — the direct antonym
  • 表皮(ひょうひ): epidermis — the outermost layer, especially of human skin
  • 中皮(ちゅうひ): mesothelium — the cell layer lining body cavities
  • 渋皮(しぶかわ): the astringent inner skin (specifically of chestnuts) — more specific culinary term
