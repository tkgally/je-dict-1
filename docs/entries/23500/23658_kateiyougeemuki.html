家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)

かていようげーむき
noun
home video game console
1. home video game console
A dedicated video-game machine designed to be connected to a television or monitor at home, as opposed to handheld systems, smartphones, arcade cabinets, or PCs. The standard industry term in Japanese for products such as PlayStation, Nintendo Switch (in dock mode), and Xbox.
新(あたら)しい家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)を買(か)った。
I bought a new home game console.
家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)市場(しじょう)は年々(ねんねん)拡大(かくだい)している。
The home console market is expanding every year.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、初(はじ)めての家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)にすっかり夢中(むちゅう)になった。
As a child, I became completely absorbed in my first home game console.
スマートフォンの普及(ふきゅう)により、家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)離(ばな)れが進(すす)んでいると言(い)われている。
It is said that the spread of smartphones has accelerated the move away from home game consoles.

Composed of 家庭(かてい) (home; household), 用(よう) (for the use of), the loanword ゲーム (game), and the suffix 機(き) (machine). The structure parallels other 'for-home-use' product names such as 家庭用(かていよう)プリンター (home printer).

USAGE:
A standard industry and journalistic term for TV-connected game consoles, contrasting with 携帯(けいたい)ゲーム機(き) (handheld console) and アーケードゲーム (arcade game). In casual conversation people more often refer to specific brands (PS5, Switch) or simply say ゲーム機(き). The full form 家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き) is preferred in industry reports, sales figures, and trade press. Sometimes shortened to 据(す)え置(お)き機(き) ('stationary console') in gamer slang.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)市場(しじょう): the home console market
  • 家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)ソフト: home console software
  • 次世代(じせだい)家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き): next-generation home console
  • 家庭用(かていよう)ゲーム機(き)本体(ほんたい): console hardware itself (as opposed to peripherals)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 携帯(けいたい)ゲーム機(き): handheld game console — the portable counterpart
  • 据(す)え置(お)き機(き): stationary machine — gamer slang for home console
  • ゲーム機(き): game machine — broader, includes handhelds and arcade machines
  • ゲーミングPC: gaming PC — the personal-computer alternative
