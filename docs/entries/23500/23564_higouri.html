非合理(ひごうり)

ひごうり
noun
irrationality; illogicality; irrational
1. irrationality; illogicality; irrational; unreasonable
The quality of being contrary to reason or logic, or of not conforming to rational thought. Used both as a noun describing irrationality itself and as a na-adjective describing things that defy reasoning — emotions, customs, behaviors, or social structures that cannot be justified by logical analysis alone.
非合理(ひごうり)な決定(けってい)だ。
It is an irrational decision.
人間(にんげん)の行動(こうどう)には非合理(ひごうり)な面(めん)がある。
Human behavior has its irrational side.
その制度(せいど)の非合理(ひごうり)さを多(おお)くの人(ひと)が指摘(してき)している。
Many people point out the irrationality of that system.
行動(こうどう)経済学(けいざいがく)は、人(ひと)が非合理(ひごうり)な判断(はんだん)をするパターンを研究(けんきゅう)する分野(ぶんや)だ。
Behavioral economics is a field that studies the patterns by which people make irrational judgments.

Formed from the prefix 非(ひ) (non-, un-) + 合理(ごうり) (rational, reasonable). Functions both as a noun and as a na-adjective (e.g., 非合理(ひごうり)な行動(こうどう)).

USAGE:

  • Commonly used in academic, philosophical, and business writing, and in discussions of economics, psychology, and ethics.
  • Can carry a critical nuance (非合理(ひごうり)な慣習(かんしゅう) — an irrational custom), but is also used neutrally to describe aspects of human nature that lie beyond pure logic.
  • 不合理(ふごうり) is a very close synonym, often used interchangeably, though 不合理(ふごうり) tends to emphasize "unreasonable/unjust" while 非合理(ひごうり) tends to emphasize "not logical/not rational."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非合理(ひごうり)な行動(こうどう): irrational behavior
  • 非合理(ひごうり)な判断(はんだん): irrational judgment
  • 非合理(ひごうり)な制度(せいど): irrational system
  • 非合理(ひごうり)性(せい): irrationality (as a property)
  • 非合理(ひごうり)主義(しゅぎ): irrationalism (philosophical position)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不合理(ふごうり): unreasonable, absurd — very close synonym; often highlights injustice or absurdity
  • 理不尽(りふじん): unreasonable, unfair — emphasizes the unjustness toward a person
  • 矛盾(むじゅん): contradiction — inconsistency between two things
  • 論理的(ろんりてき): logical — antonym of a sort, describing things that do follow logic
  • 合理(ごうり)的(てき): rational, reasonable — direct antonym
