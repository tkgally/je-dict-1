不該当(ふがいとう)

ふがいとう
noun
not applicable; not relevant
1. not applicable; not relevant; does not fall under (a category)
A checkbox or dropdown label meaning that a given item, rule, or category does not apply to the person or case in question. Common on government forms, applications, and legal documents as the opposite of 該当(がいとう) (applicable).
不該当(ふがいとう)に○をつける。
Circle "not applicable".
該当(がいとう)・不該当(ふがいとう)のいずれかを選(えら)んでください。
Please select either "applicable" or "not applicable".
この項目(こうもく)が不該当(ふがいとう)の場合(ばあい)は、空欄(くうらん)のままで結構(けっこう)です。
If this item is not applicable, you may leave it blank.
輸出(ゆしゅつ)規制(きせい)の対象品目(たいしょうひんもく)に該当(がいとう)するか不該当(ふがいとう)かを事前(じぜん)に判定(はんてい)する。
Determine in advance whether the goods are applicable or not applicable to export controls.

Composed of the negative prefix 不(ふ) (non-) and 該当(がいとう) (applicability; falling under). Pronounced ふがいとう, not ふがいとう with a nasal — treat it as a simple compound.

USAGE:
A bureaucratic form-filling word. You will see it printed next to a checkbox or as a dropdown option on application forms, visa paperwork, tax forms, export-control documents, and insurance forms. In casual speech, people say 当(あ)てはまらない or 関係(かんけい)ない instead. The word itself is rarely used as a predicate in free prose; when it is, it takes だ or である: この項目(こうもく)は不該当(ふがいとう)である ("this item is not applicable").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 該当(がいとう)・不該当(ふがいとう): applicable / not applicable (as paired options)
  • 不該当(ふがいとう)に○をつける: to circle "not applicable"
  • 不該当(ふがいとう)の場合(ばあい): if not applicable
  • 不該当(ふがいとう)判定(はんてい): determination of non-applicability

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 該当(がいとう): applicable; falling under — the positive counterpart
  • 非該当(ひがいとう): not falling under — a near-synonym, more common in export-control contexts
  • 対象外(たいしょうがい): outside the scope; excluded — focuses on being outside the target set, not on whether a rule applies
