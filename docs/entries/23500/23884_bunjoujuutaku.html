分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)

ぶんじょうじゅうたく
noun
housing for sale (as opposed to rental), for-sale housing unit
1. housing for sale — a residential property (typically in a development or condominium) that is sold individually to buyers, as opposed to being rented out
A home or condominium unit that is sold outright to an individual owner, typically as part of a housing development or apartment complex. Contrasts with 賃貸(ちんたい)住宅(じゅうたく) ('rental housing'). The term is widely used in Japanese real-estate advertising.
分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)を買(か)う。
To buy a for-sale home.
新築(しんちく)の分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)が人気(にんき)だ。
Newly built for-sale homes are popular.
この地区(ちく)では分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)の価格(かかく)が上(あ)がっている。
In this area, the prices of for-sale homes are rising.
郊外(こうがい)に小(ちい)さな庭(にわ)がついた分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)を購入(こうにゅう)した。
I bought a for-sale home with a small garden in the suburbs.

Compound of 分譲(ぶんじょう) ('dividing and selling [land or a building] in separate lots or units') and 住宅(じゅうたく) ('residence, housing').

USAGE:

  • Refers to housing sold as individual ownership units — a detached house or a condominium unit — as opposed to rentals.
  • Typically contrasted with 賃貸(ちんたい)住宅(じゅうたく) ('rental housing') in property listings and news articles about the housing market.
  • May refer to either detached houses (一戸建(いっこだ)て) or condominium units (マンション}), though {分譲(ぶんじょう)マンション is a more specific term for the latter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)を購入(こうにゅう)する: to purchase a for-sale home
  • 分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)を建(た)てる: to build a for-sale home (developer's perspective)
  • 新築(しんちく)分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく): a newly built for-sale home
  • 分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく)の販売(はんばい): the sale of for-sale housing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 賃貸(ちんたい)住宅(じゅうたく): rental housing — the direct antonym.
  • 分譲(ぶんじょう)マンション: for-sale condominium — specifically a condo unit sold to an owner, as opposed to 賃貸(ちんたい)マンション (rental apartment).
  • 建売(たてうり)住宅(じゅうたく): a pre-built spec home sold as a completed unit — overlaps with detached 分譲住宅(ぶんじょうじゅうたく).
  • 注文(ちゅうもん)住宅(じゅうたく): custom-built home — a home built to the buyer's specifications, contrasting with 建売(たてうり).
