会話力(かいわりょく)

かいわりょく
noun
conversational ability; conversation skills
1. conversational ability; skill at holding a conversation
A person's practical ability to carry on a conversation — especially the ability to express oneself and respond naturally in a foreign language, or (in a native-language context) the social skill of engaging in smooth back-and-forth exchange. Frequently contrasted with reading, writing, and listening abilities in language-learning discussions.
会話力(かいわりょく)を伸(の)ばしたい。
I want to improve my conversation skills.
英語(えいご)の会話力(かいわりょく)には自信(じしん)がある。
I'm confident in my English conversation skills.
毎日(まいにち)オンラインで外国人(がいこくじん)と話(はな)すようになってから、会話力(かいわりょく)がぐんと上(あ)がった。
After I started talking with foreigners online every day, my conversation skills improved dramatically.

Composed of 会話(かいわ) (conversation) and the suffix 〜力(りょく) (ability, -ing power). Common in language-learning contexts, where it denotes the practical speaking-and-responding ability, as opposed to knowledge of grammar or vocabulary. Outside language learning it can also describe a person's social talent for carrying a conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会話力(かいわりょく)を伸(の)ばす: to improve one's conversation skills
  • 会話力(かいわりょく)を鍛(きた)える: to train one's conversation skills
  • 会話力(かいわりょく)が上(あ)がる: one's conversation skills improve
  • 会話力(かいわりょく)が高(たか)い: to have strong conversation skills
  • 会話力(かいわりょく)不足(ぶそく): a lack of conversational ability
  • 英語(えいご)の会話力(かいわりょく): English conversational ability

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会話(かいわ): conversation — the activity itself, not the skill
  • 語学力(ごがくりょく): language ability — broader; includes reading, writing, and listening, not just speaking
  • 話(はなし)す力(ちから): speaking ability — a more colloquial way to express the same idea
  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく): communication ability — broader still; includes nonverbal and professional communication
