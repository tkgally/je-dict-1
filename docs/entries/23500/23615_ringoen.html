りんご園(えん)

りんごえん
noun
apple orchard; apple farm
1. apple orchard; apple farm
A farm or plantation where apples are grown — an orchard of apple trees. Many such places in Japan (especially in Aomori, Nagano, and other apple-producing regions) are also open to visitors for seasonal apple picking.
秋(あき)にりんご園(えん)を訪(おとず)れた。
I visited an apple orchard in autumn.
りんご園(えん)でりんご狩(が)りを楽(たの)しんだ。
We enjoyed apple picking at the apple orchard.
祖父(そふ)は長年(ながねん)、青森(あおもり)で小(ちい)さなりんご園(えん)を営(いとな)んでいた。
My grandfather ran a small apple orchard in Aomori for many years.
観光客(かんこうきゃく)の受(う)け入(い)れを始(はじ)めてから、このりんご園(えん)は地域(ちいき)の人気(にんき)スポットになった。
After it began accepting visitors, this apple orchard became a popular local attraction.

Composed of りんご (apple) and 園(えん) (garden, plantation, farm). The kanji 園(えん) is a common suffix meaning 'cultivated land' or 'grounds,' found in many similar compounds.

USAGE:
This is the standard word for an apple orchard. It is often seen on signage, tourism websites, and farm names. Many りんご園(りんごえん) in Japan offer seasonal りんご{狩(が)り} (apple picking) from roughly September through November. The word covers both commercial farms and smaller family operations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • りんご園(えん)を営(いとな)む: to run an apple orchard
  • りんご園(えん)で働(はたら)く: to work at an apple orchard
  • りんご園(えん)でりんご狩(が)り: apple picking at an orchard
  • 観光(かんこう)りんご園(えん): a tourist-friendly apple orchard

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 果樹園(かじゅえん): orchard — the general term for any fruit-tree orchard
  • ぶどう園(えん): vineyard; grape orchard — a closely parallel word for grapes
  • みかん園(えん): mandarin-orange orchard — parallel for citrus
  • りんご農家(のうか): apple farmer / apple-farming household — refers to the grower rather than the land
