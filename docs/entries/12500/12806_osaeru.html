抑(おさ)える

おさえる
ichidan verb
to suppress; to restrain; to control
1. to suppress, to restrain, to hold back
To keep feelings, impulses, or reactions from being expressed or from getting out of control.
怒(いか)りを抑(おさ)える。
To suppress one's anger.
涙(なみだ)を抑(おさ)えられなかった。
I couldn't hold back my tears.
興奮(こうふん)を抑(おさ)えて冷静(れいせい)に話(はな)した。
I controlled my excitement and spoke calmly.
2. to curb, to keep down, to limit
To prevent something from increasing or spreading, such as costs, inflation, or symptoms.
経費(けいひ)を抑(おさ)える。
To keep expenses down.
薬(くすり)で症状(しょうじょう)を抑(おさ)える。
To control the symptoms with medicine.
インフレを抑(おさ)えるために金利(きんり)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げた。
Interest rates were raised to curb inflation.

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)).

  • Negative: 抑(おさ)えない
  • Polite: 抑(おさ)えます
  • Te-form: 抑(おさ)えて
  • Past: 抑(おさ)えた
  • Potential: 抑(おさ)えられる

COMPARISON WITH 押(お)さえる:

抑(おさ)える and 押(お)さえる share the reading おさえる but use different kanji and have different core meanings:

  • 抑(おさ)える: to suppress, restrain, or limit (emotions, costs, spread)
  • 押(お)さえる: to press down, hold in place, or secure physically

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 感情(かんじょう)を抑(おさ)える: to restrain one's emotions
  • 怒(いか)りを抑(おさ)える: to suppress anger
  • 涙(なみだ)を抑(おさ)える: to hold back tears
  • 費用(ひよう)を抑(おさ)える: to keep costs down
  • 症状(しょうじょう)を抑(おさ)える: to control symptoms
