1. to carry on one's shoulders
To physically carry something on one's shoulders or back. The original, literal meaning.
天秤棒(てんびんぼう)を担(にな)う。
To carry a shoulder pole.
重(おも)い荷物(にもつ)を両肩(りょうかた)に担(にな)って山道(やまみち)を登(のぼ)った。
Shouldering heavy loads, they climbed the mountain path.
水(みず)を担(にな)って運(はこ)ぶのは大変(たいへん)な仕事(しごと)だった。
Carrying water on one's shoulders was hard work.
2. to bear, to shoulder, to take on (a role or responsibility)
To accept and carry out an important role, responsibility, or duty. The more common figurative usage.
未来(みらい)を担(にな)う若者(わかもの)たち。
Young people who will shoulder the future.
彼(かれ)はプロジェクトの中心的(ちゅうしんてき)な役割(やくわり)を担(にな)っている。
He bears a central role in the project.
地方(ちほう)の医療(いりょう)を担(にな)う医師(いし)が足(た)りない。
There are not enough doctors to shoulder regional healthcare.

担(にな)う originally meant to carry something on the shoulders, but is now primarily used figuratively for bearing responsibilities, roles, or duties.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を担(にな)う: to bear ~, to shoulder ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 役割(やくわり)を担(にな)う: to take on a role
  • 未来(みらい)を担(にな)う: to shoulder the future
  • 責任(せきにん)を担(にな)う: to bear responsibility
  • 重要(じゅうよう)な任務(にんむ)を担(にな)う: to take on an important mission

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 背負(せお)う: to carry on one's back; to bear (more personal, emotional weight)
  • 引(ひ)き受(う)ける: to undertake, to take on (more about accepting a task)
