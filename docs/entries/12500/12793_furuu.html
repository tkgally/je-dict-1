振(ふ)るう

ふるう
verb (godan)
to wield, to exercise, to display
1. to wield, to swing, to brandish
To swing or brandish a weapon, tool, or implement with force.
刀(かたな)を振(ふ)るう。
To wield a sword.
料理人(りょうりにん)が包丁(ほうちょう)を振(ふ)るって魚(さかな)をさばいた。
The chef wielded a knife and filleted the fish.
彼(かれ)はバットを力強(ちからづよ)く振(ふ)るった。
He swung the bat powerfully.
2. to exercise, to exert (power, ability, violence)
To actively use or display power, ability, or force. Often used when describing someone demonstrating their skills or exerting authority.
暴力(ぼうりょく)を振(ふ)るってはいけない。
You must not resort to violence.
彼女(かのじょ)は持(も)ち前(まえ)のリーダーシップを振(ふ)るった。
She exercised her natural leadership.
権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう立場(たちば)にある人(ひと)は、責任(せきにん)も重(おも)い。
Those in a position to exercise power also bear heavy responsibility.

振(ふ)るう means to wield or exercise something with active force — whether a physical tool or an abstract power.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を振(ふ)るう: to wield ~, to exercise ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう: to display one's skill (literally "wield one's arm")
  • 暴力(ぼうりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to resort to violence
  • 権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to wield power
  • 手腕(しゅわん)を振(ふ)るう: to demonstrate one's abilities
  • 包丁(ほうちょう)を振(ふ)るう: to wield a kitchen knife (i.e., to cook)

DO NOT CONFUSE:

  • 振(ふ)るう (to wield) vs 奮(ふる)う (to rouse oneself, to muster) — different kanji, different meanings
  • 振(ふ)るう (to wield) vs 震(ふる)える (to tremble) — different words entirely
