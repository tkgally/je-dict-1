攻略(こうりゃく)

こうりゃく
noun / suru verb
capture; strategy guide; conquest
1. capture, conquest, siege
The act of attacking and capturing a fortress, city, or position. A military term.
城(しろ)を攻略(こうりゃく)する。
To capture a castle.
敵(てき)の拠点(きょてん)を攻略(こうりゃく)するために作戦(さくせん)を練(ね)った。
We devised a strategy to capture the enemy's base.
難(むずか)しい試験(しけん)を攻略(こうりゃく)するための本(ほん)を買(か)った。
I bought a book for tackling the difficult exam.
2. strategy guide, walkthrough, tips for clearing
A guide or set of strategies for completing a game, challenge, or difficult task. Extremely common in gaming culture.
ゲームの攻略(こうりゃく)サイトを見(み)る。
To look at a game walkthrough site.
ボスの倒(たお)し方(かた)が分(わ)からなくて、攻略(こうりゃく)動画(どうが)を見(み)た。
I didn't know how to beat the boss, so I watched a strategy video.
この攻略(こうりゃく)法(ほう)を使(つか)えば、初心者(しょしんしゃ)でもクリアできる。
If you use this strategy, even beginners can clear it.

USAGE:
While sense 1 (military capture) is the original meaning, sense 2 (strategy guide / walkthrough) is far more common in everyday Japanese, especially online. The word has expanded to cover any kind of "how to conquer" guide — games, exams, travel, dating, etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 攻略(こうりゃく)法(ほう): strategy, method of conquering
  • 攻略(こうりゃく)サイト: walkthrough site
  • 攻略(こうりゃく)本(ぼん): strategy guide (book)
  • 攻略(こうりゃく)動画(どうが): strategy video
  • 攻略(こうりゃく)情報(じょうほう): strategy information, tips
