外需(がいじゅ)

がいじゅ
noun
external demand, foreign demand
1. external demand, foreign demand
Demand for goods and services from outside the country — essentially exports. An economic term contrasted with domestic demand.
外需(がいじゅ)が伸(の)びている。
External demand is growing.
日本(にほん)経済(けいざい)は外需(がいじゅ)に大(おお)きく依存(いぞん)している。
The Japanese economy depends heavily on foreign demand.
外需(がいじゅ)の減少(げんしょう)を補(おぎな)うため、政府(せいふ)は内需(ないじゅ)拡大(かくだい)策(さく)を打(う)ち出(だ)した。
To compensate for the decline in foreign demand, the government introduced measures to expand domestic demand.

USAGE:
An economics term frequently seen in news and business reporting. Contrasted with 内需(ないじゅ) (domestic demand). In economic analysis, 外需(がいじゅ) primarily refers to the net exports component of GDP.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外需(がいじゅ)依存(いぞん): dependence on external demand
  • 外需(がいじゅ)主導(しゅどう): led by external demand
  • 外需(がいじゅ)の拡大(かくだい): expansion of external demand
  • 外需(がいじゅ)と内需(ないじゅ): external and domestic demand
