所属(しょぞく)

しょぞく
noun, suru verb
belonging to, affiliation
1. belonging to, affiliation, membership
The state of being a member of or affiliated with an organization, group, or department. As a suru verb, means to belong to or be affiliated with.
サッカー部(ぶ)に所属(しょぞく)している。
I belong to the soccer club.
彼(かれ)は営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ)に所属(しょぞく)している。
He belongs to the sales department.
所属(しょぞく)先(さき)の名前(なまえ)と部署(ぶしょ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write the name and department of your affiliation.

所属(しょぞく) means belonging to or being affiliated with a group or organization. It is commonly used for workplace departments, sports teams, and academic institutions.

PATTERN:

  • 〜に所属(しょぞく)する: to belong to ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 所属(しょぞく)先(さき): one's affiliation, organization one belongs to
  • 所属(しょぞく)部署(ぶしょ): department one belongs to
  • 所属(しょぞく)選手(せんしゅ): affiliated player
  • 所属(しょぞく)事務所(じむしょ): talent agency one belongs to
