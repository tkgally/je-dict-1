山岳(さんがく)

さんがく
noun
mountains, mountain range
1. mountains, mountain range, alpine terrain
High, rugged mountain areas. Used as a formal or technical term for mountainous terrain, often in compound words related to mountaineering and geography.
山岳(さんがく)地帯(ちたい)を進(すす)む。
Advance through the mountainous region.
山岳(さんがく)救助(きゅうじょ)チームが出動(しゅつどう)した。
The mountain rescue team was dispatched.
日本(にほん)の山岳(さんがく)信仰(しんこう)は古(ふる)くから続(つづ)いており、多(おお)くの山(やま)が御神体(ごしんたい)とされている。
Mountain worship in Japan has continued since ancient times, and many mountains are regarded as sacred objects.

USAGE:
A formal, literary term for mountains. In everyday speech, 山(やま) is more common, but 山岳(さんがく) appears frequently in compound words related to mountaineering, geography, and nature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 山岳(さんがく)地帯(ちたい): mountainous region
  • 山岳(さんがく)部(ぶ): mountaineering club
  • 山岳(さんがく)救助(きゅうじょ): mountain rescue
  • 山岳(さんがく)信仰(しんこう): mountain worship
  • 山岳(さんがく)ガイド: mountain guide
