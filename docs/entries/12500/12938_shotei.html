所定(しょてい)

しょてい
noun / pre-noun adjectival
prescribed, specified, designated
1. prescribed, specified, designated
Something that has been decided or set in advance by rules, regulations, or agreement. Used as a modifier before nouns to indicate that something follows an established standard.
所定(しょてい)の用紙(ようし)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the designated form.
所定(しょてい)の手続(てつづ)きを済(す)ませてから入場(にゅうじょう)してください。
Please complete the required procedures before entering.
所定(しょてい)の位置(いち)に車(くるま)を駐車(ちゅうしゃ)しなければ、罰金(ばっきん)を取(と)られる。
If you don't park your car in the designated spot, you'll be fined.

USAGE:
Almost always used as a modifier before a noun (所定(しょてい)の + noun). Very common in formal and administrative language — forms, procedures, official documents, and regulations. Rarely used in casual conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 所定(しょてい)の用紙(ようし): prescribed/designated form
  • 所定(しょてい)の手続(てつづ)き: required procedures
  • 所定(しょてい)の位置(いち): designated position/location
  • 所定(しょてい)の時間(じかん): designated time
  • 所定(しょてい)の金額(きんがく): prescribed amount
