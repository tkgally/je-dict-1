1. to realize, to perceive, to become aware of
To come to understand something through insight rather than being told directly. Often implies perceiving a truth, hidden intention, or the nature of a situation.
自分(じぶん)の間違(まちが)いを悟(さと)った。
I realized my mistake.
相手(あいて)の嘘(うそ)を悟(さと)った。
I perceived the other person's lie.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の死(し)が近(ちか)いことを悟(さと)り、静(しず)かに受(う)け入(い)れた。
He perceived that his death was near and quietly accepted it.
2. to attain enlightenment, to reach spiritual awakening
In Buddhist contexts, to achieve enlightenment or spiritual understanding. This is the original and most elevated meaning of the word.
修行(しゅぎょう)の末(すえ)に悟(さと)りを開(ひら)いた。
After much training, he attained enlightenment.
釈迦(しゃか)は菩提樹(ぼだいじゅ)の下(もと)で悟(さと)りを得(え)たと伝(つた)えられている。
It is said that the Buddha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree.
人生(じんせい)の無常(むじょう)を悟(さと)り、出家(しゅっけ)した。
Having realized the impermanence of life, he renounced the world and became a monk.

USAGE:
In everyday use (sense 1), 悟(さと)る implies a deeper or more intuitive perception than 気(き)づく (to notice). It suggests understanding the essence or truth of something, not just noticing a surface detail.

KEY NOUN:
悟(さと)り (enlightenment; realization) is the nominal form and a well-known Buddhist concept. 悟(さと)りを開(ひら)く and 悟(さと)りを得(え)る both mean "to attain enlightenment."

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb ending in る. Transitive in sense 1 (takes を or に): Xを悟(さと)る. In sense 2, often used with the noun form 悟(さと)り.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悟(さと)りを開(ひら)く: to attain enlightenment
  • 真実(しんじつ)を悟(さと)る: to perceive the truth
  • 死(し)を悟(さと)る: to sense one's approaching death
  • 悟(さと)られないようにする: to act so as not to be found out
