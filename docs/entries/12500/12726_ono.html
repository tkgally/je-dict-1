斧(おの)

おの
noun
axe, hatchet
1. axe, hatchet
A tool with a heavy blade attached to a handle, used for chopping wood. Also appears in folktales and idioms.
斧(おの)で薪(まき)を割(わ)った。
I split firewood with an axe.
木(き)こりは斧(おの)をかついで山(やま)に入(はい)った。
The woodcutter shouldered his axe and went into the mountains.
金(きん)の斧(おの)と銀(ぎん)の斧(おの)の話(はなし)は、正直(しょうじき)の大切(たいせつ)さを教(おし)えてくれる。
The story of the golden axe and the silver axe teaches us the importance of honesty.

USAGE:
斧(おの) is the standard word for an axe. It appears in the well-known folktale 金(きん)の斧(おの)銀(ぎん)の斧(おの) (The Golden Axe and the Silver Axe), a Japanese adaptation of Aesop's fable about the honest woodcutter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 斧(おの)を振(ふ)る: to swing an axe
  • 斧(おの)で割(わ)る: to chop with an axe
  • 斧(おの)を研(と)ぐ: to sharpen an axe

COUNTER: 本(ほん) (for the tool), 丁(ちょう) (traditional)
