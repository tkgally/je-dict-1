得体(えたい)

えたい
noun
true nature, true character
1. true nature, true character, identity
The real nature or identity of something. Almost exclusively used in the negative expression 得体の知れない (of unknown nature, mysterious, suspicious).
得体(えたい)の知(し)れない生物(せいぶつ)を見(み)つけた。
I found an unidentifiable creature.
得体(えたい)の知(し)れない不安(ふあん)に襲(おそ)われた。
I was overcome by a mysterious anxiety.
得体(えたい)の知(し)れない男(おとこ)が家(いえ)の前(まえ)をうろうろしていたので、警察(けいさつ)に連絡(れんらく)した。
A suspicious man was loitering in front of the house, so I called the police.

USAGE:
Almost always used in the set phrase 得体(えたい)の知(し)れない, meaning "of unknown nature," "mysterious," or "unidentifiable." This expression modifies nouns to convey suspicion, eeriness, or the unsettling quality of something whose true identity cannot be determined. The standalone word 得体(えたい) is rarely used outside this pattern.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 得体(えたい)の知(し)れない人(ひと): a suspicious/mysterious person
  • 得体(えたい)の知(し)れない不安(ふあん): a vague, inexplicable anxiety
  • 得体(えたい)の知(し)れない物(もの): an unidentifiable thing
  • 得体(えたい)の知(し)れない液体(えきたい): an unknown liquid
