手厚(てあつ)い

てあつい
i-adjective
generous; cordial; warm
1. generous, cordial, warm, hospitable
Describes treatment, care, or hospitality that is notably generous, thorough, and considerate. Often used for services, support, or reception.
手厚(てあつ)い看護(かんご)を受(う)けた。
I received attentive nursing care.
この会社(かいしゃ)は福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)が手厚(てあつ)い。
This company has generous employee benefits.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)に対(たい)する手厚(てあつ)い支援(しえん)が各地(かくち)から届(とど)いた。
Generous support for the disaster victims arrived from all over.

USAGE:
手厚(てあつ)い is used to describe treatment, care, or services that go above and beyond what is expected. It carries a positive nuance of warmth and thoroughness. It is not typically used for physical objects.

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 手厚(てあつ)くない
  • Past: 手厚(てあつ)かった
  • Adverbial: 手厚(てあつ)く

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手厚(てあつ)い看護(かんご): attentive nursing care
  • 手厚(てあつ)い支援(しえん): generous support
  • 手厚(てあつ)い保護(ほご): thorough protection
  • 手厚(てあつ)いもてなし(もてなし): warm hospitality
  • 手厚(てあつ)く葬(ほうむ)る: to bury with full honors
