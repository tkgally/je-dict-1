山間(さんかん)

さんかん
noun
among the mountains, mountain area
1. among the mountains, mountain area, mountainous region
An area surrounded by or nestled between mountains. Used to describe remote, rural locations in mountainous terrain.
山間(さんかん)の村(むら)で育(そだ)った。
I grew up in a mountain village.
山間(さんかん)部(ぶ)では冬(ふゆ)の積雪(せきせつ)が多(おお)い。
Snowfall is heavy in winter in mountainous areas.
山間(さんかん)の温泉(おんせん)地(ち)まで車(くるま)で三時間(さんじかん)かかった。
It took three hours by car to reach the hot spring resort in the mountains.

USAGE:
Often used with 部(ぶ) as 山間(さんかん)部(ぶ) (mountainous area/region), especially in news, weather forecasts, and geographic descriptions. Evokes remoteness and rural settings.

READING NOTE:
Also read as やまあい, which has the same meaning but a more colloquial, native-Japanese feel. The Sino-Japanese reading さんかん is more common in formal and written contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 山間(さんかん)部(ぶ): mountainous area
  • 山間(さんかん)の村(むら): mountain village
  • 山間(さんかん)の集落(しゅうらく): mountain settlement
  • 山間(さんかん)地(ち): mountainous district
