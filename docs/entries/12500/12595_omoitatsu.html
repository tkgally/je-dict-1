思(おも)い立(た)つ

おもいたつ
verb (godan)
to resolve to do, to decide on impulse
1. to resolve to do, to decide suddenly, to set one's mind to
To suddenly make up one's mind to do something, often on impulse or inspiration. The decision is typically spontaneous rather than the result of long deliberation.
思(おも)い立(た)ったらすぐ(すぐ)行動(こうどう)する。
When I make up my mind, I act immediately.
ある日(ひ)突然(とつぜん)、旅(たび)に出(で)ようと思(おも)い立(た)った。
One day I suddenly resolved to go on a trip.
思(おも)い立(た)ったが吉日(きちじつ)と言(い)うから、今日(きょう)から始(はじ)めよう。
They say there's no day like the day you decide to act, so let's start today.

USAGE:
Emphasizes the moment of sudden decision or inspiration. The decision is spontaneous — if someone 思(おも)い立(た)つ something, they haven't been deliberating for a long time.

PROVERB:
思(おも)い立(た)ったが吉日(きちじつ) — "The day you decide to act is a lucky day." Encourages acting immediately on one's resolutions rather than procrastinating.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb ending in つ. Intransitive. Typically followed by と or たら: 〜しようと思(おも)い立(た)つ (resolve to do something).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 思(おも)い立(た)ったらすぐ: as soon as one decides
  • 〜しようと思(おも)い立(た)つ: to resolve to do...
  • 思(おも)い立(た)ったが吉日(きちじつ): no time like the present
