接触(せっしょく)

せっしょく
noun / suru verb
contact, touch
1. physical contact, touch
Physical touching or coming into contact between objects, surfaces, or people.
車(くるま)が壁(かべ)に接触(せっしょく)した。
The car made contact with the wall.
電線(でんせん)に接触(せっしょく)すると感電(かんでん)する恐(おそ)れがある。
There is a risk of electric shock if you touch the power lines.
接触(せっしょく)事故(じこ)で車体(しゃたい)に傷(きず)がついた。
The car body was scratched in a contact accident.
2. contact, interaction (with people or groups)
Making contact or establishing communication with a person, organization, or group. Often implies an initial or deliberate approach.
関係者(かんけいしゃ)と接触(せっしょく)を図(はか)る。
To attempt to make contact with the people involved.
異文化(いぶんか)との接触(せっしょく)は視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げてくれる。
Contact with different cultures broadens one's perspective.
感染者(かんせんしゃ)との濃厚(のうこう)接触(せっしょく)があった人(ひと)は検査(けんさ)を受(う)けてください。
People who had close contact with an infected person should get tested.

USAGE:
Used as both a noun and a suru verb. Sense 1 describes physical contact between objects or surfaces. Sense 2 describes interpersonal or cross-cultural contact and interaction. The word became widely familiar during the COVID-19 pandemic through the term 濃厚接触(のうこうせっしょく) (close contact).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 接触(せっしょく)事故(じこ): contact accident, fender bender
  • 濃厚(のうこう)接触(せっしょく): close contact (epidemiology)
  • 接触(せっしょく)を図(はか)る: to attempt contact
  • 接触(せっしょく)を避(さ)ける: to avoid contact

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 接点(せってん): point of contact, connection point
  • 触(ふ)れる: to touch (physical, more everyday)
