手(て)がける

てがける
verb (ichidan)
to handle, to work on, to produce
1. to handle, to work on, to be involved in
To take on a project, business, or creative work and be personally involved in carrying it out. Often implies hands-on involvement or responsibility.
新(あたら)しいプロジェクトを手(て)がける。
To work on a new project.
彼(かれ)は数(かず)多(おお)くの映画(えいが)を手(て)がけた監督(かんとく)だ。
He is a director who has worked on numerous films.
この建築(けんちく)事務所(じむしょ)は国内外(こくないがい)の大型(おおがた)プロジェクトを手(て)がけている。
This architecture firm handles large-scale projects both domestically and internationally.
2. to raise, to look after
To personally raise or care for a child or young person from an early age.
自分(じぶん)で手(て)がけた子(こ)だ。
This is a child I raised myself.
小(ちい)さい頃(ころ)から手(て)がけた弟子(でし)だ。
This is a student I have trained since they were young.
祖母(そぼ)が手(て)がけた庭(にわ)には、季節(きせつ)ごとに美(うつく)しい花(はな)が咲(さ)く。
The garden my grandmother tended has beautiful flowers each season.

手(て)がける means to take something on personally and work on it with hands-on involvement. It carries a nuance of dedicated effort and personal responsibility.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プロジェクトを手(て)がける: to work on a project
  • 設計(せっけい)を手(て)がける: to handle the design
  • 作品(さくひん)を手(て)がける: to produce a work
  • 事業(じぎょう)を手(て)がける: to run a business
