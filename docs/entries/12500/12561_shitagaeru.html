従(したが)える

したがえる
verb (ichidan)
to be accompanied by, to have in tow
1. to be accompanied by, to have in tow, to lead
To have subordinates, attendants, or followers accompany you. Implies a hierarchical relationship where the subject is the leader or person of higher status.
部下(ぶか)を従(したが)えて会場(かいじょう)に入(はい)った。
He entered the venue accompanied by his subordinates.
社長(しゃちょう)は秘書(ひしょ)を従(したが)えて記者(きしゃ)会見(かいけん)に臨(のぞ)んだ。
The president attended the press conference accompanied by his secretary.
大勢(おおぜい)の家来(けらい)を従(したが)えた殿様(とのさま)が城(しろ)から出(で)てきた。
A feudal lord accompanied by many retainers emerged from the castle.

USAGE:
The transitive counterpart of 従(したが)う (to follow, to obey). While 従(したが)う describes the follower's action, 従(したが)える describes the leader's state of having followers. Often used in historical, formal, or literary contexts.

GRAMMAR:
Typically used in the pattern AがBを従(したが)えて, where A is the leader and B is the follower(s).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部下(ぶか)を従(したが)える: to have subordinates in tow
  • 家来(けらい)を従(したが)える: to be accompanied by retainers
  • 大軍(たいぐん)を従(したが)える: to lead a large army
  • 弟子(でし)を従(したが)える: to have disciples in tow
