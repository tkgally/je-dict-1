悩(なや)ます

なやます
verb (godan)
to trouble, to torment, to worry
1. to trouble, to torment, to bother, to worry
To cause someone distress, worry, or difficulty. The subject is the person or thing causing the trouble, and the object is the person being troubled.
頭痛(ずつう)に悩(なや)まされている。
I'm troubled by headaches.
この問題(もんだい)は長年(ながねん)研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)を悩(なや)ませてきた。
This problem has troubled researchers for many years.
子供(こども)の反抗期(はんこうき)に悩(なや)まされる親(おや)は多(おお)い。
Many parents are troubled by their children's rebellious phase.

USAGE:
The transitive counterpart of 悩(なや)む (to worry, to be troubled). While 悩(なや)む describes the person experiencing worry, 悩(なや)ます describes what causes the worry. Very often used in the passive form 悩(なや)まされる (to be troubled by).

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb ending in す. Transitive — takes を for the person being troubled: Xを悩(なや)ます. The passive 悩(なや)まされる with に marks the cause: Xに悩(なや)まされる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • Xに悩(なや)まされる: to be troubled by X (passive, very common)
  • 相手(あいて)を悩(なや)ます: to give the opponent trouble
  • 長年(ながねん)悩(なや)まされる: to be troubled for many years
