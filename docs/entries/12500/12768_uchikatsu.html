1. to overcome, to triumph over, to conquer
To defeat or prevail against an opponent, difficulty, or adversity. Often used for overcoming internal struggles, hardships, or formidable challenges.
困難(こんなん)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ。
To overcome difficulties.
誘惑(ゆうわく)に打(う)ち勝(か)つのは簡単(かんたん)ではない。
It's not easy to overcome temptation.
彼(かれ)は病気(びょうき)に打(う)ち勝(か)って、再(ふたた)びグラウンドに立(た)った。
He conquered his illness and stood on the field once again.

打(う)ち勝(か)つ means to overcome or triumph over something. It has a strong, dramatic tone and is often used for significant challenges or adversities.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to overcome ~, to triumph over ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 困難(こんなん)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to overcome difficulties
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to resist temptation
  • 逆境(ぎゃっきょう)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to triumph over adversity
  • 病気(びょうき)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to conquer an illness
  • 恐怖(きょうふ)に打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to overcome fear

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 克服(こくふく)する: to overcome (more formal, often for abstract challenges)
  • 勝(か)つ: to win (more general)
  • 乗(の)り越(こ)える: to get over, to surmount
