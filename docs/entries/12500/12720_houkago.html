放課後(ほうかご)

ほうかご
noun
after school
1. after school, after classes
The period of time after classes end for the day. Specifically refers to the time after school dismissal, typically used in the context of elementary through high school.
放課後(ほうかご)に友達(ともだち)と遊(あそ)んだ。
I hung out with friends after school.
放課後(ほうかご)は部活(ぶかつ)の練習(れんしゅう)がある。
I have club practice after school.
放課後(ほうかご)の教室(きょうしつ)に残(のこ)って、先生(せんせい)に質問(しつもん)した。
I stayed in the classroom after school and asked the teacher a question.

USAGE:
放課後(ほうかご) specifically refers to the period after school classes end. It is primarily associated with elementary, middle, and high school contexts — not typically used for university. A culturally significant time in Japanese school life, as many students participate in 部活動(ぶかつどう) (club activities) during this period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 放課後(ほうかご)の教室(きょうしつ): the classroom after school
  • 放課後(ほうかご)に残(のこ)る: to stay after school
  • 放課後(ほうかご)の活動(かつどう): after-school activities
