持論(じろん)

じろん
noun
one's pet theory; personal opinion
1. one's pet theory, personal opinion, cherished view
A strongly held personal opinion or viewpoint that someone consistently maintains and expresses. Often implies the person has held this view for a long time and brings it up regularly.
彼(かれ)の持論(じろん)を述(の)べた。
He stated his personal theory.
早起(はやお)きは健康(けんこう)にいいというのが祖父(そふ)の持論(じろん)だ。
It's my grandfather's pet theory that waking up early is good for your health.
社長(しゃちょう)は「現場(げんば)が一番(いちばん)大事(だいじ)だ」という持論(じろん)を長年(ながねん)展開(てんかい)してきた。
The company president has long advocated his view that the workplace floor is the most important thing.

USAGE:
持論(じろん) always refers to someone's own opinion, typically one they have held for a long time and express repeatedly. It can be neutral or slightly dismissive depending on context — when used about others, it sometimes implies the opinion is idiosyncratic or unproven.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持論(じろん)を述(の)べる: to state one's view
  • 持論(じろん)を展開(てんかい)する: to develop / advocate one's theory
  • 持論(じろん)を曲(ま)げない: to not bend one's opinion
  • ～というのが持論(じろん)だ: one's stance is that ~
