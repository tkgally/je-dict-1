愛(め)でる

めでる
ichidan verb
to admire, to appreciate beauty
1. to admire, to appreciate, to take pleasure in the beauty of
To appreciate or take pleasure in the beauty of nature, art, or other aesthetically pleasing things. A somewhat literary or elevated word that conveys mindful, unhurried appreciation.
花(はな)を愛(め)でる。
To admire the flowers.
月(つき)を愛(め)でながら酒(さけ)を飲(の)んだ。
We drank sake while admiring the moon.
庭(にわ)に咲(さ)いた紅葉(もみじ)を愛(め)でるのが、秋(あき)の楽(たの)しみのひとつだ。
Admiring the autumn leaves blooming in the garden is one of the joys of fall.

愛(め)でる is a literary and refined verb for appreciating beauty, especially in nature.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: ～を愛(め)でる

ASPECT (ている):
愛(め)でている describes the ongoing act of appreciating beauty.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 花(はな)を愛(め)でる: to admire flowers
  • 月(つき)を愛(め)でる: to admire the moon
  • 紅葉(もみじ)を愛(め)でる: to admire autumn leaves
  • 桜(さくら)を愛(め)でる: to admire cherry blossoms

RELATED:
The word 愛(め)でたい (auspicious, happy) is derived from the same root. The noun form 愛(め)で (appreciation, admiration) exists but is rare in modern usage.

REGISTER:
Slightly literary and elevated. In everyday speech, 鑑賞(かんしょう)する (to appreciate) or 楽(たの)しむ (to enjoy) are more common, but 愛(め)でる carries a distinctly Japanese aesthetic sensibility.
