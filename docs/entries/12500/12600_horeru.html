惚(ほ)れる

ほれる
verb (ichidan)
to fall in love, to be charmed
1. to fall in love with, to be smitten by
To develop romantic feelings for someone, typically in a spontaneous and somewhat passionate way. More colloquial and direct than 恋をする.
彼(かれ)に惚(ほ)れてしまった。
I fell for him.
一目(ひとめ)惚(ぼ)れだった。
It was love at first sight.
彼女(かのじょ)の笑顔(えがお)に惚(ほ)れて、付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)めた。
I fell for her smile and we started going out.
2. to be captivated by, to be charmed by
To be deeply impressed or captivated by someone's skill, personality, or some quality of a thing. Not necessarily romantic.
彼(かれ)の腕前(うでまえ)に惚(ほ)れた。
I was captivated by his skill.
この景色(けしき)には惚(ほ)れ惚(ぼ)れする。
This scenery is enchanting.
職人(しょくにん)の見事(みごと)な技(わざ)に惚(ほ)れ込(こ)んで、弟子入(でしい)りした。
I was so captivated by the craftsman's splendid technique that I became his apprentice.

USAGE:
A direct, somewhat informal word for falling in love or being captivated. More spontaneous and passionate than 好(す)きになる. Often implies being struck by sudden attraction.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 一目惚(ひとめぼ)れ: love at first sight
  • 惚(ほ)れ込(こ)む: to fall deeply in love / to be thoroughly captivated
  • 惚(ほ)れ惚(ぼ)れする: to be enchanted, to gaze in admiration
  • 惚(ほ)れた弱(よわ)み: the weakness of being in love

GRAMMAR:
Ichidan verb. Intransitive. Takes に for the object of affection: Xに惚(ほ)れる.
