悪循環(あくじゅんかん)

あくじゅんかん
noun
vicious cycle, vicious circle
1. vicious cycle, vicious circle
A situation in which one problem leads to another, which in turn worsens the original problem, creating a self-reinforcing negative loop.
悪循環(あくじゅんかん)に陥(おちい)る。
To fall into a vicious cycle.
睡眠不足(すいみんぶそく)とストレスの悪循環(あくじゅんかん)を断(た)ち切(き)りたい。
I want to break the vicious cycle of lack of sleep and stress.
人口(じんこう)減少(げんしょう)と経済(けいざい)縮小(しゅくしょう)の悪循環(あくじゅんかん)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)すのは容易(ようい)ではない。
It is not easy to escape the vicious cycle of population decline and economic contraction.

USAGE:
The opposite is 好循環(こうじゅんかん) (virtuous cycle). 悪循環(あくじゅんかん) is common in health, economics, social issues, and everyday conversation about recurring problems.

The word breaks down as 悪(あく) (bad) + 循環(じゅんかん) (circulation, cycle).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪循環(あくじゅんかん)に陥(おちい)る: to fall into a vicious cycle
  • 悪循環(あくじゅんかん)を断(た)ち切(き)る: to break a vicious cycle
  • 悪循環(あくじゅんかん)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す: to escape a vicious cycle
  • 悪循環(あくじゅんかん)を繰(く)り返(かえ)す: to repeat a vicious cycle
