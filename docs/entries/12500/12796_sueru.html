据(す)える

すえる
verb (ichidan)
to set, to place, to install; to fix
1. to set, to place, to install, to position
To place something firmly in a fixed position. Used for installing equipment, placing furniture, or positioning something with care.
大砲(たいほう)を据(す)える。
To set up a cannon.
庭(にわ)に石灯籠(いしどうろう)を据(す)えた。
They placed a stone lantern in the garden.
部屋(へや)の隅(すみ)にエアコンを据(す)えた。
They installed an air conditioner in the corner of the room.
2. to fix, to settle, to focus (one's eyes, resolve)
To fix or steady something abstract — one's gaze, one's resolve, or one's stomach. Often appears in set expressions.
腹(はら)を据(す)えてかかる。
To brace oneself and take it on.
腹(はら)を据(す)える is an idiom meaning to resolve oneself, to steel one's nerves.
目(め)を据(す)えて相手(あいて)をにらんだ。
He fixed his gaze and glared at his opponent.
覚悟(かくご)を据(す)えて、新(あたら)しい挑戦(ちょうせん)に踏(ふ)み出(だ)した。
With firm resolve, I stepped into a new challenge.

据(す)える means to place something firmly and securely, both physically and figuratively.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Intransitive pair: 据(す)わる (to be set, to be settled)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を据(す)える: to set ~, to install ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腹(はら)を据(す)える: to steel oneself, to make up one's mind
  • 目(め)を据(す)える: to fix one's gaze
  • 腰(こし)を据(す)える: to settle in, to commit seriously to something
  • 据(す)え置(お)き: leaving in place; fixed (as in a fixed interest rate)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 置(お)く: to put, to place (more general, everyday)
  • 設置(せっち)する: to install, to set up (formal, institutional)
