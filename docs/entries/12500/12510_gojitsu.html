後日(ごじつ)

ごじつ
noun / adverb
another day, at a later date
1. another day, at a later date, some other time
A future point in time that is not specified. Used when postponing or deferring something, or when referring to events that occurred at some point after a reference point.
詳(くわ)しい説明(せつめい)は後日(ごじつ)いたします。
I will give a detailed explanation at a later date.
後日(ごじつ)、改(あらた)めてご連絡(れんらく)いたします。
I will contact you again on another day.
その事実(じじつ)が後日(ごじつ)になって明(あき)らかになった。
That fact came to light at a later time.

USAGE:
A formal expression used in both spoken and written Japanese. Common in business communication and polite speech. As an adverb, it modifies verbs directly (後日(ごじつ)連絡(れんらく)する). As a noun, it appears with particles (後日(ごじつ)に, 後日(ごじつ)になって).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 後日(ごじつ)改(あらた)めて: on another occasion (polite set phrase)
  • 後日(ごじつ)連絡(れんらく)する: to contact at a later date
  • 後日談(ごじつだん): sequel, epilogue, follow-up story
