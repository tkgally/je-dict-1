憂(うれ)い

うれい
noun
sorrow, grief, anxiety
1. sorrow, grief, melancholy
A deep, lingering sadness or melancholy. A literary word for emotional pain or grief that weighs heavily on the heart.
憂(うれ)いを帯(お)びた表情(ひょうじょう)。
An expression tinged with sorrow.
彼女(かのじょ)の瞳(ひとみ)には深(ふか)い憂(うれ)いが宿(やど)っていた。
A deep sadness dwelt in her eyes.
秋(あき)の夕暮(ゆうぐ)れには、どこか言(い)いようのない憂(うれ)いが漂(ただよ)う。
In the autumn twilight, an indescribable melancholy hangs in the air.
2. anxiety, worry, concern
Worry or concern about future troubles or the state of the world. Often used in the expression 'to have concerns about' or 'the worry of.'
将来(しょうらい)への憂(うれ)いがある。
I have worries about the future.
備(そな)えあれば憂(うれ)いなし。
If you are prepared, you have nothing to worry about.
A well-known proverb meaning "preparedness eliminates worry."
国(くに)の行(ゆ)く末(すえ)を憂(うれ)いている知識人(ちしきじん)は少(すく)なくない。
There are not a few intellectuals who worry about the future of the country.

USAGE:
憂(うれ)い is a literary and elegant word. In sense 1, it describes a deep, poetic sadness — common in literature and artistic contexts. In sense 2, it describes worry or concern, often about larger issues like the state of society or the future.

The verb form 憂(うれ)える means "to grieve over" or "to worry about."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 憂(うれ)いを帯(お)びる: to be tinged with sorrow
  • 憂(うれ)いを含(ふく)む: to contain sadness
  • 備(そな)えあれば憂(うれ)いなし: preparedness eliminates worry (proverb)
  • 後顧(こうこ)の憂(うれ)い: lingering worries about what one leaves behind

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 憂(うれ)い: sorrow, worry (literary, elegant)
  • 悲(かな)しみ: sadness (common, emotional)
  • 哀(かな)しみ: sorrow, pathos (deeper, more poetic than 悲(かな)しみ)
  • 不安(ふあん): anxiety (everyday, practical worry)
