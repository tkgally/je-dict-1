改(あらた)める

あらためる
verb (ichidan)
to reform, to revise; to examine
1. to reform, to revise, to correct
To change something for the better, to amend or correct faults, habits, or systems.
態度(たいど)を改(あらた)める。
To change one's attitude.
法律(ほうりつ)を改(あらた)める必要(ひつよう)がある。
There is a need to revise the law.
生活(せいかつ)習慣(しゅうかん)を改(あらた)めなければ健康(けんこう)に悪(わる)い。
If you don't change your lifestyle habits, it will be bad for your health.
2. to examine, to inspect, to check
To look at something carefully to verify or confirm it. Often used for formal inspections such as checking tickets or identification.
切符(きっぷ)を改(あらた)める。
To inspect tickets.
身分(みぶん)証明書(しょうめいしょ)を改(あらた)めさせていただきます。
I will check your identification.
改(あらた)めて確認(かくにん)したところ、間違(まちが)いが見(み)つかった。
When I checked again, I found a mistake.

USAGE:
An ichidan verb with two distinct meanings. Sense 1 (reform/revise) is the more common meaning in everyday use. Sense 2 (examine/inspect) appears in formal or official contexts. The adverb 改(あらた)めて means "again, anew, afresh" and is extremely common in both spoken and written Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 態度(たいど)を改(あらた)める: to reform one's attitude
  • 名前(なまえ)を改(あらた)める: to change one's name
  • 改(あらた)めて: again, afresh, anew
  • 改(あらた)めてお礼(れい)を申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I would like to express my thanks once again (formal)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 改(かい) appears in many compound words related to change and revision: 改善(かいぜん) (improvement), 改革(かいかく) (reform), 改正(かいせい) (amendment).
