早寝(はやね)

はやね
noun, verb (suru)
going to bed early, early bedtime
1. going to bed early, early bedtime
The practice of going to sleep early in the evening. Often paired with 早起(はやお)き (early rising) as a healthy habit.
今日(きょう)は早寝(はやね)しよう。
Let's go to bed early today.
早寝(はやね)早起(はやお)きは健康(けんこう)にいい。
Going to bed early and rising early is good for your health.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)は早寝(はやね)の習慣(しゅうかん)があったが、大人(おとな)になってからは夜更(よふ)かしが増(ふ)えた。
I had a habit of going to bed early as a child, but since becoming an adult, I've been staying up late more often.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the set phrase 早寝(はやね)早起(はやお)き (early to bed, early to rise). Can be used with する: 早寝(はやね)する (to go to bed early). The proverb 早寝(はやね)早起(はやお)き病(やまい)知(し)らず means "early to bed and early to rise keeps you healthy."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早寝(はやね)早起(はやお)き: early to bed, early to rise
  • 早寝(はやね)する: to go to bed early
  • 早寝(はやね)の習慣(しゅうかん): habit of going to bed early

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夜更(よふ)かし: staying up late (opposite habit)
