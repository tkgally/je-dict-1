技法(ぎほう)

ぎほう
noun
technique; artistic method
1. technique, method, artistic technique
A specialized method or skill used in art, craft, or other creative fields. Emphasizes the technical or methodological aspect of how something is done.
油絵(あぶらえ)の技法(ぎほう)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn oil painting techniques.
この作品(さくひん)には独特(どくとく)の技法(ぎほう)が使(つか)われている。
A unique technique is used in this work.
伝統的(でんとうてき)な染色(せんしょく)技法(ぎほう)を次(つぎ)の世代(せだい)に伝(つた)えていくことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to pass down traditional dyeing techniques to the next generation.

USAGE:
技法(ぎほう) is most commonly used in artistic and creative contexts — painting, music, literature, crafts, etc. It emphasizes the "how" of skilled work. For more general or scientific methods, 技術(ぎじゅつ) or 方法(ほうほう) are more appropriate.

COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ): broader term for technology or skill in general
  • 手法(しゅほう): method or approach, often used in writing or problem-solving
  • 技法(ぎほう): specifically artistic or craft-oriented techniques

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絵画(かいが)技法(ぎほう): painting technique
  • 演奏(えんそう)技法(ぎほう): performance technique
  • 技法(ぎほう)を磨(みが)く: to refine one's technique
  • 技法(ぎほう)を身(み)につける: to acquire a technique
  • 伝統(でんとう)技法(ぎほう): traditional technique
