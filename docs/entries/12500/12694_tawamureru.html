戯(たわむ)れる

たわむれる
verb (ichidan)
to play, to frolic, to flirt
1. to play, to frolic, to romp
To play or frolic in a lighthearted, carefree way. Often used for children or animals playing, or for playful interaction with nature. Has a literary, poetic quality.
子供(こども)たちが水辺(みずべ)で戯(たわむ)れている。
The children are frolicking by the water.
子犬(こいぬ)が草原(そうげん)で蝶(ちょう)と戯(たわむ)れていた。
The puppy was playing with a butterfly in the meadow.
春(はる)の陽気(ようき)に誘(さそ)われて、家族(かぞく)で公園(こうえん)の芝生(しばふ)の上(うえ)で戯(たわむ)れた。
Drawn out by the spring weather, the family frolicked on the park lawn.
2. to flirt, to dally, to trifle
To engage in playful or romantic interaction that is not serious. Can imply being frivolous or not taking something seriously.
女性(じょせい)と戯(たわむ)れる。
To flirt with a woman.
言葉(ことば)と戯(たわむ)れるのが好(す)きだ。
I enjoy playing with words.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は恋(こい)に戯(たわむ)れるばかりで、将来(しょうらい)のことなど考(かんが)えもしなかった。
In my younger days, I just dallied with romance and never thought about the future.

CONJUGATION:
Ichidan verb. 戯(たわむ)れる → 戯(たわむ)れない → 戯(たわむ)れた → 戯(たわむ)れて

USAGE:
戯(たわむ)れる is a literary, elegant verb. In sense 1, it describes lighthearted, carefree play — typically used for children, animals, or adults enjoying nature. In sense 2, it describes flirtation or not taking something seriously.

The noun form 戯(たわむ)れ means "play" or "jest."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)と戯(たわむ)れる: to play in the water
  • 波(なみ)と戯(たわむ)れる: to frolic in the waves
  • 言葉(ことば)と戯(たわむ)れる: to play with words
  • 恋(こい)に戯(たわむ)れる: to dally with love
