敗(やぶ)れる

やぶれる
ichidan verb
to be defeated, to lose
1. to be defeated, to lose
To lose a competition, battle, or contest. Used for sports matches, elections, wars, and other competitive situations.
決勝(けっしょう)で敗(やぶ)れた。
We lost in the final.
選挙(せんきょ)で現職(げんしょく)に敗(やぶ)れた。
He was defeated by the incumbent in the election.
強豪(きょうごう)チームに敗(やぶ)れたが、最後(さいご)まで諦(あきら)めずに戦(たたか)った。
They were defeated by the powerhouse team, but they fought without giving up until the end.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The opponent is marked with に: Xに敗(やぶ)れる (to be defeated by X).

ASPECT (ている):
敗(やぶ)れている describes the resulting state of having been defeated.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xに敗(やぶ)れる: to be defeated by X
  • Xで敗(やぶ)れる: to lose at X (venue/round)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 試合(しあい)に敗(やぶ)れる: to lose a match
  • 選挙(せんきょ)に敗(やぶ)れる: to lose an election
  • 夢(ゆめ)敗(やぶ)れる: to have one's dream shattered

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 負(ま)ける: to lose (more common in everyday speech)
  • 敗(やぶ)れる: to be defeated (more literary/formal)
  • 破(やぶ)れる: to tear, to break (different kanji, different meaning)
