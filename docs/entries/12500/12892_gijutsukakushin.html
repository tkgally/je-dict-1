技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)

ぎじゅつかくしん
noun
technological innovation
1. technological innovation
A significant advance or breakthrough in technology that brings about major changes in industry, society, or daily life. Often used in business, economic, and policy contexts.
技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)が社会(しゃかい)を変(か)える。
Technological innovation changes society.
急速(きゅうそく)な技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)により、新(あたら)しいビジネスが生(う)まれている。
Rapid technological innovation is giving rise to new businesses.
この分野(ぶんや)では技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)のスピードが速(はや)く、数年前(すうねんまえ)の製品(せいひん)はもう時代遅(じだいおく)れになっている。
In this field, the pace of technological innovation is fast, and products from just a few years ago are already outdated.

USAGE:
A compound noun combining 技術(ぎじゅつ) (technology) and 革新(かくしん) (innovation, reform). Frequently appears in news, business reports, and policy discussions. Often used with the English loanword イノベーション as a near-synonym, though 技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん) sounds more formal and traditional.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)を推進(すいしん)する: to promote technological innovation
  • 技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん)のスピード: the pace of innovation
  • 急速(きゅうそく)な技術革新(ぎじゅつかくしん): rapid technological innovation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • イノベーション: innovation (loanword, broader in scope)
  • 革新(かくしん): innovation, reform (not limited to technology)
