悪口(わるぐち)

わるぐち
noun
insult, bad-mouthing, slander
1. insult, bad-mouthing, speaking ill of someone
Negative or malicious talk about a person, often behind their back. Covers everything from mild gossip to harsh verbal abuse.
悪口(わるぐち)を言(い)う。
To bad-mouth someone.
人(ひと)の悪口(わるぐち)ばかり言(い)ってはいけない。
You shouldn't always be bad-mouthing other people.
陰(かげ)で悪口(わるぐち)を言(い)われていたことを知(し)って、とてもショックだった。
I was very shocked to learn that people had been talking behind my back.

READING:
The standard modern reading is わるぐち. The reading わるくち also exists but is less common in everyday speech.

USAGE:
The most common collocation is 悪口(わるぐち)を言(い)う (to say bad things about someone, to bad-mouth). The word often implies talking about someone negatively in their absence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪口(わるぐち)を言(い)う: to bad-mouth, to speak ill of
  • 悪口(わるぐち)を叩(たた)く: to hurl insults (stronger)
  • 陰(かげ)で悪口(わるぐち): talking behind someone's back
  • 悪口(わるぐち)雑言(ぞうごん): verbal abuse, a stream of insults
