成(な)し遂(と)げる

なしとげる
ichidan verb
to accomplish, to achieve, to carry through
1. to accomplish, to achieve, to carry through to completion
To successfully complete a difficult or significant undertaking. Emphasizes the completion of something that required sustained effort or determination.
大(おお)きなことを成(な)し遂(と)げた。
I accomplished something great.
目標(もくひょう)を成(な)し遂(と)げるまで諦(あきら)めない。
I won't give up until I achieve my goal.
困難(こんなん)を乗(の)り越(こ)え、チームは見事(みごと)にプロジェクトを成(な)し遂(と)げた。
Overcoming difficulties, the team brilliantly completed the project.

成(な)し遂(と)げる is a compound verb combining 成(な)す (to accomplish, to form) and 遂(と)げる (to carry through to the end).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: ～を成(な)し遂(と)げる

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 偉業(いぎょう)を成(な)し遂(と)げる: to accomplish a great feat
  • 目標(もくひょう)を成(な)し遂(と)げる: to achieve a goal
  • 使命(しめい)を成(な)し遂(と)げる: to fulfill a mission
  • 改革(かいかく)を成(な)し遂(と)げる: to carry out a reform

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 成(な)し遂(と)げる: to accomplish (emphasizes completion of something significant)
  • 達成(たっせい)する: to achieve, to attain (more neutral, common in business)
  • 果(は)たす: to fulfill, to carry out (duty or promise)
  • やり遂(と)げる: to see through to the end (more colloquial)
