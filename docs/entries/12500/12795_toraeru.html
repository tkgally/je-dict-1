捉(とら)える

とらえる
verb (ichidan)
to grasp, to capture, to perceive
1. to catch, to capture, to seize
To physically catch or seize a person, animal, or moving object.
犯人(はんにん)を捉(とら)えた。
They captured the criminal.
カメラが決定的(けっていてき)な瞬間(しゅんかん)を捉(とら)えた。
The camera captured the decisive moment.
逃(に)げ足(あし)の速(はや)い鳥(とり)を素手(すで)で捉(とら)えるのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to catch a fast-fleeing bird with bare hands.
2. to grasp, to perceive, to understand
To mentally grasp or comprehend something — a concept, a situation, or a phenomenon. To perceive or interpret something in a particular way.
物事(ものごと)を前向(まえむ)きに捉(とら)える。
To perceive things positively.
本質(ほんしつ)を捉(とら)えた指摘(してき)だった。
It was an observation that grasped the essence of the matter.
この問題(もんだい)をどう捉(とら)えるかは、人(ひと)によって違(ちが)う。
How one perceives this issue differs from person to person.

捉(とら)える works both physically (catching, seizing) and mentally (grasping, perceiving). The figurative sense is extremely common in written Japanese.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を捉(とら)える: to catch ~, to grasp ~
  • 〜を〜と捉(とら)える: to perceive ~ as ~
  • 〜として捉(とら)える: to regard as ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本質(ほんしつ)を捉(とら)える: to grasp the essence
  • 物事(ものごと)を捉(とら)える: to perceive things
  • 特徴(とくちょう)を捉(とら)える: to capture the characteristics
  • チャンスを捉(とら)える: to seize an opportunity
  • 瞬間(しゅんかん)を捉(とら)える: to capture a moment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 掴(つか)む: to grab, to seize (more physical, forceful)
  • 捕(つか)まえる: to catch, to arrest (emphasis on apprehending)
  • 把握(はあく)する: to grasp, to comprehend (formal, abstract understanding)
