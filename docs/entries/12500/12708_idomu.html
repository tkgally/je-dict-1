挑(いど)む

いどむ
godan verb
to challenge, to confront, to take on
1. to challenge, to confront, to take on
To boldly face a difficult opponent, task, or situation. Implies courage and determination in the face of difficulty. Often used with に to mark the challenge being faced.
新(あたら)しい課題(かだい)に挑(いど)む。
I take on a new challenge.
世界(せかい)チャンピオンに挑(いど)む若(わか)い選手(せんしゅ)がいる。
There is a young athlete challenging the world champion.
失敗(しっぱい)を恐(おそ)れず、何度(なんど)でも挑(いど)む姿勢(しせい)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to have the attitude of taking on challenges again and again without fearing failure.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The challenge or opponent is marked with に: Xに挑(いど)む.

ASPECT (ている):
挑(いど)んでいる describes an ongoing challenge or confrontation.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xに挑(いど)む: to challenge X, to take on X

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 試合(しあい)に挑(いど)む: to take on a match
  • 記録(きろく)に挑(いど)む: to attempt to break a record
  • 難題(なんだい)に挑(いど)む: to tackle a difficult problem
  • 挑(いど)む姿勢(しせい): a challenging attitude, willingness to take on challenges

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 挑戦(ちょうせん)する: to challenge (suru verb, more common in everyday speech)
  • 立(た)ち向(む)かう: to stand up against, to confront
