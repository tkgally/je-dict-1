散(ち)らばる

ちらばる
godan verb
to be scattered, to be dispersed
1. to be scattered, to be dispersed, to be strewn about
For things to be spread out irregularly across an area, or for people/objects to be distributed widely rather than gathered together.
おもちゃが床(ゆか)に散(ち)らばっている。
Toys are scattered on the floor.
落(お)ち葉(ば)が庭(にわ)に散(ち)らばっていた。
Fallen leaves were strewn across the garden.
住民(じゅうみん)は広(ひろ)い地域(ちいき)に散(ち)らばって暮(く)らしている。
The residents live scattered across a wide area.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 散(ち)らす (to scatter something).

ASPECT (ている):
散(ち)らばっている describes the resulting state of being scattered — things are in a scattered arrangement.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • Xが散(ち)らばる: X is scattered
  • Xに散(ち)らばる: scattered across/in X

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書類(しょるい)が散(ち)らばる: papers are strewn about
  • あちこちに散(ち)らばる: scattered here and there
  • 広範囲(こうはんい)に散(ち)らばる: dispersed over a wide area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 散(ち)らかる: to be messy, in disarray (implies untidiness)
  • 散(ち)らばる: to be scattered, dispersed (more neutral, spatial distribution)
