快感(かいかん)

かいかん
noun
pleasant sensation, pleasure
1. pleasant sensation, pleasure, thrill
A feeling of physical or psychological pleasure or exhilaration. Can refer to sensory enjoyment, the thrill of accomplishment, or any gratifying sensation.
快感(かいかん)を覚(おぼ)えた。
I felt a pleasant sensation.
走(はし)った後(あと)のシャワーは快感(かいかん)だ。
A shower after running is a pleasure.
難(むずか)しい問題(もんだい)を解(と)いたときの快感(かいかん)は何(なに)にも代(か)えがたい。
The thrill of solving a difficult problem is irreplaceable.

USAGE:
快感(かいかん) describes a pleasant, often exhilarating sensation. It can be physical (e.g., refreshing coolness) or psychological (e.g., the rush of achievement). The word carries a slightly stronger connotation than simply "pleasant" — it implies something actively gratifying.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 快感(かいかん)を覚(おぼ)える: to feel a pleasant sensation
  • 快感(かいかん)を得(え)る: to gain pleasure
  • 快感(かいかん)に浸(ひた)る: to bask in pleasure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 快楽(かいらく): pleasure, enjoyment (often hedonistic connotation)
  • 喜(よろこ)び: joy, happiness (more general)
  • 爽快感(そうかいかん): feeling of refreshment
