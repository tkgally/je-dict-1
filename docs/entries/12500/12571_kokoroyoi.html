1. pleasant, agreeable, refreshing
Describes something that feels good physically or emotionally — a pleasant sensation, breeze, sound, or response. Slightly more literary than 気持ちいい.
快(こころよ)い風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
A pleasant breeze is blowing.
快(こころよ)い音楽(おんがく)が流(なが)れていた。
Pleasant music was playing.
朝(あさ)の散歩(さんぽ)は快(こころよ)い疲(つか)れをもたらしてくれる。
A morning walk brings a pleasant tiredness.
2. willing, glad, ready
Describes a willing and positive attitude toward accepting a request or offer. Used when someone agrees to something readily and without reluctance.
快(こころよ)く引(ひ)き受(う)けてくれた。
They gladly agreed to take it on.
お願(ねが)いしたら、快(こころよ)く承諾(しょうだく)してくれた。
When I asked, they readily consented.
先生(せんせい)は快(こころよ)く推薦状(すいせんじょう)を書(か)いてくださった。
The teacher willingly wrote a letter of recommendation for me.

USAGE:
Sense 1 describes physical or sensory pleasantness and tends toward literary usage. Sense 2 is the more common everyday usage — the adverb form 快(こころよ)く followed by a verb of acceptance is a set pattern meaning "gladly, willingly."

GRAMMAR:
I-adjective. The adverb form 快(こころよ)く is used especially for sense 2.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 快(こころよ)く引(ひ)き受(う)ける: to gladly accept
  • 快(こころよ)く承諾(しょうだく)する: to readily consent
  • 快(こころよ)い風(かぜ): a pleasant breeze
  • 快(こころよ)い返事(へんじ): a favorable reply

Antonym: 不快(ふかい) (unpleasant)
