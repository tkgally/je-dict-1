手引(てび)き

てびき
noun
guidance, guide, handbook
1. guidance, instruction, introduction
The act of guiding or leading someone, especially helping them learn or navigate something new.
先輩(せんぱい)の手引(てび)きで仕事(しごと)を覚(おぼ)えた。
I learned the job under the guidance of my senior.
内部(ないぶ)の人間(にんげん)が犯人(はんにん)の手引(てび)きをしたらしい。
It seems an insider helped guide the criminal.
Can also mean to secretly assist someone in wrongdoing.
地元(じもと)の人(ひと)の手引(てび)きがなければ、この場所(ばしょ)にはたどり着(つ)けなかった。
Without the guidance of a local person, I could not have reached this place.
2. guide, handbook, manual
A written document that provides instructions or guidance on how to do something.
利用(りよう)の手引(てび)きを読(よ)む。
To read the usage guide.
入学(にゅうがく)手引(てび)きが届(とど)いた。
The enrollment guide arrived.
初心者(しょしんしゃ)のための手引(てび)きを作成(さくせい)した。
I created a guide for beginners.

手引(てび)き has two main uses: personal guidance (someone leading or helping you) and written guides or manuals.

NOTE: When used for guidance by a person, 手引(てび)き can have a negative connotation — secretly helping someone gain access or commit wrongdoing (similar to English 'inside help').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手引(てび)きをする: to guide, to provide assistance
  • 手引(てび)き書(しょ): guidebook, manual
  • 入門(にゅうもん)の手引(てび)き: introductory guide
  • 利用(りよう)の手引(てび)き: usage guide

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ガイド: guide (loanword, casual)
  • マニュアル: manual (loanword)
  • 案内(あんない): guidance, information
