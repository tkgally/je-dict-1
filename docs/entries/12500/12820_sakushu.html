搾取(さくしゅ)

さくしゅ
noun / suru verb
exploitation; squeezing
1. exploitation, squeezing, extortion
The act of unfairly taking advantage of others for profit or benefit, especially in labor or economic relationships. A strongly negative term implying systematic unfairness.
労働者(ろうどうしゃ)を搾取(さくしゅ)する。
To exploit workers.
低賃金(ていちんぎん)で働(はたら)かせるのは搾取(さくしゅ)だ。
Making people work for low wages is exploitation.
発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)の資源(しげん)が搾取(さくしゅ)されてきた歴史(れきし)がある。
There is a history of developing countries having their resources exploited.

USAGE:
搾取(さくしゅ) carries a strongly negative, critical nuance. It is commonly used in political, economic, and social justice contexts. The word derives from the idea of "squeezing" (like wringing juice from fruit), applied metaphorically to extracting value from people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 労働(ろうどう)搾取(さくしゅ): labor exploitation
  • 搾取(さくしゅ)構造(こうぞう): exploitative structure
  • 搾取(さくしゅ)される: to be exploited (passive)
  • 富(とみ)を搾取(さくしゅ)する: to exploit wealth/resources
