役立(やくだ)てる

やくだてる
verb (ichidan)
to put to use, to make use of
1. to put to use, to make use of
To utilize something effectively for a particular purpose. Implies actively making something serve a useful function.
経験(けいけん)を役立(やくだ)てる。
To put one's experience to use.
留学(りゅうがく)で学(まな)んだことを仕事(しごと)に役立(やくだ)てたい。
I want to put what I learned while studying abroad to use in my work.
この本(ほん)の知識(ちしき)を日常(にちじょう)生活(せいかつ)に役立(やくだ)てることができる。
You can put the knowledge from this book to use in everyday life.

USAGE:
役立(やくだ)てる is transitive — someone actively puts something to use. Compare with the intransitive 役立(やくだ)つ, which means something is useful on its own. 役立(やくだ)てる emphasizes the agent's intentional use of a resource.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)を役立(やくだ)てる: to put experience to use
  • 知識(ちしき)を役立(やくだ)てる: to put knowledge to use
  • 仕事(しごと)に役立(やくだ)てる: to put to use in one's work
  • 社会(しゃかい)に役立(やくだ)てる: to put to use for society
