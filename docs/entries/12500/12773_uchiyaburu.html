打(う)ち破(やぶ)る

うちやぶる
verb (godan)
to break through, to defeat
1. to break through, to smash, to shatter
To forcefully break through a physical barrier, wall, or obstacle.
壁(かべ)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)った。
They broke through the wall.
敵(てき)の防御(ぼうぎょ)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)って前進(ぜんしん)した。
They broke through the enemy's defenses and advanced.
嵐(あらし)で窓(まど)ガラスが打(う)ち破(やぶ)られた。
The storm shattered the window glass.
2. to defeat, to overcome, to break (a record or convention)
To defeat an opponent, overturn an established idea, or break a record. Often used for defeating something considered strong or firmly established.
常識(じょうしき)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る発想(はっそう)だ。
It's an idea that breaks conventional thinking.
若(わか)い選手(せんしゅ)が世界記録(せかいきろく)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)った。
A young athlete broke the world record.
古(ふる)い慣習(かんしゅう)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)って、新(あたら)しい制度(せいど)を導入(どうにゅう)した。
They broke with old customs and introduced a new system.

打(う)ち破(やぶ)る combines 打(う)つ (to strike) with 破(やぶ)る (to tear, break), creating a forceful compound meaning to smash through something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to break through ~, to defeat ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記録(きろく)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to break a record
  • 常識(じょうしき)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to shatter common sense / break with convention
  • 壁(かべ)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to break through a wall (literal or figurative)
  • 敵(てき)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to defeat the enemy
  • 偏見(へんけん)を打(う)ち破(やぶ)る: to break down prejudice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 突(つ)き破(やぶ)る: to pierce through (emphasizes thrusting)
  • 打(う)ち勝(か)つ: to overcome, to triumph over (emphasizes winning, not destruction)
