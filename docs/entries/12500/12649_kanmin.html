官民(かんみん)

かんみん
noun
government and people; public and private sectors
1. government and people, public and private sectors
A compound referring to both the government (public sector) and the civilian population (private sector). Used in contexts about cooperation or relationships between government and the people or between public and private entities.
官民(かんみん)一体(いったい)で取(と)り組(く)む。
To work on it with the public and private sectors united.
官民(かんみん)連携(れんけい)のプロジェクトが始(はじ)まった。
A public-private partnership project has begun.
災害(さいがい)復興(ふっこう)には官民(かんみん)挙(あ)げての協力(きょうりょく)が不可欠(ふかけつ)だ。
Full cooperation from both the public and private sectors is essential for disaster recovery.

USAGE:
官(かん) refers to the government or public sector, and 民(みん) refers to the people or private sector. The word is used almost exclusively in formal, political, or economic contexts.

Often appears as a modifier in compound expressions like 官民(かんみん)連携(れんけい) (public-private cooperation) and 官民(かんみん)一体(いったい) (public-private unity).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 官民(かんみん)連携(れんけい): public-private partnership
  • 官民(かんみん)一体(いったい): public-private unity
  • 官民(かんみん)合同(ごうどう): joint public-private
  • 官民(かんみん)ファンド: public-private fund
