時価(じか)

じか
noun
market price, current price
1. market price, current price
The price of goods, stocks, or property at the current moment, as determined by market conditions. Changes over time based on supply and demand.
この魚(さかな)は時価(じか)です。
This fish is at market price.
Common at sushi restaurants for premium items
土地(とち)の時価(じか)が大幅(おおはば)に上昇(じょうしょう)した。
The market price of the land rose significantly.
株式(かぶしき)の時価(じか)総額(そうがく)で国内(こくない)トップ10に入(はい)る企業(きぎょう)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
The companies in the top 10 domestically by market capitalization were announced.

USAGE:
Commonly seen in two contexts: (1) restaurant menus, where 時価(じか) means the price fluctuates daily (especially for fresh seafood at sushi restaurants), and (2) finance and real estate, where it refers to the current market valuation of assets.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時価(じか)総額(そうがく): market capitalization
  • 時価(じか)評価(ひょうか): mark-to-market valuation
  • 時価(じか)で: at market price

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定価(ていか): list price, retail price (fixed)
  • 相場(そうば): market price, going rate
  • 原価(げんか): cost price
