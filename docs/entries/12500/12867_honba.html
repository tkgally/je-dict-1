本場(ほんば)

ほんば
noun
home, birthplace, authentic place of origin
1. home, birthplace, place of origin
The place where something originates or where the most authentic version is found. Used especially for food, crafts, and cultural practices.
本場(ほんば)の味(あじ)を楽(たの)しみたい。
I want to enjoy the authentic flavor.
イタリアで本場(ほんば)のピザを食(た)べた。
I ate authentic pizza in Italy.
讃岐(さぬき)は本場(ほんば)のうどんで有名(ゆうめい)で、全国(ぜんこく)から観光客(かんこうきゃく)が訪(おとず)れる。
Sanuki is famous for its authentic udon, and tourists visit from all over the country.

USAGE:
Used as a noun modifier with の to mean "authentic" or "from the place of origin." 本場(ほんば)の + noun is a very common pattern, especially when talking about food and regional specialties. Often implies that only the version from the original place is truly genuine.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本場(ほんば)の味(あじ): authentic flavor
  • 本場(ほんば)もの: the real thing, authentic product
  • 本場(ほんば)で食(た)べる: to eat it in its home region
  • 本場(ほんば)仕込(しこ)み: trained in the authentic style
