断言(だんげん)

だんげん
noun / suru verb
declaration, assertion, affirmation
1. declaration, assertion, affirmation
A confident, definitive statement made without hesitation or qualification. Implies the speaker is certain of what they are saying and is willing to commit fully to the claim.
彼(かれ)は無実(むじつ)だと断言(だんげん)した。
He declared his innocence.
成功(せいこう)するとは断言(だんげん)できない。
I cannot say for certain that it will succeed.
専門家(せんもんか)は「この地域(ちいき)に大(おお)きな地震(じしん)が来(く)る」とは断言(だんげん)を避(さ)け、「可能性(かのうせい)がある」という表現(ひょうげん)にとどめた。
The expert avoided making a definitive statement that 'a large earthquake will hit this area,' limiting the expression to 'there is a possibility.'

USAGE:
Used as both a noun and a suru verb. The negative form 断言(だんげん)できない (cannot say for certain) is particularly common, as speakers often hedge their statements. When used in the positive, it conveys strong conviction and personal responsibility for the claim.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 断言(だんげん)する: to declare, to assert
  • 断言(だんげん)できない: cannot say for certain
  • 断言(だんげん)を避(さ)ける: to avoid making a definitive statement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明言(めいげん): clear statement (similar force but less emotional weight)
  • 宣言(せんげん): declaration (more official, public-facing)
