時点(じてん)

じてん
noun
point in time, as of (a certain time)
1. point in time, as of (a certain time)
A specific moment or point on a timeline. Used to indicate when something was true or when a decision was made, often emphasizing that the situation may have changed since then.
その時点(じてん)では分(わ)からなかった。
At that point in time, I didn't know.
現時点(げんじてん)では未定(みてい)です。
As of now, it is undecided.
契約(けいやく)締結(ていけつ)の時点(じてん)で、双方(そうほう)がこの条件(じょうけん)に同意(どうい)していた。
At the time the contract was signed, both parties had agreed to these conditions.

USAGE:

Very common in formal and semi-formal contexts. The most frequent patterns are:

  • Noun + の時点(じてん)で: at the time/point of [noun]
  • その時点(じてん)で: at that point
  • 現時点(げんじてん)で: at the present time, as of now
  • この時点(じてん)で: at this point

Implies that the situation described may no longer be true — it captures a snapshot of a specific moment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現時点(げんじてん): the current point in time
  • その時点(じてん)で: at that point
  • …の時点(じてん)で: as of …, at the time of …

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 段階(だんかい): stage, phase (sequential progression)
  • 瞬間(しゅんかん): moment, instant (very brief)
  • 時期(じき): time, period (broader duration)
