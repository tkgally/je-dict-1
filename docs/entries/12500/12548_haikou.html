廃校(はいこう)

はいこう
noun / suru verb
school closure, closed school
1. school closure, closing of a school; closed school
The permanent closing of a school, typically due to declining student numbers from depopulation. Also refers to the building of a school that has been closed.
少子化(しょうしか)で廃校(はいこう)になる学校(がっこう)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of schools being closed due to the declining birthrate is increasing.
廃校(はいこう)をカフェに改装(かいそう)した。
They renovated a closed school into a café.
地域(ちいき)の人口(じんこう)減少(げんしょう)により廃校(はいこう)が相次(あいつ)ぎ、跡地(あとち)の活用(かつよう)が課題(かだい)となっている。
Due to the regional population decline, school closures have been occurring one after another, and utilization of the former sites has become an issue.

USAGE:
Used both as a noun (廃校(はいこう)になる = to become a closed school) and with する (廃校(はいこう)する = to close a school). In recent years, repurposing 廃校(はいこう) buildings as community centers, art spaces, hotels, or businesses has become a national trend in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 廃校(はいこう)になる: to be closed (a school)
  • 廃校(はいこう)の活用(かつよう): repurposing of closed schools
  • 廃校(はいこう)跡(あと): former school site
  • 廃校(はいこう)舎(しゃ): closed school building
