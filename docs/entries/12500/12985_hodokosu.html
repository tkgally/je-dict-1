施(ほどこ)す

ほどこす
verb (godan)
to apply, to give (charity), to carry out
1. to apply, to perform, to carry out
To apply a treatment, decoration, or process to something. Used for putting something into effect on a target.
壁(かべ)にペンキを施(ほどこ)す。
To apply paint to the wall.
患者(かんじゃ)に応急(おうきゅう)処置(しょち)を施(ほどこ)した。
First aid was administered to the patient.
この建物(たてもの)には精巧(せいこう)な彫刻(ちょうこく)が施(ほどこ)されている。
Elaborate carvings have been applied to this building.
2. to give (charity), to bestow, to provide (aid)
To give to those in need, often implying charity or benevolence. Has a somewhat formal or literary tone.
貧(まず)しい人(ひと)に食事(しょくじ)を施(ほどこ)す。
To give food to the poor.
寺(てら)では困(こま)っている人(ひと)に施(ほどこ)しをしていた。
The temple was giving charity to those in need.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)に医療(いりょう)支援(しえん)を施(ほどこ)すために医師団(いしだん)が派遣(はけん)された。
A medical team was dispatched to provide medical aid to the disaster victims.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive).

USAGE:
Sense 1 takes に to mark the target and を to mark what is applied: 壁(かべ)にペンキを施(ほどこ)す. The passive form 施(ほどこ)されている is common for describing decorations or treatments already in place.
Sense 2 takes に for the recipient: 貧(まず)しい人(ひと)に施(ほどこ)す. The noun form 施(ほどこ)し means "alms" or "charity."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 処置(しょち)を施(ほどこ)す: to administer treatment
  • 装飾(そうしょく)を施(ほどこ)す: to apply decoration
  • 手(て)を施(ほどこ)す: to take measures
  • 施(ほどこ)しをする: to give alms

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加(くわ)える: to add, to apply (general)
  • 与(あた)える: to give (neutral, broader)
