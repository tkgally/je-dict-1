打(う)ちのめす

うちのめす
verb (godan)
to beat senseless, to devastate
1. to beat up, to beat senseless
To physically strike someone repeatedly until they cannot resist. A violent, forceful action.
相手(あいて)を打(う)ちのめした。
He beat his opponent senseless.
チャンピオンは挑戦者(ちょうせんしゃ)を完全(かんぜん)に打(う)ちのめした。
The champion completely pummeled the challenger.
ケンカで打(う)ちのめされて、立(た)ち上(あ)がれなかった。
He was beaten so badly in the fight that he couldn't stand up.
2. to devastate, to crush (emotionally or psychologically)
To deal a severe emotional or psychological blow that leaves someone unable to recover easily. Often used in passive form to describe being overwhelmed by bad news, failure, or grief.
失敗(しっぱい)に打(う)ちのめされた。
I was devastated by the failure.
現実(げんじつ)に打(う)ちのめされて、しばらく何(なに)もできなかった。
Crushed by reality, I couldn't do anything for a while.
あの知(し)らせを聞(き)いて、彼女(かのじょ)はすっかり打(う)ちのめされてしまった。
Upon hearing that news, she was completely devastated.

打(う)ちのめす has both a physical and figurative meaning. The figurative sense (sense 2) is more common in everyday conversation.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を打(う)ちのめす: to beat ~ senseless
  • 〜に打(う)ちのめされる (passive): to be devastated by ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相手(あいて)を打(う)ちのめす: to beat one's opponent
  • 現実(げんじつ)に打(う)ちのめされる: to be crushed by reality
  • 失敗(しっぱい)に打(う)ちのめされる: to be devastated by failure
  • すっかり打(う)ちのめされる: to be completely devastated

NOTE: The passive form 打(う)ちのめされる is especially common with the figurative meaning. The subject is marked with に.
