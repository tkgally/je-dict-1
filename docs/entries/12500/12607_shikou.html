志向(しこう)

しこう
noun, verb (suru)
inclination, orientation, tendency
1. inclination, orientation, tendency toward
A direction of interest or aspiration. Describes what someone or something is oriented toward or aims for. Often used as a suffix-like element in compound expressions.
健康(けんこう)志向(しこう)の人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of health-conscious people is increasing.
若者(わかもの)の安定(あんてい)志向(しこう)が強(つよ)まっている。
Young people's orientation toward stability is strengthening.
この会社(かいしゃ)は海外(かいがい)進出(しんしゅつ)を志向(しこう)している。
This company is oriented toward overseas expansion.

USAGE:
Extremely common as a suffix-like element attached to nouns: X志向(しこう) means "X-oriented" or "tendency toward X." This pattern is productive and can be applied to many nouns.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 健康(けんこう)志向(しこう): health-consciousness
  • 安定(あんてい)志向(しこう): stability-oriented, preference for stability
  • 上昇(じょうしょう)志向(しこう): upward aspiration
  • 自然(しぜん)志向(しこう): nature-oriented
  • ブランド志向(しこう): brand-conscious

SIMILAR WORDS:
Distinguish from 嗜好(しこう) (preference, taste — especially for food, drink, tobacco), 思考(しこう) (thinking, thought process), and 施行(しこう) (enforcement of a law). All are read しこう but have different meanings and kanji.
