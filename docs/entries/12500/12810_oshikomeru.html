押(お)し込(こ)める

おしこめる
ichidan verb
to confine; to shut in; to cram in
1. to confine, to shut in, to lock away
To force someone or something into a confined space and keep them there. Can be used for physical confinement or figurative suppression.
犯人(はんにん)を部屋(へや)に押(お)し込(こ)めた。
They confined the criminal in a room.
不満(ふまん)を心(こころ)の中(なか)に押(お)し込(こ)めていた。
I had been suppressing my dissatisfaction inside.
台風(たいふう)で一日中(いちにちじゅう)家(いえ)に押(お)し込(こ)められた。
I was stuck at home all day because of the typhoon.
2. to cram in, to stuff in, to force into
To push or force something into a tight or limited space.
荷物(にもつ)をスーツケースに押(お)し込(こ)めた。
I crammed the luggage into the suitcase.
大量(たいりょう)の書類(しょるい)を引(ひ)き出(だ)しに押(お)し込(こ)めた。
I stuffed a large amount of documents into the drawer.
狭(せま)い部屋(へや)に十人(じゅうにん)も押(お)し込(こ)められて、息(いき)が苦(くる)しかった。
Ten of us were crammed into a small room, and it was hard to breathe.

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)).

  • Negative: 押(お)し込(こ)めない
  • Polite: 押(お)し込(こ)めます
  • Te-form: 押(お)し込(こ)めて
  • Past: 押(お)し込(こ)めた
  • Passive: 押(お)し込(こ)められる

USAGE:
The passive form 押(お)し込(こ)められる is very common, especially for describing being stuck or confined somewhere against one's will. The figurative usage in sense 1 (suppressing emotions) is also frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部屋(へや)に押(お)し込(こ)める: to confine in a room
  • 感情(かんじょう)を押(お)し込(こ)める: to suppress feelings
  • スーツケースに押(お)し込(こ)める: to cram into a suitcase
