掲(かか)げる

かかげる
verb (ichidan)
to raise, to hold up; to advocate
1. to raise, to hold up, to hoist
To lift something up high so it can be seen — a flag, a sign, a banner, or a light.
国旗(こっき)を掲(かか)げる。
To raise the national flag.
デモ隊(たい)はプラカードを高(たか)く掲(かか)げて行進(こうしん)した。
The demonstrators marched holding placards high.
松明(たいまつ)を掲(かか)げて暗(くら)い道(みち)を進(すす)んだ。
They held up torches and advanced along the dark path.
2. to advocate, to put forward, to espouse
To publicly declare a goal, ideal, policy, or slogan. Used when a person or organization prominently promotes a principle or objective.
平和(へいわ)を目標(もくひょう)に掲(かか)げる。
To advocate peace as a goal.
会社(かいしゃ)は「環境(かんきょう)に優(やさ)しい」をスローガンに掲(かか)げている。
The company espouses "eco-friendly" as its slogan.
改革(かいかく)を掲(かか)げて選挙(せんきょ)に立候補(りっこうほ)した。
He ran for election advocating reform.
3. to publish, to post, to list
To display or publish information in a visible place — a notice, an article, or a list.
新聞(しんぶん)に記事(きじ)が掲(かか)げられた。
An article was published in the newspaper.
掲示板(けいじばん)にお知(し)らせを掲(かか)げた。
A notice was posted on the bulletin board.
以下(いか)に掲(かか)げる条件(じょうけん)を満(み)たす必要(ひつよう)がある。
You must meet the conditions listed below.

掲(かか)げる has three related meanings that all involve making something visible or prominent — physically raising something, publicly declaring principles, or publishing information.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を掲(かか)げる: to raise ~, to advocate ~, to post ~
  • 〜を目標(もくひょう)に掲(かか)げる: to put forward ~ as a goal
  • 〜をスローガンに掲(かか)げる: to adopt ~ as a slogan

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旗(はた)を掲(かか)げる: to raise a flag
  • 理念(りねん)を掲(かか)げる: to espouse a principle
  • 目標(もくひょう)を掲(かか)げる: to set forth a goal
  • 方針(ほうしん)を掲(かか)げる: to put forward a policy
