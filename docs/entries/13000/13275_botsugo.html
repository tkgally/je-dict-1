没後(ぼつご)

ぼつご
noun
after one's death; posthumous
1. after one's death; posthumously
The period following someone's death. Used as a time reference in biographical, artistic, and legal contexts to describe events, recognition, or actions that occurred after a person passed away.
没後(ぼつご)十(じゅう)年(ねん)が経(た)った。
Ten years have passed since their death.
その画家(がか)は没後(ぼつご)に評価(ひょうか)が高(たか)まった。
The painter's reputation grew after their death.
作家(さっか)の没後(ぼつご)、未発表(みはっぴょう)の原稿(げんこう)が遺族(いぞく)によって発見(はっけん)された。
After the author's death, unpublished manuscripts were discovered by the family.

USAGE:
没後(ぼつご) is the formal/written counterpart of 死後(しご) (after death). While 死後(しご) is more general and direct, 没後(ぼつご) is more respectful and commonly used in biographical and artistic contexts. The opposite is 生前(せいぜん) (during one's lifetime). 没後(ぼつご) + a number of years is a standard way to express how long ago someone died (e.g., 没後(ぼつご)百(ひゃく)年(ねん), one hundred years after death).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 没後(ぼつご)～年(ねん): ~ years after death
  • 没後(ぼつご)に評価(ひょうか)される: to be recognized posthumously
  • 没後(ぼつご)の作品(さくひん): posthumous works
  • 没後(ぼつご)出版(しゅっぱん): posthumous publication
