漏(も)れ

もれ
noun
leak; omission
1. leak, leakage
The escape of liquid, gas, light, or information through an unintended opening or breach. Used for both physical leaks (water, gas) and abstract leaks (information, data).
水(みず)漏(も)れがひどい。
The water leak is bad.
情報(じょうほう)漏(も)れが発覚(はっかく)した。
An information leak was discovered.
天井(てんじょう)からの雨(あま)漏(も)れを修理(しゅうり)してもらった。
I had the roof leak fixed.
2. omission, oversight, something left out
Something that was missed or left out from a list, count, or process. Implies an unintentional gap or oversight.
記入(きにゅう)漏(も)れがないか確認(かくにん)する。
Check that nothing has been left unfilled.
リストに漏(も)れがあった。
There was an omission in the list.
申告(しんこく)漏(も)れがないように注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful not to leave anything out of your declaration.

USAGE:
The noun form of the verb 漏(も)れる (to leak). Very productive as a suffix attached to other nouns to describe specific types of leaks or omissions.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 水(みず)漏(も)れ: water leak
  • 雨(あま)漏(も)れ: roof leak (from rain)
  • 光(ひかり)漏(も)れ: light leak
  • 情報(じょうほう)漏(も)れ: information leak
  • 記入(きにゅう)漏(も)れ: omission in filling out a form
  • 申告(しんこく)漏(も)れ: failure to declare/report
  • 抜(ぬ)け漏(も)れ: gaps and omissions
