点在(てんざい)

てんざい
noun / suru verb
scattered, dotted about
1. scattered, dotted about, spread here and there
Things being distributed or scattered across an area at intervals rather than clustered together. Used to describe the spatial distribution of buildings, islands, villages, and similar features.
湖(みずうみ)に小島(こじま)が点在(てんざい)している。
Small islands are scattered across the lake.
古(ふる)い寺院(じいん)が山(やま)の中(なか)に点在(てんざい)する。
Old temples are dotted throughout the mountains.
広大(こうだい)な平原(へいげん)に牧場(ぼくじょう)が点在(てんざい)し、のどかな風景(ふうけい)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Ranches are scattered across the vast plain, creating a peaceful landscape.

USAGE:
A literary and descriptive term. Almost always used in the suru verb form 点在(てんざい)する or its ている form 点在(てんざい)している. The pattern ～に点在(てんざい)する means "to be scattered across (a place)."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 島(しま)が点在(てんざい)する: islands are scattered
  • 集落(しゅうらく)が点在(てんざい)する: settlements are dotted about
  • 遺跡(いせき)が点在(てんざい)する: ruins are scattered
