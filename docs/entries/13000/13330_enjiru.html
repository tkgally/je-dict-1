1. to perform, to play (a role), to act
To portray a character on stage, in film, or in other performances. Also used figuratively for playing a role or part in real life.
彼女(かのじょ)は主役(しゅやく)を演(えん)じた。
She played the lead role.
悪役(あくやく)を演(えん)じるのは難(むずか)しいと言(い)われている。
It's said that playing the villain is difficult.
彼(かれ)はチームの中(なか)で重要(じゅうよう)な役割(やくわり)を演(えん)じている。
He plays an important role within the team.

USAGE:
Takes を to mark the role being performed: 役(やく)を演(えん)じる (to play a role). The figurative usage (役割(やくわり)を演(えん)じる — to play a role/part in something) is also very common.

Related to the noun 演技(えんぎ) (acting, performance). An older variant form is 演(えん)ずる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 役(やく)を演(えん)じる: to play a role
  • 主役(しゅやく)を演(えん)じる: to play the lead
  • 役割(やくわり)を演(えん)じる: to play a part (figurative)
  • 悪役(あくやく)を演(えん)じる: to play the villain
