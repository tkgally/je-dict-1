特有(とくゆう)

とくゆう
noun, adjective-no
peculiar to, characteristic of
1. peculiar to, characteristic of, unique to
A quality, trait, or phenomenon that belongs exclusively or distinctively to a particular person, place, culture, or thing. Implies that the characteristic is not shared by others.
これは日本(にほん)特有(とくゆう)の文化(ぶんか)だ。
This is a culture unique to Japan.
島(しま)特有(とくゆう)の気候(きこう)が観光客(かんこうきゃく)を引(ひ)きつける。
The island's distinctive climate attracts tourists.
思春期(ししゅんき)特有(とくゆう)の悩(なや)みは誰(だれ)にでもあるものだ。
The worries characteristic of adolescence are something everyone has.

GRAMMAR:
Used as a の-adjective, typically in the pattern ～特有(とくゆう)の + noun: 日本(にほん)特有(とくゆう)の習慣(しゅうかん) (customs unique to Japan). The preceding noun is connected directly without a particle.

USAGE:
Often used in academic, cultural, and descriptive writing. Neutral in tone — it simply states that something is distinctive to a particular context, without positive or negative connotation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日本(にほん)特有(とくゆう)の: unique to Japan
  • 地域(ちいき)特有(とくゆう)の: specific to a region
  • 特有(とくゆう)の症状(しょうじょう): characteristic symptoms
  • 特有(とくゆう)の匂(にお)い: distinctive smell
