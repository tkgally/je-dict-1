樹立(じゅりつ)

じゅりつ
noun / suru verb
to establish, to set (a record)
1. establishment, founding, setting (a record)
To firmly establish something significant, such as a policy, record, or system. Carries a sense of creating something lasting and important. Commonly used for setting records in sports or establishing diplomatic relations.
世界(せかい)記録(きろく)を樹立(じゅりつ)した。
A world record was set.
両国(りょうこく)は外交(がいこう)関係(かんけい)を樹立(じゅりつ)した。
The two countries established diplomatic relations.
彼女(かのじょ)はオリンピックで新記録(しんきろく)を樹立(じゅりつ)し、金(きん)メダルを獲得(かくとく)した。
She set a new record at the Olympics and won a gold medal.

USAGE:
A formal suru verb (樹立(じゅりつ)する). Most commonly used for setting records (記録(きろく)を樹立(じゅりつ)する) or establishing formal relations or systems. The kanji 樹(じゅ) (tree) conveys the metaphor of planting something that will stand firm.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記録(きろく)を樹立(じゅりつ)する: to set a record
  • 世界(せかい)記録(きろく)を樹立(じゅりつ)する: to set a world record
  • 外交(がいこう)関係(かんけい)を樹立(じゅりつ)する: to establish diplomatic relations
  • 政権(せいけん)を樹立(じゅりつ)する: to establish a government
