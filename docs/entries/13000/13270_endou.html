沿道(えんどう)

えんどう
noun
roadside; along the road
1. roadside; along the road; route
The area along the side of a road or route. Commonly used to describe spectators, shops, or scenery along a parade route, marathon course, or similar path.
沿道(えんどう)に人(ひと)が集(あつ)まった。
People gathered along the road.
マラソンの沿道(えんどう)から大(おお)きな声援(せいえん)が送(おく)られた。
Loud cheers were sent from the roadside along the marathon route.
桜(さくら)の季節(きせつ)になると、沿道(えんどう)の並木(なみき)が美(うつく)しく咲(さ)き誇(ほこ)る。
When cherry blossom season arrives, the trees along the road bloom beautifully.

USAGE:
沿道(えんどう) specifically means the area along the sides of a road or route. It is very commonly used in news coverage of parades, marathons, and processions to describe where spectators gather. Compare with 道端(みちばた) (roadside), which is more casual and refers to the immediate edge of any road, while 沿道(えんどう) suggests a longer stretch along a specific route.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 沿道(えんどう)の観衆(かんしゅう): spectators along the route
  • 沿道(えんどう)に並(なら)ぶ: to line the roadside
  • 沿道(えんどう)から声援(せいえん)を送(おく)る: to cheer from the roadside
  • 沿道(えんどう)の住民(じゅうみん): residents along the road
