満了(まんりょう)

まんりょう
noun, suru verb
expiration, completion of a term
1. expiration, completion, fulfillment of a term or period
The completion or expiration of a set period of time, such as a contract, lease, prison sentence, or subscription. Implies that the full designated period has been reached, not that something ended prematurely.
契約(けいやく)が満了(まんりょう)した。
The contract has expired.
任期(にんき)満了(まんりょう)に伴(ともな)い退任(たいにん)する。
I will step down upon the expiration of my term.
ビザの有効期限(ゆうこうきげん)が満了(まんりょう)する前(まえ)に更新(こうしん)手続(てつづ)きをしてください。
Please complete the renewal procedures before your visa expires.

USAGE:
A formal, administrative term. Neutral in tone — it simply indicates that the full period has elapsed. In everyday conversation, 切(き)れる (to expire) or 終(お)わる (to end) are more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 契約(けいやく)満了(まんりょう): contract expiration
  • 任期(にんき)満了(まんりょう): expiration of a term of office
  • 期間(きかん)満了(まんりょう): completion of a period
  • 刑期(けいき)満了(まんりょう): completion of a prison sentence
  • 満了(まんりょう)日(び): expiration date
  • 満了(まんりょう)に伴(ともな)い: upon expiration
