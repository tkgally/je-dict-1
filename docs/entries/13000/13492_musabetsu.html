無差別(むさべつ)

むさべつ
noun, na-adjective
indiscriminate
1. indiscriminate, without distinction
Making no distinction between targets, categories, or individuals. Often used in negative contexts such as attacks or criticism, but also in neutral contexts meaning equal treatment regardless of category.
無差別(むさべつ)に攻撃(こうげき)する。
To attack indiscriminately.
無差別(むさべつ)級(きゅう)の試合(しあい)には体重(たいじゅう)制限(せいげん)がない。
There is no weight limit in open-weight matches.
無差別(むさべつ)な伐採(ばっさい)が森林(しんりん)破壊(はかい)の原因(げんいん)になっている。
Indiscriminate logging is causing forest destruction.

USAGE:
Functions as a noun and na-adjective (無差別(むさべつ)な) or adverb (無差別(むさべつ)に). Frequently appears in news and legal contexts. In martial arts, 無差別(むさべつ)級(きゅう) refers to an open-weight category with no weight restrictions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無差別(むさべつ)攻撃(こうげき): indiscriminate attack
  • 無差別(むさべつ)殺人(さつじん): indiscriminate killing
  • 無差別(むさべつ)級(きゅう): open-weight class (martial arts)
  • 無差別(むさべつ)に: indiscriminately
  • 無差別(むさべつ)テロ: indiscriminate terrorism
