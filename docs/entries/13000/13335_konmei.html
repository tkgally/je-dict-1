1. turmoil, confusion, chaos, bewilderment
A state of disorder and uncertainty where a clear direction or resolution is lacking. Typically used for political situations, economic conditions, or social affairs that are in disarray.
政局(せいきょく)が混迷(こんめい)している。
The political situation is in turmoil.
経済(けいざい)の混迷(こんめい)が続(つづ)いている。
The economic turmoil continues.
指導者(しどうしゃ)の辞任(じにん)で党内(とうない)は混迷(こんめい)を深(ふか)めている。
The leader's resignation has deepened the turmoil within the party.

USAGE:
A formal word primarily used in news and political commentary. Describes ongoing, unresolved confusion at a large scale — not typically used for personal confusion or temporary disorder.

The expression 混迷(こんめい)を深(ふか)める (to deepen turmoil) and 混迷(こんめい)を極(きわ)める (to be in extreme turmoil) are very common in news writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 混迷(こんめい)する: to fall into turmoil
  • 混迷(こんめい)を深(ふか)める: to deepen turmoil
  • 混迷(こんめい)を極(きわ)める: to be in extreme turmoil
  • 政治(せいじ)の混迷(こんめい): political turmoil
