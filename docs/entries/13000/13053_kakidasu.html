書(か)き出(だ)す

かきだす
godan verb
to begin writing; to list out, to extract
1. to begin writing, to start writing
To start the act of writing. Used when someone begins composing a letter, essay, or other written work.
手紙(てがみ)を書(か)き出(だ)す。
To begin writing a letter.
ペンを手(て)に取(と)り、小説(しょうせつ)を書(か)き出(だ)した。
I picked up a pen and began writing the novel.
なかなか書(か)き出(だ)せなくて、最初(さいしょ)の一行(いちぎょう)に苦労(くろう)した。
I couldn't get started writing and struggled with the first line.
2. to write out, to list, to extract (in writing)
To write down items from a larger body of information; to list or enumerate things by writing them out.
必要(ひつよう)な情報(じょうほう)を書(か)き出(だ)す。
To write out the necessary information.
問題点(もんだいてん)をすべて書(か)き出(だ)してみた。
I tried listing out all the problems.
まず自分(じぶん)のやりたいことを紙(かみ)に書(か)き出(だ)して、優先(ゆうせん)順位(じゅんい)をつけよう。
First, let's write out what you want to do on paper and prioritize them.

CONJUGATION:

Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)), す-ending.

  • Negative: 書(か)き出(だ)さない
  • Polite: 書(か)き出(だ)します
  • Te-form: 書(か)き出(だ)して
  • Past: 書(か)き出(だ)した

USAGE:
Sense 1 uses 出(だ)す in its auxiliary meaning of "to begin" (like 走(はし)り出(だ)す, 泣(な)き出(だ)す). Sense 2 uses 出(だ)す in its meaning of "to take out / extract," i.e., extracting information by writing it down.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手紙(てがみ)を書(か)き出(だ)す: to start writing a letter
  • 問題点(もんだいてん)を書(か)き出(だ)す: to list out problems
  • 紙(かみ)に書(か)き出(だ)す: to write out on paper
