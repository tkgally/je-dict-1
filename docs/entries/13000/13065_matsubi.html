末尾(まつび)

まつび
noun
end, tail end, last part
1. end, tail end, last part, conclusion
The very end or final portion of a document, list, sequence, or other ordered item. Often used in written or formal contexts.
文(ぶん)の末尾(まつび)に句点(くてん)を打(う)つ。
To put a period at the end of a sentence.
名簿(めいぼ)の末尾(まつび)に名前(なまえ)を追加(ついか)した。
I added a name at the end of the roster.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)の末尾(まつび)に参考(さんこう)文献(ぶんけん)の一覧(いちらん)が載(の)っているので、確認(かくにん)してください。
A list of references is included at the end of the report, so please check it.

USAGE:
末尾(まつび) is a somewhat formal or written term. In everyday speech, 最後(さいご) (last, end) is more common. 末尾(まつび) specifically implies the spatial or sequential end of something, rather than the temporal end.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 末尾(まつび)に追加(ついか)する: to add at the end
  • 末尾(まつび)に記載(きさい)する: to note at the end
  • 文(ぶん)の末尾(まつび): end of a sentence
  • 一覧(いちらん)の末尾(まつび): end of the list

CONTRAST:
末尾(まつび) (end/tail of a document or sequence) vs. 冒頭(ぼうとう) (beginning/opening of a text). These are commonly paired as opposites in written Japanese.
