検定(けんてい)

けんてい
noun, suru verb
certification, official examination
1. certification exam, proficiency test
An officially administered examination that certifies a person's knowledge or skill level in a particular subject. Common in Japan for language proficiency, academic subjects, and traditional arts.
漢字(かんじ)検定(けんてい)を受(う)けた。
I took the kanji proficiency test.
英語(えいご)検定(けんてい)の二(に)級(きゅう)に合格(ごうかく)した。
I passed Level 2 of the English proficiency exam.
来月(らいげつ)の検定(けんてい)試験(しけん)に向(む)けて毎日(まいにち)勉強(べんきょう)している。
I study every day in preparation for next month's certification exam.
2. official inspection, certification
The process of officially examining and certifying that something meets established standards, such as textbook screening or product testing.
教科書(きょうかしょ)の検定(けんてい)が行(おこな)われた。
The textbook screening was conducted.
この製品(せいひん)は安全(あんぜん)検定(けんてい)に通(とお)っている。
This product has passed the safety certification.
国(くに)が定(さだ)めた基準(きじゅん)に基(もと)づいて検定(けんてい)を実施(じっし)する。
The certification is carried out based on standards set by the government.

USAGE:
Often abbreviated in compound names: 漢検(かんけん) (kanji proficiency test), 英検(えいけん) (English proficiency test). The word 検定(けんてい) by itself often implies a proficiency test that one can study for and take at scheduled intervals. Distinct from 試験(しけん), which is a broader term for any exam.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 検定(けんてい)試験(しけん): certification examination
  • 検定(けんてい)に合格(ごうかく)する: to pass a certification
  • 検定(けんてい)を受(う)ける: to take a certification exam
  • 教科書(きょうかしょ)検定(けんてい): textbook screening
