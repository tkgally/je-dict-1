楽(らく)ちん

らくちん
na-adjective
easy, effortless, comfortable
1. easy, effortless, comfortable, cushy
Something that requires very little effort or is physically comfortable. A casual, childish-sounding variant of 楽 (easy/comfortable). Has an endearing, lighthearted tone.
この靴(くつ)は楽(らく)ちんだ。
These shoes are comfortable.
電子(でんし)レンジで作(つく)れるから楽(らく)ちんだよ。
You can make it in the microwave, so it's easy.
在宅(ざいたく)勤務(きんむ)は通勤(つうきん)しなくていいから楽(らく)ちんだけど、運動(うんどう)不足(ぶそく)になりがちだ。
Working from home is easy because you don't have to commute, but you tend to lack exercise.

USAGE:
An informal, friendly variant of 楽(らく). The ちん suffix gives it a cute, playful tone. Commonly used in casual conversation, product descriptions (especially for comfortable clothing and shoes), and advertising. Not appropriate for formal contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 楽(らく)ちんな服(ふく): comfortable clothes
  • 楽(らく)ちんな靴(くつ): comfortable shoes
  • 楽(らく)ちんレシピ: easy recipe

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(らく): easy, comfortable (standard, all registers)
  • 簡単(かんたん): simple, easy (focuses on simplicity rather than comfort)
