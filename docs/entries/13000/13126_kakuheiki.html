核兵器(かくへいき)

かくへいき
noun
nuclear weapons
1. nuclear weapons
Weapons that derive their destructive force from nuclear reactions, either fission or fusion. Includes atomic bombs and hydrogen bombs.
核兵器(かくへいき)の廃絶(はいぜつ)を訴(うった)える。
To call for the abolition of nuclear weapons.
核兵器(かくへいき)の使用(しよう)は許(ゆる)されない。
The use of nuclear weapons is unacceptable.
世界(せかい)から核兵器(かくへいき)をなくすために、多(おお)くの国(くに)が条約(じょうやく)に署名(しょめい)した。
Many countries signed the treaty in order to eliminate nuclear weapons from the world.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 核兵器(かくへいき)の廃絶(はいぜつ): abolition of nuclear weapons
  • 核兵器(かくへいき)の禁止(きんし): prohibition of nuclear weapons
  • 核兵器(かくへいき)を保有(ほゆう)する: to possess nuclear weapons
  • 核兵器(かくへいき)禁止(きんし)条約(じょうやく): Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons

CULTURE:
As the only country to have experienced atomic bombings (広島(ひろしま) and 長崎(ながさき) in 1945), Japan has a particularly strong cultural and political sensitivity toward nuclear weapons. The phrase 非核三原則(ひかくさんげんそく) (Three Non-Nuclear Principles) — not possessing, not producing, and not permitting the introduction of nuclear weapons — is a cornerstone of Japan's postwar policy.

ETYMOLOGY:
核(かく) (nuclear) + 兵器(へいき) (weapons).
