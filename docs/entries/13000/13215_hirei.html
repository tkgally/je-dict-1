比例(ひれい)

ひれい
noun, suru verb
proportion, proportional
1. proportion, being proportional
A mathematical or general relationship where two quantities increase or decrease at the same rate. Also used in a broader sense for things that correlate with each other.
努力(どりょく)に比例(ひれい)して成果(せいか)が出(で)る。
Results come in proportion to effort.
人口(じんこう)の増加(ぞうか)に比例(ひれい)して住宅(じゅうたく)需要(じゅよう)も増(ふ)えている。
Housing demand is increasing in proportion to population growth.
収入(しゅうにゅう)と幸福度(こうふくど)は必(かなら)ずしも比例(ひれい)しない。
Income and happiness are not necessarily proportional.
2. proportional representation (in elections)
Short for proportional representation, an electoral system where seats are allocated in proportion to votes received.
比例(ひれい)代表(だいひょう)制(せい)では小(しょう)さな政党(せいとう)も議席(ぎせき)を得(え)やすい。
Under the proportional representation system, even small parties can easily win seats.
選挙(せんきょ)で比例(ひれい)と小選挙区(しょうせんきょく)の両方(りょうほう)に立候補(りっこうほ)できる。
In the election, one can run as a candidate in both proportional representation and single-seat districts.
今回(こんかい)の選挙(せんきょ)では比例(ひれい)で三(さん)議席(ぎせき)を獲得(かくとく)した。
In this election, they won three seats through proportional representation.

USAGE:
In the general sense, commonly used with に比例(ひれい)して (in proportion to). As a suru verb, it means "to be proportional." In the political sense, it is short for 比例(ひれい)代表(だいひょう) (proportional representation) and is very common in election coverage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • Xに比例(ひれい)して: in proportion to X
  • 正(せい)比例(ひれい): direct proportion
  • 反比例(はんぴれい): inverse proportion
  • 比例(ひれい)代表(だいひょう): proportional representation
  • 比例(ひれい)区(く): proportional representation block
