換(か)える

かえる
ichidan verb
to exchange, to convert
1. to exchange, to convert, to swap
To exchange one thing for another of equivalent or comparable value. Emphasizes conversion or swapping between things of the same category, such as currency exchange or converting units. Distinct from 変(か)える (to change the nature of something) and 代(か)える (to substitute one thing for another).
円(えん)をドルに換(か)える。
To exchange yen for dollars.
古(ふる)い切手(きって)を現金(げんきん)に換(か)えた。
I converted old stamps into cash.
ポイントを商品券(しょうひんけん)に換(か)えることができます。
You can exchange points for gift certificates.

USAGE:

One of several かえる verbs that learners often confuse. The key distinction:

  • 換(か)える: to exchange or convert (swap A for B of comparable value)
  • 変(か)える: to change, to alter (transform the nature of something)
  • 代(か)える: to substitute (use B in place of A)
  • 替(か)える: to replace (put a new one where the old one was)

換(か)える emphasizes the idea of exchange or conversion, especially when value is preserved. It is most natural with currency, units of measurement, and tradeable items.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 両替(りょうがえ): currency exchange (noun form using the same kanji concept)
  • 換算(かんさん)する: to convert (units, figures)
  • 換気(かんき)する: to ventilate (exchange air)
  • ポイントを換(か)える: to redeem points
