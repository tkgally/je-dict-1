民間(みんかん)

みんかん
noun
private sector; civilian
1. private sector; non-governmental; civilian
The sphere of society outside the government, including private businesses, organizations, and ordinary citizens. Used as a noun or as a modifier (民間の) to contrast with public/governmental entities.
民間(みんかん)企業(きぎょう)で働(はたら)いている。
I work at a private company.
政府(せいふ)は民間(みんかん)の力(ちから)を活用(かつよう)する方針(ほうしん)を示(しめ)した。
The government indicated a policy of utilizing private-sector capabilities.
官(かん)から民間(みんかん)への移行(いこう)が進(すす)んでいる。
The shift from public to private sector is progressing.
2. folk; popular; among the people
Traditions, beliefs, remedies, or practices that originate from and are transmitted among ordinary people rather than through official or academic channels.
民間(みんかん)療法(りょうほう)を試(ため)してみた。
I tried a folk remedy.
この地域(ちいき)には民間(みんかん)信仰(しんこう)が今(いま)も根(ね)づいている。
Folk beliefs are still deeply rooted in this region.
民間(みんかん)に伝(つた)わる昔話(むかしばなし)を収集(しゅうしゅう)して本(ほん)にまとめた。
Folk tales passed down among the people were collected and compiled into a book.

USAGE:
民間(みんかん) is most commonly used as a modifier (民間(みんかん)の or in compound form) to contrast with government or official entities. Sense 1 (private sector) is the more frequent usage in modern Japanese, especially in business and political contexts. Sense 2 (folk) appears in cultural and medical contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 民間(みんかん)企業(きぎょう): private company
  • 民間(みんかん)委託(いたく): outsourcing to private sector
  • 民間(みんかん)療法(りょうほう): folk remedy
  • 民間(みんかん)信仰(しんこう): folk belief
  • 民間(みんかん)伝承(でんしょう): folk tradition
