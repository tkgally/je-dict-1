片隅(かたすみ)

かたすみ
noun
corner, nook
1. corner, nook, out-of-the-way spot
A small, tucked-away corner or section of a larger space. Often implies a quiet, inconspicuous, or marginal location. Used both literally for physical spaces and figuratively for peripheral areas of larger domains.
部屋(へや)の片隅(かたすみ)に座(すわ)った。
I sat in a corner of the room.
庭(にわ)の片隅(かたすみ)に小(ちい)さな花(はな)が咲(さ)いていた。
Small flowers were blooming in a corner of the garden.
この世界(せかい)の片隅(かたすみ)で静(しず)かに暮(く)らしたいと思(おも)うことがある。
Sometimes I think I'd like to live quietly in a corner of this world.

USAGE:
Often preceded by a location + の: 部屋(へや)の片隅(かたすみ) (a corner of the room), 街(まち)の片隅(かたすみ) (a corner of the city). Carries a nuance of being small, quiet, or overlooked, unlike 角(かど) (a geometric corner) or 隅(すみ) (a corner of a room). The phrase 世界(せかい)の片隅(かたすみ) (a corner of the world) was popularized by the animated film of the same title.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心(こころ)の片隅(かたすみ): a corner of one's heart
  • 世界(せかい)の片隅(かたすみ): a corner of the world
  • 記憶(きおく)の片隅(かたすみ): a corner of one's memory
  • 街(まち)の片隅(かたすみ): a nook of the city
