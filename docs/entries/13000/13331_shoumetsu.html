消滅(しょうめつ)

しょうめつ
noun, suru verb
extinction, disappearance, annihilation
1. extinction, disappearance, annihilation
Complete disappearance or ceasing to exist. Used for the dying out of species, civilizations, or organizations, as well as the legal termination of rights or obligations.
その文明(ぶんめい)は消滅(しょうめつ)した。
That civilization disappeared.
多(おお)くの言語(げんご)が消滅(しょうめつ)の危機(きき)にある。
Many languages are in danger of extinction.
時効(じこう)が成立(せいりつ)すると、請求権(せいきゅうけん)は消滅(しょうめつ)する。
When the statute of limitations is reached, the right to claim is extinguished.

USAGE:
A formal word with a strong sense of finality — something ceasing to exist completely. More absolute than 消失(しょうしつ) (disappearance), which can imply something simply vanishing from view. 消滅(しょうめつ) implies permanent, irreversible disappearance.

In legal contexts, used for the termination or expiration of rights, contracts, or obligations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 消滅(しょうめつ)する: to become extinct, to disappear
  • 消滅(しょうめつ)の危機(きき): danger of extinction
  • 時効(じこう)消滅(しょうめつ): expiration by statute of limitations
  • 権利(けんり)が消滅(しょうめつ)する: a right is extinguished
