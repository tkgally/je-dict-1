泣(な)きじゃくる

なきじゃくる
godan verb
to sob, to cry convulsively
1. to sob, to cry convulsively
To cry uncontrollably with gasping, hiccupping breaths. Describes intense, convulsive crying where the person can barely catch their breath. Most commonly describes children's crying but also used for adults overwhelmed by emotion.
子(こ)どもが泣(な)きじゃくっていた。
The child was sobbing.
迷子(まいご)の女(おんな)の子(こ)が泣(な)きじゃくっている。
A lost girl is crying uncontrollably.
母親(ははおや)の腕(うで)の中(なか)で泣(な)きじゃくる赤(あか)ちゃんをあやした。
She soothed the baby that was sobbing in its mother's arms.

USAGE:
Describes a very intense, convulsive type of crying characterized by hiccupping and gasping for breath. It is more visceral than simply 泣(な)く (to cry). Most commonly used to describe small children who are crying so hard they cannot stop, but also applies to adults in moments of overwhelming grief or emotion.

The te-form 泣(な)きじゃくって is the most common form encountered, as the word typically describes an ongoing state.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泣(な)く: to cry (general, neutral)
  • 泣(な)き崩(くず)れる: to break down in tears (collapse while crying)
  • すすり泣(な)く: to weep quietly, to sniffle
