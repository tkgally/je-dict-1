本家(ほんけ)

ほんけ
noun
main family; originator
1. main family, head family, parent house
The main or senior branch of a family, as opposed to branch families. In traditional Japanese kinship, the eldest son's line forms the main house, while younger sons establish branch houses.
本家(ほんけ)と分家(ぶんけ)の関係(かんけい)。
The relationship between the main family and a branch family.
正月(しょうがつ)には親戚(しんせき)が本家(ほんけ)に集(あつ)まる。
At New Year's, relatives gather at the main family's house.
祖父(そふ)の代(だい)から続(つづ)く本家(ほんけ)は、今(いま)も田舎(いなか)にある。
The main family home, which has continued since my grandfather's generation, is still in the countryside.
2. originator, original, the real thing
The original or authentic source of something, as opposed to imitators or derivatives. Used colloquially to claim authenticity — e.g., 'the original ramen shop' or 'the birthplace of a certain dish.'
このラーメン屋(や)が本家(ほんけ)だ。
This ramen shop is the original.
本家(ほんけ)本元(ほんもと)の味(あじ)を守(まも)っている。
They preserve the authentic original taste.
数多(かずおお)くの模倣店(もほうてん)が出(で)たが、やはり本家(ほんけ)の味(あじ)にはかなわない。
Many imitator shops appeared, but they still can't match the taste of the original.

USAGE:
Sense 1: In traditional Japanese family structure, the 本家(ほんけ) is the main household of a family line (typically the eldest son's house), while branch households are called 分家(ぶんけ). The main family often keeps the family altar and is the center of family gatherings.

Sense 2: In everyday usage, 本家(ほんけ) often means "the original" or "the authentic one," especially for restaurants, recipes, or cultural practices. The emphatic form 本家(ほんけ)本元(ほんもと) (the true original) reinforces authenticity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本家(ほんけ)と分家(ぶんけ): main family and branch family
  • 本家(ほんけ)本元(ほんもと): the true original
  • 本家(ほんけ)の味(あじ): the original's taste
  • 本家(ほんけ)筋(すじ): the main family line
