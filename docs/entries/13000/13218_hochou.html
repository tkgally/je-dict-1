歩調(ほちょう)

ほちょう
noun
pace, step, cadence
1. pace, step, cadence of walking
The rhythm, speed, or manner of walking. Can refer to literal walking pace.
歩調(ほちょう)を速(はや)める。
To quicken one's pace.
子(こ)どもの歩調(ほちょう)に合(あ)わせてゆっくり歩(ある)いた。
I walked slowly, matching the child's pace.
二人(ふたり)は歩調(ほちょう)を合(あ)わせて並(なら)んで歩(ある)いた。
The two walked side by side, keeping in step with each other.
2. pace, alignment, coordination (of actions)
Used figuratively for the degree to which people, organizations, or countries coordinate their actions or move at the same speed toward a goal.
各国(かっこく)が歩調(ほちょう)を合(あ)わせて対策(たいさく)を取(と)るべきだ。
All countries should act in step with each other to take countermeasures.
チーム内(ない)で歩調(ほちょう)が乱(みだ)れている。
The team is out of sync.
政府(せいふ)と自治体(じちたい)が歩調(ほちょう)を合(あ)わせて復興(ふっこう)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
The national government and local authorities are working in coordination on the recovery.

USAGE:
The expression 歩調(ほちょう)を合(あ)わせる (to keep in step, to coordinate) is by far the most common usage. It works for both literal walking pace and figurative coordination of actions. 歩調(ほちょう)が乱(みだ)れる (to fall out of step) describes a breakdown in coordination.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歩調(ほちょう)を合(あ)わせる: to keep in step, to coordinate
  • 歩調(ほちょう)を速(はや)める: to quicken one's pace
  • 歩調(ほちょう)を緩(ゆる)める: to slow one's pace
  • 歩調(ほちょう)が乱(みだ)れる: to fall out of step

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ペース: pace (loanword, used for speed in general)
  • 足並(あしな)み: step, alignment (足並(あしな)みを揃(そろ)える = to get in line, to coordinate)
