晴(は)れやか

はれやか
na-adjective
bright, cheerful, radiant
1. bright, cheerful, radiant, beaming
Having a bright, clear, and cheerful appearance or expression. Often used to describe a person's face or expression that shows happiness, relief, or pride, especially on a momentous occasion.
晴(は)れやかな笑顔(えがお)を見(み)せた。
She showed a radiant smile.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)で晴(は)れやかな表情(ひょうじょう)の学生(がくせい)たち。
Students with beaming expressions at the graduation ceremony.
長(なが)い闘病(とうびょう)を経(へ)て、彼(かれ)は晴(は)れやかな気持(きも)ちで退院(たいいん)した。
After a long battle with illness, he left the hospital with a bright and cheerful spirit.

USAGE:
Derived from 晴(は)れ (clear weather / auspicious occasion). Used as a na-adjective (晴(は)れやかな). Strongly associated with formal or celebratory occasions such as weddings, graduations, and ceremonies. Conveys a sense of brightness, clarity of spirit, and pride.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 晴(は)れやかな笑顔(えがお): radiant smile
  • 晴(は)れやかな表情(ひょうじょう): beaming expression
  • 晴(は)れやかな気持(きも)ち: bright/cheerful feeling
  • 晴(は)れやかな姿(すがた): radiant figure/appearance
