牽引(けんいん)

けんいん
noun, verb-suru
towing, pulling; leading, driving
1. towing, pulling, traction
Physically pulling or towing something, such as a vehicle or load. Also used in medical contexts for orthopedic traction.
故障(こしょう)車(しゃ)を牽引(けんいん)する。
To tow a broken-down car.
牽引(けんいん)免許(めんきょ)がないと大型(おおがた)トレーラーは運転(うんてん)できない。
You cannot drive a large trailer without a towing license.
骨折(こっせつ)した足(あし)を牽引(けんいん)して治療(ちりょう)している。
The broken leg is being treated with traction.
2. leading, driving, spearheading
Figuratively pulling something forward — leading or driving an effort, industry, or movement. Often used in business and political contexts.
彼(かれ)がチームを牽引(けんいん)した。
He led the team.
輸出(ゆしゅつ)が経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)を牽引(けんいん)している。
Exports are driving economic growth.
新技術(しんぎじゅつ)の開発(かいはつ)で業界(ぎょうかい)を牽引(けんいん)してきた企業(きぎょう)が注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めている。
The company that has been leading the industry through new technology development is attracting attention.

USAGE:
Functions as a noun and suru-verb (牽引(けんいん)する). The kanji 牽(けん) is not in the regular-use kanji list, so this word is sometimes written as けん引(いん). Both senses share the core image of pulling something forward.

In driving contexts, 牽引(けんいん)免許(めんきょ) is a special license category required to tow trailers. In medical contexts, 牽引(けんいん)療法(りょうほう) refers to traction therapy for fractures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 牽引(けんいん)する: to tow; to lead
  • 牽引(けんいん)免許(めんきょ): towing license
  • 牽引(けんいん)力(りょく): pulling power; leadership
  • 牽引(けんいん)役(やく): driving force, leader
