横(よこ)たわる

よこたわる
godan verb
to lie down; to stretch across
1. to lie down, to recline
To lie down in a horizontal position, typically on a bed, sofa, or the ground. More literary than 寝転ぶ or 横になる.
ソファーに横(よこ)たわる。
To lie down on the sofa.
疲(つか)れて芝生(しばふ)の上(うえ)に横(よこ)たわった。
Tired, I lay down on the grass.
ベッドに静(しず)かに横(よこ)たわっている患者(かんじゃ)を見舞(みま)った。
I visited the patient who was lying quietly in bed.
2. to stretch across, to extend, to lie (across a landscape)
For something large like a mountain range, river, plain, or obstacle to extend or stretch across a wide area.
山脈(さんみゃく)が南北(なんぼく)に横(よこ)たわっている。
A mountain range stretches from north to south.
二(ふた)つの国(くに)の間(あいだ)に広大(こうだい)な砂漠(さばく)が横(よこ)たわっている。
A vast desert stretches between the two countries.
実現(じつげん)までには多(おお)くの課題(かだい)が横(よこ)たわっている。
Many challenges lie ahead before realization.

CONJUGATION:

Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)), る row.

  • Negative: 横(よこ)たわらない
  • Polite: 横(よこ)たわります
  • Te-form: 横(よこ)たわって
  • Past: 横(よこ)たわった

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive counterpart is 横(よこ)たえる (to lay something down).

TEIRU FORM:
横(よこ)たわっている describes the state of being in a lying position or stretching across a landscape.

USAGE:
More literary and descriptive than 横(よこ)になる (to lie down, everyday). Often used in novels, descriptive writing, and formal prose. In sense 2, used figuratively for obstacles or challenges that "lie ahead."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ベッドに横(よこ)たわる: to lie down on a bed
  • 平野(へいや)が横(よこ)たわる: a plain stretches out
  • 問題(もんだい)が横(よこ)たわる: a problem lies (ahead)
  • 静(しず)かに横(よこ)たわる: to lie quietly
