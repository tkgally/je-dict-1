母語(ぼご)

ぼご
noun
mother tongue, native language
1. mother tongue, native language, first language
The language a person acquires naturally from birth; one's first language. Used in academic and formal contexts to refer to native linguistic competence.
私(わたし)の母語(ぼご)は日本語(にほんご)です。
My mother tongue is Japanese.
母語(ぼご)の影響(えいきょう)で発音(はつおん)に癖(くせ)がある。
Due to the influence of my mother tongue, I have a quirk in my pronunciation.
母語(ぼご)が異(こと)なる人々(ひとびと)が共通(きょうつう)言語(げんご)として英語(えいご)を使(つか)っている。
People with different mother tongues use English as a common language.

USAGE:
A somewhat academic term. In everyday conversation, 母国語(ぼこくご) (national language of one's home country) is often used interchangeably, though strictly speaking 母語(ぼご) refers to the language acquired from birth regardless of nationality, while 母国語(ぼこくご) refers to the language of one's homeland.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 母語(ぼご)話者(わしゃ): native speaker
  • 母語(ぼご)の影響(えいきょう): influence of one's mother tongue
  • 母語(ぼご)で話(はな)す: to speak in one's mother tongue
  • 母語(ぼご)教育(きょういく): mother tongue education
