枚挙(まいきょ)

まいきょ
noun, suru verb
enumeration, listing one by one
1. enumeration, listing one by one
The act of counting or listing items individually. Almost always appears in the set phrase 枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない (too numerous to list).
例(れい)は枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない。
The examples are too numerous to list.
彼(かれ)の功績(こうせき)は枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない。
His achievements are too numerous to count.
失敗(しっぱい)の原因(げんいん)は枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがないが、最(もっと)も大(おお)きいのは準備(じゅんび)不足(ぶそく)だった。
The causes of the failure are too numerous to list, but the biggest one was insufficient preparation.

USAGE:
Almost exclusively used in the set phrase 枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない (too numerous to enumerate). This is a formal, literary expression meaning there are so many examples, cases, or instances that one could not possibly list them all. The word 暇(いとま) here means "time" or "leisure" — literally, "there is no time to enumerate them all."

FIXED PATTERN:

  • 枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない: too numerous to count/list

NOTE:
This word is rarely used outside of this fixed expression. Learning 枚挙(まいきょ)にいとまがない as a set phrase is the most practical approach.
