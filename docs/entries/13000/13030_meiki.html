明記(めいき)

めいき
noun, suru verb
clearly stating in writing
1. clearly stating in writing, specification
To write something down clearly and explicitly so there is no ambiguity. Used in formal, legal, and business contexts for information that must be recorded precisely.
住所(じゅうしょ)と氏名(しめい)を明記(めいき)してください。
Please clearly write your address and name.
契約書(けいやくしょ)には条件(じょうけん)が明記(めいき)されている。
The conditions are clearly stated in the contract.
応募(おうぼ)の際(さい)は希望(きぼう)職種(しょくしゅ)を明記(めいき)の上(うえ)、履歴書(りれきしょ)を送付(そうふ)してください。
When applying, please send your resume with the desired position clearly noted.

USAGE:
Used as a suru verb (明記(めいき)する) or noun. Very common in instructions on forms, contracts, and official documents. The pattern 明記(めいき)の上(うえ) means 'having clearly stated' and is standard in application instructions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～を明記(めいき)する: to clearly state ~
  • 明記(めいき)の上(うえ): having clearly noted (on documents)
  • ～に明記(めいき)されている: is clearly stated in ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明示(めいじ): explicit indication (showing clearly, not necessarily in writing)
  • 記載(きさい): entry, description (writing down, less emphasis on clarity)
