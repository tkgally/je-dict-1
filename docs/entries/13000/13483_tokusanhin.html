特産品(とくさんひん)

とくさんひん
noun
local specialty product
1. local specialty product, regional specialty
A product that is characteristic of or uniquely associated with a particular region or area. Often refers to food items, crafts, or agricultural products that a region is famous for.
この地域(ちいき)の特産品(とくさんひん)はりんごです。
The local specialty of this region is apples.
駅(えき)のお土産(みやげ)屋(や)で特産品(とくさんひん)を買(か)った。
I bought local specialties at the souvenir shop in the station.
各地(かくち)の特産品(とくさんひん)を集(あつ)めた物産展(ぶっさんてん)が百貨店(ひゃっかてん)で開(ひら)かれている。
A regional products fair showcasing local specialties from around the country is being held at the department store.

USAGE:
A common word in tourism, gift-giving, and regional promotion. Every prefecture and city in Japan actively promotes its 特産品(とくさんひん). The shorter form 特産(とくさん) (local product) is also used, especially as a prefix: 特産(とくさん)物(ぶつ) (specialty goods).

CULTURAL NOTE:
Buying 特産品(とくさんひん) as souvenirs (お土産(おみやげ)) when traveling is deeply ingrained in Japanese culture. Department stores regularly hold 物産展(ぶっさんてん) (regional product fairs) featuring specialties from different prefectures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地元(じもと)の特産品(とくさんひん): local specialty products
  • 特産品(とくさんひん)を買(か)う: to buy local specialties
  • 特産品(とくさんひん)売(う)り場(ば): specialty products section
