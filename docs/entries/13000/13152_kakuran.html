撹乱(かくらん)

かくらん
noun, suru verb
disruption, disturbance, agitation
1. disruption, disturbance, throwing into disorder
The act of disturbing order, stability, or composure. Used for disrupting enemy formations, market stability, public order, or someone's mental state.
敵(てき)の陣形(じんけい)を撹乱(かくらん)した。
We disrupted the enemy's formation.
デマで世論(よろん)を撹乱(かくらん)しようとする勢力(せいりょく)がある。
There are forces trying to throw public opinion into confusion with false rumors.
相手(あいて)チームは撹乱(かくらん)作戦(さくせん)に出(で)て、こちらのペースを乱(みだ)してきた。
The opposing team adopted disruption tactics and threw off our pace.

USAGE:
The kanji 撹(かく) (also written 攪(かく)) means to stir up or agitate, and 乱(らん) means disorder. Together they mean to throw into disorder by agitation. Used in military, political, and strategic contexts, as well as in science (撹乱(かくらん) of ecosystems). The suru verb form 撹乱(かくらん)する takes a direct object with を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 撹乱(かくらん)作戦(さくせん): disruption tactics
  • 撹乱(かくらん)工作(こうさく): sabotage operations
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)を撹乱(かくらん)する: to disrupt order
  • 生態系(せいたいけい)の撹乱(かくらん): ecological disturbance
