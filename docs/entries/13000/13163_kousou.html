構想(こうそう)

こうそう
noun, suru verb
concept, plan, vision
1. concept, plan, vision, idea
A broad plan or vision for something to be created or achieved. Used for large-scale ideas such as urban development plans, novel plots, business strategies, or architectural designs. Implies creative or strategic thinking at a high level.
新(あたら)しい構想(こうそう)を練(ね)る。
To work out a new concept.
小説(しょうせつ)の構想(こうそう)はできているが、まだ書(か)き始(はじ)めていない。
I have the concept for the novel, but I haven't started writing yet.
市長(しちょう)は駅前(えきまえ)の再開発(さいかいはつ)について壮大(そうだい)な構想(こうそう)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The mayor announced a grand vision for the redevelopment of the area in front of the station.

ETYMOLOGY:
構(こう) (to construct, to plan) + 想(そう) (thought, idea). Literally "constructed thought" — a carefully built-up idea.

USAGE:
構想(こうそう) implies a big-picture plan that has been thought through, but not necessarily finalized or implemented. It is commonly used with 練(ね)る (to work out, to refine) and 立(た)てる (to set up). As a する verb, 構想(こうそう)する means "to conceive a plan."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 構想(こうそう)を練(ね)る: to work out / refine a concept
  • 構想(こうそう)を立(た)てる: to formulate a plan
  • 構想(こうそう)を発表(はっぴょう)する: to announce a plan
  • 壮大(そうだい)な構想(こうそう): a grand vision
  • 構想(こうそう)段階(だんかい): planning stage
