治癒(ちゆ)

ちゆ
noun, suru verb
healing, cure, recovery
1. healing, cure, recovery (from illness or injury)
The process or result of an illness or injury being completely healed. A formal, medical term for recovery.
傷(きず)が完全(かんぜん)に治癒(ちゆ)した。
The wound has completely healed.
治癒(ちゆ)までに数(すう)か月(げつ)かかる見込(みこ)みです。
Recovery is expected to take several months.
自然(しぜん)治癒力(ちゆりょく)を高(たか)めるために睡眠(すいみん)をしっかり取(と)りましょう。
Let's get plenty of sleep to boost our natural healing ability.

USAGE:
A formal, often medical term. In everyday conversation, 治(なお)る (to heal, to get better) is more common. 治癒(ちゆ) is used in medical reports, health articles, and formal writing.

The compound 自然治癒力(しぜんちゆりょく) (natural healing power) is widely used in health contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 治癒(ちゆ)する: to heal, to be cured
  • 完全(かんぜん)治癒(ちゆ): complete recovery
  • 自然(しぜん)治癒(ちゆ): natural healing
  • 自然(しぜん)治癒力(ちゆりょく): natural healing ability
  • 治癒(ちゆ)率(りつ): cure rate
