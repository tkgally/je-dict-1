浅(あさ)はか

あさはか
na-adjective
shallow, superficial, thoughtless
1. shallow, superficial, thoughtless, foolish
Lacking depth of thought or consideration. Describes ideas, plans, or behavior that are poorly thought out or naively simplistic.
浅(あさ)はかな考(かんが)えだった。
It was a shallow way of thinking.
浅(あさ)はかな行動(こうどう)を後悔(こうかい)している。
I regret my thoughtless actions.
目先(めさき)の利益(りえき)に飛(と)びつくのは浅(あさ)はかだと言(い)われた。
I was told it was foolish to jump at short-term profits.

USAGE:
A fairly strong negative evaluation, often used to criticize decisions or thinking that lacks foresight or depth. Can be used about one's own past actions in a self-critical way. The word carries a literary or somewhat formal tone.

The kanji form 浅(あさ)はか uses 浅(あさ) (shallow), reinforcing the metaphor of insufficient depth.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 浅(あさ)はかな考(かんが)え: shallow thinking
  • 浅(あさ)はかな行動(こうどう): thoughtless behavior
  • 浅(あさ)はかな判断(はんだん): superficial judgment
  • 浅(あさ)はかな知識(ちしき): superficial knowledge
