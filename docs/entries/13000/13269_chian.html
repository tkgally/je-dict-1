治安(ちあん)

ちあん
noun
public order; public safety
1. public order; public safety; security
The state of peace and order in a society, maintained through law enforcement and social stability. Refers to the overall safety and security of a region or community.
この地域(ちいき)は治安(ちあん)がいい。
This area has good public safety.
夜(よる)の治安(ちあん)が悪化(あっか)しているので注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Public safety at night has worsened, so please be careful.
警察(けいさつ)は地域(ちいき)の治安(ちあん)維持(いじ)のために巡回(じゅんかい)を強化(きょうか)した。
The police increased patrols to maintain public safety in the area.

USAGE:
治安(ちあん) is the standard word for discussing the safety and security of an area. The most common patterns are 治安(ちあん)がいい (safe area) and 治安(ちあん)が悪(わる)い (unsafe area). This is one of the first words travelers and residents learn for evaluating neighborhoods. Japan is often described as a country with excellent 治安(ちあん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 治安(ちあん)がいい: safe, good public order
  • 治安(ちあん)が悪(わる)い: unsafe, poor public order
  • 治安(ちあん)維持(いじ): maintenance of public order
  • 治安(ちあん)が悪化(あっか)する: public safety deteriorates
  • 治安(ちあん)対策(たいさく): security measures
  • 治安(ちあん)部隊(ぶたい): security forces
