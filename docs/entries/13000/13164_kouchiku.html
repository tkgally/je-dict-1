構築(こうちく)

こうちく
noun, suru verb
construction, building, establishing
1. construction, building, establishing
The act of building or constructing something, especially abstract things like systems, relationships, theories, or networks. While it can refer to physical construction, it is most commonly used for building up non-physical things in a systematic way.
新(あたら)しいシステムを構築(こうちく)する。
To build a new system.
信頼(しんらい)関係(かんけい)の構築(こうちく)には時間(じかん)がかかる。
Building a relationship of trust takes time.
会社(かいしゃ)は海外(かいがい)との販売(はんばい)ネットワークを構築(こうちく)するために、各地(かくち)に事務所(じむしょ)を開(ひら)いた。
The company opened offices in various locations to build a sales network overseas.

ETYMOLOGY:
構(こう) (to construct, to set up) + 築(ちく) (to build, to erect). Both characters relate to building, reinforcing the meaning.

USAGE:
Primarily used as a する verb: 構築(こうちく)する. Favored in formal and technical contexts, especially in business, IT, and academic writing. For physical buildings, 建設(けんせつ) or 建築(けんちく) are more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • システムを構築(こうちく)する: to build a system
  • 関係(かんけい)を構築(こうちく)する: to build a relationship
  • ネットワークを構築(こうちく)する: to construct a network
  • 体制(たいせい)を構築(こうちく)する: to establish a framework
  • データベースを構築(こうちく)する: to build a database

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建設(けんせつ): construction (physical structures like roads, buildings)
  • 建築(けんちく): architecture, building construction (physical buildings)
  • 構成(こうせい): composition, structure (how parts make up a whole)
