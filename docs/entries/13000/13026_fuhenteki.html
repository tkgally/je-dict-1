普遍的(ふへんてき)

ふへんてき
na-adjective
universal, ubiquitous
1. universal, ubiquitous, general
Applicable to or affecting all people, places, or situations without exception. Describes values, truths, or phenomena that are not limited to a particular culture, era, or group.
愛(あい)は普遍的(ふへんてき)なテーマだ。
Love is a universal theme.
人権(じんけん)は普遍的(ふへんてき)な価値(かち)である。
Human rights are a universal value.
この物語(ものがたり)は時代(じだい)を超(こ)えた普遍的(ふへんてき)な魅力(みりょく)を持(も)っている。
This story has a universal appeal that transcends time.

USAGE:
Used as a na-adjective (普遍的(ふへんてき)な) or adverbially (普遍的(ふへんてき)に). Common in academic, philosophical, and intellectual discourse. The base noun 普遍(ふへん) (universality) also exists.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 普遍的(ふへんてき)な価値(かち): universal value
  • 普遍的(ふへんてき)な真理(しんり): universal truth
  • 普遍的(ふへんてき)なテーマ: universal theme
  • 普遍的(ふへんてき)な原理(げんり): universal principle
