渾身(こんしん)

こんしん
noun
whole body; with all one's might
1. whole body, with all one's might, putting everything into
Using one's entire body and spirit, pouring all of one's energy and effort into something. Almost always used as a modifier in the pattern 渾身(こんしん)の + noun, describing something done with maximum effort or intensity.
渾身(こんしん)の力(ちから)で投(な)げた。
I threw with all my might.
渾身(こんしん)の一撃(いちげき)を放(はな)った。
He delivered a blow with everything he had.
この作品(さくひん)は作者(さくしゃ)が渾身(こんしん)の力(ちから)を込(こ)めて書(か)いた小説(しょうせつ)だ。
This work is a novel that the author wrote pouring everything into it.

USAGE:
Almost exclusively used in the pattern 渾身(こんしん)の + noun. Rarely appears on its own. Conveys an intense, dramatic quality — the idea that someone has held nothing back.

Common in sports commentary, literary descriptions, and dramatic narratives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 渾身(こんしん)の力(ちから): all one's strength
  • 渾身(こんしん)の一撃(いちげき): a blow with everything behind it
  • 渾身(こんしん)の力(ちから)を込(こ)めて: putting all one's strength into
  • 渾身(こんしん)の作(さく): a work created with full effort
  • 渾身(こんしん)の演技(えんぎ): a performance given with everything
