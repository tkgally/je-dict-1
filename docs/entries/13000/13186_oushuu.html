欧州(おうしゅう)

おうしゅう
noun
Europe
1. Europe
The continent of Europe. A formal, written alternative to ヨーロッパ, commonly used in news, diplomacy, and official contexts.
欧州(おうしゅう)を旅行(りょこう)する。
To travel in Europe.
欧州(おうしゅう)連合(れんごう)は27か国(こく)で構成(こうせい)されている。
The European Union is made up of 27 countries.
欧州(おうしゅう)の経済(けいざい)情勢(じょうせい)が世界(せかい)市場(しじょう)に大(おお)きな影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えている。
The economic situation in Europe is having a significant impact on global markets.

ETYMOLOGY:
欧(おう) (Europe) + 州(しゅう) (continent, state). 欧(おう) is a kanji abbreviation for ヨーロッパ (Europe), derived from the Chinese transliteration.

USAGE:
欧州(おうしゅう) is the formal, written form used in news reports, official documents, and academic writing. ヨーロッパ is the everyday spoken form. In compound words and abbreviations, 欧(おう) is the standard element: 欧米(おうべい) (the West, lit. Europe and America), 欧州(おうしゅう)連合(れんごう) (European Union, abbreviated EU).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 欧州(おうしゅう)連合(れんごう): European Union (EU)
  • 欧州(おうしゅう)各国(かっこく): European countries
  • 欧州(おうしゅう)委員会(いいんかい): European Commission
  • 欧州(おうしゅう)議会(ぎかい): European Parliament
  • 欧米(おうべい): Europe and America, the West

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ヨーロッパ: Europe (everyday, spoken)
  • 欧米(おうべい): the West (Europe and America together)
