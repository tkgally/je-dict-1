洋食(ようしょく)

ようしょく
noun
Western food; Western-style cuisine
1. Western food; Western-style cuisine
Western-influenced dishes as adapted in Japan. Does not typically refer to authentic Western cuisine but rather to a distinctly Japanese style of cooking that originated from Western recipes introduced during the Meiji era. Classic examples include ハンバーグ (hamburger steak), オムライス (omurice), コロッケ (croquettes), and エビフライ (fried shrimp).
今日(きょう)は洋食(ようしょく)が食(た)べたい。
I want to eat Western food today.
この店(みせ)は老舗(しにせ)の洋食(ようしょく)屋(や)で、ハンバーグが人気(にんき)だ。
This is a long-established Western-style restaurant, and their hamburger steak is popular.
明治(めいじ)時代(じだい)に日本(にほん)に伝(つた)わった洋食(ようしょく)は、和食(わしょく)の技法(ぎほう)と融合(ゆうごう)して独自(どくじ)の進化(しんか)を遂(と)げた。
Western cuisine introduced to Japan in the Meiji era merged with Japanese cooking techniques and evolved in its own unique way.

USAGE:
洋食(ようしょく) refers specifically to the Japanese adaptation of Western dishes, not to Western cuisine as served in its countries of origin. It forms a pair with 和食(わしょく) (Japanese food). Typical 洋食(ようしょく) dishes include ハンバーグ (hamburger steak), オムライス (omurice), カレーライス (curry rice), コロッケ (croquettes), and ナポリタン (Napolitan spaghetti). A 洋食(ようしょく)屋(や) is a restaurant specializing in these dishes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洋食(ようしょく)屋(や): Western-style restaurant
  • 洋食(ようしょく)メニュー: Western-style menu
  • 和食(わしょく)と洋食(ようしょく): Japanese and Western food
  • 洋食(ようしょく)レストラン: Western-style restaurant
