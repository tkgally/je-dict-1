1. cremation
The disposal of a dead body by burning. The standard funeral practice in Japan, where the cremation rate is over 99%.
日本(にほん)では火葬(かそう)が一般的(いっぱんてき)だ。
Cremation is standard in Japan.
葬儀(そうぎ)の後(あと)、火葬場(かそうば)に向(む)かった。
After the funeral, we headed to the crematorium.
故人(こじん)の遺志(いし)により、火葬(かそう)は家族(かぞく)だけで静(しず)かに行(おこな)われた。
Following the wishes of the deceased, the cremation was held quietly with only family present.

火葬(かそう) means cremation — the burning of a body after death. Composed of 火(か) (fire) + 葬(そう) (burial/funeral).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 火葬(かそう)する: to cremate
  • 火葬場(かそうば): crematorium
  • 火葬(かそう)許可証(きょかしょう): cremation permit
  • 火葬(かそう)に付(ふ)す: to commit to cremation (formal)

CULTURE:
Japan has one of the highest cremation rates in the world (over 99%). After cremation, family members use special chopsticks to pick bones from the ashes and place them in an urn (骨壺(こつつぼ)). This ritual is called 骨上(こつあ)げ or お骨拾(こつひろ)い.

CONTRAST:

  • 火葬(かそう): cremation
  • 土葬(どそう): burial (rare in modern Japan)

HOMOPHONE:
Do not confuse with 仮想(かそう) (virtual/imaginary) or 下層(かそう) (lower layer).
