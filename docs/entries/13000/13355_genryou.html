1. weight reduction, losing weight, cutting weight
Intentionally reducing body weight, especially in the context of sports (boxing, wrestling, martial arts) where athletes must meet a weight class. Also used more broadly for dieting or reducing the weight/amount of something.
試合(しあい)前(まえ)に減量(げんりょう)する。
To cut weight before a match.
医者(いしゃ)に減量(げんりょう)を勧(すす)められた。
The doctor recommended I lose weight.
ボクサーは計量(けいりょう)に向(む)けて厳(きび)しい減量(げんりょう)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
The boxer is undertaking a strict weight cut for the weigh-in.

USAGE:
Strongly associated with combat sports where athletes must make weight for their weight class. In this context, 減量(げんりょう) refers to the often intense process of cutting weight before a weigh-in.

Also used in medical contexts when a doctor advises a patient to lose weight, and occasionally for reducing the quantity or weight of materials or waste.

Not to be confused with the homophone 原料(げんりょう) (raw materials).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ダイエット: dieting, losing weight (everyday, from English "diet")
  • 痩(や)せる: to lose weight, to get thin (verb, everyday)
  • 体重(たいじゅう)を落(お)とす: to drop weight (colloquial)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 減量(げんりょう)に成功(せいこう)する: to succeed in losing weight
  • 厳(きび)しい減量(げんりょう): strict weight cutting
  • 減量(げんりょう)中(ちゅう): in the process of cutting weight
