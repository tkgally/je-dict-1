沿(そ)う

そう
godan verb
to follow along; to run along; to be in accordance with
1. to run along, to follow along
To extend or move along the length of something, such as a road, river, or coastline.
川(かわ)に沿(そ)って歩(ある)く。
Walk along the river.
海岸線(かいがんせん)に沿(そ)った道(みち)をドライブした。
I drove along the road that runs along the coastline.
線路(せんろ)に沿(そ)って桜(さくら)の木(き)が並(なら)んでいる。
Cherry trees are lined up along the railroad tracks.
2. to be in accordance with, to conform to
To act in line with a plan, policy, wish, or expectation.
方針(ほうしん)に沿(そ)う。
To be in accordance with the policy.
ご希望(きぼう)に沿(そ)えるよう努力(どりょく)します。
I will do my best to meet your wishes.
計画(けいかく)に沿(そ)ってプロジェクトを進(すす)めてください。
Please proceed with the project in accordance with the plan.

USAGE:
Takes the particle に to mark the thing being followed: ～に沿(そ)う. The te-form ～に沿(そ)って is commonly used as an adverbial phrase meaning "along" or "in line with."

The potential negative form 沿(そ)えない is often used in polite refusals: ご期待(きたい)に沿(そ)えず申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません (I am sorry that I cannot meet your expectations).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 川(かわ)に沿(そ)う: to follow a river
  • 道(みち)に沿(そ)う: to follow a road
  • 方針(ほうしん)に沿(そ)う: to be in line with a policy
  • ご希望(きぼう)に沿(そ)う: to meet someone's wishes
  • 計画(けいかく)に沿(そ)って: in accordance with a plan
