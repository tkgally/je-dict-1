濁(にご)り

にごり
noun
cloudiness, muddiness, impurity
1. cloudiness, turbidity, muddiness
The state of a liquid or substance being cloudy, murky, or not clear. Can also describe a dull or impure quality in sound, color, or air.
水(みず)に濁(にご)りがある。
The water has some cloudiness.
雨(あめ)の後(あと)、川(かわ)の濁(にご)りがなかなか取(と)れない。
After the rain, the muddiness of the river wouldn't clear up.
彼(かれ)の目(め)には一点(いってん)の濁(にご)りもなく、澄(す)んだ光(ひかり)を宿(やど)していた。
His eyes held not a trace of cloudiness, shining with a clear light.

USAGE:
The noun form of the verb 濁(にご)る (to become cloudy/muddy). Used for liquids, air, sound, and figuratively for moral or spiritual impurity. Can also refer to the muddiness of tone in a voice or musical instrument.

In linguistics, 濁(にご)り refers to voicing (dakuten), as in the 濁(にご)り点(てん) (゛) added to kana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)の濁(にご)り: cloudiness of water
  • 濁(にご)りのない: clear, pure, unclouded
  • 濁(にご)りが取(と)れる: cloudiness clears
  • 声(こえ)の濁(にご)り: huskiness/roughness in one's voice
