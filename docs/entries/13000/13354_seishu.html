清酒(せいしゅ)

せいしゅ
noun
refined sake, clear sake
1. refined sake, clear sake
Clear, filtered Japanese rice wine. The official term for what is commonly known as 日本酒(にほんしゅ) (nihonshu). Refers specifically to sake that has been filtered and is clear, as opposed to unfiltered cloudy sake (濁(にご)り酒(ざけ)). This is the term used on product labels and in legal/tax classification.
ラベルに清酒(せいしゅ)と書(か)いてある。
It says 'seishu' on the label.
日本(にほん)の清酒(せいしゅ)は海外(かいがい)でも人気(にんき)がある。
Japanese sake is popular overseas as well.
この蔵元(くらもと)は江戸(えど)時代(じだい)から続(つづ)く清酒(せいしゅ)の醸造所(じょうぞうしょ)だ。
This brewery has been producing refined sake since the Edo period.

USAGE:
清酒(せいしゅ) is the formal and legal term for clear, filtered Japanese rice wine. In everyday conversation, 日本酒(にほんしゅ) or simply 酒(さけ) is far more common. 清酒(せいしゅ) appears primarily on product labels, in official documents, and in discussions about brewing.

The character 清(せい) means "clear" or "pure," distinguishing this from 濁(にご)り酒(ざけ) (unfiltered, cloudy sake).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日本酒(にほんしゅ): Japanese sake (everyday term)
  • 酒(さけ): sake; alcohol in general
  • 濁(にご)り酒(ざけ): unfiltered/cloudy sake
  • 地酒(じざけ): local sake
