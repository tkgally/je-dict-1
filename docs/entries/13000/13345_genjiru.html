減(げん)じる

げんじる
ichidan verb
to reduce, to diminish
1. to reduce, to decrease, to diminish
To make something smaller in amount, degree, or intensity. A formal or written-style verb used in official, academic, and journalistic contexts. Can be used both transitively (to reduce something) and intransitively (for something to decrease).
経費(けいひ)を減(げん)じる。
To reduce expenses.
刑(けい)を減(げん)じる措置(そち)がとられた。
Measures were taken to reduce the sentence.
税金(ぜいきん)を一割(いちわり)減(げん)じることが決定(けってい)された。
It was decided to reduce taxes by ten percent.

USAGE:
A formal, written-style verb. In everyday conversation, 減(へ)らす (transitive) or 減(へ)る (intransitive) are far more common. 減(げん)じる appears in legal, administrative, and academic writing.

The variant 減(げん)ずる (classical conjugation) is also used and carries an even more formal tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 刑(けい)を減(げん)じる: to reduce a sentence
  • 税(ぜい)を減(げん)じる: to reduce taxes
  • 経費(けいひ)を減(げん)じる: to reduce expenses
  • 負担(ふたん)を減(げん)じる: to reduce a burden

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 減(へ)らす: to reduce (everyday transitive)
  • 減(へ)る: to decrease (everyday intransitive)
  • 減少(げんしょう)する: to decrease (formal, noun + suru)
