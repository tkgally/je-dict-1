替(か)える

かえる
ichidan verb
to replace, to exchange, to substitute
1. to replace, to exchange, to substitute
To swap one thing for another, typically replacing something worn out, outdated, or unsuitable with a new or different one.
電球(でんきゅう)を替(か)えた。
I replaced the light bulb.
汚(よご)れたシーツを新(あたら)しいのに替(か)えてください。
Please change the dirty sheets for new ones.
そろそろタイヤを冬用(ふゆよう)に替(か)える時期(じき)だ。
It's about time to switch the tires to winter ones.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 替(か)わる.

ASPECT (ている):
替(か)えている indicates an ongoing action of replacing, or that a replacement has been made. The focus is typically on the result.

KANJI USAGE:

Japanese has several かえる verbs with different kanji, each with a distinct nuance:

  • 替(か)える: to replace one thing with another of the same kind (e.g., changing clothes, swapping a battery)
  • 変(か)える: to change the nature or state of something (e.g., changing a plan, altering a schedule)
  • 代(か)える: to substitute one person or thing for another (e.g., a substitute teacher)
  • 換(か)える: to exchange or convert (e.g., exchanging currency, converting units)

In practice, these distinctions are often blurred, and hiragana かえる is frequently used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 服(ふく)を替(か)える: to change clothes
  • 電池(でんち)を替(か)える: to replace batteries
  • 水(みず)を替(か)える: to change the water
  • 気分(きぶん)を替(か)える: to change one's mood
