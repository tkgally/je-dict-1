明(あ)け

あけ
noun
dawn; end (of a period)
1. dawn, daybreak
The time when night ends and day begins; the break of day.
夜明(よあ)けが近(ちか)い。
Dawn is near.
明(あ)けの空(そら)が赤(あか)く染(そ)まっていた。
The dawn sky was dyed red.
明(あ)けの明星(みょうじょう)が東(ひがし)の空(そら)に輝(かがや)いていた。
The morning star was shining in the eastern sky.
2. end of a period, beginning of a new era
The point at which a period or season ends and transitions into the next phase. Used for the end of the rainy season, the new year, and similar transitions.
梅雨明(つゆあ)けを待(ま)っている。
I'm waiting for the end of the rainy season.
年明(としあ)けに会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet after the new year.
連休(れんきゅう)明(あ)けは仕事(しごと)がたまっていて大変(たいへん)だ。
Work piles up after the long holiday, so it's tough.

USAGE:
The noun form of the verb 明(あ)ける (to dawn, to open). In sense 1, most commonly appears in the compound 夜明(よあ)け (dawn, daybreak). In sense 2, it forms many useful compounds:

  • 梅雨明(つゆあ)け: end of the rainy season
  • 年明(としあ)け: the new year, after the new year
  • 連休(れんきゅう)明(あ)け: after a long holiday
  • 正月(しょうがつ)明(あ)け: after New Year's
  • 夏休(なつやす)み明(あ)け: after summer vacation

The sense 2 pattern 〜明(あ)け is very productive and means "right after the end of ~."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夜明(よあ)け: dawn, daybreak
  • 梅雨明(つゆあ)け: end of rainy season
  • 年明(としあ)け: new year
  • 明(あ)けの明星(みょうじょう): the morning star (Venus)
