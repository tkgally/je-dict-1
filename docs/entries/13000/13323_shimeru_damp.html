湿(しめ)る

しめる
verb (godan)
to get damp, to become moist
1. to get damp, to become moist, to become humid
To absorb moisture from the air or surroundings, becoming damp or soggy. Used for objects, air, or conditions becoming humid or wet.
布団(ふとん)が湿(しめ)っている。
The futon is damp.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)は洗濯物(せんたくもの)が湿(しめ)りやすい。
Laundry tends to get damp during the rainy season.
せんべいが湿(しめ)ってしまったので、もう食(た)べられない。
The rice crackers got soggy, so I can't eat them anymore.

USAGE:
A godan verb (五段動詞) ending in -ru. Not to be confused with the ichidan verb 閉(し)める (to close) or 締(し)める (to tighten). Often used in the ～ている form to describe a current damp state.

The transitive counterpart is 湿(しめ)らせる (to dampen, to moisten).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空気(くうき)が湿(しめ)る: the air gets humid
  • 布団(ふとん)が湿(しめ)る: the futon gets damp
  • 湿(しめ)った空気(くうき): damp air
  • 湿(しめ)った土(つち): moist soil
