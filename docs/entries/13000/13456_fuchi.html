淵(ふち)

ふち
noun
deep pool, abyss
1. deep pool, deep spot in a river
A deep, still section of a river or stream where the water is calm on the surface but deep below. Contrasts with 瀬(せ) (shallow, fast-flowing section).
川(かわ)の淵(ふち)で魚(さかな)を釣(つ)る。
I fish in the deep pool of the river.
この辺(あた)りは淵(ふち)になっていて、泳(およ)ぐのは危険(きけん)だ。
This area is a deep pool, so swimming is dangerous.
瀬(せ)と淵(ふち)が交互(こうご)に現(あらわ)れる渓流(けいりゅう)は釣(つ)りの名所(めいしょ)だ。
A mountain stream where shallows and deep pools alternate is a famous fishing spot.
2. abyss, depths
Used figuratively for a deep, dark, or unfathomable state, such as the depths of despair or a perilous situation.
絶望(ぜつぼう)の淵(ふち)に立(た)たされた。
I was driven to the brink of despair.
会社(かいしゃ)は倒産(とうさん)の淵(ふち)にあった。
The company was on the verge of bankruptcy.
戦争(せんそう)の淵(ふち)から辛(かろ)うじて引(ひ)き返(かえ)すことができた。
They barely managed to step back from the brink of war.

USAGE:
In the literal sense, 淵(ふち) refers to a deep, quiet section of a river, as opposed to 瀬(せ) (a shallow, fast-flowing section). The proverb 淵(ふち)瀬(せ)を変(か)える (shallows change to deep pools) describes the unpredictability of fortune.

The figurative sense (brink, verge) is extremely common: ～の淵(ふち) (the brink of ~). This pattern is used with negative situations like 絶望(ぜつぼう)の淵(ふち) (the brink of despair), 死(し)の淵(ふち) (the brink of death), and 破滅(はめつ)の淵(ふち) (the brink of ruin).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶望(ぜつぼう)の淵(ふち): the brink of despair
  • 死(し)の淵(ふち): the brink of death
  • 淵(ふち)に立(た)つ: to stand on the brink
  • 瀬(せ)と淵(ふち): shallows and deep pools
