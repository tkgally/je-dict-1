新調(しんちょう)

しんちょう
noun / suru verb
to get new; to replace with new
1. getting something new, having something newly made
The act of acquiring a new item, especially clothing, furniture, or equipment, often to replace something old or worn out. Implies purchasing or having something custom-made.
スーツを新調(しんちょう)する。
To get a new suit.
入学式(にゅうがくしき)に合(あ)わせて靴(くつ)を新調(しんちょう)した。
I got new shoes in time for the entrance ceremony.
古(ふる)くなったカーテンを新調(しんちょう)しようと思(おも)って、いくつかの店(みせ)を見(み)て回(まわ)った。
I went around several stores, thinking about getting new curtains to replace the old ones.

USAGE:
新調(しんちょう)する implies purposefully acquiring something new, often with a sense of replacing something old or preparing for an occasion. It is most commonly used for clothing, accessories, and household items.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • スーツを新調(しんちょう)する: to get a new suit
  • 着物(きもの)を新調(しんちょう)する: to get a new kimono
  • 家具(かぐ)を新調(しんちょう)する: to get new furniture
  • 制服(せいふく)を新調(しんちょう)する: to get a new uniform

NOTE:
Unlike simply "buying" (買(か)う), 新調(しんちょう)する carries the nuance of replacing something or preparing something fresh for an occasion. It can also imply having something custom-made rather than buying off the shelf.
