浮(う)かべる

うかべる
verb (ichidan)
to float; to show (an expression)
1. to set afloat, to float
To place something on the surface of water or another liquid so that it floats.
池(いけ)に船(ふね)を浮(う)かべた。
I floated a boat on the pond.
お風呂(ふろ)にアヒルのおもちゃを浮(う)かべて遊(あそ)んだ。
I played with a rubber duck floating in the bath.
子(こ)どもたちはプールに色(いろ)とりどりの風船(ふうせん)を浮(う)かべた。
The children floated colorful balloons on the pool.
2. to show, to wear (an expression)
To let a facial expression appear on one's face. Most commonly used with smiles, tears, or other emotional expressions.
彼女(かのじょ)は笑(え)みを浮(う)かべた。
She wore a smile on her face.
彼(かれ)は不安(ふあん)そうな表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべていた。
He had an anxious expression on his face.
目(め)に涙(なみだ)を浮(う)かべながら、感謝(かんしゃ)の言葉(ことば)を述(の)べた。
With tears in her eyes, she expressed her gratitude.

USAGE:
The transitive counterpart of 浮(う)かぶ (to float, to come to mind). Sense 2 is very common in narrative and descriptive writing. The expression 笑(え)みを浮(う)かべる (to wear a smile) is a standard literary collocation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 船(ふね)を浮(う)かべる: to float a boat
  • 笑(え)みを浮(う)かべる: to wear a smile
  • 涙(なみだ)を浮(う)かべる: to have tears (in one's eyes)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべる: to show an expression
