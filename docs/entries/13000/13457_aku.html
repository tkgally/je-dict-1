灰汁(あく)

あく
noun
scum, foam; harshness, bitterness
See also: 悪(あく) (evil (homophone))
1. scum, foam, impurities
The foam or impurities that rise to the surface when boiling meat, vegetables, or other foods. Removing this scum (あく取(と)り) is a basic cooking technique in Japanese cuisine.
スープの灰汁(あく)を取(と)る。
I skim the scum off the soup.
肉(にく)を煮(に)ると灰汁(あく)が出(で)るので、こまめに取(と)り除(のぞ)く。
Scum comes out when you boil meat, so remove it frequently.
灰汁(あく)をしっかり取(と)ると、スープが澄(す)んで美味(おい)しくなる。
If you skim the scum thoroughly, the soup becomes clear and tasty.
2. harsh or astringent taste, bitterness
The unpleasant bitter, astringent, or harsh taste found in certain vegetables and wild plants. Removing this harshness (あく抜(ぬ)き) is necessary before eating such ingredients.
ほうれん草(そう)は灰汁(あく)が強(つよ)い。
Spinach has a strong astringent taste.
わらびは灰汁(あく)抜(ぬ)きをしてから調理(ちょうり)する。
Bracken fern must have its bitterness removed before cooking.
山菜(さんさい)は灰汁(あく)抜(ぬ)きに手間(てま)がかかるが、独特(どくとく)の風味(ふうみ)がある。
Wild mountain vegetables take effort to remove the bitterness, but they have a unique flavor.
3. strong personality, pushiness
Used figuratively to describe a person with a strong, overwhelming, or abrasive personality that can be off-putting to others.
あの人(ひと)は灰汁(あく)が強(つよ)い。
That person has a very strong personality.
彼(かれ)の演技(えんぎ)は灰汁(あく)が強(つよ)いが、独特(どくとく)の魅力(みりょく)がある。
His acting style is intense and overpowering, but it has a unique charm.
灰汁(あく)の強(つよ)いキャラクターばかりの映画(えいが)で、見(み)ていて疲(つか)れた。
The movie was full of overpowering characters, and I found it tiring to watch.

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 灰汁(あく) literally means "ash water" — historically, lye was made by filtering water through wood ash. This is an irregular (ateji) reading; the kanji cannot be read character by character to produce あく.

USAGE:
Often written in hiragana (あく) rather than kanji, especially in cooking contexts. The phrase 灰汁(あく)が強(つよ)い is used across all three senses: strong scum, strong bitterness, or strong personality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 灰汁(あく)を取(と)る: to skim scum
  • 灰汁(あく)抜(ぬ)き: removing bitterness/harshness
  • 灰汁(あく)が強(つよ)い: has strong scum/bitterness/personality
  • 灰汁(あく)取(と)り: scum skimmer (tool or action)
