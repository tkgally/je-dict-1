決(き)め込(こ)む

きめこむ
godan verb
to assume; to pretend
1. to assume, to take for granted, to decide firmly
To decide something firmly without proper evidence or consideration. Often implies an unfounded or premature assumption.
安全(あんぜん)だと決(き)め込(こ)んでいた。
I had assumed it was safe.
彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないと決(き)め込(こ)んでいた。
I had assumed he wouldn't come.
大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)だと決(き)め込(こ)んで準備(じゅんび)を怠(おこた)った。
I neglected preparations, having assumed everything would be fine.
2. to pretend, to feign, to play (a role)
To deliberately adopt a particular attitude or pretend to be in a certain state, often to avoid responsibility or confrontation. Commonly used with states like sleeping, ignorance, or innocence.
寝(ね)たふりを決(き)め込(こ)む。
To pretend to be asleep.
知(し)らないふりを決(き)め込(こ)んだ。
I played dumb.
彼(かれ)は何(なに)を聞(き)かれても黙(だま)りを決(き)め込(こ)んでいる。
No matter what he's asked, he insists on keeping silent.

USAGE:
A compound verb combining 決(き)める (to decide) and 込(こ)む (into, thoroughly). The 込(こ)む suffix adds nuance of firmly settling into a state.

Sense 1 often implies the assumption was unwarranted or led to problems. Sense 2 is very common in the pattern Xを決(き)め込(こ)む, where X is a pretended state.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 寝(ね)たふりを決(き)め込(こ)む: to pretend to be asleep
  • 知(し)らないふりを決(き)め込(こ)む: to play dumb
  • 黙(だま)りを決(き)め込(こ)む: to clam up, to insist on silence
  • 高(たか)をくくって決(き)め込(こ)む: to take something lightly and assume
