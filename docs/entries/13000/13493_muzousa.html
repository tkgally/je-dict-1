無造作(むぞうさ)

むぞうさ
na-adjective
casual, nonchalant, careless
1. casual, nonchalant, effortless
Doing something without fuss, elaboration, or special care. Can describe a relaxed, unstudied manner that appears natural, or a lack of proper attention depending on context.
無造作(むぞうさ)に髪(かみ)をまとめた。
She casually tied up her hair.
彼(かれ)は無造作(むぞうさ)に書類(しょるい)を机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に置(お)いた。
He carelessly placed the documents on the desk.
無造作(むぞうさ)に積(つ)み上(あ)げられた段(だん)ボール箱(ばこ)が倒(たお)れそうだった。
The haphazardly stacked cardboard boxes looked like they were about to topple.

USAGE:
Most commonly used adverbially as 無造作(むぞうさ)に, less often as a na-adjective (無造作(むぞうさ)な). The word can carry either a positive nuance (effortless, natural) or negative nuance (careless, sloppy) depending on context. In fashion and hairstyling, 無造作(むぞうさ) describes a deliberately casual, unstudied look.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無造作(むぞうさ)に置(お)く: to place carelessly
  • 無造作(むぞうさ)な髪型(かみがた): effortlessly casual hairstyle
  • 無造作(むぞうさ)に積(つ)む: to pile up haphazardly
  • 無造作(むぞうさ)ヘア: tousled hair (fashion term)
