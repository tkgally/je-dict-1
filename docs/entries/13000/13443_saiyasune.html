最安値(さいやすね)

さいやすね
noun
lowest price, all-time low
1. lowest price, record low price
The lowest price recorded for a product, stock, currency, or commodity. Commonly used in financial markets and online shopping to indicate the cheapest available price or a historical price floor.
最安値(さいやすね)を更新(こうしん)した。
It hit a new record low.
このサイトでは各(かく)ショップの最安値(さいやすね)を比較(ひかく)できる。
On this site you can compare the lowest prices from each shop.
円(えん)が対(たい)ドルで年初来(ねんしょらい)の最安値(さいやすね)をつけ、市場(しじょう)に不安(ふあん)が広(ひろ)がった。
The yen hit its lowest level against the dollar for the year, spreading anxiety in the market.

USAGE:
Used in two main contexts: (1) financial markets, where it refers to a stock, currency, or commodity reaching its lowest recorded price; and (2) online shopping and price comparison, where it means the cheapest price currently available across retailers.

The opposite is 最高値(さいたかね) (highest price, record high).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最安値(さいやすね)を更新(こうしん)する: to hit a new low
  • 最安値(さいやすね)をつける: to reach the lowest price
  • 年初来(ねんしょらい)最安値(さいやすね): year-to-date low
  • 過去(かこ)最安値(さいやすね): all-time lowest price
