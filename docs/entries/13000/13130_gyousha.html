業者(ぎょうしゃ)

ぎょうしゃ
noun
vendor, contractor, trader
1. vendor, contractor, trader
A person or company engaged in a particular trade or business. Often refers to outside contractors, suppliers, or service providers rather than individual employees.
業者(ぎょうしゃ)に見積(みつ)もりを頼(たの)んだ。
I asked the contractor for an estimate.
悪質(あくしつ)な業者(ぎょうしゃ)に騙(だま)されないように注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful not to be deceived by dishonest vendors.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの業者(ぎょうしゃ)を何社(なんしゃ)か比較(ひかく)してから決(き)めたほうがいい。
You should compare several moving companies before deciding.

USAGE:
Refers to an outside business entity such as a contractor, supplier, or vendor. Often used when the specific company name is unimportant — the focus is on their role. Can have a slightly impersonal or transactional nuance. 悪徳(あくとく)業者(ぎょうしゃ) (unscrupulous dealer) is a common cautionary phrase.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し業者(ぎょうしゃ): moving company
  • 悪質(あくしつ)な業者(ぎょうしゃ): dishonest vendor
  • 業者(ぎょうしゃ)に依頼(いらい)する: to hire a contractor
  • 仲介(ちゅうかい)業者(ぎょうしゃ): broker, intermediary
