切(き)り出(だ)す

きりだす
verb (godan, transitive)
to cut out; to quarry; to bring up (a topic)
1. to cut out; to quarry; to extract
To cut something out from a larger whole, such as cutting stone from a quarry or extracting material.
山(やま)から石(いし)を切(き)り出(だ)す。
They quarry stone from the mountain.
2. to bring up (a topic); to broach
To start talking about something, especially a difficult or delicate subject.
話(はなし)を切(き)り出(だ)すタイミングが難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to find the right timing to bring up the topic.
彼女(かのじょ)は別(わか)れ話(ばなし)を切(き)り出(だ)した。
She brought up the subject of breaking up.

CONJUGATION:

  • 切(き)り出(だ)す → 切(き)り出(だ)さない (negative)
  • 切(き)り出(だ)す → 切(き)り出(だ)して (te-form)
  • 切(き)り出(だ)す → 切(き)り出(だ)した (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 切(き)る (to cut) + 出(だ)す (to take out, to begin)

USAGE:

Sense 2 (broaching a topic) is more common in everyday conversation:

  • 話(はなし)を切(き)り出(だ)す (to bring up a topic)
  • 本題(ほんだい)を切(き)り出(だ)す (to get to the main point)
  • なかなか切(き)り出(だ)せない (unable to bring oneself to bring it up)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 持(も)ち出(だ)す - to bring up, to take out
  • 言(い)い出(だ)す - to start saying
