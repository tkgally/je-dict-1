1. to throw in; to toss into
To throw something carelessly or forcefully into a container or space.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を洗濯機(せんたくき)に放(ほう)り込(こ)んだ。
I tossed the laundry into the washing machine.
ゴミを袋(ふくろ)に放(ほう)り込(こ)む。
I throw the garbage into the bag.
鍋(なべ)に野菜(やさい)を次々(つぎつぎ)と放(ほう)り込(こ)んだ。
I threw vegetables into the pot one after another.
荷物(にもつ)を車(くるま)のトランクに放(ほう)り込(こ)んで出発(しゅっぱつ)した。
I tossed the luggage into the car trunk and set off.
急(いそ)いでいたので、教科書(きょうかしょ)をカバンに放(ほう)り込(こ)んで家(いえ)を出(で)た。
I was in a hurry, so I threw my textbook into my bag and left the house.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 放(ほう)る (to throw) + the compound-forming suffix 込(こ)む, which adds the sense "in, inward". This 込(こ)む is a different word from the independent verb 込(こ)む (to be crowded) and has no entry of its own.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 〜を〜に放(ほう)り込(こ)む - throw something into somewhere; the destination takes に rather than へ, because the point of arrival matters, not the direction

ASPECT:
放(ほう)り込(こ)んでいる normally reads as a repeated or in-progress action, not as a resulting state. To describe the thing as already sitting in the container, use the passive 放(ほう)り込(こ)まれている.

USAGE:

Implies carelessness, haste, or force:

  • カバンに放(ほう)り込(こ)む (to toss into a bag)
  • 車(くるま)に放(ほう)り込(こ)む (to throw into the car)
  • 牢屋(ろうや)に放(ほう)り込(こ)む (to throw in jail)

The last is figurative: with a person as the object, the verb says the person was put somewhere unpleasant with no regard for their wishes.

NUANCE:
More casual and forceful than 入(い)れる, which is neutral about manner. Compare 放(ほう)り出(だ)す, the same base verb with the opposite direction (to throw out, to abandon).

Related Words

Related: 放(ほう)る (to throw, to toss (base verb))
Contrast: 入(い)れる (to put in (carefully))
Related: 放(ほう)り出(だ)す (to throw out (opposite direction))
