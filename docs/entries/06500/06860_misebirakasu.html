1. to show off; to flaunt
To display something conspicuously to impress others, often with a sense of boasting.
新(あたら)しい車(くるま)を見(み)せびらかしている。
He's showing off his new car.
SNSで高級(こうきゅう)ブランドを見(み)せびらかすのは品(ひん)がない。
It's tacky to show off luxury brands on social media.
知識(ちしき)を見(み)せびらかすのは嫌(きら)われる。
Flaunting your knowledge makes people dislike you.
子(こ)どもが新(あたら)しいおもちゃを友達(ともだち)に見(み)せびらかしていた。
The child was showing off the new toy to friends.
彼(かれ)は海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)の写真(しゃしん)をみんなに見(み)せびらかして自慢(じまん)していた。
He was showing off his overseas travel photos to everyone and bragging.

FORMATION:
Built on 見(み)せる (to show) plus the bound element びらかす, which contributes the idea of spreading something out for others to look at. びらかす does not occur as a word on its own.

REGISTER:
Always unflattering. The speaker is criticising the display rather than admiring what is displayed — the word carries vanity, boastfulness, and indifference to how the audience feels. Speakers use it of their own behaviour only self-deprecatingly.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 財産(ざいさん)を見(み)せびらかす — to flaunt wealth
  • 成功(せいこう)を見(み)せびらかす — to show off one's success
  • 能力(のうりょく)を見(み)せびらかす — to show off one's abilities

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自慢(じまん)する is boasting in words; 見(み)せびらかす is boasting by putting the thing itself in front of people.
  • 誇(ほこ)る is neutral to positive — to be proud of something that genuinely merits pride.
  • 見栄(みえ) is the vanity that motivates the behaviour, the noun a speaker reaches for when explaining why someone shows off.
