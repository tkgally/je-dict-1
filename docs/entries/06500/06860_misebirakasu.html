見(み)せびらかす

みせびらかす
verb (godan, transitive)
to show off; to flaunt; to display proudly

Definitions

1. to show off; to flaunt
To display something conspicuously to impress others, often with a sense of boasting.

Examples

新(あたら)しい車(くるま)を見(み)せびらかしている。
He's showing off his new car.
SNSで高級(こうきゅう)ブランド(ぶらんど)を見(み)せびらかすのは品(ひん)がない。
It's tacky to show off luxury brands on social media.
知識(ちしき)を見(み)せびらかすのは嫌(きら)われる。
Flaunting your knowledge makes people dislike you.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 見(み)せびらかす → 見(み)せびらかさない (negative)
  • 見(み)せびらかす → 見(み)せびらかして (te-form)
  • 見(み)せびらかす → 見(み)せびらかした (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 見(み)せる (to show) + びらかす (to spread out/display)

REGISTER:
Slightly negative connotation. Implies vanity or boastfulness. Used critically to describe someone's behavior.

USAGE:

  • 財産(ざいさん)を見(み)せびらかす (to flaunt wealth)
  • 成功(せいこう)を見(み)せびらかす (to show off success)
  • 能力(のうりょく)を見(み)せびらかす (to show off one's abilities)
