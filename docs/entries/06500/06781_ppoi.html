っぽい

っぽい
suffix
-ish; -like; having the characteristics of
1. -ish; -like; having the characteristics of; tending toward
Productive suffix attaching to nouns, verb stems, and adjective stems.
この色(いろ)、ちょっとピンクっぽいね。
This color is kind of pinkish, isn't it?
彼(かれ)は子供(こども)っぽいところがある。
He has a childish side to him.
今日(きょう)は雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうっぽい。
It looks like it might rain today.

っぽい is a highly productive suffix meaning "-ish," "-like," or "tending toward." It creates i-adjectives from nouns, verb stems, and adjective stems.

ATTACHMENT PATTERNS:

  • Noun + っぽい: 子供(こども)っぽい (childish), 大人(おとな)っぽい (mature/adult-like)
  • Verb stem + っぽい: 忘(わす)れっぽい (forgetful), 飽(あ)きっぽい (easily bored)
  • Adjective stem + っぽい: 安(やす)っぽい (cheap-looking), 白(しろ)っぽい (whitish)

NUANCE:
With nouns, っぽい often implies a slightly negative or superficial resemblance — 子供(こども)っぽい suggests "childish" rather than genuinely childlike. With colors, it simply means "-ish": 青(あお)っぽい (bluish).

CONJUGATION:

Behaves as an i-adjective:

  • っぽくない (negative)
  • っぽかった (past)
  • っぽく (adverbial)

SIMILAR SUFFIXES:

  • 〜らしい: befitting, typical of (more positive: 男(おとこ)らしい = manly)
  • 〜みたい: looks like, resembles (more neutral)
  • 〜的(てき): -like, -esque (formal: 子供(こども)的(てき))
