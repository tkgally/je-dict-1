っぽい

っぽい
suffix
-ish; -like; having the characteristics of

Definitions

1. -ish; -like; having the characteristics of; tending toward
Productive suffix attaching to nouns, verb stems, and adjective stems.

Examples

この色(いろ)、ちょっとピンクっぽいね。
This color is kind of pinkish, isn't it?
彼(かれ)は子供(こども)っぽいところがある。
He has a childish side to him.
今日(きょう)は雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうっぽい。
It looks like it might rain today.

Notes

っぽい is a productive suffix meaning "-ish" or "-like."

ATTACHMENT:

  • Noun + っぽい: 子供(こども)っぽい (childish)
  • Verb stem + っぽい: 忘(わす)れっぽい (forgetful)
  • Adjective stem + っぽい: 安(やす)っぽい (cheap-looking)

EXAMPLES:

  • 男(おとこ)っぽい - masculine
  • 女(おんな)っぽい - feminine
  • 水(みず)っぽい - watery
  • 黒(くろ)っぽい - blackish

CONJUGATION (as い-adjective):

  • っぽい → っぽくない (negative)
  • っぽい → っぽかった (past)
