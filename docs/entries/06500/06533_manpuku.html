満腹(まんぷく)

まんぷく
noun, na-adjective
full stomach, satiety
1. full stomach, satiety
The state of having eaten enough and feeling full. A more formal word compared to the casual お腹(なか)いっぱい.
ご飯(はん)をたくさん食(た)べて満腹(まんぷく)だ。
I ate a lot and I'm full.
満腹(まんぷく)になるまで食(た)べた。
I ate until I was completely full.
満腹(まんぷく)中枢(ちゅうすう)が刺激(しげき)される。
The satiety center is stimulated.
Medical/scientific context

WORD FORMATION:

  • 満(まん) (full) + 腹(ぷく) (stomach/belly)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 空腹(くうふく) (empty stomach/hunger) - antonym
  • お腹(なか)いっぱい (I'm full - casual)
  • 腹八分(はらはちぶ) (eating until 80% full - health principle)
  • 食(た)べ過(す)ぎ (overeating)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 満腹(まんぷく)感(かん) (feeling of fullness)
  • 満腹(まんぷく)になる (to become full)
  • 腹八分目(はらはちぶんめ)に医者(いしゃ)いらず (Eating in moderation keeps the doctor away - proverb)

SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT:

  • 満腹(まんぷく)中枢(ちゅうすう) (satiety center - in the brain)
  • 満腹(まんぷく)ホルモン (satiety hormones like leptin)

DINING CULTURE:

  • Saying ごちそうさまでした indicates satisfaction with the meal
  • Japanese portion sizes often aim for 腹八分(はらはちぶ) (80% full)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 満腹(まんぷく)になる (to become full)
  • 満腹(まんぷく)感(かん)を得(え)る (to feel satisfied/full)
  • 満腹(まんぷく)で動(うご)けない (so full I can't move)

Related Words

Antonym: 空腹(くうふく) (hunger)
