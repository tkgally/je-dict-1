物足(ものた)りない

1. unsatisfying; not enough; leaving something to be desired
The feeling that something is incomplete or insufficient, leaving one wanting more.
食事(しょくじ)の量(りょう)が物足(ものた)りない。
The amount of food is not enough.
彼(かれ)の説明(せつめい)は物足(ものた)りなかった。
His explanation was unsatisfying.
どこか物足(ものた)りない気(き)がする。
I feel like something is missing.

WORD FORMATION:
もの (thing) + 足(た)りない (not sufficient) → something is lacking

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 物足(ものた)りなく
  • Noun form: 物足(ものた)りなさ (the feeling of insufficiency)
  • Negative: 物足(ものた)りなくない (double negative - rarely used)
  • Te-form: 物足(ものた)りなくて
  • Past: 物足(ものた)りなかった

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • Xが物足(ものた)りない (X is unsatisfying)
  • どこか物足(ものた)りない (something feels lacking)
  • 物足(ものた)りない気(き)がする (to feel that something is lacking)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 足(た)りない (not enough) - more direct, often about quantity
  • 物足(ものた)りない - implies emotional/qualitative insufficiency
  • 不満(ふまん) (dissatisfaction) - stronger discontent

Related Words

Related: 足(た)りる (to be sufficient (plain insufficiency))
