吸(す)い込(こ)む

すいこむ
verb (godan, transitive)
to inhale; to suck in; to absorb
1. to inhale; to breathe in
To draw air or a gas into the lungs through the nose or mouth.
深(ふか)く息(いき)を吸(す)い込(こ)んでリラックスしてください。
Please take a deep breath and relax.
2. to suck in; to draw in
To pull something inward through suction, such as a vacuum cleaner drawing in dust or a drain drawing in water.
掃除機(そうじき)がゴミを吸(す)い込(こ)んだ。
The vacuum cleaner sucked up the dust.
3. to absorb; to soak up
To take in and retain liquid or other substances, as a sponge absorbs water.
スポンジが水(みず)を吸(す)い込(こ)んだ。
The sponge absorbed the water.

CONJUGATION:

  • 吸(す)い込(こ)む → 吸(す)い込(こ)まない (negative)
  • 吸(す)い込(こ)む → 吸(す)い込(こ)んで (te-form)
  • 吸(す)い込(こ)む → 吸(す)い込(こ)んだ (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 吸(す)う (to inhale, suck) + 込(こ)む (into)

The ～込(こ)む pattern indicates drawing something inward.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空気(くうき)を吸(す)い込(こ)む - to inhale air
  • 煙(けむり)を吸(す)い込(こ)む - to inhale smoke
  • ほこりを吸(す)い込(こ)む - to suck in dust
  • 水分(すいぶん)を吸(す)い込(こ)む - to absorb moisture

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. Takes を for the thing being inhaled or absorbed.

Related Words

Related: 吸(す)う (to inhale)
Antonym: 吐(は)き出(だ)す (to spit out)
