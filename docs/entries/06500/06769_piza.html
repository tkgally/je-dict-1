ピザ

ぴざ
noun
pizza
1. pizza
Loanword from Italian, standard term for pizza in Japanese.
ピザを宅配(たくはい)で頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered pizza for delivery.
手作(てづく)りピザを焼(や)いた。
I baked homemade pizza.
みんなでピザをシェアして食(た)べた。
We all shared and ate pizza together.

ピザ is a loanword from Italian "pizza." Also written as ピッツァ in authentic Italian-style contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ピザを焼(や)く - to bake pizza
  • ピザを頼(たの)む / 注文(ちゅうもん)する - to order pizza
  • ピザを切(き)る - to cut/slice pizza
  • ピザをシェアする - to share pizza

COUNTING:
Pizzas are counted with 枚(まい): ピザ一枚(いちまい).
Slices: 一切(ひとき)れ, 一(いち)ピース.
