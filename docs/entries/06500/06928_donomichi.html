どのみち

どのみち
adverb
anyway, in any case, either way
1. anyway, in any case, either way
Indicates that the outcome or conclusion remains the same regardless of circumstances or choices.
どのみち間(ま)に合(あ)わないから、急(いそ)いでも無駄(むだ)だ。
We won't make it anyway, so there's no point in hurrying.
どのみち同(おな)じ結果(けっか)になる。
Either way, the result will be the same.
どのみち言(い)わなきゃいけないことだから、今(いま)言(い)うよ。
Since I have to say it anyway, I'll say it now.
成功(せいこう)しても失敗(しっぱい)しても、どのみち経験(けいけん)になる。
Whether you succeed or fail, either way it becomes experience.

NUANCE: Expresses inevitability — the outcome is fixed whichever path is taken.

ETYMOLOGY: どの (which) + 道(みち) (path) = "whichever path", the idea being that all roads lead to the same place.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Resignation: accepting an outcome that cannot be changed
  • Pragmatic reasoning: since X is certain anyway, let's do Y
  • Consolation: pointing out that the result is the same either way

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • いずれにせよ / いずれにしても — "in any case" (slightly more formal)
  • どうせ — "anyway" (more pessimistic or dismissive)
  • 結局(けっきょく) — "in the end" (a conclusion actually reached)
  • どっちみち — variant form, same meaning, slightly more casual

VS. どうせ:

  • どのみち states inevitability neutrally
  • どうせ usually carries a negative colouring — "it's pointless anyway", often with a note of self-deprecation

REGISTER: Neutral. Appropriate in both casual and semi-formal contexts.

Related Words

Synonym: いずれにせよ (in any case (more formal))
Contrast: どうせ (anyway (dismissive))
Related: 結局(けっきょく) (in the end)
