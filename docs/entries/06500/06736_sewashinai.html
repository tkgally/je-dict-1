せわしない

せわしない
i-adjective
restless; busy; hectic

1. restless; fidgety; hurried
Unable to stay calm or still; constantly moving or busy.
彼(かれ)はせわしなく動(うご)き回(まわ)っている。
He's restlessly moving around.
せわしなく目(め)を動(うご)かしている。
Their eyes are darting around restlessly.
彼女(かのじょ)はせわしない人(ひと)で、じっとしていられない。
She's a restless person who can't sit still.
2. busy; hectic
Full of activity with no time to rest.
年末(ねんまつ)はせわしない時期(じき)だ。
Year-end is a hectic time.
せわしない毎日(まいにち)を送(おく)っている。
I'm living a hectic life every day.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)でせわしない日々(ひび)が続(つづ)いている。
Hectic days continue as I prepare for the move.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 忙(せわ)しい (busy) + ない. The ない here is **not** the negative suffix but an emphatic one, as in 切(せつ)ない or はしたない. せわしない therefore means the same as せわしい — busy, restless — and not its opposite. This is the point learners most often get wrong.

KANJI:
Can be written 忙(せわ)しない, though hiragana is more common.

USAGE:

Describes a person who cannot sit still, or a stretch of time filled with activity:

  • せわしなく働(はたら)く (to work busily)
  • せわしない雰囲気(ふんいき) (hectic atmosphere)

Sense 1 (of a person) carries mild criticism — fidgety, unable to settle. Sense 2 (of a period) is neutral.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 忙(いそが)しい - busy (far more common; the neutral everyday choice)
  • 慌(あわ)ただしい - hurried, rushed
  • 落(お)ち着(つ)かない - restless, unable to settle

Related Words

Related: 忙(いそが)しい (busy)
Synonym: 慌(あわ)ただしい (hurried, rushed)
Related: 落(お)ち着(つ)く (to settle down (negative form is the antonym))
Related: おぼつかない (same non-negative ない formation)
