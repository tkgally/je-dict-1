かぶれる

かぶれる
verb (ichidan)
to get a rash, to be influenced by
1. to get a rash, to have an allergic reaction
To develop a skin rash or irritation from contact with plants, chemicals, or allergens.
2. to be influenced by, to be infatuated with
To be strongly influenced by or overly enthusiastic about a culture, ideology, or trend, often with a negative nuance.
漆(うるし)にかぶれて皮膚(ひふ)がかゆい。
I got a rash from lacquer and my skin is itchy.
この化粧品(けしょうひん)でかぶれることがある。
Some people get rashes from this cosmetic.
西洋(せいよう)思想(しそう)にかぶれている。
They're infatuated with Western thought.
若(わか)いころは左翼(さよく)思想(しそう)にかぶれていた。
In my youth, I was taken with leftist ideology.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive).

COMMON TRIGGERS (Sense 1):

  • 漆(うるし) (lacquer) - famous for causing rashes
  • 化粧品(けしょうひん) (cosmetics)
  • 金属(きんぞく) (metal)
  • おむつ (diapers) - おむつかぶれ (diaper rash)

NUANCE (Sense 2): Usually implies excessive or uncritical enthusiasm for something foreign or trendy. Can be slightly derogatory, suggesting superficial adoption.

RELATED NOUN: かぶれ can be used as a noun meaning 'rash' or 'someone infatuated with something.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜にかぶれる (get a rash from / be infatuated with)
  • かぶれやすい肌(はだ) (skin prone to rashes)
