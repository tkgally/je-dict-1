げっぷ

げっぷ
noun
burp, belch
1. burp, belch
The release of gas from the stomach through the mouth, often making a sound. Also called おくび in more formal contexts.
炭酸(たんさん)飲料(いんりょう)を飲(の)んだらげっぷが出(で)た。
I burped after drinking a carbonated drink.
赤(あか)ちゃんにげっぷを出(だ)させる。
Make the baby burp.
人前(ひとまえ)でげっぷをするのはマナー違反(いはん)だ。
Burping in front of others is bad manners.

FORMAL ALTERNATIVE: おくび is a more formal/literary word for burp.

USAGE: げっぷ is considered impolite in Japanese culture, especially in public or at the table. Unlike some cultures where burping may signal satisfaction with a meal, it's considered rude in Japan.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • げっぷが出(で)る (to burp - intransitive)
  • げっぷを出(だ)す (to let out a burp)
  • げっぷを出(だ)させる (to make someone burp - esp. for babies)

BABY CARE: After feeding a baby, parents pat the baby's back to help them げっぷを出(だ)す to prevent discomfort from swallowed air.

ONOMATOPOEIA: The sound of a burp may be written as ゲップ or げぷっ.
