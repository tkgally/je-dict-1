1. to jump down; to jump off
To leap from a height to a lower place.
子供(こども)が木(き)から飛(と)び降(お)りた。
The child jumped down from the tree.
電車(でんしゃ)が止(と)まる前(まえ)に飛(と)び降(お)りてはいけない。
You must not jump off before the train stops.
屋根(やね)から飛(と)び降(お)りるのは危険(きけん)だ。
Jumping off the roof is dangerous.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 飛(と)ぶ (to fly, to jump) + 降(お)りる (to descend, to get off).

USAGE:

  • 〜から飛(と)び降(お)りる (to jump down from ~)

The starting point takes から, not を. The verb is intransitive, so it never takes a direct object.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 飛(と)び乗(の)る - to jump onto (the opposite motion)
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)む - to jump/dive in
  • 飛(と)び上(あ)がる - to jump up
  • 飛(と)び出(だ)す - to jump out
