今(いま)しがた

いましがた
adverb
just now, a moment ago
1. just now, a moment ago
Indicates something that happened very recently, within the past few minutes.
今(いま)しがた届(とど)いたばかりです。
It just arrived a moment ago.
今(いま)しがた田中(たなか)さんから電話(でんわ)がありました。
Mr. Tanaka just called a moment ago.
今(いま)しがた出(で)て行(い)ったところです。
They just left a moment ago.
今(いま)しがた聞(き)いた話(はなし)なんですが。
This is something I just heard a moment ago.

ETYMOLOGY: 今(いま) (now) + しがた (a form of する marking the immediate past)

TIME FRAME: The very recent past - typically the last few minutes, not hours.

REGISTER: Slightly formal. Common in business communication, polite conversation, and news reporting; less common in very casual speech.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • たった今(いま) - "just now" (very common, neutral)
  • さっき - "a while ago" (casual, covers a longer span)
  • 先(さき)ほど - "earlier" (polite, formal)
  • ついさっき - "just a moment ago" (casual emphasis)

CONTRAST WITH さっき:

  • 今(いま)しがた: seconds to a few minutes ago
  • さっき: minutes to an hour or so ago

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 今(いま)しがた + past tense: reports a just-completed action
  • 今(いま)しがた〜ばかり: emphasizes how recent it is

Related Words

Synonym: さっき (a while ago (casual))
Synonym: 先程(さきほど) (earlier (formal))
Synonym: たった今(いま) (just now)
