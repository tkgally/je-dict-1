デッドライン

でっどらいん
noun
deadline; final date; cutoff
1. deadline; final date; cutoff
Loanword from English, common in business and publishing contexts.
デッドラインまであと三日(みっか)しかない。
There are only three days left until the deadline.
デッドラインに間(ま)に合(あ)わせるために残業(ざんぎょう)した。
I worked overtime to meet the deadline.
デッドラインを延長(えんちょう)してもらえますか。
Could you extend the deadline?

A loanword from English "deadline." Used in business, publishing, and project-management contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • デッドラインに間(ま)に合(あ)う: to meet the deadline
  • デッドラインを守(まも)る: to keep to the deadline
  • デッドラインを延長(えんちょう)する: to extend the deadline
  • デッドラインを過(す)ぎる: to miss/pass the deadline
  • デッドラインを設定(せってい)する: to set a deadline

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 締(し)め切(き)り: deadline. Far more common in everyday Japanese and broader in use — applications, submissions, sales.
  • 期限(きげん): time limit, due date. Neutral and general; also used for expiry dates.
  • 納期(のうき): delivery deadline, used specifically for delivering goods or finished work.

REGISTER:
デッドライン sounds slightly more urgent or dramatic than 締(し)め切(き)り. It is most at home in creative industries and tech workplaces.

Related Words

Synonym: 締(し)め切(き)り (native equivalent)
Related: 期限(きげん) (time limit, due date)
Related: 納期(のうき) (delivery deadline)
