1. to thrust out, to stick out
To push something outward forcefully; to protrude or jut out.
彼(かれ)は胸(むね)を突(つ)き出(だ)して堂々(どうどう)と歩(ある)いた。
He walked proudly with his chest thrust out.
岩(いわ)が海(うみ)に突(つ)き出(だ)している。
The rock juts out into the sea.
書類(しょるい)を窓口(まどぐち)に突(つ)き出(だ)す。
To thrust the documents toward the counter window.
2. to hand over, to turn in
To present or submit someone or something to authorities, especially a criminal to police.
犯人(はんにん)を警察(けいさつ)に突(つ)き出(だ)した。
They handed the criminal over to the police.
万引(まんび)き犯(はん)を店員(てんいん)が警察(けいさつ)に突(つ)き出(だ)した。
The clerk handed the shoplifter over to the police.
不審者(ふしんしゃ)を見(み)つけたら、すぐに交番(こうばん)に突(つ)き出(だ)してください。
If you find a suspicious person, please turn them in to the police box immediately.

突(つ)き出(だ)す is a compound verb: 突(つ)く (to thrust) + 出(だ)す (to put out).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを突(つ)き出(だ)す

ASPECT (ている):

  • 突(つ)き出(だ)している describes a state of protruding, not an action in progress (sense 1): 岩(いわ)が突(つ)き出(だ)している means the rock *is sticking out*, not that it is in the process of being pushed out
  • Telic for sense 2 (handing over completes the action)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 胸(むね)を突(つ)き出(だ)す - to stick out one's chest (show pride)
  • 顎(あご)を突(つ)き出(だ)す - to stick out one's chin (defiance)
  • 警察(けいさつ)に突(つ)き出(だ)す - to turn over to the police

COMPOUND VERB NOTES:
The ～出(だ)す pattern typically adds the meaning of "outward movement" or "beginning." Here it is the outward/forward direction that is emphasized, as in 飛(と)び出(だ)す (to jump/rush out).
