最早(もはや)

もはや
adverb
already; no longer; now; by this point
1. no longer; not anymore (with negative)
Used with negative expressions to indicate that something is no longer the case. Implies a change from a previous state.
彼女(かのじょ)は最早(もはや)子供(こども)ではない。
She is no longer a child.
最早(もはや)言(い)い訳(わけ)はできない。
There's no longer any excuse to be made.
あの店(みせ)は最早(もはや)存在(そんざい)しない。
That shop no longer exists.
2. already; by now; at this point
Indicates that a situation has reached a certain point. Often used to emphasize that something has become inevitable or has progressed beyond a certain stage.
最早(もはや)手遅(ておく)れだ。
It's already too late.
これは最早(もはや)災害(さいがい)レベルの暑(あつ)さだ。
This heat is already at disaster level.
ここまで来(き)たら、最早(もはや)後戻(あともど)りはできない。
Having come this far, there's no turning back now.

USAGE:
最早(もはや) marks a line already crossed. With a negative it says something is no longer so; with a plain statement it says the situation has already reached a point there is no arguing with. Either way it looks back at a change that has finished, which is what separates it from もう — the everyday word that covers the same ground without the note of finality.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 最早(もはや)〜ない: no longer ~
  • 最早(もはや)元(もと)には戻(もど)らない: will never go back to how it was
  • 最早(もはや)これまでだ: this is the end of it
  • 最早(もはや)疑(うたが)う余地(よち)はない: there is no longer room for doubt

WRITING NOTE:
Usually written in kana as もはや. The kanji spelling 最早(もはや) is a jukujikun — the reading does not follow from the individual characters — and belongs to formal or literary writing.

REGISTER:
Formal and somewhat literary. In speech もう does the same work: 最早(もはや)〜ない (written) = もう〜ない (spoken). 最早(もはや) also turns up in commentary and headlines for rhetorical weight, as in これは最早(もはや)災害(さいがい)レベルだ.

SIMILAR ADVERBS:

  • もう: already, no longer — neutral and far more common in speech
  • 今更(いまさら): too late now — stresses that acting at this point is pointless, where 最早(もはや) simply reports the state

Related Words
