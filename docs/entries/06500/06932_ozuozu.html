おずおず

おずおず
adverb
timidly, bashfully, hesitantly
1. timidly, bashfully, hesitantly
Mimetic word describing cautious, shy behavior when approaching something or someone with nervousness or uncertainty.
おずおずと手(て)を挙(あ)げた。
She timidly raised her hand.
彼(かれ)はおずおずと部屋(へや)に入(はい)ってきた。
He hesitantly entered the room.
おずおずと質問(しつもん)してもいいですか。
May I ask a question? (said timidly)
子供(こども)がおずおずと近(ちか)づいてきた。
The child approached bashfully.

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo) describing manner or attitude. Almost always used as おずおずと before a verb.

NUANCE: Shyness or nervousness, lack of confidence, caution born of uncertainty, hesitation before acting.

TYPICAL SUBJECTS: children meeting strangers, new employees, anyone in an unfamiliar or intimidating situation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • おずおずと + verb: describes a hesitant action
  • Frequent verbs: 手(て)を挙(あ)げる (raise one's hand), 近(ちか)づく (approach), 話(はな)しかける (speak to), 入(はい)る (enter)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • おどおど - nervous, unsettled manner (visible anxiety)
  • びくびく - timid from fear of something specific
  • もじもじ - fidgeting from embarrassment
  • 恐(おそ)る恐(おそ)る - fearfully, gingerly (physical caution)
  • ためらいがち - hesitantly (more formal, from ためらう)

CONTRAST:

  • おずおず: a timid, tentative approach
  • 堂々(どうどう)と: confidently, boldly
