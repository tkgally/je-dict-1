1. to dissolve into; to melt into
To become absorbed into a liquid or substance, losing its original form.
砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に溶(と)け込(こ)んだ。
The sugar dissolved into the water.
塩(しお)がスープに完全(かんぜん)に溶(と)け込(こ)んだ。
The salt completely dissolved into the soup.
夕日(ゆうひ)の光(ひかり)が空(そら)の色(いろ)に溶(と)け込(こ)んでいった。
The light of the sunset melted into the colors of the sky.
2. to blend in; to assimilate; to fit in
To become an integrated part of a group or environment, adapting to the surroundings.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)は早(はや)く職場(しょくば)に溶(と)け込(こ)んだ。
The new employee quickly blended into the workplace.
地域(ちいき)社会(しゃかい)に溶(と)け込(こ)むには時間(じかん)がかかる。
It takes time to assimilate into the local community.
転校(てんこう)したばかりだが、もう新(あたら)しいクラスに溶(と)け込(こ)んでいる。
I just transferred, but I've already blended into the new class.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 溶(と)ける (to dissolve/melt) + 込(こ)む (into)

The ～込(こ)む pattern emphasizes becoming deeply integrated or absorbed.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社会(しゃかい)に溶(と)け込(こ)む - to blend into society
  • 風景(ふうけい)に溶(と)け込(こ)む - to blend into the scenery
  • チームに溶(と)け込(こ)む - to fit into the team
  • 自然(しぜん)に溶(と)け込(こ)む - to blend naturally

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 馴染(なじ)む - to become familiar with, to fit in (more about becoming accustomed)
  • 適応(てきおう)する - to adapt (more formal, biological/psychological)
