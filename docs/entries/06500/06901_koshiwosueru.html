腰(こし)を据(す)える

こしをすえる
expression
to settle down to, to commit seriously
1. to settle down, to commit seriously to a task
To approach something with serious, sustained effort; to buckle down and work diligently.
腰(こし)を据(す)えてやろう。
Let's buckle down and do this.
腰(こし)を据(す)えて勉強(べんきょう)する時間(じかん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
I need time to buckle down and study seriously.
この問題(もんだい)には腰(こし)を据(す)えて取(と)り組(く)むべきだ。
We should tackle this problem with serious commitment.
転職(てんしょく)ばかりしていないで、そろそろ腰(こし)を据(す)えて一(ひと)つの会社(かいしゃ)で頑張(がんば)ったらどうだ。
Instead of constantly changing jobs, why don't you settle down and work hard at one company?
今度(こんど)こそ腰(こし)を据(す)えて、最後(さいご)まで諦(あきら)めずにやり遂(と)げたい。
This time I want to buckle down and see it through to the end without giving up.
2. to settle in a place, to stay put
To establish oneself in a location for a considerable time.
京都(きょうと)に腰(こし)を据(す)えて研究(けんきゅう)する。
To settle in Kyoto and conduct research.
やっと腰(こし)を据(す)える場所(ばしょ)が見(み)つかった。
I finally found a place to settle down.
海外(かいがい)を転々(てんてん)としていたが、地元(じもと)に腰(こし)を据(す)えることにした。
I had been moving around overseas, but decided to settle down in my hometown.
子供(こども)が生(う)まれたので、引(ひ)っ越(こ)しをやめて今(いま)の町(まち)に腰(こし)を据(す)えることにした。
Since we had a baby, we decided to stop moving and settle down in our current town.
十年(じゅうねん)以上(いじょう)この土地(とち)に腰(こし)を据(す)えて暮(く)らしてきた。もう他(ほか)の場所(ばしょ)には住(す)めない。
I've been settled in this area for over ten years. I couldn't live anywhere else now.

STRUCTURE: 腰(こし) (waist, hips) + を + 据(す)える (to set firmly in place) — literally 'to set one's hips down', the image of sitting down properly to work instead of hovering.

TWO USES:
1. Committing to a task: sustained, patient effort rather than dabbling. Very common in the て form before another verb — 腰(こし)を据(す)えて取(と)り組(く)む, 腰(こし)を据(す)えて勉強(べんきょう)する.
2. Settling in a place: to stop moving around and stay put — a town, a company, a country.

Both share the sense of finally putting weight down in one spot after being unsettled.

OTHER 腰(こし) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腰(こし)が重(おも)い: slow to get started, reluctant
  • 腰(こし)が低(ひく)い: humble in manner
  • 腰(こし)が軽(かる)い: quick to act, willing

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • じっくり取(と)り組(く)む: to work at something carefully and without hurry
  • 本腰(ほんごし)を入(い)れる: to get serious about something
  • 腰(こし)を落(お)ち着(つ)ける: to settle oneself down

Related Words

Contrast: 腰(こし)が低(ひく)い (humble in manner (another 腰 idiom))
Related: 腰(こし) (waist, hips (the body part in the idiom))
Related: 取(と)り組(く)む (to tackle, to grapple with (its usual companion verb))
