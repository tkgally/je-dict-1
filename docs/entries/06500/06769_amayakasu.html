甘(あま)やかす

あまやかす
verb
to spoil; to pamper; to indulge

Definitions

1. to spoil; to pamper; to indulge someone too much
To be overly lenient or indulgent with someone, especially a child, often with negative implications of producing bad behavior.

Examples

子供(こども)を甘(あま)やかしすぎると良(よ)くない。
It's not good to spoil children too much.
祖父母(そふぼ)は孫(まご)を甘(あま)やかす傾向(けいこう)がある。
Grandparents tend to spoil their grandchildren.
彼(かれ)は甘(あま)やかされて育(そだ)った。
He was raised being spoiled.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 甘(あま)える (to act spoiled, to depend on) - intransitive
  • Pattern: XがYを甘(あま)やかす

ASPECT:

  • 甘(あま)やかしている = is spoiling (ongoing action)
  • 甘(あま)やかされている = is being spoiled (passive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を甘(あま)やかす (to spoil a child)
  • 甘(あま)やかして育(そだ)てる (to raise someone in a spoiled manner)
  • 甘(あま)やかされて育(そだ)つ (to grow up spoiled)

NUANCE:
Unlike simply being kind, 甘(あま)やかす often implies excessive leniency that may have negative consequences. It suggests failing to discipline or set appropriate boundaries.

Related Words

Pair verb: 甘(あま)える (intransitive)
