物件(ぶっけん)

ぶっけん
noun
property; real estate listing; item
1. property; real estate listing
A property for sale or rent, commonly used in real estate contexts.
駅近(えきちか)の物件(ぶっけん)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a property near the station.
いい物件(ぶっけん)が見(み)つかった。
I found a good property.
賃貸(ちんたい)物件(ぶっけん)を内見(ないけん)する。
I'm going to view a rental property.
2. object; item; article
A physical object or item, especially in legal or formal contexts.
証拠(しょうこ)物件(ぶっけん)を調(しら)べる。
To examine the evidence items.
盗難(とうなん)物件(ぶっけん)が発見(はっけん)された。
Stolen items were discovered.
警察(けいさつ)は押収(おうしゅう)した物件(ぶっけん)のリストを作成(さくせい)した。
The police created a list of the seized items.

USAGE:

Sense 1 is what a learner meets first: in listings and agency talk, 物件(ぶっけん) is the unit of real estate on offer — one flat, one house, one shop.

  • 賃貸(ちんたい)物件(ぶっけん) (rental property)
  • 売買(ばいばい)物件(ぶっけん) (property for sale)
  • 中古(ちゅうこ)物件(ぶっけん) (pre-owned property)
  • 新築(しんちく)物件(ぶっけん) (newly built property)
  • 優良(ゆうりょう)物件(ぶっけん) (quality property, good deal)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物件(ぶっけん)を探(さが)す (to look for a property)
  • 物件(ぶっけん)を見(み)る (to view a property)
  • 物件(ぶっけん)情報(じょうほう) (property listing information)

SENSE 2 — LEGAL AND POLICE USE:
Outside real estate, 物件(ぶっけん) is the formal word for a physical object treated as an item in a case or record: 証拠(しょうこ)物件(ぶっけん) (item of evidence). It is not a general word for "thing" — for that, use 物(もの).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不動産(ふどうさん) - real estate as a category, including land; 物件(ぶっけん) is one listed unit of it
  • アパート / マンション - the building types a listing may be for
  • 一戸建(いっこだ)て - detached house

Related Words
