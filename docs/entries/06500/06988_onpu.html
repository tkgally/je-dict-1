音符(おんぷ)

おんぷ
noun
musical note
1. musical note
A symbol in musical notation that represents the pitch and duration of a sound. Also refers to the sound itself that the symbol represents.
音符(おんぷ)を読(よ)む。
To read musical notes.
音符(おんぷ)が多(おお)くて難(むずか)しい。
There are many notes and it's difficult.
楽譜(がくふ)の音符(おんぷ)を一(ひと)つずつ読(よ)んでいく。
I read the musical notes on the sheet music one by one.
四分(しぶん)音符(おんぷ)と八分(はちぶん)音符(おんぷ)のリズムを練習(れんしゅう)した。
I practiced the rhythm of quarter notes and eighth notes.
子(こ)どもたちが音符(おんぷ)の書(か)き方(かた)を習(なら)っている。
The children are learning how to write musical notes.

TYPES OF NOTES:

Japanese names note values by the fraction they occupy of a whole note, so they are transparent once the pattern is clear — unlike the English names, which a learner has to memorise separately.

  • 全(ぜん)音符(おんぷ): whole note (semibreve)
  • 二分(にぶん)音符(おんぷ): half note (minim)
  • 四分(しぶん)音符(おんぷ): quarter note (crotchet)
  • 八分(はちぶん)音符(おんぷ): eighth note (quaver)
  • 十六分(じゅうろくぶん)音符(おんぷ): sixteenth note (semiquaver)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音符(おんぷ)を読(よ)む: to read notes
  • 音符(おんぷ)を書(か)く: to write notes
  • 音符(おんぷ)が読(よ)めない: to be unable to read music

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 休符(きゅうふ): a rest — the silence counterpart, named on the same fractional pattern
  • 楽譜(がくふ): the score the notes are written on
  • 音程(おんてい): pitch or interval — how high a note is, as opposed to how long
