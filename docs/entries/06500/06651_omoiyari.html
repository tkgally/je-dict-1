思(おも)いやり

おもいやり
noun
consideration; thoughtfulness; compassion
1. consideration; thoughtfulness; compassion; empathy
The quality of understanding and caring about others' feelings and needs. Being able to put oneself in another's position and act accordingly.
思(おも)いやりのある人(ひと)になりたい。
I want to be a considerate person.
彼(かれ)の思(おも)いやりに感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by his thoughtfulness.
思(おも)いやりを持(も)って接(せっ)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to treat people with consideration.

思(おも)いやり is the disposition to imagine what another person is feeling and to act on it before being asked. It is a quality a person has, not an act performed, so it pairs with ある and 持(も)つ rather than with する.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 思(おも)いやりのある人(ひと) (a considerate/thoughtful person)
  • 思(おも)いやりを持(も)つ (to have consideration)
  • 思(おも)いやりに欠(か)ける (to lack consideration)
  • 思(おも)いやりの心(こころ) (a caring heart)

CULTURAL NOTE:
思(おも)いやり is treated as a core social value in Japan and appears constantly in school mottoes and public messaging. What it emphasizes is anticipating what someone needs without their having to ask — asking outright can itself be seen as putting the other person on the spot.

ETYMOLOGY:
The masu-stem of 思(おも)いやる (to sympathize with, to be considerate of) — literally 'to send one's thoughts' out to another person.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 気配(きくば)り (attentiveness — noticing and acting on small practical needs)
  • 心遣(こころづか)い (consideration shown in a specific gesture)
  • 優(やさ)しさ (kindness, gentleness — a broader trait)

Related Words

Synonym: 憐(あわ)れみ (pity; can be condescending)
