閉鎖的(へいさてき)

へいさてき
na-adjective
closed; exclusive; insular
1. closed; exclusive; insular
Resistant to outside influence, newcomers, or new ideas; characterized by exclusivity and lack of openness.
この村(むら)は閉鎖的(へいさてき)で外部(がいぶ)の人(ひと)を受(う)け入(い)れない。
This village is insular and doesn't accept outsiders.
閉鎖的(へいさてき)な組織(そしき)は変化(へんか)に弱(よわ)い。
Closed organizations are weak against change.
閉鎖的(へいさてき)な考(かんが)え方(かた)を改(あらた)める必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to change our insular way of thinking.

FORMS:

  • 閉鎖的(へいさてき)な + noun (adnominal)
  • 閉鎖的(へいさてき)に (adverbial)
  • 閉鎖的(へいさてき)だ (predicative)

FORMATION:
閉鎖(へいさ) (closure) + 的(てき) (-like, -ic)

USAGE:

Commonly describes:

  • 組織(そしき) (organizations)
  • 社会(しゃかい) (societies)
  • 地域(ちいき) (communities)
  • 考(かんが)え方(かた) (mindsets)

ANTONYM:

  • 開放的(かいほうてき) (open, receptive)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 排他的(はいたてき) - exclusive, exclusionary; 閉鎖的(へいさてき) describes a general lack of openness, while 排他的(はいたてき) names the active shutting-out of specific outsiders
  • 保守的(ほしゅてき) - conservative

Related Words

Antonym: 開放的(かいほうてき) (open)
