盆栽(ぼんさい)

ぼんさい
noun
bonsai

Definitions

1. bonsai; miniature potted tree
The Japanese art of cultivating miniature trees in containers, or the trees themselves. The practice involves careful pruning, wiring, and cultivation to create aesthetically pleasing small trees that reflect nature.

Examples

祖父(そふ)は趣味(しゅみ)で盆栽(ぼんさい)を育(そだ)てている。
My grandfather grows bonsai as a hobby.
この盆栽(ぼんさい)は百年以上(ひゃくねんいじょう)の歴史(れきし)がある。
This bonsai has a history of over a hundred years.
盆栽(ぼんさい)展(てん)に作品(さくひん)を出品(しゅっぴん)した。
I exhibited my work at a bonsai exhibition.

Notes

盆栽(ぼんさい) combines 盆(ぼん) (tray/pot) and 栽(さい) (planting/cultivation).

CULTURAL NOTES:
Bonsai is a traditional Japanese art form that originated from the Chinese practice of penjing. It requires patience and skill, with some bonsai being passed down through generations. The practice is associated with aesthetics like 侘(わ)び寂(さ)び and reflects the Japanese appreciation for nature in miniature form.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)を育(そだ)てる - to grow bonsai
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)の手入(てい)れをする - to care for bonsai
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)展(てん) - bonsai exhibition
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)園(えん) - bonsai garden
