盆栽(ぼんさい)

ぼんさい
noun
bonsai
1. bonsai; miniature potted tree
The Japanese art of cultivating miniature trees in containers, or the trees themselves. The practice involves careful pruning, wiring, and cultivation to create aesthetically pleasing small trees that reflect nature.
祖父(そふ)は趣味(しゅみ)で盆栽(ぼんさい)を育(そだ)てている。
My grandfather grows bonsai as a hobby.
この盆栽(ぼんさい)は百年(ひゃくねん)以上(いじょう)の歴史(れきし)がある。
This bonsai has a history of over a hundred years.
盆栽(ぼんさい)展(てん)に作品(さくひん)を出品(しゅっぴん)した。
I exhibited my work at a bonsai exhibition.

盆栽(ぼんさい) combines 盆(ぼん) (tray, shallow pot) and 栽(さい) (planting) — literally a plant cultivated in a tray.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Bonsai is a traditional art of growing miniature trees in containers, developed in Japan from the Chinese practice of penjing. A specimen can take decades to shape and is often passed down through a family, so age is part of its value. The aesthetic sits close to 侘(わ)び寂(さ)び: asymmetry, weathering, and restraint are prized over showiness. Note that in English 'bonsai' is sometimes used for the tree alone; in Japanese the word covers the practice as well.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)を育(そだ)てる: to grow bonsai
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)の手入(てい)れをする: to tend a bonsai
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)展(てん): a bonsai exhibition
  • 盆栽(ぼんさい)園(えん): a bonsai nursery or garden

COMMON VARIETIES:

  • 松(まつ): pine, the classic subject
  • 梅(うめ): plum, grown for its winter blossom
  • 紅葉(もみじ): Japanese maple, grown for autumn colour

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 植木(うえき): an ordinary garden or potted plant, with none of the artistic shaping that defines 盆栽(ぼんさい)
