思(おも)い浮(う)かべる

おもいうかべる
verb (ichidan, transitive)
to recall; to imagine; to picture
1. to recall; to remember; to bring to mind
To consciously remember or call up a memory, image, or thought from the past.
故郷(こきょう)の風景(ふうけい)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべた。
I recalled the scenery of my hometown.
彼(かれ)の顔(かお)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべると、涙(なみだ)が出(で)てくる。
When I picture his face, tears come to my eyes.
2. to imagine; to picture; to visualize
To form a mental image of something, whether real or imaginary.
完成(かんせい)した作品(さくひん)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべながら制作(せいさく)した。
I created it while imagining the completed work.

CONJUGATION:

  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる → 思(おも)い浮(う)かべない (negative)
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる → 思(おも)い浮(う)かべて (te-form)
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる → 思(おも)い浮(う)かべた (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 思(おも)う (to think) + 浮(う)かべる (to float, to bring to surface)

The image of "floating" a thought or memory to the surface of consciousness.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顔(かお)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべる - to picture someone's face
  • 思(おも)い出(で)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべる - to recall memories
  • 場面(ばめん)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべる - to picture a scene
  • 将来(しょうらい)を思(おも)い浮(う)かべる - to envision the future

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かぶ (intransitive) - to come to mind, to occur to
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる (transitive) - to recall, to imagine (actively)

Related Words

Pair verb: 思(おも)い浮(う)かぶ (intransitive)
