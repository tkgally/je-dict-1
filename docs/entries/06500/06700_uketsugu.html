受(う)け継(つ)ぐ

うけつぐ
verb (godan, transitive)
to inherit; to succeed to; to take over
1. to inherit; to succeed to
To receive something (property, traditions, skills, etc.) from a predecessor and continue with it.
祖父(そふ)から家業(かぎょう)を受(う)け継(つ)いだ。
I inherited the family business from my grandfather.
この伝統(でんとう)は何世代(なんせだい)にもわたって受(う)け継(つ)がれてきた。
This tradition has been passed down through many generations.
母(はは)の料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)を受(う)け継(つ)ぎたい。
I want to inherit my mother's cooking skills.

CONJUGATION:

  • 受(う)け継(つ)ぐ → 受(う)け継(つ)がない (negative)
  • 受(う)け継(つ)ぐ → 受(う)け継(つ)いで (te-form)
  • 受(う)け継(つ)ぐ → 受(う)け継(つ)いだ (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 受(う)ける (to receive) + 継(つ)ぐ (to succeed/inherit)

USAGE:

Often used in passive form 受(う)け継(つ)がれる to describe traditions or qualities being passed down:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)が受(う)け継(つ)がれる (skills are passed down)
  • 意志(いし)を受(う)け継(つ)ぐ (to carry on someone's will)
  • 財産(ざいさん)を受(う)け継(つ)ぐ (to inherit property)
  • DNAを受(う)け継(つ)ぐ (to inherit DNA/genes)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)き継(つ)ぐ - focuses on taking over duties/responsibilities
  • 継(つ)ぐ - simpler form, to succeed/inherit

Related Words

Related: 引(ひ)き継(つ)ぐ (to take over)
Related: 継(つ)ぐ (to succeed)
