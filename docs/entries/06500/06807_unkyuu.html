運休(うんきゅう)

うんきゅう
noun / suru-verb
service suspension (transportation)
1. service suspension, suspension of operations
The temporary suspension of transportation services such as trains, buses, or ferries, typically due to weather, accidents, or maintenance.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で電車(でんしゃ)が運休(うんきゅう)している。
Trains are suspended due to the typhoon.
大雪(おおゆき)のため、新幹線(しんかんせん)が運休(うんきゅう)になった。
The bullet train service was suspended due to heavy snow.
運休(うんきゅう)情報(じょうほう)はアプリで確認(かくにん)できる。
Service suspension information can be checked on the app.

FORMATION: 運(うん) (transport) + 休(きゅう) (rest/suspension).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 運休(うんきゅう)になる (become suspended)
  • 運休(うんきゅう)する (suspend service)
  • 運休(うんきゅう)情報(じょうほう) (service suspension information)
  • 運休(うんきゅう)が出(で)る (suspensions are announced)
  • 計画(けいかく)運休(うんきゅう) (planned service suspension)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 遅延(ちえん) (delay)
  • 運行(うんこう) (service operation)
  • 再開(さいかい) (resumption of service)
  • 振替(ふりかえ)輸送(ゆそう) (alternative transportation)
