持(も)ちかける

もちかける
verb (ichidan)
to propose, to make overtures
1. to propose, to suggest, to make overtures
To bring up a proposal or suggestion to someone, often implying an attempt to persuade or negotiate.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)に新(あたら)しいビジネスの話(はなし)を持(も)ちかけてきた。
He came to me with a new business proposal.
取引先(とりひきさき)から共同(きょうどう)開発(かいはつ)の話(はなし)を持(も)ちかけられた。
Our business partner proposed a joint development project.
怪(あや)しい投資話(とうしばなし)を持(も)ちかける詐欺(さぎ)に注意(ちゅうい)。
Be careful of scams that approach you with suspicious investment proposals.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive).

COMPOUND VERB: Formed from 持(も)つ (to hold) + かける (to apply/bring to). The combination conveys bringing something (usually a proposal) to someone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 話(はなし)を持(も)ちかける (bring up a topic/proposal)
  • 相談(そうだん)を持(も)ちかける (approach with a consultation)
  • 取引(とりひき)を持(も)ちかける (propose a deal)
  • 縁談(えんだん)を持(も)ちかける (propose a marriage arrangement)

USAGE: Often used with passive form 持(も)ちかけられる when receiving a proposal. Can have slightly negative nuance when describing unsolicited or suspicious proposals.
