たがね

たがね
noun
cold chisel
1. cold chisel
A metal chisel used for cutting or shaping metal, stone, or concrete. Unlike woodworking chisels, it is made entirely of hardened steel and struck with a hammer.
たがねとハンマーで金属(きんぞく)を切(き)る。
I cut metal with a cold chisel and hammer.
コンクリートをたがねで削(けず)った。
I chipped away the concrete with a cold chisel.
錆(さ)びた釘(くぎ)をたがねで叩(たた)き出(だ)した。
I knocked out the rusty nail with a cold chisel.

KANJI WRITING:

  • 鏨(たがね) (rarely used)
  • Usually written in hiragana

DIFFERENCE FROM WOOD CHISEL:

  • のみ (wood chisel) - has wooden handle, used on wood
  • たがね (cold chisel) - all metal, used on metal/stone/concrete

TYPES:

  • 平(ひら)たがね (flat cold chisel)
  • 先(さき)たがね (point chisel)
  • センターポンチ (center punch - for marking)

RELATED TOOLS:

  • のみ (wood chisel)
  • ハンマー/金槌(かなづち) (hammer)
  • 万力(まんりき) (vise)
  • グラインダー (grinder)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • たがねを打(う)つ (to strike a cold chisel)
  • たがねで切(き)る (to cut with a cold chisel)
  • たがねで割(わ)る (to split with a cold chisel)

Related Words

Contrast: のみ (wood chisel)
