キャンセル

きゃんせる
noun
cancellation; cancel
1. cancellation; cancel
Loanword from English, used for reservations, orders, appointments, and contracts.
予約(よやく)をキャンセルしたいのですが。
I'd like to cancel my reservation.
注文(ちゅうもん)のキャンセルはできますか。
Can I cancel my order?
悪天候(あくてんこう)のため、イベントがキャンセルになった。
The event was cancelled due to bad weather.

キャンセル is a loanword from English "cancel," used as both a noun and a する verb. It applies to reservations, orders, appointments, and contracts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • キャンセルする: to cancel
  • キャンセルになる: to be cancelled (by circumstance)
  • キャンセルを受(う)け付(つ)ける: to accept cancellations
  • キャンセル待(ま)ち: waitlist (waiting for a cancellation opening)
  • キャンセル料(りょう): cancellation fee

キャンセルする VS キャンセルになる:
キャンセルする names the person who cancels (私(わたし)がキャンセルした). キャンセルになる presents the cancellation as an outcome, without saying who decided — the normal way to announce that an event is off.

NATIVE ALTERNATIVES:

  • 取(と)り消(け)す: to cancel, to revoke (native verb, slightly more formal)
  • 中止(ちゅうし)する: to discontinue, to call off (events, plans)

NUANCE:
キャンセル is the most common word for cancelling reservations and orders. 中止(ちゅうし) is preferred for events and scheduled activities. 取(と)り消(け)し is used in official/legal contexts like revoking a license.

Related Words

Synonym: 取(と)り消(け)す (native equivalent)
Related: 中止(ちゅうし) (call off (events))
