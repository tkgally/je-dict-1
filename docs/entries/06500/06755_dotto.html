どっと

どっと
adverb, onomatopoeia
in a rush; all at once; suddenly
1. in a rush; all at once
A large amount or many people suddenly moving or appearing at once.
観客(かんきゃく)がどっと笑(わら)った。
The audience burst into laughter.
仕事(しごと)がどっと増(ふ)えた。
Work suddenly piled up.
開店(かいてん)と同時(どうじ)に客(きゃく)がどっと押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
Customers rushed in all at once the moment the store opened.
2. suddenly; with a crash
Something happening abruptly and forcefully.
疲(つか)れがどっと出(で)た。
Fatigue suddenly hit me all at once.
どっと倒(たお)れた。
He collapsed suddenly.
試験(しけん)が終(お)わった途端(とたん)、緊張(きんちょう)が解(と)けてどっと疲(つか)れが出(で)た。
The moment the exam ended, the tension released and exhaustion hit me all at once.

USAGE:
どっと + verb

Marks an arrival rather than a process: a mass of people, work, or feeling that was held back somewhere else lands all at once. This is why it so often follows a moment of release — a punchline, an opening door, the end of an exam.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • どっと押(お)し寄(よ)せる (to surge in)
  • どっと湧(わ)く (to well up suddenly)
  • どっと泣(な)く (to burst into tears)
  • どっと笑(わら)う (to burst out laughing)
  • どっと倒(たお)れる (to collapse)
  • どっと疲(つか)れが出(で)る (to be hit by exhaustion)

NUANCE:
The subject is overwhelmed by the volume, not merely surprised by the timing. どっと疲(つか)れが出(で)る is the classic case: the tiredness was accumulating unnoticed and arrives in one wave once the strain lifts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一気(いっき)に: all at once, in one go. Controlled and often deliberate — you drink or finish something 一気に; どっと is what happens to you.
  • 急(きゅう)に: suddenly. About timing only, with no sense of volume.
  • ばっと: in a flash. Quick and sharp, typically one action or one person, where どっと implies mass.
