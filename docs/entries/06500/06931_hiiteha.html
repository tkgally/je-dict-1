ひいては

ひいては
adverb
and eventually, by extension, furthermore
1. and eventually, by extension, furthermore
Indicates that something leads to or affects something larger or more significant, showing a chain of consequences.
個人(こじん)の健康(けんこう)、ひいては社会(しゃかい)全体(ぜんたい)の利益(りえき)につながる。
It benefits individual health, and by extension, society as a whole.
この問題(もんだい)は会社(かいしゃ)、ひいては業界(ぎょうかい)全体(ぜんたい)に影響(えいきょう)する。
This issue affects the company, and furthermore, the entire industry.
教育(きょういく)の充実(じゅうじつ)は、ひいては国(くに)の発展(はってん)につながる。
Improving education ultimately leads to national development.
一人一人(ひとりひとり)の努力(どりょく)が、ひいては大(おお)きな変化(へんか)を生(う)む。
Each person's effort eventually produces great change.

NUANCE: Marks an escalation in scope - from smaller to larger, individual to collective, immediate to eventual.

PATTERN: A、ひいてはB

  • A is the nearer, smaller effect
  • B is the broader effect that follows from it

COMMON CONTEXTS: policy discussion (individual impact to social impact), business (one firm to a whole 業界(ぎょうかい)), and cause-and-effect chains running from the immediate to the eventual.

REGISTER: Formal to semi-formal - academic writing, business presentations, speeches, and news analysis. It sounds stiff in casual conversation.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • さらには - "furthermore, moreover" (adds a point rather than widening scope)
  • そして - "and then" (simple sequence)
  • 延(ひ)いては - kanji variant of this word, same meaning
  • ともすれば - "all too easily" (a tendency, not an expansion)

NOTE: The ひいて comes from 引(ひ)く (to pull, to draw out), hence the sense of a consequence being drawn out to a wider sphere.
