共益費(きょうえきひ)

きょうえきひ
noun
common expense fee
1. common expense fee; common area maintenance fee
A fee paid by tenants or owners for the maintenance of shared facilities and common areas in an apartment building or condominium.
共益費(きょうえきひ)は水道代(すいどうだい)を含(ふく)んでいる。
The common expense fee includes the water bill.
家賃(やちん)8万円(はちまんえん)、共益費(きょうえきひ)3千円(さんぜんえん)。
Rent 80,000 yen, common expense fee 3,000 yen.
共益費(きょうえきひ)で廊下(ろうか)の照明(しょうめい)が賄(まかな)われている。
The hallway lighting is covered by the common expense fee.

ETYMOLOGY:
共益(きょうえき) (common benefit) + 費(ひ) (expense, fee)

DIFFERENCE FROM 管理費(かんりひ):

While often used interchangeably, technically:

  • 管理費(かんりひ) - more for management/administration
  • 共益費(きょうえきひ) - more for shared utilities/facilities

In practice, many landlords use one or the other, and some use both.

WHAT IT TYPICALLY COVERS:

  • 共用(きょうよう)部分(ぶぶん)の電気(でんき) (common area electricity)
  • 水道(すいどう)代(だい) (water bills - sometimes)
  • ゴミ}{処理(しょり) (garbage disposal)
  • 廊下(ろうか)/階段(かいだん)の清掃(せいそう) (hallway/stairway cleaning)

PROPERTY LISTING TERMS:

  • 賃料(ちんりょう)：¥80,000 共益費(きょうえきひ)：¥5,000
  • 共益費(きょうえきひ)込(こ)み (common fee included in rent)
  • 共益費(きょうえきひ)なし (no common fee)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 共益費(きょうえきひ)を払(はら)う (to pay the common expense fee)
  • 共益費(きょうえきひ)が発生(はっせい)する (a common fee applies)
  • 共益費(きょうえきひ)別(べつ) (common fee charged separately)

Related Words

Synonym: 管理費(かんりひ) (management fee)
