1. itching to do something; eager; restless
Feeling a strong urge or impatience to do something.
旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きたくてうずうずしている。
I'm itching to go traveling.
試合(しあい)の前(まえ)に体(からだ)がうずうずする。
My body is restless before the match.
言(い)いたいことがあってうずうずしている。
I'm dying to say something.
手(て)がうずうずする。
My hands are itching to get started.
子供(こども)たちは外(そと)で遊(あそ)びたくてうずうずしていた。
The children were restless with eagerness to play outside.

USAGE:
うずうず(と) + verb / うずうずする

Describes an urge to act that the speaker is holding back. There is always something they want to do and are not yet doing — that gap is the whole meaning of the word.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜したくてうずうずする (to be itching to do ~) — by far the most common frame
  • 待(ま)ちきれなくてうずうずする (to be unable to wait)
  • 手(て)がうずうずする (one's hands are itching — eager to get to work, or to fight)
  • 血(ち)がうずうずする (one's blood is stirring)

NUANCE:
Forward-looking and usually positive: the anticipation is welcome even when the waiting is not. It describes the person's own inner state, so it is used freely of oneself and only inferentially of others.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • むずむず: itching. Covers a literal physical itch as well, and its restlessness is milder and more irritable than eager.
  • そわそわ: fidgety. Restlessness from nerves rather than desire, and it shows on the outside.
  • わくわく: excited. Pure pleasant anticipation, with no urge to act on it — you can わくわく while sitting perfectly still.
