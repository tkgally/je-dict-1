段取(だんど)り

だんどり
noun
preparation; arrangement; program; planning
1. preparation; arrangement; plan; schedule of steps
The organized sequence of steps or preparations needed to accomplish something smoothly.
会議(かいぎ)の段取(だんど)りを決(き)めよう。
Let's decide on the arrangements for the meeting.
段取(だんど)りが悪(わる)くて時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took a long time because the planning was poor.
段取(だんど)り通(どお)りに進(すす)めてください。
Please proceed according to the plan.

ORIGIN:
Originally a theater term referring to the steps (段(だん)) of a plot or the arrangement of scenes.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 段取(だんど)りを組(く)む / 立(た)てる (to make arrangements/plans)
  • 段取(だんど)りが良(よ)い / 悪(わる)い (good/bad planning)
  • 段取(だんど)り通(どお)りに (according to plan)
  • 段取(だんど)り八分(はちぶ) (good preparation is 80% of success)

USAGE NOTE:
段取(だんど)り emphasizes the practical sequence of steps rather than abstract planning. It's commonly used in work contexts where efficiency matters.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 準備(じゅんび) (preparation) - general preparation
  • 手順(てじゅん) (procedure) - specific sequence of steps
  • 段取(だんど)り - focuses on organizing workflow efficiently
