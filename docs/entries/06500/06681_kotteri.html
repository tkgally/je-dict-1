こってり

こってり
adverb, na-adjective (の)
rich; thick; heavy (flavor); heavily (applied)
1. rich; thick; heavy (of food/flavor)
Describes food that is rich in fat, oil, or strong flavors. Often refers to dishes with thick, creamy, or oily textures and bold tastes.
2. heavily; thickly (applied)
Describes something applied in a thick, heavy layer, such as makeup or paint.
こってりしたラーメンが好(す)きだ。
I like rich, heavy ramen.
今日(きょう)はこってりした料理(りょうり)は避(さ)けたい。
Today I want to avoid heavy, rich food.
彼女(かのじょ)はこってり化粧(けしょう)をしている。
She's wearing heavy makeup.

USAGE:

As adverb: こってり + verb

  • こってり味付(あじつ)けする (to season richly)
  • こってり塗(ぬ)る (to apply thickly)

As na-adjective: こってりした/こってりの + noun

  • こってりした味(あじ) (rich flavor)
  • こってりのスープ (thick, rich soup)

Sense 1 (food):

Describes ramen, stews, fried foods, and dishes with rich broths or fatty ingredients. Commonly used when ordering ramen:

  • こってり系(けい) (rich/heavy style)
  • あっさり系(けい) (light style)

Sense 2 (applied heavily):

  • こってり化粧(けしょう) (heavy makeup)
  • こってりペンキを塗(ぬ)る (to paint thickly)

ANTONYM:

  • あっさり (light, plain, refreshing) - opposite in taste/texture

Related Words

Antonym: あっさり (light/plain)
