悩(なや)み

なやみ
noun
worry; trouble; distress; problem
1. worry; trouble; concern; problem
Something that causes mental distress or preoccupation. Personal troubles or difficulties that one struggles with.
悩(なや)みがあったら、いつでも相談(そうだん)してね。
If you have any worries, feel free to talk to me anytime.
仕事(しごと)の悩(なや)みを抱(かか)えている。
I'm carrying worries about work.
彼女(かのじょ)は人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の悩(なや)みが多(おお)い。
She has a lot of troubles with interpersonal relationships.

悩(なや)み is the noun for a personal worry or problem — something one keeps turning over in one's mind. It is countable and concrete enough to be 'had,' 'carried,' or 'confided': the thing itself, not the state of worrying (that is 悩(なや)む).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悩(なや)みがある (to have worries)
  • 悩(なや)みを抱(かか)える (to carry/have worries)
  • 悩(なや)みを相談(そうだん)する (to consult about one's worries)
  • 悩(なや)みを打(う)ち明(あ)ける (to confide one's troubles)
  • 悩(なや)みの種(たね) (source of worry, cause of trouble)

TYPES OF 悩(なや)み:

  • 仕事(しごと)の悩(なや)み (work troubles)
  • 人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の悩(なや)み (relationship troubles)
  • 健康(けんこう)の悩(なや)み (health concerns)
  • 贅沢(ぜいたく)な悩(なや)み (a luxury problem, first-world problem)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 悩(なや)む (to worry, to be troubled — the verb this noun comes from)
  • 心配(しんぱい) (worry, anxiety — directed at a specific outcome, often on someone else's behalf)
  • 苦悩(くのう) (anguish, suffering — far more intense, and literary)
