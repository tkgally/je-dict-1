しゅんと

しゅんと
adverb
dejected, downcast, crestfallen
1. dejected, downcast, crestfallen
Mimetic word describing a sad, deflated state, typically after being scolded, disappointed, or losing enthusiasm.
怒(おこ)られて、しゅんとなった。
After being scolded, they became dejected.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けてしゅんとしている。
They're crestfallen after losing the game.
犬(いぬ)がしゅんとした顔(かお)で座(すわ)っている。
The dog is sitting there with a dejected look.
元気(げんき)だったのに、急(きゅう)にしゅんとなった。
They were energetic, but suddenly became downcast.

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo) describing emotional state.

VISUAL IMAGE: The sound suggests something deflating or drooping—like a balloon losing air. Often associated with:

  • Drooping shoulders
  • Lowered head
  • Loss of energy
  • Sad eyes

COMMON TRIGGERS:

  • Being scolded
  • Disappointment
  • Rejection
  • Failure
  • Bad news

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • しゅんとなる: become dejected
  • しゅんとしている: be in a dejected state
  • しゅんとした + noun: with a dejected [expression/manner]

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • がっかり: disappointed
  • しょんぼり: dejectedly, sadly
  • うなだれる: to hang one's head
  • 元気(げんき)がない: lacking energy

CONTRAST:

  • しゅんと: deflated, dejected
  • けろっと: nonchalant, unaffected (opposite)

OFTEN USED FOR:

  • Children after being scolded
  • Pets (especially dogs)
  • People who just received bad news
