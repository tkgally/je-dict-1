しゅんと

しゅんと
adverb
dejected, downcast, crestfallen
1. dejected, downcast, crestfallen
Mimetic word describing a sad, deflated state, typically after being scolded, disappointed, or losing enthusiasm.
怒(おこ)られて、しゅんとなった。
After being scolded, they became dejected.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けてしゅんとしている。
They're crestfallen after losing the game.
犬(いぬ)がしゅんとした顔(かお)で座(すわ)っている。
The dog is sitting there with a dejected look.
元気(げんき)だったのに、急(きゅう)にしゅんとなった。
They were energetic, but suddenly became downcast.

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo) describing an emotional state.

VISUAL IMAGE: The sound suggests something deflating - drooping shoulders, a lowered head, energy going out of a person all at once.

COMMON TRIGGERS: being scolded, disappointment, rejection, failure, bad news.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • しゅんとなる - to become dejected
  • しゅんとしている - to be in a dejected state
  • しゅんとした + noun - with a dejected expression or manner

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • がっかり - disappointed (focuses on the letdown itself)
  • しょんぼり - dejectedly, forlornly (a lasting sad state)
  • うなだれる - to hang one's head (the physical gesture)
  • 元気(げんき)がない - lacking energy (plainest wording)

CONTRAST:

  • しゅんと: deflated, visibly dejected
  • けろっと: nonchalant, unaffected - the opposite reaction

OFTEN USED FOR: children after a scolding, pets (dogs especially), and anyone who has just had bad news.

Related Words
