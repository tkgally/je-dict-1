1. to crawl out, to creep out
To emerge by crawling; to come out on hands and knees or by dragging oneself.
虫(むし)が穴(あな)から這(は)い出(で)てきた。
An insect crawled out of the hole.
赤(あか)ちゃんがベッドから這(は)い出(で)た。
The baby crawled out of the bed.
瓦礫(がれき)の下(した)から這(は)い出(で)ることができた。
I was able to crawl out from under the rubble.
布団(ふとん)から這(は)い出(で)る気力(きりょく)がない。
I don't have the energy to crawl out of the futon.

這(は)い出(で)る is a compound verb: 這(は)う (to crawl) + 出(で)る (to come out).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xから這(は)い出(で)る
  • No transitive pair.

ASPECT (ている):

  • 這(は)い出(で)ている - is in the process of crawling out
  • Telic: describes the action with a clear endpoint (emerging)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 穴(あな)から這(は)い出(で)る - to crawl out of a hole
  • 布団(ふとん)から這(は)い出(で)る - to drag oneself out of bed
  • やっと這(は)い出(で)る - to finally crawl out

NEAR-SYNONYM:
這(は)い出(だ)す is also intransitive and also means 'to crawl out'. 這(は)い出(で)る focuses on having emerged; 這(は)い出(だ)す leans on the start of the crawling motion.

FIGURATIVE USE:
Beyond literal crawling, 這(は)い出(で)る is common for escaping a bad situation with great effort: 貧困(ひんこん)から這(は)い出(で)る (to claw one's way out of poverty).

COMPOUND VERB NOTES:
The ～出(で)る pattern indicates movement outward. Combined with 這(は)う, it describes emerging by crawling motion.
