雑談(ざつだん)

ざつだん
noun
chat; small talk; casual conversation
1. chat; small talk; casual conversation
Informal conversation on random topics, not focused on any particular subject or purpose.
休憩(きゅうけい)時間(じかん)に同僚(どうりょう)と雑談(ざつだん)をした。
I chatted with my coworker during the break.
雑談(ざつだん)で盛(も)り上(あ)がった。
We got excited chatting about random things.
仕事(しごと)の話(はなし)はやめて雑談(ざつだん)しよう。
Let's stop talking about work and just chat.

WORD FORMATION:
雑(ざつ) (miscellaneous, various) + 談(だん) (talk, conversation) → miscellaneous talk

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 雑談(ざつだん)する (to chat, to have small talk)
  • 雑談(ざつだん)で盛(も)り上(あ)がる (to get excited chatting)
  • 雑談(ざつだん)力(りょく) (ability to make small talk - a valued social skill)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 世間話(せけんばなし) (small talk) - more about everyday topics, news
  • おしゃべり (chatting) - more casual, often longer conversations
  • 雑談(ざつだん) - general casual talk, often in work/formal settings
  • 井戸端(いどばた)会議(かいぎ) (gossip session) - informal neighborhood gossip

USAGE NOTE:
雑談(ざつだん) is neutral and often used in workplace contexts. 雑談(ざつだん)力(りょく) (small talk skills) is considered an important social skill in Japanese business culture for building relationships.
