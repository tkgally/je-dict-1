耕作(こうさく)

こうさく
noun; suru verb
cultivation; farming; tilling
1. cultivation; farming; tilling the soil
The act of preparing and working the land for growing crops.
この土地(とち)は耕作(こうさく)に適(てき)している。
This land is suitable for cultivation.
耕作(こうさく)放棄(ほうき)地(ち)が増(ふ)えている。
Abandoned farmland is increasing.
祖父(そふ)は長年(ながねん)この畑(はたけ)を耕作(こうさく)してきた。
My grandfather has cultivated this field for many years.

WORD FORMATION:

  • 耕(こう) (till, plow) + 作(さく) (make, cultivate)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 耕作(こうさく)地(ち) (cultivated land, farmland)
  • 耕作(こうさく)放棄(ほうき)地(ち) (abandoned farmland)
  • 耕作(こうさく)面積(めんせき) (cultivated area)
  • 耕作(こうさく)者(しゃ) (farmer, cultivator)

SOCIAL CONTEXT:

  • 耕作(こうさく)放棄(ほうき)地(ち) (abandoned farmland) is a growing issue in rural Japan due to aging farmers and rural depopulation
  • About 420,000 hectares of farmland were abandoned as of recent statistics

RELATED TERMS:

  • 農業(のうぎょう) (agriculture)
  • 栽培(さいばい) (cultivation of specific crops)
  • 耕(たがや)す (to plow - native Japanese word)
