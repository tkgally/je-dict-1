兆(きざ)し

きざし
noun
sign; indication; omen; first signs
1. sign; indication; first signs of something beginning
Early, often subtle indications that something is starting or about to happen.
春(はる)の兆(きざ)しが見(み)え始(はじ)めた。
Signs of spring have begun to appear.
回復(かいふく)の兆(きざ)しがある。
There are signs of recovery.
不況(ふきょう)の兆(きざ)しが見(み)える。
Signs of recession are visible.

兆(きざ)し is a noun from the verb 兆(きざ)す (to show signs of, to begin to appear). It names the first faint indication that a change is under way.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 兆(きざ)しが見(み)える / ある (signs are visible, signs are present)
  • 兆(きざ)しを見(み)せる (to show signs of)
  • Xの兆(きざ)し (signs of X)
  • 春(はる)の兆(きざ)し (signs of spring)
  • 回復(かいふく)の兆(きざ)し (signs of recovery)

USAGE:
兆(きざ)し describes subtle, gradual indicators — seasons turning, an economy picking up, an illness easing. The connotation is neutral to positive, and the change it points to is usually welcome or at least undramatic.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 前触(まえぶ)れ: harbinger or advance notice — can be dramatic or deliberate
  • 前兆(ぜんちょう): omen, typically of something significant and often bad

Related Words

Related: 前触(まえぶ)れ (harbinger)
Synonym: 前兆(ぜんちょう) (omen, premonition)
