おぼつかない

おぼつかない
i-adjective
uncertain; unreliable; unsteady

1. uncertain; doubtful; unreliable
Not certain or dependable; unlikely to succeed or be achieved.
記憶(きおく)がおぼつかない。
My memory is unreliable.
計画(けいかく)の成功(せいこう)はおぼつかない。
The success of the plan is uncertain.
この調子(ちょうし)では締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)うかおぼつかない。
At this rate, it's doubtful whether we'll make the deadline.
2. unsteady; shaky; wobbly
Not firm or stable in movement or posture.
足取(あしど)りがおぼつかない。
Their steps are unsteady.
赤(あか)ちゃんがおぼつかない足取(あしど)りで歩(ある)いている。
The baby is walking with unsteady steps.
酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)いがおぼつかない足取(あしど)りで駅(えき)に向(む)かっていた。
A drunk was heading toward the station with unsteady steps.

KANJI:
Rarely written 覚束(おぼつか)ない; hiragana is standard.

ETYMOLOGY:
From classical Japanese. The ない is **not** the negative suffix — it is part of the word itself, so おぼつかない is not the negative of anything. Compare せわしない and 切(せつ)ない, which are built the same way.

USAGE:

Commonly used with:

  • 見通(みとお)しがおぼつかない (prospects are uncertain)
  • 記憶(きおく)がおぼつかない (memory is unreliable)
  • 足元(あしもと)がおぼつかない (unsteady on one's feet)
  • 発音(はつおん)がおぼつかない (pronunciation is shaky)

Sense 1 describes an outcome or a faculty that cannot be relied on; sense 2 describes physical unsteadiness. Both take the thing in doubt with が.

REGISTER:
Slightly formal and literary; common in writing and in careful speech, less so in casual conversation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不確(ふたし)か - uncertain (plainer, sense 1)
  • 危(あや)うい - precarious, risky
  • ふらふら - unsteady, wobbly (sense 2, more physical)

Related Words

Synonym: 不確(ふたし)か (uncertain (plainer))
Related: ふらふら (unsteady, wobbly)
Related: せわしない (same non-negative ない formation)
