どうやら

どうやら
adverb
apparently; it seems; somehow; barely
1. apparently; it seems; it looks like
Indicates inference or conjecture based on evidence. The speaker is fairly confident but not completely certain.
どうやら雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうだ。
It looks like it's going to rain.
どうやら彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないらしい。
Apparently, he's not coming.
どうやら風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いたらしく、喉(のど)が痛(いた)い。
It seems I've caught a cold—my throat hurts.
2. somehow; barely; just barely
Indicates that something was accomplished with difficulty or just barely managed.
どうやら間(ま)に合(あ)った。
Somehow, I made it in time.
どうやら無事(ぶじ)に届(とど)いたみたいで安心(あんしん)した。
It seems it arrived safely, so I was relieved.
難(むずか)しい試験(しけん)だったが、どうやら合格(ごうかく)できたようだ。
It was a difficult exam, but it seems I somehow managed to pass.

SENSE 1 - INFERENCE:

どうやら marks the sentence as an inference the speaker has drawn from evidence in front of them, not as a fact they know directly. It pairs with a sentence-final evidential:

  • どうやら〜らしい (it seems that ~, from what I can tell)
  • どうやら〜ようだ / みたい (it appears that ~)
  • どうやら〜そうだ (it looks like ~)

Leaving the ending off - ×どうやら彼(かれ)は来(こ)ない - is a common learner error; the adverb sets up an inference that the sentence ending has to complete.

SENSE 2 - BARELY:

With a past-tense verb of achievement, どうやら says the outcome was reached, but only just, and not comfortably:

  • どうやら間(ま)に合(あ)った (somehow made it in time)
  • どうやら切(き)り抜(ぬ)けた (somehow got through it)

The two senses often overlap in practice, since a speaker reporting a narrow escape is usually also hedging about it.

REGISTER:
Neutral, and slightly more written or considered than the everyday alternatives; common in news writing and narration.

SIMILAR ADVERBS:

  • どうも - also introduces a hunch, but a vaguer one, and often about how the speaker feels rather than what the evidence shows
  • たぶん - probably; a judgment of likelihood, with no implication that evidence is at hand
  • もしかしたら - perhaps; much less confident than どうやら
  • はっきり - clearly; the opposite end of the certainty scale

Related Words

Synonym: どうも (somehow, it seems (vaguer hunch))
Related: たぶん (probably)
