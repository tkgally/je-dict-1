1. losing streak, consecutive losses
A series of successive defeats without any wins in between. Used in sports, games, competitions, and other contests.
チームは今(いま)、5連敗(れんぱい)中(ちゅう)だ。
The team is currently on a 5-game losing streak.
連敗(れんぱい)を止(と)めたい。
I want to stop the losing streak.
連敗(れんぱい)が続(つづ)いてチームの士気(しき)が下(さ)がっている。
The continuous losses have lowered the team's morale.
やっと連敗(れんぱい)を脱出(だっしゅつ)して一勝(いっしょう)できた。
We finally broke out of the losing streak and got a win.
10連敗(れんぱい)もしたら監督(かんとく)の責任(せきにん)が問(と)われるだろう。
If they lose 10 in a row, the manager will surely be held accountable.

連敗(れんぱい) is a run of consecutive defeats, most often in sports reporting but also in elections, court cases, and any repeated contest. A number attaches directly with no counter in between: 3連敗(れんぱい) (three straight losses), 10連敗(れんぱい) (ten straight losses).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • As a する-verb it takes no object — the loser is the subject: チームが3連敗(れんぱい)した.
  • N連敗(れんぱい)中(ちゅう): the streak is still running.
  • 連敗(れんぱい)が続(つづ)く: the losses go on.
  • 連敗(れんぱい)を止(と)める / 連敗(れんぱい)を脱出(だっしゅつ)する: break the streak.
  • 連敗(れんぱい)を喫(きっ)する: suffer consecutive losses (formal, newspaper style).

The streak counts only losses, so a single 一勝(いっしょう) ends it — which is why the two words so often appear in the same sentence.

WORD FORMATION: 連(れん) (consecutive) + 敗(はい) (defeat). The same 連(れん) gives 連勝(れんしょう) and 連続(れんぞく).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連勝(れんしょう) is the antonym and behaves identically, except that a winning streak normally 止(と)まる (intransitive) while a losing streak is something you 止(と)める.
  • 連続(れんぞく)敗北(はいぼく) is the formal, non-sporting way to say the same thing.
  • スランプ and 低迷(ていめい) describe the poor form behind a streak rather than the losses themselves.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 連敗(れんぱい)脱出(だっしゅつ): breaking out of a losing streak (headline style)
  • 泥沼(どろぬま)の連敗(れんぱい): a quagmire of consecutive losses
  • 連敗(れんぱい)地獄(じごく): losing-streak hell (informal)

Related Words

Antonym: 連勝(れんしょう) (winning streak)
