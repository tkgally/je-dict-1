聞(き)き出(だ)す

ききだす
verb (godan, transitive)
to get information out of; to draw out

Definitions

1. to get information out of; to draw out; to extract
To skillfully question someone to obtain information they may be reluctant to share. Implies persistence or persuasion in the questioning.

Examples

刑事(けいじ)は容疑者(ようぎしゃ)から真実(しんじつ)を聞(き)き出(だ)した。
The detective got the truth out of the suspect.
子供(こども)から学校(がっこう)での出来事(できごと)を聞(き)き出(だ)すのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to get children to tell you what happened at school.
うまく聞(き)き出(だ)せば、秘密(ひみつ)を教(おし)えてくれるかもしれない。
If you ask skillfully, they might tell you the secret.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 聞(き)き出(だ)す → 聞(き)き出(だ)さない (negative)
  • 聞(き)き出(だ)す → 聞(き)き出(だ)して (te-form)
  • 聞(き)き出(だ)す → 聞(き)き出(だ)した (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 聞(き)く (to ask/listen) + 出(だ)す (out)

The ～出(だ)す pattern here indicates extracting or bringing out something hidden.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報(じょうほう)を聞(き)き出(だ)す - to extract information
  • 秘密(ひみつ)を聞(き)き出(だ)す - to get a secret out of someone
  • 本音(ほんね)を聞(き)き出(だ)す - to draw out someone's true feelings
  • 証言(しょうげん)を聞(き)き出(だ)す - to obtain testimony

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. The person questioned takes から, and the information extracted takes を.

Related Words

Related: 聞(き)く (to ask/listen)
