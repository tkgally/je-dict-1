俄雨(にわかあめ)

にわかあめ
noun
sudden rain shower; passing shower
1. sudden rain shower; passing shower
Rain that starts suddenly and usually stops after a short time. A brief, unexpected shower.
俄雨(にわかあめ)に降(ふ)られて濡(ぬ)れてしまった。
I got caught in a sudden shower and got soaked.
午後(ごご)は俄雨(にわかあめ)の可能性(かのうせい)があります。
There's a chance of sudden showers this afternoon.
俄雨(にわかあめ)が止(や)むまで軒下(のきした)で待(ま)った。
I waited under the eaves until the sudden shower stopped.

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • Starts suddenly without much warning
  • Usually stops after a short time
  • Common in summer months in Japan

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 俄(にわか) (sudden, unexpected) + 雨(あめ) (rain)

RELATED WEATHER TERMS:

  • 夕立(ゆうだち) (evening rain shower - specifically in summer, often with thunder)
  • 通(とお)り雨(あめ) (passing shower - the closest synonym)
  • ゲリラ豪雨(ごうう) (guerrilla rainstorm - modern term for sudden heavy downpours)

WEATHER FORECAST EXPRESSION:

  • 俄雨(にわかあめ)に注意(ちゅうい) (beware of sudden showers)
  • 所(ところ)により俄雨(にわかあめ) (showers in some areas)

WRITING:
Weather forecasts and news usually write this word as にわか雨(あめ), since 俄(にわか) is outside the common-use kanji list. Both spellings are read にわかあめ.

CULTURAL NOTE:
俄雨(にわかあめ) is a common occurrence in Japanese summers, especially during the 梅雨(つゆ) (rainy season) and 夏(なつ). Carrying a 折(お)りたたみ傘(がさ) (folding umbrella) is standard practice in Japan.
