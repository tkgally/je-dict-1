1. to discontinue; to terminate
To end something before its natural conclusion, often used for projects, broadcasts, or serializations.
連載(れんさい)が打(う)ち切(き)られた。
The serialization was discontinued.
その番組(ばんぐみ)は視聴率(しちょうりつ)低迷(ていめい)で打(う)ち切(き)られた。
That TV show was cancelled due to low ratings.
予算(よさん)不足(ふそく)でプロジェクトを打(う)ち切(き)ることになった。
The project was discontinued due to budget shortfall.
政府(せいふ)は被災地(ひさいち)への援助(えんじょ)を打(う)ち切(き)る方針(ほうしん)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The government announced a policy to cut off aid to the disaster-affected areas.
売(う)り上(あ)げが伸(の)びず、新商品(しんしょうひん)の生産(せいさん)は半年(はんとし)で打(う)ち切(き)られることになった。
Sales didn't grow, and production of the new product was terminated after six months.
2. to cut short; to break off
To abruptly end a conversation, meeting, or activity.
話(はなし)を打(う)ち切(き)った。
I cut short the conversation.
時間切(じかんぎ)れで議論(ぎろん)を打(う)ち切(き)った。
We cut short the discussion because we ran out of time.
司会者(しかいしゃ)が質疑応答(しつぎおうとう)を打(う)ち切(き)った。
The moderator cut short the Q&A session.
相手(あいて)の態度(たいど)に腹(はら)が立(た)って、交渉(こうしょう)を打(う)ち切(き)った。
I got angry at the other party's attitude and broke off the negotiations.
会議(かいぎ)が長引(ながび)いていたので、部長(ぶちょう)が途中(とちゅう)で打(う)ち切(き)って結論(けつろん)をまとめた。
The meeting was dragging on, so the department head cut it short and summarized the conclusion.

FORMATION:
A compound verb: 打(う)つ (to strike) + 切(き)る (to cut). It means ending something part-way rather than letting it run to its natural finish.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 番組(ばんぐみ)を打(う)ち切(き)る — to cancel a TV show
  • 連載(れんさい)を打(う)ち切(き)る — to end a serialization
  • 交渉(こうしょう)を打(う)ち切(き)る — to break off negotiations
  • 援助(えんじょ)を打(う)ち切(き)る — to cut off aid

USAGE:
The decision normally comes from an authority — a broadcaster, a publisher, a government — so the passive 打(う)ち切(き)られる is at least as common as the active form: the thing *was* discontinued, with no need to name who did it. The related noun 打(う)ち切(き)り (discontinuation, cancellation) is what appears in headlines and notices: 連載(れんさい)打(う)ち切(き)り.
