投手(とうしゅ)

とうしゅ
noun
pitcher (baseball)
1. pitcher
The player who throws the ball to the batter in baseball. One of the most important defensive positions.
先発(せんぱつ)投手(とうしゅ)が7回(ななかい)を無失点(むしってん)に抑(おさ)えた。
The starting pitcher held the opponents scoreless through seven innings.
彼(かれ)は高校(こうこう)時代(じだい)、エース投手(とうしゅ)だった。
He was the ace pitcher during high school.
投手(とうしゅ)交代(こうたい)が告(つ)げられた。
A pitching change was announced.

ALTERNATIVE TERMS:

  • ピッチャー (pitcher - loanword, equally common)

TYPES OF PITCHERS:

  • 先発(せんぱつ)投手(とうしゅ) (starting pitcher)
  • 中(なか)継(つ)ぎ (relief pitcher, middle reliever)
  • 抑(おさ)え／クローザー (closer)
  • 敗戦(はいせん)処理(しょり)投手(とうしゅ) (mop-up pitcher)

KEY STATISTICS:

  • 防御率(ぼうぎょりつ) (ERA - earned run average)
  • 奪三振(だつさんしん) (strikeouts)
  • 勝利(しょうり)投手(とうしゅ) (winning pitcher)
  • 敗戦(はいせん)投手(とうしゅ) (losing pitcher)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投手(とうしゅ)を交代(こうたい)する (to change pitchers)
  • 投手(とうしゅ)が崩(くず)れる (pitcher falls apart/loses effectiveness)
  • 投手(とうしゅ)陣(じん) (pitching staff)
  • エース投手(とうしゅ) (ace pitcher)

Related Words

Antonym: 打者(だしゃ) (batter)
Homophone: 当主(とうしゅ) (head of a household, family head)
Related: 捕手(ほしゅ) (catcher)
Contrast: 野手(やしゅ) (fielder)
Related: 投(な)げる (verb: to pitch, to throw)
