腰(こし)が低(ひく)い

こしがひくい
expression
humble, modest, unassuming
1. humble, modest, polite in manner
Describes someone who behaves with humility and politeness, not putting on airs despite their status.
社長(しゃちょう)なのに腰(こし)が低(ひく)い人(ひと)だ。
Despite being the company president, he's a humble person.
腰(こし)が低(ひく)い態度(たいど)で接客(せっきゃく)する。
To serve customers with a humble attitude.
有名(ゆうめい)になっても腰(こし)が低(ひく)いままだ。
Even after becoming famous, they remain modest.

STRUCTURE: 腰(こし) (waist/hip) + が + 低(ひく)い (low). The image is of bowing low - a sign of humility and respect in Japanese culture.

ANTONYM: 腰(こし)が高(たか)い (arrogant, haughty)

POSITIVE CONNOTATION: In Japanese culture, being 腰(こし)が低(ひく)い is generally seen as virtuous, especially for people in positions of power or fame.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 謙虚(けんきょ) (humble, modest) - adjective
  • 横柄(おうへい) (arrogant) - opposite trait
  • 偉(えら)ぶらない (not putting on airs)

CONTRAST WITH 腰(こし) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腰(こし)が低(ひく)い: humble manner
  • 腰(こし)が重(おも)い: slow to act, reluctant
  • 腰(こし)を据(す)える: to settle down and commit

Related Words

Antonym: 横柄(おうへい) (arrogant)
