吐(は)き出(だ)す

はきだす
verb (godan, transitive)
to spit out; to emit; to disgorge
1. to spit out; to vomit
To expel something from the mouth.
2. to emit; to discharge; to output
To release or produce something, often in technical or industrial contexts.
3. to let out; to confess
To reveal what one has been holding back.
口(くち)に入(はい)った砂(すな)を吐(は)き出(だ)した。
I spat out the sand that got in my mouth.
この工場(こうじょう)は大量(たいりょう)の煙(けむり)を吐(は)き出(だ)している。
This factory emits large amounts of smoke.
溜(た)め込(こ)んでいた不満(ふまん)を全部(ぜんぶ)吐(は)き出(だ)した。
I let out all the frustration I had been bottling up.

CONJUGATION:

  • 吐(は)き出(だ)す → 吐(は)き出(だ)さない (negative)
  • 吐(は)き出(だ)す → 吐(は)き出(だ)して (te-form)
  • 吐(は)き出(だ)す → 吐(は)き出(だ)した (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 吐(は)く (to spit, to vomit) + 出(だ)す (to put out)

USAGE:

  • 煙(けむり)を吐(は)き出(だ)す (to emit smoke)
  • 二酸化炭素(にさんかたんそ)を吐(は)き出(だ)す (to emit CO2)
  • 本音(ほんね)を吐(は)き出(だ)す (to reveal one's true feelings)
  • データを吐(は)き出(だ)す (to output data - computing)
