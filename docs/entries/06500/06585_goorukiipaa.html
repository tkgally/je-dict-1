ゴールキーパー

ごーるきーぱー
noun
goalkeeper; goalie
1. goalkeeper; goalie
The player in soccer, hockey, or similar sports who defends the goal and is the only player allowed to use their hands within the penalty area.
ゴールキーパーが見事(みごと)なセーブを見(み)せた。
The goalkeeper made a brilliant save.
彼(かれ)は子供(こども)の頃(ころ)からゴールキーパーをしている。
He has been playing goalkeeper since childhood.
ゴールキーパーが飛(と)び出(だ)してボール(ぼーる)をキャッチした。
The goalkeeper came out and caught the ball.

ゴールキーパー is a loanword from English "goalkeeper," referring to the player who defends the goal in sports like soccer and hockey. In Japanese soccer discussion, the abbreviated forms are far more common in casual speech.

ABBREVIATIONS:

  • GK: written abbreviation (in articles, stats, line-ups)
  • キーパー: common spoken abbreviation

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴールキーパーをする: to play goalkeeper
  • ゴールキーパーを務(つと)める: to serve as goalkeeper (formal)
  • 正(せい)ゴールキーパー: starting goalkeeper
  • 控(ひか)えのゴールキーパー: backup goalkeeper

OTHER SOCCER POSITIONS:

  • ディフェンダー (DF): defender
  • ミッドフィルダー (MF): midfielder
  • フォワード (FW): forward

RELATED TERMS:

  • セーブ: save (stopping a shot)
  • PK戦(せん): penalty shootout
  • 無失点(むしってん): clean sheet (no goals conceded)
