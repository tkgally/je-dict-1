切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)

きっぷうりば
noun
ticket booth; ticket counter
1. ticket booth; ticket counter; ticket window
A location where tickets are sold, especially at train stations.
切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)はどこですか。
Where is the ticket counter?
切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)で切符(きっぷ)を買(か)った。
I bought a ticket at the ticket counter.
切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)で定期券(ていきけん)を買(か)った。
I bought a commuter pass at the ticket counter.
駅(えき)の切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)には長(なが)い列(れつ)ができていた。
There was a long line at the station's ticket booth.
券売機(けんばいき)が使(つか)えなかったので、切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)の窓口(まどぐち)で買(か)うことにした。
Since the ticket machine wasn't working, I decided to buy at the ticket counter window.

切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば) is the place in a station or venue where tickets are sold, whether a staffed window or the ticket area as a whole. It is the everyday word a traveler uses when asking where to buy a ticket.

STRUCTURE:
切符(きっぷ) (ticket) + 売(う)り場(ば) (selling place). The second element is the general-purpose word for a place where something is sold, so the same pattern gives チケット売(う)り場(ば) and 肉(にく)売(う)り場(ば).

MODERN CONTEXT:

Many stations now have 券売機(けんばいき) (ticket vending machines), but a staffed 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば) still exists for:

  • 定期券(ていきけん) (commuter passes)
  • 特急券(とっきゅうけん) (limited express tickets)
  • Complex or multi-leg routes
  • Assistance with fares and refunds

SYNONYMS:

  • 窓口(まどぐち) - service window or counter; the staffed window inside a 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)
  • チケット売(う)り場(ば) - the usual wording for concerts, sports, and other events, where 切符(きっぷ) sounds dated

RELATED TERMS:

  • 券売機(けんばいき) - ticket vending machine
  • 改札口(かいさつぐち) - ticket gate
  • 駅(えき) - station

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)で買(か)う - to buy at the ticket counter
  • 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)に並(なら)ぶ - to queue at the ticket counter
  • 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば)はどこですか - Where is the ticket counter?
