お椀(わん)

おわん
noun
soup bowl
1. soup bowl, lacquered wooden bowl
A small bowl, typically made of lacquered wood, used for serving miso soup and other Japanese soups. Features a lid to keep the soup warm.
お椀(わん)に味噌汁(みそしる)をよそう。
Ladle miso soup into the bowl.
漆(うるし)塗(ぬ)りのお椀(わん)は手入(てい)れが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Lacquered soup bowls require careful maintenance.
お椀(わん)の蓋(ふた)を取(と)ると湯気(ゆげ)が立(た)った。
Steam rose when I removed the lid of the soup bowl.

MATERIALS AND TYPES:

  • 漆(うるし)塗(ぬ)りのお椀(わん) (lacquered soup bowl) - traditional, higher quality
  • 木製(もくせい)のお椀(わん) (wooden soup bowl)
  • プラスチックのお椀(わん) (plastic soup bowl) - common in casual settings

VS OTHER BOWLS:

  • お椀(わん) - specifically for soup, usually with a lid
  • 茶碗(ちゃわん) - rice bowl (or tea cup in tea ceremony)
  • 丼(どんぶり) - larger bowl for donburi dishes
  • 鉢(はち) - general bowl, often deeper

USAGE IN JAPANESE DINING: お椀(わん) is part of a traditional Japanese meal setting. It's held in the left hand while eating, and the lid is placed inverted on the table. At the end of the meal, replace the lid.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お椀(わん)に盛(も)る (to serve in a bowl)
  • お椀(わん)の蓋(ふた) (lid of the soup bowl)
  • 塗(ぬ)りのお椀(わん) (lacquered soup bowl)
