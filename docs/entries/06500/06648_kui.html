悔(く)い

くい
noun
regret; remorse
1. regret; remorse; repentance
A feeling of sadness or disappointment about something one did or failed to do. Often used about past actions that cannot be undone.
あの時(とき)もっと努力(どりょく)していればと悔(く)いが残(のこ)る。
I regret not having tried harder back then.
悔(く)いのない人生(じんせい)を送(おく)りたい。
I want to live a life without regrets.
彼(かれ)は一生(いっしょう)その悔(く)いを抱(かか)えて生(い)きた。
He lived his whole life carrying that regret.
あの時(とき)声(こえ)をかけていれば、こんな悔(く)いは残(のこ)らなかっただろう。
If I had spoken up back then, I probably wouldn't have this regret.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けたのは悔(くや)しいが、全力(ぜんりょく)を出(だ)し切(き)ったので悔(く)いはない。
Losing the match is frustrating, but I gave it everything I had, so I have no regrets.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 悔(く)いが残(のこ)る (to be left with regret)
  • 悔(く)いのない〜 (a 〜 without regret)
  • 悔(く)いを残(のこ)す (to leave regrets behind)
  • 悔(く)いを抱(かか)える (to carry a regret)
  • 悔(く)い改(あらた)める (to repent, to reform oneself)

USAGE:
悔(く)い is more literary than 後悔(こうかい) and appears mostly in the set phrases above; in everyday conversation 後悔(こうかい)する is the ordinary choice. 悔(く)いのない選択(せんたく)をする (to make a choice one will not regret) is common when facing a major decision.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 後悔(こうかい) (regret): the everyday word, a noun and する verb
  • 悔(く)いる (to regret): the verb this noun comes from
  • 悔(くや)む (to regret, to lament): also used of mourning the dead
  • 悔(くや)しい (frustrating, mortifying): about being beaten or thwarted, not about one's own choices
