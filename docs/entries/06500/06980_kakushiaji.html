隠(かく)し味(あじ)

かくしあじ
noun
secret ingredient; hidden flavor

Definitions

1. secret ingredient; hidden flavor
A small amount of an ingredient added to enhance flavor without being detectably present. The ingredient improves the dish subtly but isn't meant to be identified.

Examples

このカレーの隠(かく)し味(あじ)は蜂蜜(はちみつ)だ。
The secret ingredient in this curry is honey.
隠(かく)し味(あじ)に味噌(みそ)を少(すこ)し加(くわ)える。
Add a little miso as a secret ingredient.
隠(かく)し味(あじ)が効(き)いていて、深(ふか)い味(あじ)わいになっている。
The secret ingredient works well, giving it a deep flavor.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 隠(かく)し味(あじ)を加(くわ)える (to add a secret ingredient)
  • 隠(かく)し味(あじ)に〜を使(つか)う (to use ~ as a secret ingredient)
  • 隠(かく)し味(あじ)が効(き)く (the secret ingredient works/takes effect)

COMMON 隠(かく)し味(あじ) INGREDIENTS:

  • 蜂蜜(はちみつ) (honey) - in curry, dressings
  • 味噌(みそ) (miso) - in soups, stews
  • 醤油(しょうゆ) (soy sauce) - in Western dishes
  • 酢(す) (vinegar) - to brighten flavors
  • 牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう) (milk) - in tomato-based dishes
  • インスタントコーヒー (instant coffee) - in chocolate dishes

NUANCE:
The key concept is that the ingredient enhances flavor without being obviously detectable. If you can clearly taste it, it's no longer a 隠(かく)し味(あじ).

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 隠(かく)す (to hide) + 味(あじ) (flavor) = hidden flavor
