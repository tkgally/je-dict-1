不動産屋(ふどうさんや)

ふどうさんや
noun
real estate agent; real estate agency
1. real estate agent; real estate agency
A person or business that deals with the buying, selling, and renting of properties. Common casual term for real estate professionals in Japan.
不動産屋(ふどうさんや)で部屋(へや)を探(さが)した。
I looked for an apartment at a real estate agency.
駅前(えきまえ)に不動産屋(ふどうさんや)がたくさんある。
There are many real estate agencies in front of the station.
不動産屋(ふどうさんや)に仲介(ちゅうかい)手数料(てすうりょう)を払(はら)った。
I paid a brokerage fee to the real estate agent.

ETYMOLOGY:
不動産(ふどうさん) (real estate) + 屋(や) (shop/business suffix)

FORMAL ALTERNATIVES:

  • 不動産(ふどうさん)会社(かいしゃ) (real estate company)
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)業者(ぎょうしゃ) (real estate dealer)
  • 宅地(たくち)建物(たてもの)取引(とりひき)業者(ぎょうしゃ) (licensed real estate transaction business - official term)

LICENSING:

Real estate agents in Japan need:

  • 宅地(たくち)建物(たてもの)取引士(とりひきし) (real estate transaction specialist license)
  • 宅建(たっけん) (abbreviation for the above license/exam)

COMMON SERVICES:

  • 賃貸(ちんたい)物件(ぶっけん) (rental properties)
  • 売買(ばいばい)物件(ぶっけん) (properties for sale)
  • 部屋(へや)探(さが)し (apartment hunting)

FEES:

  • 仲介(ちゅうかい)手数料(てすうりょう) (brokerage fee) - typically 1 month's rent for rentals
  • 礼金(れいきん)/敷金(しききん) (key money/security deposit) - arranged through agent

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不動産屋(ふどうさんや)に行(い)く (to go to a real estate agency)
  • 不動産屋(ふどうさんや)で物件(ぶっけん)を探(さが)す (to look for properties at an agency)
  • 不動産屋(ふどうさんや)を通(とお)す (to go through a real estate agent)

Related Words
