それなのに

それなのに
conjunction
even so, nevertheless, and yet
1. even so, nevertheless, and yet
Conjunction expressing contrast or disappointment when reality doesn't match expectation, often with emotional overtone.
あんなに練習(れんしゅう)したのに。それなのに負(ま)けてしまった。
I practiced so much. And yet I lost.
約束(やくそく)したじゃない。それなのに来(こ)なかったの？
You promised, didn't you? And yet you didn't come?
彼(かれ)のために尽(つ)くした。それなのに裏切(うらぎ)られた。
I devoted myself to him. Even so, I was betrayed.
注意(ちゅうい)したのに。それなのに同(おな)じミスをした。
I warned them. Nevertheless, they made the same mistake.
毎日(まいにち)勉強(べんきょう)した。それなのに試験(しけん)に落(お)ちてしまった。本当(ほんとう)にショックだ。
I studied every day. And yet I failed the exam. I'm truly in shock.

COMPOSITION: それ (that) + なのに (despite being).

NUANCE: Carries emotional weight — frustration, disappointment, or indignation. The speaker expected a different outcome from what the first statement led them to expect.

COMPARISON:

  • それでも — "even so" (more neutral; carrying on despite the circumstances)
  • それなのに — "and yet" (emotional contrast; an expectation was violated)
  • にもかかわらず — "despite" (formal, factual)

USAGE PATTERNS:
1. Between sentences: 〜した。それなのに〜 (I did ~. And yet ~)
2. Complaint: showing frustration that effort went unrewarded
3. Betrayal: when trust has been violated

REGISTER: Neutral to slightly informal. Natural in both speech and writing.

RELATED FORMS:

  • のに joins clauses inside one sentence: 頑張(がんば)ったのにだめだった (I tried hard, but it was no good). それなのに starts a new sentence instead.
  • にもかかわらず — more formal "nevertheless"
  • それでも — more accepting "even so"

Related Words

Related: のに (same contrast, within one sentence)
Contrast: それでも (neutral 'even so')
Synonym: にも関(かか)わらず (formal 'despite')
