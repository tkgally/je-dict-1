目(め)を通(とお)す

めをとおす
expression
to look through, to skim, to glance over

Definitions

1. to look through, to skim, to glance over
To read or review something quickly to get the general content without reading every detail.

Examples

報告書(ほうこくしょ)に目(め)を通(とお)しておいてください。
Please look over the report.
契約書(けいやくしょ)には必(かなら)ず目(め)を通(とお)すべきだ。
You should always look through contracts.
朝(あさ)、新聞(しんぶん)に目(め)を通(とお)す。
I skim the newspaper in the morning.
資料(しりょう)にざっと目(め)を通(とお)した。
I quickly glanced through the materials.

Notes

STRUCTURE: 目(め) (eye) + を + 通(とお)す (to pass through). Literally 'to pass one's eyes through' something.

NUANCE: Implies quick review rather than careful, thorough reading. Often used for:

  • Documents, reports, contracts
  • Newspapers, magazines
  • Email, messages

DEGREES OF THOROUGHNESS:

  • 目(め)を通(とお)す: skim, look over (quick)
  • ざっと目(め)を通(とお)す: very quick glance
  • しっかり目(め)を通(とお)す: look through carefully

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 読(よ)む (to read) - general, neutral
  • 熟読(じゅくどく)する (to read thoroughly) - careful reading
  • 斜(なな)め読(よ)み (skimming) - reading diagonally
  • 一読(いちどく)する (to read once through)

OTHER 目(め) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)が離(はな)せない: can't take eyes off
  • 目(め)を配(くば)る: to keep an eye on
  • 目(め)を引(ひ)く: to catch one's eye
