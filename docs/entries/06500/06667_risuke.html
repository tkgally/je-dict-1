1. reschedule; rescheduling; change of schedule
To change the scheduled time of a meeting, appointment, or event. Common business slang abbreviated from リスケジュール (reschedule).
会議(かいぎ)をリスケしてもいいですか。
Can we reschedule the meeting?
急(きゅう)な予定(よてい)が入(はい)ったので、リスケをお願(ねが)いしたい。
Something urgent came up, so I'd like to ask to reschedule.
明日(あした)の打(う)ち合(あ)わせ、リスケになりました。
Tomorrow's meeting has been rescheduled.

ABBREVIATION:
リスケ is clipped from リスケジュール (reschedule), following the standard Japanese pattern of cutting a loanword to about four moras.

USAGE:

  • リスケする (to reschedule)
  • リスケになる (to be rescheduled - the usual way to report it, since the decision is treated as coming from the situation rather than from a person)
  • リスケをお願(ねが)いする (to request a reschedule)

Note that リスケ means moving an appointment to a different time, not cancelling it. In finance the same word is also used for restructuring a loan repayment schedule.

REGISTER:

Casual in-house business slang - fine in a chat message or among colleagues, out of place in a letter to a client. More formal alternatives:

  • 日程(にってい)を変更(へんこう)する (to change the schedule)
  • 予定(よてい)を調整(ちょうせい)する (to adjust the schedule)
  • 延期(えんき)する (to postpone - only when the new time is later)

SIMILAR ABBREVIATIONS:

  • アポ (appointment - from アポイントメント)
  • プレゼン (presentation - from プレゼンテーション)
