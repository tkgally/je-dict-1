ともかく

ともかく
adverb
anyhow; anyway; in any case
1. anyhow; anyway; in any case; at any rate
Sets aside other considerations to focus on the main point.
ともかく行(い)こう。
Let's go anyway.
ともかく、まず食(た)べよう。
Anyway, let's eat first.
ともかく連絡(れんらく)してみたら?
Why don't you try contacting them anyway?
理由(りゆう)はともかく、ともかく早(はや)く来(き)てください。
Never mind the reason, just come quickly.
難(むずか)しいかもしれないが、ともかくやってみなければ分(わ)からない。
It might be difficult, but you won't know unless you try anyway.
2. setting aside; leaving aside
Used in the pattern ～はともかく to mean 'leaving ~ aside'.
値段(ねだん)はともかく、味(あじ)はいい。
Setting aside the price, the taste is good.
結果(けっか)はともかく、全力(ぜんりょく)を尽(つ)くした。
Leaving the result aside, I gave it my all.
見(み)た目(め)はともかく、使(つか)いやすさが大事(だいじ)だ。
Appearance aside, ease of use is what matters.
好(す)き嫌(きら)いはともかく、体(からだ)にいいから食(た)べなさい。
Whether you like it or not aside, eat it because it's good for you.
仕事(しごと)はともかく、健康(けんこう)を大切(たいせつ)にしてほしい。
Work aside, I want you to take care of your health.

ともかく is used to set aside details or complications and focus on the main point.

TWO MAIN USES:

1. SENTENCE OPENER — "anyway, in any case":

Sets aside the previous topic to move to what matters.

  • ともかく行(い)こう: let's go anyway
  • ともかくやってみよう: let's try it anyway

2. ～はともかく — "setting ~ aside":

Acknowledges one thing but shifts focus to another. The set-aside item is marked with は, and what follows is the point being made.

  • 値段(ねだん)はともかく: price aside
  • 結果(けっか)はともかく: result aside
  • 〜はともかくとして is a slightly heavier variant of the same pattern.

CONTRAST:

  • ともかく: gently sets aside (softer, more balanced)
  • とにかく: more emphatic, insistent ("just do it!"). Only ともかく takes the 〜は…pattern of use 2; とにかく is an opener only.
  • いずれにせよ: in any case (formal, written)

VARIANT:
とにもかくにも is an emphatic literary form of ともかく.

Related Words

Synonym: とにかく (more emphatic)
Related: いずれにせよ (formal equivalent)
