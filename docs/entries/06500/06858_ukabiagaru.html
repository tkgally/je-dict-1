浮(う)かび上(あ)がる

うかびあがる
verb (godan, intransitive)
to surface; to emerge; to come to light

1. to surface; to rise to the surface
To come up from underwater or below the surface.
潜水艦(せんすいかん)が海面(かいめん)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がった。
The submarine surfaced.
泡(あわ)が水面(すいめん)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がった。
Bubbles rose to the surface.
沈(しず)んでいた船(ふね)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がってきた。
The sunken ship came back up to the surface.
ダイバーがゆっくりと海面(かいめん)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がった。
The diver slowly surfaced.
深(ふか)く潜(もぐ)った後(あと)、一気(いっき)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がるのは危険(きけん)だ。
It's dangerous to surface all at once after diving deep.
2. to emerge; to come to light; to become clear
For something hidden or unclear to become apparent or visible.
調査(ちょうさ)で新(あたら)しい事実(じじつ)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がってきた。
New facts have come to light through the investigation.
問題点(もんだいてん)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる。
Problems are emerging.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がった。
A suspect has emerged.
証拠(しょうこ)が集(あつ)まるにつれて真相(しんそう)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がってきた。
As the evidence gathered, the truth came to light.
データを分析(ぶんせき)した結果(けっか)、意外(いがい)な事実(じじつ)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がった。
As a result of analyzing the data, an unexpected fact came to light.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 浮(う)かぶ (to float) + 上(あ)がる (to rise)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 〜が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる - intransitive in every sense; the thing that surfaces or emerges takes が
  • 〜に浮(う)かび上(あ)がる - the surface or background it rises to: 海面(かいめん)に, 闇(やみ)の中(なか)に
  • 〜から浮(う)かび上(あ)がる - what it comes out of: データから, 調査(ちょうさ)から

ASPECT:
This is a change-of-state verb, so 浮(う)かび上(あ)がっている describes the resulting state - the thing is now up, or now visible - rather than an action in progress. The form 浮(う)かび上(あ)がってくる, very common in sense 2, adds the sense of the fact coming into view step by step.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is physical: 潜水艦(せんすいかん), 泡(あわ), a sunken ship, a ダイバー.

Sense 2 is standard vocabulary in news and investigation writing:

  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (a suspect emerges)
  • 疑惑(ぎわく)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (suspicions arise)
  • 真相(しんそう)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (the truth comes to light)
  • 課題(かだい)が浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (issues emerge)

A third use, not split out as a separate sense here, is visual: a shape standing out against a dark or plain background, as in ライトに照(て)らされて建物(たてもの)が闇(やみ)の中(なか)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (lit up, the building stands out of the darkness).

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 浮(う)き彫(ぼ)りになる - to be thrown into relief; a near-synonym of sense 2, and slightly more formal

CONTRAST:
The base verb 浮(う)かぶ on its own can mean "to come to mind", but 浮(う)かび上(あ)がる does not: an idea is 頭(あたま)に浮(う)かぶ, never 頭(あたま)に浮(う)かび上(あ)がる.

Related Words

Related: 浮(う)かぶ (to float; to come to mind (base verb))
Related: 上(あ)がる (to rise, to go up)
Synonym: 浮(う)き彫(ぼ)り (bringing to light (浮き彫りになる))
