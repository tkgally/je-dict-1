スタメン

すためん
noun
starting lineup; starting member
1. starting lineup; starting member; first-choice player
Abbreviation of スターティングメンバー (starting member). Refers to the players who start a game, as opposed to substitutes.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)のスタメンが発表(はっぴょう)された。
Today's starting lineup was announced.
彼(かれ)は怪我(けが)から復帰(ふっき)してスタメンに戻(もど)った。
He returned to the starting lineup after recovering from his injury.
スタメン落(お)ちして悔(くや)しい。
I'm frustrated about being dropped from the starting lineup.

Abbreviation of スターティングメンバー (starting member). Used in sports to refer to the players selected to start a game.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • スタメンに入(はい)る / スタメン入(い)り: to make the starting lineup
  • スタメン落(お)ち: to be dropped from the starting lineup
  • スタメン争(あらそ)い: competition for a starting position
  • スタメン固定(こてい): a fixed/unchanged starting lineup
  • スタメン発表(はっぴょう): starting lineup announcement

OPPOSITE:

  • ベンチ: bench (substitute players)
  • 控(ひか)え選手(せんしゅ): reserve player
  • サブ: substitute

USAGE:
Commonly used in baseball, soccer, and other team sports. In casual conversation, スタメン can also be used figuratively to mean "first choice" or "regular" in non-sports contexts.
