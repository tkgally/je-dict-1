えげつない

えげつない

1. nasty; vulgar; in bad taste
Crude, crass, or lacking refinement.
えげつない冗談(じょうだん)を言(い)うな。
Don't make such vulgar jokes.
えげつない話(はなし)はやめてくれ。
Stop with the nasty talk.
えげつない写真(しゃしん)をネットに上(あ)げるのはよくない。
It's wrong to post vulgar photos online.
2. unscrupulous; ruthless; extreme
Going too far in a negative way; having no moral limits.
えげつない商売(しょうばい)のやり方(かた)だ。
That's an unscrupulous way of doing business.
値段(ねだん)がえげつなく高(たか)い。
The price is outrageously high.
あの会社(かいしゃ)のえげつない値上(ねあ)げに消費者(しょうひしゃ)が怒(おこ)っている。
Consumers are angry about that company's outrageous price hikes.

ORIGIN:
Originally Kansai dialect, now widely used in casual speech throughout Japan.

USAGE:

A strongly negative word used to describe:

  • Vulgar or tasteless behavior or jokes (sense 1)
  • Ruthless or shameless business practices (sense 2)
  • Extreme amounts — price, difficulty, quantity (sense 2)

In sense 2 the word has drifted toward a pure intensifier, close to English "insane" or "brutal", and in young people's speech it can even be mildly admiring:

  • えげつない量(りょう) (an insane amount)
  • えげつなく強(つよ)い (ridiculously strong)

REGISTER:
Casual and colloquial, and blunt enough to sound rude if said about someone present. Avoid it in formal speech and in writing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下品(げひん) - vulgar, coarse (matches sense 1 only)
  • 汚(きたな)い - dirty; also "underhanded" (closer to sense 2)
  • エグい - slang variant covering the same range
