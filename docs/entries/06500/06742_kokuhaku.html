1. confession of love
Telling someone you have romantic feelings for them.
告白(こくはく)するタイミングを待(ま)っている。
I'm waiting for the right time to confess my feelings.
彼女(かのじょ)に告白(こくはく)したが、振(ふ)られた。
I confessed my feelings to her, but was rejected.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)の日(ひ)に勇気(ゆうき)を出(だ)して告白(こくはく)した。
I mustered my courage and confessed my feelings on graduation day.
2. confession; admission
Openly admitting to something, especially a crime or wrongdoing.
犯人(はんにん)は罪(つみ)を告白(こくはく)した。
The criminal confessed to the crime.
長年(ながねん)隠(かく)してきた秘密(ひみつ)を告白(こくはく)した。
I confessed a secret I had been hiding for years.
彼(かれ)は記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で過去(かこ)の不正(ふせい)を告白(こくはく)した。
He confessed to past wrongdoing at the press conference.

VERB FORM:
告白(こくはく)する (to confess)

SENSE 1 — CONFESSING LOVE:

This is the everyday meaning and the more frequent one. It names a specific act: telling someone directly that you like them, usually to ask them out.

  • 告白(こくはく)する (to confess one's feelings)
  • 告白(こくはく)される (to be confessed to)
  • 告白(こくはく)を受(う)ける (to receive a confession)

In Japan a relationship is often understood to start at this moment rather than drifting into being, so the word carries more weight than English "confess."

SENSE 2 — ADMITTING WRONGDOING:
Used of openly admitting a crime, a fault, or a long-kept secret, and belongs to more formal writing and speech: 罪(つみ)の告白(こくはく) (confession of sin).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自白(じはく) - confession to the police; a legal term, never used of love
  • 白状(はくじょう) - owning up, often to something minor and with a lighter tone
  • 懺悔(ざんげ) - confession in the religious sense, of sin before God
