しれっと

しれっと
adverb, onomatopoeia
nonchalantly; coolly; as if nothing happened
1. nonchalantly; coolly; brazenly
Acting calm and unaffected, especially in a situation where one might be expected to show embarrassment, guilt, or concern.
遅刻(ちこく)したのにしれっとした顔(かお)で入(はい)ってきた。
They came in with a nonchalant face despite being late.
嘘(うそ)をついてもしれっとしている。
Even after lying, they remain cool and unbothered.
彼(かれ)はしれっと約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)った。
He brazenly broke the promise.

USAGE:
しれっと + verb / しれっとした + noun

Describes acting unfazed when one should feel embarrassed or guilty:

  • しれっとした顔(かお) (a nonchalant face)
  • しれっと嘘(うそ)をつく (to lie brazenly)
  • しれっと現(あらわ)れる (to appear as if nothing happened)
  • しれっと言(い)う (to say coolly/brazenly)

NUANCE:
Often implies slight criticism or amazement at someone's audacity or lack of shame. It can be used negatively, of shameless behavior, or with grudging admiration for someone's composure.

REGISTER:
A colloquial word, at home in conversation, blogs, and social media. It does not appear in formal writing; 平然(へいぜん)と is the written equivalent.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • けろっと - nonchalantly, casually (describes recovering quickly or being unbothered; carries less criticism)
  • 平然(へいぜん)と - calmly, composedly (more formal, and neutral about whether the composure is deserved)
  • 涼(すず)しい顔(かお)で - with a cool face (a set phrase, close in meaning to しれっとした顔(かお)で)

Related Words
