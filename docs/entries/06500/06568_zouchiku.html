増築(ぞうちく)

ぞうちく
noun
building extension; addition
1. building extension; addition
The act of adding new space or rooms to an existing building, or the added section itself.
家族(かぞく)が増(ふ)えたので部屋(へや)を増築(ぞうちく)した。
We added an extension because our family grew.
二階(にかい)を増築(ぞうちく)する計画(けいかく)がある。
There are plans to add a second floor.
増築(ぞうちく)工事(こうじ)には許可(きょか)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A permit is required for extension construction.

ETYMOLOGY:
増(ぞう) (increase, add) + 築(ちく) (construction, building)

VERB FORM:

  • 増築(ぞうちく)する (to extend, add to a building)

RELATED CONSTRUCTION TERMS:

  • 新築(しんちく) (new construction)
  • 改築(かいちく) (renovation, structural alteration)
  • 修繕(しゅうぜん) (repair)
  • 建(た)て増(ま)し (building addition - more casual)
  • リフォーム (remodeling - loanword)

PERMIT REQUIREMENTS:

In Japan, 増築(ぞうちく) often requires:

  • 建築確認申請(けんちくかくにんしんせい) (building permit application)
  • Verification of 建(けん)ぺい率(りつ) (building coverage ratio)
  • Verification of 容積率(ようせきりつ) (floor area ratio)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 増築(ぞうちく)工事(こうじ) (extension work/construction)
  • 増築(ぞうちく)部分(ぶぶん) (extended portion)
  • 増築(ぞうちく)費用(ひよう) (extension costs)
  • 増築(ぞうちく)の許可(きょか) (building extension permit)
