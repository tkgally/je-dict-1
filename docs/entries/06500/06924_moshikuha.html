若(も)しくは

もしくは
conjunction
or, otherwise (formal)
1. or, otherwise
Formal conjunction presenting alternatives, used primarily in legal, official, and written contexts.
電話(でんわ)若(も)しくはメールでご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Please contact us by phone or email.
本人(ほんにん)若(も)しくは代理人(だいりにん)が申請(しんせい)できます。
The applicant or their representative may apply.
罰金(ばっきん)若(も)しくは禁固刑(きんこけい)に処(しょ)する。
Punishable by fine or imprisonment.
現金(げんきん)若(も)しくはクレジットカードでお支払(しはら)いいただけます。
You may pay by cash or credit card.

REGISTER: Formal/written. Common in:

  • Legal documents
  • Official forms and notices
  • Terms and conditions
  • Formal instructions

FORMALITY SCALE ("or"):

  • か: neutral, everyday
  • または: formal, written
  • 若(も)しくは: very formal, legal
  • あるいは: formal, with nuance of possibility

HIERARCHICAL USAGE: In legal Japanese:

  • 若(も)しくは: connects items within a group (smaller "or")
  • または: connects groups (larger "or")

EXAMPLE:
A若(も)しくはBまたはC若(も)しくはD
= (A or B) OR (C or D)

KANJI NOTE: The kanji 若(わか)い means "young," but in 若(も)しくは it's ateji (phonetic kanji use) and has no semantic connection to youth.

EVERYDAY ALTERNATIVE: In normal speech and writing, use か or または instead.
