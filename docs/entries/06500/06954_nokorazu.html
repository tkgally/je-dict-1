残(のこ)らず

のこらず
adverb
without exception; all; entirely
1. without exception; all; entirely; every single one
Indicates that something applies to every member of a group with no exceptions.
お菓子(かし)を残(のこ)らず食(た)べてしまった。
I ate all the snacks without leaving any.
参加者(さんかしゃ)は残(のこ)らず集(あつ)まった。
All the participants gathered without exception.
書類(しょるい)を残(のこ)らず確認(かくにん)した。
I checked all the documents without missing any.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 残(のこ)る (to remain) + ず (classical negative suffix). Literally "without remaining" — nothing is left behind.

USAGE:

Often used with verbs of collection, consumption, or checking to emphasize completeness. Modifies the verb directly as an adverb.

  • 残(のこ)らず食(た)べる: to eat every last bit
  • 残(のこ)らず集(あつ)める: to collect all of them
  • 残(のこ)らず確認(かくにん)する: to check every single one

COMMON VARIANT:

  • 一人(ひとり)残(のこ)らず: every single person, without exception

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 全部(ぜんぶ): all (most neutral, everyday)
  • 全(すべ)て: all, everything (slightly more formal)
  • ことごとく: each and every one (literary)
  • 余(あま)すところなく: without omission (formal)
