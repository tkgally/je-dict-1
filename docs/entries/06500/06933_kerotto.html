けろっと

けろっと
adverb
nonchalantly, casually, as if nothing happened
1. nonchalantly, casually, as if nothing happened
Mimetic word describing someone who shows no emotional reaction, guilt, or lasting effect from something that should have affected them.
怒(おこ)られたのに、けろっとしている。
Even though they were scolded, they're acting like nothing happened.
失敗(しっぱい)しても、けろっと次(つぎ)に進(すす)む。
Even after failing, they nonchalantly move on.
風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いたけど、翌日(よくじつ)にはけろっと治(なお)っていた。
I had a cold, but the next day I was completely fine.
さっき泣(な)いていたのに、もうけろっとしてる。
They were crying just now, but they're already completely fine.

TYPE: Mimetic word (gitaigo) describing an emotional state, or recovery from one.

NUANCE: Quick emotional recovery, an absence of the remorse or embarrassment the situation calls for, or surprising resilience. Depending on the speaker's sympathies it reads as admirable toughness or as shamelessness.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • けろっとしている - to be visibly unaffected
  • けろっと + verb - to do something without a flicker of concern
  • けろっと治(なお)る - to recover completely (of illness)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • しれっと - coolly, playing innocent (usually critical)
  • 平気(へいき)で - without concern
  • 何食(なにく)わぬ顔(かお)で - with an innocent face
  • あっけらかん - carefree, untroubled

CONTRAST:

  • けろっと: shows no after-effect at all
  • しゅんと: dejected, downcast - the opposite reaction
