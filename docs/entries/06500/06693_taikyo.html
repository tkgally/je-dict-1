1. moving out; vacating (a residence or property)
The act of leaving and giving up occupancy of a rented apartment, house, or other property.
来月(らいげつ)末(まつ)にアパートを退去(たいきょ)する予定(よてい)だ。
I'm planning to move out of the apartment at the end of next month.
退去(たいきょ)時(じ)には原状回復(げんじょうかいふく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Restoring the property to its original condition is required when moving out.
退去(たいきょ)費用(ひよう)がいくらかかるか心配(しんぱい)だ。
I'm worried about how much the move-out costs will be.
2. evacuation; leaving; withdrawal
The act of leaving or being made to leave a place, often due to danger or official orders.
火災(かさい)のため建物(たてもの)から退去(たいきょ)を命(めい)じられた。
We were ordered to evacuate the building due to a fire.
不法(ふほう)滞在者(たいざいしゃ)は強制(きょうせい)退去(たいきょ)の対象(たいしょう)になる。
Illegal residents are subject to forced deportation.
台風(たいふう)が近(ちか)づいているので、沿岸(えんがん)地域(ちいき)の住民(じゅうみん)に退去(たいきょ)勧告(かんこく)が出(だ)された。
With a typhoon approaching, an evacuation advisory was issued for residents in coastal areas.

FORMATION:
退(たい) (withdraw, retreat) + 去(きょ) (leave, depart)

SENSE 1 (RENTAL CONTEXT):

Common in real estate and rental agreements:

  • 退去(たいきょ)届(とどけ) - notice to vacate
  • 退去(たいきょ)日(び) - move-out date
  • 退去(たいきょ)費用(ひよう) - move-out costs
  • 原状回復(げんじょうかいふく) - restoring the original condition (often required by the 大家(おおや) or 管理会社(かんりがいしゃ))

SENSE 2 (EVACUATION, REMOVAL):

  • 強制(きょうせい)退去(たいきょ) - forced eviction; deportation
  • 退去(たいきょ)命令(めいれい) - evacuation order
  • 退去(たいきょ)勧告(かんこく) - evacuation advisory (weaker than an order)

REGISTER: Formal and administrative. Everyday speech about moving house uses 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し instead.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立(た)ち退(の)き - eviction, vacating (usually forced, legal contexts)

ANTONYM:

  • 入居(にゅうきょ) - moving in (to a residence)

Related Words

Antonym: 入居(にゅうきょ) (moving in)
Related: 立(た)ち退(の)き (eviction, vacating)
