寝袋(ねぶくろ)

ねぶくろ
noun
sleeping bag
1. sleeping bag
A padded bag designed for sleeping in, typically used for camping, hiking, or emergency situations. Provides insulation and protection from the elements.
キャンプには寝袋(ねぶくろ)を持(も)っていく。
I bring a sleeping bag when camping.
寒(さむ)い夜(よる)でも寝袋(ねぶくろ)があれば暖(あたた)かい。
Even on cold nights, a sleeping bag keeps you warm.
災害(さいがい)用(よう)に寝袋(ねぶくろ)を準備(じゅんび)しておいた。
I prepared a sleeping bag for disaster preparedness.

ETYMOLOGY:
寝(ね)る (sleep) + 袋(ふくろ) (bag). The second element voices to 袋(ぶくろ) in the compound, a regular sound change.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝袋(ねぶくろ)に入(はい)る: to get into a sleeping bag
  • 寝袋(ねぶくろ)を広(ひろ)げる: to spread out a sleeping bag
  • 寝袋(ねぶくろ)を畳(たた)む: to fold one up
  • 封筒(ふうとう)型(がた)寝袋(ねぶくろ): a rectangular ('envelope-shaped') bag
  • マミー型(がた)寝袋(ねぶくろ): a mummy-shaped bag, tapered at the feet

ALSO CALLED:
シュラフ, from German Schlafsack, is common among experienced campers and in outdoor-shop catalogues. 寝袋(ねぶくろ) is the plain everyday word and is what a non-camper will say.
