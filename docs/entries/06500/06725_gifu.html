義父(ぎふ)

ぎふ
noun
father-in-law; stepfather
1. father-in-law
One's spouse's father.
義父(ぎふ)は会社(かいしゃ)を経営(けいえい)している。
My father-in-law runs a company.
義父(ぎふ)と一緒(いっしょ)に釣(つ)りに行(い)った。
I went fishing together with my father-in-law.
結婚(けっこん)して初(はじ)めて義父(ぎふ)に会(あ)った。
I met my father-in-law for the first time after getting married.
2. stepfather
A man married to one's mother who is not one's biological father.

TERMS OF ADDRESS:

While 義父(ぎふ) is the formal/written term, in conversation people typically address their father-in-law as:

  • お義父(とう)さん - most common
  • 義(ぎ)のお父(とう)さん - explicit "in-law's father"

CONTEXT:

義父(ぎふ) is more commonly used in:

  • Written documents
  • Speaking about (not to) one's father-in-law
  • Formal contexts

FAMILY TERMS WITH 義(ぎ):

  • 義母(ぎぼ) - mother-in-law
  • 義兄(ぎけい) - brother-in-law (older)
  • 義弟(ぎてい) - brother-in-law (younger)
  • 義姉(ぎし) - sister-in-law (older)
  • 義妹(ぎまい) - sister-in-law (younger)

NUANCE:
The 義(ぎ) prefix indicates a family relationship through marriage rather than blood.

Related Words

Related: 義母(ぎぼ) (mother-in-law)
Contrast: 父(ちち) (father (biological))
