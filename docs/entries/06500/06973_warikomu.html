1. to cut in line; to push in
To force one's way into a line or queue, bypassing others who were waiting.
列(れつ)に割(わ)り込(こ)まないでください。
Please don't cut in line.
ずるい人(ひと)が列(れつ)に割(わ)り込(こ)んできた。
A rude person cut into the line.
行列(ぎょうれつ)に割(わ)り込(こ)むのはマナー違反(いはん)だ。
Cutting in line is against etiquette.
割(わ)り込(こ)まれて順番(じゅんばん)を抜(ぬ)かされた。
Someone cut in and I lost my place in line.
子供(こども)が列(れつ)に割(わ)り込(こ)もうとした。
The child tried to cut into the line.
2. to cut in (while driving); to merge aggressively
To suddenly move one's vehicle into traffic or another lane, often in a rude or dangerous manner.
急(きゅう)に車(くるま)が割(わ)り込(こ)んできて危(あぶ)なかった。
A car suddenly cut in and it was dangerous.
トラックが無理(むり)やり割(わ)り込(こ)んできた。
A truck forced its way in.
車線(しゃせん)変更(へんこう)で割(わ)り込(こ)まれた。
Someone cut in while changing lanes.
渋滞(じゅうたい)中(ちゅう)に割(わ)り込(こ)むのは危険(きけん)だ。
Cutting in during traffic is dangerous.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)で急(きゅう)に割(わ)り込(こ)まれると怖(こわ)い。
It's scary when someone suddenly cuts in on the highway.
3. to interrupt; to break into
To interject during a conversation or activity, often in an unwelcome manner.
会話(かいわ)に割(わ)り込(こ)んですみませんが、一(ひと)つ質問(しつもん)があります。
Sorry to interrupt your conversation, but I have a question.
話(はなし)に割(わ)り込(こ)まないで。
Don't interrupt the conversation.
議論(ぎろん)に割(わ)り込(こ)んで意見(いけん)を述(の)べた。
I broke into the discussion and stated my opinion.
電話(でんわ)中(ちゅう)に割(わ)り込(こ)んでくる人(ひと)がいる。
Some people interrupt while I'm on the phone.
大人(おとな)の話(はなし)に割(わ)り込(こ)むのは失礼(しつれい)だ。
It's rude to butt into adult conversations.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). The person or vehicle cutting in is the subject; the line, lane, or conversation being intruded on is marked with に: XがYに割(わ)り込(こ)む.

PASSIVE: Because the action is unwelcome, the passive 割(わ)り込(こ)まれる is very common — it presents the speaker as the injured party (車線(しゃせん)変更(へんこう)で割(わ)り込(こ)まれた, 'someone cut in on me').

ASPECT: 〜てくる is the usual form when the intrusion moves toward the speaker (割(わ)り込(こ)んでくる). ている describes a state of currently being cut in on and is comparatively rare.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 割(わ)る (to divide, split) + 込(こ)む (into). The 〜込(こ)む pattern emphasizes forcing or pushing into something.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 列(れつ)に割(わ)り込(こ)む - to cut in line
  • 車線(しゃせん)に割(わ)り込(こ)む - to cut into a lane
  • 話(はなし)に割(わ)り込(こ)む - to butt into a conversation

REGISTER:
Almost always negative, implying rudeness or lack of consideration for others. There is no neutral way to describe your own 割(わ)り込(こ)む; when you must interrupt politely, soften it with すみませんが as in example 3.
