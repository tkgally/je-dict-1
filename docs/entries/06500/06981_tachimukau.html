1. to confront; to face; to oppose
To bravely face a difficulty, challenge, or opponent rather than avoiding it. Implies courage and determination.
敵(てき)に立(た)ち向(む)かう。
To stand up against the enemy.
問題(もんだい)に立(た)ち向(む)かう勇気(ゆうき)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need the courage to face problems.
一人(ひとり)で大企業(だいきぎょう)に立(た)ち向(む)かうのは大変(たいへん)だ。
It's hard to stand up against a large corporation alone.
不正(ふせい)に立(た)ち向(む)かった記者(きしゃ)が賞(しょう)を受(う)けた。
The journalist who stood up against corruption received an award.
どんなに厳(きび)しい状況(じょうきょう)でも、彼女(かのじょ)は逃(に)げずに立(た)ち向(む)かった。
No matter how tough the situation, she faced it head-on without running away.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). The thing being confronted takes に, never を: XがYに立(た)ち向(む)かう.

ASPECT: The plain past 立(た)ち向(む)かった is the most common form, reporting a completed act of courage. ている is used of a sustained struggle (病気(びょうき)に立(た)ち向(む)かっている, 'is fighting the illness'), not of a momentary stance.

USAGE: The object of 立(た)ち向(む)かう is normally something formidable — an enemy, an injustice, a hardship — and the subject is framed as brave. It is not used for routine tasks; 問題(もんだい)に立(た)ち向(む)かう implies a serious problem, not an exercise question.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 立(た)つ (to stand) + 向(む)かう (to face toward). The compound emphasizes taking a strong stance and actively confronting something.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 困難(こんなん)に立(た)ち向(む)かう - to face difficulties
  • 敵(てき)に立(た)ち向(む)かう - to stand up against an enemy
  • 運命(うんめい)に立(た)ち向(む)かう - to confront fate
  • 逆境(ぎゃっきょう)に立(た)ち向(む)かう - to face adversity

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 挑(いど)む - to challenge (the speaker takes the initiative and attacks)
  • 対峙(たいじ)する - to confront (formal, written; two sides squared off)
  • 向(む)き合(あ)う - to face (neutral; no implication of courage or opposition)
