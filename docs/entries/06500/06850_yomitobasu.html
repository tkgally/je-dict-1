1. to skip reading; to skim over
To intentionally skip parts of a text while reading, either because it seems unimportant or to save time.
難(むずか)しい部分(ぶぶん)は読(よ)み飛(と)ばした。
I skipped over the difficult parts.
説明書(せつめいしょ)の注意事項(ちゅういじこう)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばさないでください。
Please don't skip the precautions in the manual.
時間(じかん)がなくて、途中(とちゅう)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばして最後(さいご)だけ読(よ)んだ。
I didn't have time, so I skipped the middle and just read the ending.

A compound verb: 読(よ)む (to read) + 飛(と)ばす (to skip over). It describes deliberately passing over part of a text — the reader chooses to jump ahead, rather than missing something by accident.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the passage skipped: 広告(こうこく)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばす

ASPECT (ている):
読(よ)み飛(と)ばしている describes a reading session in progress in which the reader keeps skipping ahead. It does not describe a resulting state, so it is not used to mean "has skipped."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 広告(こうこく)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばす (to skip ads)
  • 長文(ちょうぶん)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばす (to skim long texts)
  • 退屈(たいくつ)な章(しょう)を読(よ)み飛(と)ばす (to skip boring chapters)

THE ～飛(と)ばす PATTERN:
As the second half of a compound, 飛(と)ばす adds the sense of jumping over the material in between.
