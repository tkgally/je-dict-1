1. fragrant; aromatic; savory (of roasted/toasted foods)
Having a pleasant aroma from roasting, toasting, or grilling. Describes the appealing smell of browned, toasted, or caramelized foods.
香(こう)ばしい匂(にお)いがキッチンから漂(ただよ)ってきた。
A savory aroma wafted from the kitchen.
胡麻(ごま)を炒(い)ると香(こう)ばしくなる。
Sesame seeds become fragrant when you toast them.
香(こう)ばしく焼(や)いたパンにバターを塗(ぬ)る。
Spread butter on nicely toasted bread.
  • 香(こう)ばしい → 香(こう)ばしくない (negative)
  • 香(こう)ばしい → 香(こう)ばしく (adverbial form)
  • 香(こう)ばしい → 香(こう)ばしかった (past)

USAGE:

Describes the pleasant, appetizing smell of foods that have been toasted, roasted, or grilled. The aroma comes from the Maillard reaction (browning).

  • 香(こう)ばしい匂(にお)い/香(かお)り (fragrant aroma)
  • 香(こう)ばしく焼(や)く (to toast/roast until fragrant)

COMMON FOODS DESCRIBED:

  • 胡麻(ごま) (sesame seeds)
  • 煎餅(せんべい) (rice crackers)
  • 焼(や)き魚(ざかな) (grilled fish)
  • パン/トースト (bread/toast)
  • 焙煎(ばいせん)コーヒー (roasted coffee)

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES:

  • 芳(かんば)しい is literary and covers pleasant scents in general; 香(こう)ばしい is everyday and specific to roasting.

NOTE:
香(こう)ばしい specifically refers to the savory, roasted aroma—not sweet fragrances (use 甘(あま)い香(かお)り) or flowery scents (use いい香(かお)り or 芳(かんば)しい).
