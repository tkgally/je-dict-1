ドヤ顔(がお)

どやがお
noun
smug face, self-satisfied expression
1. smug face, self-satisfied expression
An expression of obvious self-satisfaction or pride, often seen as slightly annoying or amusing by others.
正解(せいかい)して、ドヤ顔(がお)をしている。
They got it right and are making a smug face.
そんなドヤ顔(がお)しなくてもいいよ。
You don't have to look so smug.
勝(か)った後(あと)のドヤ顔(がお)がうざい。
Their smug face after winning is annoying.
猫(ねこ)がドヤ顔(がお)で座(すわ)っている。
The cat is sitting there with a smug look.

ETYMOLOGY: Kansai どや ("How about that?", the regional equivalent of どうだ) + 顔(かお) (face), with the compound reading がお. It names the face someone makes at the moment of showing off. The どや part is written in katakana even though it is not a loanword - the katakana marks it as slangy.

REGISTER: Casual slang, at home online and in youth speech since roughly the late 2000s. Avoid it in writing for work or school.

NUANCE: Usually teasing rather than hostile. Calling someone's expression a ドヤ顔(がお) pokes fun at the self-satisfaction while often conceding they did well. It is also affectionately used of pets - a cat sitting proudly is a stock example - and of oneself, in self-deprecating jokes.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドヤ顔(がお)をする / している - to make (or be wearing) a smug face; the を is often dropped in speech
  • ドヤ顔(がお)で〜する - to do something with a smug expression

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 得意顔(とくいがお) - a proud or triumphant look; the standard, neutral word, usable in writing
  • 自慢(じまん) - boasting; the act of showing off rather than the face
  • 勝(か)ち誇(ほこ)った顔(かお) - a triumphant face, after actually winning something

The slang ドヤ顔(がお) differs from 得意顔(とくいがお) mainly in tone: it always carries a note of "and it's a bit much".
