ドヤ顔(がお)

どやがお
noun
smug face, self-satisfied expression
1. smug face, self-satisfied expression
An expression of obvious self-satisfaction or pride, often seen as slightly annoying or amusing by others.
正解(せいかい)して、ドヤ顔(がお)をしている。
They got it right and are making a smug face.
そんなドヤ顔(がお)しなくてもいいよ。
You don't have to look so smug.
勝(か)った後(あと)のドヤ顔(がお)がうざい。
Their smug face after winning is annoying.
猫(ねこ)がドヤ顔(がお)で座(すわ)っている。
The cat is sitting there with a smug look.

ETYMOLOGY: From Kansai dialect どや ("How about that?") + 顔(かお/がお) (face)

  • どや is the Kansai equivalent of どうだ (How's that?)
  • The expression refers to the face one makes when showing off

REGISTER: Casual/slang. Popular internet and youth vocabulary.

NUANCE: Often used:

  • Playfully or teasingly
  • To describe someone showing off
  • For pets (especially cats) looking proud
  • In memes and social media

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドヤ顔(がお)をする/している: to make/be making a smug face
  • ドヤ顔(がお)で〜する: to do something with a smug expression

INTERNET CULTURE: Very common in:

  • Twitter/social media
  • Memes (especially cat photos)
  • Gaming communities
  • Reaction images

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 得意顔(とくいがお): proud/satisfied face (more neutral)
  • 自慢顔(じまんがお): boastful expression
  • 勝(か)ち誇(ほこ)った顔(かお): triumphant face
  • ふふん顔(がお): "hmph" face (similar nuance)
