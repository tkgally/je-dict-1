添削(てんさく)

てんさく
noun, suru-verb
correction, editing, proofreading

Definitions

1. correction, editing
The act of reviewing and correcting written work, especially compositions, essays, or language exercises; making additions and deletions to improve writing.

Examples

先生(せんせい)に作文(さくぶん)を添削(てんさく)してもらった。
I had my composition corrected by the teacher.
添削(てんさく)サービスを利用(りよう)して日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)している。
I'm studying Japanese using a correction service.
赤(あか)ペンで添削(てんさく)された原稿(げんこう)が戻(もど)ってきた。
The manuscript came back with corrections in red pen.
英文(えいぶん)を添削(てんさく)してくれませんか。
Could you correct my English writing?

Notes

添削(てんさく) refers to correcting and editing written work.

ETYMOLOGY:
添(てん) (add) + 削(さく) (delete) - adding and deleting to improve writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 添削(てんさく)する - to correct, to edit
  • 添削(てんさく)してもらう - to have something corrected
  • 添削(てんさく)サービス - correction service
  • 添削(てんさく)済(ず)み - corrected, proofread

USAGE NOTE:
Commonly used in educational contexts for correcting student writing. Also popular in language learning, where native speakers provide 添削(てんさく) for learners' compositions. Online 添削(てんさく) services have become widespread.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 添削(てんさく) vs. 校正(こうせい): 添削(てんさく) is educational correction; 校正(こうせい) is proofreading for publication
  • 添削(てんさく) vs. 訂正(ていせい): 添削(てんさく) involves comprehensive improvement; 訂正(ていせい) is fixing specific errors
