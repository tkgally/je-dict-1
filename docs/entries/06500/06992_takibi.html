焚(た)き火(び)

たきび
noun
bonfire; campfire

Definitions

1. bonfire; campfire; open fire
A fire made outdoors, typically for warmth, cooking, burning waste, or enjoyment. Associated with camping, autumn activities, and traditional Japanese winter scenes.

Examples

キャンプ場(じょう)で焚(た)き火(び)を囲(かこ)んだ。
We gathered around the campfire at the campsite.
焚(た)き火(び)で焼(や)き芋(いも)を作(つく)った。
We made roasted sweet potatoes over the bonfire.
住宅地(じゅうたくち)では焚(た)き火(び)が禁止(きんし)されている。
Open fires are prohibited in residential areas.

Notes

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Can also be written as 焚火(たきび) or たき火(び).

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焚(た)き火(び)をする - to make a bonfire
  • 焚(た)き火(び)を囲(かこ)む - to gather around a fire
  • 焚(た)き火(び)台(だい) - fire pit/fireplace for camping
  • 焚(た)き火(び)を消(け)す - to put out a fire

CULTURAL NOTES:
焚(た)き火(び) is associated with nostalgic winter scenes in Japan. The famous children's song 「たきび」(1941) describes warming oneself by a fire with roasted sweet potatoes. However, open burning is now restricted in many urban and residential areas due to fire safety and air pollution concerns.
