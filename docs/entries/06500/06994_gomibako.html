ゴミ箱(ばこ)

ごみばこ
noun
trash can; garbage bin
1. trash can; garbage bin; wastebasket
A container for disposing of waste and garbage. Can be found in homes, offices, public spaces, and streets.
ゴミ箱(ばこ)にゴミを捨(す)ててください。
Please throw your trash in the garbage bin.
駅(えき)にゴミ箱(ばこ)が見当(みあ)たらない。
I can't find a trash can at the station.
分別(ぶんべつ)ゴミ箱(ばこ)が設置(せっち)されている。
Sorted waste bins are installed.

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Often written as ゴミ箱(ばこ), 塵箱(ごみばこ), or ごみ箱(ばこ). ゴミ in katakana is very common.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴミ箱(ばこ)に捨(す)てる - to throw away in a trash can
  • 分別(ぶんべつ)ゴミ箱(ばこ) - sorted/recycling bin
  • ゴミ箱(ばこ)を空(から)にする - to empty the trash can

CULTURAL NOTES:
Public trash cans are relatively rare in Japan, especially after the 1995 Tokyo subway sarin attack increased security concerns. Many people carry their trash home or look for convenience store bins.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 屑籠(くずかご) - wastebasket (more formal/traditional)
  • ダストボックス - dustbox (loanword)
  • ゴミ袋(ぶくろ) - garbage bag
