1. careless; hasty; thoughtless; scatterbrained
Tending to act without proper attention or thought, resulting in mistakes. Describes someone who rushes and makes errors due to lack of care.
そそっかしいので、よく忘(わす)れ物(もの)をする。
I'm careless, so I often forget things.
そそっかしい人(ひと)だから、確認(かくにん)を二度(にど)するようにしている。
Since I'm scatterbrained, I make sure to check things twice.
そそっかしく飛(と)び出(だ)して、鍵(かぎ)を閉(し)め忘(わす)れた。
I rushed out carelessly and forgot to lock the door.

FORMS (i-adjective):

  • Negative: そそっかしくない
  • Adverbial: そそっかしく
  • Past: そそっかしかった

USAGE:

Describes a standing personality trait, not a single lapse. The mistakes come from rushing and not looking properly, never from ignorance.

  • そそっかしい人(ひと) / そそっかしい性格(せいかく) (a careless person / personality)
  • そそっかしくて... (being careless, I...)

NUANCE:
Most often said of oneself, self-deprecatingly. Said of someone else it is a mild criticism, so it is used about family or close friends rather than about a superior.

CONTRAST:

  • そそっかしい ↔ 慎重(しんちょう) (careless ↔ careful, cautious)
  • そそっかしい ↔ 落(お)ち着(つ)いている (hasty ↔ calm, composed)

RELATED WORDS:

  • うっかり (carelessly, inadvertently): an adverb for a single slip
  • 軽率(けいそつ) (rash, thoughtless): more formal, and about poor judgment rather than haste
  • せっかち (impatient): rushing itself, without the resulting mistakes

Related Words

Antonym: 慎重(しんちょう) (careful, cautious)
Related: 軽率(けいそつ) (rash (more formal))
