眉(まゆ)をひそめる

まゆをひそめる
expression
to frown, to knit one's brows
1. to frown, to knit one's brows
To draw the eyebrows together in an expression of displeasure, concern, or disapproval.
彼(かれ)の発言(はつげん)に眉(まゆ)をひそめた。
I frowned at his remark.
眉(まゆ)をひそめるような行為(こうい)は控(ひか)えてください。
Please refrain from behavior that would make people frown.
報告(ほうこく)を聞(き)いて課長(かちょう)は眉(まゆ)をひそめた。
The section chief frowned upon hearing the report.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で大声(おおごえ)で話(はな)す人(ひと)に、周(まわ)りの乗客(じょうきゃく)は眉(まゆ)をひそめた。
The other passengers frowned at the person talking loudly on the train.
彼(かれ)の無礼(ぶれい)な態度(たいど)に、先輩(せんぱい)たちはみな眉(まゆ)をひそめた。本人(ほんにん)だけが気(き)づいていなかった。
The senior colleagues all frowned at his rude behavior. He was the only one who didn't notice.

STRUCTURE: 眉(まゆ) (eyebrow) + を + ひそめる (to draw together, to contract). ひそめる occurs almost only in this phrase.

NUANCE: Silent disapproval or concern — the reaction to something improper, worrying, or distasteful. It is a description of how others react, not an accusation, so it is a common way to report disapproval without criticizing directly. The person frowning is usually a bystander.

REGISTER: Slightly literary. It appears far more often in writing and in narration than in everyday speech, where 顔(かお)をしかめる is the ordinary way to say the same thing.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 顔(かお)をしかめる: to grimace — usually from pain or distaste
  • 眉(まゆ)を上(あ)げる: to raise one's eyebrows — surprise
  • 眉(まゆ)を寄(よ)せる: to furrow the brows — close in meaning to ひそめる
