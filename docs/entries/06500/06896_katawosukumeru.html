肩(かた)をすくめる

かたをすくめる
expression
to shrug one's shoulders
1. to shrug one's shoulders
A gesture indicating uncertainty, resignation, or indifference by raising both shoulders.
彼(かれ)は肩(かた)をすくめた。
He shrugged.
仕方(しかた)がないと肩(かた)をすくめるしかなかった。
I could only shrug, thinking there was nothing to be done.
質問(しつもん)に答(こた)えず、肩(かた)をすくめた。
Instead of answering the question, she just shrugged.
「知(し)らない」と言(い)いながら肩(かた)をすくめる彼(かれ)を見(み)て、本当(ほんとう)に知(し)らないのだと思(おも)った。
Watching him shrug while saying "I don't know," I believed he really didn't know.
遅刻(ちこく)の理由(りゆう)を聞(き)かれて、彼女(かのじょ)は困(こま)ったように肩(かた)をすくめるだけだった。
When asked why she was late, she could only shrug awkwardly.

STRUCTURE: 肩(かた) (shoulder) + を + すくめる (to draw in, to hunch). The verb すくめる is used almost only in this phrase and in 首(くび)をすくめる.

GESTURE MEANING: A shrug conveys one of:

  • "I don't know"
  • "It can't be helped" (仕方(しかた)がない)
  • resignation or indifference
  • deliberate non-commitment

CULTURAL NOTE: Shrugging is a Western gesture more than a traditional Japanese one, but it is widely understood in Japan, especially among younger speakers and in international settings. A speaker who would shrug in English often says 「仕方(しかた)がない」 instead, or tilts the head.

RELATED GESTURE EXPRESSIONS:

  • 首(くび)をかしげる: to tilt one's head — puzzlement
  • 手(て)を挙(あ)げる: to raise one's hand — volunteering, or surrender

OTHER 肩(かた) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 肩(かた)を落(お)とす: to let one's shoulders drop — dejection
  • 肩(かた)の力(ちから)を抜(ぬ)く: to relax, to stop straining

Related Words

Related: 肩(かた)を落(お)とす (to be dejected (another 肩 gesture))
Related: 肩(かた) (shoulder)
Related: 仕方(しかた)がない (it can't be helped (the sentiment a shrug conveys))
