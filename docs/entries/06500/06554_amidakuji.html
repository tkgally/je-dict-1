あみだくじ

あみだくじ
noun
ladder lottery, ghost leg
1. ladder lottery, ghost leg
A lottery method using vertical parallel lines connected by horizontal rungs. Participants follow paths from top to bottom, crossing horizontal lines when encountered, to reach randomly assigned outcomes at the bottom.
あみだくじで掃除(そうじ)当番(とうばん)を決(き)めた。
We decided the cleaning duty by ladder lottery.
あみだくじを引(ひ)いたら当(あ)たりが出(で)た。
I drew the lottery and got the winning outcome.
席替(せきが)えはあみだくじでやろう。
Let's decide the seat changes with a ladder lottery.

NAME ORIGIN:

  • Named after 阿弥陀仏(あみだぶつ) (Amitabha Buddha)
  • The radiating lines behind Buddha images resemble the lottery's vertical lines
  • Also called 阿弥陀籤(あみだくじ)

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Draw vertical parallel lines
2. Add random horizontal lines (rungs) connecting adjacent verticals
3. Assign items/prizes at the bottom
4. Participants choose a starting line at the top
5. Trace downward, turning at every horizontal line crossed
6. The endpoint determines the result

MATHEMATICAL PROPERTY:

  • Each starting point leads to exactly one unique endpoint
  • The process creates a random permutation

COMMON USES:

  • 掃除(そうじ)当番(とうばん) (cleaning duties)
  • 席替(せきが)え (seat assignments)
  • 罰(ばつ)ゲーム決(き)め (deciding punishment games)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)決(き)め (deciding order)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • あみだくじを引(ひ)く (to draw a ladder lottery)
  • あみだくじで決(き)める (to decide by ladder lottery)
  • あみだくじを作(つく)る (to make a ladder lottery)
