情(なさ)け

なさけ
noun
compassion; pity; sympathy; love
1. compassion; pity; sympathy; kindness
A feeling of care and concern for someone who is suffering or in difficulty. The warmth of human feeling that leads one to help others.
情(なさ)けをかけてくれた人(ひと)を忘(わす)れない。
I won't forget the person who showed me kindness.
情(なさ)けは人(ひと)の為(ため)ならず。
Kindness is not (just) for others' sake. (proverb)
This proverb means that kindness eventually comes back to benefit you.
情(なさ)け深(ぶか)い人(ひと)だ。
He is a compassionate person.
2. love; affection (literary/classical)
Romantic love or deep affection, especially in classical or literary contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 情(なさ)けをかける (to show compassion/pity)
  • 情(なさ)けない (pathetic, shameful - note different meaning!)
  • 情(なさ)け深(ぶか)い (compassionate, tender-hearted)
  • 情(なさ)けは人(ひと)の為(ため)ならず (proverb: kindness benefits oneself too)

NOTE ON 情(なさ)けない:

情(なさ)けない looks like the negative of 情(なさ)け but means "pathetic" or "shameful"—something that makes you feel ashamed of yourself or that situation.

  • 自分(じぶん)が情(なさ)けない (I'm pathetic/ashamed of myself)

NUANCE:
情(なさ)け has a warm, human quality suggesting genuine concern. It's often used in the context of helping those in difficult situations.

RELATED KANJI COMPOUND:

  • 人情(にんじょう) (human kindness, sympathy)
  • 愛情(あいじょう) (love, affection)
