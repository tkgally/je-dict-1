1. to deduct; to subtract
To remove an amount from a total, typically in financial or mathematical contexts.
税金(ぜいきん)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く。
To deduct taxes.
経費(けいひ)を差(さ)し引(ひ)くと利益(りえき)はほとんどない。
After deducting expenses, there's almost no profit.
税金(ぜいきん)を差(さ)し引(ひ)いた手取(てど)りは20万円(まんえん)です。
The take-home pay after deducting taxes is 200,000 yen.
2. to balance; to offset
To consider both positive and negative factors and arrive at a net result.
差(さ)し引(ひ)きゼロだ。
It balances out to zero.
良(よ)いところも悪(わる)いところも差(さ)し引(ひ)いて考(かんが)えると、悪(わる)くない条件(じょうけん)だ。
Balancing the good and the bad, it's not a bad deal.
苦労(くろう)も多(おお)かったが、差(さ)し引(ひ)きすれば楽(たの)しい経験(けいけん)だった。
There were many hardships, but on balance it was an enjoyable experience.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 差(さ)す (to insert) + 引(ひ)く (to pull, to subtract)

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 is arithmetic on a stated total: the amount removed takes を, and what remains is usually named with 差(さ)し引(ひ)いた + noun (example 3).
  • Sense 2 weighs pluses against minuses rather than money, and most often appears as the noun 差(さ)し引(ひ)き or as 差(さ)し引(ひ)いて考(かんが)える (to take everything into account).
  • Everyday subtraction is plain 引(ひ)く; 差(さ)し引(ひ)く implies deducting from a sum owed or earned, so it belongs to payroll, invoicing, and accounting language.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)き - noun form meaning "deduction" or "balance"
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)き勘定(かんじょう) - net calculation/balance
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)きゼロ - balances out to zero
  • 源泉徴収(げんせんちょうしゅう)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く (to deduct withholding tax)
  • 割引(わりびき)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く (to deduct a discount)
