差(さ)し引(ひ)く

さしひく
verb (godan, transitive)
to deduct; to subtract; to balance
1. to deduct; to subtract
To remove an amount from a total, typically in financial or mathematical contexts.
2. to balance; to offset
To consider both positive and negative factors and arrive at a net result.
税金(ぜいきん)を差(さ)し引(ひ)いた手取(てど)りは20万円(にじゅうまんえん)です。
The take-home pay after deducting taxes is 200,000 yen.
経費(けいひ)を差(さ)し引(ひ)くと利益(りえき)はほとんどない。
After deducting expenses, there's almost no profit.
差(さ)し引(ひ)きゼロだ。
It balances out to zero.

CONJUGATION:

  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)く → 差(さ)し引(ひ)かない (negative)
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)く → 差(さ)し引(ひ)いて (te-form)
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)く → 差(さ)し引(ひ)いた (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 差(さ)す (to insert) + 引(ひ)く (to pull/subtract)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)き - noun form meaning "deduction" or "balance"
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)き勘定(かんじょう) - net calculation/balance
  • 差(さ)し引(ひ)きゼロ - balances out to zero

USAGE:

Commonly used in financial and business contexts:

  • 源泉徴収(げんせんちょうしゅう)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く (to deduct withholding tax)
  • 割引(わりびき)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く (to deduct a discount)
