義母(ぎぼ)

ぎぼ
noun
mother-in-law; stepmother
1. mother-in-law
One's spouse's mother.
2. stepmother
A woman married to one's father who is not one's biological mother.
義母(ぎぼ)と同居(どうきょ)している。
I live with my mother-in-law.
義母(ぎぼ)は料理(りょうり)が上手(じょうず)だ。
My mother-in-law is good at cooking.
義母(ぎぼ)との関係(かんけい)は良好(りょうこう)です。
My relationship with my mother-in-law is good.

TERMS OF ADDRESS:

While 義母(ぎぼ) is the formal/written term, in conversation people typically address their mother-in-law as:

  • お義母(かあ)さん - most common
  • 義(ぎ)のお母(かあ)さん - explicit "in-law's mother"

CONTEXT:

義母(ぎぼ) is more commonly used in:

  • Written documents
  • Speaking about (not to) one's mother-in-law
  • Formal contexts

FAMILY TERMS WITH 義(ぎ):

  • 義父(ぎふ) - father-in-law
  • 義兄(ぎけい) - brother-in-law (older)
  • 義弟(ぎてい) - brother-in-law (younger)
  • 義姉(ぎし) - sister-in-law (older)
  • 義妹(ぎまい) - sister-in-law (younger)
  • 義理(ぎり)の息子(むすこ) - son-in-law
  • 義理(ぎり)の娘(むすめ) - daughter-in-law

NUANCE:
The 義(ぎ) prefix indicates a family relationship through marriage rather than blood.

Related Words

Related: 義父(ぎふ) (father-in-law)
Contrast: 母(はは) (mother (biological))
