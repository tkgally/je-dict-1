1. frustrating, vexing, irritating
Describes the frustration felt when something doesn't go as expected or desired, or when one cannot act as freely as they would like. Often involves impatience or the feeling that things are progressing too slowly.
言(い)いたいことが上手(うま)く伝(つた)わらなくてもどかしい。
It's frustrating when I can't convey what I want to say properly.
回復(かいふく)が遅(おそ)くてもどかしい思(おも)いをしている。
I'm feeling frustrated because my recovery is slow.
結果(けっか)を待(ま)つのがもどかしい。
It's frustrating to wait for the results.

FORMS:

  • もどかしい (plain form)
  • もどかしく (adverbial form)
  • もどかしさ (noun form - frustration)

NUANCE:

  • Unlike 苛々(いらいら)する (irritated), もどかしい often implies being unable to do anything about the situation
  • Contains a sense of helplessness or powerlessness
  • Can describe both self-directed frustration and frustration watching others

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Communication difficulties (language barriers, etc.)
  • Watching someone struggle when you can't help
  • Slow progress in recovery, learning, etc.
  • Waiting for results or outcomes
  • Romantic situations where feelings can't be expressed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 歯(は)がゆい (similar meaning - frustrating)
  • じれったい (similar - frustrating/impatient)
  • やきもきする (anxious/restless)
  • 苛立(いらだ)つ (to be irritated)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • もどかしい思(おも)いをする (to feel frustrated)
  • もどかしい気持(きも)ち (frustrated feeling)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • もどかしい思(おも)い (frustrated feeling)
  • もどかしい状況(じょうきょう) (frustrating situation)
  • もどかしいほど遅(おそ)い (frustratingly slow)
