頑固(がんこ)

がんこ
na-adjective, noun
stubborn; obstinate; headstrong

Definitions

1. stubborn; obstinate; pigheaded
Unwilling to change one's opinions, attitudes, or actions despite good reasons to do so. Can describe a person's character or specific behavior.
2. stubborn; persistent (of stains, symptoms, etc.)
Difficult to remove or get rid of. Used for things that won't go away easily.

Examples

うちの父(ちち)は頑固(がんこ)で、一度(いちど)決(き)めたら絶対(ぜったい)に変(か)えない。
My father is stubborn—once he's decided something, he absolutely won't change his mind.
頑固(がんこ)な汚(よご)れがなかなか落(お)ちない。
The stubborn stain won't come off easily.
頑固親父(がんこおやじ)だけど、根(ね)は優(やさ)しい。
He's a stubborn old man, but he's kind at heart.

Notes

USAGE:

  • 頑固(がんこ)な人(ひと) (a stubborn person)
  • 頑固(がんこ)に反対(はんたい)する (to stubbornly oppose)
  • 頑固(がんこ)を通(とお)す (to stick stubbornly to one's position)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頑固親父(がんこおやじ) (stubborn old man - often used affectionately)
  • 頑固(がんこ)な職人(しょくにん) (stubborn craftsman - can be positive, implying commitment to quality)
  • 頑固(がんこ)な風邪(かぜ) (stubborn cold that won't go away)
  • 頑固(がんこ)な汚(よご)れ} (stubborn stain)

NUANCE:
For people, usually negative but can be used with grudging respect for someone who sticks to their principles. 頑固親父(がんこおやじ) often carries affection despite the complaint.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 強情(ごうじょう) (obstinate - more negative)
  • 意地(いじ)っ張(ぱ)り (obstinate, contrary)
