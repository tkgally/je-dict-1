1. to see through; to perceive the truth
To recognize or understand someone's true nature, intentions, or a hidden truth that others might miss.
嘘(うそ)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
I saw through the lie.
彼女(かのじょ)は嘘(うそ)を見抜(みぬ)くのが得意(とくい)だ。
She's good at seeing through lies.
先生(せんせい)は生徒(せいと)の才能(さいのう)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
The teacher recognized the student's talent.
問題(もんだい)の本質(ほんしつ)を見抜(みぬ)くことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to see through to the essence of the problem.
彼(かれ)の本心(ほんしん)を見抜(みぬ)くのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to perceive his true intentions.
2. to detect; to discern
To identify something that is concealed or not immediately apparent, such as a lie, disguise, or underlying problem.
偽物(にせもの)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
I detected that it was a fake.
彼(かれ)の演技(えんぎ)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
I saw through his act.
詐欺(さぎ)の手口(てぐち)を見抜(みぬ)くのは簡単(かんたん)ではない。
It's not easy to detect the methods of a scam.
正体(しょうたい)を見抜(みぬ)かれないように気(き)をつけた。
I was careful not to have my true identity discovered.
経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ)な刑事(けいじ)は犯人(はんにん)の嘘(うそ)をすぐに見抜(みぬ)いた。
The experienced detective immediately detected the criminal's lies.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 見(み)る (to see) + 抜(ぬ)く (through, completely).
The 〜抜(ぬ)く pattern indicates doing something completely or all the way through.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for what is seen through.

ASPECT (ている):
見抜(みぬ)く is punctual — it names the moment of realization, not a process. 見抜(みぬ)いている therefore means 'has already seen through (and knows)', a resulting state, rather than an action in progress.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嘘(うそ)を見抜(みぬ)く - to see through a lie
  • 本心(ほんしん)を見抜(みぬ)く - to see someone's true feelings
  • 正体(しょうたい)を見抜(みぬ)く - to see through someone's disguise
  • 策略(さくりゃく)を見抜(みぬ)く - to see through a scheme

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見破(みやぶ)る: to see through and expose (emphasizes catching someone out)
  • 見抜(みぬ)く: to perceive what is hidden (emphasizes insight, not exposure)

Related Words

Synonym: 見破(みやぶ)る (see through and expose)
Related: 見(み)る (to see)
Related: 抜(ぬ)く (second element of the compound)
