管理費(かんりひ)

かんりひ
noun
management fee
1. management fee; maintenance fee
A regular fee paid for the management and maintenance of shared facilities, especially in condominiums and rental properties.
管理費(かんりひ)は毎月(まいつき)1万円(いちまんえん)です。
The management fee is 10,000 yen per month.
家賃(やちん)とは別(べつ)に管理費(かんりひ)がかかる。
There's a management fee on top of the rent.
管理費(かんりひ)で共用(きょうよう)部分(ぶぶん)の清掃(せいそう)が行(おこな)われる。
Common areas are cleaned using the management fee.

ETYMOLOGY:
管理(かんり) (management) + 費(ひ) (expense, fee)

WHAT IT COVERS:

  • 共用(きょうよう)部分(ぶぶん)の清掃(せいそう) (cleaning of common areas)
  • エレベーター維持(いじ) (elevator maintenance)
  • 防犯(ぼうはん)カメラ (security cameras)
  • 管理人(かんりにん)費用(ひよう) (building manager costs)
  • 共用(きょうよう)電気(でんき)代(だい) (common area electricity)

RELATED FEES:

  • 共益費(きょうえきひ) (common expense fee - similar meaning)
  • 修繕(しゅうぜん)積立金(つみたてきん) (repair reserve fund - for condos)
  • 駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)代(だい) (parking fee)

RENTAL LISTINGS:

In Japanese property listings, you'll often see:

  • 家賃(やちん) ¥80,000 + 管理費(かんりひ) ¥5,000
  • Or written as: 管理費(かんりひ)込(こ)み (management fee included)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 管理費(かんりひ)を払(はら)う (to pay the management fee)
  • 管理費(かんりひ)が高(たか)い/安(やす)い (management fee is high/low)
  • 管理費(かんりひ)込(こ)み (including management fee)
  • 月額(げつがく)管理費(かんりひ) (monthly management fee)
