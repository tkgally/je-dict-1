発着(はっちゃく)

はっちゃく
noun / suru-verb
arrival and departure
1. arrival and departure, coming and going
The combined concept of departing from and arriving at a location, typically used for transportation schedules and facilities.
電車(でんしゃ)の発着(はっちゃく)時刻(じこく)を確認(かくにん)する。
Check the train arrival and departure times.
空港(くうこう)の発着(はっちゃく)ロビーで待(ま)ち合(あ)わせた。
We met at the airport's arrivals and departures lobby.
この駅(えき)は1日(にち)500本(ほん)の列車(れっしゃ)が発着(はっちゃく)する。
500 trains arrive and depart from this station daily.

FORMATION: 発(はつ) (departure) + 着(ちゃく) (arrival). A compound of opposites that together mean 'comings and goings.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発着(はっちゃく)時刻(じこく) (arrival/departure times)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)案内(あんない) (arrival/departure guide)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)ロビー (arrivals/departures lobby)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)便(びん) (arriving/departing flights)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 出発(しゅっぱつ) (departure)
  • 到着(とうちゃく) (arrival)
  • 発車(はっしゃ) (train departure)
