1. arrival and departure, coming and going
The combined concept of departing from and arriving at a location, typically used for transportation schedules and facilities.
発着(はっちゃく)時刻(じこく)を確認(かくにん)する。
Check the arrival and departure times.
空港(くうこう)の発着(はっちゃく)ロビーで待(ま)ち合(あ)わせた。
We met at the airport's arrivals and departures lobby.
この駅(えき)は1日(にち)500本(ほん)の列車(れっしゃ)が発着(はっちゃく)する。
500 trains arrive and depart from this station daily.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で飛行機(ひこうき)の発着(はっちゃく)に遅(おく)れが出(で)ている。
Flights are experiencing delays in arrivals and departures due to the typhoon.
成田(なりた)空港(くうこう)は国際線(こくさいせん)の発着(はっちゃく)便数(びんすう)が日本(にほん)で最(もっと)も多(おお)い。
Narita Airport has the most international flight arrivals and departures in Japan.

FORMATION: 発(はつ) (departure) + 着(ちゃく) (arrival). A compound of opposites that together mean "comings and goings".

USAGE:

  • 発着(はっちゃく)する is intransitive. The vehicle is the subject and takes が; the place takes に or is left to the topic, as in example 3.
  • Most often the word is a noun modifier attached directly to another noun — 発着(はっちゃく)時刻(じこく), 発着(はっちゃく)ロビー — with no の in between.
  • Because it covers both directions at once, it belongs to schedules, signboards, and announcements rather than to talk about a single trip. To say that one train left or one plane landed, use 出発(しゅっぱつ) or 到着(とうちゃく).
  • Applies to 飛行機(ひこうき), 電車(でんしゃ), 列車(れっしゃ), バス and ships — anything that runs to a timetable.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発着(はっちゃく)時刻(じこく) (arrival/departure times)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)案内(あんない) (arrival/departure guide)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)ロビー (arrivals/departures lobby)
  • 発着(はっちゃく)便(びん) (arriving/departing flights)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 出発(しゅっぱつ) (departure only)
  • 到着(とうちゃく) (arrival only)
  • 発車(はっしゃ) (a vehicle pulling out, especially a train)
