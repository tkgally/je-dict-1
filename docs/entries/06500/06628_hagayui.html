歯(は)がゆい

はがゆい
i-adjective
frustrating; vexing; tantalizing
1. frustrating; vexing; irritating (because things aren't going as desired)
The feeling of frustration when something is close to what you want but not quite satisfactory, or when you cannot do anything about an unsatisfying situation.
彼(かれ)の優柔不断(ゆうじゅうふだん)な態度(たいど)が歯(は)がゆい。
His indecisive attitude is frustrating.
自分(じぶん)では何(なに)もできないのが歯(は)がゆい。
It's frustrating that I can't do anything myself.
歯(は)がゆい思(おも)いをした。
I felt frustrated (had a vexing experience).

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally "itchy teeth" - the sensation of wanting to bite down but being unable to, expressing impotent frustration.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 歯(は)がゆく
  • Noun form: 歯(は)がゆさ (frustration, vexation)

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 歯(は)がゆくない
  • Te-form: 歯(は)がゆくて
  • Past: 歯(は)がゆかった

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • Xが歯(は)がゆい (X is frustrating)
  • 歯(は)がゆい思(おも)いをする (to have a frustrating experience)
  • 歯(は)がゆく感(かん)じる (to feel frustrated)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • もどかしい (frustrating) - similar meaning, focused on impatience
  • いらいらする (to be irritated) - more about annoyance
  • じれったい (impatient, irritating) - similar, more casual

NUANCE:
歯(は)がゆい specifically expresses frustration at not being able to intervene or change a situation, often watching someone else fail to meet expectations.
