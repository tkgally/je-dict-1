骨董品(こっとうひん)

こっとうひん
noun
antique; curio
1. antique; antique item
An old object valued for its age, rarity, craftsmanship, or historical significance. Includes furniture, ceramics, art, and other collectibles.
祖母(そぼ)の家(いえ)には骨董品(こっとうひん)がたくさんある。
There are many antiques at my grandmother's house.
この骨董品(こっとうひん)は江戸時代(えどじだい)のものだ。
This antique is from the Edo period.
骨董品(こっとうひん)店(てん)で掛(か)け軸(じく)を買(か)った。
I bought a hanging scroll at an antique shop.

ETYMOLOGY:
骨董(こっとう) originally meant an assortment of miscellaneous old things, and 品(ひん) means goods or articles. The full form 骨董品(こっとうひん) is the ordinary word; bare 骨董(こっとう) appears mainly in compounds and shop names.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 骨董品(こっとうひん)店(てん) / 骨董品(こっとうひん)屋(や): an antique shop
  • 骨董品(こっとうひん)を集(あつ)める: to collect antiques
  • 骨董品(こっとうひん)市(いち): an antique market or fair
  • 骨董品(こっとうひん)の鑑定(かんてい): appraisal of an antique

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • アンティーク: the loanword, used mostly of Western pieces and of shop styling; 骨董品(こっとうひん) carries a more Japanese and more traditional flavour
  • 古美術(こびじゅつ): old art objects specifically, a narrower and more scholarly term
  • 古物(こぶつ): secondhand goods in the legal and trade sense, with no implication of value
  • 年代物(ねんだいもの): something old enough to be worth remarking on — used lightly, and of things like wine or a well-worn jacket as well as collectibles
