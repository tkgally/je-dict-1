志(こころざし)

こころざし
noun
aspiration; ambition; will; intention
1. aspiration; ambition; goal; will
A strong desire to achieve something significant. One's purpose or aim in life, especially regarding career, contribution to society, or personal growth.
高(たか)い志(こころざし)を持(も)って働(はたら)いている。
I work with high aspirations.
志(こころざし)を同(おな)じくする仲間(なかま)と出会(であ)えた。
I met comrades who share the same aspirations.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)の志(こころざし)を忘(わす)れないようにしている。
I try not to forget the aspirations I had when I was young.
医者(いしゃ)になるという志(こころざし)を立(た)てたのは中学生(ちゅうがくせい)の時(とき)だった。
It was in junior high that I resolved to become a doctor.
2. goodwill; kind intention; gift (as token of appreciation)
A gesture of appreciation or goodwill. Also refers to a small gift or monetary offering as an expression of gratitude or respect.
お志(こころざし)だけいただきます。
I gratefully accept your kind intention (but decline the gift itself).
頂(いただ)いたお志(こころざし)に心(こころ)から感謝(かんしゃ)しております。
We are deeply grateful for the kindness you have shown us.
Set phrase at funerals and formal occasions

志(こころざし) is a settled aspiration one holds and directs one's life by — closer to 'resolve' or 'calling' than to a wish. It is a dignified, somewhat formal word: an everyday goal is a 目標(もくひょう), while a 志(こころざし) implies something one commits to over years. It is the noun of 志(こころざ)す.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 志(こころざし)を持(も)つ (to have aspirations)
  • 高(たか)い志(こころざし) (high aspirations/ambitions)
  • 志(こころざし)を立(た)てる (to set one's resolve)
  • 志(こころざし)を同(おな)じくする (to share the same aspirations)
  • 志(こころざし)半(なか)ばで (with one's goals unfulfilled — typically of someone who died before achieving them)

SENSE 2 USAGE:

お志(こころざし) refers politely to the goodwill behind someone's gift or offering, and lets the speaker acknowledge the feeling while saying nothing about the money:

  • お志(こころざし)、ありがとうございます (Thank you for your kind gesture)
  • It belongs to funerals and other formal occasions, where it is also written on the envelope returned to mourners who have given 香典(こうでん).

RELATED WORDS:

  • 志望(しぼう) (what one is applying for — a school, a job — rather than a life aim)
  • 野心(やしん) (ambition, often self-serving and faintly disapproved of)
  • 抱負(ほうふ) (a resolution one states aloud, typically at the new year)

Related Words

Synonym: 野望(やぼう) (wild/grand ambition)
Contrast: 野心(やしん) (ambition; often negative)
Related: 志望(しぼう) (aspiration (school, career))
Related: 抱負(ほうふ) (stated resolution)
