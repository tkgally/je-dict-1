志(こころざし)

こころざし
noun
aspiration; ambition; will; intention

Definitions

1. aspiration; ambition; goal; will
A strong desire to achieve something significant. One's purpose or aim in life, especially regarding career, contribution to society, or personal growth.
2. goodwill; kind intention; gift (as token of appreciation)
A gesture of appreciation or goodwill. Also refers to a small gift or monetary offering as an expression of gratitude or respect.

Examples

高(たか)い志(こころざし)を持(も)って働(はたら)いている。
I work with high aspirations.
志(こころざし)を同(おな)じくする仲間(なかま)と出会(であ)えた。
I met comrades who share the same aspirations.
お志(こころざし)だけいただきます。
I gratefully accept your kind intention (but decline the gift itself).

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 志(こころざし)を持(も)つ (to have aspirations)
  • 高(たか)い志(こころざし) (high aspirations/ambitions)
  • 志(こころざし)を立(た)てる (to set one's goals/resolve)
  • 志(こころざし)を同(おな)じくする (to share the same aspirations)
  • 志(こころざし)半(なか)ばで (with one's goals unfulfilled)

SENSE 2 USAGE:

お志(こころざし) is used politely when referring to someone's kind gesture or offering:

  • お志(こころざし)、ありがとうございます (Thank you for your kind gesture)
  • This usage often appears in formal contexts like funerals or ceremonies

RELATED WORDS:

  • 志望(しぼう) (aspiration, desire - often for career goals)
  • 野心(やしん) (ambition - can have negative connotation)
  • 抱負(ほうふ) (aspiration, resolution - often for new year)
