にも関(かか)わらず

にもかかわらず
conjunction
despite; in spite of; although
1. despite; in spite of; although; even though
Formal expression for contrast between expectation and reality.
雨(あめ)にも関(かか)わらず、試合(しあい)は行(おこな)われた。
Despite the rain, the match was held.
忙(いそが)しいにも関(かか)わらず、手伝(てつだ)ってくれた。
Although she was busy, she helped me.
注意(ちゅうい)したにも関(かか)わらず、また同(おな)じ失敗(しっぱい)をした。
Despite being warned, he made the same mistake again.

にも関(かか)わらず is a formal conjunction meaning "despite."

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • [noun] + にも関(かか)わらず
  • [verb plain form] + にも関(かか)わらず
  • [i-adjective] + にも関(かか)わらず
  • [na-adjective + な/である] + にも関(かか)わらず

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal, often in written Japanese. In casual speech, のに is more common.

SIMILAR:

  • のに - although (casual)
  • けれども / が - but (neutral)
