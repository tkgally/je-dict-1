にも関(かか)わらず

にもかかわらず
conjunction
despite; in spite of; although
1. despite; in spite of; although; even though
Formal expression for contrast between expectation and reality.
雨(あめ)にも関(かか)わらず、試合(しあい)は行(おこな)われた。
Despite the rain, the match was held.
忙(いそが)しいにも関(かか)わらず、手伝(てつだ)ってくれた。
Although she was busy, she helped me.
注意(ちゅうい)したにも関(かか)わらず、また同(おな)じ失敗(しっぱい)をした。
Despite being warned, he made the same mistake again.
何度(なんど)も練習(れんしゅう)したにも関(かか)わらず、本番(ほんばん)では失敗(しっぱい)してしまった。
Despite practicing many times, I failed during the actual performance.
彼(かれ)は若(わか)いにも関(かか)わらず、会社(かいしゃ)で大(おお)きな仕事(しごと)を任(まか)されている。
Despite being young, he has been entrusted with major work at the company.

にも関(かか)わらず expresses that the result is contrary to what would be expected given the circumstances. It is more formal than のに and emphasizes the unexpected contrast.

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • [noun] + にも関(かか)わらず: 雨(あめ)にも関(かか)わらず (despite the rain)
  • [verb plain form] + にも関(かか)わらず: 注意(ちゅうい)したにも関(かか)わらず (despite warning)
  • [i-adjective] + にも関(かか)わらず: 若(わか)いにも関(かか)わらず (despite being young)
  • [na-adj + な/である] + にも関(かか)わらず: 元気(げんき)であるにも関(かか)わらず (despite being healthy)

REGISTER:
Formal/written. Common in news, essays, and business. In casual speech, のに is preferred.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • のに: although (casual, spoken — often implies frustration or regret, which にも関(かか)わらず does not: it simply records the contrast)
  • けれども / が: but (neutral connectors)
  • それにも関(かか)わらず: nevertheless (sentence-initial form, linking back to the previous sentence)

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Frequently written all in kana as にもかかわらず; official style guides in fact prefer the kana form, since 関(かん) is not the standard kanji for かかわる in this expression. Both are common in print.

Related Words

Related: のに (casual equivalent)
Related: けれども (but, although)
Synonym: それなのに (everyday 'and yet')
