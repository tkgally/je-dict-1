見込(みこ)み

みこみ
noun
prospect; expectation; hope; chance
1. prospect; expectation; outlook
The anticipated outcome or future possibility.
2. hope; chance; possibility
The likelihood that something will happen.
回復(かいふく)の見込(みこ)みがない。
There's no hope of recovery.
売上(うりあげ)は前年(ぜんねん)の20%増(ぞう)の見込(みこ)みです。
Sales are expected to increase by 20% from last year.
彼(かれ)は見込(みこ)みのある若者(わかもの)だ。
He's a promising young person.

FORMATION:
見込(みこ)む (to anticipate) → 見込(みこ)み (noun form)

USAGE:

  • 見込(みこ)みがある (there's hope/a chance)
  • 見込(みこ)みがない (there's no hope)
  • 見込(みこ)み違(ちが)い (miscalculation, misjudgment)
  • 見込(みこ)み客(きゃく) (prospective customer)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 成功(せいこう)の見込(みこ)み (chance of success)
  • 完成(かんせい)の見込(みこ)み (expected completion)
  • 見込(みこ)み通(どお)り (as expected)
