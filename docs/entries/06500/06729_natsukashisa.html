懐(なつ)かしさ

なつかしさ
noun
nostalgia; fondness for the past
1. nostalgia; fondness for the past
A warm, pleasant feeling of longing for the past or for familiar things from one's memories.
故郷(ふるさと)の風景(ふうけい)を見(み)て懐(なつ)かしさがこみ上(あ)げてきた。
Seeing the scenery of my hometown, nostalgia welled up inside me.
この曲(きょく)を聞(き)くと懐(なつ)かしさを感(かん)じる。
When I hear this song, I feel nostalgic.
懐(なつ)かしさに浸(ひた)る。
To immerse oneself in nostalgia.

FORMATION:
懐(なつ)かしい (i-adjective) → 懐(なつ)かしさ (noun form)

BASE ADJECTIVE:

懐(なつ)かしい describes:

  • Fond memories
  • Things from the past that evoke warm feelings
  • Familiar things one hasn't encountered in a while

USAGE:

懐(なつ)かしさ is commonly used in:

  • Descriptions of returning to familiar places
  • Reactions to old photos, music, or memories
  • Expressions of longing for the past

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 懐(なつ)かしさがこみ上(あ)げる (nostalgia wells up)
  • 懐(なつ)かしさに浸(ひた)る (to immerse oneself in nostalgia)
  • 懐(なつ)かしさを感(かん)じる (to feel nostalgic)
  • 懐(なつ)かしさと切(せつ)なさ (nostalgia and poignancy - often paired)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Nostalgia holds a special place in Japanese aesthetics. Words like 懐(なつ)かしさ connect to broader cultural concepts of appreciating the passage of time and cherishing memories.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ノスタルジー / ノスタルジア - nostalgia (loanword)
  • 郷愁(きょうしゅう) - homesickness, nostalgia (more literary)
