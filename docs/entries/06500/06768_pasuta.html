パスタ

ぱすた
noun
pasta; Italian noodle dishes
1. pasta; Italian noodle dishes
Loanword from Italian, general term for Italian pasta and noodle dishes.
お昼(ひる)はパスタを食(た)べた。
I had pasta for lunch.
このレストランのパスタは絶品(ぜっぴん)だ。
The pasta at this restaurant is exquisite.
自家製(じかせい)パスタを茹(ゆ)でる。
Boil homemade pasta.

パスタ is a loanword from Italian 'pasta,' used as a general term for Italian noodles and the dishes made from them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パスタを茹(ゆ)でる: to boil pasta
  • パスタを作(つく)る: to make pasta
  • パスタソース: pasta sauce
  • 生(なま)パスタ: fresh pasta

COMMON TYPES:

  • スパゲッティ: spaghetti
  • ペンネ: penne
  • マカロニ: macaroni

POPULAR DISHES IN JAPAN:

  • カルボナーラ: carbonara
  • ペペロンチーノ: peperoncino (garlic and chili)
  • ミートソース: meat sauce pasta (Bolognese-style)
  • ナポリタン: Napolitan (a Japanese-original ketchup-based pasta)
  • 和風(わふう)パスタ: Japanese-style pasta

ナポリタン and 和風(わふう)パスタ are Japanese creations not found in Italy.
