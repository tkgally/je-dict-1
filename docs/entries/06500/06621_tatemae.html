建前(たてまえ)

たてまえ
noun
public stance; facade; official position

Definitions

1. public stance; facade; outward appearance
What one expresses publicly to maintain social harmony, regardless of one's true feelings.
2. principle; policy; official position
The stated rule or policy that should be followed in principle.

Examples

それは建前(たてまえ)で、本当(ほんとう)は違(ちが)う。
That's just the public stance; the truth is different.
建前(たてまえ)では賛成(さんせい)だと言(い)っている。
In public, they say they agree.
会社(かいしゃ)の建前(たてまえ)としては残業(ざんぎょう)は禁止(きんし)だ。
The company's official policy is that overtime is prohibited.

Notes

CULTURAL CONCEPT:
建前(たてまえ) is the public face one presents to maintain social harmony (和(わ)). It is not considered dishonest in Japanese culture but rather a social skill for smooth relationships.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 建前(たてまえ)を言(い)う (to state the public position)
  • 建前(たてまえ)と本音(ほんね) (public stance and true feelings)
  • 建前(たてまえ)では～ (officially, in principle...)
  • 建前(たてまえ)を使(つか)い分(わ)ける (to use appropriate facades for different situations)

ORIGINAL MEANING:
建前(たてまえ) originally referred to the framework of a building (建(た)てる + 前(まえ)), symbolizing the outward structure.

Related Words

Antonym: 本音(ほんね) (true feelings)
