1. to say definitively; to declare; to assert
To state something with complete certainty and conviction, leaving no room for doubt or hedging.
はっきり言(い)い切(き)った。
I stated it clearly.
安全(あんぜん)だとは言(い)い切(き)れない。
I can't say for certain that it's safe.
彼(かれ)は「絶対(ぜったい)に成功(せいこう)する」と言(い)い切(き)った。
He declared with certainty, "I will definitely succeed."
彼女(かのじょ)が犯人(はんにん)だとは言(い)い切(き)れない。
I can't say for certain that she's the culprit.
自信(じしん)を持(も)って「間違(まちが)いない」と言(い)い切(き)れる人(ひと)は少(すく)ない。
Few people can confidently declare, "There's no mistake."
2. to finish saying; to say completely
To complete what one was saying without being interrupted or stopping midway.
最後(さいご)まで言(い)い切(き)った。
I said everything I needed to say.
話(はなし)を言(い)い切(き)る前(まえ)に遮(さえぎ)られた。
I was interrupted before I could finish speaking.
言(い)い切(き)らないうちに電話(でんわ)が切(き)れた。
The call was disconnected before I could finish what I was saying.
文(ぶん)を言(い)い切(き)ってから次(つぎ)に進(すす)みましょう。
Let's finish saying the sentence before moving on.
緊張(きんちょう)して、スピーチを最後(さいご)まで言(い)い切(き)れなかった。
I was so nervous that I couldn't finish my speech.
  • 言(い)い切(き)る → 言(い)い切(き)らない (negative)
  • 言(い)い切(き)る → 言(い)い切(き)って (te-form)
  • 言(い)い切(き)る → 言(い)い切(き)った (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 言(い)う (to say) + 切(き)る (completely)

The ～切(き)る pattern indicates doing something completely or with conviction.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜と言(い)い切(き)る - to declare that...
  • 言(い)い切(き)れない - cannot say for certain (very common negative form)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 断言(だんげん)して言(い)い切(き)る - to assert categorically
  • はっきり言(い)い切(き)る - to state clearly
  • 自信(じしん)を持(も)って言(い)い切(き)る - to state with confidence

USAGE NOTE:
The negative form 「言(い)い切(き)れない」 is especially common for expressing uncertainty or hedging statements.
