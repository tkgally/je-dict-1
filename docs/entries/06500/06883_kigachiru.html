気(き)が散(ち)る

きがちる
expression
distracted, unable to focus
1. distracted, unable to concentrate
Describes a state where one's attention is diverted and concentration is broken.
周(まわ)りがうるさくて気(き)が散(ち)る。
It's too noisy around here and I can't focus.
スマホを見(み)ると気(き)が散(ち)るから片付(かたづ)けた。
I put my phone away because it was distracting me.
気(き)が散(ち)って勉強(べんきょう)に集中(しゅうちゅう)できない。
I'm too distracted to concentrate on studying.

STRUCTURE: 気(き) (attention) + が + 散(ち)る (to scatter). Literally 'one's attention scatters.'

CAUSATIVE FORM: 気(き)を散(ち)らす (to distract someone/oneself)

  • ゲームで気(き)を散(ち)らす (to distract oneself with games)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)できない (can't concentrate)
  • 注意(ちゅうい)が散漫(さんまん) (attention is scattered - more formal)

CONTRAST:

  • 気(き)が散(ち)る: passive distraction (something distracts you)
  • 気(き)を取(と)られる: attention is captured by something
