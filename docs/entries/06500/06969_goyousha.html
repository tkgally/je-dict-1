ご容赦(ようしゃ)

ごようしゃ
noun
pardon; forgiveness; understanding
1. pardon; forgiveness; understanding; forbearance
Formal/honorific expression asking for forgiveness or understanding.
ご不便(ふべん)をおかけしますが、ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください。
We apologize for the inconvenience; please bear with us.
品切(しなぎ)れの際(さい)はご容赦(ようしゃ)ください。
Please understand if items are out of stock.
何卒(なにとぞ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)のほど、お願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
We humbly ask for your forgiveness.

STRUCTURE:
ご (honorific prefix) + 容赦(ようしゃ) (pardon, leniency).

REGISTER:
Very formal, and directed outward from an organization to its customers or audience. Learners will meet it far more often reading than speaking — in service announcements, business correspondence, official apologies, and posted notices. It is not used between friends.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY ASKS FOR:
Despite the "forgiveness" gloss, ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください rarely follows a real wrongdoing. It asks the reader to accept an inconvenience in advance — a delay, a shortage, a limit on service — and is closer to English "please bear with us" or "we appreciate your understanding."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)ください: please bear with us
  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)願(ねが)います: we ask your understanding
  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)のほど: adds a further layer of formality
  • 何卒(なにとぞ)ご容赦(ようしゃ)〜: the most humble version, standard in written apologies

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ご了承(りょうしょう)ください: please note and accept — used for facts the reader must simply acknowledge
  • ご理解(りかい)ください: please understand the reasoning
  • お許(ゆる)しください: please forgive — for an actual fault, and less institutional
