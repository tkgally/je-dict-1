楽譜(がくふ)

がくふ
noun
musical score; sheet music
1. musical score; sheet music
A written or printed representation of music using musical notation. Includes all forms from full orchestral scores to simple song sheets.
楽譜(がくふ)を見(み)ながらピアノを弾(ひ)く。
I play the piano while looking at the sheet music.
この曲(きょく)の楽譜(がくふ)をダウンロードした。
I downloaded the sheet music for this song.
楽譜(がくふ)が読(よ)めなくても耳(みみ)で覚(おぼ)えられる。
Even if you can't read sheet music, you can learn by ear.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 楽譜(がくふ)を読(よ)む: to read music
  • 楽譜(がくふ)が読(よ)めない: to be unable to read music — the usual way to say it, with the potential form
  • 楽譜(がくふ)を書(か)く: to write out music
  • 楽譜(がくふ)台(だい): a music stand
  • 楽譜(がくふ)売(う)り場(ば): the sheet music department of a shop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 譜面(ふめん): the same thing, but used more by working musicians and in band contexts; 楽譜(がくふ) is the neutral everyday word
  • 五線譜(ごせんふ): staff notation specifically — the five-line staff itself
  • 総譜(そうふ): a full orchestral score, showing every part
  • パート譜(ふ): a part, the single-instrument extract a player actually reads from

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 音符(おんぷ): a musical note
  • 休符(きゅうふ): a rest
