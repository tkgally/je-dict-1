フェス

ふぇす
noun
festival (especially music); fest
1. festival; fest (especially music festival)
A large outdoor event featuring multiple musical performances, typically spanning one or more days. Abbreviated from フェスティバル (festival).
夏(なつ)はロックフェスに行(い)きたい。
I want to go to a rock festival in summer.
このフェスは毎年(まいとし)野外(やがい)で開催(かいさい)される。
This festival is held outdoors every year.
フェスの準備(じゅんび)で忙(いそが)しい。
I'm busy with festival preparations.

ORIGIN:
Clipped from フェスティバル (festival). The short form is the everyday word; the full form sounds formal or official.

USAGE:

Used above all of outdoor music events, especially the summer season of them:

  • ロックフェス — rock festival
  • 夏(なつ)フェス — summer festival (as a season and a genre of event)
  • 野外(やがい)フェス — outdoor festival
  • 音楽(おんがく)フェス — music festival

EXTENDED USAGE:
The suffix has spread to any large themed gathering with stalls and a crowd: ビールフェス (beer festival), グルメフェス (food festival), アニメフェス (anime festival). Note that it does not replace 祭(まつ)り for a traditional shrine or town festival.
