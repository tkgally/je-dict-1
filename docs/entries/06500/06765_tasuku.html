タスク

たすく
noun
task; assignment; job
1. task; assignment; job
Loanword from English, common in business and computing contexts.
今日(きょう)のタスクをリストアップした。
I listed today's tasks.
このタスクは優先度(ゆうせんど)が高(たか)い。
This task has high priority.
タスクを完了(かんりょう)したら報告(ほうこく)してください。
Please report when you've completed the task.

タスク is a loanword from English "task." It is workplace and computing vocabulary rather than everyday speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タスクを完了(かんりょう)する - to complete a task
  • タスクを割(わ)り振(ふ)る - to assign tasks
  • タスクを管理(かんり)する - to manage tasks
  • タスクをこなす - to get through tasks

COMPUTING:
In computing, タスクマネージャー (task manager) is a common application name.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仕事(しごと) - work, job. The general everyday word; タスク is one discrete item within it.
  • 作業(さぎょう) - work, operation. Emphasizes the hands-on doing of it.
  • 課題(かだい) - assignment, challenge. Used in school and for problems to be solved.

Related Words
