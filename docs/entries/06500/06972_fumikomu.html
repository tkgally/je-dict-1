1. to step into; to set foot in
To physically enter a space by stepping inside, often with determination or force.
敵(てき)の陣地(じんち)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)んだ。
I stepped into enemy territory.
未知(みち)の領域(りょういき)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)む勇気(ゆうき)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need the courage to step into unknown territory.
他人(たにん)のプライバシーに踏(ふ)み込(こ)むべきではない。
You shouldn't intrude on other people's privacy.
2. to raid; to burst into
To forcibly enter a location, typically used in the context of police raids or investigations.
警察(けいさつ)が容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の家(いえ)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)んだ。
The police raided the suspect's house.
捜査官(そうさかん)が事務所(じむしょ)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)んで証拠(しょうこ)を押(お)さえた。
Investigators raided the office and seized evidence.
麻薬(まやく)取引(とりひき)の現場(げんば)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)んだ。
They raided the site of a drug deal.
3. to delve into; to go deeply into
Figuratively, to explore a topic, issue, or territory more deeply than before.
この問題(もんだい)についてもう少(すこ)し踏(ふ)み込(こ)んで議論(ぎろん)しましょう。
Let's delve a little deeper into this issue.
核心(かくしん)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)んだ質問(しつもん)をした。
I asked a question that got to the heart of the matter.
もう一歩(いっぽ)踏(ふ)み込(こ)んで分析(ぶんせき)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to take the analysis one step further.

  • 踏(ふ)み込(こ)む → 踏(ふ)み込(こ)んで (te-form)
  • 踏(ふ)み込(こ)む → 踏(ふ)み込(こ)んだ (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 踏(ふ)む (to step on) + 込(こ)む (into)

The ～込(こ)む pattern indicates entering into or going deeply into something.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家宅(かたく)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)む - to raid a house
  • プライバシーに踏(ふ)み込(こ)む - to intrude on someone's privacy
  • 核心(かくしん)に踏(ふ)み込(こ)む - to get to the heart of the matter
  • 一歩(いっぽ)踏(ふ)み込(こ)む - to take a step further
