三脚(さんきゃく)

さんきゃく
noun
tripod
1. tripod
A three-legged stand used to support cameras, telescopes, or other equipment. Provides stability for photography, videography, and observation.
夜景(やけい)撮影(さつえい)には三脚(さんきゃく)が必須(ひっす)だ。
A tripod is essential for night photography.
カメラを三脚(さんきゃく)に固定(こてい)した。
I mounted the camera on the tripod.
軽量(けいりょう)の三脚(さんきゃく)を旅行(りょこう)に持(も)っていく。
I take a lightweight tripod when traveling.

ETYMOLOGY:
三(さん) (three) + 脚(きゃく) (leg) — literally 'three legs'. A three-legged stand for steadying a カメラ, a telescope, or similar equipment.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 三脚(さんきゃく)を立(た)てる: to set up a tripod
  • 三脚(さんきゃく)に載(の)せる: to mount something on a tripod
  • 三脚(さんきゃく)を畳(たた)む: to fold a tripod down
  • 携帯(けいたい)三脚(さんきゃく): a portable tripod
  • ミニ三脚(さんきゃく): a mini tripod
  • スマホ三脚(さんきゃく): a phone tripod

RELATED WORDS:

  • 一脚(いっきゃく): a monopod, with a single leg
  • 雲台(うんだい): the tripod head — the swivelling part the camera actually screws onto, bought separately on serious tripods

PRACTICAL NOTE:
Many Japanese tourist sites, museums, and event venues ban tripods because of the space they take and the trip hazard. Watch for signs reading 三脚(さんきゃく)使用(しよう)禁止(きんし) or simply 三脚(さんきゃく)禁止(きんし).
