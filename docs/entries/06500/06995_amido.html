網戸(あみど)

あみど
noun
screen door; window screen
1. screen door; window screen
A door or window frame fitted with a fine mesh screen that allows air to circulate while keeping out insects. Essential for Japanese summers when windows are opened for ventilation.
夏(なつ)は網戸(あみど)にして風(かぜ)を通(とお)す。
In summer, I use the screen door to let the breeze through.
網戸(あみど)に穴(あな)が開(あ)いて蚊(か)が入(はい)ってきた。
Mosquitoes got in through a hole in the screen.
網戸(あみど)を張(は)り替(か)える時期(じき)だ。
It's time to replace the screen.

ETYMOLOGY:
網(あみ) (net/mesh) + 戸(と/ど) (door). The reading 戸(ど) is a voiced compound form.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 網戸(あみど)を閉(し)める - to close the screen door
  • 網戸(あみど)を張(は)り替(か)える - to replace the screen
  • 網戸(あみど)の網(あみ) - screen mesh
  • 網戸(あみど)を掃除(そうじ)する - to clean the screen

CULTURAL NOTES:
Screen doors are ubiquitous in Japanese homes and are essential for summer living. Most Japanese windows are designed with sliding screen panels. Replacing or repairing screens (網(あみ)の張(は)り替(か)え) is a common household maintenance task.
