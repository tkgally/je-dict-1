1. to incorporate; to build in; to integrate
To include something as an integral part of a larger system, plan, or structure. Commonly used in technical, business, and planning contexts.
仕事(しごと)に組(く)み込(こ)む。
To incorporate into one's work.
新(あたら)しい機能(きのう)が組(く)み込(こ)まれた。
New features were built in.
AIを製品(せいひん)に組(く)み込(こ)む計画(けいかく)がある。
There are plans to incorporate AI into products.
予算(よさん)に予備費(よびひ)を組(く)み込(こ)んでおくべきだ。
You should incorporate a contingency fund into the budget.
この設計(せっけい)には安全(あんぜん)装置(そうち)が最初(さいしょ)から組(く)み込(こ)まれている。
Safety devices have been built into this design from the start.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 組(く)む (to assemble) + ～込(こ)む (into). The ～込(こ)む pattern indicates putting something into, or integrating it into, something else.

ASPECT:
組(く)み込(こ)まれている describes a resultant state — "is built in", "has been incorporated" — not an action in progress. This is the most common form in technical writing.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • システムに組(く)み込(こ)む - to build into a system
  • 計画(けいかく)に組(く)み込(こ)む - to incorporate into a plan
  • 予算(よさん)に組(く)み込(こ)む - to include in a budget
  • 設計(せっけい)に組(く)み込(こ)む - to build into a design
  • 組(く)み込(こ)みシステム - embedded system (technical term)

REGISTER:
Common in technical and business contexts. The noun form 組(く)み込(こ)み is used as a modifier, as in 組(く)み込(こ)みソフトウェア (embedded software).
