切(せつ)なさ

せつなさ
noun
heartache; poignancy; bittersweet sadness

Definitions

1. heartache; poignancy; bittersweet sadness
A feeling of tender sadness or emotional pain, often associated with longing, love, or nostalgia.

Examples

別(わか)れの切(せつ)なさを感(かん)じた。
I felt the heartache of parting.
この曲(きょく)には言葉(ことば)にできない切(せつ)なさがある。
This song has an inexpressible poignancy.
胸(むね)が締(し)め付(つ)けられるような切(せつ)なさを覚(おぼ)えた。
I felt a tightening heartache in my chest.

Notes

FORMATION:
切(せつ)ない (i-adjective) → 切(せつ)なさ (noun form)

BASE ADJECTIVE:

切(せつ)ない describes:

  • Painful longing
  • Tender sadness
  • Poignant emotions
  • Heartache from love or separation

USAGE:

切(せつ)なさ is commonly used in:

  • Descriptions of music, movies, or literature
  • Expressions of romantic feelings
  • Nostalgia for the past
  • Descriptions of bittersweet moments

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 切(せつ)なさが込(こ)み上(あ)げる (heartache wells up)
  • 切(せつ)なさに耐(た)えられない (cannot bear the heartache)
  • なんとも言(い)えない切(せつ)なさ (indescribable poignancy)

CULTURAL NOTE:
切(せつ)なさ is a valued aesthetic emotion in Japanese culture, often appreciated in art, literature, and music. It's related to concepts like 物(もの)の哀(あわ)れ (pathos of things).

Related Words

Related: 切(せつ)ない (base adjective)
Related: 懐(なつ)かしさ (nostalgia)
