手掛(てが)かり

てがかり
noun
clue; hint; lead; handhold
1. clue; hint; lead
Information that helps solve a problem or find something.
事件(じけん)解決(かいけつ)の手掛(てが)かりを探(さが)す。
To search for clues to solve the case.
何(なに)か手掛(てが)かりになるものはありませんか。
Is there anything that could be a clue?
犯人(はんにん)の手掛(てが)かりは現場(げんば)に残(のこ)された指紋(しもん)だけだった。
The only clue to the culprit was the fingerprints left at the scene.
2. handhold; grip
Something to hold onto when climbing or moving.
岩(いわ)の手掛(てが)かりをつかんで登(のぼ)る。
To climb by grabbing handholds on the rock.
崖(がけ)に手掛(てが)かりがなくて、登(のぼ)れなかった。
There were no handholds on the cliff, so I couldn't climb it.
壁(かべ)のわずかな手掛(てが)かりに指(ゆび)をかけて、少(すこ)しずつ上(うえ)に進(すす)んだ。
I put my fingers on the slight handholds on the wall and moved up little by little.

A compound of 手(て) (hand) + 掛(か)かり (something to attach to). Has both abstract (clue) and concrete (handhold) senses.

SENSE 1 — CLUE:

  • 手掛(てが)かりを見(み)つける: to find a clue
  • 手掛(てが)かりを得(え)る: to obtain a lead
  • 手掛(てが)かりがない: there is no clue
  • 手掛(てが)かりをつかむ: to get a lead

SENSE 2 — HANDHOLD:

  • 岩(いわ)の手掛(てが)かり: a handhold on a rock
  • 手掛(てが)かりをつかんで登(のぼ)る: to climb by gripping handholds

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ヒント: hint (loanword, more casual)
  • 糸口(いとぐち): a starting point, a clue to unraveling something
  • 足掛(あしが)かり: foothold (physical or figurative)

Related Words
