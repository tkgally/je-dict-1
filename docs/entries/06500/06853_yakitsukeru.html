焼(や)き付(つ)ける

やきつける
verb (ichidan, transitive)
to burn in; to sear into memory; to imprint

Definitions

1. to burn in; to brand
To burn an image or mark onto something.
2. to sear into memory; to imprint
To make a strong impression that stays in one's mind.

Examples

その光景(こうけい)は目(め)に焼(や)き付(つ)いている。
That scene is seared into my eyes.
彼女(かのじょ)の笑顔(えがお)を心(こころ)に焼(や)き付(つ)けた。
I imprinted her smile in my heart.
鉄(てつ)に印(しるし)を焼(や)き付(つ)ける。
To brand a mark onto iron.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 焼(や)き付(つ)ける → 焼(や)き付(つ)けない (negative)
  • 焼(や)き付(つ)ける → 焼(や)き付(つ)けて (te-form)
  • 焼(や)き付(つ)ける → 焼(や)き付(つ)けた (past)

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 焼(や)く (to burn) + 付(つ)ける (to attach)

USAGE:

Often used in the intransitive form 焼(や)き付(つ)く:

  • 目(め)に焼(や)き付(つ)く (to be seared into one's eyes)
  • 心(こころ)に焼(や)き付(つ)く (to be imprinted on one's heart)
  • 記憶(きおく)に焼(や)き付(つ)く (to be burned into memory)

The metaphorical sense (sense 2) is more common in everyday speech.
