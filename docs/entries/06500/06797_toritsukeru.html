1. to install, to attach, to fit
To install equipment or attach something to a fixed position. Often used for mounting devices, fixtures, or appliances.
エアコンを取(と)り付(つ)ける工事(こうじ)をした。
We had the air conditioner installed.
棚(たな)を壁(かべ)に取(と)り付(つ)けた。
I attached the shelf to the wall.
玄関(げんかん)に防犯(ぼうはん)カメラを取(と)り付(つ)けた。
I installed a security camera at the entrance.
2. to secure, to obtain (an appointment/promise)
To successfully obtain an appointment, meeting, or promise from someone.
社長(しゃちょう)との面会(めんかい)の約束(やくそく)を取(と)り付(つ)けた。
I secured an appointment for a meeting with the president.
出版社(しゅっぱんしゃ)から契約(けいやく)を取(と)り付(つ)けた。
I secured a contract from the publisher.
相手(あいて)から協力(きょうりょく)の約束(やくそく)を取(と)り付(つ)けることができた。
I was able to secure a promise of cooperation from the other party.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Intransitive pair: 取(と)り付(つ)く.

COMPOUND VERB: Formed from 取(と)る (to take) + 付(つ)ける (to attach).

PARTICLES: In sense 1 the thing installed takes を and the place it is fixed to takes に. In sense 2 the person the promise is obtained from takes から.

SENSE 2: The 'secure a promise/appointment' sense is common in business and news writing, where it implies the agreement took some effort to obtain.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • エアコンを取(と)り付(つ)ける (install an air conditioner)
  • 棚(たな)を取(と)り付(つ)ける (install a shelf)
  • センサーを取(と)り付(つ)ける (install a sensor)
  • 約束(やくそく)を取(と)り付(つ)ける (secure a promise)
  • アポを取(と)り付(つ)ける (secure an appointment)

RELATED: 取(と)り外(はず)す (to remove/detach) is the opposite action.
