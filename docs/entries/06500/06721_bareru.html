1. to be found out; to be exposed
For a secret, lie, or hidden action to become known; to be discovered.
嘘(うそ)がばれた。
The lie was found out.
内緒(ないしょ)にしていたのに、親(おや)にばれた。
I was keeping it secret, but my parents found out.
ばれないように気(き)を付(つ)けていたのに。
I was being careful not to get found out, and yet...
浮気(うわき)が妻(つま)にばれてしまった。
My affair was discovered by my wife.
カンニングがばれて、試験(しけん)は不合格(ふごうかく)になり、先生(せんせい)にもひどく叱(しか)られた。
The cheating was discovered, the exam was failed, and the teacher scolded me severely.

FORMS:

  • ばれる → ばれない (negative)
  • ばれる → ばれて (te-form)
  • ばれる → ばれた (past)

ALTERNATIVE FORM:
バレる - sometimes written in katakana

USAGE:
Colloquial verb commonly used for secrets, lies, or hidden actions being discovered. More casual than 発覚(はっかく)する (to be uncovered).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に ばれる (to be found out by ~)
  • ばれないように (so as not to be found out)
  • ばれたらどうしよう (what if it's discovered)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嘘(うそ)がばれる (a lie is found out)
  • 秘密(ひみつ)がばれる (a secret is exposed)
  • 浮気(うわき)がばれる (an affair is discovered)
  • 正体(しょうたい)がばれる (true identity is revealed)
  • カンニングがばれる (cheating is caught)

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive counterpart is ばらす (to reveal, to expose): 秘密(ひみつ)がばれる (the secret gets out) vs. 秘密(ひみつ)をばらす (to let the secret out).
