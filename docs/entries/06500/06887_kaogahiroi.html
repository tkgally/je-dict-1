顔(かお)が広(ひろ)い

かおがひろい
expression
well-connected, having many acquaintances
1. well-connected, having a wide circle of acquaintances
Describes someone who knows many people in various fields or circles and has an extensive network.
彼(かれ)は業界(ぎょうかい)で顔(かお)が広(ひろ)い。
He's well-connected in the industry.
顔(かお)が広(ひろ)い人(ひと)に紹介(しょうかい)してもらった。
I was introduced by someone with many connections.
政治家(せいじか)は顔(かお)が広(ひろ)くないと務(つと)まらない。
You can't serve as a politician without being well-connected.
社長(しゃちょう)は顔(かお)が広(ひろ)いので、困(こま)ったときに頼(たよ)りになる。
The president is well-connected, so you can count on him when you're in trouble.
彼女(かのじょ)は顔(かお)が広(ひろ)くて、どこに行(い)っても知(し)り合(あ)いがいる。一緒(いっしょ)に歩(ある)くと何度(なんど)も声(こえ)をかけられる。
She's so well-connected that she has acquaintances everywhere. When I walk with her, people greet her again and again.

STRUCTURE: 顔(かお) (face) + が + 広(ひろ)い (wide). Literally 'one's face is wide' - meaning one's face is known widely.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 顔(かお)が利(き)く (to have influence, pull) - using connections
  • 人脈(じんみゃく)が広(ひろ)い (to have a wide network) - more formal
  • 知(し)り合(あ)いが多(おお)い (to have many acquaintances) - simpler expression

NUANCE: Implies not just knowing many people casually, but having meaningful connections that can be useful in business or social situations.

CULTURAL NOTE: In Japanese society, personal connections (人脈(じんみゃく)) are highly valued for getting things done. Being 顔(かお)が広(ひろ)い is seen as a valuable social asset.
