焦(こ)がす

こがす
verb (godan, transitive)
to burn; to scorch; to char

Definitions

1. to burn; to scorch; to char
To apply too much heat to something, causing it to become burnt or charred on the surface. Often refers to accidentally burning food while cooking.
2. to be consumed by (emotion); to be burning with
To be intensely preoccupied or tormented by a strong emotion such as longing, jealousy, or impatience.

Examples

フライパンで肉(にく)を焦(こ)がしてしまった。
I accidentally burned the meat in the frying pan.
パンを焦(こ)がさないように気(き)をつけて。
Be careful not to burn the bread.
彼(かれ)は嫉妬(しっと)で身(み)を焦(こ)がしている。
He is consumed by jealousy.

Notes

CONJUGATION:

  • 焦(こ)がす → 焦(こ)がさない (negative)
  • 焦(こ)がす → 焦(こ)がして (te-form)
  • 焦(こ)がす → 焦(こ)がした (past)
  • 焦(こ)がす → 焦(こ)がせる (potential)
  • 焦(こ)がす → 焦(こ)がされる (passive)

TRANSITIVITY:

焦(こ)がす is transitive: someone burns something.

Intransitive pair: 焦(こ)げる (to get burnt, to scorch)

  • パンを焦(こ)がした (I burned the bread) - transitive
  • パンが焦(こ)げた (The bread got burned) - intransitive

Sense 2 USAGE:

身(み)を焦(こ)がす - to be consumed/tormented by (emotion)

  • 恋(こい)に身(み)を焦(こ)がす (to be burning with love)
  • 嫉妬(しっと)に身(み)を焦(こ)がす (to be consumed by jealousy)

Related Words

Pair verb: 焦(こ)げる (intransitive)
