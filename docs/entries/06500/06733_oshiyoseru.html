1. to surge; to rush in; to press forward
To move forward in a mass or with force, like waves or a crowd.
大波(おおなみ)が岸(きし)に押(お)し寄(よ)せる。
Large waves surge toward the shore.
台風(たいふう)で高波(たかなみ)が海岸(かいがん)に押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
High waves surged toward the coast due to the typhoon.
不安(ふあん)が次々(つぎつぎ)と押(お)し寄(よ)せてきた。
Waves of anxiety came rushing over me one after another.
2. to crowd in; to throng
For people or things to gather or arrive in large numbers.
観客(かんきゃく)がステージに押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
The audience surged toward the stage.
注文(ちゅうもん)が押(お)し寄(よ)せて対応(たいおう)しきれない。
Orders are flooding in and we can't keep up.
セールが始(はじ)まると、大勢(おおぜい)の客(きゃく)が店(みせ)に押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
When the sale started, a huge crowd of customers thronged to the store.

FORMATION:
Compound verb: 押(お)す (to push) + 寄(よ)せる (to bring near). The compound describes a mass moving toward a point under its own pressure, so the subject is typically a collective or an uncountable force rather than a single person.

USAGE:

Often used to describe:

  • 波(なみ)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (waves surging)
  • 群衆(ぐんしゅう)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (crowd pressing in)
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせが押(お)し寄(よ)せる (inquiries flooding in)
  • 不安(ふあん)が押(お)し寄(よ)せる (anxiety welling up)

The verb is intransitive: the thing that surges is marked with が, and the place it surges toward with に.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 殺到(さっとう)する - to rush in, to flood (more intense, and used only of people, orders, or inquiries — not of waves)

Related Words
