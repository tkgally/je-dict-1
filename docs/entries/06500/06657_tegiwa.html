手際(てぎわ)

てぎわ
noun
skill; dexterity; efficiency; workmanship

Definitions

1. skill; dexterity; efficiency; workmanship
The ability to do something skillfully, smoothly, and efficiently. Refers to how well and quickly one handles tasks, especially practical or manual ones.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は手際(てぎわ)よく料理(りょうり)を作(つく)った。
She cooked the meal skillfully and efficiently.
手際(てぎわ)が悪(わる)くて、時間(じかん)がかかってしまった。
I was inefficient and it took too long.
手際(てぎわ)のいい仕事(しごと)ぶりに感心(かんしん)した。
I was impressed by the skillful way the work was done.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 手際(てぎわ)がいい/よい (skillful, efficient, deft)
  • 手際(てぎわ)が悪(わる)い (clumsy, inefficient)
  • 手際(てぎわ)よく (skillfully, efficiently - adverb)
  • 鮮(あざ)やかな手際(てぎわ) (brilliant/impressive skill)

USAGE:

手際(てぎわ) emphasizes smooth, efficient execution—doing something without wasted motion or time.

  • 手際(てぎわ)よく片付(かたづ)ける (to clean up efficiently)
  • 手際(てぎわ)よく仕事(しごと)をこなす (to handle work efficiently)

CONTEXT:
Often used for cooking, housework, crafts, and any practical tasks where efficiency and skill are valued.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 手(て) (hand) + 際(ぎわ) (edge/skill) = skill with the hands

RELATED WORDS:

  • 腕前(うでまえ) (skill, ability - more about capability level)
  • 手(て)さばき (dexterity, handling skill - especially with tools/objects)
