おのずと

おのずと
adverb
naturally; spontaneously; of itself
1. naturally; spontaneously; of itself
Happening without deliberate effort or external force; arising as a natural consequence.
練習(れんしゅう)を続(つづ)ければ、おのずと上達(じょうたつ)する。
If you keep practicing, you'll naturally improve.
時間(じかん)が経(た)てば、おのずと答(こた)えは見(み)えてくる。
If you give it time, the answer will naturally become clear.
おのずと涙(なみだ)がこぼれた。
Tears spontaneously fell.

KANJI:
Can be written as 自(おの)ずと. The kanji 自(おの) emphasizes "self" or "by itself."

ALTERNATIVE FORMS:

  • おのずから - slightly more formal/literary variant
  • 自(おの)ずと - kanji spelling

USAGE:

Describes events or outcomes that occur without forcing:

  • おのずと結果(けっか)が出(で)る (results will come naturally)
  • おのずと分(わ)かる (will naturally understand)
  • おのずと限界(げんかい)がある (there are natural limits)

NUANCE:
Implies a natural, organic process rather than forced or artificial one. Often suggests that patience or proper conditions will lead to a natural outcome.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自然(しぜん)と - naturally (more common in casual speech)
  • ひとりでに - by itself, automatically
  • いつの間(ま)にか - before one knows it
