アポ

あぽ
noun
appointment; scheduled meeting
1. appointment; scheduled meeting
A pre-arranged meeting or appointment, especially in business contexts. Abbreviated from アポイントメント (appointment).
明日(あした)、午後(ごご)3時(じ)にアポを入(い)れてあります。
I have an appointment scheduled for 3 PM tomorrow.
アポなしで訪問(ほうもん)するのは失礼(しつれい)だ。
It's rude to visit without an appointment.
新規(しんき)のお客様(きゃくさま)からアポが取(と)れた。
I got an appointment with a new client.

ABBREVIATION:
アポ is clipped from アポイントメント (appointment). The full form is rare in speech.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • アポを取(と)る (to secure an appointment - from the perspective of the person requesting it)
  • アポを入(い)れる (to put an appointment in the calendar)
  • アポがある (to have an appointment)
  • アポなし (without an appointment)

USAGE:
アポ is a business appointment - a meeting arranged in advance with a client, a contact, or a company. It is not used for a doctor's appointment or a restaurant booking, which take 予約(よやく).

  • アポ営業(えいぎょう) - appointment-based sales, as against 飛(と)び込(こ)み営業(えいぎょう) (cold calling)
  • アポなし訪問(ほうもん) - an unannounced visit, generally treated as rude in Japanese business culture

REGISTER:

Casual business slang; acceptable in speech and internal messages, but replaced in formal writing by:

  • 約束(やくそく) (appointment, arrangement)
  • 面会(めんかい)の予約(よやく) (a booked meeting)
  • 訪問(ほうもん)の予定(よてい) (a scheduled visit)

SIMILAR ABBREVIATIONS:

  • リスケ (reschedule - from リスケジュール)
  • プレゼン (presentation - from プレゼンテーション)

Related Words

Related: リスケ (reschedule (same clipping pattern))
Related: 約束(やくそく) (appointment, promise (formal equivalent))
Contrast: 予約(よやく) (reservation (restaurants, doctors))
