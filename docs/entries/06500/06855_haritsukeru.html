1. to paste; to stick on; to attach
To affix something to a surface using adhesive, or to paste digital content.
壁(かべ)にポスターを貼(は)り付(つ)けた。
I stuck a poster on the wall.
コピーした文章(ぶんしょう)をメールに貼(は)り付(つ)ける。
To paste the copied text into an email.
切手(きって)を封筒(ふうとう)に貼(は)り付(つ)けてください。
Please stick the stamp on the envelope.

A compound verb: 貼(は)る (to stick) + 付(つ)ける (to attach). The second element adds the sense that the thing ends up firmly fixed in place, so 貼(は)り付(つ)ける sounds more deliberate and more permanent than plain 貼(は)る.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • The thing stuck takes を; the surface takes に: 壁(かべ)にポスターを貼(は)り付(つ)ける

ASPECT (ている):
貼(は)り付(つ)けている is the act in progress. For the resulting state — the poster is up on the wall — use the passive 貼(は)り付(つ)けられている or, more commonly, 貼(は)るてある.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シールを貼(は)り付(つ)ける (to stick on a sticker)
  • 写真(しゃしん)を貼(は)り付(つ)ける (to paste a photo)
  • リンクを貼(は)り付(つ)ける (to paste a link)

COMPUTING USE:
For copy-and-paste, 貼(は)り付(つ)ける is the term menus use, but in speech it is usually shortened to 貼(は)る or replaced by ペーストする.

KANJI NOTE:
Also written 張(は)り付(つ)ける. 貼(は)り付(つ)ける is the standard spelling for sticking and pasting; 張(は)り is preferred when something is stretched or posted up over an area.
