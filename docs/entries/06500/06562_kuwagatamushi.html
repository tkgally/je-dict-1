クワガタムシ

くわがたむし
noun
stag beetle
1. stag beetle
A large beetle with distinctive mandibles that resemble deer antlers. Popular among Japanese children as collectible insects.
夏休(なつやす)みに山(やま)でクワガタムシを捕(つか)まえた。
I caught a stag beetle in the mountains during summer vacation.
クワガタムシは夜行性(やこうせい)だ。
Stag beetles are nocturnal.
息子(むすこ)がクワガタムシを飼(か)いたがっている。
My son wants to keep a stag beetle as a pet.

ETYMOLOGY:
The name comes from 鍬形(くわがた) (hoe-shaped decoration on samurai helmets) + 虫(むし) (insect), referring to the beetle's large mandibles that resemble this decoration.

ABBREVIATION:
Often shortened to クワガタ in casual speech.

POPULAR HOBBY:
Collecting and raising stag beetles (昆虫(こんちゅう)採集(さいしゅう)) is a beloved summer activity for Japanese children. They are often kept alongside カブトムシ (rhinoceros beetles).

COMMON SPECIES:

  • オオクワガタ (giant stag beetle) - the most prized species
  • ノコギリクワガタ (saw stag beetle)
  • ミヤマクワガタ (deep mountain stag beetle)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クワガタムシを飼(か)う (to keep a stag beetle)
  • クワガタムシを捕(つか)まえる (to catch a stag beetle)
  • クワガタムシの幼虫(ようちゅう) (stag beetle larva)
  • クワガタムシの餌(えさ) (stag beetle food - usually tree sap or jelly)
