ドリンクバー

どりんくばー
noun
drink bar; self-service drink station (all-you-can-drink)
1. drink bar; self-service drink station
A self-service beverage station at restaurants where customers can help themselves to unlimited drinks for a fixed price. Common at family restaurants, fast food chains, and karaoke establishments in Japan.
ドリンクバーを付(つ)けますか。
Would you like to add the drink bar?
ファミレスでドリンクバーを頼(たの)んで長居(ながい)した。
I ordered the drink bar at the family restaurant and stayed for a long time.
ドリンクバーは何杯(なんばい)でもおかわり自由(じゆう)です。
The drink bar offers unlimited refills.

ORIGIN:
和製英語(わせいえいご) (Japanese-made English) from "drink" + "bar"
In English, this concept would be called "self-service drinks" or "unlimited soft drinks."

TYPICAL OFFERINGS:

  • ソフトドリンク (soft drinks)
  • ジュース (juice)
  • コーヒー/紅茶(こうちゃ) (coffee/tea)
  • スープ (soup - sometimes)

WHERE FOUND:

  • ファミレス (family restaurants) - Denny's, Gusto, Saizeriya, etc.
  • ファストフード (fast food)
  • カラオケ (karaoke establishments)
  • 漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)/ネットカフェ (manga/internet cafés)

PRICING:
Typically charged as an add-on to meals, usually around ¥200-400.

CULTURAL NOTE:
ドリンクバー is popular for students studying or friends meeting to chat, as it allows extended stays with unlimited drinks.

Related Words

Related: 飲(の)み放題(ほうだい) (all-you-can-drink)
