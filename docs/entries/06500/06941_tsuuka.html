つーか

つーか
conjunction
or rather; I mean; more like

Definitions

1. or rather; I mean; more like
Very casual contraction of というか, used to correct or rephrase what was just said.

Examples

つーか、それ本当(ほんとう)なの？
I mean, is that really true?
難(むずか)しいっていうか、つーか無理(むり)だよ。
It's hard, or rather, it's impossible.
つーか、なんで来(き)たの？
More like, why did you even come?

Notes

ORIGIN:
Contraction of というか → ていうか → つーか

REGISTER:
Very casual/colloquial. Used primarily in spoken Japanese among friends and peers.

USAGE:

  • At the beginning of a sentence to redirect the conversation
  • To correct or modify a previous statement
  • Can express mild disagreement or skepticism

FORMALITY SCALE:

  • というか (neutral)
  • ていうか (casual)
  • つーか (very casual)
