名残(なごり)

なごり
noun
traces; remnants; lingering effects; parting

Definitions

1. traces; remnants; lingering effects
What remains after something has passed or ended; vestiges of the past.
2. reluctance to part; attachment at parting
The feeling of sadness or reluctance when separating from someone or something.

Examples

冬(ふゆ)の名残(なごり)がまだ残(のこ)っている。
Traces of winter still remain.
この建物(たてもの)には昔(むかし)の名残(なごり)がある。
This building has remnants of the past.
名残(なごり)惜(お)しいが、そろそろ帰(かえ)らなければ。
I'm reluctant to leave, but I must go soon.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
From 波(な)残(ごり) (waves remaining) - originally the traces left on the shore after waves recede.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 名残(なごり)惜(お)しい (reluctant to part, sad to leave)
  • 名残(なごり)の雪(ゆき) (lingering snow of late winter)
  • 名残(なごり)を惜(お)しむ (to be reluctant to part)
  • 冬(ふゆ)の名残(なごり) (remnants of winter)

CULTURAL NOTE:
名残(なごり) evokes a poetic sense of transience and the bittersweet feelings associated with endings. It's often used in seasonal references (名残(なごり)の桜(さくら), the last cherry blossoms).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 面影(おもかげ) (traces, vestiges) - focuses on visual memory or resemblance
  • 名残(なごり) - broader, includes physical traces and emotional attachment
