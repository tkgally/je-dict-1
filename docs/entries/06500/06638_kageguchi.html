陰口(かげぐち)

かげぐち
noun
gossip; speaking ill of someone behind their back
1. gossip; talking negatively about someone behind their back
Speaking ill of someone when they are not present; malicious gossip.
陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)うのはよくない。
It's not good to talk behind people's backs.
彼女(かのじょ)は陰口(かげぐち)を叩(たた)かれている。
People are gossiping about her behind her back.
陰口(かげぐち)ばかり言(い)う人(ひと)は信用(しんよう)できない。
You can't trust people who are always gossiping about others.

WORD FORMATION:
陰(かげ) (shadow, behind) + 口(くち) (mouth, speech) → speech made in the shadows

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)う / 叩(たた)く (to gossip, to badmouth)
  • 陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)われる / 叩(たた)かれる (to be gossiped about)
  • 陰口(かげぐち)が多(おお)い (there's a lot of gossip)

CONTRAST:

  • 陰口(かげぐち) (behind one's back) ↔ 面(めん)と向(む)かって (to one's face)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悪口(わるぐち) (badmouthing) - negative talk, can be direct or behind the back
  • 噂(うわさ) (rumor) - talk about someone, not necessarily negative
  • 陰口(かげぐち) - specifically negative talk behind someone's back

CULTURAL NOTE:
陰口(かげぐち) is generally considered very negative behavior in Japanese social contexts, as it damages group harmony (和(わ)).
