陰口(かげぐち)

かげぐち
noun
gossip; speaking ill of someone behind their back
1. gossip; talking negatively about someone behind their back
Speaking ill of someone when they are not present; malicious gossip.
陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)うのはよくない。
It's not good to talk behind people's backs.
彼女(かのじょ)は陰口(かげぐち)を叩(たた)かれている。
People are gossiping about her behind her back.
陰口(かげぐち)ばかり言(い)う人(ひと)は信用(しんよう)できない。
You can't trust people who are always gossiping about others.
自分(じぶん)がいない所(ところ)で陰口(かげぐち)を叩(たた)かれていると知(し)って、とても傷(きず)ついた。
I was deeply hurt when I found out people were gossiping about me behind my back.
陰口(かげぐち)は本人(ほんにん)の耳(みみ)に入(はい)ることが多(おお)いから、気(き)をつけた方(ほう)がいい。
Gossip often reaches the person it's about, so you should be careful.

陰口(かげぐち) combines 陰(かげ) (shadow, behind) and 口(くち) (speech): saying bad things about someone where they cannot hear it.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)う / 叩(たた)く (to badmouth someone behind their back)
  • 陰口(かげぐち)を言(い)われる / 叩(たた)かれる (to be talked about behind one's back)
  • 陰口(かげぐち)が多(おお)い (there is a lot of gossip)

The idiomatic verb is 叩(たた)く (literally "to hit"), which is at least as common as 言(い)う here.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悪口(わるぐち): badmouthing generally, whether to the person's face or not
  • 噂(うわさ): rumor or talk about someone, not necessarily negative
  • 陰口(かげぐち) is specifically negative talk, and specifically out of earshot

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
陰口(かげぐち) is judged harshly because it damages group 和(わ) (harmony) while giving the target no chance to answer. Being caught at it costs more social standing than the criticism itself would have.

Related Words

Synonym: 悪口(わるくち) (bad-mouthing (general))
