しかしながら

しかしながら
conjunction
however, nevertheless (formal)
1. however, nevertheless, nonetheless
Formal conjunction introducing a contrasting statement, more emphatic than しかし.
計画(けいかく)は完璧(かんぺき)だった。しかしながら、結果(けっか)は期待(きたい)に沿(そ)わなかった。
The plan was perfect. However, the results did not meet expectations.
努力(どりょく)は認(みと)める。しかしながら、結果(けっか)が全(すべ)てである。
I acknowledge the effort. Nevertheless, results are everything.
理論的(りろんてき)には可能(かのう)である。しかしながら、実現(じつげん)は困難(こんなん)だ。
Theoretically, it is possible. However, implementation is difficult.
ご要望(ようぼう)は理解(りかい)しております。しかしながら、対応(たいおう)いたしかねます。
I understand your request. However, we cannot accommodate it.

REGISTER: Formal/written. Common in:

  • Business correspondence
  • Academic papers
  • Formal speeches
  • News commentary
  • Official statements

ETYMOLOGY: しかし (however) + ながら (while/though)
The ながら adds emphasis, making it stronger than plain しかし.

FORMALITY SCALE ("however"):

  • でも: casual
  • けど/けれど: casual to neutral
  • しかし: formal
  • しかしながら: very formal
  • ただし: formal (adds condition)

NUANCE: Used when:

  • Making important contrasts
  • Delivering disappointing news politely
  • Introducing significant objections
  • Creating dramatic contrast in writing

COMMON IN BUSINESS:

  • ご提案(ていあん)は魅力的(みりょくてき)です。しかしながら... (Your proposal is attractive. However...)

AVOID IN CASUAL SPEECH: Sounds overly stiff. Use でも, けど, or ただ instead.
