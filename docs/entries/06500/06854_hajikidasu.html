1. to calculate; to compute
To work out a number or result, especially using a calculator or abacus.
コンピューターで答(こた)えを弾(はじ)き出(だ)す。
To calculate the answer with a computer.
予算(よさん)を弾(はじ)き出(だ)したら、思(おも)ったより高(たか)かった。
When I calculated the budget, it was higher than I thought.
損害額(そんがいがく)を弾(はじ)き出(だ)すのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to calculate the amount of damage.
2. to push out; to force out
To expel or exclude someone or something.
競争(きょうそう)で負(ま)けた会社(かいしゃ)は市場(しじょう)から弾(はじ)き出(だ)される。
Companies that lose the competition are pushed out of the market.
グループから弾(はじ)き出(だ)されて一人(ひとり)ぼっちになった。
I was pushed out of the group and left all alone.
安(やす)い輸入品(ゆにゅうひん)に弾(はじ)き出(だ)されて国内(こくない)メーカーは苦(くる)しんでいる。
Domestic manufacturers are struggling, pushed out by cheap imports.

A compound verb: 弾(はじ)く (to flick, to repel) + 出(だ)す (to put out). The two senses come from the two readings of that flick — flicking beads to produce a figure, or flicking something away so it ends up outside.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Sense 1 takes を for the figure produced: 数字(すうじ)を弾(はじ)き出(だ)す
  • Sense 2 takes を for the one expelled and から for the place they are expelled from

ETYMOLOGY:
The calculating sense comes from the 算盤(そろばん) (abacus), whose beads are flicked to produce a result. It still suggests working a figure out rather than merely reading it off, so it fits estimates and projections better than simple arithmetic.

SENSE 2 (exclusion):
Sense 2 is most often passive — 弾(はじ)き出(だ)される — because the interesting party is the one pushed out, not whoever did the pushing. It is stronger and more impersonal than simply being left out: a market or a group squeezes the person or company out.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 数字(すうじ)を弾(はじ)き出(だ)す (to work out figures)
  • 見積(みつも)りを弾(はじ)き出(だ)す (to calculate an estimate)
  • 仲間(なかま)から弾(はじ)き出(だ)される (to be excluded from a group)
