1. to stay overnight, to lodge
To spend the night at a place that is not one's own home, such as a hotel, friend's house, or inn.
ホテルに泊(と)まる。
To stay at a hotel.
友達(ともだち)の家(いえ)に泊(と)まった。
I stayed over at a friend's house.
京都(きょうと)で二泊(にはく)泊(と)まって、観光(かんこう)を楽(たの)しんだ。
I stayed two nights in Kyoto and enjoyed sightseeing.
今夜(こんや)はどこに泊(と)まりますか？
Where will you stay tonight?
旅行(りょこう)で三日間(みっかかん)、温泉(おんせん)に泊(と)まりました。
On my trip, I stayed at a hot spring inn for three days.
出張(しゅっちょう)のとき、いつも駅(えき)の近(ちか)くのホテルに泊(と)まることにしています。
When I'm on business trips, I always make it a point to stay at a hotel near the station.

泊(と)まる means to stay overnight. Different kanji from 止(と)まる (to stop).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Transitive pair: 泊(と)める (to accommodate, to let someone stay)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に泊(と)まる: to stay at ~
  • 一泊(いっぱく)する: to stay one night
  • 泊(と)まりがけで: staying overnight

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泊(と)まる: to stay overnight (at accommodation)
  • 止(と)まる: to stop (movement ceases)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)する: to lodge (formal)
