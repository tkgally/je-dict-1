ざわめき

ざわめき
noun
commotion; murmur; bustle
1. commotion, murmur, bustle
The combined sound of many people talking, moving, or reacting at once. A low, unsettled buzz of activity or chatter.
会場(かいじょう)にざわめきが広(ひろ)がった。
A murmur spread through the venue.
教室(きょうしつ)のざわめきが静(しず)まった。
The commotion in the classroom died down.
発表(はっぴょう)の瞬間(しゅんかん)、観客(かんきゃく)のざわめきが一斉(いっせい)に歓声(かんせい)に変(か)わった。
The moment of the announcement, the murmur of the audience turned into cheers all at once.

RELATED VERB: ざわめく (to murmur, to buzz, to be in commotion). ざわめき is the noun form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ざわめきが広(ひろ)がる: a murmur spreads
  • ざわめきが静(しず)まる: the commotion dies down
  • ざわめきが起(お)きる: a stir arises
  • 街(まち)のざわめき: the bustle of the city

ONOMATOPOEIA: Related to the onomatopoeia ざわざわ, which describes the sound and feeling of restless commotion or unease.
