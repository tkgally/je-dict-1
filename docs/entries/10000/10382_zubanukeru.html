ずば抜(ぬ)ける

ずばぬける
verb (ichidan)
to be outstanding; to stand out
1. to be outstanding, to stand out, to be far above the rest
To be exceptionally superior compared to others in a group. Implies a clear gap between the subject and the rest.
彼女(かのじょ)の成績(せいせき)はずば抜(ぬ)けている。
Her grades are outstanding.
ずば抜(ぬ)けた才能(さいのう)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
She is a person of exceptional talent.
あのチームの中(なか)で、彼(かれ)の運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)はずば抜(ぬ)けていて、誰(だれ)もかなわない。
Among that team, his athletic ability is far above the rest, and no one can match him.

CONJUGATION:

  • ずば抜(ぬ)けない（否定(ひてい)）
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けます（丁寧(ていねい)）
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けて（て形(けい)）
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けた（過去(かこ)）

COMMON FORMS:

  • ずば抜(ぬ)けている: is outstanding (state)
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けた + noun: outstanding ~ (as a modifier)
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けて + adjective: outstandingly ~ (as an adverb)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ずば抜(ぬ)けた才能(さいのう): exceptional talent
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けた能力(のうりょく): outstanding ability
  • ずば抜(ぬ)けて高(たか)い: outstandingly high

USAGE: Almost always used in a positive sense. The た-form (ずば抜(ぬ)けた) and ている-form are the most common.
