潜(くぐ)る

くぐる
godan verb
to pass through; to duck under; to go through
1. to pass through, to go under, to duck under
To move through a narrow opening, under a low barrier, or through a gate or tunnel.
トンネルを潜(くぐ)る。
Go through a tunnel.
鳥居(とりい)を潜(くぐ)って境内(けいだい)に入(はい)った。
I passed through the torii gate and entered the shrine grounds.
フェンスの下(した)を潜(くぐ)って向(む)こう側(がわ)に出(で)た。
I ducked under the fence and came out on the other side.
2. to survive, to come through (danger/difficulty)
To get through a dangerous or difficult situation, implying having survived many hardships.
修羅場(しゅらば)を潜(くぐ)ってきた。
I've been through many tough situations.
危険(きけん)を潜(くぐ)り抜(ぬ)けた。
I made it through the danger.
何度(なんど)も死線(しせん)を潜(くぐ)り抜(ぬ)けてきたベテラン兵士(へいし)だ。
He's a veteran soldier who has survived many life-threatening situations.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: トンネルを潜(くぐ)る

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 鳥居(とりい)を潜(くぐ)る: to pass through a torii gate
  • トンネルを潜(くぐ)る: to go through a tunnel
  • 暖簾(のれん)を潜(くぐ)る: to enter through a noren curtain
  • 修羅場(しゅらば)を潜(くぐ)る: to go through tough times
  • 潜(くぐ)り抜(ぬ)ける: to make it through, to slip through

NOTE: Not to be confused with 潜(もぐ)る (same kanji, different reading), which means to dive underwater or to hide.
