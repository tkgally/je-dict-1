和室(わしつ)

わしつ
noun
Japanese-style room
1. Japanese-style room
A traditional Japanese room with tatami mat flooring, typically featuring fusuma (sliding doors) and a tokonoma (alcove).
和室(わしつ)で過(す)ごす。
To spend time in a Japanese-style room.
この旅館(りょかん)には和室(わしつ)と洋室(ようしつ)がある。
This inn has both Japanese-style and Western-style rooms.
畳(たたみ)の和室(わしつ)は落(お)ち着(つ)いた雰囲気(ふんいき)がある。
A Japanese-style room with tatami has a calming atmosphere.

和室(わしつ) is composed of 和(わ) (Japanese) + 室(しつ) (room). It refers to a traditional Japanese-style room with 畳(たたみ) (tatami mat) flooring.

TYPICAL FEATURES:

  • 畳(たたみ): tatami mats (straw flooring)
  • 襖(ふすま): sliding paper doors (between rooms)
  • 障子(しょうじ): paper sliding screens (for windows)
  • 床(とこ)の間(ま): decorative alcove
  • 押(お)し入(い)れ: built-in closet for 布団(ふとん)

CONTRAST:

  • 和室(わしつ): Japanese-style room (tatami, futon, floor seating)
  • 洋室(ようしつ): Western-style room (flooring, beds, chairs)

USAGE:
Common when describing 旅館(りょかん) (Japanese inns), apartments, or house layouts. Room size is measured in 畳(じょう) (tatami mats): 六畳(ろくじょう)の和室(わしつ) (a 6-tatami Japanese room). Modern Japanese homes often have one 和室(わしつ) alongside 洋室(ようしつ).

Related Words

Antonym: 洋室(ようしつ) (Western-style room)
Related: 部屋(へや) (general word for room)
