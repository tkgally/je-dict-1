低姿勢(ていしせい)

ていしせい
noun
humble attitude; low profile
1. humble attitude; low profile; modest stance
An attitude of humility or modesty, often adopted when apologizing, making requests, or dealing with superiors. Literally 'low posture.'
彼(かれ)は低姿勢(ていしせい)で謝罪(しゃざい)した。
He apologized with a humble attitude.
低姿勢(ていしせい)でお{願(ねが)い→お願い}した。
I made a request humbly.
新人(しんじん)は低姿勢(ていしせい)でいるべきだ。
Newcomers should maintain a humble attitude.

USAGE: Describes an attitude of humility, especially in professional or formal situations. Can be genuine humility or strategic modesty.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 低姿勢(ていしせい)で〜する (to do something humbly)
  • 低姿勢(ていしせい)を貫(つらぬ)く (to maintain a humble attitude)

CONTRAST:

  • 高姿勢(こうしせい)/高飛車(たかびしゃ) (arrogant attitude)
general
