蔑(さげす)む

さげすむ
godan verb
to despise, to scorn, to look down on
1. to despise, to scorn, to look down on
To regard someone or something as inferior or unworthy of respect, often with contempt.
人(ひと)を蔑(さげす)むな。
Don't look down on people.
蔑(さげす)むような目(め)で見(み)られた。
I was looked at with contemptuous eyes.
彼(かれ)は貧(まず)しい人(ひと)たちを蔑(さげす)んでいる。
He despises poor people.
弱者(じゃくしゃ)を蔑(さげす)む社会(しゃかい)は健全(けんぜん)ではない。
A society that scorns the weak is not healthy.

蔑(さげす)む expresses contempt or looking down on someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

REGISTER:
Fairly formal or literary; the casual equivalent would be 馬鹿(ばか)にする or 見下(みくだ)す.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)を蔑(さげす)む: to despise someone
  • 蔑(さげす)むような目(め): contemptuous eyes
  • 蔑(さげす)んだ態度(たいど): scornful attitude

RELATED:

  • 蔑(ないがしろ)にする: to neglect, to disregard
  • 侮蔑(ぶべつ): contempt, disdain (noun)
  • 見下(みくだ)す: to look down on
