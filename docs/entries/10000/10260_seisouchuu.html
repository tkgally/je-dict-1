清掃中(せいそうちゅう)

せいそうちゅう
noun
cleaning in progress
1. cleaning in progress, now cleaning
A sign or status indicating that an area is currently being cleaned.
トイレは清掃中(せいそうちゅう)です。
The restroom is being cleaned.
「清掃中(せいそうちゅう)」の看板(かんばん)が出(で)ていた。
There was a "cleaning in progress" sign out.
客室(きゃくしつ)清掃中(せいそうちゅう)のため、お待(ま)ちください。
Please wait while the room is being cleaned.

Commonly seen on signs in restrooms, hotel rooms, and public facilities.

RELATED SIGNS:

  • 点検中(てんけんちゅう): under inspection
  • 工事中(こうじちゅう): under construction
  • 使用禁止(しようきんし): out of service
