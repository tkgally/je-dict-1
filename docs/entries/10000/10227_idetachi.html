出(い)で立(た)ち

いでたち
noun
attire, outfit
1. attire, outfit, getup, dress, appearance
Refers to one's clothing and overall appearance, often emphasizing the style or impression created.
派手(はで)な出(い)で立(た)ちだ。
That's a flashy outfit.
彼(かれ)は登山者(とざんしゃ)のような出(い)で立(た)ちだった。
He was dressed like a mountain climber.
その出(い)で立(た)ちでパーティーに行(い)くの？
Are you going to the party in that getup?

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 派手(はで)な出(い)で立(た)ち: flashy outfit
  • 〜のような出(い)で立(た)ち: dressed like ~
