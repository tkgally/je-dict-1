対象外(たいしょうがい)

たいしょうがい
noun
outside the scope, not applicable
1. outside the scope, not applicable, excluded
Something that is not included within the target range, criteria, or eligibility requirements.
18歳(さい)未満(みまん)は対象外(たいしょうがい)です。
Those under 18 are not eligible.
この割引(わりびき)は一部(いちぶ)商品(しょうひん)は対象外(たいしょうがい)となります。
Some products are excluded from this discount.
海外(かいがい)からの注文(ちゅうもん)は配送(はいそう)対象外(たいしょうがい)です。
Orders from overseas are not eligible for delivery.

対象外(たいしょうがい) indicates that something does not fall within the specified scope or criteria.

WORD FORMATION:
対象(たいしょう) (target, subject) + 外(がい) (outside) = outside the target

OPPOSITE:

  • 対象(たいしょう): target, subject, eligible
  • 対象内(たいしょうない): within scope (less common)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜は対象外(たいしょうがい)です: ~ is not applicable
  • 対象外(たいしょうがい)となる: to be excluded
  • 対象外(たいしょうがい)商品(しょうひん): excluded products

USAGE:
Commonly seen in business, legal documents, and promotional materials to clarify exclusions.
