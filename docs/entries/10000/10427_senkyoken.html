選挙権(せんきょけん)

せんきょけん
noun
right to vote, suffrage
1. right to vote, suffrage
The legal right to participate in elections by casting a vote.
選挙権(せんきょけん)を得(え)る。
To obtain the right to vote.
日本(にほん)では十八歳(じゅうはっさい)から選挙権(せんきょけん)がある。
In Japan, you have the right to vote from age eighteen.
成年(せいねん)に達(たっ)すると選挙権(せんきょけん)が得(え)られる。
Upon reaching adulthood, one can obtain the right to vote.

選挙権(せんきょけん) is composed of 選挙(せんきょ) (election) + 権(けん) (right).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 選挙権(せんきょけん)を持(も)つ: to have the right to vote
  • 選挙権(せんきょけん)を得(え)る: to gain the right to vote
  • 選挙権(せんきょけん)を行使(こうし)する: to exercise one's right to vote

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, the voting age was lowered from 20 to 18 in 2016.
