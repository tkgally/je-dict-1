1. to apply, to put against, to press (something) to
Describes physically placing something against another surface.
傷口(きずぐち)にガーゼをあてがう。
Apply gauze to the wound.
耳(みみ)に受話器(じゅわき)をあてがった。
I put the receiver to my ear.
壁(かべ)に定規(じょうぎ)をあてがって線(せん)を引(ひ)いた。
I drew a line by pressing a ruler against the wall.
2. to allot, to assign, to allocate, to provide
Used when assigning roles, rooms, or resources to someone.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)に仕事(しごと)をあてがう。
Assign work to the new employee.
客(きゃく)に部屋(へや)をあてがった。
I allocated a room to the guest.
子供(こども)には古(ふる)い教科書(きょうかしょ)があてがわれた。
The children were given old textbooks.

あてがう has two distinct senses: physically placing something against a surface, and assigning/allocating something to someone.

WRITING:
Usually written in hiragana. Kanji forms include 宛(あ)てがう and 充(あ)てがう.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

SENSE 1 — PHYSICAL APPLICATION:

  • 傷口(きずぐち)にガーゼをあてがう: to apply gauze to a wound
  • 耳(みみ)に受話器(じゅわき)をあてがう: to put the receiver to one's ear
  • 壁(かべ)に定規(じょうぎ)をあてがう: to press a ruler against the wall

SENSE 2 — ASSIGNING/ALLOCATING:

  • 部屋(へや)をあてがう: to assign a room
  • 仕事(しごと)をあてがう: to assign work
  • 役割(やくわり)をあてがう: to assign a role

NUANCE:
Sense 2 can carry a slightly patronizing or top-down tone, implying the recipient has little choice in the matter.
