蹴躓(けつまず)く

けつまずく
godan verb
to trip, to stumble
1. to trip, to stumble over
To lose one's balance by catching one's foot on something, nearly falling.
石(いし)に蹴躓(けつまず)いた。
I tripped on a stone.
段差(だんさ)に蹴躓(けつまず)いて転(ころ)んだ。
I tripped on a step and fell.
暗(くら)い廊下(ろうか)で何(なに)かに蹴躓(けつまず)いてしまった。
I tripped over something in the dark hallway.
2. to stumble (figuratively), to fail
To encounter an unexpected setback or obstacle in one's plans or progress.
計画(けいかく)が蹴躓(けつまず)いた。
The plan hit a snag.
最初(さいしょ)の一歩(いっぽ)で蹴躓(けつまず)く。
To stumble at the very first step.
順調(じゅんちょう)だった事業(じぎょう)が資金難(しきんなん)で蹴躓(けつまず)いてしまった。
The business that had been going well stumbled due to financial difficulties.

蹴躓(けつまず)く describes physically tripping or figuratively encountering setbacks.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Often written in hiragana as けつまずく or with partial kanji as 躓(つまず)く. The kanji 蹴(け) (kick) and 躓(つまず)く (stumble) combine to form the full word.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 石(いし)に蹴躓(けつまず)く: to trip on a stone
  • 段差(だんさ)に蹴躓(けつまず)く: to trip on a step
  • 計画(けいかく)が蹴躓(けつまず)く: the plan stumbles

RELATED:

  • 躓(つまず)く: variant form (same meaning)
