かつて

かつて
adverb
formerly; once; at one time
1. formerly, once, at one time, in the past
Refers to a time in the past, indicating something that was once the case but is no longer.
かつてここに城(しろ)があった。
There was once a castle here.
かつての同僚(どうりょう)と再会(さいかい)した。
I reunited with a former colleague.
この町(まち)はかつて炭鉱(たんこう)で栄(さか)えていた。
This town once thrived on coal mining.
2. never (with negative)
When used with a negative predicate, emphasizes that something has never happened.
かつてない規模(きぼ)の地震(じしん)だ。
It's an earthquake of unprecedented scale.
こんなに美(うつく)しい景色(けしき)はかつて見(み)たことがない。
I've never seen such a beautiful view.
かつて経験(けいけん)したことのない暑(あつ)さだった。
It was heat like I'd never experienced before.

Also written as 嘗(かつ)て, though hiragana is standard.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • かつての〜: former ~, ex-~ (modifies nouns directly)
  • かつて〜たことがない: have never ~ed
  • かつてない: unprecedented

NUANCE: かつて is more literary/formal than 昔(むかし) or 以前(いぜん). It emphasizes the contrast between a past state and the present, and is commonly used in writing, speeches, and narration. In sense 2, it adds strong emphasis to the negation.
