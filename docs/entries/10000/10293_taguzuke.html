タグ付(づ)け

たぐづけ
noun
tagging; adding tags
1. tagging, adding tags
The act of tagging someone in a social media post or photo, or adding category tags to content for organization. Widely used in the context of Instagram, Twitter/X, and other platforms.
写真(しゃしん)にタグ付(づ)けする。
To tag someone in a photo.
勝手(かって)にタグ付(づ)けしないでほしい。
I wish you wouldn't tag me without asking.
インスタでお店(みせ)のアカウントをタグ付(づ)けして投稿(とうこう)したら、リポストされた。
When I tagged the shop's account on Instagram and posted, they reposted it.

From English "tag" + 付(づ)け (attaching). Used with する: タグ付(づ)けする.

In social media contexts, タグ付(づ)け most commonly refers to mentioning or linking someone's account in a post or photo. It can also mean adding hashtags or category labels.
