1. to fulfill, to accomplish, to carry out
To complete a duty, promise, or goal; to achieve what was intended.
各々(おのおの)が自分(じぶん)の役割(やくわり)を果(は)たした。
Each person fulfilled their role.
約束(やくそく)を果(は)たす。
To keep a promise.
責任(せきにん)を果(は)たす。
To fulfill one's responsibility.
夢(ゆめ)を果(は)たすために努力(どりょく)する。
To work hard to achieve one's dream.

果(は)たす means to fulfill, accomplish, or carry out something to completion. A somewhat formal verb used for duties, promises, and goals.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を果(は)たす

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 役割(やくわり)を果(は)たす: to fulfill a role
  • 約束(やくそく)を果(は)たす: to keep a promise
  • 責任(せきにん)を果(は)たす: to fulfill one's responsibility
  • 義務(ぎむ)を果(は)たす: to carry out one's duty
  • 目的(もくてき)を果(は)たす: to achieve one's purpose
  • 夢(ゆめ)を果(は)たす: to realize one's dream

RELATED WORDS:

  • 果(は)たして: as expected; indeed; really (adverb — different usage)
  • 果(は)てる: to end, to be exhausted (intransitive)

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Often used in written language, speeches, and news. In casual speech, する or やる may replace it.
