くっつける

くっつける
verb (ichidan)
to attach; to stick together; to bring close
1. to attach, to stick, to glue together
To physically join or affix one thing to another.
紙(かみ)をのりでくっつける。
Stick the paper together with glue.
壊(こわ)れた部品(ぶひん)を接着剤(せっちゃくざい)でくっつけた。
I glued the broken part back together with adhesive.
この二(ふた)つのパーツをしっかりくっつけてから、次(つぎ)の工程(こうてい)に進(すす)んでください。
Please firmly attach these two parts before moving on to the next step.
2. to bring close together, to push together
To move things or people close together so they are touching or nearly touching.
机(つくえ)をくっつけて並(なら)べた。
We pushed the desks together.
椅子(いす)をもっとくっつけて座(すわ)ろう。
Let's sit with our chairs closer together.
狭(せま)い部屋(へや)なので、棚(たな)を壁(かべ)にくっつけて置(お)くしかない。
The room is small, so we have no choice but to put the shelf right against the wall.

Transitive form of くっつく (intransitive: to stick, to cling).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • のりでくっつける: to glue together
  • テープでくっつける: to tape together
  • 壁(かべ)にくっつける: to stick to a wall
  • 机(つくえ)をくっつける: to push desks together

PAIR: くっつく（自動詞(じどうし)）= to stick, to cling ↔ くっつける（他動詞(たどうし)）= to attach, to stick together
