陽炎(かげろう)

かげろう
noun
heat haze, heat shimmer
1. heat haze, heat shimmer
The wavering visual distortion caused by heated air rising from the ground on hot days.
夏(なつ)の道(みち)に陽炎(かげろう)が立(た)つ。
Heat shimmer rises from the summer road.
陽炎(かげろう)で遠(とお)くの景色(けしき)が揺(ゆ)れて見(み)える。
The distant scenery appears to waver due to the heat haze.
真夏(まなつ)の昼下(ひるさ)がり、アスファルトの上(うえ)に陽炎(かげろう)が揺(ゆ)らめいていた。
In the midsummer afternoon, heat haze was shimmering above the asphalt.

SEASONAL REFERENCE:
陽炎(かげろう) is a traditional spring seasonal word (季語(きご)) in haiku, though it is most commonly observed on hot summer days.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 陽炎(かげろう)が立(た)つ (heat haze rises)
  • 陽炎(かげろう)が揺(ゆ)らめく (heat haze shimmers)

The phenomenon creates a dreamlike, ephemeral quality often used as a literary metaphor for things that are fleeting or illusory.
