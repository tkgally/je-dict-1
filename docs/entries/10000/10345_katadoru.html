象(かたど)る

かたどる
verb (godan)
to model after; to pattern after; to imitate the shape of
1. to model after, to pattern after, to imitate the shape of
To create something by copying or representing the form or shape of something else.
花(はな)を象(かたど)ったアクセサリー。
An accessory shaped like a flower.
この文字(もじ)は山(やま)の形(かたち)を象(かたど)ったものだ。
This character is modeled after the shape of a mountain.
有名(ゆうめい)な建物(たてもの)を象(かたど)ったケーキが話題(わだい)になっている。
A cake modeled after a famous building has become a topic of conversation.

Etymology: from 形(かた)を取(と)る (to take the shape of). The standard kanji is 象(かたど)る, but it can also be written as 模(かたど)る.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を象(かたど)った＋名詞(めいし): something shaped/modeled after ...
  • 〜の形(かたち)を象(かたど)る: to imitate the shape of ...

CONJUGATION:

  • 象(かたど)らない（否定(ひてい)）
  • 象(かたど)ります（丁寧(ていねい)）
  • 象(かたど)って（て形(けい)）
  • 象(かたど)った（過去(かこ)）

USAGE: Often used in the past-participle form 象(かたど)った as a modifier (e.g., 星(ほし)を象(かたど)ったデザイン). Common in art, design, and descriptions of kanji origins.
