最中(さなか)

さなか
noun
in the midst of; in the middle of; at the height of
1. in the midst of, in the middle of, at the height of
Refers to the very middle of an ongoing action, event, or season. Often emphasizes that something is at its peak or most intense point.
会議(かいぎ)の最中(さなか)に電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)った。
The phone rang in the middle of the meeting.
夏(なつ)の最中(さなか)に停電(ていでん)が起(お)きた。
A power outage happened at the height of summer.
試験(しけん)勉強(べんきょう)の最中(さなか)に友(とも)だちから誘(さそ)いの連絡(れんらく)が来(き)て、集中(しゅうちゅう)できなかった。
A friend contacted me with an invitation while I was in the middle of studying for exams, so I couldn't concentrate.

READING NOTE: The same kanji 最中(さいちゅう) (read さいちゅう) is more commonly used in everyday speech, especially in the pattern 〜している最中(さいちゅう). The reading さなか is more literary.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の最中(さなか)に: in the midst of ...
  • 〜の最中(さなか)で: in the middle of ...

COMPARISON:

  • 最中(さなか): literary, emphasizes being at the peak or critical point
  • 最中(さいちゅう): neutral, common in everyday speech
  • 途中(とちゅう): in the middle of (a process, journey)
