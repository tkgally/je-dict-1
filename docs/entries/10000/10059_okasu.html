犯(おか)す

おかす
verb (godan)
to commit (a crime)
1. to commit (a crime, offense, sin)
To do something wrong or illegal; to perpetrate.
罪(つみ)を犯(おか)す。
To commit a crime.
法律(ほうりつ)を犯(おか)す。
To break the law.
2. to make (a mistake)
To make an error or blunder.
重大(じゅうだい)なミスを犯(おか)した。
I made a serious mistake.
同(おな)じ過(あやま)ちを二度(にど)と犯(おか)さない。
I won't make the same mistake twice.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを犯(おか)す.

HOMOPHONES:

  • 侵(おか)す - to invade, to violate (rights)
  • 冒(おか)す - to brave, to risk
  • 犯(おか)す - to commit (crime) (this entry)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 罪(つみ)を犯(おか)す (to commit a crime/sin)
  • 法(ほう)を犯(おか)す (to break the law)
  • ミスを犯(おか)す (to make a mistake)
  • 過(あやま)ちを犯(おか)す (to make an error)
