お気(き)に入(い)り

おきにいり
noun
favorite; preferred thing or person
1. favorite, preferred thing
Something one likes especially well, such as a favorite item, place, or brand.
お気(き)に入(い)りのカフェ。
My favorite café.
この靴(くつ)はお気(き)に入(い)りだ。
These shoes are my favorite.
お気(き)に入(い)りの曲(きょく)をプレイリストに入(い)れた。
I added my favorite songs to the playlist.
2. favorite person, pet, darling
A person who is especially liked or favored by someone, such as a teacher's pet or a boss's favorite employee.
彼女(かのじょ)は社長(しゃちょう)のお気(き)に入(い)りだ。
She is the president's favorite.
先生(せんせい)のお気(き)に入(い)りになりたい。
I want to become the teacher's favorite.
あの子(こ)はみんなのお気(き)に入(い)りで、いつも注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
That kid is everyone's favorite and always gets attention.

Literally '気(き)に入(い)る' (to take a liking to) with the polite prefix お. Often used as a modifier: お気(き)に入(い)りの + noun.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お気(き)に入(い)りの〜: one's favorite ~
  • お気(き)に入(い)りに登録(とうろく)する: to bookmark/add to favorites (web/apps)
  • お気(き)に入(い)りに入(い)れる: to add to favorites

NUANCE: In sense 2, calling someone お気(き)に入(い)り can imply favoritism and may carry a slightly negative connotation, suggesting unfair preferential treatment.
