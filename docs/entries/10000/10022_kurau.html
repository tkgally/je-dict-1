喰(く)らう

くらう
godan verb
to eat (vulgar); to receive (a blow); to suffer
1. to eat, to devour
Vulgar or rough word for eating, often used in masculine speech or to describe eating voraciously.
腹(はら)いっぱい喰(く)らった。
I ate until I was stuffed.
毎日(まいにち)肉(にく)を喰(く)らう生活(せいかつ)だ。
I live on a diet of eating meat every day.
あいつは牛丼(ぎゅうどん)を三杯(さんばい)も喰(く)らったぞ。
That guy devoured three bowls of beef bowl!
2. to receive (a blow), to get hit
To take a punch, kick, or other physical strike.
パンチを喰(く)らった。
I got punched.
顔面(がんめん)にキックを喰(く)らう。
To take a kick to the face.
相手(あいて)の強烈(きょうれつ)なアッパーを喰(く)らって倒(たお)れた。
I took a powerful uppercut from my opponent and fell down.
3. to suffer, to be subjected to
To experience negative consequences such as defeat, punishment, or misfortune.
大敗(たいはい)を喰(く)らった。
We suffered a crushing defeat.
詐欺(さぎ)に喰(く)らわされた。
I was victimized by a scam.
一週間(いっしゅうかん)の停学(ていがく)処分(しょぶん)を喰(く)らってしまった。
I ended up getting hit with a one-week suspension.

喰(く)らう is a rougher, more masculine variant of 食(た)べる.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

REGISTER:
Senses 1 (eating) is vulgar or rough; senses 2-3 (receiving blows/suffering) are colloquial but widely used.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • パンチを喰(く)らう: to take a punch
  • 一発(いっぱつ)喰(く)らう: to get hit once
  • 大損(おおぞん)を喰(く)らう: to suffer a big loss
  • 詐欺(さぎ)に喰(く)らう: to fall victim to fraud

CAUSATIVE FORM:
喰(く)らわせる/お見舞(みま)いする is used to mean 'to give someone (a blow)'.
