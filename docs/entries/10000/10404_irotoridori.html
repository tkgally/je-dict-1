色(いろ)とりどり

いろとりどり
no-adjective; na-adjective
colorful, multicolored, varied
1. colorful, multicolored, of various colors
Describes something that displays many different colors or varieties. Creates a vivid, vibrant image.
色(いろ)とりどりの花(はな)が咲(さ)いている。
Flowers of many colors are blooming.
色(いろ)とりどりの紅葉(こうよう)がきれいだった。
The colorful autumn leaves were beautiful.
テーブルには色(いろ)とりどりの料理(りょうり)が並(なら)んでいた。
A colorful array of dishes was lined up on the table.

色(いろ)とりどり describes a vivid variety of colors or types. Used as a no-adjective (色(いろ)とりどりの〜) or na-adjective (色(いろ)とりどりな〜), with the の form being more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色(いろ)とりどりの花(はな) (colorful flowers)
  • 色(いろ)とりどりの紅葉(こうよう) (colorful autumn leaves)
  • 色(いろ)とりどりの野菜(やさい) (colorful vegetables)
  • 色(いろ)とりどりの衣装(いしょう) (colorful costumes)

USAGE: While literally about color, it can also suggest variety and diversity in a broader sense. Always carries a positive, visually rich connotation.
