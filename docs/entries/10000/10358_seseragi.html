せせらぎ

せせらぎ
noun
murmuring stream; babbling brook
1. murmuring stream, babbling brook
A small, shallow stream that makes a gentle, rippling sound as it flows. Also refers to the sound itself.
せせらぎの音(おと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the sound of a babbling brook.
森(もり)の中(なか)のせせらぎに癒(いや)された。
I was soothed by the murmuring stream in the forest.
窓(まど)を開(あ)けると、近(ちか)くの川(かわ)のせせらぎが静(しず)かに響(ひび)いていた。
When I opened the window, the gentle murmur of the nearby river echoed softly.

USAGE: A poetic, evocative word frequently used in literature and nature descriptions. Conveys a peaceful, calming atmosphere.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • せせらぎの音(おと): the sound of a murmuring stream
  • 小川(おがわ)のせせらぎ: the babbling of a brook
  • せせらぎに耳(みみ)を傾(かたむ)ける: to listen to the murmur of a stream
