出来上(できあ)がる

できあがる
verb (godan)
to be completed, to be finished, to be ready
1. to be completed, to be finished, to be ready
For something to reach a state of completion. Used for food being cooked, work being done, products being manufactured, etc.
料理(りょうり)が出来上(できあ)がった。
The food is ready.
レポートがやっと出来上(できあ)がった。
The report is finally finished.
新(あたら)しいビルが出来上(できあ)がるまで、あと半年(はんとし)かかるそうだ。
I hear it will take another six months until the new building is completed.

出来上(できあ)がる is the intransitive counterpart of 仕上(しあ)げる. It focuses on the result — the state of being complete.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • No direct object; the completed thing is the subject

ASPECT (ている):

  • 出来上(できあ)がっている: is already completed/finished (resultant state)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料理(りょうり)が出来上(できあ)がる (the food is ready)
  • 作品(さくひん)が出来上(できあ)がる (a work is completed)
  • きれいに出来上(できあ)がる (to turn out beautifully)
  • うまく出来上(できあ)がる (to turn out well)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完成(かんせい)する: to be completed (more formal, for projects/works)
  • 出来上(できあ)がる: to be finished (broader use, including cooking)
