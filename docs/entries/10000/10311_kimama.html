気(き)まま

きまま
na-adjective
free-spirited; selfish; doing as one pleases
1. free-spirited, doing as one pleases, easygoing
Acting according to one's own wishes and whims without worrying about others' expectations or schedules. Can be positive (carefree) or negative (selfish), depending on context.
気(き)ままに旅(たび)をする。
Travel freely as one pleases.
一人暮(ひとりぐ)らしは気(き)ままでいい。
Living alone is nice because you can do as you please.
猫(ねこ)は気(き)ままな動物(どうぶつ)だとよく言(い)われる。
Cats are often said to be free-spirited animals.

Na-adjective: 気(き)ままな + noun (modifier), 気(き)ままに + verb (adverbial).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気(き)ままな暮(く)らし: a carefree lifestyle
  • 気(き)ままに過(す)ごす: to spend time as one pleases
  • 気(き)ままな旅(たび): an unplanned/freewheeling trip
  • 気(き)ままな性格(せいかく): a free-spirited personality

NUANCE: When describing oneself or a positive situation (travel, living alone), 気(き)まま has a positive nuance of freedom and ease. When describing someone else's behavior, it can imply selfishness or inconsiderateness — doing whatever one wants without regard for others.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • わがまま: selfish, willful (more negative)
  • 自由(じゆう): free, liberal (more neutral/positive)
  • マイペース: at one's own pace
