振(ふ)り分(わ)け

ふりわけ
noun, suru-verb
sorting, allocation, distribution
1. sorting, allocation, distribution
The act of dividing and assigning items, tasks, or people into different categories or groups.
仕事(しごと)の振(ふ)り分(わ)けをする。
To allocate work.
予算(よさん)を各部署(かくぶしょ)に振(ふ)り分(わ)ける。
To allocate the budget to each department.
荷物(にもつ)の振(ふ)り分(わ)けに時間(じかん)がかかった。
Sorting the packages took time.
担当者(たんとうしゃ)への振(ふ)り分(わ)けを見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to review the allocation to the staff in charge.

振(ふ)り分(わ)け means sorting or allocating things into groups.

VERB FORM:
振(ふ)り分(わ)ける (ichidan verb): to sort, to allocate, to distribute

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を振(ふ)り分(わ)ける: to allocate work
  • 予算(よさん)を振(ふ)り分(わ)ける: to allocate budget
  • 担当(たんとう)を振(ふ)り分(わ)ける: to assign responsibilities

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Business: task allocation, budget distribution
  • IT: routing, load balancing
  • Daily life: sorting items

RELATED:

  • 割(わ)り振(ふ)り: allocation (similar meaning)
  • 配分(はいぶん): distribution
  • 分担(ぶんたん): sharing of duties
