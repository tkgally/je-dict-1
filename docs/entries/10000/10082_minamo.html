水面(みなも)

みなも
noun
surface of the water
See also: 水面(すいめん) (common/technical reading)
1. surface of the water
The surface layer of a body of water, often used in literary or poetic contexts.
水面(みなも)が光(ひか)っている。
The water's surface is shining.
風(かぜ)で水面(みなも)が揺(ゆ)れた。
The water's surface rippled in the wind.
月(つき)が水面(みなも)に映(うつ)って美(うつく)しかった。
The moon reflected on the water's surface and it was beautiful.

READING DISTINCTION:

  • 水面(みなも): poetic/literary reading (this entry)
  • 水面(すいめん): common/technical reading

The reading みなも evokes a literary, visual quality and is preferred in poetry, fiction, and descriptions of natural beauty. The reading すいめん is used in everyday speech and scientific contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水面(みなも)に映(うつ)る: to be reflected on the water's surface
  • 水面(みなも)が揺(ゆ)れる: the water's surface ripples
  • 水面(みなも)に光(ひかり)が差(さ)す: light falls on the water's surface
  • 水面(みなも)を渡(わた)る風(かぜ): wind crossing the water's surface

ETYMOLOGY:
From 水(み) (water, archaic reading) + な (archaic possessive particle) + 面(も) (surface, archaic reading). The classical Japanese reading gives it a more poetic quality than the on'yomi すいめん.
