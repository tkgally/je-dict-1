活(い)かす

いかす
verb (godan)
to make use of, to put to good use
1. to make use of, to put to good use, to leverage
To effectively use skills, experience, resources, or qualities to achieve something.
経験(けいけん)を活(い)かす。
To make use of one's experience.
自分(じぶん)の特長(とくちょう)を活(い)かせる仕事(しごと)を探(さが)したい。
I want to find a job where I can make use of my strengths.
留学(りゅうがく)で身(み)につけた語学力(ごがくりょく)を仕事(しごと)に活(い)かしたい。
I want to put the language skills I acquired through studying abroad to use in my work.

活(い)かす means to effectively utilize something valuable.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Takes を particle: 〜を活(い)かす

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)を活(い)かす: to make use of experience
  • 能力(のうりょく)を活(い)かす: to leverage one's abilities
  • 特長(とくちょう)を活(い)かす: to put one's strengths to use
  • 資源(しげん)を活(い)かす: to utilize resources

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 活(い)かす: to put to good use (implies effective utilization)
  • 使(つか)う: to use (general, neutral)
  • 利用(りよう)する: to utilize (formal, broader scope)
