あたかも

あたかも
adverb
as if, just like
1. as if, as though, just like, exactly as if
Used to draw comparisons, often followed by ～のように, ～のごとく, or similar expressions. Has a formal or literary tone.
あたかも夢(ゆめ)のようだ。
It's just like a dream.
彼(かれ)はあたかも知(し)らないかのように振(ふ)る舞(ま)った。
He acted as if he didn't know.
その部屋(へや)はあたかも時(とき)が止(と)まったかのような静(しず)けさだった。
The room had a stillness as though time had stopped.

Usually written in hiragana. Also written as 恰(あたか)も.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あたかも〜のように: as if, just like
  • あたかも〜かのように: as though
