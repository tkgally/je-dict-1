宵(よい)の口(くち)

よいのくち
noun
early evening, early part of the night
1. early evening, early part of the night
The period shortly after sunset when the night has just begun, typically around 7-9 PM.
まだ宵(よい)の口(くち)だ。
It's still early evening.
宵(よい)の口(くち)から飲(の)み始(はじ)めた。
We started drinking from early evening.
夏(なつ)の宵(よい)の口(くち)は涼(すず)しくて気持(きも)ちがいい。
Early summer evenings are cool and pleasant.

USAGE NOTE:
Often used to suggest that the night is still young and there's plenty of time left, especially in social contexts like drinking parties.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 宵(よい): evening (literary)
  • 夜(よる): night (general term)
