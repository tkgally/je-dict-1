とうもろこし

とうもろこし
noun
corn, maize
1. corn, maize
A cereal grain and the plant that produces it, widely used as food. In Japan, sweet corn is especially popular boiled or grilled.
とうもろこしを茹(ゆ)でる。
To boil corn.
夏(なつ)はとうもろこしがおいしい季節(きせつ)だ。
Summer is the season when corn is delicious.
屋台(やたい)で焼(や)きとうもろこしを買(か)って食(た)べた。
I bought and ate grilled corn at a street stall.

Can be written in kanji as 玉蜀黍(とうもろこし), but the hiragana or katakana form (トウモロコシ) is far more common.

REGIONAL VARIANTS:

  • とうきび: used in Hokkaido and parts of Tohoku
  • なんば: used in parts of Kansai

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焼(や)きとうもろこし (grilled corn on the cob)
  • とうもろこしの粒(つぶ) (corn kernels)
  • コーンスープ (corn soup — uses the English loanword)

CULTURAL NOTE: Hokkaido is Japan's main corn-producing region. 焼(や)きとうもろこし brushed with soy sauce is a classic summer festival food.
