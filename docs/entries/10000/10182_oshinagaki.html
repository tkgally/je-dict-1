お品書(しなが)き

おしながき
noun
menu (traditional style)
1. menu, bill of fare
A traditional Japanese term for a restaurant menu, often used in more formal or traditional establishments. Conveys a sense of elegance compared to the loanword メニュー.
お品書(しなが)きを見(み)せてください。
Please show me the menu.
お品書(しなが)きは筆(ふで)で書(か)かれている。
The menu is written with a brush.
季節(きせつ)ごとにお品書(しなが)きが変(か)わります。
The menu changes with each season.

WORD FORMATION:
お (honorific prefix) + 品(しな) (item, goods) + 書(が)き (writing, list)

USAGE NOTES:
お品書(しなが)き is used in traditional Japanese restaurants, ryokan (inns), and upscale establishments. It sounds more refined than メニュー. Often written vertically on Japanese paper or displayed on wooden boards.

RELATED TERMS:

  • メニュー: menu (common loanword)
  • 献立(こんだて): meal plan, set menu
  • 本日(ほんじつ)のお品書(しなが)き: today's menu
