お好(この)み焼(や)き

おこのみやき
noun
okonomiyaki, savory pancake
1. okonomiyaki, Japanese savory pancake
A savory Japanese pancake containing various ingredients such as cabbage, meat, and seafood, cooked on a griddle and topped with sauce, mayonnaise, and bonito flakes.
お好(この)み焼(や)きを焼(や)く。
To cook okonomiyaki.
大阪(おおさか)でお好(この)み焼(や)きを食(た)べた。
I ate okonomiyaki in Osaka.
お好(この)み焼(や)きにマヨネーズとソースをかける。
Put mayonnaise and sauce on the okonomiyaki.

ETYMOLOGY:
From お好(この)み (as you like) + 焼(や)き (grilled/fried), meaning "grilled as you like it" because customers can choose their toppings.

REGIONAL STYLES:

  • 大阪(おおさか)風(ふう): ingredients mixed into batter
  • 広島(ひろしま)風(ふう): layered style with noodles

COMMON TOPPINGS:

  • ソース: okonomiyaki sauce
  • マヨネーズ: mayonnaise
  • 鰹節(かつおぶし): bonito flakes
  • 青(あお)のり: green laver seaweed
