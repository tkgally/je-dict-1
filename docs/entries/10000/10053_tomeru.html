留(と)める

とめる
verb (ichidan)
to fasten, to pin
1. to fasten, to fix, to secure
To attach or secure something in place using pins, clips, screws, or similar fasteners.
ピンで留(と)める。
To fasten with a pin.
ねじで板(いた)を留(と)める。
To fasten the board with screws.
書類(しょるい)をクリップで留(と)めた。
I fastened the documents with a clip.
ボタンが留(と)められない。
I can't fasten the button.
2. to pin up, to clip
To hold something in place, especially hair or fabric, with a pin or clip.
髪(かみ)をピンで留(と)める。
To pin up one's hair.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを留(と)める.

HOMOPHONES:

  • 止(と)める - to stop
  • 泊(と)める - to let stay overnight
  • 留(と)める - to fasten (this entry)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ピンで留(と)める (to pin)
  • クリップで留(と)める (to clip)
  • ねじで留(と)める (to screw in)
  • 髪(かみ)を留(と)める (to pin up hair)
  • ボタンを留(と)める (to fasten a button)
