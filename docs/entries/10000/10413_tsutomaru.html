1. to be fit for, to be able to fill a role, to manage a duty
To be capable of fulfilling a role, position, or responsibility. Often used in negative form to express that someone is not up to the task.
私(わたし)にはこの役(やく)は務(つと)まらない。
I'm not fit for this role.
彼(かれ)なら部長(ぶちょう)が十分(じゅうぶん)務(つと)まるだろう。
He should be well-suited for the position of department head.
経験(けいけん)がないと、この仕事(しごと)は務(つと)まらないよ。
Without experience, you won't be able to handle this job.

務(つと)まる is the intransitive counterpart of 務(つと)める (to serve as, to fill a role). It expresses whether someone is capable of the role, rather than the act of performing it.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: 〜が務(つと)まる / 〜は務(つと)まらない

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が務(つと)まる: to be capable of (the role)
  • 〜は務(つと)まらない: to not be up to (the task)
  • 〜には務(つと)まらない: (someone) is not fit for ~

USAGE: Very frequently used in negative form (務(つと)まらない). The positive form implies confident assessment of someone's abilities.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 務(つと)める: to serve as, to perform (transitive — the act of filling a role)
  • 務(つと)まる: to be fit for, to be up to (intransitive — capability for a role)
