町内会(ちょうないかい)

ちょうないかい
noun
neighborhood association
1. neighborhood association, community association
A local residents' organization that manages community affairs such as garbage collection schedules, local festivals, safety patrols, and communication among neighbors.
町内会(ちょうないかい)に入(はい)る。
Join the neighborhood association.
町内会(ちょうないかい)の会合(かいごう)がある。
There is a neighborhood association meeting.
町内会(ちょうないかい)が夏祭(なつまつ)りを主催(しゅさい)している。
The neighborhood association is hosting the summer festival.

WORD FORMATION:
町内(ちょうない) (within the neighborhood) + 会(かい) (association, group)

CULTURAL NOTES:

町内会(ちょうないかい) is a fundamental part of Japanese community life. Activities include:

  • Managing garbage collection points
  • Organizing local festivals
  • Conducting crime prevention patrols
  • Distributing 回覧板(かいらんばん) (neighborhood circulars)
  • Coordinating disaster preparedness

Membership is typically household-based, with monthly or yearly dues.

SYNONYMS:

  • 自治会(じちかい): residents' association (same meaning, different term)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 回覧板(かいらんばん): neighborhood circular
  • 町内会費(ちょうないかいひ): association dues
