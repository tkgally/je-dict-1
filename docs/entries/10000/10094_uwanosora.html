上(うわ)の空(そら)

うわのそら
noun
absentminded, distracted
1. absentminded, distracted, inattentive
A mental state where one's attention is elsewhere and not focused on the present situation or conversation.
彼(かれ)は上(うわ)の空(そら)で聞(き)いていた。
He was listening absentmindedly.
話(はなし)を聞(き)いているのに上(うわ)の空(そら)だね。
You're supposed to be listening, but you seem distracted.
試験(しけん)のことが心配(しんぱい)で、授業(じゅぎょう)中(ちゅう)も上(うわ)の空(そら)だった。
I was so worried about the exam that I was distracted throughout the class.

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally 'the upper sky' - suggesting that one's mind has floated up and away, not grounded in the present moment.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 上(うわ)の空(そら)で聞(き)く (to listen absentmindedly)
  • 上(うわ)の空(そら)の返事(へんじ) (a distracted reply)
  • 上(うわ)の空(そら)になる (to become distracted)

The expression is often used as a na-adjective: 上(うわ)の空(そら)な態度(たいど) (a distracted attitude).
