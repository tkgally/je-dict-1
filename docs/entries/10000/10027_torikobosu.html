取(と)りこぼす

とりこぼす
godan verb
to miss, to let slip, to drop (figuratively)
1. to drop, to let fall
To accidentally drop something when trying to catch or hold it.
ボールを取(と)りこぼした。
I dropped the ball.
グローブからボールを取(と)りこぼす。
To drop the ball from one's glove.
キャッチャーが送球(そうきゅう)を取(と)りこぼしてしまった。
The catcher dropped the throw.
2. to miss, to let slip, to fail to secure
To fail to obtain something that should have been easily achieved, especially opportunities, wins, or points.
勝(か)てる試合(しあい)を取(と)りこぼした。
We let slip a game we should have won.
大事(だいじ)なチャンスを取(と)りこぼす。
To miss an important opportunity.
優勝(ゆうしょう)チームが下位(かい)チーム相手(あいて)に取(と)りこぼすとは意外(いがい)だった。
It was surprising that the champion team let slip a game against a lower-ranked team.

取(と)りこぼす means to drop something or miss an opportunity.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

USAGE:
Commonly used in sports contexts when a favored team loses to a weaker opponent, or when discussing missed opportunities in business or life.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試合(しあい)を取(と)りこぼす: to drop a game
  • チャンスを取(と)りこぼす: to miss a chance
  • 勝(か)ち星(ぼし)を取(と)りこぼす: to let a win slip away
