移(うつ)す

うつす
verb (godan)
to move; to transfer
1. to move; to transfer; to shift
To move something from one place to another. The transitive counterpart to 移る (to move/change).
箱(はこ)の中身(なかみ)を別(べつ)の箱(はこ)に移(うつ)した。
I moved the contents of the box to another box.
本社(ほんしゃ)を東京(とうきょう)に移(うつ)す。
We will move the headquarters to Tokyo.
2. to infect; to pass on (disease)
To transmit an illness or disease to another person.
風邪(かぜ)を人(ひと)に移(うつ)さないように気(き)をつける。
I'm careful not to pass my cold on to others.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 移(うつ)る.

ASPECT: 移(うつ)している indicates ongoing action of moving/transferring.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを〜に移(うつ)す (to move X to ~)
  • 視線(しせん)を移(うつ)す (to shift one's gaze)
  • 風邪(かぜ)を移(うつ)す (to pass on a cold)

PAIR VERB:

  • 移(うつ)る (intransitive - to move, to change)
