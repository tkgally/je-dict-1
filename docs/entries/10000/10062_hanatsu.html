放(はな)つ

はなつ
verb (godan)
to release, to fire
1. to release, to let go, to set free
To allow something or someone to go free; to release from confinement.
鳥(とり)を空(そら)に放(はな)つ。
To release a bird into the sky.
2. to fire, to shoot, to launch
To discharge a projectile such as an arrow, bullet, or light.
的(まと)を狙(ねら)って矢(や)を放(はな)った。
I aimed at the target and shot an arrow.
3. to emit, to give off
To produce and send out light, smell, or other emanations.
光(ひかり)を放(はな)つ。
To emit light.
強(つよ)い香(かお)りを放(はな)っている。
It's giving off a strong fragrance.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを放(はな)つ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 矢(や)を放(はな)つ (to shoot an arrow)
  • 光(ひかり)を放(はな)つ (to emit light)
  • 香(かお)りを放(はな)つ (to give off a fragrance)
  • 野(の)に放(はな)つ (to release into the wild)

RELATED:

  • 放(はな)す - to release, to let go (similar meaning but different nuance)
  • 解(かい)放(ほう) - liberation, release
