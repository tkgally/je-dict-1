1. to frown, to grimace, to scowl, to knit one's brows
To contort one's face, especially the forehead and eyebrows, expressing displeasure, pain, or concentration.
顔(かお)をしかめた。
He grimaced.
苦(にが)い薬(くすり)を飲(の)んで、顔(かお)をしかめた。
She grimaced after taking the bitter medicine.
上司(じょうし)はその報告(ほうこく)を聞(き)いて眉(まゆ)をしかめ、しばらく黙(だま)り込(こ)んだ。
The boss knit his brows upon hearing the report and fell silent for a while.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

Almost always used with 顔(かお) or 眉(まゆ) as the direct object:

  • 顔(かお)をしかめる: to grimace, to make a face
  • 眉(まゆ)をしかめる: to furrow one's brows, to frown

WHAT IT EXPRESSES:

  • 痛(いた)み: pain
  • 不快感(ふかいかん): displeasure, disgust
  • 困惑(こんわく): bewilderment
  • 集中(しゅうちゅう): deep concentration

ASPECT (ている):
顔(かお)をしかめている describes the ongoing state of having a grimace on one's face.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 眉間(みけん)にしわを寄(よ)せる: to furrow one's brow (similar, slightly more literary)
  • 渋(しぶ)い顔(かお)をする: to make a sour face
  • にらむ: to glare (more aggressive)

Related Words

Related: 眉(まゆ)をひそめる (to frown in disapproval (more literary))
