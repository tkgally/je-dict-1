1. clumsy, slow-witted, awkward
Describes someone who is physically clumsy, mentally slow, or generally inept. Originally from Kansai dialect but now widely understood.
どんくさいなあ、もう。
You're so clumsy, honestly.
自分(じぶん)でもどんくさいと思(おも)う。
Even I think I'm clumsy.
どんくさくて運動会(うんどうかい)ではいつも最後(さいご)だった。
I was so clumsy that I always came in last at sports day.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: どんくさく
  • Negative: どんくさくない
  • Past: どんくさかった

ORIGIN:
Originally Kansai dialect, now used across Japan, especially in casual speech.

NUANCE:
Can be used affectionately or teasingly, but can also be rude depending on context. Often used for self-deprecation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鈍(にぶ)い: dull, slow (more standard)
  • 不器用(ぶきよう): clumsy, unskillful (more neutral)
  • のろい: slow, sluggish
