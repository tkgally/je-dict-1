お笑(わら)い

おわらい
noun
comedy; humor; something laughable
1. comedy, humor, comedic entertainment
The genre of comedy and comedic performance, especially the Japanese tradition of stand-up comedy (manzai, conte, etc.).
お笑(わら)い番組(ばんぐみ)を見(み)る。
I watch comedy shows.
彼(かれ)はお笑(わら)い芸人(げいにん)を目指(めざ)している。
He's aiming to become a comedian.
日本(にほん)のお笑(わら)い文化(ぶんか)は漫才(まんざい)とコントが中心(ちゅうしん)だ。
Japanese comedy culture centers on manzai and skit comedy.
2. something laughable, a joke, absurd
Used to describe a situation or thing that is ridiculous or not to be taken seriously.
そんな計画(けいかく)はお笑(わら)いだ。
That plan is a joke.
お笑(わら)い草(ぐさ)にされた。
I was made a laughingstock.
真剣(しんけん)に話(はな)しているのに、お笑(わら)い扱(あつか)いされるのは困(こま)る。
I'm talking seriously, so it's frustrating to be treated as a joke.

The お prefix here adds a conventional, established-word feel rather than politeness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お笑(わら)い番組(ばんぐみ): comedy show/program
  • お笑(わら)い芸人(げいにん): comedian
  • お笑(わら)い草(ぐさ): laughingstock
  • お笑(わら)いライブ: comedy live show

CULTURE: Japanese お笑(わら)い includes 漫才(まんざい) (two-person stand-up with a ボケ/ツッコミ dynamic), コント (scripted comedy skits), and 落語(らくご) (traditional comic storytelling). Major comedy competitions like M-1グランプリ are nationally televised events.
