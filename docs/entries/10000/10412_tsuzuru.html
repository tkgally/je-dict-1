綴(つづ)る

つづる
verb (godan)
to spell; to compose, to write
1. to spell, to write out letters
To write out the letters or characters that form a word.
この単語(たんご)はどう綴(つづ)りますか。
How do you spell this word?
名前(なまえ)をローマ字(じ)で綴(つづ)る。
To spell one's name in Roman letters.
この綴(つづ)りは間違(まちが)っていませんか。
Isn't this spelling wrong?
The noun form 綴(つづ)り means 'spelling.'
2. to compose, to write, to put into words
To express thoughts, feelings, or experiences in written form. Implies a more literary or heartfelt style of writing.
日記(にっき)に想(おも)いを綴(つづ)る。
To put one's feelings into words in a diary.
旅(たび)の思(おも)い出(で)を綴(つづ)った本(ほん)を出版(しゅっぱん)した。
They published a book recounting memories of their travels.
彼(かれ)は戦争(せんそう)の体験(たいけん)を丁寧(ていねい)に綴(つづ)った。
He carefully wrote about his wartime experiences.

綴(つづ)る has two distinct uses: spelling (sense 1) and literary composition (sense 2).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: 〜を綴(つづ)る

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言葉(ことば)を綴(つづ)る (to compose words)
  • 想(おも)いを綴(つづ)る (to put feelings into writing)
  • 手紙(てがみ)を綴(つづ)る (to compose a letter)
  • 日記(にっき)に綴(つづ)る (to write in a diary)

RELATED NOUN:

  • 綴(つづ)り: spelling (noun form)

USAGE: Sense 2 is more literary and implies a thoughtful, deliberate style of writing. For casual "writing," 書(か)く is used instead.
