対面(たいめん)

たいめん
noun
meeting face-to-face; in-person
1. meeting face-to-face, in-person meeting
The act of meeting someone in person, especially for the first time or after a long separation. Also used as a modifier to mean 'in-person' as opposed to online.
初(はじ)めての対面(たいめん)だった。
It was our first face-to-face meeting.
対面(たいめん)での授業(じゅぎょう)が再開(さいかい)された。
In-person classes resumed.
何年(なんねん)もオンラインだけでやりとりしていたが、やっと対面(たいめん)で会(あ)うことができた。
After years of only communicating online, we finally met in person.

USAGE AS SURU-VERB: 対面(たいめん)する means "to meet face-to-face." Example: 長年(ながねん)会(あ)えなかった父(ちち)と対面(たいめん)した。(I met with my father, whom I hadn't seen for years.)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 対面(たいめん)する: to meet face-to-face
  • 対面(たいめん)での〜: in-person ~ (classes, meetings, etc.)
  • 初(はつ)対面(たいめん): first meeting
  • 感動(かんどう)の対面(たいめん): an emotional reunion

MODERN USAGE: Since the COVID-19 pandemic, 対面(たいめん) has become widely used as a modifier meaning "in-person" as opposed to オンライン (online), as in 対面(たいめん)授業(じゅぎょう) (in-person classes) vs. オンライン授業(じゅぎょう) (online classes).
