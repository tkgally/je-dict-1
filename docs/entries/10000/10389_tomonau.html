伴(ともな)う

ともなう
verb (godan)
to accompany; to entail, to involve
1. to accompany, to go together with
To come along with something as a natural or inseparable companion.
雷(かみなり)を伴(ともな)う雨(あめ)。
Rain accompanied by thunder.
成長(せいちょう)に伴(ともな)い、体(からだ)も変(か)わる。
As one grows, the body changes too.
人口(じんこう)増加(ぞうか)に伴(ともな)って、住宅(じゅうたく)不足(ぶそく)が深刻(しんこく)になった。
Along with population growth, the housing shortage became serious.
2. to entail, to involve, to bring with it
To necessarily include or require something as a consequence. Often used for risks, costs, or changes that come with an action.
危険(きけん)を伴(ともな)う仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a job that involves danger.
手術(しゅじゅつ)には痛(いた)みが伴(ともな)う。
Surgery entails pain.
大(おお)きな改革(かいかく)には、いつも犠牲(ぎせい)が伴(ともな)うものだ。
Major reforms always come with sacrifices.

伴(ともな)う is used in both everyday and formal contexts to express that one thing comes with or brings along another.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Both transitive and intransitive
  • Transitive: 〜を伴(ともな)う (to bring along ~, to involve ~)
  • Intransitive: 〜に伴(ともな)う / 〜に伴(ともな)って (to accompany ~, along with ~)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 伴(ともな)っている: is accompanied by, involves (ongoing state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に伴(ともな)って / 〜に伴(ともな)い: along with ~, as ~ occurs
  • 〜を伴(ともな)う: to involve ~, to be accompanied by ~
  • 危険(きけん)を伴(ともな)う: to involve danger
  • 痛(いた)みを伴(ともな)う: to involve pain

USAGE: The pattern 〜に伴(ともな)い is a more formal written alternative to 〜に伴(ともな)って. Both mean "along with" or "as a result of."
