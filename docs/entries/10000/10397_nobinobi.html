伸(の)び伸(の)び

のびのび
adverb
freely, at ease, in a relaxed manner
1. freely, at ease, in a carefree manner
Describes a state of being relaxed and unrestricted, able to grow or act without pressure or constraint. Often used about children growing up or people feeling at ease.
子(こ)どもを伸(の)び伸(の)び育(そだ)てたい。
I want to raise children in a relaxed, free environment.
田舎(いなか)で伸(の)び伸(の)びと暮(く)らしている。
I'm living a carefree life in the countryside.
休日(きゅうじつ)は何(なに)も考(かんが)えず、伸(の)び伸(の)びと過(す)ごしたい。
On my days off, I want to relax and spend time without thinking about anything.

伸(の)び伸(の)び describes a state free from stress or restriction. Often used with the particle と.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 伸(の)び伸(の)びと育(そだ)つ / 育(そだ)てる: to grow up / raise freely
  • 伸(の)び伸(の)びと暮(く)らす: to live in a relaxed way
  • 伸(の)び伸(の)びと過(す)ごす: to spend time in a carefree way
  • 伸(の)び伸(の)びした〜: relaxed, easygoing ~

USAGE: Has a strongly positive connotation. Suggests a healthy, natural state unburdened by excessive rules or pressure.
