務(つと)める

つとめる
verb (ichidan)
to serve as, to fill a role
1. to serve as, to fill the role of
To take on and carry out a particular role, duty, or responsibility.
司会(しかい)を務(つと)める。
To serve as the emcee.
彼(かれ)は委員長(いいんちょう)を務(つと)めている。
He serves as the committee chair.
主役(しゅやく)を務(つと)めるのは初(はじ)めてで、とても緊張(きんちょう)した。
It was my first time playing the lead role, and I was very nervous.

務(つと)める means to fulfill a role or duty.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: 〜を務(つと)める (to serve as ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 司会(しかい)を務(つと)める: to serve as MC
  • 役(やく)を務(つと)める: to play a role
  • 委員長(いいんちょう)を務(つと)める: to serve as chair
  • 責任者(せきにんしゃ)を務(つと)める: to serve as the person in charge

SIMILAR WORDS (same reading, different kanji):

  • 務(つと)める: to serve as, to fill a role
  • 勤(つと)める: to work at, to be employed
  • 努(つと)める: to make an effort

Related Words

Homophone: 勤(つと)める (to work at)
