きっちり

きっちり
adverb
precisely; exactly; tightly
1. precisely, exactly, punctually
With exactness and no room for error, especially regarding time, amounts, or measurements.
きっちり時間(じかん)を守(まも)る。
Keep time precisely.
代金(だいきん)をきっちり払(はら)った。
I paid the exact amount.
彼(かれ)はいつもきっちり五時(ごじ)に退社(たいしゃ)する。
He always leaves the office at exactly five o'clock.
2. tightly, snugly, neatly
Fitting closely with no gaps or looseness.
ふたがきっちり閉(し)まっている。
The lid is shut tight.
服(ふく)をきっちり畳(たた)んだ。
I folded the clothes neatly.
棚(たな)に本(ほん)がきっちり並(なら)んでいる。
The books are lined up neatly on the shelf.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きちんと: properly, neatly (broader, more about doing things the right way)
  • ぴったり: exactly, perfectly fitting
  • きっかり: exactly, precisely (numbers/time only)

NUANCE: きっちり emphasizes precision and leaving no room for error or gaps. It conveys a sense of thoroughness and meticulousness. Can also be used as a na-adjective: きっちりした人(ひと) (a meticulous person).
