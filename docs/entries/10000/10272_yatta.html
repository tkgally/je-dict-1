やった

やった
interjection
yay!; I did it!; hooray!
1. yay!, hooray!, I did it!
An exclamation of joy or triumph when something good happens or a goal is achieved.
やった！合格(ごうかく)した！
Yay! I passed!
やった、試合(しあい)に勝(か)ったぞ！
Yes! We won the game!
やったー！念願(ねんがん)の一等賞(いっとうしょう)をもらった！
Hooray! I got the first prize I've been dreaming of!

Derived from the past tense of やる (to do). Used independently as an exclamation of success or joy.

Often elongated for emphasis: やったー！ The longer the vowel, the more excitement is expressed.

Used by all genders and ages. One of the most common exclamations of happiness in spoken Japanese.

CONTRAST: しまった (oh no!) expresses dismay over a mistake — the opposite emotional reaction.
