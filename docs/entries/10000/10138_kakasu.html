欠(か)かす

かかす
verb (godan)
to miss; to skip; to fail to do
1. to miss; to skip; to fail to do
To fail to do something that should be done regularly. Almost always used in the negative form 欠かさない (to never miss/skip).
祖父(そふ)は毎日(まいにち)散歩(さんぽ)を欠(か)かさない。
My grandfather never misses his daily walk.
忙(いそが)しくても、日課(にっか)だけは欠(か)かさないようにしている。
Even when I'm busy, I make sure to never skip my daily routine.
毎朝(まいあさ)コーヒーを欠(か)かさない。
I never skip my morning coffee.

USAGE: Almost exclusively used in the negative form 欠(か)かさない or 欠(か)かさず (without fail).

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を欠(か)かさない (to never miss ~)
  • 欠(か)かさず〜する (to do ~ without fail)
  • 一日(いちにち)も欠(か)かさず (without missing a single day)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 欠(か)ける (to lack; to be missing - intransitive)
  • 欠(けっ)席(せき)する (to be absent)
