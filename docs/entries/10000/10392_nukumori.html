温(ぬく)もり

ぬくもり
noun
warmth, tenderness
1. warmth, gentle heat
A pleasant, gentle warmth. Can refer to physical warmth or the figurative warmth of human kindness and affection.
手(て)の温(ぬく)もりを感(かん)じた。
I felt the warmth of a hand.
木(き)の家(いえ)には温(ぬく)もりがある。
A wooden house has a feeling of warmth.
家族(かぞく)の温(ぬく)もりに包(つつ)まれて育(そだ)った。
I grew up wrapped in the warmth of my family.

温(ぬく)もり conveys a gentle, comforting warmth — both physical and emotional. It is often used in sentimental or poetic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 温(ぬく)もりを感(かん)じる (to feel warmth)
  • 温(ぬく)もりに包(つつ)まれる (to be enveloped in warmth)
  • 温(ぬく)もりのある〜 (~ with warmth, warm and inviting ~)
  • 人(ひと)の温(ぬく)もり (human warmth)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 暖(あたた)かさ: warmth (more neutral, also used for temperature)
  • 温(ぬく)もり: warmth (more emotional, intimate, poetic)
