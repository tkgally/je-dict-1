思(おも)い

おもい
noun
thought, feeling
1. thought, feeling, emotion
One's inner thoughts, feelings, or emotions about something or someone.
思(おも)いを言葉(ことば)にする。
To put one's feelings into words.
故郷(こきょう)への思(おも)いが強(つよ)い。
I have strong feelings for my hometown.
両親(りょうしん)への感謝(かんしゃ)の思(おも)いを伝(つた)えた。
I expressed my feelings of gratitude to my parents.
2. love, affection
Romantic feelings or love for someone.
彼(かれ)への思(おも)いを告白(こくはく)した。
I confessed my love for him.
片(かた)思(おも)いの相手(あいて)に告白(こくはく)した。
I confessed to the person I had a one-sided crush on.
思(おも)いが届(とど)いた。
My feelings reached them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 思(おも)いを込(こ)める: to put one's heart into
  • 思(おも)いが届(とど)く: one's feelings reach (someone)
  • 思(おも)いを伝(つた)える: to convey one's feelings
  • 片(かた)思(おも)い: one-sided love, unrequited love
  • 思(おも)い出(で): memory, recollection
