回覧板(かいらんばん)

かいらんばん
noun
neighborhood circular, circulating notice board
1. neighborhood circular, circulating notice board
A clipboard or folder containing community notices that is passed from house to house within a neighborhood association. Each household reads the notices and passes it to the next.
回覧板(かいらんばん)が回(まわ)ってきた。
The neighborhood circular came around.
回覧板(かいらんばん)を隣(となり)に回(まわ)す。
Pass the circular to the next-door neighbor.
町内会(ちょうないかい)からの連絡(れんらく)は回覧板(かいらんばん)で届(とど)く。
Notices from the neighborhood association are delivered via the circulating board.

WORD FORMATION:
回覧(かいらん) (circulation, passing around) + 板(ばん) (board)

CULTURAL NOTES:

A traditional Japanese neighborhood communication system. The 回覧板(かいらんばん) typically contains:

  • 町内会(ちょうないかい) meeting notices
  • Community event announcements
  • Garbage collection schedule changes
  • Local safety information

Each household signs or stamps to confirm they have read it before passing it on.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 町内会(ちょうないかい): neighborhood association
  • 自治会(じちかい): residents' association
  • 回覧(かいらん)する: to circulate
