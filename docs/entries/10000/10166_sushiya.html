寿司屋(すしや)

すしや
noun
sushi restaurant
1. sushi restaurant, sushi shop
A restaurant or shop that specializes in serving sushi.
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くに寿司屋(すしや)がある。
There is a sushi restaurant near the station.
誕生日(たんじょうび)に高級(こうきゅう)寿司屋(すしや)で食(た)べた。
I ate at a high-end sushi restaurant on my birthday.
あの寿司屋(すしや)は予約(よやく)が取(と)りにくいほど人気(にんき)がある。
That sushi restaurant is so popular that it's hard to get a reservation.

寿司屋(すしや) specifically refers to restaurants that specialize in sushi.

WORD FORMATION:
寿司(すし) (sushi) + 屋(や) (shop/restaurant suffix)

TYPES OF SUSHI RESTAURANTS:

  • 回転(かいてん)寿司(ずし): conveyor belt sushi (casual, affordable)
  • 高級(こうきゅう)寿司屋(すしや): high-end sushi restaurant (often with a counter)
  • 立(た)ち食(ぐ)い寿司(ずし): standing sushi bar (quick, informal)

CULTURAL NOTE:
At traditional 寿司屋(すしや), customers sit at a counter (カウンター}) facing the {板前(いたまえ) (sushi chef). Ordering おまかせ (chef's choice) is common at higher-end establishments.

THE 屋(や) SUFFIX:
The same pattern forms many shop/restaurant names: 魚屋(さかなや) (fish shop), 花屋(はなや) (flower shop), 肉屋(にくや) (butcher).
