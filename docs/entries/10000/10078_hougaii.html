ほうがいい

ほうがいい
expression
had better, should
1. had better, should, it would be better to
Used to give advice or make suggestions, indicating one option is preferable to another.
あまり物事(ものごと)を難(むずか)しく考(かんが)えないほうがいい。
It's better not to overthink things.
Negative form: Vない + ほうがいい
早(はや)く寝(ね)たほうがいい。
You should go to bed early.
病院(びょういん)に行(い)ったほうがいいですよ。
You should go to the hospital.
Polite form
傘(かさ)を持(も)っていったほうがいい。
You should take an umbrella.

GRAMMAR PATTERN:

  • V(た) + ほうがいい (should do V) - uses past tense verb
  • V(ない) + ほうがいい (should not do V) - uses negative form

EXAMPLES:

  • 食(た)べたほうがいい (should eat)
  • 食(た)べないほうがいい (should not eat)

NUANCE:
This pattern gives advice. It's softer than a command but stronger than a mere suggestion.

POLITENESS:

  • ほうがいい (plain)
  • ほうがいいです (polite)
  • ほうがいいと思(おも)います (more indirect/polite)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜べきだ (should - stronger obligation)
  • 〜たらどうですか (why don't you - suggestion)
