年度始(ねんどはじ)め

ねんどはじめ
noun
beginning of the fiscal year
1. beginning of the fiscal year
The start of the fiscal or academic year in Japan, which falls on April 1st. A period of significant transitions including new school terms, new hires, and organizational changes.
年度始(ねんどはじ)めは忙(いそが)しい。
The beginning of the fiscal year is busy.
年度始(ねんどはじ)めに新(あたら)しい制度(せいど)が始(はじ)まる。
A new system starts at the beginning of the fiscal year.
年度始(ねんどはじ)めは人事異動(じんじいどう)や入社式(にゅうしゃしき)があり、会社(かいしゃ)は慌(あわ)ただしい。
The start of the fiscal year is hectic with personnel transfers and new employee ceremonies.

In Japan, the fiscal and academic year begins on April 1st. This is a major transition point: students enter new school years, new employees start work, and government budgets reset.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 年度末(ねんどまつ) — end of the fiscal year (March)
  • 年度(ねんど) — fiscal year
  • 新年度(しんねんど) — new fiscal year
  • 会計年度(かいけいねんど) — accounting fiscal year

年度始(ねんどはじ)め events include 入学式(にゅうがくしき) (entrance ceremonies), 入社式(にゅうしゃしき) (new employee ceremonies), and 人事異動(じんじいどう) (personnel transfers).
