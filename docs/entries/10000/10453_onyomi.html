音読(おんよ)み

おんよみ
noun
on'yomi, Chinese reading of kanji
1. on'yomi, Chinese reading of kanji
The reading of a kanji character derived from the original Chinese pronunciation. Contrasts with kun'yomi (native Japanese reading).
「山(やま)」の音読(おんよ)みは「サン」だ。
The on'yomi of "山" is "san."
熟語(じゅくご)は音読(おんよ)みで読(よ)むことが多(おお)い。
Compound words are often read with on'yomi.
ひとつの漢字(かんじ)に複数(ふくすう)の音読(おんよ)みがあることもある。
A single kanji can have multiple on'yomi readings.

音読(おんよ)み literally means "sound reading," referring to readings based on Chinese pronunciation.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 音読(おんよ)み: on'yomi (Chinese-derived reading)
  • 訓読(くんよ)み: kun'yomi (native Japanese reading)
  • 熟語(じゅくご): compound word (usually read with on'yomi)

USAGE NOTE:
On'yomi are typically used in multi-kanji compound words (熟語(じゅくご)), while kun'yomi are used when kanji appear alone or with okurigana.
