のりのり

のりのり
na-adjective
in high spirits; pumped up; energetic
1. in high spirits, pumped up, energetic
Describes a state of being highly enthusiastic, energetic, or excited about something. Often used for someone who is really getting into music, dancing, a conversation, or an activity.
今日(きょう)はのりのりだね。
You're really pumped up today.
彼(かれ)はのりのりで踊(おど)っていた。
He was dancing with great energy.
会場(かいじょう)の観客(かんきゃく)はのりのりで盛(も)り上(あ)がっていた。
The audience at the venue was pumped up and excited.

Casual/informal word formed by reduplication of ノリ (rhythm, groove, vibe). Used as a na-adjective: のりのりな状態(じょうたい) or with で: のりのりで踊(おど)る.

Often associated with music, parties, and performances. Positive nuance — the person is enjoying themselves and showing it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • のりのりで踊(おど)る — to dance energetically
  • のりのりで歌(うた)う — to sing enthusiastically
  • のりのりの曲(きょく) — an upbeat, groovy song
