ちなむ

ちなむ
verb (godan)
to be associated with; to derive from
1. to be associated with, to be named after, to derive from
To have a connection or origin related to something. Most commonly encountered in the form ちなんで (named after) or the set phrase ちなみに (incidentally, by the way).
この祭(まつ)りは古(ふる)い伝説(でんせつ)にちなんでいる。
This festival is based on an old legend.
桜(さくら)にちなんだ名前(なまえ)を付(つ)けた。
I gave a name associated with cherry blossoms.
この通(とお)りは、かつてここに住(す)んでいた有名(ゆうめい)な作家(さっか)にちなんで名(な)づけられた。
This street was named after a famous writer who once lived here.

CONJUGATION:

  • ちなまない（否定(ひてい)）
  • ちなみます（丁寧(ていねい)）
  • ちなんで（て形(けい)）
  • ちなんだ（過去(かこ)）

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜にちなんで: named after ~, associated with ~
  • 〜にちなんだ + noun: ~ associated with, ~ named after

RELATED: The common adverb ちなみに (by the way, incidentally) derives from this verb. It is used to add supplementary or related information to a conversation.

USAGE: Rarely used in its plain dictionary form. Almost always appears as ちなんで, ちなんだ, or in the adverbial form ちなみに.
