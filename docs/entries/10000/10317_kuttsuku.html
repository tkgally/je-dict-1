くっつく

くっつく
godan verb
to stick to; to cling to; to be attached
1. to stick to, to cling to, to adhere
To become physically attached or stuck to a surface or object.
ガムが靴(くつ)にくっついた。
Gum stuck to my shoe.
磁石(じしゃく)が冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)にくっつく。
The magnet sticks to the fridge.
湿気(しっけ)で服(ふく)が体(からだ)にくっついて気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い。
My clothes are sticking to my body from the humidity and it's uncomfortable.
2. to stay close, to tag along, to become a couple
To stay very close to someone, or for two people to become romantically involved.
子供(こども)がずっとくっついてくる。
The child keeps clinging to me.
あの二人(ふたり)、ついにくっついたらしい。
Those two apparently finally got together.
猫(ねこ)が飼(か)い主(ぬし)にくっついて離(はな)れない。
The cat clings to its owner and won't let go.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: くっつける (transitive, to attach/stick together)

ASPECT (ている):

  • くっついている means 'is stuck/attached' (resulting state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜にくっつく: to stick to ~
  • くっついて離(はな)れない: sticks and won't come off
  • くっついてくる: to tag along, to cling

NUANCE: In sense 2, くっつく about two people becoming a couple is casual and often used as gossip or lighthearted observation.
