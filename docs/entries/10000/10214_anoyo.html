あの世(よ)

あのよ
noun
the afterlife
1. the next world, the afterlife, the other side
Refers to the world after death in Japanese spiritual and religious belief. Contrasted with この世(よ) (this world).
あの世(よ)で会(あ)おう。
Let's meet in the afterlife.
祖母(そぼ)はあの世(よ)から見守(みまも)っている。
My grandmother is watching over us from the other side.
あの世(よ)に行(い)ってもあなたを忘(わす)れない。
Even if I go to the afterlife, I won't forget you.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • あの世(よ)に行(い)く: to pass away, to go to the afterlife
  • あの世(よ)で会(あ)う: to meet in the afterlife

OPPOSITE:

  • この世(よ): this world, the present life
general
