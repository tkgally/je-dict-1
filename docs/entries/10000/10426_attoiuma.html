あっという間(ま)

あっというま
expression
in the blink of an eye, in no time
1. in the blink of an eye, in no time, before you know it
An expression describing something happening extremely quickly, so fast that one can only say "ah!" Literally: the interval of saying "ah."
あっという間(ま)に終(お)わった。
It was over in the blink of an eye.
休(やす)みはあっという間(ま)に過(す)ぎた。
The vacation was over before I knew it.
久(ひさ)しぶりに友達(ともだち)と話(はな)していたら、時間(じかん)があっという間(ま)に過(す)ぎた。
I was chatting with a friend I hadn't seen in a while, and time flew by in no time.

あっという間(ま) literally means "the space of saying 'ah!'" — so brief that all you can say is "ah!"

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あっという間(ま)に: in no time (followed by a verb)
  • あっという間(ま)だった: it was over in a flash
  • あっという間(ま)の〜: fleeting ~ (modifying a noun)

USAGE:
Nearly always used with the particle に as あっという間(ま)に. This expression is common in both spoken and written Japanese.
