転居届(てんきょとどけ)

てんきょとどけ
noun
change of address notification
1. change of address notification, moving notice
An official form submitted to the local municipal office when moving to a new address. Required by law when changing residence in Japan.
転居届(てんきょとどけ)を出(だ)す。
To submit a change of address notification.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)したら転居届(てんきょとどけ)を忘(わす)れずに。
Don't forget to submit a change of address notification after moving.
転居届(てんきょとどけ)は引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前後(ぜんご)十四日(じゅうよっか)以内(いない)に市区町村(しくちょうそん)役場(やくば)に提出(ていしゅつ)する。
The change of address notification must be submitted to the municipal office within fourteen days before or after moving.

COMPONENTS: 転居(てんきょ) (change of residence) + 届(とどけ) (notification/report).

When moving within the same municipality, you submit a 転居届(てんきょとどけ). When moving to a different municipality, the process involves a 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ) (moving-out notice) at the old address and a 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ) (moving-in notice) at the new address.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ) — moving-out notification
  • 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ) — moving-in notification
  • 住民票(じゅうみんひょう) — resident certificate
  • 市区町村(しくちょうそん)役場(やくば) — municipal office
