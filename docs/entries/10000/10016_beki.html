べき

べき
auxiliary
should, ought to
1. should, ought to, must
An auxiliary expression attached to the dictionary form of verbs to indicate obligation, duty, or strong recommendation. Often used for giving advice or stating what is expected.
約束(やくそく)は守(まも)るべきだ。
You should keep your promises.
もっと早(はや)く言(い)うべきだった。
I should have said it sooner.
Past tense: べきだった = should have
権力(けんりょく)を持(も)つ者(もの)は、その責任(せきにん)も負(お)うべきである。
Those who hold power should also bear the responsibility.

べき expresses obligation or strong recommendation, equivalent to "should" or "ought to."

CONJUGATION:

  • Verb dictionary form + べきだ: should do
  • する → すべき / するべき (both acceptable)
  • べきだった: should have done (past)
  • べきではない: should not

FORMALITY:

  • べきだ: standard
  • べきである: more formal, written
  • べきです: polite

USAGE NOTES:

  • Used for moral obligations, duties, or strong advice
  • Can sound preachy if overused
  • More formal than 〜たほうがいい

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜たほうがいい: should, had better (softer)
  • 〜なければならない: must, have to (stronger obligation)
  • 〜はずだ: should be, is supposed to (expectation)
