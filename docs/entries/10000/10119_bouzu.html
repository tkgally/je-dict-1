坊主(ぼうず)

ぼうず
noun
Buddhist monk; shaved head
1. Buddhist monk; priest
A Buddhist monk or priest, traditionally with a shaved head.
坊主(ぼうず)がお経(きょう)を読(よ)んでいる。
The monk is reciting a sutra.
2. shaved head; buzz cut
A closely shaved or very short hairstyle, resembling a monk's head.
頭(あたま)を剃(そ)って坊主(ぼうず)にした。
I shaved my head.
夏(なつ)は坊主(ぼうず)頭(あたま)が涼(すず)しい。
A shaved head is cool in summer.
3. boy; kid
Colloquial term for a young boy, often used affectionately.

USAGE: 坊主(ぼうず) has multiple meanings depending on context. The hairstyle meaning is very common in everyday conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 坊主(ぼうず)頭(あたま) (shaved head)
  • 坊主(ぼうず)にする (to shave one's head)
  • 三日(みっか)坊主(ぼうず) (someone who can't stick to anything - lit. 'three-day monk')

IDIOM:

  • 三日(みっか)坊主(ぼうず) - A person who gives up easily or lacks perseverance
