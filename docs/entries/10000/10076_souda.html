そうだ

そうだ
auxiliary
I heard that, it seems
1. I heard that, they say that
Expresses hearsay - information the speaker learned from another source. Attaches to the plain form of verbs/adjectives.
来月(らいげつ)新(あたら)しい店(みせ)ができるそうだ。
I heard a new store will open next month.
Hearsay usage
彼(かれ)は本当(ほんとう)に努力(どりょく)したそうだ。
I heard he really worked hard.
Hearsay usage
2. it looks like, it seems
Expresses appearance or conjecture based on direct observation. Attaches to the verb stem or adjective stem.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうだ。
It looks like it's going to rain.
Appearance usage - verb stem + そうだ
このケーキはおいしそうだ。
This cake looks delicious.
Appearance usage - adjective stem + そうだ

GRAMMATICAL DISTINCTION:

1. HEARSAY (〜そうだ):

  • Attaches to plain form: 行(い)くそうだ, 高(たか)いそうだ, 静(しず)かだそうだ
  • Meaning: "I heard that..."
  • Source: Information from others

2. APPEARANCE (〜そうだ):

  • Attaches to stem: 行(い)きそうだ, 高(たか)そうだ, 静(しず)かそうだ
  • Meaning: "It looks like..."
  • Source: Speaker's own observation

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 〜そうではない / 〜そうじゃない
  • Past: 〜そうだった

NOTE: Pay attention to what そうだ attaches to - this determines the meaning completely.
