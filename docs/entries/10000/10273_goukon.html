合(ごう)コン

ごうこん
noun
group blind date; mixer
1. group blind date, mixer
A social gathering where a group of men and a group of women meet, typically at a restaurant or bar, with the aim of finding romantic partners. Usually organized by one person from each side who knows each other.
今週(こんしゅう)合(ごう)コンがある。
There's a group date this week.
友達(ともだち)に合(ごう)コンに誘(さそ)われた。
A friend invited me to a group blind date.
大学生(だいがくせい)の頃(ころ)はよく合(ごう)コンに参加(さんか)していた。
When I was a college student, I often went to group dates.

Short for 合同(ごうどう)コンパ (joint drinking party). コンパ itself comes from English "company" or German "Kompanie."

Typically involves equal numbers of men and women (often 3-on-3 or 4-on-4) meeting at a restaurant or izakaya. Very common among university students and young working adults in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合(ごう)コンを開(ひら)く — to organize a group date
  • 合(ごう)コンに参加(さんか)する — to attend a group date
  • 合(ごう)コンに誘(さそ)う — to invite someone to a group date
  • 合(ごう)コンの幹事(かんじ) — the organizer of a group date
