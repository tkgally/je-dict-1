連(つ)れ子(こ)

つれこ
noun
stepchild, child from a previous marriage
1. stepchild, child from a previous marriage
A child that one's spouse brings into a new marriage from a previous relationship.
彼女(かのじょ)には連(つ)れ子(こ)がいる。
She has a child from a previous marriage.
連(つ)れ子(こ)も自分(じぶん)の子(こ)と同(おな)じように愛(あい)している。
I love my stepchild just the same as my own child.
再婚(さいこん)する時(とき)、お互(たが)いに連(つ)れ子(こ)がいた。
When we remarried, we each had children from previous marriages.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 連(つ)れる (to bring along) + 子(こ) (child) - literally 'a child brought along.'

RELATED TERMS:

  • 継子(ままこ): stepchild (alternative term, sometimes with negative connotations as in fairytales)
  • 義理(ぎり)の子(こ): stepchild (more formal)
  • 再婚(さいこん): remarriage

USAGE NOTE:
連(つ)れ子(こ) is a neutral, commonly used term in modern Japanese for discussing blended families.
