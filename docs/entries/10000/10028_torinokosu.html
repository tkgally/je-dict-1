取(と)り残(のこ)す

とりのこす
godan verb
to leave behind, to leave out
1. to leave behind, to abandon
To go ahead while leaving someone or something behind.
子供(こども)を取(と)り残(のこ)す。
To leave a child behind.
時代(じだい)に取(と)り残(のこ)される。
To be left behind by the times.
発展(はってん)から取(と)り残(のこ)された地域(ちいき)を支援(しえん)する。
To support regions that have been left behind by development.
2. to leave out, to exclude
To fail to include someone or something when others are included.
一人(ひとり)だけ取(と)り残(のこ)された。
I was the only one left out.
彼(かれ)の名前(なまえ)が取(と)り残(のこ)されている。
His name has been left out.
仲間(なかま)から取(と)り残(のこ)されたような気持(きも)ちになった。
I felt like I had been left out by my friends.

取(と)り残(のこ)す means to leave behind or leave out.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 取(と)り残(のこ)される (passive/intransitive sense)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時代(じだい)に取(と)り残(のこ)される: to be left behind by the times
  • 社会(しゃかい)から取(と)り残(のこ)される: to be excluded from society
  • 一人(ひとり)取(と)り残(のこ)される: to be left alone

NUANCE:
Often used in passive form (取(と)り残(のこ)される) to express feeling abandoned or excluded.
