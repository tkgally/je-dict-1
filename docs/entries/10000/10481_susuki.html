すすき

すすき
noun
pampas grass, Japanese silver grass
1. pampas grass, Japanese silver grass
A tall perennial grass native to Japan with silvery plumes. It is a symbol of autumn and is traditionally displayed during the moon-viewing festival (tsukimi).
すすきが風(かぜ)に揺(ゆ)れている。
The pampas grass is swaying in the wind.
月見(つきみ)にはすすきを飾(かざ)る。
We decorate with pampas grass for moon viewing.
秋(あき)の高原(こうげん)には一面(いちめん)のすすきが広(ひろ)がり、銀色(ぎんいろ)に輝(かがや)いていた。
The highland in autumn was covered with pampas grass, shining silver.

The kanji form is 薄(すすき) or 芒(すすき), but hiragana or katakana (ススキ) is more common.

CULTURAL NOTE:
すすき is one of the 秋(あき)の七草(ななくさ) (seven grasses of autumn) and is closely associated with 月見(つきみ) (moon viewing). It is traditionally displayed alongside 月見団子(つきみだんご) during the mid-autumn festival.

RELATED:
