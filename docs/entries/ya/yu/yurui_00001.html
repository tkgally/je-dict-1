緩(ゆる)い

ゆるい
i-adjective
loose; slack; lenient; gentle

Definitions

1. loose; slack
Not tight or firm, as of clothing, knots, or connections.
2. lenient; lax; permissive
Not strict in enforcement of rules or standards.
3. gentle; gradual
Not steep or sudden, as of a slope or curve.
4. slow; leisurely
Moving at a relaxed pace.

Examples

ベルトが緩(ゆる)いので、もう一(ひと)つ穴(あな)を締(し)めた。
The belt was loose, so I tightened it one more hole.
この会社(かいしゃ)は規則(きそく)が緩(ゆる)い。
This company has lax rules.
緩(ゆる)い坂(さか)を上(のぼ)った。
I went up a gentle slope.

Notes

緩(ゆる)い is an i-adjective with several related meanings centered on the concept of not being tight or strict.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 緩(ゆる)く
  • Noun form: 緩(ゆる)さ (looseness, laxness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ベルトが緩(ゆる)い (belt is loose)
  • 規則(きそく)が緩(ゆる)い (rules are lenient)
  • 緩(ゆる)い坂(さか) (gentle slope)
  • 緩(ゆる)い流(なが)れ (slow current)

ANTONYMS:

  • 厳(きび)しい: Strict, severe (for rules)
  • 急(きゅう): Steep, sudden (for slopes)
  • きつい: Tight (for fit)

COLLOQUIAL USAGE:
In casual speech, 緩(ゆる)い can describe a relaxed, easygoing atmosphere (ゆるい雰囲気(ふんいき)) or content that is not too serious (ゆるいコンテンツ).

Related Words

Antonym: 厳(きび)しい (strict, severe)
Related: 緩(ゆる)める (to loosen)
