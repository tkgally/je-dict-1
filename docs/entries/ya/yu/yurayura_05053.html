ゆらゆら

ゆらゆら
adverb, verb (suru)
swaying, wavering

Definitions

1. swaying, wavering
Describing a slow, gentle swinging or rocking motion.

Examples

炎(ほのお)がゆらゆら揺(ゆ)れている。
The flame is wavering.
船(ふね)がゆらゆらと揺(ゆ)れた。
The boat swayed gently.
水面(すいめん)にゆらゆら映(うつ)る影(かげ)。
Shadows wavering on the water's surface.

Notes

A mimetic word describing slow, rhythmic swaying motion. Used for flames, boats, reflections, and similar gentle movements. Related to 揺(ゆ)れる (to shake/sway).
