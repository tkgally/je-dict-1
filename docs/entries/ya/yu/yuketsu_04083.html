輸血(ゆけつ)

ゆけつ
noun, suru-verb
blood transfusion

Definitions

1. blood transfusion
The medical procedure of transferring blood or blood products from one person to another.

Examples

手術(しゅじゅつ)中(ちゅう)に輸血(ゆけつ)が必要(ひつよう)になった。
A blood transfusion became necessary during the surgery.
輸血(ゆけつ)のおかげで命(いのち)が助(たす)かった。
The blood transfusion saved my life.
彼女(かのじょ)は事故(じこ)の後(あと)、大量(たいりょう)の輸血(ゆけつ)を受(う)けた。
She received a large blood transfusion after the accident.

Notes

USAGE: 輸血(ゆけつ) is used both as a noun and with する as a suru-verb.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 輸血(ゆけつ)を受(う)ける (to receive a blood transfusion)
  • 輸血(ゆけつ)する (to give a blood transfusion)
  • 輸血(ゆけつ)が必要(ひつよう) (a transfusion is necessary)

RELATED MEDICAL TERMS:

  • 献血(けんけつ) - blood donation
  • 採血(さいけつ) - blood drawing, taking blood
  • 血液型(けつえきがた) - blood type
