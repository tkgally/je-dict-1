融資(ゆうし)

ゆうし
noun (verbal)
financing

Definitions

1. financing, loan
The provision of funds by a financial institution to a business or individual.

Examples

銀行(ぎんこう)から融資(ゆうし)を受(う)ける。
Receive financing from the bank.
新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)のために融資(ゆうし)を申(もう)し込(こ)んだ。
I applied for a loan for a new business.
融資(ゆうし)条件(じょうけん)を確認(かくにん)する。
Check the loan terms.

Notes

A formal term used in banking and business finance.

Common expressions:

  • 融資(ゆうし)を受(う)ける (to receive a loan)
  • 融資(ゆうし)を申(もう)し込(こ)む (to apply for a loan)
  • 融資(ゆうし)審査(しんさ) (loan screening)

Related Words

Related: 返済(へんさい) (repayment)
