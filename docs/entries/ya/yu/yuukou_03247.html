有効(ゆうこう)

ゆうこう
な-adjective
valid, effective

Definitions

1. valid, in effect
Legally or officially acceptable and usable.
2. effective, useful
Producing the intended or desired result.

Examples

このクーポンは来月(らいげつ)まで有効(ゆうこう)です。
This coupon is valid until next month.
時間(じかん)を有効(ゆうこう)に使(つか)いましょう。
Let's use our time effectively.
この方法(ほうほう)は有効(ゆうこう)だと思(おも)う。
I think this method is effective.

Notes

USAGE: 有効(ゆうこう) is used for both (1) validity of documents, tickets, etc., and (2) effectiveness of methods, measures, etc.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 有効(ゆうこう)期限(きげん) (expiration date)
  • 有効(ゆうこう)に使(つか)う (to use effectively)
  • 有効(ゆうこう)な手段(しゅだん) (effective means)
  • 有効(ゆうこう)活用(かつよう) (effective utilization)

CONTRAST:

  • 無効(むこう) - invalid, ineffective

NOTE: Do not confuse with 友好(ゆうこう) (friendship), which has the same reading.
