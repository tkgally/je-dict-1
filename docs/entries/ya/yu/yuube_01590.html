ゆうべ

ゆうべ
noun
last night, yesterday evening

Definitions

1. last night, yesterday evening
The night or evening of the previous day. Can also be written as 昨夜(ゆうべ).

Examples

ゆうべは何(なに)をしましたか。
What did you do last night?
ゆうべよく眠(ねむ)れなかった。
I couldn't sleep well last night.
ゆうべの映画(えいが)は面白(おもしろ)かった。
The movie last night was interesting.

Notes

Time word referring to the previous night. Usually written in hiragana but can also be 昨夜(ゆうべ). Note that 昨夜(さくや) is a more formal reading.

RELATED TIME WORDS:

  • 今夜(こんや) (tonight)
  • おととい (day before yesterday)
  • 昨日(きのう) (yesterday)
  • 明日(あした) (tomorrow)
  • あさって (day after tomorrow)
