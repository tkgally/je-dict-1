郵便受(ゆうびんう)け

ゆうびんうけ
noun
mailbox

Definitions

1. mailbox (receiving)
A box or slot where incoming mail is received at a residence.

Examples

郵便受(ゆうびんう)けに手紙(てがみ)が入(はい)っていた。
There was a letter in the mailbox.
郵便受(ゆうびんう)けを確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the mailbox.
郵便受(ゆうびんう)けがいっぱい(いっぱい)だった。
The mailbox was full.

Notes

Specifically for receiving mail (not for sending). In apartments, usually located in the entrance area. Also called ポスト (from 'post') in casual speech.

Related Words

Synonym: ポスト (colloquial)
