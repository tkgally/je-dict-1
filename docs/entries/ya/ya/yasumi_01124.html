休(やす)み

やすみ
noun
rest, break, vacation

Definitions

1. rest, break
A period of relaxation.
2. vacation, day off
Time off from work or school.

Examples

休(やす)みを取(と)る。
To take a break.
明日(あした)は休(やす)みです。
Tomorrow is my day off.

Notes

休(やす)み means rest or vacation.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 夏休(なつやす)み: summer vacation
  • 冬休(ふゆやす)み: winter vacation
  • 春休(はるやす)み: spring break
  • 昼休(ひるやす)み: lunch break

GREETING:
お休(やす)みなさい: good night
