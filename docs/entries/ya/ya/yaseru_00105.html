痩(や)せる

やせる
verb (ichidan)
to lose weight, to become thin

Definitions

1. to lose weight, to become thin
For a person or animal to become lighter or thinner.

Examples

5キロ痩(や)せた。
I lost 5 kilograms.
痩(や)せたいです。
I want to lose weight.
Common desire expression
彼女(かのじょ)は痩(や)せている。
She is thin.
State of being thin

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xが痩(や)せる (X loses weight).

ASPECT: ている indicates state. 痩(や)せている = 'is thin' (state of being thin, not the process).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 痩(や)せる (lose weight)
  • 痩(や)せている (is thin—state)
  • 痩(や)せたい (want to lose weight)
  • 痩(や)せすぎ (too thin)
  • ダイエットして痩(や)せる (lose weight by dieting)

OPPOSITE: 太(ふと)る (to gain weight)

NUANCE: 痩(や)せている can be positive (slim) or negative (too thin) depending on context. More polite alternatives:

  • スリム (slim—neutral/positive)
  • 細(ほそ)い (slender—often positive)

KANJI: Often written in hiragana as やせる in casual contexts.

Related Words

See also: 太(ふと)る
Antonym: 太(ふと)る (to gain weight)
