家主(やぬし)

やぬし
noun
landlord, homeowner

Definitions

1. landlord, property owner
A person who owns a house or apartment building and rents it out to tenants.
2. head of household
The person who owns and lives in a house (less common usage).

Examples

家主(やぬし)に家賃(やちん)を払(はら)う。
I pay rent to the landlord.
家主(やぬし)の許可(きょか)がないと改装(かいそう)できない。
You can't renovate without the landlord's permission.
家主(やぬし)さんがとても親切(しんせつ)な人(ひと)で助(たす)かった。
I was grateful that the landlord was a very kind person.

Notes

USAGE: 家主(やぬし) is the standard term for a landlord in rental situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家主(やぬし)に連絡(れんらく)する (to contact the landlord)
  • 家主(やぬし)の許可(きょか) (the landlord's permission)
  • 家主(やぬし)と借主(かりぬし) (landlord and tenant)

RELATED:

  • 大家(おおや) - landlord (more colloquial, often for apartment buildings)
  • 地主(じぬし) - landowner
  • 借主(かりぬし) - tenant, renter
