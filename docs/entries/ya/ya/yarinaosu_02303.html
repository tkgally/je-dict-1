やり直(なお)す

やりなおす
verb (godan)
to redo, to do over

Definitions

1. to redo, to do over again, to start again
To do something again, usually because the first attempt was unsatisfactory or incorrect.

Examples

間違(まちが)えたので、やり直(なお)します。
I made a mistake, so I'll do it over.
最初(さいしょ)からやり直(なお)そう。
Let's start over from the beginning.
人生(じんせい)をやり直(なお)したい。
I want to start my life over.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive).

COMPOUND VERB: やる (to do) + 直(なお)す (to fix, redo)

CONJUGATION (godan): やり直(なお)す → やり直(なお)さない → やり直(なお)した → やり直(なお)して

The 〜直(なお)す pattern indicates doing something over or redoing:

  • 書(か)き直(なお)す (rewrite)
  • 作(つく)り直(なお)す (remake)
  • 見直(みなお)す (review, look again)
  • 考(かんが)え直(なお)す (reconsider)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 最初(さいしょ)からやり直(なお)す (start over from the beginning)
  • もう一度(いちど)やり直(なお)す (do it over one more time)
