焼(や)く

やく
verb (godan)
to bake, to grill, to burn

Definitions

1. to bake, to grill, to roast
To cook food using dry heat, such as in an oven or on a grill.
2. to burn
To damage or destroy something with fire or heat.
3. to make (copies, CDs)
To create copies by burning data onto a disc.

Examples

パンを焼(や)く。
Bake bread.
肉(にく)を焼(や)いて食(た)べる。
Grill meat and eat it.
CDを焼(や)く。
Burn a CD.
Modern usage for copying data

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 焼(や)ける (intransitive, to burn/be grilled). Pattern: Xを焼(や)く (bake/grill/burn X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 焼(や)いている = 'is grilling/baking.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • パンを焼(や)く (bake bread)
  • 肉(にく)を焼(や)く (grill meat)
  • 魚(さかな)を焼(や)く (grill fish)
  • CDを焼(や)く (burn a CD)
  • 日焼(ひや)けする (get sunburned—related)

CONTRAST:

  • 焼(や)く (transitive): bake/burn X
  • 焼(や)ける (intransitive): X burns/is grilled

FOOD-RELATED TERMS:

  • 焼(や)き鳥(とり) (grilled chicken skewers)
  • 焼(や)き魚(ざかな) (grilled fish)
  • お好(この)み焼(や)き (Japanese savory pancake)
