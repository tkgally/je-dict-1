休(やす)める

やすめる
ichidan verb
to rest (something)

Definitions

1. to rest, to give rest to
To allow something to recover.

Examples

体(からだ)を休める。
To rest one's body.

Notes

休める is transitive.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 休める (transitive): to rest something
  • 休(やす)む (intransitive): to rest

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 羽(はね)を休める: to take a breather
