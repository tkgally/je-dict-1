雇(やと)う

やとう
godan verb
to employ, to hire

Definitions

1. to employ, to hire
To engage someone for work or services.

Examples

新(あたら)しい社員(しゃいん)を雇う。
To hire a new employee.
アルバイトを雇う。
To hire a part-timer.

Notes

雇う is a transitive verb.

RELATED:

  • 雇用(こよう): employment
  • 雇(やと)い主(ぬし): employer
  • 解雇(かいこ)する: to fire, dismiss

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 採用(さいよう)する: to hire (more formal)
