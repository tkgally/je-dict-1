易(やさ)しい／優(やさ)しい

やさしい
adjective (i-adjective)
easy; kind, gentle

Definitions

1. easy, simple
Not difficult to do or understand. Written as 易(やさ)しい. The opposite of 難(むずか)しい.
2. kind, gentle, tender
Having a mild or caring disposition. Written as 優(やさ)しい.

Examples

この本(ほん)は易(やさ)しい日本語(にほんご)で書(か)かれています。
This book is written in easy Japanese.
Sense 1: easy
彼女(かのじょ)はとても優(やさ)しい人(ひと)です。
She is a very kind person.
Sense 2: kind
優(やさ)しく教(おし)えてください。
Please teach me gently.
Adverb form: 優しく

Notes

Two different meanings with different kanji: 易(やさ)しい (easy) vs 優(やさ)しい (kind/gentle). Context usually makes the meaning clear. Both are i-adjectives with the same conjugation.
