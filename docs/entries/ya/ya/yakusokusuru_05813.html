約束(やくそく)する

やくそくする
verb (suru)
to promise

Definitions

1. to promise, to make a commitment
To give one's word to do or not do something.
2. to make an appointment, to arrange to meet
To agree on a time and place to meet someone.

Examples

秘密(ひみつ)を守(まも)ると約束(やくそく)した。
I promised to keep the secret.
友達(ともだち)と映画(えいが)を見(み)る約束(やくそく)をした。
I made plans to see a movie with my friend.
約束(やくそく)は守(まも)らなければならない。
Promises must be kept.

Notes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 約束(やくそく)を守(まも)る: to keep a promise
  • 約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)る: to break a promise
  • 約束(やくそく)の時間(じかん): the promised time, appointment time

USAGE:
In Japanese, 約束(やくそく) is used both for formal promises/commitments and casual appointments/plans to meet.
