破(やぶ)く

やぶく
verb (godan)
to tear; to rip

Definitions

1. to tear; to rip
To pull apart paper, cloth, or similar material.

Examples

紙(かみ)を破(やぶ)いて捨(す)てた。
I tore up the paper and threw it away.
Basic usage: tearing paper
手紙(てがみ)を破(やぶ)かないでください。
Please don't tear the letter.
Negative request form
子供(こども)が本(ほん)のページを破(やぶ)いてしまった。
The child accidentally tore a page of the book.
[Note: てしまった expresses regret]

Notes

破(やぶ)く is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 破(やぶ)れる (to tear, to be torn).

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form indicates ongoing action or a resulting state when combined with other verbs (紙(かみ)が破(やぶ)けている uses the intransitive = 'the paper is torn').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]を破(やぶ)く (tear ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紙(かみ)を破(やぶ)く (tear paper)
  • 手紙(てがみ)を破(やぶ)く (tear up a letter)
  • 袋(ふくろ)を破(やぶ)く (rip open a bag)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 破(やぶ)く vs 破(やぶ)る: Both mean 'to tear,' but 破(やぶ)る also extends to figurative uses like 'to break (a record)' or 'to defeat.' 破(やぶ)く is primarily for physical tearing.

Related Words

Pair verb: 破(やぶ)れる (intransitive)
Related: 破(やぶ)る (also 'to tear', plus figurative uses)
