焼肉(やきにく)

やきにく
noun
yakiniku

Definitions

1. yakiniku, grilled meat, Korean-style BBQ
A style of cooking where diners grill bite-sized pieces of meat (typically beef) at the table. Often served with various dipping sauces.

Examples

週末(しゅうまつ)に友達(ともだち)と焼肉(やきにく)を食(た)べに行(い)きました。
I went to eat yakiniku with friends on the weekend.
焼肉(やきにく)は自分(じぶん)で焼(や)きながら食(た)べます。
You grill yakiniku yourself while eating.
カルビとロースは焼肉(やきにく)の定番(ていばん)です。
Kalbi and roast are standard yakiniku cuts.

Notes

焼肉(やきにく) (literally "grilled meat") refers to the dining style where meat is grilled at the table.

Popular cuts:

  • カルビ (short ribs)
  • ロース (sirloin)
  • タン (tongue)
  • ハラミ (skirt steak)
  • ホルモン (offal/innards)

Serving style:

  • Meat is served raw and grilled on a 七輪(しちりん) (charcoal grill) or gas grill
  • Dipped in タレ (sweet soy-based sauce) or 塩(しお) (salt)
  • Often eaten with ライス(らいす) (rice) or 冷麺(れいめん) (cold noodles)

Often eaten for celebrations or group gatherings.

焼肉(やきにく)食(た)べ放題(ほうだい) (all-you-can-eat yakiniku) is popular.
