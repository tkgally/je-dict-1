山羊(やぎ)

やぎ
noun
goat

Definitions

1. goat
A domesticated animal with horns and a beard, related to sheep.

Examples

山羊(やぎ)が草(くさ)を食(た)べている。
The goat is eating grass.
山羊(やぎ)の乳(ちち)からチーズを作(つく)る。
Cheese is made from goat's milk.
山羊(やぎ)は高(たか)いところに登(のぼ)るのが得意(とくい)だ。
Goats are good at climbing high places.

Notes

山羊(やぎ) is distinct from 羊(ひつじ) (sheep).

RELATED TERMS:

  • ヤギ乳(にゅう): goat's milk
  • ヤギチーズ: goat cheese
  • 子(こ)ヤギ: kid (baby goat)

KANJI NOTE: The kanji 山羊 literally means 'mountain sheep.'

ZODIAC: The goat/sheep is the 8th animal in the Chinese zodiac (未(ひつじ)年(どし)), though in Japanese 羊 (sheep) is more commonly used.
