安(やす)い

やすい
adjective (i-adjective)
cheap, inexpensive

Definitions

1. cheap, inexpensive
Low in price, not costing much money. The opposite of 高(たか)い (expensive).

Examples

このカバンは安(やす)いです。
This bag is cheap.
もっと安(やす)いのはありますか。
Do you have anything cheaper?
安(やす)くておいしいレストランを知(し)っていますか。
Do you know a cheap and delicious restaurant?
安(やす)くて = cheap and (te-form)

Notes

安(やす)い only means cheap in price. Don't confuse with the suffix 〜やすい meaning 'easy to do' (食(た)べやすい = easy to eat), which is written without kanji. Conjugation: 安(やす)くない, 安(やす)かった, 安(やす)くて.
