山登(やまのぼ)り

やまのぼり
noun
mountain climbing, hiking

Definitions

1. mountain climbing, hiking, mountaineering
The activity of climbing or hiking up mountains for recreation or sport.

Examples

週末(しゅうまつ)に山登(やまのぼ)りに行(い)く。
I'm going mountain climbing this weekend.
山登(やまのぼ)りが趣味(しゅみ)です。
Mountain climbing is my hobby.
山登(やまのぼ)り用(よう)の靴(くつ)を買(か)った。
I bought shoes for mountain climbing.

Notes

COMPOUND: 山(やま) (mountain) + 登(のぼ)り (climbing)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 山登(やまのぼ)りに行(い)く (go mountain climbing)
  • 山登(やまのぼ)りをする (do mountain climbing)
  • 山登(やまのぼ)り用(よう)の〜 (~ for mountain climbing)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 登山(とざん) (mountaineering - more formal)
  • ハイキング (hiking - loanword)
  • トレッキング (trekking)
  • 山(やま) (mountain)

Japan has many popular mountains for climbing, including 富士山(ふじさん) (Mt. Fuji).

Related Words

Synonym: 登山(とざん) (formal)
