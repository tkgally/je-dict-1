要旨(ようし)

ようし
noun
gist, summary, essentials

Definitions

1. gist, summary, main point
The essential meaning or most important points of a text, speech, or argument.

Examples

論文(ろんぶん)の要旨(ようし)を書(か)いてください。
Please write a summary of the thesis.
会議(かいぎ)の要旨(ようし)をまとめた。
I summarized the gist of the meeting.
発言(はつげん)の要旨(ようし)は以下(いか)の通(とお)りです。
The summary of the statement is as follows.

Notes

USAGE: 要旨(ようし) is commonly used in academic and formal contexts for summaries.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 要旨(ようし)をまとめる (to summarize the main points)
  • 要旨(ようし)を説明(せつめい)する (to explain the gist)
  • 論文(ろんぶん)の要旨(ようし) (thesis abstract)

RELATED:

  • 概要(がいよう) - outline, summary (more general)
  • 要約(ようやく) - summary, abstract
  • 要点(ようてん) - main point, key point
