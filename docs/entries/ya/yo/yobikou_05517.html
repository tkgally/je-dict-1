予備校(よびこう)

よびこう
noun
prep school

Definitions

1. prep school, preparatory school (for university entrance exams)
See notes for detailed explanation.

Examples

大学(だいがく)受験(じゅけん)のために予備校(よびこう)に通(かよ)っている。
I am attending prep school for university entrance exams.
浪人(ろうにん)して予備校(よびこう)で一年(いちねん)勉強(べんきょう)した。
I took a gap year and studied at prep school for a year.
予備校(よびこう)の夏期(かき)講習(こうしゅう)に参加(さんか)する。
I will participate in the prep school's summer intensive course.

Notes

予備校(よびこう) is specifically for students preparing for university entrance exams, especially 浪人生(ろうにんせい) (gap year students who are retaking exams).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予備校(よびこう)に通(かよ)う (attend prep school)
  • 予備校(よびこう)に入(はい)る (enter prep school)
  • 予備校(よびこう)の模試(もし) (prep school mock exam)
  • 予備校(よびこう)講師(こうし) (prep school instructor)

CONTRAST WITH 塾(じゅく):

  • 塾(じゅく): For students of all ages, various subjects
  • 予備校(よびこう): Primarily for high school students and 浪人生(ろうにんせい) targeting university entrance exams

FAMOUS 予備校(よびこう):

  • 駿台(すんだい)予備校(よびこう)
  • 河合(かわい)塾(じゅく)
  • 代々木(よよぎ)ゼミナール
