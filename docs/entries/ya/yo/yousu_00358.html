様子(ようす)

ようす
noun
state, condition, appearance

Definitions

1. state, condition, appearance
The way something or someone looks or seems.
2. situation, circumstances
The state of affairs.

Examples

彼(かれ)の様子(ようす)がおかしい。
Something seems off about him.
様子(ようす)を見(み)てから決(き)めよう。
Let's decide after seeing how things go.

Notes

Common expression: 様子(ようす)を見(み)る (to wait and see, to observe the situation).
