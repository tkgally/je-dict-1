欲望(よくぼう)

よくぼう
noun
desire, lust

Definitions

1. desire, lust, craving
A strong, often intense desire or craving for something. Often implies a physical or selfish desire, and can have negative connotations.

Examples

人間(にんげん)は様々(さまざま)な欲望(よくぼう)を持(も)っている。
Humans have various desires.
欲望(よくぼう)を抑(おさ)えることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to control one's desires.
金銭(きんせん)への欲望(よくぼう)に駆(か)られる。
To be driven by the desire for money.

Notes

USAGE NOTES:

  • 欲望(よくぼう) often has a negative connotation, implying greed, lust, or selfish desire.
  • It is stronger and more primal than 願望(がんぼう) (wish/aspiration).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 欲望(よくぼう)を抑(おさ)える (to suppress desires)
  • 欲望(よくぼう)に駆(か)られる (to be driven by desire)
  • 欲望(よくぼう)を満(み)たす (to satisfy desires)
  • 肉体的(にくたいてき)欲望(よくぼう) (physical desire)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 願望(がんぼう) - aspiration, wish (more positive)
  • 欲求(よっきゅう) - desire, need (neutral/psychological term)

Related Words

Related: 願望(がんぼう) (wish, desire)
