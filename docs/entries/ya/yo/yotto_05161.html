ヨット

よっと
noun
yacht

Definitions

1. yacht, sailboat
A sailing vessel used for racing or pleasure cruising.

Examples

夏休(なつやす)みにヨットに乗(の)った。
I went sailing on a yacht during summer vacation.
湖(みずうみ)でヨットレースが開(ひら)かれた。
A yacht race was held on the lake.
彼(かれ)は大学(だいがく)でヨット部(ぶ)に入(はい)っている。
He is in the yacht club at university.

Notes

ヨット refers to a yacht or sailboat, borrowed from English 'yacht.' In Japanese, it often refers to smaller sailing vessels rather than large luxury yachts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ヨットに乗(の)る: to sail, to go yachting
  • ヨットレース: yacht race
  • ヨット部(ぶ): yacht club (school club)
  • ヨットハーバー: yacht harbor, marina

NOTE: For larger luxury yachts, 豪華(ごうか)ヨット or クルーザー may be used.
