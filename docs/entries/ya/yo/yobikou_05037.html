予備校(よびこう)

よびこう
noun
prep school

Definitions

1. prep school, cram school for university entrance
A school that prepares students for university entrance exams, often attended by students who failed or are preparing for exams.

Examples

予備校(よびこう)に通(かよ)っている。
I attend prep school.
浪人(ろうにん)して予備校(よびこう)に行(い)った。
I went to prep school after taking a gap year.
有名(ゆうめい)な予備校(よびこう)は競争(きょうそう)が激(はげ)しい。
Famous prep schools are very competitive.

Notes

Different from 塾(じゅく), which is for younger students. 予備校(よびこう) specifically targets university entrance exam preparation and is often attended by 浪人生(ろうにんせい) (students taking a gap year to study for exams).

Common expressions:

  • 予備校(よびこう)生(せい) (prep school student)
  • 予備校(よびこう)講師(こうし) (prep school instructor)

Related Words

Related: 塾(じゅく) (cram school)
