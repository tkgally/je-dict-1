喜(よろこ)び

よろこび
noun
joy, happiness

Definitions

1. joy, happiness, delight
A feeling of great pleasure and satisfaction.

Examples

合格(ごうかく)の喜(よろこ)びを分(わ)かち合(あ)った。
We shared the joy of passing the exam.
喜びを分かち合う = to share joy
子供(こども)の成長(せいちょう)を見(み)るのは喜(よろこ)びだ。
Watching my child grow is a joy.
喜(よろこ)びの涙(なみだ)を流(なが)した。
I shed tears of joy.
喜びの涙 = tears of joy

Notes

USAGE: 喜(よろこ)び is the noun form of the verb 喜(よろこ)ぶ (to be happy). It expresses positive emotions like joy and delight.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 喜(よろこ)びを感(かん)じる (to feel joy)
  • 喜(よろこ)びを分(わ)かち合(あ)う (to share joy)
  • 喜(よろこ)びの声(こえ) (cries of joy)
  • 喜(よろこ)びの涙(なみだ) (tears of joy)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しみ - enjoyment, pleasure
  • 幸(しあわ)せ - happiness
  • 歓喜(かんき) - great joy (formal)
