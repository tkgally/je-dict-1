嫁(よめ)

よめ
noun
bride, wife

Definitions

1. bride
A woman on her wedding day or about to be married.
2. wife, daughter-in-law
A married woman (from the perspective of her husband or his family).

Examples

息子(むすこ)の嫁(よめ)が来月(らいげつ)来(く)る。
My son's wife is coming next month.
Here 嫁 means daughter-in-law
嫁(よめ)に行(い)く。
To get married (said of a woman).
嫁に行く = to marry into another family
彼女(かのじょ)を嫁(よめ)にもらった。
I took her as my wife.
嫁にもらう = to take as a wife (traditional expression)

Notes

USAGE: 嫁(よめ) can mean (1) bride, (2) wife (casual/informal), or (3) daughter-in-law. The meaning depends on context and the speaker's perspective.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 嫁(よめ)に行(い)く (to get married - for women)
  • 嫁(よめ)にもらう (to take as a wife)
  • 嫁(よめ)さん (wife - casual/affectionate)
  • お嫁(よめ)さん (bride)

RELATED:

  • 花嫁(はなよめ) - bride (at wedding)
  • 婿(むこ) - groom, son-in-law
  • 妻(つま) - wife (formal)
