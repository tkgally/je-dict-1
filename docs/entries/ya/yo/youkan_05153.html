羊羹(ようかん)

ようかん
noun
yokan

Definitions

1. yokan, sweet bean jelly
A traditional Japanese confection made from red bean paste, agar, and sugar. Has a firm, jelly-like texture and is typically served in slices.

Examples

お茶(ちゃ)と羊羹(ようかん)をいただきました。
I had tea and yokan.
虎屋(とらや)の羊羹(ようかん)は有名(ゆうめい)です。
Toraya's yokan is famous.
羊羹(ようかん)は日持(ひも)ちがするのでお土産(みやげ)に良(よ)いです。
Yokan keeps well, so it's good as a souvenir.

Notes

羊羹(ようかん) is a classic Japanese confection.

Types:

  • 練(ね)り羊羹(ようかん) (firm yokan) - most common
  • 水(みず)羊羹(ようかん) (soft/water yokan) - summer treat
  • 蒸(む)し羊羹(ようかん) (steamed yokan)
  • 芋(いも)羊羹(ようかん) (sweet potato yokan)
  • 栗(くり)羊羹(ようかん) (chestnut yokan)

Ingredients:

  • 餡子(あんこ) (red bean paste)
  • 寒天(かんてん) (agar)
  • 砂糖(さとう) (sugar)

Characteristics:

  • Long shelf life (especially 練り羊羹(ようかん))
  • Dense, sweet, satisfying
  • Pairs well with green tea
  • Often a formal gift

Famous brand: 虎屋(とらや) (Toraya) - over 500 years old
