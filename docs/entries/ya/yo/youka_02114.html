八日(ようか)

ようか
noun
eight days; eighth day

Definitions

1. eight days
A duration of eight days.
2. the eighth (day of the month)
The eighth day of any month.

Examples

八日(ようか)かかりました。
It took eight days.
3月(がつ)八日(ようか)に届(とど)きます。
It will arrive on March 8th.
八日(ようか)間(かん)の旅行(りょこう)です。
It's an eight-day trip.

Notes

八日(ようか) means both "eight days" and "the 8th."
