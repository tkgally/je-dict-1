寄(よ)る

よる
godan verb
to approach, to stop by

Definitions

1. to approach, to come near
To move closer.
2. to stop by, to drop in
To visit briefly.

Examples

コンビニに寄る。
To stop by the convenience store.

Notes

寄る is intransitive.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 寄(よ)せる (transitive): to bring near
  • 寄る (intransitive): to approach
