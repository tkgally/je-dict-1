予備(よび)

よび
noun, の-adjective
reserve; spare; preliminary

Definitions

1. reserve; spare
Something kept in case of need; a backup or extra supply.
2. preliminary; preparatory
Done in preparation for something more important or final.

Examples

予備(よび)の電池(でんち)を持(も)っている。
I have spare batteries.
予備(よび)校(こう)に通(かよ)っている。
I am attending a prep school.
予備(よび)選挙(せんきょ)で候補者(こうほしゃ)が決(き)まる。
Candidates are decided in the primary election.

Notes

USAGE: Used as both a noun and の-adjective to indicate something extra, in reserve, or preliminary.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 予備(よび)校(こう) (prep school, cram school for university entrance)
  • 予備(よび)選挙(せんきょ) (primary election)
  • 予備(よび)軍(ぐん) (reserve forces)
  • 予備(よび)費(ひ) (reserve fund)
  • 予備(よび)知識(ちしき) (background knowledge)
  • 予備(よび)日(び) (alternative date)

USAGE PATTERN:

  • 予備(よび)の + noun (spare X, reserve X)
  • 予備(よび) + noun (preparatory X)
