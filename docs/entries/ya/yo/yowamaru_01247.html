弱(よわ)まる

よわまる
godan verb
to weaken

Definitions

1. to weaken, to diminish
To become less strong or intense.

Examples

風(かぜ)が弱まった。
The wind weakened.
勢(いきお)いが弱まる。
The momentum is weakening.

Notes

弱まる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 弱(よわ)める (transitive): to weaken something
  • 弱まる (intransitive): to become weak

SIMILAR:

  • 弱(よわ)る: to become weak (state)
