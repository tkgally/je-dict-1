四(よっ)つ

よっつ
noun / counter
four (things)

Definitions

1. four (things)
The native Japanese numeral for counting four items, used with the native counting system for general objects.
2. four years old
Used to express the age of four, as in a child being four years old.

Examples

りんごを四(よっ)つください。
Four apples, please.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に四(よっ)つのコップがある。
There are four glasses on the desk.
この子(こ)は四(よっ)つです。
This child is four years old.

Notes

四(よっ)つ is part of the native Japanese counting system (ひとつ、ふたつ、みっつ...) used for counting general objects.

NATIVE COUNTING SERIES:

  • 一(ひと)つ (1), 二(ふた)つ (2), 三(みっ)つ (3), 四(よっ)つ (4), 五(いつ)つ (5)
  • 六(むっ)つ (6), 七(なな)つ (7), 八(やっ)つ (8), 九(ここの)つ (9), 十(とお) (10)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Used when counting general objects without a specific counter
  • Also used for ages up to nine (informal)
  • 四(よっ)つ is safer than 四(し)つ since 四(し) sounds like 死(し) (death)
