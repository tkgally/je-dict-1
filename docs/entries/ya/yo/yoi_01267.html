良(よ)い

よい
i-adjective
good, fine

Definitions

1. good, fine
Of high quality or standard.
2. all right, okay
Acceptable or permitted.

Examples

天気(てんき)が良い。
The weather is good.
もう帰(かえ)ってもよい。
You may go home now.

Notes

良い has variant forms and complex conjugation.

FORMS:

  • よい/いい: both are used
  • いい is more colloquial
  • Conjugations use よ-: よくない, よかった

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜てもよい: may ~, it's okay to ~
  • 〜ばよかった: should have ~
