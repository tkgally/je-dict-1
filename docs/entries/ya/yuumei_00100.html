有名(ゆうめい)

ゆうめい
na-adjective
famous, well-known

Definitions

1. famous, well-known
Widely known by many people. Can describe people, places, or things.

Examples

彼(かれ)は有名(ゆうめい)な歌手(かしゅ)です。
He is a famous singer.
有名な + noun
この料理(りょうり)は有名(ゆうめい)ですね。
This dish is famous, isn't it?
日本(にほん)で一番(いちばん)有名(ゆうめい)な山(やま)は富士山(ふじさん)です。
The most famous mountain in Japan is Mt. Fuji.

Notes

有名 is a na-adjective: 有名な (before nouns), 有名だ/です (predicate), 有名じゃない (negative), 有名だった (past). The kanji literally mean 'have name' - something so notable it has a name. Antonym: 無名 (むめい - unknown, obscure).
