騎手(きしゅ)

きしゅ
noun
jockey; rider; horseback rider
1. jockey; rider; horseback rider
A person who rides a horse, especially a professional jockey in horse racing. Can also refer more broadly to any mounted rider.
騎手(きしゅ)が馬(うま)に乗(の)る。
The jockey mounts the horse.
若(わか)い騎手(きしゅ)が初(はつ)勝利(しょうり)を挙(あ)げた。
The young jockey achieved his first victory.
競馬(けいば)では馬(うま)の能力(のうりょく)だけでなく、騎手(きしゅ)の腕前(うでまえ)も勝敗(しょうはい)を大(おお)きく左右(さゆう)する。
In horse racing, not only the horse's ability but also the jockey's skill greatly influences the outcome.

A compound of 騎(き) (riding, mounted) and 手(しゅ) (person, hand). In modern usage, primarily refers to professional jockeys in 競馬(けいば) (horse racing). Japan has a well-established horse racing industry (JRA — Japan Racing Association), and famous jockeys are widely known public figures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 騎手(きしゅ)になる: to become a jockey
  • 騎手(きしゅ)が騎乗(きじょう)する: a jockey rides (formal)
  • 騎手(きしゅ)の腕前(うでまえ): the jockey's skill
  • リーディング騎手(きしゅ): leading jockey (most wins in a season)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ジョッキー: jockey — the loanword, used interchangeably in casual contexts
  • 騎馬(きば): mounted warrior, horseman — historical or military term
  • 乗(の)り手(て): rider — more general term for anyone who rides (horses, motorcycles, etc.)
