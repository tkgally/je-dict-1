図式(ずしき)

ずしき
noun
diagram; schema; framework
1. diagram; schematic; figure
A visual representation of information, relationships, or structures using lines, shapes, and labels.
図式(ずしき)を使(つか)って説明(せつめい)した。
I explained it using a diagram.
複雑(ふくざつ)な関係(かんけい)を図式(ずしき)で表(あらわ)した。
I represented the complex relationships in a diagram.
組織(そしき)の構造(こうぞう)を図式化(ずしきか)する。
To represent the organization's structure as a diagram.
2. schema; framework; pattern; dynamic
An abstract framework or pattern describing how things relate to each other, especially power dynamics, social structures, or conceptual relationships.
与党(よとう)と野党(やとう)の対立(たいりつ)という図式(ずしき)だ。
It's a framework of ruling party versus opposition.
強者(きょうしゃ)が弱者(じゃくしゃ)を支配(しはい)するという図式(ずしき)が崩(くず)れつつある。
The dynamic of the strong dominating the weak is beginning to break down.
単純(たんじゅん)な善悪(ぜんあく)の図式(ずしき)では説明(せつめい)できない問題(もんだい)だ。
It's a problem that cannot be explained by a simple good-versus-evil framework.

Compound of 図(ず) (diagram, figure) + 式(しき) (form, style). Used both for literal visual diagrams and for abstract conceptual frameworks.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 図式化(ずしきか)する: to schematize, to diagram
  • 対立(たいりつ)の図式(ずしき): a framework of confrontation
  • 単純(たんじゅん)な図式(ずしき): a simple schema
  • 図式(ずしき)が崩(くず)れる: the dynamic breaks down
  • 図式(ずしき)に当(あ)てはめる: to fit into a framework

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 図表(ずひょう): chart, diagram — more specifically refers to visual charts and tables
  • 構図(こうず): composition, structure — used for visual composition and power dynamics
  • 枠組(わくぐ)み: framework — more general term for conceptual structure
