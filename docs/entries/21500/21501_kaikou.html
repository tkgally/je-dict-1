邂逅(かいこう)

かいこう
noun
chance meeting; unexpected encounter
1. chance meeting; unexpected encounter; serendipitous meeting
An unplanned meeting with someone, especially one that feels meaningful or fateful. More literary and elevated than 偶然(ぐうぜん)の出会(であ)い.
運命(うんめい)の邂逅(かいこう)だった。
It was a fateful encounter.
旅先(たびさき)での邂逅(かいこう)が人生(じんせい)を変(か)えた。
A chance meeting during my travels changed my life.
二人(ふたり)の邂逅(かいこう)は、まるで小説(しょうせつ)のような出来事(できごと)だった。
Their chance encounter was like something out of a novel.

A literary word for an unexpected, often significant meeting. Uses two rare kanji (邂(かい) and 逅(こう)) that are seldom seen outside this compound. The word carries a sense of destiny or fate, suggesting the meeting was more than mere coincidence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 運命(うんめい)の邂逅(かいこう): fateful encounter
  • 偶然(ぐうぜん)の邂逅(かいこう): chance encounter
  • 邂逅(かいこう)を果(は)たす: to have an encounter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出会(であ)い: meeting, encounter — the everyday word for meeting someone; neutral in tone
  • 遭遇(そうぐう): encounter — often used for unexpected or unpleasant encounters
  • 巡(めぐ)り合(あ)い: fateful meeting — similar romantic/literary nuance but more commonly used

REGISTER:
Highly literary and formal. Common in novels, poetry, and dramatic narration. Rarely used in everyday conversation. Often appears in book titles and film descriptions.
