展示会(てんじかい)

てんじかい
noun
exhibition; trade show; display event
1. exhibition; trade show; display event
An organized event where products, artworks, or other items are displayed for viewing by the public or by industry professionals.
展示会(てんじかい)に行(い)く。
To go to an exhibition.
来週(らいしゅう)、東京(とうきょう)で自動車(じどうしゃ)の展示会(てんじかい)が開(ひら)かれる。
Next week, an automobile exhibition will be held in Tokyo.
この展示会(てんじかい)には毎年(まいとし)世界中(せかいじゅう)から多(おお)くの企業(きぎょう)が出展(しゅってん)している。
Many companies from around the world exhibit at this trade show every year.

A compound of 展示(てんじ) (display/exhibition) + 会(かい) (event/meeting). Refers to organized events where products, artworks, technology, etc. are put on display. Can range from art shows to large-scale trade fairs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 展示会(てんじかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold an exhibition
  • 展示会(てんじかい)に出展(しゅってん)する: to exhibit at a trade show
  • 展示会(てんじかい)会場(かいじょう): exhibition venue
  • 合同(ごうどう)展示会(てんじかい): joint exhibition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 展覧会(てんらんかい): exhibition — primarily for art and cultural works
  • 博覧会(はくらんかい): exposition/fair — larger scale, often international
  • 見本市(みほんいち): trade fair — specifically for commercial/industry displays
