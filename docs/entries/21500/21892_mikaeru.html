1. to look back; to turn around and look
To turn one's head or body to look at something behind or beside oneself.
声(こえ)をかけられて見返(みかえ)った。
Someone called out to me, and I turned around to look.
彼女(かのじょ)はちらっと見返(みかえ)って微笑(ほほえ)んだ。
She glanced back and smiled.
振(ふ)り返(かえ)って見返(みかえ)ると、誰(だれ)もいなかった。
When I turned around to look, no one was there.
2. to get back at; to prove oneself; to show them
To succeed or achieve something impressive in order to prove wrong those who doubted, looked down on, or criticized you.
バカにした人(ひと)たちを見返(みかえ)してやる。
I'll show those people who made fun of me.
努力(どりょく)して周(まわ)りを見返(みかえ)したい。
I want to work hard and prove everyone wrong.
失敗(しっぱい)を笑(わら)った人(ひと)たちを見返(みかえ)すために、必死(ひっし)に頑張(がんば)った。
I worked desperately to prove wrong those who laughed at my failure.

An ichidan verb with two distinct meanings. Sense 1 is a simple physical action. Sense 2 carries strong emotional motivation — the desire to vindicate oneself and prove doubters wrong.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～を見返(みかえ)してやる: I'll show them! / I'll prove them wrong!
  • 周(まわ)りを見返(みかえ)す: to prove everyone around you wrong
  • バカにした人(ひと)を見返(みかえ)す: to get back at people who looked down on you

SIMILAR WORDS (sense 1):

  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to turn around; to look back — more common for this physical meaning
  • 振(ふ)り向(む)く: to turn to face someone — implies facing toward

SIMILAR WORDS (sense 2):

  • 見返(みかえ)す: to look back at — can also mean to get revenge/prove oneself (similar)
  • 仕返(しかえ)しする: to get revenge — more direct retaliation
