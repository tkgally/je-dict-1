露(あらわ)に

あらわに
adverb
openly; plainly; without concealment
1. openly; plainly; without concealment; overtly
In a way that makes something visible or obvious without any attempt to hide it. Often used for emotions, attitudes, or physical exposure.
不満(ふまん)を露(あらわ)にする。
To openly show dissatisfaction.
彼(かれ)は怒(いか)りを露(あらわ)にした。
He openly displayed his anger.
記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で社長(しゃちょう)は苛立(いらだ)ちを露(あらわ)にし、厳(きび)しい口調(くちょう)で質問(しつもん)に答(こた)えた。
At the press conference, the president openly showed his irritation and answered questions in a harsh tone.

Written with the kanji 露(あらわ), meaning 'exposed' or 'bare.' The most common usage pattern is "emotion}を{露(あらわ)にする" (to openly show an emotion). The word implies that something normally kept hidden or controlled is being displayed without restraint.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 不満(ふまん)を露(あらわ)にする: to openly show dissatisfaction
  • 怒(いか)りを露(あらわ)にする: to openly display anger
  • 敵意(てきい)を露(あらわ)にする: to openly show hostility
  • 感情(かんじょう)を露(あらわ)にする: to openly express one's emotions
  • 本性(ほんしょう)を露(あらわ)にする: to reveal one's true nature

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あからさまに: blatantly, frankly — similar meaning but can also imply rudeness or tactlessness
  • 明(あき)らかに: clearly, obviously — states something as objectively evident, without the nuance of deliberate exposure
  • むき出(だ)しに: bare, exposed — more physical; often used for raw, unrefined expression

NOTE:
The kanji 露(あらわ) can also be read as つゆ (dew) in other contexts. In the adverbial use 露(あらわ)に, it always reads あらわに.
