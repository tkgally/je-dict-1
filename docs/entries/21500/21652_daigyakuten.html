大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)

だいぎゃくてん
noun
dramatic reversal; big comeback; major turnaround
1. dramatic reversal; big comeback; major turnaround
A sudden and dramatic change of situation, especially from a losing or disadvantaged position to a winning one. Used in sports, games, business, and life situations.
九回(きゅうかい)に大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)が起(お)きた。
A dramatic comeback happened in the ninth inning.
誰(だれ)もが諦(あきら)めかけたとき、大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)が起(お)こった。
Just when everyone was about to give up, a dramatic turnaround occurred.
後半(こうはん)で三点(さんてん)を取(と)って大逆転勝(だいぎゃくてんが)ちを収(おさ)めた。
They scored three goals in the second half and pulled off a dramatic come-from-behind victory.

Compound of 大(だい) (great, major) + 逆転(ぎゃくてん) (reversal). Intensified form of 逆転(ぎゃくてん), emphasizing the dramatic nature of the turnaround.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)が起(お)きる: a big comeback occurs
  • 大逆転勝(だいぎゃくてんが)ち: come-from-behind victory
  • 大逆転負(だいぎゃくてんま)け: a dramatic loss after leading
  • 大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)を果(は)たす: to achieve a major comeback
  • 大逆転(だいぎゃくてん)ホームラン: game-changing home run

USAGE:
Very common in sports broadcasting and commentary. Also used in business and everyday life to describe any situation where the outcome completely reverses expectations.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆転(ぎゃくてん): reversal — less dramatic intensity
  • 番狂(ばんくる)わせ: upset — an unexpected result, especially when a weaker opponent wins
