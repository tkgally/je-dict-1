1. yellow autumn leaves; leaves turning yellow in autumn
The phenomenon of tree leaves turning yellow in autumn, or the yellow leaves themselves. Distinguished from the more general kouyou (紅葉(こうよう), red leaves) by specifically referring to yellow coloration, as seen in ginkgo and birch trees.
イチョウの黄葉(こうよう)が美(うつく)しい。
The yellow autumn leaves of the ginkgo are beautiful.
黄葉(こうよう)した並木道(なみきみち)を散歩(さんぽ)する。
To take a walk along a tree-lined avenue with yellow autumn leaves.
秋(あき)になると、紅葉(こうよう)と黄葉(こうよう)が山(やま)を彩(いろど)り、多(おお)くの観光客(かんこうきゃく)が訪(おとず)れる。
In autumn, red and yellow leaves color the mountains, and many tourists visit.

Written with 黄(こう) (yellow) + 葉(よう) (leaf), in contrast to 紅葉(こうよう) which uses 紅(こう) (crimson). Both are read こうよう, and in everyday speech 紅葉(こうよう) is often used as a general term for all autumn foliage including yellow leaves. 黄葉(こうよう) is used when specifically distinguishing yellow coloration.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黄葉(こうよう)する: to turn yellow (of leaves)
  • 黄葉(こうよう)が見頃(みごろ): the yellow leaves are at their peak
  • イチョウの黄葉(こうよう): ginkgo yellow leaves
  • 紅葉(こうよう)と黄葉(こうよう): red and yellow autumn leaves

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 紅葉(こうよう): autumn leaves (red) — the general term for autumn foliage; homophone
  • 落葉(らくよう): fallen leaves — leaves that have already fallen from the tree
  • 新緑(しんりょく): fresh green leaves — the spring equivalent: new green foliage

NOTE:
The representative tree for 黄葉(こうよう) is the イチョウ (ginkgo), whose brilliant golden leaves are iconic in Japanese autumn scenery. The ginkgo-lined avenue at 明治神宮外苑(めいじじんぐうがいえん) in Tokyo is one of the most famous 黄葉(こうよう) spots in Japan.
