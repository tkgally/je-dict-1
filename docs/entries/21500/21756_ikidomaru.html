1. to come to a dead end; to reach a dead end
To reach a point where a road, path, or passage ends and one cannot go further.
この道(みち)は行(い)き止(ど)まる。
This road comes to a dead end.
細(ほそ)い路地(ろじ)を進(すす)んだら壁(かべ)の前(まえ)で行(い)き止(ど)まった。
I went down a narrow alley and came to a dead end at a wall.
カーナビの案内(あんない)に従(したが)ったのに、山道(やまみち)で行(い)き止(ど)まってしまった。
I followed the car navigation but ended up at a dead end on a mountain road.
2. to reach an impasse; to be stuck
Figuratively, to reach a point where no further progress can be made in a plan, investigation, or negotiation.
調査(ちょうさ)が行(い)き止(ど)まった。
The investigation hit a dead end.
交渉(こうしょう)は完全(かんぜん)に行(い)き止(ど)まっている。
The negotiations have completely stalled.
研究(けんきゅう)が行(い)き止(ど)まったとき、まったく別(べつ)の角度(かくど)からアプローチしてみた。
When the research reached an impasse, I tried approaching it from a completely different angle.

A compound verb from 行(い)く (to go) and 止(と)まる (to stop). The related noun 行(い)き止(ど)まり (dead end) is extremely common and appears on road signs throughout Japan.

USAGE:

  • Intransitive verb — the subject is the road, path, investigation, etc.
  • The noun form 行(い)き止(ど)まり is more common in everyday use than the verb
  • Often seen on road signs: 行(い)き止(ど)まり (Dead End)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)が行(い)き止(ど)まる: the road comes to a dead end
  • 捜査(そうさ)が行(い)き止(ど)まる: the investigation hits a dead end
  • 交渉(こうしょう)が行(い)き止(ど)まる: negotiations stall
  • 行(い)き止(ど)まりの道(みち): a dead-end road

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 行(い)き詰(づ)まる: to be stuck, to reach a deadlock — emphasizes being completely blocked with no way forward; stronger and more commonly figurative
  • 行(い)き止(ど)まり: dead end (noun) — the noun form, used on signs and in directions
  • 立(た)ち往生(おうじょう)する: to be stranded, to be stuck — unable to move (often vehicles or people)
