幻影(げんえい)

げんえい
noun
phantom; illusion; vision
1. phantom; illusion; apparition; vision
Something that appears to exist but is not real; a deceptive or imaginary image that the mind perceives.
幻影(げんえい)を見(み)た。
I saw a phantom.
過去(かこ)の幻影(げんえい)にとらわれている。
I am trapped by the illusions of the past.
砂漠(さばく)の蜃気楼(しんきろう)は旅人(たびびと)に水(みず)の幻影(げんえい)を見(み)せることがある。
Desert mirages can show travelers the illusion of water.

Composed of 幻(げん) (illusion, phantom) + 影(えい) (shadow, image). Refers to something that seems real but is actually a product of the imagination or deception of the senses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 幻影(げんえい)を見(み)る: to see a phantom/vision
  • 幻影(げんえい)にとらわれる: to be captivated by an illusion
  • 幻影(げんえい)を追(お)う: to chase an illusion
  • 過去(かこ)の幻影(げんえい): illusions/phantoms of the past

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 幻覚(げんかく): hallucination — a medical/psychological term for false sensory perception; 幻影(げんえい) is more literary
  • 幻想(げんそう): fantasy, illusion — broader, often about unrealistic ideas or dreams; 幻影(げんえい) specifically implies a visual image
  • 亡霊(ぼうれい): ghost — a supernatural being; 幻影(げんえい) is not necessarily supernatural

REGISTER:
Literary and somewhat formal. Common in novels, poetry, and figurative writing. Often used metaphorically for things like past glory, lost youth, or unrealistic hopes.
