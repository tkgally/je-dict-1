1. to recall; to reminisce; to recollect; to look back on
To deliberately look back on and reflect upon past events or experiences, often with a contemplative or nostalgic tone. Common in memoirs and narrative contexts.
学生時代(がくせいじだい)を回想(かいそう)する。
To reminisce about one's student days.
祖母(そぼ)は戦時中(せんじちゅう)の経験(けいけん)を回想(かいそう)して語(かた)った。
My grandmother recalled and spoke about her wartime experiences.
定年(ていねん)退職(たいしょく)の日(ひ)、長年(ながねん)の仕事(しごと)人生(じんせい)を回想(かいそう)しながら、感慨(かんがい)深(ぶか)い気持(きも)ちになった。
On the day of retirement, I felt deeply moved as I reminisced about my long working life.

Describes the act of looking back on past events or experiences in one's mind. Combines 回(かい) (turn, revolve) and 想(そう) (think, imagine) — literally "turning one's thoughts back."

USAGE:

  • Implies a deliberate or reflective act of remembering, not just a passing memory
  • Often used with longer-ago events: childhood, youth, wartime, career
  • Common in literary and narrative contexts, especially memoirs and autobiographies
  • The noun form 回想(かいそう) is also used: 回想(かいそう)シーン (flashback scene), 回想(かいそう)録(ろく) (memoirs)

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive (takes を for the thing recalled)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 過去(かこ)を回想(かいそう)する: to look back on the past
  • 回想(かいそう)にふける: to be lost in reminiscence
  • 回想(かいそう)シーン: flashback scene
  • 回想(かいそう)録(ろく): memoirs, recollections

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember, to recall — more everyday and spontaneous
  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to look back on — can be used for both past events and physical turning
  • 追想(ついそう): recollection, reminiscence — more literary; often tinged with nostalgia
  • 懐(なつ)かしむ: to feel nostalgic about — emphasizes the emotional aspect
