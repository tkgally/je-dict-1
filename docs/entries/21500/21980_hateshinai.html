1. boundless; endless; limitless; without end
Describes something that has no apparent end or limit, either literally (in physical extent) or figuratively (in duration, scope, or intensity).
果(は)てしない大地(だいち)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Boundless land stretches out.
果(は)てしない議論(ぎろん)が続(つづ)いている。
An endless debate continues.
宇宙(うちゅう)の果(は)てしない広(ひろ)がりを想像(そうぞう)すると、自分(じぶん)の小(ちい)ささを感(かん)じる。
When I imagine the boundless expanse of the universe, I feel how small I am.

Derived from 果(は)てる (to end, to be exhausted) + しない (not). Literally 'does not end.' Despite being formed from a verb, it functions as an i-adjective and modifies nouns directly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 果(は)てしない旅(たび): an endless journey
  • 果(は)てしない議論(ぎろん): an endless debate
  • 果(は)てしない努力(どりょく): endless effort
  • 果(は)てしない広(ひろ)がり: boundless expanse
  • 果(は)てしなく続(つづ)く: to continue endlessly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 終(お)わりのない: never-ending — more everyday and conversational; 果(は)てしない is more literary and evocative
  • 限(かぎ)りない: limitless, boundless — very similar; slightly more formal
  • 永遠(えいえん)の: eternal — focuses on time; 果(は)てしない can apply to space, time, or abstract concepts
  • 際限(さいげん)のない: unlimited — more formal; often used for quantities or processes
