A very common word for canceling or stopping something that was planned or in progress. Often used with になる in the passive-like construction 中止(ちゅうし)になる (to be canceled). Can refer to both permanent cancellation and temporary suspension.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中止(ちゅうし)になる: to be canceled
  • 中止(ちゅうし)を決定(けってい)する: to decide on cancellation
  • 一時(いちじ)中止(ちゅうし): temporary halt
  • 中止(ちゅうし)命令(めいれい): stop order, cease order
  • 開催(かいさい)中止(ちゅうし): cancellation of an event

USAGE:
中止(ちゅうし)する is transitive (someone cancels something), while 中止(ちゅうし)になる describes the result (something gets canceled). The になる form is more common in announcements and news.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • キャンセルする: to cancel — more casual; commonly used for reservations and personal plans
  • 延期(えんき)する: to postpone — not canceling but moving to a later date
  • 廃止(はいし)する: to abolish — permanently ending a system, practice, or regulation
  • 取(と)り消(け)す: to cancel, revoke — often used for official decisions, approvals, or orders
