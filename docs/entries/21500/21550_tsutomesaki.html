勤(つと)め先(さき)

つとめさき
noun
place of employment; workplace
1. place of employment; workplace; one's office or company
The place where someone works, referring to the company, organization, or institution that employs them.
勤(つと)め先(さき)はどちらですか。
Where do you work?
勤(つと)め先(さき)から家(いえ)まで一時間(いちじかん)かかる。
It takes one hour from my workplace to home.
新(あたら)しい勤(つと)め先(さき)は駅(えき)から近(ちか)くてとても便利(べんり)だ。
My new workplace is close to the station and very convenient.

Compound of 勤(つと)め (work, employment) and 先(さき) (place, destination). A common everyday word for referring to where someone is employed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勤(つと)め先(さき)の住所(じゅうしょ): workplace address
  • 勤(つと)め先(さき)の電話番号(でんわばんごう): workplace phone number
  • 勤(つと)め先(さき)を変(か)える: to change jobs
  • 勤(つと)め先(さき)に届(とど)ける: to notify one's employer

USAGE:
Common on official forms and documents where one must fill in employer information. In conversation, it is a polite and neutral way to ask about someone's workplace.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 職場(しょくば): workplace — emphasizes the physical environment where one works
  • 勤(つと)め先(さき): employer — emphasizes the company or organization
  • 会社(かいしゃ): company — specifically a business corporation
  • 勤務先(きんむさき): workplace — more formal synonym, common on official forms
