1. correspondence; exchanging letters; pen pal relationship
The practice of regularly exchanging letters with someone, traditionally by physical mail. Often associated with pen pal friendships, especially among students.
海外(かいがい)の友人(ゆうじん)と文通(ぶんつう)している。
I correspond with a friend overseas.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、文通(ぶんつう)相手(あいて)がいて楽(たの)しかった。
When I was a child, I had a pen pal and it was fun.
メールが普及(ふきゅう)する前(まえ)は、手紙(てがみ)での文通(ぶんつう)が異文化(いぶんか)交流(こうりゅう)の大切(たいせつ)な手段(しゅだん)だった。
Before email became widespread, exchanging letters was an important means of cross-cultural exchange.

The practice of exchanging letters with someone on a regular basis. Combines 文(ぶん) (writing, letter) and 通(つう) (pass through, communicate).

USAGE:

  • Traditionally refers to exchanging physical letters, especially as pen pals
  • Was very popular among Japanese students in the Showa and Heisei eras as a way to make friends
  • Used with する to form the verb: 文通(ぶんつう)する (to correspond, to exchange letters)
  • While email and messaging have largely replaced physical letter writing, the word is still understood and occasionally used

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文通(ぶんつう)する: to correspond by letter
  • 文通(ぶんつう)相手(あいて): pen pal, correspondent
  • 文通(ぶんつう)を始(はじ)める: to start corresponding
  • 文通(ぶんつう)を続(つづ)ける: to continue corresponding

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ペンパル: pen pal — the loanword, referring specifically to the person
  • 通信(つうしん): communication, correspondence — broader; includes all forms of communication
  • 書簡(しょかん): written correspondence, letters — more formal and literary
