独自性(どくじせい)

どくじせい
noun
originality; uniqueness; distinctiveness
1. originality; uniqueness; distinctiveness; individuality
The quality of being unique or distinctive, having characteristics that set something apart from others.
独自性(どくじせい)を打(う)ち出(だ)す。
To highlight one's originality.
この作品(さくひん)には独自性(どくじせい)がある。
This work has originality.
競合他社(きょうごうたしゃ)との差別化(さべつか)を図(はか)るために、製品(せいひん)の独自性(どくじせい)を強調(きょうちょう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
To differentiate from competitors, we need to emphasize the uniqueness of our product.

Composed of 独自(どくじ) (unique, original, one's own) + 性(せい) (nature, quality). Describes the abstract quality of being distinctive or original.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独自性(どくじせい)を発揮(はっき)する: to demonstrate originality
  • 独自性(どくじせい)を打(う)ち出(だ)す: to highlight uniqueness
  • 独自性(どくじせい)がある: to have originality
  • 独自性(どくじせい)に欠(か)ける: to lack originality
  • 独自性(どくじせい)を保(たも)つ: to maintain distinctiveness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • オリジナリティ: originality (loanword) — used similarly, especially in creative and marketing contexts
  • 個性(こせい): individuality — broader; refers to personal character traits; 独自性(どくじせい) focuses on being different from competitors or peers
  • 特色(とくしょく): distinctive feature — refers to a specific characteristic; 独自性(どくじせい) is the abstract quality of being unique

USAGE:
Commonly used in business, academic, and creative contexts when discussing what makes something stand out. Often appears in discussions about branding, product development, and artistic expression.
