そうした

そうした
pre-noun adjectival
such; that kind of; of that sort
1. such; that kind of; of that sort
Refers to something previously described or mentioned, modifying the following noun.
そうした考(かんが)え方(かた)もある。
There is also that kind of way of thinking.
そうした事情(じじょう)から、計画(けいかく)は中止(ちゅうし)になった。
Due to such circumstances, the plan was cancelled.
そうした経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねることで、人(ひと)は成長(せいちょう)していくのだと思(おも)う。
I think that by accumulating such experiences, people grow.

A pre-noun adjectival that refers to something previously mentioned, modifying the following noun. Derived from そう (like that) + した (past form of する). Functions like "that kind of" or "such" and refers to something slightly more removed from the speaker than こうした.

USAGE:

  • Always precedes a noun: そうした + noun
  • Refers to something previously mentioned in the conversation or text
  • Common in news reporting, essays, and formal discussion
  • Cannot be used as a predicate — only as a modifier

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • そうした中(なか)で: amid such circumstances
  • そうした意味(いみ)で: in that sense
  • そうした事情(じじょう): such circumstances
  • そうした背景(はいけい): against such a background

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • こうした: this kind of — refers to something closer or just demonstrated by the speaker
  • そのような: that kind of — more formal and explicit; interchangeable in most contexts
  • そういった: that sort of — slightly more casual than そうした
  • かかる: such — literary/legal; very formal equivalent
