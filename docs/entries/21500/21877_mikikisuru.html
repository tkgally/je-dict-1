1. to see and hear; to experience firsthand; to learn through observation
To gain knowledge or information by directly seeing and hearing things. Implies personal, firsthand experience rather than learning from books or hearsay.
旅(たび)で多(おお)くのことを見聞(みき)きした。
I saw and learned many things during the trip.
現地(げんち)で見聞(みき)きしたことを報告(ほうこく)する。
To report on what I saw and heard on-site.
若(わか)いうちに海外(かいがい)で見聞(みき)きを広(ひろ)げることは、将来(しょうらい)の糧(かて)になる。
Broadening one's horizons overseas while young will be an asset for the future.

Combines 見(み)る (to see) and 聞(き)く (to hear) into a compound verb meaning to experience through one's own senses. The noun form 見聞(けんぶん) (also read みきき) means 'knowledge gained through experience.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見聞(みき)きを広(ひろ)げる: to broaden one's horizons/experience
  • 見聞(みき)きしたこと: things one has seen and heard
  • 見聞(みき)きした話(はなし): stories from firsthand experience

RELATED TERMS:

  • 見聞(けんぶん): knowledge; experience (noun, on'yomi reading)
  • 見聞録(けんぶんろく): travel account; observations record
  • 体験(たいけん)する: to experience — broader, includes any personal experience
