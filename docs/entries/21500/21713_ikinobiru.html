1. to survive; to live through; to escape death
To continue living after experiencing a life-threatening situation such as war, disaster, or severe hardship, emphasizing that death was a real possibility.
奇跡(きせき)的(てき)に生(い)き延(の)びた。
I miraculously survived.
厳(きび)しい冬(ふゆ)を生(い)き延(の)びた動物(どうぶつ)たちが春(はる)に姿(すがた)を見(み)せた。
Animals that survived the harsh winter appeared in spring.
戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き延(の)びた祖父(そふ)は、平和(へいわ)の大切(たいせつ)さをいつも語(かた)っていた。
My grandfather, who survived the war, always spoke of the importance of peace.

A compound verb meaning to continue living through danger or hardship. Combines 生(い)きる (to live) and 延(の)びる (to extend, stretch). The nuance is surviving something that could have ended one's life.

USAGE:

  • Emphasizes having survived a dangerous or difficult situation — not simply "living a long time"
  • Often used with を to mark the hardship survived: 戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き延(の)びる (to survive the war)
  • Can also be used figuratively for organizations or businesses that survive difficult times

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 戦争(せんそう)を生(い)き延(の)びる: to survive the war
  • 災害(さいがい)を生(い)き延(の)びる: to survive a disaster
  • 奇跡(きせき)的(てき)に生(い)き延(の)びる: to survive miraculously
  • なんとか生(い)き延(の)びる: to barely survive

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive (self-moving)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生(い)き残(のこ)る: to survive, to remain alive — very close in meaning; slightly more emphasis on being among those who remain
  • 助(たす)かる: to be saved, to survive — implies being rescued or having a fortunate outcome
  • 耐(た)え抜(ぬ)く: to endure through — emphasizes the act of enduring rather than the outcome of surviving
