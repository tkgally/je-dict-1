1. to be disappointed; to be let down; to feel deflated
To feel a sudden drop in mood or spirits when something does not go as hoped. A common everyday word for expressing disappointment.
テストの点数(てんすう)にがっかりした。
I was disappointed by my test score.
楽(たの)しみにしていたのに、中止(ちゅうし)になってがっかりだ。
I was looking forward to it, but it got cancelled and I'm so let down.
がっかりしないで。次(つぎ)がんばろう。
Don't be disappointed. Let's try our best next time.
有名(ゆうめい)なレストランに行(い)ったが、味(あじ)は期待(きたい)はずれでがっかりした。
I went to a famous restaurant, but the food didn't live up to expectations and I was disappointed.

One of the most common everyday words for expressing disappointment. The onomatopoeic がっかり conveys the feeling of deflation or letdown. Can be used as an adverb (がっかりする), a na-adjective-like form (がっかりだ), or as a standalone exclamation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • がっかりする: to be disappointed
  • がっかりさせる: to disappoint (someone)
  • がっかりだ: what a letdown
  • がっかりした顔(かお): a disappointed face

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 失望(しつぼう)する: to be disappointed — more formal and literary; implies deeper, more serious disappointment
  • 落胆(らくたん)する: to be disheartened — written/formal register
  • 残念(ざんねん): regrettable — milder; used to express polite regret

USAGE:
The particle に marks the cause of disappointment: 結果(けっか)にがっかりする (to be disappointed by the result). Can also take を when used with a causative: 人(ひと)をがっかりさせる (to let someone down).
