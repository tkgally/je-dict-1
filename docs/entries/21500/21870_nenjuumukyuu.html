年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)

ねんじゅうむきゅう
noun
open year-round; no holidays; 365 days a year
1. open year-round; no holidays; 365 days a year
Describes a business or facility that operates every day of the year without regular closing days. Very commonly seen on signs at shops, restaurants, and convenience stores.
この店(みせ)は年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)です。
This store is open year-round.
年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)で営業(えいぎょう)しています。
We are open every day of the year.
コンビニは年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)で24時間(じかん)営業(えいぎょう)しているので、便利(べんり)だ。
Convenience stores are open year-round, 24 hours a day, so they're convenient.

A four-character compound meaning literally 'year-through no-rest': 年中(ねんじゅう) (throughout the year) + 無休(むきゅう) (no holidays/rest). Extremely common on shop signs and business information in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)で営業(えいぎょう): open for business year-round
  • 年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう)・24時間(じかん)営業(えいぎょう): open 365 days, 24 hours

RELATED TERMS:

  • 定休日(ていきゅうび): regular closing day — the opposite concept
  • 無休(むきゅう): no holidays/days off
  • 年中(ねんじゅう): throughout the year; all year long

CULTURAL NOTE:
This phrase is ubiquitous in Japan, where many convenience stores, family restaurants, and chain businesses operate every day of the year. It is often displayed prominently on storefronts.
