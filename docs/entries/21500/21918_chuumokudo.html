注目度(ちゅうもくど)

ちゅうもくど
noun
degree of attention; level of interest; prominence
1. degree of attention; level of interest; prominence
the extent to which something attracts public attention or interest; how noteworthy or prominent something is
この映画(えいが)は注目度(ちゅうもくど)が高(たか)い。
This movie is getting a lot of attention.
SNSでの注目度(ちゅうもくど)が急上昇(きゅうじょうしょう)した。
The level of attention on social media surged.
今年(ことし)の選挙(せんきょ)は過去(かこ)に比(くら)べて注目度(ちゅうもくど)が高(たか)く、投票率(とうひょうりつ)も上(あ)がった。
This year's election attracted more attention than in the past, and voter turnout also increased.

A compound noun frequently used in media, entertainment, and marketing to quantify or describe how much attention something is receiving. Often appears with 高(たか)い (high) or 低(ひく)い (low).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注目度(ちゅうもくど)が高(たか)い: to attract much attention
  • 注目度(ちゅうもくど)が低(ひく)い: to receive little attention
  • 注目度(ちゅうもくど)が上(あ)がる: attention increases
  • 注目度(ちゅうもくど)ランキング: attention/popularity ranking
  • 注目度(ちゅうもくど)ナンバーワン: number one in attention

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 人気(にんき): popularity — focuses on being liked, not just noticed
  • 話題性(わだいせい): topicality, buzz — the quality of being talked about
  • 知名度(ちめいど): name recognition — how widely known something or someone is

ETYMOLOGY:
Composed of 注目(ちゅうもく) (attention, notice) + 度(ど) (degree, level).
