防衛線(ぼうえいせん)

ぼうえいせん
noun
defensive line; line of defense
1. defensive line; line of defense
A line or position established for defense against attack. Used literally in military contexts and figuratively for any last stand or boundary one tries to protect.
防衛線(ぼうえいせん)を築(きず)く。
To build a defensive line.
最後(さいご)の防衛線(ぼうえいせん)が突破(とっぱ)された。
The last line of defense was breached.
価格(かかく)競争(きょうそう)で利益(りえき)の防衛線(ぼうえいせん)を守(まも)るのが難(むずか)しくなっている。
It has become difficult to hold the line on profit margins amid price competition.

A compound of 防衛(ぼうえい) (defense) and 線(せん) (line). Used both literally for military defense positions and figuratively for any boundary or standard one tries to maintain.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 防衛線(ぼうえいせん)を張(は)る: to set up a defensive line
  • 防衛線(ぼうえいせん)を突破(とっぱ)する: to break through a defensive line
  • 防衛線(ぼうえいせん)を守(まも)る: to hold a defensive line
  • 最終(さいしゅう)防衛線(ぼうえいせん): last line of defense

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 防御(ぼうぎょ)ライン: defense line — uses the loanword ライン
  • 前線(ぜんせん): front line — the forward-most position, not necessarily defensive
