1. to apply; to put into effect
To apply a rule, law, standard, or system to a specific case or situation.
新(あたら)しいルールを適用(てきよう)する。
To apply the new rules.
この割引(わりびき)は学生(がくせい)にも適用(てきよう)されますか。
Does this discount also apply to students?
改正(かいせい)された法律(ほうりつ)は来月(らいげつ)から全国(ぜんこく)の事業所(じぎょうしょ)に適用(てきよう)される予定(よてい)です。
The revised law is scheduled to be applied to workplaces nationwide starting next month.

Used when applying rules, laws, regulations, standards, or systems to specific cases or situations. It implies a formal, systematic process of putting something into effect.

USAGE:

  • Takes を for what is applied: ルールを適用(てきよう)する
  • Takes に for what it is applied to: ～に適用(てきよう)する
  • Often used in passive: ～が適用(てきよう)される (~ is applied)
  • Common in legal, administrative, and business contexts

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 法律(ほうりつ)を適用(てきよう)する: to apply a law
  • 規則(きそく)を適用(てきよう)する: to apply a regulation
  • 適用(てきよう)範囲(はんい): scope of application
  • 適用(てきよう)対象(たいしょう): subject of application
  • 適用(てきよう)外(がい): exempt from application
  • 適用(てきよう)開始(かいし): start of application

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 応用(おうよう)する: to apply (knowledge or theory) — used for applying learned concepts to practice, not rules or laws
  • 当(あ)てはめる: to apply, to fit — more casual; to apply a principle or formula to a specific case
  • 施行(しこう)する: to enforce, to implement — specifically putting a law or policy into effect
