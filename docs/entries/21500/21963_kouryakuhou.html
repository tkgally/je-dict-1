攻略法(こうりゃくほう)

こうりゃくほう
noun
strategy; walkthrough; method of conquering
1. strategy; method; walkthrough; tips for success
A method or set of strategies for successfully completing or conquering something, often used for games, tests, or challenges.
ゲームの攻略法(こうりゃくほう)を調(しら)べる。
To look up a game walkthrough.
試験(しけん)の攻略法(こうりゃくほう)を教(おし)えてもらった。
I was taught strategies for passing the exam.
ネットで人気(にんき)ラーメン店(てん)の攻略法(こうりゃくほう)がまとめられていて、並(なら)ばずに入(はい)れる時間帯(じかんたい)まで書(か)いてあった。
There was a guide online about how to conquer a popular ramen shop, even listing the times you can get in without waiting in line.

Composed of 攻略(こうりゃく) (conquest, capture) + 法(ほう) (method). Originally a military term, now widely used in casual and online contexts for tips, tricks, and strategies to succeed at something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゲームの攻略法(こうりゃくほう): game walkthrough/strategy
  • 試験(しけん)の攻略法(こうりゃくほう): exam strategies
  • 攻略法(こうりゃくほう)をまとめる: to compile strategies
  • 攻略法(こうりゃくほう)を紹介(しょうかい)する: to introduce strategies/tips

USAGE:
Very common in gaming culture and internet usage. Also used more broadly for any kind of challenge—exams, popular restaurants with long lines, job interviews, travel destinations, etc. The nuance is playful and practical rather than military.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 攻略本(こうりゃくぼん): strategy guidebook (for games)
  • 攻略(こうりゃく)する: to conquer; to beat (a game, challenge)
