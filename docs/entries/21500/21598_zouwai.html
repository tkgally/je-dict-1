1. bribery; the act of giving or offering a bribe
The criminal act of offering or giving money or gifts to a public official in exchange for favorable treatment. A legal term that specifically refers to the giver's side of a bribery transaction.
贈賄(ぞうわい)の容疑(ようぎ)で逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested on suspicion of bribery.
贈賄(ぞうわい)罪(ざい)は重(おも)い刑罰(けいばつ)が科(か)される。
The crime of bribery carries heavy penalties.
その企業(きぎょう)は公務員(こうむいん)への贈賄(ぞうわい)が発覚(はっかく)し、社会的(しゃかいてき)な信用(しんよう)を失(うしな)った。
The company's bribery of a public official was discovered, and it lost public trust.

Compound of 贈(ぞう) (give, present) and 賄(わい) (bribe). A legal term specifically for the act of giving a bribe, as opposed to 収賄(しゅうわい) (receiving a bribe). Both are crimes under Japanese law.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 贈賄(ぞうわい)罪(ざい): the crime of bribery (giving)
  • 贈賄(ぞうわい)の容疑(ようぎ): suspicion of bribery
  • 贈賄(ぞうわい)事件(じけん): bribery case
  • 贈賄(ぞうわい)で起訴(きそ)される: to be indicted for bribery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 収賄(しゅうわい): accepting a bribe — the receiver's crime, the other side of the transaction
  • 賄賂(わいろ): bribe — the thing given or received; the general word for bribery
  • 汚職(おしょく): corruption — a broader term covering all forms of official misconduct
  • 買収(ばいしゅう): buying off — bribing in a broader sense, not limited to public officials
