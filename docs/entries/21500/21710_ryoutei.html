料亭(りょうてい)

りょうてい
noun
high-class Japanese restaurant
1. high-class Japanese restaurant; traditional Japanese dining establishment
An upscale traditional Japanese restaurant featuring private tatami rooms, attentive service, and elaborate multi-course cuisine, typically used for business entertaining or special occasions.
有名(ゆうめい)な料亭(りょうてい)を予約(よやく)した。
I made a reservation at a famous high-class Japanese restaurant.
この料亭(りょうてい)は創業(そうぎょう)百年(ひゃくねん)の老舗(しにせ)です。
This restaurant is a long-established place with a hundred years of history.
接待(せったい)で京都(きょうと)の料亭(りょうてい)を利用(りよう)することになり、個室(こしつ)で懐石料理(かいせきりょうり)をいただいた。
We used a traditional restaurant in Kyoto for a business dinner and enjoyed kaiseki cuisine in a private room.

A traditional, high-end Japanese restaurant that serves elaborate Japanese cuisine, typically in private rooms with tatami seating. Combines 料(りょう) (food, cooking) and 亭(てい) (pavilion, establishment).

USAGE:

  • Distinguished from ordinary restaurants by the quality of ingredients, presentation, and service
  • Typically features private rooms (個室(こしつ)), tatami floors, and attentive service by staff in kimono
  • Often used for important occasions: business entertaining (接待(せったい)), celebrations, and formal gatherings
  • Prices are significantly higher than regular restaurants

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料亭(りょうてい)で食事(しょくじ)する: to dine at a high-class restaurant
  • 老舗(しにせ)の料亭(りょうてい): a long-established traditional restaurant
  • 料亭(りょうてい)の味(あじ): the taste of a fine restaurant
  • 料亭(りょうてい)風(ふう): in the style of a traditional restaurant

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • Historically, some 料亭(りょうてい) were venues where politicians held private meetings, leading to the expression 料亭(りょうてい)政治(せいじ) (backroom politics)
  • Kyoto is especially famous for its traditional 料亭(りょうてい)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • レストラン: restaurant — general term, usually Western-style
  • 割烹(かっぽう): Japanese cooking restaurant — slightly less formal than 料亭(りょうてい), often with counter seating
  • 懐石(かいせき): kaiseki — the style of multi-course cuisine often served at 料亭(りょうてい)
