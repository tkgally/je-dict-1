1. reassignment; reshuffling; redeployment; rearrangement
The act of moving personnel to different positions or departments within an organization, or rearranging the placement of objects.
人事(じんじ)の配置換(はいちが)えがあった。
There was a personnel reshuffling.
来月(らいげつ)から別(べつ)の部署(ぶしょ)に配置換(はいちが)えになる。
I'm being reassigned to a different department starting next month.
効率(こうりつ)を上(あ)げるために、オフィスの配置換(はいちが)えを行(おこな)うことにした。
We decided to rearrange the office layout to improve efficiency.

Composed of 配置(はいち) (arrangement, placement) + 換(か)え (changing, replacing). Most commonly refers to personnel transfers within an organization but can also mean physically rearranging items or spaces.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人事(じんじ)配置換(はいちが)え: personnel reshuffling
  • 配置換(はいちが)えになる: to be reassigned
  • 配置換(はいちが)えを行(おこな)う: to carry out a reshuffling
  • 配置換(はいちが)えを命(めい)じられる: to be ordered to transfer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 異動(いどう): transfer, reassignment — the standard HR term for job transfers; 配置換(はいちが)え emphasizes the rearrangement aspect
  • 転勤(てんきん): job transfer (to different location) — implies geographic relocation; 配置換(はいちが)え can be within the same office
  • 模様替(もようが)え: redecorating, rearranging — for rooms and spaces; 配置換(はいちが)え focuses on functional arrangement
