1. easy to eat; palatable; not hard to chew or swallow
Describing food that is convenient, comfortable, or simple to consume, whether due to size, texture, flavor, or preparation.
一口(ひとくち)サイズで食(た)べやすい。
It's bite-sized and easy to eat.
この魚(さかな)は骨(ほね)が少(すく)なくて食(た)べやすい。
This fish has few bones and is easy to eat.
子(こ)どもでも食(た)べやすいように、小(ちい)さく切(き)ってから出(だ)した。
I cut it into small pieces before serving so that even children could eat it easily.

Formed from the verb 食(た)べる (to eat) + やすい (easy to do). This is a productive grammatical pattern where verb stem + やすい creates an i-adjective meaning "easy to [verb]." The opposite pattern is verb stem + にくい (hard to do).

USAGE:

  • Conjugates as a regular i-adjective: 食(た)べやすくない (not easy to eat), 食(た)べやすかった (was easy to eat)
  • Can refer to size, texture, flavor mildness, or convenience of a food
  • The ～やすい pattern is extremely productive: 読(よ)みやすい (easy to read), 使(つか)いやすい (easy to use), 分(わ)かりやすい (easy to understand)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食(た)べやすい大(おお)きさ: an easy-to-eat size
  • 食(た)べやすい味(あじ): a mild, palatable flavor
  • 食(た)べやすく切(き)る: to cut into easy-to-eat pieces
  • お年寄(としよ)りにも食(た)べやすい: easy to eat even for elderly people

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(た)べにくい: hard to eat — the opposite; food that is difficult to consume
  • 口当(くちあ)たりがいい: pleasant to the palate — focuses on flavor and texture
  • 手軽(てがる)に食(た)べられる: can be eaten easily/casually — focuses on convenience
