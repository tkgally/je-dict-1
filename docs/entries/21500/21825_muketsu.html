無欠(むけつ)

むけつ
noun
flawless; perfect; without defect
1. flawless; perfect; without defect; complete
Having no flaws, defects, or shortcomings. Most commonly encountered in the four-character compound 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ) (absolute perfection).
完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ)の計画(けいかく)はない。
There is no such thing as a flawless plan.
彼(かれ)は完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ)の人間(にんげん)ではない。
He is not a flawless human being.
完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ)に見(み)えた彼女(かのじょ)のキャリアにも、実(じつ)は多(おお)くの苦労(くろう)があった。
Behind her career that appeared absolutely flawless, there were actually many struggles.

A compound of 無(む) (without, lacking) and 欠(けつ) (defect, lack). Almost always used in the four-character compound 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ) (kanzen muketsu), meaning 'absolute perfection' or 'flawless.' On its own, 無欠(むけつ) is rarely used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ): absolutely flawless, perfect in every way
  • 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ)な人(ひと): a flawless person
  • 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ)の作品(さくひん): a flawless work

USAGE NOTE:
The phrase 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ) is often used with irony or in the negative, since true perfection is considered impossible. Saying something is 完全(かんぜん)無欠(むけつ) can sound overly idealized.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完璧(かんぺき): perfect, flawless — the most common everyday word for 'perfect'
  • 申(もう)し分(ぶん)ない: flawless, beyond reproach — a polite expression meaning there is nothing to criticize
  • 非(ひ)の打(う)ちどころがない: beyond reproach — an idiomatic expression meaning there are no faults to point out
