1. to feel one's heart pounding from nervousness or anxiety
Describes the physical sensation of a racing heartbeat caused by nervousness, fear, or anxiety.
面接(めんせつ)でドキドキした。
My heart was pounding during the interview.
発表(はっぴょう)の前(まえ)はいつもドキドキする。
I always get nervous before a presentation.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)を待(ま)っている間(あいだ)、ドキドキして落(お)ち着(つ)かなかった。
While waiting for the exam results, I was so nervous I couldn't settle down.
2. to feel one's heart pounding from excitement or anticipation
Describes the thrill or flutter of excitement, especially in romantic or thrilling situations.
彼(かれ)に会(あ)うとドキドキする。
My heart races when I see him.
初(はじ)めてのデートでドキドキした。
My heart was fluttering on my first date.
遊園地(ゆうえんち)のジェットコースターに乗(の)る前(まえ)、子供(こども)たちはドキドキしていた。
The children were excited before riding the roller coaster at the amusement park.

One of the most common Japanese onomatopoeia, mimicking the sound of a heartbeat. Written in katakana (ドキドキ) to emphasize the onomatopoeic quality, though hiragana (どきどき) is also used.

FORMS:

  • ドキドキする: verb form (to feel one's heart pounding)
  • ドキドキの: modifying a noun (heart-pounding ~)
  • ドキドキして: te-form (with a pounding heart)
  • 胸(むね)がドキドキする: explicit form (one's chest pounds)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 胸(むね)がドキドキする: one's heart pounds
  • ドキドキが止(と)まらない: one's heart won't stop racing
  • ドキドキ感(かん): thrill, sense of excitement
  • ドキドキワクワク: nervous and excited (combined with ワクワク)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ワクワクする: excited anticipation — focused on positive excitement without the nervousness
  • ハラハラする: anxious, on edge — watching something tense unfold, worried about the outcome
  • ソワソワする: restless, fidgety — unable to sit still due to anticipation or anxiety
