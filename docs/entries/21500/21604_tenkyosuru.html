1. to move; to change one's residence
To relocate from one home to another. A formal word for moving house, commonly used in official and written contexts.
来月(らいげつ)転居(てんきょ)します。
I'm moving next month.
仕事(しごと)の都合(つごう)で大阪(おおさか)に転居(てんきょ)した。
I moved to Osaka for work reasons.
転居(てんきょ)したら、二週間(にしゅうかん)以内(いない)に届(とど)け出(で)を出(だ)してください。
After moving, please submit the notification within two weeks.

A formal word for changing one's residence, frequently seen on official forms and in administrative contexts. Less casual than 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転居(てんきょ)届(とどけ): change-of-address notification
  • 転居(てんきょ)先(さき): new address, destination of the move
  • 転居(てんきょ)通知(つうち): moving notification (sent to contacts)
  • 転居(てんきょ)はがき: change-of-address postcard

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す: to move (house) — everyday, conversational
  • 移転(いてん)する: to relocate — used for offices or businesses rather than personal homes
  • 転(てん)じる: to move/transfer — broader, literary

REGISTER:
More formal than 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す. Used on 役所(やくしょ) forms, in official correspondence, and in written announcements. In casual conversation, 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す is preferred.
