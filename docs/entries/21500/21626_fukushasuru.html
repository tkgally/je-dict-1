1. to copy; to duplicate; to reproduce
To make a copy of a document, image, or other material. A formal term for copying or reproduction.
書類(しょるい)を複写(ふくしゃ)する。
To copy a document.
この資料(しりょう)は複写(ふくしゃ)禁止(きんし)です。
Copying of this material is prohibited.
原本(げんぽん)を複写(ふくしゃ)して各部署(かくぶしょ)に配(くば)った。
I copied the original and distributed it to each department.

A formal term for copying or reproducing documents and materials. Often seen in legal and administrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 複写(ふくしゃ)禁止(きんし): copying prohibited
  • 複写(ふくしゃ)機(き): copier, copying machine
  • 複写(ふくしゃ)用紙(ようし): copy paper, carbon paper
  • 無断(むだん)複写(ふくしゃ): unauthorized copying
  • 複写(ふくしゃ)権(けん): reproduction rights

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コピーする: to copy — everyday loanword, most common in conversation
  • 複製(ふくせい)する: to reproduce, to replicate — also formal, broader (includes manufacturing)
  • 写(うつ)す: to copy by hand — implies manual transcription
  • 模写(もしゃ)する: to copy (artwork) — specifically for art reproduction

REGISTER:
More formal than コピーする. Commonly found on copyright notices, library signs, and official documents. In everyday speech, コピーする is preferred.
