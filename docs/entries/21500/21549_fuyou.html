1. support; maintenance; providing for dependents
The act of financially supporting family members who cannot support themselves, such as children, elderly parents, or a non-working spouse. Commonly used in legal, tax, and administrative contexts.
子供(こども)を扶養(ふよう)する。
To support one's children.
扶養(ふよう)家族(かぞく)は何人(なんにん)いますか。
How many dependents do you have?
妻(つま)は夫(おっと)の扶養(ふよう)に入(はい)っているので、健康(けんこう)保険料(ほけんりょう)を払(はら)わなくてよい。
Since the wife is registered as a dependent of her husband, she doesn't have to pay health insurance premiums.

Composed of 扶(ふ) (help, assist) and 養(よう) (nurture, support). A key term in Japanese tax and social insurance systems.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 扶養(ふよう)家族(かぞく): dependents, family members being supported
  • 扶養(ふよう)控除(こうじょ): dependent deduction (tax term)
  • 扶養(ふよう)に入(はい)る: to be registered as a dependent
  • 扶養(ふよう)手当(てあて): dependent allowance
  • 扶養(ふよう)義務(ぎむ): obligation to support

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 養育(よういく): raising, rearing — focuses on bringing up children
  • 扶養(ふよう): support — financial maintenance of dependents, administrative/legal term
  • 不要(ふよう): unnecessary — homophone, completely different meaning

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, the 扶養(ふよう) system is an important part of the tax and social insurance framework. A spouse earning below a certain income threshold (the so-called "income wall") can be registered as a dependent, receiving health insurance coverage and a tax deduction for the supporting household member.
