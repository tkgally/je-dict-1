水面下(すいめんか)

すいめんか
noun
below the surface; behind the scenes
1. below the water surface; underwater
Literally refers to the area beneath the surface of water.
氷山(ひょうざん)の大部分(だいぶぶん)は水面下(すいめんか)にある。
Most of an iceberg is below the water surface.
水面下(すいめんか)の温度(おんど)は一年中(いちねんじゅう)ほぼ一定(いってい)だ。
The temperature below the water surface is nearly constant year-round.
潜水艦(せんすいかん)は水面下(すいめんか)を静(しず)かに移動(いどう)していた。
The submarine was moving quietly below the surface.
2. behind the scenes; below the surface; covertly
Figuratively describes activities, negotiations, or movements that are hidden from public view.
水面下(すいめんか)で交渉(こうしょう)が進(すす)んでいる。
Negotiations are proceeding behind the scenes.
両国(りょうこく)の水面下(すいめんか)での接触(せっしょく)が明(あき)らかになった。
Secret contacts between the two countries came to light.
表向(おもてむ)きは平静(へいせい)を保(たも)っていたが、水面下(すいめんか)では激(はげ)しい権力闘争(けんりょくとうそう)が繰(く)り広(ひろ)げられていた。
On the surface they maintained calm, but behind the scenes a fierce power struggle was unfolding.

Composed of 水面(すいめん) (water surface) + 下(か) (below). The literal meaning refers to what is physically underwater, while the figurative meaning describes hidden activities—particularly negotiations, deals, or conflicts that are not visible to the public.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水面下(すいめんか)で交渉(こうしょう)する: to negotiate behind the scenes
  • 水面下(すいめんか)での動(うご)き: behind-the-scenes movements
  • 水面下(すいめんか)で進(すす)む: to proceed covertly
  • 水面下(すいめんか)での接触(せっしょく): secret contacts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裏(うら)で: behind the scenes — more casual and everyday; 水面下(すいめんか) sounds more formal and is often used in news/political contexts
  • 陰(かげ)で: in secret — implies concealment, sometimes with a negative nuance

USAGE:
The figurative sense is very common in news reporting about politics, diplomacy, and business. It suggests deliberate concealment from the public eye, often with the implication that important things are happening out of sight.
