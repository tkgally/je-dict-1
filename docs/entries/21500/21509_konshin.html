懇親(こんしん)

こんしん
noun
friendly relations; fellowship; cordiality
1. friendly relations; fellowship; cordiality; getting to know each other
Warm, cordial interaction aimed at building personal connections. Most commonly seen in compound forms like 懇親会(こんしんかい) (social gathering).
懇親会(こんしんかい)に参加(さんか)する。
To attend a social gathering.
懇親(こんしん)を深(ふか)めるために食事会(しょくじかい)を開(ひら)いた。
We held a dinner party to deepen our friendly relations.
学会(がっかい)の後(あと)、参加者(さんかしゃ)どうしの懇親(こんしん)を図(はか)る場(ば)が設(もう)けられた。
After the academic conference, an opportunity was set up for attendees to build friendly connections.

Most frequently encountered in the compound 懇親会(こんしんかい) (social gathering, mixer), which is a standard event in Japanese business, academic, and organizational life. These gatherings typically involve food and drinks and are designed for networking and relationship-building.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懇親会(こんしんかい): social gathering, mixer, reception
  • 懇親(こんしん)を深(ふか)める: to deepen friendly relations
  • 懇親(こんしん)を図(はか)る: to promote fellowship
  • 懇親(こんしん)パーティー: friendship party

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 親睦(しんぼく): friendship, amity — very similar meaning; often interchangeable (親睦会(しんぼくかい) is nearly synonymous with 懇親会(こんしんかい))
  • 交流(こうりゅう): exchange, interaction — broader and more neutral; covers cultural, academic, and international exchanges

REGISTER:
Formal. Used in business, academic, and organizational contexts. Not typically used in casual conversation.
