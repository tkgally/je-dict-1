1. to keep falling; to continue to fall; to rain/snow continuously
Used for precipitation (rain, snow, etc.) that continues without stopping. A compound verb formed from 降る (to fall) + 続く (to continue).
雨(あめ)が三日間(みっかかん)降(ふ)り続(つづ)いた。
It rained continuously for three days.
雪(ゆき)が朝(あさ)から降(ふ)り続(つづ)いている。
It has been snowing since morning.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)は雨(あめ)が何日(なんにち)も降(ふ)り続(つづ)くので、洗濯物(せんたくもの)が乾(かわ)かない。
During the rainy season, it rains for days on end, so the laundry won't dry.

A compound verb formed from 降(ふ)る (to fall — precipitation) + 続(つづ)く (to continue). Used exclusively for precipitation — rain, snow, hail, etc. — falling without interruption.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り続(つづ)く: rain continues to fall
  • 雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)り続(つづ)く: snow continues to fall
  • 何日(なんにち)も降(ふ)り続(つづ)く: to keep falling for days

GRAMMAR:
Conjugates as a godan verb (く row): 降(ふ)り続(つづ)かない、降(ふ)り続(つづ)いて、etc. The transitive counterpart 降(ふ)り続(つづ)ける (to keep letting fall) is not normally used, as precipitation is not a volitional action.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 降(ふ)り止(や)まない: to not stop falling — emphasizes the lack of cessation
  • 降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ: to pour down — emphasizes intensity rather than duration
