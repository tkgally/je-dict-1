1. to return to one's country; to go back to one's home country
To travel back to one's country of origin or nationality, especially after a period of living, studying, or working abroad.
来月(らいげつ)帰国(きこく)する予定(よてい)です。
I plan to return to my country next month.
留学(りゅうがく)を終(お)えて、三年(さんねん)ぶりに帰国(きこく)した。
After finishing my studies abroad, I returned home for the first time in three years.
彼(かれ)は海外(かいがい)赴任(ふにん)を終(お)えて帰国(きこく)したが、日本(にほん)の生活(せいかつ)に慣(な)れるまで時間(じかん)がかかった。
He returned to Japan after finishing his overseas assignment, but it took time to readjust to life here.

Combines 帰(き) (return) and 国(こく) (country). Specifically refers to returning to one's own country, not just any country. The noun form 帰国(きこく) is also commonly used on its own.

USAGE:

  • The subject's "own country" is determined by nationality or where they are from
  • For Japanese speakers, 帰国(きこく) almost always means returning to Japan
  • 帰国(きこく) can also function as a noun: 帰国(きこく)の予定(よてい) (plans to return home)
  • 帰国子女(きこくしじょ) refers to children who grew up abroad and returned to Japan — a well-known cultural concept

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一時(いちじ)帰国(きこく): temporary return home
  • 帰国(きこく)予定(よてい): plans to return home
  • 帰国(きこく)子女(しじょ): returnee children
  • 帰国(きこく)後(ご): after returning home
  • 無事(ぶじ)に帰国(きこく)する: to return home safely

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 帰(かえ)る: to return, go home — general term; can mean returning to any home base
  • 渡航(とこう)する: to travel overseas — going abroad, the opposite direction
  • 入国(にゅうこく)する: to enter a country — broader; refers to entering any country, not specifically one's own
  • 里帰(さとがえ)り: returning to one's hometown — specifically visiting one's hometown or parents' home, not necessarily from abroad
