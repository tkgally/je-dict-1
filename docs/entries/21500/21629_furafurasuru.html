1. to feel dizzy; to be unsteady; to stagger
Describes a physical state of dizziness, lightheadedness, or unsteadiness, often caused by illness, exhaustion, or heat.
熱(ねつ)でふらふらする。
I feel dizzy from the fever.
立(た)ち上(あ)がったら、ふらふらした。
When I stood up, I felt lightheaded.
朝(あさ)ごはんを食(た)べなかったので、午後(ごご)にはふらふらしていた。
Because I skipped breakfast, I was feeling dizzy by the afternoon.
2. to wander aimlessly; to drift about without purpose
Describes someone moving about without a clear destination or purpose, or being indecisive about commitments.
休日(きゅうじつ)は街(まち)をふらふらする。
On days off, I wander around town.
仕事(しごと)もしないでふらふらしている。
He's just drifting around without working.
卒業(そつぎょう)してから何年(なんねん)もふらふらして、やっと就職(しゅうしょく)した。
After graduating, he drifted around for years before finally getting a job.

An onomatopoeic word describing unsteady, swaying movement. Can refer to either physical unsteadiness (dizziness, staggering) or a figurative lack of direction in life.

FORMS:

  • ふらふらする: verb form
  • ふらふらの: modifying a noun (unsteady ~)
  • ふらふらになる: to become dizzy/unsteady
  • ふらふらと歩(ある)く: to walk unsteadily

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 足元(あしもと)がふらふらする: to be unsteady on one's feet
  • ふらふらになる: to become exhausted/dizzy
  • ふらふらと出(で)かける: to wander out without a plan
  • ふらふら遊(あそ)ぶ: to idle around having fun

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ふらっと: a single, brief wandering (ふらっと立(た)ち寄(よ)る, to drop by casually)
  • よろよろ: staggering weakly, tottering — emphasizes physical weakness more than ふらふら
  • ぶらぶらする: to stroll leisurely, to loaf — more relaxed, less negative than ふらふら in sense 2
