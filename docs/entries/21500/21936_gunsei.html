1. growing in clusters; colony; gregarious growth
Plants or organisms growing together in groups or clusters in one area. Can also be used as a suru verb meaning to grow in clusters.
野原(のはら)に花(はな)が群生(ぐんせい)している。
Flowers are growing in clusters in the field.
群生(ぐんせい)する竹(たけ)の林(はやし)は美(うつく)しい景色(けしき)だ。
A grove of bamboo growing in clusters is a beautiful sight.
この地域(ちいき)では珍(めずら)しいキノコが群生(ぐんせい)しているのが発見(はっけん)された。
Rare mushrooms were discovered growing in clusters in this area.

A compound of 群(ぐん) (group, flock) and 生(せい) (growth, life). Describes plants, fungi, or other organisms growing together naturally in groups.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 群生(ぐんせい)する: to grow in clusters
  • 群生(ぐんせい)地(ち): habitat where plants grow in clusters
  • 群生(ぐんせい)が見(み)られる: clusters can be observed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 密生(みっせい): dense growth — emphasizes how tightly packed the growth is
  • 自生(じせい): growing wild — emphasizes that plants grow naturally without cultivation
  • 群落(ぐんらく): plant community — a more technical ecological term

NOTE:
Often used in nature descriptions, hiking guides, and botanical contexts. Seasonal flowers growing in clusters (群生(ぐんせい)) are popular tourist attractions in Japan.
