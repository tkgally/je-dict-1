1. to take medicine; to administer orally
To take medicine by mouth. A formal word used in medical and pharmaceutical contexts for oral medication.
薬(くすり)を服用(ふくよう)する。
To take medicine.
食後(しょくご)に一錠(いちじょう)服用(ふくよう)してください。
Please take one tablet after meals.
この薬(くすり)は長期(ちょうき)服用(ふくよう)すると副作用(ふくさよう)が出(で)ることがある。
Long-term use of this medicine can cause side effects.

A formal, medical term for taking medication orally. Found on medicine packaging, in doctor's instructions, and in pharmaceutical literature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 服用(ふくよう)量(りょう): dosage
  • 服用(ふくよう)方法(ほうほう): how to take (the medicine)
  • 服用(ふくよう)期間(きかん): period of administration
  • 長期(ちょうき)服用(ふくよう): long-term use
  • 食前(しょくぜん)/食後(しょくご)に服用(ふくよう): take before/after meals

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲(の)む: to drink/take (medicine) — everyday, conversational
  • 投与(とうよ)する: to administer (medicine) — from the doctor/nurse's perspective
  • 摂取(せっしゅ)する: to ingest — broader, includes food and nutrients

REGISTER:
More formal than 飲(の)む. In daily conversation, people say 薬(くすり)を飲(の)む rather than 服用(ふくよう)する. 服用(ふくよう) appears on prescription labels, medicine inserts, and in medical instructions.
