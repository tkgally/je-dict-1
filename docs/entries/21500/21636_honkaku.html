本格(ほんかく)

ほんかく
noun
full-scale; authentic; genuine
1. full-scale; earnest; serious
Describes something done in a thorough, comprehensive way rather than casually or partially. Often used for activities that shift from preliminary to full commitment.
本格(ほんかく)的(てき)な調査(ちょうさ)が始(はじ)まった。
A full-scale investigation has begun.
来月(らいげつ)から本格(ほんかく)的(てき)に練習(れんしゅう)を始(はじ)める。
I'll start training in earnest from next month.
梅雨(つゆ)が本格(ほんかく)的(てき)になってきた。
The rainy season has started in earnest.
2. authentic; genuine; the real thing
Describes something that meets the true standards of its category — not an imitation or simplified version.
本格(ほんかく)的(てき)なイタリア料理(りょうり)のレストラン。
A restaurant with authentic Italian cuisine.
これは本格(ほんかく)的(てき)なコーヒーだ。
This is genuine, high-quality coffee.
本格(ほんかく)派(は)のミステリー小説(しょうせつ)が好(す)きだ。
I like orthodox mystery novels.

Almost always used in compound form as 本格(ほんかく)的(てき) (na-adjective) or 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)に (adverb). Rarely used as a standalone noun in modern Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)な調査(ちょうさ): a full-scale investigation
  • 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)に始(はじ)まる: to begin in earnest
  • 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)な味(あじ): authentic flavor
  • 本格(ほんかく)派(は): orthodox school/faction (especially in literary genres)
  • 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)な冬(ふゆ): the onset of real winter

USAGE:
本格(ほんかく)的(てき)に often marks a transition from a preliminary or casual phase to the real, serious phase. For example, 本格(ほんかく)的(てき)に勉強(べんきょう)する implies moving beyond dabbling to committed study.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正式(せいしき): official, formal — emphasizes procedural correctness rather than thoroughness
  • 正真正銘(しょうしんしょうめい): genuine, authentic — stronger emphasis on being the real thing, often correcting a misconception
