本年(ほんねん)

ほんねん
noun
this year; the current year
1. this year; the current year
A formal way to say 'this year.' Used in official documents, business correspondence, speeches, and formal greetings.
本年(ほんねん)もよろしくお願(ねが)いいたします。
I look forward to your continued support this year.
本年度(ほんねんど)の予算(よさん)が承認(しょうにん)された。
This fiscal year's budget was approved.
本年(ほんねん)は創業(そうぎょう)50周年(しゅうねん)を迎(むか)える記念(きねん)すべき年(とし)でございます。
This year is a memorable year as we celebrate our 50th anniversary.

A formal equivalent of 今年(ことし) (this year). Used exclusively in formal writing and speech, such as business letters, official reports, New Year's greetings, and public announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本年(ほんねん)もよろしくお願(ねが)いいたします: standard New Year greeting in business
  • 本年度(ほんねんど): this fiscal/academic year
  • 本年中(ほんねんちゅう): during this year
  • 本年(ほんねん)は: as for this year (opening phrase in speeches)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 今年(ことし): this year — the standard, everyday word; used in all registers
  • 昨年(さくねん): last year — the formal equivalent of 去年(きょねん)
  • 来年(らいねん): next year — used in both formal and casual registers

REGISTER:
Highly formal. Using 本年(ほんねん) in casual conversation would sound stiff and unnatural. In everyday speech, always use 今年(ことし) instead.
