予約済(よやくず)み

よやくずみ
noun
reserved; already booked; reservation confirmed
1. reserved; already booked; reservation confirmed
Indicates that a reservation or booking has been completed.
この席(せき)は予約済(よやくず)みです。
This seat is reserved.
ホテルは予約済(よやくず)みなので、安心(あんしん)してください。
The hotel is already booked, so don't worry.
予約済(よやくず)みの確認(かくにん)メールが届(とど)いたら、当日(とうじつ)は受付(うけつけ)で名前(なまえ)を伝(つた)えるだけでいい。
Once you receive the reservation confirmation email, on the day itself you just need to give your name at reception.

Composed of 予約(よやく) (reservation) and 済(す)み (completed, done — the noun form of 済(す)む). The ～済(す)み suffix is a productive pattern meaning "already done" or "completed," and attaches to many nouns and verb stems.

USAGE:

  • Can function as a noun or a no-adjective: 予約済(よやくず)みの席(せき) (a reserved seat)
  • Often seen on signs, labels, and in written notifications
  • The ～済(す)み pattern is very productive: 支払(しはら)い済(す)み (paid), 確認(かくにん)済(す)み (confirmed), 登録(とうろく)済(す)み (registered)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予約済(よやくず)みの席(せき): a reserved seat
  • 予約済(よやくず)みの部屋(へや): a booked room
  • 予約済(よやくず)みの確認(かくにん): reservation confirmation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予約(よやく)完了(かんりょう): reservation completed — more formal; used in system messages
  • 確保(かくほ)済(す)み: secured — broader; used for resources, seats, or items that have been secured
  • 手配(てはい)済(す)み: arranged — implies the arrangement or procurement has been completed
