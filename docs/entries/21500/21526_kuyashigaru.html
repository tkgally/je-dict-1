1. to show frustration; to be visibly upset; to express vexation
To outwardly display feelings of frustration, regret, or bitterness, especially after a loss or missed opportunity. Formed from 悔(くや)しい (frustrating) + the suffix ～がる (to show signs of).
負(ま)けて悔(くや)しがった。
They were visibly frustrated at losing.
子(こ)どもが悔(くや)しがって泣(な)いている。
The child is crying out of frustration.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けた選手(せんしゅ)が悔(くや)しがる姿(すがた)に胸(むね)を打(う)たれた。
I was moved by the sight of the player showing frustration after the loss.

Formed from 悔(くや)しい (frustrating, vexing) + the suffix ～がる, which converts an adjective describing internal feelings into a verb describing the outward display of those feelings.

THE ～がる PATTERN:

Japanese い-adjectives describing emotions (like 悔(くや)しい, 嬉(うれ)しい, 怖(こわ)い) directly describe the speaker's own feelings. To describe someone else showing those feelings, add ～がる:

  • 悔(くや)しい → 悔(くや)しがる: to show frustration
  • 嬉(うれ)しい → 嬉(うれ)しがる: to show happiness
  • 怖(こわ)い → 怖(こわ)がる: to show fear
  • 寂(さび)しい → 寂(さび)しがる: to show loneliness

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 負(ま)けて悔(くや)しがる: to be frustrated at losing
  • ひどく悔(くや)しがる: to be deeply frustrated
  • 悔(くや)しがる様子(ようす): a frustrated demeanor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悔(くや)しい: frustrating — the base adjective describing the feeling itself
  • 悔(く)やむ: to regret — focuses on remorse over a past action
  • 残念(ざんねん)がる: to express disappointment — milder and less bitter
