1. coloring; painting; application of color
The act of applying color to something, or the colors and color scheme of a finished work. Used for art, crafts, photography, and decorative work.
仏像(ぶつぞう)に彩色(さいしょく)を施(ほどこ)す。
To apply coloring to a Buddhist statue.
鮮(あざ)やかな彩色(さいしょく)が目(め)を引(ひ)く。
The vivid coloring catches the eye.
この寺(てら)の天井(てんじょう)画(が)は何百年(なんびゃくねん)も前(まえ)の彩色(さいしょく)がまだ残(のこ)っている。
The coloring of this temple's ceiling paintings still remains from hundreds of years ago.

A compound of 彩(さい) (color, coloring) and 色(しょく) (color). Often used in contexts involving traditional arts, crafts, architecture, and restoration. The word carries a more refined, artistic nuance than simply 色(いろ)を塗(ぬ)る (to paint a color).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 彩色(さいしょく)を施(ほどこ)す: to apply coloring
  • 彩色(さいしょく)する: to color, to paint
  • 鮮(あざ)やかな彩色(さいしょく): vivid coloring
  • 彩色(さいしょく)写真(しゃしん): hand-colored photograph
  • 彩色(さいしょく)木彫(もくちょう): painted wood carving

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着色(ちゃくしょく): coloring, dyeing — more technical, used for food coloring or industrial processes
  • 配色(はいしょく): color arrangement, color scheme — focuses on the combination and placement of colors
  • 塗装(とそう): painting, coating — applying paint as a protective or decorative layer, often for buildings or vehicles

NOTE:
The alternate reading 彩色(さいしき) also exists and appears in some older or literary texts. In modern standard usage, さいしょく is more common.
