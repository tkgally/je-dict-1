フィルター

ふぃるたー
noun
filter

A loanword from English "filter." Used both for physical filters (air, water, coffee) and digital/metaphorical filtering (email, data, perception). Can be used with する to mean "to filter" or with にかける to mean "to pass through a filter."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フィルターを通(とお)す: to pass through a filter
  • フィルターにかける: to filter, to put through a filter
  • フィルターを交換(こうかん)する: to replace a filter
  • 浄水(じょうすい)フィルター: water purification filter
  • 色(いろ)フィルター: color filter

USAGE:
The expression フィルターをかける or フィルターを通(とお)して見(み)る is used figuratively to mean viewing something through a biased or selective lens.
