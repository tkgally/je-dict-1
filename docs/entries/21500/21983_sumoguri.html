1. free diving; skin diving; diving without breathing apparatus
The practice of diving underwater without scuba gear or other breathing equipment, relying only on holding one's breath.
素潜(すもぐ)りで貝(かい)を取(と)る。
To collect shellfish by free diving.
素潜(すもぐ)りで海底(かいてい)まで潜(もぐ)った。
I dove to the ocean floor by free diving.
海女(あま)さんたちは素潜(すもぐ)りで海(うみ)に潜(もぐ)り、鮑(あわび)やサザエを採(と)ってきた。
The ama divers went into the sea by free diving and harvested abalone and turban shells.

Composed of 素(す) (plain, bare, without accessories) + 潜(もぐ)り (diving). The 素(す) prefix indicates doing something in its simplest, unequipped form — here, diving without any breathing apparatus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 素潜(すもぐ)りで潜(もぐ)る: to dive by free diving
  • 素潜(すもぐ)りで魚(さかな)を捕(つか)まえる: to catch fish by free diving
  • 素潜(すもぐ)りの漁(りょう): fishing by free diving
  • 素潜(すもぐ)りをする: to do free diving

CULTURAL NOTE:
Free diving has a long tradition in Japan, particularly through the 海女(あま) (female pearl divers and shellfish gatherers) of regions like Mie Prefecture and Shima Peninsula. These divers have practiced 素潜(すもぐ)り for over 2,000 years.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スキューバダイビング: scuba diving — uses breathing equipment; the opposite approach to 素潜(すもぐ)り
  • シュノーケリング: snorkeling — uses a snorkel at the surface; 素潜(すもぐ)り involves going fully underwater
  • 潜水(せんすい): diving — a general term for going underwater
