振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)

ふりこみてすうりょう
noun
bank transfer fee; remittance charge
1. bank transfer fee; remittance charge
The fee charged by a bank or financial institution for processing a money transfer. A common everyday banking term in Japan, where bank transfers are a standard payment method.
振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)がかかる。
There is a transfer fee.
振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)は無料(むりょう)ですか。
Is the transfer fee free?
ネットバンキングを使(つか)えば振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)が安(やす)くなることが多(おお)い。
If you use online banking, the transfer fee is often cheaper.

Compound of 振込(ふりこみ) (bank transfer) and 手数料(てすうりょう) (handling fee). In Japan, bank transfers (振込(ふりこみ)) are one of the most common payment methods for rent, utilities, and business transactions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)がかかる: a transfer fee is charged
  • 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)が無料(むりょう): the transfer fee is free
  • 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)を負担(ふたん)する: to bear the transfer fee
  • 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう)込(こ)み: transfer fee included

USAGE NOTE:
Transfer fees vary depending on the bank, whether the transfer is within the same bank or to a different bank, and whether it is done at an ATM, at a counter, or online. Many Japanese banks offer lower 振込(ふりこみ)手数料(てすうりょう) for online transactions.
