1. shared use; communal use; common use
Using something together with others; facilities, equipment, or spaces available for everyone's use. Often used as a noun modifier with の.
このトイレは共用(きょうよう)です。
This restroom is shared.
共用(きょうよう)スペースは清潔(せいけつ)に使(つか)いましょう。
Let's keep the shared space clean.
マンションの共用(きょうよう)部分(ぶぶん)の修繕費(しゅうぜんひ)は住民(じゅうみん)全員(ぜんいん)で負担(ふたん)する。
Repair costs for the communal areas of the apartment building are shared by all residents.

Compound of 共(きょう) (together, mutual) + 用(よう) (use). Very common in real estate, building management, and workplace contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 共用(きょうよう)スペース: shared space
  • 共用(きょうよう)部分(ぶぶん): communal areas (of a building)
  • 共用(きょうよう)設備(せつび): shared facilities
  • 共用(きょうよう)トイレ: shared restroom
  • 男女(だんじょ)共用(きょうよう): unisex, for both men and women

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 共有(きょうゆう): shared ownership — emphasizes joint possession rather than joint use
  • 公共(こうきょう): public — broader, refers to things belonging to or for the general public
  • 兼用(けんよう): dual-purpose — one item serving multiple functions

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:
共用(きょうよう) (shared use) is distinct from 教養(きょうよう) (education, culture) and 強要(きょうよう) (coercion). Context usually makes the meaning clear.
