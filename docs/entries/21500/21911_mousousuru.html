1. to indulge in unrealistic fantasies or daydreams
to indulge in unrealistic fantasies or daydreams; to imagine scenarios that are unlikely or impossible
宝(たから)くじに当(あ)たったら何(なに)をするか妄想(もうそう)する。
I fantasize about what I would do if I won the lottery.
授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)に妄想(もうそう)にふけってしまった。
I got lost in fantasies during class.
あの人(ひと)はいつも自分(じぶん)がスターになった場面(ばめん)を妄想(もうそう)している。
That person is always fantasizing about scenes where they've become a star.
2. to have delusions
to have delusions; to suffer from false beliefs detached from reality, especially in a clinical or psychiatric sense
被害(ひがい)妄想(もうそう)に苦(くる)しんでいる。
He is suffering from persecution delusions.
妄想(もうそう)と現実(げんじつ)の区別(くべつ)がつかなくなっている。
He can no longer distinguish between delusions and reality.
医師(いし)は患者(かんじゃ)の妄想(もうそう)症状(しょうじょう)について家族(かぞく)に説明(せつめい)した。
The doctor explained the patient's delusional symptoms to the family.

Has two distinct registers of use. In casual conversation and internet culture, it means wild fantasizing or daydreaming (often with a self-deprecating or humorous tone). In medical and psychiatric contexts, it refers to clinical delusions — fixed false beliefs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 妄想(もうそう)にふける: to be lost in fantasies
  • 被害(ひがい)妄想(もうそう): persecution complex, paranoid delusions
  • 誇大(こだい)妄想(もうそう): delusions of grandeur
  • 妄想(もうそう)癖(ぐせ): habit of fantasizing
  • 妄想(もうそう)力(りょく): power of imagination (humorous)

REGISTER:
In casual and internet usage, 妄想(もうそう) is often used playfully to describe harmless daydreaming, especially about fictional characters or unlikely scenarios. In formal and medical contexts, it describes pathological delusions.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 空想(くうそう): fantasy, daydream — more neutral and literary; lacks the negative or clinical connotation
  • 幻覚(げんかく): hallucination — perceiving things that aren't there (sensory), whereas 妄想(もうそう) is about beliefs
  • 想像(そうぞう): imagination — neutral and general; the standard word for imagining things
