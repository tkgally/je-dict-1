怪異(かいい)

かいい
noun, adjective-na
mysterious; supernatural; uncanny phenomenon

Used for mysterious or supernatural phenomena, especially in the context of Japanese folklore, ghost stories, and horror fiction. As a noun, it refers to the phenomenon itself; as a na-adjective, it describes something as eerie or uncanny.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 怪異(かいい)な現象(げんしょう): mysterious phenomenon
  • 怪異(かいい)譚(たん): tale of the supernatural
  • 怪異(かいい)が起(お)きる: a supernatural event occurs
  • 怪異(かいい)な雰囲気(ふんいき): eerie atmosphere

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese literature has a rich tradition of 怪異(かいい) stories, from classical works like 今昔(こんじゃく)物語(ものがたり) to modern horror fiction. The concept overlaps with 妖怪(ようかい) (supernatural creatures) but is broader, encompassing any unexplainable phenomenon.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不思議(ふしぎ): mysterious, strange — more everyday and neutral, less eerie
  • 妖怪(ようかい): supernatural creature, monster — specifically refers to creatures, not phenomena
  • 奇怪(きかい): bizarre, strange — emphasizes oddness and absurdity
