1. disembarkation; getting off a ship; going ashore
The act of leaving a ship or boat, either at a port of call or at the final destination. Used in both formal travel contexts and everyday situations involving ferries and boats.
乗客(じょうきゃく)は順番(じゅんばん)に下船(げせん)した。
The passengers disembarked in order.
港(みなと)に着(つ)いたら下船(げせん)の準備(じゅんび)をしてください。
When we arrive at the port, please prepare to disembark.
クルーズ船(せん)の下船(げせん)手続(てつづ)きに時間(じかん)がかかったが、無事(ぶじ)に上陸(じょうりく)できた。
The disembarkation procedures for the cruise ship took a while, but we managed to get ashore safely.

Compound of 下(げ) (down, off) and 船(せん) (ship). The opposite of 乗船(じょうせん) (boarding a ship).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下船(げせん)する: to disembark
  • 下船(げせん)手続(てつづ)き: disembarkation procedures
  • 下船(げせん)許可(きょか): permission to disembark
  • 下船(げせん)時間(じかん): disembarkation time

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乗船(じょうせん): boarding a ship — the opposite action
  • 上陸(じょうりく): landing, going ashore — emphasizes stepping onto land
  • 下車(げしゃ): getting off a vehicle (train, bus, car)
  • 降機(こうき): deplaning, disembarking from an aircraft
