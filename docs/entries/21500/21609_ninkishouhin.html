人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)

にんきしょうひん
noun
popular product; best-selling item
1. popular product; best-selling item
A product that is widely liked and sells well. Commonly used in retail, marketing, and everyday shopping contexts.
これは人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)です。
This is a popular product.
人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)はすぐに売(う)り切(き)れる。
Popular products sell out quickly.
このお店(みせ)の人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)ランキングを見(み)てから買(か)い物(もの)しよう。
Let's check this store's popular products ranking before shopping.

A very common compound frequently seen in stores, online shopping, and advertising.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)ランキング: popular product ranking
  • 人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)紹介(しょうかい): popular product introduction
  • 当店(とうてん)の人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん): our store's popular products
  • 人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)トップ10: top 10 popular products

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)れ筋(すじ)商品(しょうひん): best-selling product — emphasizes actual sales volume
  • ヒット商品(しょうひん): hit product — implies a breakout success
  • 定番(ていばん)商品(しょうひん): standard/staple product — popular and consistently available
  • ロングセラー: long-seller — popular over a long period

FORMATION:
人気(にんき) (popularity) + 商品(しょうひん) (product, merchandise).
