歯(は)を食(く)いしばる

はをくいしばる
expression
to clench one's teeth; to grit one's teeth; to endure
1. to clench one's teeth; to grit one's teeth
To physically clench the teeth together tightly, often due to pain, effort, or tension.
痛(いた)みに歯(は)を食(く)いしばる。
To clench one's teeth against the pain.
重(おも)い荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ち上(あ)げるとき、歯(は)を食(く)いしばった。
I gritted my teeth when lifting the heavy luggage.
歯(は)を食(く)いしばって注射(ちゅうしゃ)に耐(た)えた。
I gritted my teeth and endured the injection.
2. to bear it; to endure; to persevere through hardship
Used figuratively to mean enduring pain, difficulty, or frustration without giving up or showing weakness.
歯(は)を食(く)いしばって注射(ちゅうしゃ)に耐(た)えた。
I gritted my teeth and endured the injection.
歯(は)を食(く)いしばって頑張(がんば)る。
To grit one's teeth and persevere.
悔(くや)しくて歯(は)を食(く)いしばった。
I clenched my teeth in frustration.
苦(くる)しい時期(じき)もあったが、歯(は)を食(く)いしばって乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
There were difficult times, but I gritted my teeth and got through them.

An idiomatic expression combining 歯(は) (teeth), を (object marker), 食(く)いしばる (to bite down hard). Expresses physical or emotional endurance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばって耐(た)える: to grit one's teeth and endure
  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばって頑張(がんば)る: to grit one's teeth and persevere
  • 痛(いた)みに歯(は)を食(く)いしばる: to clench one's teeth against pain
  • 悔(くや)しくて歯(は)を食(く)いしばる: to clench one's teeth in frustration

GRAMMAR:
The main verb 食(く)いしばる conjugates as a godan verb. Common forms: 食(く)いしばって (te-form), 食(く)いしばった (past).

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 我慢(がまん)する: to endure, to be patient — more general term for enduring
  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばる: to grit one's teeth — implies physical or visible effort to endure
  • 耐(た)え忍(しの)ぶ: to endure patiently — more literary, implies long-suffering patience
