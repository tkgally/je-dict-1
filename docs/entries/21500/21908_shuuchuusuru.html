1. to direct one's attention or mental effort on a single task or subject
to direct one's attention or mental effort on a single task or subject; to focus one's mind
勉強(べんきょう)に集中(しゅうちゅう)する。
To concentrate on studying.
静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)でないと集中(しゅうちゅう)できない。
I can't concentrate unless it's a quiet place.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)は苦手(にがて)な科目(かもく)に集中(しゅうちゅう)して取(と)り組(く)んだ。
Before the exam, I concentrated on working on my weak subjects.
2. to gather or converge in one place or on one point
to gather or converge in one place or on one point; for things to be concentrated or centralized
人口(じんこう)が都市(とし)に集中(しゅうちゅう)している。
The population is concentrated in the cities.
注文(ちゅうもん)が一(いち)時期(じき)に集中(しゅうちゅう)した。
Orders were concentrated in one period.
批判(ひはん)が一人(ひとり)の政治家(せいじか)に集中(しゅうちゅう)している。
Criticism is focused on a single politician.

One of the most common and useful suru verbs. Has two main uses: (1) focusing one's attention or effort, and (2) the convergence or concentration of things in one place or time. Takes the particle に to mark what one is concentrating on.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)力(りょく): concentration, ability to focus
  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)治療(ちりょう)室(しつ): intensive care unit (ICU)
  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)豪雨(ごうう): localized heavy rain
  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)攻撃(こうげき): concentrated attack
  • 一極(いっきょく)集中(しゅうちゅう): concentration in one center (often describing Tokyo)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～に集中(しゅうちゅう)する: to concentrate on ~
  • ～が集中(しゅうちゅう)する: ~ converges/concentrates

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 専念(せんねん)する: to devote oneself to — implies giving up other things to focus exclusively
  • 注力(ちゅうりょく)する: to focus efforts on — more business-oriented; emphasizes directing resources
  • 没頭(ぼっとう)する: to be absorbed in — implies being deeply engrossed, losing track of everything else
