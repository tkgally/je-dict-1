1. to trigger; to set off; to detonate
To cause something to happen by contact or impact, such as setting off an explosion or triggering a chain reaction of events.
爆発(ばくはつ)が連鎖(れんさ)的(てき)に触発(しょくはつ)された。
Explosions were triggered in a chain reaction.
この事故(じこ)が安全(あんぜん)基準(きじゅん)の見直(みなお)しを触発(しょくはつ)した。
This accident triggered a review of safety standards.
地震(じしん)が触発(しょくはつ)した地滑(じすべ)りにより、道路(どうろ)が寸断(すんだん)された。
A landslide triggered by the earthquake cut off the roads.
2. to inspire; to stimulate; to provoke (an action or feeling)
To move someone to action or creativity through exposure to an experience, work, or achievement. Often used in the passive as 'to be inspired by.'
彼(かれ)の作品(さくひん)に触発(しょくはつ)された。
I was inspired by his work.
友人(ゆうじん)の成功(せいこう)に触発(しょくはつ)されて、自分(じぶん)も挑戦(ちょうせん)することにした。
Inspired by my friend's success, I decided to take on the challenge myself.
海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)での体験(たいけん)に触発(しょくはつ)されて新(あたら)しいビジネスを始(はじ)めた起業家(きぎょうか)も少(すく)なくない。
There are quite a few entrepreneurs who were inspired by their experiences traveling abroad and started new businesses.

Originally a technical term meaning to detonate by contact, now widely used figuratively for triggering events or inspiring action. Combines 触(しょく) (touch, contact) and 発(はつ) (emit, discharge).

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 is the original meaning: physical triggering (explosions, earthquakes, chain reactions)
  • Sense 2 is more common in everyday use: being stimulated or inspired to action
  • The passive form 触発(しょくはつ)される (to be triggered/inspired) is very frequent
  • に触発(しょくはつ)されて is a common pattern meaning "inspired by"

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に触発(しょくはつ)される: to be inspired/triggered by
  • 触発(しょくはつ)されて～する: to do something, having been inspired
  • 連鎖(れんさ)的(てき)に触発(しょくはつ)する: to trigger in a chain

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刺激(しげき)する: to stimulate — broader; can be physical or mental stimulation
  • 誘発(ゆうはつ)する: to induce, to provoke — emphasizes causing something (often negative) to happen
  • インスパイアする: to inspire — the loanword, used casually
