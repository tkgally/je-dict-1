1. to soak and soften; to let something soak until soft
To immerse something in water (or another liquid) until it becomes soft and swollen. Commonly used in cooking for ingredients like dried foods, rice, or beans.
豆(まめ)を水(みず)でふやかす。
Soak the beans in water to soften them.
乾燥(かんそう)わかめを水(みず)でふやかしてから使(つか)う。
Soak the dried wakame in water before using it.
ゼラチンを冷水(れいすい)でふやかしてから、温(あたた)めた牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)に加(くわ)える。
Soak the gelatin in cold water to soften it, then add it to the warmed milk.

A transitive godan verb used almost exclusively in cooking contexts. The intransitive counterpart is ふやける (to become soggy/soft from soaking).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • ふやかす (transitive): to soak something until soft
  • ふやける (intransitive): to become soft/soggy from soaking

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 豆(まめ)をふやかす: to soak beans
  • 米(こめ)をふやかす: to soak rice
  • ゼラチンをふやかす: to bloom/soften gelatin
  • 乾物(かんぶつ)をふやかす: to rehydrate dried foods
  • 水(みず)でふやかす: to soften in water

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 浸(ひた)す: to soak, immerse — more general, not specifically about softening
  • 戻(もど)す: to reconstitute — specifically about restoring dried foods to their original state
  • ふやける: to become soggy — the intransitive form, can have a negative nuance (becoming undesirably soft)
