1. refining; purification; processing to remove impurities
The process of removing unwanted substances to make something purer or more refined. Commonly used for food processing (sugar, oil), petroleum, metals, and chemicals.
石油(せきゆ)を精製(せいせい)する。
To refine petroleum.
精製(せいせい)塩(しお)と天然(てんねん)塩(しお)はどう違(ちが)うのか。
What is the difference between refined salt and natural salt?
この工場(こうじょう)では原油(げんゆ)を精製(せいせい)してガソリンや灯油(とうゆ)を生産(せいさん)している。
This factory refines crude oil to produce gasoline and kerosene.

Composed of 精(せい) (refined, pure) and 製(せい) (manufacture). Describes the industrial or chemical process of purifying raw materials.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精製(せいせい)糖(とう): refined sugar
  • 精製(せいせい)塩(しお): refined salt
  • 精製(せいせい)水(すい): purified water
  • 石油(せきゆ)精製(せいせい): petroleum refining
  • 精製(せいせい)所(しょ)/精製(せいせい)工場(こうじょう): refinery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精製(せいせい): refining — removing impurities from raw materials
  • 生成(せいせい): generation, formation — creating something new (homophone, different kanji)
  • 製造(せいぞう): manufacturing — broader term for making products
  • 精錬(せいれん): smelting — specifically extracting metal from ore
  • 浄化(じょうか): purification — often used for water or air treatment
