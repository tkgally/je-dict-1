1. health management; health care; maintaining one's health
The practice of monitoring and maintaining one's physical and mental health through regular checkups, diet, exercise, and lifestyle choices.
健康管理(けんこうかんり)に気(き)をつけている。
I pay attention to my health management.
アプリを使(つか)って日々(ひび)の健康管理(けんこうかんり)をしている。
I use an app to manage my daily health.
会社(かいしゃ)は従業員(じゅうぎょういん)の健康管理(けんこうかんり)に力(ちから)を入(い)れている。
The company is putting effort into employee health management.

Compound of 健康(けんこう) (health) + 管理(かんり) (management). A widely used term in daily life, workplace contexts, and healthcare.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 健康管理(けんこうかんり)に気(き)をつける: to pay attention to health management
  • 健康管理(けんこうかんり)アプリ: health management app
  • 従業員(じゅうぎょういん)の健康管理(けんこうかんり): employee health management
  • 日々(ひび)の健康管理(けんこうかんり): daily health care
  • 自己(じこ)健康管理(けんこうかんり): self-health management

USAGE:
Very common in corporate and institutional contexts, where companies have legal obligations regarding employee health. Also commonly used in personal contexts about maintaining one's own health through diet, exercise, and regular checkups.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 健康診断(けんこうしんだん): health checkup, medical examination
  • 体調管理(たいちょうかんり): condition management — focuses on daily physical condition
