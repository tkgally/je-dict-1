区役所(くやくしょ)

くやくしょ
noun
ward office; municipal office of a city ward
1. ward office; municipal office of a city ward
The local government office that handles administrative services for a ward (区(く)) in a major city. Provides services such as resident registration, family register, tax, insurance, and other civic procedures.
区役所(くやくしょ)で住民票(じゅうみんひょう)をもらう。
To get a certificate of residence at the ward office.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)したら区役所(くやくしょ)で届(とど)け出(で)をしなければならない。
After moving, you must file a notification at the ward office.
区役所(くやくしょ)の窓口(まどぐち)は平日(へいじつ)の午前(ごぜん)八時半(はちじはん)から午後(ごご)五時(ごじ)まで開(あ)いている。
The ward office counter is open from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays.

Composed of 区(く) (ward) and 役所(やくしょ) (government office). The administrative center for a 区(く) (ward) within designated cities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 区役所(くやくしょ)に行(い)く: to go to the ward office
  • 区役所(くやくしょ)の窓口(まどぐち): ward office counter
  • 区役所(くやくしょ)で手続(てつづ)きをする: to complete procedures at the ward office
  • 区役所(くやくしょ)に届(とど)け出(で)る: to file a notification at the ward office

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 市役所(しやくしょ): city hall — for cities without wards
  • 区役所(くやくしょ): ward office — for wards within major cities
  • 町役場(まちやくば): town hall — for smaller towns
  • 村役場(むらやくば): village hall — for villages
  • 役所(やくしょ): government office — general term

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, many important life events require visits to the 区役所(くやくしょ): registering births, marriages, and deaths; reporting a change of address; obtaining a 住民票(じゅうみんひょう) (certificate of residence) or 印鑑(いんかん)証明書(しょうめいしょ) (seal registration certificate). Foreign residents also use the 区役所(くやくしょ) for residence card procedures.
