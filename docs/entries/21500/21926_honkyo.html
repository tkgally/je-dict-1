本拠(ほんきょ)

ほんきょ
noun
headquarters; base; stronghold
1. headquarters; base; stronghold; home base
The main base of operations or central location from which an organization, group, or activity is directed. Used for both literal military/organizational bases and figurative centers of activity.
本拠(ほんきょ)地(ち)はどこですか。
Where is your home base?
そのチームは大阪(おおさか)を本拠(ほんきょ)地(ち)としている。
That team is based in Osaka.
敵(てき)の本拠(ほんきょ)を攻(せ)め落(お)とすのが最終(さいしゅう)目標(もくひょう)だ。
The final objective is to capture the enemy's stronghold.

Refers to the primary base or central location of an organization, team, or military force. Most commonly appears in the compound 本拠(ほんきょ)地(ち) (home base, headquarters location).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本拠(ほんきょ)地(ち): home base, headquarters
  • 本拠(ほんきょ)を置(お)く: to be based at, to have one's headquarters at
  • 本拠(ほんきょ)を構(かま)える: to set up one's base

USAGE:
While 本拠(ほんきょ) alone means "base" or "headquarters," the compound 本拠(ほんきょ)地(ち) is far more common in modern usage. Frequently used for sports teams' home grounds, companies' main offices, and historical military strongholds.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 拠点(きょてん): base, strongpoint — more general, can refer to any strategic location
  • 本部(ほんぶ): headquarters — emphasizes the organizational center rather than the location
