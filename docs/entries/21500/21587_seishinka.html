精神科(せいしんか)

せいしんか
noun
psychiatry; psychiatric department
1. psychiatry department; psychiatric clinic
A medical department or clinic specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of mental health conditions. In Japanese hospitals, this is the department name for psychiatry.
精神科(せいしんか)を受診(じゅしん)した。
I visited the psychiatry department.
精神科(せいしんか)の先生(せんせい)に相談(そうだん)してみたらどうですか。
How about consulting a psychiatrist?
最近(さいきん)は精神科(せいしんか)に通(かよ)うことへの抵抗感(ていこうかん)が減(へ)ってきている。
Recently, the reluctance toward visiting a psychiatrist has been decreasing.

Compound of 精神(せいしん) (mind, spirit) and 科(か) (department, specialty). The standard term for the psychiatry department in Japanese hospitals and clinics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精神科(せいしんか)を受診(じゅしん)する: to visit a psychiatrist
  • 精神科(せいしんか)に通(かよ)う: to attend psychiatric treatment regularly
  • 精神科(せいしんか)医(い): psychiatrist
  • 精神科(せいしんか)病院(びょういん): psychiatric hospital

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 心療(しんりょう)内科(ないか): psychosomatic medicine department — treats physical symptoms caused by psychological factors; often perceived as less stigmatized
  • 神経(しんけい)科(か): neurology department — treats neurological conditions (not psychiatric ones)
  • カウンセリング: counseling — talk therapy, not necessarily medical

NOTE:
In Japan, some clinics use the name 心療(しんりょう)内科(ないか) (psychosomatic medicine) instead of 精神科(せいしんか) to reduce the stigma associated with psychiatric care. Both departments may treat similar conditions like depression and anxiety.
