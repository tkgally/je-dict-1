1. to leave alone; to neglect; to abandon; to leave unattended
To leave something or someone without attention or care, often implying irresponsibility or carelessness.
子供(こども)をほったらかす。
To leave the children unattended.
宿題(しゅくだい)をほったらかして遊(あそ)びに行(い)った。
I went out to play, leaving my homework undone.
庭(にわ)をほったらかしにしていたら、雑草(ざっそう)だらけになった。
I left the garden unattended and it became overgrown with weeds.

A colloquial verb meaning to leave something or someone neglected. Carries a stronger nuance of carelessness or irresponsibility than 放(ほう)っておく.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子供(こども)をほったらかす: to neglect the children
  • 仕事(しごと)をほったらかす: to leave work undone
  • ほったらかしにする: to leave in a state of neglect

USAGE:
The te-form ほったらかして is very common. The nominalized form ほったらかし is used as a noun meaning "a state of neglect," often in the pattern ほったらかしにする.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 放(ほう)っておく: to leave alone — more neutral, can mean deliberately choosing not to interfere
  • ほったらかす: to neglect — more negative, implies carelessness
  • 放置(ほうち)する: to leave unattended — more formal, used in official or written contexts

REGISTER:
Informal and colloquial. Not used in formal writing or polite speech.
