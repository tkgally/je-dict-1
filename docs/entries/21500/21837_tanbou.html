1. visit; exploration; inquiry
visiting a place or person to investigate, explore, or seek out something of interest
古(ふる)い寺(てら)を探訪(たんぼう)する。
To visit old temples.
記者(きしゃ)が現地(げんち)を探訪(たんぼう)した。
The reporter visited the area to investigate.
歴史的(れきしてき)な名所(めいしょ)を探訪(たんぼう)する旅(たび)に出(で)かけた。
I set out on a trip to explore historic sites.

Combines the nuances of visiting and investigating. Often implies going somewhere with purpose — to discover, report on, or appreciate something. Common in travel writing and journalism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名所(めいしょ)探訪(たんぼう): visiting famous places
  • 現地(げんち)探訪(たんぼう): on-site investigation
  • 史跡(しせき)探訪(たんぼう): exploring historical sites
  • 探訪(たんぼう)記(き): travel report, exploration account

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 訪問(ほうもん): visit — neutral, general-purpose visit without the exploratory nuance
  • 見学(けんがく): tour, observation visit — educational or sightseeing focus
  • 取材(しゅざい): news coverage — specifically journalistic investigation
