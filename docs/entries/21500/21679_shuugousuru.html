1. to gather; to assemble; to meet up at a designated place and time
Used when people come together at an agreed location. Common in school, work, and group activity contexts.
九時(くじ)に駅前(えきまえ)に集合(しゅうごう)してください。
Please meet at the station at nine o'clock.
全員(ぜんいん)が集合(しゅうごう)したら出発(しゅっぱつ)します。
We'll depart once everyone has gathered.
避難(ひなん)訓練(くんれん)では校庭(こうてい)に集合(しゅうごう)することになっている。
During evacuation drills, we're supposed to assemble in the schoolyard.

Very common in everyday Japanese for organizing group meetups, school events, and workplace gatherings. The noun form 集合(しゅうごう) can be used as a command: 集合(しゅうごう)！(Assemble! / Gather round!). Also used in math for "set" (集合(しゅうごう)論(ろん) = set theory).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 集合(しゅうごう)時間(じかん): meeting time
  • 集合(しゅうごう)場所(ばしょ): meeting place
  • 集合(しゅうごう)住宅(じゅうたく): apartment complex, housing complex

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 集(あつ)まる: to gather (intransitive, more natural/spontaneous)
  • 合流(ごうりゅう)する: to join up, merge (used when joining an existing group)
