1. to organize; to put in order; to tidy up
To arrange things in a logical or orderly manner, whether physical objects on a desk, information for a report, or one's own thoughts and feelings.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)を整理(せいり)した。
I organized my desk.
情報(じょうほう)を整理(せいり)してから報告(ほうこく)してください。
Please organize the information before reporting.
会議(かいぎ)の前(まえ)に論点(ろんてん)を整理(せいり)しておかないと、話(はなし)がまとまらない。
If you don't organize the discussion points before the meeting, the conversation won't come together.
2. to sort out; to consolidate; to dispose of what is unnecessary
To streamline by identifying and removing what is no longer needed, such as decluttering belongings, reducing staff, or eliminating redundant items.
不要(ふよう)な書類(しょるい)を整理(せいり)する。
To sort out unnecessary documents.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しを機(き)に荷物(にもつ)を整理(せいり)して、使(つか)わないものは処分(しょぶん)した。
I sorted through my belongings when moving and disposed of things I don't use.
会社(かいしゃ)は経営(けいえい)不振(ふしん)のため、人員(じんいん)整理(せいり)を行(おこな)わざるを得(え)なくなった。
Due to poor business performance, the company had no choice but to carry out staff reductions.

A very common and versatile verb meaning to bring order to something or to dispose of what is unnecessary. Combines 整(せい) (arrange, put in order) and 理(り) (reason, logic).

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 focuses on arranging or ordering things: physical objects, information, thoughts
  • Sense 2 focuses on streamlining by removing the unnecessary: decluttering, downsizing
  • 人員(じんいん)整理(せいり) (staff reduction) is a euphemism for layoffs
  • 気持(きも)ちを整理(せいり)する means to sort out one's feelings

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気持(きも)ちを整理(せいり)する: to sort out one's feelings
  • 情報(じょうほう)を整理(せいり)する: to organize information
  • 荷物(にもつ)を整理(せいり)する: to sort through belongings
  • 人員(じんいん)整理(せいり): staff reduction, downsizing
  • 整理(せいり)整頓(せいとん): tidying up and organizing (set phrase)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 片(かた)づける: to tidy up, to clean up — more casual and physical
  • 整頓(せいとん)する: to put things in order — focuses on neatness and arrangement
  • {まとめる}: to compile, to put together — focuses on bringing things together into a unified whole
  • 分類(ぶんるい)する: to classify, to categorize — focuses on sorting into categories
