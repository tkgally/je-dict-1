洗浄液(せんじょうえき)

せんじょうえき
noun
cleaning solution; wash liquid
1. cleaning solution; wash liquid; cleanser
A liquid solution used for cleaning or washing. Covers a wide range of products from contact lens solution to industrial cleaning fluids.
コンタクトレンズの洗浄液(せんじょうえき)を買(か)う。
To buy contact lens solution.
この洗浄液(せんじょうえき)は油(あぶら)汚(よご)れに効果的(こうかてき)だ。
This cleaning solution is effective against grease stains.
機械(きかい)の部品(ぶひん)を専用(せんよう)の洗浄液(せんじょうえき)に浸(ひた)してから組(く)み立(た)てる。
Soak the machine parts in a dedicated cleaning solution before assembling them.

A compound of 洗浄(せんじょう) (washing, cleansing) and 液(えき) (liquid). Used for any liquid cleaning product.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コンタクトレンズ洗浄液(せんじょうえき): contact lens solution
  • 洗浄液(せんじょうえき)で洗(あら)う: to wash with cleaning solution
  • 専用(せんよう)洗浄液(せんじょうえき): dedicated cleaning solution
  • 洗浄液(せんじょうえき)を補充(ほじゅう)する: to refill the cleaning solution

RELATED TERMS:

  • 洗剤(せんざい): detergent — a broader term for cleaning agents including powders
  • 消毒液(しょうどくえき): disinfectant solution — specifically for killing germs
