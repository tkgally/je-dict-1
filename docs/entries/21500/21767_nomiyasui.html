1. easy to drink; palatable; smooth; goes down easily
Describes a beverage or liquid medicine that is pleasant or not difficult to consume. Can refer to flavor, texture, or size of a drink that makes it comfortable to swallow.
このワインは飲(の)みやすい。
This wine is easy to drink.
飲(の)みやすい薬(くすり)に変(か)えてもらった。
I had my medicine changed to one that's easier to take.

This beer has little bitterness, so it's easy to drink even for beginners.

Formed from the verb 飲(の)む (to drink) + やすい (easy to ~). This is a productive grammatical pattern in Japanese: verb stem + やすい creates an adjective meaning "easy to do." Despite being grammatically predictable, 飲(の)みやすい is extremely common as a set phrase in product descriptions, reviews, and everyday conversation about beverages.
