平明(へいめい)

へいめい
adjective-na
plain; clear; easy to understand
1. plain; clear; easy to understand; lucid
Describing language, writing, or expression that is straightforward and free of complexity or pretension.
平明(へいめい)な文章(ぶんしょう)を心(こころ)がけている。
I try to write in a plain, clear style.
彼(かれ)の説明(せつめい)は平明(へいめい)で分(わ)かりやすい。
His explanations are lucid and easy to understand.
難(むずか)しい専門用語(せんもんようご)を避(さ)け、平明(へいめい)な言葉(ことば)で書(か)かれた入門書(にゅうもんしょ)だ。
It's an introductory book written in plain language, avoiding difficult technical terms.

Combines 平(へい) (flat, plain) and 明(めい) (bright, clear). Describes language or expression that is clear, easy to follow, and free from unnecessary complexity. Used almost exclusively in written and literary contexts to evaluate the quality of writing or speech.

USAGE:

  • Almost always used to describe language, writing, or explanations — not physical objects
  • A positive quality — implies clarity and accessibility without being simplistic
  • Common in literary criticism, book reviews, and discussions of writing style

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 平明(へいめい)な文章(ぶんしょう): clear, plain writing
  • 平明(へいめい)な言葉(ことば): plain language
  • 平明(へいめい)な表現(ひょうげん): clear expression
  • 平明(へいめい)な文体(ぶんたい): a lucid writing style

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明快(めいかい): clear, lucid — similar but slightly stronger emphasis on logical clarity
  • 平易(へいい): plain, easy — very close synonym; emphasizes ease of understanding
  • 簡潔(かんけつ): concise — focuses on brevity rather than clarity
  • 分(わ)かりやすい: easy to understand — more conversational; broader usage
