談話室(だんわしつ)

だんわしつ
noun
lounge; conversation room; common room
1. lounge; conversation room; common room; sitting room
A room designated for relaxation and conversation, typically found in hotels, hospitals, dormitories, and other institutions. A shared space where people can sit, talk, and socialize.
談話室(だんわしつ)でコーヒーを飲(の)む。
To drink coffee in the lounge.
病院(びょういん)の談話室(だんわしつ)で家族(かぞく)と話(はな)した。
I talked with my family in the hospital lounge.
寮(りょう)の談話室(だんわしつ)にはテレビやソファが置(お)かれていて、学生(がくせい)たちの憩(いこ)いの場(ば)になっている。
The dormitory lounge has a TV and sofas, and serves as a place of relaxation for students.

A compound of 談話(だんわ) (conversation, discussion) and 室(しつ) (room). Found in buildings where people stay for extended periods — hospitals, hotels, dormitories, retirement homes, and conference facilities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病院(びょういん)の談話室(だんわしつ): hospital lounge
  • 寮(りょう)の談話室(だんわしつ): dormitory common room
  • ホテルの談話室(だんわしつ): hotel lounge
  • 談話室(だんわしつ)で休(やす)む: to rest in the lounge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ラウンジ: lounge — loanword, used for upscale or commercial settings (airport lounges, hotel lounges)
  • 休憩室(きゅうけいしつ): break room, rest room — a room for resting, often in workplaces
  • 待合室(まちあいしつ): waiting room — specifically for waiting, not socializing
  • 応接室(おうせつしつ): reception room — a more formal room for receiving guests
