1. coroner's inquest; postmortem examination
an official examination of a dead body to determine the cause and circumstances of death, conducted by legal authorities
警察(けいさつ)が検視(けんし)を行(おこな)った。
The police conducted a coroner's inquest.
検視(けんし)の結果(けっか)、事件性(じけんせい)はないと判断(はんだん)された。
As a result of the inquest, it was determined there was no criminal involvement.
不審(ふしん)死(し)の場合(ばあい)は必(かなら)ず検視(けんし)が行(おこな)われる。
In the case of a suspicious death, a coroner's examination is always conducted.

An official legal procedure conducted when the cause of death is unclear. In Japan, 検視(けんし) is performed by a prosecutor or police officer (not a medical doctor) to determine whether foul play is involved. If further medical examination is needed, it leads to 司法(しほう)解剖(かいぼう) (judicial autopsy).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 検視(けんし)を行(おこな)う: to conduct an inquest
  • 検視(けんし)官(かん): coroner, medical examiner
  • 検視(けんし)報告(ほうこく): inquest report
  • 検視(けんし)の結果(けっか): results of the inquest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 検死(けんし): postmortem examination — variant writing, often used interchangeably but technically less formal
  • 司法(しほう)解剖(かいぼう): judicial autopsy — a more thorough medical procedure ordered when foul play is suspected
  • 解剖(かいぼう): autopsy, dissection — the medical procedure itself
