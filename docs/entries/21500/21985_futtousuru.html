1. to boil; to reach boiling point
A liquid is heated to the point where it turns into vapor and bubbles vigorously. Used literally for cooking and science.
お湯(ゆ)が沸騰(ふっとう)した。
The water boiled.
沸騰(ふっとう)したお湯(ゆ)に麺(めん)を入(い)れる。
Put the noodles into the boiling water.
水(みず)が沸騰(ふっとう)するまで強火(つよび)で加熱(かねつ)してください。
Please heat on high until the water boils.
2. to be in an uproar; to be boiling with excitement or anger
Figuratively describes a situation where emotions, public opinion, or an atmosphere becomes intensely heated or agitated.
会場(かいじょう)が沸騰(ふっとう)した。
The venue erupted in excitement.
世論(よろん)が沸騰(ふっとう)している。
Public opinion is boiling over.
不祥事(ふしょうじ)が発覚(はっかく)し、国民(こくみん)の怒(いか)りが沸騰(ふっとう)している。
After the scandal came to light, the public's anger is boiling over.

Literally means to boil (of a liquid), and is commonly used figuratively to describe heated emotions or public uproar.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お湯(ゆ)が沸騰(ふっとう)する: the water boils
  • 沸騰(ふっとう)したお湯(ゆ): boiling water
  • 世論(よろん)が沸騰(ふっとう)する: public opinion boils over
  • 会場(かいじょう)が沸騰(ふっとう)する: the venue erupts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 沸(わ)く: to boil (native Japanese, everyday); also means to well up (emotions)
  • 沸(わ)かす: to bring to a boil (transitive)
  • 煮立(にた)つ: to come to a boil (used in cooking)

NOTE:
In cooking contexts, 沸騰(ふっとう) specifically refers to a rolling boil at 100°C, while 沸(わ)く can refer to water that is merely hot enough.
