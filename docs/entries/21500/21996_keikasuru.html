1. to pass; to elapse (of time)
Describes time passing or a period going by.
3年(ねん)が経過(けいか)した。
Three years have passed.
期限(きげん)が経過(けいか)している。
The deadline has passed.
開業(かいぎょう)してから半年(はんとし)が経過(けいか)し、ようやく軌道(きどう)に乗(の)ってきた。
Half a year has passed since opening, and things are finally getting on track.
2. to progress; to develop (of a condition or process)
Describes how a condition, illness, or situation develops over time. The noun form 経過(けいか) is commonly used for the course or progress of something.
術後(じゅつご)の経過(けいか)は良好(りょうこう)です。
The post-operative progress is favorable.
経過(けいか)を見守(みまも)りましょう。
Let's monitor the progress.
病気(びょうき)の経過(けいか)が思(おも)わしくなく、入院(にゅういん)することになった。
The illness was not progressing well, so hospitalization was decided.

The noun 経過(けいか) is used even more frequently than the verb form, especially in medical and administrative contexts to describe how something has been progressing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)が経過(けいか)する: time passes
  • 経過(けいか)観察(かんさつ): follow-up observation (medical)
  • 経過(けいか)報告(ほうこく): progress report
  • 経過(けいか)良好(りょうこう): favorable progress
  • 経過(けいか)措置(そち): transitional measure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 過(す)ぎる: to pass — everyday word for time passing
  • 経(た)つ: to pass, elapse — common native Japanese word for time passing
  • 進行(しんこう)する: to progress, advance — emphasizes forward movement
  • 推移(すいい)する: to shift, transition — focuses on gradual changes over time
