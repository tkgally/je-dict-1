暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)

だんぼうきぐ
noun
heating appliance; heater
1. heating appliance; heater; heating equipment
Devices and equipment used for heating rooms or spaces. A general term covering space heaters, stoves, kotatsu, and other heating devices.
暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)を出(だ)す季節(きせつ)になった。
It's the season to bring out the heating appliances.
古(ふる)い暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)は火事(かじ)の原因(げんいん)になることがある。
Old heating appliances can cause fires.
省(しょう)エネの暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)に買(か)い替(か)えたら、電気代(でんきだい)がかなり下(さ)がった。
After switching to an energy-saving heating appliance, the electricity bill went down considerably.

A compound of 暖房(だんぼう) (heating) and 器具(きぐ) (equipment, appliance). A general term for any device used to heat a room or space.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)を使(つか)う: to use a heating appliance
  • 暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ)を片付(かたづ)ける: to put away heating appliances (at end of winter)
  • 電気(でんき)暖房器具(だんぼうきぐ): electric heating appliance

EXAMPLES OF HEATING APPLIANCES:

  • エアコン: air conditioner (with heating function)
  • ストーブ: space heater
  • こたつ: kotatsu (heated table)
  • ファンヒーター: fan heater
  • 床暖房(ゆかだんぼう): underfloor heating

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冷房器具(れいぼうきぐ): cooling appliance — the opposite, for summer
  • 暖房(だんぼう): heating — the concept or system itself, not the device
