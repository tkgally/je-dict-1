得意先(とくいさき)

とくいさき
noun
customer; client; regular patron
1. customer; client; regular patron
A regular or valued customer or business client, especially one with an ongoing relationship.
得意先(とくいさき)を回(まわ)る。
To visit clients.
新(あたら)しい得意先(とくいさき)を開拓(かいたく)することが今年(ことし)の目標(もくひょう)だ。
Developing new clients is this year's goal.
長年(ながねん)の得意先(とくいさき)との信頼関係(しんらいかんけい)を大切(たいせつ)にしている。
We value the relationships of trust we have with our long-standing clients.

Combines 得意(とくい) (valued customer; one's forte) and 先(さき) (destination, party). A business term referring to regular customers or clients with whom a company has an established relationship. The nuance is of a valued, ongoing commercial relationship rather than a one-time buyer.

USAGE:

  • Primarily used in business contexts
  • Implies a B2B (business-to-business) or professional relationship more often than individual consumers
  • 得意先(とくいさき)を回(まわ)る (to make the rounds of clients) is a very common expression for sales visits

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 得意先(とくいさき)を回(まわ)る: to visit clients
  • 得意先(とくいさき)を開拓(かいたく)する: to develop new clients
  • 得意先(とくいさき)回(まわ)り: client visits, sales rounds
  • 大口(おおぐち)の得意先(とくいさき): major client

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 顧客(こきゃく): customer, client — more formal; used broadly in business
  • お客(きゃく)さん: customer — general; used in shops and service contexts
  • クライアント: client — loanword; common in creative and consulting industries
  • 取引先(とりひきさき): business partner, trading partner — broader; includes suppliers as well as customers
