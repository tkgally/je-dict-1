1. to arrive at; to reach; to end up at (a place)
To finally reach a destination after a journey or search, often implying some difficulty or wandering along the way.
やっと駅(えき)に行(い)き着(つ)いた。
I finally reached the station.
迷(まよ)いながらも目的地(もくてきち)に行(い)き着(つ)いた。
Even while getting lost, I managed to reach my destination.
道(みち)なき道(みち)を進(すす)み、ようやく山頂(さんちょう)に行(い)き着(つ)いた。
I pushed through pathless terrain and finally reached the summit.
2. to arrive at (a conclusion); to end up with; to come to
To reach a final answer, conclusion, or state after a process of thinking, trying, or developing.
その結論(けつろん)に行(い)き着(つ)いた。
I arrived at that conclusion.
試行錯誤(しこうさくご)の末(すえ)に、この方法(ほうほう)に行(い)き着(つ)いた。
After trial and error, I arrived at this method.
考(かんが)えれば考(かんが)えるほど、同(おな)じ答(こた)えに行(い)き着(つ)く。
The more I think about it, the more I arrive at the same answer.

Compound verb of 行(い)く (to go) + 着(つ)く (to arrive). Emphasizes finally reaching a destination or conclusion, often after some effort or process.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 結論(けつろん)に行(い)き着(つ)く: to arrive at a conclusion
  • 答(こた)えに行(い)き着(つ)く: to reach an answer
  • 目的地(もくてきち)に行(い)き着(つ)く: to reach one's destination
  • 最終的(さいしゅうてき)に行(い)き着(つ)く: to ultimately end up at

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 辿(たど)り着(つ)く: to finally reach — very similar, slightly more emphasis on the difficult journey
  • 着(つ)く: to arrive — neutral, no implication of difficulty
  • 到達(とうたつ)する: to reach, to attain — more formal

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (く row). Conjugates: 行(い)き着(つ)かない, 行(い)き着(つ)いて, 行(い)き着(つ)いた.
