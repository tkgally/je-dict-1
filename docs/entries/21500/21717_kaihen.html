1. alteration; modification; revision; adaptation
The act of making deliberate changes to something that already exists, such as adapting a novel for film or modifying data. Can be neutral or carry negative connotations of tampering.
原作(げんさく)を改変(かいへん)した映画(えいが)が公開(こうかい)された。
A movie that altered the original work was released.
歴史(れきし)の改変(かいへん)は許(ゆる)されるべきではない。
Falsification of history should not be permitted.
ソフトウェアの無断(むだん)改変(かいへん)は著作権法(ちょさくけんほう)に違反(いはん)する可能性(かのうせい)がある。
Unauthorized modification of software may violate copyright law.

Refers to making changes to something that already exists, often with an implication of significant or deliberate modification. Combines 改(かい) (reform, change) and 変(へん) (change, alter).

USAGE:

  • Can be neutral (adapting a novel for film) or negative (tampering with data, falsifying records)
  • Often implies changing the substance or content, not just surface edits
  • Used with する to form the verb 改変(かいへん)する (to alter, to modify)
  • Common in discussions of creative works, data, historical records, and software

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原作(げんさく)を改変(かいへん)する: to alter the original work
  • データを改変(かいへん)する: to modify/tamper with data
  • 無断(むだん)改変(かいへん): unauthorized modification
  • 改変(かいへん)版(ばん): modified version

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 改編(かいへん): reorganization, adaptation — same reading, but focuses on restructuring (e.g., an organization or a work's structure)
  • 改正(かいせい): revision, amendment — used for laws, rules, and regulations
  • 変更(へんこう): change, modification — more general and neutral
  • 改良(かいりょう): improvement — implies the change makes something better
