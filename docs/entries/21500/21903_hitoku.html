1. concealment; keeping secret; hiding
deliberately hiding information, evidence, or facts from others; the act of keeping something secret, especially in official or legal contexts
証拠(しょうこ)を秘匿(ひとく)する。
To conceal evidence.
個人(こじん)情報(じょうほう)の秘匿(ひとく)は重要(じゅうよう)だ。
The concealment of personal information is important.
企業(きぎょう)が不祥事(ふしょうじ)を秘匿(ひとく)していたことが発覚(はっかく)した。
It was discovered that the company had been concealing a scandal.
情報(じょうほう)秘匿(ひとく)の義務(ぎむ)に違反(いはん)した場合(ばあい)、厳(きび)しい処分(しょぶん)を受(う)ける。
If you violate the obligation to keep information secret, you will face severe punishment.

A formal term for deliberately hiding or concealing information. Frequently appears in legal, journalistic, and corporate governance contexts. Often implies wrongdoing when information that should be disclosed is hidden, but can also describe legitimate confidentiality measures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 証拠(しょうこ)を秘匿(ひとく)する: to conceal evidence
  • 情報(じょうほう)の秘匿(ひとく): concealment of information
  • 秘匿(ひとく)義務(ぎむ): duty of confidentiality
  • 秘匿(ひとく)性(せい): secrecy, confidentiality
  • 事実(じじつ)を秘匿(ひとく)する: to conceal facts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 隠蔽(いんぺい): cover-up — strongly implies wrongful concealment with intent to deceive
  • 機密(きみつ): classified, confidential — refers to the classified status of information itself
  • 隠(かく)す: to hide — the everyday general word; can be used for anything from physical hiding to secrets

REGISTER:
Formal. Common in legal documents, news reports, and official communications.
