1. to be timid; to be nervous and hesitant; to be fearful
Describes a state of visible nervousness and timidity, where someone appears anxious and lacks confidence in their behavior or speech.
おどおどした態度(たいど)はやめなさい。
Stop acting so timid.
初(はじ)めての職場(しょくば)でおどおどしていた。
I was nervous and hesitant at my new workplace.
先生(せんせい)に叱(しか)られて、子供(こども)はおどおどしながら謝(あやま)った。
Scolded by the teacher, the child apologized timidly.

An onomatopoeic word describing visible timidity and nervousness. The person appears anxious, uncertain, and lacking in confidence. It is generally used to describe someone's outward demeanor rather than an internal feeling.

FORMS:

  • おどおどする: verb form
  • おどおどした: past/attributive (おどおどした目(め)つき, a timid look)
  • おどおどと: adverbial (おどおどと話(はな)す, to speak timidly)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おどおどした態度(たいど): a timid attitude
  • おどおどした声(こえ): a nervous, trembling voice
  • おどおどした表情(ひょうじょう): an anxious expression
  • おどおどと答(こた)える: to answer timidly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • びくびくする: to be nervous, jumpy — implies fear of something specific, being on edge expecting something bad
  • もじもじする: to be fidgety, bashful — lighter in tone, often used for shyness rather than fear
  • おろおろする: to be at a loss, bewildered — stronger sense of not knowing what to do in a crisis
