1. to understand; to comprehend; to grasp
To understand the content or meaning of something that has been communicated.
状況(じょうきょう)を了解(りょうかい)した。
I understood the situation.
事情(じじょう)は了解(りょうかい)しました。
I understand the circumstances.
彼(かれ)の意図(いと)を了解(りょうかい)するのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to understand his intentions.
2. to acknowledge; to agree; to roger
Used as an acknowledgment that one has understood a request or instruction and will comply. Commonly used as a short reply meaning 'understood' or 'got it.'
「了解(りょうかい)！」
"Roger!" / "Got it!"
了解(りょうかい)しました。すぐに対応(たいおう)します。
Understood. I'll take care of it right away.
予定(よてい)変更(へんこう)の件(けん)、了解(りょうかい)しました。調整(ちょうせい)しておきます。
Understood regarding the schedule change. I'll make the adjustments.

Extremely common in daily communication, especially in workplace and messaging contexts. The standalone 了解(りょうかい) or 了解(りょうかい)です is a standard casual-to-neutral acknowledgment.

USAGE NOTE:
There is a widespread belief that 了解(りょうかい)しました is rude to use with superiors, and that 承知(しょうち)しました should be used instead. While this is debated among language experts, many Japanese people follow this convention in business settings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 了解(りょうかい)しました: understood (polite)
  • 了解(りょうかい)です: got it (casual-polite)
  • 了解(りょうかい)！: roger! (casual)
  • ご了解(りょうかい)ください: please understand/accept

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 承知(しょうち)する: to acknowledge — considered more appropriate with superiors
  • 理解(りかい)する: to understand — focuses on intellectual comprehension
  • 把握(はあく)する: to grasp — focuses on getting a complete picture
  • 納得(なっとく)する: to be convinced — implies emotional acceptance
