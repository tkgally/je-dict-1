〜にて

にて
particle
at; in; by means of (formal)
1. at; in (location)
Formal equivalent of で indicating a place where something occurs.
会議室(かいぎしつ)にて開催(かいさい)します。
It will be held in the conference room.
本日(ほんじつ)、東京(とうきょう)にて記者会見(きしゃかいけん)が行(おこな)われた。
A press conference was held in Tokyo today.
大阪(おおさか)駅(えき)前(まえ)にて集合(しゅうごう)してください。
Please gather in front of Osaka Station.
2. by; by means of; with
Formal equivalent of で indicating means or method.
メールにて連絡(れんらく)いたします。
I will contact you by email.
書面(しょめん)にて回答(かいとう)いたします。
We will respond in writing.
現金(げんきん)にてお支払(しはら)いいただけますか。
Could you pay in cash?

A formal particle that is the classical/literary equivalent of で. It was the standard form in classical Japanese and survives in modern formal contexts. It can indicate location (where something happens) and means/method (how something is done).

USAGE:

  • Replaces で in formal written contexts: announcements, business correspondence, official notices
  • Very common in event announcements: ～にて開催(かいさい) (held at ~)
  • Common in business communication: メールにて, 書面(しょめん)にて, 電話(でんわ)にて
  • Sounds overly formal or archaic in casual conversation
  • Sometimes seen at the end of letters or messages: 東京(とうきょう)にて (written in Tokyo)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～にて開催(かいさい): held at ~
  • メールにて: by email
  • 現地(げんち)にて: on-site, at the location
  • 本日(ほんじつ)にて終了(しゅうりょう): ending as of today

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • で: at, in, by — the standard casual/neutral equivalent
  • において: in, at — also formal but specifically for "in the context of"; more common in academic writing
  • をもって: with, by means of — very formal; used in official documents for deadlines and methods
