1. to disband; to dissolve (an organization, parliament, group)
Used for the formal dissolution of organizations, political bodies, or companies.
バンドは去年(きょねん)解散(かいさん)した。
The band broke up last year.
首相(しゅしょう)は衆議院(しゅうぎいん)を解散(かいさん)した。
The prime minister dissolved the House of Representatives.
その会社(かいしゃ)は経営(けいえい)不振(ふしん)で解散(かいさん)することになった。
The company was dissolved due to poor management.
2. to break up; to disperse (of a gathering)
Used when a group of people gathered for a meeting, event, or outing goes their separate ways.
では、今日(きょう)はここで解散(かいさん)しましょう。
Well then, let's call it a day here.
会議(かいぎ)は三時(さんじ)に解散(かいさん)した。
The meeting broke up at three o'clock.
遠足(えんそく)の帰(かえ)りは駅前(えきまえ)で解散(かいさん)することになっている。
We're supposed to break up in front of the station on the way back from the field trip.

A versatile word used both for formal dissolution and casual dispersal. The noun form 解散(かいさん) is often used on its own as a command or announcement: 解散(かいさん)！(Dismissed!).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国会(こっかい)を解散(かいさん)する: to dissolve parliament
  • 現地(げんち)解散(かいさん): break up at the location (i.e., no group return)
  • 自主(じしゅ)解散(かいさん): voluntary dissolution

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 散会(さんかい): adjournment (of a formal meeting)
  • 別(わか)れる: to part ways (more personal)
  • 解体(かいたい)する: to dismantle (physical structures or organizations)
