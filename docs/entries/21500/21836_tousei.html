1. control; regulation; coordination
the act of controlling, regulating, or bringing under unified command; systematic control over activities or resources
価格(かかく)を統制(とうせい)する。
To control prices.
政府(せいふ)が物資(ぶっし)の統制(とうせい)を行(おこな)った。
The government carried out controls on materials.
戦時中(せんじちゅう)は食料(しょくりょう)が統制(とうせい)されていた。
During wartime, food was under government control.
統制(とうせい)のとれたチームは強(つよ)い。
A well-coordinated team is strong.

Implies centralized or systematic control, often by an authority. Frequently used in political, military, and economic contexts. The word carries connotations of top-down regulation, making it common in discussions of wartime policies and government intervention.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 統制(とうせい)を取(と)る: to maintain coordination
  • 統制(とうせい)が取(と)れている: to be well-coordinated
  • 価格(かかく)統制(とうせい): price control
  • 統制(とうせい)経済(けいざい): controlled economy
  • 品質(ひんしつ)統制(とうせい): quality control

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 管理(かんり): management — broader, covering day-to-day administration
  • 規制(きせい): regulation — focuses on rules and restrictions, often legal
  • 制御(せいぎょ): control — more technical, used for machines and systems
