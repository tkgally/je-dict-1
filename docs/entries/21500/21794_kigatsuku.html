気(き)がつく

きがつく
expression
to notice; to realize; to become aware
1. to notice; to realize; to become aware of
To perceive or become conscious of something that was not previously noticed.
間違(まちが)いに気(き)がついた。
I noticed the mistake.
彼(かれ)がいないことに、誰(だれ)も気(き)がつかなかった。
Nobody noticed that he was gone.
駅(えき)に着(つ)いてから、財布(さいふ)を忘(わす)れたことに気(き)がついて慌(あわ)てた。
After arriving at the station, I realized I had forgotten my wallet and panicked.
2. to be attentive; to be considerate; to be thoughtful
To be naturally observant of others' needs and feelings.
彼女(かのじょ)はよく気(き)がつく人(ひと)だ。
She is a very attentive person.
気(き)がつく子(こ)だね、ありがとう。
What a thoughtful child — thank you.
周(まわ)りの人(ひと)の変化(へんか)にすぐ気(き)がつくのは、彼女(かのじょ)のいいところだ。
One of her good traits is that she quickly notices changes in the people around her.

A very common expression composed of 気(き) (awareness, attention) + が + つく (to attach, to arrive at). Describes the moment of becoming aware of something, or a person's quality of being naturally attentive. Also written as 気(き)が付(つ)く.

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 pattern: ～に気(き)がつく (to notice ~)
  • Sense 2 is used as a personality trait: 気(き)がつく人(ひと) (a considerate person)
  • The contracted form 気付(きづ)く is more common in writing
  • Past form 気(き)がついた is very frequently used

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 間違(まちが)いに気(き)がつく: to notice a mistake
  • 変化(へんか)に気(き)がつく: to notice a change
  • ～ことに気(き)がつく: to realize that ~
  • よく気(き)がつく: to be very attentive/considerate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気付(きづ)く: to notice, realize — contracted form; same meaning, more common in written Japanese
  • 気(き)にする: to worry about, care about — different nuance; focuses on concern rather than noticing
  • 気(き)になる: to be on one's mind — something catches attention and lingers
  • 感(かん)づく: to sense, have a hunch — implies intuiting rather than clearly noticing
