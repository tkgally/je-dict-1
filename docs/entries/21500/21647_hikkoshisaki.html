引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)

ひっこしさき
noun
new place to move to; moving destination
1. new place to move to; moving destination
The place where someone is moving to or has moved to. Refers to the destination of a residential or office move.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)はもう決(き)まった？
Have you decided where you're moving to?
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)の住所(じゅうしょ)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the address of your new place.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)が駅(えき)から遠(とお)くて、毎朝(まいあさ)バスに乗(の)らなければならない。
My new place is far from the station, so I have to take the bus every morning.

Compound of 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し (moving, relocation) + 先(さき) (destination). Used when talking about the place someone is relocating to.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)が決(き)まる: the new place is decided
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)を探(さが)す: to look for a new place to live
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)の住所(じゅうしょ): the address of the new place

RELATED TERMS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し: the act of moving
  • 転居先(てんきょさき): new address (more formal)
  • 移転先(いてんさき): relocation destination (often for businesses)
