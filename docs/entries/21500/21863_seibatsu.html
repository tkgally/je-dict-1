Composed of 征(せい) (conquer, subjugate) and 伐(ばつ) (attack, punish). Implies a righteous or authoritative campaign against an enemy or rebel. Common in historical texts and folk tales, especially the story of 桃太郎(ももたろう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鬼(おに)征伐(せいばつ): demon slaying (as in Momotaro)
  • 蝦夷(えぞ)征伐(せいばつ): subjugation of the Ezo people
  • 征伐(せいばつ)に向(む)かう: to set out on a punitive expedition
  • 征伐(せいばつ)軍(ぐん): subjugation army

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 討伐(とうばつ): subjugation, suppression — very similar, often interchangeable in historical contexts
  • 征服(せいふく): conquest — emphasizes bringing under one's rule
  • 平定(へいてい): pacification — focuses on restoring peace after conquest
