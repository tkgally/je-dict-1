名(な)もなき

なもなき
expression
nameless; unknown; obscure
1. nameless; unknown; obscure; unnamed
A literary expression meaning without a name, unknown, or humble. Used to modify nouns and conveys a poetic or elevated tone.
名(な)もなき花(はな)が咲(さ)いている。
Nameless flowers are blooming.
名(な)もなき兵士(へいし)たちの犠牲(ぎせい)を忘(わす)れてはならない。
We must not forget the sacrifices of the unknown soldiers.
名(な)もなき人々(ひとびと)の日々(ひび)の努力(どりょく)が社会(しゃかい)を支(ささ)えている。
The daily efforts of ordinary, unknown people support society.

A literary or poetic expression meaning "nameless" or "unknown." Composed of 名(な) (name) + も (even/also) + なき (classical negative form of ある, meaning "without"). The classical なき gives it a formal, literary feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名(な)もなき花(はな): nameless flowers
  • 名(な)もなき人(ひと): unknown person; ordinary person
  • 名(な)もなき英雄(えいゆう): unsung hero
  • 名(な)もなき兵士(へいし): unknown soldier

REGISTER:
This is a literary expression. In everyday speech, 名前(なまえ)のない or 無名(むめい)の would be used instead. 名(な)もなき appears in literature, poetry, titles of works, and formal rhetoric.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無名(むめい): anonymous, unknown — neutral, everyday register
  • 名(な)もない: nameless — modern equivalent, less literary
