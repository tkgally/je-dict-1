1. conjecture; inference; guessing; surmise
The act of forming an opinion or conclusion based on incomplete information. Used in both everyday and grammatical contexts.
推量(すいりょう)にすぎない。
It's nothing more than conjecture.
彼(かれ)の行動(こうどう)から推量(すいりょう)するしかない。
We can only guess from his behavior.
日本語(にほんご)の文法(ぶんぽう)で「だろう」は推量(すいりょう)の表現(ひょうげん)として使(つか)われる。
In Japanese grammar, 'darou' is used as an expression of conjecture.

Combines 推(すい) (infer, guess) and 量(りょう) (measure, gauge). Can function as a noun or with する as a verb meaning 'to conjecture.' Frequently encountered in Japanese grammar study where 推量(すいりょう) refers to the grammatical mood of speculation or probability (e.g., the だろう/でしょう forms).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 推量(すいりょう)する: to conjecture, to surmise
  • 推量(すいりょう)にすぎない: to be nothing more than conjecture
  • 推量(すいりょう)の域(いき)を出(で)ない: to not go beyond the realm of speculation
  • 推量(すいりょう)表現(ひょうげん): conjectural expression (grammar)
  • 推量(すいりょう)形(けい): conjectural form (grammar)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推測(すいそく): guess, conjecture — more general term for guessing based on evidence
  • 憶測(おくそく): speculation — often implies baseless or unfounded guessing
  • 推理(すいり): reasoning, deduction — implies logical steps rather than simple guessing
