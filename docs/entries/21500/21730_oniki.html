音域(おんいき)

おんいき
noun
vocal range; pitch range; register
1. vocal range; pitch range; register; range of tones
The span of pitches from lowest to highest that a voice or musical instrument can produce. Commonly discussed in music, singing lessons, and karaoke.
彼女(かのじょ)は音域(おんいき)が広(ひろ)い。
She has a wide vocal range.
この曲(きょく)は音域(おんいき)が高(たか)くて歌(うた)いにくい。
This song has a high pitch range and is hard to sing.
フルートの音域(おんいき)は約(やく)3オクターブあり、高音(こうおん)楽器(がっき)の中(なか)でも表現力(ひょうげんりょく)が豊(ゆた)かだ。
The flute has a range of about three octaves and is expressively rich even among high-pitched instruments.

Refers to the range of pitches that a voice or instrument can produce. Combines 音(おん) (sound) and 域(いき) (area, range).

USAGE:

  • Used for both human voices and musical instruments
  • Commonly discussed in music, singing, and karaoke contexts
  • Described as 広(ひろ)い (wide) or 狭(せま)い (narrow), 高(たか)い (high) or 低(ひく)い (low)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音域(おんいき)が広(ひろ)い: wide vocal/pitch range
  • 音域(おんいき)が狭(せま)い: narrow range
  • 高(こう)音域(おんいき): high register
  • 低(てい)音域(おんいき): low register
  • 音域(おんいき)を広(ひろ)げる: to expand one's range

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 声域(せいいき): vocal range — specifically for the human voice
  • 音程(おんてい): pitch, interval — the distance between two notes
  • レンジ: range — the loanword, sometimes used in music contexts
