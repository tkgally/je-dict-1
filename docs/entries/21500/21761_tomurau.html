1. to mourn; to hold a memorial service; to console the spirit of the deceased
To express grief for the dead and perform rituals or acts to honor their memory. Encompasses both the emotional aspect of mourning and the religious/ceremonial aspect of memorial services.
亡(な)くなった友人(ゆうじん)を弔(とむら)う。
To mourn a deceased friend.
戦没者(せんぼつしゃ)の霊(れい)を弔(とむら)う式典(しきてん)が行(おこな)われた。
A ceremony was held to honor the spirits of the war dead.
花(はな)を手向(たむ)けて故人(こじん)を弔(とむら)い、静(しず)かに祈(いの)りを捧(ささ)げた。
We offered flowers to mourn the deceased and quietly offered our prayers.

A godan verb (u-row conjugation: 弔(とむら)わない, 弔(とむら)って). The word carries a solemn, respectful tone and is used in formal and literary contexts about death and memorial. In everyday speech, 冥福(めいふく)を祈(いの)る (to pray for someone's rest in peace) is more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 死者(ししゃ)を弔(とむら)う: to mourn the dead
  • 霊(れい)を弔(とむら)う: to console a spirit
  • 弔(とむら)いの言葉(ことば): words of condolence
  • 弔(とむら)い合戦(がっせん): a battle fought to avenge the dead

RELATED TERMS:

  • 弔辞(ちょうじ): funeral address, eulogy
  • 弔電(ちょうでん): condolence telegram
  • 弔問(ちょうもん): condolence visit
  • 追悼(ついとう): memorial, commemoration
