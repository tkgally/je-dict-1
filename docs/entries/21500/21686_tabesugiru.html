1. to overeat; to eat too much
Compound of 食(た)べる (to eat) + 過(す)ぎる (to exceed). A very common everyday word.
食(た)べ過(す)ぎてお腹(なか)が痛(いた)い。
I ate too much and my stomach hurts.
昨日(きのう)の忘年会(ぼうねんかい)で食(た)べ過(す)ぎた。
I overate at yesterday's year-end party.
甘(あま)いものを食(た)べ過(す)ぎないように気(き)をつけている。
I'm careful not to eat too many sweets.

One of the most common ～過(す)ぎる compounds. Follows the productive V-stem + 過(す)ぎる pattern that means "to do something too much." Conjugates as an ichidan verb.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 食(た)べ過(す)ぎに注意(ちゅうい): watch out for overeating
  • 食(た)べ過(す)ぎて気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い: feel sick from overeating
  • つい食(た)べ過(す)ぎてしまう: end up overeating (unintentionally)

RELATED PATTERNS:

  • 飲(の)み過(す)ぎる: to drink too much
  • 使(つか)い過(す)ぎる: to use too much
  • 働(はたら)き過(す)ぎる: to overwork
