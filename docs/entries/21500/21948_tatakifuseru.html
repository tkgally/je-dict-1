1. to knock down; to beat down; to strike down
To hit someone with enough force to knock them to the ground or into a prone position.
一撃(いちげき)で相手(あいて)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せた。
He knocked his opponent down with a single blow.
侵入者(しんにゅうしゃ)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せて取(と)り押(おさ)えた。
He knocked the intruder down and restrained him.
武術(ぶじゅつ)の達人(たつじん)は瞬時(しゅんじ)に三人(さんにん)の敵(てき)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せた。
The martial arts master floored three enemies in an instant.
2. to thoroughly defeat; to crush (an argument or opponent)
To overwhelm or silence someone completely, figuratively beating them into submission.
反論(はんろん)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せる。
To crush a counterargument.
圧倒的(あっとうてき)な証拠(しょうこ)で相手(あいて)の主張(しゅちょう)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せた。
He crushed the opponent's claims with overwhelming evidence.
批判(ひはん)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せるだけでは真(しん)の解決(かいけつ)にはならない。
Simply crushing criticism won't lead to a real solution.

A compound verb formed from 叩(たた)く (to strike, to hit) and 伏(ふ)せる (to lay face down). The combination means to strike someone down to the ground.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一撃(いちげき)で叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せる: to knock down with one blow
  • 相手(あいて)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せる: to knock down one's opponent
  • 反論(はんろん)を叩(たた)き伏(ふ)せる: to crush a counterargument

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 打(う)ち倒(たお)す: to knock down — similar meaning, slightly more neutral
  • 殴(なぐ)り倒(たお)す: to punch and knock down — specifically with fists
  • 叩(たた)き潰(つぶ)す: to smash, to crush — more destructive, implies total destruction
