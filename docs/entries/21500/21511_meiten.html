名店(めいてん)

めいてん
noun
famous shop; renowned restaurant; celebrated store
1. famous shop; renowned restaurant; celebrated store
A shop or restaurant widely recognized for its quality, history, or reputation. Often used for long-established food establishments.
あの店(みせ)はラーメンの名店(めいてん)だ。
That shop is a famous ramen restaurant.
全国(ぜんこく)の名店(めいてん)が集(あつ)まるイベントが開催(かいさい)された。
An event featuring famous shops from across the country was held.
行列(ぎょうれつ)ができるほどの名店(めいてん)で、予約(よやく)なしでは入(はい)れないことが多(おお)い。
It's such a famous restaurant that lines form, and you often can't get in without a reservation.

Composed of 名(めい) (famous, distinguished) + 店(てん) (shop, store). Particularly common in food and dining contexts, where it refers to restaurants with outstanding reputations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名店(めいてん)街(がい): famous restaurant district
  • 名店(めいてん)揃(ぞろ)い: a lineup of famous shops
  • 隠(かく)れた名店(めいてん): hidden gem restaurant
  • 老舗(しにせ)名店(めいてん): long-established famous shop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 老舗(しにせ): long-established shop — emphasizes the history and tradition of a business, not necessarily its fame
  • 人気店(にんきてん): popular shop — emphasizes current popularity rather than established reputation
  • 有名店(ゆうめいてん): well-known shop — more straightforward; 名店(めいてん) implies quality in addition to fame

USAGE:
Frequently appears in food magazines, TV shows, and online reviews. Often modified by a food type: ラーメンの名店(めいてん), すしの名店(めいてん), etc.
