独善的(どくぜんてき)

どくぜんてき
adjective-na
self-righteous; complacent; dogmatic
1. self-righteous; complacent; dogmatic
believing one's own views or actions to be correct without considering other perspectives; acting in a self-satisfied way that disregards others' opinions
独善的(どくぜんてき)な態度(たいど)は嫌(きら)われる。
A self-righteous attitude is disliked.
彼(かれ)の意見(いけん)は独善的(どくぜんてき)で他人(たにん)の話(はなし)を聞(き)かない。
His opinions are self-righteous and he doesn't listen to others.
独善的(どくぜんてき)な判断(はんだん)で物事(ものごと)を進(すす)めると、チーム全体(ぜんたい)に悪影響(あくえいきょう)が出(で)る。
If you push things forward based on self-righteous judgment, it negatively affects the whole team.
リーダーが独善的(どくぜんてき)になると、部下(ぶか)はついてこなくなる。
When a leader becomes self-righteous, subordinates will stop following.

Always carries a negative connotation. Describes people who are convinced of their own correctness and dismiss or ignore other viewpoints. Often used to criticize leaders, authority figures, or anyone who acts unilaterally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独善的(どくぜんてき)な態度(たいど): self-righteous attitude
  • 独善的(どくぜんてき)な考(かんが)え: self-righteous thinking
  • 独善的(どくぜんてき)な行動(こうどう): self-righteous behavior
  • 独善的(どくぜんてき)な判断(はんだん): self-righteous judgment
  • 独善的(どくぜんてき)になる: to become self-righteous

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 身勝手(みがって): selfish — focuses on acting for one's own convenience without considering others
  • 傲慢(ごうまん): arrogant, haughty — emphasizes looking down on others from a sense of superiority
  • 自己(じこ)中心的(ちゅうしんてき): self-centered — describes putting oneself at the center of everything
  • 独断的(どくだんてき): unilateral, arbitrary — emphasizes making decisions alone without consulting others

ETYMOLOGY:
Composed of 独善(どくぜん) (self-complacency) + 的(てき). 独(どく) means alone and 善(ぜん) means good/virtue — literally "alone in one's goodness."
