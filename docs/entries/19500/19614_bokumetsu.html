撲滅(ぼくめつ)

ぼくめつ
noun, verb (suru)
eradication; extermination; stamping out
1. eradication; extermination; stamping out
Completely eliminating or wiping out something undesirable, such as disease, crime, or social problems. A strong, decisive word implying total elimination.
犯罪(はんざい)の撲滅(ぼくめつ)を目指(めざ)す。
We aim to eradicate crime.
飲酒(いんしゅ)運転(うんてん)撲滅(ぼくめつ)キャンペーンが始(はじ)まった。
A campaign to stamp out drunk driving has begun.
国際(こくさい)社会(しゃかい)は貧困(ひんこん)の撲滅(ぼくめつ)に向(む)けて協力(きょうりょく)している。
The international community is cooperating toward the eradication of poverty.

Combines 撲(ぼく) (strike/beat) and 滅(めつ) (destroy/extinguish), conveying violent, thorough elimination. Used almost exclusively for negative things that society wants to eliminate entirely.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 犯罪(はんざい)撲滅(ぼくめつ): crime eradication
  • 撲滅(ぼくめつ)キャンペーン: eradication campaign
  • 撲滅(ぼくめつ)運動(うんどう): eradication movement
  • 貧困(ひんこん)の撲滅(ぼくめつ): eradication of poverty
  • 差別(さべつ)の撲滅(ぼくめつ): elimination of discrimination
  • 撲滅(ぼくめつ)を目指(めざ)す: aim for eradication

USAGE:
Often seen in headlines, slogans, and official campaign names. The word carries a strong, determined tone and is typically used in public discourse about social issues.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 根絶(こんぜつ): eradication, rooting out — emphasizes pulling out by the roots; often used for diseases and deeply entrenched problems
  • 排除(はいじょ): exclusion, elimination — removing something from a group or situation, less total than 撲滅(ぼくめつ)
  • 駆除(くじょ): extermination — specifically for pests, vermin, or harmful organisms
