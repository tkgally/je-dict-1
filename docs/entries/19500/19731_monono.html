ものの

ものの
particle
although; even though; but
1. although; even though; but
A conjunction indicating that while the preceding clause is true, the outcome or situation described in the following clause is contrary to what would be expected. The speaker acknowledges one fact but presents a contrasting result.
買(か)ったものの、使(つか)っていない。
I bought it, but I haven't used it.
説明(せつめい)を聞(き)いたものの、よく分(わ)からなかった。
I listened to the explanation, but I didn't really understand.
転職(てんしょく)したものの、前(まえ)の会社(かいしゃ)の方(ほう)がよかったと思(おも)うことがある。
Although I changed jobs, I sometimes think my previous company was better.

A literary-leaning conjunction that connects two clauses where the second contradicts or falls short of what the first implies. Often conveys a sense of disappointment or unmet expectation.

GRAMMAR:

Attaches to the past tense (た-form) of verbs and adjectives:

  • 買(か)ったものの (bought, but...)
  • 高(たか)かったものの (it was expensive, but...)

Sometimes used with present tense in more formal writing: 分(わ)かるものの (although one understands...).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • けれども — although; more neutral and versatile, used in both speech and writing
  • のに — even though; often carries emotional disappointment or frustration
  • とはいえ — having said that; more formal, used to introduce a counterpoint
  • にもかかわらず — despite; very formal and objective

REGISTER:
Slightly formal and often written. Common in essays, news, and reflective narration. Less common in casual daily conversation, where けど or のに would be preferred.
