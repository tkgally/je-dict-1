1. illustration, showing in a diagram
The act of explaining or representing something visually using a diagram, chart, or figure.
結果(けっか)を図示(ずし)する。
To illustrate the results in a diagram.
関係(かんけい)を図示(ずし)すると分(わ)かりやすい。
It's easier to understand when you illustrate the relationships in a diagram.
下(した)の図(ず)に図示(ずし)したように、データは三(みっ)つのグループに分(わ)かれる。
As illustrated in the diagram below, the data is divided into three groups.

A formal, technical word used in academic papers, textbooks, and presentations. Functions as a suru verb (図示(ずし)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 図示(ずし)する — to illustrate, to show in a diagram
  • 図示(ずし)したように — as illustrated
  • 図示(ずし)した結果(けっか) — the illustrated results

USAGE:
Primarily found in written academic and technical contexts. When authors want to reference a visual representation in a paper or report, they use 図示(ずし)する. In everyday language, 図(ず)で示(しめ)す (to show with a diagram) is more natural.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 図解(ずかい) — illustrated explanation (the diagram itself, or the act of explaining with diagrams)
  • 表示(ひょうじ) — display, indication (broader, includes digital displays)
