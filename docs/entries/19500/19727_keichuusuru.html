1. to devote; to concentrate; to pour one's energy into
To direct all of one's attention, effort, or resources toward a single purpose or goal.
全力(ぜんりょく)を傾注(けいちゅう)する。
To devote all one's energy.
研究(けんきゅう)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾注(けいちゅう)してきた。
I have poured my passion into research.
会社(かいしゃ)は新製品(しんせいひん)の開発(かいはつ)に多大(ただい)な資源(しげん)を傾注(けいちゅう)している。
The company is devoting enormous resources to developing new products.

Composed of 傾(けい) (to lean, incline) + 注(ちゅう) (to pour). The image is of 'tilting and pouring' one's efforts into something, like pouring water from a vessel. A formal, literary verb often used in speeches, business contexts, and written prose.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The thing being devoted (effort, passion, resources) is marked with を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全力(ぜんりょく)を傾注(けいちゅう)する — to devote all one's strength
  • 情熱(じょうねつ)を傾注(けいちゅう)する — to pour one's passion into
  • 精力(せいりょく)を傾注(けいちゅう)する — to devote one's energy to
  • 注意(ちゅうい)を傾注(けいちゅう)する — to focus one's attention on

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注(そそ)ぐ — to pour, to devote; more everyday and versatile
  • 集中(しゅうちゅう)する — to concentrate; focuses on mental attention
  • 献身(けんしん)する — to devote oneself; implies self-sacrifice for others
