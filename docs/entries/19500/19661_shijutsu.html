1. surgical procedure; medical treatment; operation
A medical or therapeutic procedure performed on a patient, ranging from surgery to non-invasive treatments like massage or acupuncture.
施術(しじゅつ)は無事(ぶじ)に終(お)わった。
The procedure was completed successfully.
施術(しじゅつ)後(ご)は安静(あんせい)にしてください。
Please rest after the procedure.
この美容(びよう)クリニックでは、最新(さいしん)の機器(きき)を使(つか)った施術(しじゅつ)を受(う)けることができる。
At this cosmetic clinic, you can receive treatments using the latest equipment.

A compound of 施(し) (to perform, to administer) and 術(じゅつ) (technique, art). Used broadly for both medical procedures and therapeutic treatments.

USAGE:
While 手術(しゅじゅつ) specifically means surgery (cutting), 施術(しじゅつ) has a wider scope that includes non-surgical treatments such as chiropractic, acupuncture, massage, and cosmetic procedures. It is the standard term used by clinics, salons, and therapists.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 施術(しじゅつ)を受(う)ける — to receive a treatment/procedure
  • 施術(しじゅつ)を行(おこな)う — to perform a procedure
  • 施術(しじゅつ)後(ご) — after the procedure
  • 施術(しじゅつ)時間(じかん) — treatment time/duration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手術(しゅじゅつ) — surgery; specifically invasive surgical operations
  • 治療(ちりょう) — treatment; broader term for medical care and healing
  • 処置(しょち) — medical treatment; often for emergency or first-aid measures
