1. naturalization
The legal process of acquiring citizenship of a country one was not born in.
日本(にほん)に帰化(きか)した。
I became a naturalized Japanese citizen.
帰化(きか)申請(しんせい)の手続(てつづ)きは複雑(ふくざつ)だ。
The naturalization application process is complicated.
帰化(きか)して十年(じゅうねん)が経(た)つが、今(いま)でも母国(ぼこく)のことを思(おも)い出(だ)す。
Ten years have passed since I became naturalized, but I still think about my home country.
2. naturalization (biology)
The process by which a non-native species becomes established in a new environment.
帰化(きか)植物(しょくぶつ)が増(ふ)えている。
Naturalized plants are increasing.
セイタカアワダチソウは代表的(だいひょうてき)な帰化(きか)植物(しょくぶつ)だ。
Goldenrod is a typical naturalized plant.
外来種(がいらいしゅ)の中(なか)には帰化(きか)して在来種(ざいらいしゅ)を脅(おびや)かすものもある。
Among introduced species, some become naturalized and threaten native species.

Has both a legal and a biological meaning. Also functions as a suru verb (帰化(きか)する). The legal sense is more commonly encountered in everyday contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 帰化(きか)する — to become naturalized
  • 帰化(きか)申請(しんせい) — naturalization application
  • 帰化(きか)人(じん) — naturalized citizen
  • 帰化(きか)植物(しょくぶつ) — naturalized plant
  • 帰化(きか)動物(どうぶつ) — naturalized animal

USAGE:
In legal contexts, 帰化(きか) specifically means acquiring citizenship through application (as opposed to by birth or marriage). In Japan, the naturalization process (帰化(きか)申請(しんせい)) is handled by the Ministry of Justice and requires meeting residence, conduct, and financial requirements.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国籍(こくせき)取得(しゅとく) — acquisition of citizenship (broader term)
  • 永住(えいじゅう) — permanent residence (different from citizenship)
