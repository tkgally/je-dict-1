岐路(きろ)

きろ
noun
crossroads, turning point
1. crossroads, fork in the road
A point where a road branches into two or more paths.
岐路(きろ)で立(た)ち止(ど)まった。
I stopped at the fork in the road.
道(みち)が岐路(きろ)に分(わ)かれている。
The road splits at a fork.
山道(やまみち)の岐路(きろ)で右(みぎ)の道(みち)を選(えら)んだ。
I chose the right path at the fork in the mountain trail.
2. turning point, critical juncture
A decisive moment in life or a situation where an important choice must be made.
人生(じんせい)の岐路(きろ)に立(た)つ。
To stand at a crossroads in life.
会社(かいしゃ)は今(いま)、重要(じゅうよう)な岐路(きろ)に立(た)たされている。
The company now finds itself at a critical juncture.
転職(てんしょく)するかどうか、人生(じんせい)の岐路(きろ)で悩(なや)んでいる。
I'm agonizing at a crossroads in life over whether to change jobs.

Literally a place where a road branches. Most commonly used figuratively to describe a critical decision point in life, career, or events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人生(じんせい)の岐路(きろ) — crossroads in life
  • 岐路(きろ)に立(た)つ — to stand at a crossroads
  • 岐路(きろ)に立(た)たされる — to be placed at a crossroads
  • 重要(じゅうよう)な岐路(きろ) — a critical juncture

USAGE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is far more common than the literal one. The phrase 人生(じんせい)の岐路(きろ)に立(た)つ is a set expression frequently used when discussing major life decisions.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分岐点(ぶんきてん) — branching point (more technical or analytical)
  • 分(わ)かれ道(みち) — forked road (more literal, everyday)
