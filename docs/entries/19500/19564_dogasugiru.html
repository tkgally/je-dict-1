度(ど)が過(す)ぎる

どがすぎる
expression
to go too far; to be excessive; to overdo it
1. to go too far; to be excessive; to overdo it
Something exceeds an acceptable or reasonable limit. Used to criticize behavior, actions, or situations that have gone beyond what is appropriate.
いたずらも度(ど)が過(す)ぎる。
The pranks have gone too far.
冗談(じょうだん)も度(ど)が過(す)ぎると、人(ひと)を傷(きず)つけることがある。
When jokes go too far, they can hurt people.
心配(しんぱい)するのはわかるけど、度(ど)が過(す)ぎると子供(こども)の自立(じりつ)を妨(さまた)げることになる。
I understand being worried, but when it goes too far, it can hinder a child's independence.

Combines 度(ど) (degree, extent) with 過(す)ぎる (to exceed). Expresses the idea that something has crossed the line of what is acceptable. Almost always used in a critical or cautionary tone.

USAGE:
Typically follows a noun with the particle も: 冗談(じょうだん)も度(ど)が過(す)ぎる (even jokes can go too far). The も adds the nuance of "even X has limits." Can also be used as a modifier: 度(ど)が過(す)ぎた行為(こうい) (an act that went too far).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冗談(じょうだん)も度(ど)が過(す)ぎる — jokes have gone too far
  • いたずらも度(ど)が過(す)ぎる — pranks have gone too far
  • 心配(しんぱい)も度(ど)が過(す)ぎる — worrying has gone too far
  • 度(ど)が過(す)ぎた行為(こうい) — an excessive act

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • やり過(す)ぎる — to overdo it; more casual, broader application
  • 行(い)き過(す)ぎる — to go too far; similar meaning, often used for actions or policies
  • 過度(かど)な — excessive; formal adjective form
