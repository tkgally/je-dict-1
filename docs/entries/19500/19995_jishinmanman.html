自信満々(じしんまんまん)

じしんまんまん
noun
full of self-confidence; brimming with confidence
1. full of self-confidence; brimming with confidence
Describes someone who is very confident, sometimes to the point of appearing overconfident or cocky. Used as a の-modifier or with a copula.
彼(かれ)は自信満々(じしんまんまん)だった。
He was full of confidence.
自信満々(じしんまんまん)の笑顔(えがお)で舞台(ぶたい)に立(た)った。
She stepped onto the stage with a confident smile.
試験(しけん)の前(まえ)は自信満々(じしんまんまん)だったのに、結果(けっか)は散々(さんざん)だった。
I was brimming with confidence before the exam, but the results were terrible.

A compound of 自信(じしん) (self-confidence) and 満々(まんまん) (overflowing, brimming). The 満々(まんまん) suffix indicates that confidence is at maximum level.

USAGE:
Can be used positively (admiring someone's confidence) or with a slightly negative nuance (suggesting overconfidence or arrogance). Context determines the tone. Typically used as a の-modifier or with だ/です.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自信満々(じしんまんまん)の表情(ひょうじょう) — a confident expression
  • 自信満々(じしんまんまん)に答(こた)える — to answer with full confidence
  • 自信満々(じしんまんまん)で臨(のぞ)む — to face something with full confidence

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自信(じしん)がある — to have confidence (neutral)
  • 堂々(どうどう)としている — to be composed and dignified
  • やる気(き)満々(まんまん) — full of motivation (parallel structure with 満々(まんまん))
