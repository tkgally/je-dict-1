画鋲(がびょう)

がびょう
noun
thumbtack; drawing pin
1. thumbtack; drawing pin; pushpin
A short pin with a flat, broad head used to fasten papers or posters to a board or wall.
画鋲(がびょう)でポスターを貼(は)った。
I pinned up the poster with thumbtacks.
床(ゆか)に落(お)ちていた画鋲(がびょう)を踏(ふ)んでしまった。
I accidentally stepped on a thumbtack that had fallen on the floor.
掲示板(けいじばん)に画鋲(がびょう)で連絡(れんらく)事項(じこう)を留(と)めてください。
Please pin the notices to the bulletin board with thumbtacks.

Combines 画(が) (picture/drawing) and 鋲(びょう) (tack/rivet). Originally used to pin drawings and documents, the name persists even though thumbtacks are now used for many purposes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 画鋲(がびょう)で留(と)める: fasten with a thumbtack
  • 画鋲(がびょう)で貼(は)る: pin up with thumbtacks
  • 画鋲(がびょう)を刺(さ)す: stick in a thumbtack
  • 画鋲(がびょう)を抜(ぬ)く: pull out a thumbtack
  • 画鋲(がびょう)を踏(ふ)む: step on a thumbtack

USAGE:
A common everyday word learned early in school life. Japanese classrooms typically use 画鋲(がびょう) to attach items to cork bulletin boards (掲示板(けいじばん)). The word is universally understood across Japan, though some regions may use the older reading 押(お)しピン.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ピン: pin — a more general loanword; can refer to various types of pins
  • 押(お)しピン: pushpin — dialectal variant (mainly Kansai region) for the same item
