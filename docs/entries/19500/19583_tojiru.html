1. to bind; to staple; to fasten together
To attach sheets of paper or other materials together by stapling, sewing, or binding.
書類(しょるい)をホチキスで綴(と)じた。
I stapled the documents together.
プリントを一(ひと)つにまとめて綴(と)じてください。
Please gather the printouts and bind them together.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)をファイルに綴(と)じて棚(たな)に戻(もど)しておいた。
I filed the report in a binder and put it back on the shelf.

An ichidan verb meaning to fasten pages or documents together. A common office and school vocabulary word. Not to be confused with the homophone 閉(と)じる (to close, to shut).

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 綴(つづり) (spelling, binding) has the 糸(いと) radical, reflecting the original meaning of sewing or stitching together.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書類(しょるい)を綴(と)じる — to bind documents
  • ファイルに綴(と)じる — to file in a binder
  • ホチキスで綴(と)じる — to staple together
  • 糸(いと)で綴(と)じる — to sew together

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 閉(と)じる — to close, to shut; a homophone with a completely different meaning (closing a door, a book, one's eyes, etc.)
  • 束(たば)ねる — to bundle together; used for grouping items into a bundle, while 綴(と)じる specifically implies fastening flat items like paper
