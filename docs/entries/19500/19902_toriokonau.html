1. to hold, to conduct, to perform (a ceremony or formal event)
To carry out a formal ceremony, ritual, or official event. A solemn, elevated word used almost exclusively for ceremonial occasions.
結婚式(けっこんしき)を執(と)り行(おこな)う。
To hold a wedding ceremony.
葬儀(そうぎ)は来週(らいしゅう)執(と)り行(おこな)われる予定(よてい)です。
The funeral is scheduled to be held next week.
神社(じんじゃ)で厳(おごそ)かに祭典(さいてん)が執(と)り行(おこな)われた。
A festival was solemnly conducted at the shrine.

A formal, elevated verb used almost exclusively for ceremonies, rituals, and official events. Not used for casual gatherings or everyday activities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 式(しき)を執(と)り行(おこな)う: to hold a ceremony
  • 結婚式(けっこんしき)を執(と)り行(おこな)う: to hold a wedding
  • 葬儀(そうぎ)を執(と)り行(おこな)う: to hold a funeral
  • 儀式(ぎしき)を執(と)り行(おこな)う: to perform a ritual
  • 祭典(さいてん)を執(と)り行(おこな)う: to conduct a festival

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 行(おこな)う: to carry out, to do — neutral and general-purpose
  • 催(もよお)す: to hold, to organize — for events, exhibitions, or parties
  • 挙行(きょこう)する: to hold (a ceremony) — formal written synonym, often used in announcements
