親(した)しみ

したしみ
noun
affection; intimacy; familiarity; friendliness
1. affection; intimacy; familiarity; friendliness
A warm, friendly feeling toward someone or something. The sense of closeness or approachability that makes someone feel comfortable.
親(した)しみを感(かん)じる。
To feel a sense of familiarity.
あの先生(せんせい)は親(した)しみやすい人(ひと)だ。
That teacher is an approachable person.
地元(じもと)の方言(ほうげん)を聞(き)くと、懐(なつ)かしさと親(した)しみがこみ上(あ)げてくる。
When I hear the local dialect, nostalgia and a warm sense of familiarity well up inside me.

The noun form of the verb 親(した)しむ (to feel close to, to be familiar with). Describes a warm, approachable quality in people, places, or things.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 親(した)しみを感(かん)じる: to feel familiarity/affection
  • 親(した)しみを込(こ)めて: with affection, affectionately
  • 親(した)しみやすい: approachable, easy to feel close to
  • 親(した)しみのある笑顔(えがお): a friendly smile
  • 親(した)しみが湧(わ)く: to feel warmth toward someone

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 愛着(あいちゃく): attachment (deeper emotional bond, often to objects or places)
  • 好意(こうい): goodwill, favorable feeling (more formal; can imply romantic interest)
  • 親近感(しんきんかん): feeling of closeness (similar but slightly more formal; often used toward public figures)
