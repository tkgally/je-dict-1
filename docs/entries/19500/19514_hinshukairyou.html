品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)

ひんしゅかいりょう
noun
selective breeding; breed improvement
1. selective breeding; breed improvement; cultivar development
The process of selectively breeding plants or animals to develop desired traits such as disease resistance, higher yield, better flavor, or improved appearance.
品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)で甘(あま)いイチゴが生(う)まれた。
Sweet strawberries were created through selective breeding.
品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)には長(なが)い年月(ねんげつ)がかかる。
Selective breeding takes many years.
日本(にほん)の米(こめ)は品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)を重(かさ)ねて、味(あじ)も収穫量(しゅうかくりょう)も大幅(おおはば)に向上(こうじょう)した。
Through repeated selective breeding, Japanese rice has greatly improved in both flavor and yield.

A compound of 品種(ひんしゅ) (breed, variety, cultivar) and 改良(かいりょう) (improvement). Used for both plant cultivation and animal breeding, this term covers traditional selective breeding methods as well as modern biotechnological approaches.

USAGE:
Frequently appears in discussions about agriculture, horticulture, animal husbandry, and food science. Japan has a long tradition of 品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう), particularly for rice, fruits, and ornamental plants. Used as a noun or with する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)を行(おこな)う — to carry out selective breeding
  • 品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)を重(かさ)ねる — to repeat selective breeding over generations
  • 品種改良(ひんしゅかいりょう)された品種(ひんしゅ) — an improved variety

RELATED TERMS:

  • 品種(ひんしゅ) — breed, variety, cultivar; the type of plant or animal
  • 遺伝子組(いでんしく)み換(か)え — genetic modification; a more controversial modern alternative
  • 交配(こうはい) — crossbreeding; one of the main techniques used in selective breeding
