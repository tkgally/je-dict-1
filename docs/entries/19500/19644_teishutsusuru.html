提出(ていしゅつ)する

ていしゅつする
suru verb
to submit; to present; to turn in
1. to submit, to present, to turn in, to file
To formally hand in or present documents, reports, applications, or other materials to an authority, organization, or instructor.
レポートを提出(ていしゅつ)する。
To submit a report.
書類(しょるい)は明日(あした)までに提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the documents by tomorrow.
確定(かくてい)申告(しんこく)の書類(しょるい)を税務署(ぜいむしょ)に提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I submitted the tax return documents to the tax office.

One of the most essential formal verbs for students, office workers, and anyone dealing with bureaucracy. Used constantly in school, work, and government contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 提出(ていしゅつ)期限(きげん): submission deadline
  • 提出(ていしゅつ)書類(しょるい): documents to be submitted
  • 提出(ていしゅつ)先(さき): place of submission
  • 提出(ていしゅつ)物(ぶつ): submitted items, homework/assignments
  • 法案(ほうあん)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a bill (legislation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出(だ)す: to turn in, to hand in — casual, everyday equivalent
  • 提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit — formal, used in official and academic contexts
  • 申請(しんせい)する: to apply for — specifically for requesting permission or a license
