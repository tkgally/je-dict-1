着実(ちゃくじつ)に

ちゃくじつに
adverb
steadily, surely
1. steadily, surely, step by step
Describes making reliable, consistent progress without rushing or cutting corners. Emphasizes that each step is solid and dependable.
着実(ちゃくじつ)に進(すす)む。
To make steady progress.
売(う)り上(あ)げが着実(ちゃくじつ)に伸(の)びている。
Sales are growing steadily.
彼(かれ)は着実(ちゃくじつ)に経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねてきた。
He has steadily accumulated experience over time.

Describes progress or action that is solid, reliable, and consistent rather than flashy or rapid. The underlying adjective 着実(ちゃくじつ) means 'steady, sound, reliable.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 着実(ちゃくじつ)に進(すす)む: to make steady progress
  • 着実(ちゃくじつ)に成長(せいちょう)する: to grow steadily
  • 着実(ちゃくじつ)に力(ちから)をつける: to steadily build strength
  • 着実(ちゃくじつ)に実績(じっせき)を上(あ)げる: to steadily produce results

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 確実(かくじつ)に: certainly, reliably — emphasizes certainty of outcome rather than steady process
  • 順調(じゅんちょう)に: smoothly — emphasizes lack of problems rather than solidity of each step
  • 地道(じみち)に: diligently, painstakingly — emphasizes patient, unglamorous effort
