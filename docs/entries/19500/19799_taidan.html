1. leaving a group; departing from a troupe or team
The act of leaving an organized group such as a sports team, theater troupe, musical group, or similar organization.
今季(こんき)限(かぎ)りで退団(たいだん)する。
He will leave the team at the end of this season.
人気(にんき)選手(せんしゅ)の退団(たいだん)がファンに衝撃(しょうげき)を与(あた)えた。
The popular player's departure shocked the fans.
彼女(かのじょ)は十年間(じゅうねんかん)在籍(ざいせき)した劇団(げきだん)を退団(たいだん)し、独立(どくりつ)した。
She left the theater troupe she had belonged to for ten years and went independent.

Most commonly used in the context of sports teams and theater troupes (especially 宝塚歌劇団(たからづかかげきだん)). The 団(だん) refers to an organized group or troupe.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 退団(たいだん)を発表(はっぴょう)する — to announce one's departure
  • 退団(たいだん)を決意(けつい)する — to decide to leave the group
  • 円満(えんまん)退団(たいだん) — amicable departure
  • 今季(こんき)限(かぎ)りで退団(たいだん) — leaving at the end of this season

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脱退(だったい) — withdrawal, secession (from a larger organization, treaty, or union; stronger nuance)
  • 退会(たいかい) — withdrawal from a club or association
  • 退社(たいしゃ) — leaving a company
  • 入団(にゅうだん) — joining a group (the opposite)
