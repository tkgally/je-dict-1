1. eating and drinking
The act of consuming food and drink, often implying indulgence or a social context.
飲(の)み食(く)いが好(す)きだ。
I love eating and drinking.
旅行(りょこう)先(さき)で飲(の)み食(く)いを楽(たの)しんだ。
I enjoyed eating and drinking at my travel destination.
飲(の)み食(く)いにお金(かね)をかけすぎて、貯金(ちょきん)ができない。
I spend too much on food and drink and can't save any money.

A compound of 飲(の)む (to drink) and 食(く)う (to eat, informal). The use of 食(く)う rather than 食(た)べる gives this word a casual, hearty feel. Also functions as a suru verb (飲(の)み食(く)いする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飲(の)み食(く)いする — to eat and drink
  • 飲(の)み食(く)いが激(はげ)しい — to eat and drink a lot
  • 飲(の)み食(く)いにお金(かね)をかける — to spend money on food and drink
  • 飲(の)み食(く)い放題(ほうだい) — all-you-can-eat-and-drink

REGISTER:
Informal and colloquial. Suitable for casual conversation but not for formal writing. The formal equivalent would be 飲食(いんしょく).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲食(いんしょく) — eating and drinking (formal, used in signs and official contexts)
  • 食(た)べ歩(ある)き — food tour, eating around (walking and sampling food)
