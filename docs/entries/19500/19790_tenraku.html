1. fall; tumble; plunge
Physically falling from a height, such as from a building, cliff, or elevated place.
屋根(やね)から転落(てんらく)した。
He fell from the roof.
登山(とざん)中(ちゅう)に崖(がけ)から転落(てんらく)する事故(じこ)が起(お)きた。
An accident occurred where someone fell from a cliff while mountaineering.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)での転落(てんらく)防止(ぼうし)のために安全(あんぜん)ネットが設置(せっち)された。
Safety nets were installed to prevent falls at the construction site.
2. decline; downfall; ruin
A figurative fall in status, position, or circumstances, such as falling into poverty or losing one's social standing.
人生(じんせい)の転落(てんらく)を味(あじ)わった。
He experienced a downfall in life.
一位(いちい)から三位(さんい)に転落(てんらく)した。
They dropped from first place to third.
借金(しゃっきん)がかさんで、貧困(ひんこん)生活(せいかつ)に転落(てんらく)してしまった。
Debts piled up and he fell into a life of poverty.

Used for both literal physical falls (sense 1) and figurative decline (sense 2). The physical sense often appears in news reports about accidents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転落(てんらく)事故(じこ) — falling accident
  • 転落(てんらく)死(し) — death from a fall
  • 転落(てんらく)防止(ぼうし) — fall prevention
  • 転落(てんらく)人生(じんせい) — a life in decline

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落下(らっか) — falling (of objects, more neutral/scientific)
  • 墜落(ついらく) — crash (specifically aircraft or from great height)
  • 没落(ぼつらく) — ruin, downfall (only figurative, especially of families or aristocracy)
