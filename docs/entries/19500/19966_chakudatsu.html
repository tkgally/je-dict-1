1. putting on and taking off; donning and removing
The act of putting on and removing clothing, accessories, or equipment. Often used when discussing ease of use.
着脱(ちゃくだつ)が簡単(かんたん)だ。
It's easy to put on and take off.
お年寄(としよ)りでも着脱(ちゃくだつ)しやすい服(ふく)。
Clothes that are easy for elderly people to put on and take off.
マジックテープで着脱(ちゃくだつ)できる靴(くつ)は子供(こども)に便利(べんり)だ。
Shoes that fasten with Velcro are convenient for children to put on and take off.
2. attaching and detaching; mounting and removing
The act of connecting and disconnecting parts or components. Used for mechanical or technical contexts.
レンズの着脱(ちゃくだつ)。
Attaching and detaching a lens.
バッテリーの着脱(ちゃくだつ)が簡単(かんたん)にできる。
The battery can be easily attached and removed.
ワンタッチで着脱(ちゃくだつ)可能(かのう)なストラップが付属(ふぞく)している。
A strap that can be attached and removed with one touch is included.

Composed of 着(ちゃく) (wearing, arriving) + 脱(だつ) (removing, taking off). A paired-opposite compound (対義(たいぎ)compound) combining 'putting on' and 'taking off' into a single concept. Used as both a noun and a する verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)が簡単(かんたん): easy to put on and take off
  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)しやすい: easy to don and remove
  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)可能(かのう): removable, detachable
  • 着脱(ちゃくだつ)式(しき): detachable type

USAGE:
Very common in product descriptions, especially for clothing, accessories, camera equipment, and modular products. The focus is almost always on the ease or convenience of the process.
