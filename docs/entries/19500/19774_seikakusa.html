正確(せいかく)さ

せいかくさ
noun
accuracy; precision; exactness
1. accuracy; precision; exactness; correctness
The quality of being accurate, exact, or free from error. The nominalized form of the na-adjective 正確, used when referring to accuracy as an abstract quality or measurable attribute.
正確(せいかく)さが求(もと)められる。
Accuracy is required.
この時計(とけい)は正確(せいかく)さに定評(ていひょう)がある。
This watch has a strong reputation for accuracy.
翻訳(ほんやく)では正確(せいかく)さと自然(しぜん)さのバランスが難(むずか)しい。
In translation, balancing accuracy and naturalness is difficult.

Formed by adding the suffix さ to the na-adjective 正確(せいかく) (accurate, precise), turning it into a noun that refers to the quality or degree of accuracy itself.

USAGE:
Used when discussing accuracy as a measurable quality or standard — in contexts like manufacturing, translation, scientific measurement, sports, and timekeeping. While 正確(せいかく) (na-adjective) describes something as accurate, 正確(せいかく)さ (noun) refers to the concept of accuracy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正確(せいかく)さを求(もと)める — to demand accuracy
  • 正確(せいかく)さに欠(か)ける — to lack accuracy
  • 正確(せいかく)さを重視(じゅうし)する — to value accuracy
  • 高(たか)い正確(せいかく)さ — high accuracy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精度(せいど) — precision, degree of accuracy; more technical, used in engineering and science
  • 正確(せいかく) — accurate (na-adjective); the base adjective form
  • 的確(てきかく)さ — aptness, being on-target; emphasizes hitting the right point rather than exactness
