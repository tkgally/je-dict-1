1. to trouble; to worry; to torment; to vex
To cause someone to suffer worry, distress, or difficulty. The causative form of 悩(なや)む (to worry), used when an external factor or person causes the trouble.
この問題(もんだい)が私(わたし)を悩(なや)ませている。
This problem is troubling me.
腰痛(ようつう)が彼(かれ)を長年(ながねん)悩(なや)ませてきた。
Back pain has plagued him for many years.
この難問(なんもん)は多(おお)くの研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)を悩(なや)ませてきた課題(かだい)である。
This difficult problem is an issue that has vexed many researchers.

The causative form of 悩(なや)む (to worry, to be troubled). While grammatically a causative, 悩(なや)ませる has become a standard transitive verb in its own right and is commonly used with inanimate subjects (problems, pain, weather, etc.) that cause trouble.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～が（人(ひと)）を悩(なや)ませる — something troubles someone
  • ～に悩(なや)ませられる — to be troubled by (passive of causative)
  • 長年(ながねん)悩(なや)ませてきた — has troubled for many years

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 困(こま)らせる — to cause difficulty for someone (more practical/concrete problems)
  • 苦(くる)しめる — to cause suffering, to torment (stronger, more painful)
  • 悩(なや)む — to worry, to be troubled (intransitive base verb)
