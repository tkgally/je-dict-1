1. to trade; to deal in; to sell
To engage in buying and selling goods as a business. A somewhat literary or traditional word for conducting trade.
魚(さかな)を商(あきな)う店(みせ)だ。
It's a shop that deals in fish.
代々(だいだい)呉服(ごふく)を商(あきな)ってきた家(いえ)だ。
It's a family that has dealt in kimono fabrics for generations.
江戸時代(えどじだい)、この通(とお)りでは多(おお)くの商人(しょうにん)が絹(きぬ)や茶(ちゃ)を商(あきな)っていた。
In the Edo period, many merchants traded in silk and tea along this street.

A godan verb (う row) meaning to trade or sell goods. More literary and traditional than 売(う)る or 販売(はんばい)する. Often used when describing established businesses, family trades, or historical commerce. Related to the noun 商(あきな)い (trade, business).

ETYMOLOGY:
Derived from 秋(あき)な}う, originally referring to the harvest-season trade that took place in autumn. The kanji {商(しょう) (commerce) is used as an ateji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魚(さかな)を商(あきな)う — to deal in fish
  • 呉服(ごふく)を商(あきな)う — to deal in kimono fabrics
  • 酒(さけ)を商(あきな)う — to deal in sake
  • 代々(だいだい)商(あきな)う — to trade for generations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)る — to sell; the everyday word for selling, while 商(あきな)う implies running a trade as an ongoing business
  • 商売(しょうばい)する — to do business; more general and modern, while 商(あきな)う has a traditional flavor
