1. dry cleaning; professional laundry service
Refers to professional cleaning of clothes, typically at a dry cleaner's shop. In Japanese, this almost always means taking clothes to a professional cleaning shop rather than doing laundry at home.
スーツをクリーニングに出(だ)した。
I took my suit to the dry cleaner's.
コートのクリーニング代(だい)は高(たか)い。
The dry cleaning fee for a coat is expensive.
冬物(ふゆもの)の衣類(いるい)をまとめてクリーニングに出(だ)してから、衣替(ころもが)えをする。
I send all my winter clothes to the cleaner's before switching out my wardrobe for the season.

From English "cleaning." In Japanese, this word specifically refers to professional garment cleaning services, not general cleaning or housework. Nearly every neighborhood in Japan has a クリーニング店(てん), making this a highly practical everyday word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クリーニングに出(だ)す — to take something to the dry cleaner's
  • クリーニングに出(だ)しに行(い)く — to go drop off dry cleaning
  • クリーニング店(てん) — dry cleaner's shop
  • クリーニング代(だい) — dry cleaning fee
  • クリーニング済(ず)み — already cleaned/laundered

RELATED TERMS:

  • 洗濯(せんたく) — laundry; doing laundry at home
  • ドライクリーニング — dry cleaning (the specific chemical process, less commonly used than クリーニング)
  • 衣替(ころもが)え — seasonal wardrobe change; a common time to use cleaning services
