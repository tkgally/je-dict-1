1. to be densely packed, to cluster together, to be crowded together
To be gathered closely together in a small area, creating a high concentration. Used for buildings, people, vegetation, and other things that can be tightly grouped.
住宅(じゅうたく)が密集(みっしゅう)している。
Houses are densely packed together.
人(ひと)が密集(みっしゅう)した場所(ばしょ)は避(さ)けてください。
Please avoid places where people are crowded together.
この地域(ちいき)には古(ふる)い木造(もくぞう)建築(けんちく)が密集(みっしゅう)しているため、火災(かさい)のリスクが高(たか)い。
Old wooden buildings are densely packed in this area, so the fire risk is high.

Often used in the pattern 密集(みっしゅう)した + noun or 密集(みっしゅう)している to describe a state. The noun form 密集(みっしゅう) is also common as a modifier.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 住宅(じゅうたく)密集(みっしゅう)地(ち): densely built residential area
  • 人口(じんこう)密集(みっしゅう)地帯(ちたい): densely populated zone
  • 密集(みっしゅう)した森(もり): dense forest
  • 密集(みっしゅう)した群衆(ぐんしゅう): packed crowd

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 混雑(こんざつ)する: to be crowded — focuses on congestion and disorder, especially with people
  • 密集(みっしゅう)する: to be densely packed — focuses on physical closeness and tight grouping of objects or people
