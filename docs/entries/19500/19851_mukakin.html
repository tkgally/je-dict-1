無課金(むかきん)

むかきん
noun, adjective (の)
free-to-play; not spending money (in games)
1. free-to-play; not spending real money on in-game purchases
Describes the practice of playing a mobile or online game without making any real-money purchases, or a player who does so. Contrasts with paying users who buy in-game items or currency.
無課金(むかきん)でクリアした。
I cleared it without spending any money.
無課金(むかきん)プレイヤーでも楽(たの)しめるゲームだ。
It's a game that even free-to-play players can enjoy.
彼(かれ)は無課金(むかきん)なのにランキング上位(じょうい)に入(はい)っている。
He's ranked high even though he hasn't spent any money.

A modern internet and gaming term composed of 無(む) (without) + 課金(かきん) (in-app purchase/billing). Widely used in discussions of mobile games and online games with microtransaction models.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無課金(むかきん)プレイヤー — free-to-play player
  • 無課金(むかきん)で攻略(こうりゃく)する — to beat (a game) without paying
  • 無課金(むかきん)勢(ぜい) — the free-to-play crowd

RELATED TERMS:

  • 課金(かきん) — in-app purchase; paying for game items
  • 微課金(びかきん) — light spending (small purchases)
  • 廃課金(はいかきん) — heavy spending (whale)
  • ガチャ — gacha (random item draw mechanic)
