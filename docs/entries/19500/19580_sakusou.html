1. complication; entanglement; complexity
A state where multiple elements are intricately intertwined, making the situation difficult to unravel or understand.
事態(じたい)が錯綜(さくそう)している。
The situation is complicated.
情報(じょうほう)が錯綜(さくそう)して混乱(こんらん)が生(しょう)じた。
Conflicting information caused confusion.
利害(りがい)が錯綜(さくそう)する問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)するのは容易(ようい)ではない。
It is not easy to resolve a problem where interests are entangled.

A する noun composed of 錯(さく) (confused, mixed up) and 綜(そう) (to interweave). Describes situations where multiple threads of information, interests, or factors become tangled together. Frequently encountered in news reporting and formal analysis.

USAGE:
Most commonly used as 錯綜(さくそう)する (to become complicated/entangled). The している form indicates an ongoing tangled state. Often describes information, situations, or interests.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報(じょうほう)が錯綜(さくそう)する — information becomes confused/contradictory
  • 事態(じたい)が錯綜(さくそう)する — the situation becomes complicated
  • 利害(りがい)が錯綜(さくそう)する — interests become entangled
  • 錯綜(さくそう)した状況(じょうきょう) — a complex situation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 複雑(ふくざつ) — complex; a general term for complexity, while 錯綜(さくそう) specifically implies tangled, interwoven threads that are hard to separate
  • 混乱(こんらん) — confusion, disorder; focuses on the resulting state of disorder, while 錯綜(さくそう) emphasizes the intertwined nature of the elements
