美術室(びじゅつしつ)

びじゅつしつ
noun
art room; art studio (in a school)
1. art room; art studio; art classroom
A room in a school designated for art classes, equipped with easels, supplies, and sinks for painting and other art activities.
美術室(びじゅつしつ)で絵(え)を描(か)く。
To paint in the art room.
放課後(ほうかご)、美術室(びじゅつしつ)で作品(さくひん)を仕上(しあ)げた。
After school, I finished my artwork in the art room.
美術室(びじゅつしつ)には石膏像(せっこうぞう)やイーゼルが並(なら)んでいた。
Plaster busts and easels were lined up in the art room.

A compound of 美術(びじゅつ) (art, fine arts) and 室(しつ) (room). A standard fixture in Japanese schools, where 美術(びじゅつ) is a required subject in junior high school.
