1. to join; to become a member; to enroll
To officially join an organization, club, association, or membership-based service.
テニス部(ぶ)に入会(にゅうかい)した。
I joined the tennis club.
入会(にゅうかい)するには申込書(もうしこみしょ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
An application form is required to join.
友人(ゆうじん)に勧(すす)められて、スポーツジムに入会(にゅうかい)することにした。
My friend recommended it, so I decided to join a sports gym.

Composed of 入(にゅう) (enter) + 会(かい) (association, group). Used for formally joining organizations with membership structures — clubs, gyms, associations, unions, and online services.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入会(にゅうかい)金(きん) — enrollment fee, membership fee
  • 入会(にゅうかい)申(もう)し込(こ)み — membership application
  • 入会(にゅうかい)資格(しかく) — membership qualifications
  • 入会(にゅうかい)手続(てつづ)き — enrollment procedures
  • 入会(にゅうかい)を申(もう)し込(こ)む — to apply for membership

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加入(かにゅう)する — to join, to subscribe; often used for insurance, unions, and larger organizations
  • 入部(にゅうぶ)する — to join a club (specifically school clubs)
  • 参加(さんか)する — to participate; does not imply formal membership

ANTONYM:

  • 退会(たいかい)する — to withdraw from, to cancel membership

Related Words

Antonym: 退会(たいかい)する (cancel membership)
