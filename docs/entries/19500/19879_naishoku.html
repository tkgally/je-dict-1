1. side job done at home; piecework; cottage industry work
Work done at home for extra income, typically simple manual tasks like assembly, packaging, or addressing envelopes. Traditionally associated with housewives earning supplementary income.
内職(ないしょく)で家計(かけい)を助(たす)ける。
To help the household finances with side work.
母(はは)は子育(こそだ)てをしながら内職(ないしょく)をしていた。
My mother did piecework at home while raising children.
在宅(ざいたく)の内職(ないしょく)で月(つき)に数万円(すうまんえん)稼(かせ)いでいる。
I earn tens of thousands of yen a month from home-based side work.
2. doing something else secretly; doing unrelated work during class or a meeting
Doing something unrelated during a meeting, class, or other activity where one should be paying attention. A colloquial extension of the original meaning.
授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)に内職(ないしょく)するな。
Don't work on other things during class.
会議中(かいぎちゅう)にこっそり内職(ないしょく)していた。
I was secretly working on something else during the meeting.
先生(せんせい)の目(め)を盗(ぬす)んで内職(ないしょく)していたら、見(み)つかってしまった。
I was secretly doing other work when the teacher wasn't looking, but got caught.

Composed of 内(ない) (inside, home) + 職(しょく) (work, job). Originally refers to piecework done at home — a traditional way for homemakers to earn extra income. The word has developed a common colloquial sense of secretly doing unrelated work during class or meetings.

USAGE:
Sense 2 is very common among students, who use 内職(ないしょく) to mean studying for another subject or doing homework during a boring class. In office settings, it refers to working on personal tasks or other projects during meetings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内職(ないしょく)をする — to do side work / to work on the side
  • 在宅(ざいたく)内職(ないしょく) — work-from-home side job
  • 内職(ないしょく)商法(しょうほう) — home-work scam (fraudulent schemes)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 副業(ふくぎょう) — side job, second job; broader and more modern term for any secondary work
  • {アルバイト} — part-time job; typically outside the home
