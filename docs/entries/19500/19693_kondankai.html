懇談会(こんだんかい)

こんだんかい
noun
informal meeting; discussion forum; get-together
1. informal meeting; discussion forum; roundtable; get-together
A relatively informal gathering where participants discuss matters in a relaxed setting. Common in school, business, and community contexts. Despite being called 'informal,' it is still organized and purposeful.
保護者(ほごしゃ)懇談会(こんだんかい)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended the parent-teacher meeting.
地域(ちいき)の懇談会(こんだんかい)で防災(ぼうさい)について話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed disaster preparedness at a community meeting.
市長(しちょう)と市民(しみん)の懇談会(こんだんかい)が来週(らいしゅう)開(ひら)かれる予定(よてい)だ。
A roundtable between the mayor and citizens is scheduled for next week.

While 懇談(こんだん) means friendly or informal talk, 懇談会(こんだんかい) as a compound refers to an organized event — not a casual chat. Very common in school settings (保護者(ほごしゃ)懇談会(こんだんかい), parent-teacher meetings) and in government or business contexts (roundtables, forums).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保護者(ほごしゃ)懇談会(こんだんかい): parent-teacher meeting
  • 懇談会(こんだんかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold an informal meeting
  • 懇談会(こんだんかい)に出席(しゅっせき)する: to attend a meeting
  • 地域(ちいき)懇談会(こんだんかい): community discussion forum

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会議(かいぎ): meeting — more formal and structured, with an agenda
  • 座談会(ざだんかい): roundtable discussion — often published or recorded, with a moderator
  • 懇親会(こんしんかい): social gathering — focused on socializing and networking, often with food and drink
