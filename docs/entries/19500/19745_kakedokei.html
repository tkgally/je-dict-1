掛(か)け時計(どけい)

かけどけい
noun
wall clock
1. wall clock
A clock designed to be hung on a wall. The most common type of clock found in homes, offices, and classrooms in Japan.
掛(か)け時計(どけい)が止(と)まっている。
The wall clock has stopped.
新(あたら)しい掛(か)け時計(どけい)を買(か)った。
I bought a new wall clock.
教室(きょうしつ)の掛(か)け時計(どけい)を見(み)ると、もう午後(ごご)三時(さんじ)だった。
When I looked at the classroom wall clock, it was already 3 PM.

Compound of 掛(か)け (hanging) and 時計(とけい) (clock). The と in 時計(とけい) voices to ど in the compound due to rendaku.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 置(お)き時計(どけい) — table clock; desk clock
  • 目覚(めざ)まし時計(どけい) — alarm clock
  • 腕時計(うでどけい) — wristwatch
  • 電波時計(でんぱどけい) — radio-controlled clock (automatically syncs to atomic time signals)
  • 柱時計(はしらどけい) — pillar clock; old-fashioned wall clock hung on a pillar

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 掛(か)け時計(どけい)を掛(か)ける — to hang a wall clock
  • 掛(か)け時計(どけい)の電池(でんち)を替(か)える — to replace the battery of a wall clock
  • 掛(か)け時計(どけい)が遅(おく)れている — the wall clock is running slow
