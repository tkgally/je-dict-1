1. to go against; to defy; to disobey
To act contrary to rules, expectations, orders, or someone's wishes.
命令(めいれい)に背(そむ)いた。
I disobeyed the order.
親(おや)の期待(きたい)に背(そむ)くのはつらい。
Going against one's parents' expectations is painful.
ルールに背(そむ)く行為(こうい)は許(ゆる)されない。
Actions that defy the rules are not permitted.
2. to turn one's back on; to betray
To turn away from someone or something, abandoning loyalty or trust.
信頼(しんらい)に背(そむ)いた。
I betrayed their trust.
仲間(なかま)に背(そむ)くわけにはいかない。
I can't turn my back on my friends.
恩人(おんじん)に背(そむ)くようなことは絶対(ぜったい)にしたくない。
I absolutely never want to betray someone who has helped me.

A godan verb (く row) meaning to act against or turn away from. The core image is of turning one's back (背(せ)) on something. Used with the particle に to indicate what is being defied or betrayed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 命令(めいれい)に背(そむ)く — to disobey an order
  • 期待(きたい)に背(そむ)く — to go against expectations
  • 信頼(しんらい)に背(そむ)く — to betray trust
  • ルールに背(そむ)く — to break the rules
  • 道(みち)に背(そむ)く — to go against what is right

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆(さか)らう — to resist, oppose; implies active resistance or defiance, while 背(そむ)く focuses on going contrary to expectations or loyalty
  • 裏切(うらぎ)る — to betray; stronger and more dramatic, often used for personal betrayals
