1. making into a habit; habituation; routinization
The process of turning an action or behavior into a regular habit or routine. Can refer to both deliberately forming a habit and the natural process of something becoming routine.
運動(うんどう)を習慣化(しゅうかんか)したい。
I want to make exercise a habit.
毎朝(まいあさ)の散歩(さんぽ)が習慣化(しゅうかんか)してきた。
My morning walks have become a habit.
新(あたら)しい行動(こうどう)を習慣化(しゅうかんか)するには、少(すく)なくとも三週間(さんしゅうかん)は続(つづ)ける必要(ひつよう)がある。
To make a new behavior a habit, you need to keep it up for at least three weeks.

Composed of 習慣(しゅうかん) (habit) + 化(か) (transformation suffix, '-ization'). A popular topic in self-improvement and productivity literature in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 習慣化(しゅうかんか)する — to make into a habit; to become habitual
  • 習慣化(しゅうかんか)のコツ — tips for forming habits
  • 習慣化(しゅうかんか)に成功(せいこう)する — to succeed in forming a habit
  • 早起(はやお)きを習慣化(しゅうかんか)する — to make early rising a habit

RELATED TERMS:

  • 習慣(しゅうかん) — habit, custom
  • 日課(にっか) — daily routine; refers to the established routine itself
  • 癖(くせ) — habit (often unconscious or negative); unlike 習慣化(しゅうかんか), not deliberately formed
  • ルーティン — routine (loanword); increasingly common, especially in sports contexts
