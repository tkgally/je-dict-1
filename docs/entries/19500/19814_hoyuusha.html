保有者(ほゆうしゃ)

ほゆうしゃ
noun
holder; owner; possessor
1. holder; owner; possessor
A person or entity that holds or possesses something, especially rights, qualifications, assets, or property in a formal or legal context.
免許(めんきょ)の保有者(ほゆうしゃ)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of license holders is increasing.
株式(かぶしき)の大口(おおぐち)保有者(ほゆうしゃ)が公表(こうひょう)された。
The major shareholders were disclosed.
特許(とっきょ)保有者(ほゆうしゃ)の許可(きょか)なく、この技術(ぎじゅつ)を使用(しよう)することはできない。
This technology cannot be used without the permission of the patent holder.

A compound of 保有(ほゆう) (possession, holding) and 者(しゃ) (person). Used in formal, legal, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 株式(かぶしき)保有者(ほゆうしゃ) — shareholder, stockholder
  • 特許(とっきょ)保有者(ほゆうしゃ) — patent holder
  • 免許(めんきょ)保有者(ほゆうしゃ) — license holder
  • 資格(しかく)保有者(ほゆうしゃ) — qualification holder

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 所有者(しょゆうしゃ) — owner (more general, emphasizes legal ownership)
  • 持(も)ち主(ぬし) — owner, possessor (everyday language, less formal)
  • 保有(ほゆう) — possession, holding (the base noun without the person suffix)
