用意周到(よういしゅうとう)

よういしゅうとう
な-adjective, noun
thoroughly prepared, meticulous
1. thoroughly prepared, meticulous, well-prepared
Describes someone or something that is carefully and completely prepared, with no detail overlooked. A four-character compound (yojijukugo) expressing thoroughness in preparation.
用意周到(よういしゅうとう)な計画(けいかく)だ。
It's a meticulously prepared plan.
彼女(かのじょ)は用意周到(よういしゅうとう)に準備(じゅんび)を進(すす)めた。
She proceeded with her preparations meticulously.
犯人(はんにん)は用意周到(よういしゅうとう)に証拠(しょうこ)を隠滅(いんめつ)していた。
The culprit had meticulously destroyed the evidence.

A yojijukugo (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) combining 用意(ようい) (preparation) and 周到(しゅうとう) (thorough, careful). It is always positive in describing the quality of preparation, though it can be used for both good and bad actors (e.g., a criminal who planned carefully).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 用意周到(よういしゅうとう)な人(ひと): a well-prepared person
  • 用意周到(よういしゅうとう)な作戦(さくせん): a meticulously planned operation
  • 用意周到(よういしゅうとう)に行動(こうどう)する: to act with thorough preparation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 周到(しゅうとう): thorough, careful — the second half of this compound, usable on its own
  • 万全(ばんぜん): perfect, flawless — emphasizes completeness of preparation
  • 念入(ねんい)り: careful, thorough — emphasizes taking extra care with details
