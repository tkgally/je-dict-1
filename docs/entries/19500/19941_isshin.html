一新(いっしん)

いっしん
noun, verb (する)
complete renewal; overhaul; fresh start
See also: 一心(いっしん) (wholeheartedness)
1. complete renewal; overhaul; total refresh
Making something entirely new or replacing something completely. Implies a thorough, dramatic change rather than a partial update.
気分(きぶん)を一新(いっしん)する。
To completely refresh one's mood.
会社(かいしゃ)の経営陣(けいえいじん)を一新(いっしん)した。
The company's management team was completely overhauled.
店内(てんない)の内装(ないそう)を一新(いっしん)して、リニューアルオープンした。
The store completely renovated its interior and reopened.

Emphasizes totality — not just a change, but a complete replacement or refresh. Common in business, organizational, and personal contexts. The word 面目(めんもく)一新(いっしん) (complete transformation of image/reputation) is a well-known set phrase.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気分(きぶん)を一新(いっしん)する: to refresh one's mood completely
  • メンバーを一新(いっしん)する: to completely change the lineup
  • 面目(めんもく)一新(いっしん): dramatic transformation of one's image
  • 体制(たいせい)を一新(いっしん)する: to overhaul the system

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刷新(さっしん): reform, overhaul — more formal, often for systems or organizations
  • リニューアル: renewal (loanword) — often for stores and websites
  • 改革(かいかく): reform — broader, can be gradual
