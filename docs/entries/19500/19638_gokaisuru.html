1. to misunderstand, to misconstrue, to get the wrong idea
To understand something incorrectly or form a wrong impression. Can refer to misunderstanding words, intentions, situations, or people.
誤解(ごかい)しないでください。
Please don't misunderstand.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)を誤解(ごかい)していた。
I had misunderstood his words.
誤解(ごかい)を招(まね)くような言(い)い方(かた)はしないほうがいい。
It's better not to phrase things in a way that invites misunderstanding.

Very common in everyday conversation and written Japanese. The noun form 誤解(ごかい) is frequently used on its own.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誤解(ごかい)を招(まね)く: to invite misunderstanding
  • 誤解(ごかい)を解(と)く: to clear up a misunderstanding
  • 誤解(ごかい)を与(あた)える: to give a wrong impression
  • 誤解(ごかい)が生(う)まれる: a misunderstanding arises
  • 誤解(ごかい)されやすい: easy to be misunderstood

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勘違(かんちが)いする: to be mistaken, to get confused — more casual, often about factual mix-ups
  • 誤解(ごかい)する: to misunderstand — can involve deeper misinterpretation of meaning or intention
