1. live demonstration; hands-on demonstration
Showing how something works or is done by actually performing it in front of an audience, especially for products, cooking, or crafts.
実演(じつえん)販売(はんばい)を行(おこな)う。
To conduct a live sales demonstration.
シェフが料理(りょうり)の作(つく)り方(かた)を実演(じつえん)した。
The chef demonstrated how to make the dish.
デパートの地下(ちか)で和菓子(わがし)の実演(じつえん)販売(はんばい)をしていたので、つい買(か)ってしまった。
They were doing a live demonstration sale of Japanese sweets in the department store basement, so I ended up buying some.
2. live performance; actual performance
Performing something live on stage or in person, as opposed to recorded or rehearsed versions.
舞台(ぶたい)で実演(じつえん)する。
To perform live on stage.
映像(えいぞう)ではなく実演(じつえん)で見(み)たい。
I want to see it performed live, not on video.
伝統(でんとう)芸能(げいのう)の実演(じつえん)を間近(まぢか)で見(み)ることができた。
I was able to see a traditional performing art demonstrated up close.

The compound 実演(じつえん)販売(はんばい) (live demonstration sale) is extremely common in Japanese retail, especially in department stores and food halls, where vendors prepare food or demonstrate products in front of customers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実演(じつえん)販売(はんばい) — live demonstration sale
  • 実演(じつえん)コーナー — demonstration area/booth
  • 実演(じつえん)を見(み)る — to watch a demonstration
  • 実演(じつえん)する — to demonstrate, to perform live

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • デモンストレーション — demonstration (loanword, often shortened to デモ)
  • 公演(こうえん) — public performance (larger-scale, theatrical)
  • 上演(じょうえん) — staging, performance (of a play or show)
