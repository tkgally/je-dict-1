1. to sprain; to wrench
To injure a joint by twisting it, especially an ankle or wrist.
足首(あしくび)を挫(くじ)いた。
I sprained my ankle.
階段(かいだん)で足(あし)を挫(くじ)いてしまった。
I twisted my foot on the stairs.
スポーツ中(ちゅう)に手首(てくび)を挫(くじ)いて病院(びょういん)に行(い)った。
I sprained my wrist during sports and went to the hospital.
2. to crush; to dampen; to thwart
To break someone's spirit, will, or enthusiasm; to frustrate their plans or ambitions.
出鼻(でばな)を挫(くじ)かれた。
I was thwarted right from the start.
失敗(しっぱい)が彼(かれ)のやる気(き)を挫(くじ)いた。
The failure crushed his motivation.
度重(たびかさ)なる不運(ふうん)に心(こころ)を挫(くじ)かれそうになったが、諦(あきら)めなかった。
Repeated misfortune nearly broke my spirit, but I didn't give up.

A godan verb (く row) with both physical and figurative meanings. The physical sense (spraining a joint) is very common in everyday conversation, while the figurative sense (crushing spirits or plans) appears in more formal or literary contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The intransitive counterpart is 挫(くじ)ける (to be broken, to lose heart).

KEY EXPRESSION:
出鼻(でばな)を挫(くじ)く is a set idiom meaning "to nip in the bud" or "to thwart at the outset."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 足首(あしくび)を挫(くじ)く — to sprain one's ankle
  • 出鼻(でばな)を挫(くじ)く — to thwart at the start
  • 気持(きも)ちを挫(くじ)く — to crush someone's feelings
  • 意気(いき)を挫(くじ)く — to dampen someone's spirits

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 捻挫(ねんざ)する — to sprain; a medical/formal term specifically for joint sprains, while 挫(くじ)く is more colloquial
  • 挫(くじ)ける — the intransitive form; to be discouraged, to lose heart
