義理堅(ぎりがた)い

ぎりがたい
i-adjective
loyal, dutiful, faithful to obligations
1. loyal, dutiful, faithful to obligations
Describes someone who takes social obligations, debts of gratitude, and interpersonal duties seriously and always reciprocates.
彼(かれ)は義理堅(ぎりがた)い人(ひと)だ。
He is a dutiful person.
義理堅(ぎりがた)いので、必(かなら)ずお返(かえ)しをする。
Being dutiful, she always returns favors.
義理堅(ぎりがた)い性格(せいかく)で、昔(むかし)の恩師(おんし)にも毎年(まいとし)年賀状(ねんがじょう)を送(おく)っている。
Being faithful to obligations, he sends New Year's cards every year even to his old teachers.

Composed of 義理(ぎり) (social obligation, duty) + 堅(かた)い (firm, strict). Describes someone who reliably fulfills social obligations and reciprocates kindness. This is generally a positive trait in Japanese culture.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 義理堅(ぎりがた)い人(ひと) — a dutiful person
  • 義理堅(ぎりがた)い性格(せいかく) — a dutiful personality
  • 義理堅(ぎりがた)く付(つ)き合(あ)う — to faithfully maintain a relationship

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese culture, 義理(ぎり) (social obligation) is a fundamental concept governing interpersonal relationships. Being 義理堅(ぎりがた)い is considered admirable — it means one does not forget debts of gratitude and always reciprocates appropriately, whether through return gifts, favors, or maintaining relationships.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 律儀(りちぎ) — conscientious, faithful (broader, includes general conscientiousness)
  • 誠実(せいじつ) — sincere, honest (focuses on honesty rather than obligation)
