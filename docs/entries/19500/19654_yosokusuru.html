1. to predict, to forecast, to project
To estimate or calculate in advance what will happen in the future based on data, patterns, or analysis. Used in weather, economics, science, and general forecasting.
結果(けっか)を予測(よそく)する。
To predict the outcome.
地震(じしん)を正確(せいかく)に予測(よそく)するのは難(むずか)しい。
It is difficult to predict earthquakes accurately.
AIを使(つか)って需要(じゅよう)を予測(よそく)するシステムが開発(かいはつ)された。
A system that uses AI to forecast demand was developed.

Implies a more analytical or data-based approach to predicting than 予想(よそう). Commonly used in scientific, technical, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予測(よそく)不能(ふのう): unpredictable
  • 予測(よそく)変換(へんかん): predictive text (input)
  • 予測(よそく)精度(せいど): prediction accuracy
  • 予測(よそく)モデル: prediction model
  • 予測(よそく)値(ち): predicted value

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予想(よそう)する: to expect, to anticipate — more subjective, based on intuition or general judgment
  • 予測(よそく)する: to predict, to forecast — more objective, based on data or scientific analysis
  • 予報(よほう)する: to forecast — specifically for weather and other official forecasts
