裁定(さいてい)

さいてい
noun / suru-verb
ruling; arbitration; decision
1. ruling; arbitration; decision by an authority
An official decision or judgment made by a person or body with authority to resolve a dispute. Used in legal, sports, and administrative contexts.
審判(しんぱん)の裁定(さいてい)に従(したが)う。
To abide by the referee's ruling.
労働(ろうどう)紛争(ふんそう)は仲裁(ちゅうさい)委員(いいん)会(かい)の裁定(さいてい)で解決(かいけつ)した。
The labor dispute was resolved by the arbitration committee's ruling.
裁定(さいてい)が不服(ふふく)な場合(ばあい)は、一定(いってい)期間(きかん)内(ない)に異議(いぎ)申(もう)し立(た)てができる。
If you are dissatisfied with the ruling, you can file an objection within a specified period.

Composed of 裁(さい) (to judge, decide) + 定(てい) (to determine, fix). A formal term for an authoritative decision that settles a matter. Commonly heard in sports (umpire decisions), labor disputes, and administrative proceedings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 裁定(さいてい)を下(くだ)す — to hand down a ruling
  • 裁定(さいてい)に従(したが)う — to follow a ruling
  • 裁定(さいてい)を求(もと)める — to seek a ruling
  • 裁定(さいてい)取引(とりひき) — arbitrage (finance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 判定(はんてい) — judgment, decision; used more for individual judgments, especially in sports
  • 判決(はんけつ) — verdict, court ruling; specifically a court's formal decision
  • 裁決(さいけつ) — adjudication; more formal, used in administrative law
