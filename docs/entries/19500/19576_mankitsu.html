1. thorough enjoyment; relishing to the full
The act of enjoying something fully and to one's complete satisfaction, savoring every aspect of an experience.
休日(きゅうじつ)を満喫(まんきつ)した。
I thoroughly enjoyed my day off.
温泉(おんせん)を満喫(まんきつ)してリフレッシュした。
I refreshed myself by fully enjoying the hot springs.
旅行先(りょこうさき)で地元(じもと)の料理(りょうり)を満喫(まんきつ)することができた。
I was able to relish the local cuisine at my travel destination.

A する noun meaning to enjoy something to the fullest extent. Composed of 満(まん) (full) and 喫(きつ) (to receive, to consume). Conveys deep, complete satisfaction rather than casual enjoyment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 休日(きゅうじつ)を満喫(まんきつ)する — to fully enjoy a holiday
  • 自然(しぜん)を満喫(まんきつ)する — to savor nature
  • 旅(たび)を満喫(まんきつ)する — to relish a trip
  • 人生(じんせい)を満喫(まんきつ)する — to enjoy life to the fullest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しむ — to enjoy; more general and everyday, while 満喫(まんきつ) emphasizes completeness and deep satisfaction
  • 堪能(たんのう) — to enjoy fully; very close in meaning but 堪能(たんのう) also means "proficient" and has a slightly more literary feel
