1. to devote oneself; to dedicate oneself selflessly
To give oneself wholeheartedly to a cause, person, or purpose, often with self-sacrifice implied.
家族(かぞく)のために献身(けんしん)する。
To devote oneself to one's family.
彼女(かのじょ)は地域(ちいき)の医療(いりょう)に献身(けんしん)してきた。
She has devoted herself to community healthcare.
生涯(しょうがい)を教育(きょういく)に献身(けんしん)し、多(おお)くの学生(がくせい)を育(そだ)てた恩師(おんし)に感謝(かんしゃ)している。
I am grateful to the mentor who devoted their entire life to education and nurtured many students.

Composed of 献(けん) (to offer, dedicate) + 身(しん) (body, self). Implies selfless dedication, often with a sense of sacrifice. The noun form 献身(けんしん) is also common, often used as a modifier: 献身的(けんしんてき) (devoted, selfless).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な — devoted, selfless, dedicated
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な介護(かいご) — devoted caregiving
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な愛(あい) — selfless love
  • 献身(けんしん)する姿勢(しせい) — attitude of devotion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 尽(つ)くす — to serve devotedly, to exhaust oneself for someone; more everyday
  • 捧(ささ)げる — to dedicate, to offer up; emphasizes giving something (life, time) to a cause
  • 傾注(けいちゅう)する — to concentrate all one's effort; more intellectual, less self-sacrificing
