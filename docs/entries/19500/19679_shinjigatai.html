信(しん)じ難(がた)い

しんじがたい
adjective
hard to believe; incredible; unbelievable
1. hard to believe; incredible; unbelievable
So surprising, extreme, or extraordinary that it is difficult to accept as true.
信(しん)じ難(がた)い話(はなし)だ。
That's a hard-to-believe story.
信(しん)じ難(がた)いことに、彼(かれ)は無傷(むきず)だった。
Incredibly, he was unharmed.
これほど短期間(たんきかん)で成功(せいこう)するとは、信(しん)じ難(がた)い結果(けっか)だ。
Succeeding in such a short time is an unbelievable result.

Formed from 信(しん)じる (to believe) + 難(がた)い (difficult to ~). The suffix ～難(がた)い attaches to verb stems to express difficulty or impossibility.

USAGE:
The ～難(がた)い pattern is productive in Japanese: 理解(りかい)し難(がた)い (hard to understand), 許(ゆる)し難(がた)い (hard to forgive). 信(しん)じ難(がた)い is one of the most commonly used combinations. It has a slightly more formal and literary tone than 信(しん)じられない.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信(しん)じ難(がた)い話(はなし) — an unbelievable story
  • 信(しん)じ難(がた)い結果(けっか) — an incredible result
  • 信(しん)じ難(がた)いことに — incredibly; unbelievably

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 信(しん)じられない — unbelievable; more colloquial and emotional
  • 考(かんが)え難(がた)い — hard to imagine; difficult to conceive
  • 耐(た)え難(がた)い — unbearable; difficult to endure
