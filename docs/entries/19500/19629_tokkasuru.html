1. to specialize in, to be specialized for, to focus exclusively on
To concentrate on a specific area, function, or purpose, often at the expense of breadth. Frequently used to describe products, services, businesses, or skills that are tailored to a particular niche.
この店(みせ)はカレーに特化(とっか)している。
This restaurant specializes in curry.
セキュリティに特化(とっか)したソフトウェア。
Software specialized for security.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は医療(いりょう)分野(ぶんや)に特化(とっか)したサービスを提供(ていきょう)している。
That company provides services specialized in the medical field.

Very common in business and technology contexts. Often used in the pattern ～に特化(とっか)した (specialized for ~). Carries a positive nuance of expertise and focus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に特化(とっか)した製品(せいひん): a product specialized for ~
  • ～に特化(とっか)した企業(きぎょう): a company specializing in ~
  • 特化(とっか)した機能(きのう): specialized function
  • 特化(とっか)型(がた): specialized type

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 専門(せんもん): specialty, expertise — a noun describing a field of specialization
  • 特化(とっか)する: to specialize — verb emphasizing the act of narrowing focus to one area
