1. to change color; to discolor; to fade
For something to undergo a change in color, typically due to aging, chemical reaction, heat, or exposure to light. Usually implies an undesirable change.
りんごが変色(へんしょく)した。
The apple changed color.
古(ふる)い写真(しゃしん)が変色(へんしょく)してしまった。
The old photograph has discolored.
切(き)ったアボカドは空気(くうき)に触(ふ)れると変色(へんしょく)するので、レモン汁(じる)をかけるとよい。
Cut avocado discolors when exposed to air, so it helps to squeeze lemon juice on it.

Composed of 変(へん) (change) + 色(しょく) (color). Most commonly used for undesirable color changes — browning of food, fading of fabrics, yellowing of paper, or tarnishing of metals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変色(へんしょく)を防(ふせ)ぐ — to prevent discoloration
  • 変色(へんしょく)した部分(ぶぶん) — discolored area
  • 熱(ねつ)で変色(へんしょく)する — to discolor from heat
  • 紫外線(しがいせん)で変色(へんしょく)する — to discolor from UV light

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 色(いろ)が変(か)わる — to change color; more casual and general, not necessarily negative
  • 褪(あ)せる — to fade (of color); specifically about losing color intensity
  • 黄(き)ばむ — to yellow, to turn yellowish; specific type of discoloration
