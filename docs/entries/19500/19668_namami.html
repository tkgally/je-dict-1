生身(なまみ)

なまみ
noun
flesh and blood; living body; mortal body
1. flesh and blood; living body; mortal body
The actual physical body of a living human being, as opposed to something mechanical, digital, or supernatural.
生身(なまみ)の人間(にんげん)には限界(げんかい)がある。
Flesh-and-blood humans have limits.
ロボットではなく、生身(なまみ)の人(ひと)と話(はな)したい。
I want to talk to a real person, not a robot.
生身(なまみ)の体(からだ)で宇宙(うちゅう)に出(で)ることはできない。
You can't go out into space with an unprotected human body.

A compound of 生(なま) (raw, living) and 身(み) (body). Emphasizes the vulnerability and reality of the physical human body.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the phrase 生身(なまみ)の人間(にんげん) (a flesh-and-blood human) or 生身(なまみ)の体(からだ) (a mortal body). Used when contrasting real people with machines, virtual beings, or superhuman entities. Carries a nuance of human vulnerability and physicality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生身(なまみ)の人間(にんげん) — a real, flesh-and-blood human
  • 生身(なまみ)の体(からだ) — a mortal/physical body
  • 生身(なまみ)で — with one's bare body; unprotected

RELATED TERMS:

  • 肉体(にくたい) — physical body; the body as opposed to the mind or spirit
  • 素手(すで) — bare hands; without tools or weapons
  • 本人(ほんにん) — the person themselves; in person
