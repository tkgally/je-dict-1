1. to implement, to carry out, to execute, to put into effect
To put a plan, policy, survey, or event into action. A formal word very commonly used in official, governmental, and business contexts.
アンケートを実施(じっし)する。
To conduct a survey.
避難(ひなん)訓練(くんれん)が実施(じっし)された。
An evacuation drill was carried out.
新(あたら)しい制度(せいど)は来年度(らいねんど)から実施(じっし)される予定(よてい)です。
The new system is scheduled to be implemented starting next fiscal year.

One of the most common formal verbs in Japanese. Appears constantly in news, official announcements, and business documents. Often used in passive form 実施(じっし)される.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 調査(ちょうさ)を実施(じっし)する: to conduct a survey/investigation
  • 訓練(くんれん)を実施(じっし)する: to carry out training
  • 計画(けいかく)を実施(じっし)する: to execute a plan
  • 実施(じっし)日(び): implementation date
  • 実施(じっし)要項(ようこう): implementation guidelines

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 行(おこな)う: to carry out, to conduct — slightly less formal, also very common
  • 施行(しこう)する: to enforce (a law) — specifically for putting laws into effect
  • 実行(じっこう)する: to execute, to carry out — emphasizes actually doing it, can be used for personal actions too
