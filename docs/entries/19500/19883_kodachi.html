木立(こだち)

こだち
noun
grove of trees; stand of trees
1. grove of trees; stand of trees; clump of trees
A group of trees standing together. Evokes a picturesque, somewhat literary image of trees clustered in a landscape.
木立(こだち)の中(なか)を歩(ある)く。
To walk through the grove.
木立(こだち)の間(あいだ)から光(ひかり)が差(さ)し込(こ)む。
Light streams in through the trees.
静(しず)かな木立(こだち)に囲(かこ)まれた小(ちい)さな家(いえ)が見(み)えた。
A small house surrounded by a quiet grove of trees came into view.

Composed of 木(こ) (tree, an older form of 木(き)) + 立(だち) (standing). Has a literary, scenic quality and frequently appears in descriptions of landscapes, gardens, and rural settings. Unlike 森(もり) (forest), 木立(こだち) suggests a smaller, more open cluster of trees.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 木立(こだち)の中(なか) — among the trees
  • 木立(こだち)の影(かげ) — the shade of the trees
  • 木立(こだち)に囲(かこ)まれた — surrounded by a grove of trees

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 林(はやし) — woods; a smaller area of trees than a forest
  • 森(もり) — forest; a larger, denser area of trees
  • 並木(なみき) — row of trees; trees planted in a line along a road
