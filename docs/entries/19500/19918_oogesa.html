大(おお)げさ

おおげさ
na-adjective
exaggerated; overdramatic
1. exaggerated; overdramatic; overblown
Describes something that is presented in an excessive or inflated way beyond what the situation warrants. Used for both actions and speech.
大(おお)げさに泣(な)く。
To cry dramatically.
そんな大(おお)げさな話(はなし)じゃないよ。
It's not that big of a deal.
彼(かれ)はいつも大(おお)げさな反応(はんのう)をするから、周(まわ)りが驚(おどろ)く。
He always has such overdramatic reactions that it surprises those around him.

Describes exaggeration in speech, actions, or reactions. The word carries a mildly critical nuance — implying that someone is making too much of something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)げさな話(はなし): exaggerated story
  • 大(おお)げさな反応(はんのう): overblown reaction
  • 大(おお)げさに言(い)う: to exaggerate (in speech)
  • 大(おお)げさに騒(さわ)ぐ: to make a big fuss

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大仰(おおぎょう): more literary/formal synonym for exaggerated
  • オーバー: casual loanword meaning "over the top"
  • 誇張(こちょう): exaggeration as a noun (more formal/objective)
