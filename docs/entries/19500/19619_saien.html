菜園(さいえん)

さいえん
noun
vegetable garden; kitchen garden
1. vegetable garden; kitchen garden
A garden where vegetables and herbs are grown, typically for personal or household consumption.
裏庭(うらにわ)に菜園(さいえん)がある。
There's a vegetable garden in the backyard.
週末(しゅうまつ)は菜園(さいえん)の手入(てい)れをしている。
I tend the vegetable garden on weekends.
定年(ていねん)退職(たいしょく)後(ご)、家庭(かてい)菜園(さいえん)を始(はじ)めたのが毎日(まいにち)の楽(たの)しみになった。
After retiring, starting a home vegetable garden became my daily pleasure.

Combines 菜(さい) (vegetables/greens) and 園(えん) (garden/park). Refers specifically to a garden for growing vegetables, as opposed to a flower garden (花壇(かだん)) or an ornamental garden (庭園(ていえん)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家庭(かてい)菜園(さいえん): home vegetable garden
  • 菜園(さいえん)を作(つく)る: make a vegetable garden
  • 菜園(さいえん)の手入(てい)れ: tending a vegetable garden
  • 市民(しみん)菜園(さいえん): community garden, allotment
  • 屋上(おくじょう)菜園(さいえん): rooftop vegetable garden

USAGE:
The most common compound is 家庭(かてい)菜園(さいえん) (home vegetable garden), a popular hobby in Japan especially among retirees. 市民(しみん)菜園(さいえん) are rented plots in urban areas where city dwellers can grow their own vegetables.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 畑(はたけ): field, farm plot — a broader word for cultivated land, typically larger in scale than a 菜園(さいえん)
  • 庭(にわ): garden, yard — general word for a garden or yard, not specifically for growing vegetables
  • 農園(のうえん): farm, plantation — a larger-scale agricultural operation
