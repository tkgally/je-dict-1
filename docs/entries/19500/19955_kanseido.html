完成度(かんせいど)

かんせいど
noun
degree of completion; level of perfection; polish
1. degree of completion; level of perfection; polish; quality of finish
How well-finished or polished something is. Used to evaluate the quality, refinement, or thoroughness of a product, artwork, performance, or other work.
完成度(かんせいど)が高(たか)い。
The level of polish is high.
この映画(えいが)は完成度(かんせいど)の高(たか)い作品(さくひん)だ。
This movie is a highly polished work.
まだ試作品(しさくひん)なので完成度(かんせいど)は低(ひく)いが、方向性(ほうこうせい)は良(よ)い。
It's still a prototype so the level of polish is low, but the direction is good.

Composed of 完成(かんせい) (completion, perfection) + 度(ど) (degree). The suffix 度(ど) creates a scale or measure, so 完成度(かんせいど) literally means 'degree of completion.' In practice, it is more about quality and refinement than mere completion — a high 完成度(かんせいど) means something is well-crafted and polished.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完成度(かんせいど)が高(たか)い: high quality; well-polished
  • 完成度(かんせいど)が低(ひく)い: low quality; rough; unfinished
  • 完成度(かんせいど)を上(あ)げる: to raise the quality; to refine
  • 完成度(かんせいど)の高(たか)い作品(さくひん): a polished work

USAGE:
Very common in reviews and evaluations of creative works, products, and performances. Often used with 高(たか)い/低(ひく)い rather than 大(おお)きい/小(ちい)さい.
