1. to find amusing; to show amusement
To outwardly display that one finds something interesting or entertaining. As a ～がる verb, it describes observable signs of amusement rather than inner feelings.
子供(こども)たちは面白(おもしろ)がった。
The children were amused.
彼(かれ)はその話(はなし)を面白(おもしろ)がって聞(き)いていた。
He listened to the story with amusement.
友達(ともだち)は私(わたし)の失敗(しっぱい)を面白(おもしろ)がって、ずっと笑(わら)っていた。
My friend found my mistake amusing and kept laughing about it.

Formed from 面白(おもしろ)い + がる, a suffix that turns i-adjectives into verbs describing observable emotional reactions. While 面白(おもしろ)い describes one's own feeling, 面白(おもしろ)がる describes someone visibly showing amusement.

GRAMMAR:
As a がる-verb, it is primarily used to describe third persons. For first person, 面白(おもしろ)い or 面白(おもしろ)く思(おも)う is more natural. The て-form (面白(おもしろ)がって) is common as an adverbial modifier.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 面白(おもしろ)がって見(み)る — to watch with amusement
  • 面白(おもしろ)がって聞(き)く — to listen with interest
  • 何(なに)でも面白(おもしろ)がる — to find everything amusing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しがる — to show enjoyment; broader than 面白(おもしろ)がる, which focuses on amusement or intellectual interest
  • 喜(よろこ)ぶ — to be pleased; expresses happiness rather than amusement
  • 珍(めずら)しがる — to show curiosity about something unusual
