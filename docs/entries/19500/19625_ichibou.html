1. sweeping view; panoramic view; commanding view
Seeing a wide area in a single glance. Used to describe looking out over a vast landscape or area from an elevated position.
山頂(さんちょう)から町(まち)が一望(いちぼう)できる。
You can see the whole town from the mountaintop.
展望台(てんぼうだい)からは海(うみ)が一望(いちぼう)のもとに広(ひろ)がっていた。
From the observation deck, the ocean spread out as far as the eye could see.
このホテルの最上階(さいじょうかい)からは、東京(とうきょう)の夜景(やけい)を一望(いちぼう)することができます。
From the top floor of this hotel, you can take in a panoramic view of Tokyo's night skyline.

Combines 一(いち) (one) and 望(ぼう) (gaze/view), literally meaning "one look" — taking in an entire landscape in a single glance. Conveys a sense of spaciousness and openness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一望(いちぼう)できる: can see everything at once
  • 一望(いちぼう)のもとに: in full view, at a glance
  • 一望(いちぼう)に見渡(みわた)す: survey with a sweeping gaze
  • 一望(いちぼう)千里(せんり): a vast unobstructed view (lit. "one look, a thousand ri")

USAGE:
Often used in travel guides, real estate descriptions, and scenic spot promotions. The phrase 〜を一望(いちぼう)できる is a selling point for hotels, restaurants, and observation decks.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 眺望(ちょうぼう): vista, distant view — emphasizes looking at something far away; more neutral about the breadth of the view
  • 展望(てんぼう): outlook, prospect — can refer to both a physical view and a figurative outlook on the future
  • 見晴(みは)らし: view, lookout — more everyday and casual; refers to how open and clear the view is from a place
