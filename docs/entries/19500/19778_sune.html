すね

すね
noun
shin; front of the lower leg
1. shin; shinbone; front of the lower leg
The front part of the leg between the knee and the ankle. Also written as 脛 in kanji, though the hiragana form is more common in everyday use.
すねを蹴(け)られた。
I was kicked in the shin.
テーブルの角(かど)にすねをぶつけた。
I banged my shin on the corner of the table.
サッカー選手(せんしゅ)はすねにレガースをつけて怪我(けが)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
Soccer players wear shin guards to prevent injuries.

Can be written as 脛(すね) in kanji, but the hiragana form is more common in everyday writing. The shin is a notoriously sensitive and unprotected area, making it a frequent site of minor injuries.

IDIOM:
The expression 親(おや)のすねをかじる (to gnaw on a parent's shin) means to live off one's parents financially — to be dependent on them for support as an adult.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すねを蹴(け)る — to kick the shin
  • すねをぶつける — to bang one's shin
  • すね当(あ)て — shin guard, shin pad
  • 親(おや)のすねをかじる — to live off one's parents

RELATED TERMS:

  • 膝(ひざ) — knee
  • ふくらはぎ — calf (back of the lower leg)
  • 足首(あしくび) — ankle
