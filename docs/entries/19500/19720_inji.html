印字(いんじ)

いんじ
noun / suru-verb
printing; imprinting characters
1. printing; imprinting; printing characters
The act of printing text or characters onto a surface, typically using a printer, stamp, or typewriter.
レシートの印字(いんじ)が薄(うす)い。
The print on the receipt is faint.
ラベルに商品名(しょうひんめい)を印字(いんじ)する。
To print the product name on a label.
感熱紙(かんねつし)のレシートは時間(じかん)が経(た)つと印字(いんじ)が消(き)えてしまうことがある。
The print on thermal paper receipts can fade over time.

Composed of 印(いん) (print, stamp) + 字(じ) (character, letter). Refers specifically to the printing of text characters, as opposed to images or graphics. Commonly used in business and manufacturing contexts for labels, receipts, and documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 印字(いんじ)する — to print (characters)
  • 印字(いんじ)が薄(うす)い — the print is faint
  • 印字(いんじ)品質(ひんしつ) — print quality
  • 印字(いんじ)速度(そくど) — print speed
  • 印字(いんじ)面(めん) — printed surface

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 印刷(いんさつ) — printing; broader term covering all types of printing, including images and mass production
  • プリント — print; loanword used for printing in general
  • 刻印(こくいん) — engraving, stamping; physically pressing characters into a surface
