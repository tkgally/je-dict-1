1. to throw in; to cast into
To throw or drop something into a place, such as casting a stone into water or throwing oneself into something.
石(いし)を池(いけ)に投(とう)じた。
I threw a stone into the pond.
彼(かれ)は身(み)を投(とう)じるように海(うみ)に飛(と)び込(こ)んだ。
He plunged into the sea as if throwing himself in.
若(わか)くして政治(せいじ)の世界(せかい)に身(み)を投(とう)じた。
At a young age, he threw himself into the world of politics.
2. to invest; to put in (resources, effort)
To commit money, resources, or effort toward a purpose.
多額(たがく)の資金(しきん)を投(とう)じた。
A large amount of money was invested.
このプロジェクトには莫大(ばくだい)な予算(よさん)が投(とう)じられている。
An enormous budget has been invested in this project.
開発(かいはつ)に多(おお)くの時間(じかん)と労力(ろうりょく)を投(とう)じてきた。
A great deal of time and effort has been invested in development.
3. to cast (a vote)
To submit a vote in an election or poll.
一票(いっぴょう)を投(とう)じた。
I cast my vote.
賛成(さんせい)に票(ひょう)を投(とう)じる人(ひと)が多(おお)かった。
Many people cast their votes in favor.
国民(こくみん)は選挙(せんきょ)で自分(じぶん)の意思(いし)を示(しめ)すために一票(いっぴょう)を投(とう)じるべきだ。
Citizens should cast their votes to express their will in elections.

An ichidan verb (じる form of 投(とう)ずる) meaning to throw into, invest, or cast. A somewhat formal and literary verb that covers physical throwing, financial/resource investment, and voting.

KEY EXPRESSIONS:

  • 身(み)を投(とう)じる — to throw oneself into (an activity, a cause); to devote oneself
  • 一票(いっぴょう)を投(とう)じる — to cast a vote
  • 一石(いっせき)を投(とう)じる — to make waves; to raise an issue (lit. "throw a stone")

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金(しきん)を投(とう)じる — to invest funds
  • 予算(よさん)を投(とう)じる — to invest a budget
  • 票(ひょう)を投(とう)じる — to cast a vote
  • 身(み)を投(とう)じる — to throw oneself into

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 投資(とうし)する — to invest (financially); specifically about financial investment, while 投(とう)じる is broader
  • 投(な)げる — to throw; the everyday word for physical throwing, while 投(とう)じる is more literary
