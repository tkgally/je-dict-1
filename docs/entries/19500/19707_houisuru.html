1. to surround; to encircle; to besiege
To completely surround something or someone, often in a military, police, or metaphorical context.
警察(けいさつ)が建物(たてもの)を包囲(ほうい)した。
The police surrounded the building.
敵(てき)の軍(ぐん)に包囲(ほうい)されて身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない。
Surrounded by the enemy army, they couldn't move.
デモ隊(たい)が国会(こっかい)議事堂(ぎじどう)を包囲(ほうい)し、抗議(こうぎ)の声(こえ)を上(あ)げた。
Protesters surrounded the National Diet Building and raised their voices in protest.

Composed of 包(ほう) (to wrap, enclose) + 囲(い) (to surround). A formal verb that implies completely encircling a target. Most commonly used in military, law enforcement, and news contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. Takes を to mark the thing being surrounded.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 包囲(ほうい)する — to surround
  • 包囲(ほうい)される — to be surrounded
  • 包囲(ほうい)を解(と)く — to lift a siege
  • 包囲(ほうい)網(もう) — encirclement net; a cordon
  • 包囲(ほうい)攻撃(こうげき) — siege attack

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 囲(かこ)む — to surround; more general and everyday, used for surrounding a table, etc.
  • 取(と)り囲(かこ)む — to surround on all sides; emphasizes closing in from multiple directions
  • 封鎖(ふうさ)する — to blockade; focuses on blocking access rather than encirclement
