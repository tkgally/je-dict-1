1. striking resemblance; close resemblance; looking almost identical
An extremely close similarity between two things or people, to the point where they could be mistaken for each other.
親子(おやこ)で顔(かお)が酷似(こくじ)している。
The parent and child look strikingly alike.
犯行(はんこう)の手口(てぐち)が酷似(こくじ)している。
The methods of the crimes bear a striking resemblance.
有名(ゆうめい)ブランドに酷似(こくじ)した偽物(にせもの)が出回(でまわ)っている。
Counterfeits that closely resemble famous brands are circulating.

Stronger than 似(に)ている (to resemble). 酷(こく) here intensifies the meaning — the resemblance is so close it is almost indistinguishable. Often used in news, police reports, and product comparisons.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 酷似(こくじ)している: to bear a striking resemblance
  • 酷似(こくじ)した商品(しょうひん): a near-identical product
  • 容姿(ようし)が酷似(こくじ): strikingly similar in appearance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 類似(るいじ): similarity — more neutral, less extreme
  • そっくり: spitting image — colloquial, often for people
  • 瓜二(うりふた)つ: like two peas in a pod — idiomatic, for people
