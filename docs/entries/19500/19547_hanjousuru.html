1. to prosper; to thrive; to do a roaring trade
Describes a business, shop, or establishment doing well and attracting many customers. Implies visible, lively success rather than abstract profitability.
あの店(みせ)はいつも繁盛(はんじょう)している。
That shop is always thriving.
駅前(えきまえ)のラーメン屋(や)は昼(ひる)も夜(よる)も繁盛(はんじょう)している。
The ramen shop in front of the station is busy both at lunch and dinner.
口(くち)コミのおかげで、開店(かいてん)から半年(はんとし)で大繁盛(だいはんじょう)するようになった。
Thanks to word of mouth, the place started doing a roaring trade within half a year of opening.

Describes a business or establishment that is thriving and attracting many customers. The word carries a warm, positive nuance of bustling activity and success. It is most naturally used for shops, restaurants, and similar customer-facing businesses rather than large corporations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 商売(しょうばい)繁盛(はんじょう) — thriving business; prosperous trade
  • 大繁盛(だいはんじょう) — booming business; great prosperity
  • 店(みせ)が繁盛(はんじょう)する — a shop thrives

CULTURAL NOTE:
商売繁盛(しょうばいはんじょう) is a common prayer or wish at shrines, especially at business-related shrines like 恵比寿(えびす) shrines. The phrase appears on good-luck charms and New Year decorations for businesses.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 栄(さか)える — to prosper; broader in scope, used for nations, cultures, or organizations
  • 流行(はや)る — to be popular, to be in vogue; focuses on trendiness rather than business success
