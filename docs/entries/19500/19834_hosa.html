1. assistance, support, counsel
The act of assisting or supporting someone in a leadership or decision-making role.
社長(しゃちょう)を補佐(ほさ)する。
To assist the company president.
業務(ぎょうむ)の補佐(ほさ)をお願(ねが)いします。
I'd like to ask for your assistance with the work.
部長(ぶちょう)を補佐(ほさ)して新(あたら)しいプロジェクトを進(すす)めている。
I'm assisting the department head in advancing the new project.
2. aide, assistant
A person who assists a superior, especially in an official or organizational capacity.
大統領(だいとうりょう)補佐(ほさ)官(かん)に任命(にんめい)された。
He was appointed as a presidential aide.
課長(かちょう)補佐(ほさ)として働(はたら)いている。
I work as an assistant section chief.
補佐(ほさ)役(やく)として長年(ながねん)チームを支(ささ)えてきた。
As an assistant, I've supported the team for many years.

Used for supporting a superior in an official or organizational context. Also functions as a suru verb (補佐(ほさ)する). As a title (sense 2), it is attached after a position name.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 補佐(ほさ)する — to assist, to support
  • 補佐(ほさ)役(やく) — supporting role, assistant
  • 補佐(ほさ)官(かん) — aide, advisor (government/military title)
  • 大統領(だいとうりょう)補佐(ほさ)官(かん) — presidential advisor
  • 課長(かちょう)補佐(ほさ) — assistant section chief

USAGE:
As a title, 補佐(ほさ) comes after the position it supports: 部長(ぶちょう)補佐(ほさ) (assistant department head). This is a common rank in Japanese government and corporate hierarchies.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 助手(じょしゅ) — assistant (general, often academic)
  • 秘書(ひしょ) — secretary (personal support role)
  • 副(ふく) — vice-, deputy- (prefix for deputy positions)
