1. aerial bombing; air strike; bombing from the air
An attack in which bombs are dropped from aircraft onto targets on the ground. A concise, modern term commonly used in news reporting.
軍事(ぐんじ)施設(しせつ)が空爆(くうばく)された。
Military facilities were bombed from the air.
空爆(くうばく)で民間人(みんかんじん)にも被害(ひがい)が出(で)ている。
Civilians are also being harmed by the air strikes.
国連(こくれん)は空爆(くうばく)の即時(そくじ)停止(ていし)を求(もと)める声明(せいめい)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The United Nations issued a statement demanding an immediate halt to the air strikes.

Composed of 空(くう) (sky, air) and 爆(ばく) (bomb, explosion). A modern, concise term favored in news headlines and contemporary reporting. Also functions as a する verb: 空爆(くうばく)する (to bomb from the air).

USAGE:
The standard term in contemporary news for describing military air strikes. More concise and modern-sounding than 爆撃(ばくげき) or 空襲(くうしゅう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空爆(くうばく)する — to carry out an air strike
  • 空爆(くうばく)を受(う)ける — to be hit by an air strike
  • 空爆(くうばく)を中止(ちゅうし)する — to halt air strikes
  • 無差別(むさべつ)空爆(くうばく) — indiscriminate bombing

RELATED TERMS:

  • 空襲(くうしゅう) — air raid; more historical, strongly associated with WWII
  • 爆撃(ばくげき) — bombing; the general military term for dropping bombs
  • 砲撃(ほうげき) — shelling; artillery fire from the ground
