1. salute; military salute
The formal gesture of raising the hand to the forehead, performed by military personnel, police officers, and similar uniformed personnel as a sign of respect.
兵士(へいし)が敬礼(けいれい)した。
The soldier saluted.
警官(けいかん)は上官(じょうかん)に敬礼(けいれい)をした。
The police officer saluted the superior officer.
国旗(こっき)に向(む)かって敬礼(けいれい)する姿(すがた)が印象的(いんしょうてき)だった。
The sight of them saluting the national flag was impressive.
2. respectful greeting; bow of respect
A formal, respectful greeting or bow. Used in ceremonial or very formal contexts.
深(ふか)く敬礼(けいれい)する。
To bow deeply in respect.
来賓(らいひん)に敬礼(けいれい)を述(の)べた。
They offered a respectful greeting to the guests of honor.
式典(しきてん)で全員(ぜんいん)が起立(きりつ)して敬礼(けいれい)した。
Everyone stood up and paid their respects at the ceremony.

Composed of 敬(けい) (respect) + 礼(れい) (etiquette, bow). Most commonly associated with the military salute gesture, but the word originally and more broadly refers to any formal expression of respect.

USAGE:
Used as a noun (敬礼(けいれい)をする) or as a suru verb (敬礼(けいれい)する). In school contexts, students may hear 起立(きりつ)、礼(れい) (Stand, bow) rather than 敬礼(けいれい), though some schools use 敬礼(けいれい) as well.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敬礼(けいれい)をする — to salute, to pay respects
  • 挙手(きょしゅ)敬礼(けいれい) — hand salute (the standard military salute)
  • 最敬礼(さいけいれい) — the deepest bow (45 degrees)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 礼(れい) — bow; the everyday word for a bow or greeting
  • 会釈(えしゃく) — a light, casual bow of acknowledgment
  • お辞儀(じぎ) — a bow; the most common general term for bowing
