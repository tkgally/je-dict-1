1. registration; recording in an official register
The legal act of recording ownership, rights, or status in an official government register, especially for real estate, companies, and legal entities.
不動産(ふどうさん)の登記(とうき)をする。
To register real estate.
会社(かいしゃ)を設立(せつりつ)するには登記(とうき)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Registration is required to establish a company.
土地(とち)の所有権(しょゆうけん)移転(いてん)登記(とうき)を司法書士(しほうしょし)に依頼(いらい)した。
I asked a judicial scrivener to handle the property ownership transfer registration.

A legal and administrative term that learners in Japan will encounter when buying property, starting a business, or dealing with official documents. The 法務局(ほうむきょく) (Legal Affairs Bureau) handles registration in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不動産(ふどうさん)登記(とうき) — real estate registration
  • 商業(しょうぎょう)登記(とうき) — commercial registration
  • 登記(とうき)簿(ぼ) — register, registry book
  • 登記(とうき)所(しょ) — registry office
  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)移転(いてん)登記(とうき) — ownership transfer registration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 登録(とうろく) — registration (more general; used for memberships, accounts, etc.)
  • 届出(とどけで) — notification, filing (e.g., marriage registration at city hall)
