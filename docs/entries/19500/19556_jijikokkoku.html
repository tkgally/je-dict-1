時々刻々(じじこっこく)

じじこっこく
adverb
moment by moment; with each passing moment
1. moment by moment; with each passing moment; constantly changing
Describes something that changes or progresses continuously over time, with noticeable differences at each moment. Often used for situations, conditions, or states that evolve rapidly.
状況(じょうきょう)は時々刻々(じじこっこく)と変(か)わっている。
The situation is changing moment by moment.
台風(たいふう)の進路(しんろ)が時々刻々(じじこっこく)と伝(つた)えられた。
The typhoon's path was reported moment by moment.
ニュースサイトでは、選挙(せんきょ)結果(けっか)が時々刻々(じじこっこく)と更新(こうしん)されていた。
On the news website, the election results were being updated moment by moment.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) that repeats 時(じ) (time) and 刻(こく) (moment) to emphasize continuous, ongoing change. It has a literary, slightly formal tone and is especially common in news and written language.

USAGE:
Almost always followed by the particle と: 時々刻々(じじこっこく)と変(か)わる. The most common verb partner is 変(か)わる (to change). Note that this is different from 時々(ときどき) (sometimes), despite sharing the same kanji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時々刻々(じじこっこく)と変(か)わる — to change moment by moment
  • 時々刻々(じじこっこく)と変化(へんか)する — to change continuously
  • 時々刻々(じじこっこく)と迫(せま)る — to approach moment by moment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刻一刻(こくいっこく)と — moment by moment; very similar meaning, slightly more common
  • 次第(しだい)に — gradually; implies a slower, more gradual change
  • 日(ひ)に日(ひ)に — day by day; a larger time scale
