1. revenge; vindication; avenging a previous defeat or humiliation
The act of overcoming a past defeat or disgrace by winning or succeeding on a subsequent occasion. Used especially in sports, competitions, and examinations.
今度(こんど)こそ雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たしたい。
This time I want to get my revenge.
前回(ぜんかい)の大会(たいかい)で負(ま)けた相手(あいて)に雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たした。
He got his revenge against the opponent who beat him in the previous tournament.
去年(きょねん)の試験(しけん)に落(お)ちた悔(くや)しさをばねに、雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たすべく猛勉強(もうべんきょう)した。
Using the frustration of failing last year's exam as motivation, I studied intensely to vindicate myself.

Literally "washing away shame" — 雪(せつ) (to wash away, from the literary reading of 雪(すす)ぐ) + 辱(じょく) (disgrace, humiliation). Despite the kanji 雪(ゆき) normally meaning "snow," here it carries the meaning of cleansing or purifying.

USAGE:
Almost always appears with 果(は)たす: 雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たす (to achieve vindication). The word carries a positive, determined tone — it implies honorable redemption through effort, not petty revenge.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たす — to get one's revenge; to vindicate oneself
  • 雪辱(せつじょく)を期(き)す — to aim for revenge/vindication
  • 雪辱戦(せつじょくせん) — rematch for revenge; revenge match

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 復讐(ふくしゅう) — revenge, vengeance; darker, more personal, implies harm to another
  • 仕返(しかえ)し — getting back at someone; more casual, can be petty
  • リベンジ — revenge (from English); casual, commonly used in everyday speech for rematches
