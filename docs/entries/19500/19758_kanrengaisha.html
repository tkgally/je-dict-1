関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)

かんれんがいしゃ
noun
affiliated company; associated company
1. affiliated company; associated company
A company in which another company has significant influence, typically through partial ownership (usually 20-50% of shares). Distinguished from a subsidiary (子会社), which is majority-owned.
関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)と協力(きょうりょく)する。
To cooperate with affiliated companies.
この会社(かいしゃ)は大手(おおて)メーカーの関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)だ。
This company is an affiliate of a major manufacturer.
関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)を含(ふく)めたグループ全体(ぜんたい)の売上(うりあげ)は過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)を記録(きろく)した。
Total group sales, including affiliated companies, reached a record high.

Compound of 関連(かんれん) (related, affiliated) and 会社(かいしゃ) (company). In accounting and legal terms, this refers specifically to a company where the parent holds significant influence (typically 20-50% ownership) but not majority control.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)を設立(せつりつ)する — to establish an affiliated company
  • 関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)の業績(ぎょうせき) — affiliated company's performance
  • 関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)への出資(しゅっし) — investment in an affiliated company
  • 関連会社(かんれんがいしゃ)を含(ふく)む — including affiliated companies

RELATED TERMS:

  • 親会社(おやがいしゃ) — parent company
  • 子会社(こがいしゃ) — subsidiary (majority-owned)
  • グループ会社(ぐるーぷがいしゃ) — group company (informal collective term)
  • 系列(けいれつ) — keiretsu; business group network
