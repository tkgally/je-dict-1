砂丘(さきゅう)

さきゅう
noun
sand dune
1. sand dune; sand hill
A mound or ridge of sand formed by wind, found in deserts, along coasts, and at riverbanks.
砂丘(さきゅう)を歩(ある)く。
To walk on a sand dune.
鳥取(とっとり)砂丘(さきゅう)は日本(にほん)で一番(いちばん)有名(ゆうめい)な砂丘(さきゅう)だ。
The Tottori Sand Dunes are the most famous sand dunes in Japan.
風(かぜ)が作(つく)り出(だ)す砂丘(さきゅう)の模様(もよう)は美(うつく)しく、毎日(まいにち)表情(ひょうじょう)が変(か)わる。
The patterns the wind creates on the sand dunes are beautiful and change every day.

Composed of 砂(さ) (sand) + 丘(きゅう) (hill, mound). Japan's most famous sand dunes are the 鳥取砂丘(とっとりさきゅう) in Tottori Prefecture, which are a major tourist destination and a designated natural monument.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 砂丘(さきゅう)を歩(ある)く: to walk on sand dunes
  • 海岸(かいがん)砂丘(さきゅう): coastal sand dune
  • 砂丘(さきゅう)地帯(ちたい): dune area
  • 砂丘(さきゅう)の風紋(ふうもん): wind ripples on sand dunes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 砂漠(さばく): desert (a much larger arid landscape; 砂丘(さきゅう) are individual formations)
  • 砂浜(すなはま): sandy beach (flat, along the shoreline)
  • 丘(おか): hill (general; not necessarily sand)
