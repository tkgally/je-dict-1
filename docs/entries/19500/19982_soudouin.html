1. general mobilization; full mobilization
Mobilizing all available people or resources for a task or effort. Originally a military term, now widely used figuratively to mean marshaling all hands or all means.
社員(しゃいん)を総動員(そうどういん)する。
To mobilize all employees.
家族(かぞく)総動員(そうどういん)で大掃除(おおそうじ)をした。
The whole family pitched in for the big cleaning.
イベントの準備(じゅんび)のため、スタッフを総動員(そうどういん)して作業(さぎょう)にあたった。
All staff were mobilized to prepare for the event.

A compound of 総(そう) (total, all) and 動員(どういん) (mobilization). Originally a military term referring to calling up all forces, now commonly used figuratively in everyday contexts to mean rallying all available people or resources.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 総動員(そうどういん)する — to fully mobilize
  • 総動員(そうどういん)をかける — to call for full mobilization
  • 家族(かぞく)総動員(そうどういん) — the whole family mobilized
  • 全力(ぜんりょく)総動員(そうどういん) — full-power mobilization

RELATED TERMS:

  • 動員(どういん) — mobilization (the base word)
  • 結集(けっしゅう) — rallying, mustering (of forces or efforts)
  • 総力(そうりょく) — total power, all-out effort
