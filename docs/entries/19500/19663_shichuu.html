支柱(しちゅう)

しちゅう
noun
pillar; support; prop; mainstay
1. pillar; support; prop; post
A physical column or post that supports a structure, plant, or object.
トマトに支柱(しちゅう)を立(た)てる。
Set up a support stake for the tomato plant.
支柱(しちゅう)が折(お)れて、テントが倒(たお)れた。
The support pole broke and the tent collapsed.
強風(きょうふう)に耐(た)えられるよう、金属(きんぞく)の支柱(しちゅう)で補強(ほきょう)した。
We reinforced it with metal supports to withstand strong winds.
2. mainstay; backbone; pillar
A person, thing, or concept that serves as the main support for a group, organization, or system.
彼(かれ)はチームの支柱(しちゅう)だ。
He is the pillar of the team.
農業(のうぎょう)は地域(ちいき)経済(けいざい)の支柱(しちゅう)である。
Agriculture is the mainstay of the local economy.
家族(かぞく)の支柱(しちゅう)を失(うしな)い、彼女(かのじょ)は途方(とほう)に暮(く)れた。
Having lost the pillar of her family, she was at a complete loss.

A compound of 支(し) (to support) and 柱(ちゅう) (pillar). Used both for literal structural supports and figuratively for people or things that hold something up.

USAGE:
In the physical sense, commonly used for garden stakes, tent poles, and structural supports. In the figurative sense, describes indispensable people or elements — the "backbone" of a family, organization, or economy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 支柱(しちゅう)を立(た)てる — to set up a support/stake
  • 支柱(しちゅう)を失(うしな)う — to lose one's pillar/mainstay
  • 経済(けいざい)の支柱(しちゅう) — economic mainstay
  • 精神的(せいしんてき)支柱(しちゅう) — emotional/spiritual pillar

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 柱(はしら) — pillar, column; more general, both literal and figurative
  • 大黒柱(だいこくばしら) — main pillar; the primary breadwinner or central support
  • 土台(どだい) — foundation; the base that supports a structure
