1. to squeeze out; to wring out
To physically extract a liquid or substance by squeezing or pressing.
チューブから絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す。
To squeeze out from a tube.
ぞうきんから水(みず)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)した。
I wrung the water out of the rag.
レモンから最後(さいご)の一滴(いってき)まで果汁(かじゅう)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)した。
I squeezed out every last drop of juice from the lemon.
2. to eke out; to muster; to produce with great effort
To produce something — words, ideas, energy, money — through great effort, as if squeezing it out of oneself.
声(こえ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)した。
I managed to squeeze out a voice.
知恵(ちえ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)して解決策(かいけつさく)を考(かんが)えた。
I racked my brains and came up with a solution.
残(のこ)りわずかな体力(たいりょく)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)して、ゴールまで走(はし)り切(き)った。
I mustered my last remaining strength and ran all the way to the finish.

A compound verb combining 絞(しぼ)る (to squeeze, to wring) and 出(だ)す (to put out, to produce). The figurative sense (sense 2) is extremely common and vivid — the image of squeezing something out of oneself with difficulty.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 声(こえ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す — to force out a voice (when one can barely speak)
  • 知恵(ちえ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す — to rack one's brains
  • 勇気(ゆうき)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す — to muster courage
  • 体力(たいりょく)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す — to summon one's last strength
  • 涙(なみだ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)す — to squeeze out tears

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 絞(しぼ)る — to squeeze, to wring; the base verb without the 'out' nuance
  • 振(ふ)り絞(しぼ)る — to muster (strength, courage); emphasizes gathering from reserves
  • 搾(しぼ)り取(と)る — to squeeze out, to exploit; often has a negative connotation of extraction
