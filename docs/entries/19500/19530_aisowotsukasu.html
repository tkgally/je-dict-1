1. to be fed up with; to lose patience with; to be disgusted with
To completely lose one's goodwill or patience toward someone due to repeated disappointment. Implies that one has reached the end of their tolerance.
彼女(かのじょ)に愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かされた。
She got fed up with me.
何度(なんど)約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)っても変(か)わらないので、愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かした。
He kept breaking promises and never changed, so I gave up on him.
社員(しゃいん)たちは会社(かいしゃ)の対応(たいおう)に愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かして、次々(つぎつぎ)と辞(や)めていった。
The employees lost patience with the company's handling of things and quit one after another.

Literally 'to exhaust one's goodwill.' 愛想(あいそ) means friendliness or amiability, and 尽(つ)かす means to exhaust or use up completely. When someone 愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かす, they have run out of all patience and goodwill.

GRAMMAR:
The person one is fed up with is marked by に: ～に愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かす. The passive form 愛想(あいそ)を尽(つ)かされる ('to have someone give up on you') is very common.

VARIANTS:

  • 愛想(あいそう)を尽(つ)かす — variant pronunciation with long vowel
  • 愛想(あいそ)尽(づ)かし — the noun form meaning 'being fed up'

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うんざりする — to be fed up, sick of; more casual, can apply to situations as well as people
  • 見限(みかぎ)る — to give up on someone; implies abandoning someone as hopeless
  • 呆(あき)れる — to be exasperated; focuses on shock/disbelief at someone's behavior
