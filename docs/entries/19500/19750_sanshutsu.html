1. production; output; yield
The act of producing or yielding natural resources, agricultural products, or other goods from a particular region. Emphasizes what a land or region naturally produces, rather than manufacturing.
石油(せきゆ)を産出(さんしゅつ)する国(くに)。
A country that produces oil.
この地域(ちいき)は金(きん)の産出(さんしゅつ)で知(し)られている。
This region is known for its gold production.
産出量(さんしゅつりょう)が年々(ねんねん)減少(げんしょう)しているため、新(あたら)しい鉱山(こうざん)の開発(かいはつ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Since output has been declining year by year, development of new mines is necessary.

Compound of 産(さん) (produce, give birth) and 出(しゅつ) (put out, emit). Primarily used for natural resources and agricultural products that a region yields, rather than manufactured goods.

Used both as a noun (産出(さんしゅつ)) and as a する verb (産出(さんしゅつ)する — to produce, to yield).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 産出量(さんしゅつりょう) — production volume; yield
  • 産出国(さんしゅつこく) — producing country
  • 石油(せきゆ)を産出(さんしゅつ)する — to produce oil
  • 鉱物(こうぶつ)を産出(さんしゅつ)する — to produce minerals

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生産(せいさん) — production; broader term including manufacturing
  • 算出(さんしゅつ) — calculation (same reading, different kanji — a common homophone)
  • 産地(さんち) — production area; place of origin
  • 採掘(さいくつ) — mining; extraction
