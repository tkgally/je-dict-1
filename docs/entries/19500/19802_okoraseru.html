1. to make angry; to anger; to provoke; to upset
To cause someone to become angry through one's words or actions. The causative form of 怒(おこ)る (to get angry), used as a standard transitive verb.
彼女(かのじょ)を怒(おこ)らせてしまった。
I ended up making her angry.
先生(せんせい)を怒(おこ)らせないほうがいい。
You'd better not anger the teacher.
約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)って友達(ともだち)を怒(おこ)らせてしまい、深(ふか)く反省(はんせい)した。
I broke a promise and made my friend angry, and I deeply regretted it.

The causative form of 怒(おこ)る (to get angry). Very commonly used in everyday conversation, especially with ～てしまう to express that one unintentionally made someone angry.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • （人(ひと)）を怒(おこ)らせる — to make someone angry
  • 怒(おこ)らせてしまった — ended up making (someone) angry (unintentional)
  • 怒(おこ)らせないように — so as not to anger (someone)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怒(おこ)る — to get angry (intransitive base verb)
  • 腹(はら)を立(た)てさせる — to make someone upset (slightly more formal)
  • 機嫌(きげん)を損(そこ)ねる — to offend, to put in a bad mood (more indirect/subtle)
  • 激怒(げきど)させる — to enrage (much stronger)
