明(あき)らか

あきらか
na-adjective
obvious, clear
1. obvious, evident
Easily seen or understood.
彼(かれ)の間違(まちが)いは明らかだ。
His mistake is obvious.
違(ちが)いは明(あき)らかだ。
The difference is obvious.
Sense 1: obvious
彼(かれ)が犯人(はんにん)なのは明(あき)らかだ。
It's obvious that he is the culprit.
Sense 1: evident
事実(じじつ)は明(あき)らかだ。
The facts are evident.
Sense 1: evident
明(あき)らかな改善(かいぜん)が見(み)られる。
There is obvious improvement.
Sense 1: obvious
2. clear, plain
Free from doubt or ambiguity.
明らかに嘘(うそ)だ。
It's clearly a lie.
Sense 2: clear
明(あき)らかな証拠(しょうこ)がある。
There is clear evidence.
Sense 2: clear
原因(げんいん)を明(あき)らかにする。
To clarify the cause.
Sense 2: to make clear
明(あき)らかに間違(まちが)っている。
It's plainly wrong.
Sense 2: plain
意図(いと)を明(あき)らかにする。
To make one's intentions clear.
Sense 2: clear

明(あき)らか is a na-adjective meaning obvious, clear, or evident.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 明(あき)らかな + noun
  • Adverb: 明(あき)らかに (clearly, obviously)
  • Verb form: 明(あき)らかにする (to clarify, to make clear)
  • Verb form: 明(あき)らかになる (to become clear)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 明(あき)らかな事実(じじつ): an obvious fact
  • 明(あき)らかな証拠(しょうこ): clear evidence
  • 明(あき)らかに違(ちが)う: clearly different
  • 明(あき)らかに間違(まちが)っている: obviously wrong
  • 原因(げんいん)を明(あき)らかにする: to clarify the cause
  • 真相(しんそう)が明(あき)らかになる: the truth becomes clear

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明確(めいかく): clear, definite (more formal)
  • 明白(めいはく): obvious, evident (formal, literary)
  • 歴然(れきぜん): evident, unmistakable
  • はっきり: clearly (adverb, casual)
  • 確(たし)か: certain, sure

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 明(あき/めい) means 'bright' or 'clear.' Related words include 明(あか)るい (bright), 説明(せつめい) (explanation), and 証明(しょうめい) (proof).

USAGE NOTE:
明(あき)らか is commonly used in both spoken and written Japanese to state that something is obviously true or evident.
