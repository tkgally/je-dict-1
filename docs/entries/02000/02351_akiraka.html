明(あき)らか

あきらか
na-adjective
obvious, clear

Definitions

1. obvious, evident
Easily seen or understood.
2. clear, plain
Free from doubt or ambiguity.

Examples

彼(かれ)の間違(まちが)いは明らかだ。
His mistake is obvious.
明らかに嘘(うそ)だ。
It's clearly a lie.

Notes

明(あき)らか is a na-adjective.

USAGE:

  • 明(あき)らかな + noun: obvious ~
  • 明(あき)らかに + verb: clearly ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明確(めいかく): clear, definite
  • 明白(めいはく): obvious, evident
