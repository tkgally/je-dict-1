1. to cook (rice)
To prepare rice by boiling.
ご飯(はん)を炊(た)く。
To cook rice.
炊飯器(すいはんき)で炊(た)く。
To cook in a rice cooker.
玄米(げんまい)を炊(た)く。
To cook brown rice.
炊(た)きたてのご飯(はん)。
Freshly cooked rice.
朝(あさ)ご飯(はん)を炊(た)く。
To cook rice for breakfast.
お祝(いわ)いの日(ひ)に赤飯(せきはん)を炊(た)いた。
I cooked red rice for the celebration.
2. to boil
To cook food in water.
大豆(だいず)を炊(た)く。
To boil soybeans.
小豆(あずき)を炊(た)く。
To boil red beans.
煮物(にもの)を炊(た)く。
To cook simmered dishes.
季節(きせつ)の野菜(やさい)をたっぷりの水(みず)で炊(た)いた。
I boiled seasonal vegetables in plenty of water.
京都(きょうと)では大根(だいこん)や里芋(さといも)をじっくり炊(た)いた料理(りょうり)が有名(ゆうめい)だ。
In Kyoto, dishes where daikon radish and taro are slowly simmered are famous.

炊(た)く specifically means to cook rice or grains by boiling.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を particle.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ご飯(はん)を炊(た)く: to cook rice
  • 炊(た)きたて: freshly cooked
  • 炊飯器(すいはんき): rice cooker
  • 炊(た)き込(こ)みご飯(はん): mixed rice (cooked with ingredients)

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 炊(た)く: to cook (rice, grains)
  • 焚(た)く: to burn, kindle (fire, incense)

CONTRAST WITH OTHER COOKING VERBS:

  • 炊(た)く: for rice/grains
  • 煮(に)る: to simmer, stew
  • 茹(ゆ)でる: to boil (pasta, vegetables)
  • 焼(や)く: to grill, bake
