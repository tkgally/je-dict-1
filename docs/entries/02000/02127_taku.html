炊(た)く

たく
verb (godan)
to cook (rice), to boil
1. to cook (rice)
To prepare rice by boiling.
ご飯(はん)を炊(た)く。
To cook rice.
炊飯器(すいはんき)で炊(た)く。
To cook in a rice cooker.
2. to boil
To cook food in water.

炊(た)く is for cooking rice/grains.

KANJI VARIANTS (both たく):

  • 炊(た)く: to cook (rice)
  • 焚(た)く: to burn, to kindle

COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご飯(はん)を炊(た)く: to cook rice
  • 炊(た)きたて: freshly cooked
