具(そな)える

そなえる
ichidan verb
to be equipped with, to possess
1. to be equipped with, to possess
To have something as a built-in feature or quality.
才能(さいのう)を具えている。
To possess talent.

具(そな)える means to possess or be equipped with inherent qualities or features.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

Three verbs read そなえる with different nuances:

  • 具(そな)える: to possess (innate qualities)
  • 備(そな)える: to prepare, equip (external)
  • 供(そな)える: to offer (to gods/deceased)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 才能(さいのう)を具(そな)える: to possess talent
  • 条件(じょうけん)を具(そな)える: to meet the requirements
  • 要素(ようそ)を具(そな)える: to have the elements

GRAMMAR NOTE:

Often used in the pattern 〜を具(そな)えた + noun:

  • 才能(さいのう)を具(そな)えた人(ひと): a talented person
  • 機能(きのう)を具(そな)えた製品(せいひん): a product equipped with features

This form is relatively formal/literary.
