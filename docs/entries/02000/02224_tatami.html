畳(たたみ)

たたみ
noun
tatami mat

Definitions

1. tatami mat
A traditional Japanese floor mat made of woven rush.

Examples

畳(たたみ)の部屋(へや)で寝(ね)る。
To sleep in a tatami room.
畳(たたみ)の上(うえ)に座(すわ)る。
To sit on the tatami.

Notes

畳(たたみ) is a traditional Japanese flooring.

MEASUREMENT:

畳(じょう) is used as a unit for room size.

  • 六畳(ろくじょう): 6 tatami mats (about 10 sq meters)

CULTURE:

  • Remove shoes before stepping on tatami
  • Never step on the edges (borders)
  • Common in traditional Japanese rooms
