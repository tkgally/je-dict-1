1. to yield, to give way
To give precedence or make way for someone.
席(せき)を譲(ゆず)る。
To give up one's seat.
道(みち)を譲(ゆず)った。
I yielded the way.
お年寄(としよ)りに席(せき)を譲(ゆず)った。
I gave up my seat for an elderly person.
2. to hand over, to transfer
To pass ownership or responsibility to another.
権利(けんり)を譲(ゆず)る。
To transfer rights.
会社(かいしゃ)を息子(むすこ)に譲(ゆず)った。
I handed over the company to my son.
祖父(そふ)が土地(とち)を私(わたし)に譲(ゆず)ってくれた。
My grandfather transferred the land to me.

譲(ゆず)る means to yield, give way, or hand over something to another person.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: AがBに〜を譲(ゆず)る (A yields/gives ~ to B)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)を譲(ゆず)る: to give way, yield the road
  • 席(せき)を譲(ゆず)る: to give up one's seat
  • 後(あと)を譲(ゆず)る: to hand over (succession)
  • 権利(けんり)を譲(ゆず)る: to transfer rights
  • 一歩(いっぽ)も譲(ゆず)らない: not give an inch

RELATED WORDS:

  • 譲(ゆず)り合(あ)う: to yield to each other, compromise
  • 譲渡(じょうと): transfer (formal/legal term)

USAGE:
Commonly used on public transport signs encouraging passengers to yield seats to elderly or disabled people: 席(せき)をお譲(ゆず)りください.
