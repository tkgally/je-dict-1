皆(みな)さん

みなさん
noun
everyone, all of you

Definitions

1. everyone, all of you, ladies and gentlemen
A polite way to address a group of people.

Examples

皆(みな)さん、おはようございます。
Good morning, everyone.
皆(みな)さんは元気(げんき)ですか。
How is everyone?
皆(みな)さんに会(あ)えてうれしいです。
I'm happy to meet all of you.

Notes

皆(みな)さん is a polite form for addressing groups.

RELATED:

  • みんな (everyone - casual)
  • 皆(みな) (everyone - neutral)
  • 皆様(みなさま) (everyone - very polite)

USAGE: Common in speeches, announcements, and formal greetings.
