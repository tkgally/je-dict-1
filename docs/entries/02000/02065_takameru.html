高(たか)める

たかめる
verb (ichidan)
to raise, to enhance
1. to raise, to elevate
To make something higher.
意識(いしき)を高(たか)める。
To raise awareness.
声(こえ)を高(たか)める。
To raise one's voice.
2. to enhance, to improve
To increase quality or level.
能力(のうりょく)を高(たか)める。
To enhance one's abilities.

高(たか)める is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to raise, elevate, or enhance.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 高(たか)まる (intransitive, to rise/increase)

ASPECT (ている):
高(たか)めている indicates an ongoing effort to improve or elevate something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 能力(のうりょく)を高(たか)める: to improve abilities
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を高(たか)める: to enhance skills
  • 質(しつ)を高(たか)める: to improve quality
  • 効率(こうりつ)を高(たか)める: to increase efficiency
  • 意識(いしき)を高(たか)める: to raise awareness
  • 士気(しき)を高(たか)める: to boost morale
  • 声(こえ)を高(たか)める: to raise one's voice

USAGE:
Often used in business and self-improvement contexts. The pair 高(たか)まる describes something rising on its own: 期待(きたい)が高(たか)まる (expectations rise).
