ぶつける

ぶつける
ichidan verb
to hit, to strike against
1. to hit, to knock
To strike something against another thing.
頭(あたま)をぶつけた。
I hit my head.
2. to direct at, to take out on
To express feelings toward someone.
怒(いか)りをぶつける。
To vent one's anger.

ぶつける means to hit something against something else, or to direct emotions at someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: ぶつかる (intransitive, to collide)

TWO MAIN USES:
1. Physical: to strike against, bump
2. Figurative: to direct (emotions) at someone

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 頭(あたま)をぶつける: to bump one's head
  • 車(くるま)をぶつける: to hit with a car
  • 怒(いか)りをぶつける: to vent anger
  • 気持(きも)ちをぶつける: to express feelings
  • 不満(ふまん)をぶつける: to voice complaints to
  • 八(や)つ当(あ)たりをぶつける: to take frustrations out on

RELATED:

  • ぶつかる: to collide with (intransitive)
  • 当(あ)たる: to hit, strike
