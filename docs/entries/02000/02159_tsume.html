爪(つめ)

つめ
noun
nail, claw, talon
1. fingernail, toenail
The hard covering at the tip of human fingers and toes.
2. claw, talon
The sharp curved nail of an animal or bird.
爪(つめ)を切(き)る。
To cut one's nails.
猫(ねこ)が爪(つめ)を立(た)てた。
The cat extended its claws.

爪(つめ) refers to nails and claws.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 爪(つめ)の垢(あか)を煎(せん)じて飲(の)む: to learn from someone's example
  • 爪(つめ)に火(ひ)を灯(とも)す: to live frugally

COMPOUNDS:

  • 爪切(つめき)り: nail clipper
  • 爪先(つまさき): tiptoe
  • 足(あし)の爪(つめ): toenail
