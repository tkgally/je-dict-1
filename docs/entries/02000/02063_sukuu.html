救(すく)う

すくう
verb (godan)
to save, to rescue

Definitions

1. to save, to rescue
To free from danger or harm.
2. to help, to relieve
To assist someone in need.

Examples

命(いのち)を救(すく)う。
To save a life.
溺(おぼ)れている人(ひと)を救(すく)った。
I rescued a drowning person.
彼(かれ)に救(すく)われた。
I was saved by him.

Notes

救(すく)う means to save or rescue.

COMMON USES:

  • 命(いのち)を救(すく)う: to save a life
  • 世界(せかい)を救(すく)う: to save the world

RELATED:

  • 救(すく)い: salvation, help
  • 救助(きゅうじょ): rescue
  • 助(たす)ける: to help (more general)
