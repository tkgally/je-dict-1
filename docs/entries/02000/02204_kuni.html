国(くに)

くに
noun
country, nation, homeland
1. country, nation
A territory with its own government.
2. homeland, hometown
One's place of origin.
どの国(くに)から来(き)ましたか。
Which country are you from?
国(くに)に帰(かえ)る。
To return to one's home country.

国(くに) means country.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 国民(こくみん): citizens
  • 外国(がいこく): foreign country
  • 国際(こくさい): international
  • 国籍(こくせき): nationality

HOMETOWN:
In older usage, 国(くに) can mean one's hometown or home region, not just country.
