りんご

りんご
noun
apple
1. apple
A round fruit with red or green skin.
りんごを食(た)べる。
To eat an apple.
りんごは赤(あか)い。
Apples are red.

りんご is typically written in hiragana, though the kanji 林檎(りんご) exists.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • りんごを剥(む)く: to peel an apple
  • りんごを切(き)る: to cut an apple
  • りんご狩(が)り: apple picking
  • りんご酢(す): apple vinegar

VARIETIES:

  • ふじ: Fuji (most popular variety)
  • つがる: Tsugaru
  • 紅玉(こうぎょく): Jonathan apple

COMPOUNDS:

  • りんごジュース: apple juice
  • りんご飴(あめ): candied apple
  • りんごパイ: apple pie

青森県(あおもりけん) is Japan's top apple-producing region, accounting for over half of domestic production. The phrase 「りんごが赤(あか)くなると医者(いしゃ)が青(あお)くなる」means apples are considered very healthy.
