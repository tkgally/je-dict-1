剥(む)く

むく
godan verb
to peel, to skin
1. to peel, to skin
To remove the outer layer or covering of something.
りんごの皮(かわ)を剥く。
To peel an apple.
目(め)を剥く。
To open one's eyes wide (in surprise).
Idiomatic expression

剥(む)く means to peel or remove the outer layer of something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 剥(む)ける (intransitive - to peel off, come off)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 皮(かわ)を剥(む)く: to peel skin/rind
  • りんごを剥(む)く: to peel an apple
  • 卵(たまご)の殻(から)を剥(む)く: to peel an egg shell

IDIOMS:

  • 目(め)を剥(む)く: to open eyes wide (in surprise)
  • 牙(きば)を剥(む)く: to bare one's fangs

HOMOPHONE DISTINCTION:

  • 剥(む)く: to peel
  • 向(む)く: to face, to turn toward
