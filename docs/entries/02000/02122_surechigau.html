1. to pass by one another
To go past each other from opposite directions.
道(みち)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other on the road.
電車(でんしゃ)がすれ違(ちが)った。
The trains passed each other.
駅(えき)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other at the station.
狭(せま)い道(みち)で車(くるま)がすれ違(ちが)うのは大変(たいへん)だ。
It's difficult for cars to pass each other on a narrow road.
知(し)らない人(ひと)と廊下(ろうか)ですれ違(ちが)ったとき、会釈(えしゃく)をした。
When I passed someone I didn't know in the hallway, I gave a slight bow.
2. to miss each other
To fail to meet or understand each other.
気持(きも)ちがすれ違(ちが)う。
Our feelings don't connect.
意見(いけん)がすれ違(ちが)った。
Our opinions didn't match.
話(はなし)がすれ違(ちが)っている。
We're talking past each other.
忙(いそが)しくてなかなか会(あ)えず、いつもすれ違(ちが)ってばかりだ。
We're so busy we can never meet — we keep missing each other.
二人(ふたり)の気持(きも)ちが少(すこ)しずつすれ違(ちが)って、最後(さいご)には別(わか)れてしまった。
Their feelings gradually drifted apart, and in the end they broke up.

すれ違(ちが)う means to pass by each other or to miss each other (physically or figuratively).

TRANSITIVITY:
Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

PHYSICAL MEANING:

  • 道(みち)ですれ違(ちが)う: to pass each other on the street
  • 電車(でんしゃ)がすれ違(ちが)う: trains pass each other

FIGURATIVE MEANING:

  • 気持(きも)ちがすれ違(ちが)う: feelings don't connect
  • 話(はなし)がすれ違(ちが)う: talking past each other
  • 意見(いけん)がすれ違(ちが)う: opinions don't match

RELATED:

  • すれ違(ちが)い (noun): passing, miscommunication

Related: 通(とお)り過(す)ぎる (to pass by (one-way))
