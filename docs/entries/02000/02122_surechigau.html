すれ違(ちが)う

すれちがう
verb (godan)
to pass by one another, to miss each other
1. to pass by one another
To go past each other from opposite directions.
道(みち)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other on the road.
電車(でんしゃ)がすれ違(ちが)った。
The trains passed each other.
駅(えき)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other at the station.
車(くるま)がすれ違(ちが)う。
Cars pass each other.
廊下(ろうか)ですれ違(ちが)った。
We passed each other in the hallway.
2. to miss each other
To fail to meet or understand each other.
気持(きも)ちがすれ違(ちが)う。
Our feelings don't connect.
意見(いけん)がすれ違(ちが)った。
Our opinions didn't match.
話(はなし)がすれ違(ちが)っている。
We're talking past each other.
タイミングがすれ違(ちが)った。
The timing didn't work out.
二人(ふたり)の思(おも)いがすれ違(ちが)った。
The two of them didn't understand each other.

すれ違(ちが)う means to pass by each other or to miss each other (physically or figuratively).

TRANSITIVITY:
Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

PHYSICAL MEANING:

  • 道(みち)ですれ違(ちが)う: to pass each other on the street
  • 電車(でんしゃ)がすれ違(ちが)う: trains pass each other

FIGURATIVE MEANING:

  • 気持(きも)ちがすれ違(ちが)う: feelings don't connect
  • 話(はなし)がすれ違(ちが)う: talking past each other
  • 意見(いけん)がすれ違(ちが)う: opinions don't match

RELATED:

  • すれ違(ちが)い (noun): passing, miscommunication
