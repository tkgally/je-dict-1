鶏肉(とりにく)

とりにく
noun
chicken (meat)

Definitions

1. chicken (meat)
The meat of a chicken used as food.

Examples

鶏肉(とりにく)を焼(や)く。
To grill chicken.
今日(きょう)は鶏肉(とりにく)を食(た)べたい。
I want to eat chicken today.
鶏肉(とりにく)のカレー
Chicken curry.

Notes

鶏肉(とりにく) means chicken meat.

MEAT WORDS:

  • 牛肉(ぎゅうにく) (beef)
  • 豚肉(ぶたにく) (pork)
  • 鶏肉(とりにく) (chicken)
  • 肉(にく) (meat in general)

CHICKEN DISHES:

  • 焼(や)き鳥(とり) (grilled chicken skewers)
  • 唐揚(からあ)げ (fried chicken)
  • 親子丼(おやこどん) (chicken and egg bowl)
