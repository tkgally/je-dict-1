退(ど)く

どく
godan verb
to get out of the way, to step aside
1. to step aside, to move out of the way
To move from one's current position to make room.
ちょっと退いてください。
Please step aside for a moment.
道(みち)を退く。
To get out of the way.
Sense 1: to move out of the way
そこを退(ど)いて！
Move out of there!
Sense 1: to step aside
危(あぶ)ないから退(ど)いて。
It's dangerous, so move away.
Sense 1: to step aside
邪魔(じゃま)だから退(ど)いてくれ。
You're in the way, so move.
Sense 1: to get out of the way
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)るから少(すこ)し退(ど)いて。
Move a bit so I can take a photo.
Sense 1: to move aside
2. to withdraw, to retire
To leave a position or place.
社長(しゃちょう)の座(ざ)を退(ど)く。
To step down as president.
Sense 2: to retire
役職(やくしょく)を退(ど)く。
To resign from a position.
Sense 2: to withdraw
引退(いんたい)して表舞台(おもてぶたい)を退(ど)いた。
Retired and withdrew from the public eye.
Sense 2: to retire
第一線(だいいっせん)を退(ど)く。
To retire from the front lines.
Sense 2: to withdraw

退(ど)く is a godan verb meaning to move out of the way or step aside.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No transitive pair with same reading

REGISTER:
Casual to neutral. Common in everyday speech for asking people to move.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)を退(ど)く: to get out of the way
  • そこを退(ど)く: to move from there
  • 座(ざ)を退(ど)く: to step down from a position

IMPERATIVE FORMS:

  • 退(ど)け！: Move! (rough command)
  • 退(ど)いて！: Please move! (te-form request)
  • 退(ど)いてください: Please step aside (polite)

ALTERNATE READING:

  • 退(しりぞ)く: to retreat, withdraw (more formal/literary)

SIMILAR:

  • どける (transitive): to move something out of the way
