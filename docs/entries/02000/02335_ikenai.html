いけない

いけない
adjective
no good, must not
1. no good, won't do
Something is unacceptable or won't work.
これではいけない。
This won't do.
2. must not
Used to express prohibition.
ここで泳(およ)いではいけない。
You must not swim here.

いけない is often used in grammar patterns.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜てはいけない: must not ~
  • 〜なければいけない: must ~
  • 〜ないといけない: have to ~

VARIANT:

  • いけません (polite)
general
