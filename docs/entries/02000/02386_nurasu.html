1. to wet, to moisten
To make something damp with water or liquid.
タオルを濡(ぬ)らす。
To wet a towel.
手(て)を濡(ぬ)らさずに済(す)む。
To manage without getting one's hands wet.
Idiomatic: to avoid effort
雨(あめ)で服(ふく)を濡(ぬ)らしてしまったので、着替(きが)えなければならなかった。
I got my clothes wet in the rain, so I had to change.

濡(ぬ)らす is a godan verb meaning to wet or moisten something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 濡(ぬ)れる (intransitive - to get wet)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを濡(ぬ)らす: to wet X
  • Xで濡(ぬ)らす: to wet with X

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タオルを濡(ぬ)らす: to wet a towel
  • 髪(かみ)を濡(ぬ)らす: to wet one's hair
  • 唇(くちびる)を濡(ぬ)らす: to moisten one's lips
  • 涙(なみだ)で頬(ほお)を濡(ぬ)らす: to wet one's cheeks with tears

IDIOM:
手(て)を濡(ぬ)らさずに: without lifting a finger (lit. without wetting one's hands) - to achieve something without effort
