教(おそ)わる

おそわる
verb (godan)
to be taught, to learn from

Definitions

1. to be taught, to learn from
To receive instruction or knowledge from someone.

Examples

先生(せんせい)に日本語(にほんご)を教(おそ)わった。
I was taught Japanese by my teacher.
母(はは)に料理(りょうり)を教(おそ)わる。
I learn cooking from my mother.
彼(かれ)から多(おお)くのことを教(おそ)わった。
I learned many things from him.

Notes

教(おそ)わる is the receiving-side counterpart of 教(おし)える.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 教(おし)える (transitive): to teach
  • 教(おそ)わる (intransitive): to be taught

PATTERN:

  • [person] に [subject] を 教(おそ)わる
  • 先生(せんせい)にピアノを教(おそ)わる: to learn piano from a teacher
