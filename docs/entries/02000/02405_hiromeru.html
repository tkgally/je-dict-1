1. to spread, to disseminate
To make something known to many people.
知識(ちしき)を広(ひろ)める。
To spread knowledge.
日本文化(にほんぶんか)を海外(かいがい)に広(ひろ)める。
To spread Japanese culture overseas.
SNSを使(つか)って自分(じぶん)の名前(なまえ)を広(ひろ)めた。
I made my name known using social media.
2. to broaden, to widen
To expand knowledge or experience.
視野(しや)を広(ひろ)める。
To broaden one's horizons.
旅行(りょこう)をして見聞(けんぶん)を広(ひろ)める。
To broaden one's experience through travel.
読書(どくしょ)は世界(せかい)を広(ひろ)めてくれる。
Reading broadens your world.

広(ひろ)める is an ichidan verb meaning to spread, disseminate, or broaden.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 広(ひろ)まる (intransitive: to spread, become widespread)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 知識(ちしき)を広(ひろ)める: to spread knowledge
  • 名(な)を広(ひろ)める: to make one's name known
  • 教(おし)えを広(ひろ)める: to spread teachings
  • 視野(しや)を広(ひろ)める: to broaden one's horizons
  • 見聞(けんぶん)を広(ひろ)める: to broaden one's experience

USAGE:
Often used for spreading ideas, knowledge, or influence. For physical spreading (like spreading a map), 広(ひろ)げる is more common.

Contrast: 広(ひろ)げる (spread physically)
