従(したが)う

したがう
verb (godan)
to follow, to obey, to comply
1. to follow, to accompany
To go along with someone.
2. to obey, to comply with
To act according to rules or orders.
規則(きそく)に従(したが)う。
To obey the rules.
指示(しじ)に従(したが)ってください。
Please follow the instructions.
3. as, in accordance with
Used to indicate proportion or correlation.
年(とし)を取(と)るに従(したが)って忘(わす)れっぽくなる。
As you get older, you become more forgetful.

従(したが)う is a godan verb meaning to follow, obey, or comply.

KANJI:
従(したが/じゅう) means 'follow, obey, accompany'

THREE MAIN USES:
1. To follow someone/something
2. To obey rules or orders
3. 〜に従(したが)って: as ~, in proportion to ~

GRAMMAR PATTERN:

  • Noun + に従(したが)う: to obey/follow [noun]
  • Verb + に従(したが)って: as [verb] (proportional change)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 規則(きそく)に従(したが)う: to follow the rules
  • 指示(しじ)に従(したが)う: to follow instructions
  • 法律(ほうりつ)に従(したが)う: to comply with the law
