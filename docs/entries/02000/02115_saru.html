1. to leave, to go away
To depart from a place.
彼(かれ)は町(まち)を去(さ)った。
He left the town.
去(さ)る者(もの)は追(お)わず。
Don't chase after those who leave.
Proverb
故郷(こきょう)を去(さ)る。
To leave one's hometown.
職場(しょくば)を去(さ)ることにした。
I decided to leave the workplace.
長年(ながねん)働(はたら)いた会社(かいしゃ)を去(さ)るのは寂(さび)しかった。
It was sad to leave the company where I had worked for many years.
彼(かれ)が舞台(ぶたい)を去(さ)ったあとも、拍手(はくしゅ)はしばらく続(つづ)いていた。
Even after he left the stage, the applause continued for a while.

去(さ)る is a literary verb meaning to leave or depart, more formal than 離(はな)れる or 出(で)る.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes を particle to mark the place being left

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 場所(ばしょ)を去(さ)る: to leave a place
  • 故郷(こきょう)を去(さ)る: to leave one's hometown
  • 時(とき)が去(さ)る: time passes
  • 嵐(あらし)が去(さ)る: the storm passes

PROVERBS:

  • 去(さ)る者(もの)は追(お)わず: Don't chase those who leave
  • 去(さ)る者(もの)日日(ひび)に疎(うと)し: Out of sight, out of mind

IN COMPOUNDS:
去(きょ) reading in compounds: 去年(きょねん) (last year), 過去(かこ) (the past)

DON'T CONFUSE:
猿(さる) (monkey) has the same reading.

ASPECT (ている):

  • 去(さ)っている: has (already) departed (resultative state)
  • Note: 去(さ)る is a punctual verb of leaving, so the ている form describes the state of being gone, not the act of leaving. 彼(かれ)は既(すで)に町(まち)を去(さ)っている means "he has already left town" (he's gone). The past form 去(さ)った is more common for a simple "has left" event.
