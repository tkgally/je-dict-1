1. to embrace, to hug
To hold someone in one's arms.
子供(こども)を抱(だ)く。
To hold a child.
彼女(かのじょ)を強(つよ)く抱(だ)いた。
I hugged her tightly.
母(はは)に抱(だ)かれる。
To be held by one's mother.
優(やさ)しく抱(だ)いた。
I hugged gently.
別(わか)れ際(ぎわ)に抱(だ)いた。
I embraced at the moment of parting.
2. to hold, to carry
To hold something against one's body.
子供(こども)を抱(だ)く。
To hold a child.

To hold a dog.

抱(だ)く means to hold someone or something in one's arms, typically embracing warmly.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Passive: 抱(だ)かれる (to be held/embraced)

READINGS:

  • 抱(だ)く: to physically hold/embrace (common, spoken)
  • 抱(いだ)く: to harbor feelings, to hold in one's heart (literary)

ASPECT (ている):
抱(だ)いている indicates the ongoing state of holding something: 子供(こども)を抱(だ)いている (is holding a child).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を抱(だ)く: to hold a child
  • 強(つよ)く抱(だ)く: to embrace tightly
  • 優(やさ)しく抱(だ)く: to hold gently
  • 枕(まくら)を抱(だ)いて寝(ね)る: to sleep hugging a pillow

RELATED:

  • 抱(だ)きしめる: to hug tightly
  • 抱(だ)っこ: being held (child's word)

Related Words

Contrast: 抱(かか)える (to hold in the arms)
