乱暴(らんぼう)

らんぼう
na-adjective
rough, violent

Definitions

1. rough, violent, rude
Describes behavior, actions, or handling that is forceful, aggressive, or lacking care. Can describe physical violence or rough treatment.
2. violence, rough behavior
As a noun, refers to violent or rough actions.

Examples

乱暴(らんぼう)な運転(うんてん)はやめてください。
Please stop driving recklessly.
物(もの)を乱暴(らんぼう)に扱(あつか)わないで。
Don't handle things roughly.
乱暴(らんぼう)を働(はたら)く。
To commit violence.
Fixed expression

Notes

Can function as a na-adjective or a noun. Also used with する as a verb (乱暴(らんぼう)する = to act violently, to rough someone up).

FORMS:

  • 乱暴(らんぼう)な + noun (乱暴(らんぼう)な言葉(ことば) = rough language)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう)に + verb (乱暴(らんぼう)に扱(あつか)う = handle roughly)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう)する (act violently toward)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乱暴(らんぼう)な運転(うんてん) (reckless driving)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう)な言葉(ことば) (rough/rude language)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう)を働(はたら)く (commit violence)

Related Words

Antonym: 穏(おだ)やか (calm, gentle)
Related: 暴力(ぼうりょく) (violence)
