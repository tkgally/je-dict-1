卵(たまご)

たまご
noun
egg
1. egg
An oval reproductive body produced by birds.
卵(たまご)を割(わ)る。
To crack an egg.
卵焼(たまごや)きを作(つく)る。
To make a Japanese omelet.
2. novice, beginner
Someone who is not yet fully developed.

卵(たまご) means egg.

ALTERNATE WRITING:
Also written as 玉子(たまご), especially for chicken eggs used in cooking.

COMMON PREPARATIONS:

  • 目玉焼(めだまや)き: fried egg
  • 卵焼(たまごや)き: Japanese omelet
  • ゆで{卵(たまご)}: boiled egg
  • 生(なま)卵(たまご): raw egg
