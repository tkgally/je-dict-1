任(まか)せる

まかせる
ichidan verb
to entrust, to leave to
1. to entrust, to leave to
To give responsibility to someone else.
仕事(しごと)を任せる。
To entrust the work.
子供(こども)の世話(せわ)を母(はは)に任(まか)せる。
To entrust the care of children to mother.
Sense 1: to entrust
お任(まか)せします。
I'll leave it to you.
Sense 1: to leave to
判断(はんだん)は君(きみ)に任(まか)せる。
I'll leave the decision to you.
Sense 1: to entrust
料理(りょうり)は彼(かれ)に任(まか)せてある。
The cooking has been entrusted to him.
Sense 1: to leave to
2. to leave (to chance)
To let something happen naturally.
成(な)り行(ゆ)きに任せる。
To let things take their course.
Sense 2: to leave to chance
運(うん)を天(てん)に任(まか)せる。
To leave it to fate.
Sense 2: to leave to chance
流(なが)れに任(まか)せる。
To go with the flow.
Sense 2: to leave to chance
自然(しぜん)に任(まか)せる。
To let nature take its course.
Sense 2: to leave to chance
時(とき)の流(なが)れに身(み)を任(まか)せる。
To let oneself be carried by the flow of time.
Sense 2: to leave to chance

任(まか)せる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to entrust something to someone or let something take its course.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [person]に〜を任(まか)せる

TWO MAIN MEANINGS:
1. To entrust to a person: 仕事(しごと)を任(まか)せる (entrust work)
2. To leave to fate/nature: 流(なが)れに任(まか)せる (go with the flow)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お任(まか)せします: I'll leave it to you (polite, often at restaurants)
  • 任(まか)せて!: Leave it to me!
  • 運(うん)を天(てん)に任(まか)せる: to leave it to fate
  • 成(な)り行(ゆ)きに任(まか)せる: to let things take their course
  • 自然(しぜん)に任(まか)せる: to let nature take its course

RESTAURANT USAGE:
お任(まか)せ means "chef's choice" - the chef decides what to serve.
