巻(ま)く

まく
godan verb
to wind, to roll
1. to wind, to coil
To wrap something around.
マフラーを首(くび)に巻く。
To wrap a scarf around one's neck.
2. to roll up
To form into a cylinder shape.
袖(そで)を巻く。
To roll up one's sleeves.

巻(ま)く means to wind, wrap, or roll something around.

TRANSITIVITY:
Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • マフラーを首(くび)に巻(ま)く: to wrap a scarf around one's neck
  • 包帯(ほうたい)を巻(ま)く: to wrap a bandage
  • 袖(そで)を巻(ま)く: to roll up sleeves

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 巻(ま)き込(こ)む: to involve, drag into
  • 巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to rewind
  • 巻(ま)き上(あ)げる: to roll up

DON'T CONFUSE (all まく):

  • 巻(ま)く: to wind, wrap
  • 蒔(ま)く: to sow (seeds)
  • 撒(ま)く: to scatter
