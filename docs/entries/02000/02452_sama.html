～様(さま)

さま
suffix
Mr., Mrs., Ms. (formal honorific)
1. formal honorific suffix
A very polite title used after names, more formal than さん.
お客(きゃく)様、こちらへどうぞ。
Dear customer, this way please.

USAGE:

  • Used in formal/business settings
  • Used for customers (お客様)
  • Used in letters and formal writing
  • Used for deities and royalty (神様, 王様)

HONORIFIC HIERARCHY:

  • ～様 (sama): very formal
  • ～さん (san): neutral/polite
  • ～君 (kun): informal (males)
  • ～ちゃん (chan): affectionate
