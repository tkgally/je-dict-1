願(ねが)う

ねがう
godan verb
to wish, to request
1. to wish for, to desire
To hope for something.
幸(しあわ)せを願う。
To wish for happiness.
2. to request, to ask for
To make a formal request.
お願いします。
Please. / I request.
Very common polite expression

願(ねが)う is a godan verb meaning to wish for or to request.

SENSE 1 (to wish for):

  • 幸(しあわ)せを願(ねが)う: to wish for happiness
  • 成功(せいこう)を願(ねが)う: to wish for success
  • 平和(へいわ)を願(ねが)う: to wish for peace

SENSE 2 (to request):

  • お願(ねが)いします: please (standard polite request)
  • お願(ねが)いがあります: I have a request
  • ～願(ねが)えますか: Could you please ~?
  • よろしくお願(ねが)いします: Thank you in advance / Nice to meet you

FORMAL/LITERARY:

  • 願(ねが)わくは: I wish that... (literary)
  • 願(ねが)ってもない: couldn't wish for more

NOUN FORM:

  • 願(ねが)い: wish, request
  • 願(ねが)いを叶(かな)える: to grant a wish

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 願書(がんしょ): application form
  • 志願(しがん): volunteering, aspiration
  • 祈願(きがん): prayer, supplication
