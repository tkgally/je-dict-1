願(ねが)う

ねがう
godan verb
to wish, to request
1. to wish for, to desire
To hope for something.
2. to request, to ask for
To make a formal request.
幸(しあわ)せを願う。
To wish for happiness.
お願いします。
Please. / I request.
Very common polite expression

願う is used in many polite expressions.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お願いします: please (request)
  • お願いがあります: I have a request
  • 願(ねが)わくは: I wish that...

RELATED:

  • 願(ねが)い: wish, request (noun)
  • 願書(がんしょ): application form
