接(つ)ぐ

つぐ
godan verb
to join, to graft, to set (bones)
1. to join, to piece together
To connect separate parts.
2. to set (bones)
To align broken bones for healing.
3. to graft (trees)
To join a part of one plant to another.
骨(ほね)を接ぐ。
To set bones.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 接(つ)ぐ: to join, set bones
  • 注(つ)ぐ: to pour
  • 継(つ)ぐ: to succeed, inherit

RELATED:

  • 接骨(せっこつ): bone setting
  • 接(つ)ぎ木(き): grafting
