接(つ)ぐ

つぐ
godan verb
to join, to graft, to set (bones)
1. to join, to piece together
To connect separate parts.
切(き)れた糸(いと)を接(つ)ぐ。
To join a broken thread.
壊(こわ)れた板(いた)を接(つ)いで直(なお)した。
I repaired the broken board by joining the pieces together.
短(みじか)い布(ぬの)を何枚(なんまい)も接(つ)いで、大(おお)きなカーテンを作(つく)った。
I joined many short pieces of cloth together to make a large curtain.
2. to set (bones)
To align broken bones for healing.
骨(ほね)を接(つ)ぐ。
To set bones.
折(お)れた骨(ほね)を接(つ)いでもらった。
I had the broken bone set.
接骨院(せっこついん)で指(ゆび)の骨(ほね)を接(つ)いでもらったら、すぐに楽(らく)になった。
When I had my finger bone set at the bonesetting clinic, it felt better right away.
3. to graft (trees)
To join a part of one plant to another.
木(き)を接(つ)ぐ。
To graft a tree.
果物(くだもの)の木(き)を接(つ)いで新(あたら)しい品種(ひんしゅ)を作(つく)る。
Create new varieties by grafting fruit trees.
桜(さくら)の枝(えだ)を別(べつ)の木(き)に接(つ)いで育(そだ)てている。
I am growing cherry blossoms by grafting branches onto another tree.

接(つ)ぐ means to join, connect, or set (bones), typically connecting separate pieces.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the object being joined

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 骨(ほね)を接(つ)ぐ: to set bones
  • 木(き)を接(つ)ぐ: to graft trees
  • 切(き)れた部分(ぶぶん)を接(つ)ぐ: to join broken parts

KANJI DISTINCTION (homophones):

  • 接(つ)ぐ: to join, connect, set bones, graft
  • 注(つ)ぐ: to pour (liquid)
  • 継(つ)ぐ: to succeed, inherit, continue
  • 次(つ)ぐ: to rank next to

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 接骨(せっこつ): bonesetting
  • 接骨院(せっこついん): bonesetting clinic
  • 接(つ)ぎ木(き): grafting (horticulture)
