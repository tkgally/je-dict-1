汲(く)む

くむ
godan verb, transitive
to draw (water), to scoop
1. to draw (water)
To collect water from a well or source.
井戸(いど)から水(みず)を汲(く)む。
To draw water from a well.
2. to scoop
To take up liquid with a container.
お風呂(ふろ)の湯(ゆ)を汲(く)む。
To scoop bath water.

汲(く)む is for drawing or scooping liquids.

DISTINCTION:

  • 汲(く)む: to draw/scoop water
  • 組(く)む: to put together, cross (arms)
  • 酌(く)む: to pour drinks
general
