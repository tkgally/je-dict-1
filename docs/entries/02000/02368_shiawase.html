幸(しあわ)せ

しあわせ
noun, na-adjective
happiness, good fortune
1. happiness
A state of well-being and contentment.
幸(しあわ)せな人生(じんせい)
A happy life
幸(しあわ)せを願(ねが)う。
To wish for happiness.
2. good fortune, luck
Being blessed with favorable circumstances.

幸せ is a fundamental word.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 幸(しあわ)せ (common)
  • 仕合(しあわ)せ (archaic)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • お幸(しあわ)せに: Best wishes (for happiness)
  • 幸(しあわ)せ者(もの): lucky person
general
