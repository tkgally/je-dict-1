1. to rely on, to depend on
To count on someone or something for help.
親(おや)に頼(たよ)る。
To rely on one's parents.
辞書(じしょ)に頼(たよ)る。
To depend on a dictionary.
人(ひと)に頼(たよ)らないで自分(じぶん)でやる。
I'll do it myself without relying on others.
2. to resort to
To turn to as a means.
薬(くすり)に頼(たよ)る。
To resort to medicine.
暴力(ぼうりょく)に頼(たよ)らないで解決(かいけつ)する。
To resolve things without resorting to violence.
翻訳(ほんやく)アプリに頼(たよ)って会話(かいわ)した。
I conversed relying on a translation app.

頼(たよ)る means to rely on, depend on, or turn to someone/something for help or support.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に particle: Xに頼(たよ)る (to rely on X)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 親(おや)に頼(たよ)る: to rely on one's parents
  • 辞書(じしょ)に頼(たよ)る: to depend on a dictionary
  • 人(ひと)に頼(たよ)らない: not relying on others

RELATED WORDS:

  • 頼(たよ)り: reliance, dependence; news, word from someone
  • 頼(たよ)りになる: to be reliable/dependable
  • 頼(たの)む: to request, ask (different reading, transitive)

NUANCE:
Often implies needing help or support, can have a slightly dependent connotation.
