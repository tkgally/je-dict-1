汚(よご)れ

よごれ
noun
dirt, stain, filth

Definitions

1. dirt, stain, filth, grime
An unwanted mark, spot, or substance that makes something dirty.

Examples

服(ふく)の汚(よご)れを落(お)とす。
To remove stains from clothes.
汚(よご)れがひどい。
The dirt is severe.
汚(よご)れが目立(めだ)つ。
The stain is noticeable.

Notes

NOUN FORM of 汚(よご)れる (to get dirty).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 汚(よご)れを落(お)とす (remove stains)
  • 汚(よご)れがつく (get dirty, stain)
  • 汚(よご)れを取(と)る (remove dirt)
  • 汚(よご)れが目立(めだ)つ (stain is noticeable)
  • 頑固(がんこ)な汚(よご)れ (stubborn stain)

RELATED:

  • 汚(よご)れる (to get dirty - intransitive verb)
  • 汚(よご)す (to make dirty - transitive verb)
  • 汚(きたな)い (dirty - adjective)
  • シミ (stain - especially on fabric)
