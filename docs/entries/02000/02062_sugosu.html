1. to spend, to pass (time)
To use time in a certain way.
休日(きゅうじつ)を家(いえ)で過(す)ごす。
To spend the holiday at home.
楽(たの)しい時間(じかん)を過(す)ごした。
I had a good time.
静(しず)かに過(す)ごす。
To spend time quietly.
夏(なつ)を海辺(うみべ)で過(す)ごした。
I spent the summer at the beach.
週末(しゅうまつ)をどう過(す)ごしましたか。
How did you spend the weekend?
何(なに)もしないで一日(いちにち)を過(す)ごした。
I spent the whole day doing nothing.
2. to live, to get by
To lead one's life.
一人(ひとり)で暮(く)らしを過(す)ごしている。
I'm living alone.
平和(へいわ)に日々(ひび)を過(す)ごす。
To live one's days peacefully.
細々(ほそぼそ)と生活(せいかつ)を過(す)ごしている。
I'm getting by on a modest living.
幸(しあわ)せに人生(じんせい)を過(す)ごす。
To live a happy life.
年金(ねんきん)で老後(ろうご)を過(す)ごす。
To live out one's old age on a pension.

過(す)ごす is a godan verb meaning to spend time or to live/get by.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: [time period]を過(す)ごす

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)を過(す)ごす: to spend time
  • 休日(きゅうじつ)を過(す)ごす: to spend a holiday
  • 夏(なつ)/冬(ふゆ)を過(す)ごす: to spend summer/winter
  • 楽(たの)しい時間(じかん)を過(す)ごす: to have a good time
  • 静(しず)かに過(す)ごす: to spend time quietly
  • 日々(ひび)を過(す)ごす: to live one's days

PATTERN:

  • [manner adverb] + 過(す)ごす: describes how time is spent
  • どう過(す)ごしましたか: How did you spend (the time)?

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 過(す)ごす: to spend (time) - transitive
  • 過(す)ぎる: to pass, to exceed, too much - intransitive

Related Words

Pair verb: 過(す)ぎる (to pass, to elapse — intransitive partner)
