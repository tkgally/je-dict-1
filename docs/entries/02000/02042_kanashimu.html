悲(かな)しむ

かなしむ
verb (godan)
to be sad, to grieve, to mourn

Definitions

1. to be sad, to feel sorrow
To experience sadness.
2. to grieve, to mourn
To feel deep sorrow, especially for a loss.

Examples

別(わか)れを悲(かな)しむ。
To feel sad about parting.
彼(かれ)の死(し)を悲(かな)しんだ。
I grieved his death.
悲(かな)しむことはない。
There's no need to be sad.

Notes

悲(かな)しむ is the verb form of 悲(かな)しい.

COMPARISON:

  • 悲(かな)しい: sad (adjective)
  • 悲(かな)しむ: to feel sad (verb)
  • 悲(かな)しみ: sadness (noun)

RELATED:

  • 嘆(なげ)く: to lament
  • 泣(な)く: to cry
