1. to be sad, to feel sorrow
To experience sadness.
別(わか)れを悲(かな)しむ。
To feel sad about parting.
悲(かな)しむことはない。
There's no need to be sad.
失敗(しっぱい)を悲(かな)しんでばかりいても仕方(しかた)がない。
It's no use just feeling sad about the failure.
2. to grieve, to mourn
To feel deep sorrow, especially for a loss.
彼(かれ)の死(し)を悲(かな)しんだ。
I grieved his death.
友人(ゆうじん)の不幸(ふこう)を深(ふか)く悲(かな)しんだ。
I deeply grieved my friend's misfortune.
国中(くにじゅう)がその知(し)らせを悲(かな)しんだ。
The whole country mourned the news.

悲(かな)しむ is a godan verb meaning "to feel sad" or "to grieve." It's the verb form derived from the adjective 悲(かな)しい.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を悲(かな)しむ

WORD FAMILY:

  • 悲(かな)しい: sad (i-adjective)
  • 悲(かな)しむ: to feel sad, to grieve (verb)
  • 悲(かな)しみ: sadness, grief (noun)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を悲(かな)しむ: to grieve over ~
  • 別(わか)れを悲(かな)しむ: to feel sad about parting
  • 死(し)を悲(かな)しむ: to mourn someone's death

ASPECT (ている):
悲(かな)しんでいる describes an ongoing state of grief or sadness.

RELATED VERBS:

  • 嘆(なげ)く: to lament, to grieve (more literary)
  • 泣(な)く: to cry
  • 悼(いた)む: to mourn (formal)
