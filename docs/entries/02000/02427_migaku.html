磨(みが)く

みがく
godan verb
to polish, to brush

Definitions

1. to polish, to shine
To make smooth and shiny by rubbing.
2. to brush (teeth)
To clean teeth with a toothbrush.
3. to improve, to refine
To develop skills or qualities.

Examples

歯(は)を磨く。
To brush one's teeth.
靴(くつ)を磨く。
To polish shoes.
腕(うで)を磨く。
To improve one's skills.

Notes

磨く has both literal and figurative uses.

COMMON USES:

  • 歯(は)を磨く: to brush teeth
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を磨く: to hone skills
  • 感性(かんせい)を磨く: to refine sensibility

RELATED:

  • 歯(は)磨(みが)き: toothpaste/brushing
