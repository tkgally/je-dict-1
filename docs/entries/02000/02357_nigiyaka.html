賑(にぎ)やか

にぎやか
na-adjective
lively, bustling

Definitions

1. lively, bustling
Full of activity and noise.
2. cheerful, animated
Having a bright, energetic atmosphere.

Examples

にぎやかな町(まち)
A bustling town
食卓(しょくたく)がにぎやかだった。
The dinner table was lively.

Notes

にぎやか has positive connotations.

OPPOSITE:

  • 静(しず)か: quiet

RELATED:

  • にぎわう: to be crowded, bustling (verb)
  • 繁華街(はんかがい): downtown, busy district
general
