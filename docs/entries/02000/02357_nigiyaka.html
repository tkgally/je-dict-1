賑(にぎ)やか

にぎやか
na-adjective
lively, bustling
1. lively, bustling
Full of activity and noise.
にぎやかな町(まち)
A bustling town
2. cheerful, animated
Having a bright, energetic atmosphere.
食卓(しょくたく)がにぎやかだった。
The dinner table was lively.

賑(にぎ)やか is a na-adjective describing lively, bustling places or atmospheres with positive connotations.

FORMS:

  • Noun modifier: 賑(にぎ)やかな町(まち) (a lively town)
  • Predicate: 駅前(えきまえ)は賑(にぎ)やかだ (The station area is bustling)
  • Adverb: 賑(にぎ)やかに (lively, in a lively manner)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 賑(にぎ)やかな通(とお)り: bustling street
  • 賑(にぎ)やかな雰囲気(ふんいき): lively atmosphere
  • 賑(にぎ)やかになる: to become lively

CONTRAST:

  • 賑(にぎ)やか: lively, bustling (positive)
  • 静(しず)か: quiet, calm (opposite)
  • うるさい: noisy (negative connotation)

RELATED VERB:
にぎわう: to be crowded, to thrive
