弱(よわ)る

よわる
godan verb
to weaken, to be troubled
1. to weaken, to grow weak
To lose strength.
体(からだ)が弱っている。
My body is weakening.
2. to be troubled, to be at a loss
To not know what to do.

弱(よわ)る is intransitive.

MEANINGS:

  • Physical: to grow weak
  • Mental: to be troubled

EXPRESSION:

  • 弱(よわ)った: I'm in trouble (interjection)
general
