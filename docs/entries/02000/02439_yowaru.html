弱(よわ)る

よわる
godan verb
to weaken, to be troubled
1. to weaken, to grow weak
To lose strength.
体(からだ)が弱っている。
My body is weakening.
2. to be troubled, to be at a loss
To not know what to do.

弱(よわ)る means to weaken or to be troubled/at a loss.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Related transitive: 弱(よわ)める (to weaken something)

TWO MAIN MEANINGS:
1. Physical weakening: 体(からだ)が弱(よわ)る (body weakens)
2. Being troubled: 困(こま)って弱(よわ)る (be troubled and at a loss)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 弱(よわ)っている: is weakened/troubled
  • 弱(よわ)った！: I'm in trouble! (interjection)
  • 弱(よわ)り切(き)る: to be completely exhausted/at one's wits' end

RELATED:

  • 弱(よわ)い: weak (adjective)
  • 弱(よわ)める: to weaken (transitive)
  • 弱(よわ)まる: to become weak (intransitive)

NOTE:
弱(よわ)った used as an exclamation expresses being troubled or at a loss, similar to "I'm stuck" or "What a problem!"
