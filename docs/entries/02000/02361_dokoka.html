どこか

どこか
pronoun, adverb
somewhere, anywhere
1. somewhere, anywhere
An unspecified place.
どこかに行(い)きたい。
I want to go somewhere.
どこかで会(あ)ったことがある。
I've met you somewhere before.
Sense 1: somewhere
鍵(かぎ)がどこかにある。
The keys are somewhere.
Sense 1: somewhere
どこかで食事(しょくじ)しよう。
Let's eat somewhere.
Sense 1: somewhere
静(しず)かなどこかに住(す)みたい。
I want to live somewhere quiet.
Sense 1: somewhere
2. somehow, in some way
In some unspecified manner.
どこか似(に)ている。
They're somehow similar.
どこか懐(なつ)かしい。
It's somehow nostalgic.
Sense 2: somehow
どこか寂(さび)しい感(かん)じがする。
It feels somehow lonely.
Sense 2: somehow
どこか変(へん)だ。
Something is off. / It's somewhat strange.
Sense 2: somehow
どこか違(ちが)う気(き)がする。
I feel like something is different.
Sense 2: somehow

どこか is an indefinite word meaning "somewhere" or "somehow." Part of the ka-series indefinites.

TWO MAIN USES:
1. Location (somewhere): どこかに行(い)く (go somewhere)
2. Manner (somehow, somewhat): どこか変(へん)だ (somehow strange)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • どこかに: to/at somewhere
  • どこかで: at somewhere (action location)
  • どこかへ: toward somewhere

RELATED INDEFINITES:

  • 何(なに)か: something
  • 誰(だれ)か: someone
  • いつか: sometime
  • どれか: one of them

USAGE NOTE:
When modifying an adjective without a particle, どこか means "somewhat" or "in some way": どこか寂(さび)しい (somehow lonely).
