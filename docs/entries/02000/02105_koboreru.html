こぼれる

こぼれる
verb (ichidan)
to spill, to overflow
1. to spill, to overflow
For liquid or objects to fall out of a container.
水(みず)がこぼれた。
The water spilled.
涙(なみだ)がこぼれる。
Tears fall.

こぼれる describes something spilling out or overflowing from a container, often accidentally.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: こぼす (transitive, to spill something)

ASPECT (こぼれている):
Indicates the resulting state: 水(みず)がこぼれている means 'water has spilled' (state).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 水(みず)がこぼれる: water spills
  • 涙(なみだ)がこぼれる: tears fall/overflow
  • 笑(え)みがこぼれる: to break into a smile (figurative)
  • 光(ひかり)がこぼれる: light filters through

KANJI:
Sometimes written as 零(こぼ)れる, though hiragana is more common.
