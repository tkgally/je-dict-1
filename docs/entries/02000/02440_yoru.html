1. to approach, to come near
To move closer.
もっと近(ちか)くに寄(よ)って。
Come closer.
猫(ねこ)が寄(よ)ってきた。
A cat came near.
岸(きし)に寄(よ)る。
To approach the shore.
人(ひと)が寄(よ)ってくる。
People are gathering.
寄(よ)ると触(さわ)ると。
At every opportunity (idiom).
2. to stop by, to drop in
To visit briefly.
コンビニに寄(よ)る。
To stop by the convenience store.
帰(かえ)りに友人(ゆうじん)の家(いえ)に寄(よ)った。
I stopped by a friend's house on the way home.
銀行(ぎんこう)に寄(よ)ってから行(い)く。
I'll go after stopping by the bank.
ちょっと寄(よ)っていかない？
Won't you drop by for a bit?
本屋(ほんや)に寄(よ)って雑誌(ざっし)を買(か)った。
I stopped by the bookstore and bought a magazine.

寄(よ)る has two main meanings: to physically approach something, or to briefly stop at a place.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 寄(よ)せる (transitive, to bring near)

ASPECT (ている):
寄(よ)っている indicates the state of being near or having stopped by.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に寄(よ)る: to stop by (a place)
  • 寄(よ)ってくる: to approach, come near
  • 寄(よ)っていく: to stop by on the way
  • 帰(かえ)りに寄(よ)る: to stop by on the way home

NOTE:
Do not confuse with 因(よ)る (to be due to) or 拠(よ)る (to be based on), which use different kanji.
