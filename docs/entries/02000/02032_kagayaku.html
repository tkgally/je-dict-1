輝(かがや)く

かがやく
verb (godan)
to shine, to sparkle, to be radiant
1. to shine, to sparkle, to glitter
To emit or reflect bright light.
2. to be radiant, to be brilliant
To be outstanding or impressive.
星(ほし)が輝(かがや)いている。
The stars are shining.
彼女(かのじょ)の目(め)が輝(かがや)いた。
Her eyes sparkled.
輝(かがや)く未来(みらい)。
A bright future.

輝(かがや)く means to shine or be radiant.

USES:

  • Physical: stars, lights, gems shining
  • Figurative: eyes sparkling, future being bright

RELATED:

  • 輝(かがや)き: brightness, radiance (noun)
  • 光(ひか)る: to shine (simpler word)
