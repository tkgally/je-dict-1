掴(つか)む

つかむ
godan verb, transitive
to grab, to grasp, to seize
1. to grab, to grasp
To take hold of something firmly.
手(て)を掴(つか)む。
To grab someone's hand.
ロープをしっかり掴(つか)んだ。
I grabbed the rope firmly.
子供(こども)が母親(ははおや)の服(ふく)を掴(つか)んで離(はな)さなかった。
The child grabbed the mother's clothes and wouldn't let go.
2. to seize, to catch
To capture an opportunity or understand something.
チャンスを掴(つか)む。
To seize an opportunity.
要点(ようてん)を掴(つか)む。
To grasp the main points.
長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)でようやく成功(せいこう)を掴(つか)んだ。
After years of effort, I finally achieved success.

掴(つか)む is a transitive verb meaning to grab or grasp firmly, both physically and figuratively.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を掴(つか)む

PHYSICAL GRASPING:

  • 手(て)を掴(つか)む: to grab someone's hand
  • ロープを掴(つか)む: to grab a rope
  • 襟(えり)を掴(つか)む: to grab by the collar

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • チャンスを掴(つか)む: to seize an opportunity
  • 心(こころ)を掴(つか)む: to capture someone's heart
  • 要点(ようてん)を掴(つか)む: to grasp the main point
  • 成功(せいこう)を掴(つか)む: to achieve success

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 握(にぎ)る: to grip, hold (sustained holding)
  • 取(と)る: to take (more general)
  • 捕(つか)まえる: to catch, capture (person/animal)
