琴(こと)

こと
noun
koto, Japanese harp
1. koto
A traditional Japanese stringed instrument with 13 strings.
琴(こと)を弾(ひ)く。
To play the koto.
琴(こと)の音色(ねいろ)が美(うつく)しい。
The sound of the koto is beautiful.

琴(こと) is a traditional Japanese instrument.

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • 13 strings stretched over movable bridges
  • Played by plucking with picks on thumb, index, and middle fingers
  • Used in traditional Japanese music and ceremonies

RELATED:

  • 三味線(しゃみせん): shamisen (3-stringed instrument)
  • 尺八(しゃくはち): shakuhachi (bamboo flute)
