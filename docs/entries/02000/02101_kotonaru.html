異(こと)なる

ことなる
verb (godan)
to differ, to vary, to be different
1. to differ, to vary
To be unlike or dissimilar.
意見(いけん)が異(こと)なる。
Opinions differ.
文化(ぶんか)は国(くに)によって異(こと)なる。
Culture varies by country.

異(こと)なる means to differ or vary—a formal/written alternative to 違(ちが)う.

REGISTER:

  • 違(ちが)う: to be different (everyday speech)
  • 異(こと)なる: to differ (formal, written, academic)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AはBと異(こと)なる: A differs from B
  • ～によって異(こと)なる: varies depending on ~
  • ～とは異(こと)なり: unlike ~, differing from ~

RELATED WORDS:

  • 異(い)文化(ぶんか): different culture
  • 異(い)常(じょう): abnormal
  • 異(い)議(ぎ): objection
  • 相違(そうい): difference (formal noun)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used in academic writing, news, and formal contexts where 違(ちが)う would sound too casual.
