握(にぎ)る

にぎる
godan verb
to grasp, to grip
1. to grasp, to grip
To hold firmly in the hand.
ハンドルを握る。
To grip the steering wheel.
2. to make (sushi)
To mold sushi rice by hand.
寿司(すし)を握る。
To make sushi.
3. to hold (power)
To have control over something.
権力(けんりょく)を握る。
To hold power.

握(にぎ)る is a godan verb meaning "to grasp" or "to grip." It has both literal and figurative uses.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

SENSE 1 - PHYSICAL GRASP:

  • ハンドルを握(にぎ)る: to grip the steering wheel
  • 手(て)を握(にぎ)る: to hold hands
  • 拳(こぶし)を握(にぎ)る: to clench one's fist

SENSE 2 - SUSHI:

  • 寿司(すし)を握(にぎ)る: to make (hand-pressed) sushi
  • 握(にぎ)り寿司(ずし): nigiri sushi

SENSE 3 - FIGURATIVE (CONTROL):

  • 権力(けんりょく)を握(にぎ)る: to hold power
  • 秘密(ひみつ)を握(にぎ)る: to hold a secret
  • 鍵(かぎ)を握(にぎ)る: to hold the key (to something)
