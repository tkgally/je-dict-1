橋(はし)

はし
noun
bridge
1. bridge
A structure built to span a physical obstacle such as a river or road.
橋(はし)を渡(わた)る。
To cross a bridge.
川(かわ)に橋(はし)がかかっている。
A bridge spans the river.

橋(はし) means bridge, a structure spanning obstacles like rivers or roads.

HOMOPHONES (same pronunciation, different words):

  • 橋(はし): bridge (this word)
  • 箸(はし): chopsticks
  • 端(はし): edge, end

These are distinguished by kanji and pitch accent in speech.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 石橋(いしばし): stone bridge
  • 歩道橋(ほどうきょう): pedestrian overpass
  • 吊(つ)り橋(ばし): suspension bridge
  • 鉄橋(てっきょう): iron/railway bridge
  • 橋(はし)渡(わた)し: bridge-building, mediation

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 橋(はし)を渡(わた)る: to cross a bridge
  • 橋(はし)がかかる: a bridge spans (over)
  • 橋(はし)を架(か)ける: to build a bridge

FIGURATIVE USE:
橋渡(はしわた)し means 'go-between' or 'mediator' - acting as a bridge between parties.
