溜(た)まる

たまる
verb (godan)
to accumulate, to pile up
1. to accumulate, to collect
To gather over time.
お金(かね)が溜(た)まった。
Money accumulated.
2. to pile up, to build up
To increase in quantity.
ストレスが溜(た)まる。
Stress builds up.
仕事(しごと)が溜(た)まっている。
Work is piling up.

溜(た)まる is a godan verb meaning to accumulate, collect, or pile up over time.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 溜(た)める (transitive: to save up, accumulate)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お金(かね)が溜(た)まる: money accumulates
  • 疲(つか)れが溜(た)まる: fatigue builds up
  • ストレスが溜(た)まる: stress builds up
  • 仕事(しごと)が溜(た)まる: work piles up
  • 埃(ほこり)が溜(た)まる: dust collects
  • 水(みず)が溜(た)まる: water pools/collects

NUANCE:
Often implies gradual, unintentional accumulation, and frequently has a negative connotation (stress, work, fatigue piling up).
