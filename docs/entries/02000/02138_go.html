碁(ご)

ご
noun
Go (board game)

Definitions

1. Go
A strategic board game originating in ancient China, played by placing black and white stones to capture territory.

Examples

碁(ご)を打(う)つ。
To play Go.
祖父(そふ)は碁(ご)が強(つよ)い。
My grandfather is good at Go.

Notes

碁(ご) is also called 囲碁(いご).

TERMS:

  • 碁盤(ごばん): Go board
  • 碁石(ごいし): Go stones
  • 碁(ご)を打(う)つ: to play Go

CULTURE:
Go is one of the four arts of the Chinese scholar, along with calligraphy, painting, and music.
