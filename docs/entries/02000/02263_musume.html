娘(むすめ)

むすめ
noun
daughter, girl
1. daughter
One's female child.
娘(むすめ)が二人(ふたり)います。
I have two daughters.
2. girl, young woman
A young unmarried woman.
あの娘(むすめ)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is that girl?

娘(むすめ) means daughter or girl.

FAMILY:

  • 息子(むすこ): son
  • 娘(むすめ): daughter

COMPOUNDS:

  • 娘婿(むすめむこ): son-in-law
  • 一人娘(ひとりむすめ): only daughter
  • お嬢(じょう)さん: daughter (polite), young lady
