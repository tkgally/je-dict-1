1. to tear, to split
To come apart or be divided.
布(ぬの)が裂(さ)けた。
The cloth tore.
傷口(きずぐち)が裂(さ)けた。
The wound split open.
地面(じめん)が大(おお)きく裂(さ)けて、中(なか)から水(みず)が出(で)てきた。
The ground split wide open, and water came out from inside.

裂(さ)ける means to tear, split, or rip apart (happening on its own).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 裂(さ)く (transitive - to tear something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 布(ぬの)が裂(さ)ける: cloth tears
  • 傷口(きずぐち)が裂(さ)ける: a wound splits open
  • 皮(かわ)が裂(さ)ける: skin splits
  • 口(くち)が裂(さ)けても: even if my mouth splits (= never, under any circumstances)

ASPECT (ている):
裂(さ)けている indicates the resulting state of being torn/split.

HOMOPHONE DISTINCTION:

  • 裂(さ)ける: to tear, split
  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid
  • 割(さ)ける: to be able to spare/allocate
