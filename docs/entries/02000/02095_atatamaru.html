温(あたた)まる

あたたまる
verb (godan)
to warm oneself, to get warm
1. to warm oneself, to get warm
To become warm (intransitive).
お風呂(ふろ)で温(あたた)まる。
To warm up in the bath.
体(からだ)が温(あたた)まった。
My body warmed up.

温(あたた)まる is an intransitive verb meaning to become warm or to warm oneself.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 温(あたた)める (transitive, to warm something)

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 温(あたた)まる: warm (body, objects, feelings)
  • 暖(あたた)まる: warm (room, weather, atmosphere)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お風呂(ふろ)で温(あたた)まる: to warm up in the bath
  • 体(からだ)が温(あたた)まる: body warms up
  • 心(こころ)が温(あたた)まる: heart warms (emotionally moved)
  • 温(あたた)まってきた: has started to warm up

ASPECT (ている):
温(あたた)まっている indicates a state of being warm.
