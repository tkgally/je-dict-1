詩(し)

し
noun
poem, poetry
1. poem, poetry
A literary work expressing feelings and ideas in verse.
詩(し)を書(か)く。
To write poetry.
この詩(し)は美(うつく)しい。
This poem is beautiful.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)から詩(し)を書(か)くのが好(す)きだった。
I've liked writing poetry ever since I was a child.

詩(し) refers to poetry or poems—literary works expressing emotions and ideas through rhythmic language and imagery.

SCOPE:
詩(し) typically refers to free verse or Western-style poetry. Traditional Japanese forms have their own terms.

TYPES OF POETRY:

  • 俳句(はいく): haiku (17 syllables)
  • 短歌(たんか): tanka (31 syllables)
  • 川柳(せんりゅう): senryū (humorous haiku-like form)
  • 漢詩(かんし): Chinese-style poetry
  • 現代詩(げんだいし): modern free verse

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 詩(し)を書(か)く: to write poetry
  • 詩(し)を読(よ)む: to read poetry
  • 詩(し)を詠(よ)む: to compose/recite poetry (literary)
  • 詩的(してき)な: poetic

RELATED WORDS:

  • 詩人(しじん): poet
  • 詩集(ししゅう): poetry collection
  • 詩歌(しいか): poetry (broader term, includes songs)
  • 韻(いん): rhyme
