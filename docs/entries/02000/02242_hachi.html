蜂(はち) is a general term for bees and wasps.

TYPES OF BEES/WASPS:

  • 蜜蜂(みつばち): honeybee
  • 雀蜂(すずめばち): hornet (Japanese giant hornet)
  • 足長蜂(あしながばち): paper wasp
  • 熊蜂(くまばち): carpenter bee

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 蜂(はち)に刺(さ)される: to be stung by a bee/wasp
  • 蜂(はち)の巣(す): beehive, wasp nest
  • 蜂蜜(はちみつ): honey
  • 養蜂(ようほう): beekeeping

SAFETY NOTE:
雀蜂(すずめばち) (Asian giant hornets) can be very dangerous. Their stings can be life-threatening, especially with multiple stings or allergic reactions.

IDIOM:
蜂(はち)の巣(す)をつついたよう: like poking a hornet's nest (causing an uproar).
