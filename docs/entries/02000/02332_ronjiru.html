論(ろん)じる

ろんじる
ichidan verb
to argue, to discuss
1. to argue, to discuss
To present reasoning about a topic.
2. to comment on, to critique
To express opinions about something.
政治(せいじ)について論じる。
To discuss politics.
是非(ぜひ)を論じる。
To debate the rights and wrongs.

論じる is formal and academic.

ALTERNATE FORM:

  • 論ずる (classical/literary)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)する: to debate
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)う: to discuss (casual)
