The repair fee was high, but I had no choice and paid it.

支払(しはら)う is a godan verb meaning to pay, typically used in formal or business contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive - takes を to mark what is being paid.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 料金(りょうきん)を支払(しはら)う: to pay the fee
  • 税金(ぜいきん)を支払(しはら)う: to pay taxes
  • 家賃(やちん)を支払(しはら)う: to pay rent
  • 現金(げんきん)で支払(しはら)う: to pay in cash
  • クレジットカードで支払(しはら)う: to pay by credit card

RELATED FORMS:

  • 支払(しはら)い: payment (noun form)
  • 支払(しはら)い方法(ほうほう): payment method
  • 支払(しはら)い期限(きげん): payment deadline
  • 支払(しはら)い済み: paid (in full)

COMPARISON WITH 払(はら)う:

  • 支払(しはら)う: more formal, used in business/official contexts
  • 払(はら)う: general, everyday usage

NOTE:
払(はら)う can also mean 'to brush off' or 'to sweep away,' but 支払(しはら)う is exclusively for monetary payment.

Related Words

Synonym: 払(はら)う (everyday equivalent)
