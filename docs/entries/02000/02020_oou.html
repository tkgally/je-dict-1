覆(おお)う

おおう
verb (godan)
to cover, to hide, to conceal
1. to cover
To put something over or upon.
雪(ゆき)が山(やま)を覆(おお)っている。
Snow covers the mountain.
顔(かお)を手(て)で覆(おお)った。
I covered my face with my hands.
雲(くも)が空(そら)を覆(おお)う。
Clouds cover the sky.
2. to hide, to conceal
To keep from sight.

覆(おお)う is a godan verb meaning to cover or conceal something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for what is being covered

ASPECT (ている):
覆(おお)っている indicates a state: 雪(ゆき)が山(やま)を覆(おお)っている (Snow covers the mountain).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AがBを覆(おお)う: A covers B
  • Bに覆(おお)われる: B is covered (passive)
  • 顔(かお)を手(て)で覆(おお)う: to cover one's face with hands

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雲(くも)が空(そら)を覆(おお)う: clouds cover the sky
  • 闇(やみ)に覆(おお)われる: to be shrouded in darkness
  • 雪(ゆき)に覆(おお)われる: to be covered in snow

RELATED KANJI:

  • 覆(おお)う: to cover
  • 覆(くつがえ)す: to overturn (different reading)
