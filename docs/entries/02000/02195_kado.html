角(かど)

かど
noun
corner, angle
1. corner
The point where two streets or edges meet.
角(かど)を曲(ま)がる。
To turn the corner.
角(かど)に{ぶつかった。
I bumped into the corner.
2. angle
A sharp edge or point.

角(かど) refers to street corners and angles.

NOTE ON READINGS:

  • 角(かど): corner of a street, edge
  • 角(すみ): corner of a room
  • 角(つの): horn (of animal)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 角(かど)が立(た)つ: to cause friction
  • 角(かど)を取(と)る: to round off corners; to mellow
general
