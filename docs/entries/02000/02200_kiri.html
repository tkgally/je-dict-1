霧(きり)

きり
noun
fog, mist
1. fog
A thick cloud of tiny water droplets near the ground.
2. mist
A thin fog or spray.
霧(きり)が出(で)ている。
It's foggy.
霧(きり)で前(まえ)が見(み)えない。
I can't see ahead because of the fog.

霧(きり) refers to fog and mist.

WEATHER:

  • 濃霧(のうむ): dense fog
  • 朝霧(あさぎり): morning mist

COMPOUNDS:

  • 霧雨(きりさめ): drizzle, misty rain
  • 霧吹(きりふ)き: spray bottle

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 五里霧中(ごりむちゅう): being in a fog, totally confused
