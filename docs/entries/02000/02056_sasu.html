1. to point at, to indicate
To direct attention to something.
指(ゆび)で指(さ)す。
To point with one's finger.
針(はり)が北(きた)を指(さ)している。
The needle is pointing north.
地図(ちず)の場所(ばしょ)を指(さ)して説明(せつめい)した。
I pointed at the location on the map and explained.
2. to refer to, to mean
To indicate a meaning or reference.
それは何(なに)を指(さ)しますか。
What does that refer to?
この言葉(ことば)は何(なに)を指(さ)しているのですか。
What does this word refer to?
「彼(かれ)」が指(さ)す人物(じんぶつ)は誰(だれ)ですか。
Who is the person that 'he' refers to?

指(さ)す is a godan verb meaning to point at or indicate something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the target

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 指(ゆび)で指(さ)す: to point with a finger
  • 〜を指(さ)す: to refer to ~, indicate ~
  • 方向(ほうこう)を指(さ)す: to point in a direction
  • 針(はり)が北(きた)を指(さ)す: needle points north

HOMOPHONES (different kanji):

  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella), shine through
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, pierce, sting
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert

CULTURAL NOTE:
Pointing directly at people with a finger is considered rude in Japan.
