逆(さか)らう

さからう
verb (godan)
to go against, to oppose, to disobey
1. to go against, to oppose
To act in opposition to something.
流(なが)れに逆(さか)らう。
To go against the current.
時代(じだい)の流(なが)れに逆(さか)らう。
To go against the times.
Sense 1: go against
運命(うんめい)に逆(さか)らう。
To go against fate.
Sense 1: oppose
自然(しぜん)に逆(さか)らう。
To go against nature.
Sense 1: go against
常識(じょうしき)に逆(さか)らう。
To go against common sense.
Sense 1: oppose
2. to disobey
To refuse to follow orders or rules.
親(おや)に逆(さか)らう。
To disobey one's parents.
上司(じょうし)に逆(さか)らう。
To disobey one's boss.
Sense 2: disobey
命令(めいれい)に逆(さか)らった。
I disobeyed the order.
Sense 2: disobey
先生(せんせい)に逆(さか)らってはいけない。
You must not disobey the teacher.
Sense 2: disobey
規則(きそく)に逆(さか)らった。
I went against the rules.
Sense 2: disobey

逆(さか)らう means to go against, oppose, or defy something or someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に particle for what is being opposed

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 親(おや)に逆(さか)らう: to disobey one's parents
  • 上司(じょうし)に逆(さか)らう: to go against one's boss
  • 流(なが)れに逆(さか)らう: to go against the current/trend
  • 運命(うんめい)に逆(さか)らう: to defy fate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対(はんたい)する: to oppose (neutral, can be positive)
  • 逆(さか)らう: to defy (often implies defiance of authority)
  • 背(そむ)く: to turn one's back on, to betray

逆(さか)らう often implies defying authority figures or established norms, and can carry a negative connotation.
