鬼(おに)

おに
noun
demon, ogre, oni
1. demon, ogre
A supernatural creature in Japanese folklore.
鬼(おに)が出(で)てくる話(はなし)を読(よ)んだ。
I read a story where demons appear.
鬼(おに)に金棒(かなぼう)。
Making something strong even stronger (proverb).
Sense 1: demon (idiom)
節分(せつぶん)に鬼(おに)を追(お)い出(だ)す。
To drive out demons during Setsubun.
Sense 1: demon
鬼(おに)ごっこをする。
To play tag.
Sense 1: ogre
桃太郎(ももたろう)が鬼(おに)を退治(たいじ)した。
Momotaro defeated the demons.
Sense 1: demon
2. fierce person
Someone who is very strict or scary.
鬼(おに)のような先生(せんせい)だ。
The teacher is very strict.
鬼(おに)のような上司(じょうし)。
A fearsome boss.
Sense 2: fierce person
仕事(しごと)の鬼(おに)だ。
A demon for work (a workaholic).
Sense 2: fierce person
心(こころ)を鬼(おに)にする。
To steel oneself.
Sense 2: strict (idiom)
練習(れんしゅう)の鬼(おに)になる。
To become a demon of practice (work extremely hard).
Sense 2: fierce person

鬼(おに) refers to Japanese demons.

IN FOLKLORE:
Oni are typically depicted with horns, wild hair, and carrying iron clubs.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 鬼(おに)ごっこ: tag (children's game)
  • 心(こころ)を鬼(おに)にする: to steel oneself
  • 鬼(おに)に金棒(かなぼう): making something strong even stronger

SETSUBUN:
During Setsubun, people throw beans to drive away 鬼(おに).
