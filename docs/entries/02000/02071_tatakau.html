戦(たたか)う

たたかう
verb (godan)
to fight, to battle, to struggle
1. to fight, to battle
To engage in combat.
2. to struggle, to compete
To make a determined effort.
敵(てき)と戦(たたか)う。
To fight the enemy.
病気(びょうき)と戦(たたか)う。
To fight against illness.
勇敢(ゆうかん)に戦(たたか)った。
They fought bravely.

戦(たたか)う means to fight.

PATTERN:

  • 〜と戦(たたか)う: to fight against ~

USES:

  • War/combat
  • Sports/competition
  • Struggles (illness, adversity)

RELATED:

  • 戦(たたか)い: fight (noun)
  • 闘(たたか)う: to fight (alternative kanji)
general
