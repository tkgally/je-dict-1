沈(しず)む

しずむ
verb (godan)
to sink, to feel depressed
1. to sink, to go down
To go below the surface.
船(ふね)が沈(しず)んだ。
The ship sank.
太陽(たいよう)が沈(しず)む。
The sun sets.
2. to feel depressed, to be downcast
To feel low or sad.
気持(きも)ちが沈(しず)む。
To feel depressed.

沈(しず)む means to sink, go down, or feel depressed. Used for physical sinking and emotional states.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 沈(しず)む (intransitive): to sink
  • 沈(しず)める (transitive): to sink something

CONJUGATION (godan む-verb):

  • 沈(しず)まない (negative)
  • 沈(しず)みます (polite)
  • 沈(しず)んだ (past)
  • 沈(しず)んで (te-form)

COMMON USES:

  • 船(ふね)が沈(しず)む: the ship sinks
  • 太陽(たいよう)が沈(しず)む: the sun sets
  • 気持(きも)ちが沈(しず)む: to feel depressed
  • 心(こころ)が沈(しず)む: to feel down, be dejected
  • 沈(しず)んだ表情(ひょうじょう): a dejected expression

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 沈没(ちんぼつ): sinking (of a ship)
  • 沈黙(ちんもく): silence
  • 沈殿(ちんでん): precipitation, sediment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落(お)ち込(こ)む: to get depressed (more colloquial)
  • 陥(おちい)る: to fall into (a state)
