沈(しず)む

しずむ
verb (godan)
to sink, to feel depressed
1. to sink, to go down
To go below the surface.
2. to feel depressed, to be downcast
To feel low or sad.
船(ふね)が沈(しず)んだ。
The ship sank.
太陽(たいよう)が沈(しず)む。
The sun sets.
気持(きも)ちが沈(しず)む。
To feel depressed.

沈(しず)む is intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 沈(しず)む (intransitive): to sink
  • 沈(しず)める (transitive): to sink something

USES:
1. Physical: ships sinking, sun setting
2. Emotional: feeling down, depressed
