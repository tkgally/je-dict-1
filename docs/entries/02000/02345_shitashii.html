親(した)しい

したしい
i-adjective
intimate, close
1. intimate, close
Having a close, friendly relationship.
親(した)しい友人(ゆうじん)
A close friend
彼とは親しい間柄(あいだがら)だ。
I have a close relationship with him.
2. familiar, friendly
Easy to approach or understand.

親しい describes emotional closeness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 親しい友人(ゆうじん): close friend
  • 親しい仲(なか): intimate relationship
  • 〜に親しむ: to be familiar with ~

RELATED VERB:

  • 親(した)しむ: to become familiar with
