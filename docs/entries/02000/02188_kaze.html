風邪(かぜ)

かぜ
noun
cold, flu

Definitions

1. cold
A common viral infection of the upper respiratory tract.

Examples

風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いた。
I caught a cold.
風邪(かぜ)が治(なお)った。
My cold got better.

Notes

風邪(かぜ) refers to the common cold.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)く: to catch a cold
  • 風邪(かぜ)が治(なお)る: to recover from a cold
  • 風邪気味(かぜぎみ): feeling a bit under the weather

NOTE:
Written with kanji 風邪}, though the reading is the same as {風(かぜ) (wind).
