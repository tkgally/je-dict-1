主張(しゅちょう)

しゅちょう
noun, suru-verb
claim, assertion, insistence

Definitions

1. claim, assertion
A statement that something is true or a fact.
2. insistence, advocacy
The act of firmly stating or demanding something.

Examples

彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の主張(しゅちょう)を曲(ま)げない。
He won't back down from his claim.
労働者(ろうどうしゃ)は賃金(ちんぎん)引(ひ)き上(あ)げを主張(しゅちょう)した。
The workers demanded a wage increase.
彼女(かのじょ)の主張(しゅちょう)には説得力(せっとくりょく)がある。データに基(もと)づいて論理的(ろんりてき)に話(はな)すので、反論(はんろん)するのが難(むずか)しい。
Her arguments are persuasive. She speaks logically based on data, making it difficult to refute her.

Notes

主張(しゅちょう) refers to making a claim, asserting something, or insisting on a position.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 主張(しゅちょう)する (to claim, to assert, to insist)
  • 主張(しゅちょう)を通(とお)す (to push one's claim through)
  • 主張(しゅちょう)を曲(ま)げる (to back down from one's position)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 強(つよ)い主張(しゅちょう) (strong assertion)
  • 主張(しゅちょう)が強(つよ)い (assertive, opinionated)
  • 自己(じこ)主張(しゅちょう) (self-assertion)
  • 領土(りょうど)主張(しゅちょう) (territorial claim)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主張(しゅちょう): claim, assertion (formal, often in debates/discussions)
  • 意見(いけん): opinion (general)
  • 要求(ようきゅう): demand (stronger, often for concrete things)
  • 訴(うった)え: appeal, claim (often emotional or legal)
