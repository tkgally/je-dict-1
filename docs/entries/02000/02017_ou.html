負(お)う

おう
verb (godan)
to bear, to owe, to sustain
1. to bear, to carry on one's back
To carry something on the back.
2. to bear (responsibility), to owe
To have responsibility or debt.
責任(せきにん)を負(お)う。
To bear responsibility.
借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)っている。
I'm in debt.
3. to sustain (injury)
To receive an injury.
怪我(けが)を負(お)った。
I sustained an injury.

負(お)う is a godan verb meaning to bear, owe, or sustain (injury).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the thing being borne

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を負(お)う: to bear responsibility
  • 怪我(けが)を負(お)う: to sustain an injury
  • 借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)う: to be in debt
  • 重荷(おもに)を負(お)う: to bear a burden

HOMOPHONES:

  • 負(お)う: to bear, owe (負 = lose/bear)
  • 追(お)う: to chase, pursue (追 = chase)
  • 覆(お)う: to cover (覆 = cover)
