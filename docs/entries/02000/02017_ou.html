1. to bear, to carry on one's back
To carry something on the back.
荷物(にもつ)を背(せ)に負(お)う。
To carry luggage on one's back.
子(こ)を負(お)って歩(ある)く。
To walk carrying a child on one's back.
重荷(おもに)を負(お)って生(い)きる。
To live carrying a heavy burden.
2. to bear (responsibility), to owe
To have responsibility or debt.
責任(せきにん)を負(お)う。
To bear responsibility.
借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)っている。
I'm in debt.
会社(かいしゃ)は大(おお)きな損失(そんしつ)を負(お)った。
The company incurred a large loss.
3. to sustain (injury)
To receive an injury.
怪我(けが)を負(お)った。
I sustained an injury.
事故(じこ)で重傷(じゅうしょう)を負(お)った。
I sustained serious injuries in the accident.
戦争(せんそう)で多(おお)くの兵士(へいし)が傷(きず)を負(お)った。
Many soldiers were wounded in the war.

負(お)う is a godan verb meaning to bear, owe, or sustain (injury).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the thing being borne

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を負(お)う: to bear responsibility
  • 怪我(けが)を負(お)う: to sustain an injury
  • 借金(しゃっきん)を負(お)う: to be in debt
  • 重荷(おもに)を負(お)う: to bear a burden

HOMOPHONES:

  • 負(お)う: to bear, owe (負(ふ) = lose/bear)
  • 追(お)う: to chase, pursue (追(つい) = chase)
  • 覆(お)う: to cover (覆(ふく) = cover)
