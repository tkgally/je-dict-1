空(あ)き家(や)

あきや
noun
vacant house, empty house

Definitions

1. vacant house, empty house
A house that is not occupied.

Examples

空(あ)き家(や)が増(ふ)えている。
Vacant houses are increasing.
あの空(あ)き家(や)は古(ふる)い。
That vacant house is old.
空(あ)き家(や)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a vacant house.

Notes

空(あ)き家(や) refers to a house with no one living in it.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 空(あ)き家(や)が増(ふ)える (vacant houses increase)
  • 空(あ)き家(や)を利用(りよう)する (use a vacant house)
  • 空(あ)き家(や)問題(もんだい) (vacant house problem)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 空(あ)き地(ち): vacant lot
  • 空(あ)き室(しつ): vacant room
  • 空(あ)き缶(かん): empty can

Note: 空(あ)き家(や) has become a social issue in Japan due to aging population and rural depopulation.
