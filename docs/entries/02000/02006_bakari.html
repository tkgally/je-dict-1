〜ばかり

ばかり
particle
just, only, nothing but
1. just (did), have just
Indicates an action was just completed.
今(いま)来(き)たばかりだ。
I just arrived.
起(お)きたばかりだ。
I just woke up.
Sense 1: just did
買(か)ったばかりの車(くるま)。
A car I just bought.
Sense 1: just did
食(た)べたばかりなのに、また食(た)べるの？
You just ate, and you're eating again?
Sense 1: just did
卒業(そつぎょう)したばかりです。
I just graduated.
Sense 1: just did
2. only, nothing but
Indicates limitation or exclusivity.
彼(かれ)は文句(もんく)ばかり言(い)う。
He does nothing but complain.
甘(あま)いものばかり食(た)べる。
I eat nothing but sweets.
Sense 2: only/nothing but
ゲームばかりしている。
You do nothing but play games.
Sense 2: only
嘘(うそ)ばかりつく。
You tell nothing but lies.
Sense 2: nothing but
男(おとこ)ばかりのパーティー。
A party with only men.
Sense 2: only
3. about, approximately
Indicates approximate quantity.
百(ひゃく)円(えん)ばかり貸(か)してください。
Please lend me about 100 yen.
一週間(いっしゅうかん)ばかり休(やす)む。
To take about a week off.
Sense 3: about/approximately
十人(じゅうにん)ばかり集(あつ)まった。
About ten people gathered.
Sense 3: approximately
三日(みっか)ばかり待(ま)ってください。
Please wait about three days.
Sense 3: approximately
あと少(すこ)しばかり頑張(がんば)ろう。
Let's try a little more.
Sense 3: about (a little)

ばかり has multiple uses.

PATTERNS:

1. Verb (た form) + ばかり: just did

  • 食(た)べたばかり: just ate

2. Noun + ばかり: only, nothing but

  • 肉(にく)ばかり: only meat

3. Number + ばかり: about, approximately

  • 三日(みっか)ばかり: about three days

VARIATIONS:

  • ばっかり/ばっか: casual forms
