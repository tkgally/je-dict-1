帰(かえ)す

かえす
verb (godan)
to send back, to let go home
1. to send back, to let go home
To allow or cause someone to return.
子供(こども)を家(いえ)に帰(かえ)す。
To send the children home.
客(きゃく)を帰(かえ)した。
I let the guest go home.
病気(びょうき)の生徒(せいと)を帰(かえ)す。
To send the sick student home.

帰(かえ)す means to send someone back or let them go home. The subject causes or allows someone to return to their original location.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 帰(かえ)る (intransitive, to return/go home)

ASPECT (ている):
帰(かえ)している means 'is in the process of sending back' (ongoing action), not a resultant state.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を家(いえ)に帰(かえ)す: to send ~ home
  • ～を帰(かえ)してあげる: to let ~ go home
  • 早(はや)く帰(かえ)す: to send home early

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 帰(かえ)す: to send back (people)
  • 返(かえ)す: to return, give back (objects, money)
  • 戻(もど)す: to return, put back (objects to original position)
