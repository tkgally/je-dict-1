越(こ)える

こえる
verb (ichidan)
to cross, to exceed, to surpass
1. to cross, to go over
To pass over or across something.
山(やま)を越(こ)える。
To cross a mountain.
2. to exceed, to surpass
To go beyond a limit or amount.
100万円(ひゃくまんえん)を越(こ)えた。
It exceeded one million yen.

越(こ)える means to cross or exceed.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 越(こ)える: to cross (physical), to exceed
  • 超(こ)える: to surpass, to transcend

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 越(こ)える (intransitive): to cross over
  • 越(こ)す (transitive): to cross, to move past
