外(はず)れる

はずれる
ichidan verb
to come off, to miss
1. to come off, to be disconnected
To become detached.
ボタンが外れた。
The button came off.
ネジが外(はず)れた。
The screw came loose.
Sense 1: come off
チェーンが外(はず)れた。
The chain came off.
Sense 1: be disconnected
肩(かた)が外(はず)れた。
My shoulder was dislocated.
Sense 1: come off
2. to be off (target), to miss
To fail to hit.
予想(よそう)が外れた。
My prediction was wrong.
狙(ねら)いが外(はず)れた。
The aim missed.
Sense 2: miss
計算(けいさん)が外(はず)れた。
The calculation was off.
Sense 2: be off
期待(きたい)が外(はず)れた。
My expectations were not met.
Sense 2: miss (expectations)
天気予報(てんきよほう)が外(はず)れた。
The weather forecast was wrong.
Sense 2: be off
的(まと)から外(はず)れた。
It missed the target.
Sense 2: miss
3. to deviate from, to be outside
To be away from the expected.
町(まち)外れに住(す)む。
To live on the outskirts of town.
常識(じょうしき)から外(はず)れている。
It deviates from common sense.
Sense 3: deviate from
道(みち)から外(はず)れた。
I went off the path.
Sense 3: be outside
話題(わだい)から外(はず)れる。
To go off topic.
Sense 3: deviate from
規則(きそく)から外(はず)れる。
To deviate from the rules.
Sense 3: deviate from

外(はず)れる is an intransitive verb with several related meanings involving something coming off, missing, or deviating.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 外(はず)す (transitive, to remove/take off)

ASPECT (ている):
外(はず)れている indicates a resulting state: something is off, disconnected, or outside of something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ボタンが外(はず)れる: a button comes off
  • 予想(よそう)が外(はず)れる: a prediction is wrong
  • 的(まと)から外(はず)れる: to miss the target
  • 道(みち)から外(はず)れる: to go off the path
  • 常識(じょうしき)から外(はず)れる: to deviate from common sense

NOUN FORM:

  • 外(はず)れ: outskirts, edge; miss, failure
  • 町外(まちはず)れ: outskirts of town
  • 期待外(きたいはず)れ: disappointment (not meeting expectations)
