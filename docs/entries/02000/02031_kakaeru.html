1. to hold in one's arms, to carry
To hold something against one's body.

抱(かか)える is an ichidan verb with both physical and figurative meanings.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the object held or borne

ASPECT (ている):
抱(かか)えている means "is holding" (physical) or "has / is burdened with" (figurative).

PHYSICAL USE (to hold in arms):

  • 荷物(にもつ)を抱(かか)える: to carry luggage in one's arms
  • 赤(あか)ちゃんを抱(かか)える: to hold a baby
  • 膝(ひざ)を抱(かか)える: to hug one's knees

FIGURATIVE USE (to have, to be burdened with):

  • 問題(もんだい)を抱(かか)える: to have problems
  • 借金(しゃっきん)を抱(かか)える: to be burdened with debt
  • 不安(ふあん)を抱(かか)える: to have anxiety
  • 悩(なや)みを抱(かか)える: to have worries
  • 社員(しゃいん)を抱(かか)える: to have employees (on staff)

CONTRAST WITH 抱(だ)く AND 持(も)つ:

  • 抱(だ)く means to hug or embrace, with the arms wrapped around someone.
  • 持(も)つ is the ordinary word for holding something in the hand.
  • 抱(かか)える is holding something bulky against the body with the arms, and is the only one of the three used for bearing burdens figuratively.
