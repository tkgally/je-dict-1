濁(にご)る

にごる
verb (godan)
to become muddy, to become cloudy
1. to become muddy, to become cloudy
For liquid to become unclear.
川(かわ)の水(みず)が濁(にご)っている。
The river water is muddy.
雨(あめ)で水(みず)が濁(にご)った。
The water became muddy from the rain.
Sense 1: become muddy
池(いけ)の水(みず)が濁(にご)っている。
The pond water is cloudy.
Sense 1: become cloudy
空気(くうき)が濁(にご)っている。
The air is murky.
Sense 1: become cloudy
目(め)が濁(にご)っている。
The eyes are clouded.
Sense 1: become cloudy
声(こえ)が濁(にご)っている。
The voice is muddy.
Sense 1: become muddy
2. to be voiced (consonant)
For a consonant to become voiced (linguistics).
「か」が濁(にご)ると「が」になる。
When 'ka' is voiced, it becomes 'ga'.
「さ」が濁(にご)ると「ざ」になる。
When 'sa' is voiced, it becomes 'za'.
Sense 2: be voiced
「た」が濁(にご)ると「だ」になる。
When 'ta' is voiced, it becomes 'da'.
Sense 2: be voiced
「は」が濁(にご)ると「ば」になる。
When 'ha' is voiced, it becomes 'ba'.
Sense 2: be voiced

濁(にご)る has two distinct meanings: liquid becoming cloudy, and consonants becoming voiced.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 濁(にご)す (transitive - to make muddy; to mumble)

SENSE 1 - CLOUDY/MUDDY:

Describes liquid losing clarity.

  • 水(みず)が濁(にご)る: water becomes cloudy
  • 目(め)が濁(にご)る: eyes become clouded
  • 声(こえ)が濁(にご)る: voice becomes muddy/unclear

SENSE 2 - VOICED (LINGUISTICS):

Describes consonant voicing (adding 濁点(だくてん)):

  • か→が, さ→ざ, た→だ, は→ば

RELATED TERMS:

  • 濁点(だくてん): voicing mark (゛)
  • 濁音(だくおん): voiced sound
  • 清音(せいおん): unvoiced sound
  • 濁(にご)り: muddiness, cloudiness
