幼(おさな)い

おさない
i-adjective
very young, childish
1. very young
Being at an early stage of life.
幼(おさな)い子供(こども)
A young child
2. childish, immature
Having characteristics of a young child.
まだ考(かんが)えが幼(おさな)い。
The thinking is still immature.

幼(おさな)い describes early childhood (roughly ages 1-6).

RELATED:

  • 幼馴染(おさななじ)み: childhood friend
  • 幼稚園(ようちえん): kindergarten

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 若(わか)い: young (broader range)
  • 子供(こども)っぽい: childish (pejorative)
