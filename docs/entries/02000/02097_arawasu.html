現(あらわ)す

あらわす
verb (godan)
to show, to reveal, to express
1. to show, to reveal
To make something visible or known.
2. to express
To convey feelings or ideas.
本性(ほんしょう)を現(あらわ)す。
To reveal one's true nature.
姿(すがた)を現(あらわ)す。
To appear, to show oneself.

現(あらわ)す means to reveal or show.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 現(あらわ)す: to reveal, to appear
  • 表(あらわ)す: to express, to represent
  • 著(あらわ)す: to write (a book)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姿(すがた)を現(あらわ)す: to appear
  • 感情(かんじょう)を表(あらわ)す: to express emotions
