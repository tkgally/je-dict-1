裏切(うらぎ)る

うらぎる
verb (godan)
to betray
1. to betray, to turn traitor
To act disloyally to someone who trusts you.
友達(ともだち)を裏切(うらぎ)った。
I betrayed my friend.
信頼(しんらい)を裏切(うらぎ)らないで。
Don't betray my trust.
2. to disappoint, to let down
To fail to meet expectations.
期待(きたい)を裏切(うらぎ)る結果(けっか)だった。
The result was disappointing.
Lit: a result that betrayed expectations

裏切(うらぎ)る means to betray or to disappoint expectations.

COMPONENTS:

  • 裏(うら): back, behind
  • 切(き)る: to cut

Literally suggests "cutting from behind" or stabbing in the back.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を裏切(うらぎ)る

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 友人(ゆうじん)を裏切(うらぎ)る: to betray a friend
  • 信頼(しんらい)を裏切(うらぎ)る: to betray trust
  • 期待(きたい)を裏切(うらぎ)る: to betray/disappoint expectations
  • 約束(やくそく)を裏切(うらぎ)る: to break a promise

RELATED WORDS:

  • 裏切(うらぎ)り: betrayal (noun)
  • 裏切(うらぎ)り者(もの): traitor
