箱(はこ)

はこ
noun
box, case, carton
1. box
A container with flat sides, typically square or rectangular.
箱(はこ)を開(あ)ける。
To open a box.
お菓子(かし)の箱(はこ)をもらった。
I received a box of sweets.
2. case, carton
A container for holding or transporting items.

箱(はこ) refers to boxes, cases, and containers - typically with flat sides.

COMMON TYPES:

  • 段(だん)ボール箱(ばこ): cardboard box
  • 弁当箱(べんとうばこ): lunch box
  • 貯金箱(ちょきんばこ): piggy bank, money box
  • ゴミ箱(ばこ): trash can, bin
  • 道具箱(どうぐばこ): toolbox
  • 本箱(ほんばこ): bookshelf
  • 救急箱(きゅうきゅうばこ): first aid kit

COUNTER:

箱(はこ) itself is used as a counter:

  • 一箱(ひとはこ): one box
  • 二箱(ふたはこ): two boxes
  • 三箱(さんばこ): three boxes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 箱(はこ)を開(あ)ける: to open a box
  • 箱(はこ)に入(い)れる: to put in a box
  • 箱(はこ)詰(づ)め: boxed, packaged
