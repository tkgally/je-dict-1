1. to burn, to kindle
To make a fire burn.
焚(た)き火(び)を焚(た)く。
To make a bonfire.
お香(こう)を焚(た)く。
To burn incense.
寒(さむ)い夜(よる)に薪(まき)を焚(た)いて暖(あたた)まった。
On a cold night, I burned firewood to warm up.

焚(た)く means to burn or kindle something intentionally, such as fires, incense, or leaves.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 火(ひ)を焚(た)く

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 焚(た)き火(び)を焚(た)く: to build a bonfire
  • お香(こう)を焚(た)く: to burn incense
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)を焚(た)く: to burn fallen leaves
  • 薪(まき)を焚(た)く: to burn firewood

DISTINCTION FROM 炊(た)く:

  • 焚(た)く: to burn (fire, incense) - uses 焚(た) kanji
  • 炊(た)く: to cook rice - uses 炊(た) kanji

Both are pronounced たく but have different meanings and kanji.
