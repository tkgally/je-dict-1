歌(うた)

うた
noun
song, singing

Definitions

1. song
A piece of music with words.
2. poem, verse
Traditional Japanese poetry such as waka or tanka.

Examples

歌(うた)を歌(うた)う。
To sing a song.
好(す)きな歌(うた)は何(なん)ですか。
What's your favorite song?

Notes

歌(うた) means song.

POETRY:

歌(うた) also refers to traditional Japanese poetry:

  • 和歌(わか): waka (31-syllable poem)
  • 短歌(たんか): tanka (similar to waka)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 歌手(かしゅ): singer
  • 歌詞(かし): lyrics
  • 国歌(こっか): national anthem
  • 民謡(みんよう): folk song
