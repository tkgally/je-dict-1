悔(くや)しい

くやしい
i-adjective
frustrating, regrettable
1. frustrating, vexing
Feeling upset about failure or unfairness.
負(ま)けて悔しい。
I'm frustrated about losing.
悔しくて泣(な)いた。
I cried out of frustration.
2. regrettable, mortifying
Causing a sense of remorse or chagrin.

悔(くや)しい is an i-adjective expressing frustration, especially over defeat, failure, or unfair situations.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 悔(くや)しく
  • Te-form: 悔(くや)しくて
  • Noun form: 悔(くや)しさ (frustration)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 悔(くや)しい思(おも)いをする: to feel frustrated
  • 悔(くや)しくて泣(な)く: to cry from frustration
  • 悔(くや)しさをバネにする: to use frustration as motivation

USAGE:
Often used in competitive contexts (sports, games, exams). The feeling can be a positive motivator to try harder next time.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 悔(く)やむ: to regret (verb)
  • 悔(く)い: regret, remorse (noun)
  • 残念(ざんねん): disappointing, unfortunate
