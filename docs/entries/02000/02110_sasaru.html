1. to stick, to be stuck
For something sharp to pierce and remain in place.
とげが指(ゆび)に刺(さ)さった。
A thorn stuck in my finger.
ナイフがテーブルに刺(さ)さっている。
A knife is stuck in the table.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)が心(こころ)に深(ふか)く刺(さ)さった。
His words struck me deeply.
Figurative: 心(こころ)に刺(さ)さる means something hits home emotionally.

刺(さ)さる means for something sharp to pierce and remain stuck in place.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 刺(さ)す (transitive, to stab/pierce)

ASPECT (ている):

刺(さ)さっている describes the state of being stuck in something.

  • ナイフが刺(さ)さっている: A knife is stuck (in something)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • とげが指(ゆび)に刺(さ)さる: a thorn gets stuck in one's finger
  • 針(はり)が刺(さ)さる: a needle gets stuck
  • 心(こころ)に刺(さ)さる: to strike a chord (figurative)

RELATED:

  • 突(つ)き刺(さ)さる: to pierce through
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, poke, sting
