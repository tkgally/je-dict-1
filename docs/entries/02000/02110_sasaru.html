刺(さ)さる

ささる
verb (godan)
to stick, to be stuck
1. to stick, to be stuck
For something sharp to pierce and remain in place.
とげが指(ゆび)に刺(さ)さった。
A thorn stuck in my finger.
ナイフがテーブルに刺(さ)さっている。
A knife is stuck in the table.

刺(さ)さる is intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 刺(さ)さる (intransitive): to get stuck
  • 刺(さ)す (transitive): to stab, to pierce
general
