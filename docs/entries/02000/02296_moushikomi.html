申(もう)し込(こ)み

もうしこみ
noun
application, entry, subscription

Definitions

1. application, entry, request
A formal request or application to participate in, subscribe to, or receive something.

Examples

申(もう)し込(こ)みは明日(あした)までです。
Applications are due by tomorrow.
コースへの申(もう)し込(こ)みをした。
I applied for the course.
申(もう)し込(こ)み方法(ほうほう)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me how to apply.

Notes

COMPOUND: 申(もう)し込(こ)む (to apply) → noun form

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 申(もう)し込(こ)みをする (to apply)
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み締(し)め切(き)り (application deadline)
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み用紙(ようし) (application form)
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み方法(ほうほう) (application method)
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み先(さき) (where to apply)

CONTEXTS:

  • Course/class registration
  • Event participation
  • Service subscription
  • Contest/competition entry

RELATED: 申込書(もうしこみしょ) (application form)
