飛(と)び出(だ)す

とびだす
godan verb
to jump out, to rush out
1. to jump out, to rush out
To suddenly emerge or exit quickly.
子供(こども)が道(みち)に飛び出した。
A child ran out into the road.
家(いえ)を飛び出す。
To run away from home.
2. to protrude, to stick out
To extend outward from a surface.

飛び出す is a compound verb (飛ぶ + 出す).

WARNING SIGN:

  • 「飛(と)び出(だ)し注意(ちゅうい)」: Watch for children/pedestrians

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • 家(いえ)を飛び出す: to leave home abruptly
general
