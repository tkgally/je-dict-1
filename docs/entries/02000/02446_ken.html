～軒(けん)

けん
counter
counter for houses/buildings

Definitions

1. counter for houses and buildings
Used to count houses, shops, and small buildings.

Examples

この通(とお)りに三(さん)軒の店(みせ)がある。
There are three shops on this street.

Notes

COUNTING:

  • 一(いっ)軒 (ikken)
  • 二(に)軒 (niken)
  • 三(さん)軒 (sanken)
  • 何(なん)軒 (nanken): how many houses?
