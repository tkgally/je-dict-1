～軒(けん)

けん
counter
counter for houses/buildings
See also: 〜軒(けん) (counter: houses (variant))
1. counter for houses and buildings
Used to count houses, shops, and small buildings.
一(いっ)軒(けん)の家(いえ)。
One house.
三(さん)軒(けん)の店(みせ)がある。
There are three shops.
この通(とお)りに何(なん)軒(けん)店(みせ)がありますか。
How many shops are there on this street?
三(さん)軒(げん)隣(となり)にパン屋(や)がある。
There's a bakery three doors down.
三(さん)軒(げん)隣(となり) is read さんげんどなり, with 軒 read as げん.
今日(きょう)は五(ご)軒(けん)の家(いえ)を回(まわ)った。
I visited five houses today.

～軒(けん) is a counter for houses, shops, and small buildings.

COUNTING:

  • 一(いっ)軒(けん): one building
  • 二(に)軒(けん): two buildings
  • 三(さん)軒(けん): three buildings
  • 何(なん)軒(けん): how many buildings?

WHAT IT COUNTS:

  • 家(いえ): houses
  • 店(みせ): shops
  • 飲食店(いんしょくてん): restaurants
  • Small buildings in general

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 三(さん)軒(げん)隣(となり): three doors down
  • 一(いっ)軒(けん)家(や): detached house
  • 軒並(のきな)み: every house, across the board

CONTRAST:

  • ～棟(むね)/棟(とう): for larger buildings/structures
