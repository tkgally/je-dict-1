卸(おろ)す

おろす
godan verb
to wholesale; to grate
1. to sell wholesale
To sell goods in large quantities to retailers.
2. to grate
To reduce food to small pieces using a grater.
大根(だいこん)を卸(おろ)す。
To grate daikon radish.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 卸(おろ)す: to wholesale, grate
  • 下(お)ろす: to lower
  • 降(お)ろす: to let off

RELATED:

  • 卸売(おろしうり): wholesale
  • 大根卸(だいこんおろ)し: grated daikon
