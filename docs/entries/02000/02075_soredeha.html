それでは

それでは
conjunction
well then, in that case

Definitions

1. well then, in that case
Used to transition to a new topic or to conclude a conversation.
2. if that's the case
Used to draw a conclusion from what was said.

Examples

それでは、始(はじ)めましょう。
Well then, let's begin.
それでは、また明日(あした)。
Well then, see you tomorrow.
それでは、私(わたし)が行(い)きます。
In that case, I'll go.

Notes

それでは is used to transition or conclude.

CONTRACTED FORMS:

  • では (shortened)
  • じゃ (casual)
  • じゃあ (casual)

USAGE:

  • Starting something: それでは、始(はじ)めます
  • Leaving: それでは、失礼(しつれい)します
  • Drawing conclusions: それでは、〜ということですね
