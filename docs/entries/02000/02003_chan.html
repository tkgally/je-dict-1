〜ちゃん

ちゃん
suffix
affectionate suffix
1. affectionate suffix for names
An endearing honorific suffix used mainly for children and young women.
花子(はなこ)ちゃん、こんにちは。
Hello, Hanako.
お婆(ばあ)ちゃんの家(いえ)に行(い)く。
I'm going to grandma's house.
赤(あか)ちゃんが泣(な)いている。
The baby is crying.

〜ちゃん is an affectionate honorific suffix.

USAGE:

  • For children (especially girls)
  • For close female friends
  • For babies and young people
  • In family terms

EXAMPLES:

  • お母(かあ)ちゃん: mommy
  • お婆(ばあ)ちゃん: grandma
  • 赤(あか)ちゃん: baby

NOTE: Less formal than 〜さん, more affectionate than 〜君(くん)
