1. to mix, to blend
To combine different things together.
よく混(ま)ぜてください。
Please mix well.
卵(たまご)を混(ま)ぜる。
To beat eggs.
材料(ざいりょう)を全部(ぜんぶ)ボウルに入(い)れて混(ま)ぜた。
I put all the ingredients in a bowl and mixed them.
2. to include, to let join
To allow someone to participate.
仲間(なかま)に混(ま)ぜる。
To let someone join the group.
「私(わたし)も混(ま)ぜて。」
"Let me join too."
子供(こども)たちの遊(あそ)びに混(ま)ぜてもらった。
I was allowed to join the children's game.

混(ま)ぜる is an ichidan transitive verb meaning to mix or blend.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 混(ま)ざる (intransitive, to be mixed)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • よく混(ま)ぜる: to mix well
  • 軽(かる)く混(ま)ぜる: to mix lightly
  • 〜に混(ま)ぜる: to mix into ~
  • 仲間(なかま)に混(ま)ぜる: to let someone join the group

COOKING USES:

  • 卵(たまご)を混(ま)ぜる: to beat eggs
  • 材料(ざいりょう)を混(ま)ぜる: to mix ingredients
  • ヘラで混(ま)ぜる: to mix with a spatula

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • かき混(ま)ぜる: to stir (more vigorous)
  • 混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる: to combine, blend together
  • 交(ま)ぜる: alternate kanji, same meaning

Related Words
