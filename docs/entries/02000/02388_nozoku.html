覗(のぞ)く

のぞく
godan verb
to peek, to look in
1. to peek, to peep
To look quickly or secretly.
2. to look in, to look into
To look inside something.
窓(まど)から覗く。
To peek through the window.
中(なか)を覗いてみる。
To take a look inside.

覗く implies looking through an opening.

HOMOPHONE:

  • 除(のぞ)く: to remove, exclude (different kanji)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • ちょっと覗く: to take a quick look
  • 顔(かお)を覗かせる: to show one's face briefly
