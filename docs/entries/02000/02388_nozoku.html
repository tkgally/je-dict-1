覗(のぞ)く

のぞく
godan verb
to peek, to look in
1. to peek, to peep
To look quickly or secretly.
窓(まど)から覗く。
To peek through the window.
穴(あな)から覗(のぞ)く。
To peek through a hole.
Sense 1: to peek
こっそり覗(のぞ)いてはいけない。
You shouldn't peek secretly.
Sense 1: to peep
顔(かお)を覗(のぞ)かせる。
To show one's face briefly.
Sense 1: to peek
隙間(すきま)から覗(のぞ)く。
To peek through a gap.
Sense 1: to peek
2. to look in, to look into
To look inside something.
中(なか)を覗いてみる。
To take a look inside.
Sense 2: to look into
箱(はこ)の中(なか)を覗(のぞ)く。
To look inside the box.
Sense 2: to look in
井戸(いど)を覗(のぞ)く。
To look into a well.
Sense 2: to look into
望遠鏡(ぼうえんきょう)を覗(のぞ)く。
To look through a telescope.
Sense 2: to look in
鏡(かがみ)を覗(のぞ)き込(こ)む。
To peer into a mirror.
Sense 2: to look into

覗(のぞ)く is a godan verb meaning to peek, peep, or look into something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for what you peek at/into

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 窓(まど)から覗(のぞ)く: to peek through a window
  • 中(なか)を覗(のぞ)く: to look inside
  • 顔(かお)を覗(のぞ)かせる: to peek one's face in
  • 望遠鏡(ぼうえんきょう)を覗(のぞ)く: to look through a telescope
  • ちょっと覗(のぞ)く: to take a quick look

HOMOPHONE (different kanji):

  • 除(のぞ)く: to remove, exclude, except

COMPOUND:

  • 覗(のぞ)き込(こ)む: to peer into (more intently)
