1. to pass by, to go past
To move past a place or point without stopping.
駅(えき)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎた。
I went past the station.
車(くるま)が通(とお)り過(す)ぎるのを待(ま)つ。
To wait for the car to pass by.
目的地(もくてきち)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎたことに気(き)づいた。
I realized I had gone past my destination.
出口(でぐち)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎてしまったので、戻(もど)らなければならなかった。
I accidentally went past the exit, so I had to go back.
友達(ともだち)が目(め)の前(まえ)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎたのに、気(き)づかなくて声(こえ)をかけられなかった。
My friend passed right in front of me, but I didn't notice and couldn't call out to them.

通(とお)り過(す)ぎる is a compound ichidan verb combining 通(とお)る (to pass through) + 過(す)ぎる (to exceed/pass).

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). Takes を particle for the place passed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 駅(えき)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎる: to go past the station
  • 目(め)の前(まえ)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎる: to pass before one's eyes
  • 車(くるま)が通(とお)り過(す)ぎる: a car passes by

USAGE NOTE:
Often used with 〜てしまう to express accidentally going past something: 駅(えき)を通(とお)り過(す)ぎてしまった (I accidentally missed/passed the station).

RELATED VERBS:

  • 通(とお)る: to pass through, go along
  • 過(す)ぎる: to pass (time), to exceed
