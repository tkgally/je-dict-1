1. to bring near, to move closer
To make something come closer.
顔(かお)を近(ちか)づける。
To bring one's face closer.
火(ひ)を近(ちか)づけないで。
Don't bring fire near.
耳(みみ)を近(ちか)づけて聞(き)いた。
I brought my ear closer and listened.

近(ちか)づける means to bring something or someone closer.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 近(ちか)づく (intransitive, to approach)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 顔(かお)を近(ちか)づける: to bring one's face closer
  • 耳(みみ)を近(ちか)づける: to bring one's ear closer (to listen)
  • 火(ひ)を近(ちか)づけない: not bring fire near
  • 距離(きょり)を近(ちか)づける: to close the distance

ASPECT (ている):
近(ちか)づけている indicates ongoing action of bringing closer.

RELATED:

  • 近(ちか)づく: to approach (intransitive)
  • 近(ちか)い: close, near (adjective)
  • 離(はな)す: to separate (opposite action)
