取(と)り上(あ)げる

とりあげる
ichidan verb
to take up, to pick up
1. to take up, to pick up
To lift or grab something.
電話(でんわ)を取り上げる。
To pick up the phone.
落(お)ちたペンを取(と)り上(あ)げた。
I picked up the dropped pen.
Sense 1: pick up
赤(あか)ちゃんを取(と)り上(あ)げる。
To deliver a baby.
Sense 1: take up (obstetrics)
受話器(じゅわき)を取(と)り上(あ)げた。
I picked up the receiver.
Sense 1: pick up
2. to take away, to confiscate
To remove something from someone's possession.
免許(めんきょ)を取り上げられた。
My license was confiscated.
手(て)から取(と)り上(あ)げられた。
It was taken from my hands.
Sense 2: take away
権利(けんり)を取(と)り上(あ)げる。
To revoke rights.
Sense 2: take away
おもちゃ(おもちゃ)を取(と)り上(あ)げられた。
My toy was confiscated.
Sense 2: confiscate
財産(ざいさん)を取(と)り上(あ)げられた。
My property was seized.
Sense 2: take away
3. to take up (a topic), to deal with
To address or discuss a subject.
ニュースで取り上げられた。
It was covered in the news.
議題(ぎだい)として取(と)り上(あ)げる。
To take up as an agenda item.
Sense 3: take up (topic)
番組(ばんぐみ)で取(と)り上(あ)げられた。
It was featured on a program.
Sense 3: deal with
問題(もんだい)を取(と)り上(あ)げる。
To address a problem.
Sense 3: take up
会議(かいぎ)で取(と)り上(あ)げる予定(よてい)だ。
It's scheduled to be discussed at the meeting.
Sense 3: deal with
新聞(しんぶん)に取(と)り上(あ)げられた。
It was covered in the newspaper.
Sense 3: take up

取(と)り上(あ)げる is a compound verb meaning to pick up, confiscate, or take up (a topic).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Compound: 取(と)る + 上(あ)げる

THREE MAIN MEANINGS:
1. Physical: to pick up, lift
2. Punitive: to confiscate, take away
3. Media/Discussion: to cover, feature, address

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電話(でんわ)を取(と)り上(あ)げる: to pick up the phone
  • 免許(めんきょ)を取(と)り上(あ)げる: to revoke a license
  • ニュースで取(と)り上(あ)げる: to cover in the news
  • 議題(ぎだい)として取(と)り上(あ)げる: to take up as an agenda item

SPECIAL USAGE:

  • 赤(あか)ちゃんを取(と)り上(あ)げる: to deliver a baby (obstetrics)
