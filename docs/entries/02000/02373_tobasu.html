飛(と)ばす

とばす
godan verb
to fly (something), to skip

Definitions

1. to fly, to send flying
To cause something to move through the air.
2. to skip, to omit
To pass over without reading or doing.
3. to drive fast, to speed
To move at high speed.

Examples

紙(かみ)ヒコーキを飛ばす。
To fly a paper airplane.
難(むずか)しい問題(もんだい)を飛ばす。
To skip a difficult question.
車(くるま)を飛ばして来(き)た。
I drove fast to get here.

Notes

飛ばす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 飛ばす (transitive): to fly something
  • 飛(と)ぶ (intransitive): to fly

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ページを飛ばす: to skip pages
  • 噂(うわさ)を飛ばす: to spread rumors
