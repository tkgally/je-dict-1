生(は)える

はえる
ichidan verb
to grow, to sprout
1. to grow, to sprout
For plants, hair, or teeth to emerge and develop.
草(くさ)が生えている。
Grass is growing.
歯(は)が生えてきた。
Teeth have started to come in.

生(は)える means for something to grow or sprout naturally.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 生(は)やす (transitive - to grow, let grow)

WHAT CAN 生(は)える:

  • 草(くさ)が生(は)える: grass grows
  • 歯(は)が生(は)える: teeth come in
  • 髭(ひげ)が生(は)える: beard grows
  • 髪(かみ)が生(は)える: hair grows
  • カビが生(は)える: mold grows

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が生(は)えている: ~ is growing (state)
  • 〜が生(は)えてきた: ~ has started to grow

HOMOPHONE DISTINCTION:

  • 生(は)える: to grow (plants, hair, teeth)
  • 映(は)える: to look good, to stand out
