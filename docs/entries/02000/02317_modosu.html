1. to return, to put back
To restore something to its original place or condition.
本(ほん)を棚(たな)に戻(もど)す。
To return the book to the shelf.
話(はなし)を戻(もど)しましょう。
Let's get back to the topic.
元(もと)の場所(ばしょ)に戻(もど)す。
To put back in the original place.
時計(とけい)の針(はり)を戻(もど)す。
To turn back the hands of a clock.
設定(せってい)を初期(しょき)に戻(もど)す。
To reset to the initial settings.
借(か)りたものを戻(もど)す。
To return something borrowed.
2. to vomit, to throw up
To expel stomach contents (euphemistic).
飲(の)みすぎて戻(もど)してしまった。
I drank too much and threw up.
車(くるま)酔(よ)いで戻(もど)しそうだ。
I feel like I'm going to throw up from motion sickness.
食(た)べたものを戻(もど)した。
I threw up what I ate.
赤(あか)ちゃんがミルクを戻(もど)した。
The baby spit up milk.
気持(きも)ち悪(わる)くて戻(もど)しそうになった。
I felt sick and almost threw up.

戻(もど)す is a godan verb meaning to return something or put it back. It also has a secondary meaning of "to vomit" (euphemistic).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 戻(もど)る (intransitive: to return, go back)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 元(もと)に戻(もど)す: to restore to original state
  • 話(はなし)を戻(もど)す: to get back to the topic
  • 巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to rewind
  • 初期(しょき)設定(せってい)に戻(もど)す: to reset to default

SECONDARY MEANING:
Used euphemistically for "to vomit": 飲(の)みすぎて戻(もど)した (I drank too much and threw up).

Related Words

Synonym: 返(かえ)す (to return (give back))
