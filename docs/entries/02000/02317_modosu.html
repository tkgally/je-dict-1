戻(もど)す

もどす
godan verb
to return, to put back
1. to return, to put back
To restore something to its original place or condition.
2. to vomit, to throw up
To expel stomach contents (euphemistic).
本(ほん)を棚(たな)に戻す。
To return the book to the shelf.
話(はなし)を戻しましょう。
Let's get back to the topic.

戻す is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 戻す (transitive): to put back
  • 戻(もど)る (intransitive): to return, go back

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 元(もと)に戻す: to restore to original state
  • 巻(ま)き戻す: to rewind
