咥(くわ)える

くわえる
verb (ichidan)
to hold in one's mouth
1. to hold in one's mouth
To grip something between the lips or teeth.
タバコを咥(くわ)える。
To hold a cigarette in one's mouth.
犬(いぬ)がボールを咥(くわ)えている。
The dog is holding a ball in its mouth.
ペンを咥(くわ)えて考(かんが)える。
To think while holding a pen in one's mouth.

咥(くわ)える means to hold something in one's mouth, gripping it with the lips or teeth without biting down.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: タバコを咥(くわ)える

ASPECT (ている):
咥(くわ)えている indicates the ongoing state of holding something in the mouth.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • タバコを咥(くわ)える: to hold a cigarette in one's mouth
  • パイプを咥(くわ)える: to hold a pipe in one's mouth
  • ペンを咥(くわ)える: to hold a pen in one's mouth

DON'T CONFUSE WITH 加(くわ)える:

  • 咥(くわ)える (hold in mouth) - uses {咥} kanji
  • 加(くわ)える (to add) - uses {加} kanji

RELATED: 噛(か)む (to bite/chew) involves biting down, while 咥(くわ)える is just holding.
