向(む)く

むく
godan verb
to face, to turn toward
1. to face, to turn toward
To be directed or oriented in a particular direction.
東(ひがし)を向く。
To face east.
2. to be suited to
To be appropriate or fitting for a purpose.
この仕事(しごと)は私(わたし)に向いている。
This job suits me.

向(む)く is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 向(む)く (intransitive): to face
  • 向(む)ける (transitive): to turn something toward

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜に向(む)いている: to be suited to ~
  • 前(まえ)を向(む)く: to face forward
  • 上(うえ)を向(む)く: to look up
