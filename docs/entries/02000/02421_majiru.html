1. to be mixed in, to be included
To be combined or present among other things.
白髪(しらが)が混(ま)じっている。
There are gray hairs mixed in.
外国人(がいこくじん)が混(ま)じっている。
There are foreigners among them.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)には嘘(うそ)が混(ま)じっていた。
There were lies mixed in with what he said.

混(ま)じる is an intransitive verb meaning to be mixed in or present among other things.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 混(ま)ぜる (transitive, to mix)

CONTRAST WITH 混(ま)ざる:

  • 混(ま)じる: emphasizes presence/inclusion (something is among)
  • 混(ま)ざる: emphasizes blending/combining

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 白髪(しらが)が混(ま)じっている: there are gray hairs mixed in
  • 外国人(がいこくじん)が混(ま)じっている: there are foreigners among them
  • 赤(あか)が混(ま)じっている: there's some red mixed in
  • 嘘(うそ)が混(ま)じっている: there are lies mixed in

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 交(まじ)わる: to intersect, associate with others
  • 混(ま)ざる: to be blended together
