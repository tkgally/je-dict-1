1. probably, perhaps, likely
Indicates probability or likelihood. More formal than たぶん.
おそらく雨(あめ)が降(ふ)るでしょう。
It will probably rain.
おそらく彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないと思(おも)う。
I think he probably won't come.
おそらくそれが原因(げんいん)だろう。
That's probably the cause.

おそらく indicates high probability — roughly equivalent to "most likely" or "in all probability." More formal and literary than たぶん.

COMPARISON (PROBABILITY SCALE):

  • きっと: surely, definitely (highest certainty)
  • おそらく: probably (high probability, formal)
  • たぶん: probably (moderate probability, casual)
  • もしかしたら/もしかすると: perhaps, maybe (lower probability)

POSITION:
Typically placed at the beginning of a clause or sentence. Often paired with だろう or でしょう.

KANJI:
Can be written as 恐(おそ)らく. The kanji 恐(おそ) means "fear" — the word originally implied apprehension about what might happen.

REGISTER:
