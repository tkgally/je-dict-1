1. to succeed, to inherit
To take over a position, business, or tradition.
家業(かぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ。
To take over the family business.
父(ちち)の後(あと)を継(つ)ぐ。
To succeed one's father.
息子(むすこ)が店(みせ)を継(つ)いで、今(いま)も営業(えいぎょう)している。
The son took over the shop, and it is still in business.
2. to follow, to continue
To come after something.
伝統(でんとう)を継(つ)ぐ。
To carry on a tradition.
先輩(せんぱい)の意志(いし)を継(つ)いで活動(かつどう)を続(つづ)ける。
To carry on the senior's wishes and continue the activities.
彼(かれ)は師匠(ししょう)の技(わざ)を継(つ)いだ。
He inherited his master's technique.

継(つ)ぐ means to succeed or take over something from a predecessor.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家業(かぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ: to take over the family business
  • 後(あと)を継(つ)ぐ: to succeed someone
  • 跡(あと)を継(つ)ぐ: to inherit a position/legacy
  • 王位(おうい)を継(つ)ぐ: to succeed to the throne

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 継(つ)ぐ: to succeed, inherit
  • 注(つ)ぐ: to pour (liquid)
  • 接(つ)ぐ: to join, graft, splice
  • 次(つ)ぐ: to rank next to

RELATED:

  • 後継者(こうけいしゃ): successor
  • 継承(けいしょう): succession, inheritance
