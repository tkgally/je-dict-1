組(く)む

くむ
verb (godan)
to assemble, to form, to cross
1. to assemble, to put together
To join parts together.
棚(たな)を組(く)む。
To assemble a shelf.
予定(よてい)を組(く)む。
To put together a schedule.
部品(ぶひん)を組(く)んで完成(かんせい)させる。
To assemble the parts and complete it.
2. to form, to organize (a group)
To create a team or group.
チームを組(く)む。
To form a team.
二人(ふたり)で組(く)んで作業(さぎょう)する。
To work in pairs.
彼(かれ)と組(く)んで仕事(しごと)をした。
I teamed up with him to work.
3. to cross, to fold (arms/legs)
To interlock parts of the body.
腕(うで)を組(く)む。
To fold one's arms.
脚(あし)を組(く)む。
To cross one's legs.
考(かんが)えながら腕(うで)を組(く)んでいた。
I was thinking with my arms folded.

組(く)む is a versatile verb whose core meaning involves interlocking or joining things together. It extends to forming groups and creating structures.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 組(く)まれる (passive)

ASPECT (ている):
組(く)んでいる describes a resulting state: 腕(うで)を組(く)んでいる (has arms folded).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • チームを組(く)む: to form a team
  • 腕(うで)を組(く)む: to fold/cross one's arms
  • 脚(あし)を組(く)む: to cross one's legs
  • 予定(よてい)を組(く)む: to put together a schedule
  • 番組(ばんぐみ)を組(く)む: to arrange a program

COMPOUNDS:

  • 組(く)み合(あ)わせる: to combine
  • 組(く)み立(た)てる: to assemble (a product)
  • 番組(ばんぐみ): TV/radio program
