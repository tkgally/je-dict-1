組(く)む

くむ
verb (godan)
to assemble, to form, to cross

Definitions

1. to assemble, to put together
To join parts together.
2. to form, to organize (a group)
To create a team or group.
3. to cross, to fold (arms/legs)
To interlock parts of the body.

Examples

チームを組(く)む。
To form a team.
腕(うで)を組(く)む。
To fold one's arms.
脚(あし)を組(く)む。
To cross one's legs.

Notes

組(く)む has multiple uses.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • チームを組(く)む: to form a team
  • 腕(うで)を組(く)む: to fold arms
  • 脚(あし)を組(く)む: to cross legs
  • 予定(よてい)を組(く)む: to make a schedule

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 組(く)む: to assemble
  • 汲(く)む: to draw water
  • 酌(く)む: to serve sake
