向(む)ける

むける
ichidan verb
to turn toward, to point
1. to turn toward, to point
To direct something in a particular direction.
カメラを向ける。
To point the camera.
2. to aim at, to direct to
To target or address something toward a specific purpose or audience.
子供(こども)向けの番組(ばんぐみ)
A program for children

VERB PAIR:

  • 向(む)ける (transitive): to turn/point something
  • 向(む)く (intransitive): to face, to turn

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に向(む)ける: to direct toward ~
  • 〜向(む)け: intended for ~, aimed at ~
  • 顔(かお)を向(む)ける: to turn one's face
  • 目(め)を向(む)ける: to turn one's eyes/attention
  • 背(せ)を向(む)ける: to turn one's back on

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子供(こども)向(む)け: for children
  • 初心者(しょしんしゃ)向(む)け: for beginners
  • 海外(かいがい)向(む)け: for overseas/export

Conjugation: 向(む)けない / 向(む)けます / 向(む)けて / 向(む)けた
