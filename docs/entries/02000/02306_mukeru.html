向(む)ける

むける
ichidan verb
to turn toward, to point

Definitions

1. to turn toward, to point
To direct something in a particular direction.
2. to aim at, to direct to
To target or address something toward a specific purpose or audience.

Examples

カメラを向ける。
To point the camera.
子供(こども)向けの番組(ばんぐみ)
A program for children

Notes

向ける is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 向ける (transitive): to turn something toward
  • 向(む)く (intransitive): to face

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜向け: intended for ~, aimed at ~
  • 顔(かお)を向ける: to turn one's face toward
