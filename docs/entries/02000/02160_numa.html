沼(ぬま)

ぬま
noun
swamp, marsh, bog
1. swamp, marsh
An area of low-lying, waterlogged land.
この沼(ぬま)には魚(さかな)がたくさんいる。
There are many fish in this marsh.
沼地(ぬまち)を歩(ある)く。
To walk through marshland.
Sense 1: swamp
沼(ぬま)の周(まわ)りを散歩(さんぽ)する。
To walk around the marsh.
Sense 1: marsh
沼(ぬま)に住(す)む鳥(とり)。
Birds that live in swamps.
Sense 1: swamp
沼(ぬま)で蓮(はす)が咲(さ)いている。
Lotus flowers are blooming in the marsh.
Sense 1: marsh
2. bog, quagmire
Wet muddy ground that is difficult to cross.
沼(ぬま)に足(あし)を取(と)られた。
My foot got stuck in the swamp.
泥沼(どろぬま)にはまった。
I got stuck in the quagmire.
Sense 2: bog
泥沼(どろぬま)の争(あらそ)い。
A quagmire of conflict.
Sense 2: quagmire (figurative)
借金(しゃっきん)の沼(ぬま)にはまる。
To fall into a quagmire of debt.
Sense 2: bog (figurative)
趣味(しゅみ)の沼(ぬま)にはまる。
To fall deep into a hobby (slang).
Sense 2: quagmire (slang)

沼(ぬま) refers to wetlands.

SLANG:
In modern Japanese, 沼(ぬま) is slang for becoming deeply obsessed with something (like a hobby or fandom), similar to "falling down a rabbit hole."

COMPOUNDS:

  • 沼地(ぬまち): marshland
  • 泥沼(どろぬま): quagmire, muddy swamp
