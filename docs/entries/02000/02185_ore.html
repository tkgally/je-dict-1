俺(おれ)

おれ
pronoun
I, me (masculine, casual)

Definitions

1. I, me
A casual first-person pronoun used mainly by males.

Examples

俺(おれ)は学生(がくせい)だ。
I'm a student.
俺(おれ)に任(まか)せろ。
Leave it to me.

Notes

俺(おれ) is a casual masculine pronoun for "I."

USAGE:

  • Casual, masculine
  • Used among friends, family, or in informal situations
  • Can sound rough or assertive
  • Not appropriate in formal or polite contexts

COMPARE:

  • 私(わたし): neutral, polite
  • 僕(ぼく): masculine, softer than 俺(おれ)
  • 俺(おれ): masculine, casual/rough
