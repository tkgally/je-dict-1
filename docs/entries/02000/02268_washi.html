和紙(わし)

わし
noun
Japanese paper
1. Japanese paper
Traditional handmade paper from Japan.
和紙(わし)で折(お)り紙(がみ)を作(つく)る。
To make origami with Japanese paper.
和紙(わし)は丈夫(じょうぶ)だ。
Japanese paper is durable.

和紙(わし) is traditional Japanese paper.

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • Made from plant fibers (mulberry, gampi, etc.)
  • Durable and long-lasting
  • UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage

USES:

  • Calligraphy, shoji screens, origami, lanterns

FAMOUS TYPES:

  • 美濃和紙(みのわし): Mino washi
  • 越前和紙(えちぜんわし): Echizen washi
