1. to recommend, to advise
To suggest something as good or suitable.
この本(ほん)を勧(すす)める。
I recommend this book.
医者(いしゃ)に運動(うんどう)を勧(すす)められた。
The doctor recommended exercise to me.
友人(ゆうじん)が面白(おもしろ)い映画(えいが)を勧(すす)めてくれた。
A friend recommended an interesting movie to me.
2. to encourage, to urge
To persuade someone to do something.
転職(てんしょく)を勧(すす)める。
To encourage a career change.
友人(ゆうじん)に留学(りゅうがく)を勧(すす)めた。
I encouraged my friend to study abroad.
周(まわ)りの人(ひと)に健康(けんこう)診断(しんだん)を受(う)けるよう勧(すす)めている。
I'm encouraging the people around me to get health checkups.

勧(すす)める means to recommend or advise someone to do something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜に〜を勧(すす)める (to recommend X to someone)

KANJI VARIANTS (same reading, different nuances):

  • 勧(すす)める: to recommend, advise (general)
  • 薦(すす)める: to recommend (more formal)
  • 進(すす)める: to advance, proceed (different meaning!)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 本(ほん)を勧(すす)める: to recommend a book
  • 医者(いしゃ)に勧(すす)められた: was recommended by a doctor
  • お勧(すす)め: recommendation (noun form)

USEFUL EXPRESSION:
お勧(すす)めは何(なん)ですか? (What do you recommend?) - common at restaurants.
