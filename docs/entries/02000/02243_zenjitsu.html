前日(ぜんじつ)

ぜんじつ
noun
previous day, day before

Definitions

1. previous day, day before
The day immediately before a particular event or date. Often used to describe preparations or events occurring the day before something important.

Examples

試験(しけん)の前日(ぜんじつ)は早(はや)く寝(ね)た方(ほう)がいい。
You should go to bed early the day before the exam.
結婚式(けっこんしき)の前日(ぜんじつ)に準備(じゅんび)をした。
I made preparations the day before the wedding.
前日(ぜんじつ)までにご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Please contact us by the day before.

Notes

USAGE: 前日(ぜんじつ) refers to 'the day before' a specific event, while 昨日(きのう) simply means 'yesterday.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の前日(ぜんじつ) (the day before ~)
  • 前日(ぜんじつ)まで (until the day before)
  • 前日(ぜんじつ)に (on the day before)

RELATED TIME EXPRESSIONS:

  • 翌日(よくじつ) (the following day)
  • 当日(とうじつ) (the day itself, that day)
  • 前夜(ぜんや) (the previous night/evening)

Related Words

Antonym: 翌日(よくじつ)
Related: 当日(とうじつ)
