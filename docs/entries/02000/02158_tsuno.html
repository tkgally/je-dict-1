角(つの)

つの
noun

Cows have horns.

Goat horns.

角(つの) refers to horns or antlers on animals.

KANJI READINGS:

The kanji 角(かく) has multiple readings with different meanings:

  • 角(つの): horn, antler (animal body part)
  • 角(かど): corner (of street, building)
  • 角(すみ): corner (of room, inside space)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 角(つの)が生(は)える: for horns to grow
  • 角(つの)が落(お)ちる: for antlers to shed
  • 一角(いっかく): one horn; a corner (of a place)

IDIOMS:

  • 角(つの)を出(だ)す: to get angry (lit. "to show one's horns")
  • 角(つの)を折(お)る: to yield, give up stubbornness
  • 角(つの)を隠(かく)して牛(うし)を殺(ころ)す: to cause more harm while trying to hide a small flaw
