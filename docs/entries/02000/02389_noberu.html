1. to state, to express
To put thoughts or opinions into words.
意見(いけん)を述(の)べる。
To express an opinion.
理由(りゆう)を述(の)べる。
To state the reason.
会議(かいぎ)で自分(じぶん)の考(かんが)えをはっきり述(の)べた。
I clearly stated my thoughts at the meeting.
2. to mention, to describe
To speak about something in detail.
経緯(けいい)を述(の)べる。
To describe the circumstances.
論文(ろんぶん)の中(なか)で詳(くわ)しく述(の)べられている。
It is described in detail in the paper.
事故(じこ)の状況(じょうきょう)を警察(けいさつ)に述(の)べた。
I described the circumstances of the accident to the police.

述(の)べる is an ichidan verb meaning to state, express, or describe formally. Used in speeches, writing, and formal contexts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を particle.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意見(いけん)を述(の)べる: to state an opinion
  • 感想(かんそう)を述(の)べる: to express impressions
  • 結論(けつろん)を述(の)べる: to state a conclusion
  • 理由(りゆう)を述(の)べる: to state the reason
  • 考(かんが)えを述(の)べる: to express one's thoughts

REGISTER:
More formal than 言(い)う (to say). Common in academic writing, speeches, and official statements.

RELATED:

  • 陳述(ちんじゅつ): statement (noun form)
