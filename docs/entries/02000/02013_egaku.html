描(えが)く

えがく
verb (godan)
to draw, to depict, to describe
1. to draw, to paint
To create pictures or images.
絵(え)を描(えが)く。
To draw a picture.
2. to depict, to describe
To represent or portray in words.
小説(しょうせつ)は戦争(せんそう)を描(えが)いている。
The novel depicts war.
3. to imagine, to picture
To form a mental image.
未来(みらい)を描(えが)く。
To envision the future.

描(えが)く is a godan verb with three related meanings: to draw/paint, to depict/portray, and to imagine/envision.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を描(えが)く

SENSE 1 - TO DRAW/PAINT:

  • 絵(え)を描(えが)く: to draw a picture
  • 円(えん)を描(えが)く: to draw a circle
  • 肖像画(しょうぞうが)を描(えが)く: to paint a portrait

SENSE 2 - TO DEPICT/PORTRAY:

  • 小説(しょうせつ)が戦争(せんそう)を描(えが)く: the novel depicts war
  • 人間(にんげん)の心(こころ)を描(えが)く: to portray the human heart

SENSE 3 - TO IMAGINE/ENVISION:

  • 未来(みらい)を描(えが)く: to envision the future
  • 夢(ゆめ)を描(えが)く: to imagine a dream

READING NOTES:

  • 描(えが)く (egaku): more literary/abstract
  • 描(か)く (kaku): same kanji, simpler/concrete
  • 書(か)く (kaku): different kanji = to write
