描(えが)く

えがく
verb (godan)
to draw, to depict, to describe
1. to draw, to paint
To create pictures or images.
2. to depict, to describe
To represent or portray in words.
3. to imagine, to picture
To form a mental image.
絵(え)を描(えが)く。
To draw a picture.
小説(しょうせつ)は戦争(せんそう)を描(えが)いている。
The novel depicts war.
未来(みらい)を描(えが)く。
To envision the future.

描(えが)く can mean draw, depict, or imagine.

COMMON USES:

  • 絵(え)を描(えが)く: to draw a picture
  • 夢(ゆめ)を描(えが)く: to imagine a dream
  • 円(えん)を描(えが)く: to draw a circle

RELATED:

  • 描(か)く: to draw (alternative reading)
  • 書(か)く: to write
