次(つ)ぐ

つぐ
verb (godan)
to succeed, to come next
1. to succeed, to follow
To come after in sequence or rank.
一位(いちい)に次(つ)ぐ。
To rank second.
東京(とうきょう)に次(つ)ぐ大(おお)きな都市(とし)。
The second largest city after Tokyo.
Sense 1: follow
災害(さいがい)が相(あい)次(つ)いだ。
Disasters occurred one after another.
Sense 1: succeed
成功(せいこう)に次(つ)ぐ成功(せいこう)。
Success after success.
Sense 1: follow
2. to inherit, to take over
To receive from a predecessor.
父(ちち)の会社(かいしゃ)を継(つ)ぐ。
To take over father's company.
Also written as 継ぐ
家業(かぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ。
To take over the family business.
Sense 2: inherit
王位(おうい)を継(つ)ぐ。
To succeed to the throne.
Sense 2: take over
伝統(でんとう)を継(つ)ぐ。
To carry on the tradition.
Sense 2: inherit
血筋(ちすじ)を継(つ)ぐ。
To inherit the bloodline.
Sense 2: take over
先代(せんだい)の技(わざ)を継(つ)ぐ。
To inherit the skills of one's predecessor.
Sense 2: inherit

つぐ has different meanings depending on the kanji used.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 次(つ)ぐ: to come next, to follow in rank
  • 継(つ)ぐ: to inherit, to succeed (position/business)

COMMON PATTERNS WITH 次(つ)ぐ:

  • Xに次(つ)ぐ: second to X, after X
  • 相(あい)次(つ)ぐ: to happen one after another

COMMON PATTERNS WITH 継(つ)ぐ:

  • 家業(かぎょう)を継(つ)ぐ: to take over family business
  • 伝統(でんとう)を継(つ)ぐ: to carry on tradition
  • 後(あと)を継(つ)ぐ: to succeed someone

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive with 次(つ)ぐ; transitive with 継(つ)ぐ.
