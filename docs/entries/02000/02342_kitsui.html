きつい

きつい
i-adjective
tight, harsh, intense
1. tight, close-fitting
Too snug or constricting.
この靴(くつ)はきつい。
These shoes are too tight.
2. harsh, severe
Strict or demanding.
きつい言(い)い方(かた)
A harsh way of speaking
3. intense, hard
Difficult to bear.
きつい仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a tough job.

きつい is an i-adjective with a wide range of meanings from physical tightness to emotional harshness.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: きつく
  • Noun form: きつさ

COMMON USES:

  • 服(ふく)がきつい: clothes are tight
  • 坂(さか)がきつい: the slope is steep/demanding
  • 性格(せいかく)がきつい: personality is harsh
  • 仕事(しごと)がきつい: work is tough/demanding
  • きつい言(い)い方(かた): harsh way of speaking
  • 匂(にお)いがきつい: smell is strong/overpowering

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きつい vs. 厳(きび)しい: きつい emphasizes difficulty or discomfort; 厳(きび)しい emphasizes strictness or severity
