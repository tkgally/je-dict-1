野党(やとう)

やとう
noun
opposition party
1. opposition party
Political parties not in power.
野党が反対(はんたい)した。
The opposition party opposed.

野党(やとう) means opposition party - political parties not currently in power.

WORD FORMATION:
野(や) (field/outside) + 党(とう) (party) - literally "parties in the field" (outside of power)

KEY POLITICAL TERMS:

  • 与党(よとう): ruling party (in power)
  • 野党(やとう): opposition party (not in power)
  • 政党(せいとう): political party (general term)
  • 連立(れんりつ): coalition

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 野党(やとう)が反対(はんたい)する: the opposition opposes
  • 野党(やとう)側(がわ): the opposition side
  • 野党(やとう)議員(ぎいん): opposition party member

NOTE:
In Japanese politics, the ruling party (与党(よとう)) has historically been the LDP (自民党(じみんとう)), with various opposition parties.
