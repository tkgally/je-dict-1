減(へ)らす

へらす
godan verb
to decrease, to reduce
1. to decrease, to reduce
To make less in number or amount.
体重(たいじゅう)を減らす。
To reduce weight.
無駄(むだ)を減らす。
To reduce waste.

減(へ)らす means to reduce, decrease, or cut down on something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 減(へ)る (intransitive) - something decreases on its own
  • Pattern: [something]を減(へ)らす

OPPOSITE PAIR:

  • 減(へ)らす ↔ 増(ふ)やす (to increase - transitive)
  • 減(へ)る ↔ 増(ふ)える (to increase - intransitive)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 体重(たいじゅう)を減(へ)らす: to lose weight
  • 無駄(むだ)を減(へ)らす: to reduce waste
  • 費用(ひよう)を減(へ)らす: to cut costs
  • 時間(じかん)を減(へ)らす: to reduce time
  • ストレスを減(へ)らす: to reduce stress

IDIOM:
腹(はら)を減(へ)らす literally 'to reduce one's stomach' means 'to get hungry.'
