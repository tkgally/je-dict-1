燃(も)える

もえる
ichidan verb
to burn, to be on fire
1. to burn, to be on fire
To undergo combustion.
2. to be passionate
To feel strong enthusiasm or emotion (figurative).
火(ひ)が燃えている。
The fire is burning.
闘志(とうし)が燃える。
Fighting spirit burns (within me).

燃える is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 燃える (intransitive): to burn
  • 燃(も)やす (transitive): to burn something

RELATED:

  • 燃(も)えるゴミ: combustible garbage
  • 燃(ねん)料(りょう): fuel
general
