詰(つ)める

つめる
verb (ichidan)
to pack, to stuff, to shorten
1. to pack, to stuff
To fill tightly with something.
2. to shorten, to narrow
To reduce space or distance.
スーツケースに荷物(にもつ)を詰(つ)める。
To pack luggage in a suitcase.
距離(きょり)を詰(つ)める。
To close the gap.

詰(つ)める means to pack or shorten.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 詰(つ)める (transitive): to pack
  • 詰(つ)まる (intransitive): to be packed, clogged
general
