冷(ひ)やす

ひやす
godan verb
to cool, to refrigerate
1. to cool, to chill
To lower the temperature of something.
ビールを冷やす。
To chill beer.
2. to calm down
To reduce excitement or tension (figurative).
頭(あたま)を冷やす。
To cool one's head (calm down).

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • 冷(ひ)やす (transitive): to cool (something)
  • 冷(ひ)える (intransitive): to get cold (on its own)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 頭(あたま)を冷(ひ)やす: to cool one's head (calm down)
  • 肝(きも)を冷(ひ)やす: to be frightened, have a close call
  • ビールを冷(ひ)やす: to chill beer

RELATED WORDS:

  • 冷(つめ)たい: cold (to the touch)
  • 冷(さ)める: to cool down (intrans., temperature or feelings)
  • 冷(さ)ます: to let cool, to dampen (enthusiasm)

Note: 冷(ひ)やす focuses on actively cooling something, while 冷(さ)ます implies letting something cool naturally.
