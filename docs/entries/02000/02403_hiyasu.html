冷(ひ)やす

ひやす
godan verb
to cool, to refrigerate

Definitions

1. to cool, to chill
To lower the temperature of something.
2. to calm down
To reduce excitement or tension (figurative).

Examples

ビールを冷やす。
To chill beer.
頭(あたま)を冷やす。
To cool one's head (calm down).

Notes

冷やす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 冷やす (transitive): to cool
  • 冷(ひ)える (intransitive): to get cold

IDIOM:

  • 肝(きも)を冷やす: to be frightened, have a close call
