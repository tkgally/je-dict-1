1. to close, to shut
To close something like eyes, a book, or a door.
目(め)を閉(と)じる。
To close one's eyes.
本(ほん)を閉(と)じる。
To close a book.
口(くち)を閉(と)じて静(しず)かにする。
To shut one's mouth and be quiet.
疲(つか)れたので目(め)を閉(と)じて少(すこ)し休(やす)んだ。
I was tired, so I closed my eyes and rested a little.
読(よ)み終(お)わった本(ほん)を閉(と)じて、机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に置(お)いた。
I closed the book I had finished reading and placed it on the desk.

閉(と)じる means to close, typically used for eyes, books, and metaphorical closings.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Can be transitive or intransitive
  • Transitive: 目(め)を閉(と)じる (to close one's eyes)
  • Intransitive: 幕(まく)が閉(と)じる (the curtain closes)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 目(め)を閉(と)じる: to close one's eyes
  • 本(ほん)を閉(と)じる: to close a book
  • 口(くち)を閉(と)じる: to shut one's mouth
  • 幕(まく)を閉(と)じる: to bring to a close (figurative)
  • 生涯(しょうがい)を閉(と)じる: to end one's life

DISTINCTION (homophones):

  • 閉(と)じる: to close (eyes, books, figurative)
  • 綴(と)じる: to bind, staple (papers)

CONTRAST WITH 閉(し)める:
閉(と)じる is for things that open like a cover or eyelids; 閉(し)める is for doors, windows, and containers.

Related Words
