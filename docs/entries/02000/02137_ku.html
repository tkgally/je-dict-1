句(く)

く
noun
phrase, clause, haiku
1. phrase, clause
A grammatical unit or expression.
2. verse, haiku
A line of poetry, especially haiku.
俳句(はいく)を詠(よ)む。
To compose haiku.
この句(く)の意味(いみ)が分(わ)からない。
I don't understand the meaning of this phrase.

句(く) refers to phrases or verses.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 俳句(はいく): haiku
  • 文句(もんく): complaint, phrase
  • 語句(ごく): words and phrases
  • 句読点(くとうてん): punctuation marks
