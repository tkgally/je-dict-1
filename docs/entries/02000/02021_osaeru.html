1. to press down, to hold down
To apply pressure to keep something in place.
傷口(きずぐち)を押(お)さえる。
To press on the wound.
帽子(ぼうし)を手(て)で押(お)さえた。
I held my hat down with my hand.
風(かぜ)で飛(と)びそうな紙(かみ)を押(お)さえてください。
Please hold down the paper that's about to blow away in the wind.
2. to restrain, to suppress
To keep under control.
感情(かんじょう)を押(お)さえる。
To suppress one's emotions.
怒(いか)りを押(お)さえる。
To suppress one's anger.
彼女(かのじょ)は涙(なみだ)を押(お)さえることができなかった。
She couldn't hold back her tears.
3. to secure, to reserve
To make sure of getting something.
席(せき)を押(お)さえておいた。
I reserved the seat.
ホテルの部屋(へや)を押(お)さえた。
I reserved a hotel room.
会議(かいぎ)の前(まえ)に要点(ようてん)を押(お)さえておこう。
Let's grasp the key points before the meeting.

押(お)さえる is a transitive ichidan verb with several meanings related to holding, restraining, or securing.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を押(お)さえる

THREE MAIN MEANINGS:
1. Physical: to press down, hold in place
2. Emotional: to suppress, restrain
3. Practical: to secure, reserve

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 傷口(きずぐち)を押(お)さえる: to press on a wound
  • 感情(かんじょう)を押(お)さえる: to suppress emotions
  • 席(せき)を押(お)さえる: to reserve a seat
  • ポイントを押(お)さえる: to grasp the key points

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 押(お)さえる: physical pressing, securing
  • 抑(おさ)える: emotional restraint (same reading, different nuance)

RELATED:

  • 押(お)す: to push (godan verb)
