結(むす)ぶ

むすぶ
godan verb
to tie, to bind, to form
1. to tie, to bind
To fasten or secure with a knot.
靴(くつ)ひもを結ぶ。
To tie shoelaces.
2. to form, to conclude
To establish a connection or reach an agreement.
契約(けいやく)を結ぶ。
To conclude a contract.

結(むす)ぶ is a transitive godan verb meaning "to tie" or "to form/conclude."

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 結(むす)ばれる (passive/intransitive - to be tied, connected)

CONCRETE USES:

  • 靴(くつ)ひもを結(むす)ぶ: to tie shoelaces
  • 髪(かみ)を結(むす)ぶ: to tie one's hair
  • リボンを結(むす)ぶ: to tie a ribbon

ABSTRACT USES:

  • 契約(けいやく)を結(むす)ぶ: to conclude a contract
  • 友情(ゆうじょう)を結(むす)ぶ: to form a friendship
  • 条約(じょうやく)を結(むす)ぶ: to sign a treaty

IDIOM:

  • 実(み)を結(むす)ぶ: to bear fruit (efforts come to fruition)
