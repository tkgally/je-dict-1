結(むす)ぶ

むすぶ
godan verb
to tie, to bind, to form

Definitions

1. to tie, to bind
To fasten or secure with a knot.
2. to form, to conclude
To establish a connection or reach an agreement.

Examples

靴(くつ)ひもを結ぶ。
To tie shoelaces.
契約(けいやく)を結ぶ。
To conclude a contract.

Notes

結ぶ is a transitive verb with both concrete and abstract uses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実(み)を結ぶ: to bear fruit (literal or figurative)
  • 友情(ゆうじょう)を結ぶ: to form a friendship
  • 髪(かみ)を結ぶ: to tie one's hair
