1. to ring, to sound
To cause something to make a noise.
ベルを鳴(な)らす。
To ring the bell.
クラクションを鳴(な)らす。
To honk the horn.
目覚(めざ)まし時計(どけい)を鳴(な)らしても、なかなか起(お)きられない。
Even when I set the alarm clock, I can't easily wake up.

鳴(な)らす means to cause something to produce sound — to ring, sound, or play.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 鳴(な)る (intransitive — the thing itself sounds)

ASPECT (ている):
鳴(な)らしている means 'is ringing/sounding (something)' — an ongoing action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ベルを鳴(な)らす: to ring a bell
  • 警報(けいほう)を鳴(な)らす: to sound an alarm
  • クラクションを鳴(な)らす: to honk a horn
  • 指(ゆび)を鳴(な)らす: to snap one's fingers
  • 太鼓(たいこ)を鳴(な)らす: to beat a drum

IDIOM:
名(な)を鳴(な)らす means to make a name for oneself, to become famous.
