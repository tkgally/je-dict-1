鳴(な)らす

ならす
godan verb
to ring, to sound
1. to ring, to sound
To cause something to make a noise.
ベルを鳴らす。
To ring the bell.
クラクションを鳴らす。
To honk the horn.

鳴らす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 鳴らす (transitive): to ring, sound
  • 鳴(な)る (intransitive): to ring, sound

COMMON OBJECTS:

  • ベルを鳴らす: to ring a bell
  • 警報(けいほう)を鳴らす: to sound an alarm
  • 名(な)を鳴らす: to make a name for oneself (idiomatic)
general
