刺(さ)す

さす
verb (godan)
to stab, to sting, to pierce
1. to stab, to pierce
To push a sharp object into something.
ナイフで刺(さ)す。
To stab with a knife.
2. to sting, to bite
For an insect to pierce with its stinger or mouth.
蚊(か)に刺(さ)された。
I was bitten by a mosquito.

刺(さ)す means to stab or sting.

KANJI VARIANTS (all さす):

  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, to sting
  • 指(さ)す: to point
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella)
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 刺(さ)す (transitive): to stab
  • 刺(さ)さる (intransitive): to be stuck
