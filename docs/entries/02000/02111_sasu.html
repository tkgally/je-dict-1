1. to stab, to pierce
To push a sharp object into something.
ナイフで刺(さ)す。
To stab with a knife.
串(くし)に肉(にく)を刺(さ)して焼(や)いた。
I skewered the meat and grilled it.
魚(さかな)を包丁(ほうちょう)で刺(さ)して、一気(いっき)にさばいた。
I pierced the fish with a kitchen knife and prepared it in one go.
2. to sting, to bite
For an insect to pierce with its stinger or mouth.
蚊(か)に刺(さ)された。
I was bitten by a mosquito.
夏(なつ)になると蚊(か)に刺(さ)されやすい。
In summer, I tend to get bitten by mosquitoes.
庭(にわ)で遊(あそ)んでいたら蜂(はち)に刺(さ)されて、腕(うで)がひどく腫(は)れた。
While playing in the garden, I was stung by a bee and my arm swelled up badly.

刺(さ)す is a godan verb meaning to stab, pierce, or sting.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 刺(さ)さる (intransitive, to be stuck in)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 蚊(か)に刺(さ)される: to be bitten by mosquito
  • 虫(むし)に刺(さ)される: to be stung by insect
  • ナイフで刺(さ)す: to stab with a knife

HOMOPHONES (all さす):

  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, sting
  • 指(さ)す: to point at
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella), to shine
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert, plug in
  • 注(さ)す: to pour (drinks)
