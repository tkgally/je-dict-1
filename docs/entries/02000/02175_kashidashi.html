貸出(かしだし)

かしだし
noun / suru-verb
lending, loan

Definitions

1. lending, loaning out
The act of lending something to someone, typically books from a library or items for temporary use.

Examples

図書館(としょかん)の貸出(かしだし)期間(きかん)は2週間(しゅうかん)です。
The library lending period is two weeks.
この本(ほん)は貸出(かしだし)中(ちゅう)です。
This book is currently on loan.
貸出(かしだし)カードを作(つく)りたいのですが。
I'd like to make a library card.
Literally 'lending card'

Notes

貸出(かしだし) is a compound noun from 貸(か)す (to lend) + 出(だ)す (to put out).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 貸出(かしだし)中(ちゅう) (currently on loan)
  • 貸出(かしだし)期間(きかん) (lending period)
  • 貸出(かしだし)カード (library card)
  • 貸出(かしだし)冊数(さっすう) (number of books that can be borrowed)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Most commonly used in library contexts
  • Also used for equipment rental in offices

RELATED WORDS:

  • 返却(へんきゃく) (return) - antonym
  • 貸(か)す (to lend) - base verb
  • 借(か)りる (to borrow) - opposite action

Related Words

Antonym: 返却(へんきゃく) (return)
Related: 貸(か)す (to lend)
