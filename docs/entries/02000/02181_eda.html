枝(えだ)

えだ
noun
branch, bough, twig

Definitions

1. branch, bough
A woody extension growing from the trunk of a tree.
2. twig
A small thin branch.

Examples

枝(えだ)を切(き)る。
To cut branches.
鳥(とり)が枝(えだ)に止(と)まっている。
A bird is perched on a branch.

Notes

枝(えだ) refers to tree branches.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 枝豆(えだまめ): edamame (soybeans)
  • 小枝(こえだ): twig
  • 枝葉(えだは): branches and leaves; minor details

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 枝(えだ)を張(は)る: to spread branches
  • 枝葉末節(しようまっせつ): trivial details
