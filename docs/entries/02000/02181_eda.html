枝(えだ)

えだ
noun
branch, bough, twig
1. branch, bough
A woody extension growing from the trunk of a tree.
枝(えだ)を切(き)る。
To cut branches.
鳥(とり)が枝(えだ)に止(と)まっている。
A bird is perched on a branch.
2. twig
A small thin branch.

枝(えだ) means branch, bough, or twig - woody extensions growing from a tree trunk.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 枝豆(えだまめ): edamame (soybeans on the branch)
  • 小枝(こえだ): twig, small branch
  • 枝葉(えだは): branches and leaves; minor details
  • 枝分(えだわ)かれ: branching, diverging

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 枝(えだ)を切(き)る: to prune branches
  • 枝(えだ)を張(は)る: to spread branches
  • 枝(えだ)に止(と)まる: to perch on a branch

IDIOM:
枝葉末節(しようまっせつ): trivial details (lit. branches, leaves, and twigs)

RELATED:

  • 幹(みき): trunk
  • 根(ね): root
