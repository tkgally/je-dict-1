神(かみ)

かみ
noun
god, deity, spirit
1. god, deity
A divine being worshipped in religion.
神社(じんじゃ)で神(かみ)に祈(いの)った。
I prayed to the gods at the shrine.
2. spirit, kami
A sacred spirit in Shinto belief.
日本(にほん)には八百万(やおよろず)の神(かみ)がいる。
In Japan, there are eight million gods.

神(かみ) refers to gods or spirits.

SHINTO:
In Shinto, 神(かみ) are spirits inhabiting nature, ancestors, and sacred places.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 神社(じんじゃ): Shinto shrine
  • 神様(かみさま): god (honorific)
  • 神話(しんわ): mythology
  • 神道(しんとう): Shinto
general
