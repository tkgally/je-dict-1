1. quiet, gentle
Being calm and not causing trouble.
おとなしい子(こ)だ。
He/She is a quiet child.
彼女(かのじょ)はおとなしい性格(せいかく)だ。
She has a gentle personality.
今日(きょう)はおとなしくしている。
I'll stay quiet today.
普段(ふだん)はおとなしい人(ひと)だ。
He's usually a quiet person.
おとなしい色(いろ)の服(ふく)。
Clothes in subdued colors.

大人(おとな)しい means quiet, gentle, or well-behaved.

KANJI NOTE:
Despite the kanji 大人(おとな) (adult), this word means 'gentle/quiet', not 'adult-like'. Often written in hiragana.

RANGE OF USE:

  • People and animals: not noisy, not troublesome, doing as told. Of children and pets this is straightforward praise; of an adult it describes a reserved, unassertive temperament.
  • Colors, patterns, and clothes: subdued, understated. おとなしい色(いろ) is a muted color, not a silent one.
  • Actions, via the adverbial おとなしく: doing something without resisting or making a fuss, which is why it turns up both in instructions to children and in descriptions of giving in.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 静(しず)か: quiet in the sense of little sound. Describes places and moments; おとなしい describes character and conduct, so a noisy room can be full of おとなしい people.
  • 穏(おだ)やか: calm, mild. Praises an even temper with none of the passivity おとなしい can suggest.
  • 従順(じゅうじゅん): obedient. Formal, and explicitly about submitting to authority.

PATTERNS:

  • おとなしくする: to behave, be quiet
  • おとなしい性格(せいかく): gentle personality
  • おとなしい色(いろ): subdued color

NUANCE:
Generally positive, but of an adult it can hint at passivity or at not speaking up. In a job reference or a self-description it is rarely read as a compliment; 穏(おだ)やか is the safer choice for a calm temperament.

Related Words

Synonym: 静(しず)か (quiet (な-adjective))
Related: 穏(おだ)やか (calm, mild (temperament))
