溶(と)ける

とける
ichidan verb, intransitive
to melt, to dissolve
1. to melt
To change from solid to liquid by heat.
2. to dissolve
To become absorbed into a liquid.
雪(ゆき)が溶(と)けた。
The snow melted.
砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に溶(と)ける。
Sugar dissolves in water.

溶(と)ける is intransitive.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 溶(と)かす: to melt/dissolve (something)

DISTINCTION:

  • 溶(と)ける: to melt/dissolve
  • 解(と)ける: to come undone
general
