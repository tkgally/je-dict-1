溶(と)ける

とける
ichidan verb, intransitive
to melt, to dissolve
1. to melt
To change from solid to liquid by heat.
雪(ゆき)が溶(と)けた。
The snow melted.
2. to dissolve
To become absorbed into a liquid.
砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に溶(と)ける。
Sugar dissolves in water.

溶(と)ける describes something changing from solid to liquid, or dissolving into a liquid.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 溶(と)かす (transitive, to melt/dissolve something)

ASPECT (ている):
溶(と)けている indicates the state of being melted or dissolved: 氷(こおり)が溶(と)けている (the ice is melted).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 雪(ゆき)が溶(と)ける: snow melts
  • 氷(こおり)が溶(と)ける: ice melts
  • 砂糖(さとう)が溶(と)ける: sugar dissolves
  • 心(こころ)が溶(と)ける: heart melts (figurative)

DISTINCTION:

  • 溶(と)ける: to melt or dissolve
  • 解(と)ける: to come undone, loosen, be solved
