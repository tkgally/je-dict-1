横切(よこぎ)る

よこぎる
godan verb
to cross, to traverse
1. to cross, to traverse
To go across something from side to side.
道(みち)を横切(よこぎ)る。
To cross the road.
猫(ねこ)が庭(にわ)を横切(よこぎ)った。
A cat crossed the yard.
子供(こども)が道(みち)を横切(よこぎ)った。
A child crossed the road.
車(くるま)が目(め)の前(まえ)を横切(よこぎ)った。
A car cut across right in front of me.
公園(こうえん)を横切(よこぎ)って駅(えき)に向(む)かった。
I cut across the park and headed for the station.

横切(よこぎ)る means to cross or traverse something horizontally, going from one side to the other.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: Xを横切(よこぎ)る

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)を横切(よこぎ)る: to cross the road
  • 庭(にわ)を横切(よこぎ)る: to cross the yard
  • 広場(ひろば)を横切(よこぎ)る: to cross the square

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 横切(よこぎ)る: to cross (horizontally, from side to side)
  • 渡(わた)る: to cross (bridge, river, road)
  • 越(こ)える: to cross over, go beyond (mountains, borders)
  • 通(とお)る: to pass through

ETYMOLOGY:
横(よこ) (side/horizontal) + 切(き)る (to cut) - to 'cut across' something.
