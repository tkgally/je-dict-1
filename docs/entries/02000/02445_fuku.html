吹(ふ)く

ふく
godan verb
to blow

Definitions

1. to blow
For wind to move or to expel air.

Examples

風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
The wind is blowing.

Notes

吹(ふ)く is used for wind and breath.

USES:

  • 風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)く: wind blows
  • 笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く: to play flute
  • 息(いき)を吹(ふ)く: to breathe out
