1. to blow
For wind to move or to expel air.
風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている。
The wind is blowing.
笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く。
To play the flute.
強(つよ)い風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いて帽子(ぼうし)が飛(と)んだ。
A strong wind blew and my hat flew off.

吹(ふ)く describes blowing air or wind movement. It can be intransitive (wind blows) or transitive (to blow something).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive: 風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)く (wind blows)
  • Transitive: 笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く (to play a flute)

ASPECT (ている):
風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いている means the wind is currently blowing (ongoing action).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)く: wind blows
  • 笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く: to play the flute
  • ラッパを吹(ふ)く: to play the trumpet
  • 息(いき)を吹(ふ)きかける: to blow breath on
  • シャボン玉(だま)を吹(ふ)く: to blow bubbles

NOTE: For wind instruments, 吹(ふ)く is the standard verb for "to play."
