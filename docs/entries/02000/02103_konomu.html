1. to like, to prefer
To have a liking for something.
彼(かれ)は甘(あま)いものを好(この)む。
He likes sweet things.
お好(この)みの色(いろ)はありますか。
Do you have a preferred color?
彼女(かのじょ)は静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)を好(この)む。
She prefers quiet places.
日本人(にほんじん)は新鮮(しんせん)な魚(さかな)を好(この)む人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
Many Japanese people prefer fresh fish.
彼(かれ)は好(この)んで難(むずか)しい仕事(しごと)を引(ひ)き受(う)ける人(ひと)だ。
He is someone who willingly takes on difficult work.
好(この)んで means 'willingly' or 'by preference.'

好(この)む is a godan verb meaning "to like" or "to prefer." It's slightly more formal than 好(す)き.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を particle

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を好(この)む: to like/prefer ~
  • 好(この)んで〜する: to willingly do ~, to like to do ~

WORD FAMILY:

  • 好(この)む: to like (verb)
  • 好(この)み: preference, taste (noun)
  • お好(この)み: one's preference (polite)
  • お好(この)み焼(や)き: savory pancake (lit. "as you like it")

CONTRAST WITH 好(す)き:

  • 好(この)む: to like (verb, slightly formal, written)
  • 好(す)き: to like (na-adjective, casual, spoken)

Example: 彼(かれ)は静(しず)かな場所(ばしょ)を好(この)む (He prefers quiet places)
