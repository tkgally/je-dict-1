済(す)ませる

すませる
verb (ichidan)
to finish, to get done, to settle

1. to finish, to complete
To bring something to completion.
仕事(しごと)を済(す)ませる。
To finish work.
食事(しょくじ)を済(す)ませてから行(い)く。
I'll go after finishing the meal.
買(か)い物(もの)を済(す)ませた。
I finished shopping.
用事(ようじ)を済(す)ませた。
I finished the errands.
出(で)かける前(まえ)に準備(じゅんび)を全部(ぜんぶ)済(す)ませた。
I finished all the preparations before going out.
会議(かいぎ)が始(はじ)まる前(まえ)に昼食(ちゅうしょく)を済(す)ませておきたい。
I want to finish lunch before the meeting starts.
2. to settle, to manage with
To deal with or settle a matter.
電話(でんわ)で済(す)ませる。
To settle it by phone.
お金(かね)で済(す)ませる。
To settle it with money.
謝(あやま)るだけで済(す)ませようとしている。
They're trying to settle it with just an apology.
今日(きょう)は忙(いそが)しいから、昼(ひる)ごはんはコンビニで済(す)ませた。
I was busy today, so I just grabbed something at a convenience store for lunch.
面倒(めんどう)なことはメールで済(す)ませないで、直接(ちょくせつ)会(あ)って話(はな)したほうがいい。
For troublesome matters, it's better to meet and talk in person rather than settling it by email.

済(す)ませる means to finish, complete, or settle something. It emphasizes getting something done or out of the way.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 済(す)む (intransitive - to be finished/done)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを済(す)ませる: to finish X
  • Xで済(す)ませる: to settle with X, to manage with just X
  • 先(さき)に済(す)ませる: to get done first
  • 早(はや)く済(す)ませる: to finish quickly

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を済(す)ませる: to finish work
  • 食事(しょくじ)を済(す)ませる: to finish a meal
  • 用事(ようじ)を済(す)ませる: to finish errands
  • 手続(てつづ)きを済(す)ませる: to complete procedures

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 終(お)える: to finish, complete (neutral)
  • 済(す)ませる: to get done, settle (emphasis on disposing of)
  • 片付(かたづ)ける: to tidy up, finish off
