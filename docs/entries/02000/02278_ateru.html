1. to address (a letter)
To write a recipient's name on a letter or package.
手紙(てがみ)を田中(たなか)さん宛(あ)てに送(おく)る。
To send a letter addressed to Mr. Tanaka.
荷物(にもつ)を山田(やまだ)さん宛(あ)てに送(おく)った。
I sent the package addressed to Ms. Yamada.
宛名(あてな)を書(か)いてから手紙(てがみ)を出(だ)す。
I write the addressee's name before sending the letter.
2. to direct at, to allocate
To assign something to a specific purpose or person.
時間(じかん)を勉強(べんきょう)に宛(あ)てる。
To allocate time for studying.
資金(しきん)を研究(けんきゅう)に宛(あ)てる。
To allocate funds to research.
休日(きゅうじつ)を旅行(りょこう)の準備(じゅんび)に宛(あ)てた。
I devoted my day off to preparing for the trip.

宛(あ)てる means to address (a letter) or to allocate/direct something toward a purpose.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を and に particles

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手紙(てがみ)を～宛(あ)てに送(おく)る: to send a letter addressed to ~
  • 時間(じかん)を～に宛(あ)てる: to allocate time to ~
  • 資金(しきん)を～に宛(あ)てる: to allocate funds to ~

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 宛先(あてさき): address, recipient (on mail)
  • 宛名(あてな): addressee's name
  • ～宛(あて): addressed to ~ (suffix)

NOTE:
Don't confuse with 当(あ)てる (to hit, to apply) or 充(あ)てる (to assign, to fill a role).
