谷(たに)

たに
noun
valley, ravine
1. valley
A low area between hills or mountains.
谷(たに)を下(くだ)る。
To go down into the valley.
谷(たに)に川(かわ)が流(なが)れている。
A river flows through the valley.
人生(じんせい)は山(やま)あり谷(たに)ありだ。
Life has its ups and downs.
2. ravine, gorge
A deep narrow valley.
深(ふか)い谷(たに)に落(お)ちた。
I fell into a deep ravine.
谷底(たにそこ)まで見(み)えないほど深(ふか)い。
It's so deep you can't see the bottom of the gorge.
橋(はし)が谷(たに)の上(うえ)にかかっている。
A bridge spans the ravine.

谷(たに) means valley—a low area of land between hills or mountains, often with a river.

READINGS:

  • 谷(たに): standard reading
  • 谷(や)/谷(やつ): in place names (渋谷(しぶや), 四谷(よつや))

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 谷(たに)を下(くだ)る: to go down into the valley
  • 谷(たに)を越(こ)える: to cross a valley
  • 谷(たに)に落(お)ちる: to fall into a ravine

COMPOUNDS:

  • 谷間(たにま): valley, ravine; cleavage
  • 谷底(たにそこ): bottom of a valley
  • 渓谷(けいこく): gorge, ravine
  • 峡谷(きょうこく): canyon

IDIOMATIC:

  • 山(やま)あり谷(たに)あり: ups and downs (in life)
  • 谷間(たにま)の時期(じき): a slump period

Related Words

Related: 盆地(ぼんち) (basin (broader, surrounded by mountains))
