認(みと)める

みとめる
ichidan verb
to recognize, to admit

Definitions

1. to recognize, to acknowledge
To accept as valid.
2. to admit, to confess
To accept as true.

Examples

才能(さいのう)を認める。
To recognize talent.

Notes

認める is a transitive verb.

COMMON USES:

  • 間違(まちが)いを認める: to admit a mistake
  • 正式(せいしき)に認める: to officially recognize
