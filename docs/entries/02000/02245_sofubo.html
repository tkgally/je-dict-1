祖父母(そふぼ)

そふぼ
noun
grandparents

Definitions

1. grandparents
One's grandfather and grandmother collectively. A compound of 祖父(そふ) (grandfather) and 祖母(そぼ) (grandmother).

Examples

祖父母(そふぼ)の家(いえ)に遊(あそ)びに行(い)った。
I went to visit my grandparents' house.
夏休(なつやす)みは祖父母(そふぼ)と過(す)ごした。
I spent the summer vacation with my grandparents.
祖父母(そふぼ)からお年玉(としだま)をもらった。
I received New Year's money from my grandparents.

Notes

FAMILY TERMS:

  • 祖父(そふ) (grandfather - formal)
  • 祖母(そぼ) (grandmother - formal)
  • 祖父母(そふぼ) (grandparents)
  • おじいさん (grandfather - common/polite)
  • おばあさん (grandmother - common/polite)

USAGE: 祖父母(そふぼ) is more formal and is commonly used when talking about grandparents in general or in written contexts. In casual speech, おじいちゃんとおばあちゃん is more common.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 父母(ふぼ) (parents - formal)
  • 両親(りょうしん) (parents)
