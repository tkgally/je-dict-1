～続(つづ)ける

つづける
auxiliary verb
to continue doing
1. to continue doing ~
Attached to the stem of a verb to indicate continued action.
毎日(まいにち)勉強(べんきょう)し続けている。
I continue studying every day.

PATTERN: Verb stem + 続ける

EXAMPLES:

  • 読(よ)み続ける: to continue reading
  • 待(ま)ち続ける: to keep waiting
  • 走(はし)り続ける: to keep running

CONTRAST:

  • ～続ける: continue doing (emphasizes duration)
  • ～ている: ongoing action (simple progressive)
