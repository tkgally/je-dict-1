土(つち)

つち
noun
soil, earth, ground
1. soil, earth
The upper layer of ground where plants grow.
土(つち)を掘(ほ)る。
To dig soil.
土(つち)に種(たね)を蒔(ま)く。
To plant seeds in the soil.
2. ground, dirt
The solid surface of the earth.

土(つち) refers to soil and earth.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 土地(とち): land, property
  • 土曜日(どようび): Saturday
  • 土産(みやげ): souvenir
  • 粘土(ねんど): clay

ELEMENT:
土(つち) is one of the five elements in East Asian philosophy.
