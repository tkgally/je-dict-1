付(つ)き合(あ)う

つきあう
godan verb, intransitive
to associate with, to date, to keep company
1. to associate with, to socialize
To have a relationship or dealings with someone.
2. to date, to go out with
To be in a romantic relationship.
3. to accompany, to keep company
To go along with someone.
彼女(かのじょ)と付(つ)き合(あ)っている。
I'm dating her.
買(か)い物(もの)に付(つ)き合(あ)う。
To accompany someone shopping.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)と上手(うま)く付(つ)き合(あ)う。
To get along well with neighbors.

付(つ)き合(あ)う covers various types of relationships.

COMMON USES:

  • 恋人(こいびと)と付(つ)き合(あ)う: to date someone
  • ～に付(つ)き合(あ)う: to accompany someone to ~
  • 長(なが)い付(つ)き合(あ)い: a long relationship
