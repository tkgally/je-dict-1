付(つ)き合(あ)う

つきあう
godan verb, intransitive
to associate with, to date, to keep company
1. to associate with, to socialize
To have a relationship or dealings with someone.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)と上手(うま)く付(つ)き合(あ)う。
To get along well with neighbors.
2. to date, to go out with
To be in a romantic relationship.
彼女(かのじょ)と付(つ)き合(あ)っている。
I'm dating her.
3. to accompany, to keep company
To go along with someone.
買(か)い物(もの)に付(つ)き合(あ)う。
To accompany someone shopping.

付(つ)き合(あ)う covers various types of relationships and social interactions.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

THREE MAIN MEANINGS:
1. To associate/socialize: 近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)と付(つ)き合(あ)う (to socialize with neighbors)
2. To date: 彼(かれ)/彼女(かのじょ)と付(つ)き合(あ)う (to date him/her)
3. To accompany: 買(か)い物(もの)に付(つ)き合(あ)う (to accompany shopping)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜と付(つ)き合(あ)う: to date/associate with ~
  • 〜に付(つ)き合(あ)う: to accompany someone to ~
  • 付(つ)き合(あ)い: relationship, association (noun form)
  • 長(なが)い付(つ)き合(あ)い: a long relationship

USEFUL EXPRESSIONS:

  • 付(つ)き合(あ)っている: are dating
  • 付(つ)き合(あ)い始(はじ)める: to start dating
  • 上手(うま)く付(つ)き合(あ)う: to get along well with
