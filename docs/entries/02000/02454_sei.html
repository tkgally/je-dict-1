～製(せい)

せい
suffix
made in, manufactured by
1. made in, manufactured by
Indicates the country of manufacture or the material something is made from.
これは日本(にほん)製の車(くるま)です。
This is a car made in Japan.

～製(せい) attaches to nouns indicating either the country/company of manufacture or the material used.

COUNTRY/MAKER:

  • 日本(にほん)製(せい): made in Japan
  • 外国(がいこく)製(せい): foreign-made
  • 国産(こくさん): domestically made (alternative)
  • 自社(じしゃ)製(せい): in-house made

MATERIAL:

  • 木(き)製(せい): made of wood
  • 金属(きんぞく)製(せい): made of metal
  • 革(かわ)製(せい): made of leather
  • プラスチック製(せい): made of plastic

OTHER:

  • 手(て)製(せい): handmade
  • 特注(とくちゅう)製(せい): custom-made

NOTE:
Often appears on product labels: 〇〇製(せい)。
