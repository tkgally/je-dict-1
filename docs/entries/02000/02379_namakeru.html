怠(なま)ける

なまける
ichidan verb
to be lazy, to neglect

Definitions

1. to be lazy, to slack off
To avoid work or effort.
2. to neglect, to be idle
To fail to do what one should.

Examples

仕事(しごと)を怠ける。
To slack off at work.
怠けてばかりいる。
To be always lazing around.

Notes

怠ける has negative connotations.

RELATED:

  • 怠(なま)け者(もの): lazy person
  • 怠惰(たいだ): laziness, indolence

SIMILAR:

  • さぼる: to skip, play hooky (colloquial)
general
