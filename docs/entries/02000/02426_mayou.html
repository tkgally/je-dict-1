迷(まよ)う

まよう
godan verb
to be lost, to hesitate

Definitions

1. to get lost, to lose one's way
To not know where one is or how to proceed.
2. to be puzzled, to hesitate
To be uncertain about a decision.

Examples

道(みち)に迷った。
I got lost.
どちらにしようか迷(まよ)っている。
I'm torn between the two.

Notes

迷う describes uncertainty.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)に迷う: to get lost
  • 〜か〜か迷う: to be torn between ~ and ~

RELATED:

  • 迷(まよ)い: hesitation, doubt
  • 迷(めい)惑(わく): trouble, nuisance
