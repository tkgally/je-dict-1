墨(すみ)

すみ
noun
ink, India ink, sumi
1. ink, India ink
Black ink made from soot, used in calligraphy and painting.
墨(すみ)を摺(す)る。
To grind ink (on an inkstone).
墨(すみ)で字(じ)を書(か)く。
To write characters with ink.

墨(すみ) is traditional East Asian ink.

CALLIGRAPHY:
Traditional ink is made by grinding an ink stick on an inkstone with water.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 墨汁(ぼくじゅう): liquid ink
  • 水墨画(すいぼくが): ink wash painting
  • 墨絵(すみえ): ink painting

RELATED:

  • 硯(すずり): inkstone
