席(せき)

せき
noun
seat, place
1. seat
A place to sit.
席(せき)を取(と)る。
To get a seat.
窓側(まどがわ)の席(せき)がいい。
I'd like a window seat.
席(せき)を譲(ゆず)ってください。
Please give up your seat.
Sense 1: seat
満席(まんせき)で座(すわ)れなかった。
It was fully booked, so I couldn't get a seat.
Sense 1: seat
この席(せき)は空(あ)いていますか。
Is this seat available?
Sense 1: seat
指定席(していせき)を予約(よやく)した。
I reserved an assigned seat.
Sense 1: seat
2. place, position
One's assigned spot or position.
席(せき)に戻(もど)ってください。
Please return to your place.
Sense 2: place/position
席(せき)を外(はず)しています。
They're away from their desk.
Sense 2: place/position
会議(かいぎ)の席(せき)で発表(はっぴょう)した。
I made a presentation at the meeting.
Sense 2: place/occasion
お酒(さけ)の席(せき)では気(き)をつけて。
Be careful at drinking occasions.
Sense 2: place/occasion

席(せき) means a seat, place, or occasion/gathering.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 座席(ざせき): seat
  • 空席(くうせき): vacant seat
  • 満席(まんせき): full, no seats available
  • 指定席(していせき): reserved seat
  • 自由席(じゆうせき): unreserved seat
  • 席順(せきじゅん): seating order

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 席(せき)を取(と)る: to get a seat
  • 席(せき)を譲(ゆず)る: to give up one's seat
  • 席(せき)を外(はず)す: to step away (from desk/seat)
  • この席(せき)は空(あ)いていますか: Is this seat free?

EXTENDED MEANING:
お酒(さけ)の席(せき) means a drinking occasion/party, not just a physical seat.
