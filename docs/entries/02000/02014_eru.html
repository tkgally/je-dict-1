得(え)る

える
verb (ichidan)
to get, to obtain, to gain
1. to get, to obtain
To acquire something.
許可(きょか)を得(え)た。
I obtained permission.
情報(じょうほう)を得(え)る。
To obtain information.
Sense 1: obtain
機会(きかい)を得(え)た。
I got an opportunity.
Sense 1: get
同意(どうい)を得(え)る。
To obtain consent.
Sense 1: obtain
資格(しかく)を得(え)る。
To obtain a qualification.
Sense 1: obtain
2. to gain, to earn
To receive as a result of effort.
知識(ちしき)を得(え)る。
To gain knowledge.
支持(しじ)を得(え)た。
I gained support.
Sense 2: gain
信頼(しんらい)を得(え)る。
To earn trust.
Sense 2: gain/earn
勝利(しょうり)を得(え)た。
I won victory.
Sense 2: gain
利益(りえき)を得(え)る。
To gain profit.
Sense 2: gain
3. can, to be able to (auxiliary)
Used after verb stems to indicate possibility.
あり得(え)ない。
It's impossible.
Lit: cannot exist
起(お)こり得(え)る事故(じこ)。
An accident that can happen.
Sense 3: can (possibility)
考(かんが)え得(え)る限(かぎ)り。
To the extent imaginable.
Sense 3: can/be able to
それはあり得(え)る。
That's possible.
Sense 3: can (possibility)
予想(よそう)し得(え)なかった。
I couldn't have predicted it.
Sense 3: be able to

得(え)る has multiple uses.

AS MAIN VERB:

  • 利益(りえき)を得(え)る: to gain profit
  • 信頼(しんらい)を得(え)る: to gain trust

AS AUXILIARY (〜得(え)る):

  • あり得(え)る: possible
  • あり得(え)ない: impossible
  • 起(お)こり得(え)る: can happen

ALTERNATIVE READING:

  • 得(う)る: classical/literary form
