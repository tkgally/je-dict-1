羨(うらや)ましい

うらやましい
i-adjective
envious, enviable
1. envious, jealous
Feeling desire for what others have.
彼(かれ)がうらやましい。
I'm envious of him.
2. enviable
Deserving envy.
うらやましい生活(せいかつ)だ。
It's an enviable lifestyle.

羨(うらや)ましい is an i-adjective expressing envy or admiration for what others have.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 羨(うらや)ましく
  • Noun form: 羨(うらや)ましさ

GRAMMAR PATTERN:

〜が羨(うらや)ましい: to be envious of ~

  • 彼(かれ)が羨(うらや)ましい: I'm envious of him
  • その生活(せいかつ)が羨(うらや)ましい: I envy that lifestyle

NUANCE:
羨(うらや)ましい expresses wanting what someone else has, but without hostility. It's often used as a mild compliment: いいなあ、羨(うらや)ましい！(Nice! I'm jealous!)

CONTRAST:

  • 羨(うらや)ましい: envy (wanting what others have)
  • 嫉妬(しっと): jealousy (stronger, can imply resentment)
  • 妬(ねた)ましい: envious with resentment

RELATED:

  • 羨(うらや)む: to envy (verb form)
