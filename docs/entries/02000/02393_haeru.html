映(は)える

はえる
ichidan verb
to look attractive, to shine
1. to look attractive, to stand out
To appear beautiful or striking.
2. to shine, to be reflected
To be illuminated or mirror light.
この服(ふく)はよく映える。
This outfit looks great.
インスタ映えする写真(しゃしん)
An Instagram-worthy photo
Modern usage

映える gained popularity with social media.

MODERN USAGE:

  • インスタ映え: Instagram-worthy, photogenic
  • 映えスポット: scenic photo spot

HOMOPHONE:

  • 生(は)える: to grow (different kanji)
