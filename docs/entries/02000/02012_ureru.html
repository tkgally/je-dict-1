売(う)れる

うれる
verb (ichidan)
to sell, to be sold

Definitions

1. to be sold
For something to find buyers.
2. to be popular, to be in demand
For something or someone to be well-known.

Examples

この本(ほん)はよく売(う)れている。
This book is selling well.
全部(ぜんぶ)売(う)れた。
Everything sold out.
彼(かれ)は売(う)れっ子(こ)の歌手(かしゅ)だ。
He's a popular singer.

Notes

売(う)れる is the intransitive form of 売(う)る.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 売(う)る (transitive): to sell
  • 売(う)れる (intransitive): to be sold

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 売(う)れっ子(こ): popular person
  • 売(う)れ行(ゆ)き: sales, demand
  • 売(う)れ残(のこ)り: unsold items
