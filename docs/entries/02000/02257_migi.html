右(みぎ)

みぎ
noun
right
1. right
The direction opposite to left.
右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がる。
To turn right.
右手(みぎて)で書(か)く。
To write with the right hand.

右(みぎ) indicates the right direction, opposite of 左(ひだり).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がる: to turn right
  • 右(みぎ)を見(み)る: to look right
  • 右(みぎ)から左(ひだり)へ: from right to left (also: in one ear and out the other)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 右手(みぎて): right hand
  • 右足(みぎあし): right foot/leg
  • 右側(みぎがわ): right side
  • 右折(うせつ): right turn (traffic term)
  • 右利(みぎき)き: right-handed

POLITICAL USAGE:
右(みぎ)/右翼(うよく) can mean "right-wing" in political contexts.

OPPOSITE:
左(ひだり): left
