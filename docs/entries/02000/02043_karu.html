1. to cut (hair, grass)
To shorten by cutting.
髪(かみ)を刈(か)る。
To cut one's hair.
草(くさ)を刈(か)る。
To cut grass.
息子(むすこ)の髪(かみ)を短(みじか)く刈(か)った。
I cut my son's hair short.
2. to mow, to harvest
To cut crops or grass from a field.
芝生(しばふ)を刈(か)る。
To mow the lawn.
稲(いね)を刈(か)る。
To harvest rice.
秋(あき)になると農家(のうか)は稲(いね)を刈(か)る。
When autumn comes, farmers harvest rice.

刈(か)る is a godan verb meaning to cut short (hair, grass) or to harvest (crops). It implies cutting at the base or close to the surface.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を刈(か)る

SENSE 1 - TO CUT (hair/grass):

  • 髪(かみ)を刈(か)る: to cut hair (short/buzz cut)
  • 芝(しば)/芝生(しばふ)を刈(か)る: to mow the lawn
  • 草(くさ)を刈(か)る: to cut grass

SENSE 2 - TO HARVEST:

  • 稲(いね)を刈(か)る: to harvest rice
  • 麦(むぎ)を刈(か)る: to harvest wheat

HOMOPHONES (different kanji):

  • 刈(か)る: to cut, mow, harvest
  • 借(か)りる: to borrow (ichidan verb)
  • 狩(か)る: to hunt
  • 駆(か)る: to drive, impel

RELATED:

  • 刈(か)り込(こ)む: to trim, prune
  • 刈(か)り取(と)る: to mow, reap
