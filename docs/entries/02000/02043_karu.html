刈(か)る

かる
verb (godan)
to cut, to mow, to harvest
1. to cut (hair, grass)
To shorten by cutting.
髪(かみ)を刈(か)る。
To cut one's hair.
2. to mow, to harvest
To cut crops or grass from a field.
芝生(しばふ)を刈(か)る。
To mow the lawn.
稲(いね)を刈(か)る。
To harvest rice.

刈(か)る means to cut (hair, grass, crops).

COMMON USES:

  • 髪(かみ)を刈(か)る: to cut hair (short)
  • 芝(しば)を刈(か)る: to mow grass
  • 稲(いね)を刈(か)る: to harvest rice

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 刈(か)る: to cut, mow
  • 借(か)りる: to borrow
  • 狩(か)る: to hunt
