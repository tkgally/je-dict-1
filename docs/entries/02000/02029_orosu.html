下(お)ろす

おろす
verb (godan)
to lower, to take down, to withdraw
1. to lower, to take down
To move something from a higher to a lower position.
荷物(にもつ)を下(お)ろす。
To unload luggage.
本(ほん)を棚(たな)から下(お)ろした。
I took the book down from the shelf.
Sense 1: take down
旗(はた)を下(お)ろす。
To lower the flag.
Sense 1: lower
腰(こし)を下(お)ろす。
To sit down (lower one's body).
Sense 1: lower
ブラインドを下(お)ろす。
To lower the blinds.
Sense 1: lower
荷物(にもつ)をトラックから下(お)ろす。
To unload luggage from the truck.
Sense 1: take down/unload
窓(まど)を下(お)ろす。
To lower the window.
Sense 1: lower
2. to drop off (a passenger)
To let someone out of a vehicle.
駅(えき)で下(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off at the station.
乗客(じょうきゃく)を下(お)ろす。
To drop off passengers.
Sense 2: drop off
ここで下(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off here.
Sense 2: drop off
子供(こども)を学校(がっこう)で下(お)ろした。
I dropped the child off at school.
Sense 2: drop off
3. to withdraw (money)
To take money from an account.
銀行(ぎんこう)でお金(かね)を下(お)ろした。
I withdrew money at the bank.
ATMでお金(かね)を下(お)ろす。
To withdraw money from an ATM.
Sense 3: withdraw
貯金(ちょきん)を下(お)ろした。
I withdrew from my savings.
Sense 3: withdraw
必要(ひつよう)な分(ぶん)だけ下(お)ろす。
To withdraw only what's needed.
Sense 3: withdraw

下(お)ろす has multiple uses.

COMMON USES:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を下(お)ろす: to unload luggage
  • お金(かね)を下(お)ろす: to withdraw money
  • 客(きゃく)を下(お)ろす: to drop off a passenger

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 下(お)ろす (transitive): to lower
  • 下(お)りる (intransitive): to descend

OTHER MEANINGS:

  • 卸(おろ)す: to sell wholesale (different kanji)
