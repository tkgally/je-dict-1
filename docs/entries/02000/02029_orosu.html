1. to lower, to take down
To move something from a higher to a lower position.
荷物(にもつ)を下(お)ろす。
To unload luggage.
本(ほん)を棚(たな)から下(お)ろした。
I took the book down from the shelf.
旗(はた)を下(お)ろす。
To lower the flag.
腰(こし)を下(お)ろす。
To sit down (lower one's body).
ブラインドを下(お)ろす。
To lower the blinds.
荷物(にもつ)をトラックから下(お)ろす。
To unload luggage from the truck.
窓(まど)を下(お)ろす。
To lower the window.
2. to drop off (a passenger)
To let someone out of a vehicle.
駅(えき)で下(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off at the station.
乗客(じょうきゃく)を下(お)ろす。
To drop off passengers.
ここで下(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off here.
子供(こども)を学校(がっこう)で下(お)ろした。
I dropped the child off at school.
タクシーの運転手(うんてんしゅ)に病院(びょういん)の前(まえ)で下(お)ろしてもらった。
I had the taxi driver drop me off in front of the hospital.
3. to withdraw (money)
To take money from an account.
銀行(ぎんこう)でお金(かね)を下(お)ろした。
I withdrew money at the bank.
ATMでお金(かね)を下(お)ろす。
To withdraw money from an ATM.
貯金(ちょきん)を下(お)ろした。
I withdrew from my savings.
必要(ひつよう)な分(ぶん)だけ下(お)ろす。
To withdraw only what's needed.
旅行(りょこう)のためにATMで五万円(ごまんえん)下(お)ろしておいた。
I withdrew fifty thousand yen from the ATM in advance for the trip.

下(お)ろす is a versatile godan verb with several related meanings, all involving the idea of moving something downward or removing it from a higher position.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 下(お)りる (intransitive — to descend, get off)

KEY MEANINGS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を下(お)ろす: to unload luggage
  • お金(かね)を下(お)ろす: to withdraw money (from bank/ATM)
  • 客(きゃく)を下(お)ろす: to drop off a passenger
  • 大根(だいこん)を下(お)ろす: to grate (daikon)

HOMOPHONES (different kanji):

  • 卸(おろ)す: to sell wholesale
  • 降(お)ろす: to let someone off (a vehicle)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 肩(かた)の荷(に)を下(お)ろす: to take a load off one's shoulders (figurative relief)
