煙突(えんとつ) refers to a chimney or smokestack.

WORD STRUCTURE:
煙(えん) (smoke) + 突(とつ) (protrude) - a structure that protrudes for smoke.

TWO CONTEXTS:
1. Residential: chimney on a house (with fireplace/stove)
2. Industrial: smokestack on factory, ship, power plant

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 煙突(えんとつ)から煙(けむり)が出(で)る: smoke comes from the chimney
  • 煙突(えんとつ)を掃除(そうじ)する: to clean the chimney
  • 煙突(えんとつ)がそびえる: a smokestack towers

RELATED WORDS:

  • 煙(けむり): smoke
  • 暖炉(だんろ): fireplace
  • 煤(すす): soot

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, サンタクロース (Santa Claus) is said to enter through the chimney, as in Western tradition.
