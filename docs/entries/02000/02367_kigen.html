機嫌(きげん)

きげん
noun
mood, temper
1. mood, temper
A person's emotional state.
機嫌(きげん)がいい。
To be in a good mood.
機嫌(きげん)を取(と)る。
To try to please someone.

機嫌 describes emotional states.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 機嫌(きげん)がいい: in a good mood
  • 機嫌(きげん)が悪(わる)い: in a bad mood
  • ご機嫌(きげん)いかが: How are you? (polite)
  • ご機嫌(きげん)よう: Goodbye (formal)
