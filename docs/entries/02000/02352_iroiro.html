色々(いろいろ)

いろいろ
na-adjective, adverb
various, many kinds of
1. various, diverse
Of many different kinds.
色々(いろいろ)な人(ひと)がいる。
There are various people.
色々(いろいろ)な料理(りょうり)を食(た)べた。
I ate various dishes.
Sense 1: various
色々(いろいろ)な国(くに)に行(い)った。
I've been to various countries.
Sense 1: various
店(みせ)には色々(いろいろ)な商品(しょうひん)がある。
The store has various products.
Sense 1: various
色々(いろいろ)な意見(いけん)がある。
There are various opinions.
Sense 1: various
2. in many ways
Adverbially, in various manners.
色々(いろいろ)ありがとうございました。
Thank you for everything.
色々(いろいろ)と考(かんが)えた。
I thought about it in many ways.
Sense 2: in many ways
色々(いろいろ)と手伝(てつだ)ってくれた。
They helped me in many ways.
Sense 2: in many ways
色々(いろいろ)大変(たいへん)でしたね。
It must have been hard in many ways.
Sense 2: in many ways
色々(いろいろ)と調(しら)べている。
I'm researching in many ways.
Sense 2: in many ways

色々(いろいろ) functions as both a na-adjective and adverb, meaning "various" or "in many ways."

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • 色々(いろいろ)な + noun: various ~ (na-adjective)
  • 色々(いろいろ)と + verb: in many ways (adverb with と)
  • 色々(いろいろ) + verb: in many ways (adverb without と)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 色々(いろいろ)な人(ひと): various people
  • 色々(いろいろ)ありがとう: thanks for everything
  • 色々(いろいろ)と大変(たいへん)でしたね: it must have been hard in many ways
  • 色々(いろいろ)考(かんが)える: to think about various things

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 様々(さまざま)(な): various (more formal, written)
  • 種々(しゅじゅ)(の): various (formal, literary)
  • 多様(たよう)(な): diverse (academic/formal)

WRITING:
Often written in hiragana as いろいろ in casual contexts.
