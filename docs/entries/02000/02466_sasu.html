1. to insert, to put in
To place something into an opening or container.
鍵(かぎ)を挿(さ)す。
To insert a key.
花(はな)を花瓶(かびん)に挿(さ)す。
To put flowers in a vase.
コンセントを挿(さ)して電気(でんき)をつけた。
I plugged it in and turned on the power.
髪(かみ)にかんざしを挿(さ)すと、とても{きれい}に見(み)える。
When you put a hairpin in your hair, it looks very pretty.
パソコンにUSBメモリを挿(さ)して、写真(しゃしん)のデータを移(うつ)した。
I inserted a USB drive into the computer and transferred the photo data.
2. to graft (plants)
To insert a cutting into soil or another plant.
枝(えだ)を土(つち)に挿(さ)す。
To insert a cutting into soil.
挿(さ)し木(き)で植物(しょくぶつ)を増(ふ)やす。
To propagate plants by cuttings.
芽(め)を土(つち)に挿(さ)して水(みず)をやると、やがて根(ね)が出(で)てくる。
If you insert a shoot into soil and water it, roots will eventually grow.
バラの枝(えだ)を切(き)って挿(さ)し木(き)にしたら、うまく育(そだ)った。
I cut a rose branch and made a cutting from it, and it grew well.
春(はる)になったら挿(さ)し木(き)をして、庭(にわ)の花(はな)を増(ふ)やそうと思(おも)っている。
When spring comes, I plan to do some cuttings and increase the flowers in the garden.

挿(さ)す means to insert or put something into an opening, slot, or container.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the object being inserted

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花(はな)を花瓶(かびん)に挿(さ)す: to put flowers in a vase
  • 鍵(かぎ)を挿(さ)す: to insert a key
  • コンセントを挿(さ)す: to plug in
  • 髪(かみ)に挿(さ)す: to put (ornament) in one's hair

COMPOUNDS:

  • 挿(さ)し木(き): plant cutting (for propagation)
  • 挿(さ)し絵(え): illustration (inserted in text)

KANJI DISTINCTION:

Several verbs read さす use different kanji:

  • 挿(さ)す: to insert
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, pierce
  • 指(さ)す: to point
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella)
  • 射(さ)す: (light) to shine
