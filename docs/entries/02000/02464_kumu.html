酌(く)む

くむ
godan verb
to pour (sake), to serve drinks
1. to pour, to serve sake
To fill a cup with sake or other alcoholic beverages.
お酒(さけ)を酌む。
To pour sake.
2. to consider, to take into account
To give consideration to circumstances.

酌(く)む means to pour sake or alcoholic drinks for someone, and figuratively to consider someone's feelings.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

MEANINGS:
1. To pour sake: お酒(さけ)を酌(く)む
2. To consider/take into account: 事情(じじょう)を酌(く)む (to consider circumstances)

HOMOPHONE DISTINCTION:

  • 酌(く)む: to pour sake
  • 汲(く)む: to draw/scoop water
  • 組(く)む: to assemble, to cross (arms/legs)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 酌(しゃく)をする: to pour drinks (for someone)
  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく): evening drink
  • お酌(しゃく): pouring drinks (polite)

CULTURE:
Pouring drinks for others (酌(しゃく)をする) is an important social custom in Japan, showing respect and building relationships.
