酌(く)む

くむ
godan verb
to pour (sake), to serve drinks
1. to pour, to serve sake
To fill a cup with sake or other alcoholic beverages.
2. to consider, to take into account
To give consideration to circumstances.
お酒(さけ)を酌む。
To pour sake.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 酌(く)む: to pour sake
  • 汲(く)む: to draw water
  • 組(く)む: to assemble

RELATED:

  • 酌(しゃく): pouring sake
  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく): evening drink
