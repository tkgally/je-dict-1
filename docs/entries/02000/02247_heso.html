臍(へそ)

へそ
noun
navel, belly button
1. navel, belly button
The scar on the abdomen from the umbilical cord.
臍(へそ)を出(だ)す。
To expose one's belly button.
臍(へそ)が出(で)ている服(ふく)。
Clothes that show the navel.

臍(へそ) means navel or belly button. Usually written in hiragana; the kanji 臍(へそ) is rarely used.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 臍(へそ)を出(だ)す: to show one's belly button
  • 出(で)べそ: outie belly button (protruding navel)

IDIOMATIC EXPRESSIONS:

  • 臍(へそ)を曲(ま)げる: to sulk, to be in a bad mood (literally: bend one's navel)
  • 臍(へそ)で茶(ちゃ)を沸(わ)かす: to find something ridiculous/absurd (literally: boil tea with one's navel)
  • 臍(へそ)くり: secret savings, pin money

RELATED:

  • 臍(へそ)の緒(お): umbilical cord
  • お腹(なか): belly, stomach
  • 腹(はら): abdomen (more informal)
