寄(よ)せる

よせる
ichidan verb
to draw near, to gather
1. to draw near, to bring close
To move something closer.
椅子(いす)を寄せる。
To draw up a chair.
2. to gather, to collect
To bring things together.
期待(きたい)を寄せる。
To place expectations (on someone).

寄(よ)せる is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 寄(よ)せる (transitive): to bring near
  • 寄(よ)る (intransitive): to approach

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 思(おも)いを寄(よ)せる: to have feelings for
  • 信頼(しんらい)を寄(よ)せる: to place trust in
general
