寄(よ)せる

よせる
ichidan verb
to draw near, to gather
1. to draw near, to bring close
To move something closer.
椅子(いす)を寄せる。
To draw up a chair.
2. to gather, to collect
To bring things together.
期待(きたい)を寄せる。
To place expectations (on someone).

寄(よ)せる is an ichidan verb meaning to bring close, gather, or direct toward something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 寄(よ)る (intransitive: to approach, to stop by)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 椅子(いす)を寄(よ)せる: to draw up a chair
  • 思(おも)いを寄(よ)せる: to have feelings for someone
  • 信頼(しんらい)を寄(よ)せる: to place trust in
  • 期待(きたい)を寄(よ)せる: to place expectations on
  • 関心(かんしん)を寄(よ)せる: to take interest in
  • 身(み)を寄(よ)せる: to take shelter with

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 寄(よ)せ集(あつ)める: to gather together
