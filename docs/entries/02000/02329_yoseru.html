寄(よ)せる

よせる
ichidan verb
to draw near, to gather

Definitions

1. to draw near, to bring close
To move something closer.
2. to gather, to collect
To bring things together.

Examples

椅子(いす)を寄せる。
To draw up a chair.
期待(きたい)を寄せる。
To place expectations (on someone).

Notes

寄せる is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 寄せる (transitive): to bring near
  • 寄(よ)る (intransitive): to approach

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 思(おも)いを寄せる: to have feelings for
  • 信頼(しんらい)を寄せる: to place trust in
