沈(しず)める

しずめる
verb (ichidan)
to sink, to calm, to subdue

Definitions

1. to sink, to submerge
To make something go underwater.
2. to calm, to quiet, to suppress
To make something peaceful or less intense.

Examples

船(ふね)を沈(しず)める。
To sink a ship.
怒(いか)りを沈(しず)める。
To calm one's anger.
痛(いた)みを沈(しず)める薬(くすり)。
Medicine to relieve pain.

Notes

沈(しず)める is transitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 沈(しず)める (transitive): to sink, calm
  • 沈(しず)む (intransitive): to sink

USES:
1. Physical: to sink something
2. Emotional: to calm down feelings
