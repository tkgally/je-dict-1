猿(さる)

さる
noun
monkey, ape

Definitions

1. monkey
A primate with a long tail.

Examples

猿(さる)が木(き)から落(お)ちた。
A monkey fell from the tree.
日光(にっこう)で猿(さる)を見(み)た。
I saw monkeys in Nikko.

Notes

猿(さる) refers to monkeys.

ZODIAC:
申(さる) is the ninth animal of the Chinese zodiac.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 猿(さる)も木(き)から落(お)ちる: even experts make mistakes
  • 犬猿(けんえん)の仲(なか): like cats and dogs

COMPOUNDS:

  • 猿芝居(さるしばい): monkey business, farce
  • 日本猿(にほんざる): Japanese macaque
