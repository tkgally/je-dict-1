痛(いた)む

いたむ
verb
to hurt, to ache, to feel pain

Definitions

1. to hurt, to ache, to feel pain
To experience physical pain.

Examples

頭(あたま)が痛(いた)む。
My head hurts.
傷(きず)が痛(いた)む。
The wound hurts.
心(こころ)が痛(いた)む。
It pains my heart.
Figurative usage

Notes

痛(いた)む is an intransitive verb meaning to feel pain or ache.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Related: 痛(いた)める (transitive, to hurt/injure)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 頭(あたま)が痛(いた)む (head hurts)
  • 胃(い)が痛(いた)む (stomach hurts)
  • 傷(きず)が痛(いた)む (wound hurts)
  • 心(こころ)が痛(いた)む (heart aches - figurative)

DISTINCTION FROM 痛(いた)い:

  • 痛(いた)い: adjective describing pain sensation
  • 痛(いた)む: verb indicating ongoing/developing pain
