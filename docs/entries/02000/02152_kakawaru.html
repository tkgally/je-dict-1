関(かか)わる

かかわる
verb
to be concerned with, to be involved

Definitions

1. to be concerned with, to be involved
To have a connection or involvement with something.

Examples

仕事(しごと)に関(かか)わる。
To be involved with work.
命(いのち)に関(かか)わる問題(もんだい)。
A matter of life and death.
深(ふか)く関(かか)わっている。
Deeply involved.

Notes

関(かか)わる is an intransitive verb meaning to be connected or involved with.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

PARTICLE USAGE:

  • 〜に関(かか)わる (to be involved with ~)
  • 〜に関(かか)わらず (regardless of ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仕事(しごと)に関(かか)わる (be involved in work)
  • 命(いのち)に関(かか)わる (a matter of life and death)
  • 深(ふか)く関(かか)わる (be deeply involved)

RELATED VERBS:

  • 関係(かんけい)する: to be related to
  • 携(たずさ)わる: to be engaged in (more formal)
