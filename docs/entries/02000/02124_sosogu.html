注(そそ)ぐ

そそぐ
verb (godan)
to pour, to flow into
1. to pour
To cause liquid to flow into a container.
お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To pour tea.
2. to flow into
For a river to empty into a larger body of water.
川(かわ)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぐ。
The river flows into the sea.
3. to devote, to concentrate
To direct attention or effort toward something.
愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To pour love into (devote love to).

注(そそ)ぐ is a godan verb with both literal (pouring) and figurative (devoting) meanings.

TRANSITIVITY:

Can be both transitive and intransitive depending on usage:

  • Transitive: お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ (to pour tea)
  • Intransitive: 川(かわ)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぐ (river flows into sea)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour tea
  • 水(みず)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour water
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour love into, to devote love
  • 力(ちから)を注(そそ)ぐ: to devote effort
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を注(そそ)ぐ: to direct attention to

COMPOUND VERB:

  • 注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour into (emphasizes thoroughness)
