1. to pour
To cause liquid to flow into a container.
お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To pour tea.
コップに水(みず)を注(そそ)いだ。
I poured water into a glass.
熱(あつ)いお湯(ゆ)をカップに注(そそ)いで紅茶(こうちゃ)を入(い)れた。
I poured hot water into the cup and made tea.
2. to flow into
For a river to empty into a larger body of water.
川(かわ)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぐ。
The river flows into the sea.
小(ちい)さな川(かわ)が湖(みずうみ)に注(そそ)いでいる。
A small river flows into the lake.
この川(かわ)は山(やま)から流(なが)れて太平洋(たいへいよう)に注(そそ)ぐ。
This river flows from the mountains and empties into the Pacific Ocean.
3. to devote, to concentrate
To direct attention or effort toward something.
愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぐ。
To devote love to.
子供(こども)に愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)いで育(そだ)てる。
To raise children by pouring love into them.
研究(けんきゅう)に全力(ぜんりょく)を注(そそ)いだ結果(けっか)、大(おお)きな成果(せいか)を上(あ)げた。
As a result of devoting all their energy to research, they achieved great results.

注(そそ)ぐ is a godan verb with both literal (pouring) and figurative (devoting) meanings.

TRANSITIVITY:

Can be both transitive and intransitive depending on usage:

  • Transitive: お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ (to pour tea)
  • Intransitive: 川(かわ)が海(うみ)に注(そそ)ぐ (river flows into sea)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お茶(ちゃ)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour tea
  • 水(みず)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour water
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour love into, to devote love
  • 力(ちから)を注(そそ)ぐ: to devote effort
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を注(そそ)ぐ: to direct attention to

COMPOUND VERB:

  • 注(そそ)ぎ込(こ)む: to pour into (emphasizes thoroughness)
