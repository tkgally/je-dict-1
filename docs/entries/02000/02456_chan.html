～ちゃん

ちゃん
suffix
affectionate name suffix
1. affectionate/cute name suffix
An informal suffix used for children, girls, or close friends. Expresses affection or familiarity.
花子(はなこ)ちゃん、おはよう。
Good morning, Hanako-chan.

USAGE:

  • For children and babies
  • For girls and young women
  • Among close friends
  • For pets
  • In baby talk: おかあちゃん (mommy), おとうちゃん (daddy)

HONORIFIC HIERARCHY:

  • ～様(さま) (sama): formal
  • ～さん (san): polite
  • ～君(くん) (kun): informal (males)
  • ～ちゃん (chan): affectionate
