～建(だ)て

だて
suffix
-story, -storied (building)
1. -story, -storied
Indicates the number of floors in a building.
二階(にかい)建(だ)ての家(いえ)。
A two-story house.
三階(さんがい)建(だ)てのビルが多(おお)い。
There are many three-story buildings.
この町(まち)には五階(ごかい)建(だ)て以上(いじょう)のビルがほとんどない。
There are almost no buildings of five stories or more in this town.
駅前(えきまえ)に高層(こうそう)建(だ)てのマンションが建(た)った。
A high-rise apartment building was built in front of the station.
2. detached, separate
Indicates a standalone structure (as in 一戸建て).
一戸(いっこ)建(だ)てに住(す)みたい。
I want to live in a detached house.
一戸(いっこ)建(だ)てとマンション、どちらがいいですか。
Which is better, a detached house or an apartment building?

～建(だ)て is a suffix indicating the number of floors in a building or describing a standalone structure.

COUNTING FLOORS:

  • 二階(にかい)建(だ)て: two-story (building)
  • 三階(さんがい)建(だ)て: three-story
  • 五階(ごかい)建(だ)て: five-story
  • 高層(こうそう)建(だ)て: high-rise

HOUSE TYPES:

  • 一戸(いっこ)建(だ)て: detached house (single-family home)
  • 二世帯(にせたい)住宅(じゅうたく): two-generation house

NOTE:
Reading can be だて or たて. When counting floors with numbers, commonly たて.

SIMILAR:

  • 階(かい): floor (counter for building levels)
  • ～造(づく)り: made of ~ (construction material)
