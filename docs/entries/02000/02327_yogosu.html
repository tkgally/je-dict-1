汚(よご)す

よごす
godan verb
to make dirty, to pollute
1. to make dirty, to soil
To cause something to become unclean.
2. to pollute, to contaminate
To make impure or unfit.
服(ふく)を汚す。
To get one's clothes dirty.
手(て)を汚す。
To dirty one's hands (lit./fig.).

汚す is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 汚す (transitive): to make dirty
  • 汚(よご)れる (intransitive): to get dirty

IDIOMATIC:

  • 手(て)を汚す: to get one's hands dirty (also means to commit crimes)
