汚(よご)す

よごす
godan verb
to make dirty, to pollute
1. to make dirty, to soil
To cause something to become unclean.
服(ふく)を汚す。
To get one's clothes dirty.
手(て)を汚す。
To dirty one's hands (lit./fig.).
Sense 1: to make dirty
泥(どろ)で靴(くつ)を汚(よご)した。
I dirtied my shoes with mud.
Sense 1: to soil
部屋(へや)を汚(よご)さないで。
Don't make a mess in the room.
Sense 1: to make dirty
子供(こども)が壁(かべ)を汚(よご)した。
The child dirtied the wall.
Sense 1: to soil
2. to pollute, to contaminate
To make impure or unfit.
川(かわ)を汚(よご)す工場(こうじょう)。
A factory that pollutes the river.
Sense 2: to pollute
空気(くうき)を汚(よご)す。
To pollute the air.
Sense 2: to contaminate
環境(かんきょう)を汚(よご)してはいけない。
We must not pollute the environment.
Sense 2: to pollute
名誉(めいよ)を汚(よご)す。
To tarnish one's honor.
Sense 2: to contaminate (figurative)
海(うみ)を汚(よご)す{ゴミ}。
Trash that pollutes the ocean.
Sense 2: to pollute

汚(よご)す is a transitive godan verb meaning "to make dirty" or "to pollute."

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 汚(よご)れる (intransitive - to get dirty)

ASPECT:

  • 汚(よご)している: is in the process of dirtying
  • 汚(よご)してある: has been intentionally dirtied

COMMON OBJECTS:

  • 服(ふく)を汚(よご)す: to dirty clothes
  • 部屋(へや)を汚(よご)す: to make a room messy
  • 空気(くうき)/川(かわ)を汚(よご)す: to pollute air/river
  • 名誉(めいよ)を汚(よご)す: to tarnish one's honor

IDIOM:
手(て)を汚(よご)す has two meanings:
1. Literal: to dirty one's hands
2. Figurative: to do something immoral/criminal
