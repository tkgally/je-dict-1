庭(にわ)

にわ
noun
garden, yard
1. garden
A piece of ground used for growing plants.
2. yard
The area of ground around a house.
庭(にわ)に花(はな)を植(う)える。
To plant flowers in the garden.
庭(にわ)で遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play in the yard.

庭(にわ) means garden or yard.

JAPANESE GARDENS:

  • 日本庭園(にほんていえん): Japanese garden
  • 枯山水(かれさんすい): dry landscape garden
  • 茶庭(ちゃにわ): tea garden

COMPOUNDS:

  • 庭師(にわし): gardener
  • 中庭(なかにわ): courtyard
  • 庭先(にわさき): front yard
