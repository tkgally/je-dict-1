すてき

すてき
na-adjective
wonderful, lovely
1. wonderful, lovely
Extremely attractive or pleasing.
すてきな服(ふく)ですね。
What a lovely outfit!
すてきな考(かんが)えだ。
That's a wonderful idea.

すてき is a na-adjective expressing genuine admiration for something wonderful or lovely.

KANJI:
Can be written as 素敵(すてき) but hiragana is common.

FORMS:

  • すてきな + noun: a lovely ~
  • すてきに: wonderfully (adverbial)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すてきな服(ふく): lovely clothes
  • すてきな考(かんが)え: wonderful idea
  • すてきな人(ひと): lovely person

USAGE NOTE:
Often used by women; expresses genuine positive admiration.

SIMILAR:

  • 素晴(すば)らしい: wonderful (more emphatic)
  • きれい: beautiful, pretty
