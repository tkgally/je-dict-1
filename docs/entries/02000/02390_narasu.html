慣(な)らす

ならす
godan verb
to accustom, to train
1. to accustom, to get used to
To make someone or oneself familiar with something.
体(からだ)を寒(さむ)さに慣らす。
To accustom one's body to the cold.
2. to train, to break in
To prepare something for use through repeated practice.
新(あたら)しい靴(くつ)を慣らす。
To break in new shoes.

慣(な)らす is a godan verb meaning "to accustom" or "to train"—to make something or someone familiar with a condition.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 慣(な)れる (intransitive, to get used to)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を〜に慣(な)らす: to accustom ~ to ~
  • 体(からだ)を寒(さむ)さに慣(な)らす: to accustom one's body to cold
  • 目(め)を暗(くら)さに慣(な)らす: to let one's eyes adjust to darkness
  • 靴(くつ)を慣(な)らす: to break in shoes

HOMOPHONES (different kanji):

  • 鳴(な)らす: to ring, to sound
  • 均(な)らす: to level, to even out
  • 馴(な)らす: alternative writing for "to tame"
