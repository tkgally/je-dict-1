慣(な)らす

ならす
godan verb
to accustom, to train
1. to accustom, to get used to
To make someone or oneself familiar with something.
2. to train, to break in
To prepare something for use through repeated practice.
体(からだ)を寒(さむ)さに慣らす。
To accustom one's body to the cold.
新(あたら)しい靴(くつ)を慣らす。
To break in new shoes.

慣らす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 慣らす (transitive): to accustom
  • 慣(な)れる (intransitive): to get used to

HOMOPHONE:

  • 鳴(な)らす: to ring (different kanji)
general
