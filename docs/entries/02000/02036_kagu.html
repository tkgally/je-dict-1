嗅(か)ぐ

かぐ
verb (godan)
to smell, to sniff
1. to smell, to sniff
To perceive odor through the nose.
花(はな)の匂(にお)いを嗅(か)ぐ。
To smell the flowers.
犬(いぬ)が地面(じめん)を嗅(か)いでいる。
The dog is sniffing the ground.
くんくん嗅(か)ぐ。
To sniff around.

嗅(か)ぐ means to smell or sniff—the active verb for perceiving odors.

TRANSITIVITY:

嗅(か)ぐ is transitive—you smell something:

  • 匂(にお)いを嗅(か)ぐ: to smell a scent
  • 香(かお)りを嗅(か)ぐ: to smell a fragrance

CONTRAST:

  • 嗅(か)ぐ: to smell (active, intentional)
  • 匂(にお)う: to smell, give off odor (intransitive)
  • 匂(にお)いがする: there is a smell

ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • くんくん嗅(か)ぐ: to sniff around (like an animal)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 嗅(か) combines the mouth and smell radicals.
