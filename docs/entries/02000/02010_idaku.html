1. to hold, to embrace
To hold someone or something in one's arms (literary).
母(はは)が赤(あか)ちゃんを抱(いだ)く。
The mother holds the baby.
恋人(こいびと)を抱(いだ)く。
To embrace one's lover.
胸(むね)に抱(いだ)いて眠(ねむ)る。
To sleep holding it to one's chest.
子(こ)を抱(いだ)く母親(ははおや)。
A mother holding her child.
彼女(かのじょ)は花束(はなたば)を胸(むね)に抱(いだ)いて微笑(ほほえ)んだ。
She smiled, holding the bouquet to her chest.
2. to harbor, to have (feelings)
To have feelings, thoughts, or ideas.
夢(ゆめ)を抱(いだ)く。
To have a dream.
疑問(ぎもん)を抱(いだ)いた。
I harbored doubts.
希望(きぼう)を抱(いだ)いて生(い)きる。
To live with hope.
不安(ふあん)を抱(いだ)いている。
I'm harboring anxiety.
野心(やしん)を抱(いだ)く若者(わかもの)。
A young person with ambition.
恨(うら)みを抱(いだ)く。
To harbor resentment.

抱(いだ)く is a literary/formal verb with two main uses: physically holding someone in one's arms, and figuratively harboring feelings or ideas. The figurative sense is far more common in modern usage.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを抱(いだ)く

ASPECT (ている):

  • 抱(いだ)いている: is harboring/holding (ongoing state)

COMMON OBJECTS (figurative):

  • 夢(ゆめ)を抱(いだ)く: to have a dream/aspiration
  • 疑問(ぎもん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor doubts
  • 希望(きぼう)を抱(いだ)く: to have hope
  • 不安(ふあん)を抱(いだ)く: to feel anxiety
  • 野心(やしん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor ambition
  • 恨(うら)みを抱(いだ)く: to bear a grudge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 抱(だ)く: same kanji, more common for physical holding; also colloquial
  • 抱(かか)える: to carry/hold in arms; also figurative for "having" problems
