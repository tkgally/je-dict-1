微笑(ほほえ)む

ほほえむ
godan verb
to smile
1. to smile
To form a gentle, pleasant expression.
やさしく微笑む。
To smile gently.
赤(あか)ちゃんが微笑んだ。
The baby smiled.

微笑む describes a gentle smile.

NUANCE:

  • 微笑む: gentle, warm smile
  • 笑(わら)う: to laugh, smile (general)
  • にやにやする: to grin, smirk

RELATED:

  • 微笑(ほほえ)み: smile (noun)
  • 微笑(びしょう): smile (literary)
