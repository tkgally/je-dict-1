裂(さ)く

さく
verb (godan)
to tear, to rip, to split
See also: 割(さ)く (spare, devote)
1. to tear, to rip
To pull apart by force.
紙(かみ)を裂(さ)く。
To tear paper.
布(ぬの)を手(て)で裂(さ)いて包帯(ほうたい)にした。
I tore the cloth by hand and used it as a bandage.
子供(こども)が絵本(えほん)のページを裂(さ)いてしまって、母親(ははおや)が怒(おこ)った。
The child tore a page of the picture book, and the mother got angry.
2. to split, to divide
To separate into parts.
二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)く。
To separate two people.
仲(なか)のいい二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)くのはよくない。
It is not good to split up two people who are close.
戦争(せんそう)は多(おお)くの家族(かぞく)を裂(さ)き、深(ふか)い悲(かな)しみをもたらした。
The war tore many families apart and brought deep sorrow.

裂(さ)く means to tear or split something apart by force.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 裂(さ)ける (intransitive, to be torn/split)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紙(かみ)を裂(さ)く: to tear paper
  • 布(ぬの)を裂(さ)く: to rip cloth
  • 二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)く: to separate two people (figurative)

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 裂(さ)く: to tear, rip
  • 咲(さ)く: to bloom (flowers) — same pronunciation, different meaning
  • 割(さ)く: to spare (time/resources) — different kanji, same pronunciation
