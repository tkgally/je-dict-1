裂(さ)く

さく
verb (godan)
to tear, to rip, to split
1. to tear, to rip
To pull apart by force.
紙(かみ)を裂(さ)く。
To tear paper.
2. to split, to divide
To separate into parts.
二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)く。
To separate two people.

裂(さ)く means to tear or split something apart by force.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 裂(さ)ける (intransitive, to be torn/split)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紙(かみ)を裂(さ)く: to tear paper
  • 布(ぬの)を裂(さ)く: to rip cloth
  • 二人(ふたり)を裂(さ)く: to separate two people (figurative)

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 裂(さ)く: to tear, rip
  • 咲(さ)く: to bloom (flowers) — same pronunciation, different meaning
  • 割(さ)く: to spare (time/resources) — different kanji, same pronunciation
