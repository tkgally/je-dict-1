生年月日(せいねんがっぴ)

せいねんがっぴ
noun
date of birth

Definitions

1. date of birth
The date on which a person was born.

Examples

生年月日(せいねんがっぴ)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in your date of birth.
生年月日(せいねんがっぴ)は1990年(ねん)5月(がつ)10日(にち)です。
My date of birth is May 10, 1990.
申込書(もうしこみしょ)に生年月日(せいねんがっぴ)を書(か)く欄(らん)がある。
There is a field for date of birth on the application form.

Notes

生年月日(せいねんがっぴ) is a formal term used on official forms.

BREAKDOWN:

  • 生年(せいねん) (year of birth)
  • 月日(がっぴ) (month and day)

USAGE:
Commonly abbreviated to 生年月日(せいねんがっぴ) on forms. Often followed by instructions to write in a specific format (e.g., 和暦 Japanese era or 西暦 Western calendar).

RELATED:

  • 誕生日(たんじょうび) (birthday - more casual)

Related Words

Related: 誕生日(たんじょうび) (birthday (casual))
