気持(きも)ち

きもち
noun
feeling, mood
1. feeling, emotion
An emotional state or sentiment.
あなたの気持(きも)ちは分(わ)かります。
I understand your feelings.
気持(きも)ちを伝(つた)えたい。
I want to convey my feelings.
嬉(うれ)しい気持(きも)ちになった。
I felt happy.
Sense 1: feeling
複雑(ふくざつ)な気持(きも)ちだ。
I have mixed feelings.
Sense 1: emotion
気持(きも)ちが落(お)ち着(つ)いた。
I calmed down.
Sense 1: feeling
2. sensation, feeling
A physical sensation, especially of comfort or discomfort.
このソファは気持(きも)ちがいい。
This sofa feels comfortable.
気持(きも)ちがいい = feels good
気持(きも)ちが悪(わる)い。
I feel sick. / It feels gross.
Sense 2: sensation
シャワーを浴(あ)びて気持(きも)ちがいい。
I feel refreshed after taking a shower.
Sense 2: sensation
風(かぜ)が気持(きも)ちいい。
The wind feels nice.
Sense 2: sensation
肌触(はだざわ)りが気持(きも)ちいい。
It feels nice to the touch.
Sense 2: feeling
3. intention, sentiment
One's underlying sentiment or the feeling behind an action.
お気持(きも)ちだけで十分(じゅうぶん)です。
Your sentiment alone is enough.
Sense 3: intention
気持(きも)ちは分(わ)かるけど。
I understand how you feel, but...
Sense 3: sentiment
感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちを込(こ)めて。
With feelings of gratitude.
Sense 3: sentiment
気持(きも)ちが大切(たいせつ)だ。
The sentiment is what matters.
Sense 3: intention
プレゼントは気持(きも)ちだから。
It's the thought that counts in a gift.
Sense 3: sentiment

Very common word covering emotions, physical sensations, and intentions.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気持(きも)ちがいい (feels good, comfortable)
  • 気持(きも)ちが悪(わる)い (feels bad, uncomfortable, creepy)
  • 気持(きも)ちを伝(つた)える (convey one's feelings)
  • 気持(きも)ちが分(わ)かる (understand someone's feelings)
  • お気持(きも)ちだけで (just the thought [is enough])

RELATED WORDS:

  • 気分(きぶん) - mood, feeling (more about general state)
  • 感情(かんじょう) - emotion (more formal/psychological)
  • 心(こころ) - heart, mind (deeper)
