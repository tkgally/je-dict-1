穏(おだ)やか

おだやか
na-adjective
calm, gentle
1. calm, peaceful
Free from disturbance or agitation.
2. gentle, mild
Having a kind, moderate nature.
穏やかな天気(てんき)
Calm weather
穏やかな性格(せいかく)
A gentle personality

穏(おだ)やか describes peaceful states.

COMMON USES:

  • 穏(おだ)やかな海(うみ): calm sea
  • 穏(おだ)やかな顔(かお): a serene face
  • 穏(おだ)やかに暮(く)らす: to live peacefully

SIMILAR:

  • 静(しず)か: quiet
  • 温和(おんわ): gentle, mild
