分(わ)ける

わける
ichidan verb
to divide, to separate
1. to divide, to split
To separate into parts.
ケーキを三(みっ)つに分ける。
To divide the cake into three.
髪(かみ)を分ける。
To part one's hair.
2. to distribute, to share
To give portions to multiple recipients.

分(わ)ける is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to divide, separate, or share.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 分(わ)ける (他動詞(たどうし)): to divide/share
  • 分(わ)かれる (自動詞(じどうし)): to split up, part ways

SENSE 1 - DIVIDE:

  • ケーキを三(みっ)つに分(わ)ける: divide cake into three
  • 髪(かみ)を分(わ)ける: to part one's hair
  • グループに分(わ)ける: to divide into groups

SENSE 2 - SHARE/DISTRIBUTE:

  • みんなに分(わ)ける: to share with everyone
  • 均等(きんとう)に分(わ)ける: to divide equally

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 区別(くべつ)して分(わ)ける: to sort/categorize
  • 勝負(しょうぶ)を分(わ)ける: to decide the match
  • 色(いろ)で分(わ)ける: to sort by color
