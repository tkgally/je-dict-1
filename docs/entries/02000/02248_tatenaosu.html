立(た)て直(なお)す

たてなおす
verb (godan)
to rebuild, to restore, to recover

Definitions

1. to rebuild, to reconstruct
To build again something that has fallen down or been destroyed.
2. to restore, to revive, to turn around
To restore something (business, finances, health, etc.) to a good state after it has declined.

Examples

地震(じしん)で壊(こわ)れた家(いえ)を立(た)て直(なお)した。
I rebuilt the house that was destroyed by the earthquake.
Sense 1: physical rebuilding
会社(かいしゃ)の経営(けいえい)を立(た)て直(なお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to turn around the company's management.
Sense 2: restore/revive
体調(たいちょう)を立(た)て直(なお)すために休(やす)んでいる。
I'm resting to recover my health.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive).

CONJUGATION (godan): 立(た)て直(なお)す → 立(た)て直(なお)さない → 立(た)て直(なお)した → 立(た)て直(なお)して

COMPOUND VERB: 立(た)てる (to build, to stand up) + 直(なお)す (to fix, to redo)

COMMON OBJECTS:

  • 経営(けいえい)を立(た)て直(なお)す (turn around management)
  • 生活(せいかつ)を立(た)て直(なお)す (rebuild one's life)
  • 体調(たいちょう)を立(た)て直(なお)す (recover one's health)
  • 計画(けいかく)を立(た)て直(なお)す (revise/redo a plan)

The 〜直(なお)す pattern indicates doing something over again or redoing it properly.
