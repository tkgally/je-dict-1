溺(おぼ)れる

おぼれる
verb (ichidan)
to drown, to be addicted to
1. to drown, to nearly drown
To be submerged in water and unable to breathe.
川(かわ)で溺(おぼ)れそうになった。
I almost drowned in the river.
子供(こども)が溺(おぼ)れている！
A child is drowning!
2. to be addicted to, to indulge in
To be excessively devoted to something.
酒(さけ)に溺(おぼ)れる。
To be addicted to alcohol.

溺(おぼ)れる means to drown (literally) or to be addicted (figuratively).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

ASPECT (ている):
溺(おぼ)れている indicates someone is currently drowning (needs help!) or is in a state of addiction.

MEANINGS:
1. Physical: to drown, to be drowning
2. Figurative: to be addicted to, to indulge in

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 川(かわ)で溺(おぼ)れる: to drown in a river
  • 溺(おぼ)れそうになる: to almost drown
  • 〜に溺(おぼ)れる: to be addicted to ~ (figurative)

FIGURATIVE EXPRESSIONS:

  • 酒(さけ)に溺(おぼ)れる: to be addicted to alcohol
  • 愛(あい)に溺(おぼ)れる: to be madly in love
  • 快楽(かいらく)に溺(おぼ)れる: to indulge in pleasure

COMPOUNDS:

  • 溺(おぼ)れ死(し)ぬ: to drown to death
  • 溺(でき)愛(あい): excessive love, doting
