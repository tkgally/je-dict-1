渡(わた)る

わたる
godan verb
to cross, to go across

Definitions

1. to cross, to go across
To move from one side to another.

Examples

橋(はし)を渡る。
To cross the bridge.

Notes

渡る is intransitive.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 渡(わた)す (transitive): to hand over
  • 渡る (intransitive): to cross
