1. to cross, to go across
To move from one side to another.
橋(はし)を渡(わた)る。
To cross the bridge.
道(みち)を渡(わた)る。
To cross the street.
信号(しんごう)が青(あお)になったら渡(わた)る。
Cross when the light turns green.
小(ちい)さな船(ふね)で川(かわ)を渡(わた)った。
I crossed the river in a small boat.
渡(わた)り鳥(どり)が海(うみ)を渡(わた)って南(みなみ)の国(くに)に行(い)く。
Migratory birds cross the ocean and go to southern countries.

渡(わた)る means to cross or go across from one side to another.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 渡(わた)す (transitive, to hand over)

PARTICLE:

Uses を for the thing being crossed:

  • 橋(はし)を渡(わた)る: to cross a bridge
  • 道(みち)を渡(わた)る: to cross a street

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 橋(はし)を渡(わた)る: to cross a bridge
  • 信号(しんごう)を渡(わた)る: to cross at a traffic light
  • 海(うみ)を渡(わた)る: to cross the ocean
  • 世(よ)に渡(わた)る: to spread widely

COMPOUNDS:

  • 渡(わた)り鳥(どり): migratory bird
  • 渡(わた)り歩(ある)く: to wander from place to place
