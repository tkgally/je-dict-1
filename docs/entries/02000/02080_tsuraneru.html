連(つら)ねる

つらねる
verb (ichidan)
to line up, to list
1. to line up, to arrange in a row
To place things in a sequence.
言葉(ことば)を連(つら)ねる。
To string words together.
机(つくえ)を横(よこ)に連(つら)ねて並(なら)べた。
We arranged the desks side by side in a row.
店(みせ)の前(まえ)に椅子(いす)を連(つら)ねて、客(きゃく)を待(ま)っていた。
They had lined up chairs in front of the shop and were waiting for customers.
2. to list, to enumerate
To mention items one after another.
名前(なまえ)を連(つら)ねる。
To list names.
理由(りゆう)を連(つら)ねて断(ことわ)った。
I listed reasons and refused.
彼(かれ)は文句(もんく)を連(つら)ねるばかりで、自分(じぶん)では何(なに)もしない。
He does nothing but string together complaints, and doesn't do anything himself.

連(つら)ねる means to arrange things in a sequence or to list items one after another.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 連(つら)なる (intransitive, to form a line)

ASPECT (ている):
連(つら)ねている indicates a continuing arrangement or that items remain listed.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 言葉(ことば)を連(つら)ねる: to string words together
  • 名前(なまえ)を連(つら)ねる: to list names
  • 理由(りゆう)を連(つら)ねる: to list reasons
  • 文句(もんく)を連(つら)ねる: to complain at length

NOTE:
Often used in written or formal contexts.
