防(ふせ)ぐ

ふせぐ
godan verb
to defend, to prevent
1. to defend against, to protect
To guard or shield from harm.
敵(てき)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To defend against the enemy.
2. to prevent, to ward off
To stop something from happening.
病気(びょうき)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent illness.

防(ふせ)ぐ is a godan verb meaning to defend against, protect from, or prevent.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を防(ふせ)ぐ

SENSE 1 - TO DEFEND/PROTECT:

  • 敵(てき)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to defend against enemies
  • 風(かぜ)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to block the wind
  • 寒(さむ)さを防(ふせ)ぐ: to protect from cold

SENSE 2 - TO PREVENT:

  • 病気(びょうき)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent illness
  • 事故(じこ)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent accidents
  • 火災(かさい)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent fires
  • 犯罪(はんざい)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent crime

RELATED WORDS:

  • 防止(ぼうし)する: to prevent (formal noun + suru)
  • 予防(よぼう)する: to take precautions against
  • 防御(ぼうぎょ)する: to defend (military/formal)
  • 防犯(ぼうはん): crime prevention
