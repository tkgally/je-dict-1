感(かん)じる

かんじる
verb (ichidan)
to feel, to sense
1. to feel, to sense
To perceive through the senses or emotions.
痛(いた)みを感(かん)じる。
To feel pain.
幸(しあわ)せを感(かん)じる。
To feel happiness.
危険(きけん)を感(かん)じた。
I sensed danger.
寒(さむ)さを感(かん)じる。
I feel the cold.
Sense 1: to feel (sensation)
疲(つか)れを感(かん)じている。
I'm feeling tired.
Sense 1: to feel
風(かぜ)を感(かん)じる。
I feel the wind.
Sense 1: to feel (physical)
2. to think, to find
To have an impression or opinion.
不思議(ふしぎ)に感(かん)じた。
I found it strange.
Sense 2: to find/think
必要(ひつよう)だと感(かん)じる。
I think it's necessary.
Sense 2: to think
魅力(みりょく)を感(かん)じる。
I find it attractive.
Sense 2: to find
変(へん)だと感(かん)じませんか。
Don't you think it's strange?
Sense 2: to think

感(かん)じる means to feel, sense, or perceive through emotions or physical sensations.

TRANSITIVITY:
Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes を for what is felt

ASPECT (ている):
感(かん)じている indicates an ongoing state of feeling: 疲(つか)れを感(かん)じている (I'm feeling tired).

PHYSICAL SENSATIONS:

  • 痛(いた)みを感(かん)じる: to feel pain
  • 寒(さむ)さを感(かん)じる: to feel cold
  • 暑(あつ)さを感(かん)じる: to feel heat

EMOTIONS:

  • 喜(よろこ)びを感(かん)じる: to feel joy
  • 不安(ふあん)を感(かん)じる: to feel anxiety
  • 責任(せきにん)を感(かん)じる: to feel responsibility

IMPRESSIONS (Sense 2):

When expressing opinions: [na-adj]に感(かん)じる or [clause]と感(かん)じる

  • 不思議(ふしぎ)に感(かん)じる: to find it strange
  • 必要(ひつよう)だと感(かん)じる: to feel it's necessary

RELATED WORDS:

  • 感(かん)じ: feeling, impression (noun)
  • 感覚(かんかく): sense, sensation
  • 感動(かんどう)する: to be moved/touched
