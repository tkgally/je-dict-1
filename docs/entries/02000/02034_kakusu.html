隠(かく)す

かくす
verb (godan)
to hide, to conceal
1. to hide, to conceal
To keep something from being seen or known.
顔(かお)を隠(かく)す。
To hide one's face.
秘密(ひみつ)を隠(かく)す。
To keep a secret.
本当(ほんとう)のことを隠(かく)さないで。
Don't hide the truth.

隠(かく)す means to hide (transitive).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 隠(かく)す (transitive): to hide
  • 隠(かく)れる (intransitive): to hide oneself

COMMON USES:

  • 顔(かお)を隠(かく)す: to hide one's face
  • 感情(かんじょう)を隠(かく)す: to hide feelings
  • 事実(じじつ)を隠(かく)す: to conceal facts
