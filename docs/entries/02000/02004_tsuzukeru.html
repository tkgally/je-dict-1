〜続(つづ)ける

つづける
auxiliary verb (ichidan)
to continue doing ~
See also: 続(つづ)ける (continue (base form)), ～続(つづ)ける (continue doing (suffix))
1. to continue doing ~
Attached to verb stems to indicate continuous action.
走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
I kept running.
勉強(べんきょう)し続(つづ)けることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to keep studying.
彼(かれ)は話(はな)し続(つづ)けた。
He kept talking.
雨(あめ)が三日間(みっかかん)降(ふ)り続(つづ)けた。
It kept raining for three days.
諦(あきら)めないで、努力(どりょく)し続(つづ)けることが大事(だいじ)だ。
Don't give up — it's important to keep making an effort.

An auxiliary verb that attaches to verb stems to express continuing an action without stopping.

PATTERN:

Verb masu-stem + 続(つづ)ける

  • 食(た)べ + 続(つづ)ける = 食(た)べ続(つづ)ける (keep eating)
  • 走(はし)り + 続(つづ)ける = 走(はし)り続(つづ)ける (keep running)
  • 働(はたら)き + 続(つづ)ける = 働(はたら)き続(つづ)ける (keep working)

CONJUGATION:
Conjugates as an ichidan verb: 続(つづ)けない, 続(つづ)けます, 続(つづ)けて, 続(つづ)けた

USAGE NOTE:
Emphasizes persistence and duration. Often implies effort or determination: 諦(あきら)めずに挑戦(ちょうせん)し続(つづ)ける (keep trying without giving up).

SIMILAR GRAMMAR:

  • 〜ている: ongoing state (neutral)
  • 〜始(はじ)める: to begin doing
  • 〜終(お)わる: to finish doing
