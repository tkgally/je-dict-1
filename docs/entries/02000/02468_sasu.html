1. to shine, to strike
For light to come in or fall upon something.
日(ひ)が射(さ)す。
The sun shines in.
窓(まど)から光(ひかり)が射(さ)してきた。
Light came shining through the window.
雲(くも)の間(あいだ)から日(ひ)が射(さ)して、森(もり)の中(なか)が明(あか)るくなった。
Sunlight shone through the gaps in the clouds, and the forest became bright.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

The verb さす has multiple kanji depending on meaning:

  • 射(さ)す: to shine (light strikes/enters)
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, pierce
  • 指(さ)す: to point at
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella), to pour
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 射(さ)す

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日(ひ)が射(さ)す: sunlight shines in
  • 光(ひかり)が射(さ)す: light shines through
  • 西日(にしび)が射(さ)す: afternoon sun shines in

USAGE NOTE:
射(さ)す describes light entering or falling on a surface. Often used in poetic or descriptive contexts about natural lighting.
