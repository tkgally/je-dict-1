角(すみ)

すみ
noun
corner, nook
1. corner
The point or area where two lines, edges, or surfaces meet.
部屋(へや)の角(すみ)に置(お)く。
To put it in the corner of the room.
角(すみ)から角(すみ)まで探(さが)した。
I searched from corner to corner.
2. nook
A secluded or out-of-the-way place.

角(すみ) refers to an interior corner or secluded spot.

KANJI NOTE:
Often written as 隅(すみ) (different kanji, same meaning for corner).

READINGS OF 角:

  • 角(すみ): interior corner (of room, page, etc.)
  • 角(かど): exterior corner (of street, building), angle
  • 角(つの): horn (of animal)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 部屋(へや)の隅(すみ): corner of a room
  • 隅々(すみずみ): every corner, every nook and cranny
  • 隅(すみ)に置(お)けない: not to be taken lightly, impressive
  • 片(かた)隅(すみ): a corner, one corner

角(すみ) VS 角(かど):
すみ is for interior corners (inside); かど is for exterior corners (outside).
