～おき

おき
suffix
every (interval), at intervals of
1. every ~, at intervals of ~
Suffix indicating regular intervals between occurrences.
一日(いちにち)おきに薬(くすり)を飲(の)む。
To take medicine every other day.
五分(ごふん)おきにバスが来(く)る。
Buses come every five minutes.
三(さん)メートルおきに木(き)が植(う)えてある。
Trees are planted every three meters.

～おき indicates regular intervals. The number specifies how much time/space passes between each occurrence.

KEY POINT:
一日(いちにち)おき means "every other day" (with one day gap between). This can be confusing - the number indicates the interval, not the frequency.

EXAMPLES:

  • 一日(いちにち)おき: every other day (Mon, Wed, Fri...)
  • 二日(ふつか)おき: every third day (with two days between)
  • 一時間(いちじかん)おき: every two hours
  • 五分(ごふん)おき: every six minutes
  • 三(さん)メートルおき: every three meters (spacing)

CONTRAST WITH ～ごとに:

  • ～おき: interval between occurrences
  • ～ごとに: each occurrence
  • 一日(いちにち)おき = every other day
  • 一日(いちにち)ごとに = every day

COMMON MISTAKE:
Learners often confuse 一日(いちにち)おき (every other day) with 毎日(まいにち) (every day).
