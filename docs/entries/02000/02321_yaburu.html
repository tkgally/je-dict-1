破(やぶ)る

やぶる
godan verb
to tear, to break, to defeat
1. to tear, to rip
To pull apart or damage by force.
2. to break, to violate
To fail to observe a rule or promise.
3. to defeat
To beat an opponent.
紙(かみ)を破る。
To tear paper.
約束(やくそく)を破る。
To break a promise.
相手(あいて)チームを破る。
To defeat the opposing team.

破る is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 破る (transitive): to tear, break
  • 破(やぶ)れる (intransitive): to be torn, to lose

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記録(きろく)を破る: to break a record
  • 沈黙(ちんもく)を破る: to break the silence
