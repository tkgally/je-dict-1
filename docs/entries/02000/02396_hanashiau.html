話(はな)し合(あ)う

はなしあう
godan verb
to discuss, to talk together
1. to discuss, to talk over
To have a conversation to reach understanding or agreement.
よく話し合いましょう。
Let's discuss it thoroughly.
問題(もんだい)について話し合う。
To discuss a problem.

話(はな)し合(あ)う means to discuss, to talk things over together.

WORD FORMATION:
話(はな)す (to speak) + 合(あ)う (together) = to speak together/mutually

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: XについてY（と）話(はな)し合(あ)う

GRAMMAR:
The 〜合(あ)う pattern indicates mutual/reciprocal action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 問題(もんだい)について話(はな)し合(あ)う: to discuss a problem
  • よく話(はな)し合(あ)う: to talk it over thoroughly
  • 家族(かぞく)と話(はな)し合(あ)う: to discuss with family

RELATED NOUN:

  • 話(はな)し合(あ)い: discussion, talk, conference

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 相談(そうだん)する: to consult, seek advice
  • 議論(ぎろん)する: to debate, argue
