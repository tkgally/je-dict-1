出会(であ)う

であう
godan verb, intransitive
to meet, to encounter, to come across
1. to meet, to encounter
To meet someone, often by chance or for the first time.
彼(かれ)とは大学(だいがく)で出会(であ)った。
I met him at university.
運命(うんめい)の出会(であ)い。
A fateful encounter.
Sense 1: to meet
駅(えき)で偶然(ぐうぜん)昔(むかし)の友人(ゆうじん)に出会(であ)った。
I happened to meet an old friend at the station.
Sense 1: to meet
人生(じんせい)を変(か)える出会(であ)い。
An encounter that changes one's life.
Sense 1: to meet
旅先(たびさき)で素敵(すてき)な人(ひと)に出会(であ)った。
I met a wonderful person while traveling.
Sense 1: to meet
2. to come across
To encounter something unexpected.
素晴(すば)らしい本(ほん)に出会(であ)った。
I came across a wonderful book.
珍(めずら)しい動物(どうぶつ)に出会(であ)った。
I came across a rare animal.
Sense 2: to come across
思(おも)いがけない困難(こんなん)に出会(であ)う。
To encounter unexpected difficulties.
Sense 2: to come across
新(あたら)しい音楽(おんがく)に出会(であ)った。
I came across some new music.
Sense 2: to come across
良(よ)い機会(きかい)に出会(であ)う。
To come across a good opportunity.
Sense 2: to come across

出会(であ)う is an intransitive verb meaning to meet or encounter, often implying a meaningful, chance, or first-time meeting.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pattern: 〜に出会(であ)う (to meet/encounter ~)

NOUN FORM:

  • 出会(であ)い: encounter, meeting
  • 運命(うんめい)の出会(であ)い: fateful encounter

CONTRAST WITH 会(あ)う:

  • 会(あ)う: to meet (general, including planned meetings)
  • 出会(であ)う: to meet/encounter (often by chance, first time, or significant)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜と出会(であ)う: to meet someone
  • 〜に出会(であ)う: to encounter something
  • 偶然(ぐうぜん)出会(であ)う: to meet by chance
  • 素敵(すてき)な人(ひと)に出会(であ)う: to meet a wonderful person

USAGE:
Often used romantically or for significant life encounters.
