収(おさ)める

おさめる
verb (ichidan)
to store, to obtain, to pay
1. to store, to put away
To place something in storage.
カメラを鞄(かばん)に収(おさ)めた。
I put the camera away in my bag.
2. to obtain, to achieve
To gain or accomplish something.
成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)めた。
I achieved success.
3. to pay, to supply
To deliver payment or goods.
税金(ぜいきん)を収(おさ)める。
To pay taxes.
Also written as 納(おさ)める

おさめる has different kanji for different meanings.

KANJI VARIATIONS:

  • 収(おさ)める: to store, obtain, achieve
  • 納(おさ)める: to pay, deliver, dedicate
  • 治(おさ)める: to govern, rule
  • 修(おさ)める: to study, master

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)める: to achieve success
  • 勝利(しょうり)を収(おさ)める: to win victory
