1. to store, to put away
To place something in storage.
カメラを鞄(かばん)に収(おさ)めた。
I put the camera away in my bag.
写真(しゃしん)に収(おさ)めた。
I captured it in a photo.
データをファイル(ふぁいる)に収(おさ)める。
To store data in a file.
博物館(はくぶつかん)に収(おさ)められている。
It is housed in the museum.
刀(かたな)を鞘(さや)に収(おさ)めた。
I sheathed the sword.
2. to obtain, to achieve
To gain or accomplish something.
成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)めた。
I achieved success.
勝利(しょうり)を収(おさ)めた。
I achieved victory.
好成績(こうせいせき)を収(おさ)めた。
I achieved good results.
大(おお)きな成果(せいか)を収(おさ)めた。
I achieved great results.
功績(こうせき)を収(おさ)めた人(ひと)。
A person who achieved great accomplishments.
3. to pay, to supply
To deliver payment or goods.
税金(ぜいきん)を収(おさ)める。
To pay taxes.
Also written as 納(おさ)める
会費(かいひ)を収(おさ)める。
To pay membership fees.
年貢(ねんぐ)を収(おさ)める。
To pay the land tax.
保険料(ほけんりょう)を収(おさ)める。
To pay insurance premiums.
授業料(じゅぎょうりょう)を収(おさ)める。
To pay tuition fees.

おさめる has different kanji depending on the meaning. This entry covers the 収(おさ)める writing.

KANJI VARIATIONS:

  • 収(おさ)める: to store, obtain, achieve
  • 納(おさ)める: to pay, deliver, dedicate
  • 治(おさ)める: to govern, rule
  • 修(おさ)める: to study, master

COMMON EXPRESSIONS (収(おさ)める):

  • 成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)める: to achieve success
  • 勝利(しょうり)を収(おさ)める: to win victory
  • 成果(せいか)を収(おさ)める: to produce results
  • 鞘(さや)に収(おさ)める: to sheathe (a sword)
  • 写真(しゃしん)に収(おさ)める: to capture in a photo

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を収(おさ)める

The kanji 収(しゅう) relates to gathering and collecting, which connects all three senses: storing (gathering in), achieving (gathering results), paying (gathering funds to deliver).

ASPECT (ている):

  • 収(おさ)めている: has achieved / currently holds (resultative state)
  • Note: In the "achieve" sense, 収(おさ)めている expresses a lasting accomplishment, not an ongoing process. 大(おお)きな成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)めている means "has achieved great success" (and that success continues), not "is in the process of succeeding."
