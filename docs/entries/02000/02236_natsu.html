夏(なつ)

なつ
noun
summer
1. summer
The warmest season of the year.
夏(なつ)は暑(あつ)い。
Summer is hot.
夏休(なつやす)みに海(うみ)に行(い)く。
I'll go to the beach during summer vacation.

夏(なつ) means summer.

SEASONS:

  • 春(はる): spring
  • 夏(なつ): summer
  • 秋(あき): autumn
  • 冬(ふゆ): winter

COMPOUNDS:

  • 夏休(なつやす)み: summer vacation
  • 真夏(まなつ): midsummer
  • 夏祭(なつまつ)り: summer festival
  • 夏服(なつふく): summer clothes
