休(やす)み

やすみ
noun
rest, break, vacation
1. rest, break
A period of relaxation.
休(やす)みを取(と)る。
To take a break.
少(すこ)し休(やす)みましょう。
Let's take a short break.
Sense 1: rest/break
休(やす)みなく働(はたら)いている。
I'm working without a break.
Sense 1: rest
昼休(ひるやす)みに散歩(さんぽ)した。
I took a walk during lunch break.
Sense 1: break
十分(じゅっぷん)の休(やす)みがある。
There's a ten-minute break.
Sense 1: break
2. vacation, day off
Time off from work or school.
明日(あした)は休(やす)みです。
Tomorrow is my day off.
夏休(なつやす)みに旅行(りょこう)する。
I'll travel during summer vacation.
Sense 2: vacation
週末(しゅうまつ)は休(やす)みですか。
Are you off on the weekend?
Sense 2: day off
休(やす)みの日(ひ)は何(なに)をしますか。
What do you do on your days off?
Sense 2: day off
冬休(ふゆやす)みは短(みじか)い。
Winter vacation is short.
Sense 2: vacation

休(やす)み is a noun meaning rest, break, or vacation (derived from the verb 休(やす)む).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 休(やす)みを取(と)る: to take a break/day off
  • 休(やす)みの日(ひ): day off
  • 休(やす)みなく: without rest, continuously
  • お休(やす)みなさい: good night (greeting)

VACATION COMPOUNDS:

  • 夏休(なつやす)み: summer vacation
  • 冬休(ふゆやす)み: winter vacation
  • 春休(はるやす)み: spring break
  • ゴールデンウィーク: Golden Week (late April-early May)

BREAK COMPOUNDS:

  • 昼休(ひるやす)み: lunch break
  • 休憩(きゅうけい): rest period, intermission

WORD FORMATION:
Nominalized form of 休(やす)む (to rest). Many Japanese nouns are formed by taking the verb stem (連用形(れんようけい)).
