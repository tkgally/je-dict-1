除(のぞ)く

のぞく
godan verb
to remove, to exclude

Definitions

1. to remove, to get rid of
To take away or eliminate.
2. to exclude, to except
To leave out from a group.

Examples

シミを除く。
To remove a stain.
子供(こども)を除いて全員(ぜんいん)
Everyone except the children

Notes

除く means to remove or exclude.

HOMOPHONE:

  • 覗(のぞ)く: to peek (different kanji)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を除いて: excluding ~, except for ~
  • 〜を除く: to remove ~
