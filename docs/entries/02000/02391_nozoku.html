除(のぞ)く

のぞく
godan verb
to remove, to exclude
1. to remove, to get rid of
To take away or eliminate.
シミを除く。
To remove a stain.
2. to exclude, to except
To leave out from a group.
子供(こども)を除いて全員(ぜんいん)
Everyone except the children

除(のぞ)く means to remove, exclude, or eliminate something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the thing being removed or excluded

HOMOPHONE ALERT:

  • 除(のぞ)く (this word): to remove, exclude
  • 覗(のぞ)く: to peek, peep (different kanji and meaning)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を除(のぞ)いて: excluding ~, except for ~
  • 〜を除(のぞ)く: to remove ~
  • 〜を除(のぞ)けば: if we exclude ~

EXAMPLE CONTEXTS:

  • 汚(よご)れを除(のぞ)く: to remove dirt
  • 例外(れいがい)を除(のぞ)く: to exclude exceptions
  • 彼(かれ)を除(のぞ)いて全員(ぜんいん): everyone except him
