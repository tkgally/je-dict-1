1. to raise, to lift up
To move something upward.
手(て)を上(あ)げる。
To raise one's hand.
声(こえ)を上(あ)げる。
To raise one's voice.
旗(はた)を上(あ)げる。
To raise a flag.
効率(こうりつ)を上(あ)げる。
To raise efficiency.
腕(うで)を上(あ)げた。
I improved my skills.
2. to give
To present to someone.
プレゼントを上(あ)げる。
To give a present.
友達(ともだち)に本(ほん)を上(あ)げた。
I gave a book to my friend.
犬(いぬ)に餌(えさ)を上(あ)げる。
To give food to the dog.

To give pocket money to a child.
花(はな)を上(あ)げましょうか。
Shall I give you flowers?

上(あ)げる is a highly versatile verb with multiple meanings and uses.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 上(あ)がる (intransitive, to rise)

MEANINGS:
1. To raise/lift: 手(て)を上(あ)げる (raise one's hand)
2. To give: プレゼントを上(あ)げる (give a present)
3. To improve: 効率(こうりつ)を上(あ)げる (raise efficiency)

AUXILIARY USE:

〜てあげる expresses doing something for someone's benefit (speaker → other):

  • 教(おし)えてあげる: to teach (for someone)
  • 手伝(てつだ)ってあげる: to help (for someone)

NOTE:
When giving to social superiors, use 差(さ)し上(あ)げる instead.

Related Words

Antonym: 下(さ)げる (to lower)
