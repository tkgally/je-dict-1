丸(まる)

まる
noun
circle, round shape
1. circle
A round shape or figure.
2. whole, entire
Used as a prefix meaning complete or whole.
丸(まる)を描(か)く。
To draw a circle.
丸(まる)ごと食(た)べる。
To eat something whole.

丸(まる) means circle or round.

USES:

  • ○: the circle mark used for "correct" in Japan
  • 丸(まる)い: round (adjective)
  • 丸(まる)ごと: whole, entirely

IN NAMES:
The suffix 丸(まる) appears in ship names (e.g., 日本丸(にっぽんまる)) and traditional boys' names.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 真ん丸(まんまる): perfectly round
  • 丸太(まるた): log
