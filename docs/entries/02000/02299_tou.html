1. to ask, to question
To seek an answer or information.
理由(りゆう)を問(と)う。
To ask the reason.
是非(ぜひ)を問(と)う。
To question the rights and wrongs.
年齢(ねんれい)を問(と)わず参加(さんか)できる。
Anyone can participate regardless of age.
～を問(と)わず means 'regardless of ~.'
2. to hold responsible
To question someone's responsibility.
責任(せきにん)を問(と)う。
To hold someone responsible.
事故(じこ)の原因(げんいん)を問(と)われた。
I was questioned about the cause of the accident.
裁判(さいばん)で罪(つみ)を問(と)われる。
To be held accountable for a crime in court.

問(と)う is a formal transitive verb meaning to question, inquire, or hold accountable. More literary than 聞(き)く.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: Xを問(と)う

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 理由(りゆう)を問(と)う: to ask the reason
  • 責任(せきにん)を問(と)う: to hold someone responsible
  • 是非(ぜひ)を問(と)う: to question right and wrong
  • ～を問(と)わず: regardless of ~

COMPOUNDS:

  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire (at a service desk)
  • 問(と)い合(あ)わせ: inquiry

REGISTER:
Formal and often used in written or official contexts. For casual questions, use 聞(き)く or 尋(たず)ねる.
