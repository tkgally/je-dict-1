可愛(かわい)らしい is an i-adjective meaning lovely, sweet, or endearingly cute, with a slightly more refined nuance than かわいい.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 可愛(かわい)らしく (sweetly, endearingly)
  • Noun form: 可愛(かわい)らしさ (sweetness, loveliness)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 可愛(かわい)らしい女(おんな)の子(こ): a sweet girl
  • 可愛(かわい)らしい笑顔(えがお): a lovely smile
  • 可愛(かわい)らしい花(はな): pretty flowers
  • 可愛(かわい)らしい仕草(しぐさ): charming gestures
  • 可愛(かわい)らしい声(こえ): a sweet voice

WORD FORMATION:
かわいい + らしい (suffix indicating 'seeming like') = more emphatic and refined sense of cuteness.

COMPARISON:

  • かわいい: cute (general, casual, versatile)
  • 可愛(かわい)らしい: sweet, endearing (more refined, emphasizes charm)
  • 愛(あい)らしい: lovable, charming (literary)

USAGE NOTE:
可愛(かわい)らしい is often used in more formal or literary contexts and by adults. It implies a genuine appreciation of charm rather than the casual exclamation of かわいい. The kanji spelling 可愛(かわい)らしい is common in writing, while hiragana かわいらしい is also widely used.
