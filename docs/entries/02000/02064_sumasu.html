1. to finish, to complete
To bring something to an end.
宿題(しゅくだい)を済(す)ます。
To finish homework.
用事(ようじ)を済(す)ませてから行(い)く。
I'll go after finishing my errands.
仕事(しごと)を済(す)ませた。
I finished the work.
支払(しはら)いを済(す)ませる。
To complete the payment.
準備(じゅんび)を済(す)ませた。
I finished the preparations.
2. to get by with, to make do
To manage with something simple.
朝食(ちょうしょく)はパンで済(す)ます。
I make do with bread for breakfast.
昼食(ちゅうしょく)はおにぎりで済(す)ます。
I make do with rice balls for lunch.
謝(あやま)らないで済(す)ますつもり？
Do you plan to get by without apologizing?
安(やす)いもので済(す)ませる。
To make do with something cheap.
お金(かね)を払(はら)わないで済(す)ませた。
I got by without paying.

済(す)ます is a godan verb meaning to finish/complete or to get by/make do with something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 済(す)む (intransitive - to be finished, to be enough)

SENSE 1 - TO FINISH/COMPLETE:

  • 宿題(しゅくだい)を済(す)ます: to finish homework
  • 用事(ようじ)を済(す)ます: to finish errands
  • 支払(しはら)いを済(す)ます: to complete payment
  • 準備(じゅんび)を済(す)ませる: to finish preparations

SENSE 2 - TO GET BY WITH:

  • パンで済(す)ます: to make do with bread
  • 安(やす)いもので済(す)ます: to get by with something cheap
  • 〜ないで済(す)ます: to get by without ~
  • 謝(あやま)らないで済(す)ます: to get by without apologizing

ASPECT:

  • 済(す)ませてから: after finishing
  • 済(す)ませた: finished (completed)

RELATED:

  • 済(す)む: to be finished/done; to suffice
  • 終(お)わる/終(お)える: to end/finish (general)
