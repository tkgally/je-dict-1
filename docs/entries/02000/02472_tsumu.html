摘(つ)む is a godan verb meaning to pluck or pick with the fingers.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 〜を摘(つ)む

ASPECT (ている):
摘(つ)んでいる describes the ongoing action of picking.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花(はな)を摘(つ)む: to pick flowers
  • 茶(ちゃ)を摘(つ)む: to pick tea leaves
  • 芽(め)を摘(つ)む: to nip buds / nip in the bud (also figurative)
  • いちごを摘(つ)む: to pick strawberries

KANJI DISTINCTION (same reading):

  • 摘(つ)む: to pluck, pick
  • 積(つ)む: to pile up, stack
  • 詰(つ)む: to be stuck, checkmated
