摘(つ)む

つむ
godan verb
to pluck, to pick, to trim
1. to pluck, to pick
To remove something by pinching with fingers.
花(はな)を摘(つ)む。
To pick flowers.
庭(にわ)でいちごを摘(つ)んだ。
I picked strawberries in the garden.
春(はる)になると、子供(こども)たちは野原(のはら)で草(くさ)を摘(つ)んで遊(あそ)ぶ。
When spring comes, children play by picking wild plants in the fields.
茶(ちゃ)を摘(つ)む季節(きせつ)になると、畑(はたけ)は緑(みどり)でいっぱいになる。
When the tea-picking season comes, the fields are full of green.
朝(あさ)早(はや)く起(お)きて、露(つゆ)がついたままの花(はな)を一(ひと)つずつ丁寧(ていねい)に摘(つ)んだ。
I got up early in the morning and carefully picked the flowers one by one while they still had dew on them.
2. to trim, to nip
To cut off the tips of plants.
芽(め)を摘(つ)む。
To nip buds.
枝(えだ)の先(さき)を摘(つ)んで整(ととの)える。
To trim the tips of branches to tidy up.
トマトの脇芽(わきめ)を摘(つ)まないと、実(み)が小(ちい)さくなる。
If you don't pinch off the side shoots of tomatoes, the fruit will be small.
花(はな)が咲(さ)く前(まえ)に余分(よぶん)なつぼみを摘(つ)んでおくと、残(のこ)った花(はな)が大(おお)きく咲(さ)く。
If you pinch off the extra buds before they bloom, the remaining flowers will bloom larger.
問題(もんだい)の芽(め)は早(はや)めに摘(つ)んでおいたほうがいい。
It's better to nip problems in the bud early.
Figurative use of 芽(め)を摘(つ)む

摘(つ)む is a godan verb meaning to pluck or pick with the fingers.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 〜を摘(つ)む

ASPECT (ている):
摘(つ)んでいる describes the ongoing action of picking.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 花(はな)を摘(つ)む: to pick flowers
  • 茶(ちゃ)を摘(つ)む: to pick tea leaves
  • 芽(め)を摘(つ)む: to nip buds / nip in the bud (also figurative)
  • いちごを摘(つ)む: to pick strawberries

KANJI DISTINCTION (same reading):

  • 摘(つ)む: to pluck, pick
  • 積(つ)む: to pile up, stack
  • 詰(つ)む: to be stuck, checkmated
