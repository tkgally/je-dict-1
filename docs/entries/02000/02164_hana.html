鼻(はな)

はな
noun
nose
1. nose
The organ of smell and breathing on the face.
鼻(はな)が高(たか)い。
To have a high nose; to be proud.
鼻(はな)をかむ。
To blow one's nose.

鼻(はな) refers to the nose.

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:

  • 鼻(はな): nose
  • 花(はな): flower

Distinguished by pitch accent.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 鼻(はな)が高(たか)い: to be proud
  • 鼻(はな)を折(お)る: to humble someone
  • 鼻(はな)で笑(わら)う: to sneer at
  • 鼻(はな)が利(き)く: to have a good sense of smell
