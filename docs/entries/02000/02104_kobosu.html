こぼす

こぼす
verb (godan)
to spill, to grumble
1. to spill
To cause liquid or small objects to fall out of a container.
2. to grumble, to complain
To express dissatisfaction.
コーヒーをこぼした。
I spilled the coffee.
愚痴(ぐち)をこぼす。
To grumble complaints.

こぼす is transitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • こぼす (transitive): to spill
  • こぼれる (intransitive): to spill, to overflow

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 涙(なみだ)をこぼす: to shed tears
  • 愚痴(ぐち)をこぼす: to grumble
