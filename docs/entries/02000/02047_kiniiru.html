気(き)に入(い)る

きにいる
expression; verb (godan)
to like, to be pleased with
1. to like, to be pleased with
To find something or someone appealing.
この服(ふく)が気(き)に入(い)った。
I liked these clothes.
彼女(かのじょ)は気(き)に入(い)らなかった。
She didn't like it.
お気(き)に入(い)りの場所(ばしょ)。
A favorite place.

気(き)に入(い)る is a common expression meaning to like or be pleased with something.

COMPONENTS:

  • 気(き): feeling, spirit
  • に: into
  • 入(い)る: to enter

Literally: "enters one's feeling" = pleases

FORMS:

  • 気(き)に入(い)る: to like (plain)
  • 気(き)に入(い)った: liked (past)
  • 気(き)に入(い)らない: to not like
  • お気(き)に入(い)り: favorite (noun form)

GRAMMAR:

  • What is liked takes が: この服(ふく)が気(き)に入(い)った
  • Person who likes can be marked with は or は topic

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お気(き)に入(い)りの〜: favorite ~
  • 気(き)に入(い)ってもらえると嬉(うれ)しい: I'd be happy if you like it
