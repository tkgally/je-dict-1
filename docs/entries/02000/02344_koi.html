濃(こ)い

こい
i-adjective
thick, dense, strong
1. thick, dense
Having high concentration or intensity.
2. dark, deep (color)
Rich or intense in color.
3. strong (flavor)
Having a pronounced taste.
濃いコーヒー
Strong coffee
濃い色(いろ)
A dark color

濃い is opposite of 薄(うす)い.

COMMON USES:

  • 濃い味(あじ): strong flavor
  • 関係(かんけい)が濃い: close relationship
  • 化粧(けしょう)が濃い: heavy makeup

OPPOSITE:

  • 薄(うす)い: thin, light, weak
general
