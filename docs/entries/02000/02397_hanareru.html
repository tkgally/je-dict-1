離(はな)れる

はなれる
ichidan verb
to leave, to be separated

Definitions

1. to leave, to go away from
To move away from a place or person.
2. to be separated, to be apart
To become distant from.

Examples

家(いえ)を離れる。
To leave home.
席(せき)を離れないでください。
Please don't leave your seat.

Notes

離れる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 離(はな)す (transitive): to separate
  • 離れる (intransitive): to be separated

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)が離せない: can't take one's eyes off
  • 手(て)が離れる: to become free (of duties)
