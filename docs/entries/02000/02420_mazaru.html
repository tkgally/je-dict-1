混(ま)ざる

まざる
godan verb
to be mixed, to mingle

Definitions

1. to be mixed, to be blended
To become combined with something else.

Examples

砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に混ざった。
The sugar mixed into the water.
赤(あか)と青(あお)が混ざって紫(むらさき)になった。
Red and blue mixed to make purple.

Notes

混ざる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 混(ま)ぜる (transitive): to mix
  • 混ざる (intransitive): to be mixed

ALTERNATE KANJI:

  • 交(ま)ざる: to mingle (with people)
