1. to be mixed, to be blended
To become combined with something else.
色(いろ)が混(ま)ざる。
The colors blend.
砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に混(ま)ざった。
The sugar mixed into the water.
赤(あか)と青(あお)が混(ま)ざって紫(むらさき)になった。
Red and blue mixed to make purple.

混(ま)ざる is a godan verb meaning to be mixed or blended.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 混(ま)ぜる (transitive - to mix)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AがBに混(ま)ざる: A mixes into B
  • AとBが混(ま)ざる: A and B mix together

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 色(いろ)が混(ま)ざる: colors blend
  • 砂糖(さとう)が水(みず)に混(ま)ざる: sugar dissolves into water
  • 人(ひと)に混(ま)ざる: to mingle with people

ALTERNATE KANJI:

  • 混(ま)ざる: to be mixed (substances)
  • 交(ま)ざる: to mingle (people, used interchangeably)

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 混(ま)じる: to be mixed (slightly more literary)
  • 混(こ)む: to be crowded (different reading)
