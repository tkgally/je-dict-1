省(はぶ)く

はぶく
godan verb
to omit, to eliminate
1. to omit, to leave out
To not include something.
詳細(しょうさい)は省く。
I'll omit the details.
2. to reduce, to cut down
To decrease effort, cost, or time.
手間(てま)を省く。
To save effort.

省(はぶ)く means to omit, leave out, or reduce unnecessary elements.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle

TWO NUANCES:
1. To omit/leave out: 詳細(しょうさい)を省(はぶ)く (omit details)
2. To save/reduce: 手間(てま)を省(はぶ)く (save effort)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手間(てま)を省(はぶ)く: to save effort/time
  • 無駄(むだ)を省(はぶ)く: to eliminate waste
  • 説明(せつめい)を省(はぶ)く: to skip explanations
  • 経費(けいひ)を省(はぶ)く: to cut expenses
  • 時間(じかん)を省(はぶ)く: to save time

RELATED WORDS:

  • 省略(しょうりゃく)する: to abbreviate, omit (more formal)
  • 省(はぶ)ける: can be omitted (potential form)
  • 手抜(てぬ)き: cutting corners
