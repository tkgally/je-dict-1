漏(も)れる

もれる
ichidan verb
to leak

Definitions

1. to leak, to escape
For liquid, gas, or information to get out.

Examples

水(みず)が漏れている。
Water is leaking.

Notes

漏れる is intransitive.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 漏(も)らす (transitive): to leak
  • 漏れる (intransitive): to leak

FIGURATIVE:

  • 情報(じょうほう)が漏れる: information leaks
general
