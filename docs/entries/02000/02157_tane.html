種(たね)

たね
noun
seed, source, material
1. seed
The unit of reproduction in plants.
花(はな)の種(たね)を蒔(ま)く。
To sow flower seeds.
2. source, cause
The origin or cause of something.
悩(なや)みの種(たね)になる。
To become a source of worry.
3. material, topic
Subject matter for discussion or creation.

種(たね) has both literal (seed) and figurative (source/topic) meanings.

LITERAL USES (seeds):

  • 種(たね)を蒔(ま)く: to sow seeds
  • 種(たね)を取(と)る: to save/harvest seeds
  • ひまわりの種(たね): sunflower seeds
  • 花(はな)の種(たね): flower seeds

FIGURATIVE USES (source/cause):

  • 話(はなし)の種(たね): topic of conversation
  • 笑(わら)いの種(たね): source of laughter
  • 心配(しんぱい)の種(たね): cause of worry
  • 悩(なや)みの種(たね): source of trouble
  • ニュースの種(たね): news material

IDIOM:
種(たね)も仕掛(しか)けもない: no tricks (literally 'no seeds or mechanisms' - used by magicians)

RELATED:

  • 苗(なえ): seedling (plant that has sprouted)
  • 実(み): fruit, seed (of fruit)
