1. to fear, to be afraid of
To feel fear towards something.
失敗(しっぱい)を恐(おそ)れる。
To fear failure.
何(なに)も恐(おそ)れることはない。
There's nothing to be afraid of.
批判(ひはん)を恐(おそ)れずに言(い)う。
To speak without fearing criticism.
2. to be worried about
To be concerned about a possible outcome.
遅刻(ちこく)を恐(おそ)れて早(はや)く出(で)た。
I left early, worried about being late.
台風(たいふう)の被害(ひがい)が恐(おそ)れられている。
There are concerns about typhoon damage.
専門家(せんもんか)は地震(じしん)がさらに大(おお)きくなることを恐(おそ)れている。
Experts are worried that the earthquake could grow even larger.

恐(おそ)れる expresses deep or serious fear, often about consequences or abstract threats.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the object of fear

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を恐(おそ)れる: to fear ~
  • 〜を恐(おそ)れずに: without fearing ~
  • 恐(おそ)れることはない: there's nothing to fear
  • 〜を恐(おそ)れて: fearing ~, because of fear of ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怖(こわ)い: scared, scary (more casual, immediate fear)
  • 恐(おそ)ろしい: frightening, terrifying (describes the source)
  • 恐(おそ)れ: fear (noun form)

USAGE:
Often used for abstract fears (failure, criticism, future). 怖(こわ)い is more common for immediate, physical fears.
