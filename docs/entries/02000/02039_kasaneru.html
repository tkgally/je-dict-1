重(かさ)ねる

かさねる
verb (ichidan)
to pile up, to stack, to repeat

Definitions

1. to pile up, to stack
To place things one on top of another.
2. to repeat, to accumulate
To do something repeatedly over time.

Examples

皿(さら)を重(かさ)ねる。
To stack plates.
努力(どりょく)を重(かさ)ねる。
To make repeated efforts.
年(とし)を重(かさ)ねる。
To grow older.
Lit: to pile up years

Notes

重(かさ)ねる is transitive (you pile things up).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 重(かさ)ねる (transitive): to stack, pile up
  • 重(かさ)なる (intransitive): to pile up

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)を重(かさ)ねる: to gain experience
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)いを重(かさ)ねる: to have repeated discussions
