積(つ)む

つむ
godan verb, transitive
to pile up, to load, to accumulate
1. to pile up, to stack
To place things on top of each other.
雪(ゆき)が積(つ)もる。
Snow piles up.
Note: 積もる is intransitive
2. to load, to pack
To put cargo onto a vehicle.
荷物(にもつ)を車(くるま)に積(つ)む。
To load luggage onto a car.
3. to accumulate
To build up experience or achievements.
経験(けいけん)を積(つ)む。
To accumulate experience.

積(つ)む means to pile up, stack, load, or accumulate—physically stacking things or figuratively building up experience.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 積(つ)もる (intransitive, to pile up by itself)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を積(つ)む: to load luggage
  • 経験(けいけん)を積(つ)む: to gain/accumulate experience
  • 練習(れんしゅう)を積(つ)む: to build up practice
  • 努力(どりょく)を積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる: to make sustained effort

COMPOUNDS:

  • 積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる: to pile up, accumulate
  • 積(つ)み込(こ)む: to load (onto vehicle)
  • 山積(やまづ)み: piled high like a mountain
