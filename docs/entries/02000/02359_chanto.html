ちゃんと is a colloquial adverb meaning "properly" or "correctly." Very common in everyday speech.

MEANINGS:
1. Properly, correctly (doing something the right way)
2. Reliably, without fail (dependably)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ちゃんと食(た)べる: to eat properly
  • ちゃんと聞(き)く: to listen properly
  • ちゃんと来(く)る: to come without fail
  • ちゃんとした: proper, decent (modifies nouns)

SIMILAR ADVERBS:

  • きちんと: properly, neatly (slightly more formal)
  • しっかり: firmly, thoroughly
  • きっちり: precisely, exactly

REGISTER:
Colloquial. Often used by parents speaking to children, but common among all ages in casual speech.

EXAMPLE CONTEXTS:

  • ちゃんとした服(ふく): proper clothes
  • ちゃんとした仕事(しごと): a proper job
  • ちゃんとした大人(おとな): a responsible adult
