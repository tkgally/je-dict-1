1. to cross, to go over; to get through (a period or difficulty)
To pass over something like a mountain or a border. By extension, to come out the other side of a stretch of time or a hardship: 冬(ふゆ)⟦を→を：00422_wo⟧越(こ)す (to get through the winter), 危機(きき)⟦を→を：00422_wo⟧越(こ)す (to get past a crisis).
山(やま)を越(こ)す。
To cross a mountain.
冬(ふゆ)を越(こ)す。
To get through winter.
国境(こっきょう)を越(こ)す。
To cross a border.
峠(とうげ)を越(こ)した。
I crossed the mountain pass.
川(かわ)を越(こ)す。
To cross a river.
危機(きき)を越(こ)した。
I overcame the crisis.
2. to move (residence)
To relocate to a new home.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)す。
To move (to a new residence).
Common compound
来月(らいげつ)引(ひ)っ越(こ)す。
I'm moving next month.
新(あたら)しいアパートに越(こ)した。
I moved to a new apartment.
田舎(いなか)に越(こ)した。
I moved to the countryside.
子供(こども)が生(う)まれるので、広(ひろ)い家(いえ)に越(こ)すことにした。
Since we're having a baby, we decided to move to a bigger house.
3. to exceed, to surpass
To go beyond a certain point or amount.
百万(ひゃくまん)円(えん)を越(こ)す。
To exceed one million yen.
予算(よさん)を越(こ)した。
It exceeded the budget.
記録(きろく)を越(こ)した。
I surpassed the record.
期待(きたい)を越(こ)す成果(せいか)。
Results that exceed expectations.
先輩(せんぱい)を越(こ)す。
To surpass one's seniors.

越(こ)す means to cross over, move past, or exceed something.

TRANSITIVITY:
Can be both transitive and intransitive depending on usage.

THREE MAIN MEANINGS:
1. To cross/pass over: 山(やま)を越(こ)す (cross a mountain)
2. To move residence: 新(あたら)しい家(いえ)に越(こ)す
3. To exceed: 予算(よさん)を越(こ)す (exceed the budget)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す: to move (residence)
  • 乗(の)り越(こ)す: to ride past one's stop
  • 追(お)い越(こ)す: to overtake, pass by
  • 年(とし)を越(こ)す: to see the New Year in

CONTRAST WITH 超(こ)える:
Both mean "to exceed," but 越(こ)す emphasizes physical crossing while 超(こ)える emphasizes surpassing limits.

Related Words

Related: 越(こ)える (same kanji, -eru form)
Contrast: 超(こ)える (exceeding a limit rather than crossing)
