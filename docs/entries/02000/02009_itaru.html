至(いた)る

いたる
verb (godan)
to reach, to arrive at, to lead to

Definitions

1. to reach, to arrive at
To come to a place or point.
2. to lead to, to result in
To eventually result in something.

Examples

この道(みち)は海(うみ)に至(いた)る。
This road leads to the sea.
現在(げんざい)に至(いた)るまで続(つづ)いている。
It has continued up to the present.
合意(ごうい)に至(いた)った。
We reached an agreement.

Notes

至(いた)る is a somewhat formal verb meaning to reach or lead to.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜に至(いた)る: to reach ~
  • 〜に至(いた)るまで: up to ~, until ~
  • 至(いた)る所(ところ): everywhere

NOTE:

  • More formal/literary than 着(つ)く or 届(とど)く
  • Often used in written Japanese
