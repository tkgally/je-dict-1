増(ま)す

ます
godan verb
to increase, to grow
1. to increase, to grow
To become greater in amount or degree.
勢(いきお)いが増す。
The momentum increases.
雨(あめ)が増してきた。
The rain is getting heavier.

増(ま)す is a godan verb meaning "to increase" or "to grow." Can be transitive or intransitive.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Can be both transitive and intransitive
  • Compare: 増(ふ)える (intransitive), 増(ふ)やす (transitive) - more common in everyday speech

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勢(いきお)いを増(ま)す: to gain momentum
  • 重要性(じゅうようせい)を増(ま)す: to grow in importance
  • 速度(そくど)を増(ま)す: to increase speed
  • 輝(かがや)きを増(ま)す: to shine more brightly

REGISTER:
増(ま)す is more literary/formal than 増(ふ)える. Common in written language and news.

RELATED:

  • 減(へ)る: to decrease (opposite)
  • ますます: more and more, increasingly
