月(つき)

つき
noun
moon, month
1. moon
The natural satellite of Earth.
2. month
One of the twelve divisions of a year.
月(つき)が綺麗(きれい)だ。
The moon is beautiful.
来月(らいげつ)引(ひ)っ越(こ)す。
I'm moving next month.

月(つき) means moon and month.

MOON PHASES:

  • 満月(まんげつ): full moon
  • 新月(しんげつ): new moon
  • 三日月(みかづき): crescent moon

MONTHS:
一月(いちがつ) through 十二月(じゅうにがつ).

CULTURE:
月見(つきみ): moon viewing, especially in autumn.
