裁(さば)く

さばく
verb (godan)
to judge, to handle, to deal with
1. to judge, to try
To make a legal judgment on a case.
罪(つみ)を裁(さば)く。
To judge a crime.
2. to handle, to deal with
To manage or process things skillfully.
大量(たいりょう)の仕事(しごと)を裁(さば)く。
To handle a large amount of work.

裁(さば)く has two main uses: to judge legally and to handle/process things skillfully.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: Xを裁(さば)く

LEGAL MEANING:

  • 罪(つみ)を裁(さば)く: to judge a crime
  • 裁判(さいばん)で裁(さば)く: to try in court
  • 神(かみ)が裁(さば)く: God judges

HANDLING MEANING:

  • 仕事(しごと)を裁(さば)く: to handle work efficiently
  • 大量(たいりょう)の注文(ちゅうもん)を裁(さば)く: to process large orders

RELATED:

  • 裁判(さいばん): trial, court case
  • 裁判官(さいばんかん): judge
