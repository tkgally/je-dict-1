避(さ)ける

さける
verb (ichidan)
to avoid, to evade

Definitions

1. to avoid
To keep away from something.
2. to evade, to dodge
To escape from something by moving aside.

Examples

危険(きけん)を避(さ)ける。
To avoid danger.
彼(かれ)は質問(しつもん)を避(さ)けた。
He avoided the question.

Notes

避(さ)ける means to avoid.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid
  • 裂(さ)ける: to tear, to split

SIMILAR:

  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid (conscious choice)
  • 逃(に)げる: to escape, to run away
