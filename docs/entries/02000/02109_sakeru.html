避(さ)ける

さける
verb (ichidan)
to avoid, to evade
1. to avoid
To keep away from something.
危険(きけん)を避(さ)ける。
To avoid danger.
彼(かれ)は質問(しつもん)を避(さ)けた。
He avoided the question.
2. to evade, to dodge
To escape from something by moving aside.

避(さ)ける is an ichidan verb meaning to avoid or evade.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 〜を避(さ)ける

ASPECT (ている):
避(さ)けている describes ongoing avoidance (he's been avoiding me).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 危険(きけん)を避(さ)ける: to avoid danger
  • 質問(しつもん)を避(さ)ける: to avoid a question
  • 人混(ひとご)みを避(さ)ける: to avoid crowds
  • 目(め)を避(さ)ける: to avert one's eyes

DON'T CONFUSE (same reading):

  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid
  • 裂(さ)ける: to tear, to split (intransitive)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid (conscious choice)
  • 逃(に)げる: to escape, run away (physical flight)
