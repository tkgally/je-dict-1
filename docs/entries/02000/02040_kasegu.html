1. to earn, to make money
To receive money in exchange for work.
お金(かね)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To earn money.
アルバイトで稼(かせ)いでいる。
I'm earning money through part-time work.
生活費(せいかつひ)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To earn a living.
夏休(なつやす)みにたくさん稼(かせ)いだ。
I earned a lot during summer vacation.
月(つき)に20万円(まんえん)稼(かせ)いでいる。
I earn 200,000 yen a month.
家族(かぞく)のために稼(かせ)ぐ。
To earn money for one's family.
2. to gain (time, points)
To acquire something through effort.
時間(じかん)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To buy time.
点(てん)を稼(かせ)いだ。
I scored points.
距離(きょり)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To cover distance.
経験(けいけん)を稼(かせ)ぐ。
To gain experience.
修理(しゅうり)が終(お)わるまで時間(じかん)を稼(かせ)がなければならない。
We need to buy time until the repairs are done.

稼(かせ)ぐ is a godan verb meaning to earn money or gain something through effort.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: Xを稼(かせ)ぐ (to earn X)

ASPECT (ている):
稼(かせ)いでいる indicates ongoing earning: アルバイトで稼(かせ)いでいる (I'm earning through part-time work).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お金(かね)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to earn money
  • 生活費(せいかつひ)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to earn a living
  • 時間(じかん)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to buy time (idiomatic)
  • 点(てん)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to score points
  • 外貨(がいか)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to earn foreign currency

CONTRAST:

  • 稼(かせ)ぐ emphasizes active earning through work
  • 儲(もう)ける emphasizes profit, making money (business)
  • 得(え)る is more general 'to obtain'

RELATED:

  • 稼(かせ)ぎ: earnings, income (noun)
  • 稼働(かどう): operation (machines working)
