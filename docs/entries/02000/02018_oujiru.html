応(おう)じる

おうじる
verb (ichidan)
to respond, to comply, to adapt
1. to respond, to answer
To react or reply to something.
2. to comply with, to meet
To act in accordance with a request or demand.
3. to adapt to, according to
To adjust based on circumstances.
要求(ようきゅう)に応(おう)じる。
To comply with a demand.
状況(じょうきょう)に応(おう)じて対応(たいおう)する。
To respond according to the situation.
招待(しょうたい)に応(おう)じた。
I accepted the invitation.

応(おう)じる means to respond or adapt.

PATTERNS:

  • 〜に応(おう)じる: to respond to ~
  • 〜に応(おう)じて: according to ~

COMMON USES:

  • 要求(ようきゅう)に応(おう)じる: to meet demands
  • 期待(きたい)に応(おう)じる: to meet expectations

ALTERNATIVE:

  • 応(おう)ずる: classical form
