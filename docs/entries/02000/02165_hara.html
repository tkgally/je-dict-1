腹(はら)

はら
noun
belly, stomach, abdomen
1. belly, stomach
The front part of the body containing the digestive organs.
腹(はら)が減(へ)った。
I'm hungry.
2. mind, heart
One's true feelings or intentions (figurative).
腹(はら)が立(た)つ。
To get angry.

腹(はら) means belly but has many figurative uses.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 腹(はら)が減(へ)る: to be hungry
  • 腹(はら)が立(た)つ: to get angry
  • 腹(はら)を割(わ)って話(はな)す: to speak frankly
  • 腹(はら)を決(き)める: to make up one's mind
  • 腹黒(はらぐろ)い: scheming, malicious

COMPOUNDS:

  • 空腹(くうふく): hunger
  • 満腹(まんぷく): full stomach
