煮(に)える

にえる
ichidan verb
to boil, to be cooked

Definitions

1. to boil, to be cooked
To reach a state of being fully cooked in liquid.

Examples

野菜(やさい)が煮えた。
The vegetables are cooked.
まだ煮えていない。
It's not cooked yet.

Notes

煮える is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 煮(に)る (transitive): to cook, boil
  • 煮える (intransitive): to be cooked

IDIOM:

  • 煮(に)ても焼(や)いても食(く)えない: impossible to deal with
