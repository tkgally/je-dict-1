持(も)ち上(あ)げる

もちあげる
ichidan verb
to lift up, to raise
1. to lift up, to raise
To elevate something physically.
重(おも)い荷物(にもつ)を持ち上げる。
To lift up heavy luggage.
2. to flatter, to praise excessively
To speak highly of someone (often to gain favor).
上司(じょうし)を持ち上げる。
To flatter one's boss.

持(も)ち上(あ)げる is a compound verb meaning to lift up physically or to flatter.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the object

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:
持(も)つ (to hold) + 上(あ)げる (to raise) = to lift up

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を持(も)ち上(あ)げる: to lift luggage
  • 箱(はこ)を持(も)ち上(あ)げる: to lift a box
  • 人(ひと)を持(も)ち上(あ)げる: to flatter someone
  • おだてて持(も)ち上(あ)げる: to butter someone up

FIGURATIVE USE:
The "flattery" meaning comes from the image of elevating someone with praise. Often used critically to describe insincere praise or sycophantic behavior.
