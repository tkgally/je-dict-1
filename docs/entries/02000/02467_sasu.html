注(さ)す

さす
godan verb
to pour (drinks), to add
1. to pour, to add (liquid)
To pour a small amount of liquid, especially when serving drinks.
目薬(めぐすり)を注(さ)す。
To put in eye drops.

注(さ)す means to pour or apply liquid in small amounts.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

KANJI DISTINCTION:

Several verbs are read さす but use different kanji:

  • 注(さ)す: to pour, apply (liquid)
  • 刺(さ)す: to stab, pierce
  • 指(さ)す: to point at
  • 挿(さ)す: to insert
  • 差(さ)す: to hold up (umbrella), to shine (light)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 目薬(めぐすり)を注(さ)す: to put in eye drops
  • 油(あぶら)を注(さ)す: to oil, lubricate
  • お酒(さけ)を注(さ)す: to pour sake (for someone)
  • 水(みず)を注(さ)す: to add water, to throw cold water on (figuratively)

USAGE NOTE:
In the context of serving drinks, especially sake, 注(さ)す implies pouring for someone else as a gesture of hospitality.
