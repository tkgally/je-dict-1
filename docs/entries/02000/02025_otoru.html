劣(おと)る

おとる
verb (godan)
to be inferior, to fall behind
1. to be inferior to, to be worse than
To be of lower quality or ability than something else.
品質(ひんしつ)が劣(おと)る。
The quality is inferior.
彼(かれ)に劣(おと)らない。
I'm not inferior to him.
どちらも劣(おと)らず素晴(すば)らしい。
Both are equally wonderful.
Lit: wonderful without being inferior

劣(おと)る means to be inferior.

PATTERNS:

  • 〜に劣(おと)る: to be inferior to ~
  • 〜に劣(おと)らず: not inferior to ~
  • 勝(まさ)るとも劣(おと)らない: at least as good as

OPPOSITE:

  • 優(すぐ)れる: to excel, be superior
  • 勝(まさ)る: to surpass
general
