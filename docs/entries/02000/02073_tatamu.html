1. to fold, to fold up
To bend something flat.
傘(かさ)を畳(たた)む。
To fold an umbrella.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を畳(たた)む。
To fold laundry.
布団(ふとん)を畳(たた)む。
To fold up a futon.
2. to close, to shut down (business)
To end a business or activity.
店(みせ)を畳(たた)む。
To close down a shop.
会社(かいしゃ)を畳(たた)む決断(けつだん)をした。
I made the decision to shut down the company.
商売(しょうばい)を畳(たた)んで田舎(いなか)に帰(かえ)った。
I closed the business and returned to the countryside.

畳(たた)む is a godan verb with two distinct meanings: to fold flat objects, and to close/shut down a business.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle

SENSE 1 - TO FOLD:

  • 傘(かさ)を畳(たた)む: to close/fold an umbrella
  • 布団(ふとん)を畳(たた)む: to fold up a futon
  • 洗濯物(せんたくもの)を畳(たた)む: to fold laundry
  • 服(ふく)を畳(たた)む: to fold clothes
  • 地図(ちず)を畳(たた)む: to fold a map

SENSE 2 - TO CLOSE (business):

  • 店(みせ)を畳(たた)む: to close down a shop
  • 会社(かいしゃ)を畳(たた)む: to shut down a company
  • 商売(しょうばい)を畳(たた)む: to close a business

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 畳(たた) is the same as 畳(たたみ) (tatami mat), reflecting the folding/layering nature of traditional Japanese flooring.
