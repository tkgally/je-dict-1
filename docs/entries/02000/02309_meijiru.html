命(めい)じる

めいじる
ichidan verb
to order, to command
1. to order, to command
To give an official order or instruction.
部下(ぶか)に仕事(しごと)を命じる。
To order a subordinate to do a task.
社長(しゃちょう)に命じられた。
I was ordered by the company president.
2. to appoint
To assign someone to a position.

命じる is formal and used in official or hierarchical contexts.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 命令(めいれい)する: to give an order (noun + suru)
  • 指示(しじ)する: to instruct
  • 頼(たの)む: to ask (less formal)

ALTERNATE FORM:

  • 命ずる (classical/literary)
general
