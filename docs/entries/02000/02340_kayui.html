かゆい

かゆい
i-adjective
itchy
1. itchy
Having an irritating sensation that makes one want to scratch.
背中(せなか)がかゆい。
My back is itchy.
かゆいところに手(て)が届(とど)く。
To be attentive to every need. (Idiom)

かゆい is often written in hiragana.

KANJI:

  • 痒(かゆ)い (less common)

RELATED:

  • かゆみ: itchiness (noun)
  • 掻(か)く: to scratch

IDIOM:

  • 痒い所(ところ)に手(て)が届(とど)く: meticulous, thorough service
general
