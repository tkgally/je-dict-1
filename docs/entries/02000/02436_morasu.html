1. to leak, to let out
To allow to escape.
水(みず)を漏(も)らす。
To leak water.
ため息(いき)を漏(も)らした。
I let out a sigh.
思(おも)わず不満(ふまん)を漏(も)らしてしまった。
I accidentally let my dissatisfaction slip.
2. to divulge, to let slip
To reveal information.
秘密(ひみつ)を漏(も)らす。
To let slip a secret.
情報(じょうほう)を漏(も)らす。
To leak information.
大事(だいじ)な情報(じょうほう)を外部(がいぶ)に漏(も)らしてはいけない。
You must not leak important information to outsiders.

漏(も)らす means to let something escape or slip out, either physically or informationally.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 漏(も)れる (intransitive, something leaks out)

MEANINGS:
1. Physical leaking: 水(みず)を漏(も)らす (to leak water)
2. Information: 秘密(ひみつ)を漏(も)らす (to divulge a secret)
3. Omitting: 名前(なまえ)を漏(も)らす (to leave out a name)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 秘密(ひみつ)を漏(も)らす: to let slip a secret
  • 情報(じょうほう)を漏(も)らす: to leak information
  • 本音(ほんね)を漏(も)らす: to let one's true feelings slip
  • ため息(いき)を漏(も)らす: to let out a sigh
