攻(せ)める

せめる
verb (ichidan)
to attack, to assault
1. to attack, to assault
To take offensive action against an enemy.
敵(てき)を攻(せ)める。
To attack the enemy.
城(しろ)を攻(せ)める。
To attack a castle.

攻(せ)める means to attack.

DON'T CONFUSE (both せめる):

  • 攻(せ)める: to attack
  • 責(せ)める: to blame, to criticize

OPPOSITE:

  • 守(まも)る: to defend

RELATED:

  • 攻撃(こうげき): attack (noun)
general
