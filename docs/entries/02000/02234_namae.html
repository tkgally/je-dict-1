名前(なまえ)

なまえ
noun
name
1. name
A word or set of words by which a person or thing is known.
お名前(なまえ)は何(なん)ですか。
What is your name?
名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name.

名前(なまえ) means name.

JAPANESE NAMES:
In Japanese, family name comes before given name.

RELATED:

  • 名字(みょうじ): surname, family name
  • 下の名前(したのなまえ): first name, given name

COMPOUNDS:

  • 名札(なふだ): name tag
  • 名刺(めいし): business card
