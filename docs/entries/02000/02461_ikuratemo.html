いくら～ても

いくらても
expression
no matter how much

Definitions

1. no matter how much, however much
Expresses that regardless of the degree or amount, the result is the same.

Examples

いくら説明(せつめい)しても分(わ)からない。
No matter how much I explain, they don't understand.

Notes

PATTERN: いくら + verb (て form) + も

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • どんなに～ても: no matter how
  • 何度(なんど)～ても: no matter how many times
