抜(ぬ)ける

ぬける
ichidan verb
to come out, to fall out
1. to come out, to fall out
To become detached or removed.
2. to be missing, to be omitted
To be absent or lacking.
3. to escape, to get through
To pass through or exit.
髪(かみ)の毛(け)が抜ける。
Hair is falling out.
力(ちから)が抜ける。
To lose strength.
トンネルを抜ける。
To get through the tunnel.

抜ける is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 抜(ぬ)く (transitive): to pull out
  • 抜ける (intransitive): to come out

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)が抜ける: to feel let down
  • 腰(こし)が抜ける: legs give way (from shock)
