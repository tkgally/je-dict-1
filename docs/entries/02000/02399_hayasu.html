生(は)やす

はやす
godan verb
to grow (hair, beard)

Definitions

1. to grow, to let grow
To allow hair, beard, or plants to develop.

Examples

ひげを生やす。
To grow a beard.
髪(かみ)を生やす。
To grow one's hair.

Notes

生やす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 生やす (transitive): to grow (deliberately)
  • 生(は)える (intransitive): to grow (naturally)

COMMON USES:

  • ひげを生やす: to grow a beard
  • 爪(つめ)を生やす: to grow nails long
