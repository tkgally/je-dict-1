生(は)やす

はやす
godan verb
to grow (hair, beard)
1. to grow, to let grow
To allow hair, beard, or plants to develop.
ひげを生やす。
To grow a beard.
髪(かみ)を生やす。
To grow one's hair.

生(は)やす is a godan verb meaning to grow (hair, beard, etc.) deliberately.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 生(は)える (intransitive: to grow naturally)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ひげを生(は)やす: to grow a beard
  • 髪(かみ)を生(は)やす: to grow one's hair out
  • 爪(つめ)を生(は)やす: to let one's nails grow long
  • 根(ね)を生(は)やす: to put down roots (also figurative)

NUANCE:
Implies intentional or deliberate growth, unlike 生(は)える which describes natural, spontaneous growth. 生(は)やす suggests agency—the person chooses to let something grow.
