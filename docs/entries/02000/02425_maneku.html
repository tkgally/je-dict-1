招(まね)く

まねく
godan verb
to invite, to beckon
1. to invite, to ask to come
To request someone's presence.
2. to beckon, to wave over
To signal someone to approach.
3. to cause, to bring about
To result in (often negative outcomes).
客(きゃく)を招く。
To invite guests.
手(て)で招く。
To beckon with one's hand.
誤解(ごかい)を招く。
To cause a misunderstanding.

招く has multiple related meanings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 災(わざわ)いを招く: to invite trouble
  • 批判(ひはん)を招く: to invite criticism
  • 招(まね)き猫(ねこ): beckoning cat (lucky charm)
general
