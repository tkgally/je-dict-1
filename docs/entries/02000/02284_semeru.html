責(せ)める

せめる
ichidan verb, transitive
to blame, to criticize, to torture
1. to blame, to criticize
To find fault with someone.
自分(じぶん)を責(せ)める。
To blame oneself.
彼(かれ)を責(せ)めないで。
Don't blame him.
2. to torture, to torment
To cause suffering (physical or mental).

責(せ)める means to blame, criticize, or torment someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 彼(かれ)を責(せ)める

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 自分(じぶん)を責(せ)める: to blame oneself
  • 責(せ)めないで: don't blame (me/them)
  • 責(せ)められる: to be blamed (passive)

NUANCES:
1. Blame/criticize: Finding fault with someone
2. Torment: Causing mental or physical distress

HOMOPHONE DISTINCTION:

  • 責(せ)める: to blame, torment (責 = responsibility)
  • 攻(せ)める: to attack (攻 = attack) - military/sports context

RELATED:

  • 責任(せきにん): responsibility
  • 非難(ひなん)する: to criticize (more formal)
