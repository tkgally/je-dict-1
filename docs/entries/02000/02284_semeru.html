責(せ)める

せめる
ichidan verb, transitive
to blame, to criticize, to torture

Definitions

1. to blame, to criticize
To find fault with someone.
2. to torture, to torment
To cause suffering (physical or mental).

Examples

自分(じぶん)を責(せ)める。
To blame oneself.
彼(かれ)を責(せ)めないで。
Don't blame him.

Notes

責(せ)める involves assigning blame or causing distress.

DISTINCTION FROM 攻(せ)める:

  • 責(せ)める: to blame, torment
  • 攻(せ)める: to attack (military)
