1. good, fine
Of high quality or standard.
天気(てんき)が良(よ)い。
The weather is good.
この店(みせ)は料理(りょうり)が良(よ)い。
This restaurant has good food.
景色(けしき)が良(よ)いですね。
The scenery is beautiful, isn't it?
彼(かれ)は頭(あたま)が良(よ)い。
He is smart.
体(からだ)に良(よ)い食(た)べ物(もの)を食(た)べよう。
Let's eat food that's good for the body.
2. all right, okay
Acceptable or permitted.
もう帰(かえ)っても良(よ)い。
You may go home now.
写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)っても良(よ)いですか。
May I take a photo?
ここに座(すわ)っても良(よ)いですか。
May I sit here?
食(た)べなくても良(よ)い。
You don't have to eat it.
明日(あした)でも良(よ)いですか。
Is tomorrow okay?

良(よ)い is an i-adjective meaning "good." Its irregular alternative いい is the ordinary form in everyday speech.

USAGE:

  • よい is the more formal, written, and older-sounding form; いい dominates conversation
  • よい persists in set phrases and greetings: よいお年(とし)を (have a good new year), 仲(なか)よく (on good terms)
  • Whichever dictionary form is used, everything else conjugates from よ-, never い-

FORMS:

  • Dictionary: よい or いい (いい is more casual)
  • Negative: よくない (NOT いくない)
  • Past: よかった (NOT いかった)
  • Adverb: よく (well, often)

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • 〜てもよい / 〜てもいい: may ~, it's okay to ~
  • 〜ばよかった: should have ~ (regret)
  • 〜なくてもいい: don't have to ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天気(てんき)がいい: good weather
  • 頭(あたま)がいい: smart
  • 体(からだ)にいい: good for the body
  • 仲(なか)がいい: on good terms
  • 運(うん)がいい: lucky

Related Words

Antonym: 悪(わる)い (bad)
