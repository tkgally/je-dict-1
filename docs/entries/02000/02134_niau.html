1. to suit, to look good on
For something to be appropriate for someone's appearance.
その服(ふく)は君(きみ)に似合(にあ)う。
Those clothes suit you.
赤(あか)が彼女(かのじょ)に似合(にあ)っている。
Red looks good on her.
その髪型(かみがた)、似合(にあ)うね。
That hairstyle suits you.
眼鏡(めがね)が似合(にあ)う。
Glasses suit you.
帽子(ぼうし)が似合(にあ)っている。
The hat looks good on you.
彼(かれ)は年齢(ねんれい)に似合(にあ)わず若(わか)く見(み)える。
He looks young for his age.
2. to match, to go well with
For things to harmonize well together.
二人(ふたり)はお似合(にあ)いだ。
The two of them are well matched.
ワインとチーズは似合(にあ)う。
Wine and cheese go well together.
この色(いろ)は部屋(へや)に似合(にあ)う。
This color matches the room.
黒(くろ)い靴(くつ)と黒(くろ)いかばんがよく似合(にあ)っている。
The black shoes and black bag go well together.
あの二人(ふたり)はいつも一緒(いっしょ)にいて、本当(ほんとう)にお似合(にあ)いのカップルだ。
Those two are always together — they're really a well-matched couple.

似合(にあ)う means to suit, match, or look good on someone.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に particle for the person/thing being suited

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に似合(にあ)う: to suit ~, to look good on ~
  • お似合(にあ)いの二人(ふたり): a well-matched couple
  • 年(とし)に似合(にあ)わず: contrary to one's age
  • 〜に似合(にあ)わない: doesn't suit ~

ASPECT (ている):
似合(にあ)っている describes the current state of something suiting well: その服(ふく)、似合(にあ)っているよ (Those clothes look good on you).

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 似合(にあ)い: suitability, match (noun)
  • お似合(にあ)い: well-matched (polite, often for couples)
  • 似合(にあ)いの: suitable, matching (attributive)
