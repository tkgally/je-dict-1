捧(ささ)げる

ささげる
verb (ichidan)
to offer, to dedicate, to devote

Definitions

1. to offer, to present
To hold up something as an offering.
2. to dedicate, to devote
To give one's time or life to something.

Examples

神(かみ)に祈(いの)りを捧(ささ)げる。
To offer prayers to God.
人生(じんせい)を研究(けんきゅう)に捧(ささ)げた。
I devoted my life to research.
犠牲者(ぎせいしゃ)に黙祷(もくとう)を捧(ささ)げる。
To offer a moment of silence for the victims.

Notes

捧(ささ)げる means to offer or devote.

COMMON USES:

  • 祈(いの)りを捧(ささ)げる: to offer prayers
  • 人生(じんせい)を捧(ささ)げる: to devote one's life
  • 敬意(けいい)を捧(ささ)げる: to pay respect

RELATED:

  • 献身(けんしん)する: to dedicate oneself
