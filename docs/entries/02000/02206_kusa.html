草(くさ)

くさ
noun
grass, weed, plant
1. grass
Plants with narrow leaves growing from the base.
草(くさ)の上(うえ)に寝転(ねころ)ぶ。
To lie down on the grass.
2. weed
Unwanted plants growing in gardens.
草(くさ)を抜(ぬ)く。
To pull weeds.
3. herb
Plants used for medicine or flavoring.

草(くさ) refers to grass and plants.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 雑草(ざっそう): weed
  • 薬草(やくそう): medicinal herb
  • 草原(そうげん): grassland
  • 草花(くさばな): flowering plants

SLANG:
草(くさ) or "w" is Japanese internet slang for laughter (like "lol").
