散(ち)る

ちる
verb (godan)
to scatter, to fall (leaves/petals)
1. to scatter, to disperse
To spread in different directions.
落(お)ち葉(ば)が散(ち)る。
Fallen leaves scatter.
2. to fall (leaves, petals)
For leaves or petals to fall from a plant.
桜(さくら)が散(ち)る。
Cherry blossoms fall.

散(ち)る describes things scattering or falling apart, especially petals and leaves.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 散(ち)らす (transitive, to scatter something)

ASPECT (ている):
散(ち)っている indicates a state where petals/leaves have fallen and are scattered.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 桜(さくら)が散(ち)る: cherry blossoms fall
  • 花(はな)びらが散(ち)る: petals scatter
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)が散(ち)る: fallen leaves scatter
  • 人(ひと)が散(ち)る: people disperse

CULTURE:
桜(さくら)が散(ち)る (cherry blossoms falling) is a significant image in Japanese aesthetics, symbolizing the transient beauty of life (無常(むじょう)). The phrase 散(ち)り際(ぎわ) (moment of falling) describes a graceful end.

RELATED:

  • 散歩(さんぽ): a walk (different kanji compound)
  • 散(ち)り散(ぢ)り: scattered about
