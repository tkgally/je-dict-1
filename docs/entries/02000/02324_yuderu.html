茹(ゆ)でる

ゆでる
ichidan verb
to boil
1. to boil
To cook food in boiling water.
卵(たまご)を茹でる。
To boil an egg.
パスタを茹でる。
To boil pasta.

茹(ゆ)でる is a transitive verb.

RELATED:

  • 茹(ゆ)で卵(たまご): boiled egg
  • 茹(ゆ)で上(あ)がる: to finish boiling

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 煮(に)る: to simmer, cook in liquid
  • 蒸(む)す: to steam
