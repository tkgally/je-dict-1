1. to think of, to come up with
To have an idea suddenly come to mind.
いい考(かんが)えを思(おも)い付(つ)いた。
I thought of a good idea.
何(なに)か思(おも)い付(つ)いたら教(おし)えて。
Let me know if you think of something.
急(きゅう)に思(おも)い付(つ)いた。
It suddenly occurred to me.
解決策(かいけつさく)を思(おも)い付(つ)いた。
I came up with a solution.
シャワーを浴(あ)びているときに新(あたら)しいアイデアを思(おも)い付(つ)くことが多(おお)い。
I often come up with new ideas while taking a shower.

思(おも)い付(つ)く is a godan verb meaning to think of or come up with an idea, often suddenly.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • The idea is the subject: いい考(かんが)えが思(おも)い付(つ)いた
  • Can also use を: いい考(かんが)えを思(おも)い付(つ)いた

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いい考(かんが)えを思(おも)い付(つ)く: to think of a good idea
  • 急(きゅう)に思(おも)い付(つ)く: to suddenly occur to one
  • 何(なに)か思(おも)い付(つ)いたら: if you think of something

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 思(おも)い付(つ)き: a (sudden) idea, whim
  • 思(おも)い付(つ)きで: on a whim

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember, recall
  • 気(き)が付(つ)く: to notice, realize
  • 考(かんが)え付(つ)く: to think up, devise

ASPECT (ている):

  • 思(おも)い付(つ)いている: has thought of / has an idea in mind (resultative state)
  • Note: 思(おも)い付(つ)く is a punctual verb — the idea arrives in an instant. The ている form describes the resulting state of already having the idea, not the process of thinking. 何(なに)か思(おも)い付(つ)いている？ is more naturally "Do you have any ideas?" than "Are you in the middle of thinking one up?"
