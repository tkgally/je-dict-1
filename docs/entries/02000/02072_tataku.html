叩(たた)く

たたく
verb (godan)
to hit, to knock, to beat
1. to hit, to strike, to beat
To hit repeatedly or with force.
太鼓(たいこ)を叩(たた)く。
To beat a drum.
2. to knock, to tap
To strike lightly to make a sound.
ドアを叩(たた)く。
To knock on the door.
肩(かた)を叩(たた)く。
To tap someone's shoulder.
3. to criticize harshly
To attack verbally.

叩(たた)く means to hit, knock, or beat. A transitive godan verb with both physical and figurative uses.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドアを叩(たた)く: to knock on a door
  • 太鼓(たいこ)を叩(たた)く: to beat a drum
  • 肩(かた)を叩(たた)く: to pat on the shoulder
  • 手(て)を叩(たた)く: to clap hands
  • キーボードを叩(たた)く: to type (lit. hit the keyboard)

FIGURATIVE USE:

叩(たた)く can mean "to criticize harshly":

  • マスコミに叩(たた)かれる: to be attacked by media
  • ネットで叩(たた)かれる: to be criticized online

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. No common intransitive pair.
