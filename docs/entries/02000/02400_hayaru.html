流行(はや)る

はやる
godan verb
to be popular, to be in fashion
1. to be popular, to be in fashion
To be widely adopted or enjoyed.
この歌(うた)が流行っている。
This song is popular.
2. to spread, to be prevalent
For a disease or trend to become widespread.
インフルエンザが流行っている。
The flu is going around.

流行(はや)る is an intransitive godan verb meaning something becomes popular or widespread.

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) - the subject spreads/becomes popular on its own.

ASPECT (ている):
流行(はや)っている means "is currently popular/going around."

READINGS:

  • 流行(はや)る (verb): to be popular, to spread
  • 流行(りゅうこう) (noun): trend, fashion, epidemic

USES:
1. Positive: trends, fashion, songs, games
2. Negative: diseases, epidemics (流行(はや)り病(やまい))

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 流行(はや)り物(もの): something trendy
  • 流行(りゅうこう)語(ご): buzzword, trendy word
  • 大流行(だいりゅうこう): huge trend, pandemic
