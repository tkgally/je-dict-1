悩(なや)む

なやむ
godan verb
to worry, to be troubled
1. to worry, to be troubled
To experience mental distress over a problem.
将来(しょうらい)のことで悩んでいる。
I'm worried about my future.
2. to suffer from
To be afflicted by (illness, etc.).
頭痛(ずつう)に悩む。
To suffer from headaches.

悩(なや)む means to worry, be troubled, or suffer from something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 悩(なや)ます (transitive - to trouble someone)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 〜で悩(なや)む: to worry about ~ (cause of worry)
  • 〜に悩(なや)む: to suffer from ~ (ongoing affliction)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 将来(しょうらい)のことで悩(なや)む: to worry about the future
  • 人間関係(にんげんかんけい)に悩(なや)む: to struggle with relationships
  • 頭痛(ずつう)に悩(なや)む: to suffer from headaches

RELATED WORDS:

  • 悩(なや)み: worry, trouble (noun)
  • 悩(なや)ましい: troubling, vexing (adjective)
