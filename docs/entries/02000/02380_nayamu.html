1. to worry, to be troubled
To experience mental distress over a problem.
将来(しょうらい)のことで悩(なや)んでいる。
I'm worried about my future.
どうしたらいいか悩(なや)んでいる。
I'm agonizing over what to do.
人間関係(にんげんかんけい)で悩(なや)んでいるなら、誰(だれ)かに相談(そうだん)した方(ほう)がいい。
If you're worried about personal relationships, you should talk to someone about it.
2. to suffer from
To be afflicted by (illness, etc.).
頭痛(ずつう)に悩(なや)む。
To suffer from headaches.
花粉症(かふんしょう)に悩(なや)む人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of people suffering from hay fever is increasing.
長年(ながねん)腰痛(ようつう)に悩(なや)まされてきたが、やっといい治療法(ちりょうほう)が見(み)つかった。
I suffered from back pain for years, but finally found a good treatment.

悩(なや)む means to worry, be troubled, or suffer from something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 悩(なや)ます (transitive - to trouble someone)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 〜で悩(なや)む: to worry about ~ (cause of worry)
  • 〜に悩(なや)む: to suffer from ~ (ongoing affliction)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 将来(しょうらい)のことで悩(なや)む: to worry about the future
  • 人間関係(にんげんかんけい)に悩(なや)む: to struggle with relationships
  • 頭痛(ずつう)に悩(なや)む: to suffer from headaches

RELATED WORDS:

  • 悩(なや)み: worry, trouble (noun)
  • 悩(なや)ましい: troubling, vexing (adjective)

Related Words

Related: 悩(なや)み (worry (noun))
Synonym: 困(こま)る (to be troubled)
