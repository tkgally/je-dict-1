通(とお)す

とおす
godan verb, transitive
to let through, to persist, to continue
1. to let through, to pass
To allow something to pass through.
光(ひかり)を通(とお)す。
To let light through.
Sense 1: let through
水(みず)を通(とお)さない素材(そざい)。
A material that doesn't let water through.
Sense 1: pass
針(はり)に糸(いと)を通(とお)す。
To thread a needle.
Sense 1: pass through
穴(あな)に紐(ひも)を通(とお)す。
To thread a string through a hole.
Sense 1: let through
2. to persist in, to carry through
To continue doing something to the end.
意見(いけん)を通(とお)す。
To get one's opinion accepted.
読(よ)み通(とお)す。
To read through to the end.
自分(じぶん)の考(かんが)えを通(とお)す。
To push through one's ideas.
Sense 2: persist in
最後(さいご)までやり通(とお)す。
To see it through to the end.
Sense 2: carry through
筋(すじ)を通(とお)す。
To be consistent / stand firm.
Sense 2: persist in
我(わ)を通(とお)す。
To have one's way.
Sense 2: carry through
3. to show in
To lead a guest inside.
お客(きゃく)さんを部屋(へや)に通(とお)す。
To show a guest to a room.
お客様(きゃくさま)をお通(とお)しください。
Please show the customer in.
Sense 3: show in
応接室(おうせつしつ)に通(とお)す。
To show to the reception room.
Sense 3: show in
こちらにお通(とお)しします。
I'll show you this way.
Sense 3: lead inside
奥(おく)の部屋(へや)に通(とお)された。
I was shown to the back room.
Sense 3: show in

通(とお)す is a versatile transitive verb with three main meanings: letting something pass through, persisting to completion, and showing someone in.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 通(とお)る (intransitive, to pass through)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 光(ひかり)を通(とお)す: to let light through
  • 針(はり)に糸(いと)を通(とお)す: to thread a needle
  • 意見(いけん)を通(とお)す: to get one's opinion accepted
  • 筋(すじ)を通(とお)す: to be consistent
  • お客(きゃく)さんを通(とお)す: to show a guest in

AS SUFFIX (-通(とお)す):

Attaches to verb stems to mean 'do through to completion':

  • 読(よ)み通(とお)す: to read through entirely
  • やり通(とお)す: to see through to the end
