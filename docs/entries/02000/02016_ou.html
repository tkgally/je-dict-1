1. to chase, to run after
To follow quickly in order to catch.
犬(いぬ)が猫(ねこ)を追(お)った。
The dog chased the cat.
警察(けいさつ)が犯人(はんにん)を追(お)った。
The police chased the criminal.
子供(こども)たちが蝶(ちょう)を追(お)って走(はし)っている。
The children are running after a butterfly.
2. to pursue, to seek
To try to achieve or obtain something.
夢(ゆめ)を追(お)う。
To pursue a dream.
流行(りゅうこう)を追(お)うのは疲(つか)れる。
Chasing trends is tiring.
時代(じだい)を追(お)って変化(へんか)する。
To change with the times.

追(お)う is a godan verb meaning to chase, pursue, or follow.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: ～を追(お)う

COMMON USES:

  • 夢(ゆめ)を追(お)う: to pursue a dream
  • 流行(りゅうこう)を追(お)う: to follow trends
  • 犯人(はんにん)を追(お)う: to chase a criminal
  • 時代(じだい)を追(お)う: to keep up with the times

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 追(お)い掛(か)ける: to run after, to chase
  • 追(お)い出(だ)す: to drive out, to expel
  • 追(お)い越(こ)す: to overtake, to pass
  • 追(お)い付(つ)く: to catch up with

ASPECT:
追(お)っている describes the ongoing state of chasing or pursuing something.
