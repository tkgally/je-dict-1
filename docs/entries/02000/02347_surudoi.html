鋭(するど)い

するどい
i-adjective
sharp, keen
1. sharp, pointed
Having a fine edge or point.
2. keen, acute
Having or showing quick perception.
鋭いナイフ
A sharp knife
鋭い目(め)つき
A sharp look
鋭い指摘(してき)
An astute observation

鋭い has both physical and abstract meanings.

COMMON USES:

  • 鋭い質問(しつもん): a probing question
  • 感覚(かんかく)が鋭い: to have keen senses
  • 勘(かん)が鋭い: to have sharp intuition

OPPOSITE:

  • 鈍(にぶ)い: dull, slow
general
