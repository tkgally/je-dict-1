万引(まんび)き

まんびき
noun
shoplifting
1. shoplifting; pilferage from a store
The act of stealing merchandise from a retail store. Also refers to the person who shoplifts.
万引(まんび)きは犯罪(はんざい)です。
Shoplifting is a crime.
防犯(ぼうはん)カメラで万引(まんび)きの瞬間(しゅんかん)が撮影(さつえい)された。
The moment of shoplifting was captured on a security camera.
万引(まんび)きによる被害額(ひがいがく)は年間(ねんかん)数百億円(すうひゃくおくえん)に上(のぼ)ると言(い)われている。
It is said that losses from shoplifting amount to tens of billions of yen annually.

USAGE:
Also used as a suru-verb: 万引(まんび)きする (to shoplift). Can also refer to the person who shoplifts (万引(まんび)き犯(はん) is more specific). Store signs often use the phrase 万引(まんび)きは犯罪(はんざい)です (shoplifting is a crime).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 万引(まんび)きする (to shoplift)
  • 万引(まんび)き犯(はん) (shoplifter)
  • 万引(まんび)き防止(ぼうし) (shoplifting prevention)
  • 万引(まんび)きで捕(つか)まる (to be caught shoplifting)
