自己推薦(じこすいせん)

じこすいせん
noun
self-recommendation; self-nomination
1. self-recommendation; self-nomination
The act of recommending or nominating oneself, especially for a position, award, or university admission. Common in Japanese educational and professional contexts.
自己推薦(じこすいせん)で大学(だいがく)に応募(おうぼ)した。
I applied to the university through self-recommendation.
自己推薦(じこすいせん)書(しょ)には自分(じぶん)の強(つよ)みを書(か)く。
You write your strengths in the self-recommendation letter.
この大学(だいがく)は自己推薦(じこすいせん)入試(にゅうし)で幅広(はばひろ)い人材(じんざい)を受(う)け入(い)れている。
This university accepts a wide range of applicants through self-recommendation admission.

USAGE:
Commonly used in the context of university admissions in Japan, where 自己推薦入試 is an admission pathway that does not require a recommendation from a teacher or school, unlike 指定校推薦.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己推薦(じこすいせん)書(しょ) (self-recommendation letter)
  • 自己推薦(じこすいせん)入試(にゅうし) (self-recommendation entrance exam)
  • 自己推薦(じこすいせん)で出願(しゅつがん)する (to apply via self-recommendation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推薦(すいせん): recommendation — a recommendation from someone else
  • 指定校推薦(していこうすいせん): designated school recommendation — admission based on a school's recommendation
