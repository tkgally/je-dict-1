一党(いっとう)

いっとう
noun
a party; a faction; a group
1. a party; a faction; a group
A group of people united by a common cause, goal, or allegiance. Often used in political contexts to refer to a political party or faction, and more broadly to any organized group or clique.
一党(いっとう)支配(しはい)が続(つづ)いている。
One-party rule continues.
彼(かれ)の一党(いっとう)は反対派(はんたいは)を抑(おさ)えた。
His faction suppressed the opposition.
一党(いっとう)独裁(どくさい)の体制(たいせい)は民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)とは相容(あいい)れない。
A one-party dictatorship is incompatible with democracy.

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered in the compound 一党支配 (one-party rule) or 一党独裁 (one-party dictatorship). Can also refer to a group or gang in historical or literary contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一党(いっとう)支配(しはい) (one-party rule)
  • 一党(いっとう)独裁(どくさい) (one-party dictatorship)
  • 一党(いっとう)体制(たいせい) (single-party system)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 政党(せいとう): political party — a neutral term for a political party
  • 派閥(はばつ): faction — a faction within a larger organization
