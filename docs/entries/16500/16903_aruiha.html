或(ある)いは

あるいは
conjunction
or; possibly; perhaps; alternatively
1. or; alternatively
Used to present an alternative option or possibility. A formal conjunction connecting two possibilities.
電車(でんしゃ)、或(ある)いはバスで行(い)ける。
You can go by train or by bus.
書面(しょめん)、或(ある)いは口頭(こうとう)でお伝(つた)えします。
I will notify you in writing or verbally.
彼(かれ)の言(い)うことは正(ただ)しいのかもしれない。或(ある)いは間違(まちが)っているかもしれない。
What he says might be right. Or it might be wrong.
2. possibly; perhaps; maybe
Used to suggest something might be the case, expressing uncertainty about which of multiple possibilities is true.
或(ある)いはそうかもしれない。
That might possibly be the case.
或(ある)いは彼(かれ)が犯人(はんにん)ではないだろうか。
Could he possibly be the culprit?
この計画(けいかく)は、或(ある)いは成功(せいこう)するかもしれないと思(おも)い始(はじ)めた。
I began to think that this plan might possibly succeed.

USAGE:
Also written あるいは in hiragana. The kanji 或(ある)いは is more formal. Sense 1 ("or") is more common in modern Japanese. In terms of formality for "or": もしくは > 或(ある)いは > または > か.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • または: or — slightly less formal, most common "or" in writing
  • もしくは: or — the most formal option
  • もしかしたら: perhaps — more common for "maybe" in conversation
