礼拝(れいはい)

れいはい
noun / verb (suru)
worship; religious service
1. worship; religious service; prayer
A religious ceremony or act of worship. Used for Christian church services as well as worship in other religions.
日曜日(にちようび)に礼拝(れいはい)に行(い)く。
I go to church on Sundays.
礼拝(れいはい)は午前(ごぜん)十時(じゅうじ)に始(はじ)まる。
The service starts at ten in the morning.
小(ちい)さな教会(きょうかい)で静(しず)かに礼拝(れいはい)をささげた。
We offered quiet worship in a small church.

USAGE:
In Christian contexts, 礼拝(れいはい) refers to a church service or worship. In Buddhist and other religious contexts, the reading らいはい is sometimes used. Many Japanese schools (especially Christian mission schools) hold 礼拝(れいはい) as a regular assembly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 礼拝(れいはい)堂(どう) (chapel, place of worship)
  • 礼拝(れいはい)に出席(しゅっせき)する (to attend a service)
  • 礼拝(れいはい)をささげる (to offer worship)
  • 朝(あさ)の礼拝(れいはい) (morning service/worship)

PRONUNCIATION NOTE:
Read as れいはい in Christian and general contexts. The Buddhist reading らいはい also exists but is less common in everyday speech.
