搬出(はんしゅつ)

はんしゅつ
noun
carrying out; removal
1. carrying out; removal; moving out
The act of transporting something out of a location. Used for goods, equipment, materials, or other items being removed from a building, site, or area.
荷物(にもつ)の搬出(はんしゅつ)を始(はじ)めた。
We started carrying out the luggage.
搬出(はんしゅつ)作業(さぎょう)は午前中(ごぜんちゅう)に完了(かんりょう)した。
The removal work was completed in the morning.
機材(きざい)の搬出(はんしゅつ)にはエレベーターを使用(しよう)してください。
Please use the elevator for carrying out equipment.

USAGE:
Used in formal and business contexts. Often seen in logistics, moving, and event management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 搬出(はんしゅつ)作業(さぎょう) (removal work)
  • 搬出(はんしゅつ)経路(けいろ) (removal route)
  • 搬出(はんしゅつ)する (to carry out)

ANTONYM:

  • 搬入(はんにゅう): carrying in, bringing in
