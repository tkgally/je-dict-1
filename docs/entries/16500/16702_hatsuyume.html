初夢(はつゆめ)

はつゆめ
noun
first dream of the new year
1. first dream of the new year
The first dream one has in the new year, traditionally on the night of January 1st or 2nd. In Japanese culture, certain dreams are considered auspicious.
初夢(はつゆめ)を見(み)た。
I had my first dream of the year.
初夢(はつゆめ)に富士山(ふじさん)が出(で)てきた。
Mt. Fuji appeared in my first dream of the year.
初夢(はつゆめ)で縁起(えんぎ)がいいのは「一(いち)富士(ふじ)二(に)鷹(たか)三(さん)なすび」と言(い)われている。
It is said that the most auspicious first dreams are 'first, Mt. Fuji; second, a hawk; third, an eggplant.'

CULTURAL NOTE:
The traditional saying 一(いち)富士(ふじ)二(に)鷹(たか)三(さん)なすび lists the three most auspicious things to dream of on New Year's: Mt. Fuji, a hawk, and an eggplant. The origin of this saying is debated but dates back to the Edo period.

初夢(はつゆめ) traditionally refers to the dream seen on the night of January 1st or 2nd.
