不公平(ふこうへい)

ふこうへい
adjective (na)
unfair; unjust; inequitable
1. unfair; unjust; inequitable; biased
Not treating people or things equally or justly. Describes situations, rules, or treatment that favor one side over another.
それは不公平(ふこうへい)だ。
That's unfair.
不公平(ふこうへい)な扱(あつか)いを受(う)けて、怒(おこ)りを感(かん)じた。
I felt angry at being treated unfairly.
税金(ぜいきん)の負担(ふたん)が不公平(ふこうへい)だという声(こえ)が多(おお)い。
There are many voices saying the tax burden is inequitable.

USAGE:
The antonym is 公平(こうへい) (fair, impartial). Both are very common in everyday conversation and formal discourse.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不公平(ふこうへい)な扱(あつか)い (unfair treatment)
  • 不公平(ふこうへい)な制度(せいど) (unfair system)
  • 不公平(ふこうへい)感(かん) (sense of unfairness)
