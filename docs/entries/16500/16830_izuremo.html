いずれも

いずれも
adverb
all; any of them; both; each
1. all; any of them; both; each one
Refers to every item or person in a group without exception. Used to make a statement that applies equally to all members of a set.
いずれも優秀(ゆうしゅう)な作品(さくひん)だ。
They are all excellent works.
候補者(こうほしゃ)はいずれも経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ)だった。
The candidates were all highly experienced.
三(みっ)つの案(あん)を検討(けんとう)したが、いずれも一長一短(いっちょういったん)があった。
We examined three proposals, but each had its pros and cons.

USAGE:
A somewhat formal word commonly found in written Japanese, news reports, and formal speech. More formal than どれも or どちらも.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どれも is the casual equivalent for three or more items
  • どちらも is the casual equivalent for two items
  • いずれも covers both two and three-or-more in a formal register
