買(か)い取(と)る

かいとる
verb (godan)
to buy up, to purchase outright
1. to buy up, to purchase outright
To purchase something outright, often used for buying back, buying used goods, or acquiring something in full.
中古(ちゅうこ)の本(ほん)を買(か)い取(と)る。
To buy used books.
この店(みせ)ではブランド品(ひん)を高(たか)く買(か)い取(と)ってくれる。
This shop buys brand-name goods at good prices.
不要(ふよう)になった家具(かぐ)をリサイクルショップに買(か)い取(と)ってもらった。
I had a secondhand shop buy the furniture I no longer needed.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 高(たか)く買(か)い取(と)る: to buy at a high price
  • 安(やす)く買(か)い取(と)る: to buy at a low price
  • 買(か)い取(と)ってもらう: to have someone buy (from you)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 買取(かいとり): purchase, buyback (noun form)
  • 買取(かいとり)価格(かかく): buyback price
