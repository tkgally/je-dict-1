指(さ)し示(しめ)す

さししめす
verb-godan
to indicate; to point to; to suggest
1. to point to; to indicate; to show
To physically point at or toward something to identify it. A compound of 指(さ)す (to point) and 示(しめ)す (to show).
地図(ちず)の場所(ばしょ)を指(ゆび)で指(さ)し示(しめ)した。
I pointed to the location on the map with my finger.
方角(ほうがく)を指(さ)し示(しめ)して道(みち)を教(おし)えた。
I pointed in the direction and gave directions.
コンパスの針(はり)が北(きた)を指(さ)し示(しめ)している。
The compass needle is pointing north.
2. to indicate; to suggest; to point to (abstract)
To show or suggest a direction, conclusion, or meaning in an abstract sense. Common in academic, analytical, and news contexts.
このデータは経済(けいざい)の回復(かいふく)を指(さ)し示(しめ)している。
This data indicates economic recovery.
研究(けんきゅう)結果(けっか)は新(あたら)しい治療法(ちりょうほう)の可能性(かのうせい)を指(さ)し示(しめ)している。
The research results point to the possibility of a new treatment method.
歴史(れきし)は同(おな)じ過(あやま)ちを繰(く)り返(かえ)す危険性(きけんせい)を指(さ)し示(しめ)している。
History points to the danger of repeating the same mistakes.

USAGE:
More emphatic and deliberate than 指(さ)す alone. Sense 2 (abstract indication) is very common in news, academic papers, and formal writing. Often used when data, evidence, or trends suggest a particular conclusion.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb conjugating on the す row. Takes を for the object being pointed to or indicated.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 指(さ)す: to point — simpler, more everyday
  • 示(しめ)す: to show, to indicate — broader meaning, not limited to pointing
  • 暗示(あんじ)する: to hint, to imply — less direct, suggests something indirectly
