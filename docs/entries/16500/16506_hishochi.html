避暑地(ひしょち)

ひしょち
noun
summer resort; retreat from the heat
1. summer resort; retreat from the heat
A place, typically in the mountains or at higher elevations, where people go to escape the summer heat. These areas are naturally cooler than cities.
軽井沢(かるいざわ)は有名(ゆうめい)な避暑地(ひしょち)だ。
Karuizawa is a famous summer resort.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)になると避暑地(ひしょち)に出(で)かける家族(かぞく)が多(おお)い。
Every summer, many families head to summer resorts.
都会(とかい)の猛暑(もうしょ)を避(さ)けて、涼(すず)しい高原(こうげん)の避暑地(ひしょち)で一週間(いっしゅうかん)ほど過(す)ごした。
Escaping the intense heat of the city, I spent about a week at a cool highland summer resort.

CULTURE:
Famous Japanese summer resorts include 軽井沢(かるいざわ) (Karuizawa), 那須(なす) (Nasu), and 箱根(はこね) (Hakone). The concept became popular during the Meiji era when foreign residents and Japanese elite began retreating to the mountains in summer. These areas typically feature cooler temperatures, forests, and a relaxed atmosphere.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 避暑地(ひしょち)に行(い)く (to go to a summer resort)
  • 避暑地(ひしょち)として知(し)られる (to be known as a summer resort)
  • 人気(にんき)の避暑地(ひしょち) (a popular summer resort)
