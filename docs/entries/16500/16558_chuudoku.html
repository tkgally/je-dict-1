中毒(ちゅうどく)

ちゅうどく
noun / verb (suru)
poisoning; addiction
1. poisoning
A condition caused by ingesting or being exposed to a harmful substance. Used in medical and everyday contexts.
食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)になった。
I got food poisoning.
一酸化炭素(いっさんかたんそ)中毒(ちゅうどく)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful of carbon monoxide poisoning.
古(ふる)い弁当(べんとう)を食(た)べて中毒(ちゅうどく)症状(しょうじょう)が出(で)た。
I ate an old boxed lunch and developed symptoms of poisoning.
2. addiction; being hooked on something
An obsessive dependence on a substance or activity. Often used figuratively and casually to describe being unable to stop doing something.
ゲーム中毒(ちゅうどく)の子(こ)どもが増(ふ)えている。
The number of children addicted to games is increasing.
彼(かれ)はカフェイン中毒(ちゅうどく)で、毎日(まいにち)コーヒーを五杯(ごはい)飲(の)む。
He's a caffeine addict and drinks five cups of coffee every day.
あのドラマは面白(おもしろ)すぎて中毒(ちゅうどく)になりそうだ。
That drama is so good I'm going to get hooked.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is the original medical meaning — actual poisoning from toxic substances. Sense 2 is a figurative extension meaning addiction or obsessive attachment, used both seriously (drug/alcohol addiction) and casually (game/coffee addiction). Often appears as a suffix: ～中毒(ちゅうどく).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく) (food poisoning)
  • 一酸化炭素(いっさんかたんそ)中毒(ちゅうどく) (carbon monoxide poisoning)
  • アルコール中毒(ちゅうどく) (alcoholism)
  • 中毒(ちゅうどく)症状(しょうじょう) (poisoning symptoms)
  • 中毒(ちゅうどく)患者(かんじゃ) (poisoning patient)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 依存(いぞん): dependence — more clinical and formal term for addiction
  • 中毒(ちゅうどく)性(せい): addictive quality — describes the property of being addictive
