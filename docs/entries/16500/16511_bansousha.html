伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)

ばんそうしゃ
noun
guide runner; pacer
1. guide runner; pacer; accompanying runner
A person who runs alongside someone else to assist, guide, or pace them. Most commonly refers to sighted guides who run with visually impaired athletes in parasports.
伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)とロープでつながって走(はし)る。
The runner runs connected to a guide runner by a rope.
パラリンピックでは伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)の役割(やくわり)がとても重要(じゅうよう)だ。
The role of guide runners is very important at the Paralympics.
視覚障害(しかくしょうがい)のある選手(せんしゅ)にとって、信頼(しんらい)できる伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)は欠(か)かせない存在(そんざい)だ。
For athletes with visual impairments, a trustworthy guide runner is indispensable.
2. supportive companion; mentor who walks alongside
Used figuratively for someone who supports another person through a process or challenge, offering guidance without taking the lead.
先生(せんせい)は伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)として見守(みまも)る。
The teacher watches over them as a supportive companion.
起業(きぎょう)の伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)として経験豊富(けいけんほうふ)なメンターがつく。
An experienced mentor serves as a supportive companion for the startup.
彼(かれ)は選手(せんしゅ)としてだけでなく、後輩(こうはい)たちの伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)としても尊敬(そんけい)されている。
He is respected not only as an athlete but also as a mentor who walks alongside the younger members.

USAGE:
In parasports, the guide runner is connected to the visually impaired runner by a short rope or tether. The 伴走者(ばんそうしゃ) must match the athlete's pace without pulling ahead. The figurative sense has become increasingly common in business and education contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)を務(つと)める (to serve as a guide runner)
  • 伴走者(ばんそうしゃ)として支(ささ)える (to support as an accompanying companion)
  • 伴走(ばんそう)支援(しえん) (accompanying support)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • {ペースメーカー}: pacemaker/pacer — in athletics, someone who sets the pace for others in a race
  • {メンター}: mentor — a more general term for a guiding figure
