度忘(どわす)れ

どわすれ
noun
sudden memory lapse; momentary forgetting
1. sudden memory lapse; momentary forgetting; mental blank
The experience of suddenly being unable to recall something you normally know well. A temporary lapse rather than genuine forgetting.
名前(なまえ)を度忘(どわす)れしてしまった。
I had a sudden lapse and forgot the name.
パスワードを度忘(どわす)れして、ログインできない。
I've blanked on my password and can't log in.
年(とし)をとると度忘(どわす)れが増(ふ)えるが、誰(だれ)にでもあることだ。
Memory lapses increase with age, but it happens to everyone.

USAGE:
Commonly used as a suru verb: 度忘(どわす)れする. Describes a temporary blank, not permanent memory loss. The word implies you normally know the information but it has momentarily slipped your mind.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 度忘(どわす)れする (to have a sudden lapse)
  • 度忘(どわす)れが増(ふ)える (memory lapses increase)
  • うっかり度忘(どわす)れする (to absentmindedly forget)
