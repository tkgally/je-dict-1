視察(しさつ)

しさつ
noun / verb (suru)
inspection; observation tour
1. inspection; observation tour; fact-finding visit
An official or professional visit to a site, facility, or region to observe conditions, operations, or progress firsthand. Implies a purposeful, structured observation.
工場(こうじょう)を視察(しさつ)した。
We inspected the factory.
大臣(だいじん)が被災地(ひさいち)を視察(しさつ)に訪(おとず)れた。
The minister visited the disaster-stricken area for an inspection.
海外(かいがい)の教育(きょういく)現場(げんば)を視察(しさつ)して、多(おお)くの学(まな)びを得(え)た。
I gained many insights from observing educational settings overseas.

USAGE:
A formal word used for official or professional observation visits. The subject is typically someone in a position of authority — a politician, executive, or expert. Not used for casual sightseeing or personal visits.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 視察(しさつ)する (to inspect, to observe)
  • 視察(しさつ)団(だん) (inspection group, observation delegation)
  • 現地(げんち)視察(しさつ) (on-site inspection)
  • 視察(しさつ)報告(ほうこく) (inspection report)
  • 視察(しさつ)に訪(おとず)れる (to visit for inspection)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見学(けんがく): study visit — less formal, often educational (e.g., factory tours for students)
  • 調査(ちょうさ): investigation, survey — broader, includes data collection beyond observation
  • 点検(てんけん): inspection — checking equipment or items for defects, not observing conditions
