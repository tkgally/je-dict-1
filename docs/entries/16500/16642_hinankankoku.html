避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)

ひなんかんこく
noun
evacuation advisory
1. evacuation advisory
An official recommendation issued by local authorities urging residents to evacuate an area due to danger from natural disasters such as typhoons, floods, or landslides.
避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)が出(で)た。
An evacuation advisory was issued.
大雨(おおあめ)のため避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)が発令(はつれい)された。
An evacuation advisory was issued due to heavy rain.
避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)を受(う)けて住民(じゅうみん)が避難所(ひなんじょ)に移動(いどう)した。
Residents moved to evacuation shelters after receiving the evacuation advisory.

NOTE:
As of May 2021, Japan's disaster response system was revised. The previous three-level system (避難準備(ひなんじゅんび), 避難勧告(ひなんかんこく), 避難指示(ひなんしじ)) was simplified, with 避難勧告(ひなんかんこく) being abolished and merged into 避難指示(ひなんしじ). However, the term remains widely known and may still appear in older materials and historical reports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)が出(で)る (an advisory is issued)
  • 避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)を発令(はつれい)する (to issue an advisory)
  • 避難勧告(ひなんかんこく)が解除(かいじょ)される (an advisory is lifted)

RELATED:

  • 避難指示(ひなんしじ): evacuation order (stronger than advisory)
  • 避難所(ひなんじょ): evacuation shelter
