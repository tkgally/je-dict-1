日(ひ)が暮(く)れる

ひがくれる
expression
the sun sets; it gets dark
1. the sun sets; it gets dark; evening falls
Describes the sun going down and daylight fading. A natural, everyday expression for sunset and the onset of evening.
日(ひ)が暮(く)れるのが早(はや)くなった。
The sun has started setting earlier.
日(ひ)が暮(く)れる前(まえ)に家(いえ)に帰(かえ)りなさい。
Come home before it gets dark.
冬(ふゆ)になると日(ひ)が暮(く)れるのが早(はや)いので、四時(よじ)にはもう暗(くら)くなっている。
In winter the sun sets early, so it's already dark by four o'clock.

USAGE:
A very common everyday expression. The subject 日(ひ) refers to the sun/daylight. 暮(く)れる means to come to an end or to darken. Often used to talk about seasonal changes in daylight hours.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日(ひ)が暮(く)れる前(まえ)に (before sunset)
  • 日(ひ)が暮(く)れるのが早(はや)い (the sun sets early)
  • 日(ひ)が暮(く)れてから (after dark)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 日(ひ)が沈(しず)む: the sun sinks/sets — focuses on the physical sun
  • 夕暮(ゆうぐ)れ: dusk, twilight — the time period itself
