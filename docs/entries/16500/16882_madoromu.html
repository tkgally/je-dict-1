まどろむ

まどろむ
verb (godan)
to slumber; to doze; to drowse
1. to slumber; to doze; to drowse
To sleep lightly or drift in and out of shallow sleep. A literary and poetic word for being in a peaceful, half-asleep state.
木陰(こかげ)でまどろむ。
To doze in the shade of a tree.
猫(ねこ)が日(ひ)だまりでまどろんでいる。
The cat is slumbering in a sunny spot.
暖(あたた)かい風(かぜ)に吹(ふ)かれながら、草原(そうげん)でしばらくまどろんだ。
Feeling the warm breeze, I dozed for a while on the grassy field.

USAGE:
A literary and poetic word that evokes a peaceful, tranquil atmosphere. More refined than ウトウトする or うたた寝(ね)する. Often used in descriptive writing, novels, and poetry.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 木陰(こかげ)でまどろむ (to doze in the shade)
  • しばしまどろむ (to slumber briefly)
  • 穏(おだ)やかにまどろむ (to slumber peacefully)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ウトウトする: to nod off — more casual and everyday
  • うたた寝(ね): nap, dozing off — a noun, less literary
