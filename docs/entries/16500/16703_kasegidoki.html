稼(かせ)ぎ時(どき)

かせぎどき
noun
peak earning time, busy season for making money
1. peak earning time, busy season for making money
The best time or season to earn money. A period when business is booming or opportunities for profit are greatest.
夏(なつ)は観光(かんこう)業(ぎょう)の稼(かせ)ぎ時(どき)だ。
Summer is the peak earning season for the tourism industry.
今(いま)が稼(かせ)ぎ時(どき)だから頑張(がんば)ろう。
Now is the time to earn, so let's work hard.
ボーナスシーズンは小売店(こうりてん)にとって最大(さいだい)の稼(かせ)ぎ時(どき)だ。
The bonus season is the biggest moneymaking period for retail stores.

WORD FORMATION:
稼(かせ)ぐ (to earn) + 時(どき) (time/season) = the time to earn

SIMILAR PATTERNS:

  • 書(か)き入(い)れ時(どき): busy season for business
  • 食(た)べ頃(ごろ): the right time to eat (ripe)
