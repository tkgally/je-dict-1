染(し)み抜(ぬ)き

しみぬき
noun
stain removal; stain remover
1. stain removal; stain remover
The act of removing stains from clothing or fabric, or a product used for that purpose.
染(し)み抜(ぬ)きをお願(ねが)いします。
I'd like to have this stain removed, please.
ワインの染(し)み抜(ぬ)きは早(はや)めにやったほうがいい。
It's better to remove wine stains quickly.
クリーニング店(てん)に出(だ)したら、染(し)み抜(ぬ)きの料金(りょうきん)が別(べつ)にかかると言(い)われた。
When I took it to the dry cleaner's, I was told that stain removal would cost extra.

USAGE:
Can refer to both the process and the product. At a dry cleaner's (クリーニング店(てん)), stain removal is often offered as a separate service with additional charges.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 染(し)み抜(ぬ)き剤(ざい) (stain remover product)
  • 染(し)み抜(ぬ)きをする (to remove a stain)
  • 染(し)み抜(ぬ)き専門(せんもん) (specializing in stain removal)
