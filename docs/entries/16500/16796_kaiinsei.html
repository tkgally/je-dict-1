会員制(かいいんせい)

かいいんせい
noun
membership system; members-only
1. membership system; members-only
A system where access to a place, service, or organization is restricted to registered members. Common for clubs, restaurants, gyms, and online services.
このバーは会員制(かいいんせい)です。
This bar is members-only.
会員制(かいいんせい)のスポーツジムに入会(にゅうかい)した。
I joined a membership-based sports gym.
そのレストランは会員制(かいいんせい)なので、紹介(しょうかい)がないと入(はい)れない。
That restaurant is members-only, so you can't get in without an introduction.

USAGE:
Often used as a modifier with の: 会員制(かいいんせい)のクラブ (a members-only club). In Japan, some high-end bars, restaurants, and golf courses operate on a membership system, sometimes requiring an existing member's introduction.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会員制(かいいんせい)クラブ (membership club)
  • 完全(かんぜん)会員制(かいいんせい) (strictly members-only)
  • 会員制(かいいんせい)のサービス (a membership-based service)
