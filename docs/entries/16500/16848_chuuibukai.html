注意深(ちゅういぶか)い

ちゅういぶかい
adjective (i)
careful; cautious; attentive
1. careful; cautious; attentive; watchful
Describes a person or action characterized by paying close attention and taking care to avoid mistakes or danger.
彼(かれ)は注意深(ちゅういぶか)い人(ひと)だ。
He is a careful person.
注意深(ちゅういぶか)く説明書(せつめいしょ)を読(よ)んでから組(く)み立(た)てた。
I carefully read the instructions before assembling it.
注意深(ちゅういぶか)い観察(かんさつ)のおかげで、小(ちい)さな異変(いへん)にも気付(きづ)くことができた。
Thanks to careful observation, I was able to notice even small irregularities.

USAGE:
The adverb form is 注意深(ちゅういぶか)く. A relatively formal adjective, more common in writing than casual speech where 気(き)をつける or 慎重(しんちょう) might be preferred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注意深(ちゅういぶか)く観察(かんさつ)する (to observe carefully)
  • 注意深(ちゅういぶか)い人(ひと) (a careful person)
  • 注意深(ちゅういぶか)く読(よ)む (to read carefully)
