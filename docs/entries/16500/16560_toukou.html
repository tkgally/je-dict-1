登校(とうこう)

とうこう
noun / verb (suru)
attending school; going to school
1. attending school; going to school
The act of going to school to attend classes. Used specifically for students traveling to and arriving at their school.
毎朝(まいあさ)八時(はちじ)に登校(とうこう)する。
I go to school at eight every morning.
台風(たいふう)のため登校(とうこう)が中止(ちゅうし)になった。
School attendance was canceled due to the typhoon.
息子(むすこ)は最近(さいきん)登校(とうこう)を嫌(いや)がるようになった。
My son has recently started to resist going to school.

USAGE:
Used as a noun or with する. Specifically refers to students going to school — not used for teachers or other staff (they use 出勤(しゅっきん)). The opposite is 下校(げこう) (leaving school to go home).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 登校(とうこう)する (to attend school)
  • 登校(とうこう)時間(じかん) (time to go to school)
  • 登校(とうこう)拒否(きょひ) (school refusal)
  • 不登校(ふとうこう) (truancy, school non-attendance)
  • 登校(とうこう)日(び) (school day)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 通学(つうがく): commuting to school — focuses on the commute itself, not the arrival
  • 出席(しゅっせき): attendance — being present at class, not the act of going
