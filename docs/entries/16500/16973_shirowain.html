白(しろ)ワイン

しろわいん
noun
white wine
1. white wine
Wine made from green or yellow grapes, or from dark grapes with the skins removed. A common beverage in restaurants and at home.
白(しろ)ワインをください。
White wine, please.
魚料理(さかなりょうり)には白(しろ)ワインが合(あ)う。
White wine goes well with fish dishes.
冷(ひ)やした白(しろ)ワインを一杯(いっぱい)飲(の)みながら夕日(ゆうひ)を眺(なが)めた。
I watched the sunset while drinking a glass of chilled white wine.

USAGE:
The Japanese term uses 白(しろ) (white) + ワイン (wine). In contrast, red wine is 赤(あか)ワイン and rosé is ロゼワイン. When ordering at a restaurant, simply 白(しろ) can be used as shorthand.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 白(しろ)ワインを注文(ちゅうもん)する (to order white wine)
  • 辛口(からくち)の白(しろ)ワイン (dry white wine)
  • 甘口(あまくち)の白(しろ)ワイン (sweet white wine)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 赤(あか)ワイン: red wine
  • ロゼワイン: rosé wine
