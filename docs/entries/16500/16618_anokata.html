あの方(かた)

あのかた
pronoun
that person (polite)
1. that person (polite)
A polite way to refer to a third person. More respectful than あの人 and appropriate for formal situations or when speaking about someone of higher status.
あの方(かた)はどなたですか。
Who is that person?
あの方(かた)にお会(あ)いしたことがあります。
I have met that person before.
あの方(かた)は以前(いぜん)この会社(かいしゃ)の社長(しゃちょう)をされていた方(かた)です。
That person was formerly the president of this company.

POLITENESS:

A polite pronoun for referring to a third person. The politeness levels for third-person reference are:

  • あいつ (very informal, can be rude)
  • あの人(ひと) (neutral)
  • あの方(かた) (polite)
  • あちらの方(かた) (very polite)

USAGE:
Use あの方(かた) in formal situations, business contexts, or when speaking about someone deserving respect.
