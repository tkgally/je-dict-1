上出来(じょうでき)

じょうでき
noun, adjective (na)
well done, good job
1. well done, excellent result
A result or outcome that exceeds expectations or is notably good. Used to praise work, performance, or results.
今回(こんかい)のテストは上出来(じょうでき)だ。
This test result is excellent.
初(はじ)めてにしては上出来(じょうでき)だね。
For a first attempt, that's really well done.
プレゼンは上出来(じょうでき)で、クライアントも満足(まんぞく)していた。
The presentation was excellent, and the client was satisfied.

OPPOSITE:

  • 不出来(ふでき): badly done, poor result

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出来(でき)がいい: well made, turned out well
  • 大成功(だいせいこう): great success

Related Words

Antonym: 不出来(ふでき) (poorly done)
