全額(ぜんがく)

ぜんがく
noun
full amount; total sum
1. full amount; total sum; entire amount
The complete monetary amount without any deduction or reduction.
全額(ぜんがく)返金(へんきん)します。
We will refund the full amount.
旅費(りょひ)は会社(かいしゃ)が全額(ぜんがく)負担(ふたん)する。
The company will cover the full travel expenses.
奨学金(しょうがくきん)のおかげで、学費(がくひ)を全額(ぜんがく)免除(めんじょ)してもらえた。
Thanks to the scholarship, I had the full tuition waived.

USAGE:
Commonly used in financial, business, and administrative contexts. Often modifies verbs related to payment, refund, or coverage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全額(ぜんがく)返金(へんきん) (full refund)
  • 全額(ぜんがく)負担(ふたん) (bearing the full cost)
  • 全額(ぜんがく)免除(めんじょ) (full exemption)
  • 全額(ぜんがく)支払(しはら)う (to pay in full)
