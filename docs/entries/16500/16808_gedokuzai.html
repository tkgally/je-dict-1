解毒剤(げどくざい)

げどくざい
noun
antidote; detoxifying agent
1. antidote; detoxifying agent
A medicine or substance that counteracts the effects of poison or a toxic substance.
解毒剤(げどくざい)を投与(とうよ)する。
To administer an antidote.
ヘビに噛(か)まれたら、すぐに解毒剤(げどくざい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
If you're bitten by a snake, you need an antidote right away.
その毒(どく)に対(たい)する解毒剤(げどくざい)はまだ開発(かいはつ)されていない。
An antidote for that poison has not yet been developed.

USAGE:
Used in medical contexts and also frequently in fiction (mystery novels, action movies). A compound of 解毒(げどく) (detoxification) + 剤(ざい) (agent/medicine).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 解毒剤(げどくざい)を飲(の)む (to take an antidote)
  • 解毒剤(げどくざい)を投与(とうよ)する (to administer an antidote)
  • 解毒剤(げどくざい)が効(き)く (the antidote works)
