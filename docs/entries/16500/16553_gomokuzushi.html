五目寿司(ごもくずし)

ごもくずし
noun
mixed sushi; scattered sushi with various toppings
1. mixed sushi; scattered sushi with various toppings
A type of sushi where vinegared rice is mixed with or topped with a variety of ingredients such as shiitake mushrooms, carrots, lotus root, egg strips, shrimp, and other colorful toppings. A festive home-cooked dish often prepared for celebrations and gatherings.
お祝(いわ)いに五目寿司(ごもくずし)を作(つく)った。
I made mixed sushi for the celebration.
母(はは)の五目寿司(ごもくずし)は具(ぐ)だくさんで美味(おい)しい。
My mother's mixed sushi is full of toppings and delicious.
ひな祭(まつ)りには五目寿司(ごもくずし)を食(た)べる家庭(かてい)が多(おお)い。
Many families eat mixed sushi on Girls' Day.

USAGE:
五目(ごもく) means 'five kinds' but in practice refers to 'assorted' or 'various.' 五目寿司(ごもくずし) is essentially the same dish as ちらし寿司(ずし) (scattered sushi), though 五目寿司(ごもくずし) tends to emphasize the mixed-in ingredients while ちらし寿司(ずし) emphasizes toppings arranged on top. A popular home-cooked dish for celebrations, especially ひな祭(まつ)り (Girls' Day).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 五目寿司(ごもくずし)を作(つく)る (to make mixed sushi)
  • 五目寿司(ごもくずし)の具(ぐ) (mixed sushi toppings/ingredients)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ちらし寿司(ずし): scattered sushi — very similar; toppings arranged on top of rice
  • 五目(ごもく)ご飯(はん): mixed rice — rice cooked with various ingredients (not sushi)
  • 手巻(てま)き寿司(ずし): hand-rolled sushi — a different interactive sushi style
