看病(かんびょう)

かんびょう
noun / verb (suru)
nursing; caring for a sick person
1. nursing; caring for a sick person
Taking care of someone who is ill, including staying by their side, helping with meals, and attending to their needs. Typically done by family members or close people rather than professional nurses.
母(はは)を看病(かんびょう)した。
I nursed my mother.
夫(おっと)が風邪(かぜ)をひいたので看病(かんびょう)している。
My husband caught a cold, so I'm taking care of him.
入院(にゅういん)した祖父(そふ)のそばで毎日(まいにち)看病(かんびょう)を続(つづ)けた。
I stayed by my hospitalized grandfather's side and continued to care for him every day.

USAGE:
Refers to personal, often family-based care rather than professional nursing. The person doing the 看病 is typically a family member, spouse, or close friend, not a hospital nurse (看護師(かんごし)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 看病(かんびょう)疲(づか)れ (exhaustion from nursing)
  • つきっきりで看病(かんびょう)する (to nurse someone constantly)
  • 献身的(けんしんてき)な看病(かんびょう) (devoted nursing care)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 看護(かんご): nursing — more professional and clinical
  • 介護(かいご): caregiving — long-term care for elderly or disabled people
