1. buying and eating snacks on the go; eating while walking
The act of buying food at shops or stalls and eating it on the spot or while walking. Often associated with children buying snacks on the way home from school.
学校(がっこう)の帰(かえ)りに買(か)い食(ぐ)いをした。
I bought snacks on the way home from school.
買(か)い食(ぐ)いは禁止(きんし)されていた。
Buying snacks on the way was forbidden.
商店街(しょうてんがい)で買(か)い食(ぐ)いしながら散歩(さんぽ)するのが楽(たの)しい。
It's fun to stroll through the shopping street, buying and eating snacks as you go.

USAGE:
Traditionally associated with school children buying snacks from shops on their way home. Many schools in Japan have rules prohibiting 買(か)い食(ぐ)い. In modern usage, it also describes adults enjoying street food at festivals, markets, or shopping streets.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 買(か)い食(ぐ)い禁止(きんし) (prohibition on buying snacks)
  • 食(た)べ歩(ある)き / 買(か)い食(ぐ)い (eating while walking / buying snacks)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(た)べ歩(ある)き: eating while walking — focuses on the walking aspect, more positive connotation
  • つまみ食(ぐ)い: snacking, nibbling — picking at food before it's served
