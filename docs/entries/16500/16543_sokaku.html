組閣(そかく)

そかく
noun / verb (suru)
formation of a cabinet
1. formation of a cabinet; forming a government
The process of selecting ministers and assembling a cabinet, typically by a newly appointed or re-elected prime minister. A key political term in Japanese news.
新(しん)首相(しゅしょう)が組閣(そかく)した。
The new prime minister formed a cabinet.
組閣(そかく)の人事(じんじ)に注目(ちゅうもく)が集(あつ)まっている。
Attention is focused on the cabinet formation appointments.
選挙(せんきょ)後(ご)の組閣(そかく)では、若手(わかて)議員(ぎいん)の起用(きよう)が期待(きたい)されている。
In the post-election cabinet formation, the appointment of younger legislators is expected.

USAGE:
Used as a noun or with する. A formal political term that appears frequently in news coverage after elections or when a prime minister is selected. In Japan, the prime minister selects cabinet ministers (閣僚(かくりょう)), and the process is closely watched by the media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 組閣(そかく)する (to form a cabinet)
  • 組閣(そかく)人事(じんじ) (cabinet appointments)
  • 組閣(そかく)の見通(みとお)し (outlook for cabinet formation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 内閣改造(ないかくかいぞう): cabinet reshuffle — reshuffling an existing cabinet rather than forming a new one
  • 入閣(にゅうかく): joining the cabinet — being appointed as a cabinet minister
