光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く

ひかりかがやく
verb-godan
to shine brilliantly; to sparkle; to gleam
1. to shine brilliantly; to sparkle; to gleam
To emit or reflect bright, radiant light. A compound verb combining 光(ひか)る (to shine) and 輝(かがや)く (to sparkle), creating an intensified meaning of brilliant luminosity.
星(ほし)が光(ひか)り輝(かがや)いている。
The stars are shining brilliantly.
朝日(あさひ)に照(て)らされて海面(かいめん)が光(ひか)り輝(かがや)いていた。
The sea surface was gleaming, lit up by the morning sun.
ダイヤモンドが光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く指輪(ゆびわ)を贈(おく)った。
I gave her a ring with a sparkling diamond.
2. to be radiant; to be brilliant (figurative)
Used figuratively to describe something or someone that stands out with exceptional beauty, talent, or achievement.
彼女(かのじょ)の光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く笑顔(えがお)に惹(ひ)かれた。
I was drawn to her radiant smile.
光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く未来(みらい)を目指(めざ)して努力(どりょく)し続(つづ)ける。
I will keep working toward a brilliant future.
若(わか)い選手(せんしゅ)たちが光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く活躍(かつやく)を見(み)せ、観客(かんきゃく)を沸(わ)かせた。
The young athletes gave brilliant performances that thrilled the audience.

USAGE:
A compound verb that intensifies the meaning of both 光(ひか)る and 輝(かがや)く. Often used in literary, poetic, and emotional contexts. The prenominal form 光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く is very common as a modifier.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb conjugating on the く row. The prenominal form 光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く + noun is common: 光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く未来(みらい), 光(ひか)り輝(かがや)く宝石(ほうせき).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 輝(かがや)く: to shine, to sparkle — single verb, slightly less emphatic
  • きらきら光(ひか)る: to glitter, to twinkle — focuses on repeated, small points of light
  • 煌(きら)めく: to glitter, to gleam — literary, suggests sparkling points of light
