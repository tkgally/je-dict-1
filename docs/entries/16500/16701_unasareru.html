1. to have a nightmare, to groan in one's sleep
To moan, cry out, or show signs of distress while sleeping due to a bad dream.
夜中(よなか)にうなされた。
I had a nightmare during the night.
子(こ)どもが怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ)でうなされている。
The child is groaning in sleep from a scary dream.
あの事故(じこ)のことを思(おも)い出(だ)すと、今(いま)でもうなされることがある。
When I remember that accident, I still sometimes have nightmares about it.

うなされる means to groan or cry out in one's sleep due to a bad dream. The base form うなす is not used independently in modern Japanese — this verb always appears in its passive-like れる form.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に to mark the cause of the nightmare

ASPECT (ている):
うなされている describes someone currently groaning in their sleep — an ongoing state visible to an observer.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 悪夢(あくむ)にうなされる: to be tormented by nightmares
  • 夜中(よなか)にうなされる: to have nightmares in the middle of the night
  • 怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ)にうなされる: to be disturbed by scary dreams
  • 〜のことでうなされる: to have nightmares about ~

USAGE:
Often used to describe the lingering psychological impact of traumatic experiences. Can also be used figuratively to mean being haunted by something: 借金(しゃっきん)にうなされる (to be tormented by debt).
