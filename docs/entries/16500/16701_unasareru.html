うなされる

うなされる
verb (ichidan)
to have a nightmare, to groan in one's sleep
1. to have a nightmare, to groan in one's sleep
To moan, cry out, or show signs of distress while sleeping due to a bad dream.
夜中(よなか)にうなされた。
I had a nightmare during the night.
子(こ)どもが怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ)でうなされている。
The child is groaning in sleep from a scary dream.
あの事故(じこ)のことを思(おも)い出(だ)すと、今(いま)でもうなされることがある。
When I remember that accident, I still sometimes have nightmares about it.

This verb is always used in the passive-like form うなされる (from うなす). The base form うなす is not used independently in modern Japanese.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪夢(あくむ)にうなされる: to have a nightmare
  • 夜中(よなか)にうなされる: to be disturbed by nightmares at night
