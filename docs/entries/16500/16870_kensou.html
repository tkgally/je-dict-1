喧騒(けんそう)

けんそう
noun
tumult; hustle and bustle; noise
1. tumult; hustle and bustle; noise; clamor
Loud, chaotic noise or commotion, especially associated with crowded places or busy environments.
都会(とかい)の喧騒(けんそう)を離(はな)れる。
To get away from the hustle and bustle of the city.
窓(まど)を閉(し)めても外(そと)の喧騒(けんそう)が聞(き)こえる。
Even with the window closed, I can hear the noise outside.
祭(まつ)りの喧騒(けんそう)が終(お)わり、静(しず)かな夜(よる)が戻(もど)ってきた。
The clamor of the festival ended, and a quiet night returned.

USAGE:
Often used in literary or descriptive writing to portray the noise and energy of cities, crowds, or events. The phrase 都会(とかい)の喧騒(けんそう) (hustle and bustle of the city) is especially common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喧騒(けんそう)を離(はな)れる (to escape the noise/bustle)
  • 喧騒(けんそう)に包(つつ)まれる (to be engulfed in noise)
  • 都会(とかい)の喧騒(けんそう) (city hustle and bustle)
