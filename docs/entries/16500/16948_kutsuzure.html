靴擦(くつず)れ

くつずれ
noun
shoe blister; chafing from shoes
1. shoe blister; chafing from shoes; shoe sore
A blister, sore, or abrasion on the foot caused by friction from ill-fitting or new shoes. A common problem when breaking in new footwear.
新(あたら)しい靴(くつ)で靴擦(くつず)れができた。
I got a blister from my new shoes.
靴擦(くつず)れが痛(いた)くて歩(ある)けない。
The shoe blister hurts so much that I can't walk.
靴擦(くつず)れを防(ふせ)ぐために、新(あたら)しい靴(くつ)は少(すこ)しずつ履(は)き慣(な)らした方(ほう)がいい。
To prevent blisters, it's better to gradually break in new shoes.

USAGE:
A very practical, everyday word. Most commonly occurs with 新(あたら)しい靴(くつ) (new shoes) or 革靴(かわぐつ) (leather shoes). Blisters typically form on the heel or sides of the foot.

FORMATION:
靴(くつ) (shoes) + 擦(ず)れ (rubbing, chafing — from the verb 擦(す)れる).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 靴擦(くつず)れができる (to get a shoe blister)
  • 靴擦(くつず)れを防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent shoe blisters)
  • 靴擦(くつず)れ防止(ぼうし)パッド (blister prevention pad)
  • 靴(くつ)を履(は)き慣(な)らす (to break in shoes)
