密(みつ)に

みつに
adverb
closely; frequently; secretly
1. closely; frequently
In close or frequent contact or communication. Describes maintaining tight coordination or interaction.
密(みつ)に連絡(れんらく)を取(と)る。
To stay in close contact.
関係者(かんけいしゃ)と密(みつ)に連携(れんけい)して進(すす)めた。
We proceeded in close coordination with the parties involved.
両社(りょうしゃ)は密(みつ)に情報(じょうほう)交換(こうかん)をしている。
The two companies are closely exchanging information.
2. secretly; covertly
In a secret or hidden manner, without others knowing.
密(みつ)に計画(けいかく)を立(た)てた。
They secretly made a plan.
密(みつ)に会(あ)っていたことが発覚(はっかく)した。
It was discovered that they had been meeting in secret.
二人(ふたり)は周囲(しゅうい)に知(し)られないように密(みつ)に交際(こうさい)していた。
The two were secretly dating without those around them knowing.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (closely) is used in business and formal contexts to describe tight coordination. Sense 2 (secretly) often appears in news and narrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 密(みつ)に連絡(れんらく)を取(と)る (to stay in close contact)
  • 密(みつ)に連携(れんけい)する (to coordinate closely)
  • 密(みつ)に会(あ)う (to meet secretly)
