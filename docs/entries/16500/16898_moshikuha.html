もしくは

もしくは
conjunction
or; otherwise; alternatively
1. or; otherwise; alternatively
A formal conjunction used to present an alternative option. More formal than または and used in written or official contexts.
電話(でんわ)もしくはメールでご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Please contact us by phone or email.
現金(げんきん)もしくはクレジットカードでお支払(しはら)いいただけます。
You can pay by cash or credit card.
本人(ほんにん)もしくは代理人(だいりにん)が窓口(まどぐち)にて手続(てつづ)きしてください。
The person themselves or a representative should complete the procedure at the counter.

USAGE:
A formal conjunction used primarily in written language, legal documents, official notices, and business communication. In order of formality: もしくは (most formal) > または (formal) > それとも (conversational, for questions) > か (casual).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • または: or — slightly less formal, widely used in writing
  • それとも: or — used in questions in conversation
  • 或(ある)いは: or, possibly — similar formality, can also mean "perhaps"
