収集家(しゅうしゅうか)

しゅうしゅうか
noun
collector
1. collector; hobbyist collector
A person who systematically gathers and keeps items of a particular type, such as art, stamps, coins, or antiques. Implies a serious, dedicated hobby or profession.
彼(かれ)は切手(きって)の収集家(しゅうしゅうか)だ。
He is a stamp collector.
美術(びじゅつ)収集家(しゅうしゅうか)がその絵(え)を高額(こうがく)で落札(らくさつ)した。
An art collector won the painting at a high price at auction.
世界中(せかいじゅう)の収集家(しゅうしゅうか)がこのオークションに注目(ちゅうもく)している。
Collectors from around the world are paying attention to this auction.

USAGE:
Often preceded by the type of item collected: 切手(きって)収集家(しゅうしゅうか) (stamp collector), 美術(びじゅつ)収集家(しゅうしゅうか) (art collector), 骨董(こっとう)収集家(しゅうしゅうか) (antique collector).

FORMATION:
収集(しゅうしゅう) (collection, gathering) + 家(か) (person who does something, expert).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コレクター: collector — loanword, more casual and widely used in everyday speech
  • 愛好家(あいこうか): enthusiast, aficionado — broader term for someone who loves a particular thing
