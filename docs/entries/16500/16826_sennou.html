1. brainwashing; indoctrination
The systematic process of changing someone's beliefs or way of thinking through coercion or repetition. Also used loosely to describe being heavily influenced by media, advertising, or a person.
それは洗脳(せんのう)だ。
That's brainwashing.
テレビCMに洗脳(せんのう)されて、つい買(か)ってしまった。
I was brainwashed by the TV commercial and ended up buying it.
カルト団体(だんたい)による洗脳(せんのう)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)すのは簡単(かんたん)ではない。
It's not easy to escape brainwashing by a cult organization.

USAGE:
Often used as a suru-verb: 洗脳(せんのう)する (to brainwash). In casual speech, frequently used hyperbolically to describe being strongly influenced, e.g., by advertisements, catchy songs, or persuasive people.

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally "brain-washing" — 洗(あら)う (to wash) + 脳(のう) (brain). A calque from the English term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洗脳(せんのう)する (to brainwash)
  • 洗脳(せんのう)される (to be brainwashed)
  • 洗脳(せんのう)を解(と)く (to deprogram)
