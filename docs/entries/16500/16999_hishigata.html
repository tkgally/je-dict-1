ひし形(がた)

ひしがた
noun
diamond shape; rhombus
1. diamond shape; rhombus; lozenge
A four-sided geometric shape with equal sides but non-right angles, resembling the shape of a water chestnut (菱(ひし)). Used in mathematics, design, and everyday descriptions.
ひし形(がた)の模様(もよう)がきれいだ。
The diamond pattern is beautiful.
算数(さんすう)でひし形(がた)の面積(めんせき)を求(もと)めた。
In math, we calculated the area of a rhombus.
この着物(きもの)にはひし形(がた)の柄(がら)が全体(ぜんたい)に入(はい)っていて、伝統的(でんとうてき)な雰囲気(ふんいき)がある。
This kimono has a diamond pattern throughout, giving it a traditional feel.

USAGE:
The name comes from 菱(ひし) (water chestnut), whose seed has this shape. The diamond pattern is one of the most common traditional Japanese patterns, frequently seen on kimono, armor, and family crests.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ひし形(がた)の模様(もよう) (diamond pattern)
  • ひし形(がた)の面積(めんせき) (area of a rhombus)
  • ひし形(がた)に並(なら)べる (to arrange in a diamond shape)
