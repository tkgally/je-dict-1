卸売業(おろしうりぎょう)

おろしうりぎょう
noun
wholesale trade; wholesale business
1. wholesale trade; wholesale business
The business of selling goods in large quantities to retailers or other businesses, rather than directly to individual consumers.
父(ちち)は卸売業(おろしうりぎょう)を営(いとな)んでいる。
My father runs a wholesale business.
卸売業(おろしうりぎょう)は生産者(せいさんしゃ)と小売店(こうりてん)をつなぐ役割(やくわり)を果(は)たす。
The wholesale business plays the role of connecting producers and retail stores.
ネット通販(つうはん)の普及(ふきゅう)で、卸売業(おろしうりぎょう)の在(あ)り方(かた)が変(か)わりつつある。
With the spread of online shopping, the nature of the wholesale business is changing.

USAGE:
A standard business term for companies that buy goods in bulk from manufacturers and sell them to retailers. Japan's wholesale industry is known for its multi-layered distribution structure.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 卸売業(おろしうりぎょう)者(しゃ) (wholesaler)
  • 卸売(おろしうり)市場(しじょう) (wholesale market)
  • 食品(しょくひん)卸売業(おろしうりぎょう) (food wholesale business)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 小売業(こうりぎょう): retail trade — selling directly to consumers
  • 問屋(とんや): wholesaler — a more traditional/everyday term for a wholesale dealer
