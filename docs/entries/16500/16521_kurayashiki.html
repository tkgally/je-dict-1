蔵屋敷(くらやしき)

くらやしき
noun
warehouse estate; domain warehouse
1. domain warehouse; feudal warehouse estate
During the Edo period, warehouses maintained by feudal domains in major commercial cities, especially Osaka, to store and sell rice and other products from their territories.
大阪(おおさか)には多(おお)くの蔵屋敷(くらやしき)があった。
There were many domain warehouses in Osaka.
各藩(かくはん)は蔵屋敷(くらやしき)を通(つう)じて年貢米(ねんぐまい)を売(う)りさばいた。
Each domain sold its tax rice through its warehouse estate.
蔵屋敷(くらやしき)は藩(はん)の経済(けいざい)を支(ささ)える重要(じゅうよう)な拠点(きょてん)であり、大阪(おおさか)が「天下(てんか)の台所(だいどころ)」と呼(よ)ばれる理由(りゆう)の一(ひと)つだった。
Domain warehouses were important bases supporting each domain's economy, and were one of the reasons Osaka was called 'the kitchen of the nation.'

CULTURE:
A key institution of Edo-period commercial life. Osaka, as the center of rice trading, had the highest concentration of 蔵屋敷(くらやしき). Each feudal domain (藩(はん)) maintained one or more of these in Osaka or Edo to convert their rice tax (年貢(ねんぐ)) into cash. The 蔵屋敷(くらやしき) system contributed to the development of Japan's early commodity markets.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大阪(おおさか)の蔵屋敷(くらやしき) (Osaka domain warehouses)
  • 藩(はん)の蔵屋敷(くらやしき) (a domain's warehouse estate)
  • 蔵屋敷(くらやしき)跡(あと) (site of a former domain warehouse)
