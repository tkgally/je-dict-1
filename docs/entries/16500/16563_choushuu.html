徴収(ちょうしゅう)

ちょうしゅう
noun / verb (suru)
collection; levy
1. collection; levy; exaction
The official act of collecting money, taxes, fees, or dues from people, typically by a government or organization.
税金(ぜいきん)を徴収(ちょうしゅう)する。
To collect taxes.
入場料(にゅうじょうりょう)は当日(とうじつ)徴収(ちょうしゅう)します。
The admission fee will be collected on the day.
未払(みばら)いの会費(かいひ)を徴収(ちょうしゅう)するために通知(つうち)を送(おく)った。
We sent a notice to collect the unpaid membership fees.

USAGE:
A formal word used for official or institutional collection of money. The collector is usually a government body, organization, or institution — not an individual collecting a personal debt. Common in administrative, legal, and financial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 税金(ぜいきん)を徴収(ちょうしゅう)する (to collect taxes)
  • 源泉(げんせん)徴収(ちょうしゅう) (tax withholding at source)
  • 徴収(ちょうしゅう)額(がく) (amount collected)
  • 強制(きょうせい)徴収(ちょうしゅう) (compulsory collection, garnishment)
  • 会費(かいひ)を徴収(ちょうしゅう)する (to collect membership fees)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 集金(しゅうきん): collecting money — less formal, used for everyday payment collection (e.g., newspaper delivery fees)
  • 回収(かいしゅう): recovery, collection — broader meaning including collecting objects, not just money
