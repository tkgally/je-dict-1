完備(かんび)

かんび
noun, verb (suru)
fully equipped, complete
1. fully equipped, complete
Having all necessary equipment, facilities, or features in place. Often used in real estate listings and facility descriptions.
エアコン完備(かんび)。
Air conditioning fully equipped.
Common in real estate listings
設備(せつび)が完備(かんび)している。
The facilities are fully equipped.
防災(ぼうさい)設備(せつび)を完備(かんび)したビルに引(ひ)っ越(こ)した。
We moved to a building fully equipped with disaster prevention facilities.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 設備(せつび)完備(かんび): fully equipped facilities
  • エアコン完備(かんび): air conditioning included
  • 冷暖房(れいだんぼう)完備(かんび): heating and air conditioning equipped

Frequently seen in property listings and hotel descriptions.
