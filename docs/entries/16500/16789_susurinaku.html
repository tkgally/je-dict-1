すすり泣(な)く

すすりなく
verb (godan)
to sob; to weep quietly
1. to sob; to weep quietly; to sniffle while crying
To cry softly with intermittent catching of breath. Describes a restrained, quiet form of weeping rather than loud wailing.
彼女(かのじょ)はすすり泣(な)いていた。
She was sobbing quietly.
映画(えいが)のラストシーンで観客(かんきゃく)がすすり泣(な)いた。
The audience sobbed at the last scene of the movie.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)から小(ちい)さくすすり泣(な)く声(こえ)が聞(き)こえてきて、心配(しんぱい)になった。
I could hear the faint sound of sobbing from the next room, and I became worried.

USAGE:
Describes quiet, restrained crying. Often used in literature and descriptions of emotional scenes. The verb conjugates as a godan verb (すすり泣(な)かない, すすり泣(な)いて, etc.).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すすり泣(な)く声(こえ) (the sound of sobbing)
  • 静(しず)かにすすり泣(な)く (to sob quietly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泣(な)く: to cry — general term for crying
  • 嗚咽(おえつ)する: to choke with sobs — more intense, convulsive crying
  • しくしく泣(な)く: to whimper — similar quiet crying, but more childlike
