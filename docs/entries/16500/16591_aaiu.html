ああいう

ああいう
pre-noun adjectival
that kind of; like that
1. that kind of; like that
Refers to something of the type or manner being discussed or pointed out, typically something distant from both speaker and listener, or something previously mentioned. Modifies a following noun.
ああいう人(ひと)は苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not good with that kind of person.
ああいう場所(ばしょ)には行(い)きたくない。
I don't want to go to that kind of place.
ああいう言(い)い方(かた)をされると、誰(だれ)でも怒(おこ)ると思(おも)う。
I think anyone would get angry if spoken to in that way.

USAGE:
One of the こそあど (ko-so-a-do) demonstrative series. ああいう refers to something distant from both the speaker and listener, or something previously established in conversation.

RELATED FORMS:

  • こういう (this kind of — near the speaker)
  • そういう (that kind of — near the listener or recently mentioned)
  • ああいう (that kind of — distant from both)
  • どういう (what kind of — question form)

The casual spoken form is ああゆう.
