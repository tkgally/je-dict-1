出席率(しゅっせきりつ)

しゅっせきりつ
noun
attendance rate
1. attendance rate
The percentage of times a person or group attended required sessions such as classes, meetings, or events out of the total possible sessions.
出席率(しゅっせきりつ)が低(ひく)い。
The attendance rate is low.
出席率(しゅっせきりつ)が八割(はちわり)を下回(したまわ)ると単位(たんい)がもらえない。
If your attendance rate falls below 80%, you won't receive credit.
この授業(じゅぎょう)は出席率(しゅっせきりつ)も成績(せいせき)に反映(はんえい)される。
In this class, attendance rate is also reflected in the grade.

出席率(しゅっせきりつ) is the percentage of sessions attended out of the total. Composed of 出席(しゅっせき) (attendance) + 率(りつ) (rate/ratio).

USAGE:
Important in Japanese educational and visa contexts. Universities and language schools often set minimum 出席率(しゅっせきりつ) requirements (typically 80%) for receiving course credit. For international students, maintaining sufficient attendance is often a condition for visa renewal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出席率(しゅっせきりつ)が高(たか)い: high attendance rate
  • 出席率(しゅっせきりつ)が低(ひく)い: low attendance rate
  • 出席率(しゅっせきりつ)を上(あ)げる: to improve one's attendance rate
  • 出席率(しゅっせきりつ)が足(た)りない: attendance rate is insufficient

RELATED TERMS:

  • 出席(しゅっせき): attendance
  • 欠席(けっせき): absence
  • 出席日数(しゅっせきにっすう): number of days attended

Related Words
