興隆(こうりゅう)

こうりゅう
noun
rise; prosperity; flourishing
1. rise; prosperity; flourishing; ascendancy
The process of growing in power, influence, or prosperity. Often used to describe the rise of civilizations, industries, or movements.
文明(ぶんめい)の興隆(こうりゅう)と衰退(すいたい)。
The rise and decline of civilizations.
IT産業(さんぎょう)の興隆(こうりゅう)が経済(けいざい)を変(か)えた。
The rise of the IT industry changed the economy.
地域(ちいき)の文化(ぶんか)の興隆(こうりゅう)に力(ちから)を注(そそ)いでいる。
They are devoting effort to the flourishing of regional culture.

USAGE:
A formal, literary word. The verb form is 興隆(こうりゅう)する. Often paired with 衰退(すいたい) (decline) to describe historical cycles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 興隆(こうりゅう)と衰退(すいたい) (rise and decline)
  • 文化(ぶんか)の興隆(こうりゅう) (cultural flourishing)
  • 産業(さんぎょう)の興隆(こうりゅう) (industrial rise)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 繁栄(はんえい): prosperity — emphasizes the state of being prosperous
  • 隆盛(りゅうせい): thriving, prosperity — similar but focuses on the peak
