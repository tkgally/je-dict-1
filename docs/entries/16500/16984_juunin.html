重任(じゅうにん)

じゅうにん
noun
heavy responsibility; important post
1. heavy responsibility; important duty; important post
A position or duty that carries significant responsibility and weight. Used formally to describe roles that require great commitment.
重任(じゅうにん)を担(にな)う。
To bear a heavy responsibility.
社長(しゃちょう)という重任(じゅうにん)を引(ひ)き受(う)けた。
I accepted the weighty responsibility of being company president.
若(わか)くして重任(じゅうにん)に就(つ)いた彼(かれ)は、周囲(しゅうい)の期待(きたい)に応(こた)えようと努力(どりょく)した。
Having taken on an important position at a young age, he worked hard to meet the expectations of those around him.

USAGE:
A formal word used in business, politics, and official contexts. Emphasizes the weight and seriousness of a responsibility or position.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重任(じゅうにん)を担(にな)う (to bear a heavy responsibility)
  • 重任(じゅうにん)を果(は)たす (to fulfill an important duty)
  • 重任(じゅうにん)に就(つ)く (to take on an important post)
  • 重任(じゅうにん)を引(ひ)き受(う)ける (to accept a weighty responsibility)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重責(じゅうせき): heavy responsibility — very similar, emphasizes the burden
  • 大任(たいにん): important task — emphasizes the scale of the duty
