指導案(しどうあん)

しどうあん
noun
lesson plan; teaching plan
1. lesson plan; teaching plan
A detailed plan for a lesson prepared by a teacher, outlining objectives, activities, materials, and assessment methods. A standard document in Japanese education.
指導案(しどうあん)を作成(さくせい)した。
I prepared a lesson plan.
研究授業(けんきゅうじゅぎょう)のために指導案(しどうあん)を書(か)き直(なお)した。
I rewrote the lesson plan for the research lesson.
新任(しんにん)の先生(せんせい)は指導案(しどうあん)の書(か)き方(かた)を先輩(せんぱい)に教(おそ)わった。
The new teacher learned how to write lesson plans from senior colleagues.

USAGE:
A fundamental document in Japanese teaching practice. Teachers create 指導案 for each class, and more detailed versions are prepared for 研究授業 (research lessons observed by other teachers). The full formal name is 学習指導案(がくしゅうしどうあん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指導案(しどうあん)を書(か)く (to write a lesson plan)
  • 指導案(しどうあん)を作成(さくせい)する (to prepare a lesson plan)
  • 学習(がくしゅう)指導案(しどうあん) (formal lesson plan)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カリキュラム: curriculum — the overall course of study
  • 時間割(じかんわり): timetable — the schedule of classes
