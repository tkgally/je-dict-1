うっとうしい

うっとうしい
adjective (i-adjective)
gloomy; annoying; bothersome
1. gloomy; dreary; depressing
Describes weather or atmosphere that feels heavy, overcast, and oppressive. Commonly used for rainy season weather.
うっとうしい天気(てんき)が続(つづ)く。
The gloomy weather continues.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)はうっとうしい日(ひ)が多(おお)い。
There are many dreary days during the rainy season.
雨(あめ)が何日(なんにち)も降(ふ)り続(つづ)いてうっとうしい気分(きぶん)になった。
The rain continued for days and I felt gloomy.
2. annoying; bothersome; irritating
Describes something or someone that is persistently annoying, getting in the way, or hard to deal with.
髪(かみ)がうっとうしい。
My hair is getting in the way.
前髪(まえがみ)が目(め)にかかってうっとうしい。
My bangs are hanging in my eyes and it's annoying.
あの人(ひと)はいつもうっとうしいことばかり言(い)うので避(さ)けている。
That person always says annoying things, so I avoid them.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (gloomy weather) is the original meaning and is commonly associated with 梅雨(つゆ) (rainy season). Sense 2 (annoying) is widely used in everyday conversation, especially when something is physically in the way (like hair) or when a person is being persistently bothersome.

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Usually written in hiragana. The kanji form 鬱陶(うっとう)しい exists but is rarely used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • うっとうしい天気(てんき) (gloomy weather)
  • 髪(かみ)がうっとうしい (hair is in the way)
  • うっとうしい人(ひと) (annoying person)
