子供(こども)っぽい

こどもっぽい
adjective (i)
childish; immature
1. childish; immature; infantile
Describes behavior, appearance, or attitudes that seem inappropriately young or lacking in maturity for the person's age. Generally carries a negative nuance.
そんな子供(こども)っぽいことを言(い)うな。
Don't say such childish things.
彼(かれ)は見(み)た目(め)が子供(こども)っぽいが、中身(なかみ)はしっかりしている。
He looks childish, but he's solid on the inside.
もう二十歳(はたち)なのに、子供(こども)っぽい趣味(しゅみ)ばかりだと言(い)われた。
I was told that even though I'm twenty, I only have childish hobbies.

USAGE:
Used to describe someone whose behavior, appearance, or tastes seem immature for their age. Usually negative. The suffix っぽい attaches to nouns to mean "like ~" or "-ish."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子供(こども)っぽい態度(たいど) (childish attitude)
  • 子供(こども)っぽい顔(かお) (baby face, youthful face)
  • 子供(こども)っぽい趣味(しゅみ) (childish hobbies)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 幼稚(ようち) is harsher and more critical than 子供(こども)っぽい
  • 子供(こども)らしい means "child-like" in a positive sense, while 子供(こども)っぽい is usually negative
