中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)

ちゅうかんほうこく
noun
interim report; progress report
1. interim report; progress report
A report given at a midpoint or during the course of a project, investigation, or study, before the final results are available.
中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I submitted an interim report.
調査(ちょうさ)の中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)が来週(らいしゅう)発表(はっぴょう)される。
The interim report of the investigation will be announced next week.
委員会(いいんかい)は中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)で問題点(もんだいてん)を指摘(してき)した。
The committee pointed out problems in the interim report.

USAGE:
Used in business, academic, and governmental contexts. A 中間報告 is given before work is complete, typically to update stakeholders on progress and preliminary findings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)を出(だ)す (to issue an interim report)
  • 中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)を発表(はっぴょう)する (to announce a progress report)
  • 中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく)書(しょ) (interim report document)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最終報告(さいしゅうほうこく): final report — the completed report at the end of a project
  • 経過報告(けいかほうこく): progress report — similar meaning, emphasizes the ongoing nature
