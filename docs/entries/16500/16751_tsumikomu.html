1. to load; to load up; to pack into
To load cargo, luggage, or goods into a vehicle, container, or ship.
荷物(にもつ)を車(くるま)に積(つ)み込(こ)んだ。
I loaded the luggage into the car.
トラックに大量(たいりょう)の段(だん)ボールを積(つ)み込(こ)む。
To load a large number of cardboard boxes onto a truck.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの日(ひ)、朝(あさ)から家具(かぐ)をトラックに積(つ)み込(こ)む作業(さぎょう)に追(お)われた。
On moving day, I was busy loading furniture onto the truck from early morning.

積(つ)み込(こ)む means to load or pack cargo into a vehicle or container.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [object]を[destination]に積(つ)み込(こ)む

ASPECT (ている):
積(つ)み込(こ)んでいる can mean either "is in the process of loading" or "has been loaded" (resulting state), depending on context.

FORMATION:
A compound verb from 積(つ)む (to pile up, to load) + 込(こ)む (into). The ～込(こ)む suffix adds the nuance of packing things into a confined space.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を積(つ)み込(こ)む: to load luggage
  • 車(くるま)に積(つ)み込(こ)む: to load into a car
  • トラックに積(つ)み込(こ)む: to load onto a truck

CONTRAST:

  • 積(つ)み下(お)ろす / 荷下(におろ)し: unloading (the opposite action)
  • 積(つ)む: simply to stack or load (without the "into" nuance)
