焙煎(ばいせん)

ばいせん
noun
roasting (coffee beans, tea leaves)
1. roasting; dry-roasting
The process of roasting raw materials by applying dry heat, most commonly associated with coffee beans but also applicable to tea leaves and other foods. A key step that develops flavor and aroma.
この豆(まめ)は深(ふか)煎(い)りで焙煎(ばいせん)されている。
These beans are dark-roasted.
自家焙煎(じかばいせん)のコーヒーショップが近所(きんじょ)にできた。
An in-house roasting coffee shop opened in my neighborhood.
焙煎(ばいせん)の度合(どあ)いによってコーヒーの味(あじ)や香(かお)りが大(おお)きく変(か)わる。
The taste and aroma of coffee change significantly depending on the degree of roasting.

USAGE:
Most commonly associated with coffee. The word has become widely known in Japan with the growth of specialty coffee culture. Can be used as a noun or with する as a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焙煎(ばいせん)する (to roast)
  • 自家焙煎(じかばいせん) (in-house roasting)
  • 焙煎(ばいせん)度(ど) / 焙煎(ばいせん)の度合(どあ)い (degree of roasting)
  • 浅煎(あさい)り (light roast)
  • 中煎(ちゅうい)り (medium roast)
  • 深煎(ふかい)り (dark roast)
  • 焙煎機(ばいせんき) (roasting machine, roaster)

FORMATION:
焙(ばい) (to roast, to parch) + 煎(せん) (to decoct, to roast).
