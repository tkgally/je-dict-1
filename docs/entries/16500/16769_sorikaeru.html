1. to bend backward; to lean back; to throw back one's body
To arch or bend the body backward. Can also describe an arrogant posture of leaning back with one's chest out.
椅子(いす)に反(そ)り返(かえ)って座(すわ)る。
To sit leaning back in a chair.
偉(えら)そうに反(そ)り返(かえ)って話(はな)す人(ひと)は苦手(にがて)だ。
I don't like people who talk with an arrogant, leaned-back posture.
体操選手(たいそうせんしゅ)が美(うつく)しく反(そ)り返(かえ)った。
The gymnast arched backward beautifully.
2. to warp; to curl up; to bend
For objects like paper, wood, or metal to bend or curl due to heat, moisture, or other forces.
板(いた)が反(そ)り返(かえ)っている。
The board is warped.
湿気(しっけ)で紙(かみ)が反(そ)り返(かえ)ってしまった。
The paper curled up from the humidity.
熱(ねつ)で金属板(きんぞくばん)が反(そ)り返(かえ)って使(つか)えなくなった。
The metal plate warped from the heat and became unusable.

反(そ)り返(かえ)る is a compound verb from 反(そ)る (to bend/warp) + 返(かえ)る (to return/reverse). The 返(かえ)る intensifies the bending, suggesting a thorough or pronounced bend.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pattern: Xが反(そ)り返(かえ)る

ASPECT (ている):

  • 反(そ)り返(かえ)っている: is bent/warped (resulting state)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 椅子(いす)に反(そ)り返(かえ)る: to lean back in a chair
  • 偉(えら)そうに反(そ)り返(かえ)る: to lean back arrogantly (implies smugness)
  • 板(いた)が反(そ)り返(かえ)る: a board warps
  • 紙(かみ)が反(そ)り返(かえ)る: paper curls up

USAGE:
For people, often implies an arrogant or self-satisfied posture. For objects, describes physical warping caused by heat, moisture, or age.
