搬入(はんにゅう)

はんにゅう
noun / verb (suru)
carrying in; moving in; delivery
1. carrying in; bringing in; delivery (of goods or equipment)
The act of transporting goods, equipment, or materials into a building, venue, or designated area. Used in logistics, event planning, and business contexts.
機材(きざい)の搬入(はんにゅう)は午前中(ごぜんちゅう)に行(おこな)う。
Equipment delivery will be done in the morning.
展示品(てんじひん)の搬入(はんにゅう)が予定(よてい)より遅(おく)れている。
The delivery of the exhibition items is running behind schedule.
イベント前日(ぜんじつ)に大量(たいりょう)の荷物(にもつ)を会場(かいじょう)に搬入(はんにゅう)しなければならないので、トラックを二台(にだい)手配(てはい)した。
Since we need to bring a large amount of cargo into the venue the day before the event, we arranged for two trucks.

USAGE:
Used as a noun or with する. Common in business, logistics, and event planning. The opposite is 搬出(はんしゅつ) (carrying out, removal).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 搬入(はんにゅう)する (to carry in, to deliver)
  • 搬入(はんにゅう)口(ぐち) (delivery entrance)
  • 搬入(はんにゅう)経路(けいろ) (delivery route)
  • 搬入(はんにゅう)作業(さぎょう) (loading-in work)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 搬出(はんしゅつ): carrying out — the opposite action of removing goods from a location
  • 納品(のうひん): delivery of goods — specifically delivering ordered merchandise to a client
