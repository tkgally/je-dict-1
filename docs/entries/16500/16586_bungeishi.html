文芸誌(ぶんげいし)

ぶんげいし
noun
literary magazine
1. literary magazine
A periodical publication that features literary works such as novels, short stories, poetry, essays, and literary criticism.
文芸誌(ぶんげいし)に小説(しょうせつ)を投稿(とうこう)した。
I submitted a novel to a literary magazine.
この文芸誌(ぶんげいし)は毎月(まいつき)発行(はっこう)されている。
This literary magazine is published monthly.
有名(ゆうめい)な文芸誌(ぶんげいし)に掲載(けいさい)されることは、作家(さっか)にとって大(おお)きな目標(もくひょう)だ。
Being published in a famous literary magazine is a major goal for writers.

USAGE:
Refers to magazines dedicated to literature, as opposed to general-interest magazines. Japan has a strong tradition of literary magazines, and many famous authors were first published in them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文芸誌(ぶんげいし)に掲載(けいさい)する (to publish in a literary magazine)
  • 文芸誌(ぶんげいし)の新人賞(しんじんしょう) (literary magazine newcomer award)
  • 総合(そうごう)文芸誌(ぶんげいし) (general literary magazine)
