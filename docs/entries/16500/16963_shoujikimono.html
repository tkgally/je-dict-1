正直者(しょうじきもの)

しょうじきもの
noun
honest person
1. honest person; straightforward person
A person who is truthful and sincere. Can be used positively (praising honesty) or with a nuance of being naive or too trusting.
彼(かれ)は正直者(しょうじきもの)だ。
He is an honest person.
正直者(しょうじきもの)が馬鹿(ばか)を見(み)る世(よ)の中(なか)だ。
It's a world where honest people end up getting the short end of the stick.
昔話(むかしばなし)では正直者(しょうじきもの)が最後(さいご)に幸(しあわ)せになる。
In folk tales, the honest person finds happiness in the end.

USAGE:
The expression 正直者(しょうじきもの)が馬鹿(ばか)を見(み)る (honest people get the short end of the stick) is a well-known proverb expressing the idea that being too honest can be disadvantageous. In Japanese folk tales (昔話(むかしばなし)), the honest character is typically rewarded while the dishonest one is punished.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正直者(しょうじきもの)が馬鹿(ばか)を見(み)る (honesty doesn't pay)
  • 正直者(しょうじきもの)は得(とく)をする (honesty pays off)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誠実(せいじつ)な人(ひと): sincere person — more formal
  • 素直(すなお)な人(ひと): straightforward, obedient person — emphasizes being receptive and unresisting
