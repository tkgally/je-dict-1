仰(おっしゃ)る通(とお)り

おっしゃるとおり
expression
just as you say; exactly right
1. just as you say; exactly as you say; you're absolutely right
A polite expression used to agree wholeheartedly with what someone has said. The respectful form of その通(とお)り.
仰(おっしゃ)る通(とお)りです。
You are exactly right.
先生(せんせい)の仰(おっしゃ)る通(とお)り、もっと練習(れんしゅう)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Just as the teacher says, more practice is needed.
仰(おっしゃ)る通(とお)り、現状(げんじょう)のままでは問題(もんだい)が解決(かいけつ)しません。
As you rightly point out, the problem won't be solved if things stay as they are.

USAGE:
A highly polite expression of agreement, appropriate in business meetings, formal conversations, and when speaking to superiors. 仰(おっしゃ)る is the honorific form of 言(い)う.

FORMALITY SCALE:

  • その通(とお)り (neutral)
  • 言(い)う通(とお)り (casual)
  • おっしゃる通(とお)り (formal/honorific)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • その通(とお)り: that's right — neutral level of agreement
  • まさに: exactly — emphasizes precision of the point
