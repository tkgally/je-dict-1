秋季(しゅうき)

しゅうき
noun
autumn season
1. autumn season; fall season
A formal term for the autumn season, used especially in compound words and official contexts such as event names and academic schedules.
秋季(しゅうき)入学(にゅうがく)の制度(せいど)がある。
There is an autumn enrollment system.
秋季(しゅうき)大会(たいかい)の日程(にってい)が決(き)まった。
The schedule for the autumn tournament has been decided.
秋季(しゅうき)限定(げんてい)メニューが人気(にんき)を集(あつ)めている。
The autumn-only menu is gaining popularity.

USAGE:
A formal, written-style word for autumn. In everyday conversation, 秋(あき) is used instead. 秋季(しゅうき) appears in official event names, academic schedules, and sports contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 秋季(しゅうき)大会(たいかい) (autumn tournament)
  • 秋季(しゅうき)入学(にゅうがく) (autumn enrollment)
  • 秋季(しゅうき)限定(げんてい) (autumn-only)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 秋(あき): everyday word for autumn
  • 春季(しゅんき): spring season (formal equivalent for spring)
