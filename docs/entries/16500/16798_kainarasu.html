飼(か)い慣(な)らす

かいならす
verb (godan)
to tame; to domesticate
1. to tame; to domesticate
To raise and train a wild animal until it becomes accustomed to humans. Can also be used figuratively to mean bringing someone under control.
野生(やせい)の馬(うま)を飼(か)い慣(な)らす。
To tame a wild horse.
飼(か)い慣(な)らされた鷹(たか)は人(ひと)の腕(うで)に止(と)まる。
A tamed hawk perches on a person's arm.
人間(にんげん)は何千年(なんぜんねん)もかけて狼(おおかみ)を飼(か)い慣(な)らし、犬(いぬ)にしたと言(い)われている。
It is said that humans tamed wolves over thousands of years and turned them into dogs.

USAGE:
A compound verb from 飼(か)う (to keep/raise an animal) + 慣(な)らす (to accustom). The passive form 飼(か)い慣(な)らされた is commonly used as a modifier meaning "tamed" or "domesticated."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 動物(どうぶつ)を飼(か)い慣(な)らす (to tame an animal)
  • 飼(か)い慣(な)らされた鳥(とり) (a tamed bird)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手懐(てなず)ける: to tame — emphasizes winning over trust through gentleness
  • 家畜化(かちくか)する: to domesticate — more scientific/formal term
