1. land development; land preparation; reclamation
The process of preparing or developing land for construction, including leveling, filling, and converting raw land into usable building sites.
宅地(たくち)を造成(ぞうせい)する。
To develop residential land.
山(やま)を切(き)り開(ひら)いて住宅地(じゅうたくち)を造成(ぞうせい)した。
They cleared the mountainside to develop a residential area.
大規模(だいきぼ)な造成(ぞうせい)工事(こうじ)が行(おこな)われており、新(あたら)しい街(まち)ができる予定(よてい)だ。
Large-scale land development work is underway, and a new town is planned.

造成(ぞうせい) refers specifically to preparing raw land for construction — leveling, filling, grading, and converting natural terrain into usable building sites.

WORD FORMATION:
造(ぞう) (create, build) + 成(せい) (form, complete)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 宅地(たくち)造成(ぞうせい): residential land development
  • 造成(ぞうせい)工事(こうじ): land development work
  • 造成(ぞうせい)地(ち): developed land
  • 大規模(だいきぼ)造成(ぞうせい): large-scale land development

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開発(かいはつ): development (broader — includes buildings, technology, products)
  • 埋(う)め立(た)て: land reclamation (specifically filling in water areas)

REGISTER:
A technical term used in real estate, urban planning, and construction. Rarely appears in casual conversation.
