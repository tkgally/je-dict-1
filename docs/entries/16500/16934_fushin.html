不審(ふしん)

ふしん
adjective-na
suspicious; questionable; dubious
1. suspicious; questionable; dubious
Describes something or someone that arouses suspicion or doubt. Often used in the context of suspicious behavior, unidentified persons, or doubtful circumstances.
不審(ふしん)な人物(じんぶつ)を見(み)かけた。
I spotted a suspicious person.
不審(ふしん)な荷物(にもつ)があったら、すぐに駅員(えきいん)に知(し)らせてください。
If you see a suspicious package, please notify station staff immediately.
彼(かれ)の説明(せつめい)には不審(ふしん)な点(てん)が多(おお)く、警察(けいさつ)はさらに調(しら)べることにした。
There were many questionable points in his explanation, and the police decided to investigate further.

USAGE:
Often used as a modifier before nouns: 不審(ふしん)な人(ひと), 不審(ふしん)な行動(こうどう), 不審(ふしん)な点(てん). Very common in police reports, news, and public safety announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不審(ふしん)者(しゃ) (suspicious person)
  • 不審(ふしん)な行動(こうどう) (suspicious behavior)
  • 不審(ふしん)な点(てん) (questionable point)
  • 不審(ふしん)物(ぶつ) (suspicious object)
  • 不審(ふしん)死(し) (suspicious death)
  • 不審(ふしん)に思(おも)う (to find something suspicious)

DO NOT CONFUSE:

  • 不振(ふしん): slump, poor performance — same reading, completely different meaning (e.g., 営業不振(えいぎょうふしん) "business slump")
  • 不信(ふしん): distrust, disbelief — also same reading, different kanji (e.g., 政治不信(せいじふしん) "political distrust")
