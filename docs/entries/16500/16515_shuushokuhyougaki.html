就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)

しゅうしょくひょうがき
noun
employment ice age; job drought era
1. employment ice age; era of severe job scarcity
A period of extremely poor job prospects for new graduates in Japan, primarily referring to the late 1990s through early 2000s following the burst of the economic bubble. The generation affected is known as the 'ice age generation.'
就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)に社会(しゃかい)に出(で)た世代(せだい)だ。
It's the generation that entered the workforce during the employment ice age.
就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)には正社員(せいしゃいん)になれない若者(わかもの)が大勢(おおぜい)いた。
During the employment ice age, there were many young people who couldn't become full-time employees.
政府(せいふ)は就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)世代(せだい)の支援策(しえんさく)として、正規雇用(せいきこよう)への転換(てんかん)を促進(そくしん)するプログラムを実施(じっし)している。
The government is implementing programs to promote conversion to regular employment as a support measure for the ice age generation.

CULTURE:
The term was coined around 1994 to describe the sharp decline in new graduate hiring after the collapse of Japan's asset price bubble. The 就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)世代(せだい) (ice age generation), born roughly between 1970 and 1984, faced extremely competitive job markets. Many were forced into non-regular employment (非正規雇用(ひせいきこよう)), and this economic disadvantage has had lasting effects on their careers, income, and family formation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)世代(せだい) (the ice age generation)
  • 就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)の影響(えいきょう) (effects of the employment ice age)
  • 就職氷河期(しゅうしょくひょうがき)対策(たいさく) (measures to address the employment ice age)
