電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)

でんどうじてんしゃ
noun
electric bicycle; e-bike
1. electric bicycle; e-bike
A bicycle equipped with an electric motor that assists the rider's pedaling. Extremely popular in Japan, especially among parents with young children, commuters, and the elderly for navigating hilly terrain.
電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)を買(か)った。
I bought an electric bicycle.
坂道(さかみち)が多(おお)いので電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)が便利(べんり)だ。
Since there are many hills, an electric bicycle is convenient.
子供(こども)を乗(の)せて通勤(つうきん)するために、電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)の購入(こうにゅう)を検討(けんとう)している。
I'm considering buying an electric bicycle to commute with my child on board.

USAGE:
In Japan, this usually refers to 電動(でんどう)アシスト自転車(じてんしゃ) (electric-assist bicycle), where the motor only assists pedaling rather than providing full propulsion. Fully electric models that move without pedaling are classified differently and may require registration.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)のバッテリー (e-bike battery)
  • 電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ)を充電(じゅうでん)する (to charge an e-bike)
  • 子供(こども)乗(の)せ電動自転車(でんどうじてんしゃ) (child-carrying e-bike)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自転車(じてんしゃ): bicycle — the general term for any bicycle
  • 電動(でんどう)アシスト自転車(じてんしゃ): electric-assist bicycle — the formal/full name
