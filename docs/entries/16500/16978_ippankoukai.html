一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)

いっぱんこうかい
noun / verb (suru)
opening to the public; public release
1. opening to the public; making available to the general public
Making a place, event, or resource accessible to the general public, as opposed to being restricted to specific groups. Used for buildings, facilities, data, or events.
庭園(ていえん)が一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)された。
The garden was opened to the public.
皇居(こうきょ)は年(ねん)に二回(にかい)一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)される。
The Imperial Palace is opened to the public twice a year.
研究(けんきゅう)データを一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)して、だれでもアクセスできるようにした。
We made the research data publicly available so anyone can access it.

USAGE:
Combines 一般(いっぱん) (general, public) and 公開(こうかい) (opening, release). Used for physical locations (historical sites, government buildings) that are normally restricted, and also for digital content (data, software). A common phrase in news and announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)する (to open to the public)
  • 一般公開(いっぱんこうかい)日(び) (public opening day)
  • 期間(きかん)限定(げんてい)で一般公開(いっぱんこうかい) (limited-time public opening)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 公開(こうかい): release, disclosure — broader term, not necessarily for the general public
  • 開放(かいほう): opening up — emphasizes removing restrictions on access
