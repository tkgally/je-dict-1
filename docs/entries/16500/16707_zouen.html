1. landscape gardening, landscaping
The art and practice of designing and creating gardens and outdoor spaces. Includes both Japanese-style and Western-style garden design.
造園(ぞうえん)業者(ぎょうしゃ)に依頼(いらい)する。
To hire a landscaping company.
造園(ぞうえん)の技術(ぎじゅつ)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn landscaping techniques.
日本庭園(にほんていえん)の造園(ぞうえん)には長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Creating a Japanese garden requires many years of experience.

造園(ぞうえん) combines 造(ぞう) (create, build) + 園(えん) (garden, park). It covers both the art and the professional practice of designing and creating gardens and outdoor spaces.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 造園(ぞうえん)業(ぎょう): landscaping industry
  • 造園(ぞうえん)業者(ぎょうしゃ): landscaping company
  • 造園(ぞうえん)技術(ぎじゅつ): landscaping techniques
  • 造園(ぞうえん)設計(せっけい): landscape design
  • 造園(ぞうえん)家(か): landscape architect/gardener

AS A SURU VERB:
造園(ぞうえん)する means to landscape, to create a garden. Less common than the noun usage.

CULTURE:
Japanese 造園(ぞうえん) has a rich tradition encompassing styles like 枯山水(かれさんすい) (dry landscape gardens) and 回遊式庭園(かいゆうしきていえん) (strolling gardens). Professional landscapers may specialize in 日本庭園(にほんていえん) (Japanese gardens) or 洋風庭園(ようふうていえん) (Western-style gardens).
