造園(ぞうえん)

ぞうえん
noun, verb (suru)
landscape gardening, landscaping
1. landscape gardening, landscaping
The art and practice of designing and creating gardens and outdoor spaces. Includes both Japanese-style and Western-style garden design.
造園(ぞうえん)業者(ぎょうしゃ)に依頼(いらい)する。
To hire a landscaping company.
造園(ぞうえん)の技術(ぎじゅつ)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn landscaping techniques.
日本庭園(にほんていえん)の造園(ぞうえん)には長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Creating a Japanese garden requires many years of experience.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 造園(ぞうえん)業(ぎょう): landscaping industry
  • 造園(ぞうえん)業者(ぎょうしゃ): landscaping company
  • 造園(ぞうえん)技術(ぎじゅつ): landscaping techniques
  • 造園(ぞうえん)設計(せっけい): landscape design
