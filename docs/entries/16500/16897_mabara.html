疎(まば)ら

まばら
na-adjective
sparse; scattered; thin
1. sparse; scattered; thin; patchy
Spread out with gaps in between, not dense or closely packed. Describes things that are few and far between.
客(きゃく)は疎(まば)らだった。
The customers were sparse.
雨(あめ)が疎(まば)らに降(ふ)っている。
Rain is falling sparsely.
平日(へいじつ)の午前中(ごぜんちゅう)は人(ひと)が疎(まば)らで、ゆっくり買(か)い物(もの)ができる。
On weekday mornings the crowd is thin, so you can shop at your leisure.

USAGE:
Often written in hiragana (まばら). Describes people, objects, hair, vegetation, or anything that should be denser but has noticeable gaps. Can modify nouns with な or be used predicatively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)が疎(まば)ら (few people, thinly populated)
  • 疎(まば)らな拍手(はくしゅ) (scattered applause)
  • 髪(かみ)が疎(まば)ら (thin/sparse hair)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 少(すく)ない: few — simply indicates small quantity
  • ちらほら: here and there — adverb describing scattered occurrence
