一昨日(いっさくじつ)

いっさくじつ
noun
the day before yesterday (formal)
1. the day before yesterday (formal)
The formal or literary reading of 一昨日, used in written language, news, and formal speech. The everyday reading is おととい.
一昨日(いっさくじつ)の会議(かいぎ)の議事録(ぎじろく)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the minutes from the meeting the day before yesterday.
一昨日(いっさくじつ)から気温(きおん)が急(きゅう)に下(さ)がった。
The temperature dropped suddenly starting from the day before yesterday.
一昨日(いっさくじつ)付(づ)けの新聞(しんぶん)に関連(かんれん)記事(きじ)が掲載(けいさい)されていた。
A related article was published in the newspaper dated the day before yesterday.

USAGE:
The formal reading いっさくじつ is used in news, official documents, and formal speech. In everyday conversation, おととい is much more common. Both readings use the same kanji 一昨日.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おととい: the day before yesterday — the everyday reading of the same word
  • 一昨年(いっさくねん): the year before last — the same pattern applied to years
