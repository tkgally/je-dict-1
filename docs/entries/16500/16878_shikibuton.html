敷布団(しきぶとん)

しきぶとん
noun
bottom futon; mattress futon
1. bottom futon; mattress futon; futon mattress
The futon placed on the floor to sleep on, as opposed to the top futon (comforter) placed over the body. Part of the traditional Japanese bedding set.
敷布団(しきぶとん)を敷(し)いてから寝(ね)る。
I lay out the bottom futon before going to sleep.
天気(てんき)がいいので、敷布団(しきぶとん)を干(ほ)した。
The weather was nice, so I aired out the futon mattress.
この敷布団(しきぶとん)は厚(あつ)みがあって、畳(たたみ)の上(うえ)でも快適(かいてき)に眠(ねむ)れる。
This futon mattress is thick enough to sleep comfortably on tatami.

USAGE:
Pairs with 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん) (top futon/comforter). Together they form a complete Japanese bedding set. Stored in a 押(お)し入(い)れ (closet) during the day.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敷布団(しきぶとん)を敷(し)く (to lay out the futon mattress)
  • 敷布団(しきぶとん)を干(ほ)す (to air out the futon mattress)
  • 敷布団(しきぶとん)を畳(たた)む (to fold up the futon mattress)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 掛(か)け布団(ぶとん): top futon, comforter — the blanket-like futon placed on top
