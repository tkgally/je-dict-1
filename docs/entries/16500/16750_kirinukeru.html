切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける

きりぬける
verb (ichidan)
to get through; to overcome
1. to get through; to overcome; to pull through
To survive or overcome a difficult situation, crisis, or challenge. Emphasizes managing to escape or endure something tough.
危機(きき)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)けた。
We got through the crisis.
なんとか困難(こんなん)な状況(じょうきょう)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)けることができた。
We somehow managed to get through the difficult situation.
会社(かいしゃ)は不景気(ふけいき)の時期(じき)をうまく切(き)り抜(ぬ)けて、今(いま)では業績(ぎょうせき)が回復(かいふく)している。
The company successfully weathered the recession and its performance has now recovered.

FORMATION:
A compound verb from 切(き)る (to cut) + 抜(ぬ)ける (to pass through). The image is of cutting one's way through obstacles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 危機(きき)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける (to get through a crisis)
  • 困難(こんなん)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける (to overcome difficulty)
  • 窮地(きゅうち)を切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける (to escape a tight spot)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乗(の)り越(こ)える: to overcome — emphasizes conquering rather than surviving
  • 凌(しの)ぐ: to endure, to weather — focuses on endurance
