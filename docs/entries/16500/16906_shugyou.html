修業(しゅぎょう)

しゅぎょう
noun
training; apprenticeship; learning a trade
1. training; apprenticeship; learning a trade; completing a course of study
The process of learning skills and knowledge through practical training, especially in a craft, trade, or profession. Emphasizes the learning and mastery aspect.
料理(りょうり)の修業(しゅぎょう)をする。
To undergo training in cooking.
彼(かれ)は京都(きょうと)で十年(じゅうねん)修業(しゅぎょう)した職人(しょくにん)だ。
He's a craftsman who trained for ten years in Kyoto.
まだ修業(しゅぎょう)が足(た)りないと師匠(ししょう)に言(い)われた。
My master told me that my training is still insufficient.

USAGE:
Distinct from 修行(しゅぎょう) (ascetic/spiritual training). 修業(しゅぎょう) focuses on practical skill development and professional apprenticeship, while 修行(しゅぎょう) focuses on spiritual or moral discipline. The verb form is 修業(しゅぎょう)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 修業(しゅぎょう)を積(つ)む (to accumulate training experience)
  • 修業(しゅぎょう)時代(じだい) (apprenticeship period)
  • 修業(しゅぎょう)年限(ねんげん) (required training period)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 修行(しゅぎょう): ascetic/spiritual training — same reading, different kanji and meaning
  • 見習(みなら)い: apprenticeship — focuses on learning by watching
