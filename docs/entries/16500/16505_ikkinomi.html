一気飲(いっきの)み

いっきのみ
noun / verb (suru)
chugging; downing a drink in one go
1. chugging; downing a drink in one go; draining a glass
The act of drinking an entire beverage without stopping, typically alcoholic drinks. Often done under social pressure at parties and gatherings.
一気飲(いっきの)みは危険(きけん)だ。
Chugging drinks is dangerous.
大学(だいがく)の飲(の)み会(かい)で一気飲(いっきの)みを強要(きょうよう)された。
I was pressured to chug at a college drinking party.
急性(きゅうせい)アルコール中毒(ちゅうどく)の事故(じこ)が続(つづ)いたため、多(おお)くの大学(だいがく)が一気飲(いっきの)みを禁止(きんし)している。
Because incidents of acute alcohol poisoning have continued, many universities have banned chugging.

CULTURE:
A practice associated with Japanese drinking culture, particularly at university welcome parties (新歓(しんかん)コンパ). It has been a serious social issue due to deaths from acute alcohol poisoning. Most universities and companies now prohibit it. The chant "一気(いっき)！一気(いっき)！" (Chug! Chug!) is used to pressure someone into drinking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一気飲(いっきの)みをする (to chug)
  • 一気飲(いっきの)みを強要(きょうよう)する (to pressure someone to chug)
  • 一気飲(いっきの)み禁止(きんし) (ban on chugging)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一気(いっき)に飲(の)む: to drink in one go — verb phrase rather than noun
