類推(るいすい)

るいすい
noun
analogy; inference by analogy
1. analogy; inference by analogy; analogical reasoning
The process of reasoning from known similarities to infer additional similarities. Drawing conclusions about something based on its resemblance to something else.
類推(るいすい)から判断(はんだん)する。
To judge by analogy.
過去(かこ)の事例(じれい)から類推(るいすい)すると、同(おな)じ結果(けっか)になるだろう。
Reasoning by analogy from past cases, the result will likely be the same.
他(ほか)の言語(げんご)の知識(ちしき)から類推(るいすい)して、新(あたら)しい言語(げんご)の文法(ぶんぽう)を理解(りかい)しようとした。
I tried to understand the grammar of the new language by drawing analogies from my knowledge of other languages.

USAGE:
Used as a suru verb (類推(るいすい)する). Common in academic, logical, and linguistic contexts. In linguistics, 類推(るいすい) refers to the process by which irregular forms are regularized based on patterns from similar words.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 類推(るいすい)する (to reason by analogy)
  • 類推(るいすい)から (from analogy)
  • 類推(るいすい)解釈(かいしゃく) (analogical interpretation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推測(すいそく): conjecture, guess — broader meaning, not necessarily based on analogy
  • 推論(すいろん): inference, deduction — more general logical reasoning
