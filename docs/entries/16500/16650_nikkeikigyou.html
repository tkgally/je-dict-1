日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)

にっけいきぎょう
noun
Japanese company (overseas)
1. Japanese company operating overseas
A company of Japanese origin or with Japanese capital that operates in a foreign country. The term emphasizes the Japanese identity of the company as seen from an overseas perspective.
日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)で働(はたら)いている。
I work at a Japanese company.
タイには多(おお)くの日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)が進出(しんしゅつ)している。
Many Japanese companies have expanded into Thailand.
現地(げんち)の日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)は日本語(にほんご)ができる人材(じんざい)を求(もと)めている。
Local Japanese companies are looking for personnel who can speak Japanese.

USAGE:
Used when discussing Japanese businesses from the perspective of their overseas operations. Common in business news, job listings, and discussions of international commerce.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)に就職(しゅうしょく)する (to get a job at a Japanese company abroad)
  • 日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)の進出(しんしゅつ) (expansion of Japanese companies overseas)
  • 日系企業(にっけいきぎょう)向(む)け (aimed at Japanese companies)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 外資系(がいしけい): foreign-affiliated — the reverse perspective, a foreign company operating in Japan
