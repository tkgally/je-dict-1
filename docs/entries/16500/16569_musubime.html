結(むす)び目(め)

むすびめ
noun
knot
1. knot; tied portion
The part where string, rope, thread, or similar material has been tied together. Refers to the physical point of a knot.
結(むす)び目(め)をしっかり作(つく)った。
I tied a firm knot.
結(むす)び目(め)が固(かた)くて解(ほど)けない。
The knot is too tight to untie.
靴紐(くつひも)の結(むす)び目(め)がほどけて、何度(なんど)も結(むす)び直(なお)した。
My shoelace knot kept coming undone, and I retied it many times.

USAGE:
A compound of 結(むす)ぶ (to tie) and 目(め) (point, spot). Refers specifically to the physical point where something is knotted. Used for ropes, strings, threads, shoelaces, and similar materials.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 結(むす)び目(め)を作(つく)る (to make a knot)
  • 結(むす)び目(め)を解(ほど)く (to untie a knot)
  • 結(むす)び目(め)が緩(ゆる)い (the knot is loose)
  • 結(むす)び目(め)が固(かた)い (the knot is tight)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 結(むす)ぶ: to tie — the verb form, describes the action rather than the result
