広告媒体(こうこくばいたい)

こうこくばいたい
noun
advertising medium; advertising channel
1. advertising medium; advertising channel
A platform or channel used to deliver advertisements to an audience, such as television, newspapers, the internet, or billboards.
テレビは有力(ゆうりょく)な広告媒体(こうこくばいたい)だ。
Television is a powerful advertising medium.
ネット広告(こうこく)は主要(しゅよう)な広告媒体(こうこくばいたい)になった。
Online advertising has become a major advertising medium.
商品(しょうひん)の特性(とくせい)に合(あ)った広告媒体(こうこくばいたい)を選(えら)ぶことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to choose an advertising medium suited to the product's characteristics.

USAGE:
Used in marketing and business contexts. Common advertising media discussed include television, newspapers, magazines, radio, the internet, and outdoor signage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 広告媒体(こうこくばいたい)を選(えら)ぶ (to choose an advertising medium)
  • 主要(しゅよう)広告媒体(こうこくばいたい) (major advertising medium)
  • デジタル広告媒体(こうこくばいたい) (digital advertising medium)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 媒体(ばいたい): medium — a more general term for any medium or vehicle
  • メディア: media — a loanword used broadly for mass media
