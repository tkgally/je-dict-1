複合機(ふくごうき)

ふくごうき
noun
multi-function printer; all-in-one machine
1. multi-function printer; all-in-one machine
A device that combines multiple functions such as printing, scanning, copying, and sometimes faxing into a single unit. Standard equipment in Japanese offices and increasingly common in homes.
複合機(ふくごうき)でコピー(こぴー)した。
I made a copy on the multi-function printer.
この複合機(ふくごうき)は両面(りょうめん)印刷(いんさつ)ができる。
This multi-function printer can do double-sided printing.
会社(かいしゃ)の複合機(ふくごうき)が故障(こしょう)したので、修理(しゅうり)を依頼(いらい)した。
The office multi-function printer broke down, so I requested a repair.

USAGE:
Commonly abbreviated as 複合機(ふくごうき) in everyday office language. In contrast, a single-function printer is simply プリンター(ぷりんたー). Most Japanese offices use a large shared multi-function printer rather than individual desk printers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 複合機(ふくごうき)で印刷(いんさつ)する (to print on a multi-function printer)
  • 複合機(ふくごうき)のリース(りーす) (leasing a multi-function printer)
  • 家庭用(かていよう)複合機(ふくごうき) (home multi-function printer)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • プリンター(ぷりんたー): printer — a device for printing only
  • コピー機(こぴーき): copier — a device primarily for copying
