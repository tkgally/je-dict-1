勧誘(かんゆう)

かんゆう
noun
solicitation; canvassing; recruitment
1. solicitation; canvassing; recruitment; invitation
The act of trying to persuade someone to join, buy, or participate in something. Often used for sales pitches, religious recruitment, or membership drives.
しつこい勧誘(かんゆう)に困(こま)っている。
I'm troubled by persistent solicitation.
電話(でんわ)での勧誘(かんゆう)はすべて断(ことわ)ることにしている。
I make it a rule to refuse all phone solicitations.
友人(ゆうじん)に勧誘(かんゆう)されて、テニスサークルに入(はい)った。
I was recruited by a friend and joined a tennis club.

USAGE:
Used as a suru-verb: 勧誘(かんゆう)する. Can be neutral (inviting someone to join a club) or negative (pushy sales tactics, religious recruitment). Context determines the nuance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勧誘(かんゆう)する (to solicit, to recruit)
  • 勧誘(かんゆう)を断(ことわ)る (to refuse a solicitation)
  • 勧誘(かんゆう)電話(でんわ) (solicitation call)
  • しつこい勧誘(かんゆう) (persistent solicitation)
