障子紙(しょうじがみ)

しょうじがみ
noun
shoji paper; paper for sliding screens
1. shoji paper
Thin, translucent white paper used to cover the wooden lattice frames of shoji sliding doors. It allows soft, diffused light to pass through while providing privacy.
障子紙(しょうじがみ)を張(は)り替(か)えた。
I replaced the shoji paper.
子供(こども)が障子紙(しょうじがみ)に穴(あな)を開(あ)けてしまった。
The child poked a hole in the shoji paper.
年末(ねんまつ)の大掃除(おおそうじ)で障子紙(しょうじがみ)を新(あたら)しくするのが毎年(まいとし)の恒例(こうれい)だ。
Replacing the shoji paper during the year-end cleaning is an annual tradition.

CULTURE:
Shoji paper is traditionally replaced at least once a year, often during the year-end cleaning (大掃除(おおそうじ)). The old paper is peeled off, the frame is cleaned, and fresh paper is pasted on. Modern alternatives include plastic-reinforced shoji paper that is more durable.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 障子紙(しょうじがみ)を張(は)る (to paste shoji paper)
  • 障子紙(しょうじがみ)を張(は)り替(か)える (to replace shoji paper)
  • 障子紙(しょうじがみ)が破(やぶ)れる (the shoji paper tears)
