朝型(あさがた)

あさがた
noun
morning person; early riser type
1. morning person; early riser type; morning-oriented
A person or lifestyle pattern characterized by waking up early and being most productive in the morning hours.
私(わたし)は朝型(あさがた)の人間(にんげん)だ。
I'm a morning person.
受験(じゅけん)勉強(べんきょう)のために朝型(あさがた)の生活(せいかつ)に切(き)り替(か)えた。
I switched to a morning-oriented lifestyle for exam preparation.
朝型(あさがた)に変(か)えてから、仕事(しごと)の効率(こうりつ)が上(あ)がった。
Since switching to a morning schedule, my work efficiency has improved.

朝型(あさがた) describes a morning-oriented person or lifestyle — someone who wakes early and is most active in the morning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 朝型(あさがた)の生活(せいかつ): morning-oriented lifestyle
  • 朝型(あさがた)の人間(にんげん): a morning person
  • 朝型(あさがた)に変(か)える/切(き)り替(か)える: to switch to a morning schedule

CONTRAST:

  • 夜型(よるがた): night-owl type (antonym)
  • 朝型(あさがた) vs. 夜型(よるがた) is a common topic in lifestyle, health, and productivity discussions in Japan.

FORMATION:
朝(あさ) (morning) + 型(がた) (type/pattern). The suffix 〜型(がた) is productive: 夜型(よるがた), A型(がた) (blood type A), etc.

Related Words

Antonym: 夜型(よるがた) (night-owl type)
