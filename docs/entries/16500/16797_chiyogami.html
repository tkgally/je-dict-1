千代紙(ちよがみ)

ちよがみ
noun
decorative patterned paper; chiyogami
1. decorative patterned paper; chiyogami
Traditional Japanese paper printed with colorful patterns such as flowers, geometric designs, or seasonal motifs. Used for origami, crafts, and decorative purposes.
千代紙(ちよがみ)で鶴(つる)を折(お)った。
I folded a crane with chiyogami paper.
お土産(みやげ)に千代紙(ちよがみ)のセットを買(か)った。
I bought a set of chiyogami paper as a souvenir.
千代紙(ちよがみ)の柄(がら)には季節(きせつ)の花(はな)や伝統的(でんとうてき)な模様(もよう)が多(おお)い。
Chiyogami patterns often feature seasonal flowers and traditional designs.

USAGE:
Originally from the Edo period, 千代紙(ちよがみ) was made by woodblock printing on washi paper. Today, mass-produced versions are widely available and popular as souvenirs for foreign visitors. The name comes from 千代(ちよ) (a thousand generations, meaning long-lasting) + 紙(かみ) (paper).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 千代紙(ちよがみ)で折(お)る (to fold with chiyogami)
  • 千代紙(ちよがみ)の柄(がら) (chiyogami pattern)
  • 和(わ)柄(がら)の千代紙(ちよがみ) (Japanese-patterned chiyogami)
