懇意(こんい)

こんい
noun
friendship; intimacy; close acquaintance
1. friendship; intimacy; close acquaintance
A close, friendly relationship, especially one built over time through mutual interaction. Often used with the na-adjective pattern 懇意な or the pattern 懇意にする.
あの人(ひと)とは懇意(こんい)にしている。
I'm on close terms with that person.
社長(しゃちょう)とは長年(ながねん)懇意(こんい)にさせていただいている。
I have been on close terms with the company president for many years.
懇意(こんい)にしている取引先(とりひきさき)から紹介(しょうかい)を受(う)けた。
I received an introduction from a business partner I'm close with.

USAGE:
Somewhat formal, often used in business and social contexts. The most common pattern is 懇意(こんい)にする / 懇意(こんい)にしている (to be on close terms). Also used as a na-adjective: 懇意(こんい)な間柄(あいだがら) (a close relationship).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懇意(こんい)にしている (to be on close terms)
  • 懇意(こんい)にさせていただく (to have the privilege of being close with — humble)
  • 懇意(こんい)な間柄(あいだがら) (a close relationship)
  • 懇意(こんい)な仲(なか) (close friendship)
