持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティー

もちよりぱーてぃー
noun
potluck party; a party where each guest brings a dish or drink to share
1. potluck party; a party where each guest brings a dish or drink to share
Common in both casual and semi-formal social gatherings.
週末(しゅうまつ)に持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティーをしよう。
Let's have a potluck party this weekend.
持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティーなので、一品(いっぴん)持参(じさん)してください。
Since it's a potluck party, please bring one dish.
手作(てづく)りの料理(りょうり)を持(も)って持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティーに参加(さんか)した。
I joined the potluck party with a homemade dish.

A compound of 持ち寄り(もちより) (bringing together/each contributing) and パーティー (party). Refers to a social gathering where participants each bring food or drinks to share, rather than a single host providing everything.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティーを開(ひら)く: to hold a potluck party
  • 持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティーに一品(いっぴん)持参(じさん): bring one dish to the potluck
  • 持(も)ち寄(よ)りパーティー形式(けいしき): potluck-style

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ポットラック: potluck (loanword, less common)
  • 飲(の)み会(かい): drinking party (usually at a restaurant)
  • ホームパーティー: home party (host provides food)

CULTURAL NOTES:
Potluck parties have become increasingly popular in Japan, especially among younger people and international communities. They offer a more affordable and interactive alternative to traditional 飲み会(のみかい) at restaurants. In Japanese potlucks, guests typically bring either homemade dishes or store-bought items like 惣菜(そうざい) (prepared dishes) or desserts.
