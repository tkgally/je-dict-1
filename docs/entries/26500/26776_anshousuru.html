1. to recite from memory; to recite by heart
To speak aloud a text, poem, or passage that one has memorized, without looking at the written source.
詩(し)を暗唱(あんしょう)する。
To recite a poem from memory.
彼女(かのじょ)はシェイクスピアの台詞(せりふ)を暗唱(あんしょう)できる。
She can recite Shakespeare's lines from memory.
小学生(しょうがくせい)の頃(ころ)、毎朝(まいあさ)九九(くく)を暗唱(あんしょう)させられた。
When I was in elementary school, I was made to recite the multiplication table every morning.

Refers specifically to the act of reciting something one has committed to memory. Often used in educational contexts where students memorize and recite texts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 詩(し)を暗唱(あんしょう)する: to recite a poem
  • 台詞(せりふ)を暗唱(あんしょう)する: to recite lines
  • 経文(きょうもん)を暗唱(あんしょう)する: to recite a sutra
  • すらすらと暗唱(あんしょう)する: to recite fluently

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 暗記(あんき)する: to memorize — focuses on the act of committing to memory rather than speaking aloud
  • 朗読(ろうどく)する: to read aloud — reading from a text rather than from memory
  • 唱(とな)える: to chant — often used for prayers or mantras, may not require memorization
