1. to spout; to gush; to spray
For liquid, steam, or gas to burst out forcefully from an opening or source.
水(みず)が噴(ふ)き出(だ)した。
Water gushed out.
鯨(くじら)が潮(しお)を噴(ふ)いている。
The whale is spouting water.
蒸気(じょうき)がやかんの口(くち)から勢(いきお)いよく噴(ふ)いている。
Steam is spouting forcefully from the spout of the kettle.
2. to erupt; to spew
For a volcano to discharge lava, ash, or gas. Also used figuratively for emotions or situations that burst out uncontrollably.
火山(かざん)が噴(ふ)いた。
The volcano erupted.
溶岩(ようがん)が噴(ふ)き出(だ)している。
Lava is spewing out.
長年(ながねん)の不満(ふまん)がついに噴(ふ)き出(だ)した。
Years of frustration finally erupted.

A godan verb describing the forceful ejection of liquid, gas, or lava. Distinguished from the homophone 吹(ふ)く (to blow) by focusing on liquid or molten substances rather than air or wind.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Both (自動詞(じどうし) and 他動詞(たどうし))
  • Intransitive: 水(みず)が噴(ふ)く (water gushes)
  • Transitive: 潮(しお)を噴(ふ)く (to spout water)

ASPECT:

  • 噴(ふ)いている: is currently spouting/erupting (ongoing action)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 潮(しお)を噴(ふ)く: to spout water (whales)
  • 蒸気(じょうき)を噴(ふ)く: to vent steam
  • 火(ひ)を噴(ふ)く: to spew fire
  • 噴(ふ)き出(だ)す: to gush out, to spew forth (compound verb)
  • 噴(ふ)き上(あ)げる: to spout upward

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吹(ふ)く: to blow — used for wind, breath, and musical instruments; never for liquids
  • 噴出(ふんしゅつ)する: to erupt, to gush — more formal and used in scientific or news contexts
