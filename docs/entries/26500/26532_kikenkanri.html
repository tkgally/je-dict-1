危険(きけん)管理(かんり)

きけんかんり
noun
hazard management; danger management; safety management
1. hazard management; danger management; safety management
The systematic identification and control of dangers and hazards, particularly in workplaces, industrial settings, and daily life. Focuses on preventing accidents and injuries.
工場(こうじょう)での危険(きけん)管理(かんり)を徹底(てってい)する。
We will thoroughly implement hazard management in the factory.
危険(きけん)管理(かんり)意識(いしき)が低(ひく)いと事故(じこ)につながる。
Low awareness of hazard management leads to accidents.
登山(とざん)では天候(てんこう)の変化(へんか)に対(たい)する危険(きけん)管理(かんり)が命(いのち)を守(まも)る。
In mountain climbing, hazard management regarding weather changes protects lives.

A compound of 危険(きけん) (danger; hazard) and 管理(かんり) (management; control). Distinguished from 危機(きき)管理(かんり) (crisis management, reactive) and リスク管理(かんり) (risk management, probabilistic), 危険(きけん)管理(かんり) emphasizes proactive identification and prevention of concrete dangers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 危険(きけん)管理(かんり)体制(たいせい): hazard management system
  • 危険(きけん)管理(かんり)意識(いしき): safety awareness
  • 危険(きけん)管理(かんり)マニュアル: hazard management manual
  • 危険(きけん)管理(かんり)教育(きょういく): safety management training

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 危機(きき)管理(かんり): crisis management — dealing with crises after they occur
  • リスク管理(かんり): risk management — assessing and mitigating probabilistic risks
  • 安全(あんぜん)管理(かんり): safety management — broader term for ensuring safety in general
