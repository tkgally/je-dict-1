1. progress report; status update
A report on the current state of progress of a project, task, or activity. Standard business and project management terminology.
週(しゅう)に一度(いちど)、進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)を行(おこな)う。
We give a progress report once a week.
上司(じょうし)に進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)のメールを送(おく)った。
I sent a progress report email to my boss.
進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)によると、プロジェクトは予定(よてい)どおり進(すす)んでいる。
According to the progress report, the project is on schedule.
毎朝(まいあさ)のミーティングで各自(かくじ)が進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)をする。
Each person gives a progress report at the morning meeting.

Essential business vocabulary in Japanese workplaces. Compound of 進捗 (progress, advancement) + 報告 (report). Regular progress reporting is a hallmark of Japanese corporate culture. Often abbreviated to 進捗 alone in casual workplace speech (進捗どうですか？ = How's the progress?).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)を行(おこな)う: to make a progress report
  • 進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)会議(かいぎ): progress report meeting
  • 進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく)メール: progress report email
  • 定期(ていき)進捗報告(しんちょくほうこく): regular progress report

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経過報告(けいかほうこく): interim report — emphasizes the passage of time and developments
  • 中間報告(ちゅうかんほうこく): mid-term report — report given at the halfway point
  • 状況報告(じょうきょうほうこく): situation report — focuses on current conditions rather than movement forward
