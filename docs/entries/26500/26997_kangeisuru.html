1. to welcome; to receive warmly
To greet or receive someone or something with pleasure and hospitality.
留学生(りゅうがくせい)を歓迎(かんげい)する。
To welcome exchange students.
新(あたら)しいメンバーを温(あたた)かく歓迎(かんげい)した。
We warmly welcomed the new members.
政府(せいふ)は海外(かいがい)からの投資(とうし)を歓迎(かんげい)する姿勢(しせい)を示(しめ)した。
The government showed a stance of welcoming investment from overseas.

Used both for welcoming people (guests, visitors, new members) and for welcoming abstract things (proposals, changes, investment). The noun form 歓迎(かんげい) is very common in signs and announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歓迎会(かんげいかい): welcome party
  • 歓迎(かんげい)の言葉(ことば): words of welcome
  • 大歓迎(だいかんげい): very welcome; a big welcome
  • ようこそ: welcome (greeting) — often paired with 歓迎(かんげい)
  • 歓迎(かんげい)ムード: welcoming atmosphere

USAGE:
The expression 大歓迎(だいかんげい) (greatly welcome) is frequently used in casual conversation to express strong approval: 初心者(しょしんしゃ)大歓迎(だいかんげい) (beginners very welcome).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 迎(むか)える: to go to meet; to welcome — more neutral; focuses on the physical act of receiving someone
  • {もてなす}: to entertain; to treat hospitably — emphasizes providing food, drink, or entertainment
  • 受(う)け入(い)れる: to accept; to take in — focuses on acceptance, less on warmth
