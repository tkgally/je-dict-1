罫線入(けいせんい)り

けいせんいり
noun
ruled; lined; with ruled lines
1. ruled; lined; with ruled lines
Having printed horizontal lines on the page, as in a lined notebook or ruled paper. A common product descriptor for stationery.
罫線入(けいせんい)りのノートを買(か)った。
I bought a lined notebook.
罫線入(けいせんい)りの便箋(びんせん)のほうが、きれいに書(か)ける。
Lined writing paper makes it easier to write neatly.
この手帳(てちょう)は罫線入(けいせんい)りのページと無地(むじ)のページが交互(こうご)になっている。
This planner alternates between lined pages and blank pages.

Composed of 罫線(けいせん) (ruled line) and 入(い)り (containing/with). The suffix 〜入(い)り is productive and means "containing" or "with," as in 砂糖入(さとうい)り (with sugar) or 絵入(えい)り (illustrated).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罫線入(けいせんい)りのノート: lined notebook
  • 罫線入(けいせんい)りの用紙(ようし): ruled paper
  • 罫線入(けいせんい)りの便箋(びんせん): lined writing paper

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 横罫(よこけい): horizontal ruling — specifies horizontal lines
  • 方眼(ほうがん): grid/graph — square grid pattern
  • 無地(むじ): plain/blank — no lines
  • 罫線(けいせん): ruled line — the base word
