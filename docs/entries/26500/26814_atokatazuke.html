1. cleanup; tidying up afterwards; putting things away
the work of cleaning up and putting things back in order after an activity, event, or meal
食事(しょくじ)の後片付(あとかたづ)けをする。
I'll clean up after the meal.
パーティーの後片付(あとかたづ)けに二時間(にじかん)かかった。
It took two hours to clean up after the party.
子供(こども)たちに遊(あそ)んだ後(あと)の後片付(あとかたづ)けを教(おし)えるのは大切(たいせつ)なことだ。
Teaching children to tidy up after playing is an important thing.

Refers specifically to the cleanup that follows an activity — distinct from general housekeeping. The word emphasizes returning things to their original state after use. Very common in domestic and workplace contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食事(しょくじ)の後片付(あとかたづ)け: cleanup after a meal
  • 後片付(あとかたづ)けをする: to do the cleanup
  • 後片付(あとかたづ)けを手伝(てつだ)う: to help with cleanup
  • 後片付(あとかたづ)けが大変(たいへん): the cleanup is a lot of work
  • イベントの後片付(あとかたづ)け: cleanup after an event

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 片付(かたづ)け: tidying up — general organizing/putting away, not specifically "after" something
  • 掃除(そうじ): cleaning — broader cleaning (sweeping, wiping), not about putting things back
  • 撤収(てっしゅう): removal/packing up — more formal, used for events, equipment, military

WORD FORMATION:
後(あと) (after) + 片付(かたづ)け (tidying). The 後(あと) specifies that this is cleanup following a specific activity.
