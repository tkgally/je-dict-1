1. to put on many layers of clothing; to bundle up; to dress warmly
To wear multiple layers of clothing, typically for warmth. Often implies dressing more heavily than usual in response to cold weather.
厚(あつ)く着込(きこ)む。
To bundle up warmly.
今日(きょう)は寒(さむ)いからたくさん着込(きこ)んだ。
I bundled up because it's cold today.
セーターの上(うえ)にコートまで着込(きこ)んで出(で)かけた。
I put on a coat over my sweater and went out.
子供(こども)たちは何枚(なんまい)も着込(きこ)んで雪遊(ゆきあそ)びに出(で)かけた。
The children bundled up in many layers and went out to play in the snow.

Compound of 着(き)る (to wear) and 込(こ)む (suffix indicating thoroughness or accumulation). The 込(こ)む adds the nuance of putting on clothes in abundance or layers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厚(あつ)く着込(きこ)む: to dress in thick layers
  • たくさん着込(きこ)む: to put on lots of layers
  • 何枚(なんまい)も着込(きこ)む: to wear many layers
  • しっかり着込(きこ)む: to bundle up properly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重(かさ)ね着(ぎ): layering clothes — a noun describing the fashion or practice of wearing layers
  • 着(き)る: to wear, put on — basic verb for wearing; no implication of multiple layers
  • 羽織(はお)る: to throw on, drape over — putting on a single outer garment casually
