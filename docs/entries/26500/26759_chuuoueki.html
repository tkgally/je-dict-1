中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)

ちゅうおうえき
noun
central station; main station
1. central station; main station
The main or central railway station in a city, typically the largest or most important station serving as the primary hub for train services. Used both as a general term and as part of specific station names.
中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)で降(お)りてください。
Please get off at the central station.
中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)の改札(かいさつ)口(ぐち)で待(ま)ち合(あ)わせた。
We arranged to meet at the ticket gate of the central station.
ヨーロッパの都市(とし)には「中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)」と名付(なづ)けられた大(おお)きな駅(えき)があることが多(おお)い。
Many European cities have large stations named 'Central Station.'

Composed of 中央(ちゅうおう) (center, central) and 駅(えき) (station). While Japan does not typically name stations this way (preferring city-name-based naming like 東京駅(とうきょうえき)), the term is commonly encountered when discussing foreign train stations or as a general descriptor. Some Japanese cities do have stations with 中央(ちゅうおう) in the name, such as 千葉中央駅(ちばちゅうおうえき).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)前(まえ): in front of the central station
  • 中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)から徒歩(とほ)で: on foot from the central station
  • 中央駅(ちゅうおうえき)周辺(しゅうへん): the area around the central station

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主要駅(しゅようえき): main station — emphasizes importance rather than location
  • 終着駅(しゅうちゃくえき): terminal station — the last stop on a line
  • 乗換駅(のりかええき): transfer station — a station for changing lines
