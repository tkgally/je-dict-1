一箇所(いっかしょ)

いっかしょ
noun
one place; one spot; a single location
1. one place; one spot; one point
A single location, spot, or point. Used both for physical places and abstract points (such as a single error in a document or a single spot on the body).
間違(まちが)いが一箇所(いっかしょ)ある。
There is one mistake.
荷物(にもつ)は一箇所(いっかしょ)にまとめてください。
Please put all luggage together in one place.
書類(しょるい)に一箇所(いっかしょ)だけ修正(しゅうせい)が必要(ひつよう)だったので、すぐに直(なお)した。
There was just one spot in the document that needed correction, so I fixed it right away.

A counting expression for places, spots, or locations. Written as 一箇所(いっかしょ) or 一(いっ)カ所(しょ) or 一(いっ)ヶ所(しょ). The counter 箇所(かしょ) is used widely for discrete spots, points of interest, or specific locations.

COUNTING PATTERN:

  • 一箇所(いっかしょ): one place
  • 二箇所(にかしょ): two places
  • 三箇所(さんかしょ): three places
  • 数箇所(すうかしょ): several places
  • 何箇所(なんかしょ): how many places

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一箇所(いっかしょ)にまとめる: to gather in one place
  • 一箇所(いっかしょ)に集(あつ)まる: to assemble in one spot
  • 一箇所(いっかしょ)だけ: just one spot

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一(ひと)つの場所(ばしょ): one place — more colloquial
  • 一(いっ)か所(しょ)に集中(しゅうちゅう)する: to concentrate in one spot — emphasizes consolidation
