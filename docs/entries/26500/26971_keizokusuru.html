1. to continue; to persist; to carry on; to maintain
To keep doing something or to maintain a state without interruption. Often used in formal or written contexts for sustained activities, policies, or conditions.
努力(どりょく)を継続(けいぞく)する。
To continue one's efforts.
治療(ちりょう)は半年間(はんとしかん)継続(けいぞく)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
Treatment needs to continue for six months.
景気(けいき)の回復(かいふく)が継続(けいぞく)している。
The economic recovery is continuing.
継続(けいぞく)は力(ちから)なりという言葉(ことば)の通(とお)り、毎日(まいにち)少(すこ)しずつでも勉強(べんきょう)を続(つづ)けることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
As the saying 'persistence is power' goes, it's important to study a little bit every day.

Composed of 継(けい) (succeed, inherit) and 続(ぞく) (continue). More formal than 続(つづ)ける and often used in written language, business, and official contexts. The proverb 継続(けいぞく)は力(ちから)なり ('persistence is power') is one of the most commonly cited Japanese proverbs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 努力(どりょく)を継続(けいぞく)する: to continue one's efforts
  • 治療(ちりょう)を継続(けいぞく)する: to continue treatment
  • 契約(けいやく)を継続(けいぞく)する: to continue a contract
  • 継続(けいぞく)的(てき)な: continuous, ongoing
  • 継続(けいぞく)は力(ちから)なり: persistence is power (proverb)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 続(つづ)ける: to continue — everyday verb; more casual and versatile
  • 持続(じぞく)する: to sustain, maintain — emphasizes maintaining something at a consistent level
  • 存続(そんぞく)する: to survive, continue to exist — focuses on the continued existence of an organization or system
