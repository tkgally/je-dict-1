1. to continue; to persist; to sustain
For a state, condition, or activity to keep going over a period of time without interruption. Can be used both intransitively (something persists) and transitively (to sustain something).
痛(いた)みが三日(みっか)持続(じぞく)した。
The pain persisted for three days.
経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)を持続(じぞく)させることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to sustain economic growth.
集中力(しゅうちゅうりょく)を長時間(ちょうじかん)持続(じぞく)するのは難(むずか)しいので、適度(てきど)に休憩(きゅうけい)を取(と)ろう。
It's hard to sustain concentration for long hours, so let's take breaks at appropriate intervals.

Emphasizes maintaining a state or activity over time. Commonly used in discussions about sustainability, durability, and endurance. The noun form 持続(じぞく) is extremely common as a modifier.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持続(じぞく)可能(かのう)な: sustainable
  • 持続(じぞく)的(てき)な: continuous; sustained
  • 持続(じぞく)力(りょく): staying power; endurance
  • 持続(じぞく)時間(じかん): duration
  • 効果(こうか)が持続(じぞく)する: the effect lasts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 続(つづ)く: to continue — the basic, everyday word for continuation
  • 継続(けいぞく)する: to continue — emphasizes deliberate, ongoing effort to keep something going
  • 維持(いじ)する: to maintain — focuses on keeping something at its current level or state
