1. to feel refreshed; to feel clean
describes the sensation of feeling clean and refreshed, typically after bathing, cutting hair, or clearing up something
シャワーを浴(あ)びてさっぱりした。
I took a shower and felt refreshed.
髪(かみ)を切(き)ったらさっぱりした気分(きぶん)になった。
I felt refreshed after getting a haircut.
汗(あせ)をかいた後(あと)にお風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)ると、体(からだ)も心(こころ)もさっぱりする。
Taking a bath after sweating makes both body and mind feel refreshed.
2. to be completely gone; to be all cleared away
describes a state where something has been completely removed or resolved, leaving nothing behind
棚(たな)の上(うえ)がさっぱりした。
The shelf is all cleared off.
古(ふる)い荷物(にもつ)を捨(す)てたら部屋(へや)がさっぱりした。
The room was all cleared up after throwing away old baggage.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に不要(ふよう)なものを全部(ぜんぶ)処分(しょぶん)したら、家(いえ)の中(なか)がさっぱりした。
After getting rid of all unnecessary things before moving, the house was completely cleared out.

Describes a pleasant sense of cleanliness or lightness, either physical (after washing, haircuts) or abstract (after clearing clutter, resolving issues). The word conveys relief and a sense of a fresh start.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • シャワーを浴(あ)びてさっぱりする: feel refreshed after a shower
  • 髪(かみ)を切(き)ってさっぱりする: feel refreshed after a haircut
  • 気分(きぶん)がさっぱりする: feel mentally refreshed
  • 部屋(へや)がさっぱりする: room is nicely cleared up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すっきりする: also means refreshed, but emphasizes clarity and mental sharpness rather than physical cleanliness
  • さわやか: describes a fresh, pleasant feeling but is an adjective describing atmosphere or personality rather than a change of state

RELATED FORMS:

  • さっぱりした (adjective): clean-tasting, refreshing, frank (personality)
  • さっぱり (adverb + negative): not at all (e.g., さっぱりわからない = I don't understand at all)
