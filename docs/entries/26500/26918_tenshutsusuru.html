1. to transfer out; to move out (of a jurisdiction)
To officially deregister from a municipality when relocating to another area. Also used for leaving a school to transfer elsewhere.
来月(らいげつ)、この市(し)から転出(てんしゅつ)する。
I'm moving out of this city next month.
転出(てんしゅつ)届(とどけ)は引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に出(だ)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
You need to submit the transfer-out notification before moving.
若者(わかもの)の転出(てんしゅつ)が続(つづ)き、この町(まち)の人口(じんこう)は年々(ねんねん)減(へ)っている。
Young people keep moving away, and the population of this town is decreasing year by year.

The administrative counterpart to 転入(てんにゅう)する. When leaving a municipality, residents must file a 転出(てんしゅつ)届(とどけ) (transfer-out notification) and receive a 転出(てんしゅつ)証明書(しょうめいしょ) (transfer-out certificate), which is then presented at the new municipality.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転出(てんしゅつ)届(とどけ): transfer-out notification
  • 転出(てんしゅつ)証明書(しょうめいしょ): transfer-out certificate
  • 転出(てんしゅつ)者(しゃ): person moving out; departing resident
  • 転出(てんしゅつ)超過(ちょうか): net outmigration
  • 人口(じんこう)転出(てんしゅつ): population outflow

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 転入(てんにゅう)する: to transfer in — the opposite; officially registering in a new municipality
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す: to move (house) — the everyday word for physically relocating
  • 退去(たいきょ)する: to vacate; to leave — focuses on physically leaving a building or premises
