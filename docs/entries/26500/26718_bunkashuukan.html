文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)

ぶんかしゅうかん
noun
culture week
1. culture week; Cultural Week
A designated week celebrating culture and the arts. In Japan, the national Cultural Week runs from November 1 to 7, centered around Culture Day (文化(ぶんか)の日(ひ)) on November 3. Schools and communities also hold their own culture weeks with exhibitions, performances, and events.
文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)に美術館(びじゅつかん)が無料(むりょう)開放(かいほう)される。
Museums are opened free of charge during Culture Week.
学校(がっこう)の文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)では、各(かく)クラスが展示(てんじ)や発表(はっぴょう)を行(おこな)う。
During the school's culture week, each class puts on exhibitions and presentations.
文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)に合(あ)わせて、地域(ちいき)の伝統(でんとう)芸能(げいのう)を紹介(しょうかい)するイベントが開催(かいさい)された。
An event introducing local traditional performing arts was held to coincide with Culture Week.

Composed of 文化(ぶんか) (culture) and 週間(しゅうかん) (week/awareness week). Japan's national 文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん) falls on November 1-7, with November 3rd being 文化(ぶんか)の日(ひ) (Culture Day), a national holiday. Many museums and cultural institutions offer free admission or special events during this period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)の行事(ぎょうじ): Culture Week events
  • 文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)に合(あ)わせて: to coincide with Culture Week
  • 文化週間(ぶんかしゅうかん)の一環(いっかん)として: as part of Culture Week

RELATED TERMS:

  • 文化(ぶんか)の日(ひ): Culture Day — November 3rd national holiday
  • 文化祭(ぶんかさい): school culture festival — a related but distinct event
  • 読書週間(どくしょしゅうかん): Reading Week — another awareness week (Oct 27-Nov 9)
