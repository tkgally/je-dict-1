1. to run into; to escape into; to take refuge in
to flee into an enclosed space or location for shelter or safety
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきたので店(みせ)に逃(に)げ込(こ)んだ。
It started raining so I ducked into a shop.
猫(ねこ)が驚(おどろ)いて茂(しげ)みに逃(に)げ込(こ)んだ。
The cat got startled and ran into the bushes.
泥棒(どろぼう)は警察(けいさつ)に追(お)われて路地(ろじ)に逃(に)げ込(こ)んだが、行(い)き止(ど)まりだった。
The thief was chased by police and ran into an alley, but it was a dead end.

A compound verb combining 逃(に)げる (to escape) with 込(こ)む (into). The 込(こ)む component adds the nuance of entering or going deep into a space. Can be used both for literal physical escape and for casual situations like ducking out of rain.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 建物(たてもの)に逃(に)げ込(こ)む: to run into a building
  • 部屋(へや)に逃(に)げ込(こ)む: to escape into a room
  • 茂(しげ)みに逃(に)げ込(こ)む: to dart into the bushes
  • 安全(あんぜん)な場所(ばしょ)に逃(に)げ込(こ)む: to take refuge in a safe place

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逃(に)げる: to escape, to flee — general fleeing without specifying a destination
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む: to rush into — emphasizes speed of entry rather than escape
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)む: to jump into — physical leaping into a space
  • 逃(に)げ出(だ)す: to run away from — emphasizes leaving rather than entering a refuge

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The destination is marked with に.
