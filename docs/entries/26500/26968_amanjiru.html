1. to accept; to resign oneself to; to submit to
To accept an unfavorable situation or outcome without resistance. Implies a passive acceptance of something undesirable, often because one feels powerless to change it or considers it deserved.
罰(ばつ)に甘(あま)んじる。
To accept one's punishment.
敗北(はいぼく)に甘(あま)んじるしかなかった。
I had no choice but to accept defeat.
批判(ひはん)を受(う)けても、それに甘(あま)んじて自分(じぶん)の信念(しんねん)を貫(つらぬ)いた。
Even when criticized, he accepted it and stuck to his convictions.
2. to content oneself with; to settle for
To be satisfied with less than what might be desired or expected. Often used with a critical nuance, suggesting the person should aspire to more.
現状(げんじょう)に甘(あま)んじてはいけない。
You must not settle for the status quo.
二位(にい)に甘(あま)んじるつもりはない。
I have no intention of settling for second place.
低(ひく)い給料(きゅうりょう)に甘(あま)んじていると、いつまでも生活(せいかつ)は楽(らく)にならない。
If you settle for a low salary, your life will never get easier.

Derived from 甘(あま)い (sweet, lenient). The verb conveys the idea of passively accepting something, as though yielding to sweetness or leniency. Most commonly appears in the pattern ～に甘(あま)んじる. The older form 甘(あま)んずる also exists but is rare in modern usage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罰(ばつ)に甘(あま)んじる: to accept punishment
  • 現状(げんじょう)に甘(あま)んじる: to settle for the status quo
  • 敗北(はいぼく)に甘(あま)んじる: to accept defeat
  • 批判(ひはん)に甘(あま)んじる: to accept criticism
  • ～に甘(あま)んじてはいけない: must not settle for ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 受(う)け入(い)れる: to accept — neutral; simply means to accept without the nuance of resignation
  • 我慢(がまん)する: to endure, put up with — emphasizes tolerating something unpleasant through willpower
  • 諦(あきら)める: to give up — focuses on abandoning hope, while 甘(あま)んじる focuses on accepting a lesser outcome
