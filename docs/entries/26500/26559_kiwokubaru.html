気(き)を配(くば)る

きをくばる
expression
to pay attention; to be attentive; to be considerate
1. to pay attention to; to be attentive to; to take care of
To direct one's awareness and consideration toward something or someone. Implies thoughtful, ongoing attention rather than a momentary glance.
安全(あんぜん)に気(き)を配(くば)る。
To pay attention to safety.
彼女(かのじょ)は周(まわ)りの人(ひと)に気(き)を配(くば)れる人(ひと)だ。
She is someone who is considerate of those around her.
健康(けんこう)に気(き)を配(くば)って、毎日(まいにち)野菜(やさい)を食(た)べるようにしている。
I'm mindful of my health, so I try to eat vegetables every day.

A common expression meaning to direct one's attention and care toward something. Composed of 気(き) (spirit, attention) and 配(くば)る (to distribute), so the literal sense is "to distribute one's attention." Implies a sustained, proactive awareness rather than a momentary focus.

PARTICLE PATTERN:

  • [noun]に気(き)を配(くば)る: to pay attention to [noun]

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安全(あんぜん)に気(き)を配(くば)る: to pay attention to safety
  • 健康(けんこう)に気(き)を配(くば)る: to be mindful of one's health
  • 周囲(しゅうい)に気(き)を配(くば)る: to be attentive to one's surroundings
  • 細部(さいぶ)に気(き)を配(くば)る: to pay attention to detail

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)をつける: to be careful — focuses on avoiding danger or mistakes, more about caution
  • 注意(ちゅうい)を払(はら)う: to pay attention — more formal, similar meaning
  • 目(め)を配(くば)る: to keep an eye on — emphasizes visual monitoring
  • 心遣(こころづか)い: consideration, thoughtfulness — noun form of a similar concept
