1. to oppose; to resist; to rebel against
To push back against an idea, authority, or pressure, often with strong feeling.
子供(こども)たちは親(おや)の意見(いけん)に反発(はんぱつ)した。
The children opposed their parents' opinions.
新(あたら)しい規則(きそく)に対(たい)して社員(しゃいん)から強(つよ)い反発(はんぱつ)があった。
There was strong opposition from employees against the new rules.
市民(しみん)は政府(せいふ)の方針(ほうしん)に激(はげ)しく反発(はんぱつ)している。
Citizens are fiercely opposing the government's policy.
2. to repel; to bounce back; to rebound
For a physical object or force to push away from another, or for a market/price to recover after a decline.
ボールが壁(かべ)に反発(はんぱつ)した。
The ball bounced off the wall.
同(おな)じ極(きょく)の磁石(じしゃく)は互(たが)いに反発(はんぱつ)する。
Magnets with the same pole repel each other.
株価(かぶか)が大幅(おおはば)に下(さ)がった後(あと)、反発(はんぱつ)して回復(かいふく)した。
After stock prices fell sharply, they rebounded and recovered.

The two senses share the core idea of pushing back — either emotionally/socially (sense 1) or physically (sense 2). The noun form 反発(はんぱつ) is very common, often used with を受(う)ける (to receive pushback) or を招(まね)く (to invite opposition).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 反発(はんぱつ)を買(か)う: to invite opposition; to provoke backlash
  • 反発力(はんぱつりょく): repulsive force; resilience
  • 反発(はんぱつ)が強(つよ)い: strong opposition/resistance
  • 株価(かぶか)の反発(はんぱつ): stock price rebound

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対(はんたい)する: to oppose — more neutral and deliberate; 反発(はんぱつ) implies stronger emotional pushback
  • 抵抗(ていこう)する: to resist — focuses on sustained resistance rather than an immediate pushback
  • 反抗(はんこう)する: to defy; to rebel — stronger, implies defiance against authority
