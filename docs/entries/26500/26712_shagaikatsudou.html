社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)

しゃがいかつどう
noun
outside-company activities; extracurricular work activities
1. outside-company activities; external activities
Activities conducted outside of one's primary company or workplace, such as volunteer work, professional associations, side projects, networking events, or community involvement.
社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)も評価(ひょうか)の対象(たいしょう)になる。
Outside-company activities are also subject to evaluation.
社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)を通(つう)じて、異(こと)なる業界(ぎょうかい)の人(ひと)と知(し)り合(あ)えた。
Through outside-company activities, I was able to meet people from different industries.
最近(さいきん)は社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)を奨励(しょうれい)する企業(きぎょう)が増(ふ)えてきている。
Recently, more companies are encouraging their employees to engage in activities outside of work.

Composed of 社外(しゃがい) (outside the company) and 活動(かつどう) (activities). Reflects the evolving Japanese work culture where employees are increasingly encouraged to develop skills and connections beyond their employer.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)に参加(さんか)する: to participate in outside-company activities
  • 社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)を奨励(しょうれい)する: to encourage outside activities
  • 社外活動(しゃがいかつどう)の報告(ほうこく): report on external activities

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 課外活動(かがいかつどう): extracurricular activities — used in school contexts
  • 社内活動(しゃないかつどう): in-company activities — the opposite
  • 副業(ふくぎょう): side job — a specific type of outside activity
