1. to dissolve; to mix (a powder or solid) into a liquid
To stir or blend a substance into liquid until it dissolves or forms a smooth mixture. Commonly used in cooking for mixing ingredients like flour, eggs, or seasonings into liquid.
卵(たまご)を溶(と)く。
Beat the eggs.
味噌(みそ)を湯(ゆ)に溶(と)いてください。
Please dissolve the miso in the hot water.
小麦粉(こむぎこ)を水(みず)で溶(と)いてから、少(すこ)しずつ鍋(なべ)に加(くわ)える。
Dissolve the flour in water, then gradually add it to the pot.

A godan verb used primarily in cooking to describe mixing a powder, paste, or solid into a liquid. The transitive counterpart of 溶(と)ける (to dissolve on its own).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 溶(と)ける (intransitive — to dissolve, to melt)
  • Pattern: Xを溶(と)く, Xを水(みず)で溶(と)く

ASPECT:

  • Type: Telic (has endpoint)
  • 溶(と)いている: in the process of dissolving/mixing

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 卵(たまご)を溶(と)く: to beat eggs
  • 味噌(みそ)を溶(と)く: to dissolve miso
  • 小麦粉(こむぎこ)を溶(と)く: to dissolve flour
  • 絵(え)の具(ぐ)を溶(と)く: to dilute paint

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:
Not to be confused with 解(と)く (to solve, to untie) or 説(と)く (to explain, to preach), which have the same reading but different meanings and kanji.
