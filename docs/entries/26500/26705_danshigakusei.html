男子学生(だんしがくせい)

だんしがくせい
noun
male student
1. male student
A male student, typically at the university or high school level. Used in formal or institutional contexts such as statistics, regulations, and news reports.
男子学生(だんしがくせい)の割合(わりあい)は約(やく)60%だ。
Male students make up about 60%.
その大学(だいがく)では男子学生(だんしがくせい)と女子学生(じょしがくせい)の数(かず)がほぼ同(おな)じだ。
At that university, the numbers of male and female students are almost equal.
アンケートに答(こた)えた男子学生(だんしがくせい)の半数(はんすう)が、将来(しょうらい)は海外(かいがい)で働(はたら)きたいと答(こた)えた。
Half of the male students who responded to the survey said they want to work abroad in the future.

Composed of 男子(だんし) (male/boy) and 学生(がくせい) (student). While 学生(がくせい) alone is gender-neutral, 男子学生(だんしがくせい) explicitly specifies male gender. Common in formal and statistical contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 男子学生(だんしがくせい)寮(りょう): men's dormitory
  • 男子学生(だんしがくせい)の比率(ひりつ): ratio of male students
  • 男子学生(だんしがくせい)向(む)け: aimed at male students

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 女子学生(じょしがくせい): female student — the counterpart
  • 男子生徒(だんしせいと): male pupil — used for middle and high school students
  • 大学生(だいがくせい): college student — gender-neutral
