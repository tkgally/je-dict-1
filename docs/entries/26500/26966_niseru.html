1. to imitate; to make to resemble; to copy the appearance of
To deliberately make something look like or resemble something else. Often used for creating reproductions, copying styles, or disguising something as another thing.
本物(ほんもの)に似(に)せて作(つく)る。
To make it to look like the real thing.
有名(ゆうめい)な画家(がか)の作風(さくふう)に似(に)せて描(えが)いた。
I painted it in imitation of a famous artist's style.
声(こえ)を似(に)せるのが上手(じょうず)で、誰(だれ)の真似(まね)でもできる。
He's good at imitating voices and can mimic anyone.
この製品(せいひん)はブランド品(ひん)に似(に)せて作(つく)られた偽物(にせもの)だ。
This product is a counterfeit made to resemble the brand-name item.

Causative-like form derived from 似(に)る (to resemble). While 似(に)る describes a state of resemblance, 似(に)せる describes the deliberate act of making something resemble something else. The word 偽(にせ) (fake, counterfeit) is etymologically related.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本物(ほんもの)に似(に)せる: to make to resemble the genuine article
  • 声(こえ)を似(に)せる: to imitate a voice
  • 形(かたち)を似(に)せる: to copy the shape
  • 似(に)せて作(つく)る: to make in imitation of

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 真似(まね)する: to imitate, mimic — broader; includes imitating behavior, gestures, and speech
  • 模倣(もほう)する: to imitate, emulate — formal; used for copying styles, methods, or works
  • 似(に)る: to resemble — describes a natural or existing resemblance, not a deliberate act
