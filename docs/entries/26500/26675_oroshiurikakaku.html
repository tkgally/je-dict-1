卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)

おろしうりかかく
noun
wholesale price
1. wholesale price
The price at which goods are sold in bulk by a wholesaler to a retailer, before being marked up for sale to the end consumer. Lower than the retail price.
卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)で仕入(しい)れた。
I purchased at wholesale price.
卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)と小売価格(こうりかかく)の差(さ)が利益(りえき)になる。
The difference between the wholesale price and retail price becomes the profit.
原材料費(げんざいりょうひ)の高騰(こうとう)により、卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)が上昇(じょうしょう)し、小売店(こうりてん)にも影響(えいきょう)が出(で)ている。
Due to soaring raw material costs, wholesale prices have risen, and the impact is being felt by retail stores as well.

Composed of 卸売(おろしうり) (wholesale) and 価格(かかく) (price). Used in business, economics, and news reporting about market trends and pricing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)で買(か)う: to buy at wholesale price
  • 卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)が上(あ)がる: wholesale prices rise
  • 卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)が下(さ)がる: wholesale prices fall
  • 卸売価格(おろしうりかかく)指数(しすう): wholesale price index

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 小売価格(こうりかかく): retail price — the price paid by the end consumer
  • 仕入(しい)れ値(ね): purchase price; cost price — the price a shop pays for stock, similar but more colloquial
  • 希望小売価格(きぼうこうりかかく): manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP)
  • 定価(ていか): list price; fixed price
