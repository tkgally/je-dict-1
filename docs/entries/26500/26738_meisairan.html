明細欄(めいさいらん)

めいさいらん
noun
details column; itemized section
1. details column; itemized section
A section on a form, invoice, receipt, or statement where individual items are listed with their specific details such as descriptions, quantities, and amounts.
明細欄(めいさいらん)に記入(きにゅう)する。
Fill in the details section.
請求書(せいきゅうしょ)の明細欄(めいさいらん)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the itemized section of the invoice.
明細欄(めいさいらん)には商品名(しょうひんめい)、数量(すうりょう)、単価(たんか)、金額(きんがく)を記載(きさい)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
The details column must include the product name, quantity, unit price, and amount.

A compound of 明細(めいさい) (details, itemization) and 欄(らん) (column, section). Appears on invoices, pay slips, bank statements, and various official forms. Understanding this term is useful for navigating Japanese financial and administrative documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 明細欄(めいさいらん)に記入(きにゅう)する: to fill in the details section
  • 明細欄(めいさいらん)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the details section
  • 給与(きゅうよ)明細(めいさい)の明細欄(めいさいらん): the itemized section of a pay slip

RELATED TERMS:

  • 明細(めいさい): details, specifics — the broader term for itemized information
  • 明細書(めいさいしょ): detailed statement — a document listing itemized information
  • 欄(らん): column, section — used in many compounds like 備考欄(びこうらん) (remarks column)
