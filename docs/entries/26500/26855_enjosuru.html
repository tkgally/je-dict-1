1. to assist; to aid; to support; to provide aid
To provide help, especially material or financial support, to someone or a group in need. Often used in the context of international development, disaster relief, and institutional support.
発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)を援助(えんじょ)する。
To provide aid to developing countries.
政府(せいふ)は被災者(ひさいしゃ)を経済的(けいざいてき)に援助(えんじょ)している。
The government is financially supporting the disaster victims.
食料(しょくりょう)や医薬品(いやくひん)を送(おく)って援助(えんじょ)する。
To provide aid by sending food and medicine.
国際的(こくさいてき)な援助(えんじょ)なしでは、この地域(ちいき)の復興(ふっこう)は難(むずか)しい。
Without international aid, the recovery of this region will be difficult.

Typically implies organized, institutional support rather than casual personal help. Very common in discussions of international relations, foreign aid, and disaster response. Japan is a major provider of ODA (政府(せいふ)開発(かいはつ)援助(えんじょ), Official Development Assistance).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経済(けいざい)援助(えんじょ): economic aid
  • 人道(じんどう)援助(えんじょ): humanitarian aid
  • 食料(しょくりょう)援助(えんじょ): food aid
  • 軍事(ぐんじ)援助(えんじょ): military aid
  • 開発(かいはつ)援助(えんじょ): development aid
  • 援助(えんじょ)金(きん): financial assistance, grants

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手伝(てつだ)う: to help — everyday personal help
  • 支援(しえん)する: to support — broader support including non-material help
  • 助(たす)ける: to save, to help — immediate help, less formal
