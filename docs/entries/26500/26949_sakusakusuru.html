1. to be crispy; to be crunchy
Describes a light, pleasant crunchiness when biting into food. The texture breaks apart cleanly and easily, like a well-fried tempura, fresh apple, or flaky pastry.
この天(てん)ぷらはサクサクしている。
This tempura is crispy.
揚(あ)げたてのコロッケがサクサクしておいしい。
The freshly fried croquettes are deliciously crispy.
外(そと)はサクサク、中(なか)はふわふわのパンが焼(や)けた。
I baked bread that's crispy on the outside and fluffy on the inside.
2. to go smoothly; to proceed briskly
Describes something proceeding smoothly and efficiently without delay. Often used for work, tasks, or digital operations like fast-loading websites.
仕事(しごと)がサクサク進(すす)む。
Work is progressing smoothly.
このアプリはサクサク動(うご)くから使(つか)いやすい。
This app runs smoothly, so it's easy to use.
新(あたら)しいパソコンに変(か)えたら作業(さぎょう)がサクサクできるようになった。
After switching to a new computer, my work started going much more smoothly.

An onomatopoeic word mimicking the sound of biting into something crispy. The reduplication (サクサク) intensifies the crisp quality. Very commonly used in food descriptions, cooking shows, and restaurant reviews.

The smooth/brisk sense (sense 2) is a newer extension, especially popular in tech contexts to describe responsive software or fast internet connections.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • サクサクの食感(しょっかん): crispy texture
  • サクサクに揚(あ)げる: to fry until crispy
  • サクサク進(すす)む: to progress briskly
  • サクサク動(うご)く: to run smoothly (software)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カリカリ: crispy — harder, more intensely crunchy texture (like crackers or burnt edges)
  • パリパリ: crispy — thin and brittle crispness (like chips or dried seaweed)
  • ザクザク: crunchy — coarser, chunkier crunch (like gravel or rough-chopped vegetables)
