残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)

ざんだかしょうめい
noun
balance certificate; proof of balance
1. balance certificate; proof of account balance
An official document issued by a bank that certifies the balance in an account at a specific point in time. Required for visa applications, loan applications, tax filings, and other official procedures.
ビザの申請(しんせい)に残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)が必要(ひつよう)です。
A balance certificate is required for the visa application.
銀行(ぎんこう)で残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)書(しょ)を発行(はっこう)してもらった。
I had the bank issue a balance certificate.
留学(りゅうがく)ビザの申請(しんせい)には、一定額(いっていがく)以上(いじょう)の残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)を提出(ていしゅつ)しなければならない。
For a student visa application, you must submit a balance certificate showing at least a certain amount.

Composed of 残高(ざんだか) (balance/remaining amount) and 証明(しょうめい) (certification/proof). The full formal term is 残高証明書(ざんだかしょうめいしょ), with 書(しょ) (document) appended. Particularly relevant for foreigners in Japan who need financial documentation for visa renewals and other immigration procedures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)書(しょ): balance certificate (full formal form)
  • 残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)を発行(はっこう)する: to issue a balance certificate
  • 残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a balance certificate
  • 残高証明(ざんだかしょうめい)を取得(しゅとく)する: to obtain a balance certificate

RELATED TERMS:

  • 残高(ざんだか): account balance — the amount of money in an account
  • 通帳(つうちょう): bankbook/passbook — another way to show balances
  • 収入証明(しゅうにゅうしょうめい): income certificate — a related financial document
