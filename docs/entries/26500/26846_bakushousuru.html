1. to burst out laughing; to roar with laughter
To laugh loudly and uncontrollably. Originally meant a large group laughing together, but in modern usage commonly refers to any instance of explosive, unrestrained laughter.
観客(かんきゃく)が爆笑(ばくしょう)した。
The audience burst out laughing.
あの動画(どうが)を見(み)て爆笑(ばくしょう)してしまった。
I burst out laughing watching that video.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)がおもしろすぎて、電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で爆笑(ばくしょう)してしまった。
His story was so funny that I burst out laughing on the train.
あの芸人(げいにん)は会場(かいじょう)を爆笑(ばくしょう)の渦(うず)に巻(ま)き込(こ)んだ。
That comedian had the entire venue in stitches.

Originally, 爆笑(ばくしょう) strictly meant a large group of people laughing simultaneously (the 爆(ばく) = "explosion" suggesting laughter spreading through a crowd). However, modern usage has shifted, and it now commonly describes any intense, explosive laughter — even by one person. This semantic shift is noted by language purists but widely accepted.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 爆笑(ばくしょう)を誘(さそ)う: to elicit roaring laughter
  • 爆笑(ばくしょう)の渦(うず): whirlpool of laughter
  • 爆笑(ばくしょう)もの: something hilarious
  • 爆笑(ばくしょう)エピソード: hilarious anecdote
  • 大(おお)爆笑(ばくしょう): huge burst of laughter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 笑(わら)う: to laugh — general-purpose laughing
  • 大笑(おおわら)いする: to laugh heartily — loud laughter, slightly less explosive
  • 吹(ふ)き出(だ)す: to burst out laughing — sudden laughter, often from trying not to laugh
