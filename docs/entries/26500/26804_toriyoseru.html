1. to order; to send away for; to have something delivered
to request that something be sent from a distant location, often specialty food or goods not available locally
北海道(ほっかいどう)からカニを取(と)り寄(よ)せた。
I ordered crab from Hokkaido.
ネットでお取(と)り寄(よ)せスイーツを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered specialty sweets for delivery online.
地方(ちほう)の名産品(めいさんひん)を取(と)り寄(よ)せて、家族(かぞく)で楽(たの)しむのが趣味(しゅみ)です。
My hobby is ordering regional specialties and enjoying them with my family.

Specifically implies ordering from a distant or special source rather than buying at a local store. Very commonly used for regional specialty foods (お取(と)り寄(よ)せグルメ). The honorific prefix お is frequently attached in commercial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お取(と)り寄(よ)せグルメ: mail-order gourmet food
  • 産地(さんち)から取(と)り寄(よ)せる: order directly from the production area
  • ネットで取(と)り寄(よ)せる: order online for delivery
  • 取(と)り寄(よ)せ品(ひん): items ordered from elsewhere

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注文(ちゅうもん)する: to order — general ordering, including at restaurants; 取(と)り寄(よ)せる specifically implies ordering from a distant source
  • 通販(つうはん)で買(か)う: to buy via mail order — more neutral; 取(と)り寄(よ)せる carries a nuance of specialty or luxury

CULTURAL NOTE:
お取(と)り寄(よ)せ culture is very popular in Japan, where people order famous local specialties (名物(めいぶつ)) from other regions. Department store catalogs and online services specialize in this.
