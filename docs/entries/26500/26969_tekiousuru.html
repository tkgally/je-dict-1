1. to adapt; to adjust; to acclimate
To change oneself or one's behavior in response to new conditions or circumstances. Used for both biological adaptation and personal adjustment to new environments.
新(あたら)しい環境(かんきょう)に適応(てきおう)する。
To adapt to a new environment.
留学先(りゅうがくさき)の生活(せいかつ)にようやく適応(てきおう)できた。
I finally managed to adjust to life at my study abroad destination.
動物(どうぶつ)は厳(きび)しい気候(きこう)にも適応(てきおう)して生(い)き延(の)びてきた。
Animals have survived by adapting even to harsh climates.
転職(てんしょく)したばかりで、まだ新(あたら)しい職場(しょくば)の文化(ぶんか)に適応(てきおう)しきれていない。
I just changed jobs and haven't fully adjusted to the culture of my new workplace yet.

Composed of 適(てき) (suitable, fitting) and 応(おう) (respond, apply). Describes the process of becoming suited to new conditions. Can be used for both biological/evolutionary adaptation and everyday personal adjustment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)に適応(てきおう)する: to adapt to an environment
  • 社会(しゃかい)に適応(てきおう)する: to adjust to society
  • 変化(へんか)に適応(てきおう)する: to adapt to changes
  • 適応(てきおう)能力(のうりょく): adaptability
  • 適応(てきおう)障害(しょうがい): adjustment disorder

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 慣(な)れる: to get used to — focuses on becoming accustomed through familiarity; more casual
  • 順応(じゅんのう)する: to acclimate — similar meaning but emphasizes conforming to surroundings; slightly more formal
  • 対応(たいおう)する: to respond, deal with — focuses on taking action in response to a situation, not changing oneself
