通信圏(つうしんけん)

つうしんけん
noun
coverage area; communication range
1. coverage area; communication range; signal range
The geographic area within which a wireless device can send and receive signals. Used for mobile phones, radio, Wi-Fi, and other wireless communication.
通信圏(つうしんけん)外(がい)で電話(でんわ)が使(つか)えない。
The phone doesn't work because it's out of coverage.
山(やま)の奥(おく)では通信圏(つうしんけん)に入(はい)れないことが多(おお)い。
Deep in the mountains, you often can't get into a coverage area.
トンネルを抜(ぬ)けてやっと通信圏(つうしんけん)に戻(もど)り、メッセージが一斉(いっせい)に届(とど)いた。
After leaving the tunnel, I finally got back into coverage and all my messages came through at once.

A compound of 通信(つうしん) (communication, telecommunications) and 圏(けん) (sphere, zone). Commonly encountered when traveling to remote areas or underground locations where mobile phone signals are weak or absent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通信圏(つうしんけん)外(がい): out of range, out of coverage
  • 通信圏(つうしんけん)内(ない): within range, in coverage
  • 通信圏(つうしんけん)に入(はい)る: to enter a coverage area
  • 通信圏(つうしんけん)から外(はず)れる: to go out of coverage

RELATED TERMS:

  • 圏外(けんがい): out of range — the shorter, more casual term displayed on Japanese phones when there is no signal
  • 圏内(けんない): within range — the opposite of 圏外(けんがい)
  • 電波(でんぱ): radio waves, signal — the physical signal itself
