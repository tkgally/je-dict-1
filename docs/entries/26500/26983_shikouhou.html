思考法(しこうほう)

しこうほう
noun
way of thinking; thinking method; thought process
1. way of thinking; thinking method; thought process
A systematic approach or framework for thinking about problems, making decisions, or analyzing situations. Often used in educational, business, and self-improvement contexts.
論理的(ろんりてき)な思考法(しこうほう)を身(み)につける。
To acquire a logical way of thinking.
この本(ほん)には問題(もんだい)解決(かいけつ)の思考法(しこうほう)が紹介(しょうかい)されている。
This book introduces methods of thinking for problem-solving.
柔軟(じゅうなん)な思考法(しこうほう)を持(も)つことが、ビジネスでは大切(たいせつ)だ。
Having a flexible way of thinking is important in business.
成功(せいこう)した経営者(けいえいしゃ)たちの思考法(しこうほう)には共通点(きょうつうてん)がある。
The thinking methods of successful business leaders have common points.

Composed of 思考(しこう) (thought, thinking) and 法(ほう) (method, way). Part of the productive ～法(ほう) pattern for methods and approaches. Commonly found in book titles and business training materials.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 論理的(ろんりてき)思考法(しこうほう): logical thinking method
  • 批判的(ひはんてき)思考法(しこうほう): critical thinking method
  • 思考法(しこうほう)を学(まな)ぶ: to learn a way of thinking
  • 思考法(しこうほう)を変(か)える: to change one's way of thinking

RELATED PATTERNS:

～法(ほう) as a suffix for methods:

  • 勉強法(べんきょうほう): study method
  • 記憶法(きおくほう): memorization method
  • 解決法(かいけつほう): solution method

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 考(かんが)え方(かた): way of thinking — more everyday and personal; less formal
  • 発想法(はっそうほう): ideation method — focuses on generating ideas rather than general thinking
  • 思考(しこう): thought, thinking — the activity of thinking itself, without the 'method' nuance
