四合瓶(よんごうびん)

よんごうびん
noun
720ml bottle; four-go bottle
1. 720ml bottle; four-go bottle
A standard-sized bottle holding 720 milliliters (four go), most commonly used for sake, shochu, and other Japanese alcoholic beverages. One of the two standard bottle sizes in the Japanese alcohol industry.
日本酒(にほんしゅ)の四合瓶(よんごうびん)を買(か)った。
I bought a 720ml bottle of sake.
お土産(みやげ)には四合瓶(よんごうびん)がちょうどいい大(おお)きさだ。
A 720ml bottle is just the right size for a souvenir.
この蔵元(くらもと)の限定品(げんていひん)は四合瓶(よんごうびん)でしか販売(はんばい)されていない。
This brewery's limited edition is only sold in 720ml bottles.

Composed of 四合(よんごう) (four go, a traditional unit of volume) and 瓶(びん) (bottle). One 合(ごう) is approximately 180ml, so four go equals 720ml. This is one of the two standard sake bottle sizes in Japan, the other being the 一升瓶(いっしょうびん) (1.8L bottle, or ten go). The 四合瓶(よんごうびん) is the more portable size and is popular for gifts and home consumption.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 四合瓶(よんごうびん)一本(いっぽん): one 720ml bottle
  • 四合瓶(よんごうびん)サイズ: 720ml bottle size
  • 四合瓶(よんごうびん)で注文(ちゅうもん)する: to order by the 720ml bottle

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一升瓶(いっしょうびん): 1.8-liter bottle — the larger standard size, roughly equivalent to a wine magnum
  • 合(ごう): a traditional Japanese unit of volume (approximately 180ml), also used for measuring rice
