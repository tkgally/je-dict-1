1. to become clear; to be definite; to be distinct
Describes something becoming clear, unambiguous, or well-defined. Used for visual clarity, certainty of facts, or definiteness of statements.
原因(げんいん)がはっきりした。
The cause became clear.
遠(とお)くの景色(けしき)がはっきり見(み)える。
The distant scenery is clearly visible.
検査(けんさ)の結果(けっか)がはっきりするまで、治療(ちりょう)方針(ほうしん)は決(き)められない。
We can't decide on a treatment plan until the test results become clear.
2. to speak plainly; to state clearly
Describes someone expressing their opinion, answer, or stance in a straightforward and unambiguous way.
はっきり言(い)ってください。
Please say it clearly.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)をはっきり述(の)べた。
He stated his opinion plainly.
好(す)きか嫌(きら)いか、はっきりさせたほうがいいと思(おも)う。
I think you should make it clear whether you like it or not.

An extremely common adverb/verb combination describing clarity, definiteness, or distinctness. はっきり is onomatopoeia-derived and used both as an adverb modifying other verbs and with する to form a verb meaning "to become clear."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • はっきり言(い)う: to say plainly, to speak frankly
  • はっきり見(み)える: to be clearly visible
  • はっきりしない: unclear, vague, indecisive
  • はっきりした態度(たいど): a clear-cut attitude
  • はっきりした答(こた)え: a definite answer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明確(めいかく): clear, explicit — more formal and used in writing; はっきり is more conversational
  • 明(あき)らか: obvious, evident — emphasizes that something is self-evidently true
  • 鮮明(せんめい): vivid, sharp — used specifically for visual or memory clarity

USAGE:
はっきりしない is commonly used to describe someone who is indecisive or wishy-washy. The phrase はっきり言(い)って ("frankly speaking") is a very frequent discourse marker used to introduce blunt opinions.
