新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)

しんぴんみしよう
noun
brand new, unused; new and unopened
1. brand new, unused; new and unopened
A label indicating that an item has never been used or opened since purchase. Extremely common in online marketplace listings and secondhand shops in Japan.
新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)の商品(しょうひん)です。
This item is brand new and unused.
新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)のバッグを半額(はんがく)で手(て)に入(い)れた。
I got a brand new, unused bag for half price.
フリマアプリでは新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)の状態(じょうたい)が一番(いちばん)高(たか)く売(う)れる。
On flea market apps, items in brand new, unused condition sell for the highest price.

A four-character compound of 新品(しんぴん) (new item) and 未使用(みしよう) (unused). This is one of the standard condition labels on Japanese online marketplaces like メルカリ (Mercari), ラクマ (Rakuma), and ヤフオク (Yahoo Auctions). It represents the highest condition grade, indicating the item was purchased but never used or taken out of its packaging.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)品(ひん): brand new, unused item
  • 新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)タグ付(つ)き: brand new with tags attached
  • 新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう)の状態(じょうたい): brand new, unused condition

CONDITION GRADES:

Japanese marketplace listings typically use these condition labels:

  • 新品未使用(しんぴんみしよう): brand new, unused
  • 未使用(みしよう)に近(ちか)い: near unused
  • 目立(めだ)った傷(きず)や汚(よご)れなし: no noticeable damage or stains
  • やや傷(きず)や汚(よご)れあり: some damage or stains
  • 傷(きず)や汚(よご)れあり: has damage or stains

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新品(しんぴん): new item — does not specify whether it has been used or opened
  • 未開封(みかいふう): unopened — focuses specifically on the packaging being sealed
