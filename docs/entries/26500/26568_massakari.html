真(ま)っ盛(さか)り

まっさかり
noun
at its peak; in full bloom; height of
1. at its peak; in full bloom; at the height of
The point at which something is at its most intense, active, or flourishing. Used for seasons, activities, careers, and other things that have a peak period.
桜(さくら)が真(ま)っ盛(さか)りだ。
The cherry blossoms are in full bloom.
夏(なつ)真(ま)っ盛(さか)りで毎日(まいにち)暑(あつ)い。
It's the height of summer and it's hot every day.
彼(かれ)は今(いま)、キャリアの真(ま)っ盛(さか)りで海外(かいがい)でも活躍(かつやく)している。
He is now at the peak of his career and is also active overseas.

An intensified form of 盛(さか)り (peak, prime), with the prefix 真(ま)っ adding emphasis meaning "right at" or "in the very middle of." Expresses that something is at its absolute zenith.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夏(なつ)真(ま)っ盛(さか)り: the height of summer
  • 冬(ふゆ)真(ま)っ盛(さか)り: the dead of winter
  • 青春(せいしゅん)の真(ま)っ盛(さか)り: in the prime of youth
  • 花(はな)盛(ざか)り: flowers in full bloom (without 真(ま)っ)
  • 働(はたら)き盛(ざか)り: in the prime working years (without 真(ま)っ)

GRAMMAR:
Often used as a predicate (〜が真(ま)っ盛(さか)りだ) or as a modifier preceding a noun (真(ま)っ盛(さか)りの〜). Also appears directly after a noun without a particle: 夏(なつ)真(ま)っ盛(さか)り.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 盛(さか)り: peak, prime — the base form, less emphatic
  • 真(ま)っ最中(さいちゅう): right in the middle of — emphasizes being in the midst of an activity
  • 絶頂(ぜっちょう): summit, climax — more dramatic, often for emotions or career highs
