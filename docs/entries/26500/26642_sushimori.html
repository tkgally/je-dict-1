寿司盛(すしも)り

すしもり
noun
sushi platter; assorted sushi selection
1. sushi platter; assorted sushi; sushi assortment
An arrangement of various pieces of sushi served together on a plate or tray. Commonly ordered at sushi restaurants or purchased at supermarkets as a pre-made assortment, typically containing a variety of nigiri and sometimes maki rolls.
寿司盛(すしも)りを一(ひと)つ注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered one sushi platter.
スーパーで寿司盛(すしも)りを買(か)って帰(かえ)ろう。
Let's buy a sushi platter at the supermarket and take it home.
お祝(いわ)いの席(せき)に、特上(とくじょう)の寿司盛(すしも)りを出前(でまえ)で頼(たの)んだ。
For the celebration, we ordered a premium sushi platter for delivery.

Composed of 寿司(すし) (sushi) and 盛(も)り (serving, heap). The ～盛(も)り suffix is a common pattern for food served as an assortment or arranged on a plate.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寿司盛(すしも)りを注文(ちゅうもん)する: to order a sushi platter
  • 特上(とくじょう)寿司盛(すしも)り: premium sushi platter
  • 上(じょう)寿司盛(すしも)り: deluxe sushi platter
  • 並(なみ)寿司盛(すしも)り: regular sushi platter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 盛(も)り合(あ)わせ: assorted platter — a general term for any assortment of food items arranged together, not limited to sushi
  • 寿司(すし)折(おり): boxed sushi — sushi packed in a wooden or paper takeout box, often given as a gift or souvenir

CULTURAL NOTE:
Supermarket 寿司盛(すしも)り are a popular everyday meal option in Japan. They are often discounted in the evening before closing time, making them an affordable dinner choice.
