1. to help; to assist; to lend a hand
To provide practical assistance to someone who needs it. Implies hands-on, direct help rather than abstract support.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しを手助(てだす)けする。
To help with a move.
困(こま)っている人(ひと)を手助(てだす)けしたい。
I want to help people who are in trouble.
近所(きんじょ)の人(ひと)たちが復旧(ふっきゅう)作業(さぎょう)を手助(てだす)けしてくれた。
The neighbors helped with the restoration work.

Formed from 手(て) (hand) + 助(たす)け (help), conveying the image of literally lending a hand. More concrete and action-oriented than abstract forms of assistance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手助(てだす)けをする: to lend a hand (with を particle variant)
  • 手助(てだす)けが必要(ひつよう): to need help
  • 手助(てだす)けを求(もと)める: to seek help
  • ちょっとした手助(てだす)け: a little bit of help

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手伝(てつだ)う: to help — the most common everyday word for helping
  • 助(たす)ける: to save; to rescue — implies someone is in danger or serious difficulty
  • 援助(えんじょ)する: to provide aid — formal; often used for institutional or international assistance
  • サポートする: to support — loanword; often used in business and technology contexts
