1. additional fertilizer; side-dressing; top-dressing
Fertilizer applied to plants after they have already been planted and are actively growing, as opposed to base fertilizer applied before planting. An essential practice in Japanese gardening and agriculture.
二週間(にしゅうかん)に一回(いっかい)、追(お)い肥(ごえ)をする。
I apply additional fertilizer once every two weeks.
トマトは実(み)がつき始(はじ)めたら追(お)い肥(ごえ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
For tomatoes, additional fertilizing is important once they start bearing fruit.
追(お)い肥(ごえ)のタイミングと量(りょう)を間違(まちが)えると、花(はな)が咲(さ)かなくなることがある。
If you get the timing or amount of additional fertilizer wrong, the flowers may not bloom.

Composed of 追(お)い (additional, follow-up) and 肥(ごえ) (fertilizer, manure). Gardening is a popular hobby in Japan, and 追(お)い肥(ごえ) is a fundamental concept that appears in gardening guides, plant care labels, and television gardening programs. The timing and type of 追(お)い肥(ごえ) varies by plant and is an important part of Japanese gardening knowledge.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 追(お)い肥(ごえ)をやる: to apply additional fertilizer
  • 追(お)い肥(ごえ)の時期(じき): timing for additional fertilizing
  • 液体(えきたい)追(お)い肥(ごえ): liquid top-dressing
  • 追(お)い肥(ごえ)不要(ふよう): no additional fertilizer needed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 元肥(もとごえ): base fertilizer — fertilizer mixed into the soil before planting
  • 肥料(ひりょう): fertilizer — the general term for any plant nutrient
  • 寒肥(かんごえ): winter fertilizer — fertilizer applied during the dormant season to prepare for spring growth
