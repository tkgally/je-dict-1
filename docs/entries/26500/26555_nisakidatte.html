に先立(さきだ)って

にさきだって
expression
prior to; before; ahead of
1. prior to; before; in advance of
A formal expression indicating that one action or event occurs before another. Used to set temporal context, especially for preparatory actions taken before a main event.
会議(かいぎ)に先立(さきだ)って資料(しりょう)を配(くば)った。
I distributed materials prior to the meeting.
開会(かいかい)に先立(さきだ)ち、議長(ぎちょう)が挨拶(あいさつ)をした。
Before the opening, the chairperson gave a greeting.
新製品(しんせいひん)の発売(はつばい)に先立(さきだ)って、記者会見(きしゃかいけん)が行(おこな)われた。
A press conference was held prior to the new product launch.

A formal expression meaning "prior to" or "before," typically used in written language, speeches, and business settings. Composed of the particle に, the verb 先立(さきだ)つ (to precede), in its te-form.

FORMS:

  • に先立(さきだ)って: te-form — most common
  • に先立(さきだ)ち: conjunctive form — slightly more formal
  • に先立(さきだ)つ + noun: attributive form (e.g., 出発(しゅっぱつ)に先立(さきだ)つ準備(じゅんび))

USAGE:
The expression takes a noun before に, indicating the main event. The clause after に先立(さきだ)って describes the preparatory action. Often used in news reports, formal announcements, and ceremony contexts.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 前(まえ)に: before — more casual and general
  • に先駆(さきが)けて: ahead of others, pioneering — implies being the first to do something
  • に際(さい)して: on the occasion of — emphasizes the event itself rather than temporal sequence
