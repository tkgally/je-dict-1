米粉(こめこ)パン

こめこぱん
noun
rice flour bread
1. rice flour bread; bread made from rice flour
Bread made using rice flour instead of or in addition to wheat flour. Popular in Japan as a gluten-free alternative and as a way to promote domestic rice consumption.
米粉(こめこ)パンはもちもちしている。
Rice flour bread has a chewy, springy texture.
小麦(こむぎ)アレルギーの子(こ)どもに米粉(こめこ)パンを焼(や)いた。
I baked rice flour bread for a child with a wheat allergy.
最近(さいきん)は学校給食(がっこうきゅうしょく)でも米粉(こめこ)パンが出(で)るようになり、地元(じもと)の米(こめ)を使(つか)ったものが人気(にんき)だ。
Rice flour bread has recently started appearing in school lunches, and versions using locally grown rice are popular.

Composed of 米粉(こめこ) (rice flour) and パン (bread, from Portuguese "pão"). Part of a broader trend in Japan toward using domestically produced rice in non-traditional ways. Rice flour bread has a distinctive moist, chewy (もちもち) texture that differs from wheat bread.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 米粉(こめこ)パン専門店(せんもんてん): rice flour bread specialty shop
  • 米粉(こめこ)パンを焼(や)く: to bake rice flour bread
  • グルテンフリーの米粉(こめこ)パン: gluten-free rice flour bread

CULTURAL NOTE:
The Japanese government has promoted 米粉(こめこ) products since the 2000s as part of efforts to increase rice consumption, which has been declining as Japanese diets become more westernized. Many school lunch programs now include rice flour bread made from locally grown rice.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食パン(しょくぱん): white bread / sandwich bread — the standard bread loaf in Japan
  • 米粉(こめこ): rice flour — the ingredient itself
