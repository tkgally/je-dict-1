不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)

ふちゅうじつ
adjective
unfaithful; disloyal; untrue
1. unfaithful; disloyal; not true to one's obligations
Lacking faithfulness or loyalty to a person, duty, or commitment. Used for romantic infidelity, professional disloyalty, and failure to honor obligations.
不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な態度(たいど)は信頼(しんらい)を失(うしな)う。
A disloyal attitude causes loss of trust.
彼(かれ)は仕事(しごと)に対(たい)して不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)だと思(おも)われている。
He is considered unfaithful to his work.
原作(げんさく)に不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な映画化(えいがか)に対(たい)してファンから批判(ひはん)の声(こえ)が上(あ)がった。
Fans criticized the film adaptation for being unfaithful to the original work.

The negative form of 忠実(ちゅうじつ) (faithful; loyal), formed with the prefix 不(ふ). Functions as a na-adjective: 不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な + noun, 不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)だ as predicate.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な態度(たいど): disloyal attitude
  • 不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な翻訳(ほんやく): unfaithful translation
  • 原作(げんさく)に不忠実(ふちゅうじつ): unfaithful to the original
  • 不忠実(ふちゅうじつ)な部下(ぶか): disloyal subordinate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不誠実(ふせいじつ): insincere; dishonest — focuses on sincerity rather than loyalty
  • 裏切(うらぎ)り: betrayal — a stronger, more active word implying deliberate treachery
  • 不義理(ふぎり): failing in one's social obligations — specifically about social duty
