ミサイル

みさいる
noun
missile
1. missile
A self-propelled weapon designed to fly toward a target, either guided or ballistic. Used in military contexts and frequently appears in news about international security.
ミサイルが発射(はっしゃ)された。
A missile was launched.
弾道(だんどう)ミサイルの発射(はっしゃ)実験(じっけん)が行(おこな)われた。
A ballistic missile launch test was conducted.
ミサイル防衛(ぼうえい)システムの整備(せいび)が急(いそ)がれている。
There is urgency in developing the missile defense system.

A loanword from English "missile." This word appears extremely frequently in Japanese news, particularly regarding North Korean missile tests and regional security discussions. Living in Japan, learners will encounter this word regularly in news broadcasts and newspaper headlines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ミサイルを発射(はっしゃ)する: to launch a missile
  • ミサイル防衛(ぼうえい): missile defense
  • 弾道(だんどう)ミサイル: ballistic missile
  • 巡航(じゅんこう)ミサイル: cruise missile
  • 迎撃(げいげき)ミサイル: interceptor missile
  • ミサイル実験(じっけん): missile test

RELATED WORDS:

  • ロケット: rocket — used for space launch vehicles as well as weapons
  • 砲弾(ほうだん): shell, artillery round — a non-self-propelled projectile
  • 魚雷(ぎょらい): torpedo — a self-propelled underwater weapon
