1. to increase rapidly; to surge; to skyrocket
For something to increase suddenly and dramatically in number, amount, or degree.
感染者(かんせんしゃ)が急増(きゅうぞう)している。
The number of infected people is surging.
SNSの利用者(りようしゃ)が近年(きんねん)急増(きゅうぞう)した。
The number of social media users has rapidly increased in recent years.
外国人観光客(がいこくじんかんこうきゃく)の急増(きゅうぞう)により、ホテルの予約(よやく)が取(と)りにくくなっている。
Due to the rapid increase in foreign tourists, hotel reservations have become hard to get.

An intransitive verb commonly used in news and reports. The noun form 急増(きゅうぞう) is equally common. Expresses a steep, sudden increase rather than gradual growth.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人口(じんこう)が急増(きゅうぞう)する: population surges
  • 需要(じゅよう)が急増(きゅうぞう)する: demand surges
  • 急増(きゅうぞう)する傾向(けいこう): trend of rapid increase
  • 前年比(ぜんねんひ)で急増(きゅうぞう): rapid increase year-over-year

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 増加(ぞうか)する: to increase — neutral; does not imply speed
  • 激増(げきぞう)する: to increase explosively — even more dramatic than 急増(きゅうぞう)
  • 急減(きゅうげん)する: to decrease rapidly — the opposite
  • 増(ふ)える: to increase — the basic native Japanese equivalent, neutral in tone
