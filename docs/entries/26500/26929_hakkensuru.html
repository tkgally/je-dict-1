1. to discover; to find
To find something previously unknown or hidden, such as a new species, a scientific principle, or historical evidence.
新(あたら)しい星(ほし)を発見(はっけん)した。
A new star was discovered.
古代(こだい)の遺跡(いせき)が偶然(ぐうぜん)発見(はっけん)された。
Ancient ruins were discovered by chance.
旅(たび)をすると、新(あたら)しい文化(ぶんか)や価値観(かちかん)を発見(はっけん)することができる。
When you travel, you can discover new cultures and perspectives.
2. to detect; to spot; to notice
To find or notice something that requires attention, such as a problem, an error, or a suspicious situation.
書類(しょるい)にミスを発見(はっけん)した。
I found an error in the documents.
病気(びょうき)を早期(そうき)に発見(はっけん)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to detect illness at an early stage.
検査(けんさ)で異常(いじょう)が発見(はっけん)されたため、精密(せいみつ)検査(けんさ)を受(う)けることになった。
An abnormality was detected during the examination, so I will undergo a detailed checkup.

Covers both major discoveries (scientific breakthroughs, archaeological finds) and everyday detection (finding errors, noticing problems). The noun form 発見(はっけん) is also very common, especially in compound expressions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(だい)発見(はっけん): great discovery
  • 新(しん)発見(はっけん): new discovery
  • 早期(そうき)発見(はっけん): early detection
  • 偶然(ぐうぜん)の発見(はっけん): accidental discovery
  • 発見(はっけん)者(しゃ): discoverer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)つける: to find — the everyday word for finding something; less formal
  • 発覚(はっかく)する: to come to light — used when something hidden (usually negative) is exposed
  • 検出(けんしゅつ)する: to detect — technical term used in science and medicine for detecting substances or signals
