1. to flee; to run away; to escape; to abscond
To escape or run away from a place, usually to avoid capture, punishment, or a dangerous situation. Implies urgency and often illegality — fleeing from the law, a prison, or persecution.
犯人(はんにん)は海外(かいがい)に逃亡(とうぼう)した。
The criminal fled overseas.
戦争(せんそう)から逃亡(とうぼう)する人々(ひとびと)が国境(こっきょう)に殺到(さっとう)した。
People fleeing the war flooded to the border.
受刑者(じゅけいしゃ)が刑務所(けいむしょ)から逃亡(とうぼう)した。
A prisoner escaped from the prison.
逃亡(とうぼう)を続(つ��)けていた容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が、十年(じゅうねん)後(ご)にようやく逮捕(たいほ)された。
The suspect who had been on the run was finally arrested ten years later.

A serious, somewhat formal word associated with crime, war, and political persecution. Stronger and more dramatic than simply 'running away' — implies a sustained escape from authorities or dire circumstances. Very common in news reports and crime dramas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 海外(かいがい)に逃亡(とうぼう)する: to flee abroad
  • 逃亡(とうぼう)生活(せいかつ): life on the run
  • 逃亡(とうぼう)を図(はか)る: to attempt to flee
  • 逃亡(とうぼう)中(ちゅう): on the run; at large
  • 逃亡(とうぼう)犯(はん): fugitive

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逃(に)げる: to run away — general and everyday; not specifically from the law
  • 亡命(ぼうめい)する: to seek asylum; to defect — political escape to another country
  • 脱走(だっそう)する: to desert; to break out — specifically from confinement or military duty
