フロアマップ

ふろあまっぷ
noun
floor map; floor plan; floor guide
1. floor map; floor plan; floor guide
A diagram or guide showing the layout of a particular floor of a building, commonly found in shopping malls, department stores, hospitals, and museums.
フロアマップは入口(いりぐち)にあります。
The floor map is at the entrance.
フロアマップを見(み)て、レストランの場所(ばしょ)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the floor map to find the location of the restaurant.
このショッピングモールのフロアマップはウェブサイトからもダウンロードできます。
The floor map of this shopping mall can also be downloaded from the website.

A wasei-eigo compound from English "floor" + "map." Very commonly seen in Japanese commercial buildings. In English, the equivalent is usually "floor plan" or "floor guide" rather than "floor map."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フロアマップを見(み)る: to look at a floor map
  • フロアマップを確認(かくにん)する: to check the floor map
  • 館内(かんない)フロアマップ: building floor map

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見取(みと)り図(ず): floor plan — a more traditional Japanese term
  • 案内図(あんないず): guide map — broader term for any informational map
  • 間取(まど)り図(ず): room layout — specifically for apartment/house layouts
