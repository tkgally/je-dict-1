タスク管理(かんり)

たすくかんり
noun
task management
1. task management; task tracking
The process of organizing, prioritizing, and tracking tasks and to-do items, especially in a work or project context. Encompasses both the concept and the tools (apps, boards, lists) used to manage tasks.
タスク管理(かんり)にアプリを使(つか)っている。
I use an app for task management.
チームのタスク管理(かんり)を改善(かいぜん)するために新(あたら)しいツールを導入(どうにゅう)した。
We introduced a new tool to improve the team's task management.
タスク管理(かんり)がうまくできないと、締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)わなくなることが多(おお)い。
If you can't manage your tasks well, you often end up missing deadlines.

Composed of タスク (task, from English) and 管理(かんり) (management). A term widely used in modern Japanese workplaces, particularly in IT, project management, and office environments. The concept has become mainstream with the spread of digital productivity tools.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タスク管理(かんり)ツール: task management tool
  • タスク管理(かんり)アプリ: task management app
  • タスク管理(かんり)を徹底(てってい)する: to thoroughly manage tasks
  • 個人(こじん)のタスク管理(かんり): personal task management

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 進捗管理(しんちょくかんり): progress management — focuses on tracking how far along work is
  • 工程管理(こうていかんり): process management — managing the sequence of work stages
  • スケジュール管理(かんり): schedule management — focuses on time and calendar coordination
  • 業務管理(ぎょうむかんり): work management — broader term covering all aspects of work operations
