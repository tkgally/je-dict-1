1. increasing sophistication; becoming more elaborate or refined
The process of something becoming more intricate, precise, or sophisticated over time. Often used in contexts of technology, craftsmanship, fraud techniques, or design.
サイバー攻撃(こうげき)の手口(てぐち)が精巧化(せいこうか)している。
Cyber attack methods are becoming increasingly sophisticated.
偽造(ぎぞう)技術(ぎじゅつ)の精巧化(せいこうか)により、偽札(にせさつ)の発見(はっけん)が難(むずか)しくなった。
Due to the increasing sophistication of counterfeiting technology, it has become harder to detect fake bills.
製品(せいひん)の精巧化(せいこうか)が進(すす)む一方(いっぽう)で、修理(しゅうり)にかかるコストも上(あ)がっている。
While products are becoming more sophisticated, repair costs are also rising.

Composed of 精巧(せいこう) (elaborate/exquisite) and 化(か) (transformation/-ization). The suffix 化(か) indicates a process of change or becoming. Often appears in news and technical writing about evolving threats, technologies, or techniques.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精巧化(せいこうか)が進(すす)む: sophistication advances
  • 精巧化(せいこうか)する: to become more sophisticated
  • 手口(てぐち)の精巧化(せいこうか): increasing sophistication of methods

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 高度化(こうどか): advancement/becoming more advanced — broader scope
  • 巧妙化(こうみょうか): becoming more cunning — carries a negative nuance (trickery)
  • 複雑化(ふくざつか): becoming more complex — focuses on complexity rather than refinement
  • 精巧(せいこう): elaborate/exquisite — the base adjective
