1. to dawn; to grow light; to break (of day)
Describes the sky gradually becoming lighter as dawn approaches. A literary and evocative word that conveys the slow, whitening spread of light before sunrise.
東(ひがし)の空(そら)が白々(しらじら)と明(あ)けてきた。
The eastern sky began to grow light.
夜(よる)を徹(てっ)して話(はな)しているうちに、空(そら)が白々(しらじら)と明(あ)けた。
While we talked through the night, the sky gradually grew light.
白々(しらじら)と明(あ)ける空(そら)の下(した)、漁師(りょうし)たちはすでに船(ふね)を出(だ)していた。
Under the brightening dawn sky, the fishermen had already set out in their boats.

Composed of 白々(しらじら) (whitish, pale) and 明(あ)ける (to open/dawn). A literary expression that paints the visual scene of the sky turning pale before sunrise. Often used in the pattern 白々(しらじら)と明(あ)ける, where と marks the manner of the dawning.

USAGE:
This word belongs to literary and poetic registers. In everyday speech, 夜(よ)が明(あ)ける (dawn breaks) or 朝(あさ)になる (it becomes morning) is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空(そら)が白々(しらじら)と明(あ)ける: the sky grows pale with dawn
  • 白々(しらじら)明(あ)けの空(そら): the brightening dawn sky

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夜明(よあ)け: dawn/daybreak — the standard neutral term
  • 明(あ)け方(がた): early dawn — refers to the time period
  • 東雲(しののめ): dawn (literary) — classical/poetic synonym
