新造語(しんぞうご)

しんぞうご
noun
neologism; newly coined word
1. neologism; newly coined word; new coinage
A word that has been recently created or invented, either by combining existing elements in a new way or by introducing an entirely new term. Used in linguistic and media discussions about language evolution.
この言葉(ことば)は最近(さいきん)の新造語(しんぞうご)だ。
This word is a recent neologism.
SNSの普及(ふきゅう)で新造語(しんぞうご)が次々(つぎつぎ)と生(う)まれている。
With the spread of social media, new coinages are being born one after another.
辞書(じしょ)の編集者(へんしゅうしゃ)は毎年(まいとし)新造語(しんぞうご)を調査(ちょうさ)して、掲載(けいさい)するかどうかを判断(はんだん)する。
Dictionary editors survey neologisms every year and decide whether to include them.

Composed of 新造(しんぞう) (newly made) and 語(ご) (word). Japanese has a rich tradition of word creation, particularly through combining kanji compounds, borrowing from English (wasei-eigo), and blending elements from multiple languages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新造語(しんぞうご)を作(つく)る: to coin a new word
  • 新造語(しんぞうご)が定着(ていちゃく)する: for a neologism to become established
  • 新造語(しんぞうご)が生(う)まれる: for a neologism to emerge

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 造語(ぞうご): coined word — a broader term that includes any artificially created word regardless of when it was coined
  • 新語(しんご): new word — a word that has recently entered the language, whether coined or borrowed
  • 流行語(りゅうこうご): buzzword, trendy expression — a word or phrase that is currently popular, but may not be newly created
