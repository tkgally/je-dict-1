ごとし

ごとし
expression
like; as if; as though
1. like; as if; as though; resembling
A literary or classical expression meaning 'like' or 'as if,' used to draw comparisons. More formal and literary than ようだ or みたいだ.
光陰(こういん)矢(や)のごとし。
Time flies like an arrow.
彼(かれ)の走(はし)りは風(かぜ)のごとく速(はや)い。
His running is as fast as the wind.
水(みず)の流(なが)れのごとく、時間(じかん)は二度(にど)と戻(もど)らない。
Like the flow of water, time never returns.

A classical Japanese expression derived from the literary language. Still commonly encountered in proverbs, set phrases, and formal or poetic writing. The adverbial form ごとく ("like," modifying a verb) is more frequent in modern usage than the terminal form ごとし.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • noun + のごとし/ごとく: 風(かぜ)のごとく (like the wind)
  • verb dictionary form + がごとし/がごとく: 燃(も)えるがごとく (as if burning)

FAMOUS PROVERBS:

  • 光陰(こういん)矢(や)のごとし: time flies like an arrow
  • 去(さ)る者(もの)は日々(ひび)に疎(うと)し: out of sight, out of mind

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ようだ: the standard modern equivalent; neutral in register
  • みたいだ: colloquial equivalent
  • ごとし is much more literary and formal than both; used primarily in writing, proverbs, and rhetoric

REGISTER:
Literary and formal. Rarely used in casual conversation except in fixed expressions. Common in literature, speeches, and classical quotations.
