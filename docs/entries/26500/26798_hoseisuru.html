1. to correct; to adjust; to calibrate; to compensate for
To make fine adjustments or corrections to bring something to a more accurate or desired state. Used in technical, budgetary, and everyday contexts.
色(いろ)を補正(ほせい)する。
To correct the color.
予算(よさん)を補正(ほせい)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
The budget needs to be revised.
カメラの手(て)ぶれを自動的(じどうてき)に補正(ほせい)する機能(きのう)がついている。
It has a function that automatically corrects camera shake.

Implies making corrections or adjustments to something that is slightly off from the ideal. Commonly used in photography/image editing, government budgets, statistics, and technical calibration.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 補正(ほせい)予算(よさん): supplementary budget; revised budget
  • 手(て)ぶれ補正(ほせい): image stabilization
  • 色(いろ)補正(ほせい): color correction
  • 補正(ほせい)値(ち): correction value; offset
  • 補正(ほせい)下着(したぎ): shapewear (body-shaping underwear)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 修正(しゅうせい)する: to revise; to amend — broader; can mean rewriting or significantly changing
  • 訂正(ていせい)する: to correct (errors) — specifically for fixing mistakes in text or statements
  • 調整(ちょうせい)する: to adjust; to coordinate — more about bringing into alignment or balance
