水域(すいいき)

すいいき
noun
waters; water area; body of water
1. waters; water area; body of water
A defined area or expanse of water, whether a river, lake, ocean, or other waterway. Often used in official, environmental, and regulatory contexts.
この水域(すいいき)は遊泳(ゆうえい)禁止(きんし)です。
Swimming is prohibited in this water area.
汚染(おせん)された水域(すいいき)の浄化(じょうか)が進(すす)んでいる。
Cleanup of the polluted waters is underway.
港(みなと)の周辺(しゅうへん)水域(すいいき)では大型(おおがた)船舶(せんぱく)の航行(こうこう)が制限(せいげん)されている。
Navigation of large vessels is restricted in the waters around the port.

A compound of 水(すい) (water) and 域(いき) (area, zone). This is a somewhat formal and technical term used in environmental science, maritime regulation, and geography.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 閉鎖(へいさ)水域(すいいき): enclosed waters (bay, lake)
  • 沿岸(えんがん)水域(すいいき): coastal waters
  • 内陸(ないりく)水域(すいいき): inland waters
  • 水域(すいいき)汚染(おせん): water area pollution
  • 保護(ほご)水域(すいいき): protected waters

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 海域(かいいき): sea area, maritime zone — specifically ocean/sea areas
  • 領海(りょうかい): territorial waters — a legal term for sovereign sea territory
  • 流域(りゅういき): river basin, watershed — the area drained by a river system
