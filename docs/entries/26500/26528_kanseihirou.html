完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)

かんせいひろう
noun
premiere; unveiling; completion ceremony
1. premiere; unveiling; completion ceremony
A public event or showing to announce and display a completed work, building, or product. Most commonly used for film premieres and building completion ceremonies.
映画(えいが)の完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)試写会(ししゃかい)が開(ひら)かれた。
A premiere screening of the film was held.
新(しん)ビルの完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)パーティーに招待(しょうたい)された。
I was invited to the completion unveiling party for the new building.
監督(かんとく)と主演(しゅえん)俳優(はいゆう)が完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)舞台挨拶(ぶたいあいさつ)に登壇(とうだん)した。
The director and lead actor appeared at the premiere stage greeting.

A compound of 完成(かんせい) (completion) and 披露(ひろう) (public announcement; unveiling). Most frequently used in the entertainment industry for film and drama premieres.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)試写会(ししゃかい): premiere screening
  • 完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)上映(じょうえい): premiere showing
  • 完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)舞台挨拶(ぶたいあいさつ): premiere stage greeting
  • 完成(かんせい)披露(ひろう)記者会見(きしゃかいけん): premiere press conference

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初公開(はつこうかい): first public showing — more general, used for any type of first reveal
  • お披露目(ひろめ): debut; unveiling — broader, used for introducing people, products, and events
  • プレミア上映(じょうえい): premiere screening (loanword) — the film industry loanword alternative
