1. escapism; escape from reality
the act of avoiding or running away from real-world problems or responsibilities by immersing oneself in fantasy, entertainment, or other distractions
ゲームは現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)の手段(しゅだん)だ。
Games are a means of escapism.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)になると現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)で掃除(そうじ)を始(はじ)める。
When exams approach, I start cleaning as a form of escapism.
現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)ばかりしていないで、そろそろ問題(もんだい)に向(む)き合(あ)わないといけない。
I need to stop escaping from reality and start facing the problem.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) widely used in casual conversation, especially among younger speakers. Often used self-deprecatingly or humorously to describe procrastination or indulging in hobbies instead of facing responsibilities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)する: to escape from reality
  • 現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)の手段(しゅだん): a means of escapism
  • 現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)に走(はし)る: to run toward escapism
  • 現実逃避(げんじつとうひ)をやめる: to stop escaping reality

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逃避(とうひ): avoidance, escape — more general, not limited to reality
  • 先延(さきの)ばし: procrastination — focuses on delay rather than psychological escape
  • 白昼夢(はくちゅうむ): daydream — one specific form of escapism

ETYMOLOGY:
現実(げんじつ) (reality) + 逃避(とうひ) (escape, avoidance). Literal meaning: "reality-escape."
