購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん)

こうにゅうとくてん
noun
purchase bonus; special gift or benefit included with a purchase
1. purchase bonus; special gift or benefit included with a purchase
Common in retail, especially for books, games, CDs, and online shopping.
購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん)としてポストカードが付(つ)く。
A postcard is included as a purchase bonus.
初回(しょかい)購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん)は数量(すうりょう)限定(げんてい)です。
The first-edition purchase bonus is limited in quantity.
この店(みせ)で予約(よやく)すると、限定(げんてい)の購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん)がもらえるらしい。
Apparently you can get an exclusive purchase bonus if you pre-order at this store.

A compound of 購入(こうにゅう) (purchase) and 特典(とくてん) (special benefit/privilege). Refers to bonus items or perks given to customers upon buying a product, particularly common in Japanese retail culture where different stores may offer different bonuses for the same product.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 初回(しょかい)購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん): first-edition purchase bonus
  • 先着(せんちゃく)購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん): first-come-first-served bonus
  • 店舗(てんぽ)別(べつ)購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん): store-specific purchase bonus
  • 購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん)付(つ)き: with purchase bonus included

CULTURAL NOTES:
Purchase bonuses are deeply embedded in Japanese retail marketing. Anime, manga, and game releases frequently have different 購入特典(こうにゅうとくてん) depending on the retailer (Animate, Toranoana, Amazon, etc.), encouraging fans to buy from multiple stores. Common bonus items include clear files, postcards, tapestries, and exclusive cover art.
