1. to wobble; to shake; to be unsteady
Describes something that is physically unstable and shaking or rocking back and forth. Used for loose teeth, shaky furniture, buildings during earthquakes, or boiling liquid.
歯(は)がぐらぐらする。
My tooth is loose.
この椅子(いす)はぐらぐらして危(あぶ)ない。
This chair wobbles and is dangerous.
地震(じしん)で建物(たてもの)がぐらぐらと揺(ゆ)れた。
The building shook and swayed during the earthquake.
2. to waver; to be indecisive
Figuratively describes a person whose resolve, opinions, or decisions are unsteady and keep changing.
気持(きも)ちがぐらぐらする。
My feelings are wavering.
彼(かれ)に説得(せっとく)されて決意(けつい)がぐらぐらした。
His persuasion made my resolve waver.
ぐらぐらしないで、自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)を貫(つらぬ)きなさい。
Don't waver — stick to your own opinion.

Mimetic verb where the reduplication ぐらぐら evokes repeated, unstable rocking or swaying motion. One of the most common onomatopoeia for physical instability. Also used for water at a rolling boil (お湯がぐらぐら沸く).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歯(は)がぐらぐらする: tooth is loose
  • ぐらぐら揺(ゆ)れる: to shake/sway
  • お湯(ゆ)がぐらぐら沸(わ)く: water boils vigorously
  • 気持(きも)ちがぐらぐらする: to feel indecisive
  • ぐらぐらの机(つくえ): a wobbly desk

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • がたがたする: to rattle, to be rickety — implies loose parts rattling, more mechanical
  • ゆらゆらする: to sway gently — slower, calmer motion; not necessarily unstable
  • ふらふらする: to be unsteady (person) — describes a person who is dizzy or staggering
