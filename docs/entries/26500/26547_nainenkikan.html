内燃機関(ないねんきかん)

ないねんきかん
noun
internal combustion engine
1. internal combustion engine; ICE
An engine that generates power by burning fuel inside the engine itself, as opposed to external combustion engines like steam engines. Includes gasoline engines, diesel engines, and gas turbines.
自動車(じどうしゃ)の多(おお)くは内燃機関(ないねんきかん)で動(うご)く。
Most automobiles run on internal combustion engines.
電気自動車(でんきじどうしゃ)の普及(ふきゅう)で内燃機関(ないねんきかん)の時代(じだい)が終(お)わると言(い)われている。
It is said that the era of internal combustion engines will end with the spread of electric vehicles.
内燃機関(ないねんきかん)の効率(こうりつ)を高(たか)めることで、燃費(ねんぴ)と排出(はいしゅつ)ガスの両方(りょうほう)を改善(かいぜん)できる。
By improving the efficiency of internal combustion engines, both fuel economy and emissions can be improved.

Composed of 内燃(ないねん) (internal combustion) and 機関(きかん) (engine, mechanism). This technical term is frequently encountered in news about the automotive industry, particularly discussions of the transition from gasoline-powered vehicles to electric vehicles. Japan's automotive industry makes this a particularly relevant topic in Japanese media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内燃機関(ないねんきかん)車(しゃ): internal combustion engine vehicle
  • 内燃機関(ないねんきかん)の廃止(はいし): phasing out of combustion engines
  • 内燃機関(ないねんきかん)の開発(かいはつ): combustion engine development
  • ガソリン内燃機関(ないねんきかん): gasoline internal combustion engine

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • エンジン: engine — the everyday loanword, used in casual conversation
  • 外燃機関(がいねんきかん): external combustion engine — burns fuel outside the engine (e.g., steam engine)
  • 電動(でんどう)モーター: electric motor — the alternative powertrain replacing combustion engines
