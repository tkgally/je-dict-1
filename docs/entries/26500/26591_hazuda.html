はずだ

はずだ
expression
should be; is expected to; is supposed to
1. should be; is expected to; ought to be
Expresses the speaker's expectation or logical conclusion that something is or should be the case, based on reasoning, evidence, or prior knowledge.
もう届(とど)いたはずだ。
It should have arrived by now.
この時間(じかん)なら電車(でんしゃ)は空(す)いているはずだ。
The train should be empty at this hour.
彼(かれ)は日本(にほん)に十年(じゅうねん)住(す)んでいたから、日本語(にほんご)が上手(じょうず)なはずだ。
He lived in Japan for ten years, so his Japanese should be good.
2. is supposed to; is scheduled to
Indicates that something was planned, arranged, or supposed to happen according to a schedule or agreement.
会議(かいぎ)は三時(さんじ)に始(はじ)まるはずだ。
The meeting is supposed to start at three.
彼女(かのじょ)は今日(きょう)来(く)るはずだったのに。
She was supposed to come today, but...
荷物(にもつ)は明日(あした)の午前中(ごぜんちゅう)に届(とど)くはずだったが、遅(おく)れているらしい。
The package was supposed to arrive by tomorrow morning, but it seems to be delayed.

Attaches to the plain form of verbs, adjectives, and nouns (+の). Expresses the speaker's confident expectation based on logic or evidence, not mere hope or wish. The core meaning comes from 筈(はず) (the notch of an arrow where the bowstring fits), suggesting something that fits naturally or logically.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • verb plain form + はずだ: 知(し)っているはずだ (should know)
  • い-adjective + はずだ: 安(やす)いはずだ (should be cheap)
  • な-adjective + なはずだ: 静(しず)かなはずだ (should be quiet)
  • noun + のはずだ: 休(やす)みのはずだ (should be a day off)
  • はずがない: there is no way that... (strong negation)
  • はずだった: was supposed to (but didn't happen)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • べきだ: expresses moral obligation or recommendation ("should" as in "ought to"), while はずだ expresses expectation or logical conclusion
  • だろう: expresses probability or supposition, while はずだ conveys stronger confidence based on evidence

REGISTER:
Used in both spoken and written Japanese. The polite form はずです is common in everyday conversation.
