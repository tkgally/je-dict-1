1. consecutive work days; working without a day off; continuous duty
Working for multiple days in a row without rest days in between. Often discussed in the context of labor regulations, overwork, and work-life balance in Japan.
十日間(とおかかん)の連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ)はきつい。
Ten consecutive days of work is tough.
連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ)が続(つづ)いて、体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)してしまった。
I fell ill because the consecutive workdays kept piling up.
労働基準法(ろうどうきじゅんほう)では、長期(ちょうき)の連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ)を防(ふせ)ぐために休日(きゅうじつ)の確保(かくほ)が義務(ぎむ)づけられている。
Under the Labor Standards Act, securing rest days is mandated to prevent extended periods of consecutive work.

Composed of 連続(れんぞく) (consecutive, continuous) and 勤務(きんむ) (duty, work). A term frequently used in discussions of workplace practices and labor law in Japan, where overwork (過労(かろう)) is a recognized social issue.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ)日数(にっすう): number of consecutive workdays
  • 連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ)時間(じかん): consecutive working hours
  • 長時間(ちょうじかん)連続勤務(れんぞくきんむ): extended consecutive work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 残業(ざんぎょう): overtime — working beyond normal hours on a single day, rather than across multiple days
  • 過重労働(かじゅうろうどう): excessive work — a broader term covering both long hours and continuous work
  • 休日出勤(きゅうじつしゅっきん): working on a day off — coming in on a scheduled rest day
