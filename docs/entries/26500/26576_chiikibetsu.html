地域別(ちいきべつ)

ちいきべつ
noun
by region; regional breakdown; area-by-area
1. by region; regional breakdown; area-by-area
Classification, sorting, or presentation of data organized according to geographic region. Commonly used in statistics, reports, and administrative contexts.
地域別(ちいきべつ)の人口(じんこう)データを見(み)る。
Look at the population data by region.
売上(うりあげ)を地域別(ちいきべつ)にまとめた報告書(ほうこくしょ)を作成(さくせい)した。
I prepared a report summarizing sales by region.
地域別(ちいきべつ)に見(み)ると、都市部(としぶ)と農村部(のうそんぶ)では大(おお)きな差(さ)があることが分(わ)かった。
Looking at it by region, it became clear that there is a significant difference between urban and rural areas.

Formed from 地域(ちいき) (region, area) and 別(べつ) (by, classification). The suffix 別(べつ) is extremely productive in Japanese and attaches to many nouns to mean "sorted by" or "categorized by." This word appears frequently in news, business reports, and government statistics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地域別(ちいきべつ)の統計(とうけい): regional statistics
  • 地域別(ちいきべつ)に分析(ぶんせき)する: to analyze by region
  • 地域別(ちいきべつ)人口(じんこう): population by region
  • 地域別(ちいきべつ)ランキング: regional ranking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年齢別(ねんれいべつ): by age — same 別(べつ) pattern applied to age
  • 都道府県別(とどうふけんべつ): by prefecture — a more specific regional breakdown
  • 国別(くにべつ): by country — the same pattern applied to nations
