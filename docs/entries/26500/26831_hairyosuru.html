1. to give consideration; to be mindful; to show concern for
To thoughtfully take something or someone into account when making decisions or taking action. Implies a conscious effort to accommodate others' needs, feelings, or circumstances.
環境(かんきょう)に配慮(はいりょ)する。
To be mindful of the environment.
相手(あいて)の気持(きも)ちに配慮(はいりょ)して発言(はつげん)する。
To speak with consideration for the other person's feelings.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)や障害者(しょうがいしゃ)に配慮(はいりょ)した設計(せっけい)になっている。
The design takes elderly and disabled people into consideration.
周(まわ)りの人(ひと)に配慮(はいりょ)しながら行動(こうどう)することが社会人(しゃかいじん)として大切(たいせつ)だ。
Acting with consideration for those around you is important as a member of society.

A word that captures an important aspect of Japanese social values — being attentive to others' needs without being asked. Often appears in formal contexts like policy documents, product descriptions, and workplace guidelines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)への配慮(はいりょ): environmental consideration
  • 人権(じんけん)に配慮(はいりょ)する: to be mindful of human rights
  • 安全(あんぜん)に配慮(はいりょ)する: to give consideration to safety
  • 配慮(はいりょ)が足(た)りない: lacking in consideration
  • 配慮(はいりょ)に欠(か)ける: to lack consideration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気(き)を遣(つか)う: to be attentive — more personal, day-to-day thoughtfulness
  • 考慮(こうりょ)する: to consider — more analytical, weighing factors in a decision
  • 思(おも)いやる: to be sympathetic — emphasizes emotional empathy
