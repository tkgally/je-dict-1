がらりと

がらりと
adverb
completely; drastically; with a sudden change
1. completely; drastically; with a sudden change
Describes a dramatic, sudden transformation where something becomes entirely different from before. Used with verbs of change.
雰囲気(ふんいき)ががらりと変(か)わった。
The atmosphere changed completely.
転職(てんしょく)して生活(せいかつ)ががらりと変(か)わった。
My life changed drastically after changing jobs.
新(あたら)しい店長(てんちょう)が来(き)てから、店(みせ)の印象(いんしょう)ががらりと変(か)わった。
Since the new manager came, the store's image has changed completely.
2. with a rattle; with a clatter
The sound of a sliding door or shutter being opened quickly, or of something heavy rolling or sliding.
戸(と)をがらりと開(あ)けた。
I slid the door open with a rattle.
玄関(げんかん)のドアががらりと開(ひら)いた。
The front door opened with a clatter.
引(ひ)き戸(ど)をがらりと開(あ)けて、大(おお)きな声(こえ)で「ただいま」と言(い)った。
I slid the door open with a clatter and said 'I'm home' in a loud voice.

Mimetic adverb with two distinct uses. Sense 1 (change) is far more common in modern usage and describes a total transformation — not a gradual shift, but a sudden, wholesale change. Sense 2 (sound) is onomatopoeic and specifically evokes the noise of a sliding door or heavy object.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • がらりと変(か)わる: to change completely
  • がらりと変(か)える: to change something completely
  • 印象(いんしょう)ががらりと変(か)わる: impression changes drastically
  • 態度(たいど)ががらりと変(か)わる: attitude changes suddenly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すっかり: completely — neutral, describes thoroughness without implying surprise
  • 一変(いっぺん)する: to change completely — more formal, written style
  • ごろごろ: rolling sound — different onomatopoeia for heavy rolling objects
