向(む)かい側(がわ)

むかいがわ
noun
opposite side; the other side; across the way
1. opposite side; the other side; across the way
The side that faces you or is directly across from a reference point. Used for locations across a street, river, table, or other space.
駅(えき)は道(みち)の向(む)かい側(がわ)にある。
The station is on the other side of the road.
向(む)かい側(がわ)の席(せき)に座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit in the seat across from here.
川(かわ)の向(む)かい側(がわ)に小(ちい)さな村(むら)が見(み)える。
A small village can be seen on the other side of the river.
向(む)かい側(がわ)のビルに新(あたら)しいレストランが開店(かいてん)した。
A new restaurant opened in the building across the way.

Common spatial term composed of 向かい (facing, opposite) + 側 (side). Very frequently used when giving directions or describing the relative positions of things. Can be used with or without a preceding noun phrase specifying what it's across from.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)の向(む)かい側(がわ): the other side of the street
  • 通(とお)りの向(む)かい側(がわ): across the street
  • 向(む)かい側(がわ)に渡(わた)る: to cross to the other side
  • 向(む)かい側(がわ)の家(いえ): the house across the way

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 向(む)かい: opposite, facing — shorter form; often used as a modifier (向(む)かいの家(いえ))
  • 反対側(はんたいがわ): the opposite side — more general; not necessarily facing you
  • 向(む)こう側(がわ): the far side — emphasizes distance; the other side of a barrier
