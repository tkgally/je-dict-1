1. to heal; to recover; to be cured
For a disease, injury, or condition to be fully healed or cured. A formal, medical term indicating complete recovery.
傷(きず)が完全(かんぜん)に治癒(ちゆ)した。
The wound healed completely.
自然(しぜん)治癒(ちゆ)力(りょく)を高(たか)めることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to strengthen the body's natural healing power.
この病気(びょうき)は早期(そうき)に治療(ちりょう)すれば治癒(ちゆ)する可能性(かのうせい)が高(たか)い。
If treated early, there is a high possibility of this disease being cured.

A formal medical term that implies complete recovery, not just improvement. The intransitive verb 治癒(ちゆ)する means the condition heals on its own or is healed, while 治療(ちりょう)する (to treat) describes the active process of medical intervention.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自然(しぜん)治癒(ちゆ): natural healing; spontaneous recovery
  • 治癒(ちゆ)率(りつ): cure rate
  • 治癒(ちゆ)力(りょく): healing power
  • 完全(かんぜん)治癒(ちゆ): complete cure
  • 治癒(ちゆ)不能(ふのう): incurable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 治(なお)る: to get better; to heal — the everyday word for recovering from illness or injury
  • 回復(かいふく)する: to recover — broader; includes recovering strength, energy, or normal function, not just curing disease
  • 治療(ちりょう)する: to treat; to cure — the active medical process, while 治癒(ちゆ) describes the result
