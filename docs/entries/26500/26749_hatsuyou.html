1. leafing out; putting out leaves; foliation
The process of a tree or plant producing new leaves, especially in spring. A botanical term used to describe the seasonal emergence of foliage.
桜(さくら)が発葉(はつよう)し始(はじ)めた。
The cherry trees have started to put out leaves.
今年(ことし)は暖冬(だんとう)の影響(えいきょう)で発葉(はつよう)が早(はや)い。
Leafing out is early this year due to the mild winter.
落葉樹(らくようじゅ)の発葉(はつよう)時期(じき)は気温(きおん)によって毎年(まいとし)変(か)わる。
The timing of leafing out in deciduous trees changes every year depending on the temperature.

A botanical term composed of 発(はつ) (emergence, development) and 葉(よう) (leaf). Describes the phenological event of trees and plants producing new leaves, particularly relevant in seasonal climate zones like Japan where the change from bare branches to green foliage marks the arrival of spring.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発葉(はつよう)時期(じき): leafing-out season
  • 発葉(はつよう)が早(はや)い: early leafing out
  • 発葉(はつよう)が遅(おく)れる: delayed leafing out
  • 発葉(はつよう)前(まえ): before leafing out

RELATED TERMS:

  • 落葉(らくよう): leaf fall, defoliation — the opposite seasonal process
  • 新緑(しんりょく): fresh green leaves — the visual result of leafing out
  • 芽吹(めぶ)き: budding, sprouting — the earlier stage before full leaf emergence
