種実(しゅじつ)

しゅじつ
noun
nuts and seeds; seed and fruit
1. nuts and seeds; seeds and fruits
A collective term for the edible seeds, nuts, and small dried fruits of plants. Used in food science, nutrition, and botany to refer to this food category as a group.
種実(しゅじつ)類(るい)は栄養(えいよう)が豊富(ほうふ)だ。
Nuts and seeds are rich in nutrition.
この料理(りょうり)には種実(しゅじつ)をたっぷり使(つか)っている。
This dish uses plenty of nuts and seeds.
アーモンドやくるみなどの種実(しゅじつ)類(るい)には良質(りょうしつ)な脂肪(しぼう)が含(ふく)まれており、毎日(まいにち)少量(しょうりょう)を食(た)べることが推奨(すいしょう)されている。
Nuts and seeds such as almonds and walnuts contain high-quality fats, and eating a small amount daily is recommended.

A compound of 種(しゅ) (seed) and 実(じつ) (fruit/nut), used as a collective term for the broad category of edible seeds and nuts. Most commonly appears as 種実類(しゅじつるい) (the category of nuts and seeds) in nutrition and food science contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 種実(しゅじつ)類(るい): nuts and seeds (as a food category)
  • 種実(しゅじつ)バター: nut/seed butter
  • 種実(しゅじつ)アレルギー: nut/seed allergy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ナッツ: nuts (loanword, commonly used in casual speech)
  • 木(き)の実(み): tree nuts; nuts from trees
  • 種子(しゅし): seed (botanical term, more technical)

USAGE:
While ナッツ is more common in everyday conversation, 種実(しゅじつ)類(るい) is the standard term in nutritional guidelines, food labeling, and academic writing.
