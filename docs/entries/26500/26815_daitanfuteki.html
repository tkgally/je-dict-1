大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)

だいたんふてき
noun
bold and fearless; dauntless; audacious
1. bold and fearless; dauntless; audacious
describes someone whose actions show extreme boldness without any fear or hesitation, often in situations where others would be intimidated
彼(かれ)は大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)な男(おとこ)だ。
He is a bold and fearless man.
犯人(はんにん)は大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)にも堂々(どうどう)と正面(しょうめん)から入(はい)った。
The criminal brazenly entered right through the front.
記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)な笑(え)みを浮(う)かべて質問(しつもん)に答(こた)えた。
He answered questions at the press conference with a fearless smile.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) that can be used either admiringly (for courage) or critically (for recklessness or audacity). Context determines whether the speaker views the boldness positively or negatively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)な行動(こうどう): bold and fearless action
  • 大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)な態度(たいど): a dauntless attitude
  • 大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)にも: audaciously (adverbial use)
  • 大胆不敵(だいたんふてき)な笑(え)み: a fearless smile

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大胆(だいたん): bold — simpler form without the fearlessness component
  • 豪胆(ごうたん): brave, stout-hearted — emphasizes inner courage without the sense of defiance
  • 厚顔無恥(こうがんむち): brazen and shameless — purely negative, about lacking shame rather than having courage
  • 勇敢(ゆうかん): brave, courageous — straightforwardly positive

ETYMOLOGY:
大胆(だいたん) (bold, daring) + 不敵(ふてき) (fearless, defiant). 不敵(ふてき) literally means "without enemy" — so fearless that one acts as if no opponent exists.
