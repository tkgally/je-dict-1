1. to install; to set up (equipment or facilities)
To put physical equipment, devices, or facilities in place for use.
防犯(ぼうはん)カメラを設置(せっち)した。
I installed a security camera.
公園(こうえん)に新(あたら)しいベンチが設置(せっち)された。
New benches were installed in the park.
災害(さいがい)に備(そな)えて各階(かくかい)に消火器(しょうかき)を設置(せっち)することが義務(ぎむ)づけられている。
It is mandatory to install fire extinguishers on each floor in preparation for disasters.
2. to establish; to set up (an organization or committee)
To formally create or establish an organization, committee, or similar body.
委員会(いいんかい)を設置(せっち)する。
To establish a committee.
市(し)は新(あたら)しい相談窓口(そうだんまどぐち)を設置(せっち)した。
The city established a new consultation desk.
政府(せいふ)は問題(もんだい)を調査(ちょうさ)するための専門(せんもん)チームを設置(せっち)した。
The government set up a specialist team to investigate the problem.

Used for both physical installation (cameras, equipment, signs) and abstract establishment (committees, organizations, systems). The passive form 設置(せっち)される is very common in news and official announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 設置場所(せっちばしょ): installation location
  • 設置工事(せっちこうじ): installation work
  • 設置基準(せっちきじゅん): installation standards
  • 設置者(せっちしゃ): installer; establishing party
  • 常設(じょうせつ): permanently installed/established

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取(と)り付(つ)ける: to install; to attach — more hands-on, physical installation of smaller items
  • 設立(せつりつ)する: to establish; to found — used for organizations and companies, not physical objects
  • 置(お)く: to put; to place — general placement, not formal installation
