1. to be noisy; to be astir; to bustle
For a place or group of people to become noisy and restless with commotion or murmuring. Describes an atmosphere of agitation or excitement among people.
教室(きょうしつ)がざわついている。
The classroom is noisy.
突然(とつぜん)のニュースに会場(かいじょう)がざわついた。
The venue became noisy with commotion at the sudden news.
有名人(ゆうめいじん)が現(あらわ)れると、周囲(しゅうい)が一斉(いっせい)にざわつき始(はじ)めた。
When the celebrity appeared, the crowd all started buzzing at once.
2. to feel uneasy; to feel unsettled
For one's heart or mind to feel restless and anxious. Describes an inner sense of agitation or unease.
胸(むね)がざわつく。
I feel uneasy.
何(なに)か嫌(いや)な予感(よかん)がして、心(こころ)がざわつく。
I have a bad feeling, and my mind feels unsettled.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が発表(はっぴょう)される前(まえ)は、いつも気持(きも)ちがざわついて落(お)ち着(つ)かない。
Before exam results are announced, I always feel restless and can't settle down.

Related to the onomatopoeia ざわざわ, which describes the sound of rustling, murmuring, or general commotion. ざわつく is the verb form expressing the state of being noisy or unsettled.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会場(かいじょう)がざわつく: the venue becomes noisy
  • 教室(きょうしつ)がざわつく: the classroom gets noisy
  • 胸(むね)がざわつく: to feel a sense of unease
  • 心(こころ)がざわつく: to feel restless/unsettled
  • 気持(きも)ちがざわつく: to feel agitated

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ざわざわする: to rustle, be noisy — onomatopoeic form with similar meaning; more descriptive of the sound itself
  • 騒(さわ)がしい: noisy, boisterous — adjective describing a loud environment; stronger and more negative than ざわつく
  • そわそわする: to be fidgety, restless — focuses on nervous physical restlessness rather than noise or inner unease
