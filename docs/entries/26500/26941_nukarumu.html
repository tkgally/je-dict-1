1. to become muddy; to turn into a quagmire
Describes the ground becoming soft and muddy, typically from rain or water saturation. The ground loses firmness and becomes difficult to walk on.
道(みち)がぬかるんでいる。
The road is muddy.
雨(あめ)で庭(にわ)がぬかるんで歩(ある)きにくい。
The garden is muddy from the rain and hard to walk through.
長雨(ながあめ)が続(つづ)いて田(た)んぼの周(まわ)りの道(みち)がひどくぬかるんでしまった。
The paths around the rice paddies became terribly muddy after the prolonged rain.

Describes the state of ground that has absorbed water and become soft, squishy, and difficult to traverse. Almost always used about outdoor surfaces like roads, paths, fields, and gardens. Most commonly encountered in the ている form (ぬかるんでいる) to describe the resulting muddy state.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)がぬかるむ: the road becomes muddy
  • 地面(じめん)がぬかるむ: the ground becomes muddy
  • 足元(あしもと)がぬかるむ: the footing becomes muddy
  • ぬかるんだ道(みち): a muddy road

RELATED WORDS:

  • ぬかるみ: mud; muddy place (the noun form)
  • 泥(どろ): mud
  • 泥濘(ぬかるみ): muddy ground (literary/formal spelling of ぬかるみ)
