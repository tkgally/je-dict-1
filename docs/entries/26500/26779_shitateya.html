仕立屋(したてや)

したてや
noun
tailor; dressmaker
1. tailor; dressmaker; tailor's shop
A person or shop that makes or alters clothing to fit individual customers. Can refer to either the craftsperson or the establishment.
仕立屋(したてや)にスーツを頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered a suit from the tailor.
この町(まち)には腕(うで)のいい仕立屋(したてや)がある。
There's a skilled tailor in this town.
祖母(そぼ)は若(わか)い頃(ころ)、仕立屋(したてや)で着物(きもの)の仕立(した)てを習(なら)った。
My grandmother learned kimono tailoring at a tailor's shop when she was young.

A traditional occupation word formed from 仕立(した)て (tailoring, sewing) + 屋(や) (shop/person). While dedicated tailor shops have become less common in modern Japan, the word is still used and understood.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕立屋(したてや)に出(だ)す: to send to the tailor
  • 仕立屋(したてや)に頼(たの)む: to commission a tailor
  • 腕(うで)のいい仕立屋(したてや): a skilled tailor

RELATED TERMS:

  • 仕立(した)てる: to tailor; to sew — the verb form
  • オーダーメイド: custom-made — the modern loanword alternative
  • 洋裁(ようさい): Western dressmaking
  • 和裁(わさい): Japanese garment making
