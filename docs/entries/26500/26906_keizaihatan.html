経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)

けいざいはたん
noun
economic collapse; financial ruin; bankruptcy
1. economic collapse; financial ruin; bankruptcy
The breakdown or failure of an economy, financial system, or an entity's finances. Used for countries, local governments, companies, or individuals.
その国(くに)は経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)に陥(おちい)った。
That country fell into economic collapse.
経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)を防(ふせ)ぐための対策(たいさく)が急(いそ)がれている。
Measures to prevent economic collapse are urgently needed.
自治体(じちたい)の経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)は住民(じゅうみん)の生活(せいかつ)に深刻(しんこく)な影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
An economic collapse of a local government has a serious impact on residents' lives.

A compound of 経済(けいざい) (economy, finances) and 破綻(はたん) (collapse, breakdown). Used at multiple scales: national economies, municipal finances, corporate bankruptcy, and personal financial ruin.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)に陥(おちい)る: to fall into economic collapse
  • 経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)を招(まね)く: to bring about economic ruin
  • 経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)の危機(きき): the risk of economic collapse
  • 経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん)を回避(かいひ)する: to avert economic collapse

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 財政(ざいせい)破綻(はたん): fiscal collapse — more specifically about government finances
  • 倒産(とうさん): bankruptcy — specifically for companies going out of business
  • 破産(はさん): bankruptcy — legal bankruptcy for individuals or entities
  • 恐慌(きょうこう): financial panic, economic crisis — a sudden, dramatic downturn

USAGE:
Frequently appears in news coverage of sovereign debt crises, municipal bankruptcies, and financial meltdowns. In Japan, the 2007 bankruptcy of Yubari City in Hokkaido is a well-known example of municipal 経済(けいざい)破綻(はたん).
