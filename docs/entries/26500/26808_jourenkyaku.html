常連客(じょうれんきゃく)

じょうれんきゃく
noun
regular customer; regular patron
1. regular customer; regular patron
a customer who frequents a particular shop, restaurant, or establishment
あの人(ひと)はこの店(みせ)の常連客(じょうれんきゃく)だ。
That person is a regular at this shop.
常連客(じょうれんきゃく)には特別(とくべつ)な割引(わりびき)がある。
There are special discounts for regular customers.
店長(てんちょう)は常連客(じょうれんきゃく)の名前(なまえ)と好(す)みを全部(ぜんぶ)覚(おぼ)えている。
The shop owner remembers all the regular customers' names and preferences.

Composed of 常連(じょうれん) (regular, habitual visitor) + 客(きゃく) (customer). The shorter form 常連(じょうれん) alone is also very common and carries the same meaning in context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 常連客(じょうれんきゃく)になる: to become a regular
  • 常連客(じょうれんきゃく)向(む)けサービス: service for regulars
  • 常連客(じょうれんきゃく)を大切(たいせつ)にする: to value regular customers
  • 常連客(じょうれんきゃく)が多(おお)い店(みせ): a shop with many regulars

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 常連(じょうれん): regular — shorter form, same meaning in casual speech
  • リピーター: repeat customer — loanword, often used in marketing contexts
  • 得意客(とくいきゃく): valued customer — more formal, emphasizes the business relationship
  • 馴染(なじ)みの客(きゃく): familiar customer — warmer, emphasizes the personal connection

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese food and drink culture, being recognized as a 常連(じょうれん) at a bar, izakaya, or restaurant often comes with perks like personalized service, off-menu items, or a reserved seat.
