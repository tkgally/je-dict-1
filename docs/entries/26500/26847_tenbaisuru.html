1. to resell; to scalp; to flip
To buy something and sell it again, typically at a higher price for profit. In modern Japanese, carries a strongly negative connotation due to association with ticket scalping and hoarding of limited goods.
チケットを転売(てんばい)する。
To resell tickets.
限定品(げんていひん)を高値(たかね)で転売(てんばい)する人(ひと)がいる。
There are people who resell limited items at high prices.
転売(てんばい)目的(もくてき)での購入(こうにゅう)はご遠慮(えんりょ)ください。
Please refrain from purchasing for resale purposes.
人気(にんき)コンサートのチケットが定価(ていか)の何倍(なんばい)もの値段(ねだん)で転売(てんばい)されている。
Popular concert tickets are being resold at many times the original price.

Has become a hot social topic in Japan. 転売(てんばい)屋(や) (resellers/scalpers) are widely criticized for buying up concert tickets, limited-edition goods, and even daily necessities during shortages. Japan passed a law in 2019 specifically banning ticket resale (チケット不正転売禁止法(ちけっとふせいてんばいきんしほう)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転売(てんばい)屋(や): scalper, reseller
  • 転売(てんばい)禁止(きんし): resale prohibited
  • 転売(てんばい)目的(もくてき): for resale purposes
  • 高額(こうがく)転売(てんばい): selling at inflated prices
  • 不正(ふせい)転売(てんばい): illegal resale

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売(う)る: to sell — neutral, general selling
  • 横流(よこなが)しする: to divert goods — selling through unofficial channels
  • 中古(ちゅうこ)で売(う)る: to sell second-hand — selling used items, neutral connotation
