ビニールシート

びにーるしーと
noun
vinyl sheet; plastic tarp; ground sheet
1. vinyl sheet; plastic tarp; ground sheet
A large flat piece of vinyl or plastic material used as a ground cover, protective sheet, or tarp. Commonly used for picnics, outdoor events, construction sites, and disaster preparedness.
ビニールシートを敷(し)いて座(すわ)った。
We spread out a vinyl sheet and sat down.
花見(はなみ)のために大(おお)きなビニールシートを持(も)っていった。
I brought a large plastic sheet for the cherry blossom viewing.
台風(たいふう)に備(そな)えて、屋根(やね)をビニールシートで覆(おお)った。
In preparation for the typhoon, we covered the roof with a vinyl tarp.

A wasei-eigo (Japanese-English compound) from ビニール (vinyl) + シート (sheet). In English, the closest equivalents are "tarp," "ground sheet," or "plastic sheet" depending on the context. The blue vinyl tarps (ブルーシート) seen at construction sites and disaster areas are the most iconic type.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ビニールシートを敷(し)く: to spread out a vinyl sheet
  • ビニールシートで覆(おお)う: to cover with a vinyl sheet
  • ビニールシートを張(は)る: to stretch/put up a tarp
  • 防水(ぼうすい)ビニールシート: waterproof vinyl sheet

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, ビニールシート is closely associated with 花見(はなみ) (cherry blossom viewing) and other outdoor gatherings. People spread blue sheets under trees to claim spots in parks. At construction sites and after natural disasters, blue tarps (ブルーシート) are ubiquitous.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ブルーシート: blue tarp — the standard blue construction/emergency tarp
  • 敷(し)き物(もの): ground covering — more general term for anything laid on the ground
  • レジャーシート: leisure sheet — specifically for recreational outings
