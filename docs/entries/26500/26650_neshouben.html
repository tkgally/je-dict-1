1. bedwetting; wetting the bed; nocturnal enuresis
Involuntary urination during sleep, typically referring to children who have not yet outgrown the habit. Used in everyday conversation about child development as well as in medical contexts.
子(こ)どもが寝小便(ねしょうべん)をした。
The child wet the bed.
寝小便(ねしょうべん)は成長(せいちょう)とともに自然(しぜん)に治(なお)ることが多(おお)い。
Bedwetting often resolves naturally as the child grows.
小学校(しょうがっこう)に入(はい)ってもまだ寝小便(ねしょうべん)が続(つづ)く場合(ばあい)は、一度(いちど)小児科(しょうにか)に相談(そうだん)したほうがいい。
If bedwetting continues even after starting elementary school, you should consult a pediatrician.

Composed of 寝(ね) (sleeping) and 小便(しょうべん) (urine, urination). A colloquial term used in everyday conversation, particularly among parents discussing child development. The verb form is 寝小便(ねしょうべん)をする.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝小便(ねしょうべん)をする: to wet the bed
  • 寝小便(ねしょうべん)が治(なお)る: for bedwetting to stop
  • 寝小便(ねしょうべん)の癖(くせ): bedwetting habit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おねしょ: bedwetting — the more common, child-friendly word used in casual conversation with children
  • 夜尿症(やにょうしょう): nocturnal enuresis — the formal medical term used by doctors and in clinical settings

USAGE NOTE:
In casual speech with children, おねしょ is far more common. 寝小便(ねしょうべん) is used more among adults or in somewhat more direct, matter-of-fact contexts.
