1. to reason; to deduce; to infer
To use logic and available evidence to arrive at a conclusion. Commonly associated with detective work and mystery fiction, but also used in academic and everyday reasoning.
犯人(はんにん)を推理(すいり)する。
To deduce who the culprit is.
証拠(しょうこ)から事件(じけん)の経緯(けいい)を推理(すいり)した。
I deduced the course of events from the evidence.
読者(どくしゃ)は探偵(たんてい)と一緒(いっしょ)に推理(すいり)を楽(たの)しむことができる。
Readers can enjoy reasoning along with the detective.
限(かぎ)られた情報(じょうほう)から正(ただ)しく推理(すいり)するのは難(むずか)しい。
It is difficult to reason correctly from limited information.

Strongly associated with the mystery genre in Japan. 推理(すいり)小説(しょうせつ) (mystery novel) is a major literary genre, and the word appears frequently in discussions of detective fiction, crime dramas, and puzzle-solving games.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 推理(すいり)小説(しょうせつ): mystery novel; detective fiction
  • 推理(すいり)ゲーム: reasoning game; deduction game
  • 推理(すいり)力(りょく): reasoning ability
  • 論理的(ろんりてき)推理(すいり): logical reasoning
  • 推理(すいり)ドラマ: mystery drama

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推測(すいそく)する: to guess, to speculate — less rigorous, based on conjecture
  • 推論(すいろん)する: to infer — more academic/formal logical deduction
  • 考察(こうさつ)する: to examine, to consider — broader intellectual analysis
