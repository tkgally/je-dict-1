1. to bring close together; to press together
To physically bring two things close together so they touch or are side by side.
猫(ねこ)が体(からだ)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せてきた。
The cat pressed its body against me.
頬(ほお)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せて甘(あま)える子(こ)ども。
A child snuggling their cheek close and acting affectionately.
両手(りょうて)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せて暖(あたた)めた。
I rubbed my hands together to warm them.
2. to reconcile; to align; to bring into agreement
To bring differing opinions, positions, or numbers into alignment. Commonly used in business and negotiations to describe the process of reconciling differences.
意見(いけん)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reconcile our opinions.
双方(そうほう)の条件(じょうけん)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せて合意(ごうい)に達(たっ)した。
We reached an agreement by bringing both sides' conditions into alignment.
予算(よさん)と実績(じっせき)の数字(すうじ)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる作業(さぎょう)に時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to reconcile the budget and actual figures.

Combines 擦(す)る (to rub) and 寄(よ)せる (to bring near). The physical sense of rubbing or pressing close extends to a figurative meaning of reconciling or aligning differences. Also written with the kanji 摺(す)り寄(よ)せる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体(からだ)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる: to press one's body close
  • 意見(いけん)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる: to reconcile opinions
  • 数字(すうじ)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる: to reconcile figures
  • 立場(たちば)を擦(す)り寄(よ)せる: to bring positions into alignment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 擦(す)り寄(よ)る: to sidle up to — intransitive version; the subject moves close, rather than bringing things together
  • 歩(あゆ)み寄(よ)る: to compromise, to meet halfway — emphasizes mutual concession
  • すり合(あ)わせる: to coordinate, to compare and adjust — used for schedules, documents, and detailed alignment
