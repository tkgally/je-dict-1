手番(てばん)

てばん
noun
one's turn (in a game); move
1. one's turn (in a game); move
The turn to make a move in a board game or strategy game. Primarily used in shogi, go, chess, and similar turn-based games. Can also refer to the advantage of having the initiative or the right to move.
次(つぎ)はあなたの手番(てばん)です。
It's your turn next.
相手(あいて)の手番(てばん)の間(あいだ)に作戦(さくせん)を考(かんが)えた。
I thought about my strategy during my opponent's turn.
将棋(しょうぎ)では先手(せんて)の手番(てばん)は有利(ゆうり)だと言(い)われている。
In shogi, it is said that having the first turn is an advantage.

Composed of 手(て) (hand; move) and 番(ばん) (turn; order). Most commonly used in the context of 将棋(しょうぎ) (Japanese chess) and 囲碁(いご) (go), but also applies to any turn-based game including modern board games and video games.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手番(てばん)が来(く)る: for one's turn to come
  • 手番(てばん)を渡(わた)す: to pass one's turn
  • 先手(せんて)の手番(てばん): the first player's turn
  • 手番(てばん)を無駄(むだ)にする: to waste one's turn

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 番(ばん): turn — more general; used in everyday contexts (次(つぎ)は私(わたし)の番(ばん))
  • 順番(じゅんばん): order/sequence — refers to the ordering of turns rather than a single turn
  • 先手(せんて): first move/initiative — specifically the right to go first
