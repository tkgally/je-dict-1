1. to hit the mark; to strike the target; to hit
For a projectile, shot, or thrown object to reach and hit its intended target. Used for physical targets such as in shooting, archery, or combat.
弾(たま)が的(まと)に命中(めいちゅう)した。
The bullet hit the target.
矢(や)が三本(さんぼん)とも命中(めいちゅう)した。
All three arrows hit the mark.
ミサイルは目標(もくひょう)に正確(せいかく)に命中(めいちゅう)した。
The missile struck the target precisely.
2. to guess correctly; to hit the nail on the head
For a prediction, guess, or assessment to turn out to be exactly right. Used figuratively.
予想(よそう)が命中(めいちゅう)した。
My prediction was right on the mark.
彼女(かのじょ)の指摘(してき)は核心(かくしん)を命中(めいちゅう)していた。
Her observation hit right at the heart of the matter.
天気予報(てんきよほう)が見事(みごと)に命中(めいちゅう)して、午後(ごご)から本当(ほんとう)に雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り出(だ)した。
The weather forecast was spot on, and it really did start raining in the afternoon.

Composed of 命(めい) (life, fate, order) and 中(ちゅう) (hit, middle). Originally a military and martial arts term that has extended into everyday figurative use. The 命中(めいちゅう)率(りつ) (hit rate, accuracy rate) is commonly used in gaming and statistics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 的(まと)に命中(めいちゅう)する: to hit the target
  • 命中(めいちゅう)率(りつ): hit rate, accuracy
  • 予想(よそう)が命中(めいちゅう)する: a prediction proves correct
  • 正確(せいかく)に命中(めいちゅう)する: to hit accurately

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 当(あ)たる: to hit — everyday word; broader in meaning (lottery, guess, etc.)
  • 的中(てきちゅう)する: to hit the mark — very similar; more commonly used for predictions and guesses
  • 当(あ)てる: to hit (transitive) — casual; to aim and successfully hit something
