フルタイム

ふるたいむ
noun
full-time
1. full-time (work or employment)
Working the standard number of hours set by an employer, typically around 40 hours per week. Contrasts with part-time work.
フルタイムで働(はたら)いている。
I work full-time.
子育(こそだ)て中(ちゅう)はフルタイムの仕事(しごと)は難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to work a full-time job while raising children.
パートからフルタイムに切(き)り替(か)えたら、給料(きゅうりょう)も福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)も良(よ)くなった。
When I switched from part-time to full-time, both my salary and benefits improved.

A loanword from English "full-time." Used primarily in the context of employment to describe working standard hours, as opposed to パートタイム (part-time).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フルタイムで働(はたら)く: to work full-time
  • フルタイムの仕事(しごと): a full-time job
  • フルタイム勤務(きんむ): full-time employment
  • フルタイム社員(しゃいん): full-time employee
  • フルタイムの主婦(しゅふ): full-time housewife/homemaker

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 常勤(じょうきん): full-time (employment) — more formal Japanese equivalent used in job postings and contracts
  • パートタイム: part-time — the antonym, also a loanword
  • 非常勤(ひじょうきん): part-time, non-regular — formal antonym of 常勤(じょうきん)

USAGE:
フルタイムの主婦(しゅふ) ("full-time housewife") is a common expression emphasizing that homemaking is a serious, full-time occupation. In sports contexts, フルタイム can also mean the full duration of a match.
