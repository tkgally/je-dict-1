1. leaving a nursery school or kindergarten; withdrawal from a preschool
The act of a child being taken out of or graduating from a nursery school (保育園(ほいくえん)) or kindergarten (幼稚園(ようちえん)). Used for both voluntary withdrawal and planned departure.
来月(らいげつ)退園(たいえん)する予定(よてい)です。
We plan to withdraw from the school next month.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しのため退園届(たいえんとどけ)を出(だ)した。
I submitted a withdrawal notice because of a move.
退園(たいえん)の手続(てつづ)きは月末(げつまつ)までに済(す)ませてください。
Please complete the withdrawal procedures by the end of the month.

Combines 退(たい) (withdraw, leave) and 園(えん) (garden, institution — here referring to 保育園(ほいくえん) or 幼稚園(ようちえん)). This is the standard administrative term used in childcare and education paperwork.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 退園届(たいえんとどけ): withdrawal notification form
  • 退園(たいえん)手続(てつづ)き: withdrawal procedures
  • 途中(とちゅう)退園(たいえん): mid-year withdrawal
  • 退園(たいえん)理由(りゆう): reason for withdrawal

RELATED TERMS:

  • 入園(にゅうえん): enrollment in a kindergarten or nursery — the opposite action
  • 退学(たいがく): leaving school — used for elementary school and above
  • 卒園(そつえん): graduation from kindergarten — a planned, positive departure at the end of the program
