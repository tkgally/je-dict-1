1. to instruct; to direct; to give directions
To tell someone what to do in a work or organizational context. Implies a hierarchical relationship but is more neutral than 命令(めいれい)する.
上司(じょうし)が作業(さぎょう)内容(ないよう)を指示(しじ)する。
The boss instructs on the work to be done.
医者(いしゃ)に指示(しじ)されたとおりに薬(くすり)を飲(の)む。
I take medicine as directed by the doctor.
係員(かかりいん)の指示(しじ)に従(したが)ってください。
Please follow the directions of the staff.
2. to indicate; to point out; to designate
To point to or identify something specific, such as indicating a direction or designating a particular item.
矢印(やじるし)が出口(でぐち)の方向(ほうこう)を指示(しじ)している。
The arrow indicates the direction of the exit.
地図(ちず)で場所(ばしょ)を指示(しじ)して説明(せつめい)した。
I explained by indicating the location on the map.
先生(せんせい)は黒板(こくばん)の図(ず)を指示(しじ)しながら説明(せつめい)した。
The teacher explained while pointing to the diagram on the blackboard.

In the workplace, 指示(しじ) is the standard neutral term for giving directions to subordinates — much softer than 命令(めいれい) (command). It's the everyday word for workplace instructions and is not perceived as harsh.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指示(しじ)を出(だ)す: to issue instructions
  • 指示(しじ)に従(したが)う: to follow instructions
  • 指示(しじ)を仰(あお)ぐ: to ask for instructions
  • 指示(しじ)待(ま)ち: waiting for instructions
  • 的確(てきかく)な指示(しじ): precise directions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 命令(めいれい)する: to command — stronger, implies authority and compulsion
  • 指導(しどう)する: to guide — includes teaching and nurturing, not just telling
  • 指図(さしず)する: to boss around — often perceived as unwelcome ordering
