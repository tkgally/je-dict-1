箱(はこ)ティッシュ

はこてぃっしゅ
noun
box tissues; tissue box
1. box tissues; tissue box; boxed facial tissues
Facial tissues sold in a rectangular box, as opposed to pocket tissues. A ubiquitous household item in Japan found in virtually every home, office, and car.
箱(はこ)ティッシュが切(き)れた。
We ran out of box tissues.
花粉(かふん)の季節(きせつ)は箱(はこ)ティッシュの消費(しょうひ)が増(ふ)える。
Tissue box consumption increases during pollen season.
ドラッグストアで五箱(ごはこ)パックの箱(はこ)ティッシュを安売(やすう)りしていたので、まとめ買(が)いした。
The drugstore had five-box packs of tissues on sale, so I bought in bulk.

A compound of 箱(はこ) (box) and ティッシュ (tissue, from English). In Japan, box tissues are typically sold in multi-packs of five and are a staple household purchase. Distinguished from ポケットティッシュ (pocket tissues), which are commonly handed out for free on streets as advertising.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 箱(はこ)ティッシュ五箱(ごはこ)パック: five-box tissue pack
  • 箱(はこ)ティッシュカバー: tissue box cover
  • 箱(はこ)ティッシュを買(か)う: to buy box tissues

RELATED TERMS:

  • ポケットティッシュ: pocket tissues — small packs given out free in Japan as advertising
  • ティッシュペーパー: tissue paper — the generic term for facial tissue
  • ウェットティッシュ: wet tissues / wet wipes
