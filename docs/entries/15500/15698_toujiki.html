陶磁器(とうじき)

とうじき
noun
ceramics; pottery and porcelain
1. ceramics; pottery and porcelain
A general term encompassing both pottery (earthenware/stoneware) and porcelain. Refers to objects made from clay that are shaped and fired in a kiln.
日本(にほん)の陶磁器(とうじき)は世界的(せかいてき)に有名(ゆうめい)だ。
Japanese ceramics are famous worldwide.
美術館(びじゅつかん)で古(ふる)い陶磁器(とうじき)の展示(てんじ)を見(み)た。
I saw an exhibition of old ceramics at the art museum.
有田焼(ありたやき)は日本(にほん)を代表(だいひょう)する陶磁器(とうじき)の一(ひと)つだ。
Arita ware is one of the most representative ceramics of Japan.

USAGE:
A comprehensive term covering both 陶器(とうき) (pottery, earthenware) and 磁器(じき) (porcelain). Japan has a long tradition of ceramic arts, with famous regional styles such as 有田焼(ありたやき), 瀬戸焼(せとやき), and 益子焼(ましこやき).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 陶磁器(とうじき)の産地(さんち) (ceramics production area)
  • 陶磁器(とうじき)市(いち) (ceramics market/fair)
  • 陶磁器(とうじき)美術館(びじゅつかん) (ceramics museum)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 陶器(とうき): pottery, earthenware (lower firing temperature)
  • 磁器(じき): porcelain (higher firing temperature, translucent)
  • 焼(や)き物(もの): pottery, ceramics (more casual term)
