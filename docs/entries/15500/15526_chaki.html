茶器(ちゃき)

ちゃき
noun
tea utensils; tea implements
1. tea utensils; tea implements; tea ware
Tools and vessels used for preparing and serving tea, especially in the context of the Japanese tea ceremony. Includes items such as tea bowls, tea caddies, and whisks.
茶器(ちゃき)を丁寧(ていねい)に扱(あつか)う。
Handle the tea utensils carefully.
この茶器(ちゃき)は江戸(えど)時代(じだい)のものだ。
This tea utensil is from the Edo period.
美術館(びじゅつかん)で名品(めいひん)の茶器(ちゃき)が展示(てんじ)されており、茶道(さどう)愛好家(あいこうか)に人気(にんき)がある。
Masterpiece tea utensils are on display at the museum, and they are popular with tea ceremony enthusiasts.

USAGE:
茶器(ちゃき) can refer to any implements used for tea, but in Japanese it most often evokes the tea ceremony (茶道(さどう)). Historically, prized 茶器(ちゃき) were symbols of wealth and power among samurai and feudal lords.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 茶器(ちゃき)を揃(そろ)える (to assemble a set of tea utensils)
  • 名品(めいひん)の茶器(ちゃき) (masterpiece tea ware)
  • 茶器(ちゃき)一式(いっしき) (a full set of tea utensils)

CULTURE:
During the Sengoku and Azuchi-Momoyama periods, famous 茶器(ちゃき) were valued as highly as land holdings and were exchanged as political gifts between warlords.
