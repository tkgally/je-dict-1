真(ま)っ先(さき)に

まっさきに
adverb
first of all, before anything else
1. first of all; before anything else; first and foremost
Indicates doing something before anyone or anything else, often implying urgency or strong priority.
真(ま)っ先(さき)に手(て)を挙(あ)げた。
I raised my hand first.
地震(じしん)のとき、真(ま)っ先(さき)に家族(かぞく)の安否(あんぴ)を確認(かくにん)した。
During the earthquake, the first thing I did was check on my family's safety.
ニュースを聞(き)いて、真(ま)っ先(さき)に思(おも)い浮(う)かんだのは彼(かれ)の顔(かお)だった。
When I heard the news, the first face that came to mind was his.

USAGE: 真(ま)っ先(さき)に emphasizes being the very first, often with a sense of eagerness or urgency. It describes both physical actions (running first, raising one's hand first) and mental reactions (thinking of something first).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真(ま)っ先(さき)に駆(か)けつける: to rush over first
  • 真(ま)っ先(さき)に思(おも)い浮(う)かぶ: to come to mind first
  • 真(ま)っ先(さき)に手(て)を挙(あ)げる: to raise one's hand first
  • 真(ま)っ先(さき)に逃(に)げる: to flee first

ETYMOLOGY: Intensified form of 先(さき)に (before, ahead). The 真(ま)っ prefix adds emphasis, meaning "the very first."
