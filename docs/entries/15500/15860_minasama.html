皆様(みなさま)

みなさま
noun
everyone; ladies and gentlemen
See also: 皆(みな)さん (standard polite form)
1. everyone; ladies and gentlemen
A polite way of addressing or referring to a group of people. More formal than みなさん, commonly used in announcements, speeches, and formal writing.
皆様(みなさま)、おはようございます。
Good morning, everyone.
皆様(みなさま)のご協力(きょうりょく)に感謝(かんしゃ)いたします。
I am grateful for everyone's cooperation.
皆様(みなさま)のご健康(けんこう)とご多幸(たこう)をお祈(いの)りいたします。
I wish everyone good health and happiness.

皆様(みなさま) is the honorific form of みなさん, using 様(さま) instead of さん.

FORMALITY LEVELS:

  • みんな: everyone (casual)
  • みなさん: everyone (standard polite)
  • 皆様(みなさま): everyone (formal/honorific)

WHERE USED:
Standard in business emails, public announcements, formal speeches, and customer-facing communication. Using みなさん in these contexts would sound too casual.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 皆様(みなさま)へ: to everyone (letter/email opening)
  • 皆様(みなさま)のおかげで: thanks to everyone
  • お客様(きゃくさま)皆様(みなさま): all of our customers
  • ご来場(らいじょう)の皆様(みなさま): all attendees
  • 皆様(みなさま)のご健勝(けんしょう)をお祈(いの)りいたします: I wish everyone good health (closing formula)
