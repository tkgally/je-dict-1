気忙(きぜわ)しい

きぜわしい
i-adjective
restless; fidgety; bustling; flustered
1. restless; fidgety; bustling; unable to settle down
Describes a state of being unable to relax, constantly feeling rushed or agitated. Can describe either a person's temperament or a hectic atmosphere.
年末(ねんまつ)は気忙(きぜわ)しい。
The end of the year is hectic.
彼(かれ)はいつも気忙(きぜわ)しく動(うご)き回(まわ)っている。
He is always bustling about restlessly.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)で気忙(きぜわ)しい毎日(まいにち)を送(おく)っている。
I'm spending hectic days preparing for the move.

USAGE:
気忙(きぜわ)しい describes an internal feeling of restlessness rather than simply being busy. A person who is 気忙(きぜわ)しい may not actually have a lot to do, but feels rushed and unsettled.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気忙(きぜわ)しい毎日(まいにち) (hectic days)
  • 気忙(きぜわ)しく走(はし)り回(まわ)る (to run around restlessly)
  • 気忙(きぜわ)しい性格(せいかく) (a restless personality)

SIMILAR WORDS:
忙(いそが)しい simply means "busy" with tasks, while 気忙(きぜわ)しい emphasizes the psychological state of being unable to calm down. 慌(あわ)ただしい describes a situation that is hurried and chaotic.
