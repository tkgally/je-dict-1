忌避(きひ)

きひ
noun
avoidance; aversion; recusal
1. avoidance; shunning; aversion
The act of avoiding or shunning something one dislikes or finds distasteful. Used for both physical avoidance and psychological aversion.
虫(むし)を忌避(きひ)する人(ひと)は多(おお)い。
Many people have an aversion to insects.
若者(わかもの)の政治(せいじ)忌避(きひ)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
Young people's avoidance of politics has become a problem.
この忌避(きひ)スプレーは蚊(か)に効果(こうか)がある。
This repellent spray is effective against mosquitoes.
2. recusal; challenge (legal)
In legal contexts, the process of disqualifying or requesting the removal of a judge or official due to bias or conflict of interest.
弁護士(べんごし)が裁判官(さいばんかん)の忌避(きひ)を申(もう)し立(た)てた。
The lawyer filed a motion for recusal of the judge.
利害(りがい)関係(かんけい)がある場合(ばあい)、委員(いいん)は忌避(きひ)の対象(たいしょう)となる。
If there is a conflict of interest, the committee member is subject to recusal.
忌避(きひ)申(もう)し立(た)てが認(みと)められ、別(べつ)の裁判官(さいばんかん)が担当(たんとう)することになった。
The recusal request was granted, and a different judge was assigned to the case.

USAGE:
忌避(きひ) is a formal word used as both a noun and a suru-verb (忌避(きひ)する). In everyday contexts, 避(さ)ける (to avoid) is more common. Sense 1 often appears in compound forms like 忌避(きひ)剤(ざい) (repellent).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 忌避(きひ)する (to avoid, to shun)
  • 忌避(きひ)剤(ざい) (repellent — for insects, etc.)
  • 忌避(きひ)反応(はんのう) (aversion response)
  • 忌避(きひ)申(もう)し立(た)て (motion for recusal)
  • 忌避(きひ)の対象(たいしょう) (subject of recusal)

SIMILAR WORDS:
回避(かいひ) means "avoidance" in the sense of steering clear of danger or risk, without the connotation of dislike. 敬遠(けいえん) means "keeping at a respectful distance" — avoiding while maintaining politeness.
