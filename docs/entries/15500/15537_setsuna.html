刹那(せつな)

せつな
noun
moment; instant; split second
1. moment; instant; split second
An extremely brief moment in time. Originally a Buddhist term for the shortest possible unit of time. Used in literary and dramatic contexts to describe a fleeting instant, often at a critical or climactic point.
その刹那(せつな)、稲妻(いなずま)が走(はし)った。
At that very instant, lightning flashed.
目(め)が合(あ)った刹那(せつな)、心臓(しんぞう)が高鳴(たかな)った。
The moment our eyes met, my heart began to race.
爆発(ばくはつ)の刹那(せつな)、彼(かれ)は地面(じめん)に伏(ふ)せて難(なん)を逃(のが)れた。
At the instant of the explosion, he threw himself to the ground and escaped harm.

USAGE:
刹那(せつな) is literary and dramatic. It typically appears in the pattern その刹那(せつな) (at that very instant) or ～の刹那(せつな) (at the moment of ~). Not used in everyday casual conversation — 瞬間(しゅんかん) is the neutral equivalent.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • その刹那(せつな) (at that very instant)
  • ～した刹那(せつな) (the moment ~ happened)
  • 一(いっ)瞬(しゅん)の刹那(せつな) (a fleeting instant)

SIMILAR WORDS:
瞬間(しゅんかん) (moment/instant) is the standard, neutral word for a brief moment. 刹那(せつな) is more literary and carries a sense of dramatic tension. 刹那(せつな)的(てき) (momentary, living for the moment) is a common derivative.
