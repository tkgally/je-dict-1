騒々(そうぞう)しい

そうぞうしい
adjective
noisy; boisterous; clamorous
1. noisy; boisterous; clamorous
Describes an environment or atmosphere that is loud, bustling, and lacking in calm. Carries a slightly more literary or formal feel than うるさい.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)が騒々(そうぞう)しい。
The room next door is noisy.
騒々(そうぞう)しい街(まち)を離(はな)れて、静(しず)かな田舎(いなか)で暮(く)らしたい。
I want to leave the noisy city and live in the quiet countryside.
選挙(せんきょ)が近(ちか)づくと政治(せいじ)の世界(せかい)が騒々(そうぞう)しくなる。
As elections approach, the political world becomes clamorous.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 騒々(そうぞう)しい: noisy, clamorous (describes an atmosphere or environment; slightly literary)
  • うるさい: noisy, annoying (more colloquial; often directed at a specific sound source or person)
  • やかましい: noisy, loud (strong annoyance; can also mean "strict")

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 騒々(そうぞう)しい雰囲気(ふんいき) (a noisy atmosphere)
  • 騒々(そうぞう)しい世(よ)の中(なか) (a turbulent world)
