平然(へいぜん)と

へいぜんと
adverb
calmly; composedly; nonchalantly
1. calmly; composedly; nonchalantly; unflinchingly
Maintaining a calm, unperturbed demeanor in a situation where most people would be flustered, embarrassed, or upset. Often implies a surprising or even brazen level of composure.
彼女(かのじょ)は平然(へいぜん)と答(こた)えた。
She answered calmly.
批判(ひはん)されても平然(へいぜん)としている。
He remains unfazed even when criticized.
あれだけの失敗(しっぱい)をしておいて平然(へいぜん)と出勤(しゅっきん)してくるとは驚(おどろ)きだ。
I'm amazed that he shows up to work so nonchalantly after making such a big mistake.

USAGE:
Used both as an adverb (平然(へいぜん)と) and in the pattern 平然(へいぜん)としている. Often carries a nuance of surprise or admiration — or disapproval — at someone's composure in a situation where emotion would be expected.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 平然(へいぜん)としている (to remain unfazed)
  • 平然(へいぜん)と〜する (to do something calmly/nonchalantly)
  • 平然(へいぜん)とした態度(たいど) (a calm/composed attitude)
