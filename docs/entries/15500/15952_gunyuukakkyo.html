群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)

ぐんゆうかっきょ
noun
rivalry of local warlords; many rivals competing for dominance
1. rivalry of local warlords; many rivals competing for dominance
A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) literally meaning "rival heroes occupying separate territories." Originally described the Warring States period when many feudal lords held their own domains. Now also used figuratively to describe any competitive landscape with many strong contenders.
戦国時代(せんごくじだい)は群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)の時代(じだい)だった。
The Warring States period was an era of rival warlords competing for dominance.
スマートフォン市場(しじょう)は群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)の状態(じょうたい)だ。
The smartphone market is in a state of fierce competition among many rivals.
かつては群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)だった業界(ぎょうかい)も、今(いま)では大手(おおて)数社(すうしゃ)に集約(しゅうやく)されている。
The industry that was once a free-for-all of competing rivals has now consolidated into a few major companies.

USAGE:
A 四字熟語(よじじゅくご) (four-character compound) used both as a noun and as a する verb (群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)する). The literal historical sense describes the Warring States period, but it is frequently used in modern business and sports contexts to describe competitive fields.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)の時代(じだい) (an era of rival powers)
  • 群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)の状態(じょうたい) (a state of multiple rivals competing)
  • 群雄割拠(ぐんゆうかっきょ)する (to compete as rival powers)

ETYMOLOGY:
群雄(ぐんゆう) (many heroes/warlords) + 割拠(かっきょ) (each occupying their own territory).
