1. to thank someone for their effort; to show appreciation for hard work; to reward with kind words
To express gratitude or appreciation for someone's labor, effort, or service. Typically directed from an equal or superior toward someone who has worked hard.
部下(ぶか)の労(ろう)を労(ねぎら)う。
To thank one's subordinates for their hard work.
長年(ながねん)の功績(こうせき)を労(ねぎら)って花束(はなたば)を贈(おく)った。
We presented flowers in appreciation of their long years of service.
大会(たいかい)の後(あと)、監督(かんとく)は選手(せんしゅ)たちの頑張(がんば)りを労(ねぎら)う言葉(ことば)をかけた。
After the tournament, the coach spoke words of appreciation for the players' hard work.

USAGE:
労(ねぎら)う is generally used by someone of equal or higher status to acknowledge the efforts of others. It is considered rude to use it toward a social superior. The noun form 労(ねぎら)い means "appreciation" or "words of thanks."

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (u-verb). Transitive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 労(ろう)を労(ねぎら)う (to thank for someone's hard work)
  • 労(ねぎら)いの言葉(ことば) (words of appreciation)
  • 互(たが)いを労(ねぎら)う (to appreciate each other's efforts)
