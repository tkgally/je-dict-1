忍耐強(にんたいづよ)い

にんたいづよい
i-adjective
patient; persevering; tenacious
1. patient; persevering; tenacious
Describes someone who endures hardship or frustration without giving up. Emphasizes inner strength and the ability to keep going over a long period.
彼(かれ)は忍耐強(にんたいづよ)い人(ひと)だ。
He is a patient person.
忍耐強(にんたいづよ)く待(ま)っていれば、きっとチャンスが来(く)る。
If you wait patiently, an opportunity will surely come.
先生(せんせい)は忍耐強(にんたいづよ)く生徒(せいと)の質問(しつもん)に答(こた)え続(つづ)けた。
The teacher patiently kept answering the students' questions.

USAGE:
忍耐強(にんたいづよ)い combines 忍耐(にんたい) (endurance, patience) with 強(つよ)い (strong). It describes a deeper, more sustained patience than simply 我慢強(がまんづよ)い.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 忍耐強(にんたいづよ)く待(ま)つ (to wait patiently)
  • 忍耐強(にんたいづよ)い性格(せいかく) (patient personality)
  • 忍耐強(にんたいづよ)く取(と)り組(く)む (to work on something perseveringly)

SIMILAR WORDS:
我慢強(がまんづよ)い focuses on tolerating discomfort or annoyance, while 忍耐強(にんたいづよ)い emphasizes long-term endurance and persistence toward a goal.
