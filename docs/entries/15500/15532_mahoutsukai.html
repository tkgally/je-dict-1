魔法使(まほうつか)い

まほうつかい
noun
wizard; sorcerer; magician
1. wizard; sorcerer; magician
A person who uses magic. Commonly appears in fairy tales, fantasy stories, and games. Composed of 魔法 (magic) + 使い (user). Can refer to both male and female magic users.
魔法使(まほうつか)いが呪文(じゅもん)を唱(とな)えた。
The wizard chanted a spell.
この物語(ものがたり)の主人公(しゅじんこう)は若(わか)い魔法使(まほうつか)いだ。
The protagonist of this story is a young wizard.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、魔法使(まほうつか)いになりたいと思(おも)っていた。
When I was a child, I wanted to become a wizard.

USAGE:
魔法使(まほうつか)い is the most common and general word for a magic user in Japanese. It is gender-neutral and appears frequently in children's stories, fantasy literature, anime, and video games.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魔法使(まほうつか)いの弟子(でし) (wizard's apprentice)
  • 魔法使(まほうつか)いの杖(つえ) (wizard's staff/wand)
  • 魔法使(まほうつか)いになる (to become a wizard)

SIMILAR WORDS:
魔女(まじょ) specifically means 'witch' (female), while 魔法使(まほうつか)い is gender-neutral. 魔術師(まじゅつし) sounds more formal and literary.
