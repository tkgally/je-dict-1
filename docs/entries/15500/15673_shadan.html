遮断(しゃだん)

しゃだん
noun
cutoff; interruption; blocking
1. cutoff; interruption; blocking; shutting off
The act of cutting off or blocking the flow or passage of something, such as electricity, sound, light, or access.
電源(でんげん)を遮断(しゃだん)する。
To cut off the power supply.
踏切(ふみきり)の遮断機(しゃだんき)が下(お)りた。
The railroad crossing gate came down.
外部(がいぶ)からの情報(じょうほう)を完全(かんぜん)に遮断(しゃだん)された状態(じょうたい)で過(す)ごした。
I spent the time in a state completely cut off from outside information.

USAGE:
Used in both technical and everyday contexts. Can describe physical blocking (a gate, wall) or abstract blocking (information, contact).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電源(でんげん)を遮断(しゃだん)する (to cut off power)
  • 遮断機(しゃだんき) (crossing gate; circuit breaker)
  • 遮断(しゃだん)器(き) (circuit breaker)
  • 音(おと)を遮断(しゃだん)する (to block out sound)
  • 情報(じょうほう)を遮断(しゃだん)する (to block information)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遮(さえぎ)る: to block, to intercept (verb; broader usage)
  • 断絶(だんぜつ): severance, breaking off (more permanent, often for relationships)
