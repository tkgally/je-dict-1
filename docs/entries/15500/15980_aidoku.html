愛読(あいどく)

あいどく
noun
enjoying reading; favorite reading
1. reading with pleasure; being a devoted reader of
The act of regularly and enthusiastically reading a particular book, author, or publication. Implies genuine enjoyment and repeated engagement rather than reading out of obligation.
この雑誌(ざっし)を愛読(あいどく)している。
I'm a devoted reader of this magazine.
彼(かれ)は子供(こども)の頃(ころ)からその作家(さっか)の本(ほん)を愛読(あいどく)してきた。
He has been an avid reader of that author's books since childhood.
祖母(そぼ)が愛読(あいどく)していた小説(しょうせつ)を本棚(ほんだな)で見(み)つけた。
I found the novel that my grandmother used to love reading on the bookshelf.

USAGE:
Most commonly used with する as 愛読(あいどく)する. The object is typically a specific book, author, magazine, or newspaper that one reads regularly and with enjoyment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 愛読(あいどく)する (to read with pleasure, to be a devoted reader of)
  • 愛読(あいどく)書(しょ) (favorite book)
  • 愛読(あいどく)者(しゃ) (devoted reader, regular reader)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 読書(どくしょ): reading in general, without the nuance of special fondness
  • 熟読(じゅくどく): reading carefully and thoroughly, focusing on comprehension rather than enjoyment
