兼用(けんよう)

けんよう
noun
dual use; multipurpose; serving double duty
1. dual use; serving two purposes; multipurpose
Using one thing for two or more purposes, or something designed to serve multiple functions. Composed of 兼 (to combine/double as) + 用 (use). Can be used as a noun or a suru-verb.
このソファはベッド兼用(けんよう)だ。
This sofa also serves as a bed.
晴雨(せいう)兼用(けんよう)の傘(かさ)を買(か)った。
I bought an umbrella that works for both rain and sun.
この部屋(へや)は書斎(しょさい)と寝室(しんしつ)を兼用(けんよう)しているので、少(すこ)し狭(せま)い。
This room serves as both a study and a bedroom, so it's a bit cramped.

USAGE:
兼用(けんよう) can be used as a noun, a no-adjective, or a suru-verb. As a noun modifier, it often appears with の: 兼用(けんよう)の部屋(へや). It is also commonly used in compound forms with a preceding word describing what is being combined: 晴雨(せいう)兼用(けんよう) (for rain and shine), 男女(だんじょ)兼用(けんよう) (unisex).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 晴雨(せいう)兼用(けんよう) (for sun and rain)
  • 男女(だんじょ)兼用(けんよう) (unisex)
  • ベッド兼用(けんよう) (doubling as a bed)
  • AとBを兼用(けんよう)する (use something for both A and B)

SIMILAR WORDS:
併用(へいよう) means "using two things together" simultaneously (e.g., two medications). 兼用(けんよう) means one thing serving two purposes. 多目的(たもくてき) means "multipurpose" and is used for facilities designed for varied uses.
