容積(ようせき)

ようせき
noun
capacity; volume; cubic content
1. capacity; volume; cubic content
The amount of space inside a container or enclosed area, measured in cubic units. Used in technical, scientific, and everyday contexts to describe how much a container can hold.
この箱(はこ)の容積(ようせき)を計算(けいさん)する。
I'll calculate the volume of this box.
スーツケースの容積(ようせき)は約(やく)80リットルだ。
The capacity of the suitcase is about 80 liters.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)を買(か)うときは容積(ようせき)だけでなく消費(しょうひ)電力(でんりょく)も確認(かくにん)したほうがいい。
When buying a refrigerator, it's a good idea to check not only the capacity but also the power consumption.

USAGE:
容積(ようせき) refers specifically to the internal volume of a container or enclosed space. It is commonly expressed in liters or cubic meters. The word is used both in everyday product specifications and in technical or scientific writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 容積(ようせき)を測(はか)る (measure the volume)
  • 容積(ようせき)を計算(けいさん)する (calculate the volume)
  • 容積(ようせき)率(りつ) (floor area ratio, in urban planning)
  • 総(そう)容積(ようせき) (total capacity)

SIMILAR WORDS:
体積(たいせき) is the volume of a solid object itself, while 容積(ようせき) is the volume of the space inside a container. 容量(ようりょう) also means capacity but is used more broadly for data storage, battery capacity, and liquid amounts.

Related Words

Contrast: 体積(たいせき) (volume of solid object)
Synonym: 容量(ようりょう) (capacity; broader)
Related: 面積(めんせき) (area (2D))
