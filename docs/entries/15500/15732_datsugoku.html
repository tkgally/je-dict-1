1. prison break; jailbreak; escape from prison
The act of escaping from a prison or jail. Used in news reports, crime fiction, and historical accounts.
囚人(しゅうじん)が脱獄(だつごく)した。
A prisoner escaped from jail.
脱獄(だつごく)犯(はん)はまだ捕(つか)まっていない。
The escaped prisoner has not been caught yet.
その映画(えいが)は実際(じっさい)にあった脱獄(だつごく)事件(じけん)を基(もと)にしている。
The movie is based on a real prison break incident.

脱獄(だつごく) means escaping from prison. Composed of 脱(だつ) (escape) + 獄(ごく) (prison).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脱獄(だつごく)する: to break out of prison
  • 脱獄(だつごく)犯(はん): escaped prisoner
  • 脱獄(だつごく)事件(じけん): prison break incident
  • 脱獄(だつごく)を企(くわだ)てる: to plan a prison break
  • 脱獄(だつごく)を図(はか)る: to attempt a prison escape
  • 脱獄(だつごく)未遂(みすい): attempted jailbreak

MODERN USAGE:
Also used informally in computing to mean "jailbreaking" a device (removing software restrictions on a phone or tablet).

RELATED:

  • 脱走(だっそう): escape, desertion (broader — from military, institution, etc.)
  • 逃亡(とうぼう): flight, running away (after escaping)
  • 投獄(とうごく): imprisonment (opposite — putting someone in prison)
