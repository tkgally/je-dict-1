報(むく)い

むくい
noun
reward; retribution; consequence
1. reward; recompense
Something received in return for good deeds or effort. A positive outcome that comes as a result of one's actions.
努力(どりょく)の報(むく)いだ。
It's the reward for hard work.
長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)がようやく報(むく)いを受(う)けた。
Years of effort finally received their reward.
あなたの親切(しんせつ)にはいつか必(かなら)ず報(むく)いがあるでしょう。
Your kindness will surely be rewarded someday.
2. retribution; punishment; consequence
Negative consequences that come as a result of bad deeds or wrongdoing. Often carries a sense of karmic justice.
これは悪事(あくじ)の報(むく)いだ。
This is retribution for wrongdoing.
嘘(うそ)をついた報(むく)いを受(う)けた。
He suffered the consequences of lying.
人(ひと)を裏切(うらぎ)った報(むく)いは必(かなら)ず自分(じぶん)に返(かえ)ってくる。
The consequences of betraying others will always come back to you.

USAGE:
Can refer to both positive rewards and negative consequences. Sense 2 is particularly common and carries a strong nuance of karmic justice or inevitable punishment. Often used in moral or philosophical contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 報(むく)いを受(う)ける (to receive one's reward/punishment)
  • 努力(どりょく)の報(むく)い (reward for effort)
  • 悪事(あくじ)の報(むく)い (retribution for wrongdoing)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 報酬(ほうしゅう): remuneration, compensation (more concrete, financial)
  • 罰(ばつ): punishment (direct and imposed, not karmic)
  • 因果応報(いんがおうほう): karmic retribution (four-character idiom)
