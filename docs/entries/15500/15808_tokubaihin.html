特売品(とくばいひん)

とくばいひん
noun
bargain item; sale item
1. bargain item; sale item; special-offer product
A product being sold at a reduced price during a special sale or promotion. Composed of 特売(とくばい) (special sale) + 品(ひん) (item).
特売品(とくばいひん)を買(か)いに行(い)く。
I'm going to buy sale items.
スーパーの特売品(とくばいひん)はすぐに売(う)り切(き)れる。
The sale items at the supermarket sell out quickly.
チラシに載(の)っている特売品(とくばいひん)を目当(めあ)てに朝(あさ)早(はや)くから並(なら)んでいる人(ひと)がいた。
There were people lining up from early morning, hoping to get the sale items advertised in the flyer.

USAGE:
特売品(とくばいひん) is commonly seen in supermarkets, department stores, and electronics shops. Items are often advertised in チラシ (flyers) or on 店頭(てんとう) (in-store) displays. The compound breaks down as 特売(とくばい) (special sale) + 品(ひん) (item/goods).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 特売品(とくばいひん)コーナー (bargain section)
  • 特売品(とくばいひん)を狙(ねら)う (go after sale items)
  • 本日(ほんじつ)の特売品(とくばいひん) (today's specials)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 特売(とくばい) is the sale event itself.
  • 目玉商品(めだましょうひん) refers to a featured sale item used as a draw.
  • 割引(わりびき) means discount.
