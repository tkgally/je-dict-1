独占的(どくせんてき)

どくせんてき
na-adjective
monopolistic; exclusive; dominant
1. monopolistic; exclusive; dominant
Describes a situation where one entity controls something exclusively, shutting out others. Often used in business and economic contexts.
独占的(どくせんてき)な地位(ちい)を築(きず)く。
To establish a monopolistic position.
その企業(きぎょう)は市場(しじょう)で独占的(どくせんてき)な力(ちから)を持(も)っている。
That company holds monopolistic power in the market.
独占的(どくせんてき)な取引(とりひき)は法律(ほうりつ)で規制(きせい)されている。
Monopolistic trading practices are regulated by law.

USAGE:
独占的(どくせんてき) is formed from 独占(どくせん) (monopoly) + 的(てき) (adjective-forming suffix). It is commonly used in economic, legal, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独占的(どくせんてき)地位(ちい) (monopolistic position)
  • 独占的(どくせんてき)な支配(しはい) (exclusive control)
  • 独占的(どくせんてき)な権利(けんり) (exclusive rights)

SIMILAR WORDS:
排他的(はいたてき) (exclusive, exclusionary) focuses on shutting others out, while 独占的(どくせんてき) focuses on one party having sole control. 寡占的(かせんてき) describes an oligopoly where a few entities share dominance.
