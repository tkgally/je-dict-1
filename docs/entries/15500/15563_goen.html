誤嚥(ごえん)

ごえん
noun, verb-suru
aspiration; accidental swallowing into the airway
1. aspiration; accidental inhalation of food or liquid into the airway
The unintentional entry of food, liquid, or other material into the trachea and lungs instead of the esophagus. A common medical concern especially among elderly people.
誤嚥(ごえん)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful of aspiration.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)は誤嚥(ごえん)による肺炎(はいえん)のリスクが高(たか)い。
Elderly people have a high risk of pneumonia caused by aspiration.
食(た)べ物(もの)を小(ちい)さく切(き)ることで誤嚥(ごえん)を防(ふせ)ぐことができる。
Cutting food into small pieces can help prevent aspiration.

Composed of 誤(ご) (mistake, error) and 嚥(えん) (to swallow). The word specifically refers to the accidental entry of food or liquid into the airway rather than the stomach.

誤嚥(ごえん)性(せい)肺炎(はいえん) (aspiration pneumonia) is a major health concern in aging societies like Japan, making this term frequently seen in health-related media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誤嚥(ごえん)性(せい)肺炎(はいえん) (aspiration pneumonia)
  • 誤嚥(ごえん)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent aspiration)
  • 誤嚥(ごえん)事故(じこ) (aspiration accident)
  • 誤嚥(ごえん)のリスク (risk of aspiration)
