気(き)を紛(まぎ)らす

きをまぎらす
expression, verb-godan
to distract oneself; to take one's mind off something
1. to distract oneself; to take one's mind off something
To do something in order to divert one's attention from worry, sadness, boredom, or discomfort.
散歩(さんぽ)をして気(き)を紛(まぎ)らす。
I take a walk to take my mind off things.
音楽(おんがく)を聴(き)いて気(き)を紛(まぎ)らした。
I listened to music to distract myself.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が気(き)になるが、料理(りょうり)をして気(き)を紛(まぎ)らすことにした。
I'm anxious about the exam results, but I decided to cook to take my mind off it.

GRAMMAR:
This is a set phrase using 紛(まぎ)らす (to divert, to distract). The verb 紛(まぎ)らす conjugates as a godan verb: 紛(まぎ)らさない / 紛(まぎ)らして / 紛(まぎ)らした.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気(き)を紛(まぎ)らすために (in order to distract oneself)
  • 気(き)を紛(まぎ)らす方法(ほうほう) (a way to take one's mind off things)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:
気(き)を散(ち)らす means to distract in a more general sense (including unwanted distractions). 気晴(きば)らし (diversion, pastime) is the noun form of the same concept.
