準拠(じゅんきょ)

じゅんきょ
noun
conformity; compliance; being based on
1. conformity; compliance; being based on
The act of following or conforming to a standard, rule, or authority. Often used in technical, legal, and business contexts to indicate that something meets or follows a particular specification or guideline.
国際(こくさい)規格(きかく)に準拠(じゅんきょ)している。
It conforms to international standards.
この製品(せいひん)はJIS規格(きかく)に準拠(じゅんきょ)して製造(せいぞう)されている。
This product is manufactured in compliance with JIS standards.
法律(ほうりつ)に準拠(じゅんきょ)した手続(てつづ)きを踏(ふ)む必要(ひつよう)がある。
It is necessary to follow procedures that comply with the law.

USAGE:
準拠(じゅんきょ) is commonly used with the particle に to indicate what standard is being followed: ～に準拠(じゅんきょ)する (conform to ~). It is a formal, written-register word found in technical documents, legal texts, and product specifications.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に準拠(じゅんきょ)する (conform to, comply with)
  • 規格(きかく)に準拠(じゅんきょ) (compliance with a standard)
  • 法令(ほうれい)準拠(じゅんきょ) (legal compliance)
  • 準拠(じゅんきょ)法(ほう) (governing law, applicable law)

SIMILAR WORDS:
基(もと)づく means 'to be based on' and is more general. 適合(てきごう) means 'conformity' and focuses on meeting specific requirements. 準拠(じゅんきょ) emphasizes following an authoritative standard or framework.
