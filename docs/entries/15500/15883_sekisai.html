1. loading; carrying; transporting (cargo or freight)
The act of loading goods or cargo onto a vehicle, ship, or other transport. Also refers to the cargo capacity of a vehicle.
積載量(せきさいりょう)を超(こ)えてはいけない。
You must not exceed the loading capacity.
このトラックの最大積載量(さいだいせきさいりょう)は五(ご)トンだ。
The maximum loading capacity of this truck is five tons.
積載量(せきさいりょう)オーバーで走行(そうこう)すると法律(ほうりつ)違反(いはん)になる。
Driving with cargo exceeding the loading limit is a legal violation.

積載(せきさい) refers to loading cargo onto a vehicle or the cargo-carrying capacity of a vehicle. A formal/technical term used in transportation, logistics, and regulations.

COMPOSITION:
積(せき) (pile up, load) + 載(さい) (carry, load onto).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 積載(せきさい)する: to load (cargo)
  • 積載量(せきさいりょう): loading capacity
  • 最大(さいだい)積載量(せきさいりょう): maximum loading capacity
  • 積載(せきさい)制限(せいげん): loading limit
  • 積載(せきさい)重量(じゅうりょう): loaded weight
  • 積載(せきさい)超過(ちょうか): overloading

USAGE:
Mainly appears in technical, legal, and regulatory contexts. You will often see 最大(さいだい)積載量(せきさいりょう) marked on commercial vehicles. Exceeding 積載(せきさい) limits is a traffic violation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 積(つ)む: to load (everyday verb)
  • 搭載(とうさい): to mount, to equip (for equipment/technology)
