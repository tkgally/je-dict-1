立案(りつあん)

りつあん
noun, verb-suru
planning; drafting; devising a plan
1. planning; drafting; devising a plan
The act of creating or drawing up a plan, proposal, or strategy. Used in business, government, and project management contexts.
新(あたら)しい計画(けいかく)を立案(りつあん)する。
To draft a new plan.
政策(せいさく)の立案(りつあん)には時間(じかん)がかかる。
Drafting a policy takes time.
プロジェクトの立案(りつあん)から実行(じっこう)まで半年(はんとし)かかった。
It took half a year from planning the project to its execution.

USAGE:
立案(りつあん) refers to the initial stage of creating a plan or proposal. It is more formal than 計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる and is often used in organizational or governmental contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 政策(せいさく)を立案(りつあん)する (to draft a policy)
  • 立案者(りつあんしゃ) (planner, author of a plan)
  • 立案(りつあん)段階(だんかい) (planning stage)
  • 企画(きかく)立案(りつあん) (project planning)

SIMILAR WORDS:
企画(きかく) focuses on conceiving creative projects or events, while 立案(りつあん) focuses on the formal drafting of plans and strategies. 策定(さくてい) is an even more formal term for establishing official policies.
