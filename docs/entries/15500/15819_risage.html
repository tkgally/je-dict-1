利下(りさ)げ

りさげ
noun
interest rate cut; rate reduction
1. interest rate cut; rate reduction
A reduction in interest rates, typically by a central bank as a monetary policy measure. Composed of 利(り) (interest) + 下(さ)げ (lowering).
中央(ちゅうおう)銀行(ぎんこう)が利下(りさ)げを決(き)めた。
The central bank decided on an interest rate cut.
利下(りさ)げの影響(えいきょう)で株価(かぶか)が上昇(じょうしょう)した。
Stock prices rose due to the effect of the rate cut.
景気(けいき)後退(こうたい)を防(ふせ)ぐために、大幅(おおはば)な利下(りさ)げが実施(じっし)される見通(みとお)しだ。
A significant rate cut is expected to be implemented to prevent an economic downturn.

USAGE:
利下(りさ)げ is a noun often used in news and financial reporting. It can also be used with する: 利下(りさ)げする (to cut interest rates). The word is frequently seen in headlines about central bank policy decisions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利下(りさ)げを行(おこな)う (carry out a rate cut)
  • 大幅(おおはば)な利下(りさ)げ (significant rate cut)
  • 追加(ついか)利下(りさ)げ (additional rate cut)
  • 利下(りさ)げ幅(はば) (size of the rate cut)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 利上(りあ)げ is the opposite, meaning an interest rate hike.
  • 金利(きんり) means interest rate.
  • 金融(きんゆう)緩和(かんわ) means monetary easing, a broader policy that includes rate cuts.
