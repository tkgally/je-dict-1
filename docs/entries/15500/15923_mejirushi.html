目印(めじるし)

めじるし
noun
landmark; mark; sign
1. landmark; mark; sign
A distinguishing feature or object used as a reference point to identify a location or thing. Used both for physical markers and visual cues for navigation.
赤(あか)い屋根(やね)が目印(めじるし)です。
The red roof is the landmark.
荷物(にもつ)にリボンを付(つ)けて目印(めじるし)にした。
I tied a ribbon on my luggage as a mark to identify it.
駅前(えきまえ)の大(おお)きな時計台(とけいだい)を目印(めじるし)にして歩(ある)いてきてください。
Please walk toward the big clock tower in front of the station as your landmark.

USAGE:
目印(めじるし) is used when giving directions ("look for X as a landmark") or when marking something for later identification. It is a very common everyday word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目印(めじるし)にする (to use as a landmark/marker)
  • 目印(めじるし)になる (to serve as a landmark)
  • 目印(めじるし)を付(つ)ける (to put a mark on something)
  • いい目印(めじるし) (a good landmark)

SIMILAR WORDS:
目標(もくひょう) means "goal" or "target," not a visual marker. 標識(ひょうしき) refers to an official sign (like a road sign), while 目印(めじるし) is any distinguishing feature used as a reference point.
