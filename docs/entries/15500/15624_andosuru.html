安堵(あんど)する

あんどする
verb
to be relieved; to feel reassured
1. to be relieved; to feel reassured
To experience a feeling of relief after worry or anxiety has been resolved. Commonly used to describe the emotional release when a feared outcome does not occur.
無事(ぶじ)だと聞(き)いて安堵(あんど)した。
I was relieved to hear that everyone was safe.
試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)して、やっと安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう)を見(み)せた。
Having passed the exam, she finally showed a look of relief.
手術(しゅじゅつ)が成功(せいこう)したという知(し)らせに、家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)が安堵(あんど)した。
The whole family was relieved at the news that the surgery was successful.

USAGE:
安堵(あんど)する is somewhat formal and literary compared to ほっとする, which is the more colloquial equivalent. 安堵(あんど) is frequently used as a noun modifier, as in 安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう) (expression of relief) or 安堵(あんど)感(かん) (sense of relief).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安堵(あんど)の表情(ひょうじょう) (expression of relief)
  • 安堵(あんど)感(かん) (sense of relief)
  • 安堵(あんど)の溜(た)め息(いき) (sigh of relief)
  • 安堵(あんど)の色(いろ)を浮(う)かべる (to show signs of relief)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ほっとする: to feel relieved (casual, everyday)
  • 安心(あんしん)する: to feel at ease, to be reassured
