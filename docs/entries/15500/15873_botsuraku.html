没落(ぼつらく)

ぼつらく
noun
downfall; ruin; decline
1. downfall; ruin; decline
The process of losing power, wealth, or status, falling from a position of prosperity or prominence. Used for families, dynasties, nations, and organizations.
名家(めいか)の没落(ぼつらく)を描(えが)いた小説(しょうせつ)だ。
It is a novel depicting the downfall of a distinguished family.
その帝国(ていこく)は戦争(せんそう)によって没落(ぼつらく)した。
That empire fell into ruin because of war.
貴族(きぞく)の没落(ぼつらく)とともに、古(ふる)い文化(ぶんか)や伝統(でんとう)も失(うしな)われていった。
Along with the decline of the aristocracy, old culture and traditions were also lost.

COMPOSITION:
没(ぼつ) (sink, fall) + 落(らく) (fall, drop). Both characters convey falling, giving the word a strong sense of decline.

GRAMMAR:
没落(ぼつらく)する is used as a する verb meaning "to fall into ruin" or "to decline."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 没落(ぼつらく)する (to fall into ruin)
  • 没落(ぼつらく)した貴族(きぞく) (a ruined aristocrat)
  • 没落(ぼつらく)の道(みち)をたどる (to follow the path of decline)
