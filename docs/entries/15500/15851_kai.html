甲斐(かい)

かい
noun
worth; effect; reward (for effort)
1. worth; effect; reward; avail
The value or result gained from an action or effort. Often used in set phrases to express whether something was worthwhile or had the desired effect.
努力(どりょく)した甲斐(かい)があった。
The effort was worth it.
練習(れんしゅう)の甲斐(かい)あって、試合(しあい)に勝(か)った。
Thanks to all the practice, we won the match.
治療(ちりょう)の甲斐(かい)なく、患者(かんじゃ)は亡(な)くなった。
Despite treatment, the patient passed away.

GRAMMAR:

甲斐(かい) typically appears in two key patterns:

  • ～した甲斐(かい)がある / あった — it was worth doing ~
  • ～の甲斐(かい)なく — despite ~, in vain
  • ～甲斐(がい)がある — worth doing (as a suffix, voiced to がい)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 生(い)き甲斐(がい) (いきがい) — purpose in life, reason for living
  • やり甲斐(がい) — worth doing, rewarding
  • 働(はたら)き甲斐(がい) — job satisfaction, worth working

NOTE:
When used as a suffix attached to verb stems, the reading changes to がい (voiced): やり甲斐(がい), 生(い)き甲斐(がい).
