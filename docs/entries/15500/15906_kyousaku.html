共作(きょうさく)

きょうさく
noun, verb (suru)
collaboration, joint work
1. collaboration; joint work; co-creation
The act of creating a work together with one or more other people. Commonly used for songs, artworks, scripts, and other creative outputs.
この曲(きょく)は二人(ふたり)の共作(きょうさく)だ。
This song is a collaboration between the two of them.
有名(ゆうめい)な作曲家(さっきょくか)と共作(きょうさく)することになった。
I ended up collaborating with a famous composer.
この映画(えいが)の脚本(きゃくほん)は三人(さんにん)の脚本家(きゃくほんか)が共作(きょうさく)したもので、それぞれの持(も)ち味(あじ)が活(い)かされている。
The screenplay for this movie was co-written by three screenwriters, and each one's strengths are reflected in it.

USAGE: 共作(きょうさく) is primarily used in creative and artistic contexts — music, film, literature, and art. It functions both as a noun and as a する verb (共作(きょうさく)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 共作(きょうさく)する: to collaborate, to co-create
  • 共作(きょうさく)曲(きょく): co-written song
  • ～との共作(きょうさく): collaboration with ~
  • 共作(きょうさく)者(しゃ): co-creator, collaborator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 共作(きょうさく): co-creation of a specific work
  • 共同(きょうどう)制作(せいさく): joint production (broader, institutional)
  • 合作(がっさく): joint production (often between countries or studios)
