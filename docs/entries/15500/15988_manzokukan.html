満足感(まんぞくかん)

まんぞくかん
noun
feeling of satisfaction; sense of fulfillment
1. feeling of satisfaction; sense of contentment
The subjective experience of being satisfied or fulfilled. Used after completing a task, eating a good meal, or achieving a goal.
大(おお)きな満足感(まんぞくかん)を得(え)た。
I felt a great sense of satisfaction.
プロジェクトが終(お)わって満足感(まんぞくかん)に浸(ひた)っている。
I'm basking in satisfaction now that the project is finished.
自分(じぶん)の力(ちから)で問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)した満足感(まんぞくかん)は何(なに)にも代(か)えがたい。
The satisfaction of solving a problem on your own is irreplaceable.

USAGE:
A compound of 満足(まんぞく) (satisfaction) + 感(かん) (feeling). While 満足(まんぞく) describes the state of being satisfied, 満足感(まんぞくかん) specifically refers to the inner emotional experience. Common verbs include 得(え)る (to obtain), 感(かん)じる (to feel), and 味(あじ)わう (to savor).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 満足感(まんぞくかん)を得(え)る (to gain a feeling of satisfaction)
  • 満足感(まんぞくかん)を感(かん)じる (to feel satisfied)
  • 満足感(まんぞくかん)に浸(ひた)る (to bask in satisfaction)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 達成感(たっせいかん): sense of accomplishment — specifically from completing or achieving something
  • 充実感(じゅうじつかん): sense of fulfillment — feeling that life or time is rich and meaningful
