投獄(とうごく)

とうごく
noun
imprisonment; incarceration
1. imprisonment; incarceration; putting in prison
The act of confining someone in prison, typically as punishment for a crime. A formal term used in legal and news contexts.
犯人(はんにん)は投獄(とうごく)された。
The criminal was imprisoned.
政治犯(せいじはん)が不当(ふとう)に投獄(とうごく)されている。
Political prisoners are being unjustly imprisoned.
活動家(かつどうか)は投獄(とうごく)されても信念(しんねん)を曲(ま)げなかった。
The activist did not waver in their convictions even when imprisoned.

USAGE:
Used both as a noun and as a suru-verb (投獄(とうごく)する). A formal term commonly seen in news articles, historical writing, and human rights discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投獄(とうごく)する (to imprison)
  • 投獄(とうごく)される (to be imprisoned)
  • 不当(ふとう)な投獄(とうごく) (unjust imprisonment)
