厳秘(げんぴ)

げんぴ
noun
strict secrecy; highly confidential
1. strict secrecy; highly confidential
The highest level of secrecy, indicating that information must be kept absolutely confidential. Used in official, military, and organizational contexts to classify sensitive information.
この情報(じょうほう)は厳秘(げんぴ)だ。
This information is strictly confidential.
計画(けいかく)は厳秘(げんぴ)扱(あつか)いとされている。
The plan is being treated as strictly confidential.
厳秘(げんぴ)事項(じこう)が外部(がいぶ)に漏(も)れたため、大(おお)きな問題(もんだい)になった。
A highly confidential matter was leaked to the outside, causing a major problem.

USAGE:
Used as a noun and as a の-modifier: 厳秘(げんぴ)の文書(ぶんしょ) (strictly confidential document). Often seen stamped on documents or used in formal organizational communication. Stronger than 秘密(ひみつ) (secret) or 機密(きみつ) (classified).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厳秘(げんぴ)扱(あつか)い (treated as strictly confidential)
  • 厳秘(げんぴ)事項(じこう) (strictly confidential matter)
  • 厳秘(げんぴ)文書(ぶんしょ) (strictly confidential document)
  • 厳秘(げんぴ)に付(ふ)す (to keep in strict secrecy)

SIMILAR WORDS:
秘密(ひみつ) (secret) is a general-purpose term, while 機密(きみつ) (classified) is used for official or state secrets. 厳秘(げんぴ) implies the strictest level of confidentiality.

Related Words

Synonym: 秘密(ひみつ) (secret (general))
Synonym: 機密(きみつ) (classified (official))
