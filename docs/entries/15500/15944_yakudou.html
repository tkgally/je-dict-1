躍動(やくどう)

やくどう
noun / verb-suru
lively motion; dynamism
1. lively motion; dynamism; vibrant energy
Energetic, pulsating movement or vitality. Used to describe things that are full of life and dynamic energy, such as performances, art, or nature.
躍動(やくどう)感(かん)のあるダンスだった。
It was a dance full of dynamic energy.
選手(せんしゅ)たちが躍動(やくどう)する姿(すがた)に感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by the sight of the athletes performing with such vitality.
この絵(え)は馬(うま)の躍動(やくどう)する瞬間(しゅんかん)を見事(みごと)にとらえている。
This painting brilliantly captures the moment of a horse in dynamic motion.

USAGE:
躍動(やくどう) is often used in descriptive or literary contexts to praise energetic, vibrant movement. The compound 躍動感(やくどうかん) (sense of dynamism) is especially common in art criticism, sports commentary, and advertising.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 躍動(やくどう)感(かん) (sense of dynamism, vitality)
  • 躍動(やくどう)する (to move dynamically, to pulsate with energy)
  • 躍動(やくどう)感(かん)あふれる (overflowing with dynamism)
  • 生命(せいめい)の躍動(やくどう) (the dynamism of life)

SIMILAR WORDS:
活気(かっき) means liveliness or vigor in a general sense, while 躍動(やくどう) specifically emphasizes dynamic physical movement and energy.
