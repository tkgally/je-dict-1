中休(なかやす)み

なかやすみ
noun
mid-break; recess; pause
1. mid-break; recess; intermission
A break or pause in the middle of an activity, event, or period. Can refer to a literal rest period or a temporary lull in something ongoing.
午前中(ごぜんちゅう)の中休(なかやす)みにコーヒーを飲(の)んだ。
I had coffee during the mid-morning break.
梅雨(つゆ)の中休(なかやす)みで久(ひさ)しぶりに晴(は)れた。
There was a break in the rainy season and it was sunny for the first time in a while.
長(なが)い会議(かいぎ)の途中(とちゅう)で十分間(じゅっぷんかん)の中休(なかやす)みを取(と)った。
We took a ten-minute break in the middle of the long meeting.

USAGE:
Often used for weather patterns — 梅雨(つゆ)の中休(なかやす)み (a break in the rainy season) and 猛暑(もうしょ)の中休(なかやす)み (a break from the intense heat) are very common expressions in weather forecasts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 梅雨(つゆ)の中休(なかやす)み (break in the rainy season)
  • 中休(なかやす)みを取(と)る (to take a mid-break)
  • 猛暑(もうしょ)の中休(なかやす)み (respite from extreme heat)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 休憩(きゅうけい): break, rest (more general and formal)
  • 一息(ひといき): a breather (shorter, more casual)
