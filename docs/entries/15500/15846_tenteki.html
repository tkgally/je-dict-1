天敵(てんてき)

てんてき
noun
natural enemy; nemesis
1. natural enemy; natural predator
An animal that naturally preys on or threatens another species in the wild.
猫(ねこ)は鼠(ねずみ)の天敵(てんてき)だ。
Cats are the natural enemy of mice.
この害虫(がいちゅう)には天敵(てんてき)がいないため、被害(ひがい)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Because this pest has no natural enemies, the damage is spreading.
農薬(のうやく)の代(か)わりに天敵(てんてき)を利用(りよう)した害虫(がいちゅう)駆除(くじょ)が注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Pest control using natural enemies instead of pesticides is attracting attention.
2. nemesis; one's worst enemy (figurative)
A person or thing that one finds impossible to deal with or defeat. Used casually to describe someone or something that is one's biggest weakness.
あの選手(せんしゅ)は彼(かれ)にとって天敵(てんてき)だ。
That player is his nemesis.
数学(すうがく)は私(わたし)の天敵(てんてき)だ。
Math is my worst enemy.
梅雨(つゆ)は洗濯物(せんたくもの)の天敵(てんてき)で、なかなか乾(かわ)かない。
The rainy season is the nemesis of laundry — it just won't dry.

COMPOSITION:
天(てん) (heaven, natural) + 敵(てき) (enemy). Literally "enemy given by nature."

USAGE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is very common in casual speech. People use it to describe anything they find difficult to deal with: a rival, a school subject, weather conditions, etc.
