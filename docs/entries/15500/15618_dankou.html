1. decisive action; carrying out resolutely; pushing through
Carrying out a plan or action with determination, especially when there is opposition or difficulty. Implies boldness and resolve.
改革(かいかく)を断行(だんこう)する。
To carry out reform decisively.
社長(しゃちょう)は反対(はんたい)を押(お)し切(き)ってリストラを断行(だんこう)した。
The company president pushed through the restructuring despite opposition.
困難(こんなん)な状況(じょうきょう)でも必要(ひつよう)な措置(そち)を断行(だんこう)する覚悟(かくご)が求(もと)められる。
Even in difficult situations, one must have the resolve to take necessary measures decisively.

USAGE:
断行(だんこう) implies that the action requires courage or determination, often because it is unpopular, risky, or faces resistance. It is commonly used in political and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改革(かいかく)を断行(だんこう)する (to push through reform)
  • 値上(ねあ)げを断行(だんこう)する (to decisively implement a price increase)
  • 断行(だんこう)する覚悟(かくご) (the resolve to take decisive action)

SIMILAR WORDS:
実行(じっこう) (execution, carrying out) is neutral and does not imply difficulty. 決行(けっこう) means to go ahead with something despite obstacles (like bad weather), but is less focused on the boldness of the decision.

Related Words

Synonym: 実行(じっこう) (neutral)
Synonym: 決行(けっこう) (despite obstacles)
