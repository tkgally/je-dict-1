打(う)ち負(ま)かす

うちまかす
verb
to defeat; to beat
1. to defeat; to beat; to overcome
To win decisively against an opponent in a competition, battle, or contest. Emphasizes a clear, forceful victory.
強豪(きょうごう)チームを打(う)ち負(ま)かした。
They defeated the powerhouse team.
相手(あいて)を打(う)ち負(ま)かして決勝(けっしょう)に進(すす)んだ。
They beat their opponent and advanced to the finals.
ライバル企業(きぎょう)を打(う)ち負(ま)かして市場(しじょう)シェアを獲得(かくとく)した。
They beat the rival company and captured market share.

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (打(う)ち負(ま)かす → 打(う)ち負(ま)かさない → 打(う)ち負(ま)かして). Transitive: takes を to mark the defeated party.

USAGE:
The 打(う)ち〜 prefix adds emphasis and forcefulness, similar to English "beat soundly" or "trounce." Stronger than 勝(か)つ (to win) and implies a more decisive victory.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勝(か)つ: to win (neutral)
  • 負(ま)かす: to defeat (less emphatic)
  • 倒(たお)す: to defeat, to bring down (also emphatic)
