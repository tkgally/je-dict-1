存分(ぞんぶん)に

ぞんぶんに
adverb
to one's heart's content; fully
1. to one's heart's content; fully; as much as one likes
Doing something without restraint or limit, to the full extent of one's desire. Implies freedom to enjoy or act without holding back.
存分(ぞんぶん)に楽(たの)しんでください。
Please enjoy yourself to your heart's content.
休日(きゅうじつ)は存分(ぞんぶん)に寝(ね)た。
I slept as much as I wanted on my day off.
せっかくの旅行(りょこう)だから、現地(げんち)の料理(りょうり)を存分(ぞんぶん)に味(あじ)わいたい。
Since it's a special trip, I want to fully savor the local cuisine.

USAGE:
Conveys a sense of doing something without holding back. Often used when encouraging someone to enjoy themselves or when describing an experience of uninhibited pleasure or effort.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に楽(たの)しむ (to enjoy to one's heart's content)
  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に味(あじ)わう (to savor fully)
  • 存分(ぞんぶん)に力(ちから)を発揮(はっき)する (to demonstrate one's ability fully)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 思(おも)う存分(ぞんぶん): to one's heart's content (synonym, slightly more emphatic)
  • 十分(じゅうぶん)に: sufficiently, fully (more about adequacy than freedom)
  • たっぷり: plenty, amply (more casual)
