完走(かんそう)する

かんそうする
verb
to finish a race; to run to completion
1. to finish a race; to run the entire course; to complete a race
To run or complete a race from start to finish, especially a long-distance race like a marathon. Can also be used figuratively for completing something challenging from beginning to end.
マラソンを完走(かんそう)した。
I finished the marathon.
初(はじ)めてのフルマラソンで完走(かんそう)できて嬉(うれ)しい。
I'm happy that I was able to finish my first full marathon.
途中(とちゅう)で足(あし)を痛(いた)めたが、何(なん)とか完走(かんそう)することができた。
I hurt my leg partway through, but I managed to finish the race.

USAGE:
完走(かんそう) emphasizes completing the full distance rather than winning. It is a significant achievement in long-distance events like marathons, triathlons, and ultramarathons. Also used figuratively for completing a long project or series.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マラソンを完走(かんそう)する (to finish a marathon)
  • 完走(かんそう)者(しゃ) (finisher)
  • 完走(かんそう)率(りつ) (completion rate)
  • 完走(かんそう)タイム (finishing time)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完走(かんそう): completion of a race (noun form)
  • ゴールする: to reach the goal, to finish
  • リタイアする: to retire, to drop out (antonym)
