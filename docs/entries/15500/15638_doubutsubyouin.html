動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)

どうぶつびょういん
noun
veterinary hospital; animal clinic
1. veterinary hospital; animal clinic; vet's office
A medical facility that provides healthcare for animals, especially pets. The standard term used in Japan for a veterinary practice.
猫(ねこ)を動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)に連(つ)れていった。
I took the cat to the vet.
近所(きんじょ)に新(あたら)しい動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)ができた。
A new animal clinic opened in the neighborhood.
犬(いぬ)の具合(ぐあい)が悪(わる)いので、今日中(きょうじゅう)に動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)に予約(よやく)を入(い)れたい。
The dog is not feeling well, so I want to make an appointment at the vet today.

USAGE:
動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん) is the standard everyday term for a veterinary clinic in Japan. Pet owners use this term regardless of the size of the facility. The abbreviated form 動物病院(どうぶつびょういん) is often seen on signs and business cards.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)に連(つ)れていく (to take to the vet)
  • 動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)に通(かよ)う (to go to the vet regularly)
  • 動物(どうぶつ)病院(びょういん)の先生(せんせい) (the vet, the veterinarian)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 獣医(じゅうい): veterinarian (the profession)
  • 獣医師(じゅういし): veterinarian (formal title)
