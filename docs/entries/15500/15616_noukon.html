濃紺(のうこん)

のうこん
noun
dark navy blue; deep indigo
1. dark navy blue; deep indigo
A very deep, dark shade of blue, darker than ordinary navy. Commonly used for suits, school uniforms, and formal clothing.
濃紺(のうこん)のスーツを着(き)ている。
He is wearing a dark navy suit.
制服(せいふく)は濃紺(のうこん)のブレザーだ。
The school uniform is a dark navy blazer.
濃紺(のうこん)は黒(くろ)に近(ちか)いが、光(ひかり)に当(あ)たると青(あお)みが分(わ)かる。
Dark navy is close to black, but you can see its blue tint in the light.

USAGE:
濃紺(のうこん) is composed of 濃(のう) (dark/rich in color) and 紺(こん) (navy blue). It is commonly used for describing clothing, especially formal or professional attire.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 濃紺(のうこん)のスーツ (a dark navy suit)
  • 濃紺(のうこん)のネクタイ (a dark navy tie)
  • 濃紺(のうこん)に近(ちか)い (close to dark navy)

RELATED COLORS:

  • 紺(こん) (navy blue — standard shade)
  • 藍色(あいいろ) (indigo — the traditional dye color)
  • ネイビー (navy — the loanword)
