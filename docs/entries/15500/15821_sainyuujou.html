再入場(さいにゅうじょう)

さいにゅうじょう
noun
re-entry; readmission
1. re-entry; readmission
The act of entering a venue again after having left, such as a concert hall, stadium, theme park, or exhibition. Often subject to specific rules or restrictions. Composed of 再(さい) (again) + 入場(にゅうじょう) (entry).
再入場(さいにゅうじょう)はできますか。
Is re-entry allowed?
このイベントは再入場(さいにゅうじょう)不可(ふか)です。
Re-entry is not permitted at this event.
再入場(さいにゅうじょう)の際(さい)は手(て)の甲(こう)のスタンプを見(み)せてください。
When re-entering, please show the stamp on the back of your hand.

USAGE:
再入場(さいにゅうじょう) is commonly seen on signs and in event rules. It can be used with する as a verbal noun: 再入場(さいにゅうじょう)する (to re-enter). Many venues stamp visitors' hands or provide wristbands for re-entry.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再入場(さいにゅうじょう)不可(ふか) (no re-entry)
  • 再入場(さいにゅうじょう)可能(かのう) (re-entry allowed)
  • 再入場(さいにゅうじょう)する (to re-enter)
  • 再入場(さいにゅうじょう)スタンプ (re-entry stamp)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 入場(にゅうじょう) means entry or admission.
  • 退場(たいじょう) means exit or leaving.
  • 入場券(にゅうじょうけん) means admission ticket.
