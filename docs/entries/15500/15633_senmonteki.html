専門的(せんもんてき)

せんもんてき
adjective
specialized; technical; professional
1. specialized; technical; professional
Relating to or requiring expert knowledge in a particular field. Describes things that are not general but belong to a specific area of expertise.
専門的(せんもんてき)な知識(ちしき)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Specialized knowledge is required.
この本(ほん)は内容(ないよう)が専門的(せんもんてき)すぎて難(むずか)しい。
This book is too technical and difficult.
もっと専門的(せんもんてき)な立場(たちば)から意見(いけん)を聞(き)きたいので、専門家(せんもんか)に相談(そうだん)しよう。
I want to hear opinions from a more specialized perspective, so let's consult an expert.

USAGE:
専門的(せんもんてき) is a na-adjective that modifies nouns with な. It describes things related to specialized fields, expert-level knowledge, or professional domains. Often contrasted with 一般的(いっぱんてき) (general).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 専門的(せんもんてき)な知識(ちしき) (specialized knowledge)
  • 専門的(せんもんてき)な用語(ようご) (technical terminology)
  • 専門的(せんもんてき)な訓練(くんれん) (specialized training)
  • 専門的(せんもんてき)な分野(ぶんや) (specialized field)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 専門(せんもん): specialty, specialization (noun)
  • 技術的(ぎじゅつてき): technical (relating to technique/technology)
  • 一般的(いっぱんてき): general (antonym)
