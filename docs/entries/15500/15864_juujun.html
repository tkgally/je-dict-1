従順(じゅうじゅん)

じゅうじゅん
adjective
obedient; docile; submissive
1. obedient; docile; submissive; meek
Describes a person or animal that follows instructions or authority without resistance. Can be used positively (well-behaved) or negatively (too submissive).
この犬(いぬ)はとても従順(じゅうじゅん)だ。
This dog is very obedient.
彼(かれ)は上司(じょうし)に従順(じゅうじゅん)な態度(たいど)を見(み)せた。
He showed a submissive attitude toward his boss.
従順(じゅうじゅん)なだけでは自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)を伝(つた)えることができない。
If you are only obedient, you cannot express your own opinions.

USAGE:
Used as a な-adjective: 従順(じゅうじゅん)な人(ひと) (an obedient person), 従順(じゅうじゅん)な性格(せいかく) (a docile personality).

COMPOSITION:
従(じゅう) (follow, obey) + 順(じゅん) (order, obedience).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 従順(じゅうじゅん)な性格(せいかく) (a docile nature)
  • 従順(じゅうじゅん)に従(したが)う (to obey obediently)
  • 従順(じゅうじゅん)な態度(たいど) (a submissive attitude)
