模倣(もほう)

もほう
noun
imitation; copying; mimicry
1. imitation; copying; mimicry
The act of copying or imitating someone or something, whether in art, behavior, or technique. Often carries a neutral or slightly negative nuance, suggesting a lack of originality. Can be used as a suru-verb. Composed of 模 (model/pattern) + 倣 (emulate).
子供(こども)は大人(おとな)を模倣(もほう)して学(まな)ぶ。
Children learn by imitating adults.
この作品(さくひん)は有名(ゆうめい)な画家(がか)の模倣(もほう)にすぎない。
This work is nothing more than an imitation of a famous painter.
模倣(もほう)犯罪(はんざい)を防(ふせ)ぐために、報道(ほうどう)の仕方(しかた)にも注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Care must be taken in how things are reported in order to prevent copycat crimes.

USAGE:
模倣(もほう) is more formal than 真似(まね) (imitation/mimicry). It is commonly used in academic, artistic, and media contexts. It can function as a suru-verb: 模倣(もほう)する (to imitate).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 模倣(もほう)する (to imitate)
  • 模倣(もほう)犯罪(はんざい) (copycat crime)
  • 模倣(もほう)品(ひん) (imitation product, counterfeit)
  • 模倣(もほう)者(しゃ) (imitator)
  • 模倣(もほう)から始(はじ)まる (start from imitation)

SIMILAR WORDS:
真似(まね) is the everyday word for "imitation" or "mimicry" and is less formal. 模写(もしゃ) specifically refers to copying a painting or artwork. 偽造(ぎぞう) means "forgery" or "counterfeiting" and implies illegal intent.
