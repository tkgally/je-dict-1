相反(あいはん)する

あいはんする
verb
to conflict with; to be contradictory
1. to conflict with; to be contradictory; to be incompatible
Used when two things are in opposition or mutually contradictory. Typically describes ideas, interests, principles, or statements that cannot coexist.
二(ふた)つの意見(いけん)が相反(あいはん)する。
The two opinions conflict with each other.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)と行動(こうどう)は相反(あいはん)している。
His words and actions are contradictory.
経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)と環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)は相反(あいはん)する目標(もくひょう)だと言(い)われることがある。
Economic growth and environmental protection are sometimes said to be conflicting goals.

USAGE:
A formal verb often used in written language, academic discussion, and news commentary. Typically takes two subjects connected by と or が, describing a mutual opposition. The noun form 相反(あいはん) can also appear in compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利害(りがい)が相反(あいはん)する (interests conflict)
  • 相反(あいはん)する意見(いけん) (conflicting opinions)
  • 相反(あいはん)する感情(かんじょう) (contradictory emotions)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 矛盾(むじゅん)する: to contradict — implies logical inconsistency, often within one person's statements
  • 対立(たいりつ)する: to oppose, to confront — implies active confrontation between parties
