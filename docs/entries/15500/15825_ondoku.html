音読(おんどく)

おんどく
noun
reading aloud; oral reading
1. reading aloud; oral reading
The act of reading text out loud, as opposed to reading silently. A common practice in Japanese schools and language learning.
教科書(きょうかしょ)を音読(おんどく)する。
I read the textbook aloud.
音読(おんどく)は語学(ごがく)学習(がくしゅう)に効果的(こうかてき)だと言(い)われている。
Reading aloud is said to be effective for language learning.
毎朝(まいあさ)子供(こども)たちが教室(きょうしつ)で音読(おんどく)の練習(れんしゅう)をしている。
Every morning, the children practice reading aloud in the classroom.

USAGE:
音読(おんどく) is used both as a noun and with する as a verbal noun: 音読(おんどく)する (to read aloud). It is a standard activity in Japanese elementary schools and language courses. Note that the same kanji 音読(おんよ)み can also be read おんよみ, meaning the Chinese-derived reading of a kanji character—a completely different word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音読(おんどく)する (to read aloud)
  • 音読(おんどく)の練習(れんしゅう) (reading-aloud practice)
  • 音読(おんどく)発表(はっぴょう) (oral reading presentation)
  • 音読(おんどく)カード (reading-aloud record card)

SIMILAR WORDS:
黙読(もくどく) is the opposite, meaning silent reading. 朗読(ろうどく) also means reading aloud but implies a more expressive, performance-like reading, as in a poetry recital or audiobook narration.
