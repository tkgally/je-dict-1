札束(さつたば)

さつたば
noun
wad of bills; bundle of banknotes
1. wad of bills; bundle of banknotes
A stack or bundle of paper currency. Often evokes an image of large amounts of cash.
札束(さつたば)を数(かぞ)えている。
He is counting a wad of bills.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に札束(さつたば)が積(つ)まれていた。
Bundles of banknotes were stacked on the desk.
映画(えいが)の中(なか)で犯人(はんにん)がスーツケースいっぱいの札束(さつたば)を持(も)って逃(に)げた。
In the movie, the criminal ran away with a suitcase full of bundles of cash.

USAGE:
札束(さつたば) literally combines 札(さつ) (banknote) and 束(たば) (bundle). It conjures images of wealth or large cash transactions and frequently appears in crime dramas and news stories.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 札束(さつたば)を数(かぞ)える (to count a wad of bills)
  • 札束(さつたば)を積(つ)む (to stack bundles of bills)
  • 札束(さつたば)で頬(ほお)をたたく (to wave money around to get what you want — lit. "to slap someone's cheek with a wad of cash")

SIMILAR WORDS:
現金(げんきん) means cash in general, while 札束(さつたば) specifically refers to a visible stack or bundle of paper money.
