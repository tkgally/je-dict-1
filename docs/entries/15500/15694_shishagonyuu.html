1. rounding; rounding off
The mathematical operation of rounding a number to the nearest whole number or specified decimal place. Literally means 'discard four, enter five' — values below five are rounded down and values of five or above are rounded up.
小数点(しょうすうてん)以下(いか)を四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)する。
Round off the decimal places.
3.7を四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)すると4になる。
3.7 rounded off becomes 4.
計算(けいさん)結果(けっか)は四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)して整数(せいすう)で答(こた)えなさい。
Round the calculation result and give the answer as a whole number.

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally "四(し)を捨(す)て五(ご)を入(い)れる" — discard (round down) at four, include (round up) at five. This four-character compound elegantly describes the standard rounding rule.

GRAMMAR:
Used with する to form a verb: 四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)する (to round off).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)する (to round off)
  • 小数点(しょうすうてん)以下(いか)を四捨五入(ししゃごにゅう)する (to round off decimal places)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(き)り上(あ)げ: rounding up
  • 切(き)り捨(す)て: rounding down, truncation
