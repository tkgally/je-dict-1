装丁(そうてい)

そうてい
noun
book design; bookbinding
1. book design; bookbinding; book cover design
The overall design and physical production of a book, including the cover, binding, paper choice, and layout. In modern usage, often refers specifically to the cover and jacket design.
この本(ほん)は装丁(そうてい)が美(うつく)しい。
This book has a beautiful design.
装丁(そうてい)に惹(ひ)かれて手(て)に取(と)った。
I picked it up because I was drawn to the cover design.
有名(ゆうめい)なデザイナーが装丁(そうてい)を手(て)がけた小説(しょうせつ)が話題(わだい)になっている。
A novel whose design was done by a famous designer has become a talking point.

USAGE:
In publishing, 装丁(そうてい) encompasses the entire physical design of a book. Book designers are called 装丁家(そうていか) and are often credited on the book's copyright page. Japanese readers often appreciate and discuss book design as an art form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 装丁(そうてい)が美(うつく)しい (to have beautiful design)
  • 装丁(そうてい)を手(て)がける (to design a book)
  • 装丁家(そうていか) (book designer)
  • 装丁(そうてい){デザイン} (book design)
