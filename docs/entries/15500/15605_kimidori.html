黄緑(きみどり)

きみどり
noun
yellow-green; chartreuse; lime green
1. yellow-green; chartreuse; lime green
A color between yellow and green, like the color of new leaves in spring or a lime. One of the traditional Japanese color names and one of the standard crayon colors.
新芽(しんめ)は黄緑(きみどり)色(いろ)をしている。
The new buds are yellow-green.
黄緑(きみどり)のクレヨンで葉(は)っぱを塗(ぬ)った。
I colored the leaves with a yellow-green crayon.
春(はる)になると山(やま)が黄緑(きみどり)から深(ふか)い緑(みどり)へと変(か)わっていく。
When spring comes, the mountains gradually change from yellow-green to deep green.

USAGE:
黄緑(きみどり) is one of the standard Japanese color names. It is commonly used in everyday life, especially for describing nature, art supplies, and clothing. It is often written as 黄緑(きみどり)色(いろ) when used as a modifier.

RELATED COLORS:

  • 緑(みどり) (green)
  • 黄色(きいろ) (yellow)
  • 若草色(わかくさいろ) (young grass green — a similar but more traditional color name)
