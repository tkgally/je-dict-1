刺(さ)し傷(きず)

さしきず
noun
puncture wound, stab wound
1. puncture wound; stab wound
A wound caused by a pointed object piercing the skin. Used in medical, news, and crime contexts.
腕(うで)に刺(さ)し傷(きず)がある。
There is a puncture wound on the arm.
被害者(ひがいしゃ)の体(からだ)には複数(ふくすう)の刺(さ)し傷(きず)があった。
The victim's body had multiple stab wounds.
釘(くぎ)を踏(ふ)んでできた刺(さ)し傷(きず)が化膿(かのう)しないよう、すぐに消毒(しょうどく)した。
I disinfected the puncture wound from stepping on a nail right away so it wouldn't get infected.

USAGE: 刺(さ)し傷(きず) refers to wounds made by sharp, pointed objects — knives, nails, thorns, etc. It appears frequently in news reports about crimes and in medical contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 刺(さ)し傷(きず)を負(お)う: to sustain a stab wound
  • 深(ふか)い刺(さ)し傷(きず): deep puncture wound
  • 刺(さ)し傷(きず)を手当(てあ)てする: to treat a stab wound

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刺(さ)し傷(きず): puncture/stab wound (from a pointed object)
  • 切(き)り傷(きず): cut, laceration (from a blade or edge)
  • 擦(す)り傷(きず): scrape, abrasion (from friction)

Related Words

Synonym: 切(き)り傷(きず) (cut, laceration)
Synonym: 擦(す)り傷(きず) (scrape, abrasion)
