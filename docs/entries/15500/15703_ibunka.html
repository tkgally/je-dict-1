異文化(いぶんか)

いぶんか
noun
different culture; foreign culture
1. different culture; foreign culture; cross-cultural
A culture different from one's own. Often used as a modifier in compounds relating to cross-cultural experiences, understanding, and exchange.
異文化(いぶんか)交流(こうりゅう)に興味(きょうみ)がある。
I'm interested in cross-cultural exchange.
異文化(いぶんか)を理解(りかい)することは大切(たいせつ)だ。
Understanding different cultures is important.
留学(りゅうがく)で異文化(いぶんか)に触(ふ)れることは、視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げる貴重(きちょう)な経験(けいけん)だ。
Encountering different cultures through study abroad is a valuable experience that broadens one's perspective.

USAGE:
Most commonly used as a modifier in compound expressions. Frequently appears in academic, educational, and international relations contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 異文化(いぶんか)交流(こうりゅう) (cross-cultural exchange)
  • 異文化(いぶんか)理解(りかい) (cross-cultural understanding)
  • 異文化(いぶんか)コミュニケーション (cross-cultural communication)
  • 異文化(いぶんか)体験(たいけん) (cross-cultural experience)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 多文化(たぶんか): multicultural
  • 外国(がいこく)の文化(ぶんか): foreign culture (more general phrase)
