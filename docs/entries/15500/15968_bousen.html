1. defensive battle; fighting to defend; holding one's ground
Fighting in a defensive position against an attack. Used both for military defense and figuratively for situations where one is under pressure and trying to hold out, such as in sports, debate, or business competition.
防戦(ぼうせん)を強(し)いられた。
We were forced into a defensive battle.
相手(あいて)チームの猛攻(もうこう)に防戦(ぼうせん)するのが精一杯(せいいっぱい)だった。
We could barely manage to defend against the opposing team's fierce attacks.
野党(やとう)の追及(ついきゅう)に対(たい)して、大臣(だいじん)は防戦(ぼうせん)一方(いっぽう)だった。
Faced with the opposition's questioning, the minister was entirely on the defensive.

USAGE:
Functions as a noun and する verb: 防戦(ぼうせん)する (to fight defensively). The expression 防戦(ぼうせん)一方(いっぽう) (entirely on the defensive) is very common and describes being completely overwhelmed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 防戦(ぼうせん)する (to fight defensively)
  • 防戦(ぼうせん)一方(いっぽう) (entirely on the defensive)
  • 防戦(ぼうせん)を強(し)いられる (to be forced to fight defensively)
  • 必死(ひっし)に防戦(ぼうせん)する (to defend desperately)

ETYMOLOGY:
防(ぼう) (defend) + 戦(せん) (battle).
