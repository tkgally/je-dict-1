受話器(じゅわき)

じゅわき
noun
telephone receiver; handset
1. telephone receiver; handset
The part of a telephone that you hold to your ear and speak into. By extension, also used for the handset of a landline phone as a whole. Composed of 受 (receive) + 話 (speech) + 器 (device).
受話器(じゅわき)を取(と)ってください。
Please pick up the receiver.
彼女(かのじょ)は受話器(じゅわき)を置(お)いて深(ふか)い溜(た)め息(いき)をついた。
She hung up the receiver and let out a deep sigh.
受話器(じゅわき)の向(む)こうから怒鳴(どな)り声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえてきた。
I could hear a shouting voice coming from the other end of the receiver.

USAGE:
受話器(じゅわき) typically refers to landline phone handsets. With mobile phones, the term is less common, though it may appear in figurative or literary contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受話器(じゅわき)を取(と)る (pick up the receiver)
  • 受話器(じゅわき)を置(お)く (hang up the receiver)
  • 受話器(じゅわき)を握(にぎ)る (grip the receiver)
  • 受話器(じゅわき)を耳(みみ)に当(あ)てる (hold the receiver to one's ear)

SIMILAR WORDS:
送話器(そうわき) is the mouthpiece/transmitter (the part you speak into), while 受話器(じゅわき) originally meant the earpiece but now generally refers to the entire handset.
