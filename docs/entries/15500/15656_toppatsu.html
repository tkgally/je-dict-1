突発(とっぱつ)

とっぱつ
noun
sudden occurrence; outbreak
1. sudden occurrence; unexpected outbreak
Something happening suddenly and without warning. Often used for accidents, incidents, or events that occur unexpectedly.
突発(とっぱつ)的(てき)な事故(じこ)が起(お)きた。
A sudden accident occurred.
突発(とっぱつ)の用事(ようじ)で会議(かいぎ)に出(で)られなかった。
I couldn't attend the meeting due to something unexpected coming up.
突発(とっぱつ)的(てき)なトラブルに備(そな)えてマニュアルを用意(ようい)しておくべきだ。
You should prepare a manual in case of unexpected trouble.

GRAMMAR:
Often used in the form 突発(とっぱつ)的(てき)(な) as a な-adjective meaning "sudden" or "unexpected." Can also be used as a noun or with する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 突発(とっぱつ)的(てき)な事故(じこ) (sudden accident)
  • 突発(とっぱつ)的(てき)な事態(じたい) (unexpected situation)
  • 突発(とっぱつ)事故(じこ) (sudden accident)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 突然(とつぜん): suddenly (adverb, more common in everyday speech)
  • 急(きゅう): sudden, abrupt (broader usage)
