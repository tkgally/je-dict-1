何故(なぜ)か

なぜか
adverb
for some reason; somehow; inexplicably
1. for some reason; somehow; inexplicably
Indicates that the speaker cannot explain the reason for something. Expresses puzzlement about why a situation exists or an event occurred.
何故(なぜ)か涙(なみだ)が出(で)た。
For some reason, tears came out.
何故(なぜ)か彼女(かのじょ)は今日(きょう)機嫌(きげん)が悪(わる)い。
For some reason, she's in a bad mood today.
何故(なぜ)か分(わ)からないが、この場所(ばしょ)に来(く)ると心(こころ)が落(お)ち着(つ)く。
I don't know why, but I feel calm when I come to this place.

USAGE:
何故(なぜ)か is often written in hiragana as なぜか. It is placed at the beginning of a clause or sentence to express that the reason is unknown or unexplainable. It adds a nuance of mild puzzlement or wonder.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 何故(なぜ)か分(わ)からないが… (I don't know why, but…)
  • 何故(なぜ)か知(し)らないが… (I don't know why, but…)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どういうわけか: for some reason (slightly more formal)
  • なんとなく: somehow, vaguely (softer, less puzzled)
  • 不思議(ふしぎ)と: mysteriously, strangely
