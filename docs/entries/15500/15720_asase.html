浅瀬(あさせ)

あさせ
noun
shoal; shallow water; shallows
1. shoal; shallow water; shallows; ford
A place in a body of water where the depth is shallow, such as a sandbar in the sea or a shallow stretch of a river that can be waded across.
浅瀬(あさせ)で遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play in the shallows.
船(ふね)が浅瀬(あさせ)に乗(の)り上(あ)げた。
The boat ran aground on a shoal.
川(かわ)の浅瀬(あさせ)を歩(ある)いて渡(わた)ることができる。
You can walk across the river at the shallows.

USAGE:
浅瀬(あさせ) can refer to shallow areas in rivers, lakes, or the sea. It appears in the proverb 浅瀬(あさせ)に仇波(あだなみ) (much ado about nothing — literally "waves in the shallows"), describing people who make a fuss over trivial matters.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 浅瀬(あさせ)に乗(の)り上(あ)げる (to run aground on a shoal)
  • 浅瀬(あさせ)を渡(わた)る (to wade across the shallows)
  • 浅瀬(あさせ)で遊(あそ)ぶ (to play in the shallows)
