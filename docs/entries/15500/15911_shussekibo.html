出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)

しゅっせきぼ
noun
attendance record, roll book
1. attendance record; roll book; attendance register
A book or record used to track attendance, especially in schools and classrooms. The teacher typically calls roll and marks attendance in this record.
先生(せんせい)が出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)を取(と)った。
The teacher took attendance.
出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)に名前(なまえ)がなかった。
My name wasn't in the attendance record.
担任(たんにん)の先生(せんせい)が毎朝(まいあさ)出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)を見(み)ながら一人(ひとり)ずつ名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)んでいく。
The homeroom teacher calls out each student's name while looking at the attendance book every morning.

USAGE: 出席簿(しゅっせきぼ) is primarily associated with schools and classrooms. The teacher uses it to record which students are present, absent, or late.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)を取(と)る: to take attendance
  • 出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)に記録(きろく)する: to record in the attendance book
  • 出席簿(しゅっせきぼ)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the attendance record

The word is a compound of 出席(しゅっせき) (attendance) and 簿(ぼ) (register, ledger).
