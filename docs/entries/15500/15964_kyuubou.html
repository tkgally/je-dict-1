窮乏(きゅうぼう)

きゅうぼう
noun
destitution; poverty; privation
1. destitution; poverty; privation
A state of extreme poverty or want, where one lacks the basic necessities of life. A formal, literary word used in written Japanese and historical descriptions.
戦後(せんご)は窮乏(きゅうぼう)した生活(せいかつ)が続(つづ)いた。
After the war, a life of destitution continued.
長引(ながび)く不況(ふきょう)で国民(こくみん)の窮乏(きゅうぼう)が深(ふか)まった。
The prolonged recession deepened the poverty of the people.
窮乏(きゅうぼう)の中(なか)でも人々(ひとびと)は助(たす)け合(あ)い、困難(こんなん)を乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
Even in the midst of destitution, people helped one another and overcame hardship.

USAGE:
A formal noun that also functions as a する verb: 窮乏(きゅうぼう)する (to become destitute). Often used in historical writing about wartime or post-war Japan, and in descriptions of economic hardship.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 窮乏(きゅうぼう)した生活(せいかつ) (a destitute life)
  • 窮乏(きゅうぼう)に耐(た)える (to endure poverty)
  • 窮乏(きゅうぼう)が深(ふか)まる (poverty deepens)
  • 窮乏(きゅうぼう)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)す (to escape from poverty)

SIMILAR WORDS:
貧困(ひんこん) (poverty) is the most common neutral term. 窮乏(きゅうぼう) emphasizes severe, pressing want and is more literary.
