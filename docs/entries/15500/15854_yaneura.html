屋根裏(やねうら)

やねうら
noun
attic; loft; space under the roof
1. attic; loft; space under the roof
The space between the ceiling of the top floor and the roof of a building. May be used for storage or converted into a living space.
屋根裏(やねうら)に荷物(にもつ)をしまった。
I stored luggage in the attic.
屋根裏(やねうら)を部屋(へや)に改装(かいそう)した。
We converted the attic into a room.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)の屋根裏(やねうら)から昔(むかし)の写真(しゃしん)や手紙(てがみ)が見(み)つかった。
Old photos and letters were found in the attic of the old house.

屋根裏(やねうら) is the space between the ceiling of the top floor and the roof. Composed of 屋根(やね) (roof) + 裏(うら) (back/underside).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 屋根裏(やねうら)部屋(へや): attic room (converted living space)
  • 屋根裏(やねうら)収納(しゅうのう): attic storage
  • 屋根裏(やねうら)に上(あ)がる: to go up to the attic
  • 屋根裏(やねうら)に荷物(にもつ)をしまう: to store things in the attic

USAGE:
In Japanese houses, 屋根裏(やねうら) is commonly used for storage. Modern houses sometimes convert the space into a ロフト (loft) for living use. In older traditional houses, 屋根裏(やねうら) may be quite spacious.

RELATED TERMS:

  • ロフト: loft (used living space under the roof)
  • 天井裏(てんじょううら): above the ceiling (similar concept, structural focus)
  • 地下室(ちかしつ): basement (opposite location in a building)
