多読(たどく)

たどく
noun
extensive reading; reading widely
1. extensive reading; reading a large quantity of material
A reading approach that emphasizes quantity and breadth, reading many books or texts rather than studying each one in depth. Widely used in language learning contexts to describe reading large amounts of comprehensible material for fluency development.
多読(たどく)で語彙(ごい)を増(ふ)やす。
Build vocabulary through extensive reading.
英語(えいご)の多読(たどく)を毎日(まいにち)続(つづ)けている。
I keep up my extensive reading in English every day.
先生(せんせい)は精読(せいどく)よりも多読(たどく)のほうが効果的(こうかてき)だと言(い)った。
The teacher said that extensive reading is more effective than intensive reading.

USAGE:
Often used with する as 多読(たどく)する. Frequently contrasted with 精読(せいどく) (intensive, careful reading). In language education, 多読(たどく) has become a well-known methodology where learners read large amounts of easy material to build fluency naturally.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多読(たどく)する (to read extensively)
  • 多読(たどく)法(ほう) (extensive reading method)
  • 英語(えいご)多読(たどく) (extensive reading in English)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精読(せいどく): intensive reading — reading carefully for full comprehension (opposite approach)
  • 乱読(らんどく): reading indiscriminately, without a plan or system
