非凡(ひぼん)

ひぼん
na-adjective, noun
extraordinary; exceptional; remarkable
1. extraordinary; exceptional; remarkable
Describes talent, ability, or quality that is clearly above the ordinary level. A somewhat literary or formal way to express admiration for someone's outstanding qualities.
彼(かれ)は非凡(ひぼん)な才能(さいのう)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
He possesses extraordinary talent.
幼(おさな)いころから非凡(ひぼん)さを見(み)せていた。
She showed exceptional ability from a young age.
その画家(がか)の作品(さくひん)には非凡(ひぼん)な感性(かんせい)が表(あらわ)れている。
That painter's works reflect a remarkable sensibility.

USAGE:
非凡(ひぼん) is the antonym of 平凡(へいぼん) (ordinary, mediocre). It is used to praise talent or ability that stands out clearly from the average. It has a somewhat formal or literary tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非凡(ひぼん)な才能(さいのう) (extraordinary talent)
  • 非凡(ひぼん)な能力(のうりょく) (exceptional ability)
  • 非凡(ひぼん)さを発揮(はっき)する (to demonstrate one's exceptional quality)

SIMILAR WORDS:
優秀(ゆうしゅう) means "excellent" in a more general, commonly used way. 非凡(ひぼん) specifically contrasts with the ordinary and implies innate or striking ability.
