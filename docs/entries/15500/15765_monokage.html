物陰(ものかげ)

ものかげ
noun
behind something; in the shadows; cover
1. behind something; in the shadow of an object; cover
The area hidden behind an object, where one cannot easily be seen. Often used to describe hiding or observing from behind objects. Composed of 物 (thing/object) + 陰 (shadow/shade).
物陰(ものかげ)に隠(かく)れる。
To hide behind something.
猫(ねこ)が物陰(ものかげ)から様子(ようす)をうかがっていた。
A cat was watching from behind cover.
不審(ふしん)な人物(じんぶつ)が物陰(ものかげ)に潜(ひそ)んでいるのを見(み)かけた。
I spotted a suspicious person lurking in the shadows.

USAGE:
物陰(ものかげ) refers to the concealed area behind any object — a wall, a tree, a pillar, etc. It is often associated with hiding, lurking, or watching secretly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物陰(ものかげ)に隠(かく)れる (hide behind something)
  • 物陰(ものかげ)に潜(ひそ)む (lurk in the shadows)
  • 物陰(ものかげ)から覗(のぞ)く (peek from behind something)
  • 物陰(ものかげ)から飛(と)び出(だ)す (jump out from behind something)

SIMILAR WORDS:
陰(かげ) alone means "shadow" or "shade" and is broader. 木陰(こかげ) specifically means "shade of a tree." 物陰(ものかげ) implies concealment behind a physical object.
