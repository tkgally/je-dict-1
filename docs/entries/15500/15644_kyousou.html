競走(きょうそう)

きょうそう
noun
race; footrace
1. race; footrace; running race
A competition in which participants race to see who is the fastest, typically on foot. Used for track events, horse racing, and other speed competitions.
百(ひゃく)メートル競走(きょうそう)に出場(しゅつじょう)した。
I competed in the 100-meter race.
運動会(うんどうかい)の競走(きょうそう)で一位(いちい)になった。
I came in first in the race at the sports day.
競走(きょうそう)馬(ば)のトレーニングは早朝(そうちょう)から始(はじ)まる。
Training of racehorses begins early in the morning.

USAGE:
競走(きょうそう) refers specifically to racing (a speed contest), while the homophone 競争(きょうそう) means competition in a broader sense (business, academic, etc.). 競走(きょうそう) is used for athletic races and animal racing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 競走(きょうそう)馬(ば) (racehorse)
  • 短距離(たんきょり)競走(きょうそう) (sprint race)
  • 長距離(ちょうきょり)競走(きょうそう) (long-distance race)
  • 徒競走(ときょうそう) (footrace, running race at school events)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 競争(きょうそう): competition (broader, not limited to racing)
  • 徒競走(ときょうそう): footrace (specifically at school sports days)
  • レース: race (loanword)
