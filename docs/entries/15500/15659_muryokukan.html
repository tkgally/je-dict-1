無力感(むりょくかん)

むりょくかん
noun
feeling of helplessness; sense of powerlessness
1. feeling of helplessness; sense of powerlessness
The emotional state of feeling unable to do anything or make a difference. Often experienced in the face of overwhelming circumstances.
無力感(むりょくかん)に襲(おそ)われた。
I was overcome by a feeling of helplessness.
何(なに)もできない無力感(むりょくかん)がつらい。
The helplessness of not being able to do anything is painful.
大(おお)きな災害(さいがい)の前(まえ)では無力感(むりょくかん)を感(かん)じずにはいられない。
In the face of a major disaster, you can't help but feel powerless.

USAGE:
Often used with verbs expressing being overwhelmed or experiencing emotions: 無力感(むりょくかん)に襲(おそ)われる, 無力感(むりょくかん)を感(かん)じる, 無力感(むりょくかん)に苛(さいな)まれる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無力感(むりょくかん)に襲(おそ)われる (to be overwhelmed by helplessness)
  • 無力感(むりょくかん)を感(かん)じる (to feel helpless)
  • 無力感(むりょくかん)に苛(さいな)まれる (to be tormented by helplessness)
  • 深(ふか)い無力感(むりょくかん) (deep sense of powerlessness)

WORD FORMATION:
無(む) (without) + 力(りょく) (power) + 感(かん) (feeling). The 〜感(かん) suffix creates emotion nouns from abstract concepts.
