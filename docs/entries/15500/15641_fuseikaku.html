不正確(ふせいかく)

ふせいかく
adjective
inaccurate; imprecise; incorrect
1. inaccurate; imprecise; incorrect
Not accurate or exact. Used to describe information, data, measurements, or statements that contain errors or lack precision.
この情報(じょうほう)は不正確(ふせいかく)だ。
This information is inaccurate.
不正確(ふせいかく)なデータに基(もと)づいた判断(はんだん)は危険(きけん)だ。
Judgments based on inaccurate data are dangerous.
翻訳(ほんやく)が不正確(ふせいかく)だったため、誤解(ごかい)が生(う)じた。
Because the translation was inaccurate, misunderstandings arose.

USAGE:
不正確(ふせいかく) is a na-adjective formed by adding the negative prefix 不(ふ) to 正確(せいかく) (accurate). It is used in formal and neutral contexts alike.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不正確(ふせいかく)な情報(じょうほう) (inaccurate information)
  • 不正確(ふせいかく)な記述(きじゅつ) (inaccurate description)
  • 不正確(ふせいかく)な翻訳(ほんやく) (inaccurate translation)
  • 不正確(ふせいかく)な数値(すうち) (imprecise figures)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正確(せいかく): accurate, precise (antonym)
  • 曖昧(あいまい): vague, ambiguous
  • 誤(あやま)り: error, mistake
