譲渡(じょうと)

じょうと
noun
transfer; assignment; conveyance
1. transfer; assignment; conveyance
The formal act of transferring ownership, rights, or property from one party to another. Used in legal, business, and financial contexts.
株式(かぶしき)を譲渡(じょうと)する。
Transfer the shares.
土地(とち)の譲渡(じょうと)には契約書(けいやくしょ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A contract is required for the transfer of land.
特許(とっきょ)の譲渡(じょうと)手続(てつづ)きが完了(かんりょう)し、新(あたら)しい所有者(しょゆうしゃ)に権利(けんり)が移(うつ)った。
The patent transfer procedure was completed, and the rights passed to the new owner.

USAGE:
譲渡(じょうと) is a formal, legal term. It is used for official transfers of property, rights, shares, or assets. In casual speech, 譲(ゆず)る (to hand over, to yield) is preferred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 譲渡(じょうと)所得(しょとく) (capital gains from transfer)
  • 譲渡(じょうと)契約(けいやく) (transfer agreement)
  • 株式(かぶしき)譲渡(じょうと) (share transfer)
  • 権利(けんり)の譲渡(じょうと) (assignment of rights)
  • 譲渡(じょうと)益(えき) (transfer profit)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 譲(ゆず)る: to hand over, to yield (general, less formal)
  • 移転(いてん): transfer, relocation (broader usage)
