速(すみ)やかに

すみやかに
adverb
promptly; quickly; without delay
1. promptly; quickly; without delay
Indicates that an action should be carried out swiftly and without unnecessary delay. A formal, written-register word commonly found in official notices, instructions, and business communication.
速(すみ)やかに対応(たいおう)してください。
Please respond promptly.
問題(もんだい)が発生(はっせい)したら速(すみ)やかに報告(ほうこく)すること。
If a problem occurs, report it immediately.
災害(さいがい)時(じ)には速(すみ)やかに指定(してい)された避難(ひなん)場所(ばしょ)へ移動(いどう)してください。
In case of a disaster, move promptly to the designated evacuation site.

USAGE:
速(すみ)やかに is the adverbial form of the na-adjective 速(すみ)やか. It is a formal word primarily used in written instructions, official announcements, and business contexts. In everyday speech, すぐに or 早(はや)く are more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 速(すみ)やかに対応(たいおう)する (respond promptly)
  • 速(すみ)やかに報告(ほうこく)する (report without delay)
  • 速(すみ)やかに行動(こうどう)する (act quickly)
  • 速(すみ)やかに退去(たいきょ)する (evacuate promptly)

SIMILAR WORDS:
すぐに is the everyday word for 'immediately.' 直(ただ)ちに is another formal alternative meaning 'at once' and is even more urgent in tone. 速(すみ)やかに falls between the two in urgency and is the standard choice in formal written instructions.

Related Words

Synonym: すぐに (immediately; everyday)
Synonym: 直(ただ)ちに (at once; more urgent)
