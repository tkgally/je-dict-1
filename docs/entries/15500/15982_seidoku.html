精読(せいどく)

せいどく
noun
intensive reading; close reading
1. intensive reading; careful, thorough reading
Reading a text slowly and carefully, paying close attention to every detail of meaning, grammar, and vocabulary. Often involves looking up unknown words and analyzing sentence structure.
この論文(ろんぶん)は精読(せいどく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
This paper requires careful reading.
古典(こてん)を精読(せいどく)して理解(りかい)を深(ふか)めた。
I deepened my understanding by reading the classic text closely.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)に教科書(きょうかしょ)を精読(せいどく)して、重要(じゅうよう)なポイントをノートにまとめた。
Before the exam, I read the textbook carefully and summarized the key points in my notes.

USAGE:
Used with する as 精読(せいどく)する. Commonly contrasted with 多読(たどく) (extensive reading) and 速読(そくどく) (speed reading). In academic and language learning contexts, 精読(せいどく) emphasizes deep comprehension over quantity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精読(せいどく)する (to read carefully/intensively)
  • 原文(げんぶん)を精読(せいどく)する (to read the original text closely)
  • 精読(せいどく)と多読(たどく) (intensive and extensive reading)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 多読(たどく): extensive reading — reading widely for quantity (opposite approach)
  • 熟読(じゅくどく): reading carefully and thoroughly, very similar to 精読(せいどく) but emphasizes repeated or deep engagement
