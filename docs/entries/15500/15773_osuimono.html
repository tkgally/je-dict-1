お吸(す)い物(もの)

おすいもの
noun
clear soup
1. clear soup; clear broth
A traditional Japanese soup made with dashi broth, lightly seasoned with soy sauce and salt. Unlike miso soup, it has a clear, transparent broth. Typically served in a lacquered bowl as part of a formal or semi-formal Japanese meal.
お吸(す)い物(もの)に三(み)つ葉(ば)を添(そ)えた。
I garnished the clear soup with mitsuba.
今日(きょう)の夕食(ゆうしょく)はお吸(す)い物(もの)と焼(や)き魚(ざかな)にしよう。
Let's have clear soup and grilled fish for dinner tonight.
料亭(りょうてい)で出(だ)されたお吸(す)い物(もの)は、出汁(だし)の香(かお)りが上品(じょうひん)で感動(かんどう)した。
The clear soup served at the traditional restaurant had such an elegant dashi aroma that it moved me.

USAGE:
お吸(す)い物(もの) is the polite form of 吸(す)い物(もの). The お prefix is almost always used in everyday speech. It is a standard part of Japanese washoku cuisine and commonly served at formal meals, celebrations, and traditional restaurants.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お吸(す)い物(もの)を作(つく)る (make clear soup)
  • 松茸(まつたけ)のお吸(す)い物(もの) (matsutake mushroom clear soup)
  • はまぐりのお吸(す)い物(もの) (clam clear soup — traditional at Hinamatsuri)

SIMILAR WORDS:
味噌汁(みそしる) is miso soup, which uses fermented soybean paste and is opaque. お吸(す)い物(もの) uses a clear dashi broth and is considered more refined. 汁物(しるもの) is a general term for any soup served as part of a Japanese meal.
