団結(だんけつ)

だんけつ
noun
unity; solidarity; union
1. unity; solidarity; union; banding together
People coming together and cooperating for a common purpose, especially in the face of adversity or to achieve a shared goal.
チームの団結(だんけつ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Team unity is important.
住民(じゅうみん)が団結(だんけつ)して反対(はんたい)運動(うんどう)を起(お)こした。
The residents banded together and launched a protest movement.
困難(こんなん)な状況(じょうきょう)の中(なか)で、社員(しゃいん)たちの団結(だんけつ)力(りょく)が試(ため)された。
In the difficult situation, the employees' solidarity was put to the test.

USAGE:
Used both as a noun and as a suru-verb (団結(だんけつ)する). Common in contexts involving labor unions, community action, sports teams, and political movements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 団結(だんけつ)する (to unite, to band together)
  • 団結(だんけつ)力(りょく) (solidarity, power of unity)
  • 団結(だんけつ)を固(かた)める (to strengthen unity)
  • 団結(だんけつ)権(けん) (right to organize — a labor rights term)
