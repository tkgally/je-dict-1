父方(ちちかた)

ちちかた
noun
paternal side; father's side (of the family)
1. paternal side; father's side of the family
Refers to relatives, lineage, or characteristics coming from the father's side of the family. Used when distinguishing between maternal and paternal family lines.
父方(ちちかた)の祖母(そぼ)に会(あ)った。
I met my paternal grandmother.
父方(ちちかた)の親戚(しんせき)は九州(きゅうしゅう)に多(おお)い。
Most of my paternal relatives live in Kyushu.
父方(ちちかた)の家系(かけい)には医者(いしゃ)が多(おお)いと聞(き)いている。
I've heard that there are many doctors in my father's side of the family.

USAGE:
父方(ちちかた) is used to specify the father's side when discussing family relationships, especially grandparents, aunts, uncles, and cousins. It is typically used as a modifier before a family term: 父方(ちちかた)の祖父(そふ) (paternal grandfather), 父方(ちちかた)の叔父(おじ) (paternal uncle).

CONTRAST:
父方(ちちかた) vs. 母方(ははかた) (maternal side) — these form a natural pair for distinguishing which side of the family is being discussed.

Related Words

Antonym: 母方(ははかた) (maternal side)
