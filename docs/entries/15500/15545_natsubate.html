夏(なつ)バテ

なつばて
noun / verb (suru)
summer heat fatigue; summer lethargy
1. summer heat fatigue; summer lethargy
A state of physical exhaustion, loss of appetite, and general malaise caused by prolonged exposure to summer heat and humidity. A common condition in Japan's hot and humid summers. Composed of 夏 (summer) + バテる (to be worn out).
夏(なつ)バテで食欲(しょくよく)がない。
I have no appetite due to summer fatigue.
夏(なつ)バテしないように、しっかり食(た)べよう。
Let's eat well so we don't get summer fatigue.
毎年(まいとし)八月(はちがつ)になると夏(なつ)バテして、何(なに)もやる気(き)が起(お)きなくなる。
Every year when August comes, I get summer fatigue and lose all motivation.

USAGE:
Can function as a noun (夏(なつ)バテ) or as a する verb (夏(なつ)バテする). Symptoms include loss of appetite, fatigue, and difficulty sleeping. Very common topic in Japanese summer conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夏(なつ)バテする (to suffer from summer fatigue)
  • 夏(なつ)バテ防止(ぼうし) / 予防(よぼう) (preventing summer fatigue)
  • 夏(なつ)バテ対策(たいさく) (measures against summer fatigue)
  • 夏(なつ)バテ気味(ぎみ) (a touch of summer fatigue)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan's humid summers make 夏(なつ)バテ a widely shared experience. Traditional remedies include eating 鰻(うなぎ) (eel) on 土用(どよう)の丑(うし)の日(ひ) (midsummer day of the ox) and consuming 梅干(うめぼ)し (pickled plums) for energy.
