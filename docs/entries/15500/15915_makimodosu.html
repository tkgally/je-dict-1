巻(ま)き戻(もど)す

まきもどす
verb, godan
to rewind
1. to rewind; to wind back
To wind something back to an earlier position. Originally used for tape and film, now also used figuratively for going back in time or reversing progress in digital contexts.
テープを巻(ま)き戻(もど)す。
I rewind the tape.
動画(どうが)を少(すこ)し巻(ま)き戻(もど)してもう一度(いちど)見(み)た。
I rewound the video a little and watched it again.
時間(じかん)を巻(ま)き戻(もど)すことができたら、違(ちが)う選択(せんたく)をしていただろう。
If I could turn back time, I would have made a different choice.

TRANSITIVITY: 巻(ま)き戻(もど)す is transitive.

ASPECT (ている): 巻(ま)き戻(もど)している describes the action of rewinding in progress.

USAGE: Originally a physical action (rewinding cassette tapes, film reels), 巻(ま)き戻(もど)す is now commonly used for digital media and figuratively for turning back time.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [tape/video/time]を巻(ま)き戻(もど)す (to rewind something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • テープを巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to rewind a tape
  • 時間(じかん)を巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to turn back time
  • 動画(どうが)を巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to rewind a video
