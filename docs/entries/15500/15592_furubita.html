古(ふる)びた

ふるびた
pre-noun adjectival
old-looking; aged; worn
1. old-looking; aged; worn; weathered
Describes something that shows visible signs of age or wear. Used as a pre-noun modifier (the past-tense form of the verb 古(ふる)びる, functioning adjectivally).
古(ふる)びた本(ほん)を見(み)つけた。
I found an old-looking book.
古(ふる)びた建物(たてもの)が並(なら)ぶ通(とお)りを歩(ある)いた。
I walked along a street lined with weathered buildings.
祖父(そふ)の家(いえ)には古(ふる)びた家具(かぐ)がたくさん残(のこ)っている。
My grandfather's house still has a lot of worn old furniture.

USAGE:
古(ふる)びた is the past-tense form of the verb 古(ふる)びる (to become old-looking), used as a pre-noun modifier. It describes objects that have taken on an aged appearance through the passage of time.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 古(ふる)びた建物(たてもの) (a weathered building)
  • 古(ふる)びた写真(しゃしん) (an old-looking photo)
  • 古(ふる)びた街並(まちな)み (an aged townscape)

NUANCE:
古(ふる)びた is neutral — it can suggest charm and character (like antiques) or simply describe something showing wear. Compare with 古(ふる)い, which simply means "old" without emphasizing the visual impression of aging.
