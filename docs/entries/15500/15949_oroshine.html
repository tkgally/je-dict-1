卸値(おろしね)

おろしね
noun
wholesale price
1. wholesale price
The price at which goods are sold from a wholesaler to a retailer, before the retail markup. Lower than the retail price that consumers pay.
卸値(おろしね)で仕入(しい)れた。
I purchased it at wholesale price.
この商品(しょうひん)の卸値(おろしね)は小売(こうり)価格(かかく)の半分(はんぶん)くらいだ。
The wholesale price of this product is about half the retail price.
原材料(げんざいりょう)の高騰(こうとう)で卸値(おろしね)が上(あ)がり、小売店(こうりてん)も値上(ねあ)げせざるを得(え)なくなった。
Wholesale prices rose due to soaring raw material costs, forcing retailers to raise prices as well.

USAGE:
卸値(おろしね) is a commerce term used in business and trade. It is sometimes encountered in everyday speech when shops advertise products at wholesale prices to attract customers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 卸値(おろしね)で売(う)る (to sell at wholesale price)
  • 卸値(おろしね)で仕入(しい)れる (to purchase at wholesale price)
  • 卸値(おろしね)が上(あ)がる (wholesale prices rise)

RELATED WORDS:
小売(こうり)価格(かかく) means retail price. 卸売(おろしうり) means wholesale (the business of selling in bulk), and 卸(おろし) alone can refer to wholesale trade.
