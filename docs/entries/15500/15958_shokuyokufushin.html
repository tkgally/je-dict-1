食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)

しょくよくふしん
noun
loss of appetite; poor appetite
1. loss of appetite; poor appetite
A condition where one has little or no desire to eat. Commonly used in medical and health contexts. Composed of 食欲(しょくよく) (appetite) + 不振(ふしん) (poor performance, slump).
食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)が続(つづ)いている。
My loss of appetite has been continuing.
ストレスが原因(げんいん)で食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)になった。
I lost my appetite due to stress.
食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)や不眠(ふみん)などの症状(しょうじょう)が出(で)たら、早(はや)めに医師(いし)に相談(そうだん)してください。
If you experience symptoms such as loss of appetite or insomnia, please consult a doctor promptly.

USAGE:
A formal medical or health term. Often listed alongside other symptoms when describing illness or side effects. Can be used as a predicate with になる or が続(つづ)く.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)になる (to lose one's appetite)
  • 食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)が続(つづ)く (loss of appetite continues)
  • 食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)の症状(しょうじょう) (symptoms of poor appetite)
  • 食欲不振(しょくよくふしん)を訴(うった)える (to report loss of appetite)

ETYMOLOGY:
食欲(しょくよく) (appetite) + 不振(ふしん) (slump, poor performance).
