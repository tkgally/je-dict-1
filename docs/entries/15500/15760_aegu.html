喘(あえ)ぐ

あえぐ
verb
to gasp; to pant; to struggle
1. to gasp; to pant; to breathe hard
To breathe with difficulty, as from physical exertion, pain, or illness.
坂(さか)を上(のぼ)って喘(あえ)いでいる。
I'm panting from climbing the hill.
猛暑(もうしょ)の中(なか)、選手(せんしゅ)たちは喘(あえ)ぎながら走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
In the intense heat, the athletes kept running while gasping for breath.
高熱(こうねつ)で苦(くる)しそうに喘(あえ)いでいた。
She was gasping in distress from a high fever.
2. to struggle; to suffer under hardship
To be in a state of difficulty or distress, struggling under the weight of problems such as poverty, debt, or oppression. A figurative extension of the physical gasping sense.
不景気(ふけいき)に喘(あえ)ぐ企業(きぎょう)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of companies struggling under the recession is increasing.
借金(しゃっきん)に喘(あえ)ぐ生活(せいかつ)から抜(ぬ)け出(だ)したい。
I want to escape a life of struggling under debt.
人手(ひとで)不足(ぶそく)に喘(あえ)ぐ医療(いりょう)現場(げんば)の声(こえ)が届(とど)いていない。
The voices of medical workplaces struggling with staff shortages are not being heard.

USAGE:
喘(あえ)ぐ is a godan verb. In sense 2, the pattern ～に喘(あえ)ぐ (to struggle under ~) is very common in news and formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 息(いき)を切(き)らして喘(あえ)ぐ (gasp out of breath)
  • 暑(あつ)さに喘(あえ)ぐ (gasp in the heat)
  • 貧困(ひんこん)に喘(あえ)ぐ (struggle under poverty)
  • 借金(しゃっきん)に喘(あえ)ぐ (be crushed by debt)
  • 不況(ふきょう)に喘(あえ)ぐ (suffer from recession)

SIMILAR WORDS:
はあはあする is an onomatopoeic expression for panting, more casual and physical. 息切(いきぎ)れする means "to become out of breath." 苦(くる)しむ means "to suffer" and is broader in meaning than the figurative sense of 喘(あえ)ぐ.
