欲張(よくば)り

よくばり
noun / na-adjective
greedy; greedy person
1. greedy; greedy person
Describes someone who wants more than their fair share, or the quality of being greedy. Used both as a noun referring to a greedy person and as a na-adjective meaning greedy.
あの子(こ)は欲張(よくば)りだ。
That kid is greedy.
欲張(よくば)りな人(ひと)は嫌(きら)われる。
Greedy people are disliked.
もう十分(じゅうぶん)もらったのに、まだ欲張(よくば)りなことを言(い)っている。
Even though you've already received plenty, you're still saying greedy things.

USAGE:
欲張(よくば)り can function as both a noun (a greedy person) and a na-adjective (欲張(よくば)りな). It carries a mildly negative connotation but is common in everyday speech, including when teasing someone lightheartedly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 欲張(よくば)りな人(ひと) (a greedy person)
  • 欲張(よくば)りなことを言(い)う (to say greedy things)
  • 欲張(よくば)りすぎる (to be too greedy)

RELATED WORDS:
The verb 欲張(よくば)る means "to be greedy" or "to want too much." 貪欲(どんよく) is a more formal and stronger term for greed.
