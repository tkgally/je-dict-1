雑多(ざった)な

ざったな
na-adjective
miscellaneous; varied; jumbled
1. miscellaneous; varied; mixed together
Describes a collection of different, often unrelated things mixed together without clear organization. Can be neutral or slightly negative depending on context.
雑多(ざった)なものが並(なら)んでいる。
A miscellaneous assortment of things is lined up.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に雑多(ざった)な書類(しょるい)が積(つ)まれていた。
A jumble of documents was piled on the desk.
この商店街(しょうてんがい)には雑多(ざった)な店(みせ)が集(あつ)まっていて、歩(ある)くだけで楽(たの)しい。
This shopping street has a varied mix of shops, and it's fun just to walk around.

USAGE:
Describes a disorganized or heterogeneous mixture. While it can sound negative (implying mess or clutter), it can also convey a lively, eclectic atmosphere when describing markets, neighborhoods, or collections.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雑多(ざった)なもの (miscellaneous things)
  • 雑多(ざった)な情報(じょうほう) (assorted information)
  • 雑多(ざった)な人々(ひとびと) (a diverse crowd of people)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 多種多様(たしゅたよう)な: diverse, of many kinds — more positive, emphasizing variety
  • 雑然(ざつぜん)とした: cluttered, disorderly — focuses on messiness of physical space
