完敗(かんぱい)

かんぱい
noun
complete defeat; total loss
1. complete defeat; total loss; utter defeat
A thorough, decisive defeat with no redeeming factors. Often used in sports and competitions.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)は完敗(かんぱい)だった。
Today's match was a complete defeat.
相手(あいて)チームに完敗(かんぱい)を喫(きっ)した。
We suffered a total defeat at the hands of the opposing team.
選挙(せんきょ)で完敗(かんぱい)した候補者(こうほしゃ)は引退(いんたい)を表明(ひょうめい)した。
The candidate who suffered a crushing defeat in the election announced their retirement.

USAGE:
Often used with 喫(きっ)する (to suffer/sustain) in the formal expression 完敗(かんぱい)を喫(きっ)する. Common in sports reporting and political commentary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完敗(かんぱい)を喫(きっ)する (to suffer a complete defeat)
  • 完敗(かんぱい)に終(お)わる (to end in total defeat)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 惨敗(ざんぱい): miserable defeat (emphasizes how devastating it was)
  • 大敗(たいはい): major defeat (large-scale loss)
  • 完勝(かんしょう): complete victory (the opposite)
