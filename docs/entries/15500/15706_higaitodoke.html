被害届(ひがいとどけ)

ひがいとどけ
noun
damage report; report to the police
1. damage report; crime report; report filed with the police
A formal report submitted to the police notifying them that one has been the victim of a crime or has suffered damage or loss. Filing this report is the first step in a police investigation.
警察(けいさつ)に被害届(ひがいとどけ)を出(だ)した。
I filed a report with the police.
盗難(とうなん)に遭(あ)ったので、すぐに被害届(ひがいとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I was the victim of theft, so I immediately filed a police report.
被害届(ひがいとどけ)を出(だ)さないと、保険(ほけん)の請求(せいきゅう)ができない場合(ばあい)がある。
In some cases, you cannot file an insurance claim without submitting a police report.

USAGE:
Filing a 被害届(ひがいとどけ) at a police station is an important practical step in Japan when one is a victim of crime or property damage. It does not automatically trigger a criminal investigation but is often necessary for insurance claims and official records.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 被害届(ひがいとどけ)を出(だ)す (to file a police report)
  • 被害届(ひがいとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit a police report, formal)
  • 被害届(ひがいとどけ)を受理(じゅり)する (to accept a police report)
  • 被害届(ひがいとどけ)を取(と)り下(さ)げる (to withdraw a police report)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 告訴(こくそ): criminal complaint (stronger, formally requesting prosecution)
  • 届出(とどけで): notification, report (general administrative term)
