身動(みうご)き

みうごき
noun
moving one's body; stirring
1. bodily movement; stirring; moving around
Physical movement of one's body. Most commonly used in the negative form to describe being unable to move due to being trapped, crowded, or otherwise constrained.
身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない。
I can't move.
満員(まんいん)電車(でんしゃ)で身動(みうご)きが取(と)れなかった。
I couldn't move on the packed train.
借金(しゃっきん)が増(ふ)えすぎて、経済的(けいざいてき)に身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない状態(じょうたい)になってしまった。
The debt piled up so much that I ended up in a situation where I couldn't move financially.

USAGE: 身動(みうご)き is almost always used in the negative pattern 身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない (unable to move). It can describe physical inability to move (in a crowd, when restrained) or figurative inability to act (due to financial problems, obligations, etc.).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない: unable to move, stuck
  • 身動(みうご)きできない: unable to stir
  • 身動(みうご)き一(ひと)つしない: not moving a muscle

The word is a compound of 身(み) (body) and 動(うご)き (movement).
