冬眠(とうみん)

とうみん
noun
hibernation; winter sleep
1. hibernation; winter sleep
The state of dormancy that certain animals enter during winter to conserve energy, characterized by lowered body temperature and reduced metabolism.
熊(くま)は冬(ふゆ)になると冬眠(とうみん)する。
Bears hibernate when winter comes.
冬眠(とうみん)中(ちゅう)の動物(どうぶつ)は心拍数(しんぱくすう)が大幅(おおはば)に下(さ)がる。
The heart rate of hibernating animals drops significantly.
蛙(かえる)は土(つち)の中(なか)で冬眠(とうみん)して春(はる)を待(ま)つ。
Frogs hibernate underground and wait for spring.
2. being inactive; lying dormant (figurative)
Used figuratively for a person or organization that is inactive or idle for an extended period.
冬(ふゆ)は冬眠(とうみん)したいくらい寒(さむ)い。
It's so cold in winter I feel like hibernating.
あのバンドは長(なが)い冬眠(とうみん)から目覚(めざ)めた。
That band has awoken from a long hibernation.
冬眠(とうみん)状態(じょうたい)だったプロジェクトがようやく動(うご)き出(だ)した。
The project that had been in hibernation finally started moving.

GRAMMAR:
冬眠(とうみん)する is used as a する verb meaning "to hibernate."

COMPOSITION:
冬(とう) (winter) + 眠(みん) (sleep).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冬眠(とうみん)に入(はい)る (to enter hibernation)
  • 冬眠(とうみん)から覚(さ)める / 目覚(めざ)める (to wake from hibernation)
  • 冬眠(とうみん)中(ちゅう) (during hibernation)
