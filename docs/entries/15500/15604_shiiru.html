強(し)いる

しいる
verb-ichidan
to force; to compel; to coerce
1. to force; to compel; to coerce
To make someone do something against their will, or to impose something unwanted on someone.
無理(むり)を強(し)いてはいけない。
You shouldn't force someone to do the impossible.
社員(しゃいん)に残業(ざんぎょう)を強(し)いる会社(かいしゃ)は問題(もんだい)だ。
Companies that force employees to work overtime are problematic.
住民(じゅうみん)は長期間(ちょうきかん)にわたって不便(ふべん)な生活(せいかつ)を強(し)いられた。
The residents were forced to endure inconvenient living conditions for a long period.

GRAMMAR:
強(し)いる is an ichidan verb despite ending in いる. The passive form 強(し)いられる is extremely common and often more natural than the active form.

PATTERNS:

  • AにBを強(し)いる (to force B on A)
  • 強(し)いられる (to be forced/compelled — very common passive)
  • 強(し)いて言(い)えば (if forced to say; if I had to say — set phrase)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 犠牲(ぎせい)を強(し)いる (to demand sacrifice)
  • 我慢(がまん)を強(し)いる (to force someone to endure)
  • 負担(ふたん)を強(し)いる (to impose a burden)

SIMILAR WORDS:
強制(きょうせい)する is a suru-verb meaning to compel or enforce, often used for rules or systems. 強(し)いる is used more for interpersonal situations or abstract impositions.
