具現(ぐげん)

ぐげん
noun
embodiment; realization; incarnation
1. embodiment; realization; incarnation; materialization
The act of giving concrete form to an abstract idea, ideal, or concept. Often used in formal or literary contexts to describe making something tangible.
理想(りそう)を具現(ぐげん)する。
Realize an ideal.
このデザインは自然(しぜん)との共生(きょうせい)を具現(ぐげん)したものだ。
This design is an embodiment of coexistence with nature.
彼(かれ)の作品(さくひん)は平和(へいわ)への願(ねが)いを具現(ぐげん)しており、多(おお)くの人(ひと)の心(こころ)を動(うご)かした。
His work embodies a wish for peace and has moved many people.

USAGE:
具現(ぐげん) is a formal, literary word. It is often used with する to form a verb meaning "to embody" or "to realize in concrete form." The related form 具現化(ぐげんか) (making concrete, materialization) is also common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 具現(ぐげん)する (to embody, to realize)
  • 具現化(ぐげんか)する (to materialize, to make concrete)
  • 理想(りそう)の具現(ぐげん) (embodiment of an ideal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 体現(たいげん): embodiment (through one's own body or actions)
  • 実現(じつげん): realization, achievement (more general and common)
  • 具体化(ぐたいか): making concrete (less literary)
