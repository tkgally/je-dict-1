1. intermediary; medium; go-between
Something or someone that serves as an intermediary or connecting agent between two parties or things. Used both for human mediators and abstract media of transmission.
友人(ゆうじん)の媒介(ばいかい)で知(し)り合(あ)った。
We met through a friend's introduction.
インターネットが情報(じょうほう)の媒介(ばいかい)として大(おお)きな役割(やくわり)を果(は)たしている。
The internet plays a major role as a medium for information.
言葉(ことば)は思想(しそう)を伝(つた)える媒介(ばいかい)である。
Language is a medium for conveying ideas.
2. vector; carrier (of disease)
In scientific and medical contexts, an organism that transmits a pathogen from one host to another, such as mosquitoes carrying malaria.
蚊(か)がマラリアを媒介(ばいかい)する。
Mosquitoes carry malaria.
この病気(びょうき)はダニに媒介(ばいかい)される。
This disease is transmitted by ticks.
感染症(かんせんしょう)の媒介(ばいかい)動物(どうぶつ)を特定(とくてい)することが予防(よぼう)の第一歩(だいいっぽ)だ。
Identifying the animal vectors of infectious disease is the first step in prevention.

USAGE:
Used both as a noun and with する as 媒介(ばいかい)する (to mediate, to transmit). Sense 1 is used in general and academic contexts. Sense 2 is primarily scientific/medical.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 媒介(ばいかい)する (to mediate; to transmit)
  • 媒介(ばいかい)者(しゃ) (intermediary, mediator)
  • 媒介(ばいかい)動物(どうぶつ) (vector animal)
  • 蚊(か)が媒介(ばいかい)する (mosquitoes transmit)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仲介(ちゅうかい): mediation, brokerage — focuses on mediating between people, especially in business
  • 媒体(ばいたい): medium, carrier — focuses on the material or channel itself (print media, digital media)

Related Words

Synonym: 仲介(ちゅうかい) (mediation (business))
