上体(じょうたい)

じょうたい
noun
upper body, torso
1. upper body, upper torso
The part of the body above the waist, including the chest, back, and arms. Commonly used in exercise, sports, and physical descriptions.
上体(じょうたい)を起(お)こす。
To raise one's upper body.
上体(じょうたい)を前(まえ)に倒(たお)す。
To bend the upper body forward.
上体(じょうたい)をひねるストレッチをした。
I did a stretch where I twisted my upper body.
ボクシングでは上体(じょうたい)の動(うご)きがとても大切(たいせつ)だ。
In boxing, upper body movement is very important.

上体(じょうたい) refers to the upper part of the body from the waist up, including the chest, back, shoulders, and arms. It appears most often in fitness, sports, and physical therapy contexts where specific movements of the torso are described, and is more technical than the near-synonym 上半身(じょうはんしん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上体(じょうたい)を起(お)こす (to raise one's upper body)
  • 上体(じょうたい)を倒(たお)す (to bend the upper body forward)
  • 上体(じょうたい)をひねる (to twist the upper body)
  • 上体(じょうたい)を反(そ)らす (to arch the upper body backward)
  • 上体(じょうたい)起(お)こし (sit-ups)

USAGE:

  • Commonly used in exercise instructions and sports commentary
  • Focuses on the torso as a unit that moves, bends, or rotates
  • 上半身(じょうはんしん) is a broader anatomical term; 上体(じょうたい) emphasizes the torso in motion

RELATED WORDS:

  • 上半身(じょうはんしん) (upper half of the body — broader synonym)
  • 下半身(かはんしん) (lower body)
  • 全身(ぜんしん) (whole body)
