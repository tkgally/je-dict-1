ほうじ茶(ちゃ)

ほうじちゃ
noun
roasted green tea
1. roasted green tea; houjicha
A Japanese green tea made by roasting tea leaves at high temperatures. It has a distinctive brown color, toasty aroma, and mild flavor with low caffeine content.
食後(しょくご)にほうじ茶(ちゃ)を飲(の)む。
I drink houjicha after meals.
ほうじ茶(ちゃ)はカフェインが少(すく)ないので夜(よる)でも飲(の)める。
Houjicha is low in caffeine, so you can drink it even at night.
最近(さいきん)はほうじ茶(ちゃ)ラテやほうじ茶(ちゃ)アイスなど、ほうじ茶(ちゃ)味(あじ)のスイーツが人気(にんき)だ。
Recently, houjicha-flavored sweets like houjicha lattes and houjicha ice cream have become popular.

Can also be written as 焙(ほう)じ茶(ちゃ), though the hiragana form is more common. One of the main types of Japanese tea alongside 煎茶(せんちゃ) (sencha), 玉露(ぎょくろ) (gyokuro), 抹茶(まっちゃ) (matcha), and 番茶(ばんちゃ) (bancha). Its low caffeine and gentle flavor make it popular with children and the elderly. In recent years, ほうじ茶(ちゃ) has become a trendy flavor for lattes, ice cream, and other sweets.
