非営利(ひえいり)

ひえいり
noun, no-adjective
non-profit; not-for-profit
1. non-profit; not-for-profit
Not conducted for the purpose of making a profit. Used to describe organizations, activities, and projects that reinvest any revenue into their mission rather than distributing it as profit.
非営利(ひえいり)団体(だんたい)で働(はたら)いている。
I work at a non-profit organization.
この活動(かつどう)は非営利(ひえいり)目的(もくてき)で行(おこな)われている。
This activity is conducted on a non-profit basis.
非営利(ひえいり)の法人(ほうじん)を設立(せつりつ)するには、行政(ぎょうせい)の認可(にんか)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To establish a non-profit corporation, you need government approval.

Compound of 非(ひ) (non-) + 営利(えいり) (profit-making). The most common context is NPO (非営利(ひえいり)団体(だんたい)), the Japanese equivalent of a non-profit organization.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非営利(ひえいり)団体(だんたい) — non-profit organization (NPO)
  • 非営利(ひえいり)法人(ほうじん) — non-profit corporation
  • 非営利(ひえいり)活動(かつどう) — non-profit activities
  • 非営利(ひえいり)目的(もくてき) — non-profit purpose
  • NPO法人(ほうじん) — NPO corporation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 営利(えいり) — profit-making (opposite)
  • 公益(こうえき) — public interest, public benefit
  • ボランティア — volunteer (related concept but refers to individuals)
