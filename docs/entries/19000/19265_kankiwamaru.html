1. to be overcome with emotion; to be deeply moved
To experience such intense emotion — joy, gratitude, sadness, or nostalgia — that one is brought to tears or speechlessness. Describes reaching the peak of emotional intensity.
感(かん)極(きわ)まって涙(なみだ)が出(で)た。
I was so moved that tears came to my eyes.
選手(せんしゅ)は優勝(ゆうしょう)して感(かん)極(きわ)まった様子(ようす)だった。
The athlete appeared overcome with emotion after winning the championship.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)で恩師(おんし)の言葉(ことば)に感(かん)極(きわ)まり、声(こえ)が出(で)なくなった。
At the graduation ceremony, I was so deeply moved by my mentor's words that I couldn't speak.

A literary expression meaning to reach the peak (極(きわ)まる) of emotion (感(かん)). Describes moments of overwhelming feeling, typically positive (joy, gratitude, pride) but can also describe deep sadness.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 感(かん)極(きわ)まって涙(なみだ)を流(なが)す: to shed tears of deep emotion
  • 感(かん)極(きわ)まった表情(ひょうじょう): a face overcome with emotion
  • 感(かん)極(きわ)まり声(こえ)を詰(つ)まらせる: to choke up with emotion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 感動(かんどう)する: to be moved, to be touched — more general and milder
  • 胸(むね)がいっぱいになる: to have one's heart full — similar emotional intensity but more colloquial

REGISTER:
Somewhat literary/formal. Common in news reports, speeches, and written narratives rather than casual conversation.
