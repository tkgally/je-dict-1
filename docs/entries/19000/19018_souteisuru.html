1. to assume, to suppose, to envision, to hypothesize
To consider a particular situation or scenario as a possibility, especially for planning or preparation purposes. Common in formal, technical, and disaster-preparedness contexts.
最悪(さいあく)の事態(じたい)を想定(そうてい)する。
To assume the worst-case scenario.
想定(そうてい)していなかった問題(もんだい)が起(お)きた。
An unforeseen problem occurred.
地震(じしん)を想定(そうてい)した避難(ひなん)訓練(くんれん)が行(おこな)われた。
An evacuation drill assuming an earthquake was carried out.

USAGE:
A formal word frequently used in news, business, and official documents. The noun form 想定(そうてい) is also very common: 想定(そうてい)外(がい) (beyond expectations) became widely known after the 2011 earthquake and nuclear disaster.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 想定(そうてい)外(がい): beyond assumptions, unexpected
  • 想定(そうてい)内(ない): within expectations
  • ～を想定(そうてい)した訓練(くんれん): drill assuming ~
  • 想定(そうてい)される被害(ひがい): anticipated damage
