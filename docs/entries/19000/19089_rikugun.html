陸軍(りくぐん)

りくぐん
noun
army; ground forces
1. army; land forces; ground forces
The branch of a country's military that conducts operations on land.
陸軍(りくぐん)に入隊(にゅうたい)した。
He enlisted in the army.
その国(くに)は陸軍(りくぐん)の兵力(へいりょく)を増強(ぞうきょう)した。
That country strengthened its army.
第二次世界大戦(だいにじせかいたいせん)では、日本(にほん)の陸軍(りくぐん)と海軍(かいぐん)が別々(べつべつ)に作戦(さくせん)を立(た)てていた。
During World War II, the Japanese army and navy planned operations separately.

One of the three main branches of the military alongside 海軍(かいぐん) (navy) and 空軍(くうぐん) (air force). In modern Japan, the equivalent is 陸上自衛隊(りくじょうじえいたい) (Ground Self-Defense Force), since Japan's postwar constitution prohibits maintaining traditional armed forces. The term 陸軍(りくぐん) is still used when referring to other countries' armies or to the historical Imperial Japanese Army (大日本帝国陸軍(だいにっぽんていこくりくぐん)).
