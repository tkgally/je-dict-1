子馬(こうま)

こうま
noun
foal; pony; young horse
1. foal; pony; young horse
A young horse, typically one that has not yet reached maturity. Can refer to both colts (male) and fillies (female).
子馬(こうま)が生(う)まれた。
A foal was born.
子馬(こうま)が母馬(ははうま)のそばを走(はし)っている。
The foal is running alongside its mother.
牧場(ぼくじょう)で子馬(こうま)に触(さわ)ることができて、子(こ)どもたちは大喜(おおよろこ)びだった。
The children were thrilled to be able to touch the foals at the ranch.

Compound of 子(こ) (young, small) + 馬(うま) (horse). Follows the common pattern of 子(こ) + animal to indicate a young animal.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 子犬(こいぬ) — puppy
  • 子猫(こねこ) — kitten
  • 子牛(こうし) — calf
  • 親馬(おやうま) — parent horse
  • 母馬(ははうま) — mare (mother horse)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Horses have a long history in Japan, particularly in 北海道(ほっかいどう) where horse breeding is still a major industry. The 日高(ひだか) region is famous for thoroughbred breeding.
