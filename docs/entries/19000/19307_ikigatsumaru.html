1. to choke; to have difficulty breathing
A physical sensation of being unable to breathe properly, such as from smoke, fumes, or a blocked airway.
煙(けむり)で息(いき)が詰(つ)まった。
I choked from the smoke.
急(いそ)いで食(た)べたら息(いき)が詰(つ)まりそうになった。
I nearly choked when I ate too quickly.
密閉(みっぺい)された部屋(へや)にいると息(いき)が詰(つ)まる。
Being in a sealed room makes it hard to breathe.
2. to feel stifled; to feel oppressed; to be tense
A figurative sense of feeling psychologically suffocated by a tense atmosphere, strict environment, or overbearing situation.
息(いき)が詰(つ)まるような緊張感(きんちょうかん)だった。
It was a suffocating tension.
厳(きび)しいルールばかりで息(いき)が詰(つ)まる。
With nothing but strict rules, it feels stifling.
あの職場(しょくば)は人間関係(にんげんかんけい)が複雑(ふくざつ)で息(いき)が詰(つ)まる思(おも)いだった。
The interpersonal relationships at that workplace were complicated, and it felt suffocating.

Literally 'breath gets blocked.' Used both for physical choking and, more commonly, for the figurative sense of feeling psychologically oppressed or tense.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 息(いき)が詰(つ)まるような — suffocating, stifling (used as a modifier)
  • 息(いき)が詰(つ)まる思(おも)い — a feeling of suffocation
  • 息(いき)が詰(つ)まる雰囲気(ふんいき) — a stifling atmosphere

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 息苦(いきぐる)しい: feeling short of breath, stifling — adjective form with similar meaning
  • 窒息(ちっそく)する: to suffocate — more clinical/severe, actual inability to breathe
  • 息抜(いきぬ)き: a breather, a break — the remedy for feeling stifled
