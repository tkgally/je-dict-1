甘受(かんじゅ)

かんじゅ
noun; verb (suru)
acceptance; resignation; submitting to
1. acceptance; resignation; submitting to
Accepting something unpleasant or difficult without resistance. Implies willingly or resignedly enduring hardship, punishment, or unfavorable circumstances.
結果(けっか)を甘受(かんじゅ)する。
To accept the consequences.
批判(ひはん)は甘受(かんじゅ)しなければならない。
One must accept the criticism.
自分(じぶん)の選択(せんたく)の結果(けっか)である以上(いじょう)、この不便(ふべん)さは甘受(かんじゅ)するしかない。
Since this is the result of my own choice, I have no choice but to accept this inconvenience.

A formal, literary word. The object of 甘受(かんじゅ) is almost always something negative or undesirable: 批判(ひはん) (criticism), 処分(しょぶん) (punishment), 不利益(ふりえき) (disadvantage), 結果(けっか) (consequences). The character 甘(かん) here means "willingly" rather than its more common meaning of "sweet." Common pattern: 〜を甘受(かんじゅ)する.
