1. policy shift; change of direction; reversal of course
A deliberate change in an established policy, strategy, or approach. Used in politics, business, and personal decisions.
政府(せいふ)が方針転換(ほうしんてんかん)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The government announced a policy shift.
売(う)り上(あ)げの低迷(ていめい)を受(う)けて経営(けいえい)方針(ほうしん)の転換(てんかん)を図(はか)った。
Facing sluggish sales, they attempted a change in business strategy.
急(きゅう)な方針転換(ほうしんてんかん)に現場(げんば)は混乱(こんらん)した。
The sudden policy reversal caused confusion on the ground.

Compound of 方針(ほうしん) (policy, course of action) and 転換(てんかん) (conversion, switch). Very common in news reporting about political and corporate decisions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 方針転換(ほうしんてんかん)を図(はか)る — to attempt a policy shift
  • 方針転換(ほうしんてんかん)を迫(せま)られる — to be forced into a policy change
  • 大幅(おおはば)な方針転換(ほうしんてんかん) — a major policy shift
  • 急(きゅう)な方針転換(ほうしんてんかん) — a sudden change of course

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 路線変更(ろせんへんこう): change of policy/route — similar meaning, slightly more informal
  • 軌道修正(きどうしゅうせい): course correction — implies a smaller adjustment rather than a full reversal
  • 転換(てんかん): conversion, shift — the broader term without specifying it is about policy
