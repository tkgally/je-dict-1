親近感(しんきんかん)

しんきんかん
noun
feeling of closeness; sense of familiarity
1. feeling of closeness; sense of familiarity; affinity
A warm feeling of familiarity or connection toward someone or something. The sense that a person, place, or thing feels approachable and relatable.
親近感(しんきんかん)を覚(おぼ)える。
To feel a sense of closeness.
あの俳優(はいゆう)には親近感(しんきんかん)がある。
That actor has an approachable quality.
同(おな)じ趣味(しゅみ)を持(も)っていると知(し)って、親近感(しんきんかん)がわいた。
When I found out we share the same hobby, I felt a sense of connection.

Composed of 親近(しんきん) (closeness) + 感(かん) (feeling). Describes a warm sense of connection or familiarity toward someone or something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 親近感(しんきんかん)を覚(おぼ)える: to feel a sense of familiarity
  • 親近感(しんきんかん)がわく: closeness wells up spontaneously
  • 親近感(しんきんかん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor a feeling of affinity (literary)
  • 親近感(しんきんかん)がある: to have an approachable quality

USAGE:
Often used to describe the appeal of public figures, brands, or products that feel relatable and down-to-earth. It expresses a positive emotional connection rather than logical assessment.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 好感(こうかん): favorable impression (broader, less about closeness)
  • 愛着(あいちゃく): attachment, fondness (deeper, more personal)
