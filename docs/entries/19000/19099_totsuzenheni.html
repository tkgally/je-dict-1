突然変異(とつぜんへんい)

とつぜんへんい
noun
mutation; genetic mutation
1. mutation; genetic mutation
A sudden, random change in the DNA sequence of an organism that can alter traits. Used in biology and genetics.
突然変異(とつぜんへんい)が起(お)きた。
A mutation occurred.
放射線(ほうしゃせん)は突然変異(とつぜんへんい)を引(ひ)き起(お)こすことがある。
Radiation can cause mutations.
ウイルスの突然変異(とつぜんへんい)によって新(あたら)しい株(かぶ)が生(う)まれた。
A new strain emerged through a mutation of the virus.
2. sudden, unexpected change; freak occurrence
Used figuratively to describe something that deviates dramatically and unexpectedly from the norm.
彼(かれ)はこの業界(ぎょうかい)の突然変異(とつぜんへんい)のような存在(そんざい)だ。
He's like a freak of nature in this industry.
平凡(へいぼん)な家庭(かてい)から突然変異(とつぜんへんい)的(てき)に天才(てんさい)が現(あらわ)れた。
A genius appeared as if by mutation from an ordinary family.
あの選手(せんしゅ)の活躍(かつやく)は、このチームにとって突然変異(とつぜんへんい)のようなものだった。
That player's performance was like a freak occurrence for this team.

A compound of 突然(とつぜん) (sudden) and 変異(へんい) (variation, mutation). The scientific sense is the primary meaning. Common collocations: 突然変異(とつぜんへんい)体(たい) (mutant), 突然変異(とつぜんへんい)を起(お)こす (to undergo mutation), and 突然変異(とつぜんへんい)的(てき) (mutant-like, freakish). The figurative sense often carries a tone of admiration for exceptional talent appearing unexpectedly.
