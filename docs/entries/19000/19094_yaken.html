野犬(やけん)

やけん
noun
stray dog; feral dog
1. stray dog; feral dog; wild dog
A dog living without an owner, roaming freely in the wild or in urban areas. Includes both abandoned domestic dogs and those born in the wild.
公園(こうえん)で野犬(やけん)を見(み)かけた。
I spotted a stray dog in the park.
この地域(ちいき)では野犬(やけん)が増(ふ)えている。
Stray dogs are increasing in this area.
野犬(やけん)に近(ちか)づくのは危険(きけん)なので、見(み)つけたら保健所(ほけんじょ)に連絡(れんらく)してください。
Approaching stray dogs is dangerous, so if you find one, please contact the public health center.

Also sometimes read as のいぬ, though やけん is the standard reading. Common collocations include 野犬(やけん)狩(が)り (rounding up stray dogs) and 野犬(やけん)の群(む)れ (a pack of stray dogs). Compare with 野良犬(のらいぬ) (stray dog, more colloquial) and 捨(す)て犬(いぬ) (abandoned dog). In Japan, stray dogs are managed by 保健所(ほけんじょ) (public health centers) and 動物愛護(どうぶつあいご)センター (animal welfare centers).
