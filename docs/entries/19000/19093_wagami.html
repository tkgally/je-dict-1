我(わ)が身(み)

わがみ
noun
oneself; one's own body
1. oneself; one's own body; one's own person
A somewhat literary or emphatic way to refer to oneself or one's own body, often used in contexts of self-reflection or self-sacrifice.
我(わ)が身(み)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る。
To reflect on oneself.
我(わ)が身(み)の安全(あんぜん)を守(まも)ることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to protect one's own safety.
他人事(ひとごと)だと思(おも)わず、我(わ)が身(み)に置(お)き換(か)えて考(かんが)えてみてほしい。
I'd like you to think about it as if it were happening to you, not dismiss it as someone else's problem.

A literary or emphatic expression. Common set phrases include 我(わ)が身(み)を顧(かえり)みず (without regard for oneself), 我(わ)が身(み)に置(お)き換(か)える (to put oneself in someone else's shoes), and 我(わ)が身(み)を犠牲(ぎせい)にする (to sacrifice oneself). The 我(わ)が pattern is a classical possessive form meaning "my" or "one's own," also seen in 我(わ)が家(や) (one's home) and 我(わ)が国(くに) (our country).
