南半球(みなみはんきゅう)

みなみはんきゅう
noun
Southern Hemisphere
1. Southern Hemisphere
The half of the Earth south of the equator. Includes most of South America, Africa, Australia, and Antarctica.
オーストラリアは南半球(みなみはんきゅう)にある。
Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere.
南半球(みなみはんきゅう)では季節(きせつ)が逆(ぎゃく)になる。
In the Southern Hemisphere, the seasons are reversed.
南半球(みなみはんきゅう)の国(くに)では十二月(じゅうにがつ)が夏(なつ)なので、クリスマスを海(うみ)で過(す)ごす人(ひと)もいる。
In Southern Hemisphere countries, December is summer, so some people spend Christmas at the beach.

Compound of 南(みなみ) (south) + 半球(はんきゅう) (hemisphere). The opposite is 北半球(きたはんきゅう) (Northern Hemisphere). Japan is located in the 北半球(きたはんきゅう). A common topic in geography classes and when discussing differences in seasons between Japan and countries like Australia or New Zealand.
