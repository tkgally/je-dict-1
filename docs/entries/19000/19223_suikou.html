推敲(すいこう)

すいこう
noun
polishing (of writing); careful revision
See also: 遂行(すいこう) (execution, carrying out)
1. polishing; careful revision; refining one's writing
The process of repeatedly reviewing and improving a piece of writing, choosing words and phrases with care. Derived from a Chinese anecdote about a poet who agonized over whether to use the character 推(すい) (push) or 敲(こう) (knock) in a line of poetry.
文章(ぶんしょう)を推敲(すいこう)する。
To polish a piece of writing.
何度(なんど)も推敲(すいこう)を重(かさ)ねた。
I went through many rounds of revision.
提出(ていしゅつ)する前(まえ)に、もう一度(いちど)推敲(すいこう)してから送(おく)りましょう。
Before submitting, let's polish it one more time and then send it.

推敲(すいこう) means to carefully revise and polish a piece of writing, choosing each word with deliberation. Used as a suru verb.

ETYMOLOGY:
From a famous Tang dynasty anecdote: the poet 賈島(かとう) agonized over whether to use 推(すい) (push) or 敲(こう) (knock) in a line of poetry. The two kanji together came to mean careful word choice.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 推敲(すいこう)する: to revise/polish
  • 推敲(すいこう)を重(かさ)ねる: to revise repeatedly
  • 推敲(すいこう)を加(くわ)える: to apply revisions
  • 文章(ぶんしょう)を推敲(すいこう)する: to polish a piece of writing

USAGE:
Used mainly for written text. Implies careful, thoughtful revision rather than casual proofreading.

HOMOPHONE NOTE:
遂行(すいこう) means 'execution/carrying out' — same reading but completely different kanji and meaning.
