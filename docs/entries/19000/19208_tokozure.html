床(とこ)ずれ

とこずれ
noun
bedsore; pressure sore
1. bedsore; pressure sore; decubitus ulcer
A skin injury caused by prolonged pressure on the body, typically occurring in people who are bedridden or unable to change position. A common concern in nursing care.
床(とこ)ずれを防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent bedsores.
長期(ちょうき)入院(にゅういん)で床(とこ)ずれができてしまった。
Bedsores developed from a long hospital stay.
定期的(ていきてき)に体位(たいい)を変(か)えることで床(とこ)ずれの予防(よぼう)になる。
Regularly changing the body position helps prevent bedsores.

Composed of 床(とこ) (bed) + ずれ (slipping, rubbing — from ずれる). The formal medical term is 褥瘡(じょくそう) (decubitus ulcer). Common collocations: 床(とこ)ずれを防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent bedsores), 床(とこ)ずれ予防(よぼう) (bedsore prevention), 床(とこ)ずれ防止(ぼうし)マット (pressure relief mat). An important concept in 介護(かいご) (caregiving) — turning bedridden patients every two hours is standard practice. In Japan's aging society, 床(とこ)ずれ prevention is a key component of both hospital and home care.
