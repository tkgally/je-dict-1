株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)

かぶしきがいしゃ
noun
corporation; stock company; Co., Ltd.
1. corporation; joint-stock company; Co., Ltd.
The most common type of business corporation in Japan, equivalent to a limited company or corporation. Abbreviated as (株) in company names.
株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)を設立(せつりつ)した。
I established a corporation.
彼(かれ)は株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)ABCの社長(しゃちょう)です。
He is the president of ABC Corporation.
日本(にほん)で株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)を作(つく)るには、資本金(しほんきん)が一円(いちえん)から可能(かのう)になった。
In Japan, it has become possible to create a corporation with as little as one yen in capital.

Often abbreviated as (株(かぶ)) in written contexts, placed either before or after the company name: 株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)トヨタ or トヨタ株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ). The position (before or after) is determined by the company's official registration. Other Japanese corporate forms include 合同会社(ごうどうがいしゃ) (LLC, abbreviated 合(ごう)), 合資会社(ごうしがいしゃ) (limited partnership), and 合名会社(ごうめいがいしゃ) (general partnership). Colloquially often shortened to 株(かぶ) when referring to shares or the stock market.
