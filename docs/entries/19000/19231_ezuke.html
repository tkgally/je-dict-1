餌付(えづ)け

えづけ
noun
feeding (wild animals or birds)
1. feeding wild animals or birds; taming by feeding
The act of regularly providing food to wild animals or birds, either to tame them or as a habitual practice. Can also refer to training animals to come to a specific location through feeding.
鳩(はと)に餌付(えづ)けをする。
To feed the pigeons.
公園(こうえん)での野良猫(のらねこ)への餌付(えづ)けは禁止(きんし)されている。
Feeding stray cats in the park is prohibited.
観光客(かんこうきゃく)による野生動物(やせいどうぶつ)への餌付(えづ)けが問題(もんだい)になっている。
Feeding of wild animals by tourists has become a problem.

Composed of 餌(え) (bait/feed) + 付(つ)け (attaching/applying). Refers to regularly feeding wild animals, often to tame them or attract them to a location.

USAGE:
Used as a する verb: 餌付(えづ)けする. The object is marked with に or への: 鳩(はと)に餌付(えづ)けする (to feed pigeons).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 餌付(えづ)け禁止(きんし): no feeding (common sign in parks)
  • 野生動物(やせいどうぶつ)への餌付(えづ)け: feeding wild animals
  • 餌付(えづ)けする: to feed (wild animals)

SOCIAL CONTEXT:
In many Japanese parks and nature reserves, 餌付(えづ)け is prohibited because it disrupts animals' natural behavior and can make them dependent on humans. Signs reading 餌付(えづ)け禁止(きんし) are common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 餌(えさ)やり: feeding (more general, including pets)
  • 給餌(きゅうじ): feeding (technical/formal)
