行(い)きつけ

いきつけ
noun; pre-noun adjectival
regular; favorite (place one frequents)
1. regular; favorite; usual (place one frequents)
A place one regularly visits or patronizes, such as a restaurant, bar, or shop. Used as a modifier before nouns.
行(い)きつけの店(みせ)に行(い)こう。
Let's go to our usual place.
行(い)きつけの美容院(びよういん)を変(か)えるのは勇気(ゆうき)がいる。
It takes courage to switch your regular hair salon.
この辺(あた)りに行(い)きつけの居酒屋(いざかや)はありますか。
Do you have a regular izakaya around here?

行(い)きつけ describes a place one visits regularly and feels comfortable at. It implies an established relationship — the staff may know you, and you feel at home there.

USAGE:
Almost always used as a pre-noun modifier: 行(い)きつけの + place noun. Rarely used as a standalone noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 行(い)きつけの店(みせ): one's regular restaurant/shop
  • 行(い)きつけの病院(びょういん): one's regular hospital/clinic
  • 行(い)きつけのバー: one's regular bar
  • 行(い)きつけの美容院(びよういん): one's regular hair salon
  • 行(い)きつけの歯医者(はいしゃ): one's regular dentist

KANJI:
Also written as 行(い)き付(つ)け, though hiragana is more common.

RELATED:
常連(じょうれん) (regular customer) describes the person, while 行(い)きつけ describes the place from the customer's perspective.
