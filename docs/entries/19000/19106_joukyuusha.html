上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)

じょうきゅうしゃ
noun
advanced-level person; expert
1. advanced-level person; expert; advanced practitioner
Someone who has reached a high level of proficiency in a skill, sport, language, or other area.
上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)向(む)けのクラスに参加(さんか)した。
I joined the class for advanced students.
この山(やま)は上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)でないと危険(きけん)だ。
This mountain is dangerous unless you're an experienced climber.
上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)でも苦戦(くせん)するほどの難(むずか)しいコースだった。
It was such a difficult course that even advanced practitioners struggled.

A compound of 上級(じょうきゅう) (advanced level) and 者(しゃ) (person). Part of a three-level series: 初級者(しょきゅうしゃ) (beginner), 中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ) (intermediate), 上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ) (advanced). Often used in the pattern 上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)向(む)け (aimed at advanced learners). Common in contexts like language learning, sports, and games.
