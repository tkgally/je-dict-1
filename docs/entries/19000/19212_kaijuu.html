海獣(かいじゅう)

かいじゅう
noun
marine mammal
1. marine mammal
A mammal that lives primarily in the ocean, such as seals, sea lions, walruses, and sea otters. A zoological term.
水族館(すいぞくかん)で海獣(かいじゅう)のショーを見(み)た。
I watched a marine mammal show at the aquarium.
アザラシやセイウチは海獣(かいじゅう)に分類(ぶんるい)される。
Seals and walruses are classified as marine mammals.
北海道(ほっかいどう)の海岸(かいがん)では野生(やせい)の海獣(かいじゅう)を観察(かんさつ)できることがある。
On the coasts of Hokkaido, it is sometimes possible to observe wild marine mammals.

Composed of 海(かい) (sea) + 獣(じゅう) (beast, animal). Refers specifically to marine mammals — not fish or other sea creatures. Includes animals like アザラシ (seal), アシカ (sea lion), セイウチ (walrus), ラッコ (sea otter), and sometimes クジラ (whale) and イルカ (dolphin). Not to be confused with the homophone 怪獣(かいじゅう) (monster, as in Godzilla). Common collocations: 海獣(かいじゅう)類(るい) (marine mammals as a group), 海獣(かいじゅう)ショー (marine mammal show). In Japanese aquariums, the 海獣(かいじゅう) section typically features pinnipeds (seals and sea lions).
