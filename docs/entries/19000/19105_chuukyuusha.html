中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)

ちゅうきゅうしゃ
noun
intermediate-level person
1. intermediate-level person; person at an intermediate level
Someone who has progressed beyond beginner level but has not yet reached advanced level in a skill, sport, language, or other area of study.
この教材(きょうざい)は中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)向(む)けです。
This textbook is for intermediate learners.
中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)から上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)へのステップアップが難(むずか)しい。
The step up from intermediate to advanced level is difficult.
スキーの中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)コースは傾斜(けいしゃ)がやや急(きゅう)になっている。
The intermediate ski course has a somewhat steep slope.

A person at an intermediate level of skill or knowledge. Used across many domains including language learning, sports, music, and other activities.

WORD FORMATION:
中級(ちゅうきゅう) (intermediate level) + 者(しゃ) (person).

LEVEL SERIES:

  • 初級者(しょきゅうしゃ): beginner
  • 中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ): intermediate
  • 上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ): advanced

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)向(む)け: aimed at intermediates (for courses, textbooks, equipment)
  • 中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)コース: intermediate course
  • 中級者(ちゅうきゅうしゃ)以上(いじょう): intermediate level and above
