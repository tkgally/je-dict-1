遺言(いごん)

いごん
noun
will; testament (legal term)
1. will; testament; testamentary document
A legal document specifying how a person's property and affairs should be handled after death. This reading is the formal legal term, as opposed to the more general ゆいごん.
遺言(いごん)を作成(さくせい)する。
To draft a will.
遺言(いごん)の内容(ないよう)が公開(こうかい)された。
The contents of the will were made public.
弁護士(べんごし)に依頼(いらい)して正式(せいしき)な遺言(いごん)を作成(さくせい)してもらった。
I had a lawyer draft a formal will for me.

The reading いごん is the standard legal term used in courts, law offices, and official documents. The more common reading 遺言(ゆいごん) is used in everyday speech and has a broader meaning that includes dying wishes and last words. Legal types include 自筆証書遺言(じひつしょうしょいごん) (holographic will), 公正証書遺言(こうせいしょうしょいごん) (notarial will), and 秘密証書遺言(ひみつしょうしょいごん) (sealed will). Common collocations: 遺言(いごん)を残(のこ)す (to leave a will), 遺言(いごん)書(しょ) (written will).
