1. deliberation; discussion; review
Formal examination and discussion of a matter, typically in legislative, judicial, or committee settings.
法案(ほうあん)を審議(しんぎ)する。
To deliberate on a bill.
委員会(いいんかい)で予算(よさん)の審議(しんぎ)が行(おこな)われた。
The budget was deliberated in committee.
この問題(もんだい)については、慎重(しんちょう)な審議(しんぎ)が必要(ひつよう)だと考(かんが)えます。
I believe careful deliberation is needed on this issue.

A formal word used mainly in legislative, judicial, and administrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 審議(しんぎ)する: to deliberate
  • 審議(しんぎ)に入(はい)る: to enter deliberation
  • 審議(しんぎ)中(ちゅう): under deliberation
  • 審議(しんぎ)を尽(つ)くす: to deliberate thoroughly
  • 審議(しんぎ)会(かい): deliberation council

HOMOPHONE:
真偽(しんぎ) (truth or falsehood) is pronounced the same but has a completely different meaning.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 審議(しんぎ): formal deliberation (legislature, committee)
  • 議論(ぎろん): discussion, debate (general)
  • 検討(けんとう): examination, consideration (less formal)
