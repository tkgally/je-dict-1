天丼(てんどん)

てんどん
noun
tempura rice bowl
1. tempura rice bowl
A bowl of rice topped with assorted tempura, typically shrimp and vegetables, drizzled with a sweet soy-based sauce. A staple of Japanese fast-casual dining.
天丼(てんどん)を注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered a tempura rice bowl.
この店(みせ)の天丼(てんどん)は海老(えび)が大(おお)きい。
The shrimp in this restaurant's tempura bowl are big.
昼(ひる)ごはんに天丼(てんどん)と味噌汁(みそしる)のセットを食(た)べた。
For lunch, I had a tempura bowl and miso soup set.

Short for 天(てん)ぷら丼(どんぶり). One of the most popular 丼(どんぶり)もの (rice bowl dishes) in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天丼(てんどん)を食(た)べる — to eat a tempura bowl
  • 天丼(てんどん)セット — tempura bowl set (with soup and pickles)
  • 上(じょう)天丼(てんどん) — premium tempura bowl (with higher-quality ingredients)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 天(てん)ぷら — tempura (the fried dish itself)
  • 親子丼(おやこどん) — chicken-and-egg rice bowl
  • 牛丼(ぎゅうどん) — beef rice bowl
  • 海鮮丼(かいせんどん) — seafood rice bowl
