1. to braid in; to weave in; to plait into
To interweave or braid something into a larger structure, such as braiding hair, weaving fibers, or incorporating elements into a knitted pattern.
髪(かみ)を編(あ)み込(こ)む。
To braid one's hair.
リボンを髪(かみ)に編(あ)み込(こ)んだ。
I braided a ribbon into my hair.
色(いろ)とりどりの糸(いと)を編(あ)み込(こ)んで、美(うつく)しい模様(もよう)のマフラーを作(つく)った。
I made a scarf with a beautiful pattern by weaving in threads of various colors.

A compound verb from 編(あ)む (to knit/braid) + 込(こ)む (into). The 込(こ)む component adds the nuance of weaving something thoroughly into or incorporating it deeply. Most commonly used for braiding hair or weaving materials into fabric/crafts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — takes a direct object (the thing being braided in).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 髪(かみ)を編(あ)み込(こ)む — to braid hair
  • 糸(いと)を編(あ)み込(こ)む — to weave thread in
  • ビーズを編(あ)み込(こ)む — to braid beads in
  • 編(あ)み込(こ)みヘア — braided hairstyle

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 編(あ)む — to knit, to braid; the base verb, without the "into" nuance
  • 織(お)り込(こ)む — to weave in; used for weaving fabric, also figuratively for incorporating ideas
  • 三(み)つ編(あ)み — a three-strand braid; a specific braiding style
