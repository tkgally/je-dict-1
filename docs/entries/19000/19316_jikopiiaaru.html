1. self-promotion; self-introduction; personal pitch
Presenting one's own strengths, skills, and qualities, especially in job interviews, resumes, or academic applications. A standard part of the Japanese job-hunting process.
自己(じこ)ＰＲを考(かんが)える。
To think about one's self-introduction.
面接(めんせつ)で自己(じこ)ＰＲを求(もと)められた。
I was asked to give a self-introduction at the interview.
履歴書(りれきしょ)の自己(じこ)ＰＲ欄(らん)には自分(じぶん)の強(つよ)みを具体的(ぐたいてき)に書(か)くことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to write your strengths concretely in the self-promotion section of your resume.

A hybrid compound of 自己(じこ) (self) and ＰＲ (public relations, from English). Despite using 'PR,' this term specifically means presenting oneself positively, not public relations in the corporate sense.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己(じこ)ＰＲ欄(らん) — self-promotion section (on a resume or application form)
  • 自己(じこ)ＰＲを書(か)く — to write a self-introduction
  • 自己(じこ)ＰＲが上手(じょうず) — to be good at self-promotion
  • 自己(じこ)ＰＲのポイント — key points of self-promotion

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
A critical component of the Japanese job-hunting (就活(しゅうかつ)) process. Students and job seekers spend considerable time crafting their 自己(じこ)ＰＲ, which is expected in resumes (履歴書(りれきしょ)) and interviews. It typically covers one's strengths, experiences, and what one can contribute to the company.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自己紹介(じこしょうかい): self-introduction — a broader term for introducing oneself in any context, not limited to selling one's strengths
