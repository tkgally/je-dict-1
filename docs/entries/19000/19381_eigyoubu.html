営業部(えいぎょうぶ)

えいぎょうぶ
noun
sales department
1. sales department; business division
The department or division within a company responsible for sales and business development.
営業部(えいぎょうぶ)に配属(はいぞく)された。
I was assigned to the sales department.
営業部(えいぎょうぶ)の会議(かいぎ)は毎週(まいしゅう)月曜日(げつようび)にある。
The sales department meeting is every Monday.
彼(かれ)は営業部(えいぎょうぶ)から企画部(きかくぶ)に異動(いどう)になった。
He was transferred from the sales department to the planning department.

The department within a company responsible for sales activities and business development. In Japanese companies, 営業部(えいぎょうぶ) staff typically handle client relations, sales targets, and revenue generation.

STRUCTURE:
営業(えいぎょう) (sales; business) + 部(ぶ) (department; division)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 営業部(えいぎょうぶ)に配属(はいぞく)される — to be assigned to the sales department
  • 営業部(えいぎょうぶ)の部長(ぶちょう) — sales department manager
  • 営業部(えいぎょうぶ)から異動(いどう)する — to transfer from the sales department

OTHER COMMON DEPARTMENTS:

  • 企画部(きかくぶ) — planning department
  • 総務部(そうむぶ) — general affairs department
  • 人事部(じんじぶ) — human resources department
  • 経理部(けいりぶ) — accounting department
