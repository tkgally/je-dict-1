前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)

ぜんにんしゃ
noun
predecessor
1. predecessor; former holder of a position
The person who held a position, role, or office before the current one. Used in workplace and organizational contexts.
前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)から引(ひ)き継(つ)ぐ。
To take over from one's predecessor.
前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)のやり方(かた)とは大(おお)きく違(ちが)う。
It's very different from the way the predecessor did things.
前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)が残(のこ)した資料(しりょう)を確認(かくにん)してから仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めた。
I checked the documents left by my predecessor before starting work.

前任者(ぜんにんしゃ) refers to the person who previously held a particular position or role. Composed of 前任(ぜんにん) (previous appointment) + 者(しゃ) (person).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)から引(ひ)き継(つ)ぐ: to take over from a predecessor
  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)の方針(ほうしん): the predecessor's policy
  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)の後(あと)を継(つ)ぐ: to succeed one's predecessor
  • 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ)に比(くら)べて: compared to the predecessor

ANTONYM:

  • 後任者(こうにんしゃ): successor (person who takes over)
  • 後任(こうにん): successor/replacement (without 者(しゃ))

RELATED TERMS:

  • 前任(ぜんにん): previous appointment/tenure (without 者(しゃ))
  • 後継者(こうけいしゃ): successor, heir (broader — not just job positions)
  • 引(ひ)き継(つ)ぎ: handover, transition of duties
