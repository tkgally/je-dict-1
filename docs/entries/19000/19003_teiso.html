1. filing a lawsuit; bringing legal action; litigation
The act of formally filing a lawsuit with a court. Used in legal and news contexts.
損害賠償(そんがいばいしょう)を求(もと)めて提訴(ていそ)した。
They filed a lawsuit seeking damages.
会社(かいしゃ)を相手(あいて)に提訴(ていそ)する。
To file a lawsuit against the company.
住民(じゅうみん)たちは環境汚染(かんきょうおせん)をめぐり、国(くに)と企業(きぎょう)を相手(あいて)に提訴(ていそ)した。
The residents filed suit against the government and the company over environmental pollution.

Used with する: 提訴(ていそ)する (to file suit). The typical pattern is XをYに提訴(ていそ)する or Yを相手(あいて)に提訴(ていそ)する (to sue Y). Common collocations: 民事(みんじ)提訴(ていそ) (civil suit), 集団(しゅうだん)提訴(ていそ) (class action), 提訴(ていそ)期限(きげん) (statute of limitations for filing). Distinguished from 訴訟(そしょう) (lawsuit, the legal proceedings themselves) and 告訴(こくそ) (criminal complaint/prosecution) — 提訴(ていそ) specifically refers to the act of filing or initiating a case.
