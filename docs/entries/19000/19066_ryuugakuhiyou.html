留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)

りゅうがくひよう
noun
study abroad expenses; cost of studying abroad
1. study abroad expenses; cost of studying abroad
The total expenses associated with studying in a foreign country, including tuition, living costs, travel, and other fees.
留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)を貯(た)めている。
I'm saving up for study abroad expenses.
留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)はどのくらいかかりますか。
About how much do study abroad expenses cost?
奨学金(しょうがくきん)のおかげで留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)の負担(ふたん)が軽(かる)くなった。
Thanks to the scholarship, the burden of study abroad expenses was lightened.

Compound of 留学(りゅうがく) (studying abroad) + 費用(ひよう) (expenses/cost).

WHAT IT INCLUDES:

  • 授業料(じゅぎょうりょう): tuition
  • 生活費(せいかつひ): living expenses
  • 渡航費(とこうひ): travel expenses
  • 保険料(ほけんりょう): insurance premiums

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)を貯(た)める: to save for study abroad
  • 留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)を準備(じゅんび)する: to prepare study abroad funds
  • 留学費用(りゅうがくひよう)を見積(みつ)もる: to estimate study abroad costs

Many students fund study abroad through 奨学金(しょうがくきん) (scholarships) or 教育(きょういく)ローン (education loans).
