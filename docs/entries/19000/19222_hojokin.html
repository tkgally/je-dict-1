補助金(ほじょきん)

ほじょきん
noun
subsidy; grant
1. subsidy; grant; financial aid
Money provided by a government, organization, or institution to support a particular activity, project, or group. Typically refers to public funding.
補助金(ほじょきん)を申請(しんせい)する。
To apply for a subsidy.
国(くに)から補助金(ほじょきん)が出(で)ることになった。
It was decided that a subsidy would be provided by the government.
中小企業(ちゅうしょうきぎょう)を対象(たいしょう)にした補助金(ほじょきん)制度(せいど)が新(あら)たに設(もう)けられた。
A new subsidy program targeting small and medium-sized businesses was established.

Composed of 補助(ほじょ) (assistance) + 金(きん) (money). Refers to financial support from government or institutions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 補助金(ほじょきん)を申請(しんせい)する: to apply for a subsidy
  • 補助金(ほじょきん)を交付(こうふ)する: to grant a subsidy
  • 補助金(ほじょきん)を受(う)ける: to receive a subsidy
  • 補助金(ほじょきん)制度(せいど): subsidy system/program

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 助成金(じょせいきん): grant, funding aid (often for research or social programs)
  • 交付金(こうふきん): allocated grant (from central to local government)
  • 給付金(きゅうふきん): benefit payment (paid directly to individuals)

USAGE:
Commonly appears in news about government economic policy, small business support, and local government programs.
