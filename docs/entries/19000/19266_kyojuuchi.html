居住地(きょじゅうち)

きょじゅうち
noun
place of residence; residential area
1. place of residence; where one lives
The location where someone officially or actually resides. Used in administrative, legal, and formal contexts.
居住地(きょじゅうち)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in your place of residence.
現在(げんざい)の居住地(きょじゅうち)は東京(とうきょう)です。
My current place of residence is Tokyo.
申請書(しんせいしょ)には居住地(きょじゅうち)の証明(しょうめい)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Proof of residence is required for the application.
2. residential area; settlement
An area where people live, as opposed to commercial or industrial zones.
この辺(あた)りは居住地(きょじゅうち)として人気(にんき)がある。
This area is popular as a residential district.
工場(こうじょう)の近(ちか)くに居住地(きょじゅうち)を作(つく)るべきではない。
Residential areas shouldn't be built near factories.
古代(こだい)の居住地(きょじゅうち)跡(あと)が発掘(はっくつ)された。
Remains of an ancient settlement were excavated.

A formal term for where someone lives. Sense 1 is most common in everyday use — appearing on forms, applications, and official documents. Sense 2 is used in urban planning, archaeology, and geography.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 居住地(きょじゅうち)の変更(へんこう): change of residence
  • 居住地(きょじゅうち)証明(しょうめい): proof of residence
  • 現在(げんざい)の居住地(きょじゅうち): current place of residence
  • 居住地(きょじゅうち)域(いき): residential zone

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 住所(じゅうしょ): address — more specific and common in everyday use
  • 住居(じゅうきょ): dwelling, residence — refers to the building itself rather than the location
  • 住(す)まい: home, residence — casual, warm tone
