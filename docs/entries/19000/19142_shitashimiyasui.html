1. approachable; friendly; easy to feel close to
Describes a person, atmosphere, or thing that makes others feel at ease and comfortable. Easy to warm up to or feel familiar with.
彼(かれ)はとても親(した)しみやすい人(ひと)だ。
He is a very approachable person.
親(した)しみやすい雰囲気(ふんいき)の店(みせ)で食事(しょくじ)をした。
We ate at a restaurant with a friendly atmosphere.
先生(せんせい)が親(した)しみやすいおかげで、質問(しつもん)しやすい授業(じゅぎょう)になっている。
Because the teacher is approachable, it's a class where it's easy to ask questions.

Compound of 親(した)しみ (familiarity, closeness — noun form of 親(した)しむ) + やすい (easy to). Describes someone or something that puts people at ease.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 親(した)しみやすい人(ひと): approachable person
  • 親(した)しみやすい雰囲気(ふんいき): friendly/welcoming atmosphere
  • 親(した)しみやすいデザイン: user-friendly design
  • 親(した)しみやすい笑顔(えがお): a friendly smile

USAGE:
Always a positive description. Often used for people in service, teaching, or leadership roles where approachability matters. Can also describe places, designs, or media that feel accessible.

FORMS:

  • 親(した)しみやすさ: approachability (noun form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気(き)さく（な）: friendly, easy-going (describes personality)
  • 話(はな)しやすい: easy to talk to
  • 取(と)っ付(つ)きやすい: approachable, accessible

Related Words
