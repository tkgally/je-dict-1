節(せつ)

せつ
noun, suffix
section; season; occasion; moderation
See also: 節(ふし) (joint, knot, melody)
1. section; clause; paragraph; verse
A division or subdivision of a text, document, or speech. Used for chapters in academic writing, verses in music or poetry, and sections of legal documents.
第(だい)三章(さんしょう)第(だい)二節(にせつ)を読(よ)んでください。
Please read Chapter 3, Section 2.
この歌(うた)は三(さん)節(せつ)ある。
This song has three verses.
論文(ろんぶん)の各(かく)節(せつ)にまとめを付(つ)けた。
I added a summary to each section of the paper.
2. season; time; occasion
A particular time or season. Appears in compounds related to seasonal divisions and occasions.
季節(きせつ)の変(か)わり目(め)に体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)しやすい。
It's easy to get sick at the change of seasons.
節分(せつぶん)に豆(まめ)をまく。
Throw beans on Setsubun.
節目(ふしめ)の年(とし)を迎(むか)えた。
I've reached a milestone year.
3. moderation; restraint; propriety
Self-control or moderation in behavior. Used in expressions about maintaining proper boundaries.
節度(せつど)ある行動(こうどう)を心(こころ)がける。
Try to act with moderation.
飲(の)み過(す)ぎないよう節制(せっせい)している。
I'm exercising restraint so as not to drink too much.
何事(なにごと)も節度(せつど)が大切(たいせつ)で、やり過(す)ぎは禁物(きんもつ)だ。
Moderation is important in everything — going overboard is a no-no.

A highly versatile kanji with meanings spanning textual divisions, seasonal markers, and moderation. The reading せつ (on'yomi) is used in compounds; the kun'yomi ふし is a separate entry.

COMMON COMPOUNDS (section/text):

  • 章節(しょうせつ) — chapters and sections
  • 条節(じょうせつ) — clauses and sections

COMMON COMPOUNDS (season/time):

  • 季節(きせつ) — season
  • 節分(せつぶん) — Setsubun (Feb. 3, bean-throwing festival)
  • 節気(せっき) — solar term

COMMON COMPOUNDS (moderation):

  • 節度(せつど) — moderation, propriety
  • 節制(せっせい) — temperance, self-restraint
  • 節約(せつやく) — economizing, saving
  • 節電(せつでん) — power saving
  • 節水(せっすい) — water conservation

DIFFERENT READINGS:

  • 節(せつ) — section; season; moderation (this entry)
  • 節(ふし) — joint; knot; melody; point (separate entry)
