盛夏(せいか)

せいか
noun
midsummer; the height of summer
1. midsummer; the height of summer
The hottest period of summer, typically referring to July and August in Japan. A somewhat literary or formal word for the peak of the summer season.
盛夏(せいか)の候(こう)、いかがお過(す)ごしですか。
In this midsummer season, how are you doing?
A formal seasonal greeting used in letters.
盛夏(せいか)を迎(むか)え、気温(きおん)が三十五度(さんじゅうごど)を超(こ)える日(ひ)が続(つづ)いている。
With midsummer upon us, temperatures exceeding 35 degrees have continued.
盛夏(せいか)にはビールの売(う)り上(あ)げが伸(の)びる。
Beer sales increase in midsummer.

A literary word often seen in seasonal greetings (時候(じこう)の挨拶(あいさつ)) and written correspondence. 盛夏(せいか)の候(こう) is a set phrase used in formal summer letters. Compare with 真夏(まなつ) (midsummer — more colloquial), 猛暑(もうしょ) (fierce heat), and 酷暑(こくしょ) (extreme heat). A homophone of 成果(せいか) (result), 聖火(せいか) (sacred flame), and 製菓(せいか) (confectionery), but written with different kanji.
