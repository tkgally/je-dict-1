未納(みのう)

みのう
noun
unpaid; overdue; non-payment
1. unpaid; overdue; non-payment
The state of not having paid taxes, fees, premiums, or other required payments by the due date. A formal/administrative term.
税金(ぜいきん)が未納(みのう)になっている。
Taxes are unpaid.
保険料(ほけんりょう)の未納(みのう)が続(つづ)くと、保障(ほしょう)が受(う)けられなくなる。
If non-payment of insurance premiums continues, you won't be able to receive coverage.
家賃(やちん)の未納(みのう)が3か月(げつ)以上(いじょう)続(つづ)いた場合(ばあい)、契約(けいやく)解除(かいじょ)となることがあります。
If rent remains unpaid for more than three months, the lease may be terminated.

Part of the 未(み)～ (not yet) prefix pattern: 未納(みのう) (not yet paid), 未払(みはら)い (unpaid), 未着(みちゃく) (not yet arrived). Common collocations: 税金(ぜいきん)の未納(みのう) (unpaid taxes), 未納分(みのうぶん) (unpaid portion), 未納者(みのうしゃ) (non-payer). Distinguished from 滞納(たいのう) (delinquent payment) — 未納(みのう) is neutral and simply means 'not yet paid,' while 滞納(たいのう) implies willful non-payment or default.
