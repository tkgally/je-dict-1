1. to feel refreshed; to feel better; to cheer up; to have one's spirits lift
A feeling of emotional relief or brightening, as if clouds have cleared from one's mind. Used when worries or gloom dissipate and one feels lighter.
散歩(さんぽ)して気(き)が晴(は)れた。
I took a walk and felt refreshed.
友達(ともだち)と話(はな)したら、少(すこ)し気(き)が晴(は)れた。
After talking with my friend, I felt a bit better.
ずっと悩(なや)んでいたが、思(おも)い切(き)って相談(そうだん)したら気(き)が晴(は)れた。
I had been worrying for a long time, but when I took the plunge and talked to someone about it, I felt much better.

A 気(き) idiom. 晴(は)れる literally means 'to clear up' (as in weather), so the image is of clouds lifting from one's mind. Implies that the person was in a gloomy or burdened state beforehand.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気(き)が晴(は)れない — to not be able to cheer up
  • 気(き)が晴(は)れる思(おも)い — a refreshing feeling
  • やっと気(き)が晴(は)れた — I finally feel better

USAGE:
The negative form 気(き)が晴(は)れない (can't cheer up, can't shake the feeling) is equally common and describes lingering unease or dissatisfaction.

RELATED 気(き) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)が重(おも)い — to feel heavy-hearted
  • 気(き)が楽(らく)になる — to feel at ease
  • 気分(きぶん)転換(てんかん) — a change of pace (noun)
