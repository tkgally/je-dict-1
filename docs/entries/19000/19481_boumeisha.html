亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)

ぼうめいしゃ
noun
exile; defector; refugee (political)
1. exile; defector; political refugee
A person who has fled their country for political reasons, often seeking asylum in another country. Implies a deliberate escape from political persecution or an oppressive regime.
亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)を保護(ほご)する。
To give protection to a political exile.
彼(かれ)は亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)として受(う)け入(い)れられた。
He was accepted as a political refugee.
冷戦(れいせん)時代(じだい)には、東側(ひがしがわ)諸国(しょこく)から多(おお)くの亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)が西側(にしがわ)に逃(に)れた。
During the Cold War era, many defectors fled from Eastern Bloc countries to the West.

Composed of 亡命(ぼうめい) (flight from one's country, exile) + 者(しゃ) (person). Specifically refers to someone who has fled for political reasons — not economic migrants or general refugees. The word often appears in news reports and historical discussions about defectors, dissidents, and political exiles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 政治(せいじ)亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ) — political exile/refugee
  • 亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)を受(う)け入(い)れる — to accept/take in a defector
  • 亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)を保護(ほご)する — to protect an exile
  • 亡命者(ぼうめいしゃ)の証言(しょうげん) — testimony of a defector

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 難民(なんみん) — refugee; broader term covering all refugees, including those fleeing war or natural disaster
  • 亡命(ぼうめい) — exile, defection; the act itself rather than the person
  • 亡命(ぼうめい)政権(せいけん) — government in exile; a related compound
