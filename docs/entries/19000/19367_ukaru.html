1. to pass (an exam, test, audition)
To succeed in an examination or selection process. The intransitive counterpart of 受(う)ける, focusing on the result rather than the act of taking the test. The thing passed is marked with に.
試験(しけん)に受(う)かった。
I passed the exam.
大学(だいがく)に受(う)かるために毎日(まいにち)勉強(べんきょう)している。
I'm studying every day to get into university.
運転(うんてん)免許(めんきょ)の試験(しけん)に一発(いっぱつ)で受(う)かるのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to pass the driver's license exam on the first try.

受(う)かる is an intransitive result verb — you do not actively "do" passing; rather, the result of passing happens to you. This contrasts with 受(う)ける, which describes the action of taking a test.

USAGE:

  • The exam or institution passed is marked with に: 試験(しけん)に受(う)かる, 大学(だいがく)に受(う)かる
  • The opposite is 落(お)ちる (to fail an exam)

CONTRAST WITH 受(う)ける:

  • 受(う)ける means "to take" an exam (the action)
  • 受(う)かる means "to pass" an exam (the result)
  • 試験(しけん)を受(う)ける = to take an exam
  • 試験(しけん)に受(う)かる = to pass an exam

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 試験(しけん)に受(う)かる — to pass an exam
  • 大学(だいがく)に受(う)かる — to get into university
  • 面接(めんせつ)に受(う)かる — to pass an interview
  • 一発(いっぱつ)で受(う)かる — to pass on the first try
