1. formal dress, ceremonial attire, formal wear
Clothing worn for formal occasions such as weddings, funerals, official ceremonies, or state events. Includes both Western formal wear and traditional Japanese formal kimono.
礼装(れいそう)で出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended in formal attire.
結婚式(けっこんしき)には礼装(れいそう)が求(もと)められる。
Formal dress is required at weddings.
招待状(しょうたいじょう)に「礼装(れいそう)でお越(こ)しください」と書(か)いてあった。
The invitation said, 'Please come in formal attire.'

USAGE:
Distinguished from 正装(せいそう) (full formal dress), which is the most formal level. 礼装(れいそう) covers a range from semi-formal to full formal. Japanese 礼装(れいそう) may include 紋付(もんつ)き袴(はかま) for men or 留袖(とめそで) for women.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 礼装(れいそう)する: to dress formally
  • 礼装(れいそう)で出席(しゅっせき)する: to attend in formal wear
  • 略礼装(りゃくれいそう): semi-formal dress
  • 和装(わそう)の礼装(れいそう): formal Japanese attire
