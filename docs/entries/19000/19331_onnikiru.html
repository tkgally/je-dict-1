恩(おん)に着(き)る

おんにきる
verb (一段, transitive)
to feel grateful; to be indebted; to appreciate a favor
1. to feel grateful; to be indebted; to appreciate a favor
To feel a sense of gratitude and indebtedness toward someone who has helped you. The image is of 'wearing' (着(き)る) someone's kindness (恩(おん)) — carrying their goodwill with you.
恩(おん)に着(き)ます。
I'm much obliged.
助(たす)けてくれたこと、恩(おん)に着(き)るよ。
I really appreciate you helping me out.
あのとき声(こえ)をかけてくださったこと、今(いま)でも恩(おん)に着(き)ています。
I'm still grateful that you spoke to me at that time.

Literally means 'to wear a debt of gratitude.' The metaphor of 着(き)る (to wear) gives the expression a sense of carrying someone's kindness with you, close to your body.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 恩(おん)に着(き)ます — I'm much obliged (polite)
  • 恩(おん)に着(き)るよ — I owe you one (casual)
  • 一生(いっしょう)恩(おん)に着(き)ます — I'll be grateful for the rest of my life

USAGE:
Expresses deeper gratitude than a simple ありがとう. Often used when someone has done a significant favor. The expression can sound slightly old-fashioned or dramatic, which makes it effective for emphasizing sincere appreciation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 感謝(かんしゃ)する — to be grateful (more standard and versatile)
  • ありがたい — grateful, thankful (adjective; more everyday)
  • 恩(おん)を感(かん)じる — to feel gratitude (similar but less idiomatic)
