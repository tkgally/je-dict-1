乱雑(らんざつ)な

らんざつな
na-adjective
cluttered; messy; disordered
1. cluttered; messy; disordered; untidy
Describes a state of physical disorder or lack of organization in a space or arrangement.
部屋(へや)が乱雑(らんざつ)だ。
The room is messy.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)が乱雑(らんざつ)に散(ち)らかっている。
The top of the desk is cluttered and messy.
書類(しょるい)が乱雑(らんざつ)に積(つ)み上(あ)げられていて、必要(ひつよう)なものが見(み)つからない。
Documents are piled up in a disorderly way, and I can't find what I need.

Used for physical spaces and arrangements rather than abstract concepts. Common collocations include 乱雑(らんざつ)な部屋(へや) (a messy room), 乱雑(らんざつ)に置(お)く (to place haphazardly), and 乱雑(らんざつ)な状態(じょうたい) (a disordered state). Compare with 散(ち)らかっている (scattered around, more colloquial) and 雑然(ざつぜん)とした (jumbled, similar formality level).
