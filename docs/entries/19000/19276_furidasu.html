1. to issue; to draw (a check or bill)
To issue a financial instrument such as a check, bill of exchange, or money order.
小切手(こぎって)を振(ふ)り出(だ)す。
To issue a check.
銀行(ぎんこう)から為替(かわせ)を振(ふ)り出(だ)した。
I drew a money order from the bank.
百万(ひゃくまん)円(えん)の手形(てがた)を振(ふ)り出(だ)した。
A bill of exchange for one million yen was issued.
2. to shake out; to sprinkle; to scatter
To shake something out of a container or distribute by shaking.
袋(ふくろ)から塩(しお)を振(ふ)り出(だ)す。
To shake salt out of a bag.
中身(なかみ)を全部(ぜんぶ)振(ふ)り出(だ)した。
I shook out all the contents.
カバンの中身(なかみ)を机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に振(ふ)り出(だ)して探(さが)した。
I emptied the contents of my bag onto the desk to search.

A compound verb combining 振(ふ)る (to shake/wave) and 出(だ)す (to put out). Sense 1 (financial) is the more commonly encountered meaning in formal/business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小切手(こぎって)を振(ふ)り出(だ)す: to issue a check
  • 手形(てがた)を振(ふ)り出(だ)す: to draw a bill of exchange
  • 為替(かわせ)を振(ふ)り出(だ)す: to issue a money order

RELATED EXPRESSION:

  • 振(ふ)り出(だ)しに戻(もど)る: to go back to square one — from the board game すごろく, where 振(ふ)り出(だ)し is the starting point

NOTE:
Not to be confused with 降(ふ)り出(だ)す (to start raining/snowing), which uses a different kanji.
