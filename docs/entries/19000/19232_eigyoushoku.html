営業職(えいぎょうしょく)

えいぎょうしょく
noun
sales position; sales job
1. sales position; sales job; sales role
A job category focused on selling products or services to customers or clients. One of the most common job types in Japanese companies.
営業職(えいぎょうしょく)に就(つ)く。
To take a sales position.
営業職(えいぎょうしょく)は人(ひと)と話(はな)すのが好(す)きな人(ひと)に向(む)いている。
Sales jobs are suited for people who like talking to others.
大学(だいがく)を卒業(そつぎょう)して最初(さいしょ)に配属(はいぞく)されたのは営業職(えいぎょうしょく)だった。
The first position I was assigned to after graduating from university was in sales.

Composed of 営業(えいぎょう) (sales/business) + 職(しょく) (occupation). Related job categories: 事務職(じむしょく) (office/clerical work), 技術職(ぎじゅつしょく) (technical position), 企画職(きかくしょく) (planning position). In Japanese job hunting (就活(しゅうかつ)), 営業職(えいぎょうしょく) is one of the most commonly recruited positions.
