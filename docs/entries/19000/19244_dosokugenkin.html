土足厳禁(どそくげんきん)

どそくげんきん
noun
no shoes allowed; shoes strictly prohibited
1. no shoes allowed; shoes strictly prohibited
A common sign or rule indicating that outdoor shoes must not be worn in a particular area. Found at the entrance of Japanese homes, temples, certain restaurants, and other spaces where shoes must be removed.
ここは土足厳禁(どそくげんきん)です。
No shoes are allowed here.
入口(いりぐち)に「土足厳禁(どそくげんきん)」の表示(ひょうじ)があった。
There was a 'no shoes' sign at the entrance.
この体育館(たいいくかん)は土足厳禁(どそくげんきん)なので、上履(うわば)きに履(は)き替(か)えてください。
This gymnasium is a no-shoes area, so please change into indoor shoes.

Composed of 土足(どそく) (outdoor shoes/shod feet) + 厳禁(げんきん) (strictly prohibited). A very common sign in Japan. 土足(どそく) literally means 'earth-footed' — feet soiled from walking outdoors. 厳禁(げんきん) is a strong prohibition. Visitors to Japan often encounter this at temples, traditional restaurants (旅館(りょかん)), and schools.
