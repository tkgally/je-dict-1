軽装(けいそう)

けいそう
noun, な-adjective
light clothing; light attire; light equipment
1. light clothing; light attire; light equipment
Wearing light, minimal clothing or equipment. Used for both everyday clothing choices and travel/outdoor contexts where carrying less is preferred.
軽装(けいそう)で出(で)かけた。
I went out in light clothing.
夏(なつ)は軽装(けいそう)で過(す)ごしたい。
I want to spend summer in light clothes.
山登(やまのぼ)りには軽装(けいそう)すぎると危険(きけん)だ。
It's dangerous to be too lightly equipped for mountain climbing.

Describes wearing light, minimal clothing or carrying light equipment. Can function as a noun (軽装(けいそう)で — in light clothing) or as a な-adjective (軽装(けいそう)な服装(ふくそう) — light attire).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 軽装(けいそう)で出(で)かける: to go out in light clothing
  • 軽装(けいそう)で登山(とざん)する: to climb a mountain lightly equipped
  • 軽装(けいそう)な格好(かっこう): a casual/light outfit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 薄着(うすぎ): wearing thin clothes — focuses specifically on the thinness of fabric, while 軽装(けいそう) is broader and can include carrying less gear
  • 重装備(じゅうそうび): heavy equipment — the opposite concept, especially in outdoor/military contexts

USAGE:
Often used in weather-related advice, outdoor activity warnings, and dress code contexts. Sometimes carries a nuance of being insufficiently prepared.
