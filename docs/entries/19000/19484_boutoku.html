1. blasphemy; profanation; desecration
The act of showing contempt or disrespect toward something sacred, revered, or deeply valued. Can refer to religious blasphemy or more broadly to treating something precious with disrespect.
神(かみ)への冒涜(ぼうとく)だ。
That is blasphemy against God.
死者(ししゃ)を冒涜(ぼうとく)するような行為(こうい)は許(ゆる)されない。
Acts that desecrate the dead are unforgivable.
あの発言(はつげん)は芸術(げいじゅつ)に対(たい)する冒涜(ぼうとく)であり、多(おお)くの批判(ひはん)を浴(あ)びた。
That remark was a desecration of art and drew much criticism.

A strong, serious word expressing contempt or violation of something sacred or highly valued. While it can refer to religious blasphemy, in Japanese it is more broadly applied to any act that profanes or degrades something considered important — the memory of the dead, art, human dignity, or cherished traditions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冒涜(ぼうとく)する — to blaspheme, to desecrate
  • 神(かみ)への冒涜(ぼうとく) — blasphemy against God
  • 人権(じんけん)の冒涜(ぼうとく) — an affront to human rights
  • 冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)な — blasphemous, sacrilegious

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 侮辱(ぶじょく) — insult, affront; directed at a person's dignity rather than something sacred
  • 不敬(ふけい) — disrespect, irreverence; showing lack of proper respect, lighter than 冒涜(ぼうとく)
  • 汚(けが)す — to defile, to pollute; a verb meaning to make something impure
