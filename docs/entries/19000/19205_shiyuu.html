1. private ownership; belonging to a private individual
Ownership by a private person or entity, as opposed to public or government ownership. Used in legal, economic, and property contexts.
私有(しゆう)地(ち)に立(た)ち入(い)り禁止(きんし)。
No trespassing on private property.
この森(もり)は私有(しゆう)林(りん)です。
This forest is privately owned.
私有(しゆう)財産(ざいさん)の権利(けんり)は憲法(けんぽう)で保障(ほしょう)されている。
The right to private property is guaranteed by the constitution.

Composed of 私(し) (private, personal) + 有(ゆう) (possess, own). The opposite is 公有(こうゆう) (public ownership) or 国有(こくゆう) (state ownership). Common collocations: 私有(しゆう)地(ち) (private land), 私有(しゆう)財産(ざいさん) (private property), 私有(しゆう)林(りん) (private forest), 私有(しゆう)物(ぶつ) (private belongings), 私有(しゆう)化(か) (privatization). Part of the 〜有(ゆう) ownership family: 所有(しょゆう) (ownership), 共有(きょうゆう) (shared ownership), 国有(こくゆう) (state-owned).
