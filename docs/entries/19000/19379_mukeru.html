1. to peel off; to come off; to flake off
For skin, bark, paint, or a surface layer to come off naturally or due to damage. An intransitive verb — the peeling happens on its own.
日焼(ひや)けで皮(かわ)が剥(む)けた。
My skin peeled from the sunburn.
壁(かべ)のペンキが剥(む)けてきた。
The paint on the wall has started to peel off.
鼻(はな)の頭(あたま)の皮(かわ)が剥(む)けて赤(あか)くなっている。
The skin on the tip of my nose has peeled off and turned red.

Describes surfaces, coatings, or skin coming off naturally or as a result of damage. As an intransitive verb, the peeling happens on its own without someone actively removing it.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 剥(む)ける (intransitive) — to peel off (by itself)
  • 剥(む)く (transitive) — to peel (something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 皮(かわ)が剥(む)ける — skin peels
  • ペンキが剥(む)ける — paint peels off
  • 塗装(とそう)が剥(む)ける — coating peels off

CONTRAST WITH 剥(は)がれる:

  • 剥(む)ける — for thin layers like skin, bark, paint
  • 剥(は)がれる — for things stuck or attached coming off (labels, stickers, wallpaper)
