模写(もしゃ)

もしゃ
noun, verb-suru
copying; reproduction; tracing
1. copying; reproduction; tracing
The act of reproducing a work of art, text, or image by hand, typically for study or practice. Implies careful, faithful reproduction rather than creative reinterpretation.
名画(めいが)を模写(もしゃ)する。
To copy a masterpiece painting.
美術(びじゅつ)の授業(じゅぎょう)で模写(もしゃ)の練習(れんしゅう)をした。
I practiced copying in art class.
ゴッホは若(わか)い頃(ころ)、日本(にほん)の浮世絵(うきよえ)を模写(もしゃ)して技法(ぎほう)を学(まな)んだ。
In his youth, Van Gogh copied Japanese ukiyo-e prints to learn technique.

Refers to the practice of carefully reproducing existing artwork or text by hand. In art education, 模写(もしゃ) is considered a legitimate and important method of learning technique. The word does not carry a negative connotation of plagiarism — it implies study and practice.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 模写(もしゃ)する — to copy, to reproduce
  • 名画(めいが)の模写(もしゃ) — reproduction of a masterpiece
  • 模写(もしゃ)作品(さくひん) — a copied/reproduced work
  • 忠実(ちゅうじつ)に模写(もしゃ)する — to faithfully reproduce

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 複写(ふくしゃ) — copying, duplication; mechanical reproduction (e.g., photocopying), not hand-copying
  • 写生(しゃせい) — sketching from life; drawing from a real scene or object, not from another artwork
  • 模倣(もほう) — imitation; broader concept of imitating style or method, not just visual copying
  • 臨書(りんしょ) — copying calligraphy; specifically for practicing calligraphy by copying a model
