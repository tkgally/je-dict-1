的(まと)

まと
noun
target; mark; bull's-eye
1. target; mark; bull's-eye
A physical target to aim at, such as in archery or shooting. Also used figuratively for the object of attention, criticism, or focus.
的(まと)に当(あ)たった。
It hit the target.
批判(ひはん)の的(まと)になっている。
It has become the target of criticism.
彼(かれ)の発言(はつげん)は的(まと)を射(い)ていて、全員(ぜんいん)が納得(なっとく)した。
His remarks were right on target, and everyone was convinced.

A target to aim at, both literally and figuratively. The figurative use — as the object of criticism, attention, or ridicule — is extremely common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 的(まと)に当(あ)たる — to hit the target
  • 的(まと)を射(い)る — to hit the mark; to be spot-on
  • 的(まと)を絞(しぼ)る — to narrow one's target/focus
  • 的(まと)を外(はず)す — to miss the mark
  • 批判(ひはん)の的(まと) — target of criticism
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)の的(まと) — center of attention
  • 嘲笑(ちょうしょう)の的(まと) — object of ridicule

IDIOM:
的(まと)を射(い)る literally means 'to hit the target' but is used idiomatically to mean 'to be accurate/on point.' Note: the correct form is 的(まと)を射(い)る, not 的(まと)を得(え)る, though the latter has become widely used.

NOTE ON 的(てき):
The same kanji is read てき as a suffix meaning '-like, -ical' (e.g., 科学的(かがくてき) 'scientific'), which is a completely different word.
