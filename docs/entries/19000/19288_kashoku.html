過食(かしょく)

かしょく
noun
overeating; binge eating
1. overeating; binge eating
Eating excessively, beyond what is needed or healthy. Also used as a clinical term for binge eating in medical and psychological contexts.
過食(かしょく)は体(からだ)に悪(わる)い。
Overeating is bad for your health.
ストレスで過食(かしょく)してしまう。
I end up overeating because of stress.
過食(かしょく)と拒食(きょしょく)を繰(く)り返(かえ)す摂食障害(せっしょくしょうがい)に悩(なや)んでいる。
I suffer from an eating disorder that cycles between binge eating and food refusal.

Compound of 過(か) (excess) + 食(しょく) (eating). Can be used casually for general overeating, but also functions as a clinical term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 過食(かしょく)する — to overeat (する verb)
  • 過食症(かしょくしょう) — binge eating disorder; bulimia
  • 過食(かしょく)嘔吐(おうと) — binge-purge cycle
  • 過食(かしょく)に走(はし)る — to turn to overeating

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(た)べ過(す)ぎ — eating too much (everyday expression)
  • 暴飲暴食(ぼういんぼうしょく) — eating and drinking excessively
  • 拒食(きょしょく) — food refusal, anorexia (opposite)
