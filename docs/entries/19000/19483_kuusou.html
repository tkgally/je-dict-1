空想(くうそう)

くうそう
noun, verb-suru
fantasy; daydream; imagination
1. fantasy; daydream; imagining
The act of imagining things that are unrealistic or unlikely to happen. Can refer to both pleasant daydreaming and impractical, groundless ideas.
空想(くうそう)にふける。
To be lost in fantasy.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、よく空想(くうそう)の世界(せかい)で遊(あそ)んだ。
As a child, I often played in an imaginary world.
そんな計画(けいかく)は空想(くうそう)にすぎないと批判(ひはん)された。
That plan was criticized as nothing more than fantasy.

Can carry either a neutral/positive tone (creative imagination, daydreaming) or a negative tone (unrealistic, groundless thinking). The context determines which nuance is intended. The word appears in the literary genre term 空想(くうそう)科学(かがく)小説(しょうせつ) (science fiction), though SF/エスエフ is more common today.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空想(くうそう)にふける — to be absorbed in fantasy
  • 空想(くうそう)の世界(せかい) — fantasy world, imaginary world
  • 空想(くうそう)科学(かがく)小説(しょうせつ) — science fiction (formal/dated term)
  • 空想(くうそう)的(てき) — fantastical, fanciful

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 妄想(もうそう) — delusion; stronger negative connotation, implies irrational or obsessive thinking
  • 想像(そうぞう) — imagination; neutral, broader term for the act of imagining
  • 夢想(むそう) — reverie, dreaming; more literary and poetic
  • 幻想(げんそう) — illusion, fantasy; implies something visionary or unreal
