鮭(さけ)

さけ
noun
salmon
See also: 酒(さけ) (alcohol, sake)
1. salmon
The fish, especially the chum salmon (Oncorhynchus keta) native to Japanese waters. One of the most important food fish in Japanese cuisine.
鮭(さけ)を焼(や)く。
Grill salmon.
今朝(けさ)は焼(や)き鮭(ざけ)と味噌汁(みそしる)だった。
This morning I had grilled salmon and miso soup.
北海道(ほっかいどう)の川(かわ)では秋(あき)になると鮭(さけ)が遡上(そじょう)してくる。
In autumn, salmon swim upstream in the rivers of Hokkaido.

The standard word for salmon in Japanese. The reading さけ is the most common, though しゃけ is also widely used in everyday speech, especially in eastern Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焼(や)き鮭(ざけ) — grilled salmon
  • 鮭(さけ)の切(き)り身(み) — salmon fillet
  • 鮭(さけ)フレーク — salmon flakes
  • 鮭(さけ)おにぎり — salmon rice ball
  • 鮭(さけ)とば — dried salmon strips (Hokkaido specialty)

PRONUNCIATION NOTE:
Both さけ and しゃけ are correct. In compound words, the reading often becomes ざけ (e.g., 焼(や)き鮭(ざけ), 塩(しお)鮭(ざけ)).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サーモン — salmon (used for raw/sashimi preparations, often referring to farmed Atlantic salmon)
  • 鱒(ます) — trout (a related but distinct fish)

Related Words

Homophone: 酒(さけ) (alcohol)
Contrast: サーモン (Atlantic salmon (raw/sushi))
