加盟国(かめいこく)

かめいこく
noun
member state; member nation
1. member state; member nation
A country that belongs to an international organization, treaty, or alliance. Frequently used in the context of the United Nations, EU, and other international bodies.
EU加盟国(かめいこく)は二十七(にじゅうなな)か国(こく)ある。
There are 27 EU member states.
新(あら)たな加盟国(かめいこく)を迎(むか)えた。
A new member state was welcomed.
国連(こくれん)加盟国(かめいこく)の代表(だいひょう)が総会(そうかい)に集(あつ)まった。
Representatives from UN member states gathered at the general assembly.

Composed of 加盟(かめい) (joining, affiliation) + 国(こく) (country). Common collocations: 加盟国(かめいこく)を増(ふ)やす (to increase member states), 加盟国(かめいこく)の義務(ぎむ) (obligations of member states). Often preceded by the organization name: 国連(こくれん)加盟国(かめいこく) (UN member state), EU加盟国(かめいこく) (EU member state), NATO加盟国(かめいこく) (NATO member state). Compare with 加盟(かめい)する (to join/become a member).
