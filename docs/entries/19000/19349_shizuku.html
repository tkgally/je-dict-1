雫(しずく)

しずく
noun
drop; droplet; drip
1. drop; droplet; drip
A small drop of liquid, such as a raindrop, teardrop, or dewdrop. Carries a poetic, evocative quality and is common in literary and artistic contexts.
葉(は)っぱから雫(しずく)が落(お)ちた。
A droplet fell from the leaf.
雨(あめ)の雫(しずく)が窓(まど)ガラスを伝(つた)っている。
Raindrops are running down the window glass.
朝露(あさつゆ)の雫(しずく)が光(ひかり)に照(て)らされて、宝石(ほうせき)のように輝(かがや)いていた。
The dewdrops were lit by the light, sparkling like jewels.

A poetic word for a drop of liquid. While 滴(しずく) is the standard kanji, 雫(しずく) (which combines 雨(あめ) and 下(した)) is widely recognized and often preferred for its visual beauty, especially in names and titles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨(あめ)の雫(しずく) — raindrop
  • 涙(なみだ)の雫(しずく) — teardrop
  • 露(つゆ)の雫(しずく) — dewdrop
  • 雫(しずく)が落(お)ちる — a drop falls
  • 雫(しずく)が垂(た)れる — a drop drips down

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 雫(しずく) is a Japanese-made character (kokuji) combining rain (雨(あめ)) and down (下(した)). The alternative kanji 滴(しずく) is more standard in dictionaries but 雫(しずく) is popular in creative contexts.

AS A NAME:
雫(しずく) is a popular feminine given name in Japan, evoking purity and beauty. It gained wider recognition through the Studio Ghibli film "Whisper of the Heart" (耳(みみ)をすませば), whose protagonist is named 雫(しずく).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 滴(しずく) — drop (same word, different kanji, more standard)
  • 一滴(いってき) — a single drop (more formal/measured)
