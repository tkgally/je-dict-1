1. reinforcement; strengthening; augmentation; buildup
The act of making something stronger or more powerful by adding to it. Often used for military forces, organizational capacity, or systems.
戦力(せんりょく)を増強(ぞうきょう)する。
To reinforce military strength.
セキュリティ体制(たいせい)の増強(ぞうきょう)が求(もと)められている。
Strengthening of the security system is being demanded.
災害(さいがい)に備(そな)えて、防災(ぼうさい)体制(たいせい)の増強(ぞうきょう)を進(すす)めている。
In preparation for disasters, they are working to strengthen the disaster prevention system.

増強(ぞうきょう) means to increase strength or capacity by adding more of something — people, equipment, resources, or capability.

WORD FORMATION:
増(ぞう) (increase) + 強(きょう) (strengthen).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 戦力(せんりょく)増強(ぞうきょう): force reinforcement
  • 軍備(ぐんび)増強(ぞうきょう): military buildup
  • 体制(たいせい)を増強(ぞうきょう)する: to strengthen a system
  • 人員(じんいん)を増強(ぞうきょう)する: to increase personnel

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 強化(きょうか): strengthening (more general and common in everyday use)
  • 補強(ほきょう): reinforcement (often physical structures or adding support)
  • 拡充(かくじゅう): expansion and enhancement (broadening scope)
