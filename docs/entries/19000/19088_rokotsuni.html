露骨(ろこつ)に

ろこつに
adverb
openly; blatantly; bluntly
1. openly; blatantly; bluntly; overtly
Doing or expressing something in an undisguised, obvious way, often with a negative connotation suggesting a lack of tact or subtlety.
彼(かれ)は露骨(ろこつ)に嫌(いや)な顔(かお)をした。
He openly showed his displeasure.
露骨(ろこつ)に無視(むし)されて傷(きず)ついた。
I was hurt by being blatantly ignored.
あの店員(てんいん)は客(きゃく)によって露骨(ろこつ)に態度(たいど)を変(か)えるので評判(ひょうばん)が悪(わる)い。
That shop clerk has a bad reputation because they blatantly change their attitude depending on the customer.

Usually carries a negative or critical nuance, suggesting the person is being too obvious or lacking in social grace. Common collocations include 露骨(ろこつ)に嫌(いや)がる (to openly show dislike), 露骨(ろこつ)な態度(たいど) (a blatant attitude), and 露骨(ろこつ)な表現(ひょうげん) (explicit expression). The な-adjective form 露骨(ろこつ)な is equally common. Can also describe sexually explicit content: 露骨(ろこつ)な描写(びょうしゃ) (explicit depiction).
