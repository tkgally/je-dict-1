1. re-employment; getting a new job after leaving a previous one
Finding employment again after having left or lost a previous job. Covers situations such as returning to work after retirement, layoff, or a career break.
再就職(さいしゅうしょく)が決(き)まった。
I found a new job.
定年後(ていねんご)の再就職(さいしゅうしょく)は簡単(かんたん)ではない。
Re-employment after retirement is not easy.
再就職(さいしゅうしょく)支援(しえん)プログラムを利用(りよう)して、新(あたら)しい仕事(しごと)を見(み)つけた。
I found a new job by using a re-employment support program.

A noun that also functions with する: 再就職(さいしゅうしょく)する (to find re-employment). Common collocations: 再就職(さいしゅうしょく)先(さき) (new place of employment), 再就職(さいしゅうしょく)支援(しえん) (job placement assistance), 再就職(さいしゅうしょく)活動(かつどう) (job hunting activities). In Japan, 再就職(さいしゅうしょく) is a major topic for people who have taken 育児休暇(いくじきゅうか) (childcare leave) or who face 定年(ていねん) (mandatory retirement). Related: 転職(てんしょく) (changing jobs, usually while still employed).
