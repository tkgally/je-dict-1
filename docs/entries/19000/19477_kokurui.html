穀類(こくるい)

こくるい
noun
grains; cereals
1. grains; cereals
The collective term for cereal grains such as rice, wheat, barley, and corn. Used in food, agriculture, and nutrition contexts.
穀類(こくるい)は主食(しゅしょく)として大切(たいせつ)だ。
Grains are important as a staple food.
穀類(こくるい)の価格(かかく)が上昇(じょうしょう)している。
Grain prices are rising.
健康(けんこう)のために、精製(せいせい)されていない穀類(こくるい)を多(おお)く取(と)るようにしている。
For my health, I try to eat more unrefined grains.

A general term encompassing cereal grains — 米(こめ) (rice), 麦(むぎ) (wheat/barley), 玉蜀黍(とうもろこし) (corn), 粟(あわ) (millet), etc. Commonly used in nutrition labels, agricultural reports, and food science. In everyday conversation, specific grain names are more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 穀類(こくるい)を食(た)べる — to eat grains
  • 全粒(ぜんりゅう)穀類(こくるい) — whole grains
  • 穀類(こくるい)の生産(せいさん) — grain production
  • 穀類(こくるい)の価格(かかく) — grain prices

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 穀物(こくもつ) — grain, cereal; very similar meaning, slightly more common in general contexts
  • 雑穀(ざっこく) — miscellaneous grains (millet, sorghum, etc.); grains other than rice and wheat
  • 主食(しゅしょく) — staple food; broader category that includes grains but also potatoes, bread, etc.
