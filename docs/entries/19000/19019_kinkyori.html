近距離(きんきょり)

きんきょり
noun
short distance; close range
1. short distance, close range, short-range
A relatively small physical distance. Used for travel, communication, combat, and other contexts involving proximity.
近距離(きんきょり)の通勤(つうきん)は楽(らく)だ。
A short-distance commute is easy.
近距離(きんきょり)から写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)った。
I took a photo from close range.
近距離(きんきょり)の引(ひ)っ越(こ)しなら自分(じぶん)でできるかもしれない。
If it's a short-distance move, you might be able to do it yourself.

近距離(きんきょり) indicates a short or close-range distance. A compound of 近(きん) (near) + 距離(きょり) (distance). Commonly used as a modifier before nouns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 近距離(きんきょり)通勤(つうきん): short-distance commute
  • 近距離(きんきょり)恋愛(れんあい): relationship with someone nearby (vs long-distance)
  • 近距離(きんきょり)攻撃(こうげき): close-range attack
  • 近距離(きんきょり)無線(むせん): short-range wireless
  • 近距離(きんきょり)引(ひ)っ越(こ)し: short-distance move

CONTRAST:

  • 長距離(ちょうきょり): long distance (opposite)
  • 中距離(ちゅうきょり): medium distance
  • 短距離(たんきょり): short distance (more commonly used for races and sprints)

Related Words

Antonym: 遠距離(えんきょり) (long distance)
