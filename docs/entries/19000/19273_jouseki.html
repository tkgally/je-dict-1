定石(じょうせき)

じょうせき
noun
established pattern; standard move; textbook approach
1. standard sequence of moves (in Go)
In the board game Go, a well-studied sequence of moves that is considered optimal for both players in a given position.
定石(じょうせき)を覚(おぼ)えることが上達(じょうたつ)の近道(ちかみち)だ。
Memorizing standard moves is a shortcut to improvement.
相手(あいて)が定石(じょうせき)を外(はず)してきた。
My opponent deviated from the standard sequence.
新(あたら)しい定石(じょうせき)が研究(けんきゅう)されている。
New standard sequences are being studied.
2. established pattern; textbook approach; proven method
By extension, any well-established, proven approach or standard procedure in a field. Implies a method that has been tested and accepted as effective.
マーケティングの定石(じょうせき)に従(したが)った。
I followed the textbook approach to marketing.
定石(じょうせき)どおりにやれば失敗(しっぱい)しない。
If you follow the established method, you won't fail.
定石(じょうせき)を破(やぶ)る大胆(だいたん)な戦略(せんりゃく)で成功(せいこう)した。
They succeeded with a bold strategy that broke from the established approach.

Originally a Go (囲碁(いご)) term, now widely used figuratively in business, strategy, and daily life to mean "the standard/proven approach."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 定石(じょうせき)どおり: according to the textbook approach
  • 定石(じょうせき)を外(はず)す: to deviate from the standard
  • 定石(じょうせき)を破(やぶ)る: to break from convention
  • 定石(じょうせき)を踏(ふ)む: to follow established practice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 常套(じょうとう)手段(しゅだん): standard tactic — can have a negative nuance (hackneyed)
  • セオリー: theory, standard practice — loanword, more casual
  • 王道(おうどう): the royal road, the orthodox approach — implies the best or most legitimate method

NOTE:
Also read as ていせき in some contexts, though じょうせき is the standard reading.
