1. points lost; runs conceded; goal conceded
Points or goals given up to the opposing team in a sport or competition. Used in baseball, soccer, and other sports to describe scoring against one's own team.
失点(しってん)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent conceding a goal.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)は失点(しってん)ゼロだった。
Today's game was a shutout.
後半(こうはん)に二(に)失点(しってん)して逆転(ぎゃくてん)された。
They conceded two goals in the second half and the game was turned around.

失点(しってん) describes points or goals given up to the opponent. Used across many sports including soccer, baseball, and basketball.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 失点(しってん)する: to concede a point/goal
  • 失点(しってん)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent conceding
  • 失点(しってん)を許(ゆる)す: to allow a score
  • 無失点(むしってん): no goals conceded (shutout)
  • 最少(さいしょう)失点(しってん): fewest goals conceded

CONTRAST:

  • 得点(とくてん): scoring a point/goal (the opposite)
  • 自責点(じせきてん): earned run (baseball-specific; points attributed to the pitcher's errors)

USAGE:
Very common in sports commentary and news. Focuses on the defensive side — how many points a team gave up rather than scored.
