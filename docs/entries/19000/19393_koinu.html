子犬(こいぬ)

こいぬ
noun
puppy
1. puppy; young dog
A young dog, typically less than one year old.
子犬(こいぬ)がしっぽを振(ふ)っている。
The puppy is wagging its tail.
隣(となり)の家(いえ)で子犬(こいぬ)が生(う)まれたので、見(み)に行(い)った。
Puppies were born at the neighbor's house, so I went to see them.
ペットショップで見(み)た子犬(こいぬ)がかわいくて、つい飼(か)いたくなってしまった。
The puppy I saw at the pet shop was so cute that I couldn't help wanting to keep it.

子犬(こいぬ) refers to a young dog, typically under one year old. It is formed from 子(こ) (child, young) + 犬(いぬ) (dog), following a productive pattern used for other young animals: 子猫(こねこ) (kitten), 子馬(こうま) (foal).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 子犬(こいぬ)を飼(か)う: to keep/raise a puppy
  • 子犬(こいぬ)を拾(ひろ)う: to find/pick up a stray puppy
  • 子犬(こいぬ)が生(う)まれる: puppies are born

COUNTER: 匹(ひき) (for small animals).
