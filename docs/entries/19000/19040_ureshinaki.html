嬉(うれ)し泣(な)き

うれしなき
noun
crying for joy; tears of happiness
1. crying for joy; tears of happiness
Shedding tears out of happiness, relief, or deep emotion rather than sadness. A compound of 嬉しい (happy) and 泣き (crying).
嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きをする。
To cry tears of joy.
合格(ごうかく)を知(し)って嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きした。
I cried for joy when I found out I had passed.
選手(せんしゅ)たちは優勝(ゆうしょう)が決(き)まった瞬間(しゅんかん)、嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きしていた。
The players were crying tears of joy the moment the championship was decided.

嬉(うれ)し泣(な)き means crying from happiness or overwhelming joy.

WORD FORMATION:
嬉(うれ)しい (happy) + 泣(な)き (crying, noun form of 泣(な)く)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きをする / 嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きする: to cry for joy
  • 嬉(うれ)し泣(な)きの涙(なみだ): tears of joy

TYPICAL SITUATIONS:

  • Winning a competition (優勝(ゆうしょう))
  • Passing an exam (合格(ごうかく))
  • Being reunited with someone
  • Receiving unexpected good news

CONTRAST:

  • 悔(くや)し泣(な)き: crying out of frustration or regret
  • 泣(な)き笑(わら)い: laughing through tears
