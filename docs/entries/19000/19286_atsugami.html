厚紙(あつがみ)

あつがみ
noun
cardboard; thick paper
1. cardboard; thick paper
Thick, stiff paper used for crafts, packaging, backing for documents, and similar purposes. Thicker than regular paper but not as heavy as corrugated cardboard.
厚紙(あつがみ)で箱(はこ)を作(つく)る。
I make a box out of cardboard.
写真(しゃしん)を送(おく)るときは厚紙(あつがみ)で挟(はさ)んでください。
When mailing photos, please sandwich them between pieces of cardboard.
子(こ)どもたちが厚紙(あつがみ)とハサミを使(つか)って工作(こうさく)をしている。
The children are doing crafts with cardboard and scissors.

Compound of 厚(あつ)い (thick) + 紙(かみ) (paper). Refers to stiff paper stock, not corrugated cardboard (which is 段(だん)ボール).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厚紙(あつがみ)で作(つく)る — to make from cardboard
  • 厚紙(あつがみ)を切(き)る — to cut cardboard
  • 厚紙(あつがみ)に印刷(いんさつ)する — to print on cardstock

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 段(だん)ボール — corrugated cardboard (heavier, used for shipping boxes)
  • 画用紙(がようし) — drawing paper (thick but for art use)
  • 薄紙(うすがみ) — thin paper, tissue paper (opposite in thickness)
