霊園(れいえん)

れいえん
noun
cemetery; memorial park
1. cemetery; memorial park
A cemetery or burial ground, often a large, well-maintained park-like facility. Used for both public and private cemeteries.
霊園(れいえん)にお墓参(はかまい)りに行(い)く。
To go visit a grave at the cemetery.
市営(しえい)霊園(れいえん)の区画(くかく)を申(もう)し込(こ)んだ。
I applied for a plot at the municipal cemetery.
この霊園(れいえん)は緑(みどり)が多(おお)く、静(しず)かな環境(かんきょう)にある。
This cemetery has lots of greenery and is in a quiet setting.

Often used in the formal names of cemeteries: 多磨(たま)霊園(れいえん) (Tama Cemetery), 青山(あおやま)霊園(れいえん) (Aoyama Cemetery). Common collocations: 公営(こうえい)霊園(れいえん) (public cemetery), 民営(みんえい)霊園(れいえん) (private cemetery), 霊園(れいえん)の管理(かんり) (cemetery maintenance). Compare with 墓地(ぼち) (graveyard — more general), 墓園(ぼえん) (cemetery — less common). 霊園(れいえん) tends to suggest a larger, more modern, park-style facility.
