無糖(むとう)

むとう
noun
sugar-free; unsweetened
1. sugar-free; unsweetened; no sugar added
Contains no sugar. Commonly seen on food and beverage labels.
無糖(むとう)のコーヒーをください。
I'll have an unsweetened coffee, please.
最近(さいきん)は無糖(むとう)の飲(の)み物(もの)が人気(にんき)だ。
Sugar-free beverages are popular these days.
健康(けんこう)のために、無糖(むとう)のヨーグルトを毎朝(まいあさ)食(た)べている。
For my health, I eat unsweetened yogurt every morning.

Commonly seen on product labels as 無糖(むとう) or in the の-adjective form 無糖(むとう)の. Part of a series of food labeling terms: 無糖(むとう) (sugar-free), 低糖(ていとう) (low sugar), 微糖(びとう) (slightly sweet, common for canned coffee), and 加糖(かとう) (sugar added). The 無(む) prefix means "without" and appears in many similar compounds like 無塩(むえん) (salt-free) and 無脂肪(むしぼう) (fat-free).
