餌食(えじき)

えじき
noun
prey; victim; easy target
1. prey; food for animals
An animal that is hunted and eaten by another animal.
小(ちい)さな魚(さかな)は大(おお)きな魚(さかな)の餌食(えじき)になる。
Small fish become prey for larger fish.
ウサギは狐(きつね)の餌食(えじき)になりやすい。
Rabbits easily become prey for foxes.
野生(やせい)の世界(せかい)では、弱(よわ)い動物(どうぶつ)はすぐに強(つよ)い動物(どうぶつ)の餌食(えじき)になってしまう。
In the wild, weak animals quickly become prey for stronger ones.
2. victim; easy target; prey (figurative)
A person or thing that falls victim to something harmful or exploitative. Often used figuratively for someone taken advantage of.
詐欺師(さぎし)の餌食(えじき)にならないように気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful not to fall prey to scammers.
不景気(ふけいき)で多(おお)くの中小企業(ちゅうしょうきぎょう)が倒産(とうさん)の餌食(えじき)になった。
Many small and medium businesses fell victim to bankruptcy due to the recession.
ネットに慣(な)れていない若者(わかもの)が、悪質(あくしつ)なオンライン詐欺(さぎ)の餌食(えじき)になるケースが増(ふ)えている。
There are increasing cases of young people unfamiliar with the internet falling victim to malicious online scams.

餌食(えじき) has both a literal sense (an animal hunted and eaten by a predator) and a more common figurative sense (a person or thing that falls victim to something harmful). The figurative usage carries a strong negative connotation of being helplessly consumed or exploited.

KEY PATTERN:

  • ～の餌食(えじき)になる — to become prey to; to fall victim to
  • This is by far the most common construction with this word

USAGE:

  • The figurative sense is more common in everyday language than the literal one
  • Often used in warnings about scams, exploitation, or vulnerability

RELATED WORDS:

  • 獲物(えもの) — prey; game (what a hunter catches)
  • 犠牲(ぎせい) — sacrifice; victim (more formal)
  • 被害者(ひがいしゃ) — victim (legal/news contexts)
