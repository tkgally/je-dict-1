1. to unite; to band together; to rally together
To come together as a group with a common purpose or cause. Often used in the context of solidarity, teamwork, or collective action.
みんなで団結(だんけつ)しよう。
Let's all unite together.
チームが団結(だんけつ)して試合(しあい)に勝(か)った。
The team united and won the game.
住民(じゅうみん)たちは開発(かいはつ)に反対(はんたい)して団結(だんけつ)し、署名(しょめい)を集(あつ)めた。
The residents banded together against the development and collected signatures.

団結(だんけつ)する means to unite with a shared purpose. Often used for collective action — labor movements, sports teams, community organizing.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pattern: (みんなで)団結(だんけつ)する

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 団結(だんけつ)力(りょく): unity, solidarity, power of unity
  • 団結(だんけつ)を固(かた)める: to strengthen unity
  • 固(かた)く団結(だんけつ)する: to firmly/solidly unite
  • 団結(だんけつ)して立(た)ち向(む)かう: to unite and stand up against

RELATED WORDS:

  • 結束(けっそく): unity, solidarity (similar but slightly more formal)
  • 連帯(れんたい): solidarity (emphasizes connection/support)
  • 協力(きょうりょく): cooperation (broader, less about unity)
