体外(たいがい)

たいがい
noun / no-adjective
outside the body; extracorporeal
1. outside the body; extracorporeal; external to the body
Referring to something that occurs or exists outside the body. Used primarily in medical and biological contexts.
体外(たいがい)受精(じゅせい)で子供(こども)を授(さず)かった。
They conceived a child through in vitro fertilization.
薬(くすり)の体外(たいがい)への排出(はいしゅつ)には時間(じかん)がかかる。
It takes time for the drug to be expelled from the body.
体外(たいがい)式(しき)除細動器(じょさいどうき)はAEDとも呼(よ)ばれ、公共(こうきょう)施設(しせつ)に設置(せっち)されている。
External defibrillators, also called AEDs, are installed in public facilities.

Composed of 体(たい) (body) + 外(がい) (outside). The opposite is 体内(たいない) (inside the body). Common collocations: 体外(たいがい)受精(じゅせい) (in vitro fertilization / IVF), 体外(たいがい)診断(しんだん) (in vitro diagnostics), 体外(たいがい)式(しき) (external type). Not to be confused with the homophone 大概(たいがい) (generally; mostly; enough already). 体外(たいがい)受精(じゅせい) is a widely discussed topic in Japan as the country grapples with declining birth rates — IVF treatment is now partially covered by health insurance.
