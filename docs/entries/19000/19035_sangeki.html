惨劇(さんげき)

さんげき
noun
tragedy; tragic event; disaster
1. tragedy, tragic event, disaster, horrific incident
A terrible event involving suffering, destruction, or death. Stronger and more dramatic than simply a sad event — implies horror and shock.
惨劇(さんげき)が起(お)きた。
A tragedy occurred.
この惨劇(さんげき)を二度(にど)と繰(く)り返(かえ)してはならない。
We must never repeat this tragedy.
戦争(せんそう)の惨劇(さんげき)を語(かた)り継(つ)ぐことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to pass on accounts of the tragedies of war.

USAGE:
A dramatic, literary word. Common in news reporting, historical accounts, and fiction. Not the same as 悲劇(ひげき) (tragedy in the theatrical sense or a sad event) — 惨劇(さんげき) implies gruesome or horrifying circumstances.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 惨劇(さんげき)が起(お)きる: a tragedy occurs
  • 惨劇(さんげき)を繰(く)り返(かえ)す: to repeat a tragedy
  • 惨劇(さんげき)の現場(げんば): scene of a tragedy
  • 惨劇(さんげき)を語(かた)り継(つ)ぐ: to pass on the story of a tragedy
