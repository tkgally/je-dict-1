血糖値(けっとうち)

けっとうち
noun
blood sugar level
1. blood sugar level; blood glucose level
The concentration of glucose in the blood. A key health indicator, especially for diabetes management and general wellness.
血糖値(けっとうち)を測(はか)る。
To measure one's blood sugar level.
食後(しょくご)は血糖値(けっとうち)が上(あ)がりやすい。
Blood sugar levels tend to rise after eating.
医者(いしゃ)に血糖値(けっとうち)が高(たか)いと言(い)われて、食事(しょくじ)に気(き)をつけるようになった。
The doctor told me my blood sugar was high, so I started watching my diet.

A compound of 血糖(けっとう) (blood sugar) and 値(ち) (value, level). A very commonly used medical and health term in everyday Japanese, frequently encountered in health checkup results and diet-related discussions.

USAGE:
Appears in medical contexts, health magazines, food labeling discussions, and daily conversation about diet and health. Japan's annual health checkups (健康診断(けんこうしんだん)) routinely measure 血糖値(けっとうち).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血糖値(けっとうち)が高(たか)い — to have high blood sugar
  • 血糖値(けっとうち)が下(さ)がる — blood sugar drops
  • 血糖値(けっとうち)をコントロールする — to control blood sugar
  • 空腹時(くうふくじ)血糖値(けっとうち) — fasting blood sugar level

RELATED TERMS:

  • 糖尿病(とうにょうびょう) — diabetes
  • 血圧(けつあつ) — blood pressure; another common health measurement
  • 低血糖(ていけっとう) — hypoglycemia; abnormally low blood sugar
