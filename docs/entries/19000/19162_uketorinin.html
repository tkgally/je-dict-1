受取人(うけとりにん)

うけとりにん
noun
recipient; addressee; payee
1. recipient; addressee; payee
The person who receives something, such as a package, letter, payment, or insurance benefit. Used in postal, financial, and legal contexts.
受取人(うけとりにん)の名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write the recipient's name.
保険金(ほけんきん)の受取人(うけとりにん)を変更(へんこう)する。
To change the insurance beneficiary.
荷物(にもつ)は受取人(うけとりにん)本人(ほんにん)にしか渡(わた)せません。
The package can only be handed to the recipient in person.

Common collocations: 受取人(うけとりにん)の住所(じゅうしょ) (recipient's address), 受取人(うけとりにん)を指定(してい)する (to designate a recipient), 受取人(うけとりにん)不在(ふざい) (recipient not home). The opposite is 差出人(さしだしにん) (sender). In insurance, the 受取人(うけとりにん) is the beneficiary designated to receive payments. Also read as うけとりびと in some older or literary contexts.
