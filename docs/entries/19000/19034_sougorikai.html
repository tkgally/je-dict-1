相互理解(そうごりかい)

そうごりかい
noun
mutual understanding
1. mutual understanding
Understanding between two or more parties where each side comprehends the other's position, feelings, or culture. Common in discussions of diplomacy, relationships, and cross-cultural communication.
相互理解(そうごりかい)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Mutual understanding is important.
相互理解(そうごりかい)を深(ふか)めるために対話(たいわ)を続(つづ)ける。
We continue dialogue to deepen mutual understanding.
国際交流(こくさいこうりゅう)は相互理解(そうごりかい)の第一歩(だいいっぽ)だ。
International exchange is the first step toward mutual understanding.

USAGE:
A compound of 相互(そうご) (mutual) + 理解(りかい) (understanding). Frequently used in formal contexts: diplomacy, education, business partnerships, and multicultural discussions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相互理解(そうごりかい)を深(ふか)める: to deepen mutual understanding
  • 相互理解(そうごりかい)を促進(そくしん)する: to promote mutual understanding
  • 相互理解(そうごりかい)に基(もと)づく: based on mutual understanding
  • 異文化(いぶんか)相互理解(そうごりかい): cross-cultural mutual understanding
