1. cheating (on an exam or test)
The act of using unauthorized methods to answer questions during an examination.
カンニングはいけない。
Cheating is wrong.
カンニングが見(み)つかって、試験(しけん)が無効(むこう)になった。
The cheating was discovered and the exam was invalidated.
カンニングをしても実力(じつりょく)がつかないので、自分(じぶん)のためにならない。
Cheating doesn't build real skills, so it doesn't benefit you.

From English "cunning," but with a completely different meaning. In English, "cunning" means clever or sly, while in Japanese カンニング specifically means cheating on a test.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • カンニングをする: to cheat (on an exam)
  • カンニングが見(み)つかる/ばれる: to get caught cheating
  • カンニングペーパー: cheat sheet (often shortened to カンペ)

This is a classic example of 和製英語(わせいえいご) (Japanese-coined English) — a word derived from English but used with a different meaning in Japanese.
