拙劣(せつれつ)

せつれつ
na-adjective / noun
clumsy; poor; unskillful
1. clumsy; poor; unskillful; crude
Describing something done poorly or lacking skill. A formal, somewhat literary term used to criticize the quality of work, performance, or technique.
拙劣(せつれつ)な演技(えんぎ)だった。
It was a poor performance.
拙劣(せつれつ)な模倣(もほう)にすぎない。
It's nothing more than a crude imitation.
政府(せいふ)の拙劣(せつれつ)な対応(たいおう)が被害(ひがい)を拡大(かくだい)させた。
The government's poor handling of the situation worsened the damage.

Composed of 拙(せつ) (clumsy, unskillful) + 劣(れつ) (inferior). A formal/written term expressing strong criticism of quality or skill. The opposite is 巧妙(こうみょう) (skillful, clever). Common collocations: 拙劣(せつれつ)な手口(てぐち) (clumsy tactics), 拙劣(せつれつ)な技術(ぎじゅつ) (poor technique), 拙劣(せつれつ)な対応(たいおう) (poor handling). More formal and harsher than 下手(へた) (unskillful, poor at). Related words: 稚拙(ちせつ) (immature, childish — implies lack of sophistication), 粗雑(そざつ) (rough, sloppy).
