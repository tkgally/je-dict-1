冬休(ふゆやす)み

ふゆやすみ
noun
winter break; winter vacation
1. winter break; winter vacation
The vacation period during winter, typically from late December to early January for schools, and around the New Year period for workplaces.
冬休(ふゆやす)みが始(はじ)まった。
Winter break has started.
冬休(ふゆやす)みに家族(かぞく)で旅行(りょこう)する予定(よてい)です。
I plan to travel with my family during winter break.
子(こ)どもたちは冬休(ふゆやす)みの宿題(しゅくだい)をまだ終(お)わらせていない。
The children still haven't finished their winter break homework.

In Japan, school winter break typically runs from late December to early January (about two weeks), shorter than 夏休(なつやす)み. It overlaps with 年末年始(ねんまつねんし) (year-end and New Year period). Compare with 夏休(なつやす)み (summer vacation) and 春休(はるやす)み (spring break).
