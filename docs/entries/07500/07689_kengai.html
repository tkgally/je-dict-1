圏外(けんがい)

けんがい
noun
out of range; out of service area; no signal
1. out of range; out of service area; no signal
Being outside the coverage area where a mobile phone can receive signal.
山(やま)の中(なか)で圏外(けんがい)になった。
I lost signal in the mountains.
ここは圏外(けんがい)だから電話(でんわ)ができない。
I can't make calls here because there's no signal.
2. outside the sphere; out of scope
Figuratively, being outside a certain area or scope of concern.
その問題(もんだい)は私(わたし)の関心(かんしん)の圏外(けんがい)だ。
That issue is outside my sphere of concern.

ETYMOLOGY:
圏(けん) (sphere, area) + 外(がい) (outside)

PHONE DISPLAY:
When a phone has no signal, the screen typically shows 「圏外(けんがい)」 where the signal bars would normally be.

RELATED:

  • 圏内(けんない) - within range (antonym)
  • 電波(でんぱ)が届(とど)かない - signal doesn't reach
  • アンテナが立(た)たない - no bars (colloquial)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 圏外(けんがい)になる - to go out of range
  • 圏外(けんがい)で〜できない - can't ~ because out of range
