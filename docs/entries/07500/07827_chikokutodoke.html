遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)

ちこくとどけ
noun
late arrival notice
1. late arrival notice
a formal notice or form submitted when arriving late to work or school
遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit a late arrival notice.
電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れたので遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a late arrival notice because the train was delayed.
遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)に理由(りゆう)を記入(きにゅう)する。
Fill in the reason on the late arrival notice.

遅刻届(ちこくとどけ) is a compound noun combining 遅刻(ちこく) (being late) + 届(とどけ) (notice/notification), referring to the formal documentation required when arriving late.

WORKPLACE DOCUMENTS (～届(とどけ)):

  • 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ) (late arrival notice)
  • 早退届(そうたいとどけ) (early departure notice)
  • 欠勤届(けっきんとどけ) (absence notice)
  • 休暇届(きゅうかとどけ) (leave request form)
  • 退職届(たいしょくとどけ) (resignation notice)

TRAIN DELAYS:
When trains are delayed in Japan, stations provide 遅延証明書(ちえんしょうめいしょ) (delay certificates) that can be attached to 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ) to verify the reason for lateness.

SCHOOL CONTEXT:
Students may also need to submit 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ) to their homeroom teacher or school office.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)を出(だ)す/提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit a late notice)
  • 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ)を書(か)く (to write a late notice)

REGISTER: Neutral to formal. Standard workplace/school vocabulary.
