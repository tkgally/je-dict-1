寂(さび)しがる

さびしがる
verb (godan)
to feel lonely
1. to feel lonely
to feel lonely; to miss (someone); to show loneliness
犬(いぬ)が飼(か)い主(ぬし)を寂(さび)しがっている。
The dog misses its owner.
子(こ)どもは親(おや)がいないと寂(さび)しがる。
Children feel lonely when their parents aren't around.
彼(かれ)は遠(とお)くに引(ひ)っ越(こ)した友人(ゆうじん)のことを寂(さび)しがっている。
He misses his friend who moved far away.

寂(さび)しがる is a godan verb formed from the adjective 寂(さび)しい (lonely) + the suffix がる.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xが寂(さび)しがる / XがYを寂(さび)しがる (X misses Y)

ASPECT (ている):
寂(さび)しがっている describes someone visibly showing loneliness or missing someone.

The がる suffix is used to describe observable emotional states, particularly in third person:

  • 寂(さび)しい (I'm lonely) → 寂(さび)しがる (to show loneliness - typically third person)

Note: Can also be spelled 淋(さび)しがる.

Related がる verbs:

  • 恥(は)ずかしがる (to show embarrassment)
  • 怖(こわ)がる (to show fear)
  • 欲(ほ)しがる (to show wanting)
