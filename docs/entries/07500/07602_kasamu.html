1. to increase; to mount up
For amounts, especially expenses or quantities, to accumulate and become larger.
出費(しゅっぴ)が嵩(かさ)んでいる。
Expenses are piling up.
予想(よそう)より費用(ひよう)が嵩(かさ)んだ。
The costs mounted up more than expected.
医療費(いりょうひ)が嵩(かさ)んで困(こま)っている。
I'm struggling because my medical expenses keep mounting up.
2. to bulk up; to take up more space
For physical volume to increase or become bulky.
荷物(にもつ)が嵩(かさ)む。
The luggage is getting bulky.
冬服(ふゆふく)は嵩(かさ)んで収納(しゅうのう)が大変(たいへん)だ。
Winter clothes are bulky and hard to store.
おみやげが嵩(かさ)んで、スーツケースに入(はい)りきらなかった。
The souvenirs got so bulky that they wouldn't all fit in the suitcase.

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
Pattern: Xが嵩(かさ)む

ASPECT (ている):
嵩(かさ)んでいる - has accumulated (resultant state)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 嵩(かさ) is a rare character. This verb is often written in hiragana as かさむ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 費用(ひよう)が嵩(かさ)む - expenses mount up
  • 出費(しゅっぴ)が嵩(かさ)む - spending increases
  • 荷物(にもつ)が嵩(かさ)む - luggage becomes bulky
  • 年齢(ねんれい)が嵩(かさ)む - to get older (literary)

NUANCE:
Often implies a negative connotation, especially when referring to expenses or problems accumulating beyond expectations.
