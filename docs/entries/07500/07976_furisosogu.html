降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ

ふりそそぐ
verb
to pour down; to shower down; to fall upon
1. to pour down; to shower down
to fall continuously and intensely from above, used for rain, sunlight, or other things that come down
太陽(たいよう)の光(ひかり)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ。
Sunlight pours down.
激(はげ)しい雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)いだ。
Heavy rain poured down.
花火(はなび)の火(ひ)の粉(こ)が夜空(よぞら)から降(ふ)り注(そそ)いでいた。
Sparks from the fireworks were showering down from the night sky.

降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ is a compound verb expressing something pouring or showering down from above.

VERB TYPE: Godan verb (u-verb), intransitive

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 降(ふ)る (to fall - rain, snow)
  • 注(そそ)ぐ (to pour)

CONJUGATION:

  • 降(ふ)り注(そそ)がない (negative)
  • 降(ふ)り注(そそ)いだ (past)
  • 降(ふ)り注(そそ)いで (te-form)

COMMON SUBJECTS:

  • 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (rain pours down)
  • 日差(ひざ)しが降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (sunlight showers down)
  • 光(ひかり)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (light pours down)
  • 雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (snow showers down)

FIGURATIVE USE:

  • 愛情(あいじょう)を降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (to shower with love) - transitive use
  • 祝福(しゅくふく)が降(ふ)り注(そそ)ぐ (blessings rain down)

NUANCE:

  • Implies abundance and intensity
  • Often poetic or descriptive
  • Evokes imagery of things cascading from above
