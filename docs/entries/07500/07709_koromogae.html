衣替(ころもが)え

ころもがえ
noun, suru-verb
seasonal wardrobe change; switching between seasonal clothes
1. seasonal wardrobe change
The practice of switching out one's wardrobe according to the season, typically putting away winter clothes for summer ones or vice versa.
そろそろ衣替(ころもが)えの季節(きせつ)だ。
It's about time for the seasonal clothing change.
衣替(ころもが)えで冬服(ふゆふく)をしまった。
I put away my winter clothes during the wardrobe change.
学校(がっこう)の衣替(ころもが)えは六月(ろくがつ)だ。
The school uniform change is in June.

ETYMOLOGY:
衣(ころも) (clothing - classical reading) + 替(か)え (change)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, 衣替(ころもが)え traditionally occurs on June 1st (from winter to summer uniforms) and October 1st (from summer to winter). Schools and many companies follow this schedule for uniform changes.

TYPICAL ACTIVITIES:

  • Packing away off-season clothes
  • Taking out seasonal clothes from storage
  • Cleaning or laundering stored items

RELATED:

  • 模様替(もようが)え - room rearrangement
  • 冬支度(ふゆじたく) - winter preparation
  • 夏服(なつふく)/冬服(ふゆふく) - summer/winter clothes
