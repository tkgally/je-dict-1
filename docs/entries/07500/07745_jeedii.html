JD

じぇーでぃー
noun
female college student
1. female college student
Abbreviation of 女子大生(じょしだいせい), referring to female university students.
JDに人気(にんき)のバイト
A part-time job popular among female college students.
姉(あね)は今(いま)JDだ。
My older sister is a college student now.
JD向(む)けのファッション雑誌(ざっし)が売(う)れている。
Fashion magazines for female college students are selling well.

ETYMOLOGY:
Abbreviation of 女子大生(じょしだいせい) (female college student). J = 女子(じょし) (female), D = 大生(だいせい) (college student).

USAGE:

Used in:

  • Marketing and advertising
  • Social media
  • Casual conversation

RELATED TERMS:

  • JK - 女子高生(じょしこうせい) (high school girl)
  • JC - 女子中学生(じょしちゅうがくせい) (middle school girl)
