紛(まぎ)れる

まぎれる
verb
to get mixed up; to be lost in; to be distracted
1. to get mixed up; to blend in; to disappear into
To become indistinguishable within a group or situation, often intentionally to hide.
犯人(はんにん)は人混(ひとご)みに紛(まぎ)れて逃(に)げた。
The criminal escaped by blending into the crowd.
騒(さわ)ぎに紛(まぎ)れて、大事(だいじ)な書類(しょるい)がなくなった。
In all the commotion, important documents went missing.
2. to be distracted; to be diverted
For one's attention or feelings to be diverted by something else.
仕事(しごと)に紛(まぎ)れて悲(かな)しみを忘(わす)れようとした。
I tried to forget my sadness by losing myself in work.

VERB TYPE:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive verb)

TRANSITIVE PAIR:
紛(まぎ)らす/紛(まぎ)らわす - to divert, to distract (transitive)

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)/下一段(しもいちだん))

  • 紛(まぎ)れない (negative)
  • 紛(まぎ)れて (te-form)
  • 紛(まぎ)れた (past)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に紛(まぎ)れる - to blend into ~, to get lost in ~
  • 紛(まぎ)れもない - unmistakable, certain (idiomatic)
  • 紛(まぎ)れもなく - unmistakably, certainly

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人混(ひとご)みに紛(まぎ)れる - to blend into a crowd
  • 闇(やみ)に紛(まぎ)れる - to disappear into darkness
  • 仕事(しごと)に紛(まぎ)れる - to be absorbed in work
  • 騒(さわ)ぎに紛(まぎ)れる - to get lost in the commotion

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)を紛(まぎ)らす - to distract oneself
