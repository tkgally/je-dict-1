恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります

おそれいります
expression
I'm sorry to trouble you; thank you very much (humble)
1. I'm sorry to trouble you; excuse me for bothering you
A humble expression used when making a request or causing inconvenience.
恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、もう一度(いちど)説明(せつめい)していただけますか。
I'm sorry to trouble you, but could you explain that once more?
恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、少々(しょうしょう)お待(ま)ちください。
I'm sorry, but please wait a moment.
恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、こちらにご署名(しょめい)をお願(ねが)いいたします。
I'm sorry to trouble you, but please sign here.
お忙(いそが)しいところ恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが、確認(かくにん)をお願(ねが)いできますか。
I know you're busy, but could I ask you to check this?
2. thank you very much; I'm much obliged
A humble expression of gratitude when someone has done something for you.
わざわざお越(こ)しいただき、恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります。
Thank you so much for taking the trouble to come.
ご配慮(はいりょ)いただき、恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります。
I'm very grateful for your consideration.

FORMALITY:
This is a very polite/humble expression commonly used in business settings and customer service. The past form 恐(おそ)れ入(い)りました is also used.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが〜 - I'm sorry to trouble you, but... (preceding a request)
  • お忙(いそが)しいところ恐(おそ)れ入(い)りますが - I know you're busy, but...
  • わざわざ〜恐(おそ)れ入(い)ります - Thank you for going out of your way to...

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません - I'm terribly sorry (apologizing for trouble caused)
  • 失礼(しつれい)いたします - Excuse me (more general)
