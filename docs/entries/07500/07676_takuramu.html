1. to scheme; to plot; to plan (something devious)
To secretly plan something, usually with malicious or self-serving intent.
悪事(あくじ)をたくらむ。
To plot wrongdoing.
何(なに)かたくらんでいるに違(ちが)いない。
He must be plotting something.
彼(かれ)らは会社(かいしゃ)の乗(の)っ取(と)りをたくらんでいた。
They were scheming to take over the company.

VERB TYPE:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive verb)
Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし))

KANJI:
Can be written as 企(たくら)む.

  • たくらまない (negative)
  • たくらんで (te-form)
  • たくらんだ (past)

NUANCE:
Almost always used with negative connotations. For neutral or positive planning, use 計画(けいかく)する or 企(くわだ)てる.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 企(たくら)む - same word with kanji
  • 企(くわだ)てる - to plan, attempt (more neutral)
  • 謀(はか)る - to plot, conspire (literary)
