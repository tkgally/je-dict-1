逐次(ちくじ)

ちくじ
adverb
sequentially; successively; one by one
1. sequentially; successively
Doing things one after another in order, without skipping steps.
情報(じょうほう)を逐次(ちくじ)報告(ほうこく)する。
Report information successively.
逐次(ちくじ)処理(しょり)でデータを分析(ぶんせき)した。
We analyzed the data using sequential processing.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)は逐次(ちくじ)メールで共有(きょうゆう)されます。
The meeting contents will be shared progressively via email.

USAGE:
逐次(ちくじ) emphasizes processing or reporting things in order, one by one. Often used in technical and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 逐次(ちくじ)報告(ほうこく) - successive reports
  • 逐次(ちくじ)処理(しょり) - sequential processing
  • 逐次(ちくじ)公開(こうかい) - successive publication
  • 逐次(ちくじ)更新(こうしん) - progressive updates

COMPARISON:

  • 逐一(ちくいち) - item by item, in detail (emphasizes thoroughness)
  • 順次(じゅんじ) - in order (emphasizes following a sequence)
  • 逐次(ちくじ) - successively (emphasizes continuous progression)
