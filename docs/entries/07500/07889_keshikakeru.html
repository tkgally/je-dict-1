嗾(けしか)ける

けしかける
ichidan verb, transitive
to incite; to egg on; to sic (a dog) on
1. to incite; to egg on; to urge on
To provoke or encourage someone to do something, often something aggressive or unwise.
彼(かれ)は部下(ぶか)を嗾(けしか)けて反対派(はんたいは)を攻撃(こうげき)させた。
He egged on his subordinates to attack the opposition.
友達(ともだち)に嗾(けしか)けられてやってしまった。
I was egged on by my friends and ended up doing it.
人(ひと)を嗾(けしか)けて喧嘩(けんか)させるのはやめろ。
Stop inciting people to fight.
子供(こども)に嗾(けしか)けられて、言(い)わなくていいことまで言(い)ってしまった。
The children egged me on and I ended up saying things I shouldn't have.
2. to sic; to set (an animal) on
To command an animal, especially a dog, to attack.
犬(いぬ)を嗾(けしか)けて追(お)い払(はら)った。
I sicced the dog on them to chase them away.
番犬(ばんけん)を嗾(けしか)ける飼(か)い主(ぬし)がいた。
There was an owner who would sic their guard dog on people.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞). Pattern: AをBに嗾(けしか)ける (to sic A on B) or Aを嗾(けしか)けて〜させる (to incite A to do ~)

KANJI NOTE:
嗾(けしか)ける is often written in hiragana as けしかける because the kanji 嗾 is uncommon.

COMPARISON:

  • 嗾(けしか)ける - to incite, egg on (often negative connotation)
  • 焚(た)き付(つ)ける - to stir up, incite
  • 煽(あお)る - to fan, provoke
  • そそのかす - to tempt, entice
