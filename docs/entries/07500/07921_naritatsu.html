成(な)り立(た)つ

なりたつ
godan verb
to consist of; to hold true; to be valid
1. to consist of; to be composed of
to be formed or made up of certain elements
社会(しゃかい)は人(ひと)と人(ひと)の関係(かんけい)で成(な)り立(た)っている。
Society consists of relationships between people.
日本語(にほんご)は漢字(かんじ)、ひらがな、カタカナで成(な)り立(た)っている。
Japanese consists of kanji, hiragana, and katakana.
2. to hold true; to be valid; to work
for an argument, agreement, or system to function properly
この理論(りろん)は成(な)り立(た)たない。
This theory doesn't hold.
商売(しょうばい)が成(な)り立(た)つか心配(しんぱい)だ。
I'm worried about whether the business will work.
契約(けいやく)は双方(そうほう)の合意(ごうい)で成(な)り立(た)つ。
A contract is valid when both parties agree.
その言(い)い訳(わけ)は成(な)り立(た)たない。
That excuse doesn't hold up.

成(な)り立(た)つ describes something being composed of elements or being valid/functional.

VERB TYPE:

  • Godan verb (つ-verb)
  • Intransitive

COMPOSITION:

  • 成(な)る (to become) + 立(た)つ (to stand)

SENSE 1 VS SENSE 2:

  • Sense 1: Composition (what something consists of)
  • Sense 2: Validity/functionality (whether something works)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～で成(な)り立(た)つ (to consist of ~)
  • ～が成(な)り立(た)つ (~ is valid)
  • 成(な)り立(た)たない (doesn't hold, doesn't work)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 理論(りろん)が成(な)り立(た)つ (theory holds)
  • 契約(けいやく)が成(な)り立(た)つ (contract is valid)
  • 商売(しょうばい)が成(な)り立(た)つ (business is viable)
  • 議論(ぎろん)が成(な)り立(た)たない (argument doesn't hold)

NEGATIVE FORM:

  • 成(な)り立(た)たない is very common
  • Used to dismiss invalid arguments or unworkable situations

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 成(な)り上(あ)がる (to rise in status)
  • 成(な)り下(さ)がる (to sink, become degraded)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 構成(こうせい)される (to be composed of - formal)
  • 通用(つうよう)する (to be valid - more general)
  • 機能(きのう)する (to function)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in both spoken and written Japanese.
