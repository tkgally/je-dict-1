とばっちり

とばっちり
noun
getting caught up in; collateral damage
1. getting caught up in; getting splashed; collateral damage
Suffering unintended negative consequences from someone else's actions or a situation one wasn't directly involved in.
とばっちりを受(う)けた。
I got caught up in it.
上司(じょうし)の機嫌(きげん)が悪(わる)くて、とばっちりを食(く)らった。
My boss was in a bad mood and I got caught in the crossfire.
友達(ともだち)の喧嘩(けんか)のとばっちりで怒(おこ)られた。
I got scolded as collateral damage from my friends' fight.

ETYMOLOGY:
Related to 飛(と)ぶ (to fly) and ちる (to scatter) - originally referring to water splashing on a bystander. Now used metaphorically.

USAGE:

Describes being an innocent victim who suffers due to:

  • Others' conflicts or mistakes
  • Bad timing or being in the wrong place
  • Chain reactions of negative events

COLLOCATIONS:

  • とばっちりを受(う)ける - to receive collateral damage
  • とばっちりを食(く)らう - to get caught up in (stronger)
  • とばっちりが来(く)る - collateral damage comes

REGISTER:
Casual, spoken Japanese. Often used humorously or with mild complaint.
