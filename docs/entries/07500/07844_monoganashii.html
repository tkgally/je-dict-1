物悲(ものがな)しい

ものがなしい
adjective (i)
melancholy
1. melancholy
sad; melancholy; feeling a vague, wistful sadness
物悲(ものがな)しい気持(きも)ちになる。
I feel melancholy.
秋(あき)の夕暮(ゆうぐ)れは物悲(ものがな)しい。
Autumn evenings feel melancholy.
古(ふる)い写真(しゃしん)を見(み)ると物悲(ものがな)しい気分(きぶん)になることがある。
Looking at old photos sometimes makes me feel wistfully sad.

物悲(ものがな)しい is an i-adjective describing a gentle, wistful sadness or melancholy.

ETYMOLOGY:

  • 物(もの) (thing, vague prefix) + 悲(かな)しい (sad)
  • The prefix adds a sense of vagueness or indefiniteness to the sadness

NUANCE:

  • Describes a gentle, atmospheric sadness rather than acute grief
  • Often associated with natural scenes, nostalgia, or fleeting moments
  • Has a literary, poetic quality

COMMON ASSOCIATIONS:

  • 秋(あき) (autumn): Season most associated with this feeling
  • 夕暮(ゆうぐ)れ (twilight): Time of day often described as 物悲(ものがな)しい
  • 思(おも)い出(で) (memories): Nostalgic memories evoke this feeling

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物悲(ものがな)しい気持(きも)ち (melancholy feeling)
  • 物悲(ものがな)しい雰囲気(ふんいき) (melancholy atmosphere)
  • 物悲(ものがな)しい表情(ひょうじょう) (sad expression)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悲(かな)しい: Sad (direct, acute)
  • 寂(さび)しい: Lonely, desolate
  • 物悲(ものがな)しい: Wistful, atmospheric melancholy (literary)

REGISTER: Neutral to literary. Common in poetry and prose describing moods and scenes.
