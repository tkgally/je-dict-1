VR

ぶいあーる
noun
virtual reality
1. virtual reality
A computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with using special equipment such as headsets.
VR体験(たいけん)をしてきた。
I had a VR experience.
VRゴーグルを買(か)った。
I bought a VR headset.
VRで海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)をした気分(きぶん)になれる。
You can feel like you're traveling abroad through VR.

ETYMOLOGY:
Abbreviation of 'Virtual Reality'.

USAGE:

Used in contexts of:

  • Gaming and entertainment
  • Training and education
  • Real estate (virtual property tours)
  • Medical applications

COLLOCATIONS:

  • VRゴーグル - VR headset/goggles
  • VR体験(たいけん) - VR experience
  • VRゲーム - VR game
