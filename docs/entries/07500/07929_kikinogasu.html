聞(き)き逃(のが)す

ききのがす
godan verb
to fail to hear; to miss hearing; to let slip
1. to fail to hear; to miss hearing
to not catch what was said, either by accident or inattention
名前(なまえ)を聞(き)き逃(のが)した。
I missed hearing the name.
大事(だいじ)な発表(はっぴょう)を聞(き)き逃(のが)さないでください。
Please don't miss the important announcement.
騒(さわ)がしくてアナウンス(あなうんす)を聞(き)き逃(のが)した。
It was noisy, so I missed the announcement.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で駅名(えきめい)を聞(き)き逃(のが)した。
I missed hearing the station name on the train.
2. to let slip; to not pursue what was heard
to deliberately overlook something problematic that was said
その発言(はつげん)は聞(き)き逃(のが)せない。
That remark cannot be ignored.
失礼(しつれい)な言葉(ことば)を聞(き)き逃(のが)すわけにはいかない。
I can't let such rude words slide.

聞(き)き逃(のが)す means either accidentally missing what was said or deliberately overlooking something.

VERB TYPE:

  • Godan verb (す-verb)
  • Transitive

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 聞(き)く (to hear) + 逃(のが)す (to let escape)
  • Part of the ～逃(のが)す compound pattern

SENSE 1 (ACCIDENTAL):

  • Missing information unintentionally
  • Common in noisy environments
  • Used with announcements, names, explanations

SENSE 2 (DELIBERATE):

  • Choosing not to respond to something problematic
  • 聞(き)き逃(のが)せない (cannot be overlooked) is very common
  • Often in contexts of insults or inappropriate remarks

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名前(なまえ)を聞(き)き逃(のが)す (miss hearing a name)
  • アナウンスを聞(き)き逃(のが)す (miss an announcement)
  • 大事(だいじ)な話(はなし)を聞(き)き逃(のが)す (miss important information)
  • 聞(き)き逃(のが)せない (cannot be ignored)

CONTRAST WITH 聞(き)き流(なが)す:

  • 聞(き)き逃(のが)す: Failed to hear or can't ignore
  • 聞(き)き流(なが)す: Deliberately ignored, let pass

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 見逃(みのが)す (to overlook, miss seeing)
  • 取(と)り逃(のが)す (to let slip, miss catching)
  • 聞(き)き流(なが)す (to let pass, ignore)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in everyday conversation.
