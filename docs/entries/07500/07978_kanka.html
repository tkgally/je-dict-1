看過(かんか)

かんか
noun
overlooking; ignoring; letting pass
1. overlooking; ignoring; letting something pass
deliberately not taking action against something that should be addressed
この問題(もんだい)は看過(かんか)できない。
This problem cannot be overlooked.
不正(ふせい)を看過(かんか)するわけにはいかない。
We cannot let wrongdoing pass.
経営陣(けいえいじん)は従業員(じゅうぎょういん)の訴(うった)えを看過(かんか)し続(つづ)けた結果(けっか)、大(おお)きな問題(もんだい)に発展(はってん)した。
As a result of management continuing to ignore employee complaints, it developed into a major problem.

看過(かんか) means to overlook or ignore something, often used critically to indicate negligence.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 看(かん) (watch, look)
  • 過(か) (pass by, exceed)

USAGE:

  • 看過(かんか)する (to overlook - suru verb)
  • 看過(かんか)できない (cannot overlook)
  • 看過(かんか)し得(え)ない (cannot be allowed to pass)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を看過(かんか)できない (cannot overlook ~)
  • 看過(かんか)するわけにはいかない (cannot let it pass)
  • 看過(かんか)し難(がた)い問題(もんだい) (a problem difficult to ignore)

NUANCE:

  • Often used in negative contexts
  • Implies criticism of inaction
  • Formal/written style

FORMALITY:

  • Literary/formal expression
  • Common in news, official statements

RELATED TERMS:

  • 見過(みす)ごす (to overlook - less formal)
  • 黙認(もくにん) (tacit approval)
  • 無視(むし)する (to ignore)
