積(つ)み上(あ)げる

つみあげる
ichidan verb
to pile up; to accumulate
1. to pile up; to stack up
to place things on top of each other to create a stack
箱(はこ)を積(つ)み上(あ)げた。
I stacked up the boxes.
子供(こども)がブロックを積(つ)み上(あ)げている。
The child is stacking up blocks.
レンガを丁寧(ていねい)に積(つ)み上(あ)げる。
I carefully stack up the bricks.
2. to accumulate; to build up
to gradually gather or develop over time through sustained effort
長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み上(あ)げてきた。
I have accumulated years of experience.
信頼(しんらい)は一日(いちにち)で積(つ)み上(あ)げられない。
Trust cannot be built in a day.
努力(どりょく)を積(つ)み上(あ)げてきた結果(けっか)だ。
This is the result of accumulated effort.

積(つ)み上(あ)げる is a compound verb meaning to pile up or accumulate.

VERB TYPE:

  • Ichidan verb (る-verb)
  • Transitive
  • Intransitive pair: 積(つ)み上(あ)がる

COMPOSITION:

  • 積(つ)む (to pile, stack) + 上(あ)げる (to raise)

SENSE 1 VS SENSE 2:

  • Sense 1: Physical stacking (boxes, blocks, bricks)
  • Sense 2: Abstract accumulation (experience, trust, achievements)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)を積(つ)み上(あ)げる (to accumulate experience)
  • 実績(じっせき)を積(つ)み上(あ)げる (to build up achievements)
  • 信頼(しんらい)を積(つ)み上(あ)げる (to build trust)
  • 荷物(にもつ)を積(つ)み上(あ)げる (to stack luggage)
  • 努力(どりょく)を積(つ)み上(あ)げる (to accumulate effort)

INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 積(つ)み上(あ)がる: Something piles up on its own
  • 積(つ)み上(あ)げる: Someone actively piles something

RELATED TERMS:

  • 積(つ)む (to pile - base verb)
  • 蓄積(ちくせき)する (to accumulate - more formal)
  • 重(かさ)ねる (to layer, repeat)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in everyday speech and business contexts.
