1. rousing oneself; mustering energy; stirring to action
summoning one's energy and determination to take action
奮起(ふんき)して仕事(しごと)に取(と)り組(く)む。
I rouse myself and tackle the work.
チームの奮起(ふんき)を促(うなが)した。
I urged the team to rouse themselves.
失敗(しっぱい)を糧(かて)に奮起(ふんき)して、再挑戦(さいちょうせん)することにした。
I decided to learn from my failure, rouse myself, and try again.

奮起(ふんき) means to muster energy and motivation, often after facing difficulties or setbacks.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 奮(ふん) (rouse, stir up)
  • 起(き) (rise, get up)

USAGE:

  • 奮起(ふんき)する (to rouse oneself - suru verb)
  • 奮起(ふんき)を促(うなが)す (to urge someone to rouse themselves)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 奮起(ふんき)して〜する (to rouse oneself and do ~)
  • 〜に奮起(ふんき)する (to be stirred to action by ~)

NUANCE:

  • Implies gathering determination after setback or challenge
  • Often used in sports, work, or personal development contexts
  • Positive connotation of taking action

RELATED TERMS:

  • 発奮(はっぷん) (being roused to action)
  • 奮闘(ふんとう) (hard struggle)
  • 奮(ふる)い立(た)つ (to be stirred up)
  • やる気(き)を出(だ)す (to get motivated - casual)

Related Words

Synonym: 奮闘(ふんとう) (hard struggle; strenuous effort)
