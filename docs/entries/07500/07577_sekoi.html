せこい

せこい
i-adjective
stingy; petty; cheap
1. stingy; miserly; tight-fisted
Reluctant to spend money, even in situations where spending would be normal or expected.
彼(かれ)はせこいから割(わ)り勘(かん)でも1円(えん)単位(たんい)まで計算(けいさん)する。
He's so stingy that even when splitting the bill, he calculates down to the last yen.
2. petty; small-minded; cheap
Engaging in petty behavior to gain small advantages, often in ways that are seen as underhanded or beneath one's dignity.
せこい手段(しゅだん)で勝(か)っても嬉(うれ)しくない。
Winning through petty means doesn't make me happy.
そんなせこいこと言(い)わないでよ。
Don't be so petty about it.

FORMS:

  • せこい (plain)
  • せこく (adverbial)
  • せこさ (noun) - stinginess, pettiness

REGISTER:
Informal/colloquial. Can be mildly insulting when used to describe someone directly.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • けち - stingy, miserly (stronger criticism)
  • 貧乏(びんぼう)くさい - looking poor, cheap
  • こすい - cunning, crafty (regional/colloquial)
  • みみっちい - petty, stingy

CONTRAST:

  • 気前(きまえ)がいい - generous
  • 太っ腹(ふとっぱら) - generous, big-hearted

USAGE NOTES:
Originally from Kansai dialect but now widely used throughout Japan. Often used to criticize not just financial stinginess but also petty behavior in general.
