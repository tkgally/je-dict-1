そそくさ

そそくさ
adverb
hurriedly; hastily; in a rush to leave
1. hurriedly; hastily; in a rush
Moving or leaving quickly, often to escape an uncomfortable situation or because one is busy. Implies departing abruptly without lingering.
彼(かれ)はそそくさと帰(かえ)っていった。
He hurried away.
食事(しょくじ)を終(お)えるとそそくさと席(せき)を立(た)った。
After finishing the meal, they hastily got up from their seat.
そそくさと準備(じゅんび)をして出(で)かけた。
They hurriedly got ready and left.

MIMETIC TYPE:
Onomatopoeia expressing quick, somewhat furtive movement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • そそくさと帰(かえ)る - to hurry home
  • そそくさと立(た)ち去(さ)る - to leave in a hurry
  • そそくさと片付(かたづ)ける - to clean up hastily

NUANCE:
Implies leaving without staying to chat or lingering. Often suggests the person wants to avoid further interaction or has somewhere urgent to be. Can have a slightly negative connotation of being unsociable.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 急(いそ)いで - in a hurry (neutral)
  • ばたばたと - frantically, busily
  • こそこそ - stealthily, sneakily
