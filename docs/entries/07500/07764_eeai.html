AI

えーあい
noun
artificial intelligence
1. artificial intelligence
Computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and decision-making.
AIが進化(しんか)している。
AI is evolving.
AIを活用(かつよう)したサービス(さーびす)が増(ふ)えた。
Services utilizing AI have increased.
AIに仕事(しごと)を奪(うば)われるという不安(ふあん)がある。
There are concerns about AI taking jobs.

ETYMOLOGY:
Abbreviation of 'Artificial Intelligence'.

USAGE:

A hot topic in modern society, discussed in contexts of:

  • Technology and innovation
  • Employment and the future of work
  • Ethics and regulation
  • Creative industries

COLLOCATIONS:

  • AI技術(ぎじゅつ) - AI technology
  • 生成(せいせい)AI - generative AI
  • AI搭載(とうさい) - AI-equipped
