ずばり

ずばり
adverb
directly; bluntly; exactly
1. directly; bluntly; frankly
Speaking or acting in a straightforward manner without beating around the bush.
2. exactly; precisely; right on target
Hitting something precisely or correctly, often used when guessing or predicting.
ずばり言(い)うと、それは間違(まちが)いだ。
To put it bluntly, that's wrong.
彼(かれ)の予想(よそう)がずばり当(あ)たった。
His prediction hit the mark exactly.
ずばり核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く質問(しつもん)だった。
It was a question that struck right at the heart of the matter.

MIMETIC ORIGIN:
Onomatopoeic word suggesting the sound of cutting through something sharply.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ずばり言(い)う - to speak bluntly
  • ずばり当(あ)たる - to hit the mark
  • ずばり指摘(してき)する - to point out directly
  • ずばり核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く - to strike at the heart of the matter

POSITION:
Typically appears before the verb it modifies.

NUANCE:
Conveys directness and precision. When used with 言(い)う, implies the speaker is being frank without softening the message.
