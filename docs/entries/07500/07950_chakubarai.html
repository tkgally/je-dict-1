着払(ちゃくばら)い

ちゃくばらい
noun
collect on delivery; freight collect
1. collect on delivery; freight collect
shipping payment collected from the recipient upon delivery
着払(ちゃくばら)いで送(おく)る。
Send it freight collect.
送料(そうりょう)は着払(ちゃくばら)いになります。
Shipping will be collect on delivery.
着払(ちゃくばら)いの荷物(にもつ)が届(とど)いた。
A collect-on-delivery package arrived.

着払(ちゃくばら)い means the recipient pays the shipping cost when the package arrives.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 着(ちゃく) (arrival)
  • 払(ばら)い (payment)
  • Literally: "arrival payment"

OPPOSITE:

  • 元払(もとばら)い (prepaid shipping - sender pays)
  • 送料(そうりょう)込(こ)み (shipping included)

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • Package delivery
  • Online shopping returns
  • Used item sales (flea market apps)
  • Business shipments

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 着払(ちゃくばら)いで送(おく)る (send freight collect)
  • 着払(ちゃくばら)いでお届(とど)け (delivered collect)
  • 着払(ちゃくばら)いは不可(ふか) (collect not accepted)
  • 着払(ちゃくばら)い対応(たいおう) (collect OK)

IN ONLINE SHOPPING:

  • Return shipping often 着払(ちゃくばら)い when seller at fault
  • Buyer pays 着払(ちゃくばら)い for "buyer's remorse" returns
  • Flea market apps: common for negotiating who pays

DELIVERY SERVICES:

  • 宅急便(たっきゅうびん) (Kuroneko Yamato)
  • ゆうパック(ゆうぱっく) (Japan Post)
  • 佐川急便(さがわきゅうびん) (Sagawa)
  • All offer 着払(ちゃくばら)い option

RELATED TERMS:

  • 元払(もとばら)い (prepaid)
  • 送料(そうりょう) (shipping cost)
  • 代金引換(だいきんひきかえ) (COD - payment for item AND shipping)
  • 配送料(はいそうりょう) (delivery fee)

DIFFERENCE FROM 代引(だいび)き:

  • 着払(ちゃくばら)い: Only shipping cost at delivery
  • 代引(だいび)き/代金引換(だいきんひきかえ): Item price + shipping at delivery

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in shipping and e-commerce contexts.
