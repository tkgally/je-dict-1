緻密(ちみつ)

ちみつ
na-adjective
meticulous; precise; elaborate
1. meticulous; precise; elaborate
extremely detailed and careful, showing great attention to accuracy
緻密(ちみつ)な計画(けいかく)を立(た)てた。
I made a detailed plan.
この絵(え)は緻密(ちみつ)に描(えが)かれている。
This painting is drawn with meticulous detail.
彼(かれ)は緻密(ちみつ)な性格(せいかく)で知(し)られている。
He is known for his meticulous personality.

緻密(ちみつ) describes exceptional attention to detail and precision.

COMPOSITION:

  • 緻(ち): Fine, detailed
  • 密(みつ): Dense, close

FORMS:

  • Attributive: 緻密(ちみつ)な + noun
  • Predicate: 緻密(ちみつ)だ/である
  • Adverbial: 緻密(ちみつ)に
  • Noun form: 緻密(ちみつ)さ (meticulousness)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 緻密(ちみつ)な計画(けいかく) (meticulous plan)
  • 緻密(ちみつ)な分析(ぶんせき) (detailed analysis)
  • 緻密(ちみつ)な作業(さぎょう) (precise work)
  • 緻密(ちみつ)な設計(せっけい) (elaborate design)
  • 緻密(ちみつ)に描(えが)かれた (meticulously drawn)

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • Plans and strategies
  • Art and craftsmanship
  • Academic research and analysis
  • Engineering and design
  • Personality descriptions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精密(せいみつ) (precise - more technical)
  • 細(こま)かい (detailed - more casual)
  • 丁寧(ていねい) (careful - broader meaning)
  • 綿密(めんみつ) (thorough - similar meaning)

REGISTER: Formal. Common in academic, technical, and written contexts.
