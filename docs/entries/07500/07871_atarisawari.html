当(あ)たり障(さわ)り

あたりさわり
noun
offense; impact; problem
1. offense; impact; harmful effect
Something that causes offense or has a negative impact on others. Most commonly used in the negative form.
当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない返事(へんじ)をした。
I gave a noncommittal response.
当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない話題(わだい)で場(ば)を繋(つな)いだ。
I kept the conversation going with safe topics.
あまり当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのないことばかり言(い)うのはつまらない。
It's boring to only say things that won't offend anyone.

USAGE:
Almost always used in the negative form 当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない, meaning "inoffensive," "harmless," or "noncommittal." The positive form is rarely used.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない話(はなし) - safe/harmless conversation
  • 当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない返事(へんじ) - noncommittal reply
  • 当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない表現(ひょうげん) - inoffensive expression

NUANCE:
Using 当(あ)たり障(さわ)りのない language can be seen as tactful in Japanese culture, but it can also suggest avoiding taking a clear position or being evasive.
