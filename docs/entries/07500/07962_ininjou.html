委任状(いにんじょう)

いにんじょう
noun
power of attorney; letter of authorization; proxy form
1. power of attorney; letter of authorization; proxy form
a written document authorizing someone to act on another's behalf
委任状(いにんじょう)を書(か)いてください。
Please write a letter of authorization.
本人(ほんにん)が来(こ)られない場合(ばあい)は、委任状(いにんじょう)が必要(ひつよう)です。
If the person cannot come in person, a power of attorney is required.
株主(かぶぬし)総会(そうかい)に出席(しゅっせき)できないので、委任状(いにんじょう)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
Since I couldn't attend the shareholders' meeting, I submitted a proxy form.

委任状(いにんじょう) is a formal document authorizing someone to act as a proxy or representative.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 委任(いにん) (entrustment, delegation)
  • 状(じょう) (document, letter)

COMMON USES:

  • Government offices: When someone else handles your paperwork
  • Bank transactions: For handling accounts on someone's behalf
  • Shareholder meetings: Voting by proxy
  • Legal matters: Authorizing lawyers to represent you

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 委任状(いにんじょう)を書(か)く (to write a power of attorney)
  • 委任状(いにんじょう)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit authorization)
  • 委任状(いにんじょう)が必要(ひつよう) (authorization required)

TYPICAL CONTENTS:

  • Name of the person being authorized
  • Specific matters they can handle
  • Date and signature/seal of the authorizing person
