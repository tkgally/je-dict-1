出資(しゅっし)

しゅっし
noun, suru verb
investment; contribution of capital
1. investment; contribution of capital
The act of providing money as capital for a business venture or company, typically in exchange for ownership or profit share.
新事業(しんじぎょう)に出資(しゅっし)する。
To invest in a new business.
出資(しゅっし)者(しゃ)を募集(ぼしゅう)している。
They are looking for investors.
出資(しゅっし)比率(ひりつ)に応(おう)じて利益(りえき)を分配(ぶんぱい)する。
Profits are distributed according to the investment ratio.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出資(しゅっし)者(しゃ) - investor, contributor
  • 出資(しゅっし)金(きん) - investment capital
  • 出資(しゅっし)比率(ひりつ) - investment ratio
  • 共同(きょうどう)出資(しゅっし) - joint investment
  • 出資(しゅっし)を募(つの)る - to seek investment

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
Used in business/corporate contexts to describe contributing capital to a company.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 投資(とうし) - investment (more general, includes stocks)
  • 融資(ゆうし) - financing, loan (lending money)
