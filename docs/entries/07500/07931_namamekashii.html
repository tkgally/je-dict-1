艶(なまめ)かしい

なまめかしい
i-adjective
alluring; seductive; bewitching
1. alluring; seductive; bewitching
having a captivating, sensual quality that attracts
艶(なまめ)かしい声(こえ)だ。
It's an alluring voice.
彼女(かのじょ)は艶(なまめ)かしい仕草(しぐさ)で魅了(みりょう)した。
She captivated with her seductive gestures.
着物(きもの)の女性(じょせい)の艶(なまめ)かしい姿(すがた)に見(み)とれた。
I was captivated by the bewitching figure of the woman in kimono.

艶(なまめ)かしい describes an elegant, sensual attractiveness, often with subtle erotic undertones.

ADJECTIVE TYPE:

  • I-adjective
  • Adverbial form: 艶(なまめ)かしく

KANJI NOTE:

  • 艶(なまめ)かしい uses 艶(なまめ) (luster, gloss, charm)
  • Related to 艶(つや) (gloss, luster)
  • Often written in hiragana: なまめかしい

NUANCE:

  • More refined and literary than 色(いろ)っぽい (sexy)
  • Suggests elegance alongside sensuality
  • Often used for traditional Japanese aesthetics
  • Can describe voice, gestures, appearance, atmosphere

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 艶(なまめ)かしい声(こえ) (alluring voice)
  • 艶(なまめ)かしい仕草(しぐさ) (seductive gestures)
  • 艶(なまめ)かしい姿(すがた) (bewitching figure)
  • 艶(なまめ)かしい雰囲気(ふんいき) (alluring atmosphere)
  • 艶(なまめ)かしい視線(しせん) (seductive gaze)

CONTRAST WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 艶(なまめ)かしい: Elegant, refined sensuality
  • 色(いろ)っぽい: Direct sexiness, more casual
  • 妖艶(ようえん) (な): Bewitching, enchanting (more literary)
  • セクシー: Western-style sexy

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • Literature and poetry
  • Descriptions of traditional arts (dance, music)
  • Fashion and appearance
  • Voice acting and performance

REGISTER: Literary to formal. More common in written Japanese.
