繰(く)り上(あ)げ

くりあげ
noun
moving up, advancement
1. moving up, advancement
moving up (a schedule, date, or deadline); advancing (to an earlier time)
予定(よてい)の繰(く)り上(あ)げをお願(ねが)いします。
Please move up the schedule.
会議(かいぎ)が1時間(じかん)繰(く)り上(あ)げになった。
The meeting was moved up by one hour.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)の繰(く)り上(あ)げが発表(はっぴょう)された。
The advancement of the graduation ceremony date was announced.
2. carrying (arithmetic)
carrying (in arithmetic, when a digit exceeds 9)
足(た)し算(ざん)の繰(く)り上(あ)げを習(なら)う。
Learn carrying in addition.
繰(く)り上(あ)げの計算(けいさん)が苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not good at carrying calculations.
二桁(ふたけた)の繰(く)り上(あ)げは難(むずか)しい。
Carrying with two-digit numbers is difficult.

繰(く)り上(あ)げ is a noun derived from the verb 繰(く)り上(あ)げる (to move up, to advance). The word has two main uses: scheduling and arithmetic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予定(よてい)の繰(く)り上(あ)げ (schedule advancement)
  • 日程(にってい)の繰(く)り上(あ)げ (moving up the date)
  • 繰(く)り上(あ)げ当選(とうせん) (winning by advancement, e.g., when a higher-ranked candidate withdraws)

VERB FORM:

  • 繰(く)り上(あ)げる (to move up, to advance)
  • 繰(く)り上(あ)がる (to be moved up - intransitive)

REGISTER: Neutral. Used in business, academic, and everyday contexts.

Related Words

Antonym: 繰(く)り下(さ)げ (moving down/postponing)
