隠蔽(いんぺい)

いんぺい
noun, suru verb
concealment; cover-up
1. concealment; cover-up
The act of deliberately hiding something, especially wrongdoing, evidence, or information that should be disclosed.
証拠(しょうこ)を隠蔽(いんぺい)する。
To conceal evidence.
会社(かいしゃ)は不正(ふせい)を隠蔽(いんぺい)しようとした。
The company tried to cover up the misconduct.
情報(じょうほう)隠蔽(いんぺい)が発覚(はっかく)し、大(おお)きなスキャンダルになった。
The information cover-up came to light and became a major scandal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 証拠(しょうこ)を隠蔽(いんぺい)する - to conceal evidence
  • 事実(じじつ)を隠蔽(いんぺい)する - to cover up facts
  • 情報(じょうほう)隠蔽(いんぺい) - information concealment
  • 隠蔽(いんぺい)工作(こうさく) - cover-up operation

NUANCE:
This word has a strongly negative connotation, implying deliberate and often illegal concealment of wrongdoing.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 発覚(はっかく) - coming to light (opposite outcome)
  • 暴露(ばくろ) - exposure (the act of revealing)
  • 捏造(ねつぞう) - fabrication

Related Words

Antonym: 暴露(ばくろ) (exposure)
Related: 発覚(はっかく) (coming to light)
