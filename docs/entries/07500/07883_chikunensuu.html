築年数(ちくねんすう)

ちくねんすう
noun
building age; years since construction
1. building age; years since construction
The number of years since a building was constructed, an important factor in real estate.
このマンションは築年数(ちくねんすう)が20年(ねん)だ。
This apartment building is 20 years old.
築年数(ちくねんすう)が浅(あさ)い物件(ぶっけん)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a property that's relatively new.
築年数(ちくねんすう)が経(た)っている分(ぶん)、家賃(やちん)が安(やす)い。
The rent is cheap because the building is older.

REAL ESTATE TERM:
A key factor in property valuations. Generally, older buildings have lower rent/prices but may have different characteristics.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 築年数(ちくねんすう)が浅(あさ)い - relatively new (lit. "shallow years")
  • 築年数(ちくねんすう)が経(た)っている - older building
  • 築(ちく)◯年(ねん) - ◯ years old (e.g., 築(ちく)10年(ねん) = 10 years old)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 築(ちく)◯年(ねん) - ◯ years old (shorter form in listings)
  • 新築(しんちく) - newly constructed
  • 中古(ちゅうこ) - second-hand, pre-owned
