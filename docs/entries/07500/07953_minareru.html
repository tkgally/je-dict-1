見慣(みな)れる

みなれる
verb
to become accustomed to seeing; to get used to seeing
1. to become accustomed to seeing; to get used to seeing
to no longer find something unusual or striking after seeing it many times
見慣(みな)れた風景(ふうけい)だ。
It's a familiar landscape.
この町(まち)にも見慣(みな)れてきた。
I've gotten used to seeing this town.
最初(さいしょ)は驚(おどろ)いたが、毎日(まいにち)見(み)ているうちに見慣(みな)れてしまった。
At first I was surprised, but after seeing it every day, I got used to it.

見慣(みな)れる is a compound verb combining 見(み)る (to see) + 慣(な)れる (to become accustomed), meaning to become accustomed to seeing something.

VERB TYPE: Ichidan verb (ru-verb), intransitive

CONJUGATION:

  • 見慣(みな)れない (negative)
  • 見慣(みな)れた (past/adjectival)
  • 見慣(みな)れて (te-form)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 見慣(みな)れた + noun (familiar ~): 見慣(みな)れた顔(かお) (familiar face)
  • 〜に見慣(みな)れる (to get used to seeing ~)
  • 見慣(みな)れない + noun (unfamiliar ~): 見慣(みな)れない人(ひと) (unfamiliar person)

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 聞(き)き慣(な)れる (to get used to hearing)
  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れる (to get used to eating)
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる (to get used to using)
