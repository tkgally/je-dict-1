1. arrival of goods; stocking
merchandise being received and stocked at a store or warehouse
新商品(しんしょうひん)が入荷(にゅうか)した。
New products have arrived.
次(つぎ)の入荷(にゅうか)は来週(らいしゅう)の予定(よてい)です。
The next shipment is scheduled for next week.
入荷(にゅうか)したら連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you when it comes in.

入荷(にゅうか) is a retail/business term for goods arriving at a store or warehouse.

WORD TYPE:

  • Noun
  • Also used as する verb: 入荷(にゅうか)する (to arrive, to come in stock)

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 入(にゅう) (enter)
  • 荷(か) (cargo, goods)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入荷(にゅうか)する (to arrive in stock)
  • 新{入荷(にゅうか)} (newly arrived stock)
  • 入荷(にゅうか)予定(よてい) (expected arrival)
  • 入荷(にゅうか)待(ま)ち (waiting for restocking)
  • 入荷(にゅうか)次第(しだい) (as soon as it arrives)

ANTONYM:

  • 出荷(しゅっか) (shipment out)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 欠品(けっぴん) (out of stock)
  • 在庫(ざいこ) (inventory)
  • 再入荷(さいにゅうか) (restocking)
  • 取(と)り寄(よ)せ (special order)

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • Retail stores
  • Online shopping
  • Customer service
  • Inventory management

COMMON SITUATIONS:

  • "When will it be back in stock?" → 入荷(にゅうか)予定(よてい)を確認(かくにん)する
  • "Just arrived!" → 本日(ほんじつ)入荷(にゅうか)!
  • "Waiting for stock" → 入荷(にゅうか)待(ま)ち

REGISTER: Business/retail vocabulary. Common in customer service.
