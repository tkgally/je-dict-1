1. to be shy
to be shy; to act embarrassed; to show embarrassment
子(こ)どもが恥(は)ずかしがっている。
The child is acting shy.
彼女(かのじょ)は褒(ほ)められると恥(は)ずかしがる。
She acts embarrassed when complimented.
そんなに恥(は)ずかしがらないで、もっと自信(じしん)を持(も)って。
Don't be so shy; have more confidence.

恥(は)ずかしがる is a godan verb formed from the adjective 恥(は)ずかしい (embarrassed) + the suffix がる.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xが恥(は)ずかしがる

ASPECT (ている):
恥(は)ずかしがっている describes someone visibly showing shyness or embarrassment at the moment.

The がる suffix converts adjectives (typically い-adjectives) into verbs that describe observable behavior, particularly useful for describing third-person emotions:

  • 恥(は)ずかしい (I feel embarrassed) → 恥(は)ずかしがる (to show embarrassment - third person)
  • 欲(ほ)しい (I want) → 欲(ほ)しがる (to show wanting)
  • 寂(さび)しい (I'm lonely) → 寂(さび)しがる (to show loneliness)
