蒸発(じょうはつ)

じょうはつ
noun, suru verb
evaporation; disappearance
1. evaporation
The physical process by which a liquid turns into vapor.
水(みず)が蒸発(じょうはつ)する。
Water evaporates.
2. disappearance; vanishing
Figuratively, when someone suddenly disappears without a trace, often to escape debts, relationships, or other problems.
夫(おっと)が蒸発(じょうはつ)して行方(ゆくえ)不明(ふめい)になった。
My husband vanished and his whereabouts are unknown.
借金(しゃっきん)を抱(かか)えて蒸発(じょうはつ)した。
He disappeared with debts.

SENSE 1 (Physical):

  • 水分(すいぶん)が蒸発(じょうはつ)する - moisture evaporates
  • 蒸発(じょうはつ)熱(ねつ) - heat of vaporization

SENSE 2 (Figurative - Disappearance):

This uniquely Japanese usage describes people who deliberately vanish from their lives. It became a social phenomenon in Japan, with people disappearing to escape debts, unhappy marriages, or other problems.

  • 夜逃(よに)げ - fleeing at night (similar concept)
  • 蒸発(じょうはつ)者(しゃ) - a person who has disappeared

CULTURAL NOTE:
The figurative use reflects a distinctly Japanese social phenomenon. There are even businesses in Japan that help people "evaporate" from their current lives.

SIMILAR PHYSICAL PROCESSES:

  • 凝縮(ぎょうしゅく) - condensation (opposite)
  • 沸騰(ふっとう) - boiling
