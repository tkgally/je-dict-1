揺(ゆ)らぐ

ゆらぐ
verb (godan)
to sway; to waver; to flicker
1. to sway; to shake
To move slowly back and forth or side to side, especially from wind or instability.
風(かぜ)で木々(きぎ)が揺(ゆ)らいでいる。
The trees are swaying in the wind.
2. to waver; to falter
To become unstable or uncertain, often referring to emotions, convictions, or positions.
彼(かれ)の決意(けつい)は少(すこ)しも揺(ゆ)らがなかった。
His resolve did not waver at all.
3. to flicker
For light or flame to move unsteadily.
ろうそくの炎(ほのお)が静(しず)かに揺(ゆ)らいでいた。
The candle flame was flickering quietly.

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
Pair: 揺(ゆ)さぶる (transitive - to shake)
Pattern: Xが揺(ゆ)らぐ

ASPECT (ている):
揺(ゆ)らいでいる - is swaying (continuous state)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 信念(しんねん)が揺(ゆ)らぐ - one's beliefs waver
  • 地位(ちい)が揺(ゆ)らぐ - one's position becomes unstable
  • 心(こころ)が揺(ゆ)らぐ - to feel emotionally unsettled
  • 炎(ほのお)が揺(ゆ)らぐ - flame flickers

NUANCE:
Suggests a gentle, slow movement rather than violent shaking. Often has poetic or emotional connotations.
