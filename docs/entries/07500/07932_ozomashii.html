おぞましい

おぞましい
i-adjective
disgusting; loathsome; horrifying
1. disgusting; loathsome; horrifying
causing intense revulsion or horror; deeply repulsive
おぞましい光景(こうけい)だった。
It was a horrifying sight.
その事件(じけん)はおぞましい記憶(きおく)として残(のこ)っている。
That incident remains as a horrifying memory.
おぞましい犯罪(はんざい)に震(ふる)えた。
I trembled at the loathsome crime.

おぞましい describes something that evokes deep revulsion or horror.

ADJECTIVE TYPE:

  • I-adjective
  • Adverbial form: おぞましく

KANJI NOTE:

  • Usually written in hiragana
  • Can be written as 悍(おぞ)ましい (rare)

INTENSITY:

  • Stronger than 気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い (unpleasant)
  • Implies visceral reaction of horror/disgust
  • Often used for crimes, cruelty, or disturbing scenes

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おぞましい光景(こうけい) (horrifying sight)
  • おぞましい事件(じけん) (horrifying incident)
  • おぞましい犯罪(はんざい) (loathsome crime)
  • おぞましい記憶(きおく) (horrifying memory)
  • おぞましい行為(こうい) (disgusting act)

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • News reports about serious crimes
  • Literature describing horror/revulsion
  • Strong condemnation of behavior
  • Not used for mild unpleasantness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 恐(おそ)ろしい (frightening, terrible)
  • 気味(きみ)が悪(わる)い (creepy)
  • 忌(い)まわしい (detestable, ominous)
  • 醜悪(しゅうあく) (な) (hideous, repulsive)

REGISTER: Formal to literary. Common in news and serious writing.
