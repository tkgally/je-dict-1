お手伝(てつだ)い

おてつだい
noun/suru-verb
help
1. help
help; assistance (polite)
お手伝(てつだ)いしましょうか。
Shall I help you?
子(こ)どもにお手伝(てつだ)いをさせる。
To have the children help out.
何(なに)かお手伝(てつだ)いできることがあればお申(もう)し付(つ)けください。
Please let me know if there's anything I can help with.
2. housemaid
housemaid; domestic helper (dated)
うちにはお手伝(てつだ)いさんがいます。
We have a housemaid at our home.
お手伝(てつだ)いさんに掃除(そうじ)を頼(たの)む。
To ask the housemaid to clean.
昔(むかし)は裕福(ゆうふく)な家庭(かてい)にはお手伝(てつだ)いさんがいた。
In the past, wealthy households had domestic helpers.

お手伝(てつだ)い is the polite form of 手伝(てつだ)い, with the honorific prefix お.

Sense 1 (help/assistance):

  • お手伝(てつだ)いする (to help - polite)
  • お手伝(てつだ)いをする (same meaning)

Sense 2 (housemaid):

  • Usually referred to as お手伝(てつだ)いさん with the respectful suffix さん
  • This usage is somewhat dated; modern terms include 家政婦(かせいふ) or ハウスキーパー

For children, helping with household chores is called お手伝(てつだ)い, an important concept in Japanese family education.
