ほんま

ほんま
adverb, na-adjective
really; truly (Kansai dialect)
1. really; truly; honestly
Kansai dialect equivalent of 本当(ほんとう). Used to emphasize truth or express genuine feelings.
ほんまに美味(おい)しいわ。
It's really delicious.
ほんま？信(しん)じられへん。
Really? I can't believe it.
それ、ほんまの話(はなし)？
Is that a true story?

DIALECT:
Kansai (関西(かんさい)) dialect, primarily used in Osaka, Kyoto, and surrounding regions.

STANDARD EQUIVALENT:
本当(ほんとう) / ほんと (standard Japanese)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ほんまに - really (adverb)
  • ほんまの - real, true (adjective)
  • ほんま？ - Really? (question)
  • ほんまや - That's true (affirmation)

USAGE NOTES:
関西弁(かんさいべん) has become widely recognized throughout Japan due to TV comedians and media. ほんま is one of the most commonly understood Kansai expressions.

RELATED DIALECT WORDS:

  • 嘘(うそ)やん - No way! / You're kidding!
  • まじで - Really/Seriously (youth slang, nationwide)
