1. to embezzle; to misappropriate
to use money or funds entrusted to one for personal purposes; to misuse funds
会社(かいしゃ)の金(かね)を使(つか)い込(こ)んだ。
He embezzled the company's money.
遺産(いさん)を使(つか)い込(こ)んでしまった。
He squandered the inheritance.
公金(こうきん)を使(つか)い込(こ)んで逮捕(たいほ)された。
He was arrested for embezzling public funds.
2. to become skilled at using; to break in
to use something repeatedly until it becomes familiar and comfortable, or until one becomes proficient with it
この道具(どうぐ)は使(つか)い込(こ)むほど味(あじ)が出(で)る。
This tool develops more character the more you use it.
使(つか)い込(こ)まれた革(かわ)の財布(さいふ)は独特(どくとく)の風合(ふうあ)いがある。
A well-used leather wallet has a unique patina.
長年(ながねん)使(つか)い込(こ)んだフライパンは手放(てばな)せない。
I can't part with a frying pan I've used for many years.

使(つか)い込(こ)む has two distinct meanings.

SENSE 1 - EMBEZZLEMENT:

  • Using funds that belong to others for personal purposes
  • Often appears in news about financial crimes
  • Related to 横領(おうりょう) (embezzlement)

SENSE 2 - WEARING IN:

  • Using something until it becomes comfortable or shows character
  • Positive connotation: appreciation for well-used items
  • Common with leather goods, tools, musical instruments

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

Sense 1:

  • 公金(こうきん)を使(つか)い込(こ)む (to embezzle public funds)
  • 会社(かいしゃ)の金(かね)を使(つか)い込(こ)む (to embezzle company money)

Sense 2:

  • 使(つか)い込(こ)まれた～ (well-used ~)
  • 使(つか)い込(こ)むほど (the more you use it)

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. Takes を for money/items.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 使(つか)い果(は)たす (to use up completely)
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる (to become accustomed to using)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in both news and everyday contexts.
