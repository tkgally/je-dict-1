せや

せや
expression
that's right (Kansai dialect)
1. that's right; yeah; exactly
Kansai dialect equivalent of そうだ (sou da), used to express agreement or affirmation.
「今日(きょう)は金曜日(きんようび)やろ？」「せや」
"It's Friday today, right?" "Yeah."
せやな、その通(とお)りや。
Yeah, that's right.
せやせや、忘(わす)れてた。
Oh yeah, I forgot.

DIALECT:
Kansai (関西(かんさい)) dialect, widely used throughout the region including Osaka, Kyoto, and Kobe.

STANDARD EQUIVALENT:
そう / そうだ (standard Japanese)

DERIVED FORMS:

  • せやな - that's right, isn't it
  • せやで - it is, you know
  • せやから - that's why
  • せやけど - but, however
  • せやろ - right? (seeking confirmation)
  • せやせや - yes yes, that's right

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • ほんま - really (Kansai)
  • そうや - that's right (variant)

USAGE:
Very common in everyday Kansai speech. Often heard in comedy shows and Osaka-based media.
