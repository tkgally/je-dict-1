まごまご

まごまご
adverb, verb (suru)
at a loss; bewildered; confused
1. at a loss; bewildered; not knowing what to do
Being confused or flustered, unable to act decisively due to uncertainty about what to do.
突然(とつぜん)のことでまごまごしてしまった。
I was bewildered by the sudden event.
まごまごしていないで早(はや)く決(き)めなさい。
Stop dithering and decide quickly.
道(みち)に迷(まよ)ってまごまごしていた。
I was lost and confused about what to do.

MIMETIC TYPE:
Onomatopoeia expressing hesitation and confusion.

GRAMMATICAL FORMS:

  • まごまごする - to be at a loss (suru-verb)
  • まごまご（と）している - to be bewildered (state)

NUANCE:
Implies being flustered and unable to act decisively. Often used when someone is caught off guard or doesn't know how to respond to a situation.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • おろおろする - to be flustered (more panic)
  • うろうろする - to wander aimlessly (more physical)
  • もたもたする - to dawdle (more about slowness)
  • きょろきょろする - to look around restlessly
