ED

いーでぃー
noun
ending theme; ending song
1. ending theme; ending song
The song or musical sequence that plays at the end of an anime, TV drama, or video game episode.
EDも良(い)い曲(きょく)だ。
The ending theme is also a good song.
EDで泣(な)いてしまった。
I cried during the ending theme.
OPよりEDの方(ほう)が好(す)きだ。
I like the ending theme more than the opening.

ETYMOLOGY:
Abbreviation of English 'ending'.

USAGE:
Used in anime and drama discussions. Ending themes often have a different mood from opening themes, frequently more emotional or reflective.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • ED曲(きょく) - ending song
  • EDテーマ - ending theme
  • ED映像(えいぞう) - ending visuals/animation

Related Words

Antonym: OP (opening theme)
