果敢(かかん)

かかん
na-adjective
bold; resolute; daring
1. bold; resolute; daring
showing courage and determination in taking action
果敢(かかん)に挑戦(ちょうせん)する。
To challenge boldly.
彼(かれ)は果敢(かかん)な決断(けつだん)を下(くだ)した。
He made a bold decision.
困難(こんなん)に果敢(かかん)に立(た)ち向(む)かった。
They resolutely faced the difficulties.

果敢(かかん) describes bold, decisive action, especially in challenging situations.

WORD TYPE:

  • Na-adjective
  • Adverbial form: 果敢(かかん)に (very common)

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 果(か) (fruit, result, decisive)
  • 敢(かん) (daring, brave)

NUANCE:

  • Always positive connotation
  • Implies action despite risks or obstacles
  • Often used in sports, business, and military contexts
  • Suggests admirable courage

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 果敢(かかん)に挑戦(ちょうせん)する (boldly challenge)
  • 果敢(かかん)に立(た)ち向(む)かう (resolutely face)
  • 果敢(かかん)な攻撃(こうげき) (bold attack)
  • 果敢(かかん)な決断(けつだん) (bold decision)
  • 果敢(かかん)な姿勢(しせい) (resolute attitude)
  • 果敢(かかん)に攻(せ)める (attack boldly)

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • Sports commentary (aggressive play)
  • Business (bold strategies)
  • News (courageous actions)
  • Military/historical accounts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大胆(だいたん) (bold, daring)
  • 勇敢(ゆうかん) (brave, courageous)
  • 積極的(せっきょくてき) (positive, proactive)

DIFFERENCES:

  • 果敢(かかん): Action-focused, decisive execution
  • 大胆(だいたん): Fearlessness, sometimes reckless
  • 勇敢(ゆうかん): Moral courage, bravery

REGISTER: Formal. Common in news reports and formal contexts.
