忖度(そんたく)

そんたく
noun, suru-verb
surmising someone's wishes; reading between the lines; anticipating desires
1. surmising someone's wishes; conjecturing someone's feelings
The act of guessing or inferring what someone wants or thinks without being told directly, often to act accordingly.
上司(じょうし)の意向(いこう)を忖度(そんたく)する。
To surmise what the boss wants.
忖度(そんたく)が働(はたら)いたのではないか。
I suspect they were anticipating someone's wishes.
忖度(そんたく)なしで意見(いけん)を言(い)ってください。
Please give your opinion without trying to guess what I want to hear.

ETYMOLOGY:
From classical Chinese: 忖(そん) (to measure, consider) + 度(たく) (to conjecture)

CULTURAL NOTE:
This term gained widespread attention in 2017 during Japanese political scandals, where it was used to describe how officials allegedly acted to please superiors without explicit instructions. It reflects the Japanese cultural emphasis on reading the air (空気(くうき)を読(よ)む) and anticipating others' wishes.

NUANCE:
Can be neutral (normal social skill) or negative (inappropriate deference or sycophancy), depending on context.

RELATED:

  • 察(さっ)する - to sense, perceive
  • 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む - to read the room
  • 推(お)し量(はか)る - to conjecture
