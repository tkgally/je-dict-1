使(つか)い捨(す)て

つかいすて
noun
disposable; single-use; throwaway
1. disposable; single-use; throwaway
Designed to be used once and then discarded; not intended for reuse.
使(つか)い捨(す)てマスク
Disposable mask.
使(つか)い捨(す)て文化(ぶんか)を見直(みなお)す必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to reconsider the throwaway culture.
環境(かんきょう)のために使(つか)い捨(す)てプラスチックを減(へ)らそう。
Let's reduce single-use plastics for the environment.

USAGE:
Used as a noun modifier (使(つか)い捨(す)ての〜) or as part of compound nouns.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 使(つか)い捨(す)てマスク - disposable mask
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)てカイロ - disposable hand warmer
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)てカメラ - disposable camera
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)てコンタクトレンズ - disposable contact lenses
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)て容器(ようき) - disposable container

ENVIRONMENTAL CONTEXT:
使(つか)い捨(す)て is often used in discussions about environmental issues, particularly regarding plastic waste and sustainable living.
