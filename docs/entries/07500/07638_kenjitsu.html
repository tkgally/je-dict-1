堅実(けんじつ)

けんじつ
na-adjective, noun
steady; solid; reliable
1. steady; solid; reliable
Characterized by being sensible, reliable, and avoiding unnecessary risks; down-to-earth and practical.
堅実(けんじつ)な経営(けいえい)。
Solid management.
彼(かれ)は堅実(けんじつ)な人柄(ひとがら)で信頼(しんらい)されている。
He is trusted for his reliable character.
堅実(けんじつ)に貯金(ちょきん)を続(つづ)けている。
I've been steadily continuing to save money.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 堅実(けんじつ)な経営(けいえい) - solid management
  • 堅実(けんじつ)な人柄(ひとがら) - reliable character
  • 堅実(けんじつ)な生活(せいかつ) - sensible lifestyle
  • 堅実(けんじつ)に働(はたら)く - to work steadily
  • 堅実(けんじつ)さ - reliability (noun form)

POSITIVE CONNOTATION:
A complimentary term suggesting someone who is dependable, avoids risky behavior, and makes sound decisions.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着実(ちゃくじつ) - steady, reliable (emphasizes consistent progress)
  • 確実(かくじつ) - certain, sure
