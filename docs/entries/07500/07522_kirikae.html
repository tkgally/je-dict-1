切(き)り替(か)え

きりかえ
noun, verb (suru)
switching; changeover; toggle
1. switching; changeover; changing over
The act of changing from one thing to another, such as switching systems, modes, or approaches.
システムの切(き)り替(か)えは深夜(しんや)に行(おこな)う。
The system switchover will be done late at night.
夏時間(なつじかん)への切(き)り替(か)えが行(おこな)われた。
The changeover to daylight saving time was implemented.
2. mental shift; change of mindset
Shifting one's mental focus or attitude, especially recovering from a setback.
気持(きも)ちの切(き)り替(か)えが大事(だいじ)だ。
A mental shift is important.

WORD STRUCTURE:
切(き)り替(か)える (to switch) → 切(き)り替(か)え (noun form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切(き)り替(か)えを行(おこな)う - to carry out a switchover
  • 気持(きも)ちの切(き)り替(か)え - mental shift
  • 画面(がめん)切(き)り替(か)え - screen switching
  • 季節(きせつ)の切(き)り替(か)え - seasonal change

IT/TECHNICAL USES:

  • システム切(き)り替(か)え - system switchover
  • 切(き)り替(か)えスイッチ - toggle switch
  • 入力(にゅうりょく)切(き)り替(か)え - input switching
  • 言語(げんご)切(き)り替(か)え - language switching

SPORTS/PSYCHOLOGY:

切(き)り替(か)え is important in sports and business - the ability to mentally recover from mistakes and refocus.

  • 失敗(しっぱい)を引(ひ)きずらずに切(き)り替(か)える - to not dwell on failure and move on
