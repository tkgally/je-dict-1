踏(ふ)まえる

ふまえる
ichidan verb, transitive
to be based on; to take into account; to consider
1. to be based on; to take into account
To use something as a foundation or basis for an action, decision, or argument.
結果(けっか)を踏(ふ)まえて判断(はんだん)する。
I'll make a decision based on the results.
過去(かこ)の経験(けいけん)を踏(ふ)まえた提案(ていあん)です。
This is a proposal based on past experience.
その点(てん)を踏(ふ)まえて、もう一度(いちど)検討(けんとう)してください。
Please reconsider with that point in mind.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 踏(ふ)む (to step on) - figuratively "standing on" something as a foundation

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) - takes a を object

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を踏(ふ)まえて - based on ~, considering ~
  • 〜を踏(ふ)まえた + noun - a ~ based on...

REGISTER:
Often used in formal or business contexts when discussing decisions or proposals based on certain factors.

RELATED:

  • 基(もと)づく - to be based on
  • 考慮(こうりょ)する - to consider
  • 前提(ぜんてい)とする - to premise on
