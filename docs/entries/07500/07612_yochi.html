余地(よち)

よち
noun
room; margin; scope; leeway
1. room; margin; space
Physical or metaphorical space that allows for something additional.
2. scope; leeway; possibility
The potential or opportunity for something to occur or be done.
改善(かいぜん)の余地(よち)がある。
There is room for improvement.
疑(うたが)う余地(よち)はない。
There is no room for doubt.
交渉(こうしょう)の余地(よち)が残(のこ)されている。
There is still room for negotiation.

ETYMOLOGY:
余(よ) (extra, surplus) + 地(ち) (ground, space)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の余地(よち)がある - there is room for ~
  • 〜の余地(よち)がない - there is no room for ~
  • 〜の余地(よち)を残(のこ)す - to leave room for ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改善(かいぜん)の余地(よち) - room for improvement
  • 議論(ぎろん)の余地(よち) - room for debate
  • 疑(うたが)いの余地(よち) - room for doubt
  • 交渉(こうしょう)の余地(よち) - room for negotiation
  • 選択(せんたく)の余地(よち) - room for choice

NUANCE:
Often used in formal or written contexts to discuss possibilities or limitations.
