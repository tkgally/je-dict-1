暴挙(ぼうきょ)

ぼうきょ
noun
outrage; reckless act; violent action
1. outrage; reckless act; violent action
a rash, violent, or outrageous action that causes harm or goes against reason
それは許(ゆる)しがたい暴挙(ぼうきょ)だ。
That is an unforgivable outrage.
彼(かれ)の暴挙(ぼうきょ)に全員(ぜんいん)が衝撃(しょうげき)を受(う)けた。
Everyone was shocked by his reckless act.
政府(せいふ)の暴挙(ぼうきょ)に対(たい)して国民(こくみん)は抗議(こうぎ)の声(こえ)を上(あ)げた。
The citizens raised their voices in protest against the government's outrage.
この暴挙(ぼうきょ)は断(だん)じて容認(ようにん)できない。
This outrageous act is absolutely unacceptable.

暴挙(ぼうきょ) refers to a violent, rash, or outrageous action, often used to condemn unjust or harmful actions.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 暴(ぼう) (violence, outrage)
  • 挙(きょ) (action, conduct)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 〜は暴挙(ぼうきょ)だ (~ is an outrage)
  • 暴挙(ぼうきょ)に出(で)る (to resort to a reckless act)
  • 暴挙(ぼうきょ)を働(はたら)く (to commit an outrage)

CONTEXTS:

  • Political criticism (government actions deemed unjust)
  • Condemning violent or reckless behavior
  • News and editorial writing
  • Historical events (rebellions, coups, etc.)

REGISTER:

  • Formal/written style
  • Common in news, editorials, and political discourse
  • Strong negative connotation

RELATED TERMS:

  • 蛮行(ばんこう) (barbaric act, atrocity)
  • 愚挙(ぐきょ) (foolish act)
  • 横暴(おうぼう) (tyranny, high-handedness)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう) (violence, rough behavior)
