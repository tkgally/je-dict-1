仕掛(しか)け

しかけ
noun
device; mechanism; trick
1. device; mechanism; contraption
a mechanical or structural arrangement designed to achieve a specific effect
この時計(とけい)には複雑(ふくざつ)な仕掛(しか)けがある。
This clock has a complex mechanism.
仕掛(しか)け絵本(えほん)を買(か)った。
I bought a pop-up book.
罠(わな)の仕掛(しか)けを解除(かいじょ)した。
I disabled the trap's mechanism.
2. trick; setup; scheme
a clever plan or arrangement designed to produce a desired outcome
マジックの仕掛(しか)けがわからない。
I can't figure out the trick behind the magic.
この映画(えいが)には意外(いがい)な仕掛(しか)けがある。
This movie has a surprising twist.
この事件(じけん)には何(なに)か仕掛(しか)けがあるはずだ。
There must be some trick behind this incident.

仕掛(しか)け refers to mechanisms, devices, or clever setups.

ETYMOLOGY:

  • From 仕掛(しか)ける (to set up, to set a trap)
  • 仕(し): To do
  • 掛(か)け: To hang, to set

SENSE 1 VS SENSE 2:

  • Sense 1: Physical mechanisms (clocks, toys, traps)
  • Sense 2: Abstract tricks or setups (plots, magic tricks)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 仕掛(しか)け絵本(えほん) (pop-up book)
  • 仕掛(しか)け花火(はなび) (fireworks display)
  • 仕掛(しか)け時計(どけい) (mechanical clock with figures)
  • からくり仕掛(しか)け (elaborate mechanism)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕掛(しか)けがある (to have a mechanism/trick)
  • 仕掛(しか)けを作(つく)る (to create a mechanism)
  • 仕掛(しか)けを見破(みやぶ)る (to see through a trick)
  • 罠(わな)の仕掛(しか)け (trap mechanism)

RELATED VERB:

  • 仕掛(しか)ける (to set up, to start, to attack)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • からくり (mechanism - traditional)
  • トリック (trick - loanword)
  • 装置(そうち) (device - more technical)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in everyday conversation.
