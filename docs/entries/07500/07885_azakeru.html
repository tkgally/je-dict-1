嘲(あざけ)る

あざける
godan verb, transitive
to mock; to ridicule; to sneer at
1. to mock; to ridicule; to sneer at
To make fun of someone in a contemptuous or scornful way.
彼(かれ)の失敗(しっぱい)を嘲(あざけ)った。
They mocked his failure.
嘲(あざけ)るような笑(わら)い声(ごえ)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard mocking laughter.
他人(たにん)の努力(どりょく)を嘲(あざけ)るのは良(よ)くない。
It's not good to mock others' efforts.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). The person/thing being mocked takes を.

RELATED FORMS:

  • 嘲(あざけ)り - mockery, ridicule (noun)
  • 嘲笑(ちょうしょう) - derision, mockery (Sino-Japanese compound)

COMPARISON:

  • 嘲(あざけ)る - to mock, ridicule (contemptuous)
  • からかう - to tease (often playful)
  • 馬鹿(ばか)にする - to make a fool of, look down on
  • 冷(ひ)やかす - to tease, make fun of

USAGE:
This word has a negative connotation, implying contempt or cruelty. It's stronger than からかう (to tease).
