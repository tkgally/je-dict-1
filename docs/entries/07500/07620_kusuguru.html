くすぐる

くすぐる
verb (godan)
to tickle; to titillate
1. to tickle
To touch someone lightly in a way that causes an itchy, often involuntary laughing response.
足(あし)の裏(うら)をくすぐられて笑(わら)い転(ころ)げた。
I was tickled on the soles of my feet and rolled around laughing.
赤(あか)ちゃんをくすぐると喜(よろこ)ぶ。
The baby is delighted when you tickle them.
2. to titillate; to appeal to
To pleasantly stimulate someone's emotions, curiosity, or vanity.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)は私(わたし)の好奇心(こうきしん)をくすぐった。
His words tickled my curiosity.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
Pattern: Xをくすぐる

KANJI:
Rarely written with kanji (擽(くすぐ)る), usually hiragana.

ASPECT (ている):
くすぐっている - is tickling (action in progress)

FIGURATIVE EXPRESSIONS:

  • 好奇心(こうきしん)をくすぐる - to pique one's curiosity
  • 虚栄心(きょえいしん)をくすぐる - to appeal to one's vanity
  • 冒険心(ぼうけんしん)をくすぐる - to appeal to one's sense of adventure

RELATED:

  • くすぐったい - ticklish; embarrassing
  • こそばゆい - ticklish (regional variant)
