ひしめく

ひしめく
verb (godan)
to crowd together
1. to crowd together
to jostle; to be packed closely together; to crowd and push against each other
人々(ひとびと)がひしめいている。
People are crowded together.
繁華街(はんかがい)には店(みせ)がひしめき合(あ)っている。
Shops are packed closely together in the downtown area.
満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)の中(なか)で乗客(じょうきゃく)がひしめき合(あ)っていた。
Passengers were packed tightly together in the crowded train.

ひしめく is an intransitive godan verb describing a state of being densely crowded or packed together, often with a sense of jostling or pressing against each other.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)).

COMMON FORMS:

  • ひしめく: Basic form
  • ひしめき合(あ)う: Emphasizes mutual crowding (people/things pressing against each other)
  • ひしめいている: Describes a current state of being crowded

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)がひしめく (people crowd together)
  • 店(みせ)がひしめく (shops are packed closely)
  • ひしめき合(あ)う (to jostle, crowd together)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often used to describe crowded urban areas, busy markets, or packed trains
  • The kanji 犇(ひしめ)く exists but is rarely used; hiragana is standard

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 込(こ)み合(あ)う: To be crowded (more neutral)
  • ごった返(がえ)す: To be packed/bustling
  • ひしめく: Emphasizes physical closeness and pressing

REGISTER: Neutral to slightly literary.
