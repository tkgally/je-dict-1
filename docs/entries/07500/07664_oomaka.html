大(おお)まか

おおまか
na-adjective
rough; approximate; broad; general
1. rough; approximate; broad; general; not detailed
Lacking in detail or precision; giving only a general outline or overview.
大(おお)まかな計画(けいかく)
A rough plan.
大(おお)まかに言(い)うと、そういうことだ。
Roughly speaking, that's what it's about.
大(おお)まかな見積(みつも)もりで百万円(ひゃくまんえん)くらいかかる。
A rough estimate would be about one million yen.

FORMS:

  • 大(おお)まかな〜 (modifier)
  • 大(おお)まかに - roughly, broadly (adverbial form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)まかな計画(けいかく) - rough plan
  • 大(おお)まかな見積(みつも)もり - rough estimate
  • 大(おお)まかに言(い)うと - roughly speaking
  • 大(おお)まかな流(なが)れ - general flow/outline

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ざっくり - roughly (casual)
  • 概(おおむ)ね - generally, roughly
  • 大体(だいたい) - roughly, approximately
