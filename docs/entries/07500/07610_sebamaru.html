1. to narrow; to become narrow
For space, width, or range to decrease or become more restricted.
道(みち)が先(さき)に行(い)くほど狭(せば)まっている。
The road narrows as you go further.
選択肢(せんたくし)が狭(せば)まってきた。
The options have narrowed down.
川幅(かわはば)が急(きゅう)に狭(せば)まる地点(ちてん)がある。
There's a point where the river width suddenly narrows.

TRANSITIVITY:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
Pair: 狭(せば)める (transitive - to narrow something)
Pattern: Xが狭(せば)まる

RELATION TO 狭(せま)い:
狭(せば)まる is the verb form derived from 狭(せま)い (narrow). Note the consonant change: せま→せば.

ASPECT (ている):
狭(せば)まっている - has narrowed (resultant state)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 視野(しや)が狭(せば)まる - one's perspective narrows
  • 範囲(はんい)が狭(せば)まる - the range narrows
  • 可能性(かのうせい)が狭(せば)まる - possibilities narrow

FIGURATIVE USE:
Often used metaphorically for options, possibilities, or scope being reduced.
