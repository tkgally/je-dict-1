借入(かりいれ)

かりいれ
noun, verb (suru)
borrowing; loan
1. borrowing; loan; borrowing money
The act of borrowing money or the borrowed amount, typically in financial or business contexts.
銀行(ぎんこう)から借入(かりいれ)をした。
I took out a loan from the bank.
借入(かりいれ)金額(きんがく)は500万円(まんえん)だ。
The borrowed amount is 5 million yen.
住宅(じゅうたく)ローンの借入(かりいれ)期間(きかん)は35年(ねん)です。
The mortgage loan period is 35 years.

WORD STRUCTURE:
借(かり) (borrow) + 入(いれ) (in/receive) - receiving borrowed funds

OPPOSITE:

  • 貸出(かしだ)し - lending

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 借入(かりいれ)する - to borrow
  • 借入金(かりいれきん) - borrowed money, loan
  • 借入(かりいれ)残高(ざんだか) - outstanding loan balance
  • 借入(かりいれ)期間(きかん) - loan period
  • 借入(かりいれ)限度額(げんどがく) - borrowing limit
  • 借入(かりいれ)利率(りりつ) - borrowing interest rate
  • 短期(たんき)借入(かりいれ) - short-term borrowing
  • 長期(ちょうき)借入(かりいれ) - long-term borrowing

RELATED FINANCIAL TERMS:

  • 融資(ゆうし) - financing, loan (from lender's perspective)
  • ローン - loan (loanword)
  • 返済(へんさい) - repayment
  • 利息(りそく) - interest
  • 担保(たんぽ) - collateral

USAGE:
Primarily used in financial and business contexts. For casual borrowing (like borrowing a book), use 借(か)りる instead.

Related Words

Antonym: 貸出(かしだ)し (lending)
