積立(つみたて)

つみたて
noun
savings; accumulation; installment savings
1. savings; accumulation; installment savings
regularly setting aside money or resources, typically for a specific purpose
毎月(まいつき)積立(つみたて)をしている。
I make regular savings every month.
積立(つみたて)貯金(ちょきん)で旅行(りょこう)資金(しきん)を貯(た)めた。
I saved up travel funds through installment savings.
将来(しょうらい)のために積立(つみたて)NISAを始(はじ)めることにした。
I decided to start a tsumitate NISA for my future.

積立(つみたて) refers to regular savings or accumulation, commonly used in financial contexts.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 積(つ)む (to pile up, accumulate)
  • 立(た)てる (to set up, establish)

USAGE:

  • 積立(つみたて)する (to save regularly - suru verb)
  • 積(つ)み立(た)てる (verb form - to accumulate)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 積立(つみたて)貯金(ちょきん) (installment savings)
  • 積立(つみたて)保険(ほけん) (accumulation-type insurance)
  • 積立(つみたて)NISA (積立(つみたて)型(がた)少額(しょうがく)投資(とうし)非課税(ひかぜい)制度(せいど))
  • 積立(つみたて)投資(とうし) (regular investment)

FINANCIAL CONTEXT:

  • Popular savings method in Japan
  • Often automatic monthly deductions
  • Used for retirement, education, travel funds

積立(つみたて)NISA:

  • Tax-advantaged investment account in Japan
  • Encourages long-term, regular investing
  • Popular since 2018

RELATED TERMS:

  • 定期(ていき)積金(つみきん) (fixed-term installment savings)
  • 貯蓄(ちょちく) (savings - general term)
  • 投資(とうし) (investment)
