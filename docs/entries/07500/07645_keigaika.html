形骸化(けいがいか)

けいがいか
noun, suru verb
becoming nominal; losing substance; becoming an empty shell
1. becoming nominal; losing substance; becoming an empty shell
The process by which something loses its original meaning, function, or vitality while retaining only its outward form; becoming a mere formality.
規則(きそく)が形骸化(けいがいか)している。
The rules have become nominal.
会議(かいぎ)が形骸化(けいがいか)して意味(いみ)がなくなった。
The meetings have become empty formalities and lost their meaning.
伝統(でんとう)行事(ぎょうじ)の形骸化(けいがいか)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent traditional events from becoming mere formalities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 規則(きそく)の形骸化(けいがいか) - rules becoming nominal
  • 制度(せいど)の形骸化(けいがいか) - system becoming an empty shell
  • 形骸化(けいがいか)を防(ふせ)ぐ - to prevent from becoming hollow
  • 形骸化(けいがいか)した組織(そしき) - an organization that has lost its substance

ETYMOLOGY:
形骸(けいがい) means "skeleton" or "mere shell," so 形骸化(けいがいか) literally means "to become a skeleton/shell."

NUANCE:
Always negative - implies something has degenerated from meaningful to meaningless.

RELATED -化(か) WORDS:

  • 空洞化(くうどうか) - hollowing out
  • 陳腐化(ちんぷか) - becoming obsolete
