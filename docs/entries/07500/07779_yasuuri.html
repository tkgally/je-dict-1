安売(やすう)り

やすうり
noun/suru-verb
bargain sale
1. bargain sale
bargain sale; selling at a discount; cheap sale
このスーパーは野菜(やさい)を安売(やすう)りしている。
This supermarket sells vegetables at a discount.
閉店(へいてん)セールで全品(ぜんぴん)安売(やすう)り中(ちゅう)です。
All items are on sale as part of the closing sale.
品質(ひんしつ)を下(さ)げずに安売(やすう)りするのは難(むずか)しい。
It's difficult to sell at a discount without lowering quality.

安売(やすう)り combines 安(やす)い (cheap) with 売(う)り (selling), meaning to sell at reduced prices.

Common expressions:

  • 安売(やすう)りする (to sell at a discount)
  • 安売(やすう)り中(ちゅう) (currently on sale)
  • 安売(やすう)り店(てん) (discount store)

Figurative usage:

  • 自分(じぶん)を安売(やすう)りする (to sell oneself short, to undervalue oneself)

Related terms:

  • セール (sale)
  • 特売(とくばい) (special sale)
  • バーゲン (bargain sale)
