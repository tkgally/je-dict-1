1. to wipe off; to wipe away
To remove liquid, dirt, or something unwanted by wiping with a cloth or hand.
汗(あせ)を拭(ぬぐ)った。
I wiped off the sweat.
タオルで汗(あせ)を拭(ぬぐ)った。
I wiped off the sweat with a towel.
涙(なみだ)を拭(ぬぐ)って笑顔(えがお)を見(み)せた。
She wiped away her tears and showed a smile.
2. to dispel; to clear away
Figuratively, to eliminate or remove something negative like doubts or suspicions.
疑惑(ぎわく)を拭(ぬぐ)えない。
I can't dispel the suspicions.
不安(ふあん)を拭(ぬぐ)い去(さ)ることができない。
I can't dispel my anxiety.
何度(なんど)説明(せつめい)を聞(き)いても、疑(うたが)いを拭(ぬぐ)い去(さ)ることはできなかった。
No matter how many times I heard the explanation, I couldn't dispel my doubts.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
Pattern: Xを拭(ぬぐ)う

ASPECT (ている):
拭(ぬぐ)っている - is wiping (action in progress)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 汗(あせ)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to wipe off sweat
  • 涙(なみだ)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to wipe away tears
  • 口(くち)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to wipe one's mouth

FIGURATIVE USAGE:

  • 不安(ふあん)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to dispel anxiety
  • 疑惑(ぎわく)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to clear away suspicions
  • 汚名(おめい)を拭(ぬぐ)う - to clear one's name

RELATED:

  • 拭(ふ)く - to wipe (more common, everyday)
  • 拭(ぬぐ)い去(さ)る - to wipe away completely

NOTE:
拭(ぬぐ)う is slightly more literary/formal than 拭(ふ)く.

Related Words

Synonym: 拭(ふ)く (to wipe (everyday))
