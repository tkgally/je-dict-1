削(そ)ぐ

そぐ
verb (godan)
to slice off; to shave; to diminish
1. to slice thin; to shave off; to chip
To cut something at an angle into thin slices or to shave off small amounts.
2. to diminish; to dampen; to weaken
Used figuratively to describe reducing or weakening something like motivation, energy, or interest.
鰹節(かつおぶし)を薄(うす)く削(そ)ぐ。
Shave the dried bonito into thin slices.
やる気(き)を削(そ)ぐような発言(はつげん)はやめてほしい。
I wish you'd stop making comments that dampen my motivation.
鶏肉(とりにく)を削(そ)ぎ切(ぎ)りにする。
Cut the chicken into thin diagonal slices.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
Pattern: Xを削(そ)ぐ

ASPECT (ている):
削(そ)いでいる indicates ongoing action (in the process of slicing/shaving).

COOKING CUTTING TECHNIQUE:
削(そ)ぎ切(ぎ)り ({sogigiri}) - diagonal slice cut, where the knife is held at an angle to create thin, wide slices. Used for chicken, fish, and vegetables.

COMMON FIGURATIVE USES:

  • やる気(き)を削(そ)ぐ - to dampen motivation
  • 気力(きりょく)を削(そ)ぐ - to sap energy
  • 興味(きょうみ)を削(そ)ぐ - to diminish interest
  • 勢(いきお)いを削(そ)ぐ - to weaken momentum

CONJUGATION:
て-form: 削(そ)いで (follows regular godan pattern with ぐ→いで)
