雑用(ざつよう)

ざつよう
noun
odd jobs; miscellaneous tasks
1. odd jobs; miscellaneous tasks; chores
various small tasks that need to be done but are not one's main duties
雑用(ざつよう)に追(お)われている。
I'm swamped with miscellaneous tasks.
新人(しんじん)は雑用(ざつよう)を任(まか)されることが多(おお)い。
Newcomers are often assigned odd jobs.
雑用(ざつよう)を片付(かたづ)けてから本業(ほんぎょう)に取(と)りかかる。
I'll finish the miscellaneous tasks before getting to my main work.

雑用(ざつよう) refers to various small tasks that are not one's primary responsibilities.

COMPOSITION:

  • 雑(ざつ): Miscellaneous, various
  • 用(よう): Task, errand

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雑用(ざつよう)に追(お)われる (to be swamped with odd jobs)
  • 雑用(ざつよう)を片付(かたづ)ける (to finish miscellaneous tasks)
  • 雑用(ざつよう)を任(まか)せる (to assign odd jobs)
  • 雑用(ざつよう)をこなす (to handle miscellaneous tasks)
  • 雑用(ざつよう)係(がかり) (person in charge of odd jobs)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Can carry a slightly negative nuance of unimportant tasks
  • Often contrasted with 本業(ほんぎょう) (main job) or 本来(ほんらい)の仕事(しごと) (actual duties)
  • New employees often start with 雑用(ざつよう)

SIMILAR TERMS:

  • 用事(ようじ) (errand, task - more general)
  • 雑事(ざつじ) (miscellaneous matters - slightly more formal)
  • 小仕事(こしごと) (small tasks)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in workplace contexts.
