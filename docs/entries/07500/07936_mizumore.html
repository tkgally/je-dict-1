水漏(みずも)れ

みずもれ
noun
water leak
1. water leak
water escaping from pipes, tanks, or other containers
水漏(みずも)れが起(お)きた。
A water leak occurred.
洗面台(せんめんだい)の下(した)で水漏(みずも)れを発見(はっけん)した。
I found a water leak under the sink.
水漏(みずも)れの修理(しゅうり)を業者(ぎょうしゃ)に依頼(いらい)した。
I asked a contractor to repair the water leak.

水漏(みずも)れ refers to water leaking from plumbing, appliances, or containers.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 水(みず) (water) + 漏(も)れ (leak, noun form of 漏(も)れる)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 水漏(みずも)れがする (there is a water leak)
  • 水漏(みずも)れが起(お)きる (a water leak occurs)
  • 水漏(みずも)れを直(なお)す (to fix a water leak)
  • 水漏(みずも)れを修理(しゅうり)する (to repair a water leak)
  • 水漏(みずも)れを防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent water leaks)

COMMON LOCATIONS:

  • 水道管(すいどうかん) (water pipes)
  • 蛇口(じゃぐち) (faucet)
  • 洗面台(せんめんだい) (sink)
  • 洗濯機(せんたくき) (washing machine)
  • トイレ (toilet)
  • 給湯器(きゅうとうき) (water heater)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 雨漏(あまも)り (roof leak)
  • 水道管(すいどうかん) (water pipe)
  • 漏電(ろうでん) (electrical leak)
  • パッキン (packing, washer - often cause of leaks)

URGENCY:

  • Often requires immediate attention
  • Can cause water damage (水濡(みずぬ)れ)
  • May affect neighbors in apartments

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • In Japanese apartments, tenants typically contact:
  • 管理会社(かんりがいしゃ) (management company)
  • 大家(おおや) (landlord)
  • Emergency contacts often provided for 水回(みずまわ)りトラブル (plumbing issues)

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in everyday conversation.
