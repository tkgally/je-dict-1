すり潰(つぶ)す

すりつぶす
godan verb, transitive
to grind; to mash; to crush by rubbing
1. to grind; to mash
To crush something into a paste or powder by rubbing or grinding.
胡麻(ごま)をすり潰(つぶ)す。
To grind sesame seeds.
茹(ゆ)でた芋(いも)をすり潰(つぶ)してマッシュポテトを作(つく)った。
I mashed the boiled potatoes to make mashed potatoes.
すり鉢(ばち)で味噌(みそ)をすり潰(つぶ)してから出汁(だし)に溶(と)かす。
Grind the miso in a mortar and then dissolve it in the broth.

CONSTRUCTION:
Compound verb from すり (rubbing, from 擦(す)る/磨(す)る) + 潰(つぶ)す (to crush). Describes crushing by grinding or rubbing action.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を for the object being ground.

TOOLS:

  • すり鉢(ばち) - mortar (Japanese style)
  • すりこぎ - pestle
  • フードプロセッサー - food processor

COMMON IN COOKING:

  • 胡麻(ごま)をすり潰(つぶ)す - to grind sesame
  • 豆(まめ)をすり潰(つぶ)す - to mash beans
  • 薬味(やくみ)をすり潰(つぶ)す - to grind condiments
