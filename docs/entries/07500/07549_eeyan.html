ええやん

ええやん
expression
it's good; that's fine (Kansai dialect)
1. it's good; that's fine; not bad
Kansai dialect expression of approval or acceptance, equivalent to いいじゃん in standard Japanese.
「これでええやん」
"This is fine."
その服(ふく)、ええやん！
Those clothes look good on you!
別(べつ)にええやん、気(き)にせんといて。
It's fine, don't worry about it.

DIALECT:
Kansai (関西(かんさい)) dialect, commonly used in Osaka, Kyoto, and surrounding areas.

STANDARD EQUIVALENT:
いいじゃん / いいじゃない (standard Japanese)

WORD STRUCTURE:

  • ええ = いい (good) in Kansai dialect
  • やん = じゃん (casual assertion particle)

VARIATIONS:

  • ええやんか - same meaning, slightly more emphatic
  • ええんちゃう？ - isn't it good? (seeking confirmation)
  • ええで - it's okay (giving permission)
  • ええわ - fine, whatever

USAGE:
Used to express approval, acceptance, or to reassure someone. Can be enthusiastic praise or casual dismissal depending on tone.

RELATED KANSAI EXPRESSIONS:

  • せや - that's right
  • あかん - no good
  • ほんま - really

Related Words

Antonym: あかん (no good (Kansai))
Related: せや (Kansai dialect)
