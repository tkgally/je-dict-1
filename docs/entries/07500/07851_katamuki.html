傾(かたむ)き

かたむき
noun
tilt
1. tilt
inclination; slope; slant; the state of being tilted or leaning
地面(じめん)の傾(かたむ)きを測(はか)る。
Measure the slope of the ground.
椅子(いす)の傾(かたむ)きを調整(ちょうせい)する。
Adjust the tilt of the chair.
建物(たてもの)に傾(かたむ)きがあるので修理(しゅうり)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
The building has a tilt, so repairs are needed.
2. tendency
a tendency or leaning toward something (figurative)
保守的(ほしゅてき)な傾(かたむ)きがある。
There's a conservative tendency.
最近(さいきん)は楽観的(らっかんてき)な傾(かたむ)きが強(つよ)い。
Lately there's been a strong optimistic tendency.
市場(しじょう)には上昇(じょうしょう)の傾(かたむ)きが見(み)られる。
An upward trend can be seen in the market.

傾(かたむ)き is the nominalized form of 傾(かたむ)く (to tilt, lean).

SENSE 1 - PHYSICAL TILT:

  • Describes the angle or slope of something
  • Used for buildings, ground, furniture, etc.

SENSE 2 - FIGURATIVE TENDENCY:

  • Describes a leaning or tendency toward something
  • Used for attitudes, trends, policies

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傾(かたむ)きを直(なお)す (to correct a tilt)
  • 傾(かたむ)きを測(はか)る (to measure the slope)
  • ～な傾(かたむ)きがある (to have a tendency toward ~)

MATHEMATICS/SCIENCE:

In mathematics, 傾(かたむ)き means "slope" (of a line or curve), as in:

  • 直線(ちょくせん)の傾(かたむ)き (slope of a line)
  • 傾(かたむ)きの角度(かくど) (angle of inclination)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 傾斜(けいしゃ): Inclination, slope (more formal/technical)
  • 勾配(こうばい): Gradient (technical term)
  • 傾向(けいこう): Tendency (for sense 2, more common)
