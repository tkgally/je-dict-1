届出(とどけで)

とどけで
noun
notification; report; registration
1. notification; report; official registration
A formal notice or report submitted to authorities or an organization, often required by law or regulations.
転居届出(てんきょとどけで)を市役所(しやくしょ)に提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I submitted a change of address notification to city hall.
届出(とどけで)が必要(ひつよう)な場合(ばあい)は早(はや)めに手続(てつづ)きしてください。
If notification is required, please complete the procedure early.
届出(とどけで)なしに営業(えいぎょう)することは違法(いほう)です。
Operating without registration is illegal.

WORD STRUCTURE:
届(とどけ) (notification) + 出(で) (submission)

COMMON TYPES OF 届出(とどけで):

  • 婚姻届出(こんいんとどけで) - marriage registration
  • 出生届出(しゅっせいとどけで) - birth registration
  • 転居届出(てんきょとどけで) - change of address notification
  • 死亡届出(しぼうとどけで) - death registration
  • 開業届出(かいぎょうとどけで) - business registration
  • 退職届出(たいしょくとどけで) - resignation notice

VARIATION:

  • 届(とどけ) - shorter form, used similarly
  • 届け出(とどけで) - alternative spelling

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 届出(とどけで)をする - to submit a notification
  • 届出(とどけで)を受(う)け付(つ)ける - to accept a registration
  • 届出(とどけで)書類(しょるい) - notification documents
  • 届出(とどけで)義務(ぎむ) - obligation to report

LEGAL CONTEXT:
Many 届出(とどけで) are legally required in Japan for life events, business activities, and regulatory compliance.
