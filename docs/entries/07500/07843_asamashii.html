浅(あさ)ましい

あさましい
adjective (i)
shameful
1. shameful
despicable; shameful; miserable; pathetically degraded
浅(あさ)ましい行為(こうい)だ。
That's a shameful act.
金(かね)に目(め)がくらむとは浅(あさ)ましい。
It's despicable to be blinded by money.
自分(じぶん)の利益(りえき)のために人(ひと)を騙(だま)すとは浅(あさ)ましい限(かぎ)りだ。
It's utterly despicable to deceive people for one's own gain.

浅(あさ)ましい is an i-adjective expressing strong moral disapproval or contempt.

USAGE:

  • Describes actions, behavior, or people that are morally degraded
  • Strong word - implies that something has fallen far below acceptable standards
  • Often used in criticism or self-reflection

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 浅(あさ)ましい限(かぎ)り (utterly despicable)
  • 浅(あさ)ましい姿(すがた) (shameful appearance/state)
  • 浅(あさ)ましい行為(こうい) (despicable act)

NUANCE:

  • Stronger than 恥(は)ずかしい (embarrassing)
  • Implies moral failing rather than just embarrassment
  • Can be used about oneself with self-deprecating nuance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 卑(いや)しい: Base, lowly, vulgar
  • 惨(みじ)め: Miserable, pitiful
  • 浅(あさ)ましい: Emphasizes moral degradation and contempt

REGISTER: Neutral to slightly literary. Strong word used for serious criticism.
