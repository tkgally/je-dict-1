謹(つつし)んで

つつしんで
adverb
respectfully; humbly; sincerely
1. respectfully; humbly; reverently
An adverb expressing respect and humility, used when making formal statements, offers, or condolences.
謹(つつし)んでお悔(く)やみ申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
Please accept my sincere condolences.
謹(つつし)んで新年(しんねん)のお慶(よろこ)びを申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
I respectfully extend New Year's greetings.
ご招待(しょうたい)を謹(つつし)んでお受(う)けいたします。
I humbly accept your invitation.

FORMALITY:
This is very formal language used in ceremonial contexts, written correspondence, and official announcements.

COMMON SET PHRASES:

  • 謹(つつし)んでお悔(く)やみ申(もう)し上(あ)げます - Please accept my condolences
  • 謹(つつし)んで新年(しんねん)のお祝(いわ)いを申(もう)し上(あ)げます - New Year's greeting
  • 謹(つつし)んでお礼(れい)申(もう)し上(あ)げます - I humbly express my gratitude
  • 謹(つつし)んでご報告(ほうこく)いたします - I respectfully report

ETYMOLOGY:
From the verb 謹(つつし)む (to be discreet, to be reverent) in て-form. The kanji 謹(きん) conveys respect and carefulness.
