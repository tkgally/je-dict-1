プラットホーム

ぷらっとほーむ
noun
platform (train station)
1. platform (train station)
The raised area at a train station where passengers board and alight from trains.
プラットホームで電車(でんしゃ)を待(ま)つ。
Wait for the train on the platform.
特急(とっきゅう)は3番(ばん)プラットホームから発車(はっしゃ)します。
The express train departs from platform 3.
プラットホームの端(はし)は危(あぶ)ないので、白線(はくせん)の内側(うちがわ)でお待(ま)ちください。
The edge of the platform is dangerous, so please wait inside the white line.

VARIANT:

Often abbreviated to ホーム in everyday speech. Both forms are common.

  • プラットホーム - full form (more formal)
  • ホーム - shortened form (casual, common in announcements)

NUMBERING:

Platforms are numbered in Japanese stations:

  • 1番(ばん)ホーム / 1番線(ばんせん) - Platform 1 / Track 1

ANNOUNCEMENTS:

  • 「3番線(ばんせん)に電車(でんしゃ)が参(まい)ります」- A train is arriving on track 3

NOTE:
The loanword comes from English "platform." In computing and business, プラットフォーム (with different vowel length) refers to software/business platforms.
