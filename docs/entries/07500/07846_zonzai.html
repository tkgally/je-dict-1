ぞんざい

ぞんざい
adjective (na)
careless
1. careless
rough; sloppy; careless in manner or treatment
ぞんざいな扱(あつか)いをする。
To treat carelessly.
仕事(しごと)をぞんざいにしてはいけない。
You shouldn't do your work sloppily.
道具(どうぐ)をぞんざいに扱(あつか)うと壊(こわ)れてしまう。
If you handle tools carelessly, they'll break.
2. rude
rude; impolite; treating others or things with disrespect
ぞんざいな言葉遣(ことばづか)いはやめなさい。
Stop speaking so rudely.
ぞんざいな態度(たいど)を取(と)るな。
Don't have such a rude attitude.
客(きゃく)にぞんざいな対応(たいおう)をして怒(おこ)らせた。
They angered the customer by treating them rudely.

ぞんざい is a na-adjective describing careless, rough, or rude behavior.

KANJI:

  • Rarely written as 粗雑(ぞんざい); hiragana is standard

SENSE 1 - CARELESS:

  • Describes sloppy handling of objects or tasks
  • Implies lack of proper care or attention

SENSE 2 - RUDE:

  • Describes disrespectful treatment of people
  • Includes rude speech, dismissive attitude, or impolite behavior

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぞんざいな扱(あつか)い (careless treatment)
  • ぞんざいな態度(たいど) (rude attitude)
  • ぞんざいな言葉遣(ことばづか)い (rude speech)
  • ぞんざいに扱(あつか)う (to handle carelessly/rudely)

GRAMMAR FORMS:

  • ぞんざいな + noun (ぞんざいな態度(たいど))
  • ぞんざいに + verb (ぞんざいに扱(あつか)う)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 雑(ざつ): Rough, crude (more general)
  • 乱暴(らんぼう): Rough, violent (more physical)
  • ぞんざい: Specifically careless/rude in handling or treatment

REGISTER: Neutral. Used in criticism or admonishment.
