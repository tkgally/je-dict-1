懲(こ)りる

こりる
verb
to learn one's lesson; to be taught a lesson
1. to learn one's lesson; to have had enough
To have such a bad experience that one decides not to repeat the same mistake or behavior.
失敗(しっぱい)に懲(こ)りて慎重(しんちょう)になった。
I became cautious after learning my lesson from failure.
彼(かれ)は何度(なんど)失敗(しっぱい)しても懲(こ)りない。
He never learns his lesson no matter how many times he fails.
二日酔(ふつかよ)いに懲(こ)りて、飲(の)み過(す)ぎないようにしている。
After learning my lesson from hangovers, I try not to drink too much.

VERB TYPE:
自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive verb)

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)/上一段(かみいちだん))

  • 懲(こ)りない (negative)
  • 懲(こ)りて (te-form)
  • 懲(こ)りた (past)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に懲(こ)りる - to learn one's lesson from ~
  • 懲(こ)りずに - without learning one's lesson
  • 懲(こ)り懲(ご)りする - to have absolutely had enough (emphatic)

IDIOMATIC EXPRESSIONS:

  • 懲(こ)りない人(ひと) - someone who never learns
  • 懲(こ)り懲(ご)りだ - I've had more than enough

USAGE NOTES:
Often used in the negative form 懲(こ)りない to describe someone who keeps making the same mistakes. The phrase 懲(こ)り懲(ご)り is an emphatic form expressing complete exasperation.
