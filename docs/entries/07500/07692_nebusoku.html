寝不足(ねぶそく)

ねぶそく
noun, na-adjective
lack of sleep; sleep deprivation
1. lack of sleep; sleep deprivation; not getting enough sleep
The state of having slept less than needed, often resulting in tiredness.
寝不足(ねぶそく)で頭(あたま)がぼーっとする。
My head is foggy from lack of sleep.
最近(さいきん)寝不足(ねぶそく)が続(つづ)いている。
I've been sleep-deprived lately.
寝不足(ねぶそく)は健康(けんこう)に良(よ)くない。
Lack of sleep is not good for your health.

ETYMOLOGY:
寝(ね) (sleep) + 不足(ふそく) (insufficiency, shortage)

RELATED SLEEP VOCABULARY:

  • 夜更(よふ)かし - staying up late
  • 二度寝(にどね) - going back to sleep
  • 睡眠不足(すいみんぶそく) - sleep deprivation (more formal)
  • 寝坊(ねぼう) - oversleeping

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 寝不足(ねぶそく)で〜 - due to lack of sleep
  • 寝不足(ねぶそく)が続(つづ)く - lack of sleep continues
  • 寝不足(ねぶそく)を解消(かいしょう)する - to catch up on sleep
