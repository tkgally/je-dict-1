問屋(とんや)

とんや
noun
wholesaler; middleman
1. wholesaler; middleman
a business that buys in bulk from manufacturers and sells to retailers
問屋(とんや)から仕入(しい)れる。
To purchase from a wholesaler.
そうは問屋(とんや)が卸(おろ)さない。
It's not that simple. (Proverb: The wholesaler won't sell that easily)
Common proverb meaning things won't go as easily as expected
問屋(とんや)街(がい)で買(か)い物(もの)をした。
I went shopping in the wholesale district.

問屋(とんや) is a traditional term for a wholesale business, still commonly used today.

READING NOTE:

  • Read as とんや, not といや
  • This is an irregular reading

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 問(とん) (question - here used phonetically)
  • 屋(や) (shop, store)

BUSINESS ROLE:

  • Buys large quantities from manufacturers
  • Sells smaller lots to retailers
  • Acts as distributor/middleman

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問屋(とんや)から仕入(しい)れる (to buy from a wholesaler)
  • 問屋(とんや)街(がい) (wholesale district)
  • 卸(おろし)問屋(とんや) (wholesale store)
  • 食品(しょくひん)問屋(とんや) (food wholesaler)

FAMOUS PROVERB:

  • そうは問屋(とんや)が卸(おろ)さない
  • Literal: "The wholesaler won't sell it that way"
  • Meaning: "Things won't go that easily" / "That's wishful thinking"
  • Very commonly used expression

FAMOUS WHOLESALE DISTRICTS:

  • 合羽橋(かっぱばし) (Tokyo - kitchenware)
  • 築地(つきじ) (Tokyo - fish)
  • 船場(せんば) (Osaka - textiles)

MODERN USAGE:

  • Traditional 問屋(とんや) system still important
  • Competing with direct manufacturer sales
  • Online wholesale also emerging

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in business and everyday conversation.
