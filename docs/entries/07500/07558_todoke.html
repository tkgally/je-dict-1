届(とど)け

とどけ
noun
notification; report; delivery
1. notification; report; registration form
A formal notice or document submitted to authorities or organizations.
退職届(たいしょくとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I submitted my resignation notice.
婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)を出(だ)す。
Submit a marriage registration.
2. delivery; reaching
The act of delivering something to its destination or the state of having reached someone.
あなたの気持(きも)ちは届(とど)けで伝(つた)わりました。
Your feelings reached me.

COMMON DOCUMENT TYPES:

  • 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ) - marriage registration
  • 出生届(しゅっせいとどけ) - birth registration
  • 死亡届(しぼうとどけ) - death registration
  • 退職届(たいしょくとどけ) - resignation notice
  • 転居届(てんきょとどけ) - change of address notification
  • 欠席届(けっせきとどけ) - absence notification
  • 不在届(ふざいとどけ) - notice of absence

RELATED FORMS:

  • 届出(とどけで) - notification, registration (more formal)
  • 届(とど)ける - to deliver, to report (verb)
  • 届(とど)く - to reach, to arrive (intransitive verb)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 届(とど)けを出(だ)す - to submit a notification
  • 届(とど)けを受(う)け取(と)る - to receive a report

NOTE:
When used as a suffix (婚姻届(こんいんとどけ), etc.), it refers to official documents. The standalone use for "delivery" (sense 2) is less common in modern usage.
