棚卸(たなおろ)し

たなおろし
noun
inventory; stocktaking
1. inventory; stocktaking
counting and recording all goods in stock
月末(げつまつ)に棚卸(たなおろ)しをする。
We do inventory at the end of the month.
棚卸(たなおろ)しのため臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)します。
We'll be temporarily closed for stocktaking.
年(ねん)に一度(いちど)、大(おお)きな棚卸(たなおろ)しがある。
There's a major inventory count once a year.
棚卸(たなおろ)し作業(さぎょう)は深夜(しんや)に行(おこな)われた。
The inventory work was done late at night.
2. critical review; scrutiny
examining something thoroughly (figurative)
自分(じぶん)の人生(じんせい)を棚卸(たなおろ)しする。
To take stock of one's life.
スキルの棚卸(たなおろ)しをして転職(てんしょく)に備(そな)える。
I'm taking inventory of my skills to prepare for a job change.

棚卸(たなおろ)し means counting inventory, and is also used figuratively for self-review.

WORD TYPE:

  • Noun
  • Also used as する verb: 棚卸(たなおろ)しする

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 棚(たな) (shelf) + 卸(おろ)し (taking down)
  • Literally: taking items down from shelves to count

SENSE 1 (INVENTORY):

  • Physical counting of stock
  • Usually done periodically (monthly, yearly)
  • Stores often close for this

SENSE 2 (FIGURATIVE):

  • Self-reflection or review
  • Common in career/life planning
  • "Taking stock of one's life"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 棚卸(たなおろ)しをする (to do inventory)
  • 棚卸(たなおろ)し作業(さぎょう) (inventory work)
  • 決算(けっさん)棚卸(たなおろ)し (fiscal year-end inventory)
  • スキルの棚卸(たなおろ)し (skill inventory)
  • 自己(じこ)棚卸(たなおろ)し (self-assessment)

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

  • Required for accounting
  • Often done at fiscal year-end
  • May require temporary store closure
  • 棚卸(たなおろ)しセール (inventory clearance sale) often follows

FIGURATIVE USE (JOB HUNTING):

  • 棚卸(たなおろ)し is common advice for career changers
  • List all skills, experiences, accomplishments
  • Popular self-help concept

REGISTER: Business/neutral. Common in retail and self-help contexts.
