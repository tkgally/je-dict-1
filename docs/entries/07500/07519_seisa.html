精査(せいさ)

せいさ
noun, verb (suru)
close examination; careful scrutiny; thorough inspection
1. close examination; careful scrutiny; thorough inspection
Examining something in detail and with great care to verify accuracy or quality.
書類(しょるい)を精査(せいさ)してから判断(はんだん)する。
I will examine the documents carefully before making a decision.
データ(でーた)の精査(せいさ)に時間(じかん)がかかる。
It takes time to carefully examine the data.
予算案(よさんあん)の精査(せいさ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A thorough examination of the budget proposal is necessary.

WORD STRUCTURE:
精(せい) (detailed, precise) + 査(さ) (examine, investigate)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精査(せいさ)を行(おこな)う - to conduct a close examination
  • 精査(せいさ)が必要(ひつよう) - thorough examination is needed
  • 精査(せいさ)の結果(けっか) - as a result of close examination
  • 精査(せいさ)中(ちゅう) - under examination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 検討(けんとう) - consideration, review (more general)
  • 調査(ちょうさ) - investigation, survey (broader scope)
  • 吟味(ぎんみ) - scrutiny, careful selection
  • 審査(しんさ) - examination, screening (often for approval)

REGISTER:
Formal. Common in business, academic, and government contexts.
