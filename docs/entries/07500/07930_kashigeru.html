傾(かし)げる

かしげる
ichidan verb
to tilt; to incline; to cock (one's head)
1. to tilt; to incline
to lean or slant something to one side
首(くび)を傾(かし)げる。
To tilt one's head.
彼女(かのじょ)は不思議(ふしぎ)そうに首(くび)を傾(かし)げた。
She tilted her head, looking puzzled.
犬(いぬ)が首(くび)を傾(かし)げて見(み)ている。
The dog is looking with its head tilted.

傾(かし)げる most commonly appears in the expression 首(くび)を傾(かし)げる (tilt one's head).

VERB TYPE:

  • Ichidan verb (る-verb)
  • Transitive

KANJI NOTE:

  • Uses 傾(かし)げる from 傾(かたむ)く (to lean)
  • Alternative reading: 傾(かた)げる (same meaning, less common)

KEY EXPRESSION:

  • 首(くび)を傾(かし)げる is by far the most common usage
  • Indicates puzzlement, confusion, or curiosity
  • Can describe humans or animals

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 首(くび)を傾(かし)げる (tilt one's head - questioning gesture)
  • 不思議(ふしぎ)そうに首(くび)を傾(かし)げる (tilt head as if puzzled)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)を感(かん)じて首(くび)を傾(かし)げる (tilt head in doubt)

NUANCE:

  • 首(くび)を傾(かし)げる typically implies doubt or confusion
  • Shows the person doesn't understand or questions something
  • Very visual, often used in descriptions

RELATED VERBS:

  • 傾(かたむ)ける (to tilt, lean - more general)
  • 傾(かたむ)く (to lean, incline - intransitive)

DIFFERENCE FROM 傾(かたむ)ける:

  • 傾(かし)げる: Usually for 首(くび) (head), expressive gesture
  • 傾(かたむ)ける: More general tilting of objects

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in both speech and writing.
