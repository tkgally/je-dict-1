作業場(さぎょうば)

さぎょうば
noun
workplace; workshop; worksite
1. workplace; workshop; worksite
a place where work or labor is performed, especially manual or industrial work
作業場(さぎょうば)で事故(じこ)があった。
There was an accident at the worksite.
職人(しょくにん)たちが作業場(さぎょうば)で働(はたら)いている。
The craftsmen are working in the workshop.
自宅(じたく)の庭(にわ)に小(ちい)さな作業場(さぎょうば)を作(つく)った。
I built a small workshop in my garden.

作業場(さぎょうば) refers to a place where work, especially manual or industrial work, is done.

COMPOSITION:

  • 作業(さぎょう): Work, operation
  • 場(ば): Place

ALTERNATIVE READING:
Also read as さぎょうじょう, with the same meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 作業場(さぎょうば)で働(はたら)く (to work at a workplace)
  • 作業場(さぎょうば)の安全(あんぜん) (workplace safety)
  • 工場(こうじょう)の作業場(さぎょうば) (factory workshop floor)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 工場(こうじょう) (factory)
  • 工房(こうぼう) (workshop, studio - often for crafts)
  • 現場(げんば) (worksite, scene)
  • 職場(しょくば) (workplace - general)
  • アトリエ (atelier, artist's studio)

NUANCE:

  • More specifically refers to the physical space where labor occurs
  • Often associated with manufacturing, construction, or crafts
  • Less formal than 職場(しょくば) which refers to workplace in general

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in workplace and industrial contexts.
