日給(にっきゅう)

にっきゅう
noun
daily wage; daily pay
1. daily wage; daily pay
payment calculated and typically paid on a per-day basis
日給(にっきゅう)は1万(まん)円(えん)です。
The daily wage is 10,000 yen.
このアルバイトは日給(にっきゅう)制(せい)ですか。
Is this part-time job paid on a daily basis?
日給(にっきゅう)で働(はたら)いているので、休(やす)むと収入(しゅうにゅう)が減(へ)る。
Since I'm paid daily, my income decreases when I take days off.

日給(にっきゅう) refers to wages paid on a daily basis, common for part-time or temporary work.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 日(にち) (day)
  • 給(きゅう) (salary, wage)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日給(にっきゅう)制(せい) (daily wage system)
  • 日給(にっきゅう)月給(げっきゅう)制(せい) (daily rate paid monthly)
  • 日給(にっきゅう)で払(はら)う (to pay daily)

RELATED WAGE TYPES:

  • 時給(じきゅう) (hourly wage)
  • 月給(げっきゅう) (monthly salary)
  • 年俸(ねんぽう) (annual salary)
  • 週給(しゅうきゅう) (weekly wage)

USAGE CONTEXT:

  • Common for construction workers, event staff, temporary jobs
  • Often seen in job listings as "日給(にっきゅう)○○円(えん)"
