朝靄(あさもや)

あさもや
noun
morning mist; morning haze
1. morning mist; morning haze
thin fog or mist that appears in the early morning, often creating a dreamy or atmospheric scene
朝靄(あさもや)が立(た)ち込(こ)めている。
The morning mist is settling in.
朝靄(あさもや)の中(なか)を散歩(さんぽ)した。
I took a walk through the morning mist.
山(やま)から昇(のぼ)る朝日(あさひ)が朝靄(あさもや)を照(て)らして幻想的(げんそうてき)だった。
The morning sun rising from the mountain illuminated the mist, creating a fantastic scene.

朝靄(あさもや) is a poetic word for morning mist, often used in literary or atmospheric descriptions.

COMPOSITION:

  • 朝(あさ): Morning
  • 靄(もや): Mist, haze

RELATED WEATHER TERMS:

  • 霧(きり) (fog - thicker than 靄(もや))
  • 靄(もや) (mist, haze - thinner than fog)
  • 霞(かすみ) (haze - often spring-related)
  • 朝霧(あさぎり) (morning fog - denser than 朝靄(あさもや))

LITERARY USAGE:

朝靄(あさもや) evokes a sense of quiet beauty and is commonly used in:

  • Nature descriptions
  • Poetry and prose
  • Travel writing about rural Japan

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 朝靄(あさもや)が立(た)つ (morning mist rises)
  • 朝靄(あさもや)が晴(は)れる (morning mist clears)
  • 朝靄(あさもや)に包(つつ)まれる (to be enveloped in morning mist)

REGISTER: Literary/poetic. Used in descriptive or artistic contexts.
