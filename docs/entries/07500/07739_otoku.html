お得(とく)

おとく
noun, na-adjective
good deal; bargain; economical
1. good deal; bargain; economical
Something that offers good value for money, or the state of getting more than expected for what you pay.
このセットはお得(とく)だ。
This set is a good deal.
まとめて買(か)うとお得(とく)になる。
It becomes more economical if you buy in bulk.
お得(とく)な情報(じょうほう)を教(おし)えてもらった。
I was told about a good deal.

ETYMOLOGY:
お (polite prefix) + 得(とく) (profit, benefit) - polite form of 得(とく).

USAGE:

Very common in:

  • Advertising and promotions
  • Shopping contexts
  • Discussing value/prices

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • お得(とく)情報(じょうほう) - bargain information
  • お得(とく)感(かん) - feeling of getting a good deal
  • お得(とく)価格(かかく) - bargain price
  • お得(とく)セット - value set

COLLOCATIONS:

  • お得(とく)に買(か)う - to buy at a good price
  • お得(とく)な商品(しょうひん) - bargain product
