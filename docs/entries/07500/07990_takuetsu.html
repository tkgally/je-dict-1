卓越(たくえつ)

たくえつ
noun
excellence; preeminence; superiority
1. excellence; preeminence; superiority
being distinctly superior to others in ability, quality, or achievement
卓越(たくえつ)した技術(ぎじゅつ)を持(も)つ。
To possess excellent skills.
彼女(かのじょ)は卓越(たくえつ)したリーダーシップを発揮(はっき)した。
She demonstrated excellent leadership.
この作品(さくひん)は技術(ぎじゅつ)の卓越(たくえつ)さで知(し)られている。
This work is known for its technical excellence.
卓越(たくえつ)した業績(ぎょうせき)により表彰(ひょうしょう)された。
She was honored for her outstanding achievements.

卓越(たくえつ) refers to being distinctly superior or excellent, often used to describe exceptional abilities, achievements, or qualities.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 卓(たく) (table; eminent, outstanding)
  • 越(えつ) (exceed, surpass)

USAGE:

  • 卓越(たくえつ)する (to excel, to be preeminent - suru verb)
  • 卓越(たくえつ)した〜 (excellent ~, outstanding ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 卓越(たくえつ)した技術(ぎじゅつ) (excellent technique/skill)
  • 卓越(たくえつ)した能力(のうりょく) (outstanding ability)
  • 卓越(たくえつ)した業績(ぎょうせき) (outstanding achievements)
  • 卓越(たくえつ)した見識(けんしき) (superior insight)

REGISTER:

  • Formal/written style
  • Common in academic, business, and award contexts
  • Often used in recommendations and evaluations

RELATED TERMS:

  • 傑出(けっしゅつ) (outstanding, distinguished)
  • 秀逸(しゅういつ) (excellent, superb)
  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう) (excellent, superior)
  • 抜群(ばつぐん) (outstanding, preeminent)
