売値(うりね)

うりね
noun
selling price; sale price
1. selling price; sale price
the price at which something is sold to customers
売値(うりね)は千(せん)円(えん)です。
The selling price is 1,000 yen.
仕入(しい)れ値(ね)と売値(うりね)の差(さ)が利益(りえき)になる。
The difference between the purchase price and selling price becomes profit.
この商品(しょうひん)の売値(うりね)を下(さ)げないと売(う)れないかもしれない。
We might not sell this product unless we lower the selling price.

売値(うりね) is a business term for the selling price or retail price of a product.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 売(う)り (selling)
  • 値(ね) (price, value)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売値(うりね)を付(つ)ける (to set a selling price)
  • 売値(うりね)を下(さ)げる (to lower the selling price)
  • 売値(うりね)を決(き)める (to determine the selling price)

ANTONYM:

  • 買値(かいね) (purchase price, buying price)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 仕入(しい)れ値(ね) (wholesale/purchase price)
  • 定価(ていか) (list price)
  • 原価(げんか) (cost price)
  • 小売(こうり)価格(かかく) (retail price - more formal)
