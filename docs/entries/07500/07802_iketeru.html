イケてる

いけてる
expression
cool, stylish
1. cool, stylish
cool; stylish; attractive; trendy
あの人(ひと)、イケてるね。
That person is cool, isn't he?
その髪型(かみがた)、すごくイケてる。
That hairstyle is really stylish.
イケてる服(ふく)を着(き)て出(で)かけたい。
I want to go out wearing stylish clothes.

イケてる is a colloquial expression meaning cool or stylish, derived from the verb 行(い)ける/イケる (the potential form of 行(い)く) with the ている ending contracted. The original sense of "being able to go" evolved into meaning "acceptable" → "good" → "cool/stylish."

ORIGIN:

  • From イケている → イケてる (contracted form)
  • Part of 1990s-2000s youth slang that became mainstream

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • As predicate: イケてるね/イケてるよ
  • As modifier: イケてる + noun (イケてる男(おとこ), イケてる服(ふく))
  • Often used to describe people, fashion, or lifestyle

RELATED WORDS:

  • イケメン (good-looking man) - derived from イケてる + 面(めん)
  • イケてない (not cool, uncool) - negative form
  • ダサい (uncool, lame) - opposite meaning

REGISTER: Casual/colloquial. Common in spoken Japanese among younger generations.
