吟味(ぎんみ)

ぎんみ
noun, verb (suru)
scrutiny; careful examination; careful selection
1. careful examination; scrutiny; close inspection
Carefully examining or investigating something to determine its quality, authenticity, or suitability.
契約(けいやく)内容(ないよう)を吟味(ぎんみ)する。
Scrutinize the contract details.
2. careful selection; choosing with care
Selecting items carefully by examining their quality, often used for ingredients or materials.
素材(そざい)を吟味(ぎんみ)して選(えら)ぶ。
Carefully select and choose the materials.
吟味(ぎんみ)された食材(しょくざい)のみを使用(しよう)しています。
We use only carefully selected ingredients.

WORD STRUCTURE:
吟(ぎん) (to recite, examine) + 味(み) (taste, examine)
Originally meant to taste and assess poetry or literature.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 吟味(ぎんみ)を重(かさ)ねる - to examine repeatedly
  • 吟味(ぎんみ)に吟味(ぎんみ)を重(かさ)ねる - to scrutinize with extreme care
  • 厳選(げんせん)吟味(ぎんみ) - strict selection and examination

USAGE CONTEXTS:

  • 食材(しょくざい)の吟味(ぎんみ) - careful selection of ingredients
  • 言葉(ことば)の吟味(ぎんみ) - careful choice of words
  • 証拠(しょうこ)の吟味(ぎんみ) - examination of evidence

FORMALITY:
Slightly formal. Often used in business, legal, and culinary contexts. 吟味(ぎんみ)された implies high quality and careful selection.
