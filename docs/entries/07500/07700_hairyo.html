配慮(はいりょ)

はいりょ
noun, suru-verb
consideration; thoughtfulness; care
1. consideration; thoughtfulness; care
The act of being mindful of others' feelings, circumstances, or needs and acting accordingly.
周(まわ)りへの配慮(はいりょ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Consideration for others is important.
ご配慮(はいりょ)いただきありがとうございます。
Thank you for your kind consideration.
環境(かんきょう)に配慮(はいりょ)した製品(せいひん)を作(つく)る。
We make environmentally conscious products.

ETYMOLOGY:
配(はい) (distribute, arrange) + 慮(りょ) (consideration, thought)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 〜に配慮(はいりょ)する - to be considerate of ~
  • 〜への配慮(はいりょ) - consideration for ~
  • ご配慮(はいりょ) - (your) consideration (honorific)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)への配慮(はいりょ) - environmental consideration
  • 安全(あんぜん)への配慮(はいりょ) - safety considerations
  • 配慮(はいりょ)に欠(か)ける - to lack consideration

RELATED:

  • 思(おも)いやり - compassion, thoughtfulness
  • 気遣(きづか)い - concern, solicitude
  • 心遣(こころづか)い - thoughtfulness
