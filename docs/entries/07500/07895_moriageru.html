盛(も)り上(あ)げる

もりあげる
ichidan verb
to liven up; to enliven
1. to liven up; to enliven; to boost
to make an event, atmosphere, or mood more exciting and energetic
パーティーを盛(も)り上(あ)げよう!
Let's liven up the party!
DJが会場(かいじょう)を盛(も)り上(あ)げた。
The DJ got the venue pumped up.
彼(かれ)は場(ば)を盛(も)り上(あ)げるのが上手(うま)い。
He's good at livening up the atmosphere.
みんなで協力(きょうりょく)してイベントを盛(も)り上(あ)げた。
Everyone worked together to make the event a success.
2. to pile up; to heap up
to physically pile something higher
ご飯(はん)を山盛(やまも)りに盛(も)り上(あ)げた。
I heaped the rice into a mound.
土(つち)を盛(も)り上(あ)げて花壇(かだん)を作(つく)った。
I piled up soil to make a flower bed.

盛(も)り上(あ)げる is a compound verb combining 盛(も)る (to heap, serve) + 上(あ)げる (to raise).

VERB TYPE:

  • Ichidan verb (る-verb)
  • Transitive
  • Intransitive pair: 盛(も)り上(あ)がる (to get excited, be in full swing)

SENSE 1 VS SENSE 2:

  • Sense 1 (more common): To make something exciting, energetic - used with events, atmosphere
  • Sense 2: Physical piling up of objects

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 場(ば)を盛(も)り上(あ)げる (to liven up the atmosphere)
  • パーティーを盛(も)り上(あ)げる (to liven up a party)
  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)を盛(も)り上(あ)げる (to create an exciting atmosphere)
  • イベントを盛(も)り上(あ)げる (to make an event exciting)

INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 盛(も)り上(あ)がる: The party/atmosphere itself becomes excited
  • 盛(も)り上(あ)げる: Someone actively makes it exciting

RELATED TERMS:

  • 盛(も)り上(あ)がる (intransitive: to get excited)
  • 活気(かっき)づける (to invigorate)
  • 高(たか)める (to heighten)

REGISTER: Neutral. Very common in casual conversation.
