検診(けんしん)

けんしん
noun
medical checkup; health screening; examination
1. medical checkup; health screening; examination
A medical examination to check for diseases or health conditions, often as a preventive measure.
健康(けんこう)検診(けんしん)を受(う)ける。
To have a health checkup.
年(ねん)に一度(いちど)のがん検診(けんしん)は大切(たいせつ)だ。
An annual cancer screening is important.
会社(かいしゃ)の検診(けんしん)で異常(いじょう)が見(み)つかった。
An abnormality was found during the company health screening.

VERB FORM:
検診(けんしん)する - to examine, screen (medical)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 健康(けんこう)検診(けんしん) - health checkup
  • がん検診(けんしん) - cancer screening
  • 歯科(しか)検診(けんしん) - dental checkup
  • 乳(にゅう)がん検診(けんしん) - breast cancer screening
  • 検診(けんしん)を受(う)ける - to have a checkup
  • 検診(けんしん)の結果(けっか) - checkup results

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 健康診断(けんこうしんだん) (健診(けんしん)) - health examination (broader)
  • 人間ドック(にんげんドック) - comprehensive medical examination
