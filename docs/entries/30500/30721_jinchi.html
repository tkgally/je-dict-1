陣地(じんち)

じんち
noun
military position; encampment; entrenchment
1. military position; encampment; fortified position
Ground that an army occupies and defends, together with the trenches, emplacements, and camp built on it. In everyday speech it is also used jokingly for territory someone has staked out.
陣地(じんち)を築(きず)いた。
They built a fortified position.
敵(てき)の陣地(じんち)に近(ちか)づくのは危険(きけん)だ。
It is dangerous to approach the enemy's position.
弟(おとうと)はソファの隅(すみ)をすっかり自分(じぶん)の陣地(じんち)にしてしまった。
My little brother has made the corner of the sofa completely his own territory.

From 陣(じん) (military camp, battle formation) plus 地(ち) (ground). The literal military sense is the core one; the playful "my patch" use is common in family and school talk.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 陣地(じんち)を築(きず)く: to build a position
  • 陣地(じんち)を守(まも)る: to hold a position
  • 敵(てき)の陣地(じんち): the enemy position
  • 陣地(じんち)取(と)り: capturing ground — also the name of children's territory games

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 基地(きち): base — a permanent installation, not a battlefield position
  • 拠点(きょてん): base of operations, foothold — used far more often of businesses and projects than of armies
