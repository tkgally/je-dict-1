注意事項(ちゅういじこう)

ちゅういじこう
noun
points to note; precautions; things to be aware of
1. points to note; precautions; items requiring attention
The set of things a reader or participant must keep in mind: warnings, conditions, and rules listed on a form, a manual, or an announcement. Nearly always plural in sense and presented as a list.
注意事項(ちゅういじこう)をお読(よ)みください。
Please read the points to note.
申込書(もうしこみしょ)の裏面(りめん)に注意事項(ちゅういじこう)が書(か)いてある。
The precautions are written on the back of the application form.
出発前(しゅっぱつまえ)に注意事項(ちゅういじこう)を確認(かくにん)しておかないと、現地(げんち)で困(こま)ることがある。
If you don't check the guidance points before departure, you may run into trouble on site.

Formed from 注意(ちゅうい) (caution, attention) plus 事項(じこう) (items, matters). Standard on forms, tickets, manuals, and event announcements; the tone is administrative rather than urgent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注意事項(ちゅういじこう)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the points to note
  • 注意事項(ちゅういじこう)をよく読(よ)む: to read the precautions carefully
  • 注意事項(ちゅういじこう)を守(まも)る: to observe the stated rules
  • 以下(いか)の注意事項(ちゅういじこう): the following points

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注意書(ちゅういが)き: a written warning — the printed text itself, often a single line
  • 警告(けいこく): warning — far stronger, used where there is real danger
