1. to change; to alter; to modify
To replace something already decided or registered — a plan, a date, a setting, an address — with something different. The thing changed is deliberate and on the record, which is what separates 変更(へんこう)する from ordinary change verbs.
予定(よてい)を変更(へんこう)します。
I will change the schedule.
住所(じゅうしょ)を変更(へんこう)する手続(てつづ)きをした。
I went through the procedure for changing my address.
天候(てんこう)が悪(わる)いため、出発(しゅっぱつ)時間(じかん)を変更(へんこう)させていただきます。
Due to the bad weather, we will be changing the departure time.
パスワードは三(さん)か月(げつ)ごとに変更(へんこう)することをおすすめします。
We recommend changing your password every three months.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — the thing being changed takes を. The passive 変更(へんこう)される is very common in announcements, where the decider is an organization: 会場(かいじょう)が変更(へんこう)されました (the venue has been changed).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予定(よてい)を変更(へんこう)する: to change one's plans
  • 日時(にちじ)を変更(へんこう)する: to change the date and time
  • 設定(せってい)を変更(へんこう)する: to change a setting
  • 計画(けいかく)を変更(へんこう)する: to revise a plan
  • 仕様(しよう)変更(へんこう): a change of specifications

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 変(か)える: to change — the everyday word, and the one to use for informal or physical changes
  • 改(あらた)める: to revise; to correct — implies improving or putting right, not merely substituting
  • 変(か)わる: to change (by itself) — intransitive; nothing is being changed by an agent
