天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)

てんかわけめ
noun
decisive battle; make-or-break moment
1. decisive battle; make-or-break moment
A contest whose outcome settles everything, leaving no second chance for the loser. Literally 'the dividing point of the realm', from the historical struggles that decided who would rule Japan. Used with の before the noun it modifies.
天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の戦(たたか)いだ。
It is a decisive battle.
明日(あす)の試合(しあい)が天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)になる。
Tomorrow's match will be the make-or-break one.
関(せき)ヶ原(はら)は天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の戦(たたか)いとして知(し)られている。
Sekigahara is known as the battle that decided the fate of the country.
この契約(けいやく)が取(と)れるかどうかは、我(わ)が社(しゃ)にとって天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の勝負(しょうぶ)だ。
Whether we land this contract is a make-or-break fight for our company.

Nearly always used attributively with の, above all in the set phrase 天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の戦(たたか)い. The historical reference point is the Battle of Sekigahara (1600), which decided who would rule Japan; modern uses transfer that weight to elections, sports finals, and business deals, often with a touch of deliberate grandiosity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の戦(たたか)い: the decisive battle
  • 天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の一戦(いっせん): the deciding match
  • 天下分(てんかわ)け目(め)の勝負(しょうぶ): a make-or-break contest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 決戦(けっせん): decisive battle — plainer and more neutral, no historical flavor
  • 正念場(しょうねんば): crucial moment — a testing phase one must get through, not a contest between two sides
