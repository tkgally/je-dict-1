ずけずけ

ずけずけ
adverb
bluntly; without mincing words
1. bluntly; frankly; without holding back
Describes saying things straight out with no regard for how they will land. Usually critical of the speaker's tactlessness, though it can be said admiringly of someone brave enough to speak up.
彼女(かのじょ)はずけずけと物(もの)を言(い)う。
She says things very bluntly.
初対面(しょたいめん)なのにずけずけと年齢(ねんれい)を聞(き)かれた。
Even though we had just met, I was bluntly asked my age.
上司(じょうし)にもずけずけと意見(いけん)を言(い)えるところが、彼(かれ)のいいところでもある。
Being able to speak his mind bluntly even to his boss is also one of his good points.

GRAMMAR:

  • Used as ずけずけ(と) before a verb of speaking; the と is optional but common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ずけずけ言(い)う: to speak bluntly
  • ずけずけと物(もの)を言(い)う: to say things without mincing words
  • ずけずけと聞(き)く: to ask something point-blank

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • はっきり: clearly — states things plainly, with no suggestion of rudeness
  • 率直(そっちょく)に: frankly — a positive word for the same directness

ずけずけ is nearly always about what someone else does; describing your own speech that way sounds boastful or self-deprecating rather than neutral.
