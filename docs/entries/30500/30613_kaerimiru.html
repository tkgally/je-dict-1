1. to look back on; to review the past
To turn one's attention to what has already happened — a career, a period of history, a year now ending. Literary in tone.
半生(はんせい)を顧(かえり)みる。
To look back on half a lifetime.
一年(いちねん)を顧(かえり)みて反省(はんせい)する。
Looking back on the year, I take stock of it.
歴史(れきし)を顧(かえり)みれば、同(おな)じ過(あやま)ちが繰(く)り返(かえ)されてきたことが分(わ)かる。
Looking back over history, one sees that the same mistakes have been repeated.
2. to give thought to; to concern oneself with
To pay heed to something that has a claim on one's attention. Most often met in the negative, describing someone who neglects family, safety, or their own health.
家庭(かてい)を顧(かえり)みない父(ちち)だった。
He was a father who paid no attention to his family.
危険(きけん)を顧(かえり)みずに飛(と)び込(こ)んだ。
He jumped in without regard for the danger.
自分(じぶん)の健康(けんこう)を顧(かえり)みることなく働(はたら)き続(つづ)けた結果(けっか)、入院(にゅういん)してしまった。
He kept working without any regard for his own health, and ended up in the hospital.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)); the object takes を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 過去(かこ)を顧(かえり)みる: to look back on the past
  • 家庭(かてい)を顧(かえり)みない: to neglect one's family
  • 危険(きけん)を顧(かえり)みず: heedless of danger
  • 顧(かえり)みて恥(は)じるところがない: to have nothing to be ashamed of on reflection

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 省(かえり)みる: to reflect on oneself — same reading, different kanji; it is self-examination, whereas 顧(かえり)みる looks outward at the past or at one's obligations
  • 振(ふ)り返(かえ)る: to look back — the everyday word for sense 1, with no literary flavor

USAGE:
Sense 2 is far more common in the negative (顧(かえり)みない, 顧(かえり)みず) than in the positive.
