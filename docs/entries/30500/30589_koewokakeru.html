声(こえ)をかける

こえをかける
expression
to call out to someone; to speak to; to invite along
1. to call out to someone; to speak to someone first
To address someone who was not already talking with you — to get their attention, greet them, or offer help. The person is marked with に.
困(こま)っている人(ひと)に声(こえ)をかけた。
I spoke to someone who seemed to be in trouble.
知(し)らない人(ひと)にいきなり声(こえ)をかけられて驚(おどろ)いた。
I was startled when a stranger suddenly spoke to me.
道(みち)に迷(まよ)っているようだったので、思(おも)い切(き)って声(こえ)をかけてみた。
They looked lost, so I worked up the nerve to call out to them.
2. to invite along; to let someone know about an occasion
To ask someone to join an outing, a project, or a team. Common in 今度(こんど)声(こえ)をかけてね, 'let me know next time.'
今度(こんど)飲(の)みに行(い)くとき声(こえ)をかけてね。
Give me a shout next time you go out drinking.
何人(なんにん)かに声(こえ)をかけたが、都合(つごう)がつく人(ひと)はいなかった。
I asked several people, but none of them were free.
新(あたら)しいプロジェクトを始(はじ)めるにあたって、経験(けいけん)のある同僚(どうりょう)に声(こえ)をかけた。
In starting the new project, I approached a colleague with experience about joining.

STRUCTURE: 声(こえ) (voice) + を + かける (to cast, to apply).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に声(こえ)をかける: to speak to ~, to invite ~
  • 声(こえ)をかけられる: to be spoken to (the passive is very common)
  • 声(こえ)をかけ合(あ)う: to look out for one another by speaking up
  • お声(こえ)がけください: please let me know (polite; the noun form 声(こえ)がけ)

USAGE:
Sense 2 is a soft way to ask to be included — 声(こえ)をかけてね leaves the other person free to decide, unlike a direct 誘(さそ)ってね. In disaster-preparedness and workplace-safety contexts, 声(こえ)かけ is a set term for checking on the people around you.
