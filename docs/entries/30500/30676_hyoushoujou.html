表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)

ひょうしょうじょう
noun
certificate of commendation; award certificate
1. certificate of commendation; award certificate
A formal document presented to praise an achievement or long service, typically read aloud at a ceremony before being handed over. Common in schools, workplaces, and local government.
校長(こうちょう)から表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)をもらった。
I received a certificate of commendation from the principal.
式(しき)では市長(しちょう)が表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)を読(よ)み上(あ)げた。
At the ceremony, the mayor read the certificate of commendation aloud.
三十年(さんじゅうねん)勤(つと)めた父(ちち)は、会社(かいしゃ)から表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)と記念品(きねんひん)を贈(おく)られた。
My father, who worked there for thirty years, was presented with a certificate of commendation and a memento by the company.

表彰(ひょうしょう) (public commendation) + 状(じょう) (written document).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)を贈(おく)る: to present a certificate
  • 表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)を授与(じゅよ)する: to confer a certificate (formal)
  • 表彰状(ひょうしょうじょう)を読(よ)み上(あ)げる: to read the certificate aloud

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 賞状(しょうじょう): award certificate — for winning a contest or placing in a competition
  • 感謝状(かんしゃじょう): certificate of appreciation — thanks for help or cooperation, not merit
  • 修了証(しゅうりょうしょう): certificate of completion — records finishing a course
