清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)

せいれんけっぱく
na-adjective, noun
upright and incorruptible
1. upright and incorruptible; spotlessly honest
Four-character idiom describing someone with nothing shameful in their conduct — above all, free of any taint of bribery or self-dealing. Used almost exclusively of politicians, officials, and others entrusted with public money or power.
彼(かれ)は清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)な政治家(せいじか)だ。
He is an incorruptible politician.
自分(じぶん)は清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)だと主張(しゅちょう)している。
He insists that he is entirely above board.
清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)を装(よそお)っていたが、裏(うら)で金(かね)を受(う)け取(と)っていた。
He put on a show of being incorruptible, but he had been taking money behind the scenes.
公(おおやけ)の金(かね)を扱(あつか)う立場(たちば)である以上(いじょう)、清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)でなければならない。
As long as one is in a position handling public money, one must be beyond reproach.

清廉(せいれん) (pure and disinterested) + 潔白(けっぱく) (innocent, unstained). The two halves reinforce each other, which is what gives the phrase its weight.

USAGE:
Because the standard it sets is so absolute, the phrase often appears in contexts of doubt — a denial (清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)を主張(しゅちょう)する) or an ironic contrast with what was later uncovered. A plain assertion that someone is 清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく) is high praise.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)な人物(じんぶつ): a person of unimpeachable integrity
  • 清廉潔白(せいれんけっぱく)を主張(しゅちょう)する: to protest one's innocence

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 潔白(けっぱく): innocence — used of a specific accusation, not of overall character
  • 誠実(せいじつ): sincere; honest — about warmth and good faith in dealings, not about corruption
