適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)

てきざいてきしょ
noun
the right person in the right place
1. the right person in the right place; well-matched assignment
The principle of assigning each person to the role that suits their abilities. A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) used constantly in discussions of staffing and team-building.
人事(じんじ)は適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)が基本(きほん)だ。
Personnel decisions come down to putting the right person in the right place.
適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)の配置(はいち)でチームが強(つよ)くなった。
The team grew stronger once everyone was placed where they fit.
能力(のうりょく)のある人(ひと)を集(あつ)めるだけでなく、適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)に配置(はいち)できるかどうかが管理職(かんりしょく)の力量(りきりょう)だ。
A manager's ability lies not just in gathering capable people but in whether they can place each one where they belong.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)の人事(じんじ): staffing that matches people to roles
  • 適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)に配置(はいち)する: to assign people where they fit
  • 適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)を心(こころ)がける: to aim for the right fit

USAGE:
Often used to praise an assignment after the fact (適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ)だ) as well as to state the principle. It can also soften a reassignment: moving someone out of a role sounds better framed as 適材(てきざい)適所(てきしょ) than as a demotion.
