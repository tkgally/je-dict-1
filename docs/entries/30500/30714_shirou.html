脂漏(しろう)

しろう
noun
seborrhea; excessive sebum secretion
1. seborrhea; excessive secretion of sebum
A medical term for the overproduction of oil by the skin's sebaceous glands, especially on the scalp, face, and chest. It appears almost entirely in compounds and in a doctor's or pharmacist's vocabulary rather than in everyday speech.
脂漏(しろう)が原因(げんいん)らしい。
It seems seborrhea is the cause.
医者(いしゃ)に脂漏性皮膚炎(しろうせいひふえん)と言(い)われた。
The doctor told me it was seborrheic dermatitis.
頭皮(とうひ)の脂漏(しろう)がひどいときは、洗(あら)いすぎない方(ほう)がいいそうだ。
They say that when scalp seborrhea is bad, it is better not to wash too much.

Written with 脂(あぶら) (fat, oil) plus 漏(ろう) (leaking), so literally "oil leakage." It is clinical vocabulary — in ordinary conversation people say 脂(あぶら)っぽい (oily) instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脂漏性皮膚炎(しろうせいひふえん): seborrheic dermatitis
  • 脂漏性(しろうせい)の湿疹(しっしん): seborrheic eczema
  • 頭皮(とうひ)の脂漏(しろう): seborrhea of the scalp

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 皮脂(ひし): sebum — the oil itself, a neutral term used in cosmetics advertising
  • 脂性(あぶらしょう): oily skin type — everyday word for a constitution, not a diagnosis
