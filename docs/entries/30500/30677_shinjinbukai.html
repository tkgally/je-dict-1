1. devout; deeply religious; pious
Describes a person whose religious faith is strong and shows in daily practice, such as praying, visiting shrines or temples, or keeping observances. Used of people and of families or communities, and works with any religion.
祖母(そぼ)はとても信心深(しんじんぶか)い。
My grandmother is very devout.
信心深(しんじんぶか)い人(ひと)たちが毎朝(まいあさ)お寺(おてら)に集(あつ)まる。
Devout people gather at the temple every morning.
信心深(しんじんぶか)い家(いえ)に育(そだ)ったので、食事(しょくじ)の前(まえ)に手(て)を合(あ)わせる習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
I grew up in a devout household, so I have the habit of putting my hands together before meals.

信心(しんじん) (faith, devotion) + 深(ふか)い (deep), with rendaku turning ふかい into ぶかい.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信心深(しんじんぶか)い人(ひと): a devout person
  • 信心深(しんじんぶか)い家庭(かてい): a religious household
  • 信心深(しんじんぶか)く育(そだ)てる: to raise someone in the faith

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 敬虔(けいけん)な: pious, reverent — more formal and literary, often used of Christians
  • 熱心(ねっしん)な: earnest, keen — about dedication in general, not specifically faith
  • 迷信深(めいしんぶか)い: superstitious — built the same way, but critical in tone
