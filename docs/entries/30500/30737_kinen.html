1. prayer; earnest wish
Praying or wishing earnestly for something good to come about. Formal, and used above all for public, collective wishes such as peace, safety, or recovery.
平和(へいわ)を祈念(きねん)する。
To pray for peace.
皆様(みなさま)のご健康(けんこう)を祈念(きねん)いたします。
I pray for everyone's good health.
式典(しきてん)では被災地(ひさいち)の一日(いちにち)も早(はや)い復興(ふっこう)が祈念(きねん)された。
At the ceremony, prayers were offered for the earliest possible recovery of the disaster-stricken area.

Do not confuse with its homophone 記念(きねん) (commemoration), which is far more common. The two appear side by side in names such as 平和(へいわ)祈念(きねん)像(ぞう) (Peace Prayer Statue) versus 記念(きねん)館(かん) (memorial hall).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 平和(へいわ)を祈念(きねん)する: to pray for peace
  • ご多幸(たこう)を祈念(きねん)申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I wish you every happiness (set closing phrase)
  • 安全(あんぜん)祈念(きねん): prayer for safety
  • 祈念(きねん)式典(しきてん): a memorial prayer ceremony

REGISTER:
Written and ceremonial. In speech, ordinary people say 祈(いの)る; 祈念(きねん)する belongs to speeches, formal letters, and ceremonies.
