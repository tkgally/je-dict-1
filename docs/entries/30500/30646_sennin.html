1. appointment to a post; formal selection of a person
Choosing a particular person and formally placing them in an office or role, typically by a decision of a meeting, a board, or a court. A legal and business word, not used of casual choices.
新(あたら)しい監査役(かんさやく)を選任(せんにん)した。
A new auditor was appointed.
弁護人(べんごにん)の選任(せんにん)を裁判所(さいばんしょ)に申(もう)し立(た)てた。
A request for the appointment of defense counsel was filed with the court.
株主総会(かぶぬしそうかい)で取締役(とりしまりやく)三名(さんめい)が選任(せんにん)され、任期(にんき)は二年(にねん)となった。
Three directors were appointed at the shareholders' meeting, with two-year terms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 役員(やくいん)を選任(せんにん)する: to appoint officers
  • 選任(せんにん)決議(けつぎ): a resolution of appointment
  • 選任(せんにん)される: to be appointed
  • 解任(かいにん): dismissal from a post — the standard opposite

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 任命(にんめい): appointment — by a superior's authority, common in government and companies
  • 選出(せんしゅつ): election, selection — emphasizes choosing from among candidates, often by vote
  • 就任(しゅうにん): taking office — seen from the appointee's side
