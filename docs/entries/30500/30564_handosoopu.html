ハンドソープ

はんどそーぷ
noun
hand soap; hand wash
1. hand soap; liquid hand wash
Soap made for washing the hands, usually a liquid or foam sold in a pump bottle and kept by a sink.
ハンドソープで手(て)を洗(あら)う。
Wash your hands with hand soap.
ハンドソープがなくなったので買(か)ってきた。
We ran out of hand soap, so I bought some.
泡(あわ)で出(で)てくるタイプのハンドソープは、子供(こども)でも使(つか)いやすい。
The kind of hand soap that comes out as foam is easy even for children to use.

From English "hand soap". Standard on product labels and in everyday speech; the bar-soap word 石鹸(せっけん) is used for solid soap.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ハンドソープで手(て)を洗(あら)う: to wash one's hands with hand soap
  • 泡(あわ)ハンドソープ: foaming hand soap
  • 薬用(やくよう)ハンドソープ: medicated hand soap
  • ハンドソープの詰(つ)め替(か)え: hand soap refill

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 石鹸(せっけん): soap — the general word, and the usual one for a solid bar
  • ボディソープ: body wash — for washing the body in the bath or shower
