目(め)を配(くば)る

めをくばる
expression
to keep an eye on; to watch over attentively
1. to keep an eye out; to watch over a whole area attentively
To spread one's attention over several people, places, or details at once so that nothing is missed. Typical of teachers, staff, and anyone responsible for a group, and carries a sense of conscientious care rather than suspicion.
周(まわ)りに目(め)を配(くば)る。
Keep an eye on your surroundings.
店長(てんちょう)は店内(てんない)全体(ぜんたい)に目(め)を配(くば)っている。
The manager keeps an eye on the whole store.
担任(たんにん)は一人一人(ひとりひとり)の様子(ようす)に目(め)を配(くば)り、小(ちい)さな変化(へんか)も見逃(みのが)さない。
The homeroom teacher watches over each student closely and misses no small change.

USAGE:
The thing watched over takes に. Because 配(くば)る means "to distribute," the phrase implies attention spread across a whole scene, so the object is often a group or an area rather than a single item.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 周囲(しゅうい)に目(め)を配(くば)る: to keep an eye on one's surroundings
  • 安全(あんぜん)に目(め)を配(くば)る: to watch out for safety
  • 細(こま)かいところまで目(め)を配(くば)る: to attend to every detail

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気(き)を配(くば)る: to be considerate — attention to others' comfort, not visual watching
  • 目(め)を光(ひか)らせる: to keep a sharp watch — suspicious and policing in tone
