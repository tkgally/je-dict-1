とびこ

とびこ
noun
flying fish roe (sushi topping)
1. flying fish roe
The tiny orange eggs of the flying fish, used as a sushi topping and as a garnish. The grains are much smaller than salmon roe and pop crisply between the teeth.
とびこの軍艦(ぐんかん)を頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered a flying-fish-roe gunkan roll.
とびこはプチプチした食感(しょっかん)が楽(たの)しい。
Flying fish roe has a fun popping texture.
巻(ま)き寿司(ずし)のまわりにとびこをまぶすと、見(み)た目(め)が一気(いっき)に華(はな)やかになる。
Coating the outside of a roll with flying fish roe makes it look far more festive at once.

Written in kana on most menus; the kanji 飛(と)び子(こ) is rare. Often dyed — green with wasabi, black with squid ink — for color on a platter.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • とびこの軍艦(ぐんかん): gunkan sushi topped with flying fish roe
  • とびこをのせる: to top something with flying fish roe
  • 緑(みどり)のとびこ: wasabi-flavored green roe

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いくら: salmon roe — large, soft, glossy orange spheres
  • 数(かず)の子(こ): herring roe — a firm block of tiny eggs, eaten at New Year
  • 明太子(めんたいこ): spicy pollock roe — sold as a whole seasoned sac
