牛(ぎゅう)タン

ぎゅうたん
noun
beef tongue (esp. grilled, as a Sendai specialty)
1. beef tongue; grilled beef tongue
Sliced beef tongue, usually salted and grilled over charcoal. It is the signature dish of 仙台(せんだい) and a standard item at yakiniku restaurants nationwide.
牛(ぎゅう)タンを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered beef tongue.
仙台(せんだい)に行(い)ったら牛(ぎゅう)タン定食(ていしょく)は外(はず)せない。
If you go to Sendai, the beef tongue set meal is a must.
厚切(あつぎ)りの牛(ぎゅう)タンを炭火(すみび)で焼(や)いて、レモンをしぼって食(た)べた。
We grilled thick-cut beef tongue over charcoal and ate it with a squeeze of lemon.

Written 牛(ぎゅう)タン, with タン from English "tongue." The all-kanji spelling 牛舌(ぎゅうぜつ) exists but is rare on menus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 牛(ぎゅう)タン定食(ていしょく): a beef tongue set meal (with barley rice and oxtail soup)
  • 厚切(あつぎ)り牛(ぎゅう)タン: thick-cut beef tongue
  • 牛(ぎゅう)タン塩(しお): salt-seasoned beef tongue, the standard preparation
  • 仙台(せんだい)名物(めいぶつ)の牛(ぎゅう)タン: Sendai's famous beef tongue

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Grilled beef tongue was developed by a Sendai restaurateur after the war and is now the city's best-known souvenir food, sold vacuum-packed at 仙台(せんだい)駅(えき). Ordering it as a set with 麦(むぎ)ごはん and テールスープ is the local convention.
