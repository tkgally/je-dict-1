持(も)ち時間(じかん)

もちじかん
noun
allotted time; time available to a player or speaker
1. allotted time; one's own share of time
The amount of time assigned to one side or one person — a player's clock in shogi, go, or chess, or the minutes given to a speaker or presenter. Once it runs out, the turn or the talk is over.
持(も)ち時間(じかん)は十分(じゅっぷん)です。
You have ten minutes.
持(も)ち時間(じかん)が残(のこ)り少(すく)なくなってきた。
There is not much time left on my clock.
この対局(たいきょく)は持(も)ち時間(じかん)が各(かく)三時間(さんじかん)で、使(つか)い切(き)ると一手(いって)一分(いっぷん)で指(さ)さなければならない。
Each player has three hours in this match, and once that is used up they must move within one minute per move.

持(も)つ (to hold) plus 時間(じかん) (time): the time one "holds" and may spend as one likes. Standard vocabulary in board games and in conference or debate programs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持(も)ち時間(じかん)を使(つか)い切(き)る: to use up one's time
  • 持(も)ち時間(じかん)が切(き)れる: to run out of time
  • 持(も)ち時間(じかん)内(ない)に収(おさ)める: to keep within the time allotted
  • 各(かく)持(も)ち時間(じかん)三時間(さんじかん): three hours each

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 制限(せいげん)時間(じかん): time limit — an upper bound on how long something may take, not a personal allowance
  • 時間切(じかんぎ)れ: running out of time — the state of having exhausted it
