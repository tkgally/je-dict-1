1. to chase after and cling to; to catch up and grab at
To run after someone who is leaving and take hold of them or their clothing, typically in desperation. Compounds 追(お)う (to chase) with すがる (to cling).
子供(こども)が母親(ははおや)に追(お)いすがった。
The child ran after their mother and clung to her.
泣(な)きながら追(お)いすがる彼女(かのじょ)を振(ふ)り切(き)った。
He shook off her tearful attempts to cling to him.
待(ま)ってくれと叫(さけ)びながら、男(おとこ)は去(さ)っていく車(くるま)に追(お)いすがった。
Shouting for them to wait, the man chased after the departing car and grabbed at it.
2. to press close behind; to close in on a leader
In races and contests, to stay right behind the leader and threaten to overtake.
二位(にい)の選手(せんしゅ)が追(お)いすがる。
The runner in second place is closing in.
必死(ひっし)に追(お)いすがったが、差(さ)は縮(ちぢ)まらなかった。
They pressed hard from behind, but the gap never narrowed.
後続(こうぞく)車(しゃ)が追(お)いすがってきたので、最終(さいしゅう)コーナーで一気(いっき)に加速(かそく)した。
With the car behind closing in, they accelerated hard at the final corner.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The person or thing pursued is marked with に, never を: 母親(ははおや)に追(お)いすがる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 泣(な)きながら追(お)いすがる: to cling while crying
  • 必死(ひっし)に追(お)いすがる: to chase desperately
  • 追(お)いすがる相手(あいて)を振(ふ)り切(き)る: to shake off a pursuer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すがる: to cling to — no sense of chasing; the person is already within reach
  • 追(お)いかける: to chase — neutral pursuit, with no clinging or grabbing
  • 追(お)い上(あ)げる: to close a gap (sports) — sense 2 only, and more clearly about gaining ground
