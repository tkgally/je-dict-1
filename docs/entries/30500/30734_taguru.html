1. to pull in hand over hand; to haul in; to reel in
To draw in something long and flexible — a rope, thread, net, or line — by moving the hands alternately along it.
糸(いと)を手繰(たぐ)る。
To pull in the thread.
ロープを手繰(たぐ)って船(ふね)を岸(きし)に寄(よ)せた。
I pulled in the rope hand over hand and brought the boat to shore.
漁師(りょうし)たちが網(あみ)を手繰(たぐ)り寄(よ)せると、銀色(ぎんいろ)の魚(さかな)が跳(は)ねていた。
When the fishermen hauled in the net, silver fish were leaping inside it.
2. to trace back; to follow (a memory, a lead)
To follow a memory, a chain of associations, or a thread of evidence back toward its source, as if pulling it in bit by bit.
記憶(きおく)を手繰(たぐ)る。
To search back through one's memory.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)の記憶(きおく)を手繰(たぐ)り寄(よ)せてみた。
I tried to draw up memories of my childhood.
当時(とうじ)の手紙(てがみ)を頼(たよ)りに記憶(きおく)を手繰(たぐ)っていくうちに、忘(わす)れていた名前(なまえ)を思(おも)い出(だ)した。
As I traced my memory back with the help of letters from that time, I remembered a name I had forgotten.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive; the thing being drawn in takes を. There is no intransitive partner.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 糸(いと)を手繰(たぐ)る: to pull in a thread
  • 網(あみ)を手繰(たぐ)る: to haul in a net
  • 記憶(きおく)を手繰(たぐ)る: to search back through one's memory
  • 手繰(たぐ)り寄(よ)せる: to draw in toward oneself (the most common form of all)

USAGE:
Sense 2 is the one learners meet most often, usually as 記憶(きおく)を手繰(たぐ)り寄(よ)せる in narration. It keeps the image of sense 1 alive: the memory is a line you pull in a little at a time until the far end comes into view.
