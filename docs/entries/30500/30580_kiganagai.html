気(き)が長(なが)い

きがながい
expression
patient; even-tempered
1. patient; even-tempered; not easily irritated
Describes someone who can wait calmly and does not get annoyed when things take a long time. Literally 'one's temperament is long.' The direct antonym of 気(き)が短(みじか)い.
母(はは)は気(き)が長(なが)い。
My mother is patient.
気(き)が長(なが)い人(ひと)が子(こ)どもの相手(あいて)に向(む)いている。
Patient people are suited to dealing with children.
十年(じゅうねん)かけて研究(けんきゅう)を続(つづ)けるとは、ずいぶん気(き)が長(なが)い話(はなし)だ。
Continuing the research for ten years — that's quite a long-haul undertaking.
祖父(そふ)は気(き)が長(なが)くて、何時間(なんじかん)待(ま)たされても文句(もんく)を言(い)わない。
My grandfather is even-tempered and doesn't complain no matter how many hours he's kept waiting.

STRUCTURE: 気(き) (temperament) + が + 長(なが)い (long).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 気(き)が長(なが)い人(ひと): a patient person
  • 気(き)長(なが)に待(ま)つ: to wait patiently (the adverbial form 気(き)長(なが)に is very common)
  • 気(き)が長(なが)い話(はなし): a long-drawn-out proposition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 我慢強(がまんづよ)い: patient, enduring — stresses putting up with hardship, not just waiting calmly
  • のんびり: laid-back — describes an unhurried pace of life rather than tolerance of delay

USAGE:
Applied to a plan rather than a person, 気(き)が長(なが)い suggests the speaker finds it impractically slow.

Related Words

Antonym: 気(き)が短(みじか)い (short-tempered, impatient)
