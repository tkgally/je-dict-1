何十年(なんじゅうねん)

なんじゅうねん
noun
decades; many years
1. decades; several tens of years
An indefinite span of several decades. Formed from 何(なん) (how many) plus 十年(じゅうねん), it states that the number of decades is large or unknown rather than asking for it.
何十年(なんじゅうねん)も前(まえ)の話(はなし)だ。
That was decades ago.
この寺(てら)は何十年(なんじゅうねん)にも渡(わた)って守(まも)られてきた。
This temple has been looked after for decades.
何十年(なんじゅうねん)働(はたら)いても家(いえ)が買(か)えないという声(こえ)が若(わか)い世代(せだい)から上(あ)がっている。
Young people are saying that even decades of work won't buy them a house.

USAGE:
Almost always followed by も, which supplies the sense of "as many as." Without も the phrase reads as a genuine question — 何十年(なんじゅうねん)かかりますか (how many decades will it take?).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 何十年(なんじゅうねん)も前(まえ): decades ago
  • 何十年(なんじゅうねん)ぶり: for the first time in decades
  • 何十年(なんじゅうねん)にも渡(わた)って: over the course of decades

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 数十年(すうじゅうねん): several decades — the written, more measured equivalent
  • 何年(なんねん)も: for many years — a smaller, vaguer span
