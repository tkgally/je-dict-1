毛抜(けぬ)き

けぬき
noun
tweezers
1. tweezers
A small pincer tool for pulling out hairs or picking up tiny objects such as splinters. The everyday household item, sold with nail clippers in any drugstore.
毛抜(けぬ)きで眉毛(まゆげ)を抜(ぬ)く。
To pluck one's eyebrows with tweezers.
毛抜(けぬ)きを貸(か)してもらえますか。
Could you lend me some tweezers?
指(ゆび)に刺(さ)さったとげを毛抜(けぬ)きで慎重(しんちょう)に抜(ぬ)き取(と)った。
I carefully pulled out the splinter in my finger with tweezers.

毛(け) (hair) + 抜(ぬ)き, the noun form of 抜(ぬ)く (to pull out). Counted with 本(ほん) or 丁(ちょう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 毛抜(けぬ)きで抜(ぬ)く: to pull out with tweezers
  • 眉毛(まゆげ)を整(ととの)える: to shape one's eyebrows (the commonest reason to reach for them)
  • とげを抜(ぬ)く: to remove a splinter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ピンセット: tweezers/forceps — from Dutch, used for laboratory, medical, and craft work rather than grooming

The two are physically similar; the choice of word follows the purpose, so a beautician uses 毛抜(けぬ)き and a technician assembling a circuit board uses ピンセット.
