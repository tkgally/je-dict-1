推進派(すいしんは)

すいしんは
noun
the pro-promotion camp; advocates; those pushing for
1. the pro-promotion camp; advocates; those pushing a plan forward
The group of people actively working to advance a policy, project, or technology. Stronger than 賛成派(さんせいは): these people do not merely approve of the plan, they campaign for it.
推進派(すいしんは)が会見(かいけん)を開(ひら)いた。
The advocates held a press conference.
計画(けいかく)の推進派(すいしんは)は早期(そうき)の着工(ちゃっこう)を求(もと)めている。
Those pushing for the plan are demanding an early start to construction.
推進派(すいしんは)と慎重派(しんちょうは)の意見(いけん)が対立(たいりつ)し、結論(けつろん)は次回(じかい)に持(も)ち越(こ)された。
The advocates and the cautious camp clashed, and the decision was carried over to the next meeting.

Formed from 推進(すいしん) (promotion, driving forward) plus the suffix 〜派(は) (faction, camp). Common in news coverage of infrastructure projects, energy policy, and legislation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 推進派(すいしんは)と慎重派(しんちょうは): the advocates and the cautious camp
  • 推進派(すいしんは)の議員(ぎいん): legislators backing the measure
  • 推進派(すいしんは)が勢(いきお)いづく: the advocates gain momentum

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 賛成派(さんせいは): the camp in favor — approves of the plan but need not be campaigning for it
  • 反対派(はんたいは): the opposing camp — works against the plan

Related Words

Related: 賛成派(さんせいは)
Antonym: 反対派(はんたいは)
