セラミック

せらみっく
noun
ceramic
1. ceramic (as a material)
Hard, heat-resistant material made by firing non-metallic minerals. Used of modern engineered ceramics in knives, cookware, dental work, and electronics — not of traditional pottery, which is 陶器(とうき).
セラミックの包丁(ほうちょう)を使(つか)っている。
I use a ceramic knife.
この鍋(なべ)はセラミック加工(かこう)なので焦(こ)げ付(つ)きにくい。
This pan has a ceramic coating, so food doesn't stick easily.
奥歯(おくば)の治療(ちりょう)で、金属(きんぞく)ではなくセラミックの詰(つ)め物(もの)を選(えら)んだ。
For the treatment of my back tooth, I chose a ceramic filling rather than a metal one.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • セラミック製(せい): made of ceramic
  • セラミック加工(かこう): ceramic coating
  • セラミックヒーター: ceramic heater
  • ファインセラミックス: fine ceramics (the industrial-materials category, usually said in the plural)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 陶器(とうき): pottery, earthenware — traditional fired clay tableware
  • 磁器(じき): porcelain — the finer, whiter, vitrified kind
  • 焼(や)き物(もの): ceramics, pottery — the craft and its products as a whole

Japanese takes セラミック as the modern technical word; a teacup is never described with it.
