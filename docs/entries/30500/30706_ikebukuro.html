池袋(いけぶくろ)

いけぶくろ
noun (proper)
Ikebukuro (major Tokyo hub district)
1. Ikebukuro; the transport and shopping hub in northern Tokyo
A district in Toshima Ward built around one of the busiest stations in Japan. It is known for its department stores, Sunshine City complex, and a large concentration of anime and manga shops aimed at a female fandom, which has earned the area the nickname 乙女(おとめ)ロード.
池袋(いけぶくろ)で待(ま)ち合(あ)わせよう。
Let's meet up in Ikebukuro.
池袋(いけぶくろ)駅(えき)は出口(でぐち)が多(おお)くて迷(まよ)いやすい。
Ikebukuro Station has so many exits that it's easy to get lost.
渋谷(しぶや)や新宿(しんじゅく)に比(くら)べると、池袋(いけぶくろ)は家賃(やちん)が安(やす)めだと言(い)われている。
Compared with Shibuya or Shinjuku, Ikebukuro is said to have somewhat cheaper rents.

Written with 池(いけ) (pond) and 袋(ふくろ) (bag), from old place names in the area.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 池袋(いけぶくろ)駅(えき): Ikebukuro Station, a terminal for the Yamanote, Seibu, and Tobu lines
  • 池袋(いけぶくろ)西口(にしぐち) / 東口(ひがしぐち): the west and east sides, each with its own department store and atmosphere
  • 乙女(おとめ)ロード: the street of anime and manga shops catering to female fans
  • 池袋(いけぶくろ)系(けい): the Ikebukuro crowd or style, contrasted with Shibuya's

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Among Tokyo's three big west-side hubs, 渋谷(しぶや) suggests youth fashion, 新宿(しんじゅく) business and nightlife, and 池袋(いけぶくろ) an everyday, slightly less polished shopping town. Its station is one of the world's busiest, so 池袋(いけぶくろ) is often invoked as shorthand for rush-hour crowds.
