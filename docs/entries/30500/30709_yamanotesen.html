山手線(やまのてせん)

やまのてせん
noun (proper)
Yamanote Line (Tokyo's loop line)
1. the Yamanote Line; the JR loop line circling central Tokyo
The circular JR line that connects Tokyo's main hubs — Tokyo, Shinagawa, Shibuya, Shinjuku, Ikebukuro, and Ueno. Because it links almost every major district, it functions as the backbone of the city and as the mental map ordinary residents use for central Tokyo.
山手線(やまのてせん)で行(い)きます。
I'll go on the Yamanote Line.
山手線(やまのてせん)は朝(あさ)のラッシュがすごい。
The morning rush on the Yamanote Line is intense.
寝過(ねす)ごしても山手線(やまのてせん)なら一周(いっしゅう)して戻(もど)ってこられる。
Even if you fall asleep past your stop, on the Yamanote Line you can just ride the loop back around.

Note the reading: the line is やまのてせん, though the place name 山手(やまて) elsewhere is read やまて.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 内回(うちまわ)り: the inner loop, running clockwise on the map
  • 外回(そとまわ)り: the outer loop, running counterclockwise
  • 山手線(やまのてせん)内(うち): inside the loop — shorthand for central, expensive Tokyo
  • 山手線(やまのてせん)ゲーム: a word-chain party game named after the line's stations

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Property ads and conversation use 山手線(やまのてせん)の内側(うちがわ) (inside the loop) as a rough boundary of central Tokyo, much as Londoners speak of Zone 1. The line's green livery is so familiar that 黄緑(きみどり)の電車(でんしゃ) alone identifies it.
