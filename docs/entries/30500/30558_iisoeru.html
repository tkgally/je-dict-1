1. to add a remark; to say in addition
To tack a short extra comment onto what one has just said, often a qualification, a reassurance, or a polite afterthought. The added remark is typically brief.
一言(ひとこと)言(い)い添(そ)えた。
I added a word.
「無理(むり)はしないで」と母(はは)は言(い)い添(そ)えた。
"Don't push yourself," my mother added.
説明(せつめい)の最後(さいご)に、彼(かれ)は「あくまで現時点(げんじてん)での予定(よてい)です」と言(い)い添(そ)えた。
At the end of his explanation, he added, "This is only the plan as of now."

A compound of 言(い)う (to say) and 添(そ)える (to attach, to accompany). Slightly literary; very common in narration and news writing to introduce a speaker's closing remark.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the words added, or a quoted remark with と

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一言(ひとこと)言(い)い添(そ)える: to add a word
  • 〜と言(い)い添(そ)える: to add, "~"
  • 最後(さいご)に言(い)い添(そ)える: to add at the end

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add — broader; works for information, conditions, or objects, not just speech
  • 付(つ)け足(た)す: to tack on — plainer and more casual
