1. to be perturbed; to be shaken; to lose one's composure
To be thrown off balance emotionally by something sudden or alarming. Almost always used in the negative to praise someone for staying calm.
彼(かれ)は少(すこ)しも動(どう)じなかった。
He wasn't the least bit fazed.
何(なに)が起(お)きても動(どう)じない人(ひと)だ。
She's someone who stays unruffled no matter what happens.
記者(きしゃ)の厳(きび)しい質問(しつもん)にも動(どう)じることなく、落(お)ち着(つ)いて答(こた)えた。
Without being rattled even by the reporters' sharp questions, he answered calmly.

USAGE:
The affirmative is unusual. Learners will meet this verb almost entirely as 動(どう)じない, 動(どう)じることなく, or 動(どう)じずに, describing composure under pressure.

COMMON FORMS:

  • 動(どう)じない: unshaken, unflappable
  • 動(どう)じることなく: without being fazed
  • 少(すこ)しも動(どう)じない: not fazed in the slightest

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The cause is marked with に: 批判(ひはん)に動(どう)じない (to be unmoved by criticism).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 慌(あわ)てる: to panic, to get flustered — the visible reaction; 動(どう)じる is the inner wobble
  • 驚(おどろ)く: to be surprised — a neutral reaction, with no loss of composure implied

動(どう)ずる is the older written form and still appears in formal prose.
