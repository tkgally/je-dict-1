賛成派(さんせいは)

さんせいは
noun
the camp in favor; supporters; those for
1. the camp in favor; supporters; those for a proposal
The group of people who support a particular proposal, plan, or position. Almost always used in contrast with 反対派(はんたいは), the opposing camp, when describing how opinion on an issue is divided.
賛成派(さんせいは)が多数(たすう)だった。
Those in favor were in the majority.
議論(ぎろん)は賛成派(さんせいは)と反対派(はんたいは)に分(わ)かれた。
The debate split into a camp in favor and a camp against.
住民(じゅうみん)への説明会(せつめいかい)のあと、賛成派(さんせいは)に回(まわ)る人(ひと)が増(ふ)えた。
After the briefing for residents, more people came around to supporting it.

Formed from 賛成(さんせい) (agreement, approval) plus the suffix 〜派(は) (faction, camp). It names a group of people, not an opinion, so it takes verbs of counting and dividing rather than verbs of thinking.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 賛成派(さんせいは)と反対派(はんたいは): the camps for and against
  • 賛成派(さんせいは)が多数(たすう)を占(し)める: supporters make up the majority
  • 賛成派(さんせいは)に回(まわ)る: to come around to supporting
  • 賛成派(さんせいは)の意見(いけん): the view of those in favor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対派(はんたいは): the opposing camp — the direct antonym
  • 推進派(すいしんは): the promotion camp — actively pushes a plan forward rather than merely approving of it

Related Words

Antonym: 反対派(はんたいは)
Related: 推進派(すいしんは)
