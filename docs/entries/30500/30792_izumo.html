出雲(いずも)

いずも
noun (proper)
Izumo (region of Shimane Prefecture)
1. Izumo; the old province in eastern Shimane Prefecture
The historical province covering the eastern half of present-day Shimane Prefecture, on the Sea of Japan coast. In Japanese cultural reference it stands above all for 出雲大社(いずもたいしゃ), the grand shrine associated with 縁結(えんむす)び — the tying of romantic and other bonds — and for the myths collected in the 古事記(こじき).
出雲大社(いずもたいしゃ)にお参(まい)りした。
I visited Izumo Grand Shrine.
縁結(えんむす)びを願(ねが)って出雲(いずも)まで足(あし)を運(はこ)ぶ人(ひと)は多(おお)い。
Many people make the trip all the way to Izumo to pray for a good match.
出雲(いずも)では十月(じゅうがつ)を神無月(かんなづき)ではなく神在月(かみありづき)と呼(よ)ぶ。
In Izumo they call the tenth month kamiarizuki, 'the month with gods', rather than kannazuki.
出雲(いずも)地方(ちほう)には古事記(こじき)に登場(とうじょう)する神話(しんわ)の舞台(ぶたい)が数多(かずおお)く残(のこ)っている。
The Izumo region retains many of the settings of the myths that appear in the Kojiki.

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Izumo is the country's best-known destination for 縁結(えんむす)び, so mentioning it in conversation often carries an implication about relationships. It is also the one place that inverts the name of the tenth lunar month: elsewhere it is 神無月(かんなづき), the month without gods, because the gods are believed to have gathered in Izumo — where the same month is therefore 神在月(かみありづき), the month with gods.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 出雲大社(いずもたいしゃ): Izumo Grand Shrine
  • 出雲(いずも){そば}: Izumo soba, a regional buckwheat noodle style
  • 出雲(いずも)神話(しんわ): the Izumo myth cycle
  • 出雲(いずも)地方(ちほう): the Izumo region

Note the reading: 出(いず) + 雲(も), an old place-name reading that follows neither the on'yomi nor the ordinary kun'yomi of the two characters.
