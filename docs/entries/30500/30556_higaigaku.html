被害額(ひがいがく)

ひがいがく
noun
amount of damage; losses in monetary terms
1. amount of damage; value of losses suffered
The monetary value of harm suffered from a disaster, accident, theft, or fraud. Standard vocabulary in news reports and insurance paperwork.
被害額(ひがいがく)はまだ分(わ)からない。
The amount of damage is not yet known.
台風(たいふう)の被害額(ひがいがく)は百億円(ひゃくおくえん)を超(こ)えた。
The damage from the typhoon exceeded ten billion yen.
警察(けいさつ)は、この詐欺(さぎ)事件(じけん)の被害額(ひがいがく)を約(やく)三千万円(さんぜんまんえん)と発表(はっぴょう)した。
Police announced that the losses in this fraud case came to about thirty million yen.

被害(ひがい) (damage, harm) plus 額(がく) (sum, amount of money). Always about money — for physical extent of damage, use 被害(ひがい)状況(じょうきょう) or 被害(ひがい)の規模(きぼ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 被害額(ひがいがく)を算出(さんしゅつ)する: to calculate the losses
  • 被害額(ひがいがく)が膨(ふく)らむ: for the losses to swell
  • 被害額(ひがいがく)は約(やく)〜円(えん): the losses come to about ~ yen
  • 総(そう)被害額(ひがいがく): total losses

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 損害額(そんがいがく): amount of loss — the same figure seen from the side of loss rather than of being victimized; common in contracts and insurance
  • 損害(そんがい): damage, loss — the harm itself, not the figure
