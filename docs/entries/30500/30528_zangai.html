残骸(ざんがい)

ざんがい
noun
wreckage; wreck; remains
1. wreckage; wreck; remains
What is left of something after it has been destroyed — a burned-out car, a crashed plane, a collapsed building. The word keeps the shape of the original object in view, so it suggests a recognizable wreck rather than formless debris.
車(くるま)の残骸(ざんがい)が残(のこ)っていた。
The wreckage of a car was left there.
火事(かじ)の後(あと)、家(いえ)は残骸(ざんがい)をさらしていた。
After the fire, the house stood there as a burned-out shell.
墜落(ついらく)した飛行機(ひこうき)の残骸(ざんがい)から、事故(じこ)の原因(げんいん)が調(しら)べられている。
The cause of the accident is being investigated from the wreckage of the crashed plane.

Also used figuratively for what survives of something abstract that has broken down, as in 帝国(ていこく)の残骸(ざんがい) (the remnants of an empire).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 残骸(ざんがい)が残(のこ)る: wreckage remains
  • 残骸(ざんがい)を片付(かたづ)ける: to clear away the wreckage
  • 船(ふね)の残骸(ざんがい): the wreck of a ship

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 瓦礫(がれき): rubble — broken pieces with no recognizable shape left
  • 廃墟(はいきょ): ruins — a whole abandoned building or site, often long derelict
