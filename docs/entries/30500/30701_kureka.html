クレカ

くれか
noun
credit card (casual abbreviation)
1. credit card; "credit card" shortened
A clipped form of クレジットカード. It belongs to casual speech, chat, and online writing; formal documents, shop signage, and staff speaking to customers all use the full クレジットカード.
クレカで払(はら)うね。
I'll pay by credit card.
その店(みせ)はクレカが使(つか)えないらしい。
Apparently that shop doesn't take credit cards.
クレカの明細(めいさい)を見(み)たら、今月(こんげつ)は使(つか)いすぎていた。
When I looked at my credit card statement, I'd spent too much this month.

A clipping of クレジット + カード that keeps the front of each part, the same abbreviating pattern as スマホ (スマートフォン) and パソコン (パーソナルコンピューター).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • クレカで払(はら)う: to pay by credit card
  • クレカが使(つか)える: credit cards are accepted
  • クレカを作(つく)る: to get a credit card issued
  • クレカ払(ばら)い: payment by credit card

REGISTER:
Casual only. Say クレジットカード in a shop, on a form, or in any business setting; クレカ is for friends, family, and informal writing.
