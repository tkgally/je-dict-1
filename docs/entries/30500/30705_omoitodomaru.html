1. to think better of it; to decide not to go through with something
To stop yourself from carrying out something you had already made up your mind to do. The decision is reversed at the last stage, often after reflection or someone's persuasion, and the subject is the person who was about to act.
最後(さいご)に思(おも)いとどまった。
At the last moment he thought better of it.
友人(ゆうじん)に説得(せっとく)されて退職(たいしょく)を思(おも)いとどまった。
A friend talked her out of it, and she gave up on resigning.
家(いえ)を売(う)るつもりだったが、家族(かぞく)の反対(はんたい)で思(おも)いとどまることにした。
I had intended to sell the house, but my family objected so I decided against it.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive: the abandoned plan takes を (退職(たいしょく)を思(おも)いとどまる). It is also very common with no object at all, when the plan is clear from context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 説得(せっとく)されて思(おも)いとどまる: to be talked out of it
  • 思(おも)いとどまるよう説得(せっとく)する: to persuade someone to reconsider
  • 寸前(すんぜん)で思(おも)いとどまる: to stop oneself at the last second

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あきらめる: to give up — to abandon a hope, often because it turned out to be impossible; 思(おも)いとどまる is a deliberate choice to hold back
  • やめる: to quit, to stop — plain and neutral, with none of the sense of restraining oneself
