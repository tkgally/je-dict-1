数十年(すうじゅうねん)

すうじゅうねん
noun
several decades; a few dozen years
1. several decades; a few dozen years
A span of roughly twenty to ninety years, given deliberately without precision. Common in writing about history, social change, and long-term forecasts.
数十年(すうじゅうねん)前(まえ)の話(はなし)だ。
That's a story from several decades ago.
この建物(たてもの)は数十年(すうじゅうねん)にわたって使(つか)われてきた。
This building has been in use for several decades.
今後(こんご)数十年(すうじゅうねん)で人口(じんこう)は大(おお)きく減(へ)ると予測(よそく)されている。
The population is projected to fall sharply over the next several decades.

The prefix 数(すう) means "several" and combines freely with counters: 数日(すうじつ) (several days), 数年(すうねん) (several years), 数十年(すうじゅうねん) (several decades), 数百年(すうひゃくねん) (several centuries).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 数十年前(すうじゅうねんまえ): several decades ago
  • 数十年間(すうじゅうねんかん): for several decades
  • 数十年(すうじゅうねん)にわたる: spanning several decades
  • ここ数十年(すうじゅうねん): over the past few decades

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 何十年(なんじゅうねん): decades and decades — more emotive, often with a sense of a very long time
  • 十数年(じゅうすうねん): ten-odd years — note the reversed order, meaning 10 to 19 years
