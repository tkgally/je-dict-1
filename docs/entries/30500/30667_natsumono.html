夏物(なつもの)

なつもの
noun
summer clothing; summer goods
1. summer clothing; summer goods
Clothes and merchandise meant for the hot months — light fabrics, short sleeves, sandals. Mainly a retail and wardrobe word, used when shops change their stock over and when households swap seasonal clothes.
そろそろ夏物(なつもの)を出(だ)そう。
It's about time to get the summer clothes out.
デパートで夏物(なつもの)のセールが始(はじ)まった。
The summer clothing sale has started at the department store.
衣替(ころもが)えをして、冬物(ふゆもの)をしまい、夏物(なつもの)を取(と)り出(だ)した。
I changed over my wardrobe, putting away the winter things and taking out the summer ones.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夏物(なつもの)を出(だ)す/しまう: to get out / put away summer clothes
  • 夏物(なつもの)セール: summer clothing sale
  • 夏物(なつもの)衣料(いりょう): summer apparel

SEASONAL SERIES:

The same 物(もの) pattern names each season's goods:

  • 春物(はるもの): spring wear
  • 秋物(あきもの): autumn wear
  • 冬物(ふゆもの): winter wear

RELATED:

  • 衣替(ころもが)え: the seasonal changeover of clothing, done around June 1 and October 1
