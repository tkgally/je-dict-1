自律神経失調症(じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう)

じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう
noun
autonomic nervous system disorder; dysautonomia
1. autonomic nervous system disorder; imbalance of the autonomic nerves
A label used widely in Japan for a cluster of symptoms — dizziness, fatigue, palpitations, sweating, insomnia — attributed to an imbalance between the sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems. Commonly linked to stress, irregular hours, and seasonal change.
自律神経失調症(じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう)と診断(しんだん)された。
I was diagnosed with an autonomic nervous system disorder.
自律神経失調症(じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう)で朝(あさ)なかなか起(お)きられない。
Because of dysautonomia, I have a hard time getting up in the morning.
季節(きせつ)の変(か)わり目(め)に体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)すので、自律神経失調症(じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう)ではないかと心配(しんぱい)している。
I fall ill whenever the seasons change, so I'm worried it might be an autonomic nervous system disorder.

A broad, everyday diagnostic label rather than a precisely defined disease; Japanese speakers use it conversationally for run-down periods with no clear organic cause.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自律神経失調症(じりつしんけいしっちょうしょう)と診断(しんだん)される: to be diagnosed with it
  • 自律神経(じりつしんけい)が乱(みだ)れる: for the autonomic nerves to get out of balance
  • 自律神経(じりつしんけい)を整(ととの)える: to restore autonomic balance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 更年期障害(こうねんきしょうがい): menopausal disorder — overlapping symptoms, but tied to a specific life stage
  • 過労(かろう): overwork, exhaustion — a cause rather than a diagnosis
