ノルマ

のるま
noun
quota; assigned target
1. quota; assigned target; workload one must complete
An amount of work or sales one is required to achieve within a set period. Usually imposed from above and felt as a burden, so the word carries a distinctly grim tone.
ノルマがきつい。
The quota is tough.
今月(こんげつ)のノルマをやっと達成(たっせい)した。
I finally hit this month's quota.
毎日(まいにち)10ページという自分(じぶん)なりのノルマを決(き)めて勉強(べんきょう)している。
I study with a quota I set for myself: ten pages a day.

From Russian *norma*, borrowed via accounts of Soviet labour targets brought back by returnees after the war — one of very few Russian loanwords in everyday Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ノルマを達成(たっせい)する: to meet a quota
  • ノルマを果(は)たす: to fulfil a quota
  • ノルマがきつい: the quota is harsh
  • ノルマに追(お)われる: to be driven by quotas

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目標(もくひょう): goal — something you aim for, with no sense of obligation
  • 割(わ)り当(あ)て: allotment — a neutral share assigned to each person

Because ノルマ implies pressure, a self-imposed target is stated as 自分(じぶん)なりのノルマ, which sounds half-joking about the strictness.
