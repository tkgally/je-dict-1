1. to add something to what one has just said
To tack a further remark onto a statement already made, usually a short clarification or afterthought rather than a new topic.
一言(ひとこと)言(い)い足(た)した。
He added a word.
説明(せつめい)のあとで、注意点(ちゅういてん)を言(い)い足(た)した。
After the explanation, she added a caution.
彼(かれ)は席(せき)を立(た)ちかけて、「明日(あした)は早(はや)めに来(き)てください」と言(い)い足(た)した。
As he started to get up from his seat, he added, "Please come a bit early tomorrow."

Compound of 言(い)う (to say) + 足(た)す (to add). Transitive: what is added takes を, and a quoted remark is marked with と.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一言(ひとこと)言(い)い足(た)す: to add a word
  • 最後(さいご)に言(い)い足(た)す: to add at the end
  • 「〜」と言(い)い足(た)す: to add, "…"

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add, to append — the standard, slightly more formal choice, and usable of writing as well as speech
  • 言(い)い添(そ)える: to add a remark — literary, often a softening or supportive comment
