敬虔(けいけん)

けいけん
adjective-na
pious; devout; reverent
1. pious; devout; reverent
Deeply and sincerely religious in attitude and conduct. Used of believers of any faith, and of the hushed, respectful manner people adopt before something sacred. A written, somewhat elevated word.
敬虔(けいけん)なクリスチャンだ。
She is a devout Christian.
祖母(そぼ)は毎朝(まいあさ)敬虔(けいけん)な気持(きも)ちで仏壇(ぶつだん)に手(て)を合(あ)わせる。
Every morning my grandmother puts her hands together before the family altar in a reverent spirit.
参拝者(さんぱいしゃ)たちは敬虔(けいけん)な面持(おもも)ちで本殿(ほんでん)の前(まえ)に立(た)ち、静(しず)かに頭(あたま)を下(さ)げた。
The worshippers stood before the main shrine with reverent expressions and quietly bowed their heads.

Chiefly written and formal; in speech, 信心深(しんじんぶか)い is the everyday equivalent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敬虔(けいけん)な信者(しんじゃ): a devout believer
  • 敬虔(けいけん)な祈(いの)り: a devout prayer
  • 敬虔(けいけん)な気持(きも)ち: a reverent frame of mind
  • 敬虔(けいけん)に祈(いの)る: to pray reverently

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 信心深(しんじんぶか)い: devout — the plain, conversational word
  • 厳(おごそ)か: solemn — describes the atmosphere of a ceremony rather than a person's faith
