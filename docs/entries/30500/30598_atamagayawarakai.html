頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかい

あたまがやわらかい
expression
flexible in thinking; open-minded; adaptable
1. flexible in thinking; open-minded; quick to adapt
Said of someone who can drop a fixed assumption and take up a new idea or method. It praises mental flexibility rather than intelligence as such.
若(わか)い人(ひと)は頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかい。
Young people are flexible in their thinking.
頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかい人(ひと)ほど新(あたら)しい道具(どうぐ)をすぐ使(つか)いこなす。
The more open-minded someone is, the faster they master a new tool.
部長(ぶちょう)は年配(ねんぱい)だが頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかく、現場(げんば)の提案(ていあん)をよく受(う)け入(い)れてくれる。
The department head is older but open-minded, and often takes up suggestions from the front line.

USAGE:
Used as a compliment, most often about adapting to new methods, technology, or unfamiliar ways of thinking. The negated form 頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかくない is rare — the opposite is normally expressed with 頭(あたま)が固(かた)い.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頭(あたま)を柔(やわ)らかくする: to loosen up one's thinking
  • 頭(あたま)が柔(やわ)らかいうちに: while one's mind is still flexible
  • 発想(はっそう)が柔(やわ)らかい: to have flexible ideas

OPPOSITE:

  • 頭(あたま)が固(かた)い: rigid, set in one's ways — the standard antonym, and much more frequent in speech than any negation of this phrase.
