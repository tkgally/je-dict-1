1. to deal with; to cope with; to conduct oneself in
To meet a situation and handle it in a fitting way. Formal and somewhat literary, used of difficult circumstances rather than everyday tasks. The situation takes に.
難局(なんきょく)に処(しょ)する。
To deal with a difficult situation.
冷静(れいせい)に事(こと)に処(しょ)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to handle matters calmly.
予期(よき)しない事態(じたい)にどう処(しょ)するかで、その人(ひと)の力量(りきりょう)が分(わ)かる。
How a person deals with an unexpected situation shows their ability.
2. to sentence to; to punish with
To impose a stated penalty on someone by legal judgment. The penalty takes に, and the verb appears mainly in statutes, court decisions, and news reports.
罰金(ばっきん)に処(しょ)する。
To impose a fine.
被告(ひこく)は懲役三年(ちょうえきさんねん)に処(しょ)された。
The defendant was sentenced to three years in prison.
この規定(きてい)に違反(いはん)した者(もの)は、十万円以下(じゅうまんえんいか)の罰金(ばっきん)に処(しょ)すると定(さだ)められている。
It is provided that anyone violating this rule shall be fined up to 100,000 yen.

USAGE:
A サ変(へん) verb written with a single kanji, conjugating like 察(さっ)する and 面(めん)する. Both senses mark what is being dealt with, or the penalty imposed, with に. Sense 2 appears overwhelmingly in the passive (処(しょ)される) in news writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事(こと)に処(しょ)する: to deal with a matter
  • 死刑(しけい)に処(しょ)する: to sentence to death
  • 罰金(ばっきん)に処(しょ)する: to impose a fine
  • 懲役(ちょうえき)に処(しょ)される: to be sentenced to imprisonment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 対処(たいしょ)する: to deal with — the ordinary word for sense 1, usable in everyday speech
  • 処分(しょぶん)する: to take disciplinary action; to dispose of — administrative rather than judicial
  • 裁(さば)く: to judge — the act of judging, not of imposing the penalty
