味(あじ)の素(もと)

あじのもと
noun (proper)
Ajinomoto (MSG seasoning brand)
1. Ajinomoto; MSG seasoning; the company that makes it
The trade name of the monosodium-glutamate seasoning first sold in 1909, and of the major food company behind it. The name is also used loosely for any MSG seasoning, the way "Hoover" once stood for vacuum cleaners.
スープに味(あじ)の素(もと)を少(すこ)し入(い)れる。
Add a little Ajinomoto to the soup.
味(あじ)の素(もと)は日本(にほん)を代表(だいひょう)する食品(しょくひん)メーカーだ。
Ajinomoto is one of Japan's leading food manufacturers.
祖母(そぼ)は何(なに)を作(つく)るときでも味(あじ)の素(もと)を振(ふ)りかけていた。
My grandmother sprinkled Ajinomoto on whatever she was cooking.

Literally "the source of flavour." The product grew out of Ikeda Kikunae's 1908 identification of 旨味(うまみ), the savoury fifth taste.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 味(あじ)の素(もと)を振(ふ)る: to sprinkle on MSG seasoning
  • 味(あじ)の素(もと)を一(ひと)振(ふ)り: one shake of the seasoning
  • 味(あじ)の素(もと)スタジアム: Ajinomoto Stadium, the Tokyo football ground the company sponsors

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Older speakers often use 味(あじ)の素(もと) generically for MSG, while younger cooks are more likely to say うま味(み)調味料(ちょうみりょう) (umami seasoning). The company itself is a household name well beyond the seasoning, selling frozen foods, instant coffee, and amino-acid products.
