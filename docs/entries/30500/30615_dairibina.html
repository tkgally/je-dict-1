内裏雛(だいりびな)

だいりびな
noun
imperial dolls; the emperor and empress pair of a hina display
1. imperial dolls; the emperor and empress pair of a hina display
The two dolls placed on the top tier of a 雛人形(ひなにんぎょう) display for the March 3 Doll Festival, dressed as the Heian imperial couple. Sold and spoken of as a pair, and often the only dolls a household displays.
内裏雛(だいりびな)を一番上(いちばんうえ)に飾(かざ)る。
We put the emperor and empress dolls on the top tier.
狭(せま)いので内裏雛(だいりびな)だけ飾(かざ)っています。
Our place is small, so we only display the imperial pair.
雛人形(ひなにんぎょう)の一番(いちばん)上(うえ)の段(だん)に内裏雛(だいりびな)、その下(した)に三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)が並(なら)ぶ。
The imperial pair goes on the top tier of the doll display, with the three court ladies on the tier below.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内裏雛(だいりびな)を飾(かざ)る: to display the imperial pair
  • 内裏雛(だいりびな)を片付(かたづ)ける: to put the dolls away (traditionally soon after March 3)
  • 親王(しんのう)飾(かざ)り: a display consisting of the imperial pair only

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In conversation the two are usually called お内裏(だいり)様(さま) (the male doll) and お雛(ひな)様(さま) (the female doll). Placement differs by region: in Tokyo-style displays the male doll sits on the viewer's left, in Kyoto-style displays on the right.
