1. offer; approach (of work, a role, or a contract)
An approach made to someone with a concrete proposal — a job, a transfer, a part in a production, an appearance on a program. From English "offer", but narrower: it is used for work and contracts, not for offers of help or of goods for sale.
オファーを受(う)けた。
I accepted the offer.
他社(たしゃ)からオファーが来(き)ている。
I have an offer from another company.
条件(じょうけん)が合(あ)わなかったので、今回(こんかい)のオファーは断(ことわ)ることにした。
The terms didn't suit me, so I decided to turn down this offer.

Business and entertainment-industry vocabulary. Also used as a suru-verb (オファーする, to make an offer), though the noun is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • オファーを受(う)ける: to accept an offer
  • オファーを断(ことわ)る: to turn down an offer
  • オファーが来(く)る: an offer comes in
  • 出演(しゅつえん)オファー: an offer to appear (on a program, in a film)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 申(もう)し出(で): offer, proposal — plain Japanese, and it can be an offer of help as well as of work
  • 内定(ないてい): informal job offer — specifically a company's advance decision to hire a new graduate
