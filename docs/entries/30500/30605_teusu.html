手(て)薄(うす)

てうす
na-adjective
short-handed; thinly staffed; poorly covered
1. short-handed; thinly staffed; thinly defended
Describes a position, area, or period where the people, stock, or defenses on hand are too few for what is needed. Used of staffing above all, but also of inventory and of weak points in a defense.
夜間(やかん)は人手(ひとで)が手(て)薄(うす)になる。
Staffing gets thin at night.
守(まも)りが手(て)薄(うす)な右(みぎ)サイドを攻(せ)めた。
They attacked the thinly defended right side.
年末(ねんまつ)は担当者(たんとうしゃ)が休(やす)みで対応(たいおう)が手(て)薄(うす)になるため、早(はや)めにご連絡(れんらく)ください。
Coverage will be thin over the New Year holidays while staff are away, so please contact us early.

USAGE:
Most often used predicatively with なる — 手(て)薄(うす)になる (to become short-handed) — and attributively as 手(て)薄(うす)な. It describes the situation, not the people: saying a section is 手(て)薄(うす) is not a criticism of the staff there.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人手(ひとで)が手(て)薄(うす)になる: to become short of hands
  • 警備(けいび)が手(て)薄(うす)だ: security is thin
  • 在庫(ざいこ)が手(て)薄(うす)だ: stock is running low
  • 手(て)薄(うす)な時間帯(じかんたい): hours with thin coverage

OPPOSITE:

  • 手厚(てあつ)い: generous, attentive — plentiful care or support, the natural antonym.
