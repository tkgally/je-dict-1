ごぼごぼ

ごぼごぼ
adverb
gurgling (sound of water draining or bubbling out)
1. with a gurgle; gurgling, glugging
The deep, throaty sound of a large amount of liquid moving through a narrow opening while air pushes back the other way — a bath draining, a bottle emptying, water welling up from a pipe.
排水口(はいすいこう)からごぼごぼと音(おと)がする。
There's a gurgling sound coming from the drain.
風呂(ふろ)の栓(せん)を抜(ぬ)くと、ごぼごぼと水(みず)が引(ひ)いていった。
When I pulled the plug, the bathwater drained away with a gurgle.
ペットボトルを逆(さか)さにすると、中(なか)の水(みず)がごぼごぼと音(おと)を立(た)てて出(で)てきた。
When I turned the plastic bottle upside down, the water inside came glugging out.

Almost always used as ごぼごぼと before a verb of sound or movement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ごぼごぼと音(おと)がする: to make a gurgling sound
  • ごぼごぼと流(なが)れる: to flow out with a gurgle
  • ごぼごぼと音(おと)を立(た)てる: to gurgle audibly

SIMILAR SOUND WORDS:

  • ごくごく: the sound of a person gulping a drink down
  • ぶくぶく: small bubbles rising, as in fizzing or blowing into water
  • ちょろちょろ: a thin trickle of water — the opposite scale
