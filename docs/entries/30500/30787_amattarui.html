甘(あま)ったるい

あまったるい

1. cloyingly sweet; sickly sweet
Sweet to the point of being unpleasant, said of food, drink, or a smell. Unlike 甘(あま)い, this word is always a complaint.
このケーキは甘(あま)ったるい。
This cake is sickly sweet.
甘(あま)ったるい匂(にお)いが部屋(へや)に残(のこ)っている。
A cloying smell is lingering in the room.
甘(あま)ったるい飲(の)み物(もの)は苦手(にがて)なので、無糖(むとう)のコーヒーにします。
I can't stand overly sweet drinks, so I'll have unsweetened coffee.
2. saccharine; syrupy; sugary (of manner or style)
Cloying in tone rather than taste — a voice, a way of speaking, a love song, or a sentimental story that lays the sweetness on too thick.
彼女(かのじょ)は甘(あま)ったるい声(こえ)で話(はな)した。
She spoke in a syrupy voice.
甘(あま)ったるいセリフに思(おも)わず笑(わら)ってしまった。
I couldn't help laughing at the saccharine line.
甘(あま)ったるい歌詞(かし)が苦手(にがて)な人(ひと)もいるが、そこがこのバンドの魅力(みりょく)だ。
Some people can't take the syrupy lyrics, but that's exactly this band's appeal.

Formed from 甘(あま)い plus the 〜ったるい ending, which adds excess and disapproval. Both senses are negative — you would never praise a dessert as 甘(あま)ったるい.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 甘(あま)ったるい匂(にお)い: a cloying smell
  • 甘(あま)ったるい声(こえ): a syrupy voice
  • 甘(あま)ったるい言葉(ことば): sugary words
  • 甘(あま)ったるくて飲(の)めない: too sweet to drink

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 甘(あま)い: sweet — neutral or positive; the plain description
  • 甘(あま)ったれた: spoiled; clingy — about a person's character, not sweetness
  • くどい: heavy; overdone — too rich or insistent, not specifically sweet
