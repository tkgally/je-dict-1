雀荘(じゃんそう)

じゃんそう
noun
mahjong parlor
1. mahjong parlor; mahjong club
A commercial establishment where customers rent a table and play mahjong, usually by the hour. They are a familiar feature of station-front buildings and of student and office-worker life.
駅前(えきまえ)に雀荘(じゃんそう)がある。
There's a mahjong parlor in front of the station.
大学時代(だいがくじだい)はよく雀荘(じゃんそう)に通(かよ)っていた。
In my university days I used to go to the mahjong parlor a lot.
雀荘(じゃんそう)で朝(あさ)まで打(う)っていたせいで、授業(じゅぎょう)は全部(ぜんぶ)寝(ね)てしまった。
Because I was playing at the mahjong parlor until morning, I slept through all my classes.

The 雀(じゃん) is from 麻雀(マージャン) (mahjong), and 荘(そう) is the same "lodge, villa" character found in apartment-building names. Note the irregular reading: 雀(じゃん) here, not すずめ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雀荘(じゃんそう)に通(かよ)う: to frequent a mahjong parlor
  • 雀荘(じゃんそう)で打(う)つ: to play (mahjong) at a parlor — 打(う)つ is the standard verb for playing mahjong
  • フリーの雀荘(じゃんそう): a parlor where solo customers are matched into a group

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Parlors advertise themselves as ノーレート} (no stakes) or by the rate played. In fiction and memoirs the {雀荘(じゃんそう) is a stock setting for all-night student life.
