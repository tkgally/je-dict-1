1. to stir up; to touch off; to whip up
To set off a reaction that spreads through a crowd or a society — debate, criticism, a craze, a sensation. The subject is usually a person, a work, or an event.
話題(わだい)を巻(ま)き起(お)こす。
To create a stir.
その発言(はつげん)は大(おお)きな議論(ぎろん)を巻(ま)き起(お)こした。
That remark touched off a major debate.
新人(しんじん)監督(かんとく)の作品(さくひん)が映画(えいが)界(かい)にブームを巻(ま)き起(お)こしている。
A new director's film is setting off a boom in the movie world.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive. The reaction takes を; the person or thing that provokes it is the subject.
  • The intransitive partner 巻(ま)き起(お)こる means that the reaction arises by itself.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 議論(ぎろん)を巻(ま)き起(お)こす: to touch off a debate
  • 物議(ぶつぎ)を巻(ま)き起(お)こす: to cause controversy
  • ブームを巻(ま)き起(お)こす: to set off a craze
  • 旋風(せんぷう)を巻(ま)き起(お)こす: to create a whirlwind, to take the field by storm

What gets stirred up is nearly always a collective reaction, so a single small incident is 起(お)こす, not 巻(ま)き起(お)こす. The compound is 巻(ま)く (to wind, to roll) + 起(お)こす (to cause), and it is common in news writing.
