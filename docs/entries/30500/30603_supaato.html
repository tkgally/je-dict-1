1. spurt; burst of speed near the finish
A deliberate acceleration in a race, especially in its closing stage. By extension, a final push of effort on any task with a deadline. From English "spurt."
ラストスパートをかける。
To put on a final spurt.
残(のこ)り百(ひゃく)メートルでスパートした。
She put on a burst of speed with a hundred meters to go.
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)いので、今週(こんしゅう)はラストスパートをかけて仕上(しあ)げる。
The deadline is close, so I'll make a final push this week and finish it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ラストスパート: final spurt — by far the most common form
  • スパートをかける: to put on a spurt
  • 早(はや)めにスパートする: to make one's move early
  • 最後(さいご)のスパート: the closing burst

USAGE:
ラストスパート is used well beyond sports — for studying before exams, year-end sales pushes, or the last stretch of any project. Plain スパート on its own stays closer to racing.
