コインロッカー

こいんろっかー
noun
coin locker; self-service luggage locker
1. coin locker; self-service luggage locker
A rentable locker found at train stations, airports, and tourist sites, used to leave luggage or shopping for a few hours. From English "coin locker"; most now take IC cards as well as coins.
駅(えき)のコインロッカーに荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)けた。
I left my luggage in a coin locker at the station.
大(おお)きいコインロッカーは空(あ)いていますか。
Are any of the large coin lockers free?
観光(かんこう)の前(まえ)にスーツケースをコインロッカーに入(い)れておこう。
Let's put our suitcases in a coin locker before we go sightseeing.
連休中(れんきゅうちゅう)は駅(えき)のコインロッカーがどこも満杯(まんぱい)で困(こま)った。
During the holidays every coin locker at the station was full, which was a problem.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コインロッカーに預(あず)ける: to leave (something) in a coin locker
  • コインロッカーが空(あ)いている: a coin locker is free
  • コインロッカーが満杯(まんぱい): the coin lockers are all full
  • コインロッカーから出(だ)す: to take (something) out of a coin locker

USAGE:
Lockers are sized 小(しょう)・中(ちゅう)・大(だい) and priced accordingly, and most have a same-day or overnight limit. For longer or oversized storage travelers use a staffed 手荷物預(てにもつあず)かり所(じょ) (baggage room) instead.
