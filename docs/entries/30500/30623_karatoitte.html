からといって

からといって
expression
just because ...; that does not mean ...
1. just because ...; the fact that ... does not mean ...
Marks a reason that the speaker then denies as sufficient. It attaches to a plain-form clause and is almost always followed by a negative conclusion such as わけではない, とは限(かぎ)らない, or a plain negative verb.
安(やす)いからといって買(か)いすぎないで。
Don't buy too much just because it's cheap.
日本(にほん)に住(す)んでいるからといって、漢字(かんじ)が書(か)けるとは限(かぎ)らない。
Living in Japan doesn't necessarily mean you can write kanji.
一度(いちど)失敗(しっぱい)したからといって、向(む)いていないと決(き)めつけるのは早(はや)すぎる。
Just because you failed once, it's too early to conclude that you aren't suited to it.

USAGE:
The following clause must be a denial — leaving it positive produces an ungrammatical sentence. In casual speech the form is shortened to からって.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜からといって〜わけではない: it doesn't follow that ...
  • 〜からといって〜とは限(かぎ)らない: it isn't necessarily the case that ...
  • 〜からといって〜すぎないほうがいい: you shouldn't overdo it just because ...

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 〜からと言(い)って: the same pattern written with kanji, common in writing
  • 〜とはいえ: even so — concedes a fact without denying it as a reason
