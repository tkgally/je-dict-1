買(か)い占(し)め

かいしめ
noun
buying up; hoarding; cornering the supply
1. buying up; panic hoarding; cornering the supply
Buying all or most of an available stock, either by consumers stripping the shelves in a shortage or by a buyer cornering a market for profit. The noun form of 買(か)い占(し)める.
マスクの買(か)い占(し)めが起(お)きた。
Panic buying of masks broke out.
買(か)い占(し)めはご遠慮(えんりょ)くださいと張(は)り紙(がみ)があった。
There was a notice asking customers to refrain from bulk-buying.
品薄(しなうす)の報道(ほうどう)が買(か)い占(し)めを招(まね)き、かえって品不足(しなぶそく)がひどくなった。
Reports of short supply triggered hoarding, which only made the shortage worse.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 買(か)い占(し)めが起(お)きる: for hoarding to break out
  • 買(か)い占(し)めを防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent hoarding
  • 土地(とち)の買(か)い占(し)め: buying up land
  • 転売(てんばい)目的(もくてき)の買(か)い占(し)め: buying up for resale

USAGE:
The word carries clear disapproval: the buyer is taking more than their share or manipulating supply. Ordinary bulk shopping is まとめ{買(が)い}, which is neutral and even thrifty.
