白物(しろもの)

しろもの
noun
whites (laundry); white goods (appliances)
1. whites; white-colored laundry
White or light-colored clothing and linen, treated as a separate load from colored items when doing laundry. Usually contrasted with 色物(いろもの).
白物(しろもの)だけ先(さき)に洗(あら)う。
I wash the whites first.
色物(いろもの)と白物(しろもの)を分(わ)けて洗濯(せんたく)してください。
Please separate the colors from the whites when you do the laundry.
白物(しろもの)は漂白剤(ひょうはくざい)を使(つか)うと黄(き)ばみが取(と)れる。
Using bleach on whites will get the yellowing out.
2. white goods; major home appliances
Large household appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners, so called because they were traditionally sold in white casings. Almost always used in the compound 白物家電(しろものかでん).
白物家電(しろものかでん)が売(う)れている。
White goods are selling well.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しを機(き)に白物家電(しろものかでん)を買(か)い替(か)えた。
I took the move as a chance to replace my major appliances.
この会社(かいしゃ)は白物家電(しろものかでん)の分野(ぶんや)で国内(こくない)トップのシェアを持(も)っている。
This company holds the top domestic market share in the white goods sector.

Sense 1 belongs to everyday laundry vocabulary; sense 2 belongs to retail and business reporting, where it appears almost exclusively as 白物家電(しろものかでん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 白物(しろもの)と色物(いろもの): whites and colors
  • 白物(しろもの)を洗(あら)う: to wash the whites
  • 白物家電(しろものかでん): white goods; major home appliances
  • 白物家電(しろものかでん)メーカー: white goods manufacturer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 色物(いろもの): colored items — the laundry counterpart of sense 1
  • 黒物家電(くろものかでん): brown goods — TVs and audio-visual equipment, the counterpart of sense 2
