荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かり

にもつあずかり
noun
baggage storage; left-luggage service
1. baggage storage; left-luggage service or counter
A service that holds luggage for a while, or the counter where it is handed over — at a station, hotel, museum, or event venue. Also written 荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かり所(じょ) when the place itself is meant.
荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かりはありますか。
Is there baggage storage?
チェックアウト後(ご)も荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かりを利用(りよう)できます。
You can use the baggage storage even after checking out.
コインロッカーが全部(ぜんぶ)埋(う)まっていたので、駅(えき)の荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かりにスーツケースを置(お)いてきた。
All the coin lockers were full, so I left my suitcase at the station's baggage storage.

荷物(にもつ) (luggage) plus the noun form of 預(あず)かる (to keep something for someone). Seen on signs and on hotel and station websites; a traveler will meet it constantly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かり所(じょ): baggage storage room
  • 荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かりサービス: baggage-holding service
  • 荷物(にもつ)預(あず)かりを利用(りよう)する: to use the baggage storage

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コインロッカー: coin locker — a self-service locker, not a staffed counter
  • 手荷物(てにもつ)一時(いちじ)預(あず)かり所(じょ): short-term baggage check — the fuller signage form used at large stations
