1. to extend one's stay; to stay extra nights
To add nights to a booking already made, staying on past the original checkout date. Standard vocabulary at hotel front desks and on booking sites, whether the reason is a delayed flight or simply wanting more time.
一泊(いっぱく)延泊(えんぱく)したいのですが。
I'd like to extend my stay by one night.
台風(たいふう)で飛行機(ひこうき)が欠航(けっこう)になり、延泊(えんぱく)することになった。
The typhoon caused my flight to be cancelled, so I ended up extending my stay.
延泊(えんぱく)の場合(ばあい)、料金(りょうきん)が変(か)わることがございます。
In the case of an extended stay, the rate may differ.
満室(まんしつ)のため延泊(えんぱく)をお断(ことわ)りする場合(ばあい)もありますので、お早(はや)めにフロントへご相談(そうだん)ください。
We may have to decline an extension if we are fully booked, so please speak to the front desk as early as you can.

延(えん) (extend) + 泊(はく) (night's lodging). The number of added nights goes before the word: 一泊(いっぱく)延泊(えんぱく)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 延泊(えんぱく)する: to extend one's stay
  • 延泊(えんぱく)を希望(きぼう)する: to request an extension
  • 延泊料金(えんぱくりょうきん): the rate for extra nights
  • 延泊(えんぱく)が可能(かのう): extension is possible

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連泊(れんぱく): consecutive nights — several nights booked as one stay from the outset, not added later
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく): lodging; staying overnight — the general term
