〜にかけて

にかけて
expression
from A to B, over the span of; when it comes to
1. from A through B; over the span of; across
Paired with から to mark a stretch of time or space treated as one continuous span: 春(はる)から夏(なつ)にかけて (from spring through summer). The endpoints are approximate, so it suits vague ranges rather than exact schedules.
春(はる)から夏(なつ)にかけて咲(さ)く。
It blooms from spring through summer.
今夜(こんや)から明日(あす)の朝(あさ)にかけて雨(あめ)が強(つよ)まるでしょう。
The rain will intensify from tonight through tomorrow morning.
関東(かんとう)から東北(とうほく)にかけての広(ひろ)い範囲(はんい)で、雪(ゆき)が降(ふ)ると予想(よそう)されている。
Snow is forecast over a wide area stretching from Kanto to Tohoku.
2. when it comes to; in terms of; at ~ nobody beats
In the pattern 〜にかけては, it names the area in which someone excels. Nearly always followed by a claim of superiority, so it reads as praise or a boast.
足(あし)の速(はや)さにかけては負(ま)けない。
When it comes to running speed, I won't lose to anyone.
料理(りょうり)にかけては母(はは)にかなわない。
When it comes to cooking, I'm no match for my mother.
記憶力(きおくりょく)にかけては、この中(なか)で彼女(かのじょ)の右(みぎ)に出(で)る者(もの)はいない。
When it comes to memory, nobody here can match her.

Sense 1 attaches to nouns of time or place and is normally paired with から; before a noun it becomes 〜にかけての. Sense 2 takes the は-marked form 〜にかけては and attaches to a skill or field.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AからBにかけて: from A through B
  • AからBにかけての〜: the ~ stretching from A to B
  • 〜にかけては誰(だれ)にも負(ま)けない: nobody beats me at ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 〜から〜まで: from ~ to ~ — marks exact endpoints; use it for 九時(くじ)から五時(ごじ)まで, not にかけて
  • 〜間(あいだ): during ~ — a period seen as a whole, without the sense of moving across it
