夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)き

ゆうしょくつき
noun
with dinner included
1. dinner included; with evening meal
Describes an accommodation plan or package whose price covers the evening meal. Used attributively with の or as a predicate, and contrasted on booking sites with breakfast-only and room-only plans.
夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きのプランを選(えら)んだ。
I chose a plan with dinner included.
この料金(りょうきん)は夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きですか。
Does this price include dinner?
到着(とうちゃく)が遅(おそ)くなるので、夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きではないプランにした。
We'll be arriving late, so we went with a plan that doesn't include dinner.
夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きの宿(やど)は便利(べんり)だが、周辺(しゅうへん)の店(みせ)で食(た)べる楽(たの)しみはなくなる。
An inn with dinner included is convenient, but you lose the fun of eating at places nearby.

The 〜付(つ)き suffix means 'comes with' and attaches freely to nouns: 朝食付(ちょうしょくつ)き, 送迎付(そうげいつ)き, 保証付(ほしょうつ)き. Note the reading is {つ}き, not {ふ}き.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きプラン: a dinner-included plan
  • 夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きで予約(よやく)する: to book with dinner included
  • 夕食付(ゆうしょくつ)きの宿(やど): an inn where dinner is provided

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 朝食付(ちょうしょくつ)き: with breakfast — the business-hotel default
  • 一泊二食付(いっぱくにしょくつ)き: one night with dinner and breakfast — the 旅館(りょかん) standard
  • 素泊(すど)まり: room only
