三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)

さんにんかんじょ
noun
the three court ladies of a hina display
1. the three court ladies of a hina display
The set of three ladies-in-waiting on the second tier of a 雛人形(ひなにんぎょう) display, below the imperial couple. They carry sake utensils, and the middle one is seated while the other two stand.
二段目(にだんめ)は三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)です。
The second tier is the three court ladies.
三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)の真(ま)ん中(なか)だけ座(すわ)っている。
Only the middle one of the three court ladies is seated.
内裏雛(だいりびな)の下(した)に三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)、さらにその下(した)に五人囃子(ごにんばやし)が並(なら)ぶ。
Below the imperial pair come the three court ladies, and below them the five musicians.

Always treated as one set, so the word is used in the singular even though it names three dolls.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)を並(なら)べる: to arrange the three court ladies
  • 三人官女(さんにんかんじょ)の段(だん): the court-ladies tier
  • 五人囃子(ごにんばやし): the five musicians — the set on the tier below

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Only larger, multi-tier displays include them; a compact modern display often stops at the imperial pair. The dolls represent the attendants who served at a Heian court wedding.
