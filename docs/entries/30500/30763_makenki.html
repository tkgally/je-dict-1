負(ま)けん気(き)

まけんき
noun
competitive spirit; refusal to lose
1. competitive spirit; fighting spirit; refusal to be beaten
The temperament of someone who hates losing and pushes back rather than giving in. Usually admiring, though it can hint at stubbornness.
負(ま)けん気(き)が強(つよ)い。
She hates to lose.
弟(おとうと)は昔(むかし)から負(ま)けん気(き)の強(つよ)い子(こ)だった。
My younger brother has always been a child who couldn't stand losing.
負(ま)けん気(き)を出(だ)して練習(れんしゅう)を続(つづ)けたおかげで、最後(さいご)には代表(だいひょう)に選(えら)ばれた。
Thanks to summoning up her competitive spirit and keeping at her training, she was chosen for the national team in the end.

From 負(ま)ける (to lose) with the old negative ん: literally "the spirit of not losing."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 負(ま)けん気(き)が強(つよ)い: to have a strong competitive streak (the standard phrase)
  • 負(ま)けん気(き)の強(つよ)い性格(せいかく): a competitive personality
  • 負(ま)けん気(き)を出(だ)す: to summon up one's fighting spirit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 向(む)こう気(き): pluck, combativeness — readiness to take someone on, with less of the hatred of losing
  • 根性(こんじょう): guts, perseverance — about enduring hardship rather than beating a rival
  • 意地(いじ)っ張(ぱ)り: stubbornness — the same refusal to yield, but plainly negative
