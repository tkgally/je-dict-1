総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)

そうしきゅうがく
noun
gross pay; total amount paid before deductions
1. gross pay; total payment before deductions
The full amount of a salary or bonus before tax and insurance are taken out. It appears at the top of a Japanese pay slip, with 手取(てど)り (take-home pay) shown at the bottom.
総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)を確認(かくにん)する。
I check the gross pay amount.
総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)から税金(ぜいきん)が引(ひ)かれる。
Taxes are deducted from the gross pay.
総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)は増(ふ)えたのに、保険料(ほけんりょう)が上(あ)がったので手取(てど)りはほとんど変(か)わらなかった。
My gross pay went up, but insurance premiums rose, so my take-home pay barely changed.

総(そう) (total) + 支給(しきゅう) (payment, provision) + 額(がく) (amount). A pay-slip term rather than a conversational one.

PAY SLIP VOCABULARY:

  • 総支給額(そうしきゅうがく): gross pay
  • 控除(こうじょ): deductions (tax, pension, insurance)
  • 差引支給額(さしひきしきゅうがく): net pay (the line labelled on the slip)
  • 手取(てど)り: take-home pay (the everyday spoken word)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)が増(ふ)える: gross pay increases
  • 総支給額(そうしきゅうがく)から引(ひ)かれる: to be deducted from gross pay

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手取(てど)り: take-home pay — what actually reaches your account; the usual word in conversation
  • 年収(ねんしゅう): annual income — normally quoted as a gross yearly figure
