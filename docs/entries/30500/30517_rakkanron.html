楽観論

らっかんろん
noun
optimistic view; optimism
1. optimistic view; optimism
An argument or outlook holding that a situation will turn out well. Like 悲観論(ひかんろん), it names a stated position in a debate rather than a personality trait, and is often used with a hint that the position may be too easy.
楽観論(らっかんろん)が目立(めだ)つ。
Optimistic views stand out.
市場(しじょう)では楽観論(らっかんろん)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Optimism is spreading in the market.
根拠(こんきょ)のない楽観論(らっかんろん)は、対策(たいさく)を遅(おく)らせるだけだ。
Groundless optimism only delays countermeasures.

楽観(らっかん) (optimism) + 論(ろん) (argument, theory). Note the small っ in らっかん.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 楽観論(らっかんろん)が広(ひろ)がる: for optimism to spread
  • 根拠(こんきょ)のない楽観論(らっかんろん): groundless optimism
  • 楽観論(らっかんろん)を唱(とな)える: to argue an optimistic case
  • 楽観論(らっかんろん)と悲観論(ひかんろん): the optimistic and pessimistic cases

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽観的(らっかんてき): optimistic — the adjective, describing a person or an outlook
  • 悲観論(ひかんろん): pessimistic view — the direct opposite
