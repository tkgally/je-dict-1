ドラマチック

どらまちっく
adjective-na
dramatic; theatrical; full of drama
1. dramatic; like something out of a story
Describes an event, scene, or turn of events so striking that it feels staged. Used approvingly of exciting sports finishes and emotional moments, not of serious problems, where 深刻(しんこく) is used instead.
ドラマチックな結末(けつまつ)だった。
It was a dramatic ending.
二人(ふたり)の出会(であ)い方(かた)はドラマチックだ。
The way the two of them met is like something out of a drama.
最後(さいご)の一球(いっきゅう)で逆転(ぎゃくてん)するという、ドラマチックな展開(てんかい)に観客(かんきゃく)は沸(わ)いた。
The crowd erupted at the dramatic turn of events, a comeback on the very last pitch.

From English 'dramatic.' Also spelled ドラマティック, which is common in music and film writing; the two are interchangeable.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ドラマチックな展開(てんかい): a dramatic turn of events
  • ドラマチックな結末(けつまつ): a dramatic ending
  • ドラマチックに変(か)わる: to change dramatically
  • ドラマチックな演出(えんしゅつ): dramatic staging

USAGE:
Unlike English 'dramatic,' the Japanese word is not used for a large change in numbers. For that, use 劇的(げきてき)に or 大幅(おおはば)に: 売(う)り上(あ)げが大幅(おおはば)に伸(の)びた, not ドラマチックに.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 劇的(げきてき)な: dramatic — the native equivalent, and the one used for sharp changes in figures
  • 感動的(かんどうてき)な: moving — focuses on the emotional effect rather than the theatrical shape
