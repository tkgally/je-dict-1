黒豆(くろまめ)

くろまめ
noun
black soybeans; sweet simmered black beans (New Year dish)
1. black soybeans; sweet simmered black beans
A black variety of soybean, and by extension the glossy sweet-simmered beans made from it that appear in every osechi box at New Year. Also sold roasted and as tea.
黒豆(くろまめ)を煮(に)る。
To simmer black beans.
おせちの黒豆(くろまめ)はつやつやしていた。
The black beans in the osechi were beautifully glossy.
祖母(そぼ)は毎年(まいとし)、暮(く)れになると黒豆(くろまめ)を一晩(ひとばん)水(みず)につけてから時間(じかん)をかけて煮(に)る。
Every year at the end of December my grandmother soaks black beans overnight and then simmers them slowly.

One of the 祝(いわ)い肴(ざかな), the core celebratory dishes of osechi. The bean is eaten as a wordplay wish: まめ also means "healthy, diligent," so the dish stands for a year of good health and hard work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黒豆(くろまめ)を煮(に)る: to simmer black beans
  • つやつやの黒豆(くろまめ): glossy black beans
  • 黒豆(くろまめ)茶(ちゃ): black soybean tea

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大豆(だいず): soybean — the ordinary yellow variety used for tofu and miso
  • 小豆(あずき): adzuki bean — red, used for sweet bean paste, not part of osechi
