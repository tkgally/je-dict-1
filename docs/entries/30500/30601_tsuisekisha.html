追跡者(ついせきしゃ)

ついせきしゃ
noun
pursuer; chaser; tracker
1. pursuer; chaser; tracker
The one doing the chasing — a police officer on a suspect's trail, a pursuer in a story, or a rival closing on the leader. Formed from 追跡(ついせき) (pursuit) plus the agent suffix 〜者(しゃ).
追跡者(ついせきしゃ)をまいた。
He shook off his pursuer.
背後(はいご)に追跡者(ついせきしゃ)の気配(けはい)を感(かん)じた。
He sensed a pursuer behind him.
首位(しゅい)のチームにとって、追跡者(ついせきしゃ)との勝(か)ち点差(てんさ)はわずか三(さん)だった。
For the league leaders, the gap to their closest pursuer was only three points.

USAGE:
More a written-language word than a conversational one — common in news reports, thrillers, and sports writing. In everyday speech people say 追(お)いかける人(ひと).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 追跡者(ついせきしゃ)をまく: to shake off a pursuer
  • 追跡者(ついせきしゃ)を振(ふ)り切(き)る: to outrun a pursuer
  • 最大(さいだい)の追跡者(ついせきしゃ): the closest rival

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追手(おって): pursuer — older and more dramatic, suggesting a party sent to capture someone
  • 尾行者(びこうしゃ): a tail — someone following secretly rather than openly chasing
