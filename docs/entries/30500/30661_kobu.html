瘤(こぶ)

こぶ
noun
lump; bump; swelling; hump
1. lump; bump; swelling; hump
A rounded protrusion — a bump raised on the head by a knock, a knot on a tree trunk, or the hump on a camel's back. Usually written in kana.
頭(あたま)にこぶができた。
I got a bump on my head.
ぶつけたところが腫(は)れてこぶになった。
The spot I hit swelled up into a lump.
ラクダの背中(せなか)のこぶには、水(みず)ではなく脂肪(しぼう)が蓄(たくわ)えられている。
A camel's hump stores fat, not water.

Normally written こぶ in kana; the kanji 瘤(こぶ) appears mainly in medical or literary writing.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • こぶができる: for a bump to form
  • たんこぶ: a bump on the head — the colloquial, slightly childish variant
  • 目(め)の上(うえ)のたんこぶ: a thorn in one's side (literally, a lump above the eye) — someone in a senior position who is always in your way

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 腫(は)れ: swelling — a spread-out puffiness rather than a distinct lump
  • できもの: a growth, boil — something that appears on the skin, often needing treatment
