1. reporting to the emperor or a deity; humbly stating to a superior
Stating something formally to the highest-ranking listener imaginable — the emperor, or a kami at a shrine. Survives today almost only in Shinto ritual language and in historical writing.
神前(しんぜん)で祝詞(のりと)を奏上(そうじょう)する。
To offer the ritual prayer before the deity.
天皇(てんのう)に調査(ちょうさ)の結果(けっか)を奏上(そうじょう)した。
He reported the results of the investigation to the emperor.
この絵巻(えまき)には、大臣(だいじん)が国(くに)の現状(げんじょう)を奏上(そうじょう)する場面(ばめん)が描(えが)かれている。
This picture scroll depicts a scene in which a minister reports the state of the country to the emperor.

An extremely formal word with no everyday use; a learner will meet it at shrines, in historical drama, and in writing about the imperial court.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 祝詞(のりと)を奏上(そうじょう)する: to offer a Shinto prayer to the deity
  • 天皇(てんのう)に奏上(そうじょう)する: to report to the emperor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上奏(じょうそう): memorial to the throne — near-synonym, more common in political and legal history
  • 報告(ほうこく): report — the neutral everyday word for telling a superior something
