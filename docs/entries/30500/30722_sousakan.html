捜査官(そうさかん)

そうさかん
noun
investigator; criminal investigator
1. investigator; criminal investigator; agent
An official whose job is investigating crimes — a detective, a prosecutor's investigator, or an agent of a foreign agency such as the FBI. It is the formal, institutional word, common in news reports and crime fiction.
捜査官(そうさかん)が現場(げんば)に入(はい)った。
Investigators entered the scene.
ベテランの捜査官(そうさかん)が事件(じけん)を担当(たんとう)している。
A veteran investigator is handling the case.
捜査官(そうさかん)たちは防犯(ぼうはん)カメラの映像(えいぞう)を一日中(いちにちじゅう)調(しら)べ続(つづ)けた。
The investigators kept going through the security camera footage all day long.

From 捜査(そうさ) (criminal investigation) plus the suffix 官(かん), which marks a public official (警察官(けいさつかん), 外交官(がいこうかん), 裁判官(さいばんかん)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 捜査官(そうさかん)を派遣(はけん)する: to send in investigators
  • 麻薬(まやく)捜査官(そうさかん): narcotics agent
  • 潜入(せんにゅう)捜査官(そうさかん): undercover investigator
  • ベテラン捜査官(そうさかん): veteran investigator

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刑事(けいじ): detective — the everyday word for a police detective, used in speech far more often
  • 警察官(けいさつかん): police officer — any officer, not specifically one who investigates
  • 探偵(たんてい): private detective — works for clients, not the state
