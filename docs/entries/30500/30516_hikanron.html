悲観論

ひかんろん
noun
pessimistic view; pessimism
1. pessimistic view; pessimism
An argument or outlook holding that a situation will turn out badly. Used of a stated position in debate — on the economy, the environment, a company's prospects — rather than of a person's temperament.
悲観論(ひかんろん)が広(ひろ)がっている。
Pessimism is spreading.
景気(けいき)については悲観論(ひかんろん)が強(つよ)い。
Pessimism about the economy is strong.
悲観論(ひかんろん)に傾(かたむ)くのは簡単(かんたん)だが、それでは何(なに)も変(か)わらない。
It is easy to lean toward pessimism, but nothing changes that way.

悲観(ひかん) (pessimism) + 論(ろん) (argument, theory). The 〜論(ろん) ending makes it a position that can be held, stated, and argued against.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悲観論(ひかんろん)が強(つよ)まる: for pessimism to grow stronger
  • 悲観論(ひかんろん)に傾(かたむ)く: to lean toward pessimism
  • 悲観論(ひかんろん)を唱(とな)える: to argue a pessimistic case
  • 根強(ねづよ)い悲観論(ひかんろん): deep-rooted pessimism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悲観的(ひかんてき): pessimistic — the adjective, describing a person or an outlook
  • 楽観論(らっかんろん): optimistic view — the direct opposite
