心血(しんけつ)

しんけつ
noun
heart and soul; one's utmost energy
1. heart and soul; one's utmost energy
The whole of a person's mental and physical energy, thought of as something poured into a long undertaking. Used almost exclusively in the set phrase 心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)ぐ.
研究(けんきゅう)に心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)いだ。
He poured his heart and soul into his research.
この作品(さくひん)は父(ちち)が心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)いで完成(かんせい)させたものだ。
This work is one my father completed by pouring his whole self into it.
十年(じゅうねん)にわたって心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)いできた計画(けいかく)が、予算(よさん)不足(ぶそく)で中止(ちゅうし)になった。
The project she had devoted herself to for ten years was cancelled for lack of funds.

心(しん) (heart, mind) + 血(けつ) (blood): the image is of giving both spirit and lifeblood.

USAGE:
Almost never used alone. The phrase 心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)ぐ takes に for the object of the effort (作品(さくひん)に心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)ぐ), and fits work carried on over years rather than a single burst of effort. Written and somewhat elevated in tone.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 全力(ぜんりょく)を尽(つ)くす: to do one's very best — neutral, and usable of a short effort
  • 精魂(せいこん)を込(こ)める: to put one's soul into — similar in weight, focused on craftsmanship
  • 丹精(たんせい)を込(こ)める: to work with painstaking care — typically of gardening or handiwork
