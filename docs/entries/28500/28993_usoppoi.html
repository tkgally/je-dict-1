1. fishy; looking fake; unconvincing
Describes something or someone that seems suspect, untrustworthy, or obviously made up. A casual word used when something doesn't ring true, whether a story, an advertisement, or a person's behavior.
その話(はなし)、うそっぽいね。
That story sounds fishy.
うそっぽい笑顔(えがお)で挨拶(あいさつ)された。
I was greeted with a fake-looking smile.
広告(こうこく)の効果(こうか)が大(おお)げさすぎて、なんだかうそっぽく感(かん)じる。
The advertised results are so exaggerated that they feel unconvincing somehow.

Formed from 嘘(うそ) (lie) + っぽい (tending toward; -ish). Casual and conversational; not used in formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • うそっぽい話(はなし): a fishy story
  • うそっぽい笑顔(えがお): a fake-looking smile
  • うそっぽく聞(き)こえる: to sound unconvincing
  • なんだかうそっぽい: somehow doesn't ring true

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 嘘(うそ)くさい: suspicious; smells like a lie — close synonym, often interchangeable
  • 怪(あや)しい: suspicious; dubious — broader, can apply to people, situations, or appearances
  • わざとらしい: forced; obviously contrived — focuses on unnatural behavior rather than truthfulness
