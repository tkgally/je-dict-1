湯割(ゆわ)り

ゆわり
noun
(spirits) cut with hot water
1. (spirits) cut with hot water; hot-water highball
An alcoholic drink, typically 焼酎(しょうちゅう) or whisky, diluted with hot water. Commonly drunk in winter to warm up. The base spirit is usually named: 焼酎(しょうちゅう)の湯割(ゆわ)り.
焼酎(しょうちゅう)の湯割(ゆわ)りを一杯(いっぱい)ください。
One shochu with hot water, please.
寒(さむ)い夜(よる)は湯割(ゆわ)りが体(からだ)を温(あたた)めてくれる。
On a cold night, a hot-water highball warms you up.
父(ちち)は毎晩(まいばん)芋焼酎(いもじょうちゅう)を湯割(ゆわ)りにして飲(の)んでいる。
My father drinks sweet-potato shochu cut with hot water every night.

Compound of 湯(ゆ) (hot water) + 割(わ)り (dilution). Contrasts with 水割(みずわ)り (cut with cold water) and ロック (on the rocks).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 焼酎(しょうちゅう)の湯割(ゆわ)り: shochu cut with hot water
  • 湯割(ゆわ)りで飲(の)む: to drink it as a yuwari
  • 湯割(ゆわ)りにする: to make it a yuwari

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 水割(みずわ)り: cut with cold water — same idea but cold
  • お{湯割(ゆわ)り}: polite form, used in restaurants/bars
  • ロック: on the rocks — no water, just ice
