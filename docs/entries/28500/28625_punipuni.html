ぷにぷに

ぷにぷに
adverb
soft and squishy; squashy
1. soft and squishy; squashy
Describes a texture that is soft, yielding, and pleasant to press — like a baby's cheeks, soft toys, or memory foam. Almost always used positively or affectionately.
猫(ねこ)の肉球(にくきゅう)はぷにぷにしている。
Cats' paw pads are soft and squishy.
赤(あか)ちゃんのぷにぷにのほっぺを触(さわ)りたくなる。
I want to touch the baby's soft, squishy cheeks.
最近(さいきん)運動(うんどう)不足(ぶそく)で、お腹(なか)がぷにぷにしてきた。
I haven't been exercising lately, and my belly is getting squishy.

Onomatopoeia for a soft, pliable, slightly chubby texture. Casual and affectionate in tone; often used about pets, babies, and cute toys, occasionally self-deprecatingly about one's own body.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぷにぷにの肉球(にくきゅう): squishy paw pads
  • ぷにぷにした触(さわ)り心地(ごこち): a squishy feel
  • ぷにぷに感(かん): a squishy feel/sensation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぷるぷる: jiggly; quivering — wobbles when moved (water-based softness)
  • ふわふわ: fluffy; soft and airy — emphasizes lightness, not squishability
  • もちもち: chewy and elastic — bounces back when pressed; firmer than ぷにぷに
