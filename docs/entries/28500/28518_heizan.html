1. end of the climbing season; closing of a mountain to climbers
The official end of the period during which a mountain (especially a popular peak like 富士山(ふじさん)) is open to climbers. Marks the seasonal cutoff after which trails, huts, and facilities are closed for winter.
富士山(ふじさん)の閉山(へいざん)は9月(がつ)初(はじ)めだ。
Mt. Fuji's climbing season ends in early September.
閉山(へいざん)後(ご)は登山道(とざんどう)が通(つう)行(こう)止(ど)めになる。
After the season ends, the climbing trails are closed off.
閉山(へいざん)前(まえ)に頂上(ちょうじょう)まで登(のぼ)っておきたい。
I want to climb to the summit before the season ends.
2. mine closure; shutting down of a mine
The closing down of a mining operation, whether temporarily or permanently. Often appears in economic and historical contexts.
鉱山(こうざん)の閉山(へいざん)で町(まち)の人口(じんこう)が減(へ)った。
The town's population fell after the mine was closed.
採算(さいさん)が合(あ)わず閉山(へいざん)を決(き)めた。
They decided to close the mine because it was not profitable.
戦後(せんご)、多(おお)くの炭鉱(たんこう)が閉山(へいざん)に追(お)い込(こ)まれた。
After the war, many coal mines were forced to close.

Compound of 閉(へい) (close) + 山(ざん) (mountain). The opposite of 開山(かいざん) (opening of the climbing season, or opening of a new mine).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 閉山(へいざん)時期(じき): end-of-season period
  • 閉山(へいざん)式(しき): closing ceremony (for climbing season)
  • 鉱山(こうざん)の閉山(へいざん): mine closure
  • 炭鉱(たんこう)閉山(へいざん): coal mine closure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開山(かいざん): opening of the climbing season; opening of a mine — antonym
  • 山開(やまびら)き: ceremonial opening of the climbing season (more colloquial)
  • 廃坑(はいこう): abandoned mine — emphasizes the abandoned state
