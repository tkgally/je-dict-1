最寄(もよ)り

もより
noun
nearest; closest
1. nearest; closest (especially of a station, store, or facility)
The one geographically closest to a given location, usually one's home, workplace, or current spot. Almost always used attributively before a noun, especially 駅(えき) (station).
最寄(もよ)りの駅(えき)はどこですか。
What's the nearest station?
最寄(もよ)りのコンビニまで歩(ある)いて五分(ごふん)です。
It's a five-minute walk to the nearest convenience store.
詳(くわ)しくは最寄(もよ)りの窓口(まどぐち)までお問(と)い合(あ)わせください。
For details, please contact your nearest service counter.

Almost exclusively used attributively, in the pattern 最寄(もよ)りの + noun. Note the irregular reading もより (not さいき or さいよ). One of the most useful directions/location words for daily life in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最寄(もよ)りの駅(えき): nearest station
  • 最寄(もよ)りのコンビニ: nearest convenience store
  • 最寄(もよ)りの店舗(てんぽ): nearest store/branch
  • 最寄(もよ)りの交番(こうばん): nearest police box
  • 最寄(もよ)り駅(えき): home station; nearest station (as a noun compound)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一番(いちばん)近(ちか)い: the closest — more conversational, can describe distance generally
  • 近(ちか)くの: nearby — looser; doesn't specifically mean the nearest one
