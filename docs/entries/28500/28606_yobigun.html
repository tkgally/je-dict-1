予備軍(よびぐん)

よびぐん
noun
reserve army; (figurative) at-risk group; pool of potential cases
1. reserves; reserve army
Literally, military reserves — soldiers held back from active deployment but available if needed.
予備軍(よびぐん)を投入(とうにゅう)した。
They sent in the reserves.
予備軍(よびぐん)の兵士(へいし)が招集(しょうしゅう)された。
Reserve soldiers were called up.
両国(りょうこく)は緊張(きんちょう)緩和(かんわ)のため、予備軍(よびぐん)の削減(さくげん)に合意(ごうい)した。
The two countries agreed to reduce their reserve forces to ease tensions.
2. at-risk group; borderline cases; pool of potential X
Figurative and now far more common: a group of people not yet diagnosed or classified as X, but on track or at risk to become so. Standard in health and social-policy reporting.
糖尿病(とうにょうびょう)予備軍(よびぐん)と言(い)われた。
I was told I'm at risk of becoming diabetic.
若者(わかもの)の失業者(しつぎょうしゃ)予備軍(よびぐん)が増(ふ)えている。
The pool of young people at risk of unemployment is growing.
健康(けんこう)診断(しんだん)の結果(けっか)、高血圧(こうけつあつ)予備軍(よびぐん)に分類(ぶんるい)されたので食事(しょくじ)に気(き)をつけている。
My checkup classified me as at risk for high blood pressure, so I'm watching my diet.

Compound of 予備(よび) (reserve/spare) + 軍(ぐん) (army/group). The figurative sense (sense 2) attaches to disease names, social categories, and economic statuses, naming a group not yet in the category but heading toward it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (sense 2):

  • 糖尿病(とうにょうびょう)予備軍(よびぐん): pre-diabetic / at risk of diabetes
  • 高血圧(こうけつあつ)予備軍(よびぐん): at risk of hypertension
  • 認知症(にんちしょう)予備軍(よびぐん): at risk of dementia / MCI
  • メタボ予備軍(よびぐん): at risk of metabolic syndrome
  • ニート予備軍(よびぐん): at risk of becoming NEET

USAGE:
In medical writing, 予備軍(よびぐん) often corresponds to English 'pre-' prefixes (pre-diabetic, pre-hypertension) or 'at risk of'.
