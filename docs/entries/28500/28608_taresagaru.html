1. to hang down; to dangle; to droop
For a thin or flexible object — a rope, hair, branch, sleeve, drop of liquid — to extend downward under its own weight. Intransitive.
ロープが垂(た)れ下(さ)がっている。
A rope is hanging down.
長(なが)い髪(かみ)が肩(かた)まで垂(た)れ下(さ)がっている。
Her long hair hangs down to her shoulders.
雪(ゆき)の重(おも)みで松(まつ)の枝(えだ)が地面(じめん)まで垂(た)れ下(さ)がっている。
Under the weight of the snow, the pine branches are hanging down to the ground.

Compound of 垂(た)れる (to hang/drip) + 下(さ)がる (to descend). Intransitive, describing a state or process — usually paired with ている for the resulting state.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The transitive counterpart is 垂(た)らす (to let dangle, to hang down) or the compound 垂(た)れ下(さ)げる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 髪(かみ)が垂(た)れ下(さ)がる: hair hangs down
  • 枝(えだ)が垂(た)れ下(さ)がる: branches droop
  • 汗(あせ)が垂(た)れ下(さ)がる: sweat trickles down
  • 紐(ひも)が垂(た)れ下(さ)がる: a string dangles

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 垂(た)れる: to hang, to drip — more general; often used for liquids
  • ぶら{下(さ)がる}: to dangle — emphasizes hanging from a fixed point, often with swinging motion
