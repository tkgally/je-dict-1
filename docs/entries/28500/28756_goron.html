ごろん

ごろん
adverb
with a thud; flopping down
1. with a thud; flopping down (lying or rolling)
Onomatopoeia for a single, heavy action of lying down or rolling over. Captures the moment of dropping onto a surface, as opposed to the continuous ごろごろ.
ごろんと横(よこ)になった。
I flopped down on my side.
子(こ)猫(ねこ)がごろんと床(ゆか)に転(ころ)がった。
The kitten rolled over onto the floor with a flop.
疲(つか)れてベッドにごろんと倒(たお)れ込(こ)んだ。
Worn out, I flopped down onto the bed.

Mimetic adverb for a single, deliberate act of flopping down or rolling onto one's side. Often paired with する or なる, and very commonly with 横(よこ)になる (to lie down on one's side).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ごろんと横(よこ)になる: to flop down onto one's side
  • ごろんと寝転(ねころ)がる: to plop down and lie sprawled
  • ごろんと倒(たお)れる: to topple over with a thud

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ごろごろ: rolling/rumbling continuously, or lazing about — repeated/ongoing rather than a single action
  • ごろり: nearly identical to ごろん, slightly more literary
  • どさっと: heavy drop with weight — more about the impact than the rolling motion
