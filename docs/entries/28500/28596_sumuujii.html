スムージー

すむーじー
noun
smoothie
1. smoothie; blended fruit/vegetable drink
A thick blended drink made from fruit or vegetables, often with yogurt, milk, or ice. The term and the drink itself spread in Japan from the late 1990s as part of the health-food boom.
朝食(ちょうしょく)にスムージーを飲(の)む。
I drink a smoothie for breakfast.
バナナと豆乳(とうにゅう)でスムージーを作(つく)った。
I made a smoothie with banana and soy milk.
毎朝(まいあさ)緑黄色(りょくおうしょく)野菜(やさい)のスムージーを飲(の)むようにしている。
I try to drink a green vegetable smoothie every morning.

From English 'smoothie'. Often associated with health and beauty trends.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • スムージーを作(つく)る: to make a smoothie
  • 野菜(やさい)スムージー: vegetable smoothie
  • グリーンスムージー: green smoothie (leafy-vegetable based)
  • スムージーボウル: smoothie bowl

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ジュース: juice — typically strained; thinner than a smoothie
  • ミキサー{ジュース}: blender drink — older term, now usually called スムージー when fruit-/veg-based
