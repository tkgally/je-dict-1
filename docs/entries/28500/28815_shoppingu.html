1. shopping; going out to shop
The leisure activity of going to stores, malls, or browsing online to buy clothing, accessories, gifts, and similar items. Carries a more recreational nuance than the everyday 買(か)い物(もの).
ショッピングに行(い)く。
I'm going shopping.
友達(ともだち)とショッピングを楽(たの)しんだ。
I enjoyed shopping with my friends.
週末(しゅうまつ)は家族(かぞく)で大(おお)きなショッピングモールに出(で)かけた。
On the weekend, my family went out to a large shopping mall.

From English 'shopping'. Often appears in compounds for retail facilities. Carries a recreational, browsing nuance — for routine errands (groceries, household items), 買(か)い物(もの) is more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ショッピングに行(い)く: to go shopping
  • ショッピングをする: to shop; to go shopping
  • ショッピングモール: shopping mall
  • ショッピングセンター: shopping center
  • ネットショッピング: online shopping

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 買(か)い物(もの): shopping; errands — more everyday, includes groceries and necessities
  • ウィンドウショッピング: window shopping — browsing without buying
