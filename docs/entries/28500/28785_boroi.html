1. shabby; worn out; tattered; run-down
Describes something that is old, worn, and in poor condition — clothes, buildings, vehicles, equipment. Casual and somewhat blunt in tone.
このアパートはぼろい。
This apartment is run-down.
ぼろい車(くるま)だけど、まだ走(はし)る。
It's a beat-up car, but it still runs.
祖父(そふ)は今(いま)でもぼろい自転車(じてんしゃ)を大事(だいじ)に乗(の)っている。
My grandfather still carefully rides his beat-up old bicycle.

Adjective form of the noun ぼろ (rag, tatters). Carries a casual, sometimes affectionate, sometimes dismissive tone. Best avoided in polite descriptions of someone else's belongings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぼろい家(いえ): a run-down house
  • ぼろい車(くるま): a beat-up car
  • ぼろい服(ふく): tattered clothes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 古(ふる)い: old — neutral; doesn't imply poor condition
  • ぼろぼろ: in tatters; falling apart — onomatopoeic, even more emphatic than ぼろい
  • 老朽化(ろうきゅうか)した: deteriorated, dilapidated — formal equivalent used for buildings
