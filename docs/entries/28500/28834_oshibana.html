押(お)し花(ばな)

おしばな
noun
pressed flower; pressed-flower craft
1. pressed flower; flower-pressing craft
Flowers or leaves dried flat under weight, used for cards, framed art, and craft. Also the craft of making them — a popular hobby for children and a long-standing decorative art.
押(お)し花(ばな)を作(つく)った。
I made pressed flowers.
本(ほん)の間(あいだ)に挟(はさ)んで押(お)し花(ばな)にした。
I pressed it between the pages of a book to make a pressed flower.
祖母(そぼ)は押(お)し花(ばな)で作(つく)ったしおりを大切(たいせつ)に保管(ほかん)していた。
My grandmother carefully kept the bookmark she had made from pressed flowers.

押(お)し (pressing) + 花(はな) (flower); the second element voices to ばな in the compound. A classic summer-vacation craft project for elementary school children.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 押(お)し花(ばな)を作(つく)る: to make pressed flowers
  • 押(お)し花(ばな)にする: to turn into a pressed flower
  • 押(お)し花(ばな)のしおり: a pressed-flower bookmark
  • 押(お)し花(ばな)教室(きょうしつ): pressed-flower class

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ドライフラワー: dried flowers — air-dried, three-dimensional, not pressed flat
  • 標本(ひょうほん): specimen — a more scientific term, for plants preserved for study
