1. to cut down (with a sword or blade); to slash; to slay
To strike or cut with a sword or other bladed weapon, especially as an act of combat. The 斬(き) kanji distinguishes this from the general 切(き)る (to cut).
刀(かたな)で斬(き)る。
To cut with a sword.
敵(てき)を斬(き)り倒(たお)した。
He cut down the enemy.
侍(さむらい)が一刀両断(いっとうりょうだん)に相手(あいて)を斬(き)った。
The samurai cut down his opponent with a single stroke.
2. to criticize sharply; to lambast
Figurative use: to attack or criticize with cutting words. Most common in journalism and commentary.
政府(せいふ)の対応(たいおう)を斬(き)る。
To take a sharp swipe at the government's response.
評論家(ひょうろんか)が時事(じじ)問題(もんだい)を鋭(するど)く斬(き)った。
The commentator sharply critiqued current affairs.
この番組(ばんぐみ)はゲストが社会(しゃかい)問題(もんだい)をユーモアで斬(き)る内容(ないよう)だ。
This show features guests humorously skewering social issues.

Same reading as 切(き)る (to cut) but a different kanji that limits the meaning to cutting with a blade — historically in combat. Sense 2 (sharp criticism) is widely used in TV-show titles, headlines, and column names.

TRANSITIVITY:

Transitive: marks what is struck with を.

  • 敵(てき)を斬(き)る: to cut down the enemy
  • 問題(もんだい)を斬(き)る (figurative): to skewer an issue

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 刀(かたな)で斬(き)る: to cut with a sword
  • 斬(き)り倒(たお)す: to cut down (someone)
  • 斬(き)り捨(す)てる: to cut down and abandon; to dismiss outright
  • 一刀両断(いっとうりょうだん)に斬(き)る: to cut clean through in one stroke

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(き)る: to cut — general-purpose cutting, no combat connotation
  • 斬殺(ざんさつ)する: to slay (with a blade) — formal compound for killing by sword
  • ぶった{斬(き)る}: to slash (vigorous, casual) — colloquial form often used figuratively for sharp criticism
