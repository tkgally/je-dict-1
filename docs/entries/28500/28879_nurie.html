塗(ぬ)り絵(え)

ぬりえ
noun
coloring picture; coloring book page
1. coloring picture; coloring book page
A pre-printed outline drawing meant to be filled in with colored pencils, markers, or paints. Also refers to the activity itself. Long popular as a children's pastime; adult coloring books became widely popular in the 2010s.
子供(こども)が塗(ぬ)り絵(え)をしている。
The child is doing coloring.
大人(おとな)向(む)けの塗(ぬ)り絵(え)本(ぼん)が流行(はや)っている。
Adult coloring books are popular.
塗(ぬ)り絵(え)は集中力(しゅうちゅうりょく)を高(たか)め、ストレス解消(かいしょう)にもなると言(い)われている。
Coloring is said to improve concentration and also relieve stress.

Compound of 塗(ぬ)る (to paint, to coat) + 絵(え) (picture). The verb stem connects directly to the noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塗(ぬ)り絵(え)をする: to do coloring
  • 塗(ぬ)り絵(え)本(ぼん): coloring book
  • 大人(おとな)の塗(ぬ)り絵(え): adult coloring (book/page)
  • キャラクターの塗(ぬ)り絵(え): character coloring pages

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
A standard preschool and early-elementary activity. The adult-coloring boom in Japan, partly imported from Europe, brought intricate mandala-style and floral books to mainstream bookstores. 塗(ぬ)り絵(え)療法(りょうほう) (coloring therapy) is sometimes used in mental-health and elderly-care settings.
