1. to drizzle in a circular motion; to pour around
To pour a liquid (oil, sauce, dressing) over food while moving the container in a circle so the liquid covers the surface evenly. Common cooking instruction.
ごま油(あぶら)を回(まわ)しかける。
Drizzle sesame oil around in a circle.
最後(さいご)にしょうゆを回(まわ)しかけて火(ひ)を止(と)める。
At the end, drizzle soy sauce around and turn off the heat.
溶(と)き卵(たまご)をフライパンの中央(ちゅうおう)から外側(そとがわ)に向(む)けて回(まわ)しかける。
Pour the beaten egg in a circular motion from the center of the pan toward the outside.

Compound of 回(まわ)す (to turn) + かける (to pour over). Almost exclusively a cooking term, found in recipes. Conveys both the action of pouring and the circular motion that distributes the liquid evenly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ごま油(あぶら)を回(まわ)しかける: to drizzle sesame oil
  • しょうゆを回(まわ)しかける: to drizzle soy sauce
  • 溶(と)き卵(たまご)を回(まわ)しかける: to pour beaten egg in a circle
  • ドレッシングを回(まわ)しかける: to drizzle dressing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • かける: to pour over — general; no implication of circular motion
  • 注(そそ)ぐ: to pour — into a container, not over food
