込(こ)み

こみ
suffix
including; ~ included
1. including; ~ included; inclusive of
Suffix attached to nouns to mean 'including ~' or 'with ~ included'. Most often seen in prices (税込(ぜいこみ), 送料込(そうりょうこみ)), but also used for general bundling.
送料(そうりょう)込(こ)みで5000円(えん)です。
5,000 yen, shipping included.
サービス料込(りょうこ)みの料金(りょうきん)を表示(ひょうじ)しています。
Prices are shown with the service charge included.
彼(かれ)の欠点(けってん)も込(こ)みで好(す)きだと彼女(かのじょ)は言(い)った。
She said she loves him, his flaws included.

Noun-suffix form of the verb 込(こ)む 'to include, to be packed in'. Attaches directly to a noun to mean 'with ~ included in the total'. Very productive in pricing and packaging contexts.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 税込(ぜいこみ): tax included
  • 送料込(そうりょうこみ): shipping included
  • 込(こ)みの値段(ねだん): the all-in price
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)込(こ)み: all-inclusive

USAGE:
Used both as a suffix (税込(ぜいこみ)) and as a standalone modifier after particles (税(ぜい)・送料込(そうりょうこみ)で). The opposite is 別(べつ) (separate), as in 税別(ぜいべつ) (tax not included).
