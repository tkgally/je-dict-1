教会式(きょうかいしき)

きょうかいしき
noun
church wedding; Christian-style wedding ceremony
1. church wedding; Christian-style wedding ceremony
A wedding performed in a Christian church or church-style chapel, with vows, an exchange of rings, and a minister officiating. The most popular wedding style in modern Japan, often chosen regardless of religion.
彼(かれ)らは教会式(きょうかいしき)で結婚(けっこん)した。
They had a church wedding.
教会式(きょうかいしき)の衣装(いしょう)はウェディングドレスが定番(ていばん)だ。
A wedding dress is the standard attire for a church wedding.
日本(にほん)では宗教(しゅうきょう)に関係(かんけい)なく教会式(きょうかいしき)を選(えら)ぶカップルが多(おお)い。
In Japan, many couples choose a church wedding regardless of their religion.

Compound of 教会(きょうかい) (church) + 式(しき) (ceremony). In Japan this often refers to a Western-style chapel inside a wedding venue rather than an actual church congregation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 教会式(きょうかいしき)を挙(あ)げる: to hold a church wedding
  • 教会式(きょうかいしき)の誓(ちか)いの言葉(ことば): the vows of a church wedding
  • チャペル式(しき): chapel-style ceremony (near-synonym, often used by venues)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 神前式(しんぜんしき): Shinto wedding — performed at a shrine
  • 人前式(じんぜんしき): secular wedding — non-religious, with guests as witnesses
