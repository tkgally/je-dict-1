向(む)かって

むかって
expression
facing; toward; addressed to
1. facing; toward; addressed to (someone)
The て form of 向(む)かう (to face, to head toward), used like a postposition: ～に向(む)かって 'toward, facing, addressed at'. Marks the direction of motion, of attention, or of speech.
駅(えき)に向(む)かって歩(ある)いた。
I walked toward the station.
窓(まど)に向(む)かって座(すわ)っている。
I'm sitting facing the window.
上司(じょうし)に向(む)かってそんな口(くち)調(ちょう)で話(はな)してはいけない。
You shouldn't speak to your boss in that tone.
未来(みらい)に向(む)かって一歩(いっぽ)踏(ふ)み出(だ)そう。
Let's take a step toward the future.

Functions almost as a postposition. The full pattern is Xに}{向(む)かって — the に is required.

COMMON USES:

  • Physical direction: 北(きた)に向(む)かって進(すす)む (head north)
  • Facing position: 鏡(かがみ)に向(む)かって (facing the mirror)
  • Target of speech or action: みんなに向(む)かって叫(さけ)んだ (shouted at everyone)
  • Abstract goal: 目標(もくひょう)に向(む)かって (toward a goal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ～に対(たい)して: against; toward — more abstract, often for stance or attitude
  • ～の方(ほう)へ: in the direction of — less directional intent than 向(む)かって
  • ～へ: to; toward — bare directional particle without the 'facing' nuance
