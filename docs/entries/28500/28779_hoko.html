矛(ほこ)

ほこ
noun
spear; halberd; pike
1. spear; halberd; pike
An ancient pole weapon with a blade or pointed head, used in Japan and elsewhere before firearms. Largely a historical or literary term in modern Japanese.
古代(こだい)の武器(ぶき)として矛(ほこ)が使(つか)われた。
Spears were used as ancient weapons.
祭(まつ)りの山車(だし)に大(おお)きな矛(ほこ)が飾(かざ)られている。
A large halberd is displayed on the festival float.
議論(ぎろん)の矛(ほこ)を相手(あいて)の失言(しつげん)に向(む)けた。
He turned the spear of his argument toward his opponent's slip of the tongue.

In modern Japanese, 矛(ほこ) is rarely used as a physical noun outside historical or festival contexts. It appears most often in set phrases and idioms.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 矛(ほこ)を収(おさ)める: to end a fight; to lay down arms (literally, to sheathe the spear)
  • 矛(ほこ)を向(む)ける: to turn one's attack toward; to target with criticism
  • 矛(ほこ)先(さき): the point of attack; the brunt of an argument or criticism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 槍(やり): spear — the everyday word for a spear; more concrete and still occasionally used
  • 刀(かたな): sword — different category of weapon, with a long curved blade
