1. to dodge; to evade (a physical attack)
To move out of the way of an oncoming object or attack — a punch, ball, vehicle, or similar. Implies quick, deliberate avoidance rather than passive escape.
パンチをかわした。
I dodged the punch.
ボクサーは相手(あいて)の攻撃(こうげき)を巧(たく)みにかわした。
The boxer skillfully dodged his opponent's attack.
急(きゅう)に飛(と)び出(だ)してきた自転車(じてんしゃ)を何(なん)とかかわすことができた。
I just barely managed to dodge a bicycle that suddenly darted out.
2. to deflect; to dodge (a question, responsibility)
To avoid an awkward question, criticism, or unwanted topic by giving a vague or off-topic response. The figurative extension of the physical dodge.
質問(しつもん)をかわした。
He sidestepped the question.
首相(しゅしょう)は記者(きしゃ)の追及(ついきゅう)をうまくかわした。
The prime minister skillfully deflected the reporters' probing.
彼(かれ)はいつも冗談(じょうだん)で真剣(しんけん)な話題(わだい)をかわそうとする。
He always tries to dodge serious topics by making jokes.

Transitive godan verb (intransitive pair: かわる is unrelated; this verb stands alone). The kanji form 躱(かわ)す exists but is uncommon — usually written in kana. Both physical and figurative senses are extremely common.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • {〜を}かわす: to dodge X (always transitive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 攻撃(こうげき)をかわす: to dodge an attack
  • パンチをかわす: to dodge a punch
  • 質問(しつもん)をかわす: to deflect a question
  • 追及(ついきゅう)をかわす: to dodge investigation/probing
  • 責任(せきにん)をかわす: to evade responsibility

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 避(さ)ける: to avoid — broader; can mean staying away from something in advance
  • 逃(に)げる: to flee — to run away; かわす implies dodging while staying in place
  • かわす (交(か)わす): a homograph in kanji meaning "to exchange (words, glances)" — different verb
