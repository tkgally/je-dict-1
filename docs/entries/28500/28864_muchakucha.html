むちゃくちゃ

むちゃくちゃ
adjective-na
absurd; chaotic; extremely
1. unreasonable; absurd; chaotic; messy
Describes something that is disorderly, illogical, or out of control. As a na-adjective it characterizes a situation, plan, or behavior; as an adverb it intensifies.
そんなのむちゃくちゃだ。
That's absurd.
部屋(へや)がむちゃくちゃになっている。
The room is a complete mess.
彼(かれ)の計画(けいかく)はむちゃくちゃで、実現(じつげん)できそうにない。
His plan is so unreasonable that it doesn't seem feasible.
2. extremely; really; super (casual intensifier)
Used adverbially in casual speech to mean 'extremely' or 'really.' Functionally equivalent to とても but much more colloquial.
むちゃくちゃおいしい！
It's incredibly delicious!
今日(きょう)はむちゃくちゃ忙(いそが)しかった。
Today was crazy busy.
このゲームはむちゃくちゃ面白(おもしろ)くて、時間(じかん)を忘(わす)れてしまった。
This game is so much fun that I lost track of time.

A casual variant of めちゃくちゃ. Common in spoken Japanese; in writing, めちゃくちゃ is slightly more standard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • むちゃくちゃな話(はなし): an absurd story
  • むちゃくちゃに怒(おこ)る: to be furious
  • むちゃくちゃ楽(たの)しい: incredibly fun
  • むちゃくちゃ食(た)べる: to eat a huge amount

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • めちゃくちゃ: same meaning, slightly more common in writing
  • むちゃ: rash; reckless — shorter form, focuses more on unreasonableness
  • とても: very — neutral; not casual

REGISTER:
Casual. Avoid in formal writing or polite speech. Sense 2 (the intensifier) is especially colloquial.
