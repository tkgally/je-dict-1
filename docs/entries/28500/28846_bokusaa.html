ボクサー

ぼくさー
noun
boxer (athlete)
1. boxer; (professional) prizefighter
A person who practices the sport of boxing, either as an amateur or a professional prizefighter. From the English word "boxer."
彼(かれ)は有名(ゆうめい)なボクサーだ。
He is a famous boxer.
プロボクサーになるには厳(きび)しい練習(れんしゅう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Becoming a professional boxer requires intense training.
そのボクサーは世界(せかい)王座(おうざ)を三度(さんど)防衛(ぼうえい)した。
That boxer defended his world title three times.

Loanword from English "boxer." Refers only to the athlete, not the dog breed or men's underwear (those are usually distinguished by context — see below).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プロボクサー: professional boxer
  • アマチュアボクサー: amateur boxer
  • 女性(じょせい)ボクサー: female boxer
  • ボクサー出身(しゅっしん): with a boxing background

OTHER MEANINGS OF ボクサー:

In other contexts ボクサー can also refer to:

  • The dog breed ("Boxer"), usually clarified as ボクサー犬(けん).
  • ボクサーパンツ (boxer shorts/briefs) for men's underwear.

The standalone word ボクサー most often means the athlete.
