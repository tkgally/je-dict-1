十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)

じゅうさんかいき
noun
thirteenth death anniversary memorial service
1. thirteenth death anniversary; memorial service held in the twelfth year after a death
A Buddhist memorial service marking the thirteenth observance of someone's death, held twelve calendar years after the death (counted inclusively).
母(はは)の十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)を迎(むか)えた。
We reached my mother's thirteenth-year memorial.
今年(ことし)は祖父(そふ)の十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)の年(とし)だ。
This is the year of my grandfather's thirteenth-year memorial.
十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)は家族(かぞく)だけで静(しず)かに執(と)り行(おこな)った。
We held the thirteenth-year memorial quietly with just family.

Like other 回忌(かいき), counted inclusively: the death year is year 1, so 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき) falls twelve calendar years after the death. By this stage attendance is usually limited to immediate family.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)法要(ほうよう): thirteenth-year memorial service
  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)を営(いとな)む: to hold the thirteenth-year service
  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき)を迎(むか)える: for the thirteenth-year memorial to come around

MEMORIAL SERVICE SEQUENCE:

  • 七回忌(ななかいき): 6 years after death
  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき): 12 years after death
  • 十七回忌(じゅうしちかいき): 16 years after death
  • 三十三回忌(さんじゅうさんかいき): 32 years after death (often the final service)
