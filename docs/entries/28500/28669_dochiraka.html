どちらか

どちらか
pronoun
one or the other; either one; one of (two things)
1. one or the other (of two options); either one
Combines どちら (which of two) with the indefinite particle か. Picks out an unspecified one from a pair — usually translated as 'either one', 'one of the two', or 'whichever'.
どちらか選(えら)んでください。
Please pick one of the two.
どちらかと言(い)えば紅茶(こうちゃ)の方(ほう)が好(す)きだ。
If I had to choose, I'd say I prefer black tea.
紙(かみ)か電子版(でんしばん)か、どちらかをお選(えら)びいただけます。
You can choose either the paper or the digital version.

Refers to a pair, not three or more. Use どれか for three or more options. The casual contraction どっちか is extremely common in speech.

FIXED EXPRESSIONS:

  • どちらかと言(い)えば: if I had to say which; if anything
  • どちらかと言(い)うと: if one had to choose between them
  • どちらか一方(いっぽう): one or the other (of the two)
  • どちらか選(えら)ぶ: to choose between two

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どちらも: both — covers the pair
  • どちらでも: either is fine; any one will do
  • どれか: any one of (three or more)
  • どっちか: casual contracted form
