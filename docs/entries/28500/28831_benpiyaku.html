便秘薬(べんぴやく)

べんぴやく
noun
laxative; constipation medicine
1. laxative; constipation medicine (over the counter)
Medicine taken to relieve constipation. The everyday, consumer-facing word — packaging at pharmacies and TV ads typically use this term rather than the medical 下剤(げざい).
便秘薬(べんぴやく)を飲(の)んだ。
I took a laxative.
薬局(やっきょく)で便秘薬(べんぴやく)を買(か)った。
I bought a laxative at the pharmacy.
便秘薬(べんぴやく)は毎日(まいにち)使(つか)わずに、食生活(しょくせいかつ)の改善(かいぜん)を先(さき)にしたほうがいい。
Rather than using a laxative every day, it's better to improve your diet first.

便秘(べんぴ) (constipation) + 薬(やく) (medicine). The everyday consumer term — pharmacy shelves and TV commercials use this rather than the medical-register 下剤(げざい).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 便秘薬(べんぴやく)を飲(の)む: to take a laxative
  • 便秘薬(べんぴやく)に頼(たよ)る: to rely on laxatives
  • 市販(しはん)の便秘薬(べんぴやく): over-the-counter laxative

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下剤(げざい): laxative — medical register; used in clinical contexts
  • 整腸(せいちょう)剤(ざい): digestive-regulating medicine — broader, also for diarrhea
  • 便秘(べんぴ): constipation (the condition itself)
