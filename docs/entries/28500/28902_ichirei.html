一例(いちれい)

いちれい
noun
an example; one instance
1. an example; one instance
One example among many; a single illustrative case. Used in writing and presentation to introduce a specific instance that demonstrates a broader pattern.
一例(いちれい)を挙(あ)げる。
To give one example.
これはほんの一例(いちれい)にすぎない。
This is just one example among many.
成功(せいこう)したケースの一例(いちれい)として、彼(かれ)の会社(かいしゃ)が紹介(しょうかい)された。
As one example of a successful case, his company was introduced.

Compound of 一(いち) (one) + 例(れい) (example). Slightly more formal than just 例(れい), with the nuance of "just one of many." Often used in writing to soften a single example into something representative rather than exhaustive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一例(いちれい)を挙(あ)げる: to give an example
  • 一例(いちれい)として: as one example
  • ほんの一例(いちれい): just one example (often "merely one example among many")
  • 一例(いちれい)にすぎない: is only one example

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 例(れい): example — the everyday word, no "just one" nuance
  • 具体例(ぐたいれい): concrete example — emphasizes specificity
  • 事例(じれい): case (study); instance — used for documented examples in research/reports
