すっぱり

すっぱり
adverb
cleanly; completely; decisively
1. cleanly (cutting); in one clean stroke
Describes a clean, decisive cut — like one sword stroke or a single snip — with no jagged edge or hesitation.
紙(かみ)をすっぱりと切(き)る。
To cut the paper cleanly.
枝(えだ)をすっぱり切(き)り落(お)とした。
He lopped the branch off in one clean stroke.
包丁(ほうちょう)は切(き)れ味(あじ)がよく、トマトをすっぱりと二(ふた)つに切(き)れた。
The knife was sharp and cut the tomato cleanly in two.
2. completely (quitting, giving up); cleanly (breaking off)
Figurative extension: making a clean break with a habit, a relationship, or a desire — quitting once and for all, without lingering attachment. The most common modern usage.
タバコをすっぱりやめた。
I quit smoking for good.
彼(かれ)とはすっぱり別(わか)れた。
I made a clean break with him.
過去(かこ)のことはすっぱり忘(わす)れて新(あたら)しい生活(せいかつ)を始(はじ)めよう。
Let's put the past completely behind us and start a new life.

Most often used today with verbs of quitting or breaking off (やめる, 別(わか)れる, 忘(わす)れる) to emphasize a decisive, no-residue break. Frequently appears with と as すっぱりと.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すっぱりやめる: to quit cleanly, for good
  • すっぱり諦(あきら)める: to give up completely
  • すっぱり忘(わす)れる: to put completely out of one's mind
  • すっぱり別(わか)れる: to make a clean break
  • すっぱり切(き)る: to cut cleanly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きっぱり: flatly; firmly — focuses on the decisiveness of refusal or declaration
  • スパッと: in one clean stroke — very similar; also covers both cutting and quitting
  • きれいに: cleanly; neatly — broader, also covers tidiness, not just decisiveness
