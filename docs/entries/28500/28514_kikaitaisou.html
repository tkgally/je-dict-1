器械体操(きかいたいそう)

きかいたいそう
noun
apparatus gymnastics; artistic gymnastics
1. apparatus gymnastics; artistic gymnastics
Competitive gymnastics performed on apparatus such as the vault, parallel bars, rings, horizontal bar, balance beam, and uneven bars. Distinguished from rhythmic gymnastics (新体操(しんたいそう)) and general floor-based exercise.
娘(むすめ)は器械体操(きかいたいそう)を習(なら)っている。
My daughter is learning artistic gymnastics.
彼(かれ)は器械体操(きかいたいそう)の選手(せんしゅ)としてオリンピックに出場(しゅつじょう)した。
He competed in the Olympics as an artistic gymnast.
器械体操(きかいたいそう)では鉄棒(てつぼう)や平行棒(へいこうぼう)などの種目(しゅもく)がある。
Artistic gymnastics has events such as the horizontal bar and parallel bars.

Compound of 器械(きかい) (apparatus) + 体操(たいそう) (gymnastics, exercise). Refers to the form of gymnastics seen at the Olympics, performed on six (men's) or four (women's) pieces of apparatus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 器械体操(きかいたいそう)の選手(せんしゅ): artistic gymnast
  • 器械体操(きかいたいそう)部(ぶ): gymnastics club
  • 器械体操(きかいたいそう)の大会(たいかい): gymnastics competition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新体操(しんたいそう): rhythmic gymnastics — uses ribbon, ball, hoop, rope, or clubs
  • 体操(たいそう): gymnastics, calisthenics — general term, includes everyday exercise
  • 床運動(ゆかうんどう): floor exercise — one event within artistic gymnastics
