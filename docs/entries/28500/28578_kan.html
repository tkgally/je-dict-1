〜感(かん)

かん
suffix
feel; sense; impression (suffix)
1. feel; sense; impression; quality of being ~
Suffix attached to nouns and na-adjective stems to mean 'a feeling/sense of ~' or 'the quality of being ~'. Highly productive in both formal writing and casual speech, especially in product descriptions, reviews, and impressions.
透明感(とうめいかん)のある肌(はだ)。
Skin with a clear, translucent feel.
この部屋(へや)は清潔感(せいけつかん)があっていい。
This room has a clean feel to it — I like it.
彼(かれ)の演技(えんぎ)にはすごい存在感(そんざいかん)がある。
There's an incredible sense of presence in his acting.

Productive suffix meaning 'a feeling or sense of ~'. Attaches to a noun or na-adjective stem to turn it into a noun describing the impression conveyed.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 透明感(とうめいかん): sense of transparency/clarity
  • 存在感(そんざいかん): sense of presence
  • 清潔感(せいけつかん): clean impression; sense of cleanliness
  • 達成感(たっせいかん): sense of achievement
  • 緊張感(きんちょうかん): sense of tension
  • 高級感(こうきゅうかん): feel of being high-class; upscale impression

USAGE:
Most compounds are used with 〜がある (has a feeling of ~) or 〜のある (with a feeling of ~). New compounds are coined freely in advertising, fashion, and product reviews.
