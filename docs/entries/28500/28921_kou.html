1. to beg; to ask for; to entreat
To earnestly request something — help, forgiveness, or mercy — from someone in a more powerful or favorable position. Literary in tone; in everyday speech replaced by 頼(たの)む or お願(ねが)いする.
許(ゆる)しを乞(こ)う。
To beg for forgiveness.
次(つぎ)回(かい)の放送(ほうそう)にご期待(きたい)を乞(こ)う。
Look forward to the next broadcast. (lit. We entreat your anticipation.)
戦争(せんそう)に敗(やぶ)れた兵(へい)は敵(てき)に命(いのち)を乞(こ)うた。
The defeated soldier begged the enemy for his life.

A literary verb appearing mostly in fixed phrases and written Japanese. Almost never used in daily conversation.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (takes を). The object is what is requested (助(たす)けを乞(こ)う).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 許(ゆる)しを乞(こ)う: to beg for forgiveness
  • 命(いのち)を乞(こ)う: to beg for one's life
  • 助(たす)けを乞(こ)う: to plead for help
  • ご期待(きたい)を乞(こ)う: please look forward to it (set TV/promotional phrase)
  • ご容赦(ようしゃ)を乞(こ)う: to ask for forbearance (formal)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頼(たの)む: to ask, to request — everyday verb for the same idea
  • お願(ねが)いする: to request (polite) — standard polite form
  • 懇願(こんがん)する: to entreat — formal Sino-Japanese verb, similar register
