美女(びじょ)

びじょ
noun
beautiful woman; beauty
1. beautiful woman; beauty
A physically attractive woman. Slightly literary or formal compared to neutral 美人(びじん); often used in set phrases, news headlines, and entertainment-industry contexts.
彼女(かのじょ)は有名(ゆうめい)な美女(びじょ)だ。
She is a famous beauty.
パーティーには美女(びじょ)が大勢(おおぜい)集(あつ)まっていた。
Many beautiful women had gathered at the party.
昔話(むかしばなし)には、王子(おうじ)が呪(のろ)いをかけられた美女(びじょ)を救(すく)う場面(ばめん)がよく出(で)てくる。
In old folk tales, scenes often appear in which a prince rescues a beautiful woman who has been put under a curse.

Slightly more literary and formal than 美人(びじん). Common in titles, headlines, idioms, and fairy-tale contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶世(ぜっせい)の美女(びじょ): a peerless beauty
  • 美女(びじょ)と野獣(やじゅう): Beauty and the Beast
  • 美女(びじょ)揃(そろ)い: a gathering of beautiful women
  • 才色兼備(さいしょくけんび)の美女(びじょ): a beauty with both talent and looks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 美人(びじん): beautiful woman — the most neutral, everyday term
  • 美少女(びしょうじょ): beautiful young girl — emphasizes youth
  • イケメン: handsome man — male counterpart in casual speech
