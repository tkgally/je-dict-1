内地(ないち)

ないち
noun
the mainland; the interior
1. the (Japanese) mainland; the main islands
In modern Japanese, mostly used by people from Hokkaido or Okinawa to refer to the main island of Honshu (and sometimes Honshu plus Shikoku and Kyushu). Roughly "the rest of Japan" from the speaker's perspective.
内地(ないち)から観光客(かんこうきゃく)が来(き)た。
Tourists came from the mainland.
沖縄(おきなわ)の人(ひと)は本州(ほんしゅう)を内地(ないち)と呼(よ)ぶ。
People in Okinawa call Honshu the mainland.
冬(ふゆ)は内地(ないち)に帰省(きせい)する道民(どうみん)が多(おお)い。
Many Hokkaido residents return to the mainland for the winter holidays.
2. the interior of a country; inland area
An inland part of a country, away from the coast or frontier. Less common; appears in some formal or older texts.
この国(くに)の内地(ないち)は乾燥(かんそう)した気候(きこう)だ。
The interior of this country has a dry climate.
港湾(こうわん)都市(とし)と内地(ないち)を結(むす)ぶ鉄道(てつどう)が整備(せいび)された。
A railway connecting the port cities to the interior was developed.
沿岸部(えんがんぶ)と比(くら)べて、内地(ないち)は気温差(きおんさ)が激(はげ)しい。
Compared to the coastal areas, the interior has a sharp temperature range.

Compound of 内(ない) (inner) + 地(ち) (land). In pre-war usage, also meant "Japan proper" as opposed to colonial territories — that usage is now historical and avoided.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内地(ないち)から来(く)る: to come from the mainland
  • 内地(ないち)に帰(かえ)る: to return to the mainland
  • 内地(ないち)勤務(きんむ): assignment on the mainland
  • 内地(ないち)留学(りゅうがく): study at a mainland school (Hokkaido/Okinawa usage)

USAGE NOTE:
The "mainland" sense is most natural from a Hokkaido or Okinawa perspective. Honshu speakers rarely call their own islands 内地(ないち); for them it just sounds like the speaker is from somewhere else.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 本土(ほんど): the mainland — neutral, can be used by anyone
  • 本州(ほんしゅう): Honshu — the specific main island
  • 外地(がいち): outer territories — historical pair of 内地(ないち)
