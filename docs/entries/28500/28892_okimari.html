お決(き)まり

おきまり
noun
the usual; a predictable pattern; one's standard order
1. the usual; a predictable pattern; one's standard order
Something fixed, predictable, or routinely the same. Often refers to a familiar pattern (an お決(き)まりのパターン) or, at restaurants and cafes, to a customer's regular order.
お決(き)まりですか。
Have you decided (on your order)?
彼(かれ)のお決(き)まりの言(い)い訳(わけ)だ。
That's his usual excuse.
日曜日(にちようび)の朝(あさ)は、コーヒーと新聞(しんぶん)がお決(き)まりのパターンだ。
Coffee and the newspaper are my usual Sunday morning pattern.

The polite-prefix form of 決(き)まり (a fixed thing, a rule). The お adds politeness, but the word is used freely in casual speech and is not strictly honorific.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お決(き)まりですか: "Have you decided?" (restaurant staff to customer)
  • お決(き)まりのパターン: the usual pattern
  • お決(き)まりのコース: the standard / set course
  • お決(き)まりの台詞(せりふ): a stock phrase; a predictable line

USAGE:
Often has a mildly humorous or affectionate tone when describing someone's predictable habits or sayings. "You always say that — お決(き)まりだね."
