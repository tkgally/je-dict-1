1. to grow accustomed to (something) through repeated learning or practice
A compound verb combining 習(なら)う (to learn) with 慣(な)れる (to get used to). Describes reaching a state where a skill or routine feels natural after enough practice.
習(なら)い慣(な)れた道(みち)を通(とお)る。
I take a route I'm used to.
毎日(まいにち)使(つか)っているので、操作(そうさ)にもう習(なら)い慣(な)れた。
I use it every day, so I've gotten used to operating it.
敬語(けいご)は習(なら)い慣(な)れるまでに時間(じかん)がかかるが、自然(しぜん)に出(で)てくるようになる。
Keigo takes time to get used to, but eventually it comes out naturally.

Compound of 習(なら)う (to learn) + 慣(な)れる (to grow accustomed). Conjugates as an ichidan verb like 慣(な)れる. The literary register is mild — common in writing about skills, languages, and work routines.

USAGE:

  • Past form most common: 習(なら)い慣(な)れた is used attributively (習(なら)い慣(な)れた道(みち), 習(なら)い慣(な)れた仕事(しごと)).
  • Predicative 習(なら)い慣(な)れる appears in writing about gradually mastering something.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 慣(な)れる: to get used to — broader and more common; covers any kind of habituation
  • 身(み)に付(つ)く: to be acquired (as a skill) — focuses on the skill becoming a permanent part of oneself
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる: to get used to using something — parallel compound for tools or systems
