内課金(ないかきん)

ないかきん
noun
in-app purchase
1. in-app purchase
Paid items, currency, or upgrades bought from within a smartphone game or app after the initial install. Almost always appears as part of the longer phrase アプリ内課金(ないかきん).
内課金(ないかきん)でアイテムを買(か)った。
I bought an item via in-app purchase.
アプリ内課金(ないかきん)に月(つき)3000円(えん)使(つか)っている。
I spend 3,000 yen a month on in-app purchases.
子(こ)どもが勝手(かって)に内課金(ないかきん)をして請求(せいきゅう)が大変(たいへん)なことになった。
My child made in-app purchases without permission and the bill turned into a disaster.

Clipped from アプリ内課金(ないかきん) (内(ない) ('inside') + 課金(かきん) ('billing/charging')). Refers especially to game purchases of virtual currency, gacha pulls, or in-game items.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アプリ内課金(ないかきん): in-app purchase (full phrase)
  • 内課金(ないかきん)でアイテムを買(か)う: to buy an item via in-app purchase
  • 内課金(ないかきん)に制限(せいげん)をかける: to set a limit on in-app purchases

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 課金(かきん): paid use; topping up — broader; covers any in-app spending
  • ガチャ: gacha — random-draw type of in-app purchase
  • 基本(きほん)無料(むりょう): free-to-play — the business model that relies on in-app purchases
