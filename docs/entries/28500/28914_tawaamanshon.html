タワーマンション

たわーまんしょん
noun
high-rise condominium; tower apartment
1. high-rise condominium; tower apartment
A condominium building of roughly 20 stories or more. Common in urban Japan since the 2000s. Often shortened to タワマン in casual speech.
都心(としん)のタワーマンションに住(す)んでいる。
I live in a high-rise condo in the city center.
タワーマンションの最上階(さいじょうかい)からの眺(なが)めは最高(さいこう)だ。
The view from the top floor of the tower apartment is amazing.
湾岸(わんがん)地区(ちく)には新(あたら)しいタワーマンションが次々(つぎつぎ)と建(た)てられている。
New high-rise condos are being built one after another in the bayside district.

Wasei-eigo from English "tower" + "mansion." In Japan, マンション refers to a condominium building, not a luxury house — see {マンション}. There is no firm legal definition, but 20 stories or more is the usual threshold.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • タワーマンションに住(す)む: to live in a high-rise condo
  • タワーマンションを購入(こうにゅう)する: to purchase a high-rise condo
  • 高層(こうそう)タワーマンション: high-rise tower apartment
  • タワマン: casual abbreviation

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Owning a high floor in a タワーマンション has become a status symbol in Tokyo and Osaka. The term タワマン民(みん) ("tower-mansion people") is sometimes used in pop culture to refer to their lifestyle.
