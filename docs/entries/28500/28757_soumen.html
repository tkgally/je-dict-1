そうめん

そうめん
noun
somen; thin wheat noodles
1. somen; thin wheat noodles (usually served cold)
Very thin Japanese wheat noodles, typically eaten cold in summer with a dipping sauce (めんつゆ}). Often written in kana, sometimes as {素麺(そうめん).
夏(なつ)はそうめんが食(た)べたくなる。
I crave somen in the summer.
そうめんを冷水(れいすい)で締(し)めた。
I rinsed the somen in cold water to firm it up.
流(なが)しそうめんは竹(たけ)の樋(とい)を流(なが)れてくる麺(めん)を箸(はし)でつかんで食(た)べる夏(なつ)の遊(あそ)びだ。
Nagashi-somen is a summer pastime where you catch flowing noodles in a bamboo flume with your chopsticks.

Very thin (about 1.3 mm or less) wheat noodles stretched with vegetable oil during production. A summer staple, usually eaten cold with a soy-based dipping sauce. Some regional varieties (e.g., 三輪(みわ)そうめん, 揖保乃糸(いぼのいと)) are well-known.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • そうめんを茹(ゆ)でる: to boil somen
  • 冷(ひ)やしそうめん: chilled somen
  • 流(なが)しそうめん: flowing-somen (summer dish)
  • そうめんつゆ: somen dipping sauce

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冷(ひ)や麦(むぎ): slightly thicker cold wheat noodles
  • うどん: much thicker wheat noodles, usually eaten hot
  • 蕎麦(そば): buckwheat noodles, eaten hot or cold
