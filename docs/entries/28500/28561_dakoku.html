1. clocking in/out; stamping the time on a record
The act of recording one's start or end of work time, traditionally by stamping a time card and now often by scanning a card or using a smartphone app.
出社(しゅっしゃ)したら打刻(だこく)してください。
Please clock in when you arrive at the office.
昨日(きのう)は退勤(たいきん)の打刻(だこく)を忘(わす)れてしまった。
Yesterday I forgot to clock out.
当社(とうしゃ)ではICカードを使(つか)って出退勤(しゅったいきん)の打刻(だこく)を行(おこな)っています。
At our company, we use IC cards to record clock-in and clock-out times.

Literally 'stamp engraving'. Originally referred to the act of physically stamping a time card; now used broadly for any electronic time-recording action. Common in workplace HR documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 打刻(だこく)する: to clock in/out
  • 出勤(しゅっきん)の打刻(だこく): clock-in (recording)
  • 退勤(たいきん)の打刻(だこく): clock-out (recording)
  • 打刻(だこく)漏(も)れ: missed clock-in/out
  • 打刻(だこく)時刻(じこく): time recorded

RELATED TERMS:

  • タイムカード: time card
  • 勤怠(きんたい)管理(かんり): attendance management
  • 打刻(だこく)機(き): time clock machine
