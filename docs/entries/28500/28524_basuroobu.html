バスローブ

ばすろーぶ
noun
bathrobe
1. bathrobe
An absorbent robe worn after bathing, typically made of toweling material. Mostly seen in hotels, spas, and Western-style home settings.
ホテルにバスローブが用意(ようい)されていた。
A bathrobe had been provided in the hotel.
風呂(ふろ)上(あ)がりにバスローブを羽織(はお)った。
I put on a bathrobe after my bath.
高級(こうきゅう)ホテル(ほてる)のバスローブはふわふわで気持(きも)ちがいい。
The bathrobes at high-end hotels are fluffy and feel wonderful.

Loanword from English "bathrobe." Not part of traditional Japanese bathing, which uses 浴衣(ゆかた) after bathing in ryokan and hot springs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • バスローブを着(き)る: to put on a bathrobe
  • バスローブを羽織(はお)る: to throw on a bathrobe
  • 高級(こうきゅう)バスローブ: luxury bathrobe

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 浴衣(ゆかた): yukata — Japanese cotton kimono, worn after bathing at ryokan
  • ガウン: gown — broader term, can include dressing gowns and academic gowns
  • パジャマ: pajamas — for sleeping, not after bathing
