外出先(がいしゅつさき)

がいしゅつさき
noun
place one has gone out to; destination when out; where one is while out
1. place one has gone out to; destination when out; while out
Where someone is when they're out of the house or office — the workplace they've visited, a café, a station, etc. Often appears with で to mean "while out and about."
外出先(がいしゅつさき)で財布(さいふ)をなくした。
I lost my wallet while I was out.
外出先(がいしゅつさき)から会社(かいしゃ)に連絡(れんらく)を入(い)れた。
I contacted the office from where I was visiting.
外出先(がいしゅつさき)でもスマホがあれば仕事(しごと)のメールを確認(かくにん)できる。
Even when I'm out, I can check work email as long as I have my smartphone.

Compound of 外出(がいしゅつ) (going out) + 先(さき) (destination, place of). The 先(さき) suffix is the same one in 勤務先(きんむさき) (workplace) and 連絡先(れんらくさき) (contact info).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外出先(がいしゅつさき)で: while out
  • 外出先(がいしゅつさき)から: from where one has gone
  • 外出先(がいしゅつさき)を伝(つた)える: to share where one is going

USAGE:
Very common in announcements and modern work-from-anywhere contexts: 外出先(がいしゅつさき)でも対応(たいおう)可能(かのう) ("can respond even when out"). Less common in casual conversation, where people usually name the specific place instead.
