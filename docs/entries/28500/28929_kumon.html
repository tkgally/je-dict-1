1. agony; anguish; deep suffering
Intense physical or mental suffering — writhing in pain, or being torn by inner torment. A literary, fairly heavy word, common in novels, news reports, and medical descriptions.
苦悶(くもん)の表情(ひょうじょう)を浮(う)かべる。
To show a look of agony.
患者(かんじゃ)は激(はげ)しい腹痛(ふくつう)で苦悶(くもん)していた。
The patient was writhing in agony from severe stomach pain.
長年(ながねん)の心(こころ)の苦悶(くもん)から解放(かいほう)されて、ようやく穏(おだ)やかな気持(きも)ちになれた。
Released from years of inner anguish, I was finally able to feel at peace.

Compound of 苦(く) (suffering) + 悶(もん) (writhing in pain). Used as a noun and as a する verb (苦悶(くもん)する). Implies severe, ongoing torment — not minor discomfort.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 苦悶(くもん)の表情(ひょうじょう): a look of agony
  • 苦悶(くもん)に満(み)ちた声(こえ): a voice full of anguish
  • 苦悶(くもん)する: to writhe/agonize
  • 心(こころ)の苦悶(くもん): inner anguish
  • 苦悶(くもん)の末(すえ): after great anguish

REGISTER:
Literary and heavy in tone. In casual speech, use 苦(くる)しむ or 悩(なや)む instead.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 苦痛(くつう): pain; suffering — broader, can be mild or severe
  • 苦悩(くのう): anguish; distress — emphasizes the mental/emotional side
  • 懊悩(おうのう): deep mental anguish — even more literary
