1. to bend (one's back or body); to stoop down
To bend a part of one's own body — usually the back, knees, or whole body — to lower oneself or duck under something. The object is one's own body part.
身(み)を屈(かが)めた。
I bent down.
低(ひく)い戸口(とぐち)で頭(あたま)を屈(かが)めて入(はい)った。
I ducked my head to enter through the low doorway.
祖父(そふ)は腰(こし)を屈(かが)めて庭(にわ)の雑草(ざっそう)を抜(ぬ)いている。
My grandfather is stooping over and pulling weeds in the garden.

Transitive counterpart of 屈(かが)む (to crouch, to stoop). Where 屈(かが)む describes the body crouching as a whole, 屈(かが)める takes a specific body part as object.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (takes を). The object is always one's own body part — 身(み), 腰(こし), 頭(あたま), 膝(ひざ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 身(み)を屈(かが)める: to bend one's body; to crouch down
  • 腰(こし)を屈(かが)める: to bend at the waist; to stoop
  • 頭(あたま)を屈(かが)める: to duck one's head
  • 膝(ひざ)を屈(かが)める: to bend one's knees

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 屈(かが)む: to crouch; to stoop — intransitive pair
  • 曲(ま)げる: to bend (something) — general, can take any object
  • しゃがむ: to squat down — fully lower to a squatting position
