髪染(かみぞ)め

かみぞめ
noun
hair dye; hair coloring
1. hair dye; hair coloring; the act of dyeing hair
Refers to both the product used to color hair and the act of dyeing one's hair. A compound of 髪(かみ) (hair) and 染(そ)め (dyeing).
髪染(かみぞ)めを買(か)う。
To buy hair dye.
自宅(じたく)で髪染(かみぞ)めをした。
I dyed my hair at home.
白髪(しらが)が増(ふ)えてきたので、���染(かみぞ)めを使(つか)い始(はじ)めた。
Since my gray hairs have been increasing, I started using hair dye.

A straightforward compound noun. Can refer to both the hair dye product and the activity of dyeing hair.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 髪染(かみぞ)めをする: to dye one's hair
  • 白髪(しらが)染(ぞ)め: gray hair dye
  • 市販(しはん)の髪染(かみぞ)め: store-bought hair dye
  • 髪(かみ)を染(そ)める: to dye one's hair (verb form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ヘアカラー: hair color — the most common commercial term; often preferred in advertising
  • 毛染(けぞ)め: hair dye — an older, less common synonym
  • 白髪染(しらがぞ)め: gray hair dye — specifically for covering gray/white hair

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, many schools and workplaces have rules about hair color. 黒染(くろぞ)め (dyeing hair back to black) is sometimes required of students who have dyed their hair.
