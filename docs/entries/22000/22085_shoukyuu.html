1. promotion in rank; advancement to a higher grade
Moving up to a higher rank or grade within a structured ranking system, particularly in martial arts, traditional arts, or civil service classifications. Distinct from 昇進, which refers to corporate promotions.
昇級(しょうきゅう)試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)した。
I passed the promotion exam.
柔道(じゅうどう)で三級(さんきゅう)から二級(にきゅう)に昇級(しょうきゅう)した。
I was promoted from third grade to second grade in judo.
昇級(しょうきゅう)するためには、一定(いってい)の成績(せいせき)と経験(けいけん)年数(ねんすう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To be promoted in rank, a certain level of performance and years of experience are required.

A compound of 昇(しょう) (to rise) and 級(きゅう) (grade, rank). Used for advancement within a grading system, particularly in martial arts (judo, karate, kendo), traditional arts (calligraphy, tea ceremony), and civil service rankings. Not typically used for corporate job promotions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 昇級(しょうきゅう)試験(しけん): rank promotion exam
  • 昇級(しょうきゅう)審査(しんさ): rank promotion review
  • 昇級(しょうきゅう)する: to be promoted in rank

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 昇給(しょうきゅう) — pay raise (homophone! different kanji: 給(きゅう) = salary vs 級(きゅう) = rank)
  • 昇段(しょうだん) — promotion to a higher dan rank (used in martial arts for dan levels)
  • 昇進(しょうしん) — promotion (in a company or organization)
  • 昇格(しょうかく) — elevation in status; upgrading
