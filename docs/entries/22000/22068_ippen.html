一遍(いっぺん)

いっぺん
adverb
once; one time
1. once; one time
A slightly casual way of saying 'once' or 'one time,' more colloquial than 一度 or 一回. Particularly common in Kansai dialect but widely understood throughout Japan.
一遍(いっぺん)やってみて。
Try it once.
一遍(いっぺん)会(あ)ったことがある。
I've met them once.
一遍(いっぺん)でいいから本場(ほんば)のピザを食(た)べてみたい。
I'd like to try authentic pizza just once.
2. all at once; in one go
Used in the pattern 一遍に to mean doing something all at once rather than in stages or portions, such as carrying everything in a single trip or eating all the food at the same time.
一遍(いっぺん)に片付(かたづ)けよう。
Let's take care of it all at once.
そんなに一遍(いっぺん)に食(た)べたらお腹(なか)を壊(こわ)すよ。
If you eat that much all at once, you'll upset your stomach.
荷物(にもつ)を一遍(いっぺん)に運(はこ)ぶのは無理(むり)だ。
It's impossible to carry the luggage all in one go.

A slightly casual adverb meaning "once" or "all at once." More colloquial than 一度(いちど) or 一回(いっかい). Particularly common in Kansai dialect but widely understood across Japan. Sense 2 (一遍(いっぺん)に) means doing something all at once rather than in stages.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一遍(いっぺん) + verb: once; one time (sense 1)
  • 一遍(いっぺん)に: all at once; in one go (sense 2)
  • 一遍(いっぺん)でいいから: just once (emphatic wish)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一度(いちど) — once (standard, slightly more formal)
  • 一回(いっかい) — one time (neutral, most common)
  • 一気(いっき)に — in one breath; at a stretch (emphasizes speed/intensity more than 一遍(いっぺん)に)

NOTE:
Do not confuse with the homophone 一変(いっぺん) (complete change), which has a completely different meaning.
