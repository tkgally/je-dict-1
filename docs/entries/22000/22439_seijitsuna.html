誠実(せいじつ)な

せいじつな
adjective
sincere; honest; faithful; genuine
1. sincere; honest; faithful; genuine
Describes a person or attitude that is truthful, dependable, and genuine in their dealings with others. Implies integrity and trustworthiness.
誠実(せいじつ)な人柄(ひとがら)だ。
He has a sincere character.
誠実(せいじつ)に対応(たいおう)してくれた。
They responded to me sincerely.
彼女(かのじょ)は仕事(しごと)に対(たい)していつも誠実(せいじつ)で、周(まわ)りから信頼(しんらい)されている。
She is always sincere about her work, and is trusted by those around her.

A highly positive character trait in Japanese culture. Describes someone who is genuine, reliable, and deals with others honestly. Often used when describing ideal qualities in a partner, colleague, or leader.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誠実(せいじつ)な人(ひと): a sincere person
  • 誠実(せいじつ)な態度(たいど): a sincere attitude
  • 誠実(せいじつ)に向(む)き合(あ)う: to deal with sincerely
  • 誠実(せいじつ)に対応(たいおう)する: to respond sincerely
  • 誠実(せいじつ)さ: sincerity (noun form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正直(しょうじき)な: honest — focuses on not lying; more about truthfulness in speech
  • 真面目(まじめ)な: earnest / diligent — focuses on being hardworking and conscientious
  • 素直(すなお)な: straightforward / obedient — open and honest, without pretense
  • 忠実(ちゅうじつ)な: faithful / loyal — emphasizes loyalty and devotion

USAGE:
Frequently appears in personal ads, job interviews, and character descriptions. 誠実(せいじつ) is one of the most valued personality traits in Japanese culture, especially in business and romantic contexts.
