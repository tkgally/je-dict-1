雨(あま)よけ

あまよけ
noun
rain shelter; rain guard; rain cover
1. rain shelter; rain guard; rain cover
Something that provides protection from rain — a shelter, awning, cover, or other structure or device that keeps rain off.
雨(あま)よけの下(した)で待(ま)った。
I waited under a rain shelter.
ベビーカーに雨(あま)よけカバーをつけた。
I put a rain cover on the stroller.
玄関(げんかん)に雨(あま)よけの庇(ひさし)があるので、荷物(にもつ)を出(だ)し入(い)れするときに濡(ぬ)れなくて済(す)む。
There's a rain awning over the entrance, so I don't get wet when bringing things in and out.

Composed of 雨(あま) (rain) + よけ (avoidance, protection). The suffix よけ (除(よ)け) means protection against or warding off, and combines with many nouns to describe protective measures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨(あま)よけカバー: rain cover
  • 雨(あま)よけの庇(ひさし): rain awning
  • 雨(あま)よけテント: rain shelter tent
  • 雨(あま)よけになる: to serve as rain protection

RELATED WORDS WITH よけ:

  • 日(ひ)よけ: sunshade, sun protection
  • 風(かぜ)よけ: windbreak
  • 虫(むし)よけ: insect repellent
  • 魔(ま)よけ: charm against evil
