1. to fail to write; to miss writing; to make a mistake in writing
To either miss the opportunity to write something down, or to make an error while writing. A compound verb combining 書(か)く (to write) with the auxiliary 損(そこ)ねる (to fail to do, to miss doing).
名前(なまえ)を書(か)き損(そこ)ねた。
I made a mistake writing the name.
住所(じゅうしょ)を書(か)き損(そこ)ねて、最初(さいしょ)から書(か)き直(なお)した。
I made an error writing the address and rewrote it from the beginning.
電話番号(でんわばんごう)を書(か)き損(そこ)ねて、連絡(れんらく)が取(と)れなくなってしまった。
I wrote the phone number down wrong and couldn't get in touch.

A compound verb: 書(か)く (to write) + 損(そこ)ねる (to fail to do, to botch). The auxiliary verb 〜損(そこ)ねる attaches to the stem of another verb to express failure or a missed opportunity. It follows the ichidan conjugation pattern.

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS WITH 〜損(そこ)ねる:

  • 言(い)い損(そこ)ねる: to fail to say, to misspeak
  • 読(よ)み損(そこ)ねる: to misread, to fail to read
  • 食(た)べ損(そこ)ねる: to miss out on eating
  • 乗(の)り損(そこ)ねる: to miss (a train, bus)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)き間違(まちが)える: to write incorrectly — focuses on the error itself rather than the failure
  • 書(か)き直(なお)す: to rewrite — the action taken after making a writing mistake
  • 書(か)き落(お)とす: to omit in writing — to accidentally leave something out
