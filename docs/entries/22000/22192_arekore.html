あれこれ

あれこれ
adverb
this and that; various things; in various ways
1. this and that; various things; one thing and another
Refers to multiple unspecified things, topics, or matters being considered, discussed, or dealt with.
あれこれ考(かんが)えた。
I thought about various things.
あれこれ悩(なや)んでも仕方(しかた)がない。
There's no point worrying about this and that.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)であれこれ忙(いそが)しくしている。
I'm busy with various things in preparation for the move.
母(はは)はいつもあれこれ口(くち)を出(だ)してくる。
My mother always meddles in this and that.

A very common conversational word combining あれ (that) and これ (this) to mean 'various things' or 'one thing after another.' Often implies a scattering of attention across multiple topics or tasks rather than focused effort.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • あれこれ考(かんが)える — to think about various things
  • あれこれ悩(なや)む — to worry about this and that
  • あれこれ口(くち)を出(だ)す — to meddle in various matters
  • あれこれ試(ため)す — to try various things
  • あれこれ言(い)う — to say this and that; to make various comments

USAGE:
Can function as an adverb modifying a verb (あれこれ考(かんが)える) or as a noun-like expression (あれこれの用事(ようじ) — various errands). As an adverb, it often carries a nuance of doing something in an unfocused or scattered way.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いろいろ — various (more neutral, general-purpose)
  • 何(なに)かと — one way or another (often implies trouble or burden)
  • とやかく — this and that (usually negative: とやかく言(い)う — to nitpick)
