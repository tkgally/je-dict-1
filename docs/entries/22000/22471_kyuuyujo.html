給油所(きゅうゆじょ)

きゅうゆじょ
noun
gas station; filling station; fuel station
1. gas station; filling station; fuel station
A place where vehicles can be refueled. A formal term for what is more commonly called ガソリンスタンド in everyday Japanese.
次(つぎ)の給油所(きゅうゆじょ)まで30キロある。
It's 30 kilometers to the next gas station.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)の給油所(きゅうゆじょ)は値段(ねだん)が高(たか)い。
Gas stations on the highway are expensive.
ガソリンが残(のこ)り少(すく)なくなったので、給油所(きゅうゆじょ)を探(さが)した。
We were running low on gas, so we looked for a filling station.

Composed of 給油(きゅうゆ) (refueling) + 所(じょ) (place). A formal term for a gas station. In everyday conversation, ガソリンスタンド (gasoline stand, a Japanese-coined English term) is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 給油所(きゅうゆじょ)に寄(よ)る: to stop at a gas station
  • 給油所(きゅうゆじょ)を探(さが)す: to look for a gas station
  • セルフ給油所(きゅうゆじょ): self-service gas station
  • 最寄(もよ)りの給油所(きゅうゆじょ): nearest gas station

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ガソリンスタンド: gas station — the most common everyday term (wasei-eigo)
  • スタンド: station — abbreviated colloquial form of ガソリンスタンド
  • 給油(きゅうゆ): refueling — the act of adding fuel, not the location

NOTE:
給油所(きゅうゆじょ) appears on road signs, in official documents, and in news reports. ガソリンスタンド is preferred in casual speech.
