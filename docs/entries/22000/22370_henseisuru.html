1. to organize; to form; to compose; to put together
To assemble people, units, or elements into an organized group or structure. Used for forming teams, military units, train configurations, budgets, and other organized arrangements.
チームを編成(へんせい)する。
To form a team.
予算(よさん)を編成(へんせい)する作業(さぎょう)が始(はじ)まった。
The work of putting together the budget has begun.
専門家(せんもんか)を集(あつ)めて調査(ちょうさ)委員会(いいんかい)を編成(へんせい)した。
They gathered experts and formed an investigation committee.

Emphasizes the deliberate assembly and arrangement of components into an organized whole. The resulting group has structure and purpose, not just a random collection.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チームを編成(へんせい)する: to form a team
  • 予算(よさん)を編成(へんせい)する: to draw up a budget
  • 部隊(ぶたい)を編成(へんせい)する: to organize a military unit
  • 番組(ばんぐみ)編成(へんせい): program scheduling (TV/radio)
  • 列車(れっしゃ)編成(へんせい): train composition/formation
  • 編成(へんせい)替(が)え: reorganization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 組織(そしき)する: to organize — broader, focuses on creating an organizational structure
  • 結成(けっせい)する: to form / to establish — for newly creating a group, party, or alliance
  • 構成(こうせい)する: to compose / to make up — focuses on what elements something consists of

REGISTER:
Formal. Standard in administrative, military, and media contexts. In casual speech, people more often say チームを作(つく)る or グループを組(く)む.
