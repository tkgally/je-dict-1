曲(ま)がり

まがり
noun
bend; curve; turn (in a road, pipe, etc.)
1. bend; curve; turn (in a road, pipe, etc.)
A point where something straight changes direction, such as a curve in a road, a bend in a river, or a turn in a pipe.
道(みち)の曲(ま)がりに注意(ちゅうい)する。
To watch out for the bend in the road.
この曲(ま)がりを過(す)ぎたら右(みぎ)に見(み)えます。
You'll see it on the right after you pass this curve.
川(かわ)の曲(ま)がりくねった流(なが)れに沿(そ)って歩(ある)いた。
I walked along the winding flow of the river.

The noun form of 曲(ま)がる (to bend, to turn). Refers to a physical bend or curve in roads, rivers, pipes, or other elongated objects.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道(みち)の曲(ま)がり: bend in the road
  • 曲(ま)がり角(かど): street corner, turning point
  • 曲(ま)がりくねる: to wind, to twist and turn

RELATED EXPRESSION:
曲(ま)がりなりにも is an idiomatic adverb meaning "for better or worse" or "in one's own imperfect way." It suggests something is not ideal but still functional or valid.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カーブ: curve — used for road curves and baseball pitches
  • 角(かど): corner — specifically a sharp turn or the corner of a building/street
