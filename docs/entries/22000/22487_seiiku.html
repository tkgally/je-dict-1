1. growth; development (of plants or organisms)
The process by which plants, crops, or other living organisms grow and develop. Primarily used in agricultural and biological contexts rather than for human growth.
植物(しょくぶつ)が順調(じゅんちょう)に生育(せいいく)している。
The plants are growing well.
日当(ひあ)たりが悪(わる)いと生育(せいいく)に影響(えいきょう)する。
Poor sunlight affects growth.
この品種(ひんしゅ)は寒冷(かんれい)な気候(きこう)でも生育(せいいく)できる。
This variety can grow even in cold climates.

Primarily used for the growth and development of plants, crops, and other organisms. While it can occasionally apply to human growth, 成長(せいちょう) and 発育(はついく) are far more common for people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生育(せいいく)環境(かんきょう): growing environment
  • 生育(せいいく)条件(じょうけん): growth conditions
  • 生育(せいいく)状況(じょうきょう): growth status
  • 生育(せいいく)期間(きかん): growing period

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 成長(せいちょう): growth — the most general term; used for people, businesses, economies, and plants
  • 発育(はついく): physical development — mainly used for human physical growth, especially of children
  • 生長(せいちょう): growth (of plants) — close synonym sometimes used interchangeably with 生育(せいいく)
