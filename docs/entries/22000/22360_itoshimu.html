1. to cherish; to love dearly; to hold dear
To feel deep affection and tenderness toward someone or something, treating them with care and devotion. Implies a gentle, enduring love rather than passionate emotion.
子(こ)どもを愛(いと)しむ気持(きも)ち。
The feeling of cherishing one's child.
彼女(かのじょ)は小(ちい)さな命(いのち)を愛(いと)しむように抱(だ)きしめた。
She held the small life tenderly, as if cherishing it.
祖母(そぼ)は庭(にわ)の花(はな)を毎日(まいにち)愛(いと)しむように世話(せわ)をしていた。
My grandmother tended to the flowers in the garden every day, cherishing them dearly.

A literary and poetic verb expressing tender, gentle love. Derived from the adjective 愛(いと)しい (dear, beloved). More literary than 大切(たいせつ)にする and conveys deeper emotional warmth.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 命(いのち)を愛(いと)しむ: to cherish life
  • 子(こ)を愛(いと)しむ: to cherish one's child
  • 愛(いと)しむように: as if cherishing; tenderly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 慈(いつく)しむ: to love tenderly; to be compassionate toward — very close synonym, slightly more formal
  • 大切(たいせつ)にする: to treasure; to take good care of — more everyday
  • 可愛(かわい)がる: to dote on; to be affectionate with — more casual and active

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (む row). Conjugates: 愛(いと)しまない, 愛(いと)しんで, 愛(いと)しみます.
