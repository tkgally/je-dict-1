一度(いちど)きり

いちどきり
expression
only once; just one time
1. only once; just one time
Emphasizes that something happens only once and cannot be repeated, often carrying a sense of preciousness or urgency about a unique opportunity.
人生(じんせい)は一度(いちど)きりだ。
You only live once.
そのチャンスは一度(いちど)きりだった。
That chance only came once.
一度(いちど)きりの出会(であ)いを大切(たいせつ)にしたい。
I want to cherish this one-time encounter.

An expression emphasizing that something happens only once and cannot be repeated. Combines 一度(いちど) (once) with きり (only, nothing more). Often carries a sense of preciousness or urgency — the one chance should not be wasted.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人生(じんせい)は一度(いちど)きり: you only live once
  • 一度(いちど)きりの体験(たいけん): a once-in-a-lifetime experience
  • 一度(いちど)きりしかない: there is only one chance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一回(いっかい)きり — same meaning but slightly more casual
  • たった一度(いちど) — emphasizes "just once" with a different nuance of smallness
  • 二度(にど)とない — never again; emphasizes irreversibility
