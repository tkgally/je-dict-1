1. to sign; to put one's signature on
To write one's name on a document, petition, or other official paper to indicate agreement, authorization, or acknowledgment.
契約書(けいやくしょ)に署名(しょめい)する。
To sign a contract.
ここに署名(しょめい)してください。
Please sign here.
両国(りょうこく)の代表(だいひょう)が条約(じょうやく)に署名(しょめい)し、歴史的(れきしてき)な合意(ごうい)が成立(せいりつ)した。
Representatives from both countries signed the treaty, and a historic agreement was reached.

A formal word for signing documents. Used in legal, business, and diplomatic contexts. In everyday situations, サインする is more commonly heard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 署名(しょめい)活動(かつどう): signature campaign (petition drive)
  • 署名(しょめい)を集(あつ)める: to collect signatures
  • 署名(しょめい)捺印(なついん): signature and seal
  • 電子(でんし)署名(しょめい): digital signature
  • 署名(しょめい)欄(らん): signature field

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サインする: to sign — more casual and widely used in daily life (signing receipts, autographs)
  • 記名(きめい)する: to write one's name — simply writing one's name, without the legal weight of a signature
  • 調印(ちょういん)する: to sign (a treaty) — specifically for signing international agreements or treaties

REGISTER:
Formal. Used in official and legal contexts. For casual situations like signing a credit card receipt, サインする is more natural.
