1. expression of one's intention; declaration of will
The act of clearly communicating one's wishes, decisions, or intentions. Used in both everyday and legal contexts.
意思表示(いしひょうじ)をはっきりする。
To clearly express one's intention.
本人(ほんにん)の意思表示(いしひょうじ)が必要(ひつよう)です。
The person's own expression of intent is required.
契約(けいやく)を解除(かいじょ)するには、書面(しょめん)による意思表示(いしひょうじ)が求(もと)められる。
To cancel the contract, a written declaration of intent is required.

Composed of 意思(いし) (will/intention) and 表示(ひょうじ) (expression/indication). In everyday use, it means making one's wishes or decisions clear. In legal contexts, it is a technical term referring to a legally binding declaration of intent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意思表示(いしひょうじ)をする: to express one's intention
  • 明確(めいかく)な意思表示(いしひょうじ): clear expression of intent
  • 書面(しょめん)による意思表示(いしひょうじ): written declaration of intent
  • 意思表示(いしひょうじ)の撤回(てっかい): withdrawal of a declaration of intent

USAGE NOTE:
Do not confuse 意思(いし) (will/intention) with 意志(いし) (willpower/determination). 意思表示(いしひょうじ) uses 意思(いし), focusing on what one intends or wishes, not on the strength of one's resolve.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 表明(ひょうめい): public declaration — more formal, often used for public statements of position
  • 申告(しんこく): declaration/report — official reporting, as in tax filing
