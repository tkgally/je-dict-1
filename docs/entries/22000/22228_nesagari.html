1. price drop; decline in price; depreciation
A decrease in the price or value of goods, stocks, property, or other assets.
株(かぶ)が値下(ねさ)がりした。
The stocks dropped in price.
野菜(やさい)の値下(ねさ)がりが続(つづ)いている。
The drop in vegetable prices is continuing.
円安(えんやす)の影響(えいきょう)で輸入品(ゆにゅうひん)の値下(ねさ)がりは期待(きたい)できない。
Due to the weak yen, we can't expect a drop in import prices.
不動産(ふどうさん)の値下(ねさ)がりを待(ま)ってから家(いえ)を買(か)おうと思(おも)っている。
I'm thinking of waiting for real estate prices to drop before buying a house.

Compound of 値(ね) (price) and 下(さ)がり (drop). Used as both a noun and a verb with する (値下(ねさ)がりする). Very common in economic and everyday commercial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 値下(ねさ)がりする: to drop in price
  • 大幅(おおはば)な値下(ねさ)がり: a significant price drop
  • 値下(ねさ)がりが続(つづ)く: price drops continue
  • 値下(ねさ)がりを待(ま)つ: to wait for prices to drop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 値上(ねあ)がり: price increase — the opposite
  • 値下(ねさ)げ: price reduction — an intentional reduction by a seller, whereas 値下(ねさ)がり is a natural market decline
  • 下落(げらく): decline; fall — more formal, used in financial and economic writing
  • 暴落(ぼうらく): crash; plunge — a sudden, dramatic drop
