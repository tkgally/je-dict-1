1. to oppose; to conflict; to confront; to be at odds
To be in opposition or conflict with another party, idea, or position. Implies two sides standing against each other, whether in opinions, interests, or physical confrontation.
二(ふた)つの意見(いけん)が対立(たいりつ)した。
The two opinions were in conflict.
与党(よとう)と野党(やとう)が対立(たいりつ)している。
The ruling party and the opposition party are at odds.
環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)と経済(けいざい)発展(はってん)が対立(たいりつ)する場面(ばめん)は少(すく)なくない。
There are many situations where environmental protection and economic development come into conflict.

Implies two opposing sides facing each other. Often used for conflicts between groups, ideas, or interests rather than personal arguments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 対立(たいりつ)が深(ふか)まる — the conflict deepens
  • 対立(たいりつ)が激(はげ)しい — the opposition is intense
  • 対立(たいりつ)関係(かんけい) — adversarial relationship
  • 対立(たいりつ)候補(こうほ) — rival candidate
  • 対立(たいりつ)を避(さ)ける — to avoid confrontation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対(はんたい)する — to oppose (a proposal or idea); focuses on disagreement with a specific thing
  • 衝突(しょうとつ)する — to clash, collide; implies a more violent or sudden conflict
  • 争(あらそ)う — to compete, to dispute; more about active fighting or competing
