1. to be stylish; to be fashionable; to be chic
To have a refined, tasteful, or fashionable quality. Most commonly used in its past participle form しゃれた as a modifier.
しゃれたレストランだね。
What a stylish restaurant.
しゃれた服装(ふくそう)で来(き)てください。
Please come dressed fashionably.
この辺(あた)りにはしゃれたカフェが多(おお)い。
There are many stylish cafés around here.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつもしゃれた格好(かっこう)をしている。
She is always dressed stylishly.

Almost always appears in the past participle form しゃれた, functioning as a modifier meaning "stylish" or "chic." The kanji form 洒落(しゃれ)る exists but is rarely used. Related to the noun 洒落(しゃれ) (wit, joke, pun), though the verb focuses on stylishness rather than humor.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しゃれた店(みせ): a stylish shop
  • しゃれた服(ふく): fashionable clothes
  • しゃれた雰囲気(ふんいき): a chic atmosphere
  • しゃれた名前(なまえ): a clever/witty name

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おしゃれ: stylish, fashionable — the more common everyday word for being well-dressed or trendy; derived from the same root
  • 粋(いき): chic, refined — carries a traditional Japanese aesthetic sensibility; suggests understated elegance
  • スタイリッシュ: stylish — a loanword from English, used for modern/Western-style trendiness
