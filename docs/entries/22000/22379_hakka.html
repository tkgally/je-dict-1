1. ignition; catching fire; combustion
The act or event of something catching fire or beginning to burn. Can refer to both spontaneous combustion and deliberate ignition.
発火(はっか)の危険(きけん)がある。
There is a risk of ignition.
バッテリーが発火(はっか)する事故(じこ)が起(お)きた。
An accident occurred in which a battery caught fire.
乾燥(かんそう)した季節(きせつ)は枯(か)れ葉(は)が自然(しぜん)発火(はっか)することがあるので注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
In dry seasons, dead leaves can spontaneously combust, so caution is needed.

Describes the moment when something begins to burn. Often used in safety warnings and accident reports. The verb form 発火(はっか)する means "to catch fire" or "to ignite."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発火(はっか)する: to catch fire / to ignite
  • 自然(しぜん)発火(はっか): spontaneous combustion
  • 発火(はっか)点(てん): ignition point / flash point
  • 発火(はっか)の原因(げんいん): cause of ignition
  • 発火(はっか)装置(そうち): ignition device
  • 発火(はっか)の危険(きけん): risk of catching fire

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 着火(ちゃっか): ignition — very similar, but often implies intentional lighting; 発火(はっか) is more often spontaneous
  • 点火(てんか): lighting a fire — deliberately setting fire to something (a stove, fireworks, etc.)
  • 出火(しゅっか): outbreak of fire — the start of a fire as an incident, used in fire investigations
  • 火災(かさい): fire (as a disaster) — the resulting fire event, not the moment of ignition
