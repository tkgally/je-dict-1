1. to associate; to be reminded of; to think of by association
To have one idea, image, or memory lead to another through mental association. When something reminds you of something else.
桜(さくら)から日本(にほん)を連想(れんそう)する。
Cherry blossoms make me think of Japan.
この曲(きょく)を聞(き)くと、学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)を連想(れんそう)する。
When I hear this song, it reminds me of my student days.
赤(あか)い色(いろ)は情熱(じょうねつ)や危険(きけん)を連想(れんそう)させることが多(おお)い。
The color red often makes people think of passion or danger.

Describes the mental process of one thing leading to thoughts of another. Common in discussions about symbols, colors, brands, and cultural associations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連想(れんそう)ゲーム: word association game
  • 連想(れんそう)させる: to make someone think of; to evoke
  • 連想(れんそう)される: to be associated with
  • 自由(じゆう)連想(れんそう): free association (psychology)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember / to recall — directly remembering something from the past
  • 想像(そうぞう)する: to imagine — creating a mental image, not necessarily triggered by association
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かべる: to think of / to bring to mind — actively calling something to mind

USAGE:
The causative form 連想(れんそう)させる (to make someone associate / to evoke) is very frequently used, especially when discussing what impressions something gives. For example, 高級感(こうきゅうかん)を連想(れんそう)させるデザイン (a design that evokes a sense of luxury).
