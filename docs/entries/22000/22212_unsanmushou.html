1. vanishing without a trace; disappearing completely; melting away like clouds and mist
Describes something — typically worries, doubts, plans, or opposition — that disappears completely, as if evaporating like clouds and mist.
不安(ふあん)が雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)した。
My anxiety vanished completely.
真相(しんそう)がわかると、疑惑(ぎわく)は雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)した。
Once the truth came out, the suspicions vanished without a trace.
あれほど盛(さか)んだった反対(はんたい)運動(うんどう)も、リーダーの逮捕(たいほ)とともに雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)してしまった。
The opposition movement, which had been so vigorous, completely disappeared with the arrest of its leader.

A four-character idiom (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)): 雲(うん) (clouds) + 散(さん) (scatter) + 霧(む) (mist) + 消(しょう) (disappear). The imagery is of clouds scattering and mist dissipating — a vivid metaphor for complete disappearance.

USAGE:
Typically used with する: 雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)する (to vanish completely). Most commonly applied to abstract things like worries, suspicions, plans, or movements — not physical objects.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不安(ふあん)が雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)する — anxiety vanishes
  • 疑惑(ぎわく)が雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)する — suspicions vanish
  • 期待(きたい)が雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)する — hopes vanish
  • 計画(けいかく)が雲散霧消(うんさんむしょう)する — plans evaporate

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 消(き)えてなくなる — to disappear completely (plain, everyday expression)
  • 跡形(あとかた)もなく — without a trace (adverbial)
  • 霧消(むしょう)する — to dissipate like mist (shorter variant with similar meaning)

REGISTER:
Literary and formal. Used in written Japanese and formal speech to add elegance or emphasis. In casual conversation, 消(き)えてなくなる or 消(き)え去(さ)る would be more natural.
