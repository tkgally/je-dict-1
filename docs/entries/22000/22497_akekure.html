1. day and night; morning and evening; all day long
Expresses the idea of doing something continuously from dawn to dusk, emphasizing constant, tireless activity without rest.
明(あ)け暮(く)れ勉強(べんきょう)している。
I study day and night.
明(あ)け暮(く)れ仕事(しごと)に追(お)われている。
I'm chased by work from morning to night.
彼(かれ)は明(あ)け暮(く)れ練習(れんしゅう)に励(はげ)んでいた。
He devoted himself to practice day in and day out.
2. to be absorbed in; to spend all one's time doing
Used with に to indicate what one is absorbed in.
読書(どくしょ)に明(あ)け暮(く)れる。
To be absorbed in reading all the time.
研究(けんきゅう)に明(あ)け暮(く)れた日々(ひび)だった。
Those were days spent completely absorbed in research.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は遊(あそ)びに明(あ)け暮(く)れていた。
When I was young, I spent all my time having fun.

Combines 明(あ)け (dawn) and 暮(く)れ (dusk) to mean "from dawn to dusk" or "all day long." As an adverb (sense 1), it modifies verbs to indicate constant, tireless activity. As a verb with する (sense 2), it takes the pattern ～に明(あ)け暮(く)れる to mean being completely absorbed in something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 明(あ)け暮(く)れ + verb: to do something all day long
  • ～に明(あ)け暮(く)れる: to be absorbed in; to spend all one's time on

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)に明(あ)け暮(く)れる: to be consumed by work
  • 勉強(べんきょう)に明(あ)け暮(く)れる: to study constantly
  • 遊(あそ)びに明(あ)け暮(く)れる: to do nothing but have fun

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日夜(にちや): day and night — more formal; often used in written language
  • 昼夜(ちゅうや): day and night — similar to 日夜(にちや); more neutral
