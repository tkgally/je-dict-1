落下物(らっかぶつ)

らっかぶつ
noun
falling object; fallen debris
1. falling object; fallen debris; dropped item
An object that has fallen or may fall from a height. Commonly used in safety warnings, traffic reports, and construction contexts.
落下物(らっかぶつ)に注意(ちゅうい)。
Caution: falling objects.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)に落下物(らっかぶつ)があった。
There was fallen debris on the highway.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)では落下物(らっかぶつ)による事故(じこ)を防(ふせ)ぐため、ヘルメットの着用(ちゃくよう)が義務(ぎむ)づけられている。
At construction sites, wearing helmets is mandatory to prevent accidents from falling objects.

Commonly seen on highway signs, construction site warnings, and safety notices. A compound of 落下(らっか) (falling) and 物(ぶつ) (object).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落下物(らっかぶつ)に注意(ちゅうい): caution for falling objects
  • 落下物(らっかぶつ)事故(じこ): falling object accident
  • 落下物(らっかぶつ)を回避(かいひ)する: to avoid a falling object
  • 道路(どうろ)の落下物(らっかぶつ): debris on the road

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落下(らっか): falling; dropping — the act of falling itself
  • 落石(らくせき): falling rocks; rockfall — specifically rocks falling on roads or mountainsides
  • 飛来物(ひらいぶつ): flying object; airborne debris — objects carried by wind or an explosion
