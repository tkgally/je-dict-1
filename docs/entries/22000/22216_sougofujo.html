相互扶助(そうごふじょ)

そうごふじょ
noun
mutual aid; mutual support; mutual assistance
1. mutual aid; mutual support; reciprocal assistance
The principle of members of a group supporting each other, especially as an organizing principle for cooperatives, insurance systems, and social organizations.
相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の精神(せいしん)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
The spirit of mutual aid is important.
保険(ほけん)は相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の仕組(しく)みで成(な)り立(た)っている。
Insurance operates on the principle of mutual aid.
農村(のうそん)では昔(むかし)から相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の伝統(でんとう)が根付(ねづ)いている。
In farming villages, the tradition of mutual support has been deeply rooted since long ago.

Composed of 相互(そうご) (mutual, reciprocal) + 扶助(ふじょ) (support, assistance). A formal compound often used in institutional, legal, and social contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の精神(せいしん) — spirit of mutual aid
  • 相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の仕組(しく)み — mutual aid system
  • 相互扶助(そうごふじょ)制度(せいど) — mutual aid system/scheme
  • 相互扶助(そうごふじょ)の原理(げんり) — principle of mutual aid

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 共助(きょうじょ) — mutual assistance (more commonly used in disaster preparedness contexts)
  • 助(たす)け合(あ)い — helping each other (everyday expression)
  • 扶助(ふじょ) — support, assistance (one-directional help, not necessarily mutual)
  • 互助(ごじょ) — mutual aid (shorter synonym, often in 互助会(ごじょかい) — mutual aid association)

REGISTER:
Formal. Common in written Japanese, institutional documents, and discussions of social systems. In casual conversation, 助(たす)け合(あ)い is more natural.
