週例(しゅうれい)

しゅうれい
noun, adjective (の)
weekly (regular event); held every week
1. weekly; held every week; regular weekly event
Something that takes place on a regular weekly basis. Most commonly used for meetings, reports, and other recurring business activities.
週例(しゅうれい)会議(かいぎ)は月曜日(げつようび)だ。
The weekly meeting is on Monday.
週例(しゅうれい)報告(ほうこく)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the weekly report.
今週(こんしゅう)の週例(しゅうれい)ミーティングでは、新(あたら)しいプロジェクトの進捗(しんちょく)を確認(かくにん)する予定(よてい)です。
At this week's regular meeting, we plan to check the progress of the new project.

A business and organizational term combining 週(しゅう) (week) and 例(れい) (regular; customary). Almost always used as a modifier before another noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 週例(しゅうれい)会議(かいぎ): weekly meeting
  • 週例(しゅうれい)報告(ほうこく): weekly report
  • 週例(しゅうれい)ミーティング: weekly meeting
  • 週例(しゅうれい)定例(ていれい)会(かい): weekly regular meeting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 月例(げつれい): monthly (regular event)
  • 日例(にちれい): daily (regular event)
  • 定例(ていれい): regular; periodic — more general, not limited to weekly
  • 毎週(まいしゅう): every week — adverb of frequency, not used as a modifier for recurring events
