1. to be refined; to be polished; to become sophisticated
To be made more elegant, tasteful, or polished through improvement or cultivation. Most commonly appears in the past participle form 洗練(せんれん)された as a modifier meaning 'refined' or 'sophisticated.'
洗練(せんれん)されたデザイン。
A refined design.
彼(かれ)の文章(ぶんしょう)は洗練(せんれん)されている。
His writing is polished.
洗練(せんれん)された雰囲気(ふんいき)のホテルに泊(と)まった。
I stayed at a hotel with a sophisticated atmosphere.
何度(なんど)も書(か)き直(なお)すうちに文章(ぶんしょう)が洗練(せんれん)されていった。
The writing became more refined through repeated revisions.

The passive form of 洗練(せんれん)する (to refine, to polish). In practice, 洗練(せんれん)される and especially 洗練(せんれん)された are far more common than the active form. The past participle 洗練(せんれん)された functions as a high-frequency modifier meaning "refined" or "sophisticated."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洗練(せんれん)されたデザイン: a refined/sophisticated design
  • 洗練(せんれん)された味(あじ): a refined flavor
  • 洗練(せんれん)された都会的(とかいてき)な雰囲気(ふんいき): a sophisticated, urban atmosphere
  • 洗練(せんれん)された表現(ひょうげん): polished expression

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上品(じょうひん): elegant, refined — describes innate good taste or breeding rather than something that has been polished
  • 洒落(しゃれ)た: stylish — more casual; focuses on fashionableness rather than cultivated refinement
  • 磨(みが)かれた: polished — emphasizes the process of improvement through practice
