1. pessimism; taking a gloomy view
A negative outlook on a situation or the future, expecting the worst outcome. Can refer to a temporary state of mind or a habitual disposition.
悲観(ひかん)するのは早(はや)い。
It's too early to be pessimistic.
将来(しょうらい)を悲観(ひかん)してはいけない。
You shouldn't be pessimistic about the future.
彼(かれ)は試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)を悲観(ひかん)して、何(なに)も手(て)につかなくなった。
He became so pessimistic about the exam results that he couldn't concentrate on anything.

Composed of 悲(ひ) (sorrow) and 観(かん) (view/outlook). Refers to viewing things in a negative light, expecting unfavorable outcomes. Often used with する to form a verb meaning 'to be pessimistic about.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悲観(ひかん)する: to be pessimistic
  • 悲観(ひかん)的(てき): pessimistic (adjective)
  • 将来(しょうらい)を悲観(ひかん)する: to be pessimistic about the future
  • 悲観(ひかん)に暮(く)れる: to be overcome with despair
  • 悲観(ひかん)論(ろん): pessimistic theory, pessimism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽観(らっかん): optimism — the direct opposite, taking a positive view
  • 絶望(ぜつぼう): despair — stronger than 悲観(ひかん), indicating complete loss of hope
  • 失望(しつぼう): disappointment — a reaction to a specific letdown, not a general outlook
