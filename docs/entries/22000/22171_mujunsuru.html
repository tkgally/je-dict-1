1. to contradict; to be inconsistent; to be contradictory
To be logically incompatible or inconsistent with something else. Used when two statements, facts, or actions cannot both be true or coexist.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)は矛盾(むじゅん)している。
His story is contradictory.
言(い)っていることと行動(こうどう)が矛盾(むじゅん)している。
What they say and what they do are contradictory.
この二(ふた)つの報告書(ほうこくしょ)の内容(ないよう)は互(たが)いに矛盾(むじゅん)しているので、調査(ちょうさ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
The contents of these two reports contradict each other, so an investigation is needed.

The word 矛盾(むじゅん) comes from a famous Chinese fable about a merchant who claimed his spear (矛(ほこ)) could pierce anything and his shield (盾(たて)) could block anything — a logical impossibility.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 矛盾(むじゅん)を感(かん)じる — to feel a contradiction
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)を指摘(してき)する — to point out a contradiction
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)が生(しょう)じる — a contradiction arises
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)を抱(かか)える — to harbor a contradiction
  • 矛盾(むじゅん)だらけ — full of contradictions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(く)い違(ちが)う — to conflict, to disagree (less formal, more about discrepancies in facts)
  • 反(はん)する — to go against, to violate (rules, expectations)
