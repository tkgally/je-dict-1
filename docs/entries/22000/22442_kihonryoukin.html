基本料金(きほんりょうきん)

きほんりょうきん
noun
basic fee; base charge; basic rate
1. basic fee; base charge; basic rate
A fixed fee charged as the minimum or starting amount for a service, before any additional usage charges are added.
タクシーの基本料金(きほんりょうきん)はいくらですか。
How much is the basic fare for a taxi?
携帯(けいたい)の基本料金(きほんりょうきん)が値上(ねあ)がりした。
The basic charge for my mobile phone went up.
電気(でんき)の基本料金(きほんりょうきん)は使用量(しようりょう)に関係(かんけい)なく毎月(まいつき)一定額(いっていがく)がかかる。
The basic electricity charge is a fixed amount every month regardless of usage.

A practical compound frequently encountered in daily life in Japan, especially when dealing with utilities, taxis, phone plans, and other services. Composed of 基本(きほん) (basic, fundamental) and 料金(りょうきん) (fee, charge).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 基本料金(きほんりょうきん)が含(ふく)まれる: the basic fee is included
  • 基本料金(きほんりょうきん)無料(むりょう): no basic fee
  • 基本料金(きほんりょうきん)+従量制(じゅうりょうせい): basic fee plus usage-based pricing
  • 基本料金(きほんりょうきん)の見直(みなお)し: review of the basic rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初乗(はつの)り料金(りょうきん): initial fare (taxis) — the starting fare for the first distance
  • 定額(ていがく)料金(りょうきん): flat-rate fee — a fixed fee regardless of usage
  • 手数料(てすうりょう): handling fee / commission — a fee for processing or service

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, utility bills (電気(でんき)・ガス・水道(すいどう)) and mobile phone plans commonly have a 基本料金(きほんりょうきん) component. Recent deregulation of the electricity market has led to more competition on basic rates.
