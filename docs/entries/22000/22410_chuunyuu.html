1. injection; infusion; pouring in
The act of pouring or injecting a liquid, substance, or figuratively, resources, energy, or ideas into something.
資金(しきん)を注入(ちゅうにゅう)する。
To inject funds.
新(あら)たなエネルギーを注入(ちゅうにゅう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to inject new energy.
政府(せいふ)は経済(けいざい)危機(きき)に対応(たいおう)するため、大量(たいりょう)の公的(こうてき)資金(しきん)を銀行(ぎんこう)に注入(ちゅうにゅう)した。
The government injected a large amount of public funds into the banks to respond to the economic crisis.

Composed of 注(ちゅう) (pour) and 入(にゅう) (enter/put in). Used both literally (pouring liquid into something) and figuratively (injecting capital, energy, ideas, etc.). The figurative usage is very common in business and news contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金(しきん)注入(ちゅうにゅう): capital injection
  • 注入(ちゅうにゅう)する: to inject, to infuse
  • 公的(こうてき)資金(しきん)の注入(ちゅうにゅう): injection of public funds
  • 注入(ちゅうにゅう)量(りょう): amount injected

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 注射(ちゅうしゃ): injection (medical) — specifically a needle injection into the body
  • 投入(とうにゅう): input / commitment — putting resources into something, broader than 注入(ちゅうにゅう)
  • 注(そそ)ぐ: to pour — the everyday verb for pouring liquids
