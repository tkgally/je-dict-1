原作者(げんさくしゃ)

げんさくしゃ
noun
original author; original creator
1. original author; original creator
The person who created the original work that a movie, anime, drama, or other adaptation is based on. Frequently used in discussions about manga-to-anime adaptations, novel-to-film adaptations, and similar contexts.
原作者(げんさくしゃ)が映画化(えいがか)に反対(はんたい)した。
The original author opposed the film adaptation.
この漫画(まんが)の原作者(げんさくしゃ)は有名(ゆうめい)な小説家(しょうせつか)だ。
The original author of this manga is a famous novelist.
アニメ化(か)にあたって、原作者(げんさくしゃ)が脚本(きゃくほん)の監修(かんしゅう)に参加(さんか)することが発表(はっぴょう)された。
It was announced that the original author would participate in supervising the screenplay for the anime adaptation.

Composed of 原作(げんさく) (original work) + 者(しゃ) (person). This word is especially common in the entertainment industry, where adaptations of manga, light novels, and novels into anime, films, and TV dramas are frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原作者(げんさくしゃ)の許可(きょか): the original author's permission
  • 原作者(げんさくしゃ)監修(かんしゅう): supervised by the original author
  • 原作者(げんさくしゃ)公認(こうにん): approved by the original author
  • 原作者(げんさくしゃ)と脚本家(きゃくほんか): original author and screenwriter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 作者(さくしゃ): author, creator — general term for the creator of any work
  • 著者(ちょしゃ): author (of a book) — specifically for written works
  • 原作(げんさく): original work — the work itself, not the person
