手間暇(てまひま)

てまひま
noun
time and effort; time and labor
1. time and effort; time and labor
The combination of effort (手間) and time (暇) invested in doing something carefully. Emphasizes that something requires both patience and work.
手間暇(てまひま)をかけて作(つく)る。
To make something with time and effort.
手間暇(てまひま)かけた料理(りょうり)は美味(おい)しい。
Food made with time and care is delicious.
この味噌(みそ)は職人(しょくにん)が手間暇(てまひま)を惜(お)しまず仕込(しこ)んだものだ。
This miso was prepared by a craftsman who spared no time or effort.

A compound of 手間(てま) (effort, labor) and 暇(ひま) (time, free time). Together they emphasize the dual investment of both effort and time. Often used positively to praise careful, dedicated work, especially in food preparation and craftsmanship.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手間暇(てまひま)をかける: to put in time and effort
  • 手間暇(てまひま)を惜(お)しまない: to spare no time or effort
  • 手間暇(てまひま)かけた料理(りょうり): food made with care

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手間(てま): effort / labor — focuses on the work involved, without the time component
  • 苦労(くろう): hardship / trouble — implies difficulty and suffering, not just investment of effort
  • 手数(てすう): trouble / bother — often used in polite expressions like お手数(てすう)ですが

USAGE:
Very common in food and craft contexts. Advertising and product descriptions often use 手間暇(てまひま)をかけた to emphasize that something was made with care. Can also be written as 手間(てま)ヒマ or 手間(てま)ひま.
