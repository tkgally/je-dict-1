1. locking; securing a lock
The act of locking a door, gate, or other opening. Used in formal and written contexts more than everyday speech.
施錠(せじょう)を忘(わす)れた。
I forgot to lock up.
外出(がいしゅつ)する前(まえ)に施錠(せじょう)を確認(かくにん)する。
I check that the door is locked before going out.
閉店(へいてん)後(ご)は全(すべ)ての出入口(でいりぐち)を施錠(せじょう)してください。
Please lock all entrances after the store closes.

A formal word for the act of locking. While 鍵(かぎ)をかける is the everyday expression for locking a door, 施錠(せじょう) is preferred in written notices, instructions, and formal speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 施錠(せじょう)する: to lock
  • 施錠(せじょう)を確認(かくにん)する: to confirm the lock
  • 施錠(せじょう)を忘(わす)れる: to forget to lock
  • 自動(じどう)施錠(せじょう): automatic locking
  • 施錠(せじょう)管理(かんり): lock management

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鍵(かぎ)をかける: to lock — the everyday colloquial expression
  • 解錠(かいじょう): unlocking — the formal antonym of 施錠(せじょう)
  • 戸締(とじ)まり: locking up (the house) — focuses on securing the whole house before leaving or sleeping
