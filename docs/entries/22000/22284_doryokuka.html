努力家(どりょくか)

どりょくか
noun
hard worker; diligent person
1. hard worker; diligent person
A person who consistently puts in effort and works hard. Used as a compliment describing someone's character.
彼女(かのじょ)は努力家(どりょくか)だ。
She is a hard worker.
努力家(どりょくか)の彼(かれ)なら成功(せいこう)するだろう。
Being the hard worker he is, he will probably succeed.
天才(てんさい)というより努力家(どりょくか)と呼(よ)ばれるほうが嬉(うれ)しい。
I'd rather be called a hard worker than a genius.

Formed with the suffix 家(か) meaning "person who does" (as in 専門家(せんもんか) or 芸術家(げいじゅつか)). Almost always used as a positive character assessment. In Japanese culture, effort is often valued more highly than innate talent, making this a meaningful compliment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 努力家(どりょくか)で知(し)られる: to be known as a hard worker
  • 相当(そうとう)な努力家(どりょくか): a considerable hard worker
  • 努力家(どりょくか)タイプ: the hardworking type

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勤勉(きんべん): diligent — describes the quality of being hardworking, not the person
  • 頑張(がんば)り屋(や): someone who perseveres — similar but more casual and emphasizes grit over steady effort
  • 働(はたら)き者(もの): hard worker — focuses on labor and productivity rather than personal effort
