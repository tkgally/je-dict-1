1. to pass through (a place); to go past without stopping
To move through or past a location without stopping there. Commonly used for trains passing through stations, vehicles passing through areas, or storms passing over regions.
この電車(でんしゃ)は次(つぎ)の駅(えき)を通過(つうか)します。
This train will pass through the next station without stopping.
台風(たいふう)が関東(かんとう)地方(ちほう)を通過(つうか)した。
The typhoon passed through the Kanto region.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)の料金所(りょうきんじょ)を通過(つうか)するのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to pass through the expressway toll gate.
2. to pass (a test, inspection, or legislative process)
To successfully get through a review, screening, or approval process.
法案(ほうあん)が国会(こっかい)を通過(つうか)した。
The bill passed through the Diet.
書類(しょるい)審査(しんさ)を通過(つうか)して、面接(めんせつ)に進(すす)んだ。
I passed the document screening and moved on to the interview.
厳(きび)しい審査(しんさ)を通過(つうか)した作品(さくひん)だけが展示(てんじ)される。
Only works that pass the strict screening will be exhibited.

A versatile verb with both physical (passing through a place) and abstract (passing a screening) meanings. Very common in news, train announcements, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通過(つうか)駅(えき): a station the train passes through
  • 通過(つうか)点(てん): a passing point; a milestone
  • 通過(つうか)儀礼(ぎれい): rite of passage
  • 審査(しんさ)を通過(つうか)する: to pass a screening
  • 国会(こっかい)を通過(つうか)する: to pass through parliament

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 通(とお)る: to pass / to go through — more general and casual; used in everyday speech
  • 通(つう)じる: to lead to / to be understood — focuses on connection or communication
  • 経由(けいゆ)する: to go via / to transit through — emphasizes the route taken

USAGE:
On Japanese trains, station announcements frequently say この電車(でんしゃ)は〇〇駅(えき)を通過(つうか)します to warn passengers that the express train won't stop at certain stations.
