懸賞金(けんしょうきん)

けんしょうきん
noun
prize money; reward money; bounty
1. prize money; reward; bounty
Money offered as a prize for winning a contest or competition, or as a reward for information or the capture of a wanted person.
懸賞金(けんしょうきん)は百万(ひゃくまん)円(えん)です。
The prize money is one million yen.
犯人(はんにん)に懸賞金(けんしょうきん)がかけられた。
A bounty was placed on the criminal.
情報(じょうほう)提供者(ていきょうしゃ)に最大(さいだい)三百万(さんびゃくまん)円(えん)の懸賞金(けんしょうきん)が支払(しはら)われることになっている。
An informant can receive up to three million yen in reward money.

Composed of 懸賞(けんしょう) (prize/reward) + 金(きん) (money). Used in two main contexts: prize money for contests and competitions, and reward money for information leading to the capture of criminals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懸賞金(けんしょうきん)をかける: to put up a reward/bounty
  • 懸賞金(けんしょうきん)が出(で)る: a reward is offered
  • 懸賞金(けんしょうきん)付(つ)き: with a bounty attached
  • 懸賞金(けんしょうきん)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive the prize/reward money

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 賞金(しょうきん): prize money — for competitions, without the "bounty" connotation
  • 報奨金(ほうしょうきん): incentive money/bonus — for good performance or tips
  • 褒賞金(ほうしょうきん): reward money — formal, from government or organizations
