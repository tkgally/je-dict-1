急坂(きゅうざか)

きゅうざか
noun
steep slope; steep hill
1. steep slope; steep hill; steep incline
A road or path with a sharp incline or decline. Used for slopes that are notably steep and difficult to climb or descend.
急坂(きゅうざか)を登(のぼ)る。
To climb a steep hill.
この急坂(きゅうざか)は自転車(じてんしゃ)では無理(むり)だ。
This steep slope is impossible by bicycle.
駅(えき)から家(いえ)まで急坂(きゅうざか)が続(つづ)くので、毎日(まいにち)の通勤(つうきん)がいい運動(うんどう)になっている。
Since there are steep hills all the way from the station to my house, my daily commute serves as good exercise.

Composed of 急(きゅう) (steep/sudden) + 坂(さか) (slope/hill). Can also be read as きゅうはん in more formal or technical contexts, though きゅうざか is the standard reading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 急坂(きゅうざか)を登(のぼ)る: to climb a steep hill
  • 急坂(きゅうざか)を下(くだ)る: to go down a steep hill
  • 急坂(きゅうざか)が続(つづ)く: steep hills continue
  • 急坂(きゅうざか)注意(ちゅうい): caution: steep slope (road sign)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 坂道(さかみち): sloping road — any road on a slope, not necessarily steep
  • 上(のぼ)り坂(ざか): uphill slope — going up, any gradient
  • 下(くだ)り坂(ざか): downhill slope — going down, any gradient
  • 勾配(こうばい): gradient/grade — technical term for slope angle
