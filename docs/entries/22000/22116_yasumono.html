安物(やすもの)

やすもの
noun
cheap item; bargain goods
1. cheap item; low-quality goods; bargain stuff
An inexpensive item, typically implying poor quality. Often used with a negative connotation to suggest something is cheaply made or not worth buying.
これは安物(やすもの)だからすぐ壊(こわ)れる。
This is a cheap item, so it breaks easily.
安物(やすもの)の傘(かさ)はすぐに壊(こわ)れてしまう。
Cheap umbrellas break right away.
安物(やすもの)買(か)いの銭(ぜに)失(うしな)いという言葉(ことば)があるように、安(やす)いものには理由(りゆう)がある。
As the saying 'buy cheap, lose money' goes, cheap things come cheap for a reason.

Composed of 安(やす)い (cheap) + 物(もの) (thing). Almost always carries a negative nuance — it does not simply mean "inexpensive" but implies low quality. The word is commonly used when criticizing the quality of goods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 安物(やすもの)の服(ふく): cheap clothes
  • 安物(やすもの)の時計(とけい): cheap watch
  • 安物(やすもの)買(か)いの銭(ぜに)失(うしな)い: penny wise, pound foolish (proverb)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 格安(かくやす): bargain-priced — neutral or positive, emphasizing good value
  • 粗悪品(そあくひん): shoddy goods — more formal and strongly negative
  • お買(か)い得(どく): good deal — positive connotation, emphasizing value

CULTURAL NOTE:
The proverb 安物(やすもの)買(か)いの銭(ぜに)失(うしな)い is widely known and means buying cheap things ends up costing more in the long run, equivalent to the English "buy cheap, buy twice."
