超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)

ちょうとっきゅう
noun
super express; at top speed
1. super express train; ultra-fast train
A train service faster than a regular express. Originally referred to the first Shinkansen services.
超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で東京(とうきょう)に向(む)かった。
I headed to Tokyo on the super express.
新幹線(しんかんせん)は超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)として開業(かいぎょう)した。
The Shinkansen was launched as a super express service.
この電車(でんしゃ)は超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)だから途中(とちゅう)停車(ていしゃ)が少(すく)ない。
This train is a super express, so it makes few intermediate stops.
2. at top speed; in a huge rush; extremely fast
Used figuratively to describe doing something at maximum speed or completing a task in an extremely short time.
超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で仕上(しあ)げてください。
Please finish it at top speed.
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)いので超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で作業(さぎょう)した。
The deadline was close, so I worked at top speed.
超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で企画書(きかくしょ)をまとめたので、内容(ないよう)に不安(ふあん)が残(のこ)る。
I put together the proposal at breakneck speed, so I'm still uneasy about the content.

Composed of 超(ちょう) (super, ultra) + 特急(とっきゅう) (limited express). Originally a railway term for trains faster than 特急(とっきゅう), notably the first Shinkansen in 1964. Now commonly used figuratively to mean doing something extremely fast.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で仕上(しあ)げる: to finish at top speed
  • 超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で対応(たいおう)する: to respond at maximum speed
  • 超特急(ちょうとっきゅう)で届(とど)ける: to deliver at top speed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 特急(とっきゅう): limited express — the standard fast train service; also used figuratively but with less intensity
  • 急行(きゅうこう): express — slower than 特急(とっきゅう)
  • 大至急(だいしきゅう): extremely urgent — similar figurative meaning but focused on urgency rather than speed
