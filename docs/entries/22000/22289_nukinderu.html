1. to stand out; to excel; to surpass others
To be markedly superior to others in ability, skill, or achievement. A literary and somewhat elevated expression.
彼(かれ)の才能(さいのう)は抜(ぬ)きん出(で)ている。
His talent is outstanding.
数学(すうがく)の成績(せいせき)で抜(ぬ)きん出(で)た存在(そんざい)だ。
He is a standout when it comes to math grades.
多(おお)くの候補者(こうほしゃ)の中(なか)から抜(ぬ)きん出(で)た実力(じつりょく)で選(えら)ばれた。
She was chosen from among many candidates thanks to her outstanding ability.

A literary verb meaning to rise above the rest. Most often appears in the ている form (抜(ぬ)きん出(で)ている) to describe an ongoing state of excellence. Originally from classical Japanese 抜(ぬ)き出(い)づ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 才能(さいのう)が抜(ぬ)きん出(で)ている: one's talent is outstanding
  • 実力(じつりょく)で抜(ぬ)きん出(で)る: to excel through ability
  • 群(ぐん)を抜(ぬ)いて抜(ぬ)きん出(で)る: to stand far above the rest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 優(すぐ)れる: to excel — more common and less literary; used in everyday contexts
  • 秀(ひい)でる: to excel — literary, similar register; emphasizes shining brilliance
  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)する: to be outstanding — more formal and written; used for notable figures
