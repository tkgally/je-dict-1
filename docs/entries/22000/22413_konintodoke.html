婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)

こんいんとどけ
noun
marriage registration form; marriage certificate
1. marriage registration form; notification of marriage
The official document submitted to a local government office to legally register a marriage in Japan. Submitting this form is what makes a marriage legally valid — no ceremony is required.
婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する。
To submit a marriage registration form.
婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)には証人(しょうにん)が二人(ふたり)必要(ひつよう)です。
Two witnesses are needed for a marriage registration form.
二人(ふたり)は記念日(きねんび)に合(あ)わせて市役所(しやくしょ)に婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)を出(だ)しに行(い)った。
The two went to the city hall to submit their marriage registration form on their anniversary.

Composed of 婚姻(こんいん) (marriage) and 届(とどけ) (notification/report). In Japan, marriage becomes legal when this form is accepted at a municipal office — no wedding ceremony is legally required. The form requires signatures from the couple and two adult witnesses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)を出(だ)す: to submit a marriage registration
  • 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a marriage registration (formal)
  • 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)が受理(じゅり)される: the marriage registration is accepted
  • 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ)の書(か)き方(かた): how to fill out a marriage registration

CULTURAL NOTE:
Many couples choose a meaningful date to submit their 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ), such as a holiday, anniversary, or auspicious day. Some municipal offices accept forms 24 hours a day. Recently, some municipalities offer decorative 婚姻届(こんいんとどけ) designs featuring local characters or landmarks.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 離婚届(りこんとどけ): divorce registration form
  • 出生届(しゅっしょうとどけ): birth registration form
  • 入籍(にゅうせき): entering the family register — often used colloquially as a synonym for getting legally married
