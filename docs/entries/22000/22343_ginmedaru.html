銀(ぎん)メダル

ぎんめだる
noun
silver medal
1. silver medal
The medal awarded for second place in a competition, especially in the Olympics and other sporting events.
彼女(かのじょ)は銀(ぎん)メダルを獲得(かくとく)した。
She won a silver medal.
オリンピックで銀(ぎん)メダルに終(お)わって悔(くや)しかった。
It was frustrating to end up with only a silver medal at the Olympics.
日本(にほん)選手団(せんしゅだん)は金(きん)メダル三(さん)個(こ)と銀(ぎん)メダル五(ご)個(こ)を獲得(かくとく)した。
The Japanese team won three gold medals and five silver medals.

Standard term for the second-place medal in competitive events, particularly the Olympics. Written with katakana メダル as it is a loanword from English.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 銀(ぎん)メダルを獲得(かくとく)する: to win a silver medal
  • 銀(ぎん)メダルに終(お)わる: to end up with a silver medal (implies disappointment)
  • 銀(ぎん)メダリスト: silver medalist

RELATED TERMS:

  • 金(きん)メダル: gold medal
  • 銅(どう)メダル: bronze medal
  • メダルラッシュ: medal rush — winning many medals in succession
