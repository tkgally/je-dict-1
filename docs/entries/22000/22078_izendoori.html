以前(いぜん)通(どお)り

いぜんどおり
expression
as before; as previously; same as before
1. as before; as previously; same as before
Indicates that something continues or should continue in the same manner as it was before a change or event. Slightly more formal than 今まで通り.
以前(いぜん)通(どお)り続(つづ)けてください。
Please continue as before.
病気(びょうき)の後(あと)も以前(いぜん)通(どお)りの生活(せいかつ)に戻(もど)れた。
After the illness, I was able to return to my life as before.
改装(かいそう)工事(こうじ)中(ちゅう)ですが、営業(えいぎょう)は以前(いぜん)通(どお)り行(おこな)っております。
We are under renovation, but business is operating as before.

A combination of 以前(いぜん) (before, previously) and 通(どお)り (as, in accordance with). Used to indicate that something continues or should continue in the same way as it was before a change or event. Slightly more formal than 今(いま)まで通(どお)り.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 以前(いぜん)通(どお)り + verb: to do something as before
  • 以前(いぜん)通(どお)りの + noun: the same ~ as before
  • 以前(いぜん)通(どお)りに: in the same manner as before

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 今(いま)まで通(どお)り — as usual; as before (more everyday/casual)
  • 従来(じゅうらい)通(どお)り — as conventionally done; as has been the case (more formal/institutional)
  • 相変(あいか)わらず — as always; same as ever (can imply mild exasperation)
