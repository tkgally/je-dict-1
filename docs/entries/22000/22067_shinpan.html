新版(しんぱん)

しんぱん
noun
new edition; revised edition
1. new edition; revised edition
新版(しんぱん)が出(で)た。
A new edition came out.
この辞書(じしょ)の新版(しんぱん)が来月(らいげつ)発売(はつばい)される。
The new edition of this dictionary will go on sale next month.
旧版(きゅうばん)と新版(しんぱん)の違(ちが)いは、巻末(かんまつ)の索引(さくいん)が充実(じゅうじつ)した点(てん)だ。
The difference between the old and new editions is that the index at the end has been expanded.

A compound of 新(しん) (new) and 版(はん/ぱん) (edition/version). Used for updated or revised versions of books, textbooks, software, and other published works. The reading is しんぱん (with rendaku on 版(はん)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新版(しんぱん)を出(だ)す: to release a new edition
  • 新版(しんぱん)が出(で)る: a new edition comes out
  • 改訂(かいてい)新版(しんぱん): revised new edition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 改訂版(かいていばん) — revised edition (emphasizes revision of content)
  • 増補版(ぞうほばん) — expanded edition (content added)
  • 旧版(きゅうばん) — old edition (the opposite)
  • 初版(しょはん) — first edition
