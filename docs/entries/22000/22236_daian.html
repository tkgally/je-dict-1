代案(だいあん)

だいあん
noun
alternative plan; counterproposal
1. alternative plan; counterproposal; alternative idea
A different plan or proposal offered as a substitute when the original one is rejected or deemed inadequate.
代案(だいあん)を出(だ)してください。
Please come up with an alternative plan.
批判(ひはん)するなら代案(だいあん)を示(しめ)せ。
If you're going to criticize, then show an alternative.
予算(よさん)の関係(かんけい)で元(もと)の計画(けいかく)は中止(ちゅうし)になり、代案(だいあん)を検討(けんとう)することになった。
Due to budget constraints, the original plan was cancelled, and we decided to consider an alternative.

Compound of 代(だい) (substitute) and 案(あん) (plan/proposal). Very commonly used in business, politics, and meetings. The phrase 代案(だいあん)を出(だ)す (to offer an alternative) is especially common — it implies that simply rejecting a proposal is not enough; one should also suggest a replacement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代案(だいあん)を出(だ)す: to offer an alternative plan
  • 代案(だいあん)を示(しめ)す: to present an alternative
  • 代案(だいあん)を検討(けんとう)する: to consider an alternative
  • 代案(だいあん)がない: there is no alternative
  • 代案(だいあん)を求(もと)める: to ask for an alternative

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 対案(たいあん): counterproposal — specifically a competing proposal, often in political or legislative contexts
  • 代替案(だいたいあん): alternative plan — longer form with essentially the same meaning
  • 案(あん): plan; proposal — the base word, without the 'substitute' nuance
