1. practice; lesson; training (especially in traditional arts or cultural activities)
Refers to taking lessons or practicing a skill, particularly in traditional Japanese arts such as tea ceremony, flower arrangement, calligraphy, dance, or music. The お- prefix adds politeness.
今日(きょう)はお稽古(けいこ)の日(ひ)だ。
Today is lesson day.
茶道(さどう)のお稽古(けいこ)に通(かよ)っている。
I attend tea ceremony lessons.
娘(むすめ)は小(ちい)さい頃(ころ)からピアノのお稽古(けいこ)を続(つづ)けていて、今(いま)ではかなり上手(じょうず)になった。
My daughter has been taking piano lessons since she was little, and now she's gotten quite good.

The base form is 稽古(けいこ), with the honorific prefix お adding politeness. 稽古(けいこ) literally means 'to study the old' (from 稽(けい) 'examine' and 古(こ) 'old'), reflecting the idea of learning from tradition.

The word is strongly associated with traditional Japanese arts and cultural activities, though it can also be used for modern lessons like piano or ballet. It implies ongoing, disciplined practice under a teacher.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お稽古(けいこ)事(ごと): lessons and hobbies (as a category)
  • お稽古(けいこ)に通(かよ)う: to attend lessons regularly
  • お稽古(けいこ)をつける: to give a lesson (from the teacher's perspective)
  • お稽古(けいこ)日(び): lesson day

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 練習(れんしゅう): practice — general practice or training, not limited to arts
  • レッスン: lesson — borrowed from English, used for modern activities
  • 修行(しゅぎょう): training / ascetic practice — more intense, often spiritual or martial arts
