1. to make worse; to aggravate; to complicate
To cause an illness, situation, or relationship to become more difficult or severe through neglect, mishandling, or stubbornness.
風邪(かぜ)をこじらせた。
I let my cold get worse.
問題(もんだい)をこじらせないように注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful not to make the problem worse.
無理(むり)をして体調(たいちょう)をこじらせてしまい、入院(にゅういん)することになった。
I overexerted myself and my condition worsened, so I ended up being hospitalized.

Often used for illnesses that worsen due to neglect or overexertion. The most common collocation is 風邪(かぜ)をこじらせる (to let a cold get worse). Also used figuratively for relationships and situations that become complicated.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 風邪(かぜ)をこじらせる: to let a cold get worse
  • 病気(びょうき)をこじらせる: to aggravate an illness
  • 関係(かんけい)をこじらせる: to complicate a relationship
  • 話(はなし)をこじらせる: to make a discussion more complicated

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悪化(あっか)させる: to make worse — more formal, used broadly for conditions and situations
  • 複雑(ふくざつ)にする: to make complicated — focuses on complexity rather than worsening

USAGE:
In modern casual speech, こじらせる is also used as slang (e.g., こじらせ女子(じょし), こじらせ男子(だんし)) to describe someone who overthinks or has complicated feelings about relationships or identity. This usage is informal and common on social media.
