1. making transparent; increasing transparency or openness
The process of making something more open, visible, or accountable. Most commonly used in the context of governance, business, or organizational processes.
情報(じょうほう)の透明化(とうめいか)を進(すす)める。
To promote information transparency.
経営(けいえい)の透明化(とうめいか)が求(もと)められている。
Greater transparency in management is being demanded.
政治(せいじ)資金(しきん)の透明化(とうめいか)は国民(こくみん)の信頼(しんらい)を得(え)るために不可欠(ふかけつ)だ。
Transparency of political funds is essential to earning public trust.

A noun formed with the suffix 化(か) (transformation), meaning the process of making something transparent or open. Widely used in discussions about governance, corporate responsibility, and organizational reform.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報(じょうほう)の透明化(とうめいか): information transparency
  • 経営(けいえい)の透明化(とうめいか): management transparency
  • 意思決定(いしけってい)の透明化(とうめいか): decision-making transparency
  • 透明化(とうめいか)を図(はか)る: to aim for transparency
  • 透明化(とうめいか)を進(すす)める: to promote transparency

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 透明性(とうめいせい): transparency (the quality itself, rather than the process)
  • 可視化(かしか): visualization — making data or processes visible, often technical
  • 公開(こうかい): disclosure — making information publicly available

FORMATION:
