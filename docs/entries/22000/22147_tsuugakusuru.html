1. to commute to school; to attend school regularly
To travel to school on a regular basis. Refers to the daily journey between home and school, not the act of studying there.
電車(でんしゃ)で通学(つうがく)する。
To commute to school by train.
自転車(じてんしゃ)で通学(つうがく)している生徒(せいと)が多(おお)い。
Many students commute to school by bicycle.
家(いえ)から学校(がっこう)まで片道(かたみち)１時間(じかん)かけて通学(つうがく)している。
I spend one hour each way commuting from home to school.

Specifically refers to the regular commute between home and school. The noun form 通学(つうがく) is commonly used in compound words related to school commuting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通学(つうがく)路(ろ): school route
  • 通学(つうがく)時間(じかん): commuting time (to school)
  • 通学(つうがく)定期(ていき): student commuter pass
  • 徒歩(とほ)通学(つうがく): walking to school
  • 通学(つうがく)圏内(けんない): within commuting distance of a school

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 通勤(つうきん)する: to commute to work — same structure but for employment rather than school
  • 登校(とうこう)する: to go to school — refers to a single trip to school on a given day, not the habitual commute

REGISTER:
Neutral. Used in both everyday conversation and formal contexts such as school regulations.
