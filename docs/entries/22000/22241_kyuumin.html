1. dormancy; hibernation; lying inactive
A state of inactivity in biological organisms (plants, animals, seeds) during unfavorable conditions.
冬(ふゆ)の間(あいだ)、種(たね)は休眠(きゅうみん)する。
During winter, the seeds go dormant.
この植物(しょくぶつ)は夏(なつ)に休眠(きゅうみん)状態(じょうたい)に入(はい)る。
This plant enters a dormant state in summer.
球根(きゅうこん)は涼(すず)しい場所(ばしょ)で休眠(きゅうみん)させてから植(う)える。
Let the bulb go dormant in a cool place before planting it.
2. lying dormant; being inactive
Used figuratively for accounts, companies, or organizations that exist but are not currently active.
休眠口座(きゅうみんこうざ)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be aware of dormant accounts.
休眠(きゅうみん)会社(かいしゃ)として届(とど)け出(で)た。
It was registered as a dormant company.
長年(ながねん)休眠(きゅうみん)していた特許(とっきょ)が再(ふたた)び注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
A patent that had been lying dormant for years is attracting attention again.

Compound of 休(きゅう) (rest) and 眠(みん) (sleep). Originally a biological term for the inactive period of organisms, now widely used in business and finance for inactive entities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 休眠(きゅうみん)する: to go dormant
  • 休眠(きゅうみん)状態(じょうたい): dormant state
  • 休眠口座(きゅうみんこうざ): dormant account
  • 休眠(きゅうみん)会社(かいしゃ): dormant company
  • 休眠(きゅうみん)期間(きかん): dormancy period

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冬眠(とうみん): hibernation — specifically winter dormancy in animals, not used figuratively for companies
  • 活動(かつどう)休止(きゅうし): suspension of activities — describes a deliberate pause, often for groups or performers
  • 休止(きゅうし): pause; suspension — more general, implies a temporary stop with intention to resume
