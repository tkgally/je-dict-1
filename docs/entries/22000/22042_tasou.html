多層(たそう)

たそう
noun
multi-layered; multilevel
1. multi-layered; multilevel; having many layers or strata
Having or consisting of multiple layers, levels, or strata. Used for physical structures, coatings, and abstract concepts like social hierarchy.
多層(たそう)構造(こうぞう)の建物(たてもの)。
A multi-story building.
多層(たそう)フィルムで食品(しょくひん)を保護(ほご)する。
To protect food with multi-layer film.
この問題(もんだい)は多層(たそう)的(てき)なアプローチで分析(ぶんせき)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
This issue needs to be analyzed with a multilayered approach.

Composed of 多(た) (many, multi-) and 層(そう) (layer, stratum). Functions primarily as a prefix or modifier (with の or 的(てき)) rather than as a standalone noun.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多層(たそう)構造(こうぞう): multilayer structure
  • 多層(たそう)的(てき): multilayered (adjectival)
  • 多層(たそう)フィルム: multilayer film
  • 多層(たそう)防御(ぼうぎょ): multilayer defense
  • 多層(たそう)基板(きばん): multilayer circuit board

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重層(じゅうそう): stratified / multi-layered — emphasizes overlapping layers, often used in academic contexts
  • 複層(ふくそう): multi-layered — often used for glass (複層(ふくそう)ガラス = double-glazed glass)
  • 単層(たんそう): single-layer — the opposite concept
