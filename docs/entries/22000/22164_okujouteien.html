屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)

おくじょうていえん
noun
rooftop garden
1. rooftop garden; roof garden
A garden or green space built on the roof of a building. Found on department stores, office buildings, and residences, providing a place to relax or grow plants in urban areas.
デパートの屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)。
A department store rooftop garden.
ビルの屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)で昼食(ちゅうしょく)を取(と)った。
I had lunch in the building's rooftop garden.
都市(とし)の緑化(りょくか)対策(たいさく)として、屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)を設置(せっち)する企業(きぎょう)が増(ふ)えている。
As an urban greening measure, more companies are installing rooftop gardens.

A compound of 屋上(おくじょう) (rooftop) and 庭園(ていえん) (garden). Rooftop gardens are found throughout Japanese cities, particularly on department stores and commercial buildings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)を設置(せっち)する: to install a rooftop garden
  • 屋上(おくじょう)庭園(ていえん)の設計(せっけい): rooftop garden design
  • 屋上(おくじょう)緑化(りょくか): rooftop greening

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 屋上(おくじょう): rooftop — the location itself, without necessarily having a garden
  • 庭園(ていえん): garden — a garden at ground level
  • 屋上(おくじょう)菜園(さいえん): rooftop vegetable garden — specifically for growing food

CULTURAL NOTE:
Many Japanese department stores traditionally have rooftop spaces, though these were originally used for amusement areas and playgrounds rather than gardens. Modern rooftop gardens serve both aesthetic and environmental purposes, helping to reduce the urban heat island effect.
