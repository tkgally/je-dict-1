1. moving in; registering as a new resident
The act of officially moving into a new municipality or area and registering at the local ward or city office.
転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)を出(だ)す。
To submit a change-of-address registration.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)したら14日以内(じゅうよっかいない)に転入届(てんにゅうとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the move-in registration within 14 days of moving.
市役所(しやくしょ)で転入(てんにゅう)の手続(てつづ)きをした。
I completed the move-in procedures at the city hall.
2. transfer; transferring in (to a new school or workplace)
Entering a new school or organization as a transfer, not from the beginning.
転入生(てんにゅうせい)を紹介(しょうかい)する。
To introduce a transfer student.
二学期(にがっき)から転入(てんにゅう)してきた。
I transferred in starting from the second semester.
転入(てんにゅう)試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)して新(あたら)しい学校(がっこう)に通(かよ)い始(はじ)めた。
I passed the transfer exam and started attending the new school.

A key administrative and school term. When moving between municipalities in Japan, residents must submit a 転出届(てんしゅつとどけ) (move-out notice) at their old address and a 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ) (move-in notice) at the new one.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 転入届(てんにゅうとどけ): move-in registration form
  • 転入(てんにゅう)手続(てつづ)き: move-in procedures
  • 転入生(てんにゅうせい): transfer student
  • 転入(てんにゅう)試験(しけん): transfer entrance exam
  • 転入(てんにゅう)する: to move in; to transfer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 転出(てんしゅつ): moving out — the opposite, leaving a municipality or school
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し: moving (house) — the general act of relocating, without the administrative nuance
  • 転校(てんこう): school transfer — specifically changing schools, subset of sense 2
  • 入居(にゅうきょ): moving in (to a dwelling) — focuses on entering a specific residence, not municipal registration
