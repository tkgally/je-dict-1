後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)

こうぶざせき
noun
back seat; rear seat
1. back seat; rear seat
The seat(s) in the back of a vehicle, especially a car. Distinguished from the front passenger seat and the driver's seat.
後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)に座(すわ)る。
To sit in the back seat.
後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)のシートベルトも必(かなら)ず締(し)めてください。
Please make sure to fasten your seatbelt in the back seat as well.
子供(こども)は後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)に乗(の)せて、チャイルドシートを使用(しよう)してください。
Please have children ride in the back seat and use a child seat.

A compound of 後部(こうぶ) (rear part) and 座席(ざせき) (seat). Primarily used for car seating but can also apply to buses and airplanes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)のシートベルト: back seat seatbelt
  • 後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)に乗(の)る: to ride in the back seat
  • 後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき)の乗客(じょうきゃく): back seat passenger

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 後(うし)ろの席(せき): the seat in the back — more casual and everyday expression
  • 助手席(じょしゅせき): front passenger seat — the seat next to the driver
  • 運転席(うんてんせき): driver's seat

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, wearing seatbelts in the 後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき) became mandatory on all roads in 2008. In taxis, VIP passengers typically sit in the 後部(こうぶ)座席(ざせき), with the seat directly behind the driver considered the most prestigious position.
