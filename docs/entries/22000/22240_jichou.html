1. self-mockery; self-deprecation; self-ridicule
The act of laughing at or making fun of oneself. Can be a coping mechanism, social strategy, or expression of humility.
自嘲(じちょう)して笑(わら)った。
He laughed at himself self-deprecatingly.
自嘲気味(じちょうぎみ)に「自分(じぶん)はダメだな」と言(い)った。
He said in a self-mocking tone, 'I'm no good.'
彼女(かのじょ)は自嘲(じちょう)を込(こ)めた笑(え)みを浮(う)かべた。
She wore a self-deprecating smile.
自嘲(じちょう)を交(まじ)えながら失敗談(しっぱいだん)を語(かた)るのが彼(かれ)のスタイルだ。
His style is to talk about his failures while mixing in self-deprecation.

Compound of 自(じ) (self) and 嘲(ちょう) (ridicule/mockery). Describes deliberately making fun of oneself, often with a bittersweet or wry tone. It differs from genuine self-hatred in that there is typically an element of humor or detachment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自嘲(じちょう)する: to mock oneself
  • 自嘲気味(じちょうぎみ)に: in a somewhat self-mocking way
  • 自嘲(じちょう)の笑(え)み: a self-deprecating smile
  • 自嘲(じちょう)を込(こ)めて: with self-mockery
  • 自嘲的(じちょうてき)な: self-deprecating (adjective)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自虐(じぎゃく): self-deprecation; self-inflicted abuse — stronger than 自嘲(じちょう), often used for humor (自虐(じぎゃく)ネタ = self-deprecating joke)
  • 謙遜(けんそん): modesty; humility — a social convention of downplaying oneself, without the mocking tone
  • 卑下(ひげ): self-abasement — genuinely putting oneself down, more negative than 自嘲(じちょう)

NOTE:
The kanji 嘲(ちょう) is the same as in 嘲笑(ちょうしょう) (ridicule) and 嘲(あざけ)る (to mock).
