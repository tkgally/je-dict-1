1. affixing; pasting; sticking on; attaching
The act of sticking or affixing something (a stamp, sticker, label, patch, etc.) onto a surface. A formal word commonly used in official and administrative contexts.
収入印紙(しゅうにゅういんし)を貼付(ちょうふ)する。
To affix a revenue stamp.
写真(しゃしん)を所定(しょてい)の欄(らん)に貼付(ちょうふ)してください。
Please affix a photo in the designated area.
申請書(しんせいしょ)に切手(きって)を貼付(ちょうふ)して提出(ていしゅつ)する。
Affix a stamp to the application form and submit it.
湿布(しっぷ)を患部(かんぶ)に貼付(ちょうふ)した。
I applied a compress to the affected area.

A formal word for sticking or affixing. The standard reading is ちょうふ, though てんぷ is also widely used (originally considered non-standard but now accepted). Very common on official forms, in medical contexts (applying patches/compresses), and in administrative instructions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 収入印紙(しゅうにゅういんし)を貼付(ちょうふ)する: to affix a revenue stamp
  • 写真(しゃしん)貼付(ちょうふ)欄(らん): photo attachment area
  • 貼付(ちょうふ)剤(ざい): adhesive patch (medical)
  • 貼付(ちょうふ)する: to affix; to paste on

READING NOTE:
The reading てんぷ is also common and accepted, though ちょうふ is the traditional standard. In everyday conversation, 貼(は)る (to stick, paste) is used instead.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 貼(は)る: to stick; to paste — the everyday verb for the same action
  • 添付(てんぷ): attachment (especially of files) — attaching a document or file, not physically sticking something on
  • 接着(せっちゃく): adhesion; bonding — emphasizes the bonding between two surfaces
