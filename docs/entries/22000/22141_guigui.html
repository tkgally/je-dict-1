ぐいぐい

ぐいぐい
adverb
forcefully; aggressively; with gusto
1. forcefully; with force; vigorously
Describes pulling, pushing, or doing something with physical force and determination.
ロープをぐいぐい引(ひ)っ張(ぱ)った。
I pulled the rope hard.
ビールをぐいぐい飲(の)んだ。
I gulped down the beer.
彼(かれ)は人混(ひとご)みをぐいぐい押(お)し分(わ)けて前(まえ)に進(すす)んだ。
He pushed his way forcefully through the crowd.
2. aggressively; persistently; pushily
Describes approaching something or someone with assertive, relentless energy. Often used for someone who is pushy or forward in social situations.
ぐいぐい来(く)る人(ひと)が苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not good with pushy people.
彼女(かのじょ)はぐいぐい距離(きょり)を詰(つ)めてくる。
She aggressively closes the distance.
新人(しんじん)なのにぐいぐい自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)を言(い)うところが頼(たの)もしい。
It's reassuring that even though they're a newcomer, they assertively speak their mind.

An onomatopoeic adverb (擬態語) that conveys the sense of forceful, repeated action. The doubled form emphasizes persistence and strength. Can have either positive (energetic, assertive) or negative (pushy, aggressive) connotations depending on context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぐいぐい引(ひ)く: to pull hard
  • ぐいぐい押(お)す: to push forcefully
  • ぐいぐい飲(の)む: to gulp down drinks
  • ぐいぐい来(く)る: to come on strong (socially)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぐんぐん: rapidly, steadily — emphasizes speed of progress rather than force
  • がぶがぶ: gulping — specifically for drinking large amounts
  • ずんずん: marching forward — emphasizes forward momentum
  • がんがん: aggressively, intensely — stronger, often for sound or headaches too
