1. to worry needlessly in advance; to borrow trouble
To worry about something before it happens, especially when the worry turns out to be unnecessary. Almost always appears in the set expression 取り越し苦労.
取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう)だったね。
It was needless worry, wasn't it?
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)は取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう)で、ちゃんと合格(ごうかく)していた。
My worries about the exam results were needless — I passed just fine.
まだ何(なに)も決(き)まっていないのに、先(さき)のことを取(と)り越(こ)して心配(しんぱい)するのはやめよう。
Nothing has been decided yet, so let's stop worrying about the future prematurely.

This verb is most commonly encountered in the compound noun 取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう) (needless worry; borrowing trouble), which is a very common expression. The standalone verb form is less frequent.

KEY EXPRESSION:

  • 取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう): needless anxiety; worrying about things that haven't happened yet

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう)だった: it was needless worry
  • 取(と)り越(こ)し苦労(くろう)をする: to worry needlessly
  • 取(と)り越(こ)して心配(しんぱい)する: to worry prematurely

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 杞憂(きゆう): groundless fears — more literary, from a Chinese story
  • 心配(しんぱい)し過(す)ぎ: worrying too much — more general

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (す row). Conjugates: 取(と)り越(こ)さない, 取(と)り越(こ)して, 取(と)り越(こ)します.
