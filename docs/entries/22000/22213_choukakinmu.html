1. overtime work; work beyond regular hours; excess duty
Work performed beyond the standard or legally mandated working hours, especially in a formal or administrative context.
超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)が多(おお)すぎる。
There is too much overtime work.
超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)手当(てあて)は正(ただ)しく支払(しはら)われるべきだ。
Overtime pay should be paid properly.
月(つき)の超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)が上限(じょうげん)を超(こ)えないよう管理(かんり)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to manage things so that monthly overtime doesn't exceed the upper limit.

Composed of 超過(ちょうか) (excess, exceeding) + 勤務(きんむ) (duty, work). A formal, administrative term for overtime. In everyday conversation, 残業(ざんぎょう) is much more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)手当(てあて) — overtime pay/allowance
  • 超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)時間(じかん) — overtime hours
  • 超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)命令(めいれい) — overtime order (official directive to work overtime)
  • 超過勤務(ちょうかきんむ)の削減(さくげん) — reduction of overtime

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 残業(ざんぎょう) — overtime (everyday term; far more commonly used in conversation)
  • 時間外(じかんがい)労働(ろうどう) — work outside regular hours (legal/labor law term)
  • 勤務(きんむ) — work, duty (general term)

REGISTER:
Primarily used in official, administrative, and legal contexts — government documents, labor regulations, and corporate HR materials. In everyday workplace conversation, 残業(ざんぎょう) is strongly preferred.
