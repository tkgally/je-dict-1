研究生(けんきゅうせい)

けんきゅうせい
noun
research student; non-degree research student
1. research student; non-degree research student; auditing researcher
A student affiliated with a university or research institution who conducts research under a professor's guidance but is not enrolled in a formal degree program. Common as a preparatory step before entering a Japanese graduate school.
大学(だいがく)の研究生(けんきゅうせい)になる。
To become a research student at a university.
研究生(けんきゅうせい)として一年間(いちねんかん)日本(にほん)の大学(だいがく)に在籍(ざいせき)した。
I was enrolled at a Japanese university as a research student for one year.
多(おお)くの留学生(りゅうがくせい)は、まず研究生(けんきゅうせい)として来日(らいにち)し、その後(あと)大学院(だいがくいん)に進学(しんがく)する。
Many international students first come to Japan as research students and then advance to graduate school.

In the Japanese university system, a 研究生(けんきゅうせい) is a special status for individuals who want to study or do research at a university without being enrolled in a degree program. This is distinct from a 大学院生(だいがくいんせい) (graduate student).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 研究生(けんきゅうせい)制度(せいど): research student system
  • 研究生(けんきゅうせい)として在籍(ざいせき)する: to be enrolled as a research student
  • 研究生(けんきゅ���せい)期間(きかん): research student period

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大学院生(だいがくいんせい): graduate student — enrolled in a formal master's or doctoral program
  • 聴講生(ちょうこうせい): auditor — attends lectures but does not earn credits
  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい): international student — refers to nationality status, not enrollment type

CULTURAL NOTE:
The 研究生(けんきゅうせい) system is particularly important for international students coming to Japan. Many use this period (typically 6 months to 2 years) to improve their Japanese, prepare for entrance exams, and build a relationship with their prospective supervisor.
