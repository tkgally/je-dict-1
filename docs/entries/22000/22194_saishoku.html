菜食(さいしょく)

さいしょく
noun
vegetarian diet; eating vegetables
1. vegetarian diet; plant-based eating; eating vegetables
A diet centered on vegetables and plant-based foods, excluding or minimizing meat and fish.
彼女(かのじょ)は菜食(さいしょく)を続(つづ)けている。
She continues to follow a vegetarian diet.
健康(けんこう)のために菜食(さいしょく)中心(ちゅうしん)の生活(せいかつ)にした。
I switched to a mainly vegetarian lifestyle for my health.
この店(みせ)は菜食(さいしょく)主義者(しゅぎしゃ)にも対応(たいおう)したメニューがある。
This restaurant has a menu that caters to vegetarians too.

Literally 'vegetable eating,' composed of 菜(さい) (vegetable) + 食(しょく) (eating/food). Refers to the practice or diet itself rather than the ideology — for the ideology, use 菜食(さいしょく)主義(しゅぎ) (vegetarianism).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 菜食(さいしょく)主義(しゅぎ) — vegetarianism
  • 菜食(さいしょく)主義者(しゅぎしゃ) — vegetarian (person)
  • 菜食(さいしょく)中心(ちゅうしん) — mainly vegetarian
  • 菜食(さいしょく)料理(りょうり) — vegetarian cuisine
  • 完全(かんぜん)菜���(さいしょく) — strict vegetarian diet; vegan diet

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ベジタリアン — vegetarian (loanword, more common in casual conversation)
  • ビーガン — vegan (loanword)
  • 精進料理(しょうじんりょうり) — Buddhist vegetarian cuisine (traditional Japanese term with religious connotations)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Traditional Japanese cuisine historically included little meat, and Buddhist 精進料理(しょうじんりょうり) has a long history. Modern 菜食(さいしょく)主義(しゅぎ) as a lifestyle choice is growing but still relatively uncommon in Japan compared to Western countries.
