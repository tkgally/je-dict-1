1. to separate; to isolate; to detach
To divide something that was joined or mixed into distinct parts, or for something to come apart from a whole. Used both transitively (to separate X) and intransitively (X separates).
油(あぶら)と水(みず)が分離(ぶんり)する。
Oil and water separate.
政治(せいじ)と宗教(しゅうきょう)を分離(ぶんり)する。
To separate politics and religion.
ロケットの一段目(いちだんめ)が切(き)り離(はな)されて本体(ほんたい)から分離(ぶんり)した。
The rocket's first stage was jettisoned and separated from the main body.

Describes the process of dividing connected or mixed things into separate parts. Can be used both for physical separation (chemicals, mechanical parts) and abstract separation (concepts, institutions).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 政教(せいきょう)分離(ぶんり): separation of church and state
  • 分離(ぶんり)独立(どくりつ): secession and independence
  • 分離(ぶんり)不安(ふあん): separation anxiety
  • 成分(せいぶん)を分離(ぶんり)する: to separate components
  • 分離(ぶんり)手術(しゅじゅつ): separation surgery (e.g., conjoined twins)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分(わ)ける: to divide / to separate — everyday word, broader usage
  • 隔離(かくり)する: to isolate / to quarantine — emphasizes keeping apart, especially for safety
  • 切(き)り離(はな)す: to detach / to cut off — physical detachment with a sense of severing
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)する: to divide / to split — dividing into portions, often equally

REGISTER:
Formal. Standard in scientific, legal, and technical writing. In everyday speech, 分(わ)ける or 離(はな)す are more common.
