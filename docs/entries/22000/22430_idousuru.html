1. to move from one place to another; to travel; to relocate
To change one's position or location, whether a person moving between places or an object being transferred.
電車(でんしゃ)で移動(いどう)する。
To travel by train.
会議室(かいぎしつ)に移動(いどう)しましょう。
Let's move to the meeting room.
台風(たいふう)は北(きた)に向(む)かって移動(いどう)しており、明日(あした)には関東(かんとう)地方(ちほう)に接近(せっきん)する見込(みこ)みだ。
The typhoon is moving northward and is expected to approach the Kanto region tomorrow.

A versatile and very common verb used for any kind of movement from one location to another. Applies to people traveling, objects being relocated, weather systems moving, or even data being transferred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 移動(いどう)手段(しゅだん): means of transportation
  • 移動(いどう)時間(じかん): travel time
  • 移動(いどう)距離(きょり): distance traveled
  • 移動(いどう)中(ちゅう): in transit, on the move
  • 大{移動(いどう): mass migration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 動(うご)く: to move — more general; includes movement in place (not just from A to B)
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す: to move (house) — specifically for changing residence
  • 転勤(てんきん)する: to transfer (job) — specifically a work transfer to a different location

USAGE:
In modern Japanese, 移動(いどう)する is commonly used for describing how people get around: 車(くるま)で移動(いどう)する (travel by car), 徒歩(とほ)で移動(いどう)する (travel on foot). It is also used in technology contexts for moving files or data.
