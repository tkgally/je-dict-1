漫才師(まんざいし)

まんざいし
noun
manzai comedian; comic dialogue performer
1. manzai comedian; comic dialogue performer
A performer of manzai, a traditional form of Japanese stand-up comedy performed by a duo consisting of a straight man (tsukkomi) and a funny man (boke).
有名(ゆうめい)な漫才師(まんざいし)。
A famous manzai comedian.
彼(かれ)は若(わか)い頃(ころ)から漫才師(まんざいし)を目指(めざ)していた。
He aspired to be a manzai comedian from a young age.
あの漫才師(まんざいし)のコンビは息(いき)がぴったり合(あ)っていて、いつも会場(かいじょう)を沸(わ)かせる。
That manzai duo has perfect chemistry and always gets the audience fired up.

Composed of 漫才(まんざい) (manzai comedy) and 師(し) (master, practitioner). Refers to professional performers of manzai, a distinctly Japanese form of rapid-fire comic dialogue.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 漫才師(まんざいし)を目指(めざ)す: to aspire to be a manzai comedian
  • 漫才師(まんざいし)のコンビ: a manzai duo
  • 人気(にんき)漫才師(まんざいし): popular manzai comedian
  • 売(う)れっ子(こ)漫才師(まんざいし): a successful / in-demand manzai comedian

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Manzai is a form of stand-up comedy especially associated with Osaka and the Kansai region. It features a two-person act: the ボケ (funny man) delivers absurd or silly remarks, while the ツッコミ (straight man) reacts with sharp corrections. The annual M-1 Grand Prix is the most prestigious manzai competition in Japan.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 漫才(まんざい): manzai comedy — the art form itself
  • 芸人(げいにん): entertainer / comedian — a broader term for any performer
  • 落語家(らくごか): rakugo storyteller — a solo comic storytelling tradition
