1. retirement from military service
The act of leaving active military duty, either upon completing one's term of service or being discharged.
退役(たいえき)軍人(ぐんじん)。
A retired soldier.
祖父(そふ)は退役(たいえき)した将校(しょうこう)だった。
My grandfather was a retired officer.
退役(たいえき)後(ご)、彼(かれ)は故郷(こきょう)に戻(もど)って農業(のうぎょう)を始(はじ)めた。
After retiring from the military, he returned to his hometown and started farming.
2. decommissioning; retirement from active use
Used for military equipment such as ships, aircraft, or weapons systems that are taken out of active service.
その艦(かん)は退役(たいえき)した。
That warship was decommissioned.
老朽化(ろうきゅうか)した戦闘機(せんとうき)が退役(たいえき)する。
Aging fighter jets are being decommissioned.
退役(たいえき)した空母(くうぼ)が博物館(はくぶつかん)として公開(こうかい)されている。
A decommissioned aircraft carrier is open to the public as a museum.

Composed of 退(たい) (withdraw, retreat) and 役(えき) (service, duty). Refers specifically to military retirement or the decommissioning of military equipment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 退役(たいえき)軍人(ぐんじん): retired soldier / military veteran
  • 退役(たいえき)将校(しょうこう): retired officer
  • 退役(たいえき)する: to retire from service / to be decommissioned
  • 退役(たいえき)後(ご): after retirement from service

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引退(いんたい): retirement — general retirement from a career, profession, or public life
  • 定年退職(ていねんたいしょく): mandatory retirement — reaching the age limit at a workplace
  • 除隊(じょたい): discharge from the military — focuses on the formal act of leaving military service

NOTE:
Also written with する to form the verb 退役(たいえき)する. The compound 退役(たいえき)軍人(ぐんじん) is the most common usage and appears frequently in news and historical contexts.
