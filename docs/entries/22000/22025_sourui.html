藻類(そうるい)

そうるい
noun
algae; seaweed (as a biological category)
1. algae; seaweed and related organisms as a biological group
A broad category of photosynthetic organisms that live in water, ranging from microscopic single-celled organisms to large seaweeds. A scientific classification term.
池(いけ)に藻類(そうるい)が繁殖(はんしょく)している。
Algae are proliferating in the pond.
藻類(そうるい)は光合成(こうごうせい)を行(おこな)う生物(せいぶつ)である。
Algae are organisms that perform photosynthesis.
近年(きんねん)、バイオ燃料(ねんりょう)の原料(げんりょう)として藻類(そうるい)が注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
In recent years, algae have been attracting attention as a raw material for biofuel.

A scientific term combining 藻(そう) (seaweed; algae) and 類(るい) (class; kind). Used in biology, environmental science, and increasingly in discussions of biofuel and sustainable technology.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 藻類(そうるい)の繁殖(はんしょく): algae proliferation
  • 藻類(そうるい)研究(けんきゅう): algae research
  • 藻類(そうるい)バイオ燃料(ねんりょう): algae biofuel
  • 微細(びさい)藻類(そうるい): microalgae
  • 緑(みどり)藻類(そうるい): green algae

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 海藻(かいそう): seaweed — specifically marine algae; the everyday word for edible seaweed
  • 藻(も): water plant; pond weed — the native Japanese word, often refers to freshwater aquatic plants
  • 水草(みずくさ): aquatic plant; water plant — broader term including non-algae aquatic plants
