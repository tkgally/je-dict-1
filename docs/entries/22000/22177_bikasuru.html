1. to beautify; to make beautiful
To make something more physically attractive or clean. Often used for environmental improvement or cleaning up public spaces.
町(まち)を美化(びか)する。
To beautify the town.
住民(じゅうみん)が協力(きょうりょく)して公園(こうえん)の美化(びか)活動(かつどう)を行(おこな)った。
The residents worked together to carry out park beautification activities.
環境(かんきょう)美化(びか)のために、ポイ捨(す)てをやめましょう。
Let's stop littering for the sake of environmental beautification.
2. to glorify; to romanticize; to idealize
To present something in a more favorable light than reality, glossing over negative aspects. Often used critically to suggest someone is distorting the truth.
過去(かこ)を美化(びか)してはいけない。
You shouldn't romanticize the past.
戦争(せんそう)を美化(びか)するのは危険(きけん)だ。
Glorifying war is dangerous.
自分(じぶん)の失敗(しっぱい)を美化(びか)して語(かた)る人(ひと)は信用(しんよう)できない。
You can't trust people who romanticize their own failures when talking about them.

Has two distinct uses: physical beautification (cleaning, improving appearance) and figurative glorification (presenting something as better than it really is). The figurative sense often carries a critical tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)美化(びか) — environmental beautification
  • 美化(びか)活動(かつどう) — beautification activities, cleanup efforts
  • 過去(かこ)を美化(びか)する — to romanticize the past
  • 自分(じぶん)を美化(びか)する — to present oneself in a flattering light

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飾(かざ)る — to decorate, embellish; more about adding decoration
  • 理想(りそう)化(か)する — to idealize; focuses on turning something into an ideal
  • きれいにする — to clean up, make pretty; everyday casual equivalent for the physical sense
