1. pull-up; chin-up
A strength-training exercise in which one hangs from a bar and pulls oneself up until the chin is above the bar. A standard fitness exercise.
懸垂(けんすい)を十(じゅっ)回(かい)やった。
I did ten pull-ups.
懸垂(けんすい)が一(いっ)回(かい)もできない。
I can't do even a single pull-up.
毎日(まいにち)公園(こうえん)の鉄棒(てつぼう)で懸垂(けんすい)のトレーニングをしている。
I train pull-ups every day on the horizontal bar in the park.
2. suspension; hanging
The state of being suspended or hanging from something. Used in technical or descriptive contexts.
懸垂(けんすい)幕(まく)が建物(たてもの)に掛(か)かっている。
A hanging banner is draped on the building.
橋(はし)の懸垂(けんすい)構造(こうぞう)が美(うつく)しい。
The suspension structure of the bridge is beautiful.
懸垂(けんすい)式(しき)のモノレールは線路(せんろ)の下(した)にぶら下(さ)がって走(はし)る。
A suspended monorail runs hanging below the track.

Composed of 懸(けん) (suspend/hang) + 垂(すい) (droop/hang down). The exercise meaning (sense 1) is the most commonly encountered usage in everyday conversation. Can also function as a する verb (懸垂(けんすい)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懸垂(けんすい)をする: to do pull-ups
  • 懸垂(けんすい)幕(まく): hanging banner/vertical banner
  • 懸垂(けんすい)式(しき): suspended type (e.g., monorail)
  • 逆(さか)手(て)懸垂(けんすい): chin-up (underhand grip)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • チンニング: chinning — English loanword alternative for pull-ups, used in gyms
  • 鉄棒(てつぼう): horizontal bar — the equipment used for pull-ups (also a gymnastics apparatus)
