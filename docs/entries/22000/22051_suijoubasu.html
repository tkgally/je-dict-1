水上(すいじょう)バス

すいじょうばす
noun
water bus; river bus; ferry
1. water bus; river bus; sightseeing ferry
A passenger boat that operates on a regular route along rivers or waterways, functioning like a bus on water. Used for both transportation and sightseeing.
水上(すいじょう)バスで浅草(あさくさ)まで行(い)く。
To go to Asakusa by water bus.
隅田川(すみだがわ)の水上(すいじょう)バスは観光客(かんこうきゃく)に人気(にんき)がある。
The water bus on the Sumida River is popular with tourists.
天気(てんき)が良(よ)い日(ひ)に水上(すいじょう)バスに乗(の)って、川(かわ)から街(まち)の景色(けしき)を楽(たの)しんだ。
On a nice day, I rode the water bus and enjoyed the city scenery from the river.

Composed of 水上(すいじょう) (on the water) and バス (bus). A boat service that runs on scheduled routes along rivers or waterways, similar to how a bus runs on roads.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水上(すいじょう)バスに乗(の)る: to ride the water bus
  • 水上(すいじょう)バスの乗(の)り場(ば): water bus boarding point
  • 水上(すいじょう)バスの路線(ろせん): water bus route
  • 水上(すいじょう)バスで観光(かんこう)する: to sightsee by water bus

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In Tokyo, water buses operate on the 隅田川(すみだがわ) (Sumida River), connecting spots like 浅草(あさくさ), 日(ひ)の出(で)桟橋(さんばし), and お台場(だいば). They are popular for sightseeing, especially during 桜(さくら) season and fireworks festivals. Other cities like Osaka and Hiroshima also have water bus services.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 遊覧船(ゆうらんせん): pleasure boat / sightseeing cruise — a broader term for recreational boat trips
  • 渡(わた)し船(ぶね): ferryboat — a smaller boat for crossing a river
  • 屋形船(やかたぶね): houseboat / yakatabune — a traditional roofed pleasure boat for dining
