ロマンチック

ろまんちっく
adjective
romantic
1. romantic; dreamy; sentimental
Having or evoking a feeling of romance, idealism, or sentimental beauty. Used for people, settings, experiences, and attitudes.
ロマンチックな夜景(やけい)だ。
What a romantic night view.
彼(かれ)はロマンチックなプロポーズをした。
He made a romantic proposal.
夕焼(ゆうや)けに染(そ)まった海辺(うみべ)を二人(ふたり)で歩(ある)くなんて、ロマンチックですね。
Walking along the seaside at sunset together — how romantic!

A loanword from English "romantic" (via German "romantisch"). Functions as a na-adjective in Japanese. Very commonly used in everyday conversation, especially when describing dates, scenery, and gestures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ロマンチックな雰囲気(ふんいき): romantic atmosphere
  • ロマンチックな場所(ばしょ): romantic place
  • ロマンチックなディナー: romantic dinner
  • ロマンチックな映画(えいが): romantic movie

USAGE NOTE:
Sometimes written as ロマンティック, which is closer to the English pronunciation. Both forms are used, but ロマンチック is more established in Japanese.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ロマンティック: romantic — variant spelling, slightly more literary
  • 甘(あま)い: sweet — can describe a romantic mood or atmosphere in casual speech
  • 情緒的(じょうちょてき): emotional, sentimental — more formal, focuses on emotional depth rather than romantic feeling
