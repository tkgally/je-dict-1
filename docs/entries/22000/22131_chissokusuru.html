1. to suffocate; to choke; to asphyxiate
To be unable to breathe due to obstruction of the airway or lack of oxygen, potentially leading to death. Used both for literal suffocation and figuratively for feeling stifled.
煙(けむり)で窒息(ちっそく)しそうになった。
I almost suffocated from the smoke.
食(た)べ物(もの)を喉(のど)に詰(つ)まらせて窒息(ちっそく)する事故(じこ)が多(おお)い。
There are many accidents where people choke on food.
密閉(みっぺい)された部屋(へや)では酸素(さんそ)が不足(ふそく)して窒息(ちっそく)する危険(きけん)があるので、換気(かんき)を忘(わす)れないようにしてください。
In a sealed room there is a risk of suffocation from lack of oxygen, so don't forget to ventilate.

Composed of 窒(ちつ) (block, obstruct) + 息(そく) (breath). A serious word primarily used in medical, safety, and emergency contexts. Can also be used figuratively to describe feeling stifled by an oppressive atmosphere or situation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 窒息(ちっそく)死(し): death by suffocation
  • 窒息(ちっそく)事故(じこ): choking accident
  • 窒息(ちっそく)しそうになる: to nearly suffocate
  • 窒息(ちっそく)の危険(きけん): risk of suffocation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • むせる: to choke (momentarily) — for coughing due to something going down the wrong way; less severe
  • 息(いき)が詰(つ)まる: to feel suffocated — often figurative, for tense or oppressive situations
  • 酸欠(さんけつ): oxygen deficiency — the technical condition, not the act of suffocating

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
窒息(ちっそく)しそうな雰囲気(ふんいき) (a suffocating atmosphere) is a common figurative expression for an oppressive or stifling environment.
