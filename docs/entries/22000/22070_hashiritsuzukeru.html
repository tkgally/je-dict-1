1. to keep running; to continue running
To physically continue running without stopping, as in a marathon, a chase, or sustained exercise over a period of time.
彼(かれ)は走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
He kept running.
ゴールまで走(はし)り続(つづ)けよう。
Let's keep running until the finish line.
雨(あめ)の中(なか)を二時間(にじかん)も走(はし)り続(つづ)けて、足(あし)がガクガクになった。
I kept running for two hours in the rain, and my legs were shaking.
2. to keep going; to keep pushing forward (figurative)
Used metaphorically to describe persisting in one's efforts, staying at the forefront, or continuing to strive toward a goal without giving up.
夢(ゆめ)に向(む)かって走(はし)り続(つづ)ける。
To keep pushing forward toward one's dream.
彼女(かのじょ)は業界(ぎょうかい)のトップを走(はし)り続(つづ)けている。
She continues to lead the industry.
創業(そうぎょう)から五十年(ごじゅうねん)、この会社(かいしゃ)は走(はし)り続(つづ)けてきた。
For fifty years since its founding, this company has kept moving forward.

A compound verb combining 走(はし)る (to run) with 続(つづ)ける (to continue). In its literal sense, it means to keep running physically. In its figurative sense, it means to keep striving or pushing forward, often used in motivational or inspirational contexts.

VERB FORM:
Ichidan verb (走(はし)り続(つづ)ける). Conjugates on the 続(つづ)ける portion: 走(はし)り続(つづ)けます, 走(はし)り続(つづ)けた, 走(はし)り続(つづ)けて, etc.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ~を走(はし)り続(つづ)ける: to keep running (a course/path)
  • ~に向(む)かって走(はし)り続(つづ)ける: to keep running toward ~
  • トップを走(はし)り続(つづ)ける: to stay at the top

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 走(はし)り抜(ぬ)く — to run through to the end; emphasizes completing the course
  • 駆(か)け続(つづ)ける — to keep dashing; more literary
  • 歩(あゆ)み続(つづ)ける — to keep walking forward; gentler metaphor
