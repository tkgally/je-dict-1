1. to be fully equipped; to be fully furnished; to have everything in place
To have all necessary equipment, facilities, or conditions thoroughly prepared and available. Often describes buildings, facilities, or systems that lack nothing.
設備(せつび)が完備(かんび)している。
The facilities are fully equipped.
エアコン完備(かんび)の部屋(へや)を希望(きぼう)します。
I would like a room with air conditioning included.
この病院(びょういん)は最新(さいしん)の医療(いりょう)機器(きき)を完備(かんび)しており、安心(あんしん)して治療(ちりょう)を受(う)けられる。
This hospital is fully equipped with the latest medical devices, so you can receive treatment with peace of mind.

Describes a state where everything needed is in place with nothing lacking. Very common in real estate listings and facility descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 設備(せつび)完備(かんび): fully equipped (facilities)
  • エアコン完備(かんび): air conditioning included
  • 冷暖房(れいだんぼう)完備(かんび): heating and cooling included
  • 防犯(ぼうはん)設備(せつび)完備(かんび): fully equipped with security systems
  • 駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)完備(かんび): parking available

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 整備(せいび)する: to maintain; to equip — focuses on the process of setting up or maintaining, not the completed state
  • 備(そな)える: to furnish; to equip — a general verb for providing or preparing
  • 充実(じゅうじつ)する: to be substantial; to be well-stocked — broader in meaning, not limited to equipment

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Frequently appears in advertising, real estate listings, and facility descriptions.
