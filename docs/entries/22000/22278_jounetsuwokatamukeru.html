情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける

じょうねつをかたむける
expression
to pour one's passion into; to devote oneself passionately
1. to pour one's passion into; to devote oneself passionately
To dedicate one's energy and enthusiasm wholeheartedly to a pursuit or cause.
音楽(おんがく)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける。
To pour one's passion into music.
彼女(かのじょ)は研究(けんきゅう)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)けている。
She is devoting her passion to research.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)から絵画(かいが)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)けてきた画家(がか)だ。
A painter who has poured passion into painting since youth.

A literary and somewhat formal expression for wholehearted devotion. 傾(かたむ)ける literally means "to tilt" or "to incline" — the image is of pouring everything one has in one direction.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 音楽(おんがく)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける: to pour passion into music
  • 研究(けんきゅう)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける: to devote passion to research
  • 仕事(しごと)に情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける: to pour passion into work

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 心血(しんけつ)を注(そそ)ぐ: to pour one's heart and soul into — stronger, implies sacrifice
  • 打(う)ち込(こ)む: to throw oneself into — more common in everyday speech
  • 没頭(ぼっとう)する: to be absorbed in — focuses on concentration rather than passion

PATTERN:
The expression follows X + に + 情熱(じょうねつ)を傾(かたむ)ける, where X is the activity or pursuit.
