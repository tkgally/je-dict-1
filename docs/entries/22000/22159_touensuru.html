1. to go to kindergarten; to go to nursery school; to attend preschool
To go to a kindergarten (幼稚園(ようちえん)) or nursery school (保育園(ほいくえん)). Used for the daily act of a child going to their preschool facility.
毎朝(まいあさ)登園(とうえん)する。
To go to kindergarten every morning.
子供(こども)が元気(げんき)に登園(とうえん)した。
The child went to kindergarten cheerfully.
熱(ねつ)があるときは無理(むり)に登園(とうえん)させないでください。
Please do not force your child to go to kindergarten when they have a fever.

Used specifically for going to a 幼稚園(ようちえん) (kindergarten) or 保育園(ほいくえん) (nursery/daycare). Part of a series of verbs that pair 登(とう) (to go to) with different institutions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 登園(とうえん)時間(じかん): arrival time at kindergarten
  • 登園(とうえん)拒否(きょひ): refusal to go to kindergarten
  • 登園(とうえん)自粛(じしゅく): voluntary absence from kindergarten
  • 登園(とうえん)バス: kindergarten bus

RELATED TERMS:

  • 降園(こうえん)する: to leave kindergarten (going home)
  • 登校(とうこう)する: to go to school (elementary school and above)
  • 登社(とうしゃ)する: to go to the office
  • 通園(つうえん)する: to commute to kindergarten (habitual)

REGISTER:
Neutral. Commonly used among parents, teachers, and in childcare-related communications such as newsletters and notices from the school.
