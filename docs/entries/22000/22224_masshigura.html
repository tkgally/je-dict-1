まっしぐら

まっしぐら
adverb
headlong; straight ahead; at full speed
1. headlong; straight ahead; at full speed
Moving directly toward something without hesitation or deviation, often with great speed or single-minded determination.
犬(いぬ)がまっしぐらに走(はし)った。
The dog ran straight ahead at full speed.
猫(ねこ)が餌(えさ)にまっしぐらに向(む)かった。
The cat headed straight for the food.
彼(かれ)は夢(ゆめ)に向(む)かってまっしぐらに進(すす)んでいる。
He is heading straight toward his dream without hesitation.
このままでは破滅(はめつ)にまっしぐらだ。
At this rate, we're heading straight for ruin.

An expressive adverb conveying rapid, unwavering forward movement toward a goal or destination. It can describe both physical motion (running straight ahead) and figurative pursuits (chasing a dream or heading for disaster). Often used with the particle に.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • まっしぐらに走(はし)る: to run straight ahead
  • まっしぐらに進(すす)む: to advance headlong
  • まっしぐらに向(む)かう: to head straight toward
  • 破滅(はめつ)にまっしぐら: heading straight for ruin
  • 成功(せいこう)にまっしぐら: heading straight for success

USAGE:
Often used for animals (especially cats drawn to food, a well-known image in Japanese) and for people pursuing goals with single-minded focus. Can have a positive nuance (determination) or negative nuance (recklessly heading for trouble).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一直線(いっちょくせん)に: in a straight line — similar meaning but more neutral, less emotional intensity
  • 一目散(いちもくさん)に: at full speed — emphasizes fleeing or rushing, often away from something
