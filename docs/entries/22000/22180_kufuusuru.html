1. to devise; to come up with a creative solution; to find a way; to think of a good idea
To use ingenuity and creative thinking to find a better way of doing something or to solve a problem. Implies thoughtful effort and resourcefulness.
料理(りょうり)の仕方(しかた)を工夫(くふう)する。
To come up with creative ways of cooking.
時間(じかん)を有効(ゆうこう)に使(つか)えるように工夫(くふう)している。
I'm finding ways to use my time effectively.
子(こ)どもが楽(たの)しく勉強(べんきょう)できるように、教(おし)え方(かた)を工夫(くふう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to devise teaching methods so that children can study enjoyably.

A very positive word implying resourcefulness, ingenuity, and making the best of a situation. The noun 工夫(くふう) alone means a clever idea or device.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らす — to elaborate, to put great thought into
  • 工夫(くふう)を重(かさ)ねる — to make repeated improvements
  • 工夫(くふう)次第(しだい) — depending on how you work at it
  • いろいろ工夫(くふう)する — to try various creative approaches

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 考(かんが)える — to think; general thinking, not specifically creative problem-solving
  • 発明(はつめい)する — to invent; implies creating something entirely new
  • アイデアを出(だ)す — to come up with ideas; more casual
