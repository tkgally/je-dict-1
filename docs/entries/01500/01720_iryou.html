衣料(いりょう)

いりょう
noun
clothing, garments

Definitions

1. clothing, garments, apparel
Clothes and related textile products, especially in commercial or industrial contexts.

Examples

衣料(いりょう)品(ひん)を買(か)いに行(い)く。
To go buy clothing items.
衣料(いりょう)業界(ぎょうかい)で働(はたら)いている。
I work in the clothing industry.
衣料(いりょう)費(ひ)を節約(せつやく)する。
To save on clothing expenses.

Notes

衣料(いりょう) refers to clothing, especially in commercial, industrial, or formal contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 衣料(いりょう)品(ひん) (clothing items/products)
  • 衣料(いりょう)費(ひ) (clothing expenses)
  • 衣料(いりょう)業界(ぎょうかい) (clothing/apparel industry)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 衣料(いりょう)品店(ひんてん) (clothing store)
  • 衣料(いりょう){メーカー} (clothing manufacturer)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 服(ふく): clothes (everyday word)
  • 衣服(いふく): clothing (slightly formal)
  • 衣料(いりょう): clothing/apparel (commercial/industry term)
  • 衣類(いるい): clothing, garments (category term)

NUANCE:

  • More commonly seen in business/commercial contexts
  • 衣料(いりょう)品(ひん) is the standard term in retail

Related Words

Synonym: 服(ふく) (clothes (everyday))
