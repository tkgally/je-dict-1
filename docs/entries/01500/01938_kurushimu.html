1. to suffer, to be in pain
To experience physical or emotional distress.
病気(びょうき)で苦(くる)しむ。
To suffer from illness.
苦(くる)しんでいる人(ひと)を助(たす)ける。
To help people who are suffering.
頭痛(ずつう)に苦(くる)しんでいる。
I'm suffering from a headache.
2. to struggle, to have difficulty
To face hardship or trouble.
借金(しゃっきん)に苦(くる)しむ。
To struggle with debt.
お金(かね)に苦(くる)しむ。
To struggle with money.
人手(ひとで)不足(ぶそく)に苦(くる)しんでいる会社(かいしゃ)が多(おお)い。
Many companies are struggling with a labor shortage.

苦(くる)しむ means to suffer, struggle, or be in distress—physically or mentally.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive verb (no object taken).

CONJUGATION (godan む-verb):

  • 苦(くる)しまない (negative)
  • 苦(くる)しみます (polite)
  • 苦(くる)しんだ (past)
  • 苦(くる)しんで (te-form)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xに苦(くる)しむ: to suffer from X
  • Xで苦(くる)しむ: to suffer due to X
  • 病気(びょうき)に苦(くる)しむ: to suffer from illness
  • 借金(しゃっきん)に苦(くる)しむ: to struggle with debt
  • 貧困(ひんこん)に苦(くる)しむ: to suffer from poverty

RELATED WORDS:

  • 苦(くる)しい: painful, difficult, agonizing
  • 苦(くる)しみ: suffering, pain, agony (noun)
  • 苦労(くろう)する: to have a hard time, struggle
  • 悩(なや)む: to worry, be troubled

NUANCE:
苦(くる)しむ emphasizes ongoing suffering or distress, while 悩(なや)む focuses more on mental worry or concern.
