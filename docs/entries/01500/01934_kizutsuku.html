傷(きず)つく

きずつく
verb (godan)
to be hurt, to be injured

Definitions

1. to be hurt, to be injured
To receive physical damage.
2. to be hurt (emotionally)
To have one's feelings damaged.

Examples

心(こころ)が傷(きず)ついた。
I was hurt emotionally.
誰(だれ)も傷(きず)つかない。
Nobody gets hurt.
傷(きず)ついた動物(どうぶつ)。
An injured animal.

Notes

傷(きず)つく means to be hurt or injured.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 傷(きず)つく (intransitive): to be hurt
  • 傷(きず)つける (transitive): to hurt, injure

USAGE:

  • Physical injury
  • Emotional hurt
  • Damage to objects

RELATED:

  • 傷(きず): wound, injury
