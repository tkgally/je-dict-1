1. to be hurt, to be injured
To receive physical damage.
傷(きず)ついた動物(どうぶつ)。
An injured animal.
誰(だれ)も傷(きず)つかない。
Nobody gets hurt.
事故(じこ)で足(あし)が傷(きず)ついた。
My leg was injured in the accident.
2. to be hurt (emotionally)
To have one's feelings damaged.
誰(だれ)も傷(きず)つかない。
Nobody gets hurt.
心(こころ)が傷(きず)ついた。
I was hurt emotionally.
その言葉(ことば)で深(ふか)く傷(きず)ついた。
I was deeply hurt by those words.

傷(きず)つく is an intransitive godan verb meaning to be hurt, injured, or damaged.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive): 傷(きず)つく - to be hurt
  • 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive): 傷(きず)つける - to hurt someone/something

ASPECT (ている):
傷(きず)ついている: is hurt/injured (resulting state).

USAGE:
1. Physical: 傷(きず)ついた動物(どうぶつ) (injured animal)
2. Emotional: 心(こころ)が傷(きず)つく (to be emotionally hurt)
3. Objects: 床(ゆか)が傷(きず)つく (the floor gets scratched)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 傷(きず): wound, injury, scratch, flaw
  • 怪我(けが): injury (physical)
