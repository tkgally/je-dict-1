触(ふ)れる

ふれる
verb (ichidan)
to touch, to mention
1. to touch, to come in contact
To make physical contact.
展示品(てんじひん)に触(ふ)れないでください。
Please don't touch the exhibits.
自然(しぜん)に触(ふ)れる。
To experience nature.
2. to mention, to refer to
To bring up a topic.
その話題(わだい)には触(ふ)れなかった。
I didn't mention that topic.

触(ふ)れる is an intransitive ichidan verb with two main meanings: to make physical contact and to mention or refer to something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に particle: Xに触(ふ)れる

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 展示品(てんじひん)に触(ふ)れないでください: Please don't touch the exhibits
  • その話題(わだい)に触(ふ)れる: to mention that topic
  • 自然(しぜん)に触(ふ)れる: to experience/connect with nature
  • 法(ほう)に触(ふ)れる: to break the law

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 触(ふ)れる: to touch (slightly formal, often light contact)
  • 触(さわ)る: to touch (casual, often implies handling)

REGISTER:
More formal than 触(さわ)る; preferred in written language and polite contexts.
