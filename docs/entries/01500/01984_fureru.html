触(ふ)れる

ふれる
verb (ichidan)
to touch, to mention
1. to touch, to come in contact
To make physical contact.
展示品(てんじひん)に触(ふ)れないでください。
Please don't touch the exhibits.
自然(しぜん)に触(ふ)れる。
To experience nature.
2. to mention, to refer to
To bring up a topic.
その話題(わだい)には触(ふ)れなかった。
I didn't mention that topic.

触(ふ)れる means to touch or mention.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 触(ふ)れる: to touch (more formal)
  • 触(さわ)る: to touch (casual)

PATTERN:

  • Xに触(ふ)れる: to touch X, mention X
