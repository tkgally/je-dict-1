伸(の)びる

のびる
verb (ichidan)
to stretch, to grow
1. to stretch, to extend
To become longer.
2. to grow, to develop
To increase in size or ability.
髪(かみ)が伸(の)びた。
My hair grew.
売(う)り上(あ)げが伸(の)びる。
Sales increase.
背(せ)が伸(の)びた。
I grew taller.

伸(の)びる means to stretch or grow.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 伸(の)びる (intransitive): to stretch
  • 伸(の)ばす (transitive): to stretch

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 伸(の)びる: to stretch, grow
  • 延(の)びる: to be extended (time)
