やっと

やっと
adverb
finally, at last
1. finally, at last
After a long time or much effort.
やっと終(お)わった。
It's finally over.
やっと春(はる)が来(き)た。
Spring has finally come.
長(なが)い冬(ふゆ)が終(お)わり、やっと暖(あたた)かくなってきた。
The long winter has ended, and it's finally getting warm.
何年(なんねん)もかかってやっと夢(ゆめ)を実現(じつげん)できた。
After many years, I was finally able to realize my dream.
2. barely, just
Only just; with difficulty.
やっと電車(でんしゃ)に間(ま)に合(あ)った。
I barely made it in time for the train.
やっと生活(せいかつ)できるくらいの給料(きゅうりょう)だ。
It's a salary I can barely live on.
今月(こんげつ)はやっと家賃(やちん)を払(はら)えるくらいしか稼(かせ)げなかった。
This month, I only earned barely enough to pay the rent.

やっと expresses that something happened after a long wait or much effort, often conveying relief.

MEANINGS:
1. Finally, at last (after waiting or effort)
2. Barely, just (with difficulty)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • やっと終(お)わった: it's finally over
  • やっと間(ま)に合(あ)った: barely made it in time
  • やっと分(わ)かった: finally understood
  • やっと会(あ)えた: finally got to meet

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ようやく: finally (more formal, similar meaning)
  • とうとう: finally (can be positive or negative outcome)
  • ついに: at last (often for significant events)

やっと implies personal relief or satisfaction after effort or waiting.
