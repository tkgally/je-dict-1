捕(つか)まる

つかまる
verb (godan)
to be caught, to hold on
1. to be caught, to be arrested
To be captured or apprehended.
犯人(はんにん)が捕(つか)まった。
The criminal was caught.
タクシーが捕(つか)まらない。
I can't catch a taxi.
泥棒(どろぼう)が捕(つか)まった。
The thief was caught.
Sense 1: be arrested
スピード違反(いはん)で捕(つか)まった。
I got caught for speeding.
Sense 1: be caught
逃(に)げたが捕(つか)まった。
I ran but got caught.
Sense 1: be captured
警察(けいさつ)に捕(つか)まった。
I was caught by the police.
Sense 1: be apprehended
2. to hold on, to grab onto
To grasp something for support.
手(て)すりに捕(つか)まる。
To hold on to the handrail.
ロープに捕(つか)まって。
Hold on to the rope.
Sense 2: hold on
吊(つ)り革(かわ)に捕(つか)まる。
To hold on to the strap.
Sense 2: grab onto
枝(えだ)に捕(つか)まって登(のぼ)った。
I climbed holding on to the branches.
Sense 2: hold on

捕(つか)まる has two main meanings: 'to be caught/arrested' and 'to hold onto something.'

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 捕(つか)まえる (transitive, to catch)

SENSE 1 - Be caught:

  • 犯人(はんにん)が捕(つか)まる: a criminal is caught
  • 警察(けいさつ)に捕(つか)まる: to be caught by police
  • タクシーが捕(つか)まらない: can't catch a taxi

SENSE 2 - Hold onto:

  • 手(て)すりに捕(つか)まる: to hold onto the handrail
  • 吊(つ)り革(かわ)に捕(つか)まる: to hold onto the strap (on train)

KANJI NOTE:
捕(つか)まる uses 捕(ほ) (capture). The related verb 掴(つか)む (to grab) uses different kanji.
