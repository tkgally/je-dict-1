1. rumor, gossip
Unverified information spread from person to person.
噂(うわさ)を聞(き)いた。
I heard a rumor.
噂(うわさ)をすれば影(かげ)。
Speak of the devil.
Proverb
彼(かれ)の噂(うわさ)が広(ひろ)まっている。
Rumors about him are spreading.
噂(うわさ)を信(しん)じないほうがいい。
You shouldn't believe rumors.
噂(うわさ)によると、彼(かれ)は辞(や)めるらしい。
According to rumors, he's going to quit.
噂(うわさ)ばかりしないで。
Don't just gossip.
2. reputation, talk
What people say about someone or something.
その店(みせ)は噂(うわさ)どおり美味(おい)しかった。
The restaurant was as delicious as people said.
悪(わる)い噂(うわさ)が立(た)っている。
Bad rumors are going around.
あの会社(かいしゃ)の噂(うわさ)を聞(き)いたことがある。
I've heard talk about that company.
いい噂(うわさ)を聞(き)いて安心(あんしん)した。
I was relieved to hear good things.
噂(うわさ)の店(みせ)に行(い)ってみたが、本当(ほんとう)に素晴(すば)らしかった。
I tried going to the much-talked-about restaurant, and it really was wonderful.

噂(うわさ) refers to unverified information passed between people. It covers both negative gossip and neutral reputation/word-of-mouth.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を噂(うわさ)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 噂(うわさ)を聞(き)く: to hear a rumor
  • 噂(うわさ)が広(ひろ)まる: a rumor spreads
  • 噂(うわさ)になる: to become the subject of rumors
  • 噂(うわさ)をする: to gossip
  • 噂(うわさ)どおり: as rumored, as people say
  • 噂(うわさ)によると: according to rumors

PROVERBS:

  • 噂(うわさ)をすれば影(かげ): Speak of the devil (lit. "gossip about someone and their shadow appears")
  • 人(ひと)の噂(うわさ)も七十五日(しちじゅうごにち): rumors die down after 75 days (time heals all gossip)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 評判(ひょうばん): reputation, public opinion (more established, often about quality)
  • ゴシップ: gossip (loanword, often about celebrities)

Related Words

Synonym: 評判(ひょうばん) (reputation (established))
