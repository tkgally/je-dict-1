乗(の)せる

のせる
verb (ichidan)
to give a ride, to put on
1. to give a ride, to let ride
To allow someone to board a vehicle.
2. to put on, to place on
To set something on a surface.
車(くるま)に乗(の)せる。
To give someone a ride.
テーブル(てーぶる)に乗(の)せる。
To put on the table.
口(くち)に乗(の)せられた。
I was tricked by their words.

乗(の)せる means to give a ride or put on.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 乗(の)せる (transitive): to put on
  • 乗(の)る (intransitive): to ride

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 乗(の)せる: to give a ride
  • 載(の)せる: to publish, load
