約束(やくそく)

やくそく
noun (verbal)
promise, appointment
1. promise
A declaration that one will do or refrain from doing something.
2. appointment, arrangement
An agreement to meet at a particular time.
約束(やくそく)を守(まも)ってください。
Please keep your promise.
明日(あした)約束(やくそく)がある。
I have an appointment tomorrow.

約束(やくそく)する is the verbal form. Common phrases: 約束(やくそく)を守(まも)る (keep a promise), 約束(やくそく)を破(やぶ)る (break a promise).
