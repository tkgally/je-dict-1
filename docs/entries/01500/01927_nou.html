能(のう)

のう
noun
Noh, ability, talent
1. Noh (theater)
A form of classical Japanese musical drama.
2. ability, talent, skill
The capacity to do something well.
能(のう)を鑑賞(かんしょう)する。
To appreciate Noh theater.
能(のう)のない人(ひと)。
An incompetent person.
能(のう)ある鷹(たか)は爪(つめ)を隠(かく)す。
A skilled hawk hides its talons. (Still waters run deep)
Proverb

能(のう) has two main meanings: Noh theater and ability.

NOH THEATER:

  • 能楽(のうがく): Noh theater (formal term)
  • 能面(のうめん): Noh mask
  • 能舞台(のうぶたい): Noh stage

ABILITY:

  • 能力(のうりょく): ability, capability
  • 才能(さいのう): talent
  • 無能(むのう): incompetent

PROVERB:

  • 能(のう)ある鷹(たか)は爪(つめ)を隠(かく)す: talented people don't show off
