1. to have a meal, to dine
A regular occasion of eating; the food eaten at such an occasion.
一緒(いっしょ)に食事(しょくじ)しませんか。
Would you like to have a meal together?
食事(しょくじ)の時間(じかん)です。
It's mealtime.
食事(しょくじ)を終(お)えてから出(で)かけます。
I'll go out after finishing my meal.
食事(しょくじ)中(ちゅう)はスマホを見(み)ないでください。
Please don't look at your phone during meals.
家族(かぞく)と一緒(いっしょ)に食事(しょくじ)する時間(じかん)を大切(たいせつ)にしています。
I value the time I spend having meals with my family.

食事(しょくじ) refers to the act of eating a meal or to a meal itself. Slightly more formal than ご飯(はん), which is the everyday word for meals.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 食事(しょくじ)する

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 食事(しょくじ)する/食事(しょくじ)を取(と)る: to have a meal
  • 食事(しょくじ)中(ちゅう): during a meal
  • 軽(かる)い食事(しょくじ): light meal
  • 食事(しょくじ)制限(せいげん): dietary restriction

MEAL TYPES:

  • 朝食(ちょうしょく)/朝(あさ)ご飯(はん): breakfast
  • 昼食(ちゅうしょく)/昼(ひる)ご飯(はん): lunch
  • 夕食(ゆうしょく)/夕(ゆう)ご飯(はん): dinner

REGISTER:
食事(しょくじ) is used in both spoken and written Japanese but carries a slightly formal tone. In everyday conversation, ご飯(はん) or 飯(めし) (casual, masculine) are more natural.
