込(こ)める

こめる
verb (ichidan)
to put into, to include

Definitions

1. to put into, to include
To put something (usually feelings) into something.
2. to load (a gun)
To put ammunition into a firearm.

Examples

心(こころ)を込(こ)めて作(つく)る。
To make with heart and soul.
願(ねが)いを込(こ)める。
To put one's wishes into something.
力(ちから)を込(こ)める。
To put strength into.

Notes

込(こ)める means to put into or include.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 心(こころ)を込(こ)める: to put one's heart into
  • 思(おも)いを込(こ)める: to put one's feelings into
  • 力(ちから)を込(こ)める: to put effort into

RELATED:

  • 込(こ)められた: filled with (passive)
