込(こ)める

こめる
verb (ichidan)
to put into, to include
1. to put into, to include
To put something (usually feelings) into something.
心(こころ)を込(こ)めて作(つく)る。
To make with heart and soul.
願(ねが)いを込(こ)める。
To put one's wishes into something.
力(ちから)を込(こ)める。
To put strength into.
愛情(あいじょう)を込(こ)めた料理(りょうり)。
Cooking made with love.
Sense 1: put into
感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちを込(こ)めて。
With feelings of gratitude.
Sense 1: put into
怒(いか)りを込(こ)めて言(い)った。
I said it with anger.
Sense 1: include
想(おも)いを込(こ)めた手紙(てがみ)。
A letter filled with feelings.
Sense 1: put into
魂(たましい)を込(こ)めて歌(うた)う。
To sing with soul.
Sense 1: put into
2. to load (a gun)
To put ammunition into a firearm.
銃(じゅう)に弾(たま)を込(こ)める。
To load bullets into a gun.
Sense 2: load
弾丸(だんがん)を込(こ)めた銃(じゅう)。
A loaded gun.
Sense 2: load

込(こ)める is an ichidan verb meaning to put into or include, often used for putting feelings or effort into something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を込(こ)める: to put ~ into
  • ～を込(こ)めて: with ~, putting ~ into
  • ～が込(こ)められた: filled with ~ (passive)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 心(こころ)を込(こ)める: to put one's heart into
  • 思(おも)いを込(こ)める: to put one's feelings into
  • 力(ちから)を込(こ)める: to put strength/effort into
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を込(こ)める: to put love into
  • 感謝(かんしゃ)を込(こ)めて: with gratitude
  • 願(ねが)いを込(こ)める: to put one's wishes into
  • 魂(たましい)を込(こ)める: to put one's soul into

SENSE 2 (load):

  • 弾(たま)を込(こ)める: to load bullets
  • 銃(じゅう)に込(こ)める: to load into a gun

RELATED: 込(こ)む (to be crowded) is a different verb.
