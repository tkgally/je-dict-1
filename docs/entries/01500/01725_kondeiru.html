混(こ)んでいる

こんでいる
expression (verb て-form + いる)
to be crowded, to be congested

Definitions

1. to be crowded, to be packed
A place that is full of people. The ている form describes the resulting state of being crowded.
2. to be congested, to be jammed
Used for traffic or roads that are backed up with vehicles.

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)が混(こ)んでいて座(すわ)れなかった。
The train was so crowded I couldn't sit down.
土曜日(どようび)はいつも店(みせ)が混(こ)んでいる。
The store is always crowded on Saturdays.
道(みち)が混(こ)んでいるから遅(おく)れそうだ。
The road is congested so I'll probably be late.

Notes

混(こ)んでいる is the ている form of 混(こ)む/込(こ)む, describing the state of being crowded.

BASE VERB:

  • 混(こ)む/込(こ)む (to become crowded)
  • This is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし))

ASPECT (ている):

  • 混(こ)んでいる describes a resulting state: 'is crowded'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)が混(こ)んでいる (train is crowded)
  • 道(みち)が混(こ)んでいる (road is congested)
  • 店(みせ)が混(こ)んでいる (store is crowded)
  • 混(こ)んでいて〜 (because it's crowded...)

ANTONYM:

  • 空(す)いている (to be empty/not crowded)

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 混(こ)んでいる (crowded with people/things mixed together)
  • 込(こ)んでいる (packed in, crammed)

Related Words

Antonym: 空(す)いている (empty, not crowded)
Related: 込(こ)む (to be crowded (base verb))
