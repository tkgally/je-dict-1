固(かた)まる

かたまる
verb (godan)
to harden, to solidify
1. to harden, to solidify
To become hard or solid.
2. to be finalized, to be decided
For plans or decisions to become fixed.
セメントが固(かた)まった。
The cement hardened.
予定(よてい)が固(かた)まった。
The plans were finalized.
緊張(きんちょう)で体(からだ)が固(かた)まった。
My body stiffened from tension.

固(かた)まる means to harden or solidify.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 固(かた)まる (intransitive): to harden
  • 固(かた)める (transitive): to harden

USAGE:

  • Physical: cement, glue hardening
  • Abstract: plans, decisions becoming fixed
  • Emotional: body stiffening from fear/tension
