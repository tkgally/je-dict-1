既(すで)に

すでに
adverb
already

Definitions

1. already
Indicates that something has happened before the expected time or before the current moment. More formal than もう.

Examples

彼(かれ)は既(すで)に出発(しゅっぱつ)しました。
He has already departed.
その事実(じじつ)は既(すで)に知(し)られています。
That fact is already known.
会議(かいぎ)は既(すで)に終(お)わっていた。
The meeting had already ended.

Notes

既(すで)に is a formal adverb meaning 'already.'

POSITION: Usually placed before the verb or at the beginning of a clause.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • もう: already (casual, everyday)
  • 既(すで)に: already (formal, written)

[Register: Formal/Written]

The kanji 既(すで) can also be read as き in compounds like 既婚(きこん) (married).
