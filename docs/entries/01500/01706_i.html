胃(い)

い
noun
stomach

Definitions

1. stomach
The organ that receives food and begins digestion.

Examples

胃(い)が痛(いた)い。
My stomach hurts.
胃(い)の調子(ちょうし)が悪(わる)い。
My stomach isn't feeling well.
胃(い)カメラの検査(けんさ)を受(う)けました。
I had a gastroscopy examination.
胃(い)カメラ = gastroscope

Notes

胃(い) refers specifically to the stomach organ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 胃(い)が痛(いた)い (stomach hurts)
  • 胃(い)の調子(ちょうし) (stomach condition)
  • 胃(い)に優(やさ)しい (gentle on the stomach)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 胃腸(いちょう): stomach and intestines, digestive system
  • 胃(い)カメラ: gastroscope
  • 胃(い)がん/胃癌(いがん): stomach cancer
  • 胃薬(いぐすり): stomach medicine

NOTE:

  • 胃(い): the organ (medical/anatomical)
  • お腹(なか): belly, tummy (general/conversational)
