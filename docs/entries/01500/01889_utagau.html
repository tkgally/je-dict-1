疑(うたが)う

うたがう
verb (godan)
to doubt, to suspect

Definitions

1. to doubt, to question
To be uncertain about something.
2. to suspect
To think someone may be guilty or something may be wrong.

Examples

彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)を疑(うたが)う。
To doubt his words.
目(め)を疑(うたが)った。
I couldn't believe my eyes.
Literally: I doubted my eyes.
犯人(はんにん)だと疑(うたが)われた。
Was suspected of being the culprit.

Notes

疑(うたが)う means to doubt or suspect.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)を疑(うたが)う: to doubt one's eyes
  • 耳(みみ)を疑(うたが)う: to doubt one's ears
  • 疑(うたが)いなく: without doubt

RELATED:

  • 疑(うたが)い: doubt, suspicion
  • 疑問(ぎもん): question, doubt
