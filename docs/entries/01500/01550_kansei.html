完成(かんせい)

かんせい
noun, suru verb
completion, perfection
1. completion, finishing
The state of being finished or the act of completing something. Used for projects, works, or constructions that have been brought to a finished state.
工事(こうじ)が完成(かんせい)しました。
The construction has been completed.
この作品(さくひん)は来月(らいげつ)完成(かんせい)する予定(よてい)です。
This work is scheduled to be completed next month.
完成(かんせい)まであと少(すこ)しです。
It's almost finished.
2. perfection
Reaching a state of full development or ideal condition.
完成(かんせい)された技術(ぎじゅつ)だ。
It's a perfected technique.
この彫刻(ちょうこく)は完成(かんせい)された美(うつく)しさがある。
This sculpture has a perfected beauty.
長年(ながねん)の練習(れんしゅう)を経(へ)て、彼(かれ)の演奏(えんそう)は完成(かんせい)の域(いき)に達(たっ)した。
After years of practice, his performance has reached a level of perfection.

完成(かんせい) refers to reaching a finished or perfected state.

TRANSITIVITY (as suru verb):

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) - Xが完成(かんせい)する
  • Pair: 完成(かんせい)させる (causative, to complete something)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 工事(こうじ)が完成(かんせい)する (construction is completed)
  • 作品(さくひん)が完成(かんせい)する (work is completed)
  • 完成(かんせい)に近(ちか)づく (approach completion)
  • 完成(かんせい)品(ひん) (finished product)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完了(かんりょう): completion of an action/task (more process-focused)
  • 終了(しゅうりょう): ending (neutral, for events/processes)
