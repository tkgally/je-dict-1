産(う)む

うむ
verb (godan)
to give birth, to produce
1. to give birth, to bear
To bring forth offspring.
子供(こども)を産(う)む。
To give birth to a child.
卵(たまご)を産(う)む。
To lay eggs.
2. to produce, to create
To bring something into existence.
新(あたら)しいアイデアを産(う)む。
To produce new ideas.

産(う)む means to give birth or produce something new.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 子供(こども)を産(う)む
  • Intransitive pair: 産(う)まれる (to be born)

ASPECT (ている):
産(う)んでいる can mean "is giving birth" (in progress) or "has given birth" (resultant state, less common).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を産(う)む: to give birth to a child
  • 卵(たまご)を産(う)む: to lay eggs
  • 利益(りえき)を産(う)む: to generate profit (figurative)
  • アイデアを産(う)む: to produce ideas

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 産(う)む: give birth, produce (standard)
  • 生(う)む: give birth (less common variant)

RELATED:

  • 出産(しゅっさん): childbirth
  • 産(う)まれる: to be born
