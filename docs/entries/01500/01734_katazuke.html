片付(かたづ)け

かたづけ
noun
tidying up, cleaning up

Definitions

1. tidying up, cleaning up, putting away
The act of organizing and putting things in order.
2. finishing, settling
Completing or settling a matter or task.

Examples

部屋(へや)の片付(かたづ)けをする。
To tidy up the room.
片付(かたづ)けが苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not good at tidying up.
食事(しょくじ)の後(あと)は片付(かたづ)けを手伝(てつだ)う。
I help clean up after meals.

Notes

片付(かたづ)け is the noun form of 片付(かたづ)ける (to tidy up).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 片付(かたづ)けをする (to do tidying up)
  • 片付(かたづ)けが上手(じょうず)/苦手(にがて) (good/bad at tidying)
  • 片付(かたづ)けを手伝(てつだ)う (to help tidy up)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 部屋(へや)の片付(かたづ)け (room tidying)
  • 食後(しょくご)の片付(かたづ)け (cleaning up after meals)
  • 大(おお)掃除(そうじ) (big cleaning, spring cleaning)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 片付(かたづ)け: tidying, organizing
  • 掃除(そうじ): cleaning (removing dirt)
  • 整理(せいり): organizing, arranging

Related Words
