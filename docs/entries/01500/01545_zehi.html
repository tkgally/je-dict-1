是非(ぜひ)

ぜひ
adverb
by all means, definitely
1. by all means, definitely, certainly
Used to express strong desire or invitation.
是非(ぜひ)来(き)てね。
Please do come.
是非(ぜひ)会(あ)いたいです。
I definitely want to meet you.
この映画(えいが)は是非(ぜひ)見(み)てください。
You should definitely watch this movie.
日本(にほん)に来(き)たら、是非(ぜひ)京都(きょうと)を訪(おとず)れてみてください。
If you come to Japan, please be sure to visit Kyoto.
今度(こんど)の週末(しゅうまつ)、是非(ぜひ)一緒(いっしょ)に食事(しょくじ)をしましょう。
Let's definitely have a meal together this weekend.

是非(ぜひ) adds strong emphasis to requests, invitations, and expressions of desire.

USAGE:
Expresses earnest desire or strong recommendation. Makes invitations sound more sincere and polite.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 是非(ぜひ)〜てください: please do (emphatic)
  • 是非(ぜひ)〜たい: I really want to...
  • 是非(ぜひ)とも: by all means, absolutely

EXAMPLES:

  • 是非(ぜひ)来(き)てください: please do come
  • 是非(ぜひ)お試(ため)しください: please do try it
  • 是非(ぜひ)また会(あ)いましょう: let's definitely meet again

REGISTER:
Appropriate for both formal and casual situations. Using 是非(ぜひ) shows politeness and sincerity.

NOTE:
是非(ぜひ) as a noun means "right and wrong" (是非(ぜひ)を問(と)う = to question right and wrong), but this usage is less common.
