寝坊(ねぼう)

ねぼう
noun, verb (suru)
oversleeping
1. oversleeping, sleeping late
Sleeping longer than intended, especially past the time one should wake up.
2. late riser, sleepyhead
A person who tends to sleep late or oversleep.
寝坊(ねぼう)して学校(がっこう)に遅刻(ちこく)した。
I overslept and was late for school.
明日(あした)寝坊(ねぼう)しないようにアラームをセットした。
I set an alarm so I won't oversleep tomorrow.
うちの子(こ)は寝坊(ねぼう)です。
My child is a late riser.
Sense 2: as a noun for a person

寝坊(ねぼう) is composed of 寝(ね) (sleep) + 坊(ぼう) (person, in compounds).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 寝坊(ねぼう)する: to oversleep
  • 寝坊(ねぼう)して遅刻(ちこく)する: to be late due to oversleeping
  • 寝坊(ねぼう)しないように: so as not to oversleep

RELATED TERMS:

  • 遅刻(ちこく): being late
  • 早起(はやお)き: early riser, waking up early
  • 朝寝坊(あさねぼう): morning oversleeping
  • 二度寝(にどね): going back to sleep

OPPOSITE: 早起(はやお)き (early riser)

Related Words

Antonym: 早起(はやお)き (early riser)
