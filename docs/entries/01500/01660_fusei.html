不正(ふせい)

ふせい
noun, na-adjective
injustice, unfairness, fraud
1. injustice, unfairness
Lack of justice or fairness. Actions or situations that are not right or proper.
不正(ふせい)を許(ゆる)すことはできません。
We cannot allow injustice.
2. fraud, corruption, dishonesty
Illegal or dishonest conduct, especially in official or business matters.
不正(ふせい)行為(こうい)が発覚(はっかく)しました。
Fraudulent conduct was discovered.
不正(ふせい)アクセスは犯罪(はんざい)です。
Unauthorized access is a crime.
IT context

不正(ふせい) refers to injustice, unfairness, or fraud.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 不正(ふせい)行為(こうい) (fraudulent act)
  • 不正(ふせい)アクセス (unauthorized access)
  • 不正(ふせい)選挙(せんきょ) (election fraud)
  • 不正(ふせい)を働(はたら)く (commit fraud)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不公平(ふこうへい): unfairness (lack of equality)
  • 不正(ふせい): injustice, fraud (illegal/improper)
  • 不法(ふほう): illegal, unlawful

ANTONYM:

  • 公正(こうせい): justice, fairness
