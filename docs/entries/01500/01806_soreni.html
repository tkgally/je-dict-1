それに

それに
conjunction
moreover, besides
1. moreover, besides, in addition
Used to add information to what has already been said.
彼(かれ)は頭(あたま)がいい。それに優(やさ)しい。
He is smart. Moreover, he is kind.
今日(きょう)は寒(さむ)い。それに雨(あめ)も降(ふ)っている。
It's cold today. Besides, it's also raining.
高(たか)いし、それにあまり美味(おい)しくない。
It's expensive, and moreover, it's not very tasty.
この店(みせ)は安(やす)い。それに量(りょう)も多(おお)い。
This restaurant is cheap. Besides, the portions are big.
時間(じかん)がない。それに天気(てんき)も悪(わる)いから、今日(きょう)は出(で)かけないことにした。
I don't have time. Besides, the weather is bad too, so I decided not to go out today.

それに is used to add more information.

USAGE:

  • Adds positive or negative information
  • Often used at the beginning of a sentence
  • More casual than その上(うえ)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • その上(うえ): moreover (formal)
  • さらに: furthermore
  • 加(くわ)えて: in addition
  • しかも: moreover (emphasizes)
