生(う)まれ

うまれ
noun
birth, birthplace, origin

Definitions

1. birth
The act or time of being born.
2. birthplace, origin
The place where one was born or originated from.
3. born in (year)
Used to indicate the year of birth.

Examples

東京(とうきょう)生(う)まれです。
I was born in Tokyo.
1990年(ねん)生(う)まれです。
I was born in 1990.
生(う)まれも育(そだ)ちも大阪(おおさか)です。
I was born and raised in Osaka.
Common expression

Notes

生(う)まれ is the noun form of 生(う)まれる (to be born).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜生(う)まれ (born in ~)
  • 生(う)まれも育(そだ)ちも (born and raised)
  • 生(う)まれつき (by nature, innately)
  • 生(う)まれ変(か)わる (to be reborn)

USAGE:

  • 東京(とうきょう)生(う)まれ: Tokyo-born
  • 〜年(ねん)生(う)まれ: born in ~ year
