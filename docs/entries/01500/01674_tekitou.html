適当(てきとう)

てきとう
na-adjective, noun
suitable; sloppy
1. suitable, appropriate, proper
Fitting or appropriate for a particular purpose or situation.
適当(てきとう)な大(おお)きさに切(き)ってください。
Please cut it to an appropriate size.
Sense 1: appropriate
適当(てきとう)な時間(じかん)に来(き)てください。
Please come at a suitable time.
Sense 1: suitable
適当(てきとう)な候補者(こうほしゃ)が見(み)つかった。
A suitable candidate was found.
Sense 1: suitable
この部屋(へや)は会議(かいぎ)に適当(てきとう)だ。
This room is suitable for meetings.
Sense 1: appropriate
適当(てきとう)な運動(うんどう)は健康(けんこう)に良(よ)い。
Moderate exercise is good for health.
Sense 1: appropriate
2. random, sloppy, half-hearted
Done without much thought or care; careless or irresponsible (often negative).
適当(てきとう)に答(こた)えないでください。
Please don't answer carelessly.
Sense 2: carelessly
適当(てきとう)なことを言(い)うな。
Don't say random things.
Sense 2: random/irresponsible
仕事(しごと)を適当(てきとう)にやってはいけない。
You must not do your work carelessly.
Sense 2: carelessly
適当(てきとう)に選(えら)んでください。
Please choose at random.
Sense 2: random
適当(てきとう)な理由(りゆう)をつけて断(ことわ)った。
I made up some random excuse and declined.
Sense 2: random/made-up

適当(てきとう) has two nearly opposite meanings depending on context.

SENSE 1 (positive): Suitable, appropriate

  • 適当(てきとう)な人(ひと): suitable person
  • 適当(てきとう)な値段(ねだん): reasonable price

SENSE 2 (negative): Careless, sloppy

  • 適当(てきとう)にやる: to do carelessly
  • 適当(てきとう)な仕事(しごと): sloppy work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 適切(てきせつ): appropriate (always positive, more formal)
  • いい加減(かげん): half-hearted, irresponsible

Context is key to understanding which meaning is intended.
