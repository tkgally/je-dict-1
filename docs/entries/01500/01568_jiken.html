事件(じけん)

じけん
noun
incident, case, affair

Definitions

1. incident, case, affair
An event or occurrence, especially one that is unusual, significant, or involves a crime or controversy.
2. criminal case
In legal contexts, refers to a criminal case or investigation.

Examples

大(おお)きな事件(じけん)がありました。
There was a major incident.
警察(けいさつ)が事件(じけん)を調(しら)べています。
The police are investigating the case.
これは歴史的(れきしてき)な事件(じけん)です。
This is a historic event.

Notes

事件(じけん) refers to notable incidents, often with legal or news implications.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 事件(じけん)が起(お)きる/起(お)こる (an incident occurs)
  • 事件(じけん)を解決(かいけつ)する (solve a case)
  • 事件(じけん)を調査(ちょうさ)する (investigate a case)
  • 殺人(さつじん)事件(じけん) (murder case)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 事故(じこ): accident (unintentional)
  • 事件(じけん): incident/case (often intentional or criminal)
  • 出来事{できごと}: event, happening (neutral)

COUNTER: 件(けん) (～件(けん)の事件(じけん))
