起(お)こる

おこる
verb (godan)
to occur, to happen
1. to occur, to happen, to take place
For an event to come about.
事故(じこ)が起(お)こった。
An accident occurred.
問題(もんだい)が起(お)こる。
A problem arises.
何(なに)が起(お)こっても大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)。
Whatever happens, it'll be fine.

起(お)こる means to occur or happen—used for events, incidents, and changes.

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 起(お)こる: to occur (events)
  • 怒(おこ)る: to get angry (different kanji)

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 起(お)こる (intransitive): something occurs
  • 起(お)こす (transitive): to cause something

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)が起(お)こる: an accident occurs
  • 問題(もんだい)が起(お)こる: a problem arises
  • 変化(へんか)が起(お)こる: a change occurs
  • 地震(じしん)が起(お)こる: an earthquake occurs
  • 戦争(せんそう)が起(お)こる: a war breaks out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発生(はっせい)する: to occur, break out (formal)
  • 生(しょう)じる: to arise (written style)
  • 勃発(ぼっぱつ)する: to break out (wars, conflicts)
