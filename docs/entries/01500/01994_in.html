〜員(いん)

いん
suffix
member of, staff of
1. member of, staff of
A suffix indicating membership or employment in an organization.
会社員(かいしゃいん)として働(はたら)いている。
I work as a company employee.
店員(てんいん)に聞(き)いてください。
Please ask the shop staff.
委員(いいん)に選(えら)ばれた。
I was chosen as a committee member.

〜員(いん) indicates membership or staff position.

COMMON WORDS:

  • 会社員(かいしゃいん): company employee
  • 店員(てんいん): shop clerk
  • 駅員(えきいん): station staff
  • 銀行員(ぎんこういん): bank employee
  • 委員(いいん): committee member
  • 会員(かいいん): member
