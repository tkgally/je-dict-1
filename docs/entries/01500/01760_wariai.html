割合(わりあい)

わりあい
noun, adverb
ratio, relatively
1. ratio, percentage, proportion
The relationship between two quantities expressed as a number.
男女(だんじょ)の割合(わりあい)は半々(はんはん)です。
The ratio of men to women is fifty-fifty.
成功(せいこう)の割合(わりあい)は高(たか)い。
The success rate is high.
2. relatively, comparatively
To a moderate degree; somewhat.
今日(きょう)は割合(わりあい)暖(あたた)かい。
It's relatively warm today.
Adverbial use

割合(わりあい) has two main uses.

1. NOUN (ratio):

  • 〜の割合(わりあい): ratio of ~
  • 割合(わりあい)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い: ratio is high/low

2. ADVERB (relatively):

  • 割合(わりあい) + adjective: relatively ~
  • Similar to わりと (casual variant)

RELATED:

  • 比率(ひりつ): ratio (formal)
  • パーセント: percent
  • 割(わ)り: rate, proportion
