一度(いちど)に

いちどに
adverb
all at once, at one time
1. all at once, at one time
Doing or happening simultaneously rather than separately.
一度(いちど)に全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べないで。
Don't eat everything at once.
一度(いちど)に三(さん)人(にん)来(き)た。
Three people came at once.
一度(いちど)に済(す)ませたい。
I want to finish it all at once.
2. in one go, at a stretch
Completing something without interruption.
一度(いちど)に読(よ)み終(お)えた。
I finished reading it in one go.
一度(いちど)にたくさん覚(おぼ)えようとしても難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to memorize a lot in one go.
階段(かいだん)を一度(いちど)に二段(にだん)ずつ上(あ)がった。
I went up the stairs two steps at a time.

一度(いちど)に means doing things all together rather than separately.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 一度(いちど)に + verb (do all at once)
  • 一度(いちど)に済(す)ます (finish all at once)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一度(いちど)に: all at once (quantity/number)
  • 一気(いっき)に: in one breath, all at once (continuous action)
  • まとめて: together, in bulk
