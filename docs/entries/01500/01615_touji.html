当時(とうじ)

とうじ
noun
at that time, in those days
1. at that time, in those days
Refers to a specific period in the past that has been mentioned or is understood from context.
当時(とうじ)、私(わたし)は学生(がくせい)でした。
At that time, I was a student.
当時(とうじ)の写真(しゃしん)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me photos from that time.
当時(とうじ)は大変(たいへん)でした。
Those days were difficult.

当時(とうじ) refers to a specific time in the past.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 当時(とうじ)の + noun (~ of that time)
  • 当時(とうじ)は (at that time)
  • 当時(とうじ)から (since that time)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • その頃(ころ)/時(とき): at that time (casual)
  • 当時(とうじ): at that time (slightly formal)
  • あの頃(ころ): in those days (nostalgic)
