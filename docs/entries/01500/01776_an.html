案(あん)

あん
noun
plan, proposal, idea
1. plan, proposal
A suggested course of action or scheme.
案(あん)を出(だ)す。
To put forward a proposal.
新(あたら)しい案(あん)を考(かんが)えてください。
Please think of a new proposal.
予算(よさん)案(あん)について話(はな)し合(あ)い、最終的(さいしゅうてき)に全員(ぜんいん)が賛成(さんせい)した。
We discussed the budget proposal, and in the end everyone agreed.
2. idea, thought
A concept or notion for doing something.
いい案(あん)が浮(う)かんだ。
A good idea came to me.
何(なに)か案(あん)はありませんか。
Do you have any ideas?
案(あん)を練(ね)りに練(ね)って、やっと良(よ)い方法(ほうほう)を見(み)つけた。
After working the idea over and over, I finally found a good approach.
3. draft, bill
A preliminary version of a document or law.
法案(ほうあん)が可決(かけつ)された。
The bill was passed.
改正(かいせい)案(あん)を作成(さくせい)する。
To draft an amendment.
この法案(ほうあん)は国会(こっかい)で長(なが)い時間(じかん)議論(ぎろん)された末(すえ)に成立(せいりつ)した。
This bill was enacted after lengthy debate in the Diet.

案(あん) has two main uses: a plan/idea, and a draft document or proposal. Very productive as a suffix in compound words.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 案(あん)を出(だ)す: to propose an idea
  • 案(あん)が浮(う)かぶ: an idea comes to mind
  • 案(あん)を練(ね)る: to work out a plan
  • 案(あん)の定(じょう): as expected (set phrase)

AS A SUFFIX (～案(あん)):

  • 法案(ほうあん): bill (legislation)
  • 予算案(よさんあん): budget proposal
  • 企画案(きかくあん): project proposal
  • 代案(だいあん): alternative plan
  • 名案(めいあん): brilliant idea
  • 改善案(かいぜんあん): improvement proposal

NOTE:
案(あん)の定(じょう) (as expected, sure enough) is a common set phrase where 案(あん) retains its older meaning of "expectation."
