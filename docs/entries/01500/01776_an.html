案(あん)

あん
noun
plan, proposal, idea
1. plan, proposal
A suggested course of action or scheme.
予算(よさん)案(あん)を提出(ていしゅつ)する。
To submit a budget proposal.
2. idea, thought
A concept or notion for doing something.
いい案(あん)が浮(う)かんだ。
A good idea came to me.
3. draft, bill
A preliminary version of a document or law.
法(ほう)案(あん)が可決(かけつ)された。
The bill was passed.

案(あん) refers to plans, ideas, or draft documents.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 案(あん)を出(だ)す (to propose an idea)
  • 案(あん)が浮(う)かぶ (an idea comes to mind)
  • 案(あん)を練(ね)る (to work out a plan)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 法案(ほうあん): bill (legislation)
  • 予算案(よさんあん): budget proposal
  • 企画案(きかくあん): project proposal
  • 代案(だいあん): alternative plan
  • 名案(めいあん): brilliant idea
