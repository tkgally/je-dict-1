誘(さそ)う

さそう
verb (godan)
to invite, to ask out
1. to invite, to ask out
To ask someone to join or go somewhere.
友達(ともだち)を映画(えいが)に誘(さそ)う。
To invite a friend to a movie.
食事(しょくじ)に誘(さそ)われた。
I was invited to dinner.
飲(の)みに誘(さそ)う。
To invite someone for drinks.
パーティーに誘(さそ)った。
I invited them to a party.
買(か)い物(もの)に誘(さそ)われた。
I was invited to go shopping.
デートに誘(さそ)う。
To ask out on a date.
2. to tempt, to lure
To attract or entice.
眠(ねむ)りを誘(さそ)う。
To induce sleep.
涙(なみだ)を誘(さそ)う話(はなし)だ。
It's a story that brings tears.
甘(あま)い香(かお)りが食欲(しょくよく)を誘(さそ)う。
The sweet aroma whets the appetite.
好奇心(こうきしん)を誘(さそ)う。
To arouse curiosity.

誘(さそ)う is a godan verb meaning to invite someone or to tempt/evoke something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle for the person/thing being invited or evoked

TWO MAIN USES:
1. To invite: 友達(ともだち)を映画(えいが)に誘(さそ)う (invite a friend to a movie)
2. To evoke: 涙(なみだ)を誘(さそ)う (bring to tears)

PARTICLE PATTERN:

  • 人を + 場所/活動に + 誘(さそ)う: to invite someone to somewhere/something

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 食事(しょくじ)に誘(さそ)う: to invite to dinner
  • 飲(の)みに誘(さそ)う: to invite for drinks
  • 眠(ねむ)りを誘(さそ)う: to induce sleep

RELATED:

  • 誘(さそ)い: invitation (noun)
  • お誘(さそ)い: invitation (polite)
  • 誘(さそ)われる: to be invited (passive)
