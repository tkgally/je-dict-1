床屋(とこや)

とこや
noun
barber, barbershop
1. barber, barbershop
A shop where men get their hair cut; also refers to the barber who works there.
床屋(とこや)に行(い)ってきます。
I'm going to the barbershop.
床屋(とこや)で髪(かみ)を切(き)りました。
I got my hair cut at the barbershop.
この辺(へん)に床屋(とこや)はありますか。
Is there a barbershop around here?
父(ちち)はいつも同(おな)じ床屋(とこや)に通(かよ)っています。
My father always goes to the same barbershop.
床屋(とこや)で短(みじか)く切(き)ってもらった。
I had them cut it short at the barbershop.

床屋(とこや) traditionally refers to men's barbershops.

BARBERSHOP vs HAIR SALON:

  • 床屋(とこや)/理髪店(りはつてん): barbershop (traditionally for men)
  • 美容院(びよういん)/美容室(びようしつ): hair salon (originally for women, now unisex)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 床屋(とこや)に行(い)く: to go to the barber
  • 床屋(とこや)で髪(かみ)を切(き)る: to get a haircut at the barber

Note: Younger generations often use 美容院(びよういん) regardless of gender. 床屋(とこや) can sound slightly old-fashioned.
