預(あず)ける

あずける
verb (ichidan)
to deposit, to entrust
1. to deposit, to entrust, to leave with
To give something to someone for safekeeping.
銀行(ぎんこう)にお金(かね)を預(あず)けました。
I deposited money in the bank.
荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)けたい。
I'd like to check my luggage.
子供(こども)を保育園(ほいくえん)に預(あず)けている。
I leave my child at nursery school.

預(あず)ける means to entrust or deposit.

PAIR VERB:

  • 預(あず)ける: to entrust, deposit [giver]
  • 預(あず)かる: to keep (for someone) [receiver]

COMMON USES:

  • お金(かね)を預(あず)ける: to deposit money
  • 荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)ける: to check luggage
  • 子供(こども)を預(あず)ける: to leave children (with someone)

Note: Ichidan verb (る-verb).
