扱(あつか)う

あつかう
verb (godan)
to handle, to deal with
1. to handle, to treat, to deal with
To manage or work with something or someone.
この機械(きかい)は丁寧(ていねい)に扱(あつか)ってください。
Please handle this machine carefully.
彼(かれ)を子供(こども)扱(あつか)いしないで。
Don't treat him like a child.
この店(みせ)は輸入品(ゆにゅうひん)を扱(あつか)っています。
This store deals in imported goods.

扱(あつか)う is a godan verb meaning to handle, treat, or deal with something or someone.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). Takes を particle.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を扱(あつか)う: to handle/deal with ~
  • 〜扱(あつか)いする: to treat as ~ (e.g., 子供(こども)扱(あつか)いする)
  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)う: to handle, manage (compound verb)
  • 丁寧(ていねい)に扱(あつか)う: to handle carefully

RELATED TERMS:

  • 取(と)り扱(あつか)い: handling, treatment, service
  • 扱(あつか)い方(かた): way of handling
  • 取扱(とりあつかい)説明書(せつめいしょ): instruction manual
