意地(いじ)

いじ
noun
stubbornness, willfulness, pride
1. stubbornness, obstinacy
A determined or persistent attitude, refusing to give in.
意地(いじ)を張(は)らないで。
Don't be so stubborn.
Common expression
意地(いじ)悪(わる)なことを言(い)う。
To say mean things.
意地(いじ)でも諦(あきら)めない。
I won't give up no matter what.
2. pride, willfulness
A sense of self-respect that prevents yielding to others.
3. disposition, nature
One's basic character or temperament.

意地(いじ) relates to stubbornness, pride, or disposition.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 意地(いじ)を張(は)る (to be stubborn)
  • 意地(いじ)が悪(わる)い (mean-spirited)
  • 意地(いじ)でも (no matter what, stubbornly)
  • 意地(いじ)になる (to become stubborn)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 意地悪(いじわる): meanness, spite
  • 食(く)い意地(いじ): gluttony, greediness for food

NOTE:

  • Different from 維持(いじ) (maintenance) - same reading, different kanji
