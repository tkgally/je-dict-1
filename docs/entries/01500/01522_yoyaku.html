予約(よやく)

よやく
noun (verbal)
reservation, booking
1. reservation, booking, appointment
An arrangement to have something at a particular time.
レストランの予約(よやく)をした。
I made a restaurant reservation.
予約(よやく)が必要(ひつよう)ですか。
Is a reservation required?
人気(にんき)のレストランなので、一週間(いっしゅうかん)前(まえ)に予約(よやく)した。
It's a popular restaurant, so I made a reservation a week in advance.

予約(よやく) means reservation, booking, or appointment. As a verbal noun, it forms 予約(よやく)する (to reserve/book).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予約(よやく)する: to make a reservation
  • 予約(よやく)を取(と)る: to make a reservation
  • 予約(よやく)を入(い)れる: to book, make an appointment
  • 予約(よやく)を取(と)り消(け)す: to cancel a reservation

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レストランの予約(よやく): restaurant reservation
  • ホテルの予約(よやく): hotel booking
  • 病院(びょういん)の予約(よやく): doctor's appointment
  • 予約(よやく)済(ず)み: already reserved

RELATED:

  • 予約(よやく)席(せき): reserved seat
  • 予約(よやく)制(せい): reservation required
  • 要(よう)予約(よやく): reservation necessary
