1. discharge from hospital
Being released from a hospital after treatment.
来週(らいしゅう)退院(たいいん)できます。
I can be discharged next week.
父(ちち)が無事(ぶじ)に退院(たいいん)しました。
My father was safely discharged from the hospital.
退院(たいいん)おめでとうございます。
Congratulations on leaving the hospital.

退院(たいいん)する means to be discharged from a hospital after receiving treatment. The noun form 退院(たいいん) refers to the discharge itself.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 退院(たいいん)する

WORD FORMATION:
退(たい) (withdraw) + 院(いん) (institution) — literally "leaving the institution."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 退院(たいいん)する: to be discharged
  • 退院(たいいん)後(ご): after discharge
  • 退院(たいいん)日(び): discharge date
  • 無事(ぶじ)に退院(たいいん)する: to be safely discharged
  • 退院(たいいん)おめでとう: congratulations on leaving the hospital

RELATED TERMS:

  • 入院(にゅういん): hospitalization (opposite)
  • 通院(つういん): visiting hospital as outpatient
  • 病院(びょういん): hospital

USAGE:
Often used with できる to express being allowed to leave: 退院(たいいん)できる (can be discharged). The decision to discharge is made by the doctor.

Related Words

Antonym: 入院(にゅういん)する (hospitalization)
