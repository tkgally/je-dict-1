1. to boil, to simmer, to stew
To cook in liquid over heat.
野菜(やさい)を煮(に)る。
To boil vegetables.
大根(だいこん)を弱火(よわび)で煮(に)る。
To simmer daikon radish on low heat.
味(あじ)が染(し)みるまでじっくり煮(に)たほうがおいしい。
It tastes better if you simmer it slowly until the flavor soaks in.

煮(に)る is an ichidan verb meaning to boil, simmer, or stew food in liquid.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 煮(に)える (intransitive, something boils/simmers)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 野菜(やさい)を煮(に)る: to boil vegetables
  • 弱火(よわび)で煮(に)る: to simmer on low heat
  • じっくり煮(に)る: to simmer slowly
  • 味(あじ)が染(し)みるまで煮(に)る: to simmer until flavor soaks in

RELATED COOKING TERMS:

  • 煮物(にもの): simmered dish (common Japanese dish)
  • 煮込(にこ)む: to stew, simmer for a long time
  • 煮(に)つける: to simmer in seasoned liquid
  • 煮(に)出(だ)す: to boil to extract (like stock)

OTHER COOKING VERBS:

  • 焼(や)く: to grill, roast, bake
  • 炒(いた)める: to stir-fry
  • 蒸(む)す: to steam
  • 揚(あ)げる: to deep-fry
