遅(おく)れ

おくれ
noun
delay, lateness, lag

Definitions

1. delay, lateness
Being late or behind schedule.
2. lag, falling behind
Being behind in development or progress.

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)に5分(ふん)の遅(おく)れが出(で)ている。
The train is running 5 minutes late.
技術(ぎじゅつ)の遅(おく)れを取(と)り戻(もど)す。
To catch up on technological lag.
遅(おく)れを取(と)らないように。
So as not to fall behind.

Notes

遅(おく)れ is the noun form of 遅(おく)れる (to be late).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 遅(おく)れが出(で)る (a delay occurs)
  • 遅(おく)れを取(と)る (to fall behind)
  • 遅(おく)れを取(と)り戻(もど)す (to make up for lost time)
  • 〜分(ふん)の遅(おく)れ (~ minutes' delay)

RELATED VERB:

  • 遅(おく)れる: to be late, to be delayed
