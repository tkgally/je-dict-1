1. consultation, discussion
Discussing a matter with someone to get advice or reach a decision.
先生(せんせい)に相談(そうだん)しました。
I consulted with the teacher.
何(なに)か相談(そうだん)したいことがあります。
There's something I'd like to discuss with you.
親(おや)に相談(そうだん)してから決(き)めます。
I'll decide after consulting with my parents.

相談(そうだん)する means to talk something over with someone, seeking their advice or input before making a decision. It emphasizes a collaborative discussion, not simply asking a question.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 相談(そうだん)するする

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に相談(そうだん)する: to consult with ~ (the person you consult takes に)
  • 〜について相談(そうだん)する: to consult about ~
  • 相談(そうだん)に乗(の)る: to hear someone out, give advice (from the adviser's perspective)
  • ご相談(そうだん)ください: Please consult with us (polite/business)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相談(そうだん)相手(あいて): person to consult
  • 相談(そうだん)窓口(まどぐち): consultation desk
  • 無料(むりょう)相談(そうだん): free consultation

ASPECT (ている):
相談(そうだん)している means "is currently discussing/consulting" — the process is ongoing.

NOTE:
The particle に marks the person consulted: 友達(ともだち)に相談(そうだん)する (consult with a friend). This is a common source of confusion for learners who expect と.
