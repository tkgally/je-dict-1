今更(いまさら)

いまさら
adverb
now (after all this time), at this late stage
1. now (after all this time), at this late stage
Indicates that it's too late for something, or questions the point of doing something now.
今更(いまさら)言(い)われても困(こま)る。
It's troublesome to be told that now (of all times).
今更(いまさら)後悔(こうかい)しても遅(おそ)い。
It's too late to regret it now.
今更(いまさら)ですが、質問(しつもん)があります。
I know it's late to ask, but I have a question.
Polite hedging usage

今更(いまさら) expresses that something is too late or pointless at this stage.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 今更(いまさら)～ても (even if ~ now...)
  • 今更(いまさら)ですが (I know it's late, but...)
  • 今更(いまさら)ながら (belatedly, even now)

NUANCES:

  • Often implies frustration or resignation
  • Can soften requests when used with ですが
  • 今更(いまさら)ながら is more literary/formal
