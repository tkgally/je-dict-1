ご存(ぞん)じ

ごぞんじ
noun
knowing (honorific)
1. knowing, being aware of (honorific)
Honorific form of 知(し)っている, used when politely asking if someone knows something or stating that someone (respected) knows something.
この言葉(ことば)をご存(ぞん)じですか。
Do you know this word?
Polite way to ask if someone knows something
ご存(ぞん)じのように、来月(らいげつ)引(ひ)っ越(こ)します。
As you know, I'm moving next month.
Common phrase: ご存じのように
先生(せんせい)はもうご存(ぞん)じだと思(おも)います。
I think the teacher already knows.
この会社(かいしゃ)のことをご存(ぞん)じですか。
Do you know about this company?
ご存(ぞん)じの通(とお)り、来週(らいしゅう)は休業(きゅうぎょう)となります。
As you know, we will be closed next week.

ご存(ぞん)じ is the honorific form of 知(し)っている (to know).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ご存(ぞん)じですか: Do you know? (polite)
  • ご存(ぞん)じのように: As you know
  • ご存(ぞん)じの通(とお)り: As you know
  • ご存(ぞん)じない: not know (honorific negative)

KEIGO FORMS OF 知る:

  • 知(し)っている: plain form
  • ご存(ぞん)じ: honorific (for others)
  • 存(ぞん)じている: humble (for self, formal)
