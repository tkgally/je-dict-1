一体(いったい)

いったい
adverb/noun
what on earth, body, whole
1. what on earth, what in the world
Used in questions to express surprise, confusion, or emphasis.
一体(いったい)何(なに)が起(お)きたの？
What on earth happened?
Emphatic question
一体(いったい)どこに行(い)くつもりですか？
Where in the world are you planning to go?
2. one body, whole, unity
A single unified body or entity.
心身(しんしん)一体(いったい)となる。
Mind and body become one.
Noun usage

一体(いったい) has two main uses: as an emphatic question word and as a noun meaning unity/body.

USAGE AS ADVERB:

  • 一体(いったい) + question word (何(なに)、どこ、誰(だれ)、etc.)
  • Adds emphasis or frustration to questions
  • "What on earth," "who in the world," etc.

USAGE AS NOUN:

  • 一体(いったい)となる (become one)
  • 一体(いったい)感(かん) (sense of unity)
  • 心身(しんしん)一体(いったい) (unity of mind and body)
