いつか

いつか
adverb
someday, sometime, one day
1. someday, sometime, one day
At an unspecified time in the future.
2. at some point, before one knows it
At an unspecified time, often referring to the past.
いつか日本(にほん)に行(い)きたい。
I want to go to Japan someday.
いつかまた会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet again sometime.
いつか見(み)た映画(えいが)だ。
It's a movie I saw at some point.
Past reference

いつか refers to an unspecified time.

FUTURE USE:

  • いつか + verb (someday will ~)
  • いつか必(かなら)ず (someday for sure)

PAST USE:

  • いつか + past verb (at some point ~)
  • いつかどこかで (sometime somewhere)

WRITING:

  • Usually hiragana
  • Can be written 何時(いつ)か

NOTE:

  • いつか (someday) vs 五日(いつか) (5th day) - context distinguishes them
