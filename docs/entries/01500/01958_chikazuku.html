近(ちか)づく

ちかづく
verb (godan)
to approach, to get close
1. to approach, to get close
To move nearer to something.
2. to draw near (time)
For a time or event to approach.
駅(えき)に近(ちか)づく。
To approach the station.
夏(なつ)が近(ちか)づいている。
Summer is approaching.
危険(きけん)に近(ちか)づかないで。
Don't get close to the danger.

近(ちか)づく means to approach.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 近(ちか)づく (intransitive): to approach
  • 近(ちか)づける (transitive): to bring close

PATTERN:

  • Xに近(ちか)づく: to approach X

USAGE:

  • Physical distance
  • Time approaching
