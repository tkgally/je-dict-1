近(ちか)づく

ちかづく
verb (godan)
to approach, to get close
1. to approach, to get close
To move nearer to something.
駅(えき)に近(ちか)づく。
To approach the station.
危険(きけん)に近(ちか)づかないで。
Don't get close to the danger.
もっと近(ちか)づいてください。
Please come closer.
Sense 1: get close
動物(どうぶつ)に近(ちか)づくな。
Don't approach the animals.
Sense 1: approach
彼(かれ)に近(ちか)づきたい。
I want to get close to him.
Sense 1: get close
2. to draw near (time)
For a time or event to approach.
夏(なつ)が近(ちか)づいている。
Summer is approaching.
試験(しけん)の日(ひ)が近(ちか)づいてきた。
The exam day is drawing near.
Sense 2: draw near (time)
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)づいている。
The deadline is approaching.
Sense 2: approach (time)
卒業(そつぎょう)の日(ひ)が近(ちか)づく。
Graduation day approaches.
Sense 2: draw near
年末(ねんまつ)が近(ちか)づいてきた。
The end of the year is approaching.
Sense 2: draw near (time)

近(ちか)づく means to approach, either physically or in terms of time.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 近(ちか)づける (transitive, to bring close)

PATTERN:
Xに近(ちか)づく: to approach X, get close to X

TWO MAIN USES:
1. Physical approach: 駅(えき)に近(ちか)づく (approach the station)
2. Time approaching: 夏(なつ)が近(ちか)づく (summer is approaching)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 危険(きけん)に近(ちか)づくな: Don't approach the danger
  • 締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)づいている: The deadline is approaching
  • もっと近(ちか)づいて: Come closer

NEGATIVE WARNING:
〜に近(ちか)づくな is a common warning meaning "don't go near ~."
