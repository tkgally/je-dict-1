振(ふ)る

ふる
verb (godan)
to wave, to shake
1. to wave, to swing
To move something back and forth.
手(て)を振(ふ)る。
To wave one's hand.
頭(あたま)を振(ふ)る。
To shake one's head.
2. to shake, to sprinkle
To scatter by shaking.
塩(しお)を振(ふ)る。
To sprinkle salt.

振(ふ)る is a godan verb meaning to wave, shake, or sprinkle.

CONJUGATION:
振(ふ)らない (neg), 振(ふ)ります (polite), 振(ふ)って (te-form), 振(ふ)った (past)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 手(て)を振(ふ)る: to wave (one's hand)
  • 首(くび)を振(ふ)る: to shake one's head (disagreement)
  • 尻尾(しっぽ)を振(ふ)る: to wag one's tail
  • 塩(しお)を振(ふ)る: to sprinkle salt
  • サイコロを振(ふ)る: to roll dice
  • 振(ふ)り向(む)く: to turn around

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 振(ふ)る: to wave, shake (transitive)
  • 降(ふ)る: to fall (rain/snow) (intransitive)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 振動(しんどう): vibration
  • 振替(ふりかえ): transfer, exchange
