1. to warm up, to get warm
To become warm; to be warmed (intransitive).
部屋(へや)が暖(あたた)まってきた。
The room has warmed up.
お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)って体(からだ)を暖(あたた)まろう。
Let's take a bath and warm up.
心(こころ)が暖(あたた)まる話(はなし)。
A heartwarming story.

暖(あたた)まる means something becomes warm on its own — a room heats up, the weather warms.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 暖(あたた)める (transitive — to warm something)

ASPECT (ている):
暖(あたた)まっている means 'is warm' (resulting state).

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 暖(あたた)まる: air/room temperature, weather warming up
  • 温(あたた)まる: body warmth, food, emotional warmth (心(こころ)が温(あたた)まる)

In practice, the distinction is often blurred and both kanji are used.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 部屋(へや)が暖(あたた)まる: the room warms up
  • 体(からだ)が暖(あたた)まる: to warm up (one's body)
  • 心(こころ)が温(あたた)まる: to be heartwarmed
