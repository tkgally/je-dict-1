暖(あたた)まる

あたたまる
verb (godan)
to warm up

Definitions

1. to warm up, to get warm
To become warm; to be warmed (intransitive).

Examples

部屋(へや)が暖(あたた)まってきた。
The room has warmed up.
お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)って体(からだ)を暖(あたた)まろう。
Let's take a bath and warm up.
心(こころ)が暖(あたた)まる話(はなし)。
A heartwarming story.

Notes

暖(あたた)まる is the intransitive form meaning to warm up.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 暖(あたた)まる (v.i.): to warm up
  • 暖(あたた)める (v.t.): to warm (something)

KANJI VARIATION:

  • 暖(あたた)まる: warm (temperature/room)
  • 温(あたた)まる: warm (body/emotional)

Note: Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし)).
