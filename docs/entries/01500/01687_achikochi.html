あちこち

あちこち
noun, adverb
here and there, all over
1. here and there, various places
Multiple locations, not just one specific place.
あちこち探(さが)しました。
I searched here and there.
あちこち旅行(りょこう)しました。
I traveled to various places.
鍵(かぎ)をあちこち探(さが)したけど見(み)つからなかった。
I searched all over for my keys but couldn't find them.
2. all over, everywhere
Many different places throughout an area.
部屋(へや)のあちこちに本(ほん)があります。
There are books all over the room.
体(からだ)のあちこちが痛(いた)い。
My body hurts all over.

あちこち means here and there or various places.

USAGE:

  • As noun: あちこちに/で/を + verb
  • As adverb: あちこち + verb

VARIATIONS:

  • あちらこちら: same meaning (slightly more formal)
  • あっちこっち: same meaning (casual)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あちこち見(み)る (look around various places)
  • あちこちに散(ち)らばる (scattered here and there)
  • あちこち行(い)く (go to various places)
