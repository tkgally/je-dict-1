清潔(せいけつ)

せいけつ
na-adjective, noun
clean, hygienic
1. clean, hygienic
Free from dirt or impurities. Often used for environments, objects, or personal cleanliness.
2. pure, clean-cut
Morally clean or having a neat, wholesome appearance.
部屋(へや)を清潔(せいけつ)に保(たも)ってください。
Please keep the room clean.
清潔(せいけつ)な環境(かんきょう)が大切(たいせつ)です。
A clean environment is important.
彼(かれ)は清潔(せいけつ)な印象(いんしょう)を与(あた)える。
He gives a clean-cut impression.

清潔(せいけつ) is a na-adjective meaning clean or hygienic.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 清潔(せいけつ)に (cleanly)
  • Noun modifier: 清潔(せいけつ)な + noun
  • Noun form: 清潔(せいけつ)さ (cleanliness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 清潔(せいけつ)に保(たも)つ (keep clean)
  • 清潔(せいけつ)な環境(かんきょう) (clean environment)
  • 清潔(せいけつ)感(かん) (sense of cleanliness)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • きれい: clean, pretty (casual)
  • 清潔(せいけつ): clean, hygienic (formal)
  • 衛生的(えいせいてき): sanitary (technical)
