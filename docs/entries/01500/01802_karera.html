彼(かれ)ら

かれら
pronoun
they (male)
1. they, them
Third-person plural pronoun, traditionally referring to males or mixed groups.
彼(かれ)らは学生(がくせい)です。
They are students.
彼(かれ)らに聞(き)いてみましょう。
Let's ask them.
彼(かれ)らの意見(いけん)は違(ちが)う。
Their opinions are different.

彼(かれ)ら is the plural of 彼(かれ).

USAGE:

  • Traditionally for males or mixed groups
  • Can be used gender-neutrally in modern usage

OTHER PRONOUNS:

  • 彼(かれ): he
  • 彼女(かのじょ): she
  • 彼女(かのじょ)たち: they (female)
  • 私(わたし)たち: we
  • あの人(ひと)たち: those people
