彼(かれ)ら

かれら
pronoun
they, them (male or mixed group)
See also: 彼(かれ) (singular form)
1. they, them
Third-person plural pronoun, traditionally referring to males or mixed groups.
彼(かれ)らは学生(がくせい)です。
They are students.
彼(かれ)らに聞(き)いてみましょう。
Let's ask them.
彼(かれ)らの意見(いけん)は違(ちが)う。
Their opinions are different.
彼(かれ)らは毎日(まいにち)一緒(いっしょ)に練習(れんしゅう)している。
They practice together every day.
彼(かれ)らが来(く)る前(まえ)に部屋(へや)を片付(かたづ)けておこう。
Let's tidy the room before they come.

彼(かれ)ら is the third-person plural pronoun, formed from 彼(かれ) (he) + ら (plural suffix).

TRADITIONAL USAGE:

  • All-male groups
  • Mixed-gender groups

MODERN USAGE:
Increasingly used gender-neutrally, especially in writing.

GRAMMAR:

  • 彼(かれ)らは: they (subject)
  • 彼(かれ)らの: their
  • 彼(かれ)らに: to them
  • 彼(かれ)らを: them (object)

PRONOUN SYSTEM:

  • 彼(かれ): he → 彼(かれ)ら: they (m/mixed)
  • 彼女(かのじょ): she → 彼女(かのじょ)たち: they (f)
  • 私(わたし): I → 私(わたし)たち: we
  • あの人(ひと): that person → あの人(ひと)たち: those people

NOTE:
In everyday speech, Japanese often omits pronouns. あの人(ひと)たち is less direct than 彼(かれ)ら.
