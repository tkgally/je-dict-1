新(あら)た

あらた
na-adjective
new, fresh
1. new, fresh
Something that has just come into being or is being done for the first time.
新(あら)たな問題(もんだい)が発生(はっせい)しました。
A new problem has arisen.
2. renewed, afresh
Starting again with a fresh perspective or approach.
新(あら)たに始(はじ)めましょう。
Let's start anew.
新(あら)たに as adverb
気持(きも)ちを新(あら)たにする。
To renew one's feelings/attitude.
Common expression

新(あら)た is a na-adjective meaning new or fresh.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 新(あら)たに (newly, afresh)
  • Noun modifier: 新(あら)たな + noun

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 新(あら)たな出発(しゅっぱつ) (fresh start)
  • 新(あら)たに始(はじ)める (start anew)
  • 気持(きも)ちを新(あら)たにする (refresh one's attitude)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新(あたら)しい: new (i-adjective, common)
  • 新(あら)た: new (na-adjective, more literary/formal)
  • フレッシュ: fresh (loanword)
