1. to be buried, to be covered
To be hidden under something.
雪(ゆき)に埋(う)まる。
To be buried in snow.
家(いえ)が土砂(どしゃ)に埋(う)まった。
The house was buried in a landslide.
落(お)ち葉(ば)に埋(う)まった道(みち)を歩(ある)く。
To walk on a path buried in fallen leaves.
2. to be filled, to be packed
To have all spaces occupied.
席(せき)が埋(う)まった。
The seats are filled.
予定(よてい)が埋(う)まっている。
My schedule is full.
会場(かいじょう)は人(ひと)で埋(う)まっていた。
The venue was packed with people.

埋(う)まる is an intransitive godan verb meaning to be buried or filled.

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • 埋(う)まる (自動詞(じどうし)): to be buried/filled
  • 埋(う)める (他動詞(たどうし)): to bury/fill

PHYSICAL BURIAL:

  • 雪(ゆき)に埋(う)まる: to be buried in snow
  • 砂(すな)に埋(う)まる: to be buried in sand
  • 落(お)ち葉(ば)に埋(う)まる: to be covered in fallen leaves

FIGURATIVE (filled up):

  • 席(せき)が埋(う)まる: seats fill up
  • 予定(よてい)が埋(う)まる: schedule fills up
  • 空白(くうはく)が埋(う)まる: gaps get filled
  • 穴(あな)が埋(う)まる: holes get filled

RELATED:

  • 埋(う)め立(た)てる: to reclaim land (by filling)
  • 埋(まい)蔵(ぞう): buried (treasure, resources)
