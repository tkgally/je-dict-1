あいにく

あいにく
adverb, na-adjective
unfortunately, unluckily
1. unfortunately, unluckily
Expresses regret that circumstances are unfavorable or that something didn't work out as hoped.
あいにく、今日(きょう)は予定(よてい)があります。
Unfortunately, I have plans today.
Polite refusal
あいにくの雨(あめ)でした。
Unfortunately, it was raining.
あいにくの + noun pattern
あいにく席(せき)がいっぱいです。
Unfortunately, there are no seats available.

あいにく expresses that something is unfortunate or inconvenient.

USAGE:

  • As adverb: あいにく + clause
  • As na-adjective: あいにくの + noun (unfortunate ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あいにくの天気(てんき) (unfortunate weather)
  • あいにく留守(るす)にしておりました (unfortunately I was out)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 残念(ざんねん)ながら: regrettably (formal)
  • あいにく: unfortunately (often for specific circumstances)
  • 生憎(あいにく): same word, kanji form (rare)
