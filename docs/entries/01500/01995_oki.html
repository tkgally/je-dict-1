〜おき

おき
suffix
every ~, at intervals of ~
1. every ~, at intervals of ~
Indicates regular intervals or spacing.
一日(いちにち)おきに走(はし)る。
I run every other day.
このバスは十分(じっぷん)おきに来(く)るので便利(べんり)だ。
This bus comes every 10 minutes, so it's convenient.
街路樹(がいろじゅ)が数(すう)メートルおきに植(う)えられていて、夏(なつ)は木陰(こかげ)が気持(きも)ちいい。
Street trees are planted every few meters, and in summer the shade feels pleasant.

〜おき indicates regular intervals.

PATTERN:

  • [number + counter] + おき

EXAMPLES:

  • 一日(いちにち)おき: every other day
  • 一週間(いっしゅうかん)おき: every other week
  • 5分(ふん)おき: every 5 minutes

NOTE:

  • 一日(いちにち)おき means "with one day between" (= every other day)
  • For "every day" use 毎日(まいにち)
