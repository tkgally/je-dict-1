案外(あんがい)

あんがい
adverb
unexpectedly, surprisingly

Definitions

1. unexpectedly, surprisingly
Contrary to what one expected. Often used when reality differs from expectations in either a positive or negative way.

Examples

案外(あんがい)簡単(かんたん)でした。
It was surprisingly easy.
彼(かれ)は案外(あんがい)優(やさ)しい人(ひと)だった。
He turned out to be unexpectedly kind.
案外(あんがい)時間(じかん)がかかりました。
It took longer than expected.

Notes

案外(あんがい) expresses that something turned out differently from expectations.

USAGE:

  • Neutral tone, can describe positive or negative surprises
  • Often modifies adjectives or verbs directly
  • Can stand alone at the beginning of a sentence

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 意外(いがい)に: unexpectedly (similar, slightly more emphatic)
  • 思(おも)ったより: more than expected
  • 案外(あんがい): unexpectedly (neutral observation)
