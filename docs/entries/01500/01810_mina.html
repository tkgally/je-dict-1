皆(みな)

みな
noun, adverb
everyone, all
1. everyone, everybody
All the people in a group.
皆(みな)さん、おはようございます。
Good morning, everyone.
皆(みな)元気(げんき)ですか。
Is everyone well?
皆(みな)で食事(しょくじ)に行(い)こう。
Let's all go eat together.
Sense 1: everyone
皆(みな)が賛成(さんせい)した。
Everyone agreed.
Sense 1: everyone
皆(みな)さんのおかげです。
It's thanks to all of you.
Sense 1: everyone (polite)
皆(みな)同(おな)じ意見(いけん)だった。
Everyone had the same opinion.
Sense 1: everyone
2. all, everything
The whole of something; every part.
お金(かね)は皆(みな)使(つか)ってしまった。
I spent all the money.
荷物(にもつ)は皆(みな)揃(そろ)いましたか。
Is all the luggage ready?
Sense 2: all
料理(りょうり)は皆(みな)食(た)べてしまった。
We ate all the food.
Sense 2: all
問題(もんだい)は皆(みな)解決(かいけつ)した。
All the problems were solved.
Sense 2: all

皆(みな) means everyone or all.

VARIATIONS:

  • 皆(みな): formal
  • みんな: casual (more common in speech)
  • 皆(みな)さん: everyone (polite address)
  • 皆(みな)様(さま): everyone (very polite)

USAGE:

  • 皆(みな)で: together, all together
  • 皆(みな)が: everyone (as subject)
