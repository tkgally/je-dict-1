料理(りょうり)

りょうり
noun (verbal)
cooking, cuisine

Definitions

1. cooking, cuisine, dish
Food that has been prepared; the act of preparing food.

Examples

日本(にほん)料理(りょうり)が好(す)きです。
I like Japanese cuisine.
母(はは)の料理(りょうり)は美味(おい)しい。
My mother's cooking is delicious.

Notes

料理(りょうり)する is the verbal form meaning "to cook." Types: 和食(わしょく) (Japanese), 洋食(ようしょく) (Western), 中華料理(ちゅうかりょうり) (Chinese).
