楽(たの)しみ

たのしみ
noun, na-adjective
enjoyment, pleasure; looking forward to
1. enjoyment, pleasure, fun
Something that brings happiness or enjoyment; a source of pleasure.
週末(しゅうまつ)の楽(たの)しみは映画(えいが)を見(み)ることです。
My weekend pleasure is watching movies.
2. looking forward to, anticipation
The feeling of eager expectation for something pleasant to come.
旅行(りょこう)が楽(たの)しみです。
I'm looking forward to the trip.
Very common expression
お会(あ)いできるのを楽(たの)しみにしています。
I'm looking forward to meeting you.
Polite expression

楽(たの)しみ is the noun form of 楽(たの)しい (enjoyable).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が楽(たの)しみです: I'm looking forward to ~
  • 〜を楽(たの)しみにする: to look forward to ~
  • 〜を楽(たの)しみにしている: to be looking forward to ~

RELATED WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しい: fun, enjoyable (adjective)
  • 楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy (verb)
  • 楽(たの)しみ方(かた): way of enjoying

Note: When expressing anticipation, 楽(たの)しみ functions as a na-adjective: 楽(たの)しみな予定(よてい) (plans I'm looking forward to)
