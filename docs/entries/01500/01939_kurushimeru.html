苦(くる)しめる

くるしめる
verb (ichidan)
to torment, to cause suffering

Definitions

1. to torment, to cause suffering
To inflict pain or distress on someone.

Examples

人(ひと)を苦(くる)しめる。
To torment someone.
病気(びょうき)が彼(かれ)を苦(くる)しめた。
The illness tormented him.
悩(なや)みが心(こころ)を苦(くる)しめる。
Worries torment the heart.

Notes

苦(くる)しめる means to torment or cause suffering.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 苦(くる)しめる (transitive): to cause suffering
  • 苦(くる)しむ (intransitive): to suffer

USAGE:

  • Xを苦(くる)しめる: to torment X
  • Can be used for people, illnesses, or abstract concepts as subjects
