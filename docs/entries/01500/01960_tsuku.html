1. to be attached, to stick
To become connected or adhered.
ゴミが付(つ)いている。
There's dirt stuck on it.
服(ふく)にペンキが付(つ)いた。
Paint got on my clothes.
毎日(まいにち)練習(れんしゅう)していたら、だんだん力(ちから)が付(つ)いてきた。
By practicing every day, I gradually built up strength.
2. to be included, to come with
To be provided along with something.
おまけが付(つ)く。
It comes with a freebie.
このプランには朝食(ちょうしょく)が付(つ)いている。
This plan includes breakfast.
新(あたら)しいパソコンには三(さん)年(ねん)の保証(ほしょう)が付(つ)いている。
The new computer comes with a three-year warranty.

付(つ)く is an intransitive godan verb meaning something attaches or sticks by itself.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive): 付(つ)く - something attaches
  • 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive): 付(つ)ける - to attach something

ASPECT (ている):
付(つ)いている indicates a resulting state: "is attached/stuck."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)が付(つ)く: to notice, become aware
  • 身(み)に付(つ)く: to acquire (a skill), to learn
  • 運(うん)が付(つ)く: to be lucky
  • 嘘(うそ)が付(つ)く: to lie (different kanji: 嘘(うそ)をつく)
  • 〜付(つ)き: with 〜 included (朝食(ちょうしょく)付(つ)き = with breakfast)
