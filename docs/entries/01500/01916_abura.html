脂(あぶら)

あぶら
noun
fat, grease, oil (animal)

Definitions

1. fat, grease
Animal fat or body oil.
2. oiliness, greasiness
The quality of being oily or greasy.

Examples

肉(にく)の脂(あぶら)を取(と)り除(のぞ)く。
To remove the fat from the meat.
脂(あぶら)がのった魚(さかな)。
A fish with good fat content.
Desirable quality
顔(かお)の脂(あぶら)を拭(ふ)く。
To wipe the oil off one's face.

Notes

脂(あぶら) refers to animal fat or body oil.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 脂(あぶら): animal fat, body oil
  • 油(あぶら): oil (vegetable, petroleum, etc.)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 脂(あぶら)がのる (to be fatty/well-marbled)
  • 脂(あぶら)っこい (greasy, oily)
  • 脂肪(しぼう): fat (scientific term)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 皮脂(ひし): sebum (skin oil)
  • 脂身(あぶらみ): fatty meat
