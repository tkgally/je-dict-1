営業(えいぎょう)

えいぎょう
noun/suru verb
business, sales, operation
1. business, trade
Commercial activity of buying and selling.
彼(かれ)は小(ちい)さな店(みせ)を営業(えいぎょう)している。
He runs a small shop.
この地域(ちいき)で営業(えいぎょう)を始(はじ)めた。
We started doing business in this area.
許可(きょか)なく営業(えいぎょう)するのは違法(いほう)だ。
Operating a business without a permit is illegal.
2. sales, sales work
The activity of selling products or services.
営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ)で働(はたら)いている。
I work in the sales department.
営業(えいぎょう)の仕事(しごと)は大変(たいへん)だ。
Sales work is tough.
新(あたら)しいお客(きゃく)さんに営業(えいぎょう)に行(い)く。
I'm going on a sales call to a new customer.
3. operation, being open for business
The state of conducting business.
営業(えいぎょう)時間(じかん)は9時(じ)からです。
Business hours are from 9 o'clock.
本日(ほんじつ)営業(えいぎょう)中(ちゅう)です。
We are open for business today.
日曜日(にちようび)は営業(えいぎょう)していますか。
Are you open on Sundays?

営業(えいぎょう) covers three related meanings: running a business, sales work, and being open for business. It is one of the most common business-related words.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 営業(えいぎょう)時間(じかん): business hours
  • 営業(えいぎょう)中(ちゅう): open for business (sign)
  • 営業(えいぎょう)部(ぶ): sales department
  • 営業(えいぎょう)マン(まん): salesperson
  • 営業(えいぎょう)所(しょ): sales office, branch
  • 営業(えいぎょう)日(び): business day

USAGE:
As a する verb, 営業(えいぎょう)する can mean "to operate a business" or "to be open." When asking a store, 営業(えいぎょう)していますか means "Are you open?" In a corporate context, 営業(えいぎょう)に行(い)く means to go on a sales call.

CONTRAST:

  • 休業(きゅうぎょう): closed for business (opposite)
  • 商売(しょうばい): trade, business (more informal, small-scale)
  • 販売(はんばい): selling (focused on the transaction itself)
