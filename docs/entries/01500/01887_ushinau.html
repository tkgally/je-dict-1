1. to lose
To no longer have something that one previously had.
自信(じしん)を失(うしな)う。
To lose confidence.
事故(じこ)で命(いのち)を失(うしな)った。
Lost their life in an accident.
意識(いしき)を失(うしな)う。
To lose consciousness.
地震(じしん)で家(いえ)を失(うしな)った人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
Many people lost their homes in the earthquake.
大切(たいせつ)なものを失(うしな)って初(はじ)めてその価値(かち)に気(き)づく。
You don't realize the value of something important until you lose it.

失(うしな)う is a godan verb meaning to lose, typically used for abstract or significant things rather than everyday objects.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: 〜を失(うしな)う

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自信(じしん)を失(うしな)う: to lose confidence
  • 意識(いしき)を失(うしな)う: to lose consciousness
  • 命(いのち)を失(うしな)う: to lose one's life
  • 信頼(しんらい)を失(うしな)う: to lose trust
  • 記憶(きおく)を失(うしな)う: to lose one's memory
  • 財産(ざいさん)を失(うしな)う: to lose one's fortune

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • なくす: to lose (concrete objects like keys, wallet — more casual)
  • 失(うしな)う: to lose (abstract things, significant losses — more formal/literary)

ASPECT (ている):
失(うしな)っている indicates a resulting state: "has lost" / "is without."
