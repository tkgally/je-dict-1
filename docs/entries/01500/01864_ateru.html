当(あ)てる

あてる
verb (ichidan)
to hit; to guess correctly
1. to hit, to apply
To make something hit a target; to apply something to.
的(まと)に当(あ)てる。
To hit the target.
光(ひかり)を当(あ)てる。
To shine light on.
2. to guess correctly
To get the right answer; to correctly identify.
答(こた)えを当(あ)ててください。
Please guess the answer.

当(あ)てる is the transitive form.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 当(あ)てる (v.t.): to hit (something)
  • 当(あ)たる (v.i.): to hit, to be hit

COMMON USES:

  • 的(まと)に当(あ)てる: to hit target
  • 光(ひかり)を当(あ)てる: to shine light on
  • 名前(なまえ)を当(あ)てる: to guess the name
