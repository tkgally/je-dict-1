1. to hit, to apply
To make something hit a target; to apply something to.
的(まと)に当(あ)てる。
To hit the target.
光(ひかり)を当(あ)てる。
To shine light on.
ボールを壁(かべ)に当(あ)てて遊(あそ)んだ。
I played by throwing a ball against the wall.
2. to guess correctly
To get the right answer; to correctly identify.
答(こた)えを当(あ)ててください。
Please guess the answer.
犯人(はんにん)を当(あ)ててみてください。
Try to guess who the culprit is.
彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)の誕生日(たんじょうび)をぴったり当(あ)てた。
She guessed my birthday exactly right.

当(あ)てる is a transitive ichidan verb with two core meanings: physically hitting a target, and correctly guessing.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 当(あ)たる (intransitive, to hit/be hit)

ASPECT (ている):
当(あ)てている indicates ongoing action of applying or aiming something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 的(まと)に当(あ)てる: to hit a target
  • 光(ひかり)を当(あ)てる: to shine light on
  • 日(ひ)に当(あ)てる: to expose to sunlight
  • 答(こた)えを当(あ)てる: to guess the answer correctly
  • くじを当(あ)てる: to win a lottery
  • 人(ひと)に当(あ)てる: to call on someone (in class)

NOTE:
The 'guess correctly' meaning often appears in games and quizzes: 犯人(はんにん)を当(あ)てる (identify the culprit).
