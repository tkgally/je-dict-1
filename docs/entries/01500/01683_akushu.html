握手(あくしゅ)

あくしゅ
noun, suru verb
handshake
1. handshake
The act of shaking hands as a greeting or sign of agreement.
握手(あくしゅ)をしましょう。
Let's shake hands.
初(はじ)めて会(あ)った時(とき)、握手(あくしゅ)しました。
We shook hands when we first met.
彼(かれ)と握手(あくしゅ)できて嬉(うれ)しかったです。
I was happy to shake hands with him.

握手(あくしゅ) means handshake.

TRANSITIVITY (as suru verb):

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし)
  • Pattern: Xと握手(あくしゅ)する (shake hands with X)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 握手(あくしゅ)をする (shake hands)
  • ～と握手(あくしゅ)する (shake hands with ~)
  • 握手(あくしゅ)を求(もと)める (ask for a handshake)
  • 握手(あくしゅ)会(かい) (meet-and-greet, handshake event)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Handshaking is less common in traditional Japanese culture (bowing is standard), but is used in business and international contexts.
