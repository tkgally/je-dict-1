身(み)

み
noun
body, oneself, position
1. body, flesh
The physical body or its flesh.
身(み)に付(つ)ける。
To wear, to acquire (a skill).
Common expression
2. oneself, one's position
One's own person or situation.
身(み)の安全(あんぜん)を守(まも)る。
To protect one's safety.
身(み)に付(つ)ける。
To wear, to acquire (a skill).
Common expression
3. meat, flesh (of fish/fruit)
The edible part of fish or fruit.
魚(さかな)の身(み)をほぐす。
To flake the fish meat.

身(み) has multiple meanings related to body and self.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 身(み)に付(つ)ける (to wear; to acquire skill)
  • 身(み)に覚(おぼ)えがある (to have experience of)
  • 身(み)を守(まも)る (to protect oneself)
  • 身(み)を引(ひ)く (to step aside, withdraw)
  • 身(み)の程(ほど) (one's station in life)
  • 身(み)の回(まわ)り (one's surroundings/belongings)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 身体(しんたい)/身体(からだ): body
  • 身分(みぶん): social status
  • 身元(みもと): identity
