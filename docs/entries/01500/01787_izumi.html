泉(いずみ)

いずみ
noun
spring, fountain, source

Definitions

1. spring, fountain
A natural source of water emerging from the ground.
2. source, wellspring
Figuratively, a source or origin of something.

Examples

山(やま)の中(なか)に泉(いずみ)がある。
There's a spring in the mountains.
泉(いずみ)の水(みず)は冷(つめ)たい。
The spring water is cold.
知識(ちしき)の泉(いずみ)。
A fountain of knowledge.
Figurative usage

Notes

泉(いずみ) refers to natural springs or figurative sources.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 泉(いずみ)が湧(わ)く (a spring wells up)
  • 〜の泉(いずみ) (source/fountain of ~)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 温泉(おんせん): hot spring
  • 噴水(ふんすい): fountain (man-made)
  • 源(みなもと): source, origin

NAME NOTE:

  • いずみ is a common Japanese given name
