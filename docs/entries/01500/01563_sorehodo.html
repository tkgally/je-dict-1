それほど

それほど
adverb
to that extent, that much

Definitions

1. to that extent, that much, so much
Indicates degree or extent, often used with negative expressions to mean 'not that much' or 'not particularly'. Can also be used in positive contexts to emphasize extent.

Examples

それほど難(むずか)しくないです。
It's not that difficult.
Common pattern: それほど + negative
それほど高(たか)くなかったので、買(か)いました。
It wasn't that expensive, so I bought it.
それほど心配(しんぱい)しなくてもいいですよ。
You don't need to worry that much.

Notes

それほど is commonly used with negative predicates to soften statements, meaning 'not particularly' or 'not that much'.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • それほど〜ない (not that ~)
  • それほど〜なくてもいい (don't need to ~ that much)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • そんなに: similar meaning, slightly more casual
  • あまり: also used with negatives, but stronger negation
  • たいして: more formal, often used in written Japanese

In positive sentences, それほど emphasizes degree: それほど大切(たいせつ)なことです (It's that important).
