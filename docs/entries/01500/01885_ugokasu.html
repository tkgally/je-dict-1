動(うご)かす

うごかす
verb (godan)
to move, to operate
1. to move, to shift
To change the position of something.
机(つくえ)を動(うご)かす。
To move the desk.
心(こころ)を動(うご)かされた。
I was moved (emotionally).
2. to operate, to run
To make a machine or system work.
機械(きかい)を動(うご)かす。
To operate a machine.

動(うご)かす is a transitive godan verb meaning "to move something" or "to operate."

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 動(うご)く (intransitive - to move)

MEANINGS:
1. To move/shift: 机(つくえ)を動(うご)かす (to move a desk)
2. To operate: 機械(きかい)を動(うご)かす (to operate a machine)
3. To affect emotionally: 心(こころ)を動(うご)かす (to move someone's heart)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 体(からだ)を動(うご)かす: to exercise (move one's body)
  • 心(こころ)を動(うご)かされる: to be moved (emotionally)
  • 手(て)を動(うご)かす: to get to work (move one's hands)

ASPECT:
動(うご)かしている: is moving (something) / is operating
