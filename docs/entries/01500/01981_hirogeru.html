広(ひろ)げる

ひろげる
verb (ichidan)
to spread, to extend
1. to spread, to open out
To make something cover a wider area.
地図(ちず)を広(ひろ)げる。
To spread out a map.
翼(つばさ)を広(ひろ)げる。
To spread wings.
2. to expand, to widen
To increase the scope of something.
視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げる。
To broaden one's horizons.

広(ひろ)げる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to spread, extend, or expand something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive): 広(ひろ)げる - to spread something
  • 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive): 広(ひろ)がる - something spreads

ASPECT (ている):
広(ひろ)げている indicates the action is ongoing or a resulting state.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 地図(ちず)を広(ひろ)げる: to spread out a map
  • 視野(しや)を広(ひろ)げる: to broaden one's horizons
  • 手(て)を広(ひろ)げる: to spread one's arms/hands
  • 翼(つばさ)を広(ひろ)げる: to spread wings
  • 事業(じぎょう)を広(ひろ)げる: to expand a business
