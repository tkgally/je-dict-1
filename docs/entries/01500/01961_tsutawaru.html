伝(つた)わる

つたわる
verb (godan)
to be conveyed, to spread
1. to be conveyed, to be communicated
For information to reach someone.
気持(きも)ちが伝(つた)わった。
My feelings got through.
噂(うわさ)が伝(つた)わる。
Rumors spread.
2. to be handed down, to be transmitted
For tradition to pass through generations.
昔(むかし)から伝(つた)わる話(はなし)。
A story passed down from old times.

伝(つた)わる is an intransitive godan verb meaning "to be conveyed" or "to be passed down."

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 伝(つた)える (transitive - to convey, to tell)

USAGES:
1. Information being communicated: 気持(きも)ちが伝(つた)わる (feelings get through)
2. Traditions being passed down: 昔(むかし)から伝(つた)わる (handed down from old times)
3. Things spreading: 噂(うわさ)が伝(つた)わる (rumors spread)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に伝(つた)わる: to reach, to be conveyed to
  • 〜から伝(つた)わる: to be passed down from

ASPECT:
伝(つた)わっている: has been passed down (resulting state)
