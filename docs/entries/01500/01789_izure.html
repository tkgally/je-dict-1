いずれ

いずれ
adverb/pronoun
someday, eventually, which
1. someday, eventually, sooner or later
At some point in the future.
いずれ分(わ)かることだ。
You'll understand eventually.
いずれ会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet someday.
いずれ結果(けっか)が出(で)る。
The results will come out eventually.
Sense 1: eventually
いずれ時間(じかん)が解決(かいけつ)する。
Time will solve it eventually.
Sense 1: sooner or later
いずれまた連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you again someday.
Sense 1: someday
2. which, whichever, either
Used when referring to one among several options.
いずれも正(ただ)しい。
Both are correct.
いずれか一(ひと)つを選(えら)んでください。
Please choose one of them.
いずれも素晴(すば)らしい。
All of them are wonderful.
Sense 2: all/both
いずれの方法(ほうほう)でも構(かま)いません。
Either method is fine.
Sense 2: whichever
いずれか当(あ)てはまるものを選(えら)べ。
Choose whichever applies.
Sense 2: whichever
3. anyway, in any case
Regardless of which option or outcome.
いずれにしても行(い)かなければならない。
Either way, I have to go.
いずれにせよ、準備(じゅんび)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
In any case, preparation is needed.
Sense 3: anyway
いずれにしても行(い)くつもりだ。
Either way, I intend to go.
Sense 3: in any case
いずれにせよ、決断(けつだん)しなければならない。
Anyway, I have to make a decision.
Sense 3: in any case
いずれにしろ、彼(かれ)に相談(そうだん)しよう。
Either way, let's consult him.
Sense 3: anyway

いずれ has multiple uses as adverb and pronoun.

AS ADVERB (someday):

  • いずれ + verb (someday will ~)
  • いずれ近(ちか)いうちに (sometime soon)

AS PRONOUN (which):

  • いずれも (both, all, either)
  • いずれか (one of them, either)
  • いずれにしても/いずれにせよ (in any case)

WRITING:

  • Can be written as 何(いず)れ or 孰(いず)れ (rare)
  • Usually written in hiragana
