けれど

けれど
conjunction
but, however, although
1. but, however
Used to connect contrasting statements.
行(い)きたいけれど、時間(じかん)がない。
I want to go, but I don't have time.
高(たか)いけれども、買(か)います。
It's expensive, but I'll buy it.
けれども is slightly more formal
雨(あめ)だけど、出(で)かけよう。
It's raining, but let's go out.
けど is casual
疲(つか)れたけれど、頑張(がんば)る。
I'm tired, but I'll do my best.
Sense 1: but
分(わ)からないけど、やってみる。
I don't understand, but I'll try.
Sense 1: but (casual)
すみませんけれど、教(おし)えてください。
I'm sorry, but could you tell me?
Sense 1: but (softening)
2. although, even though
Used to introduce a concessive clause.
難(むずか)しいけれども、面白(おもしろ)い。
Although it's difficult, it's interesting.
Sense 2: although
若(わか)いけれど、しっかりしている。
Although young, they are reliable.
Sense 2: although
小(ちい)さいけれど、便利(べんり)だ。
Although it's small, it's convenient.
Sense 2: although
遠(とお)いけれども、歩(ある)いて行(い)く。
Even though it's far, I'll walk.
Sense 2: even though

けれど is a conjunction meaning "but" or "although".

FORMS (casual → formal):

  • けど: most casual
  • けれど: casual
  • けれども: neutral/formal
  • ですけれども: polite

USAGE:

  • Attaches to plain form of verbs/adjectives
  • Can end sentences softly (trailing off)
