支(ささ)える

ささえる
verb (ichidan)
to support, to hold up
1. to support, to hold up
To keep something from falling or failing.
2. to sustain, to maintain
To provide what is needed.
家族(かぞく)を支(ささ)える。
To support a family.
経済(けいざい)を支(ささ)える。
To support the economy.
体(からだ)を支(ささ)える。
To hold up one's body.

支(ささ)える means to support or hold up.

USAGE:

  • Physical support: body, structure
  • Financial support: family, economy
  • Emotional support: person

RELATED:

  • 支(ささ)え: support
  • 支援(しえん): assistance
