1. to stretch, to extend
To make something longer.
手(て)を伸(の)ばす。
To stretch out one's hand.
髪(かみ)を伸(の)ばす。
To grow one's hair.
背筋(せすじ)を伸(の)ばして、きちんと座(すわ)ってください。
Straighten your back and sit properly.
2. to grow, to develop
To increase or improve.
能力(のうりょく)を伸(の)ばす。
To develop one's abilities.
売(う)り上(あ)げを伸(の)ばす。
To increase sales.
子供(こども)の才能(さいのう)を伸(の)ばすために、いろいろな経験(けいけん)をさせたい。
I want to give my child various experiences to develop their talents.

伸(の)ばす means to stretch, extend, or grow something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 伸(の)びる (intransitive - to stretch, grow)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手(て)を伸(の)ばす: to reach out one's hand
  • 髪(かみ)を伸(の)ばす: to grow out one's hair
  • 背筋(せすじ)を伸(の)ばす: to straighten one's back
  • 能力(のうりょく)を伸(の)ばす: to develop one's abilities
  • 記録(きろく)を伸(の)ばす: to extend a record

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 伸(の)ばす: to stretch physically, to develop
  • 延(の)ばす: to extend, postpone (time, deadlines)
