区別(くべつ)

くべつ
noun, suru verb
distinction, differentiation
1. distinction, differentiation
The act of recognizing or marking differences between things. Used when classifying or separating items based on their characteristics.
この二(ふた)つの言葉(ことば)の区別(くべつ)は難(むずか)しいです。
It's difficult to distinguish between these two words.
色(いろ)で区別(くべつ)してください。
Please differentiate them by color.
で marks the means of distinction
本物(ほんもの)と偽物(にせもの)を区別(くべつ)する。
To distinguish the real from the fake.

区別(くべつ) means to distinguish or differentiate between things.

TRANSITIVITY (as suru verb):

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: AとBを区別(くべつ)する (distinguish A from B)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AとBの区別(くべつ) (distinction between A and B)
  • 区別(くべつ)がつく/つかない (can/cannot tell apart)
  • 区別(くべつ)なく (without distinction)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 違(ちが)い: difference (the actual difference itself)
  • 差別(さべつ): discrimination (often negative, unfair treatment)
