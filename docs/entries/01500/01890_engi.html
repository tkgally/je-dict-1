演技(えんぎ)

えんぎ
noun/suru verb
acting, performance
1. acting, performance
The portrayal of a character in theater, film, etc.
2. pretense, acting
Behavior intended to deceive.
彼(かれ)の演技(えんぎ)は素晴(すば)らしい。
His acting is wonderful.
演技(えんぎ)力(りょく)が高(たか)い。
To have high acting ability.
あれは演技(えんぎ)だったのか。
Was that an act?
Pretense meaning

演技(えんぎ) refers to acting or performance.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 演技(えんぎ)する (to act, to perform)
  • 演技(えんぎ)力(りょく) (acting ability)
  • 演技(えんぎ)派(は) (method actor)
  • 演技(えんぎ)賞(しょう) (acting award)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 演技(えんぎ): acting (theatrical skill)
  • 演奏(えんそう): musical performance
  • 公演(こうえん): public performance/show
