居間(いま)

いま
noun
living room
1. living room
The main room in a house where the family gathers for everyday activities.
居間(いま)で本(ほん)を読(よ)む。
To read a book in the living room.
居間(いま)は広(ひろ)くて明(あか)るい。
The living room is spacious and bright.
居間(いま)に新(あたら)しい机(つくえ)を置(お)いた。
I put a new desk in the living room.
子供(こども)たちが居間(いま)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing in the living room.
居間(いま)の窓(まど)を開(あ)けたら、気持(きも)ちのいい風(かぜ)が入(はい)ってきた。
When I opened the living room window, a pleasant breeze came in.

WORD FORMATION:
居(い) (to be, to stay) + 間(ま) (room, space). Literally "the room where one stays."

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 居間(いま): living room (traditional Japanese term)
  • リビング: living room (loanword, very common in modern speech)
  • リビングルーム: living room (full loanword, less common)
  • 茶(ちゃ)の間(ま): family sitting room (traditional, associated with tatami)

REGISTER:
居間(いま) is an ordinary, neutral everyday word. It sounds slightly more traditional than リビング. In real estate listings and modern conversation, リビング or LDK (living-dining-kitchen) is far more common.

NOTE:
In traditional Japanese houses, the 居間(いま) was the central gathering room with tatami flooring. In modern usage, it simply means the main living area regardless of style.

Related Words

Synonym: リビング (loanword, more common)
Related: 茶(ちゃ)の間(ま) (traditional sitting room)
Synonym: リビングルーム (full loanword)
