1. to keep, to hold, to take custody of
To temporarily hold or look after something for someone else.
荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)かってもらえますか。
Could you hold my luggage for me?
お子(こ)さんを預(あず)かります。
I'll look after your child.
鍵(かぎ)を預(あず)かっています。
I'm keeping the key.
大事(だいじ)な書類(しょるい)を預(あず)かった。
I was entrusted with important documents.
旅行(りょこう)の間(あいだ)、隣(となり)の人(ひと)にペットを預(あず)かってもらった。
I had my neighbor look after my pet while I was traveling.

預(あず)かる means to temporarily hold, keep, or look after something or someone on behalf of another person. The focus is on the receiver's side — taking responsibility for safekeeping.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 預(あず)ける (to entrust, to deposit — the giver's side)

ASPECT (ている):
預(あず)かっている means "is currently keeping" or "is looking after" — an ongoing state of custody.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を預(あず)かる: to hold luggage
  • お金(かね)を預(あず)かる: to hold money
  • 子供(こども)を預(あず)かる: to look after children
  • 鍵(かぎ)を預(あず)かる: to keep a key (for someone)

USAGE:
Often used in service contexts: hotels holding luggage, daycare looking after children, banks holding deposits. The polite お預(あず)かりします is standard in customer service (e.g., receiving payment or items).
