1. to be effective, to work
To have the intended effect or result.
薬(くすり)が効(き)く。
The medicine is working.
冷房(れいぼう)が効(き)いている。
The air conditioning is on.
ブレーキがよく効(き)く。
The brakes work well.
注射(ちゅうしゃ)が効(き)いてきた。
The injection is starting to work.
暖房(だんぼう)が効(き)いている。
The heating is working.
麻酔(ますい)が効(き)いてきた。
The anesthesia is taking effect.
2. to be possible, to be able to
To be able to function or be done.
融通(ゆうずう)が効(き)く。
To be flexible.
小回(こまわ)りが効(き)く。
It's maneuverable.
無理(むり)が効(き)く体(からだ)だ。
It's a body that can handle strain.
応用(おうよう)が効(き)く技術(ぎじゅつ)だ。
It's a versatile skill.
時間(じかん)の融通(ゆうずう)が効(き)く仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a job with flexible hours.

Means something is effective, works, or takes effect. The subject is typically the thing that has the effect (medicine, brakes, air conditioning).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 効(き)く

KANJI DISTINCTION (IMPORTANT):

  • 効(き)く: to be effective (medicine, brakes, punishment)
  • 利(き)く: to work, function, be capable (融通(ゆうずう)が利(き)く, 気(き)が利(き)く)
  • 聞(き)く: to listen, hear, ask (completely different meaning)

ASPECT (ている):
効(き)いている means "is in effect" (resulting state). Example: 冷房(れいぼう)が効(き)いている (the AC is on/working).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 薬(くすり)が効(き)く: medicine works
  • ブレーキが効(き)く: brakes work
  • 冷房(れいぼう)/暖房(だんぼう)が効(き)く: AC/heating is effective
  • 効(き)いてくる: to start taking effect (gradual onset)
