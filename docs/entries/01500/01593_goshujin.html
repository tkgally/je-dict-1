ご主人(しゅじん)

ごしゅじん
noun
(your/her) husband
1. (someone else's) husband
Polite term used to refer to another person's husband. The honorific ご makes it respectful.
ご主人(しゅじん)はお元気(げんき)ですか。
How is your husband?
Polite inquiry
ご主人(しゅじん)のお仕事(しごと)は何(なん)ですか。
What does your husband do for work?
2. master, proprietor
Can also refer to the master of a house or the proprietor of a shop.
このお店(みせ)のご主人(しゅじん)は親切(しんせつ)だ。
The owner of this shop is kind.
Meaning 'proprietor'

ご主人(しゅじん) is the polite form for referring to someone else's husband.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 主人(しゅじん): my husband (humble when referring to own spouse)
  • 夫(おっと): husband (neutral/formal for own husband)
  • 旦那(だんな)さん: husband (casual-polite for someone else's)
  • 奥(おく)さん: (someone else's) wife

USAGE NOTE: Using 主人(しゅじん) for one's own husband is traditional but some consider it old-fashioned. Modern alternatives include 夫(おっと) or パートナー.
