積(つ)もる

つもる
verb (godan)
to pile up, to accumulate
1. to pile up, to accumulate
For things to collect in a heap.
雪(ゆき)が積(つ)もった。
Snow piled up.
話(はなし)が積(つ)もっている。
We have a lot to talk about.
2. to mount up (feelings)
For emotions to build up.
不満(ふまん)が積(つ)もる。
Dissatisfaction builds up.

積(つ)もる means to pile up or accumulate.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 積(つ)もる (intransitive): to pile up
  • 積(つ)む (transitive): to pile, load

EXPRESSION:

  • 積(つ)もる話(はなし): much to talk about

RELATED:

  • 積(つ)み重(かさ)ねる: to accumulate (efforts)
general
