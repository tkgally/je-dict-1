慌(あわ)てる

あわてる
verb (ichidan)
to panic, to be flustered

Definitions

1. to panic, to be flustered
To become confused or hurried due to surprise or urgency.

Examples

慌(あわ)てないでください。
Please don't panic.
時間(じかん)がなくて慌(あわ)てた。
I panicked because there was no time.
慌(あわ)てて家(いえ)を出(で)た。
I left home in a hurry.

Notes

慌(あわ)てる means to panic or be flustered.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 慌(あわ)てて: in a hurry, hastily
  • 慌(あわ)てないで: don't panic
  • 慌(あわ)て者(もの): hasty person

RELATED:

  • 焦(あせ)る: to be impatient
  • 急(いそ)ぐ: to hurry
