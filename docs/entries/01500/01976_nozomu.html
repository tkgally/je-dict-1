望(のぞ)む

のぞむ
verb (godan)
to desire, to hope for
1. to desire, to hope for
To want something to happen.
2. to look out on, to face
To have a view of.
平和(へいわ)を望(のぞ)む。
To desire peace.
望(のぞ)み通(どお)りになった。
It turned out as I wished.
海(うみ)を望(のぞ)む部屋(へや)。
A room overlooking the sea.

望(のぞ)む means to desire or hope for.

RELATED:

  • 望(のぞ)み: hope, wish
  • 希望(きぼう): hope, wish
  • 願望(がんぼう): desire

EXPRESSION:

  • お望(のぞ)み通(どお)り: as you wish
