降(お)ろす

おろす
verb (godan)
to take down, to unload
1. to take down, to lower
To move something from a higher to lower position.
棚(たな)から箱(はこ)を降(お)ろした。
I took the box down from the shelf.
Sense 1: take down
旗(はた)を降(お)ろす。
To lower the flag.
Sense 1: lower
看板(かんばん)を降(お)ろした。
I took down the sign.
Sense 1: take down
カーテンを降(お)ろす。
To lower the curtain.
Sense 1: lower
ブラインドを降(お)ろした。
I lowered the blinds.
Sense 1: lower
2. to unload, to drop off
To remove passengers or cargo from a vehicle.
荷物(にもつ)を降(お)ろす。
To unload luggage.
駅(えき)で降(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off at the station.
トラックから荷物(にもつ)を降(お)ろす。
To unload cargo from the truck.
Sense 2: unload
乗客(じょうきゃく)を降(お)ろす。
To drop off passengers.
Sense 2: drop off
ここで降(お)ろしてもらえますか。
Could you drop me off here?
Sense 2: drop off
3. to withdraw (money)
To take money out of an account.
お金(かね)を降(お)ろす。
To withdraw money.
ATMでお金(かね)を降(お)ろした。
I withdrew money from the ATM.
Sense 3: withdraw
銀行(ぎんこう)から現金(げんきん)を降(お)ろす。
To withdraw cash from the bank.
Sense 3: withdraw
貯金(ちょきん)を降(お)ろして使(つか)った。
I withdrew my savings and used it.
Sense 3: withdraw
必要(ひつよう)な分(ぶん)だけ降(お)ろす。
To withdraw only the amount needed.
Sense 3: withdraw

降(お)ろす is a transitive godan verb meaning to take down, unload, or withdraw.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 降(お)りる (intransitive, to get off/descend)

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 降(お)ろす: unload, drop off (people/cargo)
  • 下(お)ろす: lower, take down (general)
  • 卸(おろ)す: wholesale (different word)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 荷物(にもつ)を降(お)ろす: to unload luggage
  • 客(きゃく)を降(お)ろす: to drop off a passenger
  • 棚(たな)から降(お)ろす: to take down from a shelf
  • お金(かね)を降(お)ろす: to withdraw money
  • カーテンを降(お)ろす: to lower the curtain

USAGE:
For withdrawing money, both 降(お)ろす and 下(お)ろす are used. The 降(お)ろす kanji emphasizes 'bringing down' from a higher place (like an account).
