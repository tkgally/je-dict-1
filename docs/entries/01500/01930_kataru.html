1. to tell, to narrate
To recount a story or experience.
経験(けいけん)を語(かた)る。
To tell of one's experiences.
歴史(れきし)が語(かた)っている。
History tells us.
過去(かこ)を語(かた)る。
To tell about the past.
戦争(せんそう)の体験(たいけん)を語(かた)る。
To tell of war experiences.
祖父(そふ)が昔話(むかしばなし)を語(かた)ってくれた。
My grandfather told me old stories.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の人生(じんせい)を語(かた)った。
He told the story of his life.
2. to talk about, to discuss
To speak about a topic.
夢(ゆめ)を語(かた)る。
To talk about one's dreams.
将来(しょうらい)について語(かた)った。
We talked about the future.
愛(あい)について語(かた)る。
To talk about love.
政治(せいじ)を語(かた)る。
To discuss politics.
友人(ゆうじん)と人生(じんせい)について語(かた)り合(あ)った。
My friend and I talked to each other about life.

語(かた)る means to tell, narrate, or recount. It has a more literary, formal tone than 話(はな)す.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを語(かた)る / Xについて語(かた)る

ASPECT (ている):
語(かた)っている means 'is telling/narrating' (ongoing action) or 'tells us' (a fact that speaks for itself, e.g., 歴史(れきし)が語(かた)っている).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 話(はな)す: to speak, talk (everyday, casual)
  • 語(かた)る: to narrate, tell (literary, for stories and experiences)
  • 述(の)べる: to state (formal, factual)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 経験(けいけん)を語(かた)る: to tell of one's experiences
  • 夢(ゆめ)を語(かた)る: to talk about one's dreams/aspirations
  • 語(かた)り合(あ)う: to talk together, to share stories with each other

RELATED:

  • 物語(ものがたり): story, tale (noun)
  • 語(かた)り: narration, storytelling

Related Words

Contrast: 話(はな)す (to speak (everyday))
Contrast: 述(の)べる (to state (formal))
Contrast: しゃべる (to chat, to chatter (casual))
