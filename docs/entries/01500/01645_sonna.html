そんな

そんな
pre-noun adjectival
such, that kind of
1. such, that kind of, like that
Refers to something near the listener or something just mentioned in conversation. Part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system.
そんなこと言(い)わないで。
Don't say such things.
Common expression
そんな難(むずか)しい問題(もんだい)は分(わ)からない。
I don't understand such difficult problems.
そんなはずはない。
That can't be right.
Common expression of disbelief

そんな is part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system, referring to things near the listener or things just mentioned.

KO-SO-A-DO PATTERN:

  • こんな: this kind of (near speaker)
  • そんな: that kind of (near listener/just mentioned)
  • あんな: that kind of (far from both)
  • どんな: what kind of (question)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • そんなことない: That's not true
  • そんなはずはない: That can't be
  • そんな馬鹿(ばか)な: That's ridiculous

DERIVED FORM: そんなに (to that extent)
