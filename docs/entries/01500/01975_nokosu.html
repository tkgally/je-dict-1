残(のこ)す

のこす
verb (godan)
to leave behind
1. to leave behind, to leave over
To not take or use something.
食(た)べ物(もの)を残(のこ)す。
To leave food behind.
記録(きろく)を残(のこ)す。
To leave a record.
名(な)を残(のこ)す。
To leave one's name (in history).
仕事(しごと)を残(のこ)して帰(かえ)った。
I went home leaving work unfinished.
Sense 1: leave behind
足跡(あしあと)を残(のこ)す。
To leave footprints.
Sense 1: leave behind
遺産(いさん)を子供(こども)に残(のこ)す。
To leave an inheritance to one's children.
Sense 1: leave over
2. to save, to reserve
To keep something for later.
明日(あした)のために残(のこ)しておく。
I'll save it for tomorrow.
Sense 2: save
後(あと)で食(た)べるために残(のこ)す。
To save some to eat later.
Sense 2: reserve
席(せき)を残(のこ)しておいてください。
Please save a seat.
Sense 2: reserve
半分(はんぶん)残(のこ)しておく。
I'll save half.
Sense 2: save

残(のこ)す means to leave behind, save, or reserve something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 残(のこ)る (intransitive, to remain)

ASPECT (ている):
残(のこ)している indicates a state: something has been left/saved and is still in that state.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 食(た)べ物(もの)を残(のこ)す: to leave food (not finish eating)
  • 記録(きろく)を残(のこ)す: to leave a record
  • 名(な)を残(のこ)す: to leave one's name (in history)
  • 遺産(いさん)を残(のこ)す: to leave an inheritance
  • 足跡(あしあと)を残(のこ)す: to leave footprints

COMMON COMPOUND:
残(のこ)しておく: to save/keep for later (intentional action)
