見(み)かける

みかける
verb (ichidan)
to catch sight of, to happen to see
1. to catch sight of, to happen to see
To see someone or something by chance, not intentionally looking for it.
駅(えき)で彼(かれ)を見(み)かけました。
I happened to see him at the station.
この花(はな)は最近(さいきん)見(み)かけなくなった。
I don't see this flower around recently.
あの店(みせ)で有名(ゆうめい)な俳優(はいゆう)を見(み)かけたことがあります。
I once happened to see a famous actor at that store.
最近(さいきん)、町(まち)で野良猫(のらねこ)をよく見(み)かけるようになった。
Lately I've been seeing stray cats around town a lot.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で友達(ともだち)を見(み)かけた。
I happened to see a friend on the train.
彼女(かのじょ)を最近(さいきん)見(み)かけないね。
I haven't seen her around lately.
2. to notice, to spot
To become aware of something's presence.
珍(めずら)しい鳥(とり)を見(み)かけた。
I spotted a rare bird.
あの商品(しょうひん)はどこの店(みせ)でも見(み)かける。
You can see that product in any store.
新聞(しんぶん)でその記事(きじ)を見(み)かけた。
I noticed that article in the newspaper.
カフェで読書(どくしょ)している人(ひと)をよく見(み)かける。
I often see people reading at cafes.

見(み)かける means to catch sight of or happen to see.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを見(み)かける (catch sight of X)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 見(み)かけている is rarely used; typically past tense

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を見(み)かける (happen to see ~)
  • よく見(み)かける (often see)
  • 見(み)かけなくなる (stop seeing around)
  • 見(み)かけ (appearance, looks - noun)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)る: to see, watch (intentional)
  • 見(み)かける: to catch sight of (by chance)
  • 見(み)つける: to find (discovery)
