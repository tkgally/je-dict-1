油(あぶら)

あぶら
noun
oil
1. oil (cooking, vegetable)
Liquid fat used for cooking, typically from plants.
油(あぶら)で揚(あ)げました。
I deep-fried it in oil.
油(あぶら)を引(ひ)いてください。
Please add oil (to the pan).
油(あぶら)を売(う)る。
To slack off. (lit: sell oil)
Idiomatic expression
2. oil, petroleum
General term for oily substances, including machine oil.

油(あぶら) refers to oil, primarily cooking oil.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 油(あぶら)で揚(あ)げる (deep-fry in oil)
  • 油(あぶら)を引(ひ)く (add oil to a pan)
  • 油(あぶら)っこい (oily, greasy)
  • 油(あぶら)を売(う)る (idle away time - idiom)

KANJI NOTE:

  • 油(あぶら): oil (general, cooking)
  • 脂(あぶら): fat (from animals, body fat)

RELATED WORDS:

  • サラダ油(あぶら) (salad oil)
  • ごま油(あぶら) (sesame oil)
  • オリーブオイル (olive oil)
