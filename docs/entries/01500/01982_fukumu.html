含(ふく)む

ふくむ
verb (godan)
to contain, to include
1. to contain, to include
To have something as a part.
2. to hold in mouth
To keep something in one's mouth.
ビタミンを含(ふく)む。
To contain vitamins.
税(ぜい)を含(ふく)む。
Including tax.
水(みず)を口(くち)に含(ふく)む。
To hold water in one's mouth.

含(ふく)む means to contain or include.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 含(ふく)む (transitive): to contain
  • 含(ふく)まれる (passive): to be included

RELATED:

  • 含(ふく)める: to include
  • 包含(ほうがん): inclusion
