含(ふく)む

ふくむ
verb (godan)
to contain, to include
1. to contain, to include
To have something as a part.
ビタミンを含(ふく)む。
To contain vitamins.
税(ぜい)を含(ふく)む。
Including tax.
2. to hold in mouth
To keep something in one's mouth.
水(みず)を口(くち)に含(ふく)む。
To hold water in one's mouth.

含(ふく)む is a godan verb with two main meanings: to contain/include, and to hold in mouth.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: ビタミンを含(ふく)む

TWO MAIN MEANINGS:
1. To contain/include: 税(ぜい)を含(ふく)む (including tax)
2. To hold in mouth: 水(みず)を口(くち)に含(ふく)む

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を含(ふく)む: to contain/include ~
  • ～を含(ふく)めて: including ~
  • ～が含(ふく)まれる: ~ is included (passive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 税込(ぜいこ)み: tax included (abbreviation)
  • 送料(そうりょう)を含(ふく)む: including shipping
  • 意味(いみ)を含(ふく)む: to imply meaning

RELATED WORDS:

  • 含(ふく)める: to include (causative)
  • 含有(がんゆう): containing (formal)
