相手(あいて)

あいて
noun
partner, opponent, the other party
1. partner, companion
Someone you do something with, such as a conversation or activity partner.
話(はなし)相手(あいて)が欲(ほ)しいです。
I want someone to talk to.
2. opponent, rival
Someone you compete or fight against.
相手(あいて)チームは強(つよ)いです。
The opposing team is strong.
3. the other party, counterpart
The person you are dealing with or addressing.
相手(あいて)の気持(きも)ちを考(かんが)えてください。
Please consider the other person's feelings.

相手(あいて) has multiple meanings depending on context.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 話(はなし)相手(あいて) (conversation partner)
  • 結婚(けっこん)相手(あいて) (marriage partner)
  • 相手(あいて)にする (deal with, pay attention to)
  • 相手(あいて)にしない (ignore, not bother with)
  • お相手(あいて) (partner, polite)

USAGE:

  • Can mean partner, opponent, or simply 'the other person' in a two-party interaction
  • Context determines the specific meaning
