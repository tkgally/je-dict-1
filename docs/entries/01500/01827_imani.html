今(いま)に

いまに
adverb
before long, soon, eventually
1. before long, soon
In the near future, after a while.
今(いま)に分(わ)かるよ。
You'll understand soon.
今(いま)に雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうだ。
It looks like it will rain soon.
2. eventually, one of these days
At some point in the future, with a sense of certainty.
今(いま)に見(み)ていろ。
Just you wait and see.
Threatening/challenging tone

今(いま)に indicates something will happen soon or eventually.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 今(いま)に分(わ)かる (you'll understand soon)
  • 今(いま)に見(み)ていろ (just you wait)
  • 今(いま)に始(はじ)まったことではない (not something that just started)

NUANCES:

  • Often implies confidence or warning
  • Can have threatening undertone: "You'll see!"
  • 今(いま)に見(み)ていろ is a common challenging expression
