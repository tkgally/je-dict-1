違(ちが)う

ちがう
verb (godan)
to differ, to be wrong
1. to differ, to be different
To not be the same.
考(かんが)えが違(ちが)う。
We think differently.
2. to be wrong, to be incorrect
To not be right.
答(こた)えが違(ちが)う。
The answer is wrong.
違(ちが)います。
That's not right. / No.

違(ちが)う means to differ or be wrong.

PATTERNS:

  • XとYが違(ちが)う: X and Y are different
  • Xと違(ちが)って: unlike X

USAGE:

  • 違(ちが)います: polite way to say "no"

RELATED:

  • 違(ちが)い: difference
  • 間違(まちが)い: mistake
