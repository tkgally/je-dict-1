1. to differ, to be different
To not be the same.
考(かんが)えが違(ちが)う。
We think differently.
私(わたし)と彼(かれ)は性格(せいかく)が違(ちが)う。
He and I have different personalities.
文化(ぶんか)が違(ちが)うと習慣(しゅうかん)も違(ちが)う。
Different cultures have different customs.
昨日(きのう)と今日(きょう)では気温(きおん)が違(ちが)う。
The temperature is different today from yesterday.
味(あじ)が前(まえ)と違(ちが)う。
The taste is different from before.
2. to be wrong, to be incorrect
To not be right.
答(こた)えが違(ちが)う。
The answer is wrong.
違(ちが)います。
That's not right. / No.
電話番号(でんわばんごう)が違(ちが)っています。
The phone number is wrong.
ちょっと違(ちが)うと思(おも)う。
I think that's a bit wrong.
予定(よてい)と違(ちが)う。
It's not according to plan. / That's wrong.

違(ちが)う means to differ or to be wrong—a very common verb with two distinct uses.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: 違(ちが)う

SENSE 1 - DIFFERENT:

  • XとYが違(ちが)う: X and Y are different
  • Xと違(ちが)って: unlike X
  • 全然(ぜんぜん)違(ちが)う: completely different

SENSE 2 - WRONG/INCORRECT:

  • 答(こた)えが違(ちが)う: the answer is wrong
  • 違(ちが)います: No, that's not right (polite denial)
  • ちょっと違(ちが)う: a bit wrong/off

RELATED WORDS:

  • 違(ちが)い: difference (noun)
  • 間違(まちが)い: mistake
  • 間違(まちが)える: to make a mistake
  • 異(こと)なる: to differ (formal)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 違(ちが)っている: is different, is wrong (stative — describes a current state, not an ongoing action)
  • Note: 違(ちが)う is one of a small group of stative verbs (along with 優(すぐ)れる, 似(に)る, そびえる) that describe inherent states. Both plain 違(ちが)う and 違(ちが)っている are used to express the stative meaning; 違(ちが)っている often highlights that a specific item deviates from a norm or expectation. 電話番号(でんわばんごう)が違(ちが)っています = 'the phone number is wrong (compared to what it should be).' The plain 違(ちが)います is the standard polite denial 'no, that's not right.'
