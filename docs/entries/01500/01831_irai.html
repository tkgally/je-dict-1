以来(いらい)

いらい
noun/adverb
since, ever since, henceforth

Definitions

1. since, ever since
From a specific point in the past until now.
2. from now on, henceforth
From this point forward.

Examples

卒業(そつぎょう)以来(いらい)、会(あ)っていない。
I haven't seen them since graduation.
去年(きょねん)以来(いらい)のことだ。
It's the first time since last year.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)以来(いらい)だ。
It's the first time since I was a child.

Notes

以来(いらい) indicates "since" a specific time.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • X以来(いらい) (since X)
  • 〜て以来(いらい) (since doing ~)
  • 以来(いらい)ずっと (ever since then)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 以来(いらい): since (continuing until now)
  • 以後(いご): after, from then on (forward-looking)
  • 以降(いこう): from ~ onward (often written)

NOTE:

  • Different from 依頼(いらい) (request) - same reading, different kanji
