以来(いらい)

いらい
noun/adverb
since, ever since, henceforth
1. since, ever since
From a specific point in the past until now.
卒業(そつぎょう)以来(いらい)、会(あ)っていない。
I haven't seen them since graduation.
去年(きょねん)以来(いらい)のことだ。
It's the first time since last year.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)以来(いらい)だ。
It's the first time since I was a child.
結婚(けっこん)以来(いらい)、幸(しあわ)せです。
I've been happy since getting married.
Sense 1: since
会(あ)って以来(いらい)、ずっと友達(ともだち)だ。
We've been friends ever since we met.
Sense 1: ever since
入社(にゅうしゃ)以来(いらい)10年(ねん)になる。
It's been 10 years since I joined the company.
Sense 1: since
事故(じこ)以来(いらい)、車(くるま)を運転(うんてん)していない。
I haven't driven since the accident.
Sense 1: since
2. from now on, henceforth
From this point forward.
今日(きょう)以来(いらい)、禁煙(きんえん)します。
From today onwards, I'll quit smoking.
Sense 2: from now on
これ以来(いらい)、注意(ちゅうい)するようにしている。
From then on, I've been careful.
Sense 2: henceforth
今(いま)以来(いらい)、連絡(れんらく)を取(と)り合(あ)おう。
Let's keep in touch from now on.
Sense 2: from now on

以来(いらい) indicates "since" a specific time.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • X以来(いらい) (since X)
  • 〜て以来(いらい) (since doing ~)
  • 以来(いらい)ずっと (ever since then)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 以来(いらい): since (continuing until now)
  • 以後(いご): after, from then on (forward-looking)
  • 以降(いこう): from ~ onward (often written)

NOTE:

  • Different from 依頼(いらい) (request) - same reading, different kanji
