講義(こうぎ)

こうぎ
noun
lecture
1. lecture, class (at university)
An academic lecture or class, typically at a university or college level. Can also refer to the act of giving a lecture.
今日(きょう)は講義(こうぎ)が三(みっ)つあります。
I have three lectures today.
教授(きょうじゅ)の講義(こうぎ)はとても面白(おもしろ)い。
The professor's lectures are very interesting.
講義(こうぎ)を受(う)ける
to attend a lecture
Common collocation

講義(こうぎ) refers specifically to academic lectures, typically at universities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 講義(こうぎ)を受(う)ける: to attend/take a lecture
  • 講義(こうぎ)をする: to give a lecture
  • 講義(こうぎ)に出(で)る: to attend a lecture
  • 講義(こうぎ)室(しつ): lecture room

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 授業(じゅぎょう): class (more general, used for all levels)
  • セミナー: seminar (smaller, more interactive)
  • 講演(こうえん): public lecture, speech
