なる

なる
verb (godan)
to become
1. to become, to turn into
To change to a different state.
2. to result in, to end up
To have a particular outcome.
医者(いしゃ)になる。
To become a doctor.
春(はる)になった。
Spring has come.
どうなりましたか。
How did it turn out?

なる means to become.

PATTERNS:

  • Nになる: to become N
  • Adj-くなる: to become Adj
  • V-ようになる: to come to V

EXPRESSIONS:

  • どうにかなる: it'll work out
  • Xになっている: has become X
