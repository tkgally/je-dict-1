欠席(けっせき)

けっせき
noun
absence

Definitions

1. absence (from class, meeting, etc.)
The state of not being present at an event, class, or meeting where one is expected.

Examples

今日(きょう)は欠席(けっせき)する。
I'll be absent today.
欠席(けっせき)届(とどけ)を出(だ)してください。
Please submit an absence notice.
病気(びょうき)で三日(みっか)欠席(けっせき)した。
I was absent for three days due to illness.

Notes

Functions as a noun and with する as a verb (欠席(けっせき)する = to be absent).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 欠席(けっせき)する (to be absent)
  • 欠席(けっせき)届(とどけ) (absence notice)
  • 欠席(けっせき)者(しゃ) (absentee)
  • 欠席(けっせき)理由(りゆう) (reason for absence)
  • 無断(むだん)欠席(けっせき) (unexcused absence)

Related Words

Antonym: 出席(しゅっせき) (attendance)
