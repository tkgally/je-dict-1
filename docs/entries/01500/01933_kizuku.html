気付(きづ)く

きづく
verb (godan)
to notice, to realize
1. to notice, to become aware of
To perceive something one hadn't noticed before.
間違(まちが)いに気付(きづ)いた。
I noticed the mistake.
誰(だれ)も気付(きづ)かなかった。
Nobody noticed.
2. to realize, to recognize
To become conscious of a fact or situation.
大切(たいせつ)さに気付(きづ)く。
To realize the importance.

気付(きづ)く is a godan verb meaning to notice or realize something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)

PARTICLE PATTERN:

  • Xに気付(きづ)く: to notice X, to become aware of X

ASPECT (ている):
気付(きづ)いている indicates a state of awareness: 彼(かれ)は気付(きづ)いている (He is aware / He has noticed).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 間違(まちが)いに気付(きづ)く: to notice a mistake
  • 変化(へんか)に気付(きづ)く: to notice a change
  • 大切(たいせつ)さに気付(きづ)く: to realize the importance

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 気(き)がつく: alternative phrasing, same meaning
  • 気付(きづ)き: realization (noun form)

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 気付(きづ)く (きづく): to notice
  • 築(きず)く (きずく): to build, construct
