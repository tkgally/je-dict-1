気付(きづ)く

きづく
verb (godan)
to notice, to realize
1. to notice, to become aware of
To perceive something one hadn't noticed before.
間違(まちが)いに気付(きづ)いた。
I noticed the mistake.
誰(だれ)も気付(きづ)かなかった。
Nobody noticed.
2. to realize, to recognize
To become conscious of a fact or situation.
大切(たいせつ)さに気付(きづ)く。
To realize the importance.

気付(きづ)く means to notice or realize.

PATTERN:

  • Xに気付(きづ)く: to notice X

DON'T CONFUSE:

  • 気付(きづ)く: to notice (きづく)
  • 築(きず)く: to build (きずく)

RELATED:

  • 気(き)がつく: to notice (alternative form)
  • 気付(きづ)き: realization
