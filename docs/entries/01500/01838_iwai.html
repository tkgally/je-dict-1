祝(いわ)い

いわい
noun
celebration, congratulations, gift
1. celebration, festivity
An event or act of celebrating something happy.
2. congratulations
Words or actions expressing joy at someone's good fortune.
3. celebratory gift
A gift given to celebrate an occasion.
結婚(けっこん)祝(いわ)いを贈(おく)る。
To give a wedding gift.
お祝(いわ)いの言葉(ことば)をいただいた。
I received words of congratulations.
誕生日(たんじょうび)の祝(いわ)いをする。
To celebrate a birthday.

祝(いわ)い is the noun form of 祝(いわ)う (to celebrate).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お祝(いわ)い (celebration, gift - polite)
  • 祝(いわ)いの席(せき) (celebratory gathering)
  • お祝(いわ)いを述(の)べる (to offer congratulations)

COMMON TYPES:

  • 結婚(けっこん)祝(いわ)い: wedding celebration/gift
  • 出産(しゅっさん)祝(いわ)い: baby shower gift
  • 入学(にゅうがく)祝(いわ)い: school entrance celebration
  • 還暦(かんれき)祝(いわ)い: 60th birthday celebration
