迎え(むかえ)

むかえ
noun
greeting, welcome, pick-up
1. greeting, welcome
The act of receiving or welcoming someone.
2. pick-up
Going to get someone from a place.
空港(くうこう)まで迎え(むかえ)に行(い)きます。
I'll go pick you up at the airport.
お迎(むか)えが来(き)ました。
Your ride is here.

From the verb 迎(むか)える. Often used with に行(い)く/来(く)る for "go/come to pick up."
