維持(いじ)

いじ
noun, suru verb
maintenance, preservation
1. maintenance, preservation
The act of keeping something in good condition or at the same level over time.
健康(けんこう)を維持(いじ)することが大切(たいせつ)です。
It's important to maintain your health.
秩序(ちつじょ)を維持(いじ)する。
To maintain order.
維持(いじ)費(ひ)がかかります。
Maintenance costs money.
維持(いじ)費(ひ) = maintenance costs
2. to maintain, to preserve
To keep something in its existing state.

維持(いじ) means to maintain or keep in a certain state.

TRANSITIVITY (as suru verb):

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを維持(いじ)する (maintain X)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 健康(けんこう)を維持(いじ)する (maintain health)
  • 平和(へいわ)を維持(いじ)する (maintain peace)
  • 水準(すいじゅん)を維持(いじ)する (maintain standards)
  • 維持(いじ)費(ひ) (maintenance costs)
  • 維持(いじ)管理(かんり) (maintenance and management)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 保(たも)つ: to keep, maintain (native verb)
  • 維持(いじ)する: to maintain (Sino-Japanese)
  • 保存(ほぞん)する: to preserve (focus on storage)
