種類(しゅるい)

しゅるい
noun
kind, type, variety
1. kind, type, sort
A category or class of things sharing common characteristics. Used to classify or categorize items.
2. variety, assortment
The range of different types available.
どんな種類(しゅるい)の音楽(おんがく)が好(す)きですか。
What kind of music do you like?
この店(みせ)には種類(しゅるい)が多(おお)いです。
This store has many varieties.
三(さん)種類(しゅるい)のケーキがあります。
There are three kinds of cake.
Counter usage

種類(しゅるい) is a noun meaning kind, type, or variety.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • どんな種類(しゅるい)の～ (what kind of ~)
  • ～の種類(しゅるい) (kinds of ~)
  • 数字(すうじ)+種類(しゅるい) (~ kinds/types)
  • 種類(しゅるい)が多(おお)い/少(すく)ない (many/few varieties)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • タイプ: type (loanword, casual)
  • 類(るい): kind, type (formal, often in compounds)
  • 品種(ひんしゅ): variety (for plants, animals)
