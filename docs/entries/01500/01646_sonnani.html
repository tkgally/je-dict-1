そんなに

そんなに
adverb
so much, to that extent
1. so much, to that extent, that much
Indicates degree or extent, often used with negatives to mean 'not that much' or in questions/exclamations about extent.
そんなに心配(しんぱい)しなくてもいいよ。
You don't need to worry that much.
Common reassurance
そんなに食(た)べられない。
I can't eat that much.
そんなに好(す)きなの？
Do you like it that much?

そんなに indicates degree, derived from そんな + に.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • そんなに〜ない: not that much ~
  • そんなに〜の?: do you ~ that much?

KO-SO-A-DO DEGREE ADVERBS:

  • こんなに: this much
  • そんなに: that much (near listener)
  • あんなに: that much (far)
  • どんなに: how much

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • それほど: to that extent (slightly more formal)
  • あまり: (not) very (with negatives)
