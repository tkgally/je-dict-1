積極的(せっきょくてき)

せっきょくてき
na-adjective
positive, proactive, active
1. positive, proactive
Having a constructive and enthusiastic attitude. Taking initiative rather than waiting passively.
彼女(かのじょ)は積極的(せっきょくてき)な性格(せいかく)です。
She has a proactive personality.
もっと積極的(せっきょくてき)になりたい。
I want to become more proactive.
2. active, assertive
Acting decisively and with purpose.
積極的(せっきょくてき)に参加(さんか)してください。
Please participate actively.

積極的(せっきょくてき) is a na-adjective meaning positive, proactive, or active.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 積極的(せっきょくてき)に (actively, proactively)
  • Noun modifier: 積極的(せっきょくてき)な + noun

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 積極的(せっきょくてき)に取(と)り組(く)む (tackle proactively)
  • 積極的(せっきょくてき)な姿勢(しせい) (positive attitude)
  • 積極的(せっきょくてき)に行動(こうどう)する (act proactively)

ANTONYM:

  • 消極的(しょうきょくてき): passive, negative
