争(あらそ)う

あらそう
verb (godan)
to compete, to argue
1. to compete, to contend
To struggle against others for something.
一位(いちい)を争(あらそ)っている。
They are competing for first place.
2. to argue, to dispute
To disagree or quarrel with someone.
意見(いけん)を争(あらそ)う。
To dispute opinions.
争(あらそ)えない事実(じじつ)。
An undeniable fact.

争(あらそ)う means to compete or argue.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜を争(あらそ)う: to compete for ~
  • 争(あらそ)えない: undeniable, indisputable

RELATED TERMS:

  • 争(あらそ)い: dispute, conflict
  • 競争(きょうそう): competition
  • 戦(たたか)う: to fight
