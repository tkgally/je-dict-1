やはり

やはり
adverb
as expected, after all, still
1. as expected, as I thought
Used when something turns out as anticipated.
やはり彼(かれ)が犯人(はんにん)だった。
As I thought, he was the culprit.
やはり難(むずか)しかった。
As expected, it was difficult.
Sense 1: as expected
やはり雨(あめ)が降(ふ)った。
As I thought, it rained.
Sense 1: as expected
やはり間違(まちが)っていた。
As I suspected, it was wrong.
Sense 1: as expected
やはり予想(よそう)通(どお)りだった。
It was just as predicted.
Sense 1: as expected
2. after all, in the end
Used when returning to an original conclusion.
やっぱり行(い)かない。
I'm not going after all.
やっぱり is casual form
やはり無理(むり)だった。
In the end, it was impossible.
Sense 2: after all
考(かんが)え直(なお)して、やはり買(か)うことにした。
After reconsidering, I decided to buy it after all.
Sense 2: after all
やはりこれが一番(いちばん)いい。
This is the best after all.
Sense 2: after all
やはりやめます。
I'm going to quit after all.
Sense 2: after all/in the end
3. still, even so
Emphasizing that something remains unchanged.
何度(なんど)見(み)ても、やはり美(うつく)しい。
No matter how many times I see it, it's still beautiful.
やはり母(はは)の料理(りょうり)が一番(いちばん)だ。
My mother's cooking is still the best.
Sense 3: still
やはり日本(にほん)が好(す)きだ。
I still love Japan.
Sense 3: still
年(とし)を取(と)っても、やはり元気(げんき)だ。
Even getting older, he's still energetic.
Sense 3: still/even so
いくら説明(せつめい)しても、やはり分(わ)からない。
No matter how much I explain, I still don't understand.
Sense 3: still

やはり expresses that something matches expectations or remains unchanged.

FORMS:

  • やはり: standard
  • やっぱり: casual, colloquial
  • やっぱ: very casual

USAGE:

  • Often at the beginning of a sentence
  • Expresses confirmation of expectations
  • Can show a return to original opinion
