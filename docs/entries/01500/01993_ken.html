〜軒(けん)

けん
counter
counter for houses, buildings
1. counter for houses, buildings, shops
Used to count houses, buildings, and establishments.
この通(とお)りには店(みせ)が三(さん)軒(けん)ある。
There are three shops on this street.
一軒家(いっけんや)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in a detached house.
何軒(なんけん)のレストランを回(まわ)った？
How many restaurants did you go to?

〜軒(けん) is the counter for houses, shops, restaurants, and other establishments.

WHAT IT COUNTS:

  • Houses, buildings
  • Shops, stores, restaurants
  • Bars, cafes, businesses

COUNTING (note sound changes):

  • 一軒(いっけん): 1 (gemination)
  • 二軒(にけん): 2
  • 三軒(さんげん): 3 (voiced)
  • 四軒(よんけん): 4
  • 何軒(なんけん): how many?

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一軒家(いっけんや): detached house (single-family home)
  • 二軒隣(にけんどなり): two doors down
  • 居酒屋(いざかや)を何軒(なんけん)も回(まわ)る: to go bar-hopping

NOTE:
軒(のき) (eaves) uses a different reading of the same kanji.
