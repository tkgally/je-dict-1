遠(とお)く

とおく
noun, adverb
far away, distance

Definitions

1. far away, distant place
A place that is far from here; a remote location.
2. from a distance, far
Used adverbially to describe doing something from far away.

Examples

遠(とお)くに山(やま)が見(み)える。
I can see mountains in the distance.
遠(とお)くから来(き)ました。
I came from far away.
遠(とお)くへ行(い)きたい。
I want to go somewhere far away.

Notes

遠(とお)く is the noun/adverb form of 遠(とお)い (far).

FORM RELATIONSHIPS:

  • 遠(とお)い: far (i-adjective)
  • 遠(とお)く: far, distance (noun/adverb)
  • 遠(とお)くに: in the distance
  • 遠(とお)くから: from far away
  • 遠(とお)くへ: to a far place

SIMILAR PATTERN WITH OTHER ADJECTIVES:

  • 近(ちか)い → 近(ちか)く: nearby
  • 高(たか)い → 高(たか)く: high (adverb)

OPPOSITE: 近(ちか)く (nearby)

Related Words

Antonym: 近(ちか)く (nearby)
