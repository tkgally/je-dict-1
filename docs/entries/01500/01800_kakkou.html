格好(かっこう)

かっこう
noun, na-adjective
appearance, shape
1. appearance, form, shape
The outward look or style of something or someone.
2. suitable, appropriate
Being proper or fitting for a situation.
その格好(かっこう)で出(で)かけるの？
Are you going out dressed like that?
Sense 1
変(へん)な格好(かっこう)をしている。
He's wearing strange clothes.
Sense 1
格好(かっこう)いいですね。
That's cool!
かっこいい form

格好(かっこう) refers to appearance or suitability.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 格好(かっこう)いい/かっこいい: cool, stylish
  • 格好(かっこう)悪(わる)い/かっこ悪(わる)い: uncool, lame
  • 格好(かっこう)をつける: to try to look cool

ALTERNATE SPELLINGS:

  • 恰好: alternate kanji
  • かっこう: hiragana form

RELATED:

  • 姿(すがた): figure, appearance
  • 服装(ふくそう): attire, dress
