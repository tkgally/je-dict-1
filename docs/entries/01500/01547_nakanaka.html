なかなか

なかなか
adverb
quite, rather; not easily
1. quite, rather, fairly (with affirmative)
To a considerable degree.
2. not easily, hardly (with negative)
Indicating difficulty or taking a long time.
なかなか上手(じょうず)ですね。
You're quite good.
なかなか覚(おぼ)えられない。
I can't easily remember it.

Meaning changes based on whether used with affirmative or negative. With affirmatives: praise. With negatives: difficulty.
