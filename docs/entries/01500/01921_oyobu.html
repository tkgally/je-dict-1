及(およ)ぶ

およぶ
verb (godan)
to reach, to extend to
1. to reach, to extend to
To arrive at or affect a certain range.
被害(ひがい)は全国(ぜんこく)に及(およ)んだ。
The damage extended nationwide.
及(およ)びません。
There's no need. / It's beyond me.
Humble expression
2. to amount to
To total a certain number or extent.
数(かず)は百(ひゃく)に及(およ)ぶ。
The number reaches a hundred.

及(およ)ぶ is an intransitive godan verb meaning to reach, extend to, or amount to.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive): 及(およ)ぶ - reaches/extends
  • 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive): 及(およ)ぼす - to exert (influence)

PATTERN:
Xに及(およ)ぶ: to reach/extend to X.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 及(およ)びません: no need / I can't compare (humble)
  • 及(およ)ばない: not necessary, unnecessary
  • 被害(ひがい)が及(およ)ぶ: damage extends to

RELATED WORDS:

  • 及(およ)び: and, as well as (formal conjunction)
  • 及(およ)ぼす: to exert, to have (effect on)
  • 言(い)及(およ)ぶ: to mention, to refer to
