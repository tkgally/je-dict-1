及(およ)ぶ

およぶ
verb (godan)
to reach, to extend to

Definitions

1. to reach, to extend to
To arrive at or affect a certain range.
2. to amount to
To total a certain number or extent.

Examples

被害(ひがい)は全国(ぜんこく)に及(およ)んだ。
The damage extended nationwide.
及(およ)びません。
There's no need. / It's beyond me.
Humble expression
数(かず)は百(ひゃく)に及(およ)ぶ。
The number reaches a hundred.

Notes

及(およ)ぶ means to reach or extend to.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 及(およ)びません: no need / I can't compare
  • 及(およ)ばない: not necessary
  • Xに及(およ)ぶ: to extend to X

RELATED:

  • 及(およ)び: and, as well as (conjunction)
  • 及(およ)ぼす: to exert (influence)
