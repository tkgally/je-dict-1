不可(ふか)

ふか
noun/na-adjective
impossible, wrong, not allowed
1. impossible, not possible
Cannot be done or achieved.
不可能(ふかのう)ではない。
It's not impossible.
2. wrong, improper
Not acceptable or appropriate.
撮影(さつえい)不可(ふか)。
No photography allowed.
Common sign
3. failing grade
An academic grade indicating failure.
成績(せいせき)が不可(ふか)だった。
The grade was a fail.

不可(ふか) means impossible, wrong, or failing.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 不可能(ふかのう): impossible
  • 不可欠(ふかけつ): indispensable
  • 不可解(ふかかい): incomprehensible
  • 不可避(ふかひ): unavoidable

USAGE:

  • Often seen in signs: 〜不可(ふか) (~ not allowed)
  • Academic grading: 不可(ふか) = F (failing)

GRADING SCALE:

  • 優(ゆう)/秀(しゅう): A, 良(りょう): B, 可(か): C, 不可(ふか): F
