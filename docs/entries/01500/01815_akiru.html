飽(あ)きる

あきる
verb (ichidan)
to get tired of
1. to get tired of, to lose interest in
To become bored with something due to repetition or excess.
この仕事(しごと)に飽(あ)きた。
I got tired of this job.
毎日(まいにち)同(おな)じことで飽(あ)きない？
Don't you get tired of the same thing every day?
飽(あ)きずに続(つづ)けてください。
Please continue without getting tired of it.

飽(あ)きる means to become bored or tired of.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に飽(あ)きる: to get tired of ~
  • 飽(あ)きっぽい: easily bored, fickle
  • 飽(あ)きない: not get tired of
  • 飽(あ)きずに: without getting tired

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • うんざりする: to be fed up
  • 退屈(たいくつ)する: to be bored

Note: Ichidan verb (る-verb).
