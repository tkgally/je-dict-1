1. to get tired of, to lose interest in
To become bored with something due to repetition or excess.
この仕事(しごと)に飽(あ)きた。
I got tired of this job.
毎日(まいにち)同(おな)じことで飽(あ)きない？
Don't you get tired of the same thing every day?
飽(あ)きずに続(つづ)けてください。
Please continue without getting tired of it.
子供(こども)はすぐに新(あたら)しいおもちゃに飽(あ)きる。
Children quickly get tired of new toys.
この景色(けしき)は何度(なんど)見(み)ても飽(あ)きない。
I never get tired of this view no matter how many times I see it.

飽(あ)きる means to lose interest due to repetition or excess. Ichidan (る) verb.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Takes に for the thing one tires of: 〜に飽(あ)きる

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に飽(あ)きる: to get tired of ~
  • 飽(あ)きない: never get tired of (positive evaluation)
  • 飽(あ)きずに: without getting tired (perseverance)
  • 飽(あ)きっぽい: easily bored, fickle (adjective form)
  • 飽(あ)き飽(あ)きする: to be completely fed up with

ASPECT (ている):
飽(あ)きている means "is tired of" (resulting state).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うんざりする: to be fed up (stronger, negative)
  • 退屈(たいくつ)する: to be bored (from lack of stimulation)
  • 嫌(いや)になる: to start disliking (stronger rejection)

飽(あ)きる is milder than うんざりする — it implies waning interest rather than active displeasure.

Related Words
