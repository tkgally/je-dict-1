大家(おおや)

おおや
noun
landlord, landlady
1. landlord, landlady
A person who rents out property to tenants.
大家(おおや)さんに家賃(やちん)を払(はら)う。
To pay rent to the landlord.
大家(おおや)さんに連絡(れんらく)する。
To contact the landlord.
大家(おおや)さんが親切(しんせつ)だ。
The landlord is kind.

大家(おおや) means landlord.

USAGE:

  • Often called 大家(おおや)さん with honorific さん
  • Gender neutral (landlord or landlady)

READINGS:

  • おおや: landlord
  • たいか: master, expert, authority (different meaning)

RELATED:

  • 借主(かりぬし)/店子(たなこ): tenant
  • 家賃(やちん): rent
