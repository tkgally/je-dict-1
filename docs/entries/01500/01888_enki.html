Common notice

延期(えんき) means postponement — putting off a planned event or deadline to a later date. A compound of 延(えん) (extend) + 期(き) (period).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 延期(えんき)する: to postpone
  • 延期(えんき)になる: to be postponed
  • 〜に延期(えんき)する: to postpone to (a date)
  • 延期(えんき)を決(き)める: to decide on a postponement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中止(ちゅうし): cancellation (not postponed, but cancelled entirely)
  • 順延(じゅんえん): postponement to the next available day (especially for outdoor events due to rain)
  • 先送(さきおく)り: putting off, deferring (often implies avoidance)

USAGE:
Common for meetings, events, sports games, and deadlines. 雨天(うてん)延期(えんき) (postponed due to rain) is a set phrase.
