議題(ぎだい)

ぎだい
noun
agenda item; topic for discussion
1. agenda item; topic for discussion
A subject or issue to be discussed at a meeting or conference.
次(つぎ)の議題(ぎだい)に移(うつ)ります。
Let's move on to the next agenda item.
今日(きょう)の議題(ぎだい)は三(みっ)つあります。
There are three items on today's agenda.
予算(よさん)の問題(もんだい)が議題(ぎだい)に上(あ)がったが、結論(けつろん)は出(で)なかった。
The budget issue came up as an agenda item, but no conclusion was reached.

議題(ぎだい) refers to items formally put forward for discussion in a meeting.

WORD FORMATION:
議(ぎ) (deliberation) + 題(だい) (topic, subject)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 議題(ぎだい)に上(あ)がる: to come up as an agenda item
  • 議題(ぎだい)に入(はい)る: to move to the agenda items
  • 議題(ぎだい)を決(き)める: to set the agenda
  • 次(つぎ)の議題(ぎだい): next item on the agenda

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 話題(わだい): topic of conversation (casual)
  • 議題(ぎだい): formal discussion topic (meetings)
  • 課題(かだい): task, issue to be addressed
