志願(しがん)する

しがんする
noun, suru verb
to volunteer; to apply
1. to volunteer; to apply; to enlist
To offer oneself for a task, position, or service of one's own will. Often used for military enlistment or applying for a challenging role.
自(みずか)ら志願(しがん)した。
He/she volunteered of their own accord.
彼(かれ)は海外(かいがい)派遣(はけん)に志願(しがん)した。
He volunteered for overseas deployment.
危険(きけん)な任務(にんむ)だったが、多(おお)くの隊員(たいいん)が志願(しがん)して参加(さんか)した。
It was a dangerous mission, but many members volunteered to participate.

志願(しがん)する emphasizes acting of one's own will, especially for difficult or important tasks.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive) / intransitive depending on use
  • Pattern: [task/role]に志願(しがん)する or [task/role]を志願(しがん)する

WORD FORMATION:
志(し) (will, aspiration) + 願(がん) (wish, desire)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 志願(しがん)兵(へい): volunteer soldier
  • 志願(しがん)者(しゃ): volunteer, applicant
  • 自(みずか)ら志願(しがん)する: to volunteer of one's own accord

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 応募(おうぼ)する: to apply (general, for jobs/contests)
  • 志願(しがん)する: to volunteer (emphasizes personal will, often for challenging roles)
  • ボランティアする: to volunteer (for community/charity work)
