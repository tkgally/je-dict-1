主治医(しゅじい)

しゅじい
noun
attending physician, primary doctor
1. attending physician, primary doctor
The doctor who is primarily responsible for a patient's ongoing medical care. In Japan, patients often maintain a relationship with one particular doctor at a clinic or hospital.
主治医(しゅじい)に相談(そうだん)した。
I consulted my primary doctor.
主治医(しゅじい)から薬(くすり)を変(か)えると言(い)われた。
My attending physician told me they would change my medication.
入院中(にゅういんちゅう)は毎朝(まいあさ)主治医(しゅじい)が回診(かいしん)に来(く)る。
During hospitalization, the attending physician comes on rounds every morning.

USAGE:
主治医(しゅじい) refers specifically to the doctor who oversees a patient's care on an ongoing basis. In Japan's healthcare system, patients typically have a 主治医(しゅじい) at a local 診療所(しんりょうじょ) (clinic) or 病院(びょういん) (hospital) whom they see regularly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 主治医(しゅじい)に相談(そうだん)する: to consult one's primary doctor
  • 主治医(しゅじい)の許可(きょか): permission from one's attending physician
  • 主治医(しゅじい)を変(か)える: to change one's primary doctor
  • 担当(たんとう)の主治医(しゅじい): the assigned attending physician
