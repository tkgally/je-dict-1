ソーセージ

そーせーじ
noun
sausage
1. sausage
A processed meat product made from ground meat stuffed into a casing. In Japan, ソーセージ typically refers to small, smooth-textured sausages similar to frankfurters or wieners.
ソーセージを焼(や)く。
To grill sausages.
朝(あさ)ごはんにソーセージと卵(たまご)を食(た)べた。
I had sausages and eggs for breakfast.
お弁当(べんとう)に入(い)れるソーセージにタコの形(かたち)の切(き)り込(こ)みを入(い)れた。
I cut slits in the sausages for the lunch box to make them look like octopuses.

USAGE:
In Japan, ソーセージ and ウインナー are often used interchangeably in everyday speech, though technically ウインナー refers to thinner Vienna-style sausages. 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ (fish sausage) is a common Japanese snack.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ソーセージを焼(や)く: to grill sausages
  • ソーセージを炒(いた)める: to pan-fry sausages
  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ: fish sausage
  • 粗挽(あらび)きソーセージ: coarsely ground sausage
