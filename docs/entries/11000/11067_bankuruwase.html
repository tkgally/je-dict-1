番狂(ばんくる)わせ

ばんくるわせ
noun
upset, unexpected result
1. upset, unexpected result, surprise outcome
An outcome that defies expectations, especially in competitions or sports where the expected winner loses.
大(おお)きな番狂(ばんくる)わせが起(お)きた。
A big upset occurred.
優勝候補(ゆうしょうこうほ)が初戦(しょせん)で負(ま)けるという番狂(ばんくる)わせがあった。
There was an upset where the tournament favorite lost in the first round.
今大会(こんたいかい)は番狂(ばんくる)わせが多(おお)く、予想(よそう)が難(むずか)しかった。
This tournament had many upsets, making predictions difficult.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 番(ばん) (order, ranking) + 狂(くる)わせ (causing to go wrong), literally "disrupting the expected order of rankings."

USAGE:
Primarily used in sports, competitions, elections, and other contexts where outcomes are predicted. The word emphasizes that the result contradicts what was expected.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 番狂(ばんくる)わせが起(お)きる: an upset occurs
  • 大(おお)きな番狂(ばんくる)わせ: a major upset
  • 番狂(ばんくる)わせを演(えん)じる: to pull off an upset
