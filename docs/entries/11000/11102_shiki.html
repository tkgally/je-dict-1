指揮(しき)

しき
noun, verb (suru)
command, conducting, direction
1. command, direction, leadership
Leading and directing a group of people in a coordinated effort, such as military operations, emergency response, or organizational activities.
隊長(たいちょう)が現場(げんば)で指揮(しき)を執(と)った。
The team leader took command at the scene.
彼(かれ)の指揮(しき)のもとでプロジェクトが成功(せいこう)した。
The project succeeded under his direction.
消防(しょうぼう)隊(たい)は指揮(しき)系統(けいとう)に従(したが)って行動(こうどう)する。
The fire brigade acts according to the chain of command.
2. conducting (music)
Directing an orchestra, band, or choir by guiding the tempo, dynamics, and expression of a musical performance.
有名(ゆうめい)な指揮者(しきしゃ)がオーケストラを指揮(しき)した。
A famous conductor conducted the orchestra.
彼女(かのじょ)は指揮(しき)棒(ぼう)を振(ふ)りながら、オーケストラ全体(ぜんたい)をまとめていた。
She brought the entire orchestra together while waving the baton.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)で校歌(こうか)の指揮(しき)を任(まか)された。
I was entrusted with conducting the school song at the graduation ceremony.

USAGE:
指揮(しき) is used in both military/organizational and musical contexts. The word carries a sense of authoritative leadership and coordination.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指揮(しき)を執(と)る (to take command)
  • 指揮(しき)官(かん) (commander, commanding officer)
  • 指揮(しき)者(しゃ) (conductor — in music)
  • 指揮(しき)棒(ぼう) (conductor's baton)
  • 指揮(しき)系統(けいとう) (chain of command)

HOMOPHONE NOTE:
Not to be confused with 〜式(しき) (ceremony, style) or 四季(しき) (four seasons), which share the same reading.
