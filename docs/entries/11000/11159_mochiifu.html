モチーフ

もちーふ
noun
motif, theme, inspiration
1. motif, theme, design element
A recurring design, pattern, or thematic element in art, fashion, music, or storytelling. Also used to describe the inspiration or source material for a creative work.
花(はな)のモチーフのドレスだ。
It's a dress with a floral motif.
この小説(しょうせつ)は実話(じつわ)をモチーフにしている。
This novel is based on a true story.
和風(わふう)のモチーフを取(と)り入(い)れたデザインが海外(かいがい)で人気(にんき)だ。
Designs incorporating Japanese-style motifs are popular overseas.

ETYMOLOGY:
From French motif, via English.

USAGE:
In Japanese, ～をモチーフにする means "to use ～ as inspiration/the basis for" a creative work. This is one of the most common patterns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～をモチーフにする: to use ～ as a motif/inspiration
  • 花(はな)のモチーフ: floral motif
  • 動物(どうぶつ)モチーフ: animal motif
  • モチーフにした作品(さくひん): a work inspired by ～
  • デザインモチーフ: design motif
