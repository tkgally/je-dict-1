1. tweet, social media post
A post on X (formerly Twitter) or, by extension, a short post on other social media platforms. Also used as a verb with する.
面白(おもしろ)い記事(きじ)をツイートした。
I tweeted an interesting article.
あのツイートがバズった。
That tweet went viral.
有名人(ゆうめいじん)のツイートが炎上(えんじょう)して大(おお)きな話題(わだい)になった。
A celebrity's tweet caused a firestorm and became a major topic of discussion.

USAGE:
Although the platform formerly known as Twitter was renamed to X, the term ツイート remains widely used in Japanese for posts on that platform. The verb form is ツイートする.

リツイート (retweet) means to share someone else's post. 引用(いんよう)ツイート (quote tweet) means to share with added commentary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ツイートする: to tweet, to post
  • リツイート: retweet
  • 引用(いんよう)ツイート: quote tweet
  • ツイートが炎上(えんじょう)する: a tweet catches fire (causes backlash)
  • ツイートを削除(さくじょ)する: to delete a tweet
