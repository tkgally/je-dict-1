不死身(ふじみ)

ふじみ
na-adjective / noun
invulnerable, indestructible
1. invulnerable, indestructible, unkillable
Having a body or constitution that seems impervious to injury, illness, or death. Often used figuratively for someone remarkably resilient or tough.
あの人(ひと)は不死身(ふじみ)だ。
That person is invincible.
不死身(ふじみ)の体(からだ)を持(も)っている。
He has an indestructible body.
何度(なんど)大(おお)けがをしても復帰(ふっき)するので、不死身(ふじみ)の男(おとこ)と呼(よ)ばれている。
He always comes back no matter how badly he's injured, so he's called the indestructible man.

USAGE:
Often used as praise or with a tone of amazement when someone recovers quickly from illness, injury, or setbacks. Also common in fiction for characters who cannot be killed.

WORD FORMATION:
不死(ふし) (undying) + 身(み) (body)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不死身(ふじみ)の体(からだ): indestructible body
  • 不死身(ふじみ)の男(おとこ): invincible man
  • まるで不死身(ふじみ)だ: as if invulnerable
