ソロ

そろ
noun
solo
1. solo, alone
Performing, playing, or doing something alone, without a partner or group. Used in music, travel, activities, and gaming contexts.
ソロで歌(うた)う。
To sing solo.
彼女(かのじょ)はソロ活動(かつどう)を始(はじ)めた。
She started solo activities.
初(はじ)めてソロキャンプに挑戦(ちょうせん)してみたら、意外(いがい)と楽(たの)しかった。
I tried solo camping for the first time and it was surprisingly fun.

USAGE:
ソロ is widely used as a modifier with の or as a prefix: ソロ活動(かつどう) (solo activities), ソロキャンプ (solo camping), ソロ旅行(りょこう) (solo travel). The word ソロ活(かつ) (solo activities as a lifestyle) has become a popular term describing people who enjoy doing things alone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ソロ活動(かつどう): solo activities (especially in music/entertainment)
  • ソロキャンプ: solo camping
  • ソロで演奏(えんそう)する: to perform solo
  • ソロデビュー: solo debut
