ピンチ

ぴんち
noun
pinch, crisis, tight spot
1. pinch, crisis, tight spot, predicament
A difficult or dangerous situation where things could go wrong. Often used in sports, games, and everyday life to describe being in trouble.
ピンチを切(き)り抜(ぬ)けた。
I got through the crisis.
チームは大(おお)ピンチを迎(むか)えている。
The team is facing a major crisis.
ピンチをチャンスに変(か)えるのが一流(いちりゅう)の選手(せんしゅ)だ。
Turning a crisis into an opportunity is what makes a top-class player.

USAGE:
Very common in sports commentary, video games, and everyday conversation. Often contrasted with チャンス (chance, opportunity). The phrase ピンチはチャンス (a crisis is an opportunity) is a well-known motivational saying.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ピンチを切(き)り抜(ぬ)ける (get through a crisis)
  • ピンチを迎(むか)える (face a crisis)
  • ピンチに陥(おちい)る (fall into a predicament)
  • ピンチに強(つよ)い (strong under pressure)
  • 大(おお)ピンチ (major crisis)
  • ピンチはチャンス (a crisis is an opportunity)

ETYMOLOGY:
From English "pinch" (in the sense of a tight spot or difficult situation).
