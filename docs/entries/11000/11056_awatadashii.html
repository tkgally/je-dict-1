慌(あわ)ただしい

あわただしい
i-adjective
hectic, busy, hurried
1. hectic, busy, hurried, frantic
Describes a situation, period, or atmosphere where there is a rush of activity and little time to spare, creating a feeling of restlessness.
慌(あわ)ただしい朝(あさ)だった。
It was a hectic morning.
年末(ねんまつ)は慌(あわ)ただしく過(す)ぎていく。
The end of the year passes by in a hectic blur.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)で毎日(まいにち)慌(あわ)ただしい日々(ひび)を送(おく)っている。
I've been spending hectic days preparing for the move.

CONJUGATION:

  • Negative: 慌(あわ)ただしくない
  • Past: 慌(あわ)ただしかった
  • Te-form: 慌(あわ)ただしくて
  • Adverbial: 慌(あわ)ただしく

USAGE:
Describes situations rather than people directly. You would say 慌(あわ)ただしい日々(ひび) (hectic days) rather than applying it to a person. Often used for seasonal or transitional periods such as year-end, moving, or event preparation.

WRITING:
Also written as 慌(あわ)しい in older texts, but 慌(あわ)ただしい is the standard modern form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 慌(あわ)ただしい毎日(まいにち): hectic every day
  • 慌(あわ)ただしく出(で)かける: leave in a hurry
  • 慌(あわ)ただしい雰囲気(ふんいき): hectic atmosphere
