ブース

ぶーす
noun
booth, stall
1. booth, stall, stand
A small enclosed or partitioned space for a specific purpose, such as a trade show display area, event stall, or partitioned workspace.
ブースを設置(せっち)する。
To set up a booth.
展示会(てんじかい)のブースで新商品(しんしょうひん)を紹介(しょうかい)した。
I introduced new products at the trade show booth.
コミケでは朝(あさ)から人気(にんき)サークルのブースに長(なが)い列(れつ)ができる。
At Comiket, long lines form at popular circles' booths from the morning.

USAGE:
ブース is used for partitioned or designated spaces at events, exhibitions, and offices. It is the standard word for trade show displays and event stalls.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ブースを出(だ)す: to have a booth (at an event)
  • ブースを設置(せっち)する: to set up a booth
  • 展示(てんじ)ブース: exhibition booth
  • 撮影(さつえい)ブース: photo booth
  • 電話(でんわ)ブース: phone booth
