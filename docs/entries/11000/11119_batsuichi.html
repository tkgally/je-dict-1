バツイチ

ばついち
noun
once-divorced
1. once-divorced, having one divorce
A colloquial term for a person who has been divorced once. Can be used as a noun or as a modifier with の.
彼(かれ)はバツイチだ。
He's been divorced once.
バツイチで子(こ)ども二人(ふたり)を育(そだ)てている。
She's divorced and raising two children.
最近(さいきん)はバツイチでも気(き)にしない人(ひと)が増(ふ)えてきた。
Recently, more people don't mind dating someone who's been divorced.

USAGE:
A casual, somewhat lighthearted term. Not typically used in formal or official contexts, where 離婚歴(りこんれき)がある is preferred. Similarly, バツニ (twice divorced) and バツサン (three times divorced) exist but are less common.

CULTURAL NOTE:
The バツ (×) refers to the × mark placed on a person's name in the family register (戸籍(こせき)) when a marriage is dissolved. イチ means "one," so バツイチ literally means "one ×."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • バツイチ子持(こも)ち (divorced with children)
  • バツイチ同士(どうし) (both divorced)
