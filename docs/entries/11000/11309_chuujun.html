中旬(ちゅうじゅん)

ちゅうじゅん
noun
middle of the month
1. middle of the month, mid-month
The middle ten days of a month, roughly the 11th through 20th. One of the three standard divisions of a month in Japanese.
3月(がつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん)に届(とど)く予定(よてい)です。
It's expected to arrive in mid-March.
桜(さくら)は4月(がつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん)に満開(まんかい)になる。
The cherry blossoms reach full bloom around mid-April.
来月(らいげつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん)までに報告書(ほうこくしょ)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the report by the middle of next month.

Japanese divides each month into three ten-day periods:

  • 上旬(じょうじゅん) (first ten days, roughly 1st-10th)
  • 中旬(ちゅうじゅん) (middle ten days, roughly 11th-20th)
  • 下旬(げじゅん) (last ten days, roughly 21st-end)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～月(がつ)中旬(ちゅうじゅん) (mid-[month])
  • 中旬(ちゅうじゅん)ごろ (around mid-month)
  • 中旬(ちゅうじゅん)から下旬(げじゅん)にかけて (from mid to late month)

This system is commonly used in weather forecasts, delivery estimates, event planning, and seasonal descriptions.
