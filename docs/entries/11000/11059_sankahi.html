参加費(さんかひ)

さんかひ
noun
participation fee; entry fee
1. participation fee, entry fee, registration fee
A fee charged for participating in an event, class, competition, or gathering.
参加費(さんかひ)は千円(せんえん)です。
The participation fee is 1,000 yen.
参加費(さんかひ)には昼食代(ちゅうしょくだい)が含(ふく)まれている。
The participation fee includes lunch.
マラソン大会(たいかい)の参加費(さんかひ)は当日(とうじつ)受付(うけつけ)で払(はら)ってください。
Please pay the marathon entry fee at the reception desk on the day.

参加費(さんかひ) is a compound of 参加(さんか) (participation) and 費(ひ) (cost/expense).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 参加費(さんかひ)を払(はら)う: to pay the participation fee
  • 参加費(さんかひ)が無料(むりょう): free of charge to participate
  • 参加費(さんかひ)込(こ)み: participation fee included

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 入場料(にゅうじょうりょう): admission fee (for entering a venue)
  • 受講料(じゅこうりょう): tuition fee (for classes/courses)
  • 会費(かいひ): membership fee, meeting fee
