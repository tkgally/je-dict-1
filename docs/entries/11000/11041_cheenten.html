チェーン店(てん)

ちぇーんてん
noun
chain store, franchise
1. chain store, franchise outlet
A retail store or restaurant that is part of a chain with multiple locations operating under the same brand. Includes convenience stores, fast food restaurants, and retail chains.
あのラーメン屋(や)はチェーン店(てん)だ。
That ramen shop is a chain store.
チェーン店(てん)より個人(こじん)経営(けいえい)の店(みせ)が好(す)きだ。
I prefer independently run shops over chain stores.
駅前(えきまえ)にはコンビニや飲食(いんしょく)のチェーン店(てん)が並(なら)んでいて便利(べんり)だ。
There are convenience stores and restaurant chains lined up in front of the station, which is convenient.

USAGE:
チェーン店(てん) can refer to any store or restaurant that is part of a chain. It is sometimes shortened to just チェーン: 「あの店(みせ)はチェーンだよ」 (That place is a chain). The word 個人(こじん)店(てん) or 個人(こじん)経営(けいえい) is the contrast for independent shops.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大手(おおて)チェーン店(てん): major chain store
  • チェーン展開(てんかい): chain expansion, rolling out a chain
  • 飲食(いんしょく)チェーン: restaurant chain
  • チェーン店(てん)の店長(てんちょう): chain store manager
