プラチナ

ぷらちな
noun
platinum
1. platinum
The precious metal platinum, used in jewelry, industrial applications, and as an investment asset. In Japanese, also widely used figuratively to describe the highest-tier or most exclusive category of products, services, and membership levels.
プラチナの指輪(ゆびわ)をプレゼントした。
I gave a platinum ring as a present.
プラチナ会員(かいいん)になると特別(とくべつ)な特典(とくてん)がもらえる。
If you become a platinum member, you can receive special benefits.
あのコンサートのチケットはプラチナチケットで、入手(にゅうしゅ)するのが非常(ひじょう)に難(むずか)しい。
Tickets for that concert are platinum tickets — extremely difficult to get.

USAGE: プラチナ is a loanword from Spanish/Portuguese "platina." It refers both to the metal and, figuratively, to the most exclusive or hard-to-get category.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プラチナ会員(かいいん) (platinum member)
  • プラチナカード (platinum credit card)
  • プラチナチケット (platinum ticket — a ticket that is extremely hard to obtain)
  • プラチナ世代(せだい) (platinum generation — active senior citizens)
  • 白金(はっきん) (platinum — the formal/scientific term, less common in everyday use)

CULTURAL NOTE: In Japan, プラチナ is used as the top tier in loyalty programs and credit cards (above ゴールド/gold), and プラチナチケット has become a common expression for any highly sought-after, near-impossible-to-get ticket.
