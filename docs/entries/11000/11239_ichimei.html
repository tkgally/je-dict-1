一命(いちめい)

いちめい
noun
one's life
1. one's life
A person's life, especially in contexts where life is at stake or has been saved. A literary and formal word used in dramatic or serious situations.
一命(いちめい)を取(と)り留(と)めた。
He barely survived.
医師(いし)の迅速(じんそく)な対応(たいおう)で一命(いちめい)を取(と)り留(と)めた。
Thanks to the doctor's swift response, the patient's life was saved.
事故(じこ)は大(おお)きかったが、乗客(じょうきゃく)全員(ぜんいん)が一命(いちめい)を取(と)り留(と)めたのは奇跡(きせき)だ。
The accident was severe, but it was a miracle that all passengers survived.

USAGE:
Almost always appears in the fixed phrase 一命(いちめい)を取(と)り留(と)める (to survive, to narrowly escape death). This is the most common and natural collocation. The word carries a literary, dramatic weight and is typical of news reports and formal writing.

Other patterns exist but are less common:

  • 一命(いちめい)を賭(と)す: to stake one's life
  • 一命(いちめい)を落(お)とす: to lose one's life
  • 一命(いちめい)を捧(ささ)げる: to devote one's life

REGISTER:
Formal and literary. In everyday speech, 命(いのち) is the standard word for "life."
