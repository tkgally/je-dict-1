深刻化(しんこくか)

しんこくか
noun, verb (suru)
worsening, becoming serious
1. worsening, becoming serious/grave
The process of a problem or situation becoming increasingly serious, severe, or difficult to resolve.
人手不足(ひとでぶそく)が深刻化(しんこくか)している。
The labor shortage is becoming more serious.
環境問題(かんきょうもんだい)の深刻化(しんこくか)を食(く)い止(と)めなければならない。
We must stop environmental problems from getting worse.
少子高齢化(しょうしこうれいか)の深刻化(しんこくか)により、社会保障(しゃかいほしょう)制度(せいど)の見直(みなお)しが求(もと)められている。
Due to the worsening of the declining birthrate and aging population, a review of the social security system is being called for.

WORD FORMATION:
深刻(しんこく) (serious, grave) + 化(か) (transformation suffix) = becoming serious/worsening. The 〜化(か) suffix turns adjectives and nouns into verbs/nouns meaning "becoming ~."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 深刻化(しんこくか)する (to worsen, to become more serious)
  • 深刻化(しんこくか)が進(すす)む (the worsening progresses)
  • 深刻化(しんこくか)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent worsening)

SIMILAR 〜化(か) WORDS:

  • 長期化(ちょうきか) (prolongation, becoming prolonged)
  • 悪化(あっか) (deterioration — more general than 深刻化(しんこくか))
  • 複雑化(ふくざつか) (becoming more complex)
