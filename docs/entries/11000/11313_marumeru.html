丸(まる)める

まるめる
verb (ichidan)
to roll up, to crumple
1. to roll up, to crumple, to ball up
To make something round or ball-shaped by rolling, crumpling, or curling it.
紙(かみ)を丸(まる)めて捨(す)てた。
I crumpled up the paper and threw it away.
猫(ねこ)が体(からだ)を丸(まる)めて寝(ね)ている。
The cat is sleeping curled up in a ball.
粘土(ねんど)を手(て)で丸(まる)めて、小(ちい)さな玉(たま)を作(つく)った。
I rolled the clay with my hands and made small balls.
2. to smooth over, to talk someone into agreement
To persuade or manipulate someone through smooth talk, or to settle a matter by glossing over details.
うまく丸(まる)め込(こ)まれた。
I was smooth-talked into it.
彼(かれ)は口(くち)がうまくて、いつも相手(あいて)を丸(まる)める。
He's a smooth talker and always talks people around.
営業(えいぎょう)マンに丸(まる)め込(こ)まれて、高(たか)いものを買(か)ってしまった。
The salesperson talked me into buying something expensive.

丸(まる)める has both a physical and figurative meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (physical):

  • 紙(かみ)を丸(まる)める (crumple paper)
  • 体(からだ)を丸(まる)める (curl up one's body)
  • 背中(せなか)を丸(まる)める (hunch one's back)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (figurative):

  • 丸(まる)め込(こ)む (talk someone into something, sweet-talk)
  • うまく丸(まる)める (smooth things over)

The compound 丸(まる)め込(こ)む is very common for the figurative meaning and has a slightly manipulative nuance.
