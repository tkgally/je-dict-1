云々(うんぬん)

うんぬん
noun
and so on, and so forth
1. and so on, and so forth, et cetera
Used to indicate that the speaker is omitting or abbreviating the rest of what was said. Similar to "blah blah" or "such and such" in English.
彼(かれ)は「忙(いそが)しくて云々(うんぬん)」と言(い)っていた。
He was saying something about being busy and so on.
理由(りゆう)は云々(うんぬん)かんぬんあるが、結果(けっか)が大事(だいじ)だ。
There are all sorts of reasons, but the result is what matters.
2. to discuss, to comment on
Used with する to mean discussing or making comments about something, often critically. Frequently appears in the pattern ～を云々する.
他人(たにん)の生(い)き方(かた)を云々(うんぬん)するべきではない。
One should not criticize how other people live.
味(あじ)を云々(うんぬん)する前(まえ)に、まず食(た)べてみてほしい。
Before commenting on the taste, I'd like you to try it first.

USAGE:
In sense 1, 云々(うんぬん) replaces omitted content, like ellipsis in speech. It is often paired with かんぬん in the expression 云々(うんぬん)かんぬん for emphasis ("this and that and whatnot").

In sense 2, 云々(うんぬん)する means to discuss, comment on, or criticize, often implying the speaker thinks such commentary is unwelcome.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～と云々(うんぬん): ...and so on
  • 云々(うんぬん)かんぬん: this and that, blah blah
  • ～を云々(うんぬん)する: to comment on / criticize
