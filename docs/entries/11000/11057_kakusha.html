各社(かくしゃ)

かくしゃ
noun
each company; all companies
1. each company, every company, all the companies
Refers to each of the companies in a group or industry. Commonly used in news and business contexts.
各社(かくしゃ)の代表(だいひょう)が集(あつ)まった。
Representatives from each company gathered.
各社(かくしゃ)が新(あたら)しい製品(せいひん)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
Each company announced a new product.
携帯(けいたい)電話(でんわ)の料金(りょうきん)は各社(かくしゃ)によって違(ちが)うので、よく比(くら)べてから選(えら)んだほうがいい。
Mobile phone rates differ by company, so it's better to compare carefully before choosing.

各社(かくしゃ) is a compound of 各(かく) (each) and 社(しゃ) (company). It is frequently used in news, business reports, and formal writing.

SIMILAR PATTERNS WITH 各(かく):

  • 各国(かっこく): each country
  • 各地(かくち): each region/area
  • 各自(かくじ): each person, individually
  • 各種(かくしゅ): each kind, various kinds
