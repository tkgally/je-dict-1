ソーダ

そーだ
noun
soda, carbonated water
1. soda, carbonated water
Carbonated water or a carbonated soft drink. In Japanese, ソーダ usually refers to plain carbonated water or a simple carbonated flavored drink, rather than to soft drinks in general.
メロンソーダを頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered a melon soda.
ソーダ割(わ)りでお願(ねが)いします。
With soda water, please.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)にはクリームソーダが飲(の)みたくなる。
On hot days, I crave a cream soda float.

USAGE:
ソーダ most commonly appears in compound words for specific drinks. メロンソーダ (bright green melon-flavored soda) is an iconic Japanese café drink. クリームソーダ refers to a soda float topped with ice cream, not cream-flavored soda.

ソーダ割(わ)り means mixing an alcoholic drink with soda water (e.g., ウイスキーのソーダ割(わ)り = whisky and soda / highball).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メロンソーダ: melon soda
  • クリームソーダ: soda float with ice cream
  • ソーダ割(わ)り: mixed with soda water
  • ソーダ水(すい): soda water
