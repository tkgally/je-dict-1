ヨモギ

よもぎ
noun
mugwort
1. mugwort, Japanese mugwort
An aromatic herb (Artemisia indica) that grows wild throughout Japan. Used in cooking, traditional medicine, and cultural practices.
ヨモギ餅(もち)を食(た)べた。
I ate mugwort rice cake.
春(はる)になるとヨモギが道端(みちばた)に生(は)えてくる。
When spring comes, mugwort grows along the roadsides.
祖母(そぼ)は摘(つ)みたてのヨモギで草餅(くさもち)を作(つく)ってくれた。
My grandmother made grass mochi for me with freshly picked mugwort.

USAGE:
A versatile plant deeply connected to Japanese food culture. Most commonly used in 草餅(くさもち) (grass mochi) and ヨモギ餅(もち) (mugwort mochi), which have a distinctive green color and herbal aroma. Also used in 天(てん)ぷら, tea, and traditional moxibustion therapy (灸(きゅう)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ヨモギ餅(もち) (mugwort mochi)
  • 草餅(くさもち) (grass mochi — made with mugwort)
  • ヨモギ茶(ちゃ) (mugwort tea)
  • ヨモギを摘(つ)む (pick mugwort)
  • ヨモギ蒸(む)し (mugwort steam — a spa treatment)

WRITING:
Usually written in katakana. The kanji 蓬(よもぎ) exists but is uncommon in everyday use.
