ビジネス

びじねす
noun
business
1. business, commerce, work
Commercial activity, professional work, or the business world in general. Used very broadly in Japanese as a modifier for work-related contexts.
ビジネスで大阪(おおさか)に行(い)く。
I'm going to Osaka on business.
ビジネスマナーを身(み)につけることは大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to learn business etiquette.
新(あたら)しいビジネスモデルを考(かんが)えて、起業(きぎょう)する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
More people are thinking up new business models and starting companies.

USAGE:
ビジネス is extremely productive as a compound modifier in Japanese. It attaches to many words to create business-context terms that may not have direct English equivalents.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ビジネスマナー: business etiquette
  • ビジネスメール: business email
  • ビジネス敬語(けいご): business-level polite language
  • ビジネスモデル: business model
  • ビジネスパートナー: business partner
  • ビジネスホテル: business hotel (budget hotel for business travelers)
  • ビジネスクラス: business class (on a flight)
  • ビジネスマン: businessman (note: ビジネスパーソン is increasingly used as a gender-neutral alternative)
