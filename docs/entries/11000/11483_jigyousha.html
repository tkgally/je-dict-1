事業者(じぎょうしゃ)

じぎょうしゃ
noun
business operator
1. business operator, business proprietor
A person or organization that runs a business. Commonly used in legal and regulatory contexts to refer to any entity conducting commercial activity.
事業者(じぎょうしゃ)は法律(ほうりつ)を守(まも)る義務(ぎむ)がある。
Business operators have an obligation to follow the law.
中小(ちゅうしょう)事業者(じぎょうしゃ)への支援(しえん)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
Support for small and medium-sized businesses was announced.
通信(つうしん)事業者(じぎょうしゃ)はサービスの品質(ひんしつ)を維持(いじ)しなければならない。
Telecommunications operators must maintain the quality of their services.

USAGE:
事業者(じぎょうしゃ) is a formal term frequently appearing in laws, regulations, and official documents. It covers both individuals and corporations conducting business.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 中小(ちゅうしょう)事業者(じぎょうしゃ): small and medium-sized businesses
  • 通信(つうしん)事業者(じぎょうしゃ): telecommunications operator
  • 個人(こじん)事業者(じぎょうしゃ): sole proprietor
  • 飲食(いんしょく)事業者(じぎょうしゃ): food service operator
  • 事業者(じぎょうしゃ)向(む)け: aimed at businesses (B2B)
