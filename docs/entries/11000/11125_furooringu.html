フローリング

ふろーりんぐ
noun
hardwood flooring, laminate flooring
1. hardwood flooring, laminate flooring
Wooden or wood-style flooring, as opposed to tatami or carpet. A common flooring choice in modern Japanese apartments and houses.
この部屋(へや)はフローリングだ。
This room has hardwood flooring.
畳(たたみ)をフローリングに張(は)り替(か)えた。
I replaced the tatami with hardwood flooring.
フローリングの床(ゆか)はこまめに掃除(そうじ)しないとホコリが目立(めだ)つ。
If you don't clean hardwood floors frequently, the dust becomes noticeable.

USAGE:
In Japanese real estate listings, フローリング is one of the most common floor types alongside 畳(たたみ) (tatami) and カーペット (carpet). Many modern Japanese apartments feature フローリング in living areas and bedrooms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フローリングの床(ゆか) (hardwood floor)
  • フローリングに張(は)り替(か)える (replace with hardwood flooring)
  • フローリング用(よう)ワックス (floor wax for hardwood)
  • フローリングモップ (floor mop for hardwood)

ETYMOLOGY:
From English "flooring." In Japanese, this term specifically refers to wood or wood-style flooring, not flooring in general.
