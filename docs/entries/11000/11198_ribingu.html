リビング

りびんぐ
noun
living room
1. living room
The main common room in a home where the family gathers, relaxes, and socializes. Short for リビングルーム (living room).
リビングでテレビを見(み)る。
Watch TV in the living room.
新(あたら)しいソファをリビングに置(お)いた。
I put a new sofa in the living room.
家族(かぞく)みんながリビングに集(あつ)まって、夕飯(ゆうはん)を食(た)べながら会話(かいわ)を楽(たの)しんだ。
The whole family gathered in the living room, enjoying conversation over dinner.

リビング is a clipped form of リビングルーム (living room). It is the standard term in real estate listings and interior design.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リビングルーム (living room — full form)
  • リビングダイニング (combined living-dining room, often abbreviated LDK)
  • リビングに集(あつ)まる (gather in the living room)
  • 広(ひろ)いリビング (spacious living room)
  • リビング収納(しゅうのう) (living room storage)

USAGE NOTES:

  • In Japanese real estate, LDK stands for Living-Dining-Kitchen, a common layout designation (e.g., 2LDK = 2 bedrooms + living-dining-kitchen)
  • 居間(いま) is the traditional Japanese word for living room but リビング has become more common in modern homes
  • The concept of リビング reflects the shift from traditional 和室(わしつ) (Japanese-style rooms) to Western-style common spaces
