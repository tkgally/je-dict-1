ランチ

らんち
noun
lunch
1. lunch, midday meal
The midday meal, or a lunch set or lunch menu offered at a restaurant. In Japanese, ランチ often has a slightly upscale or restaurant-oriented nuance compared to the native お昼(ひる)ご飯(はん).
ランチに行(い)こう。
Let's go for lunch.
このカフェのランチセットは千円(せんえん)でお得(とく)だ。
The lunch set at this cafe is a good deal at 1,000 yen.
週末(しゅうまつ)に友達(ともだち)とおしゃれなレストランでランチを楽(たの)しんだ。
I enjoyed lunch with friends at a stylish restaurant over the weekend.

ランチ is a loanword from English "lunch." While お昼(ひる)ご飯(はん) is the everyday word for lunch, ランチ is often used in restaurant and social dining contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ランチセット (lunch set meal)
  • ランチタイム (lunch time, lunch hours)
  • ランチメニュー (lunch menu)
  • ランチ会(かい) (lunch gathering)
  • ランチに行(い)く (go out for lunch)
  • ランチを食(た)べる (eat lunch)

USAGE NOTES:

  • ランチ tends to suggest eating at a restaurant or cafe, while お昼(ひる) or お昼(ひる)ご飯(はん) is more general
  • Many restaurants offer special ランチ prices that are cheaper than dinner
  • ランチ営業(えいぎょう) means a restaurant's lunch service hours
  • ママランチ ("mama lunch") refers to mothers getting together for a lunch outing
