両立(りょうりつ)

りょうりつ
noun / verb (suru)
balancing, compatibility
1. balancing two things, compatibility, coexistence
Successfully maintaining or achieving two things at the same time, especially when they seem difficult to combine. Commonly used for work-life balance, studies and hobbies, career and family.
仕事(しごと)と育児(いくじ)を両立(りょうりつ)させる。
Balance work and child-rearing.
勉強(べんきょう)と部活(ぶかつ)の両立(りょうりつ)は大変(たいへん)だ。
Balancing studies and club activities is tough.
経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)と環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)の両立(りょうりつ)は現代(げんだい)社会(しゃかい)の大(おお)きな課題(かだい)だ。
Balancing economic growth and environmental protection is a major challenge for modern society.

両立(りょうりつ) implies successfully managing two things that are typically hard to combine.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • AとBを両立(りょうりつ)させる (balance A and B)
  • AとBの両立(りょうりつ) (the coexistence of A and B)
  • 両立(りょうりつ)が難(むずか)しい (hard to balance both)
  • 両立(りょうりつ)できる (can balance both)

FREQUENT PAIRS:

  • 仕事(しごと)と家庭(かてい) (work and family)
  • 仕事(しごと)と育児(いくじ) (work and child-rearing)
  • 勉強(べんきょう)と部活(ぶかつ) (studies and club activities)
  • 経済(けいざい)と環境(かんきょう) (economy and environment)
