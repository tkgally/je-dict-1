リピーター

りぴーたー
noun
repeat customer, regular visitor
1. repeat customer, regular visitor
A person who returns to the same shop, restaurant, tourist destination, or service multiple times. Used in business and tourism contexts.
リピーターが多(おお)い店(みせ)だ。
It's a shop with many repeat customers.
リピーターを増(ふ)やすことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to increase repeat customers.
沖縄(おきなわ)は何度(なんど)も訪(おとず)れるリピーターが多(おお)い観光地(かんこうち)だ。
Okinawa is a tourist destination with many repeat visitors.

リピーター is from English "repeater" but in Japanese is used specifically to mean a repeat customer or return visitor. This usage does not exist in English.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リピーターを増(ふ)やす: to increase repeat customers
  • リピーターが多(おお)い: to have many repeat customers
  • リピーター率(りつ): repeat customer rate
  • リピーターを獲得(かくとく)する: to acquire repeat customers

NOTE:
The related verb リピートする means to repeat a purchase or visit. リピ買(が)い (repeat buying) is also common in casual contexts.
