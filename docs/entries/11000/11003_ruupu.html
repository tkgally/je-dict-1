1. loop, cycle, repetition
A repeated sequence or circular process. Used in computing, music, and general contexts for things that repeat in a cycle.
無限(むげん)ループにはまってしまった。
I got stuck in an infinite loop.
この曲(きょく)をループで聴(き)いている。
I'm listening to this song on loop.
同(おな)じ失敗(しっぱい)をループしないように気(き)をつけている。
I'm being careful not to loop through the same mistakes.

ループ is a loanword from English "loop," referring to a repeating cycle or circular process.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 無限(むげん)ループ: infinite loop
  • ループする: to loop, to repeat in a cycle
  • ループ再生(さいせい): loop playback
  • タイムループ: time loop (in fiction)
  • ループもの: a story with a time-loop plot

USAGE BY DOMAIN:

  • Computing: a structure that repeats instructions (無限(むげん)ループ = infinite loop, a common bug)
  • Music: playing a track or section on repeat (ループ再生(さいせい))
  • Everyday: any situation stuck in repetition (同(おな)じことのループ = same thing repeating)

As a する verb, ループする means "to loop" or "to keep repeating": 同(おな)じ失敗(しっぱい)をループする (to keep repeating the same mistake).
