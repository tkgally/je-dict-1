膨張(ぼうちょう)

ぼうちょう
noun, verb (suru)
expansion, swelling, inflation
1. expansion, swelling, inflation
The process of something increasing in size, volume, or scale. Used for physical expansion (of gases, materials) and figurative growth (of budgets, organizations, problems).
金属(きんぞく)は熱(ねつ)で膨張(ぼうちょう)する。
Metal expands with heat.
予算(よさん)が膨張(ぼうちょう)して問題(もんだい)になっている。
The budget has ballooned and become a problem.
宇宙(うちゅう)は今(いま)も膨張(ぼうちょう)を続(つづ)けていると言(い)われている。
It is said that the universe is still continuing to expand.

WRITING:
Also written as 膨脹(ぼうちょう) with the older kanji 脹(ちょう). The standard modern writing uses 張(ちょう).

USAGE:
Used in both scientific/technical contexts (thermal expansion, cosmic expansion) and figurative contexts (budget inflation, organizational bloat). The figurative use often carries a negative nuance of uncontrolled growth.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 拡大(かくだい): enlargement, expansion (more general, often intentional)
  • 膨張(ぼうちょう): swelling, inflation (often implies uncontrolled or natural expansion)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 熱(ねつ)膨張(ぼうちょう): thermal expansion
  • 宇宙(うちゅう)の膨張(ぼうちょう): expansion of the universe
  • 財政(ざいせい)の膨張(ぼうちょう): fiscal expansion
  • 膨張(ぼうちょう)率(りつ): expansion rate
