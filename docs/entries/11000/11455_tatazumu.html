佇(たたず)む

たたずむ
verb (godan)
to stand still, to linger
1. to stand still, to linger, to stand quietly in one place
To stand motionless in a place, often with a sense of contemplation, solitude, or quiet presence. A literary and evocative word.
窓辺(まどべ)に佇(たたず)む。
To stand quietly by the window.
彼女(かのじょ)は雨(あめ)の中(なか)で佇(たたず)んでいた。
She was standing still in the rain.
古(ふる)いお寺(てら)の前(まえ)に佇(たたず)んで、静(しず)かな時間(じかん)を過(す)ごした。
I stood quietly in front of the old temple and spent some peaceful time.

CONJUGATION: 佇(たたず)む is a godan verb (む row). Te-form: 佇(たたず)んで.

USAGE: A literary, somewhat poetic word that evokes a quiet, still image. More expressive than simply 立(た)つ (to stand). Often used in novels, poetry, and descriptive writing to create atmosphere.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 窓辺(まどべ)に佇(たたず)む (to stand by the window)
  • 佇(たたず)まい (appearance, bearing, atmosphere of a place)

RELATED: The noun 佇(たたず)まい means the overall appearance, atmosphere, or bearing of a place or person, and is very commonly used.
