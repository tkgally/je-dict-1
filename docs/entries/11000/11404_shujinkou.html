主人公(しゅじんこう)

しゅじんこう
noun
protagonist, main character
1. protagonist, main character
The central character in a story, novel, film, game, or other narrative. Can also refer figuratively to the central person in a real-life situation.
この物語(ものがたり)の主人公(しゅじんこう)は少女(しょうじょ)だ。
The protagonist of this story is a girl.
主人公(しゅじんこう)に感情移入(かんじょういにゅう)してしまった。
I ended up emotionally identifying with the main character.
あの映画(えいが)の主人公(しゅじんこう)は最後(さいご)に大(おお)きな決断(けつだん)をする。
The protagonist of that film makes a big decision at the end.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 主人公(しゅじんこう)を演(えん)じる (to play the lead role)
  • 物語(ものがたり)の主人公(しゅじんこう) (the protagonist of a story)
  • 主人公(しゅじんこう)に感情移入(かんじょういにゅう)する (to identify with the main character)

USAGE: 主人公(しゅじんこう) is the standard literary and conversational term for the lead character of any narrative. It is used for novels, manga, anime, films, and games alike. In figurative use, 人生(じんせい)の主人公(しゅじんこう) means "the protagonist of one's own life."
