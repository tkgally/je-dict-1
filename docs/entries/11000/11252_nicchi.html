ニッチ

にっち
adjective (na), noun
niche, specialized
1. niche, specialized, narrow market
A small, specialized segment of a market or area of interest that caters to a specific group. Also used as a na-adjective meaning highly specialized or appealing to a narrow audience.
ニッチな趣味(しゅみ)を持(も)っている。
I have a niche hobby.
ニッチな市場(しじょう)を狙(ねら)った商品(しょうひん)だ。
This is a product targeting a niche market.
大手(おおて)が参入(さんにゅう)しないニッチな分野(ぶんや)で成功(せいこう)した企業(きぎょう)もある。
There are companies that have succeeded in niche fields that major firms don't enter.

USAGE:
Commonly used in business and marketing to describe a small, specialized market segment. In casual conversation, ニッチな〜 describes something with a narrow, specialized appeal — a hobby, interest, or topic that few people know about.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ニッチな市場(しじょう): niche market
  • ニッチな分野(ぶんや): niche field
  • ニッチな趣味(しゅみ): niche hobby
  • ニッチ産業(さんぎょう): niche industry
  • ニッチ戦略(せんりゃく): niche strategy
