プレミアム

ぷれみあむ
noun, adjective-na
premium, deluxe
1. premium, deluxe, high-end
Something of superior quality or higher value than the standard version. Widely used in marketing and product branding to indicate a luxury or upgraded option.
プレミアムな体験(たいけん)ができる。
You can have a premium experience.
プレミアム会員(かいいん)になると特典(とくてん)がつく。
If you become a premium member, you get special benefits.
限定(げんてい)のプレミアムモデルは通常(つうじょう)版(ばん)より倍(ばい)の値段(ねだん)がする。
The limited premium model costs twice as much as the regular version.

USAGE:
プレミアム is one of the most common marketing buzzwords in Japan. It is used as both a noun (プレミアムがつく = a premium is added) and a na-adjective modifier (プレミアムな = premium/luxurious). プレミアムフライデー was a government initiative (since 2017) encouraging early departure from work on the last Friday of each month.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • プレミアム会員(かいいん): premium member
  • プレミアムモデル: premium model
  • プレミアム商品(しょうひん): premium product
  • プレミアムがつく: a premium is attached (price goes above face value)
  • プレミアムフライデー: Premium Friday (government campaign)

SIMILAR WORDS:
高級(こうきゅう) (high-class/luxury) is the native equivalent. プレミアム sounds more modern and marketing-oriented.
