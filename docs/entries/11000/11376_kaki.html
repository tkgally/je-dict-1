下記(かき)

かき
noun / pre-noun adjectival
below-mentioned, the following
1. below-mentioned, the following
Refers to something written or listed further down in a document or text. A formal written expression used in business correspondence, official documents, and instructions.
下記(かき)をご参照(さんしょう)ください。
Please refer to the following.
下記(かき)の日程(にってい)でご都合(つごう)はいかがですか。
Would the following dates work for you?
お申(もう)し込(こ)みは下記(かき)の連絡先(れんらくさき)までお願(ねが)いいたします。
Please direct your applications to the contact information below.

USAGE: 下記(かき) is a formal written expression. Like 上記(じょうき), it is almost exclusively used in writing. In speech, people say 下(した)に書(か)いてある or 以下(いか)の instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下記(かき)の通(とお)り (as follows)
  • 下記(かき)の住所(じゅうしょ) (the address below)
  • 下記(かき)の連絡先(れんらくさき) (the contact information below)
  • 下記(かき)をご覧(らん)ください (please see below)

PAIR: The opposite is 上記(じょうき) (above-mentioned).
