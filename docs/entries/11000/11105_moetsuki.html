燃(も)え尽(つ)き

もえつき
noun
burnout, being burned out
1. burnout, being burned out
A state of physical and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress or overwork, leaving a person feeling drained and unable to function effectively.
燃(も)え尽(つ)き症候群(しょうこうぐん)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful of burnout syndrome.
仕事(しごと)を頑張(がんば)りすぎて燃(も)え尽(つ)きてしまった。
I overworked myself and burned out.
燃(も)え尽(つ)きを防(ふせ)ぐには、適度(てきど)な休息(きゅうそく)が欠(か)かせない。
Adequate rest is essential to prevent burnout.

USAGE:
燃(も)え尽(つ)き is the noun form of the verb 燃(も)え尽(つ)きる (to burn out completely). While the verb can describe literal burning, the noun 燃(も)え尽(つ)き is almost always used in the psychological/medical sense of burnout.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 燃(も)え尽(つ)き症候群(しょうこうぐん) (burnout syndrome)
  • 燃(も)え尽(つ)きを防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent burnout)
  • 燃(も)え尽(つ)き状態(じょうたい) (state of burnout)

VERB FORM:
燃(も)え尽(つ)きる means "to burn out" or "to be completely exhausted." The phrase 真(ま)っ白(しろ)に燃(も)え尽(つ)きた (burned out pure white) is a famous line from the manga and anime あしたのジョー.
