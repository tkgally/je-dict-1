スムーズ

すむーず
adjective (na)
smooth, smoothly
1. smooth, seamless, without problems
Proceeding without difficulty, delay, or obstruction. Describes processes, operations, or interactions that go well.
手続(てつづ)きがスムーズに進(すす)んだ。
The procedure went smoothly.
スムーズな対応(たいおう)ありがとうございます。
Thank you for the smooth handling.
事前(じぜん)に準備(じゅんび)しておいたおかげで、当日(とうじつ)の進行(しんこう)はとてもスムーズだった。
Thanks to preparing in advance, the event ran very smoothly on the day.

GRAMMAR:
Na-adjective. Used with に as an adverb: スムーズに進(すす)む (to go smoothly). Used with な before nouns: スムーズな対応(たいおう) (smooth response).

USAGE:
スムーズ is used for processes, procedures, conversations, transitions, and traffic flow. It does not describe physical texture (for that, なめらか or すべすべ would be used).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • スムーズに進(すす)む: to go smoothly
  • スムーズな対応(たいおう): smooth handling/response
  • スムーズに進行(しんこう)する: to proceed smoothly
  • スムーズな流(なが)れ: smooth flow
  • スムーズに移行(いこう)する: to transition smoothly
