空(あ)く

あく
verb
to become vacant; to become free; to become empty
1. to become vacant, to become free, to become available
For a space, seat, time slot, or person to become available or unoccupied.
席(せき)が空(あ)いた。
A seat became available.
来週(らいしゅう)の月曜日(げつようび)は空(あ)いていますか。
Are you free next Monday?
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)が空(あ)くまで待(ま)った。
I waited until a parking space became available.
2. to become empty, to be emptied
For a container, stomach, or space to become empty.
お腹(なか)が空(あ)いた。
I'm hungry. (Lit: My stomach has become empty.)
ペットボトルが空(あ)いたら、リサイクルに出(だ)してください。
When the plastic bottle is empty, please put it out for recycling.

空(あ)く is an intransitive godan verb meaning to become vacant or empty.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 空(あ)ける (transitive, to vacate/empty)
  • Pattern: Xが空(あ)く

ASPECT (ている):

  • 空(あ)いている means 'is available/vacant/empty' (resulting state)
  • Example: 席(せき)が空(あ)いている = The seat is available
  • Example: お腹(なか)が空(あ)いている = I'm hungry

HOMOPHONES:

  • 開(あ)く: to open (a door, lid, etc.)
  • 明(あ)く: to dawn, to end (a period)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 席(せき)が空(あ)く: a seat becomes available
  • 部屋(へや)が空(あ)く: a room becomes available
  • お腹(なか)が空(あ)く: to get hungry
  • 手(て)が空(あ)く: to have free hands / to be available

Related Words

Homophone: 開(あ)く (to open)
