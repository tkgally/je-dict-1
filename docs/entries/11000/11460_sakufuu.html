作風(さくふう)

さくふう
noun
artistic style, creative style
1. artistic style, creative style, style of work
The distinctive manner or approach characteristic of a particular artist, writer, filmmaker, or other creative person. Refers to the consistent qualities that define their body of work.
独特(どくとく)な作風(さくふう)で知(し)られている。
Known for a distinctive artistic style.
彼(かれ)の作風(さくふう)は年(とし)とともに変(か)わってきた。
His creative style has changed over the years.
この映画(えいが)監督(かんとく)の作風(さくふう)は暗(くら)くて重(おも)いテーマが多(おお)い。
This film director's style tends toward dark and heavy themes.

COMPOSITION: 作(さく) (work, creation) + 風(ふう) (style, manner). The suffix 風(ふう) appears in many style-related words: 画風(がふう) (painting style), 芸風(げいふう) (performance style), 社風(しゃふう) (company culture).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独特(どくとく)な作風(さくふう) (distinctive style)
  • 作風(さくふう)が変(か)わる (style changes)
  • ～的(てき)な作風(さくふう) (~-style creative approach)
  • 作風(さくふう)を確立(かくりつ)する (to establish one's style)
