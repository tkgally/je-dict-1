ゾーン

ぞーん
noun
zone, area
1. zone, area, section
A designated area or section, whether physical (a restricted zone, a parking zone) or abstract (a price range, a mental state of peak focus).
ここは禁煙(きんえん)ゾーンです。
This is a no-smoking zone.
危険(きけん)ゾーンには入(はい)らないでください。
Please do not enter the danger zone.
選手(せんしゅ)がゾーンに入(はい)ったように集中(しゅうちゅう)して試合(しあい)を支配(しはい)した。
The player dominated the match as if entering the zone of total concentration.

USAGE:
ゾーン is commonly used as a suffix to designate areas: 禁煙(きんえん)ゾーン (no-smoking zone), フリーゾーン (free zone), セーフティーゾーン (safety zone).

In sports and psychology, ゾーンに入(はい)る (to enter the zone) describes a state of heightened focus and peak performance, similar to English "being in the zone."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 禁煙(きんえん)ゾーン: no-smoking zone
  • 危険(きけん)ゾーン: danger zone
  • ゾーンに入(はい)る: to get in the zone
  • 価格(かかく)ゾーン: price range
