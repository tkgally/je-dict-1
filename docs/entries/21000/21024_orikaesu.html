1. to fold back; to turn up
To fold something back on itself, such as a hem or edge of fabric or paper.
裾(すそ)を折(お)り返(かえ)す。
To fold back the hem.
ズボンの裾(すそ)を少(すこ)し折(お)り返(かえ)して履(は)いている。
I'm wearing my pants with the hems rolled up a little.
紙(かみ)の端(はし)を折(お)り返(かえ)して目印(めじるし)にした。
I folded back the edge of the paper as a bookmark.
2. to turn back; to retrace one's steps
To reverse direction and go back the way one came.
頂上(ちょうじょう)で折(お)り返(かえ)す。
To turn back at the summit.
マラソンの折(お)り返(かえ)し地点(ちてん)を過(す)ぎた。
I passed the marathon's turnaround point.
途中(とちゅう)で雨(あめ)が降(ふ)り出(だ)したので折(お)り返(かえ)した。
It started raining on the way, so I turned back.
3. to call back; to reply promptly
To return a phone call or respond to a message. Very common in business contexts.
折(お)り返(かえ)しお電話(でんわ)します。
I'll call you back.
担当者(たんとうしゃ)から折(お)り返(かえ)し連絡(れんらく)いたします。
The person in charge will get back to you.
留守番(るすばん)電話(でんわ)にメッセージが入(はい)っていたので、折(お)り返(かえ)し電話(でんわ)をかけた。
There was a message on the answering machine, so I called back.

A compound verb from 折(お)る (to fold) + 返(かえ)す (to return). The physical meaning of folding back extends metaphorically to turning around and to returning communication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 折(お)り返(かえ)し電話(でんわ)する: to call back
  • 折(お)り返(かえ)し連絡(れんらく)する: to get back in touch
  • 折(お)り返(かえ)し地点(ちてん): turnaround point
  • 裾(すそ)を折(お)り返(かえ)す: to fold back the hem

USAGE:
The adverbial form 折(お)り返(かえ)し (immediately, in return) is extremely common in business Japanese, especially in phone conversations: 折(お)り返(かえ)しお電話(でんわ)します (I'll call you right back). This is one of the most important business phrases for learners to know.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive in sense 1 (folding something back). Can be used intransitively in sense 2 (turning back oneself).
