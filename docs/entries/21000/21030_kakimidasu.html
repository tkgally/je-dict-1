1. to stir up; to agitate; to churn
To physically stir or agitate something, making it disordered or turbulent.
水面(すいめん)をかき乱(みだ)す。
To stir up the surface of the water.
風(かぜ)が砂(すな)をかき乱(みだ)した。
The wind stirred up the sand.
強風(きょうふう)が海面(かいめん)をかき乱(みだ)して波(なみ)が高(たか)くなった。
Strong winds churned up the sea, making the waves higher.
2. to throw into confusion; to disturb; to disrupt
To cause mental or social disorder; to upset someone's composure or disrupt a peaceful situation.
心(こころ)をかき乱(みだ)される。
To have one's mind disturbed.
そのニュースが社会(しゃかい)の秩序(ちつじょ)をかき乱(みだ)した。
That news threw society into confusion.
彼女(かのじょ)の一言(ひとこと)が場(ば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)をかき乱(みだ)してしまった。
Her one remark ended up disrupting the atmosphere.

A compound verb from かく (to scratch, stir) + 乱(みだ)す (to disturb, disorder). The prefix かき～ intensifies the disrupting action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心(こころ)をかき乱(みだ)す: to disturb one's mind
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)をかき乱(みだ)す: to disrupt order
  • 場(ば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)をかき乱(みだ)す: to disrupt the atmosphere
  • 平和(へいわ)をかき乱(みだ)す: to disturb the peace
  • 水面(すいめん)をかき乱(みだ)す: to churn the water's surface

THE かき～ PREFIX:

Appears in several compound verbs as an intensifier:

  • かき集(あつ)める: to gather up, scrape together
  • かき消(け)す: to drown out, extinguish
  • かき混(ま)ぜる: to stir, mix thoroughly
  • かき分(わ)ける: to push through, part

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乱(みだ)す: to disturb, disorder — simpler form without the かき intensifier
  • 攪乱(かくらん)する: to disturb, throw into confusion — more formal and literary
