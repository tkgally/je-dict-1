1. to utilize, to make use of, to put to practical use
To effectively use available resources, skills, knowledge, or opportunities for a practical purpose.
経験(けいけん)を活用(かつよう)する。
To put experience to use.
この制度(せいど)をもっと活用(かつよう)すべきだ。
We should make better use of this system.
地域(ちいき)の資源(しげん)を活用(かつよう)して、新(あたら)しい産業(さんぎょう)を生(う)み出(だ)す取(と)り組(く)みが進(すす)んでいる。
Efforts to create new industries by utilizing local resources are progressing.

A compound of 活(かつ) (lively, active) and 用(よう) (use). Emphasizes making effective, productive use of something — not just using it, but leveraging it to its full potential. Very common in business, education, and policy contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資源(しげん)を活用(かつよう)する: to utilize resources
  • 人材(じんざい)を活用(かつよう)する: to make use of human talent
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を活用(かつよう)する: to leverage technology
  • データを活用(かつよう)する: to utilize data
  • 有効(ゆうこう)活用(かつよう): effective utilization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 利用(りよう)する: to use, to utilize — more general; simply using something for a purpose
  • 使用(しよう)する: to use — the most neutral and basic term for using something
  • 駆使(くし)する: to make full use of, to command — implies mastery and skillful deployment

NOTE:
The noun 活用(かつよう) also has a grammatical meaning: verb conjugation. In Japanese grammar studies, 活用(かつよう) refers to how verbs and adjectives change form.
