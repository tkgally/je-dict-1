入(い)れ替(か)え

いれかえ
noun
replacement; swapping; turnover
1. replacement; swapping; switching out
The act of removing one thing and putting another in its place. Used for replacing people, items, or contents.
選手(せんしゅ)の入(い)れ替(か)えがあった。
There was a player substitution.
電池(でんち)の入(い)れ替(か)えをする。
To replace the batteries.
衣替(ころもが)えの季節(きせつ)に、クローゼットの中身(なかみ)を入(い)れ替(か)えた。
During the seasonal wardrobe change, I swapped out the contents of the closet.
2. turnover; changeover; clearing out
The process of one group leaving and another arriving, such as audiences between shows or employees rotating in shifts.
映画館(えいがかん)は完全(かんぜん)入(い)れ替(か)え制(せい)です。
The cinema operates on a complete turnover system.
昼(ひる)と夜(よる)でスタッフの入(い)れ替(か)えがある。
There is a staff changeover between day and night.
コンサートは二部(にぶ)入(い)れ替(か)え制(せい)で、一部(いちぶ)の客(きゃく)は退場(たいじょう)しなければならない。
The concert has a two-part turnover system, so the first-part audience must leave.

The noun form of the verb 入(い)れ替(か)える (to replace, swap). Common in both everyday and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入(い)れ替(か)え制(せい): turnover system (audiences must leave between shows)
  • 入(い)れ替(か)え戦(せん): relegation/promotion playoff
  • 完全(かんぜん)入(い)れ替(か)え: complete turnover
  • メンバーの入(い)れ替(か)え: membership change; roster shake-up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交換(こうかん): exchange — mutual giving and receiving between two parties
  • 交代(こうたい): rotation — taking turns in a role or position
  • 差(さ)し替(か)え: replacement — substituting one item for another, often documents or components
