1. to divorce; to get divorced
To legally end a marriage. In Japan, divorce can be by mutual consent (協議離婚(きょうぎりこん)), mediation (調停離婚(ちょうていりこん)), or court ruling (裁判離婚(さいばんりこん)).
二人(ふたり)は離婚(りこん)した。
The two of them got divorced.
離婚(りこん)の原因(げんいん)は性格(せいかく)の不一致(ふいっち)だった。
The reason for the divorce was incompatibility.
結婚(けっこん)して三年(さんねん)で離婚(りこん)することになった。
They ended up divorcing after three years of marriage.

Compound of 離(り) (separate) + 婚(こん) (marriage). In Japan, the most common form is 協議(きょうぎ)離婚(りこん) (divorce by mutual agreement), which requires only submitting a form to the local government office — no court involvement needed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 離婚(りこん)届(とどけ): divorce registration form
  • 離婚(りこん)率(りつ): divorce rate
  • 離婚(りこん)訴訟(そしょう): divorce lawsuit
  • 協議(きょうぎ)離婚(りこん): divorce by mutual consent
  • 離婚(りこん)慰謝料(いしゃりょう): divorce alimony/consolation money

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan's divorce system allows couples to divorce simply by mutual agreement and submitting a 離婚届(りこんとどけ) (divorce form) to the city office. This 協議離婚(きょうぎりこん) accounts for about 90% of all divorces in Japan.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 結婚(けっこん)する: to marry (the opposite)
  • 別居(べっきょ)する: to live apart, to separate (without legally divorcing)
  • 再婚(さいこん)する: to remarry
