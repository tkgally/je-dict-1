1. to bring; to carry with oneself; to take along
To bring something along when going somewhere. More formal than 持(も)っていく.
昼食(ちゅうしょく)を持参(じさん)する。
To bring one's own lunch.
身分証明書(みぶんしょうめいしょ)をご持参(じさん)ください。
Please bring your identification.
参加者(さんかしゃ)は各自(かくじ)で飲(の)み物(もの)を持参(じさん)することになっている。
Participants are expected to bring their own drinks.

A formal way to say "bring" or "carry with oneself." Commonly seen in official notices, event instructions, and business communications. In everyday speech, 持(も)っていく or 持(も)ってくる are more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弁当(べんとう)持参(じさん): bringing one's own lunch (BYOL)
  • ご持参(じさん)ください: please bring (polite)
  • 各自(かくじ)持参(じさん): each person bringing their own

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 持(も)っていく: to take (something) along — everyday equivalent
  • 持(も)ってくる: to bring (something) here — everyday, direction toward speaker
  • 携帯(けいたい)する: to carry on one's person — more formal, implies keeping something readily accessible
