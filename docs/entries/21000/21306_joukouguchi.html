乗降口(じょうこうぐち)

じょうこうぐち
noun
boarding entrance; doorway (of a vehicle)
1. boarding entrance; doorway; entry/exit point (of a vehicle)
The door or opening on a bus, train, airplane, or other vehicle where passengers get on and off.
乗降口(じょうこうぐち)から離(はな)れてください。
Please step away from the doorway.
バスの乗降口(じょうこうぐち)は前(まえ)と後(うし)ろにある。
The bus has boarding entrances at the front and rear.
車椅子(くるまいす)の方(かた)のために、乗降口(じょうこうぐち)にスロープが設置(せっち)されている。
A ramp has been installed at the boarding entrance for wheelchair users.

A practical transportation term for the door or opening where passengers board and exit vehicles. Composed of 乗降(じょうこう) (boarding and alighting) + 口(ぐち) (opening, entrance). Common in announcements and signage on buses, trains, and airplanes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乗降口(じょうこうぐち)に注意(ちゅうい): caution at the doorway
  • 乗降口(じょうこうぐち)を開(あ)ける: to open the doorway
  • 乗降口(じょうこうぐち)を塞(ふさ)がない: don't block the doorway
  • 前(まえ)の乗降口(じょうこうぐち): front entrance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出入口(でいりぐち): entrance/exit — for buildings, not vehicles
  • 乗車口(じょうしゃぐち): boarding entrance — specifically for getting on
  • 降車口(こうしゃぐち): exit — specifically for getting off
  • ドア: door — general term, often used for train doors in everyday speech

WHERE YOU SEE IT:
Common in transportation announcements: "乗降口(じょうこうぐち)にご注意(ちゅうい)ください" (please be careful at the doorway).
