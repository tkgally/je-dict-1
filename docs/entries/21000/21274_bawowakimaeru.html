場(ば)をわきまえる

ばをわきまえる
expression
to know one's place; to read the room
1. to know one's place; to act appropriately for the situation; to read the room
To understand what behavior, speech, or attitude is appropriate for a given social situation and act accordingly. Implies social awareness and self-restraint.
場(ば)をわきまえた行動(こうどう)をする。
To act appropriately for the situation.
彼(かれ)は場(ば)をわきまえない発言(はつげん)が多(おお)い。
He often makes remarks that are out of place.
大人(おとな)なら場(ば)をわきまえて行動(こうどう)すべきだ。
As an adult, you should know how to conduct yourself appropriately.

Combines 場(ば) (place, situation) with わきまえる (to discern, to know the difference). A key concept in Japanese social norms — the ability to adjust one's behavior to match the formality and mood of a situation. Often used in the negative (場(ば)をわきまえない) to criticize someone who behaves inappropriately.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 場(ば)をわきまえた行動(こうどう): appropriate behavior
  • 場(ば)をわきまえた発言(はつげん): appropriate remarks
  • 場(ば)をわきまえない人(ひと): a person who doesn't read the room

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む: to read the atmosphere — a colloquial equivalent; focuses on sensing unspoken social cues
  • 分(ぶん)をわきまえる: to know one's place/limits — about understanding one's social position or capabilities
  • TPOをわきまえる: to be mindful of time, place, and occasion — uses the English abbreviation TPO, common in Japanese
