1. to hand over; to deliver; to transfer
To formally transfer something from one party to another, emphasizing the completed exchange between giver and receiver.
鍵(かぎ)を受(う)け渡(わた)す。
To hand over the key.
商品(しょうひん)は直接(ちょくせつ)受(う)け渡(わた)してください。
Please hand over the merchandise in person.
契約書(けいやくしょ)に署名(しょめい)したあと、担当者(たんとうしゃ)が書類(しょるい)を受(う)け渡(わた)してくれた。
After signing the contract, the person in charge handed over the documents.

A compound verb combining 受(う)ける (to receive) and 渡(わた)す (to hand over), expressing the act of transferring something from one party to another. The compound emphasizes the complete transaction — both the giving and receiving sides.

USAGE:

  • Often used in business, real estate, and formal transactions
  • The noun form 受(う)け渡(わた)し is very common, meaning "handover" or "delivery"
  • Can refer to physical items, documents, or abstract things like authority

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鍵(かぎ)の受(う)け渡(わた)し: key handover
  • 商品(しょうひん)の受(う)け渡(わた)し: product delivery
  • 受(う)け渡(わた)し日(び): delivery date
  • 受(う)け渡(わた)し場所(ばしょ): handover location

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 渡(わた)す: to hand over; one-directional, from giver's perspective
  • 引(ひ)き渡(わた)す: to hand over, surrender; often used in legal or formal contexts
  • 納品(のうひん)する: to deliver goods; more specifically about commercial delivery
