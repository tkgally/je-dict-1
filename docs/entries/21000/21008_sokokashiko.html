そこかしこ

そこかしこ
adverb
here and there; everywhere
1. here and there; everywhere; all over the place
In various places throughout an area, implying wide distribution. Slightly literary or elevated in register.
桜(さくら)がそこかしこに咲(さ)いている。
Cherry blossoms are blooming here and there.
街(まち)のそこかしこにカフェがある。
There are cafes all over the town.
台風(たいふう)の後(あと)、そこかしこに折(お)れた枝(えだ)や散(ち)らばったゴミが見(み)られた。
After the typhoon, broken branches and scattered debris could be seen everywhere.

A slightly literary adverb meaning "here and there" or "all over." Composed of そこ (there) + かしこ (over there), combining two demonstratives to express the idea of multiple scattered locations. More evocative and descriptive than あちこち.

USAGE:
Often used in written prose and descriptive passages. Can follow a noun with the particle に (そこかしこに) to indicate location.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あちこち: here and there — the most common everyday equivalent; neutral register
  • あちらこちら: here and there — polite form of あちこち
  • 至(いた)るところ: everywhere — emphasizes completeness of distribution; slightly formal
  • 方々(ほうぼう): all around, in many places — implies searching or moving around
