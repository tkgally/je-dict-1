お得意様(とくいさま)

おとくいさま
noun
valued customer; regular patron
1. valued customer; regular patron; loyal client
A polite and respectful term for a regular customer or client who has an ongoing business relationship. The お～様(さま) form adds honorific politeness.
あの方(かた)はお得意様(とくいさま)です。
That person is a valued customer.
お得意様(とくいさま)には特別(とくべつ)割引(わりびき)をご用意(ようい)しております。
We offer special discounts for our valued customers.
長年(ながねん)のお得意様(とくいさま)を大切(たいせつ)にすることが、商売(しょうばい)を長(なが)く続(つづ)ける秘訣(ひけつ)だ。
The secret to sustaining a business long-term is to value your long-standing customers.

The polite form of 得意先(とくいさき)/得意客(とくいきゃく). The お～様(さま) honorific wrapping makes this suitable for directly addressing or referring to customers in a respectful business context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お得意様(とくいさま)向(む)け: aimed at valued customers
  • お得意様(とくいさま)限定(げんてい): exclusive to regular customers
  • 長年(ながねん)のお得意様(とくいさま): long-standing customer

RELATED TERMS:

  • 得意先(とくいさき): client, customer (business term) — used within the company to refer to business clients
  • 常連(じょうれん): regular (customer) — more casual, used for restaurants, bars, and shops
  • 顧客(こきゃく): customer, client — formal/neutral business term without the honorific warmth
  • お客様(きゃくさま): customer — general polite term for any customer
