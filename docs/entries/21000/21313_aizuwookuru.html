合図(あいず)を送(おく)る

あいずをおくる
expression
to give a signal; to send a cue
1. to give a signal; to send a cue; to signal to someone
To communicate a message or instruction to someone using a gesture, sign, sound, or other non-verbal means.
目(め)で合図(あいず)を送(おく)った。
I signaled with my eyes.
監督(かんとく)がベンチから選手(せんしゅ)に合図(あいず)を送(おく)った。
The coach sent a signal to the players from the bench.
準備(じゅんび)ができたら、手(て)を挙(あ)げて合図(あいず)を送(おく)ってください。
When you're ready, please raise your hand to give the signal.

A common expression for communicating through signals or cues. 合図(あいず) means a signal, sign, or cue; 送(おく)る means to send.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 目(め)で合図(あいず)を送(おく)る: to signal with one's eyes
  • 手(て)で合図(あいず)を送(おく)る: to signal with one's hand
  • 合図(あいず)を送(おく)り合(あ)う: to signal to each other

OTHER 合図(あいず) EXPRESSIONS:

  • 合図(あいず)をする: to make a signal — more general
  • 合図(あいず)を出(だ)す: to give a signal — similar meaning
  • 合図(あいず)を待(ま)つ: to wait for the signal
  • 合図(あいず)と共(とも)に: at the signal

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • サインを送(おく)る: to send a sign — more casual, sometimes used in romance
  • 目配(めくば)せする: to exchange meaningful glances — specifically about eye contact
  • 手招(てまね)きする: to beckon — specifically using hand gestures to call someone over
