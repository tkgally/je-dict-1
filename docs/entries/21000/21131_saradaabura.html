サラダ油(あぶら)

さらだあぶら
noun
salad oil; cooking oil; vegetable oil
1. salad oil; cooking oil; vegetable oil
A refined, neutral-flavored vegetable oil used for everyday cooking. In Japan, this is the standard general-purpose cooking oil, typically made from rapeseed (canola), soybean, or a blend.
フライパンにサラダ油(あぶら)をひく。
Coat the frying pan with cooking oil.
サラダ油(あぶら)が切(き)れたから買(か)ってきて。
We're out of cooking oil, so please go buy some.
揚(あ)げ物(もの)にはサラダ油(あぶら)よりも米油(こめあぶら)のほうがカラッと揚(あ)がる。
Rice bran oil gives a crispier result for deep-frying than salad oil.

The most common general-purpose cooking oil in Japanese kitchens. The name "salad oil" comes from the fact that it is refined enough to be used in cold dressings without solidifying, but it is used for all cooking purposes including stir-frying and deep-frying.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • サラダ油(あぶら)をひく: to coat with oil
  • サラダ油(あぶら)で炒(いた)める: to stir-fry in cooking oil
  • サラダ油(あぶら)大(おお)さじ1: 1 tablespoon of cooking oil

OTHER COOKING OILS:

  • ごま油(あぶら): sesame oil — for flavoring
  • オリーブオイル: olive oil
  • 米油(こめあぶら): rice bran oil — increasingly popular
  • 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら): deep-frying oil

NOTE:
Despite the name "salad," this oil is rarely used just for salads in modern Japan. It is the default cooking oil, equivalent to "vegetable oil" in English-speaking countries.
