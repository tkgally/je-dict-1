1. to accelerate; to speed up; to gain momentum
To increase speed or rate, either physically (a vehicle, movement) or figuratively (a process, trend, change).
車(くるま)が加速(かそく)した。
The car accelerated.
経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)が加速(かそく)している。
Economic growth is accelerating.
技術(ぎじゅつ)革新(かくしん)のスピードが年々(ねんねん)加速(かそく)して、社会(しゃかい)の変化(へんか)が激(はげ)しくなっている。
The pace of technological innovation is accelerating year by year, and social change is intensifying.

Describes an increase in speed or rate. Used both for physical acceleration (vehicles, objects) and for abstract processes gaining momentum (trends, reforms, decline).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変化(へんか)が加速(かそく)する: change accelerates
  • 成長(せいちょう)を加速(かそく)させる: to accelerate growth
  • 少子化(しょうしか)が加速(かそく)する: declining birthrate accelerates
  • 開発(かいはつ)を加速(かそく)する: to accelerate development

TRANSITIVITY:
Used both intransitively (車(くるま)が加速(かそく)する, the car accelerates) and transitively with させる or を (改革(かいかく)を加速(かそく)する, to accelerate reform). The させる causative form is also common for transitive use: 成長(せいちょう)を加速(かそく)させる.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 速(はや)める: to hasten — emphasizes making something happen sooner rather than increasing rate
  • 促進(そくしん)する: to promote/facilitate — focuses on actively pushing a process forward
  • 減速(げんそく)する: to decelerate — the opposite, slowing down
