1. defection; estrangement; turning against; breaking away
The act of turning away from and opposing a group, leader, or cause that one previously supported, often in a political or organizational context.
支持者(しじしゃ)の離反(りはん)が進(すす)んでいる。
Supporters are increasingly turning away.
度重(たびかさ)なる不祥事(ふしょうじ)で国民(こくみん)の離反(りはん)を招(まね)いた。
Repeated scandals invited public disaffection.
党(とう)の方針(ほうしん)に不満(ふまん)を持(も)つ議員(ぎいん)たちが離反(りはん)し、新(あたら)しい会派(かいは)を結成(けっせい)した。
Legislators dissatisfied with the party's policies defected and formed a new faction.

Describes the act of turning away from or breaking ties with a group, leader, or cause that one previously supported. Combines 離(り) (separation) and 反(はん) (opposition), emphasizing both distancing and opposition.

USAGE:

  • Most commonly used in political, organizational, and social contexts
  • Can describe both the act of defecting and the resulting state of estrangement
  • Often implies a betrayal of trust or loyalty

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 離反(りはん)を招(まね)く: to invite defection/estrangement
  • 支持者(しじしゃ)の離反(りはん): defection of supporters
  • 離反(りはん)行為(こうい): act of defection
  • 党(とう)から離反(りはん)する: to defect from the party

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 離脱(りだつ): withdrawal, secession — more neutral, simply leaving a group
  • 裏切(うらぎ)り: betrayal — stronger emotional charge; implies deliberate treachery
  • 反旗(はんき)を翻(ひるがえ)す: to raise the flag of revolt — more dramatic and decisive
  • 造反(ぞうはん): rebellion, revolt — implies organized opposition within a group
