1. to press for answers; to pursue responsibility; to hold accountable; to question closely
To persistently question or investigate someone to uncover the truth or assign blame. Commonly used in political, journalistic, and legal contexts.
責任(せきにん)を追及(ついきゅう)する。
To pursue responsibility.
野党(やとう)が政府(せいふ)の対応(たいおう)を追及(ついきゅう)した。
The opposition party pressed the government on its response.
記者(きしゃ)が不正(ふせい)会計(かいけい)について徹底的(てっていてき)に追及(ついきゅう)し、ついに真相(しんそう)が明(あき)らかになった。
The journalist pursued the accounting fraud thoroughly, and the truth finally came to light.

Written with 及(きゅう) (to reach), distinguishing it from the homophone 追求(ついきゅう)する (to pursue/seek — goals, ideals) and 追究(ついきゅう)する (to investigate — the truth, causes). All three are read ついきゅう but use different kanji and have distinct meanings.

KANJI DISTINCTION:

  • 追及(ついきゅう): to press for answers, hold accountable (accountability)
  • 追求(ついきゅう): to pursue, seek (goals, happiness, profit)
  • 追究(ついきゅう): to investigate thoroughly (causes, truth)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を追及(ついきゅう)する: to hold someone accountable
  • 不正(ふせい)を追及(ついきゅう)する: to pursue wrongdoing
  • 厳(きび)しく追及(ついきゅう)する: to press hard
  • 国会(こっかい)で追及(ついきゅう)する: to question in parliament

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追求(ついきゅう)する: to pursue (goals) — seeking something desirable
  • 追究(ついきゅう)する: to investigate (truth) — scholarly or scientific inquiry
  • 糾弾(きゅうだん)する: to denounce — stronger, publicly condemning wrongdoing
