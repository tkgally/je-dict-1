1. to think up; to devise; to come up with
To create or produce an idea, plan, or solution through thinking. Emphasizes the emergence of a new idea.
いい方法(ほうほう)を考(かんが)え出(だ)す。
To come up with a good method.
彼(かれ)は新(あたら)しいアイデアを考(かんが)え出(だ)した。
He came up with a new idea.
問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)する方法(ほうほう)をみんなで考(かんが)え出(だ)そう。
Let's all come up with a way to solve the problem.
2. to begin to think; to start pondering
To start thinking about something. The ～出す suffix marks a sudden or spontaneous onset, unlike ～始(はじ)める, which simply marks the start of an action. Compare 泣(な)き出(だ)す (to burst into tears).
急(きゅう)に将来(しょうらい)のことを考(かんが)え出(だ)した。
I suddenly started thinking about the future.
夜中(よなか)に目(め)が覚(さ)めて、仕事(しごと)のことを考(かんが)え出(だ)した。
I woke up in the middle of the night and started thinking about work.
あまり考(かんが)え出(だ)すと止(と)まらなくなる。
If you start thinking about it too much, you can't stop.

A compound verb from 考(かんが)える (to think) + 出(だ)す (to put out / to begin). The two senses correspond to the two meanings of the ～出(だ)す suffix.

THE TWO MEANINGS OF ～出(だ)す:

  • Producing/extracting: 考(かんが)え出(だ)す (think something up), 思(おも)い出(だ)す (recall), 見(み)出(だ)す (find)
  • Beginning: 考(かんが)え出(だ)す (start thinking), 泣(な)き出(だ)す (start crying), 走(はし)り出(だ)す (start running)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • アイデアを考(かんが)え出(だ)す: to come up with an idea
  • 方法(ほうほう)を考(かんが)え出(だ)す: to devise a method
  • 解決策(かいけつさく)を考(かんが)え出(だ)す: to think up a solution

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 思(おも)いつく: to hit upon, to come up with — more spontaneous, like a flash of inspiration
  • 発案(はつあん)する: to propose, originate — more formal, often used for official proposals
  • 工夫(くふう)する: to devise, to figure out — emphasizes creative problem-solving through trial and effort
