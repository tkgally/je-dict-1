1. to accompany, to go together with
To go along with someone to the same destination, often in a professional, formal, or supportive capacity.
通訳(つうやく)が同行(どうこう)する。
An interpreter will accompany us.
出張(しゅっちょう)に部下(ぶか)を同行(どうこう)させた。
I had my subordinate accompany me on the business trip.
警察(けいさつ)官(かん)が容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の自宅(じたく)まで同行(どうこう)し、家宅捜索(かたくそうさく)を行(おこな)った。
Police officers accompanied the suspect to their home and conducted a house search.

A compound of 同(どう) (same, together) and 行(こう) (go). More formal than 一緒(いっしょ)に行(い)く. Often used in business, travel, and official contexts where someone goes along in a professional role.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出張(しゅっちょう)に同行(どうこう)する: to accompany on a business trip
  • 取材(しゅざい)に同行(どうこう)する: to accompany on a news report
  • 同行者(どうこうしゃ): companion, person accompanying
  • 同行(どうこう)を求(もと)める: to request someone's company

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 付(つ)き添(そ)う: to attend, to escort — implies providing care or support
  • 随行(ずいこう)する: to accompany (a superior) — more formal, specifically following a person of higher status
  • 一緒(いっしょ)に行(い)く: to go together — the casual everyday expression
