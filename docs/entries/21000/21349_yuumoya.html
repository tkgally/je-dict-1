夕靄(ゆうもや)

ゆうもや
noun
evening haze; evening mist
1. evening haze; evening mist
A light, hazy mist that appears in the evening as temperatures drop, creating a soft atmospheric effect.
夕靄(ゆうもや)が町(まち)を包(つつ)んだ。
The evening haze enveloped the town.
夕靄(ゆうもや)の向(む)こうに山(やま)が見(み)えた。
The mountains were visible beyond the evening haze.
川沿(かわぞ)いに夕靄(ゆうもや)が立(た)ちこめ、対岸(たいがん)がぼんやり霞(かす)んで見(み)えた。
Evening mist hung along the river, making the opposite bank appear faintly blurred.

A compound of 夕(ゆう) (evening) and 靄(もや) (haze/mist). A literary and atmospheric word used primarily in descriptive writing, poetry, and scenic descriptions. 靄(もや) is lighter and thinner than 霧(きり) (fog), so 夕靄(ゆうもや) suggests a gentler, more translucent effect than dense fog.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夕靄(ゆうもや)が立(た)つ: evening haze rises
  • 夕靄(ゆうもや)に包(つつ)まれる: to be wrapped in evening haze
  • 夕靄(ゆうもや)がかかる: evening haze settles
  • 夕靄(ゆうもや)の中(なか): in the evening haze

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 朝靄(あさもや): morning haze — the morning counterpart
  • 夕霧(ゆうぎり): evening fog — denser and thicker than haze
  • 靄(もや): haze/mist — the general term for light mist
  • 霞(かすみ): haze — often used poetically, especially in spring
