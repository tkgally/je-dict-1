1. to drink up; to drain; to finish a drink
To drink a beverage completely, emptying the container without leaving any liquid behind.
ビールを一気(いっき)に飲(の)み干(ほ)した。
I drained the beer in one gulp.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)をコップ一杯(いっぱい)飲(の)み干(ほ)した。
I drank up a whole glass of cold water.
彼(かれ)は残(のこ)っていたワインを最後(さいご)の一滴(いってき)まで飲(の)み干(ほ)してからグラスを置(お)いた。
He drained the remaining wine to the last drop before setting down his glass.

A compound verb formed from 飲(の)む (to drink) + 干(ほ)す (to dry out). The 干(ほ)す component adds the meaning of completely finishing or emptying a container of liquid.

USAGE:

  • Emphasizes drinking everything without leaving any behind
  • Often used with 一気(いっき)に (in one go) for dramatic effect
  • Can imply a bold or determined manner of drinking

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ビールを飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to drain a beer
  • 一気(いっき)に飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to chug; to drain in one gulp
  • 最後(さいご)の一滴(いってき)まで飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to drink to the last drop
  • グラスを飲(の)み干(ほ)す: to empty a glass

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲(の)む: the basic verb for drinking; does not imply finishing
  • 飲(の)み切(き)る: to drink completely; similar meaning but less vivid
  • がぶ飲(の)みする: to gulp down; emphasizes the rough manner of drinking rather than completeness
