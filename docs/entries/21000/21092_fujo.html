1. aid; support; assistance; relief
Help or financial support given to those in need, especially in legal and social welfare contexts. Often refers to formal obligations of support.
生活(せいかつ)扶助(ふじょ)を受(う)ける。
To receive livelihood assistance.
法律(ほうりつ)扶助(ふじょ)制度(せいど)を利用(りよう)する。
To use the legal aid system.
夫婦(ふうふ)には互(たが)いに扶助(ふじょ)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
Married couples have a mutual obligation to support each other.

A formal term for aid, support, or relief, most commonly encountered in legal and social welfare contexts. Refers to institutionalized or obligatory forms of support rather than casual help.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生活(せいかつ)扶助(ふじょ): livelihood assistance (a category of welfare benefits)
  • 法律(ほうりつ)扶助(ふじょ): legal aid
  • 扶助(ふじょ)義務(ぎむ): obligation of support
  • 扶助(ふじょ)料(りょう): support payment, maintenance
  • 相互(そうご)扶助(ふじょ): mutual aid

USAGE:
In Japanese law, 扶助(ふじょ) appears in family law (spousal and parental support obligations) and in the welfare system (生活(せいかつ)保護法(ほごほう)). It carries a more formal and institutional tone than everyday words like 助(たす)け or 支援(しえん).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 援助(えんじょ): aid, assistance — broader and more general; used for international aid, personal help, etc.
  • 支援(しえん): support — emphasizes backing or helping someone achieve something
  • 扶養(ふよう): support of dependents — specifically refers to financially supporting family members

ETYMOLOGY:
扶(ふ) (support, help) + 助(じょ) (aid, help).
