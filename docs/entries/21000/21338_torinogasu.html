1. to miss catching; to let escape; to fail to seize
To fail to catch or capture something or someone that was within reach. Used for criminals, opportunities, and things one tried but failed to get.
犯人(はんにん)を取(と)り逃(のが)した。
They let the criminal escape.
チャンスを取(と)り逃(のが)してしまった。
I missed the opportunity.
限定(げんてい)商品(しょうひん)を取(と)り逃(のが)さないように早(はや)めに並(なら)んだ。
I lined up early so as not to miss the limited-edition product.

Compound verb of 取(と)る (to take, to catch) + 逃(のが)す (to let escape, to miss). Godan verb conjugating on the す row. Implies that one had a chance to obtain or capture something but failed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 犯人(はんにん)を取(と)り逃(のが)す: to let a criminal escape
  • チャンスを取(と)り逃(のが)す: to miss an opportunity
  • 獲物(えもの)を取(と)り逃(のが)す: to let prey escape
  • 勝利(しょうり)を取(と)り逃(のが)す: to let victory slip away

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逃(のが)す: to miss, to let escape (simpler; the base verb)
  • 見逃(みのが)す: to overlook, to miss seeing
  • 乗(の)り遅(おく)れる: to miss (a vehicle — train, bus)
