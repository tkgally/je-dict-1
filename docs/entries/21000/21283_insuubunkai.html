1. factorization; factoring
The mathematical process of breaking down a number or algebraic expression into a product of its factors.
この式(しき)を因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)しなさい。
Factor this expression.
因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)は中学(ちゅうがく)数学(すうがく)で習(なら)う。
Factorization is learned in middle school math.
因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)が苦手(にがて)な生徒(せいと)が多(おお)い。
Many students struggle with factorization.
2. breaking something down into its components (figurative)
Used figuratively to mean analyzing or decomposing a complex problem or situation into its constituent parts.
複雑(ふくざつ)な問題(もんだい)を因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)して考(かんが)える。
To break down a complex problem into its components and think it through.
感情(かんじょう)を因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)してみると、不安(ふあん)と怒(いか)りが混(ま)ざっていた。
When I broke down my emotions into components, I found a mix of anxiety and anger.
ビジネスの課題(かだい)を因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)して、一(ひと)つずつ解決(かいけつ)していく。
Breaking down business challenges into components and solving them one by one.

A compound of 因数(いんすう) (factor) and 分解(ぶんかい) (decomposition, breaking down). Originally a mathematical term learned in middle school, it has gained popularity as a figurative expression in business and self-help contexts, meaning to analytically decompose a complex issue into manageable parts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)する: to factorize; to break down into components
  • 因数分解(いんすうぶんかい)して考(かんが)える: to think by decomposing into factors
  • 素因数分解(そいんすうぶんかい): prime factorization

RELATED TERMS:

  • 因数(いんすう): factor — a number or expression that divides evenly into another
  • 分解(ぶんかい): decomposition — the general term for breaking something apart
  • 展開(てんかい): expansion — the reverse operation in algebra (expanding factored expressions)
