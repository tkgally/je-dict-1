1. blushing; turning red with embarrassment or shame
The face turning red due to embarrassment, shame, or awkwardness. Can describe both the physical reaction and the feeling of embarrassment itself.
褒(ほ)められて赤面(せきめん)した。
I blushed when I was praised.
失言(しつげん)に気(き)づいて赤面(せきめん)した。
I turned red when I realized my slip of the tongue.
彼(かれ)は赤面(せきめん)しながら、好(す)きだと告白(こくはく)した。
He confessed his feelings while blushing.
過去(かこ)の失敗(しっぱい)を思(おも)い出(だ)すと、今(いま)でも赤面(せきめん)してしまう。
Whenever I recall past mistakes, I still blush.

Describes the physical act of blushing due to embarrassment or shame. The word combines 赤(せき) (red) and 面(めん) (face). More literary and formal than the everyday expression 顔(かお)が赤(あか)くなる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 赤面(せきめん)する: to blush
  • 赤面(せきめん)もの: something embarrassing (enough to make one blush)
  • 思(おも)わず赤面(せきめん)する: to blush involuntarily

REGISTER:
More literary and formal than colloquial expressions. In everyday speech, 顔(かお)が赤(あか)くなる or 恥(は)ずかしくて赤(あか)くなる are more natural.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 顔(かお)が赤(あか)くなる: to turn red in the face — everyday expression
  • 照(て)れる: to feel embarrassed, to be shy — focuses on the emotion rather than the physical reaction
  • 顔面蒼白(がんめんそうはく): turning pale — the opposite physical reaction, caused by shock or fear
