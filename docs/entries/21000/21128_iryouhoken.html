医療保険(いりょうほけん)

いりょうほけん
noun
medical insurance; health insurance
1. medical insurance; health insurance
Insurance that covers medical expenses. In Japan, this primarily refers to the public health insurance system that covers all residents, but can also refer to supplementary private medical insurance.
医療保険(いりょうほけん)に加入(かにゅう)する。
To enroll in medical insurance.
日本(にほん)では国民(こくみん)皆(かい)保険(ほけん)で、全員(ぜんいん)が医療保険(いりょうほけん)に入(はい)っている。
In Japan, under the universal insurance system, everyone is covered by medical insurance.
民間(みんかん)の医療保険(いりょうほけん)に入(はい)っておくと、入院(にゅういん)費用(ひよう)の負担(ふたん)が軽(かる)くなる。
If you take out private medical insurance, the burden of hospitalization costs becomes lighter.

Japan's public health insurance system (国民皆保険(こくみんかいほけん)) is one of the most comprehensive in the world, covering all residents. Most people pay 30% of medical costs out of pocket, with insurance covering the rest.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 医療保険(いりょうほけん)に加入(かにゅう)する: to enroll in health insurance
  • 医療保険(いりょうほけん)制度(せいど): health insurance system
  • 公的(こうてき)医療保険(いりょうほけん): public health insurance
  • 民間(みんかん)の医療保険(いりょうほけん): private medical insurance

JAPAN'S SYSTEM:

  • 健康保険(けんこうほけん) (健保(けんぽ)): employee health insurance (for company workers)
  • 国民健康保険(こくみんけんこうほけん) (国保(こくほ)): national health insurance (for self-employed and others)
  • 後期高齢者医療保険(こうきこうれいしゃいりょうほけん): medical insurance for the elderly (75+)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 保険証(ほけんしょう): insurance card — the card you present at hospitals
  • 自己負担(じこふたん): out-of-pocket expense
  • 生命保険(せいめいほけん): life insurance — different from medical insurance
