固(かた)ゆで

かたゆで
noun
hard-boiled
1. hard-boiled; boiled until firm
Describes eggs (or occasionally other foods) that have been boiled until the yolk and white are completely set and firm.
固(かた)ゆで卵(たまご)を作(つく)った。
I made hard-boiled eggs.
固(かた)ゆでにするには十二分(じゅうにふん)ほど茹(ゆ)でる。
Boil for about twelve minutes to make them hard-boiled.
サラダには半熟(はんじゅく)より固(かた)ゆでの方(ほう)が崩(くず)れなくて使(つか)いやすい。
Hard-boiled eggs are easier to use in salads than soft-boiled ones since they don't fall apart.

Also written as 固茹(かたゆ)で. A cooking term composed of 固(かた) (hard, firm) and 茹(ゆ)で (boiling). Almost always used in reference to eggs. Can function as a noun (固(かた)ゆで卵(たまご)) or as a descriptive modifier (固(かた)ゆでにする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 固(かた)ゆで卵(たまご): hard-boiled egg
  • 固(かた)ゆでにする: to hard-boil
  • 固(かた)ゆでの卵(たまご): hard-boiled egg

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 半熟(はんじゅく): soft-boiled, half-cooked — the opposite, with a runny yolk
  • 温泉卵(おんせんたまご): hot spring egg — egg with set white and runny yolk
  • 茹(ゆ)で卵(たまご): boiled egg — general term, regardless of doneness
