1. subsidy; grant; financial assistance; aid
Financial support provided by a government, organization, or foundation to promote activities in areas such as research, arts, or social welfare.
国(くに)から助成(じょせい)を受(う)ける。
To receive a subsidy from the government.
研究(けんきゅう)助成金(じょせいきん)の申請(しんせい)が始(はじ)まった。
Applications for research grants have opened.
この事業(じぎょう)は地方自治体(ちほうじちたい)の助成(じょせい)を受(う)けて運営(うんえい)されている。
This project is run with financial support from the local government.

Refers to financial support provided by governments, organizations, or foundations to promote research, arts, social welfare, or other public-interest activities. The word emphasizes the supportive and promotional aspect of the funding.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 助成金(じょせいきん): subsidy, grant money
  • 助成(じょせい)制度(せいど): subsidy system
  • 助成(じょせい)を受(う)ける: to receive a subsidy
  • 助成(じょせい)事業(じぎょう): subsidized project
  • 助成(じょせい)団体(だんたい): grant-giving organization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 補助(ほじょ): assistance, supplement — broader meaning; 補助金(ほじょきん) is often interchangeable with 助成金(じょせいきん)
  • 援助(えんじょ): aid, support — more general, includes non-financial help
  • 支援(しえん): support — broader and more commonly used in everyday contexts
  • 交付(こうふ): disbursement, granting — focuses on the act of distributing funds
