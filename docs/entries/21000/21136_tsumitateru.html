1. to save up regularly; to accumulate money; to set aside periodically
To regularly put aside money over time, building up a sum through periodic contributions. Used for savings plans, investment accounts, and any systematic accumulation of funds.
毎月(まいつき)1万円(まんえん)ずつ積(つ)み立(た)てている。
I'm setting aside 10,000 yen each month.
将来(しょうらい)のためにコツコツ積(つ)み立(た)てる。
To steadily save up for the future.
投資信託(とうししんたく)で毎月(まいつき)少額(しょうがく)から積(つ)み立(た)てることができる。
You can save up from a small amount each month through investment trusts.

A financial term meaning to regularly set aside money over time. This concept is central to Japanese personal finance, where 積(つ)み立(た)て (the noun form) is a fundamental savings and investment strategy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 毎月(まいつき)積(つ)み立(た)てる: to save up monthly
  • コツコツ積(つ)み立(た)てる: to save up steadily
  • 少額(しょうがく)から積(つ)み立(た)てる: to start saving from a small amount

RELATED FINANCIAL TERMS:

  • 積(つ)み立(た)て: regular savings/investment (noun form)
  • 積立(つみたて)NISA: tax-free investment savings plan (a popular Japanese program)
  • 積立(つみたて)預金(よきん): installment savings
  • 定期(ていき)預金(よきん): time deposit, fixed-term savings

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 貯(た)める: to save (money) — more general; doesn't imply regular periodic contributions
  • 蓄(たくわ)える: to accumulate, to store up — more literary and broader (can include food, knowledge)
