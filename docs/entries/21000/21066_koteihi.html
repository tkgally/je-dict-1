固定費(こていひ)

こていひ
noun
fixed costs; fixed expenses
1. fixed costs; fixed expenses; overhead
Costs that remain constant regardless of how much is produced or sold. Includes things like rent, insurance, and salaries. Used in both business accounting and personal finance contexts.
固定費(こていひ)を削減(さくげん)する。
To cut fixed costs.
家賃(やちん)や保険料(ほけんりょう)は固定費(こていひ)に含(ふく)まれる。
Rent and insurance premiums are included in fixed costs.
毎月(まいつき)の固定費(こていひ)を見直(みなお)すだけで、年間(ねんかん)かなりの節約(せつやく)になる。
Just by reviewing your monthly fixed expenses, you can save a considerable amount per year.

Used in both corporate accounting and personal finance. In personal finance contexts, 固定費(こていひ) typically refers to monthly bills that stay roughly the same: rent, loan payments, insurance, subscriptions, etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 固定費(こていひ)を削減(さくげん)する: to reduce fixed costs
  • 固定費(こていひ)を見直(みなお)す: to review fixed expenses
  • 固定費(こていひ)の割合(わりあい): proportion of fixed costs
  • 毎月(まいつき)の固定費(こていひ): monthly fixed expenses

EXAMPLES OF FIXED COSTS:

  • 家賃(やちん): rent
  • 保険料(ほけんりょう): insurance premiums
  • ローン返済(へんさい): loan payments
  • サブスクリプション: subscriptions

OPPOSITE:

  • 変動費(へんどうひ): variable costs — costs that fluctuate (food, utilities, entertainment)
