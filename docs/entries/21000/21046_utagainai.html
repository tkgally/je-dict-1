1. undoubted; certain; beyond question; indisputable
Expresses that something is certain and cannot be doubted. Often used at the end of a sentence with ～に{疑いない} or ～は{疑いない}.
彼(かれ)が犯人(はんにん)であることは疑(うたが)いない。
There is no doubt that he is the culprit.
この技術(ぎじゅつ)が社会(しゃかい)を変(か)えることは疑(うたが)いない。
There is no doubt that this technology will change society.
彼女(かのじょ)の才能(さいのう)は疑(うたが)いないが、経験(けいけん)がまだ足(た)りない。
Her talent is beyond question, but she still lacks experience.

Formed from 疑(うたが)い (doubt) + ない (not). Literally means "there is no doubt." A common expression for making emphatic assertions.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～ことは疑(うたが)いない: there is no doubt that ~
  • ～に疑(うたが)いはない: there is no doubt about ~
  • 疑(うたが)いなく: undoubtedly (adverbial form)
  • 疑(うたが)いのない事実(じじつ): an undoubted fact

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 間違(まちが)いない: no mistake, for certain — more colloquial; often used to confirm something is correct
  • 確(たし)かだ: certain, sure — states certainty without the double-negative emphasis
  • 紛(まぎ)れもない: unmistakable, undeniable — emphasizes that there can be no confusion

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Common in written Japanese, speeches, and serious discussion. In casual speech, 間違(まちが)いない or 絶対(ぜったい) would be more natural.
