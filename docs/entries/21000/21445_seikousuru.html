1. to succeed, to be successful, to achieve success
To accomplish a goal, achieve a desired result, or attain success in an endeavor.
実験(じっけん)が成功(せいこう)した。
The experiment succeeded.
彼(かれ)は努力(どりょく)の末(すえ)に事業(じぎょう)を成功(せいこう)させた。
He made the business a success after much effort.
何度(なんど)も失敗(しっぱい)を繰(く)り返(かえ)したが、最後(さいご)には成功(せいこう)して世界中(せかいじゅう)から注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めた。
They failed many times, but in the end succeeded and drew attention from around the world.

A compound of 成(せい) (to become, to achieve) and 功(こう) (merit, achievement). One of the most fundamental words for expressing success. The noun form 成功(せいこう) is equally common. The causative form 成功(せいこう)させる (to make something succeed) is frequently used.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)成功(せいこう): great success
  • 成功(せいこう)を収(おさ)める: to achieve success
  • 成功(せいこう)体験(たいけん): experience of success
  • 成功(せいこう)の秘訣(ひけつ): the secret to success
  • 成功(せいこう)率(りつ): success rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • {うまくいく}: to go well — casual, everyday expression for things working out
  • 達成(たっせい)する: to achieve, to accomplish — focuses on reaching a specific goal or target
  • 失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail — the antonym
