ほっぺた

ほっぺた
noun
cheek (informal)
1. cheek (informal)
The fleshy side of the face below the eye, referred to in a casual and often affectionate way, especially when talking about children.
ほっぺたが赤(あか)くなった。
My cheeks turned red.
赤(あか)ちゃんのほっぺたはやわらかい。
A baby's cheeks are soft.
このケーキはほっぺたが落(お)ちるほどおいしい。
This cake is so delicious your cheeks could fall off.

An informal, affectionate word for cheek. It is a colloquial variant of 頬(ほほ)/頬(ほお), with ほっぺ being an even shorter casual form.

USAGE:

  • Used in casual conversation, especially when talking about children or in an affectionate context
  • Not appropriate for formal or medical contexts, where 頬(ほほ) or 頬(ほお) is used instead
  • Often associated with cute, round, or soft cheeks

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ほっぺたが落(お)ちる: so delicious your cheeks could fall off (idiomatic)
  • ほっぺたをつねる: to pinch someone's cheek
  • ほっぺたが赤(あか)い: to have red cheeks
  • ほっぺたをふくらませる: to puff out one's cheeks (in displeasure)

RELATED FORMS:

  • 頬(ほほ)/頬(ほお): the standard word for cheek
  • ほっぺ: an even more casual/childish variant
  • 頬(ほほ)っぺた: variant spelling showing the etymological connection
