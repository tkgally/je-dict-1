副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)

ふくしゅうにゅう
noun
side income; extra earnings; supplementary income
1. side income; extra earnings; supplementary income
Income earned in addition to one's main salary or primary source of income. Includes money from side jobs, investments, freelance work, or any other secondary earning activity.
副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)を得(え)たい。
I want to earn some side income.
ブログで月(つき)に数万円(すうまんえん)の副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)がある。
I earn a side income of tens of thousands of yen per month from my blog.
副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)が年間(ねんかん)20万円(まんえん)を超(こ)えると確定申告(かくていしんこく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
If your side income exceeds 200,000 yen per year, you need to file a tax return.

Income earned outside of one's primary job. The topic of 副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう) has become increasingly popular in Japan as more people look for ways to supplement their salary through side jobs (副業(ふくぎょう)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)を得(え)る: to earn side income
  • 副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう)を稼(かせ)ぐ: to make extra money
  • 月(つき)〜円(えん)の副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう): side income of ~ yen per month
  • 安定(あんてい)した副収入(ふくしゅうにゅう): stable side income

SOURCES OF SIDE INCOME:

  • 副業(ふくぎょう): side job, side business
  • 投資(とうし): investment income
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)収入(しゅうにゅう): rental income
  • アフィリエイト: affiliate marketing

RELATED TERMS:

  • 副業(ふくぎょう): side job — the work itself, rather than the income it generates
  • 本業(ほんぎょう): main job, primary occupation
  • 不労所得(ふろうしょとく): unearned income, passive income
