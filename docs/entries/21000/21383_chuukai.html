1. to mediate; to act as an intermediary; to broker
To act as a go-between to facilitate a deal, agreement, or resolution between two parties. Common in real estate, business, and diplomacy.
不動産(ふどうさん)を仲介(ちゅうかい)する。
To broker a real estate deal.
友人(ゆうじん)が二人(ふたり)の間(あいだ)を仲介(ちゅうかい)してくれた。
A friend mediated between the two of them.
この取引(とりひき)は専門(せんもん)の業者(ぎょうしゃ)が仲介(ちゅうかい)しているので安心(あんしん)してください。
A specialized broker is handling this transaction, so please rest assured.

Very commonly used in real estate (不動産(ふどうさん)仲介(ちゅうかい)) and business contexts. The noun form 仲介(ちゅうかい) is often used as a modifier meaning "intermediary" or "brokerage."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仲介(ちゅうかい)業者(ぎょうしゃ): broker, intermediary agent
  • 仲介(ちゅうかい)手数料(てすうりょう): brokerage fee, commission
  • 仲介(ちゅうかい)役(やく): mediator role
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)仲介(ちゅうかい): real estate brokerage

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 媒介(ばいかい)する: to mediate — more formal/technical, also used in science for disease transmission
  • 調停(ちょうてい)する: to arbitrate — legal context, formal dispute resolution
  • 取(と)り持(も)つ: to mediate — more informal, acting as a go-between in personal relationships
