1. refining; smelting; purifying (metals)
The process of purifying metals by removing impurities, typically through heating or chemical processes.
銅(どう)の精錬(せいれん)が行(おこな)われた。
Copper refining was carried out.
この地域(ちいき)は古(ふる)くから金(きん)の精錬(せいれん)で栄(さか)えた。
This region prospered from gold refining since ancient times.
精錬(せいれん)技術(ぎじゅつ)の向上(こうじょう)により、より純度(じゅんど)の高(たか)い金属(きんぞく)が得(え)られるようになった。
Advances in refining technology have made it possible to obtain metals of higher purity.

A technical term for the process of purifying metals. Composed of 精(せい) (purify, refine) + 錬(れん) (temper, forge). Used in industrial, historical, and scientific contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精錬(せいれん)する: to refine, to smelt
  • 精錬(せいれん)所(じょ)/精錬(せいれん)工場(こうじょう): refinery, smelting plant
  • 精錬(せいれん)技術(ぎじゅつ): refining technology
  • 銅(どう)の精錬(せいれん): copper smelting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 製錬(せいれん): smelting — same reading, used specifically for extracting metal from ore; often used interchangeably
  • 精製(せいせい): refining, purification — broader; includes oil, sugar, chemicals
  • 鍛錬(たんれん): tempering, training — physical or metal forging; also used figuratively for self-discipline
  • 冶金(やきん): metallurgy — the science of working with metals

NOTE ON KANJI:
精錬(せいれん) and 製錬(せいれん) are often confused. Strictly, 製錬(せいれん) refers to extracting metal from raw ore, while 精錬(せいれん) refers to further purifying already-extracted metal.
