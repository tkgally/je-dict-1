1. to recover; to get better (health, economy, condition)
To return to a normal or good state after illness, decline, or damage. Often intransitive.
体力(たいりょく)が回復(かいふく)した。
My physical strength has recovered.
景気(けいき)が回復(かいふく)しつつある。
The economy is gradually recovering.
手術(しゅじゅつ)後(ご)、順調(じゅんちょう)に回復(かいふく)している。
After the surgery, the recovery is progressing smoothly.
2. to regain; to restore (something lost)
To get back something that was lost, such as trust, reputation, or strength. Transitive usage.
信頼(しんらい)を回復(かいふく)する。
To restore trust.
名誉(めいよ)を回復(かいふく)するために裁判(さいばん)を起(お)こした。
They filed a lawsuit to restore their honor.
失(うしな)った市場(しじょう)シェアを回復(かいふく)するのは容易(ようい)ではない。
It is not easy to regain lost market share.

Compound of 回(かい) (turn, revolve) + 復(ふく) (return). One of the most common words for recovery in Japanese, used for health, economics, systems, and abstract qualities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体力(たいりょく)回復(かいふく): physical recovery
  • 景気(けいき)回復(かいふく): economic recovery
  • 回復(かいふく)の見込(みこ)み: prospects for recovery
  • 早期(そうき)回復(かいふく): early recovery
  • 信頼(しんらい)回復(かいふく): restoring trust
  • 名誉(めいよ)回復(かいふく): restoration of honor

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive in sense 1 (景気(けいき)が回復(かいふく)する). Transitive in sense 2 (信頼(しんらい)を回復(かいふく)する).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 治(なお)る: to heal, to get better (everyday, for illness or injuries)
  • 復旧(ふっきゅう)する: to restore (infrastructure, services — after disaster)
  • 復活(ふっかつ)する: to revive, to come back (from complete absence or inactivity)
