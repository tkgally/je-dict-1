1. excessive intake; overconsumption
Consuming more of something (food, nutrients, medication, etc.) than is recommended or healthy.
塩分(えんぶん)の過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ)に注意(ちゅうい)する。
To be careful about excessive salt intake.
糖分(とうぶん)の過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ)は肥満(ひまん)の原因(げんいん)になる。
Excessive sugar intake leads to obesity.
サプリメントの過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ)は健康(けんこう)に害(がい)を及(およ)ぼすことがある。
Excessive intake of supplements can harm your health.

A compound of 過剰(かじょう) (excessive, surplus) and 摂取(せっしゅ) (intake, ingestion). Commonly used in health, nutrition, and medical contexts. Can refer to food, nutrients, medication, or substances.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塩分(えんぶん)の過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ): excessive salt intake
  • 糖分(とうぶん)の過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ): excessive sugar intake
  • カロリーの過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ): excessive calorie intake
  • 薬(くすり)の過剰摂取(かじょうせっしゅ): drug overdose

RELATED TERMS:

  • 摂取(せっしゅ): intake, ingestion — the neutral base word
  • 過剰(かじょう): excessive, surplus — also used in 過剰反応(かじょうはんのう) (overreaction)
  • 過量投与(かりょうとうよ): overdosage — a medical term for administering too much medication
