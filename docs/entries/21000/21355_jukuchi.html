熟知(じゅくち)

じゅくち
noun/verb
thorough knowledge; being well versed in
1. thorough knowledge; being well acquainted with; knowing inside out
Deep, thorough familiarity with a subject, place, or situation gained through extensive experience or study.
彼(かれ)はこの土地(とち)を熟知(じゅくち)している。
He knows this area inside and out.
法律(ほうりつ)を熟知(じゅくち)した弁護士(べんごし)に相談(そうだん)した。
I consulted a lawyer well versed in the law.
現場(げんば)の状況(じょうきょう)を熟知(じゅくち)している人(ひと)でなければ、この判断(はんだん)はできない。
Only someone with thorough knowledge of the situation on the ground could make this decision.

Implies knowledge gained through deep engagement, not just superficial familiarity. The 熟(じゅく) component means "mature" or "thorough," giving the word a sense of mastery through experience. Often used to describe professionals or experts who have internalized their knowledge.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事情(じじょう)を熟知(じゅくち)する: to know the circumstances thoroughly
  • 業界(ぎょうかい)を熟知(じゅくち)する: to know the industry inside out
  • 熟知(じゅくち)した上(うえ)で: with thorough knowledge of
  • 内情(ないじょう)を熟知(じゅくち)している: to know the inside story

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精通(せいつう): being well versed — similar depth, often used for academic or technical expertise
  • 知(し)り尽(つ)くす: to know everything about — more colloquial, emphasizes completeness
  • 博識(はくしき): wide knowledge, erudition — emphasizes breadth rather than depth
  • 通暁(つうぎょう): mastery, thorough understanding — more formal and literary
