見開(みひら)き

みひらき
noun
two-page spread; facing pages
1. two-page spread; facing pages
A pair of facing pages in a book, magazine, or newspaper that are viewed together as a single visual unit. Commonly used in publishing, manga, and design contexts.
見開(みひら)きで地図(ちず)が載(の)っている。
There is a map across the two-page spread.
この漫画(まんが)は見開(みひら)きのページが迫力(はくりょく)がある。
The two-page spreads in this manga are powerful.
雑誌(ざっし)の見開(みひら)き広告(こうこく)は目(め)を引(ひ)くデザインになっている。
The two-page spread advertisements in the magazine have eye-catching designs.

From the verb 見開(みひら)く (to open wide), 見開(みひら)き refers to the view of two facing pages when a book or magazine is laid flat. It is a standard publishing and printing term, and also widely used among manga readers to describe dramatic full-spread illustrations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見開(みひら)きページ (two-page spread)
  • 見開(みひら)き広告(こうこく) (double-page advertisement)
  • 見開(みひら)きで掲載(けいさい)する (to publish across a two-page spread)
  • 見開(みひら)きイラスト (double-page illustration)

USAGE:
In manga, a 見開(みひら)き scene is often used for dramatic climactic moments — a battle scene, a landscape reveal, or an emotional turning point. The term is also common in book design and layout discussions.
