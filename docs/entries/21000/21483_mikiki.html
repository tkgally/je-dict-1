1. things seen and heard; experience; observations; knowledge gained firsthand
Knowledge and impressions acquired through direct personal observation, such as what one witnessed and learned during travel or fieldwork.
旅行(りょこう)での見聞(みき)きを話(はな)した。
I talked about what I saw and heard on the trip.
見聞(みき)きを広(ひろ)げるために海外(かいがい)に出(で)かけた。
I went abroad to broaden my horizons.
現地(げんち)で見聞(みき)きしたことをもとに、報告書(ほうこくしょ)をまとめた。
I compiled a report based on what I saw and heard on site.

Combines 見(み)る (to see) and 聞(き)く (to hear) to refer to firsthand experience and knowledge gained through direct observation. As a noun, it means the things one has seen and heard; as a verb (見聞(みき)きする), it means to see and hear, to observe directly.

USAGE:

  • 見聞(みき)きを広(ひろ)げる is a very common expression meaning "to broaden one's experience/horizons"
  • The kanji compound 見聞(けんぶん) is a more formal reading with the same meaning
  • Often used when recounting travel experiences or field observations

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見聞(みき)きを広(ひろ)げる: to broaden one's experience
  • 見聞(みき)きしたこと: things one has seen and heard
  • 見聞(みき)きを深(ふか)める: to deepen one's knowledge through experience
  • 見聞(みき)き録(ろく): a record of observations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経験(けいけん): experience — broader, includes all types of experience
  • 見聞(けんぶん): the on'yomi reading; more formal and literary
  • 体験(たいけん): hands-on experience — emphasizes personal participation
