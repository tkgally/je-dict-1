改名(かいめい)

かいめい
noun
changing one's name
1. changing one's name; renaming
The act of legally or officially changing a person's name, or the renaming of a place, organization, or product.
結婚(けっこん)を機(き)に改名(かいめい)した。
I changed my name upon getting married.
改名(かいめい)の手続(てつづ)きには家庭裁判所(かていさいばんしょ)の許可(きょか)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Changing one's name requires permission from the family court.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は不祥事(ふしょうじ)の後(あと)、社名(しゃめい)を改名(かいめい)してイメージを一新(いっしん)しようとした。
After the scandal, that company tried to refresh its image by changing its name.

Covers changing a personal name, a company name, a place name, or any other formal name. In Japan, legal name changes for individuals require approval from a family court (家庭裁判所(かていさいばんしょ)) and are granted only for justifiable reasons.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改名(かいめい)する: to change one's name
  • 改名(かいめい)手続(てつづ)き: name-change procedures
  • 改名(かいめい)届(とど)け: name-change notification

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 改姓(かいせい): changing one's surname — specifically the family name, typically upon marriage or divorce
  • 芸名(げいめい): stage name — a name adopted for professional use in entertainment
  • 襲名(しゅうめい): name succession — inheriting a traditional name, common in kabuki and traditional arts
