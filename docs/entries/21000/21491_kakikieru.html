1. to vanish completely; to disappear without a trace
声(こえ)が風(かぜ)にかき消(き)えた。
The voice was drowned out by the wind.
彼(かれ)の姿(すがた)は闇(やみ)の中(なか)にかき消(き)えた。
His figure vanished into the darkness.
希望(きぼう)の光(ひかり)がかき消(き)えたかのように、彼女(かのじょ)はうつむいたまま黙(だま)り込(こ)んだ。
As if the light of hope had been snuffed out, she hung her head and fell silent.

A compound verb from かき (an intensifying prefix) + 消(き)える (to disappear). The かき prefix adds emphasis, indicating that something vanishes completely, suddenly, or is overwhelmed and obliterated.

USAGE:

  • Often used for sounds being drowned out by other sounds
  • Also used for people or things vanishing from sight
  • Literary and vivid; adds dramatic effect compared to plain 消(き)える
  • The transitive form かき消(け)す means "to drown out" or "to obliterate"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 声(こえ)がかき消(き)える: a voice is drowned out
  • 姿(すがた)がかき消(き)える: a figure vanishes
  • 音(おと)がかき消(き)える: a sound is drowned out
  • 闇(やみ)にかき消(き)える: to vanish into darkness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 消(き)える: to disappear — neutral, without the dramatic emphasis
  • かき消(け)す: to drown out, obliterate — the transitive counterpart
  • 消(き)え失(う)せる: to vanish completely — similar emphasis, but rarer
