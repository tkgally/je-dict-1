1. detention; custody; confinement
The legal holding of a person in custody, either as a criminal penalty (misdemeanor detention of 1-29 days) or during investigation before trial.

A legal term for holding someone in custody. In Japanese criminal law, 拘留(こ��りゅう) as a penalty specifically refers to a minor form of imprisonment lasting 1 to 29 days, used for misdemeanor offenses. It is also used more broadly for pre-trial detention.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 拘留(こうりゅう)する: to detain
  • 拘留(こ��りゅう)期間(きかん): detention period
  • 拘留(こうりゅう)を延長(えんちょう)する: to extend detention
  • 拘留(こうりゅう)中(ちゅう): during detention

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勾留(こうりゅう): pretrial detention — same reading but different kanji; specifically refers to detention during criminal investigation (before trial)
  • 拘束(こうそく): restraint, confinement — broader term for restricting someone's freedom
  • 留置(りゅうち): holding in custody — detention at a police station specifically
  • 収監(しゅうかん): imprisonment — being placed in a prison facility
