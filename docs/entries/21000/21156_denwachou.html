電話帳(でんわちょう)

でんわちょう
noun
phone book; telephone directory
1. phone book; telephone directory; contacts list
A book or digital list containing telephone numbers and contact information. Can refer to either a printed telephone directory or the contacts list on a mobile phone.
電話帳(でんわちょう)で番号(ばんごう)を調(しら)べた。
I looked up the number in the phone book.
スマホの電話帳(でんわちょう)に登録(とうろく)しておく。
I'll save it in my phone's contacts.
昔(むかし)はどの家庭(かてい)にも分厚(ぶあつ)い電話帳(でんわちょう)があったが、今(いま)ではほとんど見(み)かけない。
Every household used to have a thick phone book, but you hardly see them anymore.

Composed of 電話(でんわ) (telephone) + 帳(ちょう) (notebook, register). Traditionally referred to the printed telephone directory (like the Yellow Pages), but now more commonly refers to the contacts list on a smartphone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電話帳(でんわちょう)に載(の)っている: to be listed in the phone book
  • 電話帳(でんわちょう)に登録(とうろく)する: to register in one's contacts
  • 電話帳(でんわちょう)を整理(せいり)する: to organize one's contacts

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連絡先(れんらくさき): contact information — more general, not limited to phone numbers
  • 住所録(じゅうしょろく): address book — includes physical addresses
  • タウンページ: Town Pages — Japan's yellow pages directory (brand name)
