1. to appear in a photo or video unintentionally; to be caught on camera
Describes something or someone appearing in a photograph, video, or reflection, often unintentionally or as a background element.
知(し)らない人(ひと)が写(うつ)り込(こ)んでいる。
A stranger has appeared in the photo.
背景(はいけい)に車(くるま)が写(うつ)り込(こ)んでしまった。
A car ended up appearing in the background.
防犯(ぼうはん)カメラに不審者(ふしんしゃ)が写(うつ)り込(こ)んでいたことが判明(はんめい)した。
It was discovered that a suspicious person had been caught on the security camera.

A compound verb from 写(うつ)る (to be photographed, to appear in an image) + 込(こ)む (into). The ～込(こ)む suffix adds the nuance of something entering or getting into the frame.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 写真(しゃしん)に写(うつ)り込(こ)む: to appear in a photograph
  • 背景(はいけい)に写(うつ)り込(こ)む: to appear in the background
  • カメラに写(うつ)り込(こ)む: to be caught on camera
  • 鏡(かがみ)に写(うつ)り込(こ)む: to appear in the mirror

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 写(うつ)る: to be photographed, to appear — more neutral; simply means appearing in an image without the nuance of unintentionally getting into the frame
  • 映(うつ)り込(こ)む: to be reflected into — uses the kanji for reflection; often for reflections in glass, water, or mirrors

USAGE:
Often used with ～てしまう to express the unintended nature of the appearance. Also common in discussions of privacy and photography etiquette in Japan.
