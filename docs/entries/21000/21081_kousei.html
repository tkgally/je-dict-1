校正(こうせい)

こうせい
noun
proofreading; calibration
1. proofreading; correction of text
Checking written text for errors in spelling, grammar, punctuation, and formatting, and marking corrections. A standard step in the publishing and printing process.
原稿(げんこう)の校正(こうせい)をする。
To proofread a manuscript.
校正(こうせい)で誤字(ごじ)を見(み)つけた。
I found a typo during proofreading.
出版(しゅっぱん)の前(まえ)に何度(なんど)も校正(こうせい)を重(かさ)ねる必要(ひつよう)がある。
It's necessary to go through multiple rounds of proofreading before publication.
2. calibration; adjustment of instruments
Adjusting and verifying the accuracy of measuring instruments or equipment against a known standard.
計器(けいき)の校正(こうせい)を行(おこな)う。
To calibrate an instrument.
年(ねん)に一度(いちど)、機器(きき)の校正(こうせい)をしなければならない。
The equipment must be calibrated once a year.
正確(せいかく)な測定(そくてい)のためには定期的(ていきてき)な校正(こうせい)が欠(か)かせない。
Regular calibration is essential for accurate measurements.

Has two distinct uses: proofreading text (the more common everyday meaning) and calibrating instruments (technical). Both senses share the core idea of checking against a standard and making corrections.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 校正(こうせい)をする: to proofread / to calibrate
  • 校正(こうせい)刷(ず)り: proof sheet, galley proof
  • 校正(こうせい)を重(かさ)ねる: to go through multiple rounds of proofreading
  • 校正(こうせい)作業(さぎょう): proofreading work
  • 校正(こうせい)証明書(しょうめいしょ): calibration certificate

HOMOPHONES:

Several common words share the reading こうせい:

  • 公正(こうせい): fairness, impartiality
  • 構成(こうせい): composition, structure
  • 更生(こうせい): rehabilitation, reform
  • 恒星(こうせい): fixed star

ETYMOLOGY:
校(こう) (check, compare) + 正(せい) (correct) — "check and correct."
