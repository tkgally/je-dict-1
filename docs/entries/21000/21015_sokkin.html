即金(そっきん)

そっきん
noun
cash payment; spot cash
1. cash payment; spot cash; immediate payment
Payment made in full and in cash at the time of purchase, without installments, credit, or deferred payment.
即金(そっきん)で買(か)う。
To buy with cash on the spot.
即金(そっきん)払(ばら)いなら少(すこ)し安(やす)くしますよ。
If you pay in cash, I'll give you a small discount.
中古車(ちゅうこしゃ)を即金(そっきん)で購入(こうにゅう)したので、ローンの心配(しんぱい)がない。
I bought the used car with cash up front, so I don't have to worry about loan payments.

Composed of 即(そく) (immediate) + 金(きん) (money, cash). Emphasizes that payment is made on the spot without any delayed or installment payment arrangement. Often used in buying and selling contexts, particularly for larger purchases.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即金(そっきん)で買(か)う: to buy with cash on the spot
  • 即金(そっきん)払(ばら)い: cash payment, paying in full
  • 即金(そっきん)買取(かいとり): cash purchase (of goods for resale)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 現金(げんきん): cash — the general term for physical money; does not imply immediacy
  • 一括払(いっかつばら)い: lump-sum payment — paying the full amount at once, but not necessarily in cash
  • 分割払(ぶんかつばら)い: installment payment — the opposite; paying in multiple smaller amounts over time
