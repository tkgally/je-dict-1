1. getting on and off; boarding and alighting
The act of getting on and off vehicles such as trains, buses, or cars. Used for the combined action of boarding and disembarking.
乗(の)り降(お)りは素早(すばや)く。
Board and alight quickly.
バスの乗(の)り降(お)りに注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful when getting on and off the bus.
お年寄(としよ)りの乗(の)り降(お)りをお手伝(てつだ)いしました。
I helped an elderly person getting on and off.
この駅(えき)は段差(だんさ)がなく、車椅子(くるまいす)での乗(の)り降(お)りがしやすい。
This station has no steps, making it easy to board and alight in a wheelchair.

Refers specifically to the combined action of getting on and off vehicles. Used as a compound noun from 乗(の)る (to ride/board) + 降(お)りる (to get off/alight).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乗(の)り降(お)りが多(おお)い: frequent boarding and alighting
  • 乗(の)り降(お)りが楽(らく): easy to get on and off
  • 乗(の)り降(お)りに便利(べんり): convenient for boarding and alighting
  • 乗(の)り降(お)り自由(じゆう): free to get on and off (unlimited rides)

USAGE:
Often appears on signs and announcements at train stations and bus stops. The phrase 乗(の)り降(お)り自由(じゆう) is commonly used for day passes or unlimited-ride tickets.
