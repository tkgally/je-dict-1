1. to refund; to return money; to give a refund
To give back money that was paid, typically because of a cancellation, defective product, or overpayment.
全額(ぜんがく)返金(へんきん)します。
We will give a full refund.
不良品(ふりょうひん)だったので返金(へんきん)してもらった。
It was a defective product, so I got a refund.
キャンセルした場合(ばあい)、手数料(てすうりょう)を引(ひ)いた金額(きんがく)が返金(へんきん)されます。
In case of cancellation, the amount minus the handling fee will be refunded.

Compound of 返(へん) (return) + 金(きん) (money). A practical term frequently encountered in shopping, online purchases, and customer service situations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全額(ぜんがく)返金(へんきん): full refund
  • 返金(へんきん)保証(ほしょう): money-back guarantee
  • 返金(へんきん)手続(てつづ)き: refund procedure
  • 返金(へんきん)対応(たいおう): refund handling
  • 返金(へんきん)を求(もと)める: to request a refund

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 払(はら)い戻(もど)し: refund, reimbursement (often used for insurance or deposits)
  • 返品(へんぴん)する: to return goods (returning the product itself, not the money)
  • 弁償(べんしょう)する: to compensate (paying for damage or loss)
