1. to reap; to harvest; to mow; to cut down
To cut and gather crops, grass, or other plants. Used literally for agricultural harvesting and lawn care.
稲(いね)を刈(か)り取(と)る。
To harvest the rice.
秋(あき)になると農家(のうか)は稲(いね)を刈(か)り取(と)る。
When autumn comes, farmers harvest the rice.
雑草(ざっそう)が伸(の)びすぎたので、週末(しゅうまつ)にまとめて刈(か)り取(と)った。
The weeds had grown too tall, so I mowed them all down over the weekend.

A compound verb from 刈(か)る (to mow, cut) + 取(と)る (to take). The 取(と)る component adds the nuance of taking or gathering what has been cut.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 稲(いね)を刈(か)り取(と)る: to harvest rice
  • 麦(むぎ)を刈(か)り取(と)る: to harvest wheat
  • 雑草(ざっそう)を刈(か)り取(と)る: to mow weeds
  • 芝生(しばふ)を刈(か)り取(と)る: to mow the lawn

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刈(か)る: to mow, to cut — simpler verb; focuses on the cutting action without the gathering nuance
  • 収穫(しゅうかく)する: to harvest — broader term for harvesting any crop; more formal
  • 摘(つ)み取(と)る: to pick, pluck — for hand-picking fruits, flowers, or tea leaves

FIGURATIVE USE:
Occasionally used figuratively to mean reaping consequences or benefits, similar to English "you reap what you sow."
