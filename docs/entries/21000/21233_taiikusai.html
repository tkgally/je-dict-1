体育祭(たいいくさい)

たいいくさい
noun
sports festival; athletic meet
1. sports festival; school athletic meet
An annual school event where students compete in various athletic events and team activities. Held mainly at middle schools and high schools, typically in autumn.
体育祭(たいいくさい)は十月(じゅうがつ)にある。
The sports festival is in October.
体育祭(たいいくさい)のリレーで一位(いちい)になった。
I came first in the relay at the sports festival.
今年(ことし)の体育祭(たいいくさい)は雨(あめ)で延期(えんき)になったが、翌週(よくしゅう)無事(ぶじ)に開催(かいさい)された。
This year's sports festival was postponed due to rain, but was successfully held the following week.

A major annual school event in Japanese education, alongside 文化祭(ぶんかさい) (cultural festival). Students are divided into teams (often by color — 赤組(あかぐみ), 白組(しろぐみ), etc.) and compete in various events.

COMMON EVENTS:

  • リレー: relay race
  • 綱引(つなひ)き: tug-of-war
  • 騎馬戦(きばせん): mock cavalry battle
  • 玉入(たまい)れ: ball toss
  • 応援合戦(おうえんがっせん): cheering competition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運動会(うんどうかい): sports day — used more for elementary schools and community events; 体育祭(たいいくさい) is used for middle and high schools
  • 文化祭(ぶんかさい): cultural festival — the arts-focused counterpart

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
The 体育祭(たいいくさい) is one of the most anticipated school events. Students practice for weeks in advance, and families often come to watch. The emphasis is on teamwork and school spirit rather than individual athletic achievement.
