大群(たいぐん)

たいぐん
noun
large group; swarm; horde
1. large group; swarm; horde; throng
A very large number of people, animals, or things gathered or moving together.
イワシの大群(たいぐん)が現(あらわ)れた。
A huge school of sardines appeared.
バッタの大群(たいぐん)が農地(のうち)を襲(おそ)った。
A swarm of locusts attacked the farmland.
観光(かんこう)シーズンになると、大群(たいぐん)の観光客(かんこうきゃく)がこの島(しま)を訪(おとず)れる。
When tourist season arrives, throngs of tourists visit this island.

Composed of 大(たい) (large) + 群(ぐん) (group, flock). Used for impressively large numbers of living things — animals, insects, people — or sometimes objects. The word emphasizes the sheer scale of the group.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 蝶(ちょう)の大群(たいぐん): a swarm of butterflies
  • 大群(たいぐん)で押(お)し寄(よ)せる: to swarm in; to descend in large numbers
  • 大群(たいぐん)が発生(はっせい)する: a large group/swarm appears

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 群(む)れ: group; flock; herd — more neutral in size
  • 集団(しゅうだん): group; mass — more organized, often used for people
  • 群衆(ぐんしゅう): crowd; throng — specifically a crowd of people
