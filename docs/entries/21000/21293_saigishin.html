猜疑心(さいぎしん)

さいぎしん
noun
suspiciousness; distrust; paranoia
1. suspiciousness; distrust; paranoia
A tendency to suspect and distrust others' motives. Describes a deep-seated, often irrational mistrust.
猜疑心(さいぎしん)が強(つよ)い人(ひと)だ。
That person is very suspicious of others.
猜疑心(さいぎしん)から誰(だれ)も信(しん)じられなくなった。
Because of suspiciousness, they couldn't trust anyone anymore.
長年(ながねん)の裏切(うらぎ)りが彼(かれ)の心(こころ)に深(ふか)い猜疑心(さいぎしん)を植(う)え付(つ)けた。
Years of betrayal planted deep suspiciousness in his heart.

A literary word describing a deep, persistent tendency to distrust others. Composed of 猜疑(さいぎ) (suspicion, jealousy) + 心(しん) (heart, mind). The character 猜(さい) specifically implies jealous suspicion.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 猜疑心(さいぎしん)が強(つよ)い: to be very suspicious
  • 猜疑心(さいぎしん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor suspicion
  • 猜疑心(さいぎしん)に駆(か)られる: to be driven by suspicion
  • 猜疑心(さいぎしん)の塊(かたまり): a bundle of suspicion (very distrustful person)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 疑(うたが)い: doubt, suspicion — everyday word for doubt or suspicion
  • 不信感(ふしんかん): distrust, mistrust — more neutral; can refer to specific distrust
  • 警戒心(けいかいしん): wariness, vigilance — more positive; implies reasonable caution
  • 被害妄想(ひがいもうそう): persecution complex — stronger; implies delusion

REGISTER:
Appears in literature, psychology, and formal writing. In everyday conversation, 疑(うたが)い深(ぶか)い (suspicious, skeptical) is more natural.
