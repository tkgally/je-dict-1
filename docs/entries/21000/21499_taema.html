絶(た)え間(ま)

たえま
noun
pause; gap; interval; lull; break
1. pause; gap; interval; lull; break
A temporary interruption or gap in something continuous. Most commonly used in the negative form 絶え間なく (without pause, incessantly).
絶(た)え間(ま)なく雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っている。
It's raining without pause.
工事(こうじ)の騒音(そうおん)が絶(た)え間(ま)なく続(つづ)いている。
The construction noise continues without a break.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)びらが絶(た)え間(ま)なく散(ち)り続(つづ)け、道(みち)はまるでピンクの絨毯(じゅうたん)のようだった。
Cherry blossom petals kept falling without pause, and the path looked like a pink carpet.

The noun 絶(た)え間(ま) means a pause or break in something continuous, but it is most commonly used in the adverbial form 絶(た)え間(ま)なく (without pause, ceaselessly). In fact, 絶(た)え間(ま)なく is far more common than the bare noun.

USAGE:

  • 絶(た)え間(ま)なく (ceaselessly, without interruption) is the standard usage
  • 絶(た)え間(ま)ない (unceasing, constant) is the adjective form
  • Emphasizes that something continues without any break or pause

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶(た)え間(ま)なく続(つづ)く: to continue without pause
  • 絶(た)え間(ま)ない努力(どりょく): unceasing effort
  • 絶(た)え間(ま)なく流(なが)れる: to flow without stopping
  • 絶(た)え間(ま)ない騒音(そうおん): incessant noise

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 休(やす)みなく: without rest — simpler and more casual
  • ひっきりなしに: incessantly — emphasizes frequency of repeated events
  • 間断(かんだん)なく: without interruption — more formal/literary
  • 途切(とぎ)れなく: without breaking off — emphasizes continuity
