1. to look blank, to be dazed, to be stunned
To have a vacant, blank expression due to surprise, shock, or incomprehension. The mouth often hangs slightly open.
ぽかんとした顔(かお)で見(み)ていた。
He was watching with a blank expression.
突然(とつぜん)の知(し)らせにぽかんとしてしまった。
I was stunned by the sudden news.
説明(せつめい)が難(むずか)しすぎて、学生(がくせい)たちは皆(みな)ぽかんとしていた。
The explanation was too difficult, and the students all looked blank.
2. to space out, to be absent-minded, to be idle
To sit or stand around doing nothing with a vacant expression, not paying attention or being unproductive.
ぽかんとしていないで手伝(てつだ)ってよ。
Stop spacing out and help me.
仕事(しごと)もしないでぽかんとしている。
He's just sitting around doing nothing.
口(くち)をぽかんと開(あ)けて空(そら)を眺(なが)めていた。
He was gazing at the sky with his mouth hanging open.

A mimetic word (onomatopoeia). ぽかん suggests a gaping, vacant state — like a mouth hanging open or a mind going blank. The と is the quotative/manner particle connecting the mimetic word to する.

USAGE:
The form ぽかんとした + noun is common as a modifier: ぽかんとした表情(ひょうじょう) (a blank expression). Also used with 口(くち)をぽかんと開(あ)ける (to have one's mouth hanging open). Expressive and slightly humorous in tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぽかんとした顔(かお): a blank face
  • ぽかんとした表情(ひょうじょう): a vacant expression
  • ぽかんと口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to have one's mouth agape
  • ぽかんと立(た)っている: to stand around vacantly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぼんやりする: to be absent-minded, to space out — broader term for being vague or unfocused
  • きょとんとする: to look puzzled — suggests innocent bewilderment rather than blankness
  • あっけにとられる: to be dumbfounded — stronger surprise, often at something unexpected
