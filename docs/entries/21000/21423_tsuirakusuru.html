1. to crash, to plummet, to fall from a height
To fall suddenly from a great height to the ground. Used especially for aircraft, but also for people falling from buildings or cliffs.
飛行機(ひこうき)が墜落(ついらく)した。
The airplane crashed.
ヘリコプターが山(やま)に墜落(ついらく)して炎上(えんじょう)した。
The helicopter crashed into a mountain and burst into flames.
小型(こがた)旅客機(りょかくき)が離陸(りりく)直後(ちょくご)に墜落(ついらく)し、乗客(じょうきゃく)全員(ぜんいん)が救助(きゅうじょ)された。
A small passenger plane crashed immediately after takeoff, and all passengers were rescued.

A compound of 墜(つい) (to fall) and 落(らく) (to drop). Primarily used for aircraft crashes and falls from high places. The word carries a sense of sudden, violent, uncontrolled descent, unlike a gentle or planned descent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飛行機(ひこうき)が墜落(ついらく)する: an airplane crashes
  • 墜落(ついらく)事故(じこ): crash accident
  • 墜落(ついらく)現場(げんば): crash site
  • 墜落(ついらく)原因(げんいん): cause of a crash

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落下(らっか)する: to fall, to drop — a more general and neutral term for falling; can apply to any object
  • 転落(てんらく)する: to fall, to tumble down — implies rolling or tumbling, often from a ledge or stairs
  • 不時着(ふじちゃく)する: to make an emergency landing — a controlled crash landing, less catastrophic
