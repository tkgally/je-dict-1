機能的(きのうてき)

きのうてき
na-adjective
functional; practical; efficient
1. functional; practical; efficient in design or operation
Designed with an emphasis on practical usefulness rather than appearance. Describes things that work well and serve their purpose efficiently.
機能的(きのうてき)なデザインだ。
It's a functional design.
このキッチンは機能的(きのうてき)に作(つく)られている。
This kitchen is designed to be functional.
見(み)た目(め)はシンプルだが、非常(ひじょう)に機能的(きのうてき)なバッグで旅行(りょこう)に重宝(ちょうほう)している。
It looks simple, but it's an extremely functional bag that I find useful for travel.

Emphasizes practical utility and efficiency in design. Often used to praise products, spaces, or systems that prioritize usability over aesthetics — though the two are not mutually exclusive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 機能的(きのうてき)なデザイン: functional design
  • 機能的(きのうてき)な間取(まど)り: functional floor plan
  • 機能的(きのうてき)な服(ふく): functional clothing
  • 機能的(きのうてき)に優(すぐ)れた: functionally superior

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実用的(じつようてき): practical — emphasizes real-world usability
  • 合理的(ごうりてき): rational, reasonable — emphasizes logical efficiency
  • 効率的(こうりつてき): efficient — emphasizes doing more with less

FORMATION:
機能(きのう) (function) + 的(てき) (adjective-forming suffix)
