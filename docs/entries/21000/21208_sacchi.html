1. sensing; perceiving; detecting
The act of becoming aware of something through intuition or keen observation, often before it becomes obvious. Implies picking up on subtle cues rather than being told directly.
危険(きけん)を察知(さっち)する。
To sense danger.
動物(どうぶつ)は地震(じしん)を事前(じぜん)に察知(さっち)すると言(い)われている。
Animals are said to sense earthquakes in advance.
彼女(かのじょ)の表情(ひょうじょう)から何(なに)か問題(もんだい)があることを察知(さっち)した。
I sensed from her expression that something was wrong.

A する verb (察知(さっち)する). Composed of 察(さつ) (guess, sense) + 知(ち) (know). Implies becoming aware of something through intuition, subtle observation, or instinct rather than through direct information.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 危険(きけん)を察知(さっち)する: to sense danger
  • 異変(いへん)を察知(さっち)する: to detect something unusual
  • 気配(けはい)を察知(さっち)する: to sense a presence
  • いち早(はや)く察知(さっち)する: to be quick to detect

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 感知(かんち): detection — more technical, often used for sensors and devices detecting physical phenomena
  • 察(さっ)する: to sense, to surmise — the underlying verb, slightly more literary
  • 気(き)づく: to notice — more colloquial and general, without the implication of intuitive perception
