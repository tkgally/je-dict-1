こういう

こういう
adjective

1. this kind of; like this; such
A demonstrative expression that refers to something near the speaker or just mentioned, used to modify a noun as 'this type of' or 'this sort of.'
こういう時(とき)はどうすればいい？
What should I do at a time like this?
こういう話(はなし)は初(はじ)めて聞(き)いた。
This is the first time I've heard this kind of story.
こういう細(こま)かいところに気(き)を配(くば)れる人(ひと)は信頼(しんらい)される。
People who pay attention to small details like this earn trust.

A demonstrative expression meaning "this kind of" or "like this," referring to something near the speaker or just mentioned. It is the colloquial equivalent of このような.

USAGE:

  • Always modifies a noun: こういう + noun
  • Used in everyday conversation; このような is the more formal equivalent
  • Part of the こ/そ/あ/ど demonstrative series: こういう (this kind), そういう (that kind), ああいう (that kind, distant), どういう (what kind)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • こういう場合(ばあい): in this kind of case
  • こういう時(とき): at a time like this
  • こういうこと: this kind of thing
  • こういう人(ひと): this type of person
  • こういうもの: things like this

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • そういう: that kind of (referring to something the listener mentioned)
  • ああいう: that kind of (referring to something distant or previously observed)
  • どういう: what kind of (question form)
  • このような: this kind of (more formal equivalent)
