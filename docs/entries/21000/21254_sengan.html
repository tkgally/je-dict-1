1. face washing; cleansing one's face
The act of washing one's face, especially as part of a daily skincare or hygiene routine.
毎朝(まいあさ)洗顔(せんがん)する。
I wash my face every morning.
洗顔(せんがん)の後(あと)に化粧水(けしょうすい)をつける。
I apply toner after washing my face.
肌荒(はだあ)れを防(ふせ)ぐには、正(ただ)しい洗顔(せんがん)が大切(たいせつ)だと言(い)われている。
It is said that proper face washing is important for preventing rough skin.

Refers specifically to washing the face as part of daily hygiene or skincare. In Japanese beauty culture, the face-washing step is considered fundamental to skincare and is often discussed in detail.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洗顔(せんがん)する: to wash one's face
  • 洗顔料(せんがんりょう): face wash (product)
  • 洗顔(せんがん)フォーム: cleansing foam
  • 洗顔(せんがん)ネット: face-washing net (for lathering)
  • 朝(あさ)洗顔(せんがん): morning face wash

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 洗面(せんめん): washing one's face and hands — broader term that also covers hand washing; often used in compound 洗面所(せんめんじょ) (washroom)
  • クレンジング: cleansing — specifically removing makeup, often done before 洗顔(せんがん) in a double-cleanse routine
