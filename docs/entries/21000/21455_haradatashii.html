1. irritating, infuriating, maddening, vexing
Describes a situation, behavior, or state of affairs that provokes anger or deep frustration. Emphasizes the speaker's emotional response of rising anger.
腹立(はらだ)たしい気持(きも)ちになった。
I felt irritated.
何度(なんど)言(い)っても聞(き)かないのは腹立(はらだ)たしい。
It's infuriating that they won't listen no matter how many times you tell them.
自分(じぶん)の無力(むりょく)さが腹立(はらだ)たしくてたまらなかった。
I found my own powerlessness unbearably frustrating.

Derived from 腹(はら)が立(た)つ (to get angry, literally "the belly rises") with the adjective-forming suffix ～しい. The word describes the feeling of anger welling up inside.

USAGE:
Often used as a predicate (腹立(はらだ)たしい) or as a modifier (腹立(はらだ)たしい思(おも)い). The adverb form 腹立(はらだ)たしく is also common. Slightly more literary and restrained than 腹(はら)が立(た)つ or 頭(あたま)にくる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腹立(はらだ)たしい思(おも)い: a feeling of frustration
  • 腹立(はらだ)たしい気持(きも)ち: irritated feelings
  • 腹立(はらだ)たしくてたまらない: unbearably infuriating
  • 腹立(はらだ)たしい限(かぎ)りだ: extremely frustrating

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 腹(はら)が立(た)つ: to get angry — the verb phrase equivalent; more direct and conversational
  • 憎(にく)たらしい: detestable, hateful — focuses on the target being obnoxious rather than the speaker's anger
  • 悔(くや)しい: vexing, mortifying — anger mixed with regret or a sense of defeat
  • 苛立(いらだ)たしい: irritating — similar meaning but emphasizes restless irritation
