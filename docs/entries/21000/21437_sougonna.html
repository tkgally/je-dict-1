荘厳(そうごん)な

そうごんな
adjective
majestic; solemn; sublime
1. majestic, solemn, sublime, grand and dignified
Having an awe-inspiring, dignified quality that evokes reverence or deep respect. Often used for religious buildings, ceremonies, natural scenery, and music.
荘厳(そうごん)な雰囲気(ふんいき)に包(つつ)まれた。
We were enveloped in a solemn atmosphere.
大聖堂(だいせいどう)の中(なか)は荘厳(そうごん)な空気(くうき)に満(み)ちていた。
The inside of the cathedral was filled with a solemn air.
朝日(あさひ)に照(て)らされた山脈(さんみゃく)は荘厳(そうごん)な美(うつく)しさで、見(み)る者(もの)すべてを圧倒(あっとう)した。
The mountain range illuminated by the morning sun was of sublime beauty, overwhelming all who saw it.

A compound of 荘(そう) (solemn, dignified) and 厳(ごん) (strict, severe). In Buddhist contexts, 荘厳(しょうごん) is also read しょうごん, meaning to adorn or decorate a Buddhist altar. The reading そうごん carries the general meaning of majesty and solemnity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荘厳(そうごん)な雰囲気(ふんいき): solemn atmosphere
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な音楽(おんがく): majestic music
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な建物(たてもの): a grand, dignified building
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な儀式(ぎしき): a solemn ceremony

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 厳粛(げんしゅく)な: solemn, grave — emphasizes seriousness and formality rather than beauty
  • 崇高(すうこう)な: sublime, lofty — emphasizes moral or spiritual elevation
  • 壮大(そうだい)な: grand, magnificent — emphasizes large scale and impressiveness
