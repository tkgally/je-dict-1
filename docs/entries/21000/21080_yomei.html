余命(よめい)

よめい
noun
remaining life expectancy; time left to live
1. remaining life expectancy; time left to live
The estimated or expected amount of time a person has left to live, typically used in medical contexts when discussing terminal illness or prognosis.
余命(よめい)半年(はんとし)と宣告(せんこく)された。
I was told I had six months to live.
医師(いし)から余命(よめい)を告(つ)げられた。
The doctor told me how long I had left to live.
余命(よめい)わずかと知(し)り、残(のこ)りの時間(じかん)を家族(かぞく)と過(す)ごすことにした。
Learning that I had little time left, I decided to spend the remaining time with my family.

Refers to the estimated time a person has left to live. Most commonly encountered in medical contexts, particularly when doctors give a prognosis for terminal illness. A serious, weighty word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 余命(よめい)を宣告(せんこく)する: to give a life expectancy prognosis
  • 余命(よめい)半年(はんとし): six months to live
  • 余命(よめい)わずか: little time left to live
  • 余命(よめい)を告(つ)げる: to inform someone of their remaining lifespan
  • 平均(へいきん)余命(よめい): average life expectancy (statistical term)

USAGE:
In everyday use, 余命(よめい) almost always implies a terminal condition. The pattern 余命(よめい)＋time period＋と宣告(せんこく)される is extremely common in both real medical situations and in fiction. In demographic or actuarial contexts, 平均(へいきん)余命(よめい) refers to statistically calculated remaining lifespan.

ETYMOLOGY:
余(よ) (remaining) + 命(めい) (life) — literally "remaining life."
