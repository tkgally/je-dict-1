仮設(かせつ)

かせつ
noun; no-adjective
temporary; provisional; makeshift
1. temporary; provisional; makeshift setup
Something set up temporarily for a limited period, with the intention of being replaced or removed later. Commonly used for temporary buildings, facilities, and infrastructure.
仮設(かせつ)住宅(じゅうたく)に入居(にゅうきょ)する。
To move into temporary housing.
イベント会場(かいじょう)に仮設(かせつ)トイレが設置(せっち)された。
Portable toilets were set up at the event venue.
被災地(ひさいち)では仮設(かせつ)住宅(じゅうたく)での生活(せいかつ)が長期化(ちょうきか)している。
In the disaster-affected areas, life in temporary housing has been prolonged.

Used as a prefix-like modifier meaning "temporary" or "provisional." Most commonly appears before nouns describing structures or facilities that are intended to be temporary. Frequently seen in disaster relief and construction contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮設(かせつ)住宅(じゅうたく): temporary housing (especially disaster relief housing)
  • 仮設(かせつ)トイレ: portable toilet
  • 仮設(かせつ)舞台(ぶたい): temporary stage
  • 仮設(かせつ)建物(たてもの): temporary building
  • 仮設(かせつ)足場(あしば): scaffolding

CULTURAL NOTE:
仮設(かせつ)住宅(じゅうたく) is a term deeply associated with disaster response in Japan. After major earthquakes and other disasters, prefabricated temporary housing is provided for evacuees. The word has become particularly familiar after the 1995 Kobe earthquake and the 2011 Tohoku earthquake.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 臨時(りんじ): temporary, ad hoc — used for events, measures, and arrangements rather than physical structures
  • 仮(かり)の: temporary, provisional — more general and less formal
  • 仮説(かせつ): hypothesis — same reading but different kanji (説(せつ) instead of 設(せつ))
