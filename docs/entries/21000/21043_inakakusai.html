1. countrified; unsophisticated; provincial; hick-like
Describes someone or something that looks, sounds, or feels like it comes from the countryside, lacking urban sophistication. Often mildly derogatory.
田舎臭(いなかくさ)い服装(ふくそう)だ。
Those clothes look countrified.
そんな田舎臭(いなかくさ)いことを言(い)わないで。
Don't say such unsophisticated things.
都会(とかい)に出(で)てきたばかりのころは、自分(じぶん)が田舎臭(いなかくさ)く見(み)えないか気(き)にしていた。
When I first came to the city, I worried about whether I looked too countrified.

Composed of 田舎(いなか) (countryside, rural area) + 臭(くさ)い (smelling of, -ish). The ～臭(くさ)い suffix means "smelling of" or "having the quality of" and often carries a negative connotation.

THE ～臭(くさ)い SUFFIX:

Adds the meaning "smelling of" or "having the undesirable quality of":

  • 素人(しろうと)臭(くさ)い: amateurish
  • 年寄(としよ)り臭(くさ)い: old-fashioned, old-mannish
  • 面倒(めんどう)臭(くさ)い: bothersome, annoying
  • 胡散(うさん)臭(くさ)い: suspicious, fishy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 野暮(やぼ)ったい: unfashionable, unsophisticated — similar negativity but not specifically linked to rural origins
  • ダサい: lame, uncool — casual slang for anything considered uncool or outdated
  • 地味(じみ)な: plain, subdued — more neutral; describes something understated rather than unsophisticated

NUANCE:
Can be offensive when directed at a person, as it implies they lack the cultural awareness expected in urban settings. Used more casually when describing things (fashion, mannerisms) rather than people directly.
