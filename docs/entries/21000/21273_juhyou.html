樹氷(じゅひょう)

じゅひょう
noun
rime ice; frost-covered trees
1. rime ice on trees; frost-covered trees; ice monsters
A natural phenomenon where supercooled water droplets freeze on contact with tree branches, coating them in layers of white ice. Creates dramatic, sculptural formations that are a popular winter tourist attraction in Japan.
山頂(さんちょう)で美(うつく)しい樹氷(じゅひょう)を見(み)た。
I saw beautiful frost-covered trees at the summit.
蔵王(ざおう)の樹氷(じゅひょう)は冬(ふゆ)の名物(めいぶつ)だ。
The ice-covered trees of Zao are a famous winter attraction.
今年(ことし)は暖冬(だんとう)の影響(えいきょう)で樹氷(じゅひょう)の成長(せいちょう)が遅(おそ)いらしい。
Apparently the rime ice formations are growing slowly this year due to the mild winter.

A compound of 樹(じゅ) (tree) and 氷(ひょう) (ice). The most famous 樹氷(じゅひょう) in Japan are found at 蔵王(ざおう) in Yamagata Prefecture, where they are nicknamed 樹氷(じゅひょう)モンスター or "snow monsters" (アイスモンスター). They form when strong winds carry supercooled water droplets from the Sea of Japan onto mountain trees.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 樹氷(じゅひょう)を見(み)に行(い)く: to go see the frost-covered trees
  • 樹氷(じゅひょう)原(げん): a field of frost-covered trees
  • 樹氷(じゅひょう)ライトアップ: illuminated frost trees (tourist event)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 霧氷(むひょう): rime, frost on trees — a broader meteorological term that includes 樹氷(じゅひょう)
  • 氷柱(つらら): icicle — ice formed by dripping water
  • 霜(しも): frost — ice crystals on the ground or surfaces
