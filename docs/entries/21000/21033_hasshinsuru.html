1. to depart; to set off; to start moving
For a vehicle, aircraft, or vessel to begin moving from a stopped position. Used for cars, trains, planes, ships, etc.
信号(しんごう)が変(か)わり、車(くるま)が発進(はっしん)した。
The light changed and the car set off.
バスが発進(はっしん)するまでシートベルトを締(し)めてください。
Please fasten your seatbelt before the bus departs.
渋滞(じゅうたい)で車(くるま)が発進(はっしん)と停止(ていし)を繰(く)り返(かえ)していた。
In the traffic jam, cars kept stopping and starting.
2. to launch; to take off; to sortie
For military aircraft or spacecraft to take off on a mission. Often used in military and aviation contexts.
戦闘機(せんとうき)が基地(きち)から発進(はっしん)した。
Fighter jets launched from the base.
空母(くうぼ)から航空機(こうくうき)が次々(つぎつぎ)と発進(はっしん)した。
Aircraft launched one after another from the aircraft carrier.
スペースシャトルが発進(はっしん)準備(じゅんび)に入(はい)った。
The space shuttle entered launch preparations.

Composed of 発(はつ) (emit, depart) + 進(しん) (advance, proceed). Describes vehicles or craft beginning to move forward from a stationary position.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 車(くるま)が発進(はっしん)する: a car sets off
  • 戦闘機(せんとうき)が発進(はっしん)する: a fighter jet launches
  • 発進(はっしん)準備(じゅんび): launch preparations
  • 緊急(きんきゅう)発進(はっしん): scramble, emergency launch

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出発(しゅっぱつ)する: to depart — broader term for beginning a journey; used for people as well as vehicles
  • 発車(はっしゃ)する: to depart (of a train/bus) — specifically for trains and buses departing from a station
  • 発射(はっしゃ)する: to fire, launch — for projectiles and rockets being launched, not vehicles
  • 離陸(りりく)する: to take off — specifically for aircraft leaving the ground
