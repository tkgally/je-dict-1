1. skillfully; efficiently; deftly; adroitly
In a manner that shows skill and efficiency, getting things done smoothly without wasted effort.
手際(てぎわ)よく料理(りょうり)を作(つく)った。
She cooked skillfully.
手際(てぎわ)よく荷物(にもつ)をまとめた。
I packed my luggage efficiently.
ベテランのシェフが手際(てぎわ)よく食材(しょくざい)を切(き)り分(わ)けていく様子(ようす)に見入(みい)った。
I was captivated watching the experienced chef deftly cut and portion the ingredients.

The adverbial form of 手際(てぎわ)がいい (to be skillful/efficient). 手際(てぎわ) literally means "hand skill" or "dexterity" — the よく form turns it into an adverb meaning "in a skilled manner." Implies practiced, smooth execution without hesitation or wasted motion.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手際(てぎわ)よく片付(かたづ)ける: to tidy up efficiently
  • 手際(てぎわ)よくこなす: to handle skillfully
  • 手際(てぎわ)よく進(すす)める: to proceed efficiently
  • 手際(てぎわ)よく処理(しょり)する: to handle/process deftly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上手(じょうず)に: skillfully — more general, less emphasis on efficiency
  • 要領(ようりょう)よく: efficiently — emphasizes getting to the point, knowing the tricks
  • 器用(きよう)に: dexterously — focuses on manual skill
  • 手早(てばや)く: quickly and efficiently — emphasizes speed
