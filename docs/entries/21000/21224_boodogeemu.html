ボードゲーム

ぼーどげーむ
noun
board game
1. board game
A tabletop game played on a board, involving pieces, cards, or dice. Includes both traditional games and modern designer board games.
ボードゲームで遊(あそ)ぶ。
To play a board game.
家族(かぞく)でボードゲームを楽(たの)しんだ。
We enjoyed playing board games as a family.
最近(さいきん)はボードゲームカフェが増(ふ)えていて、友達(ともだち)と気軽(きがる)に遊(あそ)べる。
Board game cafes have been increasing recently, making it easy to play casually with friends.

A loanword from English 'board game.' Covers a wide range of tabletop games played on a board, from traditional games to modern hobby games.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ボードゲームで遊(あそ)ぶ: to play board games
  • ボードゲームカフェ: board game cafe
  • ボードゲーム愛好家(あいこうか): board game enthusiast
  • ボードゲーム大会(たいかい): board game tournament

TRADITIONAL JAPANESE BOARD GAMES:

  • 将棋(しょうぎ): shogi (Japanese chess)
  • 囲碁(いご): go
  • 双六(すごろく): sugoroku (Japanese backgammon-like race game)
  • 人生(じんせい)ゲーム: Game of Life (Japanese version)
  • オセロ: Othello/Reversi

CULTURAL NOTE:
Board game cafes (ボードゲームカフェ) have become popular in Japan since the 2010s, especially in urban areas like Tokyo and Osaka. They offer a large selection of games to play for a table fee, and have helped popularize modern European-style board games in Japan.
