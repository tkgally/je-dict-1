衣料品(いりょうひん)

いりょうひん
noun
clothing; apparel; garments
1. clothing; apparel; garments
Clothes and clothing products, especially as commercial goods or merchandise.
衣料品(いりょうひん)売(う)り場(ば)はどこですか。
Where is the clothing department?
衣料品(いりょうひん)のセールが始(はじ)まった。
The clothing sale has started.
この店(みせ)は子供(こども)向(む)けの衣料品(いりょうひん)を中心(ちゅうしん)に扱(あつか)っている。
This store mainly carries children's clothing.

Composed of 衣料(いりょう) (clothing material) + 品(ひん) (goods). A commercial and business term for clothing products. More commonly seen in department stores, retail, and industry contexts than in everyday speech, where 服(ふく) or 洋服(ようふく) are preferred.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 衣料品(いりょうひん)店(てん): clothing store
  • 衣料品(いりょうひん)売(う)り場(ば): clothing department; clothing section
  • 衣料品(いりょうひん)メーカー: clothing manufacturer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 服(ふく): clothes — everyday term
  • 洋服(ようふく): Western-style clothing — common everyday word
  • 被服(ひふく): clothing; garments — formal/technical, used in industry and military contexts
  • アパレル: apparel — industry term borrowed from English
