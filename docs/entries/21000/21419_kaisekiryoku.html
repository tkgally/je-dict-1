解析力(かいせきりょく)

かいせきりょく
noun
analytical ability; power of analysis
1. analytical ability; analytical power; capacity for analysis
The ability to break down complex information, data, or problems into components and understand their relationships. Valued in academic, technical, and business contexts.
彼女(かのじょ)は解析力(かいせきりょく)に優(すぐ)れている。
She excels in analytical ability.
データ解析力(かいせきりょく)は現代(げんだい)のビジネスに不可欠(ふかけつ)だ。
Data analysis skills are essential in modern business.
AIの解析力(かいせきりょく)は人間(にんげん)をはるかに上回(うわまわ)る分野(ぶんや)が増(ふ)えてきた。
The fields in which AI's analytical power far surpasses that of humans are increasing.

A compound of 解析(かいせき) (analysis) and 力(りょく) (power/ability). Used in academic, scientific, and business contexts to describe the capacity for detailed analysis.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 解析力(かいせきりょく)が高(たか)い: to have strong analytical ability
  • 解析力(かいせきりょく)を高(たか)める: to improve analytical ability
  • データ解析力(かいせきりょく): data analysis capability

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分析力(ぶんせきりょく): analytical ability — very similar; 分析(ぶんせき) is more common in everyday and business contexts, while 解析(かいせき) leans more technical/mathematical
  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく): insight, perceptiveness — the ability to see through to the essence of things
  • 思考力(しこうりょく): thinking ability — broader cognitive ability, not limited to analysis
