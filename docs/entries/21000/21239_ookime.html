大(おお)きめ

おおきめ
na-adjective, noun
rather large; on the large side; largish
1. rather large; on the large side; somewhat big
Indicates something is slightly larger than standard or expected. The suffix ～め softens the adjective to mean 'tending toward' or 'on the ... side.'
大(おお)きめのサイズをください。
Please give me a larger size.
野菜(やさい)は大(おお)きめに切(き)ってね。
Cut the vegetables on the larger side, okay?
このシャツは大(おお)きめに作(つく)られているので、ワンサイズ下(した)でもいいかもしれない。
This shirt is made on the large side, so one size down might be fine.

Formed by adding the suffix ～め to the adjective stem 大(おお)き. The ～め suffix indicates a slight tendency or degree — "somewhat," "on the ... side." Very common in everyday speech, especially when shopping or cooking.

PATTERN — ～め (degree suffix):

Attaches to adjective stems to indicate a slight degree:

  • 小(ちい)さめ: on the small side
  • 多(おお)め: on the generous/large side (for quantities)
  • 少(すく)なめ: on the less side
  • 早(はや)め: on the early side
  • 強(つよ)め: on the strong side

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)きめのサイズ: a larger size
  • 大(おお)きめに切(き)る: to cut into larger pieces
  • 大(おお)きめの服(ふく): oversized clothing
  • 大(おお)きめに作(つく)る: to make on the large side
