1. changeable; prone to change; unstable; fickle
Describes something that changes easily or frequently. Commonly used for weather, moods, and other things that are unpredictable or inconsistent.
山(やま)の天気(てんき)は変(か)わりやすい。
Mountain weather is changeable.
春(はる)は気温(きおん)が変(か)わりやすい季節(きせつ)だ。
Spring is a season when temperatures fluctuate easily.
彼女(かのじょ)は気分(きぶん)が変(か)わりやすいので、付(つ)き合(あ)いにくい。
She's hard to get along with because her moods change easily.

Formed from the verb 変(か)わる (to change) + やすい (easy to). Describes things that are prone to frequent or unpredictable change.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天気(てんき)が変(か)わりやすい: changeable weather
  • 気分(きぶん)が変(か)わりやすい: moody, having changeable moods
  • 変(か)わりやすい性格(せいかく): fickle personality
  • 変(か)わりやすい天候(てんこう): unstable weather conditions

GRAMMAR:
This is a compound adjective built with the ～やすい pattern (verb stem} + やすい = "easy to ~" or "tends to ~"). It conjugates as a regular i-adjective: {変(か)わりやすくない (not changeable), 変(か)わりやすかった (was changeable).

  • 不安定(ふあんてい): unstable — more formal, used for systems, situations, and conditions
  • 気(き)まぐれ: whimsical, capricious — focuses on unpredictable behavior in people
