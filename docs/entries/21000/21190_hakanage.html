儚(はかな)げ

はかなげ
na-adjective
seemingly fragile; having an evanescent quality
1. seemingly fragile; having an evanescent quality
Appearing delicate, transient, or ephemeral. Describes someone or something that gives an impression of fragility or fleeting beauty, often evoking a sense of melancholy.
儚(はかな)げな笑顔(えがお)だった。
It was a fragile-looking smile.
彼女(かのじょ)は儚(はかな)げな雰囲気(ふんいき)を持(も)っている。
She has an air of fragility about her.
桜(さくら)の花(はな)は儚(はかな)げに散(ち)っていく姿(すがた)が美(うつく)しい。
The beauty of cherry blossoms lies in their seemingly fragile scattering.

儚(はかな)い means 'fleeting' or 'transient,' and the suffix げ turns it into a na-adjective meaning 'appearing to be' or 'seemingly.' 儚(はかな)げ describes the outward impression of fragility or evanescence — something that looks like it might disappear at any moment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 儚(はかな)げな表情(ひょうじょう) (a fragile-looking expression)
  • 儚(はかな)げな美(うつく)しさ (evanescent beauty)
  • 儚(はかな)げな雰囲気(ふんいき) (a fragile atmosphere/aura)
  • 儚(はかな)げに微笑(ほほえ)む (to smile fragilely)

GRAMMAR NOTE:
The suffix げ (from 気(げ)) attaches to adjective stems to create na-adjectives meaning 'appearing to be ~' or 'seeming ~.' Other examples: 寂(さび)しげ (looking lonely), 楽(たの)しげ (looking happy), 悲(かな)しげ (looking sad).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 儚(はかな)い — fleeting, transient (the base adjective; describes the thing itself, not just its appearance)
  • か弱(よわ)い — frail, delicate (physically weak)
  • 脆(もろ)い — fragile, brittle (easily broken, physically or emotionally)
