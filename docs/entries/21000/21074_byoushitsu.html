病室(びょうしつ)

びょうしつ
noun
hospital room; sickroom
1. hospital room; sickroom; patient's room
A room in a hospital where patients stay during their admission. Also used more broadly for any room where a sick person rests.
病室(びょうしつ)は何号室(なんごうしつ)ですか。
What room number is the patient's room?
病室(びょうしつ)に花(はな)を持(も)っていった。
I brought flowers to the hospital room.
個室(こしつ)の病室(びょうしつ)は料金(りょうきん)が高(たか)いが、ゆっくり休(やす)める。
A private hospital room is expensive, but you can rest peacefully.

A standard compound: 病(びょう) (illness) + 室(しつ) (room). Very common in hospital-related contexts.

ROOM TYPES:

  • 個室(こしつ): private room (single occupancy)
  • 大部屋(おおべや): shared ward (multiple beds)
  • 二人部屋(ふたりべや): double room
  • 四人部屋(よにんべや): four-bed room

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病室(びょうしつ)を訪(たず)ねる: to visit a hospital room
  • 病室(びょうしつ)に入(はい)る: to enter the hospital room
  • 病室(びょうしつ)で過(す)ごす: to spend time in a hospital room
  • 病室(びょうしつ)の窓(まど): the hospital room window

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 病棟(びょうとう): hospital ward — the building or floor containing multiple rooms
  • 診察室(しんさつしつ): examination room — where doctors see patients for consultations
