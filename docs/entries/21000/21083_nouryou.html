納涼(のうりょう)

のうりょう
noun
cooling off in summer; enjoying the cool evening air
1. cooling off in summer; enjoying the cool evening air
The practice of seeking relief from summer heat, especially by going outdoors in the evening to enjoy the cooler air. Often associated with summer festivals and cultural events.
納涼(のうりょう)祭(まつ)りに行(い)く。
To go to a summer cooling-off festival.
川辺(かわべ)で納涼(のうりょう)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy the cool air by the riverside.
毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)になると、町内(ちょうない)で納涼(のうりょう)花火(はなび)大会(たいかい)が開(ひら)かれる。
Every summer, a fireworks festival for summer cooling is held in the neighborhood.

A seasonal word closely associated with Japanese summer culture. Refers to escaping the heat by enjoying cool breezes, often near water or in the evening. Frequently used as a prefix in event names.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 納涼(のうりょう)祭(まつ)り: summer cooling-off festival
  • 納涼(のうりょう)花火(はなび)大会(たいかい): summer fireworks display
  • 納涼(のうりょう)船(せん): pleasure boat for summer evenings (especially on the Sumida River)
  • 納涼(のうりょう)床(ゆか): riverside dining platforms in Kyoto (along the Kamo River)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, 納涼(のうりょう) events are a beloved summer tradition. Before air conditioning, people relied on natural cooling — river breezes, shaved ice, and evening outings. Many of these traditions continue today as cultural events, particularly 納涼(のうりょう)祭(まつ)り held by neighborhoods and organizations.

ETYMOLOGY:
納(のう) (receive, take in) + 涼(りょう) (cool) — literally "take in the coolness."
