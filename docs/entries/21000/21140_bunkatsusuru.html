1. to divide; to split; to partition
To separate something into two or more parts or sections, often in a planned or systematic way.
土地(とち)を分割(ぶんかつ)する。
To divide the land.
支払(しはら)いを３回(かい)に分割(ぶんかつ)できます。
You can split the payment into three installments.
大(おお)きなファイルを複数(ふくすう)の小(ちい)さなファイルに分割(ぶんかつ)してから送信(そうしん)した。
I split the large file into several smaller files before sending it.

To divide something into parts, typically in a deliberate or systematic manner. Commonly used in financial, legal, and technical contexts such as splitting payments, partitioning land, or dividing data.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 分割(ぶんかつ)払(ばら)い: installment payment
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)統治(とうち): divide and rule
  • 均等(きんとう)に分割(ぶんかつ)する: to divide equally

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分(わ)ける: to divide; to separate — more general and everyday
  • 分離(ぶんり)する: to separate; to isolate — emphasizes detachment rather than partitioning
  • 分裂(ぶんれつ)する: to split; to break apart — often implies conflict or involuntary division
