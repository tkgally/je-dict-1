1. to sprinkle over; to scatter on top of
To scatter a dry or fine-grained substance such as salt, spices, or sesame seeds onto the surface of food or another object.
ご飯(はん)にふりかけを振(ふ)りかける。
To sprinkle furikake on rice.
サラダに塩(しお)とコショウを振(ふ)りかけた。
I sprinkled salt and pepper on the salad.
仕上(しあ)げに粉(こな)チーズをたっぷり振(ふ)りかけると、風味(ふうみ)がぐっと増(ま)す。
Sprinkling plenty of grated cheese on top as a finishing touch greatly enhances the flavor.

A compound verb from 振(ふ)る (to shake, scatter) + かける (to apply onto), meaning to sprinkle or scatter something onto a surface. Most commonly used in cooking contexts.

USAGE:

  • Almost always used for dry or fine ingredients (salt, pepper, spices, cheese, sesame seeds)
  • The noun ふりかけ (rice seasoning) derives from this verb
  • Can also be used figuratively, e.g., 水(みず)を振(ふ)りかける (to sprinkle water)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塩(しお)を振(ふ)りかける: to sprinkle salt
  • ごまを振(ふ)りかける: to sprinkle sesame seeds
  • 粉(こな)チーズを振(ふ)りかける: to sprinkle grated cheese
  • パセリを振(ふ)りかける: to sprinkle parsley
  • 水(みず)を振(ふ)りかける: to sprinkle water

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • まぶす: to coat evenly; implies covering the entire surface
  • 散(ち)らす: to scatter; broader meaning, not limited to cooking
  • かける: to pour over; used for liquids rather than dry ingredients
