1. to distribute; to allocate; to share out
To divide something among multiple people or groups, giving each a portion.
利益(りえき)を分配(ぶんぱい)する。
To distribute the profits.
食料(しょくりょう)を公平(こうへい)に分配(ぶんぱい)した。
We distributed the food fairly.
限(かぎ)られた資源(しげん)をどのように分配(ぶんぱい)するかが大(おお)きな課題(かだい)だ。
How to distribute limited resources is a major challenge.

To divide and hand out shares of something to multiple recipients. Commonly used in economic, business, and organizational contexts for distributing profits, resources, or tasks.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利益(りえき)分配(ぶんぱい): profit distribution
  • 富(とみ)の分配(ぶんぱい): distribution of wealth
  • 均等(きんとう)に分配(ぶんぱい)する: to distribute equally
  • 分配(ぶんぱい)金(きん): dividend; distributed funds

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配分(はいぶん)する: to allot; to apportion — more formal, often used for budgets and resources
  • 配(くば)る: to hand out; to distribute — more everyday, physical distribution
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)する: to divide; to split — focuses on the act of dividing, not necessarily giving shares to others
