1. acting on one's own authority; acting arbitrarily without consultation
Making decisions and taking action unilaterally without consulting others or seeking approval. Carries a negative connotation of overstepping authority.
独断専行(どくだんせんこう)は許(ゆる)されない。
Acting on your own authority is not permitted.
部長(ぶちょう)の独断専行(どくだんせんこう)に部下(ぶか)たちは不満(ふまん)を募(つの)らせた。
The subordinates grew increasingly frustrated with the department head's unilateral actions.
独断専行(どくだんせんこう)で契約(けいやく)を結(むす)んでしまい、会社(かいしゃ)に大(おお)きな損害(そんがい)を与(あた)えた。
By signing the contract without authorization, they caused great damage to the company.

A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) meaning to decide and act without consulting others. Composed of 独断(どくだん) (arbitrary decision) + 専行(せんこう) (acting on one's own). Almost always negative — implies overstepping boundaries or ignoring proper procedures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 独断専行(どくだんせんこう)する: to act arbitrarily
  • 独断専行(どくだんせんこう)を許(ゆる)す: to allow unilateral action
  • 独断専行(どくだんせんこう)を戒(いまし)める: to warn against acting alone
  • 独断専行(どくだんせんこう)で決(き)める: to decide unilaterally

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 独断(どくだん): arbitrary decision — the decision alone, without the action
  • 独(ひと)り走(ばし)り: going off on one's own — more colloquial
  • {ワンマン}: one-man (management style) — focuses on autocratic leadership
  • 越権行為(えっけんこうい): act of overstepping one's authority — legal/formal term
