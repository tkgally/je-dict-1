実子(じっし)

じっし
noun
one's own child; biological child
1. one's own child; biological child; natural child
A child who is biologically related to their parent, as opposed to an adopted or step-child. A legal and formal term.
実子(じっし)と養子(ようし)の区別(くべつ)はない。
There is no distinction between biological and adopted children.
法律上(ほうりつじょう)、実子(じっし)も養子(ようし)も同(おな)じ相続権(そうぞくけん)を持(も)つ。
Legally, both biological and adopted children have the same inheritance rights.
彼(かれ)は実子(じっし)のように可愛(かわい)がっている養子(ようし)がいる。
He has an adopted child whom he cherishes as if they were his own.

A formal/legal term for a biological child. In everyday conversation, people would simply say 自分(じぶん)の子(こ) (one's own child). 実子(じっし) appears primarily in legal, medical, and formal contexts.

HOMOPHONE NOTE:
Not to be confused with 実施(じっし) (implementation, carrying out), which has the same reading.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実子(じっし)と養子(ようし): biological and adopted children
  • 実子(じっし)のように: as if one's own child
  • 実子(じっし)同様(どうよう): same as a biological child

RELATED TERMS:

  • 養子(ようし): adopted child
  • 継子(ままこ): stepchild
  • 里子(さとご): foster child
  • 実親(じつおや): biological parent
  • 養親(ようしん): adoptive parent
