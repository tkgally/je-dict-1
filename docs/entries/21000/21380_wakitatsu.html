1. to boil up; to bubble vigorously; to seethe
For a liquid to boil intensely, with steam or bubbles rising energetically.
お湯(ゆ)が沸(わ)き立(た)っている。
The water is boiling vigorously.
鍋(なべ)の中(なか)のスープが沸(わ)き立(た)っている。
The soup in the pot is bubbling vigorously.
大(おお)きな泡(あわ)を立(た)てて沸(わ)き立(た)つ温泉(おんせん)の源泉(げんせん)を見(み)た。
I saw a hot spring source bubbling up with large bubbles.
2. to surge with excitement; to be in an uproar; to be buzzing
For a place or group of people to become filled with excitement, enthusiasm, or agitation. Describes a lively, energetic atmosphere.
会場(かいじょう)が沸(わ)き立(た)った。
The venue erupted with excitement.
優勝(ゆうしょう)が決(き)まり、スタジアムが沸(わ)き立(た)った。
When the championship was decided, the stadium erupted.
選手(せんしゅ)の逆転(ぎゃくてん)ゴールに観客席(かんきゃくせき)が沸(わ)き立(た)ち、歓声(かんせい)が鳴(な)り止(や)まなかった。
The stands surged with excitement at the player's come-from-behind goal, and the cheering didn't stop.

Compound of 沸(わ)く (to boil, to well up) and 立(た)つ (to rise), giving a vivid image of steam or energy rising. The figurative sense (sense 2) is very common in sports and entertainment contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 会場(かいじょう)が沸(わ)き立(た)つ: the venue erupts
  • 観客(かんきゃく)が沸(わ)き立(た)つ: the audience buzzes with excitement
  • 怒(いか)りが沸(わ)き立(た)つ: anger surges up
  • 期待(きたい)に沸(わ)き立(た)つ: to buzz with anticipation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 沸(わ)く: to boil; to get excited — simpler and more common, less dramatic
  • 盛(も)り上(あ)がる: to get fired up — focuses on building excitement, especially at events
  • 沸騰(ふっとう)する: to boil — more technical/literal for boiling; figuratively means public opinion reaching a boiling point
