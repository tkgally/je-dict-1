1. to exterminate; to eradicate; to get rid of (pests, vermin, weeds)
To eliminate unwanted organisms such as insects, rodents, weeds, or other pests. Used for both professional pest control and everyday removal of pests.
ゴキブリを駆除(くじょ)する。
To exterminate cockroaches.
害虫(がいちゅう)を駆除(くじょ)するために薬(くすり)をまいた。
I sprayed pesticide to get rid of the pests.
専門(せんもん)業者(ぎょうしゃ)に依頼(いらい)してシロアリを駆除(くじょ)してもらった。
I had a professional company come to exterminate the termites.

Compound of 駆(く) (drive away) + 除(じょ) (remove). Specifically used for eliminating unwanted living things — pests, vermin, invasive species, weeds, etc. Not used for removing inanimate objects or abstract concepts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 害虫(がいちゅう)駆除(くじょ): pest extermination
  • ネズミ駆除(くじょ): rat extermination
  • 駆除(くじょ)業者(ぎょうしゃ): pest control company
  • 駆除(くじょ)作業(さぎょう): extermination work
  • 雑草(ざっそう)を駆除(くじょ)する: to eradicate weeds

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 退治(たいじ)する: to exterminate (more colloquial, also used figuratively for defeating enemies)
  • 除去(じょきょ)する: to remove (broader; used for stains, obstacles, substances)
  • 撲滅(ぼくめつ)する: to eradicate (used for diseases, crime — more formal and larger scale)
