外科医(げかい)

げかい
noun
surgeon
1. surgeon
A medical doctor who specializes in performing surgical operations.
外科医(げかい)になりたい。
I want to become a surgeon.
外科医(げかい)が手術(しゅじゅつ)の説明(せつめい)をしてくれた。
The surgeon explained the operation to me.
腕(うで)のいい外科医(げかい)がいると聞(き)いて、わざわざその病院(びょういん)まで通(かよ)っている。
I heard there's a skilled surgeon there, so I go out of my way to visit that hospital.

Composed of 外科(げか) (surgery, the surgical department) + 医(い) (doctor). Refers specifically to a doctor who performs surgery, as opposed to one who treats patients through medication or other non-surgical means.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心臓(しんぞう)外科医(げかい): cardiac surgeon
  • 脳(のう)外科医(げかい): neurosurgeon
  • 整形(せいけい)外科医(げかい): orthopedic surgeon
  • 腕(うで)のいい外科医(げかい): skilled surgeon

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 内科医(ないかい): internist — a doctor who treats diseases without surgery
  • 医師(いし): physician, doctor — the general term for a medical doctor
  • 執刀医(しっとうい): operating surgeon — the doctor who actually performs the surgery (more specific)
