1. omission; gap; missing part; deficiency
Something that is missing from where it should be, or the state of lacking something essential. Used for missing information, absent qualities, gaps in data, or things that have fallen away.
データに欠落(けつらく)がある。
There are gaps in the data.
常識(じょうしき)が欠落(けつらく)している。
He lacks common sense.
記憶(きおく)の一部(いちぶ)が欠落(けつらく)していて、事故(じこ)の詳細(しょうさい)を思(おも)い出(だ)せない。
Part of my memory is missing, and I can't recall the details of the accident.

Describes something that is absent or missing from a whole — a gap, omission, or deficiency. Can refer to physical things (missing pages, data gaps) or abstract qualities (lacking common sense, missing moral awareness).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 欠落(けつらく)している: to be missing, to be lacking
  • 欠落(けつらく)した部分(ぶぶん): the missing part
  • 情報(じょうほう)の欠落(けつらく): missing information
  • 記憶(きおく)の欠落(けつらく): memory gap

USAGE:
Often used in formal or analytical contexts — reports, medical descriptions, technical writing. The する form (欠落(けつらく)する) is intransitive: the thing itself falls away or is missing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 欠如(けつじょ): absence, lack — emphasizes the total absence of something expected
  • 不足(ふそく): insufficiency, shortage — implies there is some but not enough
  • 脱落(だつらく): dropping out, falling off — implies something fell away from a group or sequence
