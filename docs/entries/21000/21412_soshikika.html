1. organization; systematization; structuring into a system
The process of arranging people, activities, or information into a structured, organized system. Also refers to forming or unifying a group into an organized body.
労働者(ろうどうしゃ)の組織化(そしきか)が進(すす)んだ。
The organization of workers progressed.
情報(じょうほう)の組織化(そしきか)は業務(ぎょうむ)効率(こうりつ)を上(あ)げる。
Organizing information improves work efficiency.
ボランティア活動(かつどう)を組織化(そしきか)することで、より効果的(こうかてき)な支援(しえん)が可能(かのう)になった。
By organizing volunteer activities, more effective support became possible.

A compound of 組織(そしき) (organization) and 化(か) (the suffix meaning "-ization, turning into"). Describes the process of bringing order and structure to something that was previously unstructured.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 組織化(そしきか)する: to organize, to systematize
  • 組織化(そしきか)された: organized, structured
  • 組織化(そしきか)が進(すす)む: organization progresses
  • 組織化(そしきか)を図(はか)る: to aim for organization

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 体系化(たいけいか): systematization — emphasizes creating a logical system or framework
  • 制度化(せいどか): institutionalization — making something into an official system or institution
  • 組織(そしき): organization — the result (a structured body), not the process of creating one
