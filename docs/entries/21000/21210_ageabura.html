揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)

あげあぶら
noun
frying oil; oil for deep-frying
1. frying oil; oil for deep-frying
Cooking oil used specifically for deep-frying foods such as tempura, tonkatsu, and croquettes.
揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)を温(あたた)める。
To heat the frying oil.
揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)の温度(おんど)は百八十度(ひゃくはちじゅうど)がいい。
The frying oil should be at 180 degrees.
使(つか)い終(お)わった揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)は、固(かた)めて燃(も)えるゴミとして捨(す)てる。
Used frying oil should be solidified and disposed of as combustible waste.

Compound of 揚(あ)げる (to deep-fry) + 油(あぶら) (oil). Refers specifically to oil used for deep-frying, as opposed to cooking oil in general.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)を熱(ねっ)する: to heat frying oil
  • 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)の温度(おんど): frying oil temperature
  • 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)を処理(しょり)する: to dispose of frying oil
  • 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)を替(か)える: to change the frying oil

RELATED WORDS:

  • サラダ油(あぶら): salad oil, vegetable oil — the most common type of oil used for frying in Japanese kitchens
  • 天(てん)ぷら油(あぶら): tempura oil — often used interchangeably with 揚(あ)げ油(あぶら)
  • 廃油(はいゆ): waste oil — used frying oil that needs to be disposed of

COOKING NOTE:
Proper frying oil temperature is important in Japanese cooking: around 160°C for low-temperature frying, 170-180°C for standard frying (天(てん)ぷら, 唐揚(からあ)げ), and 180-190°C for high-temperature frying.
