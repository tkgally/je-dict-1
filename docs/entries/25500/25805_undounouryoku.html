運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)

うんどうのうりょく
noun
athletic ability; motor skills
1. athletic ability; physical fitness; motor skills
The ability to perform physical activities effectively. Encompasses coordination, speed, strength, endurance, and other physical capabilities.
彼(かれ)は運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)が高(たか)い。
He has high athletic ability.
子供(こども)の運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)は年齢(ねんれい)とともに発達(はったつ)する。
Children's motor skills develop with age.
最近(さいきん)の調査(ちょうさ)によると、子供(こども)たちの運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)が低下(ていか)しているそうだ。
According to a recent survey, children's physical fitness is reportedly declining.

A compound of 運動(うんどう) (exercise, movement) and 能力(のうりょく) (ability, capacity). Frequently appears in discussions about sports, education, and child development.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い — to have high/low athletic ability
  • 運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)が優(すぐ)れている — to have excellent athletic ability
  • 運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)テスト — fitness test
  • 運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)の向上(こうじょう) — improvement of athletic ability
  • 運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)の低下(ていか) — decline in physical fitness

RELATED TERMS:

  • 体力(たいりょく) — physical strength/stamina (more about endurance and raw physical power)
  • 運動神経(うんどうしんけい) — motor coordination; colloquially used to mean "being good/bad at sports"
  • 身体能力(しんたいのうりょく) — physical ability (broader, includes non-sport contexts)

NOTE:
In Japanese schools, students take 体力(たいりょく)テスト (運動能力(うんどうのうりょく)テスト) — standardized fitness tests measuring grip strength, flexibility, running speed, and other metrics. Results are tracked nationally, and declining scores are a topic of public concern.
