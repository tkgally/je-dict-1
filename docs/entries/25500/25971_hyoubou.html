1. professing; advocating; claiming publicly
Openly declaring or claiming to uphold a principle, identity, or stance. Often carries a nuance that the claim may not match reality.
民主主義(みんしゅしゅぎ)を標榜(ひょうぼう)する。
To profess democracy.
その政党(せいとう)は改革(かいかく)を標榜(ひょうぼう)しているが、実績(じっせき)は乏(とぼ)しい。
That political party professes reform, but its track record is lacking.
環境(かんきょう)にやさしいことを標榜(ひょうぼう)する企業(きぎょう)が増(ふ)えているが、実態(じったい)が伴(ともな)っているかは疑問(ぎもん)だ。
More companies are claiming to be environmentally friendly, but whether they live up to that is questionable.

Often used with する to form a verb. The word carries a somewhat skeptical or critical nuance — when someone 標榜(ひょうぼう)する something, there is often an implication that the claim may be hollow or that actions don't match the declared principles. It is common in political, corporate, and ideological contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自由(じゆう)を標榜(ひょうぼう)する: to profess freedom
  • 平和(へいわ)を標榜(ひょうぼう)する: to advocate peace
  • ～を標榜(ひょうぼう)する団体(だんたい): an organization that claims to stand for ~
  • 標榜(ひょうぼう)科(か): advertised medical specialty (on clinic signs)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主張(しゅちょう): assertion; claim — more neutral, simply stating a position
  • 掲(かか)げる: to hold up; to advocate — similar meaning but less skeptical in tone
  • 謳(うた)う: to tout; to proclaim — often used for advertising claims
