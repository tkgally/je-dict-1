1. to chew; to masticate
To repeatedly bite and grind food in the mouth before swallowing.
よく咀嚼(そしゃく)して食(た)べなさい。
Chew your food well before swallowing.
咀嚼(そしゃく)の回数(かいすう)を増(ふ)やすと消化(しょうか)が良(よ)くなる。
Increasing the number of chews improves digestion.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)は咀嚼(そしゃく)力(りょく)が低下(ていか)しやすい。
Elderly people tend to experience a decline in chewing ability.
2. to digest mentally; to mull over; to think through carefully
To thoroughly consider and understand the meaning or content of something, as if chewing it over in one's mind.
情報(じょうほう)を咀嚼(そしゃく)する時間(じかん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
I need time to digest the information.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)をゆっくり咀嚼(そしゃく)した。
I mulled over his words slowly.
難(むずか)しい概念(がいねん)を咀嚼(そしゃく)して分(わ)かりやすく伝(つた)えるのが彼女(かのじょ)の強(つよ)みだ。
Her strength is digesting difficult concepts and conveying them in an easy-to-understand way.

Composed of 咀嚼(そしゃく) (chewing; mastication) + する. Both the literal (eating) and figurative (understanding) senses are common. The figurative sense is especially frequent in intellectual and media contexts — "chewing over" information, ideas, or someone's words. The kanji 咀(そ) and 嚼(しゃく) are both unusual and not tested on standard kanji exams.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • よく咀嚼(そしゃく)する: to chew well; to think through carefully
  • 咀嚼(そしゃく)力(りょく): chewing ability
  • 情報(じょうほう)を咀嚼(そしゃく)する: to digest information
  • 咀嚼(そしゃく)して伝(つた)える: to explain after digesting (making it accessible)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 噛(か)む: to bite; to chew — the everyday word for chewing
  • 消化(しょうか)する: to digest — literally for digestion, figuratively for absorbing information; less active than 咀嚼(そしゃく)
  • 熟考(じゅっこう)する: to deliberate carefully — figuratively similar but without the chewing metaphor
