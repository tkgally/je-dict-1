情報機器(じょうほうきき)

じょうほうきき
noun
information device; IT equipment
1. information device; IT equipment; computing device
Electronic devices used for processing, storing, and communicating information, such as computers, smartphones, and tablets.
情報機器(じょうほうきき)の取(と)り扱(あつか)いに注意(ちゅうい)する。
To exercise caution when handling IT equipment.
学校(がっこう)では児童(じどう)に情報機器(じょうほうきき)の正(ただ)しい使(つか)い方(かた)を教(おし)えている。
Schools are teaching children the proper use of IT devices.
情報機器(じょうほうきき)の進化(しんか)により、働(はたら)き方(かた)が大(おお)きく変(か)わった。
The evolution of information devices has greatly changed the way we work.

A compound of 情報(じょうほう) (information) and 機器(きき) (equipment; devices). A broad term encompassing computers, smartphones, tablets, servers, and other digital devices. Commonly used in business, education, and policy contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報機器(じょうほうきき)の操作(そうさ): operating IT equipment
  • 情報機器(じょうほうきき)の管理(かんり): IT equipment management
  • 携帯型(けいたいがた)情報機器(じょうほうきき): portable information device
  • 情報機器(じょうほうきき)の導入(どうにゅう): introduction of IT equipment

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電子機器(でんしきき): electronic device — broader category that includes non-information electronics like appliances
  • 端末(たんまつ): terminal; device — often used for individual computing devices like smartphones or tablets
  • 通信機器(つうしんきき): communication equipment — specifically devices for transmitting and receiving data
