開閉(かいへい)ボタン

かいへいぼたん
noun
open/close button; door button
1. open/close button; door button
A button used to open or close doors, especially on elevators, trains, and automatic doors. Often appears as a pair: 開 (open) and 閉 (close).
エレベーターの開閉(かいへい)ボタンを押(お)した。
I pressed the elevator door button.
ドア(どあ)の開閉(かいへい)ボタンが故障(こしょう)している。
The door open/close button is out of order.
電車(でんしゃ)の開閉(かいへい)ボタンは冬場(ふゆば)に寒(さむ)い空気(くうき)が入(はい)らないように、乗客(じょうきゃく)が自分(じぶん)で操作(そうさ)する仕組(しく)みになっている。
Train door buttons are designed so that passengers operate them themselves, to prevent cold air from coming in during winter.

Composed of 開閉(かいへい) (opening and closing) + ボタン (button, from Portuguese/English). Commonly encountered in daily life in Japan, especially on elevators and semi-automatic train doors in rural areas where passengers press a button to open the door.

On Japanese elevators, the buttons are typically marked 開(ひらく) (◁▷) and 閉(とじる) (▷◁). Many people find the similar-looking symbols confusing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 開閉(かいへい)ボタンを押(お)す: to press the open/close button
  • エレベーターの開閉(かいへい)ボタン: elevator door button
  • ドアの開閉(かいへい)ボタン: door open/close button
  • 開閉(かいへい)ボタンが効(き)かない: the button doesn't work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 開(ひら)くボタン: open button — specifically the "open" button
  • 閉(と)じるボタン: close button — specifically the "close" button
  • 押(お)しボタン: push button — a general term for any button you press
