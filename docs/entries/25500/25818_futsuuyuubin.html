普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)

ふつうゆうびん
noun
regular mail; standard mail
1. regular mail; standard mail; ordinary mail
The basic postal delivery service without tracking, insurance, or special handling. The most common and least expensive way to send letters and small packages.
普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)で送(おく)っても大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)ですか。
Is it okay to send it by regular mail?
普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)だと届(とど)くまで二(に)、三日(さんにち)かかる。
With regular mail, it takes two or three days to arrive.
普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)には追跡(ついせき)サービスがないので、届(とど)いたかどうか確認(かくにん)できない。
Regular mail doesn't have tracking service, so you can't confirm whether it arrived.

A compound of 普通(ふつう) (ordinary, regular) and 郵便(ゆうびん) (mail, postal service). The default postal service for everyday correspondence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)で送(おく)る — to send by regular mail
  • 普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)の料金(りょうきん) — regular mail postage
  • 普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん)の届(とど)く日数(にっすう) — delivery days for regular mail

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • No tracking number
  • No compensation if lost
  • Delivered to the mailbox (no signature required)
  • Cheapest option
  • Cannot be used to send cash

RELATED TERMS:

  • 書留(かきとめ) — registered mail (tracked and insured)
  • 速達(そくたつ) — express delivery (faster but more expensive)
  • 定形郵便(ていけいゆうびん) — standard-size mail (fits specific size/weight limits)
  • 定形外郵便(ていけいがいゆうびん) — non-standard-size mail (oversized or overweight items)
  • 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい) — delivery certification

PRACTICAL NOTE:
普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん) can be posted simply by affixing the correct postage stamp and dropping it in a mailbox ({ポスト}). No visit to the post office is needed for standard-size items.
