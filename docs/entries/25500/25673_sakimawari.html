1. going ahead; getting there first; cutting someone off
Physically arriving at a destination before someone else, or taking a different route to get ahead.
近道(ちかみち)をして先回(さきまわ)りした。
I took a shortcut and got there first.
犯人(はんにん)の逃(に)げ道(みち)を先回(さきまわ)りして待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せた。
They got ahead of the criminal's escape route and set up an ambush.
渋滞(じゅうたい)を避(さ)けるために裏道(うらみち)で先回(さきまわ)りすることにした。
I decided to take back roads to get ahead and avoid the traffic jam.
2. anticipation; preemption; thinking ahead
Acting in advance to address something before it happens, or predicting and preparing for a future situation.
問題(もんだい)を先回(さきまわ)りして対処(たいしょ)する。
To deal with problems by anticipating them.
心配(しんぱい)を先回(さきまわ)りしても仕方(しかた)がない。
There's no point in worrying about things before they happen.
顧客(こきゃく)のニーズを先回(さきまわ)りして新(あたら)しいサービスを開発(かいはつ)するのが我(わ)が社(しゃ)の強(つよ)みだ。
Anticipating customer needs and developing new services is our company's strength.

A compound of 先(さき) (ahead, future) and 回(まわ)り (going around). Functions as both a noun and a suru verb (先回(さきまわ)りする). The literal sense of physically getting ahead expanded into the common figurative meaning of anticipating and acting preemptively.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先回(さきまわ)りして待(ま)つ: to get ahead and wait
  • 心配(しんぱい)を先回(さきまわ)りする: to worry in advance
  • 先回(さきまわ)りして対策(たいさく)を打(う)つ: to take preemptive measures

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先手(せんて)を打(う)つ: to make the first move — emphasizes taking initiative in a competitive context
  • 先読(さきよ)み: foresight, reading ahead — focuses on prediction and analysis
  • 予防(よぼう): prevention — specifically about preventing problems, more formal
