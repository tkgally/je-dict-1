体験談(たいけんだん)

たいけんだん
noun
personal experience story; first-hand account
1. personal experience story; first-hand account
A story or account based on someone's own direct experience, often shared to inform or help others.
留学(りゅうがく)の体験談(たいけんだん)を聞(き)く。
To listen to someone's study-abroad experience.
ネットには転職(てんしょく)の体験談(たいけんだん)がたくさん載(の)っている。
There are many job-change experience stories posted online.
先輩(せんぱい)の体験談(たいけんだん)を参考(さんこう)にして、就職活動(しゅうしょくかつどう)の準備(じゅんび)を始(はじ)めた。
I started preparing for job hunting using seniors' first-hand accounts as reference.

A compound of 体験(たいけん) (personal experience) and 談(だん) (talk, story). Very common in blogs, review sites, and informational content where people share real experiences to help others make decisions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体験談(たいけんだん)を語(かた)る: to tell one's experience
  • 体験談(たいけんだん)を募集(ぼしゅう)する: to solicit personal stories
  • 体験談(たいけんだん)を読(よ)む: to read experience stories
  • 成功(せいこう)体験談(たいけんだん): success story
  • 失敗(しっぱい)体験談(たいけんだん): failure story

USAGE:
Often appears on websites, in magazines, and at events where people share practical advice. The word implies authenticity — the story comes from direct experience, not secondhand knowledge.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経験談(けいけんだん): experience story — nearly synonymous, but 体験談(たいけんだん) emphasizes personal, firsthand involvement while 経験談(けいけんだん) can cover broader professional experience
  • 感想(かんそう): impression, thoughts — more about opinions and feelings than a narrative account
