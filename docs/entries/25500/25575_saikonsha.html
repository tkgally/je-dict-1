再婚者(さいこんしゃ)

さいこんしゃ
noun
person who has remarried; remarried person
1. person who has remarried; remarried person
someone who has married again after a previous marriage ended in divorce or the death of a spouse
再婚者(さいこんしゃ)の割合(わりあい)が増(ふ)えている。
The proportion of remarried people is increasing.
再婚者(さいこんしゃ)には子連(こづ)れの場合(ばあい)もある。
Some remarried people bring children from a previous marriage.
結婚式場(けっこんしきじょう)では再婚者(さいこんしゃ)向(む)けのプランも用意(ようい)されている。
Wedding venues also offer plans tailored for remarried couples.

A compound of 再婚(さいこん) (remarriage) and 者(しゃ) (person). Used in demographics, legal contexts, and discussions about family structures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再婚者(さいこんしゃ)の割合(わりあい): proportion of remarried people
  • 再婚者(さいこんしゃ)同士(どうし): two remarried people (marrying each other)
  • 子連(こづ)れ再婚者(さいこんしゃ): remarried person with children

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初婚者(しょこんしゃ): person marrying for the first time
  • バツイチ: divorced person (colloquial) — literally "one strike," from the × mark used to cross out a marriage record

REGISTER:
Formal. Used in statistics, legal documents, and news articles. In casual conversation, people are more likely to say 再婚(さいこん)した人(ひと) (a person who remarried).
