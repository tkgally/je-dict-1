1. to rush about; to run around busily; to be constantly on the go
To hurry from place to place in order to accomplish something, especially making efforts to arrange or resolve matters.
資金集(しきんあつ)めに東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)する。
To rush about raising funds.
選挙(せんきょ)運動(うんどう)のために東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)した。
I ran around busily for the election campaign.
営業(えいぎょう)部長(ぶちょう)は新規(しんき)顧客(こきゃく)を獲得(かくとく)するために毎日(まいにち)東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)している。
The sales manager is constantly rushing around every day to acquire new clients.

A four-character idiom (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)): 東(ひがし) (east) + 奔(ほん) (run) + 西(にし) (west) + 走(そう) (run). Literally "running east and dashing west," conveying the image of someone hurrying in all directions. Used in formal and literary contexts to describe someone making great efforts, often with a positive connotation of dedication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)の日々(ひび) — days of rushing about
  • 資金集(しきんあつ)めに東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)する — rush about raising funds
  • 解決(かいけつ)に東奔西走(とうほんせいそう)する — run around trying to resolve something

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 奔走(ほんそう)する — to run about; to make efforts — simpler two-character version, very similar meaning
  • 駆(か)け回(まわ)る — to run around — more colloquial, physical action
  • 飛(と)び回(まわ)る — to fly around — emphasizes rapid movement from place to place
