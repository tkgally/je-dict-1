前厄(まえやく)

まえやく
noun
year before an unlucky year; pre-calamity year
1. year before an unlucky year; pre-calamity year
The year immediately preceding the main unlucky year (本厄) in the yakudoshi cycle. Considered a warning period when one should begin exercising caution.
来年(らいねん)は前厄(まえやく)だ。
Next year is my pre-calamity year.
前厄(まえやく)なので神社(じんじゃ)でお祓(はら)いをしてもらった。
Since it's my pre-calamity year, I had a purification performed at the shrine.
前厄(まえやく)から気(き)をつけ始(はじ)める人(ひと)も多(おお)く、大(おお)きな決断(けつだん)を避(さ)けたり厄除(やくよ)けのお守(まも)りを持(も)ったりする。
Many people start being careful from the pre-calamity year, avoiding major decisions and carrying protective charms.

Part of the 厄年(やくどし) (unlucky year) cycle in Japanese folk belief. The three-year cycle consists of 前厄(まえやく) → 本厄(ほんやく) (main unlucky year) → 後厄(あとやく) (year after). 前厄(まえやく) is the lead-in period, and many people already begin visiting shrines for 厄払(やくばら)い (purification) during this year.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前厄(まえやく)に入(はい)る: to enter the pre-calamity year
  • 前厄(まえやく)のお祓(はら)い: purification for the pre-calamity year
  • 前厄(まえやく)から気(き)をつける: to start being careful from the pre-calamity year

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 本厄(ほんやく): main unlucky year — the central and most critical year in the cycle
  • 後厄(あとやく): year after the unlucky year — the final year of the cycle, when misfortune is thought to be fading
  • 厄年(やくどし): unlucky year — the general term for the entire system
  • 厄払(やくばら)い: purification ritual — the ceremony performed to ward off bad luck
