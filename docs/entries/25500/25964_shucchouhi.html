出張費(しゅっちょうひ)

しゅっちょうひ
noun
business trip expenses; travel expenses for work
1. business trip expenses; travel expenses for work
The total costs incurred when traveling for work, including transportation, accommodation, and daily allowances.
出張費(しゅっちょうひ)を申請(しんせい)する。
To submit a claim for business trip expenses.
今月(こんげつ)の出張費(しゅっちょうひ)は予算(よさん)を超(こ)えてしまった。
This month's business trip expenses went over budget.
会社(かいしゃ)の規定(きてい)では、出張費(しゅっちょうひ)は領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)を添(そ)えて一週間以内(いっしゅうかんいない)に精算(せいさん)することになっている。
According to company policy, business trip expenses must be settled within one week with receipts attached.

A compound of 出張(しゅっちょう) (business trip) and 費(ひ) (expense; cost). Covers all costs associated with a business trip, including transportation (交通費(こうつうひ)), accommodation (宿泊費(しゅくはくひ)), and per diem allowances (日当(にっとう)). A standard term in Japanese workplace vocabulary, commonly appearing on expense reports and reimbursement forms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出張費(しゅっちょうひ)を精算(せいさん)する: to settle business trip expenses
  • 出張費(しゅっちょうひ)を申請(しんせい)する: to file for business trip expenses
  • 出張費(しゅっちょうひ)の上限(じょうげん): expense limit for business trips
  • 出張費(しゅっちょうひ)規定(きてい): business trip expense policy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交通費(こうつうひ): transportation costs — a subcategory covering only travel fares
  • 旅費(りょひ): travel expenses — a more general term that can apply to both business and personal travel
  • 経費(けいひ): expenses — a broader business term covering all operational costs
