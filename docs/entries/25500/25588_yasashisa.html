優(やさ)しさ

やさしさ
noun
kindness; gentleness; tenderness
1. kindness; gentleness; tenderness
The quality of being kind, gentle, or considerate toward others.
彼女(かのじょ)の優(やさ)しさに感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by her kindness.
人(ひと)の優(やさ)しさは言葉(ことば)だけでは伝(つた)わらない。
A person's kindness can't be conveyed through words alone.
子(こ)どもに接(せっ)するときは、厳(きび)しさと優(やさ)しさのバランスが大切(たいせつ)だ。
When dealing with children, a balance of strictness and gentleness is important.

The nominalized form of the i-adjective 優(やさ)しい (kind, gentle). It refers to the abstract quality of kindness or tenderness as a trait or feeling.

Often used to describe warmth in personal relationships and is a highly valued quality in Japanese culture. It encompasses both emotional warmth and considerate behavior.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 優(やさ)しさに溢(あふ)れる: to overflow with kindness
  • 優(やさ)しさに触(ふ)れる: to experience someone's kindness
  • 優(やさ)しさを感(かん)じる: to feel kindness
  • 本当(ほんとう)の優(やさ)しさ: true kindness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 親切(しんせつ): kindness, helpfulness — focuses more on helpful actions toward others
  • 思(おも)いやり: consideration, compassion — emphasizes empathy and thoughtfulness
  • 温(あたた)かさ: warmth — broader sense of emotional or physical warmth
