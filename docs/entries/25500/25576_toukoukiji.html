投稿記事(とうこうきじ)

とうこうきじ
noun
submitted article; reader contribution; user-generated post
1. submitted article; reader contribution; user-generated post
an article, piece of writing, or post submitted by a reader or user for publication
投稿記事(とうこうきじ)を募集(ぼしゅう)しています。
We are accepting article submissions.
彼女(かのじょ)の投稿記事(とうこうきじ)が雑誌(ざっし)に掲載(けいさい)された。
Her submitted article was published in a magazine.
ブログの投稿記事(とうこうきじ)が話題(わだい)になって多(おお)くのアクセスを集(あつ)めた。
A blog post went viral and attracted a lot of traffic.

A compound of 投稿(とうこう) (submission, posting) and 記事(きじ) (article). Used for both traditional media submissions and modern online content.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投稿記事(とうこうきじ)を書(か)く: to write a submitted article
  • 投稿記事(とうこうきじ)を募集(ぼしゅう)する: to solicit reader submissions
  • 投稿記事(とうこうきじ)を掲載(けいさい)する: to publish a submitted article
  • 投稿記事(とうこうきじ)を編集(へんしゅう)する: to edit a submitted article

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寄稿(きこう): contributed writing — more formal, typically from invited or established writers
  • 投稿(とうこう): submission, post — the general act of submitting, not limited to articles
  • 読者(どくしゃ)投稿(とうこう): reader submission — emphasizes the reader origin

REGISTER:
Neutral. Used in both print media and online contexts.
