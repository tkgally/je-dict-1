1. to calculate; to compute; to work out (numbers)
To perform mathematical operations to determine a numerical result.
合計(ごうけい)を計算(けいさん)する。
I calculate the total.
この問題(もんだい)は暗算(あんざん)で計算(けいさん)できる。
You can calculate this problem mentally.
税込(ぜいこ)みの値段(ねだん)を計算(けいさん)してください。
Please calculate the price including tax.
2. to take into account; to factor in; to be calculating
To consider strategically or shrewdly when planning actions, often with the nuance of being scheming or self-interested.
彼(かれ)は計算(けいさん)して動(うご)く人(ひと)だ。
He's the type who acts calculatingly.
損得(そんとく)を計算(けいさん)してから決(き)める。
I decide after weighing the pros and cons.
あの人(ひと)の親切(しんせつ)は計算(けいさん)されたものだと思(おも)う。
I think that person's kindness is calculated.

計算(けいさん)する covers both literal mathematical calculation and figurative strategic thinking. In the figurative sense, 計算高(けいさんだか)い describes a person who is calculating or shrewd.

USAGE:
The object of calculation takes を: ～を計算(けいさん)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合計(ごうけい)を計算(けいさん)する: calculate the total
  • コストを計算(けいさん)する: calculate the cost
  • 暗算(あんざん)で計算(けいさん)する: calculate mentally
  • 電卓(でんたく)で計算(けいさん)する: calculate with a calculator
  • 損得(そんとく)を計算(けいさん)する: weigh the pros and cons

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 算出(さんしゅつ)する: to compute — more formal, used for deriving specific figures from data
  • 見積(みつ)もる: to estimate — focuses on approximation rather than exact calculation
  • 数(かぞ)える: to count — counting items rather than performing arithmetic
