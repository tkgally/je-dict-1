最低気温(さいていきおん)

さいていきおん
noun
minimum temperature; low temperature
1. minimum temperature; overnight low
The lowest temperature recorded during a specific period, typically a day. A standard term in weather forecasts.
今朝(けさ)の最低気温(さいていきおん)は零下(れいか)3度(ど)だった。
This morning's low temperature was minus 3 degrees.
明日(あした)の最低気温(さいていきおん)は10度(ど)の予報(よほう)だ。
Tomorrow's forecasted low is 10 degrees.
冬(ふゆ)になると最低気温(さいていきおん)が氷点下(ひょうてんか)まで下(さ)がる地域(ちいき)もある。
In winter, there are regions where the minimum temperature drops below freezing.

A compound of 最低(さいてい) (minimum; lowest) and 気温(きおん) (air temperature). One of the most common weather terms heard in daily forecasts. In Japanese weather reports, 最低気温(さいていきおん) typically refers to the overnight or early-morning low.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最低気温(さいていきおん)が下(さ)がる: the low temperature drops
  • 最低気温(さいていきおん)が氷点下(ひょうてんか)になる: the minimum temperature goes below freezing
  • 今日(きょう)の最低気温(さいていきおん): today's low
  • 最低気温(さいていきおん)の予報(よほう): forecast low temperature

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最高気温(さいこうきおん): maximum temperature; high — the counterpart, indicating the day's highest temperature
  • 気温(きおん): air temperature — the general term for temperature
  • 冷え込み(ひえこみ): cold snap; chill — describes the feeling of a sudden drop in temperature, especially overnight
