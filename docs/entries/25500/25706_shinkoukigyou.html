新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)

しんこうきぎょう
noun
startup; emerging company; new business
1. startup; emerging company; new business
A recently established company, especially one that is growing rapidly or entering a new market.
新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)が次々(つぎつぎ)と生(う)まれている。
Startups are being born one after another.
IT分野(ぶんや)の新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)に投資(とうし)する。
To invest in IT startups.
かつての新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)が今(いま)では業界(ぎょうかい)のリーダーになっている。
What was once a startup is now a leader in the industry.

新興(しんこう) (newly risen, emerging) + 企業(きぎょう) (enterprise, company). Used in business and economic news to describe companies that have recently been established and are growing. While often translated as "startup," it can also refer to any newly prominent company, including those in traditional industries.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)を立(た)ち上(あ)げる — launch a startup
  • 新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)に投資(とうし)する — invest in a startup
  • IT新興企業(しんこうきぎょう) — IT startup
  • 新興企業(しんこうきぎょう)の成長(せいちょう) — startup growth

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スタートアップ — startup — the loanword, now commonly used in Japanese business contexts
  • ベンチャー企業(きぎょう) — venture company — emphasizes innovation and risk-taking
  • 中小企業(ちゅうしょうきぎょう) — small and medium-sized enterprise — not necessarily new; refers to company size
