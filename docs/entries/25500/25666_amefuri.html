雨降(あめふ)り

あめふり
noun
rainfall; rainy weather
1. rainfall; rainy weather; rain
The state of rain falling, or a day or period characterized by rain. Used as a casual, everyday way to describe rainy conditions.
今日(きょう)は雨降(あめふ)りだ。
It's rainy today.
雨降(あめふ)りの日(ひ)は家(いえ)で本(ほん)を読(よ)む。
On rainy days, I read books at home.
三日(みっか)続(つづ)きの雨降(あめふ)りで洗濯物(せんたくもの)が乾(かわ)かなくて困(こま)っている。
Three straight days of rain have left me unable to dry the laundry.

A compound of 雨(あめ) (rain) and 降(ふ)り (falling), describing the state or weather condition of rain. More casual and colloquial than 降雨(こうう), which is the formal or meteorological term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨降(あめふ)りの日(ひ): rainy day
  • 雨降(あめふ)り続(つづ)き: continuous rain
  • 雨降(あめふ)りの中(なか): in the rain

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 降雨(こうう): rainfall — formal, used in weather reports and official writing
  • 雨天(うてん): rainy weather — formal, used for event announcements (e.g., 雨天(うてん)中止(ちゅうし))
  • 雨(あめ): rain — the most general and neutral term

CULTURAL NOTE:
The children's song 雨降(あめふ)りお月(つき)さん is a well-known traditional nursery rhyme, making this word familiar from an early age.
