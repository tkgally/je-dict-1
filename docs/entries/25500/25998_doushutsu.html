1. derivation; deduction; deriving
The process of arriving at a conclusion, formula, or result through logical reasoning or mathematical calculation. Used in academic, scientific, and logical contexts.
公式(こうしき)を導出(どうしゅつ)する。
To derive a formula.
この定理(ていり)の導出(どうしゅつ)過程(かてい)を説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain the derivation process of this theorem.
実験(じっけん)データから統計的(とうけいてき)に有意(ゆうい)な結論(けつろん)を導出(どうしゅつ)することが研究(けんきゅう)の目的(もくてき)だ。
The aim of the research is to derive statistically significant conclusions from the experimental data.

Composed of 導(どう) (lead, guide) and 出(しゅつ) (come out, produce). Used primarily in academic and technical writing — mathematics, logic, science, and engineering. The verb form 導出(どうしゅつ)する means to derive or deduce.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 公式(こうしき)を導出(どうしゅつ)する: to derive a formula
  • 結論(けつろん)を導出(どうしゅつ)する: to derive a conclusion
  • 導出(どうしゅつ)過程(かてい): derivation process
  • 数学的(すうがくてき)導出(どうしゅつ): mathematical derivation
  • 理論(りろん)の導出(どうしゅつ): derivation of a theory

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 演繹(えんえき): deduction — reasoning from general principles to specific conclusions (formal logic term)
  • 帰納(きのう): induction — reasoning from specific cases to general principles (the opposite direction)
  • 算出(さんしゅつ): calculation — computing a numerical answer rather than deriving a logical result
  • 導(みちび)く: to lead, to derive — the verb form; more general and used outside academic contexts too
