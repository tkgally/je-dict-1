1. to cause trouble for; to embarrass; to put in a difficult position
To make someone feel troubled, embarrassed, or at a loss by one's actions or words.
人(ひと)を困(こま)らせてはいけない。
You shouldn't cause trouble for others.
子(こ)どもが先生(せんせい)を困(こま)らせた。
The child gave the teacher a hard time.
そんな難(むずか)しい質問(しつもん)をされたら困(こま)らせることになるよ。
Asking such a difficult question will put them in an awkward position.
親(おや)を困(こま)らせるようなことはしないでほしい。
I don't want you to do things that trouble your parents.

Causative form of 困(こま)る (to be troubled; to be at a loss). While 困(こま)る describes the state of being troubled, 困(こま)らせる assigns agency — someone is actively causing the trouble. Very common in parenting, teaching, and relationship contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を困(こま)らせる: to trouble someone
  • 親(おや)を困(こま)らせる: to trouble one's parents
  • 先生(せんせい)を困(こま)らせる: to give the teacher a hard time
  • 困(こま)らせるつもりはない: I don't intend to cause trouble

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 迷惑(めいわく)をかける: to cause inconvenience — focuses on the burden placed on others; more formal
  • 悩(なや)ませる: to worry; to distress — implies deeper emotional distress rather than practical trouble
  • 手(て)を焼(や)かせる: to give someone a hard time — implies ongoing difficulty, often with a child or subordinate
