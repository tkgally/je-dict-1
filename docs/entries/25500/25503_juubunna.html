十分(じゅうぶん)な

じゅうぶんな
adjective
enough; sufficient; adequate; ample
1. enough; sufficient; adequate
Having as much as is needed to meet a requirement or standard.
十分(じゅうぶん)な睡眠(すいみん)を取(と)ってください。
Please get enough sleep.
準備(じゅんび)は十分(じゅうぶん)ですか。
Are the preparations sufficient?
経験(けいけん)が十分(じゅうぶん)でないため、もう少(すこ)し練習(れんしゅう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Because my experience is not sufficient, I need a bit more practice.
2. ample; plenty; thorough
Having more than enough, or being satisfactorily complete and thorough.
十分(じゅうぶん)な成果(せいか)が出(で)た。
Ample results were achieved.
この説明(せつめい)で十分(じゅうぶん)だと思(おも)います。
I think this explanation is thorough enough.
十分(じゅうぶん)な時間(じかん)をかけて検討(けんとう)した結果(けっか)、この方針(ほうしん)に決(き)めた。
After considering it with ample time, we decided on this policy.

Also used as an adverb 十分(じゅうぶん)に (sufficiently; thoroughly). Can also be read じっぷん when meaning "ten minutes," but the na-adjective meaning is always じゅうぶん. The negative form 十分(じゅうぶん)でない or 不十分(ふじゅうぶん)な (insufficient) is equally common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 十分(じゅうぶん)な時間(じかん): enough time
  • 十分(じゅうぶん)な睡眠(すいみん): enough sleep
  • 十分(じゅうぶん)な準備(じゅんび): adequate preparation
  • 十分(じゅうぶん)な証拠(しょうこ): sufficient evidence
  • 不十分(ふじゅうぶん)な: insufficient; inadequate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 充分(じゅうぶん)な: sufficient — variant kanji form with the same meaning and pronunciation; both are standard
  • 足(た)りる: to be enough — a verb rather than an adjective; more conversational
  • 豊富(ほうふ)な: abundant; rich — implies having much more than needed, especially variety
