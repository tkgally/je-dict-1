申込用紙(もうしこみようし)

もうしこみようし
noun
application form
1. application form; registration form; sign-up form
A paper form used to apply for or register for something, such as a membership, service, event, or subscription.
申込用紙(もうしこみようし)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the application form.
申込用紙(もうしこみようし)は受付(うけつけ)でもらえます。
You can get the application form at the reception desk.
申込用紙(もうしこみようし)に必要事項(ひつようじこう)を記入(きにゅう)の上(うえ)、窓口(まどぐち)にご提出(ていしゅつ)ください。
Please fill in the required information on the application form and submit it at the counter.

A compound of 申込(もうしこみ) (application, request) and 用紙(ようし) (form, blank paper). The most standard term for a physical application form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 申込用紙(もうしこみようし)に記入(きにゅう)する — to fill in an application form
  • 申込用紙(もうしこみようし)を提出(ていしゅつ)する — to submit an application form
  • 申込用紙(もうしこみようし)を配布(はいふ)する — to distribute application forms
  • 申込用紙(もうしこみようし)をダウンロードする — to download an application form

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 申込書(もうしこみしょ) — application document (often used interchangeably, but 書(しょ) implies a more formal or complete document)
  • 申請書(しんせいしょ) — formal request/petition form (used for official government or institutional applications)
  • 記入用紙(きにゅうようし) — fill-in form (any form requiring handwriting, not necessarily for applications)
  • 登録用紙(とうろくようし) — registration form

PRACTICAL NOTE:
While paper forms are becoming less common with digitization, 申込用紙(もうしこみようし) is still widely used in Japan for bank accounts, gym memberships, community activities, and government services. Many institutions offer both paper and online options.
