チーズケーキ

ちーずけーき
noun
cheesecake
1. cheesecake
A rich dessert made with cream cheese or similar soft cheese, typically on a biscuit or pastry base. A popular café and patisserie item in Japan.
チーズケーキを一(ひと)つください。
One cheesecake, please.
このカフェのチーズケーキは濃厚(のうこう)で美味(おい)しい。
This café's cheesecake is rich and delicious.
誕生日(たんじょうび)に手作(てづく)りのチーズケーキを焼(や)いてプレゼントした。
I baked a homemade cheesecake as a birthday present.

From English "cheesecake." An extremely popular dessert in Japan, found in cafés, convenience stores, and patisseries. Japan distinguishes several types that learners will encounter on menus.

TYPES:

  • ベイクドチーズケーキ: baked cheesecake — dense and rich, the classic Western style
  • レアチーズケーキ: no-bake cheesecake — lighter, chilled, not baked
  • スフレチーズケーキ: soufflé cheesecake — light and fluffy, a Japanese specialty
  • バスクチーズケーキ: Basque cheesecake — caramelized top, became a major trend in Japan

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チーズケーキを焼(や)く: to bake a cheesecake
  • 濃厚(のうこう)なチーズケーキ: rich cheesecake
  • チーズケーキ専門店(せんもんてん): cheesecake specialty shop

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ショートケーキ: shortcake — Japan's most iconic cake, a sponge cake with strawberries and cream
  • ティラミス: tiramisu — another popular cream-based Western dessert
  • タルト: tart — pastry-based dessert, sometimes with cheese filling
