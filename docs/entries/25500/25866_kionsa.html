気温差(きおんさ)

きおんさ
noun
temperature difference; temperature gap
1. temperature difference; temperature gap
The difference in air temperature between two times, places, or conditions.
朝晩(あさばん)の気温差(きおんさ)が激(はげ)しい。
The temperature difference between morning and evening is extreme.
気温差(きおんさ)で体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)しやすい。
Temperature fluctuations make it easy to get sick.
室内(しつない)と室外(しつがい)の気温差(きおんさ)が大(おお)きいと結露(けつろ)が発生(はっせい)する。
When the temperature difference between indoors and outdoors is large, condensation forms.
春(はる)は一日(いちにち)の気温差(きおんさ)が十度(じゅうど)を超(こ)えることもある。
In spring, the daily temperature difference can exceed ten degrees.

Composed of 気温(きおん) (air temperature) + 差(さ) (difference). Very common in weather forecasts, health advice, and daily conversation, especially during seasonal transitions when large temperature swings can cause health issues.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気温差(きおんさ)が激(はげ)しい: the temperature difference is extreme
  • 気温差(きおんさ)が大(おお)きい: the temperature gap is large
  • 一日(いちにち)の気温差(きおんさ): daily temperature range
  • 朝晩(あさばん)の気温差(きおんさ): morning-evening temperature difference
  • 気温差(きおんさ)で風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)く: catch a cold due to temperature changes

USAGE:
Often appears in health-related contexts. Japanese weather reports frequently warn about 寒暖差(かんだんさ) (hot-cold difference), which is synonymous. The concept of 寒暖差(かんだんさ)疲労(ひろう) (fatigue from temperature swings) is commonly discussed in health media.
