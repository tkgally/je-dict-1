1. to lie hidden; to go into hiding; to lurk
To conceal oneself and remain undetected, especially to evade pursuit or avoid attention.
犯人(はんにん)は山中(さんちゅう)に潜伏(せんぷく)していた。
The criminal was hiding in the mountains.
スパイは何年(なんねん)も敵国(てきこく)に潜伏(せんぷく)していた。
The spy had been lying low in the enemy country for many years.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が国内(こくない)のどこかに潜伏(せんぷく)しているとみられている。
The suspect is believed to be hiding somewhere in the country.
2. to be latent; to incubate
For a disease, virus, or problem to exist in a hidden or dormant state before becoming apparent.
ウイルスは体内(たいない)に潜伏(せんぷく)する。
The virus lies latent in the body.
潜伏(せんぷく)期間(きかん)は約(やく)二(に)週間(しゅうかん)だ。
The incubation period is about two weeks.
この病気(びょうき)は長期間(ちょうきかん)潜伏(せんぷく)してから突然(とつぜん)発症(はっしょう)することがある。
This disease can remain latent for a long time before suddenly manifesting symptoms.

Composed of 潜伏(せんぷく) (concealment; latency) + する. The two senses share the core idea of something hidden and not yet visible. Sense 1 is common in crime and espionage contexts; sense 2 is widely used in medical and scientific writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 潜伏(せんぷく)期間(きかん): incubation period; latent period
  • 潜伏(せんぷく)先(さき): hiding place; place of concealment
  • 潜伏(せんぷく)感染(かんせん): latent infection
  • 国内(こくない)に潜伏(せんぷく)する: to hide within the country

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 隠(かく)れる: to hide — the basic everyday word for hiding; less formal
  • 潜(ひそ)む: to lurk; to lie hidden — literary; implies something dangerous lurking beneath the surface
  • 逃亡(とうぼう)する: to flee; to go on the run — emphasizes movement and escape rather than hiding in place
