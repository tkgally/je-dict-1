略装(りゃくそう)

りゃくそう
noun
informal dress; semi-formal attire; simplified outfit
1. informal dress; semi-formal attire; simplified outfit
Clothing that is less formal than full formal dress but still appropriate for the occasion. Used when an event or situation does not require full ceremonial or business attire.
略装(りゃくそう)でお越(こ)しください。
Please come in informal attire.
式(しき)は略装(りゃくそう)でも構(かま)いません。
Semi-formal dress is fine for the ceremony.
招待状(しょうたいじょう)に「略装(りゃくそう)にて」と書(か)いてあったので、スーツにネクタイなしで出席(しゅっせき)した。
The invitation said 'semi-formal attire,' so I attended in a suit without a tie.

Composed of 略(りゃく) (abbreviation; simplification) and 装(そう) (clothing; outfit). Commonly appears on invitations and event notices as a dress code indicator. In Japanese etiquette, 略装(りゃくそう) means the attendee need not wear the most formal version of attire — for men, this typically means a suit without a tie; for women, a smart outfit that is not a full formal gown.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 略装(りゃくそう)でお越(こ)しください: please come in informal attire
  • 略装(りゃくそう)にて: in informal dress (written on invitations)
  • 略装(りゃくそう)で参加(さんか)する: to attend in semi-formal attire

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正装(せいそう): full formal dress — the most formal level of attire for ceremonies
  • 平服(へいふく): everyday clothes; informal dress — often appears on invitations and can cause confusion since it actually means 'smart casual' rather than truly casual wear
  • カジュアル: casual — the most relaxed dress code, borrowed from English
