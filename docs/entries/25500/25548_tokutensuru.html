1. to score; to gain a point; to earn points
To earn one or more points in a game, match, or test.
後半(こうはん)に得点(とくてん)した。
They scored in the second half.
先制(せんせい)得点(とくてん)を挙(あ)げたのはホームチームだった。
It was the home team that scored the first point.
テストで高(たか)い得点(とくてん)を取(と)るために毎日(まいにち)勉強(べんきょう)している。
I study every day to get a high score on the test.

得点(とくてん)する combines 得(とく) (gain) and 点(てん) (point). As a verb, it means to score in a game; as a noun, it means the score or points earned. It is used for sports, games, and tests.

USAGE:

  • 得点(とくてん)する: to score (intransitive)
  • 得点(とくてん)を挙(あ)げる: to score a point (more formal)
  • 得点(とくてん)を取(と)る: to earn points / get a score

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先制(せんせい)得点(とくてん): opening score; first point
  • 最高(さいこう)得点(とくてん): highest score
  • 合計(ごうけい)得点(とくてん): total score
  • 得点(とくてん)王(おう): top scorer
  • 得点(とくてん)差(さ): point difference

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 点(てん)を取(と)る: to get points — more casual, commonly used in everyday speech
  • 得点(とくてん)を入(い)れる: to put in a score — used in sports commentary
  • ゴールする: to score a goal — specific to goal-based sports like soccer
