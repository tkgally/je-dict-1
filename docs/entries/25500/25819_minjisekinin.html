民事責任(みんじせきにん)

みんじせきにん
noun
civil liability; civil responsibility
1. civil liability; civil responsibility
Legal responsibility under civil law for damages or losses caused to another party. This typically involves compensation through monetary payment, as opposed to criminal punishment.
加害者(かがいしゃ)は民事責任(みんじせきにん)を問(と)われた。
The perpetrator was held civilly liable.
交通事故(こうつうじこ)では刑事責任(けいじせきにん)とは別(べつ)に民事責任(みんじせきにん)も発生(はっせい)する。
In traffic accidents, civil liability arises separately from criminal liability.
未成年者(みせいねんしゃ)が損害(そんがい)を与(あた)えた場合(ばあい)、親(おや)が民事責任(みんじせきにん)を負(お)うことがある。
When a minor causes damage, the parents may bear civil liability.

A compound of 民事(みんじ) (civil affairs/law) and 責任(せきにん) (responsibility, liability). A fundamental legal term distinguishing civil disputes from criminal matters.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 民事責任(みんじせきにん)を負(お)う — to bear civil liability
  • 民事責任(みんじせきにん)を問(と)う — to hold someone civilly liable
  • 民事責任(みんじせきにん)を追及(ついきゅう)する — to pursue civil liability
  • 民事責任(みんじせきにん)が発生(はっせい)する — for civil liability to arise

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刑事責任(けいじせきにん) — criminal liability (punished by the state through fines, imprisonment, etc.)
  • 損害賠償(そんがいばいしょう)責任(せきにん) — liability for damages (a specific type of civil liability)
  • 不法行為(ふほうこうい) — tort; unlawful act (a common basis for civil liability)

KEY DISTINCTION:
民事責任(みんじせきにん) is about compensating the victim (paying damages), while 刑事責任(けいじせきにん) is about punishment by the state. The same act (such as assault or a traffic accident) can give rise to both types of liability simultaneously.
