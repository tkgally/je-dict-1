1. hasty writing; scribble; scrawl
Writing done quickly and carelessly, often illegible or abbreviated. Used for quick notes jotted down in a hurry.
メモに走(はし)り書(が)きした。
I scribbled it on a memo.
走(はし)り書(が)きで読(よ)みにくい字(じ)だった。
The writing was hard to read because it was scrawled.
電話(でんわ)の内容(ないよう)を走(はし)り書(が)きで書(か)き留(と)めた。
I jotted down the contents of the phone call in a hasty scrawl.
机(つくえ)の上(うえ)に走(はし)り書(が)きの付箋(ふせん)が貼(は)ってあった。
There was a hastily scribbled sticky note on the desk.

Composed of 走(はし)り (running) + 書(が)き (writing) — literally "running writing," conveying the idea of writing done at speed without care for neatness. Can function as a noun or as a suru verb (走(はし)り書(が)きする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 走(はし)り書(が)きする: scribble quickly
  • 走(はし)り書(が)きのメモ: a hastily scribbled note
  • 走(はし)り書(が)きで書(か)く: write in a scrawl
  • 走(はし)り書(が)きで読(よ)めない: can't read because it's scribbled

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 殴(なぐ)り書(が)き: scrawl (rougher/messier connotation)
  • 書(か)き殴(なぐ)る: dash off writing angrily or carelessly
  • 落書(らくが)き: doodling, graffiti (different nuance — not about speed)
