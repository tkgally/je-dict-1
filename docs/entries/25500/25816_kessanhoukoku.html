決算報告(けっさんほうこく)

けっさんほうこく
noun
financial report; settlement of accounts report
1. financial report; settlement of accounts report
A formal report summarizing an organization's financial performance for a given period, including revenue, expenses, profits, and losses. Presented to shareholders, members, or stakeholders.
今期(こんき)の決算報告(けっさんほうこく)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
This term's financial report was announced.
株主総会(かぶぬしそうかい)で決算報告(けっさんほうこく)を行(おこな)った。
The financial report was presented at the shareholders' meeting.
決算報告(けっさんほうこく)によると、前年比(ぜんねんひ)で売上(うりあげ)が二(に)割(わり)増加(ぞうか)している。
According to the financial report, sales increased by 20% compared to the previous year.

A compound of 決算(けっさん) (settlement of accounts, closing of books) and 報告(ほうこく) (report). A fundamental term in business and organizational management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 決算報告(けっさんほうこく)を行(おこな)う — to present a financial report
  • 決算報告(けっさんほうこく)を発表(はっぴょう)する — to announce a financial report
  • 決算報告(けっさんほうこく)書(しょ) — financial report document
  • 中間(ちゅうかん)決算報告(けっさんほうこく) — interim financial report
  • 年度(ねんど)決算報告(けっさんほうこく) — annual financial report

RELATED TERMS:

  • 決算(けっさん) — settlement of accounts; closing of books
  • 貸借対照表(たいしゃくたいしょうひょう) — balance sheet
  • 損益計算書(そんえきけいさんしょ) — profit and loss statement
  • 決算書(けっさんしょ) — financial statements (the actual documents)
  • 監査報告(かんさほうこく) — audit report

CONTEXT:
In Japan, publicly listed companies are required to publish 決算報告(けっさんほうこく) quarterly. The fiscal year for most Japanese companies ends in March, making April and May a busy season for financial reporting. Non-profit organizations, condominiums (管理組合(かんりくみあい)), and other groups also produce 決算報告(けっさんほうこく) for their members.
