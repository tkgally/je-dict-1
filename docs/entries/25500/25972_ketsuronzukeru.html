1. to conclude; to reach a conclusion; to determine
To arrive at a final judgment or decision after considering evidence or arguments.
調査(ちょうさ)の結果(けっか)、安全(あんぜん)だと結論(けつろん)づけた。
As a result of the investigation, they concluded it was safe.
早(はや)く結論(けつろん)づけるのは危険(きけん)だ。
It's dangerous to jump to conclusions.
専門家(せんもんか)たちは長(なが)い議論(ぎろん)の末(すえ)、その計画(けいかく)は実現(じつげん)不可能(ふかのう)だと結論(けつろん)づけた。
After lengthy deliberation, the experts concluded that the plan was impossible to implement.

Formed from 結論(けつろん) (conclusion) + づける (to attach; to establish). An ichidan verb conjugated as a regular -eru verb. Commonly used in academic, investigative, and formal contexts to describe reaching a final determination. Often appears in the past tense (結論(けつろん)づけた) to report findings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～だと結論(けつろん)づける: to conclude that ~
  • ～と結論(けつろん)づけられる: it is concluded that ~
  • 最終的(さいしゅうてき)に結論(けつろん)づける: to finally conclude
  • 安易(あんい)に結論(けつろん)づける: to conclude too easily

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 結論(けつろん)を出(だ)す: to reach a conclusion — more general and common in everyday speech
  • 断定(だんてい)する: to determine; to assert — stronger, implies certainty without room for doubt
  • 判断(はんだん)する: to judge — broader, covering any kind of decision or assessment
