割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)

わりびきかかく
noun
discounted price; reduced price
1. discounted price; reduced price; sale price
A price that has been lowered from the original or standard amount. Used in shopping, business, and everyday commerce.
割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)で購入(こうにゅう)できる。
You can buy it at a discounted price.
会員(かいいん)は割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)が適用(てきよう)される。
The discounted price applies to members.
期間(きかん)限定(げんてい)の割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)で販売(はんばい)しておりますので、この機会(きかい)にぜひどうぞ。
We are selling at a limited-time discounted price, so please take advantage of this opportunity.

A straightforward compound of 割引(わりびき) (discount) and 価格(かかく) (price). Widely used in advertising, store signage, and online shopping. While 割引(わりびき) alone can modify nouns, the full compound 割引(わりびき)価格(かかく) is preferred when the focus is specifically on the numerical price.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)で買(か)う: to buy at a discounted price
  • 割引(わりびき)価格(かかく)が適用(てきよう)される: a discount is applied
  • 特別(とくべつ)割引(わりびき)価格(かかく): special discounted price
  • 学生(がくせい)割引(わりびき)価格(かかく): student discount price

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定価(ていか): list price; retail price — the original price before any discount
  • 半額(はんがく): half price — a specific type of discount
  • 特価(とっか): special price; bargain price — implies a particularly good deal
