1. to finish writing
To complete the act of writing something; to write to the end.
レポートを書(か)き終(お)えた。
I finished writing the report.
手紙(てがみ)を書(か)き終(お)えてからポストに入(い)れた。
I put it in the mailbox after finishing the letter.
小説(しょうせつ)を書(か)き終(お)えるのに三年(さんねん)かかった。
It took three years to finish writing the novel.
試験(しけん)時間(じかん)の終了(しゅうりょう)直前(ちょくぜん)にやっと全問(ぜんもん)書(か)き終(お)えることができた。
I was finally able to finish writing all the answers just before the exam time ran out.

A compound verb formed from 書(か)く (to write) + 終(お)える (to finish). The auxiliary verb 終(お)える attaches to the masu-stem of verbs to express completion of an action. This is one of the most common compound verb patterns in Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レポートを書(か)き終(お)える: finish writing a report
  • 手紙(てがみ)を書(か)き終(お)える: finish writing a letter
  • 原稿(げんこう)を書(か)き終(お)える: finish writing a manuscript
  • 論文(ろんぶん)を書(か)き終(お)える: finish writing a thesis

SIMILAR PATTERNS:

  • 読(よ)み終(お)える: finish reading
  • 食(た)べ終(お)える: finish eating
  • 飲(の)み終(お)える: finish drinking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)き上(あ)げる: finish writing (with nuance of completing a major work)
  • 書(か)き終(お)わる: finish writing (intransitive variant — the writing is finished)
