コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)

こみゅにけーしょんのうりょく
noun
communication skills; interpersonal skills
1. communication skills; interpersonal skills
The ability to communicate effectively with others, including speaking, listening, reading social cues, and building relationships. Highly valued in Japanese society and frequently cited as a key trait in job recruitment.
コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)が高(たか)い人(ひと)は職場(しょくば)で重宝(ちょうほう)される。
People with strong communication skills are valued in the workplace.
就活(しゅうかつ)ではコミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)が最(もっと)も重視(じゅうし)される。
In job hunting, communication skills are considered the most important quality.
コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)を高(たか)めるためには、日頃(ひごろ)から人(ひと)と積極的(せっきょくてき)に関(かか)わることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
To improve your communication skills, it's important to actively interact with people on a daily basis.

A compound of the loanword コミュニケーション (communication) and 能力(のうりょく) (ability). Extremely frequently abbreviated to コミュ力(りょく) (komyuryoku) or simply コミュ力(りょく) in casual speech and writing.

COMMON ABBREVIATION:
コミュ力(りょく) (komyuryoku) — the standard informal abbreviation, widely used in everyday conversation, social media, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い — to have high/low communication skills
  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)を磨(みが)く — to polish one's communication skills
  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)を高(たか)める — to improve communication skills
  • コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく)を問(と)う — to assess communication ability

RELATED TERMS:

  • コミュ障(しょう) — communication difficulties (slang, short for コミュニケーション障害(しょうがい); used self-deprecatingly for social awkwardness)
  • 対人(たいじん)能力(のうりょく) — interpersonal ability
  • 社交性(しゃこうせい) — sociability
  • 話術(わじゅつ) — art of conversation; speaking skills

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese employers consistently rank コミュニケーション能力(のうりょく) as the most desired quality in new graduates. In the Japanese context, this encompasses not just speaking ability but also the capacity to read the atmosphere (空気(くうき)を読(よ)む), collaborate harmoniously, and express oneself with appropriate sensitivity to social context.
