1. dimly lit; faintly bright
Having a small amount of light; not fully bright but not completely dark either.
薄明(うすあか)るい部屋(へや)で目(め)が覚(さ)めた。
I woke up in a dimly lit room.
カーテンの隙間(すきま)から薄明(うすあか)るい光(ひかり)が差(さ)し込(こ)んでいた。
A faint light was filtering through the gap in the curtains.
夜明(よあ)け前(まえ)の薄明(うすあか)るい空(そら)を見上(みあ)げながら、静(しず)かに散歩(さんぽ)した。
I took a quiet walk while looking up at the faintly bright sky before dawn.

A compound i-adjective from 薄(うす) (thin, faint) and 明(あか)るい (bright). Describes the quality of soft, faint light — the kind found at dawn, dusk, or in rooms with indirect lighting. It evokes an atmospheric, often peaceful mood.

This word is commonly used in literary descriptions and everyday conversation about lighting conditions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薄明(うすあか)るい部屋(へや): a dimly lit room
  • 薄明(うすあか)るい空(そら): a faintly bright sky
  • 薄明(うすあか)るい光(ひかり): a faint light
  • 薄明(うすあか)るい廊下(ろうか): a dimly lit hallway

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 薄暗(うすぐら)い: dim, dusky — emphasizes darkness rather than light; the opposite perspective
  • 仄(ほの)かな: faint, subtle — more literary, applies to light, warmth, or feelings
  • 明(あか)るい: bright — the base adjective without the diminishing prefix
