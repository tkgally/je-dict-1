準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)

じゅんびかんりょう
noun
preparation complete; all set; ready to go
1. preparation complete; all set; ready to go
the state of having finished all necessary preparations
準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)。いつでも始(はじ)めら��ます。
Preparations are complete. We can start anytime.
出発(しゅっぱつ)の準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)を確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed that preparations for departure were complete.
全員(ぜんいん)の準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)を待(ま)ってから会議(かいぎ)を始(はじ)める。
We'll start the meeting once everyone is ready.

A concise compound often used as a status report or announcement. Common in both spoken and written Japanese, from casual situations to military and business operations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)です: preparations are complete (polite)
  • 準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう)を報告(ほうこく)する: to report that preparations are complete
  • 打(う)ち上(あ)げ準備完了(じゅんびかんりょう): launch preparations complete

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 用意(ようい)ができた: ready, prepared — more casual and spoken
  • スタンバイ完了(かんりょう): standby complete — uses the English loanword, common in technical or broadcast contexts
  • 態勢(たいせい)が整(ととの)った: systems are in order, ready — more formal and often used for organizational readiness

REGISTER:
Neutral. Used across contexts from casual to formal. Especially common in status updates, checklists, and operational communication.
