肉感的(にくかんてき)な

にくかんてきな
adjective
voluptuous; sensual; fleshy
1. voluptuous; sensual; curvaceous
Having a full, shapely body that conveys physical attractiveness or sensuality. Used to describe someone's figure or physical presence in a way that emphasizes bodily appeal.
肉感的(にくかんてき)な体(からだ)つきだ。
She has a voluptuous figure.
その女優(じょゆう)は肉感的(にくかんてき)な魅力(みりょく)で人気(にんき)がある。
That actress is popular for her sensual appeal.
ルネサンスの絵画(かいが)には肉感的(にくかんてき)な人物(じんぶつ)が多(おお)く描(えが)かれている。
Renaissance paintings often depict voluptuous figures.

A na-adjective formed from 肉感(にくかん) (fleshiness; sensuality) + 的(てき). Literally means "flesh-feeling-like," conveying a physical, bodily quality. Used primarily to describe women's figures in literature, art criticism, and entertainment.

The word occupies a space between neutral description and mild suggestiveness. It is more refined than overtly sexual language but clearly references physical attractiveness. Common in art criticism when describing painted or sculpted figures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肉感的(にくかんてき)な体(からだ)つき: voluptuous build/figure
  • 肉感的(にくかんてき)な魅力(みりょく): sensual charm
  • 肉感的(にくかんてき)な唇(くちびる): full/sensual lips
  • 肉感的(にくかんてき)な美(うつく)しさ: voluptuous beauty

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • グラマラスな: glamorous — loanword focusing on a curvy, attractive figure
  • セクシーな: sexy — more casual loanword
  • 豊満(ほうまん)な: plump; full-figured — focuses on fullness of body without the sensual connotation
