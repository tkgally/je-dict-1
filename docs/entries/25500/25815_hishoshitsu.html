秘書室(ひしょしつ)

ひしょしつ
noun
secretarial office; secretary's department
1. secretarial office; secretary's department
A department or office within an organization that handles secretarial functions, typically managing the schedule, correspondence, and affairs of senior executives or officials.
秘書室(ひしょしつ)に電話(でんわ)をかけた。
I called the secretarial office.
社長(しゃちょう)のスケジュールは秘書室(ひしょしつ)が管理(かんり)している。
The president's schedule is managed by the secretarial office.
来客(らいきゃく)の対応(たいおう)は秘書室(ひしょしつ)を通(とお)して行(おこな)ってください。
Please handle visitor reception through the secretarial office.

A compound of 秘書(ひしょ) (secretary) and 室(しつ) (room, office, department). In Japanese organizations, 秘書室(ひしょしつ) functions as a formal department rather than just a physical room.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 秘書室(ひしょしつ)長(ちょう) — head of the secretarial office
  • 秘書室(ひしょしつ)に配属(はいぞく)される — to be assigned to the secretarial department
  • 秘書室(ひしょしつ)を通(とお)す — to go through the secretarial office (for requests)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 秘書(ひしょ) — secretary
  • 秘書課(ひしょか) — secretarial section (an alternative organizational name, essentially the same function)
  • 総務部(そうむぶ) — general affairs department (a broader department that sometimes includes secretarial functions)
  • 受付(うけつけ) — reception desk (handles visitor greeting, a different function)

USAGE NOTE:
In Japanese companies, the 秘書室(ひしょしつ) typically serves the 社長(しゃちょう) (president) and 役員(やくいん) (executives). In government, it serves the 大臣(だいじん) (minister) or 知事(ちじ) (governor). The department acts as a gatekeeper controlling access to senior leaders.
