1. to run around busily; to make strenuous efforts; to hustle; to work hard (for a cause)
To expend great energy rushing about in order to accomplish something, typically seeking help, making arrangements, or working to solve a problem.
資金(しきん)集(あつ)めに奔走(ほんそう)する。
He runs around trying to raise funds.
市長(しちょう)は災害(さいがい)対策(たいさく)のために奔走(ほんそう)した。
The mayor hustled to implement disaster countermeasures.
彼女(かのじょ)は問題(もんだい)解決(かいけつ)のために各方面(かくほうめん)に奔走(ほんそう)してくれた。
She ran around to various parties trying to resolve the problem for us.

奔走(ほんそう)する paints a picture of someone dashing about with energy and purpose. It carries a positive nuance of dedication and effort, implying the person is working hard for a worthy cause rather than running around aimlessly. Commonly used in news and formal writing.

USAGE:

  • ～のために奔走(ほんそう)する: to hustle for the sake of ~
  • ～に奔走(ほんそう)する: to busy oneself with ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 資金(しきん)集(あつ)めに奔走(ほんそう)する: to hustle to raise funds
  • 解決(かいけつ)に奔走(ほんそう)する: to work hard to find a solution
  • 東奔西走(とうほんせいそう): rushing about in all directions (yojijukugo)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 走(はし)り回(まわ)る: to run around — literal physical running, without the positive "working hard" nuance
  • 尽力(じんりょく)する: to make every effort — formal, focuses on effort itself rather than the image of rushing about
  • 駆(か)け回(まわ)る: to rush around — more physical and literal than 奔走(ほんそう)
