年月日(ねんがっぴ)

ねんがっぴ
noun
date (year, month, and day)
1. date (year, month, and day)
A specific calendar date expressed as year, month, and day. Commonly used on official documents and forms where a full date must be written.
年月日(ねんがっぴ)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the date.
生年月日(せいねんがっぴ)は1990年(ねん)5月(がつ)10日(にち)です。
My date of birth is May 10, 1990.
契約書(けいやくしょ)に年月日(ねんがっぴ)と署名(しょめい)を忘(わす)れずに書(か)いてください。
Please don't forget to write the date and your signature on the contract.

A compound of 年(ねん) (year), 月(がつ) (month), and 日(ひ) (day). Very common on Japanese forms and official documents. In Japanese date format, the order is always year → month → day.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年月日(ねんがっぴ)を記入(きにゅう)する: to fill in the date
  • 生年月日(せいねんがっぴ): date of birth
  • 発行(はっこう)年月日(ねんがっぴ): date of issue
  • 届出(とどけで)年月日(ねんがっぴ): date of notification

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese official documents traditionally use the Japanese era system (和暦(われき)) for dates (e.g., 令和(れいわ)8年(ねん)), though Western calendar dates (西暦(せいれき)) are also widely accepted. The 生年月日(せいねんがっぴ) field appears on nearly every official form in Japan.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 日付(ひづけ): date — more general and conversational
  • 日時(にちじ): date and time
