業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)

ぎょうむれんらく
noun
work-related notice; internal business communication
1. work-related notice; internal business communication
An announcement or message related to work operations, typically circulated within an organization to share practical information.
業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)をメールで送(おく)る。
To send a business notice by email.
朝礼(ちょうれい)で業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)が伝(つた)えられた。
A work-related notice was communicated at the morning meeting.
全社員(ぜんしゃいん)への業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)として、来月(らいげつ)からシステムが変(か)わる旨(むね)が通知(つうち)された。
As a company-wide business notice, employees were informed that the system would change starting next month.

A compound of 業務(ぎょうむ) (business operations) and 連絡(れんらく) (contact, communication). Used in offices, shops, and organizations for practical announcements — schedule changes, system updates, reminders, etc.

In retail and service settings, 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく) is often heard over a PA system as a staff-only announcement, signaling that the message is for employees rather than customers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)です: "This is a staff announcement" (PA system)
  • 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)メール: business communication email
  • 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)を回(まわ)す: to circulate a work notice
  • 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく)事項(じこう): items for business communication

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 社内連絡(しゃないれんらく): internal company communication — similar but specifically within a company, while 業務連絡(ぎょうむれんらく) can be used in non-corporate settings too
  • 通達(つうたつ): official directive — more formal and authoritative, often from management to subordinates
  • お{知(し)らせ}: notice, announcement — more general and can be directed at customers or the public
