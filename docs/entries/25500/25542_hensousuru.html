1. to disguise oneself; to dress up as; to put on a disguise
To change one's appearance using clothing, wigs, makeup, or other means in order to conceal one's identity or to look like someone else.
警察官(けいさつかん)に変装(へんそう)する。
He disguises himself as a police officer.
有名人(ゆうめいじん)は変装(へんそう)して街(まち)を歩(ある)くことがある。
Celebrities sometimes walk around town in disguise.
犯人(はんにん)はサングラスとかつらで変装(へんそう)して逃(に)げたらしい。
Apparently, the criminal disguised himself with sunglasses and a wig and fled.

変装(へんそう)する means to alter one's outward appearance to avoid being recognized or to pretend to be someone else. Unlike 仮装(かそう)する, which is used for costume parties and Halloween, 変装(へんそう)する implies the intent to conceal identity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～に変装(へんそう)する: to disguise oneself as ~
  • 変装(へんそう)を見破(みやぶ)る: to see through a disguise
  • 変装(へんそう)を解(と)く: to remove a disguise
  • 変装(へんそう)用(よう)の衣装(いしょう): disguise outfit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仮装(かそう)する: to dress up in costume — for fun or events like Halloween, without the intent to deceive
  • 扮装(ふんそう)する: to dress up as a character — theatrical, for acting or performances
  • 化(ば)ける: to transform; to disguise — informal, also used for supernatural shape-shifting in folklore
