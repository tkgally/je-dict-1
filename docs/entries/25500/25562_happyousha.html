発表者(はっぴょうしゃ)

はっぴょうしゃ
noun
presenter; speaker; person making an announcement
1. presenter; speaker; person making an announcement
a person who presents information, gives a talk, or makes a public announcement
次(つぎ)の発表者(はっぴょうしゃ)はどなたですか。
Who is the next presenter?
発表者(はっぴょうしゃ)は資料(しりょう)を事前(じぜん)に準備(じゅんび)してください。
Presenters, please prepare your materials in advance.
学会(がっかい)では各(かく)発表者(はっぴょうしゃ)に十五分(じゅうごふん)の持(も)ち時間(じかん)が与(あた)えられる。
At the conference, each presenter is given fifteen minutes.

A straightforward compound of 発表(はっぴょう) (presentation, announcement) and the suffix 者(しゃ) (person). Used in academic, business, and formal settings for anyone giving a presentation or talk.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 基調(きちょう)発表者(はっぴょうしゃ): keynote speaker
  • 研究(けんきゅう)発表者(はっぴょうしゃ): research presenter
  • 次(つぎ)の発表者(はっぴょうしゃ): next presenter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 講演者(こうえんしゃ): lecturer — specifically someone giving a formal lecture or talk
  • 報告者(ほうこくしゃ): reporter, person making a report — in academic or meeting contexts
  • プレゼンター: presenter — English loanword, used more casually or in media contexts

REGISTER:
Neutral to formal. Common in academic conferences, meetings, and school settings.
