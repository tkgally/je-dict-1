配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)

はいたつしょうめい
noun
proof of delivery; delivery certification
1. proof of delivery; delivery certification; certified delivery
A postal service that provides the sender with official proof that a letter or package was delivered to the recipient. In Japan, this is a service offered by Japan Post for registered mail.
大事(だいじ)な書類(しょるい)なので配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)で送(おく)った。
Since it was an important document, I sent it with proof of delivery.
配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)をつけると、届(とど)いた日時(にちじ)が記録(きろく)される。
When you add delivery certification, the date and time of delivery are recorded.
契約(けいやく)解除(かいじょ)の通知(つうち)は、内容証明(ないようしょうめい)郵便(ゆうびん)に配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)をつけて送(おく)るのが一般的(いっぱんてき)だ。
It is common to send a contract cancellation notice by certified content mail with delivery certification.

A compound of 配達(はいたつ) (delivery) and 証明(しょうめい) (proof, certification). Refers to a Japan Post service that provides the sender with a postcard confirming when the item was delivered.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)をつける — to add delivery certification
  • 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)で送(おく)る — to send with proof of delivery
  • 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)郵便(ゆうびん) — certified delivery mail
  • 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい)付(つ)き — with delivery certification attached

RELATED TERMS:

  • 書留(かきとめ) — registered mail (tracked and insured; 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい) is an add-on to this)
  • 内容証明(ないようしょうめい) — certified content mail (certifies what was written in the letter; often combined with 配達証明(はいたつしょうめい))
  • 速達(そくたつ) — express delivery
  • 普通郵便(ふつうゆうびん) — regular mail

PRACTICAL NOTE:
配達証明(はいたつしょうめい) is commonly used for legal or official correspondence where the sender needs proof that a document was received. Typical uses include contract termination notices, demand letters, and formal complaints. It must be combined with 書留(かきとめ) (registered mail) and cannot be used alone.
