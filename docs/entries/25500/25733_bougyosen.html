防御線(ぼうぎょせん)

ぼうぎょせん
noun
line of defense; defensive line
1. line of defense; defensive line
A position or boundary established to defend against attack. Used both literally in military and sports contexts, and figuratively for any boundary one tries to hold.
防御線(ぼうぎょせん)を張(は)る。
To set up a line of defense.
敵(てき)の攻撃(こうげき)で防御線(ぼうぎょせん)が崩(くず)れた。
The defensive line collapsed under the enemy's attack.
交渉(こうしょう)では、これ以上(いじょう)値下(ねさ)げしないという防御線(ぼうぎょせん)を守(まも)り通(とお)した。
In the negotiation, I held the line on not lowering the price any further.

Composed of 防御(ぼうぎょ) (defense, protection) + 線(せん) (line). Used literally for military positions and sports formations, and figuratively in business, politics, and daily life for any boundary one tries to maintain.
