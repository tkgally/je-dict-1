水風船(みずふうせん)

みずふうせん
noun
water balloon
1. water balloon
a small balloon filled with water, used as a toy or in festival games
水風船(みずふうせん)を投(な)げて遊(あそ)んだ。
We played by throwing water balloons.
夏祭(なつまつ)りで水風船(みずふうせん)すくいをした。
I played water balloon scooping at the summer festival.
子(こ)どもたちが庭(にわ)で水風船(みずふうせん)を割(わ)り合(あ)って大(おお)はしゃぎしていた。
The children were having a blast bursting water balloons at each other in the garden.

A common summer toy in Japan. Small, colorful water balloons are a staple at Japanese summer festivals (夏祭(なつまつ)り), where 水風船(みずふうせん)すくい (water balloon scooping) is a popular game — players try to scoop floating water balloons with a paper hook.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水風船(みずふうせん)すくい: water balloon scooping (festival game)
  • 水風船(みずふうせん)を投(な)げる: to throw water balloons
  • 水風船(みずふうせん)を割(わ)る: to pop a water balloon
  • 水風船(みずふうせん)を作(つく)る: to make water balloons

CULTURAL NOTE:
At Japanese summer festivals, 水風船(みずふうせん)すくい stalls feature a tub of colorful floating water balloons. Players use a paper hook (紙(かみ)こより) to try to lift them out before the hook dissolves. The scooped balloons are kept as prizes.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 風船(ふうせん): balloon
  • ヨーヨー: yo-yo water balloon (another festival variant)
  • 金魚(きんぎょ)すくい: goldfish scooping (another classic festival game)
