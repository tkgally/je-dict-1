無思慮(むしりょ)

むしりょ
noun
thoughtlessness; lack of consideration; recklessness
1. thoughtlessness; lack of consideration; recklessness
The state of acting or speaking without sufficient thought or consideration for the consequences.
無思慮(むしりょ)な発言(はつげん)をしてしまった。
I made a thoughtless remark.
若(わか)い頃(ころ)の無思慮(むしりょ)な行動(こうどう)を反省(はんせい)している。
I regret the reckless actions of my youth.
無思慮(むしりょ)にSNSに投稿(とうこう)すると取(と)��返(かえ)しのつかないことになる。
Posting thoughtlessly on social media can lead to irreversible consequences.

無(む) (without) + 思慮(しりょ) (consideration, deliberation). Can function as a noun or as a na-adjective (無思慮(むしりょ)な). Describes a lack of careful thought, often with a critical nuance suggesting the person should have known better.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無思慮(むしりょ)な行動(こうどう) — thoughtless behavior
  • 無思慮(むしりょ)な発言(はつげん) — thoughtless remark
  • 無思慮(むしりょ)に — thoughtlessly (adverbial use)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 軽率(けいそつ) — rash; hasty — emphasizes acting too quickly without thinking
  • 不注意(ふちゅうい) — carelessness; inattention — focuses on lack of attention rather than lack of thought
  • 思慮(しりょ)深(ぶか)い — thoughtful; prudent — the positive opposite
