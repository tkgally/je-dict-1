これより

これより
expression
from now; from this point on; hereafter
1. from now; from this point on; hereafter
A formal expression indicating the start of something or marking the transition to what comes next. Used to signal that an event, announcement, or action is about to begin.
これより会議(かいぎ)を始(はじ)めます。
We will now begin the meeting.
これより式(しき)を執(と)り行(おこな)います。
The ceremony will now commence.
これより先(さき)の発言(はつげん)は議事録(ぎじろく)に記録(きろく)されますので、ご注意(ちゅうい)ください。
Please be advised that all statements from this point on will be recorded in the minutes.
2. beyond this point; from here onward
Indicates a spatial boundary, marking the area or territory that lies beyond the current position.
これより先(さき)、立入禁止(たちいりきんし)。
No entry beyond this point.
これより北(きた)は雪国(ゆきぐに)だ。
North of here is snow country.
これより奥(おく)は登山(とざん)道(どう)が険(けわ)しくなりますので、装備(そうび)を確認(かくにん)してください。
The mountain trail becomes steep beyond this point, so please check your equipment.

A formal, somewhat literary expression composed of これ (this) + より (from). Common in official announcements, ceremonies, signage, and formal writing. In everyday speech, the equivalent would be 今(いま)から (from now) for time or ここから (from here) for space.

REGISTER:
Formal. Used in ceremonies, official proceedings, signs, and literary contexts. Rarely heard in casual conversation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • これより始(はじ)めます: we will now begin
  • これより先(さき): beyond this point (temporal or spatial)
  • これより後(あと): after this

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 今(いま)から: from now — the everyday equivalent for temporal use
  • ここから: from here — the everyday equivalent for spatial use
  • 以後(いご): hereafter; from now on — formal, emphasizes ongoing applicability
  • 今後(こんご): from now on; henceforth — formal, forward-looking
