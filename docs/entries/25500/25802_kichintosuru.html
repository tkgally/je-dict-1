1. to be neat, tidy, or orderly
Describes the state or act of being physically well-organized, clean, or properly arranged.
部屋(へや)をきちんとしなさい。
Tidy up your room.
きちんとした服装(ふくそう)で面接(めんせつ)に行(い)った。
I went to the interview in neat attire.
本棚(ほんだな)の本(ほん)がきちんと並(なら)べてあるのを見(み)ると気持(きも)ちがいい。
It feels good to see the books on the shelf neatly arranged.
2. to be proper, punctual, or correct in conduct
Describes being responsible, diligent, or socially appropriate in one's behavior.
約束(やくそく)はきちんと守(まも)るべきだ。
You should properly keep your promises.
きちんと挨拶(あいさつ)ができる人(ひと)は好(す)かれる。
People who can greet others properly are well-liked.
毎朝(まいあさ)きちんと朝(あさ)ごはんを食(た)べて、時間通(じかんどお)りに出勤(しゅっきん)している。
Every morning I eat breakfast properly and commute on time.

A versatile mimetic adverb meaning neatly, properly, or precisely. Used both as an adverb (きちんと + verb) and as a suru-verb (きちんとする = to be neat/proper). The した form is commonly used as a modifier: きちんとした = neat, proper, respectable.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • きちんと + verb — does something neatly/properly
  • きちんとする — to tidy up; to be proper
  • きちんとした + noun — neat, proper, well-organized (noun)
  • きちんとしている — is neat/proper (ongoing state)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • きちんとした格好(かっこう) — neat appearance
  • きちんと片付(かたづ)ける — to tidy up properly
  • きちんと整理(せいり)する — to organize neatly
  • きちんとした生活(せいかつ) — orderly lifestyle
  • きちんと説明(せつめい)する — to explain properly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ちゃんと — similar meaning but more colloquial and slightly childish in tone; きちんと is more formal and adult-sounding
  • しっかり — emphasizes firmness and reliability rather than neatness; overlaps with sense 2 but implies strength or thoroughness
  • きっちり — emphasizes exactness and precision; more about fitting perfectly or being exact to a measurement

REGISTER:
Neutral to slightly formal. Appropriate in both spoken and written Japanese. Preferred over ちゃんと in business and formal situations.
