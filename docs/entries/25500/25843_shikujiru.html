1. to fail; to blunder; to mess up; to botch
To fail at something due to a mistake or carelessness, especially when the failure has noticeable consequences. Implies personal responsibility for the failure.
試験(しけん)でしくじった。
I blew the exam.
大事(だいじ)な商談(しょうだん)でしくじってしまった。
I messed up an important business deal.
一度(いちど)しくじったぐらいであきらめるな。
Don't give up just because you failed once.
彼(かれ)は投資(とうし)でしくじって大金(たいきん)を失(うしな)った。
He messed up with his investments and lost a large sum of money.

A colloquial verb with a blunt, honest tone. Often used when taking responsibility for one's own failure or when describing someone's mistake candidly. The TV show 「しくじり先生(せんせい)」 (Shikujiri Sensei — "Teachers Who Blew It"), where celebrities talk about their past failures, made this word widely recognized.

TRANSITIVITY:
Can be used both transitively (試験(しけん)をしくじる — to fail an exam) and intransitively (商談(しょうだん)でしくじる — to mess up in a business deal).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)をしくじる: to mess up at work
  • 試験(しけん)をしくじる: to fail an exam
  • しくじり先生(せんせい): a teacher who failed (TV show title)
  • 一度(いちど)しくじる: to fail once

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 失敗(しっぱい)する: to fail — neutral and widely used; lacks the colloquial punch of しくじる
  • へまをする: to make a blunder — colloquial, often for minor practical mistakes
  • やらかす: to screw up — very colloquial/slangy, often implies the mistake was embarrassing
