あれやこれや

あれやこれや
noun
this and that; various things; one thing and another
1. this and that; various things; one thing and another
An expression referring to a variety of different things, matters, or concerns without specifying them individually.
あれやこれやと忙(いそが)しい。
I'm busy with this and that.
あれやこれやと考(かんが)えているうちに、夜(よる)が明(あ)けてしまった。
While I was thinking about one thing and another, dawn came.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)であれやこれやと買(か)い物(もの)をしていたら、思(おも)った以上(いじょう)にお金(かね)がかかった。
Buying this and that in preparation for the move ended up costing more than I expected.

Formed from あれ (that) + や (and) + これ (this) + や (and). Often followed by the particle と when used adverbially. Conveys a sense of dealing with multiple scattered matters, often with a nuance of being overwhelmed or busy. Can be used both for physical things and abstract concerns.

USAGE:

  • あれやこれやと + verb: doing various things
  • あれやこれやで + result: because of various things

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あれこれ: this and that — shorter, more common form with essentially the same meaning
  • 色々(いろいろ): various — more neutral, simply indicating variety without the scattered nuance
  • 何(なに)やかや: one thing or another — similar nuance of miscellaneous items or concerns
