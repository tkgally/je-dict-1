なぞ解(と)き

なぞとき
noun
puzzle-solving; solving a riddle; mystery-solving
1. puzzle-solving; solving a riddle; mystery-solving
The act of solving puzzles, riddles, or mysteries. Also used for the genre of entertainment based on solving puzzles.
なぞ解(と)きゲームが好(す)きだ。
I like puzzle-solving games.
探偵(たんてい)がなぞ解(と)きに挑(いど)む映画(えいが)を見(み)た。
I watched a movie where a detective tackles solving a mystery.
友達(ともだち)となぞ解(と)きイベントに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a puzzle-solving event with friends.
この小説(しょうせつ)は最後(さいご)のなぞ解(と)きの場面(ばめん)が一番(いちばん)面白(おもしろ)い。
The final mystery-solving scene in this novel is the most interesting part.

Composed of なぞ (riddle, mystery) + 解(と)き (solving, from 解(と)く). Can also be written as 謎解(なぞと)き with kanji. The word covers a broad range from children's riddles to elaborate escape rooms and detective fiction.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • なぞ解(と)きゲーム: puzzle-solving game
  • なぞ解(と)きイベント: puzzle-solving event
  • なぞ解(と)きに挑戦(ちょうせん)する: take on a puzzle challenge
  • なぞ解(と)き脱出(だっしゅつ)ゲーム: escape room game

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Puzzle-solving entertainment has become extremely popular in Japan. 謎解(なぞと)き脱出(だっしゅつ)ゲーム (escape room games) are a major form of group entertainment, with venues in most cities. TV shows and manga also feature なぞ解(と)き as a core element, especially in the detective genre.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推理(すいり): deduction, reasoning (more detective/logical)
  • パズル: puzzle (borrowed English word, often for jigsaw or logic puzzles)
  • クイズ: quiz (knowledge-based questions rather than mystery-solving)
