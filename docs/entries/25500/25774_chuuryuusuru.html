1. to station; to be stationed
For military forces to be deployed and remain in a location, typically in a foreign country. Used for troops, military bases, and peacekeeping forces.
米軍(べいぐん)が日本(にほん)に駐留(ちゅうりゅう)している。
U.S. forces are stationed in Japan.
国連(こくれん)平和維持軍(へいわいじぐん)がその地域(ちいき)に駐留(ちゅうりゅう)している。
UN peacekeeping forces are stationed in that region.
戦後(せんご)、連合国軍(れんごうこくぐん)が日本(にほん)各地(かくち)に駐留(ちゅうりゅう)し、占領(せんりょう)政策(せいさく)を実施(じっし)した。
After the war, Allied forces were stationed throughout Japan and carried out occupation policies.

A suru-verb formed from 駐留(ちゅうりゅう), combining 駐(ちゅう) (to stay/park) and 留(りゅう) (to remain). Specifically a military term — not used for civilian stays or visits.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)米軍(べいぐん): U.S. forces stationed (in Japan)
  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)軍(ぐん): stationed military forces
  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)経費(けいひ): stationing costs (host nation support)
  • 海外(かいがい)駐留(ちゅうりゅう): overseas stationing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 駐屯(ちゅうとん)する: to garrison — similar but emphasizes a permanent military base
  • 派遣(はけん)する: to dispatch — focuses on the act of sending forces, not the ongoing presence
  • 占領(せんりょう)する: to occupy — implies forceful control over territory
