学会誌(がっかいし)

がっかいし
noun
academic journal; scholarly journal
1. academic journal; scholarly journal; proceedings
A periodical published by an academic society or professional association, containing peer-reviewed research papers, reviews, and reports in a specific field.
学会誌(がっかいし)に論文(ろんぶん)を投稿(とうこう)した。
I submitted a paper to an academic journal.
この研究(けんきゅう)は学会誌(がっかいし)に掲載(けいさい)された。
This research was published in an academic journal.
最新(さいしん)の学会誌(がっかいし)を読(よ)んで、自分(じぶん)の分野(ぶんや)の動向(どうこう)を把握(はあく)するようにしている。
I make a point of reading the latest academic journals to keep up with trends in my field.

Composed of 学会(がっかい) (academic society) + 誌(し) (journal, magazine). Refers specifically to journals published by professional academic organizations, as opposed to commercial or popular science magazines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学会誌(がっかいし)に投稿(とうこう)する: to submit to an academic journal
  • 学会誌(がっかいし)に掲載(けいさい)される: to be published in an academic journal
  • 学会誌(がっかいし)を発行(はっこう)する: to publish an academic journal
  • 学会誌(がっかいし)の査読(さどく): peer review for an academic journal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 論文誌(ろんぶんし): research journal — focuses on papers rather than the society that publishes it
  • 紀要(きよう): bulletin — institutional publication, often from a university department
  • 学術雑誌(がくじゅつざっし): academic magazine — broader term that includes commercial academic publications
