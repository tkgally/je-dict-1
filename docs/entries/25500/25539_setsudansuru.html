1. to cut off; to sever; to amputate
To physically cut something into separate pieces or remove a part by cutting, especially something thick, solid, or substantial.
ロープを切断(せつだん)する。
I cut the rope.
事故(じこ)で鉄(てつ)パイプが切断(せつだん)された。
The steel pipe was severed in the accident.
医師(いし)は壊疽(えそ)した指(ゆび)を切断(せつだん)する判断(はんだん)をした。
The doctor decided to amputate the gangrenous finger.
2. to disconnect; to cut off (a connection)
To break or interrupt a connection, communication, or supply line.
通信(つうしん)が切断(せつだん)された。
The communication was cut off.
回線(かいせん)が突然(とつぜん)切断(せつだん)された。
The line was suddenly disconnected.
停電(ていでん)で電力(でんりょく)供給(きょうきゅう)が切断(せつだん)されたため、復旧(ふっきゅう)に時間(じかん)がかかった。
Because the power supply was cut off by a blackout, it took time to restore.

切断(せつだん)する implies a clean, decisive cut of something substantial — not a casual snip. In the physical sense, it often involves tools, force, or surgical procedures. In the figurative sense, it is used for communications, networks, and supply lines being severed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ケーブルを切断(せつだん)する: to cut a cable
  • 回線(かいせん)を切断(せつだん)する: to disconnect a line
  • 血管(けっかん)を切断(せつだん)する: to sever a blood vessel
  • 切断(せつだん)面(めん): cross-section; cut surface
  • 切断(せつだん)手術(しゅじゅつ): amputation surgery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(き)る: to cut — general-purpose; used for everyday cutting with scissors, knives, etc.
  • 断(た)つ: to sever; to cut off — literary, often used for abstract things like ties, habits, or supply
  • 遮断(しゃだん)する: to block; to shut off — focuses on blocking passage or flow rather than physically cutting
