勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)

きんぞくねんすう
noun
years of continuous service; length of employment
1. years of continuous service; length of employment
The total number of years an employee has worked continuously at the same company or organization.
勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)が長(なが)いほど退職金(たいしょくきん)が増(ふ)える。
The longer the years of service, the higher the severance pay.
勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)が二十年(にじゅうねん)を超(こ)えた社員(しゃいん)が表彰(ひょうしょう)された。
Employees with over twenty years of service were recognized.
日本(にほん)では勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)に応(おう)じて給与(きゅうよ)が上(あ)がる年功序列(ねんこうじょれつ)の会社(かいしゃ)がまだ多(おお)い。
In Japan, there are still many companies with seniority-based pay systems where salary increases with years of service.

A compound of 勤続(きんぞく) (continuous service) and 年数(ねんすう) (number of years). This concept is particularly important in Japan's traditional employment system, where 勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう) directly affects salary, bonuses, retirement benefits, and promotion under the 年功序列(ねんこうじょれつ) (seniority-based) system.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)に応(おう)じて: according to years of service
  • 勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)が浅(あさ)い: short tenure
  • 勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう)三十年(さんじゅうねん): thirty years of service
  • 平均(へいきん)勤続年数(きんぞくねんすう): average years of service

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 在職期間(ざいしょくきかん): period of employment — more formal, used in official documents
  • 年功(ねんこう): seniority, years of merit — the principle that rewards increase with length of service
  • 勤務歴(きんむれき): work history — broader, can cover multiple employers
