1. to occur; to break out; to arise; to happen
For an event, problem, or phenomenon to come into existence or start happening, often something undesirable or noteworthy.
事故(じこ)が発生(はっせい)した。
An accident occurred.
台風(たいふう)による被害(ひがい)が各地(かくち)で発生(はっせい)している。
Damage from the typhoon is occurring in various areas.
システムにエラーが発生(はっせい)したため、再起動(さいきどう)してください。
An error has occurred in the system, so please restart.
2. to generate; to produce; to emit
For something to be produced or given off as a result of a process.
熱(ねつ)が発生(はっせい)する。
Heat is generated.
この化学反応(かがくはんのう)ではガスが発生(はっせい)する。
Gas is produced in this chemical reaction.
工場(こうじょう)から大量(たいりょう)の二酸化炭素(にさんかたんそ)が発生(はっせい)している。
Large amounts of carbon dioxide are being produced from the factory.

発生(はっせい)する describes something coming into being, with two main uses: events/problems occurring (sense 1) and substances being generated (sense 2). In sense 1, it is commonly used in news reports and formal contexts for incidents, disasters, and problems. The subject takes が.

USAGE:

  • ～が発生(はっせい)する (intransitive: something occurs / is generated)
  • ～を発生(はっせい)させる (causative: to cause something to occur / generate something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)が発生(はっせい)する: an accident occurs
  • 問題(もんだい)が発生(はっせい)する: a problem arises
  • 地震(じしん)が発生(はっせい)する: an earthquake occurs
  • エラーが発生(はっせい)する: an error occurs
  • 熱(ねつ)が発生(はっせい)する: heat is generated

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 起(お)きる: to happen; to occur — more general and conversational, used for everyday events
  • 生(しょう)じる: to arise; to occur — literary/formal, often for abstract things like doubts or differences
  • 発生(はっせい): as a noun, means occurrence or generation
