鮮魚店(せんぎょてん)

せんぎょてん
noun
fresh fish shop; fishmonger
1. fresh fish shop; fishmonger
A shop that specializes in selling fresh fish and seafood.
鮮魚店(せんぎょてん)で刺身(さしみ)を買(か)う。
To buy sashimi at a fresh fish shop.
商店街(しょうてんがい)の鮮魚店(せんぎょてん)は朝(あさ)が早(はや)い。
The fish shop in the shopping street opens early in the morning.
地元(じもと)の鮮魚店(せんぎょてん)には毎朝(まいあさ)新鮮(しんせん)な魚(さかな)が並(なら)ぶ。
Fresh fish is displayed every morning at the local fish shop.

鮮魚(せんぎょ) (fresh fish) + 店(てん) (shop). A traditional shop type found in shopping streets and markets throughout Japan. While supermarkets have largely replaced them for everyday shopping, 鮮魚店(せんぎょてん) remain popular at markets and in areas near fishing ports for their superior quality and expert knowledge.

  • 老舗(しにせ)の鮮魚店(せんぎょてん) — long-established fish shop
  • 鮮魚店(せんぎょてん)の店主(てんしゅ) — fish shop owner

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鮮魚(せんぎょ) — fresh fish — the product itself
  • 魚市場(うおいちば) — fish market — a larger wholesale or retail market for fish
