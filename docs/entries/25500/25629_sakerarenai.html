避(さ)けられない

さけられない
expression
unavoidable; inevitable; cannot be avoided
1. unavoidable; inevitable; cannot be avoided
Something that cannot be escaped or prevented, no matter what measures are taken.
老化(ろうか)は避(さ)けられない。
Aging is unavoidable.
この問題(もんだい)との対決(たいけつ)は避(さ)けられない。
A confrontation with this problem is unavoidable.
グローバル化(か)が進(すす)む中(なか)で、異文化(いぶんか)との接触(せっしょく)は避(さ)けられないものとなっている。
As globalization advances, contact with other cultures has become something that cannot be avoided.

The negative potential form of 避(さ)ける (to avoid). While grammatically a verb form, it functions as a set expression equivalent to English "unavoidable" or "inevitable." Very common in both spoken and written Japanese for discussing things beyond one's control.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 避(さ)けられない問題(もんだい): unavoidable problem
  • 避(さ)けられない運命(うんめい): inescapable fate
  • 避(さ)けられない結果(けっか): inevitable result
  • どうしても避(さ)けられない: absolutely unavoidable

USAGE:
Often used when acknowledging difficult realities or arguing that something must be addressed. The expression carries a tone of resignation or acceptance.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不可避(ふかひ): unavoidable — a more formal, written synonym (Sino-Japanese compound)
  • 免(まぬか)れない: cannot escape — similar meaning, slightly more literary
  • やむを得(え)ない: unavoidable, can't be helped — focuses on the lack of alternatives rather than the impossibility of avoidance
