登記簿(とうきぼ)

とうきぼ
noun
registry; register; official record book
1. registry; register; official record book
An official public record maintained by a government office, containing registered information about real estate, companies, or other legally significant matters.
登記簿(とうきぼ)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the registry.
不動産(ふどうさん)の登記簿(とうきぼ)謄本(とうほん)を取得(しゅとく)した。
I obtained a certified copy of the real estate registry.
土地(とち)を購入(こうにゅう)する際(さい)は、登記簿(とうきぼ)で所有者(しょゆうしゃ)を確認(かくにん)することが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
When purchasing land, it's important to verify the owner in the registry.

Composed of 登記(とうき) (registration; recording) + 簿(ぼ) (ledger; book). An essential term in Japanese real estate and corporate law. Anyone buying property or investigating a company in Japan will encounter this term. The 登記簿(とうきぼ)謄本(とうほん) (certified copy of the registry) is a key document in real estate transactions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 登記簿(とうきぼ)謄本(とうほん): certified copy of the registry
  • 不動産(ふどうさん)登記簿(とうきぼ): real estate registry
  • 商業(しょうぎょう)登記簿(とうきぼ): commercial registry
  • 登記簿(とうきぼ)を閲覧(えつらん)する: to inspect the registry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 戸籍(こせき): family register — records family relationships and personal status; different from property/corporate registration
  • 台帳(だいちょう): ledger; register — a more general term for any official record book
