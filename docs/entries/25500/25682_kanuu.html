カヌー

かぬー
noun
canoe; canoeing
1. canoe; canoeing
A light, narrow boat propelled by paddles. Also refers to the sport of canoeing, which includes both canoe and kayak disciplines in Japanese usage.
湖(みずうみ)でカヌーに乗(の)った。
I rode a canoe on the lake.
夏休(なつやす)みにカヌー体験(たいけん)に参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a canoeing experience during summer vacation.
川(かわ)の流(なが)れが穏(おだ)やかなので、初心者(しょしんしゃ)でも安心(あんしん)してカヌーを楽(たの)しむことができる。
The river current is gentle, so even beginners can enjoy canoeing with ease.

From English "canoe." In Japanese sports terminology, カヌー is the umbrella term covering both canoe (kneeling, single-blade paddle) and kayak (sitting, double-blade paddle) disciplines. It is an Olympic sport, and Japan has competitive athletes in both categories.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • カヌーに乗(の)る: to ride a canoe
  • カヌーを漕(こ)ぐ: to paddle a canoe
  • カヌー体験(たいけん): canoeing experience
  • カヌー競技(きょうぎ): canoe sport, canoeing competition

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カヤック: kayak — a specific type with a closed deck and double-bladed paddle
  • ボート: boat — a general term; in Japanese sports context, often refers to rowing
  • 丸木舟(まるきぶね): dugout canoe — a traditional carved-out log boat
