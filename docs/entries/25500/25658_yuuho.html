1. stroll; walking for pleasure; promenade
Walking leisurely for enjoyment or relaxation, rather than for a specific destination or purpose.
公園(こうえん)を遊歩(ゆうほ)する。
To stroll through the park.
湖畔(こはん)の遊歩道(ゆうほどう)を歩(ある)いた。
I walked along the promenade by the lake.
休日(きゅうじつ)には森(もり)の中(なか)を遊歩(ゆうほ)するのが好(す)きだ。
I like strolling through the woods on my days off.

A compound of 遊(ゆう) (play, leisure) and 歩(ほ) (walk). Most commonly encountered as part of the compound 遊歩道(ゆうほどう) (promenade, walking path/trail), which refers to paved or maintained paths designed for leisurely walking in parks, along riversides, or through scenic areas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 遊歩道(ゆうほどう): promenade, walking trail (the most common compound)
  • 遊歩(ゆうほ)コース: walking course, strolling route
  • 森林(しんりん)遊歩道(ゆうほどう): forest walking trail
  • 海岸(かいがん)遊歩道(ゆうほどう): seaside promenade

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 散歩(さんぽ): walk, stroll — the everyday word for taking a walk; more common in daily conversation
  • 散策(さんさく): strolling, rambling — slightly more literary, often used for exploring an area on foot
  • 逍遥(しょうよう): leisurely walk — literary/archaic, rarely used in modern conversation
