1. to jot down; to take notes; to make a memo
To write down something briefly for future reference, typically a quick note rather than a detailed record.
電話(でんわ)番号(ばんごう)をメモした。
I jotted down the phone number.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)をメモしておいてください。
Please take notes on the contents of the meeting.
大事(だいじ)なことはすぐにメモする習慣(しゅうかん)をつけたほうがいい。
It's good to build a habit of jotting down important things right away.

From English "memo" + する. One of the most common loanword + する combinations in everyday Japanese. Used casually for quick, informal note-taking — writing on sticky notes, typing into a phone, scribbling on paper. More casual than 記録(きろく)する or 書(か)き留(と)める.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メモを取(と)る: to take notes (noun + 取(と)る form is equally common)
  • メモ帳(ちょう): memo pad; notepad
  • メモ用紙(ようし): memo paper
  • 付箋(ふせん)にメモする: to write a memo on a sticky note

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 記録(きろく)する: to record — more formal and thorough; implies an official or complete record
  • 書(か)き留(と)める: to write down; to note — slightly more formal than メモする
  • ノートを取(と)る: to take notes — specifically in a notebook, often in lectures or classes
