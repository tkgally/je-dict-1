創作的(そうさくてき)な

そうさくてきな
adjective
creative; inventive; original
1. creative; inventive; original
Having or showing the quality of creating something new and original. Used to describe people's abilities, approaches, or works that demonstrate imagination and originality.
創作的(そうさくてき)な活動(かつどう)を続(つづ)けたい。
I want to continue creative activities.
彼(かれ)のアプローチはとても創作的(そうさくてき)で新鮮(しんせん)だ。
His approach is very creative and fresh.
子(こ)どもたちの創作的(そうさくてき)な才能(さいのう)を伸(の)ばすために、自由(じゆう)に絵(え)を描(か)かせている。
We let the children draw freely in order to nurture their creative talents.

A na-adjective formed from 創作(そうさく) (creation; creative work) + 的(てき). Emphasizes the act of bringing something new into existence through imagination. While 創造的(そうぞうてき)な focuses on the broader concept of creation and innovation, 創作的(そうさくてき)な leans more toward artistic or literary creation.

Less common than 創造的(そうぞうてき)な in everyday speech, but frequently appears in arts, education, and cultural contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 創作的(そうさくてき)な活動(かつどう): creative activities
  • 創作的(そうさくてき)な才能(さいのう): creative talent
  • 創作的(そうさくてき)な表現(ひょうげん): creative expression
  • 創作的(そうさくてき)な意欲(いよく): creative drive
  • 創作的(そうさくてき)な取(と)り組(く)み: creative endeavor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 創造的(そうぞうてき)な: creative; innovative — broader scope, used in business and science as well as arts
  • 独創的(どくそうてき)な: original; unique — emphasizes being one-of-a-kind, unprecedented
  • 芸術的(げいじゅつてき)な: artistic — focuses on aesthetic quality rather than the creative process
