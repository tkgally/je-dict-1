ジェスチャー

じぇすちゃー
noun
gesture; body language
1. gesture; body language; gesticulation
A movement of the hands, arms, or body used to express meaning or communicate, either in conversation or as a substitute for words.
ジェスチャーで伝(つた)えた。
I communicated through gestures.
言葉(ことば)が通(つう)じないときはジェスチャーが役(やく)に立(た)つ。
Gestures are useful when you can't communicate with words.
彼(かれ)は話(はな)すとき大(おお)きなジェスチャーを交(まじ)えるので、とても分(わ)かりやすい。
He uses big gestures when he talks, which makes him very easy to understand.
2. gesture; token act; show of goodwill
A symbolic action done to convey an attitude or intention, not necessarily with practical effect.
それはただのジェスチャーだ。
That's just a gesture.
友好(ゆうこう)のジェスチャーとして花束(はなたば)を贈(おく)った。
I presented flowers as a gesture of friendship.
社長(しゃちょう)の謝罪(しゃざい)は誠意(せいい)のないジェスチャーにすぎないと批判(ひはん)された。
The president's apology was criticized as nothing more than an empty gesture.

From English "gesture." Used both for physical body movements and for figurative symbolic actions. The charades-style party game is also called ジェスチャーゲーム in Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ジェスチャーで伝(つた)える: to convey through gestures
  • ジェスチャーを交(まじ)える: to mix in gestures
  • ジェスチャーゲーム: charades
  • 友好(ゆうこう)のジェスチャー: gesture of friendship

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 身振(みぶ)り: gesture, body movement — native Japanese word, often paired with 手振(てぶ)り
  • 身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)り: gestures and hand movements — a set phrase for animated gesticulation
  • ボディーランゲージ: body language — loanword, broader concept including posture and facial expressions
