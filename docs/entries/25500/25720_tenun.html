天運(てんうん)

てんうん
noun
fate; destiny; heavenly fortune
1. fate; destiny; heavenly fortune
Fortune or destiny as determined by heaven or nature, beyond human control.
これも天運(てんうん)だと思(おも)うしかない。
I can only think of this as fate.
天運(てんうん)に恵(めぐ)まれた人生(じんせい)だった。
It was a life blessed by fortune.
人事(じんじ)を尽(つ)くして天運(てんうん)を待(ま)つ。
Do everything in your power and leave the rest to fate.

天(てん) (heaven) + 運(うん) (fortune, luck). A literary and somewhat formal word for fate or destiny as governed by heaven. Often used to express the idea that certain outcomes are beyond human control and must be accepted.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 天運(てんうん)に恵(めぐ)まれる — be blessed by fortune
  • 天運(てんうん)に任(まか)せる — leave to fate
  • 天運(てんうん)を待(ま)つ — wait for fate to decide
  • 天運(てんうん)が尽(つ)きる — one's fortune runs out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運命(うんめい) — fate; destiny — the most common everyday word
  • 天命(てんめい) — heavenly mandate; destiny — emphasizes a mission or duty given by heaven
  • 宿命(しゅくめい) — fate; predestination — implies something determined before birth, inescapable
