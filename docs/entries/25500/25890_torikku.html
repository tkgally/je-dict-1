トリック

とりっく
noun
trick; deception; illusion
1. trick; ruse; deception
A clever scheme or method used to deceive someone. Commonly used in mystery fiction to describe the method behind a crime or puzzle.
犯人(はんにん)のトリックを見破(みやぶ)った。
I saw through the culprit's trick.
この小説(しょうせつ)のトリックは巧妙(こうみょう)だ。
The trick in this novel is ingenious.
密室(みっしつ)トリックの謎(なぞ)が最後(さいご)に明(あ)かされる。
The mystery of the locked-room trick is revealed at the end.
2. trick; technique; feat
A skillful or impressive technique, especially in magic, skateboarding, card games, or other performance contexts.
マジシャンがすごいトリックを見(み)せた。
The magician showed an amazing trick.
スケートボードの新(あたら)しいトリックを練習(れんしゅう)する。
I practice a new skateboarding trick.
カードのトリックを覚(おぼ)えて、友達(ともだち)に披露(ひろう)した。
I learned a card trick and showed it to my friends.

From English "trick." Particularly associated with mystery fiction in Japanese — discussing the トリック of a mystery novel is a standard part of the genre's vocabulary. Japan's rich tradition of mystery fiction (ミステリー) makes this a high-frequency word in that domain.

Also widely used in magic performance, extreme sports, and card games. Unlike the English word, it does not typically carry the sense of "trick" as in playing a prank on someone (for that, いたずら is more natural).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • トリックを見破(みやぶ)る: to see through a trick
  • 密室(みっしつ)トリック: locked-room trick (mystery genre)
  • トリックアート: trick art; optical illusion art
  • マジックトリック: magic trick
  • トリックを仕掛(しか)ける: to set up a trick

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仕掛(しか)け: mechanism; device — the physical setup behind a trick
  • いたずら: prank; mischief — playful deception aimed at surprising someone
  • 手品(てじな): magic trick; sleight of hand — specifically for stage magic
