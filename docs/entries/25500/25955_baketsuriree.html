バケツリレー

ばけつりれー
noun
bucket relay; bucket brigade
1. bucket relay; bucket brigade
A method of passing buckets of water (or other items) hand to hand along a line of people, traditionally used for firefighting or flood response.
火事(かじ)の時(とき)にバケツリレーで水(みず)を運(はこ)んだ。
During the fire, we carried water by bucket relay.
防災訓練(ぼうさいくんれん)でバケツリレーの練習(れんしゅう)をした。
We practiced bucket relays during the disaster drill.
住民(じゅうみん)たちがバケツリレーで土嚢(どのう)を積(つ)み上(あ)げた。
The residents piled up sandbags using a bucket-brigade method.
2. relay-style passing; chain of handoffs (figurative)
Any process where items or tasks are passed along a chain of people or stages in sequence.
荷物(にもつ)をバケツリレー式(しき)に運(はこ)んだ。
We moved the luggage in a relay-style chain.
書類(しょるい)がバケツリレーのように部署(ぶしょ)から部署(ぶしょ)へ回(まわ)される。
Documents are passed from department to department like a bucket relay.
支援物資(しえんぶっし)をバケツリレーでトラック(とらっく)に積(つ)み込(こ)んだ。
Relief supplies were loaded onto the truck using a bucket-brigade chain.

A wasei-eigo compound of バケツ (bucket, from English) and リレー (relay, from English). In Japan, bucket relays are a familiar part of community disaster drills (防災訓練(ぼうさいくんれん)), where residents practice passing water buckets in a line. The concept is taught in schools and practiced in neighborhoods, making it a culturally significant term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • バケツリレーで消火(しょうか)する: to fight a fire with a bucket relay
  • バケツリレーの練習(れんしゅう): bucket relay practice
  • バケツリレー方式(ほうしき): bucket-relay method
  • バケツリレー式(しき)に: in bucket-relay fashion

CULTURAL NOTE:
Bucket relays remain a standard activity in Japanese disaster preparedness drills. Schools and neighborhood associations (自治会(じちかい)) regularly conduct them, reinforcing community cooperation in emergencies. The practice dates back to the Edo period when wooden buildings made fire a constant threat.
