1. to isolate; to quarantine; to segregate; to keep apart
To separate someone or something from others, typically for medical, safety, or social reasons.
感染者(かんせんしゃ)を隔離(かくり)する。
They isolate the infected person.
陽性(ようせい)と判定(はんてい)された場合(ばあい)は自宅(じたく)で隔離(かくり)してください。
If you test positive, please quarantine at home.
その危険(きけん)な物質(ぶっしつ)は他(ほか)の薬品(やくひん)から隔離(かくり)して保管(ほかん)されている。
That dangerous substance is stored in isolation from other chemicals.

隔離(かくり)する means to physically or socially separate something or someone from others. It gained widespread everyday use during pandemic contexts, but has always been used in medical, scientific, and social contexts.

USAGE:

  • ～を隔離(かくり)する: to isolate someone/something
  • ～から隔離(かくり)する: to isolate from something

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隔離(かくり)期間(きかん): quarantine period
  • 隔離(かくり)病棟(びょうとう): isolation ward
  • 自宅(じたく)隔離(かくり): home quarantine
  • 社会(しゃかい)から隔離(かくり)される: to be isolated from society
  • 隔離(かくり)措置(そち): isolation measures

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分離(ぶんり)する: to separate — technical, used for dividing things into parts rather than keeping apart for safety
  • 孤立(こりつ)する: to become isolated — describes the resulting state of being alone, not the act of separating
  • 遮断(しゃだん)する: to cut off; to block — focuses on severing connection or passage
