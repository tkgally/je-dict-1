お座敷(ざしき)

おざしき
noun
tatami room banquet; geisha entertainment
See also: 座敷(ざしき) (without お prefix)
1. tatami room; formal Japanese-style room
A polite way to refer to a Japanese-style room with tatami flooring, especially one used for entertaining guests.
お座敷(ざしき)にどうぞ。
Please come into the tatami room.
お座敷(ざしき)に通(とお)されて、お茶(ちゃ)を出(だ)された。
I was shown to the tatami room and served tea.
立派(りっぱ)なお座敷(ざしき)のある旅館(りょかん)に泊(と)まった。
I stayed at an inn with a magnificent tatami room.
2. banquet with geisha entertainment; being called to entertain
A banquet or gathering in a tatami room where geisha perform. By extension, also refers to an invitation or booking for a geisha to attend an event.
お座敷(ざしき)がかかる。
To receive a booking (for geisha entertainment).
舞妓(まいこ)さんのお座敷(ざしき)遊(あそ)びを体験(たいけん)した。
I experienced a banquet with maiko entertainment.
京都(きょうと)では観光客(かんこうきゃく)でもお座敷(ざしき)遊(あそ)びができるプランがある。
In Kyoto, there are plans that allow even tourists to enjoy a geisha banquet experience.

POLITENESS:
The お prefix makes this a polite form of 座敷(ざしき). Both forms are used, but お座敷(ざしき) is more common in hospitality contexts.

CULTURAL NOTE:
In sense 2, お座敷(ざしき) refers to the traditional world of geisha entertainment. お座敷(ざしき)遊(あそ)び includes games, conversation, dance performances, and music by geisha or maiko in a private tatami room setting. This is a key part of Kyoto's traditional culture.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お座敷(ざしき)に上(あ)がる (to enter the tatami room)
  • お座敷(ざしき)がかかる (to receive a geisha booking)
  • お座敷(ざしき)遊(あそ)び (geisha banquet entertainment)
  • お座敷(ざしき)芸(げい) (geisha performing arts)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 座敷(ざしき): tatami room — without the polite お prefix
  • 和室(わしつ): Japanese-style room — the standard modern term for a tatami room
