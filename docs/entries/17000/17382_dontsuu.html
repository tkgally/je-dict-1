鈍痛(どんつう)

どんつう
noun
dull pain; dull ache
1. dull pain; dull ache
A persistent, heavy, throbbing pain that is not sharp. Common in medical contexts when describing symptoms.
腰(こし)に鈍痛(どんつう)がある。
I have a dull pain in my lower back.
朝(あさ)から頭(あたま)に鈍痛(どんつう)が続(つづ)いている。
I've had a dull headache since this morning.
鈍痛(どんつう)が何日(なんにち)も治(おさ)まらない場合(ばあい)は、病院(びょういん)に行(い)ったほうがいい。
If the dull pain doesn't subside for several days, you should go to the hospital.

USAGE:
A compound of 鈍(どん) (dull, blunt) and 痛(つう) (pain). Used to describe a heavy, lingering ache as opposed to a sharp pain. Common when describing symptoms to a doctor.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鈍痛(どんつう)がある (to have a dull pain)
  • 鈍痛(どんつう)が続(つづ)く (dull pain continues)
  • 鈍痛(どんつう)が走(はし)る (a dull pain runs through)
  • 鈍痛(どんつう)を感(かん)じる (to feel a dull pain)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 激痛(げきつう): intense pain — sharp, severe pain, the opposite of 鈍痛(どんつう)
  • 鋭(するど)い痛(いた)み: sharp pain — a stabbing or piercing sensation

Related Words

Antonym: 激痛(げきつう) (intense pain)
