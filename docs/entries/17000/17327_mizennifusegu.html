未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ

みぜんにふせぐ
expression
to prevent beforehand; to nip in the bud
1. to prevent beforehand; to nip in the bud; to stop before it happens
To take action to prevent a problem, accident, or negative event before it occurs. Emphasizes proactive prevention rather than reactive response.
事故(じこ)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent an accident before it happens.
犯罪(はんざい)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to prevent crime before it occurs.
定期的(ていきてき)な点検(てんけん)によって、設備(せつび)の故障(こしょう)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐことができる。
Regular inspections can prevent equipment breakdowns before they happen.

USAGE:
未然(みぜん) means "before something happens" (literally "not yet so") and 防(ふせ)ぐ means "to prevent" or "to defend against." This is a common and important expression in formal contexts such as safety management, law enforcement, and risk management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事故(じこ)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent accidents)
  • 犯罪(はんざい)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent crime)
  • 災害(さいがい)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent disasters)
  • 被害(ひがい)を未然(みぜん)に防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent damage)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予防(よぼう)する: to prevent — more general term for prevention
  • 食(く)い止(と)める: to hold back, to check — implies stopping something already in progress
