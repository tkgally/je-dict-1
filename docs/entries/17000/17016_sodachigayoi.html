育(そだ)ちが良(よ)い

そだちがよい
expression
well-bred; well-raised; from a good family
1. well-bred; well-raised; from a good upbringing
Describes someone whose manners, behavior, and character reflect a good upbringing and family background. Implies refinement, politeness, and good values instilled during childhood.
彼女(かのじょ)は育(そだ)ちが良(い)い。
She is well-bred.
言葉遣(ことばづか)いが丁寧(ていねい)で、育(そだ)ちの良(よ)さがうかがえる。
Their polite way of speaking suggests they were well-raised.
育(そだ)ちが良(い)い人(ひと)は、食事(しょくじ)のマナーや人(ひと)への接(せっ)し方(かた)に自然(しぜん)と品(ひん)が出(で)るものだ。
People who are well-raised naturally show refinement in their table manners and how they treat others.

USAGE:
The opposite expression is 育(そだ)ちが悪(わる)い (poorly raised). This expression is commonly used when commenting on someone's manners or background, but should be used carefully as it can sound judgmental. The い-adjective form 良(い)い is more common in speech than 良(よ)い.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 育(そだ)ちの良(よ)さ (the quality of one's upbringing)
  • 育(そだ)ちが良(い)い人(ひと) (a well-bred person)
  • 育(そだ)ちの良(よ)い家庭(かてい) (a well-brought-up family)
