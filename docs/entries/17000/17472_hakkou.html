1. luminescence; emission of light; giving off light
The production or emission of light by a living organism, chemical reaction, or electronic device. Used in both scientific contexts and everyday descriptions of things that glow.
蛍(ほたる)が発光(はっこう)している。
The fireflies are glowing.
LEDは電気(でんき)を使(つか)って発光(はっこう)する。
LEDs emit light using electricity.
深海(しんかい)には自(みずか)ら発光(はっこう)する生(い)き物(もの)がたくさんいて、暗闇(くらやみ)の中(なか)で美(うつく)しく光(ひか)っている。
There are many organisms in the deep sea that produce their own light, glowing beautifully in the darkness.

USAGE:
Used in both technical and everyday contexts. The compound 発光(はっこう)ダイオード (LED — light-emitting diode) is one of the most common collocations.

Be careful not to confuse this word with other はっこう homophones: 発行(はっこう) (publication/issuance), 発効(はっこう) (taking effect), and 発酵(はっこう) (fermentation).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発光(はっこう)ダイオード (light-emitting diode / LED)
  • 発光(はっこう)する生物(せいぶつ) (bioluminescent organism)
  • 発光(はっこう)体(たい) (luminous body)
  • 蛍光(けいこう)発光(はっこう) (fluorescent emission)
