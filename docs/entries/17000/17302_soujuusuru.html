1. to pilot; to operate; to steer (a vehicle or machine)
To control and operate a vehicle, aircraft, ship, or machine. The standard term for piloting aircraft.
飛行機(ひこうき)を操縦(そうじゅう)する。
To pilot an airplane.
操縦(そうじゅう)免許(めんきょ)を取(と)るために訓練(くんれん)を受(う)けている。
I'm receiving training to get a pilot's license.
ドローンを操縦(そうじゅう)するには、許可(きょか)が必要(ひつよう)な場合(ばあい)がある。
A permit may be required to operate a drone.
2. to manipulate; to control (a person)
To skillfully control or manipulate a person to act according to one's wishes. Used figuratively with a somewhat negative connotation.
彼(かれ)は人(ひと)を操縦(そうじゅう)するのが上手(うま)い。
He is good at manipulating people.
会議(かいぎ)の流(なが)れを巧(たく)みに操縦(そうじゅう)した。
He skillfully steered the flow of the meeting.
裏(うら)で組織(そしき)を操縦(そうじゅう)していた人物(じんぶつ)がついに明(あき)らかになった。
The person who had been pulling the strings of the organization behind the scenes was finally revealed.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is the primary meaning. Most commonly associated with aircraft, but also used for other vehicles and machines including drones, cranes, and boats. Sense 2 is figurative and carries a negative connotation of cunning control.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飛行機(ひこうき)を操縦(そうじゅう)する (pilot an airplane)
  • ドローンを操縦(そうじゅう)する (operate a drone)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)席(せき) (cockpit, pilot's seat)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)桿(かん) (control stick, joystick)
  • 操縦(そうじゅう)免許(めんきょ) (pilot's license)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運転(うんてん)する: to drive/operate — the standard word for driving cars and operating trains
  • 操作(そうさ)する: to operate/handle — used for machines, devices, and controls; more mechanical
