街並(まちな)み

まちなみ
noun
townscape, streetscape, row of houses
1. townscape, streetscape, row of buildings along a street
The visual appearance or character of a town or street as defined by its buildings and layout.
美(うつく)しい街並(まちな)みだ。
It's a beautiful townscape.
京都(きょうと)の古(ふる)い街並(まちな)みを散歩(さんぽ)した。
I took a walk through the old streets of Kyoto.
再開発(さいかいはつ)によって、昔(むかし)の街並(まちな)みが失(うしな)われつつある。
Due to redevelopment, the old townscape is gradually disappearing.

WORD FORMATION:
街(まち) (town, street) + 並(な)み (row, line) = row of buildings along a street, townscape

KANJI NOTE:
Also written as 町並(まちな)み. 街並(まちな)み emphasizes the urban feel, while 町並(まちな)み is more neutral.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 街並(まちな)みを楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy the townscape
  • 街並(まちな)みを保存(ほぞん)する: to preserve the streetscape
  • 古(ふる)い街並(まちな)み: an old townscape
  • ヨーロッパ風(ふう)の街並(まちな)み: a European-style streetscape
