問答無用(もんどうむよう)

もんどうむよう
expression
no argument; no questions asked
1. no argument; no questions asked; beyond discussion
Used to express that something is non-negotiable, or to describe a forceful action taken without discussion or warning. Literally means 'questions and answers are useless.'
問答無用(もんどうむよう)で追(お)い出(だ)された。
I was thrown out without any discussion.
遅刻(ちこく)したら問答無用(もんどうむよう)で減点(げんてん)される。
If you're late, you lose points — no questions asked.
規則(きそく)違反(いはん)は問答無用(もんどうむよう)で処分(しょぶん)の対象(たいしょう)となる。
Rule violations are subject to disciplinary action without discussion.

USAGE:
A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) meaning that no discussion or negotiation is possible. Often used adverbially with で: 問答無用(もんどうむよう)で (without argument). Conveys a forceful, decisive tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問答無用(もんどうむよう)で (without discussion, no questions asked)
  • 問答無用(もんどうむよう)に (without allowing argument)
