問答無用(もんどうむよう)

もんどうむよう
expression
no argument; no questions asked
1. no argument; no questions asked; beyond discussion
Used to express that something is non-negotiable, or to describe a forceful action taken without discussion or warning. Literally means 'questions and answers are useless.'
問答無用(もんどうむよう)で追(お)い出(だ)された。
I was thrown out without any discussion.
遅刻(ちこく)したら問答無用(もんどうむよう)で減点(げんてん)される。
If you're late, you lose points — no questions asked.
規則(きそく)違反(いはん)は問答無用(もんどうむよう)で処分(しょぶん)の対象(たいしょう)となる。
Rule violations are subject to disciplinary action without discussion.

問答無用(もんどうむよう) is a 四字熟語(よじじゅくご) (four-character compound) meaning that no discussion or argument is allowed. Literally: "questions and answers are useless."

WORD FORMATION:
問答(もんどう) (questions and answers, dialogue) + 無用(むよう) (useless, unnecessary).

USAGE:
Conveys a forceful, non-negotiable tone. Often used to describe situations where someone acts decisively without giving the other party a chance to object.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 問答無用(もんどうむよう)で追(お)い出(だ)す: to throw out without discussion
  • 問答無用(もんどうむよう)で処分(しょぶん)する: to discipline without argument
  • 問答無用(もんどうむよう)に切(き)り捨(す)てる: to cut down without a word

REGISTER:
Somewhat literary or dramatic. Common in historical dramas (時代劇(じだいげき)) and formal written contexts. In everyday speech, expressions like 有無(うむ)を言(い)わさず are more natural.
