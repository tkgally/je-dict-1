対岸(たいがん)

たいがん
noun
opposite shore; far bank; other side
1. opposite shore; far bank; other side (of a river, lake, etc.)
The shore or bank on the opposite side of a body of water such as a river, lake, or strait.
対岸(たいがん)が見(み)える。
I can see the opposite shore.
対岸(たいがん)に渡(わた)るには船(ふね)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need a boat to cross to the other side.
対岸(たいがん)の火事(かじ)だと思(おも)っていたが、自分(じぶん)にも影響(えいきょう)が出(で)た。
I thought it was someone else's problem, but it ended up affecting me too.
The idiom 対岸(たいがん)の火事(かじ) means treating something as irrelevant because it doesn't directly affect you.

USAGE:
Often used literally for geography and figuratively in the idiom 対岸(たいがん)の火事(かじ) (a fire on the far shore), meaning someone else's problem that doesn't concern you.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 対岸(たいがん)の火事(かじ) (someone else's problem — lit. fire on the far shore)
  • 対岸(たいがん)に渡(わた)る (to cross to the opposite shore)
  • 川(かわ)の対岸(たいがん) (the opposite bank of a river)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 向(む)こう岸(ぎし): the other shore — more colloquial equivalent
  • 此岸(しがん): this shore — literary opposite
