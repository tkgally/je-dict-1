過熱(かねつ)

かねつ
noun, suru verb
overheating; excessive heating; escalation
1. overheating; excessive heating
A state where something becomes too hot, beyond the normal or safe temperature range.
エンジンが過熱(かねつ)した。
The engine overheated.
バッテリーの過熱(かねつ)に注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful of battery overheating.
機械(きかい)が過熱(かねつ)すると自動的(じどうてき)に停止(ていし)する仕組(しく)みになっている。
The machine is designed to stop automatically when it overheats.
2. escalation; becoming excessive; overheating (figurative)
A situation, competition, or trend that has intensified beyond reasonable levels. Commonly used for economic bubbles, media frenzies, or competitive escalation.
受験(じゅけん)競争(きょうそう)が過熱(かねつ)している。
The entrance exam competition is overheating.
報道(ほうどう)が過熱(かねつ)して、本人(ほんにん)のプライバシーが侵害(しんがい)された。
Media coverage escalated to the point where the person's privacy was violated.
不動産(ふどうさん)市場(しじょう)の過熱(かねつ)を抑(おさ)えるために新(あたら)しい規制(きせい)が導入(どうにゅう)された。
New regulations were introduced to curb the overheating of the real estate market.

USAGE:
A compound of 過(か) (excessive) + 熱(ねつ) (heat). Used as a suru verb (過熱(かねつ)する). Both literal and figurative uses are common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 過熱(かねつ)気味(ぎみ) (somewhat overheated)
  • 過熱(かねつ)防止(ぼうし) (overheating prevention)
  • 景気(けいき)の過熱(かねつ) (economic overheating)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加熱(かねつ): heating — the deliberate act of applying heat (same reading, different kanji)
  • 白熱(はくねつ): white-hot — an intense, heated state (often of debate or competition)
