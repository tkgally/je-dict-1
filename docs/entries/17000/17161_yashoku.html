夜食(やしょく)

やしょく
noun
late-night snack; midnight meal
1. late-night snack; midnight meal
A meal or snack eaten late at night, typically after dinner. Often refers to food eaten while studying or working late.
夜食(やしょく)にラーメンを食(た)べた。
I ate ramen as a late-night snack.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)は毎晩(まいばん)夜食(やしょく)を食(た)べていた。
I ate a midnight snack every night before exams.
夜食(やしょく)ばかり食(た)べていると太(ふと)るので気(き)をつけている。
I'm being careful because eating late-night snacks all the time makes you gain weight.

USAGE:
Refers specifically to food eaten late at night, not simply a late dinner. Common among students studying for exams and people working late. Often associated with simple, quick foods like instant ramen or rice balls.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夜食(やしょく)を食(た)べる (to eat a late-night snack)
  • 夜食(やしょく)に〜を作(つく)る (to make ~ as a midnight snack)
  • 夜食(やしょく)代(だい)わり (as a substitute for a midnight snack)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夜(よ)ごはん: dinner, evening meal — the main evening meal, not a late-night snack
  • おやつ: snack — a daytime snack, not specifically late at night
