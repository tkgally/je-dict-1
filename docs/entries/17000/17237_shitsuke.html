しつけ

しつけ
noun
discipline, upbringing, training
1. discipline, upbringing, training in manners
The process of teaching children proper behavior, manners, and social norms. Also used for training pets.
犬(いぬ)のしつけは大変(たいへん)だ。
Training a dog is hard work.
親(おや)のしつけが厳(きび)しかった。
My parents' discipline was strict.
子(こ)どものしつけについて夫婦(ふうふ)で話(はな)し合(あ)うことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important for couples to discuss how to discipline their children.

KANJI NOTE:
Can be written as 躾(しつけ) in kanji. This is a 国字(こくじ) (character invented in Japan): 身(み) (body) + 美(び) (beautiful) = beautiful conduct. The hiragana form is more common in everyday use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しつけが厳(きび)しい: strict discipline
  • しつけが行(い)き届(とど)いている: well-disciplined
  • しつけがなっていない: poorly disciplined
  • 犬(いぬ)のしつけ: dog training
  • 子(こ)どものしつけ: child-rearing, disciplining children

NOTE:
しつけ focuses specifically on teaching proper behavior and manners, whereas 教育(きょういく) (education) is broader and includes academic learning.
