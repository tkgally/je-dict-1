1. to describe; to write down; to state in writing
To express or record something in written form. Used for academic writing, official documents, and technical descriptions.
事実(じじつ)を記述(きじゅつ)する。
To describe the facts.
論文(ろんぶん)に実験(じっけん)結果(けっか)を記述(きじゅつ)した。
I described the experimental results in the paper.
この文書(ぶんしょ)には手続(てつづ)きの方法(ほうほう)が詳(くわ)しく記述(きじゅつ)されている。
This document describes the procedures in detail.

USAGE:
A formal, written-style verb. Common in academic, technical, and official contexts. The noun form 記述(きじゅつ) means "description" or "statement" and is used in compound terms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 記述(きじゅつ)式(しき) (descriptive-style, essay-type — as in exam questions)
  • 客観的(きゃっかんてき)に記述(きじゅつ)する (to describe objectively)
  • 詳細(しょうさい)に記述(きじゅつ)する (to describe in detail)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)く: to write — everyday verb
  • 記載(きさい)する: to state, to record — more about listing or registering information
  • 描写(びょうしゃ)する: to depict, to describe — focuses on vivid or artistic description
