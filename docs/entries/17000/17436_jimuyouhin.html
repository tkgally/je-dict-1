事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)

じむようひん
noun
office supplies
1. office supplies, stationery
Items used in an office setting, such as pens, paper, folders, staplers, and other stationery and desk supplies.
事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)を注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered office supplies.
事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)はロッカーにまとめて保管(ほかん)している。
Office supplies are kept together in a locker.
新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん)のために事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)を一式(いっしき)揃(そろ)えた。
I prepared a full set of office supplies for the new employee.

USAGE:
A practical compound word used in workplace settings. Often seen in office supply stores, procurement lists, and workplace management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)店(てん) (office supply store)
  • 事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)を購入(こうにゅう)する (to purchase office supplies)
  • 事務(じむ)用品(ようひん)一式(いっしき) (a set of office supplies)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 文房具(ぶんぼうぐ): stationery — pens, notebooks, etc. (for personal/school use too)
  • 事務(じむ)用品(ようひん): office supplies — specifically for office/workplace use
