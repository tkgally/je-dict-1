備考欄(びこうらん)

びこうらん
noun
remarks column; notes section
1. remarks column; notes section; comments field
A designated space on a form, document, or application for writing additional notes, comments, or supplementary information.
備考欄(びこうらん)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the remarks column.
アレルギーがある方(かた)は備考欄(びこうらん)にお書(か)きください。
If you have any allergies, please write them in the notes section.
備考欄(びこうらん)に特記(とっき)すべきことがなければ、空欄(くうらん)のままで結構(けっこう)です。
If there is nothing special to note in the remarks column, you may leave it blank.

WORD FORMATION:
備考(びこう) (remarks, notes) + 欄(らん) (column, field). The 欄(らん) suffix is used for various sections of forms and documents.

USAGE:
Very common on Japanese forms of all kinds — application forms, medical forms, order forms, surveys, and official documents. Knowing this word is essential for navigating Japanese bureaucracy and paperwork.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 備考欄(びこうらん)に記入(きにゅう)する (to fill in the remarks section)
  • 備考欄(びこうらん)に書(か)く (to write in the notes column)
  • 備考欄(びこうらん)は空欄(くうらん)でよい (the remarks column can be left blank)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 備考(びこう): remarks, notes — the content itself, without the 'column' part
  • 欄(らん): column, field — used in many compounds like 解答欄(かいとうらん) (answer section) and 記入欄(きにゅうらん) (entry field)
