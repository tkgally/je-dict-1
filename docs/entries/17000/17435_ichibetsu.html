1. a glance, a glimpse, a brief look
A quick, brief look at something or someone. Often implies a dismissive or casual glance, or a swift survey of a situation.
彼(かれ)は書類(しょるい)に一瞥(いちべつ)をくれた。
He gave the documents a quick glance.
一瞥(いちべつ)しただけで、興味(きょうみ)なさそうに立(た)ち去(さ)った。
After just a glance, she walked away looking uninterested.
部屋(へや)を一瞥(いちべつ)すると、何(なに)かが変(か)わっていることに気(き)づいた。
When I glanced around the room, I noticed something had changed.

USAGE:
A literary or formal word for a brief look. Often implies that the glance is quick and possibly dismissive. Common in written narrative and formal speech. Can be used with する (to glance) or くれる/与(あた)える (to give a glance).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一瞥(いちべつ)する (to glance at)
  • 一瞥(いちべつ)をくれる (to give a glance)
  • 一瞥(いちべつ)もしない (to not even glance at)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ちらっと見(み)る: to glance — casual, everyday expression
  • 一瞥(いちべつ): a glance — more literary and formal
