所有権(しょゆうけん)

しょゆうけん
noun
ownership; property rights
1. ownership; ownership rights; property rights
The legal right to possess, use, and dispose of property. A fundamental concept in law covering both physical property and intellectual property.
この土地(とち)の所有権(しょゆうけん)は誰(だれ)にあるのか。
Who owns this land?
所有権(しょゆうけん)を移転(いてん)する手続(てつづ)きが必要(ひつよう)だ。
A procedure to transfer ownership is necessary.
不動産(ふどうさん)の所有権(しょゆうけん)は登記(とうき)によって公示(こうじ)される。
Real estate ownership is made public through registration.

USAGE:
A legal term, but also used in everyday contexts when discussing property, cars, and belongings. In Japanese law, 所有権(しょゆうけん) is one of the most fundamental property rights.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)を持(も)つ (to have ownership)
  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)を移転(いてん)する (to transfer ownership)
  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)を主張(しゅちょう)する (to claim ownership)
  • 所有権(しょゆうけん)放棄(ほうき) (abandonment of ownership)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 所有(しょゆう): ownership, possession — the act or state of owning, without the legal-rights nuance
  • 著作権(ちょさくけん): copyright — specifically for creative works
