1. to argue back; to refute; to counter
To present an opposing argument or objection in response to someone else's statement or claim. Implies a structured or reasoned response, not merely emotional disagreement.
彼(かれ)はすぐに反論(はんろん)した。
He immediately argued back.
その意見(いけん)に対(たい)して反論(はんろん)する人(ひと)は少(すく)なかった。
Few people countered that opinion.
記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で、社長(しゃちょう)は報道(ほうどう)の内容(ないよう)に対(たい)してデータを示(しめ)しながら反論(はんろん)した。
At the press conference, the president refuted the report's content while presenting data.

USAGE:
Often used with に対(たい)して to mark what is being argued against. The noun form 反論(はんろん) is also very common (e.g., 反論(はんろん)がある 'there is an objection').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜に対(たい)して反論(はんろん)する (argue back against ~)
  • 反論(はんろん)の余地(よち)がない (no room for rebuttal)
  • 反論(はんろん)を述(の)べる (state a counterargument)
  • 激(はげ)しく反論(はんろん)する (argue back fiercely)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反対(はんたい)する: to oppose — broader; can mean opposing a plan or idea without necessarily presenting a reasoned argument
  • 否定(ひてい)する: to deny — focuses on rejecting a claim as false
  • 言(い)い返(かえ)す: to talk back — more colloquial and can imply rudeness
