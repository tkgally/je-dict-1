道(みち)しるべ

みちしるべ
noun
guidepost, signpost, guide
See also: 道標(みちしるべ) (signpost (kanji))
1. guidepost, signpost
A sign or marker that shows the way, either literally on a road or figuratively as guidance in life.
山道(やまみち)に道(みち)しるべがあった。
There was a signpost on the mountain path.
先生(せんせい)の言葉(ことば)が人生(じんせい)の道(みち)しるべになった。
My teacher's words became a guidepost for my life.
古(ふる)い石(いし)の道(みち)しるべが旅人(たびびと)に方向(ほうこう)を示(しめ)していた。
An old stone signpost was showing travelers the direction.

WORD FORMATION:
道(みち) (road, path) + しるべ (guide, marker) = road marker, guidepost

KANJI NOTE:
Can be written as 道標(みちしるべ). The mixed writing 道(みち)しるべ is also common.

USAGE:
Often used figuratively to mean a guide or guiding principle in life, career, etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人生(じんせい)の道(みち)しるべ: a guidepost for life
  • 道(みち)しるべになる: to serve as a guide
  • 道(みち)しるべに従(したが)う: to follow the signpost
