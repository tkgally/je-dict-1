1. to be cornered; to be driven into a corner; to be backed against the wall
To be put in a situation where there is no way out or no options left. Can refer to physical pursuit, psychological pressure, or being trapped by circumstances.
犯人(はんにん)は追(お)い詰(つ)められた。
The criminal was cornered.
借金(しゃっきん)で精神的(せいしんてき)に追(お)い詰(つ)められている。
He is being mentally driven into a corner by debt.
試合(しあい)終盤(しゅうばん)、相手(あいて)チームに追(お)い詰(つ)められたが、なんとか逆転(ぎゃくてん)した。
We were cornered by the opposing team late in the game, but somehow managed to turn it around.

USAGE:
The passive form of 追(お)い詰(つ)める (to corner, to drive into a corner). Often used to describe situations where someone has run out of options due to external pressure — financial problems, workplace stress, competitive situations, or being chased. Can be used both literally (physical pursuit) and figuratively (psychological/situational pressure).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 精神的(せいしんてき)に追(お)い詰(つ)められる (to be mentally cornered)
  • 窮地(きゅうち)に追(お)い詰(つ)められる (to be driven into a tight spot)
  • 崖(がけ)っぷちに追(お)い詰(つ)められる (to be pushed to the edge of a cliff)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 窮地(きゅうち)に立(た)たされる: to be placed in a predicament — similar but more formal
  • 行(い)き詰(づ)まる: to reach a dead end — implies being stuck rather than pressured by others
