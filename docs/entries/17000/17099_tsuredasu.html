連(つ)れ出(だ)す

つれだす
verb (godan)
to take someone out; to bring someone along
1. to take someone out; to lead someone outside; to bring someone along
To take a person (or sometimes a pet) from one place to another, typically from indoors to outdoors or from home to somewhere else.
子供(こども)を公園(こうえん)に連(つ)れ出(だ)す。
To take the kids to the park.
犬(いぬ)を散歩(さんぽ)に連(つ)れ出(だ)した。
I took the dog out for a walk.
天気(てんき)がいいから、家(いえ)にこもっている弟(おとうと)を無理(むり)やり外(そと)に連(つ)れ出(だ)した。
Since the weather was nice, I dragged my brother, who was cooped up at home, outside.

USAGE:
A compound verb formed from 連(つ)れる (to take along) and 出(だ)す (to take out). Emphasizes the action of bringing someone from one location (typically indoors) to another (typically outdoors).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外(そと)に連(つ)れ出(だ)す (to take outside)
  • 散歩(さんぽ)に連(つ)れ出(だ)す (to take out for a walk)
  • 子供(こども)を連(つ)れ出(だ)す (to take the children out)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連(つ)れて行(い)く: to take along — more general, no emphasis on going outside
  • 引(ひ)っ張(ぱ)り出(だ)す: to drag out — stronger nuance of forcing someone out
