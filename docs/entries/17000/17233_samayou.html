彷徨(さまよ)う

さまよう
verb (godan)
to wander, to roam, to drift
1. to wander, to roam
To walk around without a fixed destination or purpose. Often implies being lost or aimless.
街(まち)を彷徨(さまよ)った。
I wandered around the city.
森(もり)の中(なか)を彷徨(さまよ)い続(つづ)けた。
I kept wandering through the forest.
行(い)く当(あ)てもなく夜(よる)の道(みち)を彷徨(さまよ)った。
I wandered the streets at night with nowhere to go.
2. to hover between, to waver
To be in an uncertain state, especially between life and death or between two options.
生死(せいし)の境(さかい)を彷徨(さまよ)った。
He hovered between life and death.
意識(いしき)が彷徨(さまよ)っている。
His consciousness is drifting.
患者(かんじゃ)は三日間(みっかかん)、生死(せいし)の間(あいだ)を彷徨(さまよ)った。
The patient hovered between life and death for three days.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xを彷徨(さまよ)う (place), Xが彷徨(さまよ)う (person).

ASPECT (ている):
彷徨(さまよ)っている indicates an ongoing state of wandering.

KANJI NOTE:
Often written in hiragana as さまよう. The kanji 彷徨(さまよ)う gives a more literary feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 街(まち)を彷徨(さまよ)う: to wander the streets
  • 生死(せいし)の境(さかい)を彷徨(さまよ)う: to hover between life and death
  • 当(あ)てもなく彷徨(さまよ)う: to wander aimlessly
