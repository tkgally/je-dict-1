名曲(めいきょく)

めいきょく
noun
famous song; masterpiece (music)
1. famous song; musical masterpiece; classic piece of music
A well-known and highly regarded piece of music, whether classical, pop, folk, or any other genre. Implies widespread recognition and enduring popularity.
ベートーベンの名曲(めいきょく)を聴(き)いた。
I listened to a Beethoven masterpiece.
この曲(きょく)は昭和(しょうわ)の名曲(めいきょく)として今(いま)も愛(あい)されている。
This song is still loved as a classic from the Showa era.
名曲(めいきょく)ぞろいのアルバムで、一曲目(いっきょくめ)から最後(さいご)まで飽(あ)きずに聴(き)ける。
The album is full of great songs — you can listen from the first track to the last without getting bored.

USAGE:
Applies to all genres of music. Classical music fans, J-pop listeners, and karaoke enthusiasts all use this word. Part of the 名(めい)~ family of words that indicate excellence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名曲(めいきょく)ぞろい (full of great songs)
  • 不朽(ふきゅう)の名曲(めいきょく) (an immortal masterpiece)
  • 隠(かく)れた名曲(めいきょく) (a hidden gem)
  • 名曲(めいきょく)喫茶(きっさ) (a cafe that plays classical music)

RELATED 名(めい)~ WORDS:

  • 名作(めいさく): masterpiece (literary, film, art)
  • 名画(めいが): famous painting or classic film
  • 名言(めいげん): famous quote
