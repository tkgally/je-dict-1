変化(へんげ)

へんげ
noun
transformation; shape-shifting; apparition
1. transformation; shape-shifting; metamorphosis
A supernatural transformation, especially in folklore where animals or spirits change into human form. Distinct from the common reading へんか (change), this reading specifically refers to magical or supernatural transformation.
狐(きつね)の変化(へんげ)を見(み)た。
I saw a fox's transformation.
昔話(むかしばなし)では、狸(たぬき)が人間(にんげん)に変化(へんげ)する。
In folktales, raccoon dogs transform into humans.
古(ふる)い寺(てら)の近(ちか)くで変化(へんげ)を見(み)たという噂(うわさ)が村中(むらじゅう)に広(ひろ)まった。
A rumor spread throughout the village that someone had seen a shape-shifting apparition near the old temple.
能(のう)の演目(えんもく)には、変化(へんげ)をテーマにしたものが数多(かずおお)くある。
There are many Noh plays with the theme of supernatural transformation.
2. apparition; phantom; supernatural being in disguise
A being that has transformed itself, such as a fox or raccoon dog in Japanese folklore that has taken human form.
古(ふる)い寺(てら)の近(ちか)くで変化(へんげ)を見(み)たという噂(うわさ)が村中(むらじゅう)に広(ひろ)まった。
A rumor spread throughout the village that someone had seen a shape-shifting apparition near the old temple.
あれは変化(へんげ)の仕業(しわざ)だ。
That is the doing of a shape-shifting spirit.
この森(もり)には変化(へんげ)が住(す)んでいると言(い)われている。
It is said that shape-shifting spirits live in this forest.
能(のう)の演目(えんもく)には、変化(へんげ)をテーマにしたものが数多(かずおお)くある。
There are many Noh plays with the theme of supernatural transformation.

READING NOTE:
The same kanji 変化(へんか) read as へんか means ordinary change or alteration. The reading へんげ specifically refers to supernatural transformation in folklore and religion.

CULTURE:
In Japanese folklore, foxes (狐(きつね)) and raccoon dogs (狸(たぬき)) are the most famous shape-shifting animals. Buddhist texts also use this term for manifestations of deities (権化(ごんげ)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変化(へんげ)の術(じゅつ) (the art of transformation)
  • 変化(へんげ)する (to transform supernaturally)
  • 変化(へんげ)の仕業(しわざ) (the doing of a shape-shifter)
