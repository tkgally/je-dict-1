売(う)り出(だ)し

うりだし
noun
bargain sale; debut; launch
1. bargain sale; special sale
A sale event where goods are offered at reduced prices, typically at a store or market.
売(う)り出(だ)しセールが始(はじ)まった。
The bargain sale has started.
年末(ねんまつ)の大(おお)売(う)り出(だ)しで客(きゃく)が殺到(さっとう)した。
Customers flooded in for the year-end big sale.
スーパーの売(う)り出(だ)し広告(こうこく)を見(み)て、朝(あさ)から並(なら)んでいる人(ひと)がいた。
There were people lining up from morning after seeing the supermarket's sale advertisement.
2. debut; launch; rise to prominence
The beginning of someone's career or public recognition, or the initial launch of a product onto the market.
売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう)の若手(わかて)俳優(はいゆう)だ。
He's an up-and-coming young actor.
新商品(しんしょうひん)の売(う)り出(だ)しは来月(らいげつ)の予定(よてい)だ。
The launch of the new product is scheduled for next month.
あの歌手(かしゅ)は去年(きょねん)デビューしたばかりの売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう)で、すでにファンが多(おお)い。
That singer just debuted last year and is on the rise, already having many fans.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is commonly seen on store signs and advertisements. Sense 2 is often used with 中(ちゅう): 売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう) (up-and-coming, currently gaining popularity).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)売(う)り出(だ)し (big sale)
  • 売(う)り出(だ)し中(ちゅう) (up-and-coming; on sale now)
  • 売(う)り出(だ)しセール (bargain sale)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • セール: sale — loanword equivalent for sense 1
  • バーゲン: bargain sale — loanword for clearance sales
  • デビュー: debut — loanword equivalent for sense 2
