リボ払(ばら)い

りぼばらい
noun
revolving payment; revolving credit
1. revolving payment; revolving credit
A credit card payment method where the cardholder pays a fixed monthly amount regardless of total purchases. Short for リボルビング払(ばら)い. Known for accumulating high interest charges.
リボ払(ばら)いは利息(りそく)が高(たか)い。
Revolving payments have high interest.
カードの支払(しはら)いをリボ払(ばら)いに変更(へんこう)した。
I changed my card payment to revolving credit.
リボ払(ばら)いを使(つか)い続(つづ)けると、気(き)づかないうちに借金(しゃっきん)が膨(ふく)らむことがある。
If you keep using revolving payments, your debt can grow without you noticing.

USAGE:
A widely discussed financial topic in Japan. Consumer protection groups and financial advisors often warn against using revolving payments due to the high interest rates (typically 15% annually). Credit card companies sometimes encourage switching to revolving payments through promotions or points campaigns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リボ払(ばら)いに変更(へんこう)する (to switch to revolving payment)
  • リボ払(ばら)いの残高(ざんだか) (revolving payment balance)
  • リボ払(ばら)いの手数料(てすうりょう) (revolving payment fees)
  • リボ払(ばら)い地獄(じごく) (revolving payment hell — debt trap)
