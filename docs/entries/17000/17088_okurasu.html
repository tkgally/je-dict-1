遅(おく)らす

おくらす
verb
to delay; to postpone
1. to delay; to postpone; to put off; to set back
To make something happen later than originally planned or scheduled. Can refer to deliberately pushing back a schedule or causing something to be late.
出発(しゅっぱつ)を一時間(いちじかん)遅(おく)らせた。
We delayed the departure by one hour.
会議(かいぎ)の開始(かいし)時間(じかん)を30分(ぷん)遅(おく)らしてもいいですか。
Can we push the meeting start time back by 30 minutes?
悪天候(あくてんこう)のため、試合(しあい)の開始(かいし)を遅(おく)らせざるを得(え)なかった。
Due to bad weather, we had no choice but to delay the start of the match.

USAGE:
The transitive counterpart of 遅(おく)れる (to be late, intransitive). Used when someone deliberately or actively causes a delay.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出発(しゅっぱつ)を遅(おく)らす (to delay departure)
  • 時間(じかん)を遅(おく)らす (to push back the time)
  • 予定(よてい)を遅(おく)らす (to postpone a plan)

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (遅(おく)らす → 遅(おく)らさない → 遅(おく)らして). Transitive. The intransitive form is 遅(おく)れる. Also written as 遅(おく)らせる (ichidan, causative form of 遅(おく)れる), which is more common in modern usage.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 延期(えんき)する: to postpone — more formal, used for events and official dates
  • 先延(さきの)ばしにする: to put off — more casual, often implies procrastination
