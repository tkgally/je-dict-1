左記(さき)

さき
noun
the following, mentioned below
1. the following, what is mentioned below
Refers to content that follows in a document. In traditional Japanese vertical writing, 'left' corresponds to 'below' or 'following' since text flows from right to left.
左記(さき)の通(とお)りです。
It is as stated below.
左記(さき)の住所(じゅうしょ)にお送(おく)りください。
Please send it to the address below.
詳(くわ)しくは左記(さき)の連絡先(れんらくさき)までお問(と)い合(あ)わせください。
For details, please contact us at the address listed below.

WORD FORMATION:
左(さ) (left) + 記(き) (written) = what is written to the left

In traditional vertical writing (縦書(たてが)き), text flows from right to left, so "to the left" means "what follows." In modern horizontal writing, 下記(かき) (below) is often used instead.

CONTRAST:

  • 左記(さき): the following (in vertical writing)
  • 右記(うき): the preceding (in vertical writing)
  • 下記(かき): the following (in horizontal writing)
  • 上記(じょうき): the preceding (in horizontal writing)

NOTE:
左記(さき) is a formal, written expression. It appears in official documents, letters, and forms. In modern horizontal documents, 下記(かき) is more standard.
