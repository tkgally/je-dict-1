身籠(みごも)る

みごもる
verb-godan
to become pregnant; to conceive
1. to become pregnant; to conceive
A somewhat literary or formal word meaning to become pregnant. Literally 'to harbor in one's body.' More refined than the common 妊娠(にんしん)する.
彼女(かのじょ)は子(こ)を身籠(みごも)った。
She became pregnant.
身籠(みごも)っていることがわかったのは秋(あき)だった。
It was autumn when she found out she was pregnant.
姫(ひめ)は王子(おうじ)の子(こ)を身籠(みごも)り、国中(くにじゅう)が喜(よろこ)びに包(つつ)まれた。
The princess conceived the prince's child, and the entire kingdom was filled with joy.

USAGE:
More literary and refined than 妊娠(にんしん)する. Often encountered in novels, historical texts, and formal announcements. In everyday conversation, 妊娠(にんしん)する or the colloquial できる are more common.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子(こ)を身籠(みごも)る (to conceive a child)
  • 身籠(みごも)っている (to be pregnant)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 妊娠(にんしん)する: to become pregnant — the standard, neutral medical/everyday term
  • 懐妊(かいにん)する: to become pregnant — very formal, used for royalty or public figures
