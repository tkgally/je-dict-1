1. to get used to a situation; to feel at ease in a setting
To become accustomed to a particular environment or situation through experience, so that one can act calmly and naturally without nervousness.
あの人(ひと)は場馴(ばな)れしている。
That person is used to these kinds of situations.
何度(なんど)も発表(はっぴょう)をして、すっかり場馴(ばな)れした。
After giving presentations many times, I've gotten completely used to it.
まだ新人(しんじん)なのに、場馴(ばな)れした態度(たいど)で堂々(どうどう)とスピーチをした。
Despite being a newcomer, she gave the speech confidently with a relaxed manner.

USAGE:
Often used in the form 場馴(ばな)れした (adjective form) or 場馴(ばな)れしている (stative form) to describe someone who appears calm and experienced. Can also be written as 場慣(ばな)れ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 場馴(ばな)れした態度(たいど) (an experienced, composed manner)
  • 場馴(ばな)れした様子(ようす) (an at-ease appearance)
  • すっかり場馴(ばな)れする (to become completely at ease)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 慣(な)れる: to get used to — general term for becoming accustomed to anything
  • 堂々(どうどう)とする: to be composed — describes confident behavior without implying experience
