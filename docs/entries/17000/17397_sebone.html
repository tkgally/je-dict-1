背骨(せぼね)

せぼね
noun
backbone; spine; spinal column
1. backbone; spine; spinal column
The column of vertebrae running down the center of the back, supporting the body's structure.
背骨(せぼね)が曲(ま)がっている。
My spine is curved.
転(ころ)んで背骨(せぼね)を痛(いた)めた。
I fell and hurt my spine.
姿勢(しせい)が悪(わる)いと背骨(せぼね)に負担(ふたん)がかかる。
Poor posture puts strain on the spine.
2. backbone; core; mainstay
Figuratively, the central support or foundation of something — an organization, system, or argument.
製造業(せいぞうぎょう)はこの国(くに)の経済(けいざい)の背骨(せぼね)だ。
Manufacturing is the backbone of this country's economy.
彼(かれ)は組織(そしき)の背骨(せぼね)となる存在(そんざい)だ。
He is the backbone of the organization.
背骨(せぼね)のない計画(けいかく)は失敗(しっぱい)する。
A plan without a backbone will fail.

USAGE:
A compound of 背(せ) (back) and 骨(ほね) (bone). In its literal sense, it is the everyday word for spine. The figurative sense of 'core support' mirrors English 'backbone.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 背骨(せぼね)が曲(ま)がる (the spine curves)
  • 背骨(せぼね)を伸(の)ばす (to straighten the spine)
  • 背骨(せぼね)を矯正(きょうせい)する (to correct the spine)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 脊椎(せきつい): vertebral column — the medical/anatomical term for the spine
