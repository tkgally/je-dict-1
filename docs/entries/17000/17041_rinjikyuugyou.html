臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)

りんじきゅうぎょう
noun
temporary closure; unscheduled closing
1. temporary closure; unscheduled closing
A business, shop, or facility closing temporarily outside of its regular closing days. May be due to owner illness, maintenance, weather, or special circumstances.
本日(ほんじつ)は臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)です。
We are temporarily closed today.
台風(たいふう)のため臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)いたします。
We will be temporarily closed due to the typhoon.
行(い)きつけのカフェが臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)だったので、仕方(しかた)なく別(べつ)の店(みせ)に入(はい)った。
My usual café was temporarily closed, so I reluctantly went to a different shop.

USAGE:
Often seen on signs posted on shop doors or announced on social media and websites. A common sight in Japan, especially during typhoons, public health emergencies, or when an owner-operated shop needs a day off.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)のお知(し)らせ (notice of temporary closure)
  • 臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)いたします (we will be temporarily closed — polite)
  • 臨時休業(りんじきゅうぎょう)日(び) (day of temporary closure)
