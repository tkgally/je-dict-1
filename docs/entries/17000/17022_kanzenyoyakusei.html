完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)

かんぜんよやくせい
noun
by appointment only; reservation-only system
1. by appointment only; reservation-only system
A system where customers or visitors must make a reservation in advance and walk-ins are not accepted. Common for restaurants, clinics, salons, and specialty shops in Japan.
この店(みせ)は完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)です。
This restaurant is by appointment only.
完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)のレストランは待(ま)ち時間(じかん)がない。
At reservation-only restaurants, there is no waiting time.
あの人気(にんき)の寿司屋(すしや)は完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)で、一(いっ)か月(げつ)前(まえ)から予約(よやく)しないと入(はい)れない。
That popular sushi restaurant is reservation-only, and you need to book a month in advance to get in.

USAGE:
Very common in modern Japan, especially for high-end restaurants, medical clinics, beauty salons, and specialty services. Often seen on signs, websites, and social media posts. Can be written as a sign or label.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)の店(みせ) (a reservation-only establishment)
  • 完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)で営業(えいぎょう)する (to operate on a reservation-only basis)
  • 完全予約制(かんぜんよやくせい)に変更(へんこう)する (to switch to a reservation-only system)
