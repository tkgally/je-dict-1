新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)

しんきさんにゅう
noun
new market entry; new entrant
1. new market entry; entering a new market or industry
When a company or individual enters an industry, market, or field for the first time. A key concept in business strategy and economics.
大手(おおて)IT企業(きぎょう)が金融(きんゆう)業界(ぎょうかい)に新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)した。
A major IT company entered the financial industry.
新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)を阻(はば)む規制(きせい)が多(おお)い。
There are many regulations that prevent new market entry.
新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)が相次(あいつ)いだことで競争(きょうそう)が激(はげ)しくなり、消費者(しょうひしゃ)にとっては価格(かかく)が下(さ)がるメリットがある。
As new entrants continued to appear, competition intensified, which has the benefit of lower prices for consumers.

USAGE:
A standard business and economics term frequently used in news, business reports, and strategy discussions. Deregulation (規制緩和(きせいかんわ)) often leads to increased new market entry.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)する (to enter a new market)
  • 新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)企業(きぎょう) (new entrant company)
  • 新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)を促(うなが)す (to encourage new market entry)
  • 新規参入(しんきさんにゅう)の障壁(しょうへき) (barriers to entry)
