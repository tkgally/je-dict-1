投(な)げ技(わざ)

なげわざ
noun
throwing technique; throw
1. throwing technique; throw
A technique in martial arts, especially judo, sumo, and wrestling, that involves throwing an opponent to the ground.
見事(みごと)な投(な)げ技(わざ)で勝(か)った。
He won with a splendid throwing technique.
柔道(じゅうどう)の投(な)げ技(わざ)を練習(れんしゅう)する。
To practice judo throwing techniques.
あの力士(りきし)は投(な)げ技(わざ)が得意(とくい)で、相手(あいて)を豪快(ごうかい)に投(な)げ飛(と)ばす。
That sumo wrestler is good at throwing techniques and powerfully hurls his opponents.

WORD FORMATION:
投(な)げる (to throw) + 技(わざ) (technique). The 技(わざ) suffix is used for various martial arts techniques.

USAGE:
Most commonly associated with 柔道(じゅうどう) (judo) and 相撲(すもう) (sumo), but also used in professional wrestling and other grappling arts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投(な)げ技(わざ)をかける (to apply a throw)
  • 投(な)げ技(わざ)で一本(いっぽん) (an ippon by throw in judo)
  • 投(な)げ技(わざ)が得意(とくい) (to be good at throws)
  • 大(おお)外(そと)刈(が)り (major outer reap — a specific 投(な)げ技(わざ))

RELATED TERMS:

  • 固(かた)め技(わざ): grappling/pinning technique
  • 関節(かんせつ)技(わざ): joint lock technique
  • 寝技(ねわざ): ground technique
