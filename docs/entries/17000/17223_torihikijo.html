取引所(とりひきじょ)

とりひきじょ
noun
exchange; trading market
1. exchange; trading market
A marketplace or institution where securities, commodities, or other financial instruments are bought and sold. Includes stock exchanges, commodity exchanges, and cryptocurrency exchanges.
東京証券取引所(とうきょうしょうけんとりひきじょ)。
The Tokyo Stock Exchange.
取引所(とりひきじょ)で株(かぶ)を売買(ばいばい)する。
To buy and sell stocks on an exchange.
その仮想通貨(かそうつうか)取引所(とりひきじょ)はセキュリティが強(つよ)いと評判(ひょうばん)だ。
That cryptocurrency exchange has a reputation for strong security.

WORD FORMATION:
取引(とりひき) (transaction, trade) + 所(じょ) (place). Literally 'a place for transactions.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 証券(しょうけん)取引所(とりひきじょ) (stock exchange)
  • 商品(しょうひん)取引所(とりひきじょ) (commodity exchange)
  • 仮想通貨(かそうつうか)取引所(とりひきじょ) (cryptocurrency exchange)
  • 取引所(とりひきじょ)に上場(じょうじょう)する (to list on an exchange)

NOTABLE EXAMPLES:

  • 東京証券取引所(とうきょうしょうけんとりひきじょ) (Tokyo Stock Exchange, commonly abbreviated as 東証(とうしょう))
  • 大阪取引所(おおさかとりひきじょ) (Osaka Exchange)
