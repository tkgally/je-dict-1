陰(かげ)りが見(み)える

かげりがみえる
expression
to show signs of decline; to lose momentum
1. to show signs of decline; to lose momentum; to begin to wane
Used when something that was previously thriving or successful begins to show subtle signs of weakening or deterioration. Often describes economic trends, popularity, health, or emotional states.
景気(けいき)に陰(かげ)りが見(み)えてきた。
The economy is starting to show signs of decline.
彼(かれ)の笑顔(えがお)に陰(かげ)りが見(み)えた。
His smile showed a hint of sadness.
売上(うりあげ)は好調(こうちょう)だったが、最近(さいきん)は陰(かげ)りが見(み)え始(はじ)めている。
Sales were strong, but recently they have started to show signs of slowing down.

USAGE:
陰(かげ)り means "shadow" or "dimming," and the expression suggests that a shadow is becoming visible over something previously bright or prosperous. The decline is typically subtle and in its early stages — not a full collapse but the first signs of trouble.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 景気(けいき)に陰(かげ)りが見(み)える (the economy shows signs of decline)
  • 人気(にんき)に陰(かげ)りが見(み)える (popularity is waning)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)に陰(かげ)りが見(み)える (a shadow crosses one's expression)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 衰(おとろ)える: to weaken, to decline — describes a more advanced state of deterioration
  • 下降(かこう)する: to descend, to decline — more neutral and quantitative
