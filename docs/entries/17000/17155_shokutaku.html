嘱託(しょくたく)

しょくたく
noun, suru verb
commission; non-regular employee; contract worker
1. commission; entrustment; request
The act of entrusting a task or responsibility to someone.
調査(ちょうさ)を専門家(せんもんか)に嘱託(しょくたく)した。
We commissioned the investigation to an expert.
外部(がいぶ)の弁護士(べんごし)に法務(ほうむ)を嘱託(しょくたく)する。
To entrust legal affairs to an outside lawyer.
2. non-regular employee; contract worker; part-time staff
An employee hired on a contract basis, often a retired person rehired at reduced pay and hours. Common in Japanese government offices and companies.
嘱託(しょくたく)の仕事(しごと)は週(しゅう)に三日(みっか)だ。
The contract position is three days a week.
定年(ていねん)後(ご)は嘱託(しょくたく)として働(はたら)いている。
After retirement, I am working as a contract employee.
嘱託(しょくたく)社員(しゃいん)には正社員(せいしゃいん)と同(おな)じ福利厚生(ふくりこうせい)は適用(てきよう)されない。
Contract employees do not receive the same benefits as regular employees.
市役所(しやくしょ)では嘱託(しょくたく)職員(しょくいん)を募集(ぼしゅう)している。
The city hall is recruiting contract staff.

USAGE:
A compound of 嘱(しょく) (entrust) + 託(たく) (entrust, commission). Sense 1 is used as a suru verb (嘱託(しょくたく)する). Sense 2 is commonly used as a noun modifier (嘱託(しょくたく)社員(しゃいん), 嘱託(しょくたく)職員(しょくいん)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嘱託(しょくたく)社員(しゃいん) (contract employee)
  • 嘱託(しょくたく)職員(しょくいん) (contract staff)
  • 嘱託(しょくたく)医(い) (consulting physician)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 契約(けいやく)社員(しゃいん): contract employee — a broader term for any contract-based worker
  • 派遣(はけん)社員(しゃいん): temporary worker — sent by a staffing agency
  • 正社員(せいしゃいん): regular employee — a permanent, full-time employee
