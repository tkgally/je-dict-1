1. unauthorized parking; illegal parking; parking without permission
Parking a vehicle in a place without the permission of the property owner or in violation of parking regulations. A common issue in residential areas, apartment complexes, and private lots in Japan.
無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)禁止(きんし)。
No unauthorized parking.
無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)は罰金(ばっきん)の対象(たいしょう)となります。
Unauthorized parking is subject to a fine.
マンションの敷地内(しきちない)に無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)する車(くるま)が増(ふ)えて困(こま)っている。
We're troubled by the increasing number of cars parked without permission on the apartment complex grounds.

USAGE:
無断(むだん) means "without permission" and 駐車(ちゅうしゃ) means "parking." Commonly seen on signs in parking lots, apartment complexes, and near shops. The phrase 無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)禁止(きんし) (no unauthorized parking) is one of the most frequently seen warning signs in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)禁止(きんし) (no unauthorized parking)
  • 無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)する (to park without permission)
  • 無断駐車(むだんちゅうしゃ)車両(しゃりょう) (unauthorized parked vehicle)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ): street parking — parking on the road rather than in a lot
  • 違法駐車(いほうちゅうしゃ): illegal parking — broader term including any parking violation
