四(よ)つ角(かど)

よつかど
noun
crossroads; intersection; street corner
1. crossroads; intersection; four-way junction
A place where two roads cross, forming four corners. Used when giving directions or describing locations in a neighborhood.
四(よ)つ角(かど)を右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がる。
Turn right at the intersection.
あの四(よ)つ角(かど)にコンビニがある。
There is a convenience store at that intersection.
次(つぎ)の四(よ)つ角(かど)を左(ひだり)に曲(ま)がると、駅(えき)が見(み)えてきます。
If you turn left at the next intersection, you will see the station.

USAGE:
A common word in everyday directions. Refers specifically to a four-way intersection where streets cross at roughly right angles. More colloquial and specific than 交差点(こうさてん), which is the general term for any intersection.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 四(よ)つ角(かど)を曲(ま)がる (to turn at the intersection)
  • 四(よ)つ角(かど)の手前(てまえ) (just before the intersection)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交差点(こうさてん): intersection — the standard, slightly more formal term for any road crossing
  • 十字路(じゅうじろ): crossroads — literally "cross-shaped road," more formal
