1. silent prayer; moment of silence
A period of silence observed to honor the dead or show respect, typically with eyes closed and head bowed. Common at memorial ceremonies and commemorative events.
黙祷(もくとう)を捧(ささ)げる。
Offer a moment of silent prayer.
犠牲者(ぎせいしゃ)のために一分間(いっぷんかん)の黙祷(もくとう)が行(おこな)われた。
A one-minute moment of silence was held for the victims.
式典(しきてん)は全員(ぜんいん)の黙祷(もくとう)で始(はじ)まり、静(しず)かに亡(な)くなった方々(かたがた)を偲(しの)んだ。
The ceremony began with a moment of silence by all attendees, quietly remembering those who had passed away.

USAGE:
A compound of 黙(もく) (silence) and 祷(とう) (prayer). Used as both a noun and a suru verb (黙祷(もくとう)する). At Japanese memorial services, the emcee typically announces 黙祷(もくとう) to signal the start and 黙祷(もくとう)やめ or お直(なお)りください to signal the end.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 黙祷(もくとう)を捧(ささ)げる (to offer a moment of silence)
  • 黙祷(もくとう)する (to observe a moment of silence)
  • 一分間(いっぷんかん)の黙祷(もくとう) (a one-minute moment of silence)
  • 黙祷(もくとう)！ (Silence! — announcement at ceremonies)
