粗食(そしょく)

そしょく
noun
plain food; simple diet
1. plain food; simple diet; frugal meals
A diet consisting of simple, unrefined, or modest food without luxury items. Can be used positively to suggest a healthy, natural diet or negatively to suggest poverty.
粗食(そしょく)に耐(た)える生活(せいかつ)をした。
I lived a life enduring a plain diet.
健康(けんこう)のために粗食(そしょく)を心(こころ)がけている。
I try to eat simply for my health.
祖父(そふ)は戦時中(せんじちゅう)の粗食(そしょく)の経験(けいけん)から、食(た)べ物(もの)を粗末(そまつ)にすることを嫌(きら)った。
From his experience of eating sparingly during wartime, my grandfather disliked wasting food.

USAGE:
Often discussed in the context of traditional Japanese diets, health trends, or historical hardship. In modern usage, 粗食(そしょく) is sometimes viewed positively as a return to simple, wholesome eating.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 粗食(そしょく)に耐(た)える (to endure a plain diet)
  • 粗食(そしょく)を心(こころ)がける (to aim for simple eating)
  • 粗食(そしょく)生活(せいかつ) (a life of simple food)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 質素(しっそ): frugal, simple — broader term for a modest lifestyle, not limited to food

Related Words

Synonym: 質素(しっそ) (frugal (broader, not food-specific))
general
