街(まち)

まち
noun
town; street; downtown area
See also: 町(まち) (town, city)
1. town; city streets; downtown area
The bustling, commercial part of a city or town where shops, restaurants, and people gather. Focuses on the streetscape and atmosphere rather than the administrative unit.
街(まち)に出(で)かける。
To go out to town.
休日(きゅうじつ)は街(まち)で買(か)い物(もの)をした。
On the holiday, I went shopping downtown.
クリスマスの街(まち)はイルミネーションで美(うつく)しく輝(かがや)いていた。
The town was beautifully lit up with Christmas illuminations.

USAGE:
街(まち) emphasizes the physical streetscape, commercial areas, and atmosphere of a place. It evokes images of busy streets lined with shops, restaurants, and people. Use 街(まち) when describing going out to the lively parts of a city.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 街(まち)に出(で)る (to go out to town)
  • 街(まち)を歩(ある)く (to walk around town)
  • 街並(まちな)み (townscape, rows of buildings along a street)
  • 街角(まちかど) (street corner)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 町(まち): town, municipality — refers to the administrative unit or a residential neighborhood, while 街(まち) focuses on commercial streets and urban atmosphere
  • 都市(とし): city — a formal term for a large urban area
