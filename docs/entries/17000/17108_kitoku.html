危篤(きとく)

きとく
noun
critical condition; on the verge of death
1. critical condition; on the verge of death
A medical state where a person's condition is extremely serious and death is imminent. Used specifically for life-threatening situations.
祖父(そふ)が危篤(きとく)だ。
My grandfather is in critical condition.
危篤(きとく)の知(し)らせを受(う)けて病院(びょういん)に駆(か)けつけた。
Upon receiving the news of the critical condition, I rushed to the hospital.
患者(かんじゃ)は一時(いちじ)危篤(きとく)状態(じょうたい)に陥(おちい)ったが、その後(ご)回復(かいふく)した。
The patient fell into a critical condition at one point, but recovered afterward.

USAGE:
A serious medical term indicating imminent risk of death. When a family member is 危篤(きとく), it is socially expected in Japan to rush to the hospital immediately, and employers are expected to grant emergency leave. Often used with 状態(じょうたい): 危篤(きとく)状態(じょうたい) (critical state).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 危篤(きとく)状態(じょうたい) (critical state)
  • 危篤(きとく)に陥(おちい)る (to fall into critical condition)
  • 危篤(きとく)の知(し)らせ (news of someone being critically ill)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重体(じゅうたい): serious condition — severe but not necessarily terminal
  • 重篤(じゅうとく): grave condition — similar severity, more clinical
  • 瀕死(ひんし): on the verge of death — more dramatic/literary
