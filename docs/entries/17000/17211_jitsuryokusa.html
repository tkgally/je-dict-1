実力差(じつりょくさ)

じつりょくさ
noun
difference in ability; gap in skill
1. difference in ability; gap in skill level
The gap or disparity between the actual abilities of two or more people or teams. Commonly used in sports, competition, and educational contexts.
実力差(じつりょくさ)が大(おお)きい。
There's a big difference in ability.
両(りょう)チームの実力差(じつりょくさ)は歴然(れきぜん)だった。
The gap in skill between the two teams was obvious.
練習(れんしゅう)を重(かさ)ねて、少(すこ)しずつ実力差(じつりょくさ)を縮(ちぢ)めてきた。
Through repeated practice, I've gradually closed the gap in ability.

WORD FORMATION:
実力(じつりょく) (real ability, actual skill) + 差(さ) (difference, gap). The 差(さ) suffix is productive and can be attached to many nouns to express a gap or difference.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実力差(じつりょくさ)がある (there is a skill gap)
  • 実力差(じつりょくさ)を見(み)せつける (to show the difference in ability)
  • 実力差(じつりょくさ)を埋(う)める (to close the skill gap)
  • 実力差(じつりょくさ)を縮(ちぢ)める (to narrow the ability gap)
  • 圧倒的(あっとうてき)な実力差(じつりょくさ) (an overwhelming difference in ability)
