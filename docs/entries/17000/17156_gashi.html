餓死(がし)

がし
noun, suru verb
starvation; death from hunger
1. starvation; death from hunger; starving to death
Dying as a result of prolonged lack of food. Used both literally and occasionally figuratively to express extreme hunger.
餓死(がし)する人(ひと)がまだいる。
There are still people who die of starvation.
戦時中(せんじちゅう)、多(おお)くの兵士(へいし)が餓死(がし)した。
During the war, many soldiers starved to death.
干(かん)ばつによる食糧(しょくりょう)不足(ぶそく)で、餓死者(がししゃ)が出(で)ていると報告(ほうこく)されている。
It is reported that deaths from starvation are occurring due to food shortages caused by drought.

USAGE:
A compound of 餓(が) (starve, hunger) + 死(し) (death). Used as a suru verb (餓死(がし)する). A serious and somewhat formal term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 餓死(がし)する (to starve to death)
  • 餓死者(がししゃ) (people who died of starvation)
  • 餓死(がし)寸前(すんぜん) (on the verge of starvation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飢(う)え: hunger, starvation — the state of being hungry or starving
  • 飢餓(きが): famine, starvation — a widespread severe shortage of food
