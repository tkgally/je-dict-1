一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)

いっかつこうにゅう
noun
bulk purchase; lump-sum purchase
1. bulk purchase; lump-sum purchase; buying in one go
Purchasing goods or services all at once rather than in installments or separately. Used both for buying large quantities at a discount and for paying the full price upfront.
スマホを一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)した。
I bought the smartphone with a lump-sum payment.
一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)すると割引(わりびき)が受(う)けられる。
You can get a discount by purchasing in bulk.
会社(かいしゃ)では事務用品(じむようひん)を一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)してコストを抑(おさ)えている。
The company keeps costs down by purchasing office supplies in bulk.

USAGE:
Used in two main contexts: (1) buying in large quantities for a discount, and (2) paying the full price at once instead of in installments (as with smartphones or electronics). Often contrasted with installment payments (分割払(ぶんかつばら)い).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)する (to purchase in bulk / all at once)
  • 一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)割引(わりびき) (bulk purchase discount)
  • 一括購入(いっかつこうにゅう)と分割払(ぶんかつばら)い (lump-sum vs. installment payment)
