注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)

ちゅうもんひょう
noun
order slip; order form
1. order slip; order form
A paper form used to write down and submit orders, commonly found in restaurants (especially sushi-go-round and izakaya), shops, and businesses.
注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the order slip.
注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)をスタッフに渡(わた)した。
I handed the order slip to the staff.
この居酒屋(いざかや)では紙(かみ)の注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)に書(か)いて注文(ちゅうもん)するスタイルだ。
At this izakaya, you order by writing on a paper order slip.

USAGE:
Still commonly used in many Japanese restaurants, especially izakaya and conveyor belt sushi restaurants, though tablet ordering is increasingly replacing paper slips. In business contexts, order forms are used for purchasing supplies and goods.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)に記入(きにゅう)する (to fill in an order slip)
  • 注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)を提出(ていしゅつ)する (to submit an order slip)
  • 注文票(ちゅうもんひょう)を書(か)く (to write an order slip)
