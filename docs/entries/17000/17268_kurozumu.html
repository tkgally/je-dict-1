黒(くろ)ずむ

くろずむ
verb-godan
to darken; to blacken; to become discolored
1. to darken; to blacken; to become discolored
To gradually become dark or blackish in color, typically due to age, dirt, oxidation, or other natural processes. Describes a change that happens over time rather than suddenly.
壁(かべ)が黒(くろ)ずんでいる。
The wall has become discolored.
銀(ぎん)のアクセサリーが黒(くろ)ずんできた。
The silver accessory has started to tarnish.
長年(ながねん)の煙(けむり)で天井(てんじょう)が黒(くろ)ずんでしまい、塗(ぬ)り替(か)えることにした。
The ceiling had darkened from years of smoke, so we decided to repaint it.

USAGE:
Often used in the ている form to describe a state of discoloration. The change is gradual and usually undesirable. Common for describing aging surfaces, tarnished metals, and stained materials.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁(かべ)が黒(くろ)ずむ (a wall darkens)
  • 肌(はだ)が黒(くろ)ずむ (skin becomes discolored)
  • 銀(ぎん)が黒(くろ)ずむ (silver tarnishes)
  • 黒(くろ)ずんだ汚(よご)れ (dark discoloration/stain)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 黒(くろ)くなる: to become black — a straightforward color change, while 黒(くろ)ずむ implies gradual darkening or discoloration
  • 汚(よご)れる: to get dirty — broader meaning; 黒(くろ)ずむ specifically refers to dark discoloration
