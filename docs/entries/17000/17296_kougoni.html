交互(こうご)に

こうごに
adverb
alternately; by turns; in turn
1. alternately; by turns; in turn
Describes two or more things happening in an alternating pattern, taking turns one after another.
左右(さゆう)交互(こうご)に足(あし)を上(あ)げる。
Raise your legs alternately, left and right.
赤(あか)と白(しろ)の花(はな)を交互(こうご)に植(う)えた。
I planted red and white flowers alternately.
この曲(きょく)では、ピアノとバイオリンが交互(こうご)にメロディーを奏(かな)でる構成(こうせい)になっている。
In this piece, the piano and violin take turns playing the melody.

USAGE:
The base word 交互(こうご) is a noun/na-adjective meaning 'alternation.' The adverbial form 交互(こうご)に is the most common usage. Describes a regular back-and-forth pattern between two or more elements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 交互(こうご)に繰(く)り返(かえ)す (repeat alternately)
  • 交互(こうご)に並(なら)べる (arrange alternately)
  • 交互(こうご)に行(おこな)う (do in turns)
  • 左右(さゆう)交互(こうご)に (alternating left and right)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 代(か)わる代(が)わる: in turn/one after another — emphasizes people taking turns; more colloquial
  • 順番(じゅんばん)に: in order/in sequence — follows a set order, not necessarily alternating
