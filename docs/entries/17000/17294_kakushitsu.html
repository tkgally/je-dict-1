確執(かくしつ)

かくしつ
noun
feud; friction; discord; deep-seated conflict
1. feud; friction; discord; deep-seated conflict
A persistent, deep disagreement or antagonism between people or groups. Implies an ongoing tension that is difficult to resolve, often involving personal grudges or fundamental differences in opinion.
二人(ふたり)の間(あいだ)に確執(かくしつ)がある。
There is friction between the two of them.
長年(ながねん)の確執(かくしつ)がようやく解消(かいしょう)された。
The long-standing feud was finally resolved.
兄弟(きょうだい)の間(あいだ)で遺産(いさん)をめぐる確執(かくしつ)が生(う)まれ、家族(かぞく)の関係(かんけい)が壊(こわ)れてしまった。
A feud over the inheritance arose between the siblings, and the family relationship was destroyed.

USAGE:
A somewhat formal word often seen in news articles and written accounts. Describes conflicts that are deeper and more persistent than a simple 争(あらそ)い (dispute). Often used for family feuds, political rivalries, and organizational power struggles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 確執(かくしつ)が生(う)まれる (a feud arises)
  • 確執(かくしつ)が深(ふか)まる (friction deepens)
  • 確執(かくしつ)を解消(かいしょう)する (resolve a feud)
  • 長年(ながねん)の確執(かくしつ) (a long-standing feud)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 対立(たいりつ): opposition/conflict — more neutral; can be ideological without personal animosity
  • 不和(ふわ): discord — focuses on a lack of harmony in a relationship
  • 軋轢(あつれき): friction — very similar; slightly more formal
