炭坑(たんこう)

たんこう
noun
coal mine
1. coal mine
A mine where coal is extracted. Historically important in Japan, especially in Kyushu and Hokkaido.
炭坑(たんこう)で働(はたら)いていた。
He used to work in a coal mine.
この町(まち)はかつて炭坑(たんこう)で栄(さか)えた。
This town once prospered from coal mining.
閉山(へいざん)した炭坑(たんこう)の跡地(あとち)が観光地(かんこうち)になっている。
The site of a closed coal mine has become a tourist attraction.

WORD FORMATION:
炭(たん) (coal, charcoal) + 坑(こう) (pit, mine shaft) = coal mine

KANJI NOTE:
Also written as 炭鉱(たんこう). 炭坑(たんこう) emphasizes the mine shaft itself, while 炭鉱(たんこう) emphasizes the mining operation. In practice, they are used interchangeably.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 炭坑(たんこう)夫(ふ): coal miner
  • 炭坑(たんこう)町(まち): coal mining town
  • 炭坑(たんこう)節(ぶし): Tankobushi (famous folk song from Fukuoka)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan's coal mines, particularly in 筑豊(ちくほう) (northern Kyushu) and 夕張(ゆうばり) (Hokkaido), played a major role in Japan's industrialization but most closed by the late 20th century.
