控(ひか)えめ

ひかえめ
na-adjective / noun
moderate, reserved, restrained
1. moderate, restrained, modest
Keeping something at a lower level than usual, or behaving in a reserved, understated manner.
塩(しお)は控(ひか)えめにする。
I keep the salt to a minimum.
彼女(かのじょ)は控(ひか)えめな性格(せいかく)だ。
She has a reserved personality.
医者(いしゃ)に言(い)われて、甘(あま)いものを控(ひか)えめにしている。
On my doctor's advice, I've been cutting back on sweets.

USAGE:

  • 控(ひか)えめな + noun: a moderate/reserved ~
  • 控(ひか)えめに + verb: moderately, in a restrained way
  • Xを控(ひか)えめにする: to keep X moderate, to cut back on X

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 塩分(えんぶん)控(ひか)えめ: low-salt, reduced salt
  • 控(ひか)えめな態度(たいど): a reserved attitude
  • 控(ひか)えめな性格(せいかく): a modest personality
  • 量(りょう)を控(ひか)えめにする: to reduce the amount

NOTE:
Often seen on food packaging as 控(ひか)えめ to indicate reduced amounts of sugar, salt, etc.
