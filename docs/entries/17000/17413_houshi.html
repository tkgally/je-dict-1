1. service, volunteer work, dedication
The act of serving others selflessly, often without compensation. Used for community service, religious service, and charitable activities.
地域(ちいき)に奉仕(ほうし)する。
To serve the community.
毎週(まいしゅう)土曜日(どようび)にボランティア奉仕(ほうし)活動(かつどう)をしている。
I do volunteer service activities every Saturday.
社会(しゃかい)奉仕(ほうし)の精神(せいしん)を大切(たいせつ)にしている団体(だんたい)に入(はい)った。
I joined an organization that values the spirit of community service.
2. bargain sale, special offer
A discounted sale, often used in retail contexts where goods are sold at reduced prices as a service to customers.
奉仕(ほうし)品(ひん)コーナーはどこですか。
Where is the bargain section?
年末(ねんまつ)の奉仕(ほうし)セールで安(やす)く買(か)えた。
I was able to buy it cheaply at the year-end bargain sale.
この店(みせ)は毎月(まいつき)一回(いっかい)、奉仕(ほうし)価格(かかく)で商品(しょうひん)を提供(ていきょう)している。
This store offers products at bargain prices once a month.

USAGE:
The core meaning is selfless service to others. In sense 1, it appears in contexts like community service, religious ministry, and charitable work. In sense 2, it is used in retail — the idea being that the store is "serving" customers by offering discounts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社会(しゃかい)奉仕(ほうし) (community service)
  • 奉仕(ほうし)活動(かつどう) (service activities)
  • 奉仕(ほうし)品(ひん) (bargain goods)
  • 奉仕(ほうし)価格(かかく) (bargain price)
  • 無償(むしょう)奉仕(ほうし) (unpaid service)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サービス: broader "service" including customer service — 奉仕(ほうし) implies selflessness or sacrifice
  • ボランティア: volunteer work — 奉仕(ほうし) is more formal and traditional
