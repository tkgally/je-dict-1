流動的(りゅうどうてき)

りゅうどうてき
adjective (な)
fluid; unstable; in flux
1. fluid; unstable; in flux; changeable
Describes a situation, plan, or condition that is not yet settled and may change. Used for circumstances that remain uncertain or are still developing.
状況(じょうきょう)はまだ流動的(りゅうどうてき)だ。
The situation is still fluid.
今後(こんご)の予定(よてい)は流動的(りゅうどうてき)なので、まだ決(き)められない。
Future plans are still in flux, so I can't decide yet.
政治(せいじ)情勢(じょうせい)が流動的(りゅうどうてき)なため、投資家(とうしか)たちは慎重(しんちょう)な姿勢(しせい)を取(と)っている。
Because the political situation is unstable, investors are taking a cautious stance.

USAGE:
Commonly used in news, business, and political contexts to describe situations that are uncertain or subject to change. Often appears in phrases like 情勢(じょうせい)は流動的(りゅうどうてき) (the situation is in flux).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 流動的(りゅうどうてき)な状況(じょうきょう) (a fluid situation)
  • 流動的(りゅうどうてき)な情勢(じょうせい) (an unstable state of affairs)
  • 流動的(りゅうどうてき)な計画(けいかく) (plans that are still in flux)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不安定(ふあんてい): unstable — emphasizes lack of stability, often negative
  • 可変的(かへんてき): variable — neutral, emphasizes capacity for change
