弊社(へいしゃ)

へいしゃ
noun
our company (humble)
See also: 御社(おんしゃ) (your company (honorific)), 当社(とうしゃ) (our company (neutral))
1. our company; our firm (humble)
A humble term used to refer to one's own company when speaking to clients, business partners, or other external parties. Standard in business correspondence and formal communication.
弊社(へいしゃ)の製品(せいひん)をご利用(りよう)いただきありがとうございます。
Thank you for using our company's products.
弊社(へいしゃ)では来月(らいげつ)から新(あたら)しいサービスを開始(かいし)いたします。
Our company will begin a new service starting next month.
ご不明(ふめい)な点(てん)がございましたら、弊社(へいしゃ)までお問(と)い合(あ)わせください。
If you have any questions, please contact our company.

USAGE:
A humble (謙譲語(けんじょうご)) term used exclusively in business contexts when referring to one's own company to outsiders. Essential in business emails, letters, and formal meetings. Never used for someone else's company.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 当社(とうしゃ): our company — neutral/formal, used in internal documents or press releases. Less humble than 弊社(へいしゃ)
  • 御社(おんしゃ): your company — the honorific counterpart, used when addressing someone else's company in writing
  • 貴社(きしゃ): your company — honorific, used in formal writing (more formal than 御社(おんしゃ))

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弊社(へいしゃ)の製品(せいひん) (our company's products)
  • 弊社(へいしゃ)では (at our company)
  • 弊社(へいしゃ)まで (to our company)
