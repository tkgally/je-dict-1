1. to advance; to move forward; to make progress
To move forward physically, or to make progress toward a goal. Used for both literal movement and figurative advancement of plans, negotiations, or projects.
軍(ぐん)が前進(ぜんしん)した。
The army advanced.
交渉(こうしょう)が一歩(いっぽ)前進(ぜんしん)した。
The negotiations moved one step forward.
困難(こんなん)を乗(の)り越(こ)えて計画(けいかく)は大(おお)きく前進(ぜんしん)した。
Overcoming difficulties, the plan made great progress.

USAGE:
Used for both physical forward movement (troops, vehicles) and figurative progress (negotiations, research, plans). Often appears in news and formal contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一歩(いっぽ)前進(ぜんしん)する (to take one step forward)
  • 大(おお)きく前進(ぜんしん)する (to make great progress)
  • 着実(ちゃくじつ)に前進(ぜんしん)する (to advance steadily)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 進(すす)む: to advance, to go forward — general-purpose verb for moving ahead
  • 後退(こうたい)する: to retreat, to recede — the opposite of 前進(ぜんしん)する
