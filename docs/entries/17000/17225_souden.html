1. power transmission; electricity transmission
The process of transmitting electrical power from generating stations to distribution systems and end users, typically through high-voltage power lines.
送電線(そうでんせん)が切(き)れた。
A power line was cut.
台風(たいふう)で送電(そうでん)が止(と)まった。
Power transmission was stopped by the typhoon.
発電所(はつでんしょ)から都市(とし)まで高圧(こうあつ)の送電線(そうでんせん)で電気(でんき)が送(おく)られる。
Electricity is sent from power plants to cities through high-voltage transmission lines.

WORD FORMATION:
送(そう) (to send) + 電(でん) (electricity). Literally 'sending electricity.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送電線(そうでんせん) (power line, transmission line)
  • 送電(そうでん)を止(と)める (to stop power transmission)
  • 送電(そうでん)鉄塔(てっとう) (transmission tower, pylon)
  • 送電(そうでん)ロス (transmission loss)
  • 送電(そうでん)網(もう) (power grid)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配電(はいでん): power distribution — the final stage of delivering electricity to consumers
  • 発電(はつでん): power generation — producing electricity
