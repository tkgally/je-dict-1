耳(みみ)にする

みみにする
expression
to hear; to come to one's ears
1. to hear; to happen to hear; to come to one's ears
To hear something, often incidentally or without actively trying. Implies information reaching you through conversation, rumor, or media rather than direct experience.
そのニュースを耳(みみ)にした。
I heard that news.
最近(さいきん)よく耳(みみ)にする言葉(ことば)だ。
It's a word I've been hearing a lot lately.
彼(かれ)が転職(てんしょく)するという話(はなし)を耳(みみ)にして驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised to hear that he is changing jobs.

USAGE:
A set expression that implies hearing something incidentally or indirectly, rather than being told directly. Slightly more literary or polished than simply 聞(き)く. Often used when the information is surprising, noteworthy, or comes through informal channels.

PATTERNS:

  • 〜を耳(みみ)にする (to hear ~)
  • 〜という話(はなし)を耳(みみ)にする (to hear a story that ~)
  • よく耳(みみ)にする (to hear often)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 聞(き)く: to hear, to listen — general term, more direct
  • 耳(みみ)に入(はい)る: to reach one's ears — similar but more passive, the information comes to you
  • 小耳(こみみ)に挟(はさ)む: to overhear — specifically catching something by chance

Related Words

Synonym: 聞(き)く (to hear (general))
Related: 耳(みみ)を傾(かたむ)ける (listen carefully)
