1. to emigrate; to immigrate; to relocate; to settle in a new place
To move one's residence to a different country, region, or area with the intention of living there permanently or long-term.
海外(かいがい)に移住(いじゅう)する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of people emigrating overseas is increasing.
定年後(ていねんご)に田舎(いなか)に移住(いじゅう)する計画(けいかく)を立(た)てている。
I'm making plans to relocate to the countryside after retirement.
祖父母(そふぼ)は戦後(せんご)にブラジルへ移住(いじゅう)した。
My grandparents emigrated to Brazil after the war.

USAGE:
Used for both international migration (emigration/immigration) and domestic relocation (e.g., moving from the city to the countryside). In recent years, 地方(ちほう)移住(いじゅう) (relocation to rural areas) has become a popular topic in Japan as more people seek alternatives to city life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 海外(かいがい)移住(いじゅう) (emigration overseas)
  • 地方(ちほう)移住(いじゅう) (relocation to rural areas)
  • 移住先(いじゅうさき) (destination of relocation)
  • 移住者(いじゅうしゃ) (immigrant, settler)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)す: to move (residence) — everyday word, no implication of permanence
  • 転居(てんきょ)する: to change residence — formal, more about the move itself
