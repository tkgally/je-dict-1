1. mastery through experience; learning by doing
To truly understand or master something not through study alone but through personal experience and bodily practice. Implies deep, internalized understanding.
技術(ぎじゅつ)を体得(たいとく)する。
To master a skill through practice.
長年(ながねん)の経験(けいけん)で体得(たいとく)したコツがある。
There are tricks I've learned through years of experience.
料理(りょうり)の基本(きほん)は本(ほん)で学(まな)ぶだけでなく、実際(じっさい)に作(つく)って体得(たいとく)するのが一番(いちばん)だ。
The best way to learn the basics of cooking is not just from books, but by actually cooking and learning through experience.

USAGE:
Used with する to form a verb: 体得(たいとく)する. Emphasizes that the knowledge or skill has been internalized through practice rather than mere intellectual understanding.

WORD FORMATION:
体(たい) (body) + 得(とく) (to gain). Literally 'to gain with the body,' reflecting the idea of embodied learning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を体得(たいとく)する (to master a technique)
  • コツを体得(たいとく)する (to learn the knack of something)
  • 経験(けいけん)を通(とお)じて体得(たいとく)する (to learn through experience)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 習得(しゅうとく): acquisition of a skill — more general, can include study
  • 会得(えとく): to grasp, to comprehend — focuses on understanding rather than physical mastery
  • 身(み)に付(つ)ける: to acquire, to learn — everyday expression for the same idea
