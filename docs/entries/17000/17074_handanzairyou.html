判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)

はんだんざいりょう
noun
basis for judgment; deciding factor
1. basis for judgment; information for decision-making; deciding factor
Data, facts, or other information used as the basis for making a decision or forming a judgment. Often used in business and analytical contexts.
判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)がまだ足(た)りない。
We still don't have enough information to make a judgment.
この報告書(ほうこくしょ)を判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)にしてください。
Please use this report as the basis for your decision.
投資(とうし)の判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)として、過去(かこ)五年間(ごねんかん)の業績(ぎょうせき)データを分析(ぶんせき)した。
I analyzed five years of performance data as the basis for my investment decision.

USAGE:
A formal expression used in business meetings, reports, and analytical writing. Emphasizes the need for evidence-based decision-making.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)にする (to use as a basis for judgment)
  • 判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)を集(あつ)める (to gather information for decision-making)
  • 判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)が足(た)りない (insufficient information for judgment)
  • 判断材料(はんだんざいりょう)を提供(ていきょう)する (to provide information for decision-making)
