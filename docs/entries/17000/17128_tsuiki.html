追記(ついき)

ついき
noun, suru verb
postscript; additional note; addendum
1. postscript; additional note; addendum
Text added after the main body of a document or message. Also used as a suru verb meaning to add a supplementary note.
追記(ついき)があります。
There is a postscript.
記事(きじ)に情報(じょうほう)を追記(ついき)した。
I added additional information to the article.
間違(まちが)いがあったので、ブログの最後(さいご)に追記(ついき)を入(い)れた。
Since there was an error, I added a postscript at the end of the blog post.

USAGE:
A compound of 追(つい) (supplement, follow) + 記(き) (record, write). Used both as a noun and as a suru verb (追記(ついき)する). Common in online writing, blogs, and official documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 追記(ついき)する (to add a note)
  • 追記(ついき)を入(い)れる (to insert a postscript)
  • 追記(ついき)事項(じこう) (additional items)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 補足(ほそく): supplement — adding clarification to make something more complete
  • 付記(ふき): appended note — a note attached to a document
