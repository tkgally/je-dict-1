炊(た)き出(だ)し

たきだし
noun
emergency food distribution; soup kitchen
1. emergency food distribution; soup kitchen; mass cooking for disaster relief
The activity of cooking and distributing food in large quantities, typically to help disaster victims, homeless people, or others in need. A common form of volunteer activity in Japan, especially after natural disasters.
被災地(ひさいち)で炊(た)き出(だ)しを行(おこな)った。
We carried out food distribution in the disaster area.
ボランティアが温(あたた)かい炊(た)き出(だ)しを配(くば)っている。
Volunteers are handing out warm meals.
地震(じしん)の翌日(よくじつ)から自衛隊(じえいたい)による炊(た)き出(だ)しが始(はじ)まり、避難所(ひなんじょ)の人々(ひとびと)に食事(しょくじ)が届(とど)けられた。
Food distribution by the Self-Defense Forces began the day after the earthquake, delivering meals to people in evacuation shelters.

USAGE:
Very commonly associated with disaster relief in Japan. After earthquakes, typhoons, and other natural disasters, 炊(た)き出(だ)し is one of the first forms of support provided. Also done by community groups and NPOs for homeless people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 炊(た)き出(だ)しを行(おこな)う (carry out food distribution)
  • 炊(た)き出(だ)しをする (do food distribution)
  • 炊(た)き出(だ)しに参加(さんか)する (participate in food distribution)
  • 炊(た)き出(だ)しボランティア (food distribution volunteer)

CULTURE:
In Japan, 炊(た)き出(だ)し typically involves cooking large quantities of 豚汁(とんじる) (pork miso soup), おにぎり (rice balls), or カレー (curry) — foods that can be made in bulk and served easily.
