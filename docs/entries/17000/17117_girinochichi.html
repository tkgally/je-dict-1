義理(ぎり)の父(ちち)

ぎりのちち
noun
father-in-law
1. father-in-law
One's spouse's father, or a stepfather. The 義理 prefix indicates a family relationship by marriage rather than blood.
義理(ぎり)の父(ちち)に挨拶(あいさつ)した。
I greeted my father-in-law.
義理(ぎり)の父(ちち)とは仲(なか)が良(よ)い。
I get along well with my father-in-law.
結婚(けっこん)して初(はじ)めて義理(ぎり)の父(ちち)の家(いえ)を訪(たず)ねた時(とき)は、とても緊張(きんちょう)した。
I was very nervous when I visited my father-in-law's house for the first time after getting married.

USAGE:
The 義理(ぎり)の〜 pattern creates in-law family terms: 義理(ぎり)の父(ちち) (father-in-law), 義理(ぎり)の母(はは) (mother-in-law), 義理(ぎり)の兄弟(きょうだい) (brother/sister-in-law). When speaking about one's own father-in-law to others, use 義理(ぎり)の父(ちち). When addressing him directly, use お義父(とう)さん.

NOTE: 義理(ぎり) here indicates a relationship by marriage, not by blood.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 義理(ぎり)の父(ちち)に会(あ)う (to meet one's father-in-law)
  • 義理(ぎり)の両親(りょうしん) (parents-in-law)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 義父(ぎふ): father-in-law — formal/written equivalent
  • お義父(とう)さん: father-in-law — polite address form
  • 舅(しゅうと): father-in-law — traditional term, also read しゅうとめ for mother-in-law
