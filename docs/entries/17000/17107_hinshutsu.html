頻出(ひんしゅつ)

ひんしゅつ
noun
frequent appearance; occurring often
1. frequent appearance; frequent occurrence
Something appearing or occurring frequently. Often used in academic and test-preparation contexts for vocabulary, questions, or patterns that come up repeatedly.
頻出(ひんしゅつ)単語(たんご)を覚(おぼ)える。
To memorize frequently appearing vocabulary.
この問題(もんだい)は試験(しけん)で頻出(ひんしゅつ)する。
This type of question appears frequently on exams.
頻出(ひんしゅつ)問題集(もんだいしゅう)を使(つか)って効率(こうりつ)よく受験(じゅけん)勉強(べんきょう)を進(すす)めた。
I studied efficiently for the entrance exam using a book of frequently asked questions.

USAGE:
Commonly used as a noun modifier: 頻出(ひんしゅつ)単語(たんご) (frequently appearing vocabulary), 頻出(ひんしゅつ)問題(もんだい) (frequently asked questions). Also used as a verbal noun with する. Very common in academic and test-preparation contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頻出(ひんしゅつ)単語(たんご) (high-frequency vocabulary)
  • 頻出(ひんしゅつ)問題(もんだい) (frequently asked questions)
  • 頻出(ひんしゅつ)表現(ひょうげん) (frequently used expressions)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頻繁(ひんぱん): frequent — general-use adjective for frequency
  • よく出(で)る: appears often — colloquial equivalent
