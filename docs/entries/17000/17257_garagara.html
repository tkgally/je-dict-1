ガラガラ

がらがら
adverb / na-adjective / noun
empty, deserted; rattling sound
1. empty, deserted, vacant
A place that is unusually empty or has very few people, especially when one would expect it to be busy.
電車(でんしゃ)がガラガラだった。
The train was empty.
平日(へいじつ)の映画館(えいがかん)はガラガラだ。
The movie theater is deserted on weekdays.
台風(たいふう)のせいで店(みせ)はガラガラだった。
The store was empty because of the typhoon.
2. rattling, clattering
The sound of hard objects rattling or rolling around, or of a door being slid open.
戸(と)をガラガラと開(あ)けた。
I slid the door open with a rattling sound.
スーツケースをガラガラと引(ひ)いている。
She's pulling a suitcase along with a rolling sound.
建物(たてもの)がガラガラと崩(くず)れ落(お)ちた。
The building collapsed with a crashing sound.

This is an onomatopoeia with two distinct uses.

SENSE 1 (empty):

  • ガラガラだ/な: is empty, deserted
  • Often describes trains, theaters, restaurants, etc.

SENSE 2 (rattling):

  • ガラガラと + verb: with a rattling/clattering sound
  • Describes the sound of sliding doors, wheeled objects, collapsing structures, etc.

OTHER MEANING:
ガラガラ can also refer to a baby's rattle (toy), as well as gargling (嗽(うがい)をガラガラする).

WRITING:
Usually written in katakana as ガラガラ to emphasize its onomatopoeic nature.
