宝庫(ほうこ)

ほうこ
noun
treasure house; treasury; rich source
1. treasure house; treasury; storehouse of valuables
A building or place where treasures or valuable items are stored.
王宮(おうきゅう)の宝庫(ほうこ)を見学(けんがく)した。
I visited the palace treasury.
正倉院(しょうそういん)は古代(こだい)の宝物(ほうもつ)の宝庫(ほうこ)だ。
The Shosoin is a treasure house of ancient artifacts.
敵(てき)の宝庫(ほうこ)から財宝(ざいほう)を奪(うば)い取(と)った。
They seized the treasures from the enemy's treasury.
2. rich source; treasure trove; mine of (information, resources)
Figuratively, a place, thing, or person that is an abundant source of something valuable — knowledge, natural resources, cultural heritage, etc.
この森(もり)は天然資源(てんねんしげん)の宝庫(ほうこ)だ。
This forest is a treasure trove of natural resources.
あの図書館(としょかん)は知識(ちしき)の宝庫(ほうこ)だ。
That library is a treasure house of knowledge.
日本(にほん)は温泉(おんせん)の宝庫(ほうこ)で、全国(ぜんこく)に何千(なんぜん)もの温泉地(おんせんち)がある。
Japan is a treasure trove of hot springs, with thousands of hot spring areas across the country.

USAGE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is far more common in modern usage. The pattern 〜の宝庫(ほうこ) (a treasure trove of ~) is a set phrase used to praise a place or thing as being rich in something valuable.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 知識(ちしき)の宝庫(ほうこ) (a treasure trove of knowledge)
  • 自然(しぜん)の宝庫(ほうこ) (a treasure trove of nature)
  • 文化(ぶんか)の宝庫(ほうこ) (a treasure trove of culture)
  • 情報(じょうほう)の宝庫(ほうこ) (a mine of information)
