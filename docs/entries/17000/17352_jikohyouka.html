自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)

じこひょうか
noun
self-evaluation; self-assessment
1. self-evaluation; self-assessment
The act of evaluating one's own abilities, performance, or worth. Common in workplace, education, and psychological contexts.
自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)が低(ひく)い。
My self-evaluation is low.
年末(ねんまつ)に自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)シートを提出(ていしゅつ)する。
I submit a self-evaluation form at the end of the year.
自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)が高(たか)すぎると、周(まわ)りとの認識(にんしき)にずれが生(しょう)じることがある。
If your self-evaluation is too high, a gap can arise between your perception and that of those around you.

USAGE:
Used in two main contexts: (1) workplace performance reviews where employees assess their own work, and (2) psychology, referring to a person's sense of self-worth or self-esteem.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)が高(たか)い (to have a high self-evaluation)
  • 自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)が低(ひく)い (to have a low self-evaluation)
  • 自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか)シート (self-evaluation form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自己(じこ)肯定(こうてい)感(かん): self-affirmation, self-esteem — focuses on positive self-regard
  • 評価(ひょうか): evaluation — the general term without the self-referential element
