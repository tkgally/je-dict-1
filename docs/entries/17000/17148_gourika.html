合理化(ごうりか)

ごうりか
noun, suru verb
rationalization; streamlining
1. rationalization; streamlining; making more efficient
The process of making operations, systems, or organizations more efficient and logical, often by eliminating waste or unnecessary steps. In business, can also imply downsizing.
業務(ぎょうむ)の合理化(ごうりか)を進(すす)める。
To push forward with streamlining operations.
コスト削減(さくげん)のために生産(せいさん)ラインを合理化(ごうりか)した。
We rationalized the production line to cut costs.
合理化(ごうりか)の結果(けっか)、多(おお)くの社員(しゃいん)が配置転換(はいちてんかん)になった。
As a result of rationalization, many employees were reassigned.

USAGE:
A compound of 合理(ごうり) (rational, logical) + 化(か) (-ization). Used as a suru verb (合理化(ごうりか)する). In business contexts, 合理化(ごうりか) can be a euphemism for layoffs or downsizing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 業務(ぎょうむ)の合理化(ごうりか) (streamlining of operations)
  • 合理化(ごうりか)を図(はか)る (to aim for rationalization)
  • 合理化(ごうりか)計画(けいかく) (rationalization plan)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 効率化(こうりつか): making more efficient — focuses purely on efficiency
  • 改善(かいぜん): improvement — broader term for making things better
