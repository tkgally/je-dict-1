農具(のうぐ)

のうぐ
noun
farming tool; agricultural implement
1. farming tool; agricultural implement
Tools and equipment used in farming and agriculture, such as hoes, sickles, plows, and rakes. Includes both traditional hand tools and modern implements.
農具(のうぐ)を倉庫(そうこ)にしまった。
I put the farming tools away in the shed.
昔(むかし)の農具(のうぐ)が博物館(はくぶつかん)に展示(てんじ)されている。
Old farming tools are displayed in the museum.
祖父(そふ)は農具(のうぐ)の手入(てい)れを欠(か)かさなかった。
My grandfather never neglected the maintenance of his farming tools.

USAGE:
Used in agricultural, historical, and museum contexts. Traditional Japanese 農具(のうぐ) like 鎌(かま) (sickle), 鍬(くわ) (hoe), and 鋤(すき) (plow) are important cultural artifacts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 農具(のうぐ)を使(つか)う (to use farming tools)
  • 農具(のうぐ)の手入(てい)れ (maintenance of farming tools)
  • 伝統的(でんとうてき)な農具(のうぐ) (traditional farming implements)
  • 農具(のうぐ)小屋(ごや) (tool shed)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 工具(こうぐ): tools — general tools for construction or repair work
  • 道具(どうぐ): implements, tools — the broadest general term for tools of any kind
