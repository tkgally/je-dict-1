読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)

どくしょかんそうぶん
noun
book report; reading response essay
1. book report; reading response essay
A written essay expressing one's thoughts and impressions about a book one has read. A standard school assignment in Japan, typically assigned as summer vacation homework.
夏休(なつやす)みの宿題(しゅくだい)で読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)を書(か)く。
I'm writing a book report for summer vacation homework.
読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)のコンクールで入賞(にゅうしょう)した。
I won a prize in the book report contest.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)が苦手(にがて)で、いつも夏休(なつやす)みの最後(さいご)まで後回(あとまわ)しにしていた。
When I was a child, I was bad at book reports and always put them off until the end of summer vacation.

USAGE:
One of the most well-known school assignments in Japan. The 青少年読書感想文全国コンクール(せいしょうねんどくしょかんそうぶんぜんこくこんくーる) (National Youth Book Report Contest) is a major annual event. Many Japanese adults have strong memories (often negative) of struggling to write 読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん) during summer vacation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)を書(か)く (write a book report)
  • 読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)コンクール (book report contest)
  • 読書感想文(どくしょかんそうぶん)の課題(かだい)図書(としょ) (assigned books for book reports)

COMPONENTS:

  • 読書(どくしょ): reading
  • 感想(かんそう): impressions, thoughts
  • 文(ぶん): essay, writing
