歩数計(ほすうけい)

ほすうけい
noun
pedometer; step counter
1. pedometer; step counter
A device or app that counts the number of steps a person takes. Used for health and fitness tracking.
歩数計(ほすうけい)を付(つ)けて歩(ある)いている。
I walk with a pedometer on.
スマホの歩数計(ほすうけい)で毎日(まいにち)の歩数(ほすう)を記録(きろく)している。
I record my daily steps with my smartphone's pedometer.
歩数計(ほすうけい)を見(み)たら今日(きょう)は一万歩(いちまんぽ)も歩(ある)いていた。
When I checked my pedometer, I had walked as many as 10,000 steps today.

USAGE:
Originally a standalone device, now commonly a feature in smartphones and smartwatches. The Japanese health goal of 一万歩(いちまんぽ) (10,000 steps per day) makes pedometers culturally relevant.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歩数計(ほすうけい)アプリ (pedometer app)
  • 歩数計(ほすうけい)を付(つ)ける (to wear a pedometer)
  • 歩数計(ほすうけい)で測(はか)る (to measure with a pedometer)
