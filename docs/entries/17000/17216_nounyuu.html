1. delivery; supply
The act of delivering goods or products to a customer or organization, especially in a business context.
商品(しょうひん)を納入(のうにゅう)する。
To deliver goods.
部品(ぶひん)の納入(のうにゅう)が遅(おく)れている。
The delivery of parts is delayed.
新(あたら)しいパソコンが来月(らいげつ)納入(のうにゅう)される予定(よてい)です。
The new computers are scheduled to be delivered next month.
2. payment; remittance
The act of paying fees, taxes, or other amounts due, especially to an institution or government.
授業料(じゅぎょうりょう)を納入(のうにゅう)する。
To pay tuition fees.
税金(ぜいきん)の納入(のうにゅう)期限(きげん)は今月末(こんげつまつ)です。
The tax payment deadline is the end of this month.
学費(がくひ)の納入(のうにゅう)が確認(かくにん)できない場合(ばあい)は、履修登録(りしゅうとうろく)ができません。
If payment of tuition cannot be confirmed, you will not be able to register for courses.

USAGE:
Used with する to form a verb: 納入(のうにゅう)する. A formal, business-oriented word commonly found in contracts, invoices, and official documents.

WORD FORMATION:
納(のう) (to pay, to deliver) + 入(にゅう) (to put in). The kanji combination suggests putting something in its proper place.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 商品(しょうひん)を納入(のうにゅう)する (to deliver goods)
  • 納入(のうにゅう)期限(きげん) (delivery/payment deadline)
  • 納入(のうにゅう)業者(ぎょうしゃ) (supplier, vendor)
  • 授業料(じゅぎょうりょう)の納入(のうにゅう) (tuition payment)
  • 納入(のうにゅう)先(さき) (delivery destination)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配達(はいたつ): delivery — everyday word for delivering things like mail or packages
  • 納品(のうひん): delivery of goods — specifically about product delivery, not payment
