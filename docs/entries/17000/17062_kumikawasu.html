酌(く)み交(か)わす

くみかわす
verb
to drink together; to exchange cups
1. to drink together; to exchange cups of sake
To share drinks with someone, each person pouring for the other. Implies a warm, convivial atmosphere of mutual drinking.
友人(ゆうじん)と酒(さけ)を酌(く)み交(か)わした。
I shared drinks with a friend.
久(ひさ)しぶりに旧友(きゅうゆう)と杯(さかずき)を酌(く)み交(か)わした。
After a long time, I exchanged cups of sake with an old friend.
夜遅(よるおそ)くまで酒(さけ)を酌(く)み交(か)わしながら、昔話(むかしばなし)に花(はな)を咲(さ)かせた。
We shared drinks late into the night, reminiscing about the old days.

USAGE:
A somewhat literary or elegant expression for drinking together. Implies mutual pouring and shared conversation. More refined than simply 飲(の)む.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 酒(さけ)を酌(く)み交(か)わす (to share sake)
  • 杯(さかずき)を酌(く)み交(か)わす (to exchange cups)
  • 語(かた)り合(あ)いながら酌(く)み交(か)わす (to drink while talking together)

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (酌(く)み交(か)わす → 酌(く)み交(か)わさない → 酌(く)み交(か)わして). The 交(か)わす component indicates mutual action, like 話(はな)し合(あ)う.
