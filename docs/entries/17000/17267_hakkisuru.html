発揮(はっき)する

はっきする
verb-suru
to demonstrate; to display; to exhibit
See also: 発揮(はっき) (noun form)
1. to demonstrate; to display; to exhibit
To bring out and show one's ability, power, or qualities effectively. Used when someone fully utilizes or manifests an inherent quality or capacity.
実力(じつりょく)を発揮(はっき)する。
To demonstrate one's true ability.
チームワークを発揮(はっき)して試合(しあい)に勝(か)った。
We won the game by demonstrating teamwork.
緊張(きんちょう)していたが、本番(ほんばん)では持(も)ち前(まえ)の集中力(しゅうちゅうりょく)を発揮(はっき)することができた。
I was nervous, but during the actual performance I was able to display my natural concentration.

USAGE:
Almost always used with を to mark the quality being demonstrated. The subject brings out something they inherently possess — it is not used for acquiring new skills, but for making existing ones visible or effective.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実力(じつりょく)を発揮(はっき)する (demonstrate one's ability)
  • 能力(のうりょく)を発揮(はっき)する (display one's talent)
  • 力(ちから)を発揮(はっき)する (exert one's strength)
  • リーダーシップを発揮(はっき)する (show leadership)
  • 威力(いりょく)を発揮(はっき)する (demonstrate power/effectiveness)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 示(しめ)す: to show — more general; can show things to others, not limited to inherent qualities
  • 見(み)せる: to show/display — focuses on making something visible to others, while 発揮(はっき)する emphasizes bringing out latent ability
