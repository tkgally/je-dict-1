懸念事項(けねんじこう)

けねんじこう
noun
matter of concern; issue of worry
1. matter of concern; issue of worry; point of concern
A specific issue or problem that causes anxiety or requires attention. Commonly used in business, policy, and project management contexts to list potential risks or problems.
懸念事項(けねんじこう)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す。
To identify matters of concern.
プロジェクトの懸念事項(けねんじこう)をリストにまとめた。
I compiled a list of concerns about the project.
今回(こんかい)の契約(けいやく)にはいくつか懸念事項(けねんじこう)があるので、弁護士(べんごし)に相談(そうだん)したい。
There are several concerns about this contract, so I'd like to consult a lawyer.

USAGE:
A formal term frequently used in meetings, reports, and documentation. Often appears in bullet-point lists of risks or problems that need to be addressed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懸念事項(けねんじこう)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す (to identify concerns)
  • 懸念事項(けねんじこう)を共有(きょうゆう)する (to share concerns)
  • 主(おも)な懸念事項(けねんじこう) (main concerns)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 課題(かだい): issue, challenge — broader, includes tasks and goals, not just worries
  • 懸念(けねん): concern — the feeling of worry itself, without 事項(じこう) (specific item/matter)
