悪(わる)ふざけ

わるふざけ
noun
prank; mischief; practical joke (that goes too far)
1. prank; mischief; practical joke (that goes too far)
Playful or joking behavior that crosses the line into being harmful, disruptive, or inappropriate. Stronger than simple ふざけ — implies the behavior has negative consequences or is in poor taste.
悪(わる)ふざけはやめなさい。
Stop that mischief.
悪(わる)ふざけが原因(げんいん)でけがをした。
Someone got hurt because of a prank.
子供(こども)たちの悪(わる)ふざけがエスカレートして、先生(せんせい)に厳(きび)しく叱(しか)られた。
The children's pranks escalated, and they were severely scolded by the teacher.

USAGE:
Implies behavior that has crossed a line — it's not innocent fun but mischief that causes problems. Often used by adults criticizing children's behavior, or in news reports about pranks that caused damage or injury.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪(わる)ふざけをする (to play pranks)
  • 悪(わる)ふざけが過(す)ぎる (pranks go too far)
  • 悪(わる)ふざけが原因(げんいん)で (because of a prank)
  • 度(ど)が過(す)ぎた悪(わる)ふざけ (an excessive prank)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • いたずら: prank/mischief — can be innocent or harmful; broader range
  • ふざける: to fool around — the base verb; not necessarily negative
  • 冗談(じょうだん): joke — verbal humor, not physical pranks
