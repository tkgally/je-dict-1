排斥(はいせき)

はいせき
noun
rejection; exclusion; boycott
1. rejection; exclusion; boycott; ostracism
The act of pushing out or refusing to accept someone or something. Used for social exclusion, boycotts of products, and rejection of ideas or groups.
外国人(がいこくじん)排斥(はいせき)は許(ゆる)されない。
Exclusion of foreigners is unacceptable.
異(こと)なる意見(いけん)を排斥(はいせき)してはいけない。
You must not reject differing opinions.
歴史的(れきしてき)に、少数派(しょうすうは)が社会(しゃかい)から排斥(はいせき)されることは珍(めずら)しくなかった。
Historically, it was not uncommon for minorities to be excluded from society.

USAGE:
A strong, formal word with negative connotations. Often used in discussions of discrimination, xenophobia, and social justice. Can function as a suru verb: 排斥(はいせき)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 外国人(がいこくじん)排斥(はいせき) (xenophobia, exclusion of foreigners)
  • 排斥(はいせき)運動(うんどう) (boycott movement, exclusion campaign)
  • 排斥(はいせき)する (to reject, to exclude)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 排除(はいじょ)する: to eliminate, to remove — focuses on removal rather than social rejection
  • 差別(さべつ): discrimination — broader concept of unfair treatment
