壁際(かべぎわ)

かべぎわ
noun
by the wall, wall side
1. by the wall, at the wall, wall side
The area immediately next to a wall. Used when describing the placement of furniture, where someone is standing, or spatial arrangements in a room.
壁際(かべぎわ)に棚(たな)を置(お)いた。
I placed a shelf against the wall.
彼(かれ)は壁際(かべぎわ)に立(た)って話(はなし)を聞(き)いていた。
He stood by the wall, listening to the talk.
壁際(かべぎわ)の席(せき)は落(お)ち着(つ)くので、いつもそこに座(すわ)る。
The seats by the wall are relaxing, so I always sit there.

壁際(かべぎわ) refers to the area right next to a wall. It is commonly used when describing furniture placement, seating positions, or where someone is standing in a room.

WORD FORMATION:
壁(かべ) (wall) + 際(ぎわ) (edge, verge). The ~際(ぎわ) suffix creates spatial terms meaning "right by" or "at the edge of."

SIMILAR ~際(ぎわ) WORDS:

  • 窓際(まどぎわ): by the window
  • 水際(みずぎわ): water's edge
  • 際(きわ): edge, verge (standalone)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壁際(かべぎわ)に置(お)く: to place by the wall
  • 壁際(かべぎわ)に立(た)つ: to stand by the wall
  • 壁際(かべぎわ)の席(せき): seat by the wall
  • 壁際(かべぎわ)に寄(よ)せる: to push toward the wall
