1. to be stumped; to be fed up; to be at a loss; to be unable to cope
To be so troubled, annoyed, or overwhelmed by a situation that one does not know what to do or say. Literally 'to close one's mouth' — rendered speechless by difficulty or annoyance.
この暑(あつ)さには閉口(へいこう)する。
This heat is unbearable.
彼(かれ)の長(なが)い話(はなし)には閉口(へいこう)した。
I was fed up with his long-winded talk.
毎日(まいにち)続(つづ)く残業(ざんぎょう)に閉口(へいこう)して、ついに転職(てんしょく)を決意(けつい)した。
Fed up with the daily overtime, I finally decided to change jobs.

USAGE:
The pattern 〜に閉口(へいこう)する is the most common usage, where に marks the thing causing frustration. Expresses a mixture of annoyance and helplessness — one is not just annoyed but at a loss for how to deal with the situation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜に閉口(へいこう)する (be stumped/fed up by ~)
  • 閉口(へいこう)させられる (be made to feel at a loss)
  • すっかり閉口(へいこう)する (be completely fed up)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うんざりする: to be fed up/disgusted — more colloquial; stronger emphasis on disgust
  • 困(こま)る: to be troubled — broader and more neutral; does not carry the 'speechless' nuance
  • 辟易(へきえき)する: to be intimidated/put off — similar formality; emphasizes recoiling from something overwhelming
