提言(ていげん)

ていげん
noun, suru verb
proposal; suggestion; recommendation
1. proposal; suggestion; recommendation
A formal suggestion or recommendation, typically made by an expert, committee, or advisory body to those in authority. More formal and weighty than a casual suggestion.
専門家(せんもんか)が提言(ていげん)をまとめた。
The experts compiled their recommendations.
委員会(いいんかい)は政府(せいふ)に改革(かいかく)を提言(ていげん)した。
The committee proposed reforms to the government.
環境(かんきょう)問題(もんだい)に関(かん)する提言(ていげん)が報告書(ほうこくしょ)にまとめられた。
Recommendations regarding environmental issues were compiled in a report.

USAGE:
A compound of 提(てい) (present, put forward) + 言(げん) (words). Used as a suru verb (提言(ていげん)する) to formally propose or recommend. Common in politics, business, and academic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 提言(ていげん)をまとめる (to compile recommendations)
  • 提言(ていげん)を行(おこな)う (to make a proposal)
  • 政策(せいさく)提言(ていげん) (policy recommendation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 提案(ていあん): proposal — more general, used in everyday business and conversation
  • 助言(じょげん): advice — personal guidance rather than formal recommendations
