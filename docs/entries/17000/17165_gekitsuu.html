激痛(げきつう)

げきつう
noun
intense pain; sharp pain; severe pain
1. intense pain; sharp pain; severe pain
Extremely strong physical pain. Describes pain that is sudden, sharp, or overwhelmingly intense.
激痛(げきつう)が走(はし)った。
A sharp pain shot through me.
腰(こし)に激痛(げきつう)を感(かん)じて動(うご)けなくなった。
I felt intense pain in my lower back and couldn't move.
歯(は)の激痛(げきつう)に耐(た)えられず、夜中(よなか)に救急(きゅうきゅう)病院(びょういん)に行(い)った。
Unable to bear the severe toothache, I went to the emergency hospital in the middle of the night.

USAGE:
A strong, dramatic word used to describe the most severe level of pain. Common in medical contexts and personal accounts of injury or illness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 激痛(げきつう)が走(はし)る (a sharp pain shoots through)
  • 激痛(げきつう)に襲(おそ)われる (to be struck by intense pain)
  • 激痛(げきつう)を感(かん)じる (to feel intense pain)
  • 激痛(げきつう)に耐(た)える (to endure intense pain)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鈍痛(どんつう): dull pain — a constant, throbbing pain, less sharp
  • 痛(いた)み: pain — general, neutral term for pain

Related Words

Related: 痛(いた)み (pain (general term))
Antonym: 鈍痛(どんつう) (dull pain)
