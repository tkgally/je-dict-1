予防(よぼう)する

よぼうする
verb (suru)
to prevent; to take precautions against
1. to prevent; to take precautions against
To take measures in advance to stop something undesirable from happening, especially diseases, accidents, or disasters.
風邪(かぜ)を予防(よぼう)する。
To prevent a cold.
手洗(てあら)いで感染(かんせん)を予防(よぼう)できる。
You can prevent infection by washing your hands.
虫歯(むしば)を予防(よぼう)するために毎日(まいにち)歯(は)を磨(みが)きましょう。
Let's brush our teeth every day to prevent cavities.

USAGE:
Primarily used for preventing diseases, health problems, and disasters. The noun form 予防(よぼう) is equally common and frequently appears in compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病気(びょうき)を予防(よぼう)する (to prevent illness)
  • 感染(かんせん)を予防(よぼう)する (to prevent infection)
  • 予防(よぼう)接種(せっしゅ) (vaccination)
  • 予防(よぼう)策(さく) (preventive measure)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 防止(ぼうし)する: to prevent, to check — more formal, used for regulations and institutional measures
  • 防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent, to defend against — native Japanese verb, broader usage
