早寝早起(はやねはやお)き

はやねはやおき
noun, suru verb
early to bed, early to rise
1. early to bed, early to rise; keeping early hours
The habit of going to bed early and waking up early. Considered a virtuous and healthy lifestyle in Japanese culture.
早寝早起(はやねはやお)きを心(こころ)がけている。
I try to go to bed early and wake up early.
子供(こども)には早寝早起(はやねはやお)きの習慣(しゅうかん)をつけさせたい。
I want my children to develop the habit of going to bed early and waking up early.
早寝早起(はやねはやお)きは健康(けんこう)のもとだと昔(むかし)から言(い)われている。
It has been said since long ago that early to bed, early to rise is the foundation of good health.

USAGE:
A compound of 早寝(はやね) (going to bed early) + 早起(はやお)き (waking up early). Used as a noun or suru verb (早寝早起(はやねはやお)きする). A well-known proverb-like expression emphasizing healthy living.

CULTURE:
The full proverb is 早寝早起(はやねはやお)きは三文(さんもん)の徳(とく) (early to bed and early to rise brings threefold virtue), equivalent to the English saying "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy, and wise."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早寝早起(はやねはやお)きの習慣(しゅうかん) (habit of keeping early hours)
  • 早寝早起(はやねはやお)きを心(こころ)がける (to try to keep early hours)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 規則正(きそくただ)しい生活(せいかつ): regular lifestyle — a broader concept of living in a disciplined way
