闘(たたか)い

たたかい
noun
fight; struggle; contest
See also: 戦(たたか)い (battle (戦))
1. fight; contest; bout
A physical fight, competitive match, or direct contest between opponents.
激(はげ)しい闘(たたか)いだった。
It was a fierce fight.
チャンピオンとの闘(たたか)いが始(はじ)まった。
The fight against the champion has begun.
両者(りょうしゃ)の闘(たたか)いは三(さん)ラウンドまで続(つづ)いた。
The bout between the two continued for three rounds.
2. struggle; battle against hardship
A figurative struggle against adversity, illness, or difficult circumstances.
病気(びょうき)との闘(たたか)いが続(つづ)く。
The struggle against illness continues.
彼(かれ)の闘(たたか)いは多(おお)くの人(ひと)に勇気(ゆうき)を与(あた)えた。
His struggle gave courage to many people.
差別(さべつ)との長(なが)い闘(たたか)いの末(すえ)に、法律(ほうりつ)が変(か)わった。
After a long struggle against discrimination, the law was changed.

USAGE:
The noun form of 闘(たたか)う. Written with 闘(たたか) rather than 戦(たたか) to emphasize personal combat, competitive bouts, or struggles against adversity. 戦(たたか)い tends to refer to wars and large-scale battles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 病気(びょうき)との闘(たたか)い (struggle against illness)
  • 闘(たたか)いに挑(いど)む (to take on a fight)
  • 闘(たたか)いを繰(く)り広(ひろ)げる (to wage a prolonged fight)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 戦(たたか)い: fight, battle — focuses on wars and large-scale conflicts

Related Words

Contrast: 戦(たたか)い (war/battle context)
