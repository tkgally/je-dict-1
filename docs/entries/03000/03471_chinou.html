知能(ちのう)

ちのう
noun
intelligence, intellect
1. intelligence, intellect, mental ability
The capacity for learning, reasoning, understanding, and similar forms of mental activity.
知能(ちのう)テストを受(う)けた。
I took an intelligence test.
イルカは知能(ちのう)が高(たか)い動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Dolphins are highly intelligent animals.
人工(じんこう)知能(ちのう)の研究(けんきゅう)が進(すす)んでいる。
Research on artificial intelligence is progressing.

知能(ちのう) refers to the innate capacity for learning, reasoning, and problem-solving — mental ability rather than acquired knowledge.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 人工(じんこう)知能(ちのう) (AI, artificial intelligence)
  • 知能(ちのう)テスト (intelligence test, IQ test)
  • 知能(ちのう)指数(しすう) (intelligence quotient, IQ)
  • 知能(ちのう)犯(はん) (intellectual crime, white-collar crime)
  • 高(こう)知能(ちのう) (high intelligence)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 知能(ちのう)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い (intelligence is high/low)
  • 知能(ちのう)を持(も)つ (to possess intelligence)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 知識(ちしき): knowledge — information one has learned
  • 知恵(ちえ): wisdom — practical, experience-based intelligence
  • 頭脳(ずのう): brains, intellect — focuses on thinking power
  • 知性(ちせい): intellect — refined, cultured intelligence

Related Words

Related: 知識(ちしき) (knowledge)
Related: 知恵(ちえ) (wisdom)
Contrast: 知性(ちせい) (intellect (cultivated))
