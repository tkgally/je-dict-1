1. transferring schools, changing schools
Moving from one school to another, typically due to a family relocation. Used for elementary, middle, and high school students.
父(ちち)の転勤(てんきん)で転校(てんこう)することになった。
I ended up transferring schools due to my father's job transfer.
転校(てんこう)初日(しょにち)は緊張(きんちょう)した。
I was nervous on my first day at the new school.
転校生(てんこうせい)は自己紹介(じこしょうかい)をした。
The transfer student introduced themselves.
転校生(てんこうせい) = transfer student

USAGE: 転校(てんこう) specifically refers to changing schools at the elementary, middle, or high school level. For university, use 転学(てんがく) or 編入(へんにゅう).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 転校(てんこう)する (to transfer schools)
  • 転校(てんこう)届(とどけ) (school transfer notification)
  • 転校(てんこう)手続(てつづ)き (transfer procedures)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 転校生(てんこうせい) (transfer student)
  • 転勤(てんきん) (job transfer - often the reason for 転校(てんこう))
  • 転居(てんきょ) (moving house)
  • 編入(へんにゅう) (transfer admission - for universities)
  • 退学(たいがく) (dropping out)

CULTURAL NOTE: In Japan, transferring schools mid-year is relatively common due to company job transfers. Transfer students are a common theme in anime and manga.
