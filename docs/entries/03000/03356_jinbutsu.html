人物(じんぶつ)

じんぶつ
noun
person, character, figure
1. person, figure
A person, especially one of importance or distinction.
歴史(れきし)上(じょう)の重要(じゅうよう)な人物(じんぶつ)について学(まな)んだ。
I learned about important historical figures.
2. character
A character in a story, play, or film.
この小説(しょうせつ)の主要(しゅよう)人物(じんぶつ)は三人(さんにん)いる。
There are three main characters in this novel.
3. personality, character
A person's qualities or caliber.
彼(かれ)は立派(りっぱ)な人物(じんぶつ)だ。
He is a fine person.

人物(じんぶつ) can refer to a real person, a fictional character, or someone's qualities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 登場(とうじょう)人物(じんぶつ) (character in a story)
  • 歴史(れきし)人物(じんぶつ) (historical figure)
  • 主要(しゅよう)人物(じんぶつ) (main character/person)
  • 人物(じんぶつ)像(ぞう) (portrait, figure study)
  • 人物(じんぶつ)評価(ひょうか) (character evaluation)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 人物(じんぶつ): person of note, character (formal)
  • 人(ひと): person (basic, everyday)
  • キャラクター: character (borrowed, often for fictional characters)
