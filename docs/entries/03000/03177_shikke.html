湿気(しっけ)

しっけ
noun
moisture, humidity, dampness
1. moisture, humidity, dampness
Water vapor or wetness in the air or in materials.
梅雨(つゆ)の時期(じき)は湿気(しっけ)が多(おお)い。
There is a lot of humidity during the rainy season.
湿気(しっけ)で髪(かみ)の毛(け)がまとまらない。
My hair won't stay in place because of the humidity.
この部屋(へや)は湿気(しっけ)がこもりやすいので、除湿機(じょしつき)を使(つか)っている。特(とく)に夏場(なつば)は毎日(まいにち)運転(うんてん)している。
This room tends to trap moisture, so I use a dehumidifier. I especially run it every day in summer.

湿気(しっけ) refers to moisture or dampness in the air or materials.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 湿気(しっけ)が多(おお)い (high humidity)
  • 湿気(しっけ)がこもる (moisture builds up)
  • 湿気(しっけ)を取(と)る (to remove moisture)
  • 湿気(しっけ)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent moisture)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 湿度(しつど) (humidity - as a measurement)
  • 除湿(じょしつ) (dehumidification)
  • 乾燥(かんそう) (dryness - opposite condition)

USAGE NOTE:
湿気(しっけ) focuses on the tangible feeling of dampness, while 湿度(しつど) is used for numerical humidity measurements.
