悪口(わるくち)

わるくち
noun
slander, bad-mouthing

Definitions

1. slander, bad-mouthing, speaking ill of
Negative speech about someone, typically behind their back; verbal abuse or criticism of a person.

Examples

人(ひと)の悪口(わるくち)を言(い)うのはよくない。
It's not good to speak ill of others.
悪口を言う = to speak ill of, to badmouth
彼(かれ)は陰(かげ)で私(わたし)の悪口(わるくち)を言(い)っていた。
He was speaking ill of me behind my back.
陰で = behind someone's back
悪口(わるくち)ばかり言(い)う人(ひと)は信用(しんよう)できない。
You can't trust someone who only badmouths others.

Notes

USAGE: 悪口(わるくち) refers to negative speech about someone, especially when said behind their back. It has a clear negative connotation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 悪口(わるくち)を言(い)う (to speak ill of, to badmouth)
  • 悪口(わるくち)を聞(き)く (to hear bad things said about someone)
  • 陰(かげ)で悪口(わるくち)を言(い)う (to speak ill behind someone's back)

READING NOTE: Can also be read as わるぐち, though わるくち is more common.

CONTRAST:

  • 悪口(わるくち) - speaking ill of someone
  • 批判(ひはん) - criticism (more neutral, can be constructive)
  • 陰口(かげぐち) - gossip, backbiting (specifically behind someone's back)
