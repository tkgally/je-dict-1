同級生(どうきゅうせい)

どうきゅうせい
noun
classmate
1. classmate
A student in the same grade or class.
同級生(どうきゅうせい)と遊(あそ)ぶ。
To hang out with classmates.
高校(こうこう)の同級生(どうきゅうせい)に会(あ)った。
I ran into a high school classmate.
同級生(どうきゅうせい)の名前(なまえ)を覚(おぼ)えた。
I learned my classmates' names.
同級生(どうきゅうせい)と一緒(いっしょ)に勉強(べんきょう)した。
I studied together with a classmate.
小学校(しょうがっこう)の時(とき)の同級生(どうきゅうせい)から久(ひさ)しぶりに手紙(てがみ)が来(き)た。
I received a letter for the first time in a while from an elementary school classmate.

同級生(どうきゅうせい) combines 同(どう) (same) + 級(きゅう) (grade) + 生(せい) (student).

USAGE:

  • Refers to someone in the same school year, normally at the same school. The relationship keeps this name long after graduation, so 高校(こうこう)の同級生(どうきゅうせい) works equally well for a classmate you saw yesterday and one you last saw thirty years ago.
  • It marks equal standing. Unlike 先輩(せんぱい) and 後輩(こうはい), which encode who is senior, 同級生(どうきゅうせい) implies no ranking, and speech between 同級生(どうきゅうせい) is normally plain and casual.
  • Used of other people far more than of oneself. To say who your own classmate is, the natural pattern is 彼(かれ)は同級生(どうきゅうせい)です, not a self-description.

DISTINCTIONS:

  • 同級生(どうきゅうせい): classmate in the sense of same 学年(がくねん) (grade level); the two need not share a homeroom
  • クラスメート: classmate in the narrower sense of same クラス; a loanword, lighter and more school-bound
  • 同期(どうき): the workplace equivalent — someone who joined the company in the same year
  • 先輩(せんぱい): upperclassman
  • 後輩(こうはい): underclassman

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 小学校(しょうがっこう)の同級生(どうきゅうせい) (elementary school classmate)
  • 同級生(どうきゅうせい)と再会(さいかい)する (reunite with classmates)

Related Words

Synonym: クラスメート (classmate (same class))
Related: 同期(どうき) (same-year colleague at work)
Contrast: 先輩(せんぱい) (senior, upperclassman)
Contrast: 後輩(こうはい) (junior, underclassman)
