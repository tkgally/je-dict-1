社員(しゃいん)

しゃいん
noun
company employee
1. company employee, staff member
A person employed by a company, typically as a regular full-time employee.
彼女(かのじょ)は大(おお)きな会社(かいしゃ)の社員(しゃいん)だ。
She is an employee of a large company.
新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん)の研修(けんしゅう)が始(はじ)まった。
Training for new employees has begun.
社員(しゃいん)食堂(しょくどう)で昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べる。
I eat lunch at the employee cafeteria.

社員(しゃいん) combines 社(しゃ) (company) + 員(いん) (member).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん) (new employee)
  • 正(せい)社員(しゃいん) (regular/full-time employee)
  • 社員(しゃいん)食堂(しょくどう) (employee cafeteria)
  • 社員(しゃいん)旅行(りょこう) (company trip)
  • 社員(しゃいん)割引(わりびき) (employee discount)

SIMILAR TERMS:

  • 社員(しゃいん): company employee (general)
  • 会社員(かいしゃいん): office worker, company employee
  • 従業員(じゅうぎょういん): employee (more formal/general)
  • スタッフ: staff (from English)

USAGE NOTES:

  • 社員(しゃいん) implies full-time regular employment
  • Part-time workers are usually not called 社員(しゃいん)

Related Words

Synonym: 会社員(かいしゃいん) (office worker)
Related: 新入生(しんにゅうせい) (new student)
