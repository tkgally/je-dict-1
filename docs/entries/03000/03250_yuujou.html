友情(ゆうじょう)

ゆうじょう
noun
friendship

Definitions

1. friendship
The emotional bond and affection between friends; the state of being friends.

Examples

私(わたし)たちの友情(ゆうじょう)は一生(いっしょう)続(つづ)くと思(おも)う。
I think our friendship will last a lifetime.
友情(ゆうじょう)を大切(たいせつ)にしたい。
I want to cherish our friendship.
困難(こんなん)を乗(の)り越(こ)えて友情(ゆうじょう)が深(ふか)まった。
Our friendship deepened after overcoming difficulties together.

Notes

USAGE: 友情(ゆうじょう) refers to the emotional bond between friends on a personal level. Compare with 友好(ゆうこう), which is used for diplomatic relations between countries or organizations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 友情(ゆうじょう)を育(はぐく)む (to nurture friendship)
  • 友情(ゆうじょう)が深(ふか)まる (friendship deepens)
  • 友情(ゆうじょう)を大切(たいせつ)にする (to cherish friendship)
  • 固(かた)い友情(ゆうじょう) (strong friendship)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 友好(ゆうこう) - friendship, amity (diplomatic/formal)
  • 親睦(しんぼく) - fellowship, friendship (group/organizational)
