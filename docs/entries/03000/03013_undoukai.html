運動会(うんどうかい)

うんどうかい
noun
athletic meet, sports day
1. athletic meet, sports day
A school or community sports festival.
運動会(うんどうかい)に参加(さんか)する。
To participate in the sports day.
学校(がっこう)の運動会(うんどうかい)。
School sports day.
運動会(うんどうかい)で走(はし)った。
I ran at the sports day.

運動会(うんどうかい) is a school or community sports festival, typically held in autumn.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 運動会(うんどうかい)に参加(さんか)する (participate in sports day)
  • 運動会(うんどうかい)で走(はし)る (run at sports day)
  • 運動会(うんどうかい)の練習(れんしゅう) (practice for sports day)

CULTURAL NOTE:
運動会(うんどうかい) is a major annual event at Japanese schools. Typical activities include relay races, tug-of-war, and team dances. Families often come to watch and bring お弁当(べんとう).
