窓口(まどぐち)

まどぐち
noun
counter, window, service point

Definitions

1. service counter, ticket window
A counter or window where services are provided, such as at a bank, post office, train station, or government office.
2. contact point, liaison
Figuratively, a person or department that serves as the point of contact for an organization.

Examples

窓口(まどぐち)で切符(きっぷ)を買(か)った。
I bought a ticket at the counter.
三番(さんばん)窓口(まどぐち)にお越(こ)しください。
Please come to counter number 3.
お客様(きゃくさま)窓口(まどぐち)にお問(と)い合(あ)わせください。
Please inquire at customer service.
Sense 2

Notes

COMPOUND: 窓(まど) (window) + 口(ぐち) (opening, mouth)

COMMON TYPES:

  • 切符(きっぷ)の窓口(まどぐち) (ticket counter)
  • 銀行(ぎんこう)の窓口(まどぐち) (bank counter)
  • 受付(うけつけ)窓口(まどぐち) (reception counter)
  • お客様(きゃくさま)窓口(まどぐち) (customer service)
  • 相談(そうだん)窓口(まどぐち) (consultation counter)

MODERN CONTEXT: While many transactions have moved online, 窓口(まどぐち) services remain important for complex transactions and for those who prefer face-to-face service.
