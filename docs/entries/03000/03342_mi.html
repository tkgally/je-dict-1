実(み)

み
noun
fruit, nut, seed; content

Definitions

1. fruit, nut, seed, berry
The edible reproductive part of a plant.
2. content, substance
The real content or substance of something (figurative).
3. result, fruition
The outcome or result of effort.

Examples

木(き)に実(み)がなった。
Fruit grew on the tree.
努力(どりょく)が実(み)を結(むす)んだ。
The effort bore fruit.
Idiomatic expression
殻(から)を割(わ)って実(み)を取(と)り出(だ)す。
Crack the shell and take out the nut inside.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 実(み)がなる (fruit grows, bears fruit)
  • 実(み)を結(むす)ぶ (to bear fruit, come to fruition)
  • 実(み)を付(つ)ける (to bear fruit)
  • 実(み)のある話(はなし) (substantive talk, meaningful discussion)
  • 実(み)のない話(はなし) (empty talk, meaningless discussion)

RELATED KANJI READINGS:

  • 実(み) - fruit, content
  • 実(じつ) - truth, reality (as in 実(じつ)は)
  • 実(みの)る - to ripen, bear fruit (verb)

NOTE:
Distinguish from 身(み) (body) and 味(み) (taste) which share the same reading.

Related Words

Contrast: 身(み) (body (homophone))
