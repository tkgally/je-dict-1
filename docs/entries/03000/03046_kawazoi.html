川沿(かわぞ)い

かわぞい
noun
riverside, along the river
1. along the river, riverside
The area running alongside a river; used to describe locations or paths that follow the course of a river.
川沿(かわぞ)いの道(みち)を散歩(さんぽ)する。
I take a walk along the riverside path.
川沿(かわぞ)いに桜(さくら)の木(き)が並(なら)んでいる。
Cherry trees line the riverside.
このホテルは川沿(かわぞ)いにあって、景色(けしき)がいい。
This hotel is along the river and has a nice view.

川沿(かわぞ)い is a compound: 川(かわ) (river) + 沿(そ)い (along). Note the rendaku: そい → ぞい.

SIMILAR COMPOUNDS:

  • 海沿(うみぞ)い (along the coast)
  • 道沿(みちぞ)い (along the road)
  • 線路沿(せんろぞ)い (along the train tracks)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 川沿(かわぞ)いの + noun (riverside ~)
  • 川沿(かわぞ)いに + verb (along the river)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often used for describing pleasant walking routes
  • Common in real estate descriptions for properties near rivers
