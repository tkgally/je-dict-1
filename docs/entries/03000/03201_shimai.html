姉妹(しまい)

しまい
noun
sisters
1. sisters
Female siblings; two or more sisters.
彼女(かのじょ)には姉妹(しまい)が二人(ふたり)いる。
She has two sisters.
この姉妹(しまい)はとても仲(なか)が良(よ)い。
These sisters get along very well.
東京(とうきょう)とパリ(ぱり)は姉妹(しまい)都市(とし)だ。文化(ぶんか)交流(こうりゅう)が盛(さか)んに行(おこな)われている。
Tokyo and Paris are sister cities. Cultural exchanges are actively carried out between them.
姉妹都市 = sister city

姉妹(しまい) specifically refers to female siblings (sisters).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姉妹(しまい)都市(とし) (sister city)
  • 姉妹(しまい)校(こう) (sister school)
  • 姉妹(しまい)会社(がいしゃ) (sister company)
  • 双子(ふたご)の姉妹(しまい) (twin sisters)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 兄弟(きょうだい): brothers; also used for "siblings" in general
  • 兄弟(きょうだい)姉妹(しまい): siblings (gender-neutral, formal)

USAGE NOTE:
Unlike English "siblings," Japanese traditionally uses 兄弟(きょうだい) for brothers or mixed-gender siblings and 姉妹(しまい) specifically for sisters. However, 兄弟(きょうだい) is often used broadly to mean "siblings."
