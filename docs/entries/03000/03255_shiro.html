城(しろ)

しろ
noun
castle
1. castle, fortress
A large fortified building or group of buildings, historically used as residences for lords and for military defense.
大阪城(おおさかじょう)は有名(ゆうめい)な城(しろ)だ。
Osaka Castle is a famous castle.
城(しろ)の周(まわ)りには堀(ほり)がある。
There is a moat around the castle.
日本(にほん)には今(いま)でも多(おお)くの城(しろ)が残(のこ)っている。
Many castles still remain in Japan today.

城(しろ) is the native Japanese reading, while 城(じょう) is used in compound names.

FAMOUS JAPANESE CASTLES:

  • 姫路城(ひめじじょう) (Himeji Castle - UNESCO World Heritage)
  • 大阪城(おおさかじょう) (Osaka Castle)
  • 名古屋城(なごやじょう) (Nagoya Castle)
  • 熊本城(くまもとじょう) (Kumamoto Castle)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 城(しろ)跡(あと) (castle ruins)
  • 城(しろ)下町(かまち) (castle town)
  • 城(しろ)を築(きず)く (to build a castle)

KANJI READINGS:

  • しろ: standalone noun
  • じょう: in compound names (～城(じょう))
