共通点(きょうつうてん)

きょうつうてん
noun
common point, similarity
1. common point, common feature
A characteristic or aspect that two or more things share; a similarity.
二人(ふたり)には共通点(きょうつうてん)が多(おお)い。
The two of them have a lot in common.
この二(ふた)つの問題(もんだい)の共通点(きょうつうてん)は何(なん)ですか。
What is the common point between these two problems?
共通点(きょうつうてん)を見(み)つけることで、理解(りかい)が深(ふか)まる。
By finding common points, understanding deepens.

共通点(きょうつうてん) combines 共通(きょうつう) (common) + 点(てん) (point).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 共通点(きょうつうてん)がある (to have something in common)
  • 共通点(きょうつうてん)を見(み)つける (to find common ground)
  • 共通点(きょうつうてん)が多(おお)い/少(すく)ない (to have much/little in common)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 共通(きょうつう) (common, shared)
  • 相違点(そういてん) (point of difference) - antonym
  • 類似点(るいじてん) (point of similarity)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often used when comparing people, ideas, or things
  • Common in academic and analytical contexts

Related Words

Related: 共通(きょうつう) (common)
Antonym: 相違点(そういてん) (difference)
