視点(してん)

してん
noun
viewpoint, point of view, perspective

Definitions

1. viewpoint, point of view
A particular way of considering or regarding something.
2. visual point, point of focus
The point in a visual field where one's gaze is focused.

Examples

違(ちが)う視点(してん)から考(かんが)えてみよう。
Let's try thinking from a different perspective.
消費者(しょうひしゃ)の視点(してん)に立(た)って商品(しょうひん)を開発(かいはつ)する。
We develop products from the consumer's point of view.
この小説(しょうせつ)は子(こ)どもの視点(してん)で書(か)かれている。大人(おとな)には見(み)えない世界(せかい)が描(えが)かれていて興味深(きょうみぶか)い。
This novel is written from a child's perspective. It's interesting how it depicts a world that adults can't see.

Notes

視点(してん) refers to a perspective, viewpoint, or the point from which something is viewed or considered.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 視点(してん)を変(か)える (to change one's perspective)
  • 〜の視点(してん)から (from the perspective of ~)
  • 〜の視点(してん)に立(た)つ (to stand from ~'s viewpoint)
  • 新(あたら)しい視点(してん) (new perspective)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 視点(してん): viewpoint (slightly more concrete, as if looking from a position)
  • 観点(かんてん): point of view (more abstract, analytical angle)
  • 立場(たちば): standpoint, position (social or situational position)

NOTE: 視点(してん) and 支店(してん) are homophones. Context usually makes the meaning clear.
