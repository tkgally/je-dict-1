1. passage, passing through
The act of passing through or along a road, path, or area. Also refers to the traffic or flow of people and vehicles.
この道(みち)は車(くるま)の通行(つうこう)が多(おお)い。
This road has heavy vehicle traffic.
工事(こうじ)のため通行止(つうこうど)めになっている。
It's closed to traffic due to construction.
通行止(つうこうど)め = road closed, no passage
歩行者(ほこうしゃ)の通行(つうこう)を妨(さまた)げないでください。
Please don't obstruct pedestrian traffic.
この橋(はし)は大(おお)きな車(くるま)は通行(つうこう)できない。
Large vehicles cannot pass over this bridge.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で一部(いちぶ)の道路(どうろ)が通行止(つうこうど)めになった。
Some roads were closed to traffic due to the typhoon.

USAGE:
通行(つうこう) is the passing of people or vehicles along a road, bridge, or passageway. It works as a noun and, with する, as a suru-verb. The register is official: it belongs to road signs, notices, and traffic reports. In conversation people use 通(とお)る instead.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を通行(つうこう)する: to pass along ~ (the を marks the route travelled, not an object)
  • 〜の通行(つうこう)が多(おお)い: there is heavy traffic on ~
  • 通行(つうこう)できる / 通行(つうこう)できない: passable / closed to traffic

TRANSITIVITY:
通行(つうこう)する is intransitive. The を it can take is the particle of traversal, the same one in 道(みち)を通(とお)る, so it does not make the verb transitive.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 通行止(つうこうど)め: road closed
  • 通行人(つうこうにん): passerby
  • 通行料(つうこうりょう): toll
  • 通行禁止(つうこうきんし): no thoroughfare
  • 一方通行(いっぽうつうこう): one-way traffic

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 通(とお)る: to pass through — the everyday word
  • 通過(つうか): passing through without stopping
  • 交通(こうつう): traffic, transportation as a whole (noun only)
  • 往来(おうらい): coming and going
