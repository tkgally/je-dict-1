持(も)ち物(もの)

もちもの
noun
belongings, personal effects

Definitions

1. belongings, personal effects, possessions
Items that one carries or owns; things that belong to a person.

Examples

持(も)ち物(もの)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name on your belongings.
持(も)ち物(もの)検査(けんさ)があります。
There will be a belongings inspection.
遠足(えんそく)の持(も)ち物(もの)リスト
List of things to bring for the field trip

Notes

COMPOUND: 持(も)つ (to have, hold) + 物(もの) (thing)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 持(も)ち物(もの)検査(けんさ) (belongings check/inspection)
  • 持(も)ち物(もの)リスト (packing list, list of things to bring)
  • 貴重(きちょう)品(ひん)/大切(たいせつ)な持(も)ち物(もの) (valuables)

CONTEXTS:

  • School events (field trips, camping)
  • Security checkpoints
  • Lost and found
  • Travel packing
