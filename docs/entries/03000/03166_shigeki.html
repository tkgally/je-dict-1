刺激(しげき)

しげき
noun, suru-verb
stimulus, stimulation, incentive

Definitions

1. stimulus, stimulation
Something that provokes a physical or mental response.
2. incentive, motivation
Something that encourages action or effort.

Examples

コーヒーは脳(のう)に刺激(しげき)を与(あた)える。
Coffee provides stimulation to the brain.
新(あたら)しい環境(かんきょう)は良(よ)い刺激(しげき)になる。
A new environment serves as good stimulation.
彼(かれ)の成功(せいこう)に刺激(しげき)されて、私(わたし)も挑戦(ちょうせん)することにした。
Inspired by his success, I decided to try as well.
刺激される = to be stimulated/inspired

Notes

刺激(しげき) refers to anything that provokes a response, whether physical (sensory stimulation) or psychological (motivation, inspiration).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 刺激(しげき)を与(あた)える (to give/provide stimulation)
  • 刺激(しげき)を受(う)ける (to receive stimulation)
  • 刺激(しげき)になる (to serve as stimulation)
  • 刺激(しげき)される (to be stimulated/inspired)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 強(つよ)い刺激(しげき) (strong stimulation)
  • 良(よ)い刺激(しげき) (good stimulation)
  • 経済(けいざい)刺激(しげき)策(さく) (economic stimulus measures)

USAGE NOTE:
As a suru-verb, 刺激(しげき)する means "to stimulate" (transitive). The passive form 刺激(しげき)される often means "to be inspired" when referring to motivation.
