青年(せいねん)

せいねん
noun
youth, young person

Definitions

1. youth, young person, young man
A young person, especially a young man in their late teens to twenties.

Examples

青年(せいねん)たちが活躍(かつやく)している。
Young people are playing active roles.
青年(せいねん)時代(じだい)に海外(かいがい)で暮(く)らした。
I lived abroad during my youth.
一(ひと)りの青年(せいねん)が声(こえ)をかけてきた。
A young man spoke to me.

Notes

青年(せいねん) typically refers to young adults, especially males.

AGE RANGE:

  • Generally refers to late teens through twenties
  • More commonly used for males

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 青年(せいねん)時代(じだい) (youth, young days)
  • 青年(せいねん)団(だん) (youth group)
  • 青年(せいねん)海外(かいがい)協力(きょうりょく)隊(たい) (Japan Overseas Cooperation Volunteers)

COMPARISON:

  • 青年(せいねん): youth (formal, often male, late teens-20s)
  • 若者(わかもの): young people (gender-neutral, casual)
  • 少年(しょうねん): boy (younger, roughly 7-17)

Related Words

Related: 若者(わかもの) (young person (casual))
