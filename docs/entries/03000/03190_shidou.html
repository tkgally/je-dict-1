1. guidance, instruction
The act of guiding or teaching someone how to do something.
先生(せんせい)の指導(しどう)のもとで研究(けんきゅう)を進(すす)めた。
I conducted research under the teacher's guidance.
新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん)を指導(しどう)する。
I guide/train new employees.
彼(かれ)は長年(ながねん)にわたって若手(わかて)選手(せんしゅ)の指導(しどう)に当(あ)たってきた。彼(かれ)の指導(しどう)を受(う)けた選手(せんしゅ)は数多(かずおお)い。
He has been coaching young athletes for many years. Numerous players have received his guidance.
2. leadership, direction
The act of leading or directing a group.
指導(しどう)者(しゃ)として信頼(しんらい)されている。
He is trusted as a leader.
指導(しどう)力(りょく)のある人(ひと)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
We need someone with leadership ability.
彼女(かのじょ)の指導(しどう)のもとで、チームは大(おお)きな成果(せいか)を上(あ)げることができた。
Under her leadership, the team was able to achieve great results.

指導(しどう) refers to guidance, instruction, or leadership, typically in educational or professional contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 指導(しどう)する (to guide, to instruct)
  • 指導(しどう)を受(う)ける (to receive guidance)
  • 指導(しどう)に当(あ)たる (to be in charge of instruction)
  • 〜の指導(しどう)のもとで (under the guidance of ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 指導(しどう)者(しゃ) (leader, instructor)
  • 指導(しどう)教員(きょういん) (supervising teacher)
  • 指導(しどう)力(りょく) (leadership ability)
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)指導(しどう) (technical guidance)
  • 生活(せいかつ)指導(しどう) (lifestyle guidance/counseling)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 指導(しどう): guidance, instruction (broader, includes teaching and leading)
  • 教育(きょういく): education (formal teaching)
  • リーダーシップ: leadership (general leadership quality)
