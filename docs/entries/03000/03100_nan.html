何(なん)〜

なん
prefix
what (kind of), how many
1. what (kind of)
Prefix used with certain nouns to ask 'what' or 'which' type of something.
何(なん)曜日(ようび)に会(あ)いましょうか。
What day of the week shall we meet?
何(なん)時(じ)に起(お)きますか。
What time do you wake up?
何(なん)月(がつ)に生(う)まれましたか。
What month were you born in?
Sense 1: what (kind of)
何(なん)語(ご)を勉強(べんきょう)していますか。
What language are you studying?
Sense 1: what (kind of)
何(なん)番(ばん)ですか。
What number is it?
Sense 1: what (kind of)
2. how many, how much
Used with counters to ask about quantity.
何(なん)人(にん)いますか。
How many people are there?
With counter
何(なん)回(かい)行(い)きましたか。
How many times did you go?
Sense 2: how many
何(なん)個(こ)ありますか。
How many are there?
Sense 2: how many
何(なん)歳(さい)ですか。
How old are you?
Sense 2: how many
何(なん)本(ぽん)買(か)いましたか。
How many (bottles/pens) did you buy?
Sense 2: how many

PRONUNCIATION: なん vs なに

  • なん: Before た/だ/な row sounds and counters
  • なに: Before other sounds, or standalone

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 何(なん)時(じ) (what time)
  • 何(なん)曜日(ようび) (what day of the week)
  • 何(なん)人(にん) (how many people)
  • 何(なん)個(こ) (how many items)
  • 何(なん)歳(さい) (how old)
  • 何(なん)月(がつ) (what month)
  • 何(なん)日(にち) (what day of the month)
  • 何(なん)番(ばん) (what number)

ALSO:

  • 何(なん)で (why; by what means)
  • 何(なん)でも (anything, whatever)
  • 何(なん)とか (somehow, something or other)
