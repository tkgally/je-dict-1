申告(しんこく)

しんこく
noun / suru-verb
declaration, report, filing

Definitions

1. declaration, report
An official report or statement made to authorities.
2. tax filing, customs declaration
The act of filing taxes or declaring goods at customs.

Examples

確定(かくてい)申告(しんこく)の期限(きげん)は3月(がつ)15日(にち)だ。
The deadline for filing tax returns is March 15th.
税関(ぜいかん)で申告(しんこく)する必要(ひつよう)がありますか。
Do I need to make a declaration at customs?
所得(しょとく)を正確(せいかく)に申告(しんこく)しなければならない。
You must accurately report your income.

Notes

Most commonly used in tax and customs contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 確定(かくてい)申告(しんこく) (final tax return)
  • 税関(ぜいかん)申告(しんこく) (customs declaration)
  • 所得(しょとく)申告(しんこく) (income declaration)
  • 申告(しんこく)書(しょ) (declaration form)
  • 申告(しんこく)漏(も)れ (failure to report)

NOTE:

  • 申告(しんこく) (しんこく) should not be confused with 深刻(しんこく) (serious, grave)

Related Words

Related: 税金(ぜいきん) (tax)
