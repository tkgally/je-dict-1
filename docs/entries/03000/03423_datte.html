だって

だって
conjunction, particle
but, because, even
1. but, because
Used at the beginning of a sentence to offer a reason or excuse, often with a slightly defensive or childish nuance.
だって、知(し)らなかったんだもん。
But I didn't know!
Sense 1: defensive excuse (casual)
だって仕方(しかた)ないじゃない。
But it can't be helped, can it?
Sense 1
だって、疲(つか)れてたんだもん。
But I was tired!
Sense 1: excuse/reason
だって本当(ほんとう)なんだから。
But it's true!
Sense 1: defensive reason
だって、聞(き)いてなかったよ。
But I didn't hear you!
Sense 1: excuse/reason
だって、そう言(い)われたんだもの。
But that's what I was told!
Sense 1: defensive reason
2. even, also
Attached after a noun to mean 'even' or 'also,' similar to でも.
子供(こども)だってできる。
Even a child can do it.
Sense 2: even
誰(だれ)だって間違(まちが)える。
Anyone can make mistakes.
Sense 2: even (anyone)
何(なに)だって食(た)べる。
I eat anything.
Sense 2: even (anything)
彼(かれ)だって分(わ)からなかった。
Even he didn't understand.
Sense 2: even

SENSE 1 - CONJUNCTION:

  • Used at sentence start to give a reason or excuse
  • Has a casual, sometimes childish or defensive tone
  • Often paired with 〜んだもん, 〜んだから

SENSE 2 - PARTICLE:

  • Noun + だって: Even (noun), (noun) also
  • Similar to でも but more casual/emphatic
  • 誰(だれ)だって (anyone), 何(なに)だって (anything)

REGISTER: Casual/colloquial. Not used in formal speech or writing.

SIMILAR:

  • だって: Casual, defensive
  • でも: But (more neutral)
  • だけど/けど: But (casual)
