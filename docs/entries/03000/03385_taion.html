体温(たいおん)

たいおん
noun
body temperature
1. body temperature
The temperature of the body, typically measured to check for fever or illness.
体温(たいおん)を測(はか)ってください。
Please take your temperature.
体温(たいおん)が38度(ど)もある。
My temperature is as high as 38 degrees.
病院(びょういん)に入(はい)る前(まえ)に体温(たいおん)チェックがあった。
There was a temperature check before entering the hospital.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体温(たいおん)を測(はか)る (to take one's temperature)
  • 体温(たいおん)が上(あ)がる (temperature rises)
  • 体温(たいおん)が下(さ)がる (temperature drops)
  • 体温計(たいおんけい) (thermometer)

NORMAL RANGE: In Japan, normal body temperature is typically considered around 36-37°C (degrees Celsius).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 熱(ねつ) (fever)
  • 平熱(へいねつ) (normal body temperature)
