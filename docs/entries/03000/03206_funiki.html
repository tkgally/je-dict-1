雰囲気(ふんいき)

ふんいき
noun
atmosphere, mood

Definitions

1. atmosphere, mood, ambiance
The overall feeling or character of a place, situation, or person.

Examples

このレストランは雰囲気(ふんいき)がいい。
This restaurant has a nice atmosphere.
会議(かいぎ)の雰囲気(ふんいき)が重(おも)かった。
The atmosphere of the meeting was heavy.
彼女(かのじょ)には独特(どくとく)の雰囲気(ふんいき)がある。
She has a unique aura about her.

Notes

USAGE: 雰囲気(ふんいき) describes the intangible feeling of places, events, or people. Very commonly used in everyday conversation.

PRONUNCIATION NOTE: Though written ふんいき, some speakers say ふいんき. The standard pronunciation is ふんいき.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)がいい/わるい (good/bad atmosphere)
  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)を出(だ)す (to create atmosphere)
  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)を壊(こわ)す (to ruin the atmosphere)
  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)に飲(の)まれる (to be overwhelmed by the atmosphere)
  • 独特(どくとく)の雰囲気(ふんいき) (unique atmosphere)
