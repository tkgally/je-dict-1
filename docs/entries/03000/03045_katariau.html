語(かた)り合(あ)う

かたりあう
verb (godan)
to talk together, to discuss
1. to talk together, to converse
To engage in meaningful conversation with someone, often about personal matters or shared experiences.
友達(ともだち)と夢(ゆめ)について語(かた)り合(あ)った。
I talked with my friend about our dreams.
夜(よる)を通(とお)して語(かた)り合(あ)う。
To talk through the night.
二人(ふたり)は将来(しょうらい)のことを真剣(しんけん)に語(かた)り合(あ)った。
The two of them had a serious conversation about the future.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xと語(かた)り合(あ)う (to talk with X).

語(かた)り合(あ)う is formed from 語(かた)る (to tell, narrate) + 合(あ)う (mutual action suffix). The 合(あ)う indicates both parties participating.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～と語(かた)り合(あ)う (to talk with ~)
  • ～について語(かた)り合(あ)う (to talk about ~)
  • 夜(よる)を徹(てっ)して語(かた)り合(あ)う (to talk all night long)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 語(かた)り合(あ)う: to talk together (implies depth/meaning)
  • 話(はな)し合(あ)う: to discuss (more practical/decision-focused)
  • 語(かた)る: to tell, narrate (one-directional)

Related Words

Related: 語(かた)る (to tell)
