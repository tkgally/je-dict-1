得意(とくい)

とくい
na-adjective, noun
good at, specialty
1. good at, skilled at
Being skillful or proficient at something. Used to describe one's strengths or areas of expertise.
料理(りょうり)が得意(とくい)です。
I'm good at cooking.
2. proud, triumphant
Feeling proud or self-satisfied, often shown in one's expression or manner.
得意(とくい)な顔(かお)をしている。
He has a proud/smug look on his face.
3. regular customer, valued client
A customer who frequently patronizes a business. Used in business contexts.
彼(かれ)は得意(とくい)先(さき)を回(まわ)っている。
He's visiting his regular customers.
得意先 = regular customer, client

USAGE: 得意(とくい) has three main meanings. Most commonly used to describe what one is good at.

FORMS:

  • 得意(とくい)な〜 (na-adjective: good at ~)
  • 得意(とくい) (noun: one's forte, specialty)
  • 得意(とくい)になる (to become proud/smug)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が得意(とくい)だ (to be good at ~)
  • 得意(とくい)科目(かもく) (best subject)
  • 得意(とくい)分野(ぶんや) (area of expertise)
  • 得意(とくい)先(さき)/得意(とくい)客(きゃく) (regular customer)
  • 得意(とくい)満面(まんめん) (looking very proud)

ANTONYM:

  • 苦手(にがて) (bad at, weak point) - opposite of sense 1

Note: When someone says they're 得意(とくい) at something in Japanese, they're not bragging - it's a straightforward statement of ability.
