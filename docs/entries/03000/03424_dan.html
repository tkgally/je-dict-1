段(だん)

だん
noun, counter
step, level, rank

Definitions

1. step, stair
A surface or platform forming one level of a staircase.
2. level, rank, grade
A degree of proficiency or position in a hierarchy, especially in martial arts or traditional arts.
3. paragraph, column
A section of text or a column in a newspaper.

Examples

階段(かいだん)を三段(さんだん)上(のぼ)る。
To climb three steps on the stairs.
Sense 1: counter for steps
柔道(じゅうどう)で三段(さんだん)を取(と)った。
I achieved third dan in judo.
Sense 2: rank in martial arts
新聞(しんぶん)の一段(いちだん)に記事(きじ)が載(の)っていた。
There was an article in one column of the newspaper.
Sense 3: newspaper column

Notes

COUNTER USE:

  • 一段(いちだん), 二段(にだん), 三段(さんだん)...

MARTIAL ARTS RANKING:

  • 初段(しょだん) (1st dan)
  • 二段(にだん) (2nd dan)
  • 三段(さんだん) (3rd dan)
  • ... up to 十段(じゅうだん) (10th dan)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 階段(かいだん) (stairs, staircase)
  • 段々(だんだん) (gradually, step by step)
  • 段階(だんかい) (stage, phase)
  • 段取り(だんどり) (arrangement, preparation)

NOTE: In martial arts, 段(だん) indicates high ranks (black belt levels), while 級(きゅう) indicates beginner ranks.
