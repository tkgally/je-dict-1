陽気(ようき)

ようき
な-adjective
cheerful, merry

Definitions

1. cheerful, merry, lively
Having a happy, outgoing disposition; full of good spirits.
2. weather, season
The condition of the weather or atmosphere (noun usage).

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は陽気(ようき)な性格(せいかく)だ。
She has a cheerful personality.
陽気(ようき)な音楽(おんがく)が流(なが)れている。
Cheerful music is playing.
春(はる)の陽気(ようき)に誘(さそ)われて散歩(さんぽ)に出(で)た。
Tempted by the spring weather, I went out for a walk.
Noun usage meaning 'weather/atmosphere'

Notes

USAGE: 陽気(ようき) has two main uses: (1) as a な-adjective meaning cheerful/merry, and (2) as a noun referring to weather or seasonal atmosphere.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 陽気(ようき)な人(ひと) (cheerful person)
  • 陽気(ようき)な性格(せいかく) (cheerful personality)
  • 春(はる)の陽気(ようき) (spring weather/atmosphere)

CONTRAST:

  • 陰気(いんき) - gloomy, melancholy

NOTE: Do not confuse with 容器(ようき) (container), which has the same reading.
