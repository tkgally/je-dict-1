知事(ちじ)

ちじ
noun
prefectural governor
1. governor (of a prefecture)
The elected head of a prefectural government in Japan. Each of Japan's 47 prefectures has one governor.
知事(ちじ)選挙(せんきょ)は来月(らいげつ)だ。
The gubernatorial election is next month.
東京都(とうきょうと)知事(ちじ)が会見(かいけん)を開(ひら)いた。
The Governor of Tokyo held a press conference.
新(あたら)しい知事(ちじ)は若(わか)い女性(じょせい)だ。
The new governor is a young woman.

USAGE NOTE:
知事(ちじ) specifically refers to the governor of a 都道府県(とどうふけん) (prefecture). For other types of regional leaders, different terms are used (e.g., 市長(しちょう) for mayor).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜県(けん)知事(ちじ) (governor of ~ Prefecture)
  • 知事(ちじ)選挙(せんきょ) (gubernatorial election)
  • 知事(ちじ)に当選(とうせん)する (to be elected governor)
