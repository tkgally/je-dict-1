必死(ひっし)

ひっし
na-adjective / noun
desperate, frantic

Definitions

1. desperate, frantic
Trying extremely hard with full effort, often in a dire situation.
2. inevitable death, certain death
A situation where death is unavoidable (literary/classical usage).

Examples

必死(ひっし)に勉強(べんきょう)した。
I studied desperately.
彼(かれ)は必死(ひっし)の形相(ぎょうそう)で走(はし)っていた。
He was running with a desperate look on his face.
必死(ひっし)になって説得(せっとく)したが、無駄(むだ)だった。
I tried desperately to persuade them, but it was useless.

Notes

Often used adverbially with に to mean "desperately" or "frantically."

FORMS:

  • 必死(ひっし)に〜する (to do something desperately)
  • 必死(ひっし)になって (becoming desperate)
  • 必死(ひっし)の + noun (必死(ひっし)の努力(どりょく) = desperate effort)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 必死(ひっし)に働(はたら)く (to work frantically)
  • 必死(ひっし)に逃(に)げる (to flee desperately)
  • 必死(ひっし)の覚悟(かくご) (desperate resolve)
  • 必死(ひっし)の抵抗(ていこう) (desperate resistance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい) - with all one's might (more positive)
  • 必死(ひっし) - desperate, frantic (implies urgency/danger)
