入社(にゅうしゃ)

にゅうしゃ
noun
joining a company
1. joining a company, entering a company
The act of becoming an employee of a company; starting work at a company.
来年(らいねん)の四月(しがつ)に入社(にゅうしゃ)します。
I will join the company next April.
入社(にゅうしゃ)して三(さん)年目(ねんめ)です。
This is my third year at the company.
入社(にゅうしゃ)試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)した。
I passed the company entrance exam.
入社(にゅうしゃ)式(しき)は四月(しがつ)の一日(ついたち)に行(おこな)われる。
The company entrance ceremony is held on April 1st.
入社(にゅうしゃ)してすぐは覚(おぼ)えることがたくさんあって大変(たいへん)だった。
Right after joining the company, there was so much to learn that it was tough.

COMPOUND: 入(にゅう) (enter) + 社(しゃ) (company)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)する (to join a company)
  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)式(しき) (company entrance ceremony)
  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)試験(しけん) (company entrance exam)
  • 入社(にゅうしゃ)面接(めんせつ) (job interview)
  • 新入社員(しんにゅうしゃいん) (new employee)

ANTONYM: 退社(たいしゃ) (leaving a company, resignation)

In Japan, most new graduates join companies in April, and 入社(にゅうしゃ)式(しき) (entrance ceremonies) are a significant corporate event.

Related Words

Antonym: 退社(たいしゃ)
