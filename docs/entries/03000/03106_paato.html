パート

ぱーと
noun
part-time (work)
1. part-time work, part-time job
Part-time employment, especially regular but less than full-time work. Short for パートタイム. Often refers specifically to part-time work done by housewives or middle-aged women.
母(はは)はスーパーでパートをしている。
My mother works part-time at a supermarket.
パートの仕事(しごと)を探(さが)しています。
I'm looking for a part-time job.
パートで働(はたら)いて家計(かけい)を助(たす)けている。
I work part-time to help with the household finances.
2. part-time worker
A person who works part-time, especially in retail or service industries.
パートさんが足(た)りない。
We don't have enough part-time workers.
パートさん = part-timer (person)
新(あたら)しいパートが今日(きょう)から来(く)る。
A new part-timer is starting today.
パートの人(ひと)も社員(しゃいん)と同(おな)じように保険(ほけん)に入(はい)れるようになった。
Part-time workers can now enroll in insurance the same as regular employees.

ABBREVIATION: パートタイム → パート

LOANWORD from English "part (time)."

USAGE DISTINCTION:

  • パート: Often implies regular part-time work, traditionally associated with married women or middle-aged workers
  • アルバイト (バイト): Part-time work typically done by students or younger people, often temporary

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • パートをする/パートで働(はたら)く (to work part-time)
  • パートの求人(きゅうじん) (part-time job listings)
  • パートさん (part-time worker - colloquial)
  • パート従業員(じゅうぎょういん) (part-time employee - formal)

In Japanese workplaces, パート workers typically have different benefits and status than 正社員(せいしゃいん) (full-time employees).
