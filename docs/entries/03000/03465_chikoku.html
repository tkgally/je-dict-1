1. lateness, tardiness, being late
Arriving after the scheduled or expected time.
遅刻(ちこく)しないでください。
Please don't be late.
電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れて、会社(かいしゃ)に遅刻(ちこく)した。
The train was delayed, so I was late for work.
彼(かれ)は毎朝(まいあさ)遅刻(ちこく)する。
He is late every morning.

遅刻(ちこく) means arriving after the scheduled or expected time. Functions as both a noun and a suru verb.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に遅刻(ちこく)する: to be late for ~
  • 遅刻(ちこく)の理由(りゆう): reason for being late
  • 遅刻(ちこく)届(とどけ): late arrival notice (at school/work)
  • 遅刻(ちこく)常習(じょうしゅう): habitual lateness

CULTURAL NOTE:
Punctuality is highly valued in Japan. Being late (遅刻(ちこく)) — even by a few minutes — is taken seriously at school and in the workplace. An apology or explanation is typically expected.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遅(おく)れる: to be late/delayed (more general — can apply to trains, deliveries, etc.)
  • 遅刻(ちこく): specifically a person arriving late for a scheduled event

Related Words

Antonym: 時間(じかん)どおり (on time)
Contrast: 早退(そうたい) (leaving early)
