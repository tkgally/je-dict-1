父親(ちちおや)

ちちおや
noun
father
1. father
A male parent. A more formal or objective term than 父(ちち) or お父(とう)さん.
彼(かれ)の父親(ちちおや)は医者(いしゃ)だ。
His father is a doctor.
父親(ちちおや)として、子(こ)どもの将来(しょうらい)が心配(しんぱい)だ。
As a father, I worry about my children's future.
彼女(かのじょ)は父親(ちちおや)に似(に)ている。
She resembles her father.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 父親(ちちおや): Formal, objective term for 'father' used when discussing fathers in general or describing family relationships
  • 父(ちち): Humble term used to refer to one's own father when speaking to others
  • お父(とう)さん: Polite/familiar term used to address one's father or refer to someone else's father
  • パパ: Casual, childish term for 'daddy'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 父親(ちちおや)として (as a father)
  • 父親(ちちおや)になる (to become a father)
  • 父親(ちちおや)らしい (fatherly)

Related Words

Antonym: 母親(ははおや) (mother)
Related: 父(ちち) (father (humble))
