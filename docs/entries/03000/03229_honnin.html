本人(ほんにん)

ほんにん
noun
the person himself/herself

Definitions

1. the person himself/herself
The individual being discussed; the person in question.

Examples

本人(ほんにん)に直接(ちょくせつ)聞(き)いてください。
Please ask the person directly.
本人(ほんにん)確認(かくにん)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Identity verification is required.
本人確認 = identity verification
本人(ほんにん)は何(なに)も知(し)らないようだ。
The person himself seems to know nothing.

Notes

USAGE: 本人(ほんにん) emphasizes that you're referring to the actual person involved, not someone else on their behalf.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 本人(ほんにん)に (to the person himself)
  • 本人(ほんにん)から (from the person himself)
  • 本人(ほんにん)確認(かくにん) (identity verification)
  • 本人(ほんにん)の意思(いし) (the person's own will)
  • 本人(ほんにん)不在(ふざい) (the person not being present)

CONTRAST:

  • 本人(ほんにん) (the person himself)
  • 代理人(だいりにん) (representative, proxy)
