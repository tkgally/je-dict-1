泥(どろ)

どろ
noun
mud
1. mud, mire
Wet, soft earth. Also used in compounds and idioms related to being dirty or in difficult situations.
靴(くつ)に泥(どろ)がついた。
I got mud on my shoes.
子供(こども)が泥(どろ)んこで遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing in the mud.
泥んこ = muddy, mud play
雨(あめ)の後(あと)、道(みち)が泥(どろ)だらけになった。
After the rain, the road became covered in mud.
泥だらけ = covered in mud
服(ふく)が泥(どろ)で汚(よご)れてしまった。
My clothes got dirty with mud.
大雨(おおあめ)で川(かわ)の水(みず)が泥(どろ)で茶色(ちゃいろ)く濁(にご)っていた。
The river water was brown and muddy from the heavy rain.

USAGE: 泥(どろ) is used both literally for mud and in various compounds and expressions.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 泥棒(どろぼう) (thief, burglar)
  • 泥(どろ)だらけ (covered in mud)
  • 泥(どろ)んこ (muddy, playing in mud)
  • 泥沼(どろぬま) (quagmire, swamp; figuratively: messy situation)
  • 泥酔(でいすい) (dead drunk - different reading)
  • 泥試合(どろじあい) (mud-slinging, dirty competition)

IDIOMS:

  • 泥(どろ)を塗(ぬ)る (to disgrace, bring shame upon)
  • 泥(どろ)をかぶる (to take the blame, take responsibility)

RELATED:

  • 土(つち) (earth, soil - dry)
  • 泥(どろ)と土(つち): 泥(どろ) is wet and muddy, 土(つち) is dry soil/earth
