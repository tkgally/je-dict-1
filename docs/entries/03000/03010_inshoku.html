飲食(いんしょく)

いんしょく
noun
food and drink, eating and drinking

Definitions

1. food and drink, eating and drinking
The act of eating and drinking, or food and beverages together.

Examples

飲食(いんしょく)禁止(きんし)。
No food or drink allowed.
飲食店(いんしょくてん)。
Restaurant/eatery.
飲食(いんしょく)を楽(たの)しむ。
To enjoy food and drink.

Notes

飲食(いんしょく) combines 飲(いん) (drink) + 食(しょく) (eat).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 飲食店(いんしょくてん): restaurant, eatery
  • 飲食(いんしょく)禁止(きんし): no food or drink allowed
  • 飲食(いんしょく)業(ぎょう): food and beverage industry
  • 飲食(いんしょく)物(ぶつ): food and drink items

Used primarily in formal contexts, signs, and industry terminology. In everyday speech, 食(た)べたり飲(の)んだり is more common.
