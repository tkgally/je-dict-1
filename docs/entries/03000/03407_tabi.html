旅(たび)

たび
noun
travel, trip, journey
1. travel, trip, journey
The act of traveling to a destination, especially for pleasure or exploration. Often has a poetic or literary nuance compared to 旅行.
長(なが)い旅(たび)に出(で)る。
To set out on a long journey.
旅(たび)の途中(とちゅう)で素敵(すてき)な場所(ばしょ)を見(み)つけた。
I found a wonderful place during my travels.
人生(じんせい)は旅(たび)のようなものだ。
Life is like a journey.
Metaphorical use
旅(たび)の思(おも)い出(で)を写真(しゃしん)に残(のこ)す。
To preserve travel memories in photographs.
一人(ひとり)旅(たび)は自由(じゆう)だけど、時々(ときどき)寂(さび)しくなることもある。
Traveling alone is freeing, but sometimes you get lonely too.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旅(たび)に出(で)る (to set out on a journey)
  • 旅(たび)をする (to travel)
  • 旅(たび)の思(おも)い出(で) (travel memories)
  • 旅(たび)の疲(つか)れ (travel fatigue)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 旅(たび): Journey (poetic, literary, emphasizes the experience)
  • 旅行(りょこう): Trip, travel (general, practical term)
  • 旅路(たびじ): Journey, travels (literary)

PROVERB:

  • 旅(たび)の恥(はじ)は掻(か)き捨(す)て: What happens on the road stays on the road (One can be less inhibited when traveling)

Related Words

Related: 旅行(りょこう) (trip, travel)
Contrast: 度(たび) (occasion, each time (different kanji))
