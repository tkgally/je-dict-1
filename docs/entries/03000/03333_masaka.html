まさか

まさか
adverb
surely not, no way

Definitions

1. surely not, no way, by no means
Expresses disbelief or denial that something could be true or happen.
2. just in case, in the unlikely event
Used with の/に to mean 'in case of emergency' or 'for an unexpected situation.'

Examples

まさか彼(かれ)が犯人(はんにん)だとは思(おも)わなかった。
I never thought he could be the culprit.
Expresses surprise at an unexpected truth
「合格(ごうかく)したよ」「まさか！」
"I passed!" "No way!"
Used as an interjection expressing disbelief
まさかの時(とき)のために貯金(ちょきん)している。
I'm saving money for a rainy day (in case of emergency).
まさかの時 = emergency, unexpected situation

Notes

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • まさか〜ないだろう (surely...won't)
  • まさか〜とは思(おも)わなかった (never thought that...)
  • まさか！ (No way! - as an exclamation)
  • まさかの時(とき) (in case of emergency)
  • まさかの事態(じたい) (unexpected situation)

NUANCE:
まさか conveys strong doubt or disbelief. When something actually happens that you thought was impossible, you might say まさか本当だとは... (I never thought it was actually true...).
