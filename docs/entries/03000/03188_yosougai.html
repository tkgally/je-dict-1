予想外(よそうがい)

よそうがい
noun
unexpected, unforeseen
1. unexpected, unforeseen, beyond expectations
Something that was not anticipated or predicted; outside of what was expected.
予想外(よそうがい)の結果(けっか)だった。
It was an unexpected result.
予想外(よそうがい)に早(はや)く終(お)わった。
It ended unexpectedly quickly.
予想外(よそうがい)の展開(てんかい)に驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by the unexpected development.

COMPOUND: 予想(よそう) (expectation) + 外(がい) (outside)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 予想外(よそうがい)の + Noun (unexpected ~)
  • 予想外(よそうがい)に (unexpectedly)
  • 予想外(よそうがい)だ (it's unexpected)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 意外(いがい) (unexpected, surprising)
  • 思(おも)いがけない (unexpected)
  • 予期(よき)せぬ (unforeseen)

CAN BE POSITIVE OR NEGATIVE: 予想外(よそうがい) simply indicates deviation from expectations, regardless of whether the outcome is good or bad.

Related Words

Synonym: 意外(いがい)
Related: 予想(よそう)
