翌年(よくとし)

よくとし
noun
following year, next year

Definitions

1. following year, next year
The year after a referenced event or point in time; not necessarily the calendar year following the current one.

Examples

翌年(よくとし)、会社(かいしゃ)を辞(や)めた。
The following year, I quit the company.
彼女(かのじょ)は入学(にゅうがく)の翌年(よくとし)に留学(りゅうがく)した。
She studied abroad the year after entering school.
その翌年(よくとし)に結婚(けっこん)した。
They got married the year after that.

Notes

COMPOUND: 翌(よく) (next, following) + 年(とし) (year)

ALTERNATE READING: 翌年(よくねん) is also used.

DISTINCTION:

  • 翌年(よくとし)/翌年(よくねん): the year following a referenced event
  • 来年(らいねん): next year (from the current year)

EXAMPLE: If discussing events in 2020, 翌年(よくとし) would refer to 2021, regardless of the current year.

RELATED:

  • 前年(ぜんねん) (the previous year)

Related Words

Related: 来年(らいねん)
Antonym: 前年(ぜんねん)
