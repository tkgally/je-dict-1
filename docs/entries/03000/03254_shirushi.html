印(しるし)

しるし
noun
mark, sign, symbol

Definitions

1. mark, sign, symbol
A visible indication or symbol that represents something.
2. evidence, proof, token
Something that serves as evidence or a sign of something else.

Examples

地図(ちず)に印(しるし)をつけた。
I put a mark on the map.
これは私(わたし)の感謝(かんしゃ)の印(しるし)です。
This is a token of my gratitude.
赤(あか)い印(しるし)がある箇所(かしょ)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the places with red marks.

Notes

印(しるし) can refer to both physical marks and abstract symbols or tokens.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 印(しるし)をつける (to put a mark)
  • 感謝(かんしゃ)の印(しるし) (token of gratitude)
  • 愛(あい)の印(しるし) (sign of love)
  • 成功(せいこう)の印(しるし) (sign of success)

RELATED READINGS:

The kanji {印|} has multiple readings:

  • しるし (native Japanese reading)
  • いん (Sino-Japanese reading, used in compounds like 印象(いんしょう))
