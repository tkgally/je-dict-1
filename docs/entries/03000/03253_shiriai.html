知(し)り合(あ)い

しりあい
noun
acquaintance
1. acquaintance
A person one knows but who is not a close friend.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の古(ふる)い知(し)り合(あ)いです。
He is an old acquaintance of mine.
知(し)り合(あ)いに偶然(ぐうぜん)会(あ)った。
I ran into an acquaintance by chance.
知(し)り合(あ)いの紹介(しょうかい)で仕事(しごと)を見(み)つけた。
I found a job through an acquaintance's introduction.

知(し)り合(あ)い is derived from the verb 知(し)り合(あ)う (to get to know each other).

DISTINCTION FROM "FRIEND":

  • 知(し)り合(あ)い: acquaintance (someone you know)
  • 友達(ともだち)/友人(ゆうじん): friend (closer relationship)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 知(し)り合(あ)いになる (to become acquainted)
  • 知(し)り合(あ)いを通(とお)して (through an acquaintance)
  • 知(し)り合(あ)いの知(し)り合(あ)い (an acquaintance's acquaintance)

NOTE:
Using 知(し)り合(あ)い to describe someone implies a less close relationship than 友達(ともだち).
