渋滞(じゅうたい)

じゅうたい
noun
traffic jam, congestion
1. traffic jam, congestion, delay
A situation where traffic or other movement is slowed or stopped due to high volume.
高速(こうそく)道路(どうろ)が渋滞(じゅうたい)している。
The highway is congested.
渋滞(じゅうたい)で会議(かいぎ)に遅(おく)れた。
I was late to the meeting because of traffic.
連休(れんきゅう)は渋滞(じゅうたい)がひどい。
Traffic is terrible during consecutive holidays.

渋滞(じゅうたい)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to be congested."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 渋滞(じゅうたい)する (to be congested)
  • 渋滞(じゅうたい)に巻(ま)き込(こ)まれる (to get caught in traffic)
  • 交通(こうつう)渋滞(じゅうたい) (traffic congestion)
  • 渋滞(じゅうたい)情報(じょうほう) (traffic information)
  • 渋滞(じゅうたい)を避(さ)ける (to avoid traffic)
  • 10キロの渋滞(じゅうたい) (10 km traffic backup)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Traffic jams (渋滞(じゅうたい)) are notorious during お盆(ぼん) (mid-August) and 年末(ねんまつ)年始(ねんし) (New Year period) in Japan.
