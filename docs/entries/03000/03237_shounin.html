承認(しょうにん)

しょうにん
noun
approval, recognition
1. approval, authorization
Official agreement or permission for something to proceed.
上司(じょうし)の承認(しょうにん)を得(え)てから進(すす)めてください。
Please proceed after obtaining your supervisor's approval.
新薬(しんやく)の承認(しょうにん)には時間(じかん)がかかる。
It takes time to get approval for a new drug.
2. recognition, acknowledgment
Formal acceptance of something as valid or legitimate.
国連(こくれん)は新(あたら)しい政府(せいふ)を承認(しょうにん)した。
The United Nations recognized the new government.

承認(しょうにん)する is commonly used as a する-verb in business and official contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 承認(しょうにん)を得(え)る (to obtain approval)
  • 承認(しょうにん)を求(もと)める (to seek approval)
  • 承認(しょうにん)手続(てつづ)き (approval process)
  • 事前(じぜん)承認(しょうにん) (prior approval)

HOMOPHONE NOTE:
承認(しょうにん) (approval) vs 商人(しょうにん) (merchant) - same reading, different meaning and kanji.
