〜だけ

だけ
particle
only, just, as much as
1. only, just
Limiting particle indicating exclusivity. Shows that something is limited to what is mentioned and nothing more.
水(みず)だけ飲(の)みました。
I only drank water.
Sense 1: limiting
私(わたし)だけが知(し)っている。
Only I know.
だけ + が for emphasis
一人(ひとり)だけで行(い)った。
I went alone (just by myself).
Sense 1: only
今日(きょう)だけ特別(とくべつ)です。
Only today is special.
Sense 1: only
見(み)るだけです。
I'm just looking.
Sense 1: just
2. as much as, to the extent
Indicates the full extent or amount of something, often translated as 'as much as one wants' or 'to the extent possible.'
好(す)きなだけ食(た)べてください。
Please eat as much as you like.
Sense 2: extent
できるだけ早(はや)く来(き)てください。
Please come as soon as possible.
できるだけ = as much as possible
欲(ほ)しいだけ取(と)ってください。
Please take as much as you want.
Sense 2: as much as
言(い)いたいだけ言(い)った。
I said as much as I wanted to say.
Sense 2: as much as
走(はし)れるだけ走(はし)った。
I ran as much as I could.
Sense 2: as much as

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • Noun + だけ: 水(みず)だけ (only water)
  • Verb (dictionary) + だけ: 見(み)るだけ (just look)
  • Verb (た form) + だけ: 食(た)べただけ (only ate)
  • い-adjective + だけ: 高(たか)いだけ (only expensive)
  • な-adjective + な + だけ: 静(しず)かなだけ (only quiet)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • できるだけ (as much as possible)
  • 好(す)きなだけ (as much as one likes)
  • これだけ (only this, just this much)
  • それだけ (only that, just that much)
  • 〜だけでなく (not only ~)

CONTRAST WITH しか: だけ is neutral; しか + negative emphasizes insufficiency or disappointment. 三(さん)人(にん)だけ来(き)た (only 3 came - neutral) vs. 三(さん)人(にん)しか来(こ)なかった (only 3 came - implying disappointment).

Related Words

Contrast: しか (requires negative)
