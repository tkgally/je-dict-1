脇(わき)

わき
noun
side, armpit

Definitions

1. side, beside
The area next to or beside something; the side of something.
2. armpit
The hollow area under the arm where it joins the body.

Examples

道(みち)の脇(わき)に車(くるま)を止(と)めた。
I parked the car at the side of the road.
脇(わき)に抱(かか)えて運(はこ)んだ。
I carried it tucked under my arm.
脇に抱える = to carry under one's arm
脇役(わきやく)だが重要(じゅうよう)な役(やく)だ。
It's a supporting role, but an important one.
脇役 = supporting role/actor

Notes

USAGE: 脇(わき) refers to the side of something or the armpit area. Often used in compounds.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 脇(わき)に (beside, at the side of)
  • 脇(わき)を通(とお)る (to pass by the side)
  • 脇役(わきやく) (supporting role)
  • 脇道(わきみち) (side road)
  • 脇見(わきみ) (looking away, distraction)

BODY PART: When referring to the armpit, 脇(わき)の下(した) is more specific.
