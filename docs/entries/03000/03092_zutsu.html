〜ずつ

ずつ
suffix
each, at a time, apiece
1. each, at a time
Suffix attached to quantity expressions to indicate equal distribution or gradual progression. Shows that something is done in equal portions or increments.
一(ひと)つずつ取(と)ってください。
Please take one each.
少(すこ)しずつ日本語(にほんご)が分(わ)かるようになった。
I gradually came to understand Japanese little by little.
三(さん)人(にん)ずつのグループを作(つく)ってください。
Please form groups of three people each.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • Number + counter + ずつ: 一(ひと)つずつ (one each), 二(ふた)つずつ (two each)
  • 少(すこ)しずつ (little by little, gradually)
  • 一歩(いっぽ)ずつ (step by step)
  • 一日(いちにち)ずつ (day by day)

MEANINGS:
1. Equal distribution: 一(いっ)個(こ)ずつ配(くば)る (distribute one each)
2. Gradual progression: 少(すこ)しずつ上手(じょうず)になる (get better little by little)

The suffix attaches directly to the quantity word without particles.
