裸(はだか)

はだか
noun / na-adjective
naked, nude, bare

Definitions

1. naked, nude
Without clothes; unclothed.
2. bare, exposed
Without covering or protection; figuratively, without pretense.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんが裸(はだか)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The baby is playing naked.
裸(はだか)の付(つ)き合(あ)いで友情(ゆうじょう)を深(ふか)めた。
We deepened our friendship through frank, open interaction.
裸の付き合い literally 'naked association' means honest, open relationship
裸(はだか)の木(き)が冬(ふゆ)の空(そら)に立(た)っている。
Bare trees stand against the winter sky.

Notes

Can be used literally (without clothes) or figuratively (exposed, honest, bare).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 裸(はだか)になる (to become naked, to strip down)
  • 裸(はだか)の付(つ)き合(あ)い (frank, heart-to-heart relationship)
  • 裸(はだか)一貫(かん) (starting with nothing but oneself)
  • 裸(はだか)の王様(おうさま) (The Emperor's New Clothes)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 裸足(はだし) (barefoot)
  • 裸眼(らがん) (naked eye, without glasses)
  • 素裸(すっぱだか) (stark naked - emphatic)
