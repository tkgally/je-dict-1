節(ふし)

ふし
noun
joint, knot; tune, melody

Definitions

1. joint, knot, node
A point where segments connect, such as bamboo joints or finger joints.
2. tune, melody
A musical melody or the way something is sung.
3. point, moment, occasion
A particular instance or aspect of something.

Examples

竹(たけ)の節(ふし)が見(み)える。
You can see the joints of the bamboo.
昔(むかし)ながらの節(ふし)で歌(うた)う。
To sing in an old-fashioned melody.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)には不審(ふしん)な節(ふし)がある。
There are suspicious aspects to his story.

Notes

Multiple meanings centered around points of connection or division.

MEANING 1 - JOINTS/KNOTS:

  • 竹(たけ)の節(ふし) (bamboo joint)
  • 指(ゆび)の節(ふし) (finger joint/knuckle)
  • 木(き)の節(ふし) (knot in wood)

MEANING 2 - MELODY:

  • 節(ふし)をつける (to set to a tune)
  • 節回(ふしまわ)し (way of singing, melody style)

MEANING 3 - POINT/ASPECT:

  • 〜節(ふし)がある (there is an aspect of ~)
  • 不審(ふしん)な節(ふし) (suspicious points)

HOMOPHONE (different reading):

  • 節(せつ) - section, clause; season
