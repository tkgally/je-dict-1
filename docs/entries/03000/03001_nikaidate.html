二階建(にかいだ)て

にかいだて
noun
two-story (building)

Definitions

1. two-story (building)
A building with two floors.

Examples

二階建(にかいだ)ての家(いえ)。
A two-story house.
二階建(にかいだ)てのバス。
A double-decker bus.
この建物(たてもの)は二階建(にかいだ)てだ。
This building is two stories.

Notes

二階建(にかいだ)て combines 二階(にかい) (second floor) + 建(た)て (built structure).

PATTERN:
[Number]階建(かいだ)て describes how many stories a building has.

EXAMPLES:

  • 一階建(いっかいだ)て: one-story
  • 二階建(にかいだ)て: two-story
  • 三階建(さんかいだ)て: three-story
  • 高層(こうそう)ビル: high-rise building

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 二階建(にかいだ)ての家(いえ) (two-story house)
  • 二階建(にかいだ)てバス (double-decker bus)
