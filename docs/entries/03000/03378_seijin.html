成人(せいじん)

せいじん
noun
adult, coming of age
1. adult
A person who has reached the age of majority.
成人(せいじん)向(む)けの映画(えいが)だ。
This is a movie for adults.
2. coming of age
The process or event of reaching adulthood.
成人(せいじん)式(しき)に参加(さんか)した。
I attended the coming-of-age ceremony.
日本(にほん)では十八歳(じゅうはっさい)で成人(せいじん)になる。
In Japan, you become an adult at 18.

成人(せいじん) refers to adults and the process of becoming an adult.

CULTURAL NOTE:
成人(せいじん)の日(ひ) (Coming of Age Day) is a national holiday in Japan held on the second Monday of January. Young people who turned 20 (or now 18, after the 2022 law change) during the year celebrate with ceremonies and often wear traditional clothing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 成人(せいじん)式(しき) (coming-of-age ceremony)
  • 成人(せいじん)の日(ひ) (Coming of Age Day)
  • 成人(せいじん)向(む)け (for adults)
  • 成人(せいじん)病(びょう) (adult diseases/lifestyle diseases)
  • 成人(せいじん)年齢(ねんれい) (age of majority)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 成人(せいじん): adult (legal/formal sense)
  • 大人(おとな): adult (general, everyday)
  • 成年(せいねん): age of majority (more legal/formal)
