場(ば)

ば
noun / suffix
place, scene, occasion

Definitions

1. place, location
A physical location or space.
2. scene, situation
A particular moment or situation.
3. field (physics)
A region in which a force operates.

Examples

場(ば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)を読(よ)む。
To read the atmosphere of the situation.
Common expression about social awareness
その場(ば)で決(き)めましょう。
Let's decide on the spot.
場(ば)を盛(も)り上(あ)げる。
To liven up the atmosphere.

Notes

Used independently or as a suffix. Often relates to social situations and atmosphere.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 場(ば)を読(よ)む (to read the room/situation)
  • その場(ば)で (on the spot)
  • 場(ば)を盛(も)り上(あ)げる (to liven things up)
  • 場(ば)が白(しら)ける (the mood becomes awkward)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 場所(ばしょ) (place, location)
  • 場面(ばめん) (scene)
  • 場合(ばあい) (case, situation)
  • 現場(げんば) (actual site, scene)
  • 磁場(じば) (magnetic field)

NOTE:

  • When read as 場(ば), often refers to social/situational context
  • In compounds, both ば and じょう readings occur
