幅(はば)

はば
noun
width, breadth

Definitions

1. width, breadth
The horizontal measurement of something from side to side.
2. range, scope, margin
The extent or variety of something; room for variation.

Examples

この道(みち)は幅(はば)が狭(せま)い。
This road is narrow (has little width).
幅(はば)広(ひろ)い知識(ちしき)を持(も)っている。
They have a wide range of knowledge.
幅広い = wide-ranging
値下(ねさ)げ幅(はば)は10%だ。
The price reduction margin is 10%.
〜幅 = margin of ~

Notes

USAGE: 幅(はば) refers to physical width and figurative breadth/range.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 幅(はば)が広(ひろ)い／狭(せま)い (wide/narrow)
  • 幅(はば)を広(ひろ)げる (to broaden)
  • 幅(はば)広(ひろ)く (broadly, widely)
  • 〜の幅(はば) (the width of ~, the range of ~)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 幅(はば)広(ひろ)い (wide, broad)
  • 振(ふ)り幅(はば) (range of movement, swing width)
  • 値(ね)幅(はば) (price range)
  • 上(じょう)げ幅(はば) (margin of increase/decrease)

DIMENSIONS:

  • 幅(はば) - width
  • 長(なが)さ - length
  • 高(たか)さ - height
  • 深(ふか)さ - depth
