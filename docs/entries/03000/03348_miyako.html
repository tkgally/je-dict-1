都(みやこ)

みやこ
noun
capital (city), metropolis

Definitions

1. capital city
The seat of government, especially historically.
2. metropolis, large city
A major urban center, especially one known for culture or sophistication.

Examples

京都(きょうと)は昔(むかし)の都(みやこ)だった。
Kyoto was the former capital.
花(はな)の都(みやこ)パリ。
Paris, the city of flowers.
Poetic expression for a beautiful city
都(みやこ)を離(はな)れて田舎(いなか)に住(す)む。
To leave the city and live in the countryside.

Notes

A somewhat literary or classical word for capital city.

HISTORICAL CAPITALS OF JAPAN:

  • 奈良(なら) (710-784)
  • 京都(きょうと) (794-1868)
  • 東京(とうきょう) (1868-present)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 花(はな)の都(みやこ) (beautiful city, city of flowers)
  • 古(ふる)の都(みやこ)/古都(こと) (ancient capital)
  • 都(みやこ)落(お)ち (fleeing the capital)

RELATED READINGS:

  • 都(みやこ) - capital (native reading)
  • 都(と) - metropolis (as in 東京都(とうきょうと))
  • 首都(しゅと) - capital city (modern term)
