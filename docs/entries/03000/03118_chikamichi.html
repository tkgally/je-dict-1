近道(ちかみち)

ちかみち
noun
shortcut, shorter way

Definitions

1. shortcut, shorter way
A route that is shorter or quicker than the usual path. Can also be used figuratively for a faster method to achieve something.

Examples

近道(ちかみち)を通(とお)って学校(がっこう)に行(い)った。
I took a shortcut to school.
この道(みち)は近道(ちかみち)ですか。
Is this road a shortcut?
成功(せいこう)への近道(ちかみち)はない。
There is no shortcut to success.
Figurative use

Notes

COMPOUND: 近(ちか)い (near, close) + 道(みち) (road, path)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 近道(ちかみち)を通(とお)る (take a shortcut)
  • 近道(ちかみち)をする (take a shortcut)
  • 〜への近道(ちかみち) (shortcut to ~)

ANTONYM: 遠回(とおまわ)り (detour, roundabout way)

The word can be used both literally (for physical routes) and figuratively (for methods or approaches).

Antonym: 遠回(とおまわ)り
