仲間(なかま)

なかま
noun
companion, fellow
1. companion, comrade, fellow
People who belong to the same group, share the same interests, or work together. Implies a sense of camaraderie and belonging.
彼(かれ)は信頼(しんらい)できる仲間(なかま)だ。
He is a trustworthy companion.
仲間(なかま)と一緒(いっしょ)に旅行(りょこう)に行(い)った。
I went on a trip with my friends/companions.
仲間(なかま)を裏切(うらぎ)ることはできない。
I can't betray my companions.
Sense 1: companion, comrade
仲間(なかま)と協力(きょうりょく)して問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)した。
I solved the problem by cooperating with my teammates.
Sense 1: fellow, teammate
仲間(なかま)外(はず)れにされて悲(かな)しかった。
I was sad to be left out of the group.
Sense 1: 仲間外れ = being excluded
新(あたら)しい仲間(なかま)がチーム}に{加(くわ)わった。
A new member joined the team.
Sense 1: fellow member
2. member of a group, category
Things that belong to the same category or type. Used for classification.
イルカは鯨(くじら)の仲間(なかま)です。
Dolphins are in the whale family (same category as whales).
〜の仲間 = belonging to the same category
トマトは野菜(やさい)の仲間(なかま)だ。
Tomatoes belong to the vegetable category.
Sense 2: category
この植物(しょくぶつ)はバラの仲間(なかま)です。
This plant belongs to the rose family.
Sense 2: member of a group/category
ペンギンは鳥(とり)の仲間(なかま)だ。
Penguins belong to the bird family.
Sense 2: category

USAGE: 仲間(なかま) implies a stronger bond than just 'friend'. It suggests people who share experiences, goals, or a sense of belonging together.

NUANCES:

  • Implies solidarity and loyalty
  • Often used for teammates, colleagues, or fellow group members
  • Can indicate being 'one of us'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 仲間(なかま)に入(い)る (to join a group)
  • 仲間(なかま)に入(い)れる (to include in the group)
  • 仲間(なかま)外(はず)れ (being left out, ostracism)
  • 仲間(なかま)意識(いしき) (sense of belonging)
  • 〜の仲間(なかま) (belonging to the ~ category/group)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 友達(ともだち) - friend (general)
  • 同僚(どうりょう) - colleague (work)
  • 仲良(なかよ)し - close friend
  • 同士(どうし) - fellow (suffix)
