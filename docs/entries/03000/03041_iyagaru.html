嫌(いや)がる

いやがる
verb (godan)
to dislike, to be unwilling

Definitions

1. to dislike, to hate
To show signs of disliking something; to express unwillingness or reluctance. Used when describing someone else's feelings of aversion.

Examples

子供(こども)が野菜(やさい)を嫌(いや)がる。
The child dislikes vegetables.
猫(ねこ)は水(みず)を嫌(いや)がることが多(おお)い。
Cats often dislike water.
彼(かれ)は人前(ひとまえ)で話(はな)すのを嫌(いや)がっている。
He is reluctant to speak in front of people.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを嫌(いや)がる.

GRAMMAR POINT:
嫌(いや)がる is formed from 嫌(いや) (disliked) + がる (to show signs of). The がる suffix indicates observable behavior showing an internal feeling, used when describing others' feelings (not your own).

ASPECT: 嫌(いや)がっている indicates ongoing reluctance or aversion.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 嫌(いや)がる: to show dislike (observable behavior)
  • 嫌(きら)う: to dislike (internal feeling, can be used for self)
  • 嫌(いや)だ: to dislike (direct statement of own feelings)

Related Words

Related: 嫌(きら)う (to dislike)
