違(ちが)い

ちがい
noun
difference

Definitions

1. difference, discrepancy
The state of being different or not the same. The noun form of the verb 違(ちが)う (to differ). Used to describe gaps or distinctions between things.

Examples

二(ふた)つの製品(せいひん)の違(ちが)いがわからない。
I can't tell the difference between the two products.
文化(ぶんか)の違(ちが)いを理解(りかい)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to understand cultural differences.
考(かんが)え方(かた)の違(ちが)いで意見(いけん)が合(あ)わなかった。
We couldn't agree due to differences in our ways of thinking.

Notes

USAGE: This is the noun form derived from the verb 違(ちが)う. It emphasizes the state or degree of being different.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の違(ちが)い (the difference in/of ~)
  • 違(ちが)いがある (there is a difference)
  • 違(ちが)いがわかる (to understand the difference)
  • 大(おお)きな違(ちが)い (big difference)
  • 個人(こじん)差(さ)/違(ちが)い (individual differences)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 差(さ) - emphasizes the gap or margin between things (often numerical)
  • 相違(そうい) - formal word for difference/discrepancy
  • 区別(くべつ) - distinction, differentiation (the act of distinguishing)
