中央(ちゅうおう)

ちゅうおう
noun, no-adjective
center, middle, central
1. center, middle
The middle point or part of something.
テーブルの中央(ちゅうおう)に花(はな)を置(お)いた。
I placed flowers in the center of the table.
広場(ひろば)の中央(ちゅうおう)に噴水(ふんすい)がある。
There is a fountain in the center of the plaza.
2. central (government, authority)
Relating to the main governing body or headquarters, as opposed to local or regional entities.
中央(ちゅうおう)政府(せいふ)と地方(ちほう)自治体(じちたい)の関係(かんけい)が議論(ぎろん)されている。
The relationship between the central government and local governments is being discussed.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中央(ちゅうおう): Center (formal, often used for institutions, official contexts)
  • 真(ま)ん中(なか): Center, middle (casual, everyday usage)
  • 中心(ちゅうしん): Core, center, heart (figurative center of importance)

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 中央(ちゅうおう)政府(せいふ) (central government)
  • 中央(ちゅうおう)線(せん) (Chuo Line - major train line in Tokyo)
  • 中央(ちゅうおう)銀行(ぎんこう) (central bank)
