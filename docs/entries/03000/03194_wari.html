割(わり)

わり
noun
rate, ratio, proportion, 10%

Definitions

1. rate, ratio, proportion
A comparative measure or percentage expressing the relationship between quantities.
2. ten percent
As a counter, 割(わり) represents 10% of a whole. 1割(わり) = 10%.

Examples

二割引(にわりびき)になっている。
It's 20% off.
割(わり) as 10%
成功(せいこう)の割合(わりあい)は高(たか)い。
The success rate is high.
割(わり)と簡単(かんたん)だった。
It was relatively easy.
Adverb: relatively

Notes

MULTIPLE USES:

1. As a unit (10%):

  • 1割(わり) = 10%
  • 二割(にわり) = 20%
  • 二割引(にわりびき) = 20% discount

2. In compounds:

  • 割合(わりあい) (ratio, proportion, rate)
  • 割引(わりびき) (discount)
  • 割高(わりだか) (relatively expensive)
  • 割安(わりやす) (relatively cheap)

3. As adverb (割(わり)と/割(わり)に):

  • 割(わり)と簡単(かんたん) (relatively easy)
  • 割(わり)に高(たか)い (relatively expensive)

BASEBALL: Batting average is expressed in 割(わり), 分(ぶ), 厘(りん) (e.g., 3割(わり)2分(ぶ) = .320)
