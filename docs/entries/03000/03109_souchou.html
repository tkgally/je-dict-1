早朝(そうちょう)

そうちょう
noun
early morning

Definitions

1. early morning
The very early hours of the morning, typically from dawn until around 7 or 8 AM. More formal and specific than 朝(あさ).

Examples

早朝(そうちょう)から仕事(しごと)を始(はじ)めた。
I started work from early morning.
早朝(そうちょう)のジョギングは気持(きも)ちがいい。
Jogging in the early morning feels good.
早朝(そうちょう)便(びん)で出発(しゅっぱつ)する。
I'll depart on the early morning flight.

Notes

REGISTER: More formal/written than 朝(あさ)早く(はやく).

TIME OF DAY EXPRESSIONS:

  • 早朝(そうちょう) (early morning, ~5-8 AM)
  • 朝(あさ) (morning)
  • 午前(ごぜん) (AM, before noon)
  • 昼(ひる) (noon, daytime)
  • 午後(ごご) (PM, afternoon)
  • 夕方(ゆうがた) (evening)
  • 夜(よる) (night)
  • 深夜(しんや) (late night)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早朝(そうちょう)から (from early morning)
  • 早朝(そうちょう)に (in the early morning)
  • 早朝(そうちょう)便(びん) (early morning flight)
