診察(しんさつ)

しんさつ
noun
medical examination
1. medical examination, consultation
The examination of a patient by a doctor to diagnose illness.
午前(ごぜん)中(ちゅう)に診察(しんさつ)の予約(よやく)を取(と)った。
I made an appointment for a morning examination.
先生(せんせい)が丁寧(ていねい)に診察(しんさつ)してくださった。
The doctor examined me carefully.
診察(しんさつ)室(しつ)でお待(ま)ちください。
Please wait in the examination room.

診察(しんさつ)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to examine (a patient)."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 診察(しんさつ)を受(う)ける (to receive an examination)
  • 診察(しんさつ)室(しつ) (examination room)
  • 診察(しんさつ)時間(じかん) (consultation hours)
  • 診察(しんさつ)券(けん) (patient registration card)
  • 診察(しんさつ)料(りょう) (consultation fee)

RELATED MEDICAL TERMS:

  • 初診(しょしん) (first visit/examination)
  • 再診(さいしん) (follow-up visit)
  • 問診(もんしん) (medical interview)
