名(な)

な
noun
name, reputation

Definitions

1. name
A word or set of words by which a person, place, or thing is known. More literary than 名前(なまえ).
2. reputation, fame
One's standing or repute among others. Often used in expressions about fame or honor.

Examples

名(な)を呼(よ)ばれて返事(へんじ)をした。
I responded when my name was called.
彼(かれ)は世界(せかい)に名(な)を知(し)られている。
He is known throughout the world.
名を知られる = to be famous
名(な)もない花(はな)が咲(さ)いている。
Unknown flowers are blooming.
名もない = nameless, unknown

Notes

USAGE: 名(な) is more literary and formal than 名前(なまえ). It's commonly used in set expressions and idioms about names and reputation.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 名(な)を挙(あ)げる (to make a name for oneself)
  • 名(な)を知(し)られる (to be known, famous)
  • 名(な)を残(のこ)す (to leave one's mark)
  • 名(な)もない (nameless, unknown)
  • 名(な)ばかり (in name only)
  • 名(な)を借(か)りる (to borrow someone's name)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 名前(なまえ) (name - common word)
  • 氏名(しめい) (full name - formal)
  • 名字(みょうじ)/苗字(みょうじ) (surname)
  • 名刺(めいし) (business card)
  • 名詞(めいし) (noun - grammar)
  • 有名(ゆうめい) (famous)
  • 無名(むめい) (unknown)

CONTRAST:

  • 名(な) - literary, used in expressions
  • 名前(なまえ) - everyday word for 'name'
