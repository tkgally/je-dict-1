便(たよ)り

たより
noun
news, letter, correspondence
1. news, word, tidings
Information or updates from someone, especially someone far away.
息子(むすこ)から便(たよ)りがあった。
I received word from my son.
Sense 1: news
便(たよ)りがないのは良(よ)い便(たよ)り。
No news is good news.
Proverb
2. letter, correspondence
Written communication, especially a personal letter.
友人(ゆうじん)に便(たよ)りを書(か)く。
To write a letter to a friend.
Sense 2: letter

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 便(たよ)りがある (to receive news/a letter)
  • 便(たよ)りがない (no news, no word)
  • 便(たよ)りを出(だ)す (to send a letter/news)

PROVERB:

  • 便(たよ)りがないのは良(よ)い便(たよ)り: No news is good news

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 便(たよ)り: News/letter (slightly literary/traditional)
  • 手紙(てがみ): Letter (most common word)
  • 連絡(れんらく): Contact, communication (modern/practical)

NOTE: Though somewhat old-fashioned, 便(たよ)り is still used especially when referring to news from people far away or occasional correspondence.
