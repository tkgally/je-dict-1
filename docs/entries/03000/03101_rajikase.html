ラジカセ

らじかせ
noun
radio cassette player
1. radio cassette player, boombox
A portable device combining a radio and cassette tape player/recorder. Short for ラジオカセット. While the technology is outdated, the word appears in older learning materials.
ラジカセで英語(えいご)のテープ(てーぷ)を聞(き)いた。
I listened to English tapes on a radio cassette player.
昔(むかし)はラジカセが人気(にんき)だった。
Radio cassette players used to be popular.

ABBREVIATION: ラジオカセット → ラジカセ

LOANWORD combining "radio" + "cassette."

Historical term for a portable audio device popular in the 1980s-90s. Now largely replaced by digital audio players and smartphones. Appears in older JLPT N5 vocabulary lists.
