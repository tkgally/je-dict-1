平日(へいじつ)

へいじつ
noun
weekday

Definitions

1. weekday
Days from Monday to Friday, working days.

Examples

平日(へいじつ)は仕事(しごと)。
I work on weekdays.
平日(へいじつ)の午後(ごご)。
Weekday afternoon.
平日(へいじつ)だけ営業(えいぎょう)。
Open only on weekdays.

Notes

平日(へいじつ) refers to ordinary weekdays (Monday through Friday).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 平日(へいじつ)のみ (weekdays only)
  • 平日(へいじつ)料金(りょうきん) (weekday rate)
  • 平日(へいじつ)ダイヤ (weekday schedule)

CONTRAST:

  • 平日(へいじつ): weekday
  • 休日(きゅうじつ): holiday/day off
  • 祝日(しゅくじつ): national holiday
  • 週末(しゅうまつ): weekend
