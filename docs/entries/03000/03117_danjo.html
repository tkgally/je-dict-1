男女(だんじょ)

だんじょ
noun
men and women, both genders
1. men and women, both genders
A compound referring to both males and females collectively.
男女(だんじょ)平等(びょうどう)は大切(たいせつ)だ。
Gender equality is important.
男女(だんじょ)合(あ)わせて百(ひゃく)人(にん)が参加(さんか)した。
A total of one hundred people, both men and women, participated.
男女(だんじょ)別(べつ)のトイレがある。
There are separate toilets for men and women.
この学校(がっこう)は男女(だんじょ)共学(きょうがく)です。
This school is co-educational.
男女(だんじょ)を問(と)わず、誰(だれ)でも参加(さんか)できるイベントが来月(らいげつ)開(ひら)かれる。
An event that anyone can participate in, regardless of gender, will be held next month.

COMPOUND: 男(だん) (male) + 女(じょ) (female)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 男女(だんじょ)平等(びょうどう) (gender equality)
  • 男女(だんじょ)差別(さべつ) (gender discrimination)
  • 男女(だんじょ)共同(きょうどう)参画(さんかく) (gender equality participation)
  • 男女(だんじょ)別(べつ) (separated by gender)
  • 男女(だんじょ)混合(こんごう) (mixed gender, co-ed)

NOTE: When referring to 'male and female' in contexts like animals, 雄(おす) (male) and 雌(めす) (female) are used instead.
