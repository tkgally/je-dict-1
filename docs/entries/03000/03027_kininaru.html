気(き)になる

きになる
verb
to be on one's mind, to worry about
1. to be on one's mind, to worry about
To be concerned about or interested in something.
結果(けっか)が気(き)になる。
I'm worried about the results.
あの人(ひと)が気(き)になる。
I'm interested in that person.
気(き)になることがある。
There's something on my mind.

気(き)になる is an idiomatic expression meaning to be on one's mind or to bother someone.

MEANINGS:
1. To worry about: 結果(けっか)が気(き)になる (worried about results)
2. To be interested in: あの人(ひと)が気(き)になる (interested in that person)
3. To be bothered by: 音(おと)が気(き)になる (bothered by a sound)

CONTRAST WITH 気(き)にする:

  • 気(き)になる: something weighs on your mind (unintentional)
  • 気(き)にする: to actively worry about or care about (intentional)

Note: The subject is the thing that concerns you, marked with が.
