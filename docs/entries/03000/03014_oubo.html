応募(おうぼ)

おうぼ
noun
application, entry
1. application, entry
To apply or enter for something, such as a job or contest.
求人(きゅうじん)に応募(おうぼ)する。
To apply for a job.
応募(おうぼ)締(し)め切(き)り。
Application deadline.
応募者(おうぼしゃ)が多(おお)い。
There are many applicants.

応募(おうぼ) is a noun/suru-verb meaning to apply or enter (for jobs, contests, etc.).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 応募(おうぼ)する (to apply)
  • 応募(おうぼ)締(し)め切(き)り (application deadline)
  • 応募(おうぼ)用紙(ようし) (application form)
  • 応募(おうぼ)資格(しかく) (eligibility requirements)
  • 応募者(おうぼしゃ) (applicant)

DISTINCTION:

  • 応募(おうぼ): applying for jobs, contests, programs
  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み: general application/registration
