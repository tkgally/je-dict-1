募集(ぼしゅう)

ぼしゅう
noun
recruitment, taking applications

Definitions

1. recruitment, taking applications
To seek or invite applicants for a position or event.

Examples

参加者(さんかしゃ)を募集(ぼしゅう)する。
To recruit participants.
アルバイト(あるばいと)募集(ぼしゅう)。
Part-time job opening.
募集(ぼしゅう)中(ちゅう)。
Now recruiting.

Notes

募集(ぼしゅう) is a noun/suru-verb meaning to recruit or solicit applications.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 募集(ぼしゅう)する (to recruit)
  • 募集(ぼしゅう)中(ちゅう) (now recruiting)
  • 募集(ぼしゅう)要項(ようこう) (recruitment guidelines)
  • アルバイト募集(ぼしゅう) (part-time job opening)
  • 参加者(さんかしゃ)募集(ぼしゅう) (recruiting participants)

DISTINCTION:

  • 募集(ぼしゅう): seeking applicants (employer side)
  • 応募(おうぼ): applying (applicant side)
