信用(しんよう)

しんよう
noun
trust, credit
1. trust, confidence, belief
Firm belief in the reliability or ability of someone or something.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)は信用(しんよう)できる。
His words can be trusted.
一度(いちど)失(うしな)った信用(しんよう)を取(と)り戻(もど)すのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to regain trust once it's lost.
2. credit (financial)
The ability to obtain goods or services before payment based on trust that payment will be made in the future.
信用(しんよう)カードで支払(しはら)った。
I paid with a credit card.

信用(しんよう)する is used as a する-verb meaning "to trust."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 信用(しんよう)する (to trust)
  • 信用(しんよう)できる (trustworthy)
  • 信用(しんよう)を失(うしな)う (to lose trust)
  • 信用(しんよう)を得(え)る (to gain trust)
  • 信用(しんよう)カード (credit card)
  • 信用(しんよう)金庫(きんこ) (credit union)

COMPARISON:

  • 信用(しんよう): trust (often practical, ability-based)
  • 信頼(しんらい): trust (deeper, character-based)
