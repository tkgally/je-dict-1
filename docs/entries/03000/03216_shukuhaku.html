宿泊(しゅくはく)

しゅくはく
noun, suru-verb
lodging, accommodation

Definitions

1. lodging, accommodation, staying overnight
The act of staying somewhere overnight, typically at a hotel or inn.

Examples

京都(きょうと)で一泊(いっぱく)宿泊(しゅくはく)した。
I stayed one night in Kyoto.
宿泊(しゅくはく)料金(りょうきん)は朝食(ちょうしょく)込(こ)みで8,000円(えん)だ。
The accommodation fee is 8,000 yen including breakfast.
旅行(りょこう)サイトで宿泊(しゅくはく)先(さき)を探(さが)している。口(くち)コミの評価(ひょうか)が高(たか)いところを選(えら)びたい。
I'm looking for accommodations on a travel website. I want to choose a place with high review ratings.

Notes

宿泊(しゅくはく) refers to lodging or staying overnight at a hotel, inn, etc.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)する (to stay overnight, to lodge)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)を予約(よやく)する (to book accommodation)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)料金(りょうきん) / 宿泊(しゅくはく)費(ひ) (accommodation fee)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)施設(しせつ) (lodging facility)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)先(さき) (place to stay)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)客(きゃく) (hotel guest)
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)予約(よやく) (accommodation reservation)
  • 素泊(すどま)り宿泊(しゅくはく) (room only, no meals)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 宿泊(しゅくはく): lodging (formal term)
  • 泊(と)まる: to stay (casual verb)
  • 宿(やど): lodging, inn (traditional word)
