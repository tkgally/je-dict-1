共(とも)に

ともに
adverb
together, both
1. together with, along with
Indicates doing something together with someone or something. More formal than 一緒(いっしょ)に.
2. both, as well as
Indicates that something applies to both parties or items mentioned.
3. as, at the same time as
Used in the pattern 〜と共(とも)に to mean 'as ~ happens' or 'along with ~'.
家族(かぞく)と共(とも)に過(す)ごす時間(じかん)を大切(たいせつ)にしている。
I value the time I spend with my family.
〜と共に = together with ~
苦(くる)しみも喜(よろこ)びも共(とも)に分(わ)かち合(あ)う。
We share both hardships and joys together.
時代(じだい)と共(とも)に変(か)わっていく。
It changes along with the times.
〜と共に = along with ~, as ~ changes

USAGE: 共(とも)に is more formal and literary than 一緒(いっしょ)に. It's commonly used in written Japanese and formal speech.

PATTERNS:

  • 〜と共(とも)に (together with ~)
  • AもBも共(とも)に (both A and B)
  • 〜と共(とも)に (as ~, along with ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 共(とも)に生(い)きる (to live together)
  • 共(とも)に働(はたら)く (to work together)
  • 共(とも)に学(まな)ぶ (to learn together)
  • 苦楽(くらく)を共(とも)にする (to share joys and sorrows)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一緒(いっしょ)に - together (casual)
  • 連(つ)れて - taking along
  • と同時(どうじ)に - at the same time as
