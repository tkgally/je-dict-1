裏側(うらがわ)

うらがわ
noun
back side, reverse side
1. back side, reverse side
The opposite side from the front; the hidden or less visible side of something.
2. behind the scenes
The hidden aspects or background of a situation; what goes on out of public view.
紙(かみ)の裏側(うらがわ)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name on the back of the paper.
建物(たてもの)の裏側(うらがわ)に駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)がある。
There's a parking lot behind the building.
この事件(じけん)の裏側(うらがわ)には複雑(ふくざつ)な事情(じじょう)がある。
There are complicated circumstances behind this incident.
Figurative usage

裏側(うらがわ) combines 裏(うら) (back, reverse) + 側(がわ) (side).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～の裏側(うらがわ)に (on the back side of ~)
  • 裏側(うらがわ)を見(み)る (to see the reverse side)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 表側(おもてがわ) (front side) - antonym
  • 右側(みぎがわ) (right side)
  • 左側(ひだりがわ) (left side)
  • 向(む)こう側(がわ) (the other side)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裏側(うらがわ): back side, reverse
  • 裏(うら): back (simpler form)
  • 背面(はいめん): back surface (more formal/technical)

Related Words

Antonym: 表側(おもてがわ) (front side)
Related: 裏(うら) (back)
