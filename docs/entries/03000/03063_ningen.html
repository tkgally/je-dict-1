人間(にんげん)

にんげん
noun
human being, person

Definitions

1. human being, human
A member of the human species; humanity in general.
2. person, character
An individual's personality, nature, or character.

Examples

人間(にんげん)は社会的(しゃかいてき)な動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Humans are social animals.
彼(かれ)は立派(りっぱ)な人間(にんげん)だ。
He is a fine person (of good character).
人間 here refers to character/personality
人間(にんげん)関係(かんけい)は難(むずか)しい。
Human relationships are difficult.
人間関係 = human relationships

Notes

USAGE: 人間(にんげん) can refer to humans as a species or to a person's character/nature. It's more philosophical/formal than 人(ひと).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 人間(にんげん)関係(かんけい) (human relationships, interpersonal relations)
  • 人間(にんげん)性(せい) (human nature, humanity)
  • 人間(にんげん)的(てき) (human, humane)
  • 人間(にんげん)味(み) (human touch, warmth)
  • 人間(にんげん)不信(ふしん) (distrust of people)
  • 人間(にんげん)国宝(こくほう) (Living National Treasure)

CONTRAST:

  • 人間(にんげん) - human (philosophical, species-level)
  • 人(ひと) - person (everyday, individual)
  • 人類(じんるい) - mankind, humanity (collective)

Related Words

Related: 人(ひと) (person)
Related: 人類(じんるい) (mankind)
