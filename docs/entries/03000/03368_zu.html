図(ず)

ず
noun
diagram, figure, illustration
1. diagram, figure, illustration
A visual representation such as a chart, map, or drawing.
図(ず)を見(み)てください。
Please look at the diagram.
この図(ず)は分(わ)かりやすい。
This diagram is easy to understand.
説明(せつめい)に図(ず)を使(つか)う。
Use diagrams for the explanation.

図(ず) refers to diagrams, illustrations, and visual representations.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 図表(ずひょう) (chart, diagram)
  • 図形(ずけい) (figure, shape - geometric)
  • 図解(ずかい) (illustration, graphic explanation)
  • 図書(としょ) (books) - note: reading changes to と
  • 図書館(としょかん) (library)
  • 地図(ちず) (map)
  • 設計図(せっけいず) (blueprint, design plan)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 図(ず): diagram, figure (schematic representation)
  • 絵(え): picture, drawing (artistic)
  • グラフ: graph (borrowed from English)
  • 表(ひょう): table, chart (with rows and columns)
