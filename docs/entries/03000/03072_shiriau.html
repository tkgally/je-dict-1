知(し)り合(あ)う

しりあう
verb (godan)
to get to know, to become acquainted

Definitions

1. to get to know, to become acquainted
To meet and become familiar with someone; to form an acquaintance.

Examples

大学(だいがく)で彼(かれ)と知(し)り合(あ)った。
I got to know him at university.
二人(ふたり)は仕事(しごと)を通(とお)じて知(し)り合(あ)った。
The two of them got to know each other through work.
知(し)り合(あ)ってからもう10年(ねん)になる。
It's been 10 years since we met.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xと知(し)り合(あ)う.

知(し)り合(あ)う is formed from 知(し)る (to know) + 合(あ)う (mutual action). The 合(あ)う suffix indicates both parties participate.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 知(し)り合(あ)い (acquaintance - noun form)
  • 知(し)る (to know)
  • 出会(であ)う (to meet, encounter)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～で知(し)り合(あ)う (to meet at ~)
  • ～を通(とお)じて知(し)り合(あ)う (to meet through ~)
  • 知(し)り合(あ)って～年(ねん) (have known each other for ~ years)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 知(し)り合(あ)う: to become acquainted (emphasis on getting to know)
  • 出会(であ)う: to meet (initial encounter)
  • 会(あ)う: to meet (general)
