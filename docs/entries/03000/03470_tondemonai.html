とんでもない

とんでもない
i-adjective, expression
outrageous, no way
1. outrageous, absurd, unthinkable
Describes something unexpected, unreasonable, or beyond normal bounds. Can express shock or disbelief.
とんでもないことをしてしまった。
I did something terrible/outrageous.
とんでもない値段(ねだん)だ！
What an outrageous price!
2. not at all, no way, absolutely not
Used as a humble denial or to strongly reject praise or thanks. A polite way to say 'don't mention it' or 'not at all'.
「助(たす)けてくれてありがとう」「とんでもない」
"Thank you for helping me." "Not at all."
Humble response to thanks

USAGE: とんでもない has two main uses:
1. To describe something outrageous, terrible, or shocking
2. As a humble denial (similar to 'not at all' or 'don't mention it')

AS A RESPONSE:

When someone thanks you or praises you, とんでもない is a humble way to deflect:

  • A: 「素晴(すば)らしいですね」 B: 「とんでもないです」

A: "That's wonderful!" B: "Not at all."

VARIATIONS:

  • とんでもないです (polite)
  • とんでもございません (very polite/formal)
  • とんでもない！(exclamation)

GRAMMAR NOTE: Though it ends in ない, it's treated as one unit and doesn't conjugate like a regular negative. However, some speakers do say とんでもなかった for past tense.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 信(しん)じられない (unbelievable)
  • ありえない (impossible)
  • どういたしまして (you're welcome)
