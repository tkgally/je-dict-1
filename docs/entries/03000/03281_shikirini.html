しきりに

しきりに
adverb
frequently, repeatedly, eagerly

Definitions

1. frequently, repeatedly
Describes actions that happen many times in succession.
2. eagerly, earnestly
Describes doing something with great enthusiasm or persistence.

Examples

彼(かれ)はしきりに時計(とけい)を見(み)ていた。
He kept looking at his watch repeatedly.
しきりに電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)っている。
The phone keeps ringing constantly.
彼女(かのじょ)はしきりに私(わたし)に留学(りゅうがく)を勧(すす)めた。
She eagerly kept recommending that I study abroad.

Notes

Often implies that the action is noticeable or somewhat excessive. Can have either a neutral or slightly negative nuance depending on context.

PATTERNS:

  • しきりに〜する (to do something repeatedly)
  • しきりに〜を求(もと)める (to persistently seek something)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頻繁(ひんぱん)に is more formal and neutral
  • 何度(なんど)も emphasizes the number of times
  • しきりに adds a nuance of persistence or eagerness
