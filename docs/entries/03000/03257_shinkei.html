神経(しんけい)

しんけい
noun
nerve, sensitivity

Definitions

1. nerve
Fibers in the body that transmit signals between the brain and other parts of the body.
2. nerves, sensitivity, temperament
One's mental state, sensitivity to things, or disposition.

Examples

神経(しんけい)が痛(いた)む。
My nerve hurts.
彼(かれ)は神経(しんけい)が細(こま)かい。
He is very sensitive / pays attention to details.
大勢(おおぜい)の前(まえ)で話(はな)すと神経(しんけい)が高(たか)ぶる。
I get nervous when speaking in front of many people.

Notes

神経(しんけい) is used for both physical nerves and psychological sensitivity.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 神経(しんけい)が細(こま)かい (sensitive, detail-oriented)
  • 神経(しんけい)が太(ふと)い (thick-skinned, insensitive - often negative)
  • 神経(しんけい)を使(つか)う (to be nervous, to pay careful attention)
  • 神経(しんけい)にさわる (to get on one's nerves)
  • 神経(しんけい)が高(たか)ぶる (nerves are on edge)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 神経(しんけい)質(しつ) (nervous temperament)
  • 神経(しんけい)科(か) (neurology department)
  • 自律(じりつ)神経(しんけい) (autonomic nervous system)
