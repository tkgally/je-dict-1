務(つと)め

つとめ
noun
duty, role

Definitions

1. duty, responsibility, obligation
A task or role that one is expected to fulfill. Emphasizes the sense of responsibility or obligation rather than employment.
2. service, role (in a performance)
A role or part one plays, especially in formal or ceremonial contexts.

Examples

親(おや)としての務(つと)めを果(は)たす。
To fulfill one's duty as a parent.
務めを果たす = to fulfill one's duty
司会(しかい)の務(つと)めを引(ひ)き受(う)けた。
I accepted the role of emcee.
市民(しみん)としての務(つと)めを忘(わす)れてはいけない。
We must not forget our duties as citizens.

Notes

USAGE: This kanji (務(つと)め) emphasizes duty, obligation, or role. Different from 勤(つと)め which emphasizes employment.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 務(つと)めを果(は)たす (to fulfill one's duty)
  • 務(つと)めを終(お)える (to complete one's duty)
  • 〜としての務(つと)め (one's duty as ~)
  • お務(つと)め (polite: one's duty/role)

CONTRAST WITH 勤(つと)め:

  • 務(つと)め - duty, responsibility, obligation, role
  • 勤(つと)め - employment, working for a company

Both are read つとめ but have different nuances and kanji.

Related Words

Contrast: 勤(つと)め (employment (different kanji))
Related: 勤(つと)める (verb form)
