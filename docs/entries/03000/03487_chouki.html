長期(ちょうき)

ちょうき
noun, no-adjective
long-term, long period

Definitions

1. long-term, extended period
A prolonged duration of time, often used in contexts of planning, investment, or stays.

Examples

長期(ちょうき)の休暇(きゅうか)を取(と)りたい。
I want to take a long vacation.
長期(ちょうき)計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる。
Make a long-term plan.
この投資(とうし)は長期(ちょうき)で考(かんが)えるべきだ。
This investment should be considered in the long term.

Notes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 長期(ちょうき): Long-term (months to years)
  • 短期(たんき): Short-term (days to weeks)
  • 中期(ちゅうき): Medium-term

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 長期(ちょうき)計画(けいかく) (long-term plan)
  • 長期(ちょうき)滞在(たいざい) (long stay, extended stay)
  • 長期(ちょうき)休暇(きゅうか) (long vacation)
  • 長期(ちょうき)投資(とうし) (long-term investment)
  • 長期(ちょうき)金利(きんり) (long-term interest rate)

Related Words

Antonym: 短期(たんき) (short-term)
