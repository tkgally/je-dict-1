適度(てきど)

てきど
na-adjective, noun
moderate, proper degree
1. moderate, proper, appropriate degree
An amount or degree that is neither too much nor too little; just right. Often used when discussing health, exercise, or consumption.
適度(てきど)な運動(うんどう)は健康(けんこう)に良(よ)い。
Moderate exercise is good for your health.
適度な + noun = moderate ~
適度(てきど)に休憩(きゅうけい)を取(と)ってください。
Please take breaks in moderation.
適度に = moderately
塩分(えんぶん)は適度(てきど)に摂(と)ることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to consume salt in moderation.

USAGE: 適度(てきど) is commonly used with topics related to health, lifestyle, and balance. It suggests an optimal middle ground.

FORMS:

  • 適度(てきど)な〜 (moderate ~, appropriate ~) - na-adjective
  • 適度(てきど)に (moderately, in moderation) - adverbial
  • 適度(てきど) (moderation, proper degree) - noun

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 適度(てきど)な運動(うんどう) (moderate exercise)
  • 適度(てきど)な睡眠(すいみん) (adequate sleep)
  • 適度(てきど)な緊張(きんちょう) (appropriate tension)
  • 適度(てきど)な距離(きょり) (appropriate distance)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 程(ほど)よい - just right, moderate (more casual)
  • 適切(てきせつ) - appropriate, suitable (more formal)
  • 適当(てきとう) - suitable; also 'random/half-hearted' (be careful with context)

Related Words

Related: 適切(てきせつ) (appropriate)
Contrast: 適当(てきとう) (suitable/random)
