筋(すじ)

すじ
noun
line, muscle, plot, logic
1. line, stripe, streak
A thin line or mark on a surface.
2. muscle, tendon, sinew
Muscle fibers or tendons in the body.
筋肉(きんにく)の筋(すじ)を痛(いた)めた。
I strained a muscle.
3. plot, storyline
The main story or sequence of events.
この映画(えいが)の筋(すじ)を教(おし)えて。
Tell me the plot of this movie.
4. logic, reason, principle
A coherent line of reasoning or proper procedure.
それは筋(すじ)が通(とお)らない。
That doesn't make sense. / That's illogical.

筋(すじ) has multiple related meanings centering on the concept of a "line" or "thread."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 筋(すじ)が通(とお)る (to make sense, to be logical)
  • 筋(すじ)を通(とお)す (to follow proper procedure)
  • 筋(すじ)違(ちが)い (irrelevant, off-topic; also: muscle strain)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血(ち)筋(すじ) (bloodline, lineage)
  • 道(みち)筋(すじ) (route, path)
  • 話(はなし)の筋(すじ) (storyline)
  • 筋肉(きんにく) (muscle)

SENSE CONNECTIONS:
The meanings are connected through the idea of a continuous line: physical lines → muscle fibers → storyline → logical thread.
