立場(たちば)

たちば
noun
standpoint, position
1. standpoint, position, point of view
The perspective from which one views or judges things; one's role or situation.
相手(あいて)の立場(たちば)に立(た)って考(かんが)える。
To think from the other person's standpoint.
それぞれの立場(たちば)から意見(いけん)を出(だ)した。
Everyone gave opinions from their own standpoint.
消費者(しょうひしゃ)の立場(たちば)で考(かんが)えることが大事(だいじ)だ。
It is important to think from the consumer's standpoint.
2. situation, predicament
The circumstances or position one finds oneself in, especially regarding responsibility or social role.
私(わたし)の立場(たちば)も分(わ)かってください。
Please understand my position as well.
立場(たちば)上(じょう)、言(い)えないことがある。
Due to my position, there are things I cannot say.
立場上 = due to one's position
部長(ぶちょう)という立場(たちば)から、責任(せきにん)を取(と)らなければならない。
As department head, I have to take responsibility.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立場(たちば)に立(た)つ (to stand in someone's position)
  • 立場(たちば)を考(かんが)える (to consider one's position)
  • 立場(たちば)が悪(わる)い (to be in an awkward position)
  • 立場(たちば)上(じょう) (due to one's position)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 相手(あいて)の立場(たちば)に立(た)つ (to put oneself in someone else's shoes)
  • 立場(たちば)が逆転(ぎゃくてん)する (for positions to be reversed)
  • 立場(たちば)を利用(りよう)する (to take advantage of one's position)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立場(たちば): Position, standpoint (personal perspective)
  • 観点(かんてん): Viewpoint (more analytical)
  • 地位(ちい): Status, rank (social hierarchy)
