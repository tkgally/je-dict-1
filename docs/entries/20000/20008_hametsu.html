1. ruin; downfall; destruction
Complete collapse or destruction of something, especially a person's life, career, or an organization. Often implies self-caused destruction through poor choices or excess.
会社(かいしゃ)が破滅(はめつ)した。
The company was ruined.
借金(しゃっきん)が原因(げんいん)で破滅(はめつ)に向(む)かった。
Debt led to his downfall.
ギャンブルにのめり込(こ)んで人生(じんせい)を破滅(はめつ)させてしまった。
He got absorbed in gambling and ruined his life.

Refers to complete ruin or destruction, particularly of a person's life circumstances, a family, or an organization. Carries a strong sense of irreversible collapse, often implying the destruction was brought about by the person's own actions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 破滅(はめつ)に向(む)かう: to head toward ruin
  • 破滅(はめつ)に追(お)い込(こ)む: to drive to ruin
  • 破滅(はめつ)させる: to cause someone's ruin
  • 自滅(じめつ)と破滅(はめつ): self-destruction and ruin
  • 破滅(はめつ)への道(みち): the path to ruin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 崩壊(ほうかい): collapse — focuses on structural or systemic breakdown (buildings, systems, relationships)
  • 滅亡(めつぼう): annihilation — used for nations, civilizations, or species ceasing to exist
  • 没落(ぼつらく): decline and fall — gradual loss of status or wealth, especially of families or aristocrats

USAGE:
Often used in literary or dramatic contexts. The verb form 破滅(はめつ)する means "to be ruined" and 破滅(はめつ)させる means "to ruin (someone/something)." Frequently appears in stories about characters whose flaws lead to their own destruction.
