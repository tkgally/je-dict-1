悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)

あくせんくとう
noun
desperate struggle; hard fight
1. desperate struggle; hard fight; uphill battle
A difficult, prolonged struggle against adversity or a challenging situation. Often used as a suru-verb (悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)する). A four-character idiom (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)).
悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)の末(すえ)に勝(か)った。
After a desperate struggle, we won.
新(あたら)しいソフトの操作(そうさ)に悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)している。
I'm struggling with operating the new software.
子育(こそだ)てに悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)しながらも、毎日(まいにち)楽(たの)しく過(す)ごしている。
Although I'm struggling with raising children, I enjoy every day.

A 四字熟語(よじじゅくご) (four-character idiom) describing a hard, exhausting fight or struggle. Frequently used as a する verb: 悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)する.

LITERAL MEANING:
悪(あく) (bad/difficult) + 戦(せん) (battle) + 苦(く) (suffering) + 闘(とう) (fight) — fighting a painful, difficult battle.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)する: to struggle desperately
  • 悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)の末(すえ): after a long struggle
  • 悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう)を続(つづ)ける: to keep struggling

USAGE:
Used for both literal physical struggles and figurative ones like learning a new skill, dealing with a difficult project, or handling an overwhelming situation. The tone is often sympathetic or self-deprecating rather than heroic.
