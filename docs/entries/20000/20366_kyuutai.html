球体(きゅうたい)

きゅうたい
noun
sphere; globe; ball-shaped object
1. sphere; globe; spherical body
A three-dimensional round shape where every point on the surface is the same distance from the center. Used in mathematics, science, and everyday descriptions.
地球(ちきゅう)は完全(かんぜん)な球体(きゅうたい)ではない。
The Earth is not a perfect sphere.
ガラスの球体(きゅうたい)が光(ひかり)を反射(はんしゃ)している。
A glass sphere is reflecting the light.
球体(きゅうたい)の表面積(ひょうめんせき)は4πr²で求(もと)められる。
The surface area of a sphere can be calculated with 4πr².

The standard term for a sphere in both scientific and everyday contexts. More precise than 丸(まる)い (round), which can apply to flat circles as well.

FORMATION:
球(きゅう) (ball, sphere) + 体(たい) (body, form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 球体(きゅうたい)の形(かたち): spherical shape
  • 完全(かんぜん)な球体(きゅうたい): perfect sphere
  • 球体(きゅうたい)関節(かんせつ): ball joint
  • 球体(きゅうたい)関節(かんせつ)人形(にんぎょう): ball-jointed doll (BJD)

RELATED GEOMETRIC TERMS:

  • 立方体(りっぽうたい): cube
  • 円柱(えんちゅう): cylinder
  • 円錐(えんすい): cone
  • 半球(はんきゅう): hemisphere
