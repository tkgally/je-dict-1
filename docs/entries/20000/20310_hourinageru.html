1. to throw; to toss; to hurl carelessly
To throw something with a careless or forceful motion, without much concern for where it lands.
ボールを放(ほう)り投(な)げる。
To toss a ball.
かばんを床(ゆか)に放(ほう)り投(な)げた。
I tossed my bag onto the floor.
怒(おこ)って本(ほん)を壁(かべ)に放(ほう)り投(な)げた。
In anger, I hurled the book at the wall.
2. to abandon; to give up on; to quit partway through
To stop doing something midway and walk away from it. Implies irresponsibility or frustration.
仕事(しごと)を放(ほう)り投(な)げる。
To abandon one's work.
途中(とちゅう)で嫌(いや)になって全部(ぜんぶ)放(ほう)り投(な)げた。
I got fed up partway through and abandoned everything.
責任(せきにん)ある立場(たちば)の人(ひと)が仕事(しごと)を放(ほう)り投(な)げるのは許(ゆる)されない。
It's unacceptable for someone in a position of responsibility to abandon their work.

A compound verb from 放(ほう)る (to throw, to toss) + 投(な)げる (to throw). The combination intensifies the carelessness or force of the throw. Both senses carry a nuance of roughness or disregard.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を放(ほう)り投(な)げる — to abandon one's work
  • ボールを放(ほう)り投(な)げる — to toss a ball
  • 全部(ぜんぶ)放(ほう)り投(な)げる — to give up on everything
  • 途中(とちゅう)で放(ほう)り投(な)げる — to quit midway

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 投(な)げ出(だ)す — to throw out, to abandon (very similar to sense 2; equally common)
  • 放(ほう)り出(だ)す — to throw out, to eject (emphasizes expulsion or abandonment)
  • 投(な)げる — to throw (neutral; lacks the careless or rough nuance)
