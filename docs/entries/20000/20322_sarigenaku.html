さりげなく

さりげなく
adverb
casually; nonchalantly; unobtrusively
1. casually; nonchalantly; unobtrusively; in a natural manner
Doing something in a way that appears natural and unforced, without drawing attention to the action. Often implies deliberate subtlety — the person is intentionally acting casual to avoid being noticed or to show consideration.
さりげなく席(せき)を譲(ゆず)った。
I casually offered my seat.
彼女(かのじょ)はさりげなく話題(わだい)を変(か)えた。
She subtly changed the subject.
さりげなく相手(あいて)の様子(ようす)を確認(かくにん)するのが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to unobtrusively check how the other person is doing.

The adverbial form of さりげない (appearing casual/nonchalant). The base adjective さりげない is itself composed of さる (that) + 気(け) (feeling/intention) + ない (without) — literally 'without that kind of feeling,' meaning not showing any particular intention.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • さりげなく助(たす)ける: to help unobtrusively
  • さりげなく聞(き)く: to ask casually
  • さりげなく褒(ほ)める: to compliment naturally
  • さりげない気遣(きづか)い: subtle consideration

USAGE:
Often used positively to describe considerate behavior — helping someone without making them feel embarrassed, or showing concern without being intrusive. The adjective form さりげない modifies nouns directly: さりげない優(やさ)しさ (unassuming kindness).
