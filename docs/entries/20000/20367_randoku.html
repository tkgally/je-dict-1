1. reading widely and unsystematically; indiscriminate reading
Reading many different books without a particular plan or focus, jumping from one subject or genre to another. Can be seen positively (voracious reading) or negatively (unfocused reading).
若(わか)い頃(ころ)は乱読(らんどく)していた。
When I was young, I read widely without any plan.
乱読(らんどく)も悪(わる)くない。思(おも)わぬ発見(はっけん)がある。
Reading indiscriminately isn't bad. You make unexpected discoveries.
図書館(としょかん)で手当(てあ)たり次第(しだい)に乱読(らんどく)するのが休日(きゅうじつ)の楽(たの)しみだ。
My holiday pleasure is reading whatever catches my eye at the library.

A reading style where one reads broadly across genres and topics without a structured plan. Some writers and intellectuals praise 乱読(らんどく) as a way to broaden one's horizons, while others criticize it as unfocused.

FORMATION:
乱(らん) (random, disorderly) + 読(どく) (reading)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 乱読(らんどく)する: to read indiscriminately
  • 乱読(らんどく)家(か): a voracious/indiscriminate reader
  • 乱読(らんどく)の{すすめ}: recommendation of wide reading

CONTRASTING READING STYLES:

  • 精読(せいどく): close/careful reading — reading one text thoroughly
  • 速読(そくどく): speed reading — reading quickly
  • 多読(たどく): extensive reading — reading a large quantity, but often with more purpose than 乱読(らんどく)
  • 濫読(らんどく): same meaning, alternate kanji (less common)
