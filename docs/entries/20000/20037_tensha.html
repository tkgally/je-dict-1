1. copying; transfer; transcription
The act of copying or transferring a pattern, image, or text from one surface or medium to another.
模様(もよう)を布(ぬの)に転写(てんしゃ)する。
To transfer a pattern onto fabric.
アイロンで転写(てんしゃ)シートを貼(は)り付(つ)けた。
I applied the transfer sheet with an iron.
転写(てんしゃ)シールで好(す)きなデザインをスマホケースに付(つ)けた。
I put my favorite design on my phone case using a transfer sticker.
2. transcription (in biology)
The biological process by which DNA is copied into RNA, a fundamental step in gene expression.
DNAの情報(じょうほう)がRNAに転写(てんしゃ)される。
DNA information is transcribed into RNA.
遺伝子(いでんし)の転写(てんしゃ)が始(はじ)まる。
Gene transcription begins.
転写(てんしゃ)因子(いんし)は遺伝子(いでんし)の発現(はつげん)を制御(せいぎょ)する重要(じゅうよう)なタンパク質(しつ)だ。
Transcription factors are important proteins that control gene expression.

Compound of 転(てん) (transfer, turn) and 写(しゃ) (copy, photograph). Has both an everyday meaning (transferring images/patterns) and a specialized biological meaning.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (general):

  • 転写(てんしゃ)シート: transfer sheet
  • 転写(てんしゃ)シール: transfer sticker/decal
  • 転写(てんしゃ)する: to transfer, to copy

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (biology):

  • 転写(てんしゃ)因子(いんし): transcription factor
  • 遺伝子(いでんし)転写(てんしゃ): gene transcription
  • 転写(てんしゃ)調節(ちょうせつ): transcription regulation
  • 逆(ぎゃく)転写(てんしゃ): reverse transcription

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 複写(ふくしゃ): copying, duplication — making an identical copy (photocopying)
  • 翻訳(ほんやく): translation — in biology, the process of RNA being translated into protein (the step after 転写(てんしゃ))
  • 写(うつ)す: to copy, to transcribe — the everyday verb for copying text or images
