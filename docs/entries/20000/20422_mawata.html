真綿(まわた)

まわた
noun
silk floss; silk wadding
1. silk floss; silk wadding
Soft, fluffy batting made from silkworm cocoons that have been stretched into sheets. Traditionally used as padding in high-quality bedding, winter clothing, and crafts.
真綿(まわた)のように柔(やわ)らかい。
Soft as silk floss.
この布団(ふとん)は真綿(まわた)が入(はい)っていて暖(あたた)かい。
This futon has silk wadding inside and is warm.
真綿(まわた)で首(くび)を絞(し)めるように、じわじわと追(お)い詰(つ)められた。
I was gradually cornered, as if being strangled with silk floss.

Composed of 真(ま) (true, genuine) + 綿(わた) (cotton, wadding). Despite the kanji 綿(わた), 真綿(まわた) is made from silk, not cotton. The 真(ま) distinguishes it from ordinary plant-based 綿(わた) (cotton).

IDIOMATIC EXPRESSION:
真綿(まわた)で首(くび)を絞(し)める — literally "to strangle with silk floss." Describes applying slow, gentle but persistent pressure, or gradually tightening the screws on someone. Because silk floss is soft, the strangling is slow and drawn out rather than sudden.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真綿(まわた)の布団(ふとん): silk-filled futon
  • 真綿(まわた)のように柔(やわ)らかい: soft as silk floss
  • 真綿(まわた)で包(つつ)むように: gently, as if wrapping in silk floss

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 綿(わた)/木綿(もめん): cotton — plant-based fiber, much more common and cheaper
  • 絹(きぬ): silk — the woven fabric, as opposed to the raw floss
  • 羽毛(うもう): down feathers — another premium natural filling for bedding
