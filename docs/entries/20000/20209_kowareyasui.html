1. fragile; easily broken; delicate
Liable to break or be damaged, either physically or figuratively.
ガラスは壊(こわ)れやすい。
Glass is fragile.
壊(こわ)れやすい物(もの)は丁寧(ていねい)に運(はこ)んでください。
Please carry fragile items carefully.
この機械(きかい)は精密(せいみつ)で壊(こわ)れやすいので、衝撃(しょうげき)を与(あた)えないように気(き)をつけてください。
This machine is delicate and easily broken, so please be careful not to subject it to shocks.

Formed from 壊(こわ)れる (to break) + やすい (easy to). This is a productive grammatical pattern in Japanese where verb stem + やすい means "easy to do" or "prone to."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 壊(こわ)れやすい物(もの): fragile item
  • 壊(こわ)れやすい素材(そざい): fragile material
  • 壊(こわ)れやすい関係(かんけい): fragile relationship

USAGE:
Often seen on packaging and shipping labels. The formal equivalent for labeling is 取扱注意(とりあつかいちゅうい) (handle with care) or ワレモノ注意(ちゅうい) (fragile — caution).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • もろい: brittle, fragile — emphasizes structural weakness
  • デリケートな: delicate — loanword, often for sensitive topics or skin
  • 脆弱(ぜいじゃく)な: vulnerable, fragile — formal, often used for systems or security
