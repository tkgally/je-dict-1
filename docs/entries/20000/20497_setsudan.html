切断(せつだん)

せつだん
noun
cutting; severing; amputation
1. cutting; severing; cutting off
The act of cutting something apart completely. Used for physical objects like ropes, cables, or materials.
ロープを切断(せつだん)する。
To sever a rope.
電線(でんせん)が切断(せつだん)されて停電(ていでん)した。
The power lines were cut and there was a blackout.
台風(たいふう)で通信(つうしん)ケーブルが切断(せつだん)され、インターネットが使(つか)えなくなった。
The typhoon severed communication cables, making the internet unusable.
2. amputation
The surgical removal of a limb or body part. A medical term.
足(あし)の切断(せつだん)手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)けた。
He underwent leg amputation surgery.
事故(じこ)で指(ゆび)を切断(せつだん)する怪我(けが)を負(お)った。
He suffered an injury that severed his finger in the accident.
医師(いし)は壊死(えし)した組織(そしき)の切断(せつだん)がやむを得(え)ないと判断(はんだん)した。
The doctor determined that amputation of the necrotic tissue was unavoidable.

The act of cutting something completely apart. Composed of 切(せつ) (cut) + 断(だん) (sever/break off). Often used as a する verb: 切断(せつだん)する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切断(せつだん)する: to cut; to sever
  • 切断(せつだん)面(めん): cross-section; cut surface
  • 切断(せつだん)手術(しゅじゅつ): amputation surgery
  • 電線(でんせん)の切断(せつだん): cutting of power lines

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(き)る: to cut — the general everyday verb for cutting
  • 断裂(だんれつ): rupture, tearing apart — implies forceful breaking rather than clean cutting
  • 遮断(しゃだん): shutoff, blocking — cutting off a flow or connection (electricity, traffic)

USAGE:
In the medical sense (amputation), 切断(せつだん) is the standard clinical term. In everyday contexts, it often refers to severing cables, ropes, or connections.
