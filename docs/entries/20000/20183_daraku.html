1. depravity; corruption; degradation; moral decline
A fall from a higher moral or behavioral standard to a lower one. Used for both individuals and societies. Also used as a suru-verb.
堕落(だらく)した生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)る。
To lead a dissolute life.
人間(にんげん)は楽(らく)をすると堕落(だらく)する。
People become corrupted when they take the easy way out.
権力(けんりょく)を持(も)つと堕落(だらく)しやすいと言(い)われている。
It is said that holding power tends to lead to corruption.

Refers to moral or behavioral decline, often with a strong negative connotation. The kanji 堕(だ) means "to fall" (as in 堕(お)ちる) and 落(らく) also means "to fall," reinforcing the image of a downward slide.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 堕落(だらく)する: to become corrupt, to degenerate
  • 堕落(だらく)した生活(せいかつ): a dissolute lifestyle
  • 道徳(どうとく)の堕落(だらく): moral decline
  • 堕落(だらく)の道(みち): the path of corruption

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 腐敗(ふはい): corruption — often used for institutional or political corruption
  • 退廃(たいはい): decadence — focuses on cultural or artistic decline
  • 頽廃(たいはい): same as above, with an older kanji variant

堕落(だらく) emphasizes personal moral decline, while 腐敗(ふはい) is more commonly used for systemic corruption.
