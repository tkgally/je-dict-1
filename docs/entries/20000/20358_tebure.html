手(て)ぶれ

てぶれ
noun
camera shake; hand tremor (in photography)
1. camera shake; blurring from hand movement
Blurring in a photograph or video caused by the photographer's hand shaking or moving during the shot. A common problem in low-light conditions or when using long focal lengths.
写真(しゃしん)が手(て)ぶれしている。
The photo is blurry from camera shake.
手(て)ぶれ補正(ほせい)機能(きのう)がついたカメラがほしい。
I want a camera with image stabilization.
暗(くら)い場所(ばしょ)で撮影(さつえい)すると手(て)ぶれしやすいので、三脚(さんきゃく)を使(つか)った方(ほう)がいい。
It's easy to get camera shake when shooting in dark places, so you should use a tripod.

A photography term that has become common in everyday language with the widespread use of smartphones and digital cameras. Also written as 手振(てぶ)れ or 手ブレ(てぶれ).

FORMATION:
手(て) (hand) + ぶれ (shake, blur — from ぶれる, to shake/deviate)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手(て)ぶれ補正(ほせい): image stabilization (literally "camera shake correction")
  • 手(て)ぶれする: to have camera shake
  • 手(て)ぶれ防止(ぼうし): camera shake prevention
  • 手(て)ぶれしやすい: prone to camera shake

RELATED TERMS:

  • ピンぼけ: out of focus — caused by incorrect focus, not hand movement
  • 被写体(ひしゃたい)ぶれ: subject blur — caused by the subject moving, not the camera
  • 三脚(さんきゃく): tripod — the classic solution for camera shake
