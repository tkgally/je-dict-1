1. to go bald; to lose hair
To lose hair from the head, resulting in baldness. Can describe gradual thinning or complete hair loss.
父(ちち)は若(わか)い頃(ころ)から禿(は)げていた。
My father was bald from a young age.
ストレスで頭(あたま)が禿(は)げそうだ。
I'm so stressed I feel like I'm going to go bald.
最近(さいきん)額(ひたい)のあたりが禿(は)げてきた気(き)がする。
I feel like my hairline has been receding lately.
2. to become bare; to wear off; to peel
For surfaces like mountains, paint, or coatings to become stripped or bare, losing their covering.
ペンキが禿(は)げている。
The paint has peeled off.
山(やま)の木(き)が伐採(ばっさい)されて禿(は)げてしまった。
The trees on the mountain were felled and it became bare.
長年(ながねん)の使用(しよう)で塗装(とそう)が禿(は)げてきた。
After years of use, the coating has started to wear off.

An ichidan (一段(いちだん)) verb. Also written without kanji as はげる. The kanji 禿(は)げ can be sensitive as a noun referring to a bald person — use with care in conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頭(あたま)が禿(は)げる: to go bald
  • ペンキが禿(は)げる: paint peels off
  • 山(やま)が禿(は)げる: a mountain becomes bare
  • 禿(は)げ上(あ)がる: to go completely bald (intensive)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 禿(は)げ: bald; bald person (noun) — can be considered rude
  • 薄毛(うすげ): thinning hair — a more polite, euphemistic term
  • 脱毛(だつもう): hair loss; hair removal — clinical/medical term
  • 剥(は)がれる: to peel off, come off — used for labels, stickers, surfaces
