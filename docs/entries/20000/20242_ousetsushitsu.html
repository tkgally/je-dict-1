応接室(おうせつしつ)

おうせつしつ
noun
reception room; parlor
1. reception room; parlor; sitting room for guests
A room in a home, office, or building designated for receiving and meeting with guests or visitors.
応接室(おうせつしつ)でお待(ま)ちください。
Please wait in the reception room.
お客(きゃく)さまを応接室(おうせつしつ)にご案内(あんない)した。
I showed the guest to the reception room.
社長(しゃちょう)が応接室(おうせつしつ)で取引先(とりひきさき)と打(う)ち合(あ)わせをしているので、終(お)わるまで入(はい)らないでください。
The president is having a meeting with a client in the reception room, so please don't go in until they're finished.

Composed of 応接(おうせつ) (reception, receiving guests) + 室(しつ) (room). Found in offices, larger homes, and institutional buildings. In a Japanese office, the 応接室(おうせつしつ) is typically furnished with sofas and a coffee table, separate from the open workspace.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 応接室(おうせつしつ)に案内(あんない)する: to show someone to the reception room
  • 応接室(おうせつしつ)で待(ま)つ: to wait in the reception room
  • 応接室(おうせつしつ)のソファー: reception room sofa
  • 応接室(おうせつしつ)に通(とお)す: to usher someone into the reception room

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 会議室(かいぎしつ): meeting room, conference room — for internal meetings
  • 待合室(まちあいしつ): waiting room — in hospitals, stations, etc.
  • 客間(きゃくま): guest room — in a Japanese-style home
  • 応接間(おうせつま): reception room — synonym, slightly more old-fashioned
