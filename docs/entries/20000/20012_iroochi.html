1. color fading; dye running; discoloration
The fading or running of color from fabric, hair dye, or other colored materials, typically due to washing, sunlight, or time.
このジーンズは色落(いろお)ちしやすい。
These jeans fade easily.
洗濯(せんたく)で色落(いろお)ちしないように注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful so the color doesn't run in the wash.
日光(にっこう)に長(なが)く当(あ)てると色落(いろお)ちの原因(げんいん)になる。
Exposing it to sunlight for a long time causes color fading.

Compound of 色(いろ) (color) and 落(お)ちる (to fall/drop). Refers to the gradual or sudden loss of color from a material. Very common in everyday contexts related to clothing care and hair.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色落(いろお)ちする: to fade, to lose color
  • 色落(いろお)ちしやすい: prone to fading
  • 色落(いろお)ちを防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent fading
  • 色落(いろお)ちの原因(げんいん): cause of fading
  • 色落(いろお)ちが激(はげ)しい: severe fading

CONTEXTS:

  • Clothing: jeans and dark-colored fabrics are especially prone to 色落(いろお)ち in the wash
  • Hair: hair dye fading over time or with washing
  • Furniture and interiors: fading from sunlight exposure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 色褪(いろあ)せる: to fade, to lose color — a verb with a more literary or poetic nuance, often used figuratively (memories fading)
  • 変色(へんしょく): discoloration — a broader term for any color change, including yellowing or darkening
