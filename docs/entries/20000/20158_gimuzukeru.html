1. to obligate; to make mandatory; to require by rule or law
To impose an obligation or requirement, typically through law, regulation, or organizational rules. Often used in passive form to describe what is required.
届(とど)け出(で)が義務付(ぎむづ)けられている。
Filing a notification is required.
法律(ほうりつ)で報告(ほうこく)を義務付(ぎむづ)ける。
To mandate reporting by law.
企業(きぎょう)に情報(じょうほう)公開(こうかい)が義務付(ぎむづ)けられるようになった。
Companies became required to disclose information.

An ichidan verb formed from 義務(ぎむ) (obligation, duty) + 付(づ)ける (to attach). Creates the meaning of attaching an obligation to something or someone. Very frequently used in passive form (義務付(ぎむづ)けられている) to describe existing requirements.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — takes を to mark the action being made mandatory, and に to mark who is obligated.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 義務付(ぎむづ)けられている — is required, is mandatory
  • 法律(ほうりつ)で義務付(ぎむづ)ける — to require by law
  • 義務付(ぎむづ)けられた研修(けんしゅう) — mandatory training

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 義務化(ぎむか)する — to make obligatory (noun + する form)
  • 強制(きょうせい)する — to compel, to force (stronger, more coercive)
  • 課(か)す — to impose (a duty, tax, etc.)
