取(と)りまとめ

とりまとめ
noun
compilation; coordination; putting together
1. compilation; coordination; putting together; consolidation
The act of gathering, organizing, and consolidating information, opinions, or tasks into a coherent whole. Also used for the role of coordinating people or processes.
意見(いけん)の取(と)りまとめを行(おこな)う。
To compile opinions.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)の取(と)りまとめは来週(らいしゅう)までにお願(ねが)いします。
Please have the report compiled by next week.
会議(かいぎ)で出(で)た様々(さまざま)な意見(いけん)を取(と)りまとめて、最終(さいしゅう)案(あん)を作成(さくせい)した。
I consolidated the various opinions that came up in the meeting and created a final proposal.

The noun form of the compound verb 取(と)りまとめる (to compile, to coordinate). Widely used in business and administrative contexts for the process of gathering and organizing information or coordinating tasks.

USAGE:
Very common in workplace Japanese. Often appears as 取(と)りまとめ役(やく) (coordinator, person in charge of pulling things together). The verb form 取(と)りまとめる is equally common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 取(と)りまとめを行(おこな)う — to carry out compilation/coordination
  • 取(と)りまとめ役(やく) — coordinator, organizer
  • 意見(いけん)の取(と)りまとめ — compilation of opinions
  • 最終(さいしゅう)取(と)りまとめ — final consolidation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • まとめ — summary (shorter, more casual)
  • 集約(しゅうやく) — consolidation, aggregation (more formal)
  • 調整(ちょうせい) — coordination, adjustment (focuses on balancing)
