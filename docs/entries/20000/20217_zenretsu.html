前列(ぜんれつ)

ぜんれつ
noun
front row; front line
1. front row; front line
The row or line at the front, whether of seats, people, or a formation.
前列(ぜんれつ)に座(すわ)った。
I sat in the front row.
前列(ぜんれつ)の席(せき)はもう埋(う)まっている。
The front row seats are already taken.
集合写真(しゅうごうしゃしん)を撮(と)るとき、背(せ)の低(ひく)い人(ひと)は前列(ぜんれつ)に並(なら)んでください。
When we take the group photo, shorter people please line up in the front row.

A straightforward compound of 前(ぜん) (front) + 列(れつ) (row, line). Used for seating, formations, and lineups.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前列(ぜんれつ)の席(せき): front row seat
  • 最前列(さいぜんれつ): the very front row
  • 前列(ぜんれつ)に並(なら)ぶ: to line up in the front row
  • 前列(ぜんれつ)に立(た)つ: to stand in the front row

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 後列(こうれつ): back row — the antonym
  • 最前列(さいぜんれつ): very front row — the superlative
  • 前方(ぜんぽう): the front (area) — refers to a general area, not a row
