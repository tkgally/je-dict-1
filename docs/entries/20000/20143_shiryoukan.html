資料館(しりょうかん)

しりょうかん
noun
archive; museum; resource center; reference hall
1. archive; museum; resource center; reference hall
A facility that collects, preserves, and displays materials related to a specific topic, region, or historical period. Smaller and more specialized than a general museum.
郷土(きょうど)資料館(しりょうかん)を訪(おとず)れた。
I visited the local history archive.
歴史(れきし)資料館(しりょうかん)には古(ふる)い地図(ちず)が展示(てんじ)されている。
Old maps are on display at the historical archive.
平和(へいわ)資料館(しりょうかん)で戦争(せんそう)の悲惨(ひさん)さを学(まな)んだ。
I learned about the tragedy of war at the peace museum.

Composed of 資料(しりょう) (materials, documents, data) + 館(かん) (hall, building). A common type of public institution in Japan, typically run by local governments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 郷土(きょうど)資料館(しりょうかん): local history museum/archive
  • 歴史(れきし)資料館(しりょうかん): historical archive
  • 平和(へいわ)資料館(しりょうかん): peace museum
  • 民俗(みんぞく)資料館(しりょうかん): folk culture museum

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 博物館(はくぶつかん): museum — larger, more general institution with varied collections
  • 図書館(としょかん): library — focused on books and lending; 資料館(しりょうかん) focuses on archival materials and exhibits
  • 記念館(きねんかん): memorial hall — dedicated to a specific person or event
  • 美術館(びじゅつかん): art museum — specifically for fine art collections

USAGE:
Nearly every municipality in Japan has some form of 資料館(しりょうかん). They are typically free or inexpensive to enter and serve an educational purpose. The word is often preceded by a modifier indicating the subject area.
