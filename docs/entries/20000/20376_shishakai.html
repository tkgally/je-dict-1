試写会(ししゃかい)

ししゃかい
noun
preview screening; advance showing (of a film)
1. preview screening; advance showing; press screening
A screening of a film held before its official release, typically for critics, media, and invited guests. Also includes public preview events held as promotional activities.
試写会(ししゃかい)に招待(しょうたい)された。
I was invited to a preview screening.
新作(しんさく)映画(えいが)の試写会(��しゃかい)に当選(とうせん)した。
I won tickets to the preview screening of the new movie.
試写会(ししゃかい)では主演(しゅえん)俳優(はいゆう)の舞台挨拶(ぶたいあいさつ)もあり、会場(かいじょう)は大(おお)いに盛(も)り上(あ)がった。
The preview screening also featured a stage greeting from the lead actor, and the venue was very lively.

A common term in the Japanese entertainment industry. Preview screenings serve both as promotional events and as a way for critics to review films before release.

FORMATION:
試写(ししゃ) (test screening) + 会(かい) (event, gathering)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 試写会(ししゃかい)に招待(しょうたい)する: to invite to a preview screening
  • 試写会(ししゃかい)に当選(とうせん)する: to win a spot at a preview screening
  • 試写会(しし���かい)に応募(おうぼ)する: to apply for a preview screening
  • マスコミ試写(ししゃ): press screening
  • 完成(かんせい)試写会(ししゃかい): completion preview (screening of the finished film)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, many preview screenings are open to the general public through lotteries or magazine/website promotions. Winning (当選(とうせん)) a spot is a popular way to see anticipated films early. These events often include 舞台挨拶(ぶたいあいさつ) (stage greetings) by the cast.
