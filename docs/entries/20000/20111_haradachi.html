腹立(はらだ)ち

はらだち
noun
anger; irritation; annoyance
1. anger; irritation; annoyance; indignation
A feeling of anger or irritation, often simmering or building up rather than an explosive outburst. Derived from the verb 腹立つ (to get angry).
腹立(はらだ)ちを覚(おぼ)える。
To feel irritation.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)に腹立(はらだ)ち��感(かん)じた。
I felt annoyed by his attitude.
約束(やくそく)を何度(なんど)も破(やぶ)られて、腹立(はらだ)ちが収(おさ)まらなかった。
Having had promises broken repeatedly, my anger would not subside.

The noun form of 腹(はら)が立(た)つ (to get angry, literally 'the belly rises'). Expresses anger that is felt internally, often building or simmering.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腹立(はらだ)ちを覚(おぼ)える: to feel irritation/anger
  • 腹立(はらだ)ちを感(かん)じる: to feel annoyed
  • 腹立(はらだ)ちが収(おさ)まらない: anger won't subside
  • 腹立(はらだ)ちを抑(おさ)える: to hold back one's anger
  • 腹立(はらだ)ちまぎれに: out of spite; in a fit of anger

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怒(いか)り: anger, wrath — stronger and more intense than 腹立(はらだ)ち
  • 苛立(いらだ)ち: irritation, frustration — focuses on restless, fidgety irritation
  • 立腹(りっぷく): anger, indignation — more formal synonym; same kanji in different order
  • 憤(いきどお)り: indignation, resentment — anger at something unjust

ETYMOLOGY:
From the Japanese body metaphor where the 腹(はら) (belly/gut) is the seat of emotions. When one is angry, the belly 'rises' (立(た)つ).
