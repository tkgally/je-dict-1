宿泊(しゅくはく)する

しゅくはくする
verb
to stay overnight; to lodge
1. to stay overnight; to lodge
To spend one or more nights at a hotel, inn, or other accommodation.
ホテルに宿泊(しゅくはく)した。
I stayed at a hotel.
温泉(おんせん)旅館(りょかん)に一泊(いっぱく)宿泊(しゅくはく)する予定(よてい)です。
I plan to stay one night at a hot spring inn.
出張(しゅっちょう)で大阪(おおさか)のビジネスホテルに三泊(さんぱく)宿泊(しゅくはく)することになった。
I ended up staying three nights at a business hotel in Osaka for a work trip.

A formal word for staying overnight at an accommodation. More formal than 泊(と)まる, which is the everyday equivalent. 宿泊(しゅくはく)する is commonly used in hotel and travel contexts, on booking websites, and in written announcements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ホテルに宿泊(しゅくはく)する: to stay at a hotel
  • 旅館(りょかん)に宿泊(しゅくはく)する: to stay at a Japanese inn
  • 一泊(いっぱく)宿泊(しゅくはく): one-night stay
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)料金(りょうきん): accommodation fee
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)予約(よやく): accommodation reservation
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)施設(しせつ): lodging facility
  • 宿泊(しゅくはく)客(きゃく): hotel guest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泊(と)まる: to stay overnight — everyday word, used in casual speech
  • 滞在(たいざい)する: to stay, to reside — focuses on the duration of a stay, not specifically overnight
