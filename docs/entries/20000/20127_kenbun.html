1. experience; observation; information gained firsthand
Knowledge or awareness gained through direct personal experience — things one has seen with one's own eyes and heard with one's own ears.
見聞(けんぶん)を広(ひろ)げる。
To broaden one's experience.
海外旅行(かいがいりょこう)で見聞(けんぶん)を深(ふか)めた。
I deepened my knowledge through traveling abroad.
若(わか)いうちにいろいろな見聞(けんぶん)を積(つ)むことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to accumulate a wide range of experiences while you're young.

Composed of 見(けん) (see) + 聞(ぶん) (hear). Refers to the totality of what one has witnessed and learned through direct experience, not just book knowledge.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見聞(けんぶん)を広(ひろ)げる: to broaden one's horizons/experience
  • 見聞(けんぶん)を深(ふか)める: to deepen one's knowledge
  • 見聞(けんぶん)を積(つ)む: to accumulate experience
  • 見聞(けんぶん)録(ろく): record of observations; travelogue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経験(けいけん): experience — broader term covering all types of experience, including hands-on participation
  • 知識(ちしき): knowledge — includes book learning and theoretical knowledge; not limited to firsthand observation
  • 体験(たいけん): firsthand experience — emphasizes active participation rather than observation

USAGE:
A somewhat literary or formal word. The expression 見聞(けんぶん)を広(ひろ)げる (to broaden one's horizons) is the most common usage and is often used to describe the value of travel and cultural exchange.
