直営店(ちょくえいてん)

ちょくえいてん
noun
company-operated store; directly managed shop
1. company-operated store; directly managed shop; corporate store
A retail location owned and operated directly by the parent company or brand, as opposed to a franchise or licensed store.
この店(みせ)はメーカーの直営店(ちょくえいてん)です。
This shop is a manufacturer-operated store.
直営店(ちょくえいてん)でしか買(か)えない限定(げんてい)商品(しょうひん)がある。
There are limited-edition products that can only be bought at company-operated stores.
駅前(えきまえ)にブランドの直営店(ちょくえいてん)がオープンして、連日(れんじつ)行列(ぎょうれつ)ができている。
A brand's directly managed store opened in front of the station, and lines have been forming every day.

A compound of 直営(ちょくえい) (direct management) and 店(てん) (store). Refers to retail locations where the brand or manufacturer has direct control over operations, staffing, and product selection.

USAGE:
Important in Japanese retail and business contexts, where the distinction between company-operated and franchise stores matters to consumers. 直営店(ちょくえいてん) often implies higher quality control, access to exclusive products, and authentic brand experience.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 直営店(ちょくえいてん)限定(げんてい) — exclusive to company stores
  • 直営店(ちょくえいてん)を出店(しゅってん)する — to open a company-operated store
  • メーカー直営店(ちょくえいてん) — manufacturer-operated store
  • ブランド直営店(ちょくえいてん) — brand's own store

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • フランチャイズ店(てん) — franchise store (independently owned under license)
  • 系列店(けいれつてん) — affiliated store (part of a business group)
  • 代理店(だいりてん) — agency, dealer (authorized reseller)
