便座(べんざ)

べんざ
noun
toilet seat
1. toilet seat
The seat part of a toilet that one sits on. In Japan, heated toilet seats (温水洗浄便座) are extremely common.
便座(べんざ)を上(あ)げる。
To raise the toilet seat.
この便座(べんざ)は暖(あたた)かくて気持(きも)ちいい。
This toilet seat is warm and comfortable.
温水(おんすい)洗浄(せんじょう)便座(べんざ)は日本(にほん)の家庭(かてい)に広(ひろ)く普及(ふきゅう)している。
Heated bidet toilet seats are widely used in Japanese homes.

A compound of 便(べん) (convenience; toilet) and 座(ざ) (seat). In Japan, advanced toilet seats with heated surfaces, bidet functions, and air dryers are standard in most homes and public restrooms.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 温水(おんすい)洗浄(せんじょう)便座(べんざ) — heated bidet toilet seat
  • 暖房(だんぼう)便座(べんざ) — heated toilet seat
  • 便座(べんざ)カバー — toilet seat cover
  • 便座(べんざ)を下(お)ろす — to lower the toilet seat

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japan is known worldwide for its high-tech toilet seats. The brand name Washlet (ウォシュレット) has become almost generic for any bidet-equipped toilet seat, similar to how Kleenex is used for tissues in English.
