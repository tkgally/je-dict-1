五分五分(ごぶごぶ)

ごぶごぶ
noun; na-adjective
fifty-fifty; even; toss-up
1. fifty-fifty; even; toss-up
An equal split or balance between two sides, outcomes, or possibilities. Used to describe situations where neither side has an advantage.
勝(か)つ確率(かくりつ)は五分五分(ごぶごぶ)だ。
The odds of winning are fifty-fifty.
両(りょう)チームの実力(じつりょく)は五分五分(ごぶごぶ)だと思(おも)う。
I think both teams are evenly matched.
手術(しゅじゅつ)が成功(せいこう)するかどうかは五分五分(ごぶごぶ)だと医者(いしゃ)に言(い)われた。
The doctor told me the chances of the surgery succeeding were fifty-fifty.

An expression using 分(ぶ), a traditional unit meaning one-tenth. Five-tenths plus five-tenths equals a whole, conveying the idea of a perfectly even split. Very commonly used in everyday conversation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 五分五分(ごぶごぶ)の勝負(しょうぶ): an even match
  • 五分五分(ごぶごぶ)の確率(かくりつ): a fifty-fifty chance
  • 五分五分(ごぶごぶ)の戦(たたか)い: an evenly fought battle

USAGE:
Can be used as a noun (五分五分(ごぶごぶ)だ) or as a modifier (五分五分(ごぶごぶ)の). Often used when discussing the outcome of competitions, elections, medical procedures, or any situation with uncertain results.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 互角(ごかく): evenly matched — emphasizes equal ability or strength between opponents
  • 半々(はんはん): half-and-half — used more for splitting things (costs, portions) rather than competition outcomes
  • 拮抗(きっこう): closely contested — more formal, often used in written language
