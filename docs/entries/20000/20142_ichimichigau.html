1. a cut above; distinctly different; with a special twist
Describes something that has a noticeably different and usually superior quality compared to the norm. Implies a subtle but significant difference that sets it apart.
プロの味(あじ)は一味違(いちみちが)う。
A professional's cooking is a cut above.
このブランドのコーヒーは一味違(いちみちが)う。
This brand's coffee has a distinctly different flavor.
今年(ことし)の花火大会(はなびたいかい)は例年(れいねん)とは一味違(いちみちが)った演出(えんしゅつ)だった。
This year's fireworks festival had a distinctly different presentation from the usual.

Composed of 一味(いちみ) (one flavor, a touch) + 違(ちが)う (to differ). Literally 'differs by one flavor,' suggesting a subtle but noticeable difference that makes something stand out.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一味違(いちみちが)った～: a distinctly different ~ (modifying a noun)
  • いつもと一味違(いちみちが)う: different from the usual in a special way
  • 他(ほか)とは一味違(いちみちが)う: a cut above the rest
  • ひと味(あじ)違(ちが)う: variant spelling/reading (same meaning)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひと味(あじ)違(ちが)う: same expression, variant writing — uses the native Japanese reading ひと味(あじ)
  • 一風変(いっぷうか)わった: unusual, eccentric — different in a quirky or unconventional way
  • 格別(かくべつ): exceptional, special — more emphatic; clearly superior quality

USAGE:
Almost always used as a compliment, suggesting something has an appealing quality that distinguishes it from ordinary examples. Very common in food and cooking contexts, but broadly applicable to anything from performances to products to experiences.
