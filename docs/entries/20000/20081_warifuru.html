1. to assign; to allocate; to distribute; to apportion
To divide and distribute tasks, roles, resources, or numbers among people or categories. Common in business and organizational contexts.
仕事(しごと)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る。
To assign tasks.
メンバーに役割(やくわり)を割(わ)り振(ふ)った。
I assigned roles to the members.
限(かぎ)られた予算(よさん)を各部署(かくぶしょ)に公平(こうへい)に割(わ)り振(ふ)る必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to allocate the limited budget fairly to each department.

A godan (う) verb. The noun form 割(わ)り振(ふ)り (allocation, assignment) is also commonly used.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The thing being assigned is marked with を, and the recipient with に.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仕事(しごと)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る: to assign work
  • 役割(やくわり)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る: to assign roles
  • 予算(よさん)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る: to allocate a budget
  • 番号(ばんごう)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る: to assign numbers
  • 担当(たんとう)を割(わ)り振(ふ)る: to assign responsibilities

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 配分(はいぶん)する: to distribute, to apportion — more formal; used for resources, profits, and funds
  • 振(ふ)り分(わ)ける: to divide up, to distribute — similar meaning but less common
  • 割(わ)り当(あ)てる: to assign, to allot — very similar; slightly more formal
