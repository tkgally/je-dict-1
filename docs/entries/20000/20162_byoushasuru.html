1. to depict; to describe; to portray (in words, art, or film)
To represent something vividly through writing, painting, film, or other artistic media. Focuses on detailed, vivid representation rather than simple explanation.
風景(ふうけい)を描写(びょうしゃ)する。
To depict a landscape.
この小説(しょうせつ)は戦争(せんそう)の悲惨(ひさん)さを描写(びょうしゃ)している。
This novel depicts the tragedy of war.
登場人物(とうじょうじんぶつ)の心理(しんり)を細(こま)かく描写(びょうしゃ)するのがこの作家(さっか)の特徴(とくちょう)だ。
It is characteristic of this author to describe the psychology of characters in fine detail.

A する-verb from 描写(びょうしゃ), a compound of 描(びょう) (depict, draw) and 写(しゃ) (copy, capture). A key term in literary criticism, art, and film studies. Emphasizes vivid, detailed representation rather than simple description.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — takes を to mark the thing being depicted.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心理(しんり)描写(びょうしゃ) — psychological portrayal
  • 情景(じょうけい)描写(びょうしゃ) — scene description
  • 人物(じんぶつ)描写(びょうしゃ) — character portrayal
  • 写実的(しゃじつてき)な描写(びょうしゃ) — realistic depiction
  • 描写力(びょうしゃりょく) — descriptive power

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 表現(ひょうげん)する — to express (broader, not limited to vivid representation)
  • 描(えが)く — to draw, to depict (native Japanese, also used figuratively)
  • 記述(きじゅつ)する — to describe in writing (more factual, less artistic)
