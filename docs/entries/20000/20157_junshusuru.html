1. to comply with; to observe; to abide by (rules, laws, regulations)
To follow and adhere to rules, laws, regulations, or standards. A formal word used in legal, business, and institutional contexts.
法律(ほうりつ)を遵守(じゅんしゅ)する。
To comply with the law.
交通(こうつう)ルールを遵守(じゅんしゅ)してください。
Please observe the traffic rules.
全(すべ)ての社員(しゃいん)は社内(しゃない)規定(きてい)を遵守(じゅんしゅ)する義務(ぎむ)がある。
All employees have a duty to comply with company regulations.

A する-verb from 遵守(じゅんしゅ), a compound of 遵(じゅん) (follow, obey) and 守(しゅ) (protect, observe). Very formal; a key term in legal compliance, corporate governance, and regulatory contexts. Sometimes read as そんしゅ, though じゅんしゅ is the standard modern reading.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive — takes を to mark the rules being followed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 法令(ほうれい)遵守(じゅんしゅ) — legal compliance (コンプライアンス)
  • 規則(きそく)を遵守(じゅんしゅ)する — to observe rules
  • 遵守(じゅんしゅ)事項(じこう) — matters to be observed
  • 遵守(じゅんしゅ)義務(ぎむ) — obligation to comply

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 守(まも)る — to protect, to observe (rules) (more casual, everyday)
  • 従(したが)う — to follow, to obey (broader, includes following people)
  • コンプライアンス — compliance (loanword, common in business)
