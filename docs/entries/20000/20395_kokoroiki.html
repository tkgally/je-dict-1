心意気(こころいき)

こころいき
noun
spirit; determination; mettle
1. spirit; determination; mettle; attitude
A person's spirited attitude, determination, or willingness to take on a challenge. Conveys admiration for someone's resolve or generous disposition.
その心意気(こころいき)はすばらしい。
That spirit is wonderful.
職人(しょくにん)の心意気(こころいき)を感(かん)じる仕事(しごと)だ。
This is work where you can feel the craftsman's spirit.
若(わか)い人(ひと)たちの心意気(こころいき)に期待(きたい)して、プロジェクトを任(まか)せることにした。
Counting on the young people's determination, I decided to entrust the project to them.

A warm, positive word that expresses admiration for someone's inner resolve and generous attitude. Often associated with the spirit of Edo-period townspeople (江戸(えど)っ子(こ)) and traditional craftsmanship. It suggests not just determination but a willingness to act with integrity and generosity.

FORMATION:
心(こころ) (heart, mind) + 意気(いき) (spirit, enthusiasm)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 心意気(こころいき)を見(み)せる: to show one's spirit
  • 心意気(こころいき)に感(かん)じる: to be moved by someone's spirit
  • 心意気(こころいき)がいい: to have a fine spirit
  • 心意気(こころいき)を買(か)う: to appreciate someone's determination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 意気込(いきご)み: enthusiasm, eagerness — focuses on the energy before starting something
  • 気概(きがい): spirit, backbone — stronger, emphasizes courage in the face of adversity
  • 志(こころざし): aspiration, ambition — focuses on goals and purpose rather than attitude
