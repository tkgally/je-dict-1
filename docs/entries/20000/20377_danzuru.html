1. to conclude; to judge decisively; to determine with conviction
To make a firm judgment or conclusion about something. Carries a strong, decisive nuance — the speaker is stating their conclusion with authority.
有罪(ゆうざい)と断(だん)ずる。
To judge as guilty.
彼(かれ)の行為(こうい)は不正(ふせい)であると断(だん)じた。
I concluded that his actions were dishonest.
専門家(せんもんか)はこの絵画(かいが)を本物(ほんもの)であると断(だん)じ、美術館(びじゅつかん)での展示(てんじ)が決(き)まった。
The experts determined the painting to be authentic, and its display at the museum was decided.

A literary and formal verb expressing a decisive judgment. More authoritative than 判断(はんだん)する and often used in legal, academic, and journalistic writing. The ずる conjugation is classical and gives the word a formal, weighty tone.

CONJUGATION NOTE:
Conjugates as a ずる verb (サ変(さへん) variant): 断(だん)ずる → 断(だん)じる (more common modern form) → 断(だん)じて (te-form, also used as an emphatic adverb meaning "absolutely").

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～と断(だん)ずる: to conclude that ~
  • ～であると断(だん)じる: to determine that ~ is
  • 断(だん)じて: absolutely, positively (adverbial use)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 判断(はんだん)する: to judge, to decide — more neutral and everyday
  • 結論(けつろん)づける: to conclude — focuses on reaching a conclusion
  • 決(き)めつける: to jump to conclusions — has a negative nuance of being hasty

RELATED EXPRESSION:

  • 断(だん)じて許(ゆる)さない: absolutely will not allow — 断(だん)じて as an emphatic adverb
