上質(じょうしつ)

じょうしつ
noun
high quality; superior quality
1. high quality; superior quality; fine grade
Describes something of notably good quality, especially materials, products, or ingredients. Often used as a noun modifier with の.
上質(じょうしつ)な紙(かみ)を使(つか)う。
To use high-quality paper.
この革(かわ)は上質(じょうしつ)で長持(ながも)ちする。
This leather is high quality and lasts a long time.
上質(じょうしつ)な素材(そざい)を厳選(げんせん)して作(つく)られたバッグです。
This is a bag made from carefully selected high-quality materials.

Describes superior quality of materials, products, or craftsmanship. Composed of 上(じょう) (upper/superior) + 質(しつ) (quality). Used as a noun modifier with な or の.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上質(じょうしつ)な紙(かみ): high-quality paper
  • 上質(じょうしつ)な素材(そざい): fine materials
  • 上質(じょうしつ)な革(かわ): high-grade leather
  • 上質(じょうしつ)な睡眠(すいみん): quality sleep

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 高品質(こうひんしつ): high quality — more technical, often used for manufactured products and standards
  • 良質(りょうしつ): good quality — slightly broader, also used for abstract things like education or information
  • 高級(こうきゅう): high-class, luxury — emphasizes prestige and price rather than material quality
