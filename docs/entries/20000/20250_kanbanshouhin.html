看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)

かんばんしょうひん
noun
signature product; flagship product
1. signature product; flagship product
The main product that a shop or company is known for; the product that represents the brand.
この店(みせ)の看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)はカレーパンだ。
This shop's signature product is their curry bread.
看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)の売(う)り上(あ)げが落(お)ちてきている。
Sales of our flagship product have been declining.
老舗(しにせ)の和菓子屋(わがしや)が看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)の大福(だいふく)をリニューアルして、若(わか)い世代(せだい)にも人気(にんき)が出(で)てきた。
A long-established Japanese sweets shop revamped their signature daifuku, and it has become popular with younger generations too.

Composed of 看板(かんばん) (signboard, figuratively "face" or "brand identity") + 商品(しょうひん) (product). The word uses 看板(かんばん) metaphorically — just as a signboard represents a shop, the 看板商品(かんばんしょうひん) is the product that represents the business.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)を開発(かいはつ)する: to develop a flagship product
  • 看板商品(かんばんしょうひん)を売(う)り出(だ)す: to launch a signature product
  • 店(みせ)の看板商品(かんばんしょうひん): a shop's signature item

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 看板(かんばん)メニュー: signature menu item (for restaurants)
  • 看板(かんばん)娘(むすめ): the "face" of a shop — an attractive employee who draws customers

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目玉商品(めだましょうひん): featured product, loss leader — a product offered at a special price to attract customers
  • 主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん): main product — the product generating the most revenue
  • 名物(めいぶつ): local specialty, famous product — a product a region is known for
