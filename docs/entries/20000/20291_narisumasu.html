1. to impersonate; to pose as; to pretend to be someone else
To disguise oneself as another person or assume a false identity, typically with deceptive intent. Used for both physical disguises and online impersonation.
他人(たにん)になりすます。
To impersonate someone else.
警察官(けいさつかん)になりすまして家(いえ)に入(はい)った。
He posed as a police officer and entered the house.
SNSで有名人(ゆうめいじん)になりすましたアカウントが増(ふ)えている。
There is an increasing number of accounts impersonating celebrities on social media.

Literally "to completely become" — from なる (to become) + すます (to complete/finish). The word implies deliberately assuming a false identity, not merely acting or role-playing.

USAGE:
Very common in news and online contexts. The noun form なりすまし is widely used for identity fraud and fake accounts. Often takes に to mark the person being impersonated.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • なりすまし詐欺(さぎ) — impersonation fraud
  • なりすましアカウント — fake account, impersonation account
  • なりすまし犯罪(はんざい) — identity crime
  • 本人(ほんにん)になりすます — to pose as the real person

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 装(よそお)う — to pretend, to feign (broader; can be emotions or appearances, not just identity)
  • 偽(いつわ)る — to deceive, to falsify (more formal; focuses on the dishonesty rather than the disguise)
  • ふりをする — to pretend, to act as if (lighter nuance; can be playful or casual)
