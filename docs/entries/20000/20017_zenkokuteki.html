全国的(ぜんこくてき)

ぜんこくてき
na-adjective
nationwide; countrywide; national
1. nationwide; countrywide; national
Extending across or affecting the entire country. Used to describe phenomena, trends, events, or conditions that are not limited to one region.
全国的(ぜんこくてき)に有名(ゆうめい)な店(みせ)だ。
It's a nationally famous restaurant.
全国的(ぜんこくてき)に雨(あめ)の予報(よほう)が出(で)ている。
A rain forecast has been issued nationwide.
この問題(もんだい)は一部(いちぶ)の地域(ちいき)だけでなく、全国的(ぜんこくてき)な広(ひろ)がりを見(み)せている。
This problem is not limited to certain areas — it is spreading nationwide.

Formed from 全国(ぜんこく) (the whole country) + 的(てき) (suffix making な-adjectives, similar to English "-wide" or "-al"). Very common in news, weather reports, and discussions of social trends.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全国的(ぜんこくてき)に有名(ゆうめい): nationally famous
  • 全国的(ぜんこくてき)な傾向(けいこう): nationwide trend
  • 全国的(ぜんこくてき)な広(ひろ)がり: nationwide spread
  • 全国的(ぜんこくてき)に展開(てんかい)する: to expand nationwide

USAGE:
Used both as an adverb with に (全国的(ぜんこくてき)に) and as a modifier with な (全国的(ぜんこくてき)な). Extremely common in weather forecasts: 全国的(ぜんこくてき)に晴(は)れ (clear skies nationwide).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国内(こくない): domestic, within the country — focuses on being inside the country rather than spanning it
  • 世界的(せかいてき): worldwide, global — the international equivalent
  • 地域的(ちいきてき): regional — limited to a particular area
