1. direct rays; direct sunlight; shining directly
Light or sunlight that strikes directly without being diffused, reflected, or filtered. Most commonly used for direct sunlight.
直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう)を避(さ)ける。
To avoid direct sunlight.
薬(くすり)は直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう)の当(あ)たらない場所(ばしょ)に保管(ほかん)してください。
Please store medicine in a place not exposed to direct sunlight.
真夏(まなつ)の直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう)の下(した)で長時間(ちょうじかん)作業(さぎょう)するのは危険(きけん)だ。
Working for long hours under the direct midsummer sun is dangerous.
2. direct fire; direct hit; point-blank shot
Shooting or firing directly at a target in a straight line, as opposed to indirect or arced fire.
砲弾(ほうだん)が直射(ちょくしゃ)で命中(めいちゅう)した。
The shell scored a direct hit.
戦車(せんしゃ)が敵陣(てきじん)に直射(ちょくしゃ)した。
The tank fired directly at the enemy position.
近距離(きんきょり)から直射(ちょくしゃ)すれば、厚(あつ)い装甲(そうこう)でも貫通(かんつう)できる。
If you fire directly from close range, you can penetrate even thick armor.

Composed of 直(ちょく) (direct/straight) + 射(しゃ) (shoot/radiate). Most frequently encountered in the compound 直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう) (direct sunlight).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう): direct sunlight
  • 直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう)を避(さ)ける: to avoid direct sunlight
  • 直射(ちょくしゃ)する: to shine directly; to fire directly
  • 直射(ちょくしゃ)砲(ほう): direct-fire gun

USAGE:
Sense 1 is far more common in everyday life. The phrase 直射日光(ちょくしゃにっこう)を避(さ)けて保管(ほかん) (store away from direct sunlight) appears on countless product labels and packaging in Japan.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日差(ひざ)し: sunshine, sunlight — general word for sunlight, not necessarily direct
  • 間接(かんせつ): indirect — the opposite concept; 間接照明(かんせつしょうめい) means indirect lighting
