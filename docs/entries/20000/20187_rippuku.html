1. anger; offense; indignation
A somewhat formal word for being angered or offended by someone's words or actions. Used as a suru-verb.
彼(かれ)は立腹(りっぷく)している。
He is angry.
無礼(ぶれい)な態度(たいど)に立腹(りっぷく)した。
I was offended by the rude attitude.
お客様(きゃくさま)が対応(たいおう)の悪(わる)さに大変(たいへん)立腹(りっぷく)されているようです。
The customer appears to be very upset about the poor service.

A relatively formal way to express anger. Composed of 立(りつ) (to rise) + 腹(ふく/ぷく) (belly/stomach), literally "the stomach rises" — related to the Japanese association of the belly with emotions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立腹(りっぷく)する: to be angered, to take offense
  • 大変(たいへん)立腹(りっぷく)する: to be very angry
  • ご立腹(りっぷく): your anger (respectful)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 怒(いか)り: anger — the most general word for anger
  • 憤慨(ふんがい): indignation — anger at something perceived as unjust
  • 激怒(げきど): fury, rage — much stronger intensity

REGISTER:
立腹(りっぷく) is more formal and restrained than 怒(おこ)る. It often appears in business or formal contexts, especially in the respectful form ご立腹(りっぷく).
