陣痛(じんつう)

じんつう
noun
labor pains; contractions
1. labor pains; contractions
The painful uterine contractions that occur during childbirth. Can also be used figuratively to describe the painful process of bringing something new into being.
陣痛(じんつう)が始(はじ)まった。
The contractions have started.
陣痛(じんつう)の間隔(かんかく)が短(みじか)くなってきた。
The intervals between contractions are getting shorter.
夜中(よなか)に陣痛(じんつう)が始(はじ)まり、急(いそ)いで病院(びょういん)に向(む)かった。
Contractions started in the middle of the night, and we rushed to the hospital.

Compound of 陣(じん) (battle formation, camp) + 痛(つう) (pain). The character 陣(じん) here alludes to the waves of pain that come in surges, like successive battle charges.

USAGE:
A medical and everyday term used when discussing pregnancy and childbirth. Often appears with 間隔(かんかく) (interval) since timing the frequency of contractions is important for determining when to go to the hospital. Occasionally used figuratively — 産(う)みの陣痛(じんつう) ("birth pains") can describe the painful early stages of creating something new, such as a reform or new system.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 陣痛(じんつう)が始(はじ)まる — contractions begin
  • 陣痛(じんつう)の間隔(かんかく) — interval between contractions
  • 陣痛(じんつう)促進剤(そくしんざい) — labor-inducing drug
  • 陣痛(じんつう)に耐(た)える — to endure labor pains

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分娩(ぶんべん) — delivery, childbirth (the medical process itself)
  • 出産(しゅっさん) — giving birth, childbirth (broader term)
  • 産(う)みの苦(くる)しみ — birth pains (more figurative, the agony of creation)
