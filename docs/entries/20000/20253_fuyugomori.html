1. staying indoors for winter; wintering; winter hibernation
Staying indoors or secluded during the cold winter months. Used for both people choosing to stay home and animals hibernating.
今年(ことし)の冬(ふゆ)は冬(ふゆ)ごもりしよう。
Let's hibernate at home this winter.
熊(くま)は冬(ふゆ)ごもりの季節(きせつ)に入(はい)った。
The bears have entered their hibernation season.
雪国(ゆきぐに)では冬(ふゆ)ごもりの間(あいだ)に家(いえ)の中(なか)で手仕事(てしごと)をするのが昔(むかし)からの習慣(しゅうかん)だった。
In snow country, it was a longstanding custom to do handicrafts indoors during the winter seclusion.

Composed of 冬(ふゆ) (winter) + ごもり (seclusion, from 籠(こも)る — to shut oneself in). Can be written with kanji as 冬籠(ふゆごも)り. Used both literally for animal hibernation and figuratively for people choosing to stay home during cold weather. Also used as a suru verb: 冬(ふゆ)ごもりする.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冬(ふゆ)ごもりする: to stay indoors for winter
  • 冬(ふゆ)ごもりの季節(きせつ): hibernation season
  • 冬(ふゆ)ごもりの準備(じゅんび): preparations for wintering

CULTURAL NOTE:
A seasonal word (季語(きご)) for winter in haiku poetry. In Japan's snowy regions, winter seclusion was historically a real part of life, as heavy snow made travel impossible for months.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冬眠(とうみん): hibernation — specifically biological hibernation of animals
  • 引(ひ)きこもり: staying shut in, social withdrawal — negative connotation, not seasonal
  • 巣(す)ごもり: nesting, staying home — a broader term popular since the pandemic
