縫(ぬ)い物(もの)

ぬいもの
noun
sewing; needlework
1. sewing; needlework; sewing work
The activity of sewing by hand, or the items being sewn. Refers to practical sewing rather than decorative embroidery.
母(はは)は縫(ぬ)い物(もの)が得意(とくい)だ。
My mother is good at sewing.
夜(よる)になると縫(ぬ)い物(もの)をして過(す)ごす。
I spend my evenings doing needlework.
祖母(そぼ)は目(め)が悪(わる)くなって、縫(ぬ)い物(もの)ができなくなったと嘆(なげ)いている。
My grandmother laments that her eyesight has gotten worse and she can no longer do sewing.

A compound of 縫(ぬ)う (to sew) and 物(もの) (thing). Refers both to the activity of hand sewing and to the items being sewn. Has a warm, domestic connotation.

USAGE:
Typically refers to hand sewing done at home, including mending clothes, making simple garments, or hemming. More everyday and informal than 裁縫(さいほう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 縫(ぬ)い物(もの)をする — to do sewing
  • 縫(ぬ)い物(もの)が得意(とくい) — to be good at sewing
  • 縫(ぬ)い物(もの)道具(どうぐ) — sewing supplies

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裁縫(さいほう) — sewing, needlework (more formal, broader scope)
  • 手芸(しゅげい) — handicrafts (broader, includes knitting, crochet, etc.)
  • 刺繍(ししゅう) — embroidery (decorative stitching)
