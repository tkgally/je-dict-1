1. to go to the United States; to travel to America
To travel to or move to the United States. Used for both short trips and longer relocations. A formal word commonly seen in news and biographical writing.
来月(らいげつ)渡米(とべい)する予定(よてい)です。
I plan to go to the United States next month.
彼女(かのじょ)は留学(りゅうがく)のために渡米(とべい)した。
She went to the United States to study abroad.
若(わか)くして渡米(とべい)し、現地(げんち)の大学(だいがく)で医学(いがく)を学(まな)んだ。
He went to America at a young age and studied medicine at a university there.

Formed from 渡(と) (to cross over) + 米(べい) (America, short for 米国(べいこく)). This pattern of 渡(と) + country abbreviation is productive in Japanese.

ETYMOLOGY:
The 渡(と) prefix originally referred to crossing the sea. 米(べい) is the single-character abbreviation for America (米国(べいこく)).

RELATED COUNTRY-CROSSING WORDS:

  • 渡英(とえい)する: to go to Britain
  • 渡仏(とふつ)する: to go to France
  • 渡独(とどく)する: to go to Germany
  • 渡航(とこう)する: to travel abroad (general)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 渡航(とこう)する: to travel overseas — general term for international travel; not country-specific
  • アメリカに行(い)く: to go to America — everyday, casual equivalent
