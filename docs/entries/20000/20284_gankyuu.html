眼球(がんきゅう)

がんきゅう
noun
eyeball
1. eyeball; eye globe
The spherical organ of sight. Used in medical, scientific, and general contexts.
眼球(がんきゅう)を動(うご)かす。
To move one's eyeballs.
眼球(がんきゅう)の構造(こうぞう)について学(まな)んだ。
I learned about the structure of the eyeball.
パソコンを長時間(ちょうじかん)使(つか)うと、眼球(がんきゅう)が乾(かわ)きやすくなる。
Using a computer for long periods makes the eyeballs prone to drying out.

Compound of 眼(がん) (eye) + 球(きゅう) (ball, sphere). The standard term for the physical eyeball.

USAGE:
Used in both medical/scientific contexts and everyday language. More clinical than 目(め) (eye), which is the everyday word. 眼球(がんきゅう) specifically refers to the physical globe of the eye, while 目(め) can refer to the eye, eyesight, or vision more broadly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 眼球(がんきゅう)運動(うんどう) — eye movement
  • 眼球(がんきゅう)が乾(かわ)く — eyeballs dry out
  • 眼球(がんきゅう)の表面(ひょうめん) — surface of the eyeball
  • 眼球(がんきゅう)を動(うご)かす — to move one's eyeballs

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目(め) — eye (everyday, broader usage)
  • 目玉(めだま) — eyeball (more colloquial; also means "highlight" or "featured item")
  • 瞳(ひとみ) — pupil, eye (poetic, often used for the iris/pupil area)
