1. mushroom picking; mushroom hunting; mushroom foraging
The recreational activity of going into the woods or mountains to gather wild mushrooms. A popular autumn pastime in Japan.
秋(あき)に茸狩(きのこが)りに行(い)く。
I go mushroom picking in autumn.
茸狩(きのこが)りで松茸(まつたけ)を見(み)つけた。
I found matsutake mushrooms while mushroom hunting.
茸狩(きのこが)りをするときは毒(どく)きのこに気(き)をつけなければならない。
When mushroom picking, you must be careful of poisonous mushrooms.

Composed of 茸(きのこ) (mushroom) + 狩(が)り (hunting, gathering). Part of a family of ～狩(が)り compound words for gathering activities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 茸狩(きのこが)りに行(い)く: to go mushroom picking
  • 茸狩(きのこが)りをする: to do mushroom picking
  • 茸狩(きのこが)りのシーズン: mushroom picking season

RELATED ～狩(が)り WORDS:

  • 潮干狩(しおひが)り: clam digging
  • 紅葉狩(もみじが)り: autumn leaf viewing
  • ぶどう狩(が)り: grape picking
  • いちご狩(が)り: strawberry picking

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Mushroom picking is a popular autumn activity in mountainous areas of Japan, typically from September to November. Japan has a rich mushroom culture, with prized species like 松茸(まつたけ) (matsutake), 椎茸(しいたけ) (shiitake), and 舞茸(まいたけ) (maitake). However, 毒(どく)きのこ (poisonous mushrooms) are a real hazard, and every year there are cases of poisoning from misidentified wild mushrooms. Also commonly written as きのこ狩(が)り with hiragana for the mushroom part.
