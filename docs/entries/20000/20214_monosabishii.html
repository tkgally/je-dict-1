物寂(ものさび)しい

ものさびしい
adjective
lonely; desolate; forlorn
1. lonely; desolate; forlorn; melancholy
A vague, quiet sense of loneliness or melancholy, often evoked by one's surroundings rather than a specific cause.
秋(あき)の夕暮(ゆうぐ)れは物寂(ものさび)しい。
Autumn evenings feel lonely.
人通(ひとどお)りのない商店街(しょうてんがい)が物寂(ものさび)しかった。
The deserted shopping street felt desolate.
子(こ)どもたちが巣立(すだ)った後(あと)の家(いえ)は、どこか物寂(ものさび)しい雰囲気(ふんいき)が漂(ただよ)っていた。
After the children left home, a forlorn atmosphere lingered in the house.

Composed of 物(もの) (a prefix adding a vague, indefinite quality) + 寂(さび)しい (lonely). The 物(もの) prefix softens and deepens the loneliness, giving it a more atmospheric, poetic quality — not sharp personal loneliness, but a quiet melancholy felt in one's surroundings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物寂(ものさび)しい雰囲気(ふんいき): a desolate atmosphere
  • 物寂(ものさび)しい風景(ふうけい): a lonely landscape
  • 物寂(ものさび)しい気持(きも)ち: a forlorn feeling
  • どこか物寂(ものさび)しい: somehow lonely

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 寂(さび)しい: lonely — the base word, more direct and personal
  • 侘(わび)しい: wretched, forlorn — implies poverty or wretchedness alongside loneliness
  • 物悲(ものがな)しい: melancholy, sorrowful — similar 物(もの)-prefix pattern, but sadness rather than loneliness
