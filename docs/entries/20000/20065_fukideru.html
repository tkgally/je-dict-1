1. to gush out; to spurt out; to spout
Liquid, gas, or substance erupting outward
水(みず)が地面(じめん)から吹(ふ)き出(で)た。
Water gushed out from the ground.
蒸気(じょうき)がパイプから吹(ふ)き出(で)ている。
Steam is spouting from the pipe.
火山(かざん)から溶岩(ようがん)が吹(ふ)き出(で)た。
Lava gushed out from the volcano.
2. to break out; to appear on the surface
Sweat, pimples, rashes appearing on the skin
額(ひたい)に汗(あせ)が吹(ふ)き出(で)た。
Sweat broke out on my forehead.
顔(かお)にニキビが吹(ふ)き出(で)た。
Pimples broke out on my face.
緊張(きんちょう)で全身(ぜんしん)に汗(あせ)が吹(ふ)き出(で)てきた。
Sweat broke out all over my body from nervousness.

An intransitive compound verb combining 吹(ふ)く (to blow) + 出(で)る (to come out). Describes something emerging forcefully from inside to outside. Used for liquids, gases, and things appearing on the skin.

TRANSITIVITY: intransitive (transitive pair: 吹(ふ)き出(だ)す)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)が吹(ふ)き出(で)る: water gushes out
  • 汗(あせ)が吹(ふ)き出(で)る: sweat breaks out
  • ニキビが吹(ふ)き出(で)る: pimples break out
  • 蒸気(じょうき)が吹(ふ)き出(で)る: steam spouts out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吹(ふ)き出(だ)す: to spout/burst out — the transitive counterpart; also means to burst out laughing
  • 噴(ふ)き出(だ)す: to erupt/spew — emphasizes violent eruption; also means to burst out laughing
  • 溢(あふ)れる: to overflow — liquid exceeding its container, without the forceful eruption nuance
