A technical term for something containing a specific substance or component. Composed of 含(がん) (contain/include) + 有(ゆう) (have/possess). Primarily used in scientific, medical, and regulatory contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 含有量(がんゆうりょう): content (amount contained)
  • 含有率(がんゆうりつ): content ratio; percentage contained
  • 含有(がんゆう)する: to contain (a substance)
  • 含有(がんゆう)成分(せいぶん): contained components/ingredients
  • アルコール含有(がん��う): alcohol-containing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 含(ふく)む: to contain, to include — the everyday verb; used broadly in both literal and figurative senses
  • 配合(はいごう): blending, compounding — mixing ingredients deliberately, often in cosmetics or food
  • 成分(せい���ん): ingredient, component — what a substance is made of

USAGE:
含有(がんゆう) is a written/technical word. In everyday speech, 含(ふく)む or 入(はい)っている would be used instead. 含有(がんゆう) commonly appears on product labels, nutritional information, and scientific papers.
