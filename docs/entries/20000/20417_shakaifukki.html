1. return to society; social reintegration; rehabilitation
The process of returning to normal social life after illness, injury, incarceration, or a long absence. Used in medical, legal, and general contexts.
社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)を目指(めざ)している。
I am aiming to return to society.
リハビリを続(つづ)けて、ようやく社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)できた。
After continuing rehabilitation, I was finally able to return to society.
受刑者(じゅけいしゃ)の社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)を支援(しえん)するプログラムが必要(ひつよう)だと言(い)われている。
It is said that programs to support the reintegration of inmates into society are needed.

Composed of 社会(しゃかい) (society) + 復帰(ふっき) (return, reinstatement). A widely used term in medical rehabilitation, criminal justice, and general conversation about people resuming normal life.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)する: to return to society
  • 社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)を目指(めざ)す: to aim for reintegration into society
  • 社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)を支援(しえん)する: to support someone's return to society
  • 社会復帰(しゃかいふっき)プログラム: reintegration program

USAGE:
Used for people returning to normal life after: serious illness or injury (especially long hospital stays), mental health issues, substance abuse, imprisonment, or long periods of social withdrawal (引(ひ)きこもり).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 復帰(ふっき): return, comeback — the more general term for returning to any role or position
  • 更生(こうせい): rehabilitation, reform — emphasizes moral or behavioral change, especially for offenders
  • 社会参加(しゃかいさんか): social participation — broader concept of engaging in society
