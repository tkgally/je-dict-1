長細(ながぼそ)い

ながぼそい
adjective
long and thin; elongated and narrow
1. long and thin; elongated and narrow; slender
Describing an object or shape that is notably long relative to its width. A compound adjective combining the concepts of length and thinness.
長細(ながぼそ)い箱(はこ)。
A long, thin box.
長細(ながぼそ)い形(かたち)のパンを買(か)った。
I bought a long, narrow loaf of bread.
この島(しま)は南北(なんぼく)に長細(ながぼそ)く、車(くるま)で端(はし)から端(はし)まで三時間(さんじかん)かかる。
This island is long and narrow from north to south, and it takes three hours to drive from one end to the other.

A compound adjective from 長(なが)い (long) + 細(ほそ)い (thin), with rendaku changing ほそい to ぼそい. Also written as 長(なが)細(ぼそ)い. A straightforward descriptive word used in everyday conversation.

USAGE:
Used for physical objects and shapes. The variant 細長(ほそなが)い is more common in formal writing and has the same meaning, but reverses the order of the components.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 長細(ながぼそ)い形(かたち) — a long, thin shape
  • 長細(ながぼそ)い箱(はこ) — a long, narrow box
  • 長細(ながぼそ)い島(しま) — a long, narrow island
  • 長細(ながぼそ)い顔(かお) — a long, narrow face

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 細長(ほそなが)い — long and thin (same meaning; more common in formal writing)
  • 細(ほそ)い — thin, slender (thinness only, without the length component)
  • 長(なが)い — long (length only, without the thinness component)
