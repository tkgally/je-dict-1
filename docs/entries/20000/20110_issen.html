一線(いっせん)

いっせん
noun
front line; line; boundary
1. front line; forefront; top level
The leading position in a field, profession, or activity. Often used to describe someone who is active at the highest level.
第一線(だいいっせん)で活躍(かつやく)する。
To be active on the front line.
彼女(かのじょ)は長年(ながねん)業界(ぎょうかい)の一線(いっせん)で活躍(かつやく)してきた。
She has been active at the forefront of the industry for many years.
引退(いんたい)して一線(いっせん)を退(しりぞ)いた後(あと)も、後進(こうしん)の育成(いくせい)に力(ちから)を注(そそ)いでいる。
Even after retiring from the front line, he devotes his energy to training the next generation.
2. line; boundary; dividing line
A line that separates or divides, especially a moral or social boundary that should not be crossed.
一線(いっせん)を越(こ)えてはいけない。
You must not cross the line.
友情(ゆうじょう)と恋愛(れんあい)の一線(いっせん)は難(むずか)しい。
The line between friendship and romance is a difficult one.
冗談(じょうだん)と侮辱(ぶじょく)の間(あいだ)には一線(いっせん)を画(かく)すべきだ。
One should draw a clear line between jokes and insults.

Composed of 一(いっ) (one) + 線(せん) (line). Has two primary uses: referring to the leading edge of a field, and to a boundary or dividing line.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一線(いっせん)で活躍(かつやく)する: to be active at the forefront
  • 一線(いっせん)を退(しりぞ)く: to retire from the front line
  • 一線(いっせん)を越(こ)える: to cross the line
  • 一線(いっせん)を画(かく)す: to draw a line (between); to distinguish clearly
  • 第一線(だいいっせん): the front line; the forefront

USAGE:
The expression 一線(いっせん)を画(かく)す is particularly common and means to make a clear distinction or to set oneself apart from others. 一線(いっせん)を越(こ)える often has moral or social implications — crossing a boundary that shouldn't be crossed.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最前線(さいぜんせん): the very front line — more emphatic than 一線(いっせん)
  • 境界線(きょうかいせん): boundary line — more concrete and geographical than the figurative 一線(いっせん)
