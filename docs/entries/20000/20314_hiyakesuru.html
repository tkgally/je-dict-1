1. to get sunburned; to get a tan; to become darkened by the sun
For skin to darken or burn from exposure to sunlight. Covers both deliberate tanning and accidental sunburn.
夏(なつ)に日焼(ひや)けした。
I got tanned in the summer.
海(うみ)で日焼(ひや)けして肌(はだ)が赤(あか)くなった。
I got sunburned at the beach and my skin turned red.
日焼(ひや)け止(ど)めを塗(ぬ)らないと日焼(ひや)けしやすい。
If you don't put on sunscreen, you'll burn easily.
2. to fade from sunlight; to become discolored by sun exposure
For paper, fabric, photographs, or other materials to fade, yellow, or become damaged from prolonged exposure to sunlight.
本(ほん)が日焼(ひや)けした。
The book faded from sunlight.
カーテンが日焼(ひや)けして色(いろ)が褪(あ)せた。
The curtains faded from sun exposure.
窓際(まどぎわ)に置(お)いていたので、写真(しゃしん)がすっかり日焼(ひや)けしてしまった。
Because I had left it by the window, the photograph became completely sun-faded.

A する-verb from the noun 日焼(ひや)け, composed of 日(ひ) (sun) + 焼(や)け (burning, from 焼(や)ける). The noun 日焼(ひや)け is used independently for sunburn/tan or sun damage.

USAGE:
For skin (sense 1), Japanese does not strongly distinguish between "tan" (gradual browning) and "sunburn" (painful reddening) — both are 日焼(ひや)け. Context clarifies. The compound 日焼(ひや)け止(ど)め (sunscreen) is one of the most common related words.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日焼(ひや)け止(ど)め — sunscreen
  • 日焼(ひや)けサロン — tanning salon
  • 日焼(ひや)け跡(あと) — tan lines; sun marks
  • 日焼(ひや)けした肌(はだ) — tanned/sunburned skin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 焼(や)ける — to burn (broader; cooking, fire, jealousy)
  • 色(いろ)褪(あ)せる — to fade, to lose color (general fading, not only from sun)
  • 変色(へんしょく)する — to discolor, to change color (technical; any color change)
