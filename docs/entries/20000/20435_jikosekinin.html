自己責任(じこせきにん)

じこせきにん
noun
self-responsibility; at one's own risk
1. self-responsibility; personal responsibility; at one's own risk
The principle that individuals bear responsibility for the consequences of their own choices and actions, rather than relying on others or institutions to protect them from negative outcomes.
投資(とうし)は自己責任(じこせきにん)です。
Investing is at your own risk.
ここから先(さき)は自己責任(じこせきにん)でお願(ねが)いします。
Beyond this point, proceed at your own risk.
海外(かいがい)で危険(きけん)な地域(ちいき)に行(い)くのは自己責任(じこせきにん)だという意見(いけん)もある。
There are also opinions that traveling to dangerous areas overseas is a matter of personal responsibility.

A compound of 自己(じこ) (self) and 責任(せきにん) (responsibility). This concept became a major topic of public debate in Japan in 2004 when Japanese citizens were taken hostage in Iraq, and critics argued they should bear the consequences of traveling to a war zone.

USAGE:
Commonly seen in investment disclaimers, legal warnings, and discussions about personal accountability. Often used adverbially with で: 自己責任(じこせきにん)で (at one's own risk). The term can carry a blaming nuance when directed at others.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己責任(じこせきにん)で行(おこな)う — to do at one's own risk
  • 自己責任(じこせきにん)原則(げんそく) — principle of self-responsibility
  • 自己責任(じこせきにん)論(ろん) — self-responsibility argument
  • 自己責任(じこせきにん)を問(と)う — to hold someone personally responsible

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 責任(せきにん) — responsibility (general)
  • 自業自得(じごうじとく) — reaping what you sow (stronger negative nuance)
  • 連帯責任(れんたいせきにん) — collective responsibility (opposite concept)
