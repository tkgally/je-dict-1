今昔(こんじゃく)

こんじゃく
noun
past and present; then and now
1. past and present; then and now
The contrast or comparison between how things were in the past and how they are now. Often used to reflect on changes over time.
今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん)がある。
I feel a sense of how things have changed.
この写真(しゃしん)を見(み)ると今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん)に堪(た)えない。
Looking at this photo, I can't help but feel how much things have changed.
東京(とうきょう)の今昔(こんじゃく)を写真(しゃしん)で比較(ひかく)する展示(てんじ)が人気(にんき)を集(あつ)めている。
An exhibition comparing photos of Tokyo then and now is drawing crowds.

A literary compound expressing the contrast between past and present. Most commonly encountered in the set phrase 今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん) (a feeling of how times have changed) and in the title of the famous literary work 今昔(こんじゃく)物語集(ものがたりしゅう).

FORMATION:
今(こん) (now, present) + 昔(じゃく) (past, old times). Note the on'yomi reading じゃく for 昔(じゃく), which differs from the common kun'yomi むかし.

KEY EXPRESSIONS:

  • 今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん): a feeling of how times have changed — the most common usage
  • 今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん)に堪(た)えない: to be overwhelmed by the sense of change
  • 今昔(こんじゃく)物語集(ものがたりしゅう): Konjaku Monogatari (a Heian-period collection of tales from Japan, China, and India)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 昔(むかし): the past, old times — everyday word
  • 往時(おうじ): the past, former days — literary
  • 隔世(かくせい)の感(かん): a sense of being in a different era — similar nuance to 今昔(こんじゃく)の感(かん)
