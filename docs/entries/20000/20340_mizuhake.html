水(みず)はけ

みずはけ
noun
drainage; water permeability
1. drainage; water permeability; how well water drains
The ability of soil, ground, or a surface to allow water to drain away. Commonly discussed in gardening, agriculture, and construction contexts.
この土(つち)は水(みず)はけが良(よ)い。
This soil has good drainage.
水(みず)はけの悪(わる)い場所(ばしょ)に水(みず)たまりができた。
Puddles formed in places with poor drainage.
花壇(かだん)の水(みず)はけを良(よ)くするために、砂利(じゃり)を混(ま)ぜた。
I mixed in gravel to improve the drainage of the flower bed.

Composed of 水(みず) (water) + はけ (draining away). Can also be written 水捌(みずは)け. Almost always appears in the patterns 水(みず)はけが良(よ)い (good drainage) or 水(みず)はけが悪(わる)い (poor drainage).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)はけが良(よ)い: good drainage
  • 水(みず)はけが悪(わる)い: poor drainage
  • 水(みず)はけを改善(かいぜん)する: to improve drainage
  • 水(みず)はけの良(よ)い土(つち): well-draining soil

USAGE:
Essential vocabulary for gardening in Japanese. Many plants require 水(みず)はけの良(よ)い土(つち) (well-draining soil), making this a practical word for anyone interested in Japanese gardening or agriculture.
