頼(たよ)りになる

たよりになる
expression
reliable; dependable; someone you can count on
1. reliable; dependable; someone or something you can count on
Describes a person, thing, or quality that can be trusted and relied upon. Functions as an adjectival expression modifying nouns or as a predicate.
頼(たよ)りになる人(ひと)だ。
He's a reliable person.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつも頼(たよ)りになる。
She is always dependable.
困(こま)ったときに頼(たよ)りになるのは、やっぱり家族(かぞく)だ。
When you're in trouble, family is what you can really count on.

Composed of 頼(たよ)り (reliance, dependence — the noun form of 頼(たよ)る) + になる (to become/serve as). Literally "serves as something to rely on." One of the most common ways to express reliability in everyday Japanese.

USAGE:
Used both as a predicate (頼(たよ)りになる) and as a noun modifier (頼(たよ)りになる人(ひと)). The negative form 頼(たよ)りにならない means "unreliable" and is equally common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頼(たよ)りになる人(ひと) — reliable person
  • 頼(たよ)りになる存在(そんざい) — dependable presence
  • 頼(たよ)りになる味方(みかた) — reliable ally
  • 頼(たよ)りにならない — unreliable, undependable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 信頼(しんらい)できる — trustworthy (more formal; emphasizes trust)
  • 当(あ)てになる — can be counted on (often used in the negative: 当(あ)てにならない)
  • 心強(こころづよ)い — reassuring, encouraging (emotional sense of feeling supported)
