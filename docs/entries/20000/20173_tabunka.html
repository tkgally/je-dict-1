多文化(たぶんか)

たぶんか
noun
multicultural; relating to multiple cultures
1. multicultural; relating to multiple cultures
The coexistence of multiple cultures within a society or community. Used both as a standalone noun and as a prefix modifying other nouns.
多文化(たぶんか)共生(きょうせい)を目指(めざ)す。
To aim for multicultural coexistence.
多文化(たぶんか)社会(しゃかい)では相互(そうご)理解(りかい)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Mutual understanding is important in a multicultural society.
日本(にほん)でも多文化(たぶんか)教育(きょういく)の必要性(ひつようせい)が高(たか)まっている。
In Japan too, the need for multicultural education is increasing.

A compound of 多(た) (many) and 文化(ぶんか) (culture). Increasingly used in Japanese public discourse as Japan becomes more diverse through immigration and internationalization.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多文化(たぶんか)共生(きょうせい) — multicultural coexistence (a key term in Japanese social policy)
  • 多文化(たぶんか)社会(しゃかい) — multicultural society
  • 多文化(たぶんか)教育(きょういく) — multicultural education
  • 多文化(たぶんか)理解(りかい) — cross-cultural understanding
  • 多文化(たぶんか)主義(しゅぎ) — multiculturalism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 異文化(いぶんか) — foreign culture, cross-cultural (focuses on difference)
  • 国際的(こくさいてき) — international (broader scope)
  • 多様性(たようせい) — diversity (more general, not limited to culture)
