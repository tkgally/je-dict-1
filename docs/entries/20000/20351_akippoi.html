1. easily bored; fickle; quick to lose interest
Describes a personality trait of getting bored or losing interest quickly. Used to characterize someone who starts things enthusiastically but soon tires of them.
彼(かれ)は飽(あ)きっぽい。
He gets bored easily.
飽(あ)きっぽい性格(せいかく)を直(なお)したい。
I want to fix my fickle personality.
子供(こども)は飽(あ)きっぽいから、長(なが)い映画(えいが)は向(む)かない。
Children get bored easily, so long movies aren't suited for them.

Describes someone who loses interest in things quickly — the kind of person who picks up hobbies but drops them soon after. It carries a mildly negative nuance, suggesting a lack of perseverance.

FORMATION:
飽(あ)きる (to get tired of) + っぽい (tendency suffix). The っぽい suffix indicates a habitual tendency or characteristic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飽(あ)きっぽい性格(せいかく): fickle personality
  • 飽(あ)きっぽい人(ひと): someone who gets bored easily
  • 飽(あ)きっぽくて続(つづ)かない: too fickle to stick with something

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 三日坊主(みっかぼうず): someone who can't stick with anything (literally "three-day monk") — emphasizes inability to continue, while 飽(あ)きっぽい focuses on the tendency to lose interest
  • 気(き)まぐれ: whimsical, capricious — broader meaning covering unpredictable behavior, not just boredom
