耳(みみ)を貸(か)す

みみをかす
expression
to listen; to lend an ear; to heed
1. to listen; to lend an ear; to heed; to pay attention to what someone says
To be willing to listen to someone's words, request, or advice. Often used in the negative to mean refusing to listen.
誰(だれ)も耳(みみ)を貸(か)さなかった。
Nobody would listen.
彼(かれ)は人(ひと)の忠告(ちゅうこく)に耳(みみ)を貸(か)さない。
He doesn't heed other people's advice.
ちょっと耳(みみ)を貸(か)してくれませんか。大事(だいじ)な話(はなし)があるんです。
Could you lend me your ear for a moment? I have something important to tell you.

Literally "to lend one's ear." Very commonly used in the negative (耳(みみ)を貸(か)さない) to describe someone who refuses to listen to advice, warnings, or requests.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 耳(みみ)を貸(か)さない: won't listen, refuses to heed
  • 耳(みみ)を貸(か)してくれる: to be willing to listen
  • ちょっと耳(みみ)を貸(か)して: lend me your ear for a sec (casual request)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 忠告(ちゅうこく)に耳(みみ)を貸(か)す: to heed advice
  • 意見(いけん)に耳(みみ)を貸(か)す: to listen to opinions
  • 訴(うった)えに耳(みみ)を貸(か)す: to listen to an appeal

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 耳(みみ)を傾(かたむ)ける: to incline one's ear — more formal and literary; implies careful, attentive listening
  • 聞(き)く耳(みみ)を持(も)たない: to refuse to listen at all — stronger refusal than 耳(みみ)を貸(か)さない

Related Words

Synonym: 耳(みみ)を傾(かたむ)ける (listen carefully)
