目下(もっか)

もっか
adverb
at present; currently; right now
1. at present; currently; right now
Indicates that something is happening or in progress at the current time. Often used in formal or written contexts to describe an ongoing situation.
目下(もっか)調査中(ちょうさちゅう)です。
It is currently under investigation.
目下(もっか)の課題(かだい)は人手不足(ひとでぶそく)だ。
The current issue is a shortage of workers.
この問題(もんだい)については目下(もっか)検討(けんとう)を進(すす)めているところです。
We are currently in the process of examining this issue.

A formal adverb meaning "at present" or "currently." Can also function as a noun modifier with の, as in 目下(もっか)の課題(かだい) (the current issue). More formal than 今(いま) or 現在(げんざい).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目下(もっか)の課題(かだい): the current issue
  • 目下(もっか)の問題(もんだい): the present problem
  • 目下(もっか)調査中(ちょうさちゅう): currently under investigation
  • 目下(もっか)検討中(けんとうちゅう): currently under review

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 現在(げんざい): currently — neutral and widely used in both speech and writing
  • 今(いま): now — casual, everyday word
  • 只今(ただいま): right now — polite, often used in customer service

NOTE:
The same kanji 目下(めした) is read めした and means "subordinate" or "junior" — a completely different word. See entry 08808.
