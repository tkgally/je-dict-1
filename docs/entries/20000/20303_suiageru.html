1. to suck up; to absorb; to pump up; to draw up
To draw liquid or moisture upward through suction or absorption. Used for pumps, plants, sponges, and similar mechanisms.
ポンプで水(みず)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる。
To pump up water.
植物(しょくぶつ)は根(ね)から水分(すいぶん)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる。
Plants absorb moisture through their roots.
スポンジがこぼれた水(みず)をすぐに吸(す)い上(あ)げた。
The sponge quickly absorbed the spilled water.
2. to gather up; to collect from below; to exploit
To collect money, opinions, or resources from lower levels of an organization or from the general population. Can carry a negative nuance of exploitation.
現場(げんば)の声(こえ)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる。
To gather feedback from the front lines.
市民(しみん)の意見(いけん)を吸(す)い上(あ)げて政策(せいさく)に反映(はんえい)させる。
To gather citizens' opinions and reflect them in policy.
大企業(だいきぎょう)が地方(ちほう)の富(とみ)を吸(す)い上(あ)げているという批判(ひはん)がある。
There is criticism that large corporations are siphoning wealth from rural areas.

A compound verb from 吸(す)う (to suck, to inhale) + 上(あ)げる (to raise). The upward direction distinguishes it from simply 吸(す)う.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. No standard intransitive counterpart; the passive 吸(す)い上(あ)げられる is used for the intransitive sense.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる — to suck up water
  • 意見(いけん)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる — to gather opinions
  • 富(とみ)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる — to siphon off wealth
  • 地下水(ちかすい)を吸(す)い上(あ)げる — to pump up groundwater

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吸収(きゅうしゅう)する — to absorb (more formal/scientific)
  • 汲(く)み上(あ)げる — to scoop up, to draw up (physical: water from a well; figurative: gathering opinions)
  • 搾取(さくしゅ)する — to exploit (stronger negative connotation than sense 2)
