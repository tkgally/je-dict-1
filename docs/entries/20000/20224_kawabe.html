川辺(かわべ)

かわべ
noun
riverside; riverbank
1. riverside; riverbank; river's edge
The area along the edge of a river.
川辺(かわべ)を散歩(さんぽ)した。
I took a walk along the riverbank.
川辺(かわべ)に座(すわ)って弁当(べんとう)を食(た)べた。
I sat by the river and ate my lunch.
春(はる)になると、川辺(かわべ)の桜(さくら)が一斉(いっせい)に咲(さ)き、花見(はなみ)を楽(たの)しむ人(ひと)でにぎわう。
In spring, the cherry blossoms along the river bloom all at once, and the area bustles with people enjoying hanami.

Composed of 川(かわ) (river) + 辺(べ) (vicinity, edge). A simple, everyday word for the area beside a river. Also read as かわべり in some contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 川辺(かわべ)を歩(ある)く: to walk along the riverside
  • 川辺(かわべ)に座(すわ)る: to sit by the river
  • 川辺(かわべ)の風景(ふうけい): riverside scenery
  • 川辺(かわべ)の公園(こうえん): riverside park

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 川岸(かわぎし): riverbank — focuses more on the bank/embankment itself
  • 岸辺(きしべ): shore, bank — broader, can be used for lakes and seas too
  • 河川敷(かせんしき): riverbed area — the flat area between the river and the levee
