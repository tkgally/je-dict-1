1. to use for two or more purposes; to serve double duty; to share between uses
To use a single item or space for multiple functions rather than having separate ones for each purpose.
リビングを書斎(しょさい)と兼用(けんよう)する。
To use the living room as a study as well.
この傘(かさ)は晴雨(せいう)兼用(けんよう)だ。
This umbrella serves for both sun and rain.
家族(かぞく)全員(ぜんいん)でパソコンを兼用(けんよう)しているので、順番待(じゅんばんま)ちになることがある。
The whole family shares one computer, so sometimes we have to take turns.

A する-verb from 兼用(けんよう), composed of 兼(けん) (to combine, to serve concurrently) + 用(よう) (use). Often used as a noun modifier without する: 兼用(けんよう)の (dual-purpose).

USAGE:
Very common in product descriptions and everyday conversation about shared or multi-purpose items. The noun form 兼用(けんよう) is often used directly: AとBの兼用(けんよう) (serving as both A and B).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 晴雨(せいう)兼用(けんよう) — for both sun and rain (umbrellas)
  • 男女(だんじょ)兼用(けんよう) — unisex
  • 兼用(けんよう)スペース — shared/multi-purpose space
  • AとBを兼用(けんよう)する — to use something as both A and B

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 共用(きょうよう)する — to share (among people; focus on joint access)
  • 併用(へいよう)する — to use together, to use concurrently (two things used simultaneously)
  • 流用(りゅうよう)する — to divert, to repurpose (redirecting something from its original use)
