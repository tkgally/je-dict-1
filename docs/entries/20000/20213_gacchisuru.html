1. to match; to coincide; to be in accord
For two things to agree, correspond, or be in alignment with each other.
意見(いけん)が合致(がっち)した。
Our opinions were in accord.
条件(じょうけん)に合致(がっち)する候補者(こうほしゃ)を探(さが)している。
We are looking for candidates who match the requirements.
調査(ちょうさ)結果(けっか)は事前(じぜん)の予測(よそく)とほぼ合致(がっち)しており、仮説(かせつ)が正(ただ)しかったことが確認(かくにん)された。
The survey results largely coincided with the prior predictions, confirming that the hypothesis was correct.

A formal word used when two things correspond or align. Common in academic, legal, and business writing. In everyday conversation, 合(あ)う or 一致(いっち)する are more natural.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 意見(いけん)が合致(がっち)する: opinions agree
  • 条件(じょうけん)に合致(がっち)する: to match conditions
  • 方針(ほうしん)と合致(がっち)する: to be in line with policy
  • 目的(もくてき)に合致(がっち)する: to match the objective
  • 基準(きじゅん)に合致(がっち)する: to meet standards

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一致(いっち)する: to match, to agree — more common, nearly synonymous
  • 合(あ)う: to match, to fit — everyday word
  • 該当(がいとう)する: to correspond to, to apply — focuses on meeting criteria
  • 適合(てきごう)する: to conform to — more technical, often for standards compliance
