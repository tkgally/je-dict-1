景勝地(けいしょうち)

けいしょうち
noun
scenic spot; place of scenic beauty
1. scenic spot; place of scenic beauty
A location renowned for its beautiful natural scenery. Often used for officially recognized or well-known tourist destinations featuring natural landscapes.
ここは有名(ゆうめい)な景勝地(けいしょうち)だ。
This is a famous scenic spot.
日本(にほん)には多(おお)くの景勝地(けいしょうち)がある。
Japan has many places of scenic beauty.
この湖(みずうみ)は国(くに)の名勝(めいしょう)に指定(してい)された景勝地(けいしょうち)として知(し)られている。
This lake is known as a scenic spot designated as a national place of scenic beauty.

A somewhat formal word used in travel writing, tourism, and official designations. It emphasizes natural beauty rather than historical or cultural significance.

FORMATION:
景勝(けいしょう) (scenic beauty) + 地(ち) (place)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 景勝地(けいしょうち)を巡(めぐ)る: to tour scenic spots
  • 有名(ゆうめい)な景勝地(けいしょうち): a famous scenic spot
  • 景勝地(けいしょうち)に指定(してい)される: to be designated a scenic area
  • 自然(しぜん)の景勝地(けいしょうち): a natural scenic spot

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 名所(めいしょ): famous place — broader, includes historical and cultural sites as well as scenic ones
  • 絶景(ぜっけい): breathtaking view — describes the view itself, not the location
  • 名勝(めいしょう): scenic site — an official designation for nationally protected scenic places; more formal and administrative
