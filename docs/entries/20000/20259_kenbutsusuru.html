1. to sightsee; to go see (a sight or attraction)
To visit and look at places of interest, tourist attractions, or notable sights.
京都(きょうと)のお寺(てら)を見物(けんぶつ)した。
I went sightseeing at the temples in Kyoto.
桜(さくら)の名所(めいしょ)を見物(けんぶつ)しに行(い)こう。
Let's go see the famous cherry blossom spots.
旅行(りょこう)の最終日(さいしゅうび)に市内(しない)の歴史的(れきしてき)な建物(たてもの)を見物(けんぶつ)して回(まわ)った。
On the last day of the trip, I went around sightseeing the historic buildings in the city.
2. to watch; to be a spectator
To watch an event, performance, or scene as an onlooker.
祭(まつ)りを見物(けんぶつ)する。
To watch a festival.
大勢(おおぜい)の見物人(けんぶつにん)がパレードを見(み)ていた。
A large crowd of spectators was watching the parade.
花火(はなび)大会(たいかい)を見物(けんぶつ)するために早(はや)くから場所取(ばしょと)りをした。
I went early to claim a spot to watch the fireworks festival.

Composed of 見(けん) (see, look) + 物(ぶつ) (thing). Used both as a suru verb (見物(けんぶつ)する) and as a noun (見物(けんぶつ)). Has a slightly old-fashioned or traditional tone compared to 観光(かんこう)する for tourism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見物(けんぶつ)に行(い)く: to go sightseeing
  • 見物(けんぶつ)して回(まわ)る: to go around seeing the sights
  • 見物人(けんぶつにん): spectator, onlooker
  • 見物客(けんぶつきゃく): sightseer, tourist
  • 花見(はなみ)見物(けんぶつ): cherry blossom viewing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 観光(かんこう)する: to do tourism, to sightsee — more modern and commonly used for tourism
  • 観覧(かんらん)する: to view, to watch — more formal, used for organized viewings
  • 見学(けんがく)する: to tour, to observe — educational or inspective purpose
