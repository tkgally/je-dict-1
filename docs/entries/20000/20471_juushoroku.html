住所録(じゅうしょろく)

じゅうしょろく
noun
address book; address list
1. address book; address list; directory
A list or book containing names and addresses of contacts. Can refer to both physical address books and digital contact lists.
住所録(じゅうしょろく)を更新(こうしん)する。
To update the address book.
年賀状(ねんがじょう)のために住所録(じゅうしょろく)を整理(せいり)した。
I organized the address book for New Year's cards.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しをしたので、住所録(じゅうしょろく)の情報(じょうほう)を変更(へんこう)してください。
I've moved, so please update my information in the address book.

A compound of 住所(じゅうしょ) (address) + 録(ろく) (record, register). Commonly used in both personal and organizational contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 住所録(じゅうしょろく)を作(つく)る: to create an address book
  • 住所録(じゅうしょろく)を整理(せいり)する: to organize the address book
  • 住所録(じゅうしょろく)を更新(こうしん)する: to update the address book
  • 住所録(じゅうしょろく)に載(の)せる: to include in the address book

USAGE:
Traditionally associated with physical notebooks, but now also used for digital contact lists. Particularly relevant around New Year when Japanese people send 年賀状(ねんがじょう) (New Year greeting cards) and need up-to-date addresses.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 連絡先(れんらくさき): contact information — broader term including phone numbers and email
  • 電話帳(でんわちょう): phone book — specifically for phone numbers
  • 名簿(めいぼ): directory, roster — a list of names, often for organizations
