1. rehearsal; practice run; dry run
A practice session conducted before the actual event to ensure everything goes smoothly. Used for ceremonies, performances, military exercises, and other organized events.
明日(あした)は予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)がある。
There's a rehearsal tomorrow.
卒業式(そつぎょうしき)の予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)を行(おこな)った。
We held a rehearsal for the graduation ceremony.
本番(ほんばん)の前(まえ)に何度(なんど)も予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)を重(かさ)ねて準備(じゅんび)した。
We prepared by doing multiple dry runs before the real event.

Compound of 予行(よこう) (preliminary run) and 演習(えんしゅう) (exercise/drill). A formal word for a full practice run of an event, ensuring all participants know their roles and the sequence of events.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)を行(おこな)う: to hold a rehearsal
  • 予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)を重(かさ)ねる: to do repeated rehearsals
  • 予行演習(よこうえんしゅう)に参加(さんか)する: to participate in a rehearsal
  • 卒業式(そつぎょうしき)の予行演習(よこうえんしゅう): graduation ceremony rehearsal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • リハーサル: rehearsal — the loanword, used mainly for performances, concerts, and shows
  • 練習(れんしゅう): practice — general term for practicing any skill; not specific to event run-throughs
  • 模擬(もぎ): mock, simulated — used as a prefix (模擬試験(もぎしけん) = mock exam)

USAGE:
Very common in school contexts, especially for ceremonies like 入学式(にゅうがくしき) (entrance ceremony) and 卒業式(そつぎょうしき) (graduation ceremony). Also used for military drills and disaster preparedness exercises.
