1. pervert; deviant; weirdo
A person with abnormal or deviant behavior, especially of a sexual nature. In casual usage, can be used humorously or as a lighthearted insult among friends, though it can also be a serious accusation.
この変態(へんたい)！
You pervert!
変態(へんたい)に声(こえ)をかけられた。
A creep spoke to me.
変態的(へんたいてき)な才能(さいのう)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
He's someone with freakishly extraordinary talent.
2. metamorphosis; transformation
In biology, the process by which an organism undergoes a major physical transformation during its life cycle, such as a caterpillar becoming a butterfly.
カエルは変態(へんたい)する動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Frogs are animals that undergo metamorphosis.
幼虫(ようちゅう)から成虫(せいちゅう)への変態(へんたい)は約(やく)２週間(しゅうかん)かかる。
The metamorphosis from larva to adult takes about two weeks.
完全変態(かんぜんへんたい)と不完全変態(ふかんぜんへんたい)の違(ちが)いを学(まな)んだ。
I learned the difference between complete and incomplete metamorphosis.

The characters 変(へん) (change/strange) + 態(たい) (condition/form) literally mean 'changed state.' The word has two very different registers depending on context.

Sense 1 is by far more common in everyday conversation and media. In modern slang, 変態的(へんたいてき) can also be used as praise meaning 'freakishly good' or 'insanely skilled,' especially in sports and gaming contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 変態(へんたい)する: to undergo metamorphosis (sense 2)
  • 変態的(へんたいてき)な: abnormal; freakishly extraordinary
  • 完全変態(かんぜんへんたい): complete metamorphosis
  • 不完全変態(ふかんぜんへんたい): incomplete metamorphosis

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 痴漢(ちかん): molester, groper — specifically refers to someone who gropes on trains, etc.
  • 変(へん)な人(ひと): a strange/weird person — milder than 変態(へんたい)
