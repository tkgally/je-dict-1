手(て)を伸(の)ばす

てをのばす
expression
to reach out; to extend one's hand; to expand into
1. to reach out; to stretch out one's hand
To physically extend one's arm and hand toward something, typically to grab or touch it.
棚(たな)の上(うえ)に手(て)を伸(の)ばす。
To reach up to the shelf.
子供(こども)がお菓子(かし)に手(て)を伸(の)ばした。
The child reached for the sweets.
溺(おぼ)れている人(ひと)に向(む)かって手(て)を伸(の)ばした。
I reached out my hand toward the drowning person.
2. to expand into; to extend one's reach to
To figuratively extend one's influence, business, or activity into a new area or field.
海外(かいがい)に手(て)を伸(の)ばす。
To expand overseas.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は新(あたら)しい分野(ぶんや)にも手(て)を伸(の)ばしている。
That company is expanding into new fields too.
若者(わかもの)に手(て)が届(とど)くよう、もっと手(て)を伸(の)ばすべきだ。
We should reach out more to connect with young people.

An idiomatic expression using 手(て) (hand) and 伸(の)ばす (to extend, to stretch). The literal meaning of physically reaching for something naturally extends to figurative uses about expanding influence or scope.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is common in everyday situations. Sense 2 is frequently used in business and organizational contexts to describe expanding into new markets, fields, or demographics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手(て)を伸(の)ばして取(と)る — to reach out and grab
  • 海外(かいがい)に手(て)を伸(の)ばす — to expand overseas
  • 新(あたら)しい分野(ぶんや)に手(て)を伸(の)ばす — to branch into a new field

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手(て)を差(さ)し伸(の)べる — to extend a helping hand (more about offering help)
  • 進出(しんしゅつ)する — to advance into, to expand into (business term)
  • 手(て)を広(ひろ)げる — to spread one's activities (similar figurative use)
