1. to perform music; to play an instrument; to give a musical performance
To play a musical instrument or perform music, especially in a somewhat formal or public context. Covers both solo and ensemble performances.
ピアノを演奏(えんそう)する。
To play the piano.
オーケストラが美(うつく)しい曲(きょく)を演奏(えんそう)した。
The orchestra performed a beautiful piece.
学園祭(がくえんさい)でバンドの演奏(えんそう)を披露(ひろう)することになった。
We're going to showcase our band performance at the school festival.

Composed of 演(えん) (perform) + 奏(そう) (play music). Takes を for the instrument or piece: ピアノを演奏(えんそう)する. The noun form 演奏(えんそう) is extremely common in music contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生演奏(なまえんそう): live performance
  • 独奏(どくそう): solo performance
  • 合奏(がっそう): ensemble, group performance
  • 演奏会(えんそうかい): concert, recital
  • 演奏者(えんそうしゃ): performer, musician

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 弾(ひ)く: to play (piano, guitar, strings) — casual, everyday word
  • 吹(ふ)く: to play (wind instruments) — specifically for blowing instruments
  • 叩(たた)く: to play (drums, percussion) — specifically for striking
  • 演奏(えんそう)する: more formal, encompasses all instruments and performance contexts
