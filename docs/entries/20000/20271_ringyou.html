林業(りんぎょう)

りんぎょう
noun
forestry; lumber industry
1. forestry; lumber industry; silviculture
The industry of cultivating, managing, and harvesting trees and forests for timber and other forest products.
林業(りんぎょう)が盛(さか)んな地域(ちいき)だ。
It's a region where forestry thrives.
祖父(そふ)は長年(ながねん)林業(りんぎょう)に携(たずさ)わってきた。
My grandfather has been involved in forestry for many years.
日本(にほん)の林業(りんぎょう)は後継者不足(こうけいしゃぶそく)が深刻(しんこく)な問題(もんだい)になっている。
A shortage of successors has become a serious problem for Japan's forestry industry.

Compound of 林(りん) (forest, woods) + 業(ぎょう) (industry, business). One of Japan's traditional primary industries alongside 農業(のうぎょう) (agriculture) and 漁業(ぎょぎょう) (fishery).

USAGE:
Japan has approximately 67% forest coverage, making it one of the most forested developed nations. However, the domestic forestry industry has declined significantly due to cheap imported lumber and rural depopulation. 林業(りんぎょう) appears frequently in discussions of rural economies and environmental policy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 林業(りんぎょう)従事者(じゅうじしゃ) — forestry worker
  • 林業(りんぎょう)に携(たずさ)わる — to be involved in forestry
  • 林業(りんぎょう)が衰退(すいたい)する — forestry declines
  • 林業(りんぎょう)の再生(さいせい) — revitalization of forestry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 農業(のうぎょう) — agriculture (crop farming)
  • 漁業(ぎょぎょう) — fishery (fishing industry)
  • 木材(もくざい)産業(さんぎょう) — timber industry (focuses on the product rather than the practice)
