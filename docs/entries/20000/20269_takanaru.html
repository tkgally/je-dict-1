1. to throb; to beat fast; to pound
Used for one's heart beating strongly or rapidly due to excitement, anticipation, anxiety, or other strong emotions.
胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)る。
My heart is pounding.
期待(きたい)で胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)った。
My heart raced with anticipation.
初(はじ)めての海外旅行(かいがいりょこう)を前(まえ)にして、胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)るのを抑(おさ)えられなかった。
I couldn't contain my excitement before my first trip abroad.
2. to ring out; to resound; to swell
Used for sounds, music, or voices growing louder or resonating powerfully.
太鼓(たいこ)の音(おと)が高鳴(たかな)る。
The sound of drums rings out.
歓声(かんせい)が高鳴(たかな)った。
The cheers swelled louder.
祭(まつ)りの笛(ふえ)と太鼓(たいこ)が高鳴(たかな)り、町(まち)全体(ぜんたい)が活気(かっき)づいた。
The festival flutes and drums rang out, and the whole town came alive.

Compound of 高(たか) (high) + 鳴(な)る (to sound, to ring). The word evokes something rising in intensity — whether a heartbeat quickening with emotion or sounds growing louder.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is the most common usage. Almost always appears with 胸(むね) (chest/heart) as the subject: 胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)る. This is a set expression meaning one's heart pounds with excitement or anticipation. Sense 2 is more literary and used for dramatic descriptions of sound.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)る — one's heart pounds/throbs
  • 期待(きたい)に胸(むね)が高鳴(たかな)る — one's heart races with anticipation
  • 鼓動(こどう)が高鳴(たかな)る — one's heartbeat quickens
  • 太鼓(たいこ)が高鳴(たかな)る — drums ring out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ときめく — to flutter, to feel excited (romantic or joyful excitement)
  • 鳴(な)り響(ひび)く — to resound, to echo (sounds reverberating, no heartbeat sense)
  • わくわくする — to be excited, thrilled (general excitement, more casual)
