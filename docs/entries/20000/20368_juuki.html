什器(じゅうき)

じゅうき
noun
fixtures; furnishings; store equipment
1. fixtures; furnishings; equipment (for shops, offices, events)
Movable equipment and furnishings used in commercial and institutional settings, such as display shelves, counters, tables, and racks. Distinct from permanent structural fixtures.
店舗(てんぽ)の什器(じゅうき)を新(あたら)しくした。
We replaced the store fixtures.
イベント用(よう)の什器(じゅうき)をレンタルした。
We rented fixtures for the event.
什器(じゅうき)の配置(はいち)を変(か)えたら、売(う)り場(ば)の雰囲気(ふんいき)がガラリと変(か)わった。
When we rearranged the fixtures, the sales floor atmosphere changed completely.

A business and retail term for movable equipment used in commercial spaces. Very common in the retail, hospitality, and event industries.

FORMATION:
什(じゅう) (various items, household goods) + 器(き) (vessel, equipment). The kanji 什(じゅう) originally meant "ten" or "miscellaneous items."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 什器(じゅうき)備品(びひん): fixtures and furnishings
  • 店舗(てんぽ)什器(じゅうき): store fixtures
  • 什器(じゅうき)レンタル: fixture rental
  • 什器(じゅうき)の搬入(はんにゅう): bringing in fixtures
  • 什器(じゅうき)の設置(せっち): installation of fixtures

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 備品(びひん): supplies, equipment — broader term covering office supplies and smaller items
  • 設備(せつび): facilities, installations — usually refers to permanent, built-in infrastructure
  • 家具(かぐ): furniture — for homes, while 什器(じゅうき) is primarily for commercial use
