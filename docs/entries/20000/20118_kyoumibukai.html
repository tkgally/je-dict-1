1. very interesting; fascinating; of great interest
Describes something that arouses deep interest or curiosity. More formal and emphatic than omoshiroi when expressing intellectual interest.
興味深(きょうみぶか)い結果(けっか)だ。
That's a fascinating result.
この研究(けんきゅう)は非常(ひじょう)に興味深(きょうみぶか)い。
This research is extremely interesting.
歴史的(れきしてき)な背景(はいけい)を知(し)ると、この建物(たてもの)はさらに興味深(きょうみぶか)くなる。
When you learn the historical background, this building becomes even more fascinating.

Composed of 興味(きょうみ) (interest) + 深(ふか)い (deep). Used to express genuine intellectual curiosity or fascination with a topic. More formal and objective than 面白(おもしろ)い, which can also mean 'fun' or 'amusing.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い結果(けっか): fascinating result
  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い発見(はっけん): interesting discovery
  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い事実(じじつ): interesting fact
  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)い話(はなし): interesting story
  • 興味深(きょうみぶか)く読(よ)む: to read with great interest

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 面白(おもしろ)い: interesting, fun — broader in meaning; can mean entertaining or amusing, not just intellectually interesting
  • 関心(かんしん)がある: to be interested in — expresses personal interest rather than describing the object as interesting

USAGE:
Often used in academic, journalistic, and formal contexts. The adverbial form 興味深(きょうみぶか)く is also common, as in 興味深(きょうみぶか)く拝見(はいけん)しました (I read/viewed it with great interest).
