自省(じせい)

じせい
noun
self-reflection; introspection; self-examination
1. self-reflection; introspection; self-examination
The act of looking back at one's own actions, thoughts, or behavior to evaluate them critically. Implies a serious, honest assessment of oneself.
自省(じせい)の念(ねん)にかられた。
I was overcome with self-reflection.
失敗(しっぱい)を繰(く)り返(かえ)さないために自省(じせい)することが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to reflect on oneself to avoid repeating mistakes.
リーダーには常(つね)に自省(じせい)する姿勢(しせい)が求(もと)められる。
Leaders are always expected to maintain an attitude of self-reflection.

A somewhat literary and formal word. It implies a deeper, more serious form of reflection than simply thinking back on events — there is an element of self-criticism and moral evaluation.

FORMATION:
自(じ) (self) + 省(せい) (reflect, examine)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自省(じせい)する: to reflect on oneself
  • 自省(じせい)の念(ねん): a feeling of self-reflection
  • 自省(じせい)を促(うなが)す: to encourage self-reflection
  • 自省(じせい)の姿勢(しせい): an attitude of self-examination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 反省(はんせい): reflection, remorse — more common and broader; often used for apologizing or reviewing mistakes
  • 内省(ないせい): introspection — looking inward at one's thoughts and feelings; more psychological
  • 自己反省(じこはんせい): self-reflection — a compound synonym; slightly more emphatic
