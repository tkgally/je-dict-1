窓辺(まどべ)

まどべ
noun
by the window; windowside
1. by the window; windowside; window area
The area near or beside a window. Often evokes a peaceful, contemplative atmosphere in literature and everyday descriptions.
窓辺(まどべ)に花(はな)を飾(かざ)った。
I placed flowers by the window.
猫(ねこ)が窓辺(まどべ)で日向(ひなた)ぼっこをしている。
The cat is sunbathing by the window.
窓辺(まどべ)の席(せき)に座(すわ)って、外(そと)の景色(けしき)を眺(なが)めていた。
I sat in the window seat, gazing at the scenery outside.

A compound of 窓(まど) (window) and 辺(べ) (area near, vicinity). The suffix 辺(べ) is a classical/literary way of saying "near" or "beside" and appears in other location words like 枕辺(まくらべ) (bedside).

USAGE:
A somewhat literary and evocative word. Often used to create a calm, nostalgic, or reflective atmosphere. Common in descriptions of home life, cafes, and contemplative scenes. Can function as an adverbial location with に or で.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 窓辺(まどべ)に座(すわ)る — to sit by the window
  • 窓辺(まどべ)に飾(かざ)る — to display by the window
  • 窓辺(まどべ)の席(せき) — window seat
  • 窓辺(まどべ)から — from the window area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 窓際(まどぎわ) — right next to the window; window-side (also used figuratively for sidelined employees: 窓際族(まどぎわぞく))
  • 窓(まど)のそば — near the window (plain expression)
