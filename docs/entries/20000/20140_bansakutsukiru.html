万策尽(ばんさくつ)きる

ばんさくつきる
verb
to exhaust all options; to run out of ideas
1. to exhaust all options; to run out of ideas; to have no more measures left
A situation in which every possible strategy or solution has been tried and nothing remains. Expresses a sense of helplessness after having done everything one can.
万策尽(ばんさくつ)きた。
I've run out of options.
万策尽(ばんさくつ)きて途方(とほう)に暮(く)れた。
Having exhausted all options, I was at a complete loss.
交渉(こうしょう)は難航(なんこう)し、万策尽(ばんさくつ)きたかに見(み)えたが、最後(さいご)に妥協案(だきょうあん)が見(み)つかった。
Negotiations were difficult and it seemed like all options had been exhausted, but a compromise was found at the last moment.

Composed of 万策(ばんさく) (all measures/strategies, literally 'ten thousand plans') + 尽(つ)きる (to be exhausted, to run out). An expressive set phrase conveying total helplessness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 万策尽(ばんさくつ)きた: all options exhausted (past tense, most common form)
  • 万策尽(ばんさくつ)きて: having exhausted all options (conjunctive)
  • もはや万策尽(ばんさくつ)きた: there are truly no more options left

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手詰(てづ)まり: deadlock, stalemate — a situation where no progress can be made; less dramatic
  • 八方塞(はっぽうふさ)がり: blocked in all directions — every path is closed off; emphasizes being trapped
  • お手上(てあ)げ: giving up, throwing in the towel — a more casual expression of helplessness

USAGE:
A dramatic, literary expression often used at moments of crisis. Common in news reporting, fiction, and speeches. The phrase 万策尽(ばんさくつ)きた is particularly well-known from sports and political commentary.
