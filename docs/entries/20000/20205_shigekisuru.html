1. to stimulate; to excite; to inspire
To activate or arouse interest, motivation, or a physical response.
好奇心(こうきしん)を刺激(しげき)する。
It stimulates curiosity.
海外旅行(かいがいりょこう)は五感(ごかん)を刺激(しげき)する。
Traveling abroad stimulates all five senses.
あの映画(えいが)は観客(かんきゃく)の想像力(そうぞうりょく)を大(おお)いに刺激(しげき)した。
That movie greatly stimulated the audience's imagination.
2. to irritate; to provoke; to aggravate
To cause discomfort, agitation, or an adverse reaction, either physically or emotionally.
目(め)を刺激(しげき)しないでください。
Please don't irritate your eyes.
辛(から)い食(た)べ物(もの)は胃(い)を刺激(しげき)する。
Spicy food irritates the stomach.
あまり相手(あいて)を刺激(しげき)しないように、穏(おだ)やかに話(はな)したほうがいい。
You should speak calmly so as not to provoke the other person too much.

A word with both positive (stimulate, inspire) and negative (irritate, provoke) connotations depending on context. The noun form 刺激(しげき) is equally common and can function as a な-adjective (刺激(しげき)的(てき)な — stimulating).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 刺激(しげき)を受(う)ける: to receive stimulation
  • 刺激(しげき)を与(あた)える: to give stimulation
  • 刺激(しげき)が強(つよ)い: strongly stimulating
  • 刺激(しげき)的(てき)な: stimulating, exciting
  • 経済(けいざい)を刺激(しげき)する: to stimulate the economy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 促(うなが)す: to prompt, to urge — focuses on encouraging action
  • 興奮(こうふん)させる: to excite — focuses on emotional arousal
  • 触発(しょくはつ)する: to inspire, to trigger — more literary, implies sparking an idea
