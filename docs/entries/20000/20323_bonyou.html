凡庸(ぼんよう)

ぼんよう
na-adjective
mediocre; ordinary; unremarkable
1. mediocre; ordinary; unremarkable; undistinguished
Describes a person, ability, or work that is average and lacks any outstanding qualities. Carries a negative nuance, implying disappointment at the lack of talent, originality, or distinction.
凡庸(ぼんよう)な作品(さくひん)だった。
It was a mediocre work.
彼(かれ)は決(けっ)して凡庸(ぼんよう)な人間(にんげん)ではない。
He is by no means an ordinary person.
凡庸(ぼんよう)な指導者(しどうしゃ)のもとでは、組織(そしき)は成長(せいちょう)しない。
Under a mediocre leader, an organization won't grow.

A な-adjective with a distinctly literary and formal tone. Used in writing and formal speech to critique someone or something as lacking distinction. The characters 凡(ぼん) (ordinary) and 庸(よう) (mediocre) both reinforce the sense of unremarkable averageness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 凡庸(ぼんよう)な才能(さいのう): mediocre talent
  • 凡庸(ぼんよう)な政治家(せいじか): an unremarkable politician
  • 凡庸(ぼんよう)さ: mediocrity (noun form)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 平凡(へいぼん): ordinary, common — more neutral, less critical
  • ありきたり: commonplace, run-of-the-mill — focuses on lack of originality
  • 陳腐(ちんぷ): stale, hackneyed — implies something once novel that has become worn out
  • 非凡(ひぼん): extraordinary — the antonym, using 非(ひ) (non-) prefix
