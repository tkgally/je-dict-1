入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)

にゅうもんしゃ
noun
beginner; novice; newcomer
1. beginner; novice; newcomer to a field
A person who has just started learning or entering a particular field, art, or discipline. Implies someone at the introductory stage.
入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)にもわかりやすい本(ほん)だ。
It's a book that's easy to understand even for beginners.
入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)向(む)けの講座(こうざ)に参加(さんか)した。
I attended a course aimed at beginners.
プログラミングの入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)でも簡単(かんたん)に使(つか)えるツールを開発(かいはつ)した。
We developed a tool that even programming beginners can use easily.

Compound of 入門(にゅうもん) (introduction, initiation) and 者(しゃ) (person). Originally referred to someone being accepted as a student of a traditional art or martial art (入門(にゅうもん)する = to become a disciple), but now broadly used for any beginner.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)向(む)け: aimed at beginners
  • 入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)にもわかりやすい: easy to understand for beginners too
  • 入門者(にゅうもんしゃ)から上級者(じょうきゅうしゃ)まで: from beginners to advanced learners

RELATED WORDS:

  • 入門(にゅうもん): introduction (to a subject); introductory-level
  • 入門書(にゅうもんしょ): introductory book, beginner's guide
  • 入門(にゅうもん)コース: introductory course

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初心者(しょしんしゃ): beginner — the most common word for beginner; broader and more everyday
  • 初学者(しょがくしゃ): beginning student — more academic; specifically about studying
  • 素人(しろうと): amateur, layperson — implies lack of professional skill; can sound slightly dismissive
