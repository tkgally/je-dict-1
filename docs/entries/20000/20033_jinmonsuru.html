尋問(じんもん)する

じんもんする
verb
to interrogate; to question; to examine
1. to interrogate; to question formally
To formally question someone, especially in a legal, police, or official context. Implies a structured, authoritative questioning rather than casual inquiry.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を尋問(じんもん)する。
To interrogate the suspect.
証人(しょうにん)が法廷(ほうてい)で尋問(じんもん)された。
The witness was questioned in court.
弁護士(べんごし)が証人(しょうにん)に対(たい)して厳(きび)しい尋問(じんもん)を行(おこな)った。
The lawyer conducted a rigorous examination of the witness.

Compound of 尋(じん) (inquire) and 問(もん) (question). A formal legal and procedural term for questioning someone in an official capacity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 尋問(じんもん)を行(おこな)う: to conduct an interrogation
  • 尋問(じんもん)を受(う)ける: to be interrogated
  • 厳(きび)しい尋問(じんもん): harsh interrogation
  • 反対(はんたい)尋問(じんもん): cross-examination
  • 主(しゅ)尋問(じんもん): direct examination (in court)
  • 証人(しょうにん)尋問(じんもん): witness examination

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取(と)り調(しら)べ: police interrogation — specifically police questioning of a suspect; more commonly used in news
  • 質問(しつもん): question — neutral and everyday; no implication of authority or investigation
  • 聴取(ちょうしゅ): hearing, interviewing — formal questioning to gather information, less confrontational than 尋問(じんもん)

USAGE:
Most commonly encountered in legal dramas, court reporting, and crime fiction. In legal contexts, 尋問(じんもん) specifically refers to the formal examination of witnesses in court proceedings.
