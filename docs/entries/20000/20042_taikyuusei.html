耐久性(たいきゅうせい)

たいきゅうせい
noun
durability; endurance; longevity
1. durability; endurance; longevity
この素材(そざい)は耐久性(たいきゅうせい)が高(たか)い。
This material has high durability.
耐久性(たいきゅうせい)に優(すぐ)れた製品(せいひん)を選(えら)びたい。
I want to choose a product with excellent durability.
この建物(たてもの)は耐久性(たいきゅうせい)を考慮(こうりょ)して設計(せっけい)されている。
This building was designed with durability in mind.

The quality of being able to withstand wear, pressure, or damage over time. Formed from 耐久(たいきゅう) (endurance) + 性(せい) (quality/property). Commonly used in product reviews, manufacturing, and construction contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 耐久性(たいきゅうせい)が高(たか)い: high durability
  • 耐久性(たいきゅうせい)に優(すぐ)れる: to excel in durability
  • 耐久性(たいきゅうせい)がある: to be durable
  • 耐久性(たいきゅうせい)を高(たか)める: to increase durability
  • 耐久性(たいきゅうせい)テスト: durability test

RELATED TERMS:

  • 耐久(たいきゅう): endurance — the base noun without the property suffix
  • 丈夫(じょうぶ): sturdy, strong — describes the quality of being tough in everyday language
  • 頑丈(がんじょう): robust, solidly built — emphasizes physical sturdiness
