1. to sort; to classify; to separate into categories
To divide items into groups based on type, category, or destination. Used for physical sorting (mail, laundry, garbage) and administrative categorization (budgets, accounts).
ゴミを種類(しゅるい)ごとに仕分(しわ)ける。
To sort garbage by type.
届(とど)いた荷物(にもつ)を配達先(はいたつさき)ごとに仕分(しわ)けた。
I sorted the delivered packages by destination.
予算(よさん)を項目(こうもく)ごとに仕分(しわ)けて、無駄(むだ)な支出(ししゅつ)がないか確認(かくにん)した。
I sorted the budget by category and checked for wasteful spending.

An ichidan (る) verb. The noun form 仕分(しわ)け is also very common, especially in compound forms.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. The thing being sorted is marked with を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴミを仕分(しわ)ける: to sort garbage
  • 荷物(にもつ)を仕分(しわ)ける: to sort packages
  • 予算(よさん)を仕分(しわ)ける: to screen/sort budgets
  • 郵便物(ゆうびんぶつ)を仕分(しわ)ける: to sort mail

NOTE:
The noun form 仕分(しわ)け gained prominence in Japanese politics through the 事業(じぎょう)仕分(しわ)け (project screening) process, where government spending programs were publicly reviewed and categorized for cutting.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分類(ぶんるい)する: to classify — more formal and systematic; used for academic or scientific categorization
  • 分(わ)ける: to divide, to separate — more general; doesn't necessarily imply categorization
  • 選別(せんべつ)する: to sort out, to screen — implies selecting good from bad
