鷲(わし)

わし
noun
eagle

Definitions

1. eagle
A large bird of prey with powerful talons, a hooked beak, and excellent eyesight. Eagles symbolize power and majesty.

Examples

鷲(わし)が大(おお)きな翼(つばさ)を広(ひろ)げて空(そら)を飛(と)んでいる。
An eagle is flying in the sky with its large wings spread.
鷲(わし)は鋭(するど)い目(め)で獲物(えもの)を見(み)つける。
Eagles spot their prey with sharp eyes.
北海道(ほっかいどう)では冬(ふゆ)にオオワシが見(み)られる。
In Hokkaido, you can see Steller's sea eagles in winter.
オオワシ = Steller's sea eagle

Notes

鷲(わし) refers to eagles.

Common types:

  • オオワシ (Steller's sea eagle)
  • オジロワシ (white-tailed eagle)
  • イヌワシ (golden eagle)

Common expressions:

  • 鷲(わし)鼻(ばな) (aquiline nose, hooked nose)
  • 鷲(わし)のような目(め) (eagle eyes)
  • 鷲(わし)づかみ (to grab/clutch something tightly)

Counter: 羽(わ)

Related:

  • 鷹(たか) (hawk) - smaller than eagles
  • 鳶(とび)/トビ (black kite) - common in urban areas

Note:
鷲(わし) is distinct from 鷹(たか) (hawk). Eagles are generally larger and more powerful. The distinction in Japanese follows similar criteria to English.
