割合(わりあい)

わりあい
noun, adverb
ratio; proportion; relatively

Definitions

1. ratio; proportion; percentage; rate
The quantitative relation between two amounts.
2. relatively; comparatively
Used adverbially to mean 'fairly' or 'relatively'.

Examples

男女(だんじょ)の割合(わりあい)は3対(たい)2だ。
The ratio of men to women is 3 to 2.
成功(せいこう)の割合(わりあい)が高(たか)い。
The success rate is high.
この問題(もんだい)は割合(わりあい)簡単(かんたん)だ。
This problem is relatively easy.

Notes

割合(わりあい) is composed of 割(わり) (divide) + 合(あい) (match).

USAGE:

As noun:

  • ～の割合(わりあい): the ratio/proportion of ~
  • 割合(わりあい)が高(たか)い/低(ひく)い: the ratio is high/low

As adverb:

  • 割合(わりあい) + adjective: relatively/fairly ~
  • Similar to 比較的(ひかくてき) (comparatively)
