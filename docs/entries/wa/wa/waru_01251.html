割(わ)る

わる
godan verb
to divide, to break

Definitions

1. to divide (math)
To perform division.
2. to break, to crack
To cause something to split or shatter.
3. to dilute
To add liquid to reduce strength.

Examples

10を2で割る。
To divide 10 by 2.
卵(たまご)を割る。
To crack an egg.
焼酎(しょうちゅう)を水(みず)で割る。
To dilute shochu with water.

Notes

割る is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 割る (transitive): to break
  • 割(わ)れる (intransitive): to be broken

MATH TERMS:

  • 割る: to divide
  • 掛(か)ける: to multiply
  • 足(た)す: to add
  • 引(ひ)く: to subtract
