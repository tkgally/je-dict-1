和音(わおん)

わおん
noun
chord

Definitions

1. chord
A combination of multiple notes played together in music.

Examples

ギターで和音(わおん)を弾(ひ)く。
Play a chord on the guitar.
この和音(わおん)の響(ひび)きが美(うつく)しい。
The sound of this chord is beautiful.
基本(きほん)の和音(わおん)から練習(れんしゅう)する。
I practice starting with basic chords.

Notes

Also called コード in more casual or modern musical contexts.

Common expressions:

  • 和音(わおん)を弾(ひ)く (to play a chord)
  • 三和音(さんわおん) (triad)
  • 不協和音(ふきょうわおん) (dissonance)
