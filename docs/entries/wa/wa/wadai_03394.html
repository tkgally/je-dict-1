話題(わだい)

わだい
noun
topic, subject

Definitions

1. topic, subject (of conversation)
What is being talked about; the subject matter of a discussion or conversation.
2. hot topic, trending topic
Something that is currently being widely discussed or is popular.

Examples

話題(わだい)を変(か)えましょう。
Let's change the subject.
話題を変える = to change the topic
この映画(えいが)は話題(わだい)になっている。
This movie is a hot topic. / This movie is being talked about.
話題になる = to become a topic of discussion
何(なに)か面白(おもしろ)い話題(わだい)はありますか。
Is there any interesting topic to talk about?

Notes

USAGE: 話題(わだい) refers to conversation topics or things being discussed publicly.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 話題(わだい)を変(か)える (to change the subject)
  • 話題(わだい)になる (to become a topic of discussion)
  • 話題(わだい)を提供(ていきょう)する (to provide a topic)
  • 話題(わだい)の映画(えいが)/本(ほん) (a movie/book that everyone is talking about)
  • 共通(きょうつう)の話題(わだい) (common topic of interest)

CONTRAST:

  • 話題(わだい) - topic of conversation
  • 題名(だいめい) - title (of a work)
  • {テーマ} - theme, main subject
