鰐(わに)

わに
noun
crocodile; alligator

Definitions

1. crocodile; alligator
A large semi-aquatic reptile with a long snout, powerful jaws, and a long tail. The Japanese word encompasses both crocodiles and alligators.

Examples

動物園(どうぶつえん)で大(おお)きな鰐(わに)を見(み)た。
I saw a big crocodile at the zoo.
鰐(わに)は水辺(みずべ)で獲物(えもの)を待(ま)ち伏(ふ)せしている。
The crocodile is lying in wait for prey at the water's edge.
鰐(わに)の皮(かわ)で作(つく)った財布(さいふ)は高級品(こうきゅうひん)として知(し)られている。
Wallets made from crocodile leather are known as luxury items.

Notes

鰐(わに) refers to both crocodiles and alligators. To distinguish them:

  • 鰐(わに) or クロコダイル (crocodile)
  • アリゲーター (alligator specifically)

Common expressions:

  • 鰐(わに)皮(がわ) / 鰐(わに)の皮(かわ) (crocodile leather)
  • 鰐(わに)の涙(なみだ) (crocodile tears - insincere tears)

Counter: 匹(ひき) or 頭(とう) (for large individuals)

Cultural note:
In Japanese mythology, 鰐(わに) sometimes appears as a sea creature (possibly referring to sharks in ancient texts). In the Kojiki, the deity Toyotama-hime transformed into a 鰐(わに) during childbirth.
