渡(わた)す

わたす
godan verb
to hand over, to deliver

Definitions

1. to hand over, to pass
To give directly.
2. to ferry across
To transport over water.

Examples

書類(しょるい)を渡す。
To hand over documents.

Notes

渡す is transitive.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE:

  • 渡す (transitive): to hand over
  • 渡(わた)る (intransitive): to cross
