若(わか)い

わかい
adjective (i-adjective)
young

Definitions

1. young
Having lived or existed for a short time. Used for people and sometimes animals.
2. youthful, immature
Can also describe someone who acts or looks young, or is inexperienced.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は若(わか)いです。
She is young.
若(わか)い時(とき)、たくさん旅行(りょこう)しました。
When I was young, I traveled a lot.
若(わか)く見(み)えますね。
You look young.
A common compliment in Japan

Notes

若(わか)い is primarily used for people (and sometimes animals), unlike 新(あたら)しい which is for things. 若者(わかもの) means 'young person/youth'. 若(わか)く見(み)える (to look young) is a common compliment.
