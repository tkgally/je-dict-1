我(わ)が家(や)

わがや
noun
one's home, my home

Definitions

1. one's home, my home
One's own house or home, often with affection.

Examples

我(わ)が家(や)に帰(かえ)る。
To return to my home.
我(わ)が家(や)が一番(いちばん)。
There's no place like home.
我(わ)が家(や)の猫(ねこ)。
Our family cat.

Notes

我(わ)が家(や) means "my/our home" with a sense of affection.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 我(わ)が家(や)に帰(かえ)る (return home)
  • 我(わ)が家(や)が一番(いちばん) (there's no place like home)
  • 我(わ)が家(や)の〜 (our family's ~)

GRAMMAR:

我(わ)が is a classical possessive form. This pattern appears in set expressions:

  • 我(わ)が国(くに) (our country)
  • 我(わ)が子(こ) (my child)
  • 我(わ)が社(しゃ) (our company)

Note: 我(わ)が is literary/formal. Casual: うちの家(いえ), 自分(じぶん)の家(いえ)
