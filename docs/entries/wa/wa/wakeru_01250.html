分(わ)ける

わける
ichidan verb
to divide, to separate

Definitions

1. to divide, to split
To separate into parts.
2. to distribute, to share
To give portions to multiple recipients.

Examples

ケーキを三(みっ)つに分ける。
To divide the cake into three.
髪(かみ)を分ける。
To part one's hair.

Notes

分ける is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 分ける (transitive): to divide
  • 分(わ)かれる (intransitive): to split up, part

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 区別(くべつ)して分ける: to distinguish and separate
  • 均等(きんとう)に分ける: to divide equally
