輪(わ)

わ
noun
ring, circle, wheel

Definitions

1. ring, circle
A circular shape or object.
2. wheel
A circular object that rotates on an axle.
3. loop, hoop
A circular band or ring.

Examples

輪(わ)になって座(すわ)る。
To sit in a circle.
五(いつ)つの輪(わ)はオリンピックの象徴(しょうちょう)だ。
The five rings are the symbol of the Olympics.

Notes

輪(わ) means ring or circle.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 車輪(しゃりん): wheel
  • 指輪(ゆびわ): ring (jewelry)
  • 首輪(くびわ): collar
  • 輪ゴム(わごむ): rubber band

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 輪(わ)になる: to form a circle
  • 輪(わ)を広(ひろ)げる: to expand the circle
