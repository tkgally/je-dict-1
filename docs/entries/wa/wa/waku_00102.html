沸(わ)く

わく
verb (godan)
to boil, to well up

Definitions

1. to boil
For water or liquid to reach boiling temperature.
2. to well up, to spring up
For emotions, ideas, or enthusiasm to arise.

Examples

お湯(ゆ)が沸(わ)いた。
The water boiled.
風呂(ふろ)が沸(わ)いている。
The bath is ready (heated).
Common household expression
勇気(ゆうき)が沸(わ)いてきた。
Courage welled up inside me.
Figurative usage

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 沸(わ)かす (transitive, to boil). Pattern: Xが沸(わ)く (X boils).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 沸(わ)いている = 'has boiled' / 'is ready (hot).'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お湯(ゆ)が沸(わ)く (water boils)
  • 風呂(ふろ)が沸(わ)く (bath is ready)
  • 勇気(ゆうき)が沸(わ)く (courage wells up)
  • 興味(きょうみ)が沸(わ)く (interest arises)
  • 沸(わ)き上(あ)がる (surge up)

CONTRAST:

  • 沸(わ)く (intransitive): X boils
  • 沸(わ)かす (transitive): boil X

FIGURATIVE USAGE: Often used for emotions or mental states that spontaneously arise:

  • やる気(き)が沸(わ)く (motivation wells up)
  • 疑問(ぎもん)が沸(わ)く (doubt arises)
