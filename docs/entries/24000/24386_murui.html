無類(むるい)

むるい
noun
peerless; unmatched; unparalleled
1. peerless; unmatched; unparalleled; incomparable
Having no equal or rival. Used to emphasize that something or someone is in a class of their own, with nothing comparable.
彼(かれ)は無類(むるい)の読書(どくしょ)好(ず)きだ。
He is an unparalleled lover of reading.
無類(むるい)の強(つよ)さを誇(ほこ)るチームだ。
A team that boasts unmatched strength.
祖父(そふ)は無類(むるい)の酒(さけ)好(ず)きで、毎晩(まいばん)日本酒(にほんしゅ)を飲(の)んでいた。
My grandfather was an incomparable sake lover and drank nihonshu every night.
この地域(ちいき)は無類(むるい)の美(うつく)しさを持(も)つ自然(しぜん)に恵(めぐ)まれている。
This region is blessed with nature of incomparable beauty.

Used almost exclusively in the pattern 無類(むるい)の + noun to mean "an unparalleled (something)." The most common usage by far is 無類(むるい)の〜好(ず)き, describing someone who is an extreme enthusiast of something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無類(むるい)の酒(さけ)好(ず)き: an incomparable sake lover
  • 無類(むるい)の読書(どくしょ)好(ず)き: an unparalleled book lover
  • 無類(むるい)の強(つよ)さ: unmatched strength
  • 無類(むるい)の美(うつく)しさ: incomparable beauty

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無比(むひ): unequaled — very similar meaning, slightly more literary
  • 比類(ひるい)ない: without peer — more formal, often in written prose
  • 唯一(ゆいいつ)無二(むに): one and only — emphasizes uniqueness rather than superiority
  • 天下(てんか)無双(むそう): peerless under heaven — dramatic, used in historical or literary contexts

FORMATION:
無(む) (without) + 類(るい) (category, kind) — literally "without category," meaning nothing of the same kind exists.
