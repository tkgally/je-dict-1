1. accumulation; integration
The process of adding quantities together over time to obtain a total.
積算(せきさん)温度(おんど)が一定(いってい)に達(たっ)した。
The accumulated temperature reached a fixed level.
降水量(こうすいりょう)を積算(せきさん)して年間(ねんかん)の総量(そうりょう)を求(もと)める。
Calculate the annual total by accumulating the precipitation amounts.
走行(そうこう)距離(きょり)の積算(せきさん)メーターを確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the odometer's accumulated mileage.
2. cost estimation; quantity surveying
The calculation of projected costs for a construction or engineering project based on materials, labor, and other factors.
工事(こうじ)の積算(せきさん)を行(おこな)う。
To perform cost estimation for the construction project.
積算(せきさん)基準(きじゅん)に基(もと)づいて見積(みつも)りを作成(さくせい)する。
Prepare an estimate based on the cost estimation standards.
公共(こうきょう)事業(じぎょう)の積算(せきさん)には厳格(げんかく)なルールがある。
There are strict rules for cost estimation of public works projects.

Has two main uses: (1) general accumulation of quantities over time, commonly seen with temperature, rainfall, and distance measurements; (2) a technical construction/engineering term for cost estimation, where materials, labor, and overhead are calculated to project total cost.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 積算(せきさん)温度(おんど): accumulated temperature
  • 積算(せきさん)電力量(でんりょくりょう): cumulative energy consumption
  • 積算(せきさん)基準(きじゅん): cost estimation standards
  • 積算(せきさん)価格(かかく): estimated cost

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 累計(るいけい): cumulative total — focuses on running totals in statistics or accounting
  • 見積(みつも)もり: estimate/quotation — the resulting figure, while 積算(せきさん) is the process of calculating it
