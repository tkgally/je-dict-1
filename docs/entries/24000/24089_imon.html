1. consolation visit; morale visit
Visiting someone who is suffering, hospitalized, or in difficult circumstances to offer comfort and encouragement.
病院(びょういん)を慰問(いもん)する。
To make a consolation visit to a hospital.
被災地(ひさいち)への慰問(いもん)活動(かつどう)に参加(さんか)した。
I participated in consolation activities for the disaster-stricken area.
ボランティアグループが老人(ろうじん)ホームを慰問(いもん)して音楽(おんがく)を演奏(えんそう)した。
A volunteer group visited a nursing home to perform music as a consolation visit.

Describes visiting people in difficult situations — the sick, elderly, disaster victims, or those separated from family — to provide comfort and encouragement. Often involves bringing gifts, performing entertainment, or simply spending time. Commonly done by volunteers, celebrities, politicians, and organizations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 慰問(いもん)公演(こうえん): consolation performance (entertainment for those in hardship)
  • 慰問(いもん)品(ひん): consolation gifts; care packages
  • 慰問(いもん)活動(かつどう): consolation/morale-boosting activities
  • 慰問(いもん)に訪(おとず)れる: to visit for consolation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見舞(みま)い: visiting someone who is ill or in trouble — more personal and everyday
  • 慰労(いろう): appreciation/consolation for hard work — thanking someone for their efforts rather than comforting in adversity
