四十代(よんじゅうだい)

よんじゅうだい
noun
one's forties
1. one's forties; the forties (age range)
The age range from 40 to 49 years old. Used to describe a person's approximate age or to refer to this demographic group as a whole. In Japan this is often regarded as the period of peak responsibility at work and in the household.
母(はは)は四十代(よんじゅうだい)だ。
My mother is in her forties.
四十代(よんじゅうだい)になって体力(たいりょく)の衰(おとろ)えを感(かん)じ始(はじ)めた。
Now that I'm in my forties, I've started to feel my stamina declining.
この商品(しょうひん)は四十代(よんじゅうだい)の女性(じょせい)をターゲットに開発(かいはつ)された。
This product was developed with women in their forties as the target market.
四十代(よんじゅうだい)後半(こうはん)になると、子育(こそだ)てが一段落(いちだんらく)する人(ひと)も多(おお)い。
Once people reach their late forties, many find that the busiest phase of raising children has passed.

WORD FORMATION:
四十(よんじゅう) ('forty') + 代(だい) ('generation; age bracket'). Follows the standard decade pattern: 二十代(にじゅうだい), 三十代(さんじゅうだい), 四十代(よんじゅうだい), 五十代(ごじゅうだい), etc. The reading is always よんじゅうだい — not しじゅうだい, because 四(し) is avoided as a homonym of 死(し) ('death') in age expressions.

SUBDIVISIONS:

  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)前半(ぜんはん): early forties (40-44)
  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)半(なか)ば: mid-forties (around 45)
  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)後半(こうはん): late forties (46-49)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)の男性(だんせい)/女性(じょせい): man/woman in their forties
  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)向(む)け: aimed at people in their forties
  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)になる: to turn forty (enter one's forties)
  • 四十代(よんじゅうだい)のうちに: while still in one's forties
  • 働(はたら)き盛(ざか)りの四十代(よんじゅうだい): people in their prime working years in their forties

USAGE:

  • Very common in marketing, health articles, and demographic commentary. The forties are culturally seen as 働(はたら)き盛(ざか)り ('prime working years'), often with peak responsibilities at work and at home.
  • In older literature, 四十路(よそじ) is a poetic alternative, but 四十代(よんじゅうだい) is the standard modern term in speech, print, and statistics.
  • Unlike 四十歳(よんじゅっさい) ('forty years old'), 四十代(よんじゅうだい) refers to the entire decade rather than a specific age.
