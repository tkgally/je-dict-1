書(か)きぶり

かきぶり
noun
writing style, manner of writing
1. writing style; manner of writing; tone (of writing)
The distinctive way in which someone writes — encompassing tone, word choice, sentence rhythm, and overall manner. Often used when commenting on someone's article, letter, or essay to describe the feel of the prose rather than its content.
彼(かれ)の書(か)きぶりは丁寧(ていねい)だ。
His writing style is polite and careful.
新聞記事(しんぶんきじ)の書(か)きぶりから筆者(ひっしゃ)の立場(たちば)が伝(つた)わる。
The writing style of the newspaper article conveys the author's stance.
彼女(かのじょ)のブログは独特(どくとく)の書(か)きぶりで、多(おお)くの読者(どくしゃ)を引(ひ)きつけている。
Her blog has a distinctive writing style that draws many readers.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)の書(か)きぶりから判断(はんだん)すると、彼(かれ)はこの件(けん)にあまり乗(の)り気(き)ではないようだ。
Judging from the way the report is written, he doesn't seem very enthusiastic about this matter.

Composed of 書(か)き (the masu-stem of 書(か)く, 'to write') + the suffix ぶり ('manner; style'). The suffix ぶり attaches to the stems of many verbs to produce a noun meaning 'the way someone does ~.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書(か)きぶりが丁寧(ていねい): careful/polite writing style
  • 書(か)きぶりが硬(かた)い: a stiff/formal writing style
  • 書(か)きぶりが柔(やわ)らかい: a soft/gentle writing style
  • 書(か)きぶりから判断(はんだん)する: to judge from how it is written
  • 独特(どくとく)の書(か)きぶり: a distinctive writing style
  • 報告書(ほうこくしょ)の書(か)きぶり: the writing style of a report
  • 記事(きじ)の書(か)きぶり: the way an article is written

USAGE:

  • Focuses on the overall impression of a piece of writing — its tone and manner — rather than on specific features like grammar or vocabulary.
  • Commonly used in media commentary, when someone analyzes the tone of a news story, blog post, letter, or report to infer the writer's intent or attitude.
  • Can be applied to any written text: official documents, emails, social media posts, novels, journalistic articles.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書(か)き方(かた): way of writing — broader and more neutral; can describe both technique (how to form characters) and style; the most common everyday term.
  • 文体(ぶんたい): literary style — a more technical term used in linguistics and literary analysis to classify prose (e.g., 敬体(けいたい) vs. 常体(じょうたい)).
  • 筆致(ひっち): literary touch; stroke of the pen — more literary/formal; emphasizes the finesse or brushstroke-like quality of a writer's prose.
  • 文章(ぶんしょう): prose; writing (as an object) — refers to the writing itself, not its style.

RELATED ぶり WORDS:

  • 話(はな)しぶり: manner of speaking
  • 口(くち)ぶり: tone (of speech); way of talking
  • 仕事(しごと)ぶり: work performance; way of working
  • 暮(く)らしぶり: lifestyle; way of living
