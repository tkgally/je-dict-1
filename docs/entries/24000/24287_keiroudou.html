軽労働(けいろうどう)

けいろうどう
noun
light work; light labor; light-duty work
1. light work; light labor; light-duty work
Work that does not require heavy physical exertion, such as desk work, light assembly, or tasks suitable for people with physical limitations. Used in medical, employment, and labor contexts.
医師(いし)から軽労働(けいろうどう)のみ許可(きょか)された。
I was only permitted to do light work by the doctor.
手術後(しゅじゅつご)は軽労働(けいろうどう)から徐々(じょじょ)に復帰(ふっき)するのが望(のぞ)ましい。
After surgery, it's desirable to gradually return to work starting with light duties.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)にも可能(かのう)な軽労働(けいろうどう)の求人(きゅうじん)が増(ふ)えている。
Job listings for light work that elderly people can also do are increasing.

Compound of 軽(けい) ('light') and 労働(ろうどう) ('labor; work'). Used in medical certificates, workplace accommodations, and employment descriptions to specify work that avoids physical strain.

USAGE:

  • Often appears in medical contexts: doctors may certify a patient for 軽労働(けいろうどう) only during recovery
  • In labor classifications, contrasts with 重労働(じゅうろうどう) ('heavy labor')
  • Used in job listings to indicate that the work is not physically demanding

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 軽労働(けいろうどう)に従事(じゅうじ)する: to engage in light work
  • 軽労働(けいろうどう)の許可(きょか): permission for light work
  • 軽労働(けいろうどう)から復帰(ふっき)する: to return to work via light duties
  • 軽労働(けいろうどう)可能(かのう): capable of light work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重労働(じゅうろうどう): heavy labor — physically demanding work involving lifting, digging, or sustained physical effort
  • 軽作業(けいさぎょう): light tasks; simple work — similar meaning, commonly used in part-time job listings
  • {デスクワーク}: desk work — office work performed at a desk, a common example of light work
