上機嫌(じょうきげん)

じょうきげん
na-adjective
good mood; high spirits; good humor
1. good mood; high spirits; good humor
A state of being in a cheerful, pleasant mood, often visibly so.
父(ちち)は上機嫌(じょうきげん)で帰(かえ)ってきた。
My father came home in a great mood.
試験(しけん)に受(う)かって、彼女(かのじょ)は上機嫌(じょうきげん)だった。
Having passed the exam, she was in high spirits.
上機嫌(じょうきげん)な上司(じょうし)に頼(たの)み事(ごと)をするなら今(いま)がチャンスだ。
If you want to ask the boss a favor, now's your chance while he's in a good mood.

Describes a visibly cheerful, pleasant mood. Can be used as a noun (上機嫌(じょうきげん)だ) or as a pre-noun modifier (上機嫌(じょうきげん)な). The 上(じょう) prefix elevates 機嫌(きげん) (mood, humor) to its positive extreme.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上機嫌(じょうきげん)で話(はな)す — talk in good spirits
  • 上機嫌(じょうきげん)な顔(かお) — cheerful face
  • 上機嫌(じょうきげん)に歌(うた)う — sing happily
  • ご上機嫌(じょうきげん) — polite form, used in set phrases

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 機嫌(きげん)がいい — in a good mood; more neutral and everyday than 上機嫌(じょうきげん), which implies a noticeably elevated level of cheerfulness
  • 不機嫌(ふきげん) — bad mood; the opposite of 上機嫌(じょうきげん)
  • ご{機嫌(きげん)} — mood (polite); also used in greetings like ご機嫌(きげん)いかが
