教徒(きょうと)

きょうと
noun
believer; adherent; follower (of a religion)
1. believer; adherent; follower of a religion
A person who follows a particular religion. Used as a suffix-like element combined with the name of a religion: キリスト教徒(きょうと) (Christian), イスラム教徒(きょうと) (Muslim), 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと) (Buddhist).
キリスト教徒(きょうと)。
A Christian.
彼女(かのじょ)は敬虔(けいけん)な仏教徒(ぶっきょうと)だ。
She is a devout Buddhist.
この地域(ちいき)にはイスラム教徒(きょうと)が多(おお)く暮(く)らしており、モスクもいくつかある。
Many Muslims live in this area, and there are several mosques.

Composed of 教(きょう) (teaching, religion) + 徒(と) (follower, disciple). Almost always used in combination with a religion name.

USAGE:
教徒(きょうと) is rarely used on its own. It functions as a suffix attached to the name of a religion to mean "follower of [religion]." The word is neutral and formal — it does not carry any positive or negative connotation. In news and academic writing, it is the standard way to refer to members of a religion.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • キリスト教徒(きょうと): Christian
  • イスラム教徒(きょうと): Muslim
  • 仏教徒(ぶっきょうと): Buddhist
  • ヒンドゥー教徒(きょうと): Hindu
  • ユダヤ教徒(きょうと): Jew (religious sense)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 信者(しんじゃ): believer, follower — can be used for religious followers but also for devoted fans; carries a slightly more personal/emotional tone
  • 信徒(しんと): faithful, congregation member — similar to 教徒(きょうと) but often implies active membership in a specific congregation
  • 檀家(だんか): temple patron — specifically a household affiliated with a Buddhist temple
