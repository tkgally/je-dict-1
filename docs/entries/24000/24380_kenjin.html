県人(けんじん)

けんじん
noun
person from a prefecture; native of a prefecture
1. person from a particular prefecture; native of a prefecture
Someone who comes from or is associated with a specific prefecture. Often used with a prefecture name as a prefix to identify regional origin, especially in contexts of regional identity and community.
県人会(けんじんかい)に参加(さんか)した。
I attended the prefectural association meeting.
同(おな)じ県人(けんじん)だと分(わ)かると親近感(しんきんかん)がわく。
When you find out someone is from the same prefecture, you feel a sense of closeness.
東京(とうきょう)には各地(かくち)の県人会(けんじんかい)があり、故郷(こきょう)を離(はな)れた県人(けんじん)たちの交流(こうりゅう)の場(ば)となっている。
Tokyo has prefectural associations from all over the country, serving as gathering places for people from each prefecture who have left home.

Compound of 県(けん) ('prefecture') and 人(じん) ('person'). Used to express regional identity and affiliation — a significant concept in Japanese society where people maintain strong ties to their home prefecture.

USAGE:

  • Most commonly appears in the compound 県人会(けんじんかい) ('prefectural association') — social organizations in major cities where people from the same prefecture meet regularly. These are especially active in Tokyo.
  • Can be prefixed with a specific prefecture: 秋田(あきた)県人(けんじん) ('a person from Akita Prefecture').
  • Reflects the strong regional identity in Japan — hometown (故郷(こきょう)) and prefectural origin are common topics in self-introduction and social bonding.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 県人会(けんじんかい): prefectural association
  • 同(おな)じ県人(けんじん): person from the same prefecture
  • 在京(ざいきょう)県人会(けんじんかい): Tokyo-based prefectural association

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 同郷(どうきょう): from the same hometown — emphasizes shared origin between two or more people
  • 出身者(しゅっしんしゃ): person from (a place) — a more general term for someone's place of origin
  • 県民(けんみん): prefectural resident — a current resident of the prefecture, whereas 県人(けんじん) emphasizes origin
  • 同県(どうけん): the same prefecture — used as a modifier to indicate shared prefectural origin
