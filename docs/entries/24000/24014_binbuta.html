瓶蓋(びんぶた)

びんぶた
noun
bottle cap; bottle lid
1. bottle cap; bottle lid
The cap or lid that seals a bottle — whether a twist-off plastic cap on a PET bottle, a metal crown cap on a beer bottle, or a screw-top lid on a glass jar. A general term covering all types of bottle closures.
瓶蓋(びんぶた)が固(かた)い。
The bottle cap is tight.
瓶蓋(びんぶた)を開(あ)けてくれる？
Can you open the bottle cap for me?
子供(こども)が誤(あやま)って瓶蓋(びんぶた)を飲(の)み込(こ)まないように注意(ちゅうい)してください。
Please be careful that children don't accidentally swallow the bottle cap.

Compound of 瓶(びん) ('bottle') and 蓋(ふた) ('lid; cover'), with {ふた} shifting to {ぶた} by rendaku. A general-purpose word for any type of bottle closure, from beer-bottle crown caps to water-bottle screw tops.

USAGE:

  • Often appears in everyday contexts such as asking someone to open a stuck cap, or in childproofing and recycling instructions.
  • In recycling, plastic caps are usually separated from glass or PET bottles before disposal — so the word appears in garbage-sorting guides.
  • The verb 開(あ)ける ('to open') is most common; 外(はず)す ('to remove') and 閉(し)める ('to close / tighten') also occur.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 瓶蓋(びんぶた)を開(あ)ける: to open a bottle cap
  • 瓶蓋(びんぶた)を閉(し)める: to close / tighten a bottle cap
  • 瓶蓋(びんぶた)を外(はず)す: to remove the bottle cap
  • 瓶蓋(びんぶた)が固(かた)い: the cap is tight
  • 瓶蓋(びんぶた)が開(あ)かない: the cap won't open

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 王冠(おうかん): crown cap — specifically the toothed metal cap on beer and soda bottles; also means 'crown' (as worn by royalty).
  • キャップ(きゃっぷ): cap — loanword used especially for screw-on plastic caps on PET bottles.
  • 栓(せん): stopper; plug — covers cork stoppers and similar non-screw closures on wine or sake bottles.
  • 蓋(ふた): lid — general term for any lid or cover, not limited to bottles.
  • ボトルキャップ(ぼとるきゃっぷ): bottle cap — katakana equivalent, common in product descriptions and marketing.
