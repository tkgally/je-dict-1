1. sketch; rough drawing; dessin
sketch; rough drawing; dessin
鉛筆(えんぴつ)で素描(そびょう)する。
To sketch with a pencil.
美術(びじゅつ)教室(きょうしつ)で人物(じんぶつ)の素描(そびょう)を練習(れんしゅう)した。
I practiced figure sketching in art class.
彼(かれ)の素描(そびょう)は線(せん)が繊細(せんさい)で美(うつく)しい。
His sketches have delicate, beautiful lines.
2. outline; brief portrayal (of a person, situation, or era)
outline; brief portrayal (of a person, situation, or era)
時代(じだい)の素描(そびょう)を試(こころ)みる。
To attempt a sketch of the era.
この小説(しょうせつ)は戦後(せんご)の人々(ひとびと)の暮(く)らしを素描(そびょう)している。
This novel sketches the lives of people in the postwar period.
著者(ちょしゃ)は庶民(しょみん)の日常(にちじょう)を素描(そびょう)することで、社会(しゃかい)の変化(へんか)を浮(う)き彫(ぼ)りにした。
By sketching the daily lives of ordinary people, the author brought social change into relief.

In its literal sense, refers to a drawing made with simple materials like pencil or charcoal, without color. In its figurative sense, means to give a brief, evocative portrayal of something in writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人物(じんぶつ)素描(そびょう): figure sketch; character sketch
  • 素描(そびょう)帳(ちょう): sketchbook
  • 木炭(もくたん)素描(そびょう): charcoal sketch
  • 時代(じだい)の素描(そびょう): sketch of an era

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スケッチ: sketch — the loanword equivalent; more casual and widely used in everyday conversation
  • デッサン: dessin — specifically refers to academic drawing exercises focusing on form and proportion
  • 下書(したが)き: rough draft — refers to a preliminary version of writing or drawing, not a finished sketch
