攻守(こうしゅ)

こうしゅ
noun
offense and defense; attack and defense
1. offense and defense (in sports); both attacking and defensive aspects of play
A short Sino-Japanese compound covering both halves of competitive play. Frequently used in baseball, soccer, basketball, and other sports to talk about a team or player who must handle both roles.
攻守(こうしゅ)交代(こうたい)。
Switch sides (offense/defense).
あの選手(せんしゅ)は攻守(こうしゅ)に優(すぐ)れている。
That player is excellent at both offense and defense.
今(こん)シーズンの彼(かれ)は攻守(こうしゅ)両面(りょうめん)でチームを支(ささ)える中心選手(ちゅうしんせんしゅ)に成長(せいちょう)した。
This season he has grown into a key player who supports the team on both offense and defense.
2. attack and defense (figurative); both sides of a contest or argument
Used metaphorically for any situation involving alternating attack and defense — a debate, a court case, a business negotiation, or a strategic struggle.
選挙戦(せんきょせん)では攻守(こうしゅ)が逆転(ぎゃくてん)した。
The roles of attack and defense reversed in the election campaign.
裁判(さいばん)では弁護側(べんごがわ)と検察側(けんさつがわ)の攻守(こうしゅ)が激(はげ)しく入(い)れ替(か)わった。
In the trial, the attacking and defending positions of the defense and the prosecution shifted intensely.
企業間(きぎょうかん)の価格競争(かかくきょうそう)は攻守(こうしゅ)所(ところ)を変(か)えながら長期化(ちょうきか)している。
The price war between the companies has dragged on, with attacker and defender repeatedly switching positions.

Composed of 攻(こう) ('attack') + 守(しゅ) ('defense'). The two-character compound is a tightly bound noun, almost always used as a unit rather than split into its parts.

USAGE:

  • Sense 1 dominates in sports commentary, where 攻守(こうしゅ)交代(こうたい) ('switching offense and defense') and 攻守(こうしゅ)に優(すぐ)れる ('to excel at both attack and defense') are stock phrases.
  • Sense 2 is common in journalism, politics, and law, especially in the idiom 攻守(こうしゅ)所(ところ)を変(か)える ('the attacker and defender swap positions').
  • Belongs to a slightly formal register; in casual sports talk, speakers often use the loanwords オフェンス and ディフェンス instead.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 攻守(こうしゅ)交代(こうたい): switching offense and defense (e.g., between innings)
  • 攻守(こうしゅ)に優(すぐ)れる: to excel at both offense and defense
  • 攻守(こうしゅ)両面(りょうめん): both offense and defense; both aspects
  • 攻守(こうしゅ)所(ところ)を変(か)える: roles of attack and defense reverse
  • 攻守(こうしゅ)が逆転(ぎゃくてん)する: the offense and defense reverse
  • 攻守(こうしゅ)兼備(けんび): combining offense and defense (set phrase)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 攻撃(こうげき): attack; offense — focuses only on the attacking side; not a paired concept.
  • 防御(ぼうぎょ): defense; protection — the defensive side alone; commonly paired with 攻撃(こうげき).
  • 守備(しゅび): defense; fielding (in baseball) — narrower than 攻守(こうしゅ); emphasizes the act of guarding.
  • オフェンスとディフェンス: offense and defense — modern loanword pair, often used in basketball, soccer, and American football commentary.
