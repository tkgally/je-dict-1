収益率(しゅうえきりつ)

しゅうえきりつ
noun
rate of return; profit rate; yield
1. rate of return; profit rate; yield
The ratio of profit or income generated relative to the amount invested or the revenue earned. A key metric in finance, investment, and business management.
収益率(しゅうえきりつ)が高(たか)い。
The rate of return is high.
この投資信託(とうししんたく)の年間(ねんかん)収益率(しゅうえきりつ)は5%だ。
The annual rate of return on this investment trust is 5%.
収益率(しゅうえきりつ)を上(あ)げるために、コスト削減(さくげん)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
We are working on cost reduction to improve the profit rate.
不動産(ふどうさん)投資(とうし)では、収益率(しゅうえきりつ)だけでなくリスクも考慮(こうりょ)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
In real estate investment, you need to consider not only the rate of return but also the risks.

Composed of 収益(しゅうえき) (profit, earnings) and 率(りつ) (rate, ratio). This is a standard financial term used in investment analysis, corporate reporting, and economic commentary.

The suffix ～率(りつ) is productive in forming ratio/rate terms across many domains. In finance, 収益率(しゅうえきりつ) can refer to various return metrics depending on context — return on investment, profit margin, or yield.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年間(ねんかん)収益率(しゅうえきりつ): annual rate of return
  • 期待(きたい)収益率(しゅうえきりつ): expected rate of return
  • 収益率(しゅうえきりつ)を上(あ)げる: to improve the profit rate
  • 収益率(しゅうえきりつ)が低下(ていか)する: for the rate of return to decline
  • 収益率(しゅうえきりつ)の比較(ひかく): comparison of rates of return

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 利回(りまわ)り: yield, return — commonly used for bonds and dividend-paying investments; slightly less formal
  • 利益率(りえきりつ): profit margin — the ratio of profit to sales revenue, focused on business operations
  • 投資収益率(とうししゅうえきりつ): return on investment (ROI) — specifically the return relative to the amount invested
