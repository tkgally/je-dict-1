冗長性(じょうちょうせい)

じょうちょうせい
noun
redundancy
1. redundancy (engineering, information, or systems sense)
The inclusion of extra or duplicate components in a system — whether data, equipment, or wording — beyond the minimum required. In engineering and IT, redundancy is a positive property that provides fault tolerance; in writing, it is often a negative property indicating wordiness.
冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を確保(かくほ)する。
To ensure redundancy.
このシステムは冗長性(じょうちょうせい)が高(たか)く、障害(しょうがい)に強(つよ)い。
This system has high redundancy and is resistant to failures.
文章(ぶんしょう)の冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を減(へ)らすために不要(ふよう)な語(ご)を削(けず)った。
I cut out unnecessary words to reduce the redundancy of the writing.

The noun form of the な}-adjective {冗長(じょうちょう) ('long-winded; redundant'), made into a noun with the abstract-noun suffix -性(せい). Used in technical and academic writing for the property of having extra or duplicate elements in a system.

USAGE:

  • **Positive sense (engineering, IT, biology):** having backup components or duplicate paths so that the system continues to function when part of it fails. Essential for reliable servers, networks, aircraft, power grids, and genetic codes.
  • **Negative sense (writing, speech):** wordiness; saying the same thing twice or padding text with unnecessary expressions. In this sense, 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を減(へ)らす ('reduce redundancy') means 'tighten the writing'.
  • **Information theory:** a quantifiable measure of the amount of predictable or repeated information in a signal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を確保(かくほ)する: to ensure redundancy
  • 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を持(も)たせる: to build in redundancy
  • 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)が高(たか)い: redundancy is high; highly redundant
  • 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)が低(ひく)い: redundancy is low
  • 冗長性(じょうちょうせい)を減(へ)らす: to reduce redundancy (in writing)
  • システムの冗長性(じょうちょうせい): system redundancy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冗長(じょうちょう): long-winded; redundant — the {な}-adjective root; describes text or explanations that could be shorter.
  • 重複(ちょうふく): duplication; overlap — the act or fact of two things overlapping; often undesirable.
  • 予備(よび): backup; spare; reserve — concrete noun for the backup element itself, such as a spare part or reserve team.
  • バックアップ(ばっくあっぷ): backup — loanword commonly used in IT for backup copies and standby systems.
  • 多重化(たじゅうか): multiplexing; duplication — specific technical term in communications for having multiple channels over one medium.
