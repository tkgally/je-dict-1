フレックスタイム

ふれっくすたいむ
noun
flextime
1. flextime; flexible working hours
Loanword from English. Used in business and HR contexts.
うちの会社(かいしゃ)はフレックスタイム制(せい)だ。
Our company uses a flextime system.
フレックスタイムのおかげで、朝(あさ)の通勤(つうきん)ラッシュを避(さ)けられる。
Thanks to flextime, I can avoid the morning rush hour commute.
フレックスタイム制(せい)を導入(どうにゅう)してから、社員(しゃいん)の満足度(まんぞくど)が上(あ)がった。
Since introducing the flextime system, employee satisfaction has increased.

A loanword from English "flex time." Refers to a work arrangement where employees can choose their start and end times within certain limits, as long as they work during a designated core time period and complete their required hours.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • フレックスタイム制(せい): flextime system
  • フレックスタイムを導入(どうにゅう)する: to introduce flextime
  • フレックスタイム制度(せいど): flextime system (more formal)
  • コアタイム: core time (mandatory hours within flextime)

USAGE:
In Japanese workplaces, flextime systems typically have a コアタイム (core time) when all employees must be present (e.g., 10:00-15:00) and フレキシブルタイム (flexible time) windows at the start and end of the day. The abbreviated form フレックス is also commonly used: 「うちはフレックスだから」 (We have flextime, so...).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裁量(さいりょう)労働(ろうどう)制(せい): discretionary work system (even more flexible)
  • 変則(へんそく)勤務(きんむ): irregular working hours (shift-based)
  • テレワーク: remote work (different type of flexible arrangement)
  • 時差(じさ)出勤(しゅっきん): staggered commuting hours (fixed but offset)
