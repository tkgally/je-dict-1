頬骨(ほほぼね)

ほほぼね
noun
cheekbone
1. cheekbone (zygomatic bone)
The bony prominence in the upper part of the cheek, beneath the outer corner of the eye. Often referenced when describing facial features, anatomy, or the effects of aging or weight loss.
頬骨(ほほぼね)が高(たか)い。
(Someone) has high cheekbones.
彼女(かのじょ)は頬骨(ほほぼね)の張(は)った顔立(かおだ)ちをしている。
She has facial features with prominent cheekbones.
急激(きゅうげき)に痩(や)せたせいか、彼(かれ)の頬骨(ほほぼね)が以前(いぜん)より目立(めだ)つようになった。
Perhaps because he lost weight rapidly, his cheekbones now stand out more than before.
転(ころ)んだ拍子(ひょうし)に頬骨(ほほぼね)を強(つよ)く打(う)って、病院(びょういん)で骨折(こっせつ)の有無(うむ)を調(しら)べてもらった。
When I fell, I hit my cheekbone hard, so I went to the hospital to be checked for a fracture.

Composed of 頬(ほほ) ('cheek') + 骨(ほね) ('bone'), with rendaku turning ほね into ぼね. The reading ほおぼね} (with おお) is also acceptable and reflects the alternative pronunciation of {頬(ほお) for cheek.

USAGE:

  • Used in everyday descriptions of faces, in beauty/cosmetics contexts, and in medical contexts (fractures, surgery, dental anesthesia).
  • The medical/technical term is 頬骨(きょうこつ) (zygomatic bone), used in anatomy, surgery, and orthodontics. Both readings refer to the same bone, but ほほぼね is the colloquial term.
  • Often appears in descriptions of facial structure: 'high cheekbones' is a feature commented on in modeling, fashion, and ethnographic descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)が高(たか)い: to have high cheekbones
  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)が張(は)る: cheekbones to be prominent / to stick out
  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)が出(で)る: cheekbones to protrude (often from weight loss)
  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)を打(う)つ: to hit one's cheekbone
  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)骨折(こっせつ): cheekbone fracture
  • 頬骨(ほほぼね)が目立(めだ)つ: cheekbones to stand out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頬(ほほ)・頬(ほお): cheek — the soft tissue of the cheek itself, not the underlying bone.
  • 頬骨(きょうこつ): zygomatic bone — the medical/anatomical reading of the same kanji compound.
  • 顎(あご): jaw; chin — the lower part of the face; sometimes confused in casual descriptions but anatomically distinct.
  • {こめかみ}: temple — the side of the head between the eye and the ear; close to but separate from the cheekbone.
  • {えら}: jawline; angle of the jaw — the rear, lower part of the cheek/jaw, important when describing face shape.
