1. visiting a store; coming to a shop (honorific)
Honorific form used by shop staff addressing customers.
ご来店(らいてん)ありがとうございます。
Thank you for visiting our store.
ご来店(らいてん)の際(さい)は、ご予約(よやく)をお願(ねが)いいたします。
Please make a reservation when you visit.
本日(ほんじつ)もご来店(らいてん)いただき、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。
Thank you very much for visiting us today as well.
ご来店(らいてん)いただいたお客様(きゃくさま)全員(ぜんいん)にプレゼントを差(さ)し上(あ)げます。
We will give a gift to every customer who visits.

The honorific prefix ご + 来店(らいてん) (visiting a store). This is standard keigo used by service staff to politely refer to a customer's visit. You will hear this phrase in virtually every retail and restaurant environment in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ご来店(らいてん)ありがとうございます: thank you for visiting
  • ご来店(らいてん)をお待(ま)ちしております: we look forward to your visit
  • ご来店(らいてん)の際(さい): when you visit
  • ご来店(らいてん)いただく: to have someone visit (humble/honorific)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ご来場(らいじょう): visiting a venue (events, exhibitions)
  • ご来院(らいいん): visiting a hospital or clinic
  • ご来社(らいしゃ): visiting a company office
  • ご来館(らいかん): visiting a public facility (library, museum)
