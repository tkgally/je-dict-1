勤務時間(きんむじかん)

きんむじかん
noun
working hours; work hours
1. working hours; work hours; hours of duty
The period of time during which an employee is required or expected to work. Used in both formal labor regulations and everyday workplace conversation to refer to one's scheduled work hours.
勤務時間(きんむじかん)は九時(くじ)から五時(ごじ)までだ。
Working hours are from 9 to 5.
勤務時間(きんむじかん)中(ちゅう)はスマホを使(つか)わないでください。
Please do not use your smartphone during working hours.
勤務時間(きんむじかん)の短縮(たんしゅく)が働(はたら)き方(かた)改革(かいかく)の一環(いっかん)として進(すす)められている。
Reduction of working hours is being promoted as part of work-style reform.

Compound of 勤務(きんむ) ('duty; service; work') and 時間(じかん) ('time; hours'). One of the most fundamental terms in Japanese workplace vocabulary. Japan's labor laws and the ongoing 働(はたら)き方(かた)改革(かいかく) ('work-style reform') initiative have made 勤務時間(きんむじかん) a frequently discussed topic.

USAGE:

  • 勤務時間(きんむじかん)外(がい) ('outside working hours') and 勤務時間(きんむじかん)内(ない) ('within working hours') are common modifiers.
  • In Japan, the standard legal 勤務時間(きんむじかん) is eight hours per day and forty hours per week.
  • Often discussed alongside 残業(ざんぎょう) ('overtime') and 有給休暇(ゆうきゅうきゅうか) ('paid leave').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勤務時間(きんむじかん)を守(まも)る: to observe working hours
  • 勤務時間(きんむじかん)の短縮(たんしゅく): reduction of working hours
  • 勤務時間(きんむじかん)外(がい): outside working hours
  • 勤務時間(きんむじかん)中(ちゅう): during working hours

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 労働時間(ろうどうじかん): labor hours — a more formal and legal term, often used in labor law and statistics
  • 就業時間(しゅうぎょうじかん): business hours — similar in meaning, sometimes used interchangeably; emphasizes the established schedule
  • 営業時間(えいぎょうじかん): business hours — refers to the hours a store or office is open to customers, not employee work hours
