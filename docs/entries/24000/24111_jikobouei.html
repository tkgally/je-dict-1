1. self-defense; self-protection
The act of defending or protecting oneself from physical harm, psychological attack, or adverse situations. Encompasses both physical self-defense and broader protective measures such as information security and emotional boundaries.
自己防衛(じこぼうえい)のために護身術(ごしんじゅつ)を習(なら)う。
To learn self-defense techniques for self-protection.
ネット詐欺(さぎ)に対(たい)する自己防衛(じこぼうえい)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Self-protection against internet fraud is important.
彼女(かのじょ)の冷(つめ)たい態度(たいど)は自己防衛(じこぼうえい)の表(あらわ)れだと思(おも)う。
I think her cold attitude is a manifestation of self-defense.
災害(さいがい)に備(そな)えて、日頃(ひごろ)から自己防衛(じこぼうえい)の意識(いしき)を持(も)つことが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
It is important to maintain an awareness of self-protection on a daily basis in preparation for disasters.

Composed of 自己(じこ) (self) and 防衛(ぼうえい) (defense, protection). While it can refer to physical self-defense, it is used more broadly than its English equivalent. In psychology, it describes defensive emotional reactions; in daily life, it covers practical precautions against crime, fraud, disasters, and health risks.

The 自己(じこ) prefix is highly productive, creating many important compounds: 自己紹介(じこしょうかい) (self-introduction), 自己責任(じこせきにん) (self-responsibility), 自己管理(じこかんり) (self-management), and more.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己防衛(じこぼうえい)の手段(しゅだん): means of self-defense
  • 自己防衛(じこぼうえい)本能(ほんのう): self-defense instinct
  • 自己防衛(じこぼうえい)の意識(いしき): awareness of self-protection
  • 自己防衛(じこぼうえい)のために: for self-protection
  • 自己防衛(じこぼうえい)反応(はんのう): defensive reaction

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正当防衛(せいとうぼうえい): legitimate self-defense — the legal concept justifying use of force to protect oneself
  • 護身(ごしん): self-defense — focuses on physical techniques and personal safety
  • 自衛(じえい): self-defense — often used in military/national security contexts (自衛隊(じえいたい) = Self-Defense Forces)
