姻戚(いんせき)

いんせき
noun
relative by marriage; in-law
1. relative by marriage; in-law; affinity by marriage
A relative connected not by blood but by marriage — the family and relatives of one's spouse, and by extension the network of kinship ties created by marriage. A formal term used in legal, genealogical, and literary contexts rather than everyday family conversation.
姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)にある。
To be related by marriage.
姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)を結(むす)ぶ。
To establish kinship through marriage.
両家(りょうけ)は江戸(えど)時代(じだい)から続(つづ)く姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)にある。
The two families have had ties by marriage going back to the Edo period.

Compound of 姻(いん) ('marriage tie'; the same element as in 婚姻(こんいん)) and 戚(せき) ('relative; kin'). Refers specifically to kinship created through marriage, as opposed to 血縁(けつえん) ('blood relation'). A dignified, somewhat literary word, common in legal, historical, and formal family contexts but rare in ordinary conversation.

USAGE:

  • Appears mainly in the set phrase 姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい) ('relationship by marriage; in-law relationship').
  • Used in historical and genealogical writing to describe political alliances cemented by marriage — for example between noble families, feudal lords, or merchant houses.
  • In everyday speech, people refer to specific in-laws by term (義母(ぎぼ), 義父(ぎふ), etc.) or use general phrases like 妻(つま)の家族(かぞく) ('wife's family') rather than 姻戚(いんせき).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい): relationship by marriage; in-law relationship
  • 姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)を結(むす)ぶ: to establish a marriage-based relationship
  • 姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)にある: to be related by marriage
  • 姻戚(いんせき)関係(かんけい)を持(も)つ: to have a marriage tie
  • 遠(とお)い姻戚(いんせき): a distant relative by marriage

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 血縁(けつえん): blood relation — the antonym; kinship through shared ancestry rather than marriage.
  • 親戚(しんせき): relatives — everyday umbrella term covering both blood and marital relatives; the word most people use in conversation.
  • 縁戚(えんせき): relative; kinsman — near-synonym that emphasizes the connection by ties, covering both blood and marriage.
  • 義理(ぎり)の家族(かぞく): in-laws — informal everyday expression literally meaning 'obligated family'.
  • 婚姻(こんいん): marriage; wedlock — the formal legal term for marriage, sharing the 姻(いん) kanji.
