1. accidental ingestion; swallowing something by mistake
The unintentional swallowing of a harmful or inappropriate substance — most often medicine taken in the wrong dose, household chemicals, or small objects swallowed by children. A key term in child safety and poison control contexts.
子(こ)どもの誤飲(ごいん)に注意(ちゅうい)する。
To watch out for children accidentally swallowing things.
洗剤(せんざい)の誤飲(ごいん)事故(じこ)が増(ふ)えている。
Accidental ingestion of detergent is on the rise.
誤飲(ごいん)した場合(ばあい)は、すぐに医師(いし)に相談(そうだん)してください。
If something is swallowed by accident, please consult a doctor immediately.
ボタン電池(でんち)の誤飲(ごいん)は特(とく)に危険(きけん)です。
Accidental ingestion of button batteries is especially dangerous.

Composed of 誤(ご) (mistake, error) and 飲(いん) (drink, swallow). The term is widely used in medical settings, product safety warnings, and parenting contexts. It appears on product labels, especially for cleaning supplies and small items that pose a choking risk.

Most commonly associated with young children swallowing small objects (coins, buttons, batteries, toys) or household chemicals. Also used for adults who accidentally take the wrong medication or an incorrect dose.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誤飲(ごいん)事故(じこ): accidental ingestion incident
  • 誤飲(ごいん)防止(ぼうし): prevention of accidental ingestion
  • 誤飲(ごいん)する: to accidentally swallow
  • 誤飲(ごいん)に注意(ちゅうい): caution against accidental ingestion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誤食(ごしょく): accidental eating — eating the wrong food, often used for allergic reactions from unknowingly consuming allergens
  • 誤嚥(ごえん): aspiration — accidentally inhaling food or liquid into the airway; a medical term common in elderly care
