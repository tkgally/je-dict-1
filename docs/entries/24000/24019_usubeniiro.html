薄紅色(うすべにいろ)

うすべにいろ
noun
pale pink; light crimson; soft rose
1. pale pink; light crimson; soft rose
A soft, slightly warm pink — a lightened version of the traditional color 紅(べに) ('crimson; safflower red'). Evokes cherry blossoms, peach blossoms, and the delicate tones of traditional Japanese cosmetics and kimono, and is felt as a distinctly refined, classical color name rather than a modern industrial color term.
薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の花(はな)がきれいだ。
The pale pink flowers are beautiful.
夕焼(ゆうや)けで空(そら)が薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まった。
The sky was dyed a pale pink by the sunset.
祖母(そぼ)の桜柄(さくらがら)の着物(きもの)は、全体(ぜんたい)が上品(じょうひん)な薄紅色(うすべにいろ)をしている。
My grandmother's cherry-blossom-patterned kimono is a refined pale pink throughout.

Compound of 薄(うす)- ('thin; pale'), 紅(べに) ('crimson; safflower red'), and 色(いろ) ('color'). One of the traditional Japanese color names (伝統色(でんとうしょく)) that takes a base hue and modifies it with prefixes like 薄(うす)- or 濃(こ)-. Associated with cherry blossoms, peach blossoms, and classical aesthetics.

USAGE:

  • Often used to describe natural scenes (sunsets, flowers, dawn skies) and traditional items such as kimono, 和紙(わし) ('Japanese paper'), and porcelain.
  • Common in literary and lyrical writing; less common in everyday conversation, where ピンク(ぴんく) is the default.
  • Frequently appears in fabric, paint, and cosmetics product names to evoke a refined, traditional feel.
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まる ('to be dyed a pale pink') is a stock phrase in descriptive writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の花(はな): pale pink flowers
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)に染(そ)まる: to be dyed / tinged pale pink
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の空(そら): a pale pink sky
  • 薄紅色(うすべにいろ)の頬(ほお): pale pink cheeks
  • 上品(じょうひん)な薄紅色(うすべにいろ): a refined pale pink

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ピンク(ぴんく): pink — the everyday modern loanword; covers a wider range of pinks but lacks the traditional feel.
  • 桜色(さくらいろ): cherry-blossom pink — a very pale pink named after cherry petals; often overlaps with 薄紅色(うすべにいろ) but is slightly paler and cooler.
  • 桃色(ももいろ): peach pink — slightly warmer and more vivid than 薄紅色(うすべにいろ); also has metaphorical associations with romance.
  • 紅色(べにいろ): crimson — the deeper base color; 薄紅色(うすべにいろ) is its lightened version.
  • 淡紅色(たんこうしょく): pale red; light pink — a more technical / literary Sino-Japanese synonym used in biology and medicine (e.g., flesh tones, blood-related descriptions).
