敷地面積(しきちめんせき)

しきちめんせき
noun
site area; lot area; total area of land on which a building stands
1. site area; lot area; total area of land on which a building stands
The total area of a piece of land (a building lot, factory site, school grounds, etc.), normally measured in square metres or 坪(つぼ) (a traditional unit of about 3.3 m²). A standard item in real-estate listings, building plans, and construction documents, often shown alongside the building footprint and total floor area.
敷地面積(しきちめんせき)は百(ひゃく)平米(へいべい)だ。
The site area is 100 square metres.
広(ひろ)い敷地面積(しきちめんせき)を活用(かつよう)する。
To make use of the large site area.
新(あたら)しい工場(こうじょう)の敷地面積(しきちめんせき)は、東京(とうきょう)ドーム約(やく)三(さん)つ分(ぶん)に相当(そうとう)する。
The site area of the new factory is equivalent to about three Tokyo Domes.

Compound of 敷地(しきち) ('building site; plot of land') and 面積(めんせき) ('area'). One of the standard pieces of information given in any Japanese real estate or construction document.

USAGE:

  • Standard real-estate listings show three areas: 敷地面積(しきちめんせき) (site area), 建築(けんちく)面積(めんせき) (building footprint), and 延床面積(のべゆかめんせき) (total floor area, summed across all floors).
  • Often expressed in 平米(へいべい) (m²) or 坪(つぼ).
  • 敷地(しきち) can refer to a residential lot, a school campus, a factory site, or a temple grounds; 敷地面積(しきちめんせき) is the area of any of these.
  • The Japanese building code restricts the 建蔽率(けんぺいりつ) ('building-to-land ratio') and 容積率(ようせきりつ) ('floor-area ratio') based on the 敷地面積(しきちめんせき).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敷地面積(しきちめんせき)は約(やく)〜平米(へいべい): the site area is approximately ... square metres
  • 広(ひろ)い敷地面積(しきちめんせき): a large site area
  • 敷地面積(しきちめんせき)を確保(かくほ)する: to secure (a sufficient) site area
  • 敷地面積(しきちめんせき)当(あ)たり: per unit of site area
  • 敷地面積(しきちめんせき)換算(かんさん): in terms of site area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建築(けんちく)面積(めんせき): building (footprint) area — area covered by the building when seen from above.
  • 延床面積(のべゆかめんせき): total floor area — sum of floor areas across all storeys.
  • 土地(とち)面積(めんせき): land area — close synonym, sometimes used in surveying contexts.
  • 敷地(しきち): building site; plot of land — the lot itself, without explicit reference to its area.
  • 建蔽率(けんぺいりつ): building-to-land ratio — the ratio of 建築(けんちく)面積(めんせき) to 敷地面積(しきちめんせき), used in building regulation.
