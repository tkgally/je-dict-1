会話(かいわ)を交(か)わす

かいわをかわす
expression
to exchange conversation; to have a conversation
1. to exchange conversation; to have a conversation; to converse
To engage in back-and-forth conversation with someone. Slightly more formal or literary than simply 話す(はなす), emphasizing the mutual, reciprocal nature of the exchange.
隣(となり)の人(ひと)と会話(かいわ)を交(か)わした。
I had a conversation with the person next to me.
久(ひさ)しぶりに友人(ゆうじん)と楽(たの)しい会話(かいわ)を交(か)わした。
I enjoyed a pleasant conversation with my friend after a long time.
初対面(しょたいめん)でも自然(しぜん)に会話(かいわ)を交(か)わせるようになりたい。
I want to become able to naturally converse even with people I'm meeting for the first time.

A set expression combining 会話(かいわ) ('conversation') with the verb 交(か)わす ('to exchange'). The verb 交(か)わす adds a nuance of mutual, reciprocal exchange not present in simple 話す(はなす).

USAGE:

  • More literary and descriptive than casual 話(はな)す or {しゃべる}
  • Often used in narrative and written contexts to describe social interactions
  • The same verb 交(か)わす appears in similar reciprocal expressions: 言葉(ことば)を交(か)わす ('to exchange words'), 挨拶(あいさつ)を交(か)わす ('to exchange greetings')

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 短(みじか)い会話(かいわ)を交(か)わす: to have a brief conversation
  • 楽(たの)しい会話(かいわ)を交(か)わす: to have an enjoyable conversation
  • 英語(えいご)で会話(かいわ)を交(か)わす: to converse in English
  • 目(め)で会話(かいわ)を交(か)わす: to communicate through eye contact

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 言葉(ことば)を交(か)わす: to exchange words — more general, can be brief
  • 挨拶(あいさつ)を交(か)わす: to exchange greetings — specifically about greetings
  • 話(はなし)をする: to have a talk — more casual and everyday
