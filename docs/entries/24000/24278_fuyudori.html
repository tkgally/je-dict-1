冬鳥(ふゆどり)

ふゆどり
noun
winter bird; winter visitor (bird)
1. winter bird; winter visitor; migratory bird that arrives in winter
A bird species that migrates to Japan (or another region) to spend the winter, then returns to its breeding grounds in the north for summer. Contrasts with 夏鳥(なつどり), which arrives for summer.
白鳥(はくちょう)は日本(にほん)の代表的(だいひょうてき)な冬鳥(ふゆどり)だ。
The swan is a typical winter bird in Japan.
十一月(じゅういちがつ)ごろから冬鳥(ふゆどり)が湖(みずうみ)に集(あつ)まり始(はじ)める。
Around November, winter birds start gathering at the lake.
温暖化(おんだんか)の影響(えいきょう)で冬鳥(ふゆどり)の飛来数(ひらいすう)が年(ねん)々減少(げんしょう)しているという報告(ほうこく)がある。
There are reports that the number of winter bird arrivals is decreasing year by year due to global warming.

Compound of 冬(ふゆ) ('winter') and 鳥(どり) ('bird'). The counterpart of 夏鳥(なつどり), classifying migratory birds by the season they spend in Japan. Well-known winter birds in Japan include 白鳥(はくちょう) (swans), マガモ (mallard ducks), and ツグミ (thrushes).

USAGE:

  • Used in birdwatching, nature education, and environmental monitoring
  • Winter bird observation is a popular seasonal activity at lakes and wetlands across Japan
  • The arrival and departure dates of winter birds are tracked as indicators of climate change

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冬鳥(ふゆどり)が飛来(ひらい)する: winter birds arrive
  • 冬鳥(ふゆどり)を観察(かんさつ)する: to observe winter birds
  • 冬鳥(ふゆどり)の飛来地(ひらいち): winter bird arrival site
  • 冬鳥(ふゆどり)が北(きた)へ帰(かえ)る: winter birds return north

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夏鳥(なつどり): summer bird — migratory birds that come for summer breeding
  • 留鳥(りゅうちょう): resident bird — birds that stay year-round without migrating
  • 渡り鳥(わたりどり): migratory bird — general term for any bird that migrates
