A legal term for an organization formed by a group of people (as opposed to 財団(ざいだん), which is formed around assets/property). Very commonly seen in official organizational names.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社団(しゃだん)法人(ほうじん): incorporated association (the full legal entity type)
  • 一般(いっぱん)社団(しゃだん)法人(ほうじん): general incorporated association
  • 公益(こうえき)社団(しゃだん)法人(ほうじん): public interest incorporated association

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 財団(ざいだん): foundation — a legal entity organized around a pool of assets or endowment, rather than members
  • 法人(ほうじん): legal entity, corporation — a broader term for any entity with legal personhood
  • NPO法人(ほうじん): NPO, nonprofit organization — a separate legal category for nonprofits
