1. isolation; severance; complete separation
The state of being completely cut off or separated from others, whether physically, socially, or emotionally. Implies a greater degree of separation than ordinary isolation.
外(そと)の世界(せかい)から隔絶(かくぜつ)されている。
To be completely isolated from the outside world.
文明(ぶんめい)から隔絶(かくぜつ)した村(むら)で暮(く)らす。
To live in a village cut off from civilization.
社会(しゃかい)との隔絶(かくぜつ)を感(かん)じる人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
More people are feeling a sense of severance from society.
雪(ゆき)で隔絶(かくぜつ)された山間(さんかん)の集落(しゅうらく)に救援物資(きゅうえんぶっし)が届(とど)いた。
Relief supplies reached the mountain settlement that had been cut off by snow.

Describes a thorough, complete separation — stronger than 隔離(かくり) (quarantine/isolation) or 孤立(こりつ) (solitude/standing alone). Often used for geographic remoteness or profound social disconnection. Can function as a noun or as a する verb (隔絶(かくぜつ)する).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隔絶(かくぜつ)された場所(ばしょ): an isolated place
  • 隔絶(かくぜつ)された環境(かんきょう): an isolated environment
  • 世間(せけん)から隔絶(かくぜつ)する: to be cut off from the world
  • 隔絶(かくぜつ)感(かん): sense of isolation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 孤立(こりつ): isolation — emphasizes being alone, standing apart; more common in everyday usage
  • 隔離(かくり): isolation, quarantine — often medical or enforced separation
  • 断絶(だんぜつ): severance, discontinuation — emphasizes a complete break in a relationship or connection
  • 遮断(しゃだん): cutoff, blockade — focuses on blocking communication or access

FORMATION:
隔(かく) (to separate, to partition) + 絶(ぜつ) (to sever, to cut off) — both characters reinforce the idea of complete separation.
