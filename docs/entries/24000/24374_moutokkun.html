1. rigorous intensive training; grueling practice; crash course of training
Extremely intense and demanding training or practice, often undertaken over a concentrated period to achieve rapid improvement. Implies pushing oneself or being pushed to physical or mental limits.
試合(しあい)前(まえ)に猛特訓(もうとっくん)をした。
We did intensive training before the match.
夏休(なつやす)みに英語(えいご)の猛特訓(もうとっくん)を受(う)けた。
I underwent rigorous English training during summer vacation.
監督(かんとく)の猛特訓(もうとっくん)のおかげで全国(ぜんこく)大会(たいかい)に出場(しゅつじょう)できた。
Thanks to the coach's grueling training, we were able to compete in the national tournament.

Compound of 猛(もう) ('fierce; intense') and 特訓(とっくん) ('special training; intensive practice'). An emphatic intensification of 特訓(とっくん) — if 特訓(とっくん) is intensive training, 猛特訓(もうとっくん) is training that is fierce, relentless, and grueling.

USAGE:

  • Common in sports, martial arts, and exam preparation contexts.
  • Often used with する to form a verb: 猛特訓(もうとっくん)する ('to undergo rigorous training').
  • Carries a positive connotation of dedication and determination, though it can also imply harsh or exhausting conditions.
  • Frequently appears in manga, anime, and sports journalism to describe training arcs or preparation montages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 猛特訓(もうとっくん)をする: to do rigorous training
  • 猛特訓(もうとっくん)を受(う)ける: to undergo intensive training
  • 猛特訓(もうとっくん)の成果(せいか): the results of intensive training
  • 猛特訓(もうとっくん)の日々(ひび): days of grueling practice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 特訓(とっくん): special training; intensive practice — less extreme than 猛特訓(もうとっくん)
  • 猛練習(もうれんしゅう): fierce practice — near-synonym using 練習(れんしゅう) ('practice') instead of 特訓(とっくん)
  • 合宿(がっしゅく): training camp — a residential training event, often involving intensive group practice
  • 鍛錬(たんれん): discipline; tempering — long-term character- and skill-building through effort
