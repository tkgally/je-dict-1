代表曲(だいひょうきょく)

だいひょうきょく
noun
signature song; representative piece; best-known work (of a musician or composer)
1. signature song; representative piece; best-known work (of a musician or composer)
signature song; representative piece; best-known work (of a musician or composer)
この曲(きょく)は彼(かれ)の代表曲(だいひょうきょく)だ。
This song is his signature piece.
コンサートの最後(さいご)に代表曲(だいひょうきょく)が演奏(えんそう)された。
The signature song was performed at the end of the concert.
彼女(かのじょ)にはヒット曲(きょく)が多(おお)いが、代表曲(だいひょうきょく)と言(い)えばやはりデビュー作(さく)だろう。
She has many hit songs, but her signature piece is surely her debut release.

A compound of 代表(だいひょう) (representative) and 曲(きょく) (piece of music). Refers to the single most recognized or iconic work of a musician, band, or composer.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 代表曲(だいひょうきょく)と言(い)えば: speaking of signature songs
  • 代表曲(だいひょうきょく)を歌(うた)う: to sing one's signature song
  • 代表曲(だいひょうきょく)を演奏(えんそう)する: to perform one's signature piece

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ヒット曲(きょく): hit song — a commercially successful song, but not necessarily the most representative one
  • 名曲(めいきょく): masterpiece, classic — a renowned piece of music regardless of who composed it
  • 代表作(だいひょうさく): representative work — the broader equivalent covering literature, film, art, etc., not just music
