時間(じかん)が経(た)つ

じかんがたつ
expression
time passes; time goes by
1. time passes; time goes by; time elapses
A common expression meaning that time moves forward or a period of time has elapsed. Used to describe the passage of time in both concrete and emotional contexts.
時間(じかん)が経(た)つのが早(はや)い。
Time passes quickly.
あれから三年(さんねん)時間(じかん)が経(た)った。
Three years have passed since then.
時間(じかん)が経(た)つにつれて、傷(きず)は少(すこ)しずつ癒(い)えていった。
As time went by, the wound gradually healed.

One of the most frequently used time expressions in Japanese. The verb 経(た)つ ('to elapse; to pass') is specific to the passage of time and is distinct from the homophone 立(た)つ ('to stand').

USAGE:

  • Often used with のが早(はや)い to express surprise at how quickly time passes.
  • The pattern 時間(じかん)が経(た)つにつれて ('as time passes') is very common for describing gradual change.
  • Can be modified with specific time periods: 一時間(いちじかん)が経(た)つ ('one hour passes'), 長(なが)い時間(じかん)が経(た)つ ('a long time passes').
  • In casual speech, 時間(じかん)が経(た)つ is sometimes shortened to 時(とき)が経(た)つ or 月日(つきひ)が経(た)つ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間(じかん)が経(た)つのが早(はや)い: time passes quickly
  • 時間(じかん)が経(た)つにつれて: as time goes by
  • だいぶ時間(じかん)が経(た)った: quite some time has passed
  • 時間(じかん)が経(た)っても: even after time passes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 月日(つきひ)が経(た)つ: days and months pass — a more literary or reflective variant emphasizing the slow accumulation of time
  • 時(とき)が流(なが)れる: time flows — a more poetic expression
  • 年月(ねんげつ)が過(す)ぎる: years pass — emphasizes the passage of longer periods
