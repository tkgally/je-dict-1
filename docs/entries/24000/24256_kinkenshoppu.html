金券(きんけん)ショップ

きんけんしょっぷ
noun
ticket shop; voucher shop; discount ticket store
1. ticket shop; voucher shop; discount ticket store
A shop that buys and sells various types of tickets, vouchers, and gift cards at discounted prices. A distinctively Japanese retail concept commonly found near major train stations, these shops sell discounted train tickets, stamps, gift certificates, phone cards, and event tickets.
金券(きんけん)ショップで新幹線(しんかんせん)の切符(きっぷ)を買(か)った。
I bought Shinkansen tickets at a discount ticket shop.
駅前(えきまえ)に金券(きんけん)ショップがある。
There's a ticket shop in front of the station.
使(つか)わない商品券(しょうひんけん)は金券(きんけん)ショップに売(う)れば現金(げんきん)になる。
If you sell unused gift certificates to a ticket shop, you can get cash for them.

Compound of 金券(きんけん) ('monetary voucher; ticket with cash value') and ショップ ('shop'). A uniquely Japanese type of secondhand dealer specializing in tickets and vouchers. They operate as both buyer and seller — customers can sell unwanted tickets for cash or buy discounted ones.

WHAT THEY SELL:

  • 新幹線(しんかんせん)の回数券(かいすうけん): Shinkansen discount ticket books (individual tickets sold separately)
  • 商品券(しょうひんけん): department store gift certificates
  • 切手(きって): stamps (at a small discount)
  • 株主優待券(かぶぬしゆうたいけん): shareholder benefit tickets
  • テレホンカード: phone cards
  • 映画(えいが)鑑賞券(かんしょうけん): movie tickets

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 金券(きんけん)ショップで買(か)う: to buy at a ticket shop
  • 金券(きんけん)ショップに売(う)る: to sell to a ticket shop
  • 金券(きんけん)ショップの相場(そうば): ticket shop prices/rates

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • チケットショップ: ticket shop — a synonym using the English loanword
  • 質屋(しちや): pawn shop — a broader secondhand dealer that accepts physical goods as collateral, not just tickets
  • 中古(ちゅうこ)ショップ: secondhand shop — sells used goods generally, not specifically tickets and vouchers
