生活用品(せいかつようひん)

せいかつようひん
noun
daily necessities; household goods; everyday items
1. daily necessities; household goods; everyday items
daily necessities; household goods; everyday items
生活用品(せいかつようひん)を買(か)いに行(い)く。
To go buy daily necessities.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し先(さき)で必要(ひつよう)な生活用品(せいかつようひん)をリストにした。
I made a list of daily necessities needed at the new place.
被災地(ひさいち)に生活用品(せいかつようひん)を届(とど)けるボランティア(ぼらんてぃあ)が集(あつ)まった。
Volunteers gathered to deliver daily necessities to the disaster-affected area.

A general term for items used in everyday life such as toiletries, cleaning supplies, kitchen items, and personal care products. Commonly seen in store signage and shopping contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 生活用品(せいかつようひん)売(う)り場(ば): daily necessities section (in a store)
  • 生活用品(せいかつようひん)を揃(そろ)える: to get one's daily necessities together
  • 生活用品(せいかつようひん)の支援(しえん): providing daily necessities (as aid)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 日用品(にちようひん): daily goods — very similar in meaning; 日用品(にちようひん) is slightly more common in everyday speech
  • 生活(せいかつ)必需品(ひつじゅひん): daily necessities — emphasizes the essential, indispensable nature of the items
  • 消耗品(しょうもうひん): consumable goods — items that are used up and need replacing, such as tissues or batteries
