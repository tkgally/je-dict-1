喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)

きっきんのかだい
expression
an urgent issue; a pressing problem that must be addressed
1. an urgent issue; a pressing matter; a top-priority problem
A set phrase used in formal speech, government statements, business reports, and editorials to label a problem that demands immediate attention. It carries weight and seriousness — appropriate for high-stakes policy or organizational matters, not for everyday concerns.
少子化(しょうしか)は喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)だ。
The declining birthrate is an urgent issue.
エネルギー安定供給(あんていきょうきゅう)の確保(かくほ)は喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)である。
Securing a stable supply of energy is a pressing issue.
首相(しゅしょう)は記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で、物価高対策(ぶっかだかたいさく)が政府(せいふ)にとって喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)であると強調(きょうちょう)した。
At the press conference, the prime minister stressed that addressing rising prices is a pressing issue for the government.
地域(ちいき)の医療(いりょう)体制(たいせい)を維持(いじ)するためには、若手(わかて)医師(いし)の確保(かくほ)と過疎地(かそち)への派遣(はけん)が喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)となっている。
In order to maintain the regional healthcare system, securing young doctors and dispatching them to depopulated areas has become a pressing issue.

Composed of 喫緊(きっきん) ('urgent; pressing') + の}{課題(かだい) ('issue; problem'). 喫緊(きっきん) alone means 'most urgent,' but it almost always appears in this fixed combination in modern usage.

USAGE:

  • A formal, written-style expression most at home in government statements, business reports, editorials, and academic policy discussions.
  • Frequently follows a topic marked by は or にとって: '〜は喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)だ' ('X is a pressing issue') or '〜にとって喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)となっている' ('has become a pressing issue for X').
  • Avoid in casual conversation. Spoken Japanese would say 急(きゅう)いで解決(かいけつ)しなければならない問題(もんだい) ('a problem that must be solved urgently') or just 大事(だいじ)な問題(もんだい) ('an important issue').
  • Often paired with verbs like 取(と)り組(く)む ('to tackle'), 対応(たいおう)する ('to respond to'), 解決(かいけつ)する ('to resolve').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)に取(と)り組(く)む: to tackle a pressing issue
  • 喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)となっている: has become a pressing issue
  • 喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)と位置(いち)付(づ)ける: to position something as a pressing issue
  • 喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい)解決(かいけつ): resolution of pressing issues
  • 国(くに)にとっての喫緊(きっきん)の課題(かだい): a pressing issue for the country

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 緊急(きんきゅう)課題(かだい): emergency/urgent issue — slightly stronger sense of immediate emergency; common in disaster and crisis contexts.
  • 重要(じゅうよう)課題(かだい): an important issue — stresses importance but not necessarily urgency.
  • 当面(とうめん)の課題(かだい): an immediate/near-term issue — focuses on what must be addressed in the short term, without the heavy 'urgent' connotation.
  • 差(さ)し迫(せま)った問題(もんだい): a pressing problem — more colloquial alternative; can be used in everyday speech.
