通話圏(つうわけん)

つうわけん
noun
(mobile phone) coverage area; service area
1. (mobile phone) coverage area; service area; area within signal range
The geographical area in which a mobile phone or other wireless device can make and receive calls through a carrier's network. Going outside this area results in dropped calls or no signal.
通話圏外(つうわけんがい)だ。
We're out of service range.
山(やま)の中(なか)では通話圏(つうわけん)から外(はず)れることが多(おお)い。
You often fall out of the coverage area deep in the mountains.
契約前(けいやくまえ)に自宅(じたく)が通話圏内(つうわけんない)か確認(かくにん)したほうがよい。
Before signing a contract, it's a good idea to check whether your home is in the coverage area.
最近(さいきん)は地下鉄(ちかてつ)の駅(えき)も通話圏(つうわけん)に含(ふく)まれるようになり、ずいぶん便利(べんり)になった。
Recently subway stations have also been brought into the coverage area, which has made things much more convenient.

Composed of 通話(つうわ) (phone call, communication) and 圏(けん) (sphere, range, zone). A telecommunications term that is most familiar to ordinary users through the phrase 通話圏外(つうわけんがい) (out of the coverage area) displayed on mobile phones.

USAGE:
通話圏(つうわけん) itself is less commonly seen standalone than as part of the compounds 通話圏内(つうわけんない) (in the coverage area) and 通話圏外(つうわけんがい) (out of the coverage area). These fixed pairs are the practical forms learners will encounter most often.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通話圏内(つうわけんない): within the coverage area
  • 通話圏外(つうわけんがい): out of the coverage area
  • 通話圏(つうわけん)から外(はず)れる: to fall outside the coverage area
  • 通話圏(つうわけん)が広(ひろ)い: to have a wide coverage area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • {サービスエリア}: service area — the carrier-marketing term, frequently seen in ads and coverage maps
  • 電波(でんぱ)が届(とど)く: the signal reaches — a more casual way to describe the same idea ("I have reception here")
  • 圏外(けんがい): out of range — the short form commonly shown on a phone's screen when it has no signal
