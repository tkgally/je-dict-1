1. round (of golf); a full game of 18 holes
A complete game of golf, typically 18 holes. Also used for a half-round (ハーフラウンド, 9 holes).
週末(しゅうまつ)にラウンドする。
Play a round of golf on the weekend.
今日(きょう)は天気(てんき)がいいから、気持(きも)ちのいいラウンドになった。
The weather was good today, so it was a pleasant round.
初(はじ)めてのラウンドで緊張(きんちょう)したが、同伴者(どうはんしゃ)に助(たす)けられて楽(たの)しめた。
I was nervous on my first round, but my playing partners helped me enjoy it.
2. round (of boxing, competition, negotiations)
A single period or stage in boxing, tournaments, discussions, or negotiations.
第(だい)3ラウンド開始(かいし)。
Start of round three.
交渉(こうしょう)は次(つぎ)のラウンドに持(も)ち越(こ)された。
Negotiations were carried over to the next round.
ボクシングの試合(しあい)は12ラウンドまで行(い)ったが、判定(はんてい)勝(が)ちとなった。
The boxing match went to 12 rounds, and it was decided by judges' decision.

A loanword from English "round." Most commonly associated with golf in everyday Japanese.

USAGE:
In casual conversation, ラウンド without further context almost always means a round of golf. For boxing and tournaments, the context (ボクシング, 試合(しあい), etc.) is usually provided. ラウンドする is used as a suru-verb meaning "to play a round of golf." In business contexts, ラウンド can refer to rounds of funding (資金調達(しきんちょうたつ)ラウンド) or rounds of negotiation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ラウンドする: to play a round of golf
  • ラウンドを回(まわ)る: to go around the course
  • 第(だい)1ラウンド: round one, first round
  • 最終(さいしゅう)ラウンド: final round
  • ハーフラウンド: half round (9 holes of golf)
  • 練習(れんしゅう)ラウンド: practice round

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 回戦(かいせん): round (of a tournament) — the native counter for tournament rounds (一回戦(いっかいせん) = first round)
  • 局(きょく): round, game — used for go, shogi, and similar board games
  • セット: set — used in tennis, volleyball, and similar sports
