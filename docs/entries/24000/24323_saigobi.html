最後尾(さいごび)

さいごび
noun
tail end; rear; end of the line
1. tail end; rear; very back of a line or procession
The very last position in a queue, procession, or sequence. Commonly used when referring to the end of a line of people waiting, or the rear of a group or formation.
最後尾(さいごび)はあちらです。
The end of the line is over there.
最後尾(さいごび)に並(なら)んでください。
Please line up at the end of the line.
行列(ぎょうれつ)の最後尾(さいごび)まで歩(ある)いたら、駅(えき)の角(かど)を曲(ま)がったところまで続(つづ)いていた。
When I walked to the end of the line, it extended around the corner of the station.

Combines 最後(さいご) (last) and 尾(び) (tail). The 尾(び) character reinforces the image of the very tail end — beyond merely being "last" (最後(さいご)), 最後尾(さいごび) specifically indicates the physical end position.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 最後尾(さいごび)に並(なら)ぶ: to line up at the back
  • 最後尾(さいごび)の人(ひと): the person at the very back
  • 最後尾(さいごび)札(ふだ): "end of line" sign (held by staff)
  • 行列(ぎょうれつ)の最後尾(さいごび): end of a queue

USAGE:
Very commonly seen at popular restaurants, theme parks, event venues, and shops where queues form. Staff members often hold signs reading 最後尾(さいごび) to indicate where the line ends. The word carries a polite, somewhat formal register and is the standard term used in public signage.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最後(さいご): last — refers to the final item in a sequence, but does not specifically mean the physical end of a line
  • 後方(こうほう): rear — indicates the back area in general, not specifically the tail end of a queue
  • 末尾(まつび): end, tail — used for the end of a list or text, rarely for physical queues
