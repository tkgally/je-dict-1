1. wink
A quick closing and opening of one eye, used as a signal, flirtation, or playful gesture.
彼女(かのじょ)がウィンクをした。
She winked.
カメラに向(む)かってウィンクする。
To wink at the camera.
アイドルがファンにウィンクを飛(と)ばして会場(かいじょう)が盛(も)り上(あ)がった。
The idol blew a wink at the fans and the venue erupted with excitement.

A loanword from English "wink." In Japanese culture, winking is primarily associated with entertainment, anime, and idol performances rather than everyday interpersonal communication. It carries a playful, performative connotation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ウィンクをする: to wink
  • ウィンクを飛(と)ばす: to blow/send a wink (from a distance)
  • 片目(かため)でウィンクする: to wink with one eye

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目配(めくば)せ: a meaningful glance or eye signal — used for discreet, non-verbal communication between people

CULTURAL NOTE:
Unlike in Western cultures where winking can be a casual social gesture, in Japan it is more commonly seen in performances, photo poses, and anime/manga. Winking at a stranger in daily life would be unusual.
