油彩画(ゆさいが)

ゆさいが
noun
oil painting (the artwork)
1. oil painting (the artwork)

美術館(びじゅつかん)に油彩画(ゆさいが)が飾(かざ)られている。
Oil paintings are displayed in the art museum.
この油彩画(ゆさいが)は十九世紀(じゅうきゅうせいき)のフランスで描(えが)かれた。
This oil painting was painted in nineteenth-century France.
祖父(そふ)は趣味(しゅみ)で油彩画(ゆさいが)を描(か)いており、自宅(じたく)には何十枚(なんじゅうまい)もの作品(さくひん)がある。
My grandfather paints oil paintings as a hobby, and there are dozens of works at his home.

Refers specifically to a painting created with oil-based paints. The formal term for what is colloquially called 油絵(あぶらえ). Oil painting as a technique was introduced to Japan during the Meiji period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 油彩画(ゆさいが)を描(か)く: to paint an oil painting
  • 油彩画(ゆさいが)展(てん): oil painting exhibition
  • 小品(しょうひん)の油彩画(ゆさいが): a small oil painting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 油絵(あぶらえ): oil painting — the everyday, colloquial equivalent
  • 水彩画(すいさいが): watercolor painting — uses water-soluble paints; lighter and more transparent than oil
  • 油彩(ゆさい): oil painting (technique/medium) — refers to the method rather than the finished work
