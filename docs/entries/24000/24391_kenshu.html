堅守(けんしゅ)

けんしゅ
noun
firm defense; holding fast; tenacious guarding
1. firm defense; holding fast; tenacious guarding
Defending a position, principle, or territory stubbornly and without yielding. Used in military, sports, and figurative contexts.
城(しろ)を堅守(けんしゅ)する。
To firmly defend the castle.
チームの堅守(けんしゅ)が勝利(しょうり)につながった。
The team's solid defense led to victory.
最後(さいご)まで自分(じぶん)の立場(たちば)を堅守(けんしゅ)した。
He firmly maintained his position until the end.
堅守(けんしゅ)速攻(そっこう)のスタイルで相手(あいて)チームを圧倒(あっとう)した。
They overwhelmed the opposing team with a style of solid defense and fast counterattack.

Describes unyielding, tenacious defense — whether of a physical position, a principle, or a sports strategy. The word emphasizes stubbornness and refusal to give ground.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 堅守(けんしゅ)速攻(そっこう): solid defense and quick counterattack (common sports strategy term)
  • 城(しろ)を堅守(けんしゅ)する: to firmly defend a castle
  • 立場(たちば)を堅守(けんしゅ)する: to firmly maintain one's position
  • 堅守(けんしゅ)の構(かま)え: a defensive stance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 防御(ぼうぎょ): defense — the general, neutral term for defense
  • 防衛(ぼうえい): defense, protection — often national or organizational defense
  • 死守(ししゅ): defense to the death — even stronger than 堅守(けんしゅ), implying willingness to die defending
  • 固守(こしゅ): stubborn adherence — similar meaning but more often used for clinging to ideas or customs

FORMATION:
堅(けん) (hard, firm, solid) + 守(しゅ) (defend, protect) — literally "firm defense."
