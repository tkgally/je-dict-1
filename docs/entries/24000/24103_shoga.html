書画(しょが)

しょが
noun
calligraphy and painting; written and visual art
1. calligraphy and painting; works of brush art
The combined art of calligraphy (brush writing) and painting, traditionally viewed as closely related disciplines in East Asian culture. Also refers to individual works or collections that include both calligraphic and pictorial pieces.
書画(しょが)を鑑賞(かんしょう)する。
To appreciate calligraphy and paintings.
祖父(そふ)は書画(しょが)を趣味(しゅみ)にしている。
My grandfather's hobby is calligraphy and painting.
美術館(びじゅつかん)で中国(ちゅうごく)の古(ふる)い書画(しょが)の展覧会(てんらんかい)が開(ひら)かれている。
An exhibition of old Chinese calligraphy and paintings is being held at the art museum.
床(とこ)の間(ま)に掛(か)けられた書画(しょが)が部屋(へや)の雰囲気(ふんいき)を引(ひ)き立(た)てている。
The calligraphy and painting hung in the alcove enhances the atmosphere of the room.

Composed of 書(しょ) (writing, calligraphy) and 画(が) (picture, painting). In East Asian tradition, calligraphy and painting share the same fundamental tool — the brush — and are considered sister arts. The two are often practiced, displayed, and collected together.

書画(しょが) appears frequently in art criticism, museum exhibitions, and discussions of traditional Japanese and Chinese culture. Works of 書画(しょが) are commonly displayed in the 床(とこ)の間(ま) (alcove) of a traditional Japanese room.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 書画(しょが)を鑑賞(かんしょう)する: to appreciate calligraphy and paintings
  • 書画(しょが)の展覧会(てんらんかい): calligraphy and painting exhibition
  • 書画(しょが)の骨董(こっとう): antique calligraphy and paintings
  • 書画(しょが)を集(あつ)める: to collect calligraphy and paintings
  • 書画(しょが)骨董(こっとう)品(ひん): antique art and calligraphy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書道(しょどう): calligraphy — the practice or art of calligraphy as a discipline
  • 絵画(かいが): painting — paintings as an art form, without the calligraphy component
  • 掛(か)け軸(じく): hanging scroll — a specific format for displaying 書画(しょが)
