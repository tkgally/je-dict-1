唯一性(ゆいいつせい)

ゆいいつせい
noun
uniqueness; singularity; the quality of being one of a kind
1. uniqueness; singularity; the quality of being the only one of its kind
The abstract quality of being unique — the property that nothing else is equivalent or a substitute. Used in philosophy, business, mathematics, database design, and identity-related discussions to describe the one-of-a-kind nature of a thing or value.
唯一性(ゆいいつせい)がある。
It has uniqueness.
この作品(さくひん)の唯一性(ゆいいつせい)が価値(かち)を生(う)んでいる。
The uniqueness of this work is what gives it its value.
データベースではIDの唯一性(ゆいいつせい)を保証(ほしょう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
In a database, you need to guarantee the uniqueness of IDs.
NFTはデジタルデータに唯一性(ゆいいつせい)を持(も)たせる仕組(しく)みとして注目(ちゅうもく)された。
NFTs drew attention as a mechanism for giving digital data a quality of uniqueness.

Composed of 唯一(ゆいいつ) (only one, sole) and the abstract-noun-forming suffix 〜性(せい) (-ness, -ity).

USAGE:
唯一性(ゆいいつせい) is an abstract, somewhat formal word. It is typical in writing — academic papers, legal and business documents, technical specifications, philosophical discussions — rather than casual conversation. In information-technology contexts it is often a translation of the English term "uniqueness" (as in unique key or unique identifier).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 唯一性(ゆいいつせい)を持(も)つ: to have / possess uniqueness
  • 唯一性(ゆいいつせい)を保証(ほしょう)する: to guarantee uniqueness
  • 唯一性(ゆいいつせい)がある: to be unique
  • 唯一性(ゆいいつせい)を証明(しょうめい)する: to prove uniqueness (mathematical sense)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 独自性(どくじせい): originality, distinctiveness — emphasizes being one's own and different, not merely the only instance
  • 一意性(いちいせい): uniqueness (mathematics, computing) — near-synonym, especially common in data modeling
  • 特殊性(とくしゅせい): peculiarity, special nature — the quality of being special or particular, not necessarily one-of-a-kind
  • 独創性(どくそうせい): originality, creativity — uniqueness of idea or creation
