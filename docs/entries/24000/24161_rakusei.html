1. completion of a building; inauguration of a new structure
completion of a building; inauguration of a new structure
新(あたら)しいビルが落成(らくせい)した。
The new building was completed.
落成(らくせい)式(しき)に多(おお)くの来賓(らいひん)が招(まね)かれた。
Many guests were invited to the inauguration ceremony.
図書館(としょかん)の落成(らくせい)は来年(らいねん)の春(はる)に予定(よてい)されている。
The completion of the library is scheduled for next spring.

Specifically refers to the completion of a building or large structure, often accompanied by a formal ceremony. Not used for completing tasks, projects, or abstract work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 落成(らくせい)式(しき): inauguration ceremony, completion ceremony
  • 落成(らくせい)披露(ひろう): unveiling of a completed building
  • 落成(らくせい)記念(きねん): commemoration of building completion
  • 落成(らくせい)祝(いわ)い: celebration of building completion

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 完成(かんせい): completion — general-purpose; used for any project, work, or object, not limited to buildings
  • 竣工(しゅんこう): completion of construction — technical term used in the construction industry, more formal than 落成(らくせい)
  • 開業(かいぎょう): opening of business — focuses on starting operations rather than building completion
