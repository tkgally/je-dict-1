整骨(せいこつ)

せいこつ
noun
bonesetting; osteopathic treatment
1. bonesetting; osteopathic treatment; orthopedic manipulation
A traditional form of manual therapy that treats bone fractures, joint dislocations, sprains, and musculoskeletal injuries through manipulation rather than surgery. In modern Japan, practiced by licensed judo therapists at clinics called 整骨院.
整骨院(せいこついん)に通(かよ)っている。
I go to a bonesetting clinic regularly.
腰(こし)を痛(いた)めて整骨院(せいこついん)で治療(ちりょう)を受(う)けた。
I hurt my lower back and received treatment at a bonesetting clinic.
駅前(えきまえ)に新(あたら)しい整骨院(せいこついん)ができたので、肩(かた)こりの治療(ちりょう)に行(い)ってみた。
A new bonesetting clinic opened in front of the station, so I went to get my stiff shoulders treated.
整骨(せいこつ)の技術(ぎじゅつ)は柔道(じゅうどう)の活法(かっぽう)から発展(はってん)したと言(い)われている。
Bonesetting techniques are said to have developed from the resuscitation methods of judo.

Composed of 整(せい) (arrange, set in order) and 骨(こつ) (bone). 整骨(せいこつ) has its roots in traditional Japanese medicine, particularly the healing arts associated with 柔道(じゅうどう). Practitioners were historically martial artists who knew how to set bones and treat injuries sustained during training.

In modern Japan, 整骨院(せいこついん) (bonesetting clinics) are extremely common, found on virtually every commercial street. They are staffed by 柔道整復師(じゅうどうせいふくし) (judo therapists), who hold a national license. These clinics treat sprains, muscle strains, back pain, and similar musculoskeletal complaints. Some treatments are covered by health insurance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 整骨院(せいこついん): bonesetting clinic
  • 整骨(せいこつ)治療(ちりょう): bonesetting treatment
  • 整骨(せいこつ)に行(い)く: to go to a bonesetting clinic
  • 整骨(せいこつ)に通(かよ)う: to regularly visit a bonesetting clinic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 接骨(せっこつ): bonesetting — synonym; 接骨院(せっこついん) is an alternative name for the same type of clinic
  • 整体(せいたい): chiropractic, body alignment therapy — a broader term for manual body adjustment; practitioners are not nationally licensed
  • 整形外科(せいけいげか): orthopedic surgery — the Western medical specialty; involves diagnosis, imaging, and sometimes surgery
