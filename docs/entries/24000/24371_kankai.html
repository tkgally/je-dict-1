官界(かんかい)

かんかい
noun
the bureaucratic world; government circles
1. the bureaucratic world; government circles; officialdom
The world of government officials and bureaucrats as a collective social sphere, including its culture, power dynamics, and career paths. Similar in pattern to other ~界 compounds that define professional or social spheres.
官界(かんかい)で出世(しゅっせ)する。
To rise through the ranks in government circles.
父(ちち)は長年(ながねん)官界(かんかい)に身(み)を置(お)いていた。
My father spent many years in the bureaucratic world.
政界(せいかい)と官界(かんかい)の癒着(ゆちゃく)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
The collusion between political and bureaucratic circles has become a problem.

Compound of 官(かん) ('government; officialdom') and 界(かい) ('world; sphere; circles'). Part of a productive pattern where 界(かい) defines a professional or social world: 政界(せいかい) ('political world'), 財界(ざいかい) ('business world'), 学界(がっかい) ('academic world'), 芸能界(げいのうかい) ('entertainment world').

USAGE:

  • Used in news and commentary when discussing government bureaucracy as an institution — its internal culture, promotion patterns, and relationships with other power centers.
  • Japan's 官界(かんかい) is historically dominated by graduates of elite universities (especially 東京大学(とうきょうだいがく)), a phenomenon related to 学閥(がくばつ) ('university-based cliques').
  • Often appears alongside 政界(せいかい) ('political world') and 財界(ざいかい) ('business world') in discussions of Japan's power structure.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 官界(かんかい)で出世(しゅっせ)する: to advance in government circles
  • 政界(せいかい)と官界(かんかい): political and bureaucratic worlds
  • 官界(かんかい)に入(はい)る: to enter government service
  • 官界(かんかい)の人脈(じんみゃく): connections in bureaucratic circles

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 政界(せいかい): political world — the sphere of elected politicians and party politics
  • 財界(ざいかい): business world — the sphere of corporate leaders and economic power
  • 学界(がっかい): academic world — the sphere of scholars and universities
  • 官僚(かんりょう): bureaucrat — an individual government official, especially a career civil servant
