系列店(けいれつてん)

けいれつてん
noun
affiliated store; group store; chain store within a corporate group
1. affiliated store; group store; chain store within a corporate group
A shop or outlet that belongs to the same corporate group or distribution network as a parent company — for example a car dealership operating under a single manufacturer, or a convenience store within a chain. Used especially when explaining that a store is part of a larger business network rather than an independent one.
近(ちか)くに系列店(けいれつてん)がある。
There is an affiliated store nearby.
系列店(けいれつてん)でも同(おな)じポイントが使(つか)える。
You can use the same points at affiliated stores too.
修理(しゅうり)は購入(こうにゅう)した店(みせ)だけでなく、全国(ぜんこく)の系列店(けいれつてん)でも受(う)け付(つ)けてもらえる。
Repairs can be handled not only at the store where you bought the item, but also at affiliated stores nationwide.

Compound of 系列(けいれつ) ('lineage; corporate group; network') and 店(てん) ('store'). Used to describe a store that is part of a larger corporate family, especially in retail and distribution — car dealerships, electronics stores, convenience-store chains, and restaurant groups. The word highlights the relationship to a parent or group, which affects service, pricing, and product availability.

USAGE:

  • Common in product warranties, loyalty-program descriptions, and news about retail industry reorganization.
  • Often contrasts with 独立店(どくりつてん) ('independent store') or 直営店(ちょくえいてん) ('directly-managed store').
  • The broader business term 系列(けいれつ) by itself refers to Japanese corporate groupings (e.g., the Mitsubishi 系列(けいれつ)), and 系列店(けいれつてん) applies that idea at the retail-shop level.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 系列店(けいれつてん)で買(か)う: to buy at an affiliated store
  • 全国(ぜんこく)の系列店(けいれつてん): affiliated stores nationwide
  • 系列店(けいれつてん)を展開(てんかい)する: to roll out a chain of affiliated stores
  • 系列店(けいれつてん)のネットワーク(ねっとわーく): network of affiliated stores
  • トヨタ系列店(けいれつてん): Toyota-affiliated store

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 直営店(ちょくえいてん): directly-managed store — run by the parent company itself; a 系列店(けいれつてん) may be directly managed or franchised.
  • フランチャイズ(ふらんちゃいず)店(てん): franchise store — operates under a brand through a franchise contract, with an independent owner.
  • チェーン店(ちぇーんてん): chain store — general term for one of many stores under the same brand; overlaps with 系列店(けいれつてん) but emphasizes uniform branding rather than the corporate-group link.
  • 支店(してん): branch store; branch office — a branch of the same company, usually under direct ownership.
  • 加盟店(かめいてん): member store — a store that has joined a card, point, or franchise network by contract.
