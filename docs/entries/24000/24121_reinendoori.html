例年通(れいねんどお)り

れいねんどおり
adverb
as usual; as in a normal year; same as every year
1. as usual; as in a normal year; same as every year
Describes something that follows the same pattern, schedule, or level as in typical years. Used to indicate that an event, weather pattern, or situation matches historical norms.
例年通(れいねんどお)り開催(かいさい)する。
To hold it as usual.
桜(さくら)は例年通(れいねんどお)り三月(さんがつ)末(すえ)に咲(さ)いた。
The cherry blossoms bloomed at the end of March, as usual.
今年(ことし)の夏祭(なつまつ)りは例年通(れいねんどお)り八月(はちがつ)の第一(だいいち)週末(しゅうまつ)に行(おこな)われる予定(よてい)だ。
This year's summer festival is scheduled to be held on the first weekend of August, as in previous years.

Compound of 例年(れいねん) ('a typical year; every year') and 通(どお)り ('in accordance with; as'). The pattern X通(どお)り ('as per X') is productive in Japanese: 予定通(よていどお)り ('as planned'), 期待通(きたいどお)り ('as expected'). 例年通(れいねんどお)り specifically signals that this year's event or condition matches the established annual norm.

USAGE:

  • Commonly used in weather reports, event announcements, and seasonal forecasts.
  • Functions adverbially, modifying a verb directly: 例年通(れいねんどお)り開催(かいさい)する.
  • Can also function as a noun with the particle の: 例年通(れいねんどお)りのスケジュール(すけじゅーる) ('the usual schedule').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 例年通(れいねんどお)り開催(かいさい)する: to hold as usual
  • 例年通(れいねんどお)りの気温(きおん): temperatures in line with the annual average
  • 例年通(れいねんどお)りの時期(じき): the usual time of year
  • 例年通(れいねんどお)り進(すす)める: to proceed as in previous years

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 例年(れいねん): a normal year; every year — the base noun, often used with particles like に or は to mean 'in a typical year'
  • 平年並(へいねんな)み: average; on par with a normal year — used mainly in weather contexts for temperatures and rainfall
  • いつも通(どお)り: as always — more casual and not limited to annual cycles; covers daily or habitual patterns
