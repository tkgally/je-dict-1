リスキー

りすきー
na-adjective
risky
1. risky; involving significant danger or uncertainty
Describes an action, plan, or situation that carries a high chance of failure, loss, or harm. The English loanword feels lighter and more modern than the Sino-Japanese 危険(きけん), and is common in business, finance, and everyday casual contexts.
それはリスキーな選択(せんたく)だ。
That's a risky choice.
今(いま)この会社(かいしゃ)に投資(とうし)するのはリスキーだと思(おも)う。
I think investing in this company right now is risky.
彼(かれ)は安定(あんてい)した仕事(しごと)を辞(や)めて起業(きぎょう)するという、かなりリスキーな決断(けつだん)を下(くだ)した。
He made the rather risky decision to quit his stable job and start his own business.
何(なん)の準備(じゅんび)もせずに海外(かいがい)に移住(いじゅう)するのは、いくら若(わか)いとはいえ、あまりにリスキーすぎる。
Moving abroad without any preparation, however young you may be, is just too risky.

From the English 'risky.' Treated as a {ナ}-adjective in Japanese, taking な before nouns and だ as a predicate. The loanword reading is established as リスキー with the long vowel mark.

USAGE:

  • Modifies plans, decisions, investments, behaviors, and ventures rather than objects or people.
  • Common in business, finance, and informal everyday speech. Spoken Japanese often uses リスキー where written or formal Japanese would use 危険(きけん) or リスクが高(たか)い.
  • Can be intensified with かなり ('quite'), あまりに ('overly'), or ちょっと ('a bit').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リスキーな選択(せんたく): a risky choice
  • リスキーな投資(とうし): a risky investment
  • リスキーな決断(けつだん): a risky decision
  • リスキーな賭(か)け: a risky gamble
  • リスキーすぎる: to be too risky
  • かなりリスキー: quite risky

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 危険(きけん)な: dangerous — broader and more serious; covers physical danger as well as risk; the standard formal term.
  • 危(あぶ)ない: dangerous; risky — native vocabulary, used for both physical danger and risky decisions; the most common everyday word.
  • リスクが高(たか)い: high risk — more analytical phrasing common in business and finance contexts.
  • 冒険的(ぼうけんてき): adventurous; bold — frames the risk positively, as exciting or daring rather than ill-advised.
  • ハイリスク: high-risk — another loanword, often used in {ハイリスク・ハイリターン} ('high risk, high return').
