舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)

ぶたいげいこ
noun
dress rehearsal; stage rehearsal
1. dress rehearsal; stage rehearsal; run-through on stage
A full rehearsal conducted on the actual stage with costumes, lighting, and props, typically held shortly before the opening performance. The final practice before the show goes live, testing every aspect of the production in performance conditions.
明日(あした)は舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)がある。
There's a dress rehearsal tomorrow.
舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)で衣装(いしょう)や照明(しょうめい)の最終(さいしゅう)確認(かくにん)をする。
We do final checks on costumes and lighting during the stage rehearsal.
舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)は本番(ほんばん)と同(おな)じ条件(じょうけん)で通(とお)しで行(おこな)われた。
The dress rehearsal was done as a full run-through under the same conditions as the actual performance.

Compound of 舞台(ぶたい) ('stage') and 稽古(けいこ) ('practice; rehearsal'). Equivalent to what is called a 'dress rehearsal' in English — the final rehearsal on the actual stage with all production elements in place. Also called ゲネプロ (from German Generalprobe) in the theater world.

USAGE:

  • Used for theater, opera, musicals, dance performances, and traditional performing arts like 歌舞伎(かぶき)
  • Implies a complete run-through, not just a partial or technical rehearsal
  • In sumo, 稽古(けいこ) means 'practice' in a different context — 舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ) is specifically for performing arts

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)を行(おこな)う: to conduct a stage rehearsal
  • 舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ)に入(はい)る: to begin stage rehearsals
  • 最終(さいしゅう)舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ): final dress rehearsal
  • 公開(こうかい)舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ): open dress rehearsal (with audience)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 稽古(けいこ): practice; rehearsal — the general word for rehearsal or training in traditional arts, not limited to on-stage practice
  • ゲネプロ: dress rehearsal — loanword from German Generalprobe; used interchangeably with 舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ) in theater circles
  • リハーサル: rehearsal — a general loanword for any type of rehearsal, not necessarily the final or full one
  • 本番(ほんばん): the real performance — what follows the 舞台稽古(ぶたいげいこ); the actual show
