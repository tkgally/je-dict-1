実際上(じっさいじょう)

じっさいじょう
adverb
in practice; practically; from a practical standpoint
1. in practice; practically; from a practical standpoint
Used to contrast what theoretically or officially ought to be the case with what actually happens in real-world practice. Signals that the speaker is describing how things function on the ground, as opposed to how they are defined on paper.
実際上(じっさいじょう)問題(もんだい)はない。
In practice, there is no problem.
実際上(じっさいじょう)、この規則(きそく)は守(まも)られていない。
In practice, this rule is not being observed.
理論上(りろんじょう)は可能(かのう)だが、実際上(じっさいじょう)はコストが高(たか)すぎて実現(じつげん)できない。
It is theoretically possible, but in practice the cost is too high for it to be feasible.

Compound of 実際(じっさい) ('actual reality; practice') and the suffix 上(じょう) ('on the level of; from the standpoint of'). Frequently paired in contrast with 理論上(りろんじょう) ('in theory') or 法律上(ほうりつじょう) ('legally speaking') to distinguish real-world practice from abstract principle.

USAGE:

  • Typically appears at the beginning of a clause as a sentence-level modifier, with or without a following comma.
  • Common in academic, legal, business, and technical writing where the gap between rule and practice matters.
  • Can also function as a noun modifier: 実際上(じっさいじょう)の問題(もんだい) ('a practical problem').
  • The negative pattern 実際上(じっさいじょう)...ない ('in practice, not...') is especially frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実際上(じっさいじょう)問題(もんだい)はない: in practice, there is no problem
  • 実際上(じっさいじょう)不可能(ふかのう)だ: practically impossible
  • 実際上(じっさいじょう)の影響(えいきょう): practical effect
  • 実際上(じっさいじょう)の効果(こうか): real-world effect
  • 理論上(りろんじょう)と実際上(じっさいじょう): in theory and in practice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実際(じっさい): actually; in reality — the base word; used more broadly in conversation where 実際上(じっさいじょう) would sound stiff.
  • 事実上(じじつじょう): in effect; de facto — stresses that something is effectively true even if not formally so; closer to 'for all intents and purposes'.
  • 現実的(げんじつてき)に: realistically speaking — emphasizes feasibility and common sense rather than contrast with theory.
  • 理論上(りろんじょう): in theory — direct antonym, often used in the same sentence.
  • 実用上(じつようじょう): for practical use — narrower, focused on hands-on usability of a tool, product, or method.
