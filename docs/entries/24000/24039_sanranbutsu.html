散乱物(さんらんぶつ)

さんらんぶつ
noun
scattered objects; debris; litter
1. scattered objects; debris; litter
Objects that have been scattered or strewn about — typically debris on a road after an accident, items dropped from a truck, broken glass on a station platform, or litter left in a public space. Frequently used in traffic news, road safety announcements, and reports on disaster cleanup.
道路(どうろ)に散乱物(さんらんぶつ)がある。
There is debris scattered on the road.
事故(じこ)による散乱物(さんらんぶつ)を処理(しょり)している。
Debris from the accident is being cleared away.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)に散乱物(さんらんぶつ)があるとの情報(じょうほう)があり、警察(けいさつ)が現場(げんば)に急行(きゅうこう)した。
After reports of debris on the expressway, police rushed to the scene.

Compound of 散乱(さんらん) ('scattering; being strewn about') and 物(ぶつ) ('thing; object'). A formal, written term used in road traffic information, news reports, and public-safety contexts. Less common in casual conversation, where speakers prefer phrases like 散(ち)らかった物(もの) or just ごみ ('litter; rubbish').

USAGE:

  • Often appears in expressway and highway traffic alerts: 散乱物(さんらんぶつ)あり ('debris reported'), warning drivers of fallen cargo or accident debris.
  • The companion verb 散乱(さんらん)する ('to be scattered') is common; 散乱物(さんらんぶつ) packages the result of that scattering as a noun.
  • Distinct from 'wreckage' of a vehicle (残骸(ざんがい)); 散乱物(さんらんぶつ) emphasises that the items are spread out over an area.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 散乱物(さんらんぶつ)処理(しょり): debris removal
  • 散乱物(さんらんぶつ)回収(かいしゅう): debris collection
  • 道路上(どうろじょう)の散乱物(さんらんぶつ): debris on the road
  • 散乱物(さんらんぶつ)注意(ちゅうい): debris warning (sign / advisory)
  • 落下(らっか)物(ぶつ)や散乱物(さんらんぶつ): fallen objects and debris

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 落下物(らっかぶつ): falling object; fallen object — refers to items that have fallen from above (e.g., from a truck), often a precursor to 散乱物(さんらんぶつ).
  • 瓦礫(がれき): rubble; debris — debris of buildings after demolition or disaster.
  • 残骸(ざんがい): wreckage; remains — remains of something destroyed (vehicle, building); more concentrated than 散乱物(さんらんぶつ).
  • ごみ: rubbish; litter — everyday word for unwanted waste; 散乱物(さんらんぶつ) is the formal term for ごみ scattered on roads.
  • 散(ち)らかし: mess; clutter — informal word for personal mess in a room.
