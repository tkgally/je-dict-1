契約者(けいやくしゃ)

けいやくしゃ
noun
subscriber; policyholder; contracting party
1. subscriber; policyholder; contracting party
The person who enters into a contract or subscription agreement. Used for insurance policyholders, phone subscribers, service contract holders, and parties to a legal agreement.
契約者(けいやくしゃ)本人(ほんにん)のみ利用(りよう)可能(かのう)です。
Only the subscriber themselves may use this service.
保険(ほけん)の契約者(けいやくしゃ)を変更(へんこう)する。
To change the policyholder of the insurance.
携帯(けいたい)電話(でんわ)の契約者(けいやくしゃ)情報(じょうほう)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please verify the mobile phone subscriber information.
契約者(けいやくしゃ)が死亡(しぼう)した場合(ばあい)、受取人(うけとりにん)に保険金(ほけんきん)が支払(しはら)われる。
In the event of the policyholder's death, the insurance benefit is paid to the beneficiary.

A formal term used in legal, insurance, telecommunications, and service contexts. Anyone dealing with Japanese bureaucracy, insurance, or phone contracts will encounter this word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ)本人(ほんにん): the subscriber/policyholder themselves
  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ)情報(じょうほう): subscriber/contract holder information
  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ)変更(へんこう): change of contract holder
  • 保険(ほけん)契約者(けいやくしゃ): insurance policyholder
  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ)番号(ばんごう): subscriber number

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加入者(かにゅうしゃ): member, subscriber — used for membership-based services and organizations
  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ): insured person — the person covered by insurance (may differ from the policyholder)
  • 利用者(りようしゃ): user — the person actually using the service
  • 当事者(とうじしゃ): party (to an agreement) — broader legal term for involved parties

FORMATION:
契約(けいやく) (contract) + 者(しゃ) (person) — literally "contract person."
