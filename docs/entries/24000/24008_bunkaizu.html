分解図(ぶんかいず)

ぶんかいず
noun
exploded view; breakdown diagram
1. exploded view; breakdown diagram showing the component parts of a product
A technical illustration that shows the individual parts of a product separated out along the lines they fit together, so that the viewer can see how everything is assembled. Used in manuals, parts catalogs, repair guides, and engineering documentation.
機械(きかい)の分解図(ぶんかいず)。
An exploded view of the machine.
取扱説明書(とりあつかいせつめいしょ)に分解図(ぶんかいず)が載(の)っている。
The instruction manual has an exploded view.
修理(しゅうり)をする前(まえ)に分解図(ぶんかいず)をよく見(み)て部品(ぶひん)の位置(いち)を確認(かくにん)する。
Before doing the repair, check the exploded view carefully to confirm where each part goes.
この分解図(ぶんかいず)を見(み)れば、各(かく)パーツがどのように組(く)み立(た)てられているかが一目(ひとめ)でわかる。
Looking at this exploded view, you can see at a glance how each part is assembled.

Composed of 分解(ぶんかい) (disassembly, breakdown) and 図(ず) (figure, diagram).

USAGE:
分解図(ぶんかいず) is a technical term that appears in instruction manuals, repair documentation, patent drawings, and parts-ordering catalogs. It refers specifically to a diagram where parts are drawn pulled apart along axes so their assembly relationships are visible — the English concept of an "exploded view."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 分解図(ぶんかいず)を見(み)る: to look at the exploded view
  • 分解図(ぶんかいず)を描(か)く: to draw an exploded view
  • 部品(ぶひん)の分解図(ぶんかいず): exploded view of parts
  • 詳細(しょうさい)な分解図(ぶんかいず): detailed exploded view

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 組立図(くみたてず): assembly diagram — shows the parts together and how to build them up, roughly the reverse concept
  • 構造図(こうぞうず): structural diagram — a more general term for any diagram showing internal structure
  • 配線図(はいせんず): wiring diagram — specific to electrical connections
  • 設計図(せっけいず): design drawing, blueprint — the broader design documentation a 分解図(ぶんかいず) may be part of
