1. reverence; veneration; deep respect
A deep, often religiously or spiritually motivated respect and devotion toward a deity, sacred figure, or person of great virtue. Stronger and more solemn than ordinary respect.
神(かみ)を崇敬(すうけい)する。
To revere a deity.
地元(じもと)の人々(ひとびと)から崇敬(すうけい)を集(あつ)めている神社(じんじゃ)だ。
It is a shrine that commands great reverence from the local people.
彼(かれ)は多(おお)くの弟子(でし)から崇敬(すうけい)の念(ねん)を持(も)たれていた。
He was held in deep reverence by many disciples.
この寺(てら)は千年(せんねん)にわたって崇敬(すうけい)されてきた。
This temple has been venerated for a thousand years.

Composed of 崇(すう) (revere, worship) and 敬(けい) (respect, honor). The word conveys a level of respect that goes beyond ordinary admiration — it implies awe, devotion, and often a spiritual dimension. Most commonly used in religious and historical contexts.

In Shinto, 崇敬(すうけい)者(しゃ) (devotee) refers to someone who regularly supports and worships at a particular shrine without being a member of the parish (氏子(うじこ)). Many shrines have 崇敬(すうけい)会(かい) (devotee associations) that visitors can join.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 崇敬(すうけい)する: to revere, to venerate
  • 崇敬(すうけい)の念(ねん): feelings of reverence
  • 崇敬(すうけい)を集(あつ)める: to command reverence
  • 崇敬(すうけい)者(しゃ): devotee; worshipper

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 尊敬(そんけい): respect, esteem — general respect for a person's character or achievements; less solemn than 崇敬(すうけい)
  • 崇拝(すうはい): worship, adoration — stronger than 崇敬(すうけい); can imply blind or excessive devotion
  • 敬意(けいい): respect, deference — more formal and intellectual; lacks the spiritual connotation
