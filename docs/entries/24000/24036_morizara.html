盛(も)り皿(ざら)

もりざら
noun
serving plate; large plate for arranging food
1. serving plate; large plate for arranging food
A plate, often larger and shallower than an individual dish, used to display or serve food that has been arranged on it — sashimi, fruit, sweets, side dishes, or hors d'oeuvres. Found in Japanese restaurants, izakaya, home dining, and as a sales unit ('plate of X') in food shops.
盛(も)り皿(ざら)に果物(くだもの)を並(なら)べる。
To arrange fruit on a serving plate.
大(おお)きな盛(も)り皿(ざら)に刺身(さしみ)が綺麗(きれい)に並(なら)んでいる。
Sashimi is beautifully arranged on a large serving plate.
居酒屋(いざかや)の盛(も)り皿(ざら)は、何人(なんにん)かで分(わ)けて食(た)べるのに便利(べんり)だ。
Izakaya serving plates are convenient for sharing among several people.

Compound of 盛(も)る ('to heap; to arrange (food on a plate)') and 皿(さら) ('plate'); the second element undergoes rendaku, becoming ざら. Refers specifically to a plate used to display or serve arranged food, often larger than an individual dish.

USAGE:

  • Common in restaurant menus and price lists, e.g., 刺身(さしみ)盛(も)り皿(ざら) ('sashimi serving plate') or 果物(くだもの)盛(も)り皿(ざら) ('fruit platter').
  • May refer to either the plate itself or the food arranged on it (e.g., 'a plate of sashimi').
  • The verb 盛(も)る ('to heap up; to arrange on a plate') is at the heart of Japanese 盛(も)り付(つ)け ('food presentation'), which 盛(も)り皿(ざら) supports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 盛(も)り皿(ざら)に並(なら)べる: to arrange on a serving plate
  • 大(おお)きな盛(も)り皿(ざら): a large serving plate
  • 刺身(さしみ)の盛(も)り皿(ざら): a sashimi serving plate
  • 盛(も)り皿(ざら)料理(りょうり): dishes served on a serving plate
  • 盛(も)り皿(ざら)一(ひと)つ: one serving plate (often a price unit)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大皿(おおざら): large plate — close synonym; emphasizes size of the plate rather than the act of arranging food.
  • 取(と)り皿(ざら): small plate for taking individual portions from a shared dish — counterpart used by each diner.
  • 盛(も)り合(あ)わせ: assortment; combination plate — refers to a mixed selection of food (often on a single plate), e.g., 刺身(さしみ)の盛(も)り合(あ)わせ.
  • {プレート}: plate (loanword) — used in Western-style menu language, often for single-serve plates.
  • 皿(さら): plate — the general word for any plate.
