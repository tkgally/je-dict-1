車検証(しゃけんしょう)

しゃけんしょう
noun
vehicle inspection certificate; vehicle registration document
1. vehicle inspection certificate; vehicle registration document
The official document issued after a Japanese vehicle passes its mandatory periodic inspection (車検(しゃけん)). It records the make, model, registration number, owner's name and address, weight, dimensions, and the next inspection due date. Drivers are legally required to carry it in the vehicle at all times. The full official name is 自動車検査証(じどうしゃけんさしょう), of which 車検証(しゃけんしょう) is the everyday short form.
車検証(しゃけんしょう)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me the vehicle inspection certificate.
車検証(しゃけんしょう)は車(くるま)の中(なか)に置(お)いておく。
The vehicle inspection certificate is kept inside the car.
中古車(ちゅうこしゃ)を売(う)るときは、車検証(しゃけんしょう)と印鑑(いんかん)証明(しょうめい)が必要(ひつよう)になる。
When you sell a used car, you'll need the vehicle inspection certificate and a seal certificate.

Short for 自動車検査証(じどうしゃけんさしょう) ('motor vehicle inspection certificate'). Composed of 車検(しゃけん) ('vehicle inspection') and 証(しょう) ('certificate'). One of the most important documents associated with owning a car in Japan; it must be kept in the vehicle at all times by law.

USAGE:

  • Required when selling, transferring, or scrapping a vehicle, and when buying compulsory insurance.
  • The latest version is a small, plastic-laminated card-style certificate (since the introduction of the 'electronic' 車検証(しゃけんしょう) in 2023, with key details readable via an IC chip).
  • Police officers may ask to see it during traffic stops; failing to have it on board is a minor offence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 車検証(しゃけんしょう)を提示(ていじ)する: to present the vehicle inspection certificate
  • 車検証(しゃけんしょう)を確認(かくにん)する: to check the vehicle inspection certificate
  • 車検証(しゃけんしょう)を紛失(ふんしつ)する: to lose the vehicle inspection certificate
  • 車検証(しゃけんしょう)の有効期限(ゆうこうきげん): expiry date of the certificate
  • 車検証(しゃけんしょう)の再発行(さいはっこう): reissuing the certificate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自賠責(じばいせき)保険証(ほけんしょう): compulsory automobile liability insurance certificate — separate document also required to be kept in the car.
  • 運転免許証(うんてんめんきょしょう): driver's licence — proves the person is licensed to drive, not that the vehicle is registered.
  • 自動車税(じどうしゃぜい)納税(のうぜい)証明書(しょうめいしょ): vehicle tax payment certificate — proves the annual vehicle tax has been paid.
  • 車検(しゃけん): vehicle inspection — the inspection itself, not the certificate that results from it.
