夏鳥(なつどり)

なつどり
noun
summer bird; summer visitor (bird)
1. summer bird; summer visitor; migratory bird that arrives in summer
A bird species that migrates to Japan (or another region) to breed during spring and summer, then returns south for winter. Contrasts with 冬鳥(ふゆどり), which arrives for winter.
ツバメは代表的(だいひょうてき)な夏鳥(なつどり)だ。
The swallow is a typical summer bird.
五月(ごがつ)になると夏鳥(なつどり)のさえずりが聞(き)こえてくる。
In May, you can hear the singing of summer birds.
この地域(ちいき)には毎年(まいとし)多(おお)くの夏鳥(なつどり)が飛来(ひらい)し、秋(あき)になると南(みなみ)へ渡(わた)っていく。
Many summer birds arrive in this area each year and migrate south when autumn comes.

Compound of 夏(なつ) ('summer') and 鳥(どり) ('bird'). A term from ornithology and birdwatching used to classify migratory birds by the season they spend in Japan. Birds are categorized as 夏鳥(なつどり) (summer visitors), 冬鳥(ふゆどり) (winter visitors), 留鳥(りゅうちょう) (resident birds), and 旅鳥(たびどり) (passage migrants).

USAGE:

  • Commonly used in nature writing, birdwatching guides, and environmental education
  • In Japan, well-known summer birds include ツバメ (swallows), カッコウ (cuckoos), and ホトトギス (lesser cuckoo)
  • The arrival of summer birds is considered a sign of the changing seasons

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夏鳥(なつどり)が飛来(ひらい)する: summer birds arrive
  • 夏鳥(なつどり)を観察(かんさつ)する: to observe summer birds
  • 代表的(だいひょうてき)な夏鳥(なつどり): a typical summer bird
  • 夏鳥(なつどり)の渡(わた)り: summer bird migration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 冬鳥(ふゆどり): winter bird — migratory birds that come for winter (e.g., swans, geese)
  • 留鳥(りゅうちょう): resident bird — birds that stay year-round
  • 渡り鳥(わたりどり): migratory bird — general term for any bird that migrates seasonally
