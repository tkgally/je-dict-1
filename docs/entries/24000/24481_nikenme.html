二軒目(にけんめ)

にけんめ
noun
the second place (bar, restaurant, etc.); second stop on a night out
1. the second place (bar, restaurant, etc.); second stop on a night out
The second establishment visited during a night of drinking or dining, following the pattern of hopping between venues.
二軒目(にけんめ)はカラオケにしよう。
Let's go to karaoke for the second stop.
二軒目(にけんめ)のバーで飲(の)み過(す)ぎた。
I drank too much at the second bar.
飲(の)み会(かい)の後(あと)、同僚(どうりょう)たちと二軒目(にけんめ)に行(い)くことになった。
After the drinking party, I ended up going to a second place with my coworkers.

In Japanese drinking culture, it is common to visit multiple establishments in one night. The first place (一軒目(いっけんめ)) is often a restaurant or izakaya for food and drinks, while the 二軒目(にけんめ) is typically a bar, karaoke venue, or another casual spot. This practice is called はしご酒(はしござけ) (bar-hopping).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 二軒目(にけんめ)に行(い)く: to go to a second place
  • 二軒目(にけんめ)はどこにする？: where should we go next?
  • 二軒目(にけんめ)に付(つ)き合(あ)う: to tag along to the second place

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一軒目(いっけんめ): the first place/stop
  • 三軒目(さんげんめ): the third place/stop
  • はしご酒(はしござけ): bar-hopping
  • 飲(の)み会(かい): drinking party
