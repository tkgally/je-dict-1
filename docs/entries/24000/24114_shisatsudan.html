視察団(しさつだん)

しさつだん
noun
inspection group; observation delegation; fact-finding mission
1. inspection group; observation delegation; fact-finding mission
An organized group sent to observe, inspect, or investigate a particular site, facility, project, or situation. Common in government, business, and international contexts.
視察団(しさつだん)が工場(こうじょう)を訪(おとず)れた。
The inspection group visited the factory.
海外(かいがい)視察団(しさつだん)としてヨーロッパ(よーろっぱ)に派遣(はけん)された。
I was sent to Europe as part of an overseas inspection delegation.
議会(ぎかい)の視察団(しさつだん)が被災地(ひさいち)の復興(ふっこう)状況(じょうきょう)を確認(かくにん)した。
A parliamentary inspection delegation confirmed the recovery progress in the disaster-affected area.
市長(しちょう)を団長(だんちょう)とする視察団(しさつだん)が、先進的(せんしんてき)な福祉(ふくし)施設(しせつ)を見学(けんがく)した。
An inspection delegation led by the mayor toured advanced welfare facilities.

Composed of 視察(しさつ) (inspection, observation) and 団(だん) (group, party). The suffix ～団(だん) forms organized groups: 調査団(ちょうさだん) (investigation team), 代表団(だいひょうだん) (delegation), 救助団(きゅうじょだん) (rescue team), etc.

視察団(しさつだん) are typically official or semi-official, sent by governments, legislatures, companies, or professional organizations to observe best practices, assess conditions, or gather information for policy-making.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 視察団(しさつだん)を派遣(はけん)する: to dispatch an inspection delegation
  • 視察団(しさつだん)を受(う)け入(い)れる: to receive an inspection delegation
  • 海外(かいがい)視察団(しさつだん): overseas observation delegation
  • 議会(ぎかい)視察団(しさつだん): parliamentary inspection delegation
  • 視察団(しさつだん)の報告(ほうこく): inspection delegation report

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 調査団(ちょうさだん): investigation team — focuses on detailed research and data collection rather than observation
  • 代表団(だいひょうだん): delegation — a group representing an organization or country, not necessarily for inspection purposes
  • 使節団(しせつだん): mission, envoy group — a diplomatic delegation sent for negotiations or goodwill
