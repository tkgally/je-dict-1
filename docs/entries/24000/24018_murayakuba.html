村役場(むらやくば)

むらやくば
noun
village office; village hall
1. village office; village hall
The administrative office of a village (村(むら)) — the smallest unit of Japanese local government. Handles residence registration, tax, welfare benefits, and other daily administrative services for village residents, paralleling the role of a 市役所(しやくしょ) in a city or a 町役場(まちやくば) in a town.
村役場(むらやくば)に行(い)く。
I'm going to the village office.
村役場(むらやくば)で住民票(じゅうみんひょう)を受(う)け取(と)った。
I picked up my residence certificate at the village office.
村役場(むらやくば)の職員(しょくいん)が高齢者(こうれいしゃ)の家(いえ)を訪問(ほうもん)して手続(てつづ)きを手伝(てつだ)っている。
Village office staff visit elderly residents' homes to help them with administrative procedures.

Compound of 村(むら) ('village') and 役場(やくば) ('public office'). Used specifically for the municipal office of a 村}, one of the three types of basic Japanese local government unit along with cities ({市(し)) and towns (町(まち)). Because 村} are small and often rural, the {村役場(むらやくば) tends to be a modest building and the center of daily community life.

USAGE:

  • Appears on signs, official forms, and news stories about rural administration, especially reports on depopulated regions and aging communities.
  • The word implies a rural or mountainous setting and a close-knit community; city residents would never refer to their local office this way.
  • Often paired with verbs like 行(い)く ('to go'), 訪(おとず)れる ('to visit'), or 申請(しんせい)する ('to apply') in reference to administrative errands.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 村役場(むらやくば)に行(い)く: to go to the village office
  • 村役場(むらやくば)で手続(てつづ)きする: to do paperwork at the village office
  • 村役場(むらやくば)の職員(しょくいん): village office staff
  • 村役場(むらやくば)の窓口(まどぐち): village office counter
  • 村役場(むらやくば)に問(と)い合(あ)わせる: to inquire at the village office

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 市役所(しやくしょ): city hall — office for a designated city ({市}); noticeably larger and more formal.
  • 町役場(まちやくば): town office — parallel term for a town ({町}); same function, intermediate size.
  • 区役所(くやくしょ): ward office — office for a ward ({区}) within a major city such as Tokyo or Osaka.
  • 役場(やくば): public office — the generic element, used alone when the type of municipality is already clear.
  • 村(むら): village — the municipal unit itself, as distinct from its administrative office.
