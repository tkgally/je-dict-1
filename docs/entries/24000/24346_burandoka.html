1. branding; brand-building; the process of establishing or elevating something as a recognized brand
The act or process of establishing a distinct identity and reputation for a product, service, region, or concept so that it becomes recognized as a premium or desirable brand. Used in business, marketing, and regional development contexts.
地域(ちいき)の特産品(とくさんひん)をブランド化(か)する。
Brand the region's specialty products.
ブランド化(か)に成功(せいこう)して売(う)り上(あ)げが伸(の)びた。
Sales grew after successful branding.
農産物(のうさんぶつ)のブランド化(か)を進(すす)めることで、地方(ちほう)経済(けいざい)の活性化(かっせいか)を目指(めざ)している。
By promoting the branding of agricultural products, they aim to revitalize the local economy.

Combines the loanword ブランド (brand) with the productive suffix 化(か) (-ization, turning into). A hybrid wasei compound typical of modern business Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ブランド化(か)を進(すす)める: to promote/advance branding
  • ブランド化(か)に成功(せいこう)する: to succeed in branding
  • ブランド化(か)戦略(せんりゃく): branding strategy
  • 地域(ちいき)ブランド化(か): regional branding

USAGE:
Very common in discussions of regional revitalization (地方(ちほう)創生(そうせい)) — local governments across Japan actively pursue ブランド化(か) of their agricultural products, crafts, and tourism offerings. Examples include 松阪牛(まつさかぎゅう) (Matsusaka beef) and 夕張(ゆうばり)メロン (Yubari melon). Also used in corporate marketing contexts for product branding.

RELATED 〜化(か) COMPOUNDS:

  • 商品化(しょうひんか): commercialization, product development
  • 差別化(さべつか): differentiation — distinguishing one product from competitors
  • 高級化(こうきゅうか): upscaling, making premium
  • 国際化(こくさいか): internationalization
