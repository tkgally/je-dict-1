緑色(みどりいろ)

みどりいろ
noun
green; green color
1. green; green color
The color green, as seen in leaves and grass.
緑色(みどりいろ)の葉(は)が美(うつく)しい。
The green leaves are beautiful.
信号(しんごう)が緑色(みどりいろ)に変(か)わった。
The traffic light changed to green.
この絵(え)の具(ぐ)は緑色(みどりいろ)が鮮(あざ)やかだ。
This paint has a vivid green.
春(はる)になると、山(やま)が緑色(みどりいろ)に染(そ)まる。
In spring, the mountains are dyed green.

緑(みどり) (green) + 色(いろ) (color). The explicit color-name form, used when specifying or describing the color green precisely.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 緑色(みどりいろ)の野菜(やさい): green vegetables
  • 緑色(みどりいろ)に光(ひか)る: to shine green
  • 緑色(みどりいろ)に染(そ)まる: to be dyed green
  • 濃(こ)い緑色(みどりいろ): dark green
  • 薄(うす)い緑色(みどりいろ): light green

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 緑(みどり): green — used more broadly and poetically; 緑色(みどりいろ) is more concrete and descriptive
  • 青(あお): blue/green — traditionally covers both blue and green in Japanese; traffic lights are called 青(あお) even though they appear green

USAGE:
In Japanese, 青(あお) historically encompassed both blue and green. The distinction between 青(あお) and 緑(みどり) became clearer in modern Japanese, but 青(あお) is still used for green traffic lights and green apples (青(あお)りんご). 緑色(みどりいろ) is unambiguously green.
