足指(あしゆび)

あしゆび
noun
toe; toes
1. toe; toes
The digits of the foot.
足指(あしゆび)をぶつけて痛(いた)い。
I stubbed my toe and it hurts.
足指(あしゆび)を広(ひろ)げるストレッチをしている。
I'm doing toe-spreading stretches.
冬(ふゆ)になると足指(あしゆび)が冷(ひ)えて感覚(かんかく)がなくなることがある。
In winter, my toes sometimes get so cold that I lose feeling in them.

A compound of 足(あし) (foot, leg) + 指(ゆび) (finger, digit). Japanese uses 指(ゆび) for both fingers and toes, so 足指(あしゆび) specifies the toes. Also written as 足(あし)の指(ゆび) in everyday speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 足指(あしゆび)を動(うご)かす — move one's toes
  • 足指(あしゆび)が冷(ひ)える — toes get cold
  • 足指(あしゆび)の間(あいだ) — between the toes
  • 足指(あしゆび)の爪(つめ) — toenails

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手指(てゆび)/手(て)の指(ゆび) — finger; the hand counterpart, specifying fingers rather than toes
  • 指(ゆび) — finger/toe; the general word that covers both, with context determining which
  • つま先(さき) — tip of the toes, tiptoe; refers to the front end of the foot rather than individual toes
