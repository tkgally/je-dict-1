営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)

えいぎょうしゃ
noun
company car; sales vehicle; business vehicle
1. company car; sales vehicle; business vehicle
A vehicle owned by a company and used for business purposes such as sales visits, deliveries, or service calls. Distinguished from personal vehicles and executive cars.
営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)で取引先(とりひきさき)を回(まわ)る。
I visit clients using the company car.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)に営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)が十台(じゅうだい)並(なら)んでいる。
Ten company cars are lined up in the parking lot.
営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)には会社(かいしゃ)のロゴが入(はい)っているので、運転(うんてん)マナーには特(とく)に気(き)をつけなければならない。
Since the company car has the company logo on it, you have to be especially careful about driving etiquette.

Compound of 営業(えいぎょう) ('business; sales') and 車(しゃ) ('vehicle'). Refers to vehicles used by sales staff and other employees for company business. These are typically sedans or light vans, often bearing the company name and logo.

USAGE:

  • Common in business and workplace contexts
  • Often a white sedan or light commercial vehicle provided by the company
  • Distinguished from 社用車(しゃようしゃ) (broader term for any company vehicle) and 役員車(やくいんしゃ) (executive car)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)を運転(うんてん)する: to drive a company car
  • 営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)で回(まわ)る: to go around in a company car
  • 営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)の管理(かんり): company car management
  • 営業車(えいぎょうしゃ)の駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう): company car parking area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 社用車(しゃようしゃ): company vehicle — a broader term covering any vehicle owned by the company for official use
  • 商用車(しょうようしゃ): commercial vehicle — vehicles designed for transporting goods or passengers commercially (trucks, vans, buses)
  • 自家用車(じかようしゃ): private car; personal vehicle — a vehicle owned for personal use, not business
