1. raising seedlings; nursery cultivation
the process of growing young plants from seed in a controlled environment before transplanting them
春(はる)になったら育苗(いくびょう)を始(はじ)める。
We start raising seedlings when spring comes.
育苗(いくびょう)ポットに種(たね)をまいた。
I sowed seeds in seedling pots.
この農家(のうか)では温室(おんしつ)を使(つか)って冬(ふゆ)の間(あいだ)も育苗(いくびょう)を行(おこな)い、早(はや)い時期(じき)に苗(なえ)を出荷(しゅっか)している。
This farm uses greenhouses to raise seedlings even during winter and ships them out early in the season.

The agricultural practice of growing plants from seed to a transplantable stage. Essential in rice farming, where 育苗(いくびょう)箱(ばこ) (seedling trays) are used to grow rice seedlings before they are transplanted into paddies. Also important in commercial vegetable and flower production.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 育苗(いくびょう)箱(ばこ): seedling tray/box
  • 育苗(いくびょう)ポット: seedling pot
  • 育苗(いくびょう)ハウス: seedling greenhouse
  • 育苗(いくびょう)期間(きかん): seedling raising period

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 苗(なえ): seedling — the young plant itself
  • 種(たね)まき: sowing — the act of planting seeds, the step before raising seedlings
  • 植(う)え付(つ)け: transplanting — the next step after seedlings are ready
  • 栽培(さいばい): cultivation — the broader term for growing plants
