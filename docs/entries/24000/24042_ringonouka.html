りんご農家(のうか)

りんごのうか
noun
apple farmer; apple-growing household
1. apple farmer; apple-growing household
A farmer or farming family whose main crop is apples. Like other 〜農家(のうか) ('X-growing farmer') terms, the word can refer to either the farmer as an individual or to the household / business as a whole. Often used in news about agriculture, weather damage, harvest seasons, and rural tourism, especially in apple-producing regions like Aomori, Nagano, and Iwate.
祖父(そふ)はりんご農家(のうか)だ。
My grandfather is an apple farmer.
りんご農家(のうか)にとって、台風(たいふう)は大(おお)きな脅威(きょうい)だ。
For apple farmers, typhoons are a major threat.
長野(ながの)のりんご農家(のうか)は、気候(きこう)変動(へんどう)に合(あ)わせて新(あたら)しい品種(ひんしゅ)を試(ため)し始(はじ)めている。
Apple farmers in Nagano are starting to try new varieties to adapt to climate change.

Compound of りんご ('apple') and 農家(のうか) ('farmer; farming household'). Like other crop + 農家(のうか) compounds (e.g., 米(こめ)農家(のうか) 'rice farmer', みかん{農家(のうか)} 'mandarin orange farmer'), the term covers both the individual farmer and the family business of growing apples.

USAGE:

  • The pattern X農家(のうか) can mean (a) a person who grows X for a living, or (b) the farming household/business that does so. Context decides between the two readings.
  • Contrast with りんご園(えん) ('apple orchard'), which refers to the place, not the people.
  • Common in seasonal news: harvest reports in October-November, frost or hail damage in spring, typhoon damage to fruit, and articles about pick-your-own tourism.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • りんご農家(のうか)を営(いとな)む: to run an apple-farming business
  • りんご農家(のうか)の息子(むすこ): the son of an apple-farming family
  • 老舗(しにせ)のりんご農家(のうか): a long-established apple-farming household
  • りんご農家(のうか)が被害(ひがい)を受(う)ける: apple farmers suffer damage (e.g., from weather)
  • りんご農家(のうか)の後継者(こうけいしゃ): successor to an apple-farming household

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 農家(のうか): farmer; farming household — generic term, without specifying the crop.
  • りんご園(えん): apple orchard — refers to the place, not the people.
  • 果樹(かじゅ)農家(のうか): fruit farmer — broader category that includes apple, pear, grape, etc.
  • 米(こめ)農家(のうか): rice farmer — same suffix pattern with a different crop.
  • 生産者(せいさんしゃ): producer — covers any agricultural producer, often used on packaging and in food labelling.
