1. on-street parking; parking a vehicle on a public road
The act — usually unauthorized — of parking a car on a public street rather than in a parking lot or garage. In Japan, this is heavily restricted in cities and is a frequent target of police enforcement and fines.
ここは路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)禁止(きんし)だ。
On-street parking is prohibited here.
路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)をして違反(いはん)切符(きっぷ)を切(き)られた。
I parked on the street and got a violation ticket.
都心(としん)では路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)が交通渋滞(こうつうじゅうたい)の大(おお)きな原因(げんいん)になっている。
In the city center, on-street parking is a major cause of traffic congestion.
近所(きんじょ)の狭(せま)い道(みち)に長時間(ちょうじかん)路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)している車(くるま)があり、住民(じゅうみん)から苦情(くじょう)が相次(あいつ)いでいる。
There is a car that has been parked for long stretches on a narrow neighborhood road, and complaints from residents have been pouring in.

Composed of 路上(ろじょう) ('on a road, on the street') + 駐車(ちゅうしゃ) ('parking'). The compound names a specific kind of parking — on the public roadway — as opposed to in a designated lot.

USAGE:

  • In Japanese cities, 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ) is heavily regulated. Many roads are marked 駐車禁止(ちゅうしゃきんし) ('no parking'); parking enforcement officers and police actively ticket offenders.
  • Often appears with verbs like する} ('to park on the street'), {禁止(きんし)する ('to prohibit'), 取(と)り締(し)まる ('to crack down on'), 見(み)つかる ('to be caught').
  • Distinguished from 違法(いほう)駐車(ちゅうしゃ) ('illegal parking,' which can include parking lots, fire lanes, etc.). Not all on-street parking is illegal — some streets allow it during certain hours — but the word often carries an unauthorized nuance in news contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)禁止(きんし): no on-street parking
  • 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)違反(いはん): parking violation (on the street)
  • 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)を取(と)り締(し)まる: to crack down on on-street parking
  • 長時間(ちょうじかん)路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ): long-term street parking
  • 路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ)を見(み)つかる: to be caught parking on the street
  • 迷惑(めいわく)路上駐車(ろじょうちゅうしゃ): nuisance street parking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 違法(いほう)駐車(ちゅうしゃ): illegal parking — a broader term that includes any unauthorized parking, on the street or elsewhere.
  • 駐車違反(ちゅうしゃいはん): parking violation — focuses on the legal infraction itself, not the location.
  • 放置駐車(ほうちちゅうしゃ): abandoned parking — a vehicle left for an extended period; specifically targeted by Japanese parking laws.
  • 駐車(ちゅうしゃ): parking — the general act, with no implication of where or whether it is permitted.
  • {コインパーキング}: coin-operated parking lot — a small private lot where you pay by the hour; the legal alternative to street parking.
