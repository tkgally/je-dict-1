できるだけ早(はや)く

できるだけはやく
expression, adverb
as soon as possible
1. as soon as possible; as quickly as one can
Very common in both spoken and written Japanese.
できるだけ早(はや)く来(き)てください。
Please come as soon as possible.
できるだけ早(はや)く返事(へんじ)をお願(ねが)いします。
I would appreciate a reply as soon as possible.
問題(もんだい)が見(み)つかったら、できるだけ早(はや)く報告(ほうこく)してほしい。
If you find a problem, I'd like you to report it as soon as possible.

Composed of できるだけ (as much as possible) + 早(はや)く (quickly). One of the most frequently used expressions for urgency in Japanese. Equivalent to English "ASAP" but without the acronym's abruptness.

VARIATIONS:

  • できるだけ早(はや)く: standard form
  • なるべく早(はや)く: nearly synonymous, slightly more casual
  • できる限(かぎ)り早(はや)く: slightly more formal variant
  • 至急(しきゅう): urgent (more formal and direct, used in business)

USAGE:
In business email, できるだけ早(はや)く is polite enough for most situations. For greater formality, use できる限(かぎ)り早(はや)く or add お願(ねが)いいたします. The pattern できるだけ + adjective/adverb works for other qualities too: できるだけ安(やす)く (as cheaply as possible), できるだけ静(しず)かに (as quietly as possible).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • なるべく早(はや)く: as soon as possible (slightly softer tone)
  • 至急(しきゅう): urgently (more commanding, business-like)
  • 大至急(だいしきゅう): most urgently (very strong)
