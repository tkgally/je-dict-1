旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)

きゅうやくせいしょ
noun
Old Testament
1. Old Testament
The first and larger of the two main divisions of the Christian Bible, containing the scriptures inherited from Judaism: the books of Moses, historical books, prophets, and wisdom literature. The Japanese name literally means 'the scripture of the old covenant,' contrasting with 新約聖書(しんやくせいしょ) ('New Testament').
旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)を読(よ)んでいる。
I'm reading the Old Testament.
旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の物語(ものがたり)を子供(こども)に聞(き)かせた。
She told stories from the Old Testament to her children.
旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の創世記(そうせいき)には、世界(せかい)の成(な)り立(た)ちが書(か)かれている。
Genesis in the Old Testament describes the origin of the world.
旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)は、キリスト教(きょう)だけでなくユダヤ教(きょう)の聖典(せいてん)でもある点(てん)で重要(じゅうよう)だ。
The Old Testament is important in that it is a sacred text not only of Christianity but also of Judaism.

Compound of 旧約(きゅうやく) ('old covenant') and 聖書(せいしょ) ('scripture; Bible'). The term 旧約(きゅうやく) refers to the covenant between God and Israel established through Moses, which Christians see as superseded — though not abolished — by the 新約(しんやく) ('new covenant') brought by Jesus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)を読(よ)む: to read the Old Testament
  • 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の創世記(そうせいき): Genesis (book of the Old Testament)
  • 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の預言者(よげんしゃ): prophets of the Old Testament
  • 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の物語(ものがたり): stories from the Old Testament
  • 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)の世界(せかい): the world of the Old Testament

WELL-KNOWN BOOKS:

  • 創世記(そうせいき): Genesis
  • 出(しゅつ)エジプト記(き): Exodus
  • 詩篇(しへん): Psalms
  • 箴言(しんげん): Proverbs
  • イザヤ{書(しょ)}: Book of Isaiah

USAGE:

  • A standard term used in religious, historical, and literary contexts. In academic writing it also refers to the Hebrew Bible (Tanakh), though the two have slightly different book orders and canon.
  • Often contrasted in a pair with 新約聖書(しんやくせいしょ): 旧約聖書(きゅうやくせいしょ)と新約聖書(しんやくせいしょ) ('the Old and New Testaments').
  • Commonly abbreviated as 旧約(きゅうやく) when context makes the 'Bible' part clear, e.g., 旧約(きゅうやく)の物語(ものがたり) ('Old Testament stories').

RELATED TERMS:

  • 新約聖書(しんやくせいしょ): New Testament
  • 聖書(せいしょ): Bible; scripture (general)
  • 聖典(せいてん): sacred text; scripture (of any religion)
  • キリスト{教(きょう)}: Christianity
  • ユダヤ{教(きょう)}: Judaism
