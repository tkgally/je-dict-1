りんご園(えん)

りんごえん
noun
apple orchard
1. apple orchard
An orchard where apples are grown commercially. Common throughout the apple-producing regions of Japan, especially Aomori, Nagano, and Iwate. Often appears in tourism contexts, since many orchards offer pick-your-own apple experiences (りんご{狩(が)り}) in the autumn harvest season.
りんご園(えん)を訪(おとず)れる。
To visit an apple orchard.
秋(あき)になると、りんご園(えん)は真(ま)っ赤(か)な実(み)でいっぱいになる。
When autumn comes, the apple orchards fill with bright red fruit.
青森(あおもり)のりんご園(えん)では、家族(かぞく)連(づ)れがりんご狩(が)りを楽(たの)しんでいた。
At an apple orchard in Aomori, families were enjoying apple picking.

Compound of りんご ('apple') and the suffix 園(えん) ('garden; orchard; plantation'), commonly attached to fruit names to denote orchards: also seen in ぶどう{園(えん)} ('vineyard'), みかん{園(えん)} ('mandarin orange grove'), 梨(なし)園(えん) ('pear orchard'). The headword is usually written with りんご in kana, though the kanji 林檎(りんご) also appears.

USAGE:

  • Strongly associated with northern Japan, especially Aomori (Japan's leading apple producer) and Nagano.
  • Many orchards are open to the public during the autumn harvest for りんご{狩(が)り} ('apple picking'), making the word common in tourism brochures and seasonal news.
  • Other plantation suffixes are: 園(えん) (general, for fruit/flower farms), 農園(のうえん) (a working farm garden), 果樹園(かじゅえん) (fruit orchard, more general).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • りんご園(えん)を経営(けいえい)する: to run an apple orchard
  • りんご園(えん)を訪(おとず)れる: to visit an apple orchard
  • 広(ひろ)いりんご園(えん): a vast apple orchard
  • りんご園(えん)でりんご狩(が)り: apple picking at an orchard
  • 家族(かぞく)経営(けいえい)のりんご園(えん): a family-run apple orchard

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 果樹園(かじゅえん): fruit orchard — general term for any orchard.
  • ぶどう{園(えん)}: vineyard — same suffix pattern but for grapes.
  • 農園(のうえん): farm; plantation — broader term for a cultivated farm or garden, including fruit, vegetables, and flowers.
  • 林檎農家(りんごのうか): apple farmer / apple-growing household — refers to the people, not the place.
  • りんご狩(が)り: apple picking — the seasonal activity at a りんご園(えん).
