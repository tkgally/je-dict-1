航空便(こうくうびん)

こうくうびん
noun
airmail; air freight
1. airmail; air post
Mail sent by airplane, typically for international correspondence. Faster but more expensive than surface mail.
航空便(こうくうびん)で送(おく)る。
To send by airmail.
航空便(こうくうびん)なら一週間(いっしゅうかん)で届(とど)きます。
It will arrive in a week by airmail.
国際(こくさい)郵便(ゆうびん)は航空便(こうくうびん)と船便(ふなびん)のどちらかを選(えら)べます。
For international mail, you can choose between airmail and surface mail.
2. air freight; air cargo shipment
Goods or packages transported by airplane, as opposed to sea freight or overland transport.
荷物(にもつ)を航空便(こうくうびん)で発送(はっそう)した。
I shipped the package by air freight.
航空便(こうくうびん)は料金(りょうきん)が高(たか)いが早(はや)い。
Air freight is expensive but fast.
生鮮(せいせん)食品(しょく��ん)は航空便(こうくうびん)で輸送(ゆそう)されることが多(おお)い。
Fresh food is often transported by air freight.

Compound of 航空(こうく��) ('aviation; air') and 便(びん) ('mail service; delivery; flight'). The standard term used at post offices and shipping companies for air transport of mail or cargo.

USAGE:

  • At a Japanese post office, when sending international mail you will be asked to choose between 航空便(こうくうびん) and 船便(ふなびん) ('surface mail; sea mail').
  • The word also applies to commercial cargo shipped by air, as opposed to 船便(ふなび���) or 陸便(りくびん) ('overland transport').
  • On envelopes and packages, often written in the abbreviated form エアメール (from English 'airmail') or marked with the French-origin label PAR AVION.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 航空便(こうくうびん)で送(おく)る: to send by airmail
  • 航空便(こうくうびん)で届(とど)く: to arrive by airmail
  • 航空便(こうくうびん)の料金(りょうきん): airmail postage
  • 国際(こくさい)航空便(こうくうびん): international airmail
