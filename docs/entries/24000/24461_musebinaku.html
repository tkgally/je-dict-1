1. to sob; to weep convulsively
Describes crying so hard that one can barely breathe. Godan verb (く row).
彼女(かのじょ)はむせび泣(な)いた。
She sobbed.
悲(かな)しみのあまり、むせび泣(な)く声(こえ)が聞(き)こえた。
Overcome with grief, the sound of sobbing could be heard.
母親(ははおや)は息子(むすこ)の手紙(てがみ)を読(よ)みながら、静(しず)かにむせび泣(な)いていた。
The mother was quietly sobbing as she read her son's letter.

A compound of むせぶ (to choke, to be choked with emotion) and 泣(な)く (to cry). Describes intense crying where emotion constricts the throat, causing gasping or choking between sobs. Frequently used in literature and emotional narration.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • むせび泣(な)く声(こえ): the sound of sobbing
  • 静(しず)かにむせび泣(な)く: to sob quietly
  • 悲(かな)しみにむせび泣(な)く: to sob with grief

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 泣(な)く: to cry (general, neutral)
  • すすり泣(な)く: to sniffle, cry softly (quieter, more restrained)
  • 号泣(ごうきゅう)する: to wail, cry loudly (louder and more dramatic)
  • しくしく泣(な)く: to cry softly and continuously (onomatopoeic)
