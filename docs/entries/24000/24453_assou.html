1. pressure feeding; pumping under pressure
sending liquid, gas, or other material through pipes or channels using applied pressure
コンクリートをポンプで圧送(あっそう)する。
To pump concrete under pressure.
圧送(あっそう)管(かん)を通(つう)じて汚水(おすい)を処理場(しょりじょう)に送(おく)る。
Wastewater is sent to the treatment plant through a pressure pipe.
高層(こうそう)ビルの上階(じょうかい)に水(みず)を届(とど)けるためには、強力(きょうりょく)なポンプで圧送(あっそう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
To deliver water to the upper floors of a high-rise building, powerful pumps are needed to force it through under pressure.

A technical term used in construction, plumbing, and engineering for moving materials through pipes using applied pressure. Most commonly encountered in concrete pumping (生(なま)コン圧送(あっそう)) at construction sites and in wastewater management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 圧送(あっそう)ポンプ: pressure pump
  • 圧送(あっそう)管(かん): pressure pipe
  • コンクリート圧送(あっそう): concrete pumping
  • 圧送(あっそう)方式(ほうしき): pressure-feed system

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 送水(そうすい): water delivery — sending water, not necessarily under pressure
  • 加圧(かあつ): pressurization — applying pressure in general, not specifically for sending material
  • 揚水(ようすい): water pumping/lifting — pumping water upward, as from a well
