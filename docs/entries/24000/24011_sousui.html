1. water supply; pumping or sending of water through pipes or hoses
The action of sending water from one place to another through pipes, hoses, or other conduits. Used for municipal water systems (sending water from a treatment plant to consumers), firefighting (pumping water through a hose to a fire), and industrial contexts. Can be used as a する verb.
送水(そうすい)が止(と)まった。
The water supply stopped.
消防車(しょうぼうしゃ)からホースで送水(そうすい)する。
Water is pumped from the fire truck through the hose.
工事(こうじ)の影響(えいきょう)で一時的(いちじてき)に送水(そうすい)が停止(ていし)されます。
The water supply will be temporarily stopped due to construction.
浄水場(じょうすいじょう)から各家庭(かくかてい)までの送水(そうすい)ルートには複数(ふくすう)のポンプ施設(しせつ)が含(ふく)まれている。
The water-supply route from the treatment plant to individual homes includes several pump facilities.

Composed of 送(そう) (to send) and 水(すい) (water).

USAGE:
送水(そうすい) is primarily a technical or infrastructural term used in announcements by water utilities, fire-department reports, and engineering contexts. It refers specifically to the action of moving water through a conduit. In everyday home conversation about having running water in the tap, 水道(すいどう) (plumbing, tap water) is more usual. 送水(そうすい) is a する-verb, so 送水(そうすい)する means "to pump / send water."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送水(そうすい)する: to send / pump water
  • 送水(そうすい)が止(と)まる: the water supply stops
  • 送水(そうすい)管(かん): water supply pipe
  • 送水(そうすい)ポンプ: water pump (for supply)
  • 送水(そうすい)停止(ていし): suspension of water supply

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 給水(きゅうすい): water supply (to end users) — focuses on the delivery side, often used for supplying drinking water in emergencies or for a specific building
  • 排水(はいすい): drainage — the opposite direction, sending water out
  • 取水(しゅすい): water intake — taking water from a river or reservoir, the first stage of the water cycle
  • 配水(はいすい): water distribution — distributing the water within a municipal network (note: same pronunciation as 排水(はいすい) drainage)
