1. drive; driving; propulsion
The mechanism or action of powering and moving a machine, vehicle, or device. Most commonly encountered in automotive and engineering contexts when describing how power is transmitted to wheels or other moving parts.
この車(くるま)は四輪(よんりん)駆動(くどう)だ。
This car is four-wheel drive.
モーターで駆動(くどう)する装置(そうち)を開発(かいはつ)した。
We developed a motor-driven device.
電気(でんき)駆動(くどう)の自動車(じどうしゃ)は環境(かんきょう)にやさしいと言(い)われている。
Electrically driven cars are said to be environmentally friendly.

Compound of 駆(く) ('to drive; to gallop') and 動(どう) ('to move'). Originally described driving horses at speed, now almost exclusively used in mechanical and technological contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 四輪(よんりん)駆動(くどう) (4WD): four-wheel drive
  • 前輪(ぜんりん)駆動(くどう) (FWD): front-wheel drive
  • 後輪(こうりん)駆動(くどう) (RWD): rear-wheel drive
  • 駆動(くどう)方式(ほうしき): drive system; drive type
  • 駆動(くどう)力(りょく): driving force; motive power
  • 電気(でんき)駆動(くどう): electric drive

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 動力(どうりょく): motive power; power source — emphasizes the energy source rather than the transmission mechanism
  • 推進(すいしん): propulsion; promotion — used for rockets, ships, and figuratively for driving initiatives forward
  • 運転(うんてん): operation; driving — focuses on the human act of operating a vehicle or machine
