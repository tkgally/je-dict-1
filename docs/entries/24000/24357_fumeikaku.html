不明確(ふめいかく)

ふめいかく
adjective-na
unclear; ambiguous; vague
1. unclear; ambiguous; vague; indefinite
Not clearly defined, stated, or understood. Used to describe situations, statements, boundaries, or concepts that lack precision or certainty.
基準(きじゅん)が不明確(ふめいかく)だ。
The criteria are unclear.
不明確(ふめいかく)な表現(ひょうげん)は誤解(ごかい)を招(まね)く。
Ambiguous expressions invite misunderstanding.
責任(せきにん)の所在(しょざい)が不明確(ふめいかく)なまま計画(けいかく)が進(すす)んでしまった。
The plan proceeded with the question of responsibility left unclear.

Formed with the negative prefix 不(ふ) and 明確(めいかく) ('clear; definite'). A straightforward negation meaning 'not clear' or 'not definite.' Common in formal writing, business communication, and academic contexts where precision matters.

USAGE:

  • Frequently modifies abstract nouns: 基準(きじゅん) ('criteria'), 境界(きょうかい) ('boundary'), 定義(ていぎ) ('definition'), 方針(ほうしん) ('policy').
  • In business Japanese, pointing out that something is 不明確(ふめいかく) is a diplomatic way of requesting clarification without directly criticizing.
  • Functions as a na-adjective: 不明確(ふめいかく)な点(てん) ('unclear points'), 不明確(ふめいかく)である ('is unclear').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不明確(ふめいかく)な点(てん): unclear points
  • 不明確(ふめいかく)な表現(ひょうげん): ambiguous expression
  • 不明確(ふめいかく)な基準(きじゅん): unclear criteria
  • 境界(きょうかい)が不明確(ふめいかく): the boundary is unclear

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 曖昧(あいまい): ambiguous; vague — emphasizes deliberate or inherent fuzziness rather than simple lack of clarity
  • 不明(ふめい): unknown; unclear — broader term that can mean 'identity unknown' or 'cause unclear'
  • 明確(めいかく): clear; definite — the direct antonym
  • 漠然(ばくぜん): vague; hazy — describes an even more diffuse, unfocused quality
