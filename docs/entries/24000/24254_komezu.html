米酢(こめず)

こめず
noun
rice vinegar
1. rice vinegar
Vinegar made from fermented rice, a fundamental seasoning in Japanese cuisine. It has a milder, slightly sweet flavor compared to Western grain or distilled vinegars, making it essential for sushi rice, dressings, and pickles.
米酢(こめず)で酢(す)の物(もの)を作(つく)る。
Make vinegared dishes with rice vinegar.
寿司(すし)には米酢(こめず)が欠(か)かせない。
Rice vinegar is indispensable for sushi.
純(じゅん)米酢(こめず)は米(こめ)だけを原料(げんりょう)にして作(つく)られるので風味(ふうみ)が豊(ゆた)かだ。
Pure rice vinegar is made solely from rice, so it has a rich flavor.

Compound of 米(こめ) ('rice') and 酢(す) ('vinegar'; voiced as ず in compounds). Also sometimes read as よねず. The most traditional and widely used vinegar in Japanese cooking.

TYPES:

  • 純(じゅん)米酢(こめず): pure rice vinegar — made from 100% rice; richer flavor
  • 穀物酢(こくもつす): grain vinegar — made from mixed grains; cheaper and sharper
  • 黒酢(くろず): black vinegar — aged rice vinegar with a deeper, more complex flavor; popular as a health drink

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 米酢(こめず)を使(つか)う: to use rice vinegar
  • 米酢(こめず)で味(あじ)をつける: to season with rice vinegar
  • 米酢(こめず)と砂糖(さとう)と塩(しお): rice vinegar, sugar, and salt (the classic sushi rice seasoning trio)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 穀物酢(こくもつす): grain vinegar — made from a blend of grains; a cheaper, more pungent all-purpose vinegar
  • 黒酢(くろず): black vinegar — a dark, aged rice vinegar with a mellow, rich flavor
  • 酢(す): vinegar — the general term for all types of vinegar
  • 寿司酢(すしず): sushi vinegar — pre-mixed seasoned vinegar specifically for sushi rice, containing sugar and salt
