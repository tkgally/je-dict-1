昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)

ちゅうこうせい
noun
diurnal habit; being active during the day
1. diurnal habit; being active during the day
The biological habit of being active during daytime and resting at night — applied to animals as a classification of their activity pattern. Directly contrasted with 夜行性(やこうせい) ('nocturnal habit'). Used mainly in biology, zoology, and nature writing.
犬(いぬ)は昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)だ。
Dogs are diurnal.
多(おお)くの鳥(とり)は昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)の動物(どうぶつ)だ。
Many birds are diurnal animals.
人間(にんげん)はもともと昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)なので、夜勤(やきん)を続(つづ)けると体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)しやすい。
Humans are naturally diurnal, so continuously working night shifts tends to harm one's health.

Compound of 昼(ちゅう) ('daytime'), 行(こう) ('moving; acting'), and the suffix 性(せい) ('nature; property'). Used in biology and natural history to classify animals by their daily activity pattern. Almost always contrasted with 夜行性(やこうせい) ('nocturnal') and occasionally with 薄明性(はくめいせい) ('crepuscular').

USAGE:

  • Most common in nature documentaries, biology textbooks, zoo exhibits, and pet-care articles.
  • Typically appears as a predicate noun (犬(いぬ)は昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)だ) or as a modifier (昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)の動物(どうぶつ)).
  • When discussing humans, the word often comes up in health articles warning that night-shift work disrupts our natural daytime rhythm.
  • Also written 昼光性(ちゅうこうせい) (with 光(こう), 'light') in some biological sources, though 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい) is the standard form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)の動物(どうぶつ): a diurnal animal
  • 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)である: to be diurnal
  • 完全(かんぜん)な昼行性(ちゅうこうせい): strictly diurnal
  • 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)の生活(せいかつ): a daytime lifestyle
  • 人間(にんげん)は昼行性(ちゅうこうせい)だ: humans are diurnal

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夜行性(やこうせい): nocturnal — the direct antonym; used for animals active at night such as owls, bats, and raccoons.
  • 薄明性(はくめいせい): crepuscular — active at dawn and dusk; a narrower category between 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい) and 夜行性(やこうせい).
  • 生活(せいかつ)リズム: daily rhythm; lifestyle rhythm — everyday word for people's daily routine; used when talking about humans rather than animal biology.
  • 日中(にっちゅう)活動(かつどう): daytime activity — descriptive phrase; 昼行性(ちゅうこうせい) sums the same idea up as a single biological label.
  • 概日(がいじつ)リズム: circadian rhythm — the broader biological 24-hour cycle underlying diurnal and nocturnal behavior.
