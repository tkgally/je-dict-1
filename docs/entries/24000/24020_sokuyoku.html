足浴(そくよく)

そくよく
noun
foot bath; soaking one's feet in hot water
1. foot bath; soaking one's feet in hot water
Soaking the feet and lower legs in warm or hot water, either as a relaxing home practice or as a therapeutic and nursing procedure. Popular at hot-spring resorts as a 足湯(あしゆ) facility and also used in healthcare settings to warm patients who cannot take a full bath.
足浴(そくよく)をする。
To take a foot bath.
寝(ね)る前(まえ)に足浴(そくよく)をすると眠(ねむ)りやすくなる。
Taking a foot bath before bed makes it easier to fall asleep.
入院中(にゅういんちゅう)で入浴(にゅうよく)できない患者(かんじゃ)にも、看護師(かんごし)が足浴(そくよく)を行(おこな)ってくれる。
Even for hospitalized patients who cannot take a bath, nurses will perform a foot bath for them.

Sino-Japanese compound of 足(そく) ('foot') and 浴(よく) ('bathing'). A somewhat technical term used especially in healthcare, nursing, and wellness contexts. In everyday conversation the native Japanese word 足湯(あしゆ) is far more common, especially at hot-spring resorts and roadside rest stops.

USAGE:

  • Most common in nursing, eldercare, and medical writing, where it names a standard care procedure for warming patients, improving circulation, or promoting sleep.
  • Also appears in wellness and sleep-hygiene articles recommending a foot bath as a home remedy.
  • Takes する} to form a verb: {足浴(そくよく)する ('to do a foot bath'), but 足浴(そくよく)をする} and {足浴(そくよく)を行(おこな)う are equally common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 足浴(そくよく)をする: to take a foot bath
  • 足浴(そくよく)を行(おこな)う: to perform a foot bath (clinical register)
  • 足浴(そくよく)の効果(こうか): the effects of a foot bath
  • 手浴(しゅよく)・足浴(そくよく): hand bath and foot bath (paired care term)
  • 温(あたた)かい足浴(そくよく): a warm foot bath

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 足湯(あしゆ): foot bath — native Japanese word; refers especially to public hot-spring foot-bath facilities, and more casual in tone.
  • 入浴(にゅうよく): bathing — general word for taking a bath; contrasted with 足浴(そくよく) in nursing contexts when a full bath is not possible.
  • 手浴(しゅよく): hand bath — soaking the hands in warm water, a parallel nursing procedure.
  • 半身浴(はんしんよく): half-body bath — soaking the body up to the waist only, a popular wellness practice.
  • 温浴(おんよく): warm bathing — broader term covering any warm-water bathing for therapeutic purposes.
