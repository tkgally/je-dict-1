1. hanging up the phone; ending a phone call; disconnecting
The act of ending a telephone conversation by hanging up. A concise, somewhat formal or technical term used in business communication and call center contexts.
切電(せつでん)する前(まえ)に確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed before hanging up.
お客(きゃく)さまより先(さき)に切電(せつでん)しないこと。
Do not hang up before the customer does.
切電(せつでん)後(ご)に対応(たいおう)内容(ないよう)を記録(きろく)してください。
Please record the details of the call after hanging up.
相手(あいて)が切電(せつでん)したのを確認(かくにん)してから受話器(じゅわき)を置(お)いた。
I put down the receiver after confirming the other party had hung up.

Composed of 切(せつ) (cut, sever) and 電(でん) (electricity; telephone). A compact term for ending a phone call, used primarily in business manuals, call center training materials, and telecommunications contexts.

In Japanese telephone etiquette, the caller is generally expected to hang up first. In business settings, the person of lower status should wait for the senior party or the customer to hang up. 切電(せつでん) is the technical term used in such training materials.

Not to be confused with the homophone 節電(せつでん) (energy conservation), which uses different kanji.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 切電(せつでん)する: to hang up the phone
  • 切電(せつでん)後(ご): after hanging up
  • 切電(せつでん)時(じ): at the time of hanging up
  • 着信(ちゃくしん)から切電(せつでん)まで: from incoming call to hang-up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電話(でんわ)を切(き)る: to hang up the phone — the everyday expression; 切電(せつでん) is the compact business/technical equivalent
  • 節電(せつでん): energy conservation — a homophone with completely different meaning and kanji
  • 着信(ちゃくしん): incoming call — often paired with 切電(せつでん) in call records
