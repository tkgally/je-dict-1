歴史上(れきしじょう)

れきしじょう
noun / adverb
historically, in history, historical
1. historically, in history
Used as a modifier or adverbial phrase meaning 'throughout recorded history' or 'from a historical perspective.' Commonly attached to nouns with の (歴史上(れきしじょう)の...) to mean 'historical ~' and used adverbially with verbs to mean 'in the course of history' or 'as a matter of historical record.' Frequently used in claims about how rare, important, or unprecedented an event is.
歴史上(れきしじょう)の人物(じんぶつ)を調(しら)べる。
To research historical figures.
この発見(はっけん)は歴史上(れきしじょう)初(はじ)めてのことだ。
This discovery is the first of its kind in history.
今回(こんかい)の猛暑(もうしょ)は歴史上(れきしじょう)まれに見(み)る規模(きぼ)だった。
This summer's extreme heat was on a scale rarely seen in history.
歴史上(れきしじょう)、これほど急激(きゅうげき)に技術(ぎじゅつ)が進歩(しんぽ)した時代(じだい)は存在(そんざい)しない。
Historically, there has never been an era in which technology advanced this rapidly.

Compound of 歴史(れきし) ('history') + the suffix 上(じょう) ('with respect to; on'), which attaches to abstract nouns to form expressions meaning 'from the standpoint of ~' or 'in terms of ~.' 歴史上(れきしじょう) thus conveys 'as a matter of history' or 'in the scope of history.'

KEY PATTERNS:

  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)の + noun: 'historical ~' — modifies a noun as an attributive.
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)の人物(じんぶつ): historical figure
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)の大事件(だいじけん): a great event in history
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう) + verb / adjective: used adverbially to locate something within the span of recorded history.
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)初(はじ)めての: the first in history
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)まれに見(み)る: rarely seen in history

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)の人物(じんぶつ): a historical figure
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)の出来事(できごと): a historical event
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)最大(さいだい)の: the greatest in history
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)最悪(さいあく)の: the worst in history
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)初(はじ)めて: for the first time in history
  • 歴史上(れきしじょう)例(れい)のない: unprecedented in history

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 歴史的(れきしてき): historic; historical — an adjective used attributively; emphasizes significance or pertaining to history, while 歴史上(れきしじょう) locates something within the sweep of history.
  • 史上(しじょう): in history — nearly synonymous, often interchangeable, slightly more compact and frequent in headlines (史上最高(しじょうさいこう)).
  • 歴史的(れきしてき)に: historically (in an adjectival-adverbial sense) — can express causation ('historically speaking, ...') as well as factual location in history.

USAGE:

  • Very common in journalism and academic writing when describing extremes: the greatest, the first, the rarest, the worst, etc.
  • Functions both as a noun (taking の) and as an adverb (directly modifying verbs).
  • A closely related productive pattern is 〜上(じょう) ('in terms of ~'): 教育上(きょういくじょう) ('from an educational standpoint'), 健康上(けんこうじょう) ('for health reasons'), 法律上(ほうりつじょう) ('legally; in legal terms').
