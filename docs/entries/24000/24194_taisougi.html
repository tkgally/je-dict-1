体操着(たいそうぎ)

たいそうぎ
noun
gym clothes; PE uniform
1. gym clothes; PE uniform
Clothing worn for physical education classes or exercise, typically a matching set designated by a school.
体操着(たいそうぎ)に着替(きが)える。
Change into gym clothes.
体操着(たいそうぎ)を忘(わす)れてしまった。
I forgot my gym clothes.
子(こ)どもの体操着(たいそうぎ)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote my child's name on their gym clothes.
毎週(まいしゅう)金曜日(きんようび)に体操着(たいそうぎ)を持(も)ち帰(かえ)って洗濯(せんたく)する。
Every Friday, I bring the gym clothes home and wash them.

体操(たいそう) (gymnastics, exercise) + 着(ぎ) (clothing). The standard school PE uniform in Japan, usually consisting of a white short-sleeved shirt and shorts or long pants.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体操着(たいそうぎ)に着替(きが)える: change into gym clothes
  • 体操着(たいそうぎ)を洗(あら)う: wash gym clothes
  • 体操着(たいそうぎ)袋(ぶくろ): gym clothes bag
  • 体操着(たいそうぎ)を忘(わす)れる: forget one's gym clothes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 運動着(うんどうぎ): athletic wear — a broader term for exercise clothing, not limited to school
  • 体育着(たいいくぎ): PE clothes — a synonym used in some regions
  • ジャージ: jersey, tracksuit — casual athletic clothing often worn outside of PE as well

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese schools, students are typically required to wear a designated 体操着(たいそうぎ) for PE classes. Parents usually buy these through the school, and students change in the classroom or a changing room before PE.
