休(やす)みなし

やすみなし
noun
no days off; without a break; nonstop
1. no days off; without a break; working continuously without rest
The state of having no breaks, holidays, or rest days. Used to describe continuous work, operation, or effort without pause. Can describe a work schedule, a store's operating hours, or someone's relentless effort.
年中(ねんじゅう)休(やす)みなしで営業(えいぎょう)している。
Open year-round without any days off.
今月(こんげつ)は休(やす)みなしで働(はたら)いた。
I worked this entire month without a day off.
締(し)め切(き)り前(まえ)は休(やす)みなしで準備(じゅんび)を進(すす)めなければならなかった。
Before the deadline, I had to keep preparing without any breaks.

Combines 休(やす)み (rest, break, day off) and なし (without, none). A straightforward compound using the common なし suffix.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年中(ねんじゅう)休(やす)みなし: open year-round, no holidays
  • 休(やす)みなしで働(はたら)く: to work without days off
  • 休(やす)みなし営業(えいぎょう): operating without days off
  • 休(やす)みなしの日々(ひび): days without rest

USAGE:
Often seen on shop signs and advertisements to indicate that a store is open every day (年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう) is the more formal equivalent). Also used to describe demanding work schedules or intense periods of effort. The tone can be matter-of-fact (describing a store's hours) or sympathetic/complaining (describing an exhausting workload).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年中無休(ねんじゅうむきゅう): open year-round — the formal, four-character equivalent
  • 無休(むきゅう): no holidays — more concise and formal
  • 不休(ふきゅう): without rest — literary, emphasizing tireless effort
