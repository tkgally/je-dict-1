のように

のように
expression
like; just as; in the same way as
1. like; as; in the manner of
Attaches to a noun to compare an action or state to it. The adverbial counterpart of のような, which modifies nouns instead of verbs and adjectives.
鳥(とり)のように飛(と)びたい。
I want to fly like a bird.
毎年(まいとし)のように台風(たいふう)が来(く)る。
Typhoons come almost every year.
先生(せんせい)がおっしゃったように、準備(じゅんび)が何(なに)より大切(たいせつ)だと分(わ)かった。
As the teacher said, I came to see that preparation matters more than anything.

USAGE:
After a noun, use のように (雪(ゆき)のように白(しろ)い). After a verb or i-adjective, drop の and use ように directly (言(い)ったように). With na-adjectives the linker is な (静(しず)かなように).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夢(ゆめ)のように: like a dream
  • 毎日(まいにち)のように: almost every day
  • 前(まえ)のように: as before
  • 以下(いか)のように: as follows (written)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • のような: like — the same comparison used to modify a noun (夢(ゆめ)のような話(はなし))
  • みたいに: like — casual spoken equivalent, attaches directly to the noun
  • のごとく: like, as — literary and archaic-sounding
