電動ドライバー

でんどうどらいばー
noun
power screwdriver; electric screwdriver
1. power screwdriver; electric screwdriver
A motor-driven tool that turns screws in and out. Most household models are rechargeable and are used for assembling furniture and small repairs.
電動(でんどう)ドライバーでネジを締(し)める。
I tighten the screw with a power screwdriver.
家具(かぐ)を組(く)み立(た)てるなら電動(でんどう)ドライバーがあると早(はや)い。
If you're assembling furniture, it's quicker with a power screwdriver.
力(ちから)を入(い)れすぎるとネジ山(やま)がつぶれるので、電動(でんどう)ドライバーは弱(よわ)めに設定(せってい)する。
Too much force strips the screw thread, so set the power screwdriver to a weaker setting.
充電式(じゅうでんしき)の電動(でんどう)ドライバーを一(ひと)つ買(か)っておくと、棚(たな)の取(と)り付(つ)けから修理(しゅうり)まで幅広(はばひろ)く使(つか)える。
Buying one rechargeable power screwdriver gives you a tool for everything from mounting shelves to repairs.

Formed from 電動(でんどう) (electrically powered) + ドライバー (screwdriver, from English "driver"). Note that ドライバー alone also means a car driver and a golf club; context distinguishes them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電動(でんどう)ドライバーでネジを締(し)める: to drive a screw with a power screwdriver
  • ネジを外(はず)す: to remove a screw
  • 充電式(じゅうでんしき)電動(でんどう)ドライバー: rechargeable power screwdriver

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電動(でんどう)ドリル: electric drill — bores holes rather than driving screws
  • インパクトドライバー: impact driver — adds a hammering action for tougher jobs
  • ドライバー: screwdriver — the ordinary hand tool
