署名欄(しょめいらん)

しょめいらん
noun
signature field; signature line
1. signature field; signature line
The blank space on a form, contract, or delivery slip where the person signs their name. Also used of the signature box in an email or on a web form.
署名欄(しょめいらん)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)く。
To write your name in the signature field.
署名欄(しょめいらん)が空(あ)いたままだったので、書類(しょるい)が返(かえ)された。
The signature field was left blank, so the document was sent back.
契約書(けいやくしょ)の最後(さいご)のページにある署名欄(しょめいらん)に、日付(ひづけ)と住所(じゅうしょ)も一緒(いっしょ)に記入(きにゅう)してください。
In the signature field on the last page of the contract, please also fill in the date and your address.

署名(しょめい) (signature) + 欄(らん) (column, field on a form). The suffix 欄(らん) names any labeled blank on a document: 住所(じゅうしょ)欄(らん) (address field), 備考(びこう)欄(らん) (remarks field).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 署名欄(しょめいらん)に記入(きにゅう)する: to fill in the signature field
  • 署名欄(しょめいらん)にサインする: to sign in the signature field
  • 署名欄(しょめいらん)が空欄(くうらん): the signature field is blank

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 捺印欄(なついんらん): seal field — the space for a personal seal, which many Japanese documents require instead of or alongside a signature
  • 記入欄(きにゅうらん): entry field — any blank to be filled in, not specifically for a name
