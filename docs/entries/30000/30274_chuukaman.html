中華(ちゅうか)まん

ちゅうかまん
noun
Chinese steamed bun
1. Chinese steamed bun; steamed filled bun
A soft steamed wheat bun with a savoury or sweet filling, sold hot at convenience stores from autumn through winter. The two standard kinds are 肉(にく)まん (pork) and あんまん (sweet red bean).
コンビニで中華(ちゅうか)まんを買(か)った。
I bought a steamed bun at the convenience store.
寒(さむ)くなると中華(ちゅうか)まんが食(た)べたくなる。
When it gets cold, I start wanting steamed buns.
冷(ひ)えた中華(ちゅうか)まんは、レンジで温(あたた)めるより蒸(む)した方(ほう)がおいしい。
Cold steamed buns taste better steamed again than microwaved.

From 中華(ちゅうか) (Chinese) + まん, a shortening of 饅頭(まんじゅう). The generic term; individual varieties are named by filling.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肉(にく)まん: pork bun
  • あんまん: sweet red-bean bun
  • ピザまん: pizza-filled bun
  • 中華(ちゅうか)まんを蒸(む)す: to steam a Chinese bun

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Convenience stores put heated cases of 中華(ちゅうか)まん by the register when the weather turns cold, and their appearance is a familiar seasonal marker of autumn.
