上昇幅

じょうしょうはば
noun
extent of a rise; margin of increase
1. extent of a rise; size of an increase; margin of gain
How far something has gone up — a price, a stock index, a temperature — stated as the size of the movement rather than the final figure. Common in news reporting on markets and weather.
上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)が大(おお)きい。
The increase is large.
株価(かぶか)の上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)は過去(かこ)最大(さいだい)となった。
The rise in the stock price was the largest on record.
午後(ごご)になって、気温(きおん)の上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)が予想(よそう)より小(ちい)さくなった。
In the afternoon, the temperature rise turned out to be smaller than expected.
今回(こんかい)の値上(ねあ)げは、上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)を三(さん)パーセント以内(いない)に抑(おさ)えるという。
They say this price increase will be held to within three percent.

幅(はば) (width) attaches to many words of change to name the size of the movement, so this family is worth learning together.

RELATED FORMS:

  • 下落幅(げらくはば): extent of a drop — the direct opposite
  • 値上(ねあ)げ幅(はば): size of a price increase
  • 伸(の)び幅(はば): extent of growth

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)が大(おお)きい／小(ちい)さい: for the rise to be large / small
  • 上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)を抑(おさ)える: to hold down the size of an increase
  • 上昇幅(じょうしょうはば)が縮(ちぢ)まる: for the gain to narrow
