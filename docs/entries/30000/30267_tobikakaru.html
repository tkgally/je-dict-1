1. to pounce on; to spring at; to lunge at
To leap suddenly at a person or animal in order to attack or seize them. The target takes に. Used of predators, of people attacking in anger, and figuratively of eager rushing at an opportunity.
猫(ねこ)がネズミに飛(と)び掛(か)かった。
The cat pounced on the mouse.
犬(いぬ)が急(きゅう)に飛(と)び掛(か)かってきて驚(おどろ)いた。
The dog suddenly lunged at me and startled me.
相手(あいて)に飛(と)び掛(か)かろうとした男(おとこ)を、周(まわ)りの人(ひと)たちが必死(ひっし)で止(と)めた。
The people around desperately stopped the man as he was about to lunge at his opponent.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. The target is marked with に, never を: 犬(いぬ)に飛(と)び掛(か)かる (to pounce on the dog).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 獲物(えもの)に飛(と)び掛(か)かる: to pounce on prey
  • 飛(と)び掛(か)かってくる: to come lunging at one
  • いきなり飛(と)び掛(か)かる: to lunge without warning

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 襲(おそ)う: to attack — the general word, with no implication of a leap
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)む: to jump in — into a place, not at a person
  • 掴(つか)みかかる: to grab at someone — seizing with the hands rather than leaping
