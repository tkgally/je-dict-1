1. to jump onto; to hop aboard (a train, bus, horse)
To get onto something in one quick leap, typically a vehicle that is about to leave or already moving. The nuance is haste, not athletic skill.
電車(でんしゃ)に飛(と)び乗(の)った。
I jumped onto the train.
発車(はっしゃ)まぎわのバスに飛(と)び乗(の)る。
To hop onto a bus just as it is pulling out.
知(し)らせを受(う)けて、近(ちか)くに止(と)まっていたタクシーに飛(と)び乗(の)って病院(びょういん)へ向(む)かった。
On getting the news, I jumped into a taxi parked nearby and headed for the hospital.

A compound of 飛(と)ぶ (to jump, to fly) and 乗(の)る (to board, to ride). The thing boarded is marked with に.

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive. Takes に for the vehicle, not を: 電車(でんしゃ)に飛(と)び乗(の)る, never ✗電車(でんしゃ)を飛(と)び乗(の)る.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電車(でんしゃ)に飛(と)び乗(の)る: to jump onto a train
  • 自転車(じてんしゃ)に飛(と)び乗(の)る: to hop on a bicycle
  • 馬(うま)に飛(と)び乗(の)る: to leap onto a horse

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飛(と)び降(お)りる: to jump off — the paired opposite
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む: to rush in — running into a train or building, without the leap
