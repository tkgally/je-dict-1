1. easy to use; user-friendly
Said of a tool, device, service, or interface that works smoothly and takes little effort or learning. Formed from 使(つか)う plus the suffix 〜やすい (easy to ...).
このアプリは使(つか)いやすい。
This app is easy to use.
使(つか)いやすい道具(どうぐ)を選(えら)んだ。
I chose tools that are easy to use.
新(あたら)しいサイトは前(まえ)より使(つか)いやすくなったと評判(ひょうばん)だ。
People say the new site has become easier to use than before.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 使(つか)いやすい道具(どうぐ): an easy-to-use tool
  • 使(つか)いやすさ: usability (noun form)
  • 使(つか)いやすくなる: to become easier to use
  • 使(つか)いやすいように並(なら)べる: to arrange things for easy use

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 使(つか)いにくい: hard to use — the direct opposite
  • 便利(べんり): convenient — about overall usefulness, not about ease of operation
  • 分(わ)かりやすい: easy to understand — about comprehension rather than handling
