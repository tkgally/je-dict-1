馬刺(ばさ)し

ばさし
noun
raw sliced horse meat; horse sashimi
1. raw sliced horse meat; horse sashimi
Thinly sliced raw horse meat, eaten with soy sauce, grated ginger, and garlic. A regional specialty of Kumamoto and parts of Nagano, and a common izakaya dish elsewhere.
馬刺(ばさ)しを注文(ちゅうもん)した。
I ordered basashi.
馬刺(ばさ)しは生姜(しょうが)醤油(じょうゆ)で食(た)べるとおいしい。
Basashi tastes good eaten with ginger and soy sauce.
熊本(くまもと)に行(い)ったら、名物(めいぶつ)の馬刺(ばさ)しをぜひ食(た)べてみてください。
If you go to Kumamoto, be sure to try basashi, the local specialty.

馬(ば) (horse) + 刺(さ)し, a shortening of 刺身(さしみ). The same pattern gives 鳥刺(とりさ)し (raw chicken) and 鹿刺(しかさ)し (raw venison).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 馬刺(ばさ)しを食(た)べる: to eat basashi
  • 馬刺(ばさ)しの盛(も)り合(あ)わせ: an assorted plate of basashi
  • 熊本(くまもと)名物(めいぶつ)の馬刺(ばさ)し: basashi, the specialty of Kumamoto

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
Horse meat is called 桜肉(さくらにく) ('cherry blossom meat') for its pink color, and 馬刺(ばさ)し is the most common way it is served. It is considered a delicacy rather than everyday food, and is usually eaten in restaurants rather than cooked at home.
