1. to get (something) ready in time; to make it in time
To arrange things so that a deadline or departure time is met. The transitive counterpart of 間(ま)に合(あ)う: the subject actively makes the timing work.
締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)わせる。
I'll get it done in time for the deadline.
朝(あさ)まで働(はたら)いて納品(のうひん)に間(ま)に合(あ)わせた。
We worked until morning and got the delivery in on time.
開店(かいてん)の日(ひ)に間(ま)に合(あ)わせるために、工事(こうじ)を二(に)週間(しゅうかん)早(はや)めた。
To make it in time for the opening day, we moved the construction up by two weeks.
2. to make do with; to get by using (something) temporarily
To use whatever is at hand as a stopgap because the proper thing is unavailable. Usually marked with で: ありもので間(ま)に合(あ)わせる (make do with what we have).
ありもので間(ま)に合(あ)わせよう。
Let's make do with what we have.
今日(きょう)はコンビニの弁当(べんとう)で間(ま)に合(あ)わせた。
Today I got by with a convenience store bento.
正式(せいしき)な資料(しりょう)が届(とど)くまで、コピーで間(ま)に合(あ)わせておいてください。
Until the official documents arrive, please make do with photocopies.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. It is the causative-style partner of the intransitive 間(ま)に合(あ)う: 電車(でんしゃ)に間(ま)に合(あ)う (I make the train) versus 出発(しゅっぱつ)に間(ま)に合(あ)わせる (I get things ready in time for the departure).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)わせる: to meet a deadline
  • 納期(のうき)に間(ま)に合(あ)わせる: to meet a delivery date
  • ありもので間(ま)に合(あ)わせる: to make do with what is on hand
  • 間(ま)に合(あ)わせの: makeshift, stopgap (used as a modifier)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 済(す)ませる: to finish off, to settle — about completing something, without the timing or stopgap nuance
  • 代用(だいよう)する: to substitute — a neutral, more formal word for sense 2
