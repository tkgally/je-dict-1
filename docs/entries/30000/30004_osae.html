抑(おさ)え

おさえ
noun
restraint; control; closer (baseball)
1. restraint; holding down; control; check
The act or means of keeping something under control — an emotion, a rise in prices, or one's authority over others.
感情(かんじょう)の抑(おさ)えがきかない。
I can't keep my emotions in check.
彼(かれ)は若手(わかて)への抑(おさ)えが利(き)く。
He commands the respect of the younger staff.
物価(ぶっか)上昇(じょうしょう)の抑(おさ)えとして対策(たいさく)を取(と)った。
They took measures as a check on rising prices.
2. closer; relief pitcher who finishes the game (baseball)
The relief pitcher brought in to protect a lead and record the final outs, typically in the last inning.
あのチームの抑(おさ)えは頼(たよ)りになる。
That team's closer is reliable.
九回(きゅうかい)に抑(おさ)えが登板(とうばん)した。
The closer took the mound in the ninth inning.
抑(おさ)えの投手(とうしゅ)が最後(さいご)の打者(だしゃ)を三振(さんしん)に仕留(しと)めた。
The closing pitcher struck out the final batter.

The noun form of 抑(おさ)える (to hold down, to restrain). The baseball 'closer' sense pictures the pitcher who holds the opponent down at the end of the game.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 抑(おさ)えが利(き)く: to have control or authority (over people)
  • 抑(おさ)えがきかない: to be unable to keep something in check
  • 抑(おさ)えの投手(とうしゅ): closing pitcher

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • クローザー: closer — the loanword for the same baseball role
  • 中継(なかつ)ぎ: middle reliever — pitches earlier, before the closer
