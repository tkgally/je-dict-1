1. productization; developing into a marketable product
Taking a technology, prototype, or idea and turning it into something that can actually be manufactured and sold. Used with する as a verb.
この技術(ぎじゅつ)はまだ製品化(せいひんか)されていない。
This technology has not been turned into a product yet.
試作品(しさくひん)の製品化(せいひんか)に三年(さんねん)かかった。
It took three years to turn the prototype into a product.
研究所(けんきゅうじょ)の成果(せいか)を製品化(せいひんか)するには、コスト(こすと)の問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)しなければならない。
To turn the laboratory's results into a product, the cost problem has to be solved.

製品(せいひん) (manufactured product) + 化(か) (the suffix for turning into). Belongs to the large family of 化(か) nouns that take する, like 実用化(じつようか) and 商品化(しょうひんか).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 製品化(せいひんか)する: to turn into a product
  • 製品化(せいひんか)が決(き)まる: for productization to be decided on
  • 製品化(せいひんか)の目処(めど)が立(た)つ: for the path to a product to become clear

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 商品化(しょうひんか): commercialization — emphasizes putting it on sale rather than manufacturing it
  • 実用化(じつようか): putting to practical use — the technology works in the field, whether or not it is sold
