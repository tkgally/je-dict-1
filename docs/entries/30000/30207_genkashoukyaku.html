1. depreciation; writing off an asset over time
Spreading the cost of a long-lived asset such as a building, machine, or vehicle across the years it is used, instead of treating the whole purchase as an expense at once.
減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく)の計算(けいさん)をする。
I calculate the depreciation.
この機械(きかい)は五年(ごねん)かけて減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく)する。
This machine is depreciated over five years.
建物(たてもの)の減価償却費(げんかしょうきゃくひ)が毎年(まいとし)経費(けいひ)として計上(けいじょう)される。
Depreciation expense on the building is recorded as a cost every year.
減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく)が終(お)わった設備(せつび)でも、まだ十分(じゅうぶん)に使(つか)えることが多(おお)い。
Equipment that has been fully depreciated can often still be used perfectly well.

Formed from 減価(げんか) (decline in value) + 償却(しょうきゃく) (writing off). Takes する to form a verb, and combines with 費(ひ) in 減価償却費(げんかしょうきゃくひ) (depreciation expense), which is what actually appears on the income statement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく)する: to depreciate an asset
  • 減価償却費(げんかしょうきゃくひ): depreciation expense
  • 減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく)が終(お)わる: to be fully depreciated

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 償却(しょうきゃく): writing off — the broader term, also used for debts
  • 耐用年数(たいようねんすう): useful life — the number of years used for the calculation
  • 原価(げんか): cost price — the cost of producing goods, unrelated despite the similar sound
