とともに

とともに
expression
together with; along with; as
1. together with; along with (a person or thing)
Attaches to a noun to mean 'in the company of' or 'in addition to.' Somewhat formal; と一緒(いっしょ)に is the everyday equivalent.
家族(かぞく)とともに暮(く)らす。
To live together with one's family.
感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちとともにお礼(れい)の品(しな)を送(おく)った。
I sent a gift along with my thanks.
代表(だいひょう)は選手(せんしゅ)たちとともに記者会見(きしゃかいけん)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
The representative attended the press conference together with the players.
2. as; at the same time as (something changes)
Marks two changes that proceed in step: as the first thing progresses, the second happens too. Common with nouns like 時代(じだい), 年齢(ねんれい), and 成長(せいちょう).
時代(じだい)とともに変(か)わる。
It changes with the times.
年齢(ねんれい)とともに体力(たいりょく)が落(お)ちてきた。
My physical strength has declined with age.
技術(ぎじゅつ)の進歩(しんぽ)とともに、働(はたら)き方(かた)も大(おお)きく変化(へんか)している。
Along with advances in technology, the way people work is also changing greatly.

The particle と plus 共(とも)に (together). Written とともに in kana or と共(とも)に in kanji; the kana form is more common in ordinary writing.

USAGE:

  • Noun + とともに: the only pattern for sense 1.
  • Change noun + とともに: sense 2, expressing parallel change (時代(じだい), 成長(せいちょう), 発展(はってん)).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 家族(かぞく)とともに: together with one's family
  • 時代(じだい)とともに: with the times
  • 年齢(ねんれい)とともに: with age
  • ~するとともに: while also doing ~ (formal written style)

REGISTER: more formal than と一緒(いっしょ)に, and common in news, speeches, and written prose.
