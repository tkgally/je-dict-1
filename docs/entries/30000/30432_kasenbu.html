下線部(かせんぶ)

かせんぶ
noun
underlined part; underlined portion
1. the underlined part (of a text)
The portion of a passage marked with an underline. Standard wording in exam questions and textbook exercises, which routinely ask about 下線部(かせんぶ).
下線部(かせんぶ)を訳(やく)しなさい。
Translate the underlined part.
下線部(かせんぶ)の意味(いみ)を説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain the meaning of the underlined part.
下線部(かせんぶ)が何(なに)を指(さ)しているか、本文(ほんぶん)の中(なか)から探(さが)して答(こた)えなさい。
Find in the passage what the underlined part refers to, and answer accordingly.

From 下線(かせん) (underline) + 部(ぶ) (part). When several passages are underlined they are numbered: 下線部(かせんぶ)(1), 下線部(かせんぶ)(2).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下線部(かせんぶ)を訳(やく)す: to translate the underlined part
  • 下線部(かせんぶ)について答(こた)える: to answer about the underlined part
  • 下線(かせん)を引(ひ)く: to underline

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ): the marked part — the same idea in Japanese-language exams, where the line runs beside vertical text
  • 空欄(くうらん): blank — the gap to be filled in a fill-in-the-blank question
