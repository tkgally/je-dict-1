集会場(しゅうかいじょう)

しゅうかいじょう
noun
assembly hall; meeting hall
1. assembly hall; meeting hall
A hall where groups gather for meetings, lectures, or community events. Often a shared facility belonging to a neighborhood association, apartment complex, or temple.
集会場(しゅうかいじょう)で会議(かいぎ)を開(ひら)く。
To hold a meeting in the assembly hall.
マンションの集会場(しゅうかいじょう)は予約(よやく)すれば使(つか)える。
The apartment building's meeting hall can be used if you reserve it.
地震(じしん)のあと、近(ちか)くの集会場(しゅうかいじょう)が避難所(ひなんじょ)として使(つか)われ、住民(じゅうみん)が夜(よる)を過(す)ごした。
After the earthquake, the nearby assembly hall was used as an evacuation shelter, and residents spent the night there.

集会(しゅうかい) (assembly, gathering) + 場(じょう) (place, venue). In residential areas it usually means the plain multi-purpose room used by the local 自治会(じちかい) (neighborhood association).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 集会場(しゅうかいじょう)を借(か)りる: to rent the assembly hall
  • 集会場(しゅうかいじょう)に集(あつ)まる: to gather at the assembly hall
  • 地域(ちいき)の集会場(しゅうかいじょう): the community assembly hall

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 公民館(こうみんかん): public community center — a municipal facility with classes and multiple rooms
  • 会場(かいじょう): venue — the site of any particular event, whatever the building
  • ホール: hall — usually larger, with a stage and fixed seating
