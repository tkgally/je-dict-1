1. to create; to originate; to found
To bring into existence something that did not exist before — a work of art, a company, a culture, a new future. A kanji variant of 作(つく)る chosen when the writer wants to stress originality rather than manufacture.
新(あたら)しい文化(ぶんか)を創(つく)る。
To create a new culture.
祖父(そふ)がこの会社(かいしゃ)を創(つく)った。
My grandfather founded this company.
だれかが決(き)めた道(みち)を歩(ある)くのではなく、自分(じぶん)の未来(みらい)は自分(じぶん)で創(つく)りたい。
Rather than walking a path someone else decided, I want to create my own future myself.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive; takes を. There is no separate intransitive partner — use できる or 生(う)まれる for the resulting state.

ORTHOGRAPHY:

つくる has three common kanji, all read the same and all conjugating identically:

  • 作(つく)る: the default, for making things of any size
  • 造(つく)る: for building or brewing large or physical things — ships, houses, sake
  • 創(つく)る: for originating something new — a work, a company, an era

創(つく)る is outside the standard 常用漢字(じょうようかんじ) readings, so official documents and textbooks normally write 作(つく)る instead. It is common in slogans, mission statements, and company names.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 未来(みらい)を創(つく)る: to create the future
  • 文化(ぶんか)を創(つく)る: to create a culture
  • 会社(かいしゃ)を創(つく)る: to found a company
  • 価値(かち)を創(つく)る: to create value
