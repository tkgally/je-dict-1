気象庁(きしょうちょう)

きしょうちょう
noun
Japan Meteorological Agency
1. Japan Meteorological Agency; JMA
The national agency responsible for weather forecasts, and for earthquake, tsunami, and volcano information in Japan. Its announcements are the standard source quoted in news reports.
気象庁(きしょうちょう)が注意報(ちゅういほう)を出(だ)した。
The Meteorological Agency issued an advisory.
気象庁(きしょうちょう)の発表(はっぴょう)によると、梅雨(つゆ)が明(あ)けたそうだ。
According to the Meteorological Agency's announcement, the rainy season has ended.
気象庁(きしょうちょう)は地震(じしん)の規模(きぼ)をマグニチュード六(ろく)・二(に)と発表(はっぴょう)した。
The Meteorological Agency announced the earthquake's magnitude as 6.2.

A government agency under the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism. Note that its remit is wider than weather alone: earthquakes, tsunami, and volcanic activity are also announced by the 気象庁(きしょうちょう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気象庁(きしょうちょう)の発表(はっぴょう): the Meteorological Agency's announcement
  • 気象庁(きしょうちょう)によると: according to the Meteorological Agency
  • 気象庁(きしょうちょう)が警報(けいほう)を出(だ)す: the Agency issues a warning
  • 気象庁(きしょうちょう)の予報(よほう): the Agency's forecast

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気象台(きしょうだい): meteorological observatory — a regional station, not the national agency
  • 天気予報(てんきよほう): weather forecast — the product, not the organization
