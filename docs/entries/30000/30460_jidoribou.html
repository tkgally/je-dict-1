自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)

じどりぼう
noun
selfie stick
1. selfie stick
An extendable rod that holds a smartphone or camera at arm's length so the user can photograph themselves with more of the surroundings in frame.
自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)を買(か)った。
I bought a selfie stick.
自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)を使(つか)って全員(ぜんいん)で写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)った。
We used a selfie stick to take a photo with everyone in it.
この美術館(びじゅつかん)では自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)の使用(しよう)が禁止(きんし)されている。
The use of selfie sticks is prohibited in this museum.

Composed of 自撮(じど)り (taking a photo of oneself) + 棒(ぼう) (stick, rod). A 2010s coinage that has settled in as the ordinary word for the device.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)を使(つか)う: to use a selfie stick
  • 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)を伸(の)ばす: to extend a selfie stick
  • 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)禁止(きんし): selfie sticks prohibited
  • 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)付(つ)きの三脚(さんきゃく): a tripod with a selfie-stick function

USAGE:
Signs at museums, temples, and crowded stations often read 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう)禁止(きんし), so the word is worth recognizing on notices. The English loanword セルフィースティック exists but is much rarer in speech.
