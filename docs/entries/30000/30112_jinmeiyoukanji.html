人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)

じんめいようかんじ
noun
kanji approved for use in personal names
1. kanji approved for personal names
A government-designated list of about 860 kanji that may be used in a child's given name in addition to the general-use 常用漢字(じょうようかんじ). Characters outside both lists cannot be registered at the city office.
この字(じ)は人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)に入(はい)っている。
This character is on the list of kanji approved for names.
人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)は時々(ときどき)追加(ついか)される。
Kanji approved for personal names are added from time to time.
常用漢字(じょうようかんじ)か人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)でなければ、子(こ)どもの名前(なまえ)に使(つか)うことはできない。
Unless a character is a general-use kanji or an approved name kanji, it cannot be used in a child's name.

Defined by ministerial ordinance and revised occasionally, usually when parents petition for a character. The list exists to keep names readable and registrable, so it includes many older or decorative characters that are not part of everyday writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)に追加(ついか)される: to be added to the name-kanji list
  • 人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)表(ひょう): the table of approved name kanji
  • 人名用漢字(じんめいようかんじ)以外(いがい)の字(じ): characters outside the approved list

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 常用漢字(じょうようかんじ): general-use kanji — the 2,136 characters for ordinary writing; names may also use these
  • 表外漢字(ひょうがいかんじ): kanji outside the lists — cannot be used in a newly registered name
