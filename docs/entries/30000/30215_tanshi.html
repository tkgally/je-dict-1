端子

たんし
noun
terminal; connector; port
1. terminal; connector; port
The point on a device where a wire or plug connects — a battery post, a screw terminal, or a socket such as an HDMI or USB port.
端子(たんし)が汚(よご)れている。
The terminal is dirty.
このテレビにはHDMI端子(たんし)が三(みっ)つある。
This television has three HDMI ports.
電池(でんち)の端子(たんし)にさびが付(つ)くと、電気(でんき)が流(なが)れにくくなる。
When rust forms on the battery terminals, the current has trouble flowing.
配線(はいせん)をつなぐ前(まえ)に、プラスとマイナスの端子(たんし)を間違(まちが)えていないか確認(かくにん)してください。
Before connecting the wiring, please check that you have not mixed up the positive and negative terminals.

Formed from 端(たん) (end, edge) + 子(し) (small part). Used both for bare metal contacts on batteries and circuit boards and for the sockets on consumer electronics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 端子(たんし)をつなぐ: to connect a terminal
  • プラス端子(たんし) / マイナス端子(たんし): positive / negative terminal
  • 入力(にゅうりょく)端子(たんし) / 出力(しゅつりょく)端子(たんし): input / output port

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • コネクタ: connector — usually the whole plug-and-socket assembly
  • ポート: port — the katakana term, favored for computer sockets
  • 差(さ)し込(こ)み口(ぐち): socket, outlet — everyday word for where a plug goes in
