路側(ろそく)

ろそく
noun
roadside; edge of the road
1. roadside; edge of the road
The edge or side portion of a road. A somewhat technical, traffic-related term, most familiar in 路側帯(ろそくたい) (the marked strip along the road edge for pedestrians).
路側(ろそく)に車(くるま)を止(と)める。
To stop the car at the roadside.
歩行者(ほこうしゃ)は路側帯(ろそくたい)を歩(ある)く。
Pedestrians walk in the roadside strip.
事故車(じこしゃ)が路側(ろそく)に寄(よ)せられていた。
The wrecked car had been pulled over to the roadside.

A traffic and road-engineering term for the side edge of a road. In ordinary conversation 道端(みちばた) is used instead; 路側(ろそく) appears mainly in fixed compounds and official contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 路側帯(ろそくたい): roadside band — the lined strip at the road edge for pedestrians
  • 路側(ろそく)駐車(ちゅうしゃ): roadside parking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 道端(みちばた): roadside — the everyday, colloquial word
  • 路肩(ろかた): road shoulder — the unpaved or emergency strip beside the lane
