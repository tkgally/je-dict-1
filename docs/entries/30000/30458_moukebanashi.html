儲(もう)け話(ばなし)

もうけばなし
noun
money-making scheme; get-rich proposition
1. money-making scheme; get-rich proposition
A proposal or tip presented as an easy way to make money. Usually carries a note of suspicion — the word most often appears in warnings about scams.
うまい儲(もう)け話(ばなし)には注意(ちゅうい)しよう。
Be careful of too-good-to-be-true money-making schemes.
友人(ゆうじん)から儲(もう)け話(ばなし)を持(も)ちかけられた。
A friend approached me with a money-making proposition.
必(かなら)ず儲(もう)かるという話(はなし)を信(しん)じて投資(とうし)し、結局(けっきょく)大金(たいきん)を失(うしな)った。
He believed a scheme that promised guaranteed profits, invested, and in the end lost a large sum.

Composed of 儲(もう)ける (to make a profit) + 話(はなし) (story, proposal), with the sequential voicing that turns はなし into ばなし in compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • うまい儲(もう)け話(ばなし): a too-good-to-be-true scheme
  • 儲(もう)け話(ばなし)を持(も)ちかける: to approach someone with a scheme
  • 儲(もう)け話(ばなし)に乗(の)る: to go along with a scheme
  • 儲(もう)け話(ばなし)に飛(と)びつく: to jump at a money-making offer

USAGE:
The phrase うまい儲(もう)け話(ばなし)はない ("there is no easy money") is a set warning, and police and banks use 儲(もう)け話(ばなし) in fraud-prevention campaigns. Treating one as genuine usually needs an explicit qualifier such as 本当(ほんとう)の.
