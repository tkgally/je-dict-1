反対方向(はんたいほうこう)

はんたいほうこう
noun
opposite direction
1. opposite direction; the other way
The direction directly contrary to the one in question. Everyday vocabulary for giving directions, describing traffic and train travel, and talking about movement in general.
反対方向(はんたいほうこう)の電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)ってしまった。
I got on a train going the opposite direction.
風(かぜ)が反対方向(はんたいほうこう)に変(か)わった。
The wind shifted to the opposite direction.
道(みち)を間違(まちが)えて、駅(えき)とは反対方向(はんたいほうこう)に十分(じゅっぷん)も歩(ある)いてしまった。
I took the wrong road and walked a full ten minutes in the opposite direction from the station.

反対(はんたい) (opposite, reverse) + 方向(ほうこう) (direction). The thing being reversed is marked with とは: 駅(えき)とは反対方向(はんたいほうこう) (the opposite direction from the station).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 反対方向(はんたいほうこう)に進(すす)む: to go in the opposite direction
  • 反対方向(はんたいほうこう)に曲(ま)がる: to turn the other way
  • 反対方向(はんたいほうこう)の電車(でんしゃ): a train going the other way

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆方向(ぎゃくほうこう): reverse direction — nearly interchangeable, slightly more technical
  • 逆(ぎゃく): the reverse, the opposite — a noun on its own, used far more broadly than for physical direction
