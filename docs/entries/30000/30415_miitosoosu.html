ミートソース

みーとそーす
noun
meat sauce; Bolognese sauce
1. meat sauce; Bolognese-style tomato and ground meat sauce
A tomato-based sauce made with ground meat and onions, served mainly over spaghetti. In Japan it is one of the standard pasta sauces, sold ready-made in cans and retort pouches.
ミートソースを作(つく)る。
I make meat sauce.
今日(きょう)の昼(ひる)ごはんはミートソースだ。
Today's lunch is spaghetti with meat sauce.
子(こ)どもはミートソースのスパゲッティが大好(だいす)きだ。
Children love spaghetti with meat sauce.
時間(じかん)がない時(とき)は、市販(しはん)のミートソースを温(あたた)めるだけでいい。
When you have no time, you can just heat up store-bought meat sauce.

From English "meat sauce." In everyday Japanese it usually means the whole dish (spaghetti served with the sauce), not just the sauce itself.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ミートソースのスパゲッティ: spaghetti with meat sauce
  • ミートソースを煮込(にこ)む: to simmer meat sauce
  • 手作(てづく)りのミートソース: homemade meat sauce
  • ミートソース缶(かん): canned meat sauce

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ボロネーゼ: Bolognese — the Italian name, used for restaurant-style versions
  • ナポリタン: Japanese-style ketchup spaghetti — sweeter, made with ketchup rather than a simmered tomato sauce
