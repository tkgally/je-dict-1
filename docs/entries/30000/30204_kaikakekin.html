買掛金

かいかけきん
noun
accounts payable; money owed to suppliers
1. accounts payable; trade payables
Money a business owes for goods or services it has already received on credit. A standard balance-sheet liability, and the counterpart of 売掛金(うりかけきん).
買掛金(かいかけきん)を支払(しはら)う。
We pay the accounts payable.
仕入先(しいれさき)への買掛金(かいかけきん)が今月末(こんげつまつ)に期限(きげん)を迎(むか)える。
The payable to our supplier comes due at the end of this month.
在庫(ざいこ)を増(ふ)やしたので、買掛金(かいかけきん)の残高(ざんだか)も大(おお)きくなった。
We built up inventory, so the accounts payable balance grew as well.
買掛金(かいかけきん)の支払(しはら)いを遅(おく)らせると、取引先(とりひきさき)との信用(しんよう)に関(かか)わる。
Delaying payment of accounts payable affects your credibility with business partners.

Formed from 買(か)い (buying) + 掛(か)け (on credit) + 金(きん) (money). Refers specifically to what is owed on ordinary purchases of goods for resale or production.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 買掛金(かいかけきん)を支払(しはら)う: to pay accounts payable
  • 買掛金(かいかけきん)の残高(ざんだか): payables balance
  • 買掛金(かいかけきん)が膨(ふく)らむ: for payables to swell

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 売掛金(うりかけきん): accounts receivable — the mirror image, money owed to the business
  • 未払金(みばらいきん): accrued payable — money owed for something other than ordinary purchases
  • 借入金(かりいれきん): borrowings — money owed to a lender rather than a supplier

Related Words

Antonym: 売掛金 (accounts receivable)
Related: 未払金 (accrued payable)
