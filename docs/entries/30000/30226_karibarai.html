1. cash advance; provisional payment; temporary disbursement
Money paid out before the final amount is known, to be settled up later. Typically an advance given to an employee for travel or entertainment costs, reconciled against receipts on return.
出張費(しゅっちょうひ)を仮払(かりばら)いしてもらった。
I got a cash advance for my business trip expenses.
仮払(かりばら)いの申請書(しんせいしょ)を経理(けいり)に出(だ)してください。
Please submit the cash advance request form to accounting.
帰社後(きしゃご)に領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)を添(そ)えて仮払(かりばら)いの精算(せいさん)をした。
After returning to the office, I settled the cash advance with the receipts attached.

仮(かり) (temporary, provisional) + 払(ばら)い (payment). The money is handed over first and the exact amount worked out afterward, so a 仮払(かりばら)い is always followed by a 精算(せいさん) (settlement).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮払(かりばら)いを受(う)ける: to receive a cash advance
  • 仮払(かりばら)いを申請(しんせい)する: to apply for a cash advance
  • 仮払(かりばら)いの精算(せいさん): settlement of a cash advance
  • 仮払金(かりばらいきん): the advanced money (as a balance-sheet item)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 前払(まえばら)い: advance payment — the amount is already fixed; no later settlement is needed
  • 立(た)て替(か)え: paying on someone's behalf — you use your own money first and are reimbursed
