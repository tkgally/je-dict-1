1. school closure; closing down a school
The permanent closing of a school, usually because enrolment has fallen too low to keep it running. Also used with する as a verb.
来年(らいねん)閉校(へいこう)になる。
It will be closed down next year.
生徒(せいと)が減(へ)り、閉校(へいこう)が決(き)まった。
With the number of students falling, the school's closure was decided.
百年(ひゃくねん)以上(いじょう)の歴史(れきし)を持(も)つ小学校(しょうがっこう)が今年度(こんねんど)末(まつ)で閉校(へいこう)する。
An elementary school with over a hundred years of history will close at the end of this school year.

閉(へい) (close) + 校(こう) (school). Refers to a school shutting down for good, not to the end of a school day.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 閉校(へいこう)する: to close down (of a school)
  • 閉校(へいこう)になる: to be closed down
  • 閉校(へいこう)式(しき): closing ceremony held when a school shuts down
  • 少子化(しょうしか)による閉校(へいこう): closure due to the falling birth rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 廃校(はいこう): closed school — emphasizes the abolished school itself, often the disused building
  • 休校(きゅうこう): temporary closure — classes suspended for snow, illness, etc., with the school still in operation
  • 閉店(へいてん): store closing — the same 閉(へい) used of shops
