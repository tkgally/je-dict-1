腰椎(ようつい)

ようつい
noun
lumbar vertebrae; the lower back bones
1. lumbar vertebrae; lumbar spine
The five vertebrae of the lower back, between the chest vertebrae and the pelvis. Familiar to most people from back pain, disc problems, and compression fractures.
腰椎(ようつい)を骨折(こっせつ)した。
I fractured a lumbar vertebra.
腰椎(ようつい)ヘルニアの手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)けた。
I had surgery for a lumbar disc herniation.
腰(こし)の痛(いた)みが長(なが)く続(つづ)くので、腰椎(ようつい)のMRIを撮(と)ることになった。
Since the lower back pain has continued for a long time, they decided to do an MRI of my lumbar spine.

A medical term, but common in everyday conversation about back trouble because lower-back problems are so widespread.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腰椎(ようつい)ヘルニア: lumbar disc herniation
  • 腰椎(ようつい)圧迫(あっぱく)骨折(こっせつ): lumbar compression fracture
  • 腰椎(ようつい)麻酔(ますい): spinal anesthesia
  • 腰椎(ようつい)を痛(いた)める: to injure one's lumbar spine

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 頚椎(けいつい): cervical vertebrae — the neck section of the same spine
  • 背骨(せぼね): the backbone — the everyday word for the spine as a whole
  • 腰(こし): the lower back, hips — the ordinary word for the body region
