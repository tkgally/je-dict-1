断(だん)じて

だんじて
adverb
absolutely; by no means; without exception
1. absolutely not; by no means (with a negative)
Used with a negative predicate to deny something flatly and with conviction. This is by far the more common pattern.
断(だん)じて許(ゆる)さない。
I will absolutely not allow it.
そんな事実(じじつ)は断(だん)じてない。
There is absolutely no such fact.
私(わたし)は断(だん)じてそのような発言(はつげん)をしておりません。
I have absolutely never made any such statement.
2. absolutely; without fail; come what may (with an affirmative)
Used with an affirmative predicate to declare firm resolve. Formal and emphatic, common in speeches and written declarations.
断(だん)じて実行(じっこう)する。
I will carry it out, no matter what.
断(だん)じてやり遂(と)げる覚悟(かくご)です。
I am resolved to see it through to the end.
断(だん)じて行(おこな)えば鬼神(きしん)もこれを避(さ)ける、という言葉(ことば)がある。
There is a saying that if you act with resolve, even demons will get out of your way.

The te-form of the classical 断(だん)ずる (to decide, to judge), fossilized as an adverb. Formal and emphatic; in casual speech 絶対(ぜったい) does the same work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 断(だん)じて許(ゆる)さない: will absolutely not tolerate
  • 断(だん)じて認(みと)めない: absolutely refuse to acknowledge
  • 断(だん)じてない: absolutely not so
  • 断(だん)じて行(おこな)う: to act with full resolve

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 絶対(ぜったい)に: absolutely — everyday equivalent, works with both polarities
  • 決(けっ)して: never — negative only, neutral in register and more common in writing
  • 断固(だんこ)として: resolutely, firmly — about the manner of acting, usually affirmative
