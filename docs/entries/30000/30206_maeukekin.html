前受金

まえうけきん
noun
advance payment received; deposit taken in advance
1. advance payment received; customer deposit
Money collected from a customer before the goods are delivered or the service is performed. It counts as a liability until the work is done, because the business still owes something in return.
前受金(まえうけきん)を受(う)け取(と)った。
We received an advance payment.
工事(こうじ)の着手前(ちゃくしゅまえ)に前受金(まえうけきん)をいただいています。
We receive an advance payment before construction begins.
前受金(まえうけきん)は、商品(しょうひん)を渡(わた)した時点(じてん)で売上(うりあげ)に振(ふ)り替(か)える。
The advance payment is transferred to sales at the point the goods are handed over.
注文(ちゅうもん)が取(と)り消(け)された場合(ばあい)、前受金(まえうけきん)は全額(ぜんがく)お返(かえ)しします。
If the order is cancelled, we will refund the advance payment in full.

Formed from 前(まえ) (beforehand) + 受(う)け (receiving) + 金(きん) (money). Common in construction, custom manufacturing, and subscription businesses, where payment routinely comes before delivery.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前受金(まえうけきん)を受(う)け取(と)る: to receive an advance payment
  • 前受金(まえうけきん)を返金(へんきん)する: to refund an advance payment
  • 前受金(まえうけきん)として計上(けいじょう)する: to record as an advance received

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 前払金(まえばらいきん): advance paid — the mirror image, money the business pays out early
  • 手付金(てつけきん): deposit — a smaller sum paid to secure a contract, often non-refundable
  • 内金(うちきん): part payment — a portion of the price paid up front
