冒涜的

ぼうとくてき
adjective-na
blasphemous; sacrilegious
1. blasphemous; sacrilegious; profane
Treating something sacred or deeply respected with contempt. Used of religious offence and, by extension, of any act that cheapens something people hold inviolable.
冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)な発言(はつげん)だ。
It is a blasphemous remark.
その絵(え)は冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)だと批判(ひはん)された。
The painting was criticized as sacrilegious.
遺族(いぞく)の気持(きも)ちを考(かんが)えれば、冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)な表現(ひょうげん)は避(さ)けるべきだ。
Out of consideration for the bereaved family, profane expressions should be avoided.

From the noun 冒涜(ぼうとく) (blasphemy, desecration) + the adjectival suffix 的(てき). Written 冒瀆(ぼうとく) in older texts; 涜(とく) is the simplified form in general use today.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)な行為(こうい): a sacrilegious act
  • 冒涜的(ぼうとくてき)な表現(ひょうげん): a profane expression
  • 神(かみ)への冒涜(ぼうとく): blasphemy against God

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不敬(ふけい)な: disrespectful (toward the emperor or a superior) — a narrower, formal register
  • 不謹慎(ふきんしん)な: inappropriate, in poor taste — about tactlessness, not desecration
