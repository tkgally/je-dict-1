北西(ほくせい)

ほくせい
noun
northwest
1. northwest
The compass direction midway between north and west.
冬(ふゆ)は北西(ほくせい)の風(かぜ)が強(つよ)い。
In winter the northwest wind is strong.
町(まち)の北西(ほくせい)に山(やま)が見(み)える。
You can see a mountain to the northwest of the town.
低気圧(ていきあつ)が北西(ほくせい)から近(ちか)づき、夜(よる)には雨(あめ)になる見込(みこ)みだ。
A low-pressure system is approaching from the northwest, and it is expected to rain by night.

Composed of 北(ほく) (north) + 西(せい) (west). One of the four intercardinal directions. In winter, Japan's characteristic 北西(ほくせい) monsoon wind (季節風(きせつふう)) brings cold air and heavy snow to the Sea of Japan coast.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 北西(ほくせい)の風(かぜ): northwest wind
  • 北西(ほくせい)に位置(いち)する: to be located to the northwest
  • 北西(ほくせい)方向(ほうこう): northwest direction
  • 北西部(ほくせいぶ): northwestern part/region

RELATED COMPASS DIRECTIONS:

  • 北東(ほくとう): northeast
  • 南東(なんとう): southeast
  • 南西(なんせい): southwest
