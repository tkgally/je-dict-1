コンバイン

こんばいん
noun
combine harvester
1. combine harvester
A large agricultural machine that reaps a crop, threshes it, and separates the grain from the straw in a single continuous operation. In Japan most commonly used for the rice harvest.
コンバインで稲(いね)を刈(か)る。
to harvest rice with a combine.
秋(あき)になるとコンバインが田(た)んぼを走(はし)り回(まわ)る。
When autumn comes, combines run around the rice paddies.
コンバインの普及(ふきゅう)で稲刈(いねか)りの作業(さぎょう)は大幅(おおはば)に楽(らく)になった。
The spread of combine harvesters has made the work of harvesting rice far easier.

A combine harvester. The name comes from the machine's combining of several harvest steps into one.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • コンバインで収穫(しゅうかく)する: to harvest with a combine
  • コンバインを運転(うんてん)する: to operate a combine
  • 自走式(じそうしき)コンバイン: a self-propelled combine

NOTE:
In Japanese agriculture コンバイン usually refers to the machine used for the rice (稲(いね)) harvest.
