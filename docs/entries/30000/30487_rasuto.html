ラスト

らすと
noun
last; final; the ending
1. the last one; the final part; the ending
From English 'last.' Used for the closing part of a film, song, game, or series, and attributively for the final item in a sequence.
ラストの一曲(いっきょく)です。
This is the last song.
この映画(えいが)のラストが忘(わす)れられない。
I can't forget the ending of this film.
ラストシーンで主人公(しゅじんこう)が何(なに)も言(い)わずに去(さ)っていくところが印象的(いんしょうてき)だった。
The scene where the protagonist leaves without saying anything was striking.

A loanword from English 'last,' but narrower in use: it names the final part of something, and does not mean 'previous' as English 'last week' does.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ラストシーン: the final scene
  • ラストスパート: the final push, last spurt
  • ラストオーダー: last order (in a restaurant or bar)
  • ラスト一(いち)分(ぷん): the final minute

COMMON MISTAKE: 'last week' is 先週(せんしゅう), not ラスト週(しゅう). For 'the previous ~,' use 前(まえ)の or 先(せん)~.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最後(さいご): the last, the end — the ordinary native word, usable in any register
  • 最終(さいしゅう): final — formal, used in compounds like 最終回(さいしゅうかい)
