ぽつりぽつり

ぽつりぽつり
adverb
in scattered drops; one by one; haltingly
1. in scattered drops; one by one; here and there
Describes things falling or appearing singly at intervals — the first drops of rain, people arriving a few at a time, lights dotted across a dark landscape.
雨(あめ)がぽつりぽつりと降(ふ)り出(だ)した。
The rain began to fall in scattered drops.
客(きゃく)がぽつりぽつりと入(はい)ってきた。
Customers came in a few at a time.
山道(やまみち)を下(くだ)ると、谷(たに)の向(む)こうに家(いえ)の明(あ)かりがぽつりぽつりと見(み)えた。
As we came down the mountain road, house lights were dotted here and there across the valley.
2. haltingly; bit by bit (of speaking)
Describes speech that comes out slowly in short bursts with pauses between, typically because the speaker is reluctant, tired, or choosing words with care.
彼(かれ)はぽつりぽつりと話(はな)し始(はじ)めた。
He began to speak haltingly.
母(はは)は昔(むかし)のことをぽつりぽつりと語(かた)った。
My mother spoke of the past a little at a time.
長(なが)い沈黙(ちんもく)のあと、彼女(かのじょ)はぽつりぽつりと当時(とうじ)の気持(きも)ちを説明(せつめい)してくれた。
After a long silence, she explained how she had felt at the time, a few words at a time.

A mimetic adverb, almost always used with と. It is a variant of ぽつぽつ built on ぽつり, and carries a slower, more spaced-out feeling: each drop, person, or phrase is separated by a noticeable gap.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぽつりぽつりと降(ふ)る: to fall in scattered drops
  • ぽつりぽつりと話(はな)す: to talk haltingly
  • ぽつりぽつりと集(あつ)まる: to gather a few at a time

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぽつぽつ: bit by bit; in drops — the same range of meaning, but faster and more continuous
  • ちらほら: here and there — about scattered numbers only, never about speech
