嬉(うれ)しさ

うれしさ
noun
happiness; gladness; delight
1. happiness; gladness; how glad one feels
The noun form of 嬉(うれ)しい. It names the gladness felt at a particular good thing, and often its intensity, rather than happiness as a state of life.
合格(ごうかく)した嬉(うれ)しさで泣(な)いた。
I cried from the joy of passing.
久(ひさ)しぶりに会(あ)えた嬉(うれ)しさは忘(わす)れられない。
I can't forget how glad I was to meet them after so long.
初(はじ)めて自分(じぶん)の作品(さくひん)をほめられた嬉(うれ)しさは、言葉(ことば)では表(あらわ)せない。
The gladness of having my own work praised for the first time cannot be put into words.

Formed by replacing the final い of 嬉(うれ)しい with さ, the regular way to make a noun of degree from an i-adjective.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嬉(うれ)しさのあまり: out of sheer joy
  • 嬉(うれ)しさがこみ上(あ)げる: gladness wells up
  • 嬉(うれ)しさを隠(かく)せない: to be unable to hide one's delight
  • 嬉(うれ)しさ半分(はんぶん): half glad (and half something else)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 喜(よろこ)び: joy — larger and more lasting, and usable of an occasion as well as a feeling
  • 楽(たの)しさ: fun, enjoyment — pleasure taken in an activity while it lasts

Related Words

Related: 嬉(うれ)しい (the adjective this noun is formed from)
