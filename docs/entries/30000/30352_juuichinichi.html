十一日(じゅういちにち)

じゅういちにち
noun
the 11th of the month; eleven days
1. the eleventh day of the month
The calendar date. From the eleventh onward the dates are read regularly with にち, unlike the irregular readings for the first ten days.
十一日(じゅういちにち)に会(あ)おう。
Let's meet on the eleventh.
三月(さんがつ)十一日(じゅういちにち)は忘(わす)れられない日(ひ)だ。
March 11th is an unforgettable day.
来月(らいげつ)の十一日(じゅういちにち)までに書類(しょるい)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit the documents by the eleventh of next month.
2. eleven days
A span of eleven days. Which sense is meant is clear from context, and 〜間(かん) can be added to force the duration reading.
十一日(じゅういちにち)かかった。
It took eleven days.
入院(にゅういん)は十一日間(じゅういちにちかん)だった。
The hospital stay was eleven days.
工事(こうじ)は予定(よてい)より早(はや)く、十一日(じゅういちにち)で終(お)わった。
The construction finished in eleven days, earlier than planned.

The date readings are irregular through 十日(とおか) (the tenth), then become regular: 十一日(じゅういちにち), 十二日(じゅうににち), 十三日(じゅうさんにち). Only 十四日(じゅうよっか), 二十日(はつか), and 二十四日(にじゅうよっか) break the pattern again.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 十一日(じゅういちにち)に: on the eleventh
  • 十一日間(じゅういちにちかん): for eleven days
  • 十一日(じゅういちにち)までに: by the eleventh

TELLING THE SENSES APART:
A month name before it forces the date reading (四月(しがつ)十一日(じゅういちにち)); 〜間(かん) or a verb of duration forces the span reading.
