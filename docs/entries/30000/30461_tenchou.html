店長(てんちょう)

てんちょう
noun
store manager; shop manager
1. store manager; shop manager
The person in charge of a single shop or restaurant — responsible for staff, stock, and daily operations. Used both as a job title and as a term of address.
店長(てんちょう)を呼(よ)んでください。
Please call the manager.
新(あたら)しい店長(てんちょう)はとても優(やさ)しい。
The new store manager is very kind.
三年(さんねん)アルバイトを続(つづ)けたあと、彼(かれ)はその店(みせ)の店長(てんちょう)になった。
After working part-time for three years, he became the manager of that shop.

Composed of 店(てん) (shop) + 長(ちょう) (head, chief). Scope is one location: a manager over several shops is an エリアマネージャー(えりあまねーじゃー) or 統括(とうかつ)店長(てんちょう).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 店長(てんちょう)になる: to become a store manager
  • 店長(てんちょう)を呼(よ)ぶ: to call the manager
  • 副(ふく)店長(てんちょう): assistant manager
  • 店長(てんちょう)代理(だいり): acting manager

USAGE:
Like other 長(ちょう) titles, 店長(てんちょう) is used directly as a form of address by staff — 店長(てんちょう)、お客様(きゃくさま)がお見(み)えです — with no name and no さん needed.
