1. expense reimbursement; expense settlement
The office procedure of submitting receipts for money spent on company business and being paid back for it. A routine monthly task in Japanese workplaces.
経費精算(けいひせいさん)を忘(わす)れていた。
I forgot to file my expense claim.
出張(しゅっちょう)の領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)をまとめて経費精算(けいひせいさん)した。
I gathered the receipts from my business trip and filed the expense claim.
経費精算(けいひせいさん)の締(し)め切(き)りは毎月(まいつき)五日(いつか)です。
The deadline for expense claims is the fifth of every month.
紙(かみ)の申請書(しんせいしょ)をやめてから、経費精算(けいひせいさん)がずいぶん楽(らく)になった。
Since we stopped using paper forms, expense reimbursement has become much easier.

Formed from 経費(けいひ) (business expenses) + 精算(せいさん) (settling up). Takes する. Note the kanji: 精算(せいさん) means settling an amount exactly, while the homophone 清算(せいさん) means liquidating or winding up.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経費精算(けいひせいさん)をする: to file an expense claim
  • 経費精算(けいひせいさん)システム: expense reimbursement system
  • 経費(けいひ)で落(お)とす: to put something on the company's expenses

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 立替(たてか)え: paying on someone's behalf — the outlay that the reimbursement repays
  • 仮払(かりばら)い: cash advance — money paid out before the expense is incurred
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ): receipt — the document a claim normally requires
