牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)

ぼたんなべ
noun
wild boar hot pot
1. wild boar hot pot; boar meat simmered in a pot
A winter 鍋(なべ) dish of thinly sliced wild boar meat simmered with vegetables in a miso or soy-based broth. A regional speciality of mountain areas, especially 丹波(たんば) in Hyogo Prefecture.
冬(ふゆ)は牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)が楽(たの)しみだ。
In winter I look forward to wild boar hot pot.
山(やま)の宿(やど)で牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)を食(た)べた。
We ate wild boar hot pot at an inn in the mountains.
味噌(みそ)仕立(じた)ての牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)は猪(いのしし)肉(にく)の臭(くさ)みが消(き)えて食(た)べやすい。
Miso-based wild boar hot pot loses the gaminess of the boar meat and is easy to eat.

Named for the way the sliced boar meat is arranged on the plate like the petals of a 牡丹(ぼたん) (peony). Part of an old set of flower names for game meat, used when eating meat was officially discouraged.

GAME-MEAT FLOWER NAMES:

  • 牡丹(ぼたん): wild boar (猪(いのしし))
  • もみじ: deer (鹿(しか))
  • さくら: horse (馬(うま))

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)を食(た)べる: to eat wild boar hot pot
  • 味噌(みそ)仕立(じた)ての牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ): miso-based boar hot pot
  • 牡丹鍋(ぼたんなべ)の季節(きせつ): boar hot pot season

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • しし鍋(なべ): boar hot pot — the plainer name for the same dish
  • もみじ鍋(なべ): venison hot pot — the deer counterpart
