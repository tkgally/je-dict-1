ちらほら

ちらほら
adverb
here and there; sparsely; now and then; in small numbers
1. here and there; sparsely; now and then; in small numbers
Describes things appearing in small, scattered numbers rather than all at once — a few flowers open, a few people visible, a rumor heard occasionally. Often marks the very beginning of something that will later become general.
桜(さくら)がちらほら咲(さ)き始(はじ)めた。
The cherry blossoms have started to open here and there.
そんなうわさがちらほら聞(き)こえてくる。
You hear that rumor now and then.
朝早(あさはや)い時間(じかん)なので、公園(こうえん)には人(ひと)がちらほら見(み)えるだけだった。
Since it was early in the morning, only a few people could be seen here and there in the park.

A mimetic adverb, often used with と (ちらほらと). It suggests small numbers scattered across space or time, and frequently signals an early stage: a few blossoms, a few early arrivals, the first stray comments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ちらほら見(み)える: to be visible here and there
  • ちらほら咲(さ)く: to bloom sparsely
  • ちらほら聞(き)こえる: to be heard now and then
  • ちらほら集(あつ)まる: to gather in ones and twos

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • まばら: sparse — describes a state, and can modify nouns (まばらな人影(ひとかげ))
  • ぽつぽつ: bit by bit; in drops — overlaps closely, but is also used for rain and for speaking haltingly
