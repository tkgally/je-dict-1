格子縞(こうしじま)

こうしじま
noun
check pattern; plaid; tartan
1. check pattern; checked cloth; plaid
A pattern of vertical and horizontal stripes crossing to form squares, and by extension cloth woven in that pattern. The traditional Japanese name for what is now usually called チェック.
格子縞(こうしじま)のシャツを着(き)ている。
He is wearing a checked shirt.
祖母(そぼ)は格子縞(こうしじま)の着物(きもの)を大切(たいせつ)にしていた。
My grandmother treasured a checked kimono.
赤(あか)と黒(くろ)の格子縞(こうしじま)は、遠(とお)くから見(み)ても目立(めだ)つ柄(がら)だ。
A red and black check is a pattern that stands out even from a distance.

格子(こうし) is a lattice or grid; 縞(しま) is a stripe. Often shortened to 格子(こうし) when describing a pattern.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 格子縞(こうしじま)の布(ぬの): checked cloth
  • 格子縞(こうしじま)の柄(がら): a check pattern
  • 細(こま)かい格子縞(こうしじま): a fine check

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • チェック: check — the everyday loanword; far more common in clothing shops today
  • 縞模様(しまもよう): stripes — parallel lines only, with no crossing
  • 市松模様(いちまつもよう): checkerboard pattern — alternating solid squares rather than crossing stripes
