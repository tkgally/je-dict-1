どころか

どころか
particle
far from; on the contrary; let alone
1. far from ~; on the contrary; not only not ~
Denies the preceding statement and replaces it with something at the opposite extreme: not merely "not X" but in fact the reverse. Attaches to plain-form verbs and adjectives and to bare nouns.
寒(さむ)いどころか、暑(あつ)いくらいだ。
Far from being cold, it's almost hot.
謝(あやま)るどころか、彼(かれ)は怒(おこ)り出(だ)した。
Far from apologizing, he got angry.
薬(くすり)を飲(の)んだが、良(よ)くなるどころか熱(ねつ)はさらに上(あ)がってしまった。
I took the medicine, but far from getting better, my fever went even higher.
2. let alone ~; to say nothing of ~
Used with a negative predicate to say that even a modest case does not hold, so a greater one certainly does not. The item after どころか is the smaller or easier case.
漢字(かんじ)どころか、ひらがなも読(よ)めない。
Let alone kanji, I can't even read hiragana.
海外(かいがい)旅行(りょこう)どころか、近(ちか)くの温泉(おんせん)にも行(い)く時間(じかん)がない。
To say nothing of a trip abroad, I don't even have time to visit a nearby hot spring.
忙(いそが)しくて、昼(ひる)ごはんどころか水(みず)を飲(の)む時間(じかん)さえなかった。
I was so busy that, let alone lunch, I didn't even have time to drink water.

CONNECTION:

  • Nouns attach directly: 旅行(りょこう)どころか
  • Verbs and i-adjectives take the plain form: 行(い)くどころか、寒(さむ)いどころか
  • Na-adjectives take な or the bare stem: 静(しず)かどころか

USAGE:
Sense 1 corrects an expectation and is usually followed by a stronger positive statement; sense 2 leads to a negative predicate such as ない or できない. The reversal is often reinforced by むしろ or さえ.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • はもちろん: to say nothing of ~ — adds a case to an affirmative statement, whereas どころか denies
  • ではなく: not ~ but — a plain correction, without the sense of overturning an expectation
  • おろか: let alone (literary) — close to sense 2 but distinctly formal
