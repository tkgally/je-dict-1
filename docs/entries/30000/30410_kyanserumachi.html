キャンセル待(ま)ち

きゃんせるまち
noun
waitlist; waiting for a cancellation; standby
1. waitlist; standby; waiting for a cancellation
Putting your name down for a fully booked flight, restaurant, class, or hotel in the hope that someone else cancels. Also refers to the waiting list itself.
キャンセル待(ま)ちで予約(よやく)した。
I booked it on the waitlist.
満席(まんせき)なので、キャンセル待(ま)ちに名前(なまえ)を入(い)れておいた。
It was fully booked, so I put my name on the waitlist.
人気(にんき)の宿(やど)なので、キャンセル待(ま)ちをしていたら出発(しゅっぱつ)の前日(ぜんじつ)に部屋(へや)が取(と)れた。
It's a popular inn, so I waited for a cancellation and got a room the day before departure.

Used with する: キャンセル待(ま)ちをする (to wait for a cancellation). Common in travel, restaurant booking, concert tickets, and popular lessons or childcare places.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • キャンセル待(ま)ちをする: to wait for a cancellation
  • キャンセル待(ま)ちに入(はい)る: to get on the waitlist
  • キャンセル待(ま)ちが出(で)る: a cancellation slot opens up
  • キャンセル待(ま)ちリスト: the waiting list

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 順番待(じゅんばんま)ち: waiting one's turn — waiting in an ordinary queue, not for a cancellation
  • 空(あ)き待(ま)ち: waiting for an opening — broader, includes waiting for capacity to be added
  • 予約(よやく): reservation — the confirmed booking that a waitlist is not yet
