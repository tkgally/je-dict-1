優良(ゆうりょう)

ゆうりょう
noun
superior; high-quality; excellent
1. superior quality; excellent; first-rate
Rated as clearly above standard. Applied to goods, companies, properties, borrowers, and school records — typically in official or commercial assessments rather than casual praise.
優良(ゆうりょう)な商品(しょうひん)を選(えら)ぶ。
To choose high-quality products.
この会社(かいしゃ)は優良(ゆうりょう)企業(きぎょう)として知(し)られている。
This company is known as a blue-chip firm.
駅(えき)から近(ちか)く日当(ひあ)たりもよい優良(ゆうりょう)物件(ぶっけん)なので、すぐに買(か)い手(て)がついた。
It was a prime property, close to the station and with good sunlight, so a buyer turned up right away.

Used mostly as a prefix-like modifier in compounds; the standalone な form is possible but less common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 優良(ゆうりょう)企業(きぎょう): blue-chip company
  • 優良(ゆうりょう)物件(ぶっけん): prime property
  • 優良(ゆうりょう)顧客(こきゃく): valued customer
  • 優良(ゆうりょう)運転者(うんてんしゃ): driver with a clean record

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう): excellent — used of people's ability and performance; 優良(ゆうりょう) is used of things and organizations
  • 良質(りょうしつ): good quality — about the material or content itself, without the official-rating flavor
