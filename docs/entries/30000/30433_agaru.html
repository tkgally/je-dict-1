1. to be brought up; to be cited; to be named
Said of names, examples, opinions, or candidates that come up in a discussion. This kanji is the one used when something is put forward for consideration rather than physically rising.
名前(なまえ)が挙(あ)がった。
His name came up.
会議(かいぎ)でいくつか意見(いけん)が挙(あ)がった。
Several opinions were raised at the meeting.
候補(こうほ)として三人(さんにん)の名前(なまえ)が挙(あ)がっているが、まだ決(き)まっていない。
Three names have come up as candidates, but nothing has been decided yet.
2. to be produced; to be obtained (of results or evidence)
Said of results, profits, or evidence that come to hand as the product of effort or investigation.
成果(せいか)が挙(あ)がった。
Results were achieved.
捜査(そうさ)で新(あたら)しい証拠(しょうこ)が挙(あ)がった。
New evidence came to light in the investigation.
時間(じかん)をかけた割(わり)に成果(せいか)が挙(あ)がらず、方法(ほうほう)を見直(みなお)すことにした。
Little came of it despite the time spent, so we decided to review our approach.

One of several kanji written for あがる. 挙(あ)がる is the transitive 挙(あ)げる's intransitive partner, used where something is put forward or produced.

CHOOSING THE KANJI:

  • 上(あ)がる: to go up — the general word for rising
  • 挙(あ)がる: to be cited, to be produced — names, examples, results, evidence
  • 揚(あ)がる: to be deep-fried; to be hoisted

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 名前(なまえ)が挙(あ)がる: a name comes up
  • 例(れい)が挙(あ)がる: an example is given
  • 成果(せいか)が挙(あ)がる: results are achieved
  • 証拠(しょうこ)が挙(あ)がる: evidence is obtained

TRANSITIVITY:
Intransitive; the thing raised is marked with が. The transitive partner is 挙(あ)げる (例(れい)を挙(あ)げる, to give an example).
