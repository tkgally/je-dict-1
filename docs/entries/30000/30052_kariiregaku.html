借入額(かりいれがく)

かりいれがく
noun
amount borrowed; loan amount
1. amount borrowed; loan amount
The sum of money that has been borrowed, for example under a loan or mortgage. Distinct from the interest or the amount already repaid.
借入額(かりいれがく)を減(へ)らす。
Reduce the amount borrowed.
毎月(まいつき)の返済(へんさい)で借入額(かりいれがく)が減(へ)っていく。
With each month's repayment, the amount owed goes down.
住宅(じゅうたく)ローンの借入額(かりいれがく)は年収(ねんしゅう)の五倍(ごばい)までが目安(めやす)とされる。
For a home loan, the borrowed amount is generally guided to be up to five times one's annual income.

Combines 借入(かりいれ) (borrowing) with 額(がく) (amount of money). Common in banking, loans, and mortgages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 借入額(かりいれがく)が大(おお)きい: the loan amount is large
  • 借入額(かりいれがく)を増(ふ)やす: to increase the amount borrowed
  • 借入額(かりいれがく)の上限(じょうげん): a borrowing limit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 返済額(へんさいがく): repayment amount — the sum being paid back
  • 残高(ざんだか): balance — the amount still outstanding
