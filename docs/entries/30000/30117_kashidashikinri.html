貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)

かしだしきんり
noun
lending rate; loan interest rate
1. lending rate; interest rate on loans
The rate of interest a bank charges when it lends money. Contrasted with the deposit rate a bank pays to savers; the gap between them is the bank's margin.
貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)が上(あ)がった。
Lending rates have risen.
銀行(ぎんこう)によって貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)が違(ちが)うので、比(くら)べたほうがいい。
Lending rates differ by bank, so it's better to compare.
日銀(にちぎん)が政策(せいさく)金利(きんり)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げれば、各行(かくこう)の貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)にも影響(えいきょう)が出(で)る。
If the Bank of Japan raises its policy rate, bank lending rates will be affected too.

A standard term in banking and financial news.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)が上(あ)がる／下(さ)がる: lending rates rise / fall
  • 貸出(かしだし)金利(きんり)を引(ひ)き上(あ)げる: to raise the lending rate
  • 低(てい)金利(きんり): low interest rates

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 預金(よきん)金利(きんり): deposit rate — what the bank pays you, the mirror image of this term
  • 政策(せいさく)金利(きんり): policy rate — the central bank's benchmark, which drives lending rates
  • 利息(りそく): interest — the money itself, not the rate
