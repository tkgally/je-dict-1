投資話(とうしばなし)

とうしばなし
noun
investment offer; investment pitch
1. investment offer; investment pitch
A proposal that someone put money into a venture, fund, or scheme. Usually said of an offer brought to you by another person, and very often in warnings about fraud.
うまい投資話(とうしばなし)には気(き)をつけよう。
Be careful of investment offers that sound too good.
知人(ちじん)から投資話(とうしばなし)を持(も)ちかけられた。
An acquaintance approached me with an investment offer.
必(かなら)ず儲(もう)かるという投資話(とうしばなし)は、たいてい詐欺(さぎ)だと思(おも)ったほうがいい。
You should assume that an investment offer promising guaranteed profits is usually a scam.

投資(とうし) (investment) + 話(はなし) (talk, proposal), with rendaku turning はなし into ばなし. The same pattern gives 儲(もう)け話(ばなし) (a money-making proposition) and 昔話(むかしばなし) (an old tale).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 投資話(とうしばなし)を持(も)ちかける: to approach someone with an investment offer
  • うまい投資話(とうしばなし): an investment offer that sounds too good to be true
  • 投資話(とうしばなし)に乗(の)る: to go along with an investment offer
  • 投資話(とうしばなし)を断(ことわ)る: to turn down an investment offer

REGISTER:
The word carries a faint smell of risk. A legitimate financial firm would speak of 投資(とうし)の提案(ていあん) or 運用(うんよう)の相談(そうだん); 投資話(とうしばなし) suggests something informal, often brought by an acquaintance, and is the standard word in news reports about investment fraud.
