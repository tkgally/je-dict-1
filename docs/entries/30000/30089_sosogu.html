1. to wipe out (a disgrace); to avenge; to clear (one's name)
To remove or make up for a disgrace, dishonor, or humiliating defeat, typically by achieving a later success or victory. A formal, somewhat literary word, most often used with objects such as 恥(はじ) (shame) or 汚名(おめい) (a bad name).
恥(はじ)を雪(そそ)ぐ。
to wipe out a disgrace.
前回(ぜんかい)の負(ま)けを雪(そそ)いだ。
I avenged the previous loss.
汚名(おめい)を雪(そそ)ぐために、彼(かれ)はもう一度(いちど)挑戦(ちょうせん)することを決(き)めた。
In order to clear his tarnished name, he decided to take on the challenge once more.

A formal, literary verb meaning to erase a disgrace by a subsequent achievement.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive; the thing wiped out (a disgrace, defeat, or bad reputation) takes を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 恥(はじ)を雪(そそ)ぐ: to wipe out one's shame
  • 汚名(おめい)を雪(そそ)ぐ: to clear one's tarnished name
  • 雪辱(せつじょく)を果(は)たす: to achieve one's revenge (the related noun 雪辱(せつじょく) is far more common in everyday speech)

USAGE:
The kanji 雪(そそ)ぐ is also read すすぐ, which more usually means "to rinse" (also written 濯(すす)ぐ). In the "wipe out a disgrace" sense, the reading そそぐ is standard. Note this word is distinct from 注(そそ)ぐ (to pour), a homophone.
