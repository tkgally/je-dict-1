下落幅

げらくはば
noun
extent of a decline; size of a drop
1. extent of a decline; size of a drop; margin of decrease
How far a price, index, or value has fallen, expressed as an amount or percentage. Standard vocabulary in market and economic reporting.
下落幅(げらくはば)が大(おお)きい。
The decline is steep.
午後(ごご)になって株価(かぶか)の下落幅(げらくはば)が縮(ちぢ)まった。
In the afternoon the stock's decline narrowed.
今回(こんかい)の下落幅(げらくはば)は、過去(かこ)十年(じゅうねん)で最(もっと)も大(おお)きいという。
This decline is said to be the largest in the past ten years.

Formed from 下落(げらく) (a fall in price or value) plus 幅(はば) (width, range). 幅(はば) here means the size of the movement, the same use as in 値幅(ねはば) (price range) and 上昇幅(じょうしょうはば) (extent of a rise).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 下落幅(げらくはば)が大(おお)きい: for the decline to be large
  • 下落幅(げらくはば)が縮(ちぢ)まる: for the decline to narrow
  • 下落幅(げらくはば)を広(ひろ)げる: to extend its losses

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上昇幅(じょうしょうはば): extent of a rise — the direct opposite
  • 下落率(げらくりつ): rate of decline — expressed as a percentage rather than an amount
