おしとやか

おしとやか
na-adjective
graceful; demure; ladylike
1. graceful; demure; refined in manner
Quiet, composed, and gentle in speech and movement. Traditionally used to praise women, so in modern usage it can sound old-fashioned or, said of someone acting out of character, faintly teasing.
おしとやかな人(ひと)ですね。
She is a graceful person, isn't she.
妹(いもうと)は人前(ひとまえ)ではおしとやかに振(ふ)る舞(ま)う。
My younger sister behaves demurely in front of others.
着物(きもの)を着(き)ると、いつもより少(すこ)しおしとやかな気分(きぶん)になる。
When I wear a kimono, I feel a little more demure than usual.

The polite prefix お attached to 淑(しと)やか; both forms exist, but おしとやか is the commoner one in speech and is written in kana.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おしとやかな女性(じょせい): a graceful woman
  • おしとやかに振(ふ)る舞(ま)う: to behave demurely
  • おしとやかにする: to act like a lady

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上品(じょうひん): refined, elegant — about taste and class, and applies to objects as well as people
  • 控(ひか)えめ: modest, reserved — about holding back rather than about grace of manner
  • 大人(おとな)しい: quiet, well-behaved — neutral about elegance, and freely used of men and children
