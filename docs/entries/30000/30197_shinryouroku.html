診療録

しんりょうろく
noun
medical record; patient chart
1. medical record; patient chart; clinical record
The record a doctor is required to keep of a patient's examinations, diagnoses, and treatment. This is the legal and administrative term; in day-to-day hospital speech the same thing is called カルテ.
診療録(しんりょうろく)に記入(きにゅう)する。
The doctor writes it in the medical record.
診療録(しんりょうろく)は五年間(ごねんかん)保存(ほぞん)することが義務(ぎむ)づけられている。
Medical records are required by law to be kept for five years.
患者(かんじゃ)は自分(じぶん)の診療録(しんりょうろく)の開示(かいじ)を求(もと)めることができる。
Patients can request disclosure of their own medical records.
電子(でんし)診療録(しんりょうろく)の導入(どうにゅう)で、病院(びょういん)間(かん)の情報(じょうほう)のやり取(と)りが楽(らく)になった。
The introduction of electronic medical records has made exchanging information between hospitals easier.

Composed of 診療(しんりょう) (medical examination and treatment) plus 録(ろく) (record). The suffix 録(ろく) names formal written records generally: 議事録(ぎじろく) (minutes), 記録(きろく) (a record).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 診療録(しんりょうろく)を作成(さくせい)する: to create a medical record
  • 診療録(しんりょうろく)の開示(かいじ): disclosure of medical records
  • 電子(でんし)診療録(しんりょうろく): electronic medical record

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • カルテ: chart — from German Karte; the everyday word inside hospitals
  • 病歴(びょうれき): medical history — the patient's past illnesses, not the document itself
