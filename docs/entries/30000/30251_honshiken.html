本試験(ほんしけん)

ほんしけん
noun
the actual exam; the main examination
1. the actual exam; the main examination; the real test
The official examination itself, as opposed to a practice test, a mock exam, or a makeup sitting. The 本(ほん) marks it as the genuine occasion on which the result counts.
本試験(ほんしけん)は来月(らいげつ)だ。
The actual exam is next month.
模擬(もぎ)試験(しけん)より本試験(ほんしけん)のほうが難(むずか)しかった。
The actual exam was harder than the mock exam.
本試験(ほんしけん)を受(う)けられなかった学生(がくせい)は、追試(ついし)を受(う)けることになっている。
Students who could not sit the actual exam are to take a makeup test.

本(ほん) (main, actual, this very) + 試験(しけん) (examination). The same 本(ほん) appears in 本番(ほんばん) (the real performance) and 本大会(ほんたいかい) (the main tournament).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 本試験(ほんしけん)を受(う)ける: to sit the actual exam
  • 本試験(ほんしけん)に向(む)けて: in preparation for the actual exam
  • 本試験(ほんしけん)当日(とうじつ): the day of the exam

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追試(ついし): makeup exam — taken later by those who missed or failed the main one
  • 模擬試験(もぎしけん): mock exam — practice under exam conditions, results do not count
  • 本番(ほんばん): the real thing — broader, used of performances and live broadcasts as well as tests
