予備費(よびひ)

よびひ
noun
contingency fund; reserve funds; contingency allowance
1. contingency fund; reserve funds; contingency allowance
Money set aside in a budget for unforeseen expenses. Used of national and local government budgets, company budgets, and the buffer built into a project's costs.
予備費(よびひ)を計上(けいじょう)する。
To set aside a contingency fund.
災害(さいがい)対応(たいおう)に予備費(よびひ)を使(つか)うことになった。
It was decided to use the contingency fund for disaster response.
工事(こうじ)の予算(よさん)には、想定外(そうていがい)の支出(ししゅつ)に備(そな)えた予備費(よびひ)が含(ふく)まれている。
The construction budget includes a contingency fund for unexpected expenses.

予備(よび) (spare, reserve) + 費(ひ) (expense). Common in news coverage of government budgets, where the size of the 予備費(よびひ) is often debated.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予備費(よびひ)を計上(けいじょう)する: to include a contingency in the budget
  • 予備費(よびひ)を取(と)り崩(くず)す: to draw on the contingency fund
  • 予備費(よびひ)から支出(ししゅつ)する: to pay out of the contingency fund

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予算(よさん): budget — the overall plan; 予備費(よびひ) is one line within it
  • 積立金(つみたてきん): reserve fund — money accumulated over time for a known future purpose
