後(ご)

ご
suffix
after ~; following ~
1. after ~; post-~; following ~
A suffix attached to a Sino-Japanese noun to mean "after that event": 退職後(たいしょくご) (after retirement), 卒業後(そつぎょうご) (after graduation). Written-style and very productive.
卒業後(そつぎょうご)に就職(しゅうしょく)した。
I got a job after graduating.
手術後(しゅじゅつご)は安静(あんせい)にしてください。
Please rest quietly after the operation.
退職後(たいしょくご)の生活(せいかつ)をどう過(す)ごすか、今(いま)のうちから考(かんが)えておいたほうがいい。
It is better to start thinking now about how to spend life after retirement.

The on'yomi of 後(うし)ろ・後(あと), used only as a bound suffix. It attaches directly to a Sino-Japanese noun with no particle, and the result behaves as a time noun.

COMMON EXAMPLES:

  • 卒業後(そつぎょうご): after graduation
  • 退職後(たいしょくご): after retirement
  • 食後(しょくご): after a meal
  • 戦後(せんご): postwar
  • 使用後(しようご): after use

USAGE:
It cannot be attached to native Japanese nouns — say 試合(しあい)の後(あと) rather than combining 後(ご) with a wago word.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 後(あと): after — the free-standing word, used with の after any noun
  • 前(ぜん): pre-, before — the matching suffix, as in 食前(しょくぜん) (before a meal)
