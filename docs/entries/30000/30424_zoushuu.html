増収(ぞうしゅう)

ぞうしゅう
noun
increased revenue; higher income; increased yield
1. increased revenue; increase in income or takings
A rise in the money coming in — a company's sales, a government's tax receipts, or a household's income. Standard vocabulary in business reporting.
今期(こんき)は増収(ぞうしゅう)だった。
Revenue rose this term.
新製品(しんせいひん)が増収(ぞうしゅう)につながった。
The new product led to higher revenue.
値上(ねあ)げの影響(えいきょう)で、今年度(こんねんど)は増収(ぞうしゅう)増益(ぞうえき)となる見通(みとお)しだ。
Thanks to the price increase, the company expects both higher revenue and higher profit this fiscal year.
2. increased yield; larger harvest
A rise in the amount produced, especially of a crop. Used in farming and industry contexts.
米(こめ)が増収(ぞうしゅう)になった。
The rice harvest increased.
天候(てんこう)に恵(めぐ)まれて増収(ぞうしゅう)が見込(みこ)まれる。
With favorable weather, a larger harvest is expected.
新(あたら)しい品種(ひんしゅ)を導入(どうにゅう)したことで、同(おな)じ面積(めんせき)から大幅(おおはば)な増収(ぞうしゅう)が実現(じつげん)した。
By introducing a new variety, they achieved a much larger yield from the same area of land.

From 増(ぞう) (increase) + 収(しゅう) (income, receipts). Used as a noun and with なる or となる; 増収(ぞうしゅう)する is not standard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 増収(ぞうしゅう)増益(ぞうえき): higher revenue and higher profit (a set phrase in earnings reports)
  • 増収(ぞうしゅう)となる: to end up with increased revenue
  • 大幅(おおはば)な増収(ぞうしゅう): a sharp rise in revenue
  • 税(ぜい)収(しゅう)の増収(ぞうしゅう): an increase in tax revenue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 減収(げんしゅう): decreased revenue — the direct opposite
  • 増益(ぞうえき): increased profit — profit after costs, not the money taken in
