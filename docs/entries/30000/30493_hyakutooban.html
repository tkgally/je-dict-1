1. 110; Japan's police emergency number; a call to the police
The three-digit number dialled to reach the police in an emergency. Also used with する to mean 'to call the police.' Normally written 110番(ばん) in figures.
すぐに百十番(ひゃくとおばん)してください。
Please call the police right away.
事故(じこ)を見(み)て百十番(ひゃくとおばん)に通報(つうほう)した。
I saw the accident and reported it to the police.
緊急(きんきゅう)でない相談(そうだん)は、百十番(ひゃくとおばん)ではなく警察相談(けいさつそうだん)ダイヤルにかける。
For non-emergency matters you call the police consultation line rather than 110.

Written 110番(ばん) in ordinary text; the kanji spelling 百十番(ひゃくとおばん) appears mainly when the reading has to be shown. Note that 十(とお) is read とお here, not じゅう.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 百十番(ひゃくとおばん)する: to call the police
  • 百十番(ひゃくとおばん)通報(つうほう): a 110 call, an emergency report to police
  • 百十番(ひゃくとおばん)にかける: to dial 110

OTHER EMERGENCY NUMBERS:

  • 119番(ばん): fire and ambulance
  • 118番(ばん): coast guard (maritime emergencies)
