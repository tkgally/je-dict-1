熱性(ねっせい)

ねっせい
noun
febrile; fever-related
1. febrile; caused by or accompanied by fever
A medical term used before another noun to mean that a condition arises from or comes with a fever. It appears almost only in compounds, most commonly 熱性(ねっせい)けいれん (febrile convulsion).
熱性(ねっせい)けいれんを起(お)こした。
The child had a febrile convulsion.
熱性(ねっせい)けいれんは幼(おさな)い子(こ)どもに多(おお)い。
Febrile convulsions are common in young children.
医師(いし)から熱性(ねっせい)疾患(しっかん)の可能性(かのうせい)があると説明(せつめい)を受(う)けた。
The doctor explained that it might be a febrile illness.

From 熱(ねつ) (fever, heat) + 性(せい) (nature, -ic). Note the small っ: the reading is ねっせい, not ねつせい.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 熱性(ねっせい)けいれん: febrile convulsion
  • 熱性(ねっせい)疾患(しっかん): febrile illness
  • 熱性(ねっせい)発疹(ほっしん): fever rash

USAGE:
A bound form: it does not stand alone as a subject or object, and it is not used with な or の. Outside medicine it is rare, so learners will meet it mainly at a clinic or in health information for parents.
