学習会

がくしゅうかい
noun
study group; study session; workshop
1. study group; study session; workshop
A gathering where people study or learn about a topic together, such as a class, seminar, or reading circle.
学習会(がくしゅうかい)に参加(さんか)する。
Take part in a study group.
毎週(まいしゅう)日曜(にちよう)に歴史(れきし)の学習会(がくしゅうかい)を開(ひら)く。
We hold a history study group every Sunday.
地域(ちいき)の学習会(がくしゅうかい)で防災(ぼうさい)について学(まな)んだ。
At a community study session, we learned about disaster prevention.

Formed from 学習(がくしゅう) (study, learning) + 会(かい) (gathering).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学習会(がくしゅうかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold a study session
  • 学習会(がくしゅうかい)に参加(さんか)する: to join a study group

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 勉強会(べんきょうかい): study meeting — a near-synonym, very common in workplaces
  • 講習会(こうしゅうかい): training course — more formal, led by an instructor
