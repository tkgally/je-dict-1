そこまで

そこまで
expression
that far; to that extent; that much
1. that far; up to there
As far as a place already mentioned or visible. Literally そこ (there) plus まで (as far as).
そこまで歩(ある)こう。
Let's walk that far.
そこまで行(い)けばバス停(てい)が見(み)える。
If you go that far, you'll see the bus stop.
今日(きょう)はそこまでにして、残(のこ)りは明日(あした)続(つづ)けましょう。
Let's stop there for today and continue the rest tomorrow.
2. to that extent; that much; that seriously
Extended to degree rather than distance: as far as the level the other person has described. Very often used with a negative to play something down — そこまでではない, "it's not that bad / not that big a deal."
そこまで心配(しんぱい)しなくていいよ。
You don't need to worry that much.
辛(から)いと聞(き)いていたが、そこまでではなかった。
I'd heard it was spicy, but it wasn't that bad.
そこまで言(い)うなら、一度(いちど)試(ため)してみようという気(き)になった。
If you're going to insist that strongly, I feel like giving it a try.

The degree sense (2) is far more common in conversation than the literal one, and it always measures against something the other person has just said or implied.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • そこまでではない: it's not that bad; not to that degree
  • そこまでにする: to stop there, to leave it at that
  • そこまで言(い)わなくても: you didn't have to go that far (in what you said)
  • そこまでする必要(ひつよう)はない: there's no need to go that far

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • それほど: not that much — used almost exclusively with negatives, and only about degree
  • あそこまで: that far — refers to something distant from both speakers, rather than near the listener
