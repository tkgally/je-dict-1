1. to be able to wait; (in the negative) to not be able to wait
To be capable of waiting until something finally arrives. Used almost exclusively in the negative 待(ま)ちきれない, which expresses impatient excitement — "I can't wait."
明日(あした)が待(ま)ちきれない。
I can't wait for tomorrow.
子(こ)どもたちは夏休(なつやす)みが待(ま)ちきれない様子(ようす)だ。
The children look like they can't wait for summer vacation.
結果(けっか)の発表(はっぴょう)を待(ま)ちきれずに、先生(せんせい)に直接(ちょくせつ)聞(き)きに行(い)った。
Unable to wait for the results to be announced, I went to ask the teacher directly.
新(あたら)しいゲームの発売日(はつばいび)まであと一週間(いっしゅうかん)もあるなんて、とても待(ま)ちきれない。
A whole week left until the new game comes out — I really can't wait.

The potential form of 待(ま)ちきる (待(ま)つ "wait" + 〜きる "do completely"). The affirmative is rare; learners will meet this word as 待(ま)ちきれない or the adverbial 待(ま)ちきれず(に).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ...が待(ま)ちきれない: can't wait for ...
  • 待(ま)ちきれずに〜する: to do something without waiting any longer
  • 待(ま)ちきれない様子(ようす): looking unable to wait

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しみにする: to look forward to — states anticipation calmly, without the sense of straining at the delay
  • 待(ま)ち遠(どお)しい: eagerly awaited — an adjective describing the awaited thing, not the person waiting
