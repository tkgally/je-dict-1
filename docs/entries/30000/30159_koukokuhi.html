広告費(こうこくひ)

こうこくひ
noun
advertising expenses; ad spend
1. advertising expenses; advertising budget
The money a company spends on advertising, treated as a cost item in accounting and business planning.
広告費(こうこくひ)を削(けず)ることにした。
We decided to cut advertising expenses.
今期(こんき)は広告費(こうこくひ)が売上(うりあげ)の一割(いちわり)を占(し)めた。
This term, advertising expenses accounted for ten percent of sales.
テレビからネットへ広告費(こうこくひ)を移(うつ)した結果(けっか)、若(わか)い客層(きゃくそう)が増(ふ)えた。
As a result of shifting the ad spend from television to the internet, the customer base grew younger.

広告(こうこく) (advertisement) + 費(ひ) (expense). Part of the standard family of business cost words formed with 費(ひ), alongside 人件費(じんけんひ) and 交通費(こうつうひ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 広告費(こうこくひ)を削減(さくげん)する: to cut advertising expenses
  • 広告費(こうこくひ)をかける: to spend money on advertising
  • 広告費(こうこくひ)の予算(よさん): the advertising budget

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 宣伝費(せんでんひ): publicity expenses — nearly synonymous; covers promotion beyond paid ads
  • 販促費(はんそくひ): sales promotion expenses — campaigns and giveaways rather than media buying
