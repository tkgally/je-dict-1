平常時(へいじょうじ)

へいじょうじ
noun
normal times; peacetime; non-emergency conditions
1. normal times; ordinary conditions; peacetime
The ordinary, non-emergency state of affairs, used above all in contrast with 非常時(ひじょうじ) (emergencies, disasters, wartime). Common in administrative, disaster-preparedness, and corporate planning language.
平常時(へいじょうじ)の対応(たいおう)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the procedure for normal times.
平常時(へいじょうじ)は職員(しょくいん)が三人(さんにん)で対応(たいおう)しています。
In normal times, three staff members handle it.
平常時(へいじょうじ)と災害時(さいがいじ)では、必要(ひつよう)な備(そな)えが大(おお)きく異(こと)なる。
The preparations needed in normal times and in a disaster differ greatly.

Almost always used as the counterpart of 非常時(ひじょうじ). It describes a situation, not a clock time, so it is not used to mean "usual hour."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 平常時(へいじょうじ)から備(そな)える: to prepare while things are still normal
  • 平常時(へいじょうじ)の体制(たいせい): the normal-operations setup
  • 平常時(へいじょうじ)に戻(もど)る: to return to normal conditions

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 普段(ふだん): usually, ordinarily — everyday and colloquial, about personal habit rather than institutional conditions
  • 平時(へいじ): peacetime — more formal and literary, often specifically the opposite of wartime
