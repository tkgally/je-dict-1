グルーブ

ぐるーぶ
noun
groove (musical feel); rhythmic feel
1. groove; the rhythmic feel or swing of music
The infectious rhythmic feel of a piece of music — the sense of momentum and swing that makes listeners want to move. Borrowed from English 'groove.'
この曲(きょく)はグルーブがある。
This song has groove.
バンドのグルーブに乗(の)って踊(おど)る。
I dance, riding the band's groove.
ベースとドラムが生(う)み出(だ)すグルーブが心地(ここち)よい。
The groove created by the bass and drums feels great.

Borrowed from English 'groove.' Refers to the rhythmic feel and swing of music rather than its literal beat — the quality that makes a performance feel alive. Also written グルーヴ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • グルーブがある: to have groove
  • グルーブに乗(の)る: to get into the groove
  • グルーブ感(かん): a sense of groove

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 拍(はく) / ビート: beat — the underlying pulse; groove is the feel built on top of it
  • リズム: rhythm — the pattern of sounds in time; broader than groove
