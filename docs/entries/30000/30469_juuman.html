十万(じゅうまん)

じゅうまん
number
one hundred thousand; 100,000
1. one hundred thousand; 100,000
The number 100,000, formed as 十(じゅう) (ten) × 万(まん) (10,000). English switches unit here — "hundred thousand" — while Japanese simply counts ten 万(まん).
十万(じゅうまん)円(えん)の時計(とけい)。
A hundred-thousand-yen watch.
その動画(どうが)は十万(じゅうまん)回(かい)再生(さいせい)された。
That video was viewed a hundred thousand times.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で、十万(じゅうまん)世帯(せたい)以上(いじょう)が停電(ていでん)した。
Because of the typhoon, more than a hundred thousand households lost power.

Because Japanese groups digits in fours and English in threes, this is the point where mental conversion becomes error-prone: 100,000 is 十万(じゅうまん) (ten 万(まん)), and 1,000,000 is 百万(ひゃくまん) (a hundred 万(まん)).

CONVERSION LANDMARKS:

  • 一万(いちまん): 10,000
  • 十万(じゅうまん): 100,000
  • 百万(ひゃくまん): 1,000,000
  • 一億(いちおく): 100,000,000

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 十万(じゅうまん)円(えん): one hundred thousand yen
  • 十万(じゅうまん)人(にん): one hundred thousand people
  • 十万(じゅうまん)回(かい): a hundred thousand times
