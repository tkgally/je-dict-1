ような

ような
expression
like; such as; the kind of
1. like; similar to; such as
Modifies a following noun by comparing it to something else, or by giving an example of the type meant. The noun-modifying counterpart of ように.
夢(ゆめ)のような話(はなし)だ。
It's a dream-like story.
君(きみ)のような友達(ともだち)がほしい。
I want a friend like you.
今(いま)話(はな)したような問題(もんだい)が、他(ほか)の部署(ぶしょ)でも起(お)きている。
Problems like the one I just described are happening in other departments too.

USAGE:
After a noun the linker is の (子供(こども)のような); after a verb or i-adjective ような attaches directly (思(おも)っていたような); after a na-adjective the linker is な. Before a verb or adjective, the form becomes ように.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 次(つぎ)のような: the following (written style)
  • 同(おな)じような: similar; of the same sort
  • ~のような気(き)がする: to have a feeling that…
  • ~のような人(ひと): a person like…

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ように: like, as — the adverbial form, used before verbs and adjectives
  • みたいな: like — casual spoken equivalent, attaches straight to the noun
  • らしい: typical of; seeming — about being a good example of a type, not about resemblance
