販売促進費

はんばいそくしんひ
noun
sales promotion expenses
1. sales promotion expenses; promotional costs
Money spent on activities meant to boost sales — samples, in-store displays, campaigns, and giveaways. The full form of the everyday abbreviation 販促費(はんそくひ).
販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)を見直(みなお)す。
We review the sales promotion expenses.
今年度(こんねんど)の販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)は前年(ぜんねん)より多(おお)い。
This fiscal year's sales promotion expenses are higher than last year's.
試食会(ししょくかい)の費用(ひよう)は販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)として処理(しょり)した。
The cost of the tasting event was handled as a sales promotion expense.
販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)を増(ふ)やしても、売上(うりあげ)にすぐ結(むす)びつくとは限(かぎ)らない。
Increasing promotional spending does not necessarily translate into sales right away.

Formed from 販売(はんばい) (sales) + 促進(そくしん) (promotion) + 費(ひ) (expenses). The full form appears in accounting documents and formal reports; in conversation and internal email the clipped 販促費(はんそくひ) is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)を計上(けいじょう)する: to book promotional expenses
  • 販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)の予算(よさん): promotional budget
  • 販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ)を削(けず)る: to cut promotional spending

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 販促費(はんそくひ): the everyday abbreviation of this word
  • 広告費(こうこくひ): advertising expenses — paid media, distinct from in-store promotion
  • 交際費(こうさいひ): entertainment expenses — hosting clients, booked separately
