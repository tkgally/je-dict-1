縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)

えんこさいよう
noun
hiring through personal connections; nepotistic hiring
1. hiring through personal connections; recruitment by referral
Employing someone on the strength of a family tie or personal introduction rather than an open selection process. The word is broadly neutral in business usage but often carries a critical edge.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)が多(おお)い。
That company does a lot of hiring through personal connections.
彼(かれ)は縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)ではなく、試験(しけん)を受(う)けて入(はい)った。
He didn't get in through connections; he took the exam.
縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)が続(つづ)くと、組織(そしき)に新(あたら)しい発想(はっそう)が入(はい)りにくくなる。
When hiring through connections continues, it becomes hard for fresh ideas to enter an organization.

縁故(えんこ) means a personal or family connection; 採用(さいよう) means hiring. Small firms often use the practice openly, while at large companies and public bodies the word implies unfairness.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)で入社(にゅうしゃ)する: to join a company through connections
  • 縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)を廃止(はいし)する: to abolish hiring by connection
  • 縁故(えんこ)採用(さいよう)が疑(うたが)われる: to be suspected of nepotistic hiring

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • リファラル採用(さいよう): referral hiring — the modern, positive-sounding loanword for employee referrals
  • 公募(こうぼ): open recruitment — the opposite process, advertised and open to all
