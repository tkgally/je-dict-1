桜肉(さくらにく)

さくらにく
noun
horse meat
1. horse meat
Horse meat eaten as food. The name comes from the pink, cherry-blossom-like color of the raw flesh, and it is used in place of the plainer 馬肉(ばにく) on menus and in polite conversation.
桜肉(さくらにく)を初(はじ)めて食(た)べた。
I ate horse meat for the first time.
この店(みせ)は桜肉(さくらにく)の刺身(さしみ)が有名(ゆうめい)だ。
This restaurant is famous for its horse-meat sashimi.
熊本(くまもと)では桜肉(さくらにく)を食(た)べる習慣(しゅうかん)が昔(むかし)から続(つづ)いている。
In Kumamoto, the custom of eating horse meat has continued since long ago.

A euphemistic name for 馬肉(ばにく) (horse meat), from the pink color of the raw meat. Restaurants and shops normally use 桜肉(さくらにく); 馬肉(ばにく) is the neutral technical term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 桜肉(さくらにく)の刺身(さしみ): horse-meat sashimi
  • 桜肉(さくらにく)を食(た)べる: to eat horse meat
  • 桜肉(さくらにく)料理(りょうり): horse-meat cuisine
  • 桜(さくら)鍋(なべ): hot pot made with horse meat

SIMILAR WORDS:

Animal meats have similar plant-based euphemisms:

  • 牡丹(ぼたん)肉(にく): wild boar meat — from the peony-like arrangement of the sliced meat
  • 紅葉(もみじ): venison — from the autumn-leaf color
