打線(だせん)

だせん
noun
batting lineup (baseball)
1. batting lineup; the batters as a unit (baseball)
A team's batters considered collectively as an offensive unit, especially with regard to their combined hitting power. Literally the "hitting line."
今年(ことし)のチームは打線(だせん)が強(つよ)い。
This year's team has a strong batting lineup.
相手(あいて)の好投(こうとう)で、打線(だせん)がつながらなかった。
Because of the opponent's fine pitching, our lineup couldn't string hits together.
四番(よんばん)を中心(ちゅうしん)に打線(だせん)が爆発(ばくはつ)し、一気(いっき)に十点(じってん)を取(と)った。
With the cleanup hitter at its center, the lineup exploded and scored ten runs in a burst.

A baseball term. It treats the batting order as a single force rather than individual hitters, so it collocates with words for strength, connecting hits, and scoring.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 打線(だせん)がつながる: the lineup strings hits together
  • 打線(だせん)が爆発(ばくはつ)する: the lineup explodes (for many runs)
  • 強力(きょうりょく)打線(だせん): a powerful batting lineup
  • 打線(だせん)が湿(しめ)る: the lineup goes cold

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 打順(だじゅん): batting order — the sequence in which batters come up, rather than their collective power
