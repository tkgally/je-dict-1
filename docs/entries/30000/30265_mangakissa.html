漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)

まんがきっさ
noun
manga cafe
1. manga cafe
A shop where customers pay by the hour for a seat or booth with free access to a large manga library, drinks, and usually internet terminals. Many stay open all night and are used as cheap overnight lodging.
漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)で三時間(さんじかん)過(す)ごした。
I spent three hours at a manga cafe.
終電(しゅうでん)を逃(のが)して漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)に泊(と)まった。
I missed the last train and stayed at a manga cafe.
最近(さいきん)の漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)は、シャワーや洗濯機(せんたくき)まであるところが多(おお)い。
Many manga cafes these days even have showers and washing machines.

漫画(まんが) + 喫茶(きっさ) (tea-drinking, as in 喫茶店(きっさてん)). Often shortened to 漫喫(まんきつ) in casual speech. Charged by the hour, with パック}{料金(りょうきん) (block rates) for longer stays.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)に行(い)く: to go to a manga cafe
  • 漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)に泊(と)まる: to stay overnight at a manga cafe
  • 漫画喫茶(まんがきっさ)の個室(こしつ): a private booth at a manga cafe

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ネットカフェ: internet cafe — largely the same business; the name emphasizes the terminals rather than the manga
  • 喫茶店(きっさてん): coffee shop — an ordinary cafe, priced per drink rather than per hour
