減収(げんしゅう)

げんしゅう
noun
decreased revenue; drop in income; reduced yield
1. decreased revenue; drop in income
A fall in the money coming in — a company's sales, a government's tax receipts, or a household's income. Common in business reporting.
今期(こんき)は減収(げんしゅう)だった。
Revenue fell this term.
客(きゃく)が減(へ)って減収(げんしゅう)になった。
Customer numbers dropped, and revenue went down.
景気(けいき)の悪化(あっか)で、今年度(こんねんど)は減収(げんしゅう)減益(げんえき)の見通(みとお)しだ。
With the economy worsening, the company expects both lower revenue and lower profit this fiscal year.
2. reduced yield; smaller harvest
A fall in the amount produced, especially of a crop. Used in farming contexts, often after bad weather.
台風(たいふう)で米(こめ)が減収(げんしゅう)になった。
The typhoon reduced the rice harvest.
日照(にっしょう)不足(ぶそく)で減収(げんしゅう)が心配(しんぱい)されている。
A smaller harvest is feared because of the lack of sunshine.
猛暑(もうしょ)が続(つづ)いた影響(えいきょう)で、野菜(やさい)は各地(かくち)で大幅(おおはば)な減収(げんしゅう)となった。
Because of the prolonged heat wave, vegetable yields fell sharply in many areas.

From 減(げん) (decrease) + 収(しゅう) (income, receipts). Used as a noun with なる or となる; 減収(げんしゅう)する is not standard.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 減収(げんしゅう)減益(げんえき): lower revenue and lower profit (a set phrase in earnings reports)
  • 大幅(おおはば)な減収(げんしゅう): a sharp drop in revenue
  • 税収(ぜいしゅう)の減収(げんしゅう): a fall in tax revenue
  • 減収(げんしゅう)を見込(みこ)む: to expect lower revenue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 増収(ぞうしゅう): increased revenue — the direct opposite
  • 減益(げんえき): decreased profit — profit after costs, not the money taken in

Related Words

Antonym: 増収(ぞうしゅう)
Related: 減益(げんえき)
