余暇活動

よかかつどう
noun
leisure activities; recreational activities
1. leisure activities; recreational activities
Things people do in their free time for enjoyment or relaxation, such as hobbies, sports, or travel.
余暇活動(よかかつどう)を楽(たの)しむ。
Enjoy leisure activities.
退職(たいしょく)後(ご)の余暇活動(よかかつどう)を考(かんが)える。
Think about leisure activities after retirement.
読書(どくしょ)や園芸(えんげい)など、様々(さまざま)な余暇活動(よかかつどう)に時間(じかん)を使(つか)う。
I spend time on various leisure activities such as reading and gardening.

Formed from 余暇(よか) (free time, leisure) + 活動(かつどう) (activity).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 余暇活動(よかかつどう)を楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy leisure activities
  • 余暇活動(よかかつどう)の充実(じゅうじつ): enriching one's leisure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 趣味(しゅみ): hobby — a specific pastime, narrower than the broad 余暇活動(よかかつどう)
  • レジャー: leisure — a loanword, often implying outings and recreation
