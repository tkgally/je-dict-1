三万(さんまん)

さんまん
number
thirty thousand; 30,000
1. thirty thousand; 30,000
The number 30,000, formed as 三(さん) (three) × 万(まん) (10,000). No sound change occurs — both parts keep their usual readings.
三万(さんまん)円(えん)。
Thirty thousand yen.
この町(まち)の人口(じんこう)は約(やく)三万(さんまん)人(にん)だ。
This town has a population of about thirty thousand.
中古(ちゅうこ)の自転車(じてんしゃ)を三万(さんまん)円(えん)で買(か)ったが、すぐに修理(しゅうり)が必要(ひつよう)になった。
I bought a used bicycle for thirty thousand yen, but it soon needed repairs.

Japanese groups large numbers in units of 10,000 (万(まん)), so 30,000 is "three 万(まん)" rather than "thirty thousand".

COUNTING IN 万(まん):

  • 一万(いちまん): 10,000 — note 一(いち) is required; 万(まん) alone is not used for 10,000
  • 二万(にまん): 20,000
  • 三万(さんまん): 30,000
  • 十万(じゅうまん): 100,000

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 三万(さんまん)円(えん): thirty thousand yen
  • 三万(さんまん)人(にん): thirty thousand people
  • 三万(さんまん)件(けん): thirty thousand cases or items
