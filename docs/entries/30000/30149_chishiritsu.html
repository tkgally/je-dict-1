致死率(ちしりつ)

ちしりつ
noun
fatality rate; case fatality rate
1. fatality rate; death rate among those affected
The proportion of people who die among those who catch a disease or suffer a particular injury. A medical and public-health term, common in news about epidemics.
この病気(びょうき)は致死率(ちしりつ)が低(ひく)い。
This disease has a low fatality rate.
治療法(ちりょうほう)の進歩(しんぽ)で致死率(ちしりつ)が下(さ)がった。
Advances in treatment have brought the fatality rate down.
高齢者(こうれいしゃ)の致死率(ちしりつ)が若年層(じゃくねんそう)より高(たか)いことが調査(ちょうさ)で明(あき)らかになった。
The survey showed that the fatality rate among the elderly is higher than among younger people.

致死(ちし) (causing death) + 率(りつ) (rate, ratio). Counts deaths only among people who actually contracted the disease, which is why the figure is often much larger than a mortality rate for the whole population.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 致死率(ちしりつ)が高(たか)い: to have a high fatality rate
  • 致死率(ちしりつ)を下(さ)げる: to lower the fatality rate
  • 致死率(ちしりつ)一(いち)パーセント: a fatality rate of one percent

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 死亡率(しぼうりつ): mortality rate — deaths measured against a whole population
  • 感染率(かんせんりつ): infection rate — how many people catch the disease, not how many die
