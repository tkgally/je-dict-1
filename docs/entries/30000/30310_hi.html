比

ひ
noun
ratio; comparison
1. ratio; proportion
In mathematics and statistics, the relation of one quantity to another, written 二(に)対(たい)三(さん) (2:3). Also used as a suffix meaning "compared with", as in 前年比(ぜんねんひ) (compared with the previous year).
二(に)つの数(かず)の比(ひ)を求(もと)める。
Find the ratio of the two numbers.
男女(だんじょ)の比(ひ)はほぼ一対一(いちたいいち)だ。
The ratio of men to women is roughly one to one.
売上(うりあげ)は前年比(ぜんねんひ)五(ご)パーセント増(ぞう)となった。
Sales were up five percent compared with the previous year.

As a standalone noun 比(ひ) is a technical term of arithmetic. In everyday reading it is met far more often as a suffix on a base of comparison, where it is best translated "versus" or "compared with".

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前年比(ぜんねんひ): compared with the previous year
  • 前月比(ぜんげつひ): compared with the previous month
  • 比(ひ)を求(もと)める: to find a ratio
  • 黄金比(おうごんひ): the golden ratio

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 比率(ひりつ): ratio, proportion — the ordinary word for a ratio expressed as a figure
  • 割合(わりあい): proportion, percentage — everyday register, usually a share of a whole
  • 比較(ひかく): comparison — the act of comparing, not the resulting figure
