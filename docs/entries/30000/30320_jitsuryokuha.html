実力派(じつりょくは)

じつりょくは
noun
genuinely talented performer; person of real ability
1. person of genuine ability; talent-driven performer
Someone whose reputation rests on real skill rather than looks, popularity, or promotion. Used especially of singers, actors, and other performers.
彼(かれ)は実力派(じつりょくは)の俳優(はいゆう)だ。
He is a genuinely talented actor.
アイドルから実力派(じつりょくは)歌手(かしゅ)へと成長(せいちょう)した。
She grew from an idol into a singer of real ability.
地味(じみ)だが実力派(じつりょくは)ぞろいのチームで、下馬評(げばひょう)を覆(くつがえ)した。
It was an unflashy team full of genuinely skilled players, and they overturned the predictions.

Formed from 実力(じつりょく) (real ability) plus 〜派(は) (type). Carries clear praise, often with an implied contrast against performers who sell on image.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実力派(じつりょくは)俳優(はいゆう): accomplished actor
  • 実力派(じつりょくは)揃(そろ)い: full of genuinely skilled people
  • 実力派(じつりょくは)として評価(ひょうか)される: to be rated as truly capable

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 演技派(えんぎは): skilled at acting specifically — narrower than 実力派(じつりょくは)
  • 個性派(こせいは): distinctive type — praised for uniqueness rather than technical skill
