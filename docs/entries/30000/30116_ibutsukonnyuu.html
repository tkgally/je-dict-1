異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)

いぶつこんにゅう
noun
contamination by a foreign object
1. contamination by a foreign object; foreign matter in a product
The presence of something that does not belong — a hair, a piece of plastic, a fragment of metal — in food, drink, or medicine. The fixed term used in product recalls and news reports.
異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)が見(み)つかった。
Foreign matter was found in the product.
異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)のため、商品(しょうひん)が回収(かいしゅう)された。
The product was recalled because of contamination by a foreign object.
工場(こうじょう)では異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)を防(ふせ)ぐために、金属(きんぞく)探知機(たんちき)で検査(けんさ)している。
At the factory they inspect with metal detectors to prevent contamination by foreign objects.

異物(いぶつ) is the foreign object itself; 混入(こんにゅう) is its getting mixed in. The compound is standard in food-safety and manufacturing contexts and appears constantly in recall notices.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)が発覚(はっかく)する: contamination comes to light
  • 異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)を防(ふせ)ぐ: to prevent contamination
  • 異物混入(いぶつこんにゅう)事件(じけん): a contamination incident

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく): food poisoning — illness from bacteria or toxins, not a physical object
  • 汚染(おせん): pollution; contamination — chemical or biological, and used of the environment as well as products
