三回(さんかい)

さんかい
noun
three times; third round
1. three times; three occasions
三(さん) plus the counter 回(かい), used for repeated actions and occurrences. No sound change occurs: さん + かい stays さんかい.
薬(くすり)を一日(いちにち)三回(さんかい)飲(の)む。
Take the medicine three times a day.
この映画(えいが)はもう三回(さんかい)見(み)た。
I've already seen this movie three times.
週(しゅう)に三回(さんかい)ジムに通(かよ)うようにしているが、なかなか続(つづ)かない。
I try to go to the gym three times a week, but I have trouble keeping it up.

三(さん) + the general counter 回(かい) (times, occurrences).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一日(いちにち)に三回(さんかい): three times a day
  • 週(しゅう)に三回(さんかい): three times a week
  • 三回(さんかい)目(め): the third time
  • 三回(さんかい)戦(せん): the third round (of a tournament)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 三度(さんど): three times — interchangeable in most contexts, and the usual choice in set phrases such as 三度(さんど)の食事(しょくじ) (three meals a day)
  • 三回忌(さんかいき): the second-anniversary memorial service — a fixed Buddhist term, counted differently from ordinary repetitions
