二枚(にまい)

にまい
noun
two (flat objects); two sheets
1. two (flat objects)
The counter reading for two of something flat: sheets of paper, tickets, plates, photos, or garments.
チケットを二枚(にまい)ください。
Two tickets, please.
紙(かみ)が二枚(にまい)残(のこ)っている。
Two sheets of paper are left.
寒(さむ)かったので、シャツを二枚(にまい)重(かさ)ねて着(き)て出(で)かけた。
It was cold, so I layered two shirts and went out.

二枚(にまい) is the reading for "two" with the counter 枚(まい), used for thin, flat things.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 二枚(にまい)重(かさ)ねる: to layer two of something
  • 二枚(にまい)組(ぐみ): a two-disc or two-sheet set
  • 二枚(にまい)おろし: filleting a fish into two pieces

RELATED EXPRESSION:
二枚目(にまいめ) means "a good-looking man," from the second billboard in an Edo-period theater lineup. It is not simply "the second flat object."

RELATED COUNTERS:

  • 一枚(いちまい): one flat object
  • 何枚(なんまい): how many flat objects
