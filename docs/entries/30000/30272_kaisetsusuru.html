1. to open; to establish; to set up (a facility, service, or account)
To bring something newly into operation so that it is available for use — an office, a shelter, a bank account, a website, a phone line. The emphasis is on starting the service, not on constructing the building.
口座(こうざ)を開設(かいせつ)する。
To open an account.
市(し)は体育館(たいいくかん)に避難所(ひなんじょ)を開設(かいせつ)した。
The city set up an evacuation shelter in the gymnasium.
相談(そうだん)を受(う)け付(つ)けるため、専用(せんよう)の窓口(まどぐち)を開設(かいせつ)することになった。
It has been decided to set up a dedicated help desk to receive inquiries.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive; the thing opened takes を. The plain noun 開設(かいせつ) is common in notices: 窓口(まどぐち)開設(かいせつ)のお知(し)らせ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 口座(こうざ)を開設(かいせつ)する: to open a bank account
  • 避難所(ひなんじょ)を開設(かいせつ)する: to set up an evacuation shelter
  • ホームページを開設(かいせつ)する: to launch a website
  • 窓口(まどぐち)を開設(かいせつ)する: to open a service counter

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 設立(せつりつ)する: to found, establish — used of organizations and companies as legal entities
  • 開業(かいぎょう)する: to open for business — of a shop or practice starting trade
  • 開(ひら)く: to open — the everyday verb, from doors to meetings
