上昇率(じょうしょうりつ)

じょうしょうりつ
noun
rate of increase; growth rate
1. rate of increase; rate of rise
How much a figure has gone up, expressed as a percentage of its earlier value. Used of prices, wages, temperatures, and stock indexes.
今年(ことし)の上昇率(じょうしょうりつ)は三(さん)パーセントだった。
This year's rate of increase was three percent.
物価(ぶっか)の上昇率(じょうしょうりつ)が賃金(ちんぎん)を上回(うわまわ)っている。
The rate of price increases is outpacing wages.
地価(ちか)の上昇率(じょうしょうりつ)は地域(ちいき)によって大(おお)きく違(ちが)い、都心(としん)では十(じゅう)パーセントを超(こ)えた場所(ばしょ)もある。
The rate of land price increases differs greatly by area, and in the city center there are places where it exceeded ten percent.

上昇(じょうしょう) (rise, ascent) + 率(りつ) (rate, ratio). Standard vocabulary in economic news, where it is usually compared with the same month or year before.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物価(ぶっか)の上昇率(じょうしょうりつ): the rate of price increases
  • 上昇率(じょうしょうりつ)が高(たか)い: to have a high rate of increase
  • 前年比(ぜんねんひ)の上昇率(じょうしょうりつ): the year-on-year rate of increase

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下落率(げらくりつ): rate of decline — the opposite, used of prices and markets
  • 伸(の)び率(りつ): growth rate — everyday and positive in tone, used of sales and numbers of users
