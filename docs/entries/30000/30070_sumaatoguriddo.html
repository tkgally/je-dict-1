スマートグリッド

すまーとぐりっど
noun
smart grid
1. smart grid; intelligent power grid
An electricity network that uses digital sensing and communication to balance supply and demand in real time, integrating renewable sources and reducing waste. Borrowed from English 'smart grid.'
スマートグリッドが注目(ちゅうもく)されている。
Smart grids are drawing attention.
スマートグリッドは電力(でんりょく)の無駄(むだ)を減(へ)らす。
A smart grid reduces wasted electricity.
再生可能(さいせいかのう)エネルギーを活(い)かすにはスマートグリッドが欠(か)かせない。
A smart grid is indispensable for making use of renewable energy.

Borrowed from English 'smart grid.' A power network enhanced with digital sensors and two-way communication so that electricity supply and demand can be balanced automatically, making it easier to integrate solar, wind, and other variable sources.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • スマートグリッドを導入(どうにゅう)する: to introduce a smart grid
  • 次世代(じせだい)送電網(そうでんもう): next-generation power grid (a common gloss)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 送電網(そうでんもう): power grid — the physical transmission network, without the automated control
  • スマートメーター: smart meter — a networked electricity meter, one component of a smart grid
