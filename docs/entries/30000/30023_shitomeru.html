1. to bring down; to kill (game or prey)
To kill an animal or target with a weapon, especially in hunting — to hit the mark and bring it down. Transitive, taking を.
猟師(りょうし)が鹿(しか)を仕留(しと)めた。
The hunter brought down a deer.
一発(いっぱつ)で獲物(えもの)を仕留(しと)める。
To bring down the prey with a single shot.
ハンターは息(いき)を殺(ころ)して狙(ねら)いを定(さだ)め、一撃(いちげき)で仕留(しと)めた。
The hunter held his breath, took aim, and brought it down with a single strike.
2. to finish off; to secure decisively (a win, a chance)
By extension, to finish off an opponent or seize a decisive opportunity — landing the crucial blow, hit, or goal that settles the outcome. Common in sports and competitive contexts.
チャンスを仕留(しと)める。
To seize the chance decisively.
最後(さいご)のセットを仕留(しと)めて優勝(ゆうしょう)した。
He finished off the final set and won the championship.
数少(かずすく)ないチャンスを確実(かくじつ)に仕留(しと)めたチームが勝(か)ち上(あ)がった。
The team that reliably converted its few chances advanced.

The literal sense (sense 1) is hunting vocabulary — bringing down game with a weapon. The figurative sense (sense 2) carries the same feel of a single decisive blow into sports and competition.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive; the target takes を (獲物(えもの)を仕留(しと)める).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 獲物(えもの)を仕留(しと)める: to bring down the prey
  • 一発(いっぱつ)で仕留(しと)める: to bring down with one shot
  • チャンスを仕留(しと)める: to convert a chance decisively

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 捕(と)らえる: to catch/capture — take alive, not kill
  • 倒(たお)す: to defeat/knock down — broader; does not specifically mean killing prey with a weapon
