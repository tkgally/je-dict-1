ああやって

ああやって
adverb
like that; that way (over there)
1. like that; in that way
Refers to a way of doing things that both speaker and listener can observe or already know about, but that neither is doing at the moment. Often used when commenting on a third party.
ああやって並(なら)ぶんだ。
So that's how you line up.
ああやって毎日(まいにち)練習(れんしゅう)しているらしい。
Apparently he practices like that every day.
ああやって大声(おおごえ)で叱(しか)っても、子(こ)どもは何(なに)も学(まな)ばないと思(おも)う。
I don't think children learn anything from being scolded loudly like that.

Composed of ああ (that way, at a distance) + やって (te-form of やる). Completes the demonstrative series こうやって / そうやって / ああやって.

USAGE:
そうやって points at what the listener just said or did; ああやって points at something outside the conversation that both parties can picture — a person across the room, a habit of someone absent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ああやって毎日(まいにち): like that, every day
  • ああやって言(い)われると: when you're spoken to like that
  • ああやってばかりいる: to be always carrying on like that

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • あのように: in that way — the formal written equivalent
  • あんなふうに: like that — same meaning, slightly more casual and emotive
