センターバック

せんたーばっく
noun
center back (soccer)
1. center back; central defender (soccer)
In soccer, a defender who plays in the middle of the defensive line, in front of the goalkeeper, and is responsible for marking opposing forwards.
彼(かれ)はセンターバックだ。
He is a center back.
センターバックが相手(あいて)の攻撃(こうげき)を止(と)めた。
The center back stopped the opponent's attack.
背(せ)の高(たか)い選手(せんしゅ)がセンターバックに向(む)いていると言(い)われる。
Tall players are said to be well suited to playing center back.

From English 'center back'. The central defender(s) in a soccer team's back line, positioned in front of the goalkeeper.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • センターバックを務(つと)める: to play center back
  • 守備(しゅび)の要(かなめ): linchpin of the defense (a common description of the role)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サイドバック: fullback — a defender on the flank
  • ボランチ: defensive midfielder — plays just ahead of the defenders
