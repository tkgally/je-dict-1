工作員

こうさくいん
noun
covert operative; secret agent
1. covert operative; secret agent; undercover agent
A person sent by a state or organization to carry out clandestine activity — espionage, sabotage, or influence operations — in another country or group. Almost always used of the other side, never as a neutral job title.
工作員(こうさくいん)が潜入(せんにゅう)していた。
An operative had infiltrated the group.
彼(かれ)は外国(がいこく)の工作員(こうさくいん)だと疑(うたが)われている。
He is suspected of being a foreign agent.
工作員(こうさくいん)が偽(いつわ)りの情報(じょうほう)を流(なが)して世論(よろん)を動(うご)かそうとしたらしい。
Operatives apparently tried to sway public opinion by spreading false information.

Formed from 工作(こうさく) (maneuvering, covert operations) plus the suffix 員(いん) (member, staff). Note that 工作(こうさく) on its own also means "handicrafts" — the school subject — so the covert sense comes entirely from the compound and its context.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 工作員(こうさくいん)を送(おく)り込(こ)む: to send in an operative
  • 工作員(こうさくいん)が潜入(せんにゅう)する: for an operative to infiltrate
  • 産業(さんぎょう)工作員(こうさくいん): industrial spy

REGISTER:
The word carries a strong negative charge and is common in news reports and thrillers. Online it is also thrown around loosely as an insult, accusing someone of posting on behalf of a foreign government.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スパイ: spy — broader and more neutral; focuses on gathering information
  • 諜報員(ちょうほういん): intelligence agent — formal, specifically about intelligence work
