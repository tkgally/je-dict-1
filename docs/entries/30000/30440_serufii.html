セルフィー

せるふぃー
noun
selfie
1. selfie; self-taken photo
A photograph one takes of oneself, usually with a smartphone. Borrowed from English and used mainly in fashion, beauty, and social-media writing; in everyday Japanese 自撮(じど)り is far more common.
友達(ともだち)とセルフィーを撮(と)った。
I took a selfie with a friend.
セルフィーが上手(じょうず)に撮(と)れるアプリを使(つか)っている。
I use an app that takes good selfies.
展望台(てんぼうだい)で、街(まち)の夜景(やけい)を背景(はいけい)にセルフィーを撮(と)った。
At the observation deck, I took a selfie with the city's night view behind me.

From English 'selfie'. It reads as somewhat stylish or media-flavored; a learner who wants the neutral everyday word should use 自撮(じど)り.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • セルフィーを撮(と)る: to take a selfie
  • セルフィー動画(どうが): a self-filmed video
  • セルフィーを投稿(とうこう)する: to post a selfie

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自撮(じど)り: selfie — the standard Japanese word; also used as a verb with する
  • 自撮(じど)り棒(ぼう): selfie stick — always formed on 自撮(じど)り, never on セルフィー

Related Words

Synonym: 自撮(じど)り
Related: 写真(しゃしん)
