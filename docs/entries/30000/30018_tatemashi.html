1. building extension; addition (to a house)
Adding on to an existing building to enlarge it — an extra room, floor, or wing. The everyday counterpart of the more technical 増築(ぞうちく). Takes する to mean to build such an addition.
部屋(へや)を建(た)て増(ま)しする。
To add on a room.
子供(こども)が増(ふ)えたので、家(いえ)を建(た)て増(ま)しした。
Since we had more children, we built an addition onto the house.
建(た)て増(ま)しを繰(く)り返(かえ)した結果(けっか)、家(いえ)の中(なか)が迷路(めいろ)のようになってしまった。
As a result of repeated additions, the inside of the house ended up like a maze.

The everyday word for enlarging a house by building on. 増築(ぞうちく) is the more formal, technical term used on permits and in construction contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 部屋(へや)を建(た)て増(ま)しする: to add on a room
  • 建(た)て増(ま)しを繰(く)り返(かえ)す: to keep adding on
  • 二階(にかい)を建(た)て増(ま)しする: to add a second floor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 増築(ぞうちく): extension/addition — the formal, technical equivalent
  • 改築(かいちく): rebuilding/remodeling — reconstructing an existing structure, not enlarging it
