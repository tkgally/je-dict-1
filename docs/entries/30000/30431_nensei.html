年生(ねんせい)

ねんせい
suffix
-year student; student in the nth year
1. student in the nth year (of a school)
Attached to a number to say what year of school someone is in: 一年生(いちねんせい) (first-year student), 三年生(さんねんせい) (third-year student). The school itself is understood from context or named separately.
弟(おとうと)は三年生(さんねんせい)だ。
My younger brother is a third-year student.
小学校(しょうがっこう)の一年生(いちねんせい)になった。
She started first grade in elementary school.
大学(だいがく)四年生(よねんせい)は就職活動(しゅうしょくかつどう)で忙(いそが)しい。
Fourth-year university students are busy with job hunting.
何年生(なんねんせい)ですかと聞(き)かれて、中学(ちゅうがく)二年生(にねんせい)ですと答(こた)えた。
When asked what year I was in, I answered that I was in the second year of junior high.

From 年(ねん) (year) + 生(せい) (student). The number takes the on'yomi: いち, に, さん, よ (四年生(よねんせい)), ご, ろく.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 小学(しょうがく)一年生(いちねんせい): first-grader
  • 高校(こうこう)三年生(さんねんせい): third-year high school student (final year)
  • 何年生(なんねんせい): what year (question word)
  • 新(しん)一年生(いちねんせい): incoming first-year student

USAGE:
Japanese counts school years from one within each school, so a 中学(ちゅうがく)一年生(いちねんせい) is a seventh-grader in American terms. The school year begins in April, so students change year in the spring.
