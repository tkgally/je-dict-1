自然(しぜん)と

しぜんと
adverb
naturally; without trying; before one knows it
1. naturally; of its own accord; without conscious effort
Describes something that comes about on its own, especially a feeling, habit, or ability that develops without anyone working at it. The everyday, conversational member of this group.
自然(しぜん)と笑顔(えがお)になった。
A smile came to my face without my meaning it to.
毎日(まいにち)聞(き)いていれば自然(しぜん)と覚(おぼ)えるよ。
If you listen every day, you'll pick it up naturally.
同(おな)じ時間(じかん)に通(かよ)っているうちに、自然(しぜん)と話(はな)すようになった。
As we kept commuting at the same hour, we naturally started talking.

USAGE:
自然(しぜん)と is an adverb of manner or process and modifies the verb directly. It differs from 自然(しぜん)に, which is used both this way and in the sense "in a natural manner, not artificially" (自然(しぜん)に話(はな)す = to speak in a relaxed, unforced way).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自然(しぜん)と覚(おぼ)える: to learn without trying
  • 自然(しぜん)と涙(なみだ)が出(で)る: tears come by themselves
  • 自然(しぜん)と足(あし)が向(む)く: to find oneself heading somewhere

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おのずと: naturally — more literary, about the logic of things rather than personal experience
  • ひとりでに: by itself — physical events with no agent; odd with feelings
  • いつの間(ま)にか: before one knows it — stresses not noticing, rather than absence of effort

Related Words

Related: おのずと (naturally (more literary))
