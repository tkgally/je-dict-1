おのずから

おのずから
adverb
naturally; of itself; in the nature of things
1. naturally; of its own accord; as a matter of course
Describes an outcome that follows naturally from the way things are, without anyone forcing it. Literary and somewhat elevated; common in essays, aphorisms, and formal speech.
答(こた)えはおのずから明(あき)らかだ。
The answer is naturally clear.
続(つづ)けていれば、おのずから道(みち)は開(ひら)ける。
If you keep at it, a path will open up of its own accord.
立場(たちば)が変(か)われば、物事(ものごと)の見(み)え方(かた)もおのずから変(か)わってくる。
When your position changes, the way things look naturally changes with it.

Also written 自(おの)ずから. The literary variant of おのずと, with which it is interchangeable in most contexts; おのずから sounds a shade more formal and is preferred in writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • おのずから明(あき)らかだ: is naturally obvious
  • おのずから分(わ)かる: will become clear in due course
  • おのずから限界(げんかい)がある: there are natural limits

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • おのずと: naturally — the same word in its ordinary modern form
  • 自然(しぜん)と: naturally — the casual everyday counterpart
  • ひとりでに: by itself — about the absence of an agent, not about a natural course of events
