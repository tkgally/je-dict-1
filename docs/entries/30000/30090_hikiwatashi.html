引(ひ)き渡(わた)し

ひきわたし
noun
handover; delivery; transfer; extradition
1. handover; delivery; transfer (of goods, property, or a building)
The act of handing something over to another party — delivering purchased goods, or turning over a completed building or piece of property to its owner.
家(いえ)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しは来月(らいげつ)です。
The handover of the house is next month.
商品(しょうひん)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しは店頭(てんとう)で行(おこな)う。
Goods are handed over at the store counter.
工事(こうじ)が完了(かんりょう)し、建物(たてもの)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しが済(す)んだ。
The construction was completed, and the handover of the building was finished.
2. surrender; extradition (of a person)
The handing over of a person, especially a suspect or criminal, to the authorities or to another country.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しを求(もと)める。
To demand the surrender of the suspect.
逃亡犯(とうぼうはん)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しに応(おう)じた。
They complied with the extradition of the fugitive.
両国(りょうこく)は犯罪者(はんざいしゃ)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)しについて条約(じょうやく)を結(むす)んでいる。
The two countries have concluded a treaty on the extradition of criminals.

The noun form of 引(ひ)き渡(わた)す — the act of handing something, or someone, over to another party. It ranges from the delivery of goods and the turnover of a completed building to the legal surrender or extradition of a person.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 引(ひ)き渡(わた)しを行(おこな)う: to carry out a handover
  • 引(ひ)き渡(わた)しを受(う)ける: to take delivery
  • 身柄(みがら)の引(ひ)き渡(わた)し: surrender of custody

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 譲渡(じょうと): transfer of rights or ownership — legal and financial
  • 受(う)け渡(わた)し: handover; delivery — stresses the exchange between two parties, common in transactions
