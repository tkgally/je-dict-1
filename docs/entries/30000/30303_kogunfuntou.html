1. fighting on alone; struggling without support
A four-character compound (四字熟語(よじじゅくご)) for carrying on a hard fight by oneself, with no reinforcements and no help from those around you. Used of work, sport, and campaigns far more often than of actual battle.
彼(かれ)は孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)している。
He is fighting on alone.
誰(だれ)も手伝(てつだ)ってくれず、孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)の毎日(まいにち)だ。
Nobody helps out, so every day is a lone struggle.
新(あたら)しい制度(せいど)を通(とお)すため、一人(ひとり)の議員(ぎいん)が数年間(すうねんかん)孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)を続(つづ)けた。
One legislator kept up a lone struggle for several years to get the new system passed.

From 孤軍(こぐん) (an isolated army) + 奮闘(ふんとう) (to struggle hard). The tone is sympathetic: it praises the effort while pointing out that the person should not have had to make it alone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)する: to fight on alone
  • 孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)を続(つづ)ける: to keep up a lone struggle
  • 孤軍奮闘(こぐんふんとう)ぶり: the sight of someone struggling alone

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悪戦苦闘(あくせんくとう): a desperate struggle — stresses how hard the fight is, not the isolation
  • 奮闘(ふんとう): to struggle hard — the plain word, with no sense of being alone
