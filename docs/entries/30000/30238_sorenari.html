それなり

それなり
expression
in its own way; reasonably; to a certain extent
1. in its own way; reasonably; to a certain extent
Says that something is adequate or worthwhile when judged by what it is, without claiming it is outstanding. It grants modest approval while quietly setting expectations low.
それなりに楽(たの)しかった。
It was enjoyable in its own way.
高(たか)いだけあって、それなりの価値(かち)はある。
It is expensive, but it does have a corresponding value.
初(はじ)めて作(つく)ったにしては、それなりにうまくできたと思(おも)う。
For a first attempt, I think it turned out reasonably well.

From それ (that) + なり (in a manner befitting). Rarely stands alone — it appears as それなりに before verbs and adjectives, and それなりの before nouns.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • それなりに + verb / adjective: reasonably; in its own way (それなりに面白(おもしろ)い)
  • それなりの + noun: a corresponding, appropriate amount of (それなりの理由(りゆう), それなりの値段(ねだん))
  • それなりに頑張(がんば)る: to try in one's own way

USAGE:
The tone is mild and often faintly damning: それなりにおいしい means "decent, but nothing special." Used of one's own effort or possessions, it sounds modest; used of someone else's work, it can sound lukewarm.
