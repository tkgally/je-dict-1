内通者(ないつうしゃ)

ないつうしゃ
noun
informant; inside collaborator; mole
1. inside collaborator; mole; secret informant
Someone who secretly passes information to the opposing side while remaining part of their own group. Strongly negative — the word implies betrayal, not whistleblowing.
組織(そしき)に内通者(ないつうしゃ)がいた。
There was a mole inside the organization.
内通者(ないつうしゃ)を探(さが)し出(だ)すのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to track down the inside collaborator.
作戦(さくせん)が事前(じぜん)に漏(も)れていたことから、内通者(ないつうしゃ)の存在(そんざい)が疑(うたが)われた。
Because the plan had leaked in advance, the presence of a mole was suspected.

Formed from 内通(ないつう) (secret communication with the enemy) plus 〜者(しゃ) (person who). Most at home in war stories, crime fiction, and corporate-espionage reporting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内通者(ないつうしゃ)を疑(うたが)う: to suspect someone of being a mole
  • 内通者(ないつうしゃ)狩(が)り: a hunt for the informant
  • 敵(てき)の内通者(ないつうしゃ): the enemy's inside man

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 密告者(みっこくしゃ): informer — reports others to the authorities; not necessarily an insider serving a rival
  • 裏切(うらぎ)り者(もの): traitor — the everyday word, broader and not limited to passing information
  • スパイ: spy — planted from outside, whereas an 内通者(ないつうしゃ) is a genuine member who turns
