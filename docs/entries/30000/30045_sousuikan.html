送水管

そうすいかん
noun
water pipe; water main; water conduit
1. water pipe; water main; water conduit
A pipe that carries water from one place to another, especially a large pipe in a water-supply system.
送水管(そうすいかん)が破裂(はれつ)した。
The water main burst.
地下(ちか)に太(ふと)い送水管(そうすいかん)が通(とお)っている。
A thick water main runs underground.
老朽化(ろうきゅうか)した送水管(そうすいかん)の取(と)り替(か)え工事(こうじ)が行(おこな)われた。
Replacement work was carried out on the aging water mains.

Formed from 送水(そうすい) (water conveyance) + 管(かん) (pipe, tube). Refers to supply pipes in waterworks and buildings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 送水管(そうすいかん)が破裂(はれつ)する: for a water main to burst
  • 送水管(そうすいかん)の工事(こうじ): water-main work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 水道管(すいどうかん): water pipe — the everyday word for household water pipes
  • 配管(はいかん): piping, plumbing — the pipes and the work of laying them
