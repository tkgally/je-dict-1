1. amortization; depreciation; writing off
In accounting, spreading the cost of an asset over the years it is used, or removing its remaining value from the books. Most often met in the compound 減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく).
設備(せつび)を償却(しょうきゃく)する。
To depreciate the equipment.
この機械(きかい)の償却(しょうきゃく)期間(きかん)は五年(ごねん)だ。
The depreciation period for this machine is five years.
建物(たてもの)の取得費(しゅとくひ)は、毎年(まいとし)少(すこ)しずつ償却(しょうきゃく)していく。
The acquisition cost of the building is written off little by little each year.
2. paying off; repayment (of a debt)
Clearing a debt or obligation by paying it back in full. More formal than 返済(へんさい) and largely limited to financial writing.
借金(しゃっきん)を償却(しょうきゃく)した。
The debt was paid off.
債務(さいむ)の償却(しょうきゃく)に十年(じゅうねん)かかった。
It took ten years to pay off the debt.
会社(かいしゃ)は利益(りえき)の一部(いちぶ)を借入金(かりいれきん)の償却(しょうきゃく)に充(あ)てた。
The company put part of its profits toward paying off its borrowings.

A financial term written with 償(しょう) (compensate) + 却(きゃく) (dispose of). Used as a noun and with する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 減価償却(げんかしょうきゃく): depreciation of fixed assets
  • 償却(しょうきゃく)期間(きかん): amortization period
  • 償却(しょうきゃく)資産(しさん): depreciable assets
  • 債務(さいむ)を償却(しょうきゃく)する: to pay off a debt

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 返済(へんさい): repayment — the everyday word for paying back money
  • 焼却(しょうきゃく): incineration — a homophone with a completely different meaning; distinguish by context
