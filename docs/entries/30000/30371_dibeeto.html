1. debate (formal, competitive)
A structured contest in which two sides argue for and against a set proposition under fixed rules, and judges decide the winner. Associated with classrooms and competitions rather than everyday argument.
授業(じゅぎょう)でディベートをした。
We held a debate in class.
英語(えいご)のディベート大会(たいかい)に出(で)ることになった。
I ended up entering an English debate tournament.
ディベートでは、自分(じぶん)の意見(いけん)とは反対(はんたい)の立場(たちば)を任(まか)されることもある。
In a debate you are sometimes assigned the side opposite to your own opinion.
ディベートの練習(れんしゅう)を続(つづ)けるうちに、根拠(こんきょ)を示(しめ)しながら話(はな)す習慣(しゅうかん)がついた。
As I kept practicing debate, I developed the habit of speaking while showing evidence.

From English "debate." Used with する as a verb: ディベートする.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ディベート大会(たいかい): debate tournament
  • ディベートの授業(じゅぎょう): a debate class
  • ディベートに勝(か)つ: to win a debate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 討論(とうろん): discussion, debate — the Sino-Japanese word, used of panel discussions and parliamentary debate
  • 議論(ぎろん): argument, discussion — the general everyday word, with no rules or winner
  • 口論(こうろん): quarrel — an emotional dispute, not an organized exchange
