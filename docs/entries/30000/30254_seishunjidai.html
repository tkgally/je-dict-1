青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)

せいしゅんじだい
noun
one's youth; adolescent years
1. one's youth; adolescent years; the springtime of life
The years of late adolescence and early adulthood, roughly the school and university period, seen as a time of energy, friendship, and first love. The word is warm and nostalgic, and is almost always used looking back.
青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)を思(おも)い出(だ)す。
To remember one's youth.
父(ちち)の青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)の写真(しゃしん)を見(み)せてもらった。
I was shown photographs from my father's youth.
毎日(まいにち)練習(れんしゅう)ばかりだったが、あの三年間(さんねんかん)が私(わたし)の青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)だった。
It was nothing but practice every day, but those three years were the springtime of my life.

青春(せいしゅん) (youth, literally "blue spring") + 時代(じだい) (era, period). The word carries the emotional colour of 青春(せいしゅん) — idealism, intensity, and things that cannot come again.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)を過(す)ごす: to spend one's youth
  • 青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)の思(おも)い出(で): memories of one's youth
  • 青春時代(せいしゅんじだい)に戻(もど)りたい: to want to go back to one's youth

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 若(わか)いころ: when one was young — plain and factual, without the nostalgic warmth
  • 少年時代(しょうねんじだい): boyhood — earlier, roughly the elementary and junior high years
  • 思春期(ししゅんき): puberty, adolescence — a clinical term for the developmental stage
