1. to inflate; to swell; to puff out
To make something expand by filling it with air or otherwise enlarging it. Used of balloons, cheeks, dough, and sails.
風船(ふうせん)を膨(ふく)らませる。
To inflate a balloon.
子(こ)どもが怒(おこ)って頬(ほお)を膨(ふく)らませた。
The child puffed out his cheeks in anger.
パンの生地(きじ)を暖(あたた)かい場所(ばしょ)に置(お)いて、しっかり膨(ふく)らませてください。
Put the bread dough in a warm place and let it rise well.
2. to build up (hopes, dreams, imagination); to expand (a plan)
To let expectations, imagination, or an idea grow larger. Very common with 夢(ゆめ), 期待(きたい), and 想像(そうぞう).
夢(ゆめ)を膨(ふく)らませる。
To let one's dreams grow.
新(あたら)しい生活(せいかつ)への期待(きたい)を膨(ふく)らませている。
She is building up her expectations for her new life.
最初(さいしょ)は小(ちい)さな思(おも)いつきだったが、話(はな)し合(あ)ううちに計画(けいかく)を大(おお)きく膨(ふく)らませていった。
It started as a small idea, but as they talked it over they expanded the plan considerably.

TRANSITIVITY: transitive. The intransitive counterpart is 膨(ふく)らむ (to swell, to expand on its own).

  • 風船(ふうせん)が膨(ふく)らむ: the balloon swells
  • 風船(ふうせん)を膨(ふく)らませる: to inflate the balloon

膨(ふく)らます is a shorter transitive form with the same meaning; 膨(ふく)らませる is the more common choice in modern speech and writing, especially for sense 2.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 風船(ふうせん)を膨(ふく)らませる: to blow up a balloon
  • 頬(ほお)を膨(ふく)らませる: to puff out one's cheeks (sulking)
  • 夢(ゆめ)を膨(ふく)らませる: to let one's dreams grow
  • 想像(そうぞう)を膨(ふく)らませる: to let one's imagination run
