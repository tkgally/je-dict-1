敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)

てきぜんとうぼう
noun
desertion in the face of the enemy; running away at the crucial moment
1. desertion in the face of the enemy
Fleeing from a position when the enemy is at hand — historically one of the gravest military offenses.
敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)は重(おも)い罪(つみ)だった。
Desertion in the face of the enemy was a grave crime.
兵士(へいし)は敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)の罪(つみ)で裁(さば)かれた。
The soldier was tried for deserting in the face of the enemy.
当時(とうじ)の軍法(ぐんぽう)では、敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)は死刑(しけい)になることもあった。
Under the military law of the time, desertion in the face of the enemy could carry the death penalty.
2. backing out just when one is needed most
Used figuratively, and half-jokingly, of someone who abandons a task, a match, or a group right at the decisive moment.
ここでやめたら敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)だ。
Quitting now would be running away at the crucial moment.
部長(ぶちょう)は交渉(こうしょう)当日(とうじつ)に休(やす)んで、敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)だと言(い)われた。
The manager took the day off on the day of the negotiations, and people said he'd run away from the front line.
発表(はっぴょう)の直前(ちょくぜん)に辞(じ)めるのは敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)のようで、どうしても気(き)が進(すす)まなかった。
Quitting right before the presentation felt like desertion under fire, and I just couldn't bring myself to do it.

Compound of 敵前(てきぜん) (before the enemy) and 逃亡(とうぼう) (flight, desertion).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)の罪(つみ): the crime of desertion in the face of the enemy
  • 敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)する: to desert in the face of the enemy
  • まるで敵前逃亡(てきぜんとうぼう)だ: it's practically desertion

USAGE:
Sense 2 is the one learners will actually hear, usually as criticism softened by exaggeration. Because the literal sense is severe, it is too strong for a genuinely minor absence.
