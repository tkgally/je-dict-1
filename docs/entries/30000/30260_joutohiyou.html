譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)

じょうとひよう
noun
transfer expenses; selling costs
1. transfer expenses; costs of selling an asset
The costs incurred in transferring ownership of property or another asset — brokerage commissions, stamp duty, demolition or surveying fees. In Japanese tax practice these are subtracted, along with the acquisition cost, when calculating taxable capital gains.
譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)を計算(けいさん)する。
To calculate the transfer expenses.
仲介(ちゅうかい)手数料(てすうりょう)は譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)に含(ふく)まれます。
The brokerage commission is included in the transfer expenses.
譲渡(じょうと)所得(しょとく)は、売却(ばいきゃく)価格(かかく)から取得(しゅとく)費(ひ)と譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)を差(さ)し引(ひ)いて求(もと)めます。
Capital gains are found by subtracting the acquisition cost and the transfer expenses from the sale price.

A tax and real-estate term, made of 譲渡(じょうと) (transfer, conveyance) and 費用(ひよう) (expense). Only costs directly required to complete the sale count; ordinary repairs and property tax do not.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)に当(あ)たる: to qualify as a transfer expense
  • 譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)として認(みと)められる: to be recognized as a transfer expense
  • 譲渡費用(じょうとひよう)を差(さ)し引(ひ)く: to deduct the transfer expenses

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 取得(しゅとく)費(ひ): acquisition cost — what the seller originally paid for the asset, deducted separately
  • 諸経費(しょけいひ): miscellaneous expenses — a general business term with no specific tax meaning
