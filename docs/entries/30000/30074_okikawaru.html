1. to be replaced; to be swapped out
For one thing to take the place of another. The intransitive counterpart of 置(お)き換(か)える (to replace); the new item that takes over is marked with に.
古(ふる)い機械(きかい)が新(あたら)しいものに置(お)き換(か)わる。
The old machines are being replaced with new ones.
データが自動(じどう)で置(お)き換(か)わった。
The data was swapped out automatically.
紙(かみ)の書類(しょるい)が次第(しだい)に電子(でんし)データに置(お)き換(か)わりつつある。
Paper documents are gradually being replaced by electronic data.

The intransitive counterpart of 置(お)き換(か)える (to replace something). What the subject denotes is superseded by, or takes the place of, something else, which is marked with に.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive: A が B に置(お)き換(か)わる (A is replaced by B)
  • Transitive pair: 置(お)き換(か)える (to replace A with B)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自動的(じどうてき)に置(お)き換(か)わる: to be replaced automatically
  • 徐々(じょじょ)に置(お)き換(か)わる: to be gradually replaced
