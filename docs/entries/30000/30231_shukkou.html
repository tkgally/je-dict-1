1. setting sail; departure (of a ship or aircraft)
A ship or aircraft leaving on a voyage. The focus is on the start of the journey rather than on the act of leaving a particular harbor.
船(ふね)が出航(しゅっこう)した。
The ship set sail.
台風(たいふう)のため出航(しゅっこう)が延期(えんき)された。
The departure was postponed because of the typhoon.
朝(あさ)の九時(くじ)に出航(しゅっこう)する予定(よてい)なので、一時間前(いちじかんまえ)には乗(の)ってください。
We are scheduled to sail at nine in the morning, so please board an hour beforehand.

出(しゅつ) (go out) + 航(こう) (voyage, navigation). Used as a noun and with する.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出航(しゅっこう)する: to set sail
  • 出航(しゅっこう)時刻(じこく): departure time
  • 出航(しゅっこう)を見合(みあ)わせる: to hold off on sailing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出港(しゅっこう): leaving port — a homophone that focuses on the ship leaving the harbor itself; often interchangeable in practice
  • 出発(しゅっぱつ): departure — the general word, used for any traveler or vehicle
  • 入港(にゅうこう): entering port — the opposite movement
