税務(ぜいむ)

ぜいむ
noun
tax affairs; tax matters
1. tax affairs; tax administration; tax matters
Matters and work relating to taxes — filing, assessment, payment, and the administration of tax law. Used attributively in the names of offices, jobs, and procedures.
税務(ぜいむ)の手続(てつづ)きは複雑(ふくざつ)だ。
Tax procedures are complicated.
確定申告(かくていしんこく)のために税務署(ぜいむしょ)へ行(い)った。
I went to the tax office to file my income tax return.
会社(かいしゃ)は税理士(ぜいりし)に税務(ぜいむ)に関(かん)する相談(そうだん)をしている。
The company consults a tax accountant on tax matters.

A term from business and public administration. It appears mostly in compounds rather than on its own.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 税務署(ぜいむしょ): tax office
  • 税務(ぜいむ)調査(ちょうさ): tax audit; tax investigation
  • 税務(ぜいむ)申告(しんこく): tax filing
  • 税務(ぜいむ)処理(しょり): tax processing/accounting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 財務(ざいむ): financial affairs — the broader management of an organization's finances, not specifically taxes
  • 経理(けいり): accounting/bookkeeping — the day-to-day handling of a company's accounts
