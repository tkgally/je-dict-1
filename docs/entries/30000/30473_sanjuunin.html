三十人(さんじゅうにん)

さんじゅうにん
noun
thirty people
1. thirty people
Thirty persons, formed with the counter 人(にん). From 四人(よにん) upward the counter is regularly {にん}, so no sound change occurs here.
クラスは三十人(さんじゅうにん)だ。
The class has thirty students.
約(やく)三十人(さんじゅうにん)が会議(かいぎ)に参加(さんか)した。
About thirty people took part in the meeting.
定員(ていいん)は三十人(さんじゅうにん)なので、申(もう)し込(こ)みが多(おお)い場合(ばあい)は抽選(ちゅうせん)になります。
Capacity is thirty people, so if there are many applications there will be a lottery.

三十(さんじゅう) (thirty) + the counter 人(にん). Only the first three counts are irregular; everything from four upward simply adds {にん}.

COUNTING PEOPLE:

  • 一人(ひとり): one person — irregular
  • 二人(ふたり): two people — irregular
  • 三人(さんにん): three people
  • 四人(よにん): four people — {よん} shortens to {よ}
  • 三十人(さんじゅうにん): thirty people

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 三十人(さんじゅうにん)学級(がっきゅう): a class of thirty, a common benchmark in Japanese education policy
  • 定員(ていいん)三十人(さんじゅうにん): capacity of thirty
  • 三十人(さんじゅうにん)程度(ていど): around thirty people
