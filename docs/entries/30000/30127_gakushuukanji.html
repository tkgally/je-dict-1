学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)

がくしゅうかんじ
noun
kanji taught in elementary school
1. kanji taught in elementary school; educational kanji
The set of kanji that Japanese children are taught during the six years of elementary school, assigned grade by grade under the national curriculum. The list currently contains 1,026 characters.
学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)を全部(ぜんぶ)覚(おぼ)えた。
I memorized all the elementary-school kanji.
この本(ほん)は学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)だけで書(か)かれている。
This book is written using only elementary-school kanji.
小学校(しょうがっこう)の六年間(ろくねんかん)で習(なら)う学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)は千(せん)字(じ)以上(いじょう)あり、学年(がくねん)ごとに分(わ)けられている。
There are over a thousand elementary-school kanji learned across the six years of elementary school, divided up by grade.

Formed from 学習(がくしゅう) (learning, study) + 漢字(かんじ). The official name in curriculum documents is 教育漢字(きょういくかんじ); 学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ) is the label used on dictionaries, workbooks, and kanji charts sold for children.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)一覧(いちらん): a list of the elementary-school kanji
  • 学年別(がくねんべつ)学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ): elementary-school kanji arranged by grade
  • 学習漢字(がくしゅうかんじ)を習(なら)う: to learn the elementary-school kanji

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 教育漢字(きょういくかんじ): educational kanji — the same set, under its official name
  • 常用漢字(じょうようかんじ): kanji for general use — the much larger list (2,136 characters) covering everyday adult writing
