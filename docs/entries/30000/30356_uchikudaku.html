1. to shatter; to smash to pieces
To break something hard into fragments with force. The prefix 打(う)ち〜 adds vigor to 砕(くだ)く, so the breaking is decisive and complete.
岩(いわ)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く。
To smash the rock to pieces.
波(なみ)が岸壁(がんぺき)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く勢(いきお)いだった。
The waves had enough force to smash the quay.
職人(しょくにん)は大(おお)きな石(いし)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)いて、庭(にわ)に敷(し)く砂利(じゃり)にした。
The craftsman smashed the large stones and turned them into gravel for the garden.
2. to dash; to crush (hopes, dreams, illusions)
To destroy something abstract that someone was holding on to, such as a dream, an expectation, or a belief. This figurative use is the more common one in writing.
夢(ゆめ)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)かれた。
My dream was shattered.
その一言(ひとこと)が彼(かれ)の希望(きぼう)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)いた。
That one remark dashed his hopes.
最終(さいしゅう)戦(せん)での敗北(はいぼく)は、優勝(ゆうしょう)への期待(きたい)を完全(かんぜん)に打(う)ち砕(くだ)いた。
The defeat in the final match completely shattered hopes of winning the championship.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: transitive
  • Pattern: Xを打(う)ち砕(くだ)く
  • The passive 打(う)ち砕(くだ)かれる is very common in the figurative sense, since the person is usually the one whose hopes were crushed.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夢(ゆめ)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く: to shatter someone's dream
  • 希望(きぼう)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く: to dash hopes
  • 幻想(げんそう)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く: to shatter an illusion
  • 岩(いわ)を打(う)ち砕(くだ)く: to smash a rock

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 砕(くだ)く: to crush — the plain verb, without the force and finality of 打(う)ち〜
  • 粉砕(ふんさい)する: to pulverize — Sino-Japanese and more technical, also used of crushing an opponent
