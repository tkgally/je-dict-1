前作(ぜんさく)

ぜんさく
noun
previous work; preceding installment; predecessor
1. previous work; preceding installment; predecessor
The work that came immediately before the current one by the same creator or in the same series — the earlier film, novel, game, or album. Used constantly in reviews and promotional writing.
前作(ぜんさく)より面白(おもしろ)い。
It's more interesting than the previous one.
前作(ぜんさく)を見(み)ていなくても楽(たの)しめる映画(えいが)だ。
It's a film you can enjoy even without having seen the previous installment.
この小説(しょうせつ)は前作(ぜんさく)の十年後(じゅうねんご)の世界(せかい)を描(えが)いている。
This novel depicts the world ten years after the previous book.

前(ぜん) (previous) + 作(さく) (work). Always relative to a current work, so it appears where a new release is being compared with what came before.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前作(ぜんさく)に続(つづ)く: to follow on from the previous work
  • 前作(ぜんさく)を超(こ)える: to surpass the previous work
  • 前作(ぜんさく)の続編(ぞくへん): a sequel to the previous work

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新作(しんさく): new work — the latest release, seen from the other direction
  • 代表作(だいひょうさく): representative work — the piece a creator is best known for
  • 続編(ぞくへん): sequel — the work that follows, not the one that precedes
