出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)

しゅっさんよていび
noun
due date (of a birth)
1. due date; expected date of delivery
The date on which a baby is expected to be born, as calculated by a doctor. The standard term on maternity forms and in conversation about a pregnancy.
出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)はいつですか。
When is the due date?
出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)より一週間(いっしゅうかん)早(はや)く生(う)まれた。
The baby was born a week before the due date.
出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)の六週間前(ろくしゅうかんまえ)から産休(さんきゅう)を取(と)ることができる。
You can start maternity leave from six weeks before the due date.

Frequently shortened to 予定日(よていび) once the context is clear.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)が近(ちか)づく: the due date approaches
  • 出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)を過(す)ぎる: to pass the due date
  • 出産予定日(しゅっさんよていび)より早(はや)い: earlier than the due date

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
The due date matters administratively as well as medically: Japanese maternity leave (産休(さんきゅう)) is calculated from it, normally six weeks before and eight weeks after the birth.
