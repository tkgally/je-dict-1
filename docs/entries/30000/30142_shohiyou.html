諸費用(しょひよう)

しょひよう
noun
various expenses; miscellaneous costs
1. various expenses; miscellaneous costs
The assorted smaller costs that come with a purchase or transaction on top of the main price — fees, taxes, insurance, and handling charges. Standard wording in real estate, car sales, and travel.
諸費用(しょひよう)は別(べつ)にかかります。
Miscellaneous costs are charged separately.
車(くるま)の値段(ねだん)に諸費用(しょひよう)を加(くわ)えると予算(よさん)を超(こ)えてしまう。
If you add the various fees to the price of the car, it goes over my budget.
家(いえ)を買(か)うときは、物件(ぶっけん)の価格(かかく)だけでなく、税金(ぜいきん)や手数料(てすうりょう)などの諸費用(しょひよう)も計算(けいさん)に入(い)れておく必要(ひつよう)がある。
When buying a house, you need to factor in not only the price of the property but also various costs such as taxes and handling fees.

The prefix 諸(しょ) (various, several) attached to 費用(ひよう) (expenses). It appears mainly in written quotes and contracts, where it covers items too numerous to list individually.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 諸費用(しょひよう)がかかる: for various costs to be incurred
  • 諸費用(しょひよう)を含(ふく)む: to include miscellaneous costs
  • 諸費用(しょひよう)込(こ)み: miscellaneous costs included

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手数料(てすうりょう): handling fee — one specific charge, often part of the 諸費用(しょひよう)
  • 経費(けいひ): expenses — costs a business incurs in operating, viewed from the accounting side
