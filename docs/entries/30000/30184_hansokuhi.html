販促費

はんそくひ
noun
sales promotion expenses; promotional budget
1. sales promotion expenses; promotional costs
What a company spends to push sales of a product: campaigns, samples, in-store displays, coupons, and similar activity. A shortening of 販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ), and treated as a distinct line item in a budget.
販促費(はんそくひ)を削(けず)ることになった。
It was decided to cut the sales promotion budget.
今年度(こんねんど)の販促費(はんそくひ)は昨年度(さくねんど)より多(おお)い。
This fiscal year's promotional budget is larger than last year's.
新商品(しんしょうひん)の販促費(はんそくひ)として、三千万円(さんぜんまんえん)が計上(けいじょう)された。
Thirty million yen was budgeted as promotional expenses for the new product.
売上(うりあげ)が伸(の)びなかったのは、販促費(はんそくひ)の使(つか)い方(かた)に問題(もんだい)があったからだ。
Sales did not grow because there was a problem with how the promotional budget was spent.

An abbreviation of 販売促進費(はんばいそくしんひ). The short form is what appears in budget spreadsheets and internal discussion; the full form is used in formal accounting documents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 販促費(はんそくひ)を削減(さくげん)する: to cut promotional spending
  • 販促費(はんそくひ)をかける: to spend money on promotion
  • 販促費(はんそくひ)を計上(けいじょう)する: to budget for promotional expenses

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 広告費(こうこくひ): advertising expenses — money spent on ads themselves, a narrower category
  • 経費(けいひ): business expenses — the general term covering all company spending
