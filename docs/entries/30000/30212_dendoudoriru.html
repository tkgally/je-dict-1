電動ドリル

でんどうどりる
noun
electric drill; power drill
1. electric drill; power drill
A motor-driven tool that spins a bit to bore holes in wood, metal, or concrete. Sold in both corded and rechargeable versions at any home center.
電動(でんどう)ドリルで穴(あな)を開(あ)ける。
I drill a hole with an electric drill.
壁(かべ)に棚(たな)を付(つ)けるので電動(でんどう)ドリルを借(か)りた。
I borrowed an electric drill because I'm putting up a shelf on the wall.
硬(かた)い木(き)には、手回(てまわ)しではなく電動(でんどう)ドリルを使(つか)ったほうがいい。
For hard wood, it is better to use an electric drill than a hand-cranked one.
電動(でんどう)ドリルは先端(せんたん)の刃(は)を替(か)えれば、木(き)にも金属(きんぞく)にも使(つか)える。
An electric drill can be used on both wood and metal if you change the bit on the end.

Formed from 電動(でんどう) (electrically powered) + ドリル (drill, from English). The battery-powered type is often called 充電(じゅうでん)ドリル or コードレスドリル.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電動(でんどう)ドリルで穴(あな)を開(あ)ける: to drill a hole with a power drill
  • ドリルの刃(は): drill bit
  • 電動(でんどう)ドリルを当(あ)てる: to apply the drill to a surface

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電動(でんどう)ドライバー: power screwdriver — drives screws rather than boring holes
  • インパクトドライバー: impact driver — hammering action for driving long screws
  • 錐(きり): awl, gimlet — the hand tool the drill replaced
