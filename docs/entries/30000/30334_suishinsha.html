推進者(すいしんしゃ)

すいしんしゃ
noun
promoter; driving force; advocate
1. promoter; person driving a project forward; advocate
The person who pushes a policy, reform, or project ahead and takes responsibility for its progress. Used in business and government contexts rather than casual speech.
彼女(かのじょ)が改革(かいかく)の推進者(すいしんしゃ)だ。
She is the driving force behind the reform.
この計画(けいかく)には強力(きょうりょく)な推進者(すいしんしゃ)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
This plan needs a powerful champion.
制度(せいど)の推進者(すいしんしゃ)が異動(いどう)したとたん、議論(ぎろん)は止(と)まってしまった。
The moment the person driving the system forward was transferred, the discussion ground to a halt.

Formed from 推進(すいしん) (propulsion, promotion) plus 〜者(しゃ) (person who does). The thing being promoted takes の.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改革(かいかく)の推進者(すいしんしゃ): driver of reform
  • 中心的(ちゅうしんてき)推進者(すいしんしゃ): central promoter
  • 推進者(すいしんしゃ)となる: to become the one pushing something forward

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 責任者(せきにんしゃ): person in charge — defined by formal accountability, not by momentum
  • 提唱者(ていしょうしゃ): proposer, advocate — the one who first put the idea forward, not necessarily the one carrying it out
