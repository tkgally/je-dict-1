別天地(べってんち)

べってんち
noun
another world; paradise; a place apart
1. another world; a place apart; paradise
A place so different from ordinary surroundings that it feels like a separate world. Almost always positive: quiet, beautiful, or refreshingly free of everyday pressures.
ここはまるで別天地(べってんち)だ。
This place is like another world.
山(やま)の上(うえ)は静(しず)かで、別天地(べってんち)のようだった。
The mountaintop was quiet, like a world apart.
都会(とかい)の騒音(そうおん)を離(はな)れると、この島(しま)はまさに別天地(べってんち)である。
Away from the noise of the city, this island is truly a world of its own.
同(おな)じ会社(かいしゃ)でも、研究所(けんきゅうじょ)は雰囲気(ふんいき)がまったく違(ちが)う別天地(べってんち)らしい。
Even within the same company, the research lab is apparently a world apart in atmosphere.

Formed from 別(べつ) (separate) + 天地(てんち) (heaven and earth, the world). Most often used with まるで or まさに to compare a real place to an ideal one.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • まるで別天地(べってんち)だ: it is just like another world
  • 別天地(べってんち)のような場所(ばしょ): a place like a world apart
  • ここは別天地(べってんち)だ: this is a place apart

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 桃源郷(とうげんきょう): Shangri-La — a legendary utopia, more literary and idealized
  • 楽園(らくえん): paradise — emphasizes pleasure and abundance rather than separation from the ordinary
  • 別世界(べっせかい): another world — near-synonym, but can also describe social worlds one does not belong to
