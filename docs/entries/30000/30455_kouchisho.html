拘置所(こうちしょ)

こうちしょ
noun
detention house; detention center
1. detention house; detention center
A facility that holds people awaiting or undergoing trial, and those sentenced to death. Distinct from a prison, which holds people already serving sentences.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は拘置所(こうちしょ)にいる。
The suspect is in the detention house.
弁護士(べんごし)が拘置所(こうちしょ)を訪(おとず)れた。
The lawyer visited the detention house.
判決(はんけつ)が出(で)るまで、被告(ひこく)は拘置所(こうちしょ)に収容(しゅうよう)されていた。
The defendant was held in the detention house until the verdict came down.

Composed of 拘置(こうち) (detention) + 所(しょ) (place). Holds people whose cases are still before the courts, so the people in it have not been convicted.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 拘置所(こうちしょ)に入(はい)る: to be put in a detention house
  • 拘置所(こうちしょ)に収容(しゅうよう)される: to be held in a detention house
  • 東京(とうきょう)拘置所(こうちしょ): the Tokyo Detention House
  • 拘置所(こうちしょ)を出(で)る: to leave the detention house

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 刑務所(けいむしょ): prison — for people already serving a sentence
  • 留置場(りゅうちじょう): police holding cell — attached to a police station, for the first days after arrest
