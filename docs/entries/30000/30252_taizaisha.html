滞在者(たいざいしゃ)

たいざいしゃ
noun
person staying; resident; visitor
1. person staying (somewhere); temporary resident; guest
Someone who is staying in a place for a period of time without living there permanently, such as a foreign resident, a hotel guest, or a long-term visitor. Mainly used in administrative and statistical contexts.
長期(ちょうき)滞在者(たいざいしゃ)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of long-term residents is increasing.
滞在者(たいざいしゃ)は受付(うけつけ)で名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Those staying here, please write your name at the front desk.
この制度(せいど)は、三(み)か月(つき)以上(いじょう)の滞在者(たいざいしゃ)を対象(たいしょう)としている。
This system applies to those staying for three months or longer.

滞在(たいざい) (staying, sojourn) + 者(しゃ) (person). The 者(しゃ) suffix is neutral-to-formal and appears throughout official documents; it is not used to refer to oneself.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 長期(ちょうき)滞在者(たいざいしゃ): long-term resident
  • 短期(たんき)滞在者(たいざいしゃ): short-term visitor
  • 海外(かいがい)滞在者(たいざいしゃ): person staying abroad

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 居住者(きょじゅうしゃ): resident — implies a settled place of living, not a temporary stay
  • 宿泊客(しゅくはくきゃく): overnight guest — specifically at a hotel or inn
  • 旅行者(りょこうしゃ): traveler — focuses on the trip rather than on remaining in one place
