なんとも

なんとも
adverb
indescribably; not at all
1. indescribably; somehow; ever so
With an affirmative predicate, marks a feeling the speaker cannot quite put into words. Very common in なんとも言(い)えない (indescribable) and before an adjective of impression.
なんとも言(い)えない味(あじ)だ。
It has an indescribable flavor.
なんとも不思議(ふしぎ)な話(はなし)ですね。
What a strange story that is.
久(ひさ)しぶりに故郷(こきょう)の景色(けしき)を見(み)て、なんとも懐(なつ)かしい気持(きも)ちになった。
Seeing my hometown scenery after so long filled me with an indescribable nostalgia.
2. not at all; nothing much; no problem
With a negative predicate, denies the whole of something — that anything is wrong, that anything can be said, or that anything is felt.
なんともありません。
I'm perfectly fine.
この程度(ていど)の寒(さむ)さならなんとも思(おも)わない。
Cold like this doesn't bother me in the least.
結果(けっか)が出(で)るまでは、なんとも申(もう)し上(あ)げられません。
Until the results are in, I cannot say anything at all.

From 何(なん) + と + も. Which sense applies is decided entirely by whether the predicate is affirmative or negative, so read to the end of the clause before interpreting it.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • なんとも言(い)えない: indescribable; impossible to say either way
  • なんとも思(おも)わない: to think nothing of it, to be unbothered
  • なんともない: to be fine, to be nothing serious
  • なんとも申(もう)し上(あ)げられない: I am not in a position to comment (formal)

USAGE:
Sense 1 before an adjective is a warm, slightly literary intensifier: なんとも美(うつく)しい (ever so beautiful). Sense 2's なんともない is the standard reply to "are you hurt?" or "is anything wrong?".
