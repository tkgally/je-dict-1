受取先(うけとりさき)

うけとりさき
noun
recipient; receiving party; delivery address
1. recipient; receiving party; place of receipt
The person, company, or location that receives goods, money, or a delivery. A form-filling and business term rather than conversational vocabulary.
受取先(うけとりさき)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in the recipient.
受取先(うけとりさき)をコンビニに変更(へんこう)した。
I changed the pickup location to a convenience store.
受取先(うけとりさき)の住所(じゅうしょ)が間違(まちが)っていたため、荷物(にもつ)が戻(もど)ってきてしまった。
The recipient's address was wrong, so the package came back.

Formed from 受(う)け取(と)る (to receive) plus 〜先(さき), the suffix that names the other party or destination of an action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 受取先(うけとりさき)住所(じゅうしょ): recipient's address
  • 受取先(うけとりさき)を指定(してい)する: to specify the recipient
  • 受取先(うけとりさき)変更(へんこう): change of recipient

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 送(おく)り先(さき): destination for sending — the same delivery seen from the sender's side
  • 宛先(あてさき): addressee — the address written on the item itself
  • 取引先(とりひきさき): business partner — a parallel 〜先(さき) compound worth learning alongside this one
