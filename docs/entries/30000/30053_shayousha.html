社用車(しゃようしゃ)

しゃようしゃ
noun
company car; company vehicle
1. company car; company vehicle
A car owned or leased by a company and used by employees for business purposes, as opposed to a privately owned car.
社用車(しゃようしゃ)で移動(いどう)する。
Travel by company car.
営業(えいぎょう)には社用車(しゃようしゃ)を使(つか)う。
We use a company car for sales visits.
社用車(しゃようしゃ)を私用(しよう)で使(つか)ってはいけないと決(き)められている。
It is a rule that company cars must not be used for personal purposes.

Combines 社用(しゃよう) (company business) with 車(しゃ) (vehicle). Refers to a vehicle a company provides for work use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 社用車(しゃようしゃ)を運転(うんてん)する: to drive a company car
  • 社用車(しゃようしゃ)の管理(かんり): management of company vehicles

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自家用車(じかようしゃ): privately owned car — for personal use
  • マイカー: one's own car — a colloquial term for a personal car
