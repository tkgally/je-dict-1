悲(かな)しさ

かなしさ
noun
sadness; sorrow
1. sadness; sorrow; how sad something is
The noun form of 悲(かな)しい. It names sadness as a quality or a degree — how sad a thing or an experience is — rather than the emotion in the abstract.
別(わか)れの悲(かな)しさを感(かん)じた。
I felt the sadness of parting.
その歌(うた)には言葉(ことば)にできない悲(かな)しさがある。
That song has a sadness that words cannot capture.
家族(かぞく)を失(うしな)った人(ひと)の悲(かな)しさは、周(まわ)りにはなかなか分(わ)からない。
Those around them can hardly understand the sorrow of someone who has lost their family.

Formed by replacing the final い of 悲(かな)しい with さ, the regular way to make a noun of degree from an i-adjective.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悲(かな)しさを感(かん)じる: to feel sadness
  • 悲(かな)しさがこみ上(あ)げる: sadness wells up
  • 悲(かな)しさに耐(た)える: to bear the sorrow
  • 言(い)いようのない悲(かな)しさ: indescribable sadness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 悲(かな)しみ: sorrow, grief — the emotion itself, deeper and more lasting; the usual choice for grief over a death
  • 寂(さび)しさ: loneliness — the ache of being alone rather than of loss

Related Words
