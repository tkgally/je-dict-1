1. energizing; passing an electric current through something
Applying electric current to a device or circuit so that it becomes live, typically to power equipment on for operation or testing. Takes する to form a verb.
装置(そうち)に通電(つうでん)する。
Energize the device.
通電(つうでん)すると、ランプが点灯(てんとう)する。
When you apply current, the lamp lights up.
配線(はいせん)を確認(かくにん)してから通電(つうでん)してください。
Please check the wiring before turning on the power.

Refers to letting electricity flow into a circuit or appliance, typically to power it up or test it. Takes する to become a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 通電(つうでん)確認(かくにん): checking that current flows
  • 通電(つうでん)状態(じょうたい): a powered, live state
  • 通電(つうでん)試験(しけん): an energization test

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 導電(どうでん): electrical conduction — the property of conducting, not the act of switching on
  • 停電(ていでん): power outage — a loss of electricity supply
