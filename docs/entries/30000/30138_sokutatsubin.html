速達便(そくたつびん)

そくたつびん
noun
express mail; special delivery
1. express mail; special delivery
Postal service that delivers faster than ordinary mail for an extra fee. Items are marked with a red line and handled ahead of regular post.
速達便(そくたつびん)で送(おく)った。
I sent it by express mail.
急(いそ)ぐので速達便(そくたつびん)にしてください。
It's urgent, so please send it as express mail.
願書(がんしょ)の締(し)め切(き)りに間(ま)に合(あ)わせるため、追加(ついか)料金(りょうきん)を払(はら)って速達便(そくたつびん)で出(だ)した。
To make the application deadline, I paid the extra fee and sent it by express mail.

速達(そくたつ) (express delivery) + 便(びん) (a mail or transport service). 速達(そくたつ) alone is used just as often; 速達便(そくたつびん) stresses that it is a particular service option.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 速達便(そくたつびん)で送(おく)る: to send by express mail
  • 速達便(そくたつびん)にする: to make it express mail
  • 速達便(そくたつびん)料金(りょうきん): the express mail fee

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 書留(かきとめ): registered mail — about tracking and compensation, not speed; often combined with express
  • 宅配便(たくはいびん): parcel delivery service — run by private couriers, for packages rather than letters
