ぜんまい

ぜんまい
noun
royal fern; fiddlehead of the royal fern (edible wild plant)
1. royal fern; its coiled young shoots eaten as a wild vegetable
A fern whose tightly curled spring shoots are gathered as 山菜(さんさい) (wild mountain vegetables). The shoots must be boiled and soaked before eating, and are often sold dried.
ぜんまいの煮物(にもの)が好(す)きだ。
I like simmered royal fern.
春(はる)になると山(やま)でぜんまいを採(と)る。
When spring comes, we gather royal fern in the mountains.
干(ほ)したぜんまいは水(みず)で戻(もど)してから油(あぶら)で炒(いた)めると美味(おい)しい。
Dried royal fern is delicious if you rehydrate it in water and then stir-fry it in oil.

Usually written in kana; the kanji 薇(ぜんまい) is rare. One of the classic 山菜(さんさい) of early spring, along with 蕨(わらび) and ふきのとう.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ぜんまいの煮物(にもの): simmered royal fern
  • ぜんまいを採(と)る: to gather royal fern
  • 干(ほ)しぜんまい: dried royal fern
  • ぜんまいのナムル: royal fern namul (a common Korean-style side dish)

HOMONYM:
The same-sounding ぜんまい also means a coiled spring or clockwork mechanism (ぜんまいを巻(ま)く, to wind up a spring) — the plant's tight spiral is the source of that sense.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 蕨(わらび): bracken — another spring fern shoot, thinner and used for 蕨餅(わらびもち) starch
  • ふきのとう: butterbur bud — bitter, and eaten as tempura or miso rather than simmered
