1. built-in; internal; having a component incorporated inside
Having a part or function built into a device rather than attached externally. As a する verb it means to incorporate something inside; as a modifier (内蔵(ないぞう)〜) it means 'built-in.'
このパソコンはカメラを内蔵(ないぞう)している。
This computer has a built-in camera.
内蔵(ないぞう)バッテリーは交換(こうかん)できない。
The built-in battery cannot be replaced.
最近(さいきん)のテレビは録画(ろくが)機能(きのう)を内蔵(ないぞう)している。
Recent TVs have a built-in recording function.

Combines 内(ない) (inside) with 蔵(ぞう) (store, house). Describes a component or function that is built into a device rather than external. As a modifier, 内蔵(ないぞう)〜 means 'built-in'; with する it means 'to incorporate internally.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 内蔵(ないぞう)カメラ: built-in camera
  • 内蔵(ないぞう)メモリ: built-in memory
  • 内蔵(ないぞう)ハードディスク: internal hard disk

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 外付(そとづ)け: external (attachment) — the opposite; connected from outside
  • 内臓(ないぞう): internal organs — a homophone written with a different kanji (臓(ぞう)); do not confuse
