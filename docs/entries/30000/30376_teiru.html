ている

ている
auxiliary
be ...ing; be in the state of having ...ed
1. be ...ing (action in progress)
With verbs that describe an activity lasting over time (読(よ)む, 走(はし)る, 待(ま)つ), 〜ている says the action is going on now: 本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいる (I am reading a book).
今(いま)、本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいる。
I'm reading a book right now.
子(こ)どもたちが公園(こうえん)で遊(あそ)んでいる。
The children are playing in the park.
電話(でんわ)したとき、母(はは)は夕(ゆう)ごはんを作(つく)っていた。
When I called, my mother was making dinner.
2. be in the state resulting from ...
With verbs describing an instantaneous change (死(し)ぬ, 結婚(けっこん)する, 開(あ)く), 〜ている says the change has happened and the resulting state continues: 窓(まど)が開(あ)いている (the window is open), not "is opening."
窓(まど)が開(あ)いている。
The window is open.
姉(あね)はもう結婚(けっこん)している。
My older sister is already married.
駅前(えきまえ)の店(みせ)は閉(し)まっているので、別(べつ)の店(みせ)に行(い)こう。
The shop in front of the station is closed, so let's go to a different one.
3. habitually; repeatedly
With a time expression or a plural object, 〜ている describes a repeated or habitual action: 毎朝(まいあさ)走(はし)っている (I run every morning).
毎朝(まいあさ)走(はし)っている。
I run every morning.
この学校(がっこう)では英語(えいご)とフランス語(ご)を教(おし)えている。
This school teaches English and French.
父(ちち)は三十年(さんじゅうねん)同(おな)じ会社(かいしゃ)で働(はたら)いている。
My father has worked at the same company for thirty years.

Formed from the te-form of a verb plus いる. Which meaning appears depends on the verb, not on 〜ている itself: activity verbs give the progressive reading, change-of-state verbs give the resulting-state reading.

FORMS:

  • 書(か)いている: is writing
  • 書(か)いています: is writing (polite)
  • 書(か)いていない: is not writing
  • 書(か)いていた: was writing
  • 書(か)いてる: contracted form, very common in speech

COMMON MISTAKE:

  • 死(し)んでいる means "is dead," not "is dying"; 知(し)っている means "know," not "am knowing." For verbs of instantaneous change, translate with an English state, not a progressive.

SIMILAR FORMS:

  • 〜てある: has been done (and left that way) — focuses on someone's intent behind the state (窓(まど)が開(あ)けてある, the window has been left open)
  • 〜ておく: do in advance — preparation, not continuation
  • 〜つつある: be in the process of — formal, written, and only for gradual change
