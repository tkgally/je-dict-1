囲碁盤

いごばん
noun
go board; goban
1. go board; goban
The board used to play the game of go (囲碁(いご)), marked with a grid of lines on which stones are placed at the intersections.
囲碁盤(いごばん)を出(だ)す。
Take out the go board.
囲碁盤(いごばん)の上(うえ)に石(いし)を並(なら)べる。
Arrange the stones on the go board.
祖父(そふ)は木(き)でできた立派(りっぱ)な囲碁盤(いごばん)を持(も)っている。
My grandfather has a splendid go board made of wood.

Also called simply 碁盤(ごばん). The standard board has a 19-by-19 grid of intersecting lines.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 囲碁盤(いごばん)を囲(かこ)む: to sit around a go board
  • 囲碁盤(いごばん)の目(め): the grid intersections of a go board

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 碁盤(ごばん): go board — the shorter, more common word; 碁盤(ごばん)目(め) also means a grid pattern
  • 将棋盤(しょうぎばん): shogi board — for Japanese chess
