水遊(みずあそ)び

みずあそび
noun
playing in water; water play
1. playing in water; water play; splashing about
Children's play in shallow water — a pool, a stream, a fountain, or a garden hose. It is casual play rather than swimming, and the word is used mainly of small children.
子(こ)どもたちが水遊(みずあそ)びをしている。
The children are playing in the water.
暑(あつ)い日(ひ)は庭(にわ)で水遊(みずあそ)びをさせている。
On hot days I let them play with water in the garden.
浅(あさ)い川(かわ)でも目(め)を離(はな)すと危(あぶ)ないので、水遊(みずあそ)びのときは必(かなら)ずそばにいてください。
Even a shallow river is dangerous if you look away, so please stay close by when they are playing in the water.

水(みず) (water) + 遊(あそ)び (play). Takes する: 水遊(みずあそ)びをする.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水遊(みずあそ)びをする: to play in the water
  • 水遊(みずあそ)び用(よう)のおむつ: swim diapers
  • 水遊(みずあそ)び場(ば): a water play area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 水泳(すいえい): swimming — a sport or lesson, done in a pool or the sea
  • 海水浴(かいすいよく): sea bathing — a trip to the beach to swim and play in the sea
  • 砂遊(すなあそ)び: playing in sand — the parallel word for sandpit play
