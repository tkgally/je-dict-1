傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)

ぼうせんぶ
noun
the marked passage; the underlined part
1. the marked passage; the underlined part (of a text)
The stretch of text singled out by a line drawn beside or under it. Standard wording in Japanese exam questions, which refer to a passage as 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)A or 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)(1) and then ask about it.
傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)を訳(やく)しなさい。
Translate the marked passage.
傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)の意味(いみ)を説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain the meaning of the underlined part.
次(つぎ)の文章(ぶんしょう)を読(よ)んで、傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)について筆者(ひっしゃ)の考(かんが)えを述(の)べなさい。
Read the following passage and state the author's view regarding the marked section.

傍線(ぼうせん) (a line drawn alongside text) + 部(ぶ) (part). Japanese was traditionally written vertically, so an emphasis line runs down the side of the characters rather than under them — hence 傍(ぼう) 'beside'. In horizontal text the line appears underneath, and English 'underlined part' is the natural translation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)A: marked passage A (labels used in exam questions)
  • 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)について答(こた)えなさい: answer the question about the marked passage
  • 傍線部(ぼうせんぶ)を引(ひ)く: to draw a line beside a passage

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 下線部(かせんぶ): the underlined part — the same idea described as a line underneath; more common in horizontally written material
  • 波線部(はせんぶ): the part marked with a wavy line — used when a question distinguishes two marked passages

Related Words

Synonym: 下線部(かせんぶ)
