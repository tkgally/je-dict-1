自体(じたい)

じたい
noun
itself; in itself; per se
1. itself; in itself; per se
Placed directly after a noun to isolate the thing itself from its circumstances, results, or associated details. Often used to concede that the thing itself is fine while something around it is not.
計画(けいかく)自体(じたい)は悪(わる)くない。
The plan itself isn't bad.
仕事(しごと)自体(じたい)は好(す)きだが、通勤(つうきん)が大変(たいへん)だ。
I like the work itself, but the commute is hard.
値段(ねだん)自体(じたい)よりも、送料(そうりょう)が高(たか)いことが問題(もんだい)だ。
The problem is the high shipping cost rather than the price itself.
失敗(しっぱい)したこと自体(じたい)を責(せ)めるつもりはないが、報告(ほうこく)が遅(おそ)かったのはよくない。
I don't intend to blame the failure itself, but reporting it late was not good.

Attaches directly to the preceding noun with no particle: 計画(けいかく)自体(じたい), そのこと自体(じたい). Frequently followed by は in concessive sentences of the form "X itself is fine, but…"

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • それ自体(じたい): in itself, as such
  • 問題(もんだい)自体(じたい): the problem itself
  • 存在(そんざい)自体(じたい): its very existence

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自身(じしん): oneself — used mainly with people (私(わたし)自身(じしん), I myself)
  • そのもの: the very thing — stronger and more emphatic, and can also mean "the epitome of" (健康(けんこう)そのもの, the picture of health)

NOTE: Do not confuse with the homophones 事態(じたい) (situation), 辞退(じたい) (declining), and 字体(じたい) (typeface).
