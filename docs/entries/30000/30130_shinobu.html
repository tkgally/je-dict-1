1. to remember fondly; to reminisce about
To call to mind a person who is absent or dead, or a time now past, with warmth and a touch of longing. Most often used of the deceased and of one's own younger days.
故人(こじん)を偲(しの)ぶ。
To remember the deceased fondly.
古(ふる)い写真(しゃしん)を見(み)ながら学生(がくせい)時代(じだい)を偲(しの)んだ。
Looking at old photographs, I reminisced about my student days.
毎年(まいとし)この日(ひ)には仲間(なかま)が集(あつ)まって、亡(な)くなった先生(せんせい)を偲(しの)ぶ会(かい)を開(ひら)いている。
Every year on this day, the group gathers and holds a memorial gathering for our late teacher.

A quiet, somewhat literary word. It belongs to funerals, memorial gatherings, and reflective writing rather than casual conversation.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive; takes を for the person or time remembered (故人(こじん)を偲(しの)ぶ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 故人(こじん)を偲(しの)ぶ: to remember the deceased
  • 昔(むかし)を偲(しの)ぶ: to think back on old times
  • 面影(おもかげ)を偲(しの)ぶ: to recall someone's face or traces of the past
  • 偲(しの)ぶ会(かい): a memorial gathering

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 懐(なつ)かしむ: to feel nostalgic for — similar warmth, but freely used in everyday speech
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to recall — neutral; simply retrieving a memory, with no affection implied
  • 忍(しの)ぶ: to endure; to hide oneself — a different word with the same reading; do not confuse the two
