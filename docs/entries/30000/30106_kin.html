〜金(きん)

きん
suffix
sum of money; fee; charge; allowance (suffix)
1. sum of money; fee; charge; benefit payment
Attached to a noun to name a specific sum of money set aside, owed, or paid out for the purpose named by the base word. Extremely productive in contracts, invoices, and administrative Japanese.
入学(にゅうがく)金(きん)を払(はら)った。
I paid the enrollment fee.
未払(みはら)い金(きん)が三万円(さんまんえん)残(のこ)っている。
Thirty thousand yen remains unpaid.
退職(たいしょく)金(きん)の額(がく)は、勤続(きんぞく)年数(ねんすう)によって決(き)まる。
The amount of the retirement allowance is determined by length of service.

Names a designated sum of money rather than money in general.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 料金(りょうきん): charge; fare
  • 代金(だいきん): payment for goods; price
  • 現金(げんきん): cash
  • 貯金(ちょきん): savings
  • 保証(ほしょう)金(きん): deposit; security money
  • 年金(ねんきん): pension

SIMILAR SUFFIXES:

  • 〜料(りょう): fee charged for a service (送料(そうりょう), 手数料(てすうりょう)) — narrower than 〜金(きん)
  • 〜費(ひ): expense; cost incurred (交通(こうつう)費(ひ), 生活(せいかつ)費(ひ)) — money spent, not a sum set aside
