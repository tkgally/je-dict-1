捺印欄(なついんらん)

なついんらん
noun
seal box; space for a stamp on a form
1. seal column; box for affixing a personal seal
The small marked space on a form or contract where a personal seal is to be stamped. Found on applications, delivery slips, and official paperwork.
捺印欄(なついんらん)が空(あ)いています。
The seal box has been left blank.
右下(みぎした)の捺印欄(なついんらん)に印(いん)を押(お)してください。
Please stamp your seal in the box at the bottom right.
最近(さいきん)は捺印欄(なついんらん)のない書類(しょるい)が増(ふ)え、電子(でんし)署名(しょめい)で済(す)むようになった。
Recently more documents have no seal box, and an electronic signature is enough.

捺印(なついん) (affixing a seal) + 欄(らん) (column, field on a form). Part of a family of form-field words built with 欄(らん), such as 署名欄(しょめいらん) and 記入欄(きにゅうらん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 捺印欄(なついんらん)に押(お)す: to stamp in the seal box
  • 捺印欄(なついんらん)が空白(くうはく): the seal box is blank
  • 捺印欄(なついんらん)を設(もう)ける: to provide a seal box

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 署名欄(しょめいらん): signature field — for a handwritten name instead of a seal
  • 記入欄(きにゅうらん): entry field — any blank to be filled in
