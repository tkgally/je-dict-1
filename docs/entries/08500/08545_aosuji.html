青筋(あおすじ)

あおすじ
noun
blue vein; visible vein
1. a visible blue vein, especially on the forehead when angry
refers to veins becoming visible, particularly used to describe a sign of anger or irritation
青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てて怒(おこ)る。
To get angry with veins bulging.
彼(かれ)は額(ひたい)に青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てていた。
His forehead veins were bulging.
上司(じょうし)が青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てて怒鳴(どな)った。
My boss shouted with veins popping in anger.

EXPRESSION OF ANGER:
青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てる is an idiomatic expression meaning to be visibly angry, with the image of veins becoming prominent on the forehead or temple due to intense emotion.

USAGE:
Almost always used in the expression 青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てる (to make veins stand out = to be furious).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てて怒(おこ)る - to be furiously angry
  • 額(ひたい)に青筋(あおすじ)を立(た)てる - to have forehead veins bulging
  • こめかみに青筋(あおすじ)を浮(う)かべる - to have temple veins showing
