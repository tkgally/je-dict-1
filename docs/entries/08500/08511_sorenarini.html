それなりに

それなりに
adverb
in its own way; reasonably; to some extent
1. in its own way; reasonably; to some extent
indicates that something has value or quality appropriate to its level or circumstances, without being exceptional
それなりに楽(たの)しかった。
It was enjoyable in its own way.
安(やす)いけど、それなりに使(つか)える。
It's cheap, but it works reasonably well.
努力(どりょく)すれば、それなりの結果(けっか)が出(で)る。
If you make an effort, you'll get corresponding results.
経験(けいけん)を積(つ)めばそれなりに上達(じょうたつ)する。
With experience, you'll improve to a reasonable degree.

USAGE:
Expresses that something meets expectations appropriate to its level or context. Often implies "not great, but acceptable" or "good enough for what it is."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • それなりに + adjective/verb - reasonably ~
  • それなりの + noun - appropriate/corresponding ~

NUANCE:
Can have a slightly reserved or hedging tone, acknowledging value while not overstating it.

RELATED:

  • 相応(そうおう)に - correspondingly
  • まあまあ - so-so, fairly
  • 一応(いちおう) - for the time being
