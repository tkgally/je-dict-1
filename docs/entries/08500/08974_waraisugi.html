笑(わら)いすぎ

わらいすぎ
noun
laughing too much; excessive laughter
1. laughing too much; excessive laughter; over-laughing
The state of laughing excessively, often to the point of physical discomfort like stomach pain or difficulty breathing.
笑(わら)いすぎてお腹(なか)が痛(いた)い。
My stomach hurts from laughing too much.
昨日(きのう)のコントは笑(わら)いすぎて涙(なみだ)が出(で)た。
Yesterday's comedy sketch made me laugh so hard I cried.
笑(わら)いすぎで息(いき)ができなくなった。
I laughed so much I couldn't breathe.

笑(わら)いすぎ follows the ～すぎ pattern meaning "too much" or "excessive."

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 笑(わら)いすぎてお腹(なか)が痛(いた)い: stomach hurts from laughing
  • 笑(わら)いすぎて涙(なみだ)が出(で)る: laughing until tears come out
  • 笑(わら)いすぎて息(いき)ができない: can't breathe from laughing

RELATED ～すぎ EXPRESSIONS:

  • 食(た)べすぎ: eating too much
  • 飲(の)みすぎ: drinking too much
  • 働(はたら)きすぎ: overworking
