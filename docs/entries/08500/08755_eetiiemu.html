ATM

えーてぃーえむ
noun
ATM; automated teller machine
1. ATM; automated teller machine; cash machine
an electronic banking machine that allows customers to withdraw cash, check balances, and perform other banking transactions without a teller
ATMでお金(かね)を下(お)ろす。
I withdraw money from the ATM.
コンビニのATMは24時間(にじゅうよじかん)使(つか)える。
Convenience store ATMs can be used 24 hours a day.
年末年始(ねんまつねんし)はATMの手数料(てすうりょう)が高(たか)くなることがある。
ATM fees can increase during the year-end and New Year holiday period.

DAILY VOCABULARY:
ATM (エーティーエム) machines are ubiquitous in Japan, found in banks, convenience stores (コンビニ), post offices (郵便局(ゆうびんきょく)), and shopping centers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ATMでお金(かね)を下(お)ろす/引(ひ)き出(だ)す - withdraw money from ATM
  • ATMに入金(にゅうきん)する - deposit money at ATM
  • ATMの手数料(てすうりょう) - ATM fees
  • ATMコーナー - ATM area

PRACTICAL NOTE:
Japanese ATMs often have limited hours at banks but are available 24 hours at convenience stores. International cards may not work at all ATMs—look for machines at post offices (郵便局(ゆうびんきょく)) or 7-Eleven for better compatibility.
