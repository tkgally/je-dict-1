一段(いちだん)

いちだん
noun
one level; one step
1. one level; one step; one tier
A single step or level in a staircase, hierarchy, or gradation.
階段(かいだん)を一段(いちだん)上(あ)がる。
I go up one step on the stairs.
一段(いちだん)上(うえ)のレベルを目指(めざ)す。
I aim for the next level up.
柔道(じゅうどう)で一段(いちだん)を取(と)った。
I obtained first dan in judo.
2. a degree (more/higher)
Used adverbially to mean 'even more' or 'to a greater degree.'
秋(あき)になり一段(いちだん)と寒(さむ)くなった。
It became even colder as autumn arrived.
練習(れんしゅう)して一段(いちだん)と上手(じょうず)になった。
I practiced and became even better.
新製品(しんせいひん)は一段(いちだん)と性能(せいのう)が上(あ)がった。
The new product's performance improved even more.

一段(いちだん) has multiple uses.

GRAMMAR NOTE:
In Japanese grammar, 一段(いちだん)動詞(どうし) (ichidan verbs) are verbs whose stems end in the same sound before conjugation (e.g., 食(た)べる, 見(み)る).

MARTIAL ARTS:
段(だん) is a rank in martial arts like 柔道(じゅうどう), 剣道(けんどう), 空手(からて). 一段(いちだん) (first dan) is typically the first black belt level.

ADVERBIAL USE:
一段(いちだん)と means "even more" or "to a greater degree," emphasizing an increase or improvement.
