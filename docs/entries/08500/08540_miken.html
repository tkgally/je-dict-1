眉間(みけん)

みけん
noun
between the eyebrows; glabella
1. the space between the eyebrows; glabella
the area of the forehead directly above the nose and between the eyebrows
眉間(みけん)にしわを寄(よ)せる。
To furrow one's brow.
彼(かれ)は眉間(みけん)にしわを寄(よ)せて考(かんが)え込(こ)んでいた。
He was deep in thought with his brow furrowed.
ストレスで眉間(みけん)のしわが深(ふか)くなった。
The wrinkle between my eyebrows deepened from stress.

BODY PART:
眉間(みけん) refers specifically to the small area between the eyebrows, above the bridge of the nose.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 眉間(みけん)にしわを寄(よ)せる - to furrow one's brow (showing worry, concentration, or displeasure)
  • 眉間(みけん)を撃(う)つ - to shoot between the eyes (dramatic expression)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese face reading (人相学(にんそうがく)), the 眉間(みけん) is considered an important area that reflects a person's fortune and character.
