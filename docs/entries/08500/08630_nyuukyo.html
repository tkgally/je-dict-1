入居(にゅうきょ)

にゅうきょ
noun, suru-verb
moving in; taking up residence
1. moving in; taking up residence
the act of moving into a new residence, apartment, or facility
来月(らいげつ)入居(にゅうきょ)する予定(よてい)だ。
I'm scheduled to move in next month.
このマンションは入居(にゅうきょ)率(りつ)が高(たか)い。
This apartment building has a high occupancy rate.
新築(しんちく)マンションへの入居(にゅうきょ)開始(かいし)は来年(らいねん)の春(はる)になる。
Move-ins to the new condominium will begin next spring.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 退去(たいきょ) - moving out (opposite)
  • 引(ひ)っ越(こ)し - moving (relocating in general)
  • 転居(てんきょ) - changing residence

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入居(にゅうきょ)審査(しんさ) - tenant screening
  • 入居(にゅうきょ)率(りつ) - occupancy rate
  • 入居(にゅうきょ)条件(じょうけん) - move-in conditions/requirements
  • 入居(にゅうきょ)者(しゃ) - resident, tenant
  • 入居(にゅうきょ)可能(かのう) - move-in ready

CONTEXTS:
Used for apartments, condominiums, nursing homes, dormitories, and other residential facilities. For nursing homes and care facilities, 入居(にゅうきょ) emphasizes taking up residence rather than admission (入院(にゅういん)).
