餡子(あんこ)

あんこ
noun
sweet bean paste; red bean paste
1. sweet bean paste; red bean paste
a sweet paste made from azuki beans, used as a filling in Japanese confections

Daifuku has sweet bean paste inside.

JAPANESE SWEETS (和菓子(わがし)):
餡子(あんこ) is a fundamental ingredient in Japanese confections.

TYPES OF 餡子(あんこ):

  • こしあん - smooth bean paste (strained)
  • つぶあん - chunky bean paste (with visible beans)
  • 白(しろ)あん - white bean paste (from white beans)

SWEETS WITH 餡子(あんこ):

  • 大福(だいふく) - rice cake with bean paste filling
  • 饅頭(まんじゅう) - steamed bun with bean paste
  • 羊羹(ようかん) - sweet bean jelly
  • 鯛焼(たいや)き - fish-shaped cake with bean paste
  • どら焼(や)き - dorayaki (two pancakes with bean paste)
  • 餅(もち) - mochi with bean paste
  • おはぎ/萩(はぎ)の餅(もち) - rice ball covered with bean paste

VARIANT READINGS:

  • あんこ (colloquial)
  • あん (shortened form)
  • 餡(あん) (written form)

NOTE: The 子(こ) in 餡子(あんこ) is a diminutive suffix.
