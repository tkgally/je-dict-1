実行(じっこう)力(りょく)

じっこうりょく
noun
ability to execute; ability to get things done
1. ability to execute; ability to get things done; implementation ability
the ability to carry out plans, decisions, or ideas effectively
彼(かれ)は実行力(じっこうりょく)がある。
He has the ability to get things done.
リーダーには実行力(じっこうりょく)が求(もと)められる。
Leaders are expected to have execution ability.
アイデアはいいが、実行力(じっこうりょく)に欠(か)ける。
The idea is good, but he lacks the ability to implement it.

COMPOUND:
実行(じっこう) (execution/implementation) + 力(りょく) (ability/power)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実行力(じっこうりょく)がある - to have execution ability
  • 実行力(じっこうりょく)に欠(か)ける - to lack execution ability
  • 実行力(じっこうりょく)を高(たか)める - to improve execution ability

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
Highly valued in business and leadership contexts. Often listed in job descriptions and performance evaluations.

RELATED ～力(りょく) COMPOUNDS:

  • 決断力(けつだんりょく) - decisiveness
  • 行動力(こうどうりょく) - ability to take action
  • 判断力(はんだんりょく) - judgment
