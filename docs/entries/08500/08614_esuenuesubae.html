SNS映(ば)え

えすえぬえすばえ
noun
Instagram-worthy; photogenic for social media
1. Instagram-worthy; looks good on social media
something that is visually appealing and suitable for posting on social media to attract attention and likes
SNS映(ば)えする写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る。
Take an Instagram-worthy photo.
このカフェはSNS映(ば)えするスイーツで有名(ゆうめい)だ。
This cafe is famous for its Instagram-worthy sweets.
SNS映(ば)えを意識(いしき)したメニューが増(ふ)えている。
Menus designed for social media appeal are increasing.

MODERN JAPANESE:
SNS映(ば)え became popular in the 2010s with the rise of Instagram and other social media platforms. It was a buzzword of 2017.

SYNONYMS:

  • インスタ映(ば)え - Instagram-worthy (more specific)
  • 映(ば)える - to look good (verb form)
  • フォトジェニック - photogenic

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 料理(りょうり) - food presentation
  • カフェ - cafes with aesthetic interiors
  • 旅行(りょこう)先(さき) - travel destinations
  • 服装(ふくそう) - outfits

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • SNS映(ば)えする - to be Instagram-worthy
  • SNS映(ば)えスポット - Instagram-worthy spot
  • SNS映(ば)え狙(ねら)い - aiming for social media appeal
