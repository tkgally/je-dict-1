TKG

てぃーけーじー
noun
egg over rice
1. tamago kake gohan; egg over rice
an abbreviation for 卵(たまご)かけご飯(はん) (tamago kake gohan), a simple Japanese dish of raw egg mixed with hot rice and soy sauce
朝(あさ)ごはんはTKGにした。
I had TKG for breakfast.
TKGは簡単(かんたん)で美味(おい)しい。
TKG is simple and delicious.
TKG専用(せんよう)の醤油(しょうゆ)を買(か)った。
I bought soy sauce specially made for TKG.

ETYMOLOGY:
TKG is an abbreviation of 卵(たまご)かけご飯(はん) (tamago kake gohan, literally 'egg-topped rice'). The abbreviation became popular on social media and in casual conversation.

THE DISH:
卵(たまご)かけご飯(はん) is a simple comfort food: a raw egg is cracked over hot white rice, mixed, and seasoned with soy sauce. It's eaten for breakfast or as a quick meal.

VARIATIONS:

  • 白身(しろみ)を泡立(あわだ)てる - whipping the egg white for fluffy texture
  • TKG専用(せんよう)醤油(しょうゆ) - soy sauce specially formulated for TKG
  • トッピング: 海苔(のり), ごま, ネギ - common toppings: nori, sesame, green onion

FOOD SAFETY:
In Japan, eggs are processed to be safe for raw consumption. This dish may not be safe with eggs from other countries where different safety standards apply.
