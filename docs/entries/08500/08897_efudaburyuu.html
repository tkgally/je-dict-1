FW

えふだぶりゅー
noun
forward (sports position)
1. forward (sports position)
An attacking player positioned near the opponent's goal, primarily responsible for scoring in soccer, hockey, and other sports.
FWがゴールを決(き)めた。
The forward scored a goal.
彼(かれ)はFWとしてプレーしている。
He plays as a forward.
チームには得点力(とくてんりょく)のあるFWが必要(ひつよう)だ。
The team needs a forward with scoring ability.

FW stands for Forward. It is the standard abbreviation for the attacking position in soccer.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 得点力(とくてんりょく)のあるFW: forward with scoring ability
  • エースFW: ace forward
  • センターFW (CF): center forward
  • FWの選手(せんしゅ): forward player

ALTERNATIVE TERMS:

  • フォワード: forward (full word)
  • ストライカー: striker
  • 攻撃(こうげき)陣(じん): offensive line

SOCCER POSITIONS:

  • GK: goalkeeper
  • DF: defender
  • MF: midfielder
  • FW: forward
