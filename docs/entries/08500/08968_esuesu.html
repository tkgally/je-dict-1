SS

えすえす
noun
screenshot
1. screenshot; screen capture
An abbreviation for 'screenshot,' commonly used in gaming and social media contexts in Japan.
ゲームのSSを撮(と)った。
I took a screenshot of the game.
面白(おもしろ)いシーンをSSで保存(ほぞん)した。
I saved the funny scene as a screenshot.
SSを撮(と)って証拠(しょうこ)として残(のこ)しておこう。
Let's take a screenshot and keep it as evidence.

SS is a common abbreviation in gaming and internet culture in Japan.

PRONUNCIATION:
Often pronounced as エスエス or simply read as スクショ (sukusho), a shortened form of スクリーンショット.

COMMON USAGE:

  • SSを撮(と)る: to take a screenshot
  • SS機能(きのう): screenshot function
  • SS共有(きょうゆう): sharing screenshots

NOTE:
スクショ is more commonly used in casual conversation, while SS is often seen in written form, especially in gaming contexts.
