すげー

すげー
i-adjective
amazing; awesome (casual)
1. amazing; awesome; incredible
casual/slang pronunciation of すごい; expresses strong admiration, surprise, or emphasis; commonly used by young people and in informal contexts
すげー！
Awesome!
このゲーム、すげーおもしろい。
This game is super fun.
あいつ、すげー足(あし)速(はや)いよね。
That guy runs crazy fast, doesn't he?

CASUAL/SLANG:
すげー is a casual pronunciation of すごい, typically used in informal speech among friends, especially by younger speakers and males. The elongated vowel (ー) adds emphasis.

VARIATIONS:

  • すげえ (written with え instead of ー)
  • すっげー (extra emphasis)

REGISTER:
Very casual; avoid in formal situations or with superiors. Use すごい in standard/polite contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • すげー + adjective: すげーかっこいい (super cool)
  • すげー + verb: すげーうまい (incredibly good at)
  • Stand-alone exclamation: すげー！ (Wow!)
