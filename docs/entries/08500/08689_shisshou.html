失笑(しっしょう)

しっしょう
noun, suru verb
involuntary laugh; burst of laughter (at something ridiculous)
1. involuntary laugh; burst of laughter
an uncontrollable laugh that escapes when trying to hold it back, especially at something absurd or inappropriate
思(おも)わず失笑(しっしょう)してしまった。
I couldn't help but burst out laughing.
彼(かれ)の言(い)い訳(わけ)に失笑(しっしょう)が漏(も)れた。
His excuse drew involuntary laughs.
会場(かいじょう)から失笑(しっしょう)が起(お)きた。
Involuntary laughter arose from the audience.

VERB FORM:
失笑(しっしょう)する - to laugh involuntarily, to burst out laughing

ORIGINAL MEANING:
失笑(しっしょう) literally means "losing/escaping laughter" - a laugh that slips out despite trying to hold it back. Traditionally neutral, but often used when something is so absurd one can't help laughing.

COMMON MISUNDERSTANDING:
Some people incorrectly use 失笑(しっしょう) to mean "not laughing" or "a cold response." The correct meaning is an involuntary burst of laughter.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 噴(ふ)き出(だ)す - to burst out laughing
  • 吹(ふ)き出(だ)す - to spurt out (laughter)
  • 爆笑(ばくしょう) - roar of laughter
