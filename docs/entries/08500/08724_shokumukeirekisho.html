職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ)

しょくむけいれきしょ
noun
work history document; professional resume; CV
1. work history document; career summary
a document detailing professional experience and achievements, used for job applications
職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote my work history document.
履歴書(りれきしょ)と職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit your resume and work history document.
転職(てんしょく)活動(かつどう)では職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
The work history document is important for job hunting.

CONTRAST WITH 履歴書(りれきしょ):

  • 履歴書(りれきしょ): Basic personal information, education, and employment dates (standard format)
  • 職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ): Detailed description of job duties, achievements, and skills (free format)

JAPANESE JOB APPLICATION:
When changing jobs (転職(てんしょく)) in Japan, both documents are typically required. The 履歴書(りれきしょ) uses a standardized format, while the 職務(しょくむ)経歴(けいれき)書(しょ) allows candidates to highlight their specific accomplishments.

ABBREVIATION:
職歴(しょくれき) (work history) is the abbreviated form.
