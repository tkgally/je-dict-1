車内(しゃない)放送(ほうそう)

しゃないほうそう
noun
train announcement; in-train announcement
1. train announcement; in-vehicle announcement
audio announcements made inside trains, buses, or other public transportation vehicles, typically announcing stops, transfers, delays, and safety reminders
車内(しゃない)放送(ほうそう)が聞(き)こえにくい。
The train announcement is hard to hear.
車内(しゃない)放送(ほうそう)で次(つぎ)の駅(えき)を確認(かくにん)した。
I confirmed the next station from the train announcement.
車内(しゃない)放送(ほうそう)が日本語(にほんご)と英語(えいご)で流(なが)れる。
Train announcements are made in Japanese and English.

TRANSPORTATION TERM:
車内(しゃない)放送(ほうそう) refers to announcements inside trains and other public transport. Japanese trains are known for detailed and frequent announcements.

TYPICAL ANNOUNCEMENT CONTENT:

  • 次(つぎ)は～駅(えき)です - The next station is ~
  • 乗(の)り換(か)えのご案内(あんない) - transfer information
  • 遅延(ちえん)のお知(し)らせ - delay announcements
  • マナー啓発(けいはつ) - courtesy reminders

RELATED TERMS:

  • 運行状況(うんこうじょうきょう) - service status
  • 遅延証明(ちえんしょうめい) - delay certificate
  • 乗(の)り換(か)え - transfer
  • アナウンス - announcement

NOTE:
Many Japanese train lines now offer multilingual announcements, especially in urban areas.
