いくら何(なん)でも

いくらなんでも
adverb
no matter how; even so; there's a limit
1. no matter how; even so; there's a limit to everything
used to express that something has gone too far or exceeded reasonable limits, often with a sense of criticism or disbelief
いくら何(なん)でも高(たか)すぎる。
No matter how you look at it, it's too expensive.
いくら何(なん)でも、それは失礼(しつれい)だと思(おも)う。
Even so, I think that's rude.
いくら何(なん)でも一人(ひとり)でやるのは無理(むり)がある。
No matter what, there are limits to doing it alone.

STRUCTURE:
いくら (how much) + 何(なん)でも (whatever/anything)

USAGE:

Used to express:

  • Something has exceeded reasonable bounds
  • A limit should be recognized
  • Disbelief or criticism at excess

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いくら何(なん)でも～すぎる - "it's too ... no matter how you look at it"
  • いくら何(なん)でも～は無理(むり)だ - "there's no way ... (even considering everything)"

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • いかに～ても - no matter how much
  • どんなに～ても - no matter how much
  • さすがに - even so (similar nuance of "there's a limit")
