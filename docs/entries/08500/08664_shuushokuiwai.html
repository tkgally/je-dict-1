就職(しゅうしょく)祝(いわ)い

しゅうしょくいわい
noun
job celebration gift; employment congratulation gift
1. job celebration gift; employment congratulation gift
a gift given to celebrate someone starting their first job or career
就職(しゅうしょく)祝(いわ)いに名刺入(めいしい)れをもらった。
I received a business card holder as a job celebration gift.
弟(おとうと)の就職(しゅうしょく)祝(いわ)いに何(なに)を贈(おく)ろうか考(かんが)えている。
I'm thinking about what to give my younger brother for his job celebration gift.
内定(ないてい)をもらった息子(むすこ)に就職(しゅうしょく)祝(いわ)いとしてスーツを買(か)ってあげた。
I bought my son a suit as a job celebration gift when he received his job offer.

TIMING:
Given when someone receives a job offer (内定(ないてい)) or starts their first job, typically in March-April when new graduates enter the workforce.

COMMON GIFTS:

  • スーツ - business suit
  • 名刺入(めいしい)れ - business card holder
  • ペン - quality pen
  • 腕時計(うでどけい) - wristwatch
  • 財布(さいふ) - wallet
  • 現金(げんきん) - cash (typically 10,000-30,000 yen)

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In Japan, starting one's career is a major life milestone. 就職(しゅうしょく)祝(いわ)い often includes practical items for the workplace.
