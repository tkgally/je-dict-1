OJT

おーじぇーてぃー
noun
OJT, on-the-job training
1. on-the-job training
Training conducted while working at the actual job, learning practical skills through hands-on experience.
新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん)のOJTを行(おこな)う。
We conduct on-the-job training for new employees.
OJTで仕事(しごと)を覚(おぼ)える。
I learn the job through on-the-job training.
この会社(かいしゃ)はOJTが充実(じゅうじつ)している。
This company has thorough on-the-job training.

OJT stands for 'On-the-Job Training.' It refers to practical training conducted at the workplace.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • OJTを受(う)ける: to receive on-the-job training
  • OJTを行(おこな)う: to conduct on-the-job training
  • OJT期間(きかん): OJT period
  • OJT担当(たんとう): OJT supervisor

CONTRAST WITH:

  • OFF-JT (Off-the-Job Training): training conducted away from the workplace
  • 座学(ざがく): classroom learning
  • 研修(けんしゅう): training (general term)
