堪(こら)える

こらえる
verb
to endure; to bear; to put up with
1. to endure; to bear; to put up with
To withstand pain, hardship, or unpleasant situations without giving in or expressing it outwardly.
痛(いた)みを堪(こら)えて走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
I endured the pain and kept running.
寒(さむ)さを堪(こら)えて外(そと)で待(ま)った。
I endured the cold and waited outside.
辛(つら)い時期(じき)を堪(こら)えて乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
I endured the difficult period and got through it.
2. to hold back; to suppress (emotions)
To restrain oneself from expressing emotions such as tears, laughter, or anger.
涙(なみだ)を堪(こら)えた。
I held back my tears.
笑(わら)いを堪(こら)えるのが大変(たいへん)だった。
It was hard to hold back my laughter.
怒(いか)りを堪(こら)えて冷静(れいせい)に話(はな)した。
I suppressed my anger and spoke calmly.

堪(こら)える emphasizes internal fortitude and self-control.

KANJI NOTE:
Alternatively written as 耐(こら)える, though 堪(こら)える is more common. Both share the sense of endurance.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 痛(いた)みを堪(こら)える: to endure pain
  • 涙(なみだ)を堪(こら)える: to hold back tears
  • 笑(わら)いを堪(こら)える: to suppress laughter
  • 空腹(くうふく)を堪(こら)える: to endure hunger

RELATED WORDS:

  • 耐(た)える: to endure, withstand (more formal)
  • 我慢(がまん)する: to be patient, put up with
