他力(たりき)

たりき
noun
help from others; external power
1. help from others; external power
power, assistance, or resources from others rather than one's own efforts
他力(たりき)に頼(たよ)ってはいけない。
You shouldn't rely on others' help.
他力(たりき)本願(ほんがん)ではうまくいかない。
Relying on others won't work out well.
自力(じりき)でだめなら他力(たりき)を借(か)りよう。
If I can't do it myself, I'll borrow others' help.
成功(せいこう)は他力(たりき)だけでは得(え)られない。
Success cannot be achieved through others' help alone.

USAGE:
他力(たりき)本願(ほんがん) is a common expression meaning "relying on others" (often with negative connotation in everyday use).

BUDDHIST ORIGIN:
Originally a Buddhist term referring to salvation through Amida Buddha's compassion rather than one's own efforts. In Pure Land Buddhism, 他力(たりき)本願(ほんがん) is a positive concept of relying on Buddha's vow.

CONTRAST:

  • 自力(じりき) - one's own power; self-reliance

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 他力(たりき)本願(ほんがん) - relying on others (often negative in secular usage)
  • 他力(たりき)に頼(たよ)る - to depend on others
