七福神(しちふくじん)

しちふくじん
noun
Seven Lucky Gods
1. Seven Lucky Gods; Seven Gods of Fortune
a group of seven deities from various traditions believed to bring good luck and fortune in Japanese mythology
七福神(しちふくじん)巡(めぐ)りに行(い)く。
To go on a Seven Lucky Gods pilgrimage.
七福神(しちふくじん)の絵(え)は縁起(えんぎ)がいい。
Pictures of the Seven Lucky Gods are considered good luck.
お正月(しょうがつ)に七福神(しちふくじん)を祀(まつ)る神社(じんじゃ)を参拝(さんぱい)した。
I visited shrines enshrining the Seven Lucky Gods on New Year's.

THE SEVEN GODS:
1. 恵比寿(えびす) (Ebisu) - god of fishers and merchants
2. 大黒天(だいこくてん) (Daikokuten) - god of wealth and commerce
3. 毘沙門天(びしゃもんてん) (Bishamonten) - god of warriors
4. 弁財天(べんざいてん) (Benzaiten) - goddess of arts and knowledge
5. 福禄寿(ふくろくじゅ) (Fukurokuju) - god of longevity
6. 寿老人(じゅろうじん) (Jurōjin) - god of longevity
7. 布袋(ほてい) (Hotei) - god of happiness

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
七福神(しちふくじん)巡(めぐ)り is a popular New Year's tradition where people visit temples and shrines dedicated to each of the seven gods.

SYMBOLISM:

  • 宝船(たからぶね) - treasure ship, often depicted carrying all seven gods
  • 縁起物(えんぎもの) - good luck charm
