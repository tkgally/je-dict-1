時間割(じかんわり)

じかんわり
noun
class schedule; timetable
1. class schedule; timetable
a schedule showing what classes or subjects are held at what times
時間割(じかんわり)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the class schedule.
月曜日(げつようび)の時間割(じかんわり)は何(なん)ですか。
What is the schedule for Monday?
新学期(しんがっき)から時間割(じかんわり)が変(か)わった。
The schedule changed from the new semester.

EDUCATION VOCABULARY:
時間割(じかんわり) is the standard term for a school class schedule or timetable showing which subjects are taught when.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 時間割(じかんわり)を作(つく)る - to create a schedule
  • 時間割(じかんわり)を変更(へんこう)する - to change the schedule
  • 時間割(じかんわり)表(ひょう) - schedule chart/table
  • 週(しゅう)の時間割(じかんわり) - weekly schedule

SCHOOL CONTEXT:
Japanese schools typically post 時間割(じかんわり) in classrooms. Students learn to manage their materials based on their daily schedule.
