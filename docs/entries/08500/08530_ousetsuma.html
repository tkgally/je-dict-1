応接間(おうせつま)

おうせつま
noun
reception room; parlor; drawing room
1. reception room; parlor; drawing room
a room in a house used for receiving and entertaining guests
お客(きゃく)さんを応接間(おうせつま)に通(とお)した。
I showed the guest to the reception room.
応接間(おうせつま)でお茶(ちゃ)を出(だ)した。
I served tea in the parlor.
立派(りっぱ)な応接間(おうせつま)のある家(いえ)だ。
It's a house with a fine reception room.
応接間(おうせつま)には革張(かわば)りのソファがある。
There's a leather sofa in the drawing room.

USAGE:
A formal room for receiving guests, distinct from everyday living spaces. More common in older or larger Japanese homes and Western-style houses.

TYPICAL FEATURES:

  • Sofa set
  • Coffee table
  • Formal decor

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 応接(おうせつ)セット - reception room furniture set
  • 居間(いま) - living room
  • 客間(きゃくま) - guest room
  • リビング - living room (casual, modern)
