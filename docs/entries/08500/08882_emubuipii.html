MVP

えむぶいぴー
noun
MVP, most valuable player
1. MVP, most valuable player
An award given to the best-performing player in a game, season, or tournament.
MVPを獲得(かくとく)した。
He won the MVP award.
彼女(かのじょ)はシーズンMVPに選(えら)ばれた。
She was selected as the season MVP.
決勝(けっしょう)戦(せん)でMVPに輝(かがや)いた選手(せんしゅ)がインタビューに答(こた)えた。
The player who won MVP in the final answered interview questions.

MVP stands for Most Valuable Player. It's a common sports award.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • MVPを獲得(かくとく)する: to win MVP
  • MVPに選(えら)ばれる: to be selected as MVP
  • MVPに輝(かがや)く: to shine as MVP
  • シーズンMVP: season MVP
  • 試合(しあい)MVP: game MVP
  • 日本(にほん)シリーズMVP: Japan Series MVP

JAPANESE SPORTS CONTEXT:

  • NPB (日本(にほん)プロ野球(やきゅう)): MVP for best player of the season
  • Jリーグ: MVP for best soccer player
  • 高校(こうこう)野球(やきゅう): MVP for Koshien tournament
