一本(いっぽん)

いっぽん
noun
one (long cylindrical object); one point (judo); single-mindedness
1. one (long cylindrical object)
The counter reading for one of something long and thin, such as bottles, pens, trees, or roads.
ビールを一本(いっぽん)ください。
Please give me one beer.
ペンが一本(いっぽん)足(た)りない。
One pen is missing.
道(みち)は一本(いっぽん)だけだ。
There's only one road.
2. ippon, full point (judo/martial arts)
A full point in judo or other martial arts that wins the match instantly.
一本(いっぽん)を取(と)った。
He scored an ippon.
見事(みごと)な一本(いっぽん)勝(が)ちだった。
It was a splendid ippon victory.
審判(しんぱん)が一本(いっぽん)を宣告(せんこく)した。
The referee declared ippon.
3. single-mindedness, one track
A focus on one thing, often used in compound expressions.
一本(いっぽん)気(き)な性格(せいかく)だ。
He has a single-minded personality.
芸(げい)の道(みち)一本(いっぽん)で生(い)きてきた。
I've lived by focusing solely on my art.
野球(やきゅう)一本(いっぽん)でやってきた。
I've devoted myself entirely to baseball.

一本(いっぽん) is the reading for "one" with the counter 本(ほん), used for long cylindrical objects.

COUNTER USAGE (Sense 1):

  • ビール一本(いっぽん): one beer (bottle/can)
  • 傘(かさ)一本(いっぽん): one umbrella
  • 木(き)一本(いっぽん): one tree
  • 電話(でんわ)一本(いっぽん): one phone call

JUDO USAGE (Sense 2):

  • 一本(いっぽん): full point (instant win)
  • 技(わざ)あり: half point
  • 一本(いっぽん)勝(が)ち: ippon victory

COMPOUND EXPRESSIONS (Sense 3):

  • 一本(いっぽん)気(き): single-mindedness, straightforwardness
  • 一本(いっぽん)道(みち): single path, one-track
  • 一本(いっぽん)立(だ)ち: standing on one's own, independence
