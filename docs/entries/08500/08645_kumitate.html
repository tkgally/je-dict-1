組(く)み立(た)て

くみたて
noun
assembly; construction; putting together
1. assembly; construction; putting together
the process of fitting parts together to create a whole product
家具(かぐ)の組(く)み立(た)てを自分(じぶん)でした。
I assembled the furniture myself.
この製品(せいひん)は組(く)み立(た)て式(しき)だ。
This product requires assembly.
組(く)み立(た)て説明書(せつめいしょ)を読(よ)んでから始(はじ)めた。
I started after reading the assembly instructions.

RELATED VERB:
組(く)み立(た)てる - to assemble, to put together (compound verb)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 組(く)み立(た)て式(しき) - requiring assembly, build-it-yourself
  • 組(く)み立(た)て工場(こうじょう) - assembly plant
  • 組(く)み立(た)て{ライン} - assembly line
  • 組(く)み立(た)て説明書(せつめいしょ) - assembly instructions
  • 組(く)み立(た)て作業(さぎょう) - assembly work

CONTEXTS:

  • IKEA-style furniture
  • Manufacturing
  • DIY projects
  • Model kits (プラモデル)

OPPOSITE:
