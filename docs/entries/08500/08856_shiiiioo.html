CEO

しーいーおー
noun
CEO, chief executive officer
1. CEO, chief executive officer
The highest-ranking executive in a company, responsible for major corporate decisions and overall operations.
新(あたら)しいCEOが就任(しゅうにん)した。
A new CEO took office.
CEOが記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で経営方針(けいえいほうしん)を発表(はっぴょう)した。
The CEO announced the management policy at a press conference.
テック企業(きぎょう)の若(わか)いCEOが注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めている。
Young CEOs of tech companies are attracting attention.

CEO (Chief Executive Officer) is commonly used in Japanese business contexts, especially for foreign companies and startups. Traditional Japanese companies often use 社長(しゃちょう) (president) or 代表取締役(だいひょうとりしまりやく).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • CEO就任(しゅうにん): CEO appointment
  • CEO兼(けん)創業者(そうぎょうしゃ): CEO and founder
  • 共同(きょうどう)CEO: co-CEO

RELATED TITLES:

  • CFO (Chief Financial Officer): 最高(さいこう)財務(ざいむ)責任者(せきにんしゃ)
  • COO (Chief Operating Officer): 最高(さいこう)執行(しっこう)責任者(せきにんしゃ)
  • CTO (Chief Technology Officer): 最高(さいこう)技術(ぎじゅつ)責任者(せきにんしゃ)

CEO is written in the English alphabet in Japanese text.
