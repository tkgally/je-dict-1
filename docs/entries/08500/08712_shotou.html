初冬(しょとう)

しょとう
noun
early winter; beginning of winter
1. early winter; beginning of winter
the early part of winter, typically referring to November to early December in Japan
初冬(しょとう)の寒(さむ)さが身(み)にしみる。
The early winter cold pierces the body.
初冬(しょとう)には紅葉(こうよう)がまだ残(のこ)っている。
In early winter, the autumn leaves are still lingering.
初冬(しょとう)の候(こう)、皆様(みなさま)いかがお過(す)ごしでしょうか。
In this early winter season, how is everyone doing?

COMPOUND:
初(しょ) (beginning/first) + 冬(とう) (winter)

TIME FRAME:
Typically refers to November to early December in Japan, when autumn transitions to winter.

FORMAL GREETINGS:
初冬(しょとう)の候(こう) is used in formal letters and correspondence during early winter.

SEASONAL DIVISIONS (季語(きご)):

Japanese traditionally divides winter (冬(ふゆ)) into:

  • 初冬(しょとう) - early winter (November)
  • 仲冬(ちゅうとう) - mid-winter (December)
  • 晩冬(ばんとう) - late winter (January)

RELATED SEASONAL TERMS:

  • 晩秋(ばんしゅう) - late autumn
  • 厳冬(げんとう) - severe winter
  • 真冬(まふゆ) - midwinter
