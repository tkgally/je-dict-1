マタハラ

またはら
noun
maternity harassment
1. maternity harassment; pregnancy discrimination
harassment or discrimination against women in the workplace due to pregnancy, childbirth, or childcare responsibilities; abbreviation of マタニティハラスメント
マタハラは違法(いほう)です。
Maternity harassment is illegal.
妊娠(にんしん)を報告(ほうこく)したらマタハラを受(う)けた。
I experienced maternity harassment after reporting my pregnancy.
会社(かいしゃ)はマタハラ防止(ぼうし)の研修(けんしゅう)を行(おこな)っている。
The company conducts training to prevent maternity harassment.

HARASSMENT VOCABULARY:

マタハラ is part of a series of workplace harassment terms in Japanese:

  • パワハラ (power harassment) - abuse of authority
  • セクハラ (sexual harassment)
  • マタハラ (maternity harassment)
  • モラハラ (moral harassment) - psychological abuse

LEGAL CONTEXT:
Japan's 2017 amendments to the Equal Employment Opportunity Law strengthened protections against maternity harassment, requiring employers to take preventive measures.

EXAMPLES OF マタハラ:

  • Pressure to resign due to pregnancy
  • Demotion after returning from maternity leave
  • Negative comments about pregnancy or childcare
