恥(は)ずかしがり

はずかしがり
noun
shy person; bashful person
1. shy person; bashful person
a person who tends to feel embarrassed or self-conscious easily
彼(かれ)は恥(は)ずかしがりで人前(ひとまえ)で話(はな)すのが苦手(にがて)だ。
He's shy and doesn't like speaking in front of people.
恥(は)ずかしがりな性格(せいかく)を克服(こくふく)したい。
I want to overcome my shy personality.
うちの子(こ)は恥(は)ずかしがりで、初対面(しょたいめん)の人(ひと)と話(はな)せない。
My child is shy and can't talk to people they meet for the first time.

STRUCTURE:
恥(は)ずかしい (embarrassed/shy) + がり (tendency suffix)

VARIATIONS:

  • 恥(は)ずかしがり - shy person (noun)
  • 恥(は)ずかしがり屋(や) - shy person (with 屋(や) suffix, slightly more endearing)
  • 恥(は)ずかしがりな - shy (na-adjective form)

USAGE:

Can function as a noun or as a na-adjective modifier:

  • 恥(は)ずかしがりな子(こ) - a shy child
  • 恥(は)ずかしがりだ - is shy

SIMILAR PERSONALITY TYPES:

  • 照(て)れ屋(や) - easily embarrassed person
  • 内気(うちき) - introverted, timid
  • 引(ひ)っ込(こ)み思案(じあん) - reserved, reticent

RELATED ～がり COMPOUNDS:

  • 寂(さび)しがり - person who easily gets lonely
  • 怖(こわ)がり - timid/easily scared person
