食(た)べ慣(な)れる

たべなれる
verb (ichidan)
to become used to eating
1. to become used to eating; to get accustomed to eating
to develop a taste for or comfort with eating a particular food through repeated experience
納豆(なっとう)は食(た)べ慣(な)れると美味(おい)しい。
Natto is delicious once you get used to eating it.
日本(にほん)の食(しょく)べ物(もの)に食(た)べ慣(な)れた。
I've gotten used to eating Japanese food.
辛(から)いものは食(た)べ慣(な)れていないので苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm not used to eating spicy food, so I don't handle it well.

COMPOUND VERB:
食(た)べる (to eat) + 慣(な)れる (to become accustomed)

CONJUGATION:

  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れる (dictionary form)
  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れた (past)
  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れている (progressive - accustomed state)
  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れていない (not accustomed)

PATTERN:

~慣(な)れる compound verbs (become used to ~):

  • 聞(き)き慣(な)れる - become used to hearing
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れる - become used to living
  • 見慣(みな)れる - become used to seeing
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる - become used to using

RELATED:

  • 慣(な)れる - to get used to
  • 慣(な)れ親(した)しむ - to become familiar with
