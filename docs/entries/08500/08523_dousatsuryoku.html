洞察力(どうさつりょく)

どうさつりょく
noun
insight; discernment
1. insight; discernment
the ability to perceive the true nature of things or understand situations deeply
洞察力(どうさつりょく)が鋭(するど)い。
He has keen insight.
洞察力(どうさつりょく)のある分析(ぶんせき)だった。
It was an insightful analysis.
彼女(かのじょ)は人(ひと)の心(こころ)を見抜(みぬ)く洞察力(どうさつりょく)がある。
She has the insight to see through people's hearts.
優(すぐ)れた洞察力(どうさつりょく)で問題(もんだい)の本質(ほんしつ)を見抜(みぬ)いた。
With excellent insight, he perceived the essence of the problem.

USAGE:
Refers to deep perception and understanding that goes beyond surface appearances. Valued in analytical and leadership roles.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく)が鋭(するど)い - to have sharp insight
  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく)に富(と)む - to be rich in insight
  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく)を養(やしな)う - to cultivate insight

RELATED ABILITIES:

  • 観察力(かんさつりょく) - observation skills
  • 分析力(ぶんせきりょく) - analytical ability
  • 直感力(ちょっかんりょく) - intuition
