狼狽(ろうばい)

ろうばい
noun
confusion; panic; fluster
1. confusion; panic; fluster
a state of agitation and confusion, often leading to irrational behavior
突然(とつぜん)の質問(しつもん)に狼狽(ろうばい)した。
I was flustered by the sudden question.
狼狽(ろうばい)して何(なに)を言(い)っているか分(わ)からなくなった。
I became so flustered that I didn't know what I was saying.
彼(かれ)の狼狽(ろうばい)ぶりを見(み)て何(なに)かあったと察(さっ)した。
Seeing how flustered he was, I sensed something had happened.

VERB FORM:
狼狽(ろうばい)する - to be flustered, to panic

ETYMOLOGY:
From Chinese. 狼(ろう) (wolf) and 狽(ばい) (legendary wolf-like creature) were said to be inseparable. When apart, both would panic - hence the meaning of confusion.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 慌(あわ)てる - to panic, to be flustered (more common)
  • 動揺(どうよう)する - to be shaken, disturbed
  • うろたえる - to be confused, lose composure

NUANCE:
狼狽(ろうばい) is more literary and formal than 慌(あわ)てる.
