世話(せわ)好(ず)き

せわずき
noun
person who likes to help others; meddlesome person
1. person who likes to help others; someone who enjoys taking care of others
a person who naturally tends to help and take care of people around them
彼女(かのじょ)は世話好(せわず)きで頼(たよ)りになる。
She likes helping others and is dependable.
世話好(せわず)きな性格(せいかく)で、困(こま)っている人(ひと)を見(み)ると放(ほう)っておけない。
Having a helpful nature, I can't leave someone in trouble alone.
祖母(そぼ)は世話好(せわず)きで、いつも近所(きんじょ)の子供(こども)の面倒(めんどう)を見(み)ている。
My grandmother likes to help out and always looks after the neighborhood children.
世話好(せわず)きな人(ひと)は良(よ)くも悪(わる)くも目立(めだ)つ。
People who like to help stand out, for better or worse.
2. busybody; meddlesome person
someone who interferes in others' affairs, often unwanted (negative nuance)
彼(かれ)は世話好(せわず)きで余計(よけい)なお節介(せっかい)が多(おお)い。
He's meddlesome and often gives unwanted help.
世話好(せわず)きも度(ど)を過(す)ぎると迷惑(めいわく)になる。
Being overly helpful can become a nuisance.
世話好(せわず)きな人(ひと)は良(よ)くも悪(わる)くも目立(めだ)つ。
People who like to help stand out, for better or worse.

COMPOUND:
世話(せわ) (care/help) + 好(ず)き (liking/fond of)

NUANCE:
Can be positive (caring, helpful) or negative (meddlesome, nosy) depending on context. When excessive, it can be seen as お節介(せっかい) (unsolicited interference).

GRAMMAR:

Can be used as a noun or a na-adjective:

  • 世話好(せわず)きな人(ひと) - a helpful/meddlesome person
  • 世話好(せわず)きである - to like helping others

SIMILAR ～好(ず)き COMPOUNDS:

  • 甘党(あまとう)/辛党(からとう) - person who likes sweets/spicy food
  • お人好(ひとよ)し - good-natured person
  • 面倒(めんどう)見(み)がいい - takes good care of others
