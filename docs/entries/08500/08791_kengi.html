建(けん)議(ぎ)

けんぎ
noun, suru-verb
proposal; recommendation
1. formal proposal; recommendation; suggestion
a formal proposal or recommendation made to a person of authority or an organization, often in business or government contexts
新(あたら)しい政策(せいさく)を建(けん)議(ぎ)する。
To propose a new policy.
委員(いいん)会(かい)は社長(しゃちょう)に建(けん)議(ぎ)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
The committee submitted a proposal to the president.
予算(よさん)削減(さくげん)について建(けん)議(ぎ)がなされた。
A recommendation was made regarding budget cuts.

FORMAL REGISTER:

建(けん)議(ぎ) is a formal word typically used in:

  • Government/administrative contexts
  • Business/corporate settings
  • Official recommendations to superiors

COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建(けん)議(ぎ) - formal proposal (to authority)
  • 提案(ていあん) - proposal, suggestion (general)
  • 提言(ていげん) - recommendation, proposal (formal)
  • 意見(いけん) - opinion (general)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 建(けん)議(ぎ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する - to submit a proposal
  • 建(けん)議(ぎ)書(しょ) - written proposal/recommendation
  • 建(けん)議(ぎ)を受(う)け入(い)れる - to accept a proposal
