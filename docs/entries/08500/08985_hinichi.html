日日(ひにち)

ひにち
noun
date; day (of a scheduled event)
1. date; day (of a scheduled event)
A specific date or day, often referring to when an event is scheduled or when something occurred.
会議(かいぎ)の日日(ひにち)を決(き)めよう。
Let's decide on the date for the meeting.
日日(ひにち)は後(あと)で連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll let you know the date later.
日日(ひにち)が合(あ)わなくて参加(さんか)できなかった。
The date didn't work for me, so I couldn't participate.

日日(ひにち) is commonly used when discussing scheduling or when something is dated.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日日(ひにち)を決(き)める: to decide on a date
  • 日日(ひにち)が合(あ)う: the date works/matches
  • 日日(ひにち)を変更(へんこう)する: to change the date
  • 日日(ひにち)未定(みてい): date undecided (TBD)

COMPARISON:

  • 日日(ひにち): date (scheduling focus)
  • 日付(ひづけ): date (as on a document or calendar)
  • 日程(にってい): schedule, itinerary

NOTE:
Also written as 日(ひ)にち using the same reading.
