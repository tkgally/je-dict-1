寝覚(ねざ)め

ねざめ
noun
waking up; quality of awakening
1. the act or quality of waking up
describes how one wakes up, whether refreshed or groggy
寝覚(ねざ)めがいい。
I wake up feeling refreshed.
お酒(さけ)を飲(の)みすぎると寝覚(ねざ)めが悪(わる)い。
If I drink too much, I wake up feeling bad.
早(はや)く寝(ね)たので寝覚(ねざ)めがすっきりした。
I went to bed early, so I woke up feeling refreshed.

SLEEP VOCABULARY:
寝覚(ねざ)め is the nominalized form of 寝覚(ねざ)める (to wake up from sleep). It describes the quality of waking up.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 寝覚(ねざ)めがいい/よい - to wake up feeling good/refreshed
  • 寝覚(ねざ)めが悪(わる)い - to wake up feeling bad/groggy
  • 寝覚(ねざ)めがすっきりする - to wake up feeling refreshed

IDIOM:

寝覚(ねざ)めが悪(わる)い can also mean "to feel guilty" or "to have something on one's conscience."

  • そんなことをしたら寝覚(ねざ)めが悪(わる)い - Doing such a thing would weigh on my conscience.

CONTRAST WITH 寝(ね)つき:

  • 寝(ね)つき - how well you fall asleep
  • 寝覚(ねざ)め - how well you wake up

  • 目覚(めざ)め - awakening (more general)
