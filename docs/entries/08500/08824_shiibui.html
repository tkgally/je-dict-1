CV

しーぶい
noun
character voice; voice actor
1. character voice; voice actor (for anime/games)
abbreviation for 'character voice', referring to voice actors who provide voices for anime, game, or other fictional characters
このキャラのCVは誰(だれ)?
Who's the voice actor for this character?
主人公(しゅじんこう)のCVが発表(はっぴょう)された。
The voice actor for the protagonist was announced.
好(す)きな声優(せいゆう)がCVを担当(たんとう)しているアニメだ。
It's an anime where my favorite voice actor does the character voice.

USAGE:
CV is commonly used in anime and game contexts when crediting or discussing who voices a character. It typically appears as 'CV: [voice actor name]' in credits.

EXAMPLE CREDIT FORMAT:
鈴木(すずき)一郎(いちろう)(CV: 山田(やまだ)太郎(たろう))
Suzuki Ichiro (CV: Yamada Taro)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 声優(せいゆう) - voice actor (general term)
  • 吹(ふ)き替(か)え - dubbing
  • 収録(しゅうろく) - recording (session)
  • キャスト - cast
  • キャスティング - casting

NOTE:
In English, CV typically means 'curriculum vitae' (resume), but in Japanese otaku contexts, it specifically refers to character voice/voice actor.
