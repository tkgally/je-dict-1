泊(と)まりがけ

とまりがけ
noun
overnight stay; staying overnight
1. overnight stay; staying overnight somewhere
going somewhere with the intention of staying overnight, rather than returning the same day
泊(と)まりがけで温泉(おんせん)に行(い)く。
I'm going to the hot springs for an overnight stay.
泊(と)まりがけの出張(しゅっちょう)が入(はい)った。
I have an overnight business trip.
友人(ゆうじん)の家(いえ)に泊(と)まりがけで遊(あそ)びに行(い)った。
I went to stay overnight at my friend's house.

STRUCTURE:
泊(と)まり (staying) + がけ (in the process of/about to)

USAGE:

  • 泊(と)まりがけで - with an overnight stay (adverbial use)
  • 泊(と)まりがけの～ - ～ involving an overnight stay

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 出張(しゅっちょう) (business trip)
  • 旅行(りょこう) (travel)
  • 温泉(おんせん) (hot springs)
  • 友人(ゆうじん)の家(いえ) (friend's house)

CONTRAST:

  • 泊(と)まりがけ - overnight stay (planned)
  • 日帰(ひがえ)り - day trip (returning same day)
  • 外泊(がいはく) - staying out overnight (often unplanned)
