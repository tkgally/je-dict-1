それじゃ

それじゃ
conjunction, interjection
well then; in that case
1. well then; in that case; so
casual contraction of それでは; used to transition to a new topic, make a conclusion, or indicate departure
それじゃ、また明日(あした)。
Well then, see you tomorrow.
それじゃ、始(はじ)めましょう。
Alright, let's begin.
それじゃダメだよ。
That won't do / That's no good.

CASUAL CONTRACTION:

それじゃ is a casual contraction of それでは:

  • それでは (formal) → それじゃ (casual)
  • Even more casual: じゃ, じゃあ

USAGES:
1. DEPARTURE: それじゃ、帰(かえ)ります (Well, I'm heading home)
2. TRANSITION: それじゃ、次(つぎ)の話題(わだい)に (So, on to the next topic)
3. CONSEQUENCE: それじゃ困(こま)る (That would be a problem)
4. STARTING: それじゃ、始(はじ)めよう (Alright, let's start)

FORMALITY SCALE:

  • それでは (formal)
  • それじゃ (casual)
  • じゃあ / じゃ (very casual)
