ガラケー

がらけー
noun
flip phone; feature phone
1. flip phone; feature phone; traditional mobile phone
A traditional mobile phone with physical buttons, as opposed to a smartphone. The term comes from 'Galapagos keitai' (Galapagos mobile phone), referring to Japan's uniquely evolved phone market.
祖父(そふ)はまだガラケーを使(つか)っている。
My grandfather still uses a flip phone.
ガラケーからスマホに機種変更(きしゅへんこう)した。
I changed from a flip phone to a smartphone.
ガラケーは電池(でんち)が長持(ながも)ちする。
Flip phones have long battery life.

ガラケー is short for ガラパゴス携帯(けいたい) (Galapagos mobile phone).

ETYMOLOGY:
The term references the Galapagos Islands, where species evolved uniquely due to isolation. Japanese feature phones similarly evolved with unique features (mobile payments, TV, etc.) that didn't spread globally.

COMPARISON:

  • ガラケー: traditional feature phone
  • スマホ: smartphone
  • ガラホ: a hybrid with flip phone form but smartphone OS

NUANCE:
Some people still prefer ガラケー for their simplicity, long battery life, and durability. The term is slightly nostalgic and may carry a nuance of being old-fashioned.
