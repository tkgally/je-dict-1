再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)

さいにゅうか
noun, verb (suru)
restocking; back in stock
1. restocking; replenishment of stock
receiving new inventory of a previously sold-out item; the item becoming available again for purchase
人気(にんき)商品(しょうひん)が再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)した。
The popular item is back in stock.
再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)のお知(し)らせを設定(せってい)する。
Set up a notification for when it's restocked.
再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)の予定(よてい)は未定(みてい)です。
The restocking schedule is undetermined.

SHOPPING/RETAIL TERM:
再(さい)入荷(にゅうか) is commonly seen in online shopping and retail contexts when previously sold-out items become available again.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)する - to be restocked
  • 再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)のお知(し)らせ - restock notification
  • 再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)待(ま)ち - waiting for restock
  • 再(さい)入荷(にゅうか)未定(みてい) - restock date undetermined

RELATED TERMS:

  • 入荷(にゅうか) - stock arrival, receiving goods
  • 在庫(ざいこ) - inventory, stock
  • 品切(しなぎ)れ - sold out
  • 完売(かんばい) - sold out completely
