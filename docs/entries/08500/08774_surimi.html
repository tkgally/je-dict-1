すり身(み)

すりみ
noun
fish paste; surimi
1. fish paste; surimi; ground fish meat
minced or ground fish paste used as a base ingredient for various processed seafood products like kamaboko, chikuwa, and imitation crab
すり身(み)で団子(だんご)を作(つく)る。
Make dumplings from fish paste.
カニカマはすり身(み)から作(つく)られる。
Imitation crab is made from surimi.
新鮮(しんせん)な魚(さかな)をすり身(み)にする。
Turn fresh fish into paste.

FOOD TERM:
すり身(み) is the base ingredient for many traditional Japanese fish products. The word has been adopted internationally as 'surimi.'

PRODUCTS MADE FROM すり身(み):

  • 蒲鉾(かまぼこ) - kamaboko (fish cake)
  • ちくわ - chikuwa (tube-shaped fish cake)
  • さつま揚(あ)げ - satsuma-age (fried fish cake)
  • カニカマ - imitation crab
  • 魚肉(ぎょにく)ソーセージ - fish sausage
  • はんぺん - hanpen (white fish cake)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すり身(み)にする - to grind into paste
  • 魚(さかな)のすり身(み) - fish paste
  • 白身(しろみ)魚(ざかな)のすり身(み) - white fish surimi

PROCESS:
魚(さかな)の身(み)をすりつぶす → washing → mixing → shaping
