麗々(れいれい)しい

れいれいしい
i-adjective
formal; ceremonious; showy
1. formal; ceremonious; ostentatious; showy
Describes something done with excessive formality, ceremony, or showiness, often to the point of being stiff or pretentious.
麗々(れいれい)しい式典(しきてん)だった。
It was an ostentatiously formal ceremony.
麗々(れいれい)しく発表(はっぴょう)する必要(ひつよう)はない。
There's no need to announce it with such fanfare.
彼(かれ)は麗々(れいれい)しい肩書(かたが)きを並(なら)べて自己紹介(じこしょうかい)した。
He introduced himself by ostentatiously listing his impressive titles.

麗々(れいれい)しい often carries a nuance of excessive formality or showiness that may seem unnecessary or pretentious.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 麗々(れいれい)しく飾(かざ)る: to decorate ostentatiously
  • 麗々(れいれい)しい挨拶(あいさつ): overly formal greeting
  • 麗々(れいれい)しく発表(はっぴょう)する: to announce with great fanfare

REGISTER:
This is a formal, literary word. In everyday speech, 大袈裟(おおげさ)な or 派手(はで)な are more common alternatives.
