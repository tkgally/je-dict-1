NGO

えぬじーおー
noun
NGO, non-governmental organization
1. NGO, non-governmental organization
A non-profit organization that operates independently from governments, typically engaged in humanitarian, environmental, or social causes.
NGOで働(はたら)いている。
I work for an NGO.
このNGOは途上国(とじょうこく)の支援(しえん)活動(かつどう)をしている。
This NGO provides support activities in developing countries.
NGOへの寄付(きふ)は税金(ぜいきん)控除(こうじょ)の対象(たいしょう)になることがある。
Donations to NGOs may be eligible for tax deductions.

NGO (Non-Governmental Organization) is commonly used in Japan for organizations involved in international aid, environmental protection, and human rights.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • NGO団体(だんたい): NGO organization
  • NGO活動(かつどう): NGO activities
  • 国際(こくさい)NGO: international NGO

RELATED TERMS:

  • NPO (特定非営利活動法人(とくていひえいりかつどうほうじん)): Often used for domestic non-profits in Japan
  • In Japanese usage, NGO tends to refer to organizations with international focus, while NPO is used for domestic non-profits

NGO is written in the English alphabet in Japanese text.
