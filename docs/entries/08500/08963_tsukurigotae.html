作(つく)りごたえ

つくりごたえ
noun
satisfying to make; rewarding to create
1. the satisfaction of making something; worth the effort to create
Describes the feeling of satisfaction when creating something that requires skill or effort, or the quality of being worth the effort to make.
この料理(りょうり)は作(つく)りごたえがある。
This dish is satisfying to make.
手芸(しゅげい)キットは作(つく)りごたえ十分(じゅうぶん)だ。
The craft kit is thoroughly satisfying to make.
作(つく)りごたえのある大作(たいさく)プラモデルに挑戦(ちょうせん)した。
I took on a large-scale model kit that would be satisfying to build.
