自力(じりき)

じりき
noun
one's own power; by oneself
1. one's own power; one's own efforts
the ability or effort to accomplish something without help from others
自力(じりき)で解決(かいけつ)した。
I solved it by myself.
自力(じりき)で歩(ある)けるようになった。
I became able to walk on my own.
自力(じりき)で会社(かいしゃ)を立(た)ち上(あ)げた。
I started a company on my own.
自力(じりき)では限界(げんかい)があるから、助(たす)けを求(もと)めた。
There are limits to what I can do alone, so I asked for help.

USAGE:
Used as a noun often followed by で to mean "by oneself" or "through one's own efforts."

BUDDHIST ORIGIN:
In Buddhist terminology, 自力(じりき) refers to achieving enlightenment through one's own efforts, contrasted with 他力(たりき) (reliance on Amida Buddha's power).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自力(じりき)で - by oneself
  • 自力(じりき)更生(こうせい) - self-rehabilitation
  • 自力(じりき)救済(きゅうさい) - self-help

CONTRAST:

  • 他力(たりき) - help from others; reliance on external power
