始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)

しはつでんしゃ
noun
first train (of the day)
1. the first train of the day
the earliest train service on a particular route or line
始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)に乗(の)って空港(くうこう)に向(む)かった。
I took the first train to head to the airport.
始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)は5時(じ)に出(で)る。
The first train leaves at 5 o'clock.
終電(しゅうでん)を逃(のが)して始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)を待(ま)った。
I missed the last train and waited for the first train.

TRANSPORTATION VOCABULARY:
始発電車(しはつでんしゃ) refers to the first train of the day on a particular line or route.

SHORT FORM:
Often shortened to 始発(しはつ).

TYPICAL TIMES:
始発電車(しはつでんしゃ) usually runs between 4:30-5:30 AM depending on the line.

WHO USES IT:

  • Early morning workers
  • Travelers heading to airports
  • People who missed the last train and waited overnight
  • Early risers wanting to avoid crowds

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)に乗(の)る - to catch the first train
  • 始発電車(しはつでんしゃ)を待(ま)つ - to wait for the first train
  • 始発(しはつ)で帰(かえ)る - to go home on the first train (after staying out all night)

RELATED:

  • 終電(しゅうでん) - last train
  • 始発(しはつ) - first train (short form); first departure
  • 通勤電車(つうきんでんしゃ) - commuter train
  • 早朝(そうちょう) - early morning
