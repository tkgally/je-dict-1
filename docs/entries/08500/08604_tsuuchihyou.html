通知表(つうちひょう)

つうちひょう
noun
report card; school grades report
1. report card; academic progress report
a document issued by schools showing a student's grades and evaluations for each subject
通知表(つうちひょう)をもらう。
Receive a report card.
通知表(つうちひょう)を親(おや)に見(み)せる。
Show the report card to my parents.
今学期(こんがっき)の通知表(つうちひょう)は成績(せいせき)が上(あ)がっていた。
My grades had improved on this term's report card.

JAPANESE EDUCATION SYSTEM:
通知表(つうちひょう) is typically given at the end of each term (学期(がっき)).

SYNONYMS:

  • 成績表(せいせきひょう) - grades report
  • 通信簿(つうしんぼ) - report card (older term)

GRADING SYSTEMS:

  • Elementary school: Often 3-level (よくできる/できる/もうすこし or ◎/○/△)
  • Junior high: Often 5-level (1-5)
  • High school: Often 10-point scale

CONTENTS:

  • 教科(きょうか)の成績(せいせき) - subject grades
  • 出席日数(しゅっせきにっすう) - attendance record
  • 担任(たんにん)のコメント - homeroom teacher's comments
  • 行動(こうどう)の記録(きろく) - behavior record
