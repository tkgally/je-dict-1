一泊(いっぱく)

いっぱく
noun
one night's stay, overnight stay
1. one night's stay
Staying somewhere for one night, typically at a hotel or inn.
一泊(いっぱく)いくらですか。
How much is it for one night?
温泉(おんせん)で一泊(いっぱく)した。
I stayed one night at a hot spring inn.
一泊(いっぱく)二(に)日(にち)の旅行(りょこう)に出(で)かけた。
I went on a one-night, two-day trip.

一泊(いっぱく) is used when counting overnight stays at accommodations.

COMMON ACCOMMODATION TERMS:

  • 一泊(いっぱく)二(に)日(にち): one night, two days (standard short trip)
  • 一泊(いっぱく)二(に)食(しょく)（付(つ)き）: one night with two meals (dinner and breakfast)
  • 一泊(いっぱく)朝食(ちょうしょく)付(つ)き: one night with breakfast
  • 一泊(いっぱく)素泊(すどま)り: one night, room only (no meals)

BOOKING EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一泊(いっぱく)○○円(えん): ○○ yen per night
  • 一泊(いっぱく)予約(よやく)する: to book for one night

RELATED COUNTERS:

  • 二泊(にはく): two nights
  • 三泊(さんぱく): three nights
  • 何泊(なんぱく): how many nights
