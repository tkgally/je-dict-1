エコバッグ

えこばっぐ
noun
reusable shopping bag; eco bag
1. reusable shopping bag; eco bag
A reusable bag brought by shoppers to reduce plastic bag use, popular in Japan since plastic bag charges were introduced.
エコバッグを持(も)ってきた。
I brought my reusable bag.
レジ袋(ぶくろ)が有料(ゆうりょう)になったので、エコバッグを使(つか)う。
Since plastic bags are now charged, I use an eco bag.
折(お)りたたみエコバッグをいつもカバンに入(い)れている。
I always keep a foldable eco bag in my bag.

エコバッグ is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-made English) combining "eco" and "bag."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 折(お)りたたみエコバッグ: foldable eco bag
  • 保冷(ほれい)エコバッグ: insulated eco bag
  • エコバッグを持参(じさん)する: to bring one's own bag

BACKGROUND:
Since July 2020, Japan made plastic shopping bags (レジ袋(ぶくろ)) chargeable at stores. This significantly increased eco bag usage. Many stores sell original eco bags, and they've become a popular promotional item.
