その後(ご)

そのご
adverb / noun
after that; subsequently
1. after that; subsequently; since then
refers to the time period following a previously mentioned event or point in time
その後(ご)、彼(かれ)は連絡(れんらく)してこなかった。
After that, he didn't contact me.
手術(しゅじゅつ)から一週間(いっしゅうかん)、その後(ご)順調(じゅんちょう)に回復(かいふく)した。
A week after the surgery, he recovered smoothly.
その後(ご)いかがお過(す)ごしですか。
How have you been since then?

COMPOUND:
その (that) + 後(ご) (after)

READING:

  • その後(ご) - formal reading
  • その後(あと) - casual reading (same meaning)

PATTERN:

その + noun pattern:

  • そのため - therefore
  • その前(まえ) - before that
  • その間(あいだ) - meanwhile
  • その時(とき) - at that time
  • その頃(ころ) - around that time

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • それから - after that, and then
  • それ以来(いらい) - since then
  • それ以降(いこう) - from then on
