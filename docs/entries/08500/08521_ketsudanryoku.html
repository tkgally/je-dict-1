決断力(けつだんりょく)

けつだんりょく
noun
decisiveness; decision-making ability
1. decisiveness; decision-making ability
the ability to make decisions quickly and confidently
決断力(けつだんりょく)がある人(ひと)だ。
He's a decisive person.
リーダーには決断力(けつだんりょく)が求(もと)められる。
Decisiveness is required of leaders.
決断力(けつだんりょく)に欠(か)けている。
He lacks decisiveness.
彼女(かのじょ)の決断力(けつだんりょく)のおかげで危機(きき)を乗(の)り越(こ)えた。
Thanks to her decisiveness, we overcame the crisis.

USAGE:
A valued trait in leadership and business contexts. Refers to the ability to make firm decisions without excessive hesitation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 決断力(けつだんりょく)がある - to be decisive
  • 決断力(けつだんりょく)に欠(か)ける - to lack decisiveness
  • 決断力(けつだんりょく)を発揮(はっき)する - to demonstrate decisiveness

RELATED ABILITIES:

  • 判断力(はんだんりょく) - judgment
  • 行動力(こうどうりょく) - ability to take action
  • 実行力(じっこうりょく) - ability to execute
