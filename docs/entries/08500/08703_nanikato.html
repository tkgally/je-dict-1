何(なに)かと

なにかと
adverb
one way or another; in various ways; frequently
1. one way or another; in various ways; frequently; often for one reason or another
indicates that something happens frequently or repeatedly through various circumstances or reasons
何(なに)かと忙(いそが)しい。
I'm busy with one thing or another.
何(なに)かとお世話(せわ)になっております。
Thank you for all your help (in various matters).
彼(かれ)は何(なに)かと口(くち)を出(だ)してくる。
He's always butting in about one thing or another.

STRUCTURE:
何(なに) (what) + かと (or something) → "with this and that"

USAGE:

  • 何(なに)かと忙(いそが)しい - busy with various things
  • 何(なに)かとお世話(せわ)になる - to receive help in various ways
  • 何(なに)かと大変(たいへん) - difficult in various ways

NUANCE:
Implies that something happens repeatedly or in multiple contexts, without specifying exactly what. Often used to vaguely refer to multiple instances or reasons.

FORMAL EXPRESSION:
「何(なに)かとお世話(せわ)になっております」is a common polite phrase used in business and formal settings to thank someone for ongoing support.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 色々(いろいろ)と - in various ways
  • あれこれ - this and that
