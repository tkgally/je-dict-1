瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)

しゅんぱつりょく
noun
explosive power; quick reflexes
1. explosive power; instantaneous force
the ability to generate maximum power in a short burst of time
瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)を鍛(きた)える。
To train explosive power.
スプリンターには瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Explosive power is important for sprinters.
彼女(かのじょ)は瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)に優(すぐ)れている。
She excels in explosive power.
瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)と持久力(じきゅうりょく)のバランスが大切(たいせつ)だ。
The balance between explosive power and endurance is important.

USAGE:
Refers to the ability to exert maximum force in a very short time. Essential for activities requiring quick bursts of speed or power.

SPORTS APPLICATIONS:

  • Sprinting (短距離走(たんきょりそう))
  • Jumping (跳躍(ちょうやく))
  • Throwing (投擲(とうてき))
  • Quick starts and sudden movements

CONTRAST:

  • 持久力(じきゅうりょく) - endurance (sustained effort over time)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)を鍛(きた)える - to train explosive power
  • 瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)に優(すぐ)れる - to excel in explosive power
  • 瞬発力(しゅんぱつりょく)トレーニング - explosive power training
