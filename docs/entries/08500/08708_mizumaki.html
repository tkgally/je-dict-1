水(みず)撒(ま)き

みずまき
noun
watering; sprinkling water
1. watering; sprinkling water (for plants, streets, etc.)
the act of sprinkling or spraying water, typically for garden plants or to cool down streets in summer
毎朝(まいあさ)庭(にわ)の水撒(みずま)きをする。
I water the garden every morning.
夏(なつ)は水撒(みずま)きで道路(どうろ)を冷(ひ)やす習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
In summer, there's a custom of cooling the streets by sprinkling water.
留守中(るすちゅう)の水撒(みずま)きを隣人(りんじん)にお願(ねが)いした。
I asked my neighbor to water (the plants) while I'm away.

COMPOUND:
水(みず) (water) + 撒(ま)き (sprinkling)

USES:

  • 庭(にわ)の水撒(みずま)き - watering the garden
  • 道(みち)の水撒(みずま)き - sprinkling water on the street ("uchimizu" custom)
  • 花(はな)への水撒(みずま)き - watering flowers

JAPANESE CUSTOM:
打(う)ち水(みず) (uchimizu) is a traditional Japanese practice of sprinkling water on streets and gardens to cool the surroundings in summer. This practice reduces temperature through evaporation.

  • 掃除(そうじ) - cleaning
