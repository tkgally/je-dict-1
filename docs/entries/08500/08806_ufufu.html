うふふ

うふふ
interjection
hee hee (quiet laugh)
1. hee hee; soft giggle; quiet laugh
an onomatopoeic expression representing a quiet, somewhat coy or pleased laugh; often associated with feminine speech or secretive amusement
うふふ、秘密(ひみつ)。
Hee hee, it's a secret.
うふふと笑(わら)いながら話(はな)した。
She spoke while giggling softly.
「うふふ、楽(たの)しみだな。」
"Hee hee, I'm looking forward to it."

NUANCE:

うふふ suggests a quiet, somewhat coy or pleased laugh. It often implies:

  • Amusement at something private or secret
  • Pleased anticipation
  • Slight mischief or playfulness
  • Feminine speech patterns (though not exclusively)

COMPARISON WITH OTHER LAUGHS:

  • うふふ - quiet, coy, pleased
  • ふふふ - soft chuckle, knowing
  • えへへ - sheepish, embarrassed
  • あはは - open, hearty
  • くすくす - suppressed giggle

IN MANGA/ANIME:
Often used in dialogue bubbles to show a character's quiet amusement or scheming mood.
