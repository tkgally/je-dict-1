鍵(かぎ)掛(か)け

かぎかけ
noun
key hook; key rack
1. key hook; key rack; key holder
a device for hanging keys, typically mounted near an entrance
玄関(げんかん)に鍵(かぎ)掛(か)けを付(つ)けた。
I installed a key hook in the entrance.
鍵(かぎ)掛(か)けに鍵(かぎ)を掛(か)けておく。
I'll hang the keys on the key hook.
鍵(かぎ)掛(か)けがあると鍵(かぎ)をなくさない。
Having a key hook prevents losing your keys.

ALTERNATIVE NAMES:

  • キーフック - key hook (English loanword)
  • キーホルダー掛(か)け - key holder rack
  • 鍵(かぎ)ホルダー - key holder (for carrying)

HOUSEHOLD ITEM:
A common household item in Japan, often placed near the 玄関(げんかん) (entrance). Many Japanese homes have a dedicated spot for keys to prevent them from being misplaced.
