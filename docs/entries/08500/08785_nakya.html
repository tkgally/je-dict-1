なきゃ

なきゃ
auxiliary
have to; must (casual)
1. have to; must; need to
casual contraction of なければ (nakereba); expresses obligation or necessity; often used with ならない, いけない, or だめ (omitted in very casual speech)
行(い)かなきゃ。
I gotta go.
宿題(しゅくだい)やらなきゃいけない。
I have to do my homework.
もう帰(かえ)らなきゃだめかな。
I guess I have to go home now.

CASUAL CONTRACTION:

なきゃ is a casual spoken contraction of なければ:

  • なければ → なきゃ
  • なければならない → なきゃならない → なきゃ (with ending often omitted)

VARIATIONS:

  • なきゃ (most casual, ending often dropped)
  • なくちゃ (another casual form from なくては)
  • なきゃいけない / なきゃだめ (with explicit obligation)

FORMATION:

Verb negative stem + なきゃ

  • 行(い)く → 行(い)かなきゃ
  • 食(た)べる → 食(た)べなきゃ
  • する → しなきゃ

REGISTER:
Casual speech only. Use なければなりません in formal contexts.
