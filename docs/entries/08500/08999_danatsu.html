弾圧(だんあつ)

だんあつ
noun
oppression; suppression; crackdown
1. oppression; suppression; crackdown
The use of force or authority to crush opposition, dissent, or movements, typically by governments or those in power.
政府(せいふ)による弾圧(だんあつ)が続(つづ)いている。
Oppression by the government continues.
デモ隊(たい)は弾圧(だんあつ)を受(う)けた。
The demonstrators faced a crackdown.
言論(げんろん)の自由(じゆう)への弾圧(だんあつ)に抗議(こうぎ)する声(こえ)が上(あ)がった。
Voices of protest rose against the suppression of freedom of speech.

弾圧(だんあつ) is a strong term typically used in political contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弾圧(だんあつ)を受(う)ける: to face oppression
  • 弾圧(だんあつ)を加(くわ)える: to apply pressure/oppression
  • 政治的(せいじてき)弾圧(だんあつ): political oppression
  • 宗教(しゅうきょう)弾圧(だんあつ): religious persecution
  • 思想(しそう)弾圧(だんあつ): ideological suppression

USAGE:
弾圧(だんあつ)する is used as a suru-verb meaning "to oppress" or "to suppress."
