住(す)み慣(な)れる

すみなれる
verb (ichidan)
to become accustomed to living
1. to become accustomed to living; to get used to living in a place
to become comfortable with one's living environment through experience
住(す)み慣(な)れた町(まち)を離(はな)れたくない。
I don't want to leave the town I've grown used to living in.
半年(はんとし)で新(あたら)しい場所(ばしょ)に住(す)み慣(な)れた。
I got used to living in the new place in half a year.
住(す)み慣(な)れたアパートを出(で)るのは寂(さび)しい。
It's sad to leave the apartment I've become comfortable living in.

COMPOUND VERB:
住(す)む (to live) + 慣(な)れる (to become accustomed)

CONJUGATION:

  • 住(す)み慣(な)れる (dictionary form)
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れた (past - commonly used as modifier)
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れている (currently accustomed)

COMMON USAGE:

  • 住(す)み慣(な)れた場所(ばしょ) - a place one is used to living
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れた町(まち) - a town one is comfortable in
  • 住(す)み慣(な)れた家(いえ) - a home one is accustomed to

PATTERN:

~慣(な)れる compound verbs (become used to ~):

  • 聞(き)き慣(な)れる - become used to hearing
  • 食(た)べ慣(な)れる - become used to eating
  • 見慣(みな)れる - become used to seeing
  • 使(つか)い慣(な)れる - become used to using
