行動力(こうどうりょく)

こうどうりょく
noun
ability to take action; drive
1. ability to take action; drive
the capability to translate thoughts and plans into actual action
行動力(こうどうりょく)のある人(ひと)だ。
He's a person of action.
彼女(かのじょ)の行動力(こうどうりょく)には驚(おどろ)かされる。
I'm amazed by her ability to take action.
行動力(こうどうりょく)が足(た)りない。
I lack the drive to take action.
考(かんが)えるだけでなく行動力(こうどうりょく)が大事(だいじ)だ。
It's important not just to think but to have the drive to act.

USAGE:
Highly valued in business and leadership contexts. Refers to the ability to move from planning to execution without excessive hesitation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 行動力(こうどうりょく)がある - to have drive
  • 行動力(こうどうりょく)に欠(か)ける - to lack ability to act
  • 行動力(こうどうりょく)を発揮(はっき)する - to demonstrate action capability

RELATED ABILITIES:

  • 決断力(けつだんりょく) - decisiveness
  • 実行力(じっこうりょく) - execution ability
  • 判断力(はんだんりょく) - judgment
