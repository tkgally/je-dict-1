絶対(ぜったい)に

ぜったいに
adverb
absolutely; definitely; positively
1. absolutely; definitely; without fail
emphasizes strong certainty or determination
絶対(ぜったい)に行(い)く。
I will definitely go.
彼(かれ)は絶対(ぜったい)に来(く)ると思(おも)う。
I think he will definitely come.
この試験(しけん)には絶対(ぜったい)に合格(ごうかく)したい。
I absolutely want to pass this exam.
2. never; by no means (with negative)
used with negative verbs to express strong denial or prohibition
絶対(ぜったい)に許(ゆる)さない。
I will never forgive you.
ここでは絶対(ぜったい)に写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)らないでください。
Please absolutely do not take photos here.
彼女(かのじょ)は絶対(ぜったい)にそんなことを言(い)わないと思(おも)う。
I think she would never say such a thing.

FORMATION:
絶対(ぜったい) (absolute) + に (adverbializer)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 絶対(ぜったい)に + affirmative → definitely, certainly
  • 絶対(ぜったい)に + negative → never, absolutely not

INTENSITY:
One of the strongest emphatic adverbs in Japanese. Using it conveys very strong determination or prohibition.

CAUTION:
Overuse can sound dramatic or immature. In formal contexts, 必(かなら)ず (certainly) may be more appropriate for affirmative sentences.

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絶対(ぜったい)に無理(むり) (absolutely impossible)
  • 絶対(ぜったい)に嫌(いや) (absolutely refuse)
  • 絶対(ぜったい)に間違(まちが)いない (definitely not wrong)
