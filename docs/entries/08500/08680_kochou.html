1. exaggeration; overstatement
representing something as greater or more important than it really is
それは誇張(こちょう)ではない。
That's not an exaggeration.
彼(かれ)の話(はなし)には誇張(こちょう)が多(おお)い。
His stories contain a lot of exaggeration.
誇張(こちょう)なしに言(い)えば、最高(さいこう)の体験(たいけん)だった。
Without exaggeration, it was the best experience.

誇張(こちょう) means exaggeration — making something seem greater or more extreme than it actually is. Used as both a noun and a する verb.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 誇張(こちょう)する: to exaggerate
  • 誇張(こちょう)なしに: without exaggeration
  • 誇張(こちょう)が過(す)ぎる: to exaggerate too much
  • 誇張(こちょう)表現(ひょうげん): hyperbole, exaggerated expression
  • 誇張(こちょう)して言(い)う: to say with exaggeration

LITERARY TERM:
誇張法(こちょうほう) is the rhetorical device of hyperbole.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大(おお)げさ: exaggerated, dramatic (na-adjective, more colloquial)
  • 言(い)い過(す)ぎ: overstatement, saying too much
  • オーバー: over-the-top (casual loanword usage)

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal. In casual speech, 大(おお)げさ is more common.
