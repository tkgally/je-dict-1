点々(てんてん)と

てんてんと
adverb
here and there; scattered about
1. here and there; scattered; dotted about
Describes things distributed sparsely and irregularly across a surface or area, like dots on a canvas.
島(しま)が点々(てんてん)と浮(う)かんでいる。
Islands are scattered across the water.
野原(のはら)に花(はな)が点々(てんてん)と咲(さ)いている。
Flowers are blooming here and there in the field.
雪(ゆき)の上(うえ)に動物(どうぶつ)の足跡(あしあと)が点々(てんてん)と続(つづ)いていた。
Animal footprints dotted the snow, trailing off into the distance.

USAGE:
点々(てんてん)と is an adverb describing things distributed sparsely like dots. It conveys a visual image of items spread out with space between them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 点々(てんてん)と散(ち)らばる (to be scattered about)
  • 点々(てんてん)と続(つづ)く (to continue in a dotted line)
  • 点々(てんてん)と浮(う)かぶ (to float here and there)

GRAMMAR:
点々(てんてん) can also be used as a noun meaning "dots" or "dotted marks." When used with と, it functions as an adverb.
