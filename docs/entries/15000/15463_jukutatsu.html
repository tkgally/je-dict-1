熟達(じゅくたつ)

じゅくたつ
noun
proficiency; mastery; skill
1. proficiency; mastery; becoming skilled through practice
The state of having become highly skilled at something through sustained practice and experience. Emphasizes the process of maturing into competence.
技術(ぎじゅつ)に熟達(じゅくたつ)した職人(しょくにん)だ。
He is a craftsman who has mastered the technique.
英語(えいご)に熟達(じゅくたつ)するには時間(じかん)がかかる。
It takes time to become proficient in English.
長年(ながねん)の訓練(くんれん)によって剣術(けんじゅつ)に熟達(じゅくたつ)した武士(ぶし)は少(すく)なくなかった。
There were many warriors who achieved mastery of swordsmanship through years of training.

GRAMMAR:
Used as a suru verb. The pattern is ～に熟達(じゅくたつ)する (to become proficient at ~). The particle に marks the skill or subject.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～に熟達(じゅくたつ)する (to master ~)
  • ～に熟達(じゅくたつ)した + noun (a noun skilled at ~)
  • 熟達(じゅくたつ)者(しゃ) (expert, master)

CONTRAST:

  • 上達(じょうたつ) (improvement — emphasizes getting better, can apply at any level)
  • 熟達(じゅくたつ) (mastery — emphasizes reaching a high level of skill through maturation)
  • 精通(せいつう) (being well-versed — emphasizes knowledge rather than skill)
