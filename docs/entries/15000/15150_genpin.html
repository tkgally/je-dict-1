現品(げんぴん)

げんぴん
noun
actual item; stock on hand
1. actual item; the item itself
The physical item in question, as opposed to a description, photo, or catalog listing. Often used when asking to see or inspect the real thing.
現品(げんぴん)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please check the actual item.
写真(しゃしん)と現品(げんぴん)の色(いろ)が少(すこ)し違(ちが)う場合(ばあい)があります。
The color of the actual item may differ slightly from the photo.
注文(ちゅうもん)する前(まえ)に、できれば現品(げんぴん)を見(み)ておきたい。
Before ordering, I'd like to see the actual item if possible.
2. stock on hand; current inventory; display item
The last remaining item in stock, often a display model or floor sample sold at a discount.
現品(げんぴん)限(かぎ)り半額(はんがく)セール。
Half-price sale, limited to floor stock.
この冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)は現品(げんぴん)処分(しょぶん)で安(やす)くなっている。
This refrigerator is discounted as a clearance display item.
現品(げんぴん)のため、多少(たしょう)の傷(きず)がある場合(ばあい)がございます。
As this is a display item, it may have minor scratches.

USAGE:
現品(げんぴん) is used in retail, business, and online shopping contexts. Sense 1 emphasizes seeing or verifying the real thing, while sense 2 refers specifically to floor samples or last-in-stock items sold at a discount.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 現品(げんぴん)確認(かくにん) (actual item inspection)
  • 現品(げんぴん)限(かぎ)り (limited to current stock / floor model only)
  • 現品(げんぴん)処分(しょぶん) (clearance of display items)
  • 現品(げんぴん)管理(かんり) (inventory control)
