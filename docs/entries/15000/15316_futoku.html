不徳(ふとく)

ふとく
noun
lack of virtue; immorality; misconduct
1. lack of virtue; immorality; personal failing
A formal term for moral failings or shortcomings in character. Most commonly encountered in the set phrase of apology: "owing to my lack of virtue."
不徳(ふとく)の致(いた)すところです。
It is due to my own failings.
This is a set apology phrase.
このたびの件(けん)は、すべて私(わたくし)の不徳(ふとく)の致(いた)すところです。
This matter is entirely due to my own shortcomings.
社長(しゃちょう)は記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で「不徳(ふとく)の致(いた)すところであり、深(ふか)く反省(はんせい)しております」と述(の)べた。
The company president stated at the press conference, "It is due to my own failings, and I deeply regret it."

USAGE:
不徳(ふとく) is almost always encountered in the fixed expression 不徳(ふとく)の致(いた)すところ, a very formal apology meaning "it is the result of my lack of virtue / personal failings." This phrase is commonly used by politicians, executives, and public figures when taking responsibility for scandals or mistakes.

FIXED PATTERN:
不徳(ふとく)の致(いた)すところです — a humble expression accepting full blame. The phrase implies that the speaker's personal moral shortcomings led to the unfortunate outcome.

REGISTER:
This is a very formal, humble expression used in public apologies. It would not be used in casual conversation.
