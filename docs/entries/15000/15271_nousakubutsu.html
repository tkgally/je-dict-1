農作物(のうさくぶつ)

のうさくぶつ
noun
crops; agricultural produce
1. crops; agricultural produce; farm products
Plants cultivated for food or commercial purposes. A general term covering rice, vegetables, fruits, grains, and other farm products.
台風(たいふう)で農作物(のうさくぶつ)に被害(ひがい)が出(で)た。
Crops were damaged by the typhoon.
この地域(ちいき)の主(おも)な農作物(のうさくぶつ)は米(こめ)と大豆(だいず)だ。
The main crops of this region are rice and soybeans.
気候変動(きこうへんどう)の影響(えいきょう)で、世界中(せかいじゅう)の農作物(のうさくぶつ)の収穫量(しゅうかくりょう)が減少(げんしょう)している。
Due to climate change, crop yields around the world are declining.

USAGE:
農作物(のうさくぶつ) is a formal compound of 農(のう) (agriculture) and 作物(さくぶつ) (produce, crops). It is commonly used in news, academic, and agricultural contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 農作物(のうさくぶつ)を育(そだ)てる (to grow crops)
  • 農作物(のうさくぶつ)の被害(ひがい) (crop damage)
  • 農作物(のうさくぶつ)の収穫(しゅうかく) (crop harvest)
  • 主要(しゅよう)農作物(のうさくぶつ) (major crops)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 農作物(のうさくぶつ): crops, agricultural produce (formal, comprehensive)
  • 作物(さくもつ): crops (shorter, also common)
  • 農産物(のうさんぶつ): agricultural products (includes processed goods)
