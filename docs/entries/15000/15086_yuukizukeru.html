勇気(ゆうき)づける

ゆうきづける
verb
to encourage; to cheer up
1. to encourage; to hearten; to inspire courage in
To give someone courage or confidence, helping them feel braver or more motivated. A compound verb from 勇気 (courage) + づける (to give, to imbue with).
友達(ともだち)を勇気(ゆうき)づけた。
I encouraged my friend.
彼(かれ)の言葉(ことば)に勇気(ゆうき)づけられた。
I was encouraged by his words.
落(お)ち込(こ)んでいる後輩(こうはい)を勇気(ゆうき)づけるために声(こえ)をかけた。
I spoke to my junior to encourage them when they were feeling down.

USAGE:
勇気(ゆうき)づける is an ichidan (ru-verb) that means to give someone courage. The passive form 勇気(ゆうき)づけられる (to be encouraged) is very common.

CONJUGATION:

  • 勇気(ゆうき)づける (dictionary form)
  • 勇気(ゆうき)づけない (negative)
  • 勇気(ゆうき)づけた (past)
  • 勇気(ゆうき)づけられる (passive — to be encouraged)

SIMILAR WORDS:
励(はげ)ます also means to encourage, but has a broader scope and is more commonly used in everyday speech. 勇気(ゆうき)づける specifically emphasizes giving someone courage or bravery.
