付随(ふずい)

ふずい
noun
accompanying; incidental; concomitant
1. accompanying; incidental; being attached to; concomitant
Something that comes along with or is attached to a main thing. Used in formal or written contexts to describe secondary elements, conditions, or consequences that follow from something primary.
契約(けいやく)に付随(ふずい)する条件(じょうけん)がある。
There are conditions that accompany the contract.
この仕事(しごと)には出張(しゅっちょう)が付随(ふずい)する。
Business trips are part and parcel of this job.
権利(けんり)には義務(ぎむ)が付随(ふずい)するものだ。
Rights are always accompanied by obligations.

USAGE:
付随(ふずい) is a formal word used in legal, business, and academic contexts. It describes something that naturally or necessarily accompanies something else.

GRAMMAR:
Used as a suru-verb: 付随(ふずい)する (to accompany, to be incidental to). Often appears in the pattern Xに付随(ふずい)する (accompanying X / incidental to X).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 付随(ふずい)する費用(ひよう) (incidental costs)
  • 付随(ふずい)する業務(ぎょうむ) (related duties)
  • 付随(ふずい)する条件(じょうけん) (accompanying conditions)
  • 付随(ふずい)事項(じこう) (incidental matters)
