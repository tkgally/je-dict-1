すりこぎ

すりこぎ
noun
wooden pestle; grinding stick
1. wooden pestle; grinding stick
A wooden stick used with a mortar (すり鉢) to grind sesame seeds, miso, and other ingredients. A traditional Japanese kitchen utensil, typically made from the wood of the Japanese pepper tree (山椒).
すりこぎでごまをする。
Grind sesame seeds with a pestle.
すり鉢(ばち)とすりこぎで味噌(みそ)をすりつぶす。
Grind the miso with a mortar and pestle.
祖母(そぼ)は山椒(さんしょう)の木(き)でできたすりこぎを大切(たいせつ)に使(つか)っていた。
My grandmother treasured her pestle made of Japanese pepper wood.

USAGE:
すりこぎ is always used together with すり鉢(ばち) (mortar). The pair is a staple of traditional Japanese cooking, particularly for grinding sesame seeds, making sesame dressing, or preparing 白和(しらあ)え (tofu salad).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すりこぎでする (to grind with a pestle)
  • すり鉢(ばち)とすりこぎ (mortar and pestle)

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji form 擂粉木(すりこぎ) exists but is rarely used. Hiragana is the standard written form.
