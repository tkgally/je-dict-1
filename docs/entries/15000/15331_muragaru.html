1. to swarm; to crowd; to flock; to throng
To gather in a large group around something, often in an eager or chaotic way. Used for people, animals, and insects.
蜂(はち)が花(はな)に群(むら)がっている。
Bees are swarming around the flowers.
子供(こども)たちがアイスクリームの車(くるま)に群(むら)がった。
The children crowded around the ice cream truck.
セールが始(はじ)まると、客(きゃく)が店(みせ)の前(まえ)に群(むら)がった。
When the sale started, customers thronged in front of the store.

USAGE:
An intransitive verb. The target that attracts the crowd is marked with に. Conveys a sense of many individuals converging eagerly or instinctively on one point.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 虫(むし)が群(むら)がる (insects swarm)
  • 人(ひと)が群(むら)がる (people crowd around)
  • 群(むら)がって来(く)る (to come swarming)

GRAMMAR:
Godan verb (ら行(ぎょう)). Te-form: 群(むら)がって. Past: 群(むら)がった. Negative: 群(むら)がらない.
