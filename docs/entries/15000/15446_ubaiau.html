奪(うば)い合(あ)う

うばいあう
verb-godan
to scramble for; to fight over; to compete for
1. to scramble for; to fight over; to compete for
To struggle with others to take possession of something. Formed from 奪(うば)う (to seize) + 合(あ)う (reciprocal suffix), indicating mutual competition or a contest over something.
子供(こども)たちがおもちゃを奪(うば)い合(あ)っている。
The children are fighting over a toy.
限定(げんてい)商品(しょうひん)を客(きゃく)たちが奪(うば)い合(あ)った。
Customers scrambled for the limited-edition items.
各社(かくしゃ)が優秀(ゆうしゅう)な人材(じんざい)を奪(うば)い合(あ)う状況(じょうきょう)が続(つづ)いている。
Companies continue to compete fiercely for talented workers.

FORMATION:
Compound verb from 奪(うば)う (to seize, to snatch) + 合(あ)う (reciprocal suffix). The ～合(あ)う pattern indicates that two or more parties are performing the action toward each other.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 席(せき)を奪(うば)い合(あ)う (to scramble for seats)
  • ボールを奪(うば)い合(あ)う (to fight for the ball)
  • 市場(しじょう)を奪(うば)い合(あ)う (to compete for market share)

NUANCE:
Can describe both physical struggles (children fighting over a toy) and abstract competition (companies vying for talent). The word carries an intensity beyond simple 競(きそ)う (to compete).
