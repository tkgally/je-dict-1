腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かす

こしをぬかす
expression
to be frozen with shock; to be flabbergasted
1. to be frozen with shock; to be flabbergasted; to be so surprised one cannot stand
To be so startled, frightened, or astonished that one's legs give out and one cannot stand up properly. Literally, one's lower back (腰(こし)) gives way.
驚(おどろ)いて腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かした。
I was so surprised I couldn't stand.
幽霊(ゆうれい)を見(み)て腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かしてしまった。
I was paralyzed with shock after seeing a ghost.
請求書(せいきゅうしょ)の金額(きんがく)を見(み)て、腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かすほど驚(おどろ)いた。
I was flabbergasted when I saw the amount on the bill.

USAGE:
Can describe either literal inability to stand (from fright) or figurative extreme surprise. Often used hyperbolically.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かすほど驚(おどろ)く (to be so surprised one can't stand)
  • ～て腰(こし)を抜(ぬ)かす (to be paralyzed with shock from ~)

NUANCE:
More vivid and dramatic than simply 驚(おどろ)く (to be surprised). Often used for humorous or exaggerated effect, though it can also describe genuine terror.
