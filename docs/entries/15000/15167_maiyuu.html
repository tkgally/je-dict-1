毎夕(まいゆう)

まいゆう
noun
every evening
1. every evening; each evening
Each and every evening. A somewhat formal time expression used in writing and news.
毎夕(まいゆう)ジョギングをしている。
I jog every evening.
毎夕(まいゆう)六時(ろくじ)にニュースを見(み)る。
I watch the news at six every evening.
毎夕(まいゆう)の散歩(さんぽ)が日課(にっか)になっている。
An evening walk has become part of my daily routine.

USAGE:
毎夕(まいゆう) is a formal time word meaning "every evening." In everyday conversation, 毎晩(まいばん) (every night) or 毎日(まいにち)夕方(ゆうがた) (every day in the evening) is more common.

SERIES:

Part of a series of 毎(まい) + time compounds:

  • 毎朝(まいあさ) (every morning)
  • 毎昼(まいひる) (every noon — rare)
  • 毎夕(まいゆう) (every evening)
  • 毎晩(まいばん) (every night)
  • 毎週(まいしゅう) (every week)
  • 毎月(まいつき) (every month)
