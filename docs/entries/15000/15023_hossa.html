発作(ほっさ)

ほっさ
noun
attack, fit, seizure
1. attack, fit, seizure
A sudden onset of symptoms or a medical episode, such as a seizure, asthma attack, or panic attack.
心臓(しんぞう)発作(ほっさ)を起(お)こした。
He had a heart attack.
喘息(ぜんそく)の発作(ほっさ)が出(で)た。
I had an asthma attack.
薬(くすり)のおかげで発作(ほっさ)の回数(かいすう)が減(へ)ってきた。
Thanks to the medication, the frequency of attacks has decreased.
2. sudden impulse, fit of emotion
A sudden, uncontrollable urge or burst of emotion.
笑(わら)いの発作(ほっさ)に襲(おそ)われた。
I was seized by a fit of laughter.
怒(いか)りの発作(ほっさ)で我(われ)を忘(わす)れた。
I lost myself in a fit of rage.
突然(とつぜん)の発作(ほっさ)のように泣(な)き出(だ)した。
She burst into tears as if seized by a sudden fit.

READING: Note the double consonant — ほっさ, not はっさ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発作(ほっさ)を起(お)こす: to have an attack/fit
  • 発作(ほっさ)が出(で)る: for an attack to occur
  • 発作(ほっさ)に襲(おそ)われる: to be seized by a fit
  • 心臓(しんぞう)発作(ほっさ): heart attack
  • 喘息(ぜんそく)の発作(ほっさ): asthma attack
  • パニック発作(ほっさ): panic attack
  • 発作(ほっさ)的(てき)(な): paroxysmal, impulsive
