黒幕(くろまく)

くろまく
noun
mastermind; wire-puller; power behind the scenes
1. mastermind; wire-puller; power behind the scenes
A person who secretly controls or influences events from behind the scenes, especially in politics or crime. Originally referred to the black curtain used in kabuki theater to hide stagehands.
事件(じけん)の黒幕(くろまく)は誰(だれ)だ。
Who is the mastermind behind the incident?
政治(せいじ)の黒幕(くろまく)として長年(ながねん)影響力(えいきょうりょく)を持(も)っていた。
He wielded influence for many years as a political power broker.
表(おもて)に出(で)ないが、彼(かれ)こそがこの計画(けいかく)の黒幕(くろまく)だと言(い)われている。
He doesn't appear publicly, but he is said to be the mastermind behind this plan.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 黒(くろ) (black) + 幕(まく) (curtain). In kabuki theater, a black curtain hides stagehands who move props and assist actors. By extension, the word came to mean someone who operates unseen, pulling strings from behind the scenes.

USAGE:
Used in news, politics, and crime stories. Also common in manga and anime to describe the secret villain controlling events. Almost always carries a negative connotation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事件(じけん)の黒幕(くろまく) (the mastermind behind an incident)
  • 政界(せいかい)の黒幕(くろまく) (a political power broker)
  • 黒幕(くろまく)を暴(あば)く (to expose the mastermind)
