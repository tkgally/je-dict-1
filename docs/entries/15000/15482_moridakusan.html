盛(も)りだくさん

もりだくさん
na-adjective, noun
packed; full of content; loaded
1. packed; full of content; loaded with things
Having a large amount or variety of things, especially content, events, or food. Conveys a positive sense of abundance.
盛(も)りだくさんの内容(ないよう)だった。
It was packed with content.
今日(きょう)のイベントは盛(も)りだくさんで楽(たの)しかった。
Today's event was jam-packed and fun.
ツアーは観光(かんこう)も食事(しょくじ)も盛(も)りだくさんで、あっという間(ま)に時間(じかん)が過(す)ぎた。
The tour was packed with both sightseeing and dining, and time flew by.

FORMATION:
盛(も)り (heaping, serving) + たくさん (a lot). Literally "a heaping amount."

USAGE:
Always positive in nuance — describes events, meals, programs, or schedules that are satisfyingly full. Used as a na-adjective (盛(も)りだくさんな内容(ないよう)) or with the particle の (盛(も)りだくさんの内容(ないよう)). Common in advertising and event descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 盛(も)りだくさんの内容(ないよう) (packed content)
  • 盛(も)りだくさんな一日(いちにち) (an action-packed day)
  • 盛(も)りだくさんの企画(きかく) (a program full of events)
