姓(せい)

せい
noun
surname; family name
1. surname; family name; last name
A person's family name, as opposed to their given name. In Japanese naming order, the surname comes first.
姓(せい)と名(めい)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your surname and given name.
結婚(けっこん)して姓(せい)が変(か)わった。
My surname changed when I got married.
日本(にほん)では夫婦(ふうふ)が同(おな)じ姓(せい)を名乗(なの)ることが法律(ほうりつ)で決(き)められている。
In Japan, the law requires married couples to share the same surname.

USAGE:
姓(せい) is a formal word for surname. In everyday speech, 名字(みょうじ) or 苗字(みょうじ) is more common. 姓(せい) appears frequently on official forms and in formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姓(せい)と名(めい) (surname and given name)
  • 姓(せい)を名乗(なの)る (to go by a surname)
  • 旧姓(きゅうせい) (maiden name; former surname)
  • 夫婦(ふうふ)別姓(べっせい) (separate surnames for married couples)

SIMILAR WORDS:
名字(みょうじ) is the everyday equivalent. 氏(し) is used in formal/legal contexts. 姓(せい) falls in between.
