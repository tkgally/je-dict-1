1. encouragement, urging on
Strong encouragement or rallying of someone to keep going or try harder. Stronger and more forceful than simple 応援.
選手(せんしゅ)を激励(げきれい)した。
I encouraged the athletes.
監督(かんとく)の激励(げきれい)でチームが奮起(ふんき)した。
The team was fired up by the coach's encouragement.
社長(しゃちょう)が現場(げんば)を訪(おとず)れ、社員(しゃいん)たちを激励(げきれい)して回(まわ)った。
The company president visited the work sites and went around encouraging the employees.

激励(げきれい) is a forceful, spirited form of encouragement — stronger than casual cheering. It implies rallying someone to try harder or keep going, typically from a person in a position of authority.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [person]を激励(げきれい)する

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 激励(げきれい)の言葉(ことば): words of encouragement
  • 激励(げきれい)を受(う)ける: to receive encouragement
  • 激励(げきれい)会(かい): pep rally, encouragement gathering
  • 激励(げきれい)に駆(か)けつける: to rush over to encourage someone

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 応援(おうえん): general support/cheering (broader, lighter)
  • 励(はげ)まし: gentle encouragement (softer, more personal)
  • 鼓舞(こぶ): inspiring/rousing (more literary)

REGISTER:
Slightly formal. Common in news reports, sports coverage, and business contexts. Less common in casual daily conversation, where 励(はげ)ます or 応援(おうえん)する is preferred.
