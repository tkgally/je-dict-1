彩色(さいしき)

さいしき
noun
coloring; painting; polychrome
1. coloring; painting; adding color
The act of applying color to a surface, artwork, or object. Often used in artistic, historical, and craft contexts for the application of paint or pigment.
仏像(ぶつぞう)に彩色(さいしき)を施(ほどこ)す。
To apply color to a Buddhist statue.
鮮(あざ)やかに彩色(さいしき)された天井画(てんじょうが)が美(うつく)しい。
The vividly painted ceiling mural is beautiful.
修復(しゅうふく)チームは当時(とうじ)の技法(ぎほう)で彩色(さいしき)を再現(さいげん)した。
The restoration team reproduced the coloring using techniques from the original period.

USAGE:
彩色(さいしき) is a somewhat formal or specialized term used in art, architecture, and cultural heritage contexts. It refers to the deliberate application of color, not natural coloring.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 彩色(さいしき)を施(ほどこ)す (to apply coloring)
  • 彩色(さいしき)する (to color, to paint)
  • 鮮(あざ)やかな彩色(さいしき) (vivid coloring)
  • 彩色(さいしき)木彫(もくちょう) (polychrome wood carving)

SIMILAR WORDS:
着色(ちゃくしょく) is more technical, often used for adding color to food or industrial products. 彩色(さいしき) is used for artistic or decorative coloring. 塗装(とそう) refers to industrial painting or coating.
