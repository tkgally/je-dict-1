普段着(ふだんぎ)

ふだんぎ
noun
casual clothes; everyday clothes
1. casual clothes; everyday clothes; informal wear
Clothing worn for daily life and relaxation, as opposed to formal or work attire.
普段着(ふだんぎ)で来(き)てください。
Please come in casual clothes.
休日(きゅうじつ)は普段着(ふだんぎ)でのんびり過(す)ごす。
On holidays, I relax in casual clothes.
パーティーだけど普段着(ふだんぎ)でいいと言(い)われた。
I was told that casual clothes are fine for the party.

USAGE:
Used to distinguish informal daily wear from formal attire (正装(せいそう)) or work clothes (仕事着(しごとぎ)). A practical, everyday word.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 普段着(ふだんぎ)で出(で)かける (to go out in casual clothes)
  • 普段着(ふだんぎ)のまま (still in casual clothes, as one is)
  • 普段着(ふだんぎ)に着替(きが)える (to change into casual clothes)

WORD FORMATION:
普段(ふだん) (usual, everyday) + 着(ぎ) (wearing, clothes). The き becomes ぎ due to rendaku.
