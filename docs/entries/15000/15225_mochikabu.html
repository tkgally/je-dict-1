持(も)ち株(かぶ)

もちかぶ
noun
shareholdings; stocks held
1. shareholdings; stocks one owns; equity holdings
Shares of stock that a person or entity currently holds or owns.
持(も)ち株(かぶ)を売却(ばいきゃく)した。
I sold my shareholdings.
社長(しゃちょう)の持(も)ち株(かぶ)比率(ひりつ)は三十(さんじゅう)パーセントだ。
The president's shareholding ratio is thirty percent.
従業員(じゅうぎょういん)持(も)ち株(かぶ)会(かい)に加入(かにゅう)して、毎月(まいつき)少(すこ)しずつ自社(じしゃ)株(かぶ)を買(か)っている。
I joined the employee stock ownership plan and buy a little company stock each month.

USAGE:
持(も)ち株(かぶ) refers to shares one currently holds. It is common in business and financial contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 持(も)ち株(かぶ)会社(がいしゃ) (holding company)
  • 持(も)ち株(かぶ)比率(ひりつ) (shareholding ratio)
  • 従業員(じゅうぎょういん)持(も)ち株(かぶ)会(かい) (employee stock ownership plan)
  • 持(も)ち株(かぶ)を売(う)る (to sell one's shares)
  • 持(も)ち株(かぶ)を増(ふ)やす (to increase one's shareholdings)
