二流(にりゅう)

にりゅう
noun / adjective (no-adjective)
second-rate; second-class
1. second-rate; second-class; mediocre
Not of the highest quality or rank. Used to describe people, things, or organizations that fall short of top-tier standards.
二流(にりゅう)の選手(せんしゅ)では勝(か)てない。
A second-rate player can't win.
あの会社(かいしゃ)は二流(にりゅう)だと言(い)われている。
That company is said to be second-rate.
一流(いちりゅう)の技術(ぎじゅつ)がなくても、二流(にりゅう)で終(お)わりたくないという気持(きも)ちが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Even without top-tier skills, the desire not to end up second-rate is what matters.

USAGE:
二流(にりゅう) is generally negative, implying that something or someone does not meet the highest standard. It is used with の to modify nouns.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 一流(いちりゅう) (first-rate, top-class)
  • 三流(さんりゅう) (third-rate, low-quality — more strongly negative)
  • 超一流(ちょういちりゅう) (world-class, top-tier)

NUANCE:
While 二流(にりゅう) literally means "second tier," it carries a negative connotation of being mediocre or not good enough. It is more blunt than 平凡(へいぼん) (ordinary) and less harsh than 三流(さんりゅう).
