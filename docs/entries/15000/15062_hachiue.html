鉢植(はちう)え

はちうえ
noun
potted plant
1. potted plant; plant grown in a pot
A plant cultivated in a container rather than in the ground. Also refers to the act of planting in a pot.
窓辺(まどべ)に鉢植(はちう)えを置(お)いている。
I have potted plants by the window.
鉢植(はちう)えの花(はな)に毎朝(まいあさ)水(みず)をやる。
I water the potted flowers every morning.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)し祝(いわ)いに観葉植物(かんようしょくぶつ)の鉢植(はちう)えをもらった。
I received a potted foliage plant as a housewarming gift.

USAGE:
鉢植(はちう)え refers to both the potted plant itself and the practice of growing plants in pots. It is a common everyday word for indoor and balcony gardening.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鉢植(はちう)えの花(はな) (potted flowers)
  • 鉢植(はちう)えの木(き) (potted tree)
  • 鉢植(はちう)えにする (to pot [a plant])
  • 鉢植(はちう)えを育(そだ)てる (to grow potted plants)

SIMILAR WORDS:
植木鉢(うえきばち) refers to the pot or container itself, while 鉢植(はちう)え refers to the plant-in-pot combination or the practice of container gardening.
