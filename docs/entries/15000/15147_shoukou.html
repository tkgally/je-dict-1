1. ascending and descending; going up and down
The action of moving up and down, typically referring to riding elevators, climbing stairs, or the mechanical raising and lowering of equipment.
昇降(しょうこう)口(ぐち)はどこですか。
Where is the entrance/exit (for boarding)?
エレベーターが昇降(しょうこう)する音(おと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the sound of the elevator going up and down.
階段(かいだん)の昇降(しょうこう)が困難(こんなん)な方(かた)は、エレベーターをご利用(りよう)ください。
Those who have difficulty going up and down stairs, please use the elevator.

USAGE:
昇降(しょうこう) is a formal compound combining 昇(のぼ)る (to ascend) and 降(お)りる (to descend). It appears frequently in signage, instructions, and technical descriptions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 昇降(しょうこう)口(ぐち) (entrance/exit for boarding, as on a bus or train platform)
  • 昇降(しょうこう)機(き) (elevator, lift mechanism)
  • 昇降(しょうこう)式(しき) (height-adjustable, as in 昇降(しょうこう)式(しき)デスク — standing desk)
