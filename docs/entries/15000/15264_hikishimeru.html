引(ひ)き締(し)める

ひきしめる
verb, ichidan
to tighten; to brace; to firm up
1. to tighten; to pull tight; to firm up
To make something physically tighter or more toned, such as muscles or a body.
体(からだ)を引(ひ)き締(し)めたい。
I want to tone my body.
このクリームは肌(はだ)を引(ひ)き締(し)める効果(こうか)がある。
This cream has a skin-tightening effect.
毎朝(まいあさ)ジョギングをして、少(すこ)しずつ体(からだ)を引(ひ)き締(し)めている。
I jog every morning and am gradually toning my body.
2. to brace oneself; to tighten discipline; to buckle down
To adopt a more serious, focused attitude. Often used about mental or organizational discipline.
気(き)を引(ひ)き締(し)めて頑張(がんば)ろう。
Let's buckle down and do our best.
試合前(しあいまえ)に監督(かんとく)が選手(せんしゅ)たちの気持(きも)ちを引(ひ)き締(し)めた。
Before the match, the coach braced the players' spirits.
新年度(しんねんど)を迎(むか)えるにあたり、財政(ざいせい)を引(ひ)き締(し)める方針(ほうしん)が発表(はっぴょう)された。
A policy to tighten finances was announced ahead of the new fiscal year.

引(ひ)き締(し)める is a transitive ichidan verb. It is a compound of 引(ひ)く (to pull) and 締(し)める (to tighten).

ASPECT (ている):
引(ひ)き締(し)めている indicates an ongoing process of tightening, or a resulting toned/disciplined state.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]を引(ひ)き締(し)める (to tighten something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体(からだ)を引(ひ)き締(し)める (to tone one's body)
  • 気(き)を引(ひ)き締(し)める (to brace oneself, to get serious)
  • 財政(ざいせい)を引(ひ)き締(し)める (to tighten finances)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)を引(ひ)き締(し)める (to set one's expression firmly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)き締(し)める: to tighten, to tone (emphasizes pulling tight)
  • 締(し)める: to tighten, to close (simpler, more general)
  • 緩(ゆる)める: to loosen (antonym)
