核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く

かくしんをつく
expression / verb (godan)
to get to the heart of the matter; to hit the nail on the head
1. to get to the heart of the matter; to hit the nail on the head
To identify or address the most important, central point of an issue. Used when someone makes a remark or asks a question that goes straight to the core of a problem.
核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く質問(しつもん)だった。
It was a question that hit the nail on the head.
彼女(かのじょ)の発言(はつげん)は問題(もんだい)の核心(かくしん)を突(つ)いていた。
Her remark got to the heart of the problem.
記者(きしゃ)が核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く質問(しつもん)をしたが、大臣(だいじん)は明確(めいかく)な回答(かいとう)を避(さ)けた。
The reporter asked a pointed question that went to the heart of the matter, but the minister avoided giving a clear answer.

USAGE:
核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く is used when someone identifies the central, most important point of an issue. It is often used to describe incisive questions, remarks, or analyses. The verb 突(つ)く conjugates as godan: 突(つ)く → 突(つ)いた → 突(つ)いて.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く質問(しつもん) (a question that gets to the heart of the matter)
  • 核心(かくしん)を突(つ)く発言(はつげん) (a remark that hits the mark)
  • 核心(かくしん)を突(つ)いた指摘(してき) (an observation that identifies the core issue)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 的(まと)を射(い)る (to hit the mark — similar but broader)
  • 痛(いた)いところを突(つ)く (to touch a sore spot — more personal/uncomfortable)
