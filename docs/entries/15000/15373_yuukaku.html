遊郭(ゆうかく)

ゆうかく
noun
licensed pleasure quarter
1. licensed pleasure quarter; red-light district (historical)
A walled or designated district in Edo-period Japan where prostitution was officially licensed and regulated by the government. The most famous was Yoshiwara in Edo (Tokyo). These districts were abolished in the modern era.
吉原(よしわら)は江戸(えど)で最(もっと)も有名(ゆうめい)な遊郭(ゆうかく)だった。
Yoshiwara was the most famous pleasure quarter in Edo.
遊郭(ゆうかく)は独自(どくじ)の文化(ぶんか)を生(う)み出(だ)した。
The pleasure quarters gave rise to their own distinct culture.
小説(しょうせつ)や映画(えいが)の舞台(ぶたい)として遊郭(ゆうかく)が描(えが)かれることは今(いま)も多(おお)い。
Pleasure quarters are still frequently depicted as settings in novels and films.

USAGE: 遊郭(ゆうかく) is a historical term. These districts were established by the Tokugawa shogunate to control prostitution by confining it to specific areas. The most famous were 吉原(よしわら) (Edo/Tokyo), 島原(しまばら) (Kyoto), and 新町(しんまち) (Osaka).

The 遊郭(ゆうかく) developed a rich cultural ecosystem including fashion, music, art, and literature. Many ukiyo-e prints depict life in these districts.

Also written as 遊廓(ゆうかく) (variant kanji).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 遊郭(ゆうかく)跡(あと): site of a former pleasure quarter
  • 遊郭(ゆうかく)建築(けんちく): pleasure quarter architecture
