1. suppuration; festering; becoming infected with pus
The formation of pus in a wound or infected area. A medical term used in both clinical and everyday contexts when a wound becomes infected.
傷(きず)が化膿(かのう)してしまった。
The wound became infected.
化膿(かのう)を防(ふせ)ぐために消毒(しょうどく)した。
I disinfected it to prevent infection.
傷口(きずぐち)が化膿(かのう)して赤(あか)く腫(は)れてきたので、病院(びょういん)に行(い)くことにした。
The wound festered and became red and swollen, so I decided to go to the hospital.

USAGE:
化膿(かのう) is used both as a medical term and in everyday conversation when describing infected wounds. It is commonly used with する to form a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傷(きず)が化膿(かのう)する (a wound festers)
  • 化膿(かのう)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent infection)
  • 化膿(かのう)止(ど)め (antiseptic — prevents suppuration)
  • 化膿(かのう)性(せい) (purulent, suppurative)

MEDICAL CONTEXT:
A doctor might say 化膿(かのう)しています (it's infected) when examining a wound. Over-the-counter 化膿(かのう)止(ど)め (antiseptic) products are commonly available at pharmacies.
