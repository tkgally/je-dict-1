異聞(いぶん)

いぶん
noun
strange tale; variant account; unusual story
1. strange tale; unusual story; curious report
A story about something strange or extraordinary, especially supernatural or mysterious events.
各地(かくち)の異聞(いぶん)を集(あつ)めた。
I collected strange tales from various regions.
この村(むら)には古(ふる)くから伝(つた)わる異聞(いぶん)が多(おお)い。
This village has many strange tales passed down from old times.
妖怪(ようかい)異聞(いぶん)をテーマにした小説(しょうせつ)を読(よ)んだ。
I read a novel themed around tales of yōkai.
2. variant account; alternative version
A different version or differing account of a known story or historical event.
この歴史(れきし)には異聞(いぶん)がある。
There is a variant account of this history.
正史(せいし)とは異(こと)なる異聞(いぶん)が残(のこ)されている。
An alternative account differing from the official history has been preserved.
本能寺(ほんのうじ)の変(へん)にまつわる異聞(いぶん)は数多(かずおお)く存在(そんざい)する。
Numerous variant accounts exist regarding the Incident at Honnōji.

FORMATION:
異(い) (different, strange) + 聞(ぶん) (hearing, report).

USAGE:
A literary word often found in book titles, folklore collections, and historical writing. Sense 1 emphasizes the strangeness of the story, while sense 2 emphasizes that the account differs from the standard version. Frequently appears in titles of games, manga, and novels (e.g., 〇〇異聞(いぶん)).
