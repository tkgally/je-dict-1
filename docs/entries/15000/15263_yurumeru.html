緩(ゆる)める

ゆるめる
verb, ichidan
to loosen; to slacken; to relax
1. to loosen; to slacken; to make less tight
To reduce the tightness or tension of something physical, such as a belt, screw, or grip.
ネクタイを緩(ゆる)めた。
I loosened my necktie.
ベルトを一(ひと)つ穴(あな)を緩(ゆる)めた。
I loosened my belt by one notch.
食事(しょくじ)の後(あと)、彼(かれ)はソファに座(すわ)ってネクタイを緩(ゆる)め、一息(ひといき)ついた。
After the meal, he sat down on the sofa, loosened his tie, and took a breather.
2. to relax; to ease; to let up on
To reduce the intensity or strictness of rules, vigilance, speed, or effort.
速度(そくど)を緩(ゆる)めた。
I slowed down.
規制(きせい)を緩(ゆる)める動(うご)きがある。
There is a move to relax regulations.
ここで気(き)を緩(ゆる)めると、これまでの努力(どりょく)が無駄(むだ)になってしまう。
If you let your guard down here, all your efforts so far will go to waste.

緩(ゆる)める is a transitive ichidan verb. The intransitive counterpart is 緩(ゆる)む (to become loose).

ASPECT (ている):
緩(ゆる)めている indicates the ongoing action of loosening, or a state where something has been loosened.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]を緩(ゆる)める (to loosen something)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ネクタイを緩(ゆる)める (to loosen a necktie)
  • 速度(そくど)を緩(ゆる)める (to reduce speed)
  • 規制(きせい)を緩(ゆる)める (to relax regulations)
  • 気(き)を緩(ゆる)める (to let one's guard down)
  • 手綱(たづな)を緩(ゆる)める (to slacken the reins)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 緩(ゆる)める: to loosen, to relax (general)
  • 弛(ゆる)める: to slacken (variant kanji, same reading)
  • 解(ほど)く: to untie, to undo (completely releasing a knot)
