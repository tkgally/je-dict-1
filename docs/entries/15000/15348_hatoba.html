波止場(はとば)

はとば
noun
wharf; pier; quay
1. wharf; pier; quay; dock
A structure built along a waterfront where ships can load and unload cargo or passengers. Often evokes an atmospheric, slightly nostalgic image of port towns.
波止場(はとば)に船(ふね)が停(と)まっている。
A ship is moored at the wharf.
波止場(はとば)で魚釣(さかなつ)りをしている人(ひと)がいた。
There were people fishing at the pier.
夕暮(ゆうぐ)れの波止場(はとば)を散歩(さんぽ)するのが好(す)きだ。
I like strolling along the wharf at dusk.

USAGE:
A somewhat literary or evocative word. In everyday speech, 港(みなと) (port, harbor) is more common. 波止場(はとば) often appears in literature, songs, and nostalgic descriptions of port towns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 波止場(はとば)で待(ま)つ (to wait at the wharf)
  • 波止場(はとば)の風景(ふうけい) (wharf scenery)
  • 波止場(はとば)で働(はたら)く (to work at the docks)

WORD FORMATION:
波止(はと) (breakwater) + 場(ば) (place). 波止(はと) comes from 波(なみ) (wave) + 止(と)める (to stop).
