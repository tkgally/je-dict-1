年(とし)の瀬(せ)

としのせ
noun
year-end; end of the year
1. year-end; end of the year
The closing period of the year, typically referring to December and especially the last few weeks. Carries a sense of busyness and urgency as the year draws to a close.
年(とし)の瀬(せ)が迫(せま)ってきた。
The end of the year is approaching.
年(とし)の瀬(せ)は買(か)い物(もの)で忙(いそが)しい。
The year-end is busy with shopping.
年(とし)の瀬(せ)も押(お)し迫(せま)り、大掃除(おおそうじ)を始(はじ)めなければならない。
With the end of the year closing in, I need to start the big year-end cleaning.

USAGE:
年(とし)の瀬(せ) evokes the feeling of time rushing forward like a river current (瀬(せ) means "rapids" or "shallows"). It conveys the busyness and emotional weight of the year-end period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年(とし)の瀬(せ)が迫(せま)る (the year-end approaches)
  • 年(とし)の瀬(せ)も押(お)し迫(せま)る (the year-end is closing in)
  • 年(とし)の瀬(せ)を迎(むか)える (to reach the year-end)

CULTURAL NOTE:
The Japanese year-end is marked by 大掃除(おおそうじ) (thorough house cleaning), 年賀状(ねんがじょう) (New Year's cards), and preparations for 正月(しょうがつ) (New Year).
