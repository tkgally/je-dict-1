破(やぶ)れ目(め)

やぶれめ
noun
tear; rip; rent
1. tear; rip; rent; hole
A hole or split in fabric, paper, or similar material caused by tearing.
シャツに破(やぶ)れ目(め)がある。
There's a tear in the shirt.
障子(しょうじ)の破(やぶ)れ目(め)から風(かぜ)が入(はい)ってくる。
Wind comes in through the tear in the shoji screen.
袋(ふくろ)の破(やぶ)れ目(め)から中身(なかみ)がこぼれ落(お)ちてしまった。
The contents spilled out through the tear in the bag.

USAGE:
破(やぶ)れ目(め) refers to the specific spot where something has torn. The 目(め) suffix indicates a point or spot, similar to its use in 切(き)れ目(め) (cut, gap) and 割(わ)れ目(め) (crack).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 破(やぶ)れ目(め)を繕(つくろ)う (to mend a tear)
  • 破(やぶ)れ目(め)から覗(のぞ)く (to peek through a tear)
  • 破(やぶ)れ目(め)が広(ひろ)がる (a tear spreads)
