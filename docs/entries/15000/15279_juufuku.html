重複(じゅうふく)

じゅうふく
noun, suru-verb
duplication; overlap; redundancy
1. duplication; overlap; redundancy; repetition
The state of something occurring or being included more than once, or the act of duplicating. Common in administrative, data, and organizational contexts.
データに重複(じゅうふく)がないか確認(かくにん)する。
Check whether there are any duplicates in the data.
申(もう)し込(こ)みが重複(じゅうふく)していたので、一件(いっけん)取(と)り消(け)した。
The application was duplicated, so I canceled one.
業務(ぎょうむ)の重複(じゅうふく)を避(さ)けるために、各部署(かくぶしょ)の役割(やくわり)を明確(めいかく)にする必要(ひつよう)がある。
To avoid overlap in tasks, it is necessary to clarify the roles of each department.

READING:
Both じゅうふく and ちょうふく are accepted readings. じゅうふく is the more common pronunciation in everyday use, while ちょうふく is the traditional reading preferred in formal or broadcasting contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重複(じゅうふく)を避(さ)ける (to avoid duplication)
  • 重複(じゅうふく)を排除(はいじょ)する (to eliminate duplicates)
  • 重複(じゅうふく)登録(とうろく) (duplicate registration)
  • 重複(じゅうふく)部分(ぶぶん) (overlapping portion)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 重複(じゅうふく): duplication, overlap (formal, systematic)
  • 繰(く)り返(かえ)し: repetition (general, everyday)
  • 二重(にじゅう): double, twofold (specifically two layers or occurrences)
