学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)

がくせきばんごう
noun
student ID number; enrollment number
1. student ID number; enrollment number
A unique number assigned to each student upon enrollment at a school or university, used for administrative purposes such as exams, records, and identification.
学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in your student ID number.
試験(しけん)の答案用紙(とうあんようし)に名前(なまえ)と学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)を書(か)いた。
I wrote my name and student ID number on the exam answer sheet.
入学(にゅうがく)すると学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)が割(わ)り当(あ)てられ、卒業(そつぎょう)するまでその番号(ばんごう)で管理(かんり)される。
Upon enrollment, a student ID number is assigned, and you are managed under that number until graduation.

USAGE:
学籍番号(がくせきばんごう) is a compound of 学籍(がくせき) (student registration/enrollment) and 番号(ばんごう) (number). It is used primarily in university and school contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)を記入(きにゅう)する (to fill in one's student ID number)
  • 学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)順(じゅん) (in order of student ID number)
  • 学籍番号(がくせきばんごう)で検索(けんさく)する (to search by student ID number)

SIMILAR TERMS:
出席番号(しゅっせきばんごう) (attendance number) is a similar concept used in elementary and secondary schools, typically assigned in alphabetical order by surname.
