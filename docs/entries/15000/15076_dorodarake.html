泥(どろ)だらけ

どろだらけ
adjective
covered in mud; muddy
1. covered in mud; muddy; mud-splattered
Describes something that is completely covered or smeared with mud.
子(こ)どもが泥(どろ)だらけで帰(かえ)ってきた。
The child came home covered in mud.
雨(あめ)の後(あと)、靴(くつ)が泥(どろ)だらけになった。
My shoes got muddy after the rain.
泥(どろ)だらけの手(て)で触(さわ)らないで。
Don't touch things with your muddy hands.

USAGE:
泥(どろ)だらけ uses the suffix ～だらけ, which means "covered in" or "full of" (usually something unpleasant). It functions as a の-adjective modifying nouns (泥(どろ)だらけの服(ふく)) or with the copula (泥(どろ)だらけだ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 泥(どろ)だらけの服(ふく) (mud-covered clothes)
  • 泥(どろ)だらけになる (to get covered in mud)
  • 泥(どろ)だらけの道(みち) (muddy road)

PATTERN:
The ～だらけ suffix attaches to nouns to mean "covered with" or "full of": 傷(きず)だらけ (covered in scratches), 血(ち)だらけ (covered in blood), 間違(まちが)いだらけ (full of mistakes). It almost always has a negative or unpleasant connotation.
