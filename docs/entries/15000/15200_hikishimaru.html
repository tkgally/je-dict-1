引(ひ)き締(し)まる

ひきしまる
verb (godan)
to tighten; to become firm; to brace oneself
1. to tighten; to become firm; to be toned
Used for physical things becoming firm or taut, such as muscles, skin, or a body becoming well-toned.
体(からだ)が引(ひ)き締(し)まった。
My body became toned.
ジョギングで引(ひ)き締(し)まった足(あし)になった。
Jogging gave me toned legs.
冷(つめ)たい水(みず)で顔(かお)を洗(あら)うと肌(はだ)が引(ひ)き締(し)まる。
Washing your face with cold water tightens the skin.
2. to become tense; to brace oneself
Used figuratively for a mental state of heightened focus or seriousness, such as when facing an important situation.
気持(きも)ちが引(ひ)き締(し)まった。
I braced myself mentally.
新(あたら)しい年(とし)を迎(むか)えて気(き)が引(ひ)き締(し)まる。
Welcoming the new year makes me feel a renewed sense of resolve.
部長(ぶちょう)の言葉(ことば)を聞(き)いて、チーム全体(ぜんたい)の表情(ひょうじょう)が引(ひ)き締(し)まった。
After hearing the department head's words, the whole team's expressions became serious.

USAGE:
This is an intransitive verb. The transitive counterpart is 引(ひ)き締(し)める (to tighten, to discipline).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 体(からだ)が引(ひ)き締(し)まる (the body becomes toned)
  • 気(き)が引(ひ)き締(し)まる (to feel a sense of resolve)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)が引(ひ)き締(し)まる (one's expression becomes serious)
  • 引(ひ)き締(し)まった体(からだ) (a firm, toned body)
