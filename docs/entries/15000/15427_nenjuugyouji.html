年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ)

ねんじゅうぎょうじ
noun
annual event; yearly function
1. annual event; yearly function; regular annual observance
An event or ceremony held at the same time every year, such as festivals, holidays, and seasonal observances. Can also refer figuratively to something that happens routinely every year.
花見(はなみ)は日本(にほん)の年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ)だ。
Cherry blossom viewing is a Japanese annual event.
学校(がっこう)の年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ)には運動会(うんどうかい)や文化祭(ぶんかさい)がある。
The school's annual events include sports day and the culture festival.
大掃除(おおそうじ)は年末(ねんまつ)の年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ)として定着(ていちゃく)している。
Year-end deep cleaning has become an established annual event.

USAGE:
年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ) covers both traditional events (festivals, religious observances) and modern institutional events (school ceremonies, corporate traditions). Can also be used humorously for things that happen predictably every year.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日本(にほん)の年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ) (Japanese annual events)
  • 伝統的(でんとうてき)な年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ) (traditional annual events)
  • 年中行事(ねんじゅうぎょうじ)のカレンダー (calendar of annual events)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 行事(ぎょうじ): event, function
  • 祭(まつ)り: festival
  • 風習(ふうしゅう): custom, practice
  • 恒例(こうれい): customary, annual (used as modifier)
