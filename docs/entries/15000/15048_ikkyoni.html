一挙(いっきょ)に

いっきょに
adverb
at once, in one go, all at once
1. at once, in one go, all at once
Doing or achieving something all at one time, in a single action or effort, rather than gradually.
問題(もんだい)を一挙(いっきょ)に解決(かいけつ)した。
I solved the problems all at once.
気温(きおん)が一挙(いっきょ)に下(さ)がった。
The temperature dropped all at once.
このアプリを使(つか)えば、複数(ふくすう)のファイルを一挙(いっきょ)に変換(へんかん)できる。
With this app, you can convert multiple files all at once.

USAGE: Emphasizes doing something in a single stroke or burst rather than in stages. It often appears in formal writing and news reporting but is also used in everyday speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一挙(いっきょ)に解決(かいけつ)する: to resolve all at once
  • 一挙(いっきょ)に片付(かたづ)ける: to deal with everything in one go
  • 一挙(いっきょ)に進(すす)む: to advance all at once

RELATED:

  • 一気(いっき)に: in one breath, in one go (more colloquial)
  • 一度(いちど)に: all at one time
  • 一挙両得(いっきょりょうとく): killing two birds with one stone (yojijukugo)
