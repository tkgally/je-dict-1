組(く)み伏(ふ)せる

くみふせる
verb, ichidan
to pin someone down; to wrestle to the ground
1. to pin down; to wrestle to the ground; to overpower
To grapple with someone and force them face-down onto the ground, holding them in place. Often used for police subduing suspects or in martial arts contexts.
犯人(はんにん)を組(く)み伏(ふ)せた。
He pinned the criminal down.
警察官(けいさつかん)が暴(あば)れる男(おとこ)を組(く)み伏(ふ)せて逮捕(たいほ)した。
The police officer pinned down the violent man and arrested him.
通行人(つうこうにん)が協力(きょうりょく)して逃(に)げようとした犯人(はんにん)を組(く)み伏(ふ)せ、警察(けいさつ)が到着(とうちゃく)するまで押(お)さえていた。
Passersby cooperated to pin down the fleeing suspect and held him until the police arrived.

組(く)み伏(ふ)せる is a transitive ichidan verb. It is a compound of 組(く)む (to grapple, to wrestle) and 伏(ふ)せる (to lay face-down).

ASPECT (ている):
組(く)み伏(ふ)せている indicates the resulting state of having someone pinned down.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [person]を組(く)み伏(ふ)せる (to pin someone down)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 犯人(はんにん)を組(く)み伏(ふ)せる (to pin down a criminal)
  • 地面(じめん)に組(く)み伏(ふ)せる (to wrestle to the ground)
  • 取(と)り押(お)さえて組(く)み伏(ふ)せる (to seize and pin down)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 組(く)み伏(ふ)せる: to pin down physically by wrestling
  • 取(と)り押(お)さえる: to apprehend, to restrain (more general)
  • 押(お)さえつける: to hold down, to press down
