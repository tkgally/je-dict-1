霧吹(きりふ)き

きりふき
noun
spray bottle; mist sprayer; atomizer
1. spray bottle; mist sprayer; atomizer
A container with a pump mechanism that releases liquid as a fine mist. Used for watering plants, ironing, cleaning, and other household purposes.
霧吹(きりふ)きで水(みず)をかける。
Spray water with a mist sprayer.
植物(しょくぶつ)に霧吹(きりふ)きで水(みず)をやるのが日課(にっか)だ。
Misting the plants with a spray bottle is my daily routine.
アイロンをかける前(まえ)に霧吹(きりふ)きで服(ふく)を湿(しめ)らせると皺(しわ)が取(と)れやすい。
If you dampen the clothes with a spray bottle before ironing, the wrinkles come out more easily.

USAGE:
霧吹(きりふ)き refers to any spray bottle that produces a fine mist. It is a compound of 霧(きり) (mist) and 吹(ふ)き (blowing).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 霧吹(きりふ)きで水(みず)をかける (to spray water)
  • 霧吹(きりふ)きで湿(しめ)らせる (to moisten with a spray bottle)
  • 霧吹(きりふ)きを使(つか)う (to use a spray bottle)
