上(のぼ)り下(くだ)り

のぼりくだり
noun
going up and down; ascent and descent
1. going up and down; ascent and descent
The physical act of going uphill and downhill, or ascending and descending. Used for slopes, stairs, and roads.
この道(みち)は上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りが多(おお)い。
This road has many ups and downs.
階段(かいだん)の上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りだけで息(いき)が切(き)れる。
Just going up and down the stairs leaves me out of breath.
山道(やまみち)の上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りを繰(く)り返(かえ)しながら、やっと頂上(ちょうじょう)に着(つ)いた。
After repeatedly going up and down mountain paths, we finally reached the summit.
2. ups and downs; fluctuations
Figurative use referring to fluctuations, variations, or instability in conditions, prices, or emotions.
株価(かぶか)の上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りが激(はげ)しい。
The stock price fluctuations are intense.
気温(きおん)の上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りが大(おお)きくて、体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)しやすい。
The large temperature fluctuations make it easy to get sick.
人生(じんせい)には上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りがあるものだから、落(お)ち込(こ)みすぎないほうがいい。
Life has its ups and downs, so it's better not to get too depressed.

USAGE:
上(のぼ)り下(くだ)り describes both literal vertical movement and figurative fluctuations. The literal sense is common when talking about terrain, stairs, or slopes, while the figurative sense applies to prices, temperatures, and life circumstances.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りが多(おお)い (has many ups and downs)
  • 上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りが激(はげ)しい (has extreme fluctuations)
  • 上(のぼ)り下(くだ)りを繰(く)り返(かえ)す (to repeatedly go up and down)
