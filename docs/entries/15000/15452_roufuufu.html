老夫婦(ろうふうふ)

ろうふうふ
noun
elderly couple; old married couple
1. elderly couple; old married couple
A married couple who are both elderly. Often used warmly to describe a couple who have been together for a long time.
老夫婦(ろうふうふ)が手(て)をつないで歩(ある)いていた。
An elderly couple was walking hand in hand.
隣(となり)に住(す)む老夫婦(ろうふうふ)はとても仲(なか)がいい。
The elderly couple living next door get along very well.
その老夫婦(ろうふうふ)は五十(ごじゅう)年(ねん)以上(いじょう)も一緒(いっしょ)に暮(く)らしてきたそうだ。
I hear that elderly couple has lived together for over fifty years.

FORMATION:
老(ろう) (old, elderly) + 夫婦(ふうふ) (married couple). A straightforward compound.

RELATED:

  • 若夫婦(わかふうふ) (young married couple)
  • 新婚夫婦(しんこんふうふ) (newlywed couple)
  • 老人(ろうじん) (elderly person)

NUANCE:
Generally a neutral or warm term. In stories and narratives, 老夫婦(ろうふうふ) often appears in contexts that emphasize long companionship or quiet, settled lives.
