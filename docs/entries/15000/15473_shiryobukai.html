思慮深(しりょぶか)い

しりょぶかい
i-adjective
thoughtful; prudent; considerate
1. thoughtful; prudent; considerate; deliberate
Describing someone who thinks carefully before acting, showing deep consideration and good judgment.
彼女(かのじょ)は思慮深(しりょぶか)い人(ひと)だ。
She is a thoughtful person.
思慮深(しりょぶか)い判断(はんだん)のおかげで問題(もんだい)を避(さ)けられた。
Thanks to a prudent decision, we were able to avoid the problem.
リーダーには思慮深(しりょぶか)く行動(こうどう)する能力(のうりょく)が求(もと)められる。
Leaders are expected to have the ability to act thoughtfully.

FORMATION:
Compound adjective from 思慮(しりょ) (thought, deliberation) + 深(ふか)い (deep). The reading uses sequential voicing (rendaku): ふかい → ぶかい.

USAGE:
A formal, written-style adjective used as praise for someone's careful, measured character. Often describes leaders, mentors, or wise individuals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 思慮深(しりょぶか)い人(ひと) (a thoughtful person)
  • 思慮深(しりょぶか)い判断(はんだん) (a prudent judgment)
  • 思慮深(しりょぶか)く行動(こうどう)する (to act thoughtfully)

CONTRAST:
思慮(しりょ)が浅(あさ)い describes the opposite — being shallow in thought or rash.
