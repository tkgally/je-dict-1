聴解(ちょうかい)

ちょうかい
noun
listening comprehension
1. listening comprehension; aural comprehension
The ability to understand spoken language, or the act of doing so. Used mainly in language education and proficiency testing contexts.
聴解(ちょうかい)の試験(しけん)が苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm bad at listening comprehension exams.
毎日(まいにち)ポッドキャストを聞(き)いて聴解(ちょうかい)力(りょく)を上(あ)げている。
I listen to podcasts every day to improve my listening comprehension.
JLPTの聴解(ちょうかい)セクションでは、会話(かいわ)やアナウンスを聞(き)いて答(こた)える。
In the JLPT listening section, you listen to conversations and announcements and answer questions.

USAGE:
聴解(ちょうかい) is used almost exclusively in educational and testing contexts. It is one of the standard skill categories in language proficiency tests such as the JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 聴解(ちょうかい)力(りょく) (listening comprehension ability)
  • 聴解(ちょうかい)問題(もんだい) (listening comprehension questions)
  • 聴解(ちょうかい)練習(れんしゅう) (listening comprehension practice)
  • 聴解(ちょうかい)セクション (listening section)

SIMILAR WORDS:
Paired with 読解(どっかい) (reading comprehension) in language testing. While 読解(どっかい) covers written text, 聴解(ちょうかい) covers spoken audio.
