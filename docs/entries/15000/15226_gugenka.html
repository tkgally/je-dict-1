具現化(ぐげんか)

ぐげんか
noun
embodiment; realization; materialization
1. embodiment; realization; materialization
The process of making an abstract idea, concept, or vision into something concrete and tangible. Often used for bringing plans, ideals, or creative visions to life.
アイデアを具現化(ぐげんか)する。
To bring an idea to life.
理想(りそう)を具現化(ぐげんか)するのは簡単(かんたん)ではない。
It's not easy to make ideals a reality.
デザイナーの構想(こうそう)を具現化(ぐげんか)するために、エンジニアチームが協力(きょうりょく)した。
The engineering team collaborated to bring the designer's vision to life.

USAGE:
具現化(ぐげんか) emphasizes the transformation from abstract to concrete. It is commonly used in business, design, and creative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 具現化(ぐげんか)する (to materialize, to realize)
  • アイデアの具現化(ぐげんか) (realization of an idea)
  • 理想(りそう)の具現化(ぐげんか) (embodiment of an ideal)
  • 構想(こうそう)を具現化(ぐげんか)する (to bring a concept to life)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 実現(じつげん) (realization, achievement) is more general and common in everyday use.
  • 具体化(ぐたいか) (making concrete) focuses on turning vague plans into specific details.
