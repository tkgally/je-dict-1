肉屋(にくや)

にくや
noun
butcher shop; butcher
1. butcher shop; meat shop
A shop that sells fresh meat.
肉屋(にくや)で買(か)った。
I bought it at the butcher shop.
駅前(えきまえ)の肉屋(にくや)はコロッケが有名(ゆうめい)だ。
The butcher shop in front of the station is famous for its croquettes.
この辺(あた)りには昔(むかし)ながらの肉屋(にくや)がまだ残(のこ)っている。
There are still old-fashioned butcher shops remaining in this area.
2. butcher; meat seller
A person who sells meat at a shop.
肉屋(にくや)のおじさんが親切(しんせつ)だ。
The butcher is friendly.
肉屋(にくや)に薄切(うすぎ)りにしてもらった。
I had the butcher slice it thin.
肉屋(にくや)に相談(そうだん)したら、すき焼(や)きには肩(かた)ロースがいいと勧(すす)められた。
When I asked the butcher, he recommended chuck roll for sukiyaki.

FORMATION: 肉(にく) (meat) + 屋(や) (shop/person). The suffix 屋(や) can refer to both the shop and the shopkeeper.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 肉屋(にくや)で買(か)う: to buy at a butcher shop
  • 肉屋(にくや)のコロッケ: butcher shop croquettes (a classic Japanese takeaway item)
  • 町(まち)の肉屋(にくや): neighborhood butcher shop

CULTURE: Traditional 肉屋(にくや) in Japan often sell ready-made fried items like croquettes (コロッケ) and 串(くし)カツ alongside raw meat. Many neighborhoods still have independent butcher shops, though supermarkets have become the primary place to buy meat.
