立(た)て札(ふだ)

たてふだ
noun
notice board; signpost; standing sign
1. notice board; signpost; standing sign
A sign posted upright on a board or stake, typically placed outdoors to convey warnings, information, or rules. Historically used by authorities to post official notices.
公園(こうえん)に立(た)て札(ふだ)がある。
There is a notice board in the park.
立(た)て札(ふだ)に「立入禁止(たちいりきんし)」と書(か)いてあった。
The sign read 'No Entry.'
古(ふる)い神社(じんじゃ)の前(まえ)に、歴史(れきし)を説明(せつめい)する立(た)て札(ふだ)が設置(せっち)されていた。
In front of the old shrine, a signpost explaining its history had been set up.

USAGE:
立(た)て札(ふだ) refers to a physical sign that is posted upright, often made of wood. In historical contexts, authorities posted official decrees on 立(た)て札(ふだ) at major intersections.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 立(た)て札(ふだ)を立(た)てる (to put up a sign)
  • 立(た)て札(ふだ)に従(したが)う (to follow the sign)
  • 注意(ちゅうい)の立(た)て札(ふだ) (a warning sign)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 標識(ひょうしき): sign, marker
  • 掲示板(けいじばん): bulletin board
  • 札(ふだ): tag, label, sign
