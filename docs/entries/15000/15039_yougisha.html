容疑者(ようぎしゃ)

ようぎしゃ
noun
suspect
1. suspect, suspected person
A person suspected of having committed a crime. The standard legal/media term used before charges are filed.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が逮捕(たいほ)された。
The suspect was arrested.
警察(けいさつ)は容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の行方(ゆくえ)を追(お)っている。
The police are tracking the suspect's whereabouts.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)は犯行(はんこう)を否認(ひにん)しており、捜査(そうさ)は難航(なんこう)している。
The suspect is denying the crime, and the investigation is proving difficult.

USAGE: The standard term used in news reports and legal contexts for a person suspected of a crime. Japanese media follows strict terminology conventions depending on the stage of legal proceedings.

LEGAL TERMINOLOGY PROGRESSION:

  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ): suspect (before charges are filed)
  • 被疑者(ひぎしゃ): suspect (formal legal term, same stage)
  • 被告人(ひこくにん): defendant (after charges are filed)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を逮捕(たいほ)する: to arrest a suspect
  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)を特定(とくてい)する: to identify a suspect
  • 容疑者(ようぎしゃ)の身柄(みがら)を確保(かくほ)する: to take a suspect into custody
