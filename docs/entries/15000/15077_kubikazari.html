首飾(くびかざ)り

くびかざり
noun
necklace
1. necklace
An ornamental chain, string of beads, or similar jewelry worn around the neck.
真珠(しんじゅ)の首飾(くびかざ)りを贈(おく)った。
I gave her a pearl necklace as a gift.
祖母(そぼ)から受(う)け継(つ)いだ首飾(くびかざ)りを大切(たいせつ)にしている。
I treasure the necklace I inherited from my grandmother.
ドレスに合(あ)う首飾(くびかざ)りを探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a necklace that goes with this dress.

USAGE:
首飾(くびかざ)り is the native Japanese word for necklace. While ネックレス (from English "necklace") is also widely used, 首飾(くびかざ)り has a more traditional or literary feel and is often used for formal jewelry and precious ornaments.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真珠(しんじゅ)の首飾(くびかざ)り (pearl necklace)
  • 金(きん)の首飾(くびかざ)り (gold necklace)
  • 首飾(くびかざ)りをつける (to put on a necklace)
  • 首飾(くびかざ)りを外(はず)す (to take off a necklace)

WORD FORMATION:
首(くび) (neck) + 飾(かざ)り (ornament, decoration). Similar compounds include 髪飾(かみかざ)り (hair ornament) and 腕飾(うでかざ)り (bracelet).
