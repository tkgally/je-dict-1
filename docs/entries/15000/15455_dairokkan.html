第六感(だいろっかん)

だいろっかん
noun
sixth sense; intuition; gut feeling
1. sixth sense; intuition; gut feeling
An intuitive sense beyond the five physical senses — the ability to perceive or know something without conscious reasoning. Similar to English 'sixth sense.'
第六感(だいろっかん)が働(はたら)いた。
My sixth sense kicked in.
彼女(かのじょ)は第六感(だいろっかん)が鋭(するど)い人(ひと)だ。
She is a person with keen intuition.
第六感(だいろっかん)で何(なに)か良(よ)くないことが起(お)こりそうな気(き)がした。
My gut feeling told me something bad was about to happen.

USAGE:
Used the same way as "sixth sense" in English. Often appears with 働(はたら)く (to work, to kick in) or 鋭(するど)い (sharp, keen).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 第六感(だいろっかん)が働(はたら)く (one's sixth sense kicks in)
  • 第六感(だいろっかん)が鋭(するど)い (to have keen intuition)
  • 第六感(だいろっかん)で感(かん)じる (to sense with one's sixth sense)

RELATED:

  • 直感(ちょっかん) (intuition — more commonly used in everyday speech)
  • 勘(かん) (intuition, hunch — more casual)
