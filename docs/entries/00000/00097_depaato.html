デパート

でぱーと
noun
department store

Definitions

1. department store
A large retail store selling a wide variety of goods on multiple floors. From English 'department store.'

Examples

デパートで服(ふく)を買(か)いました。
I bought clothes at the department store.
デパートの地下(ちか)には食品(しょくひん)売(う)り場(ば)があります。
There's a food section in the department store basement.
このデパートは10時(じ)に開(あ)きます。
This department store opens at 10 o'clock.

Notes

デパート is short for 'department store.' Japanese department stores often have high-quality food halls in the basement (デパ地下) and restaurants on the top floors. Famous ones include Mitsukoshi, Isetan, and Takashimaya.
