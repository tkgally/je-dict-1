匂(にお)う

におう
godan verb
to smell; to be fragrant; to stink

Definitions

1. to smell; to give off a smell
To emit an odor, whether pleasant or unpleasant.
2. to be fragrant; to smell nice
To have a pleasant aroma.
3. to smack of; to hint at
To suggest or give an impression of something (figurative).

Examples

このバラはいい匂(にお)いが匂(にお)う。
This rose has a nice fragrance.
何(なに)か焦(こ)げた匂(にお)いが匂(にお)っている。
Something smells burnt.
この話(はなし)は犯罪(はんざい)の匂(にお)いが匂(にお)う。
This story smacks of crime.

Notes

匂(にお)う is intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). There is no common transitive pair.

ASPECT ({ている}):
匂(にお)っている indicates an ongoing state of smelling or giving off an odor.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • いい匂(にお)いが匂(にお)う (to smell nice)
  • 変(へん)な匂(にお)いが匂(にお)う (to smell strange)
  • 犯罪(はんざい)の匂(にお)いが匂(にお)う (to smack of crime)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～が匂(にお)う: What is giving off the smell

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 臭(くさ)い: Adjective meaning "smelly/stinky" (negative)
  • 香(かお)る: More literary, typically positive fragrance
  • 匂(にお)い: Noun form meaning "smell/scent"

The kanji {匂} is used for general smells, while {臭} specifically refers to bad odors.
