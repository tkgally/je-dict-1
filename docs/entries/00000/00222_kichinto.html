きちんと

きちんと
adverb
properly, neatly
1. properly, neatly, exactly
In a correct, orderly, or tidy manner.
きちんと片付(かたづ)けてください。
Please tidy up properly.
きちんと説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain properly.
服(ふく)をきちんと着(き)る。
To dress neatly.

USAGE:
きちんと emphasizes doing something correctly, properly, or in an orderly fashion.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ちゃんと (properly - more casual)
  • 正(ただ)しく (correctly - more formal)
  • きっちり (precisely, exactly)
