あさって

あさって
noun
day after tomorrow

Definitions

1. day after tomorrow
The day that comes after tomorrow. Part of the sequence: きょう (today) → あした (tomorrow) → あさって (day after tomorrow).

Examples

あさって会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet the day after tomorrow.
あさっては何曜日(なんようび)ですか。
What day of the week is the day after tomorrow?
試験(しけん)はあさってです。
The exam is the day after tomorrow.

Notes

Usually written in hiragana. Can also be written as 明後日(あさって) in kanji. Often used with particles like に (on) or は (topic marker).
