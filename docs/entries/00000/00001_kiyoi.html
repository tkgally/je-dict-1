清(きよ)い

きよい
i-adjective
clear; pure; clean; noble

Definitions

1. clear; pure; clean
Free from impurities or contamination, especially of water or air.
2. pure; innocent; noble
Morally upright, untainted by wrongdoing or impure motives.

Examples

山(やま)の清(きよ)い水(みず)を飲(の)んだ。
I drank the clear mountain water.
彼女(かのじょ)は心(こころ)が清(きよ)い人(ひと)だ。
She is a person with a pure heart.
清(きよ)い空気(くうき)を吸(す)いたい。
I want to breathe clean air.

Notes

清(きよ)い is an i-adjective with both physical (clean, clear) and moral (pure, noble) meanings.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 清(きよ)く
  • Noun form: 清(きよ)さ (purity)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 心(こころ)が清(きよ)い (pure-hearted)
  • 清(きよ)い水(みず) (clear/pure water)
  • 清(きよ)い空気(くうき) (clean air)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 清(きよ)い vs. 綺麗(きれい): 清(きよ)い emphasizes purity and clarity (often literary), while 綺麗(きれい) is more everyday and covers beauty, cleanliness, and tidiness.
  • 清(きよ)い vs. 清潔(せいけつ): 清潔(せいけつ) is more practical/hygienic, while 清(きよ)い has poetic/moral nuances.

Register: Somewhat literary/formal. More commonly used in written language, poetry, or formal contexts. In everyday speech, 綺麗(きれい) or 清潔(せいけつ) is more common.

Related Words

Related: きれい (beautiful, clean)
Related: 清潔(せいけつ) (hygienic, clean)
