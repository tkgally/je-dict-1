催(もよお)し

もよおし
noun
event; function; gathering

Definitions

1. event; function; occasion
An organized social or public gathering.
2. festivities; entertainment
Activities or performances held for enjoyment.

Examples

今週(こんしゅう)は色々(いろいろ)な催(もよお)しが予定(よてい)されている。
Various events are scheduled for this week.
地域(ちいき)の催(もよお)しに参加(さんか)した。
I participated in a local event.
催(もよお)し物(もの)の案内(あんない)をもらった。
I received information about the event/entertainment.

Notes

催(もよお)し is the noun form of the verb 催(もよお)す (to hold/organize an event), referring to organized events or gatherings.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 催(もよお)しに参加(さんか)する (to participate in an event)
  • 催(もよお)しを開(ひら)く (to hold an event)
  • 催(もよお)しの案内(あんない) (event announcement)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 催(もよお)し物(もの): Event, entertainment, show
  • 催(もよお)し会場(かいじょう): Event venue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 催(もよお)し vs. イベント: イベント is the loanword commonly used; 催(もよお)し is more traditional/formal Japanese.
  • 催(もよお)し vs. 行事(ぎょうじ): 行事(ぎょうじ) refers to regular or traditional events (school events, ceremonies); 催(もよお)し is broader and includes entertainment.

Register: Neutral to slightly formal. 催(もよお)し物(もの) (entertainments) is common in formal announcements.

Related Words

Related: 催(もよお)す (to hold (event))
Synonym: 行事(ぎょうじ) (event, function)
