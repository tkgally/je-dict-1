発揮(はっき)

はっき
noun
demonstration, display, exhibition (of ability)
1. demonstration, display, exhibition
The act of fully showing or utilizing one's abilities, skills, or power. Often used with する to form a verb.
彼女(かのじょ)は試合(しあい)で実力(じつりょく)を発揮(はっき)した。
She demonstrated her true ability in the match.
リーダーシップを発揮(はっき)して、チームをまとめた。
He demonstrated leadership and brought the team together.
緊張(きんちょう)して本来(ほんらい)の力(ちから)が発揮(はっき)できなかった。
I was nervous and couldn't demonstrate my true potential.

発揮(はっき)する means to demonstrate, display, or bring out one's abilities or qualities. It implies actively showing what one is capable of.

Common collocations:

  • 実力(じつりょく)を発揮(はっき)する (to demonstrate one's ability)
  • 能力(のうりょく)を発揮(はっき)する (to display one's capabilities)
  • リーダーシップを発揮(はっき)する (to show leadership)
  • 才能(さいのう)を発揮(はっき)する (to exhibit one's talent)
  • 効果(こうか)を発揮(はっき)する (to produce an effect)

Used with できる to express whether one was able to perform at one's best:

  • 力(ちから)を発揮(はっき)できた (was able to show my abilities)
  • 発揮(はっき)できなかった (couldn't perform at my best)
