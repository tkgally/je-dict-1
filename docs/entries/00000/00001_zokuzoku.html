続々(ぞくぞく)

ぞくぞく
adverb
one after another; in succession; continuously

Definitions

1. one after another; in succession
Describing things or people arriving or happening in continuous sequence.

Examples

参加者(さんかしゃ)が続々(ぞくぞく)と集(あつ)まってきた。
Participants gathered one after another.
新商品(しんしょうひん)が続々(ぞくぞく)発売(はつばい)されている。
New products are being released in succession.
問題(もんだい)が続々(ぞくぞく)と発覚(はっかく)した。
Problems came to light one after another.

Notes

続々(ぞくぞく) is an adverb describing continuous succession or sequence, often used with the particle と.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と集(あつ)まる (to gather in succession)
  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と届(とど)く (to arrive one after another)
  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と発表(はっぴょう)される (to be announced in succession)

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:

ぞくぞく can also be written as 族々(ぞくぞく) (rare) or in hiragana ぞくぞく meaning "to shiver" or "to be thrilled." Context usually makes the meaning clear:

  • 続々(ぞくぞく): Succession (written with {続})
  • ぞくぞくする: To shiver, feel chills, or feel thrilled (usually hiragana)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 続々(ぞくぞく) vs. 次々(つぎつぎ): Both mean "one after another," but 次々(つぎつぎ) emphasizes the sequential order, while 続々(ぞくぞく) emphasizes continuous flow.

Register: Neutral. Used in both formal and casual contexts.

Related Words

Synonym: 次々(つぎつぎ) (one after another)
