ゆっくり

ゆっくり
adverb
slowly, leisurely

Definitions

1. slowly, at a slow pace
Not quickly. Taking one's time to do something.
2. leisurely, relaxed
In a relaxed manner without hurrying.

Examples

ゆっくり話(はな)してください。
Please speak slowly.
Very useful for language learners
ゆっくり休(やす)んでください。
Please take it easy and rest.
Sense 2: relaxed
ゆっくり食(た)べましょう。
Let's eat slowly.

Notes

Always written in hiragana. One of the most useful words for asking someone to slow down. ゆっくり休(やす)んでください is a common phrase wishing someone to rest well. Can also be used as a na-adjective: ゆっくりな時間(じかん) (leisurely time).
