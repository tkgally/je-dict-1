客間(きゃくま)

きゃくま
noun
guest room, parlor
1. guest room, parlor, drawing room
A room in a house designated for receiving and entertaining guests.
お客(きゃく)さんを客間(きゃくま)に通(とお)した。
I showed the guest to the parlor.
客間(きゃくま)に花(はな)を飾(かざ)った。
I decorated the guest room with flowers.
日本(にほん)の伝統的(でんとうてき)な家(いえ)には客間(きゃくま)がある。
Traditional Japanese houses have a guest room.
客間(きゃくま)の掃除(そうじ)をして、お客(きゃく)さんを待(ま)った。
I cleaned the guest room and waited for the visitor.
久(ひさ)しぶりに親戚(しんせき)が来(く)るので、客間(きゃくま)に布団(ふとん)を用意(ようい)した。
Since relatives are coming after a long time, I prepared futons in the guest room.

客間(きゃくま) is a room specifically designated for receiving guests.

In traditional Japanese homes:

  • Often a tatami room (和室(わしつ))
  • May have a 床(とこ)の間(ま) (alcove) for decoration
  • Used for formal occasions and visitors

Related terms:

  • 居間(いま) (living room - for family use)
  • 応接間(おうせつま) (reception room - more formal)
  • 座敷(ざしき) (Japanese-style room, often for guests)
  • リビング (living room - modern style)

Usage:

  • 客間(きゃくま)に通(とお)す (to show someone to the guest room)
  • 客間(きゃくま)で{もてなす} (to entertain in the guest room)
