昼間(ひるま)

ひるま
noun
daytime

Definitions

1. daytime, during the day
The period of time when it is light outside, between morning and evening.

Examples

昼間(ひるま)は仕事(しごと)をしています。
I work during the day.
昼間(ひるま)はとても暑(あつ)い。
It's very hot during the daytime.
昼間(ひるま)に電話(でんわ)してください。
Please call me during the day.

Notes

昼間(ひるま) specifically refers to daylight hours.

TIME OF DAY TERMS:

  • 朝(あさ): morning
  • 昼(ひる): noon, daytime
  • 昼間(ひるま): daytime (emphasizes daylight hours)
  • 夕方(ゆうがた): evening
  • 夜(よる): night
  • 夜中(よなか): middle of the night

昼(ひる) vs 昼間(ひるま):

  • 昼(ひる): noon, midday (also used broadly for daytime)
  • 昼間(ひるま): daytime hours (contrasts with nighttime)

OPPOSITE: 夜間(やかん) (nighttime)

Related Words

Antonym: 夜間(やかん) (nighttime)
