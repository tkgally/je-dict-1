昨日(きのう)

きのう
noun
yesterday

Definitions

1. yesterday
The day before today. One of the most basic time expressions in Japanese.

Examples

昨日(きのう)は何(なに)をしましたか。
What did you do yesterday?
昨日(きのう)の天気(てんき)はよかったです。
Yesterday's weather was good.
の connects to following noun
昨日(きのう)映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I watched a movie yesterday.
Can be used without particle

Notes

Part of the basic time sequence: おととい (day before yesterday) → 昨日(きのう) (yesterday) → 今日(きょう) (today) → 明日(あした) (tomorrow) → あさって (day after tomorrow). Like other time words, it can often be used without a particle.
