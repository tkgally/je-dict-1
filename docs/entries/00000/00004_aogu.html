扇(あお)ぐ

あおぐ
godan verb
to fan, to flap

Definitions

1. to fan
To move air with a fan or similar object to create a breeze.
2. to incite, to stir up
To provoke or encourage (feelings, actions).

Examples

うちわで顔(かお)を扇(あお)ぐ。
I fan my face with a paper fan.
火(ひ)を扇(あお)いで燃(も)やす。
I fan the fire to make it burn.
不安(ふあん)を扇(あお)る報道(ほうどう)。
News coverage that stirs up anxiety.

Notes

扇(あお)ぐ means to fan or create air movement.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 顔(かお)を扇(あお)ぐ

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • うちわで扇(あお)ぐ: to fan with a paper fan
  • 火(ひ)を扇(あお)ぐ: to fan a fire
  • 不安(ふあん)を扇(あお)る: to stir up anxiety

FIGURATIVE USE:
扇(あお)る can mean to incite or inflame (emotions, conflict). In this sense, it's often written as 煽(あお)る.

NOTE: Don't confuse with 仰(あお)ぐ (to look up at, to respect).
