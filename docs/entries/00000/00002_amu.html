編(あ)む

あむ
godan verb
to knit, to braid, to weave
1. to knit, to crochet
To create fabric by interlocking loops of yarn with needles.
セーターを編(あ)む。
I knit a sweater.
母(はは)が手編(てあ)みのマフラーを作(つく)ってくれた。
My mother made me a hand-knitted scarf.
2. to braid, to plait
To weave strands together into a pattern.
髪(かみ)を三(み)つ編(あ)みにする。
I braid my hair into a three-strand plait.

編(あ)む is used for creating fabric or patterns by interlocking or weaving strands.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: セーターを編(あ)む

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手編(てあ)み: hand-knitted
  • 三(み)つ編(あ)み: three-strand braid
  • 編(あ)み物(もの): knitting, knitted item
  • 編(あ)み針(ばり): knitting needle

RELATED:

  • 織(お)る: to weave (on a loom)
  • 縫(ぬ)う: to sew
