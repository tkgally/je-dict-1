遅(おく)れる

おくれる
verb (ichidan)
to be late, to be delayed

Definitions

1. to be late
To arrive or happen after the expected or scheduled time. Often used with に to indicate what one is late for.
2. to fall behind, to lag
To be behind schedule or others in progress.

Examples

学校(がっこう)に遅(おく)れました。
I was late to school.
電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れています。
The train is delayed.
遅(おく)れてすみません。
Sorry I'm late.
Common apology

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 遅(おく)らせる (transitive, to delay something). Pattern: Xに遅(おく)れる (be late for X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state. 遅(おく)れている = 'is delayed/running late.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 学校(がっこう)に遅(おく)れる (be late for school)
  • 電車(でんしゃ)が遅(おく)れる (train is delayed)
  • 遅(おく)れてすみません (sorry I'm late)
  • 5分(ふん)遅(おく)れる (be 5 minutes late)

The apology 遅(おく)れてすみません is extremely common.

Related Words
