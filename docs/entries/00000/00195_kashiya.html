貸家(かしや)

かしや
noun
house for rent

Definitions

1. house for rent; rental house
A house or dwelling that is available for rent. Distinguished from apartments or single rooms.

Examples

この辺(あた)りに貸家(かしや)はありますか。
Are there any houses for rent around here?
貸家(かしや)の家賃(やちん)は月(つき)10万円(まんえん)です。
The rent for the rental house is 100,000 yen per month.
古(ふる)い貸家(かしや)を探(さが)している。
I'm looking for an old house for rent.

Notes

ALTERNATIVE READING: Also read as かしいえ, but かしや is more common.

SIGNS:

  • 「貸家(かしや)」 (House for Rent)
  • 「貸家(かしや)募集(ぼしゅう)」 (Rental house available)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 貸間(かしま) (room for rent)
  • 賃貸(ちんたい)住宅(じゅうたく) (rental housing - formal)
  • 借家(しゃくや) (rented house - from tenant's perspective)
  • 持(も)ち家(いえ) (owned home - opposite)

CONTRAST:

  • 貸家(かしや) (renting out - owner's perspective)
  • 借家(しゃくや) (renting - tenant's perspective)

NOTE: In real estate contexts, 賃貸(ちんたい)一戸建(いっこだ)て (rental single-family house) is often used in modern listings.

Related Words

Contrast: 借家(しゃくや) (rented house (tenant view))
