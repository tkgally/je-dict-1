ノー

のー
noun; interjection
no
1. no
A refusal or negative response, used in compound words or as an interjection.
ノーコメントでお願(ねが)いします。
No comment, please.
ノーと言(い)える勇気(ゆうき)
the courage to say no
彼(かれ)の提案(ていあん)にノーを突(つ)きつけた。
I firmly rejected his proposal.

ノー is the English "no" used in Japanese, primarily in compound words or set phrases rather than as a standalone refusal.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ノーコメント (no comment)
  • ノーマル (normal)
  • ノーベル賞(しょう) (Nobel Prize)
  • ノースリーブ (sleeveless)
  • ノーネクタイ (no necktie - casual dress code)

USAGE NOTE:
In everyday speech, Japanese speakers typically use いいえ, いや, or ううん to say "no" rather than ノー. ノー is mainly used in loanword compounds or for emphatic effect.

The opposite is イエス (yes).

Related Words

Antonym: イエス (yes)
Related: いいえ (no (native))
