恨(うら)み

うらみ
noun
resentment, grudge, bitterness

Definitions

1. resentment, grudge
A feeling of bitterness or ill will toward someone who has wronged you.
2. regret, complaint
A feeling of disappointment about circumstances.

Examples

彼(かれ)に恨(うら)みはない。
I hold no grudge against him.
長年(ながねん)の恨(うら)みを晴(は)らす。
I settle a long-standing grudge.
恨(うら)みを買(か)うようなことはしたくない。
I don't want to do anything that would make enemies.

Notes

恨(うら)み is a strong negative emotion of resentment or grudge.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 恨(うら)みを持(も)つ: to hold a grudge
  • 恨(うら)みを晴(は)らす: to get revenge, settle a grudge
  • 恨(うら)みを買(か)う: to incur someone's resentment
  • 恨(うら)みを残(のこ)す: to leave behind resentment

RELATED:

  • 恨(うら)む (verb): to resent, to hold a grudge
  • 恨(うら)めしい (adjective): resentful, reproachful
  • 恨(うら)み言(ごと): words of resentment

CULTURAL NOTE:
恨(うら)み appears frequently in Japanese literature, drama, and ghost stories. The concept of 恨(うら)み driving spirits to haunt the living is a common theme.
