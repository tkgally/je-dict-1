予約(よやく)

よやく
noun (verbal)
reservation, booking

Definitions

1. reservation, booking, appointment
An arrangement to have something at a particular time.

Examples

レストランの予約(よやく)をした。
I made a restaurant reservation.
予約(よやく)が必要(ひつよう)ですか。
Is a reservation required?

Notes

予約(よやく)する is the verbal form. Related: 予約(よやく)済(ず)み (reserved), 予約(よやく)を取(と)る (to make a reservation).
