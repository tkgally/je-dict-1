そのうち

そのうち
adverb
before long, eventually, sooner or later
1. before long, eventually, sooner or later
At some unspecified point in the near future; in due time.
そのうち分(わ)かるようになる。
You'll understand before long.
そのうち連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you eventually.
そのうち慣(な)れるから心配(しんぱい)しないで。
You'll get used to it before long, so don't worry.

そのうち means 'eventually/before long' but with a vague, unspecified timeline. It suggests something will happen naturally in due course.

USAGE NOTE:
When used casually (like そのうち連絡(れんらく)するよ), it can sound non-committal or evasive, implying 'someday, maybe' rather than a firm promise.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 近(ちか)いうちに: soon, in the near future (more definite)
  • やがて: before long, eventually (more literary)
  • いずれ: someday, eventually (can be more distant future)
  • もうすぐ: very soon, almost (more immediate)

The nuance is that そのうち doesn't specify when—it will happen when it happens.
