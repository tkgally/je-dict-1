ゴム

ごむ
noun
rubber, eraser

Definitions

1. rubber
An elastic material made from latex or synthetic compounds.
2. eraser
A rubber tool for erasing pencil marks.
3. rubber band, elastic
A loop of rubber for holding things together.

Examples

ゴム製(せい)の手袋(てぶくろ)をはめる。
I put on rubber gloves.
ゴムで間違(まちが)いを消(け)す。
I erase the mistake with an eraser.
髪(かみ)をゴムで結(むす)ぶ。
I tie my hair with a rubber band.

Notes

ゴム (from Dutch 'gom' or Portuguese 'goma') means rubber in Japanese.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 消(け)しゴム: eraser
  • 輪(わ)ゴム: rubber band
  • ゴム手袋(てぶくろ): rubber gloves
  • ゴム長靴(ながぐつ): rubber boots
  • ゴムひも: elastic cord

NOTE: 消(け)しゴム is the standard word for 'eraser' in Japanese. Sometimes shortened to just ゴム in context.

RELATED:

  • ガム: chewing gum (different word, similar sound)
