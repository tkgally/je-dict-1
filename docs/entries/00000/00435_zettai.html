絶対(ぜったい)

ぜったい
adverb, noun, no-adjective
absolutely, definitely; absolute
1. absolutely, definitely, surely
Without any doubt or exception; certainly.
絶対(ぜったい)に約束(やくそく)を守(まも)る。
I will definitely keep my promise.
あの映画(えいが)は絶対(ぜったい)見(み)たほうがいい。
You should absolutely see that movie.
2. absolute, unconditional
Complete and without limitations or conditions.
世(よ)の中(なか)に絶対(ぜったい)はない。
Nothing in this world is absolute.

絶対(ぜったい) can be used as an adverb meaning 'absolutely/definitely' or as a noun meaning 'absolute.'

ADVERBIAL USES:

  • 絶対(ぜったい)に + positive verb: definitely will
  • 絶対(ぜったい)に + negative verb: absolutely won't/never
  • 絶対(ぜったい)大丈夫(だいじょうぶ): definitely OK

NOUN/ADJECTIVE USES:

  • 絶対的(ぜったいてき): absolute (na-adjective form)
  • 絶対(ぜったい)零度(れいど): absolute zero
  • 絶対(ぜったい)王政(おうせい): absolute monarchy

OPPOSITE:

  • 相対(そうたい): relative

Related Words

Antonym: 相対(そうたい) (relative)
