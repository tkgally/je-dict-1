大変(たいへん)

たいへん
adverb
very, terribly

Definitions

1. very, terribly
To a great degree (adverb use).
2. difficult, serious
A situation that is hard to deal with (na-adjective use).

Examples

大変(たいへん)お世話(せわ)になりました。
Thank you very much for everything.
Sense 1: adverb
大変(たいへん)ですね。
That's tough / That sounds hard.
Sense 2: na-adjective
大変(たいへん)申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません。
I'm terribly sorry.
Sense 1: adverb

Notes

As a na-adjective: 大変(たいへん)な仕事(しごと) (difficult work). Often used to express sympathy.
