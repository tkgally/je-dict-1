タイプライター

たいぷらいたー
noun
typewriter

Definitions

1. typewriter
A mechanical or electromechanical machine for writing characters by pressing keys that cause inked types to strike paper. Largely replaced by computers but still used in some contexts.

Examples

祖父(そふ)は昔(むかし)タイプライターで手紙(てがみ)を打(う)っていた。
My grandfather used to type letters on a typewriter.
古(ふる)いタイプライターが骨董品(こっとうひん)として売(う)られている。
Old typewriters are being sold as antiques.
タイプライターの音(おと)がカタカタと響(ひび)いた。
The sound of the typewriter clattered.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY: From English 'typewriter'.

USAGE: While largely obsolete due to computers, typewriters are still:

  • Collected as antiques
  • Used for certain official documents in some countries
  • Appreciated for aesthetic/nostalgic value

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 和文(わぶん)タイプライター (Japanese typewriter)
  • タイプを打(う)つ (to type)
  • ワープロ (word processor - abbreviation of ワードプロセッサー)

NOTE: Japanese typewriters were complex mechanical devices with thousands of characters, unlike Western typewriters.
