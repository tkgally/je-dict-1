袋(ふくろ)

ふくろ
noun
bag, sack

Definitions

1. bag, sack
A flexible container made of paper, plastic, or cloth used for carrying or storing things.

Examples

袋(ふくろ)に入(い)れてください。
Please put it in a bag.
レジ袋(ぶくろ)はいりますか。
Do you need a plastic bag?
Common at stores
紙袋(かみぶくろ)をください。
Please give me a paper bag.

Notes

Common compounds:

  • レジ袋(ぶくろ) (shopping bag)
  • 紙袋(かみぶくろ) (paper bag)
  • ビニール袋(ぶくろ) (plastic bag). In Japan
  • stores often ask 袋(ふくろ)はいりますか since plastic bags may cost extra or be discouraged for environmental reasons.
