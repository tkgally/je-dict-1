買(か)う

かう
verb (godan)
to buy
1. to buy, to purchase
To acquire something by paying money. The basic verb for purchasing goods or services.
本(ほん)を買(か)う。
I buy a book.
どこで買(か)いましたか。
Where did you buy it?
新(あたら)しいパソコンを買(か)いたい。
I want to buy a new computer.
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くでコーヒーを買(か)った。
I bought coffee near the station.
誕生日(たんじょうび)に新(あたら)しい服(ふく)を買(か)ってもらった。とても嬉(うれ)しかった。
I got new clothes bought for me on my birthday. I was very happy.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを買(か)う (buy X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state or repeated action. 買(か)っている = 'have bought' or 'buy regularly.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 本(ほん)を買(か)う (buy a book)
  • 〜で買(か)う (buy at ~)
  • 買(か)い物(もの) (shopping - noun)
  • 買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)く (go shopping)

Opposite: 売(う)る (to sell).
