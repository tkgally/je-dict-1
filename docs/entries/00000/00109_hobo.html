ほぼ

ほぼ
adverb
almost; nearly; roughly; approximately

Definitions

1. almost; nearly
Very close to a complete state or quantity.
2. roughly; approximately
About, giving a general idea without exact precision.

Examples

仕事(しごと)はほぼ終(お)わった。
The work is almost finished.
参加者(さんかしゃ)はほぼ全員(ぜんいん)揃(そろ)った。
Almost all the participants have gathered.
駅(えき)からほぼ十分(じゅっぷん)で着(つ)きます。
It takes roughly ten minutes from the station.

Notes

ほぼ is an adverb meaning "almost" or "approximately," indicating near-completeness or approximation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ほぼ完成(かんせい) (almost complete)
  • ほぼ全員(ぜんいん) (almost everyone)
  • ほぼ同(おな)じ (almost the same)
  • ほぼ毎日(まいにち) (almost every day)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ほぼ vs. ほとんど: Both mean "almost," but ほとんど often implies "the great majority" and can be used with negative constructions (ほとんど～ない = hardly any). ほぼ is more neutral for approximations.
  • ほぼ vs. 約(やく): 約(やく) is used specifically for numerical approximations (約(やく)10人(にん) = about 10 people), while ほぼ is broader.
  • ほぼ vs. だいたい: だいたい is more casual and can mean "generally" as well as "approximately."

Register: Neutral. Common in both spoken and written Japanese.

Related Words

Synonym: ほとんど (almost, mostly)
Related: 約(やく) (about (numbers))
