日時(にちじ)

にちじ
noun
date and time
1. date and time
The specific day and hour when something takes place or is scheduled.
会議(かいぎ)の日時(にちじ)を決(き)めましょう。
Let's decide on the date and time of the meeting.
日時(にちじ)と場所(ばしょ)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please confirm the date, time, and location.
出発(しゅっぱつ)の日時(にちじ)は後(あと)で連絡(れんらく)します。
I'll contact you later about the date and time of departure.
面接(めんせつ)の日時(にちじ)が決(き)まったらメールで知(し)らせてください。
Please let me know by email when the date and time for the interview is decided.
日時(にちじ)の変更(へんこう)があった場合(ばあい)は、なるべく早(はや)くご連絡(れんらく)ください。
If there is a change in the date and time, please contact us as soon as possible.

日時(にちじ) combines 日(ひ) (day) and 時(とき) (time) to express both the date and time of an event. It is commonly used in formal contexts such as scheduling meetings, appointments, or events.

Common collocations:

  • 日時(にちじ)を決(き)める (to decide on a date and time)
  • 日時(にちじ)を指定(してい)する (to specify a date and time)
  • 日時(にちじ)を変更(へんこう)する (to change the date and time)

Often paired with 場所(ばしょ) (location) when communicating event details.
