なさる

なさる
verb
to do (honorific)

Definitions

1. to do (honorific)
Honorific form of する, used to show respect toward the person performing the action.

Examples

何(なに)をなさっていますか。
What are you doing?
Polite inquiry
お仕事(しごと)は何(なに)をなさっていますか。
What work do you do?
Asking about someone's occupation
どうなさいますか。
What would you like to do?
Polite offer

Notes

なさる is the honorific form of する, used when speaking about the actions of someone deserving respect.

CONJUGATION:

  • なさいます (polite)
  • なさって (te-form)
  • なさった (past)
  • なさい (command - as in お静(しず)かになさい)

KEIGO TYPE:

  • 尊敬語 (sonkeigo): Honorific language
  • Humble equivalent: いたす
