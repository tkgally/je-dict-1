役目(やくめ)

やくめ
noun
duty, role, function

Definitions

1. duty, role, part
A responsibility or function that someone is expected to fulfill.
2. function, purpose
The purpose or function that something serves.

Examples

司会(しかい)の役目(やくめ)を任(まか)された。
I was entrusted with the role of host/MC.
それは私(わたし)の役目(やくめ)ではありません。
That is not my job/responsibility.
この部品(ぶひん)は重要(じゅうよう)な役目(やくめ)を果(は)たしている。
This part plays an important function.

Notes

役目(やくめ) refers to duties, roles, or functions assigned to someone or something.

Common collocations:

  • 役目(やくめ)を果(は)たす (to fulfill one's duty/role)
  • 役目(やくめ)を終(お)える (to complete one's role)
  • 役目(やくめ)を任(まか)される (to be entrusted with a role)
  • 〜の役目(やくめ)がある (to have the function of ~)

Related terms:

  • 役割(やくわり) (role - similar but more commonly used for roles in society/relationships)
  • 任務(にんむ) (duty, mission - more formal)
  • 職務(しょくむ) (official duties)
  • 務(つと)め (duty, service)

Contrast:

  • 役目(やくめ) emphasizes the specific task or function
  • 役割(やくわり) emphasizes the position or part one plays
