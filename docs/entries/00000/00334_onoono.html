各々(おのおの)

おのおの
pronoun / adverb
each; every; respectively
1. each; every (person/thing)
Referring to each individual in a group separately.
各々(おのおの)が自分(じぶん)の役割(やくわり)を果(は)たした。
Each person fulfilled their own role.
各々(おのおの)の責任(せきにん)で行動(こうどう)してください。
Please act on your own responsibility.
Sense 1: each
各々(おのおの)が準備(じゅんび)を始(はじ)めた。
Each person began their preparations.
Sense 1: every
各々(おのおの)の事情(じじょう)がある。
Each has their own circumstances.
Sense 1: each
2. respectively; individually
Each one acting or existing separately.
参加者(さんかしゃ)は各々(おのおの)好(す)きな席(せき)に座(すわ)った。
The participants each sat in their preferred seats.
意見(いけん)は各々(おのおの)異(こと)なる。
Opinions differ for each person.
各々(おのおの)の持(も)ち場(ば)で働(はたら)く。
Work at your respective positions.
Sense 2: respectively
各々(おのおの)自分(じぶん)の仕事(しごと)に集中(しゅうちゅう)する。
Each concentrates on their own work.
Sense 2: individually
各々(おのおの)帰宅(きたく)した。
Each went home separately.
Sense 2: respectively
各々(おのおの)の考(かんが)え方(かた)を尊重(そんちょう)する。
Respect each person's way of thinking.
Sense 2: individually

各々(おのおの) is a pronoun/adverb meaning "each" or "respectively," emphasizing individual members of a group.

ALTERNATIVE WRITING:
Also written as 各(おの)々(おの) or sometimes 己々(おのおの). Can also be read as かくかく in very formal contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 各々(おのおの)が～ (each one ~)
  • 各々(おのおの)の～ (each person's ~)
  • 各々(おのおの)異(こと)なる (each differs)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 各々(おのおの) vs. それぞれ: Very similar meaning. それぞれ is more common in everyday speech; 各々(おのおの) is slightly more formal.
  • 各々(おのおの) vs. 各自(かくじ): 各自(かくじ) is more formal/official ("each person"); 各々(おのおの) can refer to things as well as people.
  • 各々(おのおの) vs. 銘々(めいめい): 銘々(めいめい) specifically refers to individual people in a group.

Register: Neutral to formal. Appropriate in both written and spoken Japanese, though それぞれ is more conversational.

Related Words

Synonym: それぞれ (each, respectively)
Related: 各自(かくじ) (each person)
