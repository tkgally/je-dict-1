血圧(けつあつ)

けつあつ
noun
blood pressure
1. blood pressure
The pressure of blood against the walls of the arteries.
血圧(けつあつ)を測(はか)ってもらった。
I had my blood pressure measured.
血圧(けつあつ)が高(たか)いので薬(くすり)を飲(の)んでいる。
I'm taking medication because my blood pressure is high.
塩分(えんぶん)の取(と)りすぎは血圧(けつあつ)を上(あ)げる。
Consuming too much salt raises blood pressure.
毎朝(まいあさ)血圧(けつあつ)を測(はか)っています。
I measure my blood pressure every morning.
医者(いしゃ)に血圧(けつあつ)が高(たか)いと言(い)われて、塩分(えんぶん)に気(き)をつけています。
The doctor told me my blood pressure is high, so I am watching my salt intake.

血圧(けつあつ) is the standard word at checkups, in pharmacies, and in everyday talk about health; it is not confined to medical writing.

READINGS ON A CHART:

  • 上(うえ)の血圧(けつあつ), formally 最高(さいこう)血圧(けつあつ) (systolic, the upper number)
  • 下(した)の血圧(けつあつ), formally 最低(さいてい)血圧(けつあつ) (diastolic, the lower number)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 血圧(けつあつ)を測(はか)る (to take a blood pressure reading)
  • 血圧(けつあつ)が高(たか)い / 低(ひく)い (blood pressure is high / low)
  • 血圧(けつあつ)を下(さ)げる / 上(あ)げる (to lower / raise blood pressure)
  • 血圧(けつあつ)計(けい) (blood pressure monitor)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 高(こう)血圧(けつあつ) (hypertension, as a diagnosed condition)
  • 低(てい)血圧(けつあつ) (hypotension)
