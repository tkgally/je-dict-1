摩擦(まさつ)

まさつ
noun
friction, rubbing; conflict
1. friction, rubbing
The resistance encountered when surfaces move against each other (physics).
2. friction, conflict, discord
Disagreement or tension between people, groups, or nations (figurative).
摩擦(まさつ)で熱(ねつ)が発生(はっせい)する。
Heat is generated by friction.
両国(りょうこく)の間(あいだ)に貿易(ぼうえき)摩擦(まさつ)が生(しょう)じた。
Trade friction arose between the two countries.
人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の摩擦(まさつ)を避(さ)けたい。
I want to avoid friction in interpersonal relationships.

摩擦(まさつ) is used both literally (physical friction) and figuratively (conflict between people/groups).

Physics context:

  • 摩擦(まさつ)力(りょく) (frictional force)
  • 摩擦(まさつ)係数(けいすう) (coefficient of friction)
  • 摩擦(まさつ)熱(ねつ) (frictional heat)

Social/political context:

  • 貿易(ぼうえき)摩擦(まさつ) (trade friction)
  • 国際(こくさい)摩擦(まさつ) (international friction)
  • 人間関係(にんげんかんけい)の摩擦(まさつ) (interpersonal conflict)

Related terms:

  • 衝突(しょうとつ) (collision, clash - more intense)
