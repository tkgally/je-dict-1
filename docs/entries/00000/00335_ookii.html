大(おお)きい

おおきい
adjective (i-adjective)
big, large
1. big, large (in size)
Describes physical size. Used for objects, spaces, animals, and anything that can be measured by size. The opposite is 小(ちい)さい (chiisai, small).
大(おお)きい犬(いぬ)だ。
It's a big dog.
この家(いえ)は大(おお)きいです。
This house is big.
もっと大(おお)きいのはありますか。
Do you have a bigger one?
この部屋(へや)は思(おも)ったより大(おお)きかった。
This room was bigger than I thought.
彼(かれ)の夢(ゆめ)は大(おお)きくて、いつか世界中(せかいじゅう)を旅行(りょこう)したいと言(い)っている。
His dreams are big—he says he wants to travel around the world someday.
2. loud (of sound/voice)
When describing voices or sounds, 大(おお)きい means loud. 大(おお)きい声(こえ) (ookii koe) means a loud voice.
声(こえ)が大(おお)きい。
Your voice is loud.
もっと大(おお)きい声(こえ)で。
Louder, please.
テレビの音(おと)が大(おお)きすぎる。
The TV is too loud.
大(おお)きい音(おと)で起(お)きた。
I woke up to a loud noise.
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)から大(おお)きい音(おと)が聞(き)こえてきて、眠(ねむ)れなかった。
I couldn't sleep because of the loud sounds coming from the next room.

大(おお)きい is an i-adjective. Before nouns, it can be used as 大(おお)きい or 大(おお)きな (ookina). 大(おお)きな is a special attributive form that can only be used directly before nouns (大(おお)きな家(いえ)), while 大(おお)きい can be used both attributively and predicatively. Conjugation: 大(おお)きくない (negative), 大(おお)きかった (past).
