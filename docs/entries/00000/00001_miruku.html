ミルク

みるく
noun
milk

Definitions

1. milk
Milk, especially processed or prepared milk for coffee, tea, or baby formula.

Examples

コーヒーにミルクを入(い)れる。
To put milk in coffee.
赤(あか)ちゃんにミルクを飲(の)ませる。
To feed milk to the baby.
ミルクティー
milk tea

Notes

ミルク typically refers to processed or portioned milk, while 牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう) is fresh cow's milk.

COMMON USES:

  • コーヒーにミルク (milk in coffee - often creamer)
  • ミルクティー (milk tea)
  • ホットミルク (hot milk)
  • 粉(こな)ミルク (powdered milk/baby formula)
  • コンデンスミルク (condensed milk)

DIFFERENCE FROM 牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう):

  • ミルク: Often refers to coffee creamer, processed milk products, or baby formula
  • 牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう): Fresh cow's milk (what you buy in cartons)

Note: In Japan, small portions of ミルク for coffee are called コーヒーフレッシュ or simply フレッシュ.

Related Words

Related: 牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう) (cow's milk)
