直(じか)に

じかに
adverb
directly; in person; firsthand
1. directly; without intermediary
Without anything or anyone in between.
社長(しゃちょう)に直(じか)に会(あ)って話(はな)したい。
I want to meet the president directly and talk.
2. in person; firsthand
Through direct personal experience or contact.
社長(しゃちょう)に直(じか)に会(あ)って話(はな)したい。
I want to meet the president directly and talk.
現場(げんば)を直(じか)に見(み)てきた。
I saw the site firsthand.
3. directly on (surface)
Without any covering or barrier.
床(ゆか)に直(じか)に座(すわ)る。
To sit directly on the floor.

直(じか)に is an adverb meaning directly or firsthand, emphasizing unmediated contact or experience.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 直(じか)に会(あ)う (to meet directly/in person)
  • 直(じか)に見(み)る (to see firsthand)
  • 直(じか)に聞(き)く (to hear directly)
  • 肌(はだ)に直(じか)に触(ふ)れる (to touch directly against the skin)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 直(じか)に vs. 直接(ちょくせつ): Very similar in meaning. 直接(ちょくせつ) is slightly more formal and common; 直(じか)に has a more personal, physical nuance.
  • 直(じか)に vs. じきに: Different words! じきに means "soon" or "shortly," not "directly."

Register: Neutral. Common in both spoken and written Japanese.
