過去(かこ)

かこ
noun
past, former times

Definitions

1. the past, former times
Time that has already passed, as opposed to the present or future.

Examples

過去(かこ)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る。
To look back on the past.
過去(かこ)のことは忘(わす)れよう。
Let's forget about the past.
過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)の記録(きろく)です。
It's the best record ever.
Literally: past highest record

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 過去(かこ)に (in the past)
  • 過去(かこ)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る (to look back on the past)
  • 過去(かこ)形(けい) (past tense - grammar term)

Contrast: 現在(げんざい) (present), 未来(みらい) (future)
