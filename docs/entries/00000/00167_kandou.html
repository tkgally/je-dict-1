感動(かんどう)

かんどう
noun, suru verb
being moved, emotion
1. being deeply moved, emotion, excitement
A strong emotional response, typically positive, to something touching or impressive.
その映画(えいが)に感動(かんどう)した。
I was moved by that movie.
感動(かんどう)的(てき)なスピーチだった。
It was a moving speech.
感動(かんどう)を与(あた)える。
To move someone (emotionally).

感動(かんどう) describes being deeply moved emotionally, typically by something touching, impressive, or beautiful.

USAGE:
As a noun or suru verb. The emotion is generally positive - being moved to tears, inspired, or touched.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜に感動(かんどう)する: to be moved by ~
  • 感動(かんどう)を与(あた)える: to move someone (emotionally)
  • 感動(かんどう)を覚(おぼ)える: to feel moved
  • 感動(かんどう)的(てき)な: moving, touching (adjective)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 感動(かんどう)の涙(なみだ): tears of being moved
  • 感動(かんどう)の嵐(あらし): storm of emotion
  • 深(ふか)い感動(かんどう): deep emotion

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 心(こころ)を打(う)たれる: to be touched/struck emotionally
  • 胸(むね)が熱(あつ)くなる: to feel one's heart warm
