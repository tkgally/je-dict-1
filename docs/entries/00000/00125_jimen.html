地面(じめん)

じめん
noun
ground, earth's surface

Definitions

1. ground, earth's surface
The surface of the earth that one walks on.

Examples

地面(じめん)に座(すわ)った。
I sat on the ground.
雨(あめ)で地面(じめん)が濡(ぬ)れている。
The ground is wet from the rain.
地震(じしん)で地面(じめん)が揺(ゆ)れた。
The ground shook from the earthquake.

Notes

地面(じめん) refers to the surface of the earth or ground.

Common collocations:

  • 地面(じめん)に落(お)ちる (to fall to the ground)
  • 地面(じめん)に座(すわ)る (to sit on the ground)
  • 地面(じめん)が揺(ゆ)れる (the ground shakes)
  • 地面(じめん)を掘(ほ)る (to dig the ground)
  • 濡(ぬ)れた地面(じめん) (wet ground)

Related words:

  • 地(じ)/土(つち) (earth, soil)
  • 床(ゆか) (floor - indoors)
  • 大地(だいち) (the earth, the ground - more poetic)
  • 地表(ちひょう) (earth's surface - scientific)
