平気(へいき)

へいき
na-adjective, noun
calm, fine, unconcerned, okay
1. calm, composed, unconcerned
The state of being unaffected or unbothered by something that might normally cause distress.
彼(かれ)は失敗(しっぱい)しても平気(へいき)な顔(かお)をしている。
Even when he fails, he keeps a calm face.
彼女(かのじょ)は平気(へいき)で嘘(うそ)をつく。
She lies without a second thought.
Sense 1: unconcerned (negative nuance)
怒(おこ)られても平気(へいき)な顔(かお)だ。
Even when scolded, he keeps a calm face.
Sense 1: composed
どんな批判(ひはん)も平気(へいき)だ。
I'm unfazed by any criticism.
Sense 1: calm
2. fine, okay, all right
Not a problem; able to handle or endure something.
「大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)？」「うん、平気(へいき)だよ。」
"Are you okay?" "Yeah, I'm fine."
辛(から)い料理(りょうり)は平気(へいき)ですか。
Are you okay with spicy food?
寒(さむ)くても平気(へいき)です。
I'm fine even if it's cold.
Sense 2: okay
この程度(ていど)なら平気(へいき)だ。
If it's just this much, I'm fine.
Sense 2: all right
一人(ひとり)で平気(へいき)？
Are you okay by yourself?
Sense 2: fine
待(ま)たせても平気(へいき)？
Is it okay if I make you wait?
Sense 2: okay

平気(へいき) is a versatile word used to express that something is not a problem or that one is unaffected by something.

Common patterns:

  • 平気(へいき)だ (I'm fine / It's okay)
  • 平気(へいき)な顔(かお)をする (to keep a calm face)
  • 〜ても平気(へいき) (even if ~, it's fine)
  • 〜は平気(へいき)？ (Are you okay with ~?)

Nuances:

  • Can imply toughness or resilience
  • Sometimes used negatively to suggest someone is insensitive or unfazed when they should care
  • 平気(へいき)で + verb = to do something without concern/remorse

Similar expressions:

  • 大丈夫(だいじょうぶ) (okay, all right - more common)
  • 問題(もんだい)ない (no problem)
