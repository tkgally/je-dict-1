菓子(かし)

かし
noun
confectionery, sweets
1. confectionery, sweets, snacks
General term for sweets, candies, and confections.
お菓子(かし)を食(た)べすぎた。
I ate too many sweets.
和菓子(わがし)が好(す)きです。
I like Japanese sweets.
お菓子(かし)を買(か)ってきて。
Buy some snacks.
お土産(みやげ)に洋菓子(ようがし)を買(か)った。
I bought Western confectionery as a souvenir.
この店(みせ)は手作(てづく)りのお菓子(かし)で有名(ゆうめい)だ。
This shop is famous for its handmade sweets.

菓子(かし) means sweets or confectionery. Usually used with the polite prefix: お菓子(かし).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 和菓子(わがし): Japanese confectionery
  • 洋菓子(ようがし): Western confectionery
  • お菓子(かし)屋(や)さん: confectionery shop
  • 駄菓子(だがし): cheap sweets (kids' snacks)
  • 焼(や)き菓子(がし): baked sweets
  • 干(ほ)し菓子(がし): dried sweets

CULTURAL NOTE:
和菓子(わがし) are traditional Japanese sweets, often served with tea. They reflect the season in their design and ingredients.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • スイーツ: sweets (trendy loanword)
  • デザート: dessert
  • おやつ: snack (typically afternoon)
