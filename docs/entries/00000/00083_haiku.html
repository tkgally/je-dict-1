俳句(はいく)

はいく
noun
haiku (17-syllable poem)
1. haiku, 17-syllable Japanese poem
A traditional form of Japanese poetry consisting of 17 morae (sound units) in a 5-7-5 pattern. Haiku typically focus on nature and seasonal imagery.
松尾芭蕉(まつおばしょう)は日本(にほん)で最(もっと)も有名(ゆうめい)な俳句(はいく)詩人(しじん)だ。
Matsuo Basho is the most famous haiku poet in Japan.
俳句(はいく)には季語(きご)を入(い)れるのが基本(きほん)です。
The basic rule is to include a seasonal word in haiku.
季語(きご) = seasonal word (kigo)
学校(がっこう)の授業(じゅぎょう)で俳句(はいく)を作(つく)った。
I wrote haiku in school class.

俳句(はいく) is a traditional Japanese poetry form with strict structural requirements:

  • 17 morae in 5-7-5 pattern
  • Contains a 季語(きご) (seasonal reference word)
  • Often includes a 切(き)れ字(じ) (cutting word) for pause or emphasis

Famous haiku poets (俳人(はいじん)):

  • 松尾芭蕉(まつおばしょう) (Matsuo Basho)
  • 与謝蕪村(よさぶそん) (Yosa Buson)
  • 小林一茶(こばやしいっさ) (Kobayashi Issa)

Related terms:

  • 俳人(はいじん) (haiku poet)
  • 季語(きご) (seasonal word)
  • 川柳(せんりゅう) (senryu - similar form but focuses on human nature rather than seasons)
