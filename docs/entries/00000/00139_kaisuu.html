回数(かいすう)

かいすう
noun
number of times, frequency
1. number of times, frequency
The count of how many times something occurs or is done.
利用(りよう)回数(かいすう)に制限(せいげん)がある。
There is a limit on the number of uses.
練習(れんしゅう)回数(かいすう)を増(ふ)やしたら、上手(じょうず)になった。
I got better after increasing the number of practice sessions.
回数(かいすう)券(けん)を買(か)うと割引(わりびき)になる。
Buying a book of tickets gives you a discount.
回数(かいすう)券(けん) = multi-ride ticket, book of tickets

回数(かいすう) counts the number of times something happens or is performed.

Common expressions:

  • 回数(かいすう)券(けん) (book of tickets, multi-ride pass)
  • 利用(りよう)回数(かいすう) (number of uses)
  • 練習(れんしゅう)回数(かいすう) (number of practice sessions)
  • 回数(かいすう)を重(かさ)ねる (to accumulate instances)

Related terms:

  • 頻度(ひんど) (frequency - focuses on rate/how often)
  • 度数(どすう) (frequency, number of degrees)
