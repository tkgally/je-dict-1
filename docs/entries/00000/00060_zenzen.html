全然(ぜんぜん)

ぜんぜん
adverb
not at all

Definitions

1. not at all
Used with negative verbs to mean 'not at all'.
2. completely (colloquial)
In casual speech, used with positive adjectives to mean 'totally'.

Examples

全然(ぜんぜん)わかりません。
I don't understand at all.
Sense 1
全然(ぜんぜん)大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)です。
It's totally fine.
Sense 2: casual
日本語(にほんご)は全然(ぜんぜん)難(むずか)しくないです。
Japanese isn't difficult at all.
Sense 1

Notes

Traditionally only used with negatives, but casual modern usage allows positive: 全然(ぜんぜん)いい (totally fine).
