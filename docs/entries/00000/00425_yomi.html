読(よ)み

よみ
noun
reading, pronunciation
1. reading, pronunciation
The way a character or word is read or pronounced.
この漢字(かんじ)の読(よ)みがわからない。
I don't know how to read this kanji.
音読(おんよ)みと訓読(くんよ)みを覚(おぼ)えよう。
Let's learn the on'yomi and kun'yomi readings.
2. reading ability, interpretation
The ability to read or understand; one's interpretation of a situation.
彼(かれ)は状況(じょうきょう)の読(よ)みが深(ふか)い。
He has a deep understanding of the situation.

読(よ)み is the noun form of 読(よ)む (to read). It can refer to pronunciation or to one's interpretation/analysis of a situation.

KANJI READING TYPES:

  • 音読(おんよ)み: Chinese-derived reading (on'yomi)
  • 訓読(くんよ)み: Japanese-native reading (kun'yomi)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 読(よ)みが浅(あさ)い: shallow reading/analysis
  • 読(よ)みが深(ふか)い: deep reading/insight
  • 読(よ)みが甘(あま)い: naive interpretation
  • 読(よ)みを誤(あやま)る: to misread (a situation)

In games like shogi or go, 読(よ)み refers to reading ahead—predicting future moves.
