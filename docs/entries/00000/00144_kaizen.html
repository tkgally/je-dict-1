1. improvement, betterment
Making something better, often through continuous small changes.
生活(せいかつ)を改善(かいぜん)したい。
I want to improve my lifestyle.
品質(ひんしつ)の改善(かいぜん)に取(と)り組(く)む。
To work on quality improvement.
状況(じょうきょう)は改善(かいぜん)している。
The situation is improving.

改善(かいぜん) means improvement, especially through deliberate effort. It is also famous worldwide as a Japanese business philosophy ("kaizen") emphasizing continuous, incremental improvement.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を改善(かいぜん)する: to improve ~
  • 改善(かいぜん)の余地(よち): room for improvement
  • 改善(かいぜん)点(てん): points to improve
  • 改善(かいぜん)策(さく): improvement measures
  • 改善(かいぜん)が見(み)られる: improvement is observed

USAGE:
Applies broadly: 健康(けんこう)の改善(かいぜん) (health improvement), 環境(かんきょう)改善(かいぜん) (environmental improvement), 業務(ぎょうむ)改善(かいぜん) (work process improvement).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 改善(かいぜん): general improvement (of conditions, situations)
  • 改良(かいりょう): improvement of products, systems, or methods
  • 向上(こうじょう): enhancement, raising to a higher level (能力(のうりょく)の向上(こうじょう) = skill improvement)
