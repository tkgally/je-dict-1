縮(ちぢ)む

ちぢむ
godan verb
to shrink; to contract; to be reduced

Definitions

1. to shrink; to contract
To become smaller in size, typically of fabric, materials, or physical dimensions.
2. to be reduced; to decrease
To become smaller in scale, scope, or amount.
3. to cower; to cringe
To shrink back in fear or discomfort.

Examples

このセーターは洗(あら)うと縮(ちぢ)む。
This sweater shrinks when washed.
寒(さむ)さで体(からだ)が縮(ちぢ)んだ。
My body contracted from the cold.
会社(かいしゃ)の規模(きぼ)が縮(ちぢ)んでいる。
The company's scale is shrinking.

Notes

縮(ちぢ)む is intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 縮(ちぢ)める (to shrink something).

ASPECT ({ている}):
縮(ちぢ)んでいる indicates a resulting state of having shrunk, not an ongoing process.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • セーターが縮(ちぢ)む (sweater shrinks)
  • 距離(きょり)が縮(ちぢ)む (distance shortens)
  • 身(み)が縮(ちぢ)む (to cringe, feel small)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～が縮(ちぢ)む: The subject that shrinks

The related verb 縮(ちぢ)まる also means "to shrink" but emphasizes a more complete or natural contraction. 縮(ちぢ)む is more general.
