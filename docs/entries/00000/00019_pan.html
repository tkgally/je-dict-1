パン

ぱん
noun
bread

Definitions

1. bread
A baked food made from flour and water. Borrowed from Portuguese 'pão'.

Examples

朝(あさ)ごはんにパンを食(た)べます。
I eat bread for breakfast.
パンを買(か)いに行(い)きます。
I'm going to buy bread.
パン屋(や)はどこですか？
Where is the bakery?
パン屋 = bakery

Notes

A loanword from Portuguese, not English. Common types: 食(しょく)パン (sliced bread), メロンパン (melon bread), あんパン (bread with red bean paste).
