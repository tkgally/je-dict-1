難(なん)

なん
noun
difficulty, trouble, defect
1. difficulty, trouble, hardship
A difficult or troublesome situation; an obstacle or challenge.
難(なん)を逃(のが)れることができた。
I was able to escape the danger.
難(なん)を乗(の)り越(こ)えて成功(せいこう)した。
I overcame difficulties and succeeded.
2. defect, flaw
A fault or weakness in something.
この計画(けいかく)には一(ひと)つ難(なん)がある。
There is one flaw in this plan.

難(なん) can mean both 'difficulty/danger' and 'defect/flaw' depending on context.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 難(なん)を逃(のが)れる: to escape danger/difficulty
  • 難(なん)を乗(の)り越(こ)える: to overcome difficulties
  • 難(なん)がある: to have a flaw/problem
  • 難(なん)のない: flawless, without problems

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 困難(こんなん): difficulty, hardship
  • 災難(さいなん): disaster, misfortune
  • 避難(ひなん): evacuation, refuge
  • 難民(なんみん): refugee
