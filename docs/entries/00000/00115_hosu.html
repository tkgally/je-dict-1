干(ほ)す

ほす
godan verb
to dry, to air
1. to dry, to air out
To expose something to air or sun to remove moisture.
洗濯物(せんたくもの)を干(ほ)す。
I hang the laundry out to dry.
布団(ふとん)を干(ほ)して日光(にっこう)消毒(しょうどく)する。
I air out the futon to disinfect it with sunlight.
2. to drain (a cup)
To drink up completely.
グラスを干(ほ)す。
I drain my glass.

干(ほ)す means to dry something by exposing it to air or sun.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pair: 乾(かわ)く (intransitive, to dry)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 洗濯物(せんたくもの)を干(ほ)す: to hang laundry to dry
  • 布団(ふとん)を干(ほ)す: to air out a futon
  • 干(ほ)し物(もの): things hung out to dry
  • 干(ほ)し竿(ざお): drying pole

RELATED WORDS:

  • 干物(ひもの): dried fish
  • 干(ほ)しぶどう: raisins (dried grapes)
  • 梅干(うめぼ)し: pickled plum

The kanji 干(ほ)す relates to drying/draining.
