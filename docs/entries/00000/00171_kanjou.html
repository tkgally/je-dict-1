感情(かんじょう)

かんじょう
noun
emotion, feeling
1. emotion, feeling, sentiment
Mental states involving feelings such as joy, anger, sadness, or fear.
感情(かんじょう)を表(あらわ)に出(だ)す。
To show one's emotions openly.
感情(かんじょう)を抑(おさ)える。
To suppress one's emotions.
感情(かんじょう)的(てき)にならないで。
Don't get emotional.

感情(かんじょう) refers to emotions and emotional states such as joy, anger, sadness, and fear.

WORD FORMATION:
感(かん) (sense, feeling) + 情(じょう) (emotion, feeling)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 感情(かんじょう)を抑(おさ)える: to control one's emotions
  • 感情(かんじょう)を表(あらわ)す: to express emotions
  • 感情(かんじょう)に流(なが)される: to be carried away by emotions
  • 感情(かんじょう)を込(こ)めて: with feeling, heartfelt
  • 感情的(かんじょうてき)になる: to become emotional

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 気持(きも)ち: feeling (more casual, everyday)
  • 心(こころ): heart, mind (broader concept)
  • 情(じょう): emotion, affection (literary)
  • フィーリング: feeling (loanword, casual)
