筆記(ひっき)

ひっき
noun
note-taking, writing
1. note-taking, writing, transcription
The act of writing things down, especially taking notes during lectures or meetings.
2. written (as opposed to oral)
Used to describe tests or examinations that are written rather than spoken.
講義(こうぎ)の内容(ないよう)を筆記(ひっき)した。
I took notes on the lecture content.
筆記(ひっき)試験(しけん)と面接(めんせつ)の両方(りょうほう)がある。
There is both a written exam and an interview.
筆記(ひっき)用具(ようぐ)を持参(じさん)してください。
Please bring writing utensils.

筆記(ひっき) relates to writing and note-taking, often used in academic or formal contexts.

Common compounds:

  • 筆記(ひっき)試験(しけん) (written examination)
  • 筆記(ひっき)用具(ようぐ) (writing utensils)
  • 筆記(ひっき)体(たい) (cursive writing style)

Contrast:

  • 筆記(ひっき)試験(しけん) (written test) vs 口述(こうじゅつ)試験(しけん) (oral test)

Related terms:

  • メモ (memo, note - more casual)
  • ノート (notebook, notes)
