日課(にっか)

にっか
noun
daily routine, daily task
1. daily routine, daily task
An activity or task that is done regularly every day as part of one's routine.
朝(あさ)のジョギングが私(わたし)の日課(にっか)です。
Morning jogging is my daily routine.
毎日(まいにち)新聞(しんぶん)を読(よ)むのを日課(にっか)にしている。
I make it my daily routine to read the newspaper every day.
〜を日課(にっか)にする = to make something one's daily routine
犬(いぬ)の散歩(さんぽ)は父(ちち)の日課(にっか)だ。
Walking the dog is my father's daily task.
祖母(そぼ)は毎朝(まいあさ)ラジオ体操(たいそう)をするのを日課(にっか)にしていて、何年(なんねん)も続(つづ)けている。
My grandmother makes it her daily routine to do radio exercises every morning and has been doing it for years.
忙(いそが)しくても、日課(にっか)だけは欠(か)かさないようにしている。
Even when I'm busy, I make sure not to skip my daily routine.

日課(にっか) refers to activities performed regularly on a daily basis. It often implies a self-imposed routine rather than an obligation.

Common collocations:

  • 日課(にっか)にする (to make something a daily routine)
  • 日課(にっか)になる (to become a daily routine)
  • 日課(にっか)を続(つづ)ける (to continue one's daily routine)

Similar words:

  • 習慣(しゅうかん) (habit) - broader term, not limited to daily activities
  • 日々(ひび)の仕事(しごと) (daily work) - focuses more on work tasks
