広(ひろ)さ

ひろさ
noun
extent; spaciousness; width; area

Definitions

1. extent; spaciousness; size (of area)
The degree to which something is wide or spacious.
2. width; breadth
The measurement of how wide something is.

Examples

この部屋(へや)の広(ひろ)さはどのくらいですか。
How big is this room?
公園(こうえん)の広(ひろ)さに驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by how spacious the park was.
彼(かれ)の知識(ちしき)の広(ひろ)さには感心(かんしん)する。
I am impressed by the breadth of his knowledge.

Notes

広(ひろ)さ is the noun form of the adjective 広(ひろ)い (wide, spacious), created with the suffix ～さ.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～の広(ひろ)さ (the size/extent of ~)
  • 広(ひろ)さは～平方メートル (~ square meters in area)
  • 広(ひろ)さに驚(おどろ)く (to be surprised by the spaciousness)

MEASUREMENT USAGE:

When asking about room/area size in Japan:

  • この部屋(へや)の広(ひろ)さは？ (How big is this room?)
  • 広(ひろ)さは20畳(じょう)です (It's 20 tatami mats in size)

FIGURATIVE USAGE:

広(ひろ)さ can describe abstract breadth:

  • 心(こころ)の広(ひろ)さ (broadmindedness)
  • 知識(ちしき)の広(ひろ)さ (breadth of knowledge)

RELATED MEASUREMENTS:

  • 面積(めんせき): Area (more technical/precise)
  • 大(おお)きさ: Size (more general)
