肌着(はだぎ)

はだぎ
noun
underwear, undershirt
1. underwear, undershirt, undergarments
Clothing worn directly against the skin, under other clothes. Typically refers to undershirts, tank tops, or similar garments.
夏(なつ)は綿(めん)の肌着(はだぎ)が涼(すず)しい。
Cotton underwear is cool in summer.
赤(あか)ちゃん用(よう)の肌着(はだぎ)を買(か)った。
I bought baby undershirts.
汗(あせ)をかいたから肌着(はだぎ)を替(か)えた。
I changed my undershirt because I was sweating.
冬(ふゆ)は暖(あたた)かい肌着(はだぎ)を着(き)る。
I wear warm undergarments in winter.
肌着(はだぎ)は毎日(まいにち)洗濯(せんたく)する。
I wash my underwear every day.

肌着(はだぎ) literally means 'skin clothes' - garments worn directly against the 肌(はだ) (skin). It typically refers to upper-body undergarments like undershirts.

Related terms:

  • 下着(したぎ) - underwear (general term, includes all undergarments)
  • 肌(はだ) - skin
  • インナー - inner wear (loanword)

肌着(はだぎ) is especially common when referring to:

  • Baby undershirts
  • Cotton undershirts worn for comfort or to absorb sweat
  • Traditional Japanese undergarments
