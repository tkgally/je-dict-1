申す

もうす
verb
to say; to be called (humble)

Definitions

1. to say (humble)
Humble form of 言う, used when referring to one's own speech.
2. to be called (humble)
Used when introducing oneself humbly.

Examples

田中(たなか)と申(もう)します。
My name is Tanaka.
Humble self-introduction
何(なん)と申(もう)しましょうか。
What should I say?
申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I'm terribly sorry.
Very formal apology

Notes

申す is the humble form of 言う, used to lower oneself when speaking.

CONJUGATION:

  • 申します (polite)
  • 申して (te-form)
  • 申した (past)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜と申します: my name is...
  • 申し訳ない: I'm sorry (lit. there's no excuse)

KEIGO TYPE:

  • 謙譲語 (kenjougo): Humble language
  • Honorific equivalent: おっしゃる

Related Words

Keigo: 言(い)う (plain form)
Keigo: おっしゃる (honorific equivalent)
