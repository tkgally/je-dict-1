本部(ほんぶ)

ほんぶ
noun
headquarters, head office
1. headquarters, head office, main office
The main administrative center of an organization, company, or operation.
会社(かいしゃ)の本部(ほんぶ)は東京(とうきょう)にある。
The company's headquarters is in Tokyo.
災害(さいがい)対策(たいさく)本部(ほんぶ)が設置(せっち)された。
A disaster response headquarters was established.
本部(ほんぶ)から指示(しじ)が来(き)た。
Instructions came from headquarters.
選挙(せんきょ)本部(ほんぶ)で作戦(さくせん)を練(ね)った。
We developed a strategy at the election headquarters.
本部(ほんぶ)に報告(ほうこく)する。
I'll report to headquarters.

本部(ほんぶ) refers to the central administrative location of an organization.

Common compounds:

  • 本社(ほんしゃ) (head office - specifically for companies)
  • 本店(ほんてん) (main store/branch)
  • 対策(たいさく)本部(ほんぶ) (response headquarters)
  • 選挙(せんきょ)対策(たいさく)本部(ほんぶ) (election campaign headquarters)

Contrast:

  • 本部(ほんぶ) - headquarters (general, can be temporary)
  • 本社(ほんしゃ) - head office (specifically for corporations)
  • 支部(しぶ)/支店(してん) - branch (opposite of headquarters)
