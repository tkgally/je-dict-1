湯(ゆ)

ゆ
noun
hot water, bath

Definitions

1. hot water
Water that has been heated.
2. bath, hot spring
A bath or bathing facility, especially hot springs.

Examples

湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かしてください。
Please boil some water.
湯(ゆ)に入(はい)る。
To take a bath.
湯(ゆ)が熱(あつ)すぎます。
The hot water is too hot.

Notes

湯(ゆ) means hot water or bath.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • お湯(ゆ): hot water (polite)
  • 湯(ゆ)気(げ): steam
  • 湯(ゆ)のみ: teacup
  • 温泉(おんせん): hot spring
  • 銭湯(せんとう): public bath

COMMON VERBS:

  • 湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす: to boil water
  • 湯(ゆ)に入(はい)る: to take a bath

Note: 水(みず) is cold/room-temperature water; 湯(ゆ) is specifically heated water.
