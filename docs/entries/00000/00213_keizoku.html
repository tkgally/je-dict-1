継続(けいぞく)

けいぞく
noun, suru-verb
continuation, continuance
1. continuation, continuance
The act of continuing something without interruption.
契約(けいやく)を継続(けいぞく)しますか。
Will you continue the contract?
継続(けいぞく)は力(ちから)なり。
Persistence is power. / Continuity is strength.
Famous proverb
治療(ちりょう)を継続(けいぞく)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
Treatment needs to continue.

継続(けいぞく) means continuing or sustaining something over time.

Famous proverb:

  • 継続(けいぞく)は力(ちから)なり (Persistence is power / Continuity is strength)

Common collocations:

  • 継続(けいぞく)する (to continue)
  • 継続(けいぞく)的(てき)（な/に）(continuous(ly))
  • 契約(けいやく)を継続(けいぞく)する (to continue a contract)
  • 継続(けいぞく)課金(かきん) (recurring billing, subscription)
  • 継続(けいぞく)利用(りよう) (continued use)

Related terms:

  • 続(つづ)ける (to continue - verb)
  • 持続(じぞく) (maintenance, continuation)
  • 連続(れんぞく) (continuation, series)
