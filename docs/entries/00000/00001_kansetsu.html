間接(かんせつ)

かんせつ
noun, no-adjective
indirect

Definitions

1. indirect, not direct
Not in a direct line or manner; through an intermediary or secondary means.

Examples

間接(かんせつ)的(てき)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)えた。
It had an indirect influence.
間接(かんせつ)照明(しょうめい)で部屋(へや)の雰囲気(ふんいき)が変(か)わった。
The room's atmosphere changed with indirect lighting.
間接(かんせつ)喫煙(きつえん)も健康(けんこう)に悪(わる)い。
Secondhand smoking is also bad for your health.
間接(かんせつ)喫煙(きつえん) = passive/secondhand smoking

Notes

間接(かんせつ) means indirect, as opposed to 直接(ちょくせつ) (direct).

Common collocations:

  • 間接(かんせつ)的(てき)（な/に）(indirect(ly))
  • 間接(かんせつ)照明(しょうめい) (indirect lighting)
  • 間接(かんせつ)喫煙(きつえん) (passive smoking)
  • 間接(かんせつ)税(ぜい) (indirect tax, e.g., consumption tax)
  • 間接(かんせつ)話法(わほう) (indirect speech - grammar)

Opposite:

  • 直接(ちょくせつ) (direct)

Note: Different from 関節(かんせつ) (joint - body part), which is a homophone.
