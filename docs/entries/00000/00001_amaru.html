余(あま)る

あまる
godan verb
to be left over, to remain, to be in excess

Definitions

1. to be left over, to remain
To have a surplus after use or distribution.
2. to exceed, to be too much
To be more than what is needed or expected.

Examples

お金(かね)が余(あま)った。
There was money left over.
食(た)べ物(もの)が余(あま)っている。
There is leftover food.
力(ちから)に余(あま)る仕事(しごと)だ。
It's a job beyond my abilities.

Notes

余(あま)る is an intransitive verb meaning to be left over or remain.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 余(あま)す (transitive, to leave over)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 余(あま)っている means 'there is left over' (resulting state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お金(かね)が余(あま)る: money is left over
  • 時間(じかん)が余(あま)る: time remains
  • 力(ちから)に余(あま)る: beyond one's ability
  • 余(あま)り物(もの): leftovers

NOTE: The word 余(あま)り (too much, not very) is related to this verb.
