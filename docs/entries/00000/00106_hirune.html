昼寝(ひるね)

ひるね
noun
nap, afternoon nap, siesta
1. nap, afternoon nap, siesta
A short sleep during the daytime, typically in the afternoon.
昼食(ちゅうしょく)の後(あと)、少(すこ)し昼寝(ひるね)をした。
I took a short nap after lunch.
赤(あか)ちゃんは毎日(まいにち)昼寝(ひるね)をする。
Babies take a nap every day.
休日(きゅうじつ)は昼寝(ひるね)をしてのんびり過(す)ごす。
On holidays, I spend my time relaxing and taking naps.
昼寝(ひるね)をしすぎて夜(よる)眠(ねむ)れなくなった。
I napped too much and couldn't sleep at night.
15分(ふん)の昼寝(ひるね)で頭(あたま)がすっきりした。
A 15-minute nap cleared my head.

昼寝(ひるね) specifically refers to sleeping during the day, particularly after lunch.

Common expressions:

  • 昼寝(ひるね)をする (to take a nap)
  • 昼寝(ひるね)から起(お)きる (to wake up from a nap)

Related terms:

  • うたた寝(ね) (dozing off unintentionally)
  • 仮眠(かみん) (short sleep, power nap - more formal)
  • シエスタ (siesta - loanword)

Cultural note:
Short naps after lunch are becoming more accepted in Japanese workplaces, with some companies providing nap rooms to improve productivity.
