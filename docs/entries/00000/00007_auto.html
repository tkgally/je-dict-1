アウト

あうと
noun; na-adjective
out

Definitions

1. out (in sports)
Being put out in baseball, or the ball going out of bounds in other sports.
2. unacceptable; no good
Informal usage meaning something is not allowed, unacceptable, or failed.

Examples

ランナーはアウトになった。
The runner was out.
ボールがアウトになった。
The ball went out (of bounds).
遅刻(ちこく)したらアウトだよ。
If you're late, you're out (it's over for you).
Informal usage

Notes

アウト is a loanword from English "out" used primarily in sports contexts, but also colloquially to mean "unacceptable" or "failed."

SPORTS USAGE:

  • In baseball: 三振(さんしん)アウト (strikeout)
  • In tennis/badminton: ボールがアウト (ball is out)

INFORMAL USAGE:

  • それはアウトだ (That's not okay/acceptable)
  • アウトかセーフか (Whether it's out or safe - used metaphorically for pass/fail situations)

The opposite is セーフ (safe).

Related Words

Antonym: セーフ (safe)
