暖房(だんぼう)

だんぼう
noun
heating

Definitions

1. heating, heater
A system or device for warming a room or building. Also refers to the act of heating.

Examples

暖房(だんぼう)をつけてください。
Please turn on the heating.
暖房(だんぼう)が効(き)いていて暖(あたた)かい。
The heating is working and it's warm.
暖房(だんぼう)代(だい)が高(たか)い。
The heating costs are high.

Notes

暖房(だんぼう) refers to heating systems. The opposite is 冷房(れいぼう) (air conditioning/cooling).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 暖房(だんぼう)をつける: to turn on the heating
  • 暖房(だんぼう)を消(け)す: to turn off the heating
  • 暖房(だんぼう)が効(き)く: heating works/is effective
  • 暖房(だんぼう)器具(きぐ): heating equipment

TYPES OF HEATING:

  • エアコン: air conditioner (heating/cooling)
  • ストーブ: heater, stove
  • こたつ: heated table
  • 床(ゆか)暖房(だんぼう): floor heating

OPPOSITE: 冷房(れいぼう) (cooling, air conditioning)

Related Words

Antonym: 冷房(れいぼう) (cooling, air conditioning)
