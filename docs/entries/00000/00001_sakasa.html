逆(さか)さ

さかさ
noun / no-adjective
upside down; reverse; inverted

Definitions

1. upside down; inverted
With the top and bottom reversed.
2. reverse; opposite; backwards
In the opposite direction or order.

Examples

写真(しゃしん)が逆(さか)さになっている。
The photo is upside down.
逆(さか)さまに持(も)っていますよ。
You're holding it upside down.
物事(ものごと)を逆(さか)さに考(かんが)えてみる。
Try thinking about things in reverse.

Notes

逆(さか)さ means upside down or reversed. Often used with the variant form 逆(さか)さま.

FORMS:

  • 逆(さか)さ (noun/no-adjective)
  • 逆(さか)さま (noun/na-adjective - more common)
  • 逆(さか)さに (adverbially)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 逆(さか)さになる (to become upside down)
  • 逆(さか)さにする (to turn upside down)
  • 逆(さか)さまに持(も)つ (to hold upside down)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 逆(さか)さ吊(づ)り (hanging upside down)
  • 逆(さか)さ言葉(ことば) (words said in reverse order)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 逆(さか)さ vs. 逆(ぎゃく): 逆(ぎゃく) means "opposite" or "reverse" in general; 逆(さか)さ specifically means physical inversion (upside down).

Register: Neutral. Common in everyday conversation.

Related Words

Related: 逆(ぎゃく) (reverse, opposite)
