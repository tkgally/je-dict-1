仕事(しごと)

しごと
noun
work, job
1. work, job, occupation
Employment or tasks one does professionally. Also refers to specific tasks or duties.
仕事(しごと)は何(なん)ですか？
What is your job?
Asking about occupation
仕事(しごと)が終(お)わったら、飲(の)みに行(い)きましょう。
Let's go for drinks after work.
2. task, assignment
A specific piece of work that needs to be done.
今日(きょう)は仕事(しごと)が多(おお)いです。
I have a lot of work today.

One of the most common nouns in Japanese. Can be used as a verb with する: 仕事(しごと)をする (to work).

Related:

  • 仕事中(しごとちゅう) (at work)
  • 仕事場(しごとば) (workplace).
