数(かず)

かず
noun
number, amount

Definitions

1. number, quantity, amount
A count or total of things.

Examples

数(かず)が足(た)りない。
The number is insufficient.
参加者(さんかしゃ)の数(かず)を数(かぞ)える。
To count the number of participants.
数(かず)が多(おお)い。
There are many. / The number is large.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 数(かず)が多(おお)い/少(すく)ない (many/few in number)
  • 数(かず)を数(かぞ)える (to count the number)
  • 〜の数(かず) (number of ~)

Note: 数(すう) is the on'yomi reading used in compounds like 数学(すうがく) (mathematics).
