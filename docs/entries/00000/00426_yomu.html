読(よ)む

よむ
verb (godan)
to read
1. to read
To look at and comprehend written text. Used for books, newspapers, signs, emails, and any written material.
毎日(まいにち)新聞(しんぶん)を読(よ)みます。
I read the newspaper every day.
この漢字(かんじ)は読(よ)めません。
I can't read this kanji.
Potential form 読(よ)める
2. to read aloud, to recite
To speak written words out loud, as in reading poetry or reading to someone.
3. to guess, to predict
To anticipate or predict something, as in 'reading' a situation or someone's intentions. 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む means to read the atmosphere/room.
空気(くうき)を読(よ)んでください。
Please read the room.
Idiomatic expression

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを読(よ)む (read X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 読(よ)んでいる = 'is reading.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 本(ほん)を読(よ)む (read a book)
  • 新聞(しんぶん)を読(よ)む (read newspaper)
  • 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む (read the room - idiom)
  • 声(こえ)に出(だ)して読(よ)む (read aloud)

Godan verb: te-form 読(よ)んで. Cultural: 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む refers to understanding social cues. KY = 空気(くうき)読(よ)めない (can't read the room).
