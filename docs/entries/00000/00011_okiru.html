起(お)きる

おきる
verb (ichidan)
to wake up, to get up

Definitions

1. to wake up, to get up
Used when someone wakes from sleep and gets out of bed. Can also mean to happen or occur (for events).
2. to occur, to happen
Used for events, incidents, or phenomena that take place. Common in news and formal contexts.

Examples

毎朝(まいあさ)6時(じ)に起(お)きます。
I wake up at 6 o'clock every morning.
まだ起(お)きていますか？
Are you still awake?
Te-iru form indicating state
事故(じこ)が起(お)きました。
An accident occurred.
Sense 2: something happening

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 起(お)こす (transitive, to wake someone up). Pattern: Xが起(お)きる.

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 起(お)きている = 'is awake' (not 'is waking up'). This is important: まだ起(お)きている means 'still awake.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 朝(あさ)早(はや)く起(お)きる (wake up early)
  • 〜時(じ)に起(お)きる (wake up at ~ o'clock)
  • 事故(じこ)が起(お)きる (an accident occurs - sense 2)
  • 起(お)きていますか (are you awake?)

Opposite: 寝(ね)る (to sleep/go to bed).

Related Words
