浮(う)く

うく
godan verb
to float, to rise to the surface
1. to float
To remain on the surface of a liquid without sinking.
木(き)の葉(は)が水(みず)に浮(う)いている。
Leaves are floating on the water.
2. to be saved (money, time)
To have something left over or be economized.
この方法(ほうほう)なら時間(じかん)が浮(う)く。
This method will save time.
3. to feel out of place
To stand out awkwardly, not fit in with surroundings.
彼(かれ)はパーティーで浮(う)いていた。
He felt out of place at the party.

浮(う)く is an intransitive verb meaning to float or rise up.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Pair: 浮(う)かべる (transitive, to set afloat)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 浮(う)いている means 'is floating' (ongoing state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 水(みず)に浮(う)く: to float on water
  • 浮(う)いた話(はなし): idle talk, rumors
  • 浮(う)き沈(しず)み: ups and downs

RELATED:

  • 浮(う)かぶ: to float, to come to mind
