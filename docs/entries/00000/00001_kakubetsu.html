格別(かくべつ)

かくべつ
na-adjective, adverb
exceptional, special, particular

Definitions

1. exceptional, special, extraordinary
Something that stands out from the ordinary; particularly noteworthy.

Examples

今日(きょう)のカレーは格別(かくべつ)においしい。
Today's curry is exceptionally delicious.
格別(かくべつ)のご配慮(はいりょ)をいただき、ありがとうございます。
Thank you for your special consideration.
Formal/polite expression
夏(なつ)の冷(つめ)たいビールは格別(かくべつ)だ。
Cold beer in summer is something special.

Notes

格別(かくべつ) indicates something is notably different from or superior to the ordinary.

Usage patterns:

  • 格別(かくべつ)に + adjective (exceptionally ~)
  • 格別(かくべつ)の + noun (special ~)
  • [noun]は格別(かくべつ)だ (~ is exceptional)

Common in formal expressions:

  • 格別(かくべつ)のご高配(こうはい) (your special patronage)
  • 格別(かくべつ)のお引(ひ)き立(た)て (your exceptional support)

Similar words:

  • 特別(とくべつ) (special - more common)
  • 別格(べっかく) (exceptional, in a class of its own)
