そこで

そこで
conjunction
so, therefore, accordingly

Definitions

1. so, therefore, accordingly
Used to introduce a result, action, or response based on the preceding situation.

Examples

雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきた。そこで傘(かさ)を買(か)った。
It started to rain. So I bought an umbrella.
電車(でんしゃ)が止(と)まっていた。そこでタクシーで行(い)くことにした。
The trains were stopped. So I decided to go by taxi.
質問(しつもん)がたくさんあった。そこで説明会(せつめいかい)を開(ひら)いた。
There were many questions. Accordingly, we held an information session.

Notes

そこで is a conjunction meaning 'so/therefore' that connects a situation with a resulting action or decision. It's different from だから (because/so), which emphasizes causation.

USAGE:
そこで introduces what someone did or decided based on a situation. It implies an active response to circumstances.

SIMILAR CONJUNCTIONS:

  • だから: because, so (emphasizes cause-effect)
  • それで: and then, so (narrative continuation)
  • したがって: therefore (formal)
  • そのため: for that reason (explains reason)

そこで often implies 'given that situation, this is what was/is done.'

Related Words

Contrast: だから (because/so)
