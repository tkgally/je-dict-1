漸(ようや)く

ようやく
adverb
at last; finally; barely; with difficulty
1. at last; finally
After a long wait or process, something has happened.
三年(さんねん)かかって、漸(ようや)く卒業(そつぎょう)できた。
After three years, I finally graduated.
雨(あめ)が漸(ようや)く止(や)んだ。
The rain finally stopped.
漸(ようや)く仕事(しごと)が終(お)わった。
The work finally finished.
Sense 1: at last
漸(ようや)く春(はる)が来(き)た。
Spring has finally come.
Sense 1: finally
漸(ようや)く理解(りかい)できた。
I finally understood.
Sense 1: at last
漸(ようや)く会(あ)えた。
We finally got to meet.
Sense 1: finally
2. barely; with difficulty
Managing to do something but only just, with much effort.
漸(ようや)く試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)した。
I barely passed the exam.
漸(ようや)く間(ま)に合(あ)った。
I barely made it in time.
Sense 2: barely
漸(ようや)く生活(せいかつ)が楽(らく)になった。
Life finally became easier.
Sense 2: with difficulty
漸(ようや)く歩(ある)けるようになった。
I finally became able to walk.
Sense 2: with difficulty

漸(ようや)く is an adverb expressing that something happened after a long time or with difficulty.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 漸(ようや)く終(お)わった (finally finished)
  • 漸(ようや)く分(わ)かった (finally understood)
  • 漸(ようや)く手(て)に入(い)れた (finally obtained)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 漸(ようや)く vs. やっと: Very similar. やっと is more colloquial and often emphasizes relief or barely managing. 漸(ようや)く can be slightly more literary.
  • 漸(ようや)く vs. ついに: ついに emphasizes the culmination of events ("at last, in the end"), often for dramatic or significant events. 漸(ようや)く emphasizes the long process or difficulty.
  • 漸(ようや)く vs. とうとう: とうとう can have either positive or negative connotation for something that finally happened after a long process.

ORTHOGRAPHY:
Usually written in hiragana as ようやく. The kanji {漸} is rarely used in everyday writing.

Register: Neutral to slightly formal.

Related Words

Synonym: やっと (finally, barely)
Related: 遂(つい)に (at last, finally)
