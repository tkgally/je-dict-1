あんな

あんな
pre-noun adjectival
such, like that

Definitions

1. such, that kind of, like that
Refers to something distant from both speaker and listener, or something previously mentioned. Part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system.

Examples

あんな人(ひと)にはなりたくない。
I don't want to become that kind of person.
Often implies negative judgment
あんなに高(たか)いとは思(おも)わなかった。
I didn't think it would be that expensive.
あんなに = to that extent
あんな所(ところ)に行(い)きたくない。
I don't want to go to a place like that.

Notes

あんな is part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system and refers to things/people distant from both speaker and listener.

KO-SO-A-DO PATTERN:

  • こんな: this kind of (near speaker)
  • そんな: that kind of (near listener)
  • あんな: that kind of (far from both)
  • どんな: what kind of (question)

DERIVED FORMS:

  • あんなに: to that extent, that much
  • あんなふうに: in that way/manner

Note: あんな often carries a negative or dismissive nuance, especially when referring to people.
