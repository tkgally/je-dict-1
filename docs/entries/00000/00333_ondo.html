温度(おんど)

おんど
noun
temperature
1. temperature
The degree of heat or cold, measured in degrees.
部屋(へや)の温度(おんど)を上(あ)げてください。
Please raise the room temperature.
温度(おんど)が高(たか)すぎる。
The temperature is too high.
温度(おんど)を測(はか)る。
To measure the temperature.

温度(おんど) is the general term for temperature of objects, liquids, or environments.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 気温(きおん) (air temperature - weather)
  • 体温(たいおん) (body temperature)
  • 水温(すいおん) (water temperature)
  • 室温(しつおん) (room temperature)
