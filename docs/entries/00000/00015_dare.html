誰(だれ)

だれ
pronoun
who

Definitions

1. who
A question word asking about the identity of a person.

Examples

あの人(ひと)は誰(だれ)ですか？
Who is that person?
誰(だれ)が来(き)ましたか？
Who came?
誰(だれ)でも参加(さんか)できます。
Anyone can participate.
誰でも = anyone

Notes

One of the essential question words. More polite form is どなた (donata). Related expressions: 誰(だれ)か (someone), 誰(だれ)も (nobody, with negative), 誰(だれ)でも (anyone).
