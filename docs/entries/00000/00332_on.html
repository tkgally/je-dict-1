御(おん)〜 is a formal/literary honorific prefix, more elevated than お〜 or ご〜.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 御礼(おんれい): gratitude, thanks (formal)
  • 御社(おんしゃ): your company (in business correspondence)
  • 御中(おんちゅう): used when addressing organizations in letters
  • 御名(おんな): your name (archaic/formal)

USAGE NOTE:

This prefix is quite formal and often appears in:

  • Business correspondence
  • Formal documents
  • Historical/literary texts
  • Religious contexts

COMPARISON OF HONORIFIC PREFIXES:

  • お〜: standard polite (お名前(なまえ))
  • ご〜: polite for Sino-Japanese (ご住所(じゅうしょ))
  • 御(おん)〜: formal/literary (御礼(おんれい))
