水洗(すいせん)

すいせん
noun, suru-verb
flushing, water washing

Definitions

1. flushing, water washing
Washing or cleaning with water, especially referring to flush toilets or water-based cleaning systems.

Examples

この地域(ちいき)は水洗(すいせん)トイレが普及(ふきゅう)している。
Flush toilets are widespread in this area.
水洗(すいせん)できる素材(そざい)なので手入(てい)れが簡単(かんたん)だ。
It's a washable material, so maintenance is easy.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)を水洗(すいせん)化(か)する工事(こうじ)をした。
We did construction work to install flush plumbing in the old house.

Notes

水洗(すいせん) most commonly appears in 水洗(すいせん)トイレ (flush toilet), distinguishing it from older pit-style toilets.

COMMON USES:

  • 水洗(すいせん)トイレ: flush toilet
  • 水洗(すいせん)化(か): converting to flush/water systems
  • 水洗(すいせん)式(しき): flush type, water-wash style

NOTE: This is a homophone with 推薦(すいせん) (recommendation) and 水仙(すいせん) (narcissus/daffodil).
