揉(も)む

もむ
godan verb
to rub, to massage, to crumple

Definitions

1. to rub, to massage
To press and move hands over muscles to relieve tension.
2. to crumple, to wrinkle
To crush or squeeze paper or cloth.
3. to dispute, to quarrel
To argue or have a conflict (often in passive form).

Examples

肩(かた)を揉(も)んでください。
Please massage my shoulders.
紙(かみ)を揉(も)んで柔(やわ)らかくする。
I crumple the paper to soften it.
会議(かいぎ)で意見(いけん)が揉(も)まれた。
Opinions were debated at the meeting.

Notes

揉(も)む means to rub, massage, or work with the hands.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 肩(かた)を揉(も)む

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 肩(かた)を揉(も)む: to massage shoulders
  • 揉(も)み消(け)す: to hush up (a scandal)
  • 手(て)を揉(も)む: to wring one's hands (in anxiety)
  • 揉(も)め事(ごと): trouble, dispute

PASSIVE/CAUSATIVE:

  • 揉(も)まれる: to be kneaded, to gain experience through hardship
  • 世間(せけん)に揉(も)まれる: to be tempered by the world
