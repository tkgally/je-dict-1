身分(みぶん)

みぶん
noun
social status, position, identity

Definitions

1. social status, position, standing
One's rank or position in society or an organization.
2. identity
One's personal identity, especially for identification purposes.

Examples

身分(みぶん)証明書(しょうめいしょ)を見(み)せてください。
Please show me your ID.
昔(むかし)は身分(みぶん)によって職業(しょくぎょう)が決(き)まっていた。
In the past, one's occupation was determined by social status.
学生(がくせい)の身分(みぶん)でそんな贅沢(ぜいたく)はできない。
As a student, I can't afford such luxuries.

Notes

身分(みぶん) relates to both social standing and personal identity.

Common compounds:

  • 身分(みぶん)証明書(しょうめいしょ) (ID card, identification)
  • 身分(みぶん)証(しょう) (ID - abbreviated)
  • 身分(みぶん)を隠(かく)す (to hide one's identity)
  • 身分(みぶん)相応(そうおう) (befitting one's status)

Historical context:
身分(みぶん)制度(せいど) (status system) was important in pre-modern Japan, with strict divisions between samurai, farmers, artisans, and merchants.

Related terms:

  • 地位(ちい) (position, status - more modern/workplace-focused)
  • 立場(たちば) (standpoint, position)
