人事(じんじ)

じんじ
noun
personnel, HR
1. personnel affairs, human resources
Matters related to hiring, firing, transfers, and management of employees.
2. other people's affairs
Something that concerns others, not oneself (often in 他人事(ひとごと)).
人事(じんじ)部(ぶ)に連絡(れんらく)してください。
Please contact the HR department.
来月(らいげつ)、大(おお)きな人事(じんじ)異動(いどう)がある。
There will be major personnel changes next month.
人事(じんじ)を尽(つ)くして天命(てんめい)を待(ま)つ。
Do your best and leave the rest to fate. (proverb)

人事(じんじ) primarily refers to personnel management or HR matters in a business context.

Common collocations:

  • 人事(じんじ)部(ぶ) (HR department, personnel department)
  • 人事(じんじ)異動(いどう) (personnel transfer, job reassignment)
  • 人事(じんじ)評価(ひょうか) (personnel evaluation)
  • 人事(じんじ)担当(たんとう) (HR person in charge)

Proverb:

  • 人事(じんじ)を尽(つ)くして天命(てんめい)を待(ま)つ (Do your best and leave the rest to heaven/fate)

Note: 他人事(ひとごと/たにんごと) (other people's business, not one's concern) uses different readings.
