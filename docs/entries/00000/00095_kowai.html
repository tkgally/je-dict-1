怖(こわ)い

こわい
adjective (i-adjective)
scary, frightening

Definitions

1. scary, frightening
Describes something that causes fear or is frightening. Can also describe the feeling of being scared.

Examples

その映画(えいが)は怖(こわ)かったです。
That movie was scary.
暗(くら)いところが怖(こわ)いです。
I'm scared of dark places.
Expressing fear of something
怖(こわ)い夢(ゆめ)を見(み)ました。
I had a scary dream.

Notes

Can describe both the thing that is scary and the feeling of being scared. Unlike English, Japanese uses the same word for both 'scary' and 'scared' - context makes it clear. 怖(こわ)がる is the verb form meaning 'to be afraid of'.
