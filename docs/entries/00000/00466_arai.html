荒(あら)い

あらい
i-adjective
rough; wild; violent; rude

Definitions

1. rough; wild; violent
Characterized by intensity, force, or lack of gentleness.
2. rude; harsh; rough (manner)
Lacking politeness or gentleness in behavior or speech.
3. heavy; excessive (consumption)
Describing excessive use of money, food, etc.

Examples

波(なみ)が荒(あら)い日(ひ)は泳(およ)がないでください。
Please don't swim on days when the waves are rough.
彼(かれ)は気性(きしょう)が荒(あら)い。
He has a violent temper.
金遣(かねづか)いが荒(あら)い。
To spend money recklessly.

Notes

荒(あら)い is an i-adjective describing roughness, wildness, or intensity.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 荒(あら)く
  • Noun form: 荒(あら)さ (roughness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 波(なみ)が荒(あら)い (rough waves)
  • 気性(きしょう)が荒(あら)い (violent temper)
  • 金遣(かねづか)いが荒(あら)い (spendthrift)
  • 息(いき)が荒(あら)い (breathing heavily)
  • 言葉(ことば)が荒(あら)い (harsh words)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 荒(あら)い vs. 粗(あら)い: Both read あらい, but 荒(あら)い refers to intensity/behavior (rough, wild), while 粗(あら)い refers to texture/quality (coarse, crude).

Register: Neutral. Can describe natural phenomena, personality, or behavior.

Related Words

Contrast: 粗(あら)い (coarse, crude (different kanji))
