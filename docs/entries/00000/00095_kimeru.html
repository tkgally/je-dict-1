決(き)める

きめる
verb (ichidan)
to decide

Definitions

1. to decide, to determine
To make a choice or reach a conclusion about something. Used when settling on an option or establishing something.

Examples

旅行(りょこう)の日(ひ)を決(き)めましょう。
Let's decide on the date for the trip.
まだ決(き)めていません。
I haven't decided yet.
何(なに)を食(た)べるか決(き)めましたか。
Have you decided what to eat?

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 決(き)まる (intransitive, to be decided). Pattern: Xを決(き)める (decide X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 決(き)めている = 'have decided' (current decision).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日(ひ)を決(き)める (decide on a date)
  • 何(なに)を〜か決(き)める (decide what to ~)
  • まだ決(き)めていない (haven't decided yet)
  • 決(き)めた！ (I've decided!)

PAIR: 決(き)まる (intransitive) - 時間(じかん)が決(き)まった (the time was decided).

Related Words
