下旬(げじゅん)

げじゅん
noun
last third of a month

Definitions

1. last third of a month, late (month)
The final ten days of a month, approximately the 21st to the end.

Examples

3月(がつ)下旬(げじゅん)に桜(さくら)が咲(さ)く。
Cherry blossoms bloom in late March.
来月(らいげつ)下旬(げじゅん)に旅行(りょこう)に行(い)く予定(よてい)だ。
I plan to travel at the end of next month.
下旬(げじゅん)になると仕事(しごと)が忙(いそが)しくなる。
Work gets busy toward the end of the month.

Notes

下旬(げじゅん) refers to the last third of a month (roughly days 21-31).

Month divisions:

  • 上旬(じょうじゅん) (first third, ~1st-10th)
  • 中旬(ちゅうじゅん) (middle third, ~11th-20th)
  • 下旬(げじゅん) (last third, ~21st-end)

Common usage:

  • [month]下旬(げじゅん) (late [month])
  • 下旬(げじゅん)に (toward the end of the month)
  • 下旬(げじゅん)頃 (around the end of the month)

Related terms:

  • 月末(げつまつ) (end of month - more specific)
  • 末(まつ) (end)
