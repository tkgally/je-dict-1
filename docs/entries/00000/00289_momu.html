1. to rub, to massage
To press and move hands over muscles to relieve tension.
肩(かた)を揉(も)む。
To massage one's shoulders.
肩(かた)を揉(も)んでください。
Please massage my shoulders.
疲(つか)れた足(あし)を揉(も)んでもらった。
I had my tired legs massaged.
2. to crumple, to wrinkle
To crush or squeeze paper or cloth.
紙(かみ)を揉(も)む。
To crumple paper.
紙(かみ)を揉(も)んで柔(やわ)らかくする。
I crumple the paper to soften it.
布(ぬの)を揉(も)んでシワを取(と)る。
I rub the cloth to remove the wrinkles.
3. to thrash out, to hash out (an issue)
To subject a plan or opinion to vigorous back-and-forth discussion. Most often met in the passive 揉(も)まれる, which also means to be knocked about by hardship or by a demanding environment.
揉(も)め事(ごと)が起(お)きた。
A dispute arose.
揉(も)め事(ごと) = trouble, dispute
会議(かいぎ)で意見(いけん)が揉(も)まれた。
Opinions were debated at the meeting.
世間(せけん)に揉(も)まれて、彼(かれ)は強(つよ)くなった。
Having been tempered by the world, he became stronger.
世間(せけん)に揉(も)まれる = to be hardened by life experience

揉(も)む means to rub, massage, or work with the hands.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 肩(かた)を揉(も)む

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 肩(かた)を揉(も)む: to massage shoulders
  • 揉(も)み消(け)す: to hush up (a scandal)
  • 手(て)を揉(も)む: to wring one's hands (in anxiety)
  • 揉(も)め事(ごと): trouble, dispute

PASSIVE/CAUSATIVE:

  • 揉(も)まれる: to be kneaded, to gain experience through hardship
  • 世間(せけん)に揉(も)まれる: to be tempered by the world

Related Words

Contrast: 擦(こす)る (rub with friction)
