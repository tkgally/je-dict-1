毎晩(まいばん)

まいばん
noun, adverb
every night

Definitions

1. every night, every evening
Indicates something that happens regularly every night or evening.

Examples

毎晩(まいばん)本(ほん)を読(よ)みます。
I read books every night.
毎晩(まいばん)十一時(じゅういちじ)に寝(ね)ます。
I go to bed at eleven o'clock every night.
毎晩(まいばん)お風呂(ふろ)に入(はい)ります。
I take a bath every night.

Notes

毎晩(まいばん) refers to the nighttime/evening period, typically after dinner until bedtime. Compare with 毎朝(まいあさ) (every morning). Like other 毎(まい) words, it's often used without a particle as an adverb.
