狙(ねら)い

ねらい
noun
aim; intention; objective; target

Definitions

1. aim; target
The act of directing toward a target, or the target itself.
2. intention; objective; purpose
What one hopes to achieve or the goal behind an action.

Examples

狙(ねら)いを定(さだ)めて撃(う)った。
I took aim and fired.
この政策(せいさく)の狙(ねら)いは何(なん)ですか。
What is the aim of this policy?
狙(ねら)いどおりにいった。
It went as intended.

Notes

狙(ねら)い is the noun form of the verb 狙(ねら)う (to aim at), referring to both physical targeting and abstract objectives.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 狙(ねら)いを定(さだ)める (to take aim)
  • 狙(ねら)いどおり (as intended/planned)
  • 狙(ねら)いが外(はず)れる (to miss the mark)
  • ～の狙(ねら)い (the aim of ~)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 狙(ねら)い撃(う)ち: Targeted attack, sniping
  • 狙(ねら)い目(め): Target, good opportunity

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 狙(ねら)い vs. 目的(もくてき): 目的(もくてき) is a more formal word for "purpose" or "objective"; 狙(ねら)い emphasizes the targeting or strategic aspect.
  • 狙(ねら)い vs. 意図(いと): 意図(いと) means "intention" with a nuance of deliberate planning.

Register: Neutral. Used in both concrete (shooting) and abstract (policy) contexts.

Related Words

Related: 狙(ねら)う (verb form)
Synonym: 目的(もくてき) (purpose, objective)
