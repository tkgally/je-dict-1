快適(かいてき)

かいてき
na-adjective
comfortable, pleasant
1. comfortable, pleasant, agreeable
Describes environments or conditions that are pleasant and comfortable.
快適(かいてき)な部屋(へや)ですね。
It's a comfortable room, isn't it?
エアコンで快適(かいてき)に過(す)ごせる。
We can spend time comfortably with the air conditioning.
快適(かいてき)な生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)る。
To lead a comfortable life.

USAGE:

  • 快適(かいてき)な + noun (comfortable ~)
  • 快適(かいてき)に + verb (comfortably)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 快適(かいてき)な環境(かんきょう) (comfortable environment)
  • 快適(かいてき)な空間(くうかん) (comfortable space)
  • 快適(かいてき)な暮(く)らし (comfortable living)
