快適(かいてき)

かいてき
na-adjective
comfortable, pleasant
1. comfortable, pleasant, agreeable
Describes environments or conditions that are pleasant and comfortable.
快適(かいてき)な部屋(へや)ですね。
It's a comfortable room, isn't it?
エアコンで快適(かいてき)に過(す)ごせる。
We can spend time comfortably with the air conditioning.
快適(かいてき)な生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)る。
To lead a comfortable life.
新幹線(しんかんせん)は快適(かいてき)だ。
The Shinkansen is comfortable.
快適(かいてき)な睡眠(すいみん)のために枕(まくら)を替(か)えた。
I changed my pillow for better sleep.

WORD FORMATION:
快(かい) (pleasant) + 適(てき) (suitable).

USAGE:
快適(かいてき) describes conditions, environments, and equipment - a room, a climate, a seat, a commute - not people. There is no ×快適(かいてき)な人(ひと), and a speaker does not normally use it of their own momentary feeling either; for that, use 気持(きも)ちいい. It is a slightly formal, written-leaning word, at home in product copy, travel writing, and reviews.

GRAMMATICAL FORMS:

  • Modifier: 快適(かいてき)な部屋(へや) (a comfortable room)
  • Predicate: 部屋(へや)が快適(かいてき)だ (the room is comfortable)
  • Adverb: 快適(かいてき)に過(す)ごす (spend time comfortably)
  • Noun: 快適(かいてき)さ (comfort, the quality of being comfortable)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 快適(かいてき)な環境(かんきょう) (comfortable environment)
  • 快適(かいてき)な空間(くうかん) (comfortable space)
  • 快適(かいてき)な生活(せいかつ) (comfortable life)
  • 快適(かいてき)な睡眠(すいみん) (good sleep)
  • 快適(かいてき)な乗(の)り心地(ごこち) (a comfortable ride)
  • 快適(かいてき)さを求(もと)める (to seek comfort)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 心地(ここち)よい - pleasant to the senses; a breeze or a sound can be 心地(ここち)よい but not 快適(かいてき)
  • 居心地(いごこち)がいい - specifically about how it feels to stay somewhere, including the social atmosphere
  • 楽(らく) - comfortable in the sense of requiring little effort or strain
  • 便利(べんり) - convenient; about usefulness rather than physical comfort

OPPOSITE:
不快(ふかい) (unpleasant, disagreeable).

Related Words

Synonym: 心地(ここち)よい (pleasant to the senses)
Related: 居心地(いごこち) (how it feels to stay somewhere)
Related: 楽(らく) (comfortable in the sense of easy)
Antonym: 不快(ふかい) (unpleasant, disagreeable)
