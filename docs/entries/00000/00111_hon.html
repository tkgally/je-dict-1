本(ほん)

ほん
noun
book
1. book
A bound publication with pages, including novels, textbooks, manga, and other printed materials. The most common word for 'book' in Japanese.
本(ほん)を読(よ)む。
I read a book.
この本(ほん)は面白(おもしろ)い。
This book is interesting.
図書館(としょかん)で本(ほん)を借(か)りた。
I borrowed a book from the library.
新(あたら)しい本(ほん)を買(か)いたいけど、何(なに)がいいか分(わ)からない。
I want to buy a new book, but I don't know what's good.
寝(ね)る前(まえ)に本(ほん)を読(よ)むのが好(す)きで、毎晩(まいばん)少(すこ)なくとも三十分(さんじゅっぷん)は読(よ)んでいる。
I like reading books before bed and read for at least 30 minutes every night.

本(ほん) is also used as a counter for long, cylindrical objects (pens, bottles, trees, etc.) with readings ほん, ぼん, or ぽん depending on the number. As a prefix, 本(ほん) can mean 'this' or 'main' (e.g., 本日(ほんじつ) today, 本店(ほんてん) main store).
