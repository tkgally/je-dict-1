偉(えら)い

えらい
i-adjective
great, remarkable; important; terrible
1. great, remarkable, admirable
Worthy of admiration for achievements or status.
彼(かれ)は偉(えら)い学者(がくしゃ)だ。
He is a great scholar.
偉(えら)いね！よく頑張(がんば)ったね。
Good job! You worked hard.
Sense 1: admirable (praise)
偉(えら)い人(ひと)になりたい。
I want to become a great person.
Sense 1: great/admirable
歴史(れきし)に残(のこ)る偉(えら)い発明家(はつめいか)。
A great inventor who will be remembered in history.
Sense 1: great/remarkable
一人(ひとり)で全部(ぜんぶ)やったの？偉(えら)い！
You did it all by yourself? That's amazing!
Sense 1: admirable (praise)
2. important, high-ranking
Having high social or professional status.
会社(かいしゃ)で偉(えら)い人(ひと)になりたい。
I want to become an important person in the company.
お偉(えら)いさんが来(く)る。
A bigwig is coming.
Sense 2: important/high-ranking
偉(えら)い立場(たちば)の人(ひと)。
A person in an important position.
Sense 2: high-ranking
偉(えら)くなったものだ。
You've become quite the big shot.
Sense 2: important (sometimes sarcastic)
偉(えら)い政治家(せいじか)たち。
Important politicians.
Sense 2: high-ranking
3. terrible, awful (colloquial)
Used to emphasize how bad or difficult something is.
今日(きょう)は偉(えら)く暑(あつ)いね。
It's awfully hot today, isn't it?
Colloquial usage
偉(えら)く疲(つか)れた。
I'm terribly tired.
Sense 3: terrible (colloquial)
偉(えら)い目(め)に遭(あ)った。
I had a terrible experience.
Sense 3: terrible/awful
偉(えら)いことになった。
Things have gotten bad.
Sense 3: terrible/awful
偉(えら)く時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took an awfully long time.
Sense 3: terrible (colloquial intensifier)

偉(えら)い is an i-adjective with multiple meanings depending on context.

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 偉(えら)く (very, awfully - colloquial)
  • Noun form: 偉(えら)さ (greatness)

MEANING 1 - ADMIRABLE:

  • 偉(えら)い人(ひと): a great/admirable person
  • 偉(えら)いね！: Good job! Well done! (praise)

MEANING 2 - HIGH-RANKING:

  • 偉(えら)い人(ひと): an important/high-ranking person
  • お偉(えら)いさん: bigwig, VIP (slightly sarcastic)

MEANING 3 - COLLOQUIAL INTENSIFIER:

  • 偉(えら)く疲(つか)れた: I'm really tired
  • 偉(えら)い目(め)に遭(あ)った: I had a terrible experience
