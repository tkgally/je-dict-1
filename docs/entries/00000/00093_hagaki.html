はがき

はがき
noun
postcard

Definitions

1. postcard
A card for sending short messages by mail without an envelope. Japanese postcards are standardized in size.

Examples

旅行(りょこう)先(さき)からはがきを送(おく)りました。
I sent a postcard from my trip destination.
はがきを五(ご)枚(まい)ください。
Please give me five postcards.
年賀(ねんが)はがきを書(か)きます。
I'm writing New Year's postcards.
年賀はがき are traditional New Year greeting cards

Notes

Can also be written as 葉書(はがき). Counter: 枚(まい). 年賀(ねんが)はがき (New Year's postcards) are an important Japanese tradition—millions are sent each year to arrive on January 1st.
