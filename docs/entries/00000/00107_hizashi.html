日差(ひざ)し

ひざし
noun
sunlight, rays of the sun
1. sunlight, sunshine, rays of the sun
The light and warmth from the sun, especially when perceived as direct beams.
春(はる)の柔(やわ)らかい日差(ひざ)しが気持(きも)ちいい。
The soft spring sunlight feels nice.
強(つよ)い日差(ひざ)しを避(さ)けて帽子(ぼうし)をかぶった。
I wore a hat to avoid the strong sunlight.
窓(まど)から日差(ひざ)しが差(さ)し込(こ)んでいる。
Sunlight is streaming in through the window.

日差(ひざ)し emphasizes the quality and feeling of sunlight, often described with adjectives like 強(つよ)い (strong), 柔(やわ)らかい (soft), or 暖(あたた)かい (warm).

Alternate kanji spellings:

  • 日射(ひざ)し
  • 陽射(ひざ)し

Common collocations:

  • 日差(ひざ)しが強(つよ)い (the sunlight is strong)
  • 日差(ひざ)しを浴(あ)びる (to bask in sunlight)
  • 日差(ひざ)しが差(さ)し込(こ)む (sunlight streams in)

Related terms:

  • 太陽(たいよう) (sun)
  • 日光(にっこう) (sunlight - more formal/scientific)
  • 陽光(ようこう) (sunlight - literary)
