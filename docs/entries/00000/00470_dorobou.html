泥棒(どろぼう)

どろぼう
noun
thief, burglar

Definitions

1. thief, burglar, robber
A person who steals things, especially by breaking into houses.

Examples

泥棒(どろぼう)に入(はい)られた。
A burglar broke in. / We were robbed.
Passive form indicating victim
泥棒(どろぼう)が捕(つか)まった。
The thief was caught.
泥棒(どろぼう)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please watch out for thieves.

Notes

泥棒(どろぼう) typically refers to burglars or thieves who break into homes.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 泥棒(どろぼう)に入(はい)られる: to be burglarized
  • 泥棒(どろぼう)を捕(つか)まえる: to catch a thief
  • 泥棒(どろぼう)猫(ねこ): cat burglar; sneaky person

RELATED TERMS:

  • 窃盗(せっとう): theft (legal/formal term)
  • 空(あ)き巣(す): burglar (when residents are away)
  • 万引(まんび)き: shoplifting
  • 強盗(ごうとう): robbery (with violence/threat)

Note: 泥棒(どろぼう) is also used figuratively: 時間(じかん)泥棒(どろぼう) (time thief - something that wastes time)
