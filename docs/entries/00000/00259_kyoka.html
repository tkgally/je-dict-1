1. permission, approval, authorization
Official consent to do something.
許可(きょか)を取(と)る。
To obtain permission.
撮影(さつえい)許可(きょか)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Photography permission is required.
許可(きょか)なく入(はい)らないでください。
Please don't enter without permission.

許可(きょか) means official permission or authorization to do something.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を許可(きょか)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 許可(きょか)を取(と)る: to obtain permission
  • 許可(きょか)を得(え)る: to receive permission
  • 許可(きょか)する: to permit, grant permission
  • 許可(きょか)なく: without permission
  • 許可(きょか)を求(もと)める: to request permission

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 入場(にゅうじょう)許可(きょか): admission permission
  • 営業(えいぎょう)許可(きょか): business license
  • 撮影(さつえい)許可(きょか): photography permission
  • 輸出(ゆしゅつ)許可(きょか): export license
  • 建築(けんちく)許可(きょか): building permit

CONTRAST:

  • 許可(きょか): official permission (formal)
  • 許(ゆる)し: forgiveness, personal permission (less formal)
  • 承認(しょうにん): approval (often for documents/plans)

Related Words

Related: 承認(しょうにん) (approval (for documents/plans))
