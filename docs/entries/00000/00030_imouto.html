妹(いもうと)

いもうと
noun
younger sister

Definitions

1. younger sister
A female sibling who is younger than oneself. Used for both one's own younger sister and others' younger sisters.

Examples

妹(いもうと)は中学生(ちゅうがくせい)です。
My younger sister is a middle school student.
妹(いもうと)に日本語(にほんご)を教(おし)えています。
I'm teaching Japanese to my younger sister.
妹(いもうと)はとても可愛(かわい)いです。
My younger sister is very cute.

Notes

Unlike older siblings, the humble/polite distinction is less strict for younger siblings. 妹(いもうと)さん is used to refer to someone else's younger sister politely.

Related: 弟(おとうと) (younger brother).
