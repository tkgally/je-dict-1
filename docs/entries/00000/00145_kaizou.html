改造(かいぞう)

かいぞう
noun, suru-verb
remodeling, modification
1. remodeling, renovation, reconstruction
Significantly changing or rebuilding the structure of a building or object.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)を改造(かいぞう)して住(す)んでいる。
I live in an old house that has been remodeled.
2. modification, customization
Altering a machine, vehicle, or device to change its function or appearance.
車(くるま)を改造(かいぞう)するのが趣味(しゅみ)だ。
Modifying cars is my hobby.
違法(いほう)改造(かいぞう)されたバイク(ばいく)は危険(きけん)だ。
Illegally modified motorcycles are dangerous.

改造(かいぞう) implies significant structural changes, not just minor repairs or adjustments.

Common collocations:

  • 改造(かいぞう)車(しゃ) (modified car)
  • 内閣(ないかく)改造(かいぞう) (cabinet reshuffle)
  • 違法(いほう)改造(かいぞう) (illegal modification)
  • 改造(かいぞう)工事(こうじ) (renovation work)

Related terms:

  • リフォーム (renovation - mainly for homes)
  • カスタム (custom modification)
  • 修理(しゅうり) (repair - fixing, not changing)
  • 改装(かいそう) (refurbishment - focus on appearance)
