ワイン

わいん
noun
wine

Definitions

1. wine
An alcoholic drink made from fermented grapes. From English 'wine.'

Examples

赤(あか)ワインと白(しろ)ワイン、どちらが好(す)きですか。
Which do you prefer, red wine or white wine?
フランスのワインを買(か)いました。
I bought French wine.
肉料理(にくりょうり)にはワインが合(あ)います。
Wine goes well with meat dishes.

Notes

ワイン comes from English 'wine.' 赤ワイン (red wine) and 白ワイン (white wine) are the common types. Japan also produces its own wine, especially in regions like Yamanashi prefecture.
