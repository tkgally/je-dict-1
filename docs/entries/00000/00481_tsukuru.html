1. to make, to create, to produce
To bring something into existence through effort or skill. Used for cooking, crafting, building, or creating anything.
ご飯(はん)を作(つく)る。
I make a meal.
ケーキを作(つく)った。
I made a cake.
友達(ともだち)を作(つく)りたい。
I want to make friends.
この机(つくえ)は木(き)で作(つく)られている。
This desk is made of wood.
母(はは)は毎日(まいにち)おいしい料理(りょうり)を作(つく)ってくれる。感謝(かんしゃ)している。
My mother makes delicious food for me every day. I'm grateful.
2. to prepare, to organize
To arrange or set up something. 計画(けいかく)を作(つく)る (to make a plan), リストを作(つく)る (to make a list).
計画(けいかく)を作(つく)る。
I make a plan.
リストを作(つく)っておいた。
I made a list.
時間(じかん)を作(つく)って会(あ)いましょう。
Let's make time and meet.
機会(きかい)を作(つく)って話(はな)そう。
Let's make an opportunity and talk.
旅行(りょこう)の前(まえ)に予定表(よていひょう)を作(つく)っておくと、安心(あんしん)して楽(たの)しめる。
If you make an itinerary before a trip, you can relax and enjoy it.

TRANSITIVITY:
他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを作(つく)る (make X). Passive 作(つく)られる is the ordinary way to say what something is made of or where it is produced.

ASPECT:
ている covers both the ongoing action and the resulting state: 作(つく)っている can be "is making" or "has made (and it exists now)," and context decides which.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ご飯(はん)を作(つく)る — to cook a meal
  • 友達(ともだち)を作(つく)る — to make friends
  • 計画(けいかく)を作(つく)る — to make a plan
  • 時間(じかん)を作(つく)る — to make time (for something)
  • Xで作(つく)る — to make something out of X

KANJI CHOICE:

  • 作(つく)る is the general-purpose spelling and the safe default.
  • 造(つく)る is for things built or brewed on a large scale — ships, buildings, sake.
  • 創(つく)る is reserved for creating something that did not exist before, and is mostly seen in slogans and company mottos.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建(た)てる is the verb for putting up a building; 作(つく)る is not used of houses except loosely.
  • 作成(さくせい)する is the formal word for drawing up documents, lists, and plans — the business register of sense 2.
