暇(ひま)

ひま
na-adjective
free time, not busy

Definitions

1. free time
Time when one has nothing to do.
2. not busy
Having nothing to do; idle.

Examples

今日(きょう)は暇(ひま)です。
I'm free today.
暇(ひま)な時(とき)に来(き)てください。
Please come when you're free.
暇(ひま)がありますか？
Do you have free time?

Notes

As a noun: 暇(ひま)がある (have free time). Antonym: 忙(いそが)しい (busy).

See also: 忙(いそが)しい
