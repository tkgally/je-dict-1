頬(ほほ)

ほほ
noun
cheek

Definitions

1. cheek (of face)
The fleshy part of the face below the eye and between the nose and ear.

Examples

赤(あか)ちゃんの頬(ほほ)は柔(やわ)らかい。
A baby's cheeks are soft.
頬(ほほ)に涙(なみだ)が流(なが)れた。
Tears ran down my cheeks.
頬(ほほ)が赤(あか)くなった。
My cheeks turned red.

Notes

頬(ほほ) refers to the cheeks of the face.

READINGS:

  • 頬(ほほ): standard reading
  • 頬(ほお): alternative reading (also common)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 頬(ほほ)を膨(ふく)らます: to puff out one's cheeks (showing displeasure)
  • 頬(ほほ)が落(お)ちる: (food is) so delicious one's cheeks might fall off
  • 頬(ほほ)紅(べに): blush, rouge
  • 頬(ほほ)杖(づえ): resting one's chin in one's hand
  • 頬(ほほ)骨(ぼね): cheekbone

RELATED WORDS:

  • 頬(ほほ)える: to smile (showing on the cheeks)
  • 頬(ほほ)ずり: rubbing cheeks together (as affection)
