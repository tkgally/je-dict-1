感激(かんげき)

かんげき
noun, suru-verb
deep emotion, being moved

Definitions

1. deep emotion, being deeply moved, strong impression
A powerful emotional response, typically positive, such as being moved or inspired.

Examples

彼(かれ)の優(やさ)しさに感激(かんげき)した。
I was deeply moved by his kindness.
初(はじ)めて富士山(ふじさん)を見(み)て感激(かんげき)した。
I was deeply moved when I first saw Mt. Fuji.
お客様(おきゃくさま)からの感激(かんげき)の手紙(てがみ)をいただいた。
We received an emotional letter from a customer.

Notes

感激(かんげき) describes being profoundly moved or touched emotionally.

Common patterns:

  • 〜に感激(かんげき)する (to be moved by ~)
  • 感激(かんげき)の涙(なみだ) (tears of emotion)
  • 感激(かんげき)して泣(な)く (to cry from being moved)

Related terms:

  • 感動(かんどう) (being moved - similar but slightly less intense)
  • 感心(かんしん) (admiration, being impressed)
  • 興奮(こうふん) (excitement)

The word combines 感(かん) (feeling) + 激(げき) (intense), suggesting intense emotion.
