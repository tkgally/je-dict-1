気(き)をつけて

きをつけて
expression
take care, be careful

Definitions

1. take care, be careful
An expression telling someone to be careful or take care. Often used when someone is leaving. Comes from 気(き)をつける (to pay attention, to be careful).

Examples

気(き)をつけて行(い)ってきてね。
Take care and have a good trip.
車(くるま)に気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful of cars.
Polite form
足元(あしもと)に気(き)をつけて。
Watch your step.

Notes

気をつけて is the te-form of 気をつける used as a farewell or warning. It's commonly said when someone is leaving on a trip or going out. The polite form is 気をつけてください. It can also be used as a warning about specific dangers.
