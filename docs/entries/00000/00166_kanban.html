看板(かんばん)

かんばん
noun
sign, signboard
1. sign, signboard, billboard
A board displaying information, often a shop name or advertisement.
店(みせ)の看板(かんばん)が新(あたら)しくなった。
The shop's sign was renewed.
2. figurehead, poster child
A person or thing that represents or symbolizes something.
彼女(かのじょ)はこの会社(かいしゃ)の看板(かんばん)女優(じょゆう)だ。
She is this company's leading actress.
3. closing time
Used in expressions referring to the end of business hours.
もうすぐ看板(かんばん)の時間(じかん)です。
It's almost closing time.

看板(かんばん) has multiple meanings related to representation and display.

Common expressions:

  • 看板(かんばん)を出(だ)す (to put up a sign; to open for business)
  • 看板(かんばん)を下(お)ろす (to close down a business)
  • 看板(かんばん)商品(しょうひん) (flagship product)
  • 看板(かんばん)娘(むすめ)/看板(かんばん)女優(じょゆう) (poster girl/leading actress)
  • 看板(かんばん)に偽(いつわ)りなし (truth in advertising)

Business/manufacturing:

  • かんばん方式(ほうしき) (Kanban system - Toyota's just-in-time manufacturing method)

Note on closing time:

  • 看板(かんばん)だ (It's closing time - from the practice of taking down signs at closing)
