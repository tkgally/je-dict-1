紫(むらさき)

むらさき
noun, no-adjective
purple, violet

Definitions

1. purple, violet (color)
A color between red and blue.
2. soy sauce (slang)
A slang term for soy sauce, especially at sushi restaurants.

Examples

紫(むらさき)の花(はな)が咲(さ)いている。
Purple flowers are blooming.
この着物(きもの)は紫(むらさき)色(いろ)だ。
This kimono is purple.
紫(むらさき)をもう少(すこ)しください。
Please give me a little more soy sauce.
At a sushi restaurant

Notes

紫(むらさき) is the color purple. In traditional Japan, purple was associated with nobility and high rank.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 紫(むらさき)色(いろ): purple color
  • 紫外線(しがいせん): ultraviolet rays
  • 紫陽花(あじさい): hydrangea (written with 紫)

SUSHI RESTAURANT SLANG:

紫(むらさき) is used to mean 'soy sauce' at sushi restaurants, along with other special terms like:

  • 紫(むらさき): soy sauce
  • あがり: green tea
  • シャリ: sushi rice
  • ガリ: pickled ginger

HISTORICAL NOTE:
Purple dye was expensive and difficult to produce, so 紫(むらさき) became associated with royalty and high status in Japan.
