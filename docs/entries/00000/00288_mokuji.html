目次(もくじ)

もくじ
noun
table of contents
1. table of contents, index
A list at the beginning of a book or document showing the chapters, sections, or topics and their page numbers.
目次(もくじ)を見(み)て、読(よ)みたい章(しょう)を探(さが)した。
I looked at the table of contents and found the chapter I wanted to read.
この本(ほん)には詳(くわ)しい目次(もくじ)がついている。
This book has a detailed table of contents.
目次(もくじ)は3ページにあります。
The table of contents is on page 3.

目次(もくじ) lists the contents and organization of a book, document, or publication.

Related terms:

  • 索引(さくいん) (index - alphabetical list at the end)
  • 章(しょう) (chapter)
  • 節(せつ) (section)

Usage:

  • 目次(もくじ)を見(み)る (to look at the table of contents)
  • 目次(もくじ)から選(えら)ぶ (to choose from the table of contents)

Digital context:
In digital documents and websites, 目次(もくじ) may be interactive, with clickable links to each section.
