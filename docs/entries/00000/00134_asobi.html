遊(あそ)び

あそび
noun
play; game; amusement

Definitions

1. play, playing, game
The activity of playing, especially for children.
2. amusement, fun, recreation
Activities done for enjoyment rather than work.
3. slack, play (mechanical)
A small amount of looseness in a mechanism.

Examples

子供(こども)たちが遊(あそ)びに夢中(むちゅう)だ。
The children are absorbed in play.
遊(あそ)びに来(き)てください。
Please come over to hang out.
Common invitation
仕事(しごと)と遊(あそ)びのバランス
Work-life balance (balance between work and play)

Notes

遊(あそ)び is a noun derived from the verb 遊(あそ)ぶ (to play).

Common expressions:

  • 遊(あそ)びに行(い)く: to go out (for fun), to visit someone casually
  • 遊(あそ)びに来(く)る: to come over (casually)
  • 遊(あそ)び相手(あいて): playmate
  • 遊(あそ)び場(ば): playground

Note: 遊(あそ)びに行(い)く and 遊(あそ)びに来(く)る are very common casual expressions for visiting friends or going out, not just 'playing.'
