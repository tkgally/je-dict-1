気持ち(きもち)

きもち
noun
feeling, mood

Definitions

1. feeling, sensation
A physical or emotional sensation.
2. mood, frame of mind
One's emotional state or disposition.

Examples

今日(きょう)は気持ち(きもち)がいい天気(てんき)ですね。
It's pleasant weather today, isn't it?
彼女(かのじょ)の気持ち(きもち)が分(わ)からない。
I don't understand her feelings.
気持ち(きもち)悪(わる)い。
I feel sick. / That's disgusting.

Notes

Common expressions:

  • 気持ち(きもち)がいい - feels good, pleasant
  • 気持ち(きもち)悪(わる)い - feels bad, disgusting, creepy
  • 気持ち(きもち)を伝(つた)える - to convey one's feelings
