年度(ねんど)

ねんど
noun
fiscal year, academic year

Definitions

1. fiscal year, financial year
A 12-month period used for accounting and budgeting purposes. In Japan, the fiscal year typically runs from April 1 to March 31.
2. academic year, school year
The annual period during which schools and universities are in session. In Japan, it runs from April to March.

Examples

来(らい)年度(ねんど)の予算(よさん)を作成(さくせい)している。
We are preparing next fiscal year's budget.
2024年度(ねんど)の売上(うりあげ)は過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)だった。
Sales for fiscal year 2024 were the highest ever.
新(しん)年度(ねんど)は4月(がつ)から始(はじ)まります。
The new fiscal/academic year starts in April.

Notes

年度(ねんど) refers to an administrative period of one year that may not align with the calendar year. In Japan, both the fiscal year (会計(かいけい)年度(ねんど)) and academic year (学(がく)年度(ねんど)) run from April to March.

Common expressions:

  • 今(こん)年度(ねんど) (this fiscal/academic year)
  • 来(らい)年度(ねんど) (next fiscal/academic year)
  • 前(ぜん)年度(ねんど) (last fiscal/academic year)
  • 年度(ねんど)末(まつ) (end of fiscal year)
  • 年度(ねんど)初(はじ)め (beginning of fiscal year)

Distinct from 年(ねん) (calendar year) which follows January to December.
