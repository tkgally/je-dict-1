光(ひかり)

ひかり
noun
light

Definitions

1. light, ray
The natural agent that makes things visible; brightness from the sun, a lamp, etc.
2. hope, bright future
Used metaphorically to represent hope or a positive outlook.

Examples

太陽(たいよう)の光(ひかり)が眩(まぶ)しい。
The sunlight is dazzling.
光(ひかり)が見(み)えてきた。
A light became visible. / I can see hope.
Can be literal or figurative
光(ひかり)の速(はや)さ
the speed of light

Notes

光(ひかり) refers to light in both literal and figurative senses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 太陽(たいよう)の光(ひかり): sunlight
  • 月(つき)の光(ひかり): moonlight
  • 光(ひかり)が当(あ)たる: light shines on
  • 光(ひかり)を放(はな)つ: to emit light
  • 希望(きぼう)の光(ひかり): ray of hope

RELATED TERMS:

  • 光(ひか)る: to shine (verb)
  • 明(あか)るい: bright
  • 輝(かがや)く: to sparkle
  • 日光(にっこう): sunlight

Note: ひかり is also the name of a Shinkansen line and train series.
