科目(かもく)

かもく
noun
subject, course
1. subject, course (of study)
An academic subject or course in school curriculum.
好(す)きな科目(かもく)は何(なん)ですか。
What is your favorite subject?
必修(ひっしゅう)科目(かもく)を取(と)らなければならない。
I have to take required subjects.
選択(せんたく)科目(かもく)を選(えら)ぶ。
To choose elective subjects.

科目(かもく) refers to a subject or course of study in an academic curriculum, typically at university level.

COMMON TYPES:

  • 必修(ひっしゅう)科目(かもく): required/compulsory subject
  • 選択(せんたく)科目(かもく): elective subject
  • 専門(せんもん)科目(かもく): specialized/major subject
  • 一般(いっぱん)教養(きょうよう)科目(かもく): general education subject
  • 基礎(きそ)科目(かもく): foundational subject

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 科目(かもく)を取(と)る: to take a subject
  • 科目(かもく)を落(お)とす: to fail a subject
  • 科目(かもく)を履修(りしゅう)する: to register for a subject

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 教科(きょうか): school subject (primary/secondary school)
  • 授業(じゅぎょう): class, lesson
  • 講座(こうざ): course, lecture series
  • 単位(たんい): credit (academic)
