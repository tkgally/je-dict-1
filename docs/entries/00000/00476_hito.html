人(ひと)

ひと
noun
person, human
1. person, human being
A human individual. The basic word for referring to people in general.
人(ひと)がいる。
There is a person.
あの人(ひと)は誰(だれ)?
Who is that person?
人(ひと)が多(おお)いですね。
There are a lot of people, aren't there?
日本(にほん)には親切(しんせつ)な人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
There are many kind people in Japan.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)で本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいる人(ひと)を見(み)ると、自分(じぶん)も読(よ)みたくなる。
When I see people reading books on the train, it makes me want to read too.
2. someone, other people
Used to refer to others in general. 人(ひと)に言(い)う (to tell someone), 人(ひと)の話(はなし) (what others say).
人(ひと)に聞(き)いてみよう。
Let me ask someone.
人(ひと)のことを気(き)にしないで。
Don't worry about what others think.
人(ひと)に迷惑(めいわく)をかけてはいけません。
You shouldn't trouble other people.
人(ひと)の話(はなし)をよく聞(き)くことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to listen well to what others say.
人(ひと)の気持(きも)ちが分(わ)かる人(ひと)になりたいと思(おも)っている。
I want to become someone who understands other people's feelings.
3. character, personality
The nature or personality of a person. いい人(ひと) (a good/kind person).
いい人(ひと)だね。
They're a good person, aren't they?
彼(かれ)は優(やさ)しい人(ひと)だ。
He is a kind person.
あの人(ひと)は面白(おもしろ)い人(ひと)です。
That person is an interesting person.
彼女(かのじょ)は人(ひと)がいい。
She has a good personality.
彼(かれ)は仕事(しごと)ができる人(ひと)だけど、ちょっと厳(きび)しい人(ひと)なので、話(はな)しにくい。
He's a capable person, but he's a bit strict, so he's hard to talk to.

人(ひと) is the basic word for "person" or "people." One of the most fundamental vocabulary items.

READINGS:

  • ひと: standalone word
  • じん: nationality/type suffix (日本人(にほんじん), 外国人(がいこくじん))
  • にん: counter for people (三人(さんにん))

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)に聞(き)く: to ask someone
  • 人(ひと)の話(はなし): what others say
  • いい人(ひと): a good/kind person
  • 人(ひと)が多(おお)い: crowded (many people)

SENSE 2 - "OTHER PEOPLE":
When 人(ひと) means "others" rather than "a person," it often carries a mild reproach: 人(ひと)の気(き)も知(し)らないで (without any thought for how I feel), 人(ひと)の悪口(わるくち)を言(い)う (to speak ill of others), 人(ひと)を馬鹿(ばか)にする (to make a fool of someone). Learners often read these as a neutral "people" and miss the complaint.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 人(ひと) vs. 人間(にんげん): 人(ひと) is the everyday word; 人間(にんげん) is "human being" as a species or as a moral character (人間(にんげん)らしい, "humane")
  • 人(ひと) vs. 人々(ひとびと): the plural form refers to people collectively, as in a crowd or a population
  • 人(ひと) vs. 方(かた): 方(かた) is the polite word for a person; use it when speaking about customers, guests, or anyone deserving respect (あの方(かた), not あの人(ひと), in formal speech)
  • 人(ひと) vs. 者(もの): 者(もの) is formal or written, and is also the humble word for oneself and one's own group

IDIOMS:

  • 人(ひと)それぞれ: different for each person
  • 十人十色(じゅうにんといろ): different strokes for different folks

Related Words
