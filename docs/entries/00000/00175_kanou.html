可能(かのう)

かのう
noun, na-adjective
possible, feasible
1. possible, feasible, practicable
Capable of being done or achieved.
それは可能(かのう)ですか。
Is that possible?
実現(じつげん)可能(かのう)な計画(けいかく)。
A feasible plan.
可能(かのう)な限(かぎ)り努力(どりょく)します。
I'll try as much as possible.
不可能(ふかのう)を可能(かのう)にする。
To make the impossible possible.
配達(はいたつ)可能(かのう)な地域(ちいき)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check areas where delivery is possible.

可能(かのう) is a noun and na-adjective meaning 'possible' or 'feasible.'

FORMS:

  • 可能(かのう)な + noun: possible ~
  • 可能(かのう)だ/です: is possible
  • 可能(かのう)に + verb: make ~ possible

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 可能(かのう)な限(かぎ)り: as much as possible
  • 〜可能(かのう): ~-able, capable of ~
  • 可能(かのう)性(せい): possibility, likelihood

COMPOUNDS:

  • 実現(じつげん)可能(かのう): realizable, achievable
  • 配達(はいたつ)可能(かのう): deliverable
  • 利用(りよう)可能(かのう): available for use

GRAMMAR:
可能(かのう)形(けい) (potential form) is the verb conjugation expressing ability (e.g., 食(た)べる → 食(た)べられる).

VS. できる:
できる is the everyday verb for 'can, is possible' and is used in speech of any register. 可能(かのう) is a noun/na-adjective and belongs to written and formal contexts — notices, forms, business writing, and compounds such as 予約(よやく)可能(かのう) ('reservations accepted'). Where both fit, できる sounds natural in conversation and 可能(かのう) sounds official.

OPPOSITE:
不可能(ふかのう) (impossible)

Related Words

Antonym: 不可能(ふかのう) (impossible)
Antonym: 無理(むり) (impossible, unreasonable)
Related: 可能性(かのうせい) (possibility, likelihood)
Synonym: できる (to be possible, to be able to (verb))
