まで

まで
particle
until, to, as far as
1. until, to (time endpoint)
Marks the ending point in time. 五時(ごじ)まで働(はたら)く (work until 5 o'clock), 明日(あした)まで (until tomorrow).
十二時(じゅうにじ)まで起(お)きていました。
I was up until 12 o'clock.
Time endpoint
金曜日(きんようび)までに提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
Please submit it by Friday.
までに = by (deadline)
2. to, as far as (spatial endpoint)
Marks the ending point in space. 駅(えき)まで歩(ある)く (walk to the station).
東京(とうきょう)から大阪(おおさか)まで新幹線(しんかんせん)で行(い)きます。
I'll take the Shinkansen from Tokyo to Osaka.
から...まで for spatial range
ここまで来(き)てくれてありがとう。
Thank you for coming all the way here.
Spatial endpoint with appreciation
3. even (emphasis)
Emphasizes an extreme or unexpected extent. 彼(かれ)まで来(き)た (even he came), 子供(こども)まで知(し)っている (even children know).
子供(こども)まで分(わ)かる簡単(かんたん)な問題(もんだい)です。
It's such an easy problem that even children understand.
Emphatic 'even'
4. to the extent of
Indicates the degree to which something extends. 泣(な)くまで笑(わら)った (laughed until [I] cried).

まで often pairs with から: 九時(くじ)から五時(ごじ)まで (from 9 to 5). Unlike に (which marks a specific point), まで emphasizes the extent or continuation up to that point. までに means 'by (a deadline)': 金曜日(きんようび)までに (by Friday). As 'even,' まで is stronger than も, implying a surprising extreme.
