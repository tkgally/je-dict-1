夜(よる)

よる
noun
night, evening
1. night, evening
The dark part of the day, from sunset until dawn.
夜(よる)、何(なに)をしますか？
What do you do at night?
毎晩(まいばん)夜(よる)10時(じ)に寝(ね)ます。
I go to bed at 10 o'clock every night.
昨日(きのう)の夜(よる)は忙(いそが)しかったです。
Last night was busy.

夜(よる) is the primary word for night, covering the period from sunset to dawn.

TIME OF DAY SEQUENCE:

  • 朝(あさ): morning
  • 昼(ひる): daytime/noon
  • 夕方(ゆうがた): evening
  • 夜(よる): night

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 夜(よる)ごはん/晩(ばん)ごはん: dinner
  • 今夜(こんや): tonight
  • 昨夜(さくや)/{ゆうべ}: last night
  • 毎晩(まいばん): every night
  • 夜中(よなか): midnight, middle of the night
  • 夜遅(よるおそ)く: late at night

COMPOUNDS:

  • 夜行(やこう)バス: overnight bus
  • 夜景(やけい): night view
  • 夜勤(やきん): night shift

CONTRAST WITH 晩(ばん):
夜(よる) refers to night generally; 晩(ばん) often refers specifically to evening/dinnertime.
