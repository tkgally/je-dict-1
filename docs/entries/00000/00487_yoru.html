夜(よる)

よる
noun
night, evening
1. night, evening
The dark part of the day, from sunset until dawn.
夜(よる)、何(なに)をしますか？
What do you do at night?
毎晩(まいばん)夜(よる)10時(じ)に寝(ね)ます。
I go to bed at 10 o'clock every night.
昨日(きのう)の夜(よる)は忙(いそが)しかったです。
Last night was busy.

A fundamental time word.

Related:

  • 夜(よる)ごはん/晩(ばん)ごはん (dinner)
  • 毎晩(まいばん) (every night)
  • 今夜(こんや) (tonight). Opposite: 朝(あさ) (morning).
