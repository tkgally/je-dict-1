課長(かちょう)

かちょう
noun
section chief

Definitions

1. section chief, section manager
The manager in charge of a section (課) within a department in a company.

Examples

課長(かちょう)に相談(そうだん)してください。
Please consult with the section chief.
田中(たなか)課長(かちょう)は来週(らいしゅう)出張(しゅっちょう)です。
Section Chief Tanaka has a business trip next week.
やっと課長(かちょう)に昇進(しょうしん)しました。
I finally got promoted to section chief.

Notes

課長 manages a 課 (section), which is a subdivision of a 部 (department). It's an important first management position in Japanese companies. The typical hierarchy is 係長 → 課長 → 部長. Used as a title (田中課長).
