潰(つぶ)す

つぶす
godan verb
to crush; to smash; to kill (time); to waste
1. to crush; to smash; to squash
To press or squeeze something until it is flattened or destroyed.
2. to kill (time); to while away
To spend time in an idle or unproductive way.
3. to waste; to ruin; to spoil
To destroy the value or potential of something.
4. to shut down; to close (a business)
To cause a business or organization to fail.
空(あ)き缶(かん)を足(あし)で潰(つぶ)した。
I crushed the empty can with my foot.
電車(でんしゃ)を待(ま)つ間(あいだ)、カフェで時間(じかん)を潰(つぶ)した。
I killed time at a café while waiting for the train.
せっかくのチャンスを潰(つぶ)してしまった。
I wasted a great opportunity.

潰(つぶ)す is transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 潰(つぶ)れる (to be crushed, to collapse).

ASPECT ({ている}):
潰(つぶ)している indicates an ongoing action of crushing, or a progressive state of wasting.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時間(じかん)を潰(つぶ)す (to kill time)
  • 店(みせ)を潰(つぶ)す (to shut down a shop)
  • 顔(かお)を潰(つぶ)す (to make someone lose face)
  • 声(こえ)を潰(つぶ)す (to lose one's voice from overuse)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を潰(つぶ)す: The object being crushed/wasted
  • ～で潰(つぶ)す: The means of crushing

This verb is very versatile and appears in many idiomatic expressions.

Related Words
