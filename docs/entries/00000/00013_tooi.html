遠(とお)い

とおい
adjective (i-adjective)
far, distant

Definitions

1. far, distant
Located at a great distance. Used for physical distance or metaphorical distance (like distant memories).

Examples

駅(えき)から遠(とお)いですか？
Is it far from the station?
彼(かれ)の家(いえ)は遠(とお)くにあります。
His house is far away.
遠く = far away (adverb/noun)
それは遠(とお)い昔(むかし)のことです。
That was a long time ago (in the distant past).
Metaphorical: distant in time

Notes

An i-adjective. The adverb form is 遠(とお)く. Opposite of 近(ちか)い (chikai, near/close). Often used with から to indicate distance from something.
