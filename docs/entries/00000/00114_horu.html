彫(ほ)る

ほる
godan verb
to carve, to engrave

Definitions

1. to carve, to sculpt
To cut a shape or design into a hard material like wood or stone.
2. to engrave
To cut letters or designs into a surface.
3. to tattoo
To create permanent designs on skin.

Examples

木(き)で仏像(ぶつぞう)を彫(ほ)る。
I carve a Buddha statue from wood.
名前(なまえ)を石(いし)に彫(ほ)る。
I engrave a name in stone.
腕(うで)に刺青(いれずみ)を彫(ほ)る。
I get a tattoo on my arm.

Notes

彫(ほ)る means to carve or engrave into a hard surface.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 像(ぞう)を彫(ほ)る

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 彫刻(ちょうこく): sculpture, carving
  • 木彫(きぼ)り: woodcarving
  • 彫(ほ)り物(もの): carving, engraving
  • 彫(ほ)りが深(ふか)い: having well-defined features (of a face)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 掘(ほ)る (to dig) has the same reading but different meaning and kanji.
  • 彫(ほ)る: to carve INTO a surface
  • 掘(ほ)る: to dig OUT from the ground

Related Words

Related: 掘(ほ)る (to dig (homophone))
