規律(きりつ)

きりつ
noun
discipline; rules; order
1. discipline; rules; regulations; order
A set of rules governing conduct, or the state of controlled behavior according to such rules. Also refers to self-discipline.
軍隊(ぐんたい)では厳(きび)しい規律(きりつ)が求(もと)められる。
Strict discipline is required in the military.
規律(きりつ)正(ただ)しい生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)る。
Lead a disciplined life.
組織(そしき)の規律(きりつ)が乱(みだ)れている。
The organization's discipline is falling apart.

PATTERNS:

  • 規律(きりつ)正(ただ)しい (disciplined, orderly)
  • 規律(きりつ)を守(まも)る (to observe discipline)
  • 規律(きりつ)が乱(みだ)れる (discipline breaks down)
  • 規律(きりつ)を維持(いじ)する (to maintain discipline)

CONTEXTS:

  • 軍(ぐん)規律(きりつ) (military discipline)
  • 自己(じこ)規律(きりつ) (self-discipline)
  • 社会(しゃかい)規律(きりつ) (social order)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 規則(きそく) (rules, regulations - more specific)
  • 秩序(ちつじょ) (order - social/political)
  • 節度(せつど) (moderation, self-restraint)
  • ルール (rule - loanword, general)
