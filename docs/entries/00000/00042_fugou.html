符号(ふごう)

ふごう
noun
symbol, sign, code, mark
1. symbol, sign, mark
A written or printed mark used to represent something, such as punctuation marks or mathematical symbols.
数学(すうがく)では様々(さまざま)な符号(ふごう)を使(つか)う。
Various symbols are used in mathematics.
句読点(くとうてん)も符号(ふごう)の一種(いっしゅ)です。
Punctuation marks are also a type of symbol.
正(せい)の符号(ふごう)と負(ふ)の符号(ふごう)を区別(くべつ)してください。
Please distinguish between positive and negative signs.
2. code, cipher
A system of symbols used to represent information, especially in computing or cryptography.

符号(ふごう) refers to various types of symbols used in writing, mathematics, and computing.

Common contexts:

  • 数学(すうがく)符号(ふごう) (mathematical symbols: +, -, ×, ÷)
  • 句読(くとう)符号(ふごう) (punctuation marks)
  • 正(せい)/負(ふ)の符号(ふごう) (positive/negative signs in math)

Related terms:

  • 記号(きごう) (symbol, mark - more general term)
  • マーク (mark - loanword)
  • 暗号(あんごう) (cipher, code - for encryption)
