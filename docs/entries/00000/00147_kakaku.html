価格(かかく)

かかく
noun
price, cost

Definitions

1. price, cost, value
The monetary value of goods or services, often in formal or business contexts.

Examples

価格(かかく)が上(あ)がった。
The price went up.
価格(かかく)を比較(ひかく)する。
To compare prices.
この価格(かかく)では買(か)えない。
I can't buy it at this price.

Notes

価格(かかく) is more formal and often used in business contexts.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 値段(ねだん) (price - more casual)
  • 料金(りょうきん) (fee, charge - for services)
  • 定価(ていか) (list price, fixed price)
