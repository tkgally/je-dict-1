基本(きほん)

きほん
noun
basis, foundation

Definitions

1. basis, foundation, basics
The fundamental or essential part of something.

Examples

基本(きほん)を学(まな)ぶ。
To learn the basics.
基本(きほん)的(てき)な知識(ちしき)が必要(ひつよう)です。
Basic knowledge is necessary.
料理(りょうり)の基本(きほん)を教(おし)える。
To teach the basics of cooking.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 基本(きほん)を学(まな)ぶ (to learn the basics)
  • 基本(きほん)的(てき)な〜 (basic ~)
  • 基本(きほん)から (from the basics)

Related: 基礎(きそ) (foundation - often interchangeable)
