敬(うやま)う

うやまう
godan verb
to respect; to honor; to revere

Definitions

1. to respect; to honor
To hold someone in high esteem and show deference.
2. to revere; to venerate
To regard with deep respect, often with religious or spiritual connotation.

Examples

年上(としうえ)の人(ひと)を敬(うやま)う。
To respect one's elders.
先生(せんせい)を敬(うやま)う気持(きも)ちを忘(わす)れないでください。
Please don't forget your feelings of respect for your teacher.
彼(かれ)は神(かみ)を敬(うやま)い、毎日(まいにち)祈(いの)っている。
He reveres God and prays every day.

Notes

敬(うやま)う is transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). It takes を for the person being respected.

ASPECT ({ている}):
敬(うやま)っている indicates an ongoing state of respecting someone.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 年上(としうえ)を敬(うやま)う (to respect elders)
  • 先生(せんせい)を敬(うやま)う (to respect one's teacher)
  • 神(かみ)を敬(うやま)う (to revere God)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を敬(うやま)う: The person being respected

RELATED WORDS:

  • 尊敬(そんけい)する: To respect (more common in everyday speech)
  • 敬意(けいい): Respect (noun)
  • 敬語(けいご): Honorific language

Register: Somewhat formal/literary. 尊敬(そんけい)する is more commonly used in everyday conversation. 敬(うやま)う has a more traditional, reverent nuance.

Related Words

Synonym: 尊敬(そんけい) (respect (する verb))
