習(なら)う

ならう
verb (godan)
to learn, to take lessons

Definitions

1. to learn, to take lessons
To acquire knowledge or skills from a teacher or through study. Emphasizes learning from someone.

Examples

日本語(にほんご)を習(なら)っています。
I am learning Japanese.
ピアノを習(なら)いたいです。
I want to learn piano.
先生(せんせい)に習(なら)いました。
I learned from my teacher.
に marks the teacher

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを習(なら)う (learn X) / Xに習(なら)う (learn from X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 習(なら)っている = 'is learning/taking lessons.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 日本語(にほんご)を習(なら)う (learn Japanese)
  • ピアノを習(なら)う (learn piano)
  • 先生(せんせい)に習(なら)う (learn from teacher)
  • 習(なら)い事(ごと) (lessons/extracurricular activities)

Counterpart: 教(おし)える (to teach). Emphasizes learning from someone.
