凹(へこ)む

へこむ
godan verb
to be dented; to be indented; to feel down
1. to be dented; to be indented
To have a surface pushed inward, creating a depression.
車(くるま)のドアが凹(へこ)んでしまった。
The car door got dented.
地面(じめん)が少(すこ)し凹(へこ)んでいる。
The ground is slightly indented.
缶(かん)が凹(へこ)んだ。
The can got dented.
Sense 1: be dented
ボールが当(あ)たって壁(かべ)が凹(へこ)んだ。
The wall got dented where the ball hit it.
Sense 1: be dented
マットレスが凹(へこ)んでいる。
The mattress is indented.
Sense 1: be indented
2. to feel down; to be dejected
To feel discouraged or depressed (colloquial).
試験(しけん)に落(お)ちて凹(へこ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because I failed the exam.
失敗(しっぱい)して凹(へこ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because of my failure.
Sense 2: feel down
怒(おこ)られて凹(へこ)んだ。
I got scolded and felt dejected.
Sense 2: feel down
そんなに凹(へこ)まないで。
Don't be so down about it.
Sense 2: feel down
批判(ひはん)されても凹(へこ)まない。
I won't be discouraged even if criticized.
Sense 2: feel down / Sense 3: give in
振(ふ)られて凹(へこ)んでいる。
I'm feeling down because I got rejected.
Sense 2: feel dejected
3. to be defeated; to give in
To lose or yield to someone.
彼(かれ)に言(い)い負(ま)かされて凹(へこ)んだ。
I gave in after being out-argued by him.
Sense 3: be defeated
議論(ぎろん)で凹(へこ)まされた。
I was beaten in the argument.
Sense 3: be defeated
相手(あいて)に凹(へこ)まずに戦(たたか)った。
I fought without yielding to my opponent.
Sense 3: give in
彼(かれ)には何(なに)を言(い)っても凹(へこ)まない。
No matter what you say, he won't back down.
Sense 3: give in

凹(へこ)む is intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive pair is 凹(へこ)ます (to dent, to make someone feel down).

ASPECT ({ている}):
凹(へこ)んでいる indicates a resulting state of being dented, or an ongoing state of feeling down.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ドアが凹(へこ)む (door gets dented)
  • 凹(へこ)んでいる (to be dented; to be feeling down)
  • ちょっと凹(へこ)んだ (I'm a bit down)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～が凹(へこ)む: What is dented

COLLOQUIAL USAGE:
In casual speech, 凹(へこ)む is commonly used to describe feeling disappointed or dejected. This usage is very common among younger speakers.

The kanji 凹(おう) visually represents a depression or dent, matching the meaning.

Related Words

Pair verb: 凹(へこ)ます (transitive)
Antonym: 出(で)っ張(ぱ)る (to protrude)
