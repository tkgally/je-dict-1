何(なん)でも

なんでも
pronoun; adverb
anything; everything
1. anything; everything
Refers to any thing without restriction; whatever one wants.
何(なん)でも食(た)べる。
I eat anything.
何(なん)でもいいよ。
Anything is fine.
何(なん)でも知(し)っている。
I know everything.
困(こま)ったことがあれば何(なん)でも聞(き)いてね。
If you have any problems, ask me anything.
私(わたし)の兄(あに)は本(ほん)が好(す)きで、何(なん)でも読(よ)む。歴史(れきし)も小説(しょうせつ)も読(よ)む。
My brother likes books and reads anything. He reads history and novels.
2. I hear that; they say that
Used to introduce hearsay or reported information.
何(なん)でも、雨(あめ)らしい。
I hear it's going to rain.
何(なん)でも、彼(かれ)は転職(てんしょく)するらしい。
I hear he's changing jobs.
何(なん)でも、来月(らいげつ)新(あたら)しい店(みせ)ができるそうだ。
I hear a new store is opening next month.
何(なん)でも、先生(せんせい)は来週(らいしゅう)休(やす)みだって。
I hear the teacher is off next week.
何(なん)でも、あの二人(ふたり)は高校(こうこう)の時(とき)から友達(ともだち)らしい。今(いま)も仲(なか)がいいそうだ。
I hear those two have been friends since high school. Apparently they're still close now.

何(なん)でも combines 何(なに) with the particle でも, giving "whatever it may be". It has two quite different uses.

ANYTHING / EVERYTHING (sense 1):

  • 何(なん)でも食(た)べる (eat anything)
  • 何(なん)でもいい (anything is fine)
  • 何(なん)でも知(し)っている (know everything)

With a negative verb the meaning flips to "nothing", but Japanese uses 何(なに)も for that, not 何(なん)でも.

HEARSAY (sense 2):

Sentence-initial, and normally followed by らしい or そうだ at the end:

  • 何(なん)でも〜らしい / そうだ (I hear that ~)

It marks the whole sentence as secondhand and slightly vague - the speaker is passing on what they heard without vouching for it. A comma after 何(なん)でも is usual in writing.

CAUTION:
何(なん)でも on its own does NOT mean "by all means" or "at any cost". That is the fixed expression 何(なに)が何(なん)でも, which needs the full four-element form: 何(なに)が何(なん)でも勝(か)ちたい (I want to win no matter what), not ×何(なん)でも勝(か)ちたい. どうしても is a close everyday equivalent.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 何(なに)も (nothing - with a negative)
  • 何(なに)か (something)
  • 何(なん)でもかんでも (anything and everything, indiscriminately - emphatic, often disapproving)
  • 何(なん)でもない (it's nothing, no big deal)
  • 誰(だれ)でも (anyone), いつでも (anytime), どこでも (anywhere) - the same 〜でも pattern on other question words
