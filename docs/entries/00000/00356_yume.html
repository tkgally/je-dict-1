夢(ゆめ)

ゆめ
noun
dream

Definitions

1. dream (during sleep)
A series of images and sensations during sleep.
2. dream, aspiration
A hope or ambition for the future.

Examples

昨夜(ゆうべ)変(へん)な夢(ゆめ)を見(み)た。
I had a strange dream last night.
医者(いしゃ)になるのが夢(ゆめ)です。
My dream is to become a doctor.

Notes

Common expressions: 夢(ゆめ)を見(み)る (to have a dream), 夢(ゆめ)が叶(かな)う (dream comes true).
