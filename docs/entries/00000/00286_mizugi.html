水着(みずぎ)

みずぎ
noun
swimsuit, bathing suit
1. swimsuit, bathing suit, swimwear
Clothing worn for swimming or at the beach/pool.
新(あたら)しい水着(みずぎ)を買(か)った。
I bought a new swimsuit.
プールに行(い)くから、水着(みずぎ)を持(も)ってきてね。
We're going to the pool, so bring your swimsuit.
水着(みずぎ)姿(すがた)でビーチを歩(ある)いた。
I walked on the beach in my swimsuit.

水着(みずぎ) is a general term for swimwear of all types.

Types of swimwear:

  • ビキニ (bikini)
  • ワンピース (one-piece swimsuit)
  • 競泳(きょうえい)水着(みずぎ) (racing swimsuit)
  • 海(うみ)パン (swim trunks - casual term)

Common expressions:

  • 水着(みずぎ)を着(き)る (to wear a swimsuit)
  • 水着(みずぎ)姿(すがた) (in swimwear)

Related terms:

  • 海水浴(かいすいよく) (sea bathing)
  • プール (pool)
