希望(きぼう)

きぼう
noun, suru verb
hope, wish

Definitions

1. hope, wish, aspiration
A desire for something to happen or be true.

Examples

将来(しょうらい)に希望(きぼう)を持(も)つ。
To have hope for the future.
ご希望(きぼう)は何(なん)ですか。
What would you like? / What is your preference?
Polite way to ask preferences
希望(きぼう)どおりの結果(けっか)になった。
The result was as hoped.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 希望(きぼう)を持(も)つ (to have hope)
  • 希望(きぼう)する (to hope for, wish for)
  • ご希望(きぼう) (your preference/wish - polite)
  • 希望(きぼう)どおり (as hoped)

Related: 願(ねが)い (wish), 望(のぞ)み (hope, desire)
