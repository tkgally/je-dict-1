脱(ぬ)ぐ

ぬぐ
verb (godan)
to take off (clothes)

Definitions

1. to take off, to remove (clothing)
To remove clothing or footwear from one's body.

Examples

靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)いでください。
Please take off your shoes.
Common at Japanese entrances
コートを脱(ぬ)ぎましょうか？
Shall I take off my coat?
帽子(ぼうし)を脱(ぬ)いでください。
Please take off your hat.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xを脱(ぬ)ぐ (take off X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 脱(ぬ)いでいる = 'has taken off' (is now without).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 靴(くつ)を脱(ぬ)ぐ (take off shoes)
  • 服(ふく)を脱(ぬ)ぐ (take off clothes)
  • 帽子(ぼうし)を脱(ぬ)ぐ (take off hat)
  • 脱(ぬ)いでください (please take off)

Used for all clothing types. Opposite verbs: 着(き)る (upper body), 履(は)く (legs/feet), かぶる (head).
