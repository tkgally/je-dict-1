1. expectation, anticipation, hope
A belief that something will happen or be the case.
期待(きたい)しています。
I'm looking forward to it. / I have high hopes.
期待(きたい)に応(こた)える。
To meet expectations.
期待(きたい)はずれだった。
It didn't meet expectations.

期待(きたい) means "expectation" or "hope"—anticipating something positive will happen. Can function as a noun or する verb.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • No standard intransitive pair
  • Pattern: [noun]を期待(きたい)する

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を期待(きたい)する: to expect ~, to hope for ~
  • 期待(きたい)に応(こた)える: to meet/live up to expectations
  • 期待(きたい)を裏切(うらぎ)る: to betray expectations
  • 期待(きたい)はずれ: disappointment, letdown
  • 期待(きたい)が高(たか)まる: expectations rise

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 期待(きたい)しています: I'm looking forward to it
  • ご期待(きたい)ください: Please look forward to it (promotional)
  • 期待(きたい)どおり: as expected

USAGE NOTE: 期待(きたい) usually implies positive anticipation, unlike 予想(よそう) which is more neutral.

ASPECT (ている):

  • [X]を期待(きたい)している: is looking forward to / hopes for X (stative — describes an ongoing feeling of anticipation)
  • 期待(きたい)していない: does not expect, has no hopes for
  • Note: 期待(きたい)する describes a mental/emotional state of anticipation. The ている form is the default way to describe the ongoing hope. 期待(きたい)しています (polite) is a set expression meaning "I'm looking forward to it." The plain 期待(きたい)する is used more in written or formal contexts.

Related Words

Antonym: 失望(しつぼう) (disappointment)
Related: 待(ま)つ (to wait, to await)
