バナナ

ばなな
noun
banana

Definitions

1. banana
A yellow tropical fruit with soft sweet flesh. From English 'banana.'

Examples

朝(あさ)ごはんにバナナを食(た)べます。
I eat a banana for breakfast.
バナナを三本(さんぼん)買(か)いました。
I bought three bananas.
Bananas use the counter 本
このバナナはまだ青(あお)いです。
This banana is still green (unripe).

Notes

バナナ is from English. In Japan, bananas are a popular fruit and often eaten for breakfast. The counter 本(ほん) is used for bananas because of their long shape.
