剥(は)がす

はがす
godan verb
to tear off; to peel off; to strip
1. to tear off; to peel off
To remove something that is attached or stuck to a surface.
2. to strip; to remove
To take away a covering or outer layer.
シールを剥(は)がす。
To peel off a sticker.
壁(かべ)からポスターを剥(は)がした。
I tore the poster off the wall.
傷(きず)のかさぶたを剥(は)がさないでください。
Please don't pick at the scab on your wound.

剥(は)がす is transitive (他動詞(たどうし)). The intransitive pair is 剥(は)がれる (to peel off, come off).

ASPECT ({ている}):
剥(は)がしている indicates the action of peeling is in progress.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • シールを剥(は)がす (to peel off a sticker)
  • 皮(かわ)を剥(は)がす (to peel skin/bark)
  • 仮面(かめん)を剥(は)がす (to unmask - figurative)
  • 化(ば)けの皮(かわ)を剥(は)がす (to expose someone's true nature)

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を剥(は)がす: What is being peeled off
  • ～から剥(は)がす: From what surface

Also written as 剥(む)く for peeling fruit/vegetables, but 剥(は)がす is specifically for removing attached items.

Related Words

Pair verb: 剥(は)がれる (intransitive)
Related: 剥(む)く (to peel (fruit))
