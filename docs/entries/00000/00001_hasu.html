斜(はす)

はす
noun, na-adjective
diagonal; oblique; slanting

Definitions

1. diagonal; oblique; slanting
At an angle that is neither perpendicular nor horizontal. Often used to describe direction, position, or angle of something.

Examples

道(みち)を斜(はす)に渡(わた)った。
I crossed the road diagonally.
郵便局(ゆうびんきょく)は駅(えき)の斜(はす)向(む)かいにある。
The post office is diagonally across from the station.
[Note: 斜向かい is a common compound meaning 'diagonally opposite']
斜(はす)に構(かま)えた態度(たいど)をやめてほしい。
I want you to stop that cynical attitude.
[Note: 斜に構える is an idiom meaning 'to be cynical/standoffish']

Notes

WRITING: Also written as 斜(なな)め (more common in modern usage). The reading はす is somewhat old-fashioned but still used in set phrases.

PATTERNS:

  • 斜(はす)に構(かま)える (to be cynical, to take a standoffish attitude)
  • 斜(はす)向(む)かい (diagonally across)
  • 斜(はす)に切(き)る (to cut diagonally)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 斜(なな)め (diagonal - more common reading)
  • 対角線(たいかくせん) (diagonal line - geometric term)
