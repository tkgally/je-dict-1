辛(から)い

からい
adjective (i-adjective)
spicy, salty

Definitions

1. spicy, hot (taste)
Describes food that is spicy or hot (chili-hot, not temperature-hot).
2. salty
Can also mean salty, though しょっぱい (shoppai) is more common for 'salty' in eastern Japan.

Examples

この料理(りょうり)は辛(から)いです。
This dish is spicy.
辛(から)いものが食(た)べられますか。
Can you eat spicy food?
ちょっと辛(から)すぎます。
It's a bit too spicy.

Notes

辛い can mean both 'spicy' and 'salty,' though 'spicy' is the more common meaning. Don't confuse with 辛い (つらい), which means 'painful/hard'—same kanji, different reading and meaning. Conjugation: 辛くない (negative), 辛かった (past).
