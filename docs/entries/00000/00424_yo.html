世(よ)

よ
noun
world, society, age, era
1. world, society
The world in the sense of human society and life.
世(よ)の中(なか)にはいろいろな人(ひと)がいる。
There are all kinds of people in the world.
2. age, era, generation
A period of time or reign; a generation.
平成(へいせい)の世(よ)が終(お)わり、令和(れいわ)になった。
The Heisei era ended and Reiwa began.
親子(おやこ)三(さん)世(せい)で暮(く)らしている。
Three generations of the family live together.
[Note: 世(せい) is used when counting generations]

世(よ) refers to the world in terms of society, life, or a period of time. It has a somewhat literary or philosophical nuance.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 世(よ)の中(なか): the world, society
  • 世(よ)に出(で)る: to go out into the world; to be published
  • 世(よ)を去(さ)る: to pass away (literary)
  • 世(よ)も末(すえ): the world has gone to ruin

RELATED READINGS:

  • 世界(せかい): world (larger/global scope)
  • 世紀(せいき): century
  • 世代(せだい): generation
  • ～世(せい): ~th generation (e.g., 3世(せい) = third generation)
