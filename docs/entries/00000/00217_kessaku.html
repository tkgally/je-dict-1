傑作(けっさく)

けっさく
noun
masterpiece, great work

Definitions

1. masterpiece, great work
An outstanding work of art, literature, or craftsmanship.
2. something hilarious, a gem
Colloquially, something amusingly absurd or ridiculously funny.

Examples

この絵(え)は彼(かれ)の傑作(けっさく)だ。
This painting is his masterpiece.
世界(せかい)の傑作(けっさく)を集(あつ)めた美術館(びじゅつかん)だ。
It's a museum that has collected masterpieces from around the world.
彼(かれ)の言(い)い訳(わけ)は傑作(けっさく)だった。
His excuse was hilarious.
Colloquial/ironic usage

Notes

傑作(けっさく) has both a serious and colloquial meaning.

Serious usage:

  • 傑作(けっさく)を生(う)み出(だ)す (to create a masterpiece)
  • 傑作(けっさく)選(せん) (collection of great works)
  • 文学(ぶんがく)の傑作(けっさく) (literary masterpiece)

Colloquial/ironic usage:

  • 「あれは傑作(けっさく)だね」 (That's hilarious/ridiculous)
  • Often used sarcastically for absurd situations or excuses

Related terms:

  • 名作(めいさく) (masterpiece - similar but slightly less emphasis on excellence)
  • 代表作(だいひょうさく) (representative work)
  • 駄作(ださく) (poor work - opposite)
