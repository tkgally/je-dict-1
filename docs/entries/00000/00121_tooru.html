通(とお)る

とおる
verb (godan)
to pass through, to go through

Definitions

1. to pass through, to go through
To move through a place or along a route.
2. to pass (an exam, inspection)
To successfully get through a test, inspection, or approval process.
3. to be understood, to get through
For a message or intention to be communicated successfully.

Examples

この道(みち)を通(とお)ってください。
Please go through this road.
審査(しんさ)に通(とお)った。
I passed the screening.
'Pass' meaning
声(こえ)が通(とお)る。
The voice carries well.
Figurative usage

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 通(とお)す (transitive, to let through). Pattern: Xを通(とお)る (pass through X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing or completed action. 通(とお)っている = 'is passing' or 'has passed.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)を通(とお)る (go along a road)
  • 門(もん)を通(とお)る (pass through a gate)
  • 試験(しけん)に通(とお)る (pass an exam)
  • 声(こえ)が通(とお)る (voice carries well)
  • 話(はなし)が通(とお)る (message gets through)
  • 通(とお)り (street—noun; also: way, manner)

CONTRAST:

  • 通(とお)る (intransitive): pass through
  • 通(とお)す (transitive): let through, pass (something)

RELATED:

  • 通(かよ)う: to commute (repeated passing)

Related Words
