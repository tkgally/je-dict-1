響(ひび)き

ひびき
noun
sound; echo; reverberation; ring

Definitions

1. sound; echo; reverberation
A sound that resonates or echoes.
2. ring; tone; nuance
The quality or impression of a sound or word.

Examples

鐘(かね)の響(ひび)きが遠(とお)くまで聞(き)こえた。
The sound of the bell could be heard from far away.
この言葉(ことば)には優(やさ)しい響(ひび)きがある。
This word has a gentle ring to it.
ピアノの響(ひび)きが美(うつく)しい。
The sound of the piano is beautiful.

Notes

響(ひび)き is the noun form of the verb 響(ひび)く (to resound), referring to sounds, echoes, and the impression they create.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～の響(ひび)き (the sound/ring of ~)
  • 響(ひび)きがある (to have a ring/resonance)
  • 響(ひび)きが良(よ)い (to sound good)

USAGE FOR WORDS:

響(ひび)き can describe how a word or name "sounds" or the impression it gives. For example:

  • この名前(なまえ)は響(ひび)きがいい (This name sounds nice)
  • 言葉(ことば)の響(ひび)き (the ring of words)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 響(ひび)き vs. 音(おと): 音(おと) is a general word for sound; 響(ひび)き specifically refers to resonating or echoing sounds, or the quality/impression of sound.

Register: Neutral to slightly literary. Often used when describing the aesthetic quality of sounds.
