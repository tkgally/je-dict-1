恥(は)ずかしい

はずかしい
adjective (i-adjective)
embarrassed, shy

Definitions

1. embarrassed, ashamed
Feeling self-conscious or uncomfortable due to a situation. Can describe both the feeling of embarrassment and situations that cause it.
2. shy
Feeling reluctant to do something in front of others due to self-consciousness.

Examples

間違(まちが)えて恥(は)ずかしかったです。
I made a mistake and was embarrassed.
人前(ひとまえ)で話(はな)すのは恥(は)ずかしいです。
Speaking in front of people is embarrassing.
恥(は)ずかしいから言(い)えません。
I can't say it because I'm embarrassed.

Notes

Like many Japanese adjectives describing feelings, 恥(は)ずかしい can describe both the person feeling embarrassed and the situation causing embarrassment. The noun form is 恥(は)ずかしさ (embarrassment).
