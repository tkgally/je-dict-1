ひげ

ひげ
noun
beard; mustache; whiskers

Definitions

1. beard; mustache
Facial hair on a person.
2. whiskers
The facial hair of animals, especially cats.

Examples

ひげを剃(そ)る。
To shave one's beard.
彼(かれ)はひげを生(は)やしている。
He has a beard.
ひげを生やす = to grow a beard
猫(ねこ)のひげは長(なが)い。
Cat whiskers are long.

Notes

ひげ can refer to any facial hair. More specific terms exist for different types.

SPECIFIC TYPES:

  • 口髭(くちひげ): mustache
  • 顎髭(あごひげ): beard (chin)
  • 頬髯(ほおひげ): sideburns

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ひげを剃(そ)る: to shave
  • ひげを生(は)やす: to grow a beard
  • ひげが濃(こ)い: to have thick facial hair
