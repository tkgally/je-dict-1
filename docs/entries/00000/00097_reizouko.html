冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)

れいぞうこ
noun
refrigerator

Definitions

1. refrigerator, fridge
An appliance for keeping food cold and fresh.

Examples

牛乳(ぎゅうにゅう)を冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に入(い)れてください。
Please put the milk in the refrigerator.
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に何(なに)がありますか。
What's in the refrigerator?
新(あたら)しい冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)を買(か)いました。
I bought a new refrigerator.

Notes

冷蔵庫 combines 冷 (cold), 蔵 (storage), and 庫 (warehouse). The freezer section is called 冷凍庫 (れいとうこ). In casual speech, 冷蔵庫 is sometimes abbreviated to 冷蔵 or just called by brand names.
