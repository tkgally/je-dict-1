記念(きねん)

きねん
noun, suru verb
commemoration, memory
1. commemoration, memory, memorial
The act of remembering or honoring a person, event, or date.
記念(きねん)写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)る。
To take a commemorative photo.
結婚(けっこん)記念(きねん)日(び)を祝(いわ)う。
To celebrate a wedding anniversary.
卒業(そつぎょう)記念(きねん)にもらった。
I received it as a graduation memento.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 記念(きねん)日(び) (anniversary, memorial day)
  • 記念(きねん)品(ひん) (souvenir, memento)
  • 記念(きねん)写真(しゃしん) (commemorative photo)
  • 記念(きねん)切手(きって) (commemorative stamp)
general
