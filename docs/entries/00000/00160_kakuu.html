架空(かくう)

かくう
noun, na-adjective
imaginary; fictional; fictitious
1. imaginary; fictional; fictitious
Something that does not exist in reality; invented or made up. Used for stories, characters, scenarios, etc.
この小説(しょうせつ)は架空(かくう)の都市(とし)を舞台(ぶたい)にしている。
This novel is set in a fictional city.
架空(かくう)の人物(じんぶつ)を使(つか)った詐欺(さぎ)が増(ふ)えている。
Fraud using fictitious persons is increasing.
[Note: 架空請求 (fictitious billing) is a common scam]
2. overhead; aerial (technical)
Installed or suspended in the air. Used for cables, power lines, etc.
架空(かくう)送電線(そうでんせん)の点検(てんけん)を行(おこな)う。
We will inspect the overhead power lines.
[Note: Technical usage meaning 'aerial/overhead']

USAGE:

Sense 1 (fictional):

  • 架空(かくう)の物語(ものがたり) (fictional story)
  • 架空(かくう)の人物(じんぶつ) (fictional character)
  • 架空(かくう)請求(せいきゅう) (fictitious billing - a scam)
  • 架空(かくう)口座(こうざ) (fictitious account)

Sense 2 (overhead/aerial):

  • 架空(かくう)電線(でんせん) (overhead electric cables)
  • 架空(かくう)送電線(そうでんせん) (overhead transmission lines)
  • 架空(かくう){ケーブル} (aerial cable)

SIMILAR WORDS (fictional sense):

  • 空想(くうそう) (fantasy, imagination)
  • 虚構(きょこう) (fiction - literary term)
  • フィクション (fiction)
  • 創作(そうさく) (creative work, fiction)
