脈(みゃく)

みゃく
noun
pulse; vein

Definitions

1. pulse
The rhythmic throbbing of arteries as blood is pumped through them by the heart.
2. vein; line; streak
A continuous line or pattern, such as veins in leaves or geological formations.

Examples

脈(みゃく)を測(はか)る。
To take someone's pulse.
脈(みゃく)が速(はや)い。
The pulse is fast.
山脈(さんみゃく)が続(つづ)いている。
The mountain range continues.

Notes

脈(みゃく) has both medical and figurative uses.

MEDICAL USES:

  • 脈拍(みゃくはく): pulse rate
  • 動脈(どうみゃく): artery
  • 静脈(じょうみゃく): vein

GEOGRAPHIC/FIGURATIVE:

  • 山脈(さんみゃく): mountain range
  • 鉱脈(こうみゃく): ore vein
  • 文脈(ぶんみゃく): context
