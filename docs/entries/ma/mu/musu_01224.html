蒸(む)す

むす
godan verb
to steam, to be humid

Definitions

1. to steam
To cook food using steam.
2. to be humid, to be sultry
Describing hot and humid weather.

Examples

野菜(やさい)を蒸す。
To steam vegetables.
今日(きょう)は蒸す。
It's humid today.

Notes

蒸す can be both transitive (to steam food) and intransitive (to be humid).

RELATED:

  • 蒸(む)し暑(あつ)い: hot and humid
  • 蒸(む)し器(き): steamer (cooking tool)
  • 蒸(む)し焼(や)き: steaming and grilling
