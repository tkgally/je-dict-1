向(む)こう

むこう
noun
over there, opposite side

Definitions

1. over there, the other side
A place that is across from or on the opposite side from where the speaker is.
2. the opposite party, they
The other side in a negotiation or relationship.

Examples

向(む)こうに山(やま)が見(み)えます。
You can see mountains over there.
川(かわ)の向(む)こうに村(むら)があります。
There's a village on the other side of the river.
向(む)こうから連絡(れんらく)がありました。
We received contact from them (the other party).
Using 'other party' meaning

Notes

向(む)こう indicates a place across from or opposite the speaker's position. It implies there's something (like a road, river, or distance) between the speaker and that place. [noun]の向(む)こう means 'on the other side of [noun].' It can also refer to 'them' or 'the other party' in business or relationship contexts.
