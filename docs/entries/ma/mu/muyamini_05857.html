無闇(むやみ)に

むやみに
adverb
recklessly; excessively; thoughtlessly

Definitions

1. recklessly; thoughtlessly
Acting without proper consideration or planning; carelessly.
2. excessively; unreasonably
To an excessive or disproportionate degree.

Examples

無闇(むやみ)に信(しん)じてはいけない。
You shouldn't believe it blindly.
無闘(むやみ)に触(さわ)らないでください。
Please don't touch things carelessly.
無闇(むやみ)に約束(やくそく)してはいけない。
You shouldn't make promises recklessly.

Notes

ETYMOLOGY:
無(む) (without) + 闇(やみ) (darkness) → acting blindly, without seeing

VARIATIONS:

  • 無闇(むやみ): attributive form
  • 無闘(むやみ)やたらに: emphatically reckless

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無闇(むやみ)に手(て)を出(だ)す: to meddle carelessly
  • 無闘(むやみ)に信(しん)じる: to believe blindly
  • 無闇(むやみ)に動(うご)く: to move around recklessly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • やたらに: randomly, excessively
  • 軽率(けいそつ)に: rashly, thoughtlessly
  • みだりに: indiscriminately

Related Words

Synonym: やたらに (randomly)
