胸(むね)

むね
noun
chest, breast

Definitions

1. chest, breast
The front part of the body between the neck and stomach.
2. heart, feelings
Used figuratively to refer to one's heart or emotions.

Examples

胸(むね)が痛(いた)いです。
My chest hurts.
Physical pain
胸(むね)がいっぱいになりました。
My heart is full (with emotion).
Emotional meaning
胸(むね)に手(て)を当(あ)ててよく考(かんが)えてください。
Put your hand on your chest and think carefully.
Idiomatic expression

Notes

胸(むね) can refer to both the physical chest area and figuratively to one's heart or emotions. Many idioms use 胸(むね): 胸(むね)が痛(いた)む (feel heartache), 胸(むね)がいっぱい (heart is full), 胸(むね)を張(は)る (to be proud, literally 'puff out one's chest').
