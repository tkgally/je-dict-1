向こう(むこう)

むこう
noun
over there, the other side

Definitions

1. over there, the other side
A place at a distance or on the opposite side.
2. the other party
The person or people on the other side of an interaction.

Examples

向こう(むこう)に山(やま)が見(み)える。
You can see a mountain over there.
向こう(むこう)から連絡(れんらく)があった。
They contacted us.

Notes

Can refer to physical locations or the other party in communications.
