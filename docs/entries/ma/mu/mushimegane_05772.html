虫眼鏡(むしめがね)

むしめがね
noun
magnifying glass

Definitions

1. magnifying glass
A convex lens used to magnify small objects for easier viewing.

Examples

虫眼鏡(むしめがね)で小(ちい)さい字(じ)を読(よ)む。
To read small letters with a magnifying glass.
虫眼鏡(むしめがね)で昆虫(こんちゅう)を観察(かんさつ)する。
To observe insects with a magnifying glass.
探偵(たんてい)が虫眼鏡(むしめがね)で証拠(しょうこ)を調(しら)べる。
The detective examines evidence with a magnifying glass.

Notes

虫眼鏡(むしめがね) literally means "insect-viewing glasses," reflecting its original use for observing small insects.

ALTERNATE TERMS:

  • ルーペ: loupe (from German)
  • 拡大鏡(かくだいきょう): magnifier (formal)
