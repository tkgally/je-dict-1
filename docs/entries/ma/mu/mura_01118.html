村(むら)

むら
noun
village

Definitions

1. village
A small community in a rural area.

Examples

村(むら)に住(す)んでいる。
I live in a village.
この村(むら)は静(しず)かだ。
This village is quiet.

Notes

村(むら) means village.

ADMINISTRATIVE:
村(むら/そん) is the smallest unit of local government in Japan.

COMPARE:

  • 村(むら): village
  • 町(まち): town
  • 市(し): city

COMPOUNDS:

  • 村人(むらびと): villager
  • 村長(そんちょう): village chief
