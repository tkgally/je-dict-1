無駄(むだ)

むだ
na-adjective
useless, wasteful

Definitions

1. useless, wasteful, futile
Something that serves no purpose or achieves nothing.
2. waste
As a noun, something that is wasted or unnecessary.

Examples

時間(じかん)を無駄(むだ)にしないで。
Don't waste time.
それは無駄(むだ)だと思(おも)う。
I think that's useless.
無駄(むだ)な努力(どりょく)だった。
It was a wasted effort.

Notes

Functions as a na-adjective and noun.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 無駄(むだ)な + noun (無駄(むだ)な時間(じかん) = wasted time)
  • 無駄(むだ)にする (to waste)
  • 無駄(むだ)になる (to be wasted)
  • 無駄(むだ)遣(づか)い (wasteful spending)
  • 無駄(むだ)話(ばなし) (idle chatter)

無駄(むだ)にする is commonly used with time, money, or effort.
