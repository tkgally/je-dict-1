向(む)く

むく
godan verb
to face, to turn toward

Definitions

1. to face, to turn toward
To be directed or oriented in a particular direction.
2. to be suited to
To be appropriate or fitting for a purpose.

Examples

東(ひがし)を向く。
To face east.
この仕事(しごと)は私(わたし)に向いている。
This job suits me.

Notes

向く is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 向く (intransitive): to face
  • 向(む)ける (transitive): to turn something toward

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 〜に向いている: to be suited to ~
  • 前(まえ)を向く: to face forward
  • 上(うえ)を向く: to look up
