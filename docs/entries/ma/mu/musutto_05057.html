むすっと

むすっと
adverb, verb (suru)
sullenly, grumpily

Definitions

1. sullenly, grumpily
With a sullen or displeased expression; looking unhappy or unfriendly.

Examples

彼(かれ)はむすっとした顔(かお)で座(すわ)っていた。
He sat with a sullen face.
なぜむすっとしているの？
Why are you looking so grumpy?
むすっとした表情(ひょうじょう)で返事(へんじ)をした。
They answered with a sullen expression.

Notes

A mimetic word describing a sullen demeanor. Often used to describe someone who is upset but not expressing it verbally. Related to むっつり (taciturn, sulky).
