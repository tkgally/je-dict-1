求(もと)める

もとめる
ichidan verb
to seek, to request

Definitions

1. to seek, to search for
To look for something desired.
2. to request, to demand
To ask for something formally.
3. to buy, to purchase
To obtain by paying (formal).

Examples

幸(しあわ)せを求める。
To seek happiness.
説明(せつめい)を求める。
To request an explanation.

Notes

求める has formal nuance when meaning 'to buy'.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 助(たす)けを求める: to seek help
  • 許可(きょか)を求める: to request permission
  • 理解(りかい)を求める: to seek understanding
