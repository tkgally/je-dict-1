萌(も)える

もえる
ichidan verb
to sprout, to feel passion

Definitions

1. to sprout, to bud
For plants to begin growing.
2. to feel passion for
Slang for enthusiasm about anime/hobbies.

Examples

草木(くさき)が萌える。
Plants are sprouting.

Notes

萌える has traditional and modern uses.

HOMOPHONE:

  • 燃(も)える: to burn

SLANG:

  • 萌え: passion/obsession (otaku term)
