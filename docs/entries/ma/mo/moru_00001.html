盛(も)る

もる
godan verb
to serve (food), to heap up

Definitions

1. to serve, to dish up
To put food onto a plate or into a bowl for serving.
2. to heap up, to pile
To stack or pile things up.
3. to add poison
To secretly put poison into food or drink.

Examples

ご飯(はん)を茶碗(ちゃわん)に盛(も)る。
I serve rice into a bowl.
サラダを皿(さら)に盛(も)り付(つ)ける。
I arrange the salad on a plate.
土(つち)を盛(も)って山(やま)を作(つく)った。
We piled up soil to make a mound.

Notes

盛(も)る means to heap up or serve food into a container.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: ご飯(はん)を盛(も)る

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ご飯(はん)を盛(も)る: to serve rice
  • 盛(も)り付(つ)け: presentation, arrangement (of food)
  • 大盛(おおも)り: large serving
  • 山盛(やまも)り: heaping portion
  • てんこ盛(も)り: piled high

CAUTION:
毒(どく)を盛(も)る means 'to poison (someone)' - putting poison in food/drink.
