もっと

もっと
adverb
more

Definitions

1. more, further, to a greater degree
Used to express a greater quantity, degree, or extent than the current state.

Examples

もっと食(た)べてください。
Please eat more.
もっと大(おお)きい声(こえ)で話(はな)してください。
Please speak in a louder voice.
もっと勉強(べんきょう)したいです。
I want to study more.

Notes

もっと is used to express wanting or needing more of something. It often modifies adjectives and verbs: もっと早(はや)く (faster), もっと上手(じょうず)に (more skillfully). Compare with まだ (still/yet) and もう (already/yet).
