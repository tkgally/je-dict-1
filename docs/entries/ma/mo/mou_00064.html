もう

もう
adverb
already, anymore

Definitions

1. already
Before the expected time; by now.
2. anymore (with negatives)
No longer; not from this point forward.
3. more, another
An additional amount.

Examples

もう食(た)べましたか？
Have you already eaten?
Sense 1
もう行(い)きません。
I won't go anymore.
Sense 2
もう一(いっ)回(かい)お願(ねが)いします。
One more time, please.
Sense 3

Notes

Contrast with まだ (still, not yet). もう一(ひと)つ = one more.

Related Words

See also: まだ
