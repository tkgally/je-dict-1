持(も)ち運(はこ)び

もちはこび
noun
carrying, portability

Definitions

1. carrying, transporting
The act of carrying or moving something from one place to another.
2. portability
The quality of being easy to carry or transport.

Examples

持(も)ち運(はこ)びに便利(べんり)なサイズです。
It's a convenient size for carrying.
持(も)ち運(はこ)びが簡単(かんたん)だ。
It's easy to carry.
この機械(きかい)は持(も)ち運(はこ)びができる。
This machine is portable.

Notes

COMPOUND: 持(も)つ (to hold) + 運(はこ)ぶ (to carry)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 持(も)ち運(はこ)びに便利(べんり) (convenient for carrying)
  • 持(も)ち運(はこ)びが簡単(かんたん)/楽(らく) (easy to carry)
  • 持(も)ち運(はこ)びできる (portable, can be carried)
  • 持(も)ち運(はこ)び用(よう) (for carrying, portable)

RELATED:

  • ポータブル (portable - loanword)
  • 携帯(けいたい) (portable, carrying)
  • 運(はこ)ぶ (to carry, transport)
