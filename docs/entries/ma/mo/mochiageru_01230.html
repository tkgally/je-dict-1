持(も)ち上(あ)げる

もちあげる
ichidan verb
to lift up, to raise

Definitions

1. to lift up, to raise
To elevate something physically.
2. to flatter, to praise excessively
To speak highly of someone (often to gain favor).

Examples

重(おも)い荷物(にもつ)を持ち上げる。
To lift up heavy luggage.
上司(じょうし)を持ち上げる。
To flatter one's boss.

Notes

持ち上げる is a compound verb (持つ + 上げる).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を持ち上げる: to lift up ~
  • 〜を褒(ほ)めて持ち上げる: to praise and flatter ~
