申(もう)し上(あ)げる

もうしあげる
verb (ichidan)
to say (humble)

Definitions

1. to say, to tell (humble)
Humble form of 言う used when speaking to someone of higher status.

Examples

ひとこと申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
Allow me to say a word.
お礼(れい)を申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
I would like to express my gratitude.
お願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
I humbly request.
Very formal request

Notes

申(もう)し上(あ)げる is the humble form of 言(い)う.

POLITENESS LEVELS:

  • 言(い)う: plain
  • 申(もう)す: humble (formal)
  • 申(もう)し上(あ)げる: humble (very formal)
  • おっしゃる: honorific

COMMON SET PHRASES:

  • 申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I would like to say
  • お礼(れい)申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I express my gratitude
  • お願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I humbly request

Note: Used in very formal business and ceremonial contexts.
