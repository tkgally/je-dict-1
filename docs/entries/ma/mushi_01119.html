虫(むし)

むし
noun
insect, bug, worm

Definitions

1. insect, bug
A small invertebrate animal.
2. worm
A crawling invertebrate.

Examples

虫(むし)が飛(と)んでいる。
An insect is flying.
虫(むし)に刺(さ)された。
I was bitten by a bug.

Notes

虫(むし) refers to insects and bugs.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 虫歯(むしば): cavity (tooth decay)
  • 昆虫(こんちゅう): insect
  • 毛虫(けむし): caterpillar
  • 虫眼鏡(むしめがね): magnifying glass

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 虫(むし)がいい: selfish, asking too much
  • 虫(むし)の知(し)らせ: premonition
