前(まえ)

まえ
noun
front, before, ago

Definitions

1. front
The forward-facing part.
2. before, prior
Earlier in time.

Examples

前(まえ)を見(み)て。
Look ahead.
三年(さんねん)前(まえ)に来(き)た。
I came three years ago.

Notes

前(まえ) means front and before.

CONTRAST:

  • 前(まえ): front, before
  • 後(うしろ/あと): behind, after

COMPOUNDS:

  • 以前(いぜん): before, previously
  • 名前(なまえ): name
  • 前売(まえう)り: advance sale
  • 午前(ごぜん): morning, AM
