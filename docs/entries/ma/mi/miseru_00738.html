見(み)せる

みせる
verb (ichidan)
to show

Definitions

1. to show, to display
To present something for viewing.

Examples

写真(しゃしん)を見(み)せる。
To show photos.
見(み)せてください。
Please show me.
力(ちから)を見(み)せる。
To show one's power.

Notes

見(み)せる means to show.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 見(み)せる (transitive): to show
  • 見(み)える (intransitive): to be visible

PATTERN:

  • XにYを見(み)せる: to show Y to X
