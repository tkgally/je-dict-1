店(みせ)

みせ
noun
store, shop

Definitions

1. store, shop
A retail establishment where goods are sold. Can refer to any type of shop.

Examples

この店(みせ)は何時(なんじ)まで開(あ)いていますか？
Until what time is this store open?
駅(えき)の近(ちか)くに新(あたら)しい店(みせ)ができました。
A new store opened near the station.
あの店(みせ)のラーメンはおいしいです。
The ramen at that shop is delicious.
Also used for restaurants

Notes

A general word for shops of all kinds. Specific types: 本屋(ほんや) (bookstore), 花屋(はなや) (flower shop), 八百屋(やおや) (greengrocer). お店(みせ) is the polite form.
