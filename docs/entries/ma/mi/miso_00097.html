味噌(みそ)

みそ
noun
miso

Definitions

1. miso, fermented soybean paste
A traditional Japanese seasoning made from fermented soybeans. Used in soups, sauces, and marinades.

Examples

朝(あさ)ごはんに味噌汁(みそしる)を飲(の)みます。
I have miso soup for breakfast.
この味噌(みそ)は甘(あま)いです。
This miso is sweet.
味噌(みそ)は体(からだ)にいいです。
Miso is good for your health.

Notes

味噌 is a staple of Japanese cuisine. There are many varieties including 白味噌 (しろみそ, white/sweet), 赤味噌 (あかみそ, red), and 合わせ味噌 (あわせみそ, mixed). 味噌汁 (miso soup) is a daily essential in many Japanese households.
