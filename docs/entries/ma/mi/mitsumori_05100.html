見積(みつも)り

みつもり
noun
estimate, quotation

Definitions

1. estimate, quotation
A calculation of expected cost or price for goods or services.

Examples

見積(みつも)りを出(だ)してください。
Please give me an estimate.
修理(しゅうり)の見積(みつも)りが高(たか)すぎる。
The repair estimate is too high.
複数(ふくすう)の会社(かいしゃ)から見積(みつも)りを取(と)った。
I got estimates from multiple companies.

Notes

Common in business contexts. 見積書(みつもりしょ) is the formal written estimate/quote. Often used when comparing prices for services like repairs, construction, or projects.
