未練(みれん)

みれん
noun, na-adjective
regret, lingering attachment

Definitions

1. regret, lingering attachment
Reluctance to let go; continued attachment to something that is over.

Examples

元(もと)カノに未練(みれん)がある。
I still have feelings for my ex-girlfriend.
未練(みれん)を断(た)ち切(き)る。
To cut off lingering attachments.
未練(みれん)たらしい態度(たいど)を取(と)るな。
Don't act so clingy/regretful.

Notes

Often used about past relationships or opportunities. 未練(みれん)がましい or 未練(みれん)たらしい describe someone who can't let go. Can be seen as a negative trait (inability to move on).
