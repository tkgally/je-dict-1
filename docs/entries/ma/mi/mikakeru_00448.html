見(み)かける

みかける
verb (ichidan)
to catch sight of, to happen to see

Definitions

1. to catch sight of, to happen to see
To see someone or something by chance, not intentionally looking for it.
2. to notice, to spot
To become aware of something's presence.

Examples

駅(えき)で彼(かれ)を見(み)かけました。
I happened to see him at the station.
この花(はな)は最近(さいきん)見(み)かけなくなった。
I don't see this flower around recently.
珍(めずら)しい鳥(とり)を見(み)かけた。
I spotted a rare bird.

Notes

見(み)かける means to catch sight of or happen to see.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを見(み)かける (catch sight of X)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 見(み)かけている is rarely used; typically past tense

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～を見(み)かける (happen to see ~)
  • よく見(み)かける (often see)
  • 見(み)かけなくなる (stop seeing around)
  • 見(み)かけ (appearance, looks - noun)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)る: to see, watch (intentional)
  • 見(み)かける: to catch sight of (by chance)
  • 見(み)つける: to find (discovery)
