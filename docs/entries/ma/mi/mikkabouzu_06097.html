三日坊主(みっかぼうず)

みっかぼうず
noun
person who gives up easily; quitter

Definitions

1. person who gives up easily; quitter; short-lived enthusiasm
Someone who starts things but quickly loses interest and gives up. Also refers to short-lived efforts or hobbies.

Examples

ダイエットはいつも三日坊主(みっかぼうず)で終(お)わる。
My diets always end after three days.
私(わたし)は三日坊主(みっかぼうず)だから続(つづ)かない。
I'm a quitter, so I can't stick with things.
日記(にっき)をつけ始(はじ)めたが、三日坊主(みっかぼうず)になった。
I started keeping a diary, but it only lasted a few days.

Notes

Literally "three-day monk" - referring to someone who becomes a monk but gives up after just three days.

ORIGIN:
Monastic life is difficult, and those without true dedication would quit after only a few days, hence "three-day monk."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 三日坊主(みっかぼうず)に終(お)わる: to end after only a few days
  • 三日坊主(みっかぼうず)になる: to become a quitter

USAGE:
Often used self-deprecatingly when one admits to lacking perseverance.
