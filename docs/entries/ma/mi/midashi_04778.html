見出(みだ)し

みだし
noun
headline

Definitions

1. headline, heading
A title at the top of an article, section, or document.
2. headword (dictionary)
The entry word in a dictionary or index.

Examples

新聞(しんぶん)の見出(みだ)しを読(よ)む。
I read the newspaper headlines.
大(おお)きな見出(みだ)しで報道(ほうどう)された。
It was reported with a big headline.
辞書(じしょ)の見出(みだ)し語(ご)を調(しら)べる。
I look up the headword in the dictionary.
Dictionary context

Notes

COMPOUND: 見(み)る (to see) + 出(だ)す (to put out)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見出(みだ)しをつける (to add a headline)
  • 見出(みだ)し語(ご) (headword)
  • 大(おお)見出(みだ)し (main headline)
  • 小(こ)見出(みだ)し (subheading)

CONTEXTS:

  • 新聞(しんぶん) (newspaper) - article headlines
  • 雑誌(ざっし) (magazine) - section titles
  • 文書(ぶんしょ) (documents) - headings
  • 辞書(じしょ) (dictionary) - entry words
