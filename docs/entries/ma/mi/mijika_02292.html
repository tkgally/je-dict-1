身近(みぢか)

みぢか
noun
near oneself, familiar, close

Definitions

1. near oneself, close at hand, familiar
Something or someone that is physically or emotionally close to oneself; familiar or easily accessible.

Examples

身近(みぢか)な問題(もんだい)について考(かんが)える。
Think about problems close to home.
スマートフォンは身近(みぢか)な道具(どうぐ)だ。
Smartphones are familiar tools.
身近(みぢか)に感(かん)じる。
To feel close/familiar.

Notes

COMPOUND: 身(み) (body, oneself) + 近(ちか) (near)

Note: The reading is みぢか (not みじか), with the ぢ from rendaku.

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 身近(みぢか)な + Noun (familiar ~, nearby ~)
  • 身近(みぢか)に (closely, familiarly)
  • 身近(みぢか)に感(かん)じる (to feel close/familiar)
  • 身近(みぢか)にある (to be close at hand)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 身近(みぢか)な存在(そんざい) (someone/something close to you)
  • 身近(みぢか)な例(れい) (familiar example)
  • 身近(みぢか)な人(ひと) (someone close to you)
