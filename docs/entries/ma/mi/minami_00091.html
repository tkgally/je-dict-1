南(みなみ)

みなみ
noun
south

Definitions

1. south
The direction opposite to north, where the sun is at midday in the Northern Hemisphere.

Examples

南(みなみ)に行(い)ってください。
Please go south.
日本(にほん)の南(みなみ)は暖(あたた)かいです。
The south of Japan is warm.
南口(みなみぐち)から出(で)てください。
Please exit from the south exit.

Notes

南 is one of the four cardinal directions. Japanese stations often have named exits: 南口 (south exit), 北口 (north exit), etc.

Related:

  • 南アメリカ (South America)
  • 南極 (Antarctica—'south pole').
