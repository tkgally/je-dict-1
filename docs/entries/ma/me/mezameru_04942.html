目覚(めざ)める

めざめる
ichidan verb
to wake up, to awaken

Definitions

1. to wake up, to awaken
To transition from sleep to a state of wakefulness.
2. to awaken to, to become aware of
To realize or become conscious of something, such as a feeling, talent, or truth (figurative use).

Examples

朝(あさ)6時(じ)に目覚(めざ)めた。
I woke up at 6 in the morning.
鳥(とり)の声(こえ)で目覚(めざ)めた。
I woke up to the sound of birds.
彼(かれ)は音楽(おんがく)の才能(さいのう)に目覚(めざ)めた。
He awakened to his musical talent.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to discover or become aware of]

Notes

目覚(めざ)める is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to wake up or awaken.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 目(め) (eye) + 覚(さ)める (to become aware) → eyes become aware/open

ASPECT (ている):
目覚(めざ)めている indicates a state of being awake.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 朝(あさ)目覚(めざ)める (wake up in the morning)
  • ～に目覚(めざ)める (awaken to~, become aware of~)
  • 才能(さいのう)に目覚(めざ)める (awaken to one's talent)
  • 愛(あい)に目覚(めざ)める (awaken to love)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 目覚(めざ)まし: alarm clock (目覚(めざ)まし時計(どけい))
  • 目覚(めざ)め: awakening (noun)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 起(お)きる: to get up (focuses on getting out of bed)
  • 目覚(めざ)める focuses on the transition from sleep to wakefulness

[Register: Neutral]

Related Words

Antonym: ねつく (to fall asleep)
