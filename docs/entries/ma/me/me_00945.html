芽(め)

め
noun
bud, sprout, shoot

Definitions

1. bud, sprout
The initial growth from a seed or plant.
2. beginning, sign
The early stage or indication of something.

Examples

春(はる)になると芽(め)が出(で)る。
Sprouts appear when spring comes.
才能(さいのう)の芽(め)を育(そだ)てる。
To nurture the seeds of talent.

Notes

芽(め) refers to sprouts or beginnings.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 芽(め)が出(で)る: to sprout
  • 芽(め)を摘(つ)む: to nip in the bud
  • 新芽(しんめ): new sprout

FIGURATIVE:

  • 才能(さいのう)の芽(め): budding talent
