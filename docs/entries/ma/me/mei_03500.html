姪(めい)

めい
noun
niece

Definitions

1. niece
The daughter of one's sibling.

Examples

姪(めい)は今年(ことし)小学校(しょうがっこう)に入学(にゅうがく)する。
My niece is starting elementary school this year.
姪(めい)っ子(こ)が遊(あそ)びに来(き)た。
My niece came to visit.
姪(めい)の誕生日(たんじょうび)プレゼントを買(か)った。
I bought a birthday present for my niece.

Notes

姪(めい) refers to one's niece (sibling's daughter).

FAMILY TERMINOLOGY:

  • 姪(めい) (niece)
  • 甥(おい) (nephew)
  • 姪(めい)っ子(こ) (niece - more casual/affectionate)
  • 甥(おい)っ子(こ) (nephew - more casual/affectionate)

POLITE FORMS (for others' relatives):

  • お姪(めい)さん (your niece - polite)
  • お甥(おい)さん (your nephew - polite)

Related Words

Related: 甥(おい) (nephew)
