メンバー

めんばー
noun
member

Definitions

1. member
A person who belongs to a group, team, or organization.

Examples

チームのメンバーは五人(ごにん)だ。
The team has five members.
新(あたら)しいメンバーが加(くわ)わった。
A new member joined.
メンバー全員(ぜんいん)で話(はな)し合(あ)った。
We discussed it with all the members.

Notes

メンバー is commonly used for group members in various contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • メンバー全員(ぜんいん): all members
  • メンバーになる: to become a member
  • メンバーを集(あつ)める: to gather members
  • メンバー紹介(しょうかい): member introduction
  • メンバー構成(こうせい): member composition
  • メンバーズカード: membership card

RELATED WORDS:

  • 会員(かいいん): member (of an organization)
  • 仲間(なかま): companion, fellow member
  • 一員(いちいん): a member (of a group)
