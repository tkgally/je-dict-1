目立(めだ)つ

めだつ
godan verb
to stand out, to be conspicuous

Definitions

1. to stand out, to be noticeable
To attract attention.

Examples

赤(あか)い服(ふく)は目立つ。
Red clothes stand out.

Notes

目立つ = 目(め) (eye) + 立(た)つ (to stand).

COMMON USES:

  • 目立たない: inconspicuous
  • 目立って: noticeably
