毎年(まいとし)

まいとし
noun, adverb
every year

Definitions

1. every year
Each and every year; annually. Used to express something that happens on a yearly basis. Functions as both a noun and an adverb.

Examples

毎年(まいとし)夏(なつ)に海(うみ)へ行(い)きます。
I go to the beach every summer.
毎年(まいとし)お正月(おしょうがつ)に実家(じっか)に帰(かえ)ります。
I go back to my parents' house every New Year.
この祭(まつ)りは毎年(まいとし)八(はち)月(がつ)にあります。
This festival is held every year in August.

Notes

Can also be read as まいねん; both readings are equally correct. Related expressions: 去年(きょねん) (last year), 今年(ことし) (this year), 来年(らいねん) (next year). The まいねん reading is slightly more formal.
