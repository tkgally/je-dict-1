増(ま)す

ます
godan verb
to increase, to grow

Definitions

1. to increase, to grow
To become greater in amount or degree.

Examples

勢(いきお)いが増す。
The momentum increases.
雨(あめ)が増してきた。
The rain is getting heavier.

Notes

増す can be both transitive and intransitive.

USAGE:

  • Often describes gradual increase
  • More literary than 増(ふ)える

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 重要性(じゅうようせい)を増す: to grow in importance
  • 勢(いきお)いを増す: to gain momentum
