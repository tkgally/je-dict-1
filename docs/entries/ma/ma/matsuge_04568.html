睫毛(まつげ)

まつげ
noun
eyelash

Definitions

1. eyelash
The short hairs growing on the edge of the eyelids.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は睫毛(まつげ)が長(なが)くて綺麗(きれい)だ。
She has long, beautiful eyelashes.
睫毛(まつげ)が目(め)に入(はい)って痛(いた)い。
An eyelash got in my eye and it hurts.
マスカラで睫毛(まつげ)を上(あ)げた。
I curled my eyelashes with mascara.

Notes

睫毛(まつげ) (eyelash) is commonly written in hiragana as まつげ or まつ毛.

KANJI NOTE:
The kanji 睫(まつげ) is rarely used. Most often written as まつげ, まつ毛, or 睫毛.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 睫毛(まつげ)が長(なが)い (have long eyelashes)
  • 睫毛(まつげ)を上(あ)げる (curl eyelashes)
  • 付(つ)け睫毛(まつげ) (false eyelashes)
  • 睫毛(まつげ)エクステ (eyelash extensions)

BEAUTY CONTEXT:

  • マスカラ (mascara)
  • ビューラー (eyelash curler)
  • 睫毛(まつげ)パーマ (eyelash perm)

EYE AREA:

  • 眉(まゆ) / 眉毛(まゆげ) (eyebrow)
  • 睫毛(まつげ) (eyelash)
  • 瞼(まぶた) (eyelid)
