曲(ま)げる

まげる
verb (ichidan)
to bend, to curve

Definitions

1. to bend, to curve, to make crooked
To change something from straight to curved or angled.
2. to distort, to twist (facts, truth)
To alter or misrepresent information; to bend the rules.

Examples

膝(ひざ)を曲(ま)げてください。
Please bend your knee.
針金(はりがね)を曲(ま)げて形(かたち)を作(つく)る。
Bend the wire to make a shape.
事実(じじつ)を曲(ま)げて報道(ほうどう)する。
Report by distorting the facts.
Figurative usage

Notes

曲(ま)げる is a transitive verb meaning to bend something or to distort.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 曲(ま)がる (intransitive - to bend, to turn)
  • Pattern: Xを曲(ま)げる

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 腰(こし)を曲(ま)げる (bend one's back)
  • 膝(ひざ)を曲(ま)げる (bend one's knee)
  • 角(かど)を曲(ま)げる (turn a corner) *more commonly uses 曲(ま)がる

FIGURATIVE USAGE:

  • 事実(じじつ)を曲(ま)げる (distort facts)
  • 法律(ほうりつ)を曲(ま)げる (bend the law)
  • 意見(いけん)を曲(ま)げない (not change one's opinion)

Related Words

Pair verb: 曲(ま)がる (intransitive)
