迷子(まいご)

まいご
noun
lost child

Definitions

1. lost child
A child who has become separated from their parents or guardians.
2. lost person, someone who is lost
By extension, anyone who has lost their way (often used playfully for adults).

Examples

迷子(まいご)になった子供(こども)を保護(ほご)した。
I protected a lost child.
「迷子(まいご)のお知(し)らせです」と店内(てんない)アナウンスが流(なが)れた。
A store announcement played: 'We have a lost child announcement.'
Common announcement in Japanese stores
駅(えき)で迷子(まいご)になってしまった。
I got lost at the station.
Can be used for adults, often playfully

Notes

While literally referring to children, often used humorously for adults who are lost.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 迷子(まいご)になる (to get lost)
  • 迷子(まいご)センター (lost child center)
  • 迷子(まいご)のお知(し)らせ (lost child announcement)
  • 迷子(まいご)札(ふだ) (lost child tag - worn by children at events)

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 道(みち)に迷(まよ)う (to lose one's way)
  • はぐれる (to get separated from a group)

NOTE:
The kanji 迷(まい) means "to be lost/confused" and 子(ご) means "child."
