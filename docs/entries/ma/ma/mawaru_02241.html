回(まわ)る

まわる
verb (godan)
to turn, to revolve, to go around

Definitions

1. to turn, to revolve, to rotate
To move in a circular motion around an axis or center point.
2. to go around, to make the rounds
To visit multiple places in sequence, to circulate.
3. to spread, to circulate
For something to spread throughout or circulate (rumors, effects, etc.).

Examples

地球(ちきゅう)は太陽(たいよう)の周(まわ)りを回(まわ)っている。
The Earth revolves around the sun.
Sense 1: revolve
観光(かんこう)バスで町(まち)を回(まわ)った。
I went around the town on a sightseeing bus.
Sense 2: go around
お酒(さけ)が回(まわ)ってきた。
The alcohol is starting to take effect.
Sense 3: spread (through body)

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair verb: 回(まわ)す (transitive, to turn something).

CONJUGATION (godan): 回(まわ)る → 回(まわ)らない → 回(まわ)った → 回(まわ)って

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を回(まわ)る (go around ~)
  • 〜の周(まわ)りを回(まわ)る (revolve around ~)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)に回(まわ)る (go around in order)
  • 右(みぎ)/左(ひだり)に回(まわ)る (turn right/left)

IDIOMS:

  • 頭(あたま)が回(まわ)る (to be quick-witted)
  • 手(て)が回(まわ)る (to have time to deal with)
  • お鉢(はち)が回(まわ)る (one's turn comes around)

Related Words

Pair verb: 回(まわ)す (transitive)
