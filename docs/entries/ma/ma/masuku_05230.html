マスク

ますく
noun
mask, face mask

Definitions

1. face mask, surgical mask
A protective covering worn over the nose and mouth to prevent the spread of germs or protect against allergens and pollution.

Examples

風邪(かぜ)をひいたのでマスクをしている。
I'm wearing a mask because I have a cold.
花粉(かふん)の季節(きせつ)はマスクが欠(か)かせない。
Masks are essential during pollen season.
電車(でんしゃ)の中(なか)でマスクを外(はず)した。
I took off my mask on the train.

Notes

USAGE NOTES:

  • Mask-wearing in Japan was common even before COVID-19, especially during cold/flu season and pollen season.
  • Often worn as a courtesy when sick, to prevent spreading germs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マスクをする/つける (to put on a mask)
  • マスクを外(はず)す (to take off a mask)
  • 使(つか)い捨(す)てマスク (disposable mask)
  • 不織布(ふしょくふ)マスク (non-woven mask)

CULTURAL NOTES:

  • Some people also wear masks to hide their face when they don't feel presentable or don't want to be recognized.
