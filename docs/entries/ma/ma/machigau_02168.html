間違(まちが)う

まちがう
verb
to make a mistake, to be wrong

Definitions

1. to make a mistake, to be wrong
To do something incorrectly or be mistaken.

Examples

道(みち)を間違(まちが)った。
I took the wrong road.
答(こた)えを間違(まちが)った。
I got the answer wrong.
間違(まちが)っていたら教(おし)えて。
Tell me if I'm wrong.

Notes

間違(まちが)う is an intransitive verb meaning to be wrong or make a mistake.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • Related: 間違(まちが)える (transitive, to mistake one thing for another)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 道(みち)を間違(まちが)う (take the wrong road)
  • 答(こた)えを間違(まちが)う (get the answer wrong)
  • 間違(まちが)っている (is wrong/incorrect)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 間違(まちが)い: mistake, error (noun)
  • 間違(まちが)いない: no mistake, definitely
