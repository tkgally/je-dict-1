前(まえ)に

まえに
expression
before, in front of

Definitions

1. before (time)
Indicates that something happens prior to another event or time.
2. in front of (location)
Indicates a position in front of something.

Examples

寝(ね)る前(まえ)に歯(は)を磨(みが)きます。
I brush my teeth before going to bed.
Time: verb dictionary form + 前に
駅(えき)の前(まえ)に会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet in front of the station.
Location: noun + の前に
食事(しょくじ)の前(まえ)に手(て)を洗(あら)ってください。
Please wash your hands before the meal.

Notes

前(まえ)に has two main uses. For time, use [verb dictionary form] + 前(まえ)に or [noun] + の前(まえ)に to mean 'before doing something.' For location, use [noun] + の前(まえ)に to mean 'in front of.' The opposite for time is 後(あと)で (after).
