窓(まど)

まど
noun
window

Definitions

1. window
An opening in a wall for light and air.

Examples

窓(まど)を開(あ)ける。
To open the window.
窓(まど)から外(そと)を見(み)る。
To look outside through the window.

Notes

窓(まど) means window.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 窓口(まどぐち): counter, window (service)
  • 窓側(まどがわ): window side
  • 窓辺(まどべ): by the window
  • 出窓(でまど): bay window
