蒔(ま)く

まく
godan verb
to sow, to plant (seeds)

Definitions

1. to sow, to plant seeds
To scatter or plant seeds in soil.

Examples

種(たね)を蒔く。
To sow seeds.
畑(はたけ)に野菜(やさい)の種(たね)を蒔く。
To sow vegetable seeds in the field.

Notes

蒔く specifically means planting seeds.

HOMOPHONES:

  • 巻(ま)く: to wind, roll
  • 撒(ま)く: to scatter, sprinkle

IDIOM:

  • 蒔(ま)いた種(たね): what one has sown (consequences of actions)
