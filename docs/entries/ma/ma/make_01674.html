負(ま)け

まけ
noun
loss, defeat

Definitions

1. loss, defeat
The state of losing or being defeated in a competition, game, or contest.

Examples

3連敗(れんぱい)で負(ま)けが続(つづ)いている。
Losses continue with three consecutive defeats.
今日(きょう)の試合(しあい)は負(ま)けだった。
Today's match was a loss.
負(ま)けを認(みと)める。
To acknowledge defeat.

Notes

負(ま)け is the noun form of 負(ま)ける (to lose). It indicates defeat in competition.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 負(ま)けが込(こ)む (to be on a losing streak)
  • 負(ま)けを認(みと)める (to acknowledge defeat)
  • 負(ま)け知(し)らず (undefeated)

SPORTS/COMPETITION:

  • 勝(か)ち負(ま)け (winning and losing, victory and defeat)
  • 3勝(しょう)2敗(はい) (3 wins, 2 losses)
  • 負(ま)け越(こ)し (more losses than wins)

IDIOMATIC:

  • 負(ま)け惜(お)しみ (sour grapes, being a sore loser)
  • 負(ま)け犬(いぬ) (underdog, loser)

Related Words

Pair verb: 負(ま)ける (to lose (verb))
Antonym: 勝(か)ち (victory, win)
