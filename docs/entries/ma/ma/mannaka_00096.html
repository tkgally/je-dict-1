真(ま)ん中(なか)

まんなか
noun
center, middle

Definitions

1. center, middle, right in the middle
The exact center point of something. The 真(ま)ん emphasizes that it's the true/exact center.

Examples

テーブルの真(ま)ん中(なか)に花(はな)を置(お)きました。
I placed flowers in the middle of the table.
部屋(へや)の真(ま)ん中(なか)に立(た)ってください。
Please stand in the center of the room.
道(みち)の真(ま)ん中(なか)を歩(ある)かないでください。
Please don't walk in the middle of the road.

Notes

真(ま)ん中(なか) emphasizes the exact center, while 中(なか) alone means 'inside' or 'middle' more generally. The 真(ま)ん part means 'true' or 'exact.' Use [noun]の真(ま)ん中(なか) for 'the center of [noun].'
