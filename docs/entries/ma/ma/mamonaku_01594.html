間(ま)もなく

まもなく
adverb
soon, shortly

Definitions

1. soon, shortly, before long
Indicates something will happen in a short time.

Examples

電車(でんしゃ)が間(ま)もなく到着(とうちゃく)します。
The train will arrive shortly.
Common station announcement
間(ま)もなく始(はじ)まります。
It will begin shortly.
間(ま)もなく春(はる)が来(く)る。
Spring will come soon.

Notes

Formal/polite way to say 'soon.' More formal than もうすぐ.

COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 間(ま)もなく - soon, shortly (formal)
  • もうすぐ - soon (casual)
  • すぐ - immediately, right away
  • そのうち - before long, sometime

Often used in announcements and formal speech.

Related Words

Synonym: もうすぐ (soon (casual))
