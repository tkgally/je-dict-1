毎日(まいにち)

まいにち
noun, adverb
every day

Definitions

1. every day
Each and every day; daily. Used to express something that happens or is done on a daily basis. Can function as both a noun and an adverb.

Examples

毎日(まいにち)日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I study Japanese every day.
毎日(まいにち)の生活(せいかつ)は忙(いそが)しいです。
Daily life is busy.
Used as a noun modifier
毎日(まいにち)同(おな)じ時間(じかん)に起(お)きます。
I wake up at the same time every day.

Notes

Part of a series of 毎(まい)~ words: 毎週(まいしゅう) (every week), 毎月(まいつき) (every month), 毎年(まいとし/まいねん) (every year). When used as an adverb, it typically comes before the verb phrase. The 毎(まい) prefix means 'every' or 'each.'
