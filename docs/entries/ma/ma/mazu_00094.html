まず

まず
adverb
first, to begin with

Definitions

1. first, first of all, to begin with
Used to indicate the first action in a sequence or to introduce the first point.
2. for now, for the time being
Can indicate doing something as a first step before proceeding.

Examples

まず手(て)を洗(あら)ってください。
First, please wash your hands.
まず自己紹介(じこしょうかい)をしましょう。
Let's start with self-introductions.
まずコーヒーを飲(の)みましょう。
Let's have some coffee first.

Notes

まず is commonly used when giving instructions or explaining procedures step by step. Often followed by それから (then/after that) or 次(つぎ)に (next). まずは is a slightly more emphatic variant.
