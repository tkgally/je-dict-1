まだ

まだ
adverb
still, not yet

Definitions

1. still
Continuing to the present time.
2. not yet (with negatives)
Something has not happened up to now.

Examples

まだ雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っています。
It's still raining.
Sense 1
まだ食(た)べていません。
I haven't eaten yet.
Sense 2
まだまだです。
I still have a long way to go.
Humble expression

Notes

Contrast with もう (already). まだまだ = still far from (good enough).

See also: もう
