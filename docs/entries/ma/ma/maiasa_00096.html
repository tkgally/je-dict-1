毎朝(まいあさ)

まいあさ
noun, adverb
every morning

Definitions

1. every morning
Indicates something that happens regularly every morning.

Examples

毎朝(まいあさ)コーヒーを飲(の)みます。
I drink coffee every morning.
毎朝(まいあさ)六時(ろくじ)に起(お)きます。
I wake up at six o'clock every morning.
毎朝(まいあさ)散歩(さんぽ)をしています。
I take a walk every morning.

Notes

毎朝(まいあさ) is part of the 毎(まい) (every) series: 毎日(まいにち) (every day), 毎週(まいしゅう) (every week), 毎月(まいつき) (every month), 毎年(まいとし/まいねん) (every year). These words typically don't require a particle when used as adverbs.
