毎月(まいつき)

まいつき
noun, adverb
every month

Definitions

1. every month
Each and every month; monthly. Used to express something that happens on a monthly basis. Functions as both a noun and an adverb.

Examples

毎月(まいつき)家賃(やちん)を払(はら)います。
I pay rent every month.
毎月(まいつき)の給料(きゅうりょう)はいくらですか。
How much is your monthly salary?
Used as a noun modifier
毎月(まいつき)一(いち)回(かい)病院(びょういん)に行(い)きます。
I go to the hospital once a month.

Notes

Can also be read as まいげつ, though まいつき is more common in speech. Related time expressions: 先月(せんげつ) (last month), 今月(こんげつ) (this month), 来月(らいげつ) (next month).
