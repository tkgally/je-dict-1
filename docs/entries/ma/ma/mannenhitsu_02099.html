万年筆(まんねんひつ)

まんねんひつ
noun
fountain pen

Definitions

1. fountain pen
A pen with an internal reservoir of ink.

Examples

万年筆(まんねんひつ)で手紙(てがみ)を書(か)く。
To write a letter with a fountain pen.
これは高(たか)い万年筆(まんねんひつ)です。
This is an expensive fountain pen.
万年筆(まんねんひつ)にインクを入(い)れる。
To fill a fountain pen with ink.

Notes

万年筆(まんねんひつ) is literally "ten-thousand-year brush."

ETYMOLOGY:

  • 万年(まんねん) (ten thousand years - i.e., long-lasting)
  • 筆(ひつ) (writing brush/pen)

RELATED:

  • ボールペン (ballpoint pen)
  • 鉛筆(えんぴつ) (pencil)
  • インク (ink)
