回(まわ)す

まわす
verb (godan)
to turn, to rotate, to spin

Definitions

1. to turn, to rotate, to spin
To cause something to move in a circular motion.
2. to pass around, to circulate
To hand something around to multiple people in sequence.
3. to send, to forward, to transfer
To send or redirect something (documents, people, calls) to another place.

Examples

ハンドルを右(みぎ)に回(まわ)してください。
Please turn the handle to the right.
Sense 1: turn
書類(しょるい)を皆(みな)に回(まわ)した。
I passed the documents around to everyone.
Sense 2: circulate
電話(でんわ)を担当者(たんとうしゃ)に回(まわ)します。
I'll transfer the call to the person in charge.
Sense 3: transfer

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair verb: 回(まわ)る (intransitive, to turn/revolve).

CONJUGATION (godan): 回(まわ)す → 回(まわ)さない → 回(まわ)した → 回(まわ)して

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を回(まわ)す (turn ~, spin ~)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)に回(まわ)す (pass around in order)
  • 〜に回(まわ)す (transfer to ~, send to ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ハンドルを回(まわ)す (turn the steering wheel/handle)
  • 鍵(かぎ)を回(まわ)す (turn the key)
  • コマを回(まわ)す (spin a top)
  • 首(くび)を回(まわ)す (rotate one's neck)

Related Words

Pair verb: 回(まわ)る (intransitive)
