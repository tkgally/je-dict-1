まさに

まさに
adverb
exactly; precisely; indeed; just about to

Definitions

1. exactly; precisely
Used to emphasize that something is completely accurate or fitting.
2. indeed; truly
Used to strongly affirm or agree with something.
3. just about to; on the verge of
Indicating imminent occurrence (often with ～しようとしている).

Examples

これはまさに私(わたし)が探(さが)していたものだ。
This is exactly what I was looking for.
まさにその通(とお)りです。
That is exactly right.
電車(でんしゃ)がまさに出発(しゅっぱつ)しようとしていた。
The train was just about to depart.

Notes

まさに is an adverb used for emphasis, expressing exactness, strong agreement, or imminent action.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • まさにその通(とお)り (exactly right)
  • まさに今(いま) (right now, at this very moment)
  • まさに～しようとする (just about to do ~)
  • まさに典型的(てんけいてき) (a perfect example)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • まさに vs. ちょうど: Both can mean "exactly," but ちょうど is more for timing/amounts, while まさに emphasizes appropriateness or strong affirmation.
  • まさに vs. 本当(ほんとう)に: 本当(ほんとう)に means "really/truly" in a general sense; まさに has a stronger sense of "precisely this."

KANJI:
Can be written as 正(まさ)に, but hiragana is more common.

Register: Neutral to slightly formal. Often used in emphatic statements.

Related Words

Related: ちょうど (exactly, just)
