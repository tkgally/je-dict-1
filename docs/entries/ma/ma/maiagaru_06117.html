舞(ま)い上(あ)がる

まいあがる
verb (godan)
to soar up, to flutter up

Definitions

1. to soar up, to flutter up, to whirl up
To rise up into the air in a swirling or dancing motion. Often used for leaves, dust, birds, or other light things.
2. to get carried away, to be elated
To become overly excited or elated, losing one's composure due to joy or flattery.

Examples

風(かぜ)で落(お)ち葉(ば)が舞(ま)い上(あ)がった。
The fallen leaves swirled up in the wind.
鳥(とり)が空(そら)に舞(ま)い上(あ)がった。
The bird soared up into the sky.
褒(ほ)められて舞(ま)い上(あ)がってしまった。
I got carried away after being praised.
Figurative - emotional sense

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Compound verb: 舞(ま)う + 上(あ)がる. Pattern: Xが舞(ま)い上(あ)がる (X soars up).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 舞(ま)い上(あ)がっている = 'is swirling up' or 'is elated.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 砂(すな)ぼこりが舞(ま)い上(あ)がる (dust swirls up)
  • 天高(てんたか)く舞(ま)い上(あ)がる (soar high into the sky)
  • 成功(せいこう)して舞(ま)い上(あ)がる (get carried away by success)

NUANCE: The figurative sense (sense 2) carries a slightly negative connotation, suggesting someone has become overconfident or lost their cool.
