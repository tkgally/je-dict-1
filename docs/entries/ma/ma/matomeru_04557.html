纏(まと)める

まとめる
verb (ichidan)
to put together; to summarize; to settle

Definitions

1. to put together; to bundle
To gather scattered things into one place or group.
2. to summarize; to compile
To organize information into a coherent summary or document.
3. to settle; to bring to a conclusion
To bring negotiations, arguments, or affairs to a resolution.

Examples

荷物(にもつ)を纏(まと)めて出(で)かけた。
I gathered my things and went out.
会議(かいぎ)の内容(ないよう)を纏(まと)めてください。
Please summarize the meeting contents.
話(はなし)を纏(まと)めるのに時間(じかん)がかかった。
It took time to settle the matter.

Notes

纏(まと)める is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) used for organizing, summarizing, and concluding.

INTRANSITIVE PAIR: 纏(まと)まる (to be settled, to come together)

  • 纏(まと)める: I summarize the report.
  • 纏(まと)まる: The plan comes together.

ASPECT (ている):
纏(まと)めている indicates the organizing/summarizing process is ongoing.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [things]を纏(まと)める — to put [things] together
  • [information]を纏(まと)める — to summarize [information]
  • [negotiation]を纏(まと)める — to conclude [negotiation]

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 髪(かみ)を纏(まと)める (put up one's hair)
  • 考(かんが)えを纏(まと)める (organize one's thoughts)
  • まとめ買(が)い (buying in bulk)
  • {まとめ} (summary - noun)

OFTEN IN HIRAGANA:
Due to the kanji's complexity, this verb is very often written as まとめる.

RELATED:
