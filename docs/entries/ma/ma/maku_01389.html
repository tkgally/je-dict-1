巻(ま)く

まく
godan verb
to wind, to roll

Definitions

1. to wind, to coil
To wrap something around.
2. to roll up
To form into a cylinder shape.

Examples

マフラーを首(くび)に巻く。
To wrap a scarf around one's neck.
袖(そで)を巻く。
To roll up one's sleeves.

Notes

巻く is a transitive verb with many compounds.

HOMOPHONES:

  • 蒔(ま)く: to sow seeds
  • 撒(ま)く: to scatter

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 巻(ま)き込(こ)む: to involve
  • 巻(ま)き戻(もど)す: to rewind
