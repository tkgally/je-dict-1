まっすぐ

まっすぐ
adverb
straight

Definitions

1. straight, directly
In a direct line without curves or turns. Can describe physical direction or going somewhere without detours.
2. upright, honest
Describing a person's character as straightforward and honest.

Examples

まっすぐ行(い)ってください。
Please go straight.
Giving directions
まっすぐ家(いえ)に帰(かえ)ります。
I'm going straight home.
Without stopping anywhere
まっすぐ立(た)ってください。
Please stand up straight.

Notes

Can be written as 真(ま)っ直(す)ぐ in kanji but hiragana is very common. Also used as a na-adjective: まっすぐな道(みち) (a straight road), まっすぐな人(ひと) (an honest person).
