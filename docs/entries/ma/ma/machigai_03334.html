間違(まちが)い

まちがい
noun
mistake, error

Definitions

1. mistake, error
Something done incorrectly; an error in action, judgment, or calculation.
2. accident, mishap
An unintended event, especially something improper or unfortunate.

Examples

計算(けいさん)に間違(まちが)いがあった。
There was a mistake in the calculation.
間違(まちが)いなく届(とど)けます。
I will definitely deliver it (without fail).
間違(まちが)いなく = without mistake, certainly
それは間違(まちが)いだ。正(ただ)しい答(こた)えはこれだ。
That's wrong. This is the correct answer.

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 間違(まちが)いない (no mistake, certain, for sure)
  • 間違(まちが)いなく (definitely, without fail)
  • 間違(まちが)いを犯(おか)す (to make a mistake)
  • 間違(まちが)いを正(ただ)す (to correct a mistake)
  • 間違(まちが)い電話(でんわ) (wrong number)

RELATED VERB:

  • 間違(まちが)う (to make a mistake) - intransitive
  • 間違(まちが)える (to mistake X for Y) - transitive
