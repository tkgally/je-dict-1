1. to surpass, to exceed, to be superior to
To be better than or exceed something in comparison. Used with に to mark the thing being surpassed.
質(しつ)は量(りょう)に勝(まさ)る。
Quality surpasses quantity.
彼(かれ)は体力(たいりょく)で誰(だれ)にも勝(まさ)る。
He surpasses everyone in physical strength.
この店(みせ)のラーメンは有名店(ゆうめいてん)に勝(まさ)るとも劣(おと)らない味(あじ)だ。
The ramen at this shop tastes every bit as good as at the famous places.

Takes the pattern AはBに勝(まさ)る (A surpasses B). Often used in written or somewhat formal contexts. The opposite is 劣(おと)る (to be inferior).

KEY PATTERNS:

  • AはBに勝(まさ)る — A surpasses B
  • ～に勝(まさ)るとも劣(おと)らない — at least as good as, no less than

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 何(なに)にも勝(まさ)る — surpassing everything, above all else
  • 言葉(ことば)に勝(まさ)る — beyond words
  • 数(かず)に勝(まさ)る — superior in numbers

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 優(すぐ)れる — to excel, to be outstanding
  • 超(こ)える — to exceed, to surpass (more general, less literary)

Antonym: 劣(おと)る (to be inferior)
