入団(にゅうだん)

にゅうだん
noun, verb (suru)
joining a group, team, or troupe
1. joining a group, team, or troupe
The act of formally becoming a member of an organized group such as a sports team, theater troupe, or youth organization. The group joined is marked with に.
プロ野球(やきゅう)チームに入団(にゅうだん)した。
He joined a professional baseball team.
息子(むすこ)がサッカーの少年団(しょうねんだん)に入団(にゅうだん)した。
My son joined a youth soccer club.
入団(にゅうだん)テストに合格(ごうかく)して念願(ねんがん)のチームに入(はい)ることができた。
I passed the tryout and was able to join the team I had always dreamed of.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • チームに入団(にゅうだん)する (join a team)
  • 入団(にゅうだん)テスト (tryout, entry test)
  • 入団(にゅうだん)会見(かいけん) (joining announcement press conference)
  • 入団(にゅうだん)希望(きぼう) (desire to join a team)

USAGE NOTES:
Used specifically for organized groups (団(だん)), especially sports teams and performing arts troupes. For joining a club (部(ぶ)), use 入部(にゅうぶ). For joining a company, use 入社(にゅうしゃ). For joining an association or society, use 入会(にゅうかい).
