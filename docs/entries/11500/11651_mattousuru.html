全(まっと)うする

まっとうする
verb (suru, transitive)
to accomplish, to fulfill
1. to accomplish, to fulfill, to carry out completely
To see something through to the end, especially a duty, responsibility, or role. Implies completing something fully and properly.
責任(せきにん)を全(まっと)うする。
Fulfill one's responsibility.
自分(じぶん)の役割(やくわり)を最後(さいご)まで全(まっと)うした。
I carried out my role to the very end.
天寿(てんじゅ)を全(まっと)うして、祖母(そぼ)は穏(おだ)やかに亡(な)くなった。
Having lived out her natural lifespan, my grandmother passed away peacefully.

A formal and somewhat literary verb. Carries a sense of dignity and completeness in fulfilling one's purpose.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 責任(せきにん)を全(まっと)うする (fulfill one's responsibility)
  • 役割(やくわり)を全(まっと)うする (carry out one's role)
  • 使命(しめい)を全(まっと)うする (accomplish one's mission)
  • 天寿(てんじゅ)を全(まっと)うする (live out one's natural lifespan)
  • 任期(にんき)を全(まっと)うする (serve out one's term)

Also written as 全(まっと)うする or まっとうする. The kanji 全(まっと) is read まっとう here, not ぜん.
