副題(ふくだい)

ふくだい
noun
subtitle, secondary title
1. subtitle, secondary title
A secondary or supplementary title that appears alongside the main title of a book, article, film, or other work. Provides additional context or clarification for the main title. Not to be confused with movie subtitles (字幕(じまく)).
この本(ほん)の副題(ふくだい)は何(なん)ですか。
What is the subtitle of this book?
論文(ろんぶん)には副題(ふくだい)を付(つ)けることが多(おお)い。
Academic papers often have subtitles.
映画(えいが)の副題(ふくだい)が内容(ないよう)をよく表(あらわ)している。
The film's subtitle captures its content well.

Refers to a secondary title of a work (like a book's subtitle after a colon), not to movie/TV subtitles displayed on screen. For on-screen subtitles, use 字幕(じまく).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 副題(ふくだい)を付(つ)ける (to add a subtitle)
  • 〜という副題(ふくだい) (a subtitle that says ~)
  • 副題(ふくだい)のない (without a subtitle)
