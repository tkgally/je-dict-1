営(いとな)む

いとなむ
godan verb (transitive)
to run (a business), to conduct
1. to run, to operate, to manage (a business)
To operate or manage a business, shop, or enterprise. Slightly more formal and literary than 経営(けいえい)する.
父(ちち)は小(ちい)さな店(みせ)を営(いとな)んでいる。
My father runs a small shop.
この旅館(りょかん)は三代(さんだい)にわたって家族(かぞく)で営(いとな)んでいる。
This inn has been run by the family for three generations.
祖母(そぼ)は一人(ひとり)で静(しず)かな生活(せいかつ)を営(いとな)んでいた。
My grandmother lived a quiet life on her own.
2. to conduct, to carry out (a ceremony or life activity)
To carry out or perform certain life activities, ceremonies, or rituals. Used in more literary or formal contexts.
葬儀(そうぎ)が営(いとな)まれた。
A funeral was held.
近所(きんじょ)の寺(てら)で法要(ほうよう)が営(いとな)まれた。
A memorial service was held at the neighborhood temple.
人々(ひとびと)は自然(しぜん)の恵(めぐ)みを受(う)けながら、慎(つつ)ましい暮(く)らしを営(いとな)んできた。
People have lived modest lives, sustained by the blessings of nature.

Godan verb (む row: 営(いとな)む → 営(いとな)まない → 営(いとな)んで). Has a literary quality that makes it common in written language and formal descriptions.

Sense 1 typically takes a business or shop as its object. Sense 2 is used for ceremonies (葬儀(そうぎ)を営(いとな)む) and for broader descriptions of how one lives one's life (生活(せいかつ)を営(いとな)む).

The noun form 営(いとな)み means 'activity' or 'livelihood.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 商売(しょうばい)を営(いとな)む — to run a business
  • 店(みせ)を営(いとな)む — to operate a shop
  • 生活(せいかつ)を営(いとな)む — to lead a life
  • 葬儀(そうぎ)を営(いとな)む — to hold a funeral
