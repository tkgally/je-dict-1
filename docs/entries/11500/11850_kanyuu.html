加入(かにゅう)

かにゅう
noun, suru verb
joining, enrollment
1. joining, enrollment, subscription
The act of becoming a member of an organization, signing up for a service, or enrolling in an insurance plan.
保険(ほけん)に加入(かにゅう)する。
Enroll in insurance.
組合(くみあい)への加入(かにゅう)は任意(にんい)です。
Joining the union is optional.
新(あたら)しいサービスに加入(かにゅう)したいのですが、手続(てつづ)きはどうすればいいですか。
I'd like to subscribe to the new service — how do I go about the procedure?

Commonly used with に to indicate what one is joining. Frequently appears in contexts related to insurance, organizations, and services.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保険(ほけん)に加入(かにゅう)する (sign up for insurance)
  • 組合(くみあい)に加入(かにゅう)する (join a union)
  • 加入(かにゅう)者(しゃ) (subscriber, member)
  • 加入(かにゅう)手続(てつづ)き (enrollment procedure)
  • 加入(かにゅう)条件(じょうけん) (enrollment requirements)
