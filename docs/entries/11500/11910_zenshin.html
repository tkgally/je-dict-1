前身(ぜんしん)

ぜんしん
noun
predecessor, precursor, former form
1. predecessor, precursor, former form
The earlier version or form that something evolved from. Used for organizations, institutions, products, or concepts that were later reorganized, renamed, or transformed into their current form.
この会社(かいしゃ)の前身(ぜんしん)は小(ちい)さな工場(こうじょう)だった。
The predecessor of this company was a small factory.
大学(だいがく)の前身(ぜんしん)は明治(めいじ)時代(じだい)の私塾(しじゅく)だ。
The university's predecessor was a private school in the Meiji era.
この制度(せいど)の前身(ぜんしん)となった仕組(しく)みについて調(しら)べている。
I'm researching the system that served as the precursor to this program.

Describes a thing's earlier incarnation — the "what it used to be." Not used for people (use 前任者(ぜんにんしゃ) for a predecessor in a role).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の前身(ぜんしん) (the predecessor of ~)
  • 前身(ぜんしん)は〜だ (its predecessor was ~)
  • 前身(ぜんしん)となる (to serve as the precursor)
