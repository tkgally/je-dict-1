出回(でまわ)る

でまわる
godan verb
to appear on the market, to circulate
1. to appear on the market, to be widely available, to circulate
For goods, products, or information to become widely available or to spread through circulation.
新米(しんまい)が出回(でまわ)る季節(きせつ)だ。
It's the season when new rice appears on the market.
偽物(にせもの)が市場(しじょう)に出回(でまわ)っている。
Counterfeit goods are circulating in the market.
春(はる)になると新鮮(しんせん)な山菜(さんさい)が店頭(てんとう)に出回(でまわ)り始(はじ)める。
When spring comes, fresh mountain vegetables start appearing in stores.

Compound verb from 出(で)る (to come out) + 回(まわ)る (to go around). Describes goods, products, or information becoming widely available.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • No transitive pair

ASPECT (ている):

  • 出回(でまわ)っている = is circulating, is widely available (ongoing state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 市場(しじょう)に出回(でまわ)る (to appear on the market)
  • 店頭(てんとう)に出回(でまわ)る (to appear in stores)
  • 偽物(にせもの)が出回(でまわ)る (counterfeits circulate)
  • 情報(じょうほう)が出回(でまわ)る (information circulates)
  • 〜が出回(でまわ)る時期(じき) (the time when ~ becomes available)

USAGE NOTES:
Often used for seasonal produce (旬(しゅん)の食材(しょくざい)) appearing in stores, or negatively for counterfeit goods, rumors, or leaked information spreading.
