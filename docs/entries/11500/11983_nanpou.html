南方(なんぽう)

なんぽう
noun
the south; southern regions
1. the south, southern regions, southward
A general term for southern areas or the direction of south. Often refers to Southeast Asia or the tropical regions south of Japan in historical and geographic contexts.
南方(なんぽう)から暖(あたた)かい風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)く。
A warm wind blows from the south.
南方(なんぽう)の島々(しまじま)には独自(どくじ)の文化(ぶんか)がある。
The islands in the south have their own unique cultures.
台風(たいふう)が南方(なんぽう)海上(かいじょう)で発生(はっせい)し、北上(ほくじょう)している。
A typhoon has formed over the southern seas and is moving northward.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 南方(なんぽう)の島(しま) (southern island)
  • 南方(なんぽう)海上(かいじょう) (southern seas)
  • 南方(なんぽう)から (from the south)

USAGE NOTES:
Historically, 南方(なんぽう) was used during the wartime period to refer to Southeast Asia and the Pacific Islands. In modern usage, it simply means "southern regions" and appears often in weather forecasts and geographic descriptions. Contrasts with 北方(ほっぽう) (the north, northern regions). For the simple cardinal direction, 南(みなみ) is more common in everyday speech.
