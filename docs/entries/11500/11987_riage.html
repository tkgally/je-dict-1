利上(りあ)げ

りあげ
noun, verb (suru)
interest rate hike; raising interest rates
1. interest rate hike, raising interest rates
An increase in interest rates, typically by a central bank as a monetary policy tool. Frequently appears in economic news and financial discussions.
日銀(にちぎん)が利上(りあ)げを決定(けってい)した。
The Bank of Japan decided to raise interest rates.
利上(りあ)げの影響(えいきょう)で住宅(じゅうたく)ローンの金利(きんり)が上(あ)がった。
Mortgage interest rates went up due to the rate hike.
追加(ついか)利上(りあ)げの可能性(かのうせい)について市場(しじょう)が注目(ちゅうもく)している。
The market is watching closely for the possibility of further rate hikes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利上(りあ)げを決定(けってい)する (decide to raise rates)
  • 追加(ついか)利上(りあ)げ (additional rate hike)
  • 利上(りあ)げ幅(はば) (size of the rate increase)
  • 利上(りあ)げ観測(かんそく) (rate hike expectations)

USAGE NOTES:
The opposite is 利下(りさ)げ (interest rate cut). In Japanese economic news, 利上(りあ)げ most commonly refers to actions by the 日本銀行(にほんぎんこう) (Bank of Japan) or foreign central banks like the FRB (Federal Reserve). The word is an abbreviation of 金利引(きんりひ)き上(あ)げ.
