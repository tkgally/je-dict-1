1. doubling, sharp increase
An increase to twice the original amount, or more broadly, a dramatic increase in quantity or intensity.
売上(うりあげ)が倍増(ばいぞう)した。
Sales doubled.
予算(よさん)を倍増(ばいぞう)させる計画(けいかく)がある。
There is a plan to double the budget.
防衛費(ぼうえいひ)の倍増(ばいぞう)をめぐって国会(こっかい)で議論(ぎろん)が続(つづ)いている。
Debate continues in the Diet over doubling the defense budget.

Composed of 倍(ばい) (double, times) + 増(ぞう) (increase). Strictly means "doubling," but sometimes used loosely for any dramatic increase.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 倍増(ばいぞう)する: to double (intransitive — something doubles on its own)
  • 倍増(ばいぞう)させる: to double something (transitive/causative)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 売上(うりあげ)が倍増(ばいぞう)する: sales double
  • 予算(よさん)を倍増(ばいぞう)させる: to double the budget
  • 生産量(せいさんりょう)の倍増(ばいぞう): doubling of production
  • 防衛費(ぼうえいひ)の倍増(ばいぞう): doubling defense spending

RELATED:

  • 倍(ばい): double, times (e.g., ２倍(ばい) = twice)
  • 増加(ぞうか): increase (general term)
  • 急増(きゅうぞう): sharp/sudden increase
  • 半減(はんげん): halving (opposite pattern)
