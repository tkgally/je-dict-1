噛(か)みしめる

かみしめる
verb
to chew thoroughly; to savor; to reflect deeply on
1. to chew thoroughly, to bite down on
To chew food carefully and repeatedly, savoring its taste.
よく噛(か)みしめて食(た)べる。
To chew thoroughly and eat.
肉(にく)を噛(か)みしめると旨味(うまみ)が広(ひろ)がった。
When I chewed the meat thoroughly, the umami spread.
一口(ひとくち)ずつゆっくり噛(か)みしめながら食事(しょくじ)を楽(たの)しんだ。
I enjoyed the meal, slowly savoring each bite.
2. to savor, to reflect deeply on, to take to heart
To think carefully about the meaning or significance of something, letting it sink in emotionally.
喜(よろこ)びを噛(か)みしめる。
To savor one's joy.
先生(せんせい)の言葉(ことば)を噛(か)みしめた。
I reflected deeply on the teacher's words.
優勝(ゆうしょう)した瞬間(しゅんかん)、チーム全員(ぜんいん)が勝利(しょうり)の喜(よろこ)びを噛(か)みしめていた。
The moment they won the championship, every team member was savoring the joy of victory.

USAGE:
The figurative sense (sense 2) is very common. 喜(よろこ)びを噛(か)みしめる (to savor one's joy) is a set phrase. The metaphor of "chewing" to mean deep reflection is natural and productive in Japanese.

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)).

  • Negative: 噛(か)みしめない
  • Polite: 噛(か)みしめます
  • Te-form: 噛(か)みしめて
  • Past: 噛(か)みしめた

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 喜(よろこ)びを噛(か)みしめる: to savor one's joy
  • 言葉(ことば)を噛(か)みしめる: to reflect on someone's words
  • 幸(しあわ)せを噛(か)みしめる: to savor one's happiness
  • よく噛(か)みしめて食(た)べる: to chew well and eat
