住(す)まい

すまい
noun
residence, dwelling
1. residence, dwelling, home
A place where someone lives. A slightly refined or polite way to refer to someone's home or dwelling.
お住(す)まいはどちらですか。
Where do you live?
Polite way to ask someone's address.
新(あたら)しい住(す)まいを探(さが)している。
I'm looking for a new place to live.
快適(かいてき)な住(す)まいを実現(じつげん)するために、リフォームを決(き)めた。
I decided on a renovation to achieve a comfortable living space.

Derived from the verb 住(す)む (to live). Softer and more polite than 家(いえ) (house) and less formal than 住居(じゅうきょ) (dwelling/residence). Commonly used in real estate contexts and polite conversation. The polite form お住(す)まい is a standard way to ask where someone lives.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お住(す)まい (your residence — polite)
  • 住(す)まいを探(さが)す (look for a place to live)
  • 住(す)まいの環境(かんきょう) (living environment)
  • 住(す)まい探(さが)し (house-hunting)
