住人(じゅうにん)

じゅうにん
noun
resident; inhabitant
1. resident; inhabitant; dweller
A person who lives in a particular place. Often used for residents of a specific building, area, or region.
このマンションの住人(じゅうにん)は静(しず)かだ。
The residents of this apartment building are quiet.
島(しま)の住人(じゅうにん)たちは漁業(ぎょぎょう)で生活(せいかつ)している。
The island's inhabitants make their living from fishing.
長年(ながねん)の住人(じゅうにん)によると、この辺(あた)りは昔(むかし)は田(た)んぼだったそうだ。
According to a long-time resident, this area used to be rice paddies.

Similar to 住民(じゅうみん), but 住人(じゅうにん) tends to refer to residents of a specific place such as a building or neighborhood, while 住民(じゅうみん) is more commonly used in administrative or official contexts for residents of a town, city, or region.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マンションの住人(じゅうにん) — apartment resident
  • 島(しま)の住人(じゅうにん) — island inhabitant
  • 長年(ながねん)の住人(じゅうにん) — long-time resident
  • 近隣(きんりん)の住人(じゅうにん) — neighborhood resident
