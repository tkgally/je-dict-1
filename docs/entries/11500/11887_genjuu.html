厳重(げんじゅう)

げんじゅう
na-adjective, noun
strict, tight, stringent
1. strict, tight, stringent, secure
Thoroughly secured or tightly controlled, leaving no gaps or vulnerabilities. Commonly describes security measures, guarding, warnings, and protective arrangements.
厳重(げんじゅう)な警備(けいび)が敷(し)かれた。
Tight security was put in place.
金庫(きんこ)は厳重(げんじゅう)に管理(かんり)されている。
The safe is kept under strict security.
個人(こじん)情報(じょうほう)は厳重(げんじゅう)に保管(ほかん)し、外部(がいぶ)に漏(も)れないようにしている。
Personal information is stored under tight security to prevent any leaks.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厳重(げんじゅう)な警備(けいび) (tight security)
  • 厳重(げんじゅう)注意(ちゅうい) (stern warning)
  • 厳重(げんじゅう)に保管(ほかん)する (store securely)
  • 厳重(げんじゅう)に管理(かんり)する (manage strictly)

Focuses on thoroughness of security and protection, unlike 厳格(げんかく) (strict regarding people/discipline) or 厳密(げんみつ) (precise, exact). Often appears in news about security operations, police activities, and data protection.

Related Words

Contrast: 厳格(げんかく) (strict (of people))
