前述(ぜんじゅつ)

ぜんじゅつ
noun, verb (suru)
aforementioned, previously stated
1. aforementioned, previously stated, above-mentioned
Refers to something that has already been mentioned or discussed earlier in a text or speech. A formal, written-style expression commonly used in academic papers, reports, and official documents.
前述(ぜんじゅつ)のとおりである。
It is as stated above.
前述(ぜんじゅつ)した問題(もんだい)について検討(けんとう)する。
We will examine the issue mentioned earlier.
前述(ぜんじゅつ)の理由(りゆう)から、この計画(けいかく)は中止(ちゅうし)とすることにした。
For the aforementioned reasons, we decided to cancel this plan.

A formal, written-style word. Rarely used in casual conversation. Typically found in essays, reports, academic papers, and official documents.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 前述(ぜんじゅつ) — aforementioned (formal, written)
  • 上記(じょうき) — above-mentioned (more common in documents with visual layout)
  • 先述(せんじゅつ) — previously stated (synonym, slightly less common)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 前述(ぜんじゅつ)の〜 (the aforementioned ~)
  • 前述(ぜんじゅつ)のとおり (as mentioned above)
  • 前述(ぜんじゅつ)した〜 (the previously stated ~)
