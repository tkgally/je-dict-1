作(つく)り出(だ)す

つくりだす
verb
to create; to produce; to generate
1. to create; to produce; to bring into being
To make something new that did not exist before. Emphasizes the creative act of bringing something into existence from nothing or from raw materials.
新(あたら)しい製品(せいひん)を作(つく)り出(だ)す。
To create a new product.
この工場(こうじょう)は一日(いちにち)に千個(せんこ)の部品(ぶひん)を作(つく)り出(だ)す。
This factory produces a thousand parts per day.
芸術家(げいじゅつか)は何(なに)もないところから美(うつく)しい作品(さくひん)を作(つく)り出(だ)す。
Artists create beautiful works from nothing.
2. to generate; to give rise to; to create (an atmosphere, situation)
To cause a condition, atmosphere, or situation to come about. Used for abstract things like moods, environments, and opportunities.
温(あたた)かい雰囲気(ふんいき)を作(つく)り出(だ)す。
To create a warm atmosphere.
チームワークが新(あたら)しいアイデアを作(つく)り出(だ)した。
Teamwork produced new ideas.
照明(しょうめい)と音楽(おんがく)で幻想的(げんそうてき)な空間(くうかん)を作(つく)り出(だ)している。
They are creating a fantastical space with lighting and music.

A compound verb combining 作(つく)る (to make) with 出(だ)す (to put out, to begin). The 出(だ)す component adds the nuance of bringing something into existence or producing output.

A transitive verb. Compared to simple 作(つく)る, 作(つく)り出(だ)す emphasizes the creative or generative aspect — producing something new rather than simply assembling or constructing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雰囲気(ふんいき)を作(つく)り出(だ)す — to create an atmosphere
  • 価値(かち)を作(つく)り出(だ)す — to create value
  • 利益(りえき)を作(つく)り出(だ)す — to generate profit
  • 新(あたら)しい文化(ぶんか)を作(つく)り出(だ)す — to create a new culture
