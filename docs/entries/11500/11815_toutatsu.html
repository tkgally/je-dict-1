到達(とうたつ)

とうたつ
noun, verb (suru)
reaching, attaining, arrival
1. reaching, arriving at, attaining
The act of reaching a destination, goal, or level after a process of progress or effort. Used for both physical arrival and abstract achievement of targets or milestones.
目標(もくひょう)に到達(とうたつ)した。
We reached the goal.
登山(とざん)隊(たい)がついに山頂(さんちょう)に到達(とうたつ)した。
The climbing team finally reached the summit.
売上(うりあげ)が過去(かこ)最高(さいこう)の金額(きんがく)に到達(とうたつ)する見込(みこ)みだ。
Sales are expected to reach a record-high amount.

Takes the particle に to mark the destination or target reached. More formal than 着(つ)く and emphasizes the culmination of a process or journey.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目標(もくひょう)に到達(とうたつ)する — to reach a goal
  • 山頂(さんちょう)に到達(とうたつ)する — to reach the summit
  • 到達(とうたつ)点(てん) — point reached, attainment level
  • 到達(とうたつ)度(ど) — degree of attainment
