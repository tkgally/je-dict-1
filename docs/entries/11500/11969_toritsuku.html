取(と)り憑(つ)く

とりつく
verb (godan, intransitive)
to possess; to haunt; to be obsessed
1. to possess, to haunt
For a spirit, ghost, or supernatural entity to attach itself to a person or place. The person or thing possessed is marked with に.
悪霊(あくりょう)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれた。
I was possessed by an evil spirit.
この家(いえ)には幽霊(ゆうれい)が取(と)り憑(つ)いている。
This house is haunted by a ghost.
狐(きつね)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれたという言(い)い伝(つた)えがある。
There is a legend about someone being possessed by a fox spirit.
2. to be obsessed with, to be gripped by
Used figuratively for an idea, emotion, or obsession that takes hold of someone and won't let go, as if one were possessed.
不安(ふあん)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれている。
I am gripped by anxiety.
彼(かれ)は成功(せいこう)への執念(しゅうねん)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれていた。
He was obsessed with his fixation on success.
まるで何(なに)かに取(と)り憑(つ)かれたように働(はたら)き続(つづ)けた。
He kept working as if possessed by something.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悪霊(あくりょう)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれる (be possessed by an evil spirit)
  • 不安(ふあん)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれる (be gripped by anxiety)
  • 狐(きつね)に取(と)り憑(つ)かれる (be possessed by a fox spirit)

USAGE NOTES:
Most commonly encountered in the passive form 取(と)り憑(つ)かれる. The kanji 憑(つ)く is uncommon and the word is sometimes written as 取(と)り付(つ)く, though that spelling can also mean "to cling to" in a non-supernatural sense. In the figurative sense, it describes an overwhelming psychological state that controls someone's behavior.
