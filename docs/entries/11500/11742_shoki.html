初期(しょき)

しょき
noun
early period, initial stage
1. early period, initial stage, beginning phase
The first or early phase of a process, era, illness, or development. Emphasizes the stage rather than the exact starting point.
初期(しょき)設定(せってい)に戻(もど)す。
Reset to the initial settings.
病気(びょうき)が初期(しょき)の段階(だんかい)で見(み)つかった。
The illness was discovered at an early stage.
プロジェクトの初期(しょき)段階(だんかい)では予算(よさん)の見積(みつ)もりが難(むずか)しかった。
In the initial stages of the project, estimating the budget was difficult.

Compound of 初(しょ) (first, beginning) + 期(き) (period, stage). Used across many fields including medicine, technology, history, and project management.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 初期(しょき)段階(だんかい) (initial stage)
  • 初期(しょき)設定(せってい) (default settings, initial configuration)
  • 初期(しょき)症状(しょうじょう) (early symptoms)
  • 初期(しょき)費用(ひよう) (initial cost, startup cost)
  • 初期(しょき)不良(ふりょう) (initial defect)
  • 初期(しょき)の頃(ころ) (in the early days)
  • 江戸(えど)初期(しょき) (early Edo period)

USAGE NOTES:
In technology, 初期(しょき)設定(せってい) (default/factory settings) and 初期(しょき)化(か) (initialization) are extremely common terms. In medicine, 初期(しょき) often refers to the early stage of a disease when treatment is most effective.
