利(き)く

きく
godan verb
to be effective; to work; to be possible
1. to be effective, to work, to take effect
Describes something functioning properly or producing the intended effect. Used for medicine, brakes, air conditioning, seasoning, etc.
この薬(くすり)はよく利(き)く。
This medicine works well.
エアコンが利(き)いていて涼(すず)しい。
The air conditioning is working and it's cool.
古(ふる)い車(くるま)なのでブレーキの利(き)きが悪(わる)い。
It's an old car, so the brakes don't work well.
2. to be possible, to be able to do
Indicates that something can be done or is available as an option. Often used in the pattern ～が利く.
融通(ゆうずう)が利(き)く人(ひと)だ。
He's a flexible person.
この店(みせ)はカードが利(き)く。
This store accepts cards.
小回(こまわ)りが利(き)くので狭(せま)い道(みち)でも大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)だ。
It's maneuverable, so even narrow roads are fine.

Written with the kanji 利(き)く to distinguish from the homophones 聞(き)く (to listen/ask) and 効(き)く (to be effective — more focused on medicinal or chemical effects). In practice, 利(き)く and 効(き)く overlap considerably for sense 1, but 利(き)く covers the broader meaning of functioning or being available.

CONJUGATION:

  • 利(き)かない (negative)
  • 利(き)きます (polite)
  • 利(き)いた (past)
  • 利(き)いて (te-form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ブレーキが利(き)く — the brakes work
  • 融通(ゆうずう)が利(き)く — to be flexible/adaptable
  • 小回(こまわ)りが利(き)く — to be maneuverable
  • 気(き)が利(き)く — to be considerate/attentive
  • つぶしが利(き)く — to have versatile skills/qualifications
  • 無理(むり)が利(き)く — to be able to push oneself
