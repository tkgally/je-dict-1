供(そな)える

そなえる
verb (ichidan)
to offer, to make an offering
1. to offer, to make an offering (to gods, ancestors, the deceased)
To present food, flowers, or other items as a religious or memorial offering, typically placing them at an altar, grave, or shrine.
仏壇(ぶつだん)に花(はな)を供(そな)える。
Offer flowers at the Buddhist altar.
墓(はか)に線香(せんこう)と果物(くだもの)を供(そな)えた。
I offered incense and fruit at the grave.
毎朝(まいあさ)、神棚(かみだな)に水(みず)と米(こめ)を供(そな)えるのが日課(にっか)になっている。
It has become my daily routine to offer water and rice at the household Shinto altar every morning.

An ichidan verb. Not to be confused with the homophones 備(そな)える (to prepare, equip) or 具(そな)える (to possess, be endowed with), which use different kanji and have different meanings. The particle に marks the place or entity receiving the offering, while を marks the item being offered.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仏壇(ぶつだん)に供(そな)える (offer at the Buddhist altar)
  • 墓(はか)に供(そな)える (offer at a grave)
  • 神棚(かみだな)に供(そな)える (offer at the household shrine)
  • 花(はな)を供(そな)える (offer flowers)
  • お供(そな)え物(もの) (offering, oblation)
