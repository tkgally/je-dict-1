分割(ぶんかつ)

ぶんかつ
noun / suru verb
division, partition, splitting
1. division, partition, splitting up
The act of dividing something into separate parts or sections. Used for physical objects, payments, data, and abstract concepts.
分割(ぶんかつ)払(ばら)いにする。
I'll pay in installments.
土地(とち)を三(みっ)つに分割(ぶんかつ)した。
The land was divided into three parts.
画面(がめん)を分割(ぶんかつ)して二(ふた)つのアプリを同時(どうじ)に表示(ひょうじ)できる。
You can split the screen and display two apps at the same time.

Compound of 分(ぶん) (to divide) + 割(かつ) (to split). A common word in finance, technology, and everyday contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 分割(ぶんかつ)する (to divide, to split)
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)払(ばら)い (installment payment)
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)統治(とうち) (divide and rule)
  • 画面(がめん)分割(ぶんかつ) (split screen)
  • 株式(かぶしき)分割(ぶんかつ) (stock split)
  • 分割(ぶんかつ)ファイル (split file)
  • 〜を二(ふた)つに分割(ぶんかつ)する (to divide ~ into two)

DISTINCTIONS:

  • 分割(ぶんかつ): dividing one thing into defined parts (often deliberate, planned)
  • 分離(ぶんり): separating things that were connected
  • 分裂(ぶんれつ): splitting apart, often with a sense of conflict or fragmentation
