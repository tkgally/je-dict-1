名付(なづ)ける

なづける
verb
to name; to give a name to
1. to name, to give a name to, to call
To assign a name to a person, place, thing, or concept. Used for the act of choosing and bestowing a name.
赤(あか)ちゃんに名前(なまえ)を名付(なづ)ける。
To give a name to a baby.
父(ちち)が娘(むすめ)を「さくら」と名付(なづ)けた。
The father named his daughter 'Sakura.'
この通(とお)りは、昔(むかし)ここにあった桜(さくら)の木(き)にちなんで「さくら通(どお)り」と名付(なづ)けられた。
This street was named 'Sakura-dori' after the cherry trees that used to be here.

USAGE:
Often used in the passive form 名付(なづ)けられた (was named). The pattern AをBと名付(なづ)ける means "to name A as B." Also written as 名(な)づける.

CONJUGATION:

Ichidan verb (一段動詞(いちだんどうし)).

  • Negative: 名付(なづ)けない
  • Polite: 名付(なづ)けます
  • Te-form: 名付(なづ)けて
  • Past: 名付(なづ)けた
  • Passive: 名付(なづ)けられる

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ～と名付(なづ)ける: to name as ~
  • 名付(なづ)け親(おや): godparent; person who names something
  • ～にちなんで名付(なづ)ける: to name after ~
