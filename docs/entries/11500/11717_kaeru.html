代(か)える

かえる
ichidan verb
to substitute, to replace with
1. to substitute, to replace with, to use in place of
To use one thing in place of another. The focus is on providing a substitute or alternative for something.
バターをマーガリンに代(か)えた。
I substituted margarine for butter.
挨拶(あいさつ)に代(か)えて、メールを送(おく)った。
I sent an email in lieu of a formal greeting.
書面(しょめん)をもって報告(ほうこく)に代(か)えさせていただきます。
Please allow this written document to serve in place of a verbal report.

代(か)える is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to substitute or replace with something. The intransitive pair is 代(か)わる.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 代(か)わる (intransitive — to take the place of)
  • Pattern: AをBに代(か)える (to substitute B for A)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 代(か)えている = has substituted, is using as a substitute (resulting state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AをBに代(か)える (to substitute B for A)
  • 〜に代(か)えて (in place of, instead of)
  • 挨拶(あいさつ)に代(か)える (to serve as a greeting)

DISTINCTIONS:

  • 代(か)える: to substitute, to use as a replacement for
  • 変(か)える: to change, to alter (modify something's state)
  • 換(か)える / 替(か)える: to exchange, to swap (interchangeable items)

Related Words

Homophone: 変(か)える
Antonym: 帰(かえ)る
