1. to turn face down, to lay something face down
To place something with its open or front side facing downward.
カードを伏(ふ)せる。
Turn the card face down.
コップを伏(ふ)せて乾(かわ)かす。
Turn the cup upside down and let it dry.
食(た)べ終(お)わったら茶碗(ちゃわん)を伏(ふ)せておいてください。
When you're done eating, please turn your bowl upside down.
2. to hide, to conceal, to keep secret
To deliberately withhold information or keep something out of sight.
名前(なまえ)を伏(ふ)せる。
Conceal the name.
事実(じじつ)を伏(ふ)せて報告(ほうこく)した。
The report was made with the facts concealed.
関係者(かんけいしゃ)の名前(なまえ)は伏(ふ)せたまま記事(きじ)が公開(こうかい)された。
The article was published with the names of those involved kept secret.

伏(ふ)せる has two main meanings: to turn something face down (physical), and to hide or conceal information.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: Xを伏(ふ)せる (to turn X face down / to conceal X)

ASPECT (ている):
伏(ふ)せている means "is face down" or "is being kept hidden" (resulting state).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • カードを伏(ふ)せる: turn a card face down
  • コップを伏(ふ)せる: turn a cup upside down
  • 目(め)を伏(ふ)せる: cast one's eyes down
  • 名前(なまえ)を伏(ふ)せる: withhold a name
  • 事実(じじつ)を伏(ふ)せる: conceal a fact

USAGE:
Sense 1 (face down) appears in instructions and descriptions. Sense 2 (concealing) is common in news and formal writing, especially when sources or names are withheld.
