冷(ひ)やかす

ひやかす
godan verb
to tease, to make fun of; to window-shop
1. to tease, to make fun of, to jeer at
To mock or ridicule someone in a playful or slightly malicious way, often by making comments about their behavior or situation.
友達(ともだち)を冷(ひ)やかした。
I teased my friend.
彼(かれ)がデートに行(い)くと言(い)ったら、みんなに冷(ひ)やかされた。
When he said he was going on a date, everyone teased him.
新人(しんじん)を冷(ひ)やかすのはやめなさい。
Stop making fun of the newcomer.
2. to window-shop, to browse without buying
To look at goods in a shop or stall without intending to purchase anything.
店(みせ)を冷(ひ)やかして歩(ある)いた。
I walked around window-shopping.
買(か)うつもりはないが、骨董品(こっとうひん)店(てん)を冷(ひ)やかしに行(い)った。
I had no intention of buying, but went to browse the antique shop.
祭(まつ)りの屋台(やたい)を冷(ひ)やかしながら友人(ゆうじん)と楽(たの)しく過(す)ごした。
I had a good time with a friend browsing the festival stalls.

Originally related to 冷(ひ)やす (to cool), but the modern meanings have diverged.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: AがBを冷(ひ)やかす (A teases B / A browses B)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 冷(ひ)やかしている = is teasing / is browsing (ongoing action)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)を冷(ひ)やかす (to tease someone)
  • 店(みせ)を冷(ひ)やかす (to browse a shop)
  • 冷(ひ)やかされる (to be teased — passive)
  • 冷(ひ)やかし半分(はんぶん)に (half-jokingly)

USAGE NOTES:
The teasing sense (sense 1) is the more common usage today. The window-shopping sense (sense 2) is somewhat old-fashioned but still understood. The noun form 冷(ひ)やかし refers to either the act of teasing or of browsing without buying.
