元来(がんらい)

がんらい
adverb
originally; by nature; inherently
1. originally; by nature; inherently
Indicates that something has been the case from the very beginning, or that a quality is fundamental and innate. Used to describe original states, inherent characteristics, or the way things were meant to be.
彼(かれ)は元来(がんらい)真面目(まじめ)な人(ひと)だ。
He is by nature a serious person.
この土地(とち)は元来(がんらい)農地(のうち)だった。
This land was originally farmland.
元来(がんらい)体(からだ)が丈夫(じょうぶ)だったので、大(おお)きな病気(びょうき)をしたことがない。
Since I was inherently healthy, I've never had a serious illness.

A literary or formal adverb. In everyday speech, もともと} is more commonly used with the same meaning. {元来(がんらい) appears more frequently in writing and formal contexts.

  • 元来(がんらい)〜な性格(せいかく)だ — is by nature a ~ personality
  • 元来(がんらい)〜だった — was originally ~
  • 元来(がんらい)の目的(もくてき) — the original purpose
