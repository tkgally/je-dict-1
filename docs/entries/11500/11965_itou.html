厭(いと)う

いとう
verb (godan, transitive)
to dislike; to be reluctant; to shun
1. to dislike, to be reluctant, to shun
To feel aversion toward something and wish to avoid it. Carries a literary or formal tone compared to everyday words like 嫌(きら)う. Often used with abstract nouns like 苦労(くろう) (hardship) or 手間(てま) (effort).
苦労(くろう)を厭(いと)わない。
I don't shy away from hard work.
手間(てま)を厭(いと)わずに丁寧(ていねい)に作(つく)る。
Make it carefully without begrudging the effort.
彼(かれ)はどんな困難(こんなん)も厭(いと)わず挑戦(ちょうせん)し続(つづ)けた。
He kept taking on challenges without shunning any difficulty.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 苦労(くろう)を厭(いと)わない (not shy away from hardship)
  • 手間(てま)を厭(いと)わない (not begrudge the effort)
  • 危険(きけん)を厭(いと)わない (not shrink from danger)

USAGE NOTES:
Most commonly encountered in the negative form 厭(いと)わない, meaning "to not shy away from" or "to willingly accept." The affirmative form is less common in modern usage. The word has a literary, slightly elevated register — in casual speech, 嫌(きら)う or 嫌(いや)がる are more natural choices. The kanji 厭(いと) is relatively uncommon and the word is sometimes written in hiragana as いとう.
