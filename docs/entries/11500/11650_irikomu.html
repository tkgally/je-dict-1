入(い)り込(こ)む

いりこむ
verb (godan, intransitive)
to get into, to slip into
1. to get into, to slip into, to enter deeply
To go deep into a place, often suggesting entering somewhere that is enclosed, hidden, or not easily accessible.
猫(ねこ)が押(お)し入(い)れに入(い)り込(こ)んだ。
The cat got into the closet.
雨水(あまみず)が建物(たてもの)の中(なか)に入(い)り込(こ)んでいた。
Rainwater had gotten inside the building.
不審者(ふしんしゃ)が敷地(しきち)内(ない)に入(い)り込(こ)んだ形跡(けいせき)がある。
There are traces of a suspicious person having gotten onto the premises.
2. to become deeply involved, to be absorbed in
To become immersed in or deeply engaged with something such as a story, role, or activity.
役(やく)に入(い)り込(こ)む。
Get deeply into a role.
小説(しょうせつ)の世界(せかい)に入(い)り込(こ)んでしまった。
I became completely absorbed in the world of the novel.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の研究(けんきゅう)に入(い)り込(こ)みすぎて、周(まわ)りが見(み)えなくなる。
He gets so deeply absorbed in his research that he loses sight of his surroundings.

A compound verb combining 入(はい)る (to enter) and 込(こ)む (into, deeply). The 込(こ)む component adds the nuance of going deep or becoming immersed.

CONJUGATION (godan, む row):

  • 入(い)り込(こ)まない (negative)
  • 入(い)り込(こ)みます (polite)
  • 入(い)り込(こ)んで (te-form)
  • 入(い)り込(こ)んだ (past)

Note that this word also has the reading 入(はい)り込(こ)む (hairikomu), which is a separate entry. The いりこむ reading tends to suggest slipping in or penetrating, while はいりこむ emphasizes physically entering a space.
