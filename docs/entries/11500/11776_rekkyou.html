列強(れっきょう)

れっきょう
noun
great powers, major nations
1. great powers, major nations
The powerful nations of the world, especially in a historical context referring to the dominant imperial or military powers.
列強(れっきょう)がアジアに進出(しんしゅつ)した。
The great powers advanced into Asia.
日本(にほん)は明治(めいじ)時代(じだい)に列強(れっきょう)の仲間入(なかまい)りを果(は)たした。
Japan joined the ranks of the great powers during the Meiji era.
19世紀(せいき)の列強(れっきょう)は植民地(しょくみんち)獲得(かくとく)を競(きそ)い合(あ)った。
The great powers of the 19th century competed with each other to acquire colonies.

Primarily a historical term referring to the dominant world powers, especially during the 19th and early 20th centuries (Britain, France, Germany, Russia, the United States, etc.). Can also be used in modern contexts to refer to powerful nations.

Literally means "row of strong ones" (列(れつ) = row + 強(きょう) = strong).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 列強(れっきょう)の一(ひと)つ (one of the great powers)
  • 列強(れっきょう)に並(なら)ぶ (stand alongside the great powers)
  • 欧米(おうべい)列強(れっきょう) (Western great powers)
general
