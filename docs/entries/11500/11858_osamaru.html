1. to fit into, to be contained in
To fit or be accommodated within a space, container, or limit.
荷物(にもつ)がスーツケースに収(おさ)まった。
The luggage fit into the suitcase.
予算(よさん)の範囲(はんい)に収(おさ)まるようにした。
I made sure it stayed within budget.
全部(ぜんぶ)を一(ひと)つの箱(はこ)に収(おさ)めるのは難(むずか)しい。
It's hard to fit everything in one box.
2. to settle down, to subside, to be resolved
For a situation, disturbance, or emotion to calm down and return to a normal state.
騒(さわ)ぎはすぐに収(おさ)まった。
The commotion quickly settled down.
怒(いか)りが収(おさ)まらない。
My anger won't subside.
話(はなし)し合(あ)いで丸(まる)く収(おさ)まった。
It was resolved amicably through discussion.

The transitive counterpart is 収(おさ)める (to put in, to store; to bring under control). The kanji 収(おさ)まる focuses on containment and resolution, while the homophone 治(おさ)まる focuses on governance or healing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 丸(まる)く収(おさ)まる — to be resolved amicably
  • 怒(いか)りが収(おさ)まる — anger subsides
  • 騒(さわ)ぎが収(おさ)まる — commotion dies down
  • 予算(よさん)に収(おさ)まる — to stay within budget

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 治(おさ)まる — to be governed; to heal, to be cured
  • 納(おさ)まる — to be delivered, to be paid
