助数詞(じょすうし)

じょすうし
noun
counter word, counter suffix, classifier
1. counter word, counter suffix, numerical classifier
A grammatical term for the suffixes attached to numbers when counting things in Japanese. Different counters are used depending on what is being counted — flat things, long things, people, animals, etc. Also called 数(かぞ)え方(かた) informally.
日本語(にほんご)には多(おお)くの助数詞(じょすうし)がある。
Japanese has many counter words.
助数詞(じょすうし)の使(つか)い方(かた)は外国人(がいこくじん)にとって難(むずか)しい。
The use of counter words is difficult for foreigners.
正(ただ)しい助数詞(じょすうし)を使(つか)えるようになると、日本語(にほんご)がより自然(しぜん)に聞(き)こえる。
When you can use the correct counter words, your Japanese will sound more natural.

A linguistic term that learners of Japanese encounter frequently. Common counters include 枚(まい) (flat things), 本(ほん) (long things), 匹(ひき) (small animals), 冊(さつ) (books), 台(だい) (machines/vehicles), and 杯(はい) (cups/glasses). Knowing which counter to use with which object is one of the distinctive challenges of Japanese.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 助数詞(じょすうし)を使(つか)う (to use a counter word)
  • 助数詞(じょすうし)を覚(おぼ)える (to learn counter words)
  • 助数詞(じょすうし)の一覧(いちらん) (list of counter words)
