創業(そうぎょう)

そうぎょう
noun / suru verb
founding; establishment of a business
1. founding; establishment of a business
The act of starting or founding a business. Also refers to the time when a business was first established.
この会社(かいしゃ)は1920年(ねん)に創業(そうぎょう)した。
This company was founded in 1920.
創業(そうぎょう)100年(ねん)を迎(むか)えた老舗(しにせ)の旅館(りょかん)だ。
It is a long-established inn that has reached its 100th anniversary since founding.
父(ちち)は小(ちい)さな町工場(まちこうば)を創業(そうぎょう)し、やがて全国(ぜんこく)に知(し)られる企業(きぎょう)に育(そだ)てた。
My father founded a small town factory and eventually grew it into a nationally known company.

Commonly seen on company websites and in business contexts. Japanese companies often proudly display their founding year, especially long-established ones. Distinguished from 設立(せつりつ) (establishment), which often refers to the legal incorporation date, while 創業 refers to when business operations actually began.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 創業(そうぎょう)者(しゃ): founder
  • 創業(そうぎょう)100年(ねん): 100 years since founding
  • 創業(そうぎょう)以来(いらい): since the founding
