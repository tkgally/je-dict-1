代(か)わる

かわる
godan verb
to take the place of, to be substituted
1. to take the place of, to replace, to substitute for
For one thing or person to serve in place of another. The subject takes over the role or function of something else.
私(わたし)が代(か)わりに行(い)きます。
I'll go in your place.
電話(でんわ)を代(か)わってもらえますか。
Could you put someone else on the phone?
AIが人間(にんげん)に代(か)わって多(おお)くの仕事(しごと)をこなす時代(じだい)が来(き)ている。
An era is coming where AI handles many tasks in place of humans.

代(か)わる is an intransitive godan verb meaning for something to take the place of something else. The transitive pair is 代(か)える.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pair: 代(か)える (transitive — to substitute)
  • Pattern: AがBに代(か)わる (A takes the place of B)

ASPECT (ている):

  • 代(か)わっている = has replaced, is acting as a substitute (resulting state)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AがBに代(か)わる (A replaces B)
  • Aに代(か)わって (in place of A, on behalf of A)
  • 代(か)わりに (instead, in place of — very common)

DISTINCTIONS:

  • 代(か)わる: to take the place of, to be substituted (intransitive)
  • 変(か)わる: to change, to be altered (modification of state)
  • 換(か)わる / 替(か)わる: to be exchanged, to be swapped

Related Words

Pair verb: 代(か)える
Homophone: 変(か)わる
