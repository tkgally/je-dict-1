器(うつわ)

うつわ
noun
vessel, container; capacity, caliber
1. vessel, container, dish
A container or dish used for holding food, drink, or other items. Often refers to pottery, ceramics, or tableware with aesthetic value.
料理(りょうり)を美(うつく)しい器(うつわ)に盛(も)り付(つ)けた。
I arranged the food on a beautiful dish.
この店(みせ)は器(うつわ)にもこだわっている。
This restaurant is particular about its tableware too.
陶芸(とうげい)教室(きょうしつ)で自分(じぶん)だけの器(うつわ)を作(つく)った。
I made my own pottery dish at a ceramics class.
2. capacity, caliber, character
A person's capacity, caliber, or suitability for a role. Used figuratively to describe whether someone has the personal qualities needed for a position or responsibility.
彼(かれ)はリーダーの器(うつわ)だ。
He has the caliber to be a leader.
器(うつわ)の大(おお)きい人(ひと)になりたい。
I want to become a person of great character.
社長(しゃちょう)の器(うつわ)ではないと自分(じぶん)でも分(わ)かっている。
I know myself that I'm not cut out to be president.

In sense 1, 器(うつわ) is closely associated with Japanese food culture and aesthetics — the presentation of food on carefully chosen 器(うつわ) is considered an important part of the dining experience.

In sense 2, 器(うつわ)が大(おお)きい (big vessel) means someone is generous and broad-minded, while 器(うつわ)が小(ちい)さい (small vessel) means someone is petty or narrow-minded.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 器(うつわ)に盛(も)る — to serve in a dish
  • 器(うつわ)が大(おお)きい — broad-minded, magnanimous
  • 器(うつわ)が小(ちい)さい — petty, narrow-minded
  • リーダーの器(うつわ) — the caliber of a leader
  • 焼(や)き物(もの)の器(うつわ) — pottery dishes
