君臨(くんりん)

くんりん
noun, verb (suru)
reigning; ruling; to reign; to dominate
1. reigning, ruling, dominating
To hold supreme authority or dominant position. Originally refers to a monarch's rule, but widely used figuratively for anyone or anything that holds an unchallenged top position.
王(おう)として君臨(くんりん)する。
Reign as king.
彼(かれ)は業界(ぎょうかい)の頂点(ちょうてん)に君臨(くんりん)している。
He reigns at the top of the industry.
長年(ながねん)にわたり世界(せかい)チャンピオンとして君臨(くんりん)し続(つづ)けた。
He continued to reign as world champion for many years.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頂点(ちょうてん)に君臨(くんりん)する (reign at the summit)
  • 王者(おうじゃ)として君臨(くんりん)する (reign as champion)
  • 君臨(くんりん)し続(つづ)ける (continue to reign)

USAGE NOTES:
While the original meaning is about monarchical rule, 君臨(くんりん) is most commonly used figuratively in modern Japanese. Sports champions, market leaders, top-ranked schools, or anyone who dominates a field can be described as 君臨(くんりん)している. The word carries a sense of unchallenged authority and long-held dominance.

The related expression 君臨(くんりん)すれども統治(とうち)せず means "reigns but does not govern" — describing a constitutional monarchy.
