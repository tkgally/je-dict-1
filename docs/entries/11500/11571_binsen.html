便箋(びんせん)

びんせん
noun
letter paper, writing paper
1. letter paper, writing paper, stationery
Paper specifically designed for writing letters, typically with lines or decorative borders. A staple item in Japanese stationery culture.
便箋(びんせん)に手紙(てがみ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a letter on writing paper.
花柄(はながら)の便箋(びんせん)を買(か)った。
I bought letter paper with a floral pattern.
祖母(そぼ)に感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちを込(こ)めて、きれいな便箋(びんせん)で手紙(てがみ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a letter to my grandmother on beautiful stationery to express my gratitude.

Japan has a rich stationery culture, and 便箋(びんせん) comes in many designs, from simple lined paper to elaborate seasonal patterns. Often sold as a set together with 封筒(ふうとう) (envelopes). Handwritten letters on nice 便箋(びんせん) are considered more heartfelt than typed or digital messages.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 便箋(びんせん)と封筒(ふうとう) (letter paper and envelopes)
  • 便箋(びんせん)セット (stationery set)
  • 罫線(けいせん)入(い)りの便箋(びんせん) (lined letter paper)
