回避(かいひ)

かいひ
noun / suru verb
avoidance; evasion
1. avoidance; evasion
The act of avoiding or steering clear of something undesirable such as risk, danger, conflict, or responsibility.
リスクを回避(かいひ)する。
To avoid risk.
衝突(しょうとつ)を回避(かいひ)するために話(はな)し合(あ)った。
They discussed in order to avoid a clash.
早(はや)めに対策(たいさく)を取(と)ったおかげで、最悪(さいあく)の事態(じたい)を回避(かいひ)することができた。
Thanks to taking countermeasures early, we were able to avoid the worst-case scenario.

A formal word commonly used in news, business, and academic contexts. Often paired with negative outcomes like risks, conflicts, and crises. Distinguished from 避(さ)ける, which is more general and less formal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リスクを回避(かいひ)する: to avoid risk
  • 危険(きけん)を回避(かいひ)する: to avoid danger
  • 衝突(しょうとつ)を回避(かいひ)する: to avoid a collision/clash
  • 責任(せきにん)回避(かいひ): avoidance of responsibility
