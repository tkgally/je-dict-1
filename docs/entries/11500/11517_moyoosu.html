1. to hold, to host, to organize
To plan and carry out an event, gathering, or ceremony.
パーティーを催(もよお)す。
Hold a party.
学校(がっこう)で文化祭(ぶんかさい)が催(もよお)された。
A cultural festival was held at the school.
地域(ちいき)の住民(じゅうみん)が協力(きょうりょく)して夏祭(なつまつ)りを催(もよお)した。
The local residents worked together to hold a summer festival.
2. to feel, to experience (a physical or emotional urge)
To feel a bodily sensation or emotional impulse such as drowsiness, nausea, or the urge to cry.
眠気(ねむけ)を催(もよお)す。
Feel drowsy.
吐(は)き気(け)を催(もよお)して横(よこ)になった。
I felt nauseous and lay down.
その映画(えいが)を見(み)ていたら涙(なみだ)を催(もよお)した。
Watching that movie brought me to tears.

催(もよお)す has two distinct meanings: organizing events, and feeling involuntary urges or sensations.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)

SENSE 1 — HOLDING EVENTS:

More formal than 開(ひら)く or やる. Common in written and official contexts.

  • パーティーを催(もよお)す: to hold a party
  • 催(もよお)しを開(ひら)く: to hold an event
  • 催(もよお)し物(もの): event, function (noun form)

SENSE 2 — FEELING URGES:

Describes involuntary physical or emotional sensations.

  • 眠気(ねむけ)を催(もよお)す: to feel drowsy
  • 吐(は)き気(け)を催(もよお)す: to feel nauseous
  • 涙(なみだ)を催(もよお)す: to be moved to tears
  • 尿意(にょうい)を催(もよお)す: to feel the urge to urinate

RELATED NOUN:
催(もよお)し means "event" or "function" and is commonly seen in announcements and listings.

REGISTER:
Both senses are somewhat formal. For events, 開(ひら)く or 行(おこな)う is more common in everyday speech. For sensations, 感(かん)じる is the everyday equivalent.
