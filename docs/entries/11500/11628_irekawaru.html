入(い)れ替(か)わる

いれかわる
godan verb (intransitive)
to switch places; to be replaced
1. to switch places; to change places; to alternate
Two or more things or people swap positions or roles. Implies a mutual exchange where one goes out and another comes in.
選手(せんしゅ)が入(い)れ替(か)わった。
The players switched.
電車(でんしゃ)で降(お)りる人(ひと)と乗(の)る人(ひと)が入(い)れ替(か)わる。
People getting off and people getting on the train switch places.
二人(ふたり)の心(こころ)と体(からだ)が入(い)れ替(か)わるという物語(ものがたり)だ。
It's a story about two people whose minds and bodies switch.
2. to be replaced; to turn over; to be renewed
Members or contents of a group are gradually replaced by new ones over time.
メンバーが入(い)れ替(か)わった。
The members were replaced.
客(きゃく)が次々(つぎつぎ)と入(い)れ替(か)わる人気(にんき)店(てん)だ。
It's a popular restaurant where customers constantly turn over.
季節(きせつ)が入(い)れ替(か)わるたびに新(あたら)しいメニューが出(で)る。
New menu items come out every time the seasons change.

An intransitive verb. The transitive counterpart is 入(い)れ替(か)える (to replace, to swap). The compound 入(い)れ替(か)わり立(た)ち替(か)わり means one after another, in constant succession.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • AとBが入(い)れ替(か)わる — A and B switch places
  • 入(い)れ替(か)わり立(た)ち替(か)わり — one after another; in succession
  • メンバーが入(い)れ替(か)わる — the members are replaced
