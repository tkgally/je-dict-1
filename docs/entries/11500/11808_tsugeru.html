告(つ)げる

つげる
verb (ichidan)
to tell; to inform; to announce
1. to tell; to inform; to announce
To communicate important information or news to someone. Has a somewhat formal or literary tone and often implies delivering significant or weighty news.
別(わか)れを告(つ)げる。
To say goodbye.
医者(いしゃ)が患者(かんじゃ)に結果(けっか)を告(つ)げた。
The doctor told the patient the results.
彼(かれ)は覚悟(かくご)を決(き)めて、家族(かぞく)に真実(しんじつ)を告(つ)げた。
Having made up his mind, he told his family the truth.
2. to signal; to herald
Used figuratively when something signals or marks the beginning or end of a period, season, or event.
鐘(かね)の音(おと)が正午(しょうご)を告(つ)げた。
The bell signaled noon.
桜(さくら)が春(はる)の訪(おとず)れを告(つ)げる。
The cherry blossoms herald the arrival of spring.
試合(しあい)終了(しゅうりょう)のホイッスルが鳴(な)り、長(なが)いシーズンの終(お)わりを告(つ)げた。
The final whistle blew, signaling the end of the long season.

More literary and formal than 言(い)う or 伝(つた)える. Often used in set phrases and for emotionally significant or weighty communication. The expression 別(わか)れを告(つ)げる (to bid farewell) is particularly common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 別(わか)れを告(つ)げる: to bid farewell
  • 真実(しんじつ)を告(つ)げる: to tell the truth
  • 終(お)わりを告(つ)げる: to signal the end
  • 春(はる)を告(つ)げる: to herald spring
