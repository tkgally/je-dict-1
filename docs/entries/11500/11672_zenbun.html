全文(ぜんぶん)

ぜんぶん
noun
full text, complete text
1. full text, complete text
The entire content of a written document, speech, law, or other text, as opposed to a summary or excerpt.
スピーチの全文(ぜんぶん)を読(よ)む。
Read the full text of the speech.
記事(きじ)の全文(ぜんぶん)は有料(ゆうりょう)会員(かいいん)のみ閲覧(えつらん)できる。
The full text of the article can only be viewed by paid members.
憲法(けんぽう)の全文(ぜんぶん)が掲載(けいさい)されているサイトを見(み)つけた。
I found a site that has the complete text of the constitution posted.

Often used with documents, articles, speeches, and legal texts. Frequently seen in online media where only part of an article is shown for free.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全文(ぜんぶん)を掲載(けいさい)する (publish the full text)
  • 全文(ぜんぶん)を公開(こうかい)する (release the full text)
  • 全文(ぜんぶん)検索(けんさく) (full-text search)
  • 全文(ぜんぶん)表示(ひょうじ) (display full text)

DISTINCTIONS:

  • 全文(ぜんぶん): the complete text of a single document
  • 全集(ぜんしゅう): complete/collected works (of an author)
