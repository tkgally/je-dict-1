1. processing; manufacturing
The act of working on raw materials to change their form or properties, such as processing food, machining metal, or editing images.
木材(もくざい)を加工(かこう)する。
To process lumber.
この工場(こうじょう)では金属(きんぞく)の加工(かこう)を行(おこな)っている。
This factory carries out metal processing.
写真(しゃしん)を加工(かこう)してSNSに投稿(とうこう)する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
More and more people are editing photos and posting them on social media.

加工(かこう) means to process or work on raw materials to change their form, quality, or properties. It covers a wide range of activities from industrial manufacturing to digital editing.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Pattern: [material]を加工(かこう)する

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食品(しょくひん)加工(かこう): food processing
  • 加工(かこう)品(ひん): processed product (commonly seen on food labels)
  • 画像(がぞう)加工(かこう): image editing/retouching
  • 金属(きんぞく)加工(かこう): metalworking
  • 木材(もくざい)加工(かこう): woodworking
  • 加工(かこう)食品(しょくひん): processed food

MODERN USAGE:
画像(がぞう)加工(かこう) (photo editing/retouching) is increasingly common, especially for social media. 加工(かこう)アプリ refers to photo editing apps.
