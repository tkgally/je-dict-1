創設(そうせつ)

そうせつ
noun, verb (suru)
establishment, founding, creation
1. establishment, founding, creation
The act of creating or founding an organization, institution, or system for the first time. Emphasizes the initial creation from nothing, rather than reorganizing something existing.
新(あたら)しい学部(がくぶ)を創設(そうせつ)した。
A new faculty was established.
この会社(かいしゃ)は1950年(ねん)に創設(そうせつ)された。
This company was founded in 1950.
政府(せいふ)は新(あたら)しい研究機関(けんきゅうきかん)の創設(そうせつ)を決定(けってい)した。
The government decided to establish a new research institution.

Used for significant, formal acts of founding — typically organizations, institutions, or systems. Not used for casually starting small projects.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 設立(せつりつ) — establishing (focuses on the formal, legal process)
  • 創立(そうりつ) — founding (similar to 創設(そうせつ) but often used for schools and companies)
  • 創設(そうせつ) — emphasizes creating something entirely new

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 創設(そうせつ)する (to establish)
  • 創設(そうせつ)される (to be established)
  • 〜の創設(そうせつ) (the founding of ~)
