件名(けんめい)

けんめい
noun
subject line, title
1. subject line, title, heading
The subject line of an email or the title of a document or case. Most commonly encountered in email and business correspondence contexts.
メールの件名(けんめい)を入力(にゅうりょく)してください。
Please enter the email subject line.
件名(けんめい)に会議(かいぎ)の日付(ひづけ)を入(い)れておいた。
I put the meeting date in the subject line.
件名(けんめい)が空白(くうはく)のメールは迷惑(めいわく)メールと間違(まちが)われることがある。
Emails with a blank subject line are sometimes mistaken for spam.

Primarily used in the context of email, where it refers to the subject field. Also used in business and administrative contexts for the title or heading of a case or matter. In Japanese business culture, clear and concise subject lines are considered important for professional correspondence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • メールの件名(けんめい) (email subject line)
  • 件名(けんめい)を入力(にゅうりょく)する (enter the subject line)
  • 件名(けんめい)を変更(へんこう)する (change the subject line)
  • 件名(けんめい)欄(らん) (subject field)
