努(つと)める

つとめる
verb (ichidan, intransitive)
to strive, to endeavor
1. to strive, to endeavor, to make an effort
To consciously try hard to achieve something or maintain a certain state.
冷静(れいせい)で居(い)るように努(つと)めた。
I tried to stay calm.
健康(けんこう)管理(かんり)に努(つと)めている。
I'm making an effort to manage my health.
社員(しゃいん)一人(ひとり)ひとりがサービスの向上(こうじょう)に努(つと)めなければならない。
Every employee must strive to improve service.

Distinguished from the homophones 勤(つと)める (to work at, to be employed) and 務(つと)める (to serve in a role, to perform a duty). 努(つと)める specifically means to exert oneself or make a deliberate effort.

Typically takes the pattern ～に努(つと)める or ～ように努(つと)める.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 改善(かいぜん)に努(つと)める (strive for improvement)
  • 向上(こうじょう)に努(つと)める (strive for betterment)
  • 解決(かいけつ)に努(つと)める (work toward a resolution)
  • 平静(へいせい)を保(たも)つように努(つと)める (try to stay composed)
