効果音(こうかおん)

こうかおん
noun
sound effect
1. sound effect
An artificially created or recorded sound used in film, television, theater, radio, video games, and other media to enhance the production. Includes everything from footsteps and explosions to ambient background noise.
効果音(こうかおん)を付(つ)ける。
I add sound effects.
この映画(えいが)は効果音(こうかおん)がリアルだ。
The sound effects in this movie are realistic.
ゲームの効果音(こうかおん)を消(け)して音楽(おんがく)だけにした。
I turned off the game's sound effects and left only the music.

Often abbreviated as SE (エスイー(えすいー), from the English "sound effect") in media production contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 効果音(こうかおん)を付(つ)ける (to add sound effects)
  • 効果音(こうかおん)を入(い)れる (to put in sound effects)
  • 効果音(こうかおん)を消(け)す (to turn off sound effects)
  • 効果音(こうかおん)を作(つく)る (to create sound effects)
