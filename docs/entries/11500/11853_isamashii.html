勇(いさ)ましい

いさましい
i-adjective
brave, valiant
1. brave, valiant, gallant
Showing courage and spirited determination, often with a bold or heroic quality.
勇(いさ)ましい姿(すがた)だった。
It was a gallant figure.
兵士(へいし)たちは勇(いさ)ましく戦(たたか)った。
The soldiers fought bravely.
太鼓(たいこ)の勇(いさ)ましい音(おと)が祭(まつ)りの会場(かいじょう)に響(ひび)き渡(わた)った。
The stirring sound of drums echoed throughout the festival grounds.

Often describes people, actions, or sounds that evoke a spirited, bold, or heroic impression. Can also describe sounds like drums or marches that feel rousing and energetic.

CONJUGATION:

  • 勇(いさ)ましくない (negative)
  • 勇(いさ)ましく (adverbial form)
  • 勇(いさ)ましかった (past)
  • 勇(いさ)ましさ (noun form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勇(いさ)ましい姿(すがた) (brave/gallant figure)
  • 勇(いさ)ましい声(こえ) (bold voice)
  • 勇(いさ)ましく立(た)ち向(む)かう (stand up bravely against)
