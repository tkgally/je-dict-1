付加価値(ふかかち)

ふかかち
noun
added value
1. added value, value added
Additional value created through processing, service, or enhancement. Used in business and economics for the extra worth added to a product or service beyond its raw cost.
付加価値(ふかかち)の高(たか)い商品(しょうひん)を開発(かいはつ)する。
We develop high-value-added products.
サービスに付加価値(ふかかち)を付(つ)けることが重要(じゅうよう)だ。
It's important to add value to services.
地域(ちいき)の素材(そざい)を使(つか)って付加価値(ふかかち)の高(たか)い製品(せいひん)を生(う)み出(だ)すことが、地方(ちほう)経済(けいざい)の活性化(かっせいか)につながる。
Creating high-value-added products using local materials leads to the revitalization of regional economies.

Compound of 付加(ふか) (addition) + 価値(かち) (value). A key term in business and economics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 付加価値(ふかかち)の高(たか)い (high-value-added)
  • 付加価値(ふかかち)を付(つ)ける (to add value)
  • 付加価値(ふかかち)を生(う)む (to generate added value)
  • 付加価値(ふかかち)税(ぜい) (value-added tax, VAT)

USAGE NOTES:
付加価値(ふかかち)税(ぜい) is the formal term for VAT. In Japan, the equivalent is 消費(しょうひ)税(ぜい) (consumption tax), though 付加価値(ふかかち)税(ぜい) is used when discussing tax systems internationally.
