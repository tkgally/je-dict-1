原料(げんりょう)

げんりょう
noun
raw materials; ingredients
1. raw materials; ingredients
The basic materials from which a product is manufactured or processed. Refers to materials that undergo significant transformation in the production process.
ビールの原料(げんりょう)は麦(むぎ)とホップだ。
The raw materials of beer are barley and hops.
原料(げんりょう)の価格(かかく)が上(あ)がっている。
The prices of raw materials are rising.
この化粧品(けしょうひん)は天然(てんねん)の原料(げんりょう)だけを使(つか)って作(つく)られている。
This cosmetic product is made using only natural ingredients.

Distinguished from 材料(ざいりょう), which can refer to materials that keep their form in the finished product (like wood in furniture), while 原料(げんりょう) typically refers to materials that are chemically or physically transformed (like grapes into wine). Also distinguished from 素材(そざい), which emphasizes the inherent qualities of a material.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原料(げんりょう)を輸入(ゆにゅう)する: to import raw materials
  • 天然(てんねん)原料(げんりょう): natural ingredients
  • 原料(げんりょう)価格(かかく): raw material prices
  • 原料(げんりょう)不足(ぶそく): shortage of raw materials
