即座(そくざ)

そくざ
noun
immediately, on the spot, instantly
1. immediately, on the spot, instantly
Without any delay or hesitation; right away. Most commonly used in the adverbial form 即座(そくざ)に.
即座(そくざ)に返事(へんじ)をした。
I replied immediately.
彼(かれ)は質問(しつもん)に即座(そくざ)に答(こた)えた。
He answered the question on the spot.
問題(もんだい)が発覚(はっかく)し、即座(そくざ)に対策(たいさく)が取(と)られた。
The problem was discovered, and countermeasures were immediately taken.

Almost always used in the adverbial form 即座(そくざ)に. Conveys a sense of decisiveness and lack of hesitation. More formal than すぐに.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即座(そくざ)に対応(たいおう)する — to respond immediately
  • 即座(そくざ)に判断(はんだん)する — to judge instantly
  • 即座(そくざ)に答(こた)える — to answer on the spot
  • 即座(そくざ)に行動(こうどう)する — to act immediately

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すぐに — right away (everyday)
  • 直(ただ)ちに — immediately (formal, often for orders/instructions)
  • 瞬時(しゅんじ)に — in an instant (emphasizes speed)
