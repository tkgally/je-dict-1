品揃(しなぞろ)え

しなぞろえ
noun
product lineup, assortment
1. product lineup, assortment, selection of goods
The range and variety of products available at a store or business. Used to evaluate or describe how well-stocked or diverse a shop's offerings are.
この店(みせ)は品揃(しなぞろ)えがいい。
This store has a good selection.
品揃(しなぞろ)えが豊富(ほうふ)なスーパーが近所(きんじょ)にある。
There's a supermarket with a wide selection near my house.
季節(きせつ)ごとに品揃(しなぞろ)えを変(か)えることで、客(きゃく)を飽(あ)きさせない工夫(くふう)をしている。
By changing the product lineup with each season, they keep customers from getting bored.

A compound of 品(しな) (goods) + 揃(そろ)える (to arrange, to prepare). Commonly used when talking about shopping and retail. The quality of a store's 品揃(しなぞろ)え is a key factor in consumer reviews.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 品揃(しなぞろ)えがいい — to have a good selection
  • 品揃(しなぞろ)えが豊富(ほうふ) — to have a rich assortment
  • 品揃(しなぞろ)えを充実(じゅうじつ)させる — to enrich the product lineup
  • 品揃(しなぞろ)えが悪(わる)い — to have a poor selection
