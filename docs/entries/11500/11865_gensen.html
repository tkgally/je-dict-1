厳選(げんせん)

げんせん
noun, suru verb
careful selection, handpicking
1. careful selection, handpicking, strict selection
The act of selecting with great care and strict standards. Commonly used in marketing and advertising to emphasize quality.
厳選(げんせん)された素材(そざい)を使(つか)っている。
We use carefully selected materials.
全国(ぜんこく)から厳選(げんせん)した食材(しょくざい)を取(と)り寄(よ)せた。
We sourced carefully selected ingredients from across the country.
百(ひゃく)を超(こ)える応募(おうぼ)の中(なか)から厳選(げんせん)された十作品(じっさくひん)が展示(てんじ)された。
Ten works carefully selected from over a hundred entries were exhibited.

Also functions as a する verb (厳選(げんせん)する). Very common in advertising, restaurant descriptions, and product marketing to convey premium quality. The past participle form 厳選(げんせん)された is especially frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 厳選(げんせん)された素材(そざい) — carefully selected materials
  • 厳選(げんせん)食材(しょくざい) — handpicked ingredients
  • 厳選(げんせん)した商品(しょうひん) — carefully chosen products

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 選(えら)び抜(ぬ)く — to select with care (native Japanese equivalent)
  • 精選(せいせん) — careful selection (less common)
