ピックアップ

ぴっくあっぷ
noun / suru verb
pickup; selection, highlighting
1. selection, highlighting, featuring
The act of selecting or highlighting specific items, people, or topics from a larger group. Commonly used in media, marketing, and recommendations.
今週(こんしゅう)のピックアップ商品(しょうひん)はこちらです。
Here are this week's featured products.
注目(ちゅうもく)のニュースをピックアップした。
I picked out noteworthy news stories.
編集部(へんしゅうぶ)が厳選(げんせん)した記事(きじ)をピックアップしてお届(とど)けします。
We bring you articles carefully selected by our editorial team.
2. pickup, collecting someone
Going to pick up or collect a person, especially by car. Also used for pickup trucks.
空港(くうこう)までピックアップしてもらった。
Someone picked me up at the airport.
ホテルのピックアップサービスを利用(りよう)した。
I used the hotel's pickup service.
駅(えき)までピックアップに行(い)くから、着(つ)いたら連絡(れんらく)して。
I'll come pick you up at the station, so let me know when you arrive.

Borrowed from English "pick up." The selection/featuring sense is the more common usage in Japanese media and marketing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ピックアップする (to pick out, to highlight)
  • ピックアップ商品(しょうひん) (featured product)
  • ピックアップ記事(きじ) (featured article)
  • ピックアップトラック (pickup truck)

USAGE NOTES:
In Japanese, the "selection/featuring" sense is used more frequently than in English. Websites, shops, and media regularly use ピックアップ to mean "selected recommendations" or "editor's picks." The physical "pick someone up" sense is also common but less dominant.
