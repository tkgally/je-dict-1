作(つく)り上(あ)げる

つくりあげる
verb (ichidan)
to build up, to fabricate
1. to build up, to create, to complete
To create or construct something through sustained effort, bringing it to completion. Emphasizes the process and achievement of finishing.
会社(かいしゃ)をゼロから作(つく)り上(あ)げた。
I built the company from scratch.
十年(じゅうねん)かけて独自(どくじ)のブランドを作(つく)り上(あ)げた。
I spent ten years building up a unique brand.
選手(せんしゅ)たちが一丸(いちがん)となって強(つよ)いチームを作(つく)り上(あ)げた。
The players united to build a strong team.
2. to fabricate, to make up, to concoct
To invent or fabricate something that is not true, such as a story, image, or false scenario.
話(はなし)を作(つく)り上(あ)げるな。
Don't make up stories.
メディアが事件(じけん)を大(おお)げさに作(つく)り上(あ)げた。
The media fabricated the incident to seem bigger than it was.
犯人(はんにん)に仕立(した)て上(あ)げるために証拠(しょうこ)を作(つく)り上(あ)げた疑(うたが)いがある。
There is suspicion that evidence was fabricated in order to frame someone as the culprit.

A compound verb from 作(つく)る (to make) + 上(あ)げる (to raise, complete). The 上(あ)げる component adds the nuance of completion or finishing. An ichidan verb. In sense 1, it conveys positive effort and achievement. In sense 2, it has a negative connotation of deception.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゼロから作(つく)り上(あ)げる (build from scratch)
  • 時間(じかん)をかけて作(つく)り上(あ)げる (build up over time)
  • 話(はなし)を作(つく)り上(あ)げる (fabricate a story)
  • 証拠(しょうこ)を作(つく)り上(あ)げる (fabricate evidence)
