新商品(しんしょうひん)

しんしょうひん
noun
new product, new merchandise
1. new product, new merchandise
A product that has been recently developed or released for sale.
新商品(しんしょうひん)が発売(はつばい)された。
A new product was released.
来月(らいげつ)、新商品(しんしょうひん)の説明会(せつめいかい)がある。
There's a new product presentation next month.
毎年(まいとし)春(はる)になると、コンビニには新商品(しんしょうひん)がたくさん並(なら)ぶ。
Every spring, convenience stores line up with lots of new products.

新商品(しんしょうひん) combines 新(しん) (new) + 商品(しょうひん) (product/merchandise). It refers to a recently developed or released product.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)を開発(かいはつ)する: to develop a new product
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)を発売(はつばい)する: to release a new product
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)の紹介(しょうかい): introduction of a new product
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)が出(で)る: a new product comes out
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん)を試(ため)す: to try a new product

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 新製品(しんせいひん): new product (emphasizes manufacturing; used for electronics, industrial products)
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん): new product (emphasizes commerce; used for retail, food, consumer goods)

USAGE:
Very common in business, retail, and marketing contexts. Convenience stores and food companies frequently promote 新商品(しんしょうひん).
