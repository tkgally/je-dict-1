合流(ごうりゅう)

ごうりゅう
noun, verb (suru)
joining, merging, confluence
1. confluence, merging (of rivers, roads)
The point where rivers, roads, or traffic lanes come together and merge into one.
ここで二(ふた)つの川(かわ)が合流(ごうりゅう)する。
Two rivers merge here.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)の合流(ごうりゅう)地点(ちてん)は事故(じこ)が多(おお)い。
There are many accidents at highway merging points.
渋滞(じゅうたい)の原因(げんいん)は合流(ごうりゅう)レーンの混雑(こんざつ)だった。
The cause of the traffic jam was congestion at the merging lane.
2. joining up, meeting up, linking up
People or groups coming together to join forces, meet at a point, or unite for a common purpose.
駅前(えきまえ)で友人(ゆうじん)と合流(ごうりゅう)した。
I met up with a friend in front of the station.
別々(べつべつ)に出発(しゅっぱつ)して現地(げんち)で合流(ごうりゅう)しよう。
Let's leave separately and meet up at the destination.
二(ふた)つのチームが合流(ごうりゅう)して、大(おお)きなプロジェクトに取(と)り組(く)むことになった。
The two teams joined forces and will work on a large project together.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 川(かわ)が合流(ごうりゅう)する (rivers merge)
  • 合流(ごうりゅう)地点(ちてん) (merging point, confluence)
  • 〜と合流(ごうりゅう)する (join up with ~)
  • 合流(ごうりゅう)レーン (merging lane)

The physical sense (sense 1) is most commonly used with rivers and roads/traffic. The figurative sense (sense 2) is very common in everyday conversation for meeting up with people.
