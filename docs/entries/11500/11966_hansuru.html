反(はん)する

はんする
verb (suru, intransitive)
to be contrary to; to violate; to go against
1. to be contrary to, to contradict, to go against
To be in opposition to a principle, expectation, or standard. Used with に to mark the thing being contradicted.
予想(よそう)に反(はん)する結果(けっか)だった。
The result was contrary to expectations.
常識(じょうしき)に反(はん)する行動(こうどう)を取(と)った。
He took action that went against common sense.
彼(かれ)の意見(いけん)は科学的(かがくてき)な事実(じじつ)に反(はん)している。
His opinion contradicts scientific fact.
2. to violate, to act against
To break or act contrary to a rule, law, or agreement. Common in legal and formal contexts.
法律(ほうりつ)に反(はん)する。
To violate the law.
契約(けいやく)に反(はん)する行為(こうい)は許(ゆる)されない。
Acts that violate the contract are not permitted.
規則(きそく)に反(はん)した場合(ばあい)は処分(しょぶん)の対象(たいしょう)になる。
If you violate the rules, you will be subject to disciplinary action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予想(よそう)に反(はん)する (contrary to expectations)
  • 法律(ほうりつ)に反(はん)する (violate the law)
  • 規則(きそく)に反(はん)する (violate the rules)
  • 常識(じょうしき)に反(はん)する (go against common sense)
  • 意(い)に反(はん)する (against one's will)

USAGE NOTES:
Always takes the particle に to mark what is being contradicted or violated. The pattern ～に反(はん)して is commonly used as a connective phrase meaning "contrary to ~." For example, 予想(よそう)に反(はん)して (contrary to expectations). The noun form 反(はん)する is a する verb but is almost always intransitive.
