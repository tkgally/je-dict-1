1. to add, to append, to supplement
To include additional information, items, or elements to something already existing.
もう一言(ひとこと)付(つ)け加(くわ)える。
Let me add one more word.
説明(せつめい)に具体例(ぐたいれい)を付(つ)け加(くわ)えた。
I added concrete examples to the explanation.
最後(さいご)に一(ひと)つだけ付(つ)け加(くわ)えさせていただきます。
Allow me to add just one more thing at the end.

A compound verb from 付(つ)ける (to attach) + 加(くわ)える (to add). Means to supplement or append something to what already exists.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を for the thing being added

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一言(ひとこと)付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add a word/remark
  • 補足(ほそく)を付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add a supplement
  • 情報(じょうほう)を付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add information
  • 条件(じょうけん)を付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add a condition
  • 説明(せつめい)を付(つ)け加(くわ)える: to add an explanation

USAGE:
Commonly used in speeches, meetings, and writing when supplementing a point. The polite form 付(つ)け加(くわ)えさせていただきます (allow me to add) is standard in formal settings.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 加(くわ)える: to add (simpler, broader)
  • 追加(ついか)する: to add, to append (more formal, often for data/items)
