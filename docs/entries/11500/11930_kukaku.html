区画(くかく)

くかく
noun, verb (suru)
block, section, lot, partition
1. block, section, lot, plot of land, partition
A defined section or division of land, space, or area that has been marked off for a specific purpose. Used for land parcels, parking spaces, city blocks, and other partitioned areas.
駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)の区画(くかく)に車(くるま)を停(と)めた。
I parked my car in a parking space.
住宅地(じゅうたくち)が整然(せいぜん)と区画(くかく)されている。
The residential area is neatly divided into lots.
再開発(さいかいはつ)で区画(くかく)整理(せいり)が行(おこな)われ、道(みち)が広(ひろ)くなった。
Through redevelopment and land readjustment, the roads became wider.

Can function as both a noun (a section/lot) and a suru-verb (to partition/divide into sections).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 区画(くかく)整理(せいり) (land readjustment — urban planning term)
  • 駐車(ちゅうしゃ)区画(くかく) (parking space/bay)
  • 区画(くかく)を分(わ)ける (to divide into sections)
  • 区画(くかく)する (to partition, to section off)
