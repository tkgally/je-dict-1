リアクション

りあくしょん
noun
reaction; response
1. reaction; response; feedback
A visible or audible response to something, especially an expressive or animated reaction. In Japanese, particularly associated with exaggerated physical responses seen in comedy and variety TV shows.
リアクションが薄(うす)いね。
Your reaction is weak, huh.
彼(かれ)のリアクションがおもしろくてみんな笑(わら)った。
Everyone laughed because his reaction was funny.
SNSに投稿(とうこう)したら、予想(よそう)より大(おお)きなリアクションがあった。
When I posted on social media, the reaction was bigger than I expected.

From English "reaction," but in Japanese usage, リアクション often specifically refers to animated, visible, or physical responses — especially the kind of exaggerated reactions prized in Japanese comedy and variety shows. Comedians known for physical reactions are called リアクション芸人(げいにん).

The native Japanese word 反応(はんのう) covers the broader meaning of "reaction" in both scientific and everyday contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • リアクションが大(おお)きい — to have a big reaction
  • リアクションが薄(うす)い — to have a weak/subdued reaction
  • リアクション芸人(げいにん) — a comedian known for physical comedy
  • リアクションに困(こま)る — to not know how to react
