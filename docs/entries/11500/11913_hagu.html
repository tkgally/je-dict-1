剥(は)ぐ

はぐ
verb (godan, transitive)
to strip, to peel off, to tear off
1. to strip off, to peel off, to tear off
To forcefully remove something that covers or clings to a surface — such as skin, bark, clothing, paint, or a mask. Implies a rougher, more forceful action than simply peeling.
皮(かわ)を剥(は)いで食(た)べる。
I peel off the skin and eat it.
古(ふる)い壁紙(かべがみ)を剥(は)いでから新(あたら)しいのを貼(は)った。
I put up new wallpaper after stripping off the old one.
泥棒(どろぼう)は被害者(ひがいしゃ)の服(ふく)を剥(は)いで逃(に)げた。
The thief stripped the victim of their clothes and fled.

Conjugates as a godan verb: 剥(は)がない, 剥(は)いで, 剥(は)いだ. The action is more forceful and rough compared to 剥(む)く (to peel gently, as with fruit).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 剥(は)ぐ — to strip/tear off (forceful removal of a covering)
  • 剥(む)く — to peel (gentler; fruit, vegetables)
  • 剥(は)がす — to peel off, to remove (stickers, labels, tape)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 皮(かわ)を剥(は)ぐ (to strip off skin/bark)
  • 服(ふく)を剥(は)ぐ (to strip someone of clothes)
  • 仮面(かめん)を剥(は)ぐ (to tear off a mask — also figurative)
