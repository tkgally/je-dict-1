即席(そくせき)

そくせき
noun
instant, impromptu, offhand
1. instant, impromptu, on-the-spot
Something done or prepared immediately without advance planning. Used both for physical products (instant food) and for actions done spontaneously (impromptu speeches, offhand performances).
即席(そくせき)ラーメンを食(た)べた。
I ate instant ramen.
即席(そくせき)のスピーチを頼(たの)まれた。
I was asked to give an impromptu speech.
残(のこ)り物(もの)で即席(そくせき)の料理(りょうり)を作(つく)ったが、意外(いがい)と美味(おい)しかった。
I made an impromptu dish from leftovers, and it turned out surprisingly good.

Functions as a noun modifier using の: 即席(そくせき)の～. Very commonly associated with instant food products, especially 即席(そくせき)めん (instant noodles).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即席(そくせき)めん — instant noodles
  • 即席(そくせき)ラーメン — instant ramen
  • 即席(そくせき)の演奏(えんそう) — impromptu performance
  • 即席(そくせき)で作(つく)る — to make on the spot
