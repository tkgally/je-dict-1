出品(しゅっぴん)

しゅっぴん
noun / suru verb
exhibiting, putting up for sale
1. exhibiting, displaying (at an exhibition or show)
Submitting or displaying an item at an exhibition, trade show, or contest.
美術展(びじゅつてん)に出品(しゅっぴん)した。
I exhibited at the art exhibition.
今年(ことし)のコンテストに作品(さくひん)を出品(しゅっぴん)する予定(よてい)だ。
I plan to submit a work to this year's contest.
国際(こくさい)見本市(みほんいち)に新製品(しんせいひん)を出品(しゅっぴん)して注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めた。
We exhibited a new product at the international trade fair and attracted attention.
2. listing for sale, putting up for auction
Putting an item up for sale, especially on an online marketplace or at an auction.
フリマサイトに出品(しゅっぴん)した。
I listed it on a flea market site.
不要(ふよう)な服(ふく)をオークションに出品(しゅっぴん)した。
I put unwanted clothes up for auction.
出品(しゅっぴん)してから一(いち)時間(じかん)で売(う)れたので驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised it sold within an hour of listing it.

Compound of 出(しゅつ) (to put out) + 品(ひん) (goods, item). The word covers both formal exhibition contexts and everyday online selling.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出品(しゅっぴん)する (to exhibit, to list for sale)
  • 出品(しゅっぴん)者(しゃ) (exhibitor, seller)
  • 出品(しゅっぴん)物(ぶつ) (exhibited item, listed item)
  • 出品(しゅっぴん)作品(さくひん) (submitted work)
  • 〜に出品(しゅっぴん)する (to exhibit at / list on)

USAGE NOTES:
In modern Japanese, 出品(しゅっぴん) is frequently used for online marketplaces like メルカリ and ヤフオク in addition to its traditional meaning of exhibiting at shows or galleries.
