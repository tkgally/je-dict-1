制裁(せいさい)

せいさい
noun, verb (suru)
sanction, punishment
1. sanction, punishment, penalty
A punitive measure imposed for violating rules, laws, or norms. Used both for international sanctions between countries and for punishments imposed by organizations or groups.
経済(けいざい)制裁(せいさい)を発動(はつどう)する。
Impose economic sanctions.
国連(こくれん)は新(あら)たな制裁(せいさい)を決議(けつぎ)した。
The United Nations passed a resolution on new sanctions.
ルール違反(いはん)をした選手(せんしゅ)にはチームから厳(きび)しい制裁(せいさい)が加(くわ)えられた。
The player who violated the rules was given a harsh punishment by the team.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経済(けいざい)制裁(せいさい) (economic sanctions)
  • 制裁(せいさい)を科(か)す (impose sanctions)
  • 制裁(せいさい)を加(くわ)える (inflict punishment)
  • 制裁(せいさい)措置(そち) (punitive measures)
  • 制裁(せいさい)解除(かいじょ) (lifting of sanctions)

In news reporting, 制裁(せいさい) most commonly refers to international diplomatic or economic sanctions. In everyday usage, it can also mean punishment by an authority figure or organization.
