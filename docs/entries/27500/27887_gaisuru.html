1. to harm; to injure; to damage; to hurt (feelings, health)
To cause harm or damage to something, especially health, feelings, or interests. Common in formal or written contexts and in fixed expressions.
健康(けんこう)を害(がい)する。
It harms your health.
そんな言(い)い方(かた)では相手(あいて)の気分(きぶん)を害(がい)する。
Putting it that way will offend the other person.
過度(かど)の飲酒(いんしゅ)は健康(けんこう)を害(がい)するおそれがある。
Excessive drinking can damage your health.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)); what is harmed is marked with を.
  • Pattern: [thing]を害(がい)する

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 健康(けんこう)を害(がい)する: to damage one's health
  • 気分(きぶん)を害(がい)する: to offend; to put in a bad mood
  • 感情(かんじょう)を害(がい)する: to hurt someone's feelings
  • 利益(りえき)を害(がい)する: to harm someone's interests

REGISTER: Formal and somewhat literary; in casual speech 傷(きず)つける (to hurt) or 悪(わる)くする is more common.
