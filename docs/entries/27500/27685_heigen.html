平原(へいげん)

へいげん
noun
plain; open field; flatland
1. a broad, flat expanse of land
A large area of flat, open land with few trees or hills, often covered with grass.
広(ひろ)い平原(へいげん)が続(つづ)く。
A wide plain stretches on.
馬(うま)が平原(へいげん)を走(はし)っている。
Horses are running across the plain.
地平線(ちへいせん)まで平原(へいげん)が広(ひろ)がっていた。
The plain spread out all the way to the horizon.

A broad, flat stretch of open land. A somewhat literary or geographic word; in everyday conversation 野原(のはら) (field) is more common for smaller open areas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 広大(こうだい)な平原(へいげん): a vast plain
  • 平原(へいげん)が広(ひろ)がる: a plain spreads out

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 平野(へいや): plain — a geographic term for a low flat region, often named (such as 関東(かんとう)平野(へいや))
  • 草原(そうげん): grassland — a plain specifically covered with grass
  • 野原(のはら): field — a smaller, more familiar open area
