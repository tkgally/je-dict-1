1. being detached from ordinary worldly life
The quality of seeming removed from everyday practical concerns such as money, work, or common sense, as if living in a different world. Can be admiring or mildly critical.
かれは少(すこ)し浮世離(うきよばな)れしている。
He is a little detached from reality.
浮世離(うきよばな)れした雰囲気(ふんいき)の人(ひと)だ。
She is a person with an otherworldly air.
山奥(やまおく)で暮(く)らすうちに、浮世離(うきよばな)れした感覚(かんかく)になってきた。
Living deep in the mountains, I began to feel detached from the everyday world.

Composed of 浮世(うきよ) (the fleeting, mundane world) + 離(はな)れ (separation). Most often used as 浮世離(うきよばな)れした before a noun, or with する. Can express admiration (serene, unmaterialistic) or mild criticism (out of touch, impractical).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 浮世離(うきよばな)れした人(ひと): an unworldly person
  • 浮世離(うきよばな)れした雰囲気(ふんいき): an otherworldly atmosphere
  • 浮世離(うきよばな)れした生活(せいかつ): a life detached from worldly concerns

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 世間知(せけんし)らず: naive about the world — purely negative; implies ignorance of how society works
  • 現実離(げんじつばな)れ: detachment from reality — emphasizes being unrealistic rather than serene
