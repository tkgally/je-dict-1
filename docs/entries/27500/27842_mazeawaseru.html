1. to mix together; to blend; to combine
To stir or fold two or more substances together so they form a single uniform mixture. The 合(あ)わせる element stresses bringing the parts together into one.
材料(ざいりょう)を混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる。
Mix the ingredients together.
二(ふた)つの色(いろ)の絵(え)の具(ぐ)を混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせた。
I blended two colors of paint together.
卵(たまご)と砂糖(さとう)をよく混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせてから、小麦粉(こむぎこ)を加(くわ)える。
Blend the eggs and sugar well, then add the flour.

Compound of 混(ま)ぜる (to mix) + 合(あ)わせる (to put together). Common in cooking and craft instructions for combining multiple ingredients into one.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 材料(ざいりょう)を混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる: to combine the ingredients
  • よく混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる: to mix thoroughly
  • 均一(きんいつ)に混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる: to blend evenly

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 混(ま)ぜる: to mix — simpler; 混(ま)ぜ合(あ)わせる emphasizes blending several things into one uniform whole
