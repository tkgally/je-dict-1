ママチャリ

ままちゃり
noun
mama bike; utility bicycle with a basket
1. mama bike; utility bicycle (with basket and child seat)
A practical city bicycle with a low frame, a front basket, and often one or two child seats. The default bike type for everyday shopping, school runs, and short errands in Japan.
ママチャリで買(か)い物(もの)に行(い)く。
I'll go shopping on the mama-bike.
前(まえ)と後(うし)ろにかごのついたママチャリを買(か)った。
I bought a mama-bike with baskets in front and back.
保育園(ほいくえん)の送(おく)り迎(むか)えには電動(でんどう)アシスト付(つ)きのママチャリが便利(べんり)だ。
For dropping off and picking up at daycare, an electric-assist mama-bike is very convenient.

Casual compound of ママ (mom) + チャリ (bike, slang for 自転車(じてんしゃ)). Despite the name, used by anyone — students, men, grandparents — not only mothers.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 電動(でんどう)ママチャリ: electric-assist mama-bike
  • ママチャリに乗(の)る: to ride a mama-bike
  • ママチャリで送(おく)り迎(むか)え: to do school / daycare runs by mama-bike
  • ママチャリの前(まえ)かご: the front basket of a mama-bike

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
The ママチャリ is a Japanese icon: cheap, sturdy, three-speed at most, and built for short flat-city trips. Common features include a frame low enough to step through in a skirt, a basket large enough for grocery bags, a wheel lock, and front/rear child seats. Distinguishes itself sharply from sports bikes (ロードバイク}) and folding bikes ({折(お)り畳(たた)み自転車(じてんしゃ)).
