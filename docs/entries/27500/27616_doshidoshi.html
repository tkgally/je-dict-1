どしどし

どしどし
adverb
without hesitation; freely; one after another
1. without hesitation; freely; go ahead and ~
Encourages someone to do something boldly and without holding back. Often used in invitations to submit, apply, or contact freely. Common in announcements and customer-facing text.
ご意見(いけん)をどしどしお寄(よ)せください。
Please send us your opinions freely.
質問(しつもん)があればどしどし聞(き)いてください。
If you have questions, go ahead and ask without hesitation.
応募(おうぼ)方法(ほうほう)は下記(かき)の通(とお)りです。皆様(みなさま)どしどしご応募(おうぼ)ください。
Application instructions are as follows. We welcome applications from everyone, so please apply freely.
2. one after another; in rapid succession; thumping (footsteps)
Describes things happening or arriving one after another in quick succession, or originally the heavy thumping sound of footsteps. The 'in succession' meaning is the more common modern use.
注文(ちゅうもん)がどしどし入(はい)ってきた。
Orders kept coming in one after another.
大(おお)きな足音(あしおと)がどしどしと近(ちか)づいてきた。
Heavy footsteps came thumping closer.
セールが始(はじ)まると、客(きゃく)がどしどし店内(てんない)に入(はい)ってきた。
When the sale started, customers came pouring into the store one after another.

An onomatopoeic adverb. Often used with or without と. Especially common in sense 1 as a friendly invitation to act without holding back.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • どしどしお送(おく)りください: please send freely
  • どしどしご応募(おうぼ)ください: please apply without hesitation
  • どしどし進(すす)める: to push ahead briskly
  • どしどし売(う)れる: to sell briskly (one after another)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どんどん: rapidly; briskly — overlapping meaning; どんどん is more general, どしどし has a heavier, weightier feel
  • ばんばん: vigorously, energetically — emphasizes vigor; more colloquial

Related Words

Synonym: どんどん (less emphatic)
