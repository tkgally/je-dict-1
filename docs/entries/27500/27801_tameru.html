1. to save up; to put aside (money, points)
To accumulate money — or, by extension, points and savings — by setting it aside little by little.
お金(かね)を貯(た)める。
To save money.
旅行(りょこう)のために毎月(まいつき)貯(た)めている。
I'm saving up every month for a trip.
ポイントを貯(た)めて商品(しょうひん)と交換(こうかん)した。
I saved up points and exchanged them for a product.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive ichidan verb. Xを貯(た)める. The intransitive pair is 貯(た)まる (to accumulate).

Written with 貯(た) specifically for saving money or points. The homophone 溜(た)める covers accumulating things in general — water, stress, unfinished work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お金(かね)を貯(た)める: to save money
  • 貯金(ちょきん)を貯(た)める: to build up savings
  • ポイントを貯(た)める: to collect points

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 貯金(ちょきん)する: to save money — noun + する; emphasizes the act of depositing
  • 溜(た)める: to accumulate — general accumulation, not specifically money
