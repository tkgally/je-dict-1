マスキングテープ

ますきんぐてーぷ
noun
masking tape; washi tape
1. masking tape; decorative washi tape
Easily peelable paper tape. Besides its original use in painting, decorative versions (often shortened to マスキングテープ／マステ) are popular in Japan for crafts and journaling.
マスキングテープで飾(かざ)る。
Decorate it with masking tape.
かわいいマスキングテープを集(あつ)めている。
I collect cute masking tape.
壁(かべ)を塗(ぬ)る前(まえ)に、窓枠(まどわく)にマスキングテープを貼(は)っておいた。
Before painting the wall, I put masking tape along the window frames.

From English "masking tape," commonly clipped to マステ. In Japan it has become a craft item in its own right, with countless colors and patterns of 和紙(わし)-based decorative tape.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • マスキングテープを貼(は)る: to apply masking tape
  • マスキングテープで留(と)める: to fasten lightly with masking tape

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • セロテープ: cellophane tape — clear and meant to hold permanently
  • ガムテープ: packing tape — thick and strong, hard to remove cleanly
