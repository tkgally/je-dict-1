県庁(けんちょう)

けんちょう
noun
prefectural government office
1. prefectural government office
The central administrative office of a prefecture, and by extension the prefectural government as an organization.
県庁(けんちょう)に勤(つと)めている。
I work at the prefectural government office.
その手続(てつづ)きは県庁(けんちょう)でできる。
You can do that procedure at the prefectural office.
県庁(けんちょう)所在地(しょざいち)は、その県(けん)で最(もっと)も人口(じんこう)が多(おお)い都市(とし)とは限(かぎ)らない。
The city where the prefectural office is located is not necessarily the most populous city in the prefecture.

The administrative headquarters of a 県(けん) (prefecture). Can refer to the building itself or to the prefectural government as an organization.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 県庁(けんちょう)所在地(しょざいち): the prefectural capital (city where the office sits)
  • 県庁(けんちょう)に勤(つと)める: to work at the prefectural office
  • 県庁(けんちょう)前(まえ): in front of the prefectural office (a common place name)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 市役所(しやくしょ): city hall — the equivalent office at the municipal level
  • 都庁(とちょう): Tokyo Metropolitan Government office — the equivalent for 東京都(とうきょうと)
