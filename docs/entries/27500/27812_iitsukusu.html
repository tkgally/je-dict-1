1. to say everything; to express fully; to exhaust words
To express something completely, leaving nothing unsaid. Most often used in the negative potential form, describing feelings too great to put fully into words.
感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちは言(い)い尽(つ)くせない。
I cannot fully express my gratitude.
短(みじか)い手紙(てがみ)では思(おも)いを言(い)い尽(つ)くせなかった。
I couldn't say all I felt in a short letter.
彼女(かのじょ)の素晴(すば)らしさは、どんな言葉(ことば)を使(つか)っても言(い)い尽(つ)くせない。
No matter what words I use, I can't fully express how wonderful she is.

Compound of 言(い)う (to say) + 尽(つ)くす (to exhaust, to use up completely). Most often appears in the negative potential form 言(い)い尽(つ)くせない, describing feelings too deep to put fully into words.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 言(い)い尽(つ)くせない思(おも)い: feelings beyond words
  • 感謝(かんしゃ)を言(い)い尽(つ)くす: to express one's full gratitude
  • 魅力(みりょく)を言(い)い尽(つ)くす: to describe every charm

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 語(かた)り尽(つ)くす: to tell everything — focuses on narrating or discussing at length
  • 表現(ひょうげん)する: to express — neutral and general, without the nuance of completeness
