1. to be imminent; to draw near; to press close
Of a deadline, the year's end, or an event: to be close at hand and pressing. Conveys a sense of mounting urgency as the time approaches.
年末(ねんまつ)が押(お)し迫(せま)ってきた。
The end of the year is fast approaching.
締(し)め切(き)りが押(お)し迫(せま)っている。
The deadline is looming.
試験(しけん)が押(お)し迫(せま)り、毎晩(まいばん)遅(おそ)くまで勉強(べんきょう)している。
With the exam fast approaching, I study late every night.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)); the approaching thing is the subject (が).

ASPECT:

  • Most often used in the ている / てくる forms (押(お)し迫(せま)っている, 押(お)し迫(せま)ってきた) to express that the time is now pressing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年末(ねんまつ)が押(お)し迫(せま)る: the year-end is drawing near
  • 締(し)め切(き)りが押(お)し迫(せま)る: the deadline is looming
  • 期限(きげん)が押(お)し迫(せま)る: the time limit is approaching
