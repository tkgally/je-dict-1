治療法(ちりょうほう)

ちりょうほう
noun
treatment method; cure; therapy
1. treatment method; therapy; cure
A method of treating an illness or condition. Covers medical procedures, drug regimens, and other approaches to curing or managing a disease.
新(あたら)しい治療法(ちりょうほう)を試(ため)す。
We try a new treatment method.
この病気(びょうき)にはまだ治療法(ちりょうほう)がない。
There is still no cure for this disease.
医師(いし)と相談(そうだん)して、自分(じぶん)に合(あ)った治療法(ちりょうほう)を選(えら)ぶことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to consult a doctor and choose a treatment method that suits you.

Compound of 治療(ちりょう) (treatment) + 法(ほう) (method). The 〜法(ほう) suffix forms many "method of ~" words.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 治療法(ちりょうほう)を見(み)つける: to find a cure
  • 治療法(ちりょうほう)が確立(かくりつ)される: a treatment is established

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 治療(ちりょう): treatment — the act of treating, not the method itself
  • 療法(りょうほう): therapy — often used in compounds like 食事(しょくじ)療法(りょうほう) (diet therapy)
