いじめっ子(こ)

いじめっこ
noun
bully (especially a child); kid who picks on others
1. bully; child who picks on others
A child or young person who habitually bullies, teases, or torments others. Most often used of children, but can be extended to teenagers.
クラスにいじめっ子(こ)がいる。
There's a bully in the class.
彼(かれ)は小学校(しょうがっこう)の時(とき)、いじめっ子(こ)だった。
He was a bully back in elementary school.
いじめっ子(こ)に立(た)ち向(む)かう勇気(ゆうき)を持(も)つことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to have the courage to stand up to bullies.

From the verb いじめる (to bully) + the suffix 〜っ子(こ) (kid who is ~). The 〜っ子(こ) suffix produces colloquial labels for children based on a characteristic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • いじめっ子(こ)に遭(あ)う: to encounter a bully
  • いじめっ子(こ)と戦(たたか)う: to fight back against a bully
  • いじめっ子(こ)グループ: a group of bullies

SIMILAR 〜っ子(こ) WORDS:

  • 泣(な)き虫(むし): crybaby — uses 虫(むし) suffix instead, but similar role
  • 甘(あま)えん坊(ぼう): spoiled child

ANTONYM:

  • いじめられっ子(こ): victim of bullying — formed with the passive いじめられる + っ子(こ)
