1. slippery; easy to slip on
Describes a surface or condition where one is likely to slip. Compound of the verb 滑(すべ)る (to slip) + やすい (easy to ~). Used for floors, roads, soap, and any other surface where slipping is a risk.
床(ゆか)が滑(すべ)りやすい。
The floor is slippery.
雨(あめ)の日(ひ)は階段(かいだん)が滑(すべ)りやすいので注意(ちゅうい)してください。
On rainy days the stairs get slippery, so please be careful.
凍(こお)った道(みち)は滑(すべ)りやすく、歩(ある)くのも大変(たいへん)だった。
The frozen road was slippery, and even walking was difficult.

Compound of 滑(すべ)る (to slip) + 〜やすい (easy to / prone to). The 〜やすい suffix attaches to verb stems to indicate that the action happens easily or often.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 床(ゆか)が滑(すべ)りやすい: the floor is slippery
  • 道(みち)が滑(すべ)りやすい: the road is slippery
  • 雨(あめ)で滑(すべ)りやすい: slippery from the rain
  • 滑(すべ)りやすいので注意(ちゅうい): be careful, it's slippery (warning sign)

ANTONYM:

  • 滑(すべ)りにくい: hard to slip on; non-slip (using the opposite suffix 〜にくい)
