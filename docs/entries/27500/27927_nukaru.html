1. to become muddy; to be miry
Of ground or a road, to turn soft and muddy so that feet sink in, typically after rain or snowmelt.
道(みち)がぬかっている。
The road is muddy.
雨(あめ)で庭(にわ)がぬかってしまった。
The garden has gotten muddy from the rain.
雪(ゆき)どけで足元(あしもと)がぬかっているので気(き)をつけて。
The ground underfoot is muddy from the melting snow, so be careful.

Used of ground or roads turning soft and muddy after rain or melting snow. Despite ending in る, it is a godan verb (ぬから・ない, ぬかっ・て).

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive. The subject is the ground that becomes muddy: 道(みち)がぬかる.

ASPECT:

  • ぬかっている describes the resulting muddy state ('is muddy'), not an action in progress.

RELATED WORDS:

  • ぬかるみ: a muddy spot; mire — the noun form, naming the muddy ground itself
