1. to sever; to cut off; to break off; to give up
To deliberately end or break off something ongoing — a relationship, a supply, a route, or a habit. Often implies a decisive, final break.
酒(さけ)を断(た)つことにした。
I decided to give up alcohol.
彼(かれ)とは連絡(れんらく)を断(た)ったままだ。
I have cut off all contact with him.
敵(てき)の補給路(ほきゅうろ)を断(た)つことが作戦(さくせん)の鍵(かぎ)だった。
Cutting off the enemy's supply route was the key to the operation.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)); what is severed is marked with を.
  • Pattern: [thing]を断(た)つ

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 連絡(れんらく)を断(た)つ: to cut off contact
  • 関係(かんけい)を断(た)つ: to break off a relationship
  • 酒(さけ)を断(た)つ: to give up drinking
  • 命(いのち)を断(た)つ: to take one's own life

NOTE: Written 断(た)つ for severing a connection or habit. The homophone 絶(た)つ (to end, to exterminate) overlaps closely; 裁(た)つ means to cut cloth, and 立(た)つ (00758) means to stand.
