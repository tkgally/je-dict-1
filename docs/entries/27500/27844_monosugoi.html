1. tremendous; terrific; incredible; intense
Describes something striking in scale, force, or degree — so much so that it is almost overwhelming. Can be positive or negative depending on context.
物凄(ものすご)い人(ひと)出(で)だ。
There's a tremendous crowd.
外(そと)は物凄(ものすご)い雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っている。
It's raining incredibly hard outside.
彼(かれ)は物凄(ものすご)いスピードで仕事(しごと)を片(かた)づけていった。
He cleared through the work at an incredible speed.

An intensifying adjective stressing great scale or degree. Frequently written in hiragana (ものすごい). The adverbial 物凄(ものすご)く is extremely common as a casual intensifier meaning 'very'.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 物凄(ものすご)い勢(いきお)い: tremendous momentum
  • 物凄(ものすご)く暑(あつ)い: incredibly hot
  • 物凄(ものすご)い数(かず): a huge number

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すごい: amazing; great — milder and more everyday; ものすごい is a stronger intensification
