サービス料(りょう)

さーびすりょう
noun
service charge
1. service charge
A percentage added to a bill at restaurants, hotels, and bars to cover service. Common in higher-end establishments and often shown as a fixed rate such as 10%.
サービス料(りょう)が別(べつ)にかかります。
A service charge applies separately.
このホテルはサービス料(りょう)が含(ふく)まれている。
At this hotel the service charge is included.
料金(りょうきん)にはサービス料(りょう)と消費税(しょうひぜい)が加(くわ)わります。
A service charge and consumption tax are added to the price.

Compound of サービス (service) + 料(りょう) (fee, charge). Japan has no tipping custom, so a service charge added to the bill plays a similar role at upscale venues.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • サービス料(りょう)込(こ)み: service charge included
  • サービス料(りょう)別(べつ): service charge not included
  • サービス料(りょう)がかかる: a service charge applies

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 消費税(しょうひぜい): consumption tax — a government tax, not a service fee
  • 手数料(てすうりょう): handling fee; commission — charged for processing, not service
