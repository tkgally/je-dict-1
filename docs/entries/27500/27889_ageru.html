1. to cite; to name; to give (as an example); to raise (a hand)
To put something forward for attention — to name or enumerate an example, reason, or candidate, or to raise one's hand or voice.
例(れい)を挙(あ)げる。
To give an example.
賛成(さんせい)の人(ひと)は手(て)を挙(あ)げてください。
Those in favor, please raise your hands.
候補(こうほ)として三人(さんにん)の名前(なまえ)を挙(あ)げた。
She named three people as candidates.
2. to hold (a ceremony); to achieve; to produce (results)
To carry out a formal event such as a wedding, or to attain a concrete result, profit, or record through effort.
式(しき)を挙(あ)げる。
To hold a ceremony.
大(おお)きな成果(せいか)を挙(あ)げた。
We achieved significant results.
来年(らいねん)の春(はる)に結婚式(けっこんしき)を挙(あ)げる予定(よてい)です。
We plan to hold our wedding next spring.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Transitive (他動詞(たどうし)); the thing cited or held is marked with を.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 例(れい)を挙(あ)げる: to give an example
  • 手(て)を挙(あ)げる: to raise one's hand
  • 式(しき)を挙(あ)げる: to hold a ceremony
  • 成果(せいか)を挙(あ)げる: to produce results

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 上(あ)げる (02443): to raise physically, to give — the everyday "raise/lift" verb.
  • 揚(あ)げる (16879): to deep-fry; to hoist (a flag).

Write 挙(あ)げる specifically for citing/enumerating, holding a ceremony, or achieving results.
