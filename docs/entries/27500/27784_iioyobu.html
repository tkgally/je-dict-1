1. to mention; to go so far as to refer to
For one's remarks to extend to a particular topic in the course of speaking, especially to bring up something beyond the original subject.
話(はなし)が将来(しょうらい)のことに言(い)い及(およ)んだ。
The conversation came to touch on the future.
彼(かれ)は他人(たにん)の私生活(しせいかつ)にまで言(い)い及(およ)んだ。
He went so far as to comment on others' private lives.
報告書(ほうこくしょ)は原因(げんいん)だけでなく今後(こんご)の対策(たいさく)にも言(い)い及(およ)んでいる。
The report refers not only to the cause but also to future countermeasures.

TRANSITIVITY: Intransitive godan verb. Xに言(い)い及(およ)ぶ. Compound of 言(い)う (to say) + 及(およ)ぶ (to reach, to extend). Describes a discussion reaching or extending to a topic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 話(はなし)が〜に言(い)い及(およ)ぶ: for the talk to come around to ~
  • 〜にまで言(い)い及(およ)ぶ: to go so far as to mention ~

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 触(ふ)れる: to touch on — a lighter, brief mention; 言(い)い及(およ)ぶ implies the talk extends there
  • 言及(げんきゅう)する: to refer to — a more formal する verb with the same kanji
