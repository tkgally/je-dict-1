1. to take (measures); to devise (a plan, countermeasure)
To work out and put into effect a plan or countermeasure to deal with a problem. A formal verb used mostly with words like 対策(たいさく) or 措置(そち).
対策(たいさく)を講(こう)じる。
To take countermeasures.
政府(せいふ)は新(あら)たな措置(そち)を講(こう)じた。
The government took new measures.
被害(ひがい)が広(ひろ)がる前(まえ)に、何(なん)らかの手(て)を講(こう)じる必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to take some kind of action before the damage spreads.

A formal verb meaning to devise and carry out a plan. Almost always pairs with words for measures or steps. The variant 講(こう)ずる is identical in meaning and slightly more literary.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 対策(たいさく)を講(こう)じる: to take countermeasures
  • 措置(そち)を講(こう)じる: to take a measure
  • 手(て)を講(こう)じる: to take a step / do something about it
