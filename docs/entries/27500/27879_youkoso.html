ようこそ

ようこそ
expression
welcome
1. welcome
An expression of welcome said to someone who has come or arrived, equivalent to English 'welcome.'
ようこそ日本(にほん)へ。
Welcome to Japan.
ようこそ我(わ)が家(や)へ。
Welcome to our home.
店長(てんちょう)は「ようこそお越(こ)しくださいました」と深々(ふかぶか)と頭(あたま)を下(さ)げた。
The manager bowed deeply and said, 'Welcome, thank you for coming.'

An expression of welcome to a visitor or guest, frequently followed by a destination with へ: ようこそ〜へ ('welcome to ~').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ようこそ日本(にほん)へ: welcome to Japan
  • ようこそお越(こ)しくださいました: welcome, thank you for coming (polite)
  • ようこそいらっしゃいました: welcome (polite)

NUANCE:
Warmer and more personal than the routine shop greeting いらっしゃいませ, which is called out to any customer entering a store.
