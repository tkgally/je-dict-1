1. curve; bend
A non-straight line, or the curve of a road, river, or graph.
急(きゅう)なカーブが続(つづ)く。
Sharp curves continue.
道(みち)が大(おお)きくカーブしている。
The road curves widely.
グラフのカーブを見(み)れば、気温(きおん)の変化(へんか)が一目(ひとめ)でわかる。
Looking at the curve on the graph, you can see the temperature change at a glance.
2. curveball (baseball pitch)
In baseball, a pitch thrown with spin that makes the ball curve downward and to one side.
彼(かれ)のカーブはよく曲(ま)がる。
His curveball breaks sharply.
打者(だしゃ)はカーブを見送(みおく)った。
The batter let the curveball go by.
二(に)ストライクから投(な)げたカーブで三振(さんしん)に打(う)ち取(と)った。
With a curveball thrown on the two-strike count, he struck the batter out.

Loanword from English 'curve'. Used both for shapes/lines in everyday speech and as a baseball term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 急(きゅう)カーブ: sharp curve
  • カーブを曲(ま)がる: to go around a curve
  • カーブする: to curve (verb form)
  • カーブミラー: convex mirror at road curves
  • 学習(がくしゅう)カーブ: learning curve

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 曲線(きょくせん): curve (mathematical/abstract) — more formal, used for geometric curves
  • カーブ: everyday curves of roads, graphs, or pitches; also functions as a する verb
