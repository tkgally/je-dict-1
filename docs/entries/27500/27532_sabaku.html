1. to dress (fish or game); to fillet; to butcher
To cut up a fish, chicken, or other animal into edible parts — removing scales, bones, or organs. A standard kitchen / fishmonger skill.
魚(さかな)を捌(さば)く。
To dress a fish.
鶏肉(とりにく)を捌(さば)くのは初(はじ)めてだ。
It's my first time dressing a chicken.
板前(いたまえ)さんが目(め)の前(まえ)で大(おお)きなマグロを捌(さば)いてくれた。
The chef dressed a large tuna right in front of us.
2. to handle skillfully; to manage; to deal with
To deal smoothly with a large number of things — work, customers, tasks, traffic — without getting overwhelmed. Implies competence and speed.
お客(きゃく)さんをうまく捌(さば)く。
To handle customers smoothly.
仕事(しごと)が多(おお)くてとても捌(さば)ききれない。
There's so much work I can't possibly handle it all.
彼(かれ)は忙(いそが)しい時間帯(じかんたい)でも注文(ちゅうもん)を次々(つぎつぎ)と捌(さば)いていく。
Even during busy hours, he handles one order after another.

Transitive godan verb. Both senses share the core idea of 'cutting through and handling efficiently'. Note that 裁(さば)く (judge a case) and 砂漠(さばく) (desert) are unrelated homophones.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魚(さかな)を捌(さば)く: to dress / fillet a fish
  • 仕事(しごと)を捌(さば)く: to power through work
  • 在庫(ざいこ)を捌(さば)く: to sell off inventory
  • 交通(こうつう)を捌(さば)く: (police, etc.) to manage traffic flow

ASPECT:
捌(さば)いている often describes someone in the middle of efficiently handling work or customers, emphasizing the continuous, smooth flow of activity.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 処理(しょり)する: to process; to handle — more formal/neutral; lacks the 'skillful flow' nuance of 捌(さば)く
  • 対応(たいおう)する: to deal with — used for responding to situations / requests, more passive feel
