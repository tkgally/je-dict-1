1. to identify; to uncover; to bring to light
To find and surface previously hidden issues, causes, or items by examining something systematically. Common in business and technical contexts.
問題点(もんだいてん)を洗(あら)い出(だ)した。
We identified the issues.
原因(げんいん)を洗(あら)い出(だ)してから対策(たいさく)を考(かんが)えよう。
Let's identify the causes before considering countermeasures.
アンケートの結果(けっか)から、お客(きゃく)さんの不満(ふまん)を洗(あら)い出(だ)すことができた。
From the survey results, we were able to uncover customers' complaints.
2. to wash out; to reveal by washing
Literal sense: to wash something so that dirt, grain, or detail is brought out — e.g. washing rice, or washing wood to expose its grain.
米(こめ)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す。
Wash the rice clean.
木目(もくめ)を洗(あら)い出(だ)した床(ゆか)が美(うつく)しい。
The floor with its wood grain brought out by washing is beautiful.
砂(すな)を何度(なんど)も洗(あら)い出(だ)して、金(きん)の粒(つぶ)を探(さが)した。
We washed the sand repeatedly to look for gold flakes.

Compound of 洗(あら)う (to wash) + 出(だ)す (to bring out). Transitive verb, taking the object marker を.

The figurative sense (1) is by far the more common modern usage and appears constantly in business, IT, and analysis contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 問題(もんだい)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す: to identify problems
  • 原因(げんいん)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す: to pinpoint causes
  • 課題(かだい)を洗(あら)い出(だ)す: to surface issues to address
  • リスクを洗(あら)い出(だ)す: to enumerate risks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 見(み)つける: to find — broader; 洗(あら)い出(だ)す implies systematic, exhaustive enumeration
  • 特定(とくてい)する: to identify (specifically) — narrows down to one item, while 洗(あら)い出(だ)す lists everything found
