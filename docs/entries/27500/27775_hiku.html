1. to run over; to hit (with a vehicle)
For a vehicle to strike a person or animal and pass over them. Always refers to a vehicle causing injury or death.
車(くるま)が猫(ねこ)を轢(ひ)いた。
A car ran over a cat.
人(ひと)を轢(ひ)いてしまわないよう慎重(しんちょう)に運転(うんてん)する。
I drive carefully so as not to run anyone over.
道(みち)に飛(と)び出(だ)してきた子供(こども)を危(あや)うく轢(ひ)きそうになった。
I nearly ran over a child who darted out into the road.

TRANSITIVITY: Transitive godan verb. Xを轢(ひ)く. Always describes a vehicle hitting a person or animal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を轢(ひ)く: to run over a person
  • 車(くるま)に轢(ひ)かれる: to be hit by a car (passive)
  • 轢(ひ)き逃(に)げ: hit-and-run

USAGE NOTE:
轢(ひ)く shares its reading with 引(ひ)く (to pull), 弾(ひ)く (to play a stringed instrument), and 挽(ひ)く (to grind), but is written with its own kanji and used only for vehicle accidents.
