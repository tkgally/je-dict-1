医学生(いがくせい)

いがくせい
noun
medical student
1. medical student
A student enrolled in a faculty of medicine, training to become a doctor. The Japanese medical program is six years, so 医学生(いがくせい) typically spans first through sixth-year students.
弟(おとうと)は医学生(いがくせい)だ。
My younger brother is a medical student.
医学生(いがくせい)は試験(しけん)に追(お)われる毎日(まいにち)を送(おく)っている。
Medical students spend their days chased by exams.
六年生(ろくねんせい)の医学生(いがくせい)は病院(びょういん)で実習(じっしゅう)を行(おこな)う。
Sixth-year medical students do their clinical training at hospitals.

医学(いがく) (medicine) + 生(せい) (student). The 生(せい) suffix attaches to fields to form names for students of that field.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 医学生(いがくせい)になる: to become a medical student
  • 医学生(いがくせい)の実習(じっしゅう): medical-student clinical training
  • 医学生(いがくせい)時代(じだい): one's days as a medical student

STUDENT SUFFIXES (生(せい)):

  • 大学生(だいがくせい): university student
  • 高校生(こうこうせい): high-school student
  • 留学生(りゅうがくせい): international student
  • 歯学生(しがくせい): dental student

RELATED:

  • 研修医(けんしゅうい): medical resident (after graduation, in training)
  • 医師(いし): doctor, physician (licensed)
