1. barbecue; BBQ; outdoor grilling party
An outdoor meal where meat and vegetables are grilled over fire or charcoal. In Japan, often shortened to BBQ and treated more as a social activity than a cooking style.
週末(しゅうまつ)にバーベキューをしよう。
Let's have a barbecue this weekend.
川(かわ)沿(ぞ)いでバーベキューを楽(たの)しんだ。
We enjoyed a barbecue along the river.
雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきたので、バーベキューは屋根(やね)のあるところに移(うつ)した。
It started raining, so we moved the barbecue to a place with a roof.

Loanword from English 'barbecue'. Frequently abbreviated as BBQ in writing and casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • バーベキューをする: to have a barbecue
  • バーベキュー大会(たいかい): BBQ gathering / cookout event
  • バーベキューコンロ: BBQ grill (the equipment)
  • 手ぶら(てぶら)バーベキュー: 'empty-handed' BBQ (a venue provides all the equipment and food)

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
In Japan, バーベキュー typically refers to the social event itself rather than a cooking technique. Many parks, campgrounds, and rooftop venues offer rental setups, and gas-style griddles such as 鉄板(てっぱん) are often used alongside charcoal.
