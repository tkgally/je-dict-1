そういえば

そういえば
expression
speaking of which; come to think of it; that reminds me
1. speaking of which; come to think of it; that reminds me
A conversational connector used to introduce a topic that the speaker has just been reminded of, usually by something the other person said. Marks a slight shift to a related point.
そういえば、昨日(きのう)彼(かれ)に会(あ)ったよ。
Come to think of it, I ran into him yesterday.
そういえば、宿題(しゅくだい)出(だ)した?
By the way, did you turn in the homework?
そういえば、来週(らいしゅう)の会議(かいぎ)の場所(ばしょ)が変(か)わったって聞(き)いた気(き)がする。
Come to think of it, I think I heard the location of next week's meeting has changed.

Literally 'if (one) says so' — そう (so/that way) + 言(い)えば (if one says). Used when the current topic prompts the speaker to recall something related. The follow-up is usually loosely connected to what was just said.

GRAMMAR:

  • Sentence-initial only, followed by a comma.
  • Often paired with けど or んだけど before stating the new point.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • そういえば、昔(むかし)…: come to think of it, in the old days …
  • そういえば、聞(き)いた?: speaking of which, did you hear?
  • そういえば、そうだね: oh yeah, that's true (when reminded of something)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ところで: by the way (deliberate topic change, not necessarily reminded by context)
  • それはそうと: by the way; that aside (more deliberate shift away from current topic)
  • ちなみに: incidentally; for your information (adds related info)
