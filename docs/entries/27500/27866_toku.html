1. to comb out and smooth (hair); to disentangle
To run a comb through hair to remove tangles and smooth it. The reading とく is shared with the homophones 解(と)く (to undo) and 溶(と)く (to dissolve).
髪(かみ)を梳(と)く。
to comb one's hair.
寝癖(ねぐせ)をくしで梳(と)いた。
I combed out my bed-head with a comb.
美容師(びようし)さんが丁寧(ていねい)に髪(かみ)を梳(と)いて、もつれをほどいてくれた。
The hairdresser carefully combed my hair and undid the tangles.

To comb hair so as to smooth it and remove tangles, typically taking 髪(かみ) as its object.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive; the thing combed is marked with を: 髪(かみ)を梳(と)く.

NOTE ON KANJI:
The kanji 梳(と)く is uncommon, so the action is often written in kana as とく or expressed with the related verb 梳(と)かす (とかす, 'to comb').

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 梳(と)かす: to comb (hair) — closely related; とかす is the more common everyday verb
