1. to record; to tape (audio or video)
To capture sound or moving images, especially a TV program, onto a recording device or medium.
テレビ番組(ばんぐみ)を録(と)る。
To record a TV program.
見(み)られない時間(じかん)のドラマは録(と)っておく。
I record dramas that air at times I can't watch.
好(す)きな歌手(かしゅ)の音楽(おんがく)番組(ばんぐみ)を毎週(まいしゅう)録(と)っている。
I record my favorite singer's music program every week.

A specialized writing of とる used when the meaning is 'to record' audio or video. The plain 取(と)る is the general-purpose verb; 録(と)る narrows it specifically to recording.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 番組(ばんぐみ)を録(と)る: to record a program
  • 録(と)っておく: to record (for later)
  • ビデオに録(と)る: to record onto video

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 撮(と)る: to take (a photo); to film — used for photographs and shooting footage
  • 録音(ろくおん)する: to record sound — a more formal する verb, specifically for audio
  • 録画(ろくが)する: to record video — the formal する verb for video; more common in writing than 録(と)る
