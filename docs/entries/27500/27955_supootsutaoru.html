スポーツタオル

すぽーつたおる
noun
sports towel
1. sports towel; long thin towel for exercise
A long, narrow towel designed to be draped around the neck during sports or workouts. From English 'sports towel'.
首(くび)にスポーツタオルをかける。
I drape a sports towel around my neck.
ジムにスポーツタオルを持(も)っていく。
I take a sports towel to the gym.
応援(おうえん)グッズとして、チーム名(めい)入(い)りのスポーツタオルが売(う)られている。
Sports towels printed with the team name are sold as cheering goods.

A long, thin towel (longer than a face towel) meant to hang around the neck. Often sold with team logos or designs as fan merchandise at concerts and sporting events.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • タオル: towel — the general word
  • バスタオル: bath towel — large, for drying the body after bathing
