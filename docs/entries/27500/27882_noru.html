1. to appear in print; to be listed; to be carried (in a publication)
Of an article, photo, name, or piece of information: to appear in a newspaper, book, website, or other published medium. Also used of cargo or an object resting on or loaded onto something.
記事(きじ)が新聞(しんぶん)に載(の)った。
The article appeared in the newspaper.
その言葉(ことば)は辞書(じしょ)に載(の)っていますか。
Is that word in the dictionary?
ネットに載(の)っている情報(じょうほう)が正(ただ)しいとは限(かぎ)らない。
Information posted online is not necessarily correct.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし)); the transitive pair is 載(の)せる (to print, to put on).
  • Pattern: 情報(じょうほう)が[medium]に載(の)る

ASPECT:

  • 載(の)っている describes the resulting state — "is printed / is listed," not an ongoing action.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 新聞(しんぶん)に載(の)る: to appear in the newspaper
  • 雑誌(ざっし)に載(の)る: to be in a magazine
  • 地図(ちず)に載(の)っている: to be shown on the map

NOTE: Written 載(の)る for appearing in print or being loaded on; the homophone 乗(の)る (00511) is for boarding a vehicle.
