海沿(うみぞ)い

うみぞい
noun
along the coast; coastal; seaside
1. along the coast; coastal; seaside
Along or facing the sea — the strip of land running beside the coast. Often used attributively with の.
海沿(うみぞ)いの道(みち)をドライブした。
I drove along the coastal road.
この町(まち)は海沿(うみぞ)いにある。
This town is located along the coast.
海沿(うみぞ)いのホテルに泊(と)まって、窓(まど)から朝日(あさひ)を眺(なが)めた。
We stayed at a seaside hotel and watched the sunrise from the window.

Made of 海(うみ) (sea) + 沿(ぞ)い (along). Used attributively with の: 海沿(うみぞ)いの〜.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 海沿(うみぞ)いの道(みち): coastal road
  • 海沿(うみぞ)いの町(まち): seaside town
  • 海沿(うみぞ)いを走(はし)る: to run or drive along the coast

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 川沿(かわぞ)い: along a river — the same 〜沿(ぞ)い pattern
  • 海辺(うみべ): seaside; beach — the place itself, rather than 'running along' it
