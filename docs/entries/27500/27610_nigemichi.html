逃(に)げ道(みち)

にげみち
noun
escape route; way out
1. escape route; way out; escape path
A route or means by which one can escape danger or a difficult situation. Used both literally (a physical escape path) and figuratively (a way out of a problem or responsibility).
逃(に)げ道(みち)を探(さが)した。
I searched for an escape route.
火事(かじ)の時(とき)のために逃(に)げ道(みち)を確認(かくにん)しておこう。
Let's check the escape route in case of fire.
彼(かれ)はいつも自分(じぶん)の逃(に)げ道(みち)を用意(ようい)してから話(はな)し始(はじ)める。
He always prepares a way out for himself before he starts talking.

Compound of 逃(に)げる (to flee) + 道(みち) (path, way). Used in both literal and figurative senses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 逃(に)げ道(みち)を塞(ふさ)ぐ: to block the escape route
  • 逃(に)げ道(みち)を用意(ようい)する: to prepare a way out
  • 逃(に)げ道(みち)を失(うしな)う: to lose one's escape route
  • 逃(に)げ道(みち)がない: there's no way out

FIGURATIVE USAGE:
Frequently used metaphorically for a 'way out' of arguments, responsibilities, or commitments — leaving oneself a 逃(に)げ道(みち) means hedging one's position so retreat is possible.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 抜(ぬ)け道(みち): bypass; loophole — emphasizes a route that avoids the main path or rules
  • 出口(でぐち): exit — neutral; just the way out, not necessarily escape
