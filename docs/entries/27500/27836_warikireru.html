1. to be divisible (with no remainder)
Of a number, to divide evenly leaving no remainder.
六(ろく)は二(に)で割(わ)り切(き)れる。
Six is divisible by two.
この数(かず)は三(さん)で割(わ)り切(き)れない。
This number is not divisible by three.
奇数(きすう)は二(に)では割(わ)り切(き)れない数(かず)のことだ。
An odd number is a number that is not divisible by two.
2. to feel settled about; to come to terms with
To resolve one's feelings about something and accept it, leaving no lingering doubt or dissatisfaction. Often used in the negative to mean 'unable to fully accept'.
彼(かれ)の態度(たいど)には割(わ)り切(き)れない。
I can't quite come to terms with his attitude.
頭(あたま)では分(わ)かっても、気持(きも)ちが割(わ)り切(き)れない。
Even though I understand it in my head, I can't settle my feelings about it.
結果(けっか)には納得(なっとく)したが、どこか割(わ)り切(き)れない思(おも)いが残(のこ)った。
I accepted the result, but somewhere a feeling of dissatisfaction remained.

Potential/intransitive form of 割(わ)り切(き)る (to divide out; to resolve cleanly). Sense 1 is mathematical; sense 2 is emotional.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 二(に)で割(わ)り切(き)れる: to be divisible by two
  • 割(わ)り切(き)れない気持(きも)ち: an unresolved feeling
  • どうも割(わ)り切(き)れない: somehow can't accept it

NOTE:
Sense 2 most often appears in the negative (割(わ)り切(き)れない) to express a nagging dissatisfaction that reason alone cannot dispel.
