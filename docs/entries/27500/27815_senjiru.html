1. to decoct; to boil down; to brew (herbs, tea)
To extract the essence of herbs, medicinal plants, or tea leaves by simmering them in water. Associated with traditional herbal medicine and tea preparation.
薬草(やくそう)を煎(せん)じて飲(の)む。
I decoct medicinal herbs and drink them.
祖母(そぼ)は風邪(かぜ)のときに薬(くすり)を煎(せん)じてくれた。
My grandmother would brew medicine for me when I had a cold.
漢方薬(かんぽうやく)は、決(き)められた時間(じかん)だけ弱火(よわび)で煎(せん)じることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to decoct Chinese herbal medicine over low heat only for the prescribed time.

Refers to simmering herbs, medicinal plants, or tea in water to draw out their active components. Strongly associated with 漢方(かんぽう) (traditional Chinese herbal medicine).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 薬(くすり)を煎(せん)じる: to decoct medicine
  • 薬草(やくそう)を煎(せん)じる: to brew medicinal herbs
  • 煎(せん)じ薬(ぐすり): a decoction; herbal brew

The idiom 爪(つめ)の垢(あか)を煎(せん)じて飲(の)む means to imitate an admirable person, wishing one had their good qualities.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 煮出(にだ)す: to extract by boiling — close in meaning, used more for tea and broth
  • 淹(い)れる: to brew (tea, coffee) — for ordinary steeping, not medicinal decoction
