お願(ねが)いいたします

おねがいいたします
expression
please; if you would (very polite)
1. please; thank you in advance; if you would (very polite)
A very polite request expression formed by combining the humble verb いたす with the noun 願(ねが)い (request). Used to politely ask for help, action, or continued goodwill. More formal than the everyday お願(ねが)いします.
よろしくお願(ねが)いいたします。
I look forward to working with you (very polite).
ご確認(かくにん)のほどお願(ねが)いいたします。
I would appreciate your kind confirmation.
今後(こんご)とも変(か)わらぬお引(ひ)き立(た)てを賜(たまわ)りますよう、お願(ねが)いいたします。
I respectfully ask for your continued patronage.

Replaces します with the humble form いたします to lift the politeness of お願(ねが)いします a notch. Standard register for emails to clients, business letters, and formal customer service.

LEVELS OF "PLEASE":

  • 頼(たの)む: casual; among friends, between equals
  • お願(ねが)い: casual to neutral; everyday request
  • お願(ねが)いします: standard polite; ubiquitous
  • お願(ねが)いいたします: very polite; humble form, business and formal contexts
  • お願(ねが)い申(もう)し上(あ)げます: extremely polite; ceremonial speeches and formal letters

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • よろしくお願(ねが)いいたします: very polite "thank you in advance" / "nice to meet you"
  • 何卒(なにとぞ)お願(ねが)いいたします: "please, I humbly ask"
  • ご確認(かくにん)お願(ねが)いいたします: "please confirm" (in business email)

NOTE:
Very common as a closing line in business emails, often combined with 何卒(なにとぞ) (please, kindly) for added formality.
