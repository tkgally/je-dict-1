端(はし)っこ

はしっこ
noun
edge; corner; end (casual)
1. edge; corner; end; tip
Casual form of 端(はし) meaning 'edge' or 'end.' The っこ ending adds a familiar, slightly affectionate tone, often used in everyday conversation and with or by children.
パンの端(はし)っこが好(す)きだ。
I love the end pieces of the bread.
机(つくえ)の端(はし)っこに鍵(かぎ)を置(お)いておいた。
I left the key on the edge of the desk.
子供(こども)たちはソファの端(はし)っこに座(すわ)って絵本(えほん)を読(よ)んでいた。
The children were sitting at the edge of the sofa reading a picture book.

Casual variant of 端(はし) formed with the diminutive/familiar suffix っこ. It carries a friendlier, more concrete feel — useful in speech but generally not in formal writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 机(つくえ)の端(はし)っこ: the edge of the desk
  • パンの端(はし)っこ: the end (heel) of a loaf of bread
  • 端(はし)っこに座(すわ)る: to sit at the edge
  • 端(はし)っこを切(き)る: to cut off the end

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 端(はし): edge; end (neutral, all-purpose)
  • 隅(すみ): corner (interior corner of a room or space)
  • 先(さき): tip; point (the very end, often pointed)
