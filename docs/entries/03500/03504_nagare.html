流(なが)れ

ながれ
noun
flow, stream, current

Definitions

1. flow, stream, current
The movement of water, air, or other fluids. Can refer to rivers, streams, or any continuous movement.
2. course, progression, trend
The direction or progression of events, time, or abstract concepts.

Examples

川(かわ)の流(なが)れが速(はや)い。
The river current is fast.
時代(じだい)の流(なが)れに乗(の)る。
To ride the tide of the times.
Figurative usage
会話(かいわ)の流(なが)れを止(と)めないでください。
Please don't interrupt the flow of conversation.

Notes

USAGE: 流(なが)れ is the noun form of 流(なが)れる (to flow). It's used both literally (water, air) and figuratively (events, time, trends).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 川(かわ)の流(なが)れ (river current)
  • 時(とき)の流(なが)れ (passage of time)
  • 流(なが)れに逆(さか)らう (to go against the flow)
  • 流(なが)れに身(み)を任(まか)せる (to go with the flow)
  • 流(なが)れ作業(さぎょう) (assembly line work)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 流(なが)れ星(ぼし) (shooting star)
  • 流(なが)れ者(もの) (drifter, wanderer)
  • 血(ち)の流(なが)れ (bloodline, lineage)

Related Words

Related: 流(なが)れる (verb: to flow)
