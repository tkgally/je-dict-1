雨戸(あまど)

あまど
noun
storm shutter
1. storm shutter; sliding wooden shutters
External shutters on traditional Japanese houses that slide along tracks to protect windows from rain, storms, and intruders.
台風(たいふう)が来(く)るので雨戸(あまど)を閉(し)めた。
I closed the storm shutters because a typhoon is coming.
朝(あさ)になったら雨戸(あまど)を開(あ)ける。
Open the storm shutters when morning comes.
古(ふる)い家(いえ)には木製(もくせい)の雨戸(あまど)がある。
Old houses have wooden storm shutters.

CULTURAL NOTE: Traditional Japanese houses have 雨戸(あまど) that slide horizontally on tracks outside the windows. They are closed at night and during storms.

FUNCTIONS:

  • Protection from rain and wind
  • Security (closed at night)
  • Insulation (keeps cold out)
  • Privacy

MODERN VERSIONS:

  • Electric/motorized shutters
  • Roll-up shutters (シャッター)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨戸(あまど)を開(あ)ける (to open shutters)
  • 雨戸(あまど)を閉(し)める (to close shutters)
  • 雨戸(あまど)を立(た)てる (to put up shutters)
