像(ぞう)

ぞう
noun / suffix
statue, image

Definitions

1. statue, figure
A sculptural representation of a person, animal, or deity.
2. image, picture
A visual representation or reflection.

Examples

公園(こうえん)に銅(どう)像(ぞう)が建(た)っている。
A bronze statue stands in the park.
仏(ほとけ)像(ぞう)を拝(おが)む。
To worship a Buddha statue.
鏡(かがみ)に映(うつ)った自分(じぶん)の像(ぞう)を見(み)た。
I saw my own image reflected in the mirror.

Notes

Often used as a suffix to indicate the type of statue or image.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 銅(どう)像(ぞう) (bronze statue)
  • 石(せき)像(ぞう) (stone statue)
  • 仏(ぶつ)像(ぞう) / 仏(ほとけ)像(ぞう) (Buddha statue)
  • 彫(ちょう)像(ぞう) (carved statue, sculpture)
  • 胸(きょう)像(ぞう) (bust)
  • 映(えい)像(ぞう) (image, video)
  • 画(が)像(ぞう) (image, picture)
  • 想(そう)像(ぞう) (imagination)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 象(ぞう) (elephant)
