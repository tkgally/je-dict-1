秤(はかり)

はかり
noun
scales, weighing machine
1. scales, weighing machine, balance
A device for measuring weight.
秤(はかり)で野菜(やさい)の重(おも)さを量(はか)る。
Weigh the vegetables on the scales.
キッチン秤(はかり)で小麦粉(こむぎこ)を200グラム量(はか)った。
I measured 200 grams of flour on the kitchen scale.
昔(むかし)の市場(いちば)では、商品(しょうひん)の重(おも)さを秤(はかり)で量(はか)っていた。
In old markets, goods were weighed using scales.

秤(はかり) is the general term for a weighing device — any type of scale or balance. Also sometimes written as はかり in hiragana or 計(はかり).

TYPES OF SCALES:

  • キッチン秤(はかり): kitchen scale
  • デジタル秤(はかり): digital scale
  • 台秤(だいばかり): platform scale
  • 天秤(てんびん): balance scale (also figurative: weighing options)
  • 体重計(たいじゅうけい): bathroom/body weight scale (uses 計(けい) not 秤(はかり))

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 量(はか)る / 計(はか)る: to measure, to weigh
  • 重(おも)さ: weight
  • 計量(けいりょう): measurement

NOTE:
The kanji 秤(はかり) is not a common-use kanji (常用漢字(じょうようかんじ)), so it is often written in hiragana. In stores, 量(はか)り売(う)り means "sold by weight."
