理想(りそう)

りそう
noun
ideal

Definitions

1. ideal, vision
A conception of something in its most perfect form; a standard of excellence to aspire to.

Examples

理想(りそう)と現実(げんじつ)は違(ちが)う。
Ideals and reality are different.
Common expression
理想(りそう)の家(いえ)を見(み)つけた。
I found my ideal house.
高(たか)い理想(りそう)を持(も)って生(い)きる。
To live with high ideals.

Notes

USAGE: 理想(りそう) refers to an ideal or vision of perfection. It's often contrasted with 現実(げんじつ) (reality).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 理想(りそう)の〜 (ideal ~)
  • 理想(りそう)と現実(げんじつ) (ideals and reality)
  • 理想(りそう)を追(お)う (to pursue ideals)
  • 理想(りそう)を掲(かか)げる (to hold up ideals)
  • 理想(りそう)的(てき) (ideal - な-adjective)

CONTRAST:

  • 現実(げんじつ) - reality
  • 現実(げんじつ)的(てき) - realistic
