友好(ゆうこう)

ゆうこう
noun
friendship, amity

Definitions

1. friendship, amity, friendly relations
A state of mutual goodwill and cooperation, especially between nations, organizations, or groups.

Examples

両国(りょうこく)の友好(ゆうこう)関係(かんけい)を深(ふか)めたい。
We want to deepen the friendly relations between the two countries.
友好(ゆうこう)条約(じょうやく)が結(むす)ばれた。
A friendship treaty was concluded.
友好(ゆうこう)的(てき)な態度(たいど)で接(せっ)した。
I treated them with a friendly attitude.
友好的 = friendly (な-adjective form)

Notes

USAGE: 友好(ゆうこう) is typically used in formal contexts, especially in diplomacy and international relations. For personal friendships, 友情(ゆうじょう) is more common.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 友好(ゆうこう)関係(かんけい) (friendly relations)
  • 友好(ゆうこう)条約(じょうやく) (friendship treaty)
  • 友好(ゆうこう)都市(とし) (sister city)
  • 友好(ゆうこう)的(てき) (friendly - な-adjective)

NOTE: Do not confuse with 有効(ゆうこう) (valid, effective), which has the same reading.
