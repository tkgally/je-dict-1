風景(ふうけい)

ふうけい
noun
scenery, landscape
1. scenery, landscape, view
The natural features of an area that can be seen, especially when considered in terms of their aesthetic appeal.
山(やま)の風景(ふうけい)が美(うつく)しい。
The mountain scenery is beautiful.
窓(まど)から見(み)える風景(ふうけい)を写真(しゃしん)に撮(と)った。
I took a photo of the scenery visible from the window.
風景画(ふうけいが)を描(か)くのが趣味(しゅみ)です。
My hobby is painting landscapes.
風景画 = landscape painting

USAGE: 風景(ふうけい) typically refers to natural scenery or landscapes, often with an aesthetic connotation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 風景(ふうけい)を眺(なが)める (to gaze at the scenery)
  • 風景画(ふうけいが) (landscape painting)
  • 風景(ふうけい)写真(しゃしん) (landscape photography)
  • 美(うつく)しい風景(ふうけい) (beautiful scenery)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 景色(けしき) - scenery (more general, can include urban views)
  • 眺(なが)め - view (what one sees from a particular spot)
  • 光景(こうけい) - scene (often dramatic or memorable)
