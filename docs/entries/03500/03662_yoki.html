予期(よき)

よき
noun
expectation, anticipation
1. expectation, anticipation
The act of expecting or anticipating something to happen.
予期(よき)しない事態(じたい)が起(お)こった。
An unexpected situation occurred.
予期しない = unexpected
予期(よき)していた通(とお)りの結果(けっか)だった。
The result was as expected.
予期していた通り = as expected
予期(よき)せぬ出来事(できごと)に驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by an unforeseen event.
予期せぬ = unforeseen, unexpected

USAGE: 予期(よき) is often used with する to form a verb, and commonly appears in negative forms like 予期(よき)しない (unexpected) or 予期(よき)せぬ (unforeseen).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予期(よき)する (to expect, anticipate)
  • 予期(よき)しない (unexpected)
  • 予期(よき)せぬ (unforeseen - literary)
  • 予期(よき)に反(はん)して (contrary to expectations)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予想(よそう) - expectation, prediction
  • 予測(よそく) - forecast, prediction
  • 期待(きたい) - expectation, hope
