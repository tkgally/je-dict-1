後(のち)

のち
noun
later, afterwards
1. later, afterwards, subsequently
A point in time after the present or after a reference point.
雨(あめ)の後(のち)晴(は)れるでしょう。
It will probably clear up after the rain.
〜のち = after ~
卒業(そつぎょう)後(のち)、海外(かいがい)に行(い)った。
After graduation, I went abroad.
2. future, later time
The time to come; future circumstances.
後(のち)のことは考(かんが)えていない。
I haven't thought about what comes later.

USAGE: 後(のち) is a more formal/literary way to say 'after' or 'later' compared to 後(あと). It's commonly used in weather forecasts and formal writing.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜の後(のち) (after ~)
  • 後(のち)に (later, subsequently)
  • 後(のち)ほど (later - polite)
  • 後(のち)の世(よ) (future generations, posterity)

WEATHER EXPRESSIONS:

  • 雨(あめ)後(のち)曇(くも)り (rain, then cloudy)
  • 晴(は)れ後(のち)雨(あめ) (clear, then rain)

CONTRAST:

  • 後(のち) - afterwards (formal/literary)
  • 後(あと) - after, later (everyday)
  • 以後(いご) - after, since (formal)
  • その後(ご) - after that
