爆発(ばくはつ)

ばくはつ
noun, suru-verb
explosion, outburst
1. explosion, blast, detonation
A sudden, violent release of energy, typically with a loud noise and destruction.
ガス爆発(ばくはつ)で建物(たてもの)が壊(こわ)れた。
A gas explosion destroyed the building.
2. outburst, eruption
A sudden, intense expression of emotion or energy; a rapid increase.
人口(じんこう)爆発(ばくはつ)が問題(もんだい)になっている。
Population explosion has become a problem.
Figurative usage
怒(いか)りが爆発(ばくはつ)した。
His anger exploded.
Emotional outburst

USAGE: 爆発(ばくはつ) is used for literal explosions and figuratively for sudden outbursts or rapid increases.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 爆発(ばくはつ)する (to explode)
  • 爆発(ばくはつ)を起(お)こす (to cause an explosion)
  • 爆発(ばくはつ)的(てき) (explosive)
  • 感情(かんじょう)が爆発(ばくはつ)する (emotions explode)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 爆発(ばくはつ)物(ぶつ) (explosives)
  • 爆発(ばくはつ)事故(じこ) (explosion accident)
  • 爆発(ばくはつ)的(てき)な人気(にんき) (explosive popularity)
  • 人口(じんこう)爆発(ばくはつ) (population explosion)
  • ガス爆発(ばくはつ) (gas explosion)
  • 核(かく)爆発(ばくはつ) (nuclear explosion)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 爆弾(ばくだん) - bomb
  • 噴火(ふんか) - volcanic eruption
  • 破裂(はれつ) - bursting, rupture
