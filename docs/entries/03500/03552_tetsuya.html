徹夜(てつや)

てつや
noun, suru-verb
staying up all night, all-nighter
1. staying up all night, all-nighter
Remaining awake throughout the night, typically for work or study.
試験(しけん)前(まえ)に徹夜(てつや)した。
I pulled an all-nighter before the exam.
徹夜(てつや)で仕事(しごと)を終(お)わらせた。
I finished the work by staying up all night.
徹夜(てつや)は体(からだ)に悪(わる)い。
Staying up all night is bad for your health.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 徹夜(てつや)する (to stay up all night)
  • 徹夜(てつや)で〜する (to do ~ by staying up all night)
  • 徹夜(てつや)明(あ)け (the morning after staying up all night)

COMMON USES:

  • 徹夜(てつや)で勉強(べんきょう)する (to study all night)
  • 徹夜(てつや)で仕事(しごと)する (to work all night)
  • 徹夜(てつや)続(つづ)き (staying up multiple nights in a row)

COLLOQUIAL:

  • オール (all-nighter - casual/youth slang from "all-night")
