申(もう)し訳(わけ)

もうしわけ
noun
excuse, apology

Definitions

1. excuse, explanation, apology
Something said to explain or justify an action; commonly used in apologetic expressions.

Examples

申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません。
I'm very sorry. / I apologize.
Very polite apology expression
申(もう)し訳(わけ)ないが、今日(きょう)は無理(むり)だ。
I'm sorry, but today is impossible.
申し訳ない = sorry (slightly less formal)
遅(おく)れて申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I'm very sorry for being late.
申し訳ございません = most polite form

Notes

USAGE: 申(もう)し訳(わけ) is primarily used in apology expressions and is very polite. It literally means "excuse" or "explanation."

POLITENESS LEVELS:

  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません (most polite/formal)
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません (very polite)
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ない (casual but still apologetic)

CONTRAST:

  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ありません - very polite apology
  • すみません - standard polite apology
  • ごめんなさい - casual apology
  • ごめん - very casual apology
