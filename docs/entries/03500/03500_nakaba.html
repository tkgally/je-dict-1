半(なか)ば

なかば
noun, adverb
middle, halfway, half
1. middle, halfway point
The midpoint of a period, process, or physical distance.
8月(がつ)半(なか)ばに旅行(りょこう)に行(い)く予定(よてい)です。
I plan to go on a trip in mid-August.
〜半ば = mid-~
仕事(しごと)は半(なか)ばまで終(お)わった。
The work is halfway done.
2. partially, half
Used adverbially to indicate something is partial or incomplete.
半(なか)ば諦(あきら)めていたが、最後(さいご)に成功(せいこう)した。
I had half given up, but I succeeded in the end.
半ば〜 = half ~, partially ~

USAGE: 半(なか)ば can function as both a noun (midpoint) and an adverb (partially, half).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜月(がつ)半(なか)ば (mid-month)
  • 年(ねん)半(なか)ば (mid-year)
  • 半(なか)ば過(す)ぎ (past the halfway point)
  • 半(なか)ば諦(あきら)める (half give up)
  • 半(なか)ば強制的(きょうせいてき)に (semi-forcibly)
  • 道(みち)の半(なか)ば (halfway along the road)

NUANCE: When used adverbially with verbs or adjectives, 半(なか)ば suggests the action or state is incomplete or partial. It often carries a nuance of ambivalence.
