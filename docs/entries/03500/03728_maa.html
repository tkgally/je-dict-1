まあ

まあ
interjection / adverb
well; my!; so-so
1. well, now
Used when making a modest, hesitant, or transitional statement.
まあ、仕方(しかた)ないか。
Well, I guess it can't be helped.
まあ、いいでしょう。
Well, that should be fine.
Sense 1: well
まあ、そうですね。
Well, I suppose so.
Sense 1: now
まあ、大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)だろう。
Well, it'll probably be okay.
Sense 1: well
まあ、待(ま)ってください。
Now, please wait.
Sense 1: now
まあまあ、落(お)ち着(つ)いて。
Now, now, calm down.
Sense 1: calming interjection
2. my!, oh!
An exclamation expressing surprise (often used by women).
まあ、驚(おどろ)いた！
Oh my, what a surprise!
Often used by women
まあ、素敵(すてき)！
Oh my, how lovely!
Sense 2: my!
まあ、大変(たいへん)！
Oh dear!
Sense 2: oh!
まあ、久(ひさ)しぶり！
Oh my, it's been so long!
Sense 2: my!
3. more or less, so-so
Indicating something is acceptable but not exceptional.
まあまあの結果(けっか)だった。
The results were so-so.
まあまあ美味(おい)しい。
It's fairly good.
Sense 3: so-so
まあまあだね。
It's so-so.
Sense 3: more or less
まあまあ元気(げんき)です。
I'm doing fairly well.
Sense 3: so-so
まあまあの成績(せいせき)だった。
The grades were passable.
Sense 3: so-so

A versatile word with different nuances depending on context and intonation.

USAGE 1 - HESITATION/MODESTY:

  • まあ、いいでしょう (Well, that should be fine)
  • まあ、そうですね (Well, I suppose so)
  • まあ、大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)だろう (Well, it'll probably be okay)

USAGE 2 - SURPRISE (feminine):

  • まあ、素敵(すてき)！(Oh my, how lovely!)
  • まあ、大変(たいへん)！(Oh dear!)

USAGE 3 - SO-SO:

  • まあまあ (so-so, not bad)
  • まあまあ美味(おい)しい (fairly good)

USAGE 4 - CALMING:

  • まあまあ、落(お)ち着(つ)いて (Now, now, calm down)
