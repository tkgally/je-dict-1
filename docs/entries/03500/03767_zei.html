税(ぜい)

ぜい
noun / suffix
tax

Definitions

1. tax
A compulsory financial charge imposed by a government.

Examples

税(ぜい)込(こ)みで1000円(えん)です。
It's 1,000 yen including tax.
消費税(しょうひぜい)が10%に上(あ)がった。
The consumption tax rose to 10%.
税(ぜい)抜(ぬ)き価格(かかく)は900円(えん)です。
The price excluding tax is 900 yen.

Notes

COMMON TAX TYPES:

  • 消費税(しょうひぜい) (consumption tax, sales tax)
  • 所得税(しょとくぜい) (income tax)
  • 住民税(じゅうみんぜい) (resident tax)
  • 固定資産税(こていしさんぜい) (property tax)
  • 相続税(そうぞくぜい) (inheritance tax)
  • 贈与税(ぞうよぜい) (gift tax)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 税(ぜい)込(こ)み (tax included)
  • 税(ぜい)抜(ぬ)き (tax excluded, before tax)
  • 税金(ぜいきん) (tax, taxes - common word)
  • 納税(のうぜい) (tax payment)
  • 脱税(だつぜい) (tax evasion)
  • 免税(めんぜい) (tax exemption, duty-free)

Related Words
