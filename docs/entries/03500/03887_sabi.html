錆(さび)

さび
noun
rust
1. rust (oxidation on metal)
The reddish-brown coating that forms on iron or steel when exposed to moisture and air.
自転車(じてんしゃ)のチェーンに錆(さび)がついている。
There's rust on the bicycle chain.
錆(さび)を落(お)とすのは大変(たいへん)だ。
Removing rust is hard work.
雨(あめ)ざらしにすると錆(さび)が出(で)る。
If you leave it exposed to rain, it will rust.

錆(さび) refers to the oxidation that forms on metal surfaces, especially iron and steel when exposed to moisture.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 錆(さび)がつく: to become rusty
  • 錆(さび)が出(で)る: rust appears
  • 錆(さび)を落(お)とす: to remove rust
  • 錆(さび)止(ど)め: rust prevention/rust inhibitor
  • 錆(さび)だらけ: covered in rust

VERB FORM:
錆(さ)びる is the verb meaning 'to rust.' Example: 鉄(てつ)が錆(さ)びた (the iron rusted).

TYPES OF RUST:

  • 赤錆(あかさび): red rust (iron oxide, the most common type)
  • 青錆(あおさび): verdigris (green patina on copper or bronze)
  • 黒錆(くろさび): black rust (stable oxide, sometimes protective)

FIGURATIVE USE:
腕(うで)が錆(さ)びる means 'to lose one's skill/touch' from disuse.
