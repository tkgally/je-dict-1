墓(はか)

はか
noun
grave, tomb
1. grave, tomb
A place where a dead person is buried; a burial site with a marker or monument.
お盆(ぼん)に墓(はか)を参(まい)った。
I visited the grave during Obon.
墓を参る = to visit a grave (墓参り)
先祖(せんぞ)の墓(はか)は京都(きょうと)にある。
My ancestors' graves are in Kyoto.
墓(はか)に花(はな)を供(そな)えた。
I offered flowers at the grave.
家族(かぞく)みんなで墓(はか)の掃除(そうじ)をした。
The whole family cleaned the grave together.
祖父(そふ)が亡(な)くなってから、毎年(まいとし)お彼岸(ひがん)に墓(はか)参(まい)りに行(い)っている。
Since my grandfather passed away, I visit his grave every year during the equinox.
お彼岸(ひがん) = equinox period (traditional time for grave visits)

USAGE: 墓(はか) refers to individual graves or tombs. In Japan, families often share a single grave marker (墓石(はかいし)/墓石(ぼせき)).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 墓(はか)参(まい)り (visiting a grave)
  • 墓(はか)を建(た)てる (to erect a gravestone)
  • 墓(はか)に入(はい)る (to be buried)
  • 墓(はか)を掃除(そうじ)する (to clean the grave)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 墓(はか)参(まい)り (grave visit)
  • 墓地(ぼち) (cemetery, graveyard)
  • 墓石(はかいし)/墓石(ぼせき) (gravestone, tombstone)
  • 墓場(はかば) (graveyard)
  • お墓(はか) (grave - polite)

CULTURAL NOTE: Visiting family graves (墓参(はかまい)り) is an important tradition in Japan, especially during お盆(ぼん) (Obon) and お彼岸(ひがん) (equinox periods).
