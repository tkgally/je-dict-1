仮名(かな)

かな
noun
kana (Japanese syllabary)

Definitions

1. kana
The Japanese syllabary writing systems, including hiragana and katakana. Contrasts with kanji (Chinese characters).

Examples

子供(こども)はまず仮名(かな)を習(なら)う。
Children first learn kana.
仮名(かな)で書(か)いてください。
Please write it in kana.
平仮名(ひらがな)と片仮名(かたかな)を合(あ)わせて仮名(かな)と言(い)う。
Hiragana and katakana together are called kana.

Notes

WRITING: Can also be written as かな in hiragana.

TYPES:

  • 平仮名(ひらがな) (hiragana) - cursive syllabary
  • 片仮名(かたかな) (katakana) - angular syllabary

ETYMOLOGY: 仮(か) means 'temporary/borrowed' and 名(な) means 'name/character'. Kana were originally simplified forms of kanji used for their phonetic value.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮名(かな)遣(づか)い (kana orthography)
  • 仮名(かな)書(が)き (writing in kana)
