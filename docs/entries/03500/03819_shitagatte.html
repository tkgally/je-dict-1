したがって

したがって
conjunction
therefore, consequently
1. therefore, consequently, hence
Used to introduce a logical conclusion or result based on what was previously stated.
予算(よさん)が足(た)りない。したがって計画(けいかく)を変更(へんこう)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
The budget is insufficient. Therefore, we need to change the plan.
彼(かれ)は病気(びょうき)だ。したがって今日(きょう)は休(やす)む。
He is sick. Consequently, he will take today off.
証拠(しょうこ)が不十分(ふじゅうぶん)だ。したがって無罪(むざい)とする。
The evidence is insufficient. Therefore, the verdict is not guilty.
Common in formal/legal contexts

USAGE: したがって is a formal conjunction used to indicate logical consequence. It's commonly used in written language, speeches, and formal situations.

REGISTER: Formal/written. In casual speech, だから or それで are more common.

CONTRAST:

  • したがって - therefore (formal, logical)
  • だから - so, therefore (general, casual)
  • それで - and so, because of that (casual)
  • 従(したが)って - also written with kanji (same meaning)
  • よって - therefore (more formal/legal)
  • 故(ゆえ)に - hence (literary)
