真(ま)っ赤(か)

まっか
na-adjective / noun
bright red, deep red
1. bright red, deep red, crimson
An intense, vivid shade of red.
紅葉(もみじ)が真(ま)っ赤(か)に染(そ)まった。
The autumn leaves turned bright red.
2. flushed, blushing
Describes a face that has turned red from embarrassment, anger, or heat.
恥(は)ずかしくて顔(かお)が真(ま)っ赤(か)になった。
I was so embarrassed that my face turned red.
3. utter, complete (lie)
In the expression 真(ま)っ赤(か)な嘘(うそ), means a blatant lie.
それは真(ま)っ赤(か)な嘘(うそ)だ。
That's a blatant lie.
Common idiom

Part of a series of intensified color expressions using 真(ま)っ〜.

SIMILAR INTENSIFIED COLORS:

  • 真(ま)っ白(しろ) (pure white)
  • 真(ま)っ黒(くろ) (jet black)
  • 真(ま)っ青(さお) (deep blue; pale - from shock)
  • 真(ま)っ暗(くら) (pitch dark)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 真(ま)っ赤(か)な嘘(うそ) (outright lie, blatant lie)
  • 顔(かお)が真(ま)っ赤(か) (face is flushed/blushing)
  • 真(ま)っ赤(か)に燃(も)える (to burn bright red)
