囁(ささや)く

ささやく
verb (godan)
to whisper; to murmur

Definitions

1. to whisper; to speak in a low voice
Speaking softly so only nearby listeners can hear.
2. to murmur; to spread rumors
Quietly spreading information or speculation, often used in passive.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)の耳(みみ)に囁(ささや)いた。
She whispered in my ear.
Sense 1: speaking softly
授業中(じゅぎょうちゅう)に囁(ささや)いてはいけません。
You must not whisper during class.
[Note: ては + いけない = prohibition]
二人(ふたり)の関係(かんけい)について、いろいろと囁(ささや)かれている。
Various things are being whispered about their relationship.
Sense 2: rumors being spread (passive form)

Notes

囁(ささや)く is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)).

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form indicates ongoing action (何(なに)かを囁(ささや)いている = 'is whispering something').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [content]を囁(ささや)く (whisper ~)
  • [person]に囁(ささや)く (whisper to ~)
  • 耳(みみ)に囁(ささや)く (whisper in someone's ear)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 耳元(みみもと)で囁(ささや)く (whisper close to the ear)
  • 愛(あい)を囁(ささや)く (whisper words of love)
  • 〜と囁(ささや)かれている (it is rumored that ~)

The passive form 囁(ささや)かれる is commonly used when talking about rumors or speculation.
