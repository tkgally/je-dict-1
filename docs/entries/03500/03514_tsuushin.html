通信(つうしん)

つうしん
noun, suru-verb
communication, correspondence
1. communication, telecommunications
The transmission of information, especially electronic signals or data.
通信(つうしん)が途絶(とぜつ)えた。
Communication was cut off.
無線(むせん)通信(つうしん)で連絡(れんらく)を取(と)った。
We made contact via wireless communication.
2. correspondence
Exchange of letters or messages.
通信(つうしん)教育(きょういく)で資格(しかく)を取(と)った。
I got my qualification through distance learning.

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 通信(つうしん)教育(きょういく) (distance learning, correspondence education)
  • 無線(むせん)通信(つうしん) (wireless communication)
  • 通信(つうしん)販売(はんばい) (mail-order sales)
  • 通信(つうしん)社(しゃ) (news agency)
  • 通信(つうしん)速度(そくど) (communication speed)
  • 通信(つうしん)費(ひ) (communication expenses)

MODERN USAGE:

  • データ通信(つうしん) (data communication)
  • 衛星(えいせい)通信(つうしん) (satellite communication)
general
