疲(つか)れ

つかれ
noun
tiredness, fatigue
1. tiredness, fatigue, exhaustion
The state of being tired or weary, physically or mentally.
疲(つか)れが取(と)れない。
I can't shake off the fatigue.
一日(いちにち)の疲(つか)れを癒(いや)す。
Relieve the fatigue of the day.
疲(つか)れが顔(かお)に出(で)ている。
The tiredness shows on your face.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 疲(つか)れが取(と)れる (fatigue goes away, to feel refreshed)
  • 疲(つか)れが出(で)る (fatigue shows, to start feeling tired)
  • 疲(つか)れを癒(いや)す (to relieve fatigue)
  • 疲(つか)れを感(かん)じる (to feel tired)

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 目(め)の疲(つか)れ (eye strain)
  • 精神的(せいしんてき)疲(つか)れ (mental fatigue)
  • 肉体的(にくたいてき)疲(つか)れ (physical fatigue)

RELATED VERB:

  • 疲(つか)れる (to get tired) - this noun is derived from the verb

Related Words

Related: 疲(つか)れる (verb form)
Synonym: 疲労(ひろう) (fatigue (formal))
