苦手(にがて)

にがて
noun, na-adjective
poor at, weak point

Definitions

1. poor at, weak in, not good at
Lacking skill or ability in something; finding something difficult.
2. dislike, hard to deal with
Finding a person or thing difficult to handle or uncomfortable to be around.

Examples

数学(すうがく)が苦手(にがて)です。
I'm not good at math.
〜が苦手 = poor at ~
朝(あさ)起(お)きるのが苦手(にがて)だ。
I'm bad at waking up early.
彼(かれ)は苦手(にがて)なタイプの人(ひと)だ。
He's the type of person I find difficult to deal with.

Notes

USAGE: 苦手(にがて) is the opposite of 得意(とくい). It's used to express weakness in skills or discomfort with people/situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜が苦手(にがて)だ (to be poor at ~)
  • 苦手(にがて)な科目(かもく) (weak subject)
  • 苦手(にがて)な人(ひと) (person one finds hard to deal with)
  • 苦手(にがて)を克服(こくふく)する (to overcome a weakness)
  • 苦手(にがて)意識(いしき) (feeling of inadequacy)

NUANCE: 苦手(にがて) is a softer way to say you're bad at something compared to 下手(へた) (unskillful). It suggests personal difficulty rather than objective lack of skill.

ANTONYM:

  • 得意(とくい) (good at, specialty)

Related Words

Antonym: 得意(とくい) (good at)
Related: 下手(へた) (unskillful)
