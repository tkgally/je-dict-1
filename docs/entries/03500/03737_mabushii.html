まぶしい

まぶしい
i-adjective
dazzling, bright, radiant

Definitions

1. dazzling, glaring, too bright
So bright that it's difficult to look at directly.
2. radiant, brilliant
Figuratively describes someone or something that shines impressively.

Examples

太陽(たいよう)がまぶしい。
The sun is dazzling.
まぶしくて目(め)を開(あ)けていられない。
It's so bright I can't keep my eyes open.
彼女(かのじょ)の笑顔(えがお)はまぶしいほど輝(かがや)いていた。
Her smile was radiantly bright.
Figurative usage

Notes

Can also be written as 眩(まぶ)しい (with kanji).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • まぶしくて〜できない (so bright that one can't ~)
  • まぶしいほど (so radiant that, dazzlingly)
  • 目(め)がまぶしい (my eyes are dazzled)

FIGURATIVE USE:
Often used to describe someone who appears brilliant, impressive, or youthful - someone whose brilliance is almost intimidating to look at directly.

CONJUGATION:

  • まぶしくない (not dazzling)
  • まぶしかった (was dazzling)
  • まぶしくて (dazzling, and...)
