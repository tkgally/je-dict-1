野(の)

の
noun
field, plain
1. field, plain, open land
An open area of land, typically covered with grass or wild plants; uncultivated land.
野(の)に咲(さ)く花(はな)は美(うつく)しい。
Flowers blooming in the field are beautiful.
野(の)山(やま)を歩(ある)く。
To walk through fields and mountains.
野山 = fields and mountains, countryside
野(の)ウサギを見(み)かけた。
I spotted a wild rabbit.
野〜 = wild ~

USAGE: 野(の) refers to open fields and wild areas. It's commonly used as a prefix meaning 'wild' or 'field-'.

AS A PREFIX (野〜 = wild):

  • 野(の)ウサギ (wild rabbit)
  • 野(の)バラ (wild rose)
  • 野(の)イチゴ (wild strawberry)
  • 野(の)鳥(とり) (wild bird)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 野(の)原(はら) (field, plain)
  • 野(の)山(やま) (fields and mountains)
  • 平野(へいや) (plain)
  • 野外(やがい) (outdoors, open-air)
  • 分野(ぶんや) (field, area of study)
  • 視野(しや) (field of vision)

NOTE: The character 野 has multiple readings: の (kun), や (on). In compounds it's often read や.
