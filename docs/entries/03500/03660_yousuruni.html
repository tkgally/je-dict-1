要(よう)するに

ようするに
expression
in short, in other words

Definitions

1. in short, in other words, basically
Used to summarize or rephrase what has been said in a simpler or more direct way.

Examples

要(よう)するに、彼(かれ)は来(こ)ないということだ。
In short, he's not coming.
要(よう)するに、お金(かね)が足(た)りないんです。
Basically, we don't have enough money.
長(なが)い説明(せつめい)だったけど、要(よう)するに何(なに)が言(い)いたいの？
That was a long explanation, but in short, what are you trying to say?

Notes

USAGE: 要(よう)するに is used at the beginning of a sentence to introduce a summary or the main point of what was previously said. It's useful for cutting through complexity.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • つまり - in other words, that is to say
  • 結局(けっきょく) - after all, in the end
  • 簡単(かんたん)に言(い)えば - to put it simply
