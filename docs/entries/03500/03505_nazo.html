謎(なぞ)

なぞ
noun
riddle, mystery, puzzle

Definitions

1. riddle, puzzle
A question or statement designed to be solved or guessed; a word game or brain teaser.
2. mystery, enigma
Something that is difficult to understand or explain; an unsolved question.

Examples

謎(なぞ)を解(と)くのが好(す)きだ。
I like solving riddles.
謎を解く = to solve a riddle/mystery
この事件(じけん)は謎(なぞ)に包(つつ)まれている。
This case is shrouded in mystery.
謎に包まれている = shrouded in mystery
宇宙(うちゅう)には多(おお)くの謎(なぞ)がある。
There are many mysteries in the universe.

Notes

USAGE: 謎(なぞ) refers to both playful riddles and serious mysteries. Context determines which meaning applies.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 謎(なぞ)を解(と)く (to solve a mystery/riddle)
  • 謎(なぞ)が解(と)ける (a mystery is solved)
  • 謎(なぞ)に包(つつ)まれる (to be shrouded in mystery)
  • 謎(なぞ)が深(ふか)まる (the mystery deepens)
  • 謎(なぞ)解(と)き (solving riddles; mystery-solving)

COMPOUNDS:

  • 謎(なぞ)掛(か)け (riddle, wordplay)
  • 謎(なぞ)の人物(じんぶつ) (mysterious person)
  • 謎(なぞ)めいた (mysterious, enigmatic)

CULTURAL NOTE: 謎(なぞ)掛(か)け is a traditional Japanese form of wordplay where the answer connects two seemingly unrelated things through a pun.
