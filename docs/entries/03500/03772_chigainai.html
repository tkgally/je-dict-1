違(ちが)いない

ちがいない
expression
no doubt, must be, certain
1. no doubt, must be, certainly
Expresses strong certainty or conviction that something is true.
彼(かれ)は知(し)っているに違(ちが)いない。
He must know about it.
〜に違(ちが)いない pattern
これは本物(ほんもの)に違(ちが)いない。
This must be genuine.
犯人(はんにん)は彼(かれ)に違(ちが)いない。
The culprit must be him.
あの様子(ようす)から見(み)ると、彼女(かのじょ)は何(なに)か隠(かく)しているに違(ちが)いない。
Judging from her behavior, she must be hiding something.
こんなに道(みち)が混(こ)んでいるということは、この先(さき)で事故(じこ)があったに違(ちが)いない。
The fact that the road is this congested means there must have been an accident up ahead.

〜に違(ちが)いない expresses the speaker's strong conviction that something is the case: 'it must be ~,' 'there is no doubt that ~.' The conviction is an inference, not direct knowledge — the speaker has reasoned from evidence and is confident of the conclusion.

Grammar: it attaches to a plain-form clause, with に before it. The に is fixed and is not the usual location or direction particle.

  • Nouns: 彼(かれ)に違(ちが)いない
  • Verbs (plain form): 知(し)っているに違(ちが)いない
  • I-adjectives: 高(たか)いに違(ちが)いない
  • Na-adjectives: the bare stem, 静(しず)かに違(ちが)いない — no な

Degrees of certainty, strongest first:

  • 〜に違(ちが)いない: must be — the speaker is sure
  • 〜はずだ: should be — expected on the basis of a known reason or arrangement, and so it can be overturned by facts
  • 〜だろう: probably — plain conjecture
  • 〜かもしれない: might be — one possibility among several

Register: 違(ちが)いない is neutral and common in speech and writing alike. In formal writing you may also meet 〜に相違(そうい)ない, a stiffer equivalent found in legal and official documents.

Related Words

Related: はず (weaker: expectation from reasoning)
Contrast: かもしれない (weaker: mere possibility)
Related: だろう (weaker: conjecture)
