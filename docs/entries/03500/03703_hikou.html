非行(ひこう)

ひこう
noun
delinquency, misconduct
1. delinquency, misconduct
Antisocial or illegal behavior, especially by young people.
少年(しょうねん)非行(ひこう)が社会(しゃかい)問題(もんだい)になっている。
Juvenile delinquency has become a social problem.
非行(ひこう)に走(はし)る若者(わかもの)が増(ふ)えている。
More young people are turning to delinquency.
非行(ひこう)防止(ぼうし)の活動(かつどう)に取(と)り組(く)んでいる。
They are working on delinquency prevention activities.

Commonly used to refer to juvenile crime and antisocial behavior.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 少年(しょうねん)非行(ひこう) (juvenile delinquency)
  • 非行(ひこう)少年(しょうねん) (juvenile delinquent)
  • 非行(ひこう)に走(はし)る (to turn to delinquency)
  • 非行(ひこう)防止(ぼうし) (delinquency prevention)
  • 非行(ひこう){グループ} (delinquent group)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 飛行(ひこう) - flight, flying (aircraft)

NOTE:
The character 非(ひ) means "not" or "wrong," and 行(こう) means "conduct," so 非行(ひこう) literally means "wrong conduct."
