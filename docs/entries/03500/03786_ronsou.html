論争(ろんそう)

ろんそう
noun, suru-verb
controversy, dispute

Definitions

1. controversy, dispute, argument
A prolonged debate or disagreement, especially one conducted publicly; an argument about important issues.

Examples

この問題(もんだい)について論争(ろんそう)が続(つづ)いている。
The controversy over this issue continues.
学者(がくしゃ)たちが論争(ろんそう)を繰(く)り広(ひろ)げた。
The scholars engaged in a heated debate.
論争を繰り広げる = to engage in a dispute
政治的(せいじてき)な論争(ろんそう)を避(さ)けたい。
I want to avoid political controversies.

Notes

USAGE: 論争(ろんそう) refers to serious disputes or controversies, typically about intellectual, political, or social matters.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 論争(ろんそう)を繰(く)り広(ひろ)げる (to engage in a dispute)
  • 論争(ろんそう)が起(お)こる (a controversy arises)
  • 論争(ろんそう)を呼(よ)ぶ (to cause controversy)
  • 論争(ろんそう)の的(まと) (subject of controversy)

CONTRAST:

  • 論争(ろんそう) - intellectual/public dispute
  • 議論(ぎろん) - discussion, debate (more neutral)
  • 口論(こうろん) - verbal quarrel (more emotional)
