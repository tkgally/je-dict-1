涼(すず)む

すずむ
godan verb
to cool oneself, to enjoy the cool

Definitions

1. to cool oneself, to enjoy the cool
To seek relief from heat by going to a cool place or catching a breeze.

Examples

縁側(えんがわ)で涼(すず)む。
Enjoy the cool air on the veranda.
暑(あつ)いから木陰(こかげ)で涼(すず)んでいた。
It was hot, so I was cooling off in the shade of a tree.
夕涼(ゆうすず)みに散歩(さんぽ)に出(で)かけた。
I went out for a walk to enjoy the evening cool.

Notes

涼(すず)む means to cool oneself or enjoy cool air, especially during hot summer days.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pattern: Xで涼(すず)む (cool off at X)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 縁側(えんがわ)で涼(すず)む (to cool off on the veranda)
  • 木陰(こかげ)で涼(すず)む (to cool off in the tree shade)
  • 夕涼(ゆうすず)み (enjoying the evening cool) [noun]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Enjoying the cool breeze (涼(すず)む) is a traditional Japanese way of coping with hot summers. Activities include sitting on the 縁側(えんがわ) (veranda), listening to 風鈴(ふうりん) (wind chimes), or going out for 夕涼(ゆうすず)み in the evening.

RELATED:

  • 涼(すず)しい (cool, refreshing) [i-adjective]
