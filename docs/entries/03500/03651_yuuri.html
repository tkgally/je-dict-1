有利(ゆうり)

ゆうり
な-adjective
advantageous, favorable
1. advantageous, favorable, profitable
Providing a benefit or advantage; in a position that is likely to lead to success.
この条件(じょうけん)は私(わたし)たちに有利(ゆうり)だ。
These conditions are favorable to us.
彼(かれ)は有利(ゆうり)な立場(たちば)にいる。
He is in an advantageous position.
交渉(こうしょう)を有利(ゆうり)に進(すす)めたい。
I want to advance the negotiations in our favor.

有利(ゆうり) is a na-adjective describing situations, conditions, or positions that provide an advantage or benefit.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 有利(ゆうり)な条件(じょうけん) (favorable conditions)
  • Adverbial: 有利(ゆうり)に進(すす)める (to advance favorably)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 有利(ゆうり)な立場(たちば): advantageous position
  • 有利(ゆうり)に働(はたら)く: to work in one's favor
  • 〜に有利(ゆうり)だ: to be advantageous to ~
  • 有利(ゆうり)な条件(じょうけん): favorable terms

WORD FORMATION:
有(ゆう) (to have) + 利(り) (benefit, advantage)

CONTRAST:

  • 不利(ふり): disadvantageous, unfavorable (antonym)
  • 有益(ゆうえき): beneficial, useful (broader in meaning)

Related Words

Antonym: 不利(ふり) (disadvantageous)
