潜(もぐ)る

もぐる
verb (godan)
to dive; to get into; to slip through
1. to dive underwater; to submerge
Going beneath the surface of water.
2. to get into; to crawl into
Entering a confined space, such as getting under covers.
3. to slip through; to pass under
Going beneath or through something like a fence or barrier.
4. to go underground; to evade
Figuratively hiding from authorities or evading rules.
海(うみ)に潜(もぐ)って魚(さかな)を見(み)た。
I dove into the sea and saw fish.
Sense 1: diving underwater
寒(さむ)いので、布団(ふとん)に潜(もぐ)った。
It was cold, so I burrowed into the futon.
Sense 2: getting under covers
塀(へい)の下(した)を潜(もぐ)って庭(にわ)に入(はい)った。
I slipped under the fence and entered the garden.
Sense 3: passing through

潜(もぐ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)).

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form indicates either ongoing action (今(いま)潜(もぐ)っている = 'is diving now') or a resulting state (布団(ふとん)に潜(もぐ)っている = 'is under the covers').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • 海(うみ)に潜(もぐ)る (dive into the sea)
  • 布団(ふとん)に潜(もぐ)る (burrow into the futon)
  • 〜の下(した)を潜(もぐ)る (slip under ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 海(うみ)に潜(もぐ)る (dive into the sea)
  • 布団(ふとん)に潜(もぐ)る (get under the futon)
  • 水(みず)に潜(もぐ)る (go underwater)
