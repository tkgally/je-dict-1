相続(そうぞく)

そうぞく
noun / suru-verb
succession, inheritance
1. inheritance
The act of receiving property, money, or a title from someone who has died.
父(ちち)の財産(ざいさん)を相続(そうぞく)した。
I inherited my father's estate.
相続(そうぞく)税(ぜい)を払(はら)わなければならない。
I have to pay inheritance tax.
相続(そうぞく)を巡(めぐ)って親族(しんぞく)間(かん)で争(あらそ)いが起(お)きた。
A dispute arose among relatives over the inheritance.
2. succession
Taking over a position, role, or status from a predecessor.

Used for inheritance of property, estates, or positions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 相続(そうぞく)する (to inherit)
  • 相続(そうぞく)税(ぜい) (inheritance tax)
  • 相続(そうぞく)人(にん) (heir)
  • 相続(そうぞく)権(けん) (right of inheritance)
  • 相続(そうぞく)財産(ざいさん) (inherited property)
  • 相続(そうぞく)放棄(ほうき) (renunciation of inheritance)

LEGAL CONTEXT:
In Japan, legal inheritance follows the Civil Code's rules on statutory heirs and mandatory inheritance portions.
