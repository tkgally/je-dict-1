1. association, mental connection
The mental process of connecting one idea or thought with another; something that comes to mind when thinking of something else.
この曲(きょく)を聞(き)くと夏(なつ)を連想(れんそう)する。
When I hear this song, I associate it with summer.
〜を連想(れんそう)する = to associate with ~
赤(あか)から何(なに)を連想(れんそう)しますか。
What do you associate with the color red?
連想(れんそう)ゲームで遊(あそ)ぼう。
Let's play a word association game.
連想(れんそう)ゲーム = word association game

連想(れんそう) refers to the mental process of connecting one idea with another — what comes to mind when you think of something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜を連想(れんそう)する: to associate with, to be reminded of
  • 〜から連想(れんそう)する: to associate from (a starting point)
  • 連想(れんそう)させる: to evoke, to remind someone of

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 連想(れんそう)ゲーム: word association game
  • 自由(じゆう)連想(れんそう): free association (psychology)
  • 連想(れんそう)記憶(きおく): associative memory

USAGE:
As a する verb, it takes を for the thing being associated with: この曲(きょく)を聞(き)くと夏(なつ)を連想(れんそう)する (This song makes me think of summer). The causative 連想(れんそう)させる is very common for describing what something evokes.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 想起(そうき): recollection, recall (more formal)
  • 思(おも)い出(だ)す: to remember, to recall (everyday)
