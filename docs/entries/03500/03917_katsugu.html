担(かつ)ぐ

かつぐ
godan verb
to shoulder, to carry on one's shoulder; to trick
1. to shoulder, to carry on one's shoulder
To support and carry something heavy on one's shoulder.
荷物(にもつ)を肩(かた)に担(かつ)いで運(はこ)ぶ。
Carry the luggage on one's shoulder.
神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)ぐ。
Carry a portable shrine (on one's shoulders).
米俵(こめだわら)を担(かつ)いで運(はこ)ぶ。
Carry a rice bale on one's shoulder.
Sense 1: to shoulder
丸太(まるた)を担(かつ)いで歩(ある)く。
Walk while carrying a log on one's shoulder.
Sense 1: to carry on shoulder
祭(まつ)りで神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)いだ。
I carried the portable shrine at the festival.
Sense 1: to shoulder
カメラを担(かつ)いで出(で)かけた。
I went out with a camera over my shoulder.
Sense 1: to carry
2. to set up (as a leader), to promote
To support someone as a candidate or leader.
彼(かれ)を会長(かいちょう)に担(かつ)ぎ上(あ)げた。
They set him up as chairman.
Sense 2: to promote as leader
新人(しんじん)を候補者(こうほしゃ)に担(かつ)ぐ。
To put forward a newcomer as a candidate.
Sense 2: to set up as leader
党(とう)は彼女(かのじょ)を担(かつ)いでいる。
The party is backing her.
Sense 2: to promote
若手(わかて)を担(かつ)いで選挙(せんきょ)に臨(のぞ)む。
To contest the election with a young candidate.
Sense 2: to set up
彼(かれ)をリーダー(りーだー)に担(かつ)ぐ動(うご)きがある。
There's a movement to make him leader.
Sense 2: to promote
3. to trick, to deceive (colloquial)
To fool someone as a prank or joke.
友達(ともだち)を担(かつ)いでびっくりさせた。
I tricked my friend and surprised him.
エイプリルフールで担(かつ)がれた。
I was tricked on April Fools' Day.
Sense 3: to trick
嘘(うそ)で担(かつ)いでごめん。
Sorry for tricking you with a lie.
Sense 3: to deceive
後輩(こうはい)を担(かつ)いで笑(わら)った。
We laughed after playing a prank on our junior.
Sense 3: to trick

担(かつ)ぐ means to carry something on one's shoulder. It also has a colloquial meaning of 'to trick.'

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを担(かつ)ぐ

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)ぐ (to carry a portable shrine)
  • 荷物(にもつ)を担(かつ)ぐ (to shoulder luggage)
  • 人(ひと)を担(かつ)ぐ (to trick/deceive someone)
  • 候補者(こうほしゃ)を担(かつ)ぐ (to back a candidate)

CULTURAL NOTE:
神輿(みこし)を担(かつ)ぐ is an important activity in Japanese festivals, where teams carry heavy portable shrines through the streets.

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 背負(せお)う (to carry on one's back)
  • 抱(かか)える (to hold in one's arms)
