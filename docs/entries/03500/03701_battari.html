ばったり

ばったり
adverb
unexpectedly, with a thud

Definitions

1. unexpectedly, by chance
Meeting someone or encountering something without planning.
2. with a thud, suddenly falling
The sound or manner of something falling heavily.
3. completely stopping
Something coming to a sudden halt.

Examples

駅(えき)で友人(ゆうじん)にばったり会(あ)った。
I ran into a friend by chance at the station.
Most common usage
彼(かれ)はばったり倒(たお)れた。
He collapsed with a thud.
連絡(れんらく)がばったり途絶(とだ)えた。
Communication suddenly stopped completely.

Notes

An onomatopoeic adverb with multiple related meanings centered on suddenness.

PATTERNS:

  • ばったり会(あ)う (to bump into someone unexpectedly)
  • ばったり倒(たお)れる (to fall/collapse suddenly)
  • ばったり止(と)まる (to stop suddenly)
  • ばったり途絶(とだ)える (to suddenly cease)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 偶然(ぐうぜん) - by coincidence (more neutral)
  • 思(おも)いがけなく - unexpectedly
  • ばったり emphasizes the sudden, chance nature of the encounter
