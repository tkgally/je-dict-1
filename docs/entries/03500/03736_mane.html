真似(まね)

まね
noun / suru-verb
imitation, mimicry

Definitions

1. imitation, mimicry, copying
The act of copying someone's actions, speech, or behavior.
2. behavior, action
Often used in negative expressions to criticize certain behavior.

Examples

子供(こども)は親(おや)の真似(まね)をする。
Children imitate their parents.
そんな馬鹿(ばか)な真似(まね)はやめなさい。
Stop doing such foolish things.
〜な真似 used to criticize behavior
猿真似(さるまね)では上達(じょうたつ)しない。
You won't improve by just mindlessly copying.
猿真似(さるまね) = blind imitation

Notes

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 真似(まね)をする (to imitate)
  • 〜の真似(まね) (imitation of ~)
  • 馬鹿(ばか)な真似(まね) (foolish behavior)
  • 危(あぶ)ない真似(まね) (dangerous behavior)
  • 猿真似(さるまね) (blind imitation, monkey-see-monkey-do)

USAGE NOTE:
真似(まね) can be neutral (children learning by imitation) or negative (copying without understanding, or engaging in unacceptable behavior).

RELATED VERB:

  • 真似(まね)る (to imitate, to mimic)
