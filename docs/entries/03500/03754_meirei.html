1. order, command, instruction
An authoritative directive that must be followed.
上司(じょうし)から命令(めいれい)を受(う)けた。
I received an order from my boss.
命令(めいれい)に従(したが)わなければならない。
You must obey orders.
社長(しゃちょう)が全員(ぜんいん)に残業(ざんぎょう)を命令(めいれい)した。
The company president ordered everyone to work overtime.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)から退去(たいきょ)命令(めいれい)が出(で)た。
An eviction order was issued by the court.
彼(かれ)は部下(ぶか)に命令(めいれい)ばかりして、自分(じぶん)では何(なに)もしない。
He does nothing but order his subordinates around and doesn't do anything himself.
2. imperative (grammar)
The grammatical form used for commands.
命令形(めいれいけい)を使(つか)って文(ぶん)を作(つく)りなさい。
Make sentences using the imperative form.
Grammar instruction
「食(た)べろ」は「食(た)べる」の命令形(めいれいけい)です。
"Tabero" is the imperative form of "taberu."
日本語(にほんご)の命令形(めいれいけい)は男性(だんせい)が使(つか)うことが多(おお)い。
The imperative form in Japanese is more often used by men.
命令文(めいれいぶん)は相手(あいて)に直接(ちょくせつ)行動(こうどう)を求(もと)める表現(ひょうげん)だ。
An imperative sentence is an expression that directly demands action from the listener.
命令(めいれい)の口調(くちょう)は目上(めうえ)の人(ひと)に使(つか)ってはいけない。
You must not use a commanding tone with people above you in status.

命令(めいれい) is an authoritative directive from someone with the power to give it — a superior, a court, an employer, a program. It is stronger and less negotiable than 指示(しじ) (instruction) and far stronger than お願(ねが)い (request); using it of an equal sounds high-handed.

GRAMMAR:

  • As a する-verb the person commanded takes に and the thing commanded takes を: 社長(しゃちょう)が全員(ぜんいん)に残業(ざんぎょう)を命令(めいれい)した.
  • The passive 命令(めいれい)される (be ordered to) is common, since the receiving end is usually the speaker's viewpoint.
  • As a noun it compounds freely: 退去(たいきょ)命令(めいれい) (eviction order), 命令(めいれい)口調(くちょう) (commanding tone).

SENSE 2 (grammar):
In language teaching 命令(めいれい) is the technical label for the imperative, appearing almost always inside 命令形(めいれいけい) (imperative form) or 命令文(めいれいぶん) (imperative sentence) rather than on its own.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 命令(めいれい)する (to order, to command)
  • 命令(めいれい)を出(だ)す (to issue an order)
  • 命令(めいれい)を受(う)ける (to receive an order)
  • 命令(めいれい)に従(したが)う (to obey an order)
  • 命令(めいれい)に背(そむ)く (to disobey an order)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 指示(しじ) (instruction, direction - softer)
  • 指令(しれい) (order, command - formal/military)
  • お願(ねが)い (request - polite alternative)
