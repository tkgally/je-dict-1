慣行(かんこう)

かんこう
noun
customary practice, convention
1. customary practice, convention, established custom
A practice that has become standard through tradition or habit.
長年(ながねん)の慣行(かんこう)に従(したが)う。
To follow long-established customs.
商(しょう)慣行(かんこう)は国(くに)によって異(こと)なる。
Business practices differ by country.
その慣行(かんこう)は時代(じだい)遅(おく)れだ。
That practice is outdated.

Often used in formal or business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 商(しょう)慣行(かんこう) (business practice/custom)
  • 慣行(かんこう)に従(したが)う (to follow customary practice)
  • 慣行(かんこう)を改(あらた)める (to reform a practice)
  • 労働(ろうどう)慣行(かんこう) (labor practices)

HOMOPHONES (all かんこう):

  • 観光(かんこう) - sightseeing, tourism
  • 刊行(かんこう) - publication
  • 敢行(かんこう) - carrying out (decisively)
  • 慣行(かんこう) - customary practice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 習慣(しゅうかん) (habit, custom - personal or social)
  • 慣例(かんれい) (precedent, convention)
  • 慣行(かんこう) (established practice - often institutional)
