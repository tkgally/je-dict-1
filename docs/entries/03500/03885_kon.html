紺(こん)

こん
noun
navy blue, dark blue
1. navy blue, dark blue
A deep, dark shade of blue, commonly used for uniforms and formal attire.
紺(こん)のスーツを着(き)ている。
He's wearing a navy blue suit.
学校(がっこう)の制服(せいふく)は紺色(こんいろ)だ。
The school uniform is navy blue.
紺(こん)と白(しろ)のストライプのネクタイが似合(にあ)っている。
The navy and white striped tie suits you.

紺(こん) is a traditional Japanese color name for dark blue or navy blue. It's one of the most common colors for school uniforms and business attire in Japan.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 紺色(こんいろ) (navy blue color)
  • 紺(こん)のスーツ (navy blue suit)
  • 濃紺(のうこん) (very dark navy blue)
