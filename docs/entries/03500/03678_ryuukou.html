流行(りゅうこう)

りゅうこう
noun
trend, fashion
1. trend, fashion, vogue
A popular style, practice, or interest that is widely followed at a particular time.
2. epidemic, outbreak
The rapid spread of a disease.
今(いま)の流行(りゅうこう)は何(なん)ですか。
What's the current trend?
このデザインは去年(きょねん)流行(りゅうこう)した。
This design was popular last year.
インフルエンザが流行(りゅうこう)している。
The flu is going around.
Medical usage: epidemic/outbreak

USAGE: 流行(りゅうこう) has two main meanings: (1) fashion/trends in culture and society, and (2) the spread of diseases.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 流行(りゅうこう)する (to be in fashion, to spread)
  • 流行(りゅうこう)に乗(の)る (to follow a trend)
  • 流行(りゅうこう)を追(お)う (to chase trends)
  • 大(だい)流行(りゅうこう) (big trend, epidemic)
  • 流行(りゅうこう)語(ご) (buzzword, trendy word)

NOTE: Can also be read as はやり in casual speech, especially for fashion/trends.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • トレンド - trend (loanword)
  • ブーム - boom, craze
