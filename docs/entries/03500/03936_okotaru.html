怠(おこた)る

おこたる
verb (godan)
to neglect; to be negligent; to fail to do
1. to neglect; to be negligent
To fail to do something that should be done, often due to laziness or carelessness.
勉強(べんきょう)を怠(おこた)ると、成績(せいせき)が下(さ)がる。
If you neglect your studies, your grades will drop.
Sense 1: neglecting a duty
安全(あんぜん)確認(かくにん)を怠(おこた)ってはいけない。
You must not neglect safety checks.
[Note: 怠(おこた)っては + いけない = must not neglect]
努力(どりょく)を怠(おこた)らなければ、必(かなら)ず成功(せいこう)する。
If you don't slack off on your efforts, you will surely succeed.
Negative conditional form
2. to be lazy about; to slack off on
To not put sufficient effort into a duty or task.

怠(おこた)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)). It takes を to mark what is being neglected.

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form indicates an ongoing state of neglect (義務(ぎむ)を怠(おこた)っている = 'is neglecting one's duty').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [duty/task]を怠(おこた)る (neglect ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 義務(ぎむ)を怠(おこた)る (neglect a duty)
  • 努力(どりょく)を怠(おこた)る (slack off on effort)
  • 注意(ちゅうい)を怠(おこた)る (be negligent in attention)
  • 健康(けんこう)管理(かんり)を怠(おこた)る (neglect health management)

This verb has a somewhat formal tone and is often used in written contexts. The related noun is 怠慢(たいまん) (negligence).
