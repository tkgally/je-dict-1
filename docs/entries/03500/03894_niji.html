虹(にじ)

にじ
noun
rainbow
1. rainbow
An arc of colors visible in the sky, caused by the refraction and dispersion of sunlight through rain droplets.
雨上(あめあ)がりに虹(にじ)が出(で)た。
A rainbow appeared after the rain stopped.
空(そら)に大(おお)きな虹(にじ)がかかっている。
A large rainbow stretches across the sky.
虹(にじ)を見(み)ると幸(しあわ)せな気持(きも)ちになる。
Seeing a rainbow makes me feel happy.

虹(にじ) refers to the natural phenomenon of a rainbow.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 虹(にじ)がかかる (a rainbow appears/spans across)
  • 虹(にじ)が出(で)る (a rainbow appears)
  • 虹色(にじいろ) (rainbow colors)
  • 二重(にじゅう)の虹(にじ) (double rainbow)

JAPANESE COLOR TRADITION:
In Japanese tradition, rainbows are often described as having 7 colors: 赤(あか)、橙(だいだい)、黄(き)、緑(みどり)、青(あお)、藍(あい)、紫(むらさき) (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet).
