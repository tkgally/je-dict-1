棒(ぼう)

ぼう
noun
stick, pole, rod
1. stick, pole, rod
A long, thin piece of wood or other material.
棒(ぼう)を持(も)つ。
To hold a stick.
棒(ぼう)で地面(じめん)に線(せん)を引(ひ)いた。
I drew a line on the ground with a stick.
鉄(てつ)棒(ぼう)にぶら下(さ)がる。
To hang from an iron bar.
子供(こども)が棒(ぼう)を振(ふ)り回(まわ)して遊(あそ)んでいる。
The child is playing by swinging a stick around.
せっかくの努力(どりょく)を棒(ぼう)に振(ふ)ってしまった。
I ended up wasting all my hard work.
棒(ぼう)に振(ふ)る = to waste, throw away
2. bar, stroke
A straight line drawn on paper.
棒(ぼう)を引(ひ)く。
To draw a line.
棒(ぼう)グラフを作(つく)る。
To make a bar graph.
名前(なまえ)の上(うえ)に棒(ぼう)を引(ひ)いて消(け)した。
I crossed out the name by drawing a line over it.
棒読(ぼうよ)みで台詞(せりふ)を言(い)った。
I said the lines in a monotone.
棒読(ぼうよ)み = flat, monotone reading
赤(あか)い棒(ぼう)と青(あお)い棒(ぼう)で比較(ひかく)を表(あらわ)すグラフを書(か)いた。
I drew a graph that shows the comparison using red and blue bars.

棒(ぼう) is any straight, rigid length of material — a stick, pole, rod, or bar. Sense 2 extends this shape to marks on a surface: a straight stroke of the pen, or the bars of a bar chart.

The word is shape-based, not function-based, which is why it appears in so many compounds: whatever the object is for, if it is long and straight it can be a 棒(ぼう).

Common compounds:

  • 鉄棒(てつぼう): the horizontal bar in gymnastics
  • 棒(ぼう)グラフ: bar graph
  • 泥棒(どろぼう): thief (historically opaque — the modern word is not felt as containing 'stick')
  • 棒読(ぼうよ)み: reading aloud in a flat monotone, as though the pitch were drawn with a ruler

Idioms:

  • 棒(ぼう)に振(ふ)る: to throw away or waste something you had built up — effort, money, a career.
  • 犬(いぬ)も歩(ある)けば棒(ぼう)に当(あ)たる: 'even a dog that walks about will bump into a stick.' A proverb used both ways — act and you may meet misfortune, or act and you may stumble on luck.

Similar words: a 杖(つえ) is a stick used for support when walking; a 竿(さお) is a long slender pole, as for fishing or hanging laundry.
