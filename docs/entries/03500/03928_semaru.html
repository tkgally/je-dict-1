迫(せま)る

せまる
godan verb
to approach, to press, to urge
1. to approach, to draw near
For something (especially a deadline, danger, etc.) to come close.
締(し)め切(き)りが迫(せま)っている。
The deadline is approaching.
危険(きけん)が迫(せま)ってきた。
Danger is drawing near.
2. to press, to urge, to demand
To strongly demand or pressure someone.
彼(かれ)に回答(かいとう)を迫(せま)った。
I pressed him for an answer.
3. to be pressing, to be imminent
For a situation to become urgent.

迫(せま)る means to approach (usually something threatening or urgent) or to press/demand.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive/Transitive (自動詞(じどうし)/他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xが迫(せま)る (something approaches); Xに迫(せま)る (to press X)

ASPECT:

  • 迫(せま)っている: Is approaching, is imminent (state)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 締(し)め切(き)りが迫(せま)る (the deadline approaches)
  • 危機(きき)が迫(せま)る (a crisis looms)
  • 返事(へんじ)を迫(せま)る (to press for a reply)
  • 真相(しんそう)に迫(せま)る (to get close to the truth)
  • 迫力(はくりょく)がある (to be powerful, compelling)

NOTE: Not to be confused with 狭(せま)い (narrow).
