荒廃(こうはい)

こうはい
noun / suru-verb
ruin, devastation, decay
1. ruin, devastation
The state of being destroyed or severely damaged.
戦争(せんそう)で町(まち)が荒廃(こうはい)した。
The town was devastated by war.
荒廃(こうはい)した土地(とち)を再生(さいせい)する。
To restore the ruined land.
2. decay, desolation
Physical or moral deterioration.
心(こころ)の荒廃(こうはい)が問題(もんだい)だ。
The decay of the spirit is a problem.
Figurative usage

Can refer to physical destruction or moral/spiritual decline.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荒廃(こうはい)する (to fall into ruin)
  • 荒廃(こうはい)した町(まち)/土地(とち) (ruined town/land)
  • 国土(こくど)の荒廃(こうはい) (devastation of the land)
  • 心(こころ)の荒廃(こうはい) (spiritual decay)
  • 荒廃(こうはい)を招(まね)く (to bring about ruin)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 後輩(こうはい) - junior (in school or work)

These two words are both read こうはい but have completely different meanings.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 廃墟(はいきょ) (ruins)
  • 衰退(すいたい) (decline, decay)
  • 荒(あ)れる (to become desolate)
