父母(ふぼ)

ふぼ
noun
parents; father and mother
1. parents; father and mother
Both parents together. More formal than 両親(りょうしん).
父母(ふぼ)に感謝(かんしゃ)している。
I am grateful to my parents.
父母(ふぼ)会(かい)に出席(しゅっせき)した。
I attended the parent-teacher meeting.
父母(ふぼ)の恩(おん)は深(ふか)い。
The debt of gratitude to one's parents is deep.

USAGE: Formal written term for parents. 両親(りょうしん) is more common in everyday speech.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 父母(ふぼ)会(かい) (PTA meeting, parents' association)
  • 祖(そ)父母(ふぼ) (grandparents)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 両親(りょうしん) - parents (more common in speech)
  • お父(とう)さんとお母(かあ)さん - father and mother (casual)
  • 親(おや) - parent(s) (general term)

NOTE: Can also be read as ちちはは in some contexts, though ふぼ is more common.

Related Words

Synonym: 両親(りょうしん) (common synonym)
