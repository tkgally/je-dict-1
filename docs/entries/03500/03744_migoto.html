見事(みごと)

みごと
na-adjective / adverb
splendid, magnificent, admirable
1. splendid, magnificent, superb
Worthy of admiration due to excellence or beauty.
見事(みごと)な演技(えんぎ)だった。
It was a splendid performance.
試験(しけん)に見事(みごと)合格(ごうかく)した。
I passed the exam brilliantly.
Adverbial use
2. complete, thorough
Done completely or thoroughly (can be positive or ironic).
見事(みごと)に失敗(しっぱい)した。
I failed spectacularly.
Ironic usage - complete failure

USAGE:

1. As na-adjective:

  • 見事(みごと)な〜 (magnificent ~)
  • 見事(みごと)な桜(さくら) (magnificent cherry blossoms)

2. As adverb (見事(みごと)に):

  • 見事(みごと)に成功(せいこう)する (to succeed magnificently)
  • 見事(みごと)に失敗(しっぱい)する (to fail spectacularly - ironic)

NUANCE:
When used with negative outcomes, 見事(みごと)に adds irony, emphasizing how complete or thorough the failure was.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 素晴(すば)らしい (wonderful, marvelous)
  • 立派(りっぱ) (splendid, fine)
general
