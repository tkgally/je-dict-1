塊(かたまり)

かたまり
noun
lump, mass, cluster
1. lump, mass, chunk
A solid piece of something, or things grouped tightly together.
肉(にく)の塊(かたまり)を切(き)り分(わ)ける。
Cut up the chunk of meat.
粘土(ねんど)の塊(かたまり)で何(なに)か作(つく)ろう。
Let's make something from this lump of clay.
2. embodiment (figurative)
A person who strongly represents a particular quality.
彼(かれ)は正義感(せいぎかん)の塊(かたまり)のような人(ひと)だ。
He is the embodiment of a sense of justice.

塊(かたまり) refers to a solid mass or lump. It can also be used figuratively for someone who embodies a particular quality.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 肉(にく)の塊(かたまり) (chunk of meat)
  • 雪(ゆき)の塊(かたまり) (lump of snow)
  • ～の塊(かたまり) (the embodiment of ~)
  • 塊(かたまり)になる (to form a lump)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 固(かた)まる (to harden, to clump together) [verb]
  • 団子(だんご) (ball, dumpling)
