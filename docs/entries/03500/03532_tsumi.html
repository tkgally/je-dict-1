罪(つみ)

つみ
noun
crime, sin, guilt
1. crime, offense
An act that violates the law.
2. sin, wrongdoing
An immoral act, especially in a religious or moral sense.
3. guilt, fault
The state of being responsible for wrongdoing.
罪(つみ)を犯(おか)した。
I committed a crime/sin.
彼(かれ)に罪(つみ)はない。
He is not guilty. / It's not his fault.
罪(つみ)の意識(いしき)に苦(くる)しんでいる。
I am tormented by feelings of guilt.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 罪(つみ)を犯(おか)す (to commit a crime/sin)
  • 罪(つみ)に問(と)う (to charge with a crime)
  • 罪(つみ)を償(つぐな)う (to atone for one's sins)
  • 罪(つみ)を認(みと)める (to admit guilt)

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 犯罪(はんざい) (crime)
  • 有罪(ゆうざい) (guilty)
  • 無罪(むざい) (innocent, not guilty)
  • 罪悪感(ざいあくかん) (sense of guilt)

COMMON EXPRESSION:

  • 罪(つみ)な人(ひと) (a cruel person, someone who makes others suffer)
