総(そう)〜

そう
prefix
general; total; overall
1. total; gross; overall
Prefix indicating the complete sum or entirety.
総額(そうがく)は百万円(ひゃくまんえん)になった。
The total amount came to one million yen.
総(そう) + 額(がく) = total amount
2. general; chief
Prefix indicating the head or overall position.
総理(そうり)大臣(だいじん)が会見(かいけん)を開(ひら)いた。
The Prime Minister held a press conference.
総(そう) + 理(り) = general management (Prime Minister)
総選挙(そうせんきょ)が来月(らいげつ)行(おこな)われる。
A general election will be held next month.
総(そう) + 選挙(せんきょ) = general election

総(そう)〜 is a prefix meaning 'total' or 'general/chief.'

COMMON COMPOUNDS (TOTAL):

  • 総額(そうがく) (total amount)
  • 総数(そうすう) (total number)
  • 総計(そうけい) (grand total)
  • 総合(そうごう) (comprehensive; synthesis)
  • 総務(そうむ) (general affairs)

COMMON COMPOUNDS (GENERAL/CHIEF):

  • 総理(そうり) (Prime Minister; management)
  • 総長(そうちょう) (president of university; director-general)
  • 総裁(そうさい) (president; governor)
  • 総選挙(そうせんきょ) (general election)
  • 総会(そうかい) (general meeting)

RELATED:

  • 全(ぜん)〜 (all; whole) - similar meaning but different nuance
  • 総(そう) emphasizes aggregation; 全(ぜん) emphasizes completeness
