削(けず)る

けずる
godan verb
to shave, to plane, to whittle; to cut, to reduce
1. to shave, to plane, to whittle
To remove thin layers from a surface, especially wood.
鰹節(かつおぶし)を削(けず)って使(つか)う。
Shave dried bonito and use it.
木(き)を削(けず)って形(かたち)を作(つく)る。
Whittle wood into a shape.
Sense 1: to whittle
かんなで板(いた)を削(けず)る。
Plane a board with a plane.
Sense 1: to plane
氷(こおり)を削(けず)ってかき氷(ごおり)を作(つく)る。
Shave ice to make shaved ice.
Sense 1: to shave
爪(つめ)を削(けず)って整(ととの)える。
File and shape the nails.
Sense 1: to shave/file
2. to sharpen (a pencil)
To make a pencil point sharp by removing wood.
鉛筆(えんぴつ)を削(けず)る。
Sharpen a pencil.
鉛筆削(えんぴつけず)りで削(けず)る。
Sharpen with a pencil sharpener.
Sense 2: to sharpen
芯(しん)が丸(まる)くなったので削(けず)った。
The lead became dull, so I sharpened it.
Sense 2: to sharpen
ナイフで鉛筆(えんぴつ)を削(けず)る。
Sharpen a pencil with a knife.
Sense 2: to sharpen
色(いろ)鉛筆(えんぴつ)を削(けず)った。
I sharpened the colored pencils.
Sense 2: to sharpen
3. to cut, to reduce, to delete
To reduce or remove something, such as a budget or text.
予算(よさん)を削(けず)られた。
The budget was cut.
経費(けいひ)を削(けず)る必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to cut expenses.
Sense 3: to cut/reduce
人件費(じんけんひ)を削(けず)られた。
Personnel costs were cut.
Sense 3: to reduce
原稿(げんこう)を削(けず)って短(みじか)くした。
I edited the manuscript to make it shorter.
Sense 3: to delete
睡眠時間(すいみんじかん)を削(けず)って働(はたら)く。
To work by cutting into one's sleep time.
Sense 3: to cut

削(けず)る means to shave, scrape, or cut away material from a surface.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 削(けず)れる (intransitive - to be shaved off, to wear away)
  • Pattern: Xを削(けず)る

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 鉛筆(えんぴつ)を削(けず)る (to sharpen a pencil)
  • 予算(よさん)を削(けず)る (to cut a budget)
  • 文章(ぶんしょう)を削(けず)る (to cut/edit text)
  • 鰹節(かつおぶし)を削(けず)る (to shave dried bonito)
  • 身(み)を削(けず)る (to work extremely hard, lit: to shave one's body)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 削減(さくげん)する (to reduce, to cut - formal)
  • 彫(ほ)る (to carve, to sculpt)
