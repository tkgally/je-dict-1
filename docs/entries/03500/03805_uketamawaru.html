承(うけたまわ)る

うけたまわる
verb (godan)
to hear; to receive (humble)

Definitions

1. to hear; to be told (humble)
Humble form of 聞く (to hear). Used when receiving information from someone of higher status.
2. to receive; to accept (humble)
Humble form of receiving orders, requests, or tasks. Used in business and formal contexts.

Examples

ご注文(ちゅうもん)を承(うけたまわ)りました。
I have received your order.
Common phrase in customer service
ご予約(よやく)を承(うけたまわ)ります。
I will take your reservation.
お名前(なまえ)を承(うけたまわ)ってもよろしいですか。
May I have your name?
Very polite inquiry

Notes

REGISTER: Very formal/humble (謙譲語(けんじょうご)). Used in business and service contexts.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを承(うけたまわ)る.

USAGE: This is a humble verb used when the speaker receives something from someone of higher status.

COMMON IN:

  • Customer service (receiving orders, reservations)
  • Business settings (receiving instructions)
  • Formal correspondence

NORMAL EQUIVALENTS:

  • 聞(き)く (to hear - neutral)
  • 受(う)ける (to receive - neutral)
  • 伺(うかが)う (to hear/ask - humble but less formal)

NOTE: Often appears as 承(うけたまわ)りました (received) in service industry.

Related Words

Related: 聞(き)く (neutral form)
Related: うかがう (humble (less formal))
