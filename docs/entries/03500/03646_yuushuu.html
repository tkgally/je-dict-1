優秀(ゆうしゅう)

ゆうしゅう
な-adjective
excellent, outstanding
1. excellent, outstanding, superior
Of exceptionally high quality or ability; excelling in a particular area.
彼女(かのじょ)は優秀(ゆうしゅう)な学生(がくせい)です。
She is an excellent student.
優秀(ゆうしゅう)な成績(せいせき)で卒業(そつぎょう)した。
I graduated with excellent grades.
優秀(ゆうしゅう)な人材(じんざい)を採用(さいよう)したい。
We want to hire outstanding talent.
優秀な人材 = excellent talent/personnel

USAGE: 優秀(ゆうしゅう) is used to describe people, work, or results that are notably superior to others. It's commonly used in academic and professional contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう)な学生(がくせい) (excellent student)
  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう)な成績(せいせき) (excellent grades)
  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう)な人材(じんざい) (outstanding talent)
  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう)賞(しょう) (excellence award)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 優(すぐ)れた - excellent, superior
  • 立派(りっぱ) - splendid, admirable
