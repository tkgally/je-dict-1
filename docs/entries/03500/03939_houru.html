放(ほう)る

ほうる
verb (godan)
to throw; to toss; to abandon
1. to throw; to toss; to fling
To send something through the air with a casual motion.
ボールを放(ほう)った。
I tossed the ball.
Sense 1: throwing casually
2. to abandon; to leave alone; to neglect
To leave something without care or attention.
仕事(しごと)を放(ほう)って遊(あそ)びに行(い)った。
I abandoned my work and went to have fun.
Sense 2: neglecting/abandoning
その問題(もんだい)は放(ほう)っておけない。
I can't just leave that problem alone.
[Note: 放(ほう)っておく = leave alone]

放(ほう)る is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)).

ASPECT ({ている}):
The ている form can indicate ongoing neglect (子供(こども)を放(ほう)っている = 'is neglecting the child').

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • [object]を放(ほう)る (throw/abandon ~)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ボールを放(ほう)る (toss a ball)
  • 放(ほう)っておく (leave alone)
  • 放(ほう)っておいて (leave me alone; let it be)
  • 仕事(しごと)を放(ほう)り出(だ)す (abandon work midway)

The compound 放(ほう)っておく is extremely common in spoken Japanese for 'leave alone' or 'let it be.' The more emphatic 放(ほう)り出(だ)す means to abandon something abruptly.
