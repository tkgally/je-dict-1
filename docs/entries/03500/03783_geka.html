外科(げか)

げか
noun
surgery (medical dept.)

Definitions

1. surgery; surgical department
The branch of medicine dealing with surgical procedures. Also refers to the surgical department in a hospital.

Examples

外科(げか)で手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)けた。
I had surgery in the surgical department.
彼女(かのじょ)は外科(げか)医(い)になりたい。
She wants to become a surgeon.
整形(せいけい)外科(げか)で膝(ひざ)を診(み)てもらった。
I had my knee examined at orthopedic surgery.

Notes

USAGE: Used both for the medical specialty and the hospital department.

COMMON TYPES:

  • 整形(せいけい)外科(げか) (orthopedic surgery)
  • 脳(のう)外科(げか) (neurosurgery)
  • 心臓(しんぞう)外科(げか) (cardiac surgery)
  • 美容(びよう)外科(げか) (cosmetic surgery)

RELATED:

  • 外科(げか)医(い) (surgeon)
  • 外科(げか)手術(しゅじゅつ) (surgical operation)

CONTRAST: 内科(ないか) (internal medicine) is the non-surgical counterpart.

Related Words

Antonym: 内科(ないか) (internal medicine)
