1. to mince, to chop finely
To cut food into small pieces.
玉(たま)ねぎを細(こま)かく刻(きざ)む。
Mince the onion finely.
ニンジンを細(ほそ)く刻(きざ)む。
Chop the carrot into thin pieces.
刻(きざ)んだネギを味噌汁(みそしる)に入(い)れる。
Put chopped green onion into the miso soup.
2. to carve, to engrave
To cut or etch a design, pattern, or letters into a hard surface.
木(き)に名前(なまえ)を刻(きざ)んだ。
I carved my name into the tree.
石碑(せきひ)に名前(なまえ)が刻(きざ)まれている。
Names are engraved on the stone monument.
この言葉(ことば)を心(こころ)に刻(きざ)んでおきたい。
I want to engrave these words in my heart.
3. to tick away (time)
For time to pass moment by moment.
時計(とけい)が時(とき)を刻(きざ)んでいる。
The clock is ticking away the time.
秒針(びょうしん)が静(しず)かに時(とき)を刻(きざ)んでいる。
The second hand is quietly ticking away the time.
新(あたら)しい歴史(れきし)を刻(きざ)む瞬間(しゅんかん)だった。
It was a moment that carved a new page in history.

刻(きざ)む has two main meanings: cutting food finely, and carving/engraving.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを刻(きざ)む

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 細(こま)かく刻(きざ)む (to chop finely)
  • 時(とき)を刻(きざ)む (to tick away time)
  • 心(こころ)に刻(きざ)む (to engrave in one's heart, to remember deeply)
  • 歴史(れきし)に刻(きざ)む (to be recorded in history)

COOKING VOCABULARY:

  • 刻(きざ)みネギ(ねぎ) (chopped green onion)
  • 刻(きざ)み海苔(のり) (shredded nori seaweed)
  • みじん切(ぎ)り (dicing, similar to 刻(きざ)む)
