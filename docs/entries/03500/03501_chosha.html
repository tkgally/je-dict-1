著者(ちょしゃ)

ちょしゃ
noun
author, writer
1. author, writer
A person who has written a book or other written work.
この本(ほん)の著者(ちょしゃ)は有名(ゆうめい)な科学者(かがくしゃ)だ。
The author of this book is a famous scientist.
著者(ちょしゃ)に会(あ)いたい。
I want to meet the author.
著者(ちょしゃ)紹介(しょうかい)を読(よ)んだ。
I read the author's introduction.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 著者(ちょしゃ): Author (of books, academic works)
  • 作者(さくしゃ): Author, creator (of literary works, manga, plays)
  • 作家(さっか): Writer, novelist (professional title)
  • 筆者(ひっしゃ): Writer (of the text you're reading, often essays/articles)

USAGE NOTE:
著者(ちょしゃ) is commonly used in academic or formal contexts, while 作者(さくしゃ) is broader and includes creative works.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 著者(ちょしゃ)紹介(しょうかい) (author introduction/bio)
  • 著者(ちょしゃ)名(めい) (author's name)

Related Words

Related: 作者(さくしゃ) (creator)
