曜日(ようび)

ようび
noun, suffix
day of the week
1. day of the week
One of the seven days that make up a week.
今日(きょう)は何(なん)曜日(ようび)ですか。
What day of the week is it today?
月曜日(げつようび)から金曜日(きんようび)まで学校(がっこう)がある。
There's school from Monday to Friday.
好(す)きな曜日(ようび)は土曜日(どようび)です。
My favorite day of the week is Saturday.
毎週(まいしゅう)金曜日(きんようび)にピアノを習(なら)っている。
I take piano lessons every Friday.
曜日(ようび)を間違(まちが)えて、授業(じゅぎょう)に来(き)てしまった。
I got the day wrong and came to class by mistake.

USAGE:
曜日(ようび) is both a stand-alone noun meaning 'day of the week' as a category, and the suffix that turns one of the seven element kanji into a weekday name. The element kanji takes its on'yomi before it, so the seven names are formed regularly and only need to be learned once.

DAYS OF THE WEEK:

  • 日曜日(にちようび) (Sunday) - 日(にち) = sun
  • 月曜日(げつようび) (Monday) - 月(げつ) = moon
  • 火曜日(かようび) (Tuesday) - 火(か) = fire
  • 水曜日(すいようび) (Wednesday) - 水(すい) = water
  • 木曜日(もくようび) (Thursday) - 木(もく) = wood
  • 金曜日(きんようび) (Friday) - 金(きん) = gold/metal
  • 土曜日(どようび) (Saturday) - 土(ど) = earth

SHORTENED FORM:
In timetables, TV listings, store signs, and casual speech, the final 日(び) is often dropped: 火曜(かよう) for 火曜日(かようび), 水曜(すいよう) for 水曜日(すいようび), and so on. The short form is normal in writing and in speech among friends, but the full form with 日(び) is always safe and is what learners should use by default.

COMMON QUESTIONS:

  • 何(なん)曜日(ようび)？ (What day?)
  • 今日(きょう)は何(なん)曜日(ようび)？ (What day is today?)

RELATED TIME WORDS:
週(しゅう) (week), 平日(へいじつ) (weekday), 週末(しゅうまつ) (weekend).
