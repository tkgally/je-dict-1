チャンス

ちゃんす
noun
chance, opportunity
1. chance, opportunity
A favorable time or situation for doing something.
これはチャンスだ！
This is a chance!
チャンスを逃(のが)さないように。
Don't miss the opportunity.
チャンスを逃(のが)す = to miss a chance
海外(かいがい)で働(はたら)くチャンスを得(え)た。
I got the opportunity to work overseas.

チャンス comes from English "chance" but is used primarily in the sense of "opportunity" rather than probability or luck.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • チャンスをつかむ: to seize a chance
  • チャンスを逃(のが)す: to miss a chance
  • チャンスを与(あた)える: to give a chance
  • チャンスを生(い)かす: to make the most of a chance
  • チャンスを待(ま)つ: to wait for a chance
  • 絶好(ぜっこう)のチャンス: a golden opportunity
  • ラストチャンス: last chance

VS. 機会(きかい):

  • チャンス: opportunity (casual, positive nuance — implies favorable timing)
  • 機会(きかい): opportunity (formal, neutral — used in speeches, writing, business)

Antonym: ピンチ (pinch, crisis)
