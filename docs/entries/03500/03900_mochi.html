餅(もち)

もち
noun
rice cake, mochi

Definitions

1. rice cake, mochi
A sticky Japanese rice cake made from glutinous rice that has been pounded into a paste.

Examples

正月(しょうがつ)に餅(もち)を食(た)べる。
We eat mochi at New Year's.
餅(もち)をつく音(おと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the sound of pounding mochi.
餅(もち)は喉(のど)に詰(つ)まりやすいので気(き)をつけて。
Be careful because mochi can easily get stuck in your throat.

Notes

餅(もち) is a traditional Japanese food made from glutinous rice (餅米(もちごめ)) that is steamed and pounded.

COMMON TYPES:

  • お雑煮(ぞうに) (mochi soup eaten at New Year's)
  • 草餅(くさもち) (mugwort mochi)
  • 大福(だいふく) (mochi filled with sweet bean paste)
  • 切(き)り餅(もち) (pre-cut mochi squares)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 餅(もち)をつく (to pound mochi)
  • 餅(もち)を焼(や)く (to grill/toast mochi)
  • 餅(もち)が伸(の)びる (mochi stretches)

SAFETY NOTE:
Every year in Japan, people (especially elderly) choke on mochi. It should be eaten carefully, in small pieces.
