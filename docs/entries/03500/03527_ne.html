値(ね)

ね
noun
price, value
1. price, cost
The amount of money required to purchase something.
この品物(しなもの)は値(ね)が高(たか)い。
This item is expensive.
値が高い = expensive (lit. price is high)
値(ね)を下(さ)げてください。
Please lower the price.
2. value, worth
The importance or usefulness of something.
努力(どりょく)に見合(みあ)った値(ね)打(う)ちがある。
It has worth commensurate with the effort.
値打ち = value, worth

USAGE: 値(ね) refers to price or value. It's commonly used in expressions about pricing and worth. Note: 値 can also be read as あたい (value, especially in math/science).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 値(ね)が高(たか)い／安(やす)い (expensive/cheap)
  • 値(ね)を上(あ)げる／下(さ)げる (to raise/lower the price)
  • 値(ね)が上(あ)がる／下(さ)がる (price goes up/down)
  • 値(ね)段(だん) (price)
  • 値(ね)引(び)き (discount)
  • 値(ね)打(う)ち (value, worth)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 値(ね)段(だん) (price - most common word for price)
  • 値(ね)札(ふだ) (price tag)
  • 底値(そこね) (lowest price)
  • 定価(ていか) (list price, fixed price)

NOTE: 値段(ねだん) is the most common word for 'price' in everyday contexts.
