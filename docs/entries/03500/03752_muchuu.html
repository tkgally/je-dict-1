夢中(むちゅう)

むちゅう
noun / na-adjective
absorbed, engrossed, crazy about
1. absorbed, engrossed
Being completely focused on something to the exclusion of all else.
ゲームに夢中(むちゅう)になっている。
I'm absorbed in the game.
夢中(むちゅう)で走(はし)った。
I ran frantically (without thinking).
Adverbial use
2. crazy about, hooked on
Being extremely enthusiastic about something.
ゲームに夢中(むちゅう)になっている。
I'm absorbed in the game.
彼(かれ)は彼女(かのじょ)に夢中(むちゅう)だ。
He's crazy about her.

Literally means "in a dream" (夢(ゆめ) = dream, 中(ちゅう) = inside).

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 〜に夢中(むちゅう)になる (to become absorbed in ~)
  • 〜に夢中(むちゅう)だ (to be crazy about ~)
  • 夢中(むちゅう)で〜する (to do ~ frantically/absently)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゲームに夢中(むちゅう) (absorbed in games)
  • 仕事(しごと)に夢中(むちゅう) (absorbed in work)
  • 恋(こい)に夢中(むちゅう) (crazy in love)

NUANCE:
夢中(むちゅう) can imply being so focused that one loses awareness of surroundings or time, similar to being in a trance or dreamlike state.
