釜(かま)

かま
noun
iron pot, cauldron, kettle

Definitions

1. iron pot, cauldron
A large metal cooking pot, traditionally made of iron, used for cooking rice and other dishes.
2. boiler, kiln
Used in compounds to refer to various types of furnaces or kilns, such as pottery kilns.

Examples

釜(かま)でご飯(はん)を炊(た)く。
Cook rice in an iron pot.
昔(むかし)は家(いえ)ごとに大(おお)きな釜(かま)があった。
In the old days, every house had a large iron pot.
この窯(かま)は陶芸(とうげい)用(よう)です。
This kiln is for pottery.
[Note: 窯(かま) uses a different kanji for kiln/furnace]

Notes

釜(かま) refers to a traditional iron pot or cauldron used for cooking, particularly for rice. In modern Japan, 炊飯器(すいはんき) (rice cooker) has largely replaced the traditional 釜(かま).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 釜(かま)を炊(た)く (to cook in a pot)
  • ご飯(はん)を釜(かま)で炊(た)く (to cook rice in a traditional pot)
  • 風呂釜(ふろがま) (bathtub heater)

RELATED KANJI:

  • 釜(かま): iron pot for cooking
  • 窯(かま): kiln for pottery or bricks (same reading, different kanji)
