仏(ほとけ)

ほとけ
noun
Buddha; deceased person
1. Buddha
The enlightened one in Buddhism; a Buddhist deity.
2. deceased person, departed soul
A person who has died (respectful term).
3. merciful person
Someone who is extremely kind or forgiving.
仏(ほとけ)に祈(いの)る。
To pray to Buddha.
仏(ほとけ)の顔(かお)も三度(さんど)まで。
Even Buddha's patience has its limits.
Proverb: even a kind person loses patience after repeated offenses
仏様(ほとけさま)にお花(はな)を供(そな)える。
To offer flowers to the Buddha/deceased.

Has religious, respectful, and colloquial uses.

MEANING 1 - BUDDHA:

  • 仏像(ぶつぞう) (Buddha statue)
  • 仏教(ぶっきょう) (Buddhism)
  • 仏様(ほとけさま) (respectful form)

MEANING 2 - DECEASED:

  • 仏様(ほとけさま) (the deceased - respectful)
  • 仏壇(ぶつだん) (Buddhist altar)
  • Often used euphemistically

PROVERBS:

  • 仏(ほとけ)の顔(かお)も三度(さんど)(まで) (Even Buddha loses patience after three times)
  • 仏(ほとけ)作(つく)って魂(たましい)入(い)れず (Making a Buddha without putting in the soul - incomplete work)

NOTE ON READINGS:

  • ほとけ - native Japanese reading (kun'yomi)
  • ぶつ - Sino-Japanese reading (on'yomi, used in compounds)
