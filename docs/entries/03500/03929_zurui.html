狡(ずる)い

ずるい
i-adjective
sly, cunning, unfair

Definitions

1. sly, cunning, crafty
Cleverly deceptive or dishonest in achieving one's aims.
2. unfair, sneaky
Not following the rules or playing fairly.

Examples

彼(かれ)は狡(ずる)い手(て)を使(つか)った。
He used sneaky tactics.
ずるい！私(わたし)も欲(ほ)しい！
No fair! I want some too!
狡(ずる)いことをしないで正々堂々(せいせいどうどう)と戦(たたか)いなさい。
Don't play dirty - fight fair and square.

Notes

狡(ずる)い describes someone who is sly, cunning, or unfair in their methods.

FORMS:

  • 狡(ずる)く (adverbial form)
  • 狡(ずる)さ (noun form - slyness)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 狡(ずる)い手(て)を使(つか)う (to use sneaky tactics)
  • 狡(ずる)休|やす}みをする (to skip school/work with a fake excuse)
  • ずるいなあ (that's not fair!)

REGISTER:
Often used by children when complaining about unfairness. In adult contexts, it can describe more serious deceptive behavior.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 卑怯(ひきょう) (cowardly, unfair - stronger criticism)
  • 抜(ぬ)け目(め)がない (shrewd, not sly but clever)
  • 狡猾(こうかつ) (cunning, crafty - formal/literary)
