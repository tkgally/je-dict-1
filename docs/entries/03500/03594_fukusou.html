服装(ふくそう)

ふくそう
noun
attire, clothing
1. attire, clothing, dress
The way someone is dressed; clothes and accessories worn together.
面接(めんせつ)には正式(せいしき)な服装(ふくそう)で行(い)きましょう。
Let's go to the interview in formal attire.
服装(ふくそう)規定(きてい)を守(まも)ってください。
Please follow the dress code.
服装規定 = dress code
彼女(かのじょ)の服装(ふくそう)はいつもセンス(せんす)がいい。
Her attire always shows good taste.

USAGE: 服装(ふくそう) refers to one's overall manner of dress, not individual clothing items. It often implies appropriateness for a situation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 服装(ふくそう)規定(きてい) (dress code)
  • 正式(せいしき)な服装(ふくそう) (formal attire)
  • カジュアル}な{服装(ふくそう) (casual attire)
  • 服装(ふくそう)に気(き)をつける (to be careful about one's attire)
  • 服装(ふくそう)を整(ととの)える (to dress properly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 服(ふく) - clothes (individual items)
  • 衣装(いしょう) - costume, outfit (often special occasions)
