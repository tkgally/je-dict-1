実(じつ)に

じつに
adverb
indeed, truly, really
1. indeed, truly, really
An intensifier used to emphasize the truth or degree of something; often expresses genuine feeling or evaluation.
実(じつ)に素晴(すば)らしい景色(けしき)だ。
It's truly a wonderful view.
実(じつ)に残念(ざんねん)なことだ。
It's indeed regrettable.
これは実(じつ)に興味深(きょうみぶか)い研究(けんきゅう)だ。
This is truly interesting research.

USAGE: 実(じつ)に is a formal intensifier expressing genuine emphasis. It's commonly used in written language and speeches.

REGISTER: Formal/literary. In casual speech, 本当(ほんとう)に or すごく are more common.

CONTRAST:

  • 実(じつ)に - indeed, truly (formal, emphatic)
  • 本当(ほんとう)に - really (general use)
  • まことに/誠(まこと)に - truly (very formal, humble)
  • すごく - very (casual)
