夫人(ふじん)

ふじん
noun, suffix
Mrs., wife (formal)
1. Mrs., Madam
A formal title used for married women, especially of high social standing.
2. wife (honorific)
An honorific term for someone's wife, typically used for people of status.
大使(たいし)夫人(ふじん)がパーティーに出席(しゅっせき)された。
The ambassador's wife attended the party.
田中(たなか)夫人(ふじん)にお会(あ)いしました。
I met Mrs. Tanaka.
社長(しゃちょう)夫人(ふじん)は慈善(じぜん)活動(かつどう)に熱心(ねっしん)だ。
The company president's wife is enthusiastic about charity work.

USAGE: 夫人(ふじん) is a formal, respectful term used for married women, particularly those of social prominence.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜夫人(ふじん) (Mrs. ~, ~ 's wife)
  • 大統領(だいとうりょう)夫人(ふじん) (the president's wife, First Lady)
  • 令(れい)夫人(ふじん) (your wife - honorific)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 奥(おく)さん - wife (polite, everyday usage)
  • 妻(つま) - wife (neutral, one's own wife)
  • 婦人(ふじん) - woman, lady (general term)
