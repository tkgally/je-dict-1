費用(ひよう)

ひよう
noun
cost, expense

Definitions

1. cost, expense
The money required or spent for a particular purpose.

Examples

旅行(りょこう)の費用(ひよう)はいくらかかりますか。
How much will the travel expenses be?
費用(ひよう)は会社(かいしゃ)が負担(ふたん)します。
The company will bear the costs.
費用を負担する = to bear the costs
教育(きょういく)費用(ひよう)が年々(ねんねん)増(ふ)えている。
Education costs are increasing year by year.

Notes

USAGE: 費用(ひよう) is a formal word for costs or expenses. It's commonly used in business and official contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 費用(ひよう)がかかる (to cost, to incur expenses)
  • 費用(ひよう)を負担(ふたん)する (to bear the cost)
  • 費用(ひよう)を削減(さくげん)する (to reduce costs)
  • 費用(ひよう)対(たい)効果(こうか) (cost-effectiveness)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経費(けいひ) - operating expenses, business expenses
  • 出費(しゅっぴ) - expenditure (personal spending)
  • {コスト} - cost (loanword, often used in business)
