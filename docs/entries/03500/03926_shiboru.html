1. to wring, to squeeze
To twist something to extract liquid.
濡(ぬ)れたタオルを絞(しぼ)る。
Wring out a wet towel.
雑巾(ぞうきん)をよく絞(しぼ)ってから床(ゆか)を拭(ふ)いた。
I wrung out the cloth well before wiping the floor.
レモンを絞(しぼ)って果汁(かじゅう)を出(だ)す。
Squeeze a lemon to extract the juice.
2. to narrow down, to limit
To reduce options or focus on a specific target.
候補(こうほ)を3人(にん)に絞(しぼ)った。
I narrowed down the candidates to three people.
的(まと)を絞(しぼ)って準備(じゅんび)した方(ほう)がいい。
It's better to narrow the focus and prepare.
テーマを一(ひと)つに絞(しぼ)った。
I narrowed the topic down to one.
3. to squeeze out, to extract
To extract something through effort.
知恵(ちえ)を絞(しぼ)って解決策(かいけつさく)を考(かんが)えた。
I racked my brains to think of a solution.
最後(さいご)の力(ちから)を絞(しぼ)って走(はし)り切(き)った。
I squeezed out my last bit of energy and finished the run.
やっとのことで声(こえ)を絞(しぼ)り出(だ)した。
I barely managed to squeeze out my voice.

絞(しぼ)る means to wring, squeeze, or narrow down.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pattern: Xを絞(しぼ)る

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • タオルを絞(しぼ)る (to wring a towel)
  • 雑巾(ぞうきん)を絞(しぼ)る (to wring out a cloth)
  • 知恵(ちえ)を絞(しぼ)る (to rack one's brains)
  • 声(こえ)を絞(しぼ)る (to squeeze out one's voice)
  • 的(まと)を絞(しぼ)る (to narrow the focus)
  • 候補(こうほ)を絞(しぼ)る (to narrow down candidates)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 搾(しぼ)る (to squeeze out juice/milk - different kanji)
  • 絞(しぼ)り込(こ)む (to narrow down further)
