姓名(せいめい)

せいめい
noun
full name
1. full name, surname and given name
A person's complete name including family name and personal name.
姓名(せいめい)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in your full name.
Common instruction on forms
姓名(せいめい)判断(はんだん)をしてもらった。
I had my fortune told based on my name.
姓名(せいめい)判断(はんだん) = fortune telling by name
姓名(せいめい)の読(よ)み方(かた)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me how to read your full name.
申込書(もうしこみしょ)に姓名(せいめい)と住所(じゅうしょ)を正(ただ)しく書(か)いてください。
Please write your full name and address correctly on the application form.
日本(にほん)では姓名(せいめい)の順番(じゅんばん)が西洋(せいよう)とは逆(ぎゃく)で、姓(せい)が先(さき)に来(く)る。
In Japan, the order of the full name is the reverse of the West, with the surname coming first.

COMPONENTS:

  • 姓(せい) - surname, family name
  • 名(めい) - given name, first name

JAPANESE NAME ORDER:
In Japanese, the family name (姓(せい)) comes first, followed by the given name (名(めい)). This is the opposite of Western name order.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 姓名(せいめい)を書(か)く (to write one's full name)
  • 姓名(せいめい)判断(はんだん) (name-based fortune telling)
  • 姓名(せいめい)の記入(きにゅう) (entering full name on forms)

HOMOPHONES:

  • 声明(せいめい) - statement, declaration
  • 生命(せいめい) - life

Contrast: 声明(せいめい) (statement (homophone))
