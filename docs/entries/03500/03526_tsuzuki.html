続(つづ)き

つづき
noun
continuation, sequel

Definitions

1. continuation, sequel
The part that follows; what comes next in a series or story.
2. succession, series
Things happening consecutively.

Examples

続(つづ)きは明日(あした)話(はな)します。
I'll tell you the rest tomorrow.
この映画(えいが)の続(つづ)きが見(み)たい。
I want to see the sequel to this movie.
雨(あめ)続(つづ)きで洗濯物(せんたくもの)が乾(かわ)かない。
The laundry won't dry because of the continuous rain.

Notes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 続(つづ)きを読(よ)む/見(み)る (to read/watch the continuation)
  • 続(つづ)きは〜 (the rest is ~, to be continued ~)
  • 〜続(つづ)き (continuous ~, a series of ~)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 雨(あめ)続(つづ)き (continuous rain)
  • 不運(ふうん)続(つづ)き (a series of misfortunes)
  • 続(つづ)きもの (serial, series)

RELATED VERB:

  • 続(つづ)く (to continue) - this noun is derived from the verb

Related Words

Related: 続(つづ)く (to continue)
Related: 続(つづ)ける (to continue (trans.))
