続(つづ)き

つづき
noun
continuation, sequel
1. continuation, sequel
The part that follows; what comes next in a series or story.
続(つづ)きは明日(あした)話(はな)します。
I'll tell you the rest tomorrow.
この映画(えいが)の続(つづ)きが見(み)たい。
I want to see the sequel to this movie.
この本(ほん)の続(つづ)きはいつ出(で)ますか。
When will the continuation of this book come out?
2. succession, series
Things happening consecutively.
雨(あめ)続(つづ)きで洗濯物(せんたくもの)が乾(かわ)かない。
The laundry won't dry because of the continuous rain.
〜続(つづ)き = a series of ~, continuous ~
失敗(しっぱい)続(つづ)きで、自信(じしん)をなくしてしまった。
After a series of failures, I lost my confidence.
最近(さいきん)いいこと続(つづ)きで、毎日(まいにち)が楽(たの)しい。
Good things keep happening lately, and every day is fun.

The noun form of 続(つづ)く (to continue). Has two main uses: (1) the continuation or sequel of something, and (2) a suffix meaning a series or succession of things.

SENSE 1 — CONTINUATION:

  • 続(つづ)きを読(よ)む/見(み)る: to read/watch the continuation
  • 続(つづ)きは明日(あした): the rest is tomorrow (to be continued)
  • 続(つづ)きが気(き)になる: to be curious about what happens next
  • 続(つづ)きもの: serial, ongoing series

SENSE 2 — 〜続(つづ)き SUFFIX:

Attached to nouns to mean "a series of ~" or "continuous ~":

  • 雨(あめ)続(つづ)き: continuous rain
  • 失敗(しっぱい)続(つづ)き: a series of failures
  • 不運(ふうん)続(つづ)き: a string of bad luck
  • いいこと続(つづ)き: a run of good things
