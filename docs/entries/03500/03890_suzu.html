鈴(すず)

すず
noun
bell (small)
1. small bell
A small metal bell that makes a ringing or jingling sound when shaken.
猫(ねこ)の首(くび)に鈴(すず)をつけた。
I put a bell on the cat's neck.
風鈴(ふうりん)の音(おと)が涼(すず)しげだ。
The sound of the wind chime sounds refreshing.
神社(じんじゃ)で鈴(すず)を鳴(な)らしてから参拝(さんぱい)する。
Ring the bell before praying at the shrine.

鈴(すず) refers to small bells that jingle. This is different from 鐘(かね), which refers to large bells that are struck.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 鈴(すず)を鳴(な)らす (to ring a bell)
  • 風鈴(ふうりん) (wind chime)
  • 鈴(すず)の音(ね) (the sound of a bell)
  • 鈴(すず)なり (hanging in clusters, like bells)

CULTURAL NOTE:
At Shinto shrines, visitors ring a large 鈴(すず) to announce their presence to the deity before praying. 風鈴(ふうりん) (wind chimes) are a summer tradition in Japan.

COUNTER: 個(こ)
