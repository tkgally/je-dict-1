再(ふたた)び

ふたたび
adverb
again, once more

Definitions

1. again, once more, a second time
Indicates something happening for another time after a previous occurrence.

Examples

再(ふたた)び会(あ)える日(ひ)を楽(たの)しみにしています。
I'm looking forward to the day we can meet again.
彼(かれ)は再(ふたた)び{チャンピオン}になった。
He became champion once again.
同(おな)じ過(あやま)ちを再(ふたた)び繰(く)り返(かえ)さないでください。
Please don't repeat the same mistake again.

Notes

USAGE: 再(ふたた)び is a formal or literary way to say "again." It emphasizes that something is happening for a second time after a break or interval.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 再(ふたた)び〜する (to do ~ again)
  • 二度(にど)と〜ない...再(ふたた)び (never again... once more)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • また - again (casual, everyday)
  • もう一度(いちど) - one more time (neutral)
  • 再(さい)び/再度(さいど) - again (formal)
