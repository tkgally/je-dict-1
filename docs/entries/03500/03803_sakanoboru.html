遡(さかのぼ)る

さかのぼる
verb (godan)
to go back; to trace back

Definitions

1. to go upstream; to go against the current
To travel in the opposite direction of a river's flow.
2. to trace back; to go back in time
To examine or consider events from the past; to date back to an earlier time.

Examples

川(かわ)を遡(さかのぼ)る。
To go upstream.
歴史(れきし)を遡(さかのぼ)って調(しら)べる。
To research by tracing back through history.
この問題(もんだい)は10年前(じゅうねんまえ)に遡(さかのぼ)る。
This problem dates back to 10 years ago.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xを遡(さかのぼ)る (to go back along X).

WRITING: Can also be written as 溯(さかのぼ)る (older kanji variant).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 川(かわ)を遡(さかのぼ)る (to go upstream)
  • 時間(じかん)を遡(さかのぼ)る (to go back in time)
  • 過去(かこ)に遡(さかのぼ)る (to trace back to the past)
  • 記憶(きおく)を遡(さかのぼ)る (to go back through memories)

CONTRAST:

  • 下(くだ)る (to go downstream, descend)

Related Words

Antonym: 下(くだ)る (to descend)
