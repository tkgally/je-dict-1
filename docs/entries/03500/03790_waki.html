脇(わき)

わき
noun
side, armpit
1. side, beside
The area next to or beside something; the side of something.
脇(わき)に寄(よ)ってください。
Please move to the side.
道(みち)の脇(わき)に車(くるま)を止(と)めた。
I parked the car at the side of the road.
脇役(わきやく)だが重要(じゅうよう)な役(やく)だ。
It's a supporting role, but an important one.
脇役(わきやく) = supporting role/actor
2. armpit
The hollow area under the arm where it joins the body.
脇(わき)が甘(あま)い。
His guard is down. / He's careless.
脇(わき)が甘(あま)い = to be careless, to let one's guard down (idiomatic, from the armpit area being an unguarded spot)
脇(わき)に抱(かか)えて運(はこ)んだ。
I carried it tucked under my arm.
脇(わき)に抱(かか)える = to carry under one's arm
夏(なつ)になると脇(わき)の汗(あせ)が気(き)になる人(ひと)が多(おお)い。
In summer, many people worry about armpit sweat.

脇(わき) has two main meanings: the side or beside something, and the armpit area.

SENSE 1 — SIDE:
Refers to an area beside or off to the side of the main focus. Often used in compounds and metaphorical expressions.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 脇役(わきやく): supporting role/actor
  • 脇道(わきみち): side road, digression
  • 脇見(わきみ): looking aside, distraction (脇見(わきみ)運転(うんてん) = distracted driving)

SENSE 2 — ARMPIT:
The hollow under the arm. 脇(わき)の下(した) is a more specific way to say "armpit."

IDIOM:
脇(わき)が甘(あま)い means "to be careless" or "to let one's guard down," from the idea that the armpit area is vulnerable.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 脇(わき)に寄(よ)る: to move to the side
  • 脇(わき)に抱(かか)える: to carry under one's arm
  • 脇(わき)を通(とお)る: to pass by the side
