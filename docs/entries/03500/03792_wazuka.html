僅(わず)か

わずか
na-adjective, adverb
only, merely, slight

Definitions

1. only, merely, just
A small amount or number; used to emphasize how small or limited something is.
2. slight, small, little
Very small in degree or amount.

Examples

僅(わず)か五分(ごふん)で着(つ)いた。
I arrived in just five minutes.
僅(わず)かな違(ちが)いだ。
It's a slight difference.
僅かな + noun = a slight/small ~
残(のこ)りは僅(わず)か三人(さんにん)だけだった。
Only three people remained.

Notes

USAGE: 僅(わず)か emphasizes the smallness of quantity, degree, or extent. Can be used adverbially or as a na-adjective (僅かな).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 僅(わず)か〜だけ (only ~, just ~)
  • 僅(わず)かな差(さ) (a slight difference)
  • 僅(わず)かに (slightly - adverbial)

CONTRAST:

  • 僅(わず)か - emphasizes smallness (slightly formal)
  • たった - only, just (casual)
  • ほんの - just a little (modest)
