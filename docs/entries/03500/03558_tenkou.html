天候(てんこう)

てんこう
noun
weather
1. weather, weather conditions
The state of the atmosphere at a particular place and time, especially as it affects outdoor activities.
天候(てんこう)が悪(わる)いので、試合(しあい)は中止(ちゅうし)だ。
The game is canceled due to bad weather.
天候(てんこう)に恵(めぐ)まれた。
We were blessed with good weather.
天候(てんこう)不順(ふじゅん)が続(つづ)いている。
The unsettled weather continues.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 天候(てんこう): Weather (formal, often used in news/official contexts)
  • 天気(てんき): Weather (general, everyday usage)
  • 気候(きこう): Climate (long-term weather patterns)

天候(てんこう) is more formal than 天気(てんき) and is often used in official announcements and news reports.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 天候(てんこう)が悪(わる)い/良(よ)い (bad/good weather)
  • 天候(てんこう)に恵(めぐ)まれる (to be blessed with good weather)
  • 天候(てんこう)不順(ふじゅん) (unsettled/unstable weather)
  • 天候(てんこう)の回復(かいふく) (weather recovery)

Related Words

Contrast: 天気(てんき) (weather (casual))
Contrast: 気候(きこう) (climate)
