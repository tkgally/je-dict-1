扇子(せんす)

せんす
noun
folding fan

Definitions

1. folding fan
A hand-held fan made of paper or silk stretched over ribs that can be folded when not in use. A traditional Japanese item used for cooling and in traditional arts.

Examples

夏(なつ)には扇子(せんす)が手放(てばな)せない。
I can't do without my folding fan in summer.
扇子(せんす)を広(ひろ)げて涼(すず)んだ。
I spread open the fan and cooled off.
日本舞踊(にほんぶよう)では扇子(せんす)が重要(じゅうよう)な小道具(こどうぐ)だ。
In Japanese dance, the folding fan is an important prop.

Notes

CULTURAL NOTE: 扇子(せんす) is a symbol of Japanese culture and has been used for centuries. It's not just for cooling - folding fans are used in:

  • 日本舞踊(にほんぶよう) (Japanese dance)
  • 茶道(さどう) (tea ceremony)
  • 能(のう)・狂言(きょうげん) (Noh and Kyogen theater)
  • 落語(らくご) (comic storytelling)

VERBS USED WITH 扇子:

  • 扇子(せんす)を広(ひろ)げる (to open a fan)
  • 扇子(せんす)を閉(と)じる (to close a fan)
  • 扇子(せんす)で扇(あお)ぐ (to fan with a fan)

CONTRAST: 団扇(うちわ) is a non-folding, rigid fan - more casual and often given away free as advertising.
