生(なま)

なま
noun, prefix
raw, uncooked, fresh
1. raw, uncooked
Food that has not been cooked or heated. Often used for fish, meat, or eggs.
生(なま)の魚(さかな)は新鮮(しんせん)なうちに食(た)べよう。
Let's eat raw fish while it's fresh.
2. fresh, draft (beer)
Unpasteurized or freshly made. Commonly used for draft beer (生(なま)ビール).
生(なま)ビールを2つください。
Two draft beers, please.
生ビール = draft beer
3. live, in person
Broadcast or performance happening in real time, not recorded.
生(なま)放送(ほうそう)でミスをしてしまった。
I made a mistake during the live broadcast.
生放送 = live broadcast

USAGE: 生(なま) is versatile, used for raw food, draft beverages, and live broadcasts/performances.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 生(なま)ビール (draft beer)
  • 生(なま)卵(たまご) (raw egg)
  • 生(なま)魚(ざかな) (raw fish)
  • 生(なま)放送(ほうそう) (live broadcast)
  • 生(なま)演奏(えんそう) (live performance)
  • 生(なま)中継(ちゅうけい) (live relay)
  • 生(なま)クリーム (fresh cream)
  • 生(なま)野菜(やさい) (raw vegetables)

NOTE: In izakayas and restaurants, simply saying 「生(なま)」 typically refers to draft beer. The word is also read as き in compounds like 生(き)のまま (as is, raw).

CONTRAST:

  • 生(なま) - raw, uncooked
  • 焼(や)いた - grilled, cooked
