豊富(ほうふ)

ほうふ
na-adjective, noun
abundant, plentiful

Definitions

1. abundant, plentiful, rich
Having a large quantity or variety; well-supplied.

Examples

経験(けいけん)が豊富(ほうふ)な人(ひと)を探(さが)している。
We're looking for someone with rich experience.
この地域(ちいき)は資源(しげん)が豊富(ほうふ)だ。
This region is rich in resources.
豊富(ほうふ)な品揃(しなぞろ)えが自慢(じまん)です。
We pride ourselves on our abundant selection.

Notes

USAGE: 豊富(ほうふ) emphasizes both quantity and quality, often implying a rich variety.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 豊富(ほうふ)な〜 (abundant ~)
  • 〜が豊富(ほうふ) (~ is abundant)
  • 経験(けいけん)豊富(ほうふ) (rich in experience)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経験(けいけん)が豊富(ほうふ) (rich in experience)
  • 資源(しげん)が豊富(ほうふ) (rich in resources)
  • 知識(ちしき)が豊富(ほうふ) (knowledgeable)
  • 栄養(えいよう)が豊富(ほうふ) (nutritious)
  • バラエティー}に{豊富(ほうふ) (wide variety)
