上(のぼ)り

のぼり
noun
ascent; up-train; climbing
1. ascent; going up; climbing
The act or state of going upward.
2. up-train; inbound train
A train heading toward a major city (especially Tokyo).
この道(みち)は上(のぼ)りがきつい。
The climb on this road is steep.
Sense 1: uphill path
上(のぼ)りの電車(でんしゃ)は混(こ)んでいる。
The inbound train is crowded.
Sense 2: train toward Tokyo
上(のぼ)り坂(ざか)で自転車(じてんしゃ)を漕(こ)ぐのは大変(たいへん)だ。
It's hard to pedal a bicycle uphill.
上(のぼ)り坂(ざか) = uphill slope

上(のぼ)り is the noun form derived from the verb 上(のぼ)る (to climb, to go up).

TRAIN TERMINOLOGY:

In Japanese railway, 上(のぼ)り and 下(くだ)り indicate direction relative to major cities:

  • 上(のぼ)り: toward Tokyo (or the major terminus)
  • 下(くだ)り: away from Tokyo (or the major terminus)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 上(のぼ)り坂(ざか) (uphill slope)
  • 上(のぼ)り線(せん) (inbound line/track)
  • 上(のぼ)り列車(れっしゃ) (up-train)

ANTONYM:
下(くだ)り (descent; down-train)

Related Words

Antonym: 下(くだ)り (descent; down-train)
Related: 上(のぼ)る (verb: to climb)
