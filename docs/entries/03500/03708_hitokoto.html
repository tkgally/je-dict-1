一言(ひとこと)

ひとこと
noun
a word, brief comment

Definitions

1. a word, single word
Literally one word, but often means a brief comment or remark.
2. brief comment, few words
A short statement or remark.

Examples

一言(ひとこと)お願(ねが)いします。
Please say a few words.
Common request at events or gatherings
一言(ひとこと)言(い)わせてください。
Let me say something.
彼(かれ)は一言(ひとこと)も話(はな)さなかった。
He didn't say a single word.

Notes

Often used to request or offer a brief comment.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一言(ひとこと)お願(ねが)いします (Please say a few words)
  • 一言(ひとこと)言(い)わせてください (Let me say something)
  • 一言(ひとこと)で言(い)えば (in a word, to put it briefly)
  • 一言(ひとこと)多(おお)い (one word too many; talks too much)
  • 一言(ひとこと)も言(い)わない (not say a word)

ALTERNATIVE READING:

  • いちごん - more formal, literary reading
  • ひとこと - everyday usage

COUNTER NOTE:

  • Despite meaning "one word," 一言(ひとこと) often refers to a brief statement, not literally a single word.
