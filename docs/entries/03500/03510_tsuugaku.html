1. commuting to school, going to school
The act of traveling regularly to school.
毎日(まいにち)電車(でんしゃ)で通学(つうがく)している。
I commute to school by train every day.
通学(つうがく)に一時間(いちじかん)かかる。
It takes one hour to commute to school.
通学(つうがく)路(ろ)は安全(あんぜん)ですか。
Is the route to school safe?

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 通学(つうがく)する (to commute to school)
  • 〜で通学(つうがく)する (to commute to school by ~)
  • 通学(つうがく)に〜かかる (it takes ~ to commute to school)

COMMON COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 通学(つうがく)路(ろ) (route to school)
  • 通学(つうがく)定期(ていき) (student commuter pass)
  • 通学(つうがく)時間(じかん) (commute time to school)
  • 徒歩(とほ)通学(つうがく) (walking to school)
  • 自転車(じてんしゃ)通学(つうがく) (cycling to school)

RELATED WORD:

  • 通勤(つうきん): Commuting to work (same pattern, different destination)
