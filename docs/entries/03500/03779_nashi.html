無(な)し

なし
noun / suffix
without, none, no
1. without, none
The absence of something.
砂糖(さとう)無(な)しでお願(ねが)いします。
Without sugar, please.
Common ordering phrase
問題(もんだい)無(な)し。
No problem.
2. no, invalid, canceled
Something that doesn't count or is not accepted.
それは無(な)しだ。
That doesn't count / That's not valid.
Casual rejection

Often used as a suffix to mean "without ~" or "~-less."

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜無(な)し (without ~, ~-less)
  • 砂糖(さとう)無(な)し (no sugar)
  • 氷(こおり)無(な)し (no ice)
  • 問題(もんだい)無(な)し (no problem)
  • 予約(よやく)無(な)し (without reservation)

USAGE NOTES:

  • 無(な)しにする (to cancel, to call off)
  • それは無(な)しだ (that doesn't count)
  • 無(な)しで (without)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無(む)い (not having - classical/formal)
  • 〜ない (without - adjective form)
  • 抜(ぬ)き (without, excluding)

NOTE:
Distinguish from 梨(なし) (pear) which is a homophone.
