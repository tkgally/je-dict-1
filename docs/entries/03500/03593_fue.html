笛(ふえ)

ふえ
noun
flute, whistle
1. flute
A musical wind instrument, especially traditional Japanese flutes.
祭(まつ)りで笛(ふえ)の音(おと)が聞(き)こえた。
I heard the sound of flutes at the festival.
横笛(よこぶえ)の演奏(えんそう)を習(なら)っている。
I'm learning to play the transverse flute.
横笛 = transverse flute
2. whistle
An instrument that makes a shrill sound when blown.
審判(しんぱん)が笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)いた。
The referee blew the whistle.

USAGE: 笛(ふえ) is a general term that includes both musical flutes and signal whistles.

TYPES OF FLUTES:

  • 横笛(よこぶえ) - transverse flute (played sideways)
  • 縦笛(たてぶえ) - recorder (played vertically)
  • 尺八(しゃくはち) - shakuhachi (traditional bamboo flute)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 笛(ふえ)を吹(ふ)く (to blow a flute/whistle)
  • 笛(ふえ)の音(おと/ね) (sound of a flute)
