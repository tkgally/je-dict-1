冒険(ぼうけん)

ぼうけん
noun, suru-verb
adventure

Definitions

1. adventure
An exciting or daring experience, typically involving some risk or uncertainty.
2. risk, venture
Taking a chance on something uncertain or dangerous.

Examples

子供(こども)のころ冒険(ぼうけん)物語(ものがたり)が大好(だいす)きだった。
I loved adventure stories when I was a child.
未知(みち)の世界(せかい)への冒険(ぼうけん)に出(で)かける。
To set out on an adventure to an unknown world.
その投資(とうし)は冒険(ぼうけん)だと思(おも)う。
I think that investment is a risk.

Notes

USAGE: 冒険(ぼうけん) can refer to thrilling adventures or to taking risks in business/life.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 冒険(ぼうけん)する (to venture, to take a risk)
  • 冒険(ぼうけん)に出(で)る (to set out on an adventure)
  • 冒険(ぼうけん)心(しん) (adventurous spirit)
  • 大(だい)冒険(ぼうけん) (great adventure)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 冒険(ぼうけん)家(か) (adventurer)
  • 冒険(ぼうけん)小説(しょうせつ) (adventure novel)
  • 冒険(ぼうけん)心(しん) (adventurous spirit)
  • 冒険(ぼうけん)的(てき) (adventurous, risky)
