向(む)かい

むかい
noun
facing, opposite, across
1. facing, opposite
The position or side that is directly across from something.
駅(えき)の向(む)かいに銀行(ぎんこう)がある。
There's a bank across from the station.
向(む)かいの家(いえ)に新(あたら)しい人(ひと)が引(ひ)っ越(こ)してきた。
New people moved into the house across the street.
向(む)かい側(がわ)に渡(わた)ってください。
Please cross to the other side.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 向(む)かいの〜 (the ~ across the way)
  • 向(む)かい側(がわ) (the opposite side, across)
  • 向(む)かい合(あ)う (to face each other)
  • 向(む)かい合(あ)わせ (facing each other)

RELATED VERB:

  • 向(む)かう (to head toward, to face)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 向(む)こう (over there, the other side)
  • 正面(しょうめん) (directly in front)
