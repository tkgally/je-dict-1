根(ね)

ね
noun
root
1. root (of a plant)
The part of a plant that grows underground, absorbing water and nutrients.
この木(き)は根(ね)が深(ふか)い。
This tree has deep roots.
2. root, basis, foundation
The fundamental cause or source of something; the core or essence.
問題(もんだい)の根(ね)を探(さぐ)る。
To search for the root of the problem.
彼(かれ)は根(ね)は優(やさ)しい人(ひと)だ。
He's a kind person at heart.
根は〜 = at heart, fundamentally

USAGE: 根(ね) refers to both literal plant roots and figurative foundations/causes. It's used in many idiomatic expressions.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 根(ね)が深(ふか)い (deep-rooted)
  • 根(ね)を張(は)る (to take root)
  • 根(ね)から (from the root, fundamentally)
  • 根(ね)も葉(は)もない (groundless, baseless)
  • 根(ね)は (at heart, fundamentally)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 根(ね)性(しょう) (nature, disposition)
  • 根(ね)気(き) (perseverance, patience)
  • 根(ね)元(もと) (root, base)
  • 根(ね)拠(きょ) (basis, grounds)
  • 大根(だいこん) (daikon radish)
  • 屋根(やね) (roof)

IDIOMS:

  • 根(ね)も葉(は)もない噂(うわさ) (groundless rumor)
