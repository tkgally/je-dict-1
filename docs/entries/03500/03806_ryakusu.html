略(りゃく)す

りゃくす
verb (godan)
to abbreviate; to omit

Definitions

1. to abbreviate; to shorten
To reduce a word, phrase, or text to a shorter form.
2. to omit; to leave out
To skip or leave out parts of something, especially in explanations or presentations.

Examples

「国際連合(こくさいれんごう)」を「国連(こくれん)」と略(りゃく)す。
To abbreviate 'United Nations' to 'UN'.
説明(せつめい)を略(りゃく)して結論(けつろん)に移(うつ)ります。
I'll skip the explanation and move to the conclusion.
以下(いか)、略(りゃく)。
The rest is omitted.
Common in formal writing

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを略(りゃく)す.

ALTERNATE FORM: 略(りゃく)する (する-verb form) is also common.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 以下(いか)略(りゃく) (the rest is omitted)
  • 中(なか)略(りゃく) (middle portion omitted)
  • 前(まえ)略(りゃく) (opening omitted - in letters)
  • 後(あと)略(りゃく) (remainder omitted)

RELATED:

  • 略語(りゃくご) (abbreviation)
  • 略称(りゃくしょう) (abbreviated name)
  • 省略(しょうりゃく) (omission, abbreviation)
