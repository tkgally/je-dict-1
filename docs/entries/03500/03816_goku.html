極(ごく)

ごく
adverb
quite, very, extremely
1. quite, very, extremely
An intensifier emphasizing degree; often used before adjectives or nouns to indicate something is at an extreme or exceptional level.
それは極(ごく)普通(ふつう)のことだ。
That's perfectly normal.
極普通 = perfectly/completely normal
極(ごく)少数(しょうすう)の人(ひと)しか知(し)らない。
Only a very small number of people know.
極(ごく)最近(さいきん)のことだ。
It was very recently.

USAGE: 極(ごく) is a formal intensifier that often emphasizes ordinary or extreme qualities.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 極(ごく)普通(ふつう) (perfectly ordinary)
  • 極(ごく)少数(しょうすう) (very few)
  • 極(ごく)最近(さいきん) (very recently)
  • 極(ごく)一部(いちぶ) (only a part)
  • 極(ごく)稀(まれ)に (very rarely)
  • 極(ごく)自然(しぜん)に (very naturally)

CONTRAST:

  • 極(ごく) - quite, very (formal, limited usage)
  • とても - very (general, versatile)
  • 非常(ひじょう)に - extremely (formal)

NOTE: 極(ごく) is typically used with certain fixed expressions and is less versatile than とても.
