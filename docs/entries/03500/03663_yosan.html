予算(よさん)

よさん
noun
budget

Definitions

1. budget
An estimate of income and expenditure for a set period; the amount of money available for spending.

Examples

予算(よさん)は{いくら}ですか。
What is your budget?
予算(よさん)を超(こ)えてしまった。
I went over budget.
予算を超える = to exceed the budget
来年度(らいねんど)の予算(よさん)を組(く)む。
To draw up next year's budget.
予算を組む = to draw up/formulate a budget

Notes

USAGE: 予算(よさん) is used in both personal finance and business/government contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予算(よさん)を組(く)む (to draw up a budget)
  • 予算(よさん)を超(こ)える (to exceed the budget)
  • 予算(よさん)内(ない) (within budget)
  • 予算(よさん)案(あん) (budget proposal)
  • 予算(よさん)削減(さくげん) (budget cuts)

RELATED:

  • 国家(こっか)予算(よさん) (national budget)
  • 年間(ねんかん)予算(よさん) (annual budget)
