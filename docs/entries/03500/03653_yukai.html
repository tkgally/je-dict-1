愉快(ゆかい)

ゆかい
な-adjective
pleasant, enjoyable
1. pleasant, enjoyable, cheerful
Causing feelings of enjoyment and satisfaction; making one feel happy.
愉快(ゆかい)な仲間(なかま)と過(す)ごす時間(じかん)は楽(たの)しい。
Time spent with cheerful companions is enjoyable.
愉快(ゆかい)な話(はなし)を聞(き)いて笑(わら)った。
I laughed hearing an amusing story.
あまり愉快(ゆかい)な気分(きぶん)ではない。
I'm not in a very pleasant mood.
Often used in negative: 愉快ではない = not pleasant

USAGE: 愉快(ゆかい) describes something that brings joy or amusement. It's often used to describe people, stories, or situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 愉快(ゆかい)な人(ひと) (pleasant/cheerful person)
  • 愉快(ゆかい)な気分(きぶん) (pleasant mood)
  • 愉快(ゆかい)ではない (not pleasant, annoying)
  • 不愉快(ふゆかい) (unpleasant, disagreeable)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 楽(たの)しい - fun, enjoyable (more casual)
  • 愉快(ゆかい) - pleasant, amusing (slightly formal)

Related Words

Antonym: 不愉快(ふゆかい) (unpleasant)
Synonym: 楽(たの)しい (more casual)
