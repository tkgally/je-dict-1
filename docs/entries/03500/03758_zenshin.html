前進(ぜんしん)

ぜんしん
noun / suru-verb
advance, progress
1. advance, moving forward
Physical movement in a forward direction.
軍隊(ぐんたい)は前進(ぜんしん)を続(つづ)けた。
The army continued its advance.
2. progress, advancement
Making headway or improvement toward a goal.
交渉(こうしょう)が前進(ぜんしん)した。
The negotiations made progress.
一歩(いっぽ)前進(ぜんしん)できた。
We were able to take a step forward.

ANTONYM:

  • 後退(こうたい) (retreat, regression)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 前進(ぜんしん)する (to advance, to make progress)
  • 前進(ぜんしん)を続(つづ)ける (to continue advancing)
  • 一歩(いっぽ)前進(ぜんしん) (one step forward)
  • 前進(ぜんしん)基地(きち) (forward base - military)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 進歩(しんぽ) (progress, improvement - general)
  • 発展(はってん) (development, growth)
  • 前進(ぜんしん) (advance - often physical or concrete)

Related Words

Antonym: 後退(こうたい) (retreat)
Related: 進歩(しんぽ) (progress)
