わがまま

わがまま
na-adjective, noun
selfish, willful

Definitions

1. selfish, self-centered, willful
Behavior characterized by doing what one wants without considering others; selfishness or capriciousness.

Examples

わがままを言(い)わないで。
Don't be selfish. / Don't make selfish demands.
わがままを言う = to make selfish demands
彼(かれ)はわがままな子(こ)だ。
He is a selfish child.
わがままを許(ゆる)してください。
Please forgive my selfishness.
Often used apologetically when making a personal request

Notes

USAGE: わがまま describes selfish or willful behavior, often used for children who won't listen or adults who prioritize their own desires. Can be written 我儘(わがまま) in kanji.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • わがままを言(い)う (to make selfish demands)
  • わがままな人(ひと) (a selfish person)
  • わがままを通(とお)す (to have one's own way)

REGISTER: Can be used critically or self-deprecatingly. When used about oneself, it can be a humble way to apologize for a request.
