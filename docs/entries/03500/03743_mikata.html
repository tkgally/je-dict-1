1. ally, supporter, friend
Someone who supports you or is on your side.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の味方(みかた)だ。
He is on my side.
強(つよ)い味方(みかた)がいる。
I have a strong ally.
敵(てき)か味方(みかた)かはっきりしてほしい。
I want you to make clear whether you're friend or foe.
困(こま)ったときに味方(みかた)になってくれる友人(ゆうじん)は大切(たいせつ)だ。
Friends who become your ally when you're in trouble are precious.
彼女(かのじょ)はいつも弱(よわ)い者(もの)の味方(みかた)で、誰(だれ)からも信頼(しんらい)されている。
She is always on the side of the weak, and is trusted by everyone.
2. to side with, to support
As a suru-verb, to take someone's side.
母(はは)が味方(みかた)してくれた。
My mother sided with me.
誰(だれ)も味方(みかた)してくれなかった。
Nobody sided with me.
兄(あに)はいつも弟(おとうと)に味方(みかた)する。
The older brother always sides with the younger brother.
親(おや)はどちらの子(こ)にも味方(みかた)したくないものだ。
Parents don't want to take sides with either child.
会議(かいぎ)で部長(ぶちょう)が私(わたし)に味方(みかた)してくれたおかげで、提案(ていあん)が通(とお)った。
Thanks to the department head siding with me at the meeting, the proposal was approved.

味方(みかた) refers to someone on your side — an ally, supporter, or friend. As a noun it describes the person or group that supports you; as a suru verb it means to take someone's side.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 味方(みかた)になる: to become an ally
  • 味方(みかた)につく: to take someone's side
  • 味方(みかた)する: to side with someone
  • 敵(てき)か味方(みかた)か: friend or foe
  • 強(つよ)い味方(みかた): a strong ally/supporter

USAGE:
The verb form 味方(みかた)する marks the person being supported with に, not を: 弟(おとうと)に味方(みかた)する (to side with one's younger brother). The noun pattern 弟(おとうと)の味方(みかた)をする means the same thing and is at least as common in speech.

を appears with 味方(みかた) in the set phrase 味方(みかた)につける (to win someone over to your side), where を marks the person being recruited: 世論(よろん)を味方(みかた)につける (to get public opinion on your side).

HOMOPHONE:
Distinguish from 見方(みかた) (point of view, way of looking at things) — same reading, different kanji and meaning.

Antonym: 敵(てき) (enemy)
