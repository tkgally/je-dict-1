署名(しょめい)

しょめい
noun / suru-verb
signature
1. signature, autograph
One's name written by oneself, especially on documents for authentication or approval.
ここに署名(しょめい)をお願(ねが)いします。
Please sign here.
2. signing (a petition, treaty, etc.)
The act of adding one's signature to express support or agreement.
署名(しょめい)運動(うんどう)に参加(さんか)した。
I participated in the petition drive.
両国(りょうこく)の代表(だいひょう)が条約(じょうやく)に署名(しょめい)した。
Representatives from both countries signed the treaty.

Used in formal contexts for signing official documents, petitions, and treaties.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 署名(しょめい)する (to sign)
  • 署名(しょめい)運動(うんどう) (petition drive, signature campaign)
  • 署名(しょめい)活動(かつどう) (signature-gathering activity)
  • 署名(しょめい)欄(らん) (signature field/column)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • サイン is more casual and used for autographs from celebrities or informal signatures
  • 署名(しょめい) is more formal, used for documents and petitions
