床(ゆか)

ゆか
noun
floor
1. floor
The lower horizontal surface of a room on which one stands or walks.
床(ゆか)に座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit on the floor.
床(ゆか)を掃除(そうじ)しなければならない。
I have to clean the floor.
床(ゆか)が滑(すべ)りやすいので気(き)をつけてください。
Please be careful as the floor is slippery.

USAGE: 床(ゆか) refers to the floor of a room. Note that the same kanji can also be read as とこ, meaning "bed" or "alcove" (as in 床(とこ)の間(ま)).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 床(ゆか)を掃(は)く (to sweep the floor)
  • 床(ゆか)を拭(ふ)く (to wipe/mop the floor)
  • 床(ゆか)に座(すわ)る (to sit on the floor)
  • 床(ゆか)暖房(だんぼう) (floor heating)
