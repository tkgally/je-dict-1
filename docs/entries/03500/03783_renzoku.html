連続(れんぞく)

れんぞく
noun, suru-verb, no-adjective
consecutive, continuous
1. continuation, series, succession
A sequence of events or things happening one after another without interruption.
三日(みっか)連続(れんぞく)で雨(あめ)が降(ふ)った。
It rained for three consecutive days.
〜連続で = for ~ in a row
この番組(ばんぐみ)は連続(れんぞく)ドラマです。
This program is a serial drama.
連続ドラマ = TV series, serial drama
彼(かれ)は十年(じゅうねん)連続(れんぞく)で優勝(ゆうしょう)した。
He won the championship for ten consecutive years.

USAGE: 連続(れんぞく) emphasizes the unbroken sequence of events or things happening in succession.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 〜連続(れんぞく)で (for ~ in a row, ~ consecutive)
  • 連続(れんぞく)する (to continue, to occur consecutively)
  • 連続(れんぞく)ドラマ (TV series, serial drama)
  • 連続(れんぞく)殺人(さつじん) (serial killings)

CONTRAST:

  • 連続(れんぞく) - emphasizes consecutive occurrence
  • 継続(けいぞく) - emphasizes ongoing continuation of an action
general
