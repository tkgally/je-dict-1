孫(まご)

まご
noun
grandchild
1. grandchild
The child of one's son or daughter.
孫(まご)が生(う)まれて、とても嬉(うれ)しい。
I'm so happy that a grandchild was born.
祖父(そふ)は孫(まご)を可愛(かわい)がっている。
The grandfather dotes on his grandchild.
孫(まご)の顔(かお)を見(み)るのが楽(たの)しみだ。
I look forward to seeing my grandchild's face.
孫(まご)にお年玉(としだま)をあげた。
I gave my grandchild New Year's money.
週末(しゅうまつ)に孫(まご)が遊(あそ)びに来(く)るので、おもちゃを買(か)っておいた。
My grandchild is coming to visit this weekend, so I bought some toys in advance.

RELATED FAMILY TERMS:

  • 祖父(そふ) - grandfather
  • 祖母(そぼ) - grandmother
  • 祖父母(そふぼ) - grandparents
  • 曾孫(ひまご)/曽孫(そうそん) - great-grandchild

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 孫(まご)の手(て) - back scratcher (lit. grandchild's hand)
  • 内孫(うちまご) - grandchild from one's son (who carries on the family name)
  • 外孫(そとまご) - grandchild from one's daughter

NOTE:
In Japanese culture, grandparents often play an important role in childcare, and the relationship between grandparents and grandchildren (孫(まご)) is highly valued.
