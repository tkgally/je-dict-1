1. to float, to rise to the surface
To stay on the surface of water or other liquid without sinking.
木(き)の葉(は)が水(みず)に浮(う)かんでいる。
Leaves are floating on the water.
雲(くも)が空(そら)に浮(う)かんでいる。
Clouds are floating in the sky.
池(いけ)にボートが浮(う)かんでいるのが見(み)える。
I can see a boat floating on the pond.
2. to come to mind, to occur to
For an idea, thought, or image to suddenly appear in one's consciousness.
いい考(かんが)えが浮(う)かんだ。
A good idea came to mind.
昔(むかし)の思(おも)い出(で)が浮(う)かんできた。
Old memories came to mind.
名前(なまえ)が思(おも)い浮(う)かばない。
I can't recall the name.
3. to appear (on one's face)
For an expression to show on someone's face.
彼(かれ)の顔(かお)に笑(え)みが浮(う)かんだ。
A smile appeared on his face.
不安(ふあん)な表情(ひょうじょう)が顔(かお)に浮(う)かんだ。
An anxious expression appeared on his face.
涙(なみだ)を浮(う)かべながら話(はな)していた。
She was speaking with tears in her eyes.

浮(う)かぶ means to float or to come to mind.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Pair: 浮(う)かべる (transitive - to float something, to bring to mind)
  • Pattern: Xが浮(う)かぶ

ASPECT:

  • 浮(う)かんでいる: Is floating / has come to mind (state)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 頭(あたま)に浮(う)かぶ (to come to mind)
  • 考(かんが)えが浮(う)かぶ (an idea occurs)
  • 思(おも)い浮(う)かぶ (to recall, to come to mind)
  • 顔(かお)に浮(う)かぶ (to appear on one's face)
  • 水(みず)に浮(う)かぶ (to float on water)

Related Words

Antonym: 沈(しず)む (to sink)
Related: 浮(う)く (similar meaning)
Related: 浮(う)かび上(あ)がる (to surface, to come to light (compound))
