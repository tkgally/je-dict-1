防(ぼう)〜

ぼう
prefix
prevention; protection against

Definitions

1. prevention; protection against
Prefix indicating protection or prevention from something.

Examples

防災(ぼうさい)グッズを準備(じゅんび)しておく。
I prepare disaster prevention supplies.
防(ぼう) + 災(さい) = disaster prevention
防音(ぼうおん)の部屋(へや)で練習(れんしゅう)している。
I'm practicing in a soundproof room.
防(ぼう) + 音(おん) = soundproof
防犯(ぼうはん)カメラが設置(せっち)されている。
Security cameras are installed.
防(ぼう) + 犯(はん) = crime prevention

Notes

防(ぼう)〜 is a prefix meaning 'prevention of' or 'protection against.'

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 防災(ぼうさい) (disaster prevention)
  • 防犯(ぼうはん) (crime prevention)
  • 防音(ぼうおん) (soundproof)
  • 防水(ぼうすい) (waterproof)
  • 防火(ぼうか) (fireproof; fire prevention)
  • 防寒(ぼうかん) (protection against cold)
  • 防虫(ぼうちゅう) (insect repellent)
  • 防止(ぼうし) (prevention)
  • 防衛(ぼうえい) (defense)
  • 防御(ぼうぎょ) (defense; protection)

RELATED VERB:
防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent; to defend against) - the verb form uses kun'yomi.

NOTE:
防(ぼう)〜 creates compound nouns indicating what is being prevented or protected against.
