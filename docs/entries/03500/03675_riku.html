陸(りく)

りく
noun
land
1. land, ground
The solid surface of the earth as opposed to the sea or air.
船(ふね)が陸(りく)に着(つ)いた。
The ship reached land.
陸(りく)と海(うみ)の両方(りょうほう)を旅(たび)した。
I traveled by both land and sea.
陸(りく)に上(あ)がる。
To go ashore / to come onto land.

USAGE: 陸(りく) refers to dry land as opposed to water or air. It's often used in contrast with 海(うみ) (sea) or 空(そら) (sky).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 陸地(りくち) (land, dry land)
  • 陸上(りくじょう) (on land, land-based)
  • 大陸(たいりく) (continent)
  • 陸軍(りくぐん) (army)
  • 上陸(じょうりく) (landing, going ashore)
  • 陸路(りくろ) (overland route)

RELATED:

  • 海(うみ) - sea
  • 空(そら) - sky
