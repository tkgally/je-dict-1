何(なに)か

なにか
pronoun
something
1. something
An unspecified thing or matter.
何(なに)か食(た)べたい。
I want to eat something.
何(なに)か手伝(てつだ)いましょうか。
Can I help you with something?
何(なに)か飲(の)みますか。
Would you like something to drink?
外(そと)で何(なに)か音(おと)がした。
There was some noise outside.
2. anything
Used in questions and conditional sentences to mean 'anything at all.'
何(なに)か問題(もんだい)がありますか。
Is there any problem?
Used to ask about existence
何(なに)かあったら連絡(れんらく)してください。
Please contact me if anything happens.
何(なに)か欲(ほ)しいものある？
Is there anything you want?
何(なに)か分(わ)からないことがあったら聞(き)いて。
If there's anything you don't understand, ask me.
何(なに)かご質問(しつもん)はありますか。
Are there any questions?
3. somehow, for some reason
Used adverbially before an adjective or verb to say that something feels a certain way without the speaker being able to name the cause.
何(なに)か変(へん)だ。
Something feels off.
Adverbial use: 何か modifies 変, not a noun
何(なに)か寂(さび)しい気(き)がする。
Somehow I feel lonely.

USAGE: 何(なに)か points at a thing without identifying it. In statements it is "something"; in questions and conditionals it is "anything". Before an adjective it works adverbially and means "somehow".

NO PARTICLE AFTER IT:
何(なに)か normally drops the が or を that its grammatical role would call for: 何(なに)か食(た)べる, not 何(なに)かを食(た)べる. Other particles do attach: 何(なに)かに使(つか)う (use it for something).

THREE USES:
1. Something (statement): 何(なに)か音(おと)がした (I heard something)
2. Anything (question or conditional): 何(なに)かあったら (if anything happens)
3. Somehow (adverbial, before an adjective or a feeling): 何(なに)か変(へん)だ (something feels off)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 何(なに)か飲(の)む (to drink something)
  • 何(なに)か用(よう)ですか (Can I help you? / Do you need something?)
  • 何(なに)か手伝(てつだ)おうか (Shall I help with something?)

SPOKEN VARIANT:
なんか is the casual contraction. It covers the same ground but also works as a filler word and as a dismissive "things like", so it is not a free substitute in careful speech.

THE か SERIES:

  • 誰(だれ)か (someone)
  • どこか (somewhere)
  • いつか (sometime)

Each pairs with a も form that requires a negative: 何(なに)も (nothing at all).

Related Words
