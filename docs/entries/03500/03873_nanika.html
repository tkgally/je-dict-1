何(なに)か

なにか
pronoun
something

Definitions

1. something
An unspecified thing or matter.
2. anything
Used in questions and conditional sentences to mean 'anything at all.'

Examples

何(なに)か食(た)べたい。
I want to eat something.
何(なに)か問題(もんだい)がありますか。
Is there any problem?
Used to ask about existence
何(なに)かあったら連絡(れんらく)してください。
Please contact me if anything happens.

Notes

USAGE: 何(なに)か is used to refer to an unspecified thing. It can mean "something" in statements or "anything" in questions and conditionals.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 何(なに)か食(た)べる (to eat something)
  • 何(なに)か飲(の)む (to drink something)
  • 何(なに)かあったら (if anything happens)
  • 何(なに)か用(よう)ですか (Can I help you?/Do you need something?)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 何(なに)も (nothing - with negative)
  • 誰(だれ)か (someone)
  • どこか (somewhere)
  • いつか (sometime)

Related Words
