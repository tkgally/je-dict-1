綱(つな)

つな
noun
rope, cord, cable
1. rope, thick cord
A strong thick line made of twisted fibers or strands.
船(ふね)を綱(つな)でつないだ。
I tied the boat with a rope.
綱引(つなひ)きで勝(か)った。
We won at tug-of-war.
命(いのち)の綱(つな)だと思(おも)って離(はな)さなかった。
I held on tight, believing it was my lifeline.
2. sumo rope belt
The sacred rope worn by yokozuna (grand champion) in sumo wrestling.

綱(つな) means rope or cord, typically thicker than 紐(ひも) (string).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 綱引(つなひ)き (tug-of-war)
  • 命(いのち)の綱(つな) (lifeline)
  • 綱渡(つなわた)り (tightrope walking; risky situation)
  • 横綱(よこづな) (yokozuna, sumo grand champion)

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 紐(ひも) (string, thin cord)
  • 縄(なわ) (rope, cord - similar to 綱(つな))
  • ロープ (rope - loanword)
