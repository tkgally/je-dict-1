母親(ははおや)

ははおや
noun
mother
1. mother
A female parent. More formal or objective than 母(はは) (my mother) or お母(かあ)さん.
母親(ははおや)として責任(せきにん)を感(かん)じる。
I feel responsibility as a mother.
彼(かれ)の母親(ははおや)は教師(きょうし)だ。
His mother is a teacher.
母親(ははおや)の愛情(あいじょう)は深(ふか)い。
A mother's love is deep.

USAGE: 母親(ははおや) is a neutral/objective term for 'mother.' It's used when discussing mothers in general or referring to someone else's mother in a neutral way.

FAMILY TERM COMPARISON:

  • 母(はは) - my mother (humble, when speaking to others)
  • 母親(ははおや) - mother (neutral, objective)
  • お母(かあ)さん - mother, mom (familiar, can be used for others' mothers too)
  • ママ - mommy (casual)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 母親(ははおや)として (as a mother)
  • 母親(ははおや)の愛(あい) (mother's love)
  • 片(かた)親(おや) (single parent)
  • 実(じつ)の母親(ははおや) (biological mother)

ANTONYM:

  • 父親(ちちおや) (father)

Related Words

Antonym: 父親(ちちおや) (father)
Related: 母(はは) (my mother (humble))
