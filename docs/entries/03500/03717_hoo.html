頬(ほお)

ほお
noun
cheek
1. cheek
The fleshy part of the face below the eyes.
涙(なみだ)が頬(ほお)を伝(つた)った。
Tears ran down my cheeks.
頬(ほお)を赤(あか)らめた。
My cheeks turned red.
Blushing from embarrassment or emotion
赤(あか)ちゃんの頬(ほお)はふっくらしている。
The baby's cheeks are plump.

Can be read as either ほお or ほほ, with ほお being more common in modern usage.

ALTERNATIVE READING:

  • 頬(ほお) - more common
  • 頬(ほほ) - also used

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 頬(ほお)を染(そ)める (to blush)
  • 頬(ほお)を赤(あか)らめる (to turn red in the face)
  • 頬(ほお)を膨(ふく)らませる (to puff out one's cheeks - sulking)
  • 頬骨(ほおぼね) (cheekbone)
  • 頬(ほお)紅(べに) (blush, rouge)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 頬(ほお)が落(お)ちる (so delicious one's cheeks might fall off)
  • 頬(ほお)を{つねる} (to pinch one's cheek - to check if dreaming)
