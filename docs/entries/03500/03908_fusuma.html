襖(ふすま)

ふすま
noun
sliding screen, fusuma

Definitions

1. sliding screen, fusuma door
A traditional Japanese sliding door or room divider made of a wooden frame covered with thick paper or cloth.

Examples

襖(ふすま)を開(あ)けて隣(となり)の部屋(へや)に入(はい)った。
I opened the sliding screen and went into the next room.
古(ふる)い襖(ふすま)を張(は)り替(か)えた。
I had the old fusuma re-papered.
襖(ふすま)の絵(え)は有名(ゆうめい)な画家(がか)が描(えが)いたものだ。
The painting on the fusuma was done by a famous artist.

Notes

襖(ふすま) are traditional Japanese sliding doors used as room dividers in Japanese-style rooms. Unlike 障子(しょうじ), they are opaque.

COMPARISON:

  • 襖(ふすま): Opaque sliding screen (thick paper/cloth)
  • 障子(しょうじ): Translucent sliding screen (thin paper that lets light through)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 襖(ふすま)を開(あ)ける (to open the fusuma)
  • 襖(ふすま)を閉(し)める (to close the fusuma)
  • 襖(ふすま)を張(は)り替(か)える (to re-paper the fusuma)
  • 襖絵(ふすまえ) (painting on fusuma)

CULTURAL NOTE:
襖(ふすま) can feature elaborate paintings and are sometimes valuable works of art, especially in temples and historic buildings.
