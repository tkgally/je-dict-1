つまり

つまり
adverb, conjunction
in other words, that is to say

Definitions

1. in other words, that is to say, namely
Used to rephrase or clarify what was just said.
2. in short, in the end, after all
Used to summarize or state the conclusion.

Examples

彼女(かのじょ)は私(わたし)の父(ちち)の姉(あね)、つまり伯母(おば)です。
She is my father's older sister, in other words, my aunt.
つまり、賛成(さんせい)ということですね。
So in other words, you agree?
つまり、何(なに)が言(い)いたいの?
So what are you trying to say?

Notes

USAGE:
つまり is used to:
1. Clarify or rephrase something in simpler terms
2. Summarize a longer explanation
3. Draw a conclusion from what was said

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • つまり: In other words (rephrasing, summarizing)
  • すなわち: Namely, that is (more formal, written)
  • 要(よう)するに: In short, to sum up (emphasizes conclusion)
  • 結局(けっきょく): After all, in the end (emphasizes final result)

POSITION:
Usually appears at the beginning of a sentence or clause.

Related Words

Synonym: すなわち (namely (formal))
