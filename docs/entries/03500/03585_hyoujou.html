表情(ひょうじょう)

ひょうじょう
noun
facial expression
1. facial expression, look
The appearance of the face that conveys emotion or mood.
彼女(かのじょ)は悲(かな)しい表情(ひょうじょう)をしていた。
She had a sad expression on her face.
表情(ひょうじょう)から本心(ほんしん)を読(よ)み取(と)る。
To read someone's true feelings from their expression.
彼(かれ)は無(む)表情(ひょうじょう)のまま話(はな)し続(つづ)けた。
He continued talking with a blank expression.
無(む)表情(ひょうじょう) = expressionless

表情(ひょうじょう) refers specifically to facial expressions — the look on someone's face that conveys emotion or mood. It does not cover body language or gestures.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 表情(ひょうじょう)を見(み)せる: to show an expression
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)を変(か)える: to change one's expression
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)を読(よ)む: to read someone's expression
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)が豊(ゆた)か: expressive (rich in facial expressions)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)が硬(かた)い: having a stiff/tense expression

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 無(む)表情(ひょうじょう): expressionless, blank-faced
  • 表情(ひょうじょう)筋(きん): facial muscles

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 顔(かお)つき: facial look or features (more casual, can include permanent features)
  • 表情(ひょうじょう): specifically the emotional expression shown on the face (temporary state)
