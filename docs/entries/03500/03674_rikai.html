理解(りかい)

りかい
noun
understanding, comprehension
1. understanding, comprehension
The ability to grasp the meaning, nature, or significance of something.
この問題(もんだい)の理解(りかい)が深(ふか)まった。
My understanding of this problem deepened.
ご理解(りかい)とご協力(きょうりょく)をお願(ねが)いします。
We ask for your understanding and cooperation.
Polite request commonly used in announcements
2. sympathy, appreciation
A sympathetic awareness of another's situation or feelings.
ご理解(りかい)とご協力(きょうりょく)をお願(ねが)いします。
We ask for your understanding and cooperation.
Polite request commonly used in announcements
彼女(かのじょ)は理解(りかい)のある人(ひと)だ。
She is an understanding person.
理解のある = understanding, sympathetic

USAGE: 理解(りかい) can refer to intellectual understanding or to sympathetic appreciation of someone's situation.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 理解(りかい)する (to understand)
  • 理解(りかい)を深(ふか)める (to deepen understanding)
  • 理解(りかい)を得(え)る (to gain understanding/approval)
  • 理解(りかい)のある (understanding, sympathetic)
  • ご理解(りかい)ください (please understand - polite)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分(わ)かる - to understand (casual verb)
  • 把握(はあく) - grasp, understanding (formal)
  • 納得(なっとく) - acceptance, being convinced
