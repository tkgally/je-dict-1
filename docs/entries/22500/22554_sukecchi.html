1. sketch; quick drawing; rough illustration
A quick, informal drawing that captures the basic form of a subject. Also used for the act of sketching outdoors or from life.
風景(ふうけい)のスケッチを描(か)く。
I draw a sketch of the scenery.
公園(こうえん)でスケッチをしている人(ひと)がいた。
There was someone sketching in the park.
旅行中(りょこうちゅう)にスケッチブックに町並(まちな)みを描(か)き留(と)めた。
During the trip, I sketched the townscape in my sketchbook.
建築家(けんちくか)がまず手書(てが)きのスケッチで全体(ぜんたい)のイメージを伝(つた)えた。
The architect first conveyed the overall image with a hand-drawn sketch.
2. sketch; short descriptive piece of writing or media
A brief, impressionistic piece of writing, music, or other creative work that captures the essence of a scene or subject.
日常(にちじょう)生活(せいかつ)のスケッチを集(あつ)めたエッセイ集(しゅう)。
A collection of essays that are sketches of daily life.
下町(したまち)のスケッチというタイトルの番組(ばんぐみ)を見(み)た。
I watched a program titled 'Sketches of the Old Town.'

A loanword from English 'sketch.' Used both for the act of drawing and for the resulting drawing. スケッチする means 'to sketch.' In Japan, outdoor sketching (写生(しゃせい)) is a common school activity and hobby, especially in parks and scenic spots.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • スケッチする: to sketch
  • スケッチブック: sketchbook
  • スケッチ旅行(りょこう): sketching trip
  • 鉛筆(えんぴつ)スケッチ: pencil sketch

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 写生(しゃせい): sketching from life — the traditional Japanese term, often used in school contexts
  • デッサン: drawing, dessin — a more formal, studied drawing; from French
  • 下書(したが)き: rough draft — a preliminary drawing or writing
  • 素描(そびょう): rough sketch — a literary or artistic term for a basic drawing
