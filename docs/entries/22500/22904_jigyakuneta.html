自虐(じぎゃく)ネタ

じぎゃくねた
noun
self-deprecating joke; self-mocking humor
1. self-deprecating joke; self-mocking humor; material making fun of oneself
A joke or humorous anecdote where the speaker makes fun of themselves, their own failures, appearance, or shortcomings. A common comedic technique in Japanese culture.
彼(かれ)は自虐(じぎゃく)ネタが上手(うま)い。
He's good at self-deprecating humor.
自虐(じぎゃく)ネタで場(ば)の空気(くうき)を和(なご)ませた。
I lightened the mood with a self-deprecating joke.
自虐(じぎゃく)ネタばかり言(い)っていると、本当(ほんとう)に自信(じしん)がないように見(み)えるから気(き)をつけて。
If you keep making self-deprecating jokes, you'll actually look like you lack confidence, so be careful.

A modern colloquial term combining 自虐(じぎゃく) (self-torture, self-deprecation) with ネタ (material, joke topic). Very common in casual conversation, comedy shows, and social media.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自虐(じぎゃく)ネタを言(い)う: to tell a self-deprecating joke
  • 自虐(じぎゃく)ネタで笑(わら)いを取(と)る: to get laughs with self-deprecating humor
  • 自虐(じぎゃく)ネタが多(おお)い: to make a lot of self-deprecating jokes

USAGE:
Self-deprecating humor is widely used in Japan as a social lubricant. It can make the speaker seem approachable and humble. However, overdoing it can have the opposite effect, making others uncomfortable or giving the impression of genuine low self-esteem.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 自虐(じぎゃく): self-deprecation, self-mockery
  • 自虐的(じぎゃくてき): self-deprecating (adjective)
  • ネタ: material, joke topic, story material
