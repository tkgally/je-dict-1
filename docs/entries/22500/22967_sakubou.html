1. plot; scheme; intrigue
A secret plan devised to achieve a particular goal, typically involving deception or manipulation. Carries a negative connotation of underhanded scheming.
策謀(さくぼう)をめぐらす。
To hatch a scheme.
彼(かれ)の策謀(さくぼう)が見抜(みぬ)かれた。
His scheming was seen through.
政治(せいじ)の裏側(うらがわ)ではさまざまな策謀(さくぼう)が渦巻(うずま)いている。
Various intrigues are swirling behind the scenes of politics.

A literary word for scheming and plotting, often used in political, historical, and fictional narratives. Composed of 策(さく) (plan, scheme) and 謀(ぼう) (plot, conspiracy).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 策謀(さくぼう)をめぐらす: to devise a scheme
  • 策謀(さくぼう)を企(たくら)む: to plot an intrigue
  • 策謀(さくぼう)家(か): schemer; intriguer
  • 策謀(さくぼう)に長(た)けた: skilled at scheming

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 陰謀(いんぼう): conspiracy; plot — larger-scale, often involving multiple people
  • 謀略(ぼうりゃく): stratagem; plot — more military or strategic
  • 企(たくら)み: scheme; plan — more colloquial, can be lighter in tone
  • 画策(かくさく): scheming; maneuvering — emphasizes the planning aspect

REGISTER:
Formal and literary. Common in novels, historical dramas, and news commentary. Rarely used in casual conversation.
