1. to destroy; to demolish (physical things)
To damage something so severely that it cannot be used or no longer exists.
地震(じしん)が建物(たてもの)を破壊(はかい)した。
The earthquake destroyed the building.
古(ふる)いビルを破壊(はかい)して新(あたら)しいものを建(た)てる。
They will demolish the old building and construct a new one.
台風(たいふう)が町(まち)のインフラを完全(かんぜん)に破壊(はかい)してしまった。
The typhoon completely destroyed the town's infrastructure.
2. to ruin; to undermine (abstract things)
To damage or undo something intangible such as trust, balance, or a system.
その発言(はつげん)が信頼(しんらい)関係(かんけい)を破壊(はかい)した。
That remark destroyed the relationship of trust.
環境(かんきょう)を破壊(はかい)する行為(こうい)は許(ゆる)されない。
Acts that destroy the environment are unforgivable.
長年(ながねん)かけて築(きず)いた生態系(せいたいけい)のバランスが人間(にんげん)の活動(かつどう)によって破壊(はかい)されつつある。
The ecological balance built up over many years is being destroyed by human activity.

A strong word implying thorough or severe damage. It carries a sense of violence or force, unlike milder words like 壊(こわ)す. The noun form 破壊(はかい) is frequently used in compound expressions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 環境(かんきょう)破壊(はかい): environmental destruction
  • 自己(じこ)破壊(はかい): self-destruction
  • 大量(たいりょう)破壊(はかい)兵器(へいき): weapons of mass destruction
  • 破壊(はかい)力(りょく): destructive power
  • 破壊(はかい)的(てき): destructive

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 壊(こわ)す: to break — everyday word for breaking physical objects; less dramatic than 破壊(はかい)する
  • 潰(つぶ)す: to crush; to squash — focuses on flattening or pressing something until it loses shape
  • 崩壊(ほうかい)する: to collapse; to crumble — emphasizes falling apart or disintegrating, often from within
