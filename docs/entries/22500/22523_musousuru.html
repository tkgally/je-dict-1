1. to dream of; to fantasize about; to imagine idly
To indulge in unrealistic or fanciful thoughts about something, often something unlikely or impractical.
世界(せかい)平和(へいわ)を夢想(むそう)する。
To dream of world peace.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)、宇宙(うちゅう)飛行士(ひこうし)になることを夢想(むそう)していた。
As a child, I used to fantasize about becoming an astronaut.
彼(かれ)はいつも窓(まど)の外(そと)を見(み)ながら遠(とお)い国(くに)での生活(せいかつ)を夢想(むそう)している。
He is always gazing out the window, fantasizing about life in a faraway country.

A literary word that implies idle, unrealistic daydreaming or fantasizing. It often carries a slightly romantic or impractical nuance — the thing being dreamed of is typically distant, grand, or unlikely. The noun form 夢想(むそう) appears in compounds like 夢想家(むそうか) (dreamer, fantasist).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 夢想(むそう)にふける: to be lost in daydreams
  • 夢想家(むそうか): a dreamer; a fantasist
  • 夢想(むそう)の世界(せかい): a world of fantasy
  • 壮大(そうだい)な夢想(むそう): a grand fantasy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 夢(ゆめ)を見(み)る: to dream (while sleeping) — literal dreaming during sleep
  • 空想(くうそう)する: to daydream; to imagine — similar but more neutral; less literary than 夢想(むそう)
  • 妄想(もうそう)する: to have delusions; to fantasize — carries a more negative connotation of irrational or obsessive fantasy
