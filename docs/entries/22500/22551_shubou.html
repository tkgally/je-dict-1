1. masterminding; plotting; being the ringleader of a plan or scheme
The act of planning and leading a plot, conspiracy, or organized action. Almost always carries a negative connotation, implying criminal activity, rebellion, or wrongdoing.
事件(じけん)の首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ)が逮捕(たいほ)された。
The mastermind of the incident was arrested.
彼(かれ)がクーデターを首謀(しゅぼう)したと言(い)われている。
He is said to have masterminded the coup.
詐欺(さぎ)グループの首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ)は長年(ながねん)にわたって捜査(そうさ)の手(て)を逃(のが)れていた。
The ringleader of the fraud group had evaded investigation for many years.

Composed of 首(しゅ) (head, chief) + 謀(ぼう) (plot, scheme). Most commonly encountered in the compound 首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ) (mastermind, ringleader). The word is strongly negative, associated with criminal plots, conspiracies, rebellions, and organized wrongdoing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ): mastermind, ringleader
  • 首謀(しゅぼう)する: to mastermind, to plot
  • 事件(じけん)の首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ): the mastermind behind the incident
  • テロの首謀者(しゅぼうしゃ): the mastermind of the terrorist attack

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 黒幕(くろまく): mastermind behind the scenes — implies pulling strings from a hidden position
  • 主犯(しゅはん): principal offender — the legal term for the main perpetrator
  • 張本人(ちょうほんにん): the person responsible — the one who is the root cause of trouble
