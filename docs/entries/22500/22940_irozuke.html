色(いろ)づけ

いろづけ
noun
coloring; tinting; adding color
1. coloring; tinting; adding color
The act of applying color to something, whether literally (painting, dyeing, food decoration) or figuratively (adding character or flair to something).
料理(りょうり)の色(いろ)づけにパセリを使(つか)う。
I use parsley for adding color to the dish.
子(こ)どもたちが粘土(ねんど)に色(いろ)づけをしている。
The children are coloring the clay.
写真(しゃしん)に色(いろ)づけをして、昔(むかし)の風景(ふうけい)を鮮(あざ)やかに再現(さいげん)した。
By colorizing the photo, they vividly recreated the old scenery.

Combines 色(いろ) (color) with づけ (the noun form of 付(つ)ける, to attach/apply). Refers to the process of adding color in cooking, art, photography, and other fields.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 色(いろ)づけをする: to add color, to colorize
  • 色(いろ)づけに使(つか)う: to use for coloring
  • 食紅(しょくべに)で色(いろ)づけする: to color with food dye
  • 自然(しぜん)な色(いろ)づけ: natural coloring

USAGE:
Particularly common in cooking contexts where garnishes or ingredients are added for visual appeal rather than flavor. Also used in photography (colorizing black-and-white images) and crafts.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 色付(いろづ)き: becoming colored, taking on color — describes the natural process (e.g., autumn leaves)
  • 着色(ちゃくしょく): coloring, pigmentation — more technical/formal term
  • 彩(いろど)り: color arrangement, variety of colors — emphasizes aesthetic effect
