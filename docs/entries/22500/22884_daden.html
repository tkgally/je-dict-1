1. sending a telegram; telegraphing
The act of dispatching a telegram or cable message. A formal, somewhat historical term still encountered in literature and news reporting.
本部(ほんぶ)に打電(だでん)した。
We sent a telegram to headquarters.
救援(きゅうえん)を求(もと)める打電(だでん)が入(はい)った。
A telegram requesting rescue came in.
艦長(かんちょう)は至急(しきゅう)打電(だでん)せよと命(めい)じた。
The captain ordered them to send a telegram immediately.

Composed of 打(だ) (to strike, to hit) + 電(でん) (electricity, telegram). The verb 打(う)つ was historically used for sending telegrams because telegraph operators physically tapped a key to transmit Morse code.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 打電(だでん)する: to send a telegram
  • 至急(しきゅう)打電(だでん): urgent telegram
  • 打電(だでん)を受(う)ける: to receive a telegram

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電報(でんぽう): telegram — the general word for the message itself
  • 祝電(しゅくでん): congratulatory telegram — a specific type
  • 弔電(ちょうでん): condolence telegram — a specific type
  • 発電(はつでん): in addition to its common meaning of "power generation," can also mean sending a telegram (though this usage is rare)
