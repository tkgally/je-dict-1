1. rough; crude; violent
Describes behavior, actions, or manner that is rough, aggressive, or lacking in gentleness.
運転(うんてん)が荒(あら)っぽい。
The driving is rough.
あの人(ひと)は言葉(ことば)が荒(あら)っぽくて怖(こわ)い。
That person's language is rough and scary.
荒(あら)っぽいやり方(かた)では壊(こわ)してしまうよ。
If you do it roughly like that, you'll break it.

Describes roughness in behavior, manner, or actions. Can refer to physical roughness (handling things carelessly), verbal roughness (harsh speech), or general aggressiveness. It carries a negative connotation and implies a lack of care or refinement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荒(あら)っぽい運転(うんてん): rough driving
  • 荒(あら)っぽい言葉(ことば): rough language
  • 荒(あら)っぽいやり方(かた): rough way of doing things
  • 荒(あら)っぽい性格(せいかく): rough personality

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 荒(あら)い: rough, wild — broader in meaning; can describe rough seas, rough breathing, etc.
  • 乱暴(らんぼう)な: violent, rough — stronger than 荒(あら)っぽい, implying actual violence or lawlessness
  • 粗(あら)い: coarse — refers to texture or grain, not behavior
  • 手荒(てあら)い: rough (in handling) — specifically about rough physical treatment
