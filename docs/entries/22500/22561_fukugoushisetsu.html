複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)

ふくごうしせつ
noun
mixed-use complex; multi-purpose facility
1. mixed-use complex; multi-purpose facility
A building or development that combines multiple functions such as retail, office, residential, and entertainment spaces under one roof.
駅前(えきまえ)に複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)ができた。
A mixed-use complex was built in front of the station.
この複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)には映画館(えいがかん)や図書館(としょかん)がある。
This complex has a cinema and a library.
商業(しょうぎょう)と住宅(じゅうたく)が一体(いったい)となった大型(おおがた)複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)が来年(らいねん)完成(かんせい)する。
A large mixed-use complex combining commercial and residential spaces will be completed next year.

A compound of 複合(ふくごう) (composite, combined) and 施設(しせつ) (facility). Refers to buildings or developments that house multiple types of functions — such as retail, office, residential, cultural, and entertainment spaces — under one roof or in one development.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大型(おおがた)複合施設(ふくごうしせつ): large mixed-use complex
  • 商業(しょうぎょう)複合施設(ふくごうしせつ): commercial complex
  • 複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)の建設(けんせつ): construction of a complex
  • 複合施設(ふくごうしせつ)の開発(かいはつ): development of a complex

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 商業施設(しょうぎょうしせつ): commercial facility — focused solely on retail and commercial use
  • ショッピングモール: shopping mall — primarily retail-focused
  • 多目的施設(たもくてきしせつ): multi-purpose facility — a single facility that can be used for different purposes, rather than multiple facilities combined
