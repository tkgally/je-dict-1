酒気帯(しゅきお)び

しゅきおび
noun
being under the influence of alcohol; having alcohol in one's system
1. being under the influence of alcohol (legal/traffic term); having a detectable level of alcohol
酒気帯(しゅきお)び運転(うんてん)は法律(ほうりつ)で禁止(きんし)されている。
Driving under the influence of alcohol is prohibited by law.
酒気帯(しゅきお)びの状態(じょうたい)で自転車(じてんしゃ)に乗(の)るのも違反(いはん)だ。
Riding a bicycle while under the influence of alcohol is also a violation.
検問(けんもん)で酒気帯(しゅきお)びが発覚(はっかく)し、免許(めんきょ)停止(ていし)になった。
Alcohol was detected at a checkpoint, and his license was suspended.

Composed of 酒気(しゅき) (the smell/presence of alcohol) and 帯(お)び (wearing, having on one's person). A legal and traffic-related term describing the state of having a detectable amount of alcohol in one's body. In Japanese traffic law, this is distinguished from 酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)い運転(うんてん) (drunk driving), which implies a higher level of intoxication.

USAGE:

Almost always appears in compound form:

  • 酒気帯(しゅきお)び運転(うんてん): driving under the influence (DUI)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 酒気帯(しゅきお)び運転(うんてん): driving under the influence
  • 酒気帯(しゅきお)びの状態(じょうたい): state of having alcohol in one's system
  • 酒気帯(しゅきお)び検査(けんさ): alcohol breath test

LEGAL NOTE:
Japanese law distinguishes between 酒気帯(しゅきお)び運転(うんてん) (driving with alcohol detected, BAC 0.15mg/L or more) and 酒酔(しゅよ)い運転(うんてん) (driving while intoxicated, based on impaired ability). The penalties for 酒酔(しゅよ)い運転(うんてん) are more severe.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飲酒(いんしゅ)運転(うんてん): drunk driving — a general term covering any alcohol-impaired driving
  • 酒酔(しゅよ)い運転(うんてん): driving while intoxicated — more severe legal category
  • 酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)い: drunkard, drunk person — colloquial, not a legal term
