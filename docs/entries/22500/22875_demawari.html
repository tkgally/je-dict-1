出回(でまわ)り

でまわり
noun
appearance on the market; circulation; availability
1. appearance on the market; availability; circulation of goods
The state of products, especially seasonal foods, becoming widely available in stores and markets.
いちごの出回(でまわ)りが始(はじ)まった。
Strawberries have started appearing in stores.
新米(しんまい)の出回(でまわ)る時期(じき)だ。
It's the season when new rice hits the market.
今年(ことし)は暖冬(だんとう)の影響(えいきょう)で野菜(やさい)の出回(でまわ)りが早(はや)い。
Vegetables are appearing on the market earlier this year due to the mild winter.

The noun form of 出回(でまわ)る (to appear on the market, to be in circulation). Commonly used to discuss seasonal availability of foods, especially fruits, vegetables, and fish.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 出回(でまわ)りが早(はや)い: appearing on the market early
  • 出回(でまわ)りの時期(じき): the time when something appears in stores
  • 出回(でまわ)り量(りょう): the quantity available on the market
  • 市場(しじょう)に出回(でまわ)る: to appear on the market

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 流通(りゅうつう): distribution, circulation — broader term for the movement of goods through supply chains
  • 旬(しゅん): in season — refers to the peak season for a food item
