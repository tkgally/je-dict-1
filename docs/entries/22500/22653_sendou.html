1. guidance; leading the way; spearheading
The act of going ahead to show the way or taking the lead in guiding a group or initiative.
パレードを先導(せんどう)する。
To lead a parade.
警察(けいさつ)の車両(しゃりょう)が先導(せんどう)して進(すす)んだ。
A police vehicle led the way.
彼女(かのじょ)はプロジェクトの先導(せんどう)役(やく)を務(つと)め、チーム全体(ぜんたい)をまとめた。
She served as the project leader and brought the whole team together.

A compound of 先(せん) (ahead/first) and 導(どう) (guide/lead). Implies physically or figuratively going ahead to show the way for others to follow.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 先導(せんどう)する: to lead, to guide
  • 先導(せんどう)役(やく): leader, guide (role)
  • 先導(せんどう)車(しゃ): lead vehicle, escort car
  • 先導(せんどう)のもとに: under the guidance of

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 案内(あんない): guidance — more everyday; implies showing around or giving directions
  • 指導(しどう): instruction, coaching — implies teaching or supervising rather than going ahead
  • 誘導(ゆうどう): guidance, leading — implies directing or channeling movement, often in a controlled manner
  • 船頭(せんどう): boatman — a homophone with an unrelated meaning
