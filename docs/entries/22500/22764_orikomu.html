1. to weave in; to work into fabric
To incorporate threads, patterns, or designs into woven fabric.
金糸(きんし)を織(お)り込(こ)んだ布(ぬの)。
Fabric with gold thread woven in.
模様(もよう)を織(お)り込(こ)んだ着物(きもの)は高価(こうか)だ。
Kimono with woven-in patterns are expensive.
伝統的(でんとうてき)な技法(ぎほう)で花柄(はながら)を織(お)り込(こ)んだ帯(おび)が人気(にんき)だ。
Obi sashes with floral patterns woven in using traditional techniques are popular.
2. to factor in; to incorporate; to take into account
To include or integrate something (such as expectations, risks, or information) into a plan, calculation, or assessment. Commonly used in business and finance.
リスクを織(お)り込(こ)んで計画(けいかく)する。
To plan while factoring in the risks.
株価(かぶか)にはすでに悪(わる)い材料(ざいりょう)が織(お)り込(こ)まれている。
The bad news is already factored into the stock price.
今回(こんかい)の予算(よさん)には物価(ぶっか)上昇(じょうしょう)を織(お)り込(こ)んだ上(うえ)で作成(さくせい)した。
This budget was prepared with inflation factored in.

Compound verb from 織(お)る ('to weave') + 込(こ)む ('into'). The literal sense of weaving something into fabric extends metaphorically to mean incorporating information or expectations into plans, prices, or calculations.

The figurative sense (sense 2) is extremely common in business and financial contexts. The passive form 織(お)り込(こ)まれる is particularly frequent in stock market commentary, where it describes expectations that are already reflected in prices.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 織(お)り込(こ)み済(ず)み: already factored in, priced in
  • 市場(しじょう)に織(お)り込(こ)む: to price into the market
  • リスクを織(お)り込(こ)む: to factor in risks
  • 期待(きたい)を織(お)り込(こ)む: to factor in expectations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 考慮(こうりょ)する: to consider — more general; does not imply integration into a calculation
  • 反映(はんえい)させる: to reflect — emphasizes the result of incorporation
  • 含(ふく)める: to include — broader; lacks the nuance of integration into a framework
