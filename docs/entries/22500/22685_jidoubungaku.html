児童文学(じどうぶんがく)

じどうぶんがく
noun
children's literature
1. children's literature
Literature written for or about children, including picture books, fairy tales, and novels aimed at young readers.
児童文学(じどうぶんがく)を読(よ)むのが好(す)きだ。
I like reading children's literature.
この作家(さっか)は児童文学(じどうぶんがく)で有名(ゆうめい)だ。
This author is famous for children's literature.
日本(にほん)の児童文学(じどうぶんがく)には心温(こころあたた)まる作品(さくひん)が多(おお)い。
There are many heartwarming works in Japanese children's literature.

A compound of 児童(じどう) (children) + 文学(ぶんがく) (literature). Covers a wide range of works from picture books (絵本(えほん)) for very young children to novels for older children. Japanese 児童文学(じどうぶんがく) has a rich tradition, with well-known authors like 宮沢(みやざわ)賢治(けんじ) and 新美(にいみ)南吉(なんきち).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 児童文学(じどうぶんがく)作家(さっか): children's literature author
  • 児童文学(じどうぶんがく)賞(しょう): children's literature award
  • 児童文学(じどうぶんがく)の名作(めいさく): classic of children's literature
  • 児童文学(じどうぶんがく)の分野(ぶんや): the field of children's literature

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 絵本(えほん): picture book — a specific format within children's literature aimed at younger children
  • 童話(どうわ): fairy tale, children's story — refers to shorter stories, often folk tales or fantasies, while 児童文学(じどうぶんがく) is the broader category
