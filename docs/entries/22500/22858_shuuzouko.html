収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)

しゅうぞうこ
noun
storage facility; repository; vault
1. storage facility; repository; vault
a room or building used for storing and preserving items, especially collections at museums, archives, or libraries
美術品(びじゅつひん)は収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)に保管(ほかん)されている。
The artworks are kept in a storage facility.
博物館(はくぶつかん)の収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)は一般(いっぱん)公開(こうかい)されていない。
The museum's storage facility is not open to the public.
温度(おんど)と湿度(しつど)が管理(かんり)された収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)で、貴重(きちょう)な資料(しりょう)が守(まも)られている。
Valuable documents are protected in a storage facility with controlled temperature and humidity.

Composed of 収蔵(しゅうぞう) (storage, collection) and 庫(こ) (storehouse). Refers specifically to purpose-built storage spaces, particularly in institutional settings like museums, galleries, and archives where items are preserved under controlled conditions.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美術館(びじゅつかん)の収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ): art museum storage
  • 博物館(はくぶつかん)の収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ): museum storage
  • 収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)に保管(ほかん)する: to store in a repository
  • 収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)の見学(けんがく): tour of the storage facility

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 倉庫(そうこ): warehouse, storage — a more general term for any storage space
  • 書庫(しょこ): book storage, stacks — specifically for books and documents
  • 金庫(きんこ): safe, vault — specifically for valuables and money
  • 収蔵品(しゅうぞうひん): collection items, stored holdings — the items kept in a 収蔵庫(しゅうぞうこ)
