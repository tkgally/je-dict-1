家族(かぞく)ぐるみ

かぞくぐるみ
noun
including the whole family; as families together
1. including the whole family; involving entire families; as families together
Describes a relationship or activity that involves not just individuals but their entire families. Often used for friendships or social interactions where whole families participate together.
家族(かぞく)ぐるみの付(つ)き合(あ)いだ。
We socialize as whole families.
隣(となり)の田中(たなか)さんとは家族(かぞく)ぐるみで仲(なか)がいい。
We're close with the Tanakas next door as whole families.
子(こ)ども同士(どうし)が同(おな)じクラスで、家族(かぞく)ぐるみでバーベキューをするようになった。
Our kids are in the same class, and we started having barbecues together as families.

Used to describe relationships or activities that extend beyond individuals to include whole families. The suffix ぐるみ means "including everything" or "all-inclusive."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家族(かぞく)ぐるみの付(つ)き合(あ)い: socializing as whole families
  • 家族(かぞく)ぐるみで旅行(りょこう)する: to travel together as families
  • 家族(かぞく)ぐるみの友人(ゆうじん): a family friend (the families are close)

RELATED PATTERNS:

  • ～ぐるみ is a productive suffix meaning "including all of"
  • 会社(かいしゃ)ぐるみ: involving the whole company
  • 地域(ちいき)ぐるみ: involving the whole community
  • 町(まち)ぐるみ: involving the whole town

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一家(いっか)総出(そうで): the whole family turning out — emphasizes physical participation in an activity
