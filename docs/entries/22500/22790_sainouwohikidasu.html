才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す

さいのうをひきだす
expression
to bring out someone's talent; to draw out ability
1. to bring out someone's talent; to draw out ability
To help someone discover or develop their latent abilities.
子(こ)どもの才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す。
To bring out a child's talent.
いい先生(せんせい)は生徒(せいと)の才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)せる。
A good teacher can bring out a student's talent.
あの監督(かんとく)は若(わか)い選手(せんしゅ)の才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)すのがうまい。
That coach is good at bringing out the talent of young players.

A common expression combining 才能(さいのう) (talent, ability) with 引(ひ)き出(だ)す (to draw out, to pull out). The image is of talent being hidden inside a person and needing to be drawn out by a mentor, teacher, or environment. Frequently used in education and coaching contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隠(かく)れた才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to draw out hidden talent
  • 最大限(さいだいげん)に才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to bring out talent to its fullest
  • 才能(さいのう)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す教育(きょういく): education that draws out talent

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 力(ちから)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to draw out one's strength/ability
  • 可能性(かのうせい)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to draw out one's potential
  • 魅力(みりょく)を引(ひ)き出(だ)す: to bring out one's charm

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 才能(さいのう)を伸(の)ばす: to develop talent — focuses on growth and nurturing over time
  • 才能(さいのう)を活(い)かす: to make use of talent — emphasizes applying existing talent effectively
