南欧(なんおう)

なんおう
noun
Southern Europe
1. Southern Europe
The southern part of Europe, typically including countries such as Italy, Spain, Portugal, and Greece. Used in geography, travel, cuisine, and cultural discussions.
南欧(なんおう)を旅行(りょこう)する。
To travel in Southern Europe.
南欧(なんおう)の料理(りょうり)はオリーブオイルを多(おお)く使(つか)う。
Southern European cuisine uses a lot of olive oil.
南欧(なんおう)は地中海性気候(ちちゅうかいせいきこう)で、冬(ふゆ)でも比較的(ひかくてき)暖(あたた)かい。
Southern Europe has a Mediterranean climate and is relatively warm even in winter.

Composed of 南(なん) (south) and 欧(おう) (Europe, short for 欧州(おうしゅう)). Part of a set of geographic terms using the same pattern.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 北欧(ほくおう): Northern Europe (Scandinavia)
  • 西欧(せいおう): Western Europe
  • 東欧(とうおう): Eastern Europe
  • 中欧(ちゅうおう): Central Europe
  • 欧州(おうしゅう): Europe (formal)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 南欧(なんおう)風(ふう): Southern European style
  • 南欧(なんおう)料理(りょうり): Southern European cuisine
  • 南欧(なんおう)諸国(しょこく): the countries of Southern Europe
  • 南欧(なんおう)旅行(りょこう): trip to Southern Europe

USAGE:
Used as a modifier with の or in compound nouns. Common in travel writing, food culture, and architecture discussions. 南欧(なんおう)風(ふう) (Southern European style) is frequently used to describe Mediterranean-inspired home design and interior decoration in Japan.
