無党派(むとうは)

むとうは
noun, adjective (no)
nonpartisan; independent (politically)
1. nonpartisan; independent; not affiliated with any political party
Not belonging to or supporting any particular political party. Used to describe both politicians who run without party affiliation and voters who do not consistently support one party.
無党派(むとうは)層(そう)の票(ひょう)が選挙(せんきょ)の鍵(かぎ)を握(にぎ)る。
The votes of independent voters hold the key to the election.
彼(かれ)は無党派(むとうは)で立候補(りっこうほ)した。
He ran as an independent candidate.
若者(わかもの)の間(あいだ)では無党派(むとうは)の人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Among young people, the number of those with no party affiliation is growing.

Composed of 無(む) (without) and 党派(とうは) (political party/faction). A key term in Japanese election coverage, as 無党派(むとうは)層(そう) (the independent voter bloc) often determines election outcomes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無党派(むとうは)層(そう): independent voters; swing voters
  • 無党派(むとうは)で立候補(りっこうほ)する: to run as an independent
  • 無党派(むとうは)の有権者(ゆうけんしゃ): nonpartisan voters
  • 無党派(むとうは)候補(こうほ): independent candidate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無所属(むしょぞく): unaffiliated; independent — specifically about formal organizational membership
  • 中立(ちゅうりつ): neutral — broader, not limited to politics
  • 浮動票(ふどうひょう): floating votes; swing votes — the votes themselves, not the voters

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese politics, the 無党派(むとうは)層(そう) has grown significantly since the 1990s, reflecting voter disillusionment with established parties. Poll results often report the percentage of respondents who identify as 無党派(むとうは).
