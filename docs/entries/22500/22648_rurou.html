1. wandering; vagrancy; roaming
Traveling from place to place without a fixed home or destination. Carries a literary or dramatic nuance, often evoking hardship or a rootless existence.
流浪(るろう)の旅(たび)に出(で)る。
To set out on a wandering journey.
彼(かれ)は何年(なんねん)も流浪(るろう)の生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)った。
He led a wandering life for many years.
戦争(せんそう)で故郷(こきょう)を失(うしな)った人々(ひとびと)は各地(かくち)を流浪(るろう)するしかなかった。
People who lost their homeland in the war had no choice but to wander from place to place.

A literary term for aimless wandering, often implying hardship or displacement. More dramatic and evocative than everyday words for traveling.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 流浪(るろう)の民(たみ): wandering people, nomads
  • 流浪(るろう)の旅(たび): a wandering journey
  • 流浪(るろう)の生活(せいかつ): a vagabond life
  • 流浪(るろう)する: to wander, to roam

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 放浪(ほうろう): wandering — very similar but slightly more neutral; often used for voluntary wandering or adventure
  • 漂泊(ひょうはく): drifting — more literary, emphasizes being carried along without purpose
  • {さまよう}: to wander — a verb with a sense of being lost or confused

CULTURAL NOTE:
Famously used in the manga/anime title 流浪(るろう)に剣心(けんしん) (Rurouni Kenshin), which popularized the word among younger generations.
