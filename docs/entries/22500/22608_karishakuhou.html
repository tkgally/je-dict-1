1. parole; conditional release; early release from prison
The release of a prisoner before the completion of their sentence, subject to conditions and supervision.
受刑者(じゅけいしゃ)が仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)された。
The prisoner was released on parole.
仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)の条件(じょうけん)に違反(いはん)した。
He violated the conditions of parole.
刑期(けいき)の三分(さんぶん)の二(に)を終(お)えた時点(じてん)で仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)が認(みと)められることがある。
Parole may be granted after serving two-thirds of the sentence.

A compound of 仮(かり) (temporary, provisional) and 釈放(しゃくほう) (release, discharge). In the Japanese legal system, 仮釈放(かりしゃくほう) requires approval from the regional parole board and involves supervision by a probation officer during the remaining sentence period.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)される: to be released on parole
  • 仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)を認(みと)める: to grant parole
  • 仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)の条件(じょうけん): conditions of parole
  • 仮釈放(かりしゃくほう)取(と)り消(け)し: revocation of parole

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 釈放(しゃくほう): release; discharge — unconditional release from custody
  • 保釈(ほしゃく): bail — release before trial upon payment of money
  • 執行猶予(しっこうゆうよ): suspended sentence — a sentence that is not carried out if the offender behaves well
