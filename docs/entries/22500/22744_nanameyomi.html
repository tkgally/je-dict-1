1. skimming; speed reading; quick reading
Reading text quickly by scanning rather than reading every word carefully. The literal image is of reading 'diagonally' across the page rather than line by line.
新聞(しんぶん)を斜(なな)め読(よ)みする。
To skim through the newspaper.
時間(じかん)がないので斜(なな)め読(よ)みで済(す)ませた。
I didn't have time, so I just skimmed through it.
斜(なな)め読(よ)みでは大事(だいじ)なポイントを見落(みお)としてしまうことがある。
When skimming, you may overlook important points.

Literally 'diagonal reading' (斜(なな)め 'diagonal, slanted' + 読(よ)み 'reading'). The image is of eyes moving diagonally across the page rather than following each line. Used as a noun or with する as a verb (斜(なな)め読(よ)みする). A useful and commonly encountered word in everyday Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 斜(なな)め読(よ)みする: to skim-read
  • 斜(なな)め読(よ)みで済(す)ませる: to get by with just skimming
  • 新聞(しんぶん)の斜(なな)め読(よ)み: skimming through the newspaper

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 読(よ)み流(なが)す: to skim over — a verb meaning to read without paying close attention
  • 速読(そくどく): speed reading — a more formal term, often used for trained speed-reading techniques
  • 拾(ひろ)い読(よ)み: selective reading — picking out only certain parts to read
