年額(ねんがく)

ねんがく
noun
annual amount; yearly fee
1. annual amount; yearly fee; per-year sum
A total sum of money calculated, charged, or paid on a per-year basis, commonly used for subscriptions, insurance, and salary figures.
年額(ねんがく)で支払(しはら)う。
To pay on a yearly basis.
このサービスの年額(ねんがく)は一万円(いちまんえん)です。
The annual fee for this service is 10,000 yen.
保険料(ほけんりょう)を月額(げつがく)から年額(ねんがく)に変更(へんこう)すると少(すこ)し安(やす)くなる。
Switching the insurance premium from monthly to annual makes it a little cheaper.

A compound of 年(ねん) (year) and 額(がく) (amount). Used to express the total amount of money charged, earned, or paid over the span of one year. Common in financial, insurance, subscription, and tax contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年額(ねんがく)プラン: annual plan
  • 年額(ねんがく)料金(りょうきん): annual fee/rate
  • 年額(ねんがく)で計算(けいさん)する: to calculate on an annual basis
  • 年額(ねんがく)換算(かんさん): annualized equivalent

RELATED TERMS:

  • 月額(げつがく): monthly amount — the monthly counterpart
  • 日額(にちがく): daily amount — the daily counterpart
  • 総額(そうがく): total amount — the grand total regardless of time period
  • 年収(ねんしゅう): annual income — specifically refers to income/earnings
