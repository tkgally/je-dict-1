早期診断(そうきしんだん)

そうきしんだん
noun
early diagnosis
1. early diagnosis
The detection and identification of a disease or condition at an early stage, before it has progressed significantly.
早期診断(そうきしんだん)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Early diagnosis is important.
がんの早期診断(そうきしんだん)で命(いのち)が助(たす)かった。
Early diagnosis of cancer saved a life.
定期的(ていきてき)な健康診断(けんこうしんだん)は早期診断(そうきしんだん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう)につながる。
Regular health checkups lead to early diagnosis and early treatment.

A compound of 早期(そうき) (early stage) + 診断(しんだん) (diagnosis). Often paired with 早期治療(そうきちりょう) (early treatment) in the set phrase 早期診断(そうきしんだん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう), which is a cornerstone principle of Japanese preventive medicine.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 早期診断(そうきしんだん)・早期治療(そうきちりょう): early diagnosis and early treatment
  • 早期診断(そうきしんだん)の重要性(じゅうようせい): importance of early diagnosis
  • がんの早期診断(そうきしんだん): early diagnosis of cancer

RELATED TERMS:

  • 早期発見(そうきはっけん): early detection — finding a condition early, often used interchangeably
  • 健康診断(けんこうしんだん): health checkup — the examination that enables early diagnosis
  • 人間ドック(にんげんどっく): comprehensive medical checkup — a thorough examination popular in Japan
