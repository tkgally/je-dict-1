1. childbirth; delivery; labor
The medical or formal term for the process of giving birth.
分娩(ぶんべん)予定日(よていび)はいつですか。
When is the expected delivery date?
自然(しぜん)分娩(ぶんべん)を希望(きぼう)しています。
I'm hoping for a natural delivery.
分娩(ぶんべん)は無事(ぶじ)に終(お)わり、母子(ぼし)ともに健康(けんこう)です。
The delivery went smoothly, and both mother and child are healthy.

The formal/medical term for childbirth. Used primarily in medical contexts, official documents, and news reporting. In everyday conversation, 出産(しゅっさん) or 産(う)む is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自然(しぜん)分娩(ぶんべん): natural childbirth
  • 無痛(むつう)分娩(ぶんべん): painless delivery (epidural birth)
  • 分娩(ぶんべん)室(しつ): delivery room
  • 分娩(ぶんべん)台(だい): delivery bed
  • 分娩(ぶんべん)予定日(よていび): expected delivery date
  • 異常(いじょう)分娩(ぶんべん): abnormal delivery, complicated birth

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出産(しゅっさん): childbirth, giving birth — the standard general-purpose word
  • お産(さん): childbirth — casual, warm, and commonly used in everyday speech
  • 産(う)む: to give birth — the verb form
  • 陣痛(じんつう): labor pains, contractions — specifically the pain during labor
