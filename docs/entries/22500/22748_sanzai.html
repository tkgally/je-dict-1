1. scattered; dispersed; spread out here and there
The state of things being spread out over an area rather than concentrated in one place. Used for physical locations, settlements, objects, or abstract things like information.
村(むら)が山間部(さんかんぶ)に散在(さんざい)している。
Villages are scattered throughout the mountainous area.
資料(しりょう)があちこちに散在(さんざい)していて探(さが)しにくい。
The documents are scattered here and there, making them hard to find.
湖(みずうみ)の周(まわ)りには古(ふる)い遺跡(いせき)が散在(さんざい)している。
Ancient ruins are scattered around the lake.

Composed of 散(さん) ('scatter') and 在(ざい) ('exist, be located'). Commonly used with する as an intransitive verb (散在(さんざい)する). Frequently appears in geographical descriptions, academic writing, and when describing how things are distributed across an area. Not to be confused with the homophone 散財(さんざい) ('wasting money').

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 散在(さんざい)する: to be scattered, to be dispersed
  • 各地(かくち)に散在(さんざい)する: scattered in various places
  • 山間部(さんかんぶ)に散在(さんざい)する: scattered in mountainous areas

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 点在(てんざい): dotted, scattered — very close synonym; emphasizes being dotted like points on a map
  • 分散(ぶんさん): dispersal, distribution — emphasizes the process of spreading out rather than the resulting state
  • 散財(さんざい): spending lavishly, wasting money — a homophone with completely different meaning and kanji
