1. stationing of troops; garrison; military encampment
The act of stationing military forces at a particular location, or the state of troops being stationed there. Can also be used as a suru verb.
部隊(ぶたい)が駐屯(ちゅうとん)している。
Troops are stationed there.
駐屯地(ちゅうとんち)は市(し)の北部(ほくぶ)にある。
The garrison is in the northern part of the city.
自衛隊(じえいたい)は全国(ぜんこく)の駐屯地(ちゅうとんち)に部隊(ぶたい)を配置(はいち)している。
The Self-Defense Forces have units deployed at garrisons throughout the country.

Composed of 駐(ちゅう) (to station, park) + 屯(とん) (to camp, gather). Used primarily in military contexts for the permanent or semi-permanent stationing of troops.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駐屯地(ちゅうとんち): garrison; military base
  • 駐屯(ちゅうとん)する: to be stationed; to garrison
  • 駐屯(ちゅうとん)部隊(ぶたい): garrison troops
  • 海外(かいがい)駐屯(ちゅうとん): overseas stationing

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう): stationing (especially of foreign troops) — emphasizes staying in another country
  • 基地(きち): military base — the physical facility itself
  • 配備(はいび): deployment — placing equipment or forces in position
