オーブンレンジ

おーぶんれんじ
noun
combination microwave oven; microwave with oven function
1. combination microwave oven; microwave with oven function
A kitchen appliance that combines microwave and conventional oven functions in a single unit, widely used in Japanese households.
オーブンレンジでパンを焼(や)く。
To bake bread in the combination microwave oven.
新(あたら)しいオーブンレンジを買(か)った。
I bought a new combination microwave oven.
このオーブンレンジは温(あたた)めもグリルもできて便利(べんり)だ。
This combination microwave oven is convenient because it can both reheat and grill.

A wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) compound of "oven" + "range" (from "microwave range"). In Japan, standalone ovens are uncommon in home kitchens due to space constraints, so the オーブンレンジ serves as the standard oven for most households.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • オーブンレンジで焼(や)く: to bake/roast in a combination oven
  • オーブンレンジで温(あたた)める: to reheat in a microwave oven
  • オーブンレンジの買(か)い替(か)え: replacing one's microwave oven

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 電子(でんし)レンジ: microwave oven — has only microwave function, no oven capability
  • オーブントースター: toaster oven — smaller, simpler appliance for toasting and light baking
  • 炊飯器(すいはんき): rice cooker — another essential Japanese kitchen appliance
