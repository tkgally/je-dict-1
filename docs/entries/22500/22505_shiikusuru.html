1. to breed; to raise; to keep (animals)
To care for and raise animals, whether domesticated pets or livestock.
熱帯魚(ねったいぎょ)を飼育(しいく)している。
I keep tropical fish.
この動物園(どうぶつえん)では希少(きしょう)な動物(どうぶつ)を飼育(しいく)している。
This zoo keeps rare animals.
牛(うし)を飼育(しいく)するには広(ひろ)い土地(とち)と十分(じゅうぶん)な飼料(しりょう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Raising cattle requires ample land and sufficient feed.

A somewhat formal word for raising or keeping animals. It implies ongoing care and management, and is used more broadly than 飼(か)う, which is the everyday word for keeping a pet. 飼育(しいく)する is preferred in professional, institutional, and scientific contexts such as zoos, farms, and research facilities.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 動物(どうぶつ)を飼育(しいく)する: to raise animals
  • 家畜(かちく)を飼育(しいく)する: to raise livestock
  • 飼育(しいく)環境(かんきょう): breeding environment
  • 飼育(しいく)員(いん): animal caretaker, zookeeper
  • 飼育(しいく)方法(ほうほう): rearing method

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飼(か)う: to keep (a pet) — the everyday, casual word for having a pet at home
  • 養殖(ようしょく)する: to farm; to cultivate — used for aquaculture (fish, shellfish, seaweed)
  • 育(そだ)てる: to raise; to bring up — a general word that applies to children, plants, and animals alike
