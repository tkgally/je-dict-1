1. unstinting; generous; wholehearted; lavish
Given freely without holding back or begrudging the cost or effort.
惜(お)しみない拍手(はくしゅ)が送(おく)られた。
Generous applause was given.
彼女(かのじょ)は努力(どりょく)を惜(お)しみない人(ひと)だ。
She is a person who spares no effort.
チームの勝利(しょうり)に対(たい)して、ファンから惜(お)しみない賞賛(しょうさん)が寄(よ)せられた。
The team's victory drew wholehearted praise from the fans.

Formed from 惜(お)しむ (to begrudge, to be reluctant to part with) + ない (negation). Literally means "not begrudging" or "not holding back." Almost always used in positive contexts to praise generosity of praise, effort, or support. The adverbial form 惜(お)しみなく is also very common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 惜(お)しみない拍手(はくしゅ): generous applause
  • 惜(お)しみない賞賛(しょうさん): unstinting praise
  • 惜(お)しみない協力(きょうりょく): wholehearted cooperation
  • 努力(どりょく)を惜(お)しみない: to spare no effort
  • 惜(お)しみなく与(あた)える: to give generously

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 惜(お)しみなく: unstintingly — the adverbial form, used to modify verbs
  • 気前(きまえ)がいい: generous — describes a person's general disposition to give freely
  • 惜(お)しむ: to begrudge; to be reluctant — the base verb with opposite meaning
