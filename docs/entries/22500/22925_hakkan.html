1. launching a publication; publishing (a new periodical or series)
The act of starting to publish a new magazine, journal, newspaper, or book series. Focuses on the initial launch rather than ongoing publication.
新(あたら)しい雑誌(ざっし)を発刊(はっかん)する。
We launch a new magazine.
その学術(がくじゅつ)誌(し)は1950年(ねん)に発刊(はっかん)された。
That academic journal was first published in 1950.
地域(ちいき)の情報(じょうほう)を届(とど)けるフリーペーパーが来月(らいげつ)発刊(はっかん)される予定(よてい)だ。
A free paper delivering local information is scheduled to launch next month.

Composed of 発(はつ) (emit, launch) and 刊(かん) (publish, print). Specifically refers to the initial launch or founding of a publication such as a magazine, journal, or newspaper — not the ongoing act of publishing individual books. Can be used as a noun or with する as a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雑誌(ざっし)を発刊(はっかん)する: to launch a magazine
  • 発刊(はっかん)から〜年(ねん): since the first issue ~ years ago
  • 発刊(はっかん)記念(きねん): commemorating the launch
  • 発刊(はっかん)の辞(じ): foreword to the inaugural issue

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 出版(しゅっぱん): publishing — the general act of publishing books; broader and more common
  • 創刊(そうかん): first issue, inaugural publication — very close to 発刊(はっかん) but more specifically refers to the very first number of a periodical
  • 刊行(かんこう): publication — formal; can refer to publishing books or periodicals on an ongoing basis

NOTE ON HOMOPHONES:

  • 発汗(はっかん): sweating — same reading but completely different meaning
