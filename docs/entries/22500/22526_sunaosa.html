素直(すなお)さ

すなおさ
noun
honesty; straightforwardness; obedience; docility
1. honesty; straightforwardness; openness
The quality of being honest, genuine, and open in expressing feelings or accepting others' words.
彼女(かのじょ)の素直(すなお)さが好(す)きだ。
I like her straightforwardness.
素直(すなお)さを失(うしな)わないでほしい。
I hope you don't lose your honesty.
自分(じぶん)の気持(きも)ちに素直(すなお)さを持(も)つことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It's important to be honest with your own feelings.
2. obedience; docility; compliance
The quality of being compliant and receptive, doing as told without resistance.
子(こ)どもの素直(すなお)さに助(たす)けられた。
I was helped by the child's obedience.
素直(すなお)さがあれば上達(じょうたつ)も早(はや)い。
If you have an open and receptive attitude, you improve quickly.
先輩(せんぱい)の助言(じょげん)を受(う)け入(い)れる素直(すなお)さがなければ、成長(せいちょう)は難(むずか)しい。
Without the openness to accept advice from seniors, it's hard to grow.

The noun form of the na-adjective 素直(すなお), formed by adding さ. In Japanese culture, 素直(すなお)さ is generally seen as a very positive trait — it implies a person who is genuine, open to feedback, and not stubborn or resistant. It is especially valued in workplace and educational settings.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 素直(すなお)さがある: to have straightforwardness/openness
  • 素直(すなお)さを大切(たいせつ)にする: to value openness
  • 素直(すなお)さが足(た)りない: to lack openness
  • 子(こ)どもらしい素直(すなお)さ: childlike innocence/honesty

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 正直(しょうじき)さ: honesty — focuses on truthfulness and not lying, rather than openness of character
  • 従順(じゅうじゅん)さ: submissiveness; obedience — stronger emphasis on following orders, with a more passive connotation
  • 純粋(じゅんすい)さ: purity; innocence — emphasizes being untainted or uncorrupted
