短(みじか)さ

みじかさ
noun
shortness; brevity
1. shortness; brevity
The quality or degree of being short, whether in physical length, duration of time, or extent.
冬(ふゆ)の日(ひ)の短(みじか)さに驚(おどろ)いた。
I was surprised by the shortness of winter days.
人生(じんせい)の短(みじか)さを感(かん)じる。
I feel the brevity of life.
この映画(えいが)は短(みじか)さが魅力(みりょく)で、忙(いそが)しい人(ひと)にもおすすめだ。
The appeal of this movie is its brevity, so I recommend it even for busy people.

Formed by adding the suffix 〜さ to the adjective 短(みじか)い (short), converting it into a noun expressing the degree or quality of shortness. The 〜さ suffix is productive and can be applied to most i-adjectives (e.g., 長(なが)さ "length," 高(たか)さ "height").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人生(じんせい)の短(みじか)さ: the brevity of life
  • 日(ひ)の短(みじか)さ: the shortness of days
  • 時間(じかん)の短(みじか)さ: the shortness of time
  • 短(みじか)さが魅力(みりょく): brevity is its appeal

GRAMMAR NOTE:
The 〜さ suffix turns i-adjectives into nouns expressing degree or extent. It attaches to the stem (without the final い): 短(みじか)い → 短(みじか)さ, 長(なが)い → 長(なが)さ, 高(たか)い → 高(たか)さ.
