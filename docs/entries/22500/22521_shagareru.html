1. to become hoarse; to become raspy
For one's voice to become rough, harsh, or gravelly.
声(こえ)がしゃがれている。
My voice is hoarse.
風邪(かぜ)で声(こえ)がしゃがれてしまった。
My voice became hoarse from a cold.
長時間(ちょうじかん)叫(さけ)び続(つづ)けたせいで声(こえ)がしゃがれて、ほとんど出(で)なくなった。
From shouting for a long time, my voice became so hoarse that it could barely come out.

Describes the quality of a voice that has become rough or gravelly. Most commonly appears in the た-form as しゃがれた声(こえ) (a hoarse voice). Can describe a temporary condition (from a cold, shouting, etc.) or a permanent vocal quality (an older person's gravelly voice). Sometimes written in katakana: シャガレる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しゃがれた声(こえ): a hoarse/raspy voice
  • しゃがれ声(ごえ): hoarse voice (compound form)
  • 声(こえ)がしゃがれる: one's voice becomes hoarse
  • 酒焼(さけや)けでしゃがれた声(こえ): voice hoarse from heavy drinking

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 嗄(か)れる: to become hoarse — more formal/written; specifically about voice loss
  • 枯(か)れる: to dry up; to wither — when used for voice (声(こえ)が枯(か)れる), means the voice has dried out from overuse
  • ハスキーな: husky — a loanword with a more positive, attractive connotation
