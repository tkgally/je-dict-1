事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)

じぎょうけいかく
noun
business plan; project plan
1. business plan; project plan
A formal document or strategy outlining the objectives, methods, timeline, and financial projections for a business venture or project. Used in corporate, governmental, and nonprofit contexts.
事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)を策定(さくてい)する。
We formulate a business plan.
銀行(ぎんこう)に事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)を提出(ていしゅつ)した。
I submitted the business plan to the bank.
新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)を始(はじ)めるには、しっかりとした事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To start a new business, you need a solid business plan.

Composed of 事業(じぎょう) (business, project, enterprise) and 計画(けいかく) (plan). A fundamental term in business and government contexts. Often required when seeking bank loans, applying for grants, or proposing new projects within organizations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)を策定(さくてい)する: to formulate a business plan
  • 事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit a business plan
  • 事業計画(じぎょうけいかく)書(しょ): business plan document
  • 中期(ちゅうき)事業計画(じぎょうけいかく): medium-term business plan

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ビジネスプラン: business plan — the English loanword, common in startup culture
  • 経営(けいえい)計画(けいかく): management plan — broader, covering overall company strategy
  • 企画(きかく)書(しょ): project proposal — a document proposing a specific project or idea, less comprehensive than a full business plan
