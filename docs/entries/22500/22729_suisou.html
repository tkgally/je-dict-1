1. playing wind instruments; wind instrument performance
The act of playing wind instruments by blowing into them. Most commonly encountered in the compound 吹奏楽(すいそうがく) (concert band, wind ensemble music).
トランペットを吹奏(すいそう)する。
To play the trumpet.
吹奏楽部(すいそうがくぶ)に入(はい)った。
I joined the concert band club.
学校(がっこう)の文化祭(ぶんかさい)で吹奏楽(すいそうがく)の演奏(えんそう)が行(おこな)われた。
A concert band performance took place at the school cultural festival.

A compound of 吹(すい) (to blow) + 奏(そう) (to play music). Most commonly appears in the compound 吹奏楽(すいそうがく) (wind ensemble music / concert band). School concert bands (吹奏楽部(すいそうがくぶ), often abbreviated as 吹部(すいぶ)) are one of the most popular extracurricular activities in Japanese middle and high schools.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 吹奏楽(すいそうがく): concert band, wind ensemble music
  • 吹奏楽部(すいそうがくぶ): concert band club
  • 吹奏楽(すいそうがく)コンクール: concert band competition
  • 吹奏(すいそう)する: to play (a wind instrument)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 演奏(えんそう): musical performance — general term for playing any instrument
  • 管楽器(かんがっき): wind instrument — the instruments themselves
  • ブラスバンド: brass band — sometimes used interchangeably with 吹奏楽(すいそうがく), though technically refers only to brass instruments
