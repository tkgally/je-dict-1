単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)

たんじゅんさぎょう
noun
simple repetitive work; routine task; menial work
1. simple repetitive work; routine task; menial work
Work requiring little thought or skill that involves repeating the same actions.
単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)が続(つづ)くと飽(あ)きる。
I get bored when simple repetitive work goes on and on.
この仕事(しごと)は単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)が多(おお)い。
This job involves a lot of routine tasks.
単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)はAIやロボットに置(お)き換(か)えられるだろう。
Simple repetitive tasks will likely be replaced by AI and robots.

Work that requires little thought or skill and involves repeating the same actions. A compound of 単純(たんじゅん) (simple) and 作業(さぎょう) (work/task). Often discussed in the context of automation, job satisfaction, or labor markets.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)をこなす: to carry out routine tasks
  • 単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)の繰(く)り返(かえ)し: repeating simple tasks
  • 単純作業(たんじゅんさぎょう)を自動化(じどうか)する: to automate simple tasks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 肉体労働(にくたいろうどう): manual labor — emphasizes physical effort
  • 事務作業(じむさぎょう): clerical work — desk-based tasks
  • 雑務(ざつむ): miscellaneous duties — odd jobs, not necessarily repetitive
