私(わたくし)ども

わたくしども
pronoun
we; us (very formal, humble)
1. we; us (very formal, humble)
A highly formal and humble first-person plural pronoun used in business and service contexts.
私(わたくし)どもにお任(まか)せください。
Please leave it to us.
私(わたくし)どもの商品(しょうひん)をお選(えら)びいただき、ありがとうございます。
Thank you for choosing our products.
私(わたくし)どもは全力(ぜんりょく)でお客様(きゃくさま)のご要望(ようぼう)にお応(こた)えしてまいります。
We will do our utmost to meet our customers' needs.

A very formal humble pronoun formed from 私(わたくし) (I, formal) + ども (humble plural suffix). Used primarily in business, hospitality, and customer service to represent one's organization or group in a humble manner. The ども suffix is inherently humble, lowering the speaker's group relative to the listener.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 私(わたくし)どもの会社(かいしゃ): our company (humble)
  • 私(わたくし)どもの店(みせ): our shop (humble)
  • 私(わたくし)どもといたしましては: as far as we are concerned (very formal)

USAGE:
Primarily used in business correspondence, formal speeches, and customer-facing situations. Hotel staff, airline attendants, and salespeople frequently use this pronoun. It would sound overly stiff in casual conversation.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 私(わたし)たち: we — standard and gender-neutral; appropriate for most situations
  • 我々(われわれ): we — formal but not humble; used in speeches and announcements
  • 僕(ぼく)ら: we — informal and masculine
  • 弊社(へいしゃ): our company (humble) — specifically refers to one's company rather than a group of people
