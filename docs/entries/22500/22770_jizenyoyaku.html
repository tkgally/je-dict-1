1. advance reservation; advance booking; prior reservation
A reservation or booking made ahead of time, as opposed to a walk-in or same-day arrangement. Emphasizes that the booking is made before the event or visit.
事前予約(じぜんよやく)が必要(ひつよう)です。
An advance reservation is required.
事前予約(じぜんよやく)すれば割引(わりびき)が受(う)けられる。
If you book in advance, you can get a discount.
この展覧会(てんらんかい)は人気(にんき)が高(たか)いので、事前予約(じぜんよやく)をおすすめします。
This exhibition is very popular, so we recommend making an advance reservation.
事前予約(じぜんよやく)なしでも入場(にゅうじょう)できますが、待(ま)ち時間(じかん)が長(なが)くなる場合(ばあい)があります。
You can enter without an advance reservation, but the wait time may be longer.

Composed of 事前(じぜん) ('in advance, beforehand') and 予約(よやく) ('reservation, booking'). While 予約(よやく) by itself can imply advance booking, 事前予約(じぜんよやく) explicitly emphasizes the advance nature and is commonly used in signs, websites, and announcements.

Very commonly seen in tourism, restaurants, museums, and medical facilities. Many popular spots in Japan now require 事前予約(じぜんよやく), especially after the shift toward reservation-based systems.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事前予約(じぜんよやく)制(せい): advance reservation system
  • 事前予約(じぜんよやく)優先(ゆうせん): priority for advance bookings
  • 事前予約(じぜんよやく)限定(げんてい): limited to advance bookings
  • 事前予約(じぜんよやく)なし: without advance reservation
  • ウェブで事前予約(じぜんよやく)する: to book in advance online

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予約(よやく): reservation — the basic term; does not emphasize timing
  • 当日予約(とうじつよやく): same-day reservation — the opposite: booking on the day itself
  • 前売(まえう)り: advance sale (of tickets) — specifically about purchasing tickets before the event
