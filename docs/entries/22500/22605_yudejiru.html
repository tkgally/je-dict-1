茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)

ゆでじる
noun
cooking liquid; water used for boiling
1. cooking liquid; boiling water; water left after boiling food
The water used to boil ingredients, or the liquid that remains after boiling, often retaining flavor and nutrients from the food.
パスタの茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)を少(すこ)し取(と)っておく。
Set aside a little of the pasta cooking water.
茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)をソースに加(くわ)えると味(あじ)がまとまる。
Adding the cooking water to the sauce brings the flavors together.
そばの茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)は「そば湯(ゆ)」として飲(の)む習慣(しゅうかん)がある。
There is a custom of drinking the soba cooking water as 'soba-yu.'

A compound of 茹(ゆ)でる (to boil) and 汁(じる) (liquid, broth). In Japanese cooking, the water used to boil pasta, noodles, or vegetables is often reused — pasta cooking water is added to sauces for body, and soba cooking water (called そば湯(ゆ)) is traditionally served to drink after the meal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • パスタの茹(ゆ)で汁(じる): pasta cooking water
  • そばの茹(ゆ)で汁(じる): soba cooking water
  • 茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)を捨(す)てる: to discard the cooking water
  • 茹(ゆ)で汁(じる)を取(と)っておく: to save the cooking water

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japanese cuisine, 茹(ゆ)で汁(じる) is not simply waste water. The starch-rich liquid from boiling noodles is valued for both culinary and cultural reasons, particularly the tradition of drinking そば湯(ゆ) at soba restaurants.
