加入者(かにゅうしゃ)

かにゅうしゃ
noun
subscriber; member; enrollee
1. subscriber; member; enrollee; participant
A person who has joined or subscribed to a service, organization, insurance plan, or similar group. Used widely in insurance, telecommunications, and membership contexts.
保険(ほけん)の加入者(かにゅうしゃ)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of insurance subscribers is increasing.
加入者(かにゅうしゃ)全員(ぜんいん)にお知(し)らせを送(おく)ります。
We will send a notice to all subscribers.
年金(ねんきん)の加入者(かにゅうしゃ)数(すう)は、少子化(しょうしか)の影響(えいきょう)で減少(げんしょう)傾向(けいこう)にある。
The number of pension enrollees is on a declining trend due to the falling birthrate.

A formal term commonly used in administrative, insurance, and telecommunications contexts. Refers to anyone who has joined or enrolled in a system or service.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保険(ほけん)加入者(かにゅうしゃ): insurance subscriber
  • 年金(ねんきん)加入者(かにゅうしゃ): pension enrollee
  • 加入者(かにゅうしゃ)数(すう): number of subscribers
  • 加入者(かにゅうしゃ)負担(ふたん): subscriber's share/burden

RELATED TERMS:

  • 加入(かにゅう): joining, subscribing, enrollment
  • 加入(かにゅう)する: to join, to subscribe
  • 会員(かいいん): member — more general term for membership in clubs or organizations
  • 契約者(けいやくしゃ): contractor, policyholder — person who signs a contract

USAGE:
While 会員(かいいん) is used for voluntary memberships like clubs, 加入者(かにゅうしゃ) is more common for insurance, pension, and utility service contexts.
