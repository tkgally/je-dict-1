1. plan; scheme; attempt; design
A deliberate plan or attempt to accomplish something, often implying calculated intention. Used in formal contexts and frequently carries a negative nuance when describing someone else's plans.
クーデターを企図(きと)する。
To plan a coup d'état.
犯人(はんにん)は逃亡(とうぼう)を企図(きと)していたようだ。
It seems the criminal was planning to escape.
この政策(せいさく)は経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)と環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)の両立(りょうりつ)を企図(きと)したものだ。
This policy was designed to achieve both economic growth and environmental protection.

A compound of 企(き) (plan/scheme) and 図(と) (diagram/plan). A formal, somewhat literary word for planning or scheming. Often appears in news reporting, legal contexts, and academic writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 企図(きと)する: to plan, to attempt
  • 犯行(はんこう)を企図(きと)する: to plan a crime
  • 自殺(じさつ)を企図(きと)する: to attempt suicide (medical/clinical term)
  • 改革(かいかく)を企図(きと)する: to design a reform

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 計画(けいかく): plan — neutral and everyday; lacks the calculated/scheming nuance
  • 策謀(さくぼう): plot, intrigue — more strongly negative, implies deception
  • 企(たくら)む: to scheme — a verb with similar meaning but more colloquial and always negative
  • 意図(いと): intention — focuses on the intent itself rather than the planning action
