1. to be exceptional; to be extraordinary; to be outstanding
To deviate from the ordinary level or standard, being far above average. Almost always used in the past participle form 並外れた as a modifier meaning 'extraordinary' or 'exceptional.'
並外(なみはず)れた才能(さいのう)。
Extraordinary talent.
彼女(かのじょ)は並外(なみはず)れた体力(たいりょく)の持(も)ち主(ぬし)だ。
She has exceptional physical strength.
並外(なみはず)れた努力(どりょく)をしなければ、あの大学(だいがく)には合格(ごうかく)できない。
You can't pass that university's entrance exam without making an extraordinary effort.

Composed of 並(なみ) (ordinary, average) and 外(はず)れる (to deviate from, to miss). Literally means "to deviate from the ordinary." Almost exclusively used in the prenominal form 並外(なみはず)れた to modify nouns.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 並外(なみはず)れた才能(さいのう): extraordinary talent
  • 並外(なみはず)れた体力(たいりょく): exceptional stamina
  • 並外(なみはず)れた努力(どりょく): extraordinary effort
  • 並外(なみはず)れた集中力(しゅうちゅうりょく): exceptional concentration

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 桁外(けたはず)れ: off the charts; extraordinary — even more extreme, emphasizes being in a completely different league
  • 非凡(ひぼん): uncommon; extraordinary — more formal and literary
  • 卓越(たくえつ)した: outstanding; preeminent — formal, often used for skills or achievements
  • 抜群(ばつぐん): outstanding; preeminent — commonly used, slightly more casual

USAGE:
The form 並外(なみはず)れた (past participle used as adjective) is far more common than the dictionary form. The related noun 並外(なみはず)れ also exists but is less frequent.
