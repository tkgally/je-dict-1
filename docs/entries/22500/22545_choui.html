弔意(ちょうい)

ちょうい
noun
condolence; sympathy for the bereaved
1. condolence; sympathy for the deceased and bereaved
Feelings of sorrow and sympathy expressed toward the bereaved upon someone's death. A formal term used in the context of funerals, memorial services, and official statements.
弔意(ちょうい)を表(あらわ)す。
To express one's condolences.
首相(しゅしょう)が弔意(ちょうい)を示(しめ)した。
The prime minister expressed condolences.
遺族(いぞく)に対(たい)して深(ふか)い弔意(ちょうい)を表(あらわ)すとともに、ご冥福(めいふく)をお祈(いの)りいたします。
I express deep condolences to the bereaved family and pray for the repose of the departed.

Composed of 弔(ちょう) (mourn, condole) + 意(い) (feeling, intention). A formal word typically found in official statements, news reports, and formal condolence messages. In everyday speech, people more often say お悔(く)やみ.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弔意(ちょうい)を表(あらわ)す: to express condolences
  • 弔意(ちょうい)を示(しめ)す: to show condolences
  • 深(ふか)い弔意(ちょうい): deep condolences
  • 弔意(ちょうい)を込(こ)めて: with condolences

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • お悔(く)やみ: condolences — the everyday, polite expression
  • 哀悼(あいとう): mourning, grief — a formal synonym often used in the expression 哀悼(あいとう)の意(い)
  • 弔辞(ちょうじ): funeral address — a speech given at a funeral
  • 追悼(ついとう): memorial, commemoration — honoring and remembering the dead
