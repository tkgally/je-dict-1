1. to be concerned about; to worry about; to fear
To feel anxiety or apprehension about something that may happen, especially regarding serious or public matters.
安全性(あんぜんせい)を懸念(けねん)する声(こえ)がある。
There are voices expressing concern about safety.
専門家(せんもんか)は経済(けいざい)への影響(えいきょう)を懸念(けねん)している。
Experts are concerned about the impact on the economy.
住民(じゅうみん)たちは工場(こうじょう)の建設(けんせつ)による環境(かんきょう)汚染(おせん)を懸念(けねん)して反対(はんたい)運動(うんどう)を始(はじ)めた。
Residents started a protest movement out of concern about environmental pollution from the factory construction.

A formal word for worry or concern, used primarily in written language, news reports, and official statements. It implies concern about something serious or consequential, not everyday personal worries. The noun form 懸念(けねん) is very common on its own.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 懸念(けねん)を示(しめ)す: to express concern
  • 懸念(けねん)を抱(いだ)く: to harbor concerns
  • 懸念(けねん)材料(ざいりょう): cause for concern
  • 懸念(けねん)事項(じこう): matter of concern
  • 懸念(けねん)が広(ひろ)がる: concerns spread

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 心配(しんぱい)する: to worry — the everyday, general-purpose word for worrying, used for both personal and public matters
  • 憂慮(ゆうりょ)する: to be gravely concerned — even more formal and literary than 懸念(けねん)する, often used in diplomatic or academic contexts
  • 危惧(きぐ)する: to fear; to be apprehensive — emphasizes fear that something bad will happen, often used about endangered species or serious risks
