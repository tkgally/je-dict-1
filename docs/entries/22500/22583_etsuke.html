1. painting designs on ceramics or pottery; decorative painting
The craft of applying decorative patterns or pictures onto pottery, porcelain, or other ceramics, either before or after glazing.
絵付(えつ)けを体験(たいけん)する。
To try one's hand at ceramic painting.
この皿(さら)は手描(てが)きの絵付(えつ)けが美(うつく)しい。
This plate has beautiful hand-painted designs.
窯元(かまもと)で絵付(えつ)け体験(たいけん)ができるツアーに参加(さんか)した。
I joined a tour where you can try ceramic painting at a kiln workshop.

A compound of 絵(え) (picture, painting) and 付(つ)け (applying, attaching). Refers to the craft of painting decorative patterns or pictures onto pottery, porcelain, or other ceramics before or after glazing. A key step in traditional Japanese pottery production. Often offered as an activity at pottery studios and tourist destinations.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 絵付(えつ)け体験(たいけん): ceramic painting experience
  • 絵付(えつ)け師(し): ceramic painter (artisan)
  • 手描(てが)きの絵付(えつ)け: hand-painted decoration
  • 下絵付(したえつ)け: underglaze painting (applied before glazing)
  • 上絵付(うわえつ)け: overglaze painting (applied after initial firing)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 焼(や)き物(もの): pottery, ceramics — the broader field
  • 釉薬(ゆうやく): glaze — the coating applied before firing
  • 陶芸(とうげい): ceramic art, pottery making — the art form as a whole
