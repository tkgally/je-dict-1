1. to relate; to associate; to connect (one thing with another)
To draw or establish a logical connection between two or more things, especially when analyzing, explaining, or thinking critically about how they are related.
この事件(じけん)を過去(かこ)の事例(じれい)と関連(かんれん)づけた。
I connected this incident with past cases.
理論(りろん)と実践(じっせん)を関連(かんれん)づけて考(かんが)える。
Think by connecting theory and practice.
歴史的(れきしてき)な背景(はいけい)と関連(かんれん)づけて説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain it in relation to its historical background.

Formed from 関連(かんれん) (relation, connection) and the verb-forming suffix づける. Used when drawing connections or establishing relationships between separate things, especially in analytical, academic, or explanatory contexts.

USAGE:
The pattern is: AをBと関連(かんれん)づける (to connect A with B). Can also use に instead of と.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜と関連(かんれん)づける: to relate to ~
  • 〜に関連(かんれん)づけて考(かんが)える: to think in connection with ~
  • 知識(ちしき)を関連(かんれん)づける: to connect pieces of knowledge
  • 原因(げんいん)と関連(かんれん)づける: to connect with a cause

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 結(むす)びつける: to tie together, to link — more concrete, emphasizes binding
  • 関連(かんれん)する: to be related — intransitive, describes an existing connection
  • 関係(かんけい)づける: to relate — very similar, sometimes interchangeable
