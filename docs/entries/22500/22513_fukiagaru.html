1. to spout up; to gush forth; to erupt upward
For liquid, gas, or other material to shoot or surge upward with force.
間欠泉(かんけつせん)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がった。
The geyser spouted up.
水道管(すいどうかん)が破裂(はれつ)して水(みず)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がった。
A water pipe burst and water gushed upward.
火山(かざん)から溶岩(ようがん)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がる光景(こうけい)は圧巻(あっかん)だった。
The sight of lava erupting upward from the volcano was breathtaking.
2. to boil over; to well up (emotions)
For emotions such as laughter or anger to surge uncontrollably.
笑(わら)いが噴(ふ)き上(あ)がった。
Laughter burst forth.
怒(いか)りが噴(ふ)き上(あ)がるのを抑(おさ)えた。
I held back the anger that was welling up inside me.
観客(かんきゃく)から歓声(かんせい)が一斉(いっせい)に噴(ふ)き上(あ)がり、会場(かいじょう)が揺(ゆ)れた。
Cheers erupted from the audience all at once, shaking the venue.

A vivid, expressive verb that conveys forceful upward motion. The physical sense (sense 1) is used for geysers, volcanic eruptions, broken pipes, and similar scenarios. The figurative sense (sense 2) describes emotions or sounds bursting out uncontrollably, as if erupting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水(みず)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がる: water gushes up
  • 蒸気(じょうき)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がる: steam shoots up
  • 歓声(かんせい)が噴(ふ)き上(あ)がる: cheers erupt
  • 怒(いか)りが噴(ふ)き上(あ)がる: anger wells up

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 吹(ふ)き出(だ)す: to spurt out; to burst out laughing — emphasizes coming outward rather than upward
  • 噴出(ふんしゅつ)する: to gush out; to erupt — a more formal, scientific term
  • 湧(わ)き上(あ)がる: to well up; to spring forth — a gentler upwelling, often used for feelings of emotion
