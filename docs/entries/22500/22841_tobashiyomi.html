1. skimming; skipping through (while reading)
reading something while skipping over parts, either to save time or because of lack of interest in certain sections
飛(と)ばし読(よ)みをする。
To skim through something.
時間(じかん)がなかったので飛(と)ばし読(よ)みした。
I skimmed through it because I didn't have time.
長(なが)い小説(しょうせつ)だったので、途中(とちゅう)から飛(と)ばし読(よ)みしてしまった。
It was a long novel, so I ended up skimming through it from partway.

A compound of 飛(と)ばす (to skip) and 読(よ)み (reading). Describes the act of reading while intentionally skipping sections. Can be used as a noun or with する as a verb.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 飛(と)ばし読(よ)みする: to skim, to skip-read
  • 飛(と)ばし読(よ)みで済(す)ませる: to get by with skimming

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 斜(なな)め読(よ)み: skimming — literally "diagonal reading," a similar concept but implies scanning quickly rather than skipping sections
  • 速読(そくどく): speed reading — a systematic technique for reading quickly, not skipping content
  • 拾(ひろ)い読(よ)み: selective reading — picking out specific parts to read

USAGE:
Often carries a slightly negative connotation, implying that the reader did not give the text proper attention. In contrast, 速読(そくどく) is seen as a positive skill.
