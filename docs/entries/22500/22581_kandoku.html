1. reading through completely; finishing reading a book or text
The act of reading a book or text all the way through to the end, often implying a sense of accomplishment, especially with lengthy or challenging material.
やっと完読(かんどく)した。
I finally finished reading it.
難(むずか)しい本(ほん)だったが完読(かんどく)できた。
It was a difficult book, but I managed to read it through.
今年(ことし)の目標(もくひょう)は月(つき)に二冊(にさつ)完読(かんどく)することだ。
My goal this year is to finish two books a month.

A compound of 完(かん) (complete) and 読(どく) (reading). Emphasizes the act of reading something all the way through to the end, especially when the text is long or challenging. Often carries a sense of accomplishment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 完読(かんどく)する: to read through completely
  • 完読(かんどく)できる: to be able to finish reading
  • 一気(いっき)に完読(かんどく)する: to read through in one sitting
  • 完読(かんどく)率(りつ): completion rate (for reading)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 読了(どくりょう): finishing a book — very similar in meaning; slightly more formal
  • 読破(どくは): reading through (a difficult or long work) — implies overcoming a challenge
  • 通読(つうどく): reading through from start to finish — neutral; doesn't imply difficulty
