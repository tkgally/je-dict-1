ゴールデンウィーク

ごーるでんうぃーく
noun
Golden Week; late-April to early-May holiday period
1. Golden Week; late-April to early-May holiday period
A cluster of national holidays from late April to early May, typically spanning April 29 through May 5. One of Japan's three major vacation periods alongside New Year and Obon.
ゴールデンウィークは何日(なんにち)ある？
How many days is Golden Week?
ゴールデンウィークに沖縄(おきなわ)へ旅行(りょこう)する予定(よてい)だ。
I'm planning to travel to Okinawa during Golden Week.
ゴールデンウィークは高速道路(こうそくどうろ)が毎年(まいとし)大渋滞(だいじゅうたい)になる。
The highways get massively congested every year during Golden Week.
今年(ことし)のゴールデンウィークは最大(さいだい)で十連休(じゅうれんきゅう)になる人(ひと)もいる。
Some people will get up to ten consecutive days off this Golden Week.

A Japanese-English hybrid term (和製英語(わせいえいご)) referring to the cluster of national holidays from late April to early May. The name was coined by the film industry in the 1950s to promote cinema attendance during this period. Often abbreviated as GW in writing.

The holidays included in Golden Week are:

  • April 29: 昭和(しょうわ)の日(ひ) (Showa Day)
  • May 3: 憲法記念日(けんぽうきねんび) (Constitution Memorial Day)
  • May 4: みどりの日(ひ) (Greenery Day)
  • May 5: 子(こ)どもの日(ひ) (Children's Day)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ゴールデンウィーク中(ちゅう): during Golden Week
  • ゴールデンウィーク明(あ)け: after Golden Week ends
  • ゴールデンウィーク前(まえ): before Golden Week
  • 大型(おおがた)ゴールデンウィーク: an extra-long Golden Week

CULTURAL NOTES:
Golden Week is one of Japan's busiest travel periods. Trains, flights, and hotels are heavily booked, and tourist spots are extremely crowded. Many companies close for the entire period, making it a rare opportunity for extended rest in Japanese work culture. NHK and some formal media use the term 大型連休(おおがたれんきゅう) ('extended consecutive holiday') instead, as ゴールデンウィーク originated as a commercial term.
