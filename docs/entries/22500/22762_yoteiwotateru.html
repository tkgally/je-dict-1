予定(よてい)を立(た)てる

よていをたてる
expression (ichidan verb)
to make plans; to schedule
1. to make plans; to set up a schedule; to plan ahead
To create or arrange a plan or schedule for future activities. The verb 立(た)てる here means to set up or establish.
旅行(りょこう)の予定(よてい)を立(た)てた。
I made plans for a trip.
来週(らいしゅう)の予定(よてい)を立(た)てておこう。
Let's go ahead and make plans for next week.
早(はや)めに予定(よてい)を立(た)てないと、ホテルが取(と)れなくなる。
If we don't plan ahead early, we won't be able to get a hotel.
仕事(しごと)が忙(いそが)しくて、なかなか予定(よてい)を立(た)てられない。
I'm so busy with work that I can hardly make any plans.

A common collocation pairing 予定(よてい) ('plan, schedule') with 立(た)てる ('to set up, establish'). This is the standard way to express making plans or setting a schedule in Japanese. The verb 立(た)てる is ichidan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 旅行(りょこう)の予定(よてい)を立(た)てる: to plan a trip
  • 勉強(べんきょう)の予定(よてい)を立(た)てる: to make a study plan
  • 年間(ねんかん)の予定(よてい)を立(た)てる: to set up a yearly schedule
  • 無理(むり)のない予定(よてい)を立(た)てる: to make a reasonable plan

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる: to make a plan — more formal and often used for larger, more structured plans
  • 日程(にってい)を決(き)める: to set a schedule — focuses on fixing dates
  • 段取(だんど)りをつける: to make arrangements — emphasizes practical preparation steps

USAGE:
In everyday conversation, 予定(よてい)を立(た)てる is the most natural way to talk about planning. It can refer to anything from casual weekend plans to structured work schedules.
