1. inducement; attraction; luring; drawing in
The act of attracting or drawing something or someone toward a particular place or action. Used in both human contexts (luring, enticing) and scientific contexts (attracting insects, etc.).
害虫(がいちゅう)の誘引(ゆういん)トラップを仕掛(しか)けた。
I set up a trap to lure harmful insects.
甘(あま)い香(かお)りがハチを誘引(ゆういん)する。
The sweet fragrance attracts bees.
光(ひかり)を使(つか)って蛾(が)を誘引(ゆういん)し、個体数(こたいすう)を調査(ちょうさ)する方法(ほうほう)がある。
There is a method of using light to attract moths and survey their population.

Composed of 誘(ゆう) (entice, lure) + 引(いん) (pull, draw). Often used as a noun-verb with する. Common in scientific, agricultural, and pest control contexts, where it describes attracting insects or animals using bait, light, or scent. Can also be used more broadly for enticing people.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 誘引(ゆういん)する: to attract, to lure
  • 誘引(ゆういん)トラップ: attraction trap, lure trap
  • 誘引(ゆういん)剤(ざい): attractant (chemical)
  • 誘引(ゆういん)効果(こうか): attractive effect

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 誘惑(ゆうわく): temptation — luring someone into something undesirable or morally questionable
  • 誘導(ゆうどう): guidance, leading — directing someone to a destination or conclusion
  • 引(ひ)き寄(よ)せる: to draw toward, to attract — a more everyday verb for attraction
