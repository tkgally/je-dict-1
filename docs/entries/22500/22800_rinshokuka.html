吝嗇家(りんしょくか)

りんしょくか
noun
miser; skinflint; stingy person
1. miser; skinflint; stingy person
A person who is extremely reluctant to spend money.
あの人(ひと)は有名(ゆうめい)な吝嗇家(りんしょくか)だ。
That person is a well-known miser.
吝嗇家(りんしょくか)の彼(かれ)が寄付(きふ)するなんて珍(めずら)しい。
It's unusual for a miser like him to make a donation.
世界的(せかいてき)な大金持(おおがねも)ちなのに吝嗇家(りんしょくか)として知(し)られていた。
Despite being a global billionaire, he was known as a miser.

A literary or formal word for a miser. Composed of 吝嗇(りんしょく) (miserliness, stinginess) + 家(か) (person who is characterized by). The kanji 吝(りん) and 嗇(しょく) both carry meanings related to stinginess. While understood by most Japanese adults, it has a literary or slightly old-fashioned feel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大(おお)吝嗇家(りんしょくか): a great miser
  • 吝嗇家(りんしょくか)で有名(ゆうめい): famous for being stingy

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • けち: stingy, cheap — the most common colloquial word for stinginess
  • けちん坊(ぼう): cheapskate — informal, slightly playful
  • 守銭奴(しゅせんど): miser (literally "slave to money") — literary and strongly negative
  • 倹約家(けんやくか): frugal person — positive connotation, implying wise money management

REGISTER:
吝嗇家(りんしょくか) is literary and somewhat formal. In everyday speech, けち or けちな人(ひと) is far more common.
