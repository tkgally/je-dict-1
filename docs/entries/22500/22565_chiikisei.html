地域性(ちいきせい)

ちいきせい
noun
regional characteristics; local character
1. regional characteristics; local character; regional nature
The distinctive features, customs, or tendencies associated with a particular geographic area, such as local cuisine, dialect, or cultural practices.
地域性(ちいきせい)を活(い)かす。
To make use of regional characteristics.
料理(りょうり)には地域性(ちいきせい)が強(つよ)く現(あらわ)れる。
Regional character is strongly reflected in cuisine.
この病気(びょうき)の発生(はっせい)には地域性(ちいきせい)があり、特定(とくてい)の地方(ちほう)に集中(しゅうちゅう)している。
The occurrence of this disease has regional characteristics and is concentrated in specific areas.

A compound of 地域(ちいき) (region, area) and the suffix 性(せい) (-ness, quality). Refers to the distinctive features, customs, or tendencies associated with a particular region. Commonly used in discussions of culture, food, language, weather patterns, and demographic trends.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地域性(ちいきせい)を活(い)かす: to leverage regional characteristics
  • 地域性(ちいきせい)が強(つよ)い: to have strong regional character
  • 地域性(ちいきせい)がある: to have regional characteristics
  • 地域性(ちいきせい)を反映(はんえい)する: to reflect regional character

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 地方色(ちほうしょく): local color — emphasizes the distinctive cultural flavor of a place
  • 風土(ふうど): natural features and climate of a region — broader concept encompassing geography, climate, and culture
  • 郷土性(きょうどせい): homeland character — more emotional; connected to attachment to one's native area
