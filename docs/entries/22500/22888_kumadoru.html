1. to paint lines on the face in kabuki style; to apply kumadori makeup
To apply the distinctive bold face-painting used in kabuki theater, with colored lines that follow the contours of the face.
役者(やくしゃ)が顔(かお)を隈取(くまど)る。
The actor paints his face with kumadori.
赤(あか)と白(しろ)で隈取(くまど)られた顔(かお)は正義(せいぎ)の役(やく)を表(あらわ)す。
A face painted in red and white kumadori represents a heroic role.
子供(こども)たちが歌舞伎(かぶき)ごっこで顔(かお)を隈取(くまど)っている。
The children are painting their faces with kumadori while playing kabuki.
2. to shade; to tinge; to color the edges or surroundings
To add color or shadow around the edges of something, often used poetically for natural scenes like autumn leaves or sunset colors.
紅葉(もみじ)が山(やま)を隈取(くまど)っている。
Autumn leaves are coloring the mountain.
夕焼(ゆうや)けに隈取(くまど)られた雲(くも)が美(うつく)しい。
The clouds tinged by the sunset are beautiful.
涙(なみだ)で目元(めもと)が赤(あか)く隈取(くまど)られていた。
Her eyes were rimmed red from tears.

A godan verb derived from 隈取(くまどり) (kumadori, the kabuki face-painting technique). The noun 隈(くま) means "shade" or "dark area" (also the word for under-eye circles), and 取(ど)る here means to render or depict.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顔(かお)を隈取(くまど)る: to paint one's face with kumadori
  • 隈取(くまど)られた顔(かお): a face painted with kumadori
  • 紅葉(もみじ)に隈取(くまど)られる: to be colored by autumn leaves

CULTURAL NOTE:
隈取(くまどり) is the distinctive makeup used in kabuki theater. Red lines (紅隈(べにぐま)) represent positive traits like strength and virtue, while blue or indigo lines (藍隈(あいぐま)) represent negative traits like villainy or jealousy.
