美男(びなん)

びなん
noun
handsome man; good-looking man
1. handsome man; good-looking man
A man with attractive physical features. Somewhat literary or formal compared to everyday terms like イケメン.
彼(かれ)は美男(びなん)で有名(ゆうめい)だ。
He is famous for being handsome.
美男(びなん)美女(びじょ)のカップルだ。
They are a couple of a handsome man and a beautiful woman.
あの俳優(はいゆう)は美男(びなん)として知(し)られているが、演技力(えんぎりょく)も高(たか)い。
That actor is known as a handsome man, but he also has great acting ability.

Composed of 美(び) (beautiful) + 男(なん) (man). A classical-sounding word for a good-looking man. The fuller form 美男子(びなんし) is also common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 美男(びなん)美女(びじょ): handsome man and beautiful woman (an attractive couple)
  • 美男子(びなんし): handsome man (slightly more emphatic)
  • 美男(びなん)俳優(はいゆう): handsome actor

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • イケメン: good-looking guy — casual, modern slang; the most common everyday term
  • 男前(おとこまえ): handsome, manly — emphasizes masculine attractiveness
  • 美女(びじょ): beautiful woman — the female counterpart
