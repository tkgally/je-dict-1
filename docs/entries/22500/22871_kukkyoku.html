1. bending; curving; winding; flexion
The act or state of bending or curving, used for physical objects, roads, rivers, and in anatomy for joint flexion.
道(みち)が屈曲(くっきょく)している。
The road curves and bends.
膝(ひざ)の屈曲(くっきょく)運動(うんどう)をする。
To do knee flexion exercises.
川(かわ)は山(やま)のふもとで大(おお)きく屈曲(くっきょく)して流(なが)れている。
The river bends sharply as it flows at the foot of the mountain.

Composed of 屈(くつ) (to bend, to yield) + 曲(きょく) (to curve, to bend). Used in both everyday descriptions of winding paths and in medical/anatomical contexts for joint movement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 屈曲(くっきょく)する: to bend, to curve
  • 屈曲(くっきょく)運動(うんどう): flexion exercise
  • 屈曲(くっきょく)した道(みち): a winding road
  • 屈曲(くっきょく)部(ぶ): bent section, curve

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 湾曲(わんきょく): curve, curvature — a gentle, arc-like curve rather than a sharp bend
  • 蛇行(だこう): meandering — specifically for a river or road winding in S-shapes
  • 伸展(しんてん): extension — the opposite motion to flexion in anatomy
