1. quantification; making something measurable in numbers
The process of expressing something in numerical or measurable terms, often applied to abstract concepts like quality, satisfaction, or risk.
リスクの定量化(ていりょうか)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Quantification of risk is important.
顧客(こきゃく)満足度(まんぞくど)を定量化(ていりょうか)する方法(ほうほう)を考(かんが)える。
Think of ways to quantify customer satisfaction.
成果(せいか)を定量化(ていりょうか)することで、目標(もくひょう)達成(たっせい)の進捗(しんちょく)が明確(めいかく)になる。
By quantifying results, progress toward goal achievement becomes clear.

A compound of 定量(ていりょう) (fixed quantity; quantitative) and 化(か) (transformation suffix, "-ification"). Used extensively in business, science, and research contexts. The ability to quantify abstract concepts is a key theme in data-driven decision making.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 定量化(ていりょうか)する: to quantify
  • 定量化(ていりょうか)が難(むずか)しい: difficult to quantify
  • リスクの定量化(ていりょうか): risk quantification
  • 効果(こうか)の定量化(ていりょうか): quantification of effectiveness

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定量(ていりょう): quantitative — the base word, used as a modifier (e.g., 定量(ていりょう)分析(ぶんせき) "quantitative analysis")
  • 定性(ていせい): qualitative — the opposite approach, describing quality rather than quantity
  • 数値化(すうちか): numerification — converting into numerical values, very similar meaning
