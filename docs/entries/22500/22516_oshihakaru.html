1. to guess; to infer; to surmise
To form an estimate or judgment about something based on limited information or by analogy with one's own experience.
彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちを推(お)し量(はか)る。
To guess his feelings.
表情(ひょうじょう)から相手(あいて)の心情(しんじょう)を推(お)し量(はか)った。
I inferred the other person's feelings from their facial expression.
被災者(ひさいしゃ)の苦(くる)しみは想像(そうぞう)を絶(ぜっ)するもので、容易(ようい)には推(お)し量(はか)れない。
The suffering of the disaster victims is beyond imagination and cannot easily be surmised.

A somewhat literary or formal verb meaning to deduce or infer someone's feelings, thoughts, or a situation based on indirect evidence. It implies thoughtful consideration rather than a wild guess. Often used when trying to understand another person's inner state.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 気持(きも)ちを推(お)し量(はか)る: to guess someone's feelings
  • 心中(しんちゅう)を推(お)し量(はか)る: to surmise what someone is thinking
  • 容易(ようい)に推(お)し量(はか)れる: can be easily inferred
  • 推(お)し量(はか)ることができない: cannot surmise

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 推測(すいそく)する: to guess; to conjecture — a more neutral, general term for guessing
  • 察(さっ)する: to sense; to perceive — implies intuitive understanding with empathy
  • 見当(けんとう)をつける: to make a rough estimate — more casual, used for practical guesses about numbers or situations
