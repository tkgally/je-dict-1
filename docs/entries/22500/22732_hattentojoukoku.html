発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)

はってんとじょうこく
noun
developing country
1. developing country; developing nation
A country that is in the process of economic and industrial development but has not yet reached the level of an advanced nation.
発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)への支援(しえん)。
Aid to developing countries.
多(おお)くの発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)が経済(けいざい)成長(せいちょう)を遂(と)げている。
Many developing countries are achieving economic growth.
発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)と先進国(せんしんこく)の経済(けいざい)格差(かくさ)は依然(いぜん)として大(おお)きい。
The economic gap between developing and developed countries remains large.

The standard modern term for 'developing country.' Literally 'development-in-progress country' (発展(はってん) 'development' + 途上(とじょう) 'in the process of' + 国(くに) 'country'). This replaced the older term 後進国(こうしんこく) ('backward country'), which is now considered inappropriate.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)への援助(えんじょ): aid to developing countries
  • 発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)の経済(けいざい)発展(はってん): economic development of developing countries
  • 発展途上国(はってんとじょうこく)支援(しえん): support for developing countries

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 先進国(せんしんこく): developed country, advanced nation — the opposite term
  • 新興国(しんこうこく): emerging nation — often used for rapidly growing economies like the BRICS nations
  • 途上国(とじょうこく): developing country — abbreviated form, common in news and academic writing
