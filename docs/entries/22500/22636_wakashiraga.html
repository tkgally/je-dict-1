若白髪(わかしらが)

わかしらが
noun
premature gray hair; gray hair at a young age
1. premature gray hair; gray hair at a young age
White or gray hair that appears at an unusually young age, typically in one's teens, twenties, or thirties. Often attributed to genetics or stress.
若白髪(わかしらが)が気(き)になる。
I'm worried about my premature gray hair.
若白髪(わかしらが)は遺伝(いでん)の影響(えいきょう)が大(おお)きいと言(い)われている。
Premature gray hair is said to be largely influenced by genetics.
彼(かれ)は二十代(にじゅうだい)から若白髪(わかしらが)に悩(なや)まされていた。
He had been troubled by premature gray hair since his twenties.

Composed of 若(わか) (young) and 白髪(しらが) (gray/white hair). Refers to gray or white hair that appears at an unusually young age, typically in one's teens, twenties, or thirties. Often discussed in the context of genetics, stress, or hair care.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 若白髪(わかしらが)が多(おお)い: to have a lot of premature gray hair
  • 若白髪(わかしらが)に悩(なや)む: to be troubled by premature gray hair
  • 若白髪(わかしらが)を染(そ)める: to dye premature gray hair
  • 若白髪(わかしらが)の原因(げんいん): causes of premature gray hair

RELATED TERMS:

  • 白髪(しらが): gray/white hair — the general term regardless of age
  • 白髪染(しらがぞ)め: hair dye for gray hair
  • 遺伝(いでん): heredity — often cited as the main cause of 若白髪(わかしらが)
