1. to define; to characterize; to give a definition to
To establish or assign a clear meaning or characterization to a concept, term, or phenomenon, typically in academic, legal, or philosophical contexts.
この概念(がいねん)をどう定義(ていぎ)づけるか。
How do we define this concept?
法律(ほうりつ)で明確(めいかく)に定義(ていぎ)づけられている。
It is clearly defined by law.
成功(せいこう)を自分(じぶん)なりに定義(ていぎ)づけることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to define success in your own way.

Formed from 定義(ていぎ) (definition) and the verb-forming suffix づける (to assign, to attach). Used in academic, legal, and philosophical contexts to describe the act of establishing or assigning a definition to something.

USAGE:
The thing being defined is marked by を. Often used in the passive (定義(ていぎ)づけられる) when stating how something is officially defined.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 概念(がいねん)を定義(ていぎ)づける: to define a concept
  • 明確(めいかく)に定義(ていぎ)づける: to define clearly
  • 〜として定義(ていぎ)づける: to define as ~
  • 法的(ほうてき)に定義(ていぎ)づける: to define legally

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定義(ていぎ)する: to define — the more common suru-verb form with the same meaning
  • 規定(きてい)する: to stipulate — focuses on establishing rules or standards
  • 位置(いち)づける: to position, to characterize — describes placing something within a framework
