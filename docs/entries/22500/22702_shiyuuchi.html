私有地(しゆうち)

しゆうち
noun
private land; privately owned property
1. private land; privately owned property
Land that belongs to a private individual or entity, as opposed to public or government-owned land.
ここは私有地(しゆうち)です。
This is private property.
私有地(しゆうち)に無断(むだん)で入(はい)ってはいけない。
You must not enter private land without permission.
この森(もり)は私有地(しゆうち)なので一般(いっぱん)の人(ひと)は立(た)ち入(い)れない。
This forest is private property, so the general public cannot enter.

A compound of 私有(しゆう) (private ownership) + 地(ち) (land). Often seen on signs warning against trespassing. Japan has a strong concept of private land ownership, and 私有地(しゆうち) signs are commonly posted at the boundaries of private property.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 私有地(しゆうち)に立(た)ち入(い)る: to trespass on private land
  • 私有地(しゆうち)立入禁止(たちいりきんし): no trespassing on private property
  • 私有地(しゆうち)の売買(ばいばい): buying and selling of private land

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 公有地(こうゆうち): public land — land owned by national or local government
  • 国有地(こくゆうち): state-owned land — land owned by the national government specifically
  • 私有(しゆう): private ownership — the broader concept, not limited to land
