1. extension and remodeling; renovation and expansion (of a building)
The combined processes of adding to and remodeling an existing building. Covers both making the building larger (extension) and modifying its existing structure (remodeling).
自宅(じたく)の増改築(ぞうかいちく)を行(おこな)う。
We carry out renovation and expansion of our house.
増改築(ぞうかいちく)には建築(けんちく)許可(きょか)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
A building permit is required for renovation and expansion.
子(こ)どもが増(ふ)えたので、二階(にかい)を増改築(ぞうかいちく)して部屋(へや)を増(ふ)やすことにした。
Since we had more children, we decided to extend and remodel the second floor to add rooms.

A compound of three characters: 増(ぞう) (increase, add) + 改(かい) (modify, reform) + 築(ちく) (build, construct). Covers the full range of building modifications — from adding new rooms or floors to renovating existing spaces. Common in real estate, construction, and housing contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 増改築(ぞうかいちく)を行(おこな)う: to carry out renovation and expansion
  • 増改築(ぞうかいちく)工事(こうじ): renovation/expansion construction work
  • 増改築(ぞうかいちく)費用(ひよう): renovation/expansion costs
  • 増改築(ぞうかいちく)禁止(きんし): no renovation/expansion allowed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • リフォーム: remodeling, renovation — the popular loanword for home renovation; does not necessarily include expansion
  • 改築(かいちく): remodeling — modifying an existing structure without necessarily adding to it
  • 増築(ぞうちく): extension — adding new space to an existing building
  • リノベーション: renovation — a loanword that implies more extensive modernization than リフォーム
