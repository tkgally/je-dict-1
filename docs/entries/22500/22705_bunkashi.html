文化史(ぶんかし)

ぶんかし
noun
cultural history
1. cultural history
The study or account of the development of a society's culture, including arts, customs, beliefs, and intellectual achievements, as distinct from political or military history.
日本(にほん)の文化史(ぶんかし)を学(まな)ぶ。
To study the cultural history of Japan.
文化史(ぶんかし)の授業(じゅぎょう)で江戸時代(えどじだい)の食(しょく)について調(しら)べた。
In cultural history class, I researched food during the Edo period.
この本(ほん)は近代(きんだい)日本(にほん)の文化史(ぶんかし)をわかりやすくまとめている。
This book provides an accessible summary of the cultural history of modern Japan.

A compound of 文化(ぶんか) (culture) + 史(し) (history). While 歴史(れきし) often focuses on political events and wars, 文化史(ぶんかし) examines how people lived, what they ate, what they created, and how customs evolved.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 日本(にほん)文化史(ぶんかし): Japanese cultural history
  • 文化史(ぶんかし)の研究(けんきゅう): cultural history research
  • 食(しょく)の文化史(ぶんかし): cultural history of food
  • 文化史(ぶんかし)年表(ねんぴょう): cultural history timeline

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 政治史(せいじし): political history — history focused on political events and governance
  • 社会史(しゃかいし): social history — history focused on social structures and everyday life, broader than cultural history
  • 美術史(びじゅつし): art history — specifically about visual arts, a subset of cultural history
