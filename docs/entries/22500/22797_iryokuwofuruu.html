威力(いりょく)を振(ふ)るう

いりょくをふるう
expression
to exert power; to wield influence; to show its force
1. to exert power; to wield influence; to show its force
To demonstrate or exercise great power, strength, or effectiveness.
台風(たいふう)が威力(いりょく)を振(ふ)るった。
The typhoon showed its force.
この新(あたら)しい洗剤(せんざい)は頑固(がんこ)な汚(よご)れにも威力(いりょく)を振(ふ)るう。
This new detergent is effective even against stubborn stains.
彼(かれ)はチームの中(なか)で威力(いりょく)を振(ふ)るい、試合(しあい)の流(なが)れを変(か)えた。
He wielded his power within the team and changed the flow of the game.

威力(いりょく) means power, might, or force. 振(ふ)るう means to wield, brandish, or demonstrate. Together they express the idea of power being actively exercised or demonstrated. Can be used for natural forces (typhoons, earthquakes), tools and products (showing effectiveness), or people (wielding authority).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 威力(いりょく)を発揮(はっき)する: to demonstrate power (similar meaning, slightly more formal)
  • 威力(いりょく)がある: to have power/force
  • すさまじい威力(いりょく): tremendous force

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 力(ちから)を振(ふ)るう: to exercise power — more general
  • 腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう: to show one's skill — emphasizes demonstrating ability
  • 猛威(もうい)を振(ふ)るう: to rage, to wreak havoc — stronger and more destructive

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 猛威(もうい)を振(ふ)るう: to rage, to run rampant — implies destructive, overwhelming force (diseases, storms)
  • 権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to wield authority — specifically about political or institutional power
