1. full-speed sprint; running at full speed
Running as fast as one physically can, exerting maximum effort and speed.
全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)する。
To sprint at full speed.
選手(せんしゅ)たちが全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)でゴールを目指(めざ)した。
The athletes sprinted toward the goal at full speed.
終電(しゅうでん)に間(ま)に合(あ)うように駅(えき)まで全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)した。
I sprinted to the station at full speed to catch the last train.

A compound of 全力(ぜんりょく) (full power) and 疾走(しっそう) (sprint, dash). Describes running as fast as physically possible. Used in both sports contexts and everyday situations where someone runs with maximum effort.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)する: to sprint at full speed
  • 全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)で駆(か)ける: to dash at full speed
  • 全力疾走(ぜんりょくしっそう)で逃(に)げる: to flee at full speed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 疾走(しっそう): sprint, dash — does not necessarily imply maximum effort
  • 全速力(ぜんそくりょく): full speed — often used adverbially (全速力(ぜんそくりょく)で), describes the speed rather than the act of running
  • ダッシュ: dash — more casual; often used for short bursts of speed
