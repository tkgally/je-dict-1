暗黒街(あんこくがい)

あんこくがい
noun
underworld; criminal quarter
1. underworld; criminal quarter; world of organized crime
The world of organized crime, or a district known for criminal activity. Often used in fiction, film, and journalism.
暗黒街(あんこくがい)のボス。
A boss of the underworld.
この映画(えいが)は暗黒街(あんこくがい)を舞台(ぶたい)にした物語(ものがたり)だ。
This film is a story set in the criminal underworld.
戦後(せんご)の混乱期(こんらんき)に暗黒街(あんこくがい)が勢力(せいりょく)を拡大(かくだい)した。
The criminal underworld expanded its influence during the postwar period of chaos.

A compound of 暗黒(あんこく) (darkness) and 街(がい) (district/quarter). Evokes the image of a shadowy, lawless world. Frequently used in film noir, detective fiction, and crime dramas.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 暗黒街(あんこくがい)のボス: underworld boss
  • 暗黒街(あんこくがい)を支配(しはい)する: to rule the underworld
  • 暗黒街(あんこくがい)に足(あし)を踏(ふ)み入(い)れる: to step into the underworld

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 裏社会(うらしゃかい): underworld, shadow society — more commonly used in modern Japanese for the criminal world
  • 闇(やみ): darkness — metaphorically used for illegal or hidden activities (闇(やみ)ルート, 闇取引(やみとりひき))
  • マフィア: mafia — specifically refers to organized crime syndicates
