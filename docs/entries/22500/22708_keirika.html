経理課(けいりか)

けいりか
noun
accounting department; accounting section
1. accounting department; accounting section
The division within a company or organization responsible for managing finances, bookkeeping, and accounting.
経理課(けいりか)に書類(しょるい)を出(だ)す。
To submit documents to the accounting department.
経理課(けいりか)で経費(けいひ)の精算(せいさん)をしてもらった。
I had my expenses reimbursed by the accounting department.
来月(らいげつ)から経理課(けいりか)に異動(いどう)することになった。
I have been transferred to the accounting department starting next month.

A compound of 経理(けいり) (accounting, financial management) + 課(か) (section, department). In Japanese companies, 課(か) is a standard organizational unit below 部(ぶ) (division). The 経理課(けいりか) handles daily bookkeeping, expense processing, payroll, and financial reporting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 経理課(けいりか)に提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit to the accounting department
  • 経理課(けいりか)の担当者(たんとうしゃ): person in charge in the accounting department
  • 経理課(けいりか)長(ちょう): head of the accounting section

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 経理部(けいりぶ): accounting division — a larger organizational unit than 経理課(けいりか)
  • 総務課(そうむか): general affairs department — handles administrative matters rather than finances
  • 人事課(じんじか): human resources department
