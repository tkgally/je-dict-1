在野(ざいや)

ざいや
noun
out of office; not in power; independent
1. out of office; in opposition; not in power
Being outside the ruling party or government. Used in politics to describe parties or politicians who are not currently in power.
在野(ざいや)政党(せいとう)。
An opposition party.
選挙(せんきょ)で負(ま)けて在野(ざいや)に下(くだ)った。
They lost the election and went into opposition.
在野(ざいや)の立場(たちば)から政府(せいふ)を批判(ひはん)する。
To criticize the government from an opposition standpoint.
2. unaffiliated; independent; outside the establishment
Being outside of official or institutional organizations. Used for scholars, researchers, or professionals who work independently rather than within established institutions.
在野(ざいや)の研究者(けんきゅうしゃ)。
An independent researcher.
大学(だいがく)に属(ぞく)さず在野(ざいや)で研究(けんきゅう)を続(つづ)けている。
They continue their research independently without belonging to a university.
在野(ざいや)の歴史(れきし)研究家(けんきゅうか)が新(あたら)しい史料(しりょう)を発見(はっけん)した。
An independent history researcher discovered new historical documents.

Literally means "being in the field" — i.e., among the common people rather than in the halls of power. Composed of 在(ざい) (being at) and 野(や) (field, the wild). The contrast is with 与党(よとう) (ruling party) in politics, or with institutional affiliation in academic contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 在野(ざいや)政党(せいとう): opposition party
  • 在野(ざいや)に下(くだ)る: to go into opposition; to lose power
  • 在野(ざいや)の研究者(けんきゅうしゃ): independent researcher
  • 在野(ざいや)精神(せいしん): spirit of independence (from established power)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 野党(やとう): opposition party — the standard political term, more common
  • 民間(みんかん): private sector; civilian — broader, not specifically about opposition
  • 無所属(むしょぞく): unaffiliated; independent — specifically about organizational membership

ANTONYMS:

  • 与党(よとう): ruling party
  • 官(かん): government; official sector
