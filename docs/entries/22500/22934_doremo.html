どれも

どれも
pronoun
all of them; every one; any of them
1. all of them; every one
Refers to every item in a group without exception, emphasizing that the same quality or condition applies to each one.
どれも美味(おい)しい。
They're all delicious.
どれも同(おな)じに見(み)える。
They all look the same to me.
商品(しょうひん)はどれも品質(ひんしつ)が高(たか)いので、安心(あんしん)して買(か)えます。
All the products are high quality, so you can buy them with confidence.

Combines the interrogative pronoun どれ (which one) with the inclusive particle も to mean "all of them" or "every single one." Used when referring to multiple items or options and indicating that the same judgment applies to each.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • どれも〜ない: none of them (with negative verb)
  • どれも同(おな)じ: they're all the same
  • どれもこれも: each and every one (emphatic)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • どれも美味(おい)しい: they're all delicious
  • どれも素晴(すば)らしい: they're all wonderful
  • どれも気(き)に入(い)らない: I don't like any of them

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • どちらも: both (used for two items)
  • どれでも: any one of them / whichever (implies choosing one)
  • 全部(ぜんぶ): all, everything — more general; どれも emphasizes individual items within a visible group
