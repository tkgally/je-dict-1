1. to serve out portions; to dish out
To take food from a shared dish and divide it into individual portions.
サラダを取(と)り分(わ)けてください。
Please serve out the salad.
大皿(おおざら)の料理(りょうり)をみんなで取(と)り分(わ)けた。
We divided up the food from the large platter among everyone.
子(こ)どもの分(ぶん)を先(さき)に取(と)り分(わ)けておいた方(ほう)がいいよ。
You should set aside the children's portions first.
2. to sort out; to separate; to set aside
To select and separate specific items from a larger group.
必要(ひつよう)な書類(しょるい)を取(と)り分(わ)ける。
I'll sort out the necessary documents.
良(よ)い品(しな)と悪(わる)い品(しな)を取(と)り分(わ)けた。
I sorted out the good items from the bad ones.
検査(けんさ)のために一部(いちぶ)のサンプルを取(と)り分(わ)けて保管(ほかん)しておく必要(ひつよう)がある。
It's necessary to set aside and store some samples for testing.

Most commonly encountered at restaurants and dinner parties when serving food from shared plates. In Japanese dining culture, shared dishes (大皿(おおざら)料理(りょうり)) are common, and 取(と)り分(わ)ける describes the act of portioning food onto individual plates.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 料理(りょうり)を取(と)り分(わ)ける: to serve out food
  • 小皿(こざら)に取(と)り分(わ)ける: to portion onto small plates
  • 均等(きんとう)に取(と)り分(わ)ける: to divide equally
  • 取(と)り分(わ)け用(よう)の箸(はし): serving chopsticks

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 分(わ)ける: to divide; to separate — a more general word for dividing things
  • 盛(も)り付(つ)ける: to plate; to arrange food — focuses on presentation rather than portioning
  • 配(くば)る: to distribute; to hand out — implies giving things out to multiple people
