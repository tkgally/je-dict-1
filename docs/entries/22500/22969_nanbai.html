何倍(なんばい)

なんばい
noun
how many times; how many fold
1. how many times; how many fold
An interrogative expression asking about a multiple or ratio. Used to ask how many times greater, larger, or more something is compared to a reference point.
何倍(なんばい)になりましたか。
How many times has it increased?
この部屋(へや)はあの部屋(へや)の何倍(なんばい)の広(ひろ)さですか。
How many times larger is this room compared to that room?
去年(きょねん)より何倍(なんばい)も努力(どりょく)したのに結果(けっか)が出(で)なかった。
I put in many times more effort than last year, but didn't get results.
2. many times over; manifold
Used emphatically to express that something is much greater in degree or quantity, without asking a literal question.
今(いま)の幸(しあわ)せは昔(むかし)の何倍(なんばい)もある。
My happiness now is many times greater than before.
実物(じつぶつ)は写真(しゃしん)の何倍(なんばい)も美(うつく)しかった。
The real thing was many times more beautiful than in the photos.
彼(かれ)は私(わたし)の何倍(なんばい)もの経験(けいけん)を積(つ)んでいる。
He has accumulated many times more experience than I have.

Composed of 何(なん) (what; how many) and 倍(ばい) (times; fold). Used both as a genuine question and as an emphatic expression meaning "many times over."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 何倍(なんばい)にもなる: to increase manifold
  • 何倍(なんばい)もの努力(どりょく): many times the effort
  • 何倍(なんばい)も大(おお)きい: many times bigger
  • 何倍(なんばい)もの価値(かち): many times the value

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 何杯(なんばい): how many cups/glasses — same pronunciation but different kanji and meaning
  • 数倍(すうばい): several times; several fold — states a vague multiple without asking
  • 何度(なんど): how many times (frequency) — counts occurrences, not multiples

GRAMMAR:
Often followed by も for emphasis: 何倍(なんばい)も means "many times over." Can be followed by の to modify nouns: 何倍(なんばい)もの量(りょう) (many times the amount).
