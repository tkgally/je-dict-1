1. to raise prices; to mark up
To increase the price or cost of goods or services.
電気代(でんきだい)が値上(ねあ)げされた。
Electricity prices were raised.
この店(みせ)は毎年(まいとし)値上(ねあ)げしている。
This store raises prices every year.
原材料(げんざいりょう)の高騰(こうとう)により、商品(しょうひん)を値上(ねあ)げせざるを得(え)なかった。
Due to soaring raw material costs, they had no choice but to raise prices on their products.

Refers specifically to raising prices, as opposed to prices rising on their own. The noun form 値上(ねあ)げ is commonly used on its own to mean 'a price increase.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 家賃(やちん)を値上(ねあ)げする: to raise the rent
  • 料金(りょうきん)を値上(ねあ)げする: to raise fees
  • 大幅(おおはば)に値上(ねあ)げする: to raise prices significantly
  • 一斉(いっせい)値上(ねあ)げ: across-the-board price increase

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 値下(ねさ)げする: to lower prices — the opposite action
  • 値上(ねあ)がりする: to go up in price — describes prices rising by themselves, without specifying who raised them
  • 高(たか)くなる: to become expensive — a more general and casual expression
