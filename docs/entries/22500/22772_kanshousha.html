鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)

かんしょうしゃ
noun
viewer; appreciator; audience member
1. viewer; appreciator; person who appreciates art or performances
A person who views, listens to, or appreciates artistic works such as paintings, music, films, or performances. Emphasizes the act of aesthetic appreciation rather than passive observation.
鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)の感想(かんそう)を集(あつ)めた。
We collected impressions from the viewers.
この作品(さくひん)は鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)に深(ふか)い感動(かんどう)を与(あた)えた。
This work gave its viewers deep emotional impact.
美術館(びじゅつかん)では鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)が静(しず)かに絵画(かいが)の前(まえ)に立(た)ち止(ど)まっていた。
In the art museum, viewers stood quietly in front of the paintings.

Composed of 鑑賞(かんしょう) ('appreciation, viewing') and 者(しゃ) (suffix meaning 'person'). The word implies active aesthetic engagement rather than passive watching. It is used primarily in arts, cultural, and academic contexts.

Note the kanji 鑑(かん) (discern, appreciate) — this distinguishes 鑑賞(かんしょう) (aesthetic appreciation) from 観賞(かんしょう) (enjoyment of viewing), which uses 観(かん) (watch). 鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ) implies a more thoughtful, critical engagement with the work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)の視点(してん): the viewer's perspective
  • 鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)に訴(うった)える: to appeal to the viewer
  • 鑑賞者(かんしょうしゃ)を魅了(みりょう)する: to captivate the audience

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 観客(かんきゃく): audience, spectator — more general; used for live performances, sports, and entertainment
  • 視聴者(しちょうしゃ): TV viewer, audience — specifically for broadcast media
  • 聴衆(ちょうしゅう): audience, listeners — specifically for music or lectures
