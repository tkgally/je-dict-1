1. to stock (a range of items); to have a full selection available
各種(かくしゅ)サイズを取(と)り揃(そろ)えております。
We have all sizes in stock.
季節(きせつ)の商品(しょうひん)を幅広(はばひろ)く取(と)り揃(そろ)えています。
We stock a wide range of seasonal products.
この店(みせ)は輸入(ゆにゅう)ワインを豊富(ほうふ)に取(と)り揃(そろ)えている。
This shop has a rich selection of imported wines.
2. to gather and prepare (documents, items needed)
必要(ひつよう)な書類(しょるい)を取(と)り揃(そろ)えてください。
Please gather all the necessary documents.
申請(しんせい)に必要(ひつよう)なものを全(すべ)て取(と)り揃(そろ)えた。
I got everything needed for the application together.
旅行(りょこう)の準備(じゅんび)で持(も)ち物(もの)を取(と)り揃(そろ)えた。
I gathered my belongings in preparation for the trip.

A compound verb formed from 取(と)る (to take) and 揃(そろ)える (to arrange, to make uniform). Sense 1 is commonly used in retail and commercial contexts to describe having a variety of products available. Sense 2 means to collect and prepare everything that is needed.

USAGE:
Sense 1 is very common in polite commercial speech, often in the ている/ております form to describe what a shop carries. Sense 2 is used more generally when preparing or assembling items.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 商品(しょうひん)を取(と)り揃(そろ)える: to stock products
  • 書類(しょるい)を取(と)り揃(そろ)える: to gather documents
  • 豊富(ほうふ)に取(と)り揃(そろ)える: to have a rich selection
  • 幅広(はばひろ)く取(と)り揃(そろ)える: to stock a wide range

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 揃(そろ)える: to arrange, to put in order — more general, without the nuance of gathering from various sources
  • 品揃(しなぞろ)え: product lineup — noun form describing the range of goods available
  • 用意(ようい)する: to prepare — more general preparation, not specifically gathering a variety
