折(おり)を見(み)て

おりをみて
expression
when the time comes; when the opportunity arises
1. when the time comes; when the opportunity arises; at the right moment
An expression meaning to wait for an appropriate moment before taking action. Implies patience and timing rather than immediate action.
折(おり)を見(み)て相談(そうだん)します。
I'll consult with you when the time is right.
折(おり)を見(み)て上司(じょうし)に報告(ほうこく)しようと思(おも)っている。
I'm planning to report to my supervisor when the opportunity arises.
忙(いそが)しそうだったので、折(おり)を見(み)て声(こえ)をかけることにした。
Since they looked busy, I decided to speak to them when the moment was right.

A set expression built on 折(おり) (occasion, opportunity) and 見(み)て (watching for). Expresses the idea of waiting for a suitable opening rather than acting immediately. Common in both business and everyday contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 折(おり)を見(み)て + verb: to do something when the time is right
  • 折(おり)を見(み)て伝(つた)える: to tell someone when the opportunity arises
  • 折(おり)を見(み)て訪(たず)ねる: to visit when the time comes

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 機会(きかい)を見(み)て: when the opportunity comes — similar but slightly more formal
  • 頃合(ころあ)いを見(み)て: judging the right moment — emphasizes reading the situation
  • タイミングを見(み)て: watching for the right timing — more casual, uses loanword
  • 折(おり)に触(ふ)れて: from time to time; whenever the occasion arises — means repeatedly, not just once

REGISTER:
Polite and measured. Often used in workplace communication to signal that one will act thoughtfully rather than rushing.
