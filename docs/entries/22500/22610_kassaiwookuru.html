喝采(かっさい)を送(おく)る

かっさいをおくる
expression
to applaud; to cheer; to acclaim
1. to applaud; to cheer; to acclaim; to give an ovation
To express enthusiastic approval or admiration for someone's achievement or performance, either literally through clapping or figuratively through praise.
観客(かんきゃく)が喝采(かっさい)を送(おく)った。
The audience applauded.
選手(せんしゅ)たちに惜(お)しみない喝采(かっさい)を送(おく)った。
They gave the players wholehearted applause.
彼(かれ)の勇気(ゆうき)ある行動(こうどう)に、世界中(せかいじゅう)から喝采(かっさい)が送(おく)られた。
His courageous actions were applauded from around the world.

A set expression combining 喝采(かっさい) (cheers, applause) with 送(おく)る (to send). The expression carries a sense of warm, enthusiastic praise and is more literary than simple 拍手(はくしゅ)する. Often used in news writing and formal contexts to describe acclaim for achievements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 惜(お)しみない喝采(かっさい)を送(おく)る: to give unstinting applause
  • 大(おお)きな喝采(かっさい)を送(おく)る: to give great applause
  • 喝采(かっさい)を浴(あ)びる: to receive applause (from the recipient's perspective)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 拍手(はくしゅ)を送(おく)る: to send applause — more literal, focused on clapping
  • 声援(せいえん)を送(おく)る: to send cheers — focused on vocal support
  • 称賛(しょうさん)する: to praise — a more general word for praising
