1. to spread; to propagate; to be transmitted
For something such as a disease, culture, information, or wave to spread or be transmitted from one place or person to another.
感染症(かんせんしょう)が急速(きゅうそく)に伝播(でんぱ)した。
The infectious disease spread rapidly.
インターネットを通(つう)じて情報(じょうほう)が瞬時(しゅんじ)に伝播(でんぱ)する。
Information propagates instantly through the internet.
仏教(ぶっきょう)はインドから東(ひがし)アジア各地(かくち)に伝播(でんぱ)し、それぞれの文化(ぶんか)と融合(ゆうごう)した。
Buddhism propagated from India to various parts of East Asia and merged with each local culture.

A formal, often academic or scientific term for the spreading or transmission of something. It is commonly used in contexts involving disease, culture, religion, technology, and physical phenomena like sound or light waves. The kanji 播(は) means 'to sow' or 'to scatter,' reflecting the image of something being scattered outward.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 感染(かんせん)の伝播(でんぱ): transmission of infection
  • 文化(ぶんか)の伝播(でんぱ): cultural propagation
  • 伝播(でんぱ)速度(そくど): propagation speed
  • 伝播(でんぱ)経路(けいろ): transmission route

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 広(ひろ)まる: to spread — the everyday word for something spreading among people
  • 拡散(かくさん)する: to diffuse; to spread — often used for information going viral or substances spreading
  • 伝染(でんせん)する: to be contagious — specifically for diseases spreading from person to person
