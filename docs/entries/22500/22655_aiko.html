あいこ

あいこ
noun
tie; draw; even
1. tie; draw; even
A result where neither side wins, particularly in games like rock-paper-scissors. Can also be used figuratively to mean being even or quits.
あいこでしょ！
It's a tie! (Let's go again!)
じゃんけんで三回(さんかい)連続(れんぞく)あいこになった。
We tied three times in a row at rock-paper-scissors.
お互(たが)いに一回(いっかい)ずつ勝(か)ったから、これであいこだね。
We each won once, so we're even now.

Most commonly heard in the context of じゃんけん (rock-paper-scissors). When both players choose the same hand, the result is あいこ, and players chant あいこでしょ！ before throwing again.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • あいこでしょ: it's a tie (chant in rock-paper-scissors)
  • あいこになる: to end in a tie
  • これであいこだ: now we're even

USAGE NOTE:
Beyond games, あいこ can mean "we're even" or "quits" in everyday situations — for example, after exchanging favors. The word can also be written as 相子(あいこ), though the hiragana form is more common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)き分(わ)け: draw, tie — used in sports and formal competitions
  • 同点(どうてん): tied score — specifically about numerical scores in sports
