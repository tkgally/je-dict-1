尊大(そんだい)

そんだい
adjective-na
haughty; arrogant; overbearing
1. haughty; arrogant; overbearing; self-important
Describes an attitude of looking down on others and behaving as though one is superior. Strongly negative in connotation.
彼(かれ)は尊大(そんだい)な態度(たいど)を取(と)る。
He takes a haughty attitude.
尊大(そんだい)な物言(ものい)いが周囲(しゅうい)の反感(はんかん)を買(か)った。
His arrogant way of speaking invited resentment from those around him.
あの上司(じょうし)は部下(ぶか)に対(たい)して尊大(そんだい)にふるまうので、誰(だれ)からも好(す)かれていない。
That boss acts overbearing toward his subordinates, so nobody likes him.

Composed of 尊(そん) (noble, revered) + 大(だい) (great, big). Despite the positive meaning of its components, the word is entirely negative, describing someone who acts as if they are more important than others. It implies a conscious, deliberate display of superiority rather than mere confidence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 尊大(そんだい)な態度(たいど): haughty attitude
  • 尊大(そんだい)な物言(ものい)い: arrogant way of speaking
  • 尊大(そんだい)にふるまう: to behave arrogantly
  • 尊大(そんだい)な人(ひと): arrogant person

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 傲慢(ごうまん): arrogant — similar meaning but emphasizes insolent disregard for others
  • 横柄(おうへい): domineering — implies rudeness and high-handedness in manner
  • 偉(えら)そう: acting important — a more colloquial way to describe someone putting on airs
  • 謙虚(けんきょ): modest, humble — the opposite quality
