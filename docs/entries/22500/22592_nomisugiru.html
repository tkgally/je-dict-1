1. to drink too much; to overdrink
To consume an excessive amount of a beverage, especially alcohol.
ビールを飲(の)み過(す)ぎた。
I drank too much beer.
昨日(きのう)飲(の)み過(す)ぎて、頭(あたま)が痛(いた)い。
I drank too much yesterday and have a headache.
コーヒーを飲(の)み過(す)ぎると夜(よる)眠(ねむ)れなくなるので気(き)をつけている。
I'm careful because drinking too much coffee keeps me from sleeping at night.

A compound verb formed from 飲(の)む (to drink) + 過(す)ぎる (to exceed). While it can apply to any beverage, it is most commonly used about alcohol. The pattern 〜過(す)ぎる is productive and can attach to many verbs to mean "too much" (e.g., 食(た)べ過(す)ぎる "to eat too much").

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お酒(さけ)を飲(の)み過(す)ぎる: to drink too much alcohol
  • 飲(の)み過(す)ぎて気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い: feeling sick from drinking too much
  • 飲(の)み過(す)ぎに注意(ちゅうい)する: to be careful about overdrinking
  • 飲(の)み過(す)ぎで二日酔(ふつかよ)い: a hangover from drinking too much

USAGE:
This is an ichidan verb. The negative is 飲(の)み過(す)ぎない, the past is 飲(の)み過(す)ぎた, and the te-form is 飲(の)み過(す)ぎて.
