こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)

こげちゃいろ
noun
dark brown
1. dark brown; chocolate brown
A deep, dark shade of brown, like the color of something slightly scorched. One of the standard Japanese color terms.
こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)の靴(くつ)を買(か)った。
I bought dark brown shoes.
壁(かべ)をこげ茶色(ちゃいろ)に塗(ぬ)り替(か)えた。
I repainted the wall dark brown.
こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)の革(かわ)のバッグは、どんな服(ふく)にも合(あ)わせやすい。
A dark brown leather bag is easy to coordinate with any outfit.

A standard color term combining こげ (scorched, burnt) with 茶色(ちゃいろ) (brown). Often written in hiragana for the first part. Sometimes abbreviated to こげ茶(ちゃ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)の髪(かみ): dark brown hair
  • こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)の家具(かぐ): dark brown furniture
  • こげ茶色(ちゃいろ)の革靴(かわぐつ): dark brown leather shoes

RELATED COLOR TERMS:

  • 茶色(ちゃいろ): brown — the basic brown
  • 薄茶色(うすちゃいろ): light brown, tan
  • 栗色(くりいろ): chestnut brown — slightly reddish brown

ETYMOLOGY:
こげ comes from 焦(こ)げる (to scorch/burn). The color resembles the dark shade of something that has been lightly scorched.
