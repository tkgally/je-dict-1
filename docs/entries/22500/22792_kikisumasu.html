1. to listen carefully; to strain one's ears
To concentrate intently on hearing a sound, often in a quiet environment.
耳(みみ)を聞(き)き澄(す)ます。
To strain one's ears to listen.
遠(とお)くの物音(ものおと)に聞(き)き澄(す)ました。
I listened carefully to the distant sounds.
夜(よる)の森(もり)で息(いき)を殺(ころ)して聞(き)き澄(す)ますと、小(ちい)さな虫(むし)の声(こえ)が聞(き)こえた。
When I held my breath and listened carefully in the forest at night, I could hear the faint sounds of insects.

A compound verb formed from 聞(き)く (to listen) + 澄(す)ます (to make clear/pure). The idea is that by concentrating, one clears away distractions to hear more purely. Often appears in literary or narrative writing to describe a character focusing their hearing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 耳(みみ)を聞(き)き澄(す)ます: to prick up one's ears
  • じっと聞(き)き澄(す)ます: to listen intently, motionlessly
  • 息(いき)を殺(ころ)して聞(き)き澄(す)ます: to hold one's breath and listen carefully

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 耳(みみ)を澄(す)ます: to listen carefully — very similar meaning; this shorter form is more common in everyday speech
  • 聞(き)き入(い)る: to listen intently, to be absorbed in listening — emphasizes becoming engrossed
  • 傾聴(けいちょう)する: to listen attentively — formal; often used in counseling or business contexts
