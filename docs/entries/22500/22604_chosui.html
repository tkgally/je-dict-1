1. water storage; storing water; impoundment
The act of collecting and storing water, or the stored water itself, typically in tanks, reservoirs, or dams.
貯水(ちょすい)タンクを設置(せっち)した。
We installed a water storage tank.
ダムの貯水(ちょすい)量(りょう)が減(へ)っている。
The water storage level in the dam is decreasing.
干(かん)ばつに備(そな)えて貯水(ちょすい)施設(しせつ)の整備(せいび)が進(すす)められている。
Water storage facilities are being improved in preparation for drought.

A compound of 貯(ちょ) (to store, to save) and 水(すい) (water). Used in contexts related to water management, infrastructure, and disaster preparedness. Japan's mountainous geography and seasonal rainfall patterns make water storage an important topic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貯水(ちょすい)タンク: water storage tank
  • 貯水(ちょすい)池(いけ): reservoir; storage pond
  • 貯水(ちょすい)量(りょう): water storage volume
  • 貯水(ちょすい)施設(しせつ): water storage facility
  • 貯水(ちょすい)率(りつ): water storage rate

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 貯水池(ちょすいち): reservoir — a specific body of stored water
  • 給水(きゅうすい): water supply — the distribution of water
  • 断水(だんすい): water outage — the interruption of water supply
  • 節水(せっすい): water conservation — reducing water usage
