晩酌(ばんしゃく)

ばんしゃく
noun / verb-suru
evening drink; drinking at home in the evening
1. evening drink at home; having a drink with dinner
The custom of drinking alcohol at home in the evening, typically with dinner or a light snack. A daily ritual for many adults in Japan, distinct from social drinking at bars or restaurants.
毎晩(まいばん)晩酌(ばんしゃく)を楽(たの)しむ。
I enjoy an evening drink every night.
父(ちち)は晩酌(ばんしゃく)にビールを一杯(いっぱい)飲(の)む。
My father has a beer with his evening drink.
仕事(しごと)が終(お)わって家(いえ)に帰(かえ)ったら、晩酌(ばんしゃく)をしながら好(す)きなテレビを見(み)るのが日課(にっか)になっている。
After work, my daily routine is to come home and watch my favorite TV shows while having my evening drink.

USAGE:
晩酌(ばんしゃく) specifically refers to drinking at home in the evening, not going out to drink. It conveys a sense of relaxation and daily routine. The word has a warm, familiar connotation and is often associated with the image of a tired worker unwinding at home.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく)をする (to have an evening drink)
  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく)のお供(とも) (accompaniment for one's evening drink — i.e., snacks)
  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく)を楽(たの)しむ (to enjoy one's evening drink)
  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく)に付(つ)き合(あ)う (to join someone for their evening drink)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 晩酌(ばんしゃく): evening drink at home (routine, relaxed)
  • 飲(の)み会(かい): drinking party (social, at a restaurant or bar)
  • 一杯(いっぱい)やる: to have a drink (casual, can be anywhere)
  • 宅飲(たくの)み: drinking at home (modern slang, can be social)
