駆(か)ける

かける
verb-ichidan
to run; to dash; to gallop
1. to run; to dash; to sprint
To run swiftly, often with a sense of urgency or energy. More literary and vivid than 走(はし)る.
子(こ)どもが公園(こうえん)を駆(か)けている。
Children are running around the park.
階段(かいだん)を駆(か)け上(あ)がった。
I dashed up the stairs.
ニュースを聞(き)いて、彼(かれ)は病院(びょういん)に駆(か)けつけた。
Upon hearing the news, he rushed to the hospital.
2. to gallop; to ride at speed
For a horse or mounted rider to move at full speed. Used in historical and equestrian contexts.
馬(うま)が野原(のはら)を駆(か)けている。
A horse is galloping across the field.
武将(ぶしょう)は馬(うま)を駆(か)けて戦場(せんじょう)に向(む)かった。
The general galloped toward the battlefield.
騎手(きしゅ)がサラブレッドを駆(か)け、観客(かんきゃく)の前(まえ)を一気(いっき)に駆(か)け抜(ぬ)けた。
The jockey spurred the thoroughbred on, racing past the spectators in a flash.

An ichidan verb. More dynamic and literary than 走(はし)る (to run). Often appears in compound verbs.

COMMON COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 駆(か)け上(あ)がる (dash up)
  • 駆(か)け下(お)りる (dash down)
  • 駆(か)けつける (rush to the scene)
  • 駆(か)け抜(ぬ)ける (dash through)
  • 駆(か)け回(まわ)る (run around)
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む (rush into)

SIMILAR WORDS:
走(はし)る is the standard neutral word for running. 駆(か)ける is more vivid and implies speed, urgency, or dynamic motion. 疾走(しっそう)する is even more formal and literary.
