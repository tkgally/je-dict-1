段落(だんらく)

だんらく
noun
paragraph; section; conclusion
1. paragraph; section
A distinct section of a piece of writing, usually dealing with a single theme or idea.
次(つぎ)の段落(だんらく)を読(よ)んでください。
Please read the next paragraph.
この文章(ぶんしょう)は三(みっ)つの段落(だんらく)に分(わ)かれている。
This passage is divided into three paragraphs.
作文(さくぶん)を書(か)くときは段落(だんらく)の分(わ)け方(かた)に注意(ちゅうい)しましょう。
When writing an essay, pay attention to how you divide paragraphs.
2. end of a phase; conclusion of a stage
A natural stopping point or conclusion of a process or task. Often used in the expression 一段落(いちだんらく) to mean reaching a point where one can pause.
仕事(しごと)が一段落(いちだんらく)した。
The work has reached a stopping point.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの準備(じゅんび)がやっと一段落(いちだんらく)ついた。
The preparations for moving have finally settled down.
忙(いそが)しい時期(じき)が一段落(いちだんらく)したら、旅行(りょこう)に行(い)きたい。
Once the busy period wraps up, I want to go on a trip.

Has two distinct uses: as a writing term for paragraph, and in the compound 一段落(いちだんらく) meaning the end of a phase.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 段落(だんらく)を分(わ)ける (to divide into paragraphs)
  • 一段落(いちだんらく)つく (to reach a stopping point)
  • 一段落(いちだんらく)する (to settle down, to wrap up a phase)

READING NOTE:
The compound 一段落(いちだんらく) is traditionally read いちだんらく, though the reading ひとだんらく is also widely used in spoken Japanese.
