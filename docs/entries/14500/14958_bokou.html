母校(ぼこう)

ぼこう
noun
alma mater; one's old school
1. alma mater; one's old school
The school one graduated from or previously attended. Used with a sense of affection or nostalgia for one's former school.
久(ひさ)しぶりに母校(ぼこう)を訪(たず)ねた。
I visited my old school after a long time.
母校(ぼこう)が甲子園(こうしえん)に出場(しゅつじょう)して嬉(うれ)しい。
I'm happy that my alma mater made it to Koshien.
母校(ぼこう)の校舎(こうしゃ)が建(た)て替(か)えられて、すっかり様変(さまが)わりしていた。
My old school building had been rebuilt and looked completely different.

Literally "mother school" (母(ぼ) = mother + 校(こう) = school). The metaphor of "mother" conveys the nurturing, formative relationship.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 母校(ぼこう)を訪(たず)ねる (to visit one's alma mater)
  • 母校(ぼこう)に帰(かえ)る (to return to one's old school)
  • 母校(ぼこう)を応援(おうえん)する (to cheer for one's alma mater)

USAGE NOTE:
Can refer to any school previously attended — elementary, middle, high school, or university. Most commonly used for the school the speaker has the strongest emotional connection to.
