進入(しんにゅう)

しんにゅう
noun, verb-suru
entry; approach; penetration
1. entry; approach; entering
The act of entering or approaching a specific area or zone. Used for vehicles, aircraft, or people entering a defined space, especially in transportation and traffic contexts.
進入(しんにゅう)禁止(きんし)の標識(ひょうしき)がある。
There is a 'no entry' sign.
飛行機(ひこうき)が滑走路(かっそうろ)に進入(しんにゅう)した。
The airplane approached the runway.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)への進入(しんにゅう)路(ろ)が渋滞(じゅうたい)で混(こ)んでいた。
The on-ramp to the expressway was congested with traffic.

A する verb used mainly in transportation, aviation, and traffic contexts. Describes authorized or neutral entry into a zone.

Do not confuse with 侵入(しんにゅう) (intrusion/trespassing), which implies unauthorized or hostile entry. 進入(しんにゅう) is neutral or authorized.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 進入(しんにゅう)禁止(きんし) (no entry)
  • 進入(しんにゅう)路(ろ) (approach road; on-ramp)
  • 進入(しんにゅう)角度(かくど) (approach angle)
  • 着陸(ちゃくりく)進入(しんにゅう) (landing approach)
