得意分野(とくいぶんや)

とくいぶんや
noun
area of expertise; forte; strong suit
1. area of expertise; forte; strong suit; specialty
A field or subject in which someone excels or has particular skill and knowledge.
彼(かれ)の得意分野(とくいぶんや)はデータ分析(ぶんせき)だ。
His area of expertise is data analysis.
それぞれの得意分野(とくいぶんや)を活(い)かして協力(きょうりょく)しよう。
Let's cooperate by making use of each person's strengths.
面接(めんせつ)では自分(じぶん)の得意分野(とくいぶんや)を具体的(ぐたいてき)にアピールすることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
In interviews, it's important to concretely highlight your areas of expertise.

A compound of 得意(とくい) (strong point, good at) and 分野(ぶんや) (field, area). Commonly used in professional, academic, and interview contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 得意分野(とくいぶんや)を活(い)かす (to make use of one's expertise)
  • 得意分野(とくいぶんや)を伸(の)ばす (to develop one's strong areas)
  • 得意分野(とくいぶんや)を見(み)つける (to find one's area of strength)
  • 得意分野(とくいぶんや)が異(こと)なる (to have different specialties)

The opposite concept is 苦手(にがて)分野(ぶんや) (weak area, area of weakness).
