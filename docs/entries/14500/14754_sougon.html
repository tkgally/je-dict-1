荘厳(そうごん)

そうごん
adjective (na)
solemn; sublime; majestic
1. solemn; sublime; majestic
Describes something that inspires awe through its grandeur, dignity, or sacred quality. Used for religious settings, grand ceremonies, natural landscapes, and architecture.
荘厳(そうごん)な雰囲気(ふんいき)に包(つつ)まれた。
I was enveloped in a solemn atmosphere.
大聖堂(だいせいどう)の内部(ないぶ)は荘厳(そうごん)な美(うつく)しさに満(み)ちていた。
The interior of the cathedral was filled with sublime beauty.
山(やま)の頂(いただき)から見(み)た朝日(あさひ)は荘厳(そうごん)で、思(おも)わず息(いき)を飲(の)んだ。
The sunrise seen from the mountaintop was so majestic that I involuntarily held my breath.

USAGE: 荘厳(そうごん) is a na-adjective used in literary and formal contexts. It describes an elevated, awe-inspiring quality that goes beyond mere beauty. It is often used for religious architecture, sacred rituals, and grand natural scenery.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 荘厳(そうごん)な雰囲気(ふんいき) (solemn atmosphere)
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な儀式(ぎしき) (solemn ceremony)
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な音楽(おんがく) (majestic music)
  • 荘厳(そうごん)な建築(けんちく) (sublime architecture)

SIMILAR WORDS: Compare with 厳粛(げんしゅく) (grave, solemn), which emphasizes seriousness and gravity, while 荘厳(そうごん) emphasizes grandeur and sublimity. 荘厳(そうごん) often implies visual or spatial impressiveness, whereas 厳粛(げんしゅく) focuses on a serious, reverent mood.
