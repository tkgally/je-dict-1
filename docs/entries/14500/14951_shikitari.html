しきたり

しきたり
noun
custom; convention; established practice
1. custom; convention; established practice; tradition
An unwritten rule or practice handed down within a group, family, or community. Often refers to social customs that people follow out of long-standing habit rather than formal rules.
この地域(ちいき)のしきたりに従(したが)う。
I follow the customs of this area.
古(ふる)いしきたりにこだわる必要(ひつよう)はない。
There's no need to cling to old customs.
嫁(よめ)ぎ先(さき)のしきたりが厳(きび)しくて、慣(な)れるまで大変(たいへん)だった。
The customs of my husband's family were strict, and it was hard to get used to them.

Refers to practices passed down through generations within a specific group, family, or locality. Differs from 伝統(でんとう) (tradition), which is broader and more formal.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • しきたりに従(したが)う (to follow a custom)
  • しきたりを守(まも)る (to observe/maintain a custom)
  • しきたりを破(やぶ)る (to break with custom)
  • 古(ふる)いしきたり (old customs)

USAGE NOTE:
しきたり often carries a nuance of something that "has always been done this way" — it may feel obligatory even though it is not a formal law. It is sometimes used with a slightly negative nuance, suggesting outdated or rigid practices.
