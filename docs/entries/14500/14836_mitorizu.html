見取(みと)り図(ず)

みとりず
noun
sketch; floor plan; rough diagram
1. sketch; floor plan; rough diagram; layout drawing
A rough drawing or diagram showing the layout of a building, room, or area. Not a precise architectural blueprint, but a freehand sketch capturing the general arrangement.
部屋(へや)の見取(みと)り図(ず)を描(か)いた。
I drew a floor plan of the room.
不動産(ふどうさん)サイトで間取(まど)りの見取(みと)り図(ず)を確認(かくにん)した。
I checked the floor plan layout on the real estate website.
引(ひ)っ越(こ)しの前(まえ)に新(あたら)しい家(いえ)の見取(みと)り図(ず)を見(み)ながら、家具(かぐ)の配置(はいち)を考(かんが)えた。
Before moving, I looked at the floor plan of the new house while thinking about furniture placement.

A compound of 見取(みと)り (sketching by observation) and 図(ず) (diagram, drawing). Often used interchangeably with 間取(まど)り図(ず) in the real estate context, though 間取(まど)り specifically focuses on room layout.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 見取(みと)り図(ず)を描(か)く (to draw a sketch/floor plan)
  • 見取(みと)り図(ず)を見(み)る (to look at a floor plan)
  • 現場(げんば)の見取(みと)り図(ず) (sketch of the scene — used in crime investigations)
  • 建物(たてもの)の見取(みと)り図(ず) (building floor plan)

Also used figuratively to mean an overview or outline of a situation: 日本(にほん)経済(けいざい)の見取(みと)り図(ず) (an overview of the Japanese economy).
