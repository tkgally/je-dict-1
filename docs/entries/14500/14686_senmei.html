鮮明(せんめい)

せんめい
adjective-na
vivid; clear; distinct
1. vivid; clear; distinct
Something that is sharp, clear, and easy to perceive. Used for images, memories, colors, contrasts, and positions/stances.
記憶(きおく)が鮮明(せんめい)に残(のこ)っている。
The memory remains vivid.
鮮明(せんめい)な画像(がぞう)を撮影(さつえい)できるカメラだ。
It's a camera that can capture clear images.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)に祖母(そぼ)と過(す)ごした夏(なつ)の日々(ひび)が今(いま)でも鮮明(せんめい)に思(おも)い出(だ)される。
The summer days I spent with my grandmother as a child are still vividly remembered.

Often used with the adverbial form 鮮明(せんめい)に (vividly, clearly). Applies to both visual clarity and abstract clarity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鮮明(せんめい)な記憶(きおく) (vivid memory)
  • 鮮明(せんめい)な画像(がぞう) / 映像(えいぞう) (clear image / footage)
  • 鮮明(せんめい)な色彩(しきさい) (vivid colors)
  • 鮮明(せんめい)に覚(おぼ)えている (remember vividly)
  • 態度(たいど)を鮮明(せんめい)にする (make one's stance clear)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 明確(めいかく) emphasizes logical or factual clarity
  • 鮮明(せんめい) emphasizes perceptual vividness — sharp, bright, striking
  • 鮮(あざ)やか also means vivid but focuses more on colors and visual beauty
