鎧(よろい)

よろい
noun
armor
1. armor; suit of armor
Protective body covering worn by warriors, especially the traditional Japanese armor worn by samurai. Also used figuratively for emotional defenses or outward facades.
博物館(はくぶつかん)に鎧(よろい)が展示(てんじ)されている。
Armor is on display at the museum.
武士(ぶし)は鎧(よろい)を身(み)につけて戦(たたか)った。
The samurai fought wearing armor.
端午(たんご)の節句(せっく)には鎧(よろい)や兜(かぶと)を飾(かざ)る家庭(かてい)もある。
Some families display armor and helmets for the Boys' Day festival.

Refers specifically to Japanese-style armor unless otherwise qualified. Western-style armor is usually distinguished as 西洋(せいよう)の鎧(よろい).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鎧(よろい)を着(き)る / 鎧(よろい)を身(み)につける (to put on armor)
  • 鎧(よろい)兜(かぶと) (armor and helmet — a set phrase)
  • 鎧(よろい)を脱(ぬ)ぐ (to remove armor; figuratively, to drop one's guard)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Japanese armor (鎧(よろい)) is displayed during 端午(たんご)の節句(せっく) (Boys' Day, May 5) as a symbol of strength and protection for boys.
