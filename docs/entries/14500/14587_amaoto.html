雨音(あまおと)

あまおと
noun
sound of rain
1. sound of rain; patter of rain
The sound made by falling rain. A word with aesthetic and atmospheric associations, often used in literary and everyday contexts.
雨音(あまおと)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear the sound of rain.
雨音(あまおと)を聞(き)きながら眠(ねむ)りについた。
I fell asleep listening to the sound of the rain.
激(はげ)しい雨音(あまおと)で目(め)が覚(さ)め、窓(まど)の外(そと)を見(み)ると道(みち)が川(かわ)のようになっていた。
I was woken by the loud sound of rain, and when I looked outside, the road had turned into a river.

READING:
The kanji 雨(あめ) takes the combining form あま- in compounds: 雨音(あまおと), 雨水(あまみず), 雨雲(あまぐも). This あま- form is a common pattern in native Japanese compounds.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨音(あまおと)が聞(き)こえる (to hear the sound of rain)
  • 雨音(あまおと)を聞(き)く (to listen to the rain)
  • 激(はげ)しい雨音(あまおと) (heavy rain sound)
  • 静(しず)かな雨音(あまおと) (quiet patter of rain)

SIMILAR COMPOUNDS:
Other ～音(おと) compounds: 足音(あしおと) (footsteps), 物音(ものおと) (noise/sound), 風音(かざおと) (sound of wind).
