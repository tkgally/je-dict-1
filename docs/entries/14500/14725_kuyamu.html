1. to regret; to rue
To feel regret or remorse about a past action or decision.
あの時(とき)の失敗(しっぱい)を悔(く)やんでいる。
I regret the mistake I made then.
悔(く)やんでも悔(く)やみきれない。
No amount of regret is enough.
もっと勉強(べんきょう)しておけばよかったと今(いま)になって悔(く)やんでいる。
I'm regretting now that I didn't study harder.
2. to mourn; to offer condolences
To grieve over someone's death or to express condolences. Used in formal and ceremonial contexts.
友人(ゆうじん)の死(し)を悔(く)やむ。
To mourn a friend's death.
お悔(く)やみ申(もう)し上(あ)げます。
Please accept my condolences.
故人(こじん)の死(し)を悔(く)やみ、遺族(いぞく)にお悔(く)やみの言葉(ことば)を述(の)べた。
I mourned the deceased and offered words of condolence to the bereaved family.

SENSE 1 (regret):
Often used in the ている form to express ongoing regret. The set phrase 悔(く)やんでも悔(く)やみきれない means "no amount of regret is enough" and expresses deep, inconsolable regret.

SENSE 2 (condolences):
The noun form 悔(く)やみ means "condolence." The formal expression お悔(く)やみ申(もう)し上(あ)げます is the standard way to offer condolences at funerals.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 悔(く)やんでも悔(く)やみきれない (inconsolable regret)
  • お悔(く)やみの言葉(ことば) (words of condolence)
  • 過去(かこ)を悔(く)やむ (to regret the past)

Related: 後悔(こうかい)する is the more common word for "to regret" in everyday speech. 悔(く)やむ has a more literary or emotional tone.
