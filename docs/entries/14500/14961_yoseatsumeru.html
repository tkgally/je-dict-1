寄(よ)せ集(あつ)める

よせあつめる
verb-ichidan
to gather together; to collect; to scrape together
1. to gather together; to collect; to scrape together
To bring various things or people together from different places. Often implies gathering miscellaneous or disparate items rather than a neat, organized collection.
資料(しりょう)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)めた。
I gathered together the materials.
残(のこ)り物(もの)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)めて夕食(ゆうしょく)を作(つく)った。
I scraped together the leftovers and made dinner.
各地(かくち)から専門家(せんもんか)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)めて緊急(きんきゅう)の対策(たいさく)チームを作(つく)った。
They assembled a team of experts gathered from around the country to form an emergency response unit.

Ichidan verb (一段(いちだん)). Transitive — takes を for the things gathered.

The compound combines 寄(よ)せる (to bring near) and 集(あつ)める (to collect). Often carries a nuance of assembling things hastily or from scattered sources, unlike 集(あつ)める alone which is more neutral.

The noun form 寄(よ)せ集(あつ)め means 'a motley collection' or 'hodgepodge,' often with a slightly negative tone.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 情報(じょうほう)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)める (to gather information from various sources)
  • 人(ひと)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)める (to round up people)
  • 資金(しきん)を寄(よ)せ集(あつ)める (to scrape together funds)
