朗読(ろうどく)

ろうどく
noun, verb-suru
reading aloud; recitation
1. reading aloud; recitation
The act of reading text aloud in a clear, expressive voice. Often associated with literary works, poetry, or public readings.
詩(し)を朗読(ろうどく)する。
To read a poem aloud.
先生(せんせい)が物語(ものがたり)を朗読(ろうどく)してくれた。
The teacher read us a story aloud.
図書館(としょかん)で絵本(えほん)の朗読(ろうどく)イベントが開(ひら)かれた。
A picture-book reading event was held at the library.

朗読(ろうどく) is a noun that also functions as a suru-verb (朗読(ろうどく)する). It implies reading with expression and projection, not simply reading text out loud.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 詩(し)を朗読(ろうどく)する (to recite a poem)
  • 朗読(ろうどく)する (to read aloud)
  • 朗読(ろうどく)会(かい) (a reading/recitation event)
  • 朗読(ろうどく)ボランティア (volunteer reader)

RELATED WORDS:
音読(おんどく) is reading aloud for practice or study (often in classrooms), while 朗読(ろうどく) implies a more polished, performative reading with attention to expression and delivery. 黙読(もくどく) is silent reading.
