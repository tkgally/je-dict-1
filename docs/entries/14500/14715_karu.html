駆(か)る

かる
verb-godan
to drive; to spur on; to compel
1. to drive; to spur on; to urge forward
To drive or urge an animal (especially a horse) or vehicle forward. A literary or formal word for propelling something.
馬(うま)を駆(か)る。
Spur on a horse.
武士(ぶし)は愛馬(あいば)を駆(か)って敵陣(てきじん)に突撃(とつげき)した。
The samurai spurred his beloved horse and charged into the enemy lines.
スポーツカーを駆(か)って海岸線(かいがんせん)を走(はし)った。
I drove a sports car along the coastline.
2. to compel; to impel; to be driven by
To be urged or driven to action by an emotion or impulse. Often used in the passive form 駆(か)られる to describe being overcome by a feeling.
好奇心(こうきしん)に駆(か)られた。
I was driven by curiosity.
不安(ふあん)に駆(か)られて眠(ねむ)れなかった。
I was gripped by anxiety and couldn't sleep.
怒(いか)りに駆(か)られて思(おも)わず声(こえ)を荒(あら)げてしまったが、あとで後悔(こうかい)した。
Driven by anger, I couldn't help raising my voice, but I regretted it later.

A godan verb. Sense 2 (passive form 駆(か)られる) is the more common modern usage.

SENSE 1 (drive/spur):
Literary and somewhat archaic for the horse-riding meaning, but still used for driving vehicles in a dynamic, literary style.

SENSE 2 (compel):
Very common in the passive pattern 「Xに駆(か)られる」 (be driven by X).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 好奇心(こうきしん)に駆(か)られる (be driven by curiosity)
  • 不安(ふあん)に駆(か)られる (be gripped by anxiety)
  • 衝動(しょうどう)に駆(か)られる (be driven by impulse)
  • 怒(いか)りに駆(か)られる (be driven by anger)
  • 焦(あせ)りに駆(か)られる (be driven by impatience)

SIMILAR WORDS:
駆(か)ける (to run/dash) is intransitive, while 駆(か)る is transitive. 追(お)い立(た)てる (to drive out/chase) implies forceful pushing, while 駆(か)る focuses on the inner force or motivation.
