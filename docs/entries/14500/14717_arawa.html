露(あらわ)

あらわ
adjective-na
exposed; bare; undisguised
1. exposed; bare; uncovered
Physically exposed or revealed, with nothing covering or concealing something. Describes a state where something that is normally hidden is visible.
肌(はだ)が露(あらわ)になった。
Her skin was exposed.
工事(こうじ)で地面(じめん)の下(した)の岩盤(がんばん)が露(あらわ)になった。
The construction work exposed the bedrock beneath the ground.
崖(がけ)崩(くず)れで山(やま)の地肌(じはだ)が露(あらわ)になり、赤茶(あかちゃ)けた土(つち)が見(み)えていた。
The landslide left the bare surface of the mountain exposed, revealing reddish-brown earth.
2. undisguised; blatant; open
Openly showing emotions, intentions, or attitudes without trying to conceal them. Often implies a lack of restraint or social tact.
不満(ふまん)を露(あらわ)にした。
He openly showed his dissatisfaction.
彼(かれ)は敵意(てきい)を露(あらわ)にして近(ちか)づいてきた。
He approached with undisguised hostility.
記者会見(きしゃかいけん)で社長(しゃちょう)は怒(いか)りを露(あらわ)にし、報道(ほうどう)の内容(ないよう)を強(つよ)く否定(ひてい)した。
At the press conference, the president openly showed his anger and strongly denied the content of the reports.

Can be written as 露(あらわ) or 露(あら)わ. Functions as a na-adjective but is most commonly used in the pattern 「Xを露(あらわ)にする」 (to expose X / to openly display X).

SENSE 1 (exposed):
Describes physical exposure or uncovering.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (exposed):

  • 肌(はだ)が露(あらわ)になる (skin becomes exposed)
  • 地肌(じはだ)が露(あらわ)になる (bare ground is exposed)

SENSE 2 (undisguised):
Describes openly displaying emotions or attitudes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (undisguised):

  • 不満(ふまん)を露(あらわ)にする (openly show dissatisfaction)
  • 敵意(てきい)を露(あらわ)にする (show open hostility)
  • 怒(いか)りを露(あらわ)にする (openly display anger)
  • 本性(ほんしょう)を露(あらわ)にする (reveal one's true nature)

USAGE NOTES:
Not to be confused with 現(あらわ)れる (to appear) or 表(あらわ)す (to express). 露(あらわ) specifically implies something hidden becoming visible or someone dropping their pretense.
