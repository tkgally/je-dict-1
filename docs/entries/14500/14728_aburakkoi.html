脂(あぶら)っこい

あぶらっこい
adjective-i
greasy; oily; fatty
1. greasy; oily; fatty
Describes food that is high in oil or fat. Often carries a slightly negative nuance, suggesting the food is too oily for one's taste or digestion.
この料理(りょうり)はちょっと脂(あぶら)っこい。
This dish is a bit greasy.
年(とし)を取(と)ると脂(あぶら)っこいものが食(た)べられなくなる。
As you get older, you can't eat greasy food anymore.
脂(あぶら)っこい食事(しょくじ)の後(あと)は、さっぱりしたお茶(ちゃ)が飲(の)みたくなる。
After a greasy meal, I want to drink some refreshing tea.

Mainly used for food. Carries a mildly negative tone — the food is oilier than desired. Often contrasted with さっぱり (light, refreshing).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 油(あぶら)っぽい — similar meaning (oily), more general and can apply to skin, hair, etc.
  • こってり — rich and heavy (can be positive, describing flavorful broth)
  • 脂(あぶら)っこい — specifically about greasiness, usually slightly negative

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 脂(あぶら)っこい食(た)べ物(もの) (greasy food)
  • 脂(あぶら)っこい料理(りょうり) (greasy/oily dish)
  • 脂(あぶら)っこいものが苦手(にがて) (bad with greasy food)
