道具箱(どうぐばこ)

どうぐばこ
noun
toolbox
1. toolbox; tool chest
A box or container for storing and organizing tools. Can refer to a physical toolbox for carpentry or repairs, or a box for art supplies and other implements.
道具箱(どうぐばこ)からハンマーを取(と)り出(だ)した。
I took a hammer out of the toolbox.
図工(ずこう)の道具箱(どうぐばこ)に名前(なまえ)を書(か)いてください。
Please write your name on the arts and crafts toolbox.
道具箱(どうぐばこ)の中(なか)が散(ち)らかっていて、必要(ひつよう)なネジが見(み)つからなかった。
The toolbox was such a mess that I couldn't find the screws I needed.

USAGE:
道具箱(どうぐばこ) is a straightforward compound of 道具(どうぐ) (tools) and 箱(はこ) (box). In Japanese elementary schools, students have a 道具箱(どうぐばこ) for art and craft supplies that they keep at their desks.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道具箱(どうぐばこ)から取(と)り出(だ)す (to take out from the toolbox)
  • 道具箱(どうぐばこ)を整理(せいり)する (to organize the toolbox)
  • 道具箱(どうぐばこ)に入(い)れる (to put in the toolbox)
