ひび

ひび
noun
crack; fissure
1. crack; fissure; crevice
A thin line of breakage on a hard surface such as glass, ceramic, bone, or a wall. The object is damaged but not fully broken apart.
コップにひびが入(はい)った。
A crack formed in the glass.
壁(かべ)のひびを修理(しゅうり)してもらった。
I had the crack in the wall repaired.
転(ころ)んで骨(ほね)にひびが入(はい)ってしまった。
I fell and got a hairline fracture in my bone.
2. rift; strain (in a relationship)
A figurative crack in a relationship or bond between people. Describes a situation where trust or harmony is damaged, though the relationship has not completely ended.
二人(ふたり)の友情(ゆうじょう)にひびが入(はい)った。
A rift formed in their friendship.
些細(ささい)な誤解(ごかい)が積(つ)み重(かさ)なり、夫婦(ふうふ)の関係(かんけい)にひびが入(はい)り始(はじ)めた。
Small misunderstandings piled up, and cracks began to form in the couple's relationship.

USAGE:
ひび is most commonly used in the pattern ひびが入(はい)る (a crack forms). It describes damage that has not yet resulted in a full break — the object or relationship is weakened but still intact. Sometimes written in kanji as 罅(ひび), but the kana form is far more common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ひびが入(はい)る (a crack forms; to crack)
  • ひびが入(はい)った皿(さら) (a cracked plate)
  • ひびを修理(しゅうり)する (to repair a crack)
  • 骨(ほね)にひびが入(はい)る (to get a hairline fracture)
  • 関係(かんけい)にひびが入(はい)る (a rift forms in a relationship)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ひび: crack (thin line of damage, not fully broken)
  • 割(わ)れ目(め): split, seam (a wider opening or gap)
  • 亀裂(きれつ): crack, fissure (more formal; often used figuratively for organizations or alliances)
