保存食(ほぞんしょく)

ほぞんしょく
noun
preserved food; emergency rations
1. preserved food; long-lasting food; emergency rations
Food that has been processed or prepared to last a long time without spoiling. Includes canned goods, dried foods, freeze-dried meals, and disaster preparedness supplies.
非常用(ひじょうよう)の保存食(ほぞんしょく)を備(そな)えている。
I keep emergency preserved food on hand.
保存食(ほぞんしょく)の賞味期限(しょうみきげん)を定期的(ていきてき)に確認(かくにん)しよう。
Let's regularly check the expiration dates on preserved food.
最近(さいきん)の保存食(ほぞんしょく)は味(あじ)も種類(しゅるい)も豊富(ほうふ)で、災害(さいがい)時(じ)でもおいしく食(た)べられる。
Modern preserved food comes in a wide variety of flavors, making it possible to eat well even during disasters.

A compound of 保存(ほぞん) (preservation, storage) and 食(しょく) (food).

In Japan, 保存食(ほぞんしょく) is particularly associated with disaster preparedness (防災(ぼうさい)). Households are encouraged to keep a supply of preserved food for earthquakes and other emergencies.

Traditional Japanese preserved foods include 漬物(つけもの) (pickles), 干物(ひもの) (dried fish), and 梅干(うめぼ)し (pickled plums).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 保存食(ほぞんしょく)を備蓄(びちく)する (to stockpile preserved food)
  • 非常用(ひじょうよう)保存食(ほぞんしょく) (emergency preserved food)
  • 長期(ちょうき)保存食(ほぞんしょく) (long-term preserved food)
