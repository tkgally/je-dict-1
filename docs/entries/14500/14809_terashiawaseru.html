照(て)らし合(あ)わせる

てらしあわせる
verb (ichidan)
to check against; to compare; to cross-reference
1. to check against; to compare; to cross-reference
To compare one thing with another to verify accuracy, consistency, or correspondence. Often used when checking data, documents, or information against a reference.
リストと照(て)らし合(あ)わせてください。
Please check it against the list.
原本(げんぽん)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせて間違(まちが)いがないか確認(かくにん)した。
I checked it against the original to make sure there were no errors.
規則(きそく)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせると、この手続(てつづ)きは正(ただ)しくないことが分(わ)かった。
When I checked it against the regulations, I found that this procedure was not correct.

A compound verb formed from 照(て)らす (to shine, to illuminate) and 合(あ)わせる (to put together). The image is of holding two things up to the light to compare them.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 原本(げんぽん)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせる (to check against the original)
  • 基準(きじゅん)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせる (to compare against a standard)
  • 事実(じじつ)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせる (to check against the facts)
  • 記録(きろく)と照(て)らし合(あ)わせる (to cross-reference with records)

Often used in formal or business contexts. The particle と marks the reference item being compared against.
