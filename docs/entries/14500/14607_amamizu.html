雨水(あまみず)

あまみず
noun
rainwater
1. rainwater
Water that has fallen as rain and collected on the ground, in tanks, or other containers. Used in everyday contexts as well as in discussions of water management and gardening.
雨水(あまみず)をタンクに貯(た)める。
Collect rainwater in a tank.
庭(にわ)の花(はな)には雨水(あまみず)を使(つか)って水(みず)やりをしている。
I use rainwater to water the flowers in the garden.
屋根(やね)から流(なが)れ落(お)ちる雨水(あまみず)が排水溝(はいすいこう)に集(あつ)まり、道路(どうろ)が冠水(かんすい)するのを防(ふせ)いでいる。
Rainwater flowing off the roof collects in the drain, preventing the road from flooding.

READING NOTE:
The kanji 雨水(あまみず) can also be read うすい, which refers to the traditional calendar term for the onset of spring rains (around February 19). The reading あまみず is the everyday word for rainwater.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 雨水(あまみず)を貯(た)める (to collect/store rainwater)
  • 雨水(あまみず)を利用(りよう)する (to make use of rainwater)
  • 雨水(あまみず)タンク (rainwater tank)
  • 雨水(あまみず)排水(はいすい) (rainwater drainage)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 雨水(あまみず): rainwater (collected or fallen rain)
  • 湧(わ)き水(みず): spring water (from underground)
  • 水道水(すいどうすい): tap water (municipal supply)
