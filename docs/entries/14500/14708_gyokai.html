魚介(ぎょかい)

ぎょかい
noun
seafood; fish and shellfish
1. seafood; fish and shellfish
A collective term for edible marine animals including fish, shellfish, crustaceans, and other aquatic creatures. Commonly used in food and culinary contexts.
新鮮(しんせん)な魚介(ぎょかい)を使(つか)った料理(りょうり)だ。
It's a dish made with fresh seafood.
この地域(ちいき)は魚介類(ぎょかいるい)が豊富(ほうふ)で有名(ゆうめい)だ。
This area is famous for its abundance of seafood.
市場(いちば)では地元(じもと)で獲(と)れた魚介(ぎょかい)が並(なら)び、観光客(かんこうきゃく)にも人気(にんき)がある。
Locally caught seafood is displayed at the market, and it's popular with tourists too.

Compound of 魚(ぎょ) (fish) + 介(かい) (shellfish). Often used in the extended form 魚介類(ぎょかいるい) (seafood, with the classifier 類(るい)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魚介類(ぎょかいるい) (seafood, fish and shellfish)
  • 魚介(ぎょかい)スープ (seafood soup)
  • 魚介(ぎょかい)料理(りょうり) (seafood dish)
  • 新鮮(しんせん)な魚介(ぎょかい) (fresh seafood)
  • 魚介(ぎょかい)の盛(も)り合(あ)わせ (assorted seafood platter)

USAGE NOTES:
More formal and comprehensive than 海鮮(かいせん) (which also means seafood but is more colloquial and restaurant-oriented). 魚介(ぎょかい) includes not just fish and shellfish but also squid, octopus, shrimp, and seaweed in broader usage. The variant reading ぎょがい exists but ぎょかい is standard.
