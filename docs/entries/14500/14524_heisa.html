閉鎖(へいさ)

へいさ
noun / verb-suru
closure; shutdown; lockdown
1. closure; shutdown; closing down
The act of closing a facility, institution, road, or service, either temporarily or permanently. Used for factories, schools, roads, websites, and other operations.
あの工場(こうじょう)は閉鎖(へいさ)された。
That factory was shut down.
台風(たいふう)の影響(えいきょう)で道路(どうろ)が閉鎖(へいさ)されている。
The road is closed due to the typhoon.
経営(けいえい)悪化(あっか)により、来月(らいげつ)末(まつ)で店舗(てんぽ)を閉鎖(へいさ)することが決(き)まった。
Due to worsening business conditions, it was decided to close the store at the end of next month.

Compound of 閉(へい) (to close) + 鎖(さ) (to lock/chain). Implies a formal or official closing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 工場(こうじょう)を閉鎖(へいさ)する (to close a factory)
  • 道路(どうろ)を閉鎖(へいさ)する (to close a road)
  • 閉鎖(へいさ)に追(お)い込(こ)まれる (to be forced into closure)
  • 閉鎖(へいさ)的(てき) (closed-minded; insular)

USAGE NOTE:
The adjective form 閉鎖(へいさ)的(てき) means "closed" in the sense of being exclusive, insular, or unwelcoming of outsiders — e.g., 閉鎖(へいさ)的(てき)な社会(しゃかい) (a closed society).
