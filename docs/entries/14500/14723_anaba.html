穴場(あなば)

あなば
noun
hidden gem; little-known good spot
1. hidden gem; little-known good spot
A place that is not widely known but is excellent — a hidden gem or insider's spot. Often used for restaurants, travel destinations, and shops.
ここは穴場(あなば)のラーメン屋(や)だ。
This is a hidden-gem ramen shop.
穴場(あなば)の花見(はなみ)スポットを教(おし)えてもらった。
Someone told me about a little-known cherry blossom viewing spot.
この海岸(かいがん)は夏(なつ)でも人(ひと)が少(すく)ない穴場(あなば)で、静(しず)かに泳(およ)げる。
This beach is a hidden gem with few people even in summer, so you can swim in peace.

A positive word implying that a place is good precisely because it is not well known or crowded. Often used in travel guides and food reviews.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 穴場(あなば)スポット (hidden-gem spot)
  • 穴場(あなば)の店(みせ) (hidden-gem restaurant/shop)
  • 穴場(あなば)的(てき) (having hidden-gem quality)

USAGE NOTE: Unlike 秘境(ひきょう) (unexplored region), which implies remoteness and wilderness, 穴場(あなば) simply means a good place that most people don't know about — it can be in the middle of a city.
