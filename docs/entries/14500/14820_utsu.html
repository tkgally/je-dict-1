1. to strike down; to slay; to defeat in battle
To attack and kill or defeat an enemy, especially in a military or historical context. Often implies righteous punishment or vengeance.
敵(てき)を討(う)つ。
To strike down the enemy.
仇(かたき)を討(う)つために旅(たび)に出(で)た。
He set out on a journey to take revenge on his enemy.
忠臣蔵(ちゅうしんぐら)は主君(しゅくん)の仇(かたき)を討(う)った四十七士(しじゅうしちし)の物語(ものがたり)だ。
Chushingura is the story of the forty-seven samurai who avenged their lord.
2. to subjugate; to launch a campaign against
To mount a military expedition to conquer or suppress an enemy force or region.
反乱軍(はんらんぐん)を討(う)つ。
To subjugate the rebel forces.
将軍(しょうぐん)は大軍(たいぐん)を率(ひき)いて賊(ぞく)を討(う)ちに向(む)かった。
The general led a large army to subjugate the bandits.
朝廷(ちょうてい)は東国(とうごく)の反乱(はんらん)を討(う)つため、征夷大将軍(せいいたいしょうぐん)を任命(にんめい)した。
The imperial court appointed a shogun to subjugate the rebellion in the eastern provinces.

Distinct from the homophones 打(う)つ (to hit, to strike physically) and 撃(う)つ (to shoot). 討(う)つ specifically means to attack and defeat an enemy, often with a connotation of justice or retribution.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 敵(てき)を討(う)つ (to strike down the enemy)
  • 仇(かたき)を討(う)つ (to take revenge)
  • 賊(ぞく)を討(う)つ (to subjugate bandits)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 討伐(とうばつ) — subjugation, punitive expedition
  • 仇討(あだう)ち — revenge, vendetta
  • 討論(とうろん) — debate (different usage of 討(とう))

Primarily used in historical and literary contexts. In modern usage, 仇(かたき)を討(う)つ survives as a set phrase.
