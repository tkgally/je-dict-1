予約席(よやくせき)

よやくせき
noun
reserved seat
1. reserved seat
A seat that has been booked or set aside in advance at a restaurant, theater, train, or other venue.
テーブルに「予約席(よやくせき)」の札(ふだ)が置(お)いてあった。
There was a 'Reserved' sign on the table.
予約席(よやくせき)は窓側(まどがわ)にご用意(ようい)しております。
Your reserved seat has been prepared by the window.
人気(にんき)のレストランなので、予約席(よやくせき)を取(と)っておかないと週末(しゅうまつ)は入(はい)れない。
It's a popular restaurant, so you can't get in on weekends without reserving a seat in advance.

A compound of 予約(よやく) (reservation) and 席(せき) (seat). You will often see this word written on table placards at restaurants.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 予約席(よやくせき)を取(と)る (to reserve a seat)
  • 予約席(よやくせき)の札(ふだ) (reserved seat sign/placard)
  • 予約席(よやくせき)にご案内(あんない)する (to show someone to their reserved seat)

Distinguish from 指定席(していせき) (assigned/designated seat, as on trains), which refers to a specific numbered seat purchased with a ticket, and 自由席(じゆうせき) (non-reserved/free seating).
