続々(ぞくぞく)と

ぞくぞくと
adverb
one after another; successively
1. one after another; successively; in rapid succession
Describes multiple things or people appearing, arriving, or happening in quick succession without pause. Conveys a sense of continuous flow or momentum.
客(きゃく)が続々(ぞくぞく)と集(あつ)まった。
Customers gathered one after another.
新(あたら)しい情報(じょうほう)が続々(ぞくぞく)と入(はい)ってくる。
New information keeps pouring in one after another.
開店(かいてん)セールの日(ひ)には、朝(あさ)から買(か)い物客(ものきゃく)が続々(ぞくぞく)と店(みせ)に押(お)し寄(よ)せた。
On the day of the opening sale, shoppers flocked to the store one after another from the morning.

USAGE:
続々(ぞくぞく)と is an adverb describing things happening in rapid succession. The と particle is optional but commonly included. It is used with verbs of appearance, arrival, or emergence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と集(あつ)まる (to gather one after another)
  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と届(とど)く (to arrive in succession)
  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と発売(はつばい)される (to be released one after another)
  • 続々(ぞくぞく)と参加(さんか)する (to join one after another)

CAUTION:
Do not confuse with ぞくぞくする (to shiver, to get chills), which describes a physical sensation. 続々(ぞくぞく)と always modifies a verb describing successive events.
