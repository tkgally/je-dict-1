親睦会(しんぼくかい)

しんぼくかい
noun
social gathering; get-together; fellowship event
1. social gathering; get-together; fellowship event
An organized event where members of a group (workplace, school, neighborhood, etc.) socialize to strengthen personal relationships. More structured than a casual party.
親睦会(しんぼくかい)に参加(さんか)した。
I attended a social gathering.
新入(しんにゅう)社員(しゃいん)の歓迎(かんげい)を兼(か)ねた親睦会(しんぼくかい)が開(ひら)かれた。
A social gathering was held that also served as a welcome event for new employees.
町内会(ちょうないかい)では年(ねん)に二回(にかい)、親睦会(しんぼくかい)を開催(かいさい)して住民同士(じゅうみんどうし)の交流(こうりゅう)を深(ふか)めている。
The neighborhood association holds social gatherings twice a year to deepen interaction among residents.

A compound of 親睦(しんぼく) (friendship, fellowship) and 会(かい) (meeting, gathering).

Common in Japanese workplace and community culture. These events often involve eating and drinking together. In a company context, they may be semi-mandatory — attending shows team spirit.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 懇親会(こんしんかい) is a near-synonym, slightly more formal
  • 飲(の)み会(かい) is a more casual drinking party
  • 歓迎会(かんげいかい) specifically welcomes new members
  • 送別会(そうべつかい) is a farewell party

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 親睦会(しんぼくかい)を開(ひら)く (to hold a social gathering)
  • 親睦会(しんぼくかい)に出席(しゅっせき)する (to attend a social gathering)
  • 親睦(しんぼく)を深(ふか)める (to deepen fellowship)
