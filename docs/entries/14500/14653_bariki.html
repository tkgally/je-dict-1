馬力(ばりき)

ばりき
noun
horsepower; energy; vigor
1. horsepower (unit of power)
A unit of measurement for engine power and mechanical output.
このエンジンは200馬力(ばりき)ある。
This engine has 200 horsepower.
馬力(ばりき)の高(たか)い車(くるま)がほしい。
I want a car with high horsepower.
軽自動車(けいじどうしゃ)は馬力(ばりき)が小(ちい)さいので坂道(さかみち)はきつい。
Light cars have low horsepower, so hills are tough.
2. energy; vigor; drive
Used figuratively to describe a person's stamina, effort, or determination to get things done.
締(し)め切(き)り前(まえ)は馬力(ばりき)をかけて働(はたら)く。
I work with full steam before a deadline.
あの人(ひと)は馬力(ばりき)がある。
That person has a lot of energy.
試験(しけん)まであと一週間(いっしゅうかん)だから、馬力(ばりき)をかけて勉強(べんきょう)しよう。
There's only one week until the exam, so let's study with full force.

Compound of 馬(ば) (horse) + 力(りき) (power).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 馬力(ばりき)をかける (put in full effort, go full steam)
  • 馬力(ばりき)がある (have energy/stamina)
  • 200馬力(ばりき) (200 horsepower)

The figurative sense (sense 2) is common in everyday conversation. 馬力(ばりき)をかける is an idiomatic expression meaning to put all one's energy into something.
