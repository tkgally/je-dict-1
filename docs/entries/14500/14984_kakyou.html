佳境(かきょう)

かきょう
noun
climax; the best part; most interesting stage
1. climax; the best part; the most interesting stage
The point where something reaches its most exciting, interesting, or critical phase. Used for stories, projects, seasons, and competitions.
物語(ものがたり)は佳境(かきょう)に入(はい)った。
The story has reached its climax.
試合(しあい)は佳境(かきょう)を迎(むか)え、観客(かんきゃく)は息(いき)をのんだ。
The match reached its climax and the audience held their breath.
プロジェクトがいよいよ佳境(かきょう)に入(はい)り、チーム全員(ぜんいん)が残業(ざんぎょう)を続(つづ)けている。
The project has finally entered its critical phase, and the entire team is working overtime.

Originally 佳(か) meant 'beautiful' and 境(きょう) meant 'place,' so 佳境(かきょう) literally meant 'a beautiful place' or 'scenic spot.' The modern meaning has shifted to the most interesting or critical phase of something.

Almost always appears in the fixed expressions 佳境(かきょう)に入(はい)る (to enter the climax) or 佳境(かきょう)を迎(むか)える (to reach the climax).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 佳境(かきょう)に入(はい)る (to enter the climax / critical phase)
  • 佳境(かきょう)を迎(むか)える (to reach the climax)
  • いよいよ佳境(かきょう) (finally reaching the best part)
