青(あお)ざめる

あおざめる
verb-ichidan
to turn pale; to go white
1. to turn pale; to go white; to blanch
To lose color in the face due to fear, shock, illness, or extreme emotion. The face takes on a pale, bloodless appearance. Almost always describes a visible physical reaction to something alarming.
彼(かれ)は恐怖(きょうふ)で青(あお)ざめた。
He turned pale with fear.
事故(じこ)の知(し)らせを聞(き)いて、彼女(かのじょ)の顔(かお)が青(あお)ざめた。
Her face went pale when she heard the news of the accident.
青(あお)ざめた顔(かお)で病院(びょういん)から出(で)てきた彼(かれ)を見(み)て、何(なに)かあったのだと悟(さと)った。
Seeing him come out of the hospital with a pale face, I realized something had happened.

USAGE:
青(あお)ざめる is most often used with 顔(かお) (face) as the subject, or with the person as the subject (implying their face). The past participle 青(あお)ざめた is frequently used as a modifier: 青(あお)ざめた顔(かお) (a pale face).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 顔(かお)が青(あお)ざめる (one's face turns pale)
  • 恐怖(きょうふ)で青(あお)ざめる (to turn pale with fear)
  • 青(あお)ざめた顔(かお) (a pale/ashen face)
  • 青(あお)ざめた表情(ひょうじょう) (a pale expression)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 青(あお)ざめる: to turn pale (face loses color; fear/shock)
  • 青白(あおじろ)い: pale, pallid (adjective describing complexion)
  • 血(ち)の気(け)が引(ひ)く: the blood drains from one's face (stronger expression)
