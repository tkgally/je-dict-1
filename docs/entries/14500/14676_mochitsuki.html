餅(もち)つき

もちつき
noun
rice cake pounding
1. rice cake pounding; mochi making
The traditional process of making mochi by pounding steamed glutinous rice with a large wooden mallet in a mortar. A festive activity associated with the New Year season.
年末(ねんまつ)に餅(もち)つきをした。
We did mochi pounding at the end of the year.
町内(ちょうない)会(かい)で餅(もち)つき大会(たいかい)が開(ひら)かれた。
A mochi-pounding event was held by the neighborhood association.
子供(こども)たちは初(はじ)めての餅(もち)つき体験(たいけん)に大(おお)はしゃぎだった。
The children were thrilled by their first mochi-pounding experience.

A traditional New Year preparation activity. Steamed 餅米(もちごめ) (glutinous rice) is placed in an 臼(うす) (mortar) and pounded with a 杵(きね) (wooden mallet). One person pounds while another turns and wets the rice — this teamwork is called 合(あ)いの手(て).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 餅(もち)つきをする (to pound mochi)
  • 餅(もち)つき大会(たいかい) (mochi-pounding event)
  • 餅(もち)つき体験(たいけん) (mochi-pounding experience)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Traditionally done around December 28 or 30 for New Year preparations. Pounding on the 29th is avoided because 29 can be read as 苦(く) (suffering). Community 餅(もち)つき events remain popular, especially for children.
