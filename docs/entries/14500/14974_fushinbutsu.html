不審物(ふしんぶつ)

ふしんぶつ
noun
suspicious object; unattended item
1. suspicious object; unattended item
An unidentified object left in a public place that may pose a security threat. Commonly announced in stations, airports, and public facilities.
不審物(ふしんぶつ)を見(み)つけたら通報(つうほう)してください。
If you find a suspicious object, please report it.
駅(えき)の構内(こうない)で不審物(ふしんぶつ)が発見(はっけん)された。
A suspicious object was found inside the station.
不審物(ふしんぶつ)の通報(つうほう)を受(う)け、警察(けいさつ)が現場(げんば)を封鎖(ふうさ)して調(しら)べたところ、中身(なかみ)は衣類(いるい)だった。
After receiving a report of a suspicious object, police cordoned off the area and investigated, finding that the contents were just clothing.

A compound of 不審(ふしん) (suspicious, questionable) and 物(ぶつ) (object, thing).

Very common in Japanese public safety announcements. Train stations, airports, and public buildings regularly broadcast reminders about 不審物(ふしんぶつ).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 不審者(ふしんしゃ) means a suspicious person
  • 放置(ほうち)荷物(にもつ) means an unattended bag (less alarming tone)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不審物(ふしんぶつ)を発見(はっけん)する (to discover a suspicious object)
  • 不審物(ふしんぶつ)に触(さわ)らないでください (please don't touch suspicious objects)
  • 不審物(ふしんぶつ)の撤去(てっきょ) (removal of a suspicious object)
