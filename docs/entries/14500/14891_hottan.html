発端(ほったん)

ほったん
noun
origin; beginning; starting point
1. origin; beginning; starting point; catalyst
The event or circumstance that triggered or started something, especially a conflict, problem, or series of events.
事件(じけん)の発端(ほったん)は些細(ささい)な口論(こうろん)だった。
The incident was triggered by a trivial argument.
それが二人(ふたり)の出会(であ)いの発端(ほったん)だった。
That was the beginning of their meeting.
この問題(もんだい)の発端(ほったん)は何年(なんねん)も前(まえ)にさかのぼる。
The origin of this problem goes back many years.

発端(ほったん) emphasizes the triggering event or the very beginning of something. It is commonly used when tracing back to the cause or origin of events, especially problems or conflicts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～が発端(ほったん)だ (something was the starting point)
  • ～の発端(ほったん) (the origin of something)
  • ～を発端(ほったん)に (starting from; triggered by)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 事件(じけん)の発端(ほったん) (the origin of an incident)
  • 問題(もんだい)の発端(ほったん) (the root of a problem)
  • 紛争(ふんそう)の発端(ほったん) (the trigger for a conflict)

RELATED WORDS:
