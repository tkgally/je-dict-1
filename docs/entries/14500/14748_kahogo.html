過保護(かほご)

かほご
adjective-na
overprotective; spoiling
1. overprotective; spoiling; coddling
Protecting or caring for someone (especially a child) to an excessive degree, to the point where it hinders their independence or growth. Almost always carries a negative or critical nuance.
うちの親(おや)は過保護(かほご)だ。
My parents are overprotective.
過保護(かほご)に育(そだ)てると、子供(こども)の自立(じりつ)が遅(おく)れる。
If you raise children in an overprotective way, their independence will be delayed.
心配(しんぱい)する気持(きも)ちは分(わ)かるが、あまり過保護(かほご)にするのもよくない。
I understand the feeling of worry, but being too overprotective isn't good either.

Literally 過(か) (excessive) + 保護(ほご) (protection). Almost always used critically to describe parenting that goes too far. Can also be used as a noun.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 過保護(かほご)な親(おや) (overprotective parent)
  • 過保護(かほご)に育(そだ)てる (to raise overprotectively)
  • 過保護(かほご)にする (to be overprotective toward)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 過干渉(かかんしょう) — over-interfering (more about controlling behavior than shielding)
  • モンスターペアレント — monster parent (aggressive toward schools/institutions)
  • ヘリコプターペアレント — helicopter parent (borrowed from English)
