避(よ)ける

よける
verb, ichidan
to dodge; to step aside; to get out of the way
1. to dodge; to step aside; to get out of the way
To physically move out of the path of something approaching, such as a vehicle, thrown object, or oncoming person.
ボールを避(よ)ける。
Dodge a ball.
車(くるま)が来(き)たので、道(みち)の端(はし)に避(よ)けた。
A car was coming, so I stepped to the side of the road.
水(みず)たまりを避(よ)けながら歩(ある)いていたら、後(うし)ろから自転車(じてんしゃ)が来(き)て慌(あわ)てて避(よ)けた。
While walking and stepping around puddles, a bicycle came from behind and I hurriedly jumped out of the way.

USAGE NOTES:
避(よ)ける (ichidan verb, reading よける) focuses on the physical act of dodging or stepping aside. The same kanji 避(さ)ける (also ichidan, reading さける) means to avoid or keep away from in a broader sense — avoiding people, topics, risks, etc.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 避(さ)ける (さける) — to avoid in general (abstract or physical); broader meaning
  • 避(よ)ける (よける) — specifically to dodge or physically get out of the way; more concrete
  • 躱(かわ)す — to dodge skillfully, often used in martial arts and sports

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AをBに避(よ)ける is not used; instead: Aを避(よ)ける (dodge A)
  • 道(みち)を避(よ)ける (get out of the way)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ボールを避(よ)ける (dodge a ball)
  • 車(くるま)を避(よ)ける (get out of the way of a car)
  • 水(みず)たまりを避(よ)ける (step around a puddle)
