露天(ろてん)

ろてん
noun
open air; outdoors
1. open air; outdoors; roofless
Being outdoors without a roof or covering. Often used as a modifier to describe outdoor baths, mining operations, or other activities done under the open sky.
露天(ろてん)風呂(ぶろ)に入(はい)る。
Take an outdoor bath.
この旅館(りょかん)は露天(ろてん)風呂(ぶろ)付(つ)きの部屋(へや)がある。
This inn has rooms with private outdoor baths.
星空(ほしぞら)の下(した)で露天(ろてん)風呂(ぶろ)に浸(つ)かるのは格別(かくべつ)な気持(きも)ちだ。
Soaking in an outdoor bath under a starry sky is an exceptional feeling.

Most commonly encountered in the compound 露天(ろてん)風呂(ぶろ) (open-air bath), a major attraction at Japanese hot springs (温泉(おんせん)) and inns (旅館(りょかん)).

Do not confuse with the homophone 露店(ろてん) (street stall/vendor), which uses a different kanji for the second character (店(てん) = shop vs 天(てん) = sky/heaven).

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 露天(ろてん)風呂(ぶろ) (open-air bath)
  • 露天(ろてん)掘(ぼ)り (open-pit mining)
  • 露天(ろてん)駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう) (open-air parking lot)
