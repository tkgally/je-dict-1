追(お)い抜(ぬ)く

おいぬく
verb-godan
to pass; to overtake; to outstrip
1. to pass; to overtake
To catch up with and move ahead of someone or something that was in front. Used for physical movement like running or driving.
前(まえ)の車(くるま)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)いた。
I passed the car in front.
ゴール手前(てまえ)で一気(いっき)に三人(さんにん)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)いた。
I overtook three people all at once just before the finish line.
狭(せま)い道(みち)で無理(むり)に追(お)い抜(ぬ)こうとすると危(あぶ)ない。
It's dangerous to try to pass on a narrow road.
2. to outstrip; to surpass
To exceed someone in performance, ranking, or achievement. Used figuratively for competition, statistics, or progress.
売上(うりあげ)で先輩(せんぱい)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)いた。
I surpassed my senior colleague in sales.
この国(くに)の人口(じんこう)はやがて隣国(りんごく)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)くだろう。
This country's population will eventually surpass that of its neighbor.
後発(こうはつ)メーカーが技術力(ぎじゅつりょく)で大手(おおて)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)く例(れい)は珍(めずら)しくない。
It's not uncommon for latecoming manufacturers to surpass major companies in technical capability.

Godan verb (五段(ごだん)). Transitive — takes を for the person or thing being overtaken.

ASPECT:

  • 追(お)い抜(ぬ)いた (completed the overtaking)
  • 追(お)い抜(ぬ)いている (has overtaken; is in a state of having passed)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 追(お)い越(こ)す means the same but is slightly more common in everyday speech
  • 追(お)い抜(ぬ)く emphasizes fully passing and leaving behind, while 追(お)い越(こ)す can suggest just moving ahead momentarily

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 車(くるま)を追(お)い抜(ぬ)く (to pass a car)
  • 一気(いっき)に追(お)い抜(ぬ)く (to overtake in one burst)
  • 売上(うりあげ)で追(お)い抜(ぬ)く (to surpass in sales)
