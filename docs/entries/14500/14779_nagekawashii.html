嘆(なげ)かわしい

なげかわしい
i-adjective
deplorable; lamentable; regrettable
1. deplorable; lamentable; regrettable
Describes a situation or state of affairs that is deeply regrettable, sad, or worthy of lamentation. Expresses the speaker's sorrow or disapproval about something they find distressing.
嘆(なげ)かわしい事態(じたい)だ。
It's a deplorable situation.
マナーの悪(わる)さが嘆(なげ)かわしい。
The poor manners are lamentable.
若者(わかもの)が歴史(れきし)に関心(かんしん)を持(も)たないのは嘆(なげ)かわしいことだ。
It is lamentable that young people have no interest in history.

A literary and somewhat formal i-adjective derived from the verb 嘆(なげ)く (to lament, to grieve). It expresses the speaker's deep sense of regret or sorrow about an unsatisfactory state of affairs.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 嘆(なげ)かわしい事態(じたい) (deplorable situation)
  • 嘆(なげ)かわしい現状(げんじょう) (regrettable current state)
  • 嘆(なげ)かわしいことだ (it is lamentable)

USAGE NOTE:
Often used in editorials, speeches, and formal commentary. The speaker is not merely stating a fact but expressing personal dismay. Compared to 悲(かな)しい, which focuses on personal sadness, 嘆(なげ)かわしい conveys moral or social disapproval.
