集結(しゅうけつ)

しゅうけつ
noun, verb-suru
gathering; massing; assembling
1. gathering; massing; assembling
People, forces, or resources coming together in one place, often with a sense of purpose or urgency. Commonly used for troops, demonstrators, or talent.
軍隊(ぐんたい)が国境(こっきょう)に集結(しゅうけつ)した。
Troops massed at the border.
全国(ぜんこく)から精鋭(せいえい)が集結(しゅうけつ)するトーナメントだ。
It's a tournament where top players gather from all over the country.
抗議(こうぎ)のために数千人(すうせんにん)のデモ隊(たい)が広場(ひろば)に集結(しゅうけつ)し、政府(せいふ)に改革(かいかく)を訴(うった)えた。
Thousands of demonstrators gathered at the plaza to demand reforms from the government.

Implies a purposeful, often large-scale gathering. Stronger and more dramatic than 集(あつ)まる. Often used in military, political, or competitive contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 軍(ぐん)が集結(しゅうけつ)する (troops mass)
  • 人(ひと)が集結(しゅうけつ)する (people assemble)
  • 精鋭(せいえい)が集結(しゅうけつ)する (top talent gathers)
  • 一堂(いちどう)に集結(しゅうけつ)する (assemble all in one place)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 集合(しゅうごう) is a neutral word for meeting up or assembling
  • 集結(しゅうけつ) implies purpose, scale, or intensity
  • 終結(しゅうけつ) (same reading) means conclusion or ending — do not confuse
