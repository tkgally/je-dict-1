弾力(だんりょく)

だんりょく
noun
elasticity; flexibility; resilience
1. elasticity; springiness; bounce
The physical property of a material that allows it to return to its original shape after being stretched, compressed, or deformed.
この生地(きじ)は弾力(だんりょく)がある。
This fabric has elasticity.
肌(はだ)の弾力(だんりょく)を保(たも)つためにスキンケアをしている。
I do skincare to maintain the elasticity of my skin.
この餅(もち)は弾力(だんりょく)があって食(た)べごたえがある。
This mochi has a nice springy texture and is satisfying to eat.
2. flexibility; adaptability
The ability of a system, plan, or policy to adapt to changing circumstances. Used figuratively in business, policy, and organizational contexts.
弾力(だんりょく)のある運用(うんよう)が求(もと)められる。
Flexible management is called for.
状況(じょうきょう)に応(おう)じて弾力的(だんりょくてき)に対応(たいおう)していく方針(ほうしん)だ。
The policy is to respond flexibly according to the situation.

USAGE:
弾力(だんりょく) has both physical (sense 1) and figurative (sense 2) uses. In everyday conversation, it often describes the texture of food or skin. In formal and business contexts, 弾力的(だんりょくてき) (the na-adjective form) is frequently used to describe flexible policies or responses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 弾力(だんりょく)がある (to have elasticity/springiness)
  • 弾力(だんりょく)を失(うしな)う (to lose elasticity)
  • 弾力的(だんりょくてき)な運用(うんよう) (flexible management)
  • 弾力的(だんりょくてき)に対応(たいおう)する (to respond flexibly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 弾力(だんりょく): elasticity (physical bounce; figurative flexibility)
  • 柔軟性(じゅうなんせい): flexibility, suppleness (broader concept)
  • 伸縮性(しんしゅくせい): stretchability (specific to materials that stretch)
  • 弾性(だんせい): elasticity (technical/physics term)
