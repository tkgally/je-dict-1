食(く)いしばる

くいしばる
verb-godan
to clench (one's teeth); to grit one's teeth
1. to clench one's teeth; to grit one's teeth
To press the upper and lower teeth tightly together. Used both literally (physical clenching) and figuratively to describe enduring pain, hardship, or frustration with determination.
歯(は)を食(く)いしばって耐(た)えた。
I gritted my teeth and endured it.
痛(いた)みに歯(は)を食(く)いしばりながら、ゴールまで走(はし)り続(つづ)けた。
Gritting my teeth against the pain, I kept running to the finish line.
悔(くや)しくて涙(なみだ)が出(で)そうだったが、歯(は)を食(く)いしばって泣(な)かなかった。
I was so frustrated I nearly cried, but I gritted my teeth and held back the tears.

USAGE:
Almost always used in the phrase 歯(は)を食(く)いしばる. The figurative meaning of enduring hardship with determination is very common and carries a strong emotional tone of perseverance.

CONJUGATION:
Godan verb (る → ら/り/る/れ/ろ): 食(く)いしばらない, 食(く)いしばります, 食(く)いしばる, 食(く)いしばれば, 食(く)いしばろう

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばる (to clench/grit one's teeth)
  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばって耐(た)える (to grit one's teeth and endure)
  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばって頑張(がんば)る (to grit one's teeth and persevere)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 食(く)いしばる: to clench teeth (endurance, determination)
  • 噛(か)みしめる: to bite down on; to savor (can be physical or emotional)
  • 我慢(がまん)する: to endure, to put up with (broader, no physical image)
