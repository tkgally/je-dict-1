魔法(まほう)

まほう
noun
magic; sorcery
1. magic; sorcery; witchcraft
Supernatural power to make impossible things happen. Used for both fictional magic and figuratively for something seemingly miraculous.
魔法(まほう)をかける。
Cast a spell.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、魔法(まほう)が使(つか)えたらいいなと思(おも)っていた。
When I was a child, I wished I could use magic.
まるで魔法(まほう)のように痛(いた)みが消(き)えた。
The pain disappeared as if by magic.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魔法(まほう)をかける (cast a spell)
  • 魔法(まほう)が解(と)ける (a spell is broken)
  • 魔法(まほう)を使(つか)う (use magic)
  • 魔法(まほう)のように (as if by magic)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 魔法使(まほうつか)い (magician, wizard)
  • 魔法(まほう)の杖(つえ) (magic wand)
  • 魔法瓶(まほうびん) (thermos — literally "magic bottle")

Often appears in fantasy fiction and fairy tales. 魔法瓶(まほうびん) (thermos) is a notable everyday compound that uses this word.
