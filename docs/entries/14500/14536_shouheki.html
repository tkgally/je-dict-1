障壁(しょうへき)

しょうへき
noun
barrier; wall; obstacle
1. barrier; wall; obstacle
A physical or figurative wall that blocks progress or separates things. Used for trade barriers, language barriers, social obstacles, and literal walls or partitions.
言葉(ことば)の障壁(しょうへき)を乗(の)り越(こ)える。
To overcome the language barrier.
関税(かんぜい)は貿易(ぼうえき)の障壁(しょうへき)となっている。
Tariffs have become a barrier to trade.
新規(しんき)参入(さんにゅう)を妨(さまた)げる障壁(しょうへき)を取(と)り除(のぞ)くことが必要(ひつよう)だ。
It is necessary to remove barriers that prevent new entrants.

Compound of 障(しょう) (to obstruct) + 壁(へき) (wall). Used in both concrete and abstract senses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 障壁(しょうへき)を乗(の)り越(こ)える (to overcome a barrier)
  • 障壁(しょうへき)を取(と)り除(のぞ)く (to remove a barrier)
  • 言葉(ことば)の障壁(しょうへき) (language barrier)
  • 貿易(ぼうえき)障壁(しょうへき) (trade barrier)
  • 心理的(しんりてき)障壁(しょうへき) (psychological barrier)

SIMILAR WORDS:
壁(かべ) (wall) is more colloquial and can also mean "barrier" figuratively. 障壁(しょうへき) is more formal and tends to appear in written or official contexts. 障害(しょうがい) (obstacle/impediment) is broader and includes non-wall-like hindrances.
