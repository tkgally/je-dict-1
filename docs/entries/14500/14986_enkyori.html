遠距離(えんきょり)

えんきょり
noun
long distance; long range
1. long distance; long range; far away
A great physical distance between two places. Used for commuting, relationships, travel, and also in military/technical contexts for range.
遠距離(えんきょり)通勤(つうきん)は体(からだ)に堪(こた)える。
A long commute takes a toll on the body.
遠距離(えんきょり)恋愛(れんあい)を三年(さんねん)続(つづ)けている。
I've been in a long-distance relationship for three years.
遠距離(えんきょり)からでも鮮明(せんめい)に撮影(さつえい)できるカメラが開発(かいはつ)された。
A camera was developed that can take clear photos from a long distance.

A compound of 遠(えん) (far) and 距離(きょり) (distance). The opposite is 近距離(きんきょり) (short distance).

Very commonly used as a modifier before other nouns.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 遠距離(えんきょり)恋愛(れんあい) (long-distance relationship, often abbreviated as 遠(えん)キョリ or 遠恋(えんれん))
  • 遠距離(えんきょり)通勤(つうきん) (long commute)
  • 遠距離(えんきょり)通学(つうがく) (commuting a long distance to school)
  • 遠距離(えんきょり)攻撃(こうげき) (long-range attack)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 長距離(ちょうきょり) also means 'long distance' but is more commonly used for races and transportation (e.g., 長距離(ちょうきょり)バス)
