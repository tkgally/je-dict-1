鍵盤(けんばん)

けんばん
noun
keyboard (musical); keys (of a piano)
1. keyboard; keys (of a piano or organ)
The set of keys on a musical instrument such as a piano, organ, or synthesizer. Also used to refer to keyboard instruments in general.
ピアノの鍵盤(けんばん)に触(ふ)れた。
I touched the piano keys.
この電子(でんし)ピアノは88鍵盤(けんばん)ある。
This digital piano has 88 keys.
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、音楽(おんがく)の授業(じゅぎょう)で鍵盤(けんばん)ハーモニカを吹(ふ)いた。
As a child, I played the melodica in music class.

Primarily refers to musical instrument keys, not computer keyboards (which are キーボード). However, 鍵盤(けんばん) can sometimes refer to a keyboard instrument itself in casual speech.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鍵盤(けんばん)楽器(がっき) (keyboard instrument)
  • 鍵盤(けんばん)ハーモニカ (melodica — standard in Japanese elementary school music education)
  • 88鍵盤(けんばん) (88 keys — full piano)
  • 白(しろ)鍵(けん) / 黒(くろ)鍵(けん) (white key / black key — individual keys)

The 鍵盤(けんばん)ハーモニカ is a ubiquitous part of Japanese elementary education. Nearly every Japanese person has played one in school.
