伸(の)びしろ

のびしろ
noun
room for growth; potential for improvement
1. room for growth; potential for improvement; upside
The amount of potential remaining for someone or something to grow, improve, or develop further. Often used to describe people's abilities or a market's untapped potential.
まだまだ伸(の)びしろがある。
There is still plenty of room for growth.
この選手(せんしゅ)は伸(の)びしろが大(おお)きい。
This athlete has great potential for improvement.
若(わか)い社員(しゃいん)の伸(の)びしろに期待(きたい)して、積極的(せっきょくてき)に仕事(しごと)を任(まか)せている。
Expecting growth potential in young employees, we actively entrust them with work.

伸(の)びしろ is a relatively modern word that has become very popular. It combines 伸(の)びる (to grow, to extend) with しろ (margin, allowance — the same しろ as in 糊(のり)しろ, the margin for gluing).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伸(の)びしろがある (to have room for growth)
  • 伸(の)びしろが大(おお)きい (to have great growth potential)
  • 伸(の)びしろに期待(きたい)する (to look forward to someone's growth potential)

USAGE NOTES:
Frequently used in sports, business, and education contexts to describe someone or something that has not yet reached full potential. It carries a positive connotation — emphasizing opportunity rather than current shortcomings. Unlike 可能性(かのうせい) (possibility), which is general, 伸(の)びしろ specifically implies measurable room for improvement from the current level.
