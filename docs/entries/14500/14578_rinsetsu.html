隣接(りんせつ)

りんせつ
noun, verb-suru
adjacent; adjoining; neighboring
1. adjacent; adjoining; neighboring
Being next to or sharing a border with something. Used for physical proximity of places, areas, or buildings.
公園(こうえん)に隣接(りんせつ)した土地(とち)。
Land adjacent to the park.
ホテルは駅(えき)に隣接(りんせつ)しているので便利(べんり)だ。
The hotel is right next to the station, so it's convenient.
隣接(りんせつ)する二(ふた)つの県(けん)が共同(きょうどう)で防災(ぼうさい)訓練(くんれん)を実施(じっし)した。
Two neighboring prefectures jointly conducted disaster preparedness drills.

USAGE:
隣接(りんせつ) is a formal, written-style word meaning physically adjacent or sharing a border. It is commonly used in real estate, geography, and urban planning.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • AはBに隣接(りんせつ)している (A is adjacent to B)
  • Bに隣接(りんせつ)したA (A which is adjacent to B)
  • 隣接(りんせつ)するA (adjoining A)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 隣接(りんせつ)する地域(ちいき) (adjacent area)
  • 隣接(りんせつ)する国(くに) (neighboring country)
  • ～に隣接(りんせつ)している (to be adjacent to ~)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 隣接(りんせつ): adjacent, adjoining (formal; sharing a border)
  • 隣(となり): next door, neighboring (everyday, casual)
  • 近隣(きんりん): neighborhood, vicinity (nearby area in general)
