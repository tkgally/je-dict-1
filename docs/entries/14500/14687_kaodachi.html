顔立(かおだ)ち

かおだち
noun
facial features
1. facial features; looks; face
The arrangement and characteristics of a person's facial features, including the shape of the eyes, nose, mouth, and overall facial structure. Often used when describing someone's appearance.
整(ととの)った顔立(かおだ)ちの人(ひと)だ。
That person has well-defined facial features.
母親(ははおや)に似(に)た顔立(かおだ)ちをしている。
They have facial features similar to their mother's.
彫(ほ)りの深(ふか)い顔立(かおだ)ちで、外国人(がいこくじん)に間違(まちが)えられることがある。
With deep-set facial features, they sometimes get mistaken for a foreigner.

Focuses specifically on the structure and arrangement of facial features, not the face as a body part (顔(かお)).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 整(ととの)った顔立(かおだ)ち (well-proportioned features)
  • 彫(ほ)りの深(ふか)い顔立(かおだ)ち (deep-set/chiseled features)
  • はっきりした顔立(かおだ)ち (distinct features)
  • 優(やさ)しい顔立(かおだ)ち (gentle features)
  • ～に似(に)た顔立(かおだ)ち (features resembling ~)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 顔(かお) is the general word for face
  • 容貌(ようぼう) / 風貌(ふうぼう) are more literary terms for overall appearance
  • 顔立(かおだ)ち focuses on the physical structure of the face
