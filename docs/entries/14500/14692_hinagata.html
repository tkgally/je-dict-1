雛形(ひながた)

ひながた
noun
model; template; pattern
1. model; miniature; prototype
A small-scale model or miniature version of something, used as a reference or sample.
城(しろ)の雛形(ひながた)を作(つく)った。
I made a model of the castle.
この建物(たてもの)の雛形(ひながた)は博物館(はくぶつかん)に展示(てんじ)されている。
A miniature model of this building is displayed in the museum.
江戸時代(えどじだい)の町(まち)の雛形(ひながた)が精巧(せいこう)に再現(さいげん)されていて、見(み)ごたえがあった。
A miniature of an Edo-period town was recreated in exquisite detail, and it was well worth seeing.
2. template; standard form; boilerplate
A pre-made format or standard document used as a starting point. Common in business and administrative contexts for contracts, letters, and other documents.
契約書(けいやくしょ)の雛形(ひながた)をください。
Please give me a template for the contract.
この雛形(ひながた)を参考(さんこう)にして書類(しょるい)を作成(さくせい)してください。
Please use this template as a reference when preparing the documents.
会社(かいしゃ)では報告書(ほうこくしょ)の雛形(ひながた)が用意(ようい)されているので、新人(しんじん)でもすぐに書(か)ける。
The company provides report templates, so even new employees can write them right away.

Originally from 雛(ひな) (chick; miniature) + 形(かた) (shape), suggesting a small copy of the real thing.

SENSE 1 (model):
Refers to physical miniatures or scale models. This is the older, more traditional meaning.

SENSE 2 (template):
The dominant modern usage, especially in business. Refers to standardized document formats.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 契約書(けいやくしょ)の雛形(ひながた) (contract template)
  • 報告書(ほうこくしょ)の雛形(ひながた) (report template)
  • 雛形(ひながた)を作(つく)る (create a template/model)

SIMILAR WORDS:
テンプレート is the loanword equivalent for sense 2 and is increasingly common, especially in IT contexts. 見本(みほん) (sample) overlaps with sense 1 but emphasizes something shown as an example rather than a scaled-down replica.
