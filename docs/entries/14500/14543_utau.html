謳(うた)う

うたう
verb-godan
to extol; to advocate; to stipulate
1. to extol; to praise; to celebrate
To praise or celebrate something publicly, often in writing or speech. Used when something is widely acclaimed or touted.
自由(じゆう)と平等(びょうどう)を謳(うた)う。
To extol freedom and equality.
この商品(しょうひん)は安全性(あんぜんせい)を謳(うた)っている。
This product touts its safety.
世界一(せかいいち)の品質(ひんしつ)を謳(うた)う広告(こうこく)には注意(ちゅうい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
You need to be careful with advertisements that boast world-class quality.
2. to stipulate; to set forth; to state
To clearly state or lay out provisions in a law, regulation, contract, or similar document. A formal, written usage.
憲法(けんぽう)は基本的(きほんてき)人権(じんけん)を謳(うた)っている。
The constitution sets forth fundamental human rights.
契約書(けいやくしょ)に謳(うた)われた条件(じょうけん)を確認(かくにん)してください。
Please confirm the conditions stipulated in the contract.
法律(ほうりつ)に謳(うた)われている権利(けんり)はすべての国民(こくみん)に保障(ほしょう)される。
The rights set forth in the law are guaranteed to all citizens.

Not to be confused with 歌(うた)う (to sing). 謳(うた)う is used for proclaiming or stipulating, not for singing songs.

SENSE 1 (extol):
Often used in advertising, political speech, and media to describe something being publicly praised or promoted.

SENSE 2 (stipulate):
Appears in legal, constitutional, and formal contexts. The passive form 謳(うた)われている is very common.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自由(じゆう)を謳(うた)う (to extol freedom)
  • 憲法(けんぽう)に謳(うた)われている (stipulated in the constitution)
  • 理念(りねん)を謳(うた)う (to advocate a principle)
