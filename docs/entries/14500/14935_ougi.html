奥義(おうぎ)

おうぎ
noun
secret teachings; mysteries; essence of an art
1. secret teachings; mysteries; hidden essence of an art
The deepest, most advanced principles of an art, discipline, or skill. Originally referred to the inner teachings of martial arts or traditional arts, now also used more broadly for mastering the core of any field.
師匠(ししょう)から奥義(おうぎ)を学(まな)んだ。
I learned the secret teachings from my master.
料理(りょうり)の奥義(おうぎ)は素材(そざい)を生(い)かすことだ。
The essence of cooking is bringing out the best in the ingredients.
何十年(なんじゅうねん)も修行(しゅぎょう)して、ようやく剣術(けんじゅつ)の奥義(おうぎ)を極(きわ)めた。
After decades of training, he finally mastered the deepest secrets of swordsmanship.

A word rooted in traditional martial arts and performing arts. It suggests knowledge that is not easily acquired and can only be attained through long dedication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 奥義(おうぎ)を極(きわ)める (to master the deepest secrets)
  • 奥義(おうぎ)を伝(つた)える (to pass on secret teachings)
  • 奥義(おうぎ)を授(さず)かる (to receive secret teachings)

READING NOTE:
Also read おくぎ, but おうぎ is the more common and standard reading.
