車間距離(しゃかんきょり)

しゃかんきょり
noun
following distance; distance between vehicles
1. following distance; distance between vehicles
The space maintained between one's vehicle and the vehicle ahead while driving. A key concept in safe driving and traffic regulations.
車間距離(しゃかんきょり)を取(と)る。
Keep a safe following distance.
雨(あめ)の日(ひ)は車間距離(しゃかんきょり)を多(おお)めに取(と)りましょう。
On rainy days, keep a greater following distance.
高速道路(こうそくどうろ)で車間距離(しゃかんきょり)を詰(つ)めすぎると、追突事故(ついとつじこ)の原因(げんいん)になる。
Closing the following distance too much on the expressway can cause rear-end collisions.

USAGE NOTES:
車間距離(しゃかんきょり) is a compound of 車間(しゃかん) (between vehicles) and 距離(きょり) (distance). It is used frequently in driving instructions, traffic safety campaigns, and news reports about accidents.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 車間距離(しゃかんきょり)を取(と)る (maintain following distance)
  • 車間距離(しゃかんきょり)を詰(つ)める (close the gap between vehicles)
  • 車間距離(しゃかんきょり)が短(みじか)い (following distance is too short)
  • 十分(じゅうぶん)な車間距離(しゃかんきょり) (sufficient following distance)

DRIVING NOTE:
In Japan, the recommended 車間距離(しゃかんきょり) on expressways is the distance traveled in about 2 seconds. Tailgating (煽(あお)り運転(うんてん)) has been a significant traffic safety issue and was made a criminal offense in 2020.
