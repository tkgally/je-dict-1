金物(かなもの)

かなもの
noun
hardware; metal fittings; ironware
1. hardware; metal fittings; ironware; metalwork
Metal tools, fittings, and household implements. Includes items such as nails, hinges, knives, pots, and other metal goods.
金物(かなもの)屋(や)でネジを買(か)った。
I bought screws at the hardware store.
建築(けんちく)に使(つか)う金物(かなもの)を揃(そろ)える。
Gather the metal fittings needed for construction.
この町(まち)は昔(むかし)から金物(かなもの)の産地(さんち)として知(し)られ、包丁(ほうちょう)やハサミの製造(せいぞう)が盛(さか)んだ。
This town has been known as a center of metalwork since long ago, with thriving production of knives and scissors.

A general term for metal goods, tools, and fittings. The 金物(かなもの)屋(や) (hardware store) is a traditional type of shop in Japan.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 金物(かなもの)屋(や) (hardware store)
  • 金物(かなもの)店(てん) (hardware shop)
  • 建築(けんちく)金物(かなもの) (construction hardware)
  • 金物(かなもの)の産地(さんち) (metalwork production area)

Famous 金物(かなもの) production areas in Japan include 三木(みき) (Miki, Hyogo) for tools and 燕三条(つばめさんじょう) (Tsubame-Sanjo, Niigata) for cutlery and kitchen items.
