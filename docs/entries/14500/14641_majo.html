魔女(まじょ)

まじょ
noun
witch
1. witch; sorceress
A woman believed to possess magical powers. Used in fairy tales, fantasy, and historical contexts. Can also refer figuratively to a bewitchingly attractive or intimidating woman.
魔女(まじょ)が呪(のろ)いをかけた。
The witch cast a curse.
魔女(まじょ)の宅急便(たっきゅうびん)はジブリの名作(めいさく)だ。
Kiki's Delivery Service is a Ghibli masterpiece.
ヨーロッパでは中世(ちゅうせい)に多(おお)くの女性(じょせい)が魔女(まじょ)として裁(さば)かれた。
In Europe during the Middle Ages, many women were tried as witches.

Compound of 魔(ま) (demon/evil) + 女(じょ) (woman).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 魔女(まじょ)狩(が)り (witch hunt)
  • 魔女(まじょ)裁判(さいばん) (witch trial)
  • 魔女(まじょ)の宅急便(たっきゅうびん) (Kiki's Delivery Service)

A familiar word to Japanese pop culture fans through anime and manga. In figurative use, may describe a woman who seems mysteriously youthful — 美魔女(びまじょ) (beautiful witch) is a popular term for women who look much younger than their age.
