嘲笑(あざわら)う

あざわらう
verb, godan
to sneer at; to ridicule; to mock
1. to sneer at; to ridicule; to mock
To laugh at someone or something with contempt or scorn. Stronger and more hostile than simply laughing at someone.
彼(かれ)の失敗(しっぱい)を嘲笑(あざわら)った。
They sneered at his failure.
人(ひと)の努力(どりょく)を嘲笑(あざわら)うべきではない。
You shouldn't mock other people's efforts.
周囲(しゅうい)に嘲笑(あざわら)われても、彼女(かのじょ)は自分(じぶん)の夢(ゆめ)を諦(あきら)めなかった。
Even though those around her ridiculed her, she did not give up on her dream.

嘲笑(あざわら)う is a transitive godan verb. It carries a strongly negative nuance — contemptuous or scornful laughter directed at someone.

ASPECT (ている):
嘲笑(あざわら)っている describes the ongoing act of mocking someone.

PARTICLE PATTERNS:

  • ～を嘲笑(あざわら)う (to mock someone/something)
  • ～に嘲笑(あざわら)われる (to be ridiculed by someone — passive)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人(ひと)を嘲笑(あざわら)う (to mock a person)
  • 失敗(しっぱい)を嘲笑(あざわら)う (to sneer at a failure)
  • 嘲笑(あざわら)うような態度(たいど) (a mocking attitude)

RELATED WORDS:
笑(わら)う is neutral laughter, 馬鹿(ばか)にする is general making fun of. 嘲笑(あざわら)う is specifically contemptuous, scornful laughter — stronger and more literary than either.
