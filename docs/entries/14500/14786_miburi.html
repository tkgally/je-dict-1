身振(みぶ)り

みぶり
noun
gesture; body language
1. gesture; body language; physical movement to convey meaning
Movements of the body, especially the hands and arms, used to express or emphasize something. Often paired with 手振り in the set phrase 身振り手振り.
身振(みぶ)りで伝(つた)えた。
I communicated through gestures.
身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)りを交(まじ)えて説明(せつめい)した。
I explained using gestures and hand movements.
言葉(ことば)が通(つう)じなかったので、身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)りで道(みち)を教(おし)えてもらった。
Since we didn't share a language, they gave me directions using gestures.

Very commonly used in the compound phrase 身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)り, which emphasizes the use of the whole body and hands together to communicate.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)り (gestures and hand movements)
  • 身振(みぶ)りで伝(つた)える (to communicate through gestures)
  • 身振(みぶ)りを交(まじ)える (to use gestures along with speech)
  • 大(おお)きな身振(みぶ)り (exaggerated gestures)

USAGE NOTE:
While 身振(みぶ)り can be used alone, the paired form 身振(みぶ)り手振(てぶ)り is far more common in everyday speech. The compound ジェスチャー is also used, especially in casual contexts.
