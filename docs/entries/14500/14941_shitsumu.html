執務(しつむ)

しつむ
noun, suru verb
performing one's duties; office work
1. performing one's duties; office work; conducting official business
Carrying out one's professional or official duties, especially administrative or managerial work. Implies formal, responsible work in an office or government setting.
社長(しゃちょう)は執務室(しつむしつ)にいる。
The president is in the executive office.
執務(しつむ)時間(じかん)は午前(ごぜん)九時(くじ)から午後(ごご)五時(ごじ)までです。
Office hours are from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
大臣(だいじん)は海外(かいがい)出張中(しゅっちょうちゅう)も執務(しつむ)を続(つづ)けた。
The minister continued performing official duties even during the overseas trip.

A formal term most often associated with high-ranking officials, executives, or government work.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 執務室(しつむしつ) (office, executive office)
  • 執務(しつむ)時間(じかん) (office hours, working hours)
  • 執務(しつむ)にあたる (to attend to one's duties)

USAGE NOTE:
執務(しつむ) is more formal than 仕事(しごと) and implies duties with official responsibility. It is typically used for high-level positions — a company president, a mayor, or a government minister 執務(しつむ)する, while a regular employee simply 仕事(しごと)する.
