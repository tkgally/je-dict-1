申(もう)し送(おく)り

もうしおくり
noun
handover briefing; passing on information; report
1. handover briefing; passing on information
communicating important information from one person or shift to another, especially in workplaces
申(もう)し送(おく)りを忘(わす)れないでください。
Please don't forget to do the handover briefing.
夜勤(やきん)から日勤(にっきん)への申(もう)し送(おく)りがある。
There's a handover briefing from the night shift to the day shift.
患者(かんじゃ)の状態(じょうたい)を申(もう)し送(おく)りで伝(つた)えた。
I communicated the patient's condition during the handover.

FORMATION:
申(もう)す (humble: to say) + 送(おく)る (to send) → send a message/report

VERB FORM:
申(もう)し送(おく)る (to pass on information, to hand over)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 申(もう)し送(おく)り事項(じこう) (handover items)
  • 申(もう)し送(おく)りノート (handover notebook)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 引(ひ)き継(つ)ぎ (handover, succession - broader scope)
  • 連絡(れんらく) (communication, contact)
  • 報告(ほうこく) (report)

WORKPLACE USAGE:
申(もう)し送(おく)り is especially common in hospitals, care facilities, and factories with shift work. It ensures continuity of care or operations. 引(ひ)き継(つ)ぎ} tends to be used for longer-term handovers (job changes), while 申(もう)し送(おく)り is often for daily or shift-based information transfer.
