寝汗(ねあせ)

ねあせ
noun
night sweat
1. night sweat; sweating during sleep
perspiration that occurs while sleeping
寝汗(ねあせ)をかいた。
I broke out in a night sweat.
夏(なつ)は寝汗(ねあせ)がひどい。
Night sweats are bad in summer.
寝汗(ねあせ)で目(め)が覚(さ)めてパジャマを着替(きが)えた。
I woke up from night sweats and changed my pajamas.

USAGE:

  • 寝汗(ねあせ)をかく (to have night sweats)
  • 寝汗(ねあせ)がひどい (to have bad night sweats)

CAUSES:
Can be caused by hot weather, illness, fever, or stress. Persistent night sweats may indicate a medical condition.

RELATED:

  • 汗(あせ) (sweat)
  • 冷(ひ)や汗(あせ) (cold sweat - from fear/nervousness)
  • 脂汗(あぶらあせ) (greasy sweat - from pain/anxiety)
