貸(か)し切(き)り

かしきり
noun
reserved; chartered; exclusive rental; private hire
1. reserved; chartered; exclusive rental; private hire
renting something exclusively for one's own use, such as a bus, venue, or facility
貸(か)し切(き)りバスで遠足(えんそく)に行(い)く。
We're going on a field trip by chartered bus.
今日(きょう)はレストランを貸(か)し切(き)りにした。
We reserved the restaurant exclusively for today.
貸(か)し切(き)り露天風呂(ろてんぶろ)でのんびりした。
I relaxed in a private open-air bath.

FORMATION:
貸(か)す (to lend/rent) + 切(き)る (completely) → exclusive rental

COMMON USES:

  • 貸(か)し切(き)りバス (chartered bus)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)り電車(でんしゃ) (chartered train)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)り風呂(ふろ) (private bath)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)り会場(かいじょう) (private venue)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)りパーティー (private party)

VERB FORM:

  • 貸(か)し切(き)る (to charter, reserve exclusively)
  • 貸(か)し切(き)りにする (to make it a private rental)

USAGE:
Also used as a na-adjective: 貸(か)し切(き)りの (exclusively reserved)

ONSEN CONTEXT:
貸(か)し切(き)り風呂(ふろ) (private bath) is a popular service at Japanese hot spring resorts, allowing guests to use a bath privately.

RELATED:

  • チャーター (charter)
  • 予約(よやく) (reservation)
  • 専用(せんよう) (exclusive use)
