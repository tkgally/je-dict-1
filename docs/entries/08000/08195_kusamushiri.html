1. weeding; pulling up weeds
the activity of removing unwanted plants by hand
庭(にわ)の草(くさ)むしりをする。
I weed the garden.
草(くさ)むしりは腰(こし)が痛(いた)くなる。
Weeding makes my back hurt.
夏(なつ)は草(くさ)むしりが大変(たいへん)だ。
Weeding is tough in summer.

Compound of 草(くさ) (grass, weeds) + むしる (to pluck, pull out). Refers specifically to pulling weeds by hand.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 草(くさ)むしりをする: to weed, to pull weeds
  • 庭(にわ)の草(くさ)むしり: garden weeding
  • 草(くさ)むしりに疲(つか)れる: to get tired from weeding

CULTURAL NOTE:
A common household chore in Japan, especially during the humid summer months when weeds grow rapidly. School students are often assigned 草(くさ)むしり as a group activity on school grounds.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 草(くさ)取(と)り: weeding (alternative, slightly more casual term)
  • 除草(じょそう): weeding (formal, includes chemical methods)
  • 雑草(ざっそう): weeds (the plants themselves)
  • 草刈(くさか)り: mowing grass (uses tools, not pulling by hand)

Related Words

Related: 芝刈(しばか)り (lawn mowing)
