言(い)い掛(が)かり

いいがかり
noun
false accusation; unfounded complaint; pretext
1. false accusation; unfounded complaint; pretext
an unreasonable accusation or complaint made without proper basis, often to pick a fight or find fault
それは言(い)い掛(が)かりだ。
That's a baseless accusation.
言(い)い掛(が)かりをつけられて困(こま)った。
I was troubled by someone picking a fight with me.
酔(よ)っ払(ぱら)いに言(い)い掛(が)かりをつけられた。
A drunk person started an argument with me over nothing.

USAGE:

  • 言(い)い掛(が)かりをつける (to make a false accusation, to pick a fight)
  • 言(い)い掛(が)かりをつけられる (to be falsely accused)

NUANCE:
Implies the accusation or complaint is unreasonable or fabricated. Often used when someone is looking for an excuse to start a confrontation.

RELATED:

  • 因縁(いんねん)をつける (to pick a quarrel)
  • 難癖(なんくせ)をつける (to find fault, nitpick)
