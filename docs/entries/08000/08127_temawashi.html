手回(てまわ)し

てまわし
noun
hand-operated; preparation; arrangements
1. hand-operated; hand-cranked; manual
operated by turning a handle by hand rather than by electricity or motor
手回(てまわ)しの発電機(はつでんき)。
A hand-cranked generator.
昔(むかし)は手回(てまわ)しのミシンを使(つか)っていた。
In the old days, they used hand-operated sewing machines.
手回(てまわ)しオルガンの音(おと)が聞(き)こえてきた。
The sound of a hand-cranked organ could be heard.
2. preparation; arrangements; making arrangements in advance
preparing or arranging things beforehand, often behind the scenes
手回(てまわ)しよく準備(じゅんび)してあった。
Preparations had been made efficiently in advance.
彼(かれ)は手回(てまわ)しがいい。
He is good at making arrangements.
既(すで)に手回(てまわ)しが済(す)んでいたようだ。
It seems the arrangements had already been made.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (hand-operated) typically appears as a modifier before nouns.
Sense 2 (arrangements) often appears in expressions like 手回(てまわ)しがいい (good at making arrangements) or 手回(てまわ)しよく (efficiently/with good preparation).

RELATED TERMS:

  • 根回(ねまわ)し (laying the groundwork; behind-the-scenes negotiations)
