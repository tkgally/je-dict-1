照(て)れ笑(わら)い

てれわらい
noun
embarrassed smile; bashful laugh
1. embarrassed smile; bashful laugh; sheepish grin
a smile or laugh that comes from feeling embarrassed, shy, or self-conscious
彼(かれ)は照(て)れ笑(わら)いした。
He gave an embarrassed smile.
褒(ほ)められて照(て)れ笑(わら)いを浮(う)かべた。
She gave a bashful smile when complimented.
頭(あたま)を掻(か)きながら照(て)れ笑(わら)いする姿(すがた)がかわいい。
The way they scratch their head while laughing bashfully is cute.

COMPOUND:
照(て)れる (to be embarrassed) + 笑(わら)い (smile, laugh)

NUANCE:
This is generally a positive or endearing expression, suggesting modesty or shyness rather than discomfort. It often accompanies receiving praise or attention.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 照(て)れ笑(わら)いする - to smile bashfully
  • 照(て)れ笑(わら)いを浮(う)かべる - to wear an embarrassed smile

RELATED:

  • 苦笑(にがわら)い - bitter/wry smile
  • 薄笑(うすわら)い - smirk
  • 愛想笑(あいそうわら)い - polite smile
