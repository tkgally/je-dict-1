差(さ)し押(お)さえ

さしおさえ
noun
seizure; attachment; garnishment
1. seizure; attachment; garnishment
the legal process of seizing property or assets by court order, typically to satisfy a debt or judgment
財産(ざいさん)を差(さ)し押(お)さえる。
To seize property.
給料(きゅうりょう)の差(さ)し押(お)さえを受(う)けた。
My salary was garnished.
裁判所(さいばんしょ)から差(さ)し押(お)さえ命令(めいれい)が出(で)た。
A seizure order was issued by the court.

差(さ)し押(お)さえ is a legal term for court-ordered seizure of assets.

VERB FORMATION:

  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえる (to seize - ichidan verb)

COMMON TARGETS OF SEIZURE:

  • 財産(ざいさん) (property)
  • 預金(よきん) (bank deposits)
  • 給料(きゅうりょう)/給与(きゅうよ) (salary)
  • 不動産(ふどうさん) (real estate)
  • 動産(どうさん) (movable property)

LEGAL COLLOCATIONS:

  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえ命令(めいれい) (seizure order)
  • 仮(かり)差(さ)し押(お)さえ (provisional seizure)
  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえ解除(かいじょ) (release of seizure)
  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえ禁止(きんし) (prohibition of seizure)

USAGE:

  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえを受(う)ける (to have assets seized)
  • 差(さ)し押(お)さえられる (to be seized)

NOTE:
Used in contexts of debt collection, legal judgments, and tax enforcement.
