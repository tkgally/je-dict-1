肌荒(はだあ)れ

はだあれ
noun
rough skin; skin irritation
1. rough skin; skin irritation; chapped skin
condition where the skin becomes rough, dry, or irritated due to weather, stress, diet, or other factors
冬(ふゆ)は肌荒(はだあ)れしやすい。
Skin gets rough easily in winter.
肌荒(はだあ)れを防(ふせ)ぐために保湿(ほしつ)クリームを塗(ぬ)る。
I apply moisturizing cream to prevent rough skin.
ストレスで肌荒(はだあ)れがひどくなった。
My skin irritation got worse from stress.

CAUSES:
Common causes include dry weather, stress, lack of sleep, poor diet, and allergies.

VERB FORMS:

  • 肌(はだ)が荒(あ)れる (skin becomes rough)
  • 肌荒(はだあ)れする (to have rough skin)

RELATED:

  • 乾燥肌(かんそうはだ) (dry skin)
  • 敏感肌(びんかんはだ) (sensitive skin)
  • 保湿(ほしつ) (moisturizing)
