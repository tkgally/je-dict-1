撒(ま)き散(ち)らす

まきちらす
verb
to scatter; to spread around; to strew
1. to scatter; to spread around; to strew about
to throw or spread things in various directions so they fall or lie about
種(たね)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らす。
To scatter seeds.
子供(こども)がおもちゃ(おもちゃ)を部屋中(へやじゅう)に撒(ま)き散(ち)らした。
The child scattered toys all over the room.
風(かぜ)が落(お)ち葉(ば)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らした。
The wind scattered the fallen leaves.
2. to spread (rumors, disease, etc.)
to disseminate or spread something, often negative, widely
噂(うわさ)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らす。
To spread rumors.

COMPOUND VERB:
Combines 撒(ま)く (to scatter, sow) + 散(ち)らす (to scatter), intensifying the scattering action.

CONJUGATION:

  • 撒(ま)き散(ち)らさない (negative)
  • 撒(ま)き散(ち)らして (te-form)
  • 撒(ま)き散(ち)らした (past)

RELATED:

  • 撒(ま)く (to scatter, sow)
  • 散(ち)らす (to scatter)
  • 散(ち)らばる (to be scattered - intransitive)
  • ばらまく (to scatter, distribute widely)
