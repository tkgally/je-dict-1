1. to scatter; to spread around; to strew about
to throw or spread things in various directions so they fall or lie about
種(たね)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らす。
To scatter seeds.
子供(こども)がおもちゃを部屋中(へやじゅう)に撒(ま)き散(ち)らした。
The child scattered toys all over the room.
風(かぜ)が落(お)ち葉(ば)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らした。
The wind scattered the fallen leaves.
2. to spread (rumors, disease, etc.)
to disseminate or spread something, often negative, widely
噂(うわさ)を撒(ま)き散(ち)らす。
To spread rumors.

COMPOUND VERB:
Combines 撒(ま)く (to scatter, sow) + 散(ち)らす (to scatter about). The combination intensifies the scattering action — things are spread widely and messily.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Takes を particle: 〜を撒(ま)き散(ち)らす

ASPECT (ている):
撒(ま)き散(ち)らしている describes an ongoing scattering action or a resulting state of things being scattered about.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 撒(ま)く: to scatter, sow (basic)
  • 散(ち)らす: to scatter (basic)
  • 散(ち)らばる: to be scattered (intransitive)
  • ばらまく: to scatter, hand out indiscriminately
