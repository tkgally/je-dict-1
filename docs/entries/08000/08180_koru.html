凝(こ)る

こる
godan verb (intransitive)
to become stiff; to be absorbed in
1. to become stiff; to stiffen (muscles)
used for muscles that have tensed up due to fatigue or strain
肩(かた)が凝(こ)った。
My shoulders are stiff.
長時間(ちょうじかん)パソコンを使(つか)うと首(くび)が凝(こ)る。
My neck gets stiff when I use the computer for a long time.
肩(かた)が凝(こ)って頭(あたま)も痛(いた)くなってきた。
My shoulders are stiff and I'm getting a headache too.
2. to be absorbed in; to be obsessed with; to be elaborate
to put great effort into something, often to an excessive degree
あの人(ひと)は料理(りょうり)に凝(こ)っている。
That person is really into cooking.
凝(こ)ったデザインの家具(かぐ)を買(か)った。
I bought elaborately designed furniture.
彼女(かのじょ)は最近(さいきん)カメラに凝(こ)っていて毎日(まいにち)写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)っている。
She's been really into cameras lately and takes photos every day.

SENSE 1 - MUSCLE STIFFNESS:

  • 肩(かた)が凝(こ)る (stiff shoulders) - very common expression
  • 首(くび)が凝(こ)る (stiff neck)
  • Common in Japan due to long work hours

SENSE 2 - BEING ABSORBED:

  • に凝(こ)る / に凝(こ)っている (to be into something)
  • 凝(こ)った + noun = elaborate, intricate
  • 凝(こ)り性(しょう) (obsessive personality)

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Intransitive (自動詞(じどうし))
  • Related transitive: 凝(こ)らす (to concentrate, focus)
