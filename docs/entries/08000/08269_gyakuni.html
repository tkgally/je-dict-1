逆(ぎゃく)に

ぎゃくに
adverb
conversely; on the contrary; rather; instead
1. conversely; on the contrary; in reverse
indicates the opposite or reverse of what was stated or expected
逆(ぎゃく)に考(かんが)えてみよう。
Let's think about it the other way around.
良(よ)くなるどころか、逆(ぎゃく)に悪(わる)くなった。
Far from getting better, it actually got worse.
逆(ぎゃく)に言(い)えば、チャンスでもある。
Conversely speaking, it's also an opportunity.
2. rather; instead; actually
introduces an unexpected or contrasting result or perspective
怒(おこ)られると思(おも)ったが、逆(ぎゃく)に褒(ほ)められた。
I thought I'd be scolded, but instead I was praised.
節約(せつやく)したつもりが、逆(ぎゃく)に出費(しゅっぴ)が増(ふ)えた。
I intended to save money, but expenses increased instead.
逆(ぎゃく)に聞(き)きたいんだけど。
Actually, I'd like to ask you something.

USAGE:

  • 逆(ぎゃく)に言(い)えば (conversely speaking)
  • 逆(ぎゃく)に考(かんが)えると (if you think about it the other way)
  • 逆(ぎゃく)に聞(き)くけど (let me ask you instead)

CONVERSATIONAL USE:

逆(ぎゃく)に is very common in casual conversation to introduce:

  • An opposite perspective
  • An unexpected result
  • A question turned back to the listener

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 反対(はんたい)に (on the contrary - more formal)
  • かえって (rather, on the contrary - similar nuance)
  • むしろ (rather, instead)

RELATED:

  • 逆(ぎゃく) (reverse, opposite - noun/adjective)
  • 反対(はんたい) (opposite)
  • かえって (rather, instead)
