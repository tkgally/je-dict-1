窓際(まどぎわ)

まどぎわ
noun
by the window; window seat; sidelined
1. by the window; near the window
the area or seat next to a window
窓際(まどぎわ)の席(せき)に座(すわ)った。
I sat in the seat by the window.
窓際(まどぎわ)に花(はな)を飾(かざ)っている。
I'm displaying flowers by the window.
猫(ねこ)が窓際(まどぎわ)で日向(ひなた)ぼっこしている。
The cat is basking in the sun by the window.
2. sidelined; marginalized (in a company)
being given no meaningful work, as in the expression 窓際族
窓際(まどぎわ)に追(お)いやられた。
I was sidelined at work.
窓際(まどぎわ)社員(しゃいん)として毎日(まいにち)暇(ひま)を持(も)て余(あま)している。
As a sidelined employee, I have too much time on my hands every day.
彼(かれ)は窓際(まどぎわ)部署(ぶしょ)に異動(いどう)になった。
He was transferred to a dead-end department.

FORMATION:
窓(まど) (window) + 際(ぎわ) (edge, beside) → beside the window

SECOND MEANING:
The second sense comes from the expression 窓際(まどぎわ)族(ぞく) (literally "window-side tribe"), referring to employees who have been given no meaningful work and spend their days sitting by the window with nothing to do. This is a uniquely Japanese corporate phenomenon where companies don't easily fire employees, but instead marginalize them.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 窓際(まどぎわ)族(ぞく) (sidelined employees)
  • 窓際(まどぎわ)に追(お)いやられる (to be sidelined)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 通路側(つうろがわ) (aisle seat - contrast)
  • 左遷(させん) (demotion, transfer to a lesser position)
