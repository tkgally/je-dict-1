生(なま)ゴミ

なまごみ
noun
food waste; raw garbage; kitchen waste
1. food waste; raw garbage; kitchen waste
organic waste from food preparation and leftovers that can decompose and smell
生(なま)ゴミは毎日(まいにち)出(だ)す。
I take out the food waste every day.
夏(なつ)は生(なま)ゴミの臭(にお)いがひどい。
In summer, the smell of food waste is terrible.
生(なま)ゴミは水分(すいぶん)を切(き)ってから捨(す)てる。
Drain the moisture from food waste before throwing it away.

WORD FORMATION:

  • 生(なま) (raw, fresh)
  • ゴミ (garbage, waste)

DISPOSAL:
In most Japanese municipalities, 生(なま)ゴミ is classified as 可燃(かねん)ゴミ (burnable garbage).

TIPS:

  • 水(みず)を切(き)る (drain water to reduce smell and weight)
  • 新聞紙(しんぶんし)で包(つつ)む (wrap in newspaper)
  • 冷凍(れいとう)する (freeze until collection day)

COMPOSTING:
生(なま)ゴミ処理機(しょりき) (food waste processor) or コンポスト (compost) can turn food waste into fertilizer.
