判断力(はんだんりょく)

はんだんりょく
noun
judgment; ability to judge
1. judgment; ability to judge
the mental capacity to make sensible decisions and form sound opinions
判断力(はんだんりょく)のある人(ひと)だ。
That person has good judgment.
緊急時(きんきゅうじ)には冷静(れいせい)な判断力(はんだんりょく)が求(もと)められる。
In emergencies, calm judgment is required.
疲(つか)れていると判断力(はんだんりょく)が鈍(にぶ)る。
When you're tired, your judgment becomes impaired.

FORMATION:
判断(はんだん) (judgment, decision) + 力(りょく) (power, ability) → power of judgment

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 判断力(はんだんりょく)がある (to have good judgment)
  • 判断力(はんだんりょく)が鈍(にぶ)る (judgment becomes dull)
  • 判断力(はんだんりょく)に欠(か)ける (to lack judgment)
  • 冷静(れいせい)な判断力(はんだんりょく) (calm judgment)

RELATED ～力(りょく) COMPOUNDS:

  • 決断力(けつだんりょく) (decisiveness - ability to make firm decisions)
  • 洞察力(どうさつりょく) (insight)
  • 観察力(かんさつりょく) (observation skills)

LEGAL CONTEXT:
In Japanese law, 判断力(はんだんりょく) is important in determining mental capacity for contracts, wills, etc.
