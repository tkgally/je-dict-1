通勤(つうきん)ラッシュ

つうきんらっしゅ
noun
commuter rush; rush hour
1. commuter rush; morning rush hour
the period of heavy traffic congestion when people commute to work, typically in the morning
通勤(つうきん)ラッシュを避(さ)けたい。
I want to avoid the commuter rush.
通勤(つうきん)ラッシュの電車(でんしゃ)は地獄(じごく)だ。
The train during rush hour is hell.
フレックスタイムで通勤(つうきん)ラッシュを避(さ)けられる。
With flextime, I can avoid the commuter rush.

COMPOUND:
通勤(つうきん) (commuting) + ラッシュ (rush, from English)

TIMING:

Typically refers to:

  • 7:30-9:00 AM in major Japanese cities
  • Peak congestion on trains can reach 180-200% capacity

CONTRAST:

  • 通勤(つうきん)ラッシュ (morning rush) ↔ 帰宅(きたく)ラッシュ (evening rush)

RELATED:

  • 満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ) - packed train
  • 時差出勤(じさしゅっきん) - staggered commuting hours
  • ラッシュアワー - rush hour
  • 混雑(こんざつ) - congestion
