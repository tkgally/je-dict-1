見(み)え隠(かく)れ

みえかくれ
noun
appearing and disappearing; glimpses; showing partially
1. appearing and disappearing; glimpses
the state of something being partially visible, alternately showing and hiding
木(き)の間(あいだ)から月(つき)が見(み)え隠(かく)れしている。
The moon appears and disappears between the trees.
雲(くも)の間(あいだ)から見(み)え隠(かく)れする太陽(たいよう)。
The sun peeking through the clouds.
遠(とお)くに山(やま)が見(み)え隠(かく)れしていた。
Mountains were visible in glimpses in the distance.
2. hint of; traces of
something that subtly shows through or becomes apparent
彼(かれ)の不安(ふあん)が言葉(ことば)の端々(はしばし)に見(み)え隠(かく)れしていた。
His anxiety was subtly showing through his words.
本音(ほんね)が見(み)え隠(かく)れする。
True feelings are peeking through.
野心(やしん)が見(み)え隠(かく)れしている。
Ambition is showing through.

WORD FORMATION:

  • 見(み)える (to be visible)
  • 隠(かく)れる (to hide)

→ An alternating state of visibility

VERB USAGE:
見(み)え隠(かく)れする (to appear and disappear, to show glimpses)

CONTEXT:
Sense 2 is commonly used to describe emotions, intentions, or character traits that someone tries to hide but which occasionally become apparent.
