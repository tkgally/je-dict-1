勤務先(きんむさき)

きんむさき
noun
workplace; place of employment
1. workplace; place of employment
the company or location where one works
勤務先(きんむさき)の住所(じゅうしょ)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me your workplace address.
勤務先(きんむさき)は駅(えき)から近(ちか)いですか。
Is your workplace close to the station?
書類(しょるい)に勤務先(きんむさき)の電話番号(でんわばんごう)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Please fill in your workplace phone number on the form.

FORMATION:
勤務(きんむ) (work, duty) + 先(さき) (place, destination) → place of work

RELATED WORDS:

  • 職場(しょくば) (workplace - more common in casual speech)
  • 会社(かいしゃ) (company)
  • 勤(つと)め先(さき) (place of employment - similar)
  • 勤務(きんむ) (work, duty)

USAGE:
勤務先(きんむさき) is commonly used on official forms and documents. In casual conversation, 職場(しょくば) or simply 会社(かいしゃ) is more common. 勤(つと)め先(さき) has a similar meaning and is also used on forms.

FORM FIELDS:

  • 勤務先(きんむさき)名(めい) (employer name)
  • 勤務先(きんむさき)住所(じゅうしょ) (work address)
  • 勤務先(きんむさき)電話番号(でんわばんごう) (work phone number)
