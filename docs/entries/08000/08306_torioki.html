取(と)り置(お)き

とりおき
noun
holding an item; reservation (of goods)
1. holding an item; reservation of goods; setting aside
the practice of a store holding an item for a customer to purchase later; reserving merchandise before payment
この商品(しょうひん)の取(と)り置(お)きをお願(ねが)いできますか。
Could you hold this item for me?
取(と)り置(お)きは三日間(みっかかん)まで可能(かのう)です。
We can hold items for up to three days.
電話(でんわ)で取(と)り置(お)きを頼(たの)んでおいた本(ほん)を取(と)りに来(き)た。
I came to pick up the book I had asked them to hold over the phone.

FORMATION:
取(と)り置(お)く (to set aside, to hold) → 取(と)り置(お)き (noun form)

USAGE PATTERNS:

  • 取(と)り置(お)きをする (to hold an item)
  • 取(と)り置(お)きを頼(たの)む (to ask for an item to be held)
  • 取(と)り置(お)き期間(きかん) (holding period)

SHOPPING CONTEXT:
Many Japanese stores offer 取(と)り置(お)き service, allowing customers to reserve items for a limited period before purchasing. This is common in bookstores, clothing stores, and specialty shops.

RELATED:

  • 取(と)り寄(よ)せ (ordering in, special order)
  • 予約(よやく) (reservation - broader term)
