1. allocation; appropriation; application
assigning or applying money, resources, or personnel to a particular purpose
予算(よさん)を人件費(じんけんひ)に充当(じゅうとう)する。
To allocate the budget to personnel costs.
余剰金(よじょうきん)を設備投資(せつびとうし)に充当(じゅうとう)した。
We allocated the surplus funds to capital investment.
売上(うりあげ)の一部(いちぶ)を返済(へんさい)に充当(じゅうとう)する。
To apply a portion of sales to repayment.
新規(しんき)事業(じぎょう)に資金(しきん)を充当(じゅうとう)する計画(けいかく)だ。
The plan is to allocate funds to new business ventures.

充当(じゅうとう) refers to allocating or applying resources (especially money) to a specific purpose.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 充(じゅう) (fill, satisfy, allocate)
  • 当(とう) (hit, appropriate, apply)

USAGE:

  • 充当(じゅうとう)する (to allocate, to appropriate - suru verb)
  • 〜に充当(じゅうとう)する (to allocate to ~)
  • 充当(じゅうとう)額(がく) (allocated amount)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 予算(よさん)を〜に充当(じゅうとう)する (to allocate budget to ~)
  • 資金(しきん)を充当(じゅうとう)する (to allocate funds)
  • 利益(りえき)を返済(へんさい)に充当(じゅうとう)する (to apply profits to repayment)

BUSINESS/LEGAL CONTEXT:

  • Common in financial planning and budgeting
  • Used in accounting and legal contexts
  • Often appears in corporate reports and financial statements

RELATED TERMS:

  • 配分(はいぶん) (allocation, distribution)
  • 割(わ)り当(あ)てる (to allocate, assign)
  • 計上(けいじょう) (recording, booking)
  • 引(ひ)き当(あ)てる (to allocate, appropriate)
