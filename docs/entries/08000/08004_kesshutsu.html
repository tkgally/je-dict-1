傑出(けっしゅつ)

けっしゅつ
noun, suru verb
outstanding; distinguished; prominent
1. outstanding; distinguished; prominent
being remarkably superior to others in ability, achievement, or quality
傑出(けっしゅつ)した才能(さいのう)を持(も)つ。
To have outstanding talent.
彼(かれ)は傑出(けっしゅつ)した科学者(かがくしゃ)だった。
He was a distinguished scientist.
傑出(けっしゅつ)した業績(ぎょうせき)で歴史(れきし)に名(な)を残(のこ)した。
He left his name in history through outstanding achievements.
この分野(ぶんや)で傑出(けっしゅつ)した人物(じんぶつ)は数少(かずすく)ない。
There are few distinguished figures in this field.

傑出(けっしゅつ) describes someone or something remarkably superior to others, used to praise exceptional ability or achievement.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 傑(けつ) (outstanding person, hero)
  • 出(しゅつ) (come out, emerge)

USAGE:

  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)する (to be outstanding - suru verb)
  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)した〜 (outstanding ~, distinguished ~)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)した人物(じんぶつ) (distinguished person)
  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)した才能(さいのう) (outstanding talent)
  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)した業績(ぎょうせき) (distinguished achievements)
  • 傑出(けっしゅつ)した作品(さくひん) (outstanding work)

REGISTER:

  • Formal/written style
  • Common in biographical descriptions, awards, and academic contexts
  • High praise, often for historical figures or exceptional individuals

SIMILAR TERMS:

  • 卓越(たくえつ) - excellence (more about ability/quality)
  • 秀逸(しゅういつ) - excellent, superb
  • 出色(しゅっしょく) - outstanding, remarkable

RELATED TERMS:

  • 卓越(たくえつ) (excellence, preeminence)
  • 優秀(ゆうしゅう) (excellent, superior)
  • 抜群(ばつぐん) (outstanding, preeminent)
  • 偉大(いだい) (great, mighty)
