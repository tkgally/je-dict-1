察(さっ)する

さっする
verb
to guess; to sense; to infer; to sympathize
1. to guess; to sense; to infer
to understand or perceive something without being told directly, based on context or subtle cues
彼(かれ)の気持(きも)ちを察(さっ)する。
To sense his feelings.
状況(じょうきょう)を察(さっ)して行動(こうどう)する。
To act based on reading the situation.
言(い)わなくても察(さっ)してほしい。
I want you to understand without me having to say it.
2. to sympathize with; to understand someone's feelings
to perceive and empathize with someone's unspoken feelings or situation
ご心中(しんちゅう)察(さっ)します。
I understand how you must feel.
彼女(かのじょ)の苦労(くろう)を察(さっ)する。
To sympathize with her hardships.
親(おや)の心(こころ)を察(さっ)する。
To understand a parent's feelings.

CULTURAL IMPORTANCE:
This verb is central to Japanese high-context communication. The ability to 察(さっ)する is highly valued in Japanese culture, where direct expression can be avoided in favor of implicit communication.

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 空気(くうき)を読(よ)む (to read the atmosphere)
  • 行間(ぎょうかん)を読(よ)む (to read between the lines)
  • 言(い)わなくても分(わ)かる (to understand without being told)

FORMAL EXPRESSION:
「ご心中(しんちゅう)察(さっ)します」is a polite way to express sympathy.
