寝落(ねお)ち

ねおち
noun
falling asleep unintentionally; dozing off
1. falling asleep unintentionally; dozing off while doing something
the act of falling asleep without meaning to, often while using a phone, watching TV, or chatting online
スマホを見(み)ながら寝落(ねお)ちした。
I fell asleep while looking at my phone.
電話中(でんわちゅう)に寝落(ねお)ちしてしまった。
I accidentally fell asleep while on the phone.
昨夜(さくや)は映画(えいが)を見(み)ていたら寝落(ねお)ちして、最後(さいご)まで見(み)られなかった。
Last night I fell asleep while watching a movie and couldn't finish it.

FORMATION:
寝(ね) (sleep) + 落(お)ちる (to fall) → to fall into sleep

VERB FORM:
寝落(ねお)ちする (to fall asleep unintentionally)

MODERN USAGE:

This term became popular with the rise of smartphones and online communication. It's commonly used when:

  • Falling asleep while texting or on social media
  • Dozing off during an online video call
  • Falling asleep while gaming

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • LINEで話(はな)していて寝落(ねお)ちした (fell asleep while chatting on LINE)
  • 通話(つうわ)中(ちゅう)に寝落(ねお)ち (falling asleep during a call)
  • ゲーム中(ちゅう)に寝落(ねお)ち (falling asleep while gaming)

RELATED WORDS:

  • うたた寝(ね) (dozing off lightly)
  • 居眠(いねむ)り (nodding off while sitting)
  • 仮眠(かみん) (intentional nap)
