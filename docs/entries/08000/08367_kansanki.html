閑散期(かんさんき)

かんさんき
noun
slow season; off-peak period; slack period
1. slow season; off-peak period; slack period
a time of year when business or activity is at its lowest level
閑散期(かんさんき)に旅行(りょこう)すると安(やす)い。
Traveling during the off-season is cheaper.
閑散期(かんさんき)は暇(ひま)すぎて困(こま)る。
The slow season is so quiet it's troublesome.
閑散期(かんさんき)を利用(りよう)して設備(せつび)のメンテナンスを行(おこな)う。
We use the slow season to do equipment maintenance.

FORMATION:
閑散(かんさん) (deserted, quiet) + 期(き) (period) → quiet period

ANTONYM:
繁忙期(はんぼうき) (busy season, peak period)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 閑散(かんさん) (deserted, quiet)
  • オフシーズン (off-season)
  • 暇(ひま) (free time, not busy)

BUSINESS STRATEGY:
Many businesses use 閑散期(かんさんき) for staff training, renovations, or offering discounts to attract customers. Travel and hospitality industries often have significant price differences between 繁忙期(はんぼうき) and 閑散期(かんさんき).

Related Words

Antonym: 繁忙期(はんぼうき) (busy season)
