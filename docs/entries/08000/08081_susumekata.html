進(すす)め方(かた)

すすめかた
noun
way of proceeding; approach; method of advancing
1. way of proceeding; approach; how to proceed
the method or manner in which something is carried out or advanced
プロジェクトの進(すす)め方(かた)を相談(そうだん)する。
To discuss how to proceed with the project.
会議(かいぎ)の進(すす)め方(かた)について意見(いけん)がある。
I have an opinion about how to run the meeting.
仕事(しごと)の進(すす)め方(かた)は人(ひと)それぞれだ。
Everyone has their own approach to work.

進(すす)め方(かた) means "way of proceeding" or "approach to doing something."

PATTERN:
Formed with 〜方(かた) (way of doing), a productive suffix attached to verb stems (進(すす)める → 進(すす)め方(かた)).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • プロジェクトの進(すす)め方(かた): how to proceed with a project
  • 会議(かいぎ)の進(すす)め方(かた): how to run a meeting
  • 仕事(しごと)の進(すす)め方(かた): approach to work
  • 進(すす)め方(かた)を相談(そうだん)する: to discuss how to proceed

RELATED 〜方(かた) WORDS:

  • 話(はな)し方(かた): way of speaking
  • 考(かんが)え方(かた): way of thinking
  • やり方(かた): way of doing
  • 書(か)き方(かた): way of writing
