じりじり

じりじり
adverb
scorching; gradually; impatiently
1. scorching; burning hot
describes intense heat, especially from the sun beating down
太陽(たいよう)がじりじり照(て)りつける。
The sun beats down scorchingly.
2. gradually; little by little
describes slow, steady movement or change, often with a sense of pressure or tension
気温(きおん)がじりじり上(あ)がっている。
The temperature is gradually rising.
敵(てき)がじりじりと迫(せま)ってくる。
The enemy is closing in slowly.
3. impatiently; restlessly
describes a state of frustration or impatience while waiting
返事(へんじ)を待(ま)ってじりじりする。
To wait impatiently for a reply.

ONOMATOPOEIA:
This is a mimetic word (擬態語(ぎたいご)/擬音語(ぎおんご)) that vividly conveys sensations and states.

NUANCE:
All senses share an underlying tension or intensity - whether from heat, gradual pressure, or impatient waiting.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • じりじり照(て)りつける (sun beats down scorchingly)
  • じりじりと迫(せま)る (to close in gradually)
  • じりじりする (to feel restless/impatient)

RELATED:

  • かんかん (blazing hot - stronger)
  • じわじわ (gradually, slowly - more neutral)
