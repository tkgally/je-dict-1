利子(りし)

りし
noun
interest (on money)
1. interest (on money)
the amount paid for borrowing money or earned on savings and investments
銀行(ぎんこう)の利子(りし)が低(ひく)い。
The bank's interest rate is low.
借金(しゃっきん)の利子(りし)を払(はら)う。
I pay the interest on my debt.
預金(よきん)につく利子(りし)はほとんどない。
There's almost no interest on savings deposits.

FORMATION:
利(り) (profit, benefit) + 子(し) (small part) → small profit from money

SYNONYMS:

  • 利息(りそく) (interest - more formal, often used in banking contexts)
  • 金利(きんり) (interest rate)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 利子(りし)が付(つ)く (to accrue interest)
  • 利子(りし)を払(はら)う (to pay interest)
  • 利子(りし)を受(う)け取(と)る (to receive interest)

JAPAN'S LOW INTEREST ERA:
Since the 1990s, Japan has maintained very low interest rates, making 利子(りし)が低(ひく)い a common observation about bank savings.
