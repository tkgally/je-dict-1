言(い)い逃(のが)れる

いいのがれる
verb (ichidan)
to make excuses; to wriggle out of
1. to make excuses; to talk one's way out of
to avoid responsibility or blame by making excuses or evasive explanations
責任(せきにん)から言(い)い逃(のが)れる。
To make excuses to avoid responsibility.
どう言(い)い逃(のが)れても無駄(むだ)だ。
No matter how you try to excuse yourself, it's useless.
彼(かれ)は上手(じょうず)に言(い)い逃(のが)れた。
He skillfully talked his way out of it.

言(い)い逃(のが)れる is a compound verb with a negative connotation.

VERB FORMATION:

  • 言(い)う (to say) + 逃(のが)れる (to escape)

CONJUGATION:

  • Ichidan verb
  • Negative: 言(い)い逃(のが)れない
  • Past: 言(い)い逃(のが)れた
  • Te-form: 言(い)い逃(のが)れて

NOUN FORM:

  • 言(い)い逃(のが)れ (excuse, evasion)

USAGE NOTES:

  • Often implies dishonesty or avoiding rightful blame
  • Has a negative connotation

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 言(い)い訳(わけ)をする (to make an excuse)
  • 逃(に)げ口上(こうじょう) (excuse, pretext)
  • 弁解(べんかい)する (to justify oneself)
