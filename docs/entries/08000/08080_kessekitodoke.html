欠席届(けっせきとどけ)

けっせきとどけ
noun
absence notice; absence report
1. absence notice; absence report
a formal notification submitted when unable to attend school, work, or a meeting
欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する。
To submit an absence notice.
体調不良(たいちょうふりょう)のため欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を出(だ)した。
I submitted an absence notice due to feeling unwell.
事前(じぜん)に欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を出(だ)しておいてください。
Please submit your absence notice in advance.

欠席届(けっせきとどけ) is a formal document submitted to notify school or work of an absence.

COMMON VERBS:

  • 欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を出(だ)す: to submit an absence notice
  • 欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を提出(ていしゅつ)する: to submit (formal)
  • 欠席届(けっせきとどけ)を書(か)く: to write an absence notice

USAGE:
Used in schools, universities, and workplaces. Often requires stating the reason (理由(りゆう)) and expected duration. May need to be submitted in advance (事前(じぜん)に) or as soon as possible after the absence begins.

RELATED DOCUMENTS:

  • 遅刻届(ちこくとどけ): late arrival notice
  • 早退届(そうたいとどけ): early departure notice
  • 休暇届(きゅうかとどけ): leave request form
