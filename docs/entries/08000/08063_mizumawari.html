水回(みずまわ)り

みずまわり
noun
water-related areas; plumbing areas
1. water-related areas in a home; plumbing fixtures
collective term for areas of a home that use water, such as the kitchen, bathroom, toilet, and laundry
水回(みずまわ)りの掃除(そうじ)をする。
To clean the water-related areas.
水回(みずまわ)りのリフォームは費用(ひよう)がかかる。
Renovating the plumbing areas is expensive.
この物件(ぶっけん)は水回(みずまわ)りがきれいに改装(かいそう)されている。
This property has had its water-related areas nicely renovated.

水回(みずまわ)り refers to all water-related areas in a home collectively.

INCLUDES:

  • 台所(だいどころ)/キッチン: kitchen
  • 風呂場(ふろば): bathroom
  • トイレ: toilet
  • 洗面所(せんめんじょ): washroom/laundry area

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 水回(みずまわ)りの掃除(そうじ): cleaning water-related areas
  • 水回(みずまわ)りのリフォーム: renovating plumbing areas
  • 水回(みずまわ)りがきれい: clean plumbing fixtures

USAGE:
Commonly used in real estate listings and renovation discussions.
