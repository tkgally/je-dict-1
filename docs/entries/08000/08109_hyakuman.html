the number 1,000,000, written as 百万(ひゃくまん) (100 × 10,000) in the Japanese numbering system

The population of this city is about one million people.

JAPANESE NUMBER SYSTEM:

Japanese uses a system based on units of 10,000 (万(まん)) rather than 1,000. Thus:

  • 百万(ひゃくまん) = 100 × 万(まん) = 1,000,000
  • 千万(せんまん) = 1,000 × 万(まん) = 10,000,000
  • 一億(いちおく) = 100,000,000

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 百万(ひゃくまん)長者(ちょうじゃ) (millionaire)
  • 百万(ひゃくまん)ドル (one million dollars)
  • 百万(ひゃくまん)分(ぶん)の一(いち) (one in a million)
