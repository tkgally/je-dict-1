裏付(うらづ)け

うらづけ
noun
backing; evidence; support
1. backing; support; corroboration
evidence or facts that support or prove a claim, theory, or statement
理論(りろん)の裏付(うらづ)けが必要(ひつよう)だ。
Evidence to support the theory is needed.
証言(しょうげん)を裏付(うらづ)ける証拠(しょうこ)が見(み)つかった。
Evidence corroborating the testimony was found.
この主張(しゅちょう)には科学(かがく)的(てき)な裏付(うらづ)けがある。
This claim has scientific backing.

裏付(うらづ)け refers to evidence or facts that support a statement, claim, or theory.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 裏(うら) (back, reverse side)
  • 付(づ)ける (to attach)

ETYMOLOGY:
Literally "attaching from behind" - providing hidden support or backing.

USAGE:

  • 裏付(うらづ)ける (to back up, to corroborate - ichidan verb)
  • 裏付(うらづ)けがある (to have backing/evidence)
  • 裏付(うらづ)けがない (to lack evidence)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 科学(かがく)的(てき)裏付(うらづ)け (scientific backing)
  • 証拠(しょうこ)の裏付(うらづ)け (evidentiary support)
  • 裏付(うらづ)け調査(ちょうさ) (corroborating investigation)
  • 裏付(うらづ)け捜査(そうさ) (corroborating investigation - by police)
