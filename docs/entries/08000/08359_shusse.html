出世(しゅっせ)

しゅっせ
noun
career advancement; promotion; success in life
1. career advancement; promotion; getting ahead at work
rising in one's career or position within an organization
彼(かれ)は若(わか)くして出世(しゅっせ)した。
He advanced in his career at a young age.
出世(しゅっせ)のために残業(ざんぎょう)を厭(いと)わない。
I don't mind working overtime for career advancement.
出世(しゅっせ)コースから外(はず)れてしまった。
I fell off the fast track for promotion.
2. success in life; rising in the world; making one's way up
achieving social or financial success, improving one's status in society
田舎(いなか)から出(で)てきて出世(しゅっせ)した。
He came from the countryside and made it big.
彼女(かのじょ)は芸能界(げいのうかい)で出世(しゅっせ)した。
She rose to success in the entertainment industry.
出世(しゅっせ)払(ばら)いで借金(しゃっきん)を返(かえ)す約束(やくそく)をした。
I promised to pay back the debt when I make it big.

FORMATION:
出(しゅつ) (exit, emerge) + 世(せ) (world) → emerge into the world

VERB FORM:
出世(しゅっせ)する (to advance in one's career)

RELATED COMPOUNDS:

  • 出世(しゅっせ)コース (fast track for promotion)
  • 出世(しゅっせ)街道(かいどう) (path to success)
  • 出世(しゅっせ)払(ばら)い (paying back when successful)
  • 出世(しゅっせ)頭(がしら) (person risen to high position)
  • 出世(しゅっせ)魚(うお) (fish whose name changes as it grows)

出世(しゅっせ)魚(うお):
Some fish like 鰤(ぶり) (yellowtail) are called 出世(しゅっせ)魚(うお) because their names change as they grow larger, symbolizing advancement.

WORK CULTURE:
In traditional Japanese corporate culture (年功序列(ねんこうじょれつ)), 出世(しゅっせ) was largely based on seniority. Modern companies increasingly emphasize merit-based promotion.
