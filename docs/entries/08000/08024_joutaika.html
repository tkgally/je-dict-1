常態化(じょうたいか)

じょうたいか
noun
normalization; becoming routine
1. normalization; becoming routine
the process of something unusual or temporary becoming normal, accepted, or routine
残業(ざんぎょう)が常態化(じょうたいか)している。
Overtime work has become the norm.
テレワークの常態化(じょうたいか)が進(すす)んでいる。
Remote work is becoming normalized.
異常(いじょう)事態(じたい)が常態化(じょうたいか)してはならない。
Abnormal situations must not become normalized.

常態化(じょうたいか) often describes problematic situations becoming accepted.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 常(じょう) (normal, regular, constant)
  • 態(たい) (state, condition)
  • 化(か) (-ization)

USAGE:

  • 常態化(じょうたいか)する (to become normalized - suru verb)
  • 常態化(じょうたいか)させる (to normalize something)
  • 常態化(じょうたいか)している (has become routine/normalized)

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 残業(ざんぎょう)の常態化(じょうたいか) (normalization of overtime)
  • 違反(いはん)の常態化(じょうたいか) (normalization of violations)
  • 赤字(あかじ)の常態化(じょうたいか) (persistent deficits)

NOTE:
Often used with negative connotation to describe undesirable situations that have become accepted as normal.
