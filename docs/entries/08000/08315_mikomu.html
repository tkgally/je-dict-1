見込(みこ)む

みこむ
verb
to expect; to anticipate; to estimate
1. to expect; to anticipate; to forecast
to predict or expect something to happen or be the case based on available information
売上(うりあげ)は百億円(ひゃくおくえん)を見込(みこ)んでいる。
We're expecting sales of 10 billion yen.
完成(かんせい)は来月(らいげつ)と見込(みこ)まれている。
Completion is expected next month.
参加者(さんかしゃ)は五百人(ごひゃくにん)ほどを見込(みこ)んでいます。
We're expecting around 500 participants.
2. to see potential in; to have high hopes for
to recognize someone's potential or ability and expect great things from them
彼(かれ)を見込(みこ)んでお願(ねが)いしたい。
I want to ask him because I see potential in him.
君(きみ)を見込(みこ)んでこの仕事(しごと)を任(まか)せる。
I'm entrusting this job to you because I have high hopes for you.
才能(さいのう)を見込(みこ)まれてスカウトされた。
He was scouted because of his recognized talent.

CONJUGATION:

Godan verb (五段動詞(ごだんどうし))

  • 見込(みこ)む → 見込(みこ)まない → 見込(みこ)んで → 見込(みこ)んだ

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし))

RELATED WORDS:

  • 見込(みこ)み (expectation, prospect, hope - noun form)
  • 見込(みこ)み客(きゃく) (prospective customer)
  • 見込(みこ)み違(ちが)い (miscalculation, wrong expectation)

GRAMMATICAL PATTERNS:

  • ～を見込(みこ)む (to expect [amount/result])
  • ～と見込(みこ)まれる (to be expected to ~)
  • ～を見込(みこ)んで (expecting/anticipating ~)

USAGE CONTEXT:
Sense 1 is common in business and formal contexts for forecasts and projections. Sense 2 is used when expressing trust in someone's abilities.
