嘔吐(おうと)

おうと
noun
vomiting; throwing up
1. vomiting; throwing up
the involuntary expulsion of stomach contents through the mouth
嘔吐(おうと)が止(と)まらない。
I can't stop vomiting.
吐(は)き気(け)と嘔吐(おうと)の症状(しょうじょう)がある。
I have symptoms of nausea and vomiting.
食中毒(しょくちゅうどく)で嘔吐(おうと)した。
I vomited due to food poisoning.

MEDICAL TERM:
嘔(おう) (vomit) + 吐(と) (spit out) - formal/medical term for vomiting.

VERB FORM:

  • 嘔吐(おうと)する (to vomit)

COMMON/CASUAL ALTERNATIVES:

  • 吐(は)く (to throw up - common)
  • 戻(もど)す (to vomit - euphemistic)
  • ゲロを吐(は)く (to puke - very casual/crude)

RELATED SYMPTOMS:

  • 吐(は)き気(け) (nausea)
  • 胃(い)もたれ (upset stomach)
  • 悪心(おしん) (nausea - medical term)
