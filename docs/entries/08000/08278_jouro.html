じょうろ

じょうろ
noun
watering can; watering pot
1. watering can; watering pot
a container with a spout and handle used for watering plants
じょうろで花(はな)に水(みず)をやる。
To water the flowers with a watering can.
じょうろに水(みず)を入(い)れてきて。
Go fill up the watering can.
子供(こども)が小(ちい)さなじょうろで植物(しょくぶつ)の世話(せわ)をしている。
The child is caring for plants with a small watering can.

KANJI:
Can be written as 如雨露(じょうろ), though hiragana is more common.

ETYMOLOGY:
Derived from Portuguese "jorro" (spout/jet of water) brought during the Edo period.

USAGE:

  • じょうろで水(みず)をやる / あげる (to water with a watering can)
  • じょうろに水(みず)を入(い)れる (to fill the watering can)

RELATED:

  • 水(みず)やり (watering plants)
  • ホース (hose)
  • スプレー (spray bottle)
