寝違(ねちが)え

ねちがえ
noun
stiff neck; crick in the neck
1. stiff neck from sleeping; crick in the neck
neck pain or stiffness caused by sleeping in an awkward position
今朝(けさ)寝違(ねちが)えた。
I woke up with a stiff neck this morning.
寝違(ねちが)えで首(くび)が痛(いた)い。
My neck hurts because I slept wrong.
寝違(ねちが)えがひどくて首(くび)が回(まわ)らない。
My neck is so stiff from sleeping wrong that I can't turn it.

VERB FORM:
寝違(ねちが)える (to sleep in an awkward position causing neck pain)

USAGE:

  • 寝違(ねちが)えた (I slept wrong / woke up with a stiff neck)
  • 寝違(ねちが)えがひどい (the stiff neck is severe)
  • 寝違(ねちが)えを治(なお)す (to cure the stiff neck)

CAUSES:

  • 枕(まくら)が合(あ)わない (pillow doesn't fit)
  • 変(へん)な姿勢(しせい)で寝(ね)た (slept in a strange position)

REMEDIES:

  • 湿布(しっぷ)を貼(は)る (apply a compress)
  • マッサージをする (get a massage)

Related Words

Related: ぎっくり腰(こし) (slipped disc; acute back pain)
