湯冷(ゆざ)め

ゆざめ
noun / suru-verb
catching cold after a bath
1. catching cold after a bath
getting chilled or catching a cold after bathing, typically from not drying off or dressing properly
湯冷(ゆざ)めしないように。
Be careful not to catch cold after your bath.
髪(かみ)を乾(かわ)かさないと湯冷(ゆざ)めするよ。
If you don't dry your hair, you'll catch cold.
風呂上(ふろあ)がりに薄着(うすぎ)でいたら湯冷(ゆざ)めしてしまった。
I caught a cold because I was lightly dressed after my bath.

COMPOUND:
湯(ゆ) (hot water, bath) + 冷(さ)め (getting cold)

CULTURAL CONTEXT:

In Japan, bathing culture emphasizes warming up fully in the bath. 湯冷(ゆざ)め is a common concern, especially:

  • In winter months
  • For children and elderly
  • After 温泉(おんせん) or 銭湯(せんとう)

PREVENTION:

  • 髪(かみ)を乾(かわ)かす - dry your hair
  • 体(からだ)をよく拭(ふ)く - dry body thoroughly
  • 暖(あたた)かい服(ふく)を着(き)る - wear warm clothes

RELATED:

  • 風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)く - to catch a cold
  • 寒気(さむけ) - feeling chilly
  • 湯上(ゆあ)がり - after bathing
