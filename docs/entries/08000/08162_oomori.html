大盛(おおも)り

おおもり
noun
large serving; extra portion
1. large serving; extra-large portion
a larger than standard serving size of food, especially rice or noodles
ご飯(はん)大盛(おおも)りでお願(ねが)いします。
A large serving of rice, please.
ラーメンを大盛(おおも)りにした。
I got a large portion of ramen.
大盛(おおも)りは百円(ひゃくえん)追加(ついか)です。
A large serving costs an extra 100 yen.
お腹(なか)が空(す)いていたので大盛(おおも)りを頼(たの)んだ。
I ordered a large portion because I was hungry.
この店(みせ)は大盛(おおも)りが無料(むりょう)だ。
This restaurant offers free large servings.

RELATED SERVING SIZES:

  • 小盛(こも)り (small serving)
  • 並盛(なみも)り / 普通盛(ふつうも)り (regular serving)
  • 大盛(おおも)り (large serving)
  • 特盛(とくも)り (extra-large serving)
  • メガ盛(も)り (mega serving)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 大盛(おおも)りにする (to make it a large serving)
  • 大盛(おおも)り無料(むりょう) (free large portion upgrade)
  • ご飯(はん)大盛(おおも)り (large rice serving)

NOTE:
Many Japanese restaurants offer 大盛(おおも)り for free or at a small extra charge. Some ramen shops particularly are known for generous 大盛(おおも)り options.
