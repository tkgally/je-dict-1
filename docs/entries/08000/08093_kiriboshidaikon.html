切(き)り干(ほ)し大根(だいこん)

きりぼしだいこん
noun
dried shredded daikon; dried radish strips
1. dried shredded daikon; dried radish strips
daikon radish that has been cut into thin strips and sun-dried, a traditional preserved food in Japanese cuisine
切(き)り干(ほ)し大根(だいこん)を水(みず)で戻(もど)す。
Rehydrate the dried daikon in water.
切(き)り干(ほ)し大根(だいこん)の煮物(にもの)は定番(ていばん)の家庭料理(かていりょうり)だ。
Simmered dried daikon is a standard home-cooked dish.
切(き)り干(ほ)し大根(だいこん)には食物繊維(しょくもつせんい)が豊富(ほうふ)に含(ふく)まれている。
Dried daikon is rich in dietary fiber.

PREPARATION:
切(き)り干(ほ)し大根(だいこん) needs to be rehydrated before cooking, typically for 15-20 minutes in water. The soaking water is often used in cooking for added flavor and nutrients.

COOKING:
Commonly simmered (煮物(にもの)) with oil, soy sauce, and dashi. Also used in salads and stir-fries after rehydrating.

NUTRITION:
Drying concentrates the nutrients, making it higher in calcium, iron, and fiber than fresh daikon. A traditional winter staple food.
