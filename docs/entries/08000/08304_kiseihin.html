既製品(きせいひん)

きせいひん
noun
ready-made product; off-the-shelf item
1. ready-made product; off-the-shelf item; mass-produced goods
products made in advance and sold in standard sizes or specifications, as opposed to custom-made or handmade items
既製品(きせいひん)のスーツを買(か)った。
I bought an off-the-rack suit.
既製品(きせいひん)では合(あ)うサイズがなかった。
There was no suitable size among the ready-made products.
手作(てづく)りより既製品(きせいひん)のほうが安(やす)くて早(はや)い。
Ready-made products are cheaper and faster than handmade ones.

FORMATION:
既製(きせい) (ready-made, pre-made) + 品(ひん) (item, goods)

CONTRAST:

  • 手作(てづく)り (handmade) - opposite concept
  • オーダーメイド (custom-made) - opposite concept
  • 注文品(ちゅうもんひん) (custom order item)

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 既製品(きせいひん)の服(ふく) (ready-to-wear clothing)
  • 既製(きせい)服(ふく) (ready-made clothes)

NUANCE:
While 既製品(きせいひん) is neutral, it can sometimes imply less personalization compared to custom or handmade items.
