あいつ

あいつ
pronoun
that guy; that person (rough/informal)
1. that guy; that person (rough/informal)
an informal or rough pronoun referring to a third person who is distant from both speaker and listener
あいつは信用(しんよう)できない。
That guy can't be trusted.
あいつが言(い)ったことは本当(ほんとう)だ。
What that person said is true.
あいつとはもう(もう)二度(にど)と会(あ)いたくない。
I never want to see that person again.

REGISTER:
Informal/rough. Can sound contemptuous or rude depending on context. Often used among male friends casually.

KO-SO-A-DO SYSTEM:

Part of the demonstrative pronoun system for people:

  • こいつ (this guy - near speaker)
  • そいつ (that guy - near listener)
  • あいつ (that guy - distant from both)
  • どいつ (which guy - interrogative)

KANJI:
Can be written 彼奴(あいつ) but usually in hiragana.

NUANCE:

  • Casual/rough among friends: affectionate
  • About strangers/enemies: contemptuous
  • Neutral gender but often used for males

POLITE ALTERNATIVES:

  • あの人(ひと) (that person - polite)
  • あの方(かた) (that person - formal)
  • 彼(かれ)/彼女(かのじょ) (he/she - neutral)

RELATED:

  • こいつ (this guy)
  • そいつ (that guy)
  • どいつ (which guy)
  • あの人(ひと) (that person - polite)
