満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)

まんいんでんしゃ
noun
packed train; crowded train
1. packed train; crowded train; train at full capacity
a train that is extremely crowded with passengers, typically during rush hours in urban Japan
毎朝(まいあさ)満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)で通勤(つうきん)している。
I commute on a packed train every morning.
満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)に乗(の)るのはストレスがたまる。
Riding packed trains is stressful.
満員電車(まんいんでんしゃ)を避(さ)けるために、早(はや)めに家(いえ)を出(で)るようにしている。
I try to leave home early to avoid the packed trains.

FORMATION:
満員(まんいん) (full capacity) + 電車(でんしゃ) (train) → full-capacity train

JAPANESE RUSH HOUR:
During peak commuting hours (通勤(つうきん)ラッシュ) in major cities like Tokyo and Osaka, trains can reach 180-200% of their designed capacity.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 通勤(つうきん)ラッシュ (commuter rush)
  • 帰宅(きたく)ラッシュ (evening rush)
  • 吊(つ)り革(かわ) (hanging strap)
  • 押(お)し屋(や) (train pusher - staff who push passengers into trains)

COMMON EXPERIENCES:

  • 身動(みうご)きが取(と)れない (unable to move)
  • 潰(つぶ)されそう (feeling crushed)
  • スマホも見(み)られない (can't even look at phone)

TIPS FOR AVOIDING:

  • 時差出勤(じさしゅっきん) (staggered commuting)
  • リモートワーク (remote work)
  • 各駅停車(かくえきていしゃ)に乗(の)る (take the local train)
