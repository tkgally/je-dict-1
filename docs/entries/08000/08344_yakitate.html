焼(や)き立(た)て

やきたて
noun, no-adjective
freshly baked; just out of the oven
1. freshly baked; just grilled; hot out of the oven
describes baked or grilled food that has just been cooked and is still hot
焼(や)き立(た)てのクロワッサンを買(か)った。
I bought a freshly baked croissant.
焼(や)き立(た)てのパンの香(かお)りがたまらない。
The smell of freshly baked bread is irresistible.
この店(みせ)は焼(や)き立(た)てのピザを提供(ていきょう)している。
This restaurant serves freshly baked pizza.

FORMATION:
焼(や)く (to bake, grill) + 立(た)て (just after ~ing) → just after baking

FUNCTIONS AS:

  • Noun: 焼(や)き立(た)てがおいしい (freshly baked is delicious)
  • No-adjective: 焼(や)き立(た)てのパン (freshly baked bread)

COMMON USES:

  • 焼(や)き立(た)てパン (freshly baked bread)
  • 焼(や)き立(た)てクッキー (freshly baked cookies)
  • 焼(や)き立(た)て肉(にく) (freshly grilled meat)
  • 焼(や)き立(た)て焼(や)き鳥(とり) (freshly grilled yakitori)

RELATED ～立(た)て WORDS:

  • 出来立(できた)て (freshly made - general)
  • 揚(あ)げ立(た)て (freshly fried)
  • 炊(た)き立(た)て (freshly cooked rice)

POPULAR CULTURE:
焼(や)きたて!!ジャぱん (Yakitate!! Japan) is a famous manga/anime about bread-making that popularized the term.
