タイムセール

たいむせーる
noun
time-limited sale; flash sale
1. time-limited sale; flash sale
a sale that is only available for a limited period, often a few hours
タイムセールが始(はじ)まった。
The time-limited sale has started.
夕方(ゆうがた)のタイムセールで弁当(べんとう)が半額(はんがく)になる。
Bento boxes are half price during the evening time sale.
Amazon のタイムセールで欲(ほ)しかったものを安(やす)く買(か)えた。
I was able to buy what I wanted cheaply during Amazon's flash sale.

タイムセール is a wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) term for limited-time sales promotions.

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • Supermarkets: Evening タイムセール on 弁当(べんとう), 惣菜(そうざい), and 刺身(さしみ) before closing
  • Online shopping: Amazon, 楽天(らくてん) flash sales lasting a few hours
  • Department stores: Seasonal clearance events

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • タイムセールが始(はじ)まる: the flash sale starts
  • タイムセールで買(か)う: to buy during a flash sale
  • タイムセール中(ちゅう): during the sale period

RELATED TERMS:

  • 特売(とくばい): special sale (general)
  • 半額(はんがく)セール: half-price sale
  • 閉店(へいてん)セール: closing sale
  • ゲリラセール: unannounced flash sale
