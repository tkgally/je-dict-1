風化(ふうか)

ふうか
noun
weathering; fading from memory
1. weathering; erosion
the natural process by which rocks, soil, or structures are worn down by wind, rain, and other environmental factors
岩(いわ)が風化(ふうか)する。
The rock weathers.
長年(ながねん)の風化(ふうか)で彫刻(ちょうこく)が傷(いた)んでいる。
The sculpture is damaged from years of weathering.
風化(ふうか)作用(さよう)で地形(ちけい)が変(か)わる。
The terrain changes due to weathering.
2. fading from memory; being forgotten
the gradual process of events, memories, or issues losing their significance or being forgotten over time
記憶(きおく)の風化(ふうか)を防(ふせ)ぐ。
To prevent memories from fading.
事件(じけん)を風化(ふうか)させてはならない。
We must not let the incident fade from memory.
時間(じかん)とともに事実(じじつ)が風化(ふうか)していく。
Facts gradually fade from memory over time.

風化(ふうか) has both a geological and a metaphorical meaning.

KANJI BREAKDOWN:

  • 風(ふう) (wind)
  • 化(か) (change, transform)

SENSE 1 (Geological weathering):

  • 風化(ふうか)作用(さよう) (weathering action/process)
  • 物理(ぶつり)的(てき)風化(ふうか) (physical weathering)
  • 化学(かがく)的(てき)風化(ふうか) (chemical weathering)

SENSE 2 (Fading from memory):

This metaphorical usage is common in discussions about historical events, tragedies, or social issues.

  • 記憶(きおく)の風化(ふうか) (fading of memory)
  • 風化(ふうか)させない (to not let something be forgotten)
  • 風化(ふうか)を防(ふせ)ぐ (to prevent fading from memory)

USAGE:

  • 風化(ふうか)する (to weather; to fade from memory - suru verb)
