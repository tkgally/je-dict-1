寂(さび)しさ

さびしさ
noun
loneliness; feeling of loneliness; sadness
1. loneliness; feeling of loneliness; sadness
the emotional state or feeling of being lonely or desolate
寂(さび)しさを感(かん)じる。
To feel loneliness.
別(わか)れの寂(さび)しさに耐(た)える。
To endure the sadness of parting.
一人(ひとり)暮(ぐ)らしの寂(さび)しさを紛(まぎ)らわす。
To distract oneself from the loneliness of living alone.

FORMATION:
寂(さび)しい (lonely - adjective) + さ (nominalizing suffix)

USAGE:

  • 寂(さび)しさを感(かん)じる (to feel loneliness)
  • 寂(さび)しさに耐(た)える (to endure loneliness)
  • 寂(さび)しさを紛(まぎ)らわす (to distract from loneliness)
  • 寂(さび)しさを埋(う)める (to fill the loneliness)

VARIANT:
淋(さび)しさ is an alternative kanji spelling with the same meaning.

RELATED EMOTIONS:

  • 孤独(こどく) (solitude, isolation)
  • 切(せつ)なさ (bittersweetness, sadness)
  • 虚(むな)しさ (emptiness)

RELATED:

  • 寂(さび)しい (lonely - adjective)
  • 孤独感(こどくかん) (feeling of loneliness)
  • 郷愁(きょうしゅう) (nostalgia, homesickness)
