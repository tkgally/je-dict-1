早期退職(そうきたいしょく)

そうきたいしょく
noun
early retirement
1. early retirement
retirement from work before the standard retirement age, often with incentive packages
早期退職(そうきたいしょく)を選(えら)んだ。
I chose early retirement.
会社(かいしゃ)が早期退職(そうきたいしょく)制度(せいど)を導入(どうにゅう)した。
The company introduced an early retirement program.
早期退職(そうきたいしょく)すれば割増(わりまし)退職金(たいしょくきん)がもらえる。
If you take early retirement, you can receive an enhanced severance package.

早期退職(そうきたいしょく) refers to retiring from work before the standard retirement age, often with incentive packages.

WORD FORMATION:
早期(そうき) (early stage) + 退職(たいしょく) (retirement)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 早期退職(そうきたいしょく)を選(えら)ぶ: to choose early retirement
  • 早期退職(そうきたいしょく)制度(せいど): early retirement program
  • 早期退職(そうきたいしょく)優遇(ゆうぐう): early retirement incentives

RELATED TERMS:

  • 希望退職(きぼうたいしょく): voluntary retirement
  • 定年退職(ていねんたいしょく): mandatory retirement
  • 退職金(たいしょくきん): severance pay
  • 割増(わりまし)退職金(たいしょくきん): enhanced severance

CONTEXT:
Often offered during corporate restructuring. Japan's standard retirement age is typically 60-65.
