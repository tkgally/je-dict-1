置(お)き換(か)える

おきかえる
verb (transitive)
to replace; to substitute; to rephrase
1. to replace; to substitute
to put something in place of another
古(ふる)い部品(ぶひん)を新(あたら)しいものに置(お)き換(か)えた。
I replaced the old parts with new ones.
AをBに置(お)き換(か)えて考(かんが)える。
Think of it by substituting A with B.
変数(へんすう)を値(あたい)に置(お)き換(か)える。
Replace the variable with the value.
2. to rephrase; to put in different terms
to express the same idea using different words
別(べつ)の言葉(ことば)に置(お)き換(か)えると。
To put it in other words.
自分(じぶん)の言葉(ことば)に置(お)き換(か)えて説明(せつめい)する。
Explain it by putting it in your own words.
難(むずか)しい表現(ひょうげん)を簡単(かんたん)な言葉(ことば)に置(お)き換(か)えた。
I rephrased the difficult expression in simple words.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))

USAGE:

  • AをBに置(お)き換(か)える (to replace A with B)
  • 言葉(ことば)に置(お)き換(か)える (to put into words)

RELATED:

  • 入(い)れ替(か)える (to swap, exchange)
  • 取(と)り替(か)える (to exchange, replace)
  • 言(い)い換(か)える (to rephrase - speaking)
