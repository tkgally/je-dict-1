収支(しゅうし)

しゅうし
noun
income and expenses; balance
1. income and expenses; revenue and expenditure; balance
the combined consideration of money coming in (income) and money going out (expenses); used to discuss financial balance
収支(しゅうし)のバランスを取(と)る。
To balance income and expenses.
今月(こんげつ)の収支(しゅうし)は赤字(あかじ)だった。
This month's balance was in the red.
家計(かけい)の収支(しゅうし)を記録(きろく)している。
I'm keeping a record of our household income and expenses.

FORMATION:
収(しゅう) (income, taking in) + 支(し) (expenditure, paying out) → income and outflow

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 収支(しゅうし)が合(あ)う (to break even)
  • 収支(しゅうし)トントン (breaking even, neither profit nor loss)
  • 収支(しゅうし)報告(ほうこく) (financial report)
  • 収支(しゅうし)決算(けっさん) (balance of accounts)

TYPES:

  • 年間(ねんかん)収支(しゅうし) (annual balance)
  • 月間(げっかん)収支(しゅうし) (monthly balance)
  • 貿易(ぼうえき)収支(しゅうし) (trade balance)
  • 経常(けいじょう)収支(しゅうし) (current account balance)

RELATED:

  • 収入(しゅうにゅう) (income)
  • 支出(ししゅつ) (expenditure)
  • 黒字(くろじ) (profit, in the black)
  • 赤字(あかじ) (deficit, in the red)
