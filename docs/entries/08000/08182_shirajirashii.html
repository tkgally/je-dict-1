白々(しらじら)しい

しらじらしい
i-adjective
feigned; unconvincing; blatant
1. feigned; unconvincing; obviously fake
describes behavior or words that are clearly pretended or insincere
白々(しらじら)しい嘘(うそ)をつくな。
Don't tell such an obvious lie.
彼(かれ)の言(い)い訳(わけ)は白々(しらじら)しかった。
His excuse was unconvincing.
知(し)っているくせに白々(しらじら)しい顔(かお)をしている。
Even though they know, they're pretending not to with a straight face.

USAGE:

  • 白々(しらじら)しい嘘(うそ) (blatant lie)
  • 白々(しらじら)しい態度(たいど) (feigned attitude)
  • 白々(しらじら)しい顔(かお)をする (to put on an innocent face)

NUANCE:
Strongly implies the speaker can see through the pretense. Used when someone's deception or insincerity is painfully obvious.

RELATED:

  • 空々(そらぞら)しい (similar meaning - forced, unnatural)
  • 白(しら)を切(き)る (to feign ignorance)

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 白々(しらじら)しく
general
