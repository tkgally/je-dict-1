1. to be shy; to be embarrassed; to feel awkward
to feel self-conscious or bashful, typically when receiving praise or attention
褒(ほ)められて照(て)れた。
I was embarrassed when they praised me.
彼(かれ)は照(て)れながら返事(へんじ)をした。
He replied shyly.
そんなに見(み)ないで、照(て)れるから。
Don't stare at me like that, it's embarrassing.
愛(あい)してるって言(い)われて照(て)れてしまった。
I got embarrassed when they said 'I love you.'
彼女(かのじょ)は写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)られると照(て)れる性格(せいかく)だ。
She's the type who gets shy when her photo is taken.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 褒(ほ)められて照(て)れる (get embarrassed when praised)
  • 照(て)れながら (shyly, bashfully)
  • 照(て)れくさい (feel awkward/shy)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 照(て)れ隠(かく)し (hiding one's embarrassment)
  • 照(て)れ屋(や) (shy person)
  • 恥(は)ずかしい (embarrassed, ashamed - broader meaning)

NUANCE:
照(て)れる implies a cute, bashful kind of embarrassment, often in positive situations like receiving compliments or attention. It differs from 恥(は)ずかしい which can be used for more serious embarrassment or shame.

Related Words

Contrast: 恥(は)ずかしい (embarrassed (broader))
