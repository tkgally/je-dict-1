中古品(ちゅうこひん)

ちゅうこひん
noun
used item; secondhand goods
1. used item; secondhand goods; pre-owned product
an item that has been previously owned or used; sold at a lower price than new items
この車(くるま)は中古品(ちゅうこひん)です。
This car is used.
中古品(ちゅうこひん)でも状態(じょうたい)がよければ問題(もんだい)ない。
Even a used item is fine if it's in good condition.
中古品(ちゅうこひん)を買(か)ったら半額(はんがく)で済(す)んだ。
I bought a used item and it cost only half the price.

FORMATION:
中古(ちゅうこ) (used, secondhand) + 品(ひん) (item, goods)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 新品(しんぴん) (brand new item) - opposite
  • 中古車(ちゅうこしゃ) (used car)
  • 中古(ちゅうこ)マンション (pre-owned apartment)
  • リサイクルショップ (secondhand shop)

USAGE NOTE:
中古(ちゅうこ) alone can function as a no-adjective meaning "used" or "secondhand." Adding 品(ひん) emphasizes that it's a product or goods.

ALTERNATE READING:
The word 中古(ちゅうこ) is sometimes read as ちゅうぶる in certain dialects or older usage.
