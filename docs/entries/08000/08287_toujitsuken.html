当日券(とうじつけん)

とうじつけん
noun
same-day ticket; day ticket; door ticket
1. same-day ticket; day ticket; door ticket
a ticket purchased on the day of the event, as opposed to tickets bought in advance
当日券(とうじつけん)は売(う)り切(き)れだった。
The same-day tickets were sold out.
当日券(とうじつけん)は前売(まえう)りより高(たか)い。
Same-day tickets are more expensive than advance tickets.
当日券(とうじつけん)を求(もと)めて朝(あさ)から並(なら)んだ。
I lined up from the morning to get same-day tickets.

FORMATION:
当日(とうじつ) (same day) + 券(けん) (ticket)

PRICING:
当日券(とうじつけん) is typically priced higher than 前売(まえう)り券(けん) (advance tickets), often by 500-1000 yen.

AVAILABILITY:

For popular events, 当日券(とうじつけん) may:

  • Sell out quickly
  • Not be available at all
  • Be limited in quantity

RELATED TERMS:

  • 前売(まえう)り券(けん) / 前売券(ぜんばいけん) (advance ticket)
  • 整理券(せいりけん) (numbered ticket for queueing)
  • チケット (ticket)
  • 入場券(にゅうじょうけん) (admission ticket)

RELATED:

  • 前売(まえう)り (advance sale)
  • 即売(そくばい) (immediate sale)
  • 売(う)り切(き)れ (sold out)

Related Words

Antonym: 前売(まえう)り (advance sale)
