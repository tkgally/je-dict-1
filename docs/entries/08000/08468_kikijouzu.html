聞(き)き上手(じょうず)

ききじょうず
noun
good listener
1. good listener; skilled at listening
a person who is skilled at listening attentively and making others feel heard
彼女(かのじょ)は聞(き)き上手(じょうず)だ。
She's a good listener.
聞(き)き上手(じょうず)な人(ひと)は信頼(しんらい)される。
People who are good listeners are trusted.
聞(き)き上手(じょうず)になるには、相槌(あいづち)を打(う)つことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
To become a good listener, it's important to give appropriate responses.

COMPOUND:
聞(き)く (to listen) + 上手(じょうず) (skilled at)

CULTURAL IMPORTANCE:

In Japanese culture, being a 聞(き)き上手(じょうず) is highly valued. It involves:

  • 相槌(あいづち) - giving verbal feedback (うん、へぇ、そうなんだ)
  • Not interrupting
  • Showing genuine interest
  • Creating a comfortable atmosphere for sharing

NA-ADJECTIVE USE:
聞(き)き上手(じょうず)な人(ひと) - a person who's good at listening

RELATED:

  • 話(はな)し上手(じょうず) - good speaker
  • 傾聴(けいちょう) - active listening (formal term)
  • 聞(き)き役(やく) - the role of listener
