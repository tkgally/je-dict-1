接戦(せっせん)

せっせん
noun
close game; close contest
1. close game; close contest; close match
a competition or match where the scores are very close and the outcome is uncertain until the end
接戦(せっせん)を制(せい)した。
They won the close game.
最後(さいご)まで接戦(せっせん)だった。
It was a close game until the end.
両(りょう)チームの接戦(せっせん)が続(つづ)いた。
The close battle between both teams continued.
接戦(せっせん)の末(すえ)、一点差(いってんさ)で勝(か)った。
After a close game, we won by one point.
選挙(せんきょ)は接戦(せっせん)になると予想(よそう)されている。
The election is expected to be a close race.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 接戦(せっせん)を制(せい)する (to win a close game)
  • 接戦(せっせん)を演(えん)じる (to engage in a close battle)
  • 接戦(せっせん)の末(すえ) (after a close game)
  • 接戦(せっせん)になる (to become a close match)

RELATED WORDS:

  • 大差(たいさ) (large margin - opposite)
  • 熱戦(ねっせん) (fierce battle)
  • 激戦(げきせん) (fierce competition)
  • シーソーゲーム (seesaw game)

USAGE:
Used for sports, elections, and any competitive situation where the outcome is close.
