付箋(ふせん)

ふせん
noun
sticky note; Post-it; tag
1. sticky note; Post-it; tag
a small piece of paper with adhesive backing used for notes, bookmarks, or labels
付箋(ふせん)を貼(は)る。
To stick a Post-it note.
大事(だいじ)なページに付箋(ふせん)を付(つ)けた。
I put a sticky note on the important page.
カラフルな付箋(ふせん)でノートを整理(せいり)する。
I organize my notebook with colorful sticky notes.

USAGE:

  • 付箋(ふせん)を貼(は)る (to stick a note)
  • 付箋(ふせん)を付(つ)ける (to attach a note)
  • 付箋(ふせん)を剥(は)がす (to peel off a note)

TYPES:

  • ポストイット (Post-it - brand name, often used generically)
  • 付箋紙(ふせんし) (sticky note paper)
  • 見出(みだ)し付箋(ふせん) (index tabs)

USES:

  • Bookmarking pages
  • Writing reminders
  • Labeling items
  • Color-coding information

OFFICE/SCHOOL:
Essential stationery item in Japanese offices and schools.

RELATED:

  • メモ (memo, note)
  • 付箋紙(ふせんし) (sticky note paper)
  • しおり (bookmark)
