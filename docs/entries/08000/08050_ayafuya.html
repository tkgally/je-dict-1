あやふや

あやふや
na-adjective
uncertain; vague; unreliable
1. uncertain; vague; unreliable; shaky
lacking certainty or reliability; not clearly defined or remembered
記憶(きおく)があやふやだ。
My memory is hazy.
あやふやな知識(ちしき)で話(はな)すな。
Don't speak based on uncertain knowledge.
説明(せつめい)があやふやで分(わ)かりにくかった。
The explanation was vague and hard to understand.

USAGE:

  • あやふやな記憶(きおく) (hazy memory)
  • あやふやな態度(たいど) (uncertain attitude)
  • あやふやな返事(へんじ) (vague answer)

NUANCE:
あやふや often describes something that should be clear but isn't—like incomplete knowledge, unreliable information, or a shaky foundation.

COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR WORDS:

  • うやむや: deliberately left unclear, often to avoid responsibility
  • あやふや: inherently uncertain or poorly understood
  • 曖昧(あいまい): ambiguous, could be interpreted multiple ways
