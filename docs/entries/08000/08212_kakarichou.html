係長(かかりちょう)

かかりちょう
noun
section chief; subsection head
1. section chief; subsection head
a junior management position in Japanese companies, between supervisor and manager
来月(らいげつ)から係長(かかりちょう)になります。
I will become a section chief starting next month.
係長(かかりちょう)、この書類(しょるい)に印鑑(いんかん)をお願(ねが)いします。
Section chief, could you please stamp this document?
彼(かれ)は入社(にゅうしゃ)五(ご)年目(ねんめ)で係長(かかりちょう)に昇進(しょうしん)した。
He was promoted to section chief in his fifth year at the company.

USAGE:

  • 係長(かかりちょう)に昇進(しょうしん)する (to be promoted to section chief)
  • Can be used as a title when addressing someone

JAPANESE CORPORATE HIERARCHY:
平社員(ひらしゃいん) → 主任(しゅにん) → 係長(かかりちょう) → 課長(かちょう) → 部長(ぶちょう)
(Regular employee → Supervisor → Section chief → Manager → Department head)

NOTE:
係長(かかりちょう) is typically the first official management position in traditional Japanese companies. The title comes from 係(かかり) (charge, duty) + 長(ちょう) (chief, head).
