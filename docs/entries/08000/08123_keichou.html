軽重(けいちょう)

けいちょう
noun
light and heavy; relative importance; weight
1. relative importance; priority; significance
the degree of importance or priority given to something
罪(つみ)の軽重(けいちょう)を判断(はんだん)する。
To judge the severity of a crime.
仕事(しごと)の軽重(けいちょう)に応(おう)じて担当(たんとう)を決(き)める。
To assign responsibilities according to the importance of the work.
命(いのち)の軽重(けいちょう)は問(と)えない。
One cannot weigh the value of lives.
税金(ぜいきん)の軽重(けいちょう)は収入(しゅうにゅう)によって決(き)まる。
Tax burden is determined by income.
責任(せきにん)の軽重(けいちょう)にかかわらず全員(ぜんいん)で対応(たいおう)する。
We will respond as a team regardless of the level of responsibility.
2. light and heavy; weight difference
the physical difference in weight between things
荷物(にもつ)の軽重(けいちょう)を確認(かくにん)する。
To check the weight of the luggage.

USAGE:
Sense 1 (relative importance) is more common in modern usage. Often appears in formal or legal contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 軽重(けいちょう)を問(と)う (to question the relative importance)
  • 軽重(けいちょう)を判断(はんだん)する (to judge the severity/importance)
general
