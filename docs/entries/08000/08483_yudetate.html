茹(ゆ)で立(た)て

ゆでたて
noun / no-adjective
freshly boiled
1. freshly boiled; just boiled
food that has just been boiled and is still hot
茹(ゆ)で立(た)ての卵(たまご)は熱(あつ)い。
A freshly boiled egg is hot.
茹(ゆ)で立(た)ての蕎麦(そば)をすすった。
I slurped up freshly boiled soba noodles.
茹(ゆ)で立(た)てのとうもろこしに塩(しお)をかけて食(た)べた。
I sprinkled salt on freshly boiled corn and ate it.

COMPOUND:
茹(ゆ)でる (to boil) + 立(た)て (just done)

PATTERN:

The ~立(た)て pattern indicates something freshly done:

  • 揚(あ)げ立(た)て - freshly fried
  • 焼(や)き立(た)て - freshly baked
  • 出来立(できた)て - freshly made
  • 採(と)れ立(た)て - freshly picked

COMMON FOODS:

  • 茹(ゆ)で立(た)ての麺(めん) - freshly boiled noodles
  • 茹(ゆ)で立(た)ての枝豆(えだまめ) - freshly boiled edamame
  • 茹(ゆ)で立(た)ての卵(たまご) - freshly boiled egg
