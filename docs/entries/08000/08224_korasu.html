1. to concentrate; to focus; to strain (one's eyes, ears, attention)
to direct one's senses or attention intensely toward something
目(め)を凝(こ)らして見(み)た。
I strained my eyes to look.
耳(みみ)を凝(こ)らして聞(き)いた。
I listened intently.
暗闇(くらやみ)に目(め)を凝(こ)らした。
I peered into the darkness.
2. to elaborate; to put great effort into
to apply careful effort and ingenuity
趣向(しゅこう)を凝(こ)らした料理(りょうり)。
Cuisine with elaborate creativity.
工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らしたデザイン。
A design with great ingenuity.
知恵(ちえ)を凝(こ)らして問題(もんだい)を解決(かいけつ)した。
I racked my brain to solve the problem.

凝(こ)らす means to focus or concentrate one's senses intensely, or to put elaborate effort into something. It always takes an object describing what is being focused or elaborated.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive)
  • Related: 凝(こ)る (intransitive, to be absorbed in; to stiffen)

ASPECT (ている):
凝(こ)らしている describes the ongoing state of focusing intently or putting effort into something.

COMMON PATTERNS:

Sense 1 (focus senses):

  • 目(め)を凝(こ)らす: to strain one's eyes, to peer
  • 耳(みみ)を凝(こ)らす: to listen intently

Sense 2 (elaborate effort):

  • 趣向(しゅこう)を凝(こ)らす: to devise with ingenuity
  • 工夫(くふう)を凝(こ)らす: to apply creative effort
  • 知恵(ちえ)を凝(こ)らす: to rack one's brains

USAGE:
This verb almost always appears in set phrases with specific objects. It sounds literary or formal compared to more everyday alternatives like 集中(しゅうちゅう)する.

Related Words

Related: 凝(こ)る (to become stiff; to be absorbed in (intransitive))
