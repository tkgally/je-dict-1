食(しょく)あたり

しょくあたり
noun
food poisoning; upset stomach from food
1. food poisoning; upset stomach caused by food
illness caused by eating contaminated or spoiled food, typically resulting in stomach pain, nausea, or diarrhea
食(しょく)あたりを起(お)こした。
I got food poisoning.
生(なま)ものを食(た)べて食(しょく)あたりになった。
I got food poisoning from eating raw food.
夏(なつ)は食(しょく)あたりに気(き)をつけなければならない。
You have to be careful of food poisoning in summer.

USAGE:

  • 食(しょく)あたりになる (to get food poisoning)
  • 食(しょく)あたりを起(お)こす (to have food poisoning)

SYMPTOMS:
Common symptoms include 吐(は)き気(け) (nausea), 腹痛(ふくつう) (stomach pain), and 下痢(げり) (diarrhea).

RELATED:

  • 食中毒(しょくちゅうどく) (food poisoning - more formal/medical term)
  • 傷(いた)む (to go bad - of food)
