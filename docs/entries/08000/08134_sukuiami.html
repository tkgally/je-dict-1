すくい網(あみ)

すくいあみ
noun
skimmer; strainer; scooping net
1. skimmer; strainer; scooping net (for cooking)
a mesh utensil used for scooping or straining food from liquids
すくい網(あみ)で麺(めん)を取(と)り出(だ)す。
To scoop out the noodles with a strainer.
揚(あ)げ物(もの)をすくい網(あみ)で油(あぶら)から上(あ)げる。
To lift fried food out of the oil with a skimmer.
すくい網(あみ)で天(てん)かすを取(と)り除(のぞ)く。
To remove tempura bits with a skimmer.
細(こま)かいすくい網(あみ)が便利(べんり)だ。
A fine-mesh skimmer is convenient.
2. scooping net; dip net
a net used for scooping fish or other items from water
すくい網(あみ)で金魚(きんぎょ)をすくう。
To scoop goldfish with a net.
落(お)ち葉(ば)をすくい網(あみ)で集(あつ)める。
To collect fallen leaves with a scooping net.

ALSO CALLED:

  • 網杓子(あみじゃくし) (mesh ladle)
  • ザル (colander/strainer - for draining)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 灰汁取(あくと)り (scum skimmer)
  • 穴杓子(あなじゃくし) (slotted spoon)
