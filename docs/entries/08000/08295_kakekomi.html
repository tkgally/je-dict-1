駆(か)け込(こ)み

かけこみ
noun
last-minute rush; rushing in; emergency shelter
1. last-minute rush; rushing in at the last moment
doing something hastily just before a deadline, or rushing into a closing door/entrance
駆(か)け込(こ)み乗車(じょうしゃ)は危険(きけん)です。
Rushing onto trains is dangerous.
締(し)め切(き)り前(まえ)に駆(か)け込(こ)みで申請(しんせい)した。
I applied at the last minute before the deadline.
増税(ぞうぜい)前(まえ)の駆(か)け込(こ)み需要(じゅよう)で売上(うりあげ)が増(ふ)えた。
Sales increased due to last-minute demand before the tax hike.
2. emergency shelter; refuge
a place where people can take refuge in emergencies; also used for shelters for domestic abuse victims
駆(か)け込(こ)み寺(てら)に助(たす)けを求(もと)める。
To seek help at a refuge shelter.
DVの被害者(ひがいしゃ)を駆(か)け込(こ)みシェルターで保護(ほご)する。
To protect domestic violence victims at an emergency shelter.
交番(こうばん)は地域(ちいき)の駆(か)け込(こ)み場所(ばしょ)になっている。
Police boxes serve as emergency refuge points in the community.

FORMATION:
駆(か)ける (to run) + 込(こ)む (to rush into) → rushing in

SENSE 1 - LAST-MINUTE:

  • 駆(か)け込(こ)み乗車(じょうしゃ) (rushing onto trains - dangerous and discouraged)
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)み需要(じゅよう) (last-minute demand, e.g., before price increases)
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)み申請(しんせい) (last-minute application)

SENSE 2 - REFUGE:

  • 駆(か)け込(こ)み寺(てら) (temple refuge - historically for abused women)
  • 駆(か)け込(こ)みシェルター (emergency shelter)

TRAIN SAFETY:
Announcements on Japanese trains frequently warn against 駆(か)け込(こ)み乗車(じょうしゃ) (rushing onto trains before doors close).

VERB FORM:

  • 駆(か)け込(こ)む (to rush in)

RELATED:

  • 滑(すべ)り込(こ)み (slipping in at the last moment)
  • 飛(と)び込(こ)み (jumping in unexpectedly)
