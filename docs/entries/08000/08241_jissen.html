実践(じっせん)

じっせん
noun, suru-verb
practice; action; putting into practice
1. practice; action; putting into practice
actively putting ideas, theories, or plans into action; practical application rather than theory
理論(りろん)を実践(じっせん)する。
To put theory into practice.
計画(けいかく)を実践(じっせん)に移(うつ)す。
To put a plan into action.
彼(かれ)は自分(じぶん)の信念(しんねん)を日々(ひび)実践(じっせん)している。
He puts his beliefs into practice every day.

USAGE:

  • 実践(じっせん)する (to practice, to put into action)
  • 実践(じっせん)に移(うつ)す (to put into practice)
  • 実践的(じっせんてき) (practical - adjective form)

CONTRAST:

  • 理論(りろん) (theory) vs 実践(じっせん) (practice)
  • 学(まな)ぶ (to learn) vs 実践(じっせん)する (to practice)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 実践(じっせん)経験(けいけん) (practical experience)
  • 実践(じっせん)知識(ちしき) (practical knowledge)
  • 実践(じっせん)倫理(りんり) (practical ethics)

RELATED:

  • 実行(じっこう) (execution, carrying out)
  • 実施(じっし) (implementation)
  • 応用(おうよう) (application)
