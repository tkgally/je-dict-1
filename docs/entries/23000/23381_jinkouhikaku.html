人工皮革(じんこうひかく)

じんこうひかく
noun
synthetic leather; artificial leather; man-made leather
1. a man-made material designed to imitate the look, feel, and performance of animal leather; synthetic or artificial leather
An engineered material that imitates real leather. Commonly made with a polyurethane or PVC coating on a fiber base of polyester or nylon. Used in shoes, bags, jackets, furniture, car interiors, sports equipment, and soccer balls. Compared with genuine leather (本革(ほんがわ)), it is cheaper, lighter, easier to clean, and largely animal-free, but tends to be less breathable and less durable over the long term.
この靴(くつ)は人工皮革(じんこうひかく)製(せい)だ。
These shoes are made of synthetic leather.
本革(ほんがわ)より人工皮革(じんこうひかく)のほうが手入(てい)れが楽(らく)だ。
Synthetic leather is easier to take care of than real leather.
最近(さいきん)のサッカーボールは、ほとんどが人工皮革(じんこうひかく)で作(つく)られている。
Recent soccer balls are almost all made from synthetic leather.
人工皮革(じんこうひかく)は動物(どうぶつ)由来(ゆらい)の素材(そざい)を避(さ)けたい人(ひと)にも選(えら)ばれている。
Synthetic leather is also chosen by people who want to avoid materials of animal origin.

Composed of 人工(じんこう) (artificial, man-made) + 皮革(ひかく) (leather, hide). The compound 皮革(ひかく) is itself a more formal/technical word for leather than the everyday 革(かわ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく)製(せい): made of synthetic leather
  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく)の靴(くつ): synthetic-leather shoes
  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく)素材(そざい): synthetic-leather material
  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく)を使(つか)う: to use synthetic leather
  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく)ソファ: synthetic-leather sofa

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 合成皮革(ごうせいひかく): synthetic leather — a near-synonym used especially on product labels; often abbreviated to 合皮(ごうひ).
  • 合皮(ごうひ): pleather — the everyday short form of 合成皮革(ごうせいひかく); very common in fashion and retail.
  • 本革(ほんがわ): genuine / real leather — the standard opposite term.
  • 天然皮革(てんねんひかく): natural leather — a more formal opposite used in product specifications.
  • 革(かわ): leather, hide — the general everyday word, can refer to real or artificial depending on context.

TECHNICAL NOTE:

In the Japanese leather industry, 人工皮革(じんこうひかく) and 合成皮革(ごうせいひかく) are not always perfect synonyms:

  • 合成皮革(ごうせいひかく): a fabric base with a thin plastic (PU or PVC) coating on the surface; structure is clearly two-layer.
  • 人工皮革(じんこうひかく): a nonwoven fiber base impregnated with resin; structure is more three-dimensional and closer to genuine leather in feel and breathability. Ultrasuede and similar premium materials fall in this category.

In everyday conversation, however, the two are often used interchangeably.

TYPICAL PRODUCTS:

  • Shoes and sneakers
  • Bags and wallets
  • Jackets
  • Car seats and interior trim
  • Sofa upholstery
  • Soccer balls and baseball gloves (modern models)
