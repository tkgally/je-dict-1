1. stationing of military forces; maintaining a military presence in a location
The deployment and continued presence of military forces in a particular area, typically a foreign country. A common term in news about international military affairs.
米軍(べいぐん)が日本(にほん)に駐留(ちゅうりゅう)している。
U.S. forces are stationed in Japan.
駐留(ちゅうりゅう)軍(ぐん)の撤退(てったい)が決(き)まった。
The withdrawal of the stationed forces was decided.
海外(かいがい)駐留(ちゅうりゅう)の費用(ひよう)は毎年(まいとし)巨額(きょがく)に上(のぼ)る。
The cost of overseas stationing amounts to an enormous sum every year.

Composed of 駐(ちゅう) (stationed, parked) + 留(りゅう) (stay, remain). Specifically refers to military forces being stationed in a location, usually abroad. One of the key terms for discussing U.S. military bases in Japan and other international security topics.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)軍(ぐん): stationed forces
  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)米軍(べいぐん): U.S. forces stationed (in Japan/abroad)
  • 海外(かいがい)駐留(ちゅうりゅう): overseas stationing
  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)経費(けいひ): stationing costs (host nation support)
  • 駐留(ちゅうりゅう)協定(きょうてい): stationing agreement (SOFA)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 駐屯(ちゅうとん): garrisoning — more specifically about troops being quartered at a base or garrison
  • 派遣(はけん): dispatch, deployment — the act of sending forces, rather than their continued presence
  • 配備(はいび): deployment, positioning — placing military equipment or forces in strategic positions
