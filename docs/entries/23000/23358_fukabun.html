不可分(ふかぶん)

ふかぶん
adjective-na
indivisible, inseparable
1. indivisible, inseparable
So closely connected to something else that the two cannot meaningfully be separated. A formal, abstract word used in writing about concepts, rights, relationships, or processes that must be treated as a unified whole.
心(こころ)と体(からだ)は不可分(ふかぶん)だ。
Mind and body are inseparable.
自由(じゆう)と責任(せきにん)は不可分(ふかぶん)の関係(かんけい)にある。
Freedom and responsibility are in an inseparable relationship.
言語(げんご)と文化(ぶんか)は不可分(ふかぶん)なものだと言(い)われている。
Language and culture are said to be inseparable.
経済(けいざい)発展(はってん)と環境(かんきょう)保護(ほご)は不可分(ふかぶん)の課題(かだい)として議論(ぎろん)されている。
Economic development and environmental protection are being discussed as inseparable issues.

Composed of 不(ふ) (not) + 可(か) (able, possible) + 分(ぶん) (divide, part). Literally 'not-able-to-divide' — meaning something that cannot be split apart. A written, conceptual word that almost always appears with の}{関係(かんけい) (relationship of inseparability) or in the form 不可分(ふかぶん)なX.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不可分(ふかぶん)の関係(かんけい): an inseparable relationship (the most common phrase)
  • 不可分(ふかぶん)の一部(いちぶ): an inseparable part (of something)
  • 不可分(ふかぶん)の存在(そんざい): an inseparable presence
  • 不可分(ふかぶん)な問題(もんだい): an inseparable problem
  • AとBは不可分(ふかぶん)だ: A and B are inseparable
  • AとBは不可分(ふかぶん)に結(むす)びついている: A and B are inseparably linked

USAGE:
Typically used to claim that two things cannot be discussed, solved, or understood in isolation from each other. Common subjects include abstract pairs: mind/body, language/culture, rights/duties, economy/environment. The word is neutral or approving — it frames the connection as essential, not as an awkward entanglement.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 切(き)り離(はな)せない: cannot be separated — an everyday native-Japanese equivalent, more conversational.
  • 一体(いったい): united as one, integrated — emphasizes that two things are one body, rather than that they cannot be divided.
  • 表裏(ひょうり)一体(いったい): two sides of the same coin — a four-character idiom for things that are different faces of one whole.

ANTONYMS:

  • 可分(かぶん): divisible — the direct opposite, but far less common in everyday use.
