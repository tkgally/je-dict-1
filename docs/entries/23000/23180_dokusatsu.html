1. poisoning (to death); killing by poison
The act of killing a person or animal by administering poison. Used in criminal, historical, and literary contexts.
王(おう)は毒殺(どくさつ)された。
The king was poisoned to death.
毒殺(どくさつ)事件(じけん)の犯人(はんにん)が逮捕(たいほ)された。
The perpetrator of the poisoning was arrested.
ミステリー小説(しょうせつ)では毒殺(どくさつ)がよく使(つか)われるトリックだ。
Poisoning is a trick often used in mystery novels.

Composed of 毒(どく) (poison) and 殺(さつ) (killing). Refers specifically to killing by poison, as opposed to other methods. Frequently encountered in historical accounts (court intrigues, assassinations), crime fiction, and news reports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 毒殺(どくさつ)する: to poison (someone) to death
  • 毒殺(どくさつ)事件(じけん): poisoning case
  • 毒殺(どくさつ)未遂(みすい): attempted poisoning
  • 毒殺(どくさつ)された: was poisoned to death

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 中毒(ちゅうどく): poisoning — refers to the medical condition of being poisoned, not necessarily intentional killing
  • 暗殺(あんさつ): assassination — killing by any method, typically for political reasons
  • 殺害(さつがい): killing, murder — general term for killing, regardless of method
