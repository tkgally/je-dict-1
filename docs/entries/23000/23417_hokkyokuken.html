北極圏(ほっきょくけん)

ほっきょくけん
noun
the Arctic Circle; the Arctic region
1. the Arctic Circle; the imaginary line of latitude at about 66°33′ north, marking the southern limit of the area where the sun does not set on at least one day a year
Refers to the parallel of latitude that defines the boundary of the Arctic. North of this line, there is at least one day per year on which the sun does not set (the midnight sun) and at least one on which it does not rise (polar night). The same word is also used loosely to mean the entire region inside that line — the high-latitude lands and seas of the far north, including parts of Russia, Canada, the United States (Alaska), Greenland, Iceland, and the Scandinavian countries.
北極圏(ほっきょくけん)は寒(さむ)い。
The Arctic Circle is cold.
オーロラを見(み)に北極圏(ほっきょくけん)まで行(い)った。
I went all the way to the Arctic Circle to see the northern lights.
北極圏(ほっきょくけん)では夏(なつ)の間(あいだ)、太陽(たいよう)が沈(しず)まない日(ひ)がある。
In the Arctic Circle, there are days during the summer when the sun does not set.
近年(きんねん)、地球温暖化(ちきゅうおんだんか)の影響(えいきょう)で北極圏(ほっきょくけん)の氷(こおり)が急速(きゅうそく)に減(へ)っていると報告(ほうこく)されている。
It has been reported that in recent years, the ice in the Arctic region has been rapidly shrinking due to the effects of global warming.

Composed of 北極(ほっきょく) ('the North Pole; the Arctic') + 圏(けん) ('sphere, region, zone'). The suffix 圏(けん) forms a productive set of geographic and conceptual zone terms: 首都圏(しゅとけん) ('the Tokyo metropolitan area'), 成層圏(せいそうけん) ('the stratosphere'), 勢力圏(せいりょくけん) ('sphere of influence').

USAGE:
In strict geographical use, 北極圏(ほっきょくけん) denotes the latitude line at about 66°33′N — the formal boundary of the Arctic. In journalistic and everyday use it usually refers more broadly to the Arctic region as a whole, including the lands and seas inside that line.

It is the standard word in Japanese news coverage of climate change, polar exploration, and the indigenous peoples of the far north.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)に住(す)む: to live in the Arctic
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)を旅行(りょこう)する: to travel in the Arctic
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)の氷(こおり): Arctic ice
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)の動物(どうぶつ): Arctic animals
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)の先住民(せんじゅうみん): indigenous peoples of the Arctic
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)を越(こ)える: to cross the Arctic Circle
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)諸国(しょこく): Arctic nations
  • 北極圏(ほっきょくけん)の気温(きおん): temperatures in the Arctic

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 北極(ほっきょく): the North Pole; the Arctic — refers to the pole itself or the polar region in general; less specific than 北極圏(ほっきょくけん) as to boundary.
  • 南極圏(なんきょくけん): the Antarctic Circle; the Antarctic region — the southern parallel.
  • 南極(なんきょく): the South Pole; Antarctica.
  • 極地(きょくち): polar region — a more general term covering both polar areas.
  • 寒帯(かんたい): frigid zone — one of the traditional climatic zones of the earth, roughly corresponding to the polar areas.
  • 亜寒帯(あかんたい): subarctic zone — the cold-temperate region just south of the Arctic.

DOMAIN NOTE:
The Arctic Circle is a topic of growing strategic and environmental importance in Japanese news. Articles on Arctic shipping routes (北極海航路(ほっきょっかいこうろ)), polar bears, indigenous peoples, and climate change frequently use this term.
