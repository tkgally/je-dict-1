鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)

とりひきにく
noun
ground chicken; minced chicken
1. ground chicken; minced chicken
Chicken meat that has been finely chopped or passed through a grinder. A common ingredient in Japanese cooking, especially for meatballs, dumplings, and simmered dishes.
鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)でつくねを作(つく)る。
To make tsukune (chicken meatballs) with ground chicken.
鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)は豚(ぶた)ひき肉(にく)よりあっさりしている。
Ground chicken is lighter in flavor than ground pork.
鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)に生姜(しょうが)と醤油(しょうゆ)を混(ま)ぜて、餃子(ぎょうざ)の具(ぐ)にした。
I mixed ground chicken with ginger and soy sauce to use as dumpling filling.

A compound of 鶏(とり) (chicken), ひき (ground/minced, from 挽(ひ)く 'to grind'), and 肉(にく) (meat). Also written as 鶏挽肉(とりひきにく) or 鶏挽(とりひ)き肉(にく), though the mixed kana-kanji spelling is most common on packaging.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)のそぼろ: crumbled seasoned chicken (for rice bowls)
  • 鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)のつくね: chicken meatballs
  • 鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)のハンバーグ: chicken hamburger patty
  • 鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく)百(ひゃく)グラム: 100 grams of ground chicken

RELATED WORDS:

  • 豚(ぶた)ひき肉(にく): ground pork — fattier, used in 餃子(ぎょうざ) and 麻婆豆腐(まーぼーどうふ)
  • 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく): ground beef — used for hamburgers and meat sauce
  • 合(あい)いびき肉(にく): ground meat blend (usually pork and beef)
  • ひき肉(にく): ground meat — the general term

USAGE:
鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく) is considered the healthiest of the common ground meats due to its low fat content. It is a staple ingredient for 鶏(とり)そぼろ丼(どん) (chicken soboro rice bowl), a popular lunch item.
