月齢(げつれい)

げつれい
noun
moon age; lunar age; age in months (of a baby)
1. moon age; lunar age; number of days since the new moon
The number of days elapsed since the last new moon, used to describe the current phase of the moon.
今日(きょう)の月齢(げつれい)は十五(じゅうご)だ。
Today's moon age is fifteen.
月齢(げつれい)によって潮(しお)の満(み)ち引(ひ)きが変(か)わる。
The tides change depending on the lunar age.
釣(つ)りに行(い)くときは、月齢(げつれい)を確認(かくにん)してから計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる。
When going fishing, I check the moon age before making plans.
2. age in months (of a baby or infant)
The age of a baby measured in months rather than years. Commonly used in childcare and pediatric contexts.
月齢(げつれい)六(ろっ)か月(げつ)の赤(あか)ちゃん。
A six-month-old baby.
月齢(げつれい)に合(あ)った離乳食(りにゅうしょく)を選(えら)ぶ。
To choose baby food appropriate for the baby's age in months.
この予防接種(よぼうせっしゅ)は月齢(げつれい)三(さん)か月(げつ)から受(う)けられます。
This vaccination can be given from three months of age.

A word with two distinct usage contexts: astronomy/nature and childcare. The astronomical sense is important for fishing, gardening, and traditional calendar activities.

SENSE 1 — ASTRONOMY:
月齢(げつれい) counts the days from the new moon (新月(しんげつ), 月齢(げつれい) 0). A full moon is approximately 月齢(げつれい) 15. Fishermen, gardeners, and tide watchers use this information regularly.

SENSE 2 — CHILDCARE:
In Japanese childcare, babies' ages are often given in months (月齢(げつれい)) rather than years until they turn one or two. Developmental milestones and vaccination schedules are typically described by 月齢(げつれい).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 月例(げつれい): monthly (event) — a homophone with different kanji; means 'regular monthly' (e.g., 月例(げつれい)会議(かいぎ) monthly meeting)
  • 年齢(ねんれい): age (in years) — the standard word for a person's age
  • 週齢(しゅうれい): age in weeks — used in animal husbandry and early infant care
