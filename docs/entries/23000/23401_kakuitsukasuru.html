1. to make a group of things, people, or systems all the same — in form, content, or treatment — regardless of individual differences; to standardize, to make uniform, to homogenize
To forcibly bring a varied group into a single uniform shape, often with a negative nuance of ignoring individuality or local differences. The word is most commonly used to criticize overly uniform education, fashion, architecture, or business practices that suppress diversity. It is often translated as 'standardize' or 'homogenize,' but unlike neutral English 'standardize,' Japanese 画一化(かくいつか)する usually implies 'flatten into sameness' rather than 'bring up to a quality standard.'
教育(きょういく)を画一化(かくいつか)する。
To make education uniform.
街(まち)の風景(ふうけい)が画一化(かくいつか)されてしまった。
The townscape has been homogenized.
製品(せいひん)の品質(ひんしつ)を画一化(かくいつか)することで、不良(ふりょう)率(りつ)を下(さ)げた。
We lowered the defect rate by standardizing product quality.
子(こ)どもの個性(こせい)を無視(むし)して画一化(かくいつか)するような教(おし)え方(かた)では、創造性(そうぞうせい)は育(そだ)たない。
Creativity cannot grow under a style of teaching that ignores children's individuality and tries to make everyone alike.

Composed of 画一(かくいつ) (uniform, identical) + 化(か) (-ization, a common suffix turning a noun into a verb meaning 'to make into') + する. 画一(かくいつ) itself is built from 画(かく) ('stroke, line') + 一(いつ) ('one'), originally evoking the idea of drawing everything with a single line — all exactly the same.

USAGE:

  • Object + を画一化(かくいつか)する: to standardize / homogenize [object]
  • Subject + が画一化(かくいつか)する (intransitive usage): to become uniform
  • The passive 画一化(かくいつか)される is very common, as this kind of flattening is typically felt as something that happens to a system rather than something someone actively chooses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 教育(きょういく)を画一化(かくいつか)する: to make education uniform
  • 街並(まちな)みが画一化(かくいつか)する: (for) townscapes to become homogenized
  • 品質(ひんしつ)を画一化(かくいつか)する: to standardize quality (neutral / positive nuance)
  • 価値観(かちかん)の画一化(かくいつか): homogenization of values
  • 文化(ぶんか)の画一化(かくいつか): cultural homogenization

NUANCE:
画一化(かくいつか)する often carries a critical or negative tone — the speaker is complaining that individuality and diversity are being lost. When it is used in a positive technical sense (e.g., standardizing manufacturing quality), the neutral word 標準化(ひょうじゅんか)する is usually preferred.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 標準化(ひょうじゅんか)する: to standardize — neutral, technical; used for setting agreed specifications.
  • 均一化(きんいつか)する: to make even / uniform — emphasizes evening out levels or amounts.
  • 統一(とういつ)する: to unify — broader; can mean bringing separate things under a single system or format.
  • 一律(いちりつ)にする: to treat all cases alike / apply uniformly — often refers to applying the same rule to everyone.
  • 没個性化(ぼつこせいか)する: to deprive of individuality — a more explicitly negative near-synonym.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 画一的(かくいつてき): uniform, standardized (adjective form, often with the same critical nuance)
  • 画一主義(かくいつしゅぎ): uniformism, one-size-fits-all approach
  • 多様化(たようか)する: to diversify — the direct opposite.

REGISTER:
Written and somewhat formal. Common in editorials, essays, and academic writing about education, urban planning, and culture. Not an everyday conversational verb.
