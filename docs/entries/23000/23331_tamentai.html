多面体(ためんたい)

ためんたい
noun
polyhedron
1. polyhedron (geometry)
A three-dimensional solid whose surface consists of flat polygonal faces joined at straight edges and sharp corners (vertices). A standard term from geometry.
正(せい)多面体(ためんたい)は五(いつ)つある。
There are five regular polyhedra.
サイコロは立方体(りっぽうたい)という多面体(ためんたい)だ。
A die is a polyhedron called a cube.
この問題(もんだい)では、多面体(ためんたい)の表面積(ひょうめんせき)を計算(けいさん)する。
In this problem, we calculate the surface area of a polyhedron.
美術館(びじゅつかん)の中央(ちゅうおう)には、ガラスでできた巨大(きょだい)な多面体(ためんたい)の彫刻(ちょうこく)が置(お)かれていた。
In the center of the museum stood a huge polyhedron sculpture made of glass.

Composed of 多(た) (many) + 面(めん) (face, surface) + 体(たい) (body, solid) — literally "many-faced solid." A technical term from geometry that refers to any solid with flat polygonal faces.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 正(せい)多面体(ためんたい): regular polyhedron (Platonic solid)
  • 多面体(ためんたい)の頂点(ちょうてん): vertex of a polyhedron
  • 多面体(ためんたい)の辺(へん): edge of a polyhedron
  • 多面体(ためんたい)の面(めん): face of a polyhedron
  • 多面体(ためんたい)の体積(たいせき): volume of a polyhedron

RELATED TERMS:

  • 立方体(りっぽうたい): cube — a regular polyhedron with six square faces
  • 四面体(しめんたい): tetrahedron — a polyhedron with four triangular faces
  • 八面体(はちめんたい): octahedron — a polyhedron with eight faces
  • 十二面体(じゅうにめんたい): dodecahedron — a polyhedron with twelve faces
  • 二十面体(にじゅうめんたい): icosahedron — a polyhedron with twenty faces

USAGE:
The word is used in classrooms, textbooks, and technical writing about mathematics, crystallography, and computer graphics. In casual descriptions of shapes, Japanese speakers more often refer to the specific shape (e.g., サイコロ型(がた), cube-shaped) rather than using the umbrella word 多面体(ためんたい).
