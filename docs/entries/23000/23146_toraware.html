とらわれ

とらわれ
noun
obsession; fixation; being bound by preconceptions
1. obsession; fixation; being captive to an idea or preconception
The state of being mentally trapped or fixated on a particular idea, convention, or way of thinking. Often implies rigidity or inability to think freely.
常識(じょうしき)へのとらわれを捨(す)てよう。
Let's let go of our fixation on conventional thinking.
過去(かこ)のとらわれから自由(じゆう)になりたい。
I want to be free from being captive to the past.
形式(けいしき)へのとらわれを捨(す)てて、もっと柔軟(じゅうなん)に考(かんが)えるべきだ。
You should let go of your fixation on formality and think more flexibly.

The noun form of the verb 囚(とら)われる (to be captured, to be bound by). As a noun, it refers to mental captivity — being stuck in a particular mindset, assumption, or preconception. Written in hiragana rather than kanji in most modern usage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • とらわれを捨(す)てる: to let go of one's fixations
  • とらわれから解放(かいほう)される: to be freed from one's obsessions
  • 常識(じょうしき)へのとらわれ: fixation on common sense/convention
  • 過去(かこ)のとらわれ: captivity to the past

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 執着(しゅうちゃく): attachment, clinging — stronger emotional attachment to something one does not want to let go of
  • 固定観念(こていかんねん): fixed idea, preconception — the idea itself, rather than the state of being bound by it
  • 偏見(へんけん): prejudice, bias — specifically a biased view, often toward people or groups
  • こだわり: particular about, picky — can be positive (dedication to quality) or negative (stubbornness)
