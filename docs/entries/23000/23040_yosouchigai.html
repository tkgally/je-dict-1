予想違(よそうちが)い

よそうちがい
noun
wrong prediction; unexpected outcome; contrary to expectations
1. wrong prediction; unexpected outcome; contrary to expectations
When the actual result differs from what was predicted or expected. Can describe a pleasant or unpleasant surprise.
結果(けっか)は予想違(よそうちが)いだった。
The result was contrary to expectations.
天気(てんき)が予想違(よそうちが)いで雨(あめ)になった。
The weather turned out differently than expected and it rained.
選挙(せんきょ)の結果(けっか)は多(おお)くの人(ひと)にとって予想違(よそうちが)いで、野党(やとう)が大勝(たいしょう)した。
The election results were unexpected for many people, with the opposition winning a landslide.

Composed of 予想(よそう) (prediction, expectation) and 違(ちが)い (difference, mistake). Part of a family of ～違(ちが)い compounds that express something being different from what was intended or expected.

RELATED ～違(ちが)い COMPOUNDS:

  • 見当違(けんとうちが)い: wrong guess, barking up the wrong tree
  • 勘違(かんちが)い: misunderstanding, wrong impression
  • 場違(ばちが)い: out of place, inappropriate for the setting
  • 筋違(すじちが)い: unreasonable, misdirected

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予想外(よそうがい): unexpected — similar meaning but used as an adjective/adverb (予想外(よそうがい)の展開(てんかい), unexpected development)
  • 期待外(きたいはず)れ: disappointment — specifically when reality falls short of hopes
  • 想定外(そうていがい): beyond assumption — more formal, often used for things that could not have been foreseen
