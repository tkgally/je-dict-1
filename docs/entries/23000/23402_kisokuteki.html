規則的(きそくてき)

きそくてき
adjective
regular; systematic; following a fixed pattern; orderly
1. occurring or arranged according to a clearly recognizable rule or fixed interval, not random or haphazard; regular, systematic, orderly
Describes something that follows a clear, consistent pattern — whether in time (a regular rhythm, regular hours), in space (a regular arrangement of objects), or in structure (a regular shape, a regular grammatical form). It is commonly applied to daily routines, heartbeats and pulses, the arrangement of buildings or plantings, scientific observations, and grammatical conjugations. The opposite is 不規則(ふきそく) ('irregular').
規則的(きそくてき)な生活(せいかつ)を送(おく)る。
To lead a regular life.
時計(とけい)の音(おと)が規則的(きそくてき)に響(ひび)いている。
The sound of the clock is ticking regularly.
患者(かんじゃ)の脈(みゃく)は規則的(きそくてき)で、特(とく)に異常(いじょう)は見(み)られない。
The patient's pulse is regular, and no particular abnormality is seen.
この庭園(ていえん)では、木(き)や花壇(かだん)が左右(さゆう)対称(たいしょう)に規則的(きそくてき)に配置(はいち)されている。
In this garden, the trees and flower beds are arranged regularly in left-right symmetry.

Composed of 規則(きそく) ('rule, regulation') + 的(てき) (the common -teki suffix that turns a noun into a na-adjective meaning 'characterized by X' or 'of the nature of X'). Literally 'of the rule-nature,' i.e. exhibiting the property of following a rule.

GRAMMAR:

規則的(きそくてき) is a na-adjective. Before a noun it takes {な}, and as an adverb it takes {に}.

  • 規則的(きそくてき)な + noun: regular [noun]
  • 規則的(きそくてき)に + verb: regularly [verb]

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 規則的(きそくてき)な生活(せいかつ): a regular / well-ordered life
  • 規則的(きそくてき)な運動(うんどう): regular (physical) exercise
  • 規則的(きそくてき)なリズム: a regular rhythm
  • 規則的(きそくてき)に並(なら)ぶ: to be lined up regularly
  • 規則的(きそくてき)に繰(く)り返(かえ)す: to repeat regularly
  • 規則的(きそくてき)な呼吸(こきゅう): regular breathing

ANTONYM:

  • 不規則(ふきそく): irregular — the direct opposite; extremely common in phrases like 不規則(ふきそく)な生活(せいかつ) (an irregular/unhealthy lifestyle) and 不規則(ふきそく)な脈(みゃく) (an irregular pulse).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 定期的(ていきてき): periodic, regular — emphasizes fixed intervals (e.g., regular check-ups, quarterly reports). Narrower than 規則的(きそくてき).
  • 規則正(きそくただ)しい: regular, disciplined — a closely related adjective meaning 'properly ordered.' Common in the fixed phrase 規則正(きそくただ)しい生活(せいかつ) 'a well-regulated lifestyle.'
  • 整然(せいぜん)とした: orderly, well-arranged — emphasizes tidy visual arrangement.
  • 秩序(ちつじょ)ある: orderly, having order — emphasizes social or systemic order.
  • 均等(きんとう): even, uniform — emphasizes equal size or equal spacing.

REGISTER:
Neutral and widely used across both written and spoken language. Common in medical contexts (脈(みゃく)が規則的(きそくてき)だ), health advice (規則的(きそくてき)な運動(うんどう)), scientific description, and general talk about routines and patterns.
