核弾頭(かくだんとう)

かくだんとう
noun
nuclear warhead
1. nuclear warhead
The warhead portion of a nuclear weapon — the part containing the fissile or fusion material that delivers the explosive yield. Typically used in news, policy, and security contexts when discussing missiles that carry nuclear payloads.
核弾頭(かくだんとう)を搭載(とうさい)したミサイル。
A missile loaded with a nuclear warhead.
その国(くに)は数(すう)百(ひゃく)発(ぱつ)の核弾頭(かくだんとう)を保有(ほゆう)している。
That country possesses several hundred nuclear warheads.
新条約(しんじょうやく)のもと、両国(りょうこく)は実戦配備(じっせんはいび)された核弾頭(かくだんとう)の数(かず)を削減(さくげん)することに合意(ごうい)した。
Under the new treaty, the two countries agreed to reduce the number of deployed nuclear warheads.

Composed of 核(かく) (nuclear, from 核兵器(かくへいき) "nuclear weapon") and 弾頭(だんとう) (warhead, literally "tip/head of a projectile"). The word refers specifically to the warhead — the part of the missile that actually detonates — rather than the delivery vehicle (missile, bomber, submarine) itself.

USAGE:

  • Most often counted with the counter 発(はつ) (rounds): 十(じゅう)発(ぱつ)の核弾頭(かくだんとう) (ten nuclear warheads).
  • Appears constantly in newspaper articles about arms control, proliferation, and security policy.
  • A single missile can carry multiple warheads — referred to as 多弾頭(ただんとう) (multiple warheads) or MIRVs in English.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 核弾頭(かくだんとう)を搭載(とうさい)する: to load/mount a nuclear warhead
  • 核弾頭(かくだんとう)を保有(ほゆう)する: to possess nuclear warheads
  • 核弾頭(かくだんとう)を削減(さくげん)する: to reduce nuclear warheads
  • 核弾頭(かくだんとう)の配備(はいび): deployment of nuclear warheads
  • 実戦配備(じっせんはいび)の核弾頭(かくだんとう): operationally deployed nuclear warheads

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 核兵器(かくへいき): nuclear weapon — the broader term covering warheads and delivery systems
  • 弾頭(だんとう): warhead — the generic term, not necessarily nuclear
  • ミサイル: missile — the delivery vehicle
  • 核(かく): nuclear — used as a prefix in many compounds (核実験(かくじっけん) "nuclear test," 核保有国(かくほゆうこく) "nuclear-weapon state")
  • 原爆(げんばく): atomic bomb — a historical/specific term often referring to the bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki
