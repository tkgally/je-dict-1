1. submersion; flooding; sinking underwater
The state of something being submerged or covered by water, whether from natural flooding, dam construction, rising sea levels, or accidental immersion.
道路(どうろ)が水没(すいぼつ)した。
The road was flooded.
台風(たいふう)で地下(ちか)駐車場(ちゅうしゃじょう)が水没(すいぼつ)した。
The underground parking garage was flooded by the typhoon.
ダム建設(けんせつ)によって、いくつかの村(むら)が水没(すいぼつ)する予定(よてい)だ。
Several villages are scheduled to be submerged due to dam construction.

Composed of 水(すい) (water) + 没(ぼつ) (sink, submerge). Describes things being covered or engulfed by water. Commonly used in news reports about flood damage, climate change, and infrastructure damage.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 道路(どうろ)が水没(すいぼつ)する: a road floods/becomes submerged
  • 車(くるま)が水没(すいぼつ)する: a car gets submerged
  • 水没(すいぼつ)被害(ひがい): flood damage
  • 水没(すいぼつ)地域(ちいき): flooded/submerged area

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 浸水(しんすい): flooding, inundation — water entering a building or area; focuses on water intrusion rather than complete submersion
  • 冠水(かんすい): inundation — fields or roads covered with water; often used for agricultural and road flooding
  • 沈没(ちんぼつ): sinking — used for ships and vessels sinking; implies going down rather than being covered
