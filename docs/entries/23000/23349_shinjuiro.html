真珠色(しんじゅいろ)

しんじゅいろ
noun
pearly white; pearl color; mother-of-pearl sheen
1. pearly white; the iridescent color of a pearl
A soft, slightly silvery white with a faint opalescent or iridescent sheen, like the surface of a real pearl. Used in writing about cosmetics, fashion, cars, and nature to describe any object with that characteristic subtle, lustrous glow.
真珠色(しんじゅいろ)のネイル。
Pearly white nails.
彼女(かのじょ)は真珠色(しんじゅいろ)のドレスを着(き)ていた。
She was wearing a pearly white dress.
朝日(あさひ)が当(あ)たる海(うみ)の面(おもて)が、真珠色(しんじゅいろ)に輝(かがや)いていた。
The surface of the sea, struck by the morning sun, shone with a pearly glow.
この車(くるま)は真珠色(しんじゅいろ)の特殊(とくしゅ)塗装(とそう)が施(ほどこ)されており、角度(かくど)によって色合(いろあ)いが微妙(びみょう)に変化(へんか)する。
This car has a special pearly-white paint finish, and its tone changes subtly depending on the angle.

Composed of 真珠(しんじゅ) (pearl) + 色(いろ) (color). Describes a luminous, soft white with a delicate shimmer — the kind of look aimed at in pearlescent cosmetics, car paint, and evening wear.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真珠色(しんじゅいろ)の肌(はだ): pearly-white skin — used in cosmetics marketing
  • 真珠色(しんじゅいろ)の光沢(こうたく): pearly luster / iridescent sheen
  • 真珠色(しんじゅいろ)に輝(かがや)く: to shine with a pearly glow
  • 真珠色(しんじゅいろ)のネイル: pearly-white nail polish
  • 真珠色(しんじゅいろ)の塗装(とそう): pearlescent paint finish

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 乳白色(にゅうはくしょく): milky white — a similar soft white, but opaque rather than iridescent.
  • 銀色(ぎんいろ): silver — much brighter and more metallic; lacks the soft iridescence of 真珠色(しんじゅいろ).
  • 白(しろ): white — the plain, unqualified word; 真珠色(しんじゅいろ) suggests a specific refined shade.
  • パールホワイト: pearl white — a loanword equivalent common in cosmetics and car paint descriptions; nearly synonymous in practice.
  • オフホワイト: off-white — a warm or slightly yellowish white, not necessarily iridescent.

USAGE:
You will see 真珠色(しんじゅいろ) most often in descriptive writing — novels, ad copy, and reviews — rather than in everyday conversation. In spoken language, パールホワイト or simply 白(しろ) is more common; 真珠色(しんじゅいろ) carries a slightly poetic or refined tone.
