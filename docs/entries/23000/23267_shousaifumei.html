詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)

しょうさいふめい
expression
details unknown; particulars unknown
1. details unknown; particulars unknown
A set phrase meaning that the specific details of something have not been determined or are not yet available. Common in news reports, police announcements, catalog descriptions, and database records where only partial information is known.
原因(げんいん)は詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)。
The cause is unknown in detail.
被害(ひがい)の状況(じょうきょう)は現在(げんざい)のところ詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)です。
The extent of the damage is currently unknown in detail.
事故(じこ)の発生(はっせい)は確認(かくにん)されたが、負傷者(ふしょうしゃ)の数(かず)については詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)のままだ。
The occurrence of the accident has been confirmed, but the number of injured people remains unknown in detail.

Composed of 詳細(しょうさい) (details, particulars) and 不明(ふめい) (unknown, unclear). A fixed compound used mainly in written and journalistic contexts to indicate that specifics have not been confirmed. It treats 詳細(しょうさい) like an unknown quantity: "the details = unknown."

USAGE:

  • Frequently appears as a predicate ending a sentence: 原因(げんいん)は詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)だ / です.
  • Also used attributively with の: 詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)の人物(じんぶつ) (a person whose details are unknown).
  • Common in news reporting, police blotters, archaeological records, and old photograph captions.
  • Has a somewhat cold, bureaucratic feel — in casual conversation, native speakers are more likely to say よくわからない or 詳(くわ)しいことはわからない.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)の事件(じけん): a case whose details are unknown
  • 詳細不明(しょうさいふめい)のまま: still with the details unknown
  • 現在(げんざい)のところ詳細不明(しょうさいふめい): currently unknown in detail
  • 原因(げんいん)詳細不明(しょうさいふめい): cause unknown in detail
  • 身元(みもと)詳細不明(しょうさいふめい): identity unknown in detail

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 詳細(しょうさい): details, particulars — the positive noun
  • 不明(ふめい): unknown, unclear — the second half, used alone in phrases like 行方不明(ゆくえふめい) (whereabouts unknown)
  • 未詳(みしょう): not yet known — used in academic/cataloguing contexts, e.g., 作者未詳(さくしゃみしょう) (author unknown)
  • 不詳(ふしょう): unknown — archaic feel; common in old records (生年不詳(せいねんふしょう) "year of birth unknown")
  • 未確認(みかくにん): unconfirmed — something exists but has not been verified
