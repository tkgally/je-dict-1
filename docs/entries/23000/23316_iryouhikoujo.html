医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)

いりょうひこうじょ
noun
medical expense deduction (tax)
1. medical expense deduction (Japanese income tax)
An income tax deduction that allows Japanese taxpayers to deduct qualifying medical expenses above a certain threshold (generally ¥100,000 per year) from their taxable income. Claimed by filing a final tax return (確定申告(かくていしんこく)).
医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)を申請(しんせい)する。
To apply for the medical expense deduction.
医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)のために領収書(りょうしゅうしょ)を保管(ほかん)している。
I'm keeping the receipts for the medical expense deduction.
一年間(いちねんかん)の医療費(いりょうひ)が十万円(じゅうまんえん)を超(こ)えれば医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)を受(う)けられる。
If your annual medical expenses exceed 100,000 yen, you can receive the medical expense deduction.
確定申告(かくていしんこく)の際(さい)、医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)を忘(わす)れずに申告(しんこく)しましょう。
When filing your tax return, don't forget to claim the medical expense deduction.

Composed of 医療費(いりょうひ) (medical expenses) + 控除(こうじょ) (deduction, subtraction). A specific category of 所得控除(しょとくこうじょ) (income deduction) in the Japanese tax system. Generally, the portion of household medical expenses exceeding ¥100,000 per year (or 5% of total income, whichever is lower) can be deducted from taxable income.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)を受(う)ける: to receive the medical expense deduction
  • 医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)を申請(しんせい)する: to apply for the medical expense deduction
  • 医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)の対象(たいしょう): eligible for the medical expense deduction
  • 医療費(いりょうひ)控除(こうじょ)の上限(じょうげん): the upper limit of the medical expense deduction

RELATED TERMS:

  • 確定申告(かくていしんこく): final tax return — the process through which the deduction is claimed
  • 所得控除(しょとくこうじょ): income deduction — the broader category
  • 扶養(ふよう)控除(こうじょ): dependent deduction — another category of income deduction
  • 領収書(りょうしゅうしょ): receipt — required as proof of medical expenses

USAGE:
Eligible expenses include doctor visits, prescription medication, hospital stays, dental care, and certain transportation costs to medical appointments. The claim is made annually by filing a tax return with a summary of the year's medical expenses.
