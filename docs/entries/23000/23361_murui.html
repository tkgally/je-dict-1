無類(むるい)

むるい
noun
peerless, matchless, without equal
1. peerless, matchless, without equal
Having nothing of the same kind that can stand beside it. Used to praise a quality, skill, or enthusiasm that is in a class of its own. Typically appears as 無類(むるい)の + noun, especially in phrases like 無類(むるい)の酒好(さけず)き ('a peerless lover of sake').
彼(かれ)は無類(むるい)の酒好(さけず)きだ。
He is a peerless lover of sake.
彼女(かのじょ)は無類(むるい)の本好(ほんず)きで、毎日(まいにち)読書(どくしょ)をしている。
She is a peerless book lover and reads every day.
この作家(さっか)は構想力(こうそうりょく)においては無類(むるい)の存在(そんざい)だ。
This author is peerless when it comes to imaginative power.
彼(かれ)の犬(いぬ)に対(たい)する愛情(あいじょう)の深(ふか)さは無類(むるい)のもので、友人(ゆうじん)たちはみんな感心(かんしん)している。
The depth of his love for dogs is unmatched, and his friends are all impressed.

Composed of 無(む) (without) + 類(るい) (kind, class, category). Literally 'without a (similar) kind' — meaning there is nothing of the same sort with which it can be compared. A formal word most often encountered in the pattern 無類(むるい)の + noun.

TYPICAL PATTERN:
The phrase 無類(むるい)のXずき}, where X is a food, drink, or hobby and {ずき} is the 連体形 of {好(す)き, is especially common. It is a set way of saying 'a matchless lover of X.'

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 無類(むるい)の酒好(さけず)き: a peerless lover of sake
  • 無類(むるい)の甘(あま)いもの好(ず)き: a peerless lover of sweets
  • 無類(むるい)の犬好(いぬず)き: a peerless dog lover
  • 無類(むるい)の本好(ほんず)き: a peerless book lover
  • 無類(むるい)の強(つよ)さ: unparalleled strength
  • 無類(むるい)の面白(おもしろ)さ: unrivaled fun/interest

REGISTER:
A slightly literary and formal word, but not archaic — it appears in book reviews, profiles, food writing, and everyday praise of a person's enthusiasm. 無類(むるい)の〜{好(ず)き} in particular is a well-worn cliché that anyone will recognize.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 無比(むひ): matchless, unparalleled — more formal and literary; usually the second element in compounds like 正確(せいかく)無比(むひ). See 23360_muhi.
  • 無双(むそう): matchless, incomparable — old-fashioned, associated with warriors and game titles.
  • 類(るい){まれ}: rare, exceptional — less strong, meaning 'of a rare kind' rather than 'of no kind at all.'
  • 比類(ひるい)ない: having no equal — a more neutral, written synonym.

NOTE ON 類(るい):
The 類(るい) here is the same character used in words like 分類(ぶんるい) (classification) and 種類(しゅるい) (kind). 無類(むるい) literally says 'without a category it belongs to,' meaning nothing else fits in the same bucket.
