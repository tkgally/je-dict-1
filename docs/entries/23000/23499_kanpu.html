1. refund; reimbursement; return (of money)
The return of money that was overpaid or is owed back, especially in official or administrative contexts such as tax refunds, insurance reimbursements, and government payments. More formal than everyday words for refunds.
税金(ぜいきん)の還付(かんぷ)を受(う)けた。
I received a tax refund.
確定申告(かくていしんこく)をしたら還付金(かんぷきん)が戻(もど)ってきた。
After filing my tax return, I got a refund back.
医療費(いりょうひ)の還付(かんぷ)申請(しんせい)は期限(きげん)までに済(す)ませてください。
Please complete the application for medical expense reimbursement by the deadline.
過払(かばら)い分(ぶん)の還付(かんぷ)手続(てつづ)きに数週間(すうしゅうかん)かかる場合(ばあい)がある。
The refund process for overpayments may take several weeks.

Composed of 還(かん) (return, give back) and 付(ふ) (attach, deliver), with voicing ふ→ぷ. The word carries an official, administrative tone — it is the standard term used in tax, insurance, and government contexts, rather than in casual shopping situations.

The most common context is 税金(ぜいきん)の還付(かんぷ) (tax refund), which many residents of Japan encounter during 確定申告(かくていしんこく) (annual tax filing). The derived noun 還付金(かんぷきん) (refund money) is also very frequent.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 還付金(かんぷきん): refund money
  • 還付(かんぷ)申請(しんせい): refund application
  • 還付(かんぷ)を受(う)ける: to receive a refund
  • 税金(ぜいきん)の還付(かんぷ): tax refund
  • 還付(かんぷ)手続(てつづ)き: refund process/procedure

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 返金(へんきん): refund — the general everyday word for getting money back (e.g., from a store)
  • 払(はら)い戻(もど)し: reimbursement, payout — used for insurance payouts and refunds
  • 返還(へんかん): return, restitution — returning something (not only money) to its rightful owner
