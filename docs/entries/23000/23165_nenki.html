年忌(ねんき)

ねんき
noun
memorial anniversary; anniversary of a death
1. memorial anniversary; anniversary of a person's death observed with a Buddhist ceremony
Specific anniversaries of a death on which memorial services are held. In Japanese Buddhism, these occur at fixed intervals and are important family and religious occasions.
祖父(そふ)の三回忌(さんかいき)の年忌(ねんき)法要(ほうよう)。
The third-year memorial service for my grandfather.
年忌(ねんき)には親族(しんぞく)が集(あつ)まってお経(きょう)を上(あ)げる。
At memorial anniversaries, relatives gather and have sutras recited.
来年(らいねん)は母(はは)の七回忌(ななかいき)なので、年忌(ねんき)法要(ほうよう)の準備(じゅんび)を始(はじ)めた。
Since next year is my mother's seventh-year memorial, I started preparing for the memorial service.

Composed of 年(ねん) (year) + 忌(き) (mourning, memorial). Refers to the Buddhist tradition of holding memorial services at specific intervals after death.

MEMORIAL YEAR SCHEDULE:

The major 年忌(ねんき) in Japanese Buddhism are:

  • 一周忌(いっしゅうき): 1st anniversary
  • 三回忌(さんかいき): 3rd year (actually 2 years after death — counts the year of death as year 1)
  • 七回忌(ななかいき): 7th year
  • 十三回忌(じゅうさんかいき): 13th year
  • 三十三回忌(さんじゅうさんかいき): 33rd year (often the final service)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 年忌(ねんき)法要(ほうよう): memorial service
  • 年忌(ねんき)を営(いとな)む: to hold a memorial service
  • 年忌(ねんき)に当(あ)たる: to fall on a memorial year

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 法事(ほうじ): Buddhist memorial service — the event itself, while 年忌(ねんき) refers to the anniversary
  • 命日(めいにち): anniversary of death — the specific date, observed annually; 年忌(ねんき) refers to the special milestone anniversaries
  • 法要(ほうよう): Buddhist ceremony — the formal religious service held at a 年忌(ねんき)
