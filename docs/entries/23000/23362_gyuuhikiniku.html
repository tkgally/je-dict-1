牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)

ぎゅうひきにく
noun
ground beef; minced beef
1. beef that has been ground into small pieces; ground/minced beef
A standard grocery-store cut of beef that has been mechanically ground. Used in hamburgers, meatballs, meat sauce, tacos, stuffed peppers, and many other home-cooked dishes. Sold in trays, usually labeled with the fat percentage.
牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)を買(か)う。
To buy ground beef.
牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)を炒(いた)めてから、玉(たま)ねぎを加(くわ)えてください。
First stir-fry the ground beef, then add the onion.
ハンバーグを作(つく)るときは、牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)と豚(ぶた)ひき肉(にく)を混(ま)ぜるのがおすすめです。
When making hamburg steaks, mixing ground beef with ground pork is recommended.
今日(きょう)の夕食(ゆうしょく)は、牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)とトマトを煮込(にこ)んだミートソースのスパゲッティだった。
Tonight's dinner was spaghetti with a meat sauce made by simmering ground beef and tomatoes.

Composed of 牛(ぎゅう) (cow, beef) + 挽(ひ)き肉(にく) (ground meat, from the verb 挽(ひ)く "to grind, mince"). The 挽(ひ) kanji is very often written in hiragana, so both 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく) and 牛(ぎゅう)挽(ひ)き肉(にく) are acceptable; supermarket packaging usually uses the hiragana form.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)を炒(いた)める: to stir-fry ground beef
  • 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)を使(つか)う: to use ground beef (in a recipe)
  • 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)100グラム: 100 grams of ground beef
  • 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく)と豚(ぶた)ひき肉(にく)の合(あ)いびき: a mix of ground beef and ground pork

RELATED FOOD TERMS:

  • 豚(ぶた)ひき肉(にく): ground pork
  • 鶏(とり)ひき肉(にく): ground chicken
  • 合(あ)いびき肉(にく): a mixture of ground beef and pork (very common in Japanese home cooking)
  • ハンバーグ: hamburg steak (Japanese-style patty)
  • ミートボール: meatball
  • ミートソース: meat sauce
  • そぼろ: crumbly seasoned ground meat served over rice
  • タコライス: taco rice (Okinawan dish using ground beef)

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, the most common choice for home cooking is actually 合(あ)いびき肉(にく) — a beef-pork mix — which is considered ideal for hamburg steaks and meat sauce. Pure 牛(ぎゅう)ひき肉(にく) is more often chosen for Western-style dishes like tacos, chili, and shepherd's pie. Supermarket prices per 100 grams are typically higher for beef than for pork or chicken.
