1. exaggerated; pompous; showy; overblown
Describes something that is unnecessarily dramatic, grandiose, or attention-seeking, often to the point of being ridiculous or off-putting.
そんなに仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しくしないで。
Don't make such a big deal out of it.
仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しいタイトルの本(ほん)だな。
That book has a pretty grandiose title.
ちょっとした風邪(かぜ)なのに、仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しく救急車(きゅうきゅうしゃ)を呼(よ)ぶのはやめてほしい。
I wish they'd stop making a fuss and calling an ambulance for a minor cold.

Always carries a negative or mocking tone. Used to criticize behavior, language, or presentation that is disproportionately dramatic relative to the actual situation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しい態度(たいど): a pompous attitude
  • 仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しい言(い)い方(かた): an exaggerated way of speaking
  • 仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しい演出(えんしゅつ): over-the-top staging
  • 仰々(ぎょうぎょう)しいタイトル: a grandiose title

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 大袈裟(おおげさ): exaggerated — more commonly used in everyday speech, slightly lighter tone
  • 大仰(おおぎょう): exaggerated, dramatic — similar meaning, slightly more literary
  • 派手(はで): flashy, showy — neutral or positive; focuses on visual impact rather than inappropriateness
