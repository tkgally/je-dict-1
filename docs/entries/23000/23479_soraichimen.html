空一面(そらいちめん)

そらいちめん
noun
the entire sky; the whole sky; across the sky
1. the entire sky; the whole sky; all across the sky
Describes the sky in its entirety, typically when it is completely covered or filled by something — clouds, stars, a sunset glow, etc.
空一面(そらいちめん)に雲(くも)が広(ひろ)がった。
Clouds spread across the entire sky.
夕焼(ゆうや)けが空一面(そらいちめん)を赤(あか)く染(そ)めた。
The sunset dyed the entire sky red.
空一面(そらいちめん)に星(ほし)が輝(かがや)いていて、息(いき)を飲(の)むほど美(うつく)しかった。
Stars were shining across the entire sky, so beautiful it took my breath away.

A compound of 空(そら) (sky) and 一面(いちめん) (the whole surface, all over). Paints a picture of the sky as a single vast expanse uniformly covered or filled by something. Common in literary and descriptive writing.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 空一面(そらいちめん)に広(ひろ)がる: to spread across the whole sky
  • 空一面(そらいちめん)を覆(おお)う: to cover the entire sky
  • 空一面(そらいちめん)の雲(くも): clouds across the whole sky
  • 空一面(そらいちめん)が灰色(はいいろ)になる: the entire sky turns gray

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 一面(いちめん)の雪(ゆき): snow covering everything — 一面(いちめん) can be combined with other surfaces too
  • 見渡(みわた)す限(かぎ)りの空(そら): as far as the eye can see across the sky

NOTE:
The pattern ～一面(いちめん) is productive and can be applied to other surfaces: 海一面(うみいちめん) (the entire sea), 野原一面(のはらいちめん) (across the whole field). It always conveys the image of something stretching uniformly over the entire area.
