お蔵入(くらい)り

おくらいり
noun
shelved; never released; buried
1. shelved; buried; never released; put away permanently
Describes a project, work, plan, or product that was completed or nearly completed but never made public or was permanently set aside.
あの映画(えいが)はお蔵入(くらい)りになった。
That movie was shelved.
企画(きかく)がお蔵入(くらい)りにならないといいけど。
I hope the project doesn't get shelved.
撮影(さつえい)は終(お)わっていたが、問題(もんだい)が発覚(はっかく)してお蔵入(くらい)りになった。
Filming was finished, but problems came to light and it was shelved.

Literally "put into the storehouse." The 蔵(くら) was a traditional storehouse where things were kept out of sight. The お prefix adds a slightly formal or set-phrase quality. Commonly used in the entertainment industry for unreleased works.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • お蔵入(くらい)りになる: to be shelved
  • お蔵入(くらい)りにする: to shelve (something)
  • 長年(ながねん)お蔵入(くらい)りだった: had been shelved for years
  • お蔵入(くらい)り作品(さくひん): a shelved work

USAGE:
Frequently used for movies, TV episodes, albums, or projects that were completed but never released, typically due to scandals, legal issues, quality concerns, or changes in circumstances. Also used more broadly for any plan or idea that gets permanently set aside.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ボツになる: to be rejected, scrapped — implies the idea was actively rejected before completion
  • お蔵入(くらい)り implies something completed but withheld from release
