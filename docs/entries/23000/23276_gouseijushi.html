合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)

ごうせいじゅし
noun
synthetic resin; plastic
1. synthetic resin; plastic
A class of man-made, resin-like materials produced by chemical synthesis from petroleum or other feedstocks — in ordinary Japanese, the technical-sounding equivalent of "plastic." Used on product labels, in materials science, and in regulatory language, often where the looser everyday word プラスチック would sound too casual.
合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)の容器(ようき)。
A synthetic-resin container.
この部品(ぶひん)は軽(かる)い合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)で作(つく)られている。
This part is made of lightweight synthetic resin.
自動車(じどうしゃ)の内装(ないそう)は、金属(きんぞく)の代(か)わりに合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)が多(おお)く使(つか)われるようになった。
Synthetic resin has come to be used widely in car interiors in place of metal.

Composed of 合成(ごうせい) (synthesis, combining) and 樹脂(じゅし) (resin — originally the sticky exudate of trees). Although 樹脂(じゅし) originally meant tree resin, in modern usage 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし) is essentially the formal Japanese word for plastic. Labels on household products and packaging often use it rather than プラスチック because it sounds more technical and precise.

USAGE:

  • Appears on product labels such as material-composition notes: 材質(ざいしつ)：合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし) (Material: plastic).
  • Used in manufacturing, chemistry, and environmental contexts — e.g., discussions of plastic waste often use 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし) when sources cite statistics.
  • In everyday conversation, speakers usually default to プラスチック or more specific names like ペットボトル (PET bottle).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)製(せい): made of synthetic resin
  • 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)の容器(ようき): a synthetic-resin container
  • 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)を使用(しよう)する: to use synthetic resin
  • 熱可塑性(ねつかそせい)合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし): thermoplastic resin
  • 合成樹脂(ごうせいじゅし)塗料(とりょう): synthetic-resin paint

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 樹脂(じゅし): resin — the broader term, including both natural tree resin and synthetic resin
  • プラスチック: plastic — the everyday loanword
  • ビニール: vinyl — often used colloquially for plastic sheeting or bags
  • ポリエチレン: polyethylene — a specific common plastic
  • 合成繊維(ごうせいせんい): synthetic fiber — a related category of man-made materials used for fabrics
