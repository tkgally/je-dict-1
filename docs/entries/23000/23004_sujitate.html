筋立(すじた)て

すじたて
noun
plot; storyline; outline of a story
1. plot; storyline; outline of a story
The sequence of events and narrative structure of a story, play, film, or other narrative work.
この映画(えいが)の筋立(すじた)てはおもしろい。
The plot of this movie is interesting.
筋立(すじた)てが複雑(ふくざつ)で分(わ)かりにくかった。
The plot was complicated and hard to follow.
小説(しょうせつ)を書(か)く前(まえ)に、まず筋立(すじた)てを考(かんが)えることが大切(たいせつ)だ。
Before writing a novel, it's important to think about the plot first.

Refers to the overall narrative structure or sequence of events in a story. More focused on the progression of the story from beginning to end than on individual scenes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 筋立(すじた)てが面白(おもしろ)い: the plot is interesting
  • 筋立(すじた)てを考(かんが)える: to think up a plot
  • 筋立(すじた)てが単純(たんじゅん): the plot is simple
  • 巧(たく)みな筋立(すじた)て: a skillful plot

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 筋(すじ): plot, storyline — can mean the same thing in shorter form (筋(すじ)がいい = good plot), but 筋(すじ) has many other meanings
  • あらすじ: synopsis, summary — a condensed summary of the plot, not the plot itself
  • プロット: plot — English loanword used especially in creative writing and screenwriting
  • 展開(てんかい): development — refers to how the story unfolds, not the overall structure
