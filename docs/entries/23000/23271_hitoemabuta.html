一重(ひとえ)まぶた

ひとえまぶた
noun
single eyelid; monolid (eyelid without a crease)
1. single eyelid; monolid (eyelid without a crease)
An eyelid that does not have a visible fold or crease above the eyelashes — one of the two basic Japanese descriptive categories for eyelid shape, the other being 二重(ふたえ)まぶた (double eyelid). Common in East Asian populations and a major topic in makeup and cosmetic-surgery discussions in Japan.
彼女(かのじょ)は一重(ひとえ)まぶただ。
She has single eyelids.
一重(ひとえ)まぶたに似合(にあ)うメイクを探(さが)している。
I am looking for makeup that suits single eyelids.
子(こ)どもの頃(ころ)は一重(ひとえ)まぶただったが、成長(せいちょう)とともに二重(ふたえ)になった。
I had single eyelids when I was a child, but they became double as I grew up.

Composed of 一重(ひとえ) (single layer, one-fold) and まぶた (eyelid). Describes an eyelid with no visible crease above the lash line. It is one of the standard physical-description terms in Japanese — not considered negative in itself, though many people who have 一重(ひとえ)まぶた discuss makeup tricks or cosmetic surgery (二重(ふたえ)整形(せいけい)) to create a crease.

USAGE:

  • Used both attributively (一重(ひとえ)まぶたの人(ひと) "a person with single eyelids") and as a predicate (私(わたし)は一重(ひとえ)まぶたです "I have single eyelids").
  • Paired frequently with its opposite 二重(ふたえ)まぶた (double eyelid). Note that 二重(ふたえ) here is read ふたえ, not にじゅう.
  • Common in beauty magazines, makeup tutorials, and cosmetic-surgery advertisements. In casual speech, people often drop まぶた and say just 一重(ひとえ) ("I'm a hitoe").

CULTURAL NOTE:
In Japan, eyelid shape is discussed much more openly than in many Western countries. Double eyelids (二重(ふたえ)まぶた) are widely considered more fashionable, and products such as double-eyelid adhesive tapes and glues (アイプチ, アイテープ) are sold in drugstores. There are also people with 奥二重(おくぶたえ) — a partial crease hidden behind the eyelid — which is often counted as its own category.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一重(ひとえ)まぶたの人(ひと): a person with single eyelids
  • 一重(ひとえ)まぶたから二重(ふたえ)になる: to go from single to double eyelids
  • 一重(ひとえ)まぶた向(む)けのメイク: makeup designed for single eyelids
  • 一重(ひとえ)まぶたを活(い)かす: to make the most of single eyelids

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 二重(ふたえ)まぶた: double eyelid — the contrasting shape with a visible crease
  • 奥二重(おくぶたえ): hidden double eyelid — a crease that is partly hidden by the upper lid
  • まぶた: eyelid — the base noun
  • 目元(めもと): the area around the eyes — a broader cosmetic term
