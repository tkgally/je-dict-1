1. long-term stay; extended stay
Staying in a place for an extended period, as opposed to a short visit. Used for travel, immigration, work assignments abroad, and accommodation contexts.
海外(かいがい)に長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)する。
To stay abroad for an extended period.
長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)用(よう)のホテルを探(さが)している。
I am looking for a hotel for a long-term stay.
長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)ビザを取得(しゅとく)するには、収入(しゅうにゅう)の証明(しょうめい)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To obtain a long-term stay visa, proof of income is required.

Composed of 長期(ちょうき) (long term) and 滞在(たいざい) (stay/sojourn). Very common in travel and immigration contexts. What counts as "long-term" depends on context — it could mean weeks for a hotel stay, or months to years for an overseas assignment.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)ビザ: long-term stay visa
  • 長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)者(しゃ): long-term resident/visitor
  • 長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)型(がた)ホテル: extended-stay hotel
  • 長期滞在(ちょうきたいざい)向(む)け: designed for long-term stays

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 短期滞在(たんきたいざい): short-term stay — the opposite, typically for tourism or brief visits
  • 在留(ざいりゅう): residence (in a foreign country) — a more formal/legal term used in immigration law
  • 駐在(ちゅうざい): stationing, being posted — specifically for being assigned to a post, especially overseas work assignments
