1. delegation; entrusting; mandate; authorization
The act of entrusting someone with authority, responsibility, or a task. Commonly used in legal and business contexts for formal delegation of powers.
権限(けんげん)を委任(いにん)する。
To delegate authority.
弁護士(べんごし)に手続(てつづ)きを委任(いにん)した。
I entrusted the procedures to a lawyer.
総会(そうかい)に出席(しゅっせき)できない場合(ばあい)は、委任状(いにんじょう)を提出(ていしゅつ)してください。
If you cannot attend the general meeting, please submit a proxy form.

A formal word composed of 委(い) (entrust) + 任(にん) (responsibility/duty). Primarily used in legal, business, and administrative contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 委任(いにん)する: to delegate, to entrust
  • 委任状(いにんじょう): letter of proxy, power of attorney
  • 委任(いにん)契約(けいやく): mandate contract
  • 権限(けんげん)を委任(いにん)する: to delegate authority

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 委託(いたく): consignment, outsourcing — entrusting a specific task or item to another party; often commercial
  • 任命(にんめい): appointment — formally assigning someone to a position
  • 代理(だいり): proxy, representation — acting on behalf of someone else
  • 託(たく)す: to entrust — a more general and literary verb
