自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)

じこちゅうしん
noun
egocentrism, self-centeredness
1. egocentrism, self-centeredness; (being) self-centered
Placing oneself at the center of all things — thinking or acting as if one's own feelings, preferences, and convenience matter more than anyone else's. Almost always negative in tone. Very commonly used in the adjectival form 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき) ('self-centered, egocentric') to describe a person or behavior.
彼(かれ)は自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)だ。
He is self-centered.
自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な考(かんが)え方(かた)を改(あらた)めたほうがいい。
You should change your self-centered way of thinking.
自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)の人(ひと)は周(まわ)りの人(ひと)の気持(きも)ちに気(き)づかないことが多(おお)い。
Self-centered people often fail to notice how those around them feel.
子(こ)どもは発達(はったつ)の段階(だんかい)によっては自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な行動(こうどう)をとるのが普通(ふつう)だと言(い)われている。
It is said that, depending on their stage of development, children normally behave in self-centered ways.

Composed of 自己(じこ) (self) + 中心(ちゅうしん) (center). Literally 'the self at the center.' As a standalone noun it describes the mindset; with the な-adjective suffix 的(てき) it becomes 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき), the form most speakers actually use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な人(ひと): a self-centered person
  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な考(かんが)え方(かた): a self-centered way of thinking
  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な行動(こうどう): self-centered behavior
  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)主義(しゅぎ): egoism (as an ideology or principle)
  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)に考(かんが)える: to think only of oneself

REGISTER AND NUANCE:
Definitely negative in social commentary and personal criticism — calling someone 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき) is a serious complaint. In psychology and child-development writing, however, the word is used more neutrally, as a technical term for the developmental stage in which a young child cannot yet take another person's point of view.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自分(じぶん)勝手(がって): selfish, doing as one pleases — more casual and conversational, focuses on acting without consideration.
  • {わがまま}: selfish, willful — an everyday word especially for children or partners; emphasizes indulging one's own wishes.
  • 利己(りこ)的(てき): self-interested — emphasizes looking out for one's own benefit, often in a calculating way.
  • エゴイスト}: egoist — a loanword used similarly to {自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき)な{人(ひと)}, but more fashionable in tone.

ANTONYMS:

  • 利他(りた)的(てき): altruistic
  • 思(おも)いやりのある: considerate, thoughtful of others
