1. the act of mailing a letter or postcard by dropping it into a public mailbox or post-office collection box; the act of dropping a slip into a designated collection box (e.g. a comment box, raffle box, or election ballot box)
The standard formal word for the action of putting a letter, postcard, application form, or similar item into a collection box. Most often used about postal mail dropped into a 郵便(ゆうびん)ポスト (street mailbox), but it also covers ballot voting, prize-draw entries, and any context in which something is dropped into a labelled box. Used as a noun and as a する-verb (投函(とうかん)する 'to mail, to post').
手紙(てがみ)を投函(とうかん)した。
I mailed the letter.
駅前(えきまえ)のポストに葉書(はがき)を投函(とうかん)してきた。
I just dropped a postcard in the mailbox in front of the station.
応募(おうぼ)用紙(ようし)は切手(きって)を貼(は)って、締(し)め切(き)り日(び)までに投函(とうかん)してください。
Please put a stamp on the application form and mail it by the deadline.
雨(あめ)の中(なか)を近(ちか)くのポストまで歩(ある)いて、大切(たいせつ)な書類(しょるい)を投函(とうかん)した。
I walked to the nearby mailbox in the rain and mailed an important document.

Composed of 投(とう) ('to throw') + 函(かん) ('box'). The character 函(かん) is the same as 箱(はこ) ('box') and is the formal Sino-Japanese reading. Literally 'to throw into a box'.

USAGE:
投函(とうかん) is the standard, slightly formal word used in postal contexts and on instructions for mail and applications. It is the word the Japanese postal service (日本(にっぽん)郵便(ゆうびん)) uses on its signs and notices, and it is what application forms instruct you to do. In casual speech, Japanese speakers often instead say ポストに入(い)れる ('to put in the mailbox') or 手紙(てがみ)を出(だ)す ('to send a letter').

Used both as a plain noun and as a する-verb: 投函(とうかん)する 'to mail, to post (something)'.

GRAMMAR:

Transitive in its する-verb form. The thing being mailed is marked with を, and the location with に:

  • 手紙(てがみ)をポストに投函(とうかん)する: to drop a letter in the mailbox
  • The passive 投函(とうかん)される is also common in postal-service notices.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 手紙(てがみ)を投函(とうかん)する: to mail a letter
  • 葉書(はがき)を投函(とうかん)する: to mail a postcard
  • ポストに投函(とうかん)する: to drop into a mailbox
  • 投票(とうひょう)用紙(ようし)を投函(とうかん)する: to drop a ballot into the ballot box
  • 投函(とうかん)日(び): posting date, mailing date
  • 投函(とうかん)時(じ): at the time of mailing
  • 投函口(とうかんぐち): mail slot, drop-off slot (the opening of the box)
  • 投函(とうかん)完了(かんりょう): 'mailed' (a status notification)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 手紙(てがみ)を出(だ)す: to send a letter — the everyday spoken expression; covers both the act of mailing and the broader act of writing and sending. More casual.
  • ポストに入(い)れる: to put in the mailbox — a plain spoken paraphrase of 投函(とうかん)する.
  • 郵送(ゆうそう): mailing, sending by post — refers to the broader act of sending something through the postal system, including parcels handed over at a post office counter.
  • 発送(はっそう): dispatch, sending out — more general; commonly used by online retailers about shipping orders.
  • 差(さ)し出(だ)す: to send (a letter); to submit — formal verb for handing over or submitting documents.

REGISTER:
Formal/written. Common in postal-service signage, instructions on application forms, and news articles. In conversation, native speakers usually prefer the more colloquial 出(だ)す or ポストに入(い)れる.
