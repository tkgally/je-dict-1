即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)

そくじつはいそう
noun
same-day delivery
1. same-day delivery
A shipping option in which an order placed is delivered to the recipient on the same day it is ordered. Offered by many online retailers and services in Japan.
即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)に対応(たいおう)しています。
We offer same-day delivery.
午前(ごぜん)中(ちゅう)に注文(ちゅうもん)すれば即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)が可能(かのう)です。
If you order in the morning, same-day delivery is possible.
急(いそ)いでいるので、即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)のサービスを選(えら)んだ。
Since I was in a hurry, I chose the same-day delivery service.
即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)は一部(いちぶ)の地域(ちいき)のみ対応(たいおう)となっております。
Same-day delivery is available only in certain areas.

Composed of 即日(そくじつ) (same day, that very day) + 配送(はいそう) (delivery, shipping). A common commercial term in Japan for fast-delivery services offered by online retailers like Amazon, Rakuten, and major department stores.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)可能(かのう): same-day delivery available
  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)対応(たいおう): same-day delivery supported
  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)サービス: same-day delivery service
  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)無料(むりょう): free same-day delivery
  • 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)の締(し)め切(き)り: same-day delivery deadline (cutoff time)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 翌日(よくじつ)配送(はいそう): next-day delivery — delivery the day after ordering
  • 当日(とうじつ)配送(はいそう): day-of delivery — essentially synonymous with 即日(そくじつ)配送(はいそう)
  • 速達(そくたつ): express mail — the postal service's fast option

USAGE:
Typically subject to conditions: orders must be placed before a cutoff time (e.g., 午前(ごぜん)中(ちゅう)) and are often available only in certain service areas.
