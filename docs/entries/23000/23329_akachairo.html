赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)

あかちゃいろ
noun
reddish brown; russet
1. reddish brown; russet
A brown color with a reddish tint, such as the color of dried leaves, rust, cinnamon, or certain woods. A common everyday color term.
赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)の靴(くつ)。
Reddish-brown shoes.
秋(あき)になって葉(は)が赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)に変(か)わった。
With autumn, the leaves turned reddish brown.
古(ふる)い鉄(てつ)のドアが赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)にさびていた。
The old iron door had rusted to a reddish-brown color.
テーブルは赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)のチークでできていて、部屋(へや)に落(お)ち着(つ)いた雰囲気(ふんいき)を与(あた)えていた。
The table was made of reddish-brown teak, giving the room a calm atmosphere.

A compound color term formed by 赤(あか) (red) + 茶色(ちゃいろ) (brown; literally "tea color"). Describes brown with a clear reddish tinge — the color of cinnamon, dried autumn leaves, rust on old iron, many wood finishes, and certain hair and fur.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)の髪(かみ): reddish-brown hair
  • 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)に染(そ)まる: to be dyed reddish brown
  • 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)にさびる: to rust to a reddish brown
  • 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)の葉(は): reddish-brown leaves
  • 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)のレンガ: reddish-brown brick

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 茶色(ちゃいろ): brown — the general brown color, without the reddish tint.
  • 栗色(くりいろ): chestnut color — brown with a reddish tint, but more specifically the warm brown of chestnut shells. Often used for hair color.
  • 銅色(どういろ): copper color — similar but more metallic; the color of polished copper.
  • 朱色(しゅいろ): vermilion — much brighter and more red-orange than reddish brown.

USAGE:
Like most X色(いろ) color terms, 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ) functions as a noun. To modify another noun, attach の: 赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)のジャケット (a reddish-brown jacket). With the copula, describe appearance: 葉(は)は赤茶色(あかちゃいろ)だ (the leaves are reddish brown).
