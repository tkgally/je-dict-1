始点(してん)

してん
noun
starting point; origin; beginning point
1. starting point; origin; beginning point
The point where something begins, such as a line, route, or process. Used in mathematics, geography, and general contexts.
ここが始点(してん)です。
This is the starting point.
始点(してん)から終点(しゅうてん)まで歩(ある)いた。
I walked from the starting point to the endpoint.
この道路(どうろ)の始点(してん)は東京駅前(とうきょうえきまえ)で、終点(しゅうてん)は日本橋(にほんばし)だ。
The starting point of this road is in front of Tokyo Station, and the endpoint is Nihonbashi.

A compound of 始(し) (beginning) and 点(てん) (point). The opposite of 終点(しゅうてん) (endpoint). Used for routes, lines, and processes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 始点(してん)から終点(しゅうてん)まで: from start to finish
  • 始点(してん)に戻(もど)る: to return to the starting point
  • 始点(してん)を決(き)める: to set the starting point

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 起点(きてん): starting point; base point — often used for the origin of routes, measurements, or reasoning
  • 出発点(しゅっぱつてん): departure point; starting point — emphasizes the place from which one departs
  • 終点(しゅうてん): endpoint; terminus — the opposite of 始点(してん)
  • 視点(してん): viewpoint; perspective — same reading but different kanji and meaning
  • 支店(してん): branch office — same reading, different kanji
