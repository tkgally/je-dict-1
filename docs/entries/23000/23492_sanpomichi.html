散歩道(さんぽみち)

さんぽみち
noun
walking path; strolling route
1. walking path; strolling route; usual walk
A path or route that one regularly walks for pleasure or exercise. Unlike 遊歩道(ゆうほどう), which refers to a purpose-built walkway, 散歩道(さんぽみち) can be any route — streets, riverbanks, park paths — that someone habitually uses for their daily walk.
川沿(かわぞ)いの散歩道(さんぽみち)が好(す)きだ。
I like the walking path along the river.
犬(いぬ)との散歩道(さんぽみち)を変(か)えてみた。
I tried changing the route for my walk with the dog.
桜並木(さくらなみき)の散歩道(さんぽみち)は、春(はる)になると花見(はなみ)の人(ひと)で賑(にぎ)わう。
The walking path lined with cherry trees bustles with hanami visitors when spring arrives.

A compound of 散歩(さんぽ) (stroll, walk) and 道(みち) (path, road). Refers either to a physical path suitable for walking or to one's habitual walking route. The word has a warm, personal quality — it often implies a route that someone has grown attached to through regular use.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 散歩道(さんぽみち)を歩(ある)く: to walk along a walking path
  • 散歩道(さんぽみち)を変(か)える: to change one's walking route
  • お{気(き)に入(い)りの散歩道(さんぽみち): one's favorite walking path
  • 朝(あさ)の散歩道(さんぽみち): morning walk route

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 遊歩道(ゆうほどう): promenade, walkway — a purpose-built, maintained path for pedestrians, typically in a park or scenic area
  • 歩道(ほどう): sidewalk, pavement — a functional pedestrian path along a road
  • 小道(こみち): lane, trail — a small or narrow path, often with a quaint or scenic connotation
