1. suppression; subjugation; gaining control by force
Using force or authority to bring a situation, area, or group under control. Often used in military, security, and sports contexts.
暴動(ぼうどう)を制圧(せいあつ)する。
To suppress a riot.
警察(けいさつ)が現場(げんば)を制圧(せいあつ)した。
The police gained control of the scene.
相手(あいて)チームを圧倒的(あっとうてき)な攻撃力(こうげきりょく)で制圧(せいあつ)した。
They dominated the opposing team with overwhelming offensive power.

A compound of 制(せい) (control/restrain) + 圧(あつ) (pressure). Implies the use of force or overwhelming power to bring something under control.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 制圧(せいあつ)する: to suppress, to subdue
  • 武力(ぶりょく)制圧(せいあつ): armed suppression
  • 制圧(せいあつ)射撃(しゃげき): suppressive fire
  • 完全(かんぜん)制圧(せいあつ): complete subjugation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鎮圧(ちんあつ): suppression, quelling — focuses on calming a disturbance
  • 制御(せいぎょ): control — managing or regulating something (less forceful)
  • 征服(せいふく): conquest — complete subjugation of a people or territory
  • 弾圧(だんあつ): oppression — suppressing people or movements through force
