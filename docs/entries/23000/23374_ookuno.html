多(おお)くの

おおくの
expression
many; a lot of; much
1. many; a large number of; a lot of (attributive modifier)
An attributive phrase formed from the noun 多(おお)く (a great many, a lot) plus the particle の. Used before a noun to mean "many of X" or "a large number of X." More written and formal than たくさんの; a standard choice in news, essays, and academic writing.
多(おお)くの人(ひと)が集(あつ)まった。
Many people gathered.
多(おお)くの場合(ばあい)、この方法(ほうほう)が有効(ゆうこう)だ。
In many cases, this method is effective.
多(おお)くの学生(がくせい)が、卒業(そつぎょう)後(ご)も恩師(おんし)と連絡(れんらく)を取(と)り続(つづ)けている。
Many students continue to stay in touch with their former teachers even after graduation.
この研究(けんきゅう)は、医療(いりょう)現場(げんば)における多(おお)くの課題(かだい)を解決(かいけつ)する手(て)がかりを与(あた)えてくれた。
This research provided clues for solving many of the issues facing the medical field.

Built from the noun 多(おお)く ("many; a lot" — the nominal form of the i-adjective 多(おお)い) + の. The resulting phrase functions as an attributive modifier, always placed before a noun.

CORE PATTERN:
多(おお)くの + noun = "many [noun]" / "a lot of [noun]"

KEY GRAMMATICAL CONTRAST (important!):

多(おお)い and 多(おお)くの behave differently:

  • 多(おお)い is an i-adjective, but it is NOT used attributively before a noun in modern Japanese: *多(おお)い人(ひと) is unnatural.
  • Instead, use 多(おお)くの人(ひと) to mean "many people."
  • Or use 多(おお)い as a predicate: 人(ひと)が多(おお)い ("the people are many / there are many people").

So the choice between 多(おお)くの and 多(おお)い depends on the position:

  • Attributive (before noun): 多(おお)くの + noun
  • Predicate (after noun): noun + が/は + 多(おお)い

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 多(おお)くの人(ひと): many people
  • 多(おお)くの場合(ばあい): in many cases
  • 多(おお)くの時間(じかん): a lot of time
  • 多(おお)くの問題(もんだい): many problems
  • 多(おお)くの国(くに): many countries
  • 多(おお)くの学生(がくせい): many students
  • 多(おお)くの企業(きぎょう): many companies

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • たくさんの: many, lots of — conversational; used with both countable and uncountable nouns. More frequent in speech.
  • 大勢(おおぜい)の: many (only for people; crowds of people)
  • 数(かず)多(おお)くの: numerous (even more emphatic than 多(おお)くの; written)
  • 数多(あまた): many (literary; somewhat archaic)
  • いろんな / 様々(さまざま)な: various (emphasizes variety rather than raw count)

REGISTER:
多(おお)くの is the standard written and semi-formal choice. It appears in newspapers, essays, textbooks, speeches, and business writing. In everyday conversation, たくさんの or simply たくさん is more natural.

COMMON MISTAKE:
Learners who start from the adjective 多(おお)い often try *多(おお)い人(ひと) or *多(おお)い問題(もんだい). These are ungrammatical in modern Japanese. Whenever you need to say "many X" as a modifier before a noun, use 多(おお)くの + noun.
