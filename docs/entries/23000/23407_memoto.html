目元(めもと)

めもと
noun
the area around the eyes; one's eyes (as a feature of facial expression)
1. the area around the eyes; the part of the face surrounding the eyes, including the eyelids, lashes, and skin near the corners of the eyes
Refers to the region of the face surrounding the eyes, rather than the eyeballs themselves. This is a useful word in beauty and skincare contexts (eye creams, makeup), in descriptions of facial features and expressions, and in everyday talk about tired eyes or wrinkles. It often carries an implicit sense of how someone looks, since the area around the eyes is one of the most expressive and noticeable parts of the face.
目元(めもと)が涼(すず)しい。
She has refreshing-looking eyes.
母(はは)に目元(めもと)がそっくりだ。
My eyes look exactly like my mother's.
疲(つか)れているのか、目元(めもと)にクマができている。
He must be tired — he has dark circles under his eyes.
年齢(ねんれい)が出(で)やすい目元(めもと)を、専用(せんよう)のクリームで丁寧(ていねい)にケアしている。
I take careful care of the area around my eyes — where age tends to show — using a special cream.

Composed of 目(め) ('eye') + 元(もと) ('the base or area around something'). The suffix 元(もと) creates a series of words referring to areas around facial features: 目元(めもと) 'around the eyes', 口元(くちもと) 'around the mouth', 耳元(みみもと) 'right next to the ear', 足元(あしもと) 'around the feet, underfoot'.

USAGE:
Unlike 目(め) (which usually refers to the eyeball or the sense of sight), 目元(めもと) refers to the visual area around the eyes — the eyelids, lashes, eye corners, and surrounding skin. This makes it especially useful in three contexts:

  • describing physical features and resemblance (e.g. saying that someone has eyes like their parent's)
  • talking about facial expressions and impressions (目元(めもと) can look gentle, sharp, sleepy, etc.)
  • skincare and makeup, where the eye area is treated as a distinct zone

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 目元(めもと)が涼(すず)しい: to have clear, attractive eyes (lit. 'cool eyes')
  • 目元(めもと)が優(やさ)しい: to have gentle-looking eyes
  • 目元(めもと)が鋭(するど)い: to have sharp, piercing eyes
  • 目元(めもと)が似(に)ている: the eye area resembles (someone)
  • 目元(めもと)にクマができる: to get dark circles around the eyes
  • 目元(めもと)のしわ: wrinkles around the eyes
  • 目元(めもと)のメイク: eye makeup
  • 目元(めもと)のケア: eye-area skincare
  • 目元(めもと)専用(せんよう)クリーム: eye cream

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 目(め): eye, eyes; sight — the general word; refers primarily to the eyeball or the sense of vision.
  • 目(まな)差(ざ)し: gaze, look — focuses on the way someone looks at something, the expression in their eyes.
  • 目付(めつ)き: look in one's eyes, expression of the eyes — emphasizes how the eyes appear (kindly, sharply, suspiciously, etc.).
  • 瞼(まぶた): eyelid — refers specifically to the lid that covers the eye.
  • 口元(くちもと): area around the mouth — parallel construction; useful for describing mouth shape and expression.

REGISTER:
Neutral; common in everyday speech as well as in beauty advertising, cosmetic product descriptions, and literary descriptions of characters' faces.
