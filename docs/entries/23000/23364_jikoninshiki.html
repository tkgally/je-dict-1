自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)

じこにんしき
noun
self-awareness, self-recognition, self-perception
1. self-awareness, self-recognition, self-perception
The mental act of recognizing and understanding oneself — one's character, abilities, position, limitations, or feelings. Used both in everyday self-improvement talk and as a technical term in psychology, education, and philosophy.
自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が甘(あま)い。
One's self-awareness is lacking.
正(ただ)しい自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)を持(も)つことが大切(たいせつ)だ。
It is important to have an accurate self-awareness.
彼女(かのじょ)は自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が高(たか)く、自分(じぶん)の強(つよ)みと弱(よわ)みをよく理解(りかい)している。
She has a high degree of self-awareness and understands her own strengths and weaknesses well.
就職(しゅうしょく)活動(かつどう)の前(まえ)に、自分(じぶん)の興味(きょうみ)や価値観(かちかん)について自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)を深(ふか)めておくといい。
Before job hunting, it's a good idea to deepen your self-awareness about your own interests and values.

Composed of 自己(じこ) (self) + 認識(にんしき) (recognition, cognition, awareness). Literally 'cognition of the self' — the ability to see and understand oneself accurately.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が高(たか)い: to have a high level of self-awareness
  • 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が甘(あま)い: to be lacking in self-awareness (literally 'sweet / loose')
  • 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が足(た)りない: to lack sufficient self-awareness
  • 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)を深(ふか)める: to deepen one's self-awareness
  • 正(ただ)しい自己(じこ)認識(にんしき): accurate self-awareness
  • 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)能力(のうりょく): the capacity for self-awareness

CONTEXTS OF USE:

  • Career coaching and job-hunting advice — knowing one's strengths, weaknesses, and values.
  • Psychology and counseling — as a clinical term for self-insight.
  • Self-help books — on personal growth and emotional intelligence.
  • Management and HR — in writing about leadership and team dynamics.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自覚(じかく): self-awareness, consciousness (of one's situation or role) — more about recognizing a specific fact ('I'm now a parent / a manager / ill') than about long-term understanding of oneself. See 01150_jikaku if present.
  • 自己(じこ)分析(ぶんせき): self-analysis — the act of systematically examining oneself, especially for job applications.
  • 自己(じこ)理解(りかい): self-understanding — very close in meaning, perhaps slightly softer and more intuitive.
  • 内省(ないせい): introspection — the inward-looking process of reflection.

USAGE NOTE:
The expression 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が甘(あま)い is a frequent criticism; it literally says that someone's self-view is 'loose' or 'easy on oneself' — i.e., they overestimate their abilities or fail to notice problems. The opposite, 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が厳(きび)しい ('strict'), is not idiomatic; Japanese speakers simply say 自己(じこ)認識(にんしき)が高(たか)い or 正(ただ)しい.
