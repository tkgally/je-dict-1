1. casting; founding; minting
The process of making metal objects by melting metal and pouring it into a mold. Used for producing items such as coins, statues, tools, and industrial parts. Can be used as a noun on its own or with する as a verb meaning "to cast" or "to mint."
銅像(どうぞう)の鋳造(ちゅうぞう)。
The casting of a bronze statue.
新(あたら)しい硬貨(こうか)が鋳造(ちゅうぞう)される。
New coins are being minted.
この工場(こうじょう)では昔(むかし)ながらの方法(ほうほう)で鉄器(てっき)を鋳造(ちゅうぞう)している。
At this factory, they cast iron implements using traditional methods.

Composed of 鋳(ちゅう) (to cast metal) and 造(ぞう) (to make, to build). A technical term belonging to manufacturing, metallurgy, and traditional crafts. It is frequently encountered in contexts involving coin production, bronze statues, temple bells, and industrial metal parts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 硬貨(こうか)を鋳造(ちゅうぞう)する: to mint coins
  • 銅像(どうぞう)を鋳造(ちゅうぞう)する: to cast a bronze statue
  • 鋳造(ちゅうぞう)技術(ぎじゅつ): casting technology
  • 鋳造(ちゅうぞう)工場(こうじょう): a foundry

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鋳物(いもの): cast metal goods — the finished products of 鋳造(ちゅうぞう)
  • 鍛造(たんぞう): forging — shaping hot metal by hammering, a different process
  • 製造(せいぞう): manufacturing — the general term for producing goods
  • 成型(せいけい): molding — shaping materials (often plastics) using a form
