合意書(ごういしょ)

ごういしょ
noun
agreement; written agreement; memorandum of understanding
1. written agreement; memorandum of understanding; accord document
A formal written document recording the terms agreed upon by two or more parties. Less binding than a full contract but carries legal and moral weight.
合意書(ごういしょ)に署名(しょめい)する。
To sign an agreement.
両者(りょうしゃ)の間(あいだ)で合意書(ごういしょ)を交(か)わした。
Both parties exchanged a written agreement.
離婚(りこん)の条件(じょうけん)について合意書(ごういしょ)を作成(さくせい)し、弁護士(べんごし)に確認(かくにん)してもらった。
We drafted an agreement on the divorce conditions and had a lawyer review it.

A compound of 合意(ごうい) (agreement/consensus) + 書(しょ) (document). Used in legal, business, and personal contexts where parties need to formally record their agreement.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 合意書(ごういしょ)を作成(さくせい)する: to draft an agreement
  • 合意書(ごういしょ)に署名(しょめい)する: to sign an agreement
  • 合意書(ごういしょ)を交(か)わす: to exchange agreements
  • 合意書(ごういしょ)の内容(ないよう): contents of the agreement

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 契約書(けいやくしょ): contract — a more formal and legally binding document
  • 覚書(おぼえがき): memorandum — a written record of understanding, similar in function
  • 同意書(どういしょ): consent form — a document expressing consent to something specific
  • 誓約書(せいやくしょ): written pledge — a document with a promise or oath
