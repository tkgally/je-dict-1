1. conveniently; fortunately; by good timing
Things working out in a way that happens to suit the situation or the person. Can be genuinely positive.
都合(つごう)よく席(せき)が空(あ)いていた。
Conveniently, a seat was open.
都合(つごう)よく雨(あめ)が止(や)んだ。
Luckily, the rain stopped.
天気(てんき)予報(よほう)が外(はず)れて、都合(つごう)よく晴(は)れてくれた。
The weather forecast was wrong, and fortunately it cleared up for us.
2. self-servingly; in a way that suits one's own interests
Interpreting or arranging things to serve one's own convenience or advantage. Carries a critical nuance, suggesting someone is being selectively convenient.
都合(つごう)よく解釈(かいしゃく)するな。
Don't interpret things to suit yourself.
自分(じぶん)に都合(つごう)よく話(はなし)を変(か)えている。
You're changing the story to suit yourself.
そんなに都合(つごう)よく物事(ものごと)は進(すす)まない。
Things don't go that conveniently.

The adverbial form of 都合(つごう)がいい (convenient, favorable). In sense 1, it describes fortunate timing or circumstances. In sense 2, it implies someone is being self-serving or naively optimistic. Context and tone determine which nuance is intended.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 都合(つごう)よく解釈(かいしゃく)する: to interpret self-servingly
  • 自分(じぶん)に都合(つごう)よく: to suit oneself
  • 都合(つごう)よく忘(わす)れる: to conveniently forget

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 好都合(こうつごう)に: conveniently — more formal; emphasizes favorable circumstances
  • 折(おり)よく: opportunely, at a good time — emphasizes timing
  • 幸(さいわ)い: fortunately — a broader term for general good luck
