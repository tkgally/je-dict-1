鑑定書(かんていしょ)

かんていしょ
noun
appraisal certificate; expert evaluation document
1. appraisal certificate; expert evaluation document; authentication certificate
An official document produced by a qualified expert that certifies the value, authenticity, or quality of an item. Used for jewelry, art, real estate, and forensic evidence.
鑑定書(かんていしょ)付(つ)きのダイヤモンドだ。
This is a diamond with an appraisal certificate.
不動産(ふどうさん)の鑑定書(かんていしょ)を依頼(いらい)した。
I requested a real estate appraisal certificate.
この絵画(かいが)は専門家(せんもんか)の鑑定書(かんていしょ)があるので、本物(ほんもの)であることが証明(しょうめい)されている。
This painting has an expert's appraisal certificate, so its authenticity is proven.

Composed of 鑑定(かんてい) (appraisal, expert evaluation) and 書(しょ) (document). A 鑑定書(かんていしょ) carries professional authority — it is issued by a certified appraiser or expert and serves as proof of an item's value, quality, or authenticity.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鑑定書(かんていしょ)付(つ)き: with an appraisal certificate
  • 鑑定書(かんていしょ)を発行(はっこう)する: to issue an appraisal certificate
  • 鑑定書(かんていしょ)を依頼(いらい)する: to request an appraisal
  • 宝石(ほうせき)の鑑定書(かんていしょ): gemstone appraisal certificate
  • DNA鑑定書(かんていしょ): DNA test report

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鑑別書(かんべつしょ): identification certificate — certifies what something is (e.g., type of gemstone) but not its grade or value
  • 証明書(しょうめいしょ): certificate, proof — a general term for any official certificate
  • 鑑定(かんてい): appraisal — the act of appraising, without reference to the document itself
