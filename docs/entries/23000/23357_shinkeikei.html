神経系(しんけいけい)

しんけいけい
noun
nervous system
1. nervous system
The network of nerves, the brain, and the spinal cord that transmits signals throughout the body of an animal. A technical term used in biology, medicine, and health writing.
神経系(しんけいけい)を調(しら)べる。
To examine the nervous system.
この病気(びょうき)は神経系(しんけいけい)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える。
This illness affects the nervous system.
神経系(しんけいけい)は脳(のう)と脊髄(せきずい)と末梢(まっしょう)神経(しんけい)から成(な)り立(た)っている。
The nervous system is made up of the brain, the spinal cord, and the peripheral nerves.
交通(こうつう)事故(じこ)の後遺症(こういしょう)で神経系(しんけいけい)の異常(いじょう)が出(で)ることがある。
Abnormalities of the nervous system sometimes appear as aftereffects of traffic accidents.

Composed of 神経(しんけい) (nerve, nerves) + 系(けい) (system, lineage, line). The suffix 系(けい) here turns a body-part noun into 'the system of that part,' parallel to 呼吸(こきゅう)器(き)系(けい) (respiratory system) and 消化器(しょうかき)系(けい) (digestive system).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中枢(ちゅうすう)神経系(しんけいけい): central nervous system (brain and spinal cord)
  • 末梢(まっしょう)神経系(しんけいけい): peripheral nervous system
  • 自律(じりつ)神経系(しんけいけい): autonomic nervous system
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)の疾患(しっかん): disorders of the nervous system
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)に影響(えいきょう)を与(あた)える: to affect the nervous system
  • 神経系(しんけいけい)が発達(はったつ)する: for the nervous system to develop

REGISTER AND USAGE:
A specialist term from biology and medicine. In casual conversation people more often say 神経(しんけい) alone (e.g., 'the nerves are damaged'); 神経系(しんけいけい) is used when the emphasis is on the whole network as a system, especially in textbooks, articles, and clinical writing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 神経(しんけい): nerve(s) — the basic body-part word; refers to individual nerves or nervous tissue rather than the whole system.
  • 脳神経(のうしんけい): cranial nerves — the nerves emerging from the brain.
  • 神経細胞(しんけいさいぼう): neuron; nerve cell — the individual cells that make up the nervous system.
