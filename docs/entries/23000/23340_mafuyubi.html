真冬日(まふゆび)

まふゆび
noun
day when the highest temperature stays below freezing
1. day when the maximum temperature stays below 0°C
A weather-service term for a day on which the highest temperature of the day does not rise above zero degrees Celsius. Such days are common in Hokkaido and mountain areas during midwinter and appear regularly in winter weather reports.
今日(きょう)は真冬日(まふゆび)だ。
Today is a day when the high stays below freezing.
北海道(ほっかいどう)では真冬日(まふゆび)が続(つづ)いている。
In Hokkaido, days with highs below freezing are continuing.
天気(てんき)予報(よほう)によると、明日(あした)は今年(ことし)最初(さいしょ)の真冬日(まふゆび)になる見(み)込(こ)みだ。
According to the forecast, tomorrow is expected to be the first day of this winter when the high stays below freezing.
寒波(かんぱ)の影響(えいきょう)で東京(とうきょう)でも真冬日(まふゆび)が観測(かんそく)され、水道管(すいどうかん)の凍結(とうけつ)被害(ひがい)が相次(あいつ)いだ。
Due to the cold wave, a day with highs below freezing was observed even in Tokyo, and there were a string of cases of water pipes freezing.

Composed of 真(ま) (true, truly) + 冬(ふゆ) (winter) + 日(び) (day). Literally "a true winter day." A technical term from the Japan Meteorological Agency, defined as a day whose maximum temperature does not exceed 0°C. The same set of terms defines various types of hot and cold days — see below.

TEMPERATURE DAY TERMS (Japan Meteorological Agency):

  • 冬日(ふゆび): winter day — minimum temperature below 0°C (a frosty night, but the day may warm up)
  • 真冬日(まふゆび): midwinter day — maximum temperature below 0°C (stays frozen all day)
  • 夏日(なつび): summer day — maximum temperature at or above 25°C
  • 真夏日(まなつび): midsummer day — maximum temperature at or above 30°C
  • 猛暑日(もうしょび): extremely hot day — maximum temperature at or above 35°C
  • 熱帯夜(ねったいや): tropical night — minimum temperature at or above 25°C

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 真冬日(まふゆび)が続(つづ)く: days with highs below freezing continue
  • 真冬日(まふゆび)を観測(かんそく)する: to record/observe a sub-freezing day
  • 今年(ことし)初(はじ)めての真冬日(まふゆび): the first sub-freezing day of this year
  • 真冬日(まふゆび)日数(にっすう): the number of sub-freezing days

USAGE:
The word appears mainly in weather forecasts, news reports, and climate statistics. Because the definition is precise (maximum below 0°C), it has none of the vague feel of English "freezing day" — a 真冬日(まふゆび) is something that can be counted and tallied by weather stations. In Hokkaido, reports often highlight the number of consecutive 真冬日(まふゆび) during cold snaps.
