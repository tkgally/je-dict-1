人間(にんげん)ドック

にんげんどっく
noun
comprehensive medical checkup; thorough physical examination
1. comprehensive medical checkup; thorough physical examination
A comprehensive one-day or multi-day health examination that includes blood tests, imaging, internal organ checks, and other detailed screening. More thorough than a standard health checkup and typically paid for privately.
人間(にんげん)ドックを受(う)ける。
To have a comprehensive medical checkup.
毎年(まいとし)人間(にんげん)ドックを受(う)けている。
I have a comprehensive medical checkup every year.
人間(にんげん)ドックで胃(い)に異常(いじょう)が見(み)つかった。
An abnormality was found in my stomach during the comprehensive checkup.
四十歳(よんじゅっさい)を過(す)ぎてから、人間(にんげん)ドックを定期的(ていきてき)に受(う)けるようになった。
After turning forty, I started getting comprehensive medical checkups regularly.

Composed of 人間(にんげん) (human) + ドック (dock), borrowing the nautical metaphor of a ship entering dry dock for thorough maintenance. A Japanese-coined term referring to a voluntary, comprehensive health checkup that goes well beyond the basic annual checkup. Not usually covered by public health insurance, though many employers and insurance plans subsidize it.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 人間(にんげん)ドックを受(う)ける: to have a comprehensive medical checkup
  • 人間(にんげん)ドックの結果(けっか): comprehensive checkup results
  • 人間(にんげん)ドックの予約(よやく): comprehensive checkup reservation
  • 一(いち)日(にち)人間(にんげん)ドック: one-day comprehensive checkup
  • 日帰(ひがえ)り人間(にんげん)ドック: same-day (non-overnight) comprehensive checkup

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 健康診断(けんこうしんだん): health checkup — the basic annual checkup, usually free through employers or municipalities
  • 定期健診(ていきけんしん): regular health checkup — routine, employer-provided
  • 脳(のう)ドック: brain checkup — a specialized version focusing on the brain (using MRI/CT)

CULTURAL NOTE:
人間(にんげん)ドックs are a distinctively Japanese institution and are associated with taking preventive care of one's health, especially from middle age onward. They are often considered a responsible "adult" habit.
