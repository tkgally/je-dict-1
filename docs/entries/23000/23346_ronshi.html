論旨(ろんし)

ろんし
noun
the point of an argument; the gist; the main thrust
1. the point of an argument; the gist; the main thrust
The central point a written argument or essay is trying to make — what the author is actually claiming, as opposed to the surrounding examples, background, or asides. A word from formal writing and academic language.
論旨(ろんし)が明確(めいかく)だ。
The point of the argument is clear.
この文章(ぶんしょう)の論旨(ろんし)をまとめなさい。
Summarize the main point of this passage.
筆者(ひっしゃ)の論旨(ろんし)には賛成(さんせい)だが、例(れい)の選(えら)び方(かた)には疑問(ぎもん)がある。
I agree with the author's main argument, but I have doubts about the choice of examples.
論旨(ろんし)が途中(とちゅう)で変(か)わってしまっており、読(よ)み手(て)には筆者(ひっしゃ)が何(なに)を言(い)いたいのか分(わ)かりにくい。
The thrust of the argument shifts partway through, and it is hard for the reader to tell what the author wants to say.

Composed of 論(ろん) (argument, discussion, theory) + 旨(し) (meaning, purport, gist). A formal written word meaning "the core point the argument is making." It appears constantly in essays, academic writing, book reviews, and high-school Japanese textbooks — especially in reading-comprehension questions that ask students to identify the author's 論旨(ろんし).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 論旨(ろんし)が明確(めいかく)だ: the point is clear / the argument is well-focused
  • 論旨(ろんし)が不明確(ふめいかく)だ: the point is unclear
  • 論旨(ろんし)をまとめる: to summarize the main point
  • 論旨(ろんし)を追(お)う: to follow the line of an argument
  • 論旨(ろんし)がずれる: the argument drifts off topic
  • 論旨(ろんし)明快(めいかい): clear and lucid in its argument — a common set phrase of praise for an essay
  • 論旨(ろんし)に賛同(さんどう)する: to agree with the main point of an argument

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 主旨(しゅし): main point; gist — the central point of any piece of writing or speech; overlaps heavily with 論旨(ろんし) but is not limited to arguments.
  • 要旨(ようし): summary; gist — the condensed content of a passage (the abstract of a paper, for example).
  • 趣旨(しゅし): intent; purpose; gist — the aim or intent behind something, not just its argumentative point; used for proposals, laws, meetings, and essays.
  • 結論(けつろん): conclusion — the final claim arrived at, not the overall line of reasoning.

USAGE:
Use 論旨(ろんし) only when talking about writing or discussion that has an "argument" in it — an essay, editorial, paper, or debate. For the general idea of a casual conversation or a story, use 話(はなし)の要点(ようてん) or 要旨(ようし) instead. Common in school reading exercises: 次(つぎ)の文章(ぶんしょう)の論旨(ろんし)を百字(ひゃくじ)以内(いない)でまとめなさい (summarize the main argument of the following passage in 100 characters or less).
