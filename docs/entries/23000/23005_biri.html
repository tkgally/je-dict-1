ビリ

びり
noun
last place; dead last; the bottom
1. last place; dead last; the bottom; the worst position
The very last position in a ranking, race, competition, or group. A casual, somewhat humorous word.
マラソンでビリだった。
I came in last in the marathon.
テストの成績(せいせき)がクラスでビリだった。
My test score was the lowest in the class.
ビリから二番目(にばんめ)だったから、ビリよりはましだと自分(じぶん)を慰(なぐさ)めた。
I was second to last, so I consoled myself that at least it was better than being dead last.

A casual, colloquial word for being in last place. It has a somewhat lighthearted tone and is commonly used in everyday conversation, especially among children and students.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • ビリになる: to come in last
  • ビリから二番目(にばんめ): second to last
  • ビリっけつ: dead last (slang, emphatic; from ビリ + 尻(けつ) 'butt')
  • 万年(まんねん)ビリ: perennially last place

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 最下位(さいかい): last place — formal equivalent; used in official rankings and sports standings
  • ドベ: dead last — dialectal (Nagoya/Kansai area) variant with the same meaning
  • 最後(さいご): last — broader meaning; can mean 'last' in sequence, not necessarily worst

REGISTER:
Informal and conversational. Not used in formal writing or official contexts. Written in katakana (ビリ).
