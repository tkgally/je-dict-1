維持費(いじひ)

いじひ
noun
maintenance cost; upkeep expenses
1. maintenance cost; upkeep expenses; running cost
The ongoing expenses required to keep something in working condition or operational state, such as a building, vehicle, or organization.
車(くるま)の維持費(いじひ)がかかる。
A car costs a lot to maintain.
マンションの維持費(いじひ)は月(つき)に三万円(さんまんえん)ほどだ。
The maintenance cost for the apartment is about 30,000 yen per month.
古(ふる)い建物(たてもの)は維持費(いじひ)がかさむため、建(た)て替(か)えを検討(けんとう)している。
Because maintenance costs are piling up for the old building, they are considering rebuilding.

A compound of 維持(いじ) (maintenance) + 費(ひ) (cost/expense). A practical, frequently used word in discussions about budgets, property ownership, and vehicle expenses.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 維持費(いじひ)がかかる: maintenance costs are high
  • 維持費(いじひ)がかさむ: maintenance costs pile up
  • 維持費(いじひ)を抑(おさ)える: to keep maintenance costs down
  • 年間(ねんかん)維持費(いじひ): annual maintenance cost

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ランニングコスト: running cost — loanword for ongoing operational costs
  • 管理費(かんりひ): management fee — fee for property management (often for apartments)
  • 修繕費(しゅうぜんひ): repair expenses — specifically for fixing and restoring
  • 経費(けいひ): expenses — broader term for general costs
