中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)

ちゅうとうきょういく
noun
secondary education
1. the middle stage of formal schooling that sits between primary and higher education, generally corresponding to junior high school and high school; secondary education
The level of formal education between elementary school and university. In the Japanese system, 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく) covers 中学校(ちゅうがっこう) (junior high school, ages 12-15) and 高等学校(こうとうがっこう) (senior high school, ages 15-18). There are also combined 中等教育学校(ちゅうとうきょういくがっこう) that run for six years and teach both stages as one program. The term is used mainly in official education policy documents, statistics, and academic writing; in everyday life people talk about 中学校(ちゅうがっこう) or 高校(こうこう) directly.
日本(にほん)の中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)は六年間(ろくねんかん)だ。
Japan's secondary education lasts six years.
中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)段階(だんかい)で進路(しんろ)を考(かんが)え始(はじ)める。
Students start thinking about their future careers at the secondary education stage.
初等教育(しょとうきょういく)と中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)の連携(れんけい)が重要(じゅうよう)だ。
Coordination between primary and secondary education is important.
中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)を修了(しゅうりょう)した時点(じてん)で就職(しゅうしょく)する人(ひと)も少(すく)なくない。
There are also quite a few people who get a job at the point where they finish secondary education.

Composed of 中等(ちゅうとう) (middle level, intermediate) + 教育(きょういく) (education). Part of a three-part set of terms that divide formal schooling by level: 初等(しょとう) (primary) → 中等(ちゅうとう) (secondary) → 高等(こうとう) (higher / tertiary).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)を受(う)ける: to receive secondary education
  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)学校(がっこう): combined secondary school (6-year junior+senior high)
  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)機関(きかん): secondary education institution
  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)段階(だんかい): the secondary education stage
  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく)の課程(かてい): the secondary education curriculum

THE THREE STAGES OF EDUCATION:

  • 初等教育(しょとうきょういく): primary education — elementary school (小学校(しょうがっこう)), grades 1-6.
  • 中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく): secondary education — junior high (中学校(ちゅうがっこう)) + senior high (高等学校(こうとうがっこう)), grades 7-12.
  • 高等教育(こうとうきょういく): higher / tertiary education — university (大学(だいがく)), graduate school (大学院(だいがくいん)), junior college, etc.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 義務教育(ぎむきょういく): compulsory education — in Japan, this covers elementary school plus lower secondary (中学校(ちゅうがっこう)), i.e. nine years total. Upper secondary (高校(こうこう)) is not legally compulsory but is attended by almost all students.
  • 前期(ぜんき)中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく): lower secondary education (中学校(ちゅうがっこう)).
  • 後期(こうき)中等教育(ちゅうとうきょういく): upper secondary education (高等学校(こうとうがっこう)).

REGISTER:
Formal and academic. Almost exclusively used in government documents, education policy discussions, research papers, and comparative international education statistics. In ordinary conversation, Japanese speakers simply say 中学校(ちゅうがっこう) and 高校(こうこう).
