伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん)

でんたつしゅだん
noun
means of communication; method of transmission
1. means of communication; method of transmission
A method or tool used to convey information from one party to another, such as letters, telephone, email, or social media.
便利(べんり)な伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん)。
A convenient means of communication.
メールは最(もっと)も一般的(いっぱんてき)な伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん)の一(ひと)つだ。
Email is one of the most common means of communication.
緊急時(きんきゅうじ)にはどのような伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん)を使(つか)うか、事前(じぜん)に決(き)めておく必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to decide in advance what means of communication to use in an emergency.

Composed of 伝達(でんたつ) (transmission, conveying) and 手段(しゅだん) (means, method). A relatively formal and technical term used in writing and discussion about how information is conveyed. It can refer to both traditional means (letters, telegrams, telephone) and modern ones (email, messaging apps, social media). In casual speech, people are more likely to say 連絡手段(れんらくしゅだん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん)を選(えら)ぶ: to choose a means of communication
  • 新(あたら)しい伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん): a new means of communication
  • 主要(しゅよう)な伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん): the main/primary means of communication
  • 情報(じょうほう)伝達手段(でんたつしゅだん): means of transmitting information

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 連絡手段(れんらくしゅだん): means of contact — everyday word for how you reach someone (phone, email, etc.)
  • 通信手段(つうしんしゅだん): means of telecommunication — emphasizes the technological/communication infrastructure
  • メディア: media — broader, often refers to mass communication
