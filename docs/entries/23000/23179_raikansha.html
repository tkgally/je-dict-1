来館者(らいかんしゃ)

らいかんしゃ
noun
visitor (to a museum, library, or facility)
1. visitor; patron (of a museum, library, or public facility)
A person who visits a public building or facility such as a museum, library, community center, or exhibition hall.
来館者(らいかんしゃ)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of visitors is increasing.
来館者(らいかんしゃ)は受付(うけつけ)で名前(なまえ)を記入(きにゅう)してください。
Visitors, please write your name at the reception desk.
この美術館(びじゅつかん)は年間(ねんかん)百万人(ひゃくまんにん)の来館者(らいかんしゃ)を迎(むか)えている。
This art museum receives one million visitors per year.

Composed of 来(らい) (coming), 館(かん) (hall, building), and 者(しゃ) (person). Specifically refers to visitors to buildings designated by 館(かん) — museums, libraries, exhibition halls, and community centers. Not used for visitors to stores, temples, or homes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 来館者(らいかんしゃ)数(すう): number of visitors
  • 来館者(らいかんしゃ)アンケート: visitor survey
  • 来館者(らいかんしゃ)が増(ふ)える: visitors increase
  • 来館者(らいかんしゃ)を迎(むか)える: to welcome visitors

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 来場者(らいじょうしゃ): visitor to an event venue — used for fairs, concerts, exhibitions
  • 来店者(らいてんしゃ): visitor to a shop — customers coming to a store
  • 訪問者(ほうもんしゃ): visitor — more general term for anyone who visits a place or person
  • 入館者(にゅうかんしゃ): person entering a facility — emphasizes the act of entering rather than visiting
