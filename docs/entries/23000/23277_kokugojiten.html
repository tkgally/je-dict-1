国語辞典(こくごじてん)

こくごじてん
noun
Japanese dictionary (Japanese-Japanese)
1. Japanese-language dictionary; monolingual Japanese dictionary
A dictionary that defines Japanese words in Japanese, used by native speakers and advanced learners. Distinct from bilingual dictionaries such as 和英辞典(わえいじてん) (Japanese-English dictionary).
国語辞典(こくごじてん)で調(しら)べる。
To look it up in a Japanese dictionary.
新(あたら)しい国語辞典(こくごじてん)を買(か)った。
I bought a new Japanese dictionary.
この言葉(ことば)の意味(いみ)が分(わ)からないから、国語辞典(こくごじてん)を引(ひ)いてみよう。
I don't know what this word means, so let me look it up in a Japanese dictionary.
小学生(しょうがくせい)でも使(つか)える国語辞典(こくごじてん)がたくさん出版(しゅっぱん)されている。
Many Japanese dictionaries that even elementary school students can use are published.

A dictionary that explains Japanese words in Japanese, comparable to monolingual English dictionaries like Merriam-Webster or the Oxford English Dictionary. These are the standard reference works used in Japanese schools and homes.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国語辞典(こくごじてん)を引(ひ)く: to consult a Japanese dictionary
  • 国語辞典(こくごじてん)で調(しら)べる: to look up in a Japanese dictionary
  • 国語辞典(こくごじてん)に載(の)っている: to be listed in a Japanese dictionary
  • 電子(でんし)国語辞典(こくごじてん): an electronic Japanese dictionary

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 和英辞典(わえいじてん): Japanese-English dictionary — translates Japanese words into English
  • 英和辞典(えいわじてん): English-Japanese dictionary — translates English words into Japanese
  • 漢和辞典(かんわじてん): kanji dictionary — focused on the meanings and readings of individual kanji
  • 辞書(じしょ): dictionary (general term) — covers any type of dictionary

CULTURAL NOTE:
Well-known Japanese dictionaries include 広辞苑(こうじえん), 大辞林(だいじりん), and 新明解国語辞典(しんめいかいこくごじてん). Each has its own editorial character — 新明解(しんめいかい) is famous for its unusually opinionated definitions.
