1. reexamination; retest; follow-up test (especially medical)
Running a test, measurement, or examination a second time, usually to confirm an abnormal result, monitor a change, or double-check a decision. Most often heard in medical contexts — for example, after a health check-up returns borderline numbers — but also used for inspections and technical tests in general.
来月(らいげつ)、再検査(さいけんさ)を受(う)ける。
I will have a follow-up test next month.
健康(けんこう)診断(しんだん)の結果(けっか)、再検査(さいけんさ)が必要(ひつよう)だと言(い)われた。
Based on the health check-up results, I was told I needed a follow-up test.
血液(けつえき)の数値(すうち)に異常(いじょう)があったので、医者(いしゃ)は再検査(さいけんさ)を指示(しじ)した。
Because there was an abnormality in the blood values, the doctor ordered a follow-up test.
工場(こうじょう)の製品(せいひん)に欠陥(けっかん)が見(み)つかったため、全(すべ)てのロットで再検査(さいけんさ)が行(おこな)われた。
Because a defect was found in the factory's products, a reexamination was carried out on all the lots.

Composed of the prefix 再(さい) (again, re-) + 検査(けんさ) (inspection, examination, test). Appears most frequently in the medical phrase 再検査(さいけんさ)を受(う)ける (to undergo a follow-up test), since Japanese annual health check-ups routinely flag borderline values and ask the patient to come back for confirmation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再検査(さいけんさ)を受(う)ける: to undergo a follow-up test
  • 再検査(さいけんさ)が必要(ひつよう)だ: a follow-up test is needed
  • 再検査(さいけんさ)を受(う)けるよう指示(しじ)する: to instruct (someone) to undergo a follow-up test
  • 再検査(さいけんさ)通知(つうち): notice of the need for a follow-up test
  • 血液(けつえき)の再検査(さいけんさ): a follow-up blood test

VERBAL USE:
The word can take する to act as a verb: 再検査(さいけんさ)する — to reexamine, to retest. In that form, the subject is the one doing the checking: 医者(いしゃ)が数値(すうち)を再検査(さいけんさ)した (the doctor retested the values). In patient-side usage the passive-like 再検査(さいけんさ)を受(う)ける is much more common.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 精密(せいみつ)検査(けんさ): detailed examination / thorough test — a more in-depth follow-up, usually ordered when the initial screening shows a clear problem.
  • 追加(ついか)検査(けんさ): additional test — tests added on top of what was originally planned, not necessarily a redo.
  • 再診(さいしん): a return visit to the doctor — refers to the appointment itself, not to the test.

USAGE:
Beyond medicine, you will encounter the word in quality control (factory inspections, food safety), academic contexts (retaking a test), and engineering (retesting equipment). In all these uses, it keeps the core sense of "checking again."
