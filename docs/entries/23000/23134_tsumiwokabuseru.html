1. to pin a crime on someone; to frame someone; to shift the blame
To make someone else take the blame for something they did not do. Literally means to "put guilt/crime on top of someone." Used for both serious criminal framing and everyday blame-shifting.
他人(たにん)に罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)せた。
He pinned the blame on someone else.
無実(むじつ)の人(ひと)に罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)せるのは許(ゆる)せない。
It's unforgivable to frame an innocent person.
自分(じぶん)の失敗(しっぱい)の罪(つみ)を部下(ぶか)に被(かぶ)せるような上司(じょうし)は信頼(しんらい)されない。
A boss who pins the blame for their own failures on subordinates won't be trusted.

Composed of 罪(つみ) (crime, guilt, blame) + を (object marker) + 被(かぶ)せる (to put on top of, to cover with). The verb 被(かぶ)せる literally means to place something over or on top of someone, like putting a hat on them — here, the "crime" is placed on them. Compare with 罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)る (to take the blame), where the person themselves "wears" the guilt.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)せられる: to have the blame pinned on oneself (passive)
  • 無実(むじつ)の人(ひと)に罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)せる: to frame an innocent person
  • 他人(たにん)に罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)せる: to pin blame on others

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 罪(つみ)を着(き)せる: to frame, to accuse falsely — very similar in meaning; uses the metaphor of "dressing" someone in guilt
  • 罪(つみ)を被(かぶ)る: to take the blame — the inverse; the person accepts the guilt themselves
  • 濡(ぬ)れ衣(ぎぬ)を着(き)せる: to put a false charge on someone — a more literary expression meaning to falsely accuse
