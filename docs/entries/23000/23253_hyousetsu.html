1. plagiarism
The act of copying another person's writing, ideas, or creative work and passing them off as one's own, especially without proper attribution. Used in academic, journalistic, and legal contexts. Can also be used as a verb with する ("to plagiarize").
論文(ろんぶん)の剽窃(ひょうせつ)。
Plagiarism of a paper.
他人(たにん)の作品(さくひん)を剽窃(ひょうせつ)してはいけない。
You must not plagiarize other people's work.
彼(かれ)の論文(ろんぶん)は剽窃(ひょうせつ)が発覚(はっかく)し、学位(がくい)を取(と)り消(け)されることになった。
Plagiarism was discovered in his paper, and his degree was ultimately revoked.

Composed of 剽(ひょう) (to steal, to plunder) and 窃(せつ) (to steal stealthily). Both characters mean "steal," reflecting the strong negative force of the word. 剽窃(ひょうせつ) is a formal, somewhat technical term used in academia, publishing, and legal discussions. In everyday speech, the katakana loanword パクる (to rip off) is much more common.

USAGE:
Frequently takes する to form a verb: 剽窃(ひょうせつ)する (to plagiarize). It can also be modified to 剽窃(ひょうせつ)行為(こうい) (an act of plagiarism) for abstract reference.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 論文(ろんぶん)の剽窃(ひょうせつ): plagiarism in a paper
  • 剽窃(ひょうせつ)疑惑(ぎわく): suspicion of plagiarism
  • 剽窃(ひょうせつ)が発覚(はっかく)する: plagiarism is uncovered
  • 剽窃(ひょうせつ)行為(こうい): an act of plagiarism

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 盗用(とうよう): unauthorized use of someone's work — very close in meaning
  • 盗作(とうさく): plagiarism of a creative work (literature, music)
  • 無断転載(むだんてんさい): unauthorized reproduction — often of online content
  • パクリ / パクる: rip-off / to rip off — casual, slangy equivalent
