英和辞典(えいわじてん)

えいわじてん
noun
English-Japanese dictionary
1. a dictionary in which English headwords are defined and translated into Japanese
A bilingual dictionary used by Japanese speakers to look up English words. The headwords are in English, with Japanese translations, pronunciations, and example sentences. Standard reference tool for Japanese learners of English.
英和辞典(えいわじてん)で調(しら)べる。
To look (something) up in an English-Japanese dictionary.
分(わ)からない単語(たんご)があったら、英和辞典(えいわじてん)を引(ひ)いてみてください。
If there's a word you don't know, try looking it up in an English-Japanese dictionary.
最近(さいきん)はスマホのアプリで英和辞典(えいわじてん)を使(つか)う人(ひと)が増(ふ)えている。
Recently, more and more people are using English-Japanese dictionaries as smartphone apps.
紙(かみ)の英和辞典(えいわじてん)は重(おも)いけれど、例文(れいぶん)がたくさん載(の)っていて勉強(べんきょう)に役立(やくだ)つ。
Paper English-Japanese dictionaries are heavy, but they contain many example sentences and are useful for study.

Composed of 英(えい) (English) + 和(わ) (Japanese, in the Sino-Japanese sense of "Japan") + 辞典(じてん) (dictionary). The compound is read without any special connecting morphemes, and the ordering — source language first, target language second — matches the direction of lookup: English → Japanese.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 英和辞典(えいわじてん)を引(ひ)く: to consult an English-Japanese dictionary
  • 英和辞典(えいわじてん)で調(しら)べる: to look up in an English-Japanese dictionary
  • 英和辞典(えいわじてん)を買(か)う: to buy an English-Japanese dictionary
  • 学習(がくしゅう)英和辞典(えいわじてん): a learner's English-Japanese dictionary
  • 電子(でんし)英和辞典(えいわじてん): an electronic English-Japanese dictionary

PARALLEL DICTIONARY TYPES:

  • 和英辞典(わえいじてん): Japanese-English dictionary (headwords in Japanese, definitions in English)
  • 国語辞典(こくごじてん): a monolingual Japanese dictionary
  • 漢和辞典(かんわじてん): a Chinese character dictionary (kanji headwords, Japanese definitions)
  • 英英辞典(えいえいじてん): an English-English dictionary (monolingual English)
  • 仏和辞典(ふつわじてん): a French-Japanese dictionary
  • 独和辞典(どくわじてん): a German-Japanese dictionary

The pattern [Source Language] + 和(わ) + 辞典(じてん) is extremely productive: any bilingual dictionary translating into Japanese follows it.

CULTURAL NOTE:
For decades, a paper 英和辞典(えいわじてん) was a standard possession of every Japanese junior high and high school student. In recent years, electronic dictionaries (電子(でんし)辞書(じしょ)) and smartphone apps have largely replaced the paper versions, but the word 英和辞典(えいわじてん) remains in common use regardless of the medium.
