1. the process of forming crystals from a solution, melt, or vapor
In chemistry, physics, and food science, the process by which solid crystal structures form out of a liquid or gas. Used literally to describe sugar, salt, minerals, ice, and other substances taking crystalline form.
砂糖(さとう)が結晶化(けっしょうか)する。
The sugar crystallizes.
溶液(ようえき)を冷(ひ)やすと、塩(しお)が結晶化(けっしょうか)してきた。
When the solution was cooled, the salt started to crystallize.
はちみつは時間(じかん)が経(た)つと一部(いちぶ)が結晶化(けっしょうか)することがあるが、品質(ひんしつ)に問題(もんだい)はない。
Over time, part of honey can crystallize, but there is no problem with the quality.
2. (figurative) the process of intangible efforts or ideas taking clear, concrete form
A metaphorical extension: an accumulation of effort, experience, or thought finally "crystallizing" into a finished, recognizable result — a product, work of art, theory, or achievement. Common in writing about creative and intellectual work.
この作品(さくひん)は彼(かれ)の長年(ながねん)の研究(けんきゅう)の結晶化(けっしょうか)と言(い)えるだろう。
This work could be called the crystallization of his many years of research.
アイデアを結晶化(けっしょうか)させるには、時間(じかん)と試行(しこう)錯誤(さくご)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To crystallize an idea, you need time and trial and error.
技術者(ぎじゅつしゃ)たちの努力(どりょく)が、最終的(さいしゅうてき)にこの新(あたら)しい製品(せいひん)として結晶化(けっしょうか)した。
The engineers' efforts finally crystallized into this new product.

Composed of 結晶(けっしょう) (crystal) + 化(か) (the nominal suffix "-ization"), patterned on other -化(か) compounds like 液化(えきか) (liquefaction), 固化(こか) (solidification), and 気化(きか) (vaporization). Takes する to form a suru verb.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)する: intransitive — "to crystallize" (something undergoes crystallization)
  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)させる: transitive — "to cause to crystallize" (someone brings something to crystalline form)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)する: to crystallize
  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)させる: to crystallize (something)
  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)が進(すす)む: crystallization progresses
  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)温度(おんど): crystallization temperature
  • 結晶化(けっしょうか)過程(かてい): crystallization process

LITERAL VS FIGURATIVE USE:
SENSE 1 (literal) appears in scientific and technical writing, cooking, geology, and materials science. It describes a concrete physical process.

SENSE 2 (figurative) appears in reviews, essays, speeches, and prefaces. A typical pattern is "X years of effort / research / experience が結晶化(けっしょうか)した Y" — "a Y that is the crystallization of X." In this figurative sense, the noun form 結晶(けっしょう) (crystal, fruit of one's labor) is even more frequent: 長年(ながねん)の努力(どりょく)の結晶(けっしょう) (the crystallization of many years of effort).

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 具体化(ぐたいか): taking concrete form — more neutral, emphasizes moving from abstract to concrete without the imagery of compression.
  • 実現(じつげん): realization; bringing to reality — emphasizes the gap between plan and completion.
  • 結実(けつじつ): bearing fruit (literal or figurative) — a similar metaphor using plant imagery.
