痛(いた)み止(ど)め

いたみどめ
noun
painkiller; pain reliever; analgesic
1. painkiller; pain reliever; analgesic
A medicine or drug that reduces or eliminates pain. The most common everyday word for over-the-counter and prescription pain medication.
痛(いた)み止(ど)めを飲(の)んだ。
I took a painkiller.
頭痛(ずつう)がひどいので痛(いた)み止(ど)めをください。
I have a bad headache, so please give me a painkiller.
医者(いしゃ)に痛(いた)み止(ど)めを処方(しょほう)してもらった。
I had the doctor prescribe me a painkiller.
痛(いた)み止(ど)めが切(き)れて、また歯(は)が痛(いた)くなってきた。
The painkiller wore off, and my tooth started hurting again.

Composed of 痛(いた)み (pain, from the verb 痛(いた)む) and 止(ど)め (stopping, from the verb 止(と)める), with the voicing change と→ど. Literally "pain-stopper." This is the standard everyday term for painkillers of any kind — the word learners are most likely to need at a pharmacy or doctor's office.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 痛(いた)み止(ど)めを飲(の)む: to take a painkiller
  • 痛(いた)み止(ど)めを処方(しょほう)する: to prescribe a painkiller
  • 痛(いた)み止(ど)めが効(き)く: the painkiller takes effect
  • 痛(いた)み止(ど)めが切(き)れる: the painkiller wears off
  • 市販(しはん)の痛(いた)み止(ど)め: over-the-counter painkiller

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 鎮痛剤(ちんつうざい): analgesic — the more formal/medical term for the same thing
  • 解熱鎮痛剤(げねつちんつうざい): antipyretic analgesic — a fever reducer that also relieves pain
  • 湿布(しっぷ): compress/patch — a topical pain relief method rather than oral medication
