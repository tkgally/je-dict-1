1. sinking; subsidence; settling; sedimentation
The process of sinking or settling downward. Used in scientific and technical contexts for geological subsidence, the settling of particles in liquid, or the sinking of land.
地盤(じばん)の沈降(ちんこう)が進(すす)んでいる。
Land subsidence is progressing.
粒子(りゅうし)が沈降(ちんこう)するのを待(ま)つ。
Wait for the particles to settle.
地下水(ちかすい)の過剰(かじょう)なくみ上(あ)げにより、広範囲(こうはんい)で地盤(じばん)沈降(ちんこう)が起(お)きた。
Excessive pumping of groundwater caused land subsidence over a wide area.

A compound of 沈(ちん) (sink) and 降(こう) (descend). A technical/formal term used primarily in geology, chemistry, and engineering contexts. The opposite concept is 隆起(りゅうき) (uplift; elevation).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 地盤(じばん)沈降(ちんこう): land subsidence; ground subsidence
  • 沈降(ちんこう)速度(そくど): rate of sinking; settling velocity
  • 沈降(ちんこう)する: to sink; to settle; to subside
  • 沈降(ちんこう)現象(げんしょう): subsidence phenomenon

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 沈下(ちんか): subsidence; sinking — very similar meaning, often interchangeable in geological contexts
  • 隆起(りゅうき): uplift; elevation — the opposite process, where land rises
  • 沈殿(ちんでん): precipitation; sedimentation — specifically the settling of dissolved substances in chemistry
