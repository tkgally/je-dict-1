自動振込(じどうふりこみ)

じどうふりこみ
noun
automatic bank transfer; standing order
1. automatic bank transfer; automatic payment; standing order
A banking service where money is automatically transferred from one account to another on a set schedule. Used for recurring payments like rent, utilities, and subscriptions.
家賃(やちん)は自動振込(じどうふりこみ)にしている。
I have my rent set up as an automatic transfer.
自動振込(じどうふりこみ)の手続(てつづ)きは銀行(ぎんこう)の窓口(まどぐち)でできる。
You can set up automatic transfers at the bank counter.
毎月(まいつき)の支払(しはら)いを自動振込(じどうふりこみ)に変更(へんこう)したら、払(はら)い忘(わす)れがなくなった。
After switching my monthly payments to automatic transfer, I stopped forgetting to pay.

Composed of 自動(じどう) (automatic) + 振込(ふりこみ) (bank transfer). A practical banking term for recurring automatic payments. In Japan, many recurring payments like rent, utility bills, and tuition are paid via 自動振込(じどうふりこみ) or the related 自動引(じどうひ)き落(お)とし (automatic debit).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自動振込(じどうふりこみ)を設定(せってい)する: to set up automatic transfer
  • 自動振込(じどうふりこみ)の手続(てつづ)き: automatic transfer procedures
  • 自動振込(じどうふりこみ)を停止(ていし)する: to cancel automatic transfer
  • 毎月(まいつき)の自動振込(じどうふりこみ): monthly automatic transfer

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自動引(じどうひ)き落(お)とし: automatic debit — money is pulled from your account by the payee; 自動振込(じどうふりこみ) is where you push money to another account
  • 振込(ふりこみ): bank transfer — a one-time manual transfer
  • 口座振替(こうざふりかえ): account transfer — often used interchangeably with 自動引(じどうひ)き落(お)とし for utility payments
