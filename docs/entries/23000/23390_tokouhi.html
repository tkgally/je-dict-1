渡航費(とこうひ)

とこうひ
noun
travel expenses (for overseas travel); airfare and travel costs
1. the money required to travel abroad, especially the cost of international transportation (airfare or ship fare); overseas travel expenses
The cost of traveling overseas, most often used for airfare but also covering related transportation costs such as port charges, fuel surcharges, and taxes. Because 渡航(とこう) specifically means 'crossing overseas,' 渡航費(とこうひ) is used almost exclusively for international travel, not for domestic trips. The word is common in business contexts — for example, when a company reimburses an employee for a trip abroad, or when a scholarship covers a student's airfare to study overseas.
会社(かいしゃ)が渡航費(とこうひ)を負担(ふたん)してくれた。
The company paid for my travel expenses.
奨学金(しょうがくきん)には渡航費(とこうひ)も含(ふく)まれている。
The scholarship also includes travel expenses.
円安(えんやす)で渡航費(とこうひ)が大幅(おおはば)に上(あ)がってしまった。
Because of the weak yen, travel expenses have gone up dramatically.
渡航費(とこうひ)は自己(じこ)負担(ふたん)ですが、現地(げんち)での滞在費(たいざいひ)は主催者側(しゅさいしゃがわ)が支払(しはら)います。
You have to pay your own travel expenses, but the organizers will cover your living costs at the destination.

Composed of 渡航(とこう) (crossing overseas, traveling abroad) + 費(ひ) (expenses, cost). The morpheme 渡(と) means 'to cross' and 航(こう) means 'navigation / voyage,' so the word literally means 'crossing-voyage expenses.' Because of 渡航(とこう)'s specific nuance of going overseas by ship or plane, 渡航費(とこうひ) is normally reserved for international travel.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 渡航費(とこうひ)を負担(ふたん)する: to bear / cover travel expenses
  • 渡航費(とこうひ)を支給(しきゅう)する: to provide travel expenses (as an allowance)
  • 渡航費(とこうひ)自己(じこ)負担(ふたん): travel expenses at one's own cost
  • 渡航費(とこうひ)が高騰(こうとう)する: (for) travel expenses to soar
  • 渡航費(とこうひ)を工面(くめん)する: to scrape together the travel money

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 交通費(こうつうひ): transportation expenses — the general word for travel costs, including commuting and domestic trips; not specifically tied to going abroad.
  • 旅費(りょひ): travel expenses — a broader term covering all costs of a trip (transportation, lodging, meals); used for both domestic and overseas travel.
  • 航空券(こうくうけん)代(だい): airfare — specifically the ticket price, a narrower term than 渡航費(とこうひ).
  • 出張費(しゅっちょうひ): business-trip expenses — used when a company pays for an employee's work trip, whether domestic or international.
  • 滞在費(たいざいひ): living / accommodation expenses during a stay — the usual counterpart, often paid on top of or in place of 渡航費(とこうひ).

REGISTER:
Somewhat formal and written. Common in scholarship announcements, company travel policies, official announcements about overseas events, and news articles about international travel. In casual conversation, Japanese speakers are more likely to say 航空券(こうくうけん)代(だい) or simply ''ticket''.
