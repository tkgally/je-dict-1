あんぐり

あんぐり
adverb
gaping; open-mouthed; agape
1. gaping; open-mouthed; with mouth wide open in surprise or shock
Describes the state of having one's mouth open wide, typically from shock, surprise, or disbelief. A vivid mimetic expression.
あんぐり口(くち)を開(あ)ける。
To gape open-mouthed.
請求書(せいきゅうしょ)の金額(きんがく)を見(み)てあんぐりした。
I was left gaping when I saw the amount on the bill.
彼(かれ)の突拍子(とっぴょうし)もない発言(はつげん)に、全員(ぜんいん)があんぐりと口(くち)を開(あ)けた。
Everyone gaped open-mouthed at his outrageous remark.

A mimetic word that vividly evokes the image of a mouth hanging open in shock. Often used with 口(くち)を開(あ)ける (to open one's mouth) or simply as あんぐりする.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • あんぐりする: to gape, to be left open-mouthed
  • あんぐり口(くち)を開(あ)ける: to gape with mouth wide open
  • あんぐりと口(くち)を開(あ)けた: gaped open-mouthed

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • ぽかんと: blankly — mouth open from being dazed or spacing out; less shock
  • 唖然(あぜん)と: speechless — formal word for being struck dumb with shock
  • 開(あ)いた口(くち)が塞(ふさ)がらない: jaw-dropping (idiom) — so shocked one can't close one's mouth
  • びっくり: surprised — general surprise; not specifically about the mouth
