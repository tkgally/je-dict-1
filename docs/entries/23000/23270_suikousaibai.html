1. hydroponics; hydroponic cultivation
The cultivation of plants in water containing dissolved nutrients, without using soil. Widely used in commercial vegetable farming (especially leafy greens and tomatoes), in school science projects, and in home gardening kits. Can be used as a する verb.
水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)のレタス。
Hydroponically grown lettuce.
家庭(かてい)でも手軽(てがる)に水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)ができるキットが売(う)られている。
Kits that allow you to easily do hydroponic cultivation at home are being sold.
この工場(こうじょう)では、一年中(いちねんじゅう)水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)で新鮮(しんせん)な野菜(やさい)を生産(せいさん)している。
This factory produces fresh vegetables hydroponically all year round.

Composed of 水耕(すいこう) (water cultivation, from 水(みず) "water" and 耕(こう) "cultivate") and 栽培(さいばい) (cultivation, growing plants). Refers to growing plants with their roots suspended in nutrient-enriched water instead of soil — a technique used both in industrial greenhouses (植物工場(しょくぶつこうじょう)) and in small home setups.

USAGE:

  • Typically used as a noun, but can take する to mean "to grow hydroponically": レタスを水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)する (to grow lettuce hydroponically).
  • Commonly combined with names of crops: 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)のトマト (hydroponic tomato), 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)野菜(やさい) (hydroponic vegetables).
  • Frequently seen in contexts of sustainable agriculture, urban farming, and disaster relief (since it doesn't require usable farmland).
  • The opposite — soil-based cultivation — is 土耕(どこう) or more commonly 土(つち)で育(そだ)てる.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)を行(おこな)う: to carry out hydroponic cultivation
  • 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)で育(そだ)てる: to grow by hydroponics
  • 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)キット: a hydroponics kit
  • 水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい)野菜(やさい): hydroponic vegetables
  • 家庭(かてい)水耕栽培(すいこうさいばい): home hydroponics

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 栽培(さいばい): cultivation, growing (plants) — the general term
  • 土耕(どこう): soil cultivation — the contrasting method
  • 養液栽培(ようえきさいばい): nutrient-solution cultivation — a broader technical umbrella term that includes hydroponics
  • 植物工場(しょくぶつこうじょう): plant factory — an industrial facility where hydroponics is commonly used
  • 園芸(えんげい): horticulture, gardening — broader and more hobby-oriented
