1. to admonish; to reprove gently; to scold mildly
To correct or caution someone for improper behavior in a restrained, gentle manner, rather than harshly scolding them.
母(はは)にやんわり窘(たしな)められた。
My mother gently admonished me.
行儀(ぎょうぎ)の悪(わる)い子(こ)どもを窘(たしな)めた。
I reproved the child for bad manners.
上司(じょうし)が失礼(しつれい)な発言(はつげん)をした部下(ぶか)を静(しず)かに窘(たしな)めた。
The boss quietly admonished the subordinate who had made a rude remark.

Implies a calm, measured correction — not a harsh scolding. The person admonishing typically has higher status or authority (parent, teacher, boss) and addresses the issue with restraint and composure.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • やんわり窘(たしな)める: to admonish gently
  • 静(しず)かに窘(たしな)める: to admonish quietly
  • 行儀(ぎょうぎ)を窘(たしな)める: to correct someone's manners
  • 子(こ)どもを窘(たしな)める: to reprove a child

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 叱(しか)る: to scold — stronger and more direct; can involve raising one's voice
  • 注意(ちゅうい)する: to caution, to warn — more neutral; can be used between equals
  • 諭(さと)す: to admonish, to counsel — emphasizes explaining why something is wrong, more didactic
