1. to ponder; to turn over in one's mind; to reflect on
To think about something from many angles, allowing one's thoughts to circle around the topic. It implies unhurried, meditative thinking rather than quick or practical calculation, and is often used for memories, emotions, or big questions.
過去(かこ)を思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす。
To reflect on the past.
彼女(かのじょ)は窓(まど)の外(そと)を眺(なが)めながら、昔(むかし)のことを思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らしていた。
She was gazing out the window, turning over memories of the past in her mind.
人生(じんせい)の意味(いみ)について思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らしていると、なかなか眠(ねむ)ることができなかった。
As I was pondering the meaning of life, I found I couldn't get to sleep.

A compound verb formed from 思(おも)う (to think) and 巡(めぐ)らす (to let something go around, to make something circulate). The image is of thoughts "going around and around" in one's head, which is why the word is best for reflective, unhurried thinking. It has a somewhat literary, thoughtful tone and is more common in writing than in casual conversation.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive. Takes the thing being thought about marked with を: 昔(むかし)のことを思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 昔(むかし)を思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす: to reflect on old times
  • 将来(しょうらい)を思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす: to ponder one's future
  • あれこれ思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす: to turn various things over in one's mind
  • 人生(じんせい)について思(おも)い巡(めぐ)らす: to reflect on life

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 考(かんが)える: to think — the general-purpose word
  • 思(おも)い返(かえ)す: to look back on, to recall
  • 熟考(じゅっこう)する: to consider carefully — more formal, suggests weighing a decision
  • 黙想(もくそう)する: to meditate silently — stronger sense of inner contemplation
