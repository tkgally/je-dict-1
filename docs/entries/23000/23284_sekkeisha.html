設計者(せっけいしゃ)

せっけいしゃ
noun
designer; architect; engineer who designs something
1. designer; architect; (engineering) designer
A person who creates the plans or blueprints for something to be built or made — a building, machine, system, or software. Used for architects, mechanical engineers, software architects, and similar roles.
この橋(はし)の設計者(せっけいしゃ)。
The designer of this bridge.
設計者(せっけいしゃ)と直接(ちょくせつ)話(はな)せる機会(きかい)があった。
I had a chance to speak directly with the designer.
図面(ずめん)には設計者(せっけいしゃ)の名前(なまえ)と日付(ひづけ)が記載(きさい)されている。
The drawing lists the designer's name and the date.
有名(ゆうめい)な設計者(せっけいしゃ)が手(て)がけた美術館(びじゅつかん)が、今月(こんげつ)開館(かいかん)する。
A museum designed by a famous architect opens this month.

The agent noun from 設計(せっけい) (design) + 者(しゃ) (person). Covers a wide range of design fields from architecture to engineering to software.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 〜の設計者(せっけいしゃ): the designer of ~
  • 設計者(せっけいしゃ)の意図(いと): the designer's intent
  • 建物(たてもの)の設計者(せっけいしゃ): the architect of the building
  • 機械(きかい)の設計者(せっけいしゃ): the designer of the machine
  • 設計者(せっけいしゃ)一覧(いちらん): list of designers

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 建築家(けんちくか): architect — specifically a professional architect of buildings
  • デザイナー: designer — a borrowing; common in fashion, graphic design, and product design contexts
  • 技術者(ぎじゅつしゃ): engineer; technician — broader, refers to anyone with technical expertise
  • 考案者(こうあんしゃ): devisor; person who came up with an idea — emphasizes the conceptual invention rather than detailed design work

USAGE:
設計者(せっけいしゃ) is the standard technical and legal term for whoever created the design of something. In everyday conversation about buildings, 建築家(けんちくか) is more common; for clothing or web pages, デザイナー} is typical. {設計者(せっけいしゃ) shines in engineering contexts and on official documents.
