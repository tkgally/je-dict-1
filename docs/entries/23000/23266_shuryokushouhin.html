主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)

しゅりょくしょうひん
noun
flagship product; main product; mainstay product
1. flagship product; main product; mainstay product
A company's central product line — the one that accounts for the largest share of sales, represents the brand, or drives profits. Often contrasted with supplementary or newly launched products.
我(わ)が社(しゃ)の主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)。
Our company's flagship product.
この会社(かいしゃ)は家電(かでん)を主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)としている。
This company's main products are home electronics.
売上(うりあげ)の減少(げんしょう)を受(う)けて、会社(かいしゃ)は主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)の価格(かかく)を見直(みなお)すことにした。
In response to falling sales, the company decided to reconsider the price of its flagship product.

Composed of 主力(しゅりょく) (main strength, chief force) and 商品(しょうひん) (product, merchandise). Refers to the product that represents the core of a company's business — typically the best-seller, the one most associated with the brand, or the main driver of revenue.

USAGE:

  • Frequently appears in business news, annual reports, and press releases.
  • Often used with とする / としている: 家電(かでん)を主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)としている (treats home electronics as its main product).
  • Can also refer to a category of products, not just a single item: たとえば自動車(じどうしゃ)部品(ぶひん)が主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)だ (auto parts are the main product line, for example).
  • Contrasts with 新商品(しんしょうひん) (new product), 看板商品(かんばんしょうひん) (signature product), or サブ商品(さぶしょうひん) (side product).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)を販売(はんばい)する: to sell the flagship product
  • 主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)の売上(うりあげ): sales of the flagship product
  • 主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)となる: to become the flagship product
  • 主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん)を投入(とうにゅう)する: to launch the flagship product
  • 会社(かいしゃ)の主力商品(しゅりょくしょうひん): the company's flagship product

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 看板商品(かんばんしょうひん): signature/landmark product — the most famous product, emphasizing recognition
  • 目玉商品(めだましょうひん): loss-leader / featured item — the product highlighted in an ad or sale
  • 新商品(しんしょうひん): new product — recently released item
  • ヒット商品(ひっとしょうひん): hit product — a commercial success
  • 定番商品(ていばんしょうひん): standard / staple product — a classic, stable seller
