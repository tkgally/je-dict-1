二重(ふたえ)まぶた

ふたえまぶた
noun
double eyelid (eyelid with a crease)
1. double eyelid (eyelid with a crease)
An eyelid that has a visible horizontal fold or crease above the lashes, making the upper eye area look as if it has two layers. The opposite of 一重(ひとえ)まぶた. A central concept in Japanese discussions of eye shape, makeup, and cosmetic surgery.
きれいな二重(ふたえ)まぶた。
Beautiful double eyelids.
二重(ふたえ)まぶたにする整形手術(せいけいしゅじゅつ)を受(う)ける人(ひと)も多(おお)い。
Many people undergo cosmetic surgery to get double eyelids.
朝(あさ)起(お)きると、片方(かたほう)だけ二重(ふたえ)まぶたになっていることがある。
Sometimes when I wake up in the morning, only one of my eyelids has a double-fold.

Composed of 二重(ふたえ) (double layer, two-fold) and まぶた (eyelid). Describes an eyelid with a distinct crease above the lash line. Note that 二重(ふたえ) in this word is read ふたえ — not にじゅう, which would be the more general reading of the same kanji.

USAGE:

  • Used attributively (二重(ふたえ)まぶたの目(め) "eyes with double eyelids") and as a predicate (彼女(かのじょ)は二重(ふたえ)まぶただ "She has double eyelids").
  • In casual conversation, many speakers drop まぶた and just say 二重(ふたえ): きれいな二重(ふたえ) (beautiful double eyelids).
  • Commonly paired with its opposite 一重(ひとえ)まぶた (single eyelid).
  • An unusual in-between category is 奥二重(おくぶたえ) — a crease that is partly hidden behind the upper lid.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Double eyelids are considered a desirable feature in mainstream Japanese beauty culture, and the industry around creating them is enormous: drugstore products like アイプチ (eyelid glue) and アイテープ (eyelid tape), as well as surgical procedures known as 二重(ふたえ)整形(せいけい), are widely advertised. Many adults describe fluctuating eyelid shape — especially having 二重(ふたえ) on swollen mornings or after sleeping poorly.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 二重(ふたえ)まぶたにする: to give oneself double eyelids (via makeup or surgery)
  • 二重(ふたえ)まぶたになる: to become double (naturally or otherwise)
  • 二重(ふたえ)まぶたの線(せん): the crease line of a double eyelid
  • 自然(しぜん)な二重(ふたえ)まぶた: natural-looking double eyelids
  • 二重(ふたえ)まぶたの整形(せいけい): double-eyelid cosmetic surgery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 一重(ひとえ)まぶた: single eyelid — the opposite shape, without a crease
  • 奥二重(おくぶたえ): hidden double eyelid — a crease that is mostly concealed
  • まぶた: eyelid — the base noun
  • アイプチ: eyelid glue — a drugstore product used to create a temporary double eyelid
  • 二重(ふたえ)整形(せいけい): double-eyelid surgery — the cosmetic procedure
