研修会(けんしゅうかい)

けんしゅうかい
noun
training session; workshop; seminar
1. training session; workshop; in-service training; professional development seminar
An organized gathering held for education or professional skill development, typically for employees, teachers, medical staff, or members of a group. Differs from a simple meeting (会議(かいぎ)) in that the primary purpose is learning, not decision-making.
研修会(けんしゅうかい)に参加(さんか)する。
To attend a training session.
来週(らいしゅう)、新人(しんじん)研修会(けんしゅうかい)があります。
Next week there is a training session for new employees.
教員(きょういん)研修会(けんしゅうかい)で、最新(さいしん)の教育(きょういく)方法(ほうほう)について学(まな)んだ。
At the teachers' training session, I learned about the latest teaching methods.
月(つき)に一度(いちど)、社員(しゃいん)向(む)けの研修会(けんしゅうかい)を開催(かいさい)している。
We hold a training session for employees once a month.

A broad term for any organized training event. Very common in corporate, educational, and professional contexts. Literally 研修(けんしゅう) (training) + 会(かい) (gathering).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 研修会(けんしゅうかい)を開(ひら)く: to hold a training session
  • 研修会(けんしゅうかい)を開催(かいさい)する: to hold/organize a training session (more formal)
  • 研修会(けんしゅうかい)に参加(さんか)する: to attend a training session
  • 新人(しんじん)研修会(けんしゅうかい): new employee training
  • 教員(きょういん)研修会(けんしゅうかい): teacher training session
  • 安全(あんぜん)研修会(けんしゅうかい): safety training

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 講習会(こうしゅうかい): course; training course — typically more structured and curriculum-based, often with an instructor teaching specific content
  • セミナー: seminar — a borrowed word; often used for academic or business lectures with discussion
  • ワークショップ: workshop — emphasizes hands-on, participatory learning
  • 勉強会(べんきょうかい): study group — more informal, often peer-led

USAGE:
研修会(けんしゅうかい) is the standard word used in workplace settings. The training can last a few hours, a full day, or several days (a 泊(と)まりがけ研修(けんしゅう)).
