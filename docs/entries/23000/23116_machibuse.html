1. ambush; lying in wait
The act of hiding and waiting to surprise or attack someone. Used in military, criminal, and everyday contexts such as waiting to catch someone off guard.
敵(てき)に待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せされた。
We were ambushed by the enemy.
警察(けいさつ)が犯人(はんにん)を待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せした。
The police lay in wait for the criminal.
帰(かえ)り道(みち)で待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せしていた友人(ゆうじん)に驚(おどろ)かされた。
I was startled by a friend who had been lying in wait for me on the way home.

Composed of 待(ま)ち (waiting) and 伏(ぶ)せ (lying face down, hiding). Literally "waiting while hiding." Can be used as a noun or with する to form a verb (待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せする).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せする: to ambush, to lie in wait
  • 待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せに遭(あ)う: to be ambushed
  • 待(ま)ち伏(ぶ)せ攻撃(こうげき): ambush attack

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 奇襲(きしゅう): surprise attack — a military term emphasizing the sudden, unexpected nature of the attack
  • 伏兵(ふくへい): hidden troops, dark horse — soldiers lying in ambush, or figuratively an unexpected competitor
  • 不意打(ふいう)ち: surprise attack, catching off guard — emphasis on the target being unprepared
