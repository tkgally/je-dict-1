1. to wield power; to exercise authority
To actively use the power or authority one holds, often with a connotation of forceful or overbearing use. Frequently has a negative nuance when describing abuse of power.
独裁者(どくさいしゃ)が権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう。
A dictator wields power.
上司(じょうし)が部下(ぶか)に権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るうのは許(ゆる)されない。
It is not acceptable for a boss to wield power over subordinates.
長年(ながねん)にわたって国(くに)の中枢(ちゅうすう)で権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るってきた政治家(せいじか)が、ついに辞任(じにん)を表明(ひょうめい)した。
The politician who had wielded power at the heart of the government for many years finally announced his resignation.

A set expression using 振(ふ)るう (to wield, to brandish, to exercise). 振(ふ)るう is used in Japanese to describe actively and forcefully bringing something into use — it is also seen in collocations like 腕(うで)を振(ふ)るう (to show off one's skill) and 勇気(ゆうき)を振(ふ)るう (to muster courage). When paired with 権力(けんりょく), it frequently carries a negative connotation, suggesting an overbearing or oppressive use of authority.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to wield power
  • 絶大(ぜつだい)な権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to wield enormous power
  • 弱者(じゃくしゃ)に権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)るう: to wield power over the weak
  • 権力(けんりょく)を振(ふ)りかざす: to brandish one's authority — stronger, more negative nuance

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 権力(けんりょく)を握(にぎ)る: to seize/hold power — emphasizes gaining and holding, not necessarily using
  • 権力(けんりょく)を行使(こうし)する: to exercise power — more neutral, formal; often legal or institutional
  • 支配(しはい)する: to rule, to dominate — broader, focused on the state of control
