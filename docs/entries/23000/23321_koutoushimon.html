口頭試問(こうとうしもん)

こうとうしもん
noun
oral examination; viva voce
1. oral examination; viva voce
An examination conducted through spoken question-and-answer rather than in writing. In Japan, commonly used for university entrance interviews, thesis defenses, and certain certification exams.
口頭試問(こうとうしもん)を受(う)ける。
To take an oral examination.
大学院(だいがくいん)の入試(にゅうし)には口頭試問(こうとうしもん)がある。
The graduate school entrance exam includes an oral examination.
口頭試問(こうとうしもん)で専門(せんもん)分野(ぶんや)について詳(くわ)しく聞(き)かれた。
In the oral examination, I was asked in detail about my field of specialization.
博士(はかせ)論文(ろんぶん)の口頭試問(こうとうしもん)は二時間(にじかん)ほど続(つづ)いた。
The oral examination for my doctoral dissertation lasted about two hours.

Composed of 口頭(こうとう) (oral, verbal) + 試問(しもん) (examination, questioning). A formal term used in academic and testing contexts, distinct from the English loanword インタビュー (interview). 口頭試問(こうとうしもん) emphasizes evaluation through direct questioning, typically by a panel of examiners.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 口頭試問(こうとうしもん)を受(う)ける: to take an oral examination
  • 口頭試問(こうとうしもん)に臨(のぞ)む: to face an oral examination
  • 口頭試問(こうとうしもん)に合格(ごうかく)する: to pass the oral examination
  • 口頭試問(こうとうしもん)の対策(たいさく): preparation for the oral examination
  • 博士(はかせ)論文(ろんぶん)口頭試問(こうとうしもん): doctoral thesis defense

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 面接(めんせつ): interview — broader; used for both job interviews and general academic interviews
  • 試験(しけん): exam, test — general term; can be written or oral
  • 論文審査(ろんぶんしんさ): thesis examination — the formal review of the thesis document, often paired with 口頭試問(こうとうしもん)
  • 口述(こうじゅつ)試験(しけん): oral test — alternative term with the same basic meaning

USAGE:
In the Japanese academic context, 口頭試問(こうとうしもん) is a key step in graduate-school admissions and the granting of advanced degrees, functioning as the Japanese equivalent of a "viva voce" or thesis defense.
