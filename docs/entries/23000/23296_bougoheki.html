防護壁(ぼうごへき)

ぼうごへき
noun
protective wall; barrier; shielding wall
1. protective wall; barrier; shielding wall
A wall or barrier built to protect against danger — for example, against flying debris at a construction site, falling rocks on a highway, radiation in a nuclear facility, or blasts in a military bunker.
防護壁(ぼうごへき)を設置(せっち)する。
To install a protective wall.
工事(こうじ)現場(げんば)の周(まわ)りには防護壁(ぼうごへき)が立(た)てられている。
A protective wall has been put up around the construction site.
原子炉(げんしろ)は厚(あつ)い防護壁(ぼうごへき)に囲(かこ)まれている。
The nuclear reactor is surrounded by thick shielding walls.
落石(らくせき)から道路(どうろ)を守(まも)るために、山(やま)側(がわ)に防護壁(ぼうごへき)が設(もう)けられた。
To protect the road from falling rocks, a protective wall was built on the mountain side.

A physical barrier built to shield people, equipment, or facilities from harm. Composed of 防護(ぼうご) (protection, defense) + 壁(へき) (wall).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 防護壁(ぼうごへき)を設置(せっち)する: to install a protective wall
  • 防護壁(ぼうごへき)を築(きず)く: to build a protective wall
  • 防護壁(ぼうごへき)に囲(かこ)まれる: to be surrounded by a protective wall
  • コンクリート防護壁(ぼうごへき): concrete barrier
  • 放射線(ほうしゃせん)防護壁(ぼうごへき): radiation shielding wall

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 防壁(ぼうへき): barrier; bulwark — more literary; often used figuratively as in "a barrier against ~"
  • バリア: barrier (loanword) — often used in figurative senses (psychological, social barriers)
  • 防音(ぼうおん)壁(かべ): soundproofing wall — specifically for noise
  • 防波堤(ぼうはてい): breakwater — a barrier built in the sea to block waves
  • 遮蔽(しゃへい)壁(かべ): shielding wall — emphasizes blocking line of sight or radiation

USAGE:
防護壁(ぼうごへき) is used both literally (physical walls at construction sites, nuclear plants, or highways) and figuratively in contexts like "emotional barriers" or "a last line of defense." Related figurative uses can be found in news articles about cybersecurity, in which 防護壁(ぼうごへき) may refer to firewalls.
