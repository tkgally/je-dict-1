紀要(きよう)

きよう
noun
(academic) bulletin; university journal; proceedings
1. (academic) bulletin; university journal; proceedings
A scholarly periodical published by a university, research institute, or academic society, collecting research articles by its members. Often includes work that may be more exploratory or local in scope than articles in peer-reviewed international journals.
大学(だいがく)の紀要(きよう)。
A university bulletin.
論文(ろんぶん)を紀要(きよう)に投稿(とうこう)する。
To submit a paper to an academic bulletin.
研究(けんきゅう)の成果(せいか)を所属(しょぞく)大学(だいがく)の紀要(きよう)に掲載(けいさい)してもらうことになった。
It has been decided that the results of my research will be published in the bulletin of my home university.

Composed of 紀(き) (record, chronicle) and 要(よう) (important, essential matter). In modern Japanese academic life, 紀要(きよう) refers specifically to the in-house scholarly publications of a university or institute. They are important venues for early-career researchers and for work reporting on local or Japan-specific topics.

This word is a homophone of 器用(きよう) (dexterous) and 起用(きよう) (to appoint, to recruit). Context — usually "university," "paper," "publish" — makes the meaning clear.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 大学(だいがく)紀要(きよう): a university bulletin
  • 紀要(きよう)論文(ろんぶん): a paper published in a bulletin
  • 紀要(きよう)に掲載(けいさい)する: to publish in a bulletin
  • 紀要(きよう)に投稿(とうこう)する: to submit to a bulletin

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 学会誌(がっかいし): academic society journal — published by a learned society
  • 学術誌(がくじゅつし): academic journal (general term)
  • 論文(ろんぶん): research paper
  • 専門誌(せんもんし): specialized journal/magazine
