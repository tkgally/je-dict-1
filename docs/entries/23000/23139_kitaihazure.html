期待外(きたいはず)れ

きたいはずれ
noun, adjective-no
disappointment; letdown; falling short of expectations
1. disappointment; letdown; something that fails to meet expectations
Describes a situation, result, or thing that does not live up to what was hoped for or expected. Used both as a noun and as a modifier with の.
映画(えいが)は期待外(きたいはず)れだった。
The movie was a disappointment.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が期待外(きたいはず)れで落(お)ち込(こ)んだ。
I was depressed because the exam results were a letdown.
期待外(きたいはず)れの結果(けっか)に終(お)わったが、次(つぎ)に向(む)けて頑張(がんば)りたい。
It ended in a disappointing result, but I want to try harder for next time.

A compound of 期待(きたい) (expectation) and 外(はず)れ (miss, deviation). Expresses the gap between what was anticipated and the actual outcome. Very commonly used in everyday conversation, reviews, and news reporting.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 期待外(きたいはず)れの結果(けっか): disappointing result
  • 期待外(きたいはず)れに終(お)わる: to end in disappointment
  • 完全(かんぜん)に期待外(きたいはず)れ: a complete letdown
  • 期待外(きたいはず)れの展開(てんかい): an unexpected/disappointing development

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 失望(しつぼう): disappointment — more emotional and formal; focuses on the feeling of losing hope
  • 落胆(らくたん): discouragement — more literary; emphasizes the emotional deflation
  • 肩透(かたす)かし: anticlimax — implies being let down after bracing for something bigger

USAGE:
Can function as a predicate with だ (期待外(きたいはず)れだった) or as a noun modifier with の (期待外(きたいはず)れの結果(けっか)). Not used with な.
