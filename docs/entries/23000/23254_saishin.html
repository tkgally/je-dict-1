1. follow-up medical visit; re-examination (by a doctor)
A return visit to a doctor or clinic by a patient who has already been seen for the same condition. Contrasts with 初診(しょしん) (the first visit). It is a key billing term in the Japanese medical system, since re-examinations are charged at a different rate than first visits.
再診(さいしん)の予約(よやく)。
A follow-up appointment.
来週(らいしゅう)、再診(さいしん)のために病院(びょういん)に行(い)きます。
Next week I'll go to the hospital for a follow-up visit.
初診(しょしん)の料金(りょうきん)は再診(さいしん)よりも高(たか)く設定(せってい)されている場合(ばあい)がほとんどだ。
In most cases, the fee for a first visit is set higher than that for a follow-up visit.

Composed of 再(さい) (again, re-) and 診(しん) (medical examination, diagnosis). Used specifically for a second or later visit by the same patient to the same clinic for the same condition. It appears on medical receipts, insurance forms, and hospital signs. Can be used as a verb with する: 再診(さいしん)する (for the doctor to re-examine a patient).

This word is a homophone of 再審(さいしん) (retrial) and 最新(さいしん) (newest). Context — hospital, clinic, appointment — makes the meaning unambiguous.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 再診(さいしん)料(りょう): re-examination fee
  • 再診(さいしん)予約(よやく): follow-up appointment
  • 再診(さいしん)を受(う)ける: to receive a follow-up examination
  • 再診(さいしん)の際(さい): at the time of a follow-up visit

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 初診(しょしん): first visit — the opposite term
  • 診察(しんさつ): medical examination (general)
  • 受診(じゅしん): seeing a doctor, consultation
  • 通院(つういん): going to the hospital/clinic as an outpatient (repeatedly)
