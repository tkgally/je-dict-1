レモン汁(じる)

れもんじる
noun
lemon juice
1. lemon juice
The juice squeezed from lemons, used in cooking, drinks, and as a seasoning. Can refer to both freshly squeezed juice and bottled lemon juice.
レモン汁(じる)をかける。
To drizzle lemon juice on something.
料理(りょうり)にレモン汁(じる)を加(くわ)える。
To add lemon juice to the dish.
りんごの変色(へんしょく)を防(ふせ)ぐために、レモン汁(じる)を少(すこ)し垂(た)らすとよい。
To prevent apples from discoloring, it helps to add a few drops of lemon juice.

A straightforward compound of the loanword レモン (lemon) and 汁(じる) (juice, liquid). In recipes, レモン汁(じる) is one of the most frequently called-for ingredients. The reading じる (not しる) is standard in this compound.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レモン汁(じる)をかける: to sprinkle lemon juice
  • レモン汁(じる)を絞(しぼ)る: to squeeze lemon juice
  • レモン汁(じる)大(おお)さじ1: one tablespoon of lemon juice
  • レモン汁(じる)を加(くわ)える: to add lemon juice

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • レモン果汁(かじゅう): lemon juice — a more formal term, often used on product labels
  • 柑橘類(かんきつるい)の果汁(かじゅう): citrus juice — the broader category
  • 酢(す): vinegar — sometimes used as a substitute for lemon juice in cooking
