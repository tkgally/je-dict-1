1. decoration; an ornamental addition designed to make something look more festive, attractive, or special; the act of adding such decorations
Borrowed from English 'decoration'. Used in Japanese for any added ornamental element placed on cakes, rooms, shop windows, walls, holiday displays, gifts, or personal possessions. Also used as a する-verb (デコレーションする 'to decorate'). It is especially associated with food (デコレーションケーキ 'decorated cake') and with the Japanese subculture of decorating phones, nails, and gadgets with rhinestones and stickers (デコ).
ケーキにデコレーションをした。
I decorated the cake.

The whole family had fun making the room decorations for the birthday party.

That shop's window display changes its decorations with each season, so I look forward to seeing it whenever I pass by.

Borrowed from English 'decoration'. Used as a noun and as a する-verb (デコレーションする 'to decorate'). The shortened form デコ exists as a productive prefix in informal Japanese for decorated objects: デコ弁(べん) ('decorated bento'), デコ電(でん) ('decorated phone'), デコ爪(づめ) or デコネイル ('decorated nails').

USAGE:

The word is most commonly used about three things:

  • decorated cakes and other foods (especially birthday and Christmas cakes)
  • holiday and event decorations for rooms and shops (Christmas, Halloween, weddings, parties)
  • decorative items for personal possessions (phone cases, nails, school supplies)

It is broader than the everyday word 飾(かざ)り ('ornament'), and tends to feel slightly more deliberate or stylish.

GRAMMAR:
Used as a plain noun and as a する-verb. The patterns ケーキにデコレーションをする and ケーキをデコレーションする are both common and roughly interchangeable. The use as a な-modifier of a noun is uncommon — instead, use the noun-noun compound デコレーション + N (e.g. デコレーションケーキ).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • デコレーションをする: to decorate (something)
  • デコレーションする: to decorate (verb form)
  • デコレーションを施(ほどこ)す: to apply decorations (more formal)
  • デコレーションケーキ: a decorated cake (e.g. a birthday cake with cream and fruit)
  • クリスマスのデコレーション: Christmas decorations
  • ハロウィンのデコレーション: Halloween decorations
  • 部屋(へや)のデコレーション: room decorations
  • 壁(かべ)のデコレーション: wall decorations
  • 華(はな)やかなデコレーション: gorgeous decorations
  • 手作(てづく)りのデコレーション: handmade decorations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 飾(かざ)り: decoration, ornament — the everyday native word; broader and more neutral. Less stylistically marked than デコレーション.
  • 飾(かざ)り付(つ)け: decoration (especially the act of decorating a place or object) — the native verbal noun; very close to デコレーション in meaning and usage.
  • 装飾(そうしょく): ornamentation, decoration — a more formal Sino-Japanese word; common in design, architecture, and historical contexts.
  • デコ: deco — the abbreviated, casual form, used as a prefix for decorated items (デコ弁(べん), デコ電(でん), デコ爪(づめ)).
  • デコる: to decorate, to deck out — a colloquial verb derived from デコ; common in youth slang.

CULTURAL NOTE:
A デコレーションケーキ is a key part of Japanese birthday and Christmas celebrations. The classic Japanese Christmas cake is a sponge cake with whipped cream and strawberries. デコ culture — decorating phones, nails, school items, and bento boxes with cute additions — has been a notable part of Japanese youth fashion since the early 2000s.
