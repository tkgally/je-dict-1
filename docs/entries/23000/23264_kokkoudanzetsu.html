国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)

こっこうだんぜつ
noun
severance of diplomatic relations; breaking off diplomatic ties
1. severance of diplomatic relations; breaking off diplomatic ties
The formal act of ending official diplomatic relations between two countries, typically by recalling ambassadors, closing embassies, and ceasing government-level contact. Usually taken as a strong protest in response to serious incidents, acts of aggression, or deep policy disputes.
国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)を宣言(せんげん)する。
To declare the severance of diplomatic relations.
両国(りょうこく)は長年(ながねん)国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)の状態(じょうたい)にある。
The two countries have been in a state of severed diplomatic relations for many years.
事件(じけん)を受(う)けて、政府(せいふ)は相手国(あいてこく)との国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)も辞(じ)さない構(かま)えを見(み)せた。
In response to the incident, the government signaled that it was prepared even to sever diplomatic relations with the other country.

Composed of 国交(こっこう) (diplomatic relations, literally "country contact") and 断絶(だんぜつ) (severance, cutoff). Refers to one of the most serious measures a country can take short of war: formally ending all official diplomatic channels, usually by withdrawing ambassadors and closing embassies.

USAGE:

  • Typically appears in political and news contexts rather than everyday conversation.
  • Often paired with する to function verbally: 国交(こっこう)を断絶(だんぜつ)する (to sever diplomatic relations).
  • Contrasts with lesser measures such as recalling an ambassador for consultations, downgrading relations, or imposing sanctions — 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ) is the most severe diplomatic step.
  • Citizens of the countries involved may lose consular protection after severance, so the term often appears alongside 在留邦人(ざいりゅうほうじん) (Japanese nationals abroad) in news reports.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)を宣言(せんげん)する: to declare the severance of diplomatic relations
  • 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)に踏(ふ)み切(き)る: to go ahead with severing diplomatic relations
  • 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)も辞(じ)さない: to be prepared even to sever ties
  • 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)の危機(きき): a crisis of severed relations
  • 国交断絶(こっこうだんぜつ)状態(じょうたい): a state of severed diplomatic relations

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 国交回復(こっこうかいふく): restoration of diplomatic relations — the opposite action
  • 国交樹立(こっこうじゅりつ): establishing diplomatic relations — setting up ties for the first time
  • 断交(だんこう): severance of relations — a shorter, more formal synonym
  • 経済制裁(けいざいせいさい): economic sanctions — a less drastic alternative
  • 大使(たいし)召還(しょうかん): recall of an ambassador — a step below full severance
