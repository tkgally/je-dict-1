レンタカー

れんたかー
noun
rental car
1. rental car
A car hired temporarily from a rental service, commonly used by travelers and those without personal vehicles.
レンタカーを借(か)りた。
I rented a car.
空港(くうこう)でレンタカーを予約(よやく)した。
I booked a rental car at the airport.
沖縄(おきなわ)旅行(りょこう)ではレンタカーがあると便利(べんり)だ。
Having a rental car is convenient when traveling in Okinawa.

A wasei-eigo (Japanese-coined English) term combining "rent" and "car." In English, "rental car" or "hire car" is used; the Japanese abbreviation レンタカー is the standard term.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • レンタカーを借(か)りる: to rent a car
  • レンタカーを返(かえ)す: to return a rental car
  • レンタカーの予約(よやく): rental car reservation
  • レンタカー会社(がいしゃ): car rental company
  • レンタカー料金(りょうきん): rental car fee

USAGE:
Rental car shops are widespread in Japan, especially near major train stations, airports, and tourist areas. Renting typically requires a valid driver's license (international visitors need an International Driving Permit or a translation of their license). Unlike some countries, Japanese rental cars are returned with a full tank of gas.
