1. replacing the lock (of a door); lock replacement
Removing the existing lock — and usually the old key — from a door and installing a new one. Routine when a key is lost, after a break-in, or between residents when a rental property changes hands.
鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)を頼(たの)む。
To request a lock replacement.
鍵(かぎ)を失(な)くしたので鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
I lost my key, so I need to replace the lock.
入居(にゅうきょ)のときに大家(おおや)さんが鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)をしてくれた。
When I moved in, the landlord replaced the lock for me.
古(ふる)いシリンダー錠(じょう)を防犯(ぼうはん)性能(せいのう)の高(たか)いものに鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)してもらった。
I had the old cylinder lock replaced with one with higher security performance.

Composed of 鍵(かぎ) (key; lock) + 交換(こうかん) (exchange, replacement). In Japanese, 鍵(かぎ) refers both to the key and, loosely, to the lock itself, so 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん) most often means replacing the lock cylinder — not just getting a new key cut.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)を頼(たの)む: to ask to have the lock replaced
  • 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)の費用(ひよう): the cost of a lock replacement
  • 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)の業者(ぎょうしゃ): a locksmith / lock replacement contractor
  • 入居(にゅうきょ)時(じ)の鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん): lock replacement at move-in
  • 防犯(ぼうはん)用(よう)の鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん): lock replacement for security

VERBAL USE:
With する the word acts as a verb: 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん)する — to replace the lock. {してもらう} (to have it done for you) is very common, since most people hire a locksmith rather than doing the work themselves.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 合鍵(あいかぎ)を作(つく)る: to make a duplicate key — cutting a copy of an existing key, not replacing the lock itself.
  • 錠(じょう)前(まえ)交換(こうかん): lock (cylinder) replacement — a more technical synonym; 錠前(じょうまえ) refers unambiguously to the lock mechanism.
  • 鍵(かぎ)修理(しゅうり): lock repair — fixing a faulty lock rather than replacing it.

USAGE:
In Japanese rental contracts, 鍵(かぎ)交換(こうかん) is often charged to the new tenant as part of the move-in fees. News stories about break-ins and landlord-tenant disputes also commonly feature the word. Locksmiths advertise heavily under this keyword.
