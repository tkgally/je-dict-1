産油国(さんゆこく)

さんゆこく
noun
oil-producing country
1. oil-producing country; petroleum-producing nation
A country that extracts crude oil and exports it as a significant part of its economy. Often used in discussions of international economics, energy policy, and geopolitics.
中東(ちゅうとう)の産油国(さんゆこく)。
Oil-producing countries in the Middle East.
産油国(さんゆこく)は石油(せきゆ)の価格(かかく)を巡(めぐ)って対立(たいりつ)している。
Oil-producing countries are at odds over petroleum prices.
サウジアラビアは世界(せかい)有数(ゆうすう)の産油国(さんゆこく)である。
Saudi Arabia is one of the world's leading oil-producing countries.
主要(しゅよう)産油国(さんゆこく)が減産(げんさん)に合意(ごうい)したことで、原油(げんゆ)価格(かかく)が上昇(じょうしょう)した。
After major oil-producing countries agreed to cut production, crude oil prices rose.

A frequently used term in news about energy markets and international politics. Composed of 産油(さんゆ) (oil production) + 国(こく) (country).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 産油国(さんゆこく)機構(きこう): organization of oil-producing countries (i.e., OPEC)
  • 主要(しゅよう)産油国(さんゆこく): major oil-producing countries
  • 世界(せかい)有数(ゆうすう)の産油国(さんゆこく): one of the world's leading oil-producing countries
  • 産油国(さんゆこく)の経済(けいざい): the economies of oil-producing countries
  • 非(ひ)産油国(さんゆこく): non-oil-producing countries

RELATED TERMS:

  • OPEC / 石油(せきゆ)輸出国機構(ゆしゅつこくきこう): Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries
  • 原油(げんゆ): crude oil
  • 石油(せきゆ): petroleum; oil
  • 消費国(しょうひこく): consumer country — the opposite side of the oil trade
  • 埋蔵量(まいぞうりょう): (underground) reserves

USAGE:
Japan is not a 産油国(さんゆこく) and imports almost all its oil, so the word often appears in Japanese news from the perspective of a country affected by producers' decisions.
