1. planting crops; cultivation; crop planning
The act of planting crops in a field, or the plan for what crops to plant in which fields. Used in agriculture and news reporting about farming.
今年(ことし)の作付(さくづ)け面積(めんせき)は減少(げんしょう)した。
This year's planted acreage decreased.
米(こめ)の作付(さくづ)け計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる。
To make a rice planting plan.
被災地(ひさいち)では土壌(どじょう)汚染(おせん)の影響(えいきょう)で、作付(さくづ)けが制限(せいげん)されている地域(ちいき)がある。
In the disaster area, there are regions where crop planting is restricted due to soil contamination.

A compound of 作(さく) (crop, cultivation) and 付(つ)け (attaching, applying — here meaning 'putting into the ground'). This is the standard agricultural term for crop planting and appears frequently in news about Japanese farming, food supply, and agricultural policy.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 作付(さくづ)け面積(めんせき): planted acreage, crop area
  • 作付(さくづ)け計画(けいかく): crop planting plan
  • 作付(さくづ)け制限(せいげん): planting restrictions
  • 作付(さくづ)けする: to plant crops
  • 転作(てんさく)作付(さくづ)け: crop rotation planting

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 栽培(さいばい): cultivation — broader term covering the entire growing process, not just planting
  • 田植(たう)え: rice planting — specifically the act of transplanting rice seedlings into paddies
  • 播種(はしゅ): sowing seeds — more technical, focuses on the seeding itself
  • 農作(のうさく): farming, crop production — the general activity

USAGE:
You will encounter this word in news about agricultural policy, crop yields, and disaster recovery. After natural disasters, 作付(さくづ)け制限(せいげん) (planting restrictions) are often imposed due to contaminated soil or damaged irrigation systems.
