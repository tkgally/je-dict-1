自己資金(じこしきん)

じこしきん
noun
one's own funds; personal capital; equity (as opposed to borrowed money)
1. one's own funds; personal capital; own-source funding
Money that belongs to the person or company using it, as opposed to money borrowed from a bank or other source. Especially common in discussions of home purchases, starting a business, or investment.
自己資金(じこしきん)が足(た)りない。
I don't have enough of my own funds.
自己資金(じこしきん)で家(いえ)を買(か)うのは難(むずか)しい。
Buying a house with one's own funds alone is difficult.
事業(じぎょう)を始(はじ)めるにあたって、ある程度(ていど)の自己資金(じこしきん)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
To start a business, you need a certain amount of personal capital.
住宅(じゅうたく)ローンを組(く)むには、物件(ぶっけん)価格(かかく)の二割(にわり)程度(ていど)の自己資金(じこしきん)を用意(ようい)しておくのが望(のぞ)ましい。
When taking out a home loan, it's advisable to have prepared personal funds equal to about 20% of the property price.

A financial term that distinguishes one's own money from borrowed money. Composed of 自己(じこ) (self) + 資金(しきん) (funds/capital). Commonly used in banking, real estate, and business contexts.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自己資金(じこしきん)が足(た)りる / 足(た)りない: to have (not) enough of one's own funds
  • 自己資金(じこしきん)を用意(ようい)する: to prepare one's own funds
  • 自己資金(じこしきん)で購入(こうにゅう)する: to purchase with one's own funds
  • 自己資金(じこしきん)比率(ひりつ): equity ratio (ratio of own funds to total capital)
  • 自己資金(じこしきん)ゼロ: zero down payment (no own funds)

SIMILAR WORDS / ANTONYMS:

  • 借入金(かりいれきん): borrowed money; loan — the opposite; money borrowed from a bank or other lender
  • 頭金(あたまきん): down payment — the portion of one's own funds used at the start of a large purchase
  • 運転資金(うんてんしきん): working capital — funds used for day-to-day business operations
  • 資本金(しほんきん): capital (of a company) — the registered capital of a corporation

USAGE:
When applying for a home loan or business loan in Japan, banks typically ask how much 自己資金(じこしきん) the applicant can contribute. A higher 自己資金(じこしきん) ratio usually means better loan terms.
