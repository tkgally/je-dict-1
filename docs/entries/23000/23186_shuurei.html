終礼(しゅうれい)

しゅうれい
noun
end-of-day meeting; closing assembly
1. end-of-day meeting; closing assembly; wrap-up meeting
A brief meeting held at the end of a school day or work shift to review the day's events, share information, and make announcements. Paired with the morning meeting.
終礼(しゅうれい)の時間(じかん)だ。
It's time for the end-of-day meeting.
先生(せんせい)が終礼(しゅうれい)で明日(あした)の予定(よてい)を伝(つた)えた。
The teacher communicated tomorrow's schedule at the end-of-day assembly.
工場(こうじょう)では毎日(まいにち)終礼(しゅうれい)で安全(あんぜん)確認(かくにん)を行(おこな)う。
At the factory, a safety check is conducted at the daily end-of-day meeting.

Composed of 終(しゅう) (end) and 礼(れい) (ceremony, greeting). The counterpart of 朝礼(ちょうれい) (morning assembly). In schools, 終礼(しゅうれい) is a brief gathering at the end of the last period where the teacher makes announcements and students report on the day. In workplaces, especially manufacturing and construction, it serves as a daily wrap-up.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 終礼(しゅうれい)の時間(じかん): time for the closing meeting
  • 終礼(しゅうれい)で報告(ほうこく)する: to report at the closing meeting
  • 朝礼(ちょうれい)と終礼(しゅうれい): morning and closing assemblies

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 朝礼(ちょうれい): morning assembly — the corresponding start-of-day meeting
  • 帰(かえ)りの会(かい): closing homeroom — the school-specific term used in many elementary and middle schools
  • ミーティング: meeting — general loanword for any meeting
