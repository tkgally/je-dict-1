麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)

まーぼーなす
noun
mapo eggplant; spicy eggplant stir-fry
1. mapo eggplant; spicy eggplant stir-fry
A Chinese-origin dish of eggplant stir-fried with ground meat in a spicy bean paste sauce. A popular adaptation of mapo tofu, substituting eggplant for tofu.
今日(きょう)の夕飯(ゆうはん)は麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)にしよう。
Let's have mapo eggplant for dinner tonight.
麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)の素(もと)を使(つか)えば簡単(かんたん)に作(つく)れる。
If you use a mapo eggplant sauce mix, it's easy to make.
この店(みせ)の麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)は山椒(さんしょう)が効(き)いていて、本格的(ほんかくてき)な味(あじ)だ。
This restaurant's mapo eggplant has a nice kick of Sichuan pepper and tastes authentic.

A Japanese adaptation of Chinese cuisine. 麻婆(まーぼー) comes from the name of the Sichuan dish, and 茄子(なす) means eggplant. In Japan, this dish typically uses Japanese eggplant (茄子(なす)), which is thinner and more tender than Chinese varieties.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)の素(もと): mapo eggplant sauce mix (a popular convenience product)
  • 麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)丼(どん): mapo eggplant rice bowl
  • 麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす)を作(つく)る: to make mapo eggplant

RELATED DISHES:

  • 麻婆豆腐(まーぼーどうふ): mapo tofu — the original dish; one of the most popular Chinese dishes in Japan
  • 麻婆春雨(まーぼーはるさめ): mapo glass noodles — another Japanese adaptation using bean thread noodles
  • 回鍋肉(ほいこーろー): twice-cooked pork — another popular Chinese-Japanese home cooking staple

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
麻婆茄子(まーぼーなす) is a staple of Japanese home cooking, especially in summer when eggplant is in season. Packaged sauce mixes (素(もと)) from brands like Cook Do make it one of the easiest Chinese-style dishes to prepare at home.
