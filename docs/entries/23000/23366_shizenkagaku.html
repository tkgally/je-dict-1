自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)

しぜんかがく
noun
natural science, the natural sciences
1. natural science, the natural sciences
The collective term for the branches of science that study the physical and biological world: physics, chemistry, biology, earth science, astronomy, and so on. Distinguished in academic contexts from 人文(じんぶん)科学(かがく) (the humanities) and 社会(しゃかい)科学(かがく) (the social sciences).
自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)を専攻(せんこう)する。
To major in natural science.
物理(ぶつり)や化学(かがく)は自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)に含(ふく)まれる。
Physics and chemistry are included in the natural sciences.
自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)の研究(けんきゅう)には実験(じっけん)と観察(かんさつ)が欠(か)かせない。
Experiments and observation are indispensable to research in the natural sciences.
大学(だいがく)では自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)系(けい)の学部(がくぶ)に進(すす)みたいと考(かんが)えている。
At university, I'm thinking of going into a faculty in the natural sciences.

Composed of 自然(しぜん) (nature) + 科学(かがく) (science). The 'sciences of nature' — the empirical disciplines whose object of study is the physical and biological world.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)系(けい): natural-science track (in a school or university)
  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)の研究(けんきゅう): research in the natural sciences
  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)の分野(ぶんや): the field of natural science
  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)者(しゃ): natural scientist
  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)を学(まな)ぶ: to study natural science
  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく)的(てき)方法(ほうほう): the methods of natural science

THE THREE MAJOR CATEGORIES OF SCIENCE (in Japanese academic usage):

  • 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく): natural science — physics, chemistry, biology, etc.
  • 社会(しゃかい)科学(かがく): social science — economics, sociology, political science, etc.
  • 人文(じんぶん)科学(かがく): the humanities — literature, history, philosophy, etc.

BRANCHES OF NATURAL SCIENCE (examples):

  • 物理学(ぶつりがく): physics
  • 化学(かがく): chemistry
  • 生物学(せいぶつがく): biology
  • 天文学(てんもんがく): astronomy
  • 地学(ちがく) / 地球(ちきゅう)科学(かがく): earth / geoscience

REGISTER AND USAGE:
A standard academic term used in education, university brochures, scientific publishing, and any serious discussion of academic disciplines. There is no casual counterpart — in conversation people would say 理系(りけい) ('science track') when choosing between arts and sciences at school.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 理系(りけい): the sciences (informal high-school/college track term). A student on the 理系(りけい) path is headed toward 自然(しぜん)科学(かがく) and engineering.
  • 文系(ぶんけい): the humanities and social sciences (informal track term).
  • 応用(おうよう)科学(かがく): applied science.
  • 基礎(きそ)科学(かがく): basic / pure science.
