不断(ふだん)

ふだん
noun
constant, unceasing, unremitting; indecisive
1. constant, unceasing, unremitting
Continuing without a break; going on without any interruption. Used especially of effort, striving, progress, or change, often in literary or written contexts. Mainly appears as a modifier in set phrases like 不断(ふだん)の努力(どりょく) (unremitting effort).
不断(ふだん)の努力(どりょく)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Unremitting effort is what matters.
彼(かれ)は不断(ふだん)の努力(どりょく)で成功(せいこう)をつかんだ。
He achieved success through ceaseless effort.
社会(しゃかい)は不断(ふだん)に変化(へんか)している。
Society is changing without pause.
2. indecisive, irresolute
Unable to make up one's mind; wavering between choices. This sense appears almost exclusively in the compound 優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん) (indecisive, wishy-washy), where it means that the decision is never cut off or finalized.
彼(かれ)は優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん)でなかなか決断(けつだん)できない。
He is indecisive and has a hard time making up his mind.
優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん)な性格(せいかく)を直(なお)したい。
I want to fix my indecisive personality.
大切(たいせつ)な場面(ばめん)で優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん)になってしまい、チャンスを逃(のが)した。
I became indecisive at a crucial moment and missed the chance.

Composed of 不(ふ) (not) + 断(だん) (cut off, sever). Literally 'not cut off' — meaning something that never breaks or stops. A formal, somewhat literary word; the everyday sense of 'usually / ordinarily' is written with the different kanji 普段(ふだん).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不断(ふだん)の努力(どりょく): unremitting effort (the most common phrase by far)
  • 不断(ふだん)の警戒(けいかい): constant vigilance
  • 不断(ふだん)の改革(かいかく): continuous reform
  • 不断(ふだん)の注意(ちゅうい): unceasing attention
  • 不断(ふだん)に変化(へんか)する: to change without pause
  • 優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん): indecisive, wavering (four-character idiom)

CRITICAL DISTINCTION — two different words:

  • 不断(ふだん): constant, unceasing; indecisive (this entry) — formal, written register.
  • 普段(ふだん): usually, normally; everyday — completely different meaning; the ordinary conversational word. See 00041_fudan.

These are homophones with different kanji and meanings. In writing they can only be told apart by context and by the kanji. Modern dictionaries treat them as distinct words with separate etymologies.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 絶(た)え間(ま)ない: ceaseless, continuous — a more conversational, adjective-like equivalent of sense 1.
  • 持続(じぞく)的(てき): sustained, continuous — neutral and common in current writing.
  • 優柔(ゆうじゅう)不断(ふだん): indecisive — the standard idiom for sense 2; 不断(ふだん) alone is almost never used to mean 'indecisive' on its own.

USAGE:
As a modifier, 不断(ふだん) is normally followed by の and a noun: 不断(ふだん)の努力(どりょく), 不断(ふだん)の注意(ちゅうい). The adverbial form 不断(ふだん)に is possible but sounds quite formal. Outside of set phrases this word is uncommon; learners can safely recognize it rather than actively produce it.
