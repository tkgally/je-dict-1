調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)

ちょうりようぐ
noun
cooking utensils; kitchen tools; cookware
1. cooking utensils; kitchen tools; cookware
Tools and instruments used to prepare and cook food, such as knives, ladles, whisks, spatulas, and peelers. Usually refers to hand tools rather than large appliances.
調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)を揃(そろ)える。
To get a set of cooking utensils.
引(ひ)き出(だ)しに調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)が入(はい)っている。
Cooking utensils are in the drawer.
新(あたら)しい調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)を買(か)ったので、料理(りょうり)が楽(たの)しくなった。
I bought new cooking utensils, which made cooking more enjoyable.
使(つか)い終(お)わった調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)は、すぐに洗(あら)って清潔(せいけつ)に保(たも)ちましょう。
Cooking utensils should be washed right after use to keep them clean.

A general term for tools used in cooking. Literally 調理(ちょうり) (cooking) + 用具(ようぐ) (tools, implements).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)を揃(そろ)える: to get a set of cooking utensils
  • 調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)を洗(あら)う: to wash cooking utensils
  • 調理用具(ちょうりようぐ)一式(いっしき): a full set of cooking utensils
  • 基本(きほん)の調理用具(ちょうりようぐ): basic cooking utensils
  • アウトドア用(よう)調理用具(ちょうりようぐ): outdoor cooking utensils

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 調理器具(ちょうりきぐ): cooking implements — very close in meaning; may imply slightly larger tools including some small appliances
  • 台所(だいどころ)用品(ようひん): kitchen goods — broader, including containers, cleaning items, and consumables
  • キッチン用品(ようひん): kitchen goods — the more modern version of 台所(だいどころ)用品(ようひん)
  • 鍋(なべ)釜(かま): pots and pans (fixed expression)

EXAMPLES OF 調理用具(ちょうりようぐ):

  • 包丁(ほうちょう): kitchen knife
  • まな板(いた): cutting board
  • お玉(たま): ladle
  • フライ返(がえ)し: spatula
  • 泡立(あわだ)て器(き): whisk
  • 菜箸(さいばし): cooking chopsticks
  • 計量(けいりょう)カップ: measuring cup
