トートバッグ

とーとばっぐ
noun
tote bag
1. tote bag
A large, open-topped bag with parallel handles, used for carrying everyday items. From English 'tote bag.'
トートバッグに荷物(にもつ)を入(い)れた。
I put my things in the tote bag.
布(ぬの)のトートバッグを買(か)い物(もの)に使(つか)っている。
I use a cloth tote bag for shopping.
レジ袋(ぶくろ)が有料(ゆうりょう)になってから、トートバッグを持(も)ち歩(ある)く人(ひと)が増(ふ)えた。
Since plastic bags became paid, more people carry tote bags around.

A loanword from English 'tote bag.' In Japan, tote bags became especially popular after the introduction of charges for plastic shopping bags (レジ袋(ぶくろ)有料化(ゆうりょうか)) in 2020. Many people carry a folding tote bag as an eco-bag (エコバッグ) for shopping.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 布(ぬの)のトートバッグ: cloth tote bag
  • 革(かわ)のトートバッグ: leather tote bag
  • トートバッグを持(も)つ: to carry a tote bag
  • 折(お)りたたみトートバッグ: folding tote bag

RELATED TERMS:

  • エコバッグ: eco-bag; reusable shopping bag — often a tote bag used as a plastic bag replacement
  • 手提(てさ)げ袋(ぶくろ): handbag; carry bag — general term for any bag with handles
  • ショルダーバッグ: shoulder bag — worn over the shoulder, unlike a tote
