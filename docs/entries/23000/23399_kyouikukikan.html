教育機関(きょういくきかん)

きょういくきかん
noun
educational institution; school; academic institution
1. an organization established to provide formal education, such as a school, college, university, vocational school, or other accredited teaching body; an educational institution
A formal cover term for any organization whose main purpose is to provide education. It includes elementary schools, junior high and high schools, universities, graduate schools, technical colleges, vocational schools, and special-support schools. The word is common in laws, regulations, statistics, and formal announcements. In everyday conversation Japanese speakers usually refer to specific institutions by type, such as 大学(だいがく), 高校(こうこう), or 専門学校(せんもんがっこう).
この町(まち)には教育機関(きょういくきかん)が多(おお)い。
There are many educational institutions in this town.
教育機関(きょういくきかん)と連携(れんけい)して研究(けんきゅう)を進(すす)める。
We advance the research in cooperation with educational institutions.
高等(こうとう)教育機関(きょういくきかん)に進学(しんがく)する学生(がくせい)が増(ふ)えている。
The number of students advancing to higher educational institutions is increasing.
教育機関(きょういくきかん)は、社会(しゃかい)に必要(ひつよう)な知識(ちしき)と技術(ぎじゅつ)を若(わか)い世代(せだい)に伝(つた)える役割(やくわり)を担(にな)っている。
Educational institutions play the role of passing on to younger generations the knowledge and skills society needs.

Composed of 教育(きょういく) (education) + 機関(きかん) (institution, organization, body). 機関(きかん) is a productive second element that forms the names of many kinds of institutions — e.g., 金融機関(きんゆうきかん) (financial institution), 医療機関(いりょうきかん) (medical institution), 報道機関(ほうどうきかん) (news organization).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 高等(こうとう)教育機関(きょういくきかん): higher educational institution (college, university)
  • 中等(ちゅうとう)教育機関(きょういくきかん): secondary educational institution
  • 教育機関(きょういくきかん)向(む)け: for educational institutions
  • 教育機関(きょういくきかん)と連携(れんけい)する: to collaborate with educational institutions
  • 公的(こうてき)教育機関(きょういくきかん): public educational institution

TYPICAL EXAMPLES:

  • 小学校(しょうがっこう): elementary school
  • 中学校(ちゅうがっこう): junior high school
  • 高等学校(こうとうがっこう) (高校(こうこう)): senior high school
  • 大学(だいがく): university
  • 大学院(だいがくいん): graduate school
  • 専門学校(せんもんがっこう): vocational / technical school
  • 高等(こうとう)専門学校(せんもんがっこう) (高専(こうせん)): technical college
  • 幼稚園(ようちえん): kindergarten (in Japan, a kind of 教育機関(きょういくきかん))

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 学校(がっこう): school — the everyday word, much less formal.
  • 教育施設(きょういくしせつ): educational facility — essentially a synonym, emphasizing physical facilities (buildings, campus) rather than the organization itself.
  • 学園(がくえん): academy, campus — a more literary or brand-like term often used in the names of private schools (~学園(がくえん)).
  • 教育団体(きょういくだんたい): educational body / organization — broader, can include NPOs, research groups, and associations.

REGISTER:
Formal and written. Appears in laws and government statistics, company recruiting documents ('We welcome cooperation from 教育機関(きょういくきかん)'), news reports about education policy, and research papers. Somewhat stiff for conversation, where speakers prefer 学校(がっこう) or the name of a specific school.
