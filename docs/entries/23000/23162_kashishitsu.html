貸室(かししつ)

かししつ
noun
rental room; room for hire
1. rental room; room available for hire
A room that is rented out for temporary use, such as meeting rooms, event spaces, or rooms in commercial buildings available for lease.
貸室(かししつ)あります。
Rooms available for rent.
貸室(かししつ)を会議(かいぎ)に使(つか)う。
To use a rental room for a meeting.
このビルには貸室(かししつ)が三(みっ)つあり、時間(じかん)単位(たんい)で借(か)りられる。
This building has three rental rooms that can be booked by the hour.

Composed of 貸(か)し (lending, renting out) + 室(しつ) (room). Often seen on signs outside buildings advertising available rental space. The 貸(か)し〜 prefix is productive for things available to rent: 貸(か)し部屋(べや), 貸(か)し会議室(かいぎしつ), 貸(か)しスタジオ, etc.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 貸室(かししつ)あり: rooms available for rent (sign language)
  • 貸室(かししつ)を借(か)りる: to rent a room
  • 貸室(かししつ)料金(りょうきん): rental room rate
  • 時間貸(じかんが)し: hourly rental

RELATED 貸(か)し〜 COMPOUNDS:

  • 貸(か)し部屋(べや): rented room (residential)
  • 貸(か)し会議室(かいぎしつ): rental meeting room
  • 貸(か)しスタジオ: rental studio
  • 貸(か)し衣装(いしょう): rental costumes/clothes

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • レンタルスペース: rental space — the modern katakana equivalent, often used for online booking platforms
  • 貸(か)し部屋(へや): rented room — more residential, like a room in a shared house
