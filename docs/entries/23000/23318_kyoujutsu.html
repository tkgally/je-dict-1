1. testimony; statement; deposition (in a legal context)
A formal statement given by a suspect, witness, or defendant to investigators or a court during a criminal investigation or trial. A specialized legal term.
容疑者(ようぎしゃ)が供述(きょうじゅつ)を始(はじ)めた。
The suspect began making a statement.
警察(けいさつ)に事件(じけん)の経緯(けいい)を供述(きょうじゅつ)した。
He gave a statement to the police about the circumstances of the incident.
被告(ひこく)の供述(きょうじゅつ)は事実(じじつ)と食(く)い違(ちが)っていた。
The defendant's testimony was inconsistent with the facts.
彼(かれ)は黙秘権(もくひけん)を行使(こうし)し、一切(いっさい)供述(きょうじゅつ)しなかった。
He exercised his right to remain silent and made no statement at all.

A formal legal term composed of 供(きょう) (offer, provide) + 述(じゅつ) (state, relate). Refers specifically to statements made by suspects, defendants, or witnesses in a criminal investigation or trial. Unlike general words for "statement," 供述(きょうじゅつ) is confined to the legal context and implies an official, on-the-record testimony that may be used as evidence.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 供述(きょうじゅつ)する: to give a statement; to testify
  • 供述(きょうじゅつ)を取(と)る: to take a statement
  • 供述(きょうじゅつ)調書(ちょうしょ): statement record; written statement
  • 供述(きょうじゅつ)内容(ないよう): content of the statement
  • 供述(きょうじゅつ)拒否権(きょひけん): right to refuse to make a statement (right to remain silent)
  • 容疑(ようぎ)を認(みと)める供述(きょうじゅつ): a statement admitting the allegation

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 発言(はつげん): remark; utterance — general term for any spoken statement; not legal
  • 証言(しょうげん): testimony — closely related, but typically refers to statements made by witnesses in court; 供述(きょうじゅつ) includes statements made during police investigation
  • 陳述(ちんじゅつ): declaration; statement — used for formal declarations, often in civil proceedings

USAGE:
供述(きょうじゅつ) is the word used in Japanese news coverage and crime reporting when describing what a suspect has told investigators. It suggests a formal, investigatory context and is rarely used in daily conversation.
