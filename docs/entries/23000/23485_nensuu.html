年数(ねんすう)

ねんすう
noun
number of years; duration in years
1. number of years; duration in years
The count or span of years involved in something, such as a period of service, the age of a building, or the time required to complete a process.
勤続(きんぞく)年数(ねんすう)は十(じゅう)年(ねん)だ。
The number of years of continuous service is ten.
建(た)ってからの年数(ねんすう)が経(た)つほど、修繕(しゅうぜん)が必要(ひつよう)になる。
The more years pass since a building was constructed, the more repairs become necessary.
資格(しかく)を取(と)るには、一定(いってい)の実務(じつむ)年数(ねんすう)が求(もと)められる。
A certain number of years of practical experience is required to obtain the qualification.

A compound of 年(ねん) (year) and 数(すう) (number, count). Used when referring to how many years something has lasted, how long something takes, or the duration of an activity measured in years. Common in formal and administrative contexts such as employment records, real estate, and certification requirements.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 勤続(きんぞく)年数(ねんすう): years of continuous service
  • 経過(けいか)年数(ねんすう): elapsed years
  • 耐用(たいよう)年数(ねんすう): useful life (of equipment or buildings)
  • 年数(ねんすう)が経(た)つ: years pass
  • 年数(ねんすう)を重(かさ)ねる: to accumulate years (of experience)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 年月(ねんげつ): years and months — emphasizes the passage of time rather than a specific count
  • 期間(きかん): period, duration — a more general term not limited to years
  • 年限(ねんげん): fixed number of years, time limit — implies a set term or deadline
