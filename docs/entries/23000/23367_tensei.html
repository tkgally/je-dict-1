天性(てんせい)

てんせい
noun
innate nature; natural disposition; inborn talent
1. a quality one is born with, rather than something acquired through effort or training
A slightly formal word for something that is innate — a natural ability, disposition, or character trait a person has from birth. Often used admiringly of talented people, with the implication that no amount of training could fully substitute for this gift.
天性(てんせい)の才能(さいのう)。
An innate talent.
彼(かれ)には音楽(おんがく)の天性(てんせい)がある。
He has a natural gift for music.
子(こ)どもが好(す)きなのは彼女(かのじょ)の天性(てんせい)で、保育士(ほいくし)の仕事(しごと)がとても合(あ)っている。
Loving children is part of her nature, and the job of nursery teacher suits her very well.
どれだけ練習(れんしゅう)しても、天性(てんせい)のリズム感(かん)を持(も)つ演奏者(えんそうしゃ)にはかなわないと感(かん)じることがある。
Sometimes you feel that no matter how much you practice, you can't match a performer who has an innate sense of rhythm.

Composed of 天(てん) (heaven; nature) + 性(せい) (nature; quality). Literally, "nature given by heaven." Used to describe something inborn — an ability, personality trait, or tendency that one has from birth and that sets a person apart.

TYPICAL PATTERNS:

  • 天性(てんせい)の + noun: an innate / natural X
  • 天性(てんせい)の才能(さいのう): innate talent
  • 天性(てんせい)の明(あか)るさ: a natural cheerfulness
  • 天性(てんせい)のリーダー: a born leader
  • noun + は + 天性(てんせい) + だ: X is part of one's nature
  • 天性(てんせい) + を + verb:
  • 天性(てんせい)を生(い)かす: to make use of one's natural gift
  • 天性(てんせい)を伸(の)ばす: to develop an innate talent

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 生(う)まれつき: by birth — a slightly more conversational word, used as both a noun and an adverb; focuses on the simple fact of being born a certain way.
  • 素質(そしつ): aptitude; raw material — potential that can be developed, not necessarily visible at birth.
  • 才能(さいのう): talent; ability — a more general word; can be either innate or cultivated.
  • 性分(しょうぶん): one's nature; disposition — usually about habits and temperament rather than skills.
  • 本能(ほんのう): instinct — automatic, unlearned behavior shared across a species.

NUANCE:
天性(てんせい) is mildly admiring. Saying someone has 音楽(おんがく)の天性(てんせい) suggests that their musical ability seems almost supernatural — as though granted by heaven rather than earned by practice. It is used almost exclusively in positive or neutral contexts; negative traits are described with 生(う)まれつき or 性分(しょうぶん) instead.

REGISTER:
Written or semi-formal. Common in essays, biographies, sports commentary, and artistic reviews. In casual speech, speakers are more likely to say 生(う)まれつき or 才能(さいのう)がある.
