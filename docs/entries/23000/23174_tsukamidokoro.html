掴(つか)み所(どころ)

つかみどころ
noun
defining characteristic; point to grasp; clue
1. defining characteristic; identifiable quality; point to pin down
A distinctive trait or quality that allows one to understand or characterize something or someone. Most commonly used in the negative form, describing someone or something that is hard to pin down or define.
彼(かれ)は掴(つか)み所(どころ)がない。
He is hard to pin down.
あの人(ひと)の性格(せいかく)は掴(つか)み所(どころ)がなくて困(こま)る。
That person's personality is so elusive, it's frustrating.
この映画(えいが)は掴(つか)み所(どころ)のないストーリーで評価(ひょうか)が分(わ)かれた。
This movie's elusive story divided critical opinion.

Composed of 掴(つか)み (grasping) and 所(どころ) (place, point). Almost always appears in the negative pattern 掴(つか)み所(どころ)がない, describing something or someone that is vague, elusive, or hard to define. Used for people whose personality is unclear or ambiguous, or for abstract things that lack a clear focus.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 掴(つか)み所(どころ)がない: hard to pin down, elusive
  • 掴(つか)み所(どころ)のない人(ひと): an elusive person
  • 掴(つか)み所(どころ)のない話(はなし): a vague, unfocused story

USAGE:
The positive form (掴(つか)み所(どころ)がある) is rare. The word is predominantly encountered in the negative. It can also be written 掴(つか)み所(どころ) as a single unit or in kana as つかみどころ.
