自己(じこ)

じこ
noun
self, oneself, one's own
1. self, oneself, one's own
The self as a conceptual object — a formal word for the person as an individual being or agent. Very common as a prefix in compound nouns (自己(じこ)〜) to mean 'self-' (self-introduction, self-awareness, self-centered, etc.). Rarely used as a standalone noun in everyday conversation.
自己(じこ)紹介(しょうかい)をお願(ねが)いします。
Please introduce yourself.
自己(じこ)責任(せきにん)で参加(さんか)してください。
Please participate at your own responsibility.
彼女(かのじょ)は自己(じこ)主張(しゅちょう)が強(つよ)い人(ひと)だ。
She is a person with a strong sense of self-assertion.
自己(じこ)と他者(たしゃ)の関係(かんけい)について考(かんが)えることは哲学(てつがく)の基本(きほん)問題(もんだい)の一(ひと)つだ。
Thinking about the relationship between the self and others is one of the fundamental problems of philosophy.

A Sino-Japanese word meaning 'self' or 'oneself' in a formal, conceptual sense. Composed of 自(じ) (self, own) + 己(こ) (oneself). On its own it is rarely heard in daily conversation — speakers use 自分(じぶん) instead — but it is extremely productive as the first element of compound nouns, where it corresponds exactly to English 'self-'.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS (as a prefix):

  • 自己(じこ)紹介(しょうかい): self-introduction
  • 自己(じこ)責任(せきにん): personal responsibility; at one's own risk
  • 自己(じこ)主張(しゅちょう): self-assertion; self-expression
  • 自己(じこ)満足(まんぞく): self-satisfaction; self-complacency
  • 自己(じこ)評価(ひょうか): self-evaluation
  • 自己(じこ)管理(かんり): self-management; self-control
  • 自己(じこ)嫌悪(けんお): self-loathing
  • 自己(じこ)中心(ちゅうしん)的(てき): self-centered, egocentric

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 自分(じぶん): oneself — the ordinary, conversational word for 'oneself'; grammatically functions more like a pronoun.
  • 自身(じしん): oneself (emphatic) — often attached after a noun or pronoun to emphasize ('he himself,' 'the country itself').
  • 本人(ほんにん): the person in question; oneself — used to point out the very person being discussed, especially in news, forms, and legal contexts.

USAGE:
自己(じこ) is sometimes pronounced じこ} alone in academic or philosophical prose ('the self'), but this use sounds abstract and scholarly. In everyday speech, choose {自分(じぶん) for 'I' or 'oneself,' and reserve 自己(じこ) for set compounds and formal writing.
