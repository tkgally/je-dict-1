翌年(よくねん)

よくねん
noun
the following year; the next year
1. the following year; the year after a particular reference year
A written, somewhat formal way of saying "the year after" a previously mentioned year or event. Commonly appears in news articles, biographies, timelines, and historical or academic writing. Unlike 来年(らいねん) (next year from now), 翌年(よくねん) is relative to a reference point established in context — usually the year just mentioned.
翌年(よくねん)結婚(けっこん)した。
They got married the following year.
翌年(よくねん)の春(はる)に東京(とうきょう)へ引(ひ)っ越(こ)した。
I moved to Tokyo in the spring of the following year.
彼(かれ)は大学(だいがく)を卒業(そつぎょう)し、翌年(よくねん)には大(おお)きな会社(かいしゃ)に就職(しゅうしょく)した。
He graduated from university and, the following year, took a job at a large company.
地震(じしん)が起(お)きたのは1995年(せんきゅうひゃくきゅうじゅうごねん)で、翌年(よくねん)から本格的(ほんかくてき)な復興(ふっこう)作業(さぎょう)が始(はじ)まった。
The earthquake occurred in 1995, and full-scale reconstruction work began the following year.

A relative time noun meaning "the year after (a previously mentioned year)." The reference year must be established in context — 翌年(よくねん) does not mean "next year from now."

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 翌年(よくねん)の春(はる): the spring of the following year
  • 翌年(よくねん)3月(さんがつ): March of the following year
  • 翌年(よくねん)に入(はい)る: to enter the following year
  • 翌年(よくねん)から: starting the following year
  • 翌年(よくねん)までに: by the following year

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 来年(らいねん): next year — relative to the current year from the speaker's standpoint.
  • 翌年(よくねん): the following year — relative to a year previously mentioned in context.
  • 明年(みょうねん): next year — a more formal, written equivalent of 来年(らいねん), used in letters and announcements.
  • 翌(よく)年度(ねんど): the following fiscal year — used when talking about fiscal or academic years rather than calendar years.

REGISTER:
Written or semi-formal. Common in news, biographies, historical writing, official reports, and narrative descriptions of sequences of events. In casual conversation, Japanese speakers often use 次(つぎ)の年(とし) (the next year) instead.

RELATED TIME NOUNS:

  • 翌日(よくじつ): the following day
  • 翌週(よくしゅう): the following week
  • 翌月(よくげつ): the following month
  • 翌朝(よくあさ): the following morning

These all follow the same pattern: 翌(よく) + time unit = "the following X." They all require a reference point from context.
