すりごま

すりごま
noun
ground sesame; crushed sesame seeds
1. ground sesame seeds; crushed sesame
Sesame seeds that have been ground or crushed in a mortar, releasing their oils and aroma. A staple seasoning in Japanese home cooking.
サラダにすりごまをかける。
Sprinkle ground sesame on the salad.
胡麻(ごま)和(あ)えにはすりごまを使(つか)う。
Ground sesame is used for sesame-dressed vegetables.
すりごまは風味(ふうみ)が良(よ)いので、味噌汁(みそしる)の仕上(しあ)げにも合(あ)う。
Ground sesame has great flavor, so it also works well as a finishing touch for miso soup.

Written as すりごま (hiragana) or 擂(す)り胡麻(ごま) (kanji). Made by grinding 胡麻(ごま) (sesame seeds) in a 擂(す)り鉢(ばち) (mortar). Pre-ground sesame is also sold in supermarkets.

SESAME FORMS IN JAPANESE COOKING:

  • 炒(い)りごま: toasted whole sesame seeds
  • すりごま: ground sesame seeds
  • 練(ね)りごま: sesame paste (tahini-like)
  • ごま油(あぶら): sesame oil

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • すりごまをかける: to sprinkle ground sesame
  • すりごまを混(ま)ぜる: to mix in ground sesame
  • 胡麻(ごま)和(あ)え: sesame-dressed vegetables (made with ground sesame)
  • すりごまで和(あ)える: to dress with ground sesame

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 炒(い)りごま: toasted sesame — whole seeds that have been dry-roasted; crunchy texture
  • 練(ね)りごま: sesame paste — ground further into a smooth paste; used in sauces and dressings
  • ごま: sesame — the general term for sesame seeds
