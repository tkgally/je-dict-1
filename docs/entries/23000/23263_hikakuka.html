1. denuclearization
The process of eliminating nuclear weapons from a country, region, or facility — a central concept in diplomacy, arms control, and peace negotiations. Can be used as a する verb (非核化(ひかくか)する) to mean "to denuclearize."
朝鮮半島(ちょうせんはんとう)の非核化(ひかくか)。
The denuclearization of the Korean Peninsula.
両国(りょうこく)は非核化(ひかくか)に向(む)けて交渉(こうしょう)を続(つづ)けている。
The two countries are continuing negotiations toward denuclearization.
完全(かんぜん)かつ検証可能(けんしょうかのう)な非核化(ひかくか)が国際社会(こくさいしゃかい)の共通(きょうつう)の目標(もくひょう)である。
Complete and verifiable denuclearization is the common goal of the international community.

Composed of the prefix 非(ひ) (non-, un-), 核(かく) (nuclear), and the suffix 化(か) (-ization, the process of becoming). Literally "non-nuclear-ization" — the process of removing or renouncing nuclear weapons. Appears constantly in news about arms control, peace talks, and non-proliferation.

USAGE:

  • Often combined with qualifying adjectives such as 完全(かんぜん)な (complete), 検証可能(けんしょうかのう)な (verifiable), 不可逆的(ふかぎゃくてき)な (irreversible), which together form the slogan CVID (完全(かんぜん)で検証可能(けんしょうかのう)かつ不可逆的(ふかぎゃくてき)な非核化(ひかくか)) frequently used in East Asian diplomacy.
  • Can be used with する as a verb: この地域(ちいき)を非核化(ひかくか)する (to denuclearize this region).
  • Appears in compounds like 非核化交渉(ひかくかこうしょう) (denuclearization talks) and 非核化(ひかくか)プロセス (denuclearization process).

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 非核化(ひかくか)を目指(めざ)す: to aim for denuclearization
  • 非核化(ひかくか)を進(すす)める: to advance denuclearization
  • 非核化(ひかくか)交渉(こうしょう): denuclearization talks
  • 非核化(ひかくか)の実現(じつげん): the realization of denuclearization
  • 朝鮮半島(ちょうせんはんとう)の非核化(ひかくか): the denuclearization of the Korean Peninsula

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 核軍縮(かくぐんしゅく): nuclear disarmament — reducing rather than fully eliminating nuclear arsenals
  • 核廃絶(かくはいぜつ): abolition of nuclear weapons — the complete global elimination of nuclear weapons
  • 非核(ひかく): non-nuclear — used in compounds like 非核三原則(ひかくさんげんそく) (Japan's three non-nuclear principles)
  • 核不拡散(かくふかくさん): nuclear non-proliferation — preventing the spread of nuclear weapons
