季節(きせつ)

きせつ
noun
season (of the year)
1. season (of the year)
One of the four divisions of the year (spring, summer, autumn, winter).
好(す)きな季節(きせつ)は何(なん)ですか。
What's your favorite season?
季節(きせつ)が変(か)わる。
The season changes.
季節(きせつ)の果物(くだもの)
Seasonal fruit

季節(きせつ) refers to the seasons of the year.

The four seasons:

  • 春(はる): spring
  • 夏(なつ): summer
  • 秋(あき): autumn/fall
  • 冬(ふゆ): winter

Common expressions:

  • 季節(きせつ)の変(か)わり目(め): changing of the seasons
  • 季節(きせつ)外(はず)れ: out of season
  • 四季(しき): the four seasons (more literary)

Note: Japan's distinct four seasons are an important cultural concept, often referenced in literature, art, and daily conversation.
