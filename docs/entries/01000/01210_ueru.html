植(う)える

うえる
verb (ichidan)
to plant

Definitions

1. to plant (seeds, trees, flowers)
To put plants, seeds, or trees into the ground so they can grow. The transitive act of cultivation.
2. to implant, to instill
Figuratively, to implant ideas, values, or attitudes in someone's mind.

Examples

庭(にわ)に花(はな)を植(う)えました。
I planted flowers in the garden.
に marks the location, を marks what is planted
春(はる)に野菜(やさい)の種(たね)を植(う)えます。
I plant vegetable seeds in spring.
子供(こども)に良(よ)い習慣(しゅうかん)を植(う)えつける。
Instill good habits in children.
植(う)えつける is a compound meaning 'to implant firmly'

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). No common intransitive pair. Pattern: Xに Yを植(う)える (plant Y in/at X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 植(う)えてある = 'has been planted' (resultative, focus on the state); 植(う)えている = 'is planting' (progressive) or 'has planted.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 木(き)を植(う)える (plant a tree)
  • 花(はな)を植(う)える (plant flowers)
  • 種(たね)を植(う)える (plant seeds)
  • 庭(にわ)に植(う)える (plant in the garden)
  • 田植(たう)え (rice planting—noun form)

COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 植(う)えつける (to implant firmly)
  • 植(う)え替(か)える (to transplant)

HOMOPHONE: Don't confuse with 飢(う)える (to starve), which is pronounced the same but written differently.
