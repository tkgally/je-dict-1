よす

よす
godan verb
to stop, to give up

Definitions

1. to stop, to cease
To discontinue an action.
2. to give up, to quit
To abandon an attempt or habit.

Examples

そんなことはよせ。
Stop that!
Imperative form
酒(さけ)をよした。
I quit drinking.

Notes

よす is colloquial.

USAGE:

  • Often used in imperative: よせ! (Stop it!)
  • Casual/rough register

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • やめる: to stop, quit (more neutral)
  • 止(と)める: to stop (more formal)
