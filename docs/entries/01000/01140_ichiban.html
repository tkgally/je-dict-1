一番(いちばん)

いちばん
adverb, noun
most, best, number one

Definitions

1. most, -est (superlative)
Used before adjectives to form superlatives, meaning 'the most' or '-est'.
2. number one, first, best
The first in order or rank; the best among a group.

Examples

これが一番(いちばん)おいしいです。
This is the most delicious.
Superlative usage
日本(にほん)で一番(いちばん)高(たか)い山(やま)は富士山(ふじさん)です。
The highest mountain in Japan is Mt. Fuji.
健康(けんこう)が一番(いちばん)です。
Health is the most important thing.
Used as noun

Notes

一番(いちばん) is used to create superlatives: 一番(いちばん) + adjective = 'most/~est'. For example: 一番(いちばん)大(おお)きい (biggest), 一番(いちばん)好(す)き (like the most). The kanji literally mean 'one' + 'number/order'. Also used to express 'number one' or 'first' as a noun.
