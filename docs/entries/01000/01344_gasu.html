ガス

がす
noun
gas (fuel); gas (substance)

Definitions

1. gas (fuel)
Natural gas or propane used for cooking and heating.
2. gas (substance)
A gaseous substance in general.

Examples

ガスを止(と)めてください。
Please turn off the gas.
ガス代(だい)が高(たか)い。
The gas bill is expensive.
ガス漏(も)れに気(き)をつけて。
Be careful of gas leaks.

Notes

From English 'gas'.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ガス代(だい): gas bill
  • ガスコンロ: gas stove
  • ガス漏(も)れ: gas leak
  • 天然(てんねん)ガス: natural gas
