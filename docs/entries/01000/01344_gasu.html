ガス

がす
noun
gas (fuel); gas (substance)
1. gas (fuel)
Natural gas or propane used for cooking and heating.
ガスを止(と)めてください。
Please turn off the gas.
ガス代(だい)が高(たか)い。
The gas bill is expensive.
ガス漏(も)れに気(き)をつけて。
Be careful of gas leaks.
2. gas (substance)
A gaseous substance in general.

From English 'gas'. In daily life, usually refers to fuel gas for cooking and heating.

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • ガス代(だい): gas bill
  • ガスコンロ: gas stove/burner
  • ガス漏(も)れ: gas leak
  • 天然(てんねん)ガス: natural gas
  • 都市(とし)ガス: city gas
  • プロパンガス: propane gas

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ガスを止(と)める: to turn off the gas
  • ガスをつける: to turn on the gas
  • ガスが止(と)まっている: the gas is off

NOTE:
For scientific 'gas' (gaseous state), ガス or 気体(きたい) is used.
