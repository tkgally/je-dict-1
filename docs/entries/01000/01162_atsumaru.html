集(あつ)まる

あつまる
verb (godan)
to gather, to assemble
1. to gather, to come together
For people or things to come together in one place. The gathering happens naturally or voluntarily—this is the intransitive form (things gather themselves).
公園(こうえん)に人(ひと)が集(あつ)まっています。
People are gathered in the park.
Use に for the location where gathering occurs
来週(らいしゅう)、友達(ともだち)が家(いえ)に集(あつ)まります。
My friends will gather at my house next week.
2. to be collected, to accumulate
For things like money, information, or materials to be gathered or accumulated in one place.
募金(ぼきん)がたくさん集(あつ)まりました。
A lot of donations were collected.
Subject (donations) gathers—intransitive usage

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 集(あつ)める (transitive, to collect/gather something). Pattern: Xが集(あつ)まる (X gathers).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 集(あつ)まっている = 'are gathered' (state), not 'are in the process of gathering.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 人(ひと)が集(あつ)まる (people gather)
  • お金(おかね)が集(あつ)まる (money is collected)
  • 情報(じょうほう)が集(あつ)まる (information is gathered)
  • 一(ひと)か所(しょ)に集(あつ)まる (gather in one place)

WHEN NOT USED: Don't use for forcibly bringing things together—use 集(あつ)める instead. 集(あつ)まる implies voluntary or natural coming together.
