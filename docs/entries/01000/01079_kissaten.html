喫茶店(きっさてん)

きっさてん
noun
coffee shop
1. coffee shop, café
A traditional Japanese-style café serving coffee, tea, and light meals.
喫茶店(きっさてん)でコーヒーを飲(の)みました。
I had coffee at the café.
この喫茶店(きっさてん)は静(しず)かで落(お)ち着(つ)きます。
This coffee shop is quiet and relaxing.
友達(ともだち)と喫茶店(きっさてん)で待(ま)ち合(あ)わせました。
I met my friend at the coffee shop.
この喫茶店(きっさてん)はナポリタンが有名(ゆうめい)です。
This coffee shop is famous for its Napolitan spaghetti.
昔(むかし)ながらの喫茶店(きっさてん)は最近(さいきん)少(すく)なくなりましたが、若(わか)い人(ひと)の間(あいだ)で人気(にんき)が出(で)ています。
Traditional coffee shops have become fewer recently, but they are gaining popularity among young people.

喫茶店(きっさてん) are traditional Japanese cafés, often with a retro atmosphere. They differ from chain coffee shops like Starbucks (カフェ). Many serve ナポリタン (spaghetti) and other 喫茶店(きっさてん) menu items. They often serve as quiet places to read or work.
