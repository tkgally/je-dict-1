喫茶店(きっさてん)

きっさてん
noun
coffee shop
1. coffee shop, café
A traditional Japanese-style café serving coffee, tea, and light meals.
喫茶店(きっさてん)でコーヒーを飲(の)みました。
I had coffee at the café.
この喫茶店(きっさてん)は静(しず)かで落(お)ち着(つ)きます。
This coffee shop is quiet and relaxing.
友達(ともだち)と喫茶店(きっさてん)で待(ま)ち合(あ)わせました。
I met my friend at the coffee shop.

喫茶店 are traditional Japanese cafés, often with a retro atmosphere. They differ from chain coffee shops like Starbucks (カフェ). Many serve ナポリタン (spaghetti) and other 喫茶店 menu items. They often serve as quiet places to read or work.
