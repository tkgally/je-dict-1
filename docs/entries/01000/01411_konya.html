今夜(こんや)

こんや
noun
tonight, this evening

Definitions

1. tonight, this evening
The night of the current day.

Examples

今夜(こんや)は映画(えいが)を見(み)ます。
I'm going to watch a movie tonight.
今夜(こんや)空(あ)いていますか。
Are you free tonight?

Notes

Related time expressions:\n- 今朝(けさ) - this morning\n- 今日(きょう) - today\n- 今晩(こんばん) - this evening (similar to 今夜(こんや))
