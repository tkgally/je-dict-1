米(こめ)

こめ
noun
rice (uncooked)

Definitions

1. rice (uncooked)
Rice grains before cooking.

Examples

米(こめ)を炊(た)く。
To cook rice.
米(こめ)を買(か)いに行(い)く。
To go buy rice.
日本(にほん)の米(こめ)はおいしい。
Japanese rice is delicious.

Notes

米(こめ) specifically refers to uncooked rice.

Rice-related vocabulary:

  • 米(こめ): uncooked rice
  • ご飯(はん): cooked rice; meal
  • 稲(いね): rice plant
  • 玄米(げんまい): brown rice
  • 白米(はくまい): white rice

Cultural note: Rice is the staple food of Japan and has deep cultural significance. The word for 'meal' (ご飯(はん)) literally means 'cooked rice.'

Counter: 合(ごう) (traditional unit) or キロ
