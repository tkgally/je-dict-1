変(へん)

へん
na-adjective
strange, odd, weird
1. strange, odd, weird
Unusual or unexpected in a way that causes surprise or unease.
変(へん)な音(おと)がする。
There's a strange sound.
このスープは変(へん)な味(あじ)がする。
This soup tastes weird.
何(なに)か変(へん)ですか。
Is something wrong/strange?
最近(さいきん)、彼(かれ)は変(へん)な行動(こうどう)をとるようになった。
Lately, he's started acting strangely.
変(へん)な人(ひと)がついてきた。
A strange person followed me.
変(へん)なにおいがする。
There's a strange smell.
2. wrong, unusual
Not normal or correct; something that seems off.
何(なに)か変(へん)ですか。
Is something wrong/strange?
変(へん)だな、さっきまでここにあったのに。誰(だれ)か持(も)っていったのかな。
That's strange—it was here just a moment ago. I wonder if someone took it.
体調(たいちょう)が変(へん)だから病院(びょういん)に行(い)く。
I feel off, so I'm going to the hospital.
この機械(きかい)、動(うご)きが変(へん)だ。
This machine is acting weird.
天気(てんき)が変(へん)だね。
The weather is strange, isn't it?

変(へん) is a na-adjective meaning strange or odd.

FORMS:

  • 変(へん)な + noun: strange (attributive)
  • 変(へん)だ/です: is strange (predicate)
  • 変(へん)に: strangely (adverb)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 変(へん)な音(おと): strange sound
  • 変(へん)な味(あじ): weird taste
  • 変(へん)だな: That's strange (casual reflection)
  • 何(なに)か変(へん): something is off

RELATED KANJI READINGS:

The kanji 変(へん) also appears in:

  • 変(か)える (to change something)
  • 変(か)わる (to change, intransitive)
  • 変化(へんか) (change, transformation)

TALKING ABOUT PEOPLE:
変(へん)な人(ひと) is not a mild word. It suggests someone unsettling or creepy rather than merely unusual, and saying it to or about someone present is rude. For harmless eccentricity, Japanese uses 変(か)わっている (he's a bit different / one of a kind), which can even be affectionate.

NEARBY WORDS:

  • おかしい: also 'off, not right', and the usual word for a machine or a body not working properly (調子(ちょうし)がおかしい). It additionally means 'funny, amusing', which 変(へん) never does.
  • 奇妙(きみょう): stiffer and more written; describes something curiously strange rather than merely wrong.
  • 不思議(ふしぎ): strange in an interesting, wonder-provoking way. 変(へん) carries unease; 不思議(ふしぎ) carries curiosity.
  • Opposite: 普通(ふつう) (ordinary, normal).

Related Words

Synonym: 奇妙(きみょう) (strange, peculiar)
Related: おかしい (off, not right)
Antonym: 普通(ふつう) (ordinary)
Related: 不思議(ふしぎ) (strange, mysterious)
