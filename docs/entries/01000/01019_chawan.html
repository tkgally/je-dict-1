茶碗(ちゃわん)

ちゃわん
noun
rice bowl
1. rice bowl
A bowl used for eating rice. Each family member typically has their own designated rice bowl.
2. tea cup
Originally and still sometimes used to refer to a cup for drinking tea.
茶碗(ちゃわん)にご飯(はん)をよそってください。
Please serve rice into the bowl.
この茶碗(ちゃわん)は私(わたし)のです。
This rice bowl is mine.
茶碗(ちゃわん)を洗(あら)いました。
I washed the rice bowls.

茶碗 literally means 'tea bowl' (茶 tea + 碗 bowl), but in modern usage it most commonly refers to a rice bowl. In Japanese households, each person often has their own personal rice bowl. お茶碗 is the polite form.
