医学(いがく)

いがく
noun
medical science; medicine (field)
1. medical science, medicine
The academic field and science of medicine.
医学(いがく)を勉強(べんきょう)しています。
I'm studying medicine.
医学部(いがくぶ)に入(はい)りたい。
I want to enter medical school.
医学(いがく)の進歩(しんぽ)
Advances in medical science

医学(いがく) refers to medical science as an academic discipline.

Related compounds:

  • 医学部(いがくぶ): medical school/department
  • 医学生(いがくせい): medical student
  • 医学者(いがくしゃ): medical scientist
  • 医学的(いがくてき): medical (adjective)

Note: 医学(いがく) refers to the field/science of medicine. For 'medicine' as in medication, use 薬(くすり).
