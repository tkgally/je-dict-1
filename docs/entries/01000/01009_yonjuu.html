四十(よんじゅう)

よんじゅう
number
forty

Definitions

1. forty
The number forty. Formed by combining 四(よん) (four) and 十(じゅう) (ten). Note that よん is used instead of し.

Examples

父(ちち)は四十(よんじゅう)歳(さい)です。
My father is forty years old.
四十(よんじゅう)分(ぷん)以内(いない)に終(お)わります。
It will be finished within forty minutes.
四十(よんじゅう)人(にん)が参加(さんか)しました。
Forty people participated.

Notes

For numbers containing four, よん is generally preferred over し because し sounds like 死(し) (death). So forty is typically よんじゅう rather than しじゅう, though both can be heard.
