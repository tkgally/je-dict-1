負(ま)ける

まける
verb (ichidan)
to lose, to be defeated
1. to lose, to be defeated
To fail to win in a game, competition, or conflict.
試合(しあい)に負(ま)けた。
I lost the game.
彼(かれ)は負(ま)けず嫌(ぎら)いだから、いつも最後(さいご)まで頑張(がんば)る。
He hates losing, so he always gives it his all until the end.
負けず嫌い = competitive, hates to lose
相手(あいて)に負(ま)けた。
I lost to my opponent.
選挙(せんきょ)に負(ま)けた。
I lost the election.
じゃんけんで負(ま)けた。
I lost at rock-paper-scissors.
負(ま)けても諦(あきら)めない。
Even if I lose, I won't give up.
2. to give in, to yield
To concede or surrender to pressure or temptation.
誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)けてしまった。
I gave in to temptation.
暑(あつ)さに負(ま)けないで、水分(すいぶん)をしっかり取(と)って、夏(なつ)を乗(の)り越(こ)えよう。
Don't give in to the heat—stay hydrated and get through the summer.
寒(さむ)さに負(ま)けた。
I gave in to the cold.
圧力(あつりょく)に負(ま)けた。
I yielded to pressure.
病気(びょうき)に負(ま)けないで。
Don't give in to the illness.
3. to give a discount
Colloquially, to reduce the price as a favor.
少(すこ)し負(ま)けてくれませんか。
Could you give me a small discount?
Discount meaning—casual
値段(ねだん)を負(ま)けてください。
Please give me a discount.
もう少(すこ)し負(ま)けてよ。
Give me a bit more discount.
千円(せんえん)負(ま)けてあげる。
I'll give you a 1000 yen discount.
常連(じょうれん)のお客(きゃく)さんだから、今回(こんかい)は特別(とくべつ)に五百円(ごひゃくえん)負(ま)けてあげるよ。
Since you're a regular customer, I'll give you a special 500-yen discount this time.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xに負(ま)ける (lose to X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 負(ま)けている = 'is losing' or 'has lost.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試合(しあい)に負(ま)ける (lose a game)
  • 相手(あいて)に負(ま)ける (lose to an opponent)
  • 誘惑(ゆうわく)に負(ま)ける (yield to temptation)
  • 負(ま)けず嫌(ぎら)い (competitive, hates to lose)
  • 負(ま)け犬(いぬ) (loser—lit. 'losing dog')

OPPOSITE: 勝(か)つ (to win)

DISCOUNT USAGE (colloquial):

  • 負(ま)けてよ (Give me a discount!)
  • 負(ま)けてあげる (I'll give you a discount)
