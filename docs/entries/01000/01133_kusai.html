臭(くさ)い

くさい
adjective (i-adjective)
smelly, stinky
1. smelly, stinky, bad-smelling
Having an unpleasant odor.
このゴミは臭(くさ)いですね。
This garbage smells bad, doesn't it?
靴下(くつした)が臭(くさ)い！
The socks stink!
2. suspicious, fishy
In figurative usage, means something seems suspicious or questionable.
あの話(はなし)は臭(くさ)いと思(おも)う。
I think that story is fishy.
Figurative meaning: suspicious

臭い conjugates as an i-adjective: 臭くない (negative), 臭くて (te-form), 臭かった (past). As a suffix ～くさい, it means 'smelling of' or '-ish' (e.g., 汗臭い - smelling of sweat, 嘘くさい - seems like a lie).

Related: 臭い/匂い (におい - smell, odor).
