1. wanted, desired
Used to express that you want something (a noun). The thing wanted is marked with が.
水(みず)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want water.
Object is marked with が, not を
何(なに)が欲(ほ)しいですか。
What do you want?
新(あたら)しい本(ほん)が欲(ほ)しいです。
I want a new book.
子供(こども)のとき、犬(いぬ)が欲(ほ)しかったけど、家(いえ)が小(ちい)さかったから飼(か)えなかった。
When I was a child, I wanted a dog, but I couldn't have one because my house was small.
Past tense: 欲しかった
時間(じかん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want more time.
新(あたら)しい車(くるま)が欲(ほ)しくなった。
I started wanting a new car.
2. to want (someone) to do
In the pattern 「～てほしい」, expresses wanting someone else to do something.
手伝(てつだ)ってほしいんです。
I want you to help me.
～てほしい pattern
早(はや)く来(き)てほしい。
I want you to come quickly.
静(しず)かにしてほしいです。
I want you to be quiet.
もっと努力(どりょく)してほしかった。
I wanted you to try harder.
子供(こども)には好(す)きなことを見(み)つけて、楽(たの)しく生(い)きてほしいと思(おも)っています。
I want my children to find what they love and live happily.

An i-adjective expressing desire. Used for wanting things (nouns), not for wanting to do actions (use ～たい for that).

KEY GRAMMAR:

  • Noun + が + 欲(ほ)しい: I want [noun] (the desired thing takes が, not を)
  • ～て欲(ほ)しい: I want someone to do ~ (the person takes に)

COMMON MISTAKE:
Using を instead of が. The correct pattern is 水(みず)が欲(ほ)しい (not 水(みず)を欲(ほ)しい).

CONTRAST:

  • 欲(ほ)しい: want a thing (noun)
  • ～たい: want to do (verb)

Related Words

Related: たい (want to do (verb))
Related: 欲(ほ)しがる (third person wanting)
Synonym: 望(のぞ)む (to desire (formal))
