欲(ほ)しい

ほしい
adjective (i-adjective)
wanted, desired
1. wanted, desired
Used to express that you want something (a noun). The thing wanted is marked with が.
水(みず)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want water.
Object is marked with が, not を
何(なに)が欲(ほ)しいですか。
What do you want?
新(あたら)しい本(ほん)が欲(ほ)しいです。
I want a new book.
子供(こども)のとき、犬(いぬ)が欲(ほ)しかったけど、家(いえ)が小(ちい)さかったから飼(か)えなかった。
When I was a child, I wanted a dog, but I couldn't have one because my house was small.
Past tense: 欲しかった
時間(じかん)が欲(ほ)しい。
I want more time.
Sense 1: wanted
新(あたら)しい車(くるま)が欲(ほ)しくなった。
I started wanting a new car.
Sense 1: wanted
2. to want (someone) to do
In the pattern 「～てほしい」, expresses wanting someone else to do something.
手伝(てつだ)ってほしいんです。
I want you to help me.
～てほしい pattern
早(はや)く来(き)てほしい。
I want you to come quickly.
Sense 2: want someone to do
静(しず)かにしてほしいです。
I want you to be quiet.
Sense 2: want someone to do
もっと努力(どりょく)してほしかった。
I wanted you to try harder.
Sense 2: wanted someone to do (past)

欲(ほ)しい conjugates as an i-adjective: 欲(ほ)しくない (negative), 欲(ほ)しくて (te-form), 欲(ほ)しかった (past). Important: Use 欲(ほ)しい for wanting things (nouns), but use ～たい for wanting to do actions (verbs). The pattern ～て欲(ほ)しい means you want someone else to do something.
