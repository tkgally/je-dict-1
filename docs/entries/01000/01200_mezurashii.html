珍(めずら)しい

めずらしい
adjective (i-adjective)
unusual; rare

Definitions

1. rare, uncommon
Describes something that is not often seen or encountered.
2. unusual, surprising
Describes an unexpected occurrence, often used when someone acts out of character.
3. novel, curious
Describes something new and interesting that attracts attention.

Examples

これは珍(めずら)しい花(はな)ですね。
This is a rare flower, isn't it?
珍(めずら)しく早起(はやお)きした。
Unusually, I woke up early.
Adverbial form
彼(かれ)が料理(りょうり)するなんて珍(めずら)しい。
It's unusual for him to cook.
Expressing surprise at unexpected behavior
外国(がいこく)の珍(めずら)しいお土産(みやげ)をもらった。
I received an unusual/exotic souvenir from abroad.
Modifier usage

Notes

珍(めずら)しい is an i-adjective meaning 'rare,' 'unusual,' or 'novel.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 珍(めずら)しく
  • Noun form: 珍(めずら)しさ

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 珍(めずら)しくない
  • Te-form: 珍(めずら)しくて
  • Past: 珍(めずら)しかった

Usage pattern: Often used as a predicate to express surprise: 〜するなんて珍(めずら)しい (It's unusual for [someone] to ~).

Note: Unlike English 'rare,' 珍(めずら)しい is not typically used for meat doneness. For steak, use レア.
