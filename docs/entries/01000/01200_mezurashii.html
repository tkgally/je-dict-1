珍(めずら)しい

めずらしい
adjective (i-adjective)
unusual; rare
1. rare, uncommon
Describes something that is not often seen or encountered.
これは珍(めずら)しい花(はな)ですね。
This is a rare flower, isn't it?
この鳥(とり)は日本(にほん)では珍(めずら)しい。
This bird is rare in Japan.
珍(めずら)しい名前(なまえ)ですね。
That's an unusual name.
Sense 1: rare/uncommon
珍(めずら)しい本(ほん)を見(み)つけた。
I found a rare book.
Sense 1: rare
2. unusual, surprising
Describes an unexpected occurrence, often used when someone acts out of character.
珍(めずら)しく早起(はやお)きした。
Unusually, I woke up early.
Adverbial form
彼(かれ)が料理(りょうり)するなんて珍(めずら)しい。
It's unusual for him to cook.
Expressing surprise at unexpected behavior
今日(きょう)は珍(めずら)しく雨(あめ)だ。
It's unusually rainy today.
Sense 2: unusual (adverb form)
珍(めずら)しく遅刻(ちこく)した。
Unusually, I was late.
Sense 2: unusual
お父(とう)さんが料理(りょうり)するのは珍(めずら)しい。
It's unusual for Dad to cook.
Sense 2: surprising
3. novel, curious
Describes something new and interesting that attracts attention.
外国(がいこく)の珍(めずら)しいお土産(みやげ)をもらった。
I received an unusual/exotic souvenir from abroad.
Modifier usage
珍(めずら)しいものを見(み)てみたい。
I want to see something novel.
Sense 3: novel/curious
珍(めずら)しい料理(りょうり)を食(た)べた。
I ate an unusual dish.
Sense 3: novel
珍(めずら)しい体験(たいけん)をした。
I had a novel experience.
Sense 3: novel/unique
子供(こども)たちは珍(めずら)しそうに見(み)ていた。
The children were watching curiously.
Sense 3: curious (adverb form)
珍(めずら)しいデザインの服(ふく)。
Clothes with a novel design.
Sense 3: novel

珍(めずら)しい is an i-adjective meaning 'rare,' 'unusual,' or 'novel.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 珍(めずら)しく
  • Noun form: 珍(めずら)しさ

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 珍(めずら)しくない
  • Te-form: 珍(めずら)しくて
  • Past: 珍(めずら)しかった

Usage pattern: Often used as a predicate to express surprise: 〜するなんて珍(めずら)しい (It's unusual for [someone] to ~).

Note: Unlike English 'rare,' 珍(めずら)しい is not typically used for meat doneness. For steak, use レア.
