鍋(なべ)

なべ
noun
pot, pan
1. pot, pan
A deep cooking vessel with handles, used for boiling, stewing, etc.
大(おお)きい鍋(なべ)でスープを作(つく)ります。
I make soup in a large pot.
鍋(なべ)を火(ひ)にかけて、お湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かしてください。
Put the pot on the stove and boil the water.
鍋(なべ)を洗(あら)ってください。
Please wash the pot.
Sense 1: pot/pan
新(あたら)しい鍋(なべ)を買(か)った。
I bought a new pot.
Sense 1: pot
2. hot pot dish
A type of Japanese dish where ingredients are cooked together in a pot at the table.
今日(きょう)の晩(ばん)ごはんは鍋(なべ)です。
Tonight's dinner is hot pot.
Referring to the dish
冬(ふゆ)は鍋料理(なべりょうり)がおいしいです。
Hot pot dishes are delicious in winter.
家族(かぞく)みんなで鍋(なべ)を囲(かこ)んで食(た)べるのは楽(たの)しいです。
It's fun to sit around the hot pot and eat with the whole family.
今夜(こんや)は鍋(なべ)にしよう。
Let's have hot pot tonight.
Sense 2: hot pot dish
鍋(なべ)パーティーを開(ひら)いた。
I hosted a hot pot party.
Sense 2: hot pot
鍋(なべ)の具(ぐ)を入(い)れてください。
Please add the hot pot ingredients.
Sense 2: hot pot dish

鍋(なべ) refers both to the cooking vessel and to hot pot dishes (鍋料理(なべりょうり)). Popular nabe dishes include すき焼(や)き (sukiyaki), しゃぶしゃぶ (shabu-shabu), and 寄(よ)せ鍋(なべ) (yosenabe). Nabe is a popular winter meal often shared among family or friends.
