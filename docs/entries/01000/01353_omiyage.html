お土産(みやげ)

おみやげ
noun
souvenir; gift (from travels)
1. souvenir, gift from travels
A present bought while traveling to give to friends, family, or colleagues.
京都(きょうと)のお土産(みやげ)を買(か)った。
I bought souvenirs from Kyoto.
お土産(みやげ)に何(なに)がいいですか。
What would be good as a souvenir?
職場(しょくば)へのお土産(みやげ)を選(えら)ぶ。
To choose souvenirs for the workplace.
お土産(みやげ)ありがとうございます。
Thank you for the souvenir.
旅行(りょこう)のお土産(みやげ)を配(くば)った。
I distributed souvenirs from my trip.

お土産(みやげ) refers to gifts brought back from trips.

Cultural note: In Japan, it's customary to bring お土産(みやげ) for coworkers, friends, and family after traveling. This is an important social obligation.

Types:

  • Often local specialties (特産品(とくさんひん)) of the destination
  • Food items are very common (sweets, snacks)
  • Usually individually packaged for easy distribution at work

Related:

  • 名物(めいぶつ): local specialty/famous product
  • お土産(みやげ)屋(や): souvenir shop
  • お土産(みやげ)話(ばなし): stories from one's travels
