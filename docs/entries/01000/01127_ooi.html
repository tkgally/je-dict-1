多(おお)い

おおい
adjective (i-adjective)
many, much

Definitions

1. many, much, numerous
Large in number or amount. The opposite of 少ない (すくない - few). Used for both countable and uncountable things.

Examples

今日(きょう)は人(ひと)が多(おお)いですね。
There are many people today, aren't there?
日本(にほん)は地震(じしん)が多(おお)い。
Japan has many earthquakes.
宿題(しゅくだい)が多(おお)すぎる。
There's too much homework.
Using ～すぎる (too much)

Notes

多(おお)い conjugates as an i-adjective: 多(おお)くない (negative), 多(おお)くて (te-form), 多(おお)かった (past). Important: 多(おお)い usually cannot directly modify nouns (× 多(おお)い人(ひと)). Instead, say 人(ひと)が多(おお)い or use たくさんの. However, 多(おお)くの (おおくの - many) can modify nouns: 多(おお)くの人(ひと) (many people).
