向(む)かう

むかう
verb (godan)
to face, to head toward
1. to face, to turn toward
To position oneself facing a particular direction or thing.
窓(まど)に向(む)かって座(すわ)る。
Sit facing the window.
2. to head toward, to go toward
To proceed in the direction of a destination.
空港(くうこう)に向(む)かっている。
I'm heading to the airport.
未来(みらい)に向(む)かって進(すす)む。
Move forward toward the future.
Figurative usage

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pattern: Xに向(む)かう (face toward X / head toward X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing movement or state. 向(む)かっている = 'is heading toward' or 'is facing.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xに向(む)かう (head toward X, face X)
  • 向(む)かい合(あ)う (face each other)
  • 向(む)かって右(みぎ) (on your right as you face it)
  • 向(む)かい側(がわ) (opposite side)
  • 未来(みらい)に向(む)かう (face the future)

RELATED:

  • 向(む)く: to turn, to face (more general)
  • 向(む)ける: to turn something toward (transitive)
  • 向(む)かい: opposite, facing (noun)
