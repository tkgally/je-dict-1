技術(ぎじゅつ)

ぎじゅつ
noun
technique, technology, skill
1. technique, skill
A method or ability acquired through practice.
サッカーの技術(ぎじゅつ)を磨(みが)きたい。
I want to improve my soccer skills.
技術(ぎじゅつ)が必要(ひつよう)な仕事(しごと)です。
It's a job that requires skill.
2. technology
Scientific knowledge applied for practical purposes.
日本(にほん)は技術(ぎじゅつ)が進(すす)んでいる。
Japan has advanced technology.

技術(ぎじゅつ) has two main meanings: practical skill/technique and technology.

TWO MEANINGS:
1. Skill/technique: サッカーの技術(ぎじゅつ) (soccer skills)
2. Technology: 最新(さいしん)技術(ぎじゅつ) (latest technology)

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 技術者(ぎじゅつしゃ): engineer, technician
  • 技術的(ぎじゅつてき): technical
  • 技術{革新(かくしん)|ぎじゅつかくしん}: technological innovation
  • 高(こう)技術(ぎじゅつ): high technology

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を身(み)につける: to acquire a skill
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)を磨(みが)く: to polish one's skills
  • 技術(ぎじゅつ)が高(たか)い: highly skilled

RELATED:

  • 技(わざ): technique, skill (more artistic/martial arts)
