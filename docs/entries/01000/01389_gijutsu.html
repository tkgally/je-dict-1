技術(ぎじゅつ)

ぎじゅつ
noun
technique, technology, skill
1. technique, skill
A method or ability acquired through practice.
サッカーの技術(ぎじゅつ)を磨(みが)きたい。
I want to improve my soccer skills.
2. technology
Scientific knowledge applied for practical purposes.
日本(にほん)は技術(ぎじゅつ)が進(すす)んでいる。
Japan has advanced technology.

Can refer to both practical skills (技術(ぎじゅつ)を身(み)につける - to acquire a skill) and technology (技術(ぎじゅつ)革新(かくしん) - technological innovation).
