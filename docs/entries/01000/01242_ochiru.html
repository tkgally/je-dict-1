落(お)ちる

おちる
verb (ichidan)
to fall, to drop
1. to fall, to drop
For something to move downward due to gravity. The intransitive form—the object falls by itself.
葉(は)っぱが木(き)から落(お)ちた。
The leaves fell from the tree.
から marks the origin point of falling
日(ひ)が落(お)ちる前(まえ)に家(いえ)に帰(かえ)りたいと思(おも)っているから、もう行(い)こう。
I want to get home before the sun sets, so let's go now.
日(ひ)が落(お)ちる = the sun sets
階段(かいだん)から落(お)ちて足(あし)を怪我(けが)してしまったので、今日(きょう)は学校(がっこう)を休(やす)むことにした。
I fell down the stairs and hurt my leg, so I decided to stay home from school today.
2. to fail (an exam)
To not pass an examination or test.
会社(かいしゃ)に落(お)ちてしまった。
I didn't get the job.
に marks what you failed to get into; 落(お)ちる = fail (selection/application)
3. to come off, to be removed
For stains, dirt, or attachments to come off or be removed from a surface.
洗(あら)っても汚(よご)れが落(お)ちなくて困(こま)った。
The dirt wouldn't come off even after washing, so I was stuck.
汚(よご)れが落(お)ちる = dirt comes off

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 落(お)とす (transitive, to drop something). Pattern: Xが落(お)ちる (X falls).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 落(お)ちている = 'has fallen' (is lying on the ground) or 'is off' (for stains).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 木(き)から落(お)ちる (fall from a tree)
  • 床(ゆか)に落(お)ちる (fall onto the floor)
  • 試験(しけん)に落(お)ちる (fail an exam)
  • 汚(よご)れが落(お)ちる (dirt comes off)
  • 雷(かみなり)が落(お)ちる (lightning strikes—lit. 'falls')
  • 日(ひ)が落(お)ちる (the sun sets)

NOTE ON KANJI: The vocabulary list shows 落(お)る but the standard form is 落(お)ちる with the ち included.

IDIOMS:

  • 落(お)ち着(つ)く (to calm down—compound verb)
  • 気(き)が落(お)ちる (to feel down)

REGISTER: Neutral.
