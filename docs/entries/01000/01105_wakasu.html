沸(わ)かす

わかす
verb (godan)
to boil, to heat

Definitions

1. to boil water, to heat
To heat water or another liquid until it boils.
2. to draw a bath
To prepare bathwater by heating it.

Examples

お湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす。
Boil water.
お風呂(ふろ)を沸(わ)かしておいて。
Please draw the bath.
Common request
やかんで水(みず)を沸(わ)かした。
I boiled water in the kettle.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 沸(わ)く (intransitive, to boil). Pattern: Xを沸(わ)かす (boil X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 沸(わ)かしている = 'is boiling/heating.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • お湯(ゆ)を沸(わ)かす (boil water)
  • お風呂(ふろ)を沸(わ)かす (draw a bath)
  • やかんで沸(わ)かす (boil in a kettle)
  • 沸(わ)かしておく (boil in advance)

CONTRAST:

  • 沸(わ)かす (transitive): boil X
  • 沸(わ)く (intransitive): X boils

USAGE NOTE: お風呂(ふろ)を沸(わ)かす refers to heating bathwater, traditionally done manually. Modern usage may still use this even with automatic bath systems.
