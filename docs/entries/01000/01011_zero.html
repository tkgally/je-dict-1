ゼロ

ぜろ
number
zero

Definitions

1. zero
The number zero. This loanword from English is commonly used in casual speech and everyday situations.

Examples

点数(てんすう)はゼロだった。
The score was zero.
ゼロから始(はじ)めましょう。
Let's start from zero.
Common expression
気温(きおん)がゼロ度(ど)以下(いか)になりました。
The temperature dropped below zero degrees.

Notes

ゼロ is borrowed from English and is the most common way to say 'zero' in everyday Japanese conversation. The native Japanese word is 零(れい), which is more formal. In expressions like 'start from scratch,' ゼロから is commonly used.
