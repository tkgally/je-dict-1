気分(きぶん)

きぶん
noun
feeling; mood
1. feeling, mood
One's emotional or mental state.
2. physical feeling, condition
How one feels physically, especially regarding nausea or comfort.
今日(きょう)は気分(きぶん)がいい。
I'm in a good mood today.
気分(きぶん)が悪(わる)い。
I feel sick. / I'm in a bad mood.
Can be physical or emotional
気分(きぶん)転換(てんかん)に散歩(さんぽ)する。
To take a walk for a change of mood.

気分(きぶん) means 'mood' or 'feeling.'

Common expressions:

  • 気分(きぶん)がいい: to feel good, be in a good mood
  • 気分(きぶん)が悪(わる)い: to feel bad (physically nauseous or emotionally)
  • 気分(きぶん)転換(てんかん): change of pace, refreshing break
  • 気分(きぶん)次第|しだい}: depending on one's mood

気分(きぶん) vs. 気持(きも)ち:

  • 気分(きぶん): overall mood, how you generally feel
  • 気持(きも)ち: specific feeling/emotion, often deeper
