揺(ゆ)れる

ゆれる
verb (ichidan)
to shake, to sway

Definitions

1. to shake, to tremble
To move back and forth or up and down repeatedly, especially during an earthquake.
2. to sway, to swing
To move gently from side to side, like branches or a hanging object.
3. to waver, to be unsettled
Figuratively, for one's heart or feelings to be unstable.

Examples

地震(じしん)で建物(たてもの)が揺(ゆ)れた。
The building shook in the earthquake.
木(き)の葉(は)が風(かぜ)で揺(ゆ)れている。
The leaves are swaying in the wind.
心(こころ)が揺(ゆ)れる。
My heart wavers.
Figurative usage

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 揺(ゆ)らす (transitive, to shake something). Pattern: Xが揺(ゆ)れる (X shakes).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state. 揺(ゆ)れている = 'is shaking/swaying.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 地震(じしん)で揺(ゆ)れる (shake from earthquake)
  • 風(かぜ)で揺(ゆ)れる (sway in the wind)
  • 心(こころ)が揺(ゆ)れる (heart wavers)
  • ゆらゆら揺(ゆ)れる (sway gently)
  • 大(おお)きく揺(ゆ)れる (shake greatly)

CONTRAST:

  • 揺(ゆ)れる (intransitive): X shakes
  • 揺(ゆ)らす (transitive): shake X

ONOMATOPOEIA:

  • ゆらゆら (swaying gently)
  • グラグラ (shaking unsteadily)

Related Words
