製品(せいひん)

せいひん
noun
product, manufactured goods
1. product, manufactured goods
An article or item that has been manufactured.
この会社(かいしゃ)の製品(せいひん)は品質(ひんしつ)が良(い)い。
This company's products are of good quality.
新製品(しんせいひん)を発売(はつばい)した。
They released a new product.
製品(せいひん)の説明(せつめい)を読(よ)んだ。
I read the product description.

製品(せいひん) refers to items that have been manufactured or produced, emphasizing the production process rather than commercial sale.

CONTRAST:

  • 製品(せいひん): manufactured product (emphasis on production)
  • 商品(しょうひん): merchandise (emphasis on being for sale)
  • 品物(しなもの): goods, article (general term)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 新製品(しんせいひん): new product
  • 製品(せいひん)開発(かいはつ): product development
  • 製品(せいひん)管理(かんり): product management
  • 乳製品(にゅうせいひん): dairy products
  • 工業(こうぎょう)製品(せいひん): industrial products

USAGE:
Often used in business and industrial contexts. When discussing items in stores, 商品(しょうひん) is more natural.
