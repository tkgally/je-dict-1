製品(せいひん)

せいひん
noun
product, manufactured goods
1. product, manufactured goods
An article or item that has been manufactured.
この会社(かいしゃ)の製品(せいひん)は品質(ひんしつ)が良(い)い。
This company's products are of good quality.
新製品(しんせいひん)を発売(はつばい)した。
They released a new product.

Emphasizes manufactured items. Compare with 商品(しょうひん) (goods for sale) which focuses on the commercial aspect.

general
