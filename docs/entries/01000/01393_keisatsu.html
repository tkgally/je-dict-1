警察(けいさつ)

けいさつ
noun
police
1. police, police station
The civil force responsible for maintaining public order.
警察(けいさつ)に届(とど)けを出(だ)した。
I filed a report with the police.
警察(けいさつ)が事故(じこ)を調(しら)べている。
The police are investigating the accident.
警察(けいさつ)を呼(よ)んでください。
Please call the police.
財布(さいふ)を盗(ぬす)まれて、警察(けいさつ)に行(い)った。
My wallet was stolen, so I went to the police.
警察(けいさつ)が犯人(はんにん)を捕(つか)まえた。
The police caught the criminal.

警察(けいさつ) refers to the police force or, by extension, the police station.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 警察官(けいさつかん): police officer
  • 警察署(けいさつしょ): police station
  • 交番(こうばん): police box (neighborhood mini-station)
  • おまわりさん: police officer (casual/friendly)
  • 刑事(けいじ): detective

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 警察(けいさつ)を呼(よ)ぶ: to call the police
  • 警察(けいさつ)に届(とど)ける: to report to the police
  • 警察(けいさつ)に届(とど)けを出(だ)す: to file a report
  • 警察(けいさつ)に捕(つか)まる: to be caught by police

EMERGENCY:
Dial 110 for police in Japan (百十番(ひゃくとおばん)).
