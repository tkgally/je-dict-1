布(ぬの)

ぬの
noun
cloth, fabric

Definitions

1. cloth, fabric, textile
Woven or felted material used for making clothes or other items.

Examples

布(ぬの)を買(か)ってカーテン(かーてん)を作(つく)った。
I bought fabric and made curtains.
この布(ぬの)は柔(やわ)らかい。
This cloth is soft.

Notes

General term for fabric. Related: 布団(ふとん) (futon), 雑巾(ぞうきん) (cleaning rag).
