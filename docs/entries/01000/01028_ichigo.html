いちご

いちご
noun
strawberry
1. strawberry
A red, sweet fruit with seeds on its surface. Popular in Japan, especially in desserts.
いちごが大好(だいす)きです。
I love strawberries.
いちごを使(つか)ったケーキを作(つく)りました。
I made a cake using strawberries.
春(はる)はいちご狩(が)りに行(い)きます。
In spring, I go strawberry picking.

いちご (also written 苺(いちご)) is very popular in Japan and appears in many desserts like ショートケーキ (strawberry shortcake). いちご狩(が)り (strawberry picking) is a popular spring activity. Japanese strawberries are known for being large, sweet, and expensive.
