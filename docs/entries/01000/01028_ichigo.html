いちご

いちご
noun
strawberry
1. strawberry
A red, sweet fruit with seeds on its surface. Popular in Japan, especially in desserts.
いちごが好(す)き。
I like strawberries.
いちごが大好(だいす)きです。
I love strawberries.
いちごを使(つか)ったケーキを作(つく)りました。
I made a cake using strawberries.
春(はる)はいちご狩(が)りに行(い)きます。
In spring, I go strawberry picking.
日本(にほん)のいちごは大(おお)きくて甘(あま)いので、海外(かいがい)でも人気(にんき)があります。
Japanese strawberries are large and sweet, so they're popular overseas too.

いちご is the common word for strawberry. Usually written in hiragana; kanji 苺(いちご) is less common.

POPULAR USES:

  • いちごケーキ: strawberry cake
  • ショートケーキ: strawberry shortcake
  • いちご大福(だいふく): strawberry mochi
  • いちごジャム: strawberry jam
  • いちごミルク: strawberry milk

SEASONAL ACTIVITY:

  • いちご狩(が)り: strawberry picking (popular in spring, Dec-May)

JAPANESE STRAWBERRIES:
Japan produces premium strawberry varieties (あまおう, とちおとめ) known for being large, sweet, and expensive. They're popular as gifts.

EXPRESSIONS:

  • いちごが好(す)き: I like strawberries
  • いちごを食(た)べる: to eat strawberries
