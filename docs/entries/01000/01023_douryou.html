同僚(どうりょう)

どうりょう
noun
colleague
1. colleague, coworker
A person one works with at the same company or organization.
同僚(どうりょう)に聞(き)く。
To ask a colleague.
同僚(どうりょう)と昼(ひる)ごはんを食(た)べました。
I had lunch with my colleague.
同僚(どうりょう)はみんな親切(しんせつ)です。
All my colleagues are kind.
新(あたら)しい同僚(どうりょう)が入(はい)ってきました。
A new colleague joined us.
同僚(どうりょう)と仕事(しごと)の後(あと)で飲(の)みに行(い)くことがありますが、毎週(まいしゅう)ではありません。
I sometimes go drinking with colleagues after work, but not every week.

同僚(どうりょう) refers to coworkers at a similar level in the same organization.

ETYMOLOGY:
同(どう) (same) + 僚(りょう) (companion)

SCOPE:

同僚(どうりょう) specifically means peers or equals at work. It does not include:

  • 上司(じょうし): superiors, bosses
  • 部下(ぶか): subordinates

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 同僚(どうりょう)と働(はたら)く: to work with colleagues
  • 元(もと)同僚(どうりょう): former colleague
  • 同僚(どうりょう)関係(かんけい): colleague relationship

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 同僚(どうりょう): colleague (same organization, peer level)
  • 仲間(なかま): companion, member of a group
  • 同期(どうき): someone who joined at the same time
