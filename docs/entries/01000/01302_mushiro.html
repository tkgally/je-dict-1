むしろ

むしろ
adverb
rather, instead

Definitions

1. rather, instead
Indicating preference of one option over another.

Examples

むしろ歩(ある)いたほうがいい。
It's better to walk instead.
むしろ嬉(うれ)しい。
I'm rather happy (about it).

Notes

むしろ compares alternatives.

PATTERN:

  • AよりむしろB: B rather than A

SIMILAR:

  • 却(かえ)って: on the contrary
  • 逆(ぎゃく)に: conversely
