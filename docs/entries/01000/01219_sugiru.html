1. to pass, to go by
For time or a point to pass by.
12時(じ)を過(す)ぎた。
It's past 12 o'clock.
Time passing
時間(じかん)が過(す)ぎるのは早(はや)い。
Time passes quickly.
夏(なつ)が過(す)ぎた。
Summer has passed.
期限(きげん)が過(す)ぎた。
The deadline has passed.
嵐(あらし)が過(す)ぎ去(さ)った。
The storm has passed.
2. to exceed, to go beyond
To go past a limit or boundary.
駅(えき)を過(す)ぎてから気(き)がついた。
I noticed after I had passed the station.
Main verb usage: pass by a place
制限(せいげん)速度(そくど)を過(す)ぎた。
I exceeded the speed limit.
予算(よさん)を過(す)ぎてしまった。
I went over budget.
限度(げんど)を過(す)ぎる。
To exceed the limit.
定員(ていいん)を過(す)ぎたので入(はい)れなかった。
We couldn't get in because it exceeded capacity.
3. too much (auxiliary verb)
Attached to verb stems or adjective stems to mean 'too much' or 'excessively.'
食(た)べ過(す)ぎた。
I ate too much.
Auxiliary usage: verb stem + すぎる
この問題(もんだい)は難(むずか)し過(す)ぎる。
This problem is too difficult.
Auxiliary usage with adjective
飲(の)み過(す)ぎた。
I drank too much.
高(たか)過(す)ぎて買(か)えない。
It's too expensive to buy.
言(い)い過(す)ぎた。
I said too much.
量(りょう)が多(おお)過(す)ぎる。
The quantity is too much.

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive) as a main verb. Its transitive partner is 過(す)ごす (to spend time). Compare 時間(じかん)が過(す)ぎる (time passes, by itself) with 時間(じかん)を過(す)ごす (someone spends the time). Note that 過(す)ぎる itself takes を in the sense 'pass by a place' (駅(えき)を過(す)ぎる) — that を marks a route, not an object, so the verb is still intransitive.

ASPECT: ている gives a resulting state. 過(す)ぎている = 'has already passed', as of a deadline or an hour.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 時間(じかん)が過(す)ぎる: time passes
  • 駅(えき)を過(す)ぎる: go past a station
  • 期限(きげん)が過(す)ぎる: a deadline passes
  • 〜過(す)ぎる: the auxiliary use, below

AUXILIARY USE (very common):

Attached to a stem, 過(す)ぎる means 'too much'. It is normally written in hiragana as すぎる in this use, and it conjugates as an ordinary ichidan verb (すぎた, すぎて, すぎない).

  • Verbs — masu-stem: 食(た)べすぎる (eat too much), 飲(の)みすぎる (drink too much)
  • I-adjectives — drop the い: 高(たか)すぎる (too expensive), 大(おお)きすぎる (too big)
  • Na-adjectives — attach to the stem: 静(しず)かすぎる (too quiet)
  • Two irregulars worth memorising: いい becomes よすぎる, and ない becomes なさすぎる

The auxiliary almost always carries criticism or worry — 'too much' in the sense of a problem, not simply 'very'. 高(たか)すぎる says the price is unacceptable, not merely high.

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 経(た)つ: also 'time passes', but only of elapsed duration, never of passing a place or exceeding a limit
  • 通(とお)り過(す)ぎる: pass by and keep going, with the passing itself in focus

Related Words

Pair verb: 過(す)ごす (to spend (time) — transitive partner)
Related: 通(とお)り過(す)ぎる (to pass by, to go past)
Related: 経(た)つ (to pass (of time), to elapse)
