さっき

さっき
adverb
a little while ago; just now
1. a little while ago; just now
Refers to a short time before the present moment.
さっき電話(でんわ)があったよ。
There was a phone call just now.
さっき言(い)ったでしょう。
I told you just a moment ago, didn't I?
さっきまでここにいたのに。
He was here until just a moment ago.

さっき refers to a very recent past, usually within the last few minutes to an hour.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • さっき: a few minutes ago (very recent)
  • このあいだ: the other day (days to weeks ago)
  • 前(まえ)に: before (general past)
