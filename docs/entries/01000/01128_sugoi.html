1. amazing, incredible, awesome
Expressing that something is impressive, remarkable, or extraordinary. Very commonly used in casual conversation.
すごい！上手(じょうず)ですね。
Amazing! You're so good at it.
Common exclamation
すごい人(ひと)がたくさんいますね。
There are so many people!
Meaning 'a lot' or 'extreme'
あの映画(えいが)はすごかった。
That movie was amazing.
彼女(かのじょ)は語学(ごがく)の才能(さいのう)がすごくて、五(ご)か国語(こくご)も話(はな)せるんだって。
She has an incredible talent for languages—apparently she can speak five languages.
すごい景色(けしき)だね！
What an amazing view!
2. terrible, dreadful
Originally meant something frightening or extreme (can still be used this way).
台風(たいふう)の風(かぜ)がすごくて、外(そと)に出(で)られなかった。木(き)も何本(なんぼん)か倒(たお)れたらしい。
The typhoon winds were terrible, so we couldn't go outside. Apparently several trees fell over too.
Uses sense 2: terrible/dreadful
雨(あめ)がすごくて出(で)られない。
The rain is so bad I can't go out.
すごい音(おと)がしたけど、何(なに)？
There was a terrible noise—what was that?
渋滞(じゅうたい)がすごかった。
The traffic jam was terrible.
すごい暑(あつ)さだ。
The heat is intense.

USAGE:
すごい has two core meanings — (1) amazing, impressive (positive) and (2) terrible, extreme (negative) — and context alone decides which is meant. What both share is that the speaker is struck by the sheer degree of something, whether that degree is welcome or not. Sense 2 attaches naturally to nouns for unpleasant conditions: 雨(あめ), 渋滞(じゅうたい), 音(おと).

FORMS:

  • すごくない (negative)
  • すごくて (te-form)
  • すごかった (past)
  • Adverbial すごく: very, extremely
  • The kanji 凄(すご)い exists but hiragana is far more common

AS AN INTENSIFIER:
すごく is an everyday intensifier: すごくおいしい (very delicious), すごく寒(さむ)い (extremely cold). In casual speech すごい is often used in that adverbial slot instead of すごく (すごいおいしい). Learners will hear this constantly, but it is non-standard and should be avoided in writing.

CASUAL VARIANTS:

  • すげー / すげえ: rough, mostly masculine slang
  • すっごい: emphatic, with the doubled consonant carrying the emphasis

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES:

  • 素晴(すば)らしい: wonderful — positive only, and more formal; safe in writing where すごい is not
  • ひどい: terrible — negative only, and about badness rather than degree
  • やばい: the younger-generation equivalent, likewise both positive and negative, but far more casual

REGISTER:
Informal. One of the most frequent exclamations in spoken Japanese, but in formal writing 素晴(すば)らしい or a more specific adjective is preferred.

Related Words

Related: 素晴(すば)らしい (wonderful, splendid (more formal))
Related: ひどい (terrible, awful)
Related: やばい (amazing; terrible (very casual))
Related: すごく (very, extremely (adverbial form))
