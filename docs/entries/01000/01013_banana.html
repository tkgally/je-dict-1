バナナ

ばなな
noun
banana
1. banana
A yellow tropical fruit with soft sweet flesh. From English 'banana.'
朝(あさ)ごはんにバナナを食(た)べます。
I eat a banana for breakfast.
バナナを三本(さんぼん)買(か)いました。
I bought three bananas.
Bananas use the counter 本
このバナナはまだ青(あお)いです。
This banana is still green (unripe).

バナナ is a common fruit in Japan, imported mainly from the Philippines and Ecuador.

ETYMOLOGY:
From English "banana."

COUNTER:
本(ほん) is used because of the long shape: バナナ一本(いっぽん) (one banana).

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • バナナを剥(む)く: to peel a banana
  • バナナの皮(かわ): banana peel
  • 完熟(かんじゅく)バナナ: fully ripe banana
  • 青(あお)いバナナ: green (unripe) banana

RIPENESS:

  • 青(あお)い: green, unripe
  • 黄色(きいろ)い: yellow, ripe
  • 茶色(ちゃいろ)い斑点(はんてん): brown spots (very ripe)

CULTURE:
Bananas are popular breakfast items and snacks in Japan. They're also commonly used in smoothies and desserts.
