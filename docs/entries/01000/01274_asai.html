浅(あさ)い

あさい
adjective (i-adjective)
shallow; superficial

Definitions

1. shallow (physical depth)
Describes something with little depth, such as water or a container.
2. superficial; shallow (figurative)
Describes limited knowledge, experience, or understanding.
3. light (sleep, color)
Describes sleep that is not deep or colors that are pale/light.

Examples

この川(かわ)は浅(あさ)いから、歩(ある)いて渡(わた)れます。
This river is shallow, so you can walk across it.
Physical depth
知識(ちしき)が浅(あさ)い。
My knowledge is shallow/limited.
Figurative usage
眠(ねむ)りが浅(あさ)くて、すぐに目(め)が覚(さ)めてしまう。
My sleep is light, so I wake up easily.

Notes

浅(あさ)い is an i-adjective meaning 'shallow' or 'superficial.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 浅(あさ)く
  • Noun form: 浅(あさ)さ

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 浅(あさ)くない
  • Te-form: 浅(あさ)くて
  • Past: 浅(あさ)かった

Antonym: 深(ふか)い (deep).

Common collocations: 浅(あさ)い川(かわ) (shallow river), 浅(あさ)い眠(ねむ)り (light sleep), 経験(けいけん)が浅(あさ)い (inexperienced).
