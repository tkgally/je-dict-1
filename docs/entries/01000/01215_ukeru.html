1. to receive, to get
To receive something that is given, sent, or directed toward you. Often used for abstract things like impressions, influence, or damage.
彼(かれ)は大(おお)きな影響(えいきょう)を受(う)けた。
He received a big influence. / He was greatly influenced.
影響(えいきょう)を受(う)ける = to be influenced
台風(たいふう)で大(おお)きな被害(ひがい)を受(う)けた。
We suffered major damage from the typhoon.
被害(ひがい)を受(う)ける = to suffer damage
印象(いんしょう)を受(う)けた。
I received an impression.
批判(ひはん)を受(う)けた。
I received criticism.
注目(ちゅうもく)を受(う)けている。
It is receiving attention.
2. to take (an examination, interview)
To undergo or sit for an exam, test, interview, or medical examination.
来週(らいしゅう)試験(しけん)を受(う)けます。
I will take an exam next week.
試験(しけん)を受(う)ける is the standard expression for 'take a test'
面接(めんせつ)を受(う)けに行(い)く。
I'm going to have an interview.
手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)けた。
I underwent surgery.
検査(けんさ)を受(う)ける。
To undergo an examination.
入学(にゅうがく)試験(しけん)を受(う)けた。
I took the entrance exam.
授業(じゅぎょう)を受(う)ける。
To attend a class.
3. to be popular, to be well-received (colloquial)
In casual speech, to be funny or successful with an audience. Often used as ウケる in katakana.
その冗談(じょうだん)、すごくウケた！
That joke was a big hit!
Colloquial usage meaning 'went over well'
その話(はなし)はウケるよ。
That story will go over well.
若者(わかもの)にウケている。
It's popular with young people.
この映画(えいが)は海外(かいがい)でもウケた。
This movie was a hit overseas too.
彼(かれ)の物真似(ものまね)はいつもクラスでウケている。
His impressions are always a hit in class.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを受(う)ける (receive/take X). No intransitive pair.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state or completed action with continuing relevance. 受(う)けている = 'is receiving/undergoing' or 'has received.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 試験(しけん)を受(う)ける (take an exam)
  • 面接(めんせつ)を受(う)ける (have an interview)
  • 影響(えいきょう)を受(う)ける (be influenced)
  • 被害(ひがい)を受(う)ける (suffer damage)
  • 印象(いんしょう)を受(う)ける (receive an impression)
  • 手術(しゅじゅつ)を受(う)ける (undergo surgery)

NUANCE: Unlike もらう (to receive from someone), 受(う)ける often implies receiving something abstract or undergoing an experience. For concrete gifts, もらう is more natural.

COLLOQUIAL: ウケる (often in katakana) means 'to be funny/popular.' 彼(かれ)の話(はなし)がウケた = 'His story went over well.'

Related Words

Related: 受(う)け入(い)れる (to accept, to receive)
