つもり

つもり
noun
intention, plan
1. intention, plan
Expresses the speaker's intention or plan to do something.
2. belief, assumption
Indicates what the speaker believes or assumes to be the case (sometimes contrary to reality).
来年(らいねん)日本(にほん)に行(い)くつもりです。
I plan to go to Japan next year.
Expressing intention
手伝(てつだ)うつもりだったんですが...
I intended to help, but...
Past intention
ちゃんとやったつもりです。
I believe I did it properly.
Expressing belief about one's own action

つもり follows the plain form of verbs to express intention: 行くつもり (intend to go), 行かないつもり (intend not to go). Can also express one's belief or self-assessment: Aのつもり (I think/believe I am A). The kanji 積もり exists but hiragana is standard. Compare with 予定 (よてい - scheduled plan).
