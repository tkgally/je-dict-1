つもり

つもり
noun
intention, plan
1. intention, plan
Expresses the speaker's intention or plan to do something.
来年(らいねん)日本(にほん)に行(い)くつもりです。
I plan to go to Japan next year.
Expressing intention
手伝(てつだ)うつもりだったんですが...
I intended to help, but...
Past intention
来月(らいげつ)引(ひ)っ越(こ)すつもりだったけど、予定(よてい)が変(か)わった。
I was planning to move next month, but my plans changed.
2. belief, assumption
Indicates what the speaker believes or assumes to be the case (sometimes contrary to reality).
ちゃんとやったつもりです。
I believe I did it properly.
Expressing belief about one's own action
若(わか)いつもりでも、体(からだ)は正直(しょうじき)です。
Even if you think you're young, your body is honest.
Xのつもり = believing oneself to be X
説明(せつめい)したつもりだったのに、全然(ぜんぜん)伝(つた)わっていなかった。
I thought I had explained it, but it didn't get through at all.

つもり follows the plain form of verbs to express intention: 行くつもり (intend to go), 行かないつもり (intend not to go). Can also express one's belief or self-assessment: Aのつもり (I think/believe I am A). The kanji 積(つ)もり exists but hiragana is standard. Compare with 予定(よてい) (scheduled plan).
