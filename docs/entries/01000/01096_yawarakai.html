柔(やわ)らかい

やわらかい
adjective (i-adjective)
soft, tender
1. soft, tender
Yielding easily to pressure, not hard or firm. Used for food, textures, and materials.
このお肉(にく)は柔(やわ)らかいですね。
This meat is tender, isn't it?
赤(あか)ちゃんの肌(はだ)は柔(やわ)らかい。
A baby's skin is soft.
2. gentle, mild
When describing personality, light, or movements, means gentle and not harsh.
柔(やわ)らかい光(ひかり)が部屋(へや)に入(はい)った。
Soft light entered the room.
3. flexible
Easily bent or adapted, not rigid.

柔らかい conjugates as an i-adjective: 柔らかくない (negative), 柔らかくて (te-form), 柔らかかった (past). The opposite is 硬い (かたい - hard). 軟らかい is an alternative kanji spelling with the same meaning.
