売(う)り場(ば)

うりば
noun
sales floor; counter; department
1. sales floor, counter, department
The area in a store where goods are sold; a section of a store.
食品(しょくひん)売(う)り場(ば)は地下(ちか)にあります。
The food section is in the basement.
紳士服(しんしふく)売(う)り場(ば)は何階(なんかい)ですか。
What floor is the men's clothing department on?
チケット売(う)り場(ば)
Ticket counter/booth

売(う)り場(ば) refers to a place where goods are sold, typically a section or department in a store.

Formation: 売(う)る (to sell) + 場(ば) (place)

Common compounds:

  • 食品(しょくひん)売(う)り場(ば): food section
  • 衣料(いりょう)売(う)り場(ば): clothing department
  • チケット売(う)り場(ば): ticket booth
  • 切符(きっぷ)売(う)り場(ば): ticket counter

Related:

  • 売(う)り場(ば)面積(めんせき): sales floor area
  • 乗(の)り場(ば): boarding area (similar pattern)
