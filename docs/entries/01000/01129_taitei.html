たいてい

たいてい
adverb
usually, mostly
1. usually, mostly, generally
In most cases or instances; describing what normally happens or is true.
たいてい七時(しちじ)に起(お)きます。
I usually wake up at seven o'clock.
週末(しゅうまつ)はたいてい家(いえ)にいます。
I'm usually at home on weekends.
この店(みせ)はたいてい混(こ)んでいます。
This shop is usually crowded.

たいてい means 'usually' or 'mostly' - similar to 普通 (ふつう) and いつも, but たいてい suggests 'in most cases' with room for exceptions. The kanji 大抵 can be used but hiragana is more common.

Related:

  • いつも (always)
  • 普通 (usually, normally)
  • 大体 (だいたい - roughly, mostly).
