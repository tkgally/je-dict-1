君(きみ)

きみ
pronoun
you (informal)
1. you (informal)
An informal second-person pronoun, typically used by men when addressing someone of equal or lower status.
君(きみ)も来(く)る？
Are you coming too?
君(きみ)は何(なに)が好(す)き？
What do you like?
君(きみ)の意見(いけん)を聞(き)かせてほしい。
I want to hear your opinion.
君(きみ)がそう言(い)うなら、私(わたし)も一緒(いっしょ)に行(い)くよ。
If you say so, I'll go with you.
君(きみ)がいないとみんな寂(さび)しいから、また時間(じかん)がある時(とき)に遊(あそ)びに来(き)てね。
We all miss you when you're not around, so come visit again when you have time.

Usage considerations:

  • Primarily used by male speakers
  • Used toward peers, subordinates, or close friends
  • Can sound condescending if used toward someone of higher status
  • In romantic contexts, can be affectionate
  • Different from {あなた} which is more formal/neutral
