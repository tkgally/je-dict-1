計画(けいかく)

けいかく
noun, suru verb
plan, project
1. plan, project, scheme
A detailed proposal or intention for doing or achieving something.
夏休(なつやす)みの計画(けいかく)を立(た)てましょう。
Let's make plans for summer vacation.
計画を立てる is a common collocation
計画(けいかく)どおりに進(すす)んでいます。
Things are proceeding according to plan.
2. to plan (suru verb)
When used as a suru verb, means 'to plan' or 'to scheme.'
旅行(りょこう)を計画(けいかく)しています。
I'm planning a trip.
Using 計画する

計画(けいかく) refers to plans, projects, or schemes. Can function as a noun or suru verb.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 計画(けいかく)を立(た)てる: to make a plan
  • 計画(けいかく)どおり: according to plan
  • 計画(けいかく)を実行(じっこう)する: to execute a plan
  • 計画的(けいかくてき)に: systematically, in a planned manner

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 予定(よてい): schedule (more concrete, time-based)
  • つもり: intention (personal, informal)
  • 企画(きかく): project, planning (often business/creative)

NOTE: 計画(けいかく)する means "to plan" as a verb.
