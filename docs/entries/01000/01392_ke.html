毛(け)

け
noun
hair, fur

Definitions

1. hair, fur
The fine threadlike strands growing from the skin of humans or animals.

Examples

猫(ねこ)の毛(け)がソファについている。
There's cat fur on the sofa.
腕(うで)の毛(け)が濃(こ)い。
The hair on my arms is thick.

Notes

Refers to body hair or animal fur. For head hair, 髪(かみ) or 髪の毛(かみのけ) is typically used.
