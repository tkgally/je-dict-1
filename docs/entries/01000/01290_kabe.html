壁(かべ)

かべ
noun
wall
1. wall
A vertical structure that encloses or divides an area.
壁(かべ)に絵(え)を掛(か)ける。
To hang a picture on the wall.
壁(かべ)にぶつかる。
To hit a wall / To encounter an obstacle.
Can be literal or figurative
2. barrier, obstacle
Figuratively, something that blocks progress.
言葉(ことば)の壁(かべ)
Language barrier
Figurative usage
壁(かべ)にぶつかる。
To hit a wall / To encounter an obstacle.
Can be literal or figurative

壁(かべ) means 'wall' both literally and figuratively.

Common collocations:

  • 壁(かべ)に掛(か)ける: to hang on a wall
  • 壁(かべ)を塗(ぬ)る: to paint a wall
  • 壁(かべ)にぶつかる: to hit a wall (literal/figurative)

Figurative:

  • 言葉(ことば)の壁(かべ): language barrier
  • 心(こころ)の壁(かべ): emotional barrier
  • 壁(かべ)を乗(の)り越(こ)える: to overcome a barrier
general
