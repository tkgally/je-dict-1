頑張(がんば)って

がんばって
expression
good luck, do your best
1. good luck, do your best, hang in there
An expression of encouragement. It comes from the verb 頑張(がんば)る (to persevere, to do one's best). Used to cheer someone on.
試験(しけん)、頑張(がんば)って！
Good luck on your exam!
頑張(がんば)ってください。
Please do your best.
Polite form
もう少(すこ)しだから、頑張(がんば)って！
Just a little more, hang in there!
明日(あした)の試合(しあい)、頑張(がんば)ってね。応援(おうえん)しているよ。
Good luck in tomorrow's game. I'm cheering for you.
彼(かれ)は仕事(しごと)で疲(つか)れていたけど、頑張(がんば)って最後(さいご)までやり遂(と)げました。
He was tired from work, but he persevered and finished it to the end.
Using 頑張る verb form

頑張(がんば)って is the て-form of 頑張(がんば)る used on its own as a set expression of encouragement. It is one of the most frequently heard phrases in daily Japanese life.

WHEN IT IS USED:

  • Before an exam, interview, competition, or performance
  • To someone facing a difficult period (illness, heavy workload, moving house)
  • As a parting phrase to someone heading into something demanding

POLITENESS LEVELS (least to most polite):

  • 頑張(がんば)って: plain; friends, family, juniors
  • 頑張(がんば)ってね: plain and softer, warmer
  • 頑張(がんば)ってください: polite; the safe default with strangers, colleagues, and superiors

RELATED FORMS OF THE SAME VERB:

  • 頑張(がんば)れ: blunt imperative — shouted encouragement at sports events; too rough for most conversation
  • 頑張(がんば)ろう: 'let's do our best' — includes the speaker, used for shared efforts
  • 頑張(がんば)ります: 'I'll do my best' — the standard reply when someone says 頑張(がんば)って to you

CULTURAL NOTE: English 'good luck' appeals to chance, while 頑張(がんば)って appeals to effort and endurance — it asks the listener to keep pushing. Because of this, it can occasionally land badly on someone who is already exhausted or who cannot change their situation; 無理(むり)しないでね ('don't overdo it') is gentler in those cases.

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • ファイト: 'Go for it!' — casual, youthful, borrowed from English 'fight'
  • 応援(おうえん)しているよ: 'I'm rooting for you'
  • うまくいくといいね: 'I hope it goes well' — closer to English 'good luck'

Related Words

Related: 頑張(がんば)る (to do one's best (source verb))
Related: ファイト (Go for it! (casual))
Related: 応援(おうえん) (support, cheering)
