ございます

ございます
verb
to be; to exist (polite/humble)
1. to be; to exist (polite)
Very polite form of ある, used in formal situations.
質問(しつもん)がございますか。
Do you have any questions?
Formal business speech
何(なに)かご質問(しつもん)がございましたら、お知(し)らせください。
If you have any questions, please let us know.
Formal business correspondence
お時間(じかん)がございましたら、ご連絡(れんらく)ください。
If you have time, please contact us.
Formal request
こちらにございます。
It's right here.
Sense 1: to exist (polite)
在庫(ざいこ)はございますか。
Do you have it in stock?
Sense 1: to exist
ご予約(よやく)はございますか。
Do you have a reservation?
Sense 1: to exist
2. to be (polite copula)
Used with adjectives in polite speech, as in ありがとうございます.
ありがとうございます。
Thank you very much.
Most common usage
おはようございます。
Good morning.
Polite morning greeting
おめでとうございます。
Congratulations.
Sense 2: polite copula
申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I'm very sorry.
Sense 2: polite copula

ございます is a very polite form of ある, commonly used in formal and business contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ありがとうございます: thank you
  • おはようございます: good morning
  • おめでとうございます: congratulations
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません: I'm very sorry

KEIGO TYPE:

  • 丁寧語(ていねいご): Polite language
