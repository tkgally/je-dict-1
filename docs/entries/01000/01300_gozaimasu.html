1. to be; to exist (polite)
Very polite form of ある, used in formal situations.
質問(しつもん)がございますか。
Do you have any questions?
Formal business speech
何(なに)かご質問(しつもん)がございましたら、お知(し)らせください。
If you have any questions, please let us know.
Formal business correspondence
お時間(じかん)がございましたら、ご連絡(れんらく)ください。
If you have time, please contact us.
Formal request
こちらにございます。
It's right here.
在庫(ざいこ)はございますか。
Do you have it in stock?
ご予約(よやく)はございますか。
Do you have a reservation?
2. to be (polite copula)
Used with adjectives in polite speech, as in ありがとうございます.
ありがとうございます。
Thank you very much.
Most common usage
おはようございます。
Good morning.
Polite morning greeting
おめでとうございます。
Congratulations.
申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません。
I'm very sorry.
お忙(いそが)しいところ、誠(まこと)にありがとうございます。
Thank you very much for taking time out of your busy schedule.

The very polite form of ある (to be, to exist). One of the most fundamental keigo expressions, used extensively in business, customer service, and formal speech.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive)
  • No standard transitive pair
  • Pattern: ございます

COMMON FIXED EXPRESSIONS:

  • ありがとうございます: thank you very much
  • おはようございます: good morning
  • おめでとうございます: congratulations
  • 申(もう)し訳(わけ)ございません: I'm terribly sorry

AS A POLITE ある:

  • ご質問(しつもん)がございますか: Do you have any questions?
  • 在庫(ざいこ)はございますか: Do you have it in stock?
  • こちらにございます: It's right here.

KEIGO TYPE:
丁寧語(ていねいご) (polite language). Replaces ある/あります in formal speech. Not used in casual conversation.
