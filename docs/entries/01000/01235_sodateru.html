育(そだ)てる

そだてる
verb (ichidan)
to raise, to bring up

Definitions

1. to raise, to bring up
To nurture a child or young person until they mature. The transitive form—someone raises someone/something.
2. to grow, to cultivate
To nurture plants or develop skills/talents.

Examples

子供(こども)を育(そだ)てるのは大変(たいへん)です。
Raising children is hard.
野菜(やさい)を育(そだ)てている。
I'm growing vegetables.
才能(さいのう)を育(そだ)てる。
Develop talent.
Abstract usage

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 育(そだ)つ (intransitive, to grow up). Pattern: Xを育(そだ)てる (raise/grow X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 育(そだ)てている = 'is raising/growing.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を育(そだ)てる (raise children)
  • 野菜(やさい)を育(そだ)てる (grow vegetables)
  • 人材(じんざい)を育(そだ)てる (develop human resources)
  • 才能(さいのう)を育(そだ)てる (cultivate talent)
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を込(こ)めて育(そだ)てる (raise with love)

CONTRAST:

  • 育(そだ)てる (transitive): (someone) raises X
  • 育(そだ)つ (intransitive): X grows up

Example pair:

  • 親(おや)が子供(こども)を育(そだ)てる (Parents raise children)
  • 子供(こども)が育(そだ)つ (Children grow up)
