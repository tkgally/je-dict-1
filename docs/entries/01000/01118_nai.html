ない

ない
adjective (i-adjective)
nonexistent, not have
1. nonexistent, there is no
Indicates the absence or non-existence of something. The negative counterpart of ある.
水(みず)がない。
There is no water.
時間(じかん)がない。
I don't have time. / There is no time.
問題(もんだい)はないですか。
Are there no problems? / Is there no issue?
ここには何(なに)もない。
There's nothing here.
Sense 1: nonexistent
心配(しんぱい)はない。
There's nothing to worry about.
Sense 1: there is no
2. to not have
Indicates that someone doesn't possess something.
時間(じかん)がない。
I don't have time. / There is no time.
お金(かね)がないので、買(か)えません。
I can't buy it because I don't have money.
今日(きょう)は仕事(しごと)がないから、家(いえ)でゆっくり休(やす)みます。
I don't have work today, so I'll relax at home.
経験(けいけん)がないので、不安(ふあん)だ。
I'm anxious because I don't have experience.
Sense 2: to not have
自信(じしん)がない。
I don't have confidence.
Sense 2: to not have
興味(きょうみ)がなかった。
I wasn't interested.
Sense 2: to not have (past)

ない conjugates as an i-adjective: なくない (negative - 'is it not nonexistent?'), なくて (te-form), なかった (past). ない is the negative form of ある (to exist/have for inanimate things). For animate things (people/animals), the negative of いる is いない. The kanji 無(な)い is sometimes used but hiragana is more common.
