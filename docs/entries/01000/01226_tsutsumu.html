包(つつ)む

つつむ
verb (godan)
to wrap, to cover

Definitions

1. to wrap, to pack
To cover something by folding paper, cloth, or other material around it.
2. to envelop, to surround
To cover or surround something completely.

Examples

プレゼントを紙(かみ)で包(つつ)む。
Wrap the present in paper.
で marks wrapping material
包(つつ)んでいただけますか。
Could you wrap it for me?
Common request at stores
霧(きり)に包(つつ)まれた山(やま)。
A mountain enveloped in fog.
Passive/figurative usage

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: XをYで包(つつ)む (wrap X in/with Y).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing or completed action. 包(つつ)んでいる = 'is wrapping' or 'has wrapped.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 紙(かみ)で包(つつ)む (wrap in paper)
  • プレゼントを包(つつ)む (wrap a present)
  • 風呂敷(ふろしき)で包(つつ)む (wrap in furoshiki)
  • 包(つつ)み紙(がみ) (wrapping paper)
  • 霧(きり)に包(つつ)まれる (be enveloped in fog)

RELATED:

  • 包(つつ)み: wrapping, package (noun)
  • 包装(ほうそう): packaging (more formal noun)
