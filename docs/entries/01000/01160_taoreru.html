倒(たお)れる

たおれる
verb (ichidan)
to collapse, to fall down
1. to fall down, to collapse
For a standing person or object to fall over. The intransitive form.
強(つよ)い風(かぜ)で木(き)が倒(たお)れた。
The tree fell down in the strong wind.
2. to collapse (from exhaustion/illness)
To fall down due to illness, exhaustion, or overwork.
過労(かろう)で倒(たお)れた。
I collapsed from overwork.
Health-related collapse
3. to fall, to be overthrown
For a government, organization, or system to collapse or fail.
政権(せいけん)が倒(たお)れた。
The government fell.
Political collapse

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 倒(たお)す (transitive, to knock down). Pattern: Xが倒(たお)れる (X falls down).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 倒(たお)れている = 'is lying fallen' or 'has collapsed.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 木(き)が倒(たお)れる (tree falls)
  • 病気(びょうき)で倒(たお)れる (collapse from illness)
  • 過労(かろう)で倒(たお)れる (collapse from overwork)
  • 政府(せいふ)が倒(たお)れる (government falls)
  • ばったり倒(たお)れる (fall down suddenly)

CONTRAST:

  • 倒(たお)れる (intransitive): X falls/collapses
  • 倒(たお)す (transitive): (someone) knocks X down
