下(さ)がる

さがる
verb (godan)
to go down, to drop

Definitions

1. to go down, to descend
To move to a lower position or level. The intransitive form—something goes down by itself.
2. to drop, to fall, to decrease
For a value, temperature, or level to decrease.
3. to step back, to move back
To move backward or retreat from a position.

Examples

気温(きおん)が下(さ)がった。
The temperature dropped.
値段(ねだん)が下(さ)がるのを待(ま)つ。
Wait for the price to go down.
少(すこ)し下(さ)がってください。
Please step back a little.
'Move back' meaning

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). Pair: 下(さ)げる (transitive, to lower). Pattern: Xが下(さ)がる (X goes down).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 下(さ)がっている = 'has gone down' or 'is low.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 気温(きおん)が下(さ)がる (temperature drops)
  • 値段(ねだん)が下(さ)がる (price drops)
  • 熱(ねつ)が下(さ)がる (fever goes down)
  • 後(うし)ろに下(さ)がる (step back)
  • 成績(せいせき)が下(さ)がる (grades drop)

CONTRAST:

  • 下(さ)がる (intransitive): X goes down
  • 下(さ)げる (transitive): (someone) lowers X

OPPOSITE: 上(あ)がる (to go up, to rise)

Related Words

Pair verb: 下(さ)げる (transitive, to lower)
Antonym: 上(あ)がる (to go up, to rise)
