1. shirt
A general term for shirts, including T-shirts and undershirts. From English 'shirt.'
白(しろ)いシャツを着(き)ています。
I'm wearing a white shirt.
このシャツは大(おお)きすぎます。
This shirt is too big.
Tシャツを買(か)いました。
I bought a T-shirt.
暑(あつ)いので、シャツの袖(そで)をまくりました。
It's hot, so I rolled up my shirt sleeves.
このシャツはしわになりにくい素材(そざい)でできています。
This shirt is made of a material that doesn't wrinkle easily.

シャツ is a general term for shirts, borrowed from English "shirt."

TYPES OF SHIRTS:

  • Tシャツ: T-shirt
  • ワイシャツ: dress shirt (from "white shirt")
  • ポロシャツ: polo shirt
  • 長袖(ながそで)シャツ: long-sleeved shirt
  • 半袖(はんそで)シャツ: short-sleeved shirt

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • シャツを着(き)る: to wear a shirt
  • シャツを脱(ぬ)ぐ: to take off a shirt
  • シャツにアイロンをかける: to iron a shirt
  • シャツの裾(すそ): shirt hem/tail

NOTE:
ワイシャツ specifically refers to dress shirts worn with suits. The term comes from "white shirt" but now includes colored dress shirts too.
