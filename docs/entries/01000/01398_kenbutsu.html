見物(けんぶつ)

けんぶつ
noun (verbal)
sightseeing, watching
1. sightseeing, viewing
The activity of visiting and looking at places of interest.
京都(きょうと)でお寺(おてら)を見物(けんぶつ)した。
I went sightseeing at temples in Kyoto.
2. watching, spectating
Watching an event or performance.
祭(まつ)りの見物(けんぶつ)に行(い)きましょう。
Let's go watch the festival.

見物(けんぶつ)する is the verbal form. Related: 見物人(けんぶつにん) - spectator, onlooker.
