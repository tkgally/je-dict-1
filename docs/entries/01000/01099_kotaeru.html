答(こた)える

こたえる
verb (ichidan)
to answer, to respond

Definitions

1. to answer, to reply
To give a response to a question or inquiry. The question or person being answered is marked with に.

Examples

質問(しつもん)に答(こた)えてください。
Please answer the question.
質問に答える is a common collocation
彼(かれ)は何(なに)も答(こた)えなかった。
He didn't answer anything.
正直(しょうじき)に答(こた)えます。
I will answer honestly.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xに答(こた)える (answer X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 答(こた)えている = 'is answering.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 質問(しつもん)に答(こた)える (answer a question)
  • 正直(しょうじき)に答(こた)える (answer honestly)
  • 電話(でんわ)に答(こた)える (answer the phone - less common)
  • 答(こた)え (noun: answer)

IMPORTANT: Takes に particle, not を. Compare: 聞(き)く takes を for questions.
