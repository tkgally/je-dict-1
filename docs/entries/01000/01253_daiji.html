大事(だいじ)

だいじ
adjective (na-adjective)
important; valuable; precious
1. important, significant
Describes something of great importance or significance.
健康(けんこう)は大事(だいじ)です。
Health is important.
Predicate usage
これは大事(だいじ)な書類(しょるい)です。
This is an important document.
Modifier usage
2. precious, valuable, cherished
Describes something treasured or held dear.
家族(かぞく)を大事(だいじ)にしてください。
Please cherish your family.
〜を大事(だいじ)にする pattern
お大事(だいじ)に。
Take care (of yourself). / Get well soon.
Set expression for someone who is sick
3. serious (matter)
Describes a serious or grave situation (often in 大事になる).
大事(だいじ)にならなくてよかった。
I'm glad it didn't become serious.
大事(だいじ)になる = to become serious/a big deal

大事(だいじ) is a na-adjective meaning 'important,' 'valuable,' or 'serious.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 大事(だいじ)に
  • Modifier: 大事(だいじ)な + noun

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 大事(だいじ)ではない / 大事(だいじ)じゃない
  • Te-form: 大事(だいじ)で
  • Past: 大事(だいじ)だった

Key expressions:

  • 〜を大事(だいじ)にする: to cherish, to take care of, to value
  • お大事(だいじ)に: Take care / Get well soon (said to someone who is sick)
  • 大事(だいじ)になる: to become serious/a big deal

Similar words:

  • 大事(だいじ) vs. 大切(たいせつ): Both mean 'important/precious.' 大切(たいせつ) emphasizes emotional value and care; 大事(だいじ) is broader and can include 'serious (matter).'
  • 大事(だいじ) vs. 重要(じゅうよう): 重要(じゅうよう) is more formal and objective, often used for official/business matters.
