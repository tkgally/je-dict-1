行(い)ってらっしゃい

いってらっしゃい
expression
have a good trip
1. have a good trip, see you later
A set phrase said to someone who is leaving. It is the standard response to 行(い)ってきます. Literally means 'go and come back (please).'
行(い)ってらっしゃい！
Have a good trip!
「行(い)ってきます」「行(い)ってらっしゃい」
"I'm leaving." "Have a good trip."
気(き)をつけて、行(い)ってらっしゃい。
Take care, and have a good trip.
行(い)ってらっしゃいませ。
Have a safe trip.
母(はは)は毎朝(まいあさ)、家(いえ)を出(で)る私(わたし)に「行(い)ってらっしゃい、今日(きょう)も頑張(がんば)ってね」と言(い)います。
Every morning, my mother says to me as I leave home, 'Have a good trip, do your best today.'

行(い)ってらっしゃい is said to someone leaving, expressing a wish for them to go safely and return.

USAGE:
This is the standard response to 行(い)ってきます. It literally means 'please go and come back.'

FORMS:

  • 行(い)ってらっしゃい: standard polite
  • 行(い)ってらっしゃいませ: very polite (formal contexts, service staff)

COMMON ADDITIONS:

  • 気(き)をつけて行(い)ってらっしゃい: take care on your way
  • 行(い)ってらっしゃい、頑張(がんば)ってね: have a good day, do your best

RELATED EXPRESSIONS:

  • 行(い)ってきます: I'm leaving (said by the one departing)
  • ただいま: I'm home (upon returning)
  • お帰(かえ)りなさい: welcome back (response to ただいま)
