行(い)ってらっしゃい

いってらっしゃい
expression
have a good trip

Definitions

1. have a good trip, see you later
A set phrase said to someone who is leaving. It is the standard response to 行(い)ってきます. Literally means 'go and come back (please).'

Examples

「行(い)ってきます」「行(い)ってらっしゃい」
"I'm leaving." "Have a good trip."
Standard exchange
気(き)をつけて、行(い)ってらっしゃい。
Take care, and have a good trip.
Adding concern
行(い)ってらっしゃいませ。
Have a safe trip.
Polite form

Notes

This is the response to 行(い)ってきます. It expresses a wish for the person to go safely and return. The polite form 行(い)ってらっしゃいませ is used in formal contexts or by service staff.
