行(い)ってきます

いってきます
expression
I'm leaving
1. I'm leaving, I'll be back
A set phrase said when leaving home or the office. Literally means 'I will go and come back.' The response is 行(い)ってらっしゃい.
行(い)ってきます！
I'm leaving!
Said when leaving home
じゃ、行(い)ってきますね。
Well then, I'm off.
Casual farewell
学校(がっこう)に行(い)ってきます。
I'm going to school.
Specifying destination

This is one of the most common daily expressions in Japanese. It is said when leaving home, the office, or any place you intend to return to. The phrase implies you will come back. The listener responds with 行(い)ってらっしゃい.
