階(かい)

かい
counter
floors, stories
1. floors, stories (of a building)
A counter for floors or stories of a building.
私(わたし)の部屋(へや)は五(ご)階(かい)です。
My room is on the fifth floor.
何(なん)階(かい)に行(い)きますか。
What floor are you going to?
一(いっ)階(かい)にトイレがあります。
There is a restroom on the first floor.
いっかい with small っ
エレベーターで十(じゅっ)階(かい)まで行(い)きましょう。
Let's take the elevator to the tenth floor.
受付(うけつけ)は二(に)階(かい)にありますが、会議室(かいぎしつ)は五(ご)階(かい)にあります。
The reception is on the second floor, but the meeting room is on the fifth floor.

階(かい) is the counter for building floors and stories.

IRREGULAR READINGS:

  • 一(いっ)階(かい): first floor (with っ)
  • 三(さん)階(がい): third floor (voiced が)
  • 何(なん)階(がい): what floor? (voiced が)

COUNTING:

  • 二(に)階(かい), 四(よん)階(かい), 五(ご)階(かい), 六(ろっ)階(かい), 七(なな)階(かい), 八(はっ)階(かい)/八(はち)階(かい), 九(きゅう)階(かい), 十(じゅっ)階(かい)

JAPANESE VS. BRITISH ENGLISH:
The first floor in Japan (一(いっ)階(かい)) corresponds to the ground floor in British English. There is no separate 'ground floor' and 'first floor' distinction as in British usage.
