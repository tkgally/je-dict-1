取(と)り替(か)える

とりかえる
verb (ichidan)
to exchange, to replace
1. to exchange, to swap
To exchange one thing for another with someone.
席(せき)を取(と)り替(か)えてもらえますか。
Could you exchange seats with me?
友達(ともだち)と弁当(べんとう)のおかずを取(と)り替(か)えた。
I swapped side dishes with my friend.
と marks the person you exchange with
サイズが合(あ)わなかったので、お店(みせ)で取(と)り替(か)えてもらった。
The size didn't fit, so I had it exchanged at the store.
2. to replace, to change
To remove something and put a new one in its place.
電池(でんち)を取(と)り替(か)える。
Replace the batteries.
古(ふる)いものを新(あたら)しいものに取(と)り替(か)えた。
I replaced the old one with a new one.
に marks what it's replaced with
タイヤを取(と)り替(か)える時期(じき)が来(き)た。
It's time to replace the tires.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを取(と)り替(か)える (replace/exchange X) / XをYに取(と)り替(か)える (replace X with Y).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed action. 取(と)り替(か)えている = 'has replaced.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 電池(でんち)を取(と)り替(か)える (replace batteries)
  • 電球(でんきゅう)を取(と)り替(か)える (change a light bulb)
  • 席(せき)を取(と)り替(か)える (exchange seats)
  • AをBに取(と)り替(か)える (replace A with B)

COMPOUND VERB: This is a compound of 取(と)る (take) + 替(か)える (change).

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 交換(こうかん)する: to exchange (more formal)
  • 替(か)える: to change, replace (simpler form)
