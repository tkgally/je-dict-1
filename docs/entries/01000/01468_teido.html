程度(ていど)

ていど
noun
degree, extent

Definitions

1. degree, extent, level
The amount, level, or extent of something.
2. about, approximately
Used to indicate an approximate amount.

Examples

どの程度(ていど)難(むずか)しいですか。
How difficult is it?
1時間(じかん)程度(ていど)かかります。
It takes about one hour.

Notes

As a suffix, indicates approximation: 三日程度(ていど) (about three days). Related expression: ある程度(ていど) (to some extent).
