瓶(びん)

びん
noun
bottle
1. bottle
A container typically made of glass, used for liquids.
瓶(びん)を割(わ)った。
I broke a bottle.
ワインの瓶(びん)を開(あ)けました。
I opened a bottle of wine.
空(から)の瓶(びん)を捨(す)ててください。
Please throw away the empty bottles.
瓶(びん)ビールと生(なま)ビール、どちらがいいですか。
Which would you prefer, bottled beer or draft beer?
日本(にほん)では瓶(びん)や缶(かん)のリサイクルが進(すす)んでいて、分別(ぶんべつ)して出(だ)さなければなりません。
In Japan, bottle and can recycling is well-established, and you must sort them before disposing.

瓶(びん) typically refers to glass bottles used for liquids.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 空(あ)き瓶(びん): empty bottle
  • 瓶(びん)ビール: bottled beer
  • 瓶(びん)詰(づ)め: bottled (food preservation)
  • ワインの瓶(びん): wine bottle
  • 一升瓶(いっしょうびん): large sake bottle (1.8L)

CONTRAST:

  • 瓶(びん): glass bottle
  • ペットボトル: plastic (PET) bottle
  • 缶(かん): can

RECYCLING IN JAPAN:
Glass bottles are separated for recycling. Categories include 瓶(びん)/缶(かん)/ペットボトル. Some bottles (一升瓶(いっしょうびん), beer bottles) can be returned for deposit refund.
