叱(しか)る

しかる
verb (godan)
to scold

Definitions

1. to scold, to reprimand
To speak angrily to someone because they have done something wrong. Often used by parents, teachers, or supervisors.

Examples

母(はは)に叱(しか)られた。
I was scolded by my mother.
Passive form 叱(しか)られる is very common
先生(せんせい)が生徒(せいと)を叱(しか)った。
The teacher scolded the student.
遅刻(ちこく)して叱(しか)られるかもしれない。
I might get scolded for being late.

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを叱(しか)る (scold X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 叱(しか)っている = 'is scolding.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)を叱(しか)る (scold a child)
  • 叱(しか)られる (be scolded—passive, very common)
  • 厳(きび)しく叱(しか)る (scold harshly)
  • 愛情(あいじょう)を込(こ)めて叱(しか)る (scold with love)

CONTRAST WITH 怒(おこ)る:

  • 叱(しか)る: to scold (corrective, educational purpose)
  • 怒(おこ)る: to get angry (emotional reaction)

In theory, 叱(しか)る is done for the other person's benefit, while 怒(おこ)る is simply expressing anger. In practice, these often overlap.

REGISTER: Neutral.
