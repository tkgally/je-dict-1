自由(じゆう)

じゆう
na-adjective, noun
free, freedom
1. free, unrestricted
Having the ability to act, speak, or think without restrictions or constraints.
自由(じゆう)な時間(じかん)がほしい。
I want free time.
自由(じゆう)に使(つか)ってください。
Please use it freely.
自由に as adverb
子供(こども)の頃(ころ)は自由(じゆう)に遊(あそ)べてよかった。
It was nice to be able to play freely when I was a child.
2. freedom, liberty (noun)
The state of being free; the power to act without constraint.
自由(じゆう)は大切(たいせつ)です。
Freedom is important.
Used as a noun
自由(じゆう)を求(もと)めて戦(たたか)う。
To fight for freedom.
言論(げんろん)の自由(じゆう)はとても大事(だいじ)だと思(おも)います。
I think freedom of speech is very important.
言論(げんろん)の自由(じゆう) = freedom of speech

自由(じゆう) functions as both a na-adjective (自由(じゆう)な) and a noun. As a na-adjective: 自由(じゆう)な (before nouns), 自由(じゆう)に (adverbially, meaning 'freely'). The kanji mean 'self' + 'reason/cause' - acting according to one's own will. Related compounds: 不自由(ふじゆう) (inconvenient, disabled), 自由席(じゆうせき) (unreserved seat).
