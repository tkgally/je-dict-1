景色(けしき)

けしき
noun
scenery, landscape
1. scenery, landscape, view
The natural features of an area viewed as a whole.
山(やま)からの景色(けしき)がきれいだった。
The scenery from the mountain was beautiful.
窓(まど)から海(うみ)の景色(けしき)が見(み)える。
I can see the ocean view from the window.
秋(あき)の景色(けしき)がとてもきれいだ。
The autumn scenery is very beautiful.
景色(けしき)のいい場所(ばしょ)でお弁当(べんとう)を食(た)べた。
We ate lunch in a place with a nice view.
電車(でんしゃ)から見(み)る景色(けしき)が好(す)きだ。
I like the scenery seen from the train.

景色(けしき) refers to the visual appearance of a place, especially natural scenery viewed as a whole.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 景色(けしき)がいい: the scenery is nice
  • 景色(けしき)を楽(たの)しむ: to enjoy the scenery
  • 景色(けしき)が変(か)わる: the scenery changes
  • 美(うつく)しい景色(けしき): beautiful scenery

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 景色(けしき): scenery, landscape (everyday word)
  • 風景(ふうけい): scenery (more formal/literary, used in art)
  • 眺(なが)め: view (from a specific vantage point)
  • 光景(こうけい): scene, sight (often dramatic or memorable)
