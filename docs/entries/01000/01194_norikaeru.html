1. to transfer, to change (trains, buses)
To get off one vehicle and board another to continue a journey.
東京駅(とうきょうえき)で乗(の)り換(か)えてください。
Please transfer at Tokyo Station.
で marks the transfer point
新幹線(しんかんせん)に乗(の)り換(か)える。
Transfer to the Shinkansen.
に marks what you're transferring to
乗(の)り換(か)えなしで行(い)ける。
You can go there without transferring.
次(つぎ)の駅(えき)で乗(の)り換(か)えてください。
Please transfer at the next station.
ここで地下鉄(ちかてつ)からバスに乗(の)り換(か)える。
I transfer from the subway to the bus here.
Shows transfer between different vehicle types
2. to switch, to change over
To change from one thing to another (e.g., changing providers or methods).
別(べつ)の会社(かいしゃ)に乗(の)り換(か)えた。
I switched to a different company.
新(あたら)しいスマホに乗(の)り換(か)える。
I'm switching to a new smartphone.
電気(でんき)自動車(じどうしゃ)に乗(の)り換(か)えた。
I switched to an electric car.
別(べつ)の携帯(けいたい)会社(かいしゃ)に乗(の)り換(か)える。
I'm changing to a different mobile carrier.
格安(かくやす)のプランに乗(の)り換(か)えた。
I switched to a budget plan.

TRANSITIVITY: Used both ways, so the tag is 'both'. The everyday learner patterns are intransitive — Xで乗(の)り換(か)える (transfer at X, a place) and Xに乗(の)り換(か)える (transfer to X, a new line, vehicle, or service). The transitive Xを乗(の)り換(か)える (電車(でんしゃ)を乗(の)り換(か)える, change trains) is also correct but much less frequent; に is the safer default.

ASPECT: ている describes the resulting state, not an action in progress. 乗(の)り換(か)えている normally means 'has already switched (and is now on the other one)'.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 次(つぎ)の駅(えき)で乗(の)り換(か)える: transfer at the next station
  • 新幹線(しんかんせん)に乗(の)り換(か)える: transfer to the Shinkansen
  • 乗(の)り換(か)え: the noun, a transfer
  • 乗(の)り換(か)え案内(あんない): transfer guidance (station signage and apps)
  • 乗(の)り換(か)え駅(えき): a transfer station

SENSE 2 — SWITCHING PROVIDERS: The same verb covers switching phone carrier, electricity supplier, insurance plan, or car, and by extension switching allegiance or jobs. Japanese advertising uses it heavily, so a learner meets sense 2 as often as sense 1. It is neutral in tone, though used of a person changing romantic partners it is pointedly critical.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE: 乗(の)る (to ride) + 換(か)える (to exchange) — literally 'ride and swap'.

SIMILAR VERBS AND TRAVEL VOCABULARY:

  • 直通(ちょくつう): a direct, no-transfer service — the opposite situation
  • 乗(の)り換(か)えなし: 'no transfers', the phrase used in route search results
