すり

すり
noun
pickpocket
1. pickpocket
A person who steals from others' pockets or bags in public places.
すりに気(き)をつけてください。
Please watch out for pickpockets.
Common warning
電車(でんしゃ)ですりに遭(あ)った。
I was pickpocketed on the train.
すりが捕(つか)まった。
The pickpocket was caught.

すり is written in hiragana. The kanji 掏摸 exists but is rarely used.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • すりに遭(あ)う: to be pickpocketed
  • 財布(さいふ)を盗(ぬす)まれる: to have one's wallet stolen
