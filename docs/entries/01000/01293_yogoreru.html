汚れる

よごれる
verb
to become dirty; to get stained
1. to become dirty; to get stained
For something to become soiled or unclean.
服(ふく)が汚(よご)れてしまった。
My clothes got dirty.
手(て)が汚(よご)れているよ。
Your hands are dirty.
汚れている = state of being dirty
白(しろ)い靴(くつ)はすぐ汚(よご)れる。
White shoes get dirty easily.

汚れる is an intransitive verb describing something becoming dirty on its own.

TRANSITIVITY PAIR:

  • 汚(よご)れる (intransitive): to become dirty
  • 汚(よご)す (transitive): to make dirty, to soil

TE-IRU FORM:

  • 汚れている: is dirty (resultant state)
