お酒(さけ)

おさけ
noun
alcohol, sake
1. alcohol, alcoholic drinks
A general term for alcoholic beverages. The お is an honorific prefix.
2. sake, Japanese rice wine
Specifically refers to nihonshu, the traditional Japanese rice-based alcoholic drink.
お酒(さけ)は飲(の)みますか。
Do you drink alcohol?
お酒(さけ)は二十歳(はたち)からです。
You can drink alcohol from age 20.
Legal drinking age in Japan
日本(にほん)のお酒(さけ)を飲(の)んでみてください。
Please try Japanese sake.

お酒(さけ) has two meanings: (1) alcohol in general (beer, wine, etc.) and (2) specifically Japanese sake (nihonshu). Context usually makes the meaning clear. 日本酒(にほんしゅ) specifically refers to sake. The legal drinking age in Japan is 20.
