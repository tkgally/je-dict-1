入(い)れる

いれる
verb (ichidan)
to put in, to insert
1. to put in, to insert
To place something inside a container, space, or location. The opposite of 出す (to take out).
コーヒーに砂糖(さとう)を入(い)れますか。
Do you take sugar in your coffee?
Literally: Do you put sugar in your coffee?
鍵(かぎ)をかばんに入(い)れた。
I put the key in my bag.
お風呂(ふろ)を入(い)れてください。
Please draw a bath.
Common idiomatic usage
電源(でんげん)を入(い)れてもいいですか。
May I turn on the power?
Idiomatic: 電源を入れる means to turn on
冷蔵庫(れいぞうこ)に入(い)れておいてください。
Please put it in the refrigerator.
Sense 1: to put in
パスワードを入(い)れてください。
Please enter your password.
Sense 1: to input
力(ちから)を入(い)れて押(お)してください。
Please push with force.
Sense 1: to put (force)
2. to include, to add
To include someone or something as part of a group or mixture.
私(わたし)もチームに入(い)れてください。
Please include me in the team too.
名前(なまえ)をリストに入(い)れた。
I added the name to the list.
Sense 2: to include
予算(よさん)に入(い)れておきましょう。
Let's include it in the budget.
Sense 2: to include
今度(こんど)の旅行(りょこう)の計画(けいかく)に私(わたし)も入(い)れてほしい。
I want to be included in the travel plans too.
Sense 2: to include
次(つぎ)の試合(しあい)には彼(かれ)もチームに入(い)れよう。
Let's include him on the team for the next game.
Sense 2: to include

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 入(はい)る (intransitive, to enter). Pattern: XをYに入(い)れる (put X into Y).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 入(い)れている = 'have put in (and it's still in there)' or 'is putting in.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 砂糖(さとう)を入(い)れる (add sugar)
  • かばんに入(い)れる (put in a bag)
  • お風呂(ふろ)を入(い)れる (draw a bath)
  • 電源(でんげん)を入(い)れる (turn on power)

Opposite: 出(だ)す (to take out).
