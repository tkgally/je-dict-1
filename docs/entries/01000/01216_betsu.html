別(べつ)

べつ
adjective (na-adjective)
different; separate; another

Definitions

1. different, another
Describes something that is not the same as what was mentioned.
2. separate, apart
Describes things that are kept or considered separately.
3. special, particular
Used in certain expressions to mean 'not particularly' or 'nothing special.'

Examples

別(べつ)の方法(ほうほう)を考(かんが)えましょう。
Let's think of a different method.
Modifier usage
それは別(べつ)の話(はなし)です。
That's a different matter.
会計(かいけい)は別(べつ)にしてください。
Please make the bills separate.
Adverbial usage
男女(だんじょ)別(べつ)のトイレ
Separate restrooms for men and women
Suffix usage meaning 'separated by'
別(べつ)に構(かま)いません。
I don't particularly mind.
別(べつ)に + negative = not particularly

Notes

別(べつ) is a na-adjective meaning 'different,' 'separate,' or 'another.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 別(べつ)に
  • Modifier: 別(べつ)の/な + noun (の is more common)

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 別(べつ)ではない / 別(べつ)じゃない
  • Te-form: 別(べつ)で
  • Past: 別(べつ)だった

Special usage - 別(べつ)に:

  • 別(べつ)に + negative verb: not particularly, not especially
  • 別(べつ)に欲(ほ)しくない (I don't particularly want it)
  • 別(べつ)に何(なに)も (nothing in particular)
  • Can also mean 'separately': 別(べつ)に払(はら)う (to pay separately)

Note: 別(べつ)の is more common than 別(べつ)な when modifying nouns.

As suffix: 〜別(べつ) indicates categorization: 年齢(ねんれい)別(べつ) (by age), 国(くに)別(べつ) (by country).
