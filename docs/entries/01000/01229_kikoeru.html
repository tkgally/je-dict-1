聞(き)こえる

きこえる
verb (ichidan)
to be heard, to be audible
1. to be heard, to be audible
For a sound to reach one's ears and be perceivable. The intransitive/potential form of hearing.
鳥(とり)の声(こえ)が聞(き)こえる。
I can hear birds singing.
が marks what is heard
隣(となり)の部屋(へや)から音楽(おんがく)が聞(き)こえてきた。
Music came from the next room (could be heard).
聞(き)こえてくる = come to be heard
2. to sound like, to seem
To give the impression of a certain quality when heard.
それは言(い)い訳(わけ)に聞(き)こえる。
That sounds like an excuse.
'Sound like' meaning

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). This is the spontaneous/potential form related to 聞(き)く (to hear/listen). Pattern: Xが聞(き)こえる (X can be heard).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state. 聞(き)こえている = 'can be heard' (continuous).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 声(こえ)が聞(き)こえる (a voice can be heard)
  • 音(おと)が聞(き)こえる (a sound can be heard)
  • よく聞(き)こえない (can't hear well)
  • 聞(き)こえてくる (come to be heard, start hearing)
  • 〜に聞(き)こえる (sound like ~)

CONTRAST:

  • 聞(き)く: active listening/asking (transitive)
  • 聞(き)こえる: passive hearing, audibility (intransitive)

RELATED PAIR: 見(み)る → 見(み)える (see → be visible) follows the same pattern.
