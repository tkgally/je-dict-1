うかがう

うかがう
verb (godan)
to ask (humble), to visit (humble)

Definitions

1. to ask, to inquire (humble)
Humble form of 聞(き)く (to ask). Used when politely asking someone of higher status.
2. to visit (humble)
Humble form of 訪(たず)ねる or 行(い)く. Used when humbly stating you will visit someone's place.
3. to hear (humble)
Humble form of 聞(き)く (to hear). Used when politely saying you heard something.

Examples

ちょっとうかがいたいのですが。
I'd like to ask you something.
Very polite way to start a question
明日(あした)、お宅(たく)にうかがいます。
I will visit your home tomorrow.
Humble way to say 'I will come to your place'
お名前(なまえ)をうかがってもよろしいですか。
May I ask your name?
Polite request for information

Notes

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xをうかがう / Xにうかがう.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing or completed action. うかがっている = 'have heard/asked.'

KEIGO (HUMBLE): This verb is 謙譲語(けんじょうご) (humble language). Use when:

  • Asking someone of higher status
  • Visiting someone's home or office
  • Referring to information you received from someone respected

EQUIVALENTS:

  • うかがう (humble) ↔ 聞(き)く (neutral) ↔ お聞(き)きになる (honorific)
  • うかがう (humble) ↔ 訪(たず)ねる/行(い)く (neutral) ↔ いらっしゃる (honorific)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • うかがいたいのですが (I'd like to ask...)
  • うかがってもよろしいですか (May I ask...)
  • お話(はなし)をうかがう (hear what someone has to say)

WRITING: Can be written as 伺(うかが)う but hiragana is common.
