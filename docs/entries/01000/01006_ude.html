腕(うで)

うで
noun
arm, skill
1. arm
The upper limb of the body from shoulder to hand.
2. skill, ability
One's ability or skill at something, especially a craft or profession.
腕(うで)を上(あ)げてください。
Please raise your arm.
彼(かれ)は料理(りょうり)の腕(うで)がいい。
He has good cooking skills.
Skill meaning
腕(うで)時計(どけい)をしています。
I'm wearing a wristwatch.
腕時計 = wristwatch

腕(うで) refers to the entire arm, not just a part of it. It's also commonly used to mean 'skill' or 'ability,' especially in contexts of craftsmanship: 腕(うで)がいい (skilled), 腕(うで)を磨(みが)く (to improve one's skills). 腕(うで)時計(どけい) is a wristwatch, 腕(うで)組(ぐ)み is folding one's arms.
