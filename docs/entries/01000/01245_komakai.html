細(こま)かい

こまかい
adjective (i-adjective)
small; fine; detailed; meticulous
1. small, fine (in size/particles)
Describes things that are small in size or fine in texture.
細(こま)かい砂(すな)が足(あし)についた。
Fine sand stuck to my feet.
Physical size/texture
2. detailed, minute
Describes something done with great attention to detail.
細(こま)かいところまで説明(せつめい)してください。
Please explain even the small details.
3. small change (money)
Refers to coins or small denominations of money.
細(こま)かいお金(かね)がありますか。
Do you have small change?
Money meaning
4. meticulous, fussy, particular
Describes someone who pays close attention to small details, sometimes excessively.
彼(かれ)は細(こま)かいことを気(き)にしすぎる。
He worries too much about small things.
Personality trait

細(こま)かい is an i-adjective meaning 'small,' 'fine,' or 'detailed.'

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 細(こま)かく
  • Noun form: 細(こま)かさ

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 細(こま)かくない
  • Te-form: 細(こま)かくて
  • Past: 細(こま)かかった

Similar words:

  • 細(こま)かい vs. 小(ちい)さい: 細(こま)かい emphasizes fineness or detail, while 小(ちい)さい simply means 'small' in size.
  • 細(こま)かい vs. 詳(くわ)しい: 詳(くわ)しい means 'detailed' in terms of information/knowledge; 細(こま)かい refers to small physical details or meticulous behavior.

Common collocations: 細(こま)かい字(じ) (small writing), 細(こま)かいお金(かね) (small change), 細(こま)かい作業(さぎょう) (detailed work).
