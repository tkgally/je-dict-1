お礼(れい)

おれい
noun
thanks; gratitude; reward
1. thanks, gratitude, appreciation
Expression of thankfulness for something received or done.
2. reward, return gift
Something given in return for a favor or service.
お礼(れい)を言(い)いたいです。
I want to express my thanks.
お礼(れい)の手紙(てがみ)を書(か)いた。
I wrote a thank-you letter.
お礼(れい)に何(なに)かあげたい。
I want to give something as thanks.

お礼(れい) is a polite noun expressing gratitude.

Common expressions:

  • お礼(れい)を言(い)う: to thank, express gratitude
  • お礼(れい)を申(もう)し上(あ)げます: I express my gratitude (very formal)
  • お礼(れい)に: in return, as thanks
  • お礼(れい)の品(しな): thank-you gift

Note: Can also refer to a 'reward' or monetary compensation for a service, but commonly means 'thanks.'
