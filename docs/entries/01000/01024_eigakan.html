映画館(えいがかん)

えいがかん
noun
movie theater
1. movie theater, cinema
A building where films are shown to an audience.
映画館(えいがかん)で新(あたら)しい映画(えいが)を見(み)ました。
I watched a new movie at the theater.
この映画館(えいがかん)は音(おと)がいいです。
This movie theater has good sound.
映画館(えいがかん)のチケット(ちけっと)を予約(よやく)しました。
I reserved movie theater tickets.

映画館(えいがかん) is a compound of 映画(えいが) (movie) + 館(かん) (hall/building).

ALTERNATIVE WORDS:

  • シネマ: cinema (loanword)
  • シアター: theater (loanword)
  • 劇場(げきじょう): theater (formal)

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 映画館(えいがかん)に行(い)く: to go to the movies
  • 映画館(えいがかん)で見(み)る: to watch at the theater
  • 映画館(えいがかん)の席(せき): movie theater seat

JAPAN-SPECIFIC:

  • Tickets often reserved in advance with assigned seats
  • 映画(えいが)の日(ひ) (Movie Day, 1st of each month): discounted tickets
  • Talking during movies is considered very rude
  • Many theaters offer premium options like 4DX, IMAX

OTHER 館(かん) BUILDINGS:

  • 図書館(としょかん): library
  • 美術館(びじゅつかん): art museum
  • 水族館(すいぞくかん): aquarium
