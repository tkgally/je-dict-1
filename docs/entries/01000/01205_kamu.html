噛(か)む

かむ
verb (godan)
to bite, to chew
1. to bite
To use teeth to cut into or grip something.
2. to chew
To grind food with teeth before swallowing.
犬(いぬ)に噛(か)まれた。
I was bitten by a dog.
Passive form 噛(か)まれる
よく噛(か)んで食(た)べてください。
Please chew well before eating.
Common advice about eating
ガムを噛(か)む。
Chew gum.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xを噛(か)む (bite/chew X). No intransitive pair.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. 噛(か)んでいる = 'is biting/chewing.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ガムを噛(か)む (chew gum)
  • よく噛(か)む (chew well)
  • 指(ゆび)を噛(か)む (bite one's finger)
  • 噛(か)みつく (to bite into, attack—compound)
  • 噛(か)み切(き)る (to bite off)

KANJI NOTE: Often written in hiragana (かむ) in everyday contexts. The kanji 噛(か)む is more formal.

IDIOMS:

  • 舌(した)を噛(か)む (bite one's tongue; also: stumble over words)
  • 歯(は)を食(く)いしばる (grit one's teeth—different expression for similar context)
