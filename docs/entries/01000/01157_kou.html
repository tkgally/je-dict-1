こう

こう
adverb
this way, like this

Definitions

1. this way, like this, in this manner
Refers to a manner or method close to the speaker or being demonstrated. Part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system.

Examples

こうしてください。
Please do it like this.
Often used when demonstrating
こう書(か)きます。
You write it like this.
こう言(い)えばいいですか。
Should I say it like this?

Notes

こう belongs to the ko-so-a-do system: こう (this way, near speaker), そう (that way, near listener), ああ (that way, far from both). Used when demonstrating or explaining how to do something.

Related:

  • このように (in this way)
  • こうやって (like this, while doing this).
