見(み)つける

みつける
verb (ichidan)
to find, to discover
1. to find, to discover
To locate something that was being looked for or something previously unknown. The transitive form.
2. to catch, to spot
To notice or discover someone doing something.
鍵(かぎ)を見(み)つけた。
I found the key.
やっといい}{店(みせ)を見(み)つけた。
I finally found a good restaurant.
彼(かれ)が隠(かく)れているのを見(み)つけた。
I spotted him hiding.
'Catch/spot' meaning

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 見(み)つかる (intransitive, to be found). Pattern: Xを見(み)つける (find X).

ASPECT: ている indicates completed action. 見(み)つけている = 'has found.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • Xを見(み)つける (find X)
  • 答(こた)えを見(み)つける (find an answer)
  • 仕事(しごと)を見(み)つける (find a job)
  • 見(み)つけ出(だ)す (find out, discover)

CONTRAST:

  • 見(み)つける (transitive): find X
  • 見(み)つかる (intransitive): X is found

SIMILAR VERBS:

  • 探(さが)す: to look for, search (the searching process)
  • 見(み)つける: to find (successful result of searching)
