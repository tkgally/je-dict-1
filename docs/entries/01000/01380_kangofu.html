看護婦(かんごふ)

かんごふ
noun
(female) nurse
1. (female) nurse
A traditional term for a female nurse. Note: This term is now considered dated; the gender-neutral term 看護師(かんごし) is now the standard professional title.
看護婦(かんごふ)さんが注射(ちゅうしゃ)をしてくれた。
The nurse gave me an injection.
姉(あね)は病院(びょういん)で看護婦(かんごふ)として働(はたら)いています。
My older sister works as a nurse at a hospital.
看護婦(かんごふ)さんを呼(よ)んでください。
Please call the nurse.

This term specifically refers to female nurses. Modern Japanese prefers the gender-neutral 看護師(かんごし) (kangoshi), which is now the official professional term used in healthcare settings. You may still encounter 看護婦(かんごふ) in older texts or informal speech.
