マンション

まんしょん
noun
condominium
1. condominium, apartment building
A larger, modern concrete apartment building, often owner-occupied. Despite coming from English 'mansion,' it doesn't mean a large house.
新(あたら)しいマンションを買(か)いました。
I bought a new condominium.
このマンションは10階建(かいだ)てです。
This building is 10 stories tall.
マンションの管理費(かんりひ)は毎月(まいつき)払(はら)います。
I pay the condo management fee every month.

マンション is a 'false friend' from English 'mansion.' In Japan, it refers to a reinforced concrete apartment/condominium building, not a large house. マンション are generally considered higher quality than アパート and are often purchased rather than rented.
