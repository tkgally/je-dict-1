よろしく

よろしく
expression
please, best regards
1. nice to meet you, please treat me well
Used when meeting someone for the first time, often after はじめまして. Full form is よろしくお願(ねが)いします.
2. please (do something for me)
Used when asking someone to do something or take care of something.
3. give my regards to
Used when asking someone to pass along greetings.
はじめまして。田中(たなか)です。よろしく。
Nice to meet you. I'm Tanaka. Pleased to meet you.
Casual introduction
よろしくお願(ねが)いします。
Thank you in advance. / Pleased to meet you.
Polite form
山田(やまだ)さんによろしく伝(つた)えてください。
Please give my regards to Mr. Yamada.

よろしく is an extremely versatile expression. It comes from the adjective よろしい (good, fine). The polite form よろしくお願いします is essential for business and formal situations. It can be used when meeting someone, ending an email, or asking for a favor.
