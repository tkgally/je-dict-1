握(にぎ)る

にぎる
godan verb
to grasp, to grip

Definitions

1. to grasp, to grip
To hold firmly in the hand.
2. to make (sushi)
To mold sushi rice by hand.
3. to hold (power)
To have control over something.

Examples

ハンドルを握る。
To grip the steering wheel.
寿司(すし)を握る。
To make sushi.
権力(けんりょく)を握る。
To hold power.

Notes

握る has both literal and figurative uses.

SUSHI TERM:

  • 握(にぎ)り寿司(ずし): hand-pressed sushi

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 拳(こぶし)を握る: to clench one's fist
  • 秘密(ひみつ)を握る: to hold a secret
