ナイフ

ないふ
noun
knife
1. knife
A cutting tool with a blade, used for eating or cutting. From English 'knife.'
ナイフとフォークを使(つか)います。
I use a knife and fork.
ナイフで肉(にく)を切(き)ってください。
Please cut the meat with a knife.
このナイフはよく切(き)れます。
This knife cuts well.

ナイフ is from English 'knife' and typically refers to eating utensils. For kitchen knives, 包丁 (ほうちょう) is the more common term. When eating Western food in Japan, ナイフ and フォーク are used.
