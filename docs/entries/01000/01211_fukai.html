深(ふか)い

ふかい
adjective (i-adjective)
deep; profound
Conjugation

1. deep (physical depth)
Describes something with great depth, such as water, a hole, or snow.
このプールは深(ふか)いから、気(き)をつけて。
This pool is deep, so be careful.
Physical depth
この川(かわ)は深(ふか)い。
This river is deep.
雪(ゆき)が深(ふか)くて歩(ある)けない。
The snow is too deep to walk.
穴(あな)は思(おも)ったより深(ふか)かった。
The hole was deeper than I thought.
深(ふか)い傷(きず)を負(お)った。
I suffered a deep wound.
2. profound, deep (abstract)
Describes deep meaning, understanding, relationships, or feelings.
深(ふか)い意味(いみ)がある。
It has a deep/profound meaning.
Abstract meaning
二人(ふたり)は深(ふか)い関係(かんけい)にある。
The two have a deep relationship.
深(ふか)い愛情(あいじょう)を感(かん)じる。
I feel deep affection.
この問題(もんだい)は深(ふか)い。
This problem is deep/complex.
深(ふか)い知識(ちしき)を持(も)っている。
They have profound knowledge.
深(ふか)い眠(ねむ)りについた。
I fell into a deep sleep.
深(ふか)い感謝(かんしゃ)の気持(きも)ちを伝(つた)えたい。
I want to express my deep gratitude.
3. dense, thick
Describes dense fog, thick forest, or dark/rich colors.
霧(きり)が深(ふか)い朝(あさ)だった。
It was a morning with thick fog.
Dense/thick meaning
深(ふか)い森(もり)に迷(まよ)い込(こ)んだ。
I got lost in a dense forest.
深(ふか)い緑(みどり)の葉(は)っぱ。
Leaves of a deep green color.
深(ふか)い青(あお)の海(うみ)。
A sea of deep blue.
深(ふか)い闇(やみ)に包(つつ)まれた。
It was enveloped in deep darkness.
4. late (time of night)
Describes late hours of the night.
夜(よる)深(ふか)くまで勉強(べんきょう)した。
I studied until late at night.
Time expression
夜(よる)が深(ふか)くなった。
The night grew late.
深夜(しんや)まで働(はたら)いた。
I worked until late at night.
夜(よる)深(ふか)くに電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)った。
The phone rang late at night.
深(ふか)い夜(よる)の静(しず)けさの中(なか)で本(ほん)を読(よ)んでいた。
I was reading a book in the stillness of the late night.

USAGE:
深(ふか)い covers a wide span of meanings that English splits between 'deep,' 'profound,' 'thick/dense,' and 'late.' The common thread is extent measured inward or downward rather than across. It works both as a noun modifier (深(ふか)い川(かわ)) and as a predicate (川(かわ)が深(ふか)い).

  • Physical depth: water, holes, snow, wounds
  • Abstract profundity: meaning, knowledge, relationships, emotions
  • Density or richness: fog, forest, colors
  • Late night: 夜(よる)が深(ふか)い, 深(ふか)い夜(よる)

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 深(ふか)く (深(ふか)く考(かんが)える, to think deeply)
  • Noun of measurement: 深(ふか)さ (depth as a quantity)
  • Noun of quality: 深(ふか)み (depth in the sense of richness or a deep spot)
  • Paired verbs: 深(ふか)まる (to deepen, intransitive), 深(ふか)める (to deepen, transitive)

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 深(ふか)い意味(いみ): a deep meaning
  • 深(ふか)い関係(かんけい): a close, deep relationship
  • 深(ふか)い眠(ねむ)り: a deep sleep
  • 深(ふか)い感謝(かんしゃ): deep gratitude
  • 霧(きり)が深(ふか)い: the fog is thick
  • 深(ふか)い森(もり): a dense forest

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES:

  • 浅(あさ)い: the direct antonym, shallow in both the physical and the abstract sense
  • 濃(こ)い: also 'thick, dense,' but about concentration (strong tea, dark makeup); for colors, 深(ふか)い suggests richness while 濃(こ)い suggests intensity
  • 奥深(おくぶか)い: profound only, never physical depth downward

REGISTER:
Neutral and usable at any level of politeness. The physical senses are everyday speech; the abstract senses (深(ふか)い洞察(どうさつ), deep insight) are more frequent in writing and formal speech.
