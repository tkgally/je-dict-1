ご覧(らん)

ごらん
noun
looking, seeing (honorific)
1. looking, seeing (honorific)
Honorific form of 見る, used when respectfully referring to someone looking at something.
どうぞご覧(らん)ください。
Please have a look.
こちらをご覧(らん)になりましたか。
Have you seen this?
ご覧(らん)のとおり、問題(もんだい)はありません。
As you can see, there are no problems.
こちらの写真(しゃしん)をご覧(らん)になってください。とてもきれいですよ。
Please take a look at these photos. They're very beautiful.
新(あたら)しい商品(しょうひん)のカタログをご覧(らん)いただければ、詳(くわ)しいことが分(わ)かります。
If you take a look at our new product catalog, you'll find the details.

ご覧(らん) is the honorific (尊敬語(そんけいご)) form of 見(み)る, used to respectfully refer to someone looking or seeing.

KEY PATTERNS:

  • ご覧(らん)ください: please look (polite request)
  • ご覧(らん)になる: to look/see (honorific verb form)
  • ご覧(らん)いただく: to have someone look (謙譲語(けんじょうご))
  • ご覧(らん)のとおり: as you can see
  • ご覧(らん)の通(とお)り: as you can see (alternate writing)

USAGE NOTES:
Used in formal situations, customer service, and business contexts. ご覧(らん)ください is softer than 見(み)てください. Often heard in stores, museums, and presentations.

RELATED:

  • 拝見(はいけん)する: to look/see (humble form, used for oneself)

Related Words
