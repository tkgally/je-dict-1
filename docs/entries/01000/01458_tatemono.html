建物(たてもの)

たてもの
noun
building

Definitions

1. building, structure
A structure with a roof and walls.

Examples

この建物(たてもの)は古(ふる)い。
This building is old.
新(あたら)しい建物(たてもの)ができた。
A new building was constructed.

Notes

General term for any building. More specific terms include: ビル} (office building), {家(いえ) (house), {アパート} (apartment building).
