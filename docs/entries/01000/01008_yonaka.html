夜中(よなか)

よなか
noun, adverb
middle of the night
1. middle of the night, midnight
The late night hours, typically from around midnight to early morning when most people are asleep.
夜中(よなか)に目(め)が覚(さ)めました。
I woke up in the middle of the night.
夜中(よなか)に電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)りました。
The phone rang in the middle of the night.
夜中(よなか)まで勉強(べんきょう)しました。
I studied until the middle of the night.

夜中(よなか) refers to the deep night hours when most people are sleeping. It's later than 夜(よる) (night) and typically suggests the hours around and after midnight. Use 夜中(よなか)に for 'in the middle of the night' and 夜中(よなか)まで for 'until the middle of the night.'
