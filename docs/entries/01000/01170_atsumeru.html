集(あつ)める

あつめる
verb (ichidan)
to collect, to gather
1. to collect, to gather (things or people)
To bring things or people together in one place. This is the transitive form—someone actively collects or gathers something.
2. to attract, to draw (attention, interest)
To draw or attract something abstract like attention or interest toward oneself or something.
切手(きって)を集(あつ)めています。
I collect stamps.
を marks the direct object being collected
先生(せんせい)は生徒(せいと)を教室(きょうしつ)に集(あつ)めました。
The teacher gathered the students in the classroom.
この映画(えいが)は世界中(せかいじゅう)の注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)めている。
This movie is attracting attention from around the world.
注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)める is a common collocation

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 集(あつ)まる (intransitive, to gather). Pattern: Xを集(あつ)める (collect X).

ASPECT: ている can indicate ongoing action or resulting state. 集(あつ)めている = 'is collecting' (ongoing) or 'has collected' (resulting state with accumulated items).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 切手(きって)を集(あつ)める (collect stamps)
  • 情報(じょうほう)を集(あつ)める (gather information)
  • 注目(ちゅうもく)を集(あつ)める (attract attention)
  • 人気(にんき)を集(あつ)める (gain popularity)
  • 資料(しりょう)を集(あつ)める (collect materials/documents)

REGISTER: Neutral. Used in both casual and formal contexts.
