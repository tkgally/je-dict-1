そう

そう
adverb
so, that way, like that

Definitions

1. so, that way, like that
Refers to a manner or state that has been mentioned or is understood from context. Part of the ko-so-a-do demonstrative system.
2. I see, is that so
Used as a response to indicate understanding or acknowledgment.

Examples

そう思(おも)います。
I think so.
そうですか。
Is that so? / I see.
Common response showing understanding
そうしましょう。
Let's do that.

Notes

そう belongs to the ko-so-a-do system: こう (this way), そう (that way, near listener), ああ (that way, far from both). It refers to things mentioned or known from context.

Common expressions:

  • そうですね (That's right / Let me see)
  • そうですか (Is that so?)
  • そうそう (Yes, yes / That's right).
