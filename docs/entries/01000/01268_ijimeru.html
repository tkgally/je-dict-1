いじめる

いじめる
verb (ichidan)
to bully, to torment
1. to bully, to pick on
To repeatedly mistreat or harass someone weaker. A serious social issue in Japanese schools and workplaces.
弱(よわ)い者(もの)をいじめてはいけない。
You must not bully those who are weak.
を marks the victim
学校(がっこう)でいじめられている。
I'm being bullied at school.
Passive form いじめられる
動物(どうぶつ)をいじめないで。
Don't torment animals.
2. to torment, to tease cruelly
To cause suffering through persistent unkind treatment.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pattern: Xをいじめる (bully X). No intransitive pair.

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing action. いじめている = 'is bullying.' いじめられている = 'is being bullied.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 子供(こども)をいじめる (bully a child)
  • いじめられる (be bullied—passive)
  • いじめっ子(こ) (bully—noun, the one who bullies)
  • いじめ問題(もんだい) (bullying problem)

WRITING: Usually written in hiragana. Can be written as 苛(いじ)める but hiragana is more common.

CULTURAL NOTE: いじめ (bullying) is a recognized social problem in Japan, particularly in schools. The noun form いじめ is often used in news and discussions about this issue.

REGISTER: Neutral, but the topic itself is serious.
