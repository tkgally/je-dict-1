質(しつ)

しつ
noun
quality
1. quality, nature, character
The standard of something when compared to other things.
この製品(せいひん)は質(しつ)が良(い)い。
This product is of good quality.
量(りょう)より質(しつ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Quality is more important than quantity.
睡眠(すいみん)の質(しつ)を上(あ)げたい。
I want to improve the quality of my sleep.

質(しつ) refers to quality, nature, or character of something.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 質(しつ)が良(い)い/高(たか)い: good/high quality
  • 質(しつ)が悪(わる)い/低(ひく)い: poor/low quality
  • 質(しつ)を上(あ)げる: to improve quality
  • 質(しつ)を保(たも)つ: to maintain quality

COMMON COMPOUNDS:

  • 品質(ひんしつ): product quality
  • 性質(せいしつ): nature, character
  • 体質(たいしつ): constitution, physique
  • 質問(しつもん): question

CONTRAST:

  • 量(りょう)より質(しつ): quality over quantity
  • 質(しつ)より量(りょう): quantity over quality
