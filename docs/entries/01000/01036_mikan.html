みかん

みかん
noun
mandarin orange
1. mandarin orange, tangerine
A small, sweet citrus fruit with easy-to-peel skin. A winter staple in Japan.
こたつでみかんを食(た)べます。
I eat mandarin oranges under the kotatsu.
A typical winter scene in Japan
みかんを三個(さんこ)買(か)いました。
I bought three mandarin oranges.
このみかんは甘(あま)いです。
This mandarin orange is sweet.
冬(ふゆ)になると、スーパーにはたくさんのみかんが並(なら)びます。
When winter comes, many mandarin oranges are displayed at the supermarket.
みかんの皮(かわ)をむくのは簡単(かんたん)なので、子(こ)どもでも一人(ひとり)で食(た)べられます。
Peeling a mandarin orange is easy, so even children can eat them by themselves.

みかん (also written 蜜柑(みかん)) is the most common citrus fruit in Japan.

CULTURE:
Eating みかん while warming up under a こたつ (heated table) is a classic Japanese winter tradition. They are in season from November to February. 愛媛県(えひめけん) and 和歌山県(わかやまけん) are famous production areas.

COUNTING:
みかんは一個(いっこ)、二個(にこ)... (uses the general counter 個(こ))

VARIETIES:

  • 温州(うんしゅう)みかん: satsuma mandarin (most common)
  • ポンカン: ponkan
  • デコポン: dekopon (sweet variety)

RELATED:

  • 柑橘類(かんきつるい): citrus fruits
  • 皮(かわ)をむく: to peel
  • 薄皮(うすかわ): thin inner skin

Related Words

Contrast: オレンジ (orange (Western citrus; also the color))
