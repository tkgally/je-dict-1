みかん

みかん
noun
mandarin orange
1. mandarin orange, tangerine
A small, sweet citrus fruit with easy-to-peel skin. A winter staple in Japan.
こたつでみかんを食(た)べます。
I eat mandarin oranges under the kotatsu.
A typical winter scene in Japan
みかんを三個(さんこ)買(か)いました。
I bought three mandarin oranges.
このみかんは甘(あま)いです。
This mandarin orange is sweet.

みかん (also written 蜜柑) is the most common citrus fruit in Japan. Eating みかん while warming up under a こたつ (heated table) is a classic Japanese winter tradition. They are in season from November to February.
