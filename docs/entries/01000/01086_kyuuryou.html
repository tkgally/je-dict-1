給料(きゅうりょう)

きゅうりょう
noun
salary
1. salary, pay, wages
Money paid regularly to an employee for their work.
給料(きゅうりょう)は毎月(まいつき)25日(にち)に出(で)ます。
Salary is paid on the 25th of every month.
給料(きゅうりょう)が上(あ)がりました。
My salary went up.
給料(きゅうりょう)だけでは生活(せいかつ)が大変(たいへん)です。
It's hard to live on just my salary.

給料 is the general term for salary or wages. 月給 (げっきゅう) means monthly salary, and 年収 (ねんしゅう) means annual income. 給料日 (きゅうりょうび) is payday. In Japan, many companies pay on the 25th of each month.
