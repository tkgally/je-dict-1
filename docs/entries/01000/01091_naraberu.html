並(なら)べる

ならべる
verb (ichidan)
to line up, to arrange
1. to line up, to arrange
To place things in a row or arrange them in an orderly manner. This is the transitive verb - you line something up.
本(ほん)を棚(たな)に並(なら)べてください。
Please arrange the books on the shelf.
椅子(いす)をきれいに並(なら)べた。
I lined up the chairs neatly.
2. to display, to set out
To arrange items for display, such as merchandise in a store.
お皿(さら)をテーブルに並(なら)べましょう。
Let's set out the plates on the table.

TRANSITIVITY: 他動詞(たどうし) (transitive). Pair: 並(なら)ぶ (intransitive). Pattern: Xを並(なら)べる (line up X).

ASPECT: ている indicates resulting state. 並(なら)べている = 'have lined up' or 'is arranging.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 本(ほん)を並(なら)べる (arrange books)
  • 椅子(いす)を並(なら)べる (line up chairs)
  • お皿(さら)を並(なら)べる (set out plates)
  • きれいに並(なら)べる (arrange neatly)

PAIR: 並(なら)ぶ (intransitive) - 人(ひと)が並(なら)んでいる (people are lined up).
