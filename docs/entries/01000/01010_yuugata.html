夕方(ゆうがた)

ゆうがた
noun, adverb
evening, dusk
1. evening, dusk, late afternoon
The time period in the late afternoon when the sun begins to set, roughly from 4-7 PM.
夕方(ゆうがた)になった。
It became evening.
夕方(ゆうがた)に帰(かえ)ります。
I'll come home in the evening.
夕方(ゆうがた)の空(そら)がきれいです。
The evening sky is beautiful.
夕方(ゆうがた)五(ご)時(じ)ごろ会(あ)いましょう。
Let's meet around 5 PM in the evening.
夕方(ゆうがた)になると外(そと)が涼(すず)しくなるので、散歩(さんぽ)に出(で)かけます。
When evening comes, it gets cool outside, so I go out for a walk.

夕方(ゆうがた) refers to evening or dusk—the time when the sun sets, roughly 4-7 PM.

COMPONENTS:
夕(ゆう) (evening) + 方(がた) (direction/time) = evening time

TIME SEQUENCE:

  • 朝(あさ): morning
  • 昼(ひる): noon/daytime
  • 夕方(ゆうがた): evening/dusk
  • 夜(よる): night

RELATED 夕(ゆう) WORDS:

  • 夕食(ゆうしょく): dinner
  • 夕日(ゆうひ): sunset, setting sun
  • 夕焼(ゆうや)け: evening glow
  • 夕刊(ゆうかん): evening newspaper
  • 夕暮(ゆうぐ)れ: twilight, dusk

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 夕方(ゆうがた)に帰(かえ)る: to return home in the evening
  • 夕方(ゆうがた)になる: evening comes
  • 夕方(ゆうがた)から: from evening

夕方(ゆうがた) often signals the end of work and commuting home time.
