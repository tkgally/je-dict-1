よろしい

よろしい
adjective (i-adjective)
(formal/honorific) good; all right; acceptable

Definitions

1. good, fine, all right (honorific)
Polite/formal form of いい. Used to grant permission or express approval.
2. acceptable, satisfactory
Used to confirm that something meets standards or is acceptable.

Examples

これでよろしいですか。
Is this all right? / Will this do?
Polite confirmation
入(はい)ってもよろしいですか。
May I come in?
Asking permission formally
はい、よろしいです。
Yes, that's fine.
Granting permission
ご注文(ちゅうもん)はこれでよろしいでしょうか。
Is this your complete order? / Is this correct for your order?
Service industry usage

Notes

よろしい is an i-adjective that serves as the formal/honorific equivalent of いい (good).

Forms:

  • Adverbial: よろしく (also used in greetings)
  • Note: Noun form よろしさ is not commonly used

Conjugation:

  • Negative: よろしくない
  • Te-form: よろしくて
  • Past: よろしかった

[Register: Formal/Honorific]

Similar words:

  • よろしい vs. いい: よろしい is more formal and polite. いい is neutral/casual.

Note: よろしく is frequently used in the set phrase よろしくお願(ねが)いします (Please treat me favorably / Nice to meet you).

Orthography: Usually written in hiragana. The kanji 宜(よろ)しい exists but is rarely used.
