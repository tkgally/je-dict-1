急行(きゅうこう)

きゅうこう
noun
express (train/bus)

Definitions

1. express train or bus
A train or bus that skips some stops to provide faster service.

Examples

急行(きゅうこう)で行(い)けば早(はや)く着(つ)きます。
If you take the express, you'll arrive quickly.
この駅(えき)には急行(きゅうこう)は止(と)まりません。
The express doesn't stop at this station.

Notes

Japanese train types from slowest to fastest typically include:

  • 普通(ふつう)/各駅停車(かくえきていしゃ) - local (stops at all stations)
  • 快速(かいそく) - rapid
  • 急行(きゅうこう) - express
  • 特急(とっきゅう) - limited express
