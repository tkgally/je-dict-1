失礼(しつれい)

しつれい
adjective (na-adjective)
rude; impolite; discourteous
See also: 失礼(しつれい)する (verb form)
1. rude, impolite, discourteous
Describes behavior or words that lack proper manners or respect.
そんなことを言(い)うのは失礼(しつれい)です。
It's rude to say such things.
Predicate usage
失礼(しつれい)な態度(たいど)を取(と)らないでください。
Please don't take a rude attitude.
Modifier usage
失礼(しつれい)なことを言(い)ってすみません。
I'm sorry for saying something rude.
失礼(しつれい)な人(ひと)だね。
What a rude person.
失礼(しつれい)にあたる。
It would be considered rude.
2. excuse me (set phrase)
Used as a polite phrase when interrupting, leaving, or apologizing for minor inconveniences.
失礼(しつれい)します。
Excuse me. (when entering/leaving)
Set expression for entering/leaving
失礼(しつれい)しました。
I'm sorry. / Excuse me. (apologizing)
Apologizing for rudeness
お先(さき)に失礼(しつれい)します。
Excuse me for leaving before you.
When leaving work/a gathering before others
失礼(しつれい)ですが、お名前(なまえ)は？
Excuse me, but what is your name?
ちょっと失礼(しつれい)。
Excuse me for a moment.

失礼(しつれい) is a na-adjective meaning 'rude' and also functions as a noun/set expression.

Forms:

  • Adverbial: 失礼(しつれい)に (rarely used)
  • Modifier: 失礼(しつれい)な + noun

Conjugation:

  • Negative: 失礼(しつれい)ではない / 失礼(しつれい)じゃない
  • Te-form: 失礼(しつれい)で
  • Past: 失礼(しつれい)だった

Key expressions:

  • 失礼(しつれい)します: Used when entering someone's space, leaving a room, or before doing something that might inconvenience others
  • 失礼(しつれい)しました: Apologizing for past rudeness
  • お先(さき)に失礼(しつれい)します: Said when leaving work before colleagues
  • 失礼(しつれい)ですが...: "Excuse me, but..." (before asking a potentially sensitive question)

USAGE — WHERE YOU HEAR IT:

  • Ending a 電話(でんわ) call to a customer or a 目上(めうえ) person: 失礼(しつれい)します is the standard sign-off, not さようなら.
  • Leaving a 会議(かいぎ) or an office before others: お先(さき)に失礼(しつれい)します.
  • In business writing and formal speech the humble 失礼(しつれい)いたします replaces 失礼(しつれい)します.

SIMILAR ADJECTIVES AND PHRASES:

  • すみません: apologizes for trouble caused; 失礼(しつれい)します announces the act itself and needs no fault
  • ごめんなさい: casual apology between equals or to children; never used as 失礼(しつれい)します is
  • 無礼(ぶれい): rude in a stronger, more formal register — used of serious offense, and never as a set greeting
  • 礼儀(れいぎ): manners, etiquette (the noun for the standard 失礼(しつれい) falls short of)

Related Words

Synonym: 無礼(ぶれい)
Related: 礼儀(れいぎ)
Contrast: すみません
Antonym: 丁寧(ていねい)
