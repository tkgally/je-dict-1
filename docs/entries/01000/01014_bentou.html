弁当(べんとう)

べんとう
noun
boxed lunch
1. boxed lunch, packed lunch
A meal packed in a box, typically containing rice and various side dishes. A key part of Japanese food culture.
弁当(べんとう)を食(た)べる。
To eat a bento.
コンビニで弁当(べんとう)を買(か)いました。
I bought a bento at the convenience store.
母(はは)が弁当(べんとう)を作(つく)ってくれました。
My mother made a bento for me.
毎朝(まいあさ)弁当(べんとう)を作(つく)るのは大変(たいへん)です。
Making a bento every morning is hard work.
新幹線(しんかんせん)に乗(の)る前(まえ)に駅(えき)で弁当(べんとう)を買(か)って、車内(しゃない)で食(た)べました。
Before getting on the bullet train, I bought a bento at the station and ate it on board.

弁当(べんとう) is a fundamental part of Japanese culture. お弁当(べんとう) (with honorific お) is also common. 駅弁(えきべん) are bentos sold at train stations, often featuring local specialties. キャラ弁(べん) are character-themed bentos made for children.
