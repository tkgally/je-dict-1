機会(きかい)

きかい
noun
chance; opportunity; occasion

Definitions

1. opportunity, chance
A favorable time or situation for doing something.
2. occasion
A particular time when something happens.

Examples

良(よ)い機会(きかい)だ。
It's a good opportunity.
機会(きかい)があれば行(い)きたい。
If I have the chance, I'd like to go.
この機会(きかい)に感謝(かんしゃ)します。
I appreciate this opportunity.
Formal
機会(きかい)を逃(のが)した。
I missed the opportunity.

Notes

機会(きかい) means 'opportunity' or 'chance.'

Homophone: 機械(きかい) (machine) - same reading, different meaning!

Common collocations:

  • 機会(きかい)がある: to have an opportunity
  • 機会(きかい)を逃(のが)す: to miss an opportunity
  • 機会(きかい)を利用(りよう)する: to take advantage of an opportunity
  • この機会(きかい)に: on this occasion

Similar words:

  • チャンス: chance (casual)
  • 好機(こうき): favorable opportunity (formal)
