中身(なかみ)

なかみ
noun
contents, interior
1. contents, inside
What is physically contained inside something.
2. substance, essence
The real quality or meaning of something.
箱(はこ)の中身(なかみ)は何(なん)ですか。
What are the contents of the box?
見(み)た目(め)より中身(なかみ)が大切(たいせつ)だ。
Substance is more important than appearance.

Used for both physical contents and figurative substance. Compare with 内容(ないよう) which is more abstract.
