名刺(めいし)

めいし
noun
business card
1. business card
A small card bearing a person's name, job title, company name, and contact information. In Japan, exchanging business cards (名刺交換) is an important ritual in business settings, governed by specific etiquette.
名刺(めいし)を交換(こうかん)しましょう。
Let's exchange business cards.
Common phrase in business settings
名刺(めいし)を忘(わす)れてしまいました。
I forgot my business cards.
An embarrassing situation in Japanese business culture
この名刺(めいし)をどうぞ。
Here is my business card.

名刺(めいし) is a business card. Exchanging cards is an important ritual in Japanese business culture.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 名刺(めいし)交換(こうかん): business card exchange
  • 名刺(めいし)を渡(わた)す: to hand over a business card
  • 名刺(めいし)を頂(いただ)く: to receive a business card (humble)

BUSINESS ETIQUETTE:

  • Present and receive cards with both hands
  • Hold at corners, don't cover the name
  • Study the card briefly before putting away
  • Never write on someone's card in their presence
  • Place received cards on the table during meetings
