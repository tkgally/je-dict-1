研究室(けんきゅうしつ)

けんきゅうしつ
noun
research lab, professor's office
1. research laboratory, research room
A room or facility where research is conducted.
研究室(けんきゅうしつ)で実験(じっけん)をしている。
We're conducting experiments in the research lab.
研究室(けんきゅうしつ)の学生(がくせい)たちと話(はな)した。
I talked with the students in the lab.
2. professor's office
At universities, refers to a professor's private office where they meet with students.
先生(せんせい)の研究室(けんきゅうしつ)に行(い)った。
I went to the professor's office.

研究室(けんきゅうしつ) can mean research laboratory or professor's office.

MEANINGS:
1. Research laboratory/room where experiments or studies are conducted
2. Professor's office at a university (common meaning in academic contexts)

UNIVERSITY CONTEXT:
Often refers to a professor's office where students go for consultation, rather than a scientific lab.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 研究室(けんきゅうしつ)に行(い)く: to go to the professor's office/lab
  • 研究室(けんきゅうしつ)の学生(がくせい): students in the lab/research group
  • 研究室(けんきゅうしつ)に所属(しょぞく)する: to belong to a research lab

RELATED WORDS:

  • 実験室(じっけんしつ): laboratory (for experiments)
  • 研究所(けんきゅうじょ): research institute
  • ゼミ: seminar (university study group)
