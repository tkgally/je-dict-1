ステレオ

すてれお
noun
stereo; stereo system
1. stereo system
Audio equipment that reproduces sound from two or more channels.
ステレオで音楽(おんがく)を聴(き)く。
I listen to music on the stereo.
新(あたら)しいステレオを買(か)った。
I bought a new stereo system.
ステレオの音(おと)が大(おお)きすぎる。
The stereo is too loud.
2. stereo (sound)
Sound that uses multiple channels to create a spatial effect.

ステレオ is a loanword from English "stereo," referring to both stereo systems and stereo sound.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • ステレオで聴(き)く: to listen on the stereo
  • ステレオの音量(おんりょう): stereo volume
  • ステレオをつける: to turn on the stereo

RELATED TERMS:

  • ステレオ放送(ほうそう): stereo broadcast
  • ステレオ音声(おんせい): stereo sound
  • モノラル: mono (single channel)
  • コンポ: component stereo system

NOTE:
ステレオ can refer to both the audio system (device) and the sound format (stereo vs. mono). Modern usage often refers to older-style audio systems, as newer terms like Bluetoothスピーカー are more common.
