引(ひ)っ張(ぱ)る

ひっぱる
godan verb
to pull, to drag

Definitions

1. to pull, to tug
To draw toward oneself with force.
2. to drag, to stretch
To extend or prolong something.
3. to take (someone) along
To bring someone by persuasion or force.

Examples

ロープを引っ張る。
To pull a rope.
話(はなし)を引っ張る。
To drag out a story.
友達(ともだち)を引っ張ってきた。
I brought my friend along.

Notes

引っ張る is more emphatic than 引(ひ)く.

COMPOUND VERB:

  • 引く + っ + 張る (emphatic form)

EXPRESSIONS:

  • 足(あし)を引っ張る: to hold someone back
  • 引(ひ)っ張(ぱ)りだこ: very much in demand
