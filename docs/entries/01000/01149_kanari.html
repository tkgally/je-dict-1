かなり

かなり
adverb
fairly, quite, considerably
1. fairly, quite, considerably
To a significant degree; more than expected but not extremely. Indicates something is substantial.
今日(きょう)はかなり暑(あつ)いですね。
It's quite hot today, isn't it?
かなり時間(じかん)がかかりました。
It took quite a while.
日本語(にほんご)がかなり上手(じょうず)ですね。
Your Japanese is quite good.
この仕事(しごと)はかなり難(むずか)しいけど、やりがいがある。
This job is fairly difficult, but it's rewarding.
彼(かれ)はかなりのお金持(かねも)ちだと聞(き)いたけど、見(み)た目(め)は普通(ふつう)の人(ひと)と変(か)わらない。
I heard he's quite wealthy, but he looks like an ordinary person.
かなりの + noun = quite a/considerable

かなり indicates something is more than a little but not extreme - 'quite' or 'fairly'. It's stronger than 少し (a little) but weaker than とても (very). The kanji 可成り is rarely used; hiragana is standard. Similar words: なかなか (quite, rather), 結構 (けっこう - fairly, quite).
