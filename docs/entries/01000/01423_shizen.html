自然(しぜん)

しぜん
noun
nature, natural
1. nature, the natural world
The physical world and everything in it not made by humans.
自然(しぜん)を大切(たいせつ)にしましょう。
Let's take care of nature.
2. natural, spontaneous
Existing in or derived from nature; not artificial.
自然(しぜん)な表現(ひょうげん)を使(つか)う。
I use natural expressions.

Can function as a noun or な-adjective. Related: 自然(しぜん)に - naturally, spontaneously.
