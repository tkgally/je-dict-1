About the TKG Japanese-English Learner's Dictionary


This dictionary is for intermediate and advanced learners of Japanese who are actively trying to build their Japanese vocabulary.

I began this dictionary project in early January 2026 in the hopes of creating the sort of dictionary that I desperately wanted when I was studying Japanese more than forty years ago. Nothing like this dictionary existed then. Some years later, I proposed creating a similar dictionary to several Japanese publishers, but they all rejected the idea, on the (very reasonable) grounds that it would have taken a team of lexiographers working for several years to complete and that the dictionary would never sell enough copies to recoup the investment. I gave up the idea until very recently, when AI writing and agentic coding tools became good enough to do almost all of the work.

All of the entry writing and coding and most of the conceptualization is being done by Claude Opus 4.5 using Claude Code for the Web. I am serving mainly as project supervisor. As of January 20, 2026, the content is still a bit choppy, and many improvements remain to be made. But it should be usable by learners even in its present form.

All of the dictionary files are available at the dictionary’s GitHub repository. The dictionary is released into the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. Anybody may use and adapt the dictionary data for any purpose. While credit to me and to Claude would be welcome, I don’t insist on it.
