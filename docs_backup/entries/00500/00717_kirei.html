きれい

きれい
adjective (na-adjective)
beautiful, clean
1. beautiful, pretty
Describes something or someone that is aesthetically pleasing. Often used for scenery, flowers, people (especially women), and objects.
この花(はな)はきれいですね。
This flower is beautiful, isn't it?
きれいな景色(けしき)を見(み)ました。
I saw a beautiful view.
na-adjective form before noun
空(そら)がとてもきれいですね。星(ほし)がたくさん見(み)えます。
The sky is so beautiful. You can see many stars.
きれいな人(ひと)ですね。
She's a beautiful person, isn't she?
Sense 1: beautiful
夕焼(ゆうや)けがきれいだった。
The sunset was beautiful.
Sense 1: beautiful
2. clean, neat, tidy
Describes something free of dirt or mess. Used for rooms, clothes, hands, etc.
部屋(へや)をきれいにしてください。
Please clean the room.
Using the 'clean' meaning
手(て)をきれいに洗(あら)ってください。
Please wash your hands thoroughly.
この服(ふく)はまだきれいなので、洗(あら)わなくてもいいです。
These clothes are still clean, so you don't need to wash them.
毎日(まいにち)机(つくえ)の上(うえ)をきれいにしています。勉強(べんきょう)する前(まえ)に物(もの)をかたづけます。
I clean my desk every day. I tidy things up before studying.
きれいに食(た)べてね。
Eat it all up nicely.
Sense 2: clean/completely

Usually written in hiragana, though the kanji 綺麗(きれい) exists. As a na-adjective, use きれいな before nouns. The two meanings (beautiful and clean) are both very common. For people, きれい is more commonly used for women, while かっこいい is often used for men.
