値段(ねだん)

ねだん
noun
price
1. price, cost
The amount of money required to buy something. The monetary value assigned to goods or services.
この値段(ねだん)は高(たか)いです。
This price is expensive.
値段(ねだん)を教(おし)えてください。
Please tell me the price.
値段(ねだん)が下(さ)がりました。
The price went down.
この店(みせ)は値段(ねだん)が安(やす)くていいです。
This store is nice because the prices are cheap.
野菜(やさい)の値段(ねだん)は季節(きせつ)によって変(か)わりますから、買(か)う前(まえ)に見(み)た方(ほう)がいいですよ。
Vegetable prices change depending on the season, so it's better to check before buying.

More specific than just asking いくら (how much). 値段(ねだん)が高(たか)い/安{やす}い means expensive/cheap.

Related:

  • 定価(ていか) (fixed price)
  • 価格(かかく) (price, more formal).
