不安(ふあん)

ふあん
adjective (na-adjective)
anxious, uneasy
1. anxious, uneasy, worried
A feeling of worry or unease about something uncertain or potentially negative. Can be used as a na-adjective or noun.
試験(しけん)の結果(けっか)が不安(ふあん)です。
I'm anxious about the exam results.
将来(しょうらい)に不安(ふあん)を感(かん)じます。
I feel anxious about the future.
Used as noun with を感じる
不安(ふあん)にならないでください。
Please don't become anxious.

Combines 不(ふ) (not) and 安(あん) (peace). Opposite of 安心(あんしん) (peace of mind). Can function as both a na-adjective (不安(ふあん)な気持(きも)ち - anxious feeling) and a noun (不安(ふあん)がある - there is anxiety).
