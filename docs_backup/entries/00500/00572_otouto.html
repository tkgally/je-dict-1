弟(おとうと)

おとうと
noun
younger brother
1. younger brother
A male sibling who is younger than oneself. Used for both one's own younger brother and others' younger brothers.
弟(おとうと)は高校生(こうこうせい)です。
My younger brother is a high school student.
弟(おとうと)と一緒(いっしょ)に遊(あそ)びました。
I played with my younger brother.
弟(おとうと)は私(わたし)より5歳(さい)年下(としした)です。
My younger brother is 5 years younger than me.
弟(おとうと)は毎日(まいにち)サッカーの練習(れんしゅう)をしています。将来(しょうらい)、プロになりたいそうです。
My younger brother practices soccer every day. He says he wants to become a pro in the future.
弟(おとうと)が小(ちい)さい時(とき)、私(わたし)がよく面倒(めんどう)を見(み)ていました。今(いま)では弟(おとうと)の方(ほう)が背(せ)が高(たか)いです。
When my younger brother was small, I often took care of him. Now he's taller than me.

Unlike older siblings, the humble/polite distinction is less strict for younger siblings. 弟(おとうと)さん is used to refer to someone else's younger brother politely.

Related: 妹(いもうと) (younger sister).
