鹿(しか)

しか
noun
deer
1. deer
A hoofed mammal with antlers (in males). Deer are common in Japan and are famously tame in places like Nara.
奈良公園(ならこうえん)には鹿(しか)がたくさんいる。
There are many deer in Nara Park.
鹿(しか)せんべいを買(か)って鹿(しか)にあげ(あげ)た。
I bought deer crackers and gave them to the deer.
雄(おす)の鹿(しか)は立派(りっぱ)な角(つの)を持(も)っている。
Male deer have magnificent antlers.

鹿(しか) refers to deer. Also pronounced 鹿(か) in some compounds.

Common expressions:

  • 鹿(しか)せんべい (deer crackers - sold in Nara for feeding deer)
  • 鹿(しか)の角(つの) (deer antler)
  • 馬鹿(ばか) (fool - uses the kanji for horse and deer)
  • 鹿(しか)肉(にく) (venison)

Counter: 頭(とう) or 匹(ひき)

Cultural note:
The deer of 奈良(なら) are famous worldwide and considered sacred as messengers of the gods of 春日大社(かすがたいしゃ). They roam freely in Nara Park and bow to visitors for crackers. Miyajima's Itsukushima Shrine also has free-roaming deer. In other areas, deer overpopulation causes agricultural damage.
