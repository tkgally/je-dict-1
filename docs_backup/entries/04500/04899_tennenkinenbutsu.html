天然記念物(てんねんきねんぶつ)

てんねんきねんぶつ
noun
natural monument
1. natural monument, protected natural treasure
Animals, plants, or geological formations designated for protection under Japanese law.
柴犬(しばいぬ)は天然記念物(てんねんきねんぶつ)に指定(してい)されている。
The Shiba Inu is designated as a natural monument.
天然記念物(てんねんきねんぶつ)を保護(ほご)する。
To protect natural monuments.
この木(き)は国(くに)の天然記念物(てんねんきねんぶつ)だ。
This tree is a national natural monument.

A Japanese legal designation for protected species and natural features. Includes certain dog breeds (Shiba Inu, Akita), birds (Japanese crested ibis), and trees. Harming them is illegal.
