焦(あせ)る

あせる
verb (godan)
to be impatient; to be in a hurry; to panic
1. to be impatient; to be anxious
To feel rushed or anxious about achieving something.
締(し)め切(き)りが近(ちか)いので焦(あせ)っている。
I'm feeling rushed because the deadline is near.
焦(あせ)らないで、ゆっくりやりましょう。
Don't rush, let's take it slow.
2. to panic; to be flustered
To become nervous and lose composure due to pressure or unexpected situations.
試験(しけん)中(ちゅう)に焦(あせ)って{ミス}をした。
I panicked during the exam and made mistakes.

焦(あせ)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)). The transitive form is 焦(あせ)らす (to make someone impatient).

ASPECT ({ている}):
焦(あせ)っている indicates an ongoing state of feeling rushed or anxious.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 焦(あせ)らないで - don't rush, take it easy
  • 焦(あせ)る必要(ひつよう)はない - there's no need to rush
  • 焦(あせ)りは禁物(きんもつ) - haste makes waste (being hasty is forbidden)
  • 焦(あせ)って失敗(しっぱい)する - to fail due to rushing

RELATED WORDS:

  • 焦(あせ)り - impatience, anxiety (noun form)
  • 慌(あわ)てる - to panic, to be flustered (similar but emphasizes confusion)
  • 急(いそ)ぐ - to hurry (more neutral, without negative emotional connotation)

NUANCE:
焦(あせ)る implies a negative emotional state where anxiety interferes with calm judgment, while 急(いそ)ぐ simply means to hurry without this psychological burden.
