思(おも)い切(き)る

おもいきる
godan verb
to make up one's mind, to give up, to resign oneself
1. to make up one's mind, to decide firmly
To resolve to do something decisively, often after hesitation.
思(おも)い切(き)って告白(こくはく)した。
I took the plunge and confessed my feelings.
[Note: 思(おも)い切(き)って is a very common adverbial form meaning 'decisively' or 'daringly']
思(おも)い切(き)った行動(こうどう)が必要(ひつよう)だ。
Bold action is necessary.
[Note: 思(おも)い切(き)った is used as an adjective meaning 'bold' or 'drastic']
2. to give up, to resign oneself
To abandon hope or attachment to something and accept the situation.
あきらめて、思(おも)い切(き)ることにした。
I decided to give up and let go.

思(おも)い切(き)る is an intransitive verb (自動詞(じどうし)) meaning to make a firm decision or to give up attachment.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 思(おも)う (to think) + 切(き)る (to cut) → to cut through one's thoughts/hesitation

ASPECT (ている):
思(おも)い切(き)っている indicates a state of having made up one's mind.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 思(おも)い切(き)って + verb (do something decisively)
  • 思(おも)い切(き)った決断(けつだん) (bold decision)
  • 思(おも)い切(き)った行動(こうどう) (bold action)
  • 思(おも)い切(き)りがいい (decisive, able to let go easily)

DERIVED FORMS:

  • 思(おも)い切(き)って: adverb meaning "daringly," "boldly"
  • 思(おも)い切(き)った: adjective meaning "bold," "drastic"
  • 思(おも)い切(き)り: noun/adverb meaning "to one's heart's content" or "with all one's might"
