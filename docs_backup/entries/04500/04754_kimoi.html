キモい

きもい
adjective (i-adjective)
gross, creepy
1. gross, disgusting, creepy
Describing something that causes disgust or discomfort. Slang abbreviation of 気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い.
この虫(むし)、キモい！
This bug is gross!
あの人(ひと)の行動(こうどう)がキモい。
That person's behavior is creepy.
キモいものを見(み)てしまった。
I saw something gross.

Slang abbreviation of 気持(きも)ち悪(わる)い (unpleasant, disgusting). Common among youth. Can be considered rude when directed at people. Often written in katakana. Conjugates as i-adjective: キモくない, キモかった.
