追(お)い掛(か)ける

おいかける
ichidan verb
to chase, to pursue, to run after
1. to chase, to run after
To follow someone or something quickly in order to catch them.
犬(いぬ)が猫(ねこ)を追(お)い掛(か)けている。
The dog is chasing the cat.
泥棒(どろぼう)を追(お)い掛(か)けて捕(つか)まえた。
I chased and caught the thief.
2. to pursue, to go after
To seek or strive for something such as a goal, dream, or career.
夢(ゆめ)を追(お)い掛(か)けて上京(じょうきょう)した。
I moved to Tokyo pursuing my dream.
[Note: Figurative use meaning to pursue a dream]

追(お)い掛(か)ける is a transitive verb (他動詞(たどうし)) meaning to chase or pursue.

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 追(お)う (to chase) + 掛(か)ける (to hang on, to start) → to keep chasing

ASPECT (ている):
追(お)い掛(か)けている indicates an ongoing chase or pursuit.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 犯人(はんにん)を追(お)い掛(か)ける (chase a criminal)
  • 夢(ゆめ)を追(お)い掛(か)ける (pursue a dream)
  • 目標(もくひょう)を追(お)い掛(か)ける (pursue a goal)
  • 追(お)い掛(か)けっこ (a game of chase/tag)

VARIANT:

  • 追(お)いかける: same verb, sometimes written without {掛}

RELATED WORDS:

  • 追(お)い越(こ)す: to overtake (catch up and pass)
  • 追(お)い掛(か)ける focuses on the act of chasing, not necessarily catching
