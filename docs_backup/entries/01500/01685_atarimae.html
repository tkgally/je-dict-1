当(あ)たり前(まえ)

あたりまえ
na-adjective, noun
natural, obvious, commonplace
1. natural, obvious, expected
Something that is expected or goes without saying. A matter of course.
それは当(あ)たり前(まえ)のことです。
That's only natural.
Common expression
当(あ)たり前(まえ)だと思(おも)っていました。
I thought it was obvious.
2. ordinary, commonplace
Normal, usual, nothing special.
当(あ)たり前(まえ)のことを当(あ)たり前(まえ)にやる。
Do ordinary things ordinarily.
Common saying

当(あ)たり前(まえ) indicates something is natural or expected.

FORMS:

  • Na-adjective: 当(あ)たり前(まえ)の/な + noun
  • Noun: 当(あ)たり前(まえ)だ (is natural/obvious)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ)のことだ (it's only natural)
  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ)に (naturally, as a matter of course)
  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ)と思(おも)う (take for granted)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 当然(とうぜん): natural, obvious (formal)
  • 当(あ)たり前(まえ): natural, obvious (casual)
  • もちろん: of course (affirmation)
general
