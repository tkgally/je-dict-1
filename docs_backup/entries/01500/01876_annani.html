あんなに

あんなに
adverb
to that extent, so much
1. to that extent, so much, that much
To such a degree (referring to something distant from both speaker and listener).
あんなに頑張(がんば)ったのに。
Even though I tried so hard.
あんなに美味(おい)しい料理(りょうり)は初(はじ)めて。
This is the first time I've had such delicious food.
あんなに言(い)ったのに、聞(き)かなかった。
Even though I told him so much, he didn't listen.

あんなに means "to that extent" or "that much."

KO-SO-A-DO SERIES:

  • こんなに: this much (near speaker)
  • そんなに: that much (near listener)
  • あんなに: that much (far from both)
  • どんなに: how much (question)

USAGE:

  • Often used with past tense
  • Expresses surprise or emphasis
