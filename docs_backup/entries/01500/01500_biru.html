ビル

びる
noun
building
1. building, office building
A multi-story structure, typically for commercial use.
会社(かいしゃ)は駅前(えきまえ)のビルにある。
The company is in a building in front of the station.
高(たか)いビルが建(た)った。
A tall building was constructed.
このビルの5階(かい)にオフィスがある。
The office is on the 5th floor of this building.

ビル is a loanword from English "building," typically referring to multi-story commercial buildings.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 高(たか)いビル: tall building
  • オフィスビル: office building
  • ビルの5階(かい): 5th floor of the building
  • ビルの屋上(おくじょう): building rooftop

COMPARISON:

  • ビル: commercial/office building (loanword)
  • 建物(たてもの): building (general, native word)
  • マンション: apartment building (residential)
  • 高層(こうそう)ビル: high-rise building

NOTE:
ビル typically implies a concrete/steel commercial structure. Residential buildings are more commonly called マンション or アパート.
