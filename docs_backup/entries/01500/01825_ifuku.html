衣服(いふく)

いふく
noun
clothing, clothes, garments
1. clothing, clothes
Items worn to cover the body.
衣服(いふく)を整(ととの)える。
To arrange one's clothing.
衣服(いふく)費(ひ)を節約(せつやく)する。
To save on clothing expenses.
衣服(いふく)の乱(みだ)れを直(なお)す。
To fix disheveled clothing.
2. garments, attire
Formal term for articles of dress.

衣服(いふく) is a formal term for clothing.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 衣服(いふく): clothing (formal, written)
  • 服(ふく): clothes (common, everyday)
  • 衣類(いるい): clothing, garments (collective)
  • 着物(きもの): clothes, kimono
  • 洋服(ようふく): Western-style clothes

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 衣服(いふく)を着(き)る (to wear clothes)
  • 衣服(いふく)費(ひ) (clothing expenses)
  • 衣食住(いしょくじゅう) (food, clothing, shelter)
