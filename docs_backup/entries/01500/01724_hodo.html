程(ほど)

ほど
particle, noun
degree, extent; about
1. degree, extent
Indicates the degree or extent of something, often used in comparisons.
日本語(にほんご)は難(むずか)しいほど面白(おもしろ)い。
The more difficult Japanese is, the more interesting it is.
〜ば〜ほど pattern
彼(かれ)ほど上手(じょうず)な人(ひと)はいない。
There's no one as skilled as him.
Comparison pattern
2. about, approximately
Indicates an approximate quantity or amount.
1時間(じかん)ほど待(ま)ちました。
I waited about one hour.
Approximate quantity

程(ほど) is a versatile word used for degree and approximation.

KEY PATTERNS:

1. Comparison (〜ほど〜ない):

  • 彼(かれ)ほど速(はや)くない: not as fast as him

2. Proportional (〜ば〜ほど):

  • 練習(れんしゅう)すればするほど: the more you practice

3. Approximate amount:

  • 3日(にち)ほど: about 3 days

4. To the extent (〜ほど):

  • 泣(な)きたいほど嬉(うれ)しい: so happy I could cry

Note: ほど often softens statements compared to より.
