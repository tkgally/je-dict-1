同志(どうし)

どうし
noun
comrade, like-minded person
1. comrade, like-minded person
A person who shares the same beliefs, goals, or cause.
彼(かれ)らは同志(どうし)として協力(きょうりょく)した。
They cooperated as comrades.
同志(どうし)を募(つの)る。
To recruit like-minded people.
革命(かくめい)の同志(どうし)だった。
They were revolutionary comrades.
同志(どうし)として固(かた)い絆(きずな)で結(むす)ばれている。
We are bound by strong ties as comrades.
苦労(くろう)を共(とも)にした同志(どうし)だ。
We are comrades who shared hardships together.
Sense 1: comrade
同志(どうし)を失(うしな)うのは辛(つら)い。
Losing a comrade is painful.
Sense 1: comrade
同志(どうし)たちと目標(もくひょう)に向(む)かって進(すす)む。
We move toward our goal with our comrades.
Sense 1: like-minded people
2. fellow, companion
People who share a common characteristic or situation.
同志(どうし)の名前(なまえ)を呼(よ)び合(あ)った。
They called each other by comrade.
留学生(りゅうがくせい)同志(どうし)で助(たす)け合(あ)った。
We helped each other as fellow international students.
Sense 2: fellow
学生(がくせい)同志(どうし)で意見(いけん)を交換(こうかん)した。
We exchanged opinions as fellow students.
Sense 2: fellow

同志(どうし) refers to people who share common goals or beliefs.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 同志(どうし)を集(あつ)める (gather like-minded people)
  • 同志(どうし)として (as comrades)

USAGE NOTE:

  • Can have political connotations (communist/socialist movements)
  • Also used more generally for allies with shared goals

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 仲間(なかま): companion, colleague (everyday)
  • 同志(どうし): comrade (ideological connotation)
  • 同胞(どうほう): compatriot, fellow countryman
