降(お)ろす

おろす
verb (godan)
to take down, to unload
1. to take down, to lower
To move something from a higher to lower position.
2. to unload, to drop off
To remove passengers or cargo from a vehicle.
荷物(にもつ)を降(お)ろす。
To unload luggage.
駅(えき)で降(お)ろしてください。
Please drop me off at the station.
3. to withdraw (money)
To take money out of an account.
お金(かね)を降(お)ろす。
To withdraw money.

降(お)ろす means to take down or unload.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 降(お)ろす: to take down, unload
  • 下(お)ろす: to lower, let down
  • 卸(おろ)す: wholesale (different meaning)

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 降(お)ろす (transitive): to unload
  • 降(お)りる (intransitive): to get off
