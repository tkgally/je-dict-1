超(こ)える

こえる
verb (ichidan)
to exceed, to cross over
1. to exceed, to surpass
To go beyond a limit or amount.
予算(よさん)を超(こ)える。
To exceed the budget.
100人(にん)を超(こ)えた。
It exceeded 100 people.
2. to cross over, to pass
To go across or beyond a boundary.
国境(こっきょう)を超(こ)える。
To cross the border.

超(こ)える means to exceed or cross over.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 超(こ)える: to exceed, surpass
  • 越(こ)える: to cross over, pass

COMMON USES:

  • 限界(げんかい)を超(こ)える: to exceed limits
  • 想像(そうぞう)を超(こ)える: to exceed imagination
  • 壁(かべ)を超(こ)える: to overcome a barrier
