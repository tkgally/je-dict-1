お金持(かねも)ち

おかねもち
noun
rich person, wealthy person
1. rich person, wealthy person
Someone who has a lot of money.
あの人(ひと)はお金持(かねも)ちだ。
That person is rich.
お金持(かねも)ちになりたい。
I want to become rich.
彼女(かのじょ)はお金持(かねも)ちの家(いえ)に生(う)まれた。
She was born into a wealthy family.

お金持(かねも)ち is a polite/casual term for a wealthy person.

FORMS:

  • 金持(かねも)ち: less polite form
  • お金持(かねも)ち: polite form with お prefix

RELATED:

  • 裕福(ゆうふく)な: wealthy (adjective)
  • 貧乏(びんぼう): poor
