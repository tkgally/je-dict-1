捕(つか)まる

つかまる
verb (godan)
to be caught, to hold on
1. to be caught, to be arrested
To be captured or apprehended.
犯人(はんにん)が捕(つか)まった。
The criminal was caught.
タクシーが捕(つか)まらない。
I can't catch a taxi.
2. to hold on, to grab onto
To grasp something for support.
手(て)すりに捕(つか)まる。
To hold on to the handrail.

捕(つか)まる means to be caught or hold on.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • 捕(つか)まる (intransitive): to be caught
  • 捕(つか)まえる (transitive): to catch

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 捕(つか)まる: to be caught
  • 掴(つか)まる: to hold onto
