協議(きょうぎ)

きょうぎ
noun / suru-verb
conference, discussion, negotiation
1. conference, discussion, consultation
A formal meeting or discussion to reach an agreement or decision.
この件(けん)について協議(きょうぎ)する必要(ひつよう)がある。
We need to discuss this matter.
協議(きょうぎ)の結果(けっか)、合意(ごうい)に達(たっ)した。
As a result of the discussions, an agreement was reached.
2. negotiation
Discussions aimed at reaching an agreement between parties.
両国(りょうこく)は貿易(ぼうえき)協議(きょうぎ)を行(おこな)った。
The two countries held trade negotiations.

協議(きょうぎ) is used for formal discussions and negotiations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 協議(きょうぎ)する (to discuss, to confer)
  • 協議(きょうぎ)を行(おこな)う (to hold discussions)
  • 協議(きょうぎ)の結果(けっか) (as a result of discussions)

COMPOUND WORDS:

  • 労使(ろうし)協議(きょうぎ) (labor-management negotiations)
  • 協議(きょうぎ)会(かい) (conference, council)
  • 協議(きょうぎ)離婚(りこん) (divorce by mutual consent)

HOMOPHONE:

  • 競技(きょうぎ): athletic competition, sport (different meaning)
