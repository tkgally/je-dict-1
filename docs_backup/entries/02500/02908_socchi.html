そっち

そっち
pronoun
that way, there (near you)
1. that way, that direction, there
Casual form indicating a direction or place near the listener.
そっちの方(ほう)に行(い)って。
Go that way (towards you).
そっちに本(ほん)がありますか。
Is there a book over there (near you)?
そっちへ行(い)って。
Go that way.
Sense 1: that direction
そっちを見(み)て。
Look that way.
Sense 1: that direction
そっちに座(すわ)ってください。
Please sit there.
Sense 1: there (near you)
2. that one, your side
Can refer to the listener's side or something near them.
そっちはどうですか。
How is it over there (where you are)?
そっちの天気(てんき)はどう？
How's the weather over there?
Sense 2: your side
そっちは忙(いそが)しい？
Are you busy over there?
Sense 2: your side
そっちにしよう。
Let's go with that one.
Sense 2: that one
そっちは何時(なんじ)？
What time is it there?
Sense 2: your side/location

そっち is the casual form of そちら.

KO-SO-A-DO SERIES:

  • こっち (this way - near speaker)
  • そっち (that way - near listener)
  • あっち (over there - away from both)
  • どっち (which way - question)

FORMALITY:

  • そっち (casual)
  • そちら (polite)
