奥(おく)ゆかしい

おくゆかしい
adjective (i-adjective)
refined; elegant; modest; graceful
1. refined; elegant; modest; graceful
describes someone or something with a dignified, understated elegance that doesn't seek attention
奥(おく)ゆかしい人柄(ひとがら)に惹(ひ)かれた。
I was drawn to their refined personality.
彼女(かのじょ)の振(ふ)る舞(ま)いは奥(おく)ゆかしい。
Her manner is elegant and modest.
京都(きょうと)には奥(おく)ゆかしい雰囲気(ふんいき)のお店(みせ)が多(おお)い。
There are many shops with a refined atmosphere in Kyoto.

USAGE:

  • 奥(おく)ゆかしい人(ひと) (a refined/modest person)
  • 奥(おく)ゆかしい雰囲気(ふんいき) (an elegant atmosphere)
  • 奥(おく)ゆかしさ (noun form: refinement, modesty)

NUANCE:
A distinctly Japanese aesthetic concept. Describes a quality of understated elegance, reserve, and depth. Someone 奥(おく)ゆかしい doesn't flaunt their virtues but has a quiet dignity that becomes apparent over time. Often associated with traditional Japanese culture and values.

ETYMOLOGY:
From 奥(おく) (deep, inner) + ゆかしい (elegant, refined)
