部署(ぶしょ)

ぶしょ
noun
department; division; section; post
1. department; division; section
an organizational unit within a company or institution
新(あたら)しい部署(ぶしょ)に異動(いどう)した。
I was transferred to a new department.
各(かく)部署(ぶしょ)の代表者(だいひょうしゃ)が集(あつ)まった。
Representatives from each department gathered.
この案件(あんけん)はどの部署(ぶしょ)が担当(たんとう)していますか。
Which department is in charge of this matter?

USAGE:

  • 部署(ぶしょ)異動(いどう) (department transfer)
  • 部署(ぶしょ)を移(うつ)る (to move to another department)
  • 各(かく)部署(ぶしょ) (each department)
  • 担当(たんとう)部署(ぶしょ) (department in charge)

COMPARISON:

  • 部署(ぶしょ) - organizational section, post
  • 部門(ぶもん) - division, field (broader)
  • 課(か) - section, subsection (smaller unit)
  • 部(ぶ) - department (often larger)
