こだわり

こだわり
noun
obsession; commitment; attention to detail
1. commitment to quality; attention to detail; dedication to something
a positive sense of being particular or meticulous about something, showing care and dedication
素材(そざい)にこだわりがある。
We're particular about our ingredients.
このラーメン店(てん)はスープへのこだわりがすごい。
This ramen shop has an incredible commitment to their soup.
職人(しょくにん)のこだわりが感(かん)じられる作品(さくひん)だ。
You can feel the craftsman's dedication in this work.
2. fixation; obsession; being hung up on something
being overly attached to or unable to let go of something, sometimes with negative connotations
過去(かこ)へのこだわりを捨(す)てよう。
Let's let go of our fixation on the past.
形式(けいしき)へのこだわりが強(つよ)すぎる。
You're too hung up on formalities.
つまらないことにこだわりすぎないで。
Don't obsess over trivial things.

VERB FORM:
こだわる (to be particular about; to be obsessed with)

SEMANTIC SHIFT:
Originally, こだわる had mainly negative connotations (being hung up on something). In modern usage, it has developed a positive meaning of "being dedicated to quality" or "attention to detail," especially in business and food contexts.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • ～にこだわりがある (to be particular about ~)
  • ～へのこだわり (commitment to ~)
  • こだわりの～ (carefully selected/crafted ~)

MARKETING USE:

こだわり is commonly used in marketing and advertising:

  • こだわりの食材(しょくざい) (carefully selected ingredients)
  • こだわりの一杯(いっぱい) (a carefully crafted drink)
  • こだわりの逸品(いっぴん) (a quality item made with care)

NOTE ON NUANCE:

  • Positive: shows dedication, craftsmanship, quality
  • Negative: suggests inflexibility, being picky, inability to let go
