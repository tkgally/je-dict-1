秋晴(あきば)れ

あきばれ
noun
clear autumn weather; fine fall day
1. clear autumn weather; beautiful autumn sky; fine fall day
the characteristic clear, crisp weather of autumn in Japan, with blue skies and pleasant temperatures
今日(きょう)は秋晴(あきば)れのいい天気(てんき)だ。
Today is beautiful clear autumn weather.
秋晴(あきば)れの日(ひ)は散歩(さんぽ)するのに最高(さいこう)だ。
Clear autumn days are perfect for taking walks.
運動会(うんどうかい)の日(ひ)は秋晴(あきば)れに恵(めぐ)まれ、楽(たの)しい一日(いちにち)になった。
The sports day was blessed with clear autumn weather, making it an enjoyable day.

FORMATION:
秋(あき) (autumn) + 晴(ば)れ (clear weather) → autumn clear weather

CHARACTERISTICS:

  • 澄(す)んだ青空(あおぞら) (clear blue sky)
  • 爽(さわ)やかな空気(くうき) (crisp, refreshing air)
  • 適度(てきど)な気温(きおん) (moderate temperature)

SEASONAL CONTEXT:

秋晴(あきば)れ is most common after the 秋雨前線(あきさめぜんせん) (autumn rain front) passes, typically from late September through November. It's considered ideal weather for:

  • 運動会(うんどうかい) (sports festivals)
  • 紅葉狩(もみじが)り (autumn leaf viewing)
  • 遠足(えんそく) (school excursions)

RELATED SEASONAL WEATHER:

  • 五月晴(さつきば)れ (clear weather in May)
  • 小春日和(こはるびより) (Indian summer)
  • 秋雨(あきさめ) (autumn rain)
