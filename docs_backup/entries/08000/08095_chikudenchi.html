蓄電池(ちくでんち)

ちくでんち
noun
storage battery; rechargeable battery
1. storage battery; rechargeable battery; accumulator
a type of battery that can be recharged by passing an electric current through it, allowing it to be used repeatedly
家庭用(かていよう)蓄電池(ちくでんち)を設置(せっち)した。
We installed a home storage battery.
蓄電池(ちくでんち)があれば停電(ていでん)時(じ)も電気(でんき)が使(つか)える。
With a storage battery, you can use electricity even during power outages.
太陽光発電(たいようこうはつでん)と蓄電池(ちくでんち)を組(く)み合(あ)わせることで電気代(でんきだい)を節約(せつやく)できる。
By combining solar power generation with a storage battery, you can save on electricity costs.

USAGE:
蓄電池(ちくでんち) typically refers to larger rechargeable batteries used for home energy storage or industrial applications. For smaller rechargeable batteries like those in phones, 充電池(じゅうでんち) is more commonly used.

RELATED TERMS:

  • 充電池(じゅうでんち) (rechargeable battery - for smaller devices)
  • 乾電池(かんでんち) (dry cell battery)
  • 太陽光発電(たいようこうはつでん) (solar power generation)
  • 蓄電(ちくでん) (electricity storage)
