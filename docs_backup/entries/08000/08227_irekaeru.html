入(い)れ替(か)える

いれかえる
verb (transitive)
to replace; to swap; to exchange
1. to replace; to swap; to exchange
to remove one thing and put another in its place; to switch positions
電池(でんち)を入(い)れ替(か)えた。
I replaced the batteries.
順番(じゅんばん)を入(い)れ替(か)える。
To change the order.
冬服(ふゆふく)と夏服(なつふく)を入(い)れ替(か)えた。
I swapped out my winter and summer clothes.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
Intransitive pair: 入(い)れ替(か)わる (to be replaced, to change places)

USAGE:

  • 〜を入(い)れ替(か)える (to replace/swap ~)
  • AとBを入(い)れ替(か)える (to swap A and B)

COMMON CONTEXTS:

  • 電池(でんち)を入(い)れ替(か)える (replace batteries)
  • 選手(せんしゅ)を入(い)れ替(か)える (substitute players)
  • 順番(じゅんばん)を入(い)れ替(か)える (change order)
  • 衣替(ころもが)え (seasonal clothing change)

RELATED:

  • 入(い)れ替(か)わる (intransitive: to switch places)
  • 取(と)り替(か)える (to exchange)
  • 置(お)き換(か)える (to replace, substitute)
