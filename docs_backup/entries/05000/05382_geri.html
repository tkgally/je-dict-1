下痢(げり)

げり
noun
diarrhea
1. diarrhea
A condition of having frequent loose or watery bowel movements.
下痢(げり)が続(つづ)いている。
I've been having continuous diarrhea.
食(た)べ過(す)ぎて下痢(げり)になった。
I got diarrhea from eating too much.
下痢止(げりど)めを飲(の)んだ。
I took anti-diarrhea medicine.

下痢 is a common medical condition. The opposite is 便秘 (べんぴ, constipation).

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 下痢をする/になる: to have diarrhea
  • 下痢が続く: diarrhea continues
  • 下痢止め (げりどめ): anti-diarrhea medicine

RELATED WORDS:

  • お腹を壊す (おなかをこわす): to have an upset stomach

Related Words

Antonym: 便秘(べんぴ)
