右(みぎ)

みぎ
noun
right
1. right
The direction opposite to left.
右(みぎ)に曲(ま)がる。
To turn right.
右手(みぎて)で書(か)く。
To write with the right hand.

右(みぎ) means right.

DIRECTIONS:

  • 右(みぎ): right
  • 左(ひだり): left

COMPOUNDS:

  • 右手(みぎて): right hand
  • 右側(みぎがわ): right side
  • 右折(うせつ): right turn
  • 右利(みぎき)き: right-handed
