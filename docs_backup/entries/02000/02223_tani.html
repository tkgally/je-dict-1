谷(たに)

たに
noun
valley, ravine
1. valley
A low area between hills or mountains.
谷(たに)を下(くだ)る。
To go down into the valley.
谷(たに)に川(かわ)が流(なが)れている。
A river flows through the valley.
2. ravine, gorge
A deep narrow valley.

谷(たに) means valley.

ALTERNATE READING:
谷(や) in some place names.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 渓谷(けいこく): valley, gorge
  • 谷間(たにま): valley, ravine
  • 谷底(たにそこ): bottom of a valley
general
