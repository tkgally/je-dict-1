離(はな)れる

はなれる
ichidan verb
to leave, to be separated
1. to leave, to go away from
To move away from a place or person.
家(いえ)を離れる。
To leave home.
席(せき)を離れないでください。
Please don't leave your seat.
Sense 1: to leave
仕事(しごと)を離(はな)れる。
To leave work.
Sense 1: to go away from
現実(げんじつ)から離(はな)れる。
To escape from reality.
Sense 1: to leave
目(め)が離(はな)せない。
I can't take my eyes off it.
Sense 1: to leave (idiomatic)
手(て)を離(はな)さないで。
Don't let go of my hand.
Sense 1: to leave
2. to be separated, to be apart
To become distant from.
親(おや)から離(はな)れて暮(く)らす。
To live apart from one's parents.
Sense 2: to be separated
二人(ふたり)は離(はな)れ離(ばな)れになった。
The two became separated.
Sense 2: to be apart
駅(えき)から少(すこ)し離(はな)れている。
It's a bit far from the station.
Sense 2: to be apart
心(こころ)が離(はな)れてしまった。
Our hearts have grown apart.
Sense 2: to be separated

離(はな)れる is an intransitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 離(はな)す (transitive): to separate
  • 離(はな)れる (intransitive): to be separated

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 目(め)が離(はな)せない: can't take one's eyes off
  • 手(て)が離(はな)れる: to become free (of duties)
