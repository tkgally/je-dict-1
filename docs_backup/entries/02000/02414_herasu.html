減(へ)らす

へらす
godan verb
to decrease, to reduce
1. to decrease, to reduce
To make less in number or amount.
体重(たいじゅう)を減らす。
To reduce weight.
無駄(むだ)を減らす。
To reduce waste.

減らす is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 減らす (transitive): to decrease
  • 減(へ)る (intransitive): to decrease

OPPOSITE:

  • 増(ふ)やす: to increase
general
