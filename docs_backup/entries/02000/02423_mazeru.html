混(ま)ぜる

まぜる
ichidan verb
to mix, to stir
1. to mix, to blend
To combine different things together.
よく混ぜてください。
Please mix well.
2. to include, to let join
To allow someone to participate.
仲間(なかま)に混ぜる。
To let someone join the group.

混ぜる is a transitive verb.

TRANSITIVE/INTRANSITIVE PAIR:

  • 混ぜる (transitive): to mix
  • 混(ま)ざる (intransitive): to be mixed

COOKING:

  • よく混ぜる: to mix well
  • 軽(かる)く混ぜる: to mix lightly
