跡(あと)

あと
noun
trace, mark, remains
1. trace, mark
A visible sign left by something.
足跡(あしあと)が残(のこ)っている。
Footprints remain.
傷(きず)の跡(あと)がある。
There's a scar.
2. remains, ruins
What is left after something is gone.

跡(あと) refers to traces and remains.

COMPOUNDS:

  • 足跡(あしあと): footprint
  • 傷跡(きずあと): scar
  • 遺跡(いせき): ruins, remains
  • 跡継(あとつ)ぎ: successor, heir

NOTE:
Different from 後(あと) (after, behind) which is a different word.
