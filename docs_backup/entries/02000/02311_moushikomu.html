申(もう)し込(こ)む

もうしこむ
godan verb
to apply for, to propose
1. to apply for
To submit a formal request or application.
会員(かいいん)に申し込む。
To apply for membership.
2. to propose, to offer
To formally suggest or request something.
試合(しあい)を申し込む。
To challenge to a match.

申(もう)し込(こ)む is a compound verb (申(もう)す + 込(こ)む).

RELATED NOUN:

  • 申(もう)し込(こ)み: application

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 入会(にゅうかい)を申(もう)し込(こ)む: to apply for admission
  • 購読(こうどく)を申(もう)し込(こ)む: to subscribe
