騙(だま)す

だます
godan verb, transitive
to deceive, to trick, to cheat
1. to deceive, to trick
To make someone believe something false.
人(ひと)を騙(だま)す。
To deceive someone.
騙(だま)されないで。
Don't be fooled.
詐欺師(さぎし)に騙(だま)された。
I was tricked by a scammer.

騙(だま)す implies intentional deception.

PASSIVE FORM:

  • 騙(だま)される: to be deceived

RELATED:

  • 詐欺(さぎ): fraud, scam
