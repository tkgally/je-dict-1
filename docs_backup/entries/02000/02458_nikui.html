～にくい

にくい
auxiliary adjective
hard to, difficult to
1. hard to ~, difficult to ~
Attached to verb stems to indicate difficulty in performing an action.
この漢字(かんじ)は読(よ)みにくい。
This kanji is hard to read.

PATTERN: Verb stem + にくい

OPPOSITE:

  • ～やすい: easy to ~

EXAMPLES:

  • 分(わ)かりにくい: hard to understand
  • 食(た)べにくい: hard to eat
  • 使(つか)いにくい: hard to use
