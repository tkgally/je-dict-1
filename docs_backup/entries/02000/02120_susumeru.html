勧(すす)める

すすめる
verb (ichidan)
to recommend, to advise, to encourage
1. to recommend, to advise
To suggest something as good or suitable.
この本(ほん)を勧(すす)める。
I recommend this book.
医者(いしゃ)に運動(うんどう)を勧(すす)められた。
The doctor recommended exercise to me.
2. to encourage, to urge
To persuade someone to do something.

勧(すす)める means to recommend.

KANJI VARIANTS:

  • 勧(すす)める: to recommend
  • 進(すす)める: to advance, to promote
  • 薦(すす)める: to recommend (formal)

PATTERN:

  • 〜を勧(すす)める: to recommend ~
  • 〜に〜を勧(すす)める: to recommend ~ to someone
