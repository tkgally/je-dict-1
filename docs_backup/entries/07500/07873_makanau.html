賄(まかな)う

まかなう
godan verb
to provide meals; to cover expenses; to manage
1. to provide meals; to feed
to prepare and serve food, especially for workers, guests, or staff
社員(しゃいん)食堂(しょくどう)で昼食(ちゅうしょく)を賄(まかな)う。
The company cafeteria provides lunch.
大(おお)きなイベントの食事(しょくじ)を一人(ひとり)で賄(まかな)った。
I single-handedly provided the food for the big event.
2. to cover expenses; to finance
to pay for or provide the funds needed for something
費用(ひよう)は寄付金(きふきん)で賄(まかな)われている。
The costs are covered by donations.
自分(じぶん)の収入(しゅうにゅう)だけで生活費(せいかつひ)を賄(まかな)えるだろうか。
Will I be able to cover my living expenses with just my income?

賄(まかな)う has two main meanings: providing food and covering costs.

SENSE 1 - FOOD:

Originally referred to managing household affairs and preparing meals.

  • Common in contexts of catering, staff meals, or group dining
  • Related noun: 賄(まかな)い (staff meal at restaurants)

SENSE 2 - FINANCES:

Extended to mean covering or financing expenses.

  • Often used in passive: 費用(ひよう)が賄(まかな)われる

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 食事(しょくじ)を賄(まかな)う (to provide meals)
  • 費用(ひよう)を賄(まかな)う (to cover costs)
  • 経費(けいひ)を賄(まかな)う (to cover expenses)
  • 賄(まかな)い付(つ)き (with meals included)

RELATED NOUN まかない:
賄(まかな)い refers to staff meals at restaurants, a beloved aspect of food service culture.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. Takes を for the object being provided or covered.

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in both everyday and business contexts.
