発覚(はっかく)

はっかく
noun, suru verb
detection; exposure; coming to light
1. detection; exposure; coming to light
The discovery or revelation of something that was hidden, especially wrongdoing, crimes, or scandals.
不正(ふせい)が発覚(はっかく)した。
The misconduct came to light.
事件(じけん)が発覚(はっかく)して大(おお)きなニュースになった。
The incident was exposed and became big news.
長年(ながねん)の横領(おうりょう)が内部(ないぶ)調査(ちょうさ)で発覚(はっかく)した。
Years of embezzlement was discovered through an internal investigation.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 不正(ふせい)が発覚(はっかく)する - misconduct comes to light
  • 事件(じけん)が発覚(はっかく)する - an incident is exposed
  • 発覚(はっかく)を恐(おそ)れる - to fear discovery
  • 発覚(はっかく)を免(まぬが)れる - to escape detection

USAGE NOTES:
Typically used with the intransitive pattern: Xが発覚(はっかく)する (X comes to light)
Almost always refers to negative things being discovered.

RELATED WORDS:

  • 隠蔽(いんぺい) - concealment (what is done before 発覚(はっかく))
  • 暴露(ばくろ) - exposure (active revelation by someone)
