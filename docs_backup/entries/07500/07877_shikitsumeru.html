敷(し)き詰(つ)める

しきつめる
ichidan verb, transitive
to spread all over; to cover entirely; to pave
1. to spread all over; to cover entirely
To lay or spread something across an entire surface without gaps.
庭(にわ)に砂利(じゃり)を敷(し)き詰(つ)めた。
I spread gravel all over the garden.
床(ゆか)にタイルを敷(し)き詰(つ)める工事(こうじ)が始(はじ)まった。
Work to tile the entire floor has begun.
部屋(へや)一面(いちめん)に畳(たたみ)を敷(し)き詰(つ)めた和室(わしつ)は落(お)ち着(つ)く。
A Japanese-style room with tatami mats covering the entire floor is calming.

CONSTRUCTION:
Compound verb from 敷(し)く (to spread, lay) + 詰(つ)める (to pack, fill). The 詰(つ)める component adds the sense of leaving no gaps.

TRANSITIVITY:
Transitive verb. The thing being spread takes を, and the surface takes に.

COMMON OBJECTS:

  • 砂利(じゃり)を敷(し)き詰(つ)める - to spread gravel
  • タイルを敷(し)き詰(つ)める - to lay tiles
  • 畳(たたみ)を敷(し)き詰(つ)める - to cover with tatami
  • 芝生(しばふ)を敷(し)き詰(つ)める - to cover with lawn
  • レンガを敷(し)き詰(つ)める - to pave with bricks
