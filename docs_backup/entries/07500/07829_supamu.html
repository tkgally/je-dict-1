スパム

すぱむ
noun
spam
1. spam (email)
spam; unwanted electronic messages, especially bulk email or messages sent indiscriminately
スパムメールが多(おお)くて困(こま)る。
I'm troubled by the amount of spam emails.
スパムフィルターを設定(せってい)した。
I set up a spam filter.
SNSでスパムアカウントをブロックする。
Block spam accounts on social media.
2. SPAM (food)
SPAM; the canned precooked meat product
沖縄(おきなわ)ではスパムがよく食(た)べられている。
SPAM is commonly eaten in Okinawa.
スパムおにぎりが人気(にんき)だ。
SPAM rice balls are popular.
スパムと卵(たまご)を炒(いた)めた。
I fried SPAM with eggs.

スパム has two distinct meanings in Japanese: unwanted electronic messages (from internet culture) and the canned meat product SPAM (popular in Okinawan cuisine).

DIGITAL USAGE (Sense 1):

  • スパムメール (spam email)
  • スパム行為(こうい) (spamming)
  • スパムフィルター (spam filter)
  • スパムアカウント (spam account)
  • スパムを報告(ほうこく)する (to report as spam)

FOOD USAGE (Sense 2):

SPAM became popular in Okinawa during the U.S. military occupation after WWII. It's now integral to Okinawan cuisine.

  • スパムおにぎり (SPAM rice ball/musubi)
  • スパム丼(どん) (SPAM rice bowl)
  • ゴーヤチャンプルー (Okinawan stir-fry often includes SPAM)

ORIGIN:
The term "spam" for unwanted messages comes from a Monty Python sketch about the repetitive nature of SPAM (the food), applied to repetitive unwanted messages.

REGISTER: Neutral. Context determines meaning.
