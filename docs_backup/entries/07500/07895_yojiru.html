よじる

よじる
godan verb, transitive
to twist; to wrench; to wring
1. to twist; to wrench
To forcefully twist or wring something by rotating.
体(からだ)をよじって逃(に)げようとした。
I tried to escape by twisting my body.
笑(わら)いすぎてお腹(なか)をよじった。
I laughed so hard my stomach hurt (I twisted my belly from laughing).
腕(うで)をよじられて痛(いた)かった。
My arm was twisted and it hurt.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 身(み)をよじる - to twist one's body (often from pain, tickling, or trying to escape)
  • 腹(はら)/お腹(なか)をよじる - to twist one's belly (from laughing hard)
  • 腕(うで)をよじる - to twist someone's arm

CONJUGATION NOTE:
Godan verb conjugating with る. Dictionary form: よじる

RELATED:

  • 捻(ひね)る - to twist, turn (more general)
  • 絞(しぼ)る - to wring, squeeze
  • よじ登(のぼ)る - to climb (by twisting one's body)
