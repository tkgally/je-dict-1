乗(の)り過(す)ごす

のりすごす
godan verb
to ride past; to miss one's stop
1. to ride past; to miss one's stop
to stay on a train, bus, etc. past the intended destination
駅(えき)を乗(の)り過(す)ごした。
I missed my station.
居眠(いねむ)りして終点(しゅうてん)まで乗(の)り過(す)ごしてしまった。
I dozed off and rode all the way to the terminal.
スマホに夢中(むちゅう)で乗(の)り過(す)ごさないように。
Don't miss your stop because you're absorbed in your phone.

乗(の)り過(す)ごす is commonly used for missing stops on public transportation.

VERB TYPE:

  • Godan verb (す-verb)
  • Transitive (takes を)

COMPOUND STRUCTURE:

  • 乗(の)る (to ride) + 過(す)ごす (to spend/pass)
  • Part of the productive ～過(す)ごす compound pattern

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 駅(えき)を乗(の)り過(す)ごす (miss a station)
  • バス停(てい)を乗(の)り過(す)ごす (miss a bus stop)
  • 終点(しゅうてん)まで乗(の)り過(す)ごす (ride to the terminal)
  • うっかり乗(の)り過(す)ごす (accidentally ride past)

COMMON CAUSES:

  • 居眠(いねむ)り (dozing)
  • スマホ (smartphone)
  • 本(ほん) (reading a book)
  • 考(かんが)え事(ごと) (lost in thought)

RELATED COMPOUND VERBS:

  • 通(とお)り過(す)ぎる (to pass by)
  • 見過(みすご)ごす (to overlook)
  • 聞(き)き過(す)ごす (to fail to hear)

SIMILAR EXPRESSIONS:

  • 乗(の)り過(す)ぎる (to ride too much/long - different meaning)

CULTURAL NOTE:

  • Very common experience in Japan given extensive train/subway use
  • Station announcements help prevent this
  • Apps like 乗換案内(のりかえあんない) have alarm features

REGISTER: Neutral. Very common in everyday conversation.
