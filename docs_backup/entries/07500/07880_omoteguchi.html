表口(おもてぐち)

おもてぐち
noun
front entrance; main entrance
1. front entrance; main entrance
the main or formal entrance to a building, as opposed to back or side entrances
表口(おもてぐち)から入(はい)ってください。
Please enter through the front entrance.
表口(おもてぐち)は閉(し)まっているので裏口(うらぐち)へ回(まわ)ってください。
The front entrance is closed, so please go around to the back door.
店(みせ)の表口(おもてぐち)に行列(ぎょうれつ)ができていた。
There was a line at the front entrance of the store.

表口(おもてぐち) refers to the main or front entrance of a building.

COMPOSITION:

  • 表(おもて): Front, surface, outside
  • 口(ぐち): Opening, entrance

CONTRAST WITH 裏口(うらぐち):

  • 表口(おもてぐち): Front entrance (formal, public)
  • 裏口(うらぐち): Back entrance (informal, service)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 表口(おもてぐち)から入(はい)る (to enter from the front)
  • 表口(おもてぐち)で待(ま)つ (to wait at the front entrance)
  • 表口(おもてぐち)に回(まわ)る (to go around to the front)

RELATED TERMS:

  • 裏口(うらぐち) (back entrance)
  • 玄関(げんかん) (entrance, entryway)
  • 入口(いりぐち) (entrance - general)
  • 正面(しょうめん)玄関(げんかん) (main entrance - more formal)

NUANCE:

  • 表口(おもてぐち) emphasizes the "front" or public-facing nature
  • More commonly used for shops and traditional buildings
  • 正面(しょうめん)入口(いりぐち) or 正面(しょうめん)玄関(げんかん) is more formal for official buildings

REGISTER: Neutral. Common in everyday speech.

Related Words

Antonym: 裏口(うらぐち) (back entrance)
