偏食(へんしょく)

へんしょく
noun, suru-verb
picky eating; unbalanced diet
1. picky eating; unbalanced diet; food preferences
Eating only certain types of food or avoiding specific foods, resulting in a nutritionally unbalanced diet.
子供(こども)の偏食(へんしょく)に悩(なや)んでいる。
I'm worried about my child's picky eating.
偏食(へんしょく)は健康(けんこう)に良(よ)くない。
Picky eating is not good for your health.
偏食(へんしょく)を直(なお)そうと努力(どりょく)している。
I'm trying to fix my picky eating habits.

ETYMOLOGY:
偏(へん) (biased, one-sided) + 食(しょく) (eating) - eating that is biased toward certain foods.

USAGE:

Describes:

  • Children who refuse vegetables
  • Adults with limited food preferences
  • Nutritionally unbalanced diets

COLLOCATIONS:

  • 偏食(へんしょく)が激(はげ)しい - to be very picky
  • 偏食(へんしょく)を直(なお)す - to fix picky eating
  • 偏食(へんしょく)傾向(けいこう) - tendency toward picky eating

RELATED:

  • 好(す)き嫌(きら)い - likes and dislikes (food)
  • 間食(かんしょく) - snacking
