効率化(こうりつか)

こうりつか
noun/suru-verb
streamlining; making more efficient
1. streamlining; making efficient; efficiency improvement
The process of making operations, processes, or systems more efficient and productive.
業務(ぎょうむ)の効率化(こうりつか)を進(すす)める。
We are promoting the streamlining of operations.
IT導入(どうにゅう)で仕事(しごと)が効率化(こうりつか)された。
Work was streamlined through IT implementation.
コスト削減(さくげん)と効率化(こうりつか)が課題(かだい)だ。
Cost reduction and efficiency improvement are challenges.

WORD STRUCTURE:
効率(こうりつ) (efficiency) + 化(か) (-ization)

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 効率化(こうりつか)を図(はか)る - to promote efficiency
  • 効率化(こうりつか)を進(すす)める - to advance streamlining
  • 効率化(こうりつか)が求(もと)められる - efficiency is required

BUSINESS CONTEXTS:

  • 生産(せいさん)の効率化(こうりつか) - production efficiency
  • 物流(ぶつりゅう)の効率化(こうりつか) - logistics streamlining
  • 事務(じむ)の効率化(こうりつか) - office work efficiency

RELATED -化(か) WORDS:

  • 自動化(じどうか) - automation
  • 最適化(さいてきか) - optimization
  • 合理化(ごうりか) - rationalization
