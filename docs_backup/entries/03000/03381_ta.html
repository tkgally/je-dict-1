他(た)

た
noun
other, another
1. other, another
Something or someone else; things or people besides the one(s) mentioned. Often refers to other places, things, or matters.
他(た)に質問(しつもん)はありますか。
Are there any other questions?
他に = besides this, in addition
他(た)の人(ひと)に聞(き)いてみてください。
Please try asking someone else.
他の + noun = other/another
この問題(もんだい)は他(た)にも影響(えいきょう)する。
This problem affects other things as well.

GRAMMAR PATTERNS:

  • 他(た)に: besides, in addition, else
  • 他(た)の + noun: other/another (noun)
  • その他(た): and others, etc.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 他(た): Refers to 'other' in general (places, things, matters)
  • 別(べつ): Emphasizes 'different' or 'separate'
  • 外(ほか): Often interchangeable with 他, but slightly more casual

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 他(た)でもない (none other than)
  • 他(た)ならぬ (no other than, the very)

Related Words

Related: 外(ほか) (similar meaning)
