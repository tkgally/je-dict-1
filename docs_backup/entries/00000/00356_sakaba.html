酒場(さかば)

さかば
noun
bar, tavern
1. bar, tavern, pub
A place where alcoholic drinks are served.
仕事(しごと)の後(あと)、酒場(さかば)に寄(よ)った。
After work, I stopped by a bar.
この通(とお)りには古(ふる)い酒場(さかば)が多(おお)い。
There are many old bars on this street.
大衆(たいしゅう)酒場(さかば)で安(やす)く飲(の)める。
You can drink cheaply at a public bar.

酒場(さかば) is a general term for a bar or tavern where alcohol is served.

Common collocations:

  • 大衆(たいしゅう)酒場(さかば) (public bar, working-class bar)
  • 酒場(さかば)で飲(の)む (to drink at a bar)
  • 酒場(さかば)に行(い)く (to go to a bar)

Related words for drinking establishments:

  • バー (bar - often Western-style)
  • 居酒屋(いざかや) (izakaya - Japanese-style pub with food)
  • スナック (snack bar - hostess bar)
  • 飲(の)み屋(や) (drinking place - casual term)
  • パブ (pub)
