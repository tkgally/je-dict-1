処分(しょぶん)

しょぶん
noun
disposal; punishment
1. disposal; getting rid of
The act of throwing away or selling something no longer needed.
不用品(ふようひん)を処分(しょぶん)する。
To dispose of unwanted items.
書類(しょるい)を処分(しょぶん)する。
To dispose of documents.
2. punishment; disciplinary action
A penalty or action taken against someone who has done wrong.
懲戒(ちょうかい)処分(しょぶん)を受(う)けた。
I received disciplinary action.

処分(しょぶん) means disposal or punishment.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 処分(しょぶん)する: to dispose of; to punish
  • 懲戒(ちょうかい)処分(しょぶん): disciplinary action
  • 処分(しょぶん)品(ひん): clearance items

USAGE:
Context determines whether it means disposal or punishment.
