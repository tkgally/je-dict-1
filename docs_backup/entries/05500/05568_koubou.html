興亡(こうぼう)

こうぼう
noun
rise and fall; prosperity and decline
1. rise and fall; prosperity and decline
The cycle of flourishing and declining, especially of nations, dynasties, or civilizations.
帝国(ていこく)の興亡(こうぼう)を学(まな)ぶ。
To study the rise and fall of empires.
歴史(れきし)は国(くに)の興亡(こうぼう)の繰(く)り返(かえ)しだ。
History is a repetition of the rise and fall of nations.
興亡(こうぼう)の歴史(れきし)を振(ふ)り返(かえ)る。
To look back on the history of rise and fall.

興亡(こうぼう) combines 興(こう) (rise, prosper) and 亡(ぼう) (perish, decline). It's a literary term often used in historical contexts.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 国(くに)の興亡(こうぼう): the rise and fall of a nation
  • 興亡(こうぼう)の歴史(れきし): history of rise and decline

Related Words

Synonym: 盛衰(せいすい) (similar)
