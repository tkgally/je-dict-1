一貫性(いっかんせい)

いっかんせい
noun
consistency; coherence
1. consistency; coherence
The quality of being consistent throughout; logical connection between parts.
政策(せいさく)に一貫性(いっかんせい)がない。
The policy lacks consistency.
主張(しゅちょう)に一貫性(いっかんせい)を持(も)たせる。
To maintain consistency in one's argument.
一貫性(いっかんせい)のある対応(たいおう)が求(もと)められる。
A consistent response is required.

一貫性(いっかんせい) is formed from 一貫(いっかん) (consistency) + 性(せい). It's important in logic, policy, and communication.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 一貫性(いっかんせい)がある/ない: to have/lack consistency
  • 一貫性(いっかんせい)を保(たも)つ: to maintain consistency
general
