極(きわ)めて

きわめて
adverb
extremely; exceedingly
1. extremely; exceedingly; very
To a very high degree; used to emphasize the extent of something.
極(きわ)めて重要(じゅうよう)な問題(もんだい)。
An extremely important issue.
極(きわ)めて珍(めずら)しい。
Extremely rare.
成功(せいこう)の可能性(かのうせい)は極(きわ)めて低(ひく)い。
The chance of success is extremely low.

極(きわ)めて is an adverb meaning extremely.

USAGE:
More formal than とても or 非常(ひじょう)に.

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • とても: very
  • 非常(ひじょう)に: very, extremely
  • 大変(たいへん): very, greatly
