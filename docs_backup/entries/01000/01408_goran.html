ご覧(らん)

ごらん
noun
looking, seeing (honorific)
1. looking, seeing (honorific)
Honorific form of 見る, used when respectfully referring to someone looking at something.
どうぞご覧(らん)ください。
Please have a look.
こちらをご覧(らん)になりましたか。
Have you seen this?
ご覧(らん)のとおり、問題(もんだい)はありません。
As you can see, there are no problems.
メニューをご覧(らん)になってください。
Please take a look at the menu.
資料(しりょう)をご覧(らん)いただけますか。
Would you please look at the documents?

Honorific form. Common patterns:\n- ご覧(らん)ください - please look\n- ご覧(らん)になる - to look (honorific verb)\n- ご覧(らん)のとおり - as you can see
