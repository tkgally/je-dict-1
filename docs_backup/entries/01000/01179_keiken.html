経験(けいけん)

けいけん
noun, suru verb
experience
1. experience
Practical contact with and observation of facts or events; knowledge or skill gained through involvement.
いい経験(けいけん)になりました。
It was a good experience.
Common expression
仕事(しごと)の経験(けいけん)がありますか。
Do you have work experience?
2. to experience (suru verb)
When used as a suru verb, means 'to experience' or 'to go through.'
初(はじ)めて経験(けいけん)しました。
I experienced it for the first time.
Using 経験する

経験 is used for personal experiences.

Common patterns:

  • ～の経験がある (to have experience of)
  • 経験を積む (けいけんをつむ - to gain experience)
  • 経験者 (けいけんしゃ - experienced person). Compare with 体験 (たいけん - firsthand experience, often for specific events or activities).
