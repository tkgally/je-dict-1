滑(なめ)らか

なめらか
na-adjective
smooth, silky
1. smooth, silky, sleek
Having an even surface without roughness or bumps. Often describes texture.
赤(あか)ちゃんの肌(はだ)は滑(なめ)らかだ。
A baby's skin is smooth.
2. smooth, fluent, flowing
Moving or proceeding without difficulties or interruptions. Used for speech, movements, or processes.
滑(なめ)らかな動(うご)きで踊(おど)る。
To dance with smooth movements.
会議(かいぎ)は滑(なめ)らかに進(すす)んだ。
The meeting proceeded smoothly.
Adverbial usage

FORMS:

  • Adverb: 滑(なめ)らかに (smoothly)
  • Noun: 滑(なめ)らかさ (smoothness)

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 滑(なめ)らかな肌(はだ) (smooth skin)
  • 滑(なめ)らかな口調(くちょう) (smooth tone of voice)
  • 滑(なめ)らかに進(すす)む (proceed smoothly)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • すべすべ - smooth to the touch (often for skin)
  • つるつる - slippery smooth (can imply too slippery)
  • 滑(なめ)らか is more literary and can describe both texture and abstract smoothness.
