煩(わずら)わしい

わずらわしい
i-adjective
troublesome, annoying
1. troublesome, bothersome, annoying
Describes something complicated or requiring effort that one would rather avoid.
手続(てつづ)きが煩(わずら)わしくて嫌(いや)になる。
The procedures are so troublesome that I'm fed up.
人間関係(にんげんかんけい)が煩(わずら)わしいと感(かん)じることがある。
Sometimes I find interpersonal relationships bothersome.
煩(わずら)わしい質問(しつもん)には答(こた)えたくない。
I don't want to answer annoying questions.

FORMS:

  • Adverbial: 煩(わずら)わしく
  • Noun form: 煩(わずら)わしさ

煩(わずら)わしい describes things that are complicated, require effort, or cause mental burden. Often used for:

  • Administrative procedures
  • Social obligations
  • Complicated relationships

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 手続(てつづ)きが煩(わずら)わしい (procedures are troublesome)
  • 煩(わずら)わしいことを避(さ)ける (avoid troublesome things)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 面倒(めんどう): General trouble or bother
  • 煩(わずら)わしい: More formal, emphasizes mental burden
  • 鬱陶(うっとう)しい: Gloomy/oppressive annoyance

REGISTER: Slightly formal; used in both written and spoken Japanese.
