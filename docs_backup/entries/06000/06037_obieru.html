怯(おび)える

おびえる
verb (ichidan)
to be frightened, to cower
1. to be frightened, to be scared, to cower
To feel fear or terror, often showing visible signs of being afraid. Used for both sudden fright and ongoing anxiety or dread.
子供(こども)たちは雷(かみなり)に怯(おび)えていた。
The children were frightened of the thunder.
犬(いぬ)が花火(はなび)の音(おと)に怯(おび)えて隠(かく)れた。
The dog cowered and hid, frightened by the fireworks.
怯(おび)えた目(め)で彼(かれ)を見(み)た。
She looked at him with frightened eyes.
Uses attributive form

TRANSITIVITY: 自動詞(じどうし) (intransitive). No pair verb. Pattern: Xに怯(おび)える (be frightened of/by X).

ASPECT: ている indicates ongoing state of fear. 怯(おび)えている = 'is scared' or 'is living in fear.'

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • 恐怖(きょうふ)に怯(おび)える (cower in fear)
  • 影(かげ)に怯(おび)える (be afraid of shadows; figuratively, be paranoid)
  • 怯(おび)えた様子(ようす) (frightened appearance)

SIMILAR WORDS: 恐(こわ)がる is more casual and common; 怯(おび)える emphasizes visible signs of fear or cowering.
