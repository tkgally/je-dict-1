もしも

もしも
conjunction, adverb
if, in case
1. if, supposing, in case
Used to introduce a hypothetical condition; an emphatic form of もし.
もしも雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ったら、中止(ちゅうし)します。
If it rains, we'll cancel.
もしも私(わたし)が鳥(とり)だったら。
If I were a bird...
Used for counterfactual/imaginary conditions
もしもの時(とき)のために準備(じゅんび)しておく。
To prepare for emergencies.
もしもの時 = in case of emergency

USAGE: もしも is a more emphatic version of もし (if). It adds emotional weight or emphasis to hypothetical situations.

COMMON PATTERNS:

  • もしも〜たら (if ~ were to happen)
  • もしも〜なら (if ~ is the case)
  • もしもの時(とき) (in case of emergency)
  • もしもし (hello - on the phone, derived form)

CONTRAST:

  • もし - standard "if"
  • もしも - emphatic "if" (more emotional/dramatic)
  • 仮(かり)に - supposing, hypothetically (formal)
  • 万一(まんいち) - in the unlikely event
