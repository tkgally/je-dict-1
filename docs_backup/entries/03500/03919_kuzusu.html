崩(くず)す

くずす
godan verb
to break, to demolish; to make change (money)
1. to break, to demolish, to tear down
To cause something to fall apart or collapse.
古(ふる)い建物(たてもの)を崩(くず)す。
Demolish an old building.
2. to break (a bill), to make change
To exchange a large bill for smaller denominations.
この一万円(いちまんえん)札(さつ)を崩(くず)してもらえますか？
Could you break this 10,000-yen bill for me?
3. to ruin, to upset (health, posture, etc.)
To cause something to deviate from its proper state.
体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)して休(やす)んでいる。
I'm resting because I'm not feeling well.

崩(くず)す means to break down, demolish, or cause to collapse.

TRANSITIVITY:

  • Type: Transitive (他動詞(たどうし))
  • Pair: 崩(くず)れる (intransitive - to collapse)
  • Pattern: Xを崩(くず)す

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • お金(かね)を崩(くず)す (to break money, get change)
  • 体調(たいちょう)を崩(くず)す (to become unwell)
  • 姿勢(しせい)を崩(くず)す (to relax one's posture)
  • 形(かたち)を崩(くず)す (to lose shape)
  • バランスを崩(くず)す (to lose balance)

NOTE: 崩(くず)す is very common for breaking bills into smaller change.
