糊(のり)

のり
noun
glue, paste, starch
1. glue, paste, adhesive
A sticky substance used for joining things together.
糊(のり)で紙(かみ)を貼(は)り合(あ)わせる。
Paste papers together with glue.
封筒(ふうとう)の糊(のり)がはがれた。
The glue on the envelope came off.
2. starch (for clothes)
A substance used to stiffen fabric.
ワイシャツに糊(のり)をきかせる。
Starch a dress shirt.

糊(のり) refers to paste, glue, or starch. It's traditionally made from rice or wheat starch.

COMMON EXPRESSIONS:

  • 糊(のり)で貼(は)る (to paste with glue)
  • 糊(のり)をつける (to apply glue)
  • 糊付(のりづ)け (starching, pasting)
  • 糊(のり)がきいている (well-starched, stiff)

NOTE: Not to be confused with 海苔(のり) (seaweed/nori for sushi), which has the same reading but different kanji.

RELATED VOCABULARY:

  • 接着剤(せっちゃくざい) (adhesive, general term)
  • スティックのり (glue stick)
