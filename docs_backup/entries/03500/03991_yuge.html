湯気(ゆげ)

ゆげ
noun
steam; vapor
1. steam; vapor
The visible mist rising from hot water or food. Specifically refers to water vapor that can be seen.
お風呂(ふろ)から湯気(ゆげ)が立(た)っている。
Steam is rising from the bath.
熱(あつ)い料理(りょうり)から湯気(ゆげ)が出(で)ている。
Steam is coming from the hot food.
湯気(ゆげ)で眼鏡(めがね)が曇(くも)った。
My glasses fogged up from the steam.

USAGE: Specifically refers to steam from hot water or food - the visible vapor.

COMMON COLLOCATIONS:

  • 湯気(ゆげ)が立(た)つ (steam rises)
  • 湯気(ゆげ)が出(で)る (steam comes out)
  • 湯気(ゆげ)が立(た)ち上(のぼ)る (steam rises up)

SIMILAR WORDS:

  • 蒸気(じょうき) - steam (more technical, also for engines)
  • 水蒸気(すいじょうき) - water vapor (scientific term)
  • 煙(けむり) - smoke (from fire/burning)
